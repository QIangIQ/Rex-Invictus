### **Vice-Lord (소교주, 小敎主)**[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fnano-mashine.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHeavenly_Demon_Divine_Cult%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D6&uselang=en "Sign in to edit")

Is the child of the Lord born from the Holy Maiden that was chosen by the Holy Fire, an item originated from the Fire Faith Order. Its rank is next to the Lord. but their authority is usually similar to or lower than that of the [Council Elder](https://nano-mashine.fandom.com/wiki/Council_Elder "Council Elder"). But due to the incident that occurred 500 years ago that led to the abolishment of the Holy Fire system, the Lord took up wives from the [Six Clans](https://nano-mashine.fandom.com/wiki/Six_Clans "Six Clans") and the offspring will become **Vice-Lord Candidates** and fight for the position known as the "Battle of the Heirs"[[3]](https://nano-mashine.fandom.com/wiki/Heavenly_Demon_Divine_Cult#cite_note-:0-3).

### **Holy Maiden or the Demonic Lady (신녀, 神女)**[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fnano-mashine.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHeavenly_Demon_Divine_Cult%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D7&uselang=en "Sign in to edit")

## Clans[](https://auth.fandom.com/signin?redirect=https%3A%2F%2Fnano-mashine.fandom.com%2Fwiki%2FHeavenly_Demon_Divine_Cult%3Fveaction%3Dedit%26section%3D12&uselang=en "Sign in to edit")

The Heavenly Demon Divine Cult has a long history that was composed of many different clans, ranging from high-ranking to small sized clans. The cult is centered on the [Cheon Family](https://nano-mashine.fandom.com/wiki/Cheon_Family "Cheon Family") who has the highest ranking, along with the founding [Guardian Families](https://nano-mashine.fandom.com/wiki/Guardian_Family "Guardian Family").

After Cheon Yeo Woon became the 24th Lord, he disbanded the Six Clan and did away their corrupt system return it to a Family system. Other personnel would belong to the Cult with resources and martial arts based on their own individual strength and talent, so the cult can become stronger ever without a monopolization on power.
