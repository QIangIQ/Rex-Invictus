The [[Zeus-Wrath Hunter Killer Satellites]] designates a class of autonomous orbital sterilization platforms engineered by [[Athena]] under directive authority of the [[Olympian High Council]]. Each satellite functions as a high-orbit strategic extermination asset capable of independent navigation, extended stealth drift, and full biosphere-level annihilation.
## Primary Weapon System — [[Pathogen Alpha-7]]

### Deployment Method

- Dispersed via scatter-launch kinetic re-entry warheads
    
- Atmospheric fracture canisters aerosolize payload at multi-kilometer altitude
    
- Distribution vectors include atmospheric circulation, hydrological systems, and surface contact pathways
    

---

## Phase I — Biospheric Assimilation

[[Pathogen Alpha-7]] initiates immediate aggressive organic breakdown upon exposure.

- Rapid cellular membrane destabilization
    
- Structural liquefaction of organic matter
    
- Exponential replication across airborne and aqueous mediums
    
- Near-instant systemic failure in exposed biological organisms
    

Mortality rate approaches totality within the exposed biosphere.

---

## Phase II — Volatile Byproduct Generation

Assimilation produces dense accumulations of:

- Highly flammable volatile vapor
    
- Toxic aerosolized particulate
    
- Heavy hydrocarbon sludge residues
    

These byproducts are lethal to surviving biological entities and create an atmosphere saturated with combustible gases.

---

## Phase III — [[Hyperium]] Ignition Protocol

Warheads simultaneously deploy secondary ignition payloads consisting of [[Hyperium]] detonation charges.

Upon ignition:

- Atmospheric vapor cloud combusts
    
- Planet-wide firestorm cascade initiated
    
- Oxygen content rapidly consumed
    
- Surface temperatures escalate beyond oceanic boiling threshold
    

Projected effects include:

- Ocean flash-vaporization
    
- Continental vitrification
    
- Atmospheric ignition wave propagation
    
- Total erasure of biological and ecological architecture
