Within [[Divine Hardening]], the equivalent of [[Single Event Effects (SEE)]] are not caused by particles, but by **discrete incursions of divine intent**—brief, localized manifestations of authority, symbolism, or will that alter system behavior without sustained exposure.

These are categorized as **[[Divine Single Event Effect (DSEE)]]**.

### [[DSEE-Ω Manifestation Spike]]

**Description:**  
A brief but intense spike of divine presence causes a system to instantiate a **partial metaphysical construct** within its operational layer.

**Mechanism:**  
The system becomes a temporary anchor point for a divine concept, often aligned with the domain of the interfering entity.

**Observed Behavior:**

- HUD overlays display symbolic entities (e.g., eyes, crowns, chains) that interact with targets
- Defensive systems generate constructs resembling shields, wings, or sigils with no physical basis
- AI subsystems begin speaking in non-native linguistic or liturgical structures

**Duration:**  
Instantaneous to several seconds

**Notes:**  
Considered one of the most dangerous DSEE classes. Repeated exposure can lead to **permanent system “conversion”** or partial alignment with a divine domain.
