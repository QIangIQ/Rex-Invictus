**Ekdromoi** (singular *Ekdromos*) are the black-division special operations stormtroopers of the [[Undying Olympian Empire]]. Drawn almost exclusively from [[Legacy]] and [[Neo-demigod]] bloodlines, they are selected, equipped and tasked by the [[Répertoire du Savoir Athénien]] (AtRep) and are rated under [[CODE 24E]], the Empire's threat classification for special suppression forces.

Ekdromoi operate in small, tightly integrated cells. Each operator is a divine combatant in their own right, and each is armored, drugged and armed with equipment that the wider [[UOE]] military will never see issued. They are only ever deployed on missions of strategic weight: deep reconnaissance in denied space, the hunting of high-value targets, and the removal of individuals that line formations cannot touch.

> *"Code 24E individuals are rated for powers equivalent to High-level demigods of the Empire, sir. I barely qualify for High tier 24E, and back then we used to only assign 24E's to special forces."*
> [[Eleni Theodosia]], [[Ch21 - Rex Invictus XIV|Rex Invictus XIV]]

### Overview

Where the Legions of the Empire win through mass, the Ekdromoi win through selection. A single Ekdromoi cell carries more divine output, more electronic warfare capability and more precision firepower than an entire company of the Undying Army, and it does so while remaining invisible to most forms of mortal, divine and machine detection.

Ekdromoi are feared for their coordination as much as their power. Operators fight as a single organism. Fire is distributed across converging lanes, powers are layered over one another to cover each operator's weaknesses, and the cell reforms instantly after being scattered. An enemy that survives the opening seconds of contact usually finds itself fixed under a cone of suppressive fire while one operator repositions for the kill.

Their existence is not publicly acknowledged. Line soldiers speak of them the way civilians speak of [[PSF Enforcer|Enforcers]]: rarely, quietly, and never by name.

---

### Name

The name is taken from the *ekdromoi* ("those who run out") of classical Greek warfare: the youngest and fastest hoplites of a phalanx, detached from the line to sprint down and kill enemy skirmishers before they could escape. The Imperial usage keeps the original sense. The Ekdromoi are the part of the Empire's army that leaves the formation, runs its quarry down, and returns before the line has moved.

---

### Origins

War-college histories of the [[UOE]] trace Ekdromoi doctrine back to the demigod strike teams of the [[Battle of Alaska Hill]] in the 2030s. There, small groups of Greek and Roman demigods operating under [[Percy Jackson]] fought alongside [[Parlamentum mk.I]] in one of the first wars decided by control of the electromagnetic spectrum. Those raids, cued by machine-correlated [[Electronic Intelligence (ELINT)]] and directed against high-value coalition targets, became the template for every later Imperial formation that paired divine combatants with advanced electronic warfare.

Over the following millennia, and especially after [[The Great Expansion]], these teams were formalized. AtRep assumed direct responsibility for their selection and tasking, and their equipment became a showcase for the most advanced output of [[Institute Academy]] and [[Hadrian Harmonics]].

### Selection

Ekdromoi are chosen by AtRep. There is no application process. Candidates are identified through continuous monitoring of the Empire's divine population, and those whose abilities, psychological profile and bloodline meet the required thresholds are pulled from their postings without warning.

> *"There* is *a reason AtRep chose us."*
> Unnamed Ekdromoi operator, [[Ch20 - Rex Invictus XIII|Rex Invictus XIII]]

Selection criteria are understood to include:

- A minimum rating in the High tier of [[CODE 24E]], equivalent to a high-level Imperial demigod
- Stable divine output under suppression, since many operations take place in pantheonically "dead" regions
- Tolerance for long-term chemical enhancement
- Compatibility with complementary operators, favoring siblings and paired bloodlines

Most operators are [[Legacy|legacies]] or [[Neo-demigod|neo-demigods]], second- and third-generation descendants of the Olympians whose powers are weaker than those of a [[Pure Demigod]] but more stable and more easily augmented. Mixed lineages are prized. The Poseidon-Zeus legacy [[Straban]] is typical of the kind of operator the program seeks.

---

### Divine Lineage

AtRep selects Ekdromoi for aggression, independence and raw power. Cells are expected to operate for weeks with no chain of command above them, so the program favors bloodlines that act on their own initiative and fight without hesitation. Nearly a third of all operators descend from the Big Three.

| Lineage | Share | Contribution |
|---|---|---|
| [[Ares]] | 22% | Aggression, weapon mastery, pain tolerance, refusal to break |
| [[Hermes]] | 16% | Speed, independence, improvisation, infiltration |
| [[Zeus]] | 12% | Aerokinesis, electrokinesis, flight, raw output |
| [[Poseidon]] | 11% | Hydrokinesis, earthshaking, endurance |
| [[Hades]] | 7% | Shadow travel, fear, resistance to death |
| [[Hecate]] | 9% | Runic construction, wards, anti-scry, mist |
| [[Athena]] | 7% | Tactical judgment, combat analysis |
| [[Hephaestus]] | 6% | Machine interfacing, field repair, augment compatibility |
| Other lineages | 10% | Varied, often [[Neo-demigod]] mixes |

Mixed Big Three lineages such as Straban's Poseidon-Zeus bloodline are especially prized. No augmented mortals serve as Ekdromoi.

The [[Peltast]] corps is the most common source of candidates, followed by the [[Sciritae]] for Hermes legacies.

### Organization

The standard Ekdromoi formation is a five-operator cell. Cells are built around role coverage, and every operator is expected to be capable of independent action if the rest of the cell is killed. A typical cell includes:

- **Heavy Vanguard.** An operator in full power armor who absorbs fire, breaches positions and carries the cell's heaviest countermeasure packages. [[Merek]], a Roman legacy, filled this role in the Alaska cell.
- **Arcanist Pair.** Two casters working a shared construct, usually siblings whose divine output can be synchronized. They lay runic platforms for descent, reinforce structures, and screen the cell with smoke, chaff and wards. The [[Hecate]] twins [[Ione]] and [[Iane]] filled this role.
- **Elemental Operator.** A combatant with direct control over the surrounding medium, able to propel a dropship through vacuum, conceal the cell in weather, or shield it from hostile elementals. This was [[Straban]].
- **Systems and Air Operator.** A technically augmented operator who interfaces with the cell's dropship and sensors, flies overwatch on a wing rig, and manages the cell's electronic warfare picture. This was [[Neumond]], a [[Hephaestus|Hephaestean]] legacy.

Command within a cell is informal. Operators bicker, trade insults and ignore protocol in transit, then fall into perfect synchronization on contact.

### Doctrine and Tactics

Ekdromoi doctrine assumes that every target is stronger than any single operator. The cell wins by coordination, attrition of the target's defenses, and weight of precision fire.

#### Contact Drill

On receipt of a hostile emitter lock, every helmet in the cell emits a keening threat-warning tone and the cell goes to ground at once. Cover is manufactured if none exists. In Alaska, [[Neumond]] used a thimble-sized thermal anti-armor grenade to blast a van-sized trench in the permafrost within a second of the warning. Operators then:

1. Inject combat stimulants
2. Deploy multi-spectral smoke and ECM to break the enemy's targeting
3. Raise anti-divine countermeasures around the cell
4. Establish overwatch from the air
5. Switch safeties off and open fire in coordinated lanes

#### Converging Fire

The signature Ekdromoi tactic is the disciplined fire cone. All five rifles are laid onto converging lines that deny every angle of movement to the target, and fire is walked across the target's position until its defenses fail under cumulative damage. Operators switch ammunition types mid-engagement to match what they observe of the target's resistances.

#### Dispersal and Reformation

When struck by area effects, Ekdromoi deliberately break cohesion, spread out, and continue firing from new angles. Numbers are treated as the cell's primary advantage, and operators will reposition toward the enemy through broken terrain rather than retreat.

#### Anti-Divine Warfare

Ekdromoi expect to fight gods' children. Their doctrine assumes the enemy can reach into their blood, their air and their minds. Cells therefore operate under the cover of a projected [[Pariah Field]], deploy portable anti-divine suppression spheres when an operator comes under direct elemental attack, and carry munitions built around [[Divine-Metallurgical Binding Impurities (DMBI)]] to defeat regeneration and Achillean hardening.

### Equipment

Ekdromoi are among the few Imperial personnel authorized for black-tier ordnance. Their kit is individually fitted and frequently decades ahead of line issue.

#### Armor

- **Power armor** of layered imperial gold alloy plating over bronze circuitry, with inertial dampers capable of absorbing an unassisted high-altitude landing. Heavy vanguard suits are fitted with integrated life support that keeps the wearer alive after the suit has locked in failure.
- **Helmets** with red lidar oculars, threat-warning receivers and full sensor fusion.
- **Wing rigs** for air operators, deploying in segmented plates with micro-thrusters along the edges for controlled glide and hover.
- **Cloaks** of semi-autonomous material that lags behind its wearer's movements.

#### Arms

- [[VA-RF-762 “MORROW-IX”]] standard infantry battle rifle, the cell's primary weapon
- [[VAS-762R APDS “QVARTUS”]], a long-rod penetrator with anti-reconstitution inclusions, used against armored and regenerating targets
- [[VAS-762R HE “PHOEBUS-3”]] high-explosive rounds
- [[VAS-762R RIP ”NAIIL”]] frangible anti-personnel rounds
- [[IA-762R THMNCLR “PROMETHEUS-IX”]], a micro-thermonuclear round restricted to black-tier deployment
- Miniature thermal anti-armor grenades

#### Electronic Warfare

Every Ekdromoi cell is a self-contained electronic warfare detachment. Standard fits include:

- Imperial IFF
- Anti-Scry and anti-Augur shrouding against divination
- [[Electronic Countermeasures (ECM)]] and [[Electronic Counter-Countermeasures (ECCM)]] suites
- ECOTI emitter targeting
- [[DIFM-CFT Smoke|HH-IFG-73 “LUNA-ASH”]] quick-ECM smoke dispensers, often mounted on wing rigs
- [[Chaff]] and smoke dispensers built into armor

Against a force without Imperial technology, this suite makes a cell effectively unobservable.

#### Mobility

- [[Hexenlaufgerät Erweiterte Thaumaturgische Bewegungsplattform für Runische Vorfeldkonstruktion und Kontinuierliche Glyphenvernetzungseinheit (Tochter des Elysium Serienstandard–Variante IX Beta-Null Mk.CLVI|Hexenlaufgerät]] runic boots, special issue from upper command. They etch load-bearing glyphs into any surface the wearer moves across and allow paired casters to build shared descent platforms in freefall.
- Mag-locking boots for shipboard and zero-gravity operations

#### Sustainment

Ekdromoi are built to operate for long periods without resupply, including in regions where divine power is suppressed.

- **Divine stimulants**, carried in quantities sufficient for a week of continuous operation. A loaded operator does not need to eat, drink, sleep or excrete for months. Stimulant saturation turns an operator's blood a drug-laced neon orange.
- **[[Perfluoro-tert-butyl-perfluorodecalin - Perfluorohexyl-perfluoroisopropyl Ether - Fluorinated Silica Shear-Thickening Oxygenated Suspension Medium|Oxygenated suspension medium]]**, a shear-thickening perfluorocarbon liquid that fills the lungs of heavily armored operators and replaces breathing entirely. Spare barrels are carried in field packs.
- Automated organ stimulation built into the life support of heavy suits
- Personalized regenerative cocktails in the style of [[Regen-EX]]

### Insertion

Ekdromoi deploy by cloaked, armored orbital dropship. These craft are able to thread planetary defense lattices, dodge satellites and hold position invisibly in the upper atmosphere while the cell drops. Where the [[Aquila-Pattern Assault Lander]] is designed to deliver line stormtroopers into a contested zone by brute force, Ekdromoi craft are designed never to be seen at all.

Cells descend without parachutes. Casters ride runic platforms down, elemental operators walk on the air, air operators glide on their wing rigs, and the heavy vanguard simply falls and lets the suit absorb the impact.

### Known Deployments

#### Gaia Reconnaissance

*Main articles: [[Ch20 - Rex Invictus XIII|Rex Invictus XIII]], [[Ch21 - Rex Invictus XIV|Rex Invictus XIV]]*

A five-operator cell consisting of [[Merek]], [[Ione]], [[Iane]], [[Straban]] and [[Neumond]] was dispatched by AtRep from Mars to [[Gaia]] to reconnoiter [[Alaska]] and two other sites. The cell inserted onto the steppe, crossed the Labyrinth in twelve hours, and emerged onto the Alaskan tundra, the "land beyond the gods" where Imperial records place the construction of the first AtRep.

On exiting, the cell made contact with [[Percy Jackson]] and [[Eleni Theodosia]]. [[Parlamentum mk.I|Minerva]] identified the cell by its Imperial IFF, anti-Scry, anti-Augur and ECM, and classified it as a [[CODE 24E]] threat.

The engagement showed Ekdromoi doctrine at full strength. The cell countered Percy's hydrokinesis with an anti-divine sphere, blocked Eleni's powers under a [[Pariah Field]], and put QVARTUS rounds through Percy's Styx-hardened hand and chest. Percy was driven to his knees and Eleni was shot through the side of the head. Both demigods were only able to win by collapsing the terrain and the air itself onto the scattered operators.

All five Ekdromoi survived in critical condition. Percy stabilized them using their own suspension medium and injectors, then carried them through the Labyrinth to [[Camp Half-Blood]] as prisoners.
