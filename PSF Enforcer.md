**PSF Enforcers** constitute the first operational rank within the Planetary Security Forces authorized to directly interface with the [Grids](Grids.md) and request support from planetary and orbital security assets. They represent the transition between conventional planetary security and the PSF's specialized intervention formations.

While Heavy Security is intended to physically control dangerous situations on the ground, Enforcers are trained to **identify, isolate, and eliminate high-value threats** before they can develop into larger security crises. Their authority allows them to draw upon resources that ordinary PSF personnel cannot access, including orbital surveillance, precision fires, electronic warfare, and specialized rapid-response assets.

Enforcers are consequently deployed against **high-profile assassination attempts, organized rebel movements, terrorist organizations, hostile infiltrators, rogue supernatural entities, and limited divine threats**. 
### Primary Duties

- High-profile anti-assassination operations
- Counter-terrorism
- Counter-insurgency and anti-rebel operations
- Hunting high-value criminal and insurgent targets
- Protection of senior governmental and PSF personnel
- Counter-infiltration operations
- Counter-sabotage
- Hostage rescue
- High-risk apprehension of dangerous individuals
- Investigation and neutralization of organized supernatural threats
- Limited anti-divine operations
- Detection and tracking of hostile supernatural entities
- Coordination of orbital surveillance
- Coordination of precision orbital fire support
- Battlefield intelligence and target designation
- Electronic-warfare coordination
- Establishment of temporary exclusion zones
- Emergency coordination between PSF and planetary military forces
- Command of smaller specialized PSF detachments during emergencies

### Planetary Grid Authority

The defining characteristic of an Enforcer is their authorization to interface with the **Planetary Grid**.

The Grid consists of the planetary network of security satellites, orbital platforms, surveillance systems, communications infrastructure, electronic-warfare assets, and other strategic systems available to planetary authorities.

An Enforcer does not necessarily possess unrestricted command over these systems. Instead, their authorization permits them to **request and coordinate specific Grid services** within their authority.

Common requests include:

- Orbital reconnaissance
- Satellite tracking
- High-resolution orbital surveillance
- Infrared observation
- Multispectral target identification
- IR illumination
- Encrypted communications
- Localized communications jamming
- Electronic countermeasures
- Electronic intelligence collection
- Signals intelligence support
- Orbital insertion
- Rapid deployment of specialized PSF personnel
- Orbital sniper deployment
- Precision orbital strikes
- Emergency extraction
- Target designation
- Atmospheric and environmental monitoring

This gives an Enforcer an enormous advantage over conventional PSF personnel.

A Heavy Security officer might need to physically pursue an armed terrorist through a city.

An Enforcer can potentially identify the target, have the Planetary Grid track their vehicle through buildings and weather, jam their communications, illuminate their position with an orbital IR system, and coordinate an interception team before the target realizes they have been located.

---

### Rules of Grid Employment

Grid authority is deliberately structured around **request and authorization**, rather than giving every Enforcer unrestricted access to planetary weapons.

An Enforcer can generally authorize or request routine intelligence and surveillance support without additional approval.

More destructive capabilities require progressively greater authorization.

For example:

|Grid Support|Typical Enforcer Authority|
|---|---|
|Satellite surveillance|Direct request|
|Satellite tracking|Direct request|
|Infrared observation|Direct request|
|IR illumination|Direct request|
|Secure communications|Direct request|
|Localized jamming|Direct request|
|Electronic surveillance|Direct request|
|Orbital sniper|Authorized request|
|Orbital insertion|Authorized request|
|Specialized strike team|Authorized request|
|Precision orbital strike|Restricted / mission-dependent|
|Strategic orbital weapons|Outside normal Enforcer authority|
|Planetary-scale weapons|Prohibited|

The exact authority can also depend upon the Enforcer's mission.

An Enforcer investigating a terrorist cell might be permitted to employ localized jamming and orbital surveillance independently, while an Enforcer conducting a counter-divine operation may require authorization from a higher PSF command authority before using destructive Grid assets.

The intent is to prevent Enforcers from becoming **walking authorization codes for orbital bombardment**.

### Anti-Assassination Operations

One of the Enforcer's most common assignments is the protection of individuals whose deaths could have significant political, military, economic, or supernatural consequences.

These assignments can involve:

- Government ministers
- Senior PSF officers
- Military commanders
- Diplomats
- Scientists
- Religious figures
- Important corporate executives
- Divine representatives
- High-value witnesses
- Political leaders

Rather than simply acting as bodyguards, Enforcers establish a **layered security environment** around the target.

They may monitor surrounding communications, identify suspicious movement, track vehicles entering the area, inspect nearby buildings through orbital surveillance, and coordinate PSF personnel across several kilometers.

The objective is to detect an assassination attempt **before the attacker reaches the target**.

If an attack does occur, the Enforcer becomes the tactical coordinator, directing local security forces while using the Planetary Grid to locate and isolate the perpetrators.

---

### Counter-Terrorism

Enforcers are the PSF's principal response to terrorist organizations that exceed the capabilities of ordinary security formations.

They are particularly valuable against groups possessing:

- Military-grade weapons
- Advanced communications equipment
- Magical capabilities
- Supernatural creatures
- Explosives
- Hostages
- Access to government infrastructure
- Foreign support
- Divine or pantheonic affiliations

Their doctrine emphasizes **intelligence-driven intervention** rather than mass deployment.

A typical operation might involve an Enforcer identifying a terrorist cell, using orbital surveillance to map its compound, requesting localized communications disruption, coordinating a Heavy Security cordon, and then directing a specialized assault team against the leadership.

The Enforcer is therefore less a traditional frontline soldier and more a **tactical node connecting ground forces to the entire planetary security architecture**.

---

### Anti-Rebel Operations

Enforcers are also employed against organized rebellions and insurgencies.

They are generally not deployed to fight entire armies. Instead, they target the organizational structure supporting the rebellion.

Typical targets include:

- Rebel commanders
- Intelligence officers
- Communications hubs
- Weapons depots
- Safehouses
- Financial infrastructure
- Propaganda facilities
- Transportation networks
- Magical infrastructure
- Foreign supply networks

The Planetary Grid allows Enforcers to maintain persistent surveillance over suspected rebel areas while conventional PSF forces conduct arrests and security operations.

This makes them particularly useful during the early stages of an uprising, when destroying the organization's leadership may prevent a localized rebellion from becoming a planetary security crisis.

---

### Limited Anti-Divine Operations

The Enforcer is the **lowest conventional PSF rank expected to possess any meaningful capability against divine entities**.

This does not mean that an Enforcer is individually capable of defeating a god.

They are not.

Instead, their advantage comes from coordination.

An Enforcer may identify a divine entity, determine its approximate capabilities and location, establish a security perimeter, coordinate specialized personnel, and employ the Planetary Grid to provide surveillance, electronic disruption, illumination, orbital precision fire, or emergency extraction.

This is particularly useful against **minor divine entities, avatars, divine servants, weakened gods, rogue demigods, and supernatural beings operating beneath full divine power**.

Against a major deity operating at full strength, an Enforcer's role changes from **neutralization** to **containment and coordination**.

They may be responsible for keeping the entity occupied, evacuating civilians, maintaining surveillance, requesting appropriate divine or military assistance, and preventing collateral damage until a higher authority arrives.

### Tactical Interface

The most important piece of equipment carried by an Enforcer is arguably not a weapon.

It is their **Planetary Grid terminal**.

The terminal provides an authenticated interface through which the Enforcer can communicate with the wider planetary security network.

Through it, an Enforcer can potentially see:

- Orbital satellite feeds
- Nearby PSF units
- Military transponder locations
- Aircraft
- Ground vehicles
- Registered communications networks
- Sensor detections
- Thermal signatures
- Electronic activity
- Restricted geographic information
- Known threat databases

The interface allows the Enforcer to build a constantly changing tactical picture around themselves.

Their helmet display can therefore become something approaching a **localized command center**.

---

### Orbital Sniper Support

One of the more feared capabilities available to an Enforcer is the ability to request **orbital sniper support**.

Orbital sniper platforms do not necessarily fire enormous weapons. Their purpose is precision.

Once a target has been positively identified and the appropriate authorization obtained, an orbital platform can provide a highly accurate long-range shot against a designated target.

This allows an Enforcer to engage targets that are:

- Too distant to reach
- Hidden inside fortified positions
- Attempting to flee
- Protected by hostile personnel
- Holding a strategic position
- Too dangerous to approach conventionally

---

### Orbital Insertion

Enforcers may also request orbital insertion when conventional transportation is too slow or dangerous.

A specialized PSF insertion team can be deployed directly into an operational area, allowing an Enforcer to rapidly reinforce a developing crisis.

This capability is particularly valuable when:

- A high-value target is actively fleeing
- A terrorist attack is underway
- A remote facility has been compromised
- A hostage situation is developing
- Conventional transportation routes are blocked
- A supernatural incident has isolated an area
- Multiple locations are simultaneously under attack

Enforcers can therefore act as the **ground-level controller for rapidly deployed PSF assets**.

---

### Localized Electronic Warfare

Enforcers possess considerably greater electronic-warfare authority than ordinary PSF personnel.

They can request localized jamming against hostile communications, disrupt unauthorized transmissions, interfere with remote detonators, and isolate hostile networks from the wider communications infrastructure.

The emphasis is deliberately **localized**.

Planetary Security does not want individual Enforcers casually shutting down communications across an entire city.

Their electronic-warfare capabilities are consequently intended to create temporary tactical advantages while minimizing disruption to civilian infrastructure.
