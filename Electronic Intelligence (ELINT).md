[[Electronic Intelligence (ELINT)]] is a subcategory of [[Signals Intelligence (SIGINT)]] involving the interception, recording, analysis, and exploitation of **non-communication electromagnetic emissions**, primarily from electronic systems such as [[Radar]], [[Weapons Guidance Systems]], [[Navigation Systems]], and [[Electronic Warfare Systems]], for the purpose of determining their [[Function]], [[Capabilities]], [[Location]], [[Operational Status]], and [[Technical Characteristics]].

---

## Core Distinction

[[Electronic Intelligence (ELINT)]] specifically excludes [[Communications Intelligence (COMINT)]].

- [[Electronic Intelligence (ELINT)]] → analyzes [[Non-Communication Emitters]]
    
- [[COMINT]] → analyzes [[Communication Signals]]
    

Both fall under [[Signals Intelligence (SIGINT)]].

## Primary Targets of [[Electronic Intelligence (ELINT)]]

### 1. [[Radar Systems]]

Examples of extracted parameters:

- [[Frequency]]
    
- [[Pulse Repetition Frequency (PRF)]]
    
- [[Pulse Width]]
    
- [[Scan Pattern]]
    
- [[Beam Width]]
    
- [[Transmit Power]]
    
- [[Modulation Type]]
    

These allow determination of:

- [[Radar Type]]
    
- [[Detection Range]]
    
- [[Tracking Capability]]
    
- [[Fire Control Capability]]
    

---

### 2. [[Weapons Guidance Systems]]

Includes emissions from:

- [[Surface-to-Air Missile (SAM) Guidance Radar]]
    
- [[Air-to-Air Missile (AAM) Seekers]]
    
- [[Fire Control Radar (FCR)]]
    

Purpose:

- Identify [[Weapon System Type]]
    
- Detect [[Target Lock Events]]
    
- Provide [[Threat Warning]]
    

---

### 3. [[Electronic Warfare Systems]]

Includes:

- [[Jammers]]
    
- [[Electronic Countermeasure (ECM) Pods]]
    
- [[Electronic Attack Systems]]
    

Purpose:

- Identify [[Jammer Type]]
    
- Determine [[Jamming Technique]]
    
- Enable [[Electronic Counter-Countermeasures (ECCM)]]

## Core Processes in [[Electronic Intelligence (ELINT)]]

### [[Interception]]

Detection of electromagnetic emissions using:

- [[Radar Warning Receiver (RWR)]]
    
- [[Electronic Support Measures (ESM)]]
    
- [[Wideband Receivers]]
    

---

### [[Signal Analysis]]

Extraction of signal parameters:

- [[Frequency Analysis]]
    
- [[Time Domain Analysis]]
    
- [[Spectral Analysis]]
    
- [[Pulse Analysis]]
    

---

### [[Emitter Identification]]

Matching signal to known emitter using:

- [[Emitter Libraries]]
    
- [[Signal Fingerprinting]]
    
- [[Pattern Recognition]]
    

Result:

- Identification of specific [[Radar System]]
    
- Identification of specific [[Weapon System]]
    

---

### [[Direction Finding (DF)]]

Determines direction of emitter using:

- [[Amplitude Comparison]]
    
- [[Phase Interferometry]]
    
- [[Time Difference of Arrival (TDOA)]]
    

Result:

- [[Bearing]] to emitter
    

---

### [[Geolocation]]

Determines exact position using:

- [[Triangulation]]
    
- [[Multilateration]]
    
- [[Time Difference of Arrival (TDOA)]]
    

Result:

- [[Emitter Coordinates]]

## Relationship to [[Electronic Warfare (EW)]]

[[Electronic Warfare (EW)]] has three components:

|Component|Function|
|---|---|
|[[Electronic Support (ES)]]|Detection and analysis of emitters ([[Electronic Intelligence (ELINT)]] occurs here)|
|[[Electronic Attack (EA)]]|Jamming and deception|
|[[Electronic Protection (EP)]]|Protection against jamming|

[[Electronic Intelligence (ELINT)]] is a primary function within [[Electronic Support (ES)]].

---

Within the war-doctrine of the [[UOE]], [[Electronic Intelligence (ELINT)]] is defined as:

> The systematic interception, collection, analysis, and exploitation of non-communications electromagnetic emissions from hostile, neutral, or unknown systems for the purpose of identifying, locating, characterizing, and countering emitters within the [[Electromagnetic Spectrum (EMS)]].

### Core Functional Elements

- Detection of [[Radar Emissions]]
    
- Measurement of [[Radio Frequency (RF)]] parameters
    
- Analysis of [[Pulse Repetition Frequency (PRF)]]
    
- Measurement of [[Pulse Width (PW)]]
    
- Identification of [[Emitter Signature]]
    
- Determination of [[Order of Battle (EOB)]]
    
- Geolocation via [[Direction Finding (DF)]]
    
- Signal exploitation under [[Signals Intelligence (SIGINT)]]
    
- Operational integration into [[Electronic Warfare (EW)]]
    

ELINT does **not** focus on voice or message content. It focuses on emitter physics: waveform structure, modulation patterns, scan types, frequency agility, and timing characteristics. It is the science of understanding a system purely by how it radiates.
