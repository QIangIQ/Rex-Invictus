## Description

The **Aquila-Pattern Assault Lander** is a heavily armored orbital dropship designed to deliver **[[UOE Stormtroopers]]** directly into contested zones under full-spectrum hostile conditions. Its architecture emphasizes:

- **Extreme survivability under orbital re-entry and anti-ship fire**
    
- **High-mass kinetic stability during descent**
    
- **Localized dominance during landing window**
    

The hull is constructed as a **layered divine-composite carapace**, integrating:

- Adamantine-ceramite lattice
    
- Aegis-reactive plating
    
- Sub-dermal energy dissipation mesh
    

The craft is powered by **[[Hyperium]] combustion-reactive drives**, enabling disproportionate thrust output relative to fuel volume.
