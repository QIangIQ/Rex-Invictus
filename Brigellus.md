[[Legio XIII Lupae Fridulus]]

Commander: [[Brigellus]]  
Stationed: [[Luna Secundus]], Southern Trench Network

History:  
Born from the failure after the [[Yamamoto Chokepoint Relay]] breach, [[Legio XIII Lupae Fridulus]] earned infamy for warcrime-level sabotage against besieging fleets. Originally condemned to disbandment, their redemptive operations during the [[Defensive Consolidation of the Tears of Lysandra]] earned them full reinstatement.
