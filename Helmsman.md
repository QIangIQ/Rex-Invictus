## Definition

A **[[Helmsman]]** is a permanently integrated, biologically derived control core created from direct descendants of [[Poseidon]], surgically interred within a naval vessel to provide **instantaneous, system-wide control authority** independent of conventional control limitations.

All [[Helmsman]] units are **pre-emptively lobotomized**, reducing higher cognition and personality to a minimal baseline. This allows the subject to function as a **biological operating system**, rather than an autonomous pilot.

This role is standard across all [[UOE Navy]] vessels, from **500m line ships** to **20km+ capital hulls**, where traditional control systems are vulnerable to:

- [[Electronic Warfare (EW)]]
- [[Divine Interference]]
- [[Battle Damage]]
- [[Relativistic Delay]]

---

## Selection Criteria

Eligible candidates must meet strict requirements:

- Direct descent from [[Poseidon]]
- Strong innate affinity for:
    - [[Hydrodynamic Control]]
    - [[Fluid-State Perception]]
- High baseline tolerance for:
    - Neural rewriting
    - [[Noospheric Stress]]

Candidates are selected prior to cognitive reduction and are considered **non-recoverable assets** upon induction.

---

## Cognitive Reduction

Prior to integration, subjects undergo **complete lobotomization and neural restructuring**.

### Resulting State

- Removal of:
    - Personality
    - Memory continuity
    - Independent decision-making
- Retention of:
    - Reflexive neural response
    - Domain-linked instinctive processing
- Conversion into:
    - A **low-level cognition substrate** capable of sustaining system-wide control

The resulting entity is not considered a person, but a **biological control medium**.

---

## Integration Process

The transformation into a [[Helmsman]] is irreversible.

### Procedure

- Full-body amputation of non-essential structures
- Expansion and exposure of the neural spine
- Direct connection to:
    - [[Ship Core Systems]]
    - [[Navigation Matrix]]
    - [[Structural Control Grid]]
- Encapsulation within a:
    - Sealed [[Helmsman Sarcophagus]]

---

## Interface Requirements

A [[Helmsman]] cannot operate independently and requires external systems to provide direction.

### Control Interface

- Linked to:
    - [[Command Throne Systems]]
    - [[Bridge Neural Interfaces]]
    - [[AI Mediation Layers]] (optional)
- Commands are:
    - Translated into neural patterns
    - Executed instantly through the Helmsman substrate

Without a valid interface:

- The Helmsman maintains:
    - Idle stabilization
    - Passive system equilibrium
- No active maneuvering or combat action is performed

---

## Operational Function

A [[Helmsman]] does not interpret commands—it **executes state changes across the vessel directly**.

Through inherited linkage to [[Poseidon]]’s domain:

- The ship is treated as a **continuous medium**, not discrete components
- Control occurs:
    - Simultaneously across all systems
    - Without signal propagation delay

This enables:

- Immediate adjustment of:
    - Orientation
    - Velocity vectors
    - Internal system states

    - [[Core Compromise]]
