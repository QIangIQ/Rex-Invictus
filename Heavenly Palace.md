_“If one was to name the greatest achievement of the martial arts world, the first would be the emergence of the [[Internal Energy Actuator (Qi-Virus)]]"_

_– Excerpt from ‘Basic Martial Arts History’ at the [[Central Archive of the Martial Arts Orthodox Alliance]], B-548n Star System._

Loosely allied with the [[Amaterasu Dominion]], Neutral against [[Greek Pantheon]]

She had seen a master of external techniques, born with a silver spoon, who had covered his entire body with battleship-grade adamantite alloy, deflecting military-grade beam weapons.

She had witnessed a madman from an evil sect who had inserted polonium into his dantian, claiming it to be the universe’s ultimate poison technique, only to melt from internal radiation exposure when his dantian ruptured.

She had even seen a Shaolin monk who had implanted an ion cannon into his severed arm, boasting about his Myriad Steps Divine Fist, and then getting beaten up like a dog and dragged away for violating the Murim Weapons Treaty.



“This is truly lamentable. To think that third-rate fools with no understanding of martial arts dare to discuss techniques while relying on bizarre contraptions.”

“How dare you lowlifes, who haven’t even mastered the basics of martial arts, attempt to lay hands on my esteemed self?”

“Urgh… A natural dares to lay hands on a martial artist of the Heavenly Demon Divine Sect? Whatever trick you used, you’re making a grave mistake today, young hero.”
