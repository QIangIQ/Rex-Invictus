## Known or Suspected Composition 

Independent macro-corporate intelligence divisions, including orbital biosurveillance firms and rogue [[Augurs]], have narrowed the formulation to a multi-component hybrid construct combining engineered extremophile organisms with catalytic organometallic phases.

While no confirmed sample has survived intact, consensus analysis suggests a composite of the following:

### 1. Engineered Hyperthermophilic Mycophage Consortium

Likely based on modified strains similar to:

- _Thermococcus gorgonii_-class archaea
    
- Anaerobic sulfur-metabolizing extremophiles
    
- Synthetic collagen-digesting fungal vectors

---

### 2. Organometallic Catalytic Phase

Macrocorp modeling strongly suggests embedded catalytic particulates including:

- Hafnium(IV) perfluoroalkoxide complexes
    
- Rhenium(VII) oxo-fluoride clusters
    
- Stygiopheride-bound actinide microphases

---

### 3. Volatile Hydrocarbon Amplification Byproduct

Metabolic pathways appear tuned to produce:

- Fluorinated hydrocarbon vapors
    
- Hydrogen-rich aerosol clouds
    
- Dense combustible sludge precursors
