PSF Light Cavalry constitutes the threshold rank between planetary security work and the wider imperial military apparatus. Where [[PSF Subject Guard|Subject Guards]] represent the ceiling of what a _fixed_ PSF asset can be — maximal survivability, anti-divine capability, deep [[Planetary Grids|Grid]] integration, all of it built to hold a single city or subject against catastrophic escalation — Light Cavalry exists because the UOE cannot afford to let its best conventional personnel remain fixed. A Subject Guard is a wall. A Light Cavalryman is the answer to the question the wall cannot answer: _what happens when the threat is not here, but is about to be, three systems over, and no one has fifty years to build another wall in time to stop it._

Promotion into the Cavalry track is the point at which a PSF career stops being purely planetary. It is the first rank whose jurisdiction routinely crosses atmosphere, and the first rank explicitly understood — by the personnel themselves, by their commanders, and by the bureaucratic ledgers that track such things — as a farm system for the officer corps and, eventually, for whatever a given individual proves capable of becoming beneath the weight of [[PSF Divinitas|Divinitas]]. Nobody is promoted into Light Cavalry to stay there. They are promoted into it to be tested at a scope no static posting can replicate, with the explicit understanding that the survivors are being groomed — for Heavy Cavalry, for the Chief Sergeant tracks, for a commission, or for something the UOE does not put on paper.

This is also, quietly, a manpower economy. With the overwhelming bulk of the Empire's trained violence permanently mortgaged to the Tartarus theater — where the count of the dead is measured in the tens of millions per year and the count of the deployed in the tens of millions more — Realspace worlds are policed by whatever competence can be spared. Light Cavalry is the mechanism by which that scarcity gets stretched across an entire star system: a small number of extremely capable, extremely mobile operators substituting for the standing garrisons a healthier empire would simply station in place.

## Doctrine

Where a Subject Guard's defining trait is depth — the ability to stand and absorb what would kill anything else — a Light Cavalryman's defining trait is **reach**. They are built and equipped for rapid orbital-to-surface insertion, sustained operational tempo across multiple engagements without resupply, and disengagement discipline: the ability to break contact and redeploy before a fight calcifies into the kind of grinding, attritional confrontation that Subject Guards are built to win and Cavalry is not. A Subject Guard is sent to contain something. A Light Cavalry detachment is sent to _arrive before containment becomes necessary_, and to be gone again — planetside, off-world, or three engagements deep into a pursuit — before the enemy's advantage has time to mature.

Their operational theater is correspondingly wider than any rank beneath them. A Subject Guard's jurisdiction is a city, a subject, a facility. A Light Cavalry detachment's jurisdiction is a system: the orbital lanes, the transit stations, the border colonies, the handful of worlds too minor to warrant a permanent Subject Guard presence but too strategically inconvenient to leave entirely unwatched. This is the first rank at which "extraplanetary" stops being an exceptional circumstance and starts being simply part of the job description.

## Primary Duties

- Suppression of super-heavy riots and mass civil unrest that exceed Heavy Security's holding capacity
- Rapid interdiction of rogue or hostile Private Military Companies, including [[Triumvirate Holdings|Triumvirate]]-contracted formations operating outside their charter
- Minor anti-extraplanetary assassination and elimination taskings — targets who have fled, relocated, or operate across orbital or interplanetary boundaries beyond a Subject Guard's fixed jurisdiction
- Orbital-to-surface quick reaction deployment against threats a standing garrison cannot reach in time
- Interception and boarding actions against hostile or fleeing vessels within a system's transit lanes
- Mobile crowd fragmentation and containment-line enforcement in advance of Heavy Security reinforcement
- High-speed pursuit across planetary, orbital, and cislunar boundaries
- Forward screening and denial operations ahead of heavier PSF or military elements
- Perimeter collapse prevention during large-scale civil disturbances on minor or unstable worlds
- Extraction of PSF, diplomatic, or allied personnel from destabilized colonies and outposts
- Denial of key terrain — orbital platforms, transit hubs, border checkpoints — during fast-escalating incidents
- Interception of smuggling, trafficking, or artifact-recovery convoys tied to hostile organizations or rival pantheonic interests
- Rapid redeployment between worlds within a single system, or between adjacent systems where Grid infrastructure permits
- Screening and softening operations preceding commitment of Heavy Cavalry, Subject Guard, or Enforcer elements
- Suppression of armed insurrectionist or separatist cells before they entrench on a colony world
- Security of Hyperium logistics corridors, orbital shipping lanes, and other infrastructure critical to a system's supply chain
- Snatch-and-hold operations against high-value fleeing targets, including minor rogue Legacies without full divine backing
- Coordination with local garrison, militia, and colonial administration during large-scale incidents
- De-escalation-through-presence in volatile but not yet supernatural crowds
- Rapid triage handoff to Subject Guard or Enforcer elements the moment an incident reveals supernatural, divine, or anti-divine dimensions beyond Cavalry's mandate
- Serving as the training and proving ground through which candidates are evaluated for Heavy Cavalry, the Chief Sergeant tracks, and eventual commission

## Doctrine & Limitations

Light Cavalry defers immediately and without ego the moment an incident is confirmed to involve serious supernatural, divine, or anti-divine dimensions. This is not a matter of pride but of design: their loadout, training, and unit cohesion are built around mobility, tempo, and conventional-to-lightly-enhanced hostile action, not the sustained anti-divine attrition a Subject Guard is armored and trained to survive. A Light Cavalry detachment that finds itself facing a genuine divine servant, a rampaging Legacy at full power, or an uncontained supernatural entity is doctrinally required to hold, screen, and call it in — not to engage past its mandate. Cavalry that ignores this distinction accounts for a disproportionate share of the rank's casualties.

Because their jurisdiction routinely crosses into orbital and interplanetary space, Light Cavalry detachments carry lighter, more modular equipment than Subject Guards — optimized for insertion speed and multi-engagement endurance rather than raw survivability against anti-divine threats. Grid integration remains present but is tuned for tracking, pursuit, and interdiction across a wider and thinner sensor net, rather than the dense, saturating coverage a Subject Guard enjoys within a single city.

Chain of command places Light Cavalry detachments answerable to system-level PSF command rather than a single city or facility administration, reflecting their broader remit. It is common for a Light Cavalry officer to report to, or be evaluated by, the same command structure that will eventually decide their fitness for [[PSF Heavy Cavalry|Heavy Cavalry]] or a commissioned track.
