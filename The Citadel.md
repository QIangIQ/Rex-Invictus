[[The Citadel]] is the principal [[Demigod]] and [[Legacy]] training complex of the [[UOE]]. It functions as a **system-scale military academy, strategic fortress, and doctrinal development complex** designed to produce and maintain the empire’s divine-blooded command corps.

The installation is a **Dyson-class containment megastructure** spanning an enormous spherical shell that encloses multiple inhabited worlds and training environments within a sealed stellar interior. Unlike classical Dyson constructs, the sphere does not merely harvest stellar energy. It serves as a **contained training ecosystem** where entire planetary surfaces function as combat laboratories.

Administrative command is maintained by [[Chiron]], who serves as the primary operational director of demigod education. Strategic oversight is shared with a reduced divine fragment of [[Dionysus]], retained as a behavioral authority and institutional continuity anchor.

# Structural Form

## [[Dyson Sphere]]

[[The Citadel]] forms a **3 AU radius spherical megastructure** enclosing several planetary bodies, orbital training zones, and artificial combat theaters.

Key parameters:

|Parameter|Specification|
|---|---|
|Radius|3 AU|
|Diameter|6 AU|
|Interior volume|~1.13×10³⁵ m³|
|Interior environment|Fully oxygenated|
|Primary purpose|Demigod military training|

The sphere’s structure contains massive atmospheric retention systems that allow breathable gas to fill the entire interior volume. Multiple stellar-radiation filtration layers prevent atmospheric ionization.

Core structural layers include:

- [[Atmospheric Containment Grid (ACG)]]
    
- [[Thermal Radiative Sink Mesh (TRSM)]]
    
- [[Solar Flux Regulator Network (SFRN)]]
    
- [[Divine-Metallurgical Binding Impurities (DMBI)]]
    
- [[Celestial Bronze Structural Mesh]]
    

These materials allow the shell to tolerate the immense mechanical stresses generated by a sealed atmosphere on astronomical scales.

Primary shell reinforcement uses a hybrid lattice composed of divine and ultra-advanced materials.

Examples:

- [[DMBI-A7 Structural Alloy]]
    
- [[CB-12 Celestial Bronze Laminate]]
    
- [[AEG-TRX Hyperlattice Mesh]]
    
- [[GR-9 Vacuum Shear Composite]]
    

The outer hull contains trillions of modular panels that can individually seal breaches or redistribute structural loads.

Thermal regulation is handled by distributed radiative towers embedded across the sphere’s exterior:

- [[TRS-441 Heat Radiator Towers]]
    
- [[SFR-98 Solar Flux Dissipators]]
    

These prevent internal overheating from stellar radiation and planetary activity.

The interior of [[The Citadel]] is maintained as a stable biosphere environment.

Primary control systems include:

- [[ACG-L7 Atmospheric Stabilizers]]
    
- [[RF-220 Radiation Filters]]
    
- [[CLM-80 Planetary Climate Modulators]]
    
- [[GR-ARC Artificial Gravity Regulators]]
    

Atmospheric density varies slightly between planetary environments but remains breathable throughout the interior.

This configuration allows:

- orbital habitats
    
- planetary-scale battlefields
    
- controlled wilderness regions

# Strategic Role

Within the doctrine of the [[UOE]], [[The Citadel]] serves as:

- the primary training ground for divine-blooded military personnel
    
- the central doctrinal development center for demigod warfare
    
- a strategic reserve fortress for the empire
    
- the institutional successor to [[Camp Half-Blood]]
