**Parzival (symbol: Pz)** is the standard unit of magical energy used in modern thaumaturgical systems. It is named after **[[Isaac Parzival]]**, a Hecate-aligned demigoddess active in the late 21st century (c. 2077), whose work formalized quantitative mana theory and established cross-domain magical standardization.
Consumption: Parzival Seconds: Pz/S


---

### **Definition**

One **Parzival (1 Pz)** is defined as the amount of magical energy required to:

- Lift a mass of **10 kilogram**
    
- Under **standard planetary gravity (15.00 m/s²)**
    
- Across a displacement of **10 meters** 

- By a standard divine being of 100 years of age and neutral divine affinity

- Or its equivalent in joules (1500)
    
- Without directional bias (vertical or horizontal equivalent under magical modeling)
    

---

### **Spell Power Application**

The **Parzival** is not only a unit of energy but also a **standardized measure of spell output**:

- Spell cost (Pz) = total mana required to perform a defined physical or metaphysical effect
    
- Spell power (Pz/s or total Pz) determines:
    
    - magnitude of effect
        
    - duration stability
        
    - resistance against opposing forces
