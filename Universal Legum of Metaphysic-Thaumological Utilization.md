**I. Lex Fontis (Source of Power)**  
Magic must arise from a defined origin and be sustained by a source, a minimium defined as more than 1μPz/S [Parzival](Parzival.md), anthing below this is classified as non-divine/mortal divine prowess

**II. Lex Structurae (System Order)**  
Magic must exist either within ordered rules or as inherently unstable behavior.

**III. Lex Limitationis (Constraint and Cost)**  
Magic must require cost, effort, or limitation in its use.

**IV. Lex Persistens Ontologica (Persistence of Effect)**  
Magical effects must either endure independently or cease without support.

**V. Lex Interactionis (Resistance and Counteraction)**  
Magic must be capable of being resisted, opposed, or negated, unless the difference in [Divine Power](Divine%20Power.md) is far greater or a [[Direct Counter-Affinity (DCA)]] is present.

**VI. Lex Accessus (Eligibility and Distribution)**  
Magic is and always shall be distributed unequally or conditionally among users. This is a direct divine limitation of Fate, Fortune, and Primordial Frivolity ([Chaos](Chaos.md))

**VII. Lex Methodi (Means of Invocation)**  
Magic must be enacted through a defined method or process, this process must be repeatable or mimicable by individuals with a combination of equal or greater knowledge, affinity, and [Divine Power](Divine%20Power.md).

**VIII. Lex Nexus (Connection and Sympathy)**  
Magic must operate through connections, links, or representations. All types of [Divine Manifestations](Divine%20Manifestations) that do not fall under this category is classified as [[Obscurus Magicae]] and banned by the [Répertoire du Savoir Athénien](Répertoire%20du%20Savoir%20Athénien.md) or reserved for [[UOE Black Division]] members and higher. Any violation of this will result in immedate Re-Verdictization to [CODE 78C](CODE%2078C.md), and immediate [CODE 44](CODE%2044.md) 

**IX. Lex Correspondentiae (Pattern Alignment)**  
Magic must align with underlying patterns or symbolic structures. This law is meant to recapture all grey fringe-cases of ULMTU-VIII.

**X. Lex Aequilibrii (Systemic Balance)**  
Magic must redistribute existing forces rather than create or destroy without limit, unless being is of [Primordial](Primordial.md) strength or higher (If doubtful, consult top 50 rankings of the [UOE Unified Alltime Rankings](UOE%20Unified%20Alltime%20Rankings.md)).

**XI. Lex Consequentiae (Backlash and Risk)**  
Magic must carry the possibility of failure or unintended effect due to improper handling, strength, emotional state, physical state, divine strength, divine affinity, and materiae.

**XII. Lex Reversibilitatis (Reversal of Effect)**  
Magic must be capable of being undone or altered by individuals with a combination of far greater knowledge, affinity, and [Divine Power](Divine%20Power.md).

**XIII. Lex Impermanentiae (Temporal Instability)**  
Magic must tend toward decay or reversion over time, unless they are in compliance wth ULMTU-I.

**XIV. Lex Voluntatis (Will and Consent)**  
Magic must interact with the will or resistance of affected entities, all non-interacting manifestational abilities are to be classified as [[Exusai Magicae]] and banned under the [Répertoire du Savoir Athénien](Répertoire%20du%20Savoir%20Athénien.md) or under similar circumstances as ULMTU-VIII.

**XV. Lex Reciprocitatis Moralis (Moral Feedback)**  
Magic must return consequences proportional to its use.

**XVI. Lex Interdictorum (Prohibited Acts)**  
Magic cannot be perfomed that violates either: [[ULotE-III]] or greater than 100 MegaParzival-Seconds unless granted permission by direct acknowledgement from [Répertoire du Savoir Athénien](Répertoire%20du%20Savoir%20Athénien.md). Any violation of this will result in identical punishment as ULMTU-VIII with a Verdict of [CODE 78D](CODE%2078D.md) instead.

**XVII. Lex Praevalentiae (Prevalence)**  
Magic must exist at a defined level of rarity or commonality, not to be confused with ULMTU-VI, instead determining quality of the Magic itself, not the user.

**XVIII. Lex Curvae Magisterii (Mastery Gradient)**  
Magic must require a defined progression from access to mastery, unless created within 1000 years or by an Entity-Individual above rank 1000 on [UOE Unified Alltime Rankings](UOE%20Unified%20Alltime%20Rankings.md). All undocumented Magicae is to be classified as [[XVIII-Non-Alpha Cases]]
[[Legum Magicae (XVIII-Alpha)]]

**XIX. Lex Specialisationis Thematicae (Thematic Specialization)**  
Magic must be either general in scope or divided into a specialized [Divine Affinity](Divine%20Affinity).
