The ground cracked under Ione’s first step.

Clean line. Controlled burn. No excess.

Merek came in half a beat later—longer stride, heavier contact.

The rune widened.

“Thoughts are like fish,” he muttered, watching the line flare outward, “they slip away if you blink.”

“Then stop blinking,” Ione said.

---

They moved in tandem.

Not synchronized.

Not yet.

Two different patterns trying to become one system.

Behind them, the terrain began to fold into structure—lines intersecting, branching, stabilizing.

Merek glanced back.

“…That’s not subtle.”

“It isn’t supposed to be.”

“Feels like we’re writing our location in big glowing letters.”

“We are.”

He snorted.

“Good. Saves them the trouble of looking.”

---

Movement ahead.

Shadows pulling themselves upright.

Cultists.

Too many.

Merek tilted his head.

“Well, well, if it isn’t—”

“Finish the sentence and I’ll correct your stride manually.”

“Still playing hero, I see,” he finished anyway, grin audible in his voice.

Ione didn’t respond.

---

First contact.

A cultist charged across an incomplete line.

It completed under him.

Instantly.

His legs locked mid-stride, runes burning upward through muscle and bone. He dropped, twitching once before the ground reclaimed him.

Merek whistled.

“Do behave,” he said lightly, stepping forward. “The system’s in a good mood.”

“Focus.”

“I am focused.”

“You’re talking.”

“I can do both.”

---

Incoming fire.

Rounds cut across their path.

Ione adjusted trajectory—tight arc, minimal deviation.

Merek didn’t.

He stepped through the fireline instead, boots slamming down harder, runes flaring brighter as they chained faster.

“You’re oversaturating,” Ione said.

“I’m accelerating.”

“You’re sloppy.”

“I’m effective.”

---

A cluster broke right.

Ione pivoted.

“Right flank. Anchor.”

“Finally,” Merek replied. “Orders.”

---

They split.

Clean.

Deliberate.

Ione’s steps shortened—dense, controlled, reinforcing internal structure.

Merek widened his path—long arcs, stretching the outer boundary into something unstable but expansive.

Two halves of a construct forming under pressure.

---

A cultist stumbled into one of Merek’s lines.

Nothing happened.

He laughed.

“Thought that’d do something.”

“Because you broke sequence.”

“Because it looked cool.”

“Fix it.”

---

He corrected.

Next step landed clean.

The line snapped into coherence, linking back into Ione’s structure.

The rune flared.

The cultist didn’t get a second chance.

---

“Mine senses detect something afoul brewing,” Merek said, tone mocking, but quieter now.

“Distance?”

“Close enough.”

“Then stop narrating.”

---

The formation tightened.

Their paths crossed once.

Twice.

Three times.

Each intersection locking another segment into place.

The circle wasn’t visible.

But it was there.

Waiting.

---

Heavy unit incoming.

Armored.

Walking straight through the outer lines.

Unaffected.

Merek exhaled slowly.

“Alright. That’s new.”

“Adjust.”

“On it.”

---

They moved without speaking now.

Ione shifted left.

Merek mirrored wide.

No wasted motion.

No excess heat.

Every step placed with intent.

---

“On your mark,” he said.

No humor now.

“Three,” Ione replied.

“Two.”

“One.”

---

Final step.

Simultaneous.

The construct completed.

---

The reaction inverted inward.

Runes ignited across the entire lattice, forcing pressure toward the center. The armored unit held for a fraction longer than expected.

Then collapsed.

Internally.

Armor folding around nothing.

---

Silence.

Residual heat.

The ground still glowing faintly with embedded lines.

---

Merek rolled his shoulder.

“…You ever think this is overkill?”

“Yes.”

“And?”

“We’re still alive.”

He considered that.

“Fair.”

---

They resumed walking.

Same direction.

Same pattern.

No discussion.

---

After a few steps—

“Something’s off,” Merek muttered.

Ione didn’t look back.

“It always is.”

---

Behind them, the battlefield remained.

Burned.

Structured.

A complete runic network carved by two incompatible styles forced into cooperation.

Precise.

Reckless.

Functionally identical in the only way that mattered.
