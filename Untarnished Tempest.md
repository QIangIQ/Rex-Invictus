The system is derived from experimental **liquid respiration technologies** utilizing oxygenated perfluorocarbon compounds.

## [[Intracranial Stabilization Foam Implant]]

Within the **subarachnoid space surrounding the [[Cerebrum]]**, a reservoir of specialized **[[Neuroreactive Suspension Fluid]]** is maintained.

## Activation Mechanism

During high-acceleration events detected by the Titan’s inertial sensors, the implant triggers a rapid chemical reaction within the fluid.

The fluid undergoes **instantaneous polymerization**, transforming into a **microcellular stabilization foam**.

---

## Functional Effects

The foam expands within the cerebrospinal fluid cavity, producing several stabilizing effects:

- immobilization of the [[Cerebrum]] within the [[Cranium]]
    
- reduction of neural shear forces
    
- stabilization of vascular structures
