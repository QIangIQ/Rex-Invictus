Within advanced conflict doctrine, [[Electronic Counter-Counter-Countermeasures (ECCCM)]] represents:

> The third-order adaptation cycle within [[Electronic Warfare (EW)]], wherein an adversary deploys techniques specifically designed to defeat existing [[Electronic Counter-Countermeasures (ECCM)]] protections, thereby restoring effectiveness of deception, jamming, or denial operations.

### Engagement Hierarchy

1. [[Electronic Countermeasures (ECM)]]
    
    - Jamming
        
    - Deception
        
    - False target generation
        
2. [[Electronic Counter-Countermeasures (ECCM)]]
    
    - Frequency agility
        
    - Pulse compression hardening
        
    - Adaptive thresholding
        
    - Home-on-jam capability
        
3. [[Electronic Counter-Counter-Countermeasures (ECCCM)]]
    
    - Exploitation of ECCM adaptation latency
        
    - Coherent replay with waveform conformity
        
    - DRFM-based ECCM mimicry
        
    - Algorithmic saturation of tracking filters
        
    - Cross-domain deception integration (RF + IR + EM)
        

ECCCM is therefore a war of feedback loops. It targets the adaptive logic layer of the adversary's sensor architecture rather than merely the sensor itself.
