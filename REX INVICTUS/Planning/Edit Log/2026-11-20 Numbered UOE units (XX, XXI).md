# 2026-11-20: Numbered UOE units as italic numerals (XX, XXI)

**Request:** In XX and XXI, every UOE regiment and formation number written as a word ("Third", "Forty-Seventh", "Twenty-Third") becomes its numeral ("3rd", "47th", "23rd") and the numeral is italicized, so readers can pick it out of the sentence. Example: *3rd* Armored Regiment, *47th* Siege Army.

**Correction (2026-11-20, same day):** first pass italicized the whole unit name; the author asked for only the number in italics. Unit words (Siege Army, Regiment, Company, and so on) went back to their original style.

## Style rule (applies from now on)
- UOE unit numbers are numerals and only the numeral is italic: *47th* Siege Army, *2nd* Line Phalanx, *9th* Trench Regiment, *3rd* Company, *9th* Stack, *2nd* Cohort.
- Short forms count too: "the *9th*", "the *17th*'s mortar company" (the possessive 's stays roman).
- Pairs: *1st* and *3rd* Line Phalanxes, *9th* and *12th* Mortar Batteries.
- All-caps headers and net messages keep caps: *47TH* SIEGE ARMY, THE *3RD* SUPERHEAVY MORTAR BATTERY. Text that was already italic (net speech, the 0011 ORBAT list) stays fully italic.

## Left alone
- Streets and avenues (Fifth Avenue, 42nd Street, "Sixth and Madison"), floors, "second death", "second try", "Third generation", "Seventy-first Spirit".
- Non-UOE units: the Fifth Cohort with Reyna (Rome), MAID Urban Tactical Corps Third Company and "First Footman" (Triumvirate) and the Eleventh Airborne at Coldfoot (US Army).
- Judgement call, converted: the Alaska dead in Hrafn's and the memorial roll (*4th Cohort*, *9th Company*) were treated as UOE. Ishara Vell's former unit became *2nd Trophimoi Circle* (Parlamentum and the Trophimoi are UOE per the vault).
