# 2026-11-20: Moonachie mage duel (XXI 0621, draft)

**Request:** At the peak of the battle an enemy faction brings in a few Bretonnian highland mages from the UK (tier 7, D&D-style slots, apprentices slightly weaker, big stereotypical spells, runic robes, talismans and curios). UOE answers with Hecatian counter-maj casters of equal tier who rarely cast above 3rd tier and win with combinations. Both master casters lecture their side and insult the other. When ordered to actually try, the UOE side sobers up and kills them with Slipglue Field, Anti-Mage Primer, Ghost Battery and Magical Railcannon. Reference ULMTU and the Parzival Thaumaturgy vault. At least 2,000 words.

**Draft:** project file `rex-invictus/drafts/xxi-mage-duel/XXI 0621 - Moonachie mage duel (draft).md` (about 6,600 words). Not inserted into the XXI Google Doc yet; insert after the author approves, with a doc-text backup first.

## Revision 3 (2026-11-20, author requests)
- **Combo lesson:** in the lull after "teaching moment", Snakeeye has Bockrill do a half-speed dry run of Slipglue Field and explains combo order with order of operations (2 + 3 x 4). Order: Summon Liquid (T2, about 630 kg, placed on target), then the Transfiguration Inheritance binder (carries every link, ULMTU-VIII), then Glue bound live, then Grease bound dormant (cant closes on a shut vowel, kata ends on a closed fist), then Geyser throws the bound fluid out to 20 m.
- **Misinheritance cases (new canon proposal):** (1) Grease bound before Glue: the field lands slick and the switch glues an empty runway.
