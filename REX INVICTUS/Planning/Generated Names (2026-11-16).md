# Generated Names (2026-11-16)

These are 100 new character names, written to match the style of [[WWI Leaders (Name Reference)]]. Each one is meant to pass as a real statesman, general, royal or officer from 1910 to 1920, so a reader holding both lists shouldn't be able to tell which names are invented. The forms follow the real list: nobiliary particles ("von", "de"), peerage tails ("1st Baron ..."), royal styles ("Archduke ... of Austria-Teschen", "Duke ... in Bavaria"), Ottoman styles ("Pasha", "Sultan", "Şehzade ... Efendi"), surname-first order for Japanese and Chinese names, and American middle initials.

**How the 100 are split:** each faction gets names in proportion to how much space it already has in [[OWN CHARACTERS]] and [[(Potential) Pilot Names]]. MAID and BUTLER were added on request and split roughly by headcount: MAID has about 186,000 operators and BUTLER about 30,000, but BUTLER still needs a full high command. I dropped the 40k crossover entries.

## MAID and BUTLER household precedence (Victorian)

Both corps rank their people by Victorian household precedence, and that precedence works as military rank. A person's place in the house decides their command authority. **MAID is entirely female and BUTLER is entirely male.** The two corps are drawn from the same families: sisters join MAID and brothers join BUTLER.

The **Family** is the high command. The Master and the Mistress are the heads of the dynasties and are not servants. Below them the **Upper Servants** act as officers. In a real Victorian house they took their meals apart from the rest of the staff, in the housekeeper's room, which was nicknamed "Pugs' Parlour". The **Lower Servants** act as the ranks. Footmen were traditionally matched by height and paid more the taller they were, and BUTLER still selects its footmen that way.

The high-command families are cadet lines descended from the Central Powers monarchies. None of them uses the old ruling house name openly. Each line is signalled by a cadet title or a seat.

- **Hohenzollern-Hechingen** stands for the Prussian and German imperial house.
- **Austria-Teschen** stands for the Habsburg-Lorraine archdukes, through the Teschen line.
- **"in Bavaria"** stands for the Wittelsbachs.
- **Saxe-Coburg and Gotha-Koháry** stands for the Bulgarian royal house.
- **Sultan / Şehzade Efendi** stands for the House of Osman.
- **Württemberg** and **Baden** stand for the two remaining South German royal and grand-ducal houses.
