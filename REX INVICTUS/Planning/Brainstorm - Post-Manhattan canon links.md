# Brainstorm: Linking Rex Invictus to the post-Manhattan PJO timeline

*2026-11-21. Everything here is a PROPOSAL until you pick it. Canon order stays Rex Invictus > NF > MTWABV, and Riordan canon is raw material we bend, not a rail we follow.*

---

## 1. Where the two timelines stand

**Riordan canon after the Battle of Manhattan.** Luke dies, Percy turns down godhood and asks the gods to claim every child and give the minor gods cabins. Rachel becomes the Oracle and speaks the Great Prophecy of the Seven. Then Hera swaps Percy and Jason, and Percy loses eight months of memory. Gaea wakes, the Giants are reborn, Thanatos is chained in Alaska and the Doors of Death open. In *House of Hades* Percy and Annabeth fall into Tartarus, drink from the Phlegethon and close the Doors from the inside. After that, Apollo is made mortal and the Triumvirate (Nero, Commodus, Caligula) comes out of hiding. Jason dies, Reyna joins the Hunt, and Percy chases recommendation letters for New Rome University (*Chalice of the Gods*), then does a job for Hecate (*Wrath of the Triple Goddess*). Nico and Will go down to Tartarus for Bob (*The Sun and the Star*).

**Rex Invictus after XXII.** Several of those beats have already happened early, sideways or not at all:

| Canon beat | Rex Invictus status |
|---|---|
| Greek and Roman camps kept apart | **Broken already.** Percy claimed Legio XXI Rapax and beat Jason in the Trial by Steel. |
| Battle of the Labyrinth | **Folded into Manhattan.** Daedalus dead, the maze collapsed. Kampe is dead; her Kampeid brood fought in XIX. |
| Luke's death | **Done.** "Did it have doors?" |
| Triumvirate stays hidden until after Gaea | **Out early.** Roman Triumvirate Holdings, the $430M bounty, Nero's tower, MAID and BUTLER. |
| Thanatos busy, death works | **Bent.** The Martians die 6,108 times with zero permanent losses. Thanatos stood at Grand Central and watched it happen. |
| Mortals stay blind | **Broken.** NASA and the White House know. Mars is a forge world. |
| Percy's memory is Hera's to take | **Contested.** Artemis already collects Percy's memories as Minerva's debt payment. |
| Zoë dies, Bianca dies | **Neither.** Zoë is "MISSING. NOT DEAD." Bianca is under Everest in 16231. |

**The far-future siege.** Fourteen thousand years on, the *3rd* Siege Army has taken the Pyriphlegethon Arsenal and Thalia's Hunt has sealed the Kindlers' door. Echidna guarded the door after Kampe fell. The door was fed by the Echo Mast, which stripped 4.31 million uploads from the dead. Minerva wears Percy's vat-grown body, and has done so since she closed his dream road (Ch12).

The best links are the places where a present-day canon event leaves something physical, legal or remembered that the siege can find fourteen thousand years later.

## 2. Link points

Each entry gives the canon beat, what Rex Invictus already has, the link, and how it could play in each era.

### A. Tartarus and the door

**A1. The Doors of Death are the Kindlers' prototype.** *(Strongest link.)*
- **Canon:** Gaea opens the Doors of Death so dead monsters come straight back. They have two ends, one in Tartarus and one in the Necromanteion of Epirus, and Thanatos's chains hold them open.
- **Rex Invictus:** the Kindlers built a door on the Arsenal's casting floor, held open by stripped uploads fed down from the Echo Mast. Echidna guarded it, and the Hunt sealed it, leaving "a long pale scar across the stone."
- **The link:** the Kindlers didn't invent the idea. They rebuilt the Doors of Death from records, and the Tabularium is where those records were kept.
- **Present-day play:** if the Gaea arc runs, the Doors close in the 21st century, and someone files a full UOE-style report on how they worked. Annabeth would be the obvious candidate, given her High-Lord-Engineer future. That report becomes the blueprint.
- **Siege payoff:** a single line in a reread of Ch35, where Sarn or Kestrel recognises the geometry: "This is the old pattern. The Epirus pattern."

**A2. Echidna as a family business.**
- **Canon:** Echidna appears once, at the St. Louis Arch with the Chimera, and calls Percy "my boy" with real menace.
- **Rex Invictus:** Kampe is dead in the present (XIX). In the siege, Echidna "came up behind Kampe": "Kampe was the key. Echidna is the door."
- **The link:** in the present, Echidna learns Kampe died to a demigod in Percy's company. Fourteen thousand years later her line to the Hunt is "You have killed my sister's keys." That grudge has been running the whole time.
- **Present-day play:** a short Echidna villain scene after XXII, with her alone at the Arch. Her children in the present are Gaea's best shock troops, and she starts collecting them.

**A3. The Phlegethon's water heals.**
- **Canon:** in *House of Hades*, Percy and Annabeth survive Tartarus by drinking the burning river. It keeps you alive while it hurts.
- **Rex Invictus:** the siege runs on the Phlegethon. There are quench-tank brines, fire nets and the Arsenal's whole name.
- **The link:** the UOE knows the river heals because a demigod survived on it once. Imperial combat medicine (Regen-Ex, quench-brine) could carry a doctrine note naming the precedent.
- **Siege payoff:** a Moonwall healer uses river-water on Roe in Ch35. A one-line touch.

**A4. Bob, Damasen and the Unremembered.**
- **Canon:** Percy wiped the Titan Iapetus with Lethe water, and "Bob" came back a gentle janitor who later gives his life for Percy in Tartarus.
- **Rex Invictus:** the Lethe faction are the Unremembered, under Legate Ysander Pale. The Grey Seventh is a memory solvent, and Verrill filters it with Mnemosyne-salt.
- **The link:** the Unremembered have a founding myth, the Titan who forgot himself and was kinder for it. They worship Lethe because of Bob, and they have the story backwards.
- **Present-day play:** Rex Invictus's Percy hasn't met Iapetus yet. The Labyrinth's collapse could be what drops Iapetus into the Lethe here instead.

**A5. Hades' Wall becomes the Styx front.**
- **Canon:** none. This one is Rex Invictus's own.
- **Rex Invictus:** Hades said "Do not fill my halls carelessly" and plans a Wall on the Styx. The siege has a Styx Watch and the Forsworn.
- **The link:** the Wall built in the present is the oldest fortification on the future front. The Forsworn's self-unmaking with a hand to the nape would mirror an oath Hades demanded from the Wall's first garrison.

### B. Memory as currency

**B1. Hera's swap meets Artemis's ledger.** *(Best drama.)*
- **Canon:** Hera puts Percy to sleep for eight months and takes his memories so he arrives at Camp Jupiter blank.
- **Rex Invictus:** Percy's memories are already promised. Minerva pays Artemis one day of them a year, and Artemis took three years in a single giddy pull (Interlude II). Percy learns his memories are being spent as debt (arc plan).
- **The link:** Hera tries her canon move and hits a lien. Artemis has a claim on those memories, and the Hunt reacts as if someone has robbed a bank.
- **Present-day play:** a council scene where Hera and Artemis argue over who owns Percy's past. Its comedy comes from the bookkeeping and its threat from Hera. It also gives Hera's arc a new reason to exist, because the camps are already united.
- **Siege echo:** the siege runs on the same economics. The Mast takes uploads, Mirren holds them and they are "going home."

**B2. The Arai and the Ledger of Nemesis.**
- **Canon:** in Tartarus the Arai curse Percy with the dying words of everyone he has killed. Nemesis charges Leo an eye for a fortune cookie and "balances" the Seven.
- **Rex Invictus:** the siege has the Cohors Nemesis Prima and a chapter called "Ledger of Nemesis." Nemesis rounds already exist in XX and XXI and turn harm back on whoever threw it. In XIX Ethan carries a notebook.
- **The link:** Nemesis keeps a real ledger, and in the present she opens a page for Percy's Manhattan kills. Fourteen thousand years later the Cohort of Nemesis is still collecting on it.
- **Present-day play:** if Ethan survived XXI, he becomes Nemesis's clerk. He'd be the first collector, still with the eyepatch and still carrying the notebook. If he didn't survive, the notebook passes to someone.

**B3. Percy's memory gap as Minerva's alibi.**
- **Canon:** Percy's eight lost months.
- **Rex Invictus:** Minerva closed Percy's dream road at Ch12. Nobody in the present knows why.
- **The link:** whatever Hera takes, Minerva can hide inside. A lost memory of the bridge, of "Lord of Demigods," or of the last time he wore the body explains why Percy never notices the road has closed.

### C. The census and the tithe

**C1. Percy's wish becomes the Tithe.** *(Thematic spine.)*
- **Canon:** at the end of *The Last Olympian*, Percy's reward is a promise that every demigod gets claimed by thirteen. It is the kindest thing he does.
- **Rex Invictus:** the Arsenal held a "tithe of unregistered Hecatean strays, waiting for Tithe Day." The Callistus undercity has Tithe Day conscription into Legio XX.
- **The link:** the promise to claim every child turns into a register, the register into a census, and the census into a draft. The UOE's whole conscription machine grew from Percy's best wish. Mirren is a stray because nobody claimed her, which is the exact failure Percy tried to end.
- **Present-day play:** at the XXII banquet or just after, Percy asks his canon question. Minerva, or Athena, says it could be done properly with a database. Readers who know the siege feel the trap close.
- **Siege echo:** none needed. Ch3 and Ch35 already carry the weight.

**C2. Hecate's children.**
- **Canon:** Hecate sided with Kronos, then got a cabin through Percy's deal. She runs the Mist, and in *Wrath of the Triple Goddess* she has Percy pet-sit.
- **Rex Invictus:** in XIX "Hecate was wounded, somewhere," and the Mist was thin. Hecate rounds and lineage gunners like Varga exist. Mirren reaches for souls in the dark.
- **The link:** why Hecatean strays specifically? Because Hecate's line was the one the empire registered last and trusted least. Her Titan-war betrayal never washed out of the records.
- **Present-day play:** a *Wrath*-style errand for a recovering Hecate. It's funny, involves a pet, and ends with her asking Percy whether her children will be safe under his empire. He says yes.

### D. Augurs, prophecy and oracles

**D1. Octavian is the first Haruspex.**
- **Canon:** Octavian, a legacy of Apollo, reads omens in stuffed animals and wants power, then dies by his own onager.
- **Rex Invictus:** Octavian refused to move the legion in XIX ("grey stuffing"). The siege has Haruspex Ilan Dace, who made the Archflamen and cut his own throat on the Mast.
- **The link:** the UOE's augur office was founded by a Roman augur who wanted a seat at the table. Its rites trace back to his. Dace's notebook going blank and his self-killing rhyme with Octavian's end.
- **Present-day play:** give Octavian a win. He becomes the augur Percy has to work with, and he founds the office legally. That's worse than a villain.

**D2. The Great Prophecy of the Seven.** *(Your call.)*
- **Canon:** Rachel speaks it at the end of *The Last Olympian*.
- **Rex Invictus:** Rachel hasn't appeared on the page, the camps are already united, and the UOE outguns Gaea's armies on paper. Your options:
  1. **It happens and misfires.** It names seven, and the gods argue over whether Imperials count as "half-bloods." Eleni, Marien and the Ekdromoi all have godly blood.
  2. **Python chokes it.** Bring *Trials of Apollo* forward. Delphi is seized, Rachel can't speak, and the Prophecy arrives broken or late.
  3. **It already happened.** The CODE 15 "Guardian of the Past" file is the Great Prophecy as the future recorded it.
- **My recommendation:** option 3 with a touch of option 2. Rachel speaks it, and Minerva recognises the wording from the CODE 15 file.

**D3. The Sibylline Books and the Tabularium.**
- **Canon:** Ella the harpy carries the lost Sibylline Books in her head.
- **Rex Invictus:** the Tabularium is a hall of records and cells.
- **The link:** the Tabularium's oldest shelf holds Ella's reconstruction, or at least someone claims it does.

### E. Giants, Gaea and Alaska

**E1. Alaska is Alcyoneus's ground.** *(Neatest link.)*
- **Canon:** in *Son of Neptune*, Thanatos is chained on the Hubbard Glacier. Alcyoneus is immortal in Alaska, his homeland, which is "beyond the gods."
- **Rex Invictus:** Alaska Hill is the first Parlamentum Mk. II site, with geothermal and cave networks. The first real AtRep computer is built in Alaska, "land beyond the gods." The Arctic Resource War comes in the mid-2030s.
- **The link:** you ruled Alaska Hill hides nothing, and this keeps that ruling. Nothing is hidden there. The ground simply belongs to the Giants and not the gods, and that is why the UOE's first thinking machine had to be built there. AtRep was born where gods were blind.
- **Present-day play:** Alcyoneus wakes to find a Parlamentum site sitting on his ground, and treats it as squatting.

**E2. Porphyrion's long cycle.**
- **Canon:** Giants can only be killed by a god and a demigod working together. That is the whole thesis of *Heroes of Olympus*.
- **Rex Invictus:** in 16231, Porphyrion is buried at the Wound and "will reform in 11 years." The Anabasis Accord binds gods and Imperials.
- **The link:** the present-day Gigantomachy is where the god-and-demigod rule becomes doctrine. The Accord is that rule written into law, and Porphyrion is the enemy who keeps coming back to test it in both eras.

**E3. Thanatos's grievance.**
- **Canon:** Gaea chains Thanatos and death stops working.
- **Rex Invictus:** the Martians already beat death with the Watchpoint noosphere and vats under the Tharsis forges. Thanatos watched in XXI.
- **The link:** death was broken before Gaea touched it. Gaea's pitch to Thanatos, or to Hades, is that the Olympians have allowed a mortal machine to steal from the dead. Some of the gods find that persuasive.
- **Siege echo:** the 4.31M "nulls" are what happens when the machine goes wrong. A Thanatos line in the present ("Someday the river will not give them back") can pay off in Ch35.

### F. Engineers and the road home

**F1. Leo and Odysseus's astrolabe.**
- **Canon:** Leo builds the Argo II and repairs Odysseus's astrolabe to find his way back to Ogygia, an island outside normal time and space.
- **Rex Invictus:** Article IX promises a road home built from both ends. Verrill builds the far end, and Minerva says only foreign pantheons have roads through time. The vault already has an Argo note, and Mars has the Hephaestus Forge Cohorts.
- **The link:** the astrolabe is the first Greek tool that navigates out of a place time doesn't reach. It's the one exception to Minerva's rule, and the present end of the road gets its core from it.
- **Present-day play:** introduce Leo as the kid the Forge Cohorts stop and stare at. He is the reason a Hephaestus demigod outranks their foundry-masters.

**F2. The Chase family line.**
- **Canon:** Magnus Chase is Annabeth's cousin, and Norse.
- **Rex Invictus:** Verrill Chase, AnnChase-class ships, Annabeth as High-Lord-Engineer, and the Norse already at Alcatraz.
- **The link:** Verrill descends from the Chase family, through both the Annabeth and Magnus lines, with Athena and Frey blood. That explains her harsh-mint aura, her engineering and how she got a foothold with the Norse.
- **Present-day play:** a cameo by a homeless Boston kid named Magnus whom Annabeth worries about. That's all it needs.

**F3. The Athena Parthenos and Minerva's name.**
- **Canon:** in *The Mark of Athena* the Romans' theft of the Parthenos splits Athena into a Greek self and a Roman self, "Minerva." The statue's return heals her.
- **Rex Invictus:** the AI is called Minerva, and Athena's exam of her was cut off halfway. You've already ruled that Artemis can split into Artemis and Diana.
- **The link:** the AI's name is not a coincidence. Athena named her Roman half and then built a machine to hold it. Recovering the Parthenos could heal Athena and threaten the AI in the same stroke, or the AI might want the statue for herself.
- **Siege echo:** Minerva's "reasons of my own" for closing the road could connect here.

### G. People who reach the far future

**G1. Thalia, after Jason.**
- **Canon:** Jason dies at Caligula's hand in *The Burning Maze*, and Thalia grieves as a Hunter.
- **Rex Invictus:** Caligula's Triumvirate is already active, and in the siege Thalia leads the Hunt as a punishment and fears heights.
- **The link:** the brother she lost is part of why siege-era Thalia is so hard. If Jason dies in the present-day Triumvirate war, the siege doesn't need to say so. One line on the Mast about a pine sprig and "Pinecone Face" carries it.

**G2. Reyna in the Hunt.**
- **Canon:** at the end of *Trials of Apollo*, Reyna joins the Hunters.
- **Rex Invictus:** Reyna holds Baal's toll coin from XIX. Her dogs follow Percy.
- **The link:** Reyna is the one other present-day face who could plausibly stand on the siege's war-table, as Hunt-Elder or as the Hunt's oldest lieutenant. She'd be a free payoff for readers.

**G3. Nico hears the nulls go home.**
- **Canon:** Nico and Will go down to Tartarus because a voice is calling, which turns out to be Bob.
- **Rex Invictus:** Nico hears the dead. He heard the 406 Imperial dead, and Charon's Heralds came "from further down" than Hades has been.
- **The link:** Tartarus doesn't keep time cleanly. When the 4.31M uploads are released in Ch35, a 21st-century Nico hears four million voices go quiet at once and doesn't know why.
- **Present-day play:** that becomes the *Sun and the Star* hook, rewritten so Nico goes down after an echo from the future.

**G4. Apollo's punishment, early.**
- **Canon:** Zeus makes Apollo mortal after the Gaea war.
- **Rex Invictus:** Apollo hid Percy's wounds and Kappa-Seven from Poseidon and Artemis, and Zeus is back and angry.
- **The link:** Zeus punishes Apollo for exactly that, earlier than in canon. Lester Papadopoulos, with the UOE watching, could be the comic arc after XXII.

## 3. What canon cannot do here

- **Hera's "unite the camps" scheme is moot**, because Percy already did it by force (B1 replaces it).
- **The Labyrinth is gone**, so canon roads that ran through it need new routes: the Pit market, the Tartarus road, foreign pantheons.
- **Gaea's armies can't threaten the UOE head-on.** Her real weapon has to be legitimacy (E3) and turning gods against the Accord, not numbers.
- **Romance stays off the page.** Canon's Percy and Annabeth couple and Rex Invictus's Artemis bond both stay non-romantic on the page for Percy's current body, per the content limit.

## 4. A suggested spine (my recommendation)

1. **After XXII, the wish.** Percy asks for every child to be claimed (C1). Rachel speaks the Prophecy, and Minerva hears the CODE 15 wording in it (D2, option 3). Thanatos tells Percy the river won't always give his soldiers back (E3).
2. **Hera's theft.** She takes Percy and hits Artemis's lien. The camps don't need uniting, so Hera's real aim is to get Percy away from the UOE (B1, B3).
3. **Alaska.** Alcyoneus wakes under the Parlamentum Mk. II site, and Thanatos's chains are on the same glacier (E1, E2).
4. **Tartarus.** Percy goes through the Doors of Death and drinks the Phlegethon. Someone takes notes that will become the Kindlers' blueprint (A1, A3, A4). Echidna is waiting (A2).
5. **Echo.** In the far future the Mast releases its 4.31M uploads, and in the present Nico hears it (G3).

This keeps every siege payoff to a line or two, so the siege chapters stay as they are. Only the present-day chapters carry new weight.
