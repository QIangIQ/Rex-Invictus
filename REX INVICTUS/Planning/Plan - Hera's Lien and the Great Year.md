# Plan: Hera's Lien and the Great Year

*2026-11-21. This expands idea B1 of [[Brainstorm - Post-Manhattan canon links]]. The author has set the direction: steer into idea 2, add a timeskip that ages Percy to 23, have Minerva fit Council of Olympic Sentinels augments, and have Marien hand training to Percy. The beats, names and implant designs below are proposals.*

---

## 1. The steer

Hera moves to take Percy's memories, as she does in canon. Artemis catches her through the heart link and the Director, and calls Athena. Diana tears herself free of Artemis and doubles the moon's power. Together they break the Queen's hold.

The gods want it settled, but when Hera tries to formalise a betrothal she finds the bond already formed, and stronger than any she has ever made. Zeus still gets one thing he wants: Percy is put off the board for seven years on the Cadmus precedent.

Percy serves the term on Mars, where Ares is the garrison's patron. There:
- UOE resources speed up Minerva's repair of the Aegis Vestments.
- Percy reforms the Council of Olympic Sentinels.
- Once the body passes eighteen, Minerva starts threading the Sentinel implants into him.
- Marien, young and burnt out, hands him the training of the army.

He comes back at twenty-three, married, built differently, and severe with everyone except his wife.

## 2. Why Hera wants his memories here

In canon, Hera wipes Percy so he arrives at Camp Jupiter blank and the two camps join. In Rex Invictus Percy has already united them by force, so that reason is gone. The motive comes from material that's already on the page:

- **CODE 18 named only Poseidon's line.** Zeus and Hera resent the verdict (Plot Threads 8). Percy is Lord Imperator of an empire that answers to a verdict, not to Zeus's throne.
- **Zeus is angry Percy was taken out of his hands, as Hercules was.** Hera hated Hercules, and she knows the shape of a hero who outgrows the King of the Gods.
- **The future lives in Percy's head.** Every Imperial asset in the present, from the Accord and the Mars lane to the Watchpoint handshake, rests on what Percy knows and remembers. Wipe it and the Lord Imperator becomes a sixteen-year-old son of Poseidon with an odd heart.

So Hera's aim is to cut the future out of him and give Zeus his throne back. She dresses it as mercy: "The boy is carrying fourteen thousand years. Let me take the weight."

## 3. The bond before the betrothal

*Revised 2026-11-21 per the author.*

**Starting point.** Percy and Artemis both have foreknowledge that they end up married:
- Percy carries it from the old timeline.
- Artemis has Eleni's report, the Anaklusmos vision, and her own memories of "centuries yet to come".

So neither of them waits for the future to arrive. They are a couple from the start, and they treat the marriage as already decided. Each of them is fiercely, possessively protective of the other: yandere toward anyone who threatens the other, and calm about almost everything else.

**On the page before the timeskip.** Percy's body is a teen, so the relationship shows as loyalty, jealousy, protectiveness and banter. No kissing, flirting or physical romance. What carries it is how dangerous they both become when the other is touched. The romance itself waits for the post-timeskip chapters.

**The heart link.** Three bonds already run between them:
- **The Ch18 bond.** Percy fed Artemis his blood, ichor, core and part of his curse at the Aventine shrine.
- **The silver crescent.** Interlude II's mark on the future body.
- **Artemis and the Director.** The present Artemis is joined to her future self, the Director, who has tracked Percy's scent for fourteen thousand years.

Together these make a link that rings the moment anyone reaches into Percy. Hera doesn't know about the third strand.

**The memory lien** from the first draft of this plan still stands in the background. Minerva pays Artemis in memories, so Hera's theft would be a theft from Artemis twice over. It no longer drives the plot, because Artemis catches Hera before the law matters.

## 4. Beat sheet

### Act I: The Theft (directly after XXII)

1. **Hera comes at night.** On the night of the Olympus banquet she comes to Percy in the Huntress palace guest wing, as the kindly old woman with a shawl. She wants to cut the future out of him and hand it to Zeus (section 2). Minerva flags an unauthorised divine signature and is overridden by Olympian command tokens.
2. **The pull.** Percy goes under, and Hera starts drawing. Minerva counts the days lost: forty, then a hundred and ten.
3. **The link rings.** It rings through the heart link and through the Director. Fourteen thousand years away, the Director feels a hand on the boy she has been hunting for and wakes the present Artemis from inside. Artemis is at the door before Hera has the third thread out, with her bow already drawn and her face perfectly still. That stillness is her yandere side: quiet, brooding and absolute.
4. **Artemis can't win alone.** Hera is Queen of the Gods, the room is in her husband's palace, and the threads are still in her hand. Artemis knows it. Her line: "Put him down, stepmother."
5. **Artemis calls Athena.**
    - **Why Athena answers at once:** her concern is the knowledge, not the boy. Fourteen thousand years of future events, technology and strategy sit in Percy's head. In Hera's hands it would go to Hera and Zeus and stay there.
    - **Athena's line:** "Wisdom hoarded by a throne is wisdom wasted. That library belongs to all of us, Hera, or to none."
    - **What she does:** she takes custody of the memories Hera has already pulled and seals them in an owl-warded archive. Hera can't spend them, and Athena won't. Athena holds them in trust, which replaces the first draft's Hera escrow.
6. **Two against the Queen.** It's a short, ugly fight: the hunt and wisdom against marriage and the throne. Keep it inside the palace and fast, with the Huntress palace's marble cracking. Hera is still winning on raw rank.
7. **Diana steps out.** She comes from behind Artemis without being asked.
    - **Artemis's reaction:** mortified, she kicks her alternate self in the shin and hisses at her to get back in.
    - **Why Diana stays out:** Diana has been feeding on the energy of everything between Artemis and Percy since the Aventine shrine, and she has had enough. She separates fully, for good, and doesn't go back.
    - **The result:** the moon now has two goddesses at full strength, which doubles its power in one move.
    - **Hera's position:** she is facing two moons and Athena, and she lets go.
8. **Diana's first line about Percy** shows the difference between the two at once. She is loud and red-faced and denies everything. Her line: "I am not here for *him*. I am here because she was losing, and it was embarrassing to watch."
9. **Artemis says nothing.** She steps over the broken floor, puts herself between Percy and the door, and looks at Hera until Hera leaves.

### Act II: The Hearing

10. **The council convenes at dawn.** Hera files first, charging that a maiden goddess and the goddess of wisdom assaulted the Queen in her own husband's house.
11. **Athena argues the knowledge.** Her case is that the future in Percy's head is a common inheritance, and that Hera tried to make it a royal one.
12. **Hades confirms that memory is currency below.** He has seen dead men pay Charon with their last day. His line: "Taking a man's days is theft. On my side of the river we hang people for it."
13. **Poseidon nearly breaks the hearing.** He learns that his son was robbed in his sleep under Olympus's roof. Then he learns his son has been courting the maiden goddess for months, and that she has just split in two over it.
14. **The two moons.** Artemis and Diana stand side by side and answer every question in unison, so the gods see the sync for the first time. On anything that isn't Percy they move as one: they take the same stance, finish each other's sentences without trying, and draw their bows in the same instant. On Percy they split. Diana flusters, contradicts herself and protests far too much. Artemis goes quiet and cold and watches whoever asked.
15. **Zeus rules.** He has to save his wife's face and keep the new moon in hand. The theft is struck from the record, the Queen is "mistaken", and Athena keeps the archive in trust. Then he gets what he actually wants: Percy off the board. He orders the betrothal formalised, if they're so set on it, and the boy sent to serve a term before any wedding, on the Cadmus precedent. Hera adds, with what dignity she has left: "Cadmus served eight. I am generous. Seven."

### Act III: The Betrothal

16. **The rite of formalisation.** Hera must perform it as goddess of marriage. It's meant to be her last revenge: she will bind them under her sacrament, on her terms, in front of everyone.
17. **The bond is already there.** When Hera reaches for the cord she means to tie, she finds it already tied, and older than the rite. It was made from foreknowledge, the Aventine bond, the Director's fourteen thousand years and the crescent, and the whole council can see it. It runs from Percy to both moons, and it is thicker and brighter than any marriage-bond Hera has ever made. She made Zeus and Hera's own, and this is stronger.
18. **The Olympians' reaction.** They are surprised, then uneasy, and Apollo laughs out loud. Aphrodite gets the best line: "Oh, she didn't make this. It made itself."
19. **Hera adds nothing.** She speaks the formula over a bond she couldn't break and doesn't need to seal, and everyone watching knows it. That's the scene's victory, quiet and total.
20. **The contract names both aspects.** Diana objects loudly to being named and signs anyway. Artemis signs without a word, then looks at Percy for the first time all day and finally smiles.
21. **Where he serves.** On Mars. Ares is patron of the garrison under the Accord, and Mars is Ares's planet under another name. The Cadmus precedent fits: a hero serving the war god before he weds. Percy leaves with Eleni.
22. **Which moon goes with him.** Diana, the Roman aspect, takes Mars as part of her new domain, so Rome's war planet gets a Roman moon. Artemis stays with the Hunt and visits, which is where the "switch" at 23 comes from.

### Act IV: The Great Year (the timeskip, ages 16 to 23)

This plays as a montage interlude plus a few full scenes. The training scene is drafted.

- **Year One. The Vestments.** On Mars, Minerva gets what she never had in the present: a forge world. She has Hephaestus Forge Cohort fabricators, Tharsis medicae and Watchpoint diagnostics. The Aegis repairs, which would have taken decades on Earth, take a year. The Recovery demand penalty ("increased by 150%") is retired. The Ghost Augments become permanent.
- **Year Two. The Council.** Percy reforms the Council of Olympic Sentinels (section 7).
- **Year Two to Three. The handover.** Marien hands him the training (section 8).
- **Year Three onward. The implants.** At eighteen the body passes maturity, and Minerva begins fitting the Sentinel implants, which are standard in the old timeline (section 9). He bulks, retrains and relearns how to fight inside a different body.
- **Year Four. "The Gilded."** The training scene with the house-born candidate officers.
- **Year Five. A real war.** The Mars front can be the Giants' first strike off-world, or a Tartarean breach on Phobos, or the Triumvirate's corporate fleet. Percy commands for the first time in this life.
- **Year Six.** Bulk and retraining finished. His temper hardens, and the troopers start calling him something.
- **Year Seven. The return.** He comes home at twenty-three.

### Act V: The Rite (return, age 23)

- **The wedding.** Hera officiates again, over a bond she couldn't break. Athena opens the archive and returns the stolen months as her wedding gift. Percy gets back the camper he was at sixteen, all at once, inside the head of the man he became. That's a chapter-sized scene. Keep the wedding ceremonial and non-physical.
- **Then the Gaea war's last act.** Tartarus, the Doors of Death and Echidna come next (Brainstorm A1 to A3), with a twenty-three-year-old Sentinel in place of the canon seventeen-year-old.

## 6. Percy at twenty-three

**Body.** He's 1.93 m, broad through the shoulders and chest, and heavier than he looks, since the bones are laced (section 9). The Lycaon heart still beats on the left and his own, regrown, beats on the right. The circuitry under his skin shows only when the Vestments wake. The silver crescent Artemis put on the body in Interlude II is now a visible mark on the inside of his left wrist.

**Bearing.** He's severe, heavy-handed and serious. The flippant teenager ("Nanomachines, Son") has been worn down into the old Lord-Commander. He gives orders in one line and doesn't explain them twice. Discipline in his cohort is famous and a little feared.

**The switch.** In Artemis's presence he brightens at once. Diana is with him on Mars, and she gets a softer version of it, which she complains about loudly while staying in the room. Troopers have learned to read his mood from whether the Huntress is in the building. Keep it to affection and lightness, not romance on the page: he jokes again, says "Arty", and lets her win arguments he would never let a general win. A good recurring gag is officers timing their bad news for when she's visiting.

**Minerva on the switch.** She can blame the bond anchor (implant 26) as a chemical cause. Percy never argues with her about it, and nobody believes her.

**Sample lines:**

> To a colonel: "Your flank was open for eleven minutes. Fix it or I'll find someone who will."

> To Artemis, the same afternoon: "You came all the way to Mars to tell me I'm too serious? That's sweet, Arty. Sit down, I stole the good coffee from the Third."

> On Hera, at the wedding: "Thanks for the gift. I'd like the receipt."

- **What it is.** The Sentinels are re-founded as a joint body under the Accord: demigods from both camps, Imperial legacies, and Firstborn volunteers. They are Olympus's praetorian guard, and the UOE's elite at the same time.
- **Head.** Percy.
- **Vice-President.** This one is open. You could repeat the old timeline with Annabeth, now the Architect in earnest after the Parthenos. Or you could break the pattern with Eleni. Annabeth keeps the old shape, which may be the point, or the point may be that it can't come back.

- **The augments.** The standard Sentinel kit (section 9) is the old-timeline package. Percy gets the full set. Other Sentinels get reduced versions, cut down for bodies without Minerva in them.
