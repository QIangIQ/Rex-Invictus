**[[Vestal Virgins]]** were the six priestesses of [[Vesta]] who kept the sacred fire of Rome burning.

---

## Roman History (IRL)

Tradition credits Numa Pompilius with founding the order. Chosen as girls between six and ten from patrician families, they served for thirty years under the authority of the Pontifex Maximus and lived in the House of the Vestals by the temple in the Forum. They guarded the fire and the sacred objects of the state, including the Palladium, and enjoyed rare privileges: they could make wills, rode in carriages and could pardon a condemned man they met by chance. A Vestal who broke her vow of chastity was buried alive. Theodosius I ended the order in 394 AD.

## Rex Invictus

Their successors guarded the sacred site later secured by the [[Vestal Fringe Corporation]]. Greek counterpart: [[Hestia]].
