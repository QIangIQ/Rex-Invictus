**[[Marcus Porcius Cato]]** is the name of two famous Roman statesmen.

---

## Roman History (IRL)

**Cato the Elder (234 to 149 BC).** Censor in 184 BC, champion of old Roman austerity against Greek luxury, author of the first Latin prose history (*Origines*) and of *On Agriculture*. He ended speeches with "Carthage must be destroyed."

**Cato the Younger (95 to 46 BC).** His great-grandson, a Stoic and the Senate's most stubborn defender of the Republic against [[Julius Caesar]]. After Caesar's victory at Thapsus he killed himself at Utica rather than accept Caesar's pardon, and became the model of republican virtue for later ages.
