**[[Rubicon]]** is a small river in northeast Italy that marked the border between the province of Cisalpine Gaul and Italy proper.

---

## Roman History (IRL)

A governor who led an army across it into Italy committed treason. In January 49 BC [[Julius Caesar]] crossed it with the [[Legio XIII Gemina|Thirteenth Legion]], saying, by Suetonius' account, "the die is cast" (*alea iacta est*). The crossing began the civil war against Pompey and the Senate that ended the Republic. "Crossing the Rubicon" now means passing a point of no return.
