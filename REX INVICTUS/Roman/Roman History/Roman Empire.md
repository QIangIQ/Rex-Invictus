**[[Roman Empire]]** is the Roman state under emperors, from [[Augustus (Roman Emperor)|Augustus]] (27 BC) to the fall of the West (476 AD) and of Constantinople (1453).

---

## Roman History (IRL)

After the civil wars that followed [[Julius Caesar]]'s crossing of the [[Rubicon]], [[Octavian]] defeated [[Sextus Pompeius]] and then Antony and Cleopatra, and ruled as Augustus. At its height under Trajan (117 AD) the empire ran from Britain to Mesopotamia and was held by about thirty [[Roman Legions]]. Diocletian split its administration, Constantine moved the capital to Constantinople, and the western half fell to Germanic kingdoms when Odoacer deposed Romulus Augustulus. The eastern (Byzantine) empire lasted another thousand years.

## UOE (far future)

[[Legio XV Apollinaris]] and other legions merged under the gods after the empire dissolved, carrying its name and numbering into the [[Undying Olympian Empire]].
