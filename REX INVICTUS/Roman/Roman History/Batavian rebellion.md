**[[Batavian rebellion]]** (69 to 70 AD) was a revolt of the Batavi of the Rhine delta against Rome.

---

## Roman History (IRL)

During the civil wars of the Year of the Four Emperors, the Batavian prince Julius Civilis, a Roman citizen and auxiliary commander, rose with his people and allied Germanic and Gallic tribes. They besieged Vetera, where the [[Legio V Alaudae|Fifth Alaudae]] and the Fifteenth Primigenia surrendered and were massacred, and several other legions went over to a short-lived "Gallic Empire". Quintus Petillius Cerialis put the revolt down for Vespasian in 70 AD. Tacitus tells the story in Books 4 and 5 of the *Histories*. Some historians think the Alaudae was rebuilt and lost later in Dacia at the [[Battle of Tapae]].
