**[[Battle of Tapae]]** is the name of battles fought at the [[Iron]] Gates of Transylvania, the pass into the Dacian heartland.

---

## Roman History (IRL)

**First battle (86 or 87 AD).** Under Domitian, the praetorian prefect Cornelius Fuscus invaded Dacia and was ambushed and killed by the Dacians under Decebalus. A legion, possibly the [[Legio V Alaudae|Fifth Alaudae]], was destroyed with its eagle lost.

**Second battle (88 AD).** Tettius Julianus defeated the Dacians on the same ground.

**Third battle (101 AD).** Trajan won a hard victory there in his First Dacian War, shown on Trajan's Column. Dacia became a province in 106 AD.
