**[[Sextus Pompeius]]** (about 67 to 35 BC) was the younger son of Pompey the Great and the last leader of the Pompeian cause.

---

## Roman History (IRL)

After [[Julius Caesar]]'s murder he built a fleet, seized [[Sicily]], Sardinia and Corsica, and blockaded Rome's grain supply, calling himself the son of [[Neptune]]. The Pact of Misenum in 39 BC briefly recognized his rule. [[Octavian (Roman Emperor)|Octavian]] raised new legions, among them [[Legio XV Apollinaris]], and his admiral Agrippa destroyed Sextus' fleet at Naulochus in 36 BC. Sextus fled east and was executed in Miletus by Antony's men.
