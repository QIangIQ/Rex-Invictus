**[[Jupiter]]** is the Roman king of the gods, counterpart of [[Zeus]]. As Jupiter Optimus Maximus he was worshipped with [[Juno]] and [[Minerva (AI)|Minerva]] in the Capitoline Triad on the Capitoline Hill in Rome. Generals celebrating a triumph dressed as him. In [[The Heroes of Olympus]] he is the father of [[Jason Grace]] and the patron of [[Camp Jupiter]]. See also [[Roman Pantheon]].
