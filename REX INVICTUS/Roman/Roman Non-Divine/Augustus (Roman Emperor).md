**[[Augustus (Roman Emperor)]]** (63 BC to 14 AD) was the first Roman emperor, born [[Octavian (Roman Emperor)|Gaius Octavius]] and adopted by [[Julius Caesar]]. After defeating Antony and Cleopatra at Actium (31 BC) he received the title Augustus from the Senate in 27 BC and ruled for 41 years, founding the Pax Romana. He reorganized the [[Roman Legions]], including the [[Legio X Fretensis]] and [[Legio XII Fulminata]]. See [[Octavian (Roman Emperor)]] for his early career and [[The Augusti]] for the UOE family named after him.
