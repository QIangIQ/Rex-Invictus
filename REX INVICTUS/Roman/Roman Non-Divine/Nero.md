**[[Nero]]** (37 to 68 AD) was the last Julio-Claudian emperor of Rome. He was blamed for the Great Fire of 64, persecuted Christians, built the Golden House and killed himself as the Senate declared him a public enemy. In [[The Trials of Apollo]] he is the hidden leader of the Triumvirate and the final villain of [[The Tower of Nero]], with Meg McCaffrey as his abused stepdaughter.
