**[[Romulus and Remus]]** were the twin brothers of Mars Ultor and Zephaniah. (Lore adapted from *The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** Flamel material is adapted into the Rex Invictus present (c. 2021). Rex Invictus wins any conflict; [[Robespierre]] fills [[Dr. John Dee]]'s role and hunts the Philosopher's Stone rather than [[The Codex]]. See [[Flamel Series#Integration rules]].

## Real-World Background (IRL)

In Roman legend Romulus and Remus are the twin sons of Mars and [[Rhea]] Silvia, suckled by a she-wolf. Romulus killed Remus and founded Rome, traditionally in 753 BC. In PJO canon the she-wolf is [[Lupa]].
