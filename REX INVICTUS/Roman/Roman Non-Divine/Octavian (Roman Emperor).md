**[[Octavian (Roman Emperor)]]** is the name historians use for [[Augustus (Roman Emperor)|Augustus]] between his adoption by [[Julius Caesar]] in 44 BC and 27 BC. He formed the Second Triumvirate with Mark Antony and Lepidus, avenged Caesar at Philippi and defeated Antony at Actium. Not to be confused with the PJO augur [[Octavian]], his supposed descendant.
