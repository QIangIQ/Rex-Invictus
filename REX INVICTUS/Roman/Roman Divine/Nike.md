### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 622
May your path be laden with Invicta Victoria.
—Nike to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] while granting him her Complete Blessing, Chapter 630
Nike, also known as Victoria, is a Third Generation Major Goddess of the former [[Greek Pantheon]] and the principal Greek Goddess of Victory, with additional Divine associations to Strength and Speed. Despite possessing one of the most strategically dangerous Divinities among the Olympian Gods, Nike is notably pacifistic and avoids initiating violence unless an opponent is actively threatening herself, her companions or innocent people.[1][2]
Nike possesses a fully developed Major Divinity in Victory and was one of only a small number of Goddesses evacuated from [[Olympus]] whose Concept had matured to that level.[3] Her Authority allows the Concept of Victory itself to influence events in her favor, producing a limited form of Causality Manipulation that can create viable paths toward a victorious outcome. Although her Divinity cannot ignore differences in raw power or override higher-order Concepts without limit, it is sufficiently dangerous that even stronger Gods historically treated Nike carefully.[2]
After [[Typhon]]'s awakening transformed the Olympian civil war into a potential extinction event, Nike abandoned Olympus alongside [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood, [[Hestia]] Elderblood, [[Demeter]], [[Rhea]], [[Tyche]] and dozens of other Goddesses. She subsequently entered Victor's Faction, gave Victor her rare Complete Blessing of Victory, helped protect the surviving Amazons and later became incorporated into the newly created [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]].[4][5]

### Physical Appearance
Nike is an extraordinarily beautiful woman with long black hair and striking sapphire-blue eyes. Her body is described as exceptionally well proportioned without possessing exaggerated physical features; unlike the more voluptuous [[Demeter]], Nike has a balanced athletic and feminine build.[1]
Her overall appearance is elegant and serious. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] formally examined the approximately fifty Olympian Goddesses brought into his faction, he humorously thought that Nike would resemble a very serious professional secretary if dressed appropriately.[3]
Nike's mature and disciplined appearance contrasts somewhat with her internal personality. Although outwardly composed, she is capable of becoming flustered, sarcastic and openly annoyed when dealing with people such as Victor.

### Personality
Nike is intelligent, disciplined, analytical and remarkably level-headed for an Olympian God. Her mentality is heavily influenced by her Concept of Victory; rather than viewing battle merely through the lens of individual strength, she naturally thinks in terms of conditions for victory, logistics, leadership, advantages, objectives and the countless ways an apparently weaker side can defeat a stronger one.[6]
Despite being one of the most useful military Goddesses who escaped [[Olympus]], Nike is fundamentally a pacifist. She explicitly states that she only learned how to fight to protect herself and does not willingly attack others without cause. She does not oppose killing when genuinely necessary, nor does her pacifism extend toward Demons, malicious supernatural creatures or individuals who intentionally harm others for pleasure, but she strongly dislikes being treated as a weapon simply because her Divinity is extraordinarily useful.[2][7]
This reluctance partially developed from the politics surrounding her Divinity. Gods desired Nike's Blessing because having Victory itself supporting them dramatically increased their chances of obtaining a favorable outcome. Nike therefore became highly cautious about granting Blessings and worried that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction might exploit her after [[Aphrodite]] effectively negotiated Nike's Complete Blessing as part of the Goddesses' payment for protection.[2]
Victor's refusal to exploit her immediately relieved much of this anxiety. Although he insisted that Nike continue training and remain capable of protecting the faction, he promised not to force her into unnecessary conflicts. Nike accepted this arrangement, agreeing that she would defend the group if attacked while refusing to initiate wars herself.[2][7]
Nike is also compassionate. During Aphrodite's harsh argument with [[Demeter]], Nike objected to the unnecessary cruelty of Aphrodite's words even though she understood the logic behind them.[1] When the Amazons were massacred, Nike's immediate priority was assessing the number of casualties, understanding what had happened and helping survivors instead of pursuing revenge.[6]
She is fiercely protective of [[Hestia]]. When [[Hera]] attempted to exploit Hestia's kindness during the confrontation surrounding the Amazons, Nike refused before Hestia could be pressured into helping her and openly accused Hera of only treating Hestia like an older sister when she needed something.[5]
Nike also has a tendency to underestimate herself. When explaining her Divinity, she emphasized its weaknesses and limitations, prompting [[Tyche]] to remind her that the number of Beings capable of naturally countering Victory through higher Concepts was extremely small and that Nike should stop undervaluing her own Authority.[2]
Despite her restrained disposition, Nike is not devoid of pride. During the confrontation with [[Athena]], Hera and [[Artemis]] she confidently reminded them that the Goddess of Victory was standing with their opponents, making their chances of accomplishing anything extremely low.[5] She is also mildly narcissistic in a humorous manner; when silently wishing for Hestia to retain her kindness, Nike effectively prayed to herself because, in her own mind, there was no more appropriate God to invoke than the Goddess of Victory.[5]

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Nike first appeared during the final stages of the Greek civil war. [[Aphrodite]], having received information that [[Typhon]] had awakened, began attempting to evacuate Goddesses she considered either personally important or strategically valuable. She approached Nike alongside [[Hestia]] and [[Demeter]].[1]
Nike entered the conversation while Aphrodite was harshly explaining to Demeter that Agriculture would contribute little against Typhon. Although Nike understood Aphrodite's argument, she criticized her for speaking unnecessarily harshly to an emotionally distressed Demeter. Aphrodite then explained that Typhon's awakening had fundamentally changed the situation: what had previously been another [[Titanomachy]]-like civil war had become a potential extinction event for [[Olympus]].[1]
Nike understood the strategic implications immediately. Aphrodite specifically wanted Victory on her side and also intended to retrieve [[Tyche]], since combining Victory with Luck would give their developing faction an enormous advantage. Nike quickly realized why Aphrodite was attempting to gather multiple strategically unusual Goddesses rather than simply rescuing combatants.[1]
When Typhon's roar spread through Olympus, Nike and Hestia abandoned any remaining hesitation. Together with Aphrodite, Demeter and others, they gathered [[Rhea]], Tyche, numerous Minor Goddesses and several valuable resources from the Olympian treasury before fleeing to the territory controlled by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction. Their departure was catastrophic for [[Zeus]]: not only had Olympus lost dozens of Goddesses, but Concepts such as Victory and Luck were now actively supporting another faction.[4]
After reaching Victor's territory, Nike was formally introduced among approximately fifty Greek Goddesses. Victor recognized her as the Goddess of Victory, and she was identified as one of only a handful of refugees possessing a genuine Major Divinity in a Concept.[3]
The Goddesses were initially isolated from the rest of the faction while Aphrodite and Hestia attempted to reduce the arrogance many of them displayed toward mortals. During a later meeting concerning the Amazons, Victor identified Nike alongside Hestia, Aphrodite and [[Thetis]] as one of the refugee group's principal military assets.[2]
Nike then surprised Victor by giving him her Complete Blessing. Aphrodite had privately negotiated the action as part of the price for protecting the Olympian refugees, but Nike also justified the choice strategically: Victor had become the pillar holding their faction together, meaning his defeat would endanger everyone dependent upon him.[2]
The Blessing dramatically increased Victor's Courage, Determination and Confidence. More importantly, Nike explained that it could occasionally change an otherwise inevitable defeat into a positive result such as successful escape or even Victory. Sasha immediately recognized the ability as a limited form of Causality Manipulation, which Nike confirmed.[2]
Nike subsequently revealed the mechanics of her own full Divinity. Victory did not grant unconditional omnipotent success. Instead, it operated within the possibilities permitted by the reality surrounding her. If Nike fought a vastly stronger opponent such as [[Ares]], she would normally lose, but if the stronger opponent made a sufficiently exploitable mistake her Divinity could transform a smaller achievement—such as touching him with her blade—into the conditions required for her to obtain a valid "Victory".[2]
Ruby immediately realized the horrifying potential of this ability. If Nike possessed raw strength equal to or greater than her opponent, her Divinity could make overcoming her extraordinarily difficult because the power itself would continually push the confrontation toward a victorious result. Nike confirmed Ruby's interpretation while explaining that her full Victory Authority could not be transferred through a Blessing.[2]
She also disclosed the Divinity's limitations. Higher-order Concepts such as END, DEATH and DESTRUCTION can nullify Victory's influence, while the ability does not automatically protect Nike from unrelated powers such as Aphrodite's Charm or the corruptive influence of Hell.[2]
Nike explained these weaknesses because she was afraid Victor would treat her like an inexhaustible strategic weapon. Her fear was understandable: Gods had coveted her Blessings for ages, and Nike considered bestowing one extremely personal. Victor instead promised not to force her into wars, though he insisted that she become stronger so she could defend their faction if necessary.[2]
Victor consequently decided to train her. Nike strongly objected once she realized that Victor's interest in her abilities had awakened his battle-maniac tendencies, but Rhea calmly advised her to surrender because Victor would not abandon the idea. Nike reluctantly agreed to train with him.[7]
Nike and Hestia then accompanied the expedition toward the Amazons. Upon reaching their refuge, the two discovered the aftermath of an enormous massacre. Nike rapidly evaluated the battlefield and noticed something that initially escaped Hestia: many of the dead attackers were Low-Level Demons rather than forces individually powerful enough to defeat the Amazons.[6]
Drawing upon her understanding of Victory, Nike explained that large-scale conflicts are not decided purely by raw strength. Superior leadership, logistics, positioning and strategy can allow weaker forces to defeat individually stronger combatants. She therefore deduced that the Demons possessed an exceptionally competent commander orchestrating their attack.[6]
Instead of immediately joining [[Scathach]]'s confrontation with the enemy commander, Nike prioritized helping survivors together with Hestia.[6]
Nike later became involved in the standoff between [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], Natashia, [[Agnes Elderblood Snow]] and the Olympian Goddesses [[Athena]], [[Artemis]] and [[Hera]]. After the Countesses demonstrated that they could overpower the three Olympians, Nike bluntly reinforced the hopelessness of their position by reminding them that the Goddess of Victory was standing against them. Their chances of successfully taking the Amazons were therefore practically nonexistent.[5]
Nike openly rejected the idea of condemning the entire Amazon race for Zeus' mistakes and stated that Zeus and his inner circle could die for all she cared. When Hera attempted to exploit Hestia's kindness by asking to flee with them, Nike refused on Hestia's behalf, criticizing Hera for treating Hestia as family only when she needed something.[5]
The confrontation ended without Nike needing to engage directly, and the surviving Amazons were ultimately incorporated into Victor's growing faction.

#### King Among Kings
Following the Demonic War, Nike remained among the Greek Goddesses who permanently abandoned [[Olympus]] and integrated themselves into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s increasingly organized faction.
Although [[Rhea]] was eventually selected as the formal representative of the Goddesses within the newly established Council of [[Races (Flamel)|Races]], Nike remained one of their most strategically valuable members because of her Major Divinity and Blessing.[8]
By this point, her earlier concern that Victor's faction would exploit her had largely faded. She remained subject to the faction's expectations regarding training and defense but was not forced into aggressive wars merely because of the usefulness of Victory.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Nike continued living with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Divine faction after the destruction of [[Olympus]].
When Victor returned following his evolution into the Blood Dragon Primogenitor, the assembled Goddesses reacted with disbelief at the enormous changes that had occurred. Nike was particularly bewildered by the idea that someone could somehow progress from a Vampire into an actual Dragon, bluntly arguing that the "logistics" of such a transformation made no sense.[9]
Her confusion increased when she saw [[Metis]] Elderblood, whose Soul Victor had restored and whose existence had subsequently transformed into a Dragon Goddess. Nike immediately recognized Metis despite the drastic differences in her appearance and species, causing the other Greek Goddesses to focus upon her as well.[9]
When Victor announced that [[Zeus]] and Kronus had died, [[Demeter]] celebrated loudly. Nike, considerably more socially restrained, immediately pinched Demeter in an attempt to stop her from celebrating the deaths in front of [[Rhea]].[9]
Nike subsequently became part of the new Divine order built around Victor. The destruction of Olympus ended her old Pantheon permanently, while Victor's creation of Dragons Nest provided the former Olympian Goddesses with a new Pantheon and Divine home.

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Nike remained relevant even after the enormous rise in power surrounding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s civilization made ordinary Pantheon Gods comparatively weak.
During preparations for a covert operation involving another Pantheon, [[Scathach]] specifically proposed bringing Nike, [[Tyche]], [[Gaia]], [[Metis]], [[Ares]], [[Persephone]] and Nyx into the plan. Violet questioned whether Nike, Tyche and Ares could still provide meaningful assistance because they had not become Dragon Gods and were comparatively weak by the new standards of Victor's faction.[10]
Velnorah explained why Nike remained useful despite the difference in raw power. Nike's Divine Concept was exceptionally well developed, and Concepts such as Victory and Luck remained extraordinarily valuable in desperate situations even when their wielders were individually weaker than the faction's Dragon Gods. For the operation being discussed, Velnorah specifically judged Victory to be even more important than Luck.[10]

### Relationships

#### Aphrodite Elderblood
[[Aphrodite]] is one of Nike's closest Divine allies and the person responsible for bringing her into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction.
Aphrodite deliberately sought Nike during the [[Typhon]] crisis because she understood the enormous strategic advantage provided by having the Goddess of Victory on her side.[1] She later convinced Nike to give Victor her Complete Blessing as partial payment for the safety provided to the refugee Goddesses.[2]
Nike was not entirely happy about being used in this way. Giving a Blessing was deeply personal to her, and she feared Aphrodite's arrangement might establish a precedent in which Victor's faction continuously demanded more. Victor's response ultimately resolved those concerns.[2]
Despite this, Nike remained on friendly terms with Aphrodite and clearly trusted her enough to follow her away from [[Olympus]] during an extinction-level crisis.

#### Hestia Elderblood
[[Hestia]] is one of Nike's closest friends.
The two display a strong mutual understanding and were capable of communicating silently through exchanged looks while deciding whether to abandon [[Olympus]] after [[Typhon]]'s awakening.[1]
Nike is fiercely protective of Hestia's kindness. During the Amazon confrontation, she refused to allow [[Hera]] to manipulate Hestia into rescuing her and openly criticized Hera for recognizing Hestia as an older sister only when she needed something.[5]
Nike also accompanied Hestia during the mission to save the Amazons. Their complementary personalities were evident during the aftermath: Hestia provided emotional support and Divine protection while Nike analyzed the battlefield and determined how the Amazons had been defeated.[6]

#### Rhea
Nike treats [[Rhea]] with considerable respect, addressing her as Lady Rhea.[7]
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] insisted that Nike train with him, Nike desperately attempted to avoid the prospect. Rhea calmly advised her to surrender because Victor clearly would not give up until she agreed. Nike reluctantly accepted the advice.[7]
Rhea's senior position among the Greek Goddesses later helped stabilize the group politically, allowing Nike to focus more upon her own role rather than becoming involved in leadership disputes.

#### Demeter
Nike is friendly with [[Demeter]] despite their very different personalities.
During Demeter's argument with [[Aphrodite]], Nike criticized Aphrodite for treating the emotionally distressed Goddess too harshly.[1]
Nike nevertheless also restrains Demeter when necessary. After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] announced [[Zeus]] and Kronus' deaths, Demeter immediately began celebrating. Nike silently pinched her to remind her that [[Rhea]] was present and might not appreciate hearing one of her daughters enthusiastically celebrating the deaths of her husband and son.[9]

#### Victor Elderblood
Nike's relationship with [[Victor Elderblood]] began primarily as a strategic alliance.
After fleeing [[Olympus]], she gave Victor her Complete Blessing because he had become the principal pillar supporting the refugee Goddesses. Nike reasoned that if Victor lost, their new faction would likely collapse with him.[2]
[[Aphrodite]] had encouraged the decision, which made Nike initially suspicious that Victor might exploit her. She openly explained her Divinity, including several of its weaknesses, specifically because she did not want Victor to repeatedly send her into battle simply because Victory could manipulate outcomes.[2]
Victor assured Nike that he had no intention of forcing her to fight offensively. He only required her to remain capable of defending their people if attacked. The response noticeably relaxed Nike and became the foundation of genuine trust between them.[2]
Victor nevertheless insisted upon personally training her, much to Nike's horror once she realized how excited he was by the prospect of testing himself against the Goddess of Victory.[7]
Nike's Blessing remained active upon Victor and became one of the many permanent supernatural advantages accumulated during his rise.[2]
By the later Empire era, Nike remained one of the Gods whose Concept was considered valuable enough for Victor's family to invoke during strategic operations despite the enormous difference between Nike's raw power and that of the Elderblood Dragon Gods.[10]

### Skills & Abilities
- God Physiology: Nike is an ancient Third Generation Greek Goddess possessing a fully developed Major Divinity. As a Divine Being, she possesses the supernatural physical and spiritual characteristics common to high-ranking Gods.
- [[Immortality]] / Agelessness: Nike has existed for eons without suffering conventional biological aging.
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: As a Major Goddess and trained warrior, Nike possesses supernatural strength, speed, reflexes, durability, stamina and agility beyond ordinary supernatural species.
- [[Divine Energy]] Manipulation: Nike can produce and manipulate Divine Power through her Divinities.
- Divine Senses: As an ancient Goddess, Nike possesses enhanced supernatural perception capable of identifying Divine and Demonic phenomena.
- Regeneration: Nike possesses the regenerative capabilities inherent to Divine physiology.
- Flight: Nike is traditionally identified as the Winged Goddess; however, her wings have not been emphasized during the currently examined direct novel appearances.
- Major Divinity — Victory: Nike's defining and most powerful ability is her fully developed Major Divinity in the Concept of Victory.[3][2]
- Victory Manipulation: Nike can directly influence the conditions surrounding a conflict, competition or objective so that events move toward a valid form of "Victory" for herself.[2]
- Limited Causality Manipulation: Nike explicitly confirms that her Victory Authority operates as a small-scale form of Causality Manipulation.[2]
- Outcome Manipulation: Her Divinity can influence an ongoing sequence of events so that a viable victorious result becomes more likely to occur.
- Victory Condition Manipulation: Victory does not necessarily mean killing or overpowering an opponent. Depending upon the circumstances, winning can mean touching an opponent with a weapon, surviving, escaping, winning an argument, completing an objective or otherwise fulfilling a condition that qualifies as Victory.[2]
- Opportunity Exploitation: Against a stronger opponent, Nike's Divinity can exploit mistakes, openings or arrogance in order to create an attainable form of Victory even when direct physical domination is impossible.[2]
- Superior-Opponent Victory — Conditional: Nike uses [[Ares]] as an example. Ares is stronger and would ordinarily defeat her, but if he underestimated Nike or allowed a valid opening, her Divinity could capitalize upon that mistake and permit Nike to achieve a lesser victory condition.[2]
- Near-Guaranteed Victory against Inferior Opponents: Ruby reasoned that if Nike possessed raw power equal to or greater than her opponent, fighting her would become extraordinarily difficult because her Divinity would continually enforce favorable victory conditions. Nike confirmed Ruby's analysis.[2]
- Conflict Manipulation: Nike's Victory Authority applies to more than direct physical combat; she explicitly identifies contests and debates as situations in which Victory can be defined.[2]
- Strategic Victory Influence: The presence of Nike herself is strategically important enough that she could tell [[Athena]], [[Hera]] and [[Artemis]] that their chances of accomplishing their objective were extremely low simply because the Goddess of Victory stood with the opposing faction.[5]
Important: Nike does not automatically defeat every opponent. Her Divinity must operate within possibilities permitted by the Reality and circumstances surrounding her. Raw power differences remain relevant.[2]
- Complete Blessing of Victory: Nike can grant an exceptionally powerful Complete Blessing derived from Victory. She rarely grants it because of its value and the personal significance of a God bestowing a Blessing.[2]
- Courage Enhancement: The Blessing increases the recipient's courage.[2]
- Determination Enhancement: The recipient gains greater determination and resolve.[2]
- Confidence Enhancement: Nike's Blessing strengthens confidence even in already exceptionally self-assured individuals such as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[2]
- Indomitable [[Will Solace|Will]]: Through the combined increase in courage, confidence and determination, the recipient becomes substantially harder to discourage or mentally break.
- Limited Causality Manipulation: The Blessing possesses a weaker derivative of Nike's Victory Authority and can alter certain causal outcomes in the recipient's favor.[2]
- Defeat Mitigation: At specific moments an otherwise inevitable defeat can be transformed into something positive.[2]
- Escape Facilitation: A situation that should have ended in complete defeat may instead produce an opportunity to escape.[2]
- Conditional Victory Inducement: Under favorable circumstances, the Blessing can convert what should have been defeat into actual Victory.[2]
- Battlefield Fortune — Victory-Based: The Blessing does not provide ordinary Luck like [[Tyche]]'s Concept; instead, it specifically manipulates outcomes through the framework of Victory.
Important: Nike's Blessing does not grant another person her complete personal Divinity. Nike explicitly states that her full Victory Authority is exclusive to herself.[2]
- Victory Curse / Failure Inducement: Nike can invert the function of her Concept and curse a target so that they are unable to achieve Victory.[2]
- Permanent Failure Curse: Nike describes her curse as preventing the affected individual from ever again obtaining "Victory", causing their attempts and efforts to fail.[2]
- Causality Interference: Because the curse modifies whether a target can successfully accomplish an objective, it functions as another application of Nike's limited Causality Authority.
- Attempt Nullification: A God who attempted to sexually assault Nike was cursed with an inability to achieve Victory; the very action he was attempting consequently failed.[2]
- Objective Failure: The curse can theoretically affect more than combat, since Nike's Concept defines Victory broadly enough to include contests, objectives and other forms of success.
Note: The exact duration, removal conditions and maximum targets of Nike's Victory Curse have not yet been fully explained.)
- Strength Divinity: Chapter 622 identifies Nike as the Goddess of Strength in addition to Victory and Speed.[1]
- Strength Enhancement: As a Goddess possessing Divine association with Strength, Nike can reinforce her already superhuman physical power through Divine Energy.
Note: The novel has not yet provided a detailed individual explanation of Nike's Strength Divinity comparable to the extensive explanation given for Victory. It should therefore not be expanded into unsupported techniques.)
- Speed Divinity: Nike is likewise identified as a Goddess of Speed.[1]
- Divine Speed: Nike possesses supernatural movement and combat speed associated with her Divine nature.
Note: No examined chapter demonstrates Nike surpassing dedicated Speed specialists such as [[Hermes]], Natashia or later Victor. Her Speed title should not be used to invent unsupported scaling.)
- Blessing Bestowal: As a Major Goddess, Nike can grant Divine Blessings to mortals and supernatural beings. Her own Complete Blessing is so valuable that other Gods historically sought her favor specifically to obtain it.[2]
- Curse Bestowal: Nike can impose Divine Curses based upon the inverse application of Victory.[2]
- Tactical Analysis / Strategy: Nike possesses exceptional understanding of the conditions that determine Victory.
- Battlefield Analysis: Upon examining the Amazon massacre, Nike quickly recognized that Low-Level Demons had been responsible for many of the casualties and concluded that raw power alone could not explain the result.[6]
- Leadership Analysis: Nike correctly deduced that the Demonic army possessed a highly competent commander.[6]
- War Theory: Nike understands that large-scale war depends upon leadership, logistics, coordination, positioning and strategic decision-making rather than simply which side possesses individually stronger warriors.[6]
- Victory Condition Analysis: Her Concept gives her a natural understanding of the many different conditions by which a side can be considered victorious.
- Risk Assessment: Nike understood the hopelessness of [[Olympus]]' situation after [[Typhon]] awakened and chose evacuation rather than meaningless resistance.[1]
- Combat Ability: Although pacifistic, Nike is a trained Divine warrior.
- Self-Defense Training: Nike explicitly states that she learned to fight so she could protect herself from Gods who might attempt to exploit or attack her.[2]
- Defensive Combat: She willingly fights when herself, her allies or innocents are endangered.
- Demonic Combat: Nike's pacifism does not extend to Demons, evil creatures or malicious beings who harm others for sadistic purposes.[7]
- Titan-War Era Experience: Nike is an ancient Greek Goddess whose existence spans the major wars and political conflicts of Olympus.
- Continued Training: After joining Victor's faction, Nike reluctantly agreed to train under or alongside Victor so she would not stagnate and could defend the faction when necessary.[7]
- Divine Knowledge: Nike possesses extensive knowledge regarding Greek Gods, Divine Concepts, Pantheon politics and supernatural warfare acquired across her eons of existence.

### Power and Status
Nike is a Major Goddess and one of the principal military-capable Goddesses among the roughly fifty Olympian refugees who joined [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
Chapter 627 explicitly separates Nike, [[Hestia]], [[Tyche]], [[Demeter]], [[Rhea]], [[Thetis]], [[Aphrodite]] and the Panacea-related Goddesses from the many Minor Goddesses because these select individuals possessed fully developed Major Divinities.[3]
Victor later identified only four members of the refugee Goddess faction as immediately significant military assets:
- Hestia Elderblood
- Nike
- Aphrodite Elderblood
- Thetis[2]
Nike is nevertheless weaker in raw combat than several elite Olympians. She openly uses [[Ares]] as the example of a God who would ordinarily defeat her through superior strength.[2]
Her importance comes from the interaction between her personal strength and the extraordinary nature of Victory. A weaker Nike can manipulate circumstances toward limited victories; an equally matched or superior Nike can become an extremely difficult opponent because her Concept continuously influences viable outcomes in her favor.[2]
By Chapter 950, Nike is considered comparatively weak next to Victor's Dragon Gods, but her Victory Concept remains so strategically valuable that [[Scathach]] proposed using her in a major operation and Velnorah explicitly emphasized the usefulness of Victory despite Nike's lower raw power.[10]

### Notes
- Nike's principal confirmed Major Divinity is Victory.[3][2]
- Chapter 622 additionally identifies her as the Goddess of Strength and Speed, but neither secondary authority has received an extensive mechanical explanation comparable to Victory.[1]
- Nike's full Victory Divinity cannot be transferred to another person through a Blessing.[2]
- Her Complete Blessing nevertheless contains a weaker causal component capable of converting certain defeats into favorable outcomes.[2]
- Nike rarely gives her Blessing because it is both highly valuable and personally significant.[2]
- Male Gods historically sought Nike's favor because fighting or acting with the Goddess of Victory supporting them drastically reduced their chances of complete defeat.[2]
- Nike's own assessment of her power is noticeably conservative; [[Tyche]] directly told her not to underestimate herself.[2]
- Victory is not synonymous with killing an opponent. Nike can define Victory through successful escape, touching an opponent, winning a debate or completing another objective.[2]
- The higher Concepts END, DEATH and DESTRUCTION can override or nullify Nike's Victory effect.[2]
- Nike's Divinity cannot protect her from every unrelated ability, including [[Aphrodite]]'s Charm.[2]
- Although Nike identifies as a pacifist, she is not opposed to defending herself or killing genuinely dangerous enemies when necessary.[2][7]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] regarded Nike as one of the most useful Goddesses who joined his faction, alongside Aphrodite and [[Rhea]].[7]
- Her understanding of Victory gives her substantial strategic and military-analysis ability despite her preference to avoid warfare.[6]
- Even after the Elderblood faction reached Dragon-God levels of power, Nike's Concept remained valuable enough to be considered for major operations.[10]

- Nike refused to allow Hera to exploit [[Hestia]]'s kindness after Hera suddenly began calling Hestia her older sister when she needed protection.[5]
- When [[Demeter]] loudly celebrated Zeus and Kronus' deaths in front of Rhea, Nike quietly pinched her in an attempt to make her show some tact.[9]
- By the later Elderblood era, Nike had been surpassed enormously in raw strength by Victor's Dragon Gods, but her Victory Divinity was still considered exceptionally useful because of how advanced the Concept itself was.[10]
