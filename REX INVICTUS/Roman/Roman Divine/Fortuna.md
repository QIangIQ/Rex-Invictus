**[[Fortuna]]** is the Roman goddess of luck, the counterpart of [[Tyche]].

---

## Roman History (IRL)

Her cult was said to go back to King Servius Tullius. Her great sanctuary as Fortuna Primigenia stood at Praeneste, where an oracle drew lots carved on oak. Romans honored many aspects of her, such as Fortuna Redux for safe return and Fortuna Virilis. She is shown with a cornucopia, a rudder and a globe or wheel, the origin of the medieval Wheel of Fortune.

## PJO Canon

In [[The Son of Neptune]] the Feast of Fortuna is the deadline for the quest to free [[Thanatos]], because the Romans believed whatever happens on that day sets one's luck for the year.

## UOE (far future)

In the [[War of Princes]], the Heart of Calamity called in a high-level favor from Lady Fortuna herself.
