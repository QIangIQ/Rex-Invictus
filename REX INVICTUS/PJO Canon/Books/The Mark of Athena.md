**[[The Mark of Athena]]** (2012) is book 3 of [[The Heroes of Olympus]]. The Seven sail the Argo II to Rome. [[Annabeth Chase]] follows the Mark of [[Athena]] to recover the [[Athena Parthenos]] from [[Arachne]]. [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] and [[Annabeth Chase|Annabeth]] fall into [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]].
