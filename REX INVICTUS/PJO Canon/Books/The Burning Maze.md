**[[The Burning Maze]]** (2018) is the third book of [[The Trials of Apollo]] by Rick Riordan.

---

## PJO Canon

[[Apollo]], as the mortal Lester Papadopoulos, and his master Meg McCaffrey travel through the [[Labyrinth]] under southern California, which Medea and the emperor Caligula have set burning to trap the Erythraean Sibyl. With [[Grover Underwood|Grover]], Piper McLean and [[Jason Grace]] they free the Sibyl, but [[Jason (Argonaut)|Jason]] is killed by Caligula aboard the emperor's yachts. The book ends with Apollo bringing Jason's body to [[Camp Jupiter]], leading into [[The Tyrant's Tomb]].
