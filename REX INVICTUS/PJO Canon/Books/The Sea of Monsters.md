**[[The Sea of Monsters]]** (2006) is book 2 of [[Percy Jackson and the Olympians]].

## Plot
[[Thalia's Pine Tree]] is poisoned and camp's borders fail. [[Tantalus]] replaces [[Chiron]]. [[Clarisse]] gets the quest for the [[Golden Fleece]], but [[Percy Jackson|Percy]], [[Annabeth Chase]] and his Cyclops half-brother Tyson go too, to rescue [[Grover Underwood]] from Polyphemus. They pass [[Scylla]] and Charybdis, [[Circe]]'s island and the Sirens in the [[Sea of Monsters]]. The Fleece heals the tree and revives [[Thalia Grace]]. [[Luke Castellan]] commands the *Princess Andromeda*.

## Rex Invictus divergence
[[Percy Jackson|Percy]] takes the Fleece with future knowledge.
