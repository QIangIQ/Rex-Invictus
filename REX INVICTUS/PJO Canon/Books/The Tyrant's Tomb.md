**[[The Tyrant's Tomb]]** (2019) is book 4 of [[The Trials of Apollo]]. [[Apollo]] defends [[Camp Jupiter]] alongside [[Reyna Avila Ramírez-Arellano]] and [[Frank Zhang]] against the emperors Caligula and Commodus and the undead raised by Tarquin.
