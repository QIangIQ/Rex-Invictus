**[[The Trials of Apollo]]** (2016 to 2020) follows [[Apollo]], made mortal as [[Apollo|Lester Papadopoulos]], and his master Meg McCaffrey, a daughter of [[Demeter]], as they free the oracles from the Triumvirate of emperors (Commodus, Caligula, [[Nero]]).

## Books
1. *The Hidden Oracle* (2016)
2. *The Dark Prophecy* (2017)
3. *The Burning Maze* (2018), in which [[Jason Grace]] dies
4. [[The Tyrant's Tomb]] (2019), the battle of [[Camp Jupiter]]
5. [[The Tower of Nero]] (2020)
