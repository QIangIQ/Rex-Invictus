**[[The Lightning Thief]]** (2005) is book 1 of [[Percy Jackson and the Olympians]].

## Plot
Twelve-year-old [[Percy Jackson]] learns he is a son of [[Poseidon]] after the [[Fury]] [[Minotaur|Mrs. Dodds]] attacks him at Yancy Academy. With [[Grover Underwood]] and his mother [[Sally Jackson]] he flees to [[Camp Half-Blood]] and kills the [[Minotaur]]. [[Zeus]] accuses him of stealing the [[Master Bolt]]. [[Percy Jackson|Percy]], [[Annabeth Chase]] and [[Grover Underwood|Grover]] travel west, fight [[Medusa]], escape the [[Lotus Hotel and Casino]], and reach the [[Greek Underworld]] through Los Angeles. [[Hades]] has lost his [[Helm of Darkness]] too. Percy duels [[Ares]], who was manipulated by [[Kronos]], and returns both items. [[Luke Castellan]] is revealed as the thief.

## Rex Invictus divergence
In [[Rex Invictus]] the returned [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] wins [[Poseidon|the Master]] Bolt from [[Ares]] with future knowledge ([[Ch06 - Rex Natus III]], [[Ch07 - Rex Natus IV]]) and kills the [[Minotaur]] again.
