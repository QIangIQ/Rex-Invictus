**[[The Tower of Nero]]** (2020) is book 5 of [[The Trials of Apollo]]. [[Apollo]] and Meg defeat [[Nero]] in Manhattan and then Python at Delphi. [[Apollo]] regains his godhood. [[Reyna Avila Ramírez-Arellano]] joins the [[Hunters of Artemis]].
