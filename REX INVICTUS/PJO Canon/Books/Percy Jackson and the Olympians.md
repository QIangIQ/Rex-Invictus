**[[Percy Jackson and the Olympians]]** is Rick Riordan's five-book fantasy series (2005 to 2009) about [[Percy Jackson]], a son of [[Poseidon]], and the Second Titan War against [[Kronos]]. It is the canon base that [[Rex Invictus]] rewrites.

## Books
1. [[The Lightning Thief]] (2005)
2. [[The Sea of Monsters]] (2006)
3. [[The Titan's Curse]] (2007)
4. [[The Battle of the Labyrinth]] (2008)
5. [[The Last Olympian]] (2009)

## Companion and sequel series
- [[The Heroes of Olympus]] (2010 to 2014)
- [[The Trials of Apollo]] (2016 to 2020)
- [[The Sun and the Star]] (2023, with Mark Oshiro)
- [[The Chalice of the Gods]] (2023)
- [[Percy Jackson's Greek Gods]] (2014)
- [[The Demigod Files]] (2009)
- [[The Ship of the Dead]] ([[Magnus Chase]], 2017)

## Adaptations
Two films (2010, 2013), the Broadway musical *The Lightning Thief* (2019) and the Disney+ series (2023 onward).

## Key concepts
[[Camp Half-Blood]], [[Great Prophecy]], [[The Mist]], [[Demigod]], [[Big Three]], [[Olympians]], [[Titans]], [[Monsters (Greek)]].
