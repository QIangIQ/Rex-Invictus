**[[The Lost Hero]]** (2010) is book 1 of [[The Heroes of Olympus]]. [[Jason Grace]] wakes without memory beside [[Piper]] and [[Leo]]. They rescue [[Hera]] from the [[Wolf House]], where [[Porphyrion]] rises, after fighting [[Lycaon]]'s wolves with the [[Hunters of Artemis]] and [[Thalia Grace]].
