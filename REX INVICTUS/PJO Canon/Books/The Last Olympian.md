**[[The Last Olympian]]** (2009) is book 5 of [[Percy Jackson and the Olympians]].

## Plot
[[Charles Beckendorf]] dies blowing up the *Princess Andromeda*. [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] bathes in the [[Styx]] for the [[Curse of Achilles]]. While the gods fight [[Typhon]], [[Kronos]] attacks Manhattan ([[Battle of Manhattan]]). [[Silena Beauregard]] is revealed as the spy and dies a hero. [[Hestia]] shows Percy [[Luke Castellan|Luke]]'s past and keeps [[Pandora's Pithos]]. Percy gives Luke [[Annabeth Chase|Annabeth]]'s knife, and [[Luke Castellan]] kills himself to stop [[Kronos]]. Percy turns down godhood, asks the gods to claim their children and honor minor gods, and Rachel Elizabeth Dare becomes the new [[Oracle of Delphi]] and speaks the next [[Great Prophecy]].
