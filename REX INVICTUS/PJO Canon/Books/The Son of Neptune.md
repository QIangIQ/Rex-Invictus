**[[The Son of Neptune]]** (2011) is book 2 of [[The Heroes of Olympus]]. An amnesiac [[Percy Jackson]] reaches [[Camp Jupiter]], quests to Alaska with [[Hazel Levesque]] and [[Frank Zhang]] to free [[Thanatos]], recovers the [[Legio XII Fulminata|Twelfth Legion]]'s eagle and defeats [[Polybotes]]. [[Iris]] and the harpy [[Ella]] appear.
