**[[The Battle of the Labyrinth]]** (2008) is book 4 of [[Percy Jackson and the Olympians]].

## Plot
[[Annabeth Chase]] leads a quest into [[Daedalus]]' [[Labyrinth]] to stop [[Luke Castellan]]'s army from invading [[Camp Half-Blood]]. With [[Percy Jackson|Percy]], [[Grover Underwood]] and Tyson she meets the Sphinx, [[Hephaestus]], [[Calypso]] on [[Ogygia]] and the Titan [[Iapetus]]. [[Daedalus]], disguised as [[Quintus]], gives [[Annabeth Chase|Annabeth]] his laptop. [[Grover Underwood|Grover]] finds the dying god [[Pan]]. [[Kronos]] rises in [[Luke Castellan|Luke]]'s body. The battle at camp is won when Daedalus dies and the maze collapses.
