**[[The Titan's Curse]]** (2007) is book 3 of [[Percy Jackson and the Olympians]].

## Plot
[[Percy Jackson|Percy]], [[Annabeth Chase]] and [[Thalia Grace]] rescue [[Bianca di Angelo]] and [[Nico di Angelo]] from [[Dr. Thorn]] at [[Westover Hall]]. [[Annabeth Chase|Annabeth]] is captured. [[Artemis]] recruits [[Bianca di Angelo|Bianca]] to the [[Hunters of Artemis]], then disappears. [[Zoe Nightshade]], Bianca, [[Thalia Grace|Thalia]], [[Grover Underwood]] and Percy go west ([[Quest for Artemis]]). They fight the [[Nemean Lion]], meet [[Apollo]], cross [[Hephaestus]]' junkyard, where Bianca dies, and reach the [[Garden of the Hesperides]] and [[Mt. Othrys]]. Percy holds the sky. [[Zoe Nightshade|Zoë]] dies fighting [[Atlas]]. Thalia becomes [[Artemis]]'s lieutenant.

## Rex Invictus divergence
In [[Rex Invictus]] the quest is rewritten ([[Ch15 - Rex Invictus VIII]] to [[Ch19 - Rex Invictus XII]]). [[Bianca di Angelo|Bianca]] and [[Zoe Nightshade|Zoë]] both survive, the route passes [[New Rome]], [[Nicholas Flamel]] and [[Robespierre]] appear, and Othrys detonates.
