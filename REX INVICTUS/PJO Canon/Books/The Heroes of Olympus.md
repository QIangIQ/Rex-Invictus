**[[The Heroes of Olympus]]** (2010 to 2014) is Rick Riordan's sequel series to [[Percy Jackson and the Olympians]], about the Seven of the second [[Great Prophecy]] and the war with [[Gaia]] and the [[Giant (Greek)|Giants]].

## Books
1. [[The Lost Hero]] (2010)
2. [[The Son of Neptune]] (2011)
3. [[The Mark of Athena]] (2012)
4. [[The House of Hades]] (2013)
5. [[The Blood of Olympus]] (2014)

## The Seven
[[Percy Jackson]], [[Annabeth Chase]], [[Jason Grace]], [[Piper]], [[Leo]], [[Frank Zhang]], [[Hazel Levesque]].

## Key ideas
Greek and Roman demigods have separate camps, [[Camp Half-Blood]] and [[Camp Jupiter]]. The gods' Roman aspects ([[Jupiter]], [[Neptune]], [[Pluto]], [[Mars Ultor|Mars]]) have different personalities. [[Hera]] swaps [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] and [[Jason Grace|Jason]] to unite the camps.
