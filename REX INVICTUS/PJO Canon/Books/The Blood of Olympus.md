**[[The Blood of Olympus]]** (2014) is book 5 of [[The Heroes of Olympus]]. [[Reyna Avila Ramírez-Arellano]], [[Nico di Angelo]] and Coach Hedge carry the [[Athena Parthenos]] to [[Camp Half-Blood]] while hunted by the giant Orion, who kills [[Phoebe]] and other [[Hunters of Artemis]]. The Seven defeat [[Porphyrion]] at Athens and [[Leo]] fights [[Gaia]] in the sky.
