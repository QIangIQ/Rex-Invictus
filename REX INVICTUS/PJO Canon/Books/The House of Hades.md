**[[The House of Hades]]** (2013) is book 4 of [[The Heroes of Olympus]]. [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] and [[Annabeth Chase|Annabeth]] cross [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] with Bob ([[Iapetus]]) and Damasen, while the others reach the House of [[Hades]] in Epirus to close the Doors of Death. [[Hecate]] trains [[Hazel Levesque]] in the [[The Mist|Mist]], and [[Nico di Angelo]] reveals his secrets.
