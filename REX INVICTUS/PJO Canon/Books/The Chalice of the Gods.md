**[[The Chalice of the Gods]]** (2023) is the sixth [[Percy Jackson]] novel. [[Percy Jackson]] needs three recommendation letters from gods for college. With [[Annabeth Chase]] and [[Grover Underwood]] he recovers Ganymede's chalice, finds [[Iris]] innocent and contends with Geras. [[Sally Jackson]] gives birth to [[Estelle Jackson]].
