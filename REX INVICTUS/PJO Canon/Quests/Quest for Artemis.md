**[[Quest for Artemis]]** is the quest of [[The Titan's Curse]] to rescue [[Artemis]] from [[Atlas]] before the winter solstice.

## Prophecy
"Five shall go west to the goddess in chains, / One shall be lost in the land without rain, / The bane of [[Olympus]] shows the trail, / Campers and [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] combined prevail, / The Titan's curse must one withstand, / And one shall perish by a parent's hand."

## Members (canon)
[[Zoe Nightshade]], [[Bianca di Angelo]] (replacing [[Phoebe]]), [[Thalia Grace]], [[Grover Underwood]], with [[Percy Jackson]] following.

## Rex Invictus
The quest is rewritten in [[Ch15 - Rex Invictus VIII]] to [[Ch19 - Rex Invictus XII]]: [[Eleni Theodosia]] joins, the party passes through [[New Rome]], and both [[Bianca di Angelo|Bianca]] and [[Zoe Nightshade|Zoë]] survive.
