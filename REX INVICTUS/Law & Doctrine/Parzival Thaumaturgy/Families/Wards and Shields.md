**[[Wards and Shields]]** is a function family of the [[Spell Registry]] in [[Parzival Thaumaturgy]], with 31 workings. Workings that absorb, deflect or forbid harm: personal shields, armour fields, protective auras and warded spheres. In Parzival terms they are priced by absorption capacity at 0.20 Pz per point of injury held.
