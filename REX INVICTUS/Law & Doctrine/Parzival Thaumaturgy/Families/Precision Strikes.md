**[[Precision Strikes]]** is a function family of the [[Spell Registry]] in [[Parzival Thaumaturgy]], with 41 workings. Workings that deliver injury to one or a few targets by ray, bolt, touch or projectile. Single-target delivery is cheap per point of injury, so most of their draw is pattern.
