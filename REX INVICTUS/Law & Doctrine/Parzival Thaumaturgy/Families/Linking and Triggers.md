**[[Linking and Triggers]]** is a function family of the [[Spell Registry]] in [[Parzival Thaumaturgy]], with 11 workings. Workings that connect, store or release other workings: field threads, fuzes, contingencies and glyphs. They are the wiring of every combination in [[Combat Combination Analysis]].
