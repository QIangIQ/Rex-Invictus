**[[Cold and Ice Workings]]** is a element family of the [[Spell Registry]] in [[Parzival Thaumaturgy]], with 9 workings. Workings that deal cold injury, freeze or produce ice and frost. κ = 0.90 Pz per injury point, the highest in the table, since removing heat is costly.
