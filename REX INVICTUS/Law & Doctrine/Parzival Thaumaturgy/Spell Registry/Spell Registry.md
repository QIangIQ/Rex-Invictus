The **[[Spell Registry]]** is the catalogue of every documented working in [[Parzival Thaumaturgy]]: 319 source corpus spells, 107 from [[Science Spellbook Registry|The Science Spellbook]] and 19 Rex Invictus originals. Each working has its own profile note with an energy model, glyph, incantation, somatic kata, spectral signature, aspect vector, MINT and MCM profile and ULMTU compliance table.
