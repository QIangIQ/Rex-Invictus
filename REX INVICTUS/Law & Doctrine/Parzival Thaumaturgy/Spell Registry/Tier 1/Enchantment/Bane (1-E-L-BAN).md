**Bane** (`1-E-L-BAN`) is a 1st Tier working of the [[Enchantment]] school in [[Parzival Thaumaturgy]]. Source corpus spell (mechanics reference: System Reference Document 5.1). Its modelled draw is **6.3 Pz** against a slot quantum of 350 Pz, which puts its energy-equivalent tier at T_E -3.02 and its handling grade at **Low**.
