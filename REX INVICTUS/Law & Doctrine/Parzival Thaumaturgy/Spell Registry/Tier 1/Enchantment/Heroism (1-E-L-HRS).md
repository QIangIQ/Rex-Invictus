**Heroism** (`1-E-L-HRS`) is a 1st Tier working of the [[Enchantment]] school in [[Parzival Thaumaturgy]]. Source corpus spell (mechanics reference: System Reference Document 5.1). Its modelled draw is **10.7 Pz** against a slot quantum of 350 Pz, which puts its energy-equivalent tier at T_E -2.49 and its handling grade at **Low**.
