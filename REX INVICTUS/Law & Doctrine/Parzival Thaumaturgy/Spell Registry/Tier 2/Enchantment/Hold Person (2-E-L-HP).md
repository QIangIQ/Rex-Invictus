**Hold Person** (`2-E-L-HP`) is a 2nd Tier working of the [[Enchantment]] school in [[Parzival Thaumaturgy]]. Source corpus spell (mechanics reference: System Reference Document 5.1). Its modelled draw is **14.6 Pz** against a slot quantum of 951 Pz, which puts its energy-equivalent tier at T_E -2.18 and its handling grade at **Low**.
