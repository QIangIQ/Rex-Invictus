**[[Spell Components]]** are the three supports a caster uses to hold a working's pattern while it forms: the spoken word, the traced kata and a material focus. In [[Parzival Thaumaturgy]] they are the physical carriers of the [[Spell Attribute Set]].

## The three components

| Component | Carrier | Notation | What it holds |
|---|---|---|---|
| Verbal (V) | Sound | [[Spell Speech (Incantation)]] | The six attributes as a sound pattern |
| Somatic (S) | Movement | [[Somatic Notation (Gesture)]] | The glyph traced as a kata |
| Material (M) | Matter | Focus or reagent | A physical analogue of one attribute, usually the damage or effect |

The source corpus's reading is that components are memory aids. Rex Invictus keeps that reading and adds a field role: each component reinforces the pattern on its field minimum, so dropping one raises the risk of losing field gain or misfiring. A working with all three is the most stable.

## Material components

| Kind | Example | Energy role |
|---|---|---|
| Focus (not consumed) | a crystal, a rod, a holy symbol | Lowers pattern cost; no energy contribution |
| Analogue reagent (consumed, low value) | bat guano and sulfur for Fireball | Pattern anchor; negligible energy |
| Costly material (has a value) | a diamond for a resurrection | Holds part of the pattern so the caster does not have to; counts as a handling point |
| Consumed costly material | the same, used up | Stored pattern spent in the working; also a handling point |

A [[Mana Crystal]] is a focus that also stores field return and can stand in for a costly material.

## Missing components

| Missing | Effect |
|---|---|
| Verbal | Silenced casters can trace and hold a focus but lose the sound layer; over-curve workings fall back to their quantum. |
| Somatic | Bound casters can speak; complex workings (four layers or more of long chords) fail. |
| Material | Workings with a costly material cannot be cast; others cost one more handling point. |

Military kit replaces components where it can: throat-mic incantation, gauntlet-traced kata and loaded foci in weapon furniture.
