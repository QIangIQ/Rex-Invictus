## The corpus

All texts below are by The Gorilla of Destiny unless noted. They are used as reference for a fan work; no text is reproduced beyond short tables of values.

| Text | Date | Pages | Contributed | Recovery |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| *How to Write Spells* (Spell Writing Guide v4.3) | 27 Oct 2022 | 17 | Glyph method, 13-point layout, attribute order, key tables, duplicate dots | Full. The dictionary labels are image-only (pp. 14–17) and were read from the images. |
| *Spell Writing Guide: Dictionary* (v2) | 14 Mar 2023 | 93 | Runes for the standard workings | Full. The damage chapter's labels swap Area and Range; the drawings follow the guide. ST and MT mean Single-Target and Multiple-Targets. |
| *Spell Writing Guide: Dictionary* (new, v1) | 21 Jul 2024 | text only | Names and attribute lists | Partial. The 2024 keys are image-only and were not in the supplied copy, so the registry uses the 2022/2023 keys. |
| *The Spell Saying Guide* | 29 Oct 2023 | 9 | Sound tables and spoken names | Full. The generator matches all 311 published spoken names. |
| *Arcane Spectroscopy* (v3), with Clarissa Cavendish and The Morrigan's Champion | | 26 | Emission, diffraction and redshift theory; 297 profile plates; instruments | Full. The line-position rule was recovered from the plates and explains every measured line ([[Spectral Key Tables]]). Plates stop at Tree Stride. |
| *The Warlock's Spell Compendium* | | 29 | Ten aspects, interpolation, derived damage types, the Alnish evolution tree | Full text. Aspect glyphs are a custom font. Damage compositions beyond four are reconstructed ([[Aspect Theory of Magic]]). |

## Standard workings

The standard workings (319 in the registry) follow the System Reference Document 5.1 by Wizards of the Coast LLC, used under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence. Mechanical values (tier, school, range, area, duration, damage dice) come from it. Descriptions in the registry are paraphrased in-world summaries, and none of its prose is reproduced.

## Known gaps

- The 2024 dictionary's new keys could not be read.
- Wish and 21 other late-alphabet workings have no published plate; the registry computes their spectra from the rule.
- No source gives the aspect make-up of schools, areas, ranges or durations. The registry's school vectors are a Rex Invictus proposal.

## Canon precedence

Where the corpus and Rex Invictus disagree, Rex Invictus wins, then Nanomachines Flamel, then MTWABV. The corpus supplies method and data; the setting supplies units, laws ([[Universal Legum of Metaphysic-Thaumological Utilization]]) and cost.
