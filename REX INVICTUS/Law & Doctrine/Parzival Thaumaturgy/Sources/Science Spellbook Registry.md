The **[[Science Spellbook Registry]]** indexes the 107 workings of *The Science Spellbook* (The Gorilla of Destiny), a corpus of spells built on real physics, chemistry and biology. Each has a full profile note in the [[Spell Registry]]. Its *Supermassive Black Hole* is the only 10th-level working in the corpus and the reason the tier scale in [[Mana-Parzival Conversion]] continues past 9.
