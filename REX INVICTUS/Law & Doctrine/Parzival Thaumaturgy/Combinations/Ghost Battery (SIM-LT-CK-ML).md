**Ghost Battery** (`SIM-LT-CK-ML`) is a deception combination that baits enemy countermeasures. It is costed under [[Recast Tension and Combination Affinity]] and draws about **196 Pz** per use.

## How it works

[[Silent Image (1-I-L-SI)|Silent Image]] raises false caster figures at several firing points and [[Magelight (0-V-L-MGL)|Magelight]] gives them a decoy arcane luminosity. The real caster works under [[Cloak (2-I-L-CLK)|Cloak]] and moves by [[Misted Leap (2-C-L-ML)|Misted Leap]] after each real shot.

## Tactical use

Automated counter suites spend their pre-loaded trigrams on the decoys. At about 200 Pz it is the cheapest way to drain an enemy's counter stack before the real strike.

## Countermeasures

Decoys show luminosity without the k3 to k6 lines of a real working, so a spectrometer that waits for a full six-line match is not fooled. That wait is itself the gain: it slows the counter loop. See [[Magical Countermeasures and Magical Intelligence (MCM-MINT)]].
