**Periscope Eye** (`AE-LK-CK-SK`) is a low-cost observation combination for trench and urban reconnaissance. It is costed under [[Recast Tension and Combination Affinity]] and draws about **542 Pz** per use.

## How it works

[[Arcane Eye (4-D-L-AE)|Arcane Eye]] creates a sensor that [[Skyward (1-T-L-SKY)|Skyward]] holds 100 m above the line, [[Cloak (2-I-L-CLK)|Cloak]] hides it, and [[Magic Link (1-P-L-MLK)|Magic Link]] passes its view to a second observer, usually the battery fire controller.

## Tactical use

It gives a platoon a quiet hour-long periscope without a drone's radio emission. It costs about 540 Pz, most of it in the Arcane Eye pattern.

## Countermeasures

The cloaked eye is hard to see but its Link thread is a steady low-rate emitter. MINT teams hunt the thread with spectrometers and follow it back to the observer. See [[Magical Countermeasures and Magical Intelligence (MCM-MINT)]].
