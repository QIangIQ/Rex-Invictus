**Slipglue Field** (`SL-TI-GR-GL-GY`) is a five-working area denial combination that coats roughly a 40 m circle in a sticky film and can switch it to a frictionless one on command. It is costed under [[Recast Tension and Combination Affinity]] and draws about **1,171 Pz** per use.

## How it works

[[Summon Liquid (1-C-L-SL)|Summon Liquid]] cast at Tier 2 supplies about 630 kg of fluid. [[Transfiguration Inheritance (2-T-L-TI)|Transfiguration Inheritance]] binds the glue and grease properties into it in that order, with grease held dormant. [[Geyser (2-V-L-GYS)|Geyser]] throws the bound fluid out to a 20 m radius. The film first behaves as glue; when the caster releases the grease property through the binder, every coated surface goes slick at once.

## Tactical use

Defensively it pins an assault in the open, then turns a stair, ramp or trench parapet into a surface nobody can climb. Offensively it can be thrown onto a held position so that defenders lose footing at the moment of the assault. The whole field costs about a third of a standard 2nd-tier mage's reservoir, which matches the field reports.

## Countermeasures

The binder is the weak point. A counter with the `2·T·None` trigram pre-loaded costs about 48 Pz and leaves an inert puddle. Teams that expect counters cast the binder last and release it in the same second as the geyser. See [[Magical Countermeasures and Magical Intelligence (MCM-MINT)]].
