**Haze Screen** (`ML`) is the field use of [[Misted Leap (2-C-L-ML)|Misted Leap]] as on-demand smoke rather than as transport. It is costed under [[Recast Tension and Combination Affinity]] and draws about **82.5 Pz** per use.

## How it works

Each leap vents the folded field as two spheres of divine haze, one at each end of the hop. The haze carries a flame-inhibiting charge, so incendiary fires of mortal origin inside it go out, while divine fire keeps burning because its source satisfies Lex Fontis. Almost all of the draw goes to the haze; the hop itself costs under 0.1 Pz.

## Tactical use

A rifle section can lay a smoke line across a street by bounding through it, one leap per man, with no grenades carried. Because a single leap draws about 83 Pz over a second or two, the casting rate stays near the edge of Gaian-age ELINT detection when it is paced. Thermal sights still see through the haze; it defeats eyes, lasers and mortal flame.

## Countermeasures

A spectrometer classifies the k1 to k3 trigram `2·C·None` in the first half second. Counter teams usually let the leap finish and contest the haze instead, since a dispel of the haze costs more than the haze itself. See [[Magical Countermeasures and Magical Intelligence (MCM-MINT)]].
