**Magical Railcannon** (`SM-S-G-A-C`) is a kinetic killing combination that turns a conjured iron shard into an invisible hypervelocity bolt. It is costed under [[Recast Tension and Combination Affinity]] and draws about **730 Pz** per use.

## How it works

[[Summon Metal (1-C-L-SM)|Summon Metal]] draws a 0.25 kg shard that [[Sharpen (0-T-L-SHR)|Sharpen]] forms into a 12 mm bolt with a hard tip. [[Grease (1-C-L-GRS)|Grease]] puts a slip layer on it that sheds air drag, [[Accelerate (2-T-L-ACC)|Accelerate]] brings it to about 2,500 m/s, and [[Cloak (2-I-L-CLK)|Cloak]] hides it from sight and damps the luminosity that a fast working would otherwise shed. Its kinetic energy is about 780 kJ, which is close to a 30 mm cannon round.

## Tactical use

One caster can fire a bolt every six seconds or so, or build a stack of bolts on a [[Tripwire (0-A-L-TRP)|Tripwire]] and release them together on an Alarm timer as a volley. Splitting the iron into ten 25 g flechettes gives a shotgun pattern for close defence. A Blast and Alarm sealed in the bolt's core turn it into a delayed-fuze airburst round against troops in trenches or against drones.

## Countermeasures

Cloak damps emission, so passive spectrometers often see the bolt only at release. The counter is to the launch: a spectrometer that sees the `2·T·None` trigram of Accelerate building on a static object can counter it before release. See [[Magical Countermeasures and Magical Intelligence (MCM-MINT)]].
