**Skyfall Mortar** (`SM-BL-AL-SK`) is an indirect-fire combination for trench lines. It is costed under [[Recast Tension and Combination Affinity]] and draws about **1,247 Pz** per use.

## How it works

[[Summon Metal (1-C-L-SM)|Summon Metal]] draws a 2 kg casing around a [[Blast (1-V-L-BLS)|Blast]] filler with an [[Alarm (1-A-L-ALR)|Alarm]] proximity fuze. [[Skyward (1-T-L-SKY)|Skyward]] lofts the round 150 m and walks it out over the enemy line, then releases it to fall straight into the trench bay.

## Tactical use

It puts a plunging round into a trench that flat fire cannot reach, from a caster who stays under cover. It is slower than a real mortar but needs no tube and no ammunition train, and its signature is small until the blast.

## Countermeasures

The Skyward lift is the long, visible stage. A spectrometer sees `1·T·None` climbing with a metal mass under it, and countering the lift drops the round on the caster's own side of the wire. See [[Magical Countermeasures and Magical Intelligence (MCM-MINT)]].
