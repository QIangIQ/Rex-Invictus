**Anti-Mage Primer** (`SP-TI-MM-DM-SH-AC-BL-AL`) is a breaching combination that opens a hole in a mage's ward stack so that a real killing working can follow it through. It is costed under [[Recast Tension and Combination Affinity]] and draws about **1,838 Pz** per use.

## How it works

An [[Accelerate (2-T-L-ACC)|Accelerated]] [[Magic Missile (1-P-L-MM)|Magic Missile]] carries a bound package: a [[Splash (0-C-L-SPL)|Splash]] liner, [[Dispel Magic (3-A-L-DM)|Dispel Magic]], [[Shield (1-P-L-SHL)|Shield]] and [[Blast (1-V-L-BLS)|Blast]], tied together by [[Transfiguration Inheritance (2-T-L-TI)|Transfiguration Inheritance]] and set off by an [[Alarm (1-A-L-ALR)|Alarm]] fuze. On contact the blast collapses the liner into a Munroe jet. The jet's fluence is many orders above the areal strength of arcane ward, mage armour, shield and bulwark layers, so it punches a finger-sized channel. The dispel rides inside the jet and unknits the ward around the hole, the liner stays fluid and coats the channel, and the foreign shield braces it open so the target cannot close the gap for a round.

## Tactical use

The primer makes a killing working cheap. A [[Lance of Longinus (8-V-L-LL)|Lance of Longinus]] fired against an intact eighth-tier ward stack spends about 150,000 Pz on ward override; through a braced breach it needs only about 4,800 Pz. A [[Magical Railcannon (SM-S-G-A-C)|railcannon]] bolt through the same breach needs no ward budget at all.

## Countermeasures

Primers are the main reason UOE heavy casters run layered counter-counter suites. A spectrometer sees a composite trigram led by the carrier dart, then the binder. The defence either counters the binder in flight or re-knits the ward faster than the foreign shield can brace it. See [[Magical Countermeasures and Magical Intelligence (MCM-MINT)]].
