**Mire Line** (`SL-TI-GL-FC`) is a trench-denial combination that turns a trench floor into adhesive mud. It is costed under [[Recast Tension and Combination Affinity]] and draws about **1,077 Pz** per use.

---

## Hierarchical Placement

- [[Parzival Thaumaturgy]]
  - [[Combat Combination Analysis]]
    - **Mire Line** (this note)

## How it works

[[Summon Liquid (1-C-L-SL)|Summon Liquid]] cast at Tier 3 soaks the trench floor, [[Transfiguration Inheritance (2-T-L-TI)|Transfiguration Inheritance]] binds [[Glue (1-T-L-GLU)|Glue]] into the mud, and [[Fog Cloud (1-C-L-FC)|Fog Cloud]] blinds the bay while the defenders are stuck.

## Tactical use

Defenders in the bay cannot move to fire steps or exits, which sets up an assault or an artillery strike. It costs about 1,100 Pz for a 100 m trench sector.

## Countermeasures

The binder is again the weak link; a pre-loaded counter leaves only wet ground. See [[Magical Countermeasures and Magical Intelligence (MCM-MINT)]].
