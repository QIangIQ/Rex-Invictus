**Breach Stack** (`KN-SI-BL-LT-ML`) is an urban close-quarters combination for silent room entry. It is costed under [[Recast Tension and Combination Affinity]] and draws about **1,395 Pz** per use.

## How it works

[[Silence (2-I-L-SLN)|Silence]] goes up first so that nothing after it is heard. [[Knock (2-T-L-KNC)|Knock]] opens the lock and bar, [[Blast (1-V-L-BLS)|Blast]] goes off inside the room, and a [[Magelight (0-V-L-MGL)|Magelight]] driven to a 5 kW flash dazzles the occupants. The stack leader then enters by [[Misted Leap (2-C-L-ML)|Misted Leap]], and the haze covers the threshold for the rest of the team.

## Tactical use

It replaces a breaching charge, a flashbang and a smoke grenade with one caster and makes no sound outside the room. It costs about 1,400 Pz, so a 2nd-tier caster can run two entries before the reservoir runs low.

## Countermeasures

Most occupants cannot react inside the 5 s stack. Prepared defenders keep a pre-loaded `1·V·Thunder` counter for the blast and accept the light. See [[Magical Countermeasures and Magical Intelligence (MCM-MINT)]].
