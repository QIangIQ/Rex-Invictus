**Skymark Flare** (`MM-LK-AL-LT-SK`) is the standard marker round of Divine Counter-Artillery Battery crews that lack an Apollonian or Artemisian element. It is costed under [[Recast Tension and Combination Affinity]] and draws about **45.9 Pz** per use.

## How it works

A single [[Magic Missile (1-P-L-MM)|Magic Missile]] dart carries an [[Alarm (1-A-L-ALR)|Alarm]] reshaped into an impact fuze, joined to the payload by [[Magic Link (1-P-L-MLK)|Magic Link]]. A force dart has no mass to speak of, so it has no drop, no wind drift and no angular error inside its range. On impact the fuze releases a [[Magelight (0-V-L-MGL)|Magelight]] mote that [[Skyward (1-T-L-SKY)|Skyward]] lifts to 300 m, where it hangs as an aiming point.

## Tactical use

Crews with Mana Sensing acquire a hostile caster, and the marker turns that fix into a visible point for guns, drones and air. At under 50 Pz a round, a single 1st-tier caster can mark several targets per minute, and a six-flare shoot costs about 230 Pz with recast tension included.

## Countermeasures

The flare is cheap to kill but fast. Counter teams aim at the hanging light rather than the dart, since the light is a slow, bright, long target with the `0·V·None` trigram. See [[Magical Countermeasures and Magical Intelligence (MCM-MINT)]].
