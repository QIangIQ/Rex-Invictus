**[[Spell Evolution]]** is the drift of a working's aspects under sustained outside pressure, so that a line of variants descends from one parent pattern. The idea and its only worked example, the evolution of [[Magic Missile (1-P-L-MM)|Magic Missile]] into [[Eldritch Blast (0-P-L-EB)|Eldritch Blast]], come from the Alnish chapter of *The Warlock's Spell Compendium*. It applies [[Aspect Theory of Magic]].

## The Alnish account

Researchers in the hills near the Alnish Valley found a sealed site behind an obsidian door: a dead wizard on a throne, an orb full of endlessly circling Magic Missile darts, and a tome of hand-drawn rune blocks. Speaking with the remains by necropathy, they learned that a demon had commissioned the wizard to breed a new working. The darts were run through trial rooms ("the room of hells" among them), and the variants that survived each room were copied and run again. The finished product was Eldritch Blast. The demon's price was the wizard's soul, kept in the body with no death allowed, until the researchers dispelled the orb and released it.

The source's conclusion: a working's aspects are a real, heritable feature. Outside pressure can shift them one unit at a time, and a skilled caster can copy the result. The variants are not always stable, and the process needs a self-sustaining working to run long enough, which current theory says should be impossible.

## The tree

Each node is a six-row block of four aspects. Row 5 holds the patron's emblem in place of one aspect (in the Alnish copy, the university's emblem stands in). Changed aspects are marked in the source in red; they are in bold here.

| Node | Parent | Row 1 | Row 2 | Row 4 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Standard | none | i d F H | b j D h | j h j g | root (Magic Missile) |
| Tight | Standard | as parent | as parent | j **A** j g | Flexibility to Within |
| Piercing | Tight | as parent | as parent | j **A** j **B** | Extraspection to Cold |
| High energy force | Standard | as parent | as parent | j **f** j g | Flexibility to Energy |
| Heavy | High energy | as parent | as parent | j f j **H** | Extraspection to Rigidity |
| Slashing | High energy | as parent | as parent | j f j **B** | Extraspection to Cold |
| Unstable | Standard | **I** d F H | as parent | as parent | Hope to Hopelessness |
| Quasi-stable | Unstable | I d **f** H | as parent | as parent | Potential to Energy (rows 1 and 5) |
| Darkened bolt | Unstable | I **C** F H | as parent | as parent | Life to Dark |
| Stagnant dark bolt | Darkened | I C F H | as parent | **J** h **J** g | Movement to Stagnancy ×2 |
| Still dark bolt | Stagnant | I C F H | b **J** D h | J h J g | Movement to Stagnancy |
| **Eldritch Blast** | Darkened | I C **A** H | as parent | as parent | Potential to Within |

Rows 3 (G G A A), 5 (a, emblem, F) and 6 (h E D A) never change.

## Reading the rows

The source does not label the rows. The registry's reading:

- **Row 4 is the damage row.** It changes only in the branches named for damage (Piercing, Slashing, Heavy, High energy). This makes Magic Missile's Force read as 2j 1g 1h in this lineage, a variant of the registry Force composition ([[Aspect Theory of Magic#Damage types]]).
- **Row 1 is the stability row.** It changes in the Unstable, Quasi-stable and Darkened branches. Hope to Hopelessness destabilises the pattern; Potential to Energy partly restores it.
- **Rows 3, 5 and 6 are the frame.** They hold the tier, the patron link and the delivery, which the trials never touched.

Eldritch Blast is therefore Magic Missile's frame with a darkened, hopeless, inward-turned stability row. That fits its glyph and spectrum: Eldritch Blast and Fire Bolt share three of four plate lines, and it remains a Force working ([[Eldritch Blast (0-P-L-EB)]]).

## Evolution in Parzival terms

Each one-unit change moves the working's affinity vector. While a variant sits off its parent's field minimum, it pays tension in the same way as a recast does ([[Recast Tension and Combination Affinity]]). Once the new lattice point is stable it becomes its own minimum and the tension drops to zero; the variant is then a working in its own right and is registered as one.

| Stage | Cost model |
|---|---|
| Pressure (trial room, ward, hostile field) | the working's upkeep plus 1.05^N recast growth for each forced re-formation |
| Unstable variant | +χ link cost against the parent's vector until it settles |
| Stable variant | a new registry entry; ordinary budget |

UOE glyph engines use the same principle on a small scale: they run candidate counter-patterns against captured hostile spectra and keep the variants that close fastest ([[Magical Countermeasures and Magical Intelligence (MCM-MINT)]]).
