**[[Necromancy (School)]]** is one of the eight schools of [[Parzival Thaumaturgy]]. For necromancy as an Elder Magic tradition, see [[Necromancy]]. Workings on life force, death and the soul. Aspect lean toward Within, Dark and strong Death. Soul work carries the re-anchoring constant and falls under ULMTU-XV.
