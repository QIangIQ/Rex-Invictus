**[[Abjuration]]** is one of the eight schools of [[Parzival Thaumaturgy]]. Protective and negating workings: wards, counters, banishment and alarms. Aspect lean toward Within, Rigidity, Hope and Stagnancy, which is why abjurations hold still and resist change.
