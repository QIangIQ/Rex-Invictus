**[[Conjuration]]** is one of the eight schools of [[Parzival Thaumaturgy]]. Workings that bring matter, creatures or the caster across distance or planes. Aspect lean toward Beyond, Prodigious, Potential and Movement. Conjuration is the school that pays the translocation and conjured-matter terms.
