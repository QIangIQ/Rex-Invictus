**[[Enchantment]]** is one of the eight schools of [[Parzival Thaumaturgy]]. Workings that act on the will of a subject. Aspect lean toward Within, Introspection, Flexibility and Hope. Every enchantment falls under ULMTU-XIV and must leave a resistance path.
