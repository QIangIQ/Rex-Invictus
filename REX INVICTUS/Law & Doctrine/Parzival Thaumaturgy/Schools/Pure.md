**[[Pure]]** is an affinity class of [[Parzival Thaumaturgy]]. A Rex Invictus affinity class for raw field constructs with no school bias: force darts, force walls, field threads. The source corpus files most of these under Evocation or Abjuration; the registry tags them P when their only output is magical force.
