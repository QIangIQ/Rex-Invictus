**[[Evocation]]** is one of the eight schools of [[Parzival Thaumaturgy]]. Workings that release field return as raw energy: heat, cold, light, sound, force and lightning. Aspect lean toward Heat, strong Energy and Movement. Evocation has the highest field gain on area damage, so many evocations run over their tier curve.
