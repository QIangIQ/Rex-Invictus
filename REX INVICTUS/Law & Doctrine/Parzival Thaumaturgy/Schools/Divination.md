**[[Divination]]** is one of the eight schools of [[Parzival Thaumaturgy]]. Workings that gather information across distance, time or minds. Aspect lean toward Beyond, Light and strong Extraspection. Divinations draw very little energy and are priced mainly through their pattern and link costs.
