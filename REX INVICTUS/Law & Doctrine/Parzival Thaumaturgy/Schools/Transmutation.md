**[[Transmutation]]** is one of the eight schools of [[Parzival Thaumaturgy]]. Workings that change the properties, shape or motion of existing matter. Aspect lean toward Life, strong Flexibility and Movement. Transmutation binders reduce friction between other workings in a combination.
