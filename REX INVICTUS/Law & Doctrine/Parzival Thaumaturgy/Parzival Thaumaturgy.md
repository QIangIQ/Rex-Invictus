**[[Parzival Thaumaturgy]]** is the Rex Invictus science of magic measured in [[Parzival|Parzivals]]. It treats every working as a pattern held on the thaumic field: a set of six attributes that can be written as a glyph, spoken as an incantation, traced as a kata, seen as a spectrum and broken into aspects, and whose cost in Pz follows from what it physically does. It ports the University of Theoretical Magic corpus ([[Source Texts (Theoretical Magic Corpus)]]) and the standard workings into the setting's units and laws, under the [[Universal Legum of Metaphysic-Thaumological Utilization]].

Most standard damage workings draw far below their quantum (Magic Missile uses under 2% of its Tier 1 quantum). Area evocations such as Fireball run several times over it on field gain, which is why they grade Medium and why they are the main target of counter rings.

## Setting notes

- The UOE fields this system at scale: throat-mic incantation, gauntlet kata, loaded foci, and spectrometer-driven counter rings from line infantry up to the Armoured Titans ([[Magical Countermeasures and Magical Intelligence (MCM-MINT)]]).
- Gaian-age forces detect field draw through [[Electronic Intelligence (ELINT)|ELINT]] but cannot read attributes.
- Divine casters work the same patterns with a [[Divine Affinity]] lean; their [[Divine Power]] sets what may be countered at all (ULMTU-V).
