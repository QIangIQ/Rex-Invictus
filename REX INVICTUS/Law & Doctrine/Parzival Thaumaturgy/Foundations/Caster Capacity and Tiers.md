**[[Caster Capacity and Tiers]]** sets how much field return a caster can command in a day and what a tier means in Parzival terms. It turns the source corpus's spell levels and slots into a reservoir measured in [[Parzival]], so that tactics such as the [[Slipglue Field (SL-TI-GR-GL-GY)|Slipglue Field]] can be stated as a fraction of a mage's budget.

## Tiers

A tier is the depth of field minimum a caster can reach. A **Tier-T caster** can cast workings of tier T and below; a **Tier-T working** is supplied by a quantum Q_T = 350·e^(T−1) Pz. The tier of a working equals its source level, so the registry stays one-to-one with the corpus.

| Tier | Quantum (Pz) | Practical meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | 129 | Cantrips: unlimited minor workings, limited only by fatigue |
| 1 to 2 | 350 to 951 | Line infantry casters, field medics, sappers |
| 3 to 5 | 2,586 to 19,109 | Specialist and section-level casters; area evocations |
| 6 to 7 | 51,945 to 141,200 | Senior battle mages, demigods in their prime |
| 8 to 9 | 383,822 to 1,043,335 | Major divine powers, archmages |
| 10 to 13 | 2.84 to 56.96 MPz | Rex Invictus extension; strategic workings |
| 14 and up | 154.8 MPz and up | Above the 100 MPz ceiling of [[Universal Legum of Metaphysic-Thaumological Utilization\|ULMTU-XVI]]; needs the [[Répertoire du Savoir Athénien]] |

## Reservoir

A caster's daily reservoir is the sum of the quanta of the slots their tier grants. Using the full-caster progression of the source corpus at the first level where each tier opens:

| Caster tier | Slots (T1 to T9) | Reservoir (Pz) | Energy |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2 | 700 | 1.05 MJ |
| 2 (standard 2nd-tier mage) | 4·T1 + 2·T2 | 3,303 | 4.95 MJ |
| 3 | 4·T1 + 3·T2 + 2·T3 | 9,427 | 14.1 MJ |
| 4 | 4, 3, 3, 1 | 19,043 | 28.6 MJ |
| 5 | 4, 3, 3, 3, 1 | 52,212 | 78.3 MJ |
| 6 | 4, 3, 3, 3, 2, 1 | 123,266 | 185 MJ |
| 7 | 4, 3, 3, 3, 2, 1, 1 | 264,466 | 397 MJ |
| 8 | 4, 3, 3, 3, 2, 1, 1, 1 | 648,288 | 972 MJ |
| 9 | 4, 3, 3, 3, 2, 1, 1, 1, 1 | 1.69 MPz | 2.5 GJ |
| 9 (fully matured) | 4, 3, 3, 3, 3, 2, 2, 1, 1 | 1.9 MPz | 2.9 GJ |

Cantrips draw on the same reservoir but at a rate the field refills as fast as a caster can cast. Rex Invictus divine beings top their reservoirs from [[Ichor]] and [[Divine Energy]]; a demigod's reservoir is that of their caster tier scaled by their [[Divine Affinity]] factor (0.5 to 3).

## Sustained power

A reservoir is energy; a caster also has a maximum sustained draw. Field practice takes it as the largest quantum spread over one six-second cast: a Tier-2 mage peaks at about 160 Pz/s, a Tier-5 at about 3,200 Pz/s. Pacing below the [[Electronic Intelligence (ELINT)|ELINT]] detection threshold of 6 Pz/s means casting at a few percent of that peak.

## Mastery gradient

[[Universal Legum of Metaphysic-Thaumological Utilization\|ULMTU-XVIII]] requires a defined path from access to mastery. In Parzival terms:

1. **Access:** the caster can reach the tier's field minimum (draws Q_T).
2. **Competence:** the caster holds the pattern cleanly enough to take field gain on over-curve workings.
3. **Mastery:** the caster upcasts, writes new glyphs and runs combinations with low link friction ([[Recast Tension and Combination Affinity]]).

## Upcasting

A working cast in a higher slot is supplied by that slot's quantum. Its incantation takes the new tier's consonant plus *i* as a prefix ([[Spell Speech (Incantation)]]) and its glyph gains a new k1 layer.
