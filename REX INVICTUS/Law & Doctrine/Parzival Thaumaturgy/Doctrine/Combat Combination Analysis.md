**[[Combat Combination Analysis]]** is the costed catalogue of the combinations used or proposed for UOE and allied field casters. Each combination binds several low-tier workings into one effect that no single working of its tier provides. Every figure here comes from [[Recast Tension and Combination Affinity]] applied to the member workings in the [[Spell Registry]].

## The three field tactics

The analysis began with three tactics already in use. Ranked by cost they are Skymark Flare (46 Pz), Haze Screen (83 Pz) and Slipglue Field (1,171 Pz). The Slipglue Field costs about 14 Haze Screens or 25 flares.

- **Haze Screen** is cheap because a Misted Leap's hop costs almost nothing; the price is the conjured haze. Repetition is what grows it: ten leaps cost 20% more than ten single leaps.
- **Slipglue Field** is the only one with real mass (about 630 kg of conjured fluid) and five members. Its binder holds four links, and the Geyser contrasts with the binder.
- **Skymark Flare** is the cheapest per use. In a battery shoot tension, not the flares, sets the price: six flares a minute cost five times one flare.

## The five proposals

Five further combinations were designed for urban and trench fighting: [[Breach Stack (KN-SI-BL-LT-ML)|Breach Stack]], [[Skyfall Mortar (SM-BL-AL-SK)|Skyfall Mortar]], [[Periscope Eye (AE-LK-CK-SK)|Periscope Eye]], [[Ghost Battery (SIM-LT-CK-ML)|Ghost Battery]] and [[Mire Line (SL-TI-GL-FC)|Mire Line]]. They are proposals, not canon, until used in a chapter.

## The primer payoff

The Anti-Mage Primer exists because ward stacks multiply the cost of everything else. A [[Lance of Longinus (8-V-L-LL)|Lance of Longinus]] thrown against an intact divine ward stack draws about 154,835 Pz, most of it spent breaking wards. Thrown through the hole a primer opens, it draws about 4,835 Pz. The primer costs 1,838 Pz, so primer and lance together cost about 6,700 Pz, roughly 4% of the unprimed figure.

The same logic drives UOE doctrine against Armoured Titans and Behemoths: cheap layered breachers first, the killing working second ([[Magical Countermeasures and Magical Intelligence (MCM-MINT)]]).

## Kinetic combinations and MCM

The Magical Railcannon is the attacker's answer to strong countermeasures. Its working ends when the bolt leaves the rails; after that the bolt is iron moving at about 2,500 m/s and a counter has nothing to unmake. A defender must counter it during the build, which [[Cloak (2-I-L-CLK)|Cloak]] makes hard to see.

## Reading a combination note

Each note gives the member table (tier, base Pz, N_eff, tension factor, charged Pz), the link table (cos θ, χ, binder, Λ), the total and its T_E, reservoir shares, variants, tactical use, countermeasures and ULMTU notes. Registry IDs take the form `X-T-code` ([[Spell Registry Identifier Standard#Combination tags]]).
