**[[Practical Magical Combat]]** sets out how mages actually fight in Rex Invictus once both sides understand [[Parzival Thaumaturgy]]. Magic is fantastical and dangerous, but the enemy holds the same tools. Every gimmick has a counter, every counter has a price in Parzivals, and every working leaves a physical signature. The result is cautious, information-driven fighting closer to modern combat than to duels decided by tier. This note records the author's rulings of 16 November 2026.

## Tier is not destiny

Older schools of fighting assumed that the higher-tier caster wins, so a lower-tier mage's chances were written off. Parzival Thaumaturgy broke that assumption. A caster who understands a working down to its attributes, aspects and energy terms can turn a cheap working into a lethal one, and a caster who understands the enemy's working can unmake it for a fraction of its cost.

Mastery means exploiting the basis of the system. It does not mean owning the biggest spell.

### Exploiting basic workings

| Base working | Exploit | What stops it |
|---|---|---|
| [[Geyser (2-V-L-GYS)\|Geyser]] | Driven through a target's blood and out of the body | ULMTU-XIV: a working inside another being contests its will, so the draw rises steeply against a resisting mage; wards and Mage Armor sit in the way |
| [[Glue (1-T-L-GLU)\|Glue]] with [[Transfiguration Inheritance (2-T-L-TI)\|Transfiguration Inheritance]] | Blood turned adhesive | The same will contest; a pre-loaded counter costs 0.05 × Q_T; the binder is a single point of failure |
| [[Magic Missile (1-P-L-MM)\|Magic Missile]] with a guidance working | A dart that tracks, or glue that follows a target | Every added member is another link cost and another line in the spectrum |

These exploits are why a well-read Tier 2 mage is a real threat to a careless Tier 5. They are also why the counters below exist.

### Magic Missile in Rex Invictus

The standard dart flies straight and bursts on impact. It does not track; the source's never-miss behaviour is not canon. Under [[Universal Legum of Metaphysic-Thaumological Utilization|ULMTU-XII]] a caster of far greater knowledge, affinity or power can undo or alter it outright, so Magic Missile weakens sharply in high-tier combat. Its strengths are cost, flat trajectory and use as a carrier ([[Skymark Flare (MM-LK-AL-LT-SK)|Skymark Flare]], [[Anti-Mage Primer (SP-TI-MM-DM-SH-AC-BL-AL)|Anti-Mage Primer]]).

## The counterplay chain

1. **Identify before release.** The hard part of countering is reading what the mage is casting before the working closes. This is the job of MINT ([[Arcane Spectrometer]]).
2. **Counter.** The clean counter is an anti-phase copy of the same pattern. The crude counter is an opposed working: fire against ice, warmth against cold. In aspect terms it pushes the field toward the opposite pole ([[Aspect Theory of Magic]]); it costs more than an anti-phase counter because it must match energy as well as pattern.
3. **Deceive.** The attacker masks the real working. A typical package: an [[Silent Image (1-I-L-SI)|illusion]] and [[Cloak (2-I-L-CLK)|Cloak]] hide the real circle while a decoy [[Fireball (3-V-M-FRB)|Fireball]] forms in plain view. A defender who counters the decoy has spent a chamber of the counter ring on nothing and has fed the attacker's plan. Catching the masked working needs an extra dispel and more Parzivals.
4. **Escalate.** Each layer can be answered by another: MCM, MCCM, MCCCM and beyond ([[Magical Countermeasures and Magical Intelligence (MCM-MINT)#MCM: the counter ladder]]).

### "Doesn't it come down to who runs out of mana first?"

Only between two casters who read each other perfectly and never deceive. In practice decoys, cloaks, kinetic workings and exchange ratios decide the fight first. A counter against an evocation costs about a hundredth of the evocation, so massed evocation is a poor trade, and the side that reads better and baits better wins long before either reservoir is empty.

### "Doesn't this make cloaking too strong?"

Cloaking is strong. It cannot fake physics. A working must draw energy (ULMTU-I), and drawing energy emits light ([[Arcane Spectroscopy]]): a damped working still shows at release, from a second angle, or as unexplained draw. Long, bright or fast workings are the hardest to hide. Cloaking buys seconds and doubt, not invisibility.

## The perception economy

### Active and passive workings

Under ULMTU-IV a magical effect either endures on its own or ceases without support. Keeping an awareness working open (a "third eye" that reads every casting nearby) is a consistent use of an ability. It is active, so it must be fed for as long as it runs. A passive working such as a [[Shield (1-P-L-SHL)|Shield]] can be bound to prepared materia and wait at the dormant rate (1% of active, rule C4 of [[Parzival Energy Budget]]).

| Always-on sensing | Draw per hour | Draw per day | Share of a Tier 5 reservoir per day |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[Detect Magic (1-D-L-DMT)\|Detect Magic]] (recast every 10 min) | about 35 Pz | about 850 Pz | 1.6% |
| [[Arcane Eye (4-D-L-AE)\|Arcane Eye]] | about 270 Pz | about 6,500 Pz | 12% |
| [[True Seeing (6-D-L-TSU)\|True Seeing]] | about 800 Pz | about 19,300 Pz | 37% |

Sustained sensing has two further costs. It is a steady emitter that hostile MINT can track, and under ULMTU-XIII it decays and must be re-formed, which adds recast tension in a fight.

### Ways to make perception cheaper

| Method | How it works | Cost | Weakness |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bound to materia | The working sits dormant in a prepared object and wakes on demand | 1% of active rate while idle | The object can be found, countered or destroyed |
| Homunculus | A crafted body, of any shape, holds the working; the mage communes with it | Communion draws less than holding the working directly | Fragile to MCM and to plain destruction; no sentience, so it cannot judge whether what it sees is real and falls to illusions of equal or higher tier, even with divination |
| Inscribed tattoos | Perception sigils in the skin | Behave like runes: the symbols persist but need energy to activate | Each activation is a visible draw; the symbols can be read |
| Imbued glass eye | A crafted eye replaces a real one | Needs an active working or a permanent transfiguration (costly) to see at all, plus communion | Silica has no receptors; without the working it is glass. It must also be protected like any other focus |

The mage who builds these is not cheating. They are paying up front, in materia and preparation, for what another pays in Parzivals during the fight.

## Why mages are not simply assassins

A covert kill (teleporting a heart out of a body, for example) is easy against a mortal. Against a mage of equal power it is a chain of costly, detectable steps:

1. **Find the target.** The defender's side runs passive curios that only need recharging, and hidden high-tier mages hold wide-area detection and protection. Friendly lines such as a trench may also have divine countermeasures.
2. **Mark the target.** The marker must be bright enough for friendly sensors and dim enough to stay under enemy detection thresholds.
3. **Move the object.** Remote translocation draws heavily (a [[Teleport (7-C-L-TLP)|Teleport]] is about 73,000 Pz), and a moving target adds terms.
4. **Breach.** The working must bore through layered wards.
5. **Withdraw.** It must break back out without counterspell interference, backlash or counter-tracking.
6. **Pay for safety.** Matching materiae must be spent to avoid backlash (ULMTU-XI), and the caster still needs self-protection while doing all of this.

A caster strong enough to do all of this and stay protected is so far above the target that a direct kill would be cheaper. If an array of several mages shares the load, the concentrated draw leaks a signature that MINT catches at once. Stealth remains powerful, but it is expensive, and the enemy is waiting for exactly that mistake.

## Against modern weapons

Conventional munitions are physics with no pattern to counter, so the mage defends with detection, standoff and hardening rather than counterspells. The worked case is the [[Mist Picket (FC-TI-TR-AL)|Mist Picket]] against a Hellfire-class missile. It shows three lessons:

- A 425 m/s missile gives a third of a second of warning at 150 m, so the whole defence must be pre-drawn and released by a trigger.
- A shaped-charge jet goes through ward stacks, so the round must be set off early.
- Saturation defeats the picket just as it defeats a counter ring.

Munitions blessed by a divine patron are a counter-countermeasure in their own right.

## The real-war analogy

Modern firefights are cautious: blind fire around corners, grenades before entry, and each side waiting for the other to slip. Magical combat works the same way. If you know the range of the enemy's artillery and its counter-battery envelope, you stay just outside it, and the enemy must pay in time, cost or exposure to move forward. Information asymmetry decides who gets caught in the open. Magic raises the stakes; it does not repeal the logic.

## Design standard

The aim is that every apparent disparity has a reasonable execution cost or a corresponding weakness. Before any new working or combination is integrated, it must satisfy three checks:

1. **Magical spectroscopy.** Its lines, brightness and detectability are defined ([[Arcane Spectroscopy]]).
2. **Spell circle standardization.** It has a glyph, kata, incantation and registry tag ([[Spell Breakdown Procedure]]).
3. **Magical calculus.** Its energy, tension, link costs and counter costs are computed ([[Parzival Energy Budget]], [[Recast Tension and Combination Affinity]]).

Its likely MCM, MCCM and MCCCM interactions are worked out at the same time.
