**[[Anabasis Accord]]** is the treaty agreed at the end of [[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV]] between the Olympian Council and the Pyrrhuiesian Command of [[Marien Phyrrus]], "in the presence of the [[Legio XII Fulminata]] and the Director of [[Camp Half-Blood]]". It follows the Battle of Long Island and the [[CODE 18]] verdict that recognized [[Percy Jackson]] as [[Percy Jackson|Lord Imperator]].

## Articles
1. **I.** The Command stays on Mars and does not come to Earth without leave. [[Ares]] is patron of the garrison.
2. **II.** Concealment from mortals, with [[Hecate]] and [[The Mist]].
3. **III.** Water for Mars from [[Poseidon]].
4. **IV.** [[Poseidon]] provides a [[Helmsman]], [[Triton]], for supply and defence vessels only.
5. **V.** [[Artemis]], as Director of Anti-Espionage and Celestial Surveillance, is sole liaison.
6. **VI.** The [[Legio XII Fulminata|Legio XII]] is "the military partner of the Command upon Earth", with joint operations under Roman and Imperial officers. [[Artemis]] invokes it at once for the Brooks Range operation to recover [[Ekdromoi Cell Kappa-Seven]]'s dropship.
7. **VII.** Mutual defence against [[Kronos]] and [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]].
8. **VIII.** [[Hades]] keeps the Imperial dead and their names, and will build them "a Wall" on the [[Styx]] shore.
9. **IX.** The Council helps find a road home through [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]].
10. **X.** "Any breach... is a breach by [[Olympus]]."

## Signatories and parties
[[Zeus]], [[Hera]], [[Poseidon]], [[Demeter]], [[Athena]], [[Apollo]], [[Artemis]], [[Ares]], [[Aphrodite]], [[Hephaestus]], [[Hermes]], [[Dionysus]], [[Hades]], [[Hestia]] (tending the hearth), [[Reyna Avila Ramírez-Arellano]] for the legion, and [[Marien Phyrrus|Marien Pyrrhus]].

## Name
*Anabasis* (Greek "going up", a march inland) is the title of Xenophon's account of the Ten Thousand, Greek mercenaries stranded deep in Persia in 401 BC who fought their way home. The four million stranded on Mars are in the same position.
