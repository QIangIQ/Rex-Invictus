**[[Fading]]** is a figure of Rex Invictus lore. (Lore adapted from *The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** Flamel material is adapted into the Rex Invictus present (c. 2021). Rex Invictus wins any conflict; [[Robespierre]] fills [[Dr. John Dee]]'s role and hunts the Philosopher's Stone rather than [[The Codex]]. See [[Flamel Series#Integration rules]].

---

## Flamel Series Canon (SOFTINF)

> Fading is a process in which [[Elders]] die when a Shadowrealm that has been bound to a being's life-force (such as [[Hecate|Hekate]] and her Shadowrealm) is destroyed. They cannot be restored to life, though they can be contacted using [[Necromancy]] for a short time after their death.
> Contrary to popular belief, Hekate was not killed this way; she was crushed under by the shards [[Yggdrasil]] after it was shattered after being stabbed by [[Excalibur]] (Yggrasil also stretched across multiple [[Shadowrealms]]; the tree may have been bound to her power but it was not the source of it).
