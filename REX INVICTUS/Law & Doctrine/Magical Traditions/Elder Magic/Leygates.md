**[[Leygates]]** is a figure of Rex Invictus lore. (Lore adapted from *The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** Flamel material is adapted into the Rex Invictus present (c. 2021). Rex Invictus wins any conflict; [[Robespierre]] fills [[Dr. John Dee]]'s role and hunts the Philosopher's Stone rather than [[The Codex]]. See [[Flamel Series#Integration rules]].

---

## Flamel Series Canon (SOFTINF)

> A Leygate is a sort of portal that Sorcerers and Magicians can open at crossings of the [[Ley lines]] and use to travel large distances or to different [[Shadowrealms]] instantly in the Secret Of The Immortal [[Nicholas Flamel]] Series. The [[Swords of Power]] ([[Excalibur]], [[Clarent]], [[Joyeuse]], and [[Durendal]]) can create them anywhere when all four are used together.
