**[[Warlocks]]** is a figure of Rex Invictus lore. (Lore adapted from *The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** Flamel material is adapted into the Rex Invictus present (c. 2021). Rex Invictus wins any conflict; [[Robespierre]] fills [[Dr. John Dee]]'s role and hunts the Philosopher's Stone rather than [[The Codex]]. See [[Flamel Series#Integration rules]].

---

## Flamel Series Canon (SOFTINF)

> Warlocks, in the old language of [[Danu Talis]] also called waerloga what literally means oath breaker, are those who have broken their word, for good or for bad, or immortals who disobeyed their Elder masters, often Magicians, Sorcerers, Alchemysts, or some other form of [[Magic]] user.

### Known Warlocks
- [[Niccolo Machiavelli]]
- [[Dr. John Dee]]
- [[Billy the Kid]]
- [[Mars Ultor]]
