**[[Air magic]]** is a figure of Rex Invictus lore. (Lore adapted from *The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** Flamel material is adapted into the Rex Invictus present (c. 2021). Rex Invictus wins any conflict; [[Robespierre]] fills [[Dr. John Dee]]'s role and hunts the Philosopher's Stone rather than [[The Codex]]. See [[Flamel Series#Integration rules]].

## Flamel Series Canon (SOFTINF)

> "It is said that the [[Magic]] of Fire, Water, or even Earth is the strongest magic. They are wrong. The Magic of Air surpasses all others."
> — [[Zephaniah|Witch of Endor]] upon [[Sophie Newman|Sophie]]'s Training
> Air magic is one of the four main elemental magics, along with Fire, Earth, and [[Water magic]]. Masters of air are capable of creating and manipulating air and winds using auric energy to incredible effect. Mastering air magic typically takes lifetimes of study, however those who have are among the most powerful wielders of magic in the Myriad [[Shadowrealms]]. In addition to wielding massive destructive power, air magic can be used for the power of flight and to control the weather.

### The Alchemyst
The [[Zephaniah|Witch of Endor]] trains [[Sophie Newman]] in air magic. The magic is immediately useful for fending off [[Dr. John Dee|Dee]]'s undead army in [[Ojai]] - Sophie summons tornadoes to try and blow the skeletal attackers away, though she becomes too tired to control the twisters before the army is destroyed.

### The Magician
Immediately after arriving in [[Paris]], [[Nicholas Flamel|Nicholas]], [[Josh Newman|Josh]], [[Sophie Newman|Sophie]], and [[Scathach]] are surrounded by the French Police. To escape, Sophie uses air magic to generate a fog when trying to create a distraction police in, allowing their escape. Air magic is again useful when fighting a gargoyle army created by [[Dr. John Dee|Dee]] and [[Niccolo Machiavelli|Machiavelli]]: Sophie, enhanced by the strength of her brother's aura, combines Air and [[Fire magic]] to rapidly heat and cool the stone creatures, shattering them.

### Powers & Abilities
Masters of air can manipulate the substance in many ways, including:
Master of Air
- Aeromancy: Masters of air can greatly compress and decompress air. Air can be manipulated to form tornadoes, walls, and shields. Highly pressurized air can be shaped into blades, orbs, blasts, and explosions.
- Exnomophis: Masters of air can alter the balance of the components that make up air.
- Flight: Masters of air can fly by manipulating air currents.
- Manipulation of Weather: Masters of air can create powerful tornadoes, hurricanes, twisters, whirlwinds etc.
- Thermokinesis: Masters of air can change the temperature of air, either freezing it or heating it to extreme temperatures.
- Typhokinesis: Masters of air can manipulate air particulates, such as smoke, mist, and clouds.
