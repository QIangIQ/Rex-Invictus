**[[Elemental magic]]** is a figure of Rex Invictus lore. (Lore adapted from *The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** Flamel material is adapted into the Rex Invictus present (c. 2021). Rex Invictus wins any conflict; [[Robespierre]] fills [[Dr. John Dee]]'s role and hunts the Philosopher's Stone rather than [[The Codex]]. See [[Flamel Series#Integration rules]].

---

## Flamel Series Canon (SOFTINF)

> Elemental magic is one of the most powerful forms of magic. Air, Water, Fire, and [[Earth magic]] are the classical Aristotelian elements, though the magic of Time is usually considered a form of elemental magic as well. Few beings are capable of controlling even one of the elements, usually taking several lifetimes to understand completely. Those who have studied these elements and learned them to their potential are called masters, and are considered to be among the most powerful beings in the Myriad [[Shadowrealms]].
