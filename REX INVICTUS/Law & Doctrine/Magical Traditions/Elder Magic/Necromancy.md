**[[Necromancy]]** is a figure of Rex Invictus lore. (Lore adapted from *The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** Flamel material is adapted into the Rex Invictus present (c. 2021). Rex Invictus wins any conflict; [[Robespierre]] fills [[Dr. John Dee]]'s role and hunts the Philosopher's Stone rather than [[The Codex]]. See [[Flamel Series#Integration rules]].

---

## Real-World Background (IRL)

Necromancy is magic that communicates with the dead, often to learn the future; Odysseus' summoning of the shades in *Odyssey* Book 11 is an early example.

---

## Flamel Series Canon (SOFTINF)

> A Necromancer is a [[Magician (Flamel)|Magician]] who can use Necromancy, the ability to raise, communicate, and control the dead. There are multiple known necromancers in the series, including: [[Dr. John Dee]] and [[Josh Newman]], and others that have shown some abilities in that area, such as [[Nicholas Flamel|Perenelle Flamel]]. Dee was taught necromancy by his teacher, [[Roger Bacon]], also a necromancer. They aren't as common as other kinds of magicians because it is considered a very dark area of magic.
