**[[Magician (Flamel)]]** is a figure of Rex Invictus lore. (Lore adapted from *The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** Flamel material is adapted into the Rex Invictus present (c. 2021). Rex Invictus wins any conflict; [[Robespierre]] fills [[Dr. John Dee]]'s role and hunts the Philosopher's Stone rather than [[The Codex]]. See [[Flamel Series#Integration rules]].

---

## Flamel Series Canon (SOFTINF)

> This article is about those who use their aura to tap into mystic powers. For the book in the series of the same title, see [[The Magician]].
> A magician is a practitioner of the mystic arts. They can open [[Leygates]] at crossing of the Ley Lines. Their powers resemble those of a Sorcerer.

### Powers and Abilities
The powers and strengths of magicians vary depending upon the knowledge and strength of the individual, but powers may include:
Magician
- Practitioner of the Magical Arts: The powers wielded by magicians are never clearly distinguished from those of a Sorcerer or from other magic users, but known Magicians are able to create servants such as [[Golems]] and [[Tulpa]], and are able to use [[Scrying]].
