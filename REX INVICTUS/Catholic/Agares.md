Agares is a high-ranking Demon, one of the 72 Dukes of Hell, and the Second Key of Solomon. Formerly one of [[Diablo]]'s most dangerous subordinates, Agares stood among the highest-ranking Pillar Demons of Hell and was particularly feared because of his unusual ability to move through another plane of existence.[1]
Rather than relying only on overwhelming Demonic power, Agares specialized in assassination and interdimensional movement. His ability allowed him to effectively disappear from the normal world, approach his target from an inaccessible plane and remanifest parts of his body directly beside them. Even Victor Elderblood considered the ability considerably more troublesome than conventional teleportation.[1]
Following Diablo's death and Victor's conquest of Hell, Agares fled rather than submit. Victor eventually ordered his capture, regarding Agares' dimensional ability as far too useful to simply discard. Agares was cornered by forces commanded by [[Oda Nobunaga]] alongside Nyx Elderblood and [[Zaladrac Zeovnur Elderblood]]. Once Zaladrac sealed the surrounding Space and prevented him from using his greatest advantage, Agares was defeated and taken alive.[2][3]

### Physical Description
Agares normally possesses a humanoid form sufficiently Human-like that he could interact with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] without immediately assuming his complete Demonic transformation, although his hands can transform into monstrous claws when attacking.[1]
When assuming his Demon Form, Agares becomes an almost completely black Demonic creature covered by red and white accents. He possesses large, sharp horns and a long pointed tail, while his overall appearance becomes considerably more monstrous. A red light surrounds him during the transformation and his Demonic power rises considerably afterward.[3]
During his final battle against Victor's forces, Agares' appearance became increasingly distorted by his Miasma. After burning his own vitality for additional power, his body was temporarily transformed into a creature composed almost entirely of Miasma.[3]

### Personality and Traits
Agares embodies many of the ruthless qualities historically associated with Hell's highest-ranking Demons. During the imprisonment of [[Lilith Tepes]], he showed no sympathy toward the prisoners and was described as having the desire to subject Lilith to psychological and physical torture, refraining only because [[Diablo]] had expressly forbidden his subordinates from harming the hostages.[4]
He is also considerably more calculating than a simple brute. Agares' fighting style revolves around exploiting the unusual properties of his dimensional ability, hiding his existence in another plane and attacking only when the opponent presents an opening. Against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], he immediately attempted to strike vulnerable areas from outside normal perception and withdrew whenever interacting with Victor's Blood would place him in unnecessary danger.[1]
Agares possesses substantial confidence in his abilities, particularly his ability to escape pursuit by moving between dimensions. This confidence was great enough that he repeatedly used his ability to irritate Zaladrac in the past, an insult the Dragon continued to remember long afterward.[2]
Despite his arrogance, Agares possesses strong survival instincts. Following Diablo's death, he avoided Victor's expanding influence and continuously remained on the move rather than attempting to challenge the new Demon King directly. He eventually accepted work from an unidentified outside contractor while attempting to remain beyond Victor's reach.[2]
Agares is nevertheless extremely prideful. When cornered and deliberately spared by Victor's soldiers so that he could be captured alive, he became furious upon realizing that his opponents were intentionally avoiding killing him. Rather than surrender, he continued fighting despite accumulating injuries and ultimately burned his own vitality in a desperate final attack. Even after being dismembered and restrained, Agares demanded that his captors kill him rather than accept defeat quietly.[3]

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Agares became directly involved in [[Diablo]]'s campaign during the Demonic War. While Diablo's forces attacked several fronts simultaneously, Demonic hordes destabilized Samar and created an opening for Agares to infiltrate Werewolf territory. Agares personally abducted [[Thomas Fenrir]], the youngest son of [[Volk Fenrir]], giving Diablo another politically valuable hostage who could be used to restrain one of the world's major supernatural factions.[5]
The kidnapping formed part of a larger strategy involving the heirs and relatives of several supernatural rulers. [[Lilith Tepes]], Thomas Fenrir and [[Emily Moriarty]] were eventually imprisoned within the same Demonic facility while Diablo used their captivity as leverage against their respective families. Agares was among the Demon Dukes responsible for the security of the prisoners.[4]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually invaded the facility after destroying a massive portion of the Demonic forces protecting it. After locating the prisoners, he immediately killed the high-ranking Demons guarding their room and began extracting the hostages. Agares then intervened and attempted to prevent their escape.[1]
Agares' interdimensional ability initially made him extremely difficult to confront. He could remove himself or individual parts of his body from the ordinary plane of existence and remanifest them beside Victor with virtually no warning. His first attack nearly struck Lilith before Victor moved her away, while subsequent attacks forced Victor to continuously react to claws emerging from apparently empty Space.[1]
Victor had already obtained information about Agares from Morgana, whose memories and experience as a former Demonic General allowed him to understand the weakness of the Duke's ability. Agares could not attack something remaining in the normal plane while he himself was fully shifted into the other plane; he first needed to partially or completely manifest within the same plane as his target. Victor consequently focused on reacting during those brief moments rather than attempting to attack Agares while he was inaccessible.[1]
Neither combatant could easily commit to an unrestricted attack. Victor recognized that Agares could partially shift his body away from attacks and retaliate from unexpected directions, while Agares understood that carelessly touching Victor's Blood carried the risk of having his Soul affected or destroyed. Their confrontation therefore developed into a short stalemate while Victor waited for the prisoner-rescue operation to finish.[1]
Once Victor received confirmation that the objective had been completed, he used a large Light spell to temporarily blind Agares. Agares instinctively retreated into his alternate plane and attempted to exploit the apparent opening, exactly as Victor intended. Victor avoided the resulting surprise attack, retrieved Emily, and escaped, successfully liberating the hostages while Agares was left furious at having been deceived.[1]
Agares consequently survived the Demonic War and remained free even as Victor progressively destroyed Diablo's forces. Diablo himself was eventually killed and Hell fell under Victor's control, leaving Agares without his former master or the political structure that had once protected the Dukes.[6]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Following [[Diablo]]'s death, Agares refused to submit to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and became one of the remaining enemies operating beyond the new Demon King's immediate control. Knowing that Victor was unlikely to forget their earlier confrontation, Agares remained mobile and relied extensively upon his ability to move between dimensions to avoid capture.[2]
At some point, Agares accepted employment from an unidentified contractor connected to forces outside Victor's immediate territory. The precise identity and position of this contractor have not been definitively established. Agares was provided with an emergency device intended to summon assistance if necessary, suggesting that whoever employed him possessed resources beyond those ordinarily available to an independent fugitive.[2][3]
Victor eventually decided to eliminate the remaining unresolved threats within his Sector and turned his attention back toward Agares. Unlike many of his former enemies, however, Victor specifically ordered that Agares be captured alive. His dimensional ability was considered sufficiently valuable that killing him outright would have wasted an exceptionally useful power.[2]
A specialized force was consequently assembled around the primary weakness of Agares' ability. [[Oda Nobunaga]] commanded Victor's personal operatives, including large numbers of Shadow Demons and assassins from the [[Blank Clan]], while Nyx Elderblood and [[Zaladrac Zeovnur Elderblood]] accompanied the operation. When Agares attempted to escape, Zaladrac completely sealed the surrounding Space, preventing his normal dimensional movement and severing the escape route on which he had historically relied.[2]
Agares immediately understood the seriousness of the trap. Zaladrac had deliberately participated because she remembered how Agares had previously mocked her by repeatedly escaping between dimensions, while Nyx used her authority over Night to strengthen the nocturnal creatures surrounding him. Completely surrounded and unable to escape through his usual ability, Agares transformed into his Demon Form and chose to fight.[2][3]
Hundreds of Shadow Demons and trained Blank Clan assassins attacked Agares with extremely precise coordination. Every time Agares avoided or blocked one attack, another fighter exploited the resulting opening, while Shadow Demons continuously rescued allies whenever Agares came close to killing them. Agares eventually realized that his opponents were deliberately refraining from killing him because Victor wanted him captured alive, something that enraged the proud Duke even further.[3]
Refusing to surrender, Agares released increasingly greater amounts of Demonic Power and Miasma. After conventional resistance failed, he began burning his own vitality, causing his power to surge while transforming himself into an almost pure Miasma creature. Unable to eliminate the highly coordinated assassins, Agares abandoned them and attempted to directly attack Nyx and Zaladrac instead.[3]
Oda immediately changed the objective from controlled capture to complete incapacitation. Blank Clan assassins surrounded the area in Pure Darkness and used the technique Helheim, creating an enormous shadowy hand that dragged Agares down and restrained him. By the time his Miasma transformation collapsed, Agares had lost his tail, his left leg and his right arm. Shadow Demons then physically restrained what remained of him.[3]
Even in this state, Agares refused submission and demanded death. Victor's Shadow Demons rejected the request, explicitly informing him that he remained useful to their King and therefore would not be allowed to die. Oda subsequently reported the successful completion of the mission directly to Victor.[3]
Agares' ultimate fate following his capture has not yet been explicitly established. There is currently no confirmed scene showing his execution, absorption, release or incorporation into Victor's forces. His safest canonical status is therefore Captured.[3]

### Skills and Abilities
Agares is an exceptionally powerful Pillar Demon and historically one of the strongest members of the Ars Goetia. His position as the Second Key of Solomon was supported by genuine combat power rather than political status alone. Agares was considered among the extremely small number of Demons capable of fighting the strongest members of [[Diablo]]'s forces on equal terms, and even after War attained Demon Godhood, Agares and [[Bael]] remained exceptional enough to be treated as opponents capable of confronting him.[7]
- High-Ranking Demon Physiology: As one of Hell's most powerful Pillar Demons, Agares possesses physical and supernatural capabilities vastly beyond ordinary Demons.
- Superhuman Strength: Agares can physically clash with powerful opponents using his claws and was capable of forcing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to actively defend himself during their confrontation.[1]
- Superhuman Speed and Reflexes: Even without relying entirely upon his dimensional ability, Agares possesses considerable natural speed. Victor explicitly noted that although Agares was slower than himself, his speed was still impressive.[1]
- Enhanced Durability: Agares continued fighting after receiving numerous deep wounds during his final battle and remained conscious even after losing his right arm, left leg and tail.[3]
- Enhanced Stamina: He endured a prolonged assault from hundreds of Shadow Demons and [[Blank Clan]] assassins while continuing to resist capture.[3]
- Demonic Power: Agares can release large quantities of Demonic Energy to reinforce his body and increase his combat capabilities.[3]
- Miasma Manipulation: Like other powerful Demons, Agares can generate large amounts of Miasma. When pushed to his limit, Miasma completely enveloped his body.[3]
- Demon Form: Agares can transform into his true Demonic appearance, becoming a black horned creature with red and white accents and a pointed tail. Entering this state produces a noticeable increase in his overall power.[3]
- Natural Weapons: Agares can transform his hands into monstrous Demonic claws and use them as his principal close-range weapons.[1]
- Poison Generation: His claws can secrete a black poisonous substance, making even a successful surprise strike potentially more dangerous than the physical wound alone.[1]
- Miasma Transformation: By releasing extreme amounts of Demonic Power and Miasma, Agares can transform his physical form into a creature composed primarily of Miasma.[3]
- Vitality Empowerment: Agares can deliberately burn his own vitality to temporarily increase his power beyond its normal level. He used this desperate technique after realizing that prolonged combat against Victor's forces would inevitably result in his capture.[3]

#### Dimensional Abilities
Interdimensional Movement: Agares' signature ability allows him to interact with and travel through another plane of existence. Unlike ordinary teleportation, this does not simply move Agares from one location in normal Space to another; his existence actually shifts into a separate plane and travels from there.[1]
- [[Black and White World]]: Agares can enter an unusual plane in which ordinary spatial orientation does not function normally. There is no conventional up, down, left or right. The surrounding existence appears white while individual existences appear as black points or forms.[1]
- Existence Perception: While within this plane, Agares can identify individuals through the contrast created by their existence against the surrounding white environment, allowing him to approach a target before returning to the normal plane.[1]
- Abnormal Movement: Conventional speed does not interact with this plane in exactly the same manner as the normal world. Agares instead moves through momentum, allowing his method of approach to function differently from ordinary physical movement.[1]
- Dimensional Navigation: Because the plane lacks conventional directions, an inexperienced person could easily become lost within it. Agares is sufficiently accustomed to the environment to move through it naturally.[1]
- Dimensional Phasing: Agares does not need to shift his entire body between planes. He can transfer only selected portions of himself, causing a hand or claw to disappear from one plane and manifest elsewhere.[1]
- Partial Intangibility: Agares can move targeted portions of his body into the alternate plane to avoid conventional attacks.[1]
- Surprise Attacks: By approaching a target through the alternate plane and remanifesting a claw beside them, Agares can launch attacks with almost no conventional warning.[1]
- Dimensional Inaccessibility: While Agares exists completely within the alternate plane, beings remaining exclusively in the ordinary world cannot conventionally attack him.[1]
- Cross-Plane Limitation: The same restriction applies to Agares himself. He cannot physically strike someone in the normal world while remaining completely separated from their plane and must remanifest at least the attacking portion of his body first.[1]
- Near-Absolute Stealth: Moving entirely into the alternate plane removes Agares from ordinary perception. This makes him extremely difficult to locate without a means of sensing across different layers of existence.[1]
- Teleportation Bypass: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] specifically considered Agares' ability more troublesome than normal teleportation. Teleportation produces a brief transition while moving a being from one point to another, whereas Agares can already be positioned beside a target within another plane and simply manifest his attack into normal Reality.[1]
- Dimensional Escape: Agares can use his ability defensively to retreat from dangerous situations and cross between dimensions, making conventional pursuit extraordinarily difficult.[1][2]
- Spatial-Sealing Weakness: Agares' greatest ability can be neutralized by sufficiently powerful Spatial sealing. Zaladrac completely sealed the surrounding Space during Victor's operation, preventing Agares from escaping into another dimension. Without Dimension Hopping, Agares became considerably easier for Victor's elite forces to overwhelm.[2][3]

#### Combat and Other Skills
- Master Assassin: Agares is an extremely experienced assassin. His dimensional ability is particularly suited to assassination because it allows him to approach a target while inaccessible and attack vulnerable areas from outside their normal perception. During his capture, Agares himself recognized the techniques being used against him from the perspective of an experienced assassin.[1][3]
- Ambush Expertise: Agares immediately attempts to exploit distraction and openings in an enemy's awareness, including targeting Lilith without warning and repeatedly appearing behind [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] during their battle.[1]
- Combat Intelligence: Agares understood the danger posed by Victor's Blood and deliberately withdrew his hand rather than allow direct interaction with it. He also recognized when Victor's forces were intentionally sparing him instead of attempting to kill him.[1][3]
- Battlefield Awareness: Agares can rapidly evaluate changes in combat and alter his priorities. When he realized that the coordinated Shadow Demons and [[Blank Clan]] assassins were too difficult to kill, he abandoned them and attempted to attack Nyx and Zaladrac directly.[3]
- Supernatural Willpower: Agares continued fighting despite being completely surrounded, deprived of his greatest ability, severely wounded and aware that outside assistance was not coming. Even after being dismembered and restrained, he refused to willingly surrender.[3]

### Weaknesses
- Agares cannot attack beings located in another plane while he remains completely within the [[Black and White World]]. He must partially or completely manifest in the target's plane first, creating a brief opportunity for sufficiently fast opponents to counterattack.[1]
- His dimensional ability is highly dependent upon being able to access the surrounding Space. Powerful Spatial sealing can prevent him from Dimension Hopping entirely, as demonstrated by Zaladrac.[2][3]
- Although Agares remains an extremely powerful Pillar Demon without his dimensional ability, much of the danger produced by his fighting style comes from the enormous tactical advantage it provides. Once the ability was sealed, coordinated elite assassins were capable of progressively overwhelming and capturing him.[3]

### Trivia
- In traditional demonology, Agares is generally listed as the second spirit of the Ars Goetia and is described as a Duke.
- In several traditional accounts, Agares is depicted riding a crocodile while carrying a hawk or falcon.
- The Grand Grimoire also associates Agares with the hierarchy of Hell.
- Agares' position as the Second Key of Solomon in the novel directly parallels his traditional position as the second spirit listed in the Ars Goetia.
- Agares repeatedly used his dimensional ability to escape and mock Zaladrac. Because Dragons do not easily forget insults, Zaladrac retained the grudge until she personally participated in his capture.[2]
- During his confrontation with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Agares' dimensional movement was considered even more troublesome than ordinary teleportation because there was effectively no comparable transition delay when he remanifested to attack.[1]
- Victor deliberately ordered Agares captured alive instead of killed because of the potential usefulness of his dimensional ability.[2][3]
