### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 674
Albu is one of the two primordial-age Keepers of Hell, alongside his twin brother [[Zahal]]. Created by the [[Qliphoth|Negative World Tree of Earth]] long before the fall of [[Lucifer]] Morningstar, the twins existed in [[Biblical Hell]] when the Dimension was still little more than a barren wasteland and were entrusted with a single overriding responsibility: ensure that Hell continues to exist and properly fulfills its cosmic purpose.[2][1]
Unlike the 72 Dukes of Hell, Seven Deadly Sins, Four Horsemen of The Apocalypse or ordinary servants of the Demon King, Albu and Zahal exist largely outside the conventional political hierarchy. They do not automatically owe personal allegiance to whoever claims the throne. Their loyalty traditionally belongs to Hell itself, and a King who threatens the Dimension's existence or prevents it from properly judging, punishing and recycling Souls can be regarded by the Keepers as an inept King who must no longer be supported.[2]
This changed dramatically after [[Victor Elderblood]] conquered Hell. When Victor reached the Demon King's throne, Albu and Zahal initially confronted him with enough supernatural pressure to stop [[Vine]] Elderblood, Helena Gremory Elderblood and [[Vepar Elderblood]] from continuing forward. However, they immediately recognized Energy associated with their creator within Victor and witnessed the throne itself accept him. The twins consequently knelt before Victor and proclaimed him the True King of Hell and Master of Life and Death.[2]
Victor subsequently elevated both twins from simple Keepers into the official [[Elders]] of Hell. Their new responsibilities included continuing their original guardianship of the Dimension, preserving its history, recording all major events and educating Victor and his Generals regarding Hell's ancient political structure and metaphysical systems. Unlike previous rulers, Victor also placed them directly beneath himself, declaring that no one in Hell could command the twins unless Victor personally granted that authority.[2]
Over the following centuries Albu and Zahal became intensely devoted to Victor personally, no longer displaying the detached attitude they historically maintained toward Hell's rulers. When [[Heavenly Father]] and Ariel later visited Hell, both immediately recognized the twins. The narration states that Albu and Zahal were as ancient as those two Divine beings and were considered as dangerous as, if not more dangerous than, the previous Rulers of Hell. Their existence alone caused Heaven's representatives to reassess the military power surrounding Victor.[1]

### Physical Description
Albu and [[Zahal]] are physically identical twin Demons distinguished primarily by the color of their skin. Albu is the red-skinned twin, while Zahal possesses dark gray skin. Both have glowing red eyes and intimidating Demonic appearances.[2]
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] first encountered them, the twins stood motionless before the Demon King's throne with their eyes closed. The moment the intruders entered, their eyes opened with a red glow and an enormous supernatural pressure filled the throne room. The difference in power was immediately obvious: [[Vine]], Helena and Vepar—already among Victor's strongest Demonic followers—were forced to stop advancing beneath the pressure generated by the two Keepers.[2]
Despite their frightening appearances, both twins are capable of comparatively gentle expressions. During Victor's later return to Hell, Albu and Zahal smiled while welcoming Ophis, [[Nero]] and [[Metis]] as Imperial Princesses, although the narration notes that the smiles still appeared somewhat malevolent because of their natural Demonic features.[1]

### Personality and Traits
Albu's personality is defined primarily by duty. For nearly his entire existence, neither he nor [[Zahal]] showed significant interest in ordinary Demonic politics, territorial disputes or personal ambition. Their obsession was simply ensuring that Hell remained Hell: a functional [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] capable of receiving Souls, punishing sinners and maintaining the reincarnation system connected to the greater cosmic order.[2][1]
This makes Albu fundamentally different from most ancient Demons. Rank, wealth, Legions and political prestige appear largely meaningless to him. Demon Kings can rise and fall without affecting his loyalty so long as Hell continues functioning correctly. Albu and Zahal were therefore loyal to previous Kings only conditionally; if a ruler became sufficiently incompetent that his decisions endangered the Dimension, the twins' duty to Hell took precedence over obedience to the throne.[2]
Albu is blunt when discussing rulers he considers failures. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] asked why the flow of Souls had become dysfunctional, Zahal openly called [[Diablo]] a fool for refusing to become a proper Ruler, while Albu complained that the responsibilities previously shared between Diablo and Lilith could no longer be fulfilled because neither suitable Ruler remained present within Hell.[2]
The twins also possess an instinctive reverence toward their creator. When Victor manifested a living branch containing the combined qualities of Life and Negativity, Albu and Zahal became visibly emotional and stared at it with outright fanaticism. Roxanne subsequently confirmed that the Keepers had originally been created by [[Qliphoth|Earth's Negative World Tree]] and that Victor's Energy was sufficiently similar to their Mother's that the twins effectively perceived him as someone directly descended from her.[2]
Victor deliberately exploited this connection by promising to eventually create a garden containing living vegetation for them to protect. Because natural life had historically been impossible within Hell, the idea of guarding a living tree descended from their creator affected both [[Elders]] profoundly. They immediately offered Victor unrestricted service and declared themselves his to command.[2]
Their initial reverence eventually evolved into genuine fanaticism toward Victor independent of Qliphoth. By Chapter 910, the narration explicitly notes that their attitude had fundamentally changed from the detached guardianship they once displayed. They were now fully committed to Victor's rule, and the purity of that devotion was obvious even to the [[Heavenly Father]] and Ariel.[1]
Albu also possesses a rigid view of the Demon King's authority under Victor's regime. When [[Nero]] questioned why she was considered a Princess despite not being a Demon, Albu explained that race was irrelevant: Victor had declared Nero his daughter, meaning she was the daughter of the Demon King and therefore a Princess until Victor himself stated otherwise. In Albu's worldview, the legitimate King's [[Will Solace|Will]] within Hell is absolute; if the King establishes something as law, his subjects are expected to make that declaration reality.[1]
This devotion should not be confused with stupidity. Albu and Zahal possess extraordinary knowledge accumulated across the entire history of Hell and understand their Dimension's cosmic purpose better than almost any ordinary Demon. Victor specifically selected them to teach his Generals because their experience stretched back to the earliest stages of the Underworld itself.[2]

### History

#### Creation of the Keepers
Albu and [[Zahal]] were created by the [[Qliphoth|Negative World Tree of Earth]], the entity currently known as Qliphoth.[2] Their creation predates the fall of [[Lucifer]] and therefore predates much of the recognizable political history of [[Biblical Hell]].
By Chapter 910, the twins are explicitly stated to have existed since Hell was nothing more than a barren wasteland without structures. The [[Heavenly Father]] and Ariel knew of them personally and recognized them as beings roughly comparable to themselves in antiquity.[1]
The exact method through which Qliphoth created the twins has not been revealed. Their nature nevertheless resembles other powerful guardians created by [[World Trees]]: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] himself internally compares Albu and Zahal's relationship with Hell to the relationship between [[Diablo|Big Guy]] and [[Roxanne Elderblood]]. Their purpose is not conquest or personal advancement but protection of the environment and function their creator entrusted to them.[2]

#### Guardians of Hell
The twins became known as the Keepers or Guardians of Hell. Their responsibility was broader than defending the Dimension from invading armies. They were also expected to protect Hell from its own ruler if the King's incompetence threatened the Dimension's continued existence.[2]
Hell is not merely a homeland for Demons. It forms part of the cosmic mechanism responsible for receiving, punishing, judging and recycling Souls. If the Dimension ceases performing that function, higher authorities such as [[The Judges of The Abyss]] and [[The Universal Tree]] can intervene and destroy the dysfunctional Hell entirely.[2]
Consequently, Albu and [[Zahal]] judge Kings according to whether they properly maintain this system rather than whether they are politically popular or militarily successful.
The twins remained within Hell throughout the reigns of [[Lucifer]], Lilith and [[Diablo]] but generally stayed outside ordinary political affairs. Their isolation became so complete that even ancient powers eventually stopped actively thinking about them. Chapter 910 notes that both the [[Heavenly Father]] and Ariel had effectively forgotten the two "monsters" still remained inside Hell because the Keepers could remain completely discreet whenever they desired.[1]

#### Diablo's Reign
[[Diablo]]'s refusal to properly assume the responsibilities of a Ruler severely damaged Hell's metaphysical systems. According to Albu and [[Zahal]], the work had previously been maintained through the combined influence of Diablo and [[Lilith Elderblood]], but the absence of an appropriate active Ruler disrupted the flow of Souls.[2]
The situation became dangerous enough that Hell theoretically should have faced intervention from the greater administrators of the afterlife. Diablo temporarily avoided that outcome through a deal with the Judges of the Abyss involving billions of Souls. Albu admitted that neither Keeper knew the exact details but believed the agreement prevented the Primordial authorities from immediately interfering for a limited period.[2]
Albu showed obvious irritation toward Diablo over this situation. The King's choices had forced the Dimension the twins existed to protect into an unstable state and placed Hell increasingly close to violating the cosmic purpose for which it existed.
Despite this dissatisfaction, Albu and Zahal did not simply seize the throne themselves. Their role was guardianship rather than kingship, and their extraordinary age and power did not automatically translate into a desire to administer Demonic civilization.

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Albu first appeared after Alucard had conquered his way into the deepest regions of [[Biblical Hell]]. Accompanied by [[Vine]], Helena and Vepar, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] entered the Demon King's enormous castle and eventually arrived before the central throne.[2]
Two identical Demons stood protecting it: [[Zahal]], whose skin was dark gray, and Albu, whose skin was red.
The Keepers opened their glowing red eyes and released such immense pressure that Vine, Helena and Vepar immediately understood they were confronting beings far above themselves. All three Generals were forced to stop advancing while Victor continued forward alone.[2]
Victor answered their pressure with his own red supernatural Energy.
The twins immediately recognized something familiar.
They sensed what they described as their Lady's power within Victor. Before Victor had even formally claimed the throne, both Keepers dropped to their knees and hailed him as the True King of Hell, Master of Life and Death.[2]
Victor ignored the declaration temporarily and approached the throne. As he climbed the steps, the gigantic seat automatically changed size and color until it matched him. The moment Victor sat down, the entire castle transformed and information concerning the responsibilities of the King of Hell and a true Ruler entered his consciousness.[2]
Albu regarded the throne's acceptance as definitive proof. He and Zahal immediately concluded that [[Diablo]] was no longer necessary and that the Keys of Hell still carried by the former King needed to be returned to their rightful owner.[2]
Once Victor understood the position, he immediately noticed that Hell's Soul circulation was malfunctioning.
Albu and Zahal explained that their Dimension existed to punish sinners and recycle Souls, but Diablo's refusal to fully accept the responsibilities of a Ruler had damaged the system. Diablo and Lilith had previously shared enough influence to maintain certain functions, but circumstances surrounding both figures had left Hell without adequate management.[2]
Victor explained the severity of the problem to his Generals. A Hell that permanently ceases fulfilling its role can eventually be destroyed by the Universal Tree and the Judges of the Abyss because such a Dimension becomes a malfunctioning component of the cosmic Soul system.[2]
Albu then revealed that Diablo had only avoided immediate intervention by making an unknown agreement with the Judges involving billions of Souls. Although the Keepers did not know every detail, they believed the bargain had purchased a limited period in which the Primordial authorities would not directly intervene.[2]
The information gave Victor a much clearer understanding of Diablo's desperate actions and the limited amount of time remaining before the former King's plans forced a larger response.
Victor questioned why the twins were addressing him personally as their King when their traditional loyalty belonged to Hell rather than its ruler.
Zahal answered that Victor was different.
Albu explained that the Energy of their Mother existed within him.[2]
Victor manifested a living branch containing the influence of Life and Negativity. The reaction was immediate: the ancient Keepers stared at the branch with intense emotion and described its existence as something only the True King could accomplish.[2]
Roxanne then confirmed the explanation privately to Victor. Albu and Zahal had been created by [[Qliphoth|Earth's Negative World Tree]]. Because Victor carried Energy extremely similar to that World Tree through his connections to Roxanne and the wider duality of the [[World Trees]], the twins interpreted his presence as directly related to their creator.[2]
The moment also revealed how alien ordinary life was to ancient Hell. Vine and Vepar were shocked merely by seeing a living tree branch exist inside the Dimension, while the Keepers regarded the possibility of protecting one as an extraordinary gift.
Victor promised that he would eventually create a garden for them to guard.
In exchange, he wanted everything they knew.
Albu and Zahal immediately accepted.[2]
Victor formally bestowed a new title upon the twins: [[Elders]] of Hell.[2]
Their original role as Guardians would continue, but they were now additionally responsible for preserving the complete history of Hell. Victor considered historical records essential because future generations could study previous failures instead of repeating them.
He also privately recognized the political benefit. By controlling how Hell's history was preserved and taught, Victor could ensure future Demons compared the competence of his reign with the failures of previous Kings and increasingly regarded him as Hell's natural sovereign.[2]
Albu initially reacted with surprise at being granted such a responsibility. Victor reminded both twins that beings who had existed from Hell's beginning were the natural candidates for preserving its history.[2]
Their authority was also clarified. From that point onward, no individual within Hell possessed the right to order Albu or Zahal except Victor himself or someone Victor explicitly authorized.
Victor further instructed them to teach Helena, Vine and Vepar everything they knew concerning Hell's politics, history and administration because the three women would become essential figures in the new government.[2]
The twins responded with complete obedience:
Victor's [[Will Solace|Will]] was now their Will.
As Victor's government matured, Albu and Zahal fulfilled the historical responsibilities he had given them. They compiled books documenting the history of Hell, including political events, earlier rulers and the development of the Dimension itself.[3]
Those records became particularly popular among younger Demons who had never personally experienced the ancient periods the twins described. The writings also had an important political effect: the contrast between Hell's previous rulers and Victor's reforms naturally reinforced the idea of Victor as the definitive ruler of the Dimension.[3]
The Elders' records additionally became valuable to Victor's administrators. Helena, Vine, Vepar and other senior officials could consult histories covering periods that predated almost every modern Demon.
Their records even preserve information concerning the physical growth of Hell itself. Later observers discovered that the Dimension continued expanding over time, and the writings of Albu and Zahal became one of the historical benchmarks used to compare its modern size with its ancient state.[3]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
By Chapter 910, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Hell had changed beyond recognition compared with the barren Dimension known to the twins during their earliest existence. Abaddon had become an advanced Demonic metropolis, the Demon King's castle had been rebuilt around Victor's enormous physical requirements and the ancient conflict between Heaven and Hell had been reduced enough that the [[Heavenly Father]] and Ariel could enter the Dimension as guests.[1]
Albu and [[Zahal]] remained in the throne room.
When Victor returned with Heavenly Father, Ariel, Ophis, [[Nero]] and [[Metis]], the twins immediately knelt and announced the return of the Tyrannical Demon King Victor Alucard.[1]
Their behavior surprised Heaven's representatives for a different reason than the ordinary Demons' fanaticism.
The Heavenly Father and Ariel already knew exactly who Albu and Zahal were.
The narration explicitly describes the twins as as ancient as the Heavenly Father and Ariel. They had remained in Hell since it was an empty wasteland and were considered dangerous enough that Heaven regarded them as comparable to, or potentially more dangerous than, the previous rulers of Hell.[1]
Their presence increased Ariel and the Heavenly Father's caution toward Victor because the two had effectively forgotten that these ancient monsters still existed. Unlike ordinary Demons, Albu and Zahal could remain completely discreet if they wished and historically cared about almost nothing beyond Hell's continued existence.[1]
That neutrality had disappeared.
Both were now visibly and completely devoted to Victor.
Victor instructed the twins to introduce themselves to his daughters and guests. Albu and Zahal identified themselves as the [[Elders]] of Hell and welcomed Ophis, Nero and Metis to the castle as Princesses.[1]
Nero objected because she was not a Demon and had not been born from Victor's Soul or biological essence.
Albu immediately rejected the distinction.
According to him, Victor had declared Nero his daughter. Therefore, she was the Demon King's daughter. Her species, biological origin and previous identity were irrelevant to that declaration; unless Victor personally revoked the relationship, Nero possessed the status of his daughter and therefore Princess of Hell.[1]
Albu's explanation illustrates how completely the Elders accepted Victor's sovereignty. Within his Hell, the King's legitimate declaration possessed absolute political authority. The narration compares their devotion to the obedience the Heavenly Father once received from Michael.[1]
Victor's trust in the twins had also grown substantially. Later during the visit, Victor assigned Albu and Zahal to accompany or accommodate his Heavenly guests.[1]
Ariel immediately recognized the additional purpose behind the gesture. The Elders were not present only to act as courteous hosts; they were also the safest available insurance in the event the Heavenly Father suddenly became hostile inside Victor's territory.[1]
Victor was therefore willing to use Albu and Zahal as security even when the potential threat was the Creator God of the [[Biblical Pantheon]].
Their assignment provides one of the strongest practical indicators of the level of trust and power associated with the Keepers without requiring an unsupported numerical Divine classification.

### Relationship with Zahal
Albu and [[Zahal]] are twin brothers and have existed together since their creation by [[Qliphoth|Earth's Negative World Tree]]. Their duties, political status and historical roles are almost inseparable.
The twins frequently speak in sequence or simultaneously, displaying an exceptionally synchronized worldview. Zahal may begin an explanation and Albu complete it, or both deliver the same declaration together.[2]
Their personalities have not been differentiated enough in the novel to establish radically different individual traits. Both prioritize Hell, both revere their creator, both accepted [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] as True King and both became fanatically loyal to his later reign.
Albu nevertheless speaks independently when circumstances require it. He is the twin who explicitly explains why [[Nero]] qualifies as Victor's Princess in Chapter 910, and during Victor's accession he personally comments upon the failure of [[Diablo]] and Lilith to maintain the necessary Ruler functions.[2][1]

### Relationship with Qliphoth
Albu's creator is the [[Qliphoth|Negative World Tree of Earth]], currently identified as Qliphoth.[2]
This corrects the older page's claim that [[Yggdrasil]] created him. Yggdrasil is Earth's Positive World Tree; Albu and [[Zahal]] are explicitly stated to originate from its Negative counterpart.
Their attachment to their creator remains extraordinarily strong despite the incomprehensible period that has passed since their creation. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] produced living World Tree Energy inside Hell, both [[Elders]] immediately became emotional and nearly ignored everything around them while staring at the branch.[2]
The possibility of receiving a garden containing vegetation connected to their Mother was sufficient to turn their already favorable response to Victor into open fanaticism.

### Relationship with Victor Elderblood
Albu's relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is fundamentally different from his relationship with previous Demon Kings.
Traditionally, Albu served the office of King only insofar as its holder protected Hell. A sufficiently incompetent King could lose the Keepers' loyalty because their ultimate responsibility was toward the Dimension itself.[2]
Victor immediately broke this pattern.
The Demon King's throne accepted him, he carried Energy resembling their Mother's, he understood Hell's neglected Soul system almost immediately and he offered the twins something no previous ruler had apparently provided: the possibility of genuine Life existing within the Infernal Dimension.[2]
Albu and [[Zahal]] consequently recognized Victor as the True King of Hell before he had even recovered the complete Keys to the Dimension.
Victor later rewarded their service by elevating them to [[Elders]], granting them privileged authority, entrusting them with Hell's complete historical record and promising them a garden connected to the World Tree.[2]
By Chapter 910, their loyalty had become openly personal. Their devotion was visible enough that the [[Heavenly Father]] immediately understood these were no longer politically neutral Keepers merely tolerating the latest King.[1]
They belonged to Victor's regime completely.

### Skills and Abilities
Albu's complete combat repertoire has never been directly displayed. Nevertheless, his age, origin, supernatural pressure and the reactions of other extraordinarily powerful beings establish that he is one of the most dangerous native Demons associated with [[Biblical Hell]].
Ancient Demon Physiology: Albu is a Demon created directly by [[Qliphoth|Earth's Negative World Tree]] rather than an ordinary descendant of later Demonic populations. He possesses the supernatural properties expected from extremely ancient Demons at a level vastly beyond ordinary members of the race.
- Immense Physical Power: Although Albu has not fought directly on-panel, the [[Heavenly Father]] and Ariel consider Albu and [[Zahal]] dangerous enough to compare them with the previous rulers of Hell.[1]
- Immense Durability: His status as one of Hell's oldest guardian beings and the confidence [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] places in him against high-level threats indicate extraordinary survivability, although the exact limits remain undemonstrated.
- Extreme Longevity: Albu has existed since before [[Lucifer]]'s Fall and was already present when Hell remained an undeveloped wasteland.[2][1]
- Agelessness: Like other sufficiently powerful Demons, Albu does not appear to suffer from ordinary biological aging.
- Miasma Compatibility: Albu exists naturally within even the deepest and most Miasma-saturated regions of Hell without adverse effects.
- Infernal Adaptation: His body is naturally adapted to the extreme conditions of Hell and has survived changes to the Dimension across its entire recorded history.
World Tree-Created Existence: Albu was created by Earth's Negative World Tree rather than born through ordinary Demonic reproduction.[2]
This origin separates him from the majority of Demons and explains the overwhelming response both brothers display toward Energy belonging to their Mother.
- Negativity Affinity: Albu instinctively recognizes Energy associated with Earth's Negative World Tree.[2]
- World Tree Energy Perception: He immediately recognized the similarity between Victor's Energy and that of his creator before Victor explicitly manifested the branch.[2]
- Life/Negativity Perception: Albu and Zahal could distinguish both the Negative and Life-related properties within Victor's manifested World Tree branch.[2]
Immense Supernatural Pressure: Merely opening his eyes alongside Zahal released enough pressure to stop [[Vine]], Helena and Vepar from advancing through the throne room.[2]
The three women were already extremely powerful Demons by this point and had been personally strengthened by Victor, making the response a significant demonstration of the gap between the [[Elders]] and conventional Pillar-level beings.
- Aura Projection: Albu can exert supernatural pressure over his surroundings without needing to physically attack.
- Intimidation: Weaker high-ranking Demons can be forced into defensive or immobilized states simply by entering the area affected by the twins' combined pressure.[2]
Presence Suppression / Concealment: Chapter 910 states that Albu and Zahal could remain completely discreet if they wished. Their isolation was effective enough that even the Heavenly Father and Ariel had effectively stopped accounting for them despite personally knowing of their existence.[1]
This should not automatically be interpreted as supernatural invisibility, but it demonstrates exceptional ability to suppress their presence and avoid involvement with the wider supernatural world.

#### Keeper of Hell
Hell Guardianship: Albu's principal purpose is the preservation of Hell. Together with [[Zahal]], he monitors whether the Dimension continues fulfilling its required role in the cosmic Soul cycle.[2]
- Dimensional Stewardship: The Keepers monitor Hell's continued stability and functionality.
- Ruler Evaluation: They judge whether the reigning King properly performs the responsibilities necessary for Hell to survive.[2]
- Authority to Reject an Inept King: Their loyalty does not obligate them to continue serving a ruler whose actions threaten Hell's existence.[2]
- Soul-System Knowledge: Albu understands Hell's role in punishing and recycling Souls and recognized the damage caused by [[Diablo]]'s refusal to properly act as Ruler.[2]
- Afterlife Knowledge: He possesses knowledge concerning the Universal Tree, Judges of the Abyss and the relationship between Rulers, Hells and the wider Soul cycle.[2]
- Keys of Hell Knowledge: Albu immediately knew that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s authority would remain incomplete until the Keys of Hell were recovered from Diablo.[2]

#### Knowledge and Intelligence
Ancient Knowledge: Having existed since the earliest state of [[Biblical Hell]], Albu possesses direct historical knowledge covering periods predating [[Lucifer]]'s reign and almost every surviving Demonic civilization.[2][1]
Historical Record-Keeping: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] officially tasked Albu and [[Zahal]] with recording and preserving all of Hell's historical events.[2]
The books produced by the twins later became widely read among younger Demons and important enough that Hell's leaders regularly consulted them for information.[3]
Political Knowledge: Victor specifically ordered the twins to explain Hell's historical and contemporary political climate to himself and his Generals.[2]
Institutional Memory: Because they survived every major regime, the twins function as living continuity between the rule of Lucifer, Lilith, [[Diablo]] and Victor.
Dimensional History: Their records preserve information concerning the physical development and expansion of Hell itself.[3]
