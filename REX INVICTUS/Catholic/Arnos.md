### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 869
Arnos Nightshade Imperious is an ancient Imp and Lesser Demon living within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s New Hell. Despite belonging to one of the traditionally weakest classes of Demons, Arnos became an exceptionally successful businessman under Victor Elderblood's reign, founding the Nightshade Corporation and becoming the first Lesser Demon recorded as creating a mega-corporation in Hell. His accomplishments eventually drew the direct attention of Victor himself, another achievement specifically recorded by Hell's administrative system.[1]
At 78,469 years of Hell Age, Arnos is considerably older than many powerful supernatural beings despite his status as a Lesser Demon. Victor's government officially classifies him as an Exemplary Citizen of Hell, with no recorded crimes and more than fifty registered achievements. Victor personally used Arnos as an example of the kind of Demon his new society was intended to reward: competent, productive, loyal and capable of advancing through merit rather than ancestry or raw strength.[1]

### Physical Appearance
Arnos is a small Imp Demon. Unlike the stereotypically savage appearance associated with many Lesser Demons, he presents himself in an exceptionally neat and professional manner and wears a formal business suit appropriate to his position as a corporate executive.[1]
His appearance contributed to the unusual impression created during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s demonstration before the assembled supernatural leaders: an ancient Imp dressed like a corporate executive and publicly identified by Hell's government as an exemplary citizen rather than a disposable monster.[1]

### Personality
Arnos is extraordinarily loyal to [[Victor Elderblood]] and displays an almost fanatical degree of respect toward the Demon King. When Victor summoned him, Arnos declared himself honored merely to have been selected and stated that he existed to serve the Demon King of Tyranny. When Victor asked him to cooperate with the demonstration, Arnos enthusiastically promised to devote an exaggerated 10,000% effort to the King's eternal glory.[1]
His loyalty is not portrayed as simple terror. Arnos takes genuine pride in Victor's regime and in his own accomplishments beneath it. When Victor later presented Arnos as an example of a Demon who had worked hard, remained obedient and accumulated achievements worthy of reward, Arnos proudly puffed out his chest.[1]
Arnos is also remarkably self-assured for a Lesser Demon. While standing directly before the [[Heavenly Father]], he displayed virtually no fear despite the Heavenly Father's existence being naturally antithetical to Demons. He addressed the Creator with a bored expression and casually instructed him on how to operate Victor's identification Rune. His behavior surprised not only the observers but the Heavenly Father himself, who was unaccustomed to an Imp showing absolutely no instinctive fear before him.[1]
This confidence appears heavily connected to Arnos' absolute faith in Victor and the new order of Hell. He was standing under the protection and authority of his King and consequently behaved as though the overwhelming supernatural beings surrounding him were of little personal concern.[1]
Beyond his loyalty, Arnos is demonstrably ambitious, industrious and competent. His governmental record contains more than fifty achievements, including becoming the first Lesser Demon to establish a mega-corporation. His rise is one of the clearest examples of Victor's meritocratic policies allowing Lesser Demons to obtain status through ability and accomplishment rather than being permanently subordinated to Ancient Demons and the old aristocracy.[1]

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Arnos was publicly introduced during the supernatural gathering in which [[Victor Elderblood]] explained the governmental reforms he had implemented throughout his territories. One of Victor's objectives was changing the perception of Demons from irrational monsters into a recognized population of sapient citizens who could be judged according to their individual conduct.[1]
Victor explained that every ordinary Demon recognized as a citizen of his realm was required to register with the government. Their identity, age, occupation and other information would become part of a magically maintained identification system created through Victor's Runes. The system served considerably more functions than a normal Human identification document: Victor could locate registered Demons, monitor important changes in their status and detect dangerous behavior or betrayal directed against his government.[1]
To demonstrate how the system worked, Victor requested permission from the Owner of Limbo before opening a portal and summoning one of his citizens. The Demon who appeared was Arnos, a small Imp dressed neatly in a formal suit.[1]
Arnos immediately greeted Victor with extreme enthusiasm, addressing him as the Demon King of Tyranny and declaring himself deeply honored to have been chosen for the demonstration. Victor thanked Arnos for his usual service and instructed him to cooperate. Arnos responded by promising to give 10,000% effort for the eternal glory of his King.[1]
He then turned toward the [[Heavenly Father]]. In sharp contrast to the reverence he had shown Victor, Arnos regarded the ancient Creator with a bored expression and simply told him to proceed. When the Heavenly Father momentarily failed to understand what he meant, Arnos calmly explained that he only needed to point Victor's Rune toward him.[1]
The behavior astonished the supernatural audience. The Heavenly Father's existence was essentially the natural opposite of a Demon, yet Arnos—a mere Imp—showed no meaningful fear in his presence. Even the Heavenly Father himself was briefly confused by the Lesser Demon's complete lack of intimidation.[1]
When the Rune was directed toward Arnos, a red information tablet appeared and displayed his governmental record:

The information surprised the gathered supernatural beings for several reasons. Arnos was more than seventy-eight thousand years old despite appearing to be an insignificant Imp, possessed a long list of achievements and—most strangely from their perspective—was officially described as an "Exemplary Citizen of Hell." The coexistence of concepts such as citizenship, corporate achievement and civic responsibility with Hell contradicted the image many outsiders possessed of Demons as creatures defined entirely by violence and sin.[1]
Victor deliberately chose Arnos for precisely that reason. After presenting him, Victor summoned a second Lesser Demon named Festifiolioedes, whose record identified him as a murderer, traitor and wanted criminal who had violated Victor's direct order prohibiting unauthorized Demonic travel to the Human World. The comparison demonstrated that Victor's system distinguished between Demons based upon behavior rather than condemning the entire race indiscriminately.[1]
Victor subsequently explained the principle by contrasting Arnos with the criminal Demon. Demons who remained competent, worked hard and accumulated worthwhile achievements would be rewarded, as Arnos had been. Those who disobeyed the King, produced unnecessary chaos and behaved according to uncontrolled impulses would instead be punished.[1]
Arnos reacted visibly proudly when Victor presented him as the positive example of that philosophy. After Victor completed the demonstration, Arnos was returned to Hell while Victor continued negotiating with the gathered supernatural factions.[1]

### Nightshade Corporation
Nightshade Corporation is the mega-corporation founded and led by Arnos. Its creation is specifically recorded by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s governmental system as a historic achievement because Arnos became the first Lesser Demon in Hell to successfully establish a mega-corporation.[1]
The accomplishment is particularly significant because of the position traditionally occupied by Imps and other Lesser Demons. The old structure of Hell overwhelmingly favored powerful Demon Dukes, ancient bloodlines and beings possessing superior personal strength. Arnos' success demonstrates the social mobility introduced under Victor's reign, where even a Lesser Demon could accumulate wealth, influence and official recognition if sufficiently competent.[1]
The novel confirms Arnos as both the Founder and current CEO of Nightshade Corporation but does not provide a detailed explanation of every service, subsidiary or commercial activity conducted by the corporation in Chapter 869. Accordingly, specific corporate functions should not be treated as confirmed without additional chapter evidence.[1]

### Skills & Abilities
- Demon Physiology: Arnos is a Demon of the Imp species and therefore possesses the supernatural physiology characteristic of Hell's Demonic population.[1]
- Supernatural Longevity: Arnos' registered Hell Age is 78,469, demonstrating the extreme lifespan available even to members of the Lesser Demon population.[1]
- Demonic Energy: As a Demon, Arnos possesses the Demonic energy inherent to his race. The novel has not demonstrated any specialized combat application unique to him.
- Imp Physiology: Arnos belongs to the Imp species and is explicitly classified as a Lesser Demon. His extraordinary social position comes from achievement rather than belonging to one of Hell's physically dominant Demonic species.[1]
- Enhanced Mental Fortitude: Arnos remained completely composed in the immediate presence of the [[Heavenly Father]] despite the Creator's nature being fundamentally opposed to Demons. Even the Heavenly Father was surprised that such a minor Demon displayed no visible fear.[1]
- Business Management: Arnos created and currently manages Nightshade Corporation, the first mega-corporation founded by a Lesser Demon in Hell.[1]
- Entrepreneurship: Arnos successfully developed a business large enough to receive official mega-corporation status and governmental recognition.[1]
- Leadership: As founder and CEO of a mega-corporation, Arnos possesses authority over a major commercial organization within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Hell.[1]
- Administrative Competence: His record of more than fifty achievements, lack of criminal offenses and classification as an exemplary citizen indicate exceptional competence within Victor's new social structure.[1]

#### Combat Ability
Arnos has no significant combat feats currently shown. Although he possesses the basic supernatural characteristics of a Demon, his importance comes from economic and administrative achievement rather than military strength. He should therefore not be assigned advanced Demonic combat abilities merely because older or higher-ranking Demons possess them.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
Arnos possesses extreme loyalty and admiration toward [[Victor Elderblood]]. He refers to Victor by his title as the Demon King of Tyranny and considers serving him an essential purpose of his existence.[1]
Victor, meanwhile, appears to regard Arnos as one of the successful products of his reforms. He thanked Arnos for his usual service, personally selected him for an important demonstration before the world's supernatural leadership and publicly held him up as an example of the citizens he intended to reward.[1]
Victor's recognition is itself one of Arnos' registered achievements, making him the first Lesser Demon known to have gained the Demon King's direct attention through personal accomplishment.[1]

#### Heavenly Father
Arnos briefly interacted with the [[Heavenly Father]] during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s demonstration. Despite the enormous difference in status and power between them, Arnos treated the Creator almost casually, explaining the identification system with a bored expression and showing no visible fear.[1]
The Heavenly Father was sufficiently surprised that he briefly hesitated, as he had apparently never encountered a Lesser Demon capable of remaining so unconcerned in his presence.[1]

### Significance
Arnos represents one of the clearest individual examples of the changes [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] introduced into Hell. Under previous Demonic regimes, political and social influence was concentrated heavily among ancient Demons, powerful bloodlines, the Seven Deadly Sins and the 72 Pillars. Victor instead established a system in which competence, usefulness and achievement could elevate even a Lesser Demon.[1]
Arnos' existence consequently served Victor's argument that Demons should not be indiscriminately classified as monsters. A seventy-eight-thousand-year-old Imp with no criminal history had become a productive citizen, successful entrepreneur and major corporate executive precisely because the new system gave him the opportunity and incentive to direct his ambition productively.[1]
Victor contrasted Arnos against Demons who continued murdering without reason or violating governmental orders. The point was not that Victor intended to make Demons peaceful by Human standards, but that he intended to transform Hell from uncontrolled chaos into a functioning civilization where actions carried predictable rewards and consequences.[1]
