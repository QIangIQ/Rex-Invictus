### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 522
Paimon was the 9th Key of Solomon, a high-ranking member of the Ars Goetia and one of the principal Demonic commanders deployed by [[Diablo]] during the Demonic War. Unlike most naturally born Demons, Paimon was a Fallen Angel, having originally possessed an Angelic existence before becoming integrated into [[Biblical Hell]]. By the modern era he occupied the ninth position among the 72 Pillars and commanded a personal Clan, Legions and a specialized bloodline centered around large-scale Demon summoning.[1][2]
During Diablo's invasion of [[Nightingale]], Paimon attacked the Royal Capital alongside [[Bael]] and [[Dantalion]]. His rank and combat ability were significant enough that [[Morgana Elderblood]], who possessed extensive experience fighting Hell's elite, considered the simultaneous presence of Bael and Paimon substantially more troublesome than confronting the weaker Dukes individually.[1]
Paimon's principal opponent became [[Jeanne Elderblood]], who countered his mass summoning through the [[Heavenly Father]]'s Blessing and Judgment of Israel. His downfall ultimately resulted from threatening Jeanne's son, [[Adam Tepes]], prompting her to invoke the protection of [[The Universal Tree]]. Rather than simply killing Paimon, the Universal Tree destroyed his body, shattered his Soul, exposed and dismantled the deepest core of his existence and explicitly erased the individual known as Paimon. The remaining spiritual material was reconstructed into a new Soul and returned to the reincarnation cycle.[3]

### Physical Appearance
Paimon's first appearance during the invasion of [[Nightingale]] was deliberately monstrous. He appeared as an enormous primitive-looking Demonic creature with gigantic wings while dense Miasma continuously escaped from his mouth. His presence was immediately recognizable to Morgana as belonging to the 9th Key of Solomon.[1]
This appearance was later revealed to be an external Demonic shell. When Jeanne confronted him, she stripped away the shell and exposed the form beneath it. Paimon's underlying body was far more humanoid and elegant, possessing an effeminate male face, a crown and two long wings described as completely black.[2]
Jeanne mockingly referred to him as "Crow", a nickname fitting his black wings and Fallen Angel appearance.[2]
Despite his comparatively graceful true appearance, Paimon's body remained physically powerful and highly resilient. He survived Jeanne crushing his throat, being thrown directly through his own Darkness spears and receiving repeated high-impact attacks without immediately dying.[2][3]

### Personality
Paimon was arrogant, lustful and deeply accustomed to the status associated with being one of Hell's highest-ranked Pillars. His position as the 9th Key placed him above most members of the Ars Goetia, and he regularly behaved as though weaker supernatural beings should naturally recognize his superiority.[1][2]
He continued displaying this arrogance even after Morgana released enough power to intimidate ordinary Demons. Rather than reassess the danger around him, Paimon remained confrontational and contemptuous toward [[Nightingale]]'s defenders.[2]
Paimon also possessed strong resentment toward Heaven and those still connected to it. Upon recognizing Jeanne as Jeanne d'Arc, the Saint of Orleans, he reacted with immediate hostility. The fact that Jeanne had become a Vampire yet could still call upon the [[Heavenly Father]]'s Blessing angered him even further, particularly when she manifested golden Angelic characteristics and used Holy power directly against his Demons.[2]
His arrogance was accompanied by pronounced lust toward attractive young men. During his battle with Jeanne, Paimon openly expressed interest in her son [[Adam Tepes]] and stated his intention to make Adam his sexual plaything.[3]
This immediately changed the nature of the battle. Jeanne had previously treated Paimon as a military opponent, but his threat toward Adam transformed the conflict into a personal matter. Paimon quickly realized he had made a catastrophic mistake.[3]
Beneath his confidence, Paimon possessed an extremely strong survival instinct. Once Jeanne's behavior and power made clear that his life was genuinely in danger, his loyalty toward [[Diablo]] disappeared from his priorities. He internally abandoned the mission and wanted only to escape Nightingale alive.[3]
His fear became absolute when Jeanne invoked the authority of the Universal Tree. Paimon recognized the nature of the green presence surrounding her before Morgana understood it and immediately understood that he had attracted the attention of an existence against which resistance was meaningless. Upon seeing the portal Jeanne summoned, he trembled, cried and lost control of his body in terror.[3]
Paimon's confidence was therefore highly dependent upon his belief that he possessed either greater power or sufficient resources to control a confrontation. When faced with an existence incomparably beyond him, that arrogance disappeared almost instantly.

### History
Paimon was originally an Angel and is explicitly identified in the novel as a Fallen Angel.[3] The exact circumstances behind his fall from Heaven have not been fully explained.
At some point after becoming a Demon, Paimon entered the Ars Goetia and eventually rose to become the 9th Key of Solomon. By the modern era he commanded his own Clan and Legions and possessed a specialized bloodline allowing him to summon large numbers of Demons directly into battle.[1][2]
His rank placed him among the upper members of the 72 Pillars. Morgana, who had personally experienced Hell's hierarchy during her existence as one of Lilith's Generals, treated Paimon's presence beside [[Bael]] as a serious battlefield complication.[1]
By the time of [[Diablo]]'s war, Paimon had become one of the forces aligned with the Biblical Demon King and was entrusted with the assault on [[Nightingale]]'s Royal Capital.

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Paimon entered [[Nightingale]] when [[Diablo]] launched a coordinated invasion across the Vampire world. Different Demon Dukes were assigned to separate territories while [[Bael]], Paimon and [[Dantalion]] directly attacked the Royal Capital.[1]
Dantalion's presence did not particularly concern Morgana, and she believed her increased strength gave her a reasonable chance of handling Bael. Paimon's arrival changed that assessment. Fighting Bael and the 9th Key simultaneously represented a much more dangerous confrontation.[1]
The Royal Capital battle initially revolved around Morgana's history as a former Demon and the attackers' attempts to exploit her connection with Lilith. Paimon remained confident even after Morgana displayed enough Demonic pressure to frighten many of the lesser forces surrounding them.[2]
Morgana eventually separated the opponents and sent Paimon directly toward [[Jeanne Elderblood]]. Jeanne caught the incoming Demon by the throat and immediately recognized him.[2]
Paimon identified Jeanne as the Saint of Orleans, while Jeanne mocked him as "Crow" and demanded that he stop hiding beneath his external Demonic shell. Through her supernatural power, the monstrous form surrounding Paimon disappeared, exposing his crowned humanoid appearance and long black wings.[2]
Paimon responded by manifesting several spears composed of supernatural Darkness. Jeanne did not merely evade them; she physically threw Paimon into his own weapons, causing the Darkness spears to impale their creator.[2]
When Jeanne approached again, Paimon released a powerful roar or repulsive shockwave that forced her backward. He immediately followed this by using the ability for which Clan Paimon was particularly dangerous.[2]
Numerous red [[Magic]] Circles spread throughout the Royal Capital. Paimon used the formations to summon the twin Demon Kings Bebal and Abalam, enormous Demons standing more than four meters tall. One possessed blood-red skin while the other appeared gray and surrounded by Darkness.[2]
Paimon continued opening additional summoning circles across the city, preparing to bring portions of his Legions directly onto the battlefield. If allowed to continue, the technique would have drastically increased the number of Demons inside the Royal Capital.[2]
Jeanne responded by invoking the Divine Blessing she had retained from the [[Heavenly Father]]. Despite becoming a Vampire, her connection to Heaven had not completely disappeared. Golden power surrounded her and the sword associated with Michael manifested in her hands.[2]
Jeanne activated Judgment of Israel. Golden flames spread throughout an enormous circular region covering the Royal Capital, destroying Paimon's summoning circles, dispersing Demonic Miasma and weakening the Demons caught within the territory. The technique effectively prevented Paimon from continuing his principal battlefield strategy.[2]
Paimon angrily ordered Bebal and Abalam to destroy Jeanne with Hellfire. Although channeling such intense Holy power through a Vampire body injured Jeanne internally, the Heavenly Father informed her that she could safely maintain the state for approximately ten minutes. Jeanne considered that more than enough time.[2]
By the time Morgana again observed the confrontation, Jeanne had already overwhelmed Paimon's summons. One of the twins had been cut in half while Jeanne was physically tormenting the other.[3]
Paimon's confidence was already deteriorating, but he committed his decisive mistake by openly speaking about his attraction toward Jeanne's son, [[Adam Tepes]], and declaring that he intended to make Adam his "boytoy".[3]
Jeanne's demeanor changed immediately. The battle was no longer simply an encounter with an enemy Duke. To Jeanne, Paimon had personally threatened and insulted her child.
Paimon recognized the change too late. He attempted to explain himself and internally began abandoning Diablo's mission entirely, caring only about escaping alive.[3]
Jeanne told him that even the Heavenly Father would not be able to protect his Soul from what she intended to do. The Holy golden power surrounding her was replaced by a completely different presence: a gentle green authority connected to Nature.[3]
Morgana could not immediately identify it.
Paimon could.
As a Fallen Angel old enough to understand the deeper Biblical supernatural hierarchy, he recognized that Jeanne was invoking [[The Universal Tree]]. His remaining confidence disappeared immediately.[3]
Jeanne seized Paimon by the neck and called upon her elder brother. A green portal opened beside her. Although it appeared peaceful to Morgana, Paimon reacted with absolute horror. He began crying, trembling and losing control of his body because he understood what existed beyond it.[3]
Jeanne threw him through the portal.
Paimon emerged inside an apparently peaceful forest and was immediately addressed by an existence he could not properly comprehend. His body ceased obeying him and was lifted through external authority.[3]
The Being before him was the Universal Tree.
Paimon's perception could not properly interpret the Primordial existence. Merely attempting to look upon it began killing him, while the Tree casually remarked upon how extraordinary Paimon's mistake had been: Jeanne was normally extremely kind, yet Paimon had provoked her enough to ask her elder brother for direct intervention.[3]
The Universal Tree then destroyed Paimon's physical body.
His remains exploded throughout the surrounding forest while his Demonic Soul was forcibly exposed. The Universal Tree declared that his existence would be shattered, and Paimon's spiritual form began breaking apart piece by piece.[3]
The destruction continued beyond conventional Soul death. His spiritual limbs, torso and head were progressively shattered until only the deepest core of his existence remained. This core appeared as a sphere containing white flames.[3]
The Universal Tree explicitly declared that the being known as Paimon would cease to exist while the Soul itself would be recycled.[3]
The core broke apart into thousands of white particles. Those particles later gathered into another, smaller spiritual sphere, but this reconstructed Soul no longer represented Paimon.[3]
The Universal Tree gave the new existence another name in an ancient language and placed the reconstructed Soul into one of its branches. It was then returned to the universal reincarnation process, with the Judges of the Abyss responsible for its subsequent processing.[3]
The Universal Tree intended that new existence to eventually watch over Jeanne should immense spans of time ever cause her to suffer another loss of memory.[3]
Paimon himself did not survive this process. His memories, identity and individual existence were explicitly erased before the recycled Soul foundation was created.

### Relationships

#### Jeanne Elderblood
Jeanne became Paimon's primary opponent during the invasion of [[Nightingale]]. Their confrontation was particularly hostile because Paimon immediately recognized her history as Jeanne d'Arc and resented that someone who had become a Vampire could still access the [[Heavenly Father]]'s Divine Blessing.[2]
Jeanne systematically dismantled Paimon's battlefield advantages, first exposing his true appearance, then using his own Darkness constructs against him and finally destroying his mass summoning network through Judgment of Israel.[2]
Their confrontation became personal when Paimon threatened Adam. Jeanne subsequently invoked the Universal Tree and arranged a fate considerably more permanent than ordinary Demonic death.[3]

#### Adam Tepes
Paimon never directly fought Adam, but his expressed attraction toward Jeanne's son became the immediate cause of his permanent destruction. His declaration that he intended to make Adam his sexual plaything enraged Jeanne enough for her to summon the Universal Tree's intervention.[3]

#### Diablo
Paimon served within [[Diablo]]'s faction during the Demonic War and was trusted with a strategically important assault on [[Nightingale]]'s Royal Capital.[1]
His loyalty was nevertheless not absolute. Once Jeanne became overwhelmingly threatening, Paimon internally abandoned concern for Diablo and the invasion, thinking only about escaping with his life.[3]

#### The Universal Tree
[[The Universal Tree]] was the direct cause of Paimon's permanent end. Ordinary Demonic resurrection could potentially restore a Demon whose body had been destroyed while its Soul remained intact, but the Universal Tree bypassed this mechanism entirely by destroying Paimon's Soul, exposing its deepest core and erasing the identity defining him as Paimon.[3]
The spiritual material remaining afterward was recycled into an entirely new Soul rather than restoring Paimon.

### Skills & Abilities
As the 9th Key of Solomon, Paimon was one of the highest-ranking members of the old Ars Goetia. His greatest demonstrated battlefield advantage was his ability to rapidly deploy powerful Demons and entire Legions through Clan Paimon's summoning system.[1][2]

#### Fallen Angel and Demon Physiology
- High-Ranking Demon Physiology: By the modern era Paimon possessed the supernatural physical and spiritual properties of an extremely powerful Demon.
- Superhuman Strength: Paimon possessed strength vastly exceeding ordinary supernatural beings, although Jeanne physically overpowered him during their confrontation.[2]
- Superhuman Speed and Reflexes: His status as the 9th Pillar allowed him to participate in battles involving some of [[Nightingale]] and Hell's strongest combatants.
- Enhanced Durability: Paimon survived having his throat crushed and being impaled by several of his own Darkness spears.[2]
- Regeneration: As a high-ranking Demon, Paimon could recover from severe conventional physical injuries, although the checked chapters do not establish the absolute limits of his regeneration.
- Extreme Longevity: Paimon survived from the ancient period associated with the Fallen Angels into the modern supernatural era.
- Dependent Demonic [[Immortality]]: Conventional destruction of Paimon's body would not necessarily have constituted permanent death if his Soul remained capable of returning through Hell. [[The Universal Tree]]'s destruction of his Soul core bypassed this mechanism entirely.[3]
- Former Angelic Physiology: Paimon originated as an Angel before becoming a Fallen Angel and Demon.[3]
- The chapters depicting Paimon do not show him actively using Holy Light, Heavenly Blessings or other conventional Angelic techniques. Such abilities should therefore not be automatically indexed as retained powers.
- Flight: Paimon possessed functional wings in both his monstrous shell and revealed Fallen Angel form.[1][2]
- Miasma Manipulation: Dense Demonic Miasma continuously leaked from Paimon's monstrous form and surrounded his forces during battle.[1]
- Demonic Shell: Paimon could exist beneath or manifest a grotesque external Demonic form significantly different from his true humanoid appearance. Jeanne forcibly removed this shell.[2]
- Primordial Awareness: Paimon's age and Fallen Angel background allowed him to recognize the nature of the Universal Tree's influence before Morgana could identify it.[3]

#### Darkness Manipulation
- Umbrakinesis: Paimon could generate and shape supernatural Darkness for combat.[2]
- Darkness [[Spears]]: He could create several condensed spears of Darkness around himself and use them as offensive weapons.[2]
- Darkness Constructs: The spears retained enough physical stability to pierce Paimon's own Demonic body after Jeanne threw him through them.[2]

#### Repulsive Roar
- Sonic/Force Repulsion: Paimon could release a powerful roar or shout capable of forcibly throwing an approaching opponent backward. He used this against Jeanne after she impaled him with his own Darkness spears.[2]
- The exact mechanism is not fully explained, so it should not be classified more specifically than a supernatural repulsive vocal attack.

#### Clan Paimon Summoning
Paimon's most important demonstrated supernatural ability was the summoning system associated with his Clan and bloodline.[2]
- Demon Summoning: Paimon could summon Demons directly into a distant battlefield through red [[Magic]] Circles belonging to Clan Paimon.[2]
- Mass Summoning Circles: He could generate numerous summoning formations simultaneously across a large urban area.[2]
- Demon King Summoning: Paimon personally summoned King Bebal and Abalam, powerful twin Demons more than four meters tall.[2]
- Legion Deployment: After summoning Bebal and Abalam, Paimon began activating additional circles across [[Nightingale]]'s Royal Capital to summon further members of his Legions.[2]
- Bloodline Magic: The formations were explicitly associated with Clan Paimon, indicating that the summoning system was connected to his Clan or Demonic bloodline rather than merely being a generic spell every Demon possessed.[2]
- Remote Army Deployment: Paimon's system allowed him to reinforce a battlefield with large numbers of Demons without transporting them conventionally.
- Summoned Combatants: Paimon's summoned Demons possessed their own supernatural techniques. Bebal and Abalam, for example, were capable of using Hellfire under his command.[2]

#### Command Skills
- Demonic Legion Command: Paimon commanded personal Legions as one of the 72 Pillars and could coordinate those troops through his summoning network.[2]
- Military Experience: [[Diablo]] selected Paimon for the assault on the Royal Capital alongside [[Bael]], demonstrating the strategic importance attributed to him during the invasion.[1]
- Supernatural Knowledge: His existence as a Fallen Angel and ancient Duke gave him knowledge of Heaven, Primordial beings and supernatural hierarchies beyond that possessed by many younger Demons.[3]

### Weaknesses
- Holy/Divine Power: Jeanne's Judgment of Israel was extremely effective against Paimon's summoning system and Demonic forces. The Holy field erased his summoning circles, dispersed Miasma and weakened the Demons inside its range.[2]
- Dependence on Summoned Forces: A substantial portion of Paimon's battlefield advantage came from deploying other Demons through his bloodline. Jeanne's ability to shut down the summoning network sharply reduced that advantage.[2]
- Arrogance: Paimon's habit of underestimating opponents and speaking without restraint directly contributed to his death. Threatening Adam transformed Jeanne's hostility into a personal vendetta.[3]
- Cowardice Before Overwhelming Power: Once Paimon understood that the Universal Tree was involved, his confidence collapsed completely and he became incapable of meaningful resistance.[3]
- Primordial Authority: Paimon's Demonic immortality, Soul structure and high rank provided no protection against the Universal Tree, which could destroy and reconstruct the deepest layers of his existence through vastly superior authority.[3]

### Fate
Paimon's destruction differs fundamentally from ordinary death or Demonic resurrection.
[[The Universal Tree]] first destroyed his physical body and then shattered his Soul until only the deepest spiritual core remained.[3]
It then explicitly erased the identity known as Paimon. The core was broken into thousands of particles and reconstructed into a smaller Soul foundation that received another name and entered the reincarnation cycle.[3]
Therefore:
- Paimon is permanently deceased.
- Paimon's original personality and identity were not preserved as an ongoing individual.
- The reconstructed Soul created from his spiritual material is a new existence.
- The new existence was intended to eventually watch over Jeanne if she again suffered memory loss.
- Chapter 536 does not reveal the eventual identity of that reincarnated being.
- Paimon himself never became Jeanne's guardian and should not be treated as a member of Dragons Nest.
