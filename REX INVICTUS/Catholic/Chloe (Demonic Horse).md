### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 68
Chloe, sometimes rendered as Chloé, is a female Demonic Horse belonging to [[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]] and one of the supernatural warhorses traditionally used by the forces of the [[Adrasteia Clan]].[1][2]
Demonic Horses are a rare supernatural breed originating from an isolated region in the western territories of [[Nightingale]]. They are considerably stronger, tougher and faster than ordinary horses and are notoriously difficult to accept riders. Members of Eleanor's personal army must spend a long period developing a relationship with their individual horse until the partnership becomes comparable to a bond between siblings.[2]
Chloe is especially notable for her unusually selective personality. According to Eleanor, she ordinarily refuses to allow strangers to touch her at all. Nevertheless, she immediately accepted [[Victor Elderblood]] during their first meeting, allowing him to pet her and subsequently carry him toward the Royal Capital despite having never undergone the lengthy bonding process normally required by her species.[1][2]
The connection became so immediate that Chloe visibly preferred Victor after only a short period together, much to Eleanor's disbelief and jealousy. Even after Victor left, Chloe's attitude toward her longtime owner had noticeably changed, although she continued allowing Eleanor to ride her because the two shared an old and established bond.[3][4]

### Physical Appearance
Chloe is a supernatural mare belonging to the breed known as Demonic Horses.
Her most clearly described supernatural feature is her pair of blood-red eyes, a trait shared by the Demonic Horses used by Eleanor's personal army.[1]
The novel has not provided a detailed description of Chloe's coat color, exact height or additional anatomical differences from conventional horses. Visually, however, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] immediately recognized that Chloe and the other horses accompanying the Adrasteia soldiers were not normal animals even before their species was explained.[1]
Despite their ominous name, Ruby considered the Demonic Horses beautiful enough that she thought the term sounded unnecessarily frightening for creatures of their appearance.[2]

### Personality
Chloe is intelligent, selective, proud, affectionate toward those she accepts, and unusually expressive for a horse.
She is extremely cautious around unfamiliar people. Eleanor was shocked when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] simply approached Chloe and began stroking her because Chloe ordinarily never allowed strangers to touch her.[1]
Her initial reaction to Victor was completely different. Chloe immediately responded happily to his affection, moved her head closer to him, struck the ground excitedly, and allowed him to continue petting her.[1]
Chloe also appears capable of understanding far more than an ordinary animal. Victor spoke to her as though she could understand him, and Chloe repeatedly responded through movements and neighs appropriate to the conversation. When Victor jokingly promised to "save" her from Eleanor, Chloe reacted emotionally enough that those watching could interpret her response immediately.[1]
She rapidly became attached to Victor. During the journey to the Royal Capital she allowed him to ride her despite the fact that Demonic Horses normally require a prolonged bonding process with their riders.[2]
Chloe's attachment became strong enough to make Eleanor feel as though her own horse had abandoned her. Eleanor watched Victor riding Chloe and openly lamented that Chloe had effectively chosen him over her.[2]
Despite this new attachment, Chloe remains loyal to Eleanor. When Eleanor later mounted her, Chloe visibly showed that she was no longer enthusiastic about carrying her owner, but nevertheless permitted it because Eleanor was described as an ancient friend.[4]
This suggests that Chloe's relationship with Eleanor is considerably deeper than simple ownership; the two have spent enough time together to possess the sibling-like bond required between an Adrasteia rider and a Demonic Horse.

### Nature

#### Demonic Horse
Chloe belongs to a supernatural species referred to simply as Demonic Horses.[2]
The breed inhabits an isolated region in western [[Nightingale]] and is used by Eleanor Adrasteia's forces because of its superior physical abilities and highly selective bonding behavior.[2]
Demonic Horses are:
- Much stronger than conventional horses.[2]
- Much tougher than conventional horses.[2]
- Capable of extraordinary running speeds.[2]
- Highly resistant to accepting unfamiliar riders.[2]
- Intelligent enough to form deep emotional bonds with individual riders.[2]
Their temperament is one of the reasons Eleanor's cavalry is considered unusual. Simply possessing the strength to ride one is not sufficient; the horse itself must accept the rider.

#### Rider Bond
Eleanor explained that members of her forces undergo a lengthy process before a Demonic Horse properly accepts them.[2]
The relationship is expected to develop until the horse and rider possess a bond comparable to that of a brother and sister.[2]
This explains why Sasha was unable to simply mount one of the horses. When she tried, the animal immediately kicked her off.[2]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s interaction with Chloe was therefore highly abnormal. He bypassed a bonding process that normally requires considerable time almost instantaneously.[1][2]

### Synopsis

#### The Awakening
Chloe first appeared when [[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]] arrived with her personal army during the final portion of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s six-month training period under [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]].[1]
Victor was immediately fascinated by Eleanor's military force. Her soldiers wore black armor, carried enormous black lances and rode supernatural horses with blood-red eyes. Rather than becoming intimidated by the army's pressure, Victor ignored the soldiers and walked directly toward the horses because they interested him more.[1]
He quickly noticed that the animals were abnormal.
Among them was a riderless mare—Chloe.
Victor approached her without hesitation and placed his hand upon her head. Chloe immediately neighed happily rather than rejecting him.[1]
Victor began affectionately praising her as a "good girl", and Chloe responded enthusiastically by striking the ground and moving her head closer so that he could continue petting her.[1]
The interaction stunned Eleanor.
According to her, Chloe had never allowed a stranger to touch her before, yet Victor had been accepted immediately without making any effort to establish the normal rider bond.[1]
Victor quickly became attached to Chloe in return. When Eleanor objected to how casually he was treating her horse, Victor jokingly acted as though Chloe needed to be rescued from an insensitive owner. Chloe played along with the interaction strongly enough that Eleanor became increasingly frustrated at being portrayed as the villain while her own mare sided with Victor.[1]
When Eleanor and the others later prepared to travel toward the Royal Capital, Victor became bored with remaining inside the carriage and chose to ride Chloe instead.[2]
The experience greatly impressed him.
Chloe ran at an extremely high speed—faster than any automobile Victor had personally ridden before becoming a Vampire. Victor thoroughly enjoyed the sensation and spent much of the journey laughing while riding her.[2]
Eleanor, watching the situation from the carriage, reacted as though she had been romantically abandoned and asked Chloe why she had deserted her.[2]
The conversation surrounding Chloe also caused Eleanor to explain the Demonic Horse species in greater detail. She revealed that the animals lived in an isolated western region, possessed much greater physical strength and toughness than ordinary horses, and could not simply be ridden by anyone who approached them.[2]
Sasha provided an immediate example: she had attempted to ride one of the horses but had simply been kicked off.[2]
Eleanor explained that her soldiers needed to spend a long period acclimating their horses to them. The bond eventually had to become sufficiently close that horse and rider related to one another like siblings.[2]
Victor's immediate success with Chloe therefore stood out even more strongly.
The following day, the group reached the Royal Capital after reducing what would normally have been approximately two days of moderate travel to only one day.[3]
Victor was still riding Chloe when they arrived.
He considered her extremely fast and obedient and even briefly contemplated kidnapping her for himself before deciding that stealing Eleanor's horse would be going too far.[3]
At the Capital gate, Victor dismounted and affectionately stroked Chloe's mane. She responded happily but also appeared somewhat saddened that the ride had ended.[3]
This brief encounter left a lasting impression not only on Chloe but also on Eleanor. Much later, Eleanor remembered her first meeting with Victor specifically as the moment in which he played with her mare Chloe, identifying it as one of the earliest events that caused Victor to begin attracting her attention.[5]

#### Later Appearances
Chloe continued serving as Eleanor's personal mount after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s departure.
When Eleanor later prepared to leave following another meeting, she climbed onto Chloe as usual. Chloe responded with visible displeasure and deliberately turned her head away from her longtime master.[4]
Eleanor was shocked.
Walter immediately commented that Chloe no longer seemed to enjoy Eleanor riding her.[4]
Despite her dissatisfaction, Chloe did not actually reject Eleanor. The narration explains that she did not want to be ungrateful to her Master and, because Eleanor was an ancient friend, she continued allowing her to ride.[4]
Eleanor immediately blamed Victor for the change, internally complaining that he had effectively stolen her horse's affection after only a brief interaction.[4]
The incident further demonstrated how unusual Chloe's immediate attachment to Victor had been. A relationship that normally required considerable time to develop had been established between them almost instantly and remained strong enough to alter Chloe's behavior after Victor was gone.

### Skills & Abilities
Demonic Horse Physiology: Chloe belongs to the supernatural Demonic Horse species used by Eleanor Adrasteia's personal forces. Her species possesses substantially superior physical abilities compared with ordinary horses.[2]
- Supernatural Strength: Demonic Horses are explicitly much stronger than ordinary horses and are suitable for carrying heavily armed supernatural warriors belonging to Eleanor's personal army.[2]
- Supernatural Durability: Eleanor states that Demonic Horses are considerably tougher than ordinary horses, making them better suited for supernatural military service and extended high-speed travel.[2]
- Supernatural Speed: Chloe possesses exceptional running speed. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] described her as faster than any car he had personally ridden before his supernatural transformation.[2]
- Enhanced Stamina: Chloe maintained extremely high travel speed during the journey toward the Royal Capital, allowing Eleanor's group to significantly reduce the time required to cross the distance.[2][3]
- Enhanced Intelligence: Chloe displays emotional understanding far exceeding that of an ordinary horse. She reacts appropriately to conversations, recognizes individuals, expresses preferences regarding riders and appears to understand Victor when he speaks directly to her.[1][4]
- Selective Bonding: Chloe does not readily accept unfamiliar beings. Before Victor, she was known for refusing to allow strangers even to touch her.[1]
- Rider Rejection: Demonic Horses can physically reject unwanted riders. Sasha's attempt to ride one of Eleanor's horses ended with the animal throwing or kicking her off.[2] Although this feat was performed by another member of Chloe's species rather than Chloe personally, it demonstrates the breed's ability to refuse riders.

#### Animal Communication
Chloe can communicate through neighs, gestures, movement and emotional reactions.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was able to understand and respond to her almost immediately during their first encounter.[1]
His unusually effortless interaction with Chloe is later consistent with Victor's broader affinity for supernatural animals and Demonic Beasts, but Chloe herself is also clearly intelligent enough to express complex likes and dislikes without speech.

### Relationships

#### Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia
[[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]] is Chloe's owner, rider and longtime companion.
Their relationship predates Chloe's introduction by a considerable amount of time. Eleanor describes the bond required between a rider and Demonic Horse as comparable to siblinghood, while Chapter 98 explicitly identifies Eleanor as Chloe's ancient friend.[2][4]
Before meeting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Chloe was evidently deeply accustomed to Eleanor and served as her personal mount.
Victor's arrival complicated the relationship somewhat. Chloe became so fond of him that Eleanor repeatedly reacted with jealousy and disbelief, feeling as though her own mare had abandoned her.[2]
After Victor left, Chloe even displayed reluctance when Eleanor mounted her. Nevertheless, her longstanding loyalty and friendship with Eleanor remained strong enough that she continued carrying her despite her obvious preference for Victor.[4]

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] formed an extraordinary bond with Chloe immediately upon meeting her.
Although Chloe normally refused to allow strangers to touch her, she accepted Victor the moment he approached and happily allowed him to stroke her head.[1]
She later permitted him to ride her without the lengthy bonding process normally required by Demonic Horses.[2]
Victor likewise became extremely fond of Chloe. He spent the journey toward the Royal Capital riding her rather than remaining in Eleanor's luxurious carriage and briefly considered kidnapping her because of how much he enjoyed the experience.[2][3]
Even after they separated, Victor's influence remained obvious. Chloe became noticeably less enthusiastic when Eleanor rode her, causing Eleanor to conclude that Victor had effectively "stolen" her horse.[4]
The first interaction between Victor and Chloe also became personally important to Eleanor. Much later, when reflecting upon her growing feelings for Victor, Eleanor remembered that their relationship had begun with the strange man who effortlessly befriended her normally antisocial mare.[5]
