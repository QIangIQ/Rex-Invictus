### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 684
Uriel is an ancient Angel, a Seraph and one of the Seven Heavenly Virtues serving the [[Heavenly Father]]. He represents the Heavenly Virtue of Patience, placing him among the Concept-bearing Angels who stand above ordinary Seraphim within the hierarchy of Seventh Heaven.[1]
Uriel made his first direct appearance during the climax of the Demonic War, observing the battlefield alongside Ariel and [[Cassiel]] after [[Victor Elderblood]] opened the Gates of Hell and deployed the armies of his newly conquered realm. Unlike the more restrained Ariel, Uriel reacted openly and profanely to Victor's power, immediately recognizing that Victor had effectively driven Heaven out of the conflict by transforming the battlefield into an environment dominated by extraordinarily dense Demonic Miasma.[2]

### Physical Appearance
Uriel's normal physical appearance has not yet been described in sufficient detail within the novel.
As one of the Seven Heavenly Virtues, Uriel is a Seraph and therefore belongs to Heaven's six-winged Angelic hierarchy. However, details such as his hair color, eye color, height, armor and other individual characteristics have not been explicitly established in the chapters currently depicting him.
The gallery includes artwork depicting a True Form, but the novel has not yet provided enough direct narration concerning such a transformation to treat every visual feature of the artwork as canonical.

### Personality
Despite representing the Virtue of Patience, Uriel is surprisingly blunt, informal and expressive.
His introduction immediately distinguishes him from the more disciplined and ceremonious Angels surrounding him. Upon witnessing the enormous quantity of power [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] released onto the Demonic battlefield, Uriel openly swore in disbelief. When Ariel reprimanded him for his language, Uriel dismissed the complaint and told her to look at what was happening before expecting him to behave normally.[2]
This exchange indicates that Uriel is significantly less concerned with maintaining the dignified image traditionally associated with Heaven than Ariel.
He is nevertheless highly perceptive. Uriel quickly understood the strategic implications of Victor's intervention. The Miasma flooding the battlefield had become so dense that Heaven's Angels could no longer safely participate, meaning Victor had effectively removed the Angelic faction from the war while replacing them with his own Demonic forces.[2]
Uriel summarized the situation clearly: the conflict was no longer a conventional confrontation between Light and Darkness. With Heaven removed from the immediate battlefield, it had become Darkness against Darkness—Victor's overwhelming crimson Negativity opposing [[Diablo]]'s Demonic forces.[2]
He was also one of the first Angels present to verbally recognize how extreme Victor's supernatural nature had become. Uriel compared Victor's Darkness to an abyss of Negativity and depravity and observed that Diablo appeared almost insignificant beside him.[2]
Although expressive, Uriel is not shown disobeying his Father. Like the other Heavenly Virtues, he ultimately accepts the Heavenly Father's judgment concerning whether Heaven should intervene.

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Uriel made his direct debut during the final stages of the Demonic War after [[Victor Elderblood]] arrived on the battlefield as the newly recognized King of [[Biblical Hell]].[2]
Victor's entry radically changed the conditions of the conflict.
After passing through the Gates of Hell without the sacrificial requirements previously limiting [[Diablo]]'s forces, Victor summoned high-ranking Demons and then released the Legions he had cultivated within Hell.[2]
Enormous quantities of pure Miasma began flowing from the Gates.
The concentration was unlike the ordinary Demonic contamination produced during the existing war. The Miasma spread across the battlefield like a supernatural ocean, killing weaker Demons belonging to Diablo and creating an environment increasingly incompatible with Angelic existence.[2]
Even [[Azrael]], the Angel of Death and one of Heaven's most powerful Original Seraphim, was forced to move away from the advancing concentration.[2]
Uriel watched the event alongside Ariel and [[Cassiel]].
Ariel could barely reconcile the entity before her with the Vampire who had previously saved her life, while Uriel was considerably less restrained in expressing his disbelief.[2]
He openly cursed after witnessing Victor's display of power.
Ariel immediately reprimanded him for his language, but Uriel dismissed her objection and pointed toward the battlefield, essentially arguing that nobody could reasonably be expected to remain composed while witnessing what Victor was doing.[2]
Cassiel focused upon the environmental consequences, noting that the land might require centuries to recover from the amount of Miasma being released.
Uriel considered that a secondary problem.
He instead emphasized the immediate strategic reality: Victor had effectively kicked Heaven out of the war.[2]
The Angels could no longer safely enter the central battlefield because of the overwhelming infernal environment, while Victor had deployed hundreds of thousands of Demonic soldiers capable of operating freely within it.
Uriel recognized that the nature of the war had therefore fundamentally changed.
What had previously been a struggle between Heaven and Hell—Light against Darkness—had become a confrontation between two forms of Darkness.[2]
Victor's presence represented one side: an enormous abyss of crimson Negativity, hatred and Demonic power.
Diablo represented the other.
From Uriel's perspective, the difference between them had become so extreme that Diablo appeared almost like a child beside Victor.[2]
Uriel was particularly disturbed by the quantity of Negative Energy Victor could contain inside himself. He questioned how any single existence could accumulate such an enormous amount of Negativity without being destroyed by it.[2]
Ariel subsequently contacted the [[Heavenly Father]] and asked what the Angels should do.
The Heavenly Father ordered them to do nothing.
He explained that Victor was beginning to walk a path directly opposite his own. Where the Heavenly Father embodied Creation, Light and Positivity, Victor was developing toward Destruction, Darkness and Negativity. The seed of that Divinity was still immature, but its growth was occurring at an extraordinary rate.[2]
Uriel and the other Angels consequently remained outside the central battlefield while Victor took control of the war against Diablo.

### Seven Heavenly Virtue
Uriel is one of the Seven Heavenly Virtues, an elite group of Concept-bearing Seraphim serving directly beneath the highest orders of Heaven.
Although members of the group remain Angels rather than conventional Gods, possession of their respective Concepts places them above ordinary Seraphim in supernatural authority.[1]
The known hierarchy consists broadly of:
- Original Seraphim — the earliest and highest-order Angels possessing exceptional spiritual authority.
- Seven Heavenly Virtues — Seraphim granted Conceptual powers corresponding to the seven Virtues.
- Ordinary Seraphim — six-winged Angels beneath the Virtues.[1]
Uriel embodies Patience within this structure.
Later explanations concerning the Virtues establish that their Concepts were granted by the [[Heavenly Father]]. These powers allow the Virtues to wield forms of Conceptual authority comparable in principle to Divine powers, although the Virtues themselves are not automatically equivalent to complete Gods.[3]

### Skills & Abilities
Uriel has not yet received a dedicated battle demonstrating the complete extent of his powers. His confirmed abilities primarily come from his racial status and position as the Virtue of Patience.

#### Angel Physiology
- Seraph Physiology: Uriel is a Seraph and possesses the supernatural physical and spiritual characteristics of Heaven's highest general Angelic order.
- Superhuman Strength: As a Seraph and Heavenly Virtue, Uriel possesses physical capabilities far beyond ordinary Angels and mortal supernatural beings.
- Superhuman Speed and Reflexes: His Angelic physiology grants supernatural movement and reaction speeds.
- Enhanced Durability: Uriel possesses the resilient spiritual body expected of a high-ranking Angel.
- Enhanced Stamina: He can operate within environments and conflicts involving Divine and Demonic beings vastly beyond mortal limitations.
- Extreme Longevity: Uriel has existed for eons and predates modern Humanity.
- Flight: As a Seraph, Uriel possesses six wings allowing supernatural flight.
- [[Holy Energy]]: Uriel naturally possesses the Positive Holy Energy associated with Heaven.
- Holy Aura: As one of Heaven's high-ranking Seraphim, Uriel possesses a Divine Angelic presence.
- Miasma Resistance: Uriel can endure levels of Demonic contamination far beyond ordinary Angels.
- This resistance is not unlimited. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s extraordinarily concentrated Miasma made the battlefield unsuitable for Heaven's forces and effectively forced Uriel and the other Angels away from the central conflict.[2]

#### Conceptual Authority
- Concept of Patience: Uriel possesses the Concept corresponding to the Heavenly Virtue of Patience.[1][3]
- Conceptual Empowerment: Possession of a Concept grants Uriel supernatural authority beyond that of ordinary Seraphim.[1]
- Conceptual Existence: Patience is not merely a ceremonial title; it represents an actual supernatural Concept granted through the [[Heavenly Father]]'s authority.[3]
- The specific applications of Uriel's Patience have not yet been directly demonstrated. Abilities such as temporal manipulation, infinite stamina, emotional suppression, damage negation or automatic resistance should therefore not be attributed to him without further evidence.

#### Energy Perception
- Supernatural Energy Perception: Uriel immediately recognized the abnormal scale and quality of the power [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] released during the Demonic War.[2]
- Negativity Perception: He could perceive the extraordinary quantity of Negative Energy surrounding Victor and understood that the phenomenon far exceeded ordinary Demonic Miasma.[2]
- Environmental Analysis: Uriel recognized that Victor's Miasma had fundamentally changed the tactical environment and removed Heaven's ability to freely participate in the battlefield.[2]

#### Strategic Awareness
- Battlefield Analysis: Uriel rapidly understood the political and military consequences of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s arrival, correctly concluding that the conflict had ceased being Heaven against Hell and had become a battle between Victor and [[Diablo]]'s respective forms of Darkness.[2]
- Tactical Judgment: While [[Cassiel]] initially focused upon the long-term environmental destruction caused by the Miasma, Uriel prioritized the immediate military consequence that Heaven had effectively been displaced from the conflict.[2]

### Power
Uriel's exact combat strength has not been directly demonstrated.
His position as one of the Seven Heavenly Virtues places him above ordinary Seraphim in authority due to his possession of a Concept.[1]
However, he should not automatically be scaled to Original Seraphim such as Michael, [[Gabriel]] or [[Azrael]]. Those Angels occupy a different position within Heaven's hierarchy and possess substantially greater direct feats.
Likewise, no checked chapter establishes that Uriel personally possesses the combat power of a God-King or Primordial God.
Until Uriel participates in a significant direct battle, his most reliable classification remains:
Heavenly Virtue / Concept-bearing Seraph

### Relationships

#### Ariel Elderblood
Ariel is Uriel's sister and fellow Heavenly Virtue.
Their Chapter 684 interaction suggests a familiar sibling relationship. When Uriel cursed while reacting to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s power, Ariel immediately reprimanded him for his language.[2]
Uriel did not respond with the formality expected from a subordinate. Instead, he dismissed the complaint and told Ariel to look at the battlefield before expecting him to behave normally.[2]
The exchange demonstrates that while Ariel eventually became Heaven's principal military commander, Uriel interacts with her primarily as a sibling rather than a distant superior.

#### Cassiel
[[Cassiel]] is another of Uriel's siblings and fellow Heavenly Virtue.
The two observed [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s intervention together in Chapter 684. Cassiel focused upon the environmental damage caused by Victor's Miasma, while Uriel redirected attention toward the immediate strategic consequences for Heaven.[2]
Their differing reactions illustrate their personalities: Cassiel was analytical and concerned with the damage being caused, whereas Uriel was bluntly focused on what the event meant for the ongoing war.

#### Heavenly Father
The [[Heavenly Father]] is Uriel's creator, sovereign and the source of the Concept associated with his Virtue.
Uriel remains loyal to the Heavenly Father and follows Heaven's larger strategic decisions even when he expresses himself considerably less formally than several of his siblings.

#### Victor Elderblood
Uriel has not had a meaningful direct conversation with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] in the checked chapters, but his first appearance was dominated by witnessing Victor's intervention in the Demonic War.
Uriel immediately recognized that Victor had become something far beyond an ordinary Vampire or Demon King. He was particularly disturbed by the amount of Negative Energy Victor could contain and by the ease with which Victor transformed the entire supernatural environment around the battlefield.[2]
Uriel's assessment that Victor had effectively removed Heaven from the war was correct: the Angels could no longer operate normally within the increasingly dense Miasma while Victor's Demonic Legions were perfectly adapted to it.[2]
