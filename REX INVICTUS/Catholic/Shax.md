### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 654
Shax was the 44th Demon Pillar of the Ars Goetia, traditionally holding the rank of Marquess among the seventy-two high-ranking Demons who formed the nobility of [[Biblical Hell]]. He governed his own territory and commanded tens of thousands of Demons under [[Diablo]]'s regime. Shax encountered [[Victor Elderblood]] during Victor's conquest of Hell, when Victor arrived at the capital of the 44th Pillar and offered every Demon present a simple choice: submit to him or die.[1]
Unlike the majority of the Demons within his territory, Shax refused to kneel. He attempted to order his forces into battle after Victor's countdown had already begun, but his authority collapsed as more than sixty percent of the Demons chose survival over loyalty to their Pillar. When the countdown reached zero, Victor moved through the territory at such speed that the surviving observers could perceive only streaks of Lightning. Approximately three seconds later, Shax and the tens of thousands of Demons who had refused submission had been slaughtered, while those who had knelt immediately joined Victor's rapidly expanding army.[1]

### Physical Appearance
Shax possessed a gigantic and highly monstrous Demonic body standing approximately 10 meters tall. His anatomy combined characteristics belonging to several different creatures: he had the face of a horse, the eyes and wings of an owl, two equine hind legs, and the muscular torso of a man possessing four arms.[1]
Despite his enormous and intimidating form, Shax's appearance offered him no psychological advantage when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] reached his capital. He was visibly terrified before Victor had even crossed the city gate, with his fear increasing simply from watching the Vampire Progenitor casually approach his territory.[1]

### Personality
Shax was proud, temperamental and strongly attached to the authority granted by his position as a Demon Pillar, but his confrontation with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] revealed that this confidence depended heavily upon the existing hierarchy of Hell. Before Victor even entered the city, Shax was already terrified by his approach and desperately demanded information concerning reinforcements from [[Diablo]], the Seven Deadly Sins and the Four Horsemen.[1]
His fear did not prevent him from becoming aggressive toward his own subordinates. When a Lesser Demon nervously informed him that Victor had arrived, Shax angrily berated the servant despite already seeing Victor himself. He subsequently expressed frustration at the absence of Hell's greater elites, particularly criticizing Sloth for her inactivity and believing that her unusual Power might have been useful for stopping Victor.[1]
Shax also displayed considerable stubbornness and poor adaptability. News of Victor annihilating [[Vine]]'s Legions had already spread through Hell, and the Demons under Shax understood that surrender offered them a genuine chance of survival. Shax nevertheless attempted to organize resistance even while most of his territory was visibly abandoning him. His refusal to accept the new balance of power contrasted with the pragmatism displayed by his own soldiers, many of whom immediately submitted once Victor's ultimatum made their options clear.[1]
Although frightened, Shax did not personally kneel. He chose resistance and attempted to command his remaining forces until Victor's countdown ended, resulting in his immediate death.

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Shax governed the territory belonging to the 44th Demon Pillar during the period in which [[Diablo]] still claimed authority over [[Biblical Hell]]. His position placed him among the Ars Goetia, the elite Demonic nobles whose territories, armies and political authority formed much of Diablo's traditional power structure.
That order began collapsing after [[Victor Elderblood]] was forcibly transported into Hell. Instead of remaining trapped, Victor began moving through the realm and systematically destroying or absorbing the armies placed in his path. His campaign rapidly became known throughout Hell after he annihilated the thirty-eight Legions belonging to [[Vine]]. Information travelled quickly through Demonic servants and neighboring territories, meaning that by the time Victor approached Shax's capital, its inhabitants already understood that an overwhelmingly dangerous invader was advancing through the Demon World.[1]
Shax watched Victor approaching from the edge of his territory in visible terror. Victor was wearing full black armor and carrying [[Junketsu]], casually walking toward the city despite being surrounded by an enemy territory. Shax desperately questioned his subordinate about Diablo's response and learned that the Demon King had requested assistance from Pride, but Pride was occupied in the Hell belonging to the [[Egyptian Pantheon]] and would require considerable time to arrive. With immediate aid unavailable, Shax demanded to know where the Four Horsemen and the other Deadly Sins were, even complaining that Sloth should finally make herself useful because her Power might be capable of stopping Victor.[1]
Before Shax could organize a meaningful defense, Victor reached the city gate and released enough pressure to immediately focus the attention of the entire capital upon himself. Rather than attack without warning, Victor offered every Demon the same choice he had increasingly used throughout his campaign: kneel in submission or die. He then gave them five seconds to decide.[1]
The ultimatum shattered Shax's authority almost immediately. Many of the more pragmatic Demons knelt as soon as they understood what Victor was offering, particularly because news of Vine's destroyed Legions had already established what happened to forces attempting to oppose him. Other Demons panicked and struggled to choose between their existing ruler and the invader standing at their gates. When Victor's count reached three, Shax finally attempted to command everyone to prepare for battle, but the order was interrupted as Victor continued counting.[1]
By the time Victor reached two, more than sixty percent of Shax's territory had already knelt, including the Lesser Demon who had previously been reporting directly to Shax. Shax could only react with frustration as the majority of his subjects effectively abandoned him while he was still standing before them.[1]
When the countdown reached zero, Victor drew Junketsu and disappeared from ordinary perception. The kneeling Demons could see only streaks of Lightning moving throughout the territory as Victor eliminated everyone who had chosen resistance. Within approximately three seconds, he returned to the same position and sheathed his weapon. A moment later, blood erupted throughout the city as the bodies of Shax and the resisting Demons collapsed. Shax himself had been reduced to minced flesh before he could meaningfully fight back.[1]
Approximately 40,000 Demons were destroyed alongside Shax during the purge, while the majority who had submitted survived.[1] Shax's former servant was particularly shaken by how effortlessly Victor had killed a Demon Pillar, as members of the Ars Goetia had traditionally been regarded by ordinary Demons as some of the strongest Beings in Hell. Rather than merely producing fear, however, Shax's death also inspired ambition among the witnesses; seeing the enormous difference between Victor and their former master caused many of them to desire greater strength for themselves.[1]
Victor then ordered the surviving Demons to rise and follow him. None refused. The forces that had belonged to Shax therefore became another component of the expanding Demonic army following Victor during his conquest of Hell.[1]
Shax's Soul did not enter the ordinary Demonic cycle after his death. Victor was consuming the Souls of the enemies killed throughout his campaign, preventing those Demons from simply reforming within Hell and allowing him to accumulate their memories, knowledge and supernatural resources.[1]

### Skills & Abilities
Shax was one of the seventy-two Demon Pillars and consequently stood above ordinary Demons within [[Diablo]]'s former hierarchy. However, his confrontation with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was so one-sided that the novel never provides an opportunity for Shax to demonstrate a unique named ability or individual combat technique. Powers should therefore be limited to characteristics established through his Demonic physiology, physical form and political position rather than assigning abilities from traditional mythology without evidence.
- Demon Physiology: Shax was a high-ranking Infernal Demon and member of the Ars Goetia. His supernatural body possessed physical and metaphysical characteristics vastly beyond ordinary mortal life.[1]
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics: Shax's enormous ten-meter body possessed the strength, durability and other enhanced attributes expected from a Demon powerful enough to become one of Hell's seventy-two Pillars. The exact limits of these attributes are unknown because Victor killed him before he could demonstrate them in battle.[1]
- Gigantic Size: Shax stood approximately ten meters tall, giving him vastly greater natural reach and mass than a Human-sized opponent.[1]
- Four-Armed Physiology: His muscular humanoid torso possessed four arms, theoretically allowing him to attack, grapple or manipulate multiple objects simultaneously.[1]
- Natural Flight: Shax possessed enormous owl-like wings. Although the chapter does not depict him entering aerial combat, these wings form part of his natural Demonic anatomy.[1]
- Enhanced Vision: Shax possessed owl-like eyes as part of his hybrid Demonic anatomy. The novel does not establish any supernatural ocular ability beyond their physical appearance, so abilities such as supernatural night vision should not be assumed without further evidence.[1]
- Natural Weapons: His enormous hybrid body combined four arms, equine legs and other non-Human anatomical characteristics that could function as physical weapons in close combat.
- Demonic Energy: As a Demon Pillar, Shax possessed the supernatural Energy characteristic of Hell's high-ranking Demons, although no individualized application is shown before his death.
- Miasma Compatibility: Shax was naturally adapted to the Miasma-filled environment of [[Biblical Hell]] as a native Infernal Demon.
- Demonic Longevity: High-ranking Demons possess extremely long lifespans compared with mortal species. Shax's exact age is never stated.
- Conditional [[Immortality]] / Demonic Reconstitution: Demons normally possess the ability to eventually reform within Hell after conventional death, with more powerful Demons requiring greater periods to return. This process can be bypassed through certain methods, including Holy Light, specialized anti-Demon powers and the complete absorption of the Demon's Soul. Shax was unable to benefit from normal Demonic reconstitution because Victor consumed the Souls of the Demons he killed during his campaign.[1]
- Demonic Command: As the 44th Demon Pillar, Shax possessed command authority over the Demons inhabiting his territory and could issue military orders to his subordinate forces.[1]
- Large-Scale Military Command: Tens of thousands of Demons lived or served within Shax's territory. Approximately 40,000 ultimately chose resistance and were killed, while more than sixty percent of the population submitted to Victor.[1]
- Territorial Authority: Shax ruled the capital and surrounding domain belonging to the 44th Pillar under Diablo's political system.

#### Power
Shax was considered one of the Demon Pillars, a class of Demons traditionally viewed by the ordinary inhabitants of Hell as among the strongest members of their Race. The reaction of Shax's surviving servant demonstrates how deeply this reputation was embedded in Demonic society: witnessing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] reduce Shax to pieces within seconds shattered his belief that a Pillar represented an almost unreachable level of power.[1]
However, Shax possesses almost no usable individual combat scaling because his only direct confrontation was against Victor during a period when the latter had already become overwhelmingly superior to ordinary Pillar-class Demons. Victor killed Shax and approximately 40,000 resisting Demons across the territory in roughly three seconds, giving Shax no opportunity to demonstrate a technique, weapon or serious exchange of attacks.[1]
The feat consequently establishes Victor's superiority far more clearly than it establishes Shax's exact level relative to other Pillars. Shax should not automatically be treated as comparable to exceptional members of the Ars Goetia such as [[Bael]] or [[Agares]] merely because all belonged to the same seventy-two-Pillar political system.

#### Weaknesses
- Inferior Speed: Shax was completely incapable of reacting to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] once the latter attacked. Victor crossed the territory, eliminated Shax and tens of thousands of other resisting Demons, and returned to his original position within approximately three seconds.[1]
- Fear: Victor's approach visibly terrified Shax before the battle had even begun, affecting his composure and causing him to desperately search for reinforcements.[1]
- Dependence on the Old Hierarchy: Shax expected [[Diablo]], the Horsemen or the Seven Deadly Sins to provide assistance and failed to adapt when those reinforcements were unavailable.[1]
- Poor Political Adaptability: Unlike the majority of his subordinates, Shax refused to recognize that surrender was the only realistic means of surviving Victor's campaign.
- Soul Absorption: A Demon normally has the possibility of eventually reforming after conventional death, but consumption of the Soul prevents that process. Victor consumed Shax after killing him, making his death permanent.[1]

### Relationships

#### Diablo
[[Diablo]] was Shax's sovereign and the ruler beneath whom he served as the 44th Demon Pillar. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] reached Shax's capital, Shax immediately demanded to know what Diablo intended to do about the invasion, demonstrating that he still expected the Demon King's hierarchy to protect the territories beneath it.[1]
Diablo had apparently attempted to send Pride to assist against Victor, but Pride was occupied with matters involving the [[Egyptian Pantheon]]'s Hell and could not arrive quickly enough. Shax was therefore left without the elite reinforcement he believed necessary to survive Victor's attack.[1]

#### Victor Elderblood
Shax encountered [[Victor Elderblood]] only once, during Victor's conquest of Hell. By the time Victor approached the 44th Pillar's capital, Shax already knew enough about the destruction of neighboring Demonic forces to be terrified of him.[1]
Victor nevertheless gave Shax the same opportunity offered to the other Demons: kneel and survive or remain hostile and die. Shax refused submission and attempted to organize resistance. When the countdown ended, Victor killed him almost instantly and consumed his Soul, while the majority of Shax's surviving subjects abandoned their former allegiance and followed Victor instead.[1]

#### Sloth Abyss Verneila
Shax specifically mentioned Sloth while desperately searching for possible reinforcements. He complained that she had contributed almost nothing to the war and believed her Power might be useful for stopping [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[1]
His comments provide one of the earlier references to Sloth's extreme inactivity before her direct participation in the Demonic War.

#### Pride
Pride was the Deadly Sin [[Diablo]] attempted to send toward Shax's territory after becoming aware of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s advance. Pride was unable to arrive in time because he was occupied within the [[Egyptian Pantheon]]'s Hell.[1]

### Legacy
Shax's death became another important demonstration of the collapse of the old Ars Goetia hierarchy during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s conquest. Ordinary Demons had spent their lives regarding the Pillars as the strongest and most authoritative members of their society, but Victor reduced the 44th Pillar and tens of thousands of his loyalists to corpses in only a few seconds.[1]
The psychological effect was immediate. Shax's surviving servant questioned whether killing a Pillar could truly be so easy, while the other Demons simultaneously developed fear of Victor and a renewed desire to become stronger. Their loyalty shifted from Shax's abolished authority toward the overwhelmingly stronger ruler standing before them.[1]
The surviving population subsequently joined Victor's growing army, meaning Shax's decision to resist not only resulted in his permanent death but also transferred most of the military resources remaining in his territory directly into the hands of the enemy he had attempted to oppose.[1]
