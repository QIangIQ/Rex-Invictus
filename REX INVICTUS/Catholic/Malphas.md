### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 658
Malphas, also known as President Malphas, was a High-Level Demon, the 39th Key of Solomon and ruler of the 39th-ranked Pillar territory of Hell.[1] He belonged to the old 72 Pillars of Hell under [[Diablo]] and controlled a major Demonic city populated by more than 500,000 Demons, in addition to his personal Legions and Generals.[1]
Malphas was one of the Pillar Demons who refused to submit when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] Alucard began systematically conquering Hell. Unlike [[Vine]] and Vepar, who eventually recognized the impossibility of opposing Victor and joined him, Malphas chose open resistance even after Victor arrived with more than one hundred Demonic Legions and personally offered every Demon in the city an opportunity to surrender.[1]
His decision proved fatal. After most of the population abandoned the city and even his most trusted Generals attempted to flee, Malphas fought Victor personally. He transformed his Miasma into an enormous twenty-meter Demonic body and successfully destroyed the first lightning beast Victor summoned against him, demonstrating that he was far stronger than the ordinary Demons surrounding him. Victor simply responded by summoning several more beasts simultaneously and annihilating Malphas' remaining resistance.[1]
Malphas was subsequently killed during Victor's conquest and his Soul became one of the numerous Demonic Souls absorbed during Victor's campaign through Hell.

### Physical Appearance
Malphas was an enormous Demon standing approximately 7 meters tall in his normal form. He possessed long Demonic wings, a large tail and prominent horns.[1]
His appearance reflected the monstrous physiology common among powerful ancient Demons rather than the more Human-like forms used by several other Pillar Demons.
During combat, Malphas surrounded himself with enormous quantities of Miasma and expanded his form until he became a 20-meter-tall Demon composed from concentrated Miasma.[1]
The transformation dramatically increased his apparent size and physical power, allowing him to directly intercept and destroy one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s lightning constructs with a punch.

### Personality
Malphas was proud, stubborn, experienced and extremely confident in his own strength.
When informed that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was approaching his territory with more than one hundred Legions of Demons, Malphas did not initially display fear. He could already sense Victor's presence from a great distance and immediately rejected the idea of retreating, simply declaring that they would fight.[1]
His confidence appears to have developed from considerable battlefield experience. Malphas believed himself to be an experienced warrior who had survived far worse conflicts before Victor's birth and later shouted that Victor should not underestimate someone who had been fighting for far longer than the young Vampire had existed.[1]
However, his pride badly distorted his judgment.
Victor gave every Demon in the city five seconds to choose between surrender and remaining Malphas' enemy. Thousands abandoned the territory almost immediately. Malphas nevertheless remained where he was and allowed the countdown to finish, apparently assuming that his armies and personal strength could still resist the invasion.[1]
Even his own Generals quickly abandoned that belief.
When Victor began gathering enough golden Lightning to cover the city, Malphas' two most trusted Generals attempted to flee rather than die beside him. Malphas was visibly shocked by their betrayal, demonstrating that he had overestimated not only the strength of his faction but the loyalty of those closest to him.[1]
His survival instinct finally overcame his pride once he understood the scale of Victor's attack. Malphas attempted to call for Victor to wait and recognized that even he would not survive what was coming.
By then, Victor refused.
Malphas had already been given an opportunity to surrender.
Despite this fatal misjudgment, Malphas did not simply collapse without resistance. Once battle became unavoidable, he gathered his Miasma, transformed and personally destroyed Victor's first summoned beast. Even after understanding that Victor was vastly stronger than him, he continued fighting until Victor overwhelmed him completely.[1]

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
During [[Victor Elderblood]]'s campaign to conquer Hell, the old Pillar system began collapsing at extraordinary speed. Victor first accumulated enormous numbers of Demons into his own army and then began moving upward through the traditional rankings, either forcing Pillars to submit or destroying them entirely.
By the time Victor approached the 39th-ranked territory, Malphas ruled a city containing more than 500,000 Demons.[1]
A Lesser Demon rushed into Malphas' palace and warned that Alucard had arrived with more than one hundred Legions.
Malphas was already aware.
Victor made no attempt to conceal himself, and Malphas could sense his presence from an enormous distance. When asked what they should do, the Pillar Demon considered the question ridiculous.
They would fight.
[1]
Malphas summoned his Generals and confirmed that the defenses were prepared. His subordinates initially remained confident, claiming that despite the enormous number of Demons accompanying Victor, they had fought worse battles before.[1]
The confidence disappeared the moment Victor announced himself.
An overwhelming pressure descended across the entire city.
Victor stood upon a mountain of Ice accompanied by Vepar, who had already abandoned the old Pillar hierarchy and allied herself with him.
Victor gave the Demons a single choice:
Surrender within five seconds or remain inside the city as his enemies.
[1]
Thousands immediately fled toward Victor's army.
Some flew.
Others ran through the streets or jumped over buildings.
Several even grabbed companions and carried them while escaping.
Within only a few seconds, virtually everyone unwilling to die for Malphas had abandoned the city, leaving behind Malphas and the Legions still loyal to him.[1]
Victor reached zero.
Golden Lightning began filling the sky.
He handed [[Junketsu]] to Vepar and warned her not to touch its hilt before launching himself above the city. Lightning covered his armor, his Vampiric features became more pronounced and enormous bat-like wings emerged from his back as a storm formed overhead.[1]
At that point, even Malphas' two Generals abandoned him.
Both attempted to escape, openly declaring that they did not intend to die alongside a fool.
Malphas could only stare in disbelief as the men he considered his most trusted Generals fled from his side.[1]
The true scale of Victor's attack finally broke Malphas' confidence.
He called out for Victor to wait.
Victor refused.
Malphas had been given five seconds and had consciously chosen resistance. Victor declared that he only spoke once and that Malphas would now live with the consequences of his choice.[1]
Victor raised a finger toward him.
"Kirin."
[1]
The golden storm condensed into a gigantic lightning beast which descended toward the city.
Malphas immediately fought back.
Massive quantities of Miasma gathered around his body and expanded outward until a 20-meter Demon formed entirely from concentrated Demonic Energy. Malphas struck the descending creature directly in the face and destroyed it, dispersing the construct into streams of electricity.[1]
The successful defense restored enough confidence for Malphas to boast that he had been fighting long before Victor had even been born and that the young Vampire was nothing compared with his experience.
Victor applauded.
He then asked Malphas what he intended to do about the rest.
Malphas looked upward.
Several enormous red eyes had appeared throughout the golden storm.
A chorus of monstrous roars shook the territory as multiple additional lightning beasts began emerging from the clouds.[1]
Only then did Malphas fully understand the difference between them.
He questioned why someone as absurdly powerful as Victor was even bothering to attack what Malphas now described as a lowly Demon.
Victor offered no sympathy.
"Become dog food, Malphas."
[1]
The summoned beasts descended simultaneously upon Malphas and the remaining city.
The resulting attack engulfed the territory in an enormous explosion, ending Malphas' resistance and removing another Pillar from Victor's path toward the throne of Hell.[1]
Malphas' defeat became another demonstration of Victor's conquest strategy. Demons willing to submit were incorporated into his growing army and given opportunities for advancement, while rulers who rejected his ultimatum were annihilated regardless of their previous status.
The fall of the 39th Pillar also reinforced the lesson already demonstrated by the defeats surrounding [[Vine]], [[Shax]] and the other Pillars: the old numerical hierarchy of the Ars Goetia no longer provided meaningful protection against Victor.
Malphas' Soul was subsequently included among the Demonic Souls absorbed during Victor's conquest of Hell.

### Skills & Abilities
Malphas was the 39th-ranked Pillar Demon and possessed strength vastly beyond ordinary Demons. Although his screen time was brief, Chapter 658 directly demonstrates several significant abilities.

#### High-Level Demon Physiology
As one of the 72 Pillars of Hell, Malphas possessed the supernatural characteristics of a powerful Biblical Demon.
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Malphas possessed strength, speed, durability, stamina and reflexes far beyond Lesser Demons and ordinary supernatural beings.
- Demonic Physiology: His true body possessed horns, wings and a prehensile Demonic tail.[1]
- Natural Weaponry: His Demonic body provided horns, claws, wings and other natural combat features.
- Flight: Malphas possessed large functional Demonic wings.
- Supernatural Longevity: Malphas had existed long enough to regard [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] as an inexperienced youth and claimed to have fought battles before Victor was born.[1]
- Demonic Energy: Malphas possessed substantial quantities of Negative/Demonic Energy.
- Miasma Generation: Like other powerful Demons, he could produce large amounts of corrosive Demonic Miasma.
The exact limits of his regeneration and other standard Demonic traits were never individually demonstrated.

#### Miasma Manipulation
Malphas could freely gather and manipulate enormous quantities of Miasma.[1]
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] summoned his first lightning beast, Malphas immediately concentrated Miasma around himself until the Energy completely reshaped his body.
Known applications include:
- Miasma Generation
- Miasma Manipulation
- Energy Concentration
- Demonic Energy Reinforcement
- Miasma Body Creation

#### Miasma Giant Form
Malphas' most impressive demonstrated ability was creating an enormous body of concentrated Miasma around himself.
His normal body stood approximately 7 meters tall.
After gathering Miasma, Malphas expanded into a 20-meter-tall Demonic form.[1]
This transformation granted:
- Size Enhancement
- Physical Augmentation
- Miasma Armor / Body Reinforcement
- Enhanced Strength
The exact nature of the form is not fully explained. The chapter describes the giant Demon as being formed from pure Miasma, suggesting that Malphas either enlarged his existing body through Demonic Energy or created an external Miasma construct around himself.

#### Superhuman Strength
Malphas possessed considerable physical power.
While transformed into his twenty-meter Miasma form, he punched [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s first Kirin directly in the face and destroyed the summoned lightning beast, reducing it into dispersed electrical Energy.[1]
The feat demonstrates that Malphas was not merely politically ranked among the Pillars but possessed genuine combat strength appropriate to a High-Level Demon.

#### Enhanced Supernatural Perception
Malphas could sense [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] from an enormous distance before the invading army physically reached his palace.
When his subordinate reported Victor's arrival, Malphas stated that he already knew because Victor's presence could be felt despite how far away he remained.[1]
This grants:
- Energy Detection
- Presence Detection
- Enhanced Supernatural Senses
Victor was deliberately not hiding his presence at the time, so the feat should not be interpreted as proof that Malphas could detect perfectly concealed opponents.

#### Combat Experience
Malphas possessed considerable battle experience.
He directly claimed that he had been fighting since before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was born and considered the battles he had experienced sufficient reason not to fear an apparently younger opponent.[1]
His Generals similarly believed their forces had previously survived battles worse than facing Victor's enormous army.
His experience, however, could not compensate for the sheer difference in power.

#### Leadership
As the 39th Pillar, Malphas governed one of Hell's established territories.
His authority included:
- Territorial Rule: Malphas controlled an entire Pillar City.
- Military Command: He commanded multiple Demonic Legions.
- Generals: At least two Generals directly served beneath him.
- Large Population Authority: His city contained over 500,000 Demons.[1]
His authority proved dependent upon fear and the existing Pillar structure rather than absolute loyalty. Once [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] offered an alternative, thousands of inhabitants abandoned Malphas immediately and even his own Generals fled.

### Power
Malphas was powerful enough to hold the 39th position within the old Pillar hierarchy of Hell.
The ranking of the 72 Pillars depended upon a mixture of:
- personal strength;
- lineage;
- wealth;
- political influence;
- and military resources.
Malphas was therefore significantly above ordinary Demons and lower-ranked Pillars but remained far beneath the uppermost entities of Hell such as the Seven Deadly Sins, Four Horsemen, top-ranked Pillars and Demon Kings.
His direct confrontation with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] provides the clearest demonstration of his level.
Malphas successfully destroyed one of Victor's golden lightning beasts while using his twenty-meter Miasma form.[1]
However, Victor was not meaningfully threatened.
The moment Victor produced several additional beasts simultaneously, Malphas understood that he had no realistic means of stopping the attack.
His relative position can therefore be summarized as:

### Relationships

#### Diablo
[[Diablo]] was Malphas' ruler and leader of the faction to which the 39th Pillar belonged.
Malphas remained loyal to the political system created beneath Diablo and refused to recognize [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s conquest even after several surrounding Pillars had already been defeated or converted.
His decision to remain aligned with the old order ultimately resulted in his death.
No substantial personal interaction between Malphas and Diablo is shown.

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] was Malphas' killer and the individual responsible for destroying his territory.
Malphas initially underestimated Victor because of his age. He considered his own centuries or millennia of combat experience proof that the younger Vampire could not simply overpower him.[1]
Victor offered Malphas and every Demon within his city an opportunity to surrender.
Malphas refused.
Only after witnessing Victor gather an attack powerful enough to destroy the city did Malphas attempt to stop the confrontation, but Victor considered the decision already made.[1]
Malphas managed to destroy Victor's first summoned lightning beast and briefly boasted about his superiority.
Victor responded by summoning several more.
Malphas was subsequently overwhelmed and killed.
His defeat demonstrates one of Victor's consistent policies during the conquest of Hell: submission was rewarded, but deliberately rejecting an explicit opportunity to surrender removed any expectation of mercy.

#### Vepar Elderblood
[[Vepar Elderblood]] accompanied [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to Malphas' city after abandoning the old Pillar hierarchy.
Malphas' subordinates were shocked to see the former 42nd-ranked Pillar openly standing beside the invader.[1]
Her presence visually demonstrated how quickly Victor's faction was absorbing members of [[Diablo]]'s former political structure.
Vepar did not personally fight Malphas.

#### Unnamed Generals
Malphas possessed at least two unnamed Generals who initially claimed to be prepared for battle.[1]
Their loyalty vanished once [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] began preparing his attack.
Both attempted to flee, with one explicitly declaring that he did not intend to die alongside Malphas.
The betrayal visibly shocked Malphas, who had considered them his most trusted Generals.

### The 39th Pillar
Malphas held the 39th position within the 72 Pillars of Hell, also known as:
- The 72 Keys of Solomon;
- Solomon's 72 Keys;
- The Ars Goetia;
- The Lemegeton.
The previous article referred to Malphas as the 39th Duke of Hell. This is misleading.
Although the entire ruling group is often collectively called the 72 Dukes of Hell, individual members possess different titles. Malphas' specific Ars Goetia title is President Malphas, while his numerical position is the 39th Key/Pillar.[1]
The corrected terminology is therefore:
President Malphas — 39th Key of Solomon / 39th Pillar of Hell
Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s conquest, the hereditary Pillar hierarchy was abolished entirely.
