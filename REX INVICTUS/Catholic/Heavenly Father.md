The Heavenly Father, also known as Yahweh, Jehovah and the Celestial Father, is the God of the Bible, the ruler of the [[Biblical Pantheon]] and one of the oldest native Gods associated with Earth. He is an Original Primordial God and one of the greatest known native practitioners of the Divine Concept of Creation. He created the original Biblical Angels, directly produced the complete lives and Souls of Adam and Lilith, established the celestial civilization of Heaven and remained its God-King throughout the supernatural history surrounding Earth.[2][5]
Despite the word Primordial in his classification, the Heavenly Father is not one of the [[Primordial Entities]] or [[Regulators of Existence]]. A Primordial God is an exceptionally ancient and developed God, whereas Beings such as [[The Universal Tree]], [[True Death]], Infinity, [[The Judges of The Abyss]] and The Owner of Limbo occupy actual universal administrative positions and embody fundamental components required for the Cosmos to function. The Heavenly Father's Creation Divinity therefore exists within the universal system administered by the Primordials rather than placing him above that system.[7][8]
His defining specialty is not conventional warfare but Creation. The Heavenly Father has spent eons refining the act of giving form to matter, Life, supernatural systems and Divine creations with extraordinary efficiency. Even after Victor Elderblood developed Creation to an exceptional level, the System placed Victor's authority immediately beneath the Heavenly Father's established mastery of the Concept.[9] During the Imperial Era, his granddaughter [[Ariella Victoria Elderblood]] remained visibly impressed by his ability to create living animals while wasting practically no Energy.[6]
The Heavenly Father should also not be confused with the much higher universal powers later introduced under similar terminology. His Holy Light and ability to manipulate Positive Energy do not make him the embodiment of <Positivity>, and his occasional identification with "Light" is unrelated to the Upper-Sector Being known as <Light>. Positivity is one of the highest universal principles descending directly from [[Primordial Chaos]], while the Heavenly Father is a native Creator God operating beneath that cosmological framework.[8]

### Physical Appearance
The Heavenly Father is capable of assuming multiple forms and does not appear dependent upon ordinary mortal anatomy.
During his first major physical appearance, he manifested primarily as a humanoid silhouette composed of overwhelming white and golden Light.[2] This form emphasized his Divine and Holy nature rather than displaying identifiable biological characteristics.
When interacting personally with others inside Heaven, he prefers a much more mundane appearance. He commonly manifests as a middle-aged or elderly man with fair skin, gray or white hair and, during the later story, a white beard.[7][10]
The modest appearance is intentional and reflects his association with the Seventh Heaven of Humility. Despite possessing enough power to create spectacular Divine structures without difficulty, he deliberately surrounds himself with simplicity. During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s visit, the Seventh Heaven appeared as a vast peaceful landscape rather than an extravagant royal court.[7]
By the Imperial Era, he lived inside an extremely simple house situated upon an enormous plain of clouds. [[Ariel Elderblood]] noted that her father periodically changed the surrounding landscape, confirming that the apparent simplicity of the area represented preference rather than limitation.[6]

### Personality
The Heavenly Father is ancient, patient, observant and generally far more pragmatic than the absolute religious image constructed around him by Humanity. His enormous age has made him comfortable thinking over centuries and millennia rather than reacting immediately to every crisis.
One of his most important stated principles is free will. When Jeanne questioned why he allowed suffering and conflict instead of continuously intervening, he argued that free will had been granted precisely so living Beings could make their own decisions. An existence in which the Creator personally interrupted every undesirable choice would reduce individual agency to meaninglessness.[11]
This philosophy is not perfectly reflected by everything he created. The original Angels were designed as Divine instruments and possessed an extremely powerful instinctive obligation to obey their Creator. Directly rejecting the Heavenly Father's authority could destabilize their Angelic existence and contribute to becoming a Fallen Angel.[10][12] Ariel's later experience of unrestricted freedom demonstrated that his belief in free will and the obedience built into his early creations were not always perfectly compatible.
The Heavenly Father himself appears to recognize the importance of correcting mistakes. His Seventh Heaven is associated with Humility, and he explained to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] that even a Being possessing enormous Power needed humility in order to recognize personal flaws and avoid decisions that would later become regrets.[7]
He can be remarkably casual when interacting with Beings who do not treat him with religious reverence. Victor repeatedly gave him informal nicknames, ignored conventional Divine etiquette and spoke to him as one ruler to another. Although initially surprised, the Heavenly Father eventually came to enjoy the relationship rather than demanding ritual submission.[2][7]
Victor's genuine affection toward his family strongly affected his opinion. Previous Demon Kings had largely been violent, arrogant or megalomaniacal Beings whose rule made Hell a permanent threat to Heaven. Observing Victor openly loving and protecting his wives convinced the Heavenly Father that the new Demon King possessed attachments and responsibilities beyond personal ambition.[2]
He is also capable of changing his worldview when confronted by evidence. Thousands of years of Heaven-Hell warfare had caused him to associate Demonic and Dark-aligned races with danger, yet Victor's civilization forced him to acknowledge that species did not automatically determine morality. Hell under Victor remained dangerous, but it had cities, law, commerce, families and citizens whose lives could not honestly be reduced to indiscriminate evil.[13][14][15]
The Heavenly Father is considerably more cunning than his relaxed behavior suggests. He understands his own creations on a deep spiritual level and is comfortable preparing developments that may require millennia before becoming useful. The dormant Conceptual seed of Love placed inside Ariel's Soul remained inactive for roughly fifteen hundred years until her experiences with Victor allowed it to mature naturally.[16]
He is likewise not excessively attached to political pride. Once the difference between Victor and the remaining old Pantheons became overwhelming, the Heavenly Father concluded that preserving Heaven and retaining the ability to govern his people under a larger Imperial structure would be preferable to sacrificing them in a hopeless war merely to protect his title.[10]

### Nature and Classification

#### Primordial God
The Heavenly Father's classification as an Original Primordial God refers to his status among the oldest and most developed native Gods. It should not be confused with the completed [[Primordial Entities]] responsible for universal functions.
A Primordial God remains a Divine Being who possesses Divinities, accumulates Energy, develops understanding and can theoretically grow or decline. A completed Primordial Entity instead occupies an administrative office within the architecture of the Universe itself.
The distinction is particularly important for the Heavenly Father because his greatest Divinity, Creation, remains subject to rules imposed by the deeper cosmic system. His ability to create Souls and completely new living existences eventually came under the supervision of [[The Universal Tree]], [[The Judges of The Abyss]] and the System.[5]

#### God of Creation
Creation is the Heavenly Father's defining Divine Concept.
Creation allows him to give form to matter, Energy, living organisms and supernatural systems. More importantly, thousands of years of continuous experience have given him extraordinary understanding of how efficiently a creation should be designed rather than merely how much Energy can be forced into it.[7][9][6]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s development provides the clearest benchmark. When Victor used Creation as the primary foundation of the World of Water, his authority over the Concept increased dramatically and stopped immediately beneath the Heavenly Father's established position.[9]
This should not be interpreted as meaning the Heavenly Father permanently exceeds Victor in every possible creation feat. Victor later acquired powers and forms of existence the Heavenly Father does not possess. The comparison specifically demonstrates the Heavenly Father's enormous mastery and authority over Creation as a Divine Concept at that point in the story.

#### Creation and Primordial Regulation
Creation is not an unlimited loophole allowing Creator Gods to manufacture infinite Souls or civilizations without consequence.
The higher cosmic authorities regulate Divine creation when it begins interacting with fundamental systems such as Life, Soul and reincarnation. Creator Gods operate using Creation authority permitted within the structure of Reality, and attempts to generate entirely new Souls or abnormal living systems can require approval from the Primordials responsible for those functions.[5][8]
The Heavenly Father is the clearest historical example of this limitation. His earliest creations included complete Souls manufactured through his own Creation authority, but after the problems surrounding the original Angelic generations and the increasing population of Creation, his freedom to repeat such acts was severely restricted.[5]
The novel consequently distinguishes ordinary Divine creation from genuine first-generation Soul creation. Producing a body, animal or artificial structure is vastly easier than introducing a completely new Soul into the universal cycle.

The Heavenly Father's greatest ancient Creation feat was the manufacture of complete living existences possessing new Souls.
Adam and Lilith were the last two individuals explicitly identified as Beings he created in this complete manner.[5]

#### Angelic Creation and Reproduction
The Biblical Angels are creations of the Heavenly Father, but the manner of their creation changed over time.
The earliest Angelic generations were far closer to complete Divine lives. After the disasters surrounding the original Angels, the Heavenly Father stopped freely creating complete Souls and later Angels were instead made primarily from the positive portions of existing Souls.[5]
This produced several long-term consequences. Later Angels remained extraordinarily powerful Holy Beings, but their spiritual foundations were more restricted than exceptional first-generation creations such as [[Lucifer]].
Natural reproduction eventually became increasingly important to Heaven. During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s enormous temporal-development project, the Heavenly Father personally participated so that [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood's Fertility Blessing could be applied to the Angels, allowing Heaven's population to grow naturally at an accelerated rate rather than depending entirely upon direct Divine manufacture.[17]

#### Holy Light and Positive Energy
The Heavenly Father possesses overwhelming Holy and Light-based powers, but these should be separated from the later cosmological concept of <Light>.
His Holy Light belongs to the Biblical Divine system and is particularly destructive toward Demonic Energy, Miasma and Beings vulnerable to sacred properties.[11][18]
He can additionally manipulate Positive Energy. During the final phase of the Demonic War, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] specifically instructed him to stop using ordinary Holy Light and instead use Positive Energy. The Heavenly Father generated white-golden Positive Energy while Victor supplied its Negative counterpart, allowing both powers to operate together through Balance.[2]
Possessing and manipulating Positive Energy does not make the Heavenly Father the Primordial embodiment of <Positivity>. The auxiliary cosmology places Positivity vastly higher in the conceptual structure, with Creation existing several levels beneath it.[8]
Likewise, the later Upper-Sector ruler <Light> is an entirely separate Being.

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
The Heavenly Father's first substantial direct participation occurred when [[Jeanne Elderblood]], despite having become a Vampire, called upon the Divine power she once possessed as a Saint. The Heavenly Father answered her prayer and allowed her to temporarily use his Blessing, but warned that her transformed Vampire body was fundamentally incompatible with [[Holy Energy]] and that overuse could destroy her existence.[11]
With that permission Jeanne invoked Judgment of Israel. Michael's sword manifested through the technique while enormous quantities of golden Holy flame spread through [[Nightingale]], destroying Demonic magical structures, cleansing Miasma and weakening the invading forces.[11]
Their conversation also revealed the Heavenly Father's philosophy concerning free will. Jeanne questioned why a Being possessing his power allowed so many tragedies to occur. He answered that some events should not simply be interrupted by Divine force because free will existed precisely so people could make genuine choices.[11]
The Heavenly Father continued acting through the larger Biblical system as the war expanded. After Alexander died during the conflict surrounding the Inquisition, his Soul was ultimately sent into [[The Limbo Realm]].[19]
When [[Diablo]]'s offensive reached its decisive stage, the Heavenly Father ordered the Angels to sound the trumpets announcing Divine Judgment.[20] He subsequently entered the conflict personally and released an enormous Heavenly Judgment upon the Demonic forces. Six of the Seven Deadly Sins involved in the confrontation were destroyed, alongside large numbers of other high-ranking Demons and Pillars.[18]
The Heavenly Father later encountered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] after the latter had conquered [[Biblical Hell]] and become the new Demon King. Their first meaningful cooperation immediately demonstrated how different Victor was from previous rulers of Hell. Rather than beginning another ideological conflict, Victor asked the Heavenly Father to prevent the battle between [[Lilith Elderblood]] and Diablo from devastating the surrounding world.[2]
The Heavenly Father created the required barrier. When Victor concluded that Holy power alone was not the best method, the two deliberately combined opposing forces: the Heavenly Father supplied Positive Energy while Victor supplied Negative Energy. Their cooperation reinforced the principle that opposing sides of Creation could produce greater stability when used in Balance rather than attempting to annihilate one another.[2]
Observing Victor's obvious love for his wives dramatically improved the Heavenly Father's opinion of him. A Demon King who possessed genuine emotional attachments and understood responsibility was far preferable to the destructive rulers who had historically occupied Hell's throne.[2]
The Heavenly Father also demonstrated an important limitation during the war's aftermath. Raw Energy alone did not make someone the greatest fighter. Victor's centuries of constant combat development allowed him to surpass opponents whose total reserves could exceed his in individual areas, while the Heavenly Father himself was recognized more as an ancient Creator and administrator than a martial specialist.[21]

#### King Among Kings
By the later [[Supernatural Gathering]], the relationship between Heaven and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction had developed into a genuine strategic alliance.
Victor entered the Gathering commanding a coalition containing Vampires, Demons, Dragons, Gods, Witches, [[Werewolves]], Youkai and other supernatural races. Once negotiations with the factions surrounding [[New Dawn]] and the [[Egyptian Pantheon]] failed, Victor declared war.[22]
The Heavenly Father openly supported him.
The Angels will support the Demon King.
—Heavenly Father, Chapter 871
Considering the ancient hostility between Angels and Demons, the declaration represented an extraordinary reversal. Heaven was no longer treating the ruler of Hell as its automatic enemy; the Heavenly Father had decided that Victor's coalition represented a more important source of stability than preserving ancient racial hostilities.[23]
When the resulting hunt began, Heaven supplied major combatants including [[Azrael]] and Ariel, with Ariel occupying the military position previously associated with Michael.[24]
The cooperation established the foundation for the much deeper political relationship between Heaven and Dragon's Nest that developed after Victor became the Dragon God of [[Chaos]].

#### Dragon God of Chaos
After becoming the Dragon God of [[Chaos]], [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] traveled personally into the highest regions of [[Biblical Heaven]] with Ophis, [[Nero]], [[Metis]] and Ariel.[25]
The group passed through Eden and other sacred territories before Victor reached the Seventh Heaven of Humility, where the Heavenly Father personally received him.[26][7]
The meeting quickly moved beyond diplomacy. The Heavenly Father asked Victor one of the most important questions of his Divine development:
What was he?
Victor possessed an enormous collection of valid identities—Human, Vampire, Progenitor, Demon King, Dragon, God and countless others—but the Heavenly Father warned that relying upon external labels could transform those identities into self-imposed limitations. A God's development required understanding what actually existed beneath the titles.[7]
The discussion helped Victor recognize that his Blood Dragon body represented his primary external form while the incomprehensible Eldritch existence inside him remained another equally genuine part of himself. The Heavenly Father emphasized that self-understanding was not something completed upon becoming a God; if anything, Godhood made the process more important.[7]
Victor then revealed the fragment of Creation he had obtained through [[Amaterasu]] Elderblood. The Heavenly Father refused to simply teach Victor to imitate his methods. Creation was intrinsically connected to the Creator's own understanding, so Victor needed to discover the version of Creation appropriate to Victor rather than becoming an inferior copy of Yahweh.[7]
Their political conversation addressed cooperation between the Biblical religion, the [[Blood God Religion]] and Dragon's Nest. The Heavenly Father wanted a deeper alliance and raised the traditional possibility of securing it through marriage to one of his Angelic daughters. Victor did not reject further cooperation but refused to enter a marriage purely because political convention expected it.[7]
Victor then invited the Heavenly Father into Hell. The realm the Heavenly Father remembered had changed almost beyond recognition. Alcantara and Abaddon had become organized cities with currency, commerce, entertainment, government and hidden security forces.[13][27]
When the Heavenly Father initially questioned the apparent absence of security, Victor invited him to search properly. A release of Positive Energy exposed the enormous number of Shadow Demons already watching the visitors from concealment. The Heavenly Father was impressed enough by Victor's government to half-seriously ask whether the Dragon intended to rule Heaven as well.[27]
The visit also forced the Heavenly Father to confront the accumulated hatred between Angels and Demons. Neither ruler believed thousands of years of warfare could be erased through a single treaty. The practical solution was gradual cooperation, changing generations and interests strong enough to make coexistence preferable to repeating the same ancient war.[14][15]
The greatest cosmological revelation of the visit involved War. Victor reconstructed War into a Progenitor combining Demonic, Divine, Negative, Positive and Holy characteristics that traditional supernatural theory considered contradictory.[5][28]
The Heavenly Father initially described the resulting Being as an abomination. Victor immediately rejected the idea that the old categories of Creation possessed moral authority over whether his son deserved to exist.[29]
Their disagreement illustrated a major difference between the two rulers. The Heavenly Father had spent eons learning how to work perfectly within Creation's laws; Victor had increasingly dedicated himself to proving that those laws could be bypassed, rewritten or transcended.
Despite the dispute, neither abandoned their alliance. Victor emphasized that Heaven would be safer cooperating with Dragon's Nest when the Sector eventually encountered stronger external civilizations.[30]
The discussion surrounding War also exposed the Heavenly Father's ancient Creation history. Adam and Lilith had been among the last complete living Souls he personally manufactured, while later Angels were created using only the positive portions of pre-existing Souls because entirely new Soul creation had become restricted by the universal administrators.[5]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
By the time [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became God Emperor, the Heavenly Father had accepted that the old political order surrounding Earth could not survive unchanged.
In a conversation with Ariel, he evaluated Heaven's geopolitical position as relatively secure but understood that Victor's power and influence were continuing to increase. Rather than imagining Heaven could preserve absolute independence indefinitely through pride alone, he concluded that if Victor eventually demanded submission, negotiating peaceful incorporation while retaining local authority would be preferable to condemning his children to an unwinnable war.[10]
The same period exposed the structure of the Seven Heavenly Virtues more clearly. The highest Virtues did not merely possess stronger [[Holy Energy]]; the Heavenly Father had granted them genuine Divine Concepts corresponding to the ideals they represented.[10]
His decision to strengthen the alliance through Ariel became much more controversial. Ariel was sent toward Victor as a potential political bride despite never personally requesting the arrangement.[31][12]
Victor's wives temporarily suppressed the Heavenly Father's influence over Ariel, allowing her to experience completely unrestricted free will. The result revealed thousands of years of accumulated frustration. Ariel angrily cursed her Creator for effectively "selling" her into a political marriage and finally expressed complaints that her Angelic nature had previously made extremely difficult to voice openly.[12]
Her reaction did not mean her loyalty to Heaven had been artificial. Even while furious with the Heavenly Father, Ariel refused to trade important Heavenly resources for her own freedom. Her loyalty, duty and affection remained genuinely hers once the compulsive component of her Angelic nature was removed.[12]
Victor ultimately refused to treat the political arrangement itself as proof of love. Ariel entered his family only after the relationship developed voluntarily, transforming what began as the Heavenly Father's diplomatic maneuver into a genuine personal bond.[32][33]
The Heavenly Father had nevertheless prepared one part of Ariel's future long beforehand. He planted a dormant seed connected to the Concept of Love inside her Soul. The seed did not force Ariel to love anyone; it remained dormant until roughly fifteen hundred years of personal experience allowed her to genuinely understand the Concept for herself.[16]
His relationship with Victor became familial once Ariel fully joined the Elderblood family. The Heavenly Father became Victor's father-in-law and later the maternal grandfather of [[Ariella Victoria Elderblood]].[16][6]
Victor's development of Creation also attracted the Heavenly Father's attention. When Victor created the World of Water, his Creation Authority rose until it stopped immediately beneath the Heavenly Father's established authority. Rather than interpreting the younger God's growth solely as a threat, the Heavenly Father reacted with amazement at the speed with which his son-in-law had developed a Concept that normally required immense periods of understanding.[9]
The population problem surrounding Heaven also changed. Direct Divine creation of additional complete Angels was no longer a sustainable solution, so the Heavenly Father participated in Victor's enormous temporal project specifically to allow [[Aphrodite]]'s Fertility Blessing to affect Angelic reproduction.[17]
This represented a fundamental change in Heaven's future. Instead of relying upon Yahweh continuously manufacturing his people, later Angelic civilization could grow increasingly through ordinary reproduction and inherited Divine characteristics.

#### Those Who Rule Reality
As the [[Elderblood Empire]] prepared for war against civilizations from the Upper Sectors, the Heavenly Father became one of the allied rulers incorporated into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s larger strategic preparations.
Victor temporarily assumed supreme authority over the coalition's military preparations because the coming conflict operated on a scale none of Earth's old Pantheons could individually confront.[34]
Heaven was not simply ordered to wait for the Empire to protect it. Victor deliberately supplied knowledge and resources intended to strengthen allied powers enough that they could contribute to their own survival.
Ariel eventually returned to Heaven accompanied by her daughter [[Ariella Victoria Elderblood]].[6]
Ariella had inherited Ariel's facial characteristics, Victor's black hair and violet eyes and an extraordinary combination of their powers. She possessed all Seven Heavenly Virtues simultaneously as Divine Concepts while her True Dragon nature prevented those powers from automatically forcing her personality to conform perfectly to their behavioral ideals.[6]
The Heavenly Father examined his granddaughter and found remarkably little wickedness in her, regarding her curiosity as comparable to that of a newly created Angel. Ariella immediately destroyed the solemnity of the reunion by innocently asking why her grandfather was so weak compared with Victor.[6]
Rather than becoming offended, the Heavenly Father acknowledged that comparing most Beings to Victor had become unreasonable and explained that his own greatest strength had never been brute-force battle.
He demonstrated Creation.
With extraordinary precision, he created a living swallow while wasting essentially no Energy. Ariella immediately recognized the quality of the feat and admitted that she could not reproduce the same degree of control. The Heavenly Father continued creating additional animals until his modest home began resembling a miniature Garden of Eden.[6]
The demonstration provided one of the clearest distinctions between scale and mastery. Victor's later creations could be vastly larger and more exotic, yet the Heavenly Father remained an exceptionally refined technician whose eons of experience made even simple Life creation almost perfectly efficient.
Ariella then discovered the Celestial System responsible for administering the Heavens. The Heavenly Father disconnected the system from the active dimension so she could experiment safely.[6]
What began as curiosity turned into a near-total rewrite. Ariella considered large portions of the old system outdated and reconstructed its internal code so extensively that describing the process as a simple modification became inaccurate. To the Heavenly Father's surprise, the new architecture was genuinely better than his old design.[6]
The incident forced him to confront another weakness: stagnation. His intelligence had not disappeared, but Heaven's long periods of stability had given him little reason to continuously redesign systems that already functioned. The Elderblood Empire, in contrast, treated constant improvement as a cultural requirement.[6]
Impressed by Ariella's purity, intelligence, Seven Virtues, Creation affinity and talent for Heavenly infrastructure, the Heavenly Father considered her capable of eventually becoming his successor. Ariel immediately rejected the idea of predetermining the girl's future, explaining that Ariella would choose her own path when she matured. The Heavenly Father approved of that answer specifically because of his belief in free will.[6]
Ariel's true purpose for the visit was military preparation. She provided accumulated Elderblood research concerning Angel physiology and methods of helping Angels become increasingly Divine, then explained the strength of the enemies waiting beyond their Sector.[6]
The report completely recalibrated the Heavenly Father's understanding of future warfare. Enemies classified merely as CODE: V-03 could already possess continental-scale power while existing in numbers large enough to function as cannon fodder. More alarming were weapons such as the [[Solar System Destroyer-Class Spaceship]], capable of destroying an entire stellar system by attacking its star from interstellar distances.[6]
The Heavenly Father recognized that being directly struck by such a weapon could severely injure even him. He was equally disturbed by the strategic mentality required to accept the destruction of inhabited solar systems or galaxies as ordinary collateral damage.[6]
Ariel instructed him to account for those standards when creating, training and improving future generations of Angels. The Heavenly Father accepted the warning rather than dismissing it as fearmongering.[6]
The later military briefing delivered to [[Shiva]] confirmed that Victor had treated the Heavenly Father similarly to his other important allies: each ruler was given whatever information, knowledge or resources would most efficiently improve their faction before the barrier separating the cosmic regions disappeared.[35]

### Skills & Abilities
The Heavenly Father is an Original Primordial God, God-King and one of the most accomplished Creator Gods in the known native Sector. His greatest advantages are his immense Divine reserves, conceptual understanding, Creation mastery and authority over Heaven rather than martial specialization.
- Primordial God Physiology: The Heavenly Father is one of the oldest and most developed native Gods associated with Earth.[2]
- [[Immortality]]: He has existed since the earliest eras of Creation and does not age according to normal mortal biology.[2]
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics: His Divine body possesses strength, speed, durability, stamina and senses enormously beyond ordinary supernatural species.
- [[Divine Energy]] Manipulation: He possesses enormous reserves of Divine Energy appropriate for a God-King and can apply them through his Divinities, Holy powers and large-scale supernatural techniques.[21]
- Regeneration: As a high-ranking God, he can recover from conventional bodily damage unless the attack sufficiently affects his Divine existence.
- Divine Soul: His Soul is the spiritual foundation of an ancient God rather than a mortal organism.
- Conceptual Manipulation: He can directly exercise Divine Concepts rather than relying entirely upon ordinary magic or physical forces.
- Creation Divinity: Creation is the Heavenly Father's defining and most developed Divine Concept.[7][9]
- Creation Manipulation: He can produce and shape phenomena through the Divine authority of Creation.
- Matter Creation: He can create physical matter directly through Creation Energy.
- Object Creation: Furniture and other physical structures can be manifested casually through thought.[7]
- Life Creation: His Creation can produce genuine living organisms.[5][6]
- Animal Creation: He effortlessly produced a living swallow and numerous additional animals while demonstrating Creation to Ariella.[6]
- Precision Creation: Ariella observed that practically none of his Energy was wasted during the process, demonstrating exceptional efficiency accumulated across eons of practice.[6]
- Complex Creation: His authority extends beyond simple matter into supernatural organisms and Divine systems.
- Creation Energy Manipulation: He can directly use the Energy associated with the Creation Divinity.
- Creation Sensing: He detected [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s use of Creation from the Seventh Heaven when Victor constructed the World of Water.[9]

### Combat Ability
The Heavenly Father possesses enormous raw power, but the novel repeatedly distinguishes his strength from that of a dedicated combat specialist.
He is fundamentally a Creator, administrator and craftsman. His Energy reserves and Divine authorities are sufficient to erase major Demonic armies, create barriers around God-King-level battles and remain one of the strongest powers of the old Earth-based supernatural order, but his martial experience is not comparable to Beings who spent their lives continuously pursuing combat.[18][2][21]
This distinction became increasingly important as characters such as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and [[Scathach]] demonstrated that raw Energy, Divine age and rank could not replace technique, mentality, experience and efficient use of available power.[21]
The Heavenly Father is therefore dangerous because of the quality and versatility of his Divine authorities rather than because he is portrayed as the best close-quarters fighter among Gods.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] began as everything the Heavenly Father should traditionally have opposed: a Vampire Progenitor, King of Demons and Being carrying enormous quantities of Negative Energy.
Their relationship instead became one of the strangest alliances in the supernatural world.
The Heavenly Father's first substantial personal evaluation of Victor occurred after Victor became King of Hell. Observing Victor's love for his family convinced him that the new Demon King was fundamentally different from the previous rulers of the realm.[2]
Their relationship became increasingly informal. Victor called him names such as "Good Guy", "Angel King" and other irreverent nicknames while the Heavenly Father stopped expecting formal Divine reverence from him.
The two also proved unusually complementary. The Heavenly Father was a master of Creation and the established Rules of the Cosmos, while Victor increasingly specialized in combining contradictory systems and breaking assumptions the older Creator had accepted for eons.
The Heavenly Father became one of the most important teachers in Victor's Divine development when he helped Victor understand that genuine Godhood required self-understanding rather than accumulation of external titles.[7]
Their disagreements could still become severe, particularly when the Heavenly Father initially treated War's contradictory existence as an abomination.[29] The alliance survived because neither ruler required complete ideological agreement.
The relationship ultimately became familial when Ariel married Victor. By the Imperial Era, the Heavenly Father openly regarded Victor as his son-in-law and Ariella as his granddaughter.[6]

#### Ariel Elderblood
[[Ariel Elderblood]] is one of the Heavenly Father's oldest and greatest Angelic creations.
For eons, Ariel served Heaven as one of the Seven Virtues and eventually became one of its principal military commanders. Her loyalty was genuine, but the obedience built into Angelic nature made it difficult for her to express the full extent of her frustration toward her Creator.[10][12]
The Heavenly Father's attempt to use Ariel in a marriage alliance with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became a major breaking point. Once his influence was suppressed and Ariel experienced unrestricted free will, she immediately unleashed thousands of years of complaints and anger.[12]
Importantly, this did not make her abandon Heaven. Ariel's continued loyalty after obtaining freedom demonstrated that much of what she felt toward her father and people was authentic rather than merely programmed obedience.
Their later relationship became healthier precisely because Ariel could criticize him. She eventually visited him voluntarily with Ariella and spoke to him as a daughter rather than as a perfectly obedient Divine instrument.[6]
The Heavenly Father also planted a dormant seed of Love within Ariel's Soul long before she developed that Concept. It only awakened after Ariel herself acquired the understanding necessary to support it, demonstrating one of the more constructive sides of his long-term planning.[16]

#### Ariella Victoria Elderblood
[[Ariella Victoria Elderblood]] is the Heavenly Father's granddaughter through Ariel and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
He immediately recognized her extraordinary purity and potential. Ariella possessed all Seven Heavenly Virtues simultaneously, traces of Creation and the physiology of a True Dragon.[6]
Their first meeting rapidly changed from solemn family reunion into an impromptu lesson in Creation. Ariella genuinely admired the Heavenly Father's precision after watching him create Life without wasting Energy.[6]
She later embarrassed him by rewriting large portions of Heaven's Celestial System and producing improvements he acknowledged were superior to the stagnant architecture he had maintained.[6]
The Heavenly Father considered Ariella capable of becoming his eventual successor. Ariel immediately emphasized that her daughter would choose her own future. In a fitting conclusion to the conversation, the Heavenly Father approved specifically because he respected the principle of free will.[6]

#### Jeanne Elderblood
[[Jeanne Elderblood]] once served as one of the Heavenly Father's Saints and retained enough of his Blessing to invoke him even after becoming a Vampire.[11]
Their relationship was not one of unquestioning devotion. Jeanne openly questioned his decisions and criticized the suffering he allowed to continue, while the Heavenly Father defended his philosophy of free will.[11]
He nevertheless allowed Jeanne to invoke Judgment of Israel during the Demonic War despite her transformed state, warning her carefully about the danger [[Holy Energy]] posed to her Vampire body.[11]
Jeanne eventually moved far beyond her former identity as his Saint and became part of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family, but the history remains one of the clearest examples of the Heavenly Father's relationship with chosen mortal champions.

#### The Angels
The Angels are simultaneously the Heavenly Father's creations, children, soldiers and civilization.
His relationship with them is consequently complicated. He clearly cares about Heaven's survival and repeatedly chooses policies intended to preserve his people, yet the original Angelic design placed immense emphasis upon obedience and duty.
The first generations possessed more complete Souls, while later Angels were constructed from positive portions of existing Souls after unrestricted Soul creation became prohibited.[5]
By the later story, the Angels were developing greater individuality and Heaven itself was slowly moving away from a civilization of perfectly obedient Divine instruments toward a society containing increasingly independent people.[10]

#### Lucifer Morningstar
[[Lucifer]] Morningstar was one of the Heavenly Father's greatest original Angels and one of the clearest examples of both the potential and danger present in the earliest Angelic generations.
The consequences of Lucifer's rebellion permanently affected how the Heavenly Father approached later creations. After the disaster involving the original Angels, he stopped casually creating new Angels with complete Souls.[5]
Lucifer's history therefore influenced not merely Heaven's politics but the actual spiritual architecture of future Angelic generations.

#### Adam and Lilith
Adam and [[Lilith Elderblood]] occupy a unique position among the Heavenly Father's creations because both were complete living Beings whose Souls he directly manufactured.[5]
Neither remained what their Creator originally intended.
Adam became associated with the foundation of Humanity, while Lilith ultimately became the Progenitor of the Demonic Race and one of the most important members of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family.
Their development demonstrates one of the central themes surrounding the Heavenly Father's philosophy: once genuine Life exists, Creation does not guarantee permanent control over what that Life chooses to become.

#### Lilith Elderblood and the Demonic Race
The Heavenly Father and [[Lilith Elderblood]] possess an ancient, complicated history originating from his own act of creating her.
By the later story, the Heavenly Father acknowledged that the Demons of their local cosmology ultimately existed because of developments originating from his own creations.[37]
This makes the historical Heaven-Hell war deeply ironic. A large portion of the "opposing" Demonic civilization developed from consequences originating within the Creator God's own celestial order.
His later willingness to cooperate with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] therefore represented more than ordinary diplomacy; it marked gradual acceptance that the absolute separation between Heaven's creations and Hell's inhabitants had never been as clean as religious doctrine suggested.

### Trivia
- The Heavenly Father's classification as a Primordial God should not be confused with a Primordial Entity. He remains a God operating inside the universal system rather than one of the Regulators who embody that system.
- Older descriptions calling him the God of Positivity are misleading when interpreted literally. He can manipulate Positive Energy, but the universal Concept <Positivity> exists vastly above ordinary Divine Creation and is not confirmed to belong to him.[2][8]
- Likewise, his Holy/Biblical Light should not be confused with the Upper-Sector ruler <Light> introduced much later.
- The Heavenly Father is primarily a Creator and craftsman rather than a martial specialist. His enormous reserves do not automatically translate into equivalent close-quarters combat skill.[21]
- Chapter 1073 establishes the Heavenly Father as the highest known native benchmark for the Creation Divinity when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s rapidly increasing authority stops directly beneath his.[9]
- By Chapter 1149, his greatest visible advantage in Creation is refinement. Ariella is particularly impressed that he creates genuine Life without wasting measurable Energy.[6]
- Adam and Lilith are the only explicitly identified individuals he created as completely new Life with newly created Souls before the process became heavily restricted.[5]
- Eve was created differently and should not be grouped with Adam and Lilith as another completely new Soul.[5]
- Adam and Lilith later becoming Progenitors does not prove the Heavenly Father possesses unrestricted Progenitor Creation. Their Progenitor status emerged through the greater cosmological system rather than being a normal product of his Creation Divinity.[5]
- The original Angelic rebellion permanently affected how he created later generations. Afterward, most Angels were made using positive portions of already existing Souls rather than complete new Souls.[5]
- His stated support for free will is deliberately complicated by the fact that conventional Angels were originally designed with extremely powerful obedience toward him.[11][12]
- Ariel's unrestricted free will caused her to immediately spend a significant amount of time insulting and complaining about her Creator, yet she remained genuinely loyal to Heaven afterward.[12]
- The Heavenly Father planted the seed of Love inside Ariel but did not directly force the finished Divinity to manifest. Ariel required approximately fifteen hundred years of genuine experience and understanding before the seed awakened.[16]
- His relationship with Victor is one of the stranger political reversals in the novel: one of Heaven's oldest rulers eventually became a military ally and father-in-law of the Vampire Progenitor who conquered Hell.
- After touring Victor's transformed Hell, the Heavenly Father seriously asked whether Victor intended to govern Heaven as well.[27]
- When Heaven eventually faced the possibility of Victor's political dominance, the Heavenly Father preferred peaceful submission with retained local governance over sacrificing his people for pride.[10]
- During Victor's visit, the Heavenly Father identified the Seventh Heaven as the Heaven of Humility and described Humility as a guide that prevents Power from blinding its wielder.[7]
- The Seventh Heaven serves as his most private residence, whereas the wider Biblical Heavens contain Heaven's broader governmental and population infrastructure.
- His Imperial-Era residence is an extremely simple house on a vast cloud plain despite his ability to create enormously elaborate surroundings at will.[6]
- Ariella's immediate question about why he was weaker than Victor did not offend him; he recognized that the remark came from childish innocence rather than disrespect.[6]
- Ariella later rewrote so much of the Celestial System that the Heavenly Father realized she was effectively rebuilding its source code rather than merely modifying it.[6]
- He considered Ariella a potential successor, but accepted Ariel's insistence that her daughter would choose her own path.[6]
- The report concerning Upper-Sector warfare forced him to reconsider the scale of future enemies. He accepted that a Solar-System-destroying weapon could severely injure him if it struck directly.[6]
- The Heavenly Father finds the casual destruction of potentially inhabited solar systems and galaxies abhorrent despite understanding why vastly larger civilizations may consider such losses strategically acceptable.[6]
- According to [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]], he was responsible for the ancient Great Flood that nearly reset Human civilization.[39]
- The traditional Christian claim that Jesus was literally the Heavenly Father's Divine incarnation or uniquely created biological son does not perfectly correspond to the novel's supernatural history. [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], who personally knew Jesus, described him as a Human possessing ordinary bodily needs while acknowledging his exceptional goodness and potential.[40]
- The Heavenly Father eventually admitted that the Demonic civilization of their cosmology arose indirectly from consequences produced by his own creations.[37]
- His cooperation with Victor during Chapter 686 provides one of the earliest direct demonstrations that Positive and Negative Energy are opposing but complementary cosmic polarities rather than simple synonyms for moral good and evil.[2]
