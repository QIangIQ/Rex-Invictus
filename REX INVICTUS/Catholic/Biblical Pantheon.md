The Biblical Pantheon, also known as the Biblical Faction, is one of the ancient major Pantheons originating from Earth, later renamed [[Yggdrasil]]. Historically, it encompassed two major opposing supernatural civilizations: [[Biblical Heaven]], governed by the Heavenly Father and inhabited primarily by Angels, and [[Biblical Hell]], inhabited primarily by Demons and historically governed by a succession of infernal Kings and Rulers. The Pantheon also possesses one of Humanity's largest religious systems and historically maintained extensive influence through Churches, Saints, priests, exorcists and other organizations.
Unlike many Pantheons whose upper hierarchy consists of numerous conventional Gods, the Biblical Pantheon centers around a single dominant God-King, the Heavenly Father, with powerful Angels and original Seraphim performing many roles ordinarily occupied by Major Gods.[1] The historical division between Heaven and Hell corresponded broadly to Positive- and Negative-aligned supernatural forces, but Chapter 1045 explicitly rejects interpreting this as an absolute division between moral Good and Evil. Angels and Demons are opposing species and political civilizations whose moral behavior depends upon the individuals involved.[2]
Following the rise of Victor Elderblood, the Pantheon's structure changed permanently. Victor conquered Biblical Hell, was recognized as its True King and later transferred the entire infernal realm into his Soul, where it was merged with other Underworlds and incorporated into the Dragons Nest Pantheon.[3][4] The modern Biblical Pantheon therefore consists primarily of Biblical Heaven, the Heavenly Father, the Angels and the associated religious structure. By the beginning of the [[Imperial Calendar]], it had also been incorporated into the greater [[Elderblood Empire]] while retaining internal government under the Heavenly Father.[5]

Main article: Biblical HeavenBiblical Heaven is the celestial half of the historical Biblical supernatural system and remains the principal territory of the modern Pantheon. It contains the Seven Heavens, serves as the homeland of the Angels and remains governed internally by the [[Heavenly Father]]. Detailed information concerning the Seven Heavens, their geography, Paradise, Soul allocation and the Garden of Eden belongs on their dedicated pages rather than here.

Main article: [[Biblical Hell]]

Unlike Heaven, whose supreme ruler remained the [[Heavenly Father]], Hell repeatedly changed leadership.

#### Human Religion
The Pantheon possesses extensive influence among Humanity through the religions devoted to the [[Heavenly Father]]. Human institutions include Churches, Saints, priests, supernatural Orders and the Inquisition, although the Heavenly Father does not directly control every action performed by organizations claiming to represent him. His emphasis upon Free [[Will Solace|Will]] historically allowed Human religious institutions to develop independent politics, corruption and extremist elements.

### History

#### Foundation and the Angels
The [[Heavenly Father]] established the celestial civilization of the Biblical Pantheon and created its original Angels. Several of his earliest creations possessed complete Souls and substantially greater potential than later Angelic generations.[1] After problems surrounding these early creations, the Heavenly Father ceased producing ordinary Angels in exactly the same manner, with most later Angels instead being formed using purified or positive portions of Souls. This left the majority of modern Angels with incomplete Souls and made natural progression toward Godhood considerably more difficult.
The original generation included exceptionally powerful Seraphim such as [[Lucifer]] Morningstar, Michael, [[Gabriel]] and [[Azrael]]. Several possessed Concepts and abilities comparable to the Divinities of important Gods, allowing the Biblical Pantheon to maintain a high-level supernatural hierarchy despite its comparatively small population of conventional Gods.

#### Lucifer and Biblical Hell
[[Lucifer]] eventually rebelled against Heaven and became the most famous Fallen Angel in Biblical history.[6] He later became the first legitimate King and Ruler of [[Biblical Hell]], establishing the political duality that defined the Pantheon for millennia: the [[Heavenly Father]] ruled the celestial side while an independent infernal King governed Hell.
Lucifer eventually abandoned the position and divided Hell's Key between [[Diablo]] and [[Lilith Elderblood]].[3] Diablo later defeated and enslaved Lilith, becoming the dominant Demon King, but deliberately refused the actual Ruler position because accepting its Soul-management responsibilities would restrict his ability to operate outside Hell. Lilith was consequently forced to perform the temporary administrative functions necessary to keep the infernal Soul system operating.

#### Demonic War and Victor Elderblood
[[Diablo]]'s ambitions eventually produced the Demonic War, drawing Heaven, Hell and most of Earth's major supernatural civilizations into direct conflict. Michael and [[Gabriel]] were killed during the war, causing [[Azrael]] to retaliate violently against the Demonic armies, while the [[Heavenly Father]] eventually intervened with a massive Judgment that devastated much of Diablo's remaining force.[7][8]
The decisive political change came through [[Victor Elderblood]], who entered Hell, gained the allegiance of influential Demons and was recognized by the Throne as its True King.[3] Victor reorganized the infernal government, military, economy and Soul administration before eventually killing and consuming Diablo.[9] For a period afterward the historical Biblical system therefore possessed two independent rulers: the Heavenly Father in Heaven and Victor in Hell.

#### Alliance with Dragons Nest
Unlike previous Demon Kings, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had no interest in preserving an eternal Angel–Demon war. His relationship with the [[Heavenly Father]] gradually developed from cautious cooperation into a formal alliance. By Chapter 871 the Heavenly Father publicly declared support for the Demon King, and during Victor's visit to Heaven he proposed both an alliance between the Biblical Religion and Victor's [[Blood God Religion]] and a symbolic marriage alliance between their factions.[10][11]
The marriage component later developed through [[Ariel Elderblood]], one of Heaven's most important surviving Angels. Ariel eventually joined Victor's Family and later evolved beyond her original Angelic state, creating a direct familial link between the Heavenly Father and Clan Elderblood.

#### Removal of Biblical Hell
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later reorganized Earth's Underworlds with the involvement of [[The Judges of The Abyss]] and [[The Universal Tree]]. [[Biblical Hell]] and other infernal territories were transferred into his Soul and merged into the new Hell belonging to Dragons Nest.[4] The old [[Biblical Heaven]]–Hell political structure therefore ceased to exist even though both realms remain historically part of the Biblical religious cosmology.

#### Imperial Era
The [[Heavenly Father]]'s decision to cooperate with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] allowed the Biblical Pantheon to survive the formation of the [[Elderblood Empire]] with considerably greater institutional continuity than Pantheons that directly opposed him. Chapter 1086 establishes that every surviving Pantheon and major supernatural Faction on Earth ultimately became incorporated into Victor's Empire while internally cooperative Pantheons retained substantial self-government.[5] The Heavenly Father consequently remains the Biblical Pantheon's God-King and internal sovereign beneath Victor's greater Imperial authority.

### Angels
Main article: Angels
Angels are the principal supernatural Race of the modern Biblical Pantheon and historically served as the inhabitants, military force, administrators and messengers of Heaven. The most important distinction within Angelkind concerns Soul structure: several Original Angels possessed complete Souls, whereas most later Angels were constructed from positive Soul portions and consequently possess incomplete Souls.[1] The original generation's greater potential allowed beings such as [[Lucifer]], Michael, [[Gabriel]] and [[Azrael]] to attain powers comparable to important Gods.
The Pantheon also contains the Seven Heavenly Virtues, Concept-bearing Angels associated with specific Virtues. This system should not automatically be equated with the Seven Heavens; no chapter presently establishes that each numbered Heaven corresponds to one particular Virtue.

### Demons
Main article: Demons
Demons formed the historical infernal population of the Biblical Pantheon, developing their own nobility, military hierarchy, bloodlines and organizations within [[Biblical Hell]]. Major historical groups included the Ars Goetia, Seven Deadly Sins and Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse. Following Hell's transfer into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul, these Demons became politically associated with Dragons Nest rather than the modern Biblical Pantheon, although their historical origin remains Biblical.

### Current Status
The Biblical Pantheon remains active but no longer possesses the same dual political structure it maintained historically. [[Biblical Heaven]], its Angels and the [[Heavenly Father]] remain the modern core of the Pantheon, while former [[Biblical Hell]] and its infernal civilization belong to Dragons Nest. The Pantheon itself exists within the [[Elderblood Empire]], where the Heavenly Father continues to exercise internal sovereignty beneath [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Imperial authority.[5]
