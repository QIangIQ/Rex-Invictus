### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 682
Wrath was a powerful Demon, one of the Seven Deadly Sins serving [[Diablo]], and the Demon who represented the Sin of Wrath. He was born within the Fields of Hatred, one of the dangerous Lesser Hells where Souls carrying overwhelming hatred were sent and where exceptionally powerful Demons could emerge.[1] Wrath was counted among Diablo's strongest individual combatants; prior to War becoming a [[Demon God]], he was one of only a small number of Demons considered capable of fighting the Horseman evenly.[2]
During the climax of the Demonic War, Wrath personally confronted [[Thor]], openly declaring his desire to kill a God. He later suffered severe wounds while fighting the Seven Virtues and was removed from the battlefield alongside Envy and Pride. Shortly afterward, [[Heavenly Father]] unleashed Heavenly Judgment across the battlefield, and Wrath was among the Deadly Sins completely destroyed by its Holy Light.[3][4]

### Physical Appearance
Wrath's complete physical appearance is never described in detail in the novel. During his confrontation with [[Thor]], the narration only characterizes him as a crazed Demon immediately before he attacks the Norse God.[3] No canonical description of his height, hair, eyes, horns, wings, clothing or True Form is presently given.

### Personality and Traits
Wrath displayed an intensely aggressive and battle-seeking temperament befitting his position as the Sin of Wrath. Rather than hesitate when [[Thor]] entered the Demonic War and began slaughtering Demons, Wrath immediately intercepted him. When Thor recognized him, Wrath revealed that he had always wanted to kill a God and willingly collided with the strongest active warrior of the [[Norse Pantheon]] despite knowing Thor's reputation.[3]
His behavior indicates considerable confidence in his own strength. This confidence was not baseless: long before Wrath personally appeared in the war, [[Diablo]] and [[Bael]] discussed the strongest combatants available to Hell and placed Wrath among the extremely small group capable of fighting War evenly before the Horseman's ascension to Demon Godhood.[2] Unlike lesser Demons who relied upon superior numbers, Wrath was therefore recognized as an elite individual fighter even among Diablo's highest-ranking forces.
Wrath's origin also reflected his defining Sin. He was born from the Fields of Hatred, a Lesser Hell associated with Mortals who died while carrying enormous amounts of hatred. Demons produced in this region were noted for being unusually powerful, with Wrath later cited as a prime example of what the Fields could produce.[1]

### History

#### Origins
Wrath originated within the Fields of Hatred, one of the specialized Lesser Hells existing inside [[Biblical Hell]]. Mortals whose Souls carried extraordinary hatred could be directed toward this region, and the resulting environment was known for producing particularly powerful Infernal Demons. Wrath was specifically identified as a Demon born there.[1]
At some point he rose through Hell's hierarchy and became one of the Seven Deadly Sins, an elite group directly associated with [[Diablo]]. Wrath represented the Sin corresponding to rage and wrath and became one of Diablo's most powerful subordinates.
Wrath's position among Hell's combat elite is demonstrated during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s battle against the Four Horsemen. When Victor Elderblood confronted War, the Horseman recalled that only exceptionally powerful Demons could normally deal with beings of his level. Later, after Victor defeated War, Diablo explicitly assessed the Horseman's strength and stated that before War attained Godhood, only Wrath, Pride, [[Agares]] and [[Bael]] could fight him evenly. After War became a [[Demon God]], that list was reduced to Bael and Agares.[2]
Wrath was therefore considerably stronger than ordinary Demons and most of the 72 Pillars of Hell, although he was below the level War reached after attaining Divinity.

#### Demonic War
During the final phase of the Demonic War, the Seven Deadly Sins formed part of [[Diablo]]'s remaining elite forces while Hell and Heaven fought across Earth. The conflict eventually escalated far beyond the armies of ordinary Angels and Demons, drawing in God-King-level combatants, the Seven Virtues, the Four Horsemen, [[Lilith Elderblood]], the Pillar Demons and outside forces from multiple Pantheons.[3]
When [[Thor]] entered the battlefield on Heaven's side, his arrival immediately changed the balance of the conflict. Thor began raining Lightning across the battlefield and eliminating Demons, prompting multiple Deadly Sins to move against him.[3]
After the battlefield became increasingly chaotic, Thor descended among the Demonic forces and continued attacking. Wrath charged him almost immediately. Thor avoided Wrath's initial approach, recognized the Demon by name and confronted him directly. Wrath responded that he had always wanted to kill a God, while Thor declared that Wrath would not achieve that ambition against him. The two then collided with enough force that the resulting explosion isolated their confrontation from the surrounding armies.[3]
The novel does not provide the complete exchange between Wrath and Thor, nor does it establish a decisive victor in their individual battle. The greater war continued simultaneously across numerous fronts.
After the deaths of Michael and [[Gabriel]] and the intervention of [[Azrael]], Heaven reorganized beneath Ariel. The Seven Virtues and other Heavenly elites began pushing back against Diablo's forces. Wrath, Envy and Pride eventually suffered numerous wounds from the Seven Virtues and had to be rescued and carried to the rear of the Demonic army. All three were temporarily incapacitated, with the narration emphasizing that Angelic attacks were exceptionally lethal to Demons because of their Holy nature.[4]
Their removal did not save them. With Heaven's elite forces being rapidly depleted, Ariel appealed directly to the [[Heavenly Father]]. He ordered the Angels to retreat from the battlefield and activated Heavenly Judgment, a large-scale weapon of Holy Light capable of annihilating Demons across an enormous area.[4]
Beams of Holy Light descended across the battlefield and destroyed approximately half of the remaining Demonic forces. Wrath and the other Deadly Sins caught within the Judgment were completely evaporated. Sloth was the sole surviving member of the original Seven Deadly Sins, protecting herself inside an enormous concentration of Miasma that prevented the Judgment from fully reaching her.[4]
Wrath's destruction was permanent. Demons normally possess the ability to eventually reconstitute themselves within Hell after being killed, with stronger Demons requiring longer periods to reform. This process does not function when a Demon is destroyed by Holy Light, certain specialized weapons, or powers capable of absorbing them. Because Wrath was evaporated by the Heavenly Father's Holy Judgment, he could not return through normal Demonic reconstitution.[1]

#### Legacy
Long after the Demonic War and [[Diablo]]'s fall, Wrath continued to be remembered within Hell as an example of the kind of Demon produced by its most dangerous Lesser Hells.
During [[Victor Elderblood]]'s later consolidation of the entire realm, [[Lily Elderblood]] conquered regions known as the Fields of Hatred, Famine, Destruction and Fear. These regions were noted for generating some of Hell's strongest Infernal Demons. When the Fields of Hatred were discussed, Wrath was specifically used as the prime historical example of a powerful Demon born from that territory.[1]

### Skills & Abilities
As one of the Seven Deadly Sins and one of [[Diablo]]'s highest-level combatants, Wrath possessed power vastly above ordinary Demons. However, his direct appearance was extremely brief, and the novel never reveals the exact mechanics of his unique Sin of Wrath ability. Abilities that have not been demonstrated or explained should therefore not be attributed to him solely because they are thematically associated with anger.
- Demon Physiology: Wrath was a high-ranking Infernal Demon born within [[Biblical Hell]]. Demons are supernatural Beings closely associated with corruption and infernal Energy and can survive conditions that would kill ordinary living creatures.[1]
- Conditional [[Immortality]] / Reconstitution: Demons who are killed through conventional means can eventually return to Hell and reconstruct their bodies. More powerful Demons require longer periods to reconstitute. This form of survival can be bypassed by Holy Light, specialized anti-Demon weapons or abilities capable of absorbing the Demon entirely.[1]
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics: Wrath possessed physical and supernatural attributes sufficient to directly engage [[Thor]]. Their initial collision produced an explosion of power large enough to separate their confrontation from the surrounding battle.[3]
- Extreme Combat Power: Wrath belonged to the highest combat echelon beneath Diablo. Before War's ascension into a [[Demon God]], Diablo considered Wrath one of only four Demons—alongside Pride, [[Agares]] and [[Bael]]—capable of fighting War evenly.[2]
- Close-Quarters Combat: Wrath willingly engaged Thor at close range rather than relying upon support from the surrounding Demonic army. Although his complete fighting style is not shown, his position and comparison to War demonstrate that he was a capable direct combatant.[2][3]
- Hatred Affinity: Wrath was born within the Fields of Hatred, a Lesser Hell formed around Souls carrying extraordinary hatred. Demons emerging from this territory were noted for being unusually powerful. The novel does not explicitly explain whether Wrath could manipulate Hatred itself, so this should be treated as an aspect of his origin rather than confirmed Hatred Manipulation.[1]
- Sin of Wrath: Wrath possessed the status of one of the original Seven Deadly Sins. The Seven Sins possessed individual powers associated with their positions, but Wrath died before the novel revealed the mechanics of his personal Sin ability. Consequently, abilities such as Rage Empowerment, Anger Manipulation or Wrath Inducement cannot presently be confirmed for him from the novel.
- Demonic Resilience: Wrath endured combat with Thor and later survived long enough to be removed from the battlefield despite suffering numerous wounds from the Seven Virtues. His injuries nevertheless incapacitated him due to the exceptional lethality of Angelic/Holy attacks against Demons.[3][4]

#### Power
Wrath was one of the strongest Demons serving [[Diablo]].
Before War attained Divinity, Diablo's assessment placed Wrath in the same general combat bracket as the Horseman, with only Wrath, Pride, [[Agares]] and [[Bael]] stated to be capable of fighting War evenly. Once War evolved into a [[Demon God]] during his battle with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Wrath and Pride were no longer included among those capable of matching him, leaving only Agares and Bael.[2]

#### Weaknesses
- [[Holy Energy]] / Light: Holy power is naturally extremely lethal to Demons. The Seven Virtues inflicted severe injuries upon Wrath, Pride and Envy, forcing all three from active combat.[4]
- Permanent Destruction by Holy Light: A Demon's normal reconstitution can be prevented when they are killed through Holy Light. Wrath was permanently destroyed after being caught in the [[Heavenly Father]]'s Judgment.[4][1]
- Higher Divine Power: Despite his standing among Hell's elites, Wrath remained vulnerable to sufficiently powerful Divine opponents. The collective Seven Virtues incapacitated him, and the Heavenly Father's mass Judgment annihilated him completely.[4]

### Relationships

#### Diablo
Wrath served [[Diablo]] as one of his Seven Deadly Sins and belonged to the Demon King's elite military forces. Diablo's own evaluation placed Wrath among the limited number of Demons strong enough to contest the Four Horsemen before War's Divine evolution, demonstrating the value Diablo placed upon his combat strength.[2]
Wrath remained among Diablo's forces during the final Demonic War and fought until he was incapacitated by Heaven's elites.[3][4]

#### Seven Deadly Sins
Wrath was one of [[Diablo]]'s Seven Deadly Sins alongside Pride, Envy, Greed, Gluttony, Lust and Sloth. During the Demonic War, Wrath, Pride and Envy were all severely wounded by the Seven Virtues before being removed from combat. Heavenly Judgment subsequently destroyed every remaining Deadly Sin caught within its range except Sloth.[4]

#### Thor
[[Thor]] was Wrath's only individually depicted opponent. Wrath deliberately intercepted him after the Norse God entered the battlefield and began destroying Demons. Rather than showing fear toward one of the [[Norse Pantheon]]'s premier warriors, Wrath expressed excitement at finally having the opportunity to kill a God. Their collision produced a large explosion and separated their battle from the surrounding armies.[3]
No conclusive result of their individual battle is shown.
