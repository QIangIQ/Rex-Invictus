#### Debut
Chapter 531
My King... My Father... My Creator... I exist to serve you, just like all the others who will come after me.
—War to [[Victor Elderblood]], Chapter 912
War is an ancient former member of the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse, an ascended [[Demon God]], and the first [[Chaos]] Progenitor intentionally created by Victor Elderblood. Originally one of the strongest and most loyal servants of [[Diablo]], the former King of [[Biblical Hell]], War served as the Horseman of War and represented one of the greatest military threats during the Demonic War. His extraordinary fighting spirit, unwavering determination and refusal to surrender against overwhelmingly powerful opponents distinguished him from other high-ranking Demons. These qualities ultimately earned Victor's respect, leading the Vampire Progenitor to spare War's life after defeating him, rather than consume him alongside his three brothers.[6][7]
During his final confrontation with Victor, War surpassed the limitations of his original Demonic nature and underwent a natural ascension into genuine Godhood, becoming the first Demon to achieve such a transformation since the ancient era associated with [[Lilith Elderblood]]. Although the ascension dramatically increased his strength, he was ultimately defeated and suffered catastrophic injuries to both his body and Soul. His loyal horse carried him away from the battlefield, allowing the fallen Horseman to begin a new existence within the Flaming Inferno. War subsequently awakened without clear memories of his previous life, but the warrior instincts and personality that had defined him remained intact.[8][9]
Following Victor's ascension as the Dragon God of Chaos, War underwent an even greater transformation. Recognizing his extraordinary potential and devotion, Victor reconstructed his existence using Positive Energy, Negative Energy, Dragon Fire, the characteristics inherited from the other three Horsemen, a fragment of his own Essence and the influence of his Divinity of Beginning. The result was a genuine Chaos Progenitor, a fundamentally new supernatural existence whose body contained characteristics that would ordinarily be incompatible under Creation's established divisions. Despite appearing to consist almost entirely of Darkness, War possessed genuine Holy characteristics and an extraordinary connection to Victor's Chaos Essence. His memories were also restored, allowing him to retain the experiences of his original life rather than remain permanently separated from his former identity.[10]
Victor formally acknowledged War as his son, while War recognized the Dragon God as his King, Father and Creator. He subsequently became the original Progenitor of the [[Heralds of Chaos]], a supernatural lineage created to execute Victor's will and protect the interests of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. As the Empire expanded, War developed into its foremost Herald, acting as an Imperial messenger, investigator, military commander and executor of the Emperor's judgments. His strength eventually surpassed that of [[Shiva]], and by the later Imperial Era he belonged to the rare category of supernatural combatants capable of threatening entire galaxies.[11][12][13]
During the opening conflict with the Upper Sectors, Victor entrusted War with the sentient Eldritch greatsword [[Gol-goroth]], whose empowerment through chaotic Eldritch War allowed him to manifest incarnations of Famine, Pestilence and Death. War entered the Upper Sectors as Victor's forward combatant and confronted [[Azrakar]], a Chief God of the [[Black Eclipse Pantheon]], in a battle devastating star systems and affecting neighboring galaxies. The intervention of [[Kaelith]], [[Nerath]] and [[Vhalor]] turned the confrontation into a struggle between four Chief Gods and the four Eldritch Horsemen. When his brothers came under increasing pressure, War revealed deeper Eldritch powers he had previously concealed, recalled them and joined them within an isolated Eldritch domain created by Gol-goroth. He transferred his power and temporarily synchronized their senses, enabling a coordinated attack in which he decapitated Azrakar and pierced his heart before all four Horsemen erased the God of the Void from existence. Their victory provoked the Great Darkness to summon further Chief Gods and order the Horsemen captured or killed. War did not wait. He drove Kaelith, Nerath and Vhalor to the edge of erasure inside the same domain until their patron forced him back and [[Vaelthar]], Throne of Origin, broke the field with the remaining Chief Gods. War shut the domain down. He refused to be surrendered to Justice, [[Elarion]] or Black Eclipse. Victor then arrived, praised him, killed Kaelith through her Soul, and the wider assault opened on a projection of the Emperor, not on War. War did not take those blows. When the projection left, it took him and the fallen Gods with it. Gol-goroth went with its wielder.[2][14][4][15][16][17]

### Physical Appearance
War's appearance has undergone several fundamental transformations throughout his existence, reflecting his development from an ancient Hell-Fire Demon into a naturally ascended [[Demon God]] and ultimately a [[Chaos]] Progenitor. In his original form, War appeared as an enormous, heavily muscular humanoid Demon exceeding two meters in height. His imposing physique was covered by heavy armor, and he wielded a massive greatsword appropriate to his extraordinary size and strength. Beneath his helmet, he possessed completely white eyes without conventional pupils and long black hair resembling floating embers. His body radiated black-and-red Miasma, reinforcing the intimidating appearance of an infernal warrior. He was commonly accompanied by a supernatural red horse whose eyes, hooves and tail burned with Hellfire, emphasizing his identity as one of the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse.[18]
Following his natural ascension into Demon Godhood, War's appearance became increasingly imposing as his body acquired genuine Divine characteristics. His supernatural flames intensified, his physical constitution was restored and reinforced, and his connection to the infernal environment deepened. Although the transformation substantially increased his strength and altered his spiritual existence, he remained recognizable as the same enormous warrior who had served [[Diablo]]. His later rebirth within the Flaming Inferno temporarily stripped him of the established identity and equipment associated with the original Horseman, leaving him to develop as a nameless Demon guided by instinctive combat ability and the remnants of his former personality.[6][8][9]
War's most significant physical transformation occurred when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] reconstructed him as a Chaos Progenitor. His new body stood approximately 250 centimeters tall and possessed an extraordinarily muscular constitution. The most noticeable change was his face, which became a featureless region of pure Darkness, concealing his original eyes, hair and recognizable facial characteristics. This Darkness was not merely an external covering or a consequence of insufficient lighting, but an inherent property of his reconstructed supernatural existence. Black Miasma surrounded his enormous body, while portions formerly covered by Hellfire became infused with violet Dragon Fire. His original greatsword also underwent a transformation, acquiring a more sinister appearance that reflected the changes to his body and Essence.[5][10][3]
Six enormous wings emerged from War's back, composed primarily of supernatural Darkness but accompanied by a faint white aura and genuine Holy characteristics. Their contradictory nature astonished the [[Heavenly Father]] and Ariel, who initially struggled to understand how a former Demon could possess characteristics resembling those of an Angel. Although his outward appearance suggested an existence born entirely from Darkness, his new body contained Positive Energy, Negative Energy, Dragon Fire and Victor's Chaos Essence, producing an unusual combination of supernatural properties that did not conform to the ordinary classification of Demons, Angels or Gods. War's horse underwent a corresponding evolution, becoming more powerful and acquiring violet Dragon Fire in place of its original red Hellfire while retaining its bond with its master.[5][10]
During the Imperial Era, War commonly appeared in dark armor and hooded garments that concealed much of his already featureless face. His extraordinary height, six wings and overwhelming supernatural presence distinguished him from ordinary humanoid beings, while his appearance became closely associated with the direct intervention of the Emperor. The Heralds descended from him inherited several recognizable characteristics, including faces obscured by supernatural Darkness and wings reflecting their Chaos-derived lineage. When Victor later created [[Gol-goroth]], War's armament changed again. The new weapon was an enormous purple greatsword possessing a gigantic supernatural eye, and it briefly fused with his arm upon being accepted. During its awakening, a jagged grin lined with sharp teeth momentarily appeared beneath War's hood, reflecting the influence of his newly acquired Eldritch War powers. His appearance during the confrontation with [[Azrakar]] remained that of a towering armored Chaos Progenitor wielding Gol-goroth, although his body accumulated visible injuries as the battle extended across celestial regions.[13][1][2]
War's previously concealed Eldritch powers became evident when he reunited the four Horsemen against Black Eclipse. He raised Gol-goroth as its enormous eye opened and the weapon established an isolated Eldritch domain. Four pillars of raw Energy rose around the combatants, and nearby stars disappeared under the pressure despite the Great Darkness's containment of the battlefield. The clearest physical transformations involved his three manifested brothers: their faces disappeared, their armor turned completely black and wings of dark Energy extended from their backs, consuming the surrounding light. War already possessed a face composed of Darkness and six supernatural wings; no separate new configuration of his own features was explicitly established.[4]

### Personality
War is a disciplined, intelligent and intensely loyal warrior whose personality revolves around combat, personal strength, determination and the fulfillment of his master's will. Although his identity is closely connected to the violence of [[Biblical Hell]] and the Concept of War, he is not a mindless berserker who acts solely through aggression. Even during his original service beneath [[Diablo]], War demonstrated considerable military experience and tactical judgment, regularly evaluating battlefield developments, communicating intelligence and recommending appropriate responses to emerging threats. His conduct reflected the mentality of an experienced soldier who understood the importance of preparation, discipline and the consequences of failing to recognize a dangerous opponent. Nevertheless, his greatest personal satisfaction came from confronting powerful enemies and testing the limits of his own abilities.[18][6]
War possesses an extraordinary attraction toward battle, regarding sufficiently powerful opponents as opportunities for improvement rather than threats to be avoided. He values martial excellence, courage and determination, and his enjoyment of combat is not dependent exclusively upon the possibility of victory. During his confrontation with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], he continued fighting despite suffering injuries that would have incapacitated or destroyed most Demons, refusing to surrender before exhausting his abilities. His determination ultimately pushed him beyond the limitations of his original species and triggered his natural ascension into Demon Godhood. Even after that transformation, War remained unable to overcome Victor, but he regarded the confrontation as meaningful because it had allowed him to challenge an opponent who genuinely surpassed him.[6][7]
Beneath his disciplined exterior, War possesses genuine emotions and attachments. The deaths of his fellow Horsemen caused him considerable grief, and Victor recognized the loneliness and pain hidden beneath his determination to continue fighting. However, War did not attempt to deny the legitimacy of their defeat or excuse his own inability to protect them. His worldview had been shaped by the brutal laws of Hell, where powerful opponents could overcome weaker beings through combat, and he accepted that challenging an enemy carried the possibility of death. Rather than surrender to despair, he directed his grief toward the desire to become stronger. Victor recognized this attitude as genuine warrior's pride and chose to spare him out of respect, establishing a relationship founded upon mutual recognition rather than simple domination.[7]
War's loyalty toward Diablo had originally been absolute. He regarded the Demonic King's commands as obligations that deserved his full commitment, accepting dangerous assignments without hesitation and considering obedience a fundamental part of his identity. Nevertheless, his devotion did not eliminate independent thought. He remained capable of assessing military situations, offering recommendations and understanding the behavior of other supernatural factions. His later rebirth demonstrated that his attraction toward combat existed more deeply than conscious memory, as the nameless Demon awakened within the Flaming Inferno immediately sought battle and personal improvement despite lacking clear recollection of his former sovereign, brothers or military responsibilities.[18][9]
Following his reconstruction as a [[Chaos]] Progenitor, War's loyalty became focused entirely upon Victor. Unlike his former allegiance to Diablo, this relationship incorporated profound gratitude, admiration and genuine familial attachment. Victor had recognized his worth, preserved his opportunity for another life, cultivated his development and ultimately created his current existence. War therefore regarded him not merely as a sovereign but simultaneously as his King, Father and Creator, considering the fulfillment of Victor's will the highest purpose of his existence. His devotion did not transform him into an emotionless instrument; War continued possessing independent judgment, personal pride and the ability to experience happiness. When Victor congratulated him for successfully completing his final trial, War remained outwardly composed, but Victor could perceive the genuine satisfaction hidden beneath his disciplined behavior.[5][10]
[[Roxanne Elderblood]] recognized that War's loyalty would remain unchanged regardless of how powerful he eventually became. His connection to Victor was not simply the consequence of possessing a fragment of the Dragon God's Essence, but was reinforced by his own memories, convictions and gratitude. This devotion allowed Victor to entrust him with responsibilities that might otherwise have been dangerous to grant to an individual possessing extraordinary supernatural power. As the first Chaos Progenitor and the strongest of the Emperor's Heralds, War consequently developed a reputation for absolute reliability, carrying out assignments with the expectation that his personal preferences would never supersede Victor's explicit commands.[5][10][13]
Despite his enjoyment of conflict, War possesses a structured understanding of punishment and duty. He does not indiscriminately execute every prisoner delivered to him, instead distinguishing between individuals condemned to death, those suitable for transformation into Heralds and those whose punishment has already been determined by Victor. His personal desire for battle remains subordinate to his responsibility as the Emperor's executor. However, crimes involving Victor's family provoke particularly severe hostility. War regards deliberate harm against members of the Elderblood household as an offense deserving extraordinary punishment, and his treatment of [[Kurama]] demonstrated his willingness to impose consequences far beyond conventional execution when an enemy had contributed to the suffering of one of Victor's daughters.[19][11]
War's devotion also extends toward the protection of the Imperial family and the preservation of Victor's interests. He has personally positioned himself between members of the Elderblood household and dangerous supernatural influences, while his subordinate Heralds established additional protective measures. Although he is capable of devastating entire celestial regions, he does not regard indiscriminate destruction as an acceptable substitute for completing an assignment properly. During his investigation of foreign interference on an Imperial moon, War destroyed the compromised celestial body but reconstructed an equivalent replacement to preserve the stability of the planet beneath it, demonstrating extraordinary restraint and consideration for the consequences of his actions when Imperial territory was involved.[20][13]
His battlefield personality remained recognizable throughout his later development. War responds poorly to opponents who rely upon declarations of superiority rather than demonstrating their strength through combat, and his confrontation with [[Azrakar]] revealed a dry, occasionally contemptuous sense of humor. He repeatedly interrupted the Primordial God's speeches with direct attacks and expressed irritation toward unnecessary posturing, preferring action over intimidation. Nevertheless, he demonstrated exceptional composure when faced with unfamiliar Divine techniques, employing precise timing, calculated movements and analysis of structural weaknesses rather than attempting to overcome every obstacle through raw strength. The manifestation of Eldritch incarnations corresponding to his deceased brothers also carried personal significance, allowing him to fight alongside beings embodying their former apocalyptic roles without establishing that their original Souls had been restored. Across his development from an ancient Horseman into a [[Demon God]], Chaos Progenitor and Imperial Herald, War's fundamental personality remained rooted in the same qualities that originally earned Victor's respect: loyalty, discipline, warrior's pride and an unrelenting determination to overcome powerful opponents.[2][14]
His later battle against Black Eclipse revealed that War could abandon an unfavorable personal engagement when the safety of his brothers and the success of his mission required it. Rather than continually attempt to overpower Azrakar's shifting spatial domain, he learned to exploit the moments when the God needed to readjust its structure. Even after falling for an intentionally exposed opening and receiving a blow to the chest, War shortened his next attack and severed the support of the trap before it fully formed. His adaptation combined strength with careful timing, battlefield awareness and the same discipline that originally earned Victor's respect.[4]
War had deliberately avoided displaying the deeper Eldritch characteristics of his Progenitor lineage, fearing that their revelation could attract unwanted attention. As the four Chief Gods gained the advantage over his brothers, he set aside this caution and ordered the Horsemen to regroup. He transferred his Energy to them and temporarily synchronized their senses, prioritizing collective victory over continuing his duel alone. His willingness to reveal a dangerous hidden ability, coordinate four combatants as one and strike a vulnerable opponent decisively demonstrates both his loyalty and the depth of his military judgment.[4]

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
War first became directly involved in the events surrounding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction as one of [[Diablo]]'s principal military servants during the Demonic invasion. As the conflict expanded across the Human World, he appeared before the Demonic King in [[Biblical Hell]] to report important military developments, including the death of [[Sitri|Duke Sitri]] at the hands of [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] and the activities of Phoenix. Rather than rely entirely upon spoken testimony, War generated a visual reconstruction through his black-and-red Miasma, allowing Diablo to observe the circumstances surrounding the losses suffered by his forces. His assessment demonstrated that he understood the growing danger represented by Victor and the allied Vampire Clans. When Diablo requested recommendations, War advised activating contingency preparations and treating the Vampires as genuine enemies rather than dismissing their increasingly successful resistance. The King subsequently ordered him to strengthen Hell's defenses, gather available personnel and prepare further operations involving the other Horsemen.[18]
War later cooperated with his brother, the [[Horseman of Death]], during an operation targeting the Vatican. Following the infiltration of the Inquisition and the temporary neutralization of Ariel, the two Horsemen launched a coordinated attack against Pope Alexander. Death severed the Pope's arms, preventing him from effectively resisting, while War impaled him through the heart with his greatsword. The execution coincided with the withdrawal of Divine power previously granted to Alexander, exposing the limitations of his mortal existence and leaving his Soul condemned to Limbo. The operation demonstrated War's position as one of Diablo's most dependable executioners and established the Horsemen as threats capable of eliminating important religious authorities despite the protections normally associated with their positions.[21]
The original Four Horsemen eventually confronted Victor after he entered Biblical Hell. War fought alongside Death, Famine and Pestilence, whose respective supernatural characteristics allowed them to employ coordinated attacks involving Darkness, Hunger, Decay and direct physical violence. Despite their combined strength and extensive experience, Victor progressively overwhelmed their abilities and defeated the three other Horsemen, consuming their bodies and obtaining characteristics associated with their supernatural natures. War remained as the final surviving member, witnessing the destruction of the brothers with whom he had served for an extraordinary length of time. Rather than abandon the confrontation, he continued fighting, motivated by grief, determination and his refusal to accept defeat without exhausting every possibility available to him.[22][23][24]
The confrontation gradually developed into a genuine contest between two warriors who enjoyed challenging powerful opponents. War's original Demonic abilities proved insufficient to overcome Victor, but the extraordinary pressure of the battle caused his strength, reactions and martial techniques to improve. His determination eventually triggered a natural ascension into Godhood, making him the first Demon to achieve such a transformation since the ancient era associated with Lilith. War's Soul and body underwent reconstruction, his supernatural power increased dramatically, and his connection to the foundations of Biblical Hell deepened. The newly ascended [[Demon God]] continued fighting Victor at a considerably higher level, exchanging attacks powerful enough to inflict lasting damage upon the surrounding infernal environment. Nevertheless, his opponent's extraordinary regeneration, adaptability and supernatural versatility prevented him from securing victory.[6][7][8]
Victor ultimately defeated War after an exhausting confrontation, leaving his body and Soul catastrophically damaged. Despite possessing the opportunity to consume the fallen Demon God, Victor recognized the extraordinary determination and warrior's pride displayed throughout their battle and chose to spare him. War's loyal horse recovered its master's body and carried him toward the region associated with his origin, while the [[Will Solace|Will]] of Hell protected the area to preserve his opportunity for recovery. The defeat effectively ended the original Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse as an organization: Death, Famine and Pestilence had been consumed, and War survived only through Victor's decision to preserve the life of an opponent he genuinely respected.[7][8]

#### King Among Kings
Following his defeat, War underwent an unusual rebirth within the Flaming Inferno, awakening as a nameless Demon without clear memories of his original identity. Although his consciousness no longer retained an organized recollection of his brothers, allegiance to [[Diablo]] or confrontation with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], the instincts that had defined him remained deeply embedded within his existence. He immediately sought opportunities for combat and personal development, confronting powerful creatures within the infernal environment and progressing through repeated battles. His determination, attraction toward conflict and refusal to surrender demonstrated that the foundation of his personality had survived despite the destruction of much of his former conscious identity.[9]
Victor eventually recognized the potential of the reborn warrior and deliberately cultivated his development. Rather than simply restore him to his original condition or demand immediate service, Victor observed his progress and encouraged the qualities that had originally earned his admiration. War's growth became part of Victor's broader understanding of how supernatural beings could exceed the limitations normally imposed by their races, particularly when their development was supported by appropriate training, resources and extraordinary supernatural circumstances. His eventual transformation would demonstrate that Victor's powers were capable of producing an entirely new Progenitor rather than merely strengthening an existing Demon.[5]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
War's greatest transformation occurred after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] ascended as the Dragon God of [[Chaos]] and visited the highest regions of the [[Biblical Heaven]]. During his discussions with the [[Heavenly Father]], Victor explored the distinction between ordinary supernatural evolution and the creation of fundamentally new forms of existence. War was brought before him as an example of a Demon who had already surpassed his original limits through natural Divine ascension, but whose potential remained far from exhausted. Although Victor initially intended to demonstrate an improvement to Demonic physiology, War's performance and loyalty convinced him to attempt something considerably more ambitious. Instead of merely restoring his previous strength, Victor decided to reconstruct the warrior through the combined properties of supernatural Essences normally considered incompatible.[5]
Victor gathered Positive Energy, Negative Energy and Dragon Fire into a supernatural foundation whose composition consisted of approximately 1% Positive Energy, 1% Negative Energy and 98% Dragon Fire. He then incorporated the characteristics acquired from the other original Horsemen: Death's Darkness, Famine's Hunger and Pestilence's Decay. These elements were combined with War's existing Divine nature and a fragment of Victor's own Essence, while his developing Divinity of Beginning influenced the reconstruction. The resulting process exceeded conventional racial evolution, fundamentally altering War's body, Soul and supernatural identity. Flames surrounding his body transformed into Dragon Fire, his face became pure Darkness, his greatsword acquired a more sinister appearance, and six enormous wings emerged from his back. Despite their dark composition, the wings possessed genuine Holy characteristics, astonishing the Heavenly Father and Ariel because they contradicted the conventional distinction between Demonic and Angelic beings.[5][10]
The transformation produced a genuine Chaos Progenitor, an existence whose supernatural composition incorporated both Positive and Negative characteristics without being destroyed by their opposition. [[Roxanne Elderblood]] recognized the significance of the result, identifying War as the foundation of a new supernatural lineage rather than an enhanced member of his former species. At the reconstruction itself that lineage was the origin of the Bearers of Damnation; the servants he later raised from it were organized as the [[Heralds of Chaos]]. His memories of the original existence were restored during the reconstruction, allowing him to retain his identity as the former Horseman of War while acknowledging the new life created through Victor. When Victor formally recognized him as his son born from Darkness, War accepted his hand and declared that his existence was dedicated to serving his King, Father and Creator. The declaration established the personal relationship that would subsequently define War's place within the Elderblood family and its growing Empire.[5][10]
War's horse underwent a corresponding evolution, acquiring a stronger supernatural constitution and violet Dragon Fire in place of its original Hellfire. Its bond with War remained intact despite the fundamental changes to both rider and mount, demonstrating that the transformation had preserved the personal relationships underlying their identities. The Heavenly Father initially reacted negatively to the apparent contradiction represented by War's existence, but Victor rejected the assumption that the established divisions of Creation should determine which beings were permitted to exist. The event became one of the earliest demonstrations of Victor's ability to deliberately create supernatural lineages through his understanding of Beginning, Chaos and the relationship between opposing Essences.[5][10]
The newly created Progenitor subsequently received responsibilities involving individuals who had previously opposed Victor. Among them were Jimmy, Thomas, Lucy and Karen, whose histories connected them to the earliest stages of Victor's supernatural life. Rather than executing every former enemy, Victor selected individuals who could be given another opportunity through service. War supervised their transformation into members of the Heralds of Chaos, fundamentally reconstructing their bodies and supernatural characteristics through his new lineage. The conversions established that War could create additional members of his species and that the Heralds were genuine supernatural beings rather than ordinary soldiers temporarily empowered through equipment or magical contracts.[19][11]
Other prisoners received substantially different treatment. War considered the crimes committed by [[Kurama]] against Victor's family particularly severe and regarded conventional execution as an insufficient punishment. Acting according to the Emperor's judgment, he subjected Kurama to an extraordinary fate connected to the terrifying supernatural forces under Victor's authority. His conduct demonstrated that the Heralds were intended to enforce Imperial decisions rather than operate solely as an extermination force, with War responsible for ensuring that each judgment was carried out according to the Emperor's intentions.[11]
War later cooperated with Nyx Elderblood during operations involving Nidhoggr and the [[Norse Pantheon]]. Victor had personally supplied him with specialized knowledge concerning methods for hunting Blood Dragon subspecies and [[True Dragons]], making him particularly useful when confronting dangerous Draconic beings. His responsibilities were not exclusively military, however, as he also performed assignments involving the transportation of [[Idun]]'s Apples between dimensions. These operations demonstrated that Victor trusted War with sensitive logistical and protective duties rather than regarding his extraordinary combat strength as the only valuable aspect of his service.[25][26]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
As [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] consolidated his territories and established the foundations of a unified Empire, War increasingly assumed the role of an Imperial Herald whose appearance carried the authority of the Emperor himself. During the dispute involving the [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]], War confronted [[Shiva]] and made clear that disrespect toward Victor would no longer be regarded as harmless Divine politics. His intervention demonstrated that the Emperor no longer needed to personally appear before every foreign ruler who challenged his authority, as the Heralds possessed sufficient power and influence to communicate his decisions directly. War's strength was explicitly recognized as greater than Shiva's, while several of his subordinates had already developed abilities comparable to those of the Hindu God-King. The incident illustrated the enormous difference between the supernatural hierarchy that existed during the Demonic War and the military power accumulated by Victor's faction.[3][12]
War continued supervising the expansion of the [[Heralds of Chaos]], transforming them into a specialized Imperial organization whose responsibilities included covert operations, intelligence gathering, protection of the Imperial Bloodline and the elimination of supernatural threats beyond the capabilities of ordinary soldiers. Their extraordinary loyalty originated from the characteristics of their lineage and their dedication to the Emperor, but individual Heralds retained recognizable personalities and independent judgment. War's position as their Progenitor allowed him to direct their development, while his own connection to Victor gave him the authority to act as a messenger, investigator or executor whenever circumstances required direct Imperial intervention. As the Empire expanded beyond its original supernatural territories, the Heralds became increasingly important because their missions frequently involved dangerous entities and unusual supernatural phenomena that conventional military forces were not prepared to confront.[27][13]
During an operation involving dangerous Eldritch organisms associated with the Abyss, War demonstrated the importance of his protective responsibilities. Victor prepared to reveal a portion of his deeper existence, exposing those nearby to supernatural characteristics capable of overwhelming the minds of ordinary beings. Recognizing the potential danger to [[Hela Elderblood]], War immediately positioned himself between her and the source of the disturbance, while subordinate Heralds established additional protective measures. [[Roxanne Elderblood]] and [[Amara Elderblood]] also participated in containing the consequences of the event. War's conduct reflected both the unusual compatibility of his [[Chaos]]-derived existence with Eldritch phenomena and his instinctive determination to protect members of Victor's family regardless of the personal danger involved.[20]
War later participated in preparations for Victor's reconnaissance operations beyond his familiar territories. When the Emperor prepared a younger clone for exploration of unfamiliar cosmic civilizations, he ordered the surrounding Solar System isolated to prevent external interference. War responded alongside other Heralds, securing the region while Victor completed the necessary preparations. The assignment demonstrated that War's responsibilities had expanded beyond hunting individual enemies to protecting operations involving the security of the Empire and the investigation of civilizations possessing technologies or supernatural systems unfamiliar to its native Sector.[28]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
During the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s accelerated developmental era, War became one of the most powerful and trusted individuals directly serving beneath [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. While ordinary Imperial elites could possess sufficient power to devastate entire Solar Systems, warriors comparable to the strongest Herald belonged to a much rarer category capable of threatening entire galaxies. War's position as the strongest of the Emperor's Heralds reflected the combination of his [[Chaos]] Progenitor physiology, extraordinary combat experience, Eldritch characteristics and unwavering loyalty. His responsibilities increasingly extended into intelligence operations, the investigation of foreign supernatural interference and the protection of the Imperial family against enemies originating beyond the Sector. Although the Empire could manufacture powerful artificial soldiers and develop advanced military races, Victor recognized that reproducing War's combination of judgment, stability, individuality and devotion was considerably more difficult than creating a stronger physical body.[13][29][30]
Approximately 170,000 years into the accelerated Imperial Era, War investigated an incident involving [[Nikolai Francis Nichev]], a [[Demigod]] descended from the Celtic God [[Dagda]]. Nikolai had become involved with foreign supernatural powers originating from the Upper Sectors and was attempting to obtain information connected to Victor's Bloodline. The confrontation occurred within the Sagittarius Dwarf Elliptical Galaxy, approximately 80,000 light-years from [[Yggdrasil]], on an Imperial-controlled moon. War appeared before Nikolai as a hooded figure whose face consisted of incomprehensible Darkness, and the supernatural pressure produced by his existence threatened the Demigod's sanity. Nikolai avoided immediate psychological collapse partly because of the foreign corruption already affecting his existence, but he quickly understood that the arrival of the Emperor's strongest Herald meant his conspiracy had been discovered.[13]
War accused Nikolai of betraying the Emperor in favor of foreign Gods and demanded information concerning his assignment. His command carried supernatural authority capable of imposing an overwhelming burden upon the surrounding Reality, compelling the Demigod to respond despite his unwillingness. Nikolai eventually revealed that his foreign patron sought information about Victor's Bloodline, intending to investigate or reproduce characteristics associated with the Emperor's extraordinary existence. The foreign corruption within Nikolai reacted violently to the disclosure and began destroying his body and spiritual condition, confirming that the conspiracy involved powers beyond the normal supernatural systems of the Empire. War eliminated the compromised threat, while four subordinate Heralds captured the remaining conspirators and delivered them to the Third Legion. The operation demonstrated both the danger of foreign infiltration and the efficiency with which the Heralds could respond when threats reached the Emperor's personal attention.[13]
The incident also provided an extraordinary demonstration of War's control over supernatural power. During the elimination of the compromised installation, he compressed an entire moon into a small object in less than two seconds, then reconstructed an identical replacement to prevent the surrounding planet from suffering the consequences normally associated with the sudden disappearance of its satellite. Rather than treating the destruction of Imperial infrastructure as irrelevant collateral damage, War restored the necessary physical conditions before departing. His actions demonstrated the combination of overwhelming strength and disciplined precision expected from one of Victor's highest servants, while his connection to the Emperor allowed him to function as an extension of Victor's perception and influence during sensitive investigations.[13]
As Imperial civilization continued developing, Victor maintained a distinction between the Heralds and other powerful individuals operating within the Empire. [[Tatsuya Rider]] was among the rare men he respected enough to consider for the position of Imperial Herald, but Tatsuya declined the offer, demonstrating that the role was not automatically assigned to every capable warrior who supported Victor. War's place in the organization remained unique because his current existence had been personally created through Victor's Essence and because his devotion extended beyond ordinary political allegiance. Meanwhile, the Heralds became integrated into the Empire's intelligence network, monitoring dangerous supernatural disturbances and communicating information concerning foreign influence to the appropriate Imperial authorities. During an incident involving [[Ryujin Ikaros]] and an unidentified Divine fragment, their broader institution was already involved in observing the situation before the information reached the highest levels of Imperial administration.[31][30]
The situation deteriorated as the barrier separating Victor's Sector from the Upper Sectors began weakening. Foreign factions attempted to infiltrate Imperial territory using supernatural objects containing fragments of Divine Concepts, while Victor struggled to maintain the enormous ritual supporting the Empire's continued development and resist pressure from the opposite side of the barrier. The undertaking placed considerable strain upon his Soul, and he deliberately avoided employing certain Eldritch characteristics that might interfere with the Empire's infrastructure. When [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] reported that research into paradox-based weapons had not yet produced a workable breakthrough, Victor ordered the Empire to prepare for war within a year. Rather than wait passively for foreign armies to penetrate his territory, he decided to send a powerful combatant through the barrier and begin retaliating against those attempting to conquer his civilization.[30][1]
War answered Victor's summons and emerged from supernatural Darkness in full armor, his enormous wings obscuring the surrounding starlight. Victor explained that foreign factions considered the Empire vulnerable and intended to seize its territory, making a direct demonstration of Imperial strength necessary. War accepted the assignment without hesitation and reaffirmed that his will belonged entirely to his master. Victor then manifested a massive purple greatsword formed from pure Dragon Power and awakened it through an Eldritch declaration. The weapon changed shape, developed a gigantic supernatural eye and acknowledged Victor as Great Chaos. Its special condition established that an Emperor-recognized bearer would become War personified, allowing the weapon to serve as the foundation of an extraordinary new form of Eldritch warfare. Victor named it [[Gol-goroth]] and entrusted it to his most faithful Herald.[1]
When War grasped the weapon's hilt, Gol-goroth briefly fused with his arm before returning to its greatsword form, granting him access to unfamiliar powers associated with chaotic Eldritch War. Unlike ordinary Divine warfare, the power represented a violent and unpredictable form of conflict that could endanger allies as readily as enemies. War understood that its greatest effectiveness would be achieved when surrounded entirely by hostile forces, and a jagged grin appeared beneath his hood as he considered the prospect of fighting alone against powerful opponents. Victor subsequently opened a portal through the Sector barrier, while War transformed into a comet of Darkness that consumed the surrounding light and accelerated toward the passage at faster-than-light speed. The Emperor intended his deployment to precede the arrival of the Imperial Legions, allowing the Empire to assess its enemies before committing the enormous Leviathans and Dragons held in reserve.[1]
Violet questioned whether even a warrior of War's caliber should be sent against potentially enormous enemy forces without support. Victor reassured her that War belonged to the Empire's rarest class of military assets, possessing enough power to threaten entire galaxies, and explained that their supernatural connection would allow him to revive the Herald if he were killed. He also indicated that War would not truly be alone, although the nature of that assurance remained unexplained until the confrontation in the Upper Sectors. His deployment represented a deliberate act of Imperial retaliation rather than an unsanctioned pursuit of battle, and War understood that his objective was to execute the Emperor's judgment against the forces threatening his civilization.[1]
War's arrival brought him into direct conflict with [[Azrakar]], the Primordial God of the Void and one of the ten Chief Gods serving the [[Black Eclipse Pantheon]]. Azrakar was aboard a living vessel receiving reports concerning the infiltration of Victor's Sector through fragments of Divine Concepts. Although the fragments had crossed portions of the weakening barrier, Imperial defenders continued eliminating them before suitable hosts could be established. War remained concealed even after Azrakar sensed an abnormality and searched the surrounding Solar System through his spatial Divinity. When the Chief God demanded that the unidentified presence reveal itself, War attacked without warning, impaling Azrakar's assistant and destroying the living vessel along with its crew. Azrakar survived the destruction and responded by releasing supernatural Darkness across thousands of star systems, extinguishing the surrounding light as the confrontation escalated into direct combat.[2]
Their initial collision released enormous waves of Energy that devastated star systems and displaced planets from their orbits. Azrakar attempted to restrain War through the authority of his Void Divinity, imprisoning him within distorted regions of space where ordinary movement no longer produced corresponding changes in position. War responded by concentrating supernatural power through Gol-goroth and striking the affected space until the confinement shattered, immediately resuming his offensive. Azrakar retaliated with concentrated darkness and spatial attacks capable of hurling War through asteroid belts, planetary systems and even a star that disintegrated upon contact. Despite suffering visible injuries, War repeatedly regained his position and returned to the battle, demonstrating extraordinary durability and endurance against a Primordial God whose destructive capabilities threatened entire galaxies.[2]
As the confrontation intensified, their attacks began affecting neighboring Galactic structures, scattering stellar matter and causing widespread astronomical destruction. Azrakar attempted to prevent War from approaching by compressing spatial pathways, expanding darkness and erasing portions of the regions through which his opponent intended to move. War repeatedly broke through these effects with Gol-goroth, employing rapid movement, precise strikes and direct physical combat to reach the Primordial God. He hurled Azrakar through celestial bodies, struck him with both sword and fists, and demonstrated enough tactical understanding to exploit openings created by the Chief God's attempts to dominate the battlefield through raw Divine power. Although Azrakar remained extraordinarily dangerous, War showed little interest in his declarations of superiority and repeatedly answered lengthy speeches with attacks, eventually expressing open irritation at the opponent's excessive talking.[2]
The confrontation expanded when [[Kaelith]], Goddess of the Night and another Chief God of Black Eclipse, arrived to reinforce Azrakar. She believed that Victor had sent War into enemy territory without meaningful assistance, but War rejected the assumption and declared that his brothers and the Emperor remained with him. Gol-goroth responded by absorbing the Energy emanating from War, opening its gigantic eye and manifesting three Eldritch beings from his body. The newcomers were identified as Eldritch Famine incarnate, Eldritch Pestilence incarnate and Eldritch Death incarnate, representing the apocalyptic roles of War's deceased brothers in a newly manifested form. Their appearance effectively restored the Four Horsemen as an Eldritch combat group, although the original Demonic Souls and uninterrupted identities of the deceased brothers were not confirmed to have returned. The three manifestations possessed extraordinary independent combat capabilities and immediately joined the battle, preventing Kaelith from overwhelming War through numerical superiority.[2]
During the subsequent exchanges, War demonstrated the importance of Victor's martial teachings by overcoming complex Divine techniques through precision rather than wasteful expenditure of Energy. Azrakar compressed an enormous Void attack into a projectile whose supernatural properties distorted the surrounding space and threatened everything along its trajectory. Instead of attempting to overpower the attack directly, War adjusted the angle of Gol-goroth and deflected it toward distant star systems, where its destructive effects caused thousands of stars to collapse. He subsequently exploited openings in the Primordial God's defensive movements, forcing Azrakar to reconsider his reliance upon overwhelming quantities of Divine power. When Azrakar created a larger spatial prison supported by multiple convergence points, War analyzed the distribution of its distortions and recognized that severing the connection between two supporting points required considerably less power than destroying the entire structure. His targeted strike destabilized the prison and allowed him to break through, demonstrating extraordinary battlefield analysis and mastery of efficient martial combat.[14]
Meanwhile, the Eldritch incarnations of Famine, Pestilence and Death confronted Kaelith in coordinated formation. Death manifested an enormous sword of pure Miasma and wounded the Goddess, while Famine employed a concentrated Energy knife to pierce her abdomen and Pestilence used cutting attacks to sever her limbs. Kaelith regenerated through her Darkness, but the confrontation demonstrated that the manifested Horsemen possessed distinguishable fighting styles and could coordinate their attacks against a powerful Primordial opponent. Their actions should be distinguished from War's personal techniques, as the incarnations were separate combatants rather than manifestations of every ability being performed directly through War's body.[14]
The expanding devastation eventually attracted the attention of Equilibrium and the Judge of the Abyss, who recognized that continued fighting could destroy increasingly large portions of the Upper Sectors. The galaxies involved were vastly larger than those familiar to the lower regions and contained enormous populations whose deaths threatened to provoke direct intervention by the cosmic authorities. Before the situation escalated further, Darkness appeared within his own territory and used his Authority to stabilize the surrounding Solar Systems and galaxies, preventing additional uncontrolled destruction. He also agreed to surrender the Souls of those killed during the conflict to Soul, ensuring that the observing [[Primordial Entities]] would not intervene directly. The action was motivated by his desire to preserve control over his territory rather than sympathy toward War or the populations affected by the confrontation.[14]
Although Darkness's intervention restricted the environmental consequences of their attacks, War and Azrakar continued fighting. Gol-goroth detected an unfamiliar presence and warned War that another powerful Being had entered the battlefield, while War himself recognized the increased supernatural pressure surrounding the region. Kaelith subsequently joined Azrakar in a coordinated attempt to overwhelm him, employing her Night-based authority to interfere with his perception, spatial orientation and ability to distinguish real presences from deceptive manifestations. One of her attacks successfully wounded War's arm, demonstrating that his Eldritch nature did not grant unconditional immunity to such techniques. War adapted by examining the differences in the darkness surrounding her projections and modifying his defensive movements, while the three Eldritch Horsemen returned to support him after recognizing that the opposing Gods were coordinating their powers.[14]
The confrontation reached another turning point when two additional Chief Gods arrived. [[Nerath]], Goddess of Silence, appeared as an unnaturally elongated white Being whose presence suppressed sound, while [[Vhalor]], God of Shadows, possessed a distorted black body surrounded by supernatural shadows that appeared to draw matter away from ordinary space. Azrakar identified them as two of the ten Chief Gods serving Black Eclipse, bringing the number of opposing leaders present to four. Nerath revealed that the Great Silence wanted War captured alive, while Azrakar agreed that they would take him prisoner rather than execute him. War showed no sign of intimidation, declaring that the only consequence of their arrival was an increase in the number of heads he would bring to his Emperor. The confrontation continued as each Horseman faced increasingly dangerous combinations of the Chief Gods' supernatural authorities.[14]
The expanding confrontation increasingly favored Black Eclipse. Azrakar abandoned his reliance upon overwhelming destructive Energy and began cooperating with Kaelith's illusions, disrupting War's sense of distance and position while continuously rebuilding his spatial domain. Their first coordinated deception caused War to attack a false target and allowed Azrakar to strike his flank. War immediately counterattacked with Gol-goroth, wounding the God's arm. When the wound remained open, Azrakar reorganized his body to prevent it from deepening, revealing an opening that War recognized: the God needed to continually readjust his distortions to maintain their effects. War began striking during those intervals, forcing his opponent to choose between defending himself and controlling the battlefield.[4]
Azrakar then altered the points from which his attacks originated, opening spatial rifts around the Herald and directing strikes from apparently impossible angles. War parried one attack, avoided a second and was struck in the shoulder by a third, but closed the distance before Azrakar could exploit the injury. He punched the God in the torso and swung Gol-goroth, only for Azrakar to trap part of the blade within a spatial distortion. War released one hand from his weapon and punched the God in the face, interrupting the technique and retrieving the greatsword. He continued alternating broad swings and short attacks, denying Azrakar the distance required to establish another powerful spatial construction.[4]
Azrakar responded by deliberately leaving openings that could function as traps. War initially fell for the deception, allowing the God to catch Gol-goroth and strike him in the chest. Although he was thrown backward, he retained the sword and rapidly regained his footing. During the next attempt, War cut his movement short, changed the angle of his strike and severed the structure sustaining the distortion before it became effective. He followed this with repeated attacks against Azrakar's arms, torso and head, accumulating injuries that the God struggled to repair. Kaelith eventually exchanged Azrakar's position with one of her Night illusions to provide him with a brief opportunity to recover.[4]
The other Horsemen were also struggling. Famine and Death confronted Kaelith's increasingly substantial Night projections, which could deliver real attacks and restrain their targets. Although they pierced her compressed defensive barrier and temporarily interrupted her regeneration, the Goddess continued fighting. Pestilence was placed at a still greater disadvantage when Nerath's Silence suppressed his corruption and Vhalor intervened with Shadow restraints that bound his arms and legs. The combined interference of the two Gods forced him to divide his attention between escaping their bindings and restoring the Energy needed to attack. Meanwhile, the conflict between eight combatants capable of threatening galaxies had brought the transport of hostile Divine spheres into the Elderblood Empire to a complete halt, even as the possibility of further reinforcements threatened the Horsemen.[4]
War recognized that continuing the separate battles would lead toward defeat. Abandoning his pursuit of Azrakar, he raised Gol-goroth and summoned the other Horsemen with a single command. Famine, Pestilence and Death immediately answered him, vanished from their individual engagements and appeared at his side. As their powers gathered, four pillars of raw Energy erupted into the surrounding space. The resulting pressure erased nearby stars without conventional explosions despite the Great Darkness's continuing containment of the region. Gol-goroth's enormous eye opened and the weapon generated an isolated Eldritch domain, separating the battle from the ordinary conditions of the surrounding Universe.[4]
Within the domain, the normal laws of the Universe ceased applying in their previous form, and the changed conditions favored Eldritch beings. The dormant Eldritch aspects of the Horsemen awakened. Famine, Pestilence and Death developed faces devoid of features, pitch-black armor and wings of dark Energy that consumed the surrounding light. War had previously avoided openly displaying the deeper powers of his Eldritch Progenitor lineage out of fear that other beings would take notice, but his brothers' worsening situation left him unwilling to maintain that restraint. He transferred his own power to them and temporarily synchronized their senses with his, allowing the four to operate through a shared awareness and coordinated movements.[4]
The new environment deprived the Chief Gods of the battlefield advantages provided by their ordinary Divine authorities. Azrakar's Void, Kaelith's Night, Nerath's Silence and Vhalor's Shadows ceased functioning effectively against the Horsemen while the Eldritch domain governed the surrounding space. This interference did not demonstrate permanent destruction of the Gods' Divinities; it instead gave War and his brothers a decisive opportunity within the altered territory. War directed their combined attention toward the already injured Azrakar, whom Kaelith attempted to warn while Nerath and Vhalor tried to intervene.[4]
The Four Horsemen moved before the Chief Gods could protect Azrakar. Death immobilized him with Miasma, Pestilence weakened him and Famine severed his arms and legs. War then decapitated Azrakar and pierced his heart, personally completing the decisive weapon attacks against the God of the Void. All four Horsemen subsequently raised their hands and released a combined burst of Eldritch power that erased Azrakar from existence. The final erasure was a collective feat, while the decapitation and heart impalement were performed by War himself. The death of a Chief God prompted reactions throughout the Upper Sectors, and the Great Darkness ordered the Horsemen brought before him alive or dead. One of the [[Seven Black Thrones]] obeyed and summoned the remaining Chief Gods, ensuring that the Imperial offensive had gained a major victory without resolving the wider war.[4]

He advanced with Famine, Pestilence and Death and struck before the three Chief Gods could build a defensive formation. Vhalor was the first target. The God of Shadows tried to disperse his body and slip out of reach, but Famine cut the path off. His blades pierced the shadow realm and forced Vhalor to pull his manifestation back together. That interruption was enough for War to arrive.[15]

He spread his influence across the domain, calling shadows up around the knights, but the realm was already weakened and no longer touched them. Gol-goroth cut through what remained. Every attempt to escape opened another wound. When Vhalor finally vanished and reappeared at a distance, Death had already read the path. The scythe of pure Miasma struck him as he materialized, stopped the flight and pierced his abdomen.[15]

A compact barrier and a wave of energy shoved the two knights back long enough for the God to gather a little strength, and no longer than that. Pestilence had already seeded corruption through the region. Some of it reached the structures holding the barrier up. The defense lost stability, Gol-goroth broke through, and although Vhalor avoided the killing stroke the blade opened a deep wound across his upper body. Death closed in to finish him. The exchange felt long. It had lasted seconds.[15]

They drew on their sovereign as far as that power would go. A pillar of darkness rose through both of them. It was only enough to dull Gol-goroth's influence, but the opening let them join Night and Silence, pull Vhalor out of Death's reach, and raise a small field with him that tried to resist the Eldritch conditions around them.[15]

The foreign energy was difficult to regenerate. Famine used the opening and tore a large hole through her. She understood the intention at once: the knights were trying to peel her away from the other two, and distance from them meant death. She fell back toward Nerath instead of running.[15]
Nerath's Silence still interrupted some of Pestilence's manifestations, but corruption was already sitting at many points across the field. Removing one concentration only exposed another. Pestilence crossed the Silence before his power could be fully cut off, contaminated one of her floating structures, and forced her to sever it. A concentrated discharge then deformed her upper body. Death and Famine arrived with the wounded Kaelith while Pestilence prepared another discharge, and Gol-goroth's influence continued to refuse Nerath's laws. She could not clear the field and rescue her allies at the same time.[15]

Vhalor answered that the incarnation of War was the key, and that a counterattack had to be aimed at him or they would die. The balance had reversed. Vhalor was spending energy just to keep his manifestation from coming apart. Kaelith had lost her illusions and was carrying cuts that slowed her regeneration. Nerath could no longer hold Silence over a wide area without stripping her own defense.[15]
War broke through space with Gol-goroth and appeared in the middle of them. His presence pushed their attempted field down further. Famine shattered Vhalor's shadow defense before it finished forming. Death intercepted Kaelith when she moved to help. Pestilence pinned the space around Nerath. The counterattack failed, and each God was driven into fighting alone against opponents who acted as one body.[15]

Death answered that their patron's energy was keeping them alive, and that something else was already approaching. War drove Gol-goroth through Vhalor's manifestation and dropped him to his knees. Nerath, watching the same bodies occupy different places from one instant to the next, concluded that the four were not separate fighters at all. Her perception had come apart. Without her patron she would already have been dead. The chaotic battle was the condition Eldritch War was built for, and the sword Victor had given War was made to drive that condition to its limit.[15]

Pure darkness burst out of her body and drove War back. It did not make the interference less effective.[15]
War turned back to the three. Vhalor was still on the ground trying to reassemble himself. Kaelith could not rebuild her illusions. Nerath could barely remain upright after the outburst. War raised Gol-goroth against Vhalor. Death set the scythe for Kaelith. Famine closed her escape. Pestilence gathered the last corruption on Nerath. None of the three still had a stable defense. Beings of equal rank were not easy to finish unless their caution slipped, as Azrakar's had. Once the God of the Void was gone, the rest had become wary. ### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is War's sovereign, creator and acknowledged father. Their relationship began as that of enemies during the Demonic War, when War confronted Victor after the destruction of the other Horsemen and ultimately achieved natural Demon Godhood through the extraordinary pressure of their battle. Despite defeating War and possessing the opportunity to consume him, Victor recognized his exceptional determination and warrior's pride and chose to spare his life. That decision established a foundation of mutual respect which subsequently developed into a profound personal bond. Victor later cultivated the reborn warrior's potential and reconstructed him into a [[Chaos]] Progenitor, restoring his memories and granting him the foundation of an entirely new supernatural lineage. War consequently regards Victor as his King, Father and Creator, considering the fulfillment of his will the highest purpose of his existence.[7][5][10]
Victor likewise demonstrates extraordinary trust in War, entrusting him with sensitive assignments involving the protection of the Imperial family, investigation of foreign supernatural interference, execution of judgments and direct military operations against powerful enemies. Their relationship extends beyond that of an Emperor and subordinate because War's reconstructed existence incorporates a fragment of Victor's Essence, establishing a supernatural connection through which he can function as an extension of the Emperor's perception and influence. Victor considers War one of his most valuable servants, recognizing that his combination of personal strength, judgment, emotional stability and genuine devotion cannot easily be reproduced through artificial creation. The Emperor's willingness to send him into the Upper Sectors alone also reflects his confidence in War's abilities and their connection, including his intention to revive the Herald if he were killed during the mission.[13][29][1]

#### Diablo and the Original Horsemen
[[Diablo]] was War's original sovereign and the King of [[Biblical Hell]] under whom the Four Horsemen served. War regarded his commands as absolute obligations, participating in military operations, carrying out executions and providing intelligence concerning threats to the Demonic Army. Although he possessed independent tactical judgment, his original loyalty remained directed toward Diablo until the destruction of the Four Horsemen and his subsequent rebirth. His later relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] differed fundamentally because it combined allegiance with genuine gratitude, familial attachment and recognition of the individual who had created his new existence.[18][5]
Death, Famine and Pestilence were War's original brothers and fellow members of the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse. Their relationship extended beyond ordinary military cooperation, as they shared an extraordinary history of service, possessed complementary supernatural specializations and had even developed a private language with their mounts as a joke. Death cooperated directly with War during the assassination of Pope Alexander, while all three later fought beside him against Victor. Their eventual defeat and consumption caused War genuine grief, although he refused to deny the legitimacy of Victor's victory. The supernatural characteristics of his brothers were subsequently incorporated into War's [[Chaos]] Progenitor reconstruction, while Eldritch incarnations corresponding to their apocalyptic roles later manifested through [[Gol-goroth]] during the Upper-Sector conflict. These manifestations represented the return of the Horsemen's roles in a new form rather than confirmed restoration of their original Demonic Souls.[21][7][5][2]
Their connection became particularly apparent when War summoned the Eldritch incarnations back from their separate battles and ordered them to fight together. They immediately answered and regrouped, while War transferred his power to them and temporarily synchronized their senses with his. Together they immobilized, dismembered and executed [[Azrakar]] before erasing the God of the Void through a shared burst of Eldritch power. Their new unity established a close combat bond without confirming the restoration of the original Horsemen's former Souls or uninterrupted identities.[4]

#### War's Horse
War's supernatural horse is one of his oldest and most loyal companions, having accompanied him throughout his original existence as a Horseman of the Apocalypse. Unlike ordinary mounts, the horse possesses extraordinary intelligence, supernatural abilities and a longstanding relationship with its master. Its loyalty became particularly significant following War's defeat by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], when it recovered his devastated body and carried him toward the region where his existence could be preserved. The horse later underwent transformation alongside War, acquiring a stronger supernatural body and violet Dragon Fire in place of its original Hellfire while retaining the intimate connection established during their previous lives. Their relationship survived both War's memory loss and his reconstruction into a [[Chaos]] Progenitor.[7][5]

#### Heralds of Chaos
The [[Heralds of Chaos]] are the supernatural lineage originating from War's transformation into a [[Chaos]] Progenitor. As their original source, War possesses authority over their creation, development and organization, while their purpose is closely connected to the execution of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s will and the protection of the Empire. Jimmy, Thomas, Lucy and Karen were among the earliest individuals transformed into Heralds, receiving new supernatural bodies and characteristics rather than merely being recruited as ordinary soldiers. War supervised their conversion and subsequently directed the expansion of the organization into an elite Imperial institution. Although the Heralds possess extraordinary devotion toward Victor, they retain individual identities and responsibilities, operating as investigators, protectors, intelligence agents and specialized combatants beneath War's leadership.[10][11][27]

#### Members of the Elderblood Family
War maintains a protective relationship toward the members of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family, regarding threats against them as direct offenses against the Emperor. He has cooperated with Nyx Elderblood during operations involving dangerous Draconic beings and personally protected [[Hela Elderblood]] from the supernatural consequences of perceiving Victor's unrestricted Eldritch existence. His interactions with [[Roxanne Elderblood]] are also significant because she recognized the nature of his [[Chaos]] Progenitor transformation and understood that his loyalty would remain unchanged regardless of his eventual development. War's position within the [[Elderblood Empire]] is consequently not limited to military service, as he functions as one of the most reliable guardians and executors available to the Imperial household.[10][25][20]

#### The Heavenly Father
The [[Heavenly Father]] witnessed War's reconstruction during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s visit to Heaven and initially reacted with astonishment at the contradictory supernatural characteristics produced by the transformation. The former Demon had acquired genuine Holy properties while retaining a body visibly associated with Darkness, challenging the conventional distinctions between Angels, Demons and and other supernatural species. The Heavenly Father's initial interpretation of War as an unnatural existence provoked Victor's opposition, as the Dragon God refused to accept the assumption that Creation's established classifications should determine whether his newly created son deserved to exist. Their disagreement highlighted the extraordinary significance of War's transformation and Victor's developing ability to create supernatural lineages beyond conventional limitations.[5][10]

#### Kurama and Nikolai Francis Nichev
[[Kurama]] and [[Nikolai Francis Nichev]] represent two different aspects of War's responsibilities as the Emperor's executor. Kurama was among the individuals subjected to Imperial judgment because of his involvement in harming one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters, prompting War to impose an extraordinary punishment rather than conventional execution. Nikolai, in contrast, was a [[Demigod]] corrupted by foreign supernatural powers who attempted to obtain information concerning Victor's Bloodline. War personally investigated his activities, compelled him to disclose his patron's objective and eliminated the compromised threat while preserving the surrounding Imperial infrastructure. Both cases demonstrate War's willingness to act decisively against individuals who endanger the Emperor's family or betray the Empire in favor of foreign powers.[11][13]

#### Gol-goroth
[[Gol-goroth]] is the sentient Eldritch greatsword created by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and entrusted to War immediately before the Upper-Sector offensive. The weapon recognized Victor as Great [[Chaos]] and briefly integrated with War upon being accepted, granting him additional powers associated with chaotic Eldritch War and the personification of warfare. Gol-goroth possesses independent awareness and can communicate with its wielder, including warning War when unfamiliar supernatural influences enter the battlefield. Its most extraordinary demonstrated capability is the manifestation of three Eldritch incarnations corresponding to Famine, Pestilence and Death, effectively allowing War to fight alongside beings embodying the roles of his deceased brothers. The sword's unpredictable nature makes it particularly suitable for solitary combat against large enemy forces, and War has demonstrated exceptional proficiency in combining its powers with his own martial techniques.[1][2][14]
Gol-goroth's deeper function emerged when War reunited the Horsemen. The weapon opened its eye and established an isolated Eldritch domain whose conditions favored its wielder and the other Eldritch combatants while interfering with the opposing Gods' authority over the battlefield. War subsequently shared power and senses with his brothers and wielded Gol-goroth during the execution of [[Azrakar]]. The sword's domain was essential to the coordinated victory, but the God of the Void's final erasure was accomplished by the four Horsemen together rather than the weapon acting independently.[4]

#### Black Eclipse Pantheon
War's relationship with the [[Black Eclipse Pantheon]] is defined by his assignment to retaliate against the foreign factions threatening the [[Elderblood Empire]]. [[Azrakar]], Primordial God of the Void, became his first major opponent in the Upper Sectors, engaging him in an extended battle involving spatial imprisonment, destructive darkness and enormous astronomical devastation. War repeatedly overcame particular applications of Azrakar's Void powers through concentrated strikes and precise martial analysis, but the Primordial God remained capable of damaging him and interrupting his movements. [[Kaelith]], Goddess of the Night, subsequently intervened and employed perception-distorting manifestations that successfully wounded War before he adapted to her techniques. Their confrontation demonstrated that the Chief Gods possessed extraordinary abilities even against an Eldritch-empowered [[Chaos]] Progenitor.[2][14]
The arrival of [[Nerath]], Goddess of Silence, and [[Vhalor]], God of Shadows, escalated the encounter by bringing the number of opposing Chief Gods to four. Nerath conveyed the Great Silence's order to capture War alive, while Azrakar accepted the directive. As the Gods gained the advantage, War recalled the Eldritch Horsemen and revealed their deeper powers through [[Gol-goroth]]'s isolated domain. He personally decapitated Azrakar and pierced his heart after Death, Pestilence and Famine immobilized, weakened and dismembered the God; the four then erased Azrakar from existence together. Kaelith, Nerath and Vhalor survived that engagement, while the [[Vharzath|Great Darkness]] ordered further reinforcements and demanded the capture or elimination of War and his brothers.[14][4]
War did not give them the pause. He, Famine, Death and Pestilence drove the three to the edge of erasure inside the Eldritch domain. The Great Darkness kept Nerath alive by force, and [[Vaelthar]] arrived with the rest of the Chief Gods before the executions landed. Vaelthar restored the three and rebuked them for disappointing Darkness. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s arrival ended Kaelith: the Emperor erased her body and took her Soul. Nerath was still there when the projection cut the Gods in two. Vhalor does not appear in that fight. Vaelthar spent the restraints, the chest blow, and the domain prison on the projection and ended under its foot. War did not take those blows. He left with the projection and the fallen Gods when the living were ordered taken prisoner. The duel is no longer his alone.[15][16][17]

#### Darkness
Darkness is the higher-sector sovereign whose territory encompassed the galaxies devastated during War's confrontation with Black Eclipse. The escalating destruction attracted the attention of Equilibrium and the Judge of the Abyss, prompting Darkness to intervene and stabilize the surrounding astronomical structures through his Authority. He also surrendered the Souls of those killed during the conflict to Soul, preventing direct intervention by the observing [[Primordial Entities]]. His actions were motivated by the preservation of his own territory and the avoidance of further cosmic interference rather than direct allegiance to War or [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. [[Gol-goroth]] detected his arrival and warned War of an unfamiliar presence, establishing that the higher-sector sovereign was observing the conflict without personally entering the battle against the Herald.[14]
Even after the Great Darkness contained the astronomical destruction, the raw Energy released by War and the other Horsemen erased several nearby stars. [[Azrakar]]'s subsequent death provoked the sovereign to order that all four Horsemen be brought before him alive or dead, and a member of the [[Seven Black Thrones]] summoned the remaining Chief Gods. Darkness thus became directly responsible for escalating the military response to War's victory, although the two had not personally fought one another.[4]

### Current Status
War remains alive. He is still the Emperor's forward Herald in the Upper Sectors, with [[Gol-goroth]] and the Eldritch incarnations of Death, Famine and Pestilence beside him. He decapitated and heart-pierced [[Azrakar]], then erased him together with his brothers, and afterward drove [[Kaelith]], [[Nerath]] and [[Vhalor]] to the point of execution inside Gol-goroth's domain. Their patron kept them alive. [[Vaelthar]], Throne of Origin, broke the domain with the remaining Chief Gods, and War shut the domain down rather than hold it against a leader he judged stronger than himself, with no escape and only fresh enemies around him.[4][15][16]
He refused to be handed to Justice, to [[Elarion]], or to Black Eclipse. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] arrived in answer, praised him, killed Kaelith by striking her Soul, and named [[Vharzath]] for extinction while more than twenty Throne-level fighters, all women, struck Black Eclipse's worlds from every side. The attack Vaelthar then ordered was spent on a projection. War did not swing those cuts. He left with that projection and with the fallen Gods. Gol-goroth went with him. The opening duel is over. The war around War is not.[16][17]

#### Powers & Abilities

War is a former Demon of the highest rank, the sole surviving member of the original Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse, a naturally ascended [[Demon God]], and the Progenitor of the [[Heralds of Chaos]]. His supernatural abilities have undergone several fundamental transformations, beginning with his original Hell-Fire Demon physiology, progressing through the Divine characteristics acquired during his battle against Victor Elderblood, and culminating in his reconstruction as a [[Chaos]] Progenitor directly connected to Victor's Essence.[1][2][3][4]
Unlike ordinary Demons whose nature is primarily associated with Negative Energy, War possesses a reconstructed existence incorporating Positive Energy, Negative Energy, Dragon Fire, the characteristics of all four original Horsemen, and a fragment of Victor's Essence. These elements were united through Victor's authority over Beginning, creating a genuine Progenitor whose composition does not conform to ordinary divisions between Positive and Negative supernatural beings.[3][4]
Following this transformation, War became one of the most powerful servants of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. His abilities include exceptional physical characteristics, supernatural perception, direct interaction with minds and Souls, authority over his own Progenitor lineage, dimensional movement, Chaos-based concealment and an overwhelming presence capable of affecting the minds of powerful supernatural beings. His connection to Victor also allows him to act as a direct executor of the Emperor's will.[5][6][7]
During the later Imperial Era, War was recognized as the strongest Herald and one of the rare Imperial combatants capable of threatening entire galaxies. Victor further increased his combat potential by entrusting him with the sentient greatsword [[Gol-goroth]], granting additional powers associated with chaotic Eldritch War. His subsequent battle against [[Azrakar]], Primordial God of the Void, demonstrated extraordinary resistance to spatial confinement, high-speed combat across astronomical distances, and the ability to overcome complex Divine techniques through precise martial application rather than relying exclusively upon greater destructive output. In the battle's final phase, War and the three Eldritch Horsemen combined their powers to kill and erase Azrakar.[8][9][10][11][12]
War's present abilities must be distinguished from those belonging to Victor, Gol-goroth and the three Eldritch incarnations of the other Horsemen. Victor's Divinity of Beginning was used to create War's current existence, but War has not independently demonstrated unrestricted mastery of Beginning. Gol-goroth supplies additional war-related powers and enables the manifestation of other combatants, whose individual techniques are not automatically abilities performed by War's own body.[3][10][11]
Against four Chief Gods of the [[Black Eclipse Pantheon]], War finally revealed the deeper Eldritch power of his lineage. He recalled the other Horsemen, shared his strength with them and temporarily synchronized their senses, while Gol-goroth created an isolated Eldritch domain in which the ordinary laws of the surrounding Universe no longer operated normally. Their combined assault suppressed the Chief Gods' influence over the battlefield and erased Azrakar from existence. The domain belongs to Gol-goroth, the sensory synchronization and power transfer to War, and the final erasure to the four Horsemen acting together; none should be described as a separate unlimited power possessed by War alone.[8]
The same state continued after that erasure. War did not let [[Kaelith]], [[Nerath]] and [[Vhalor]] reform. Inside the domain their Night, Silence and Shadows stayed weakened, the four Horsemen kept exchanging places until the Gods could no longer tell them apart, and War broke through space with Gol-goroth to strike from inside their formation. The three were driven to the point of execution. They were not finished. Their patron's energy kept them alive, and a direct infusion from the [[Vharzath|Great Darkness]] forced War back. When [[Vaelthar]] strained the barrier, Gol-goroth held it through one wave and began to fail on the second. War then shut the domain down. None of this is an unlimited power belonging to War's body alone.[13][14]

#### Current Physiology

##### Chaos Progenitor Physiology
War's present existence originated from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s reconstruction of his former Demonic and Divine nature into an entirely new supernatural lineage. [[Roxanne Elderblood]] recognized the result as the creation of a genuine Progenitor rather than an ordinary racial enhancement. War possesses the defining characteristics of the [[Heralds of Chaos]] and can produce additional individuals belonging to his species.[3][4]
- Progenitor Physiology: War possesses the supernatural characteristics of a Progenitor, functioning as the original source of the Heralds of Chaos and possessing the authority to produce additional members of the race.[4][6]
- [[Chaos]] Being Physiology: His reconstructed existence combines characteristics associated with Darkness, Light, Positive Energy, Negative Energy, Divine power and Victor's Chaos Essence, preventing him from being classified exclusively as an ordinary Demon, Angel or God.[3][4]
- Eldritch Physiology: War possesses an Eldritch component originating from Victor's Essence. This contributes to his exceptional supernatural characteristics and the existential pressure produced by his presence.[7]
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics: War possesses extraordinary physical strength, speed, durability, agility, reflexes and stamina, allowing him to confront exceptionally powerful supernatural opponents.[1][11]
- Superhuman Strength: War possesses enormous physical power and can overwhelm powerful opponents through direct physical attacks, including strikes capable of hurling [[Azrakar]] across astronomical distances.[11]
- Superhuman Speed: War can move and attack at extraordinary speeds, including traveling faster than light before his Upper-Sector deployment and maintaining combat mobility across immense distances.[10][11]
- Enhanced Reflexes: His reaction speed allows him to respond to rapid Divine attacks, spatial distortions and changes in an opponent's movements.[11][12]
- Enhanced Agility: Despite his enormous physique, War maintains exceptional coordination and mobility, allowing precise movements during extremely high-speed combat.[1][12]
- Superhuman Durability: War survives devastating physical and supernatural attacks, including being hurled through stars and planetary bodies during his confrontation with Azrakar.[11]
- Enhanced Stamina: War can continue fighting despite prolonged exertion, severe injuries and the enormous Energy demands associated with battles against high-ranking Gods.[2][11]
- Regenerative Healing Factor: War possessed advanced Demonic regeneration before ascending into Godhood. His natural ascension subsequently restored severe injuries and strengthened his damaged existence.[1]
- Enhanced Soul: War's Soul was strengthened through his ascension and later reconstruction into a Chaos Progenitor.[1][3]
- [[Immortality]]: War possesses supernatural longevity and an existence capable of surviving beyond ordinary biological limitations. Victor also indicated that War could return to him and be revived if killed, but this protection depends upon his connection to Victor rather than a demonstrated independent resurrection technique.[10]
- Enhanced Senses: War possesses extraordinary supernatural perception, allowing him to examine powerful presences, spiritual characteristics and phenomena inaccessible to ordinary senses.[5][7]
- Flight: War can fly through the six supernatural wings developed during his Chaos Progenitor transformation.[3]
- Faster-than-Light Flight: War transformed into a comet of Darkness that consumed surrounding light and traveled toward the Sector barrier faster than light. Victor created the portal through which War entered the Upper Sectors.[10]
- Darkness Manifestation: War can emerge from supernatural Darkness and cloak his movement within it, as demonstrated immediately before his deployment into the Upper Sectors.[10]
- Darkness-Based Body: War's current body possesses characteristics of supernatural Darkness, including a face appearing as a featureless absence of recognizable matter.[3]
- Six Wings of Darkness: War possesses six wings composed primarily of dark supernatural Energy, accompanied by genuine Holy characteristics and a faint white aura.[3][4]
- Supernatural Adaptation: War demonstrates the ability to adjust and improve his combat characteristics when confronting superior opponents.[1]
- Accelerated Combat Development: During his battle with Victor, War's strength, reactions and martial techniques improved under extreme combat pressure.[1]

Gol-goroth is the source of the domain; War is the wielder who commands and coordinates its beneficiaries.[8]
- Eldritch Domain Creation: The sword produces a bounded Eldritch environment that isolates the battlefield from its former supernatural conditions.[8]
- Localized Law Override: Within the created region, the surrounding Universe's ordinary laws cease to apply in their usual way, favoring the Eldritch Horsemen.[8]
- Divine Authority Suppression (Local): The domain defeats the existing battlefield effects of the Chief Gods' Void, Silence and Shadow authorities without establishing the permanent destruction of those Divinities.[8]
- Eldritch Aspect Activation: The Sword's deeper ability allows the Horsemen's previously dormant Eldritch traits to awaken.[8]
- Eldritch Appearance Transformation (Manifested Brothers): During the activation, the other Horsemen become increasingly faceless, their armor turns pitch-black, and dark Energy wings emerge from their backs. These physical changes are described for the brothers, not independently for War.[8]
- Collective Energy Release: Its activation accompanies four pillars of power whose pressure erases nearby stars. The destructive effect is attributable to the four Horsemen's combined release rather than a standalone sword beam.[8]
- Eldritch Power Combination: Gol-goroth's domain provides the environment in which War's power transfer and sensory synchronization allow all four incarnations to coordinate their attack.[8]
- Collective Existence Erasure: After War decapitates and impales [[Azrakar]], all four Horsemen release a combined burst of Eldritch power that erases the God. The domain enables their advantage, but the actual erasure is performed by the Horsemen together.[8]
- Vertical Structure Break: Gol-goroth comes down through a raised shadow structure, holds against it for a moment, then shatters it.[13]
- Failed-Realm Cutting: The sword tears through a shadow realm that has already stopped affecting the knights.[13]
- Space Breakthrough: War and Gol-goroth break through space and emerge inside the enemy formation. His influence then spreads and weakens the small field the Gods had raised.[13]
- Manifestation Pierce: The sword is driven through [[Vhalor]]'s manifestation, aborting his technique and dropping him to his knees.[13]
- Chaotic-Battle Amplification: The weapon pushes Eldritch War's advantage in a chaotic fight to its limit. The Gods lose the ability to track which knight is which.[13]
- Law Resistance inside the Domain: [[Nerath]]'s attempt to expand Silence and push the knights back fails because Gol-goroth's influence refuses her laws. This is local to the domain, not the erasure of Silence as a Divinity.[13]
- External-Pressure Hold: When reinforcement pressure strikes, the sword extends its influence to keep the barrier up. It does not survive the second wave intact.[13]
- Wielder Deactivation: War shuts the domain down when [[Vaelthar]]'s presence strains it and the field is no longer worth holding.[14]
