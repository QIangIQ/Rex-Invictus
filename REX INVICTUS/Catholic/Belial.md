### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 247
Belial is a supporting antagonist in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]]. He is an ancient Demon, the 68th Key of Solomon and one of the 72 Dukes of Hell serving the Primordial Demon [[Diablo]]. A powerful but notoriously disreputable noble of Hell, Belial is best known for his excessive lust, arrogance, obsession with [[Aphrodite]] and bitter rivalry with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] Alucard.[1][2]
His conflict with Victor began when he insulted [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett|Scathach Scarlett]] during their first encounter at Aphrodite's residence. Victor immediately destroyed the Human body Belial was possessing, humiliating the Duke without killing his original Demonic body. Their hostility subsequently escalated through Aphrodite's curse, the death of Belial's son, an attack upon his territory and Victor's attempt to frame [[Beelzebub]] for the destruction.[3][4]
Despite these incidents, Belial survived and continued serving Diablo. He was still alive during the opening of the Demonic War, directly reporting to Diablo in Chapter 521. His individual fate after the fall of Diablo's regime has not been conclusively established by the identified appearances.[5]

### Physical Appearance
Belial possesses several appearances because powerful Demons cannot freely transport their original bodies into the Human World. Instead, he operates through Human vessels connected to souls that he has obtained or taken under his control.[6]
In his Human-like form, Belial initially appeared as an adult man wearing a formal suit when he visited [[Aphrodite]]. Following the destruction of that body, he obtained another Human vessel, demonstrating that the loss of a possessed body did not permanently restrict his ability to operate outside Hell. One of his subsequent appearances involved a tall, muscular male body with artificially blond hair.[1][3]
His True Form is substantially more monstrous. Belial is depicted as an enormous, horned and winged Demon with an irregular body resembling a combination of several different creatures. His anatomy has a distinctly chimeric appearance rather than that of a conventional humanoid Demon. This is the body associated with his existence in Hell, and it should not be confused with the comparatively ordinary Human vessels used during his excursions to Earth.[2]

### Personality
Belial is arrogant, vindictive, lustful and intensely conscious of his position within Hell's hierarchy. He believes that his rank as a Duke entitles him to admiration, obedience and privileges that lesser beings should never question. This attitude is demonstrated during his first meeting with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], when he attempts to use his noble title as proof of his importance despite Victor showing virtually no interest in his identity. Belial's confidence is particularly pronounced around those he considers weaker, although he becomes considerably more cautious in the presence of authorities capable of punishing him, such as [[Diablo]] and [[Bael]].[1][7]
His most notorious characteristic is his obsession with sexual conquest. Belial pursues women regardless of their interest in him, treating rejection as an inconvenience rather than a reason to withdraw. His reputation is so infamous that other supernatural beings compare him to [[Zeus]], the King of [[Olympus]], and refer to him as the latter's twin because of their similarly scandalous behavior. Belial is also associated with a collection of photographs documenting his conquests, reinforcing his tendency to regard relationships as trophies of status rather than personal attachments.[2]
This mentality drives his obsession with [[Aphrodite]]. Belial considers the Goddess of Love and Beauty a suitable queen for a Demon of his supposed importance, and her legendary reputation makes him even more determined to possess her. However, Aphrodite repeatedly and explicitly rejects him, explaining that she finds Demonic Energy repulsive and has no intention of becoming his wife. Belial nevertheless continues pursuing her, interpreting even ordinary expressions and gestures as encouragement whenever doing so supports his desires.[1]
Although he frequently behaves foolishly, Belial is not entirely incapable of understanding danger. He recognizes the overwhelming authority of Diablo and understands that disobedience to the Demon King can result in execution. His survival within Hell's political system therefore depends on his willingness to submit to genuinely superior Demons even while displaying excessive arrogance toward outsiders.[7]
Belial is also extraordinarily spiteful. When Victor humiliates him, kills his heir and indirectly causes Aphrodite to curse him, Belial becomes determined to retaliate against someone Victor values rather than limit the conflict to the two of them. His willingness to target family members demonstrates that his cruelty extends beyond insults and personal grudges.[4][2]

### Synopsis

#### A New Pillar
Belial's involvement in the story begins during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s prolonged separation from [[Nightingale]]. While investigating the disappearance of access to the Vampire World, Victor encounters Demonic activity connected to Belial's faction. Members of the Demonic forces operating around Victor's territory become involved in the developing conflict, establishing the foundation for the hostility that later erupts between their respective masters.[8]
Victor subsequently visits the residence of [[Aphrodite]] in search of information about Nightingale. Upon arriving, he discovers Belial pestering the Goddess to accept his marriage proposal. Aphrodite has already rejected the Duke countless times and makes no attempt to conceal her irritation. Belial insists upon his status as one of Hell's 72 Dukes, believing that his position should make him a desirable husband, but Aphrodite remains unmoved.[1]
When Victor introduces himself as Alucard, Belial similarly announces his identity and title. Victor pays little attention to him, remaining focused on the information he came to obtain. Belial's behavior then crosses a line when he makes an offensive remark concerning [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett|Scathach Scarlett]], Victor's teacher. Victor immediately attacks, moving with sufficient speed to catch the Demon off guard and using lightning-enhanced Blood attacks to destroy the body Belial is possessing.[1]
The encounter leaves Belial humiliated. Although the physical destruction of his Human vessel does not kill his original Demonic body in Hell, the connection he maintained through the possessed soul is severed. He is unable to simply reestablish control over the destroyed vessel and must resort to another Human body to continue his activities on Earth.[1]
Aphrodite is surprised by Victor's response, particularly his willingness to attack an established Demon Duke over an insult without first considering the possible political consequences. She nevertheless continues her discussion with Victor and helps him investigate what happened to Nightingale.[1][3]
After learning that Vlad had sealed Nightingale and altered its temporal relationship with the outside world, Victor prepares to depart. He encounters Belial again and deliberately exploits the Demon's obsession with Aphrodite. Rather than simply provoking another fight, Victor encourages Belial to believe that Aphrodite secretly possesses feelings for him and that his continued pursuit might eventually succeed. Victor uses his supernatural influence and carefully chosen words to reinforce the deception, knowing that the Goddess has absolutely no interest in the Duke.[3]
Belial consequently continues harassing Aphrodite. His behavior eventually exhausts her patience, and she curses him with impotence, depriving him of the very sexual capability around which much of his pride and identity revolve. Belial considers the curse a devastating humiliation and blames Alucard for engineering the circumstances that caused it.[3][4]
During the approximately eighteen months Victor spends separated from most of his wives, their conflict develops further. Victor repeatedly intervenes against hostile supernatural forces and criminal activity while operating alongside his growing [[Maid Squad]]. One incident involves a young girl whom Victor protects from Demonic activity associated with Belial's interests. The girl is later identifiable through Victor's earlier involvement in [[Nero]]'s circumstances, which becomes important when her past is revisited.[4][9]
Victor subsequently investigates the Demon Duke's connections. With assistance from Witches knowledgeable about Demons, he tracks down Belial's son and heir and kills him. Rather than allowing the Duke to remain unaware of his loss, Victor preserves the severed head as evidence of what he has done and as a means of deliberately provoking Belial.[4]
Their confrontation escalates when Belial communicates through a lesser Demon subordinate operating in Victor's vicinity. Through this intermediary, Belial expresses his outrage over the death of his son and the curse imposed by Aphrodite. His fury and Demonic influence place enormous pressure upon the subordinate acting as his representative.[4]
Victor remains largely indifferent to Belial's anger. He presents the head of the Duke's son, ensuring that Belial understands the extent of his losses, before eliminating the subordinate. Rather than ending the matter there, Victor decides to bring the conflict directly into Hell.[4]
Using the remains of the defeated Demon and dangerous supernatural materials in his possession, Victor constructs a compressed explosive weapon capable of devastating an enormous area. He then arranges for a portal to open above Belial's castle, allowing the destructive object to be transported into the Duke's territory without Victor personally entering Hell.[4]
Victor's final act is deliberately deceptive. Immediately before launching the attack, he impersonates [[Beelzebub]], another major Demon, and declares war against Belial while inventing an accusation involving Beelzebub's wife. The false declaration is intended to make Belial believe the strike originated from another influential member of Hell's hierarchy, potentially creating internal conflict between Demonic factions.[4]
The explosive weapon passes through the portal and detonates above Belial's territory. What began as a dispute over Aphrodite and an insult directed at [[Scathach]] has now escalated into the destruction of a Demon Duke's holdings and a deliberate attempt to manipulate Hell's political relationships.[4]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
The narrative subsequently shifts to Hell, where Belial is shown raging over the devastation caused by the explosion. The attack has destroyed his city and inflicted extensive damage upon his territory. Despite its destructive power, Belial himself survives, and his hatred toward [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] becomes even more intense.[2]
Belial's attendants attempt to manage the consequences of the disaster. He considers how to retaliate against Alucard, including striking someone close to the Vampire rather than confronting him directly. His choices are nevertheless constrained by the restrictions governing travel between Hell and the Human World.[2]
Unlike lesser supernatural beings that can cross between realms more easily, powerful Demons cannot necessarily leave Hell in their true bodies whenever they desire. Belial must rely upon available Human vessels, subordinate Demons and the limited means of establishing connections between dimensions. Obtaining another suitable Human soul is not entirely straightforward, since the strongest Demons receive priority when such resources become available.[2][6]
Belial also cannot freely deploy every subordinate under his authority without considering [[Diablo]]'s commands. Although he is a Duke possessing his own followers and territory, he remains a member of Diablo's faction and is therefore subject to the Demon King's authority.[2]
The staged declaration of war causes confusion within Hell. [[Beelzebub]] and Belial are not supposed to be openly fighting one another, and the accusation involving Beelzebub's family encourages speculation about Belial's notorious personal affairs. Rumors surrounding the Duke's relationships make Victor's fabricated motive more plausible than it otherwise would have been.[2]
However, the deception does not escape the attention of Hell's highest authorities. Diablo realizes that the supposed declaration of war does not properly match Beelzebub's manner of speaking and behavior. Suspecting outside interference, he orders [[Bael]], one of his most trusted subordinates, to investigate the incident.[6]
Belial's predicament becomes more serious when Bael convenes a meeting to discuss the incidents involving his faction. Bael questions the Duke on Diablo's behalf, making it clear that cooperation is not optional. Belial understands that the Demon King's orders possess absolute priority and that refusal to answer could result in his own destruction.[7]
The meeting reveals the difference between the appearance of freedom enjoyed by Hell's nobles and the actual conditions under which Diablo rules them. Dukes such as Belial possess armies, servants and political influence, yet none of those advantages protect them if they openly disobey their master. The same gathering also takes place against the background of Diablo's growing preparations for war, making the dispute surrounding Belial one concern within a much larger Demonic strategy.[7]

#### Demonic War
When [[Diablo]] begins the invasion that develops into the Demonic War, Belial is still alive and serving his original master. His continued participation confirms that neither [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s initial destruction of his Human vessel nor the explosion over his city had killed him.[5]
Following Diablo's appearance at the gathering of supernatural leaders, the Demon King returns to Earth and arrives in the ruins of Moscow. Belial approaches and kneels before him, addressing him as his master. When Diablo requests an update on their operations, Belial confirms that the plans are progressing without interference.[5]
Belial is also present when representatives associated with [[The Judges of The Abyss]] appear before Diablo. These beings are connected to an arrangement involving approximately two billion souls, illustrating the enormous supernatural transactions underlying the invasion. Although Belial occupies an important position within Diablo's forces, he remains subordinate to the Primordial Demon and follows the exchange without attempting to interfere.[5]
After the representatives depart, Diablo issues further instructions. Belial is directed to reinforce the restrictions imposed upon Lilith, whose will has been compromised, and to coordinate the movement of Demonic forces. Diablo specifically changes his initial deployment plans and orders troops associated with Lilith's faction to be sent toward Samar, while continuing the wider operations intended to distract other supernatural powers.[5]
Belial accepts the instructions and continues functioning as one of Diablo's operational subordinates. His role during this portion of the war is therefore primarily associated with reporting, coordination and execution of commands rather than a documented individual battle against Victor.[5]
Diablo's regime eventually collapses after Victor's intervention in Hell and the wider Demonic War. Victor claims the infernal throne, defeats the former ruling structure and establishes himself as the new King of Demons. However, the confirmed material does not establish an individual scene in which Belial is personally killed, devoured or otherwise permanently destroyed during this transition.[10]

### Relationships
[[Diablo]] — Master
Belial belongs to Diablo's faction and recognizes the Primordial Demon as his superior. His position as a Duke gives him considerable influence over lesser Demons, but his authority remains subject to Diablo's orders. His fear of punishment becomes particularly evident when [[Bael]] investigates the destruction of his territory.[7][5]
[[Aphrodite]] Elderblood — Unrequited Obsession
Belial persistently attempts to make Aphrodite his wife, viewing her legendary beauty and reputation as qualities worthy of his desired queen. Aphrodite rejects him repeatedly and has no romantic interest in the Demon. His refusal to respect those rejections ultimately leads to her placing a curse of impotence upon him.[1][3]
[[Victor Elderblood]] — Enemy
Victor becomes Belial's most personally hated enemy after destroying his Human vessel, manipulating his interactions with Aphrodite, killing his son and devastating his territory. Belial desires revenge against Victor's family, while Victor treats the Duke as an enemy whose title offers no protection from retaliation.[1][4][2]
[[Beelzebub]] — Fellow Demon and Political Rival
Beelzebub becomes involved in Belial's conflict after Victor impersonates him during the attack on Belial's castle. The deception exploits Belial's scandalous reputation and attempts to provoke hostilities between two powerful Demons who would otherwise be expected to cooperate within Hell's political system. The accusations concerning Beelzebub's family are entangled with rumors and should be distinguished from independently confirmed relationships.[4][2]
Unnamed Son — Deceased Heir
Belial had at least one son who was regarded as his heir. Victor investigated and killed this Demon during his extended separation from [[Nightingale]], later using the severed head to reveal what he had done and deliberately provoke Belial. The loss of his heir became one of the principal reasons behind the Duke's determination to retaliate.[4]
Bael — Superior within Diablo's Faction
Bael functions as a representative of Diablo's authority when investigating the incidents surrounding Belial's territory. Belial understands that Bael's questions effectively carry the Demon King's command and cannot safely be ignored.[7]

### Skills & Abilities
As one of the 72 Dukes of Hell, Belial possesses the natural characteristics of a high-ranking Demon alongside abilities associated with his chimeric body and command over lesser Demons. His demonstrated capabilities are distinct from those of ordinary Demonic soldiers, although the novel does not provide a complete individual inventory of his combat techniques.

#### Racial Abilities
- Demon Physiology: Belial possesses the supernatural constitution of a higher Demon, including physical capabilities beyond ordinary Humans, a powerful Demonic presence and a body fundamentally adapted to the conditions of Hell.[1][2]
- Superhuman Strength: His racial constitution and position among Hell's upper nobility place his physical capabilities above those of ordinary Humans. His precise lifting strength and striking power have not been individually quantified.
- Superhuman Speed: Belial benefits from the enhanced physical performance associated with powerful Demons, although [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s initial attack demonstrates that a stronger or faster opponent can destroy his Human vessel before he responds effectively.[1]
- Superhuman Durability: His original Demonic body is considerably more formidable than the disposable Human vessels through which he operates outside Hell. The destruction of a vessel does not itself establish the destruction of his true body.[1][2]
- Enhanced Stamina: As a higher Demon, Belial possesses supernatural endurance and is not limited to ordinary Human physical performance.
- Regeneration: Belial possesses the regenerative characteristics associated with powerful Demons. No specific regeneration tier or demonstration of recovery from complete soul destruction has been established for him individually.
- Longevity: Belial has existed for millennia, greatly exceeding the natural lifespan of ordinary Humans.[2]
- Demonic Energy Manipulation: Belial possesses and releases Demonic Energy, allowing him to exert supernatural pressure and operate through Demonic vessels and subordinates. His enraged manifestation through a subordinate demonstrates the dangerous intensity of his power when insufficiently controlled.[4]
- Miasma Manipulation: Belial possesses the impure Demonic Energy associated with Hell's inhabitants. His specific offensive applications of Miasma are not separately demonstrated in the confirmed encounters.[1][6]
- Chimeric Physiology: His original form combines anatomical characteristics resembling multiple monstrous species. This includes an enormous body, prominent horns and Demonic wings.[2]
- Flight: His true form possesses wings, although the provided encounters do not establish a detailed individual aerial-combat feat.
- Soul-Based Possession: Belial can operate in the Human World through deceased Human bodies by establishing a supernatural connection involving a soul under his control. This allows him to act outside Hell without personally transporting his true body into the mortal realm.[1][3]
- Human Vessel Replacement: After Victor destroys his first possessed body, Belial returns using a different Human vessel. His ability to replace destroyed vessels distinguishes such losses from the death of his original Demonic body.[3]
- Soul Connection: The possession mechanism depends upon a connection to the soul associated with the vessel. Victor's attack severs the connection to the first body, preventing Belial from simply reclaiming it.[1]
- Remote Manifestation: Belial can project his influence through a Demonic subordinate to communicate across the boundary separating Hell from the Human World. The strain of his enraged manifestation can overwhelm a weaker intermediary.[4]

#### Combat and Other Abilities
- Supernatural Intimidation: Belial's status and Demonic presence allow him to exert pressure over lesser subordinates. His rage during the confrontation with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] threatens the stability of the intermediary through which he communicates.[4]
- Command over Demons: As a Duke of Hell, Belial possesses subordinates, attendants and forces operating within his territory. He can organize Demonic activity and report directly to the highest authorities of [[Diablo]]'s faction.[2][5]
- Military Coordination: Belial participates in the implementation of Diablo's invasion plans, receives strategic orders concerning troop deployments and reports on the progress of the Demonic operation.[5]
- Interdimensional Operations: Belial's faction can conduct activities involving the Human World through possession and Demonic agents. However, he is subject to restrictions on interdimensional movement and cannot independently cross every boundary or deploy unlimited forces whenever he wishes.[2][6]
- Supernatural Knowledge: His long existence within Hell and position in Diablo's organization give him knowledge of Demonic society, its hierarchy and the methods used by its members to interfere with the Human World.[7]

#### Limitations and Weaknesses
- Vessel Dependence: Belial's activities on Earth are constrained by the need for a suitable possessed vessel. Destroying that body can interrupt his presence and sever the supernatural connection maintaining it, even if his original Demonic body survives.[1][2]
- Interdimensional Restrictions: His true body cannot simply enter the Human World without regard for the supernatural barriers separating dimensions. Replacement vessels and the deployment of minions are affected by the resources and permissions available within [[Diablo]]'s faction.[2][6]
- Susceptibility to Divine Curses: [[Aphrodite]] successfully inflicts impotence upon Belial after becoming fed up with his harassment. His status as a Demon Duke does not provide automatic immunity against the authority of a powerful Goddess.[3][4]
- Limited Resistance to Mental Influence: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is capable of exploiting Belial's emotional weaknesses and reinforcing a false belief regarding Aphrodite. This does not demonstrate that Victor can completely dominate the Duke's mind, but it establishes that Belial is not immune to psychological and supernatural manipulation.[3]
- Political Subordination: Belial cannot openly disobey Diablo without risking severe punishment. His political freedom is therefore significantly narrower than his title might suggest.[7]
- Emotional Recklessness: His lust, pride and desire for revenge repeatedly impair his judgment. Victor successfully exploits these weaknesses both in manipulating his pursuit of Aphrodite and in constructing the false-flag attack against his territory.[3][4]

### Trivia
- Belial is identified as the 68th Key of Solomon and the 68th Duke of Hell, connecting his character to the Ars Goetia tradition used for the novel's Demonic hierarchy.
- His nickname "Zeus's Twin" reflects the similarities between his scandalous sexual behavior and the reputation of [[Zeus]], not an actual familial connection between the two characters.[2]
- Although sometimes addressed as King Belial, his established rank within [[Diablo]]'s faction is that of a Duke. He is not one of Hell's two original Demon Kings.
- [[Aphrodite]] repeatedly refuses his marriage proposals and openly expresses disgust toward Demonic Energy. His continued pursuit ultimately results in her cursing him with impotence.[1][3]
- The first body [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] destroys is a possessed Human vessel. This incident should not be treated as Belial's death, since he appears again afterward and is confirmed alive as late as Chapter 521.[1][5]
- Belial is one of the earliest named Demon Dukes to develop a personal feud with Victor, long before Victor becomes the King of Hell.
- Victor's attack on Belial's territory was deliberately disguised as an act committed by [[Beelzebub]]. Diablo eventually suspects the deception because the purported attacker's voice and behavior do not match Beelzebub.[4][6]
- The bomb Victor sends into Hell is constructed after he destroys Belial's Demonic subordinate. The severed head of Belial's son is separately used to provoke the Duke and should not be confused with the explosive's original vessel.[4]
- Belial's son's death and the destruction of his city are separate events, both associated with Victor's retaliation during his extended separation from [[Nightingale]].[4][2]
- Belial's notorious reputation and alleged affairs with Beelzebub's family help explain why Victor chooses that particular Demon as the false identity behind the attack.[2]
- Belial remains alive during Diablo's invasion of Earth, directly reporting to the Demon King and receiving orders in the ruins of Moscow.[5]
- In historical demonological traditions, Belial is associated with wickedness and infernal nobility. These traditional depictions are separate from his actual characterization and demonstrated powers within the novel.
