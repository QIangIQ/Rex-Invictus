Phoenix, also known as Marquess Phoenix or Duke Phoenix, is a high-ranking Demon, a member of the Ars Goetia, and the former 37th Duke of [[Biblical Hell]]. He served under [[Diablo]] during the Demonic War, where he was assigned to attack Clan Fulger's territory in [[Nightingale]].[1] After Diablo's defeat and [[Victor Elderblood]]'s conquest of Hell, Phoenix later submitted to Victor alongside [[Zagan]].[2]
Phoenix is notable for his overwhelming regenerative and resurrection-based survivability. As a Demon of the Phoenix race, he can revive from ashes so long as his Soul is not destroyed, and he can even restore fallen subordinates as long as a part of their bodies remains intact.[3][4] This near-immortality shaped both his arrogance and his reputation among the Demons of Hell.

### Physical Appearance
Phoenix is described as a man with fiery wings on his back, a feature that immediately distinguishes him from most other Demons.[3] His appearance reflects his race and abilities, as fire is deeply tied to both his offense and his regenerative nature.
Although the novel has not yet provided an especially exhaustive physical profile for him, Phoenix consistently presents himself with the bearing of a high-ranking Demonic noble rather than a monstrous brute. His presence is meant to project superiority and confidence, fitting his position as one of the Dukes of Hell.

### Personality
Phoenix is arrogant, proud, and dismissive of weaker or younger opponents.
His near-absolute survivability caused him to underestimate enemies who, on paper, appeared beneath him. This was most clearly shown during the attack on Fulger territory, where he did not take [[Tatsuya Rider]] and [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] seriously enough, allowing the situation to escalate into a humiliating defeat for his forces.[5]
Despite this arrogance, Phoenix is not fearless. He is fully aware that there are beings capable of killing him permanently, and he becomes far more cautious when facing opponents or races who can threaten his Soul rather than merely destroy his body. In that sense, his pride is tied directly to the limits of his immortality: he is bold when he expects to survive, but wary around those who can bypass or end that survival.
Phoenix is also pragmatic rather than blindly fanatical. Although he served [[Diablo]], he later adjusted to the new order of Hell after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s victory and chose submission over futile resistance.[2] His later association with [[Zagan]] likewise suggests that he is willing to think politically and strategically, rather than acting only as a reckless frontline warrior.[6]

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Phoenix first entered the story during [[Diablo]]'s coordinated assault on [[Nightingale]]. Alongside Dukes such as [[Bael]], [[Sitri]], [[Paimon]], [[Zagan]], and [[Dantalion]], he was dispatched to invade one of the Count territories so that the Vampires would be too occupied to interfere in the larger war involving Demons, Angels, and Humans. Phoenix was specifically assigned to attack the territory of Clan Fulger.[1]
During the invasion, Phoenix led a Demonic army against the Fulger domain. However, the attack ran into fierce resistance from [[Tatsuya Rider]], [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]], and [[Hecate Moriarty]], who fought back with enough strength and coordination to devastate his forces.[3][5] Phoenix's status as a Duke and his exceptional regenerative abilities made him a serious threat, but his army itself was gradually worn down and repeatedly countered.
One of Phoenix's greatest advantages in this battle was his ability to revive his soldiers. As long as even a piece of a subordinate remained, he could restore them through his flames.[3] This made the invasion significantly harder to stop by ordinary means and emphasized why Phoenix was such a dangerous commander despite not relying solely on overwhelming raw destructive power.
Even so, Tatsuya and Sasha proved capable of stalling him directly. Their battle demonstrated that Phoenix's arrogance had practical consequences. Because he did not initially regard them as opponents worthy of caution, he allowed the engagement to progress in a way that exposed weaknesses in his assumptions.[5]
The clash eventually escalated to the point where Phoenix was killed once. However, true to his race and title, he resurrected from the ashes as long as his Soul remained intact.[4] This made clear that defeating Phoenix physically was not enough; one had to destroy or otherwise neutralize the deeper basis of his existence.
The overall situation changed when powerful reinforcements, including [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]], arrived.[4] At that stage, Phoenix's offensive momentum had already been broken, and the assault on Fulger territory had effectively failed. What was supposed to be a suppressive invasion against the Vampires instead became one of the clearest demonstrations that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s expanding faction was rapidly growing beyond what Diablo's subordinates had expected.

#### Demonic War
Later in the war, Phoenix was sent by [[Diablo]] to observe the new King of Hell.[6] This placed him in a more political role than during the earlier invasion of [[Nightingale]].
During this stage, Phoenix met [[Zagan]], who had taken a neutral and opportunistic stance toward the growing conflict in Hell.[6] Their interaction showed that not all of Diablo's remaining high-ranking Demons were equally loyal or equally willing to gamble everything on their old King. Some, like Zagan and later Phoenix himself, were perfectly willing to evaluate the new balance of power.
Phoenix's presence during this part of the story reinforces that he was not simply a brute-force combatant. He also functioned as a high-level observer and political actor among Hell's upper ranks.

#### King Among Kings
After [[Victor Elderblood]] emerged victorious in the Demonic War and solidified his rule over Hell, Phoenix ultimately submitted to him alongside [[Zagan]].[2]
This submission marked the end of Phoenix's service under [[Diablo]] and the beginning of his place under the new order of Hell ruled by Victor. Unlike Demons who were killed, consumed, or forced into obedience through harsher means, Phoenix chose to align himself with the winner once the result became undeniable.
His decision reflects both self-preservation and pragmatism. Phoenix had already witnessed Victor's overwhelming rise and the collapse of the old Hell. Continuing resistance would have been pointless, whereas submission allowed him to retain relevance under the new regime.

### Skills & Abilities
Demon Physiology: As one of the Ars Goetia and a Duke of Hell, Phoenix possesses the advanced physical abilities common to higher Demons, including immense strength, speed, durability, supernatural stamina, Miasma generation, and high-level regeneration.

#### Phoenix Abilities
- [[Pyrokinesis]]: As a Phoenix-type Demon, Phoenix naturally controls fire and uses it as the foundation of both his offense and his regenerative powers.[3]
- Fire Wings: Phoenix manifests fiery wings, which are both a racial characteristic and a visual symbol of his Demonic-Phoenix nature.[3]
- Enhanced Regeneration: Phoenix can regenerate from wounds at a rate beyond normal Demons by using his flames.[4]
- Resurrection: As long as his Soul is not destroyed, Phoenix can be reborn from ashes after death.[4] This is his defining survival ability and the reason he is so difficult to kill permanently.
- Revival of Others: Phoenix can revive his soldiers so long as at least one part of their bodies remains intact.[3] However, he cannot bring them back if they are completely destroyed.[5]

#### Miasma
- Miasma Generation: Phoenix emits powerful Demonic Miasma capable of affecting the environment around him.
- Corrosion and Suppression: His Miasma is dangerous enough to corrode the surroundings and interfere with a Vampire's natural regeneration.[4]

#### Immortality and Weakness
Phoenix's greatest strength is also the source of his tactical weakness.
Because he can resurrect from ashes, he developed a strong tendency to underestimate enemies he assumes cannot destroy his Soul. This makes him dangerous in prolonged warfare but also vulnerable to overconfidence.[5]
At the same time, Phoenix is very cautious around beings who might permanently kill him. Opponents capable of Soul destruction, absolute erasure, or equivalent effects represent a much more serious threat to him than ordinary powerful fighters.

### Relationships

#### Diablo
[[Diablo]] was Phoenix's former King and master.
Phoenix served as one of Diablo's notable subordinates and participated directly in the invasion of [[Nightingale]] as part of Diablo's broader war strategy.[1] Later, Phoenix was also entrusted with observing the newly risen King of Hell, indicating that Diablo considered him useful not only in battle but also as a reliable high-ranking subordinate for important missions.[6]
This relationship ended after Diablo's defeat.

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] became Phoenix's new sovereign after conquering Hell.
Although Phoenix initially opposed Victor indirectly through service to [[Diablo]], the later stages of the Demonic War forced him to recognize the new balance of power. After Victor's victory, Phoenix submitted to him alongside [[Zagan]].[2]
Phoenix's shift in allegiance reflects the broader restructuring of Hell under Victor's rule.

#### Zagan
[[Zagan]] is Phoenix's closest known friend among the Demons of Hell.[6]
The two are associated with one another during the post-[[Diablo]] political transition in Hell and eventually submit to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] together.[6][2] Their relationship suggests a measure of mutual trust or at least reliable cooperation uncommon among Demonic nobles.

#### Tatsuya Rider
[[Tatsuya Rider]] was one of the principal defenders who resisted Phoenix during the invasion of Fulger territory.
Phoenix underestimated Tatsuya because of his youth, but Tatsuya proved strong enough to help devastate his army and hold him back in direct combat.[5][4]

#### Sasha Elderblood Fulger
[[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] likewise played a crucial role in Phoenix's failed assault on Fulger territory.
Together with Tatsuya and [[Hecate]], she contributed to the destruction of his forces and demonstrated enough power to force Phoenix into a far more difficult battle than he expected.[3][5]

#### Hecate Moriarty
[[Hecate Moriarty]] participated alongside Tatsuya and Sasha in defending Fulger territory against Phoenix's invasion.[3][5]
Her presence further complicated the assault and contributed to the eventual collapse of Phoenix's offensive operations in that area.

### Trivia
- Phoenix's mythological counterpart is more commonly rendered as Phenex or Pheynix rather than simply Phoenix.
- He is described as one of the higher-ranking Demons of Hell and served as the 37th Duke of Hell.
- Phoenix's signature characteristic is his ability to resurrect from ashes so long as his Soul remains intact.[4]
- He can also revive subordinates as long as their bodies are not completely destroyed.[3][5]
- His overconfidence during the invasion of Fulger territory contributed significantly to the defeat of his army.[5]
- Despite his arrogance, he is notably more cautious around beings capable of permanently killing him.
