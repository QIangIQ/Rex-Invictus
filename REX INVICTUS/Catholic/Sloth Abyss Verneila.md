Sloth Abyss Verneila, usually referred to simply as Sloth, is an ancient Demon who embodies the Sin of Sloth. She was formerly one of the Seven Deadly Sins serving [[Diablo]] and later became a subordinate of [[Victor Elderblood]] after his conquest and unification of [[Biblical Hell]].[1][2]
Despite being infamous for almost never exerting herself, Sloth possesses extremely powerful [[Magic]] and one of the most unusual passive abilities among the former Deadly Sins. Her Sin continuously rewards the very behavior she embodies: the more she sleeps, the stronger her power becomes. It also automatically protects her body from impurities and external attacks while requiring essentially no conscious effort from her.[3][2]
Sloth was also the only one of Diablo's active Seven Deadly Sins to survive the [[Heavenly Father]]'s Heavenly Judgment during the Demonic War. Whereas the other Sins caught within the attack were completely evaporated by its Holy Light, Sloth protected herself beneath an enormous concentration of Miasma and survived with comparatively minor wounds.[4]

### Physical Appearance
Sloth is a woman with extremely long black hair. By the time she was rediscovered more than seven centuries after the Demonic War, her hair had grown beyond the length of her entire body and spread across the bed on which she slept.[3]
When Aline located her in a ruined castle within one of Hell's unexplored territories, Sloth was still wearing the same clothing she had worn during the Demonic War over 700 years earlier. Despite neither changing those clothes nor apparently bathing throughout her prolonged sleep, her body possessed no foul smell or accumulated impurities. Vepar explained that this was a consequence of the Power belonging to the Sin of Sloth, which automatically maintained and protected Sloth's body.[3]
During Heavenly Judgment, she was depicted lying on a nearby building with a massive quantity of dark Miasma surrounding her while small Holy-Light wounds remained upon her body.[4] Beyond her black hair and generally lethargic presentation, the novel has not provided a definitive description of her eye color, exact height, build or the detailed appearance of her clothing.

### Personality
Sloth embodies extreme laziness to such an extent that sleeping is effectively the central activity of her existence. Even before her direct appearance, other Demons regarded her inactivity as completely normal; while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was devastating Hell's forces, [[Shax]] complained that Sloth was probably doing nothing "as usual" despite believing her Power could have been useful against Victor.[5]
Her laziness should not be mistaken for stupidity. Sloth is sufficiently perceptive to recognize when remaining on a battlefield would accomplish nothing. When Victor's Demonic forces began annihilating [[Diablo]]'s army, she did not remain behind out of pride or loyalty and instead immediately used the surrounding Miasma to escape. The narration explicitly describes her as lazy, but not stupid.[6]
Among the Seven Deadly Sins, Sloth was considered unusually reasonable. Vepar described her as the most reasonable of the group, explaining that Sloth generally had no interest in bothering anyone so long as nobody bothered her. Her instinctive preference is not conquest, sadism or needless confrontation, but simply being left alone so that she can sleep. Because of this, Vepar strongly recommended diplomacy when Victor's forces rediscovered her instead of attempting to forcibly capture her.[1]
Her defining laziness nevertheless severely limits how useful she is as an active subordinate. Victor considers her inefficient and finds her refusal to train irritating, but he simultaneously recognizes that she is an ancient Demon who becomes highly competent when sufficiently pressured. He therefore continues keeping her nearby as a contingency rather than discarding her simply because she spends most of her existence asleep.[2]

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Long before Sloth directly entered the battlefield, her inactivity was already notorious among [[Diablo]]'s forces. During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s campaign through Hell, [[Shax]] desperately questioned the absence of Diablo's strongest elites and complained that Sloth had failed to meaningfully contribute to the conflict, despite believing that her unusual Power could potentially help stop Victor.[5]
Sloth eventually entered the Demonic War when the balance of the conflict began shifting against Diablo. Diablo personally ordered Sloth and Envy to act. The two disappeared through a portal before suddenly emerging beside Michael. Sloth addressed Michael in a slow, weary tone, telling the Archangel that he looked busy and tired. Her words immediately began imposing supernatural drowsiness upon him, forcing Michael to actively resist the urge to sleep. Envy then exploited the opening by temporarily draining Michael's Energy reserves; the Energy drain itself belonged to Envy, while Sloth's contribution was the supernatural fatigue placed upon Michael.[7]
The war eventually deteriorated into complete chaos. After Heaven suffered devastating losses, the [[Heavenly Father]] personally intervened and unleashed Heavenly Judgment, causing enormous quantities of Holy Light to descend across the battlefield. Approximately half of the remaining Demonic forces were destroyed, and the other active Deadly Sins caught within the attack were evaporated.[4]
Sloth was the exception. [[Merlin]] found her lying upon a nearby building beneath an enormous concentration of Miasma. Even the Heavenly Father's Holy Light had been unable to completely penetrate the "pit of darkness" surrounding her. Some of the attack nevertheless passed through and left small Holy wounds on Sloth's body. Merlin noted the strange sight of her apparently sleeping during the attack and questioned whether pretending to sleep was necessary for the ability she was using. The narration clarified that Sloth survived because she possessed an enormous quantity of Miasma and because Heavenly Judgment had been distributed across the entire battlefield rather than concentrated specifically upon her.[4]
Sloth had no intention of testing her survival a second time. When Victor's Demonic Beasts and Legions began tearing through what remained of Diablo's army, several intelligent elites abandoned the battle. Sloth was among them. She manipulated her Miasma and used a technique that allowed her to disappear into the surrounding Miasma, escaping before Victor's forces could reach her.[6]
Her ability to do this was connected directly to her origin. Sloth was born in the lowest regions of Hell from a river of Miasma, making movement through and immersion within Miasma virtually instinctive to her. The narration describes this affinity as a passive characteristic of her existence and states that she was extremely accustomed to "swimming" through the River of Miasma because it was the place from which she had originally been born.[6]

#### King Among Kings
Sloth remained hidden for more than 700 years after the Demonic War while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] gradually unified Hell beneath his rule. During this period, Victor's Generals and subordinates searched for surviving members of [[Diablo]]'s former regime, bringing most remaining Demons under the new Demon King's authority.[3]
Aline Valefar eventually discovered Sloth while using the Ruler's Authority to search Hell for Souls. Sloth was located inside a ruined room of a decaying castle situated within a barren and largely unexplored region of Hell. Despite centuries passing, she had apparently done almost nothing except sleep. Her hair had grown beyond the length of her body, and she was still wearing the same clothing she had worn during the war.[3]
The discovery led Vepar to reveal previously unknown information regarding Sloth's Sin. According to knowledge recorded by the ancient Demons Albu and [[Zahal]], the Sin of Sloth automatically protects Sloth's entire body. She does not need to bathe because smells and physical impurities cannot accumulate upon her even if she sleeps for thousands of years. More importantly, the same passive effect also protects her from outside attacks. Vepar warned that although Sloth appeared defenseless while sleeping, anyone who attacked her would suffer a "very horrible fate", although the exact mechanism responsible for that retaliation was not explained.[3]
When the question of capturing Sloth arose, Vepar advised against using force. Although Victor's Generals did not believe Sloth could defeat their combined forces, forcibly taking her would cause unnecessary destruction because of the defensive properties of her Sin. Vepar instead recommended negotiation, describing Sloth as the most reasonable of the Deadly Sins: if nobody disturbed her, she normally had no reason to attack anyone and would simply continue sleeping.[1]
Victor's forces therefore chose to locate and approach her discreetly rather than immediately storming the castle. Lesser Demon messengers were used because of their speed and ability to travel without drawing unnecessary attention.[1] The exact scene in which Sloth formally entered Victor's service has not been shown, but by the time of Victor's later reign she had become one of his subordinates and was living inside his own castle.[2]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Long after the consolidation of Hell, Sloth remained almost completely unchanged in behavior. While [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was accompanying the [[Heavenly Father]] and Ariel through his domain, he asked [[Zahal]] where Sloth was. Zahal reported that Sloth Abyss Verneila was occupying Victor's fourth lower chamber and had been sleeping there ever since.[2]
Victor referred to her as "that lazy woman" but explained internally why he continued tolerating someone so inefficient. Sloth was an ancient Demon who, despite her laziness, was highly competent when actually forced to act. Victor therefore retained her as a precautionary asset who could potentially fill an important vacancy should he ever require a replacement among the forces governing his domain.[2]
More importantly, Sloth's inactivity did not prevent her from developing. Her Sin causes her power to grow stronger the longer she sleeps. Victor considered her extraordinarily fortunate because she did not need conventional training to increase her magical power; sleeping, the activity she enjoyed above everything else, was itself enough to continuously strengthen her. Among the former Deadly Sins, Sloth was the only one Victor had never seen training.[2]
This advantage came with an equally severe flaw. Sloth's physical abilities remained extraordinarily poor despite her accumulated magical strength, being described as only around the level of an ordinary Demon. Victor compared her through RPG terminology to a "glass cannon mage": a combatant possessing powerful [[Magic]] while having extremely weak physical attributes. Rather than attempt the difficult task of waking her, Victor simply chose to begin the ceremony he was preparing without her.[2]

### Skills & Abilities
Sloth's abilities are unusual even by the standards of the Seven Deadly Sins. Her overall combat capability is heavily unbalanced: physically she is remarkably weak for an ancient Demon, while her magical reserves, Miasma abilities and passive Sin provide exceptional supernatural power and defense.[2]
- Demon Physiology: Sloth is an ancient Infernal Demon born in the lowest regions of [[Biblical Hell]]. Like other Demons, her existence is naturally compatible with Miasma and the corruptive environment of Hell. Sloth's connection is particularly pronounced because she was directly born from a river of Miasma.[6][2]
- Conditional [[Immortality]] / Reconstitution: Demons that are killed through ordinary means do not permanently die; they eventually return to Hell and reconstruct themselves, with more powerful Demons requiring longer periods to reform. This process can be bypassed through Holy Light, specially effective weapons or abilities that completely absorb the Demon.[3]
- Sin of Sloth: Sloth possesses the supernatural Power corresponding to the Sin she embodies. Unlike a conventional technique requiring constant conscious control, several aspects of the Sin function automatically and directly reward Sloth for remaining inactive.[3][2]
- Sleep Empowerment: Sloth continuously becomes more powerful through sleeping. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly notes that she has never needed to train like the other former Sins because the longer she sleeps, the stronger her power becomes. Her centuries-long periods of sleep therefore function as passive supernatural growth rather than simple inactivity.[2]
- Lethargy Inducement / Sleep Inducement: Sloth can supernaturally impose overwhelming tiredness upon others. When she addressed Michael in a lazy, weary voice, the Archangel immediately began feeling sleepy and had to actively resist the urge to succumb.[7]
- Automatic Defense: The Sin of Sloth automatically protects Sloth's body from external attacks without requiring her to consciously defend herself. This protection remains functional while she is asleep. Vepar warned that attacking the sleeping Sloth could produce an extremely dangerous consequence for the aggressor, though the exact mechanism responsible for this retaliation has never been revealed.[3]
- Self-Purification: Sloth's passive Sin prevents physical impurities from accumulating upon her body. She can sleep for thousands of years without bathing and still produce no odor or bodily impurity. This effect remained active throughout the more than seven centuries she spent asleep after the Demonic War.[3]
- Miasma Manipulation: Sloth possesses exceptional control over Miasma. She can surround herself with enormous quantities of it for defense and employ it as the medium for specialized techniques.[4][6]
- Miasma Barrier / Defensive Miasma: Sloth can concentrate Miasma around herself into an extremely powerful defensive layer. During Heavenly Judgment, the quantity surrounding her was great enough that even the [[Heavenly Father]]'s widespread Holy-Light attack could not completely penetrate it. The defense was not absolute, however, as several portions of Holy Light reached her and caused minor wounds.[4]
- Miasma Travel / Concealment: Sloth can disappear directly into surrounding Miasma, allowing her to escape through it. She used this ability to abandon the Demonic War when Victor's Legions began destroying [[Diablo]]'s forces.[6]
- Miasma Affinity: Sloth's exceptional ease of movement within Miasma comes from her origin. She was born from a river of Miasma in the lowest parts of Hell and is described as being accustomed to "swimming" through it. Her capability to integrate herself into Miasma is therefore an innate characteristic rather than merely a learned spell.[6]
- Powerful [[Magic]]: Despite her poor physical abilities, Sloth possesses extremely strong magical power. Victor specifically characterizes her as the equivalent of a powerful glass-cannon mage whose supernatural output is disproportionately greater than her physical capabilities.[2]
- Supernatural Longevity: Sloth is an ancient Demon capable of remaining dormant for enormous spans of time. She spent more than seven centuries sleeping after the Demonic War with no indication that such prolonged inactivity harmed her, and her Sin is capable of maintaining her body through periods potentially lasting thousands of years.[3][2]

#### Power
Sloth should not be evaluated through physical strength alone. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly considers her physically comparable to a relatively ordinary Demon, yet still retains her as a useful contingency because of her competence, magical power and the continued growth provided by her Sin.[2]
Her greatest demonstrated defensive feat was surviving the [[Heavenly Father]]'s Heavenly Judgment. The same attack evaporated the other active Deadly Sins caught within it and destroyed approximately half of the Demonic forces on the battlefield. Sloth survived by enveloping herself in an enormous concentration of Miasma, although she still suffered several minor Holy-Light wounds and the narration clarifies that the Judgment was spread across the entire battlefield rather than focused specifically upon her.[4]
Her later passive defense makes attacking her while dormant particularly troublesome. Vepar, who possessed information regarding the Sins through the writings of Albu and [[Zahal]], warned Victor's forces that forcibly capturing Sloth could produce extensive damage despite the confidence of Victor's Generals that they could defeat her.[3][1]

#### Weaknesses
- Extremely Poor Physical Ability: Sloth's largest direct combat weakness is her body. Despite being an ancient Demon and possessing substantial magical power, her physical strength is described as being almost comparable to that of an ordinary Demon. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] consequently likens her to a glass-cannon mage.[2]
- Extreme Laziness: Her Sin encourages the behavioral trait she already embodies. Sloth has essentially no motivation to train, participate in ceremonies or involve herself in conflicts unless circumstances force her to act. Although sleeping strengthens her Power, this makes her extremely unreliable as a conventional active combatant or subordinate.[5][2]
- [[Holy Energy]] / Holy Light: Like other Demons, Sloth is naturally vulnerable to Holy power. Her Miasma protected her from most of Heavenly Judgment, but portions of the attack pierced her defense and wounded her. Her survival therefore demonstrates defensive power rather than immunity to Holy Energy.[4]

### Relationships

#### Diablo
[[Diablo]] was Sloth's former master during her period as one of the Seven Deadly Sins. Although her inactivity frustrated other members of his army, she obeyed Diablo when he personally commanded her to intervene during the Demonic War and joined Envy in attacking Michael.[7]
Her loyalty nevertheless had limits. Once [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Legions began annihilating Diablo's army and the conflict had clearly become hopeless, Sloth abandoned the battlefield rather than die defending Diablo's collapsing rule.[6]

#### Victor Elderblood
Sloth originally stood on the opposing side of [[Victor Elderblood]] as one of [[Diablo]]'s Deadly Sins, although she avoided directly confronting him during the Demonic War. After Victor became the new Demon King and spent centuries unifying Hell, his forces eventually located her dormant castle.[3]
By Victor's later reign, Sloth had entered his faction and was openly treated as one of his subordinates. She occupied the fourth lower chamber of his castle, where she continued doing what she had always preferred: sleeping. Victor regards her as highly inefficient and exceptionally lazy but nevertheless acknowledges that she is competent when forced to act and possesses enough potential to be retained as a contingency asset.[2]

#### Seven Deadly Sins
Sloth was one of [[Diablo]]'s Seven Deadly Sins alongside Pride, Envy, Wrath, Greed, Gluttony and Lust.[7] During Heavenly Judgment, every other active Sin caught within the battlefield was evaporated while Sloth survived through her enormous Miasma defenses, making her the only one of Diablo's active Seven Deadly Sins to survive the attack.[4]
Unlike several of the other Sins, Sloth was considered unusually reasonable. Vepar believed she could be approached peacefully because her natural response to being left alone was simply to continue sleeping rather than seek conflict.[1]

### Trivia
- Sloth is female. The novel consistently refers to her as a woman, despite some early secondary descriptions incorrectly using male pronouns.
- Her full canonical name is Sloth Abyss Verneila; the spelling "Vernalia" is incorrect.[2]
- Sloth was born from a river of Miasma in the lowest regions of Hell.[6]
- She is one of the few characters whose defining Sin directly rewards inactivity: sleeping itself causes her Power to increase.[2]
- She was the only active Deadly Sin to survive Heavenly Judgment.[4]
- Sloth spent more than 700 years sleeping without changing her clothing. Her Sin prevented odor and impurities from affecting her throughout that period.[3]
- According to Vepar, Sloth could potentially remain asleep for thousands of years without needing to bathe because her Sin automatically maintains her body.[3]
- Sloth was considered the most reasonable of the Seven Deadly Sins because she generally had no interest in interfering with anyone who left her alone.[1]
- Despite continuously becoming stronger through sleep, she possesses extremely poor physical strength and has never been observed training. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] compared her to an RPG glass-cannon mage.[2]
- Before Sloth's first direct appearance, [[Shax]] had already complained about her tendency to do nothing and noted that she had contributed almost nothing to the ongoing conflict.[5]
- The exact offensive or retaliatory effect of her passive Sin remains unknown. Vepar only stated that someone attacking Sloth while she slept would meet a "very horrible fate".[3]
