### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 674
Zahal is one of the two ancient Keepers of Hell, alongside his twin brother Albu. Created directly by [[Qliphoth|Earth's Negative World Tree]], Qliphoth, the twins have existed since before the fall of [[Lucifer]] Morningstar and were already present when [[Biblical Hell]] was still little more than an empty wasteland. Their purpose was never simply to serve a particular Demon King; they were created to preserve Hell itself, ensure that the Dimension continued fulfilling its function in the cosmic Soul cycle and intervene if an incompetent ruler threatened the continued existence of the realm.[2][1]
Zahal and Albu consequently exist outside most of Hell's conventional political hierarchy. They are not members of the 72 Dukes of Hell, Seven Deadly Sins or Four Horsemen of The Apocalypse, and historically possessed little interest in the struggles between those factions. Their allegiance was fundamentally institutional: they obeyed the King of Hell only while that King properly maintained the Dimension. If a ruler endangered Hell, the twins considered that sovereign an inept King whose authority no longer outweighed their duty to their creator and the [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] itself.[2]
This ancient neutrality changed after [[Victor Elderblood]] conquered Biblical Hell. Zahal and Albu sensed Energy associated with their creator within Victor, witnessed the Demon King's throne recognize him and immediately acknowledged Victor as the True King of Hell. Victor subsequently elevated them from simple Keepers into the formal [[Elders]] of Hell, entrusted them with preserving the entire recorded history of the Dimension and placed them directly beneath himself within the new governmental structure.[2]
Unlike their conditional loyalty to previous Kings, their relationship with Victor eventually became openly personal and fanatical. By Chapter 910, both [[Heavenly Father]] and Ariel immediately noticed that the ancient Keepers who had historically cared about almost nothing beyond Hell were now completely committed to Victor's reign. The chapter also establishes their current strength far more precisely: after Victor's intervention, Zahal and Albu had reached the level of fully developed Second Generation [[Primordial Gods]] and were described as unquestionably the strongest Demons in Hell. Victor intended to grant them his Blessing of Destruction in the future, which would raise them to the level of First Generation Primordial Gods.[1]

### Physical Description
Zahal is one of two physically identical twin Demons. He is distinguished from Albu primarily by his skin color: Zahal has dark grey skin, while Albu has red skin. Both possess glowing red eyes and naturally intimidating Demonic appearances.[2]
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] first entered the throne room of the former Demon King, Zahal and Albu stood motionlessly in front of the throne with their eyes closed. The moment they opened them, an immense supernatural pressure descended upon the room. [[Vine]] Elderblood, Helena Gremory Elderblood and [[Vepar Elderblood]], all of whom had already been significantly strengthened by Victor and were among his most powerful Demonic followers, were immediately forced to stop advancing. The reaction established that the two Keepers existed on an entirely different level from conventional Pillar-class Demons even before the later power increase explicitly described in Chapter 910.[2]
Despite their threatening natural appearance, the twins are capable of displaying comparatively gentle expressions. When Victor later returned to Hell with Ophis, [[Nero Elderblood]] and [[Metis]], Zahal and Albu welcomed the girls as Princesses and smiled at them. The narration humorously notes that the smiles still appeared somewhat sinister because of the brothers' naturally Demonic faces.[1]

### Personality and Traits
Zahal is intensely dutiful, blunt and almost completely uninterested in the ordinary ambitions that motivate most Demons. Wealth, Pillar Rank, territory and political prestige appear to mean very little to him. His primary concern throughout most of his existence was the continued survival and functionality of Hell as an [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]].[2]
Of the two brothers, Zahal is frequently the first to speak when institutional matters are being discussed. During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s first meeting with the twins, Zahal bluntly blamed the existing crisis of Hell upon [[Diablo]], describing him as a fool who refused to become a proper Ruler and consequently damaged the entire Soul-processing system.[2] Albu followed by explaining how Diablo and Lilith had previously shared portions of those responsibilities. This does not establish a major personality difference between the twins, but Zahal is generally shown expressing their judgments in a more immediate and direct manner.
His loyalty historically belonged to Hell before its King. Zahal did not believe the title of Demon King automatically deserved unconditional obedience. A King who threatened Hell's continued existence ceased being worthy of the Keepers' support because the twins' duty originated from a higher purpose established by their creator.[2]
This attitude makes Zahal unusual among Demons. He is capable of respecting overwhelming power but does not appear obsessed with strength for its own sake. The continued existence of Hell's Soul cycle matters more to him than whether one political faction defeats another.
Zahal's relationship with Victor altered this detached worldview. He immediately recognized Victor as extraordinary because the Energy of their Mother existed within him. When Victor manifested a living branch containing both Life and Negative Energy, Zahal and Albu reacted with overwhelming emotion and fanaticism. The simple sight of living vegetation connected to their creator affected them more deeply than conventional political rewards ever could.[2]
Victor then promised that he would one day create a garden for the twins to protect. To beings who had spent virtually their entire lives inside a realm where genuine natural life was considered impossible, the promise was extraordinary. Zahal and Albu immediately declared themselves completely at Victor's command.[2]
Their devotion eventually exceeded simple reverence toward [[Qliphoth]]'s Energy. By Chapter 910, Zahal speaks on behalf of both twins when Victor asks how they have been, answering with explicit respect and describing their current state as a result of their King's extraordinary capabilities. Both brothers display such intense fanaticism that the [[Heavenly Father]] is briefly reminded of Michael, one of his most absolutely loyal children.[1]
Zahal also completely accepts the absolute legal authority Victor possesses inside his own Hell. The [[Elders]] treat Victor's declared family relationships, political appointments and commands as reality that the rest of Hell is obligated to recognize. They no longer behave like detached custodians tolerating another temporary King; they view Victor's reign as the proper state of their Dimension.

### History

#### Creation by the Negative World Tree
Zahal and Albu were created directly by the Negative aspect of Earth's World Tree, the entity currently known as [[Qliphoth]].[2]
This makes their origin fundamentally different from that of most Demons. They were not ordinary condemned Souls that transformed into Demonic beings, nor were they members of one of the later bloodlines created under the political systems of [[Lucifer]], Lilith or [[Diablo]]. They were created specifically to serve as custodians of the [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] produced by Earth's Negative World Tree.
Their existence predates Lucifer's fall from Heaven. By Chapter 910, the narration goes even further, stating that Zahal and Albu had occupied the cursed lands of Hell since those lands were still barren and without buildings. The [[Heavenly Father]] and Ariel immediately recognize them and are described as roughly comparable to the twins in age.[1]
This places Zahal among the oldest surviving individual beings associated with the Biblical supernatural system.

#### Keeper of Hell
Zahal's original title was Keeper of Hell or Guardian of Hell. The duty carried far more authority than simple castle protection.
Hell exists as part of a much larger metaphysical structure responsible for processing the dead. Souls are punished, transformed, recycled and eventually returned to the greater cycle of existence. If an [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] ceases performing this function, higher cosmic authorities such as [[The Universal Tree]] and [[The Judges of The Abyss]] can intervene and destroy the malfunctioning realm.[2]
Zahal and Albu were therefore responsible for protecting not merely Hell's land but its function.
A King who properly maintained Hell could expect their cooperation. A King whose incompetence threatened the Dimension could become one of the very threats the twins were created to oppose.[2]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] immediately recognized this relationship after meeting them, comparing their existence to the way [[Diablo|Big Guy]] functioned in relation to [[Roxanne Elderblood]]. The twins existed less like ordinary political subordinates and more like ancient custodians created to protect the interests of a World Tree and the realm connected to it.[2]

#### Lucifer, Lilith and Diablo
Zahal and Albu survived throughout the reigns of several Kings and political systems. They were already present before [[Lucifer]] Morningstar arrived in Hell and remained after Lucifer's departure, the fragmentation of authority between [[Lilith Elderblood]] and [[Diablo]], and Diablo's later attempt to dominate the Dimension.
The twins generally remained removed from those political struggles so long as Hell itself continued functioning.
Diablo eventually created a serious problem by refusing to properly become a Ruler. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] questioned why the Soul flow throughout Hell had become dysfunctional, Zahal directly blamed Diablo, saying that his refusal had "messed up the whole system".[2]
Albu explained that portions of the necessary influence had previously been maintained collectively through Diablo and Lilith, but circumstances eventually left both unable to provide what Hell required.
The failure placed the Dimension in a dangerous position. A Hell that no longer fulfilled its role should eventually attract intervention from the Judges of the Abyss and the Universal Tree. Diablo apparently prevented immediate destruction through an unknown agreement with the Judges involving billions of Souls, buying additional time before the higher authorities intervened.[2]
Neither Zahal nor Albu knew the complete terms of the transaction.

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Following his conquest through [[Biblical Hell]], [[Victor Elderblood]] eventually entered the enormous castle belonging to the Demon King accompanied by [[Vine]] Elderblood, Helena Gremory Elderblood and [[Vepar Elderblood]]. After traversing the fortress, the group reached the central throne room.[2]
Standing before the throne were two identical Demons.
The dark-grey twin introduced himself first:
Zahal.
The red-skinned twin then identified himself as Albu.
Together they declared themselves the Keepers of Hell.[2]
Vine and Vepar immediately recognized the names and explained to Helena that the two had existed even before [[Lucifer]]'s fall. Their purpose was to protect Hell from destruction, including destruction caused by an incompetent King.[2]
The Keepers released their pressure, forcing Victor's three Generals to stop. Victor himself continued walking forward.
Rather than challenge him, the twins soon knelt.
Victor questioned why Zahal and Albu were calling him their King when he understood that their loyalty had traditionally belonged to Hell itself rather than whoever occupied its throne.
Zahal gave the first answer:
Victor was special.[2]
Albu clarified that the Energy of their Mother flowed through him.
Both then declared that Victor was the True King of Hell.[2]
Victor tested their reaction by manifesting a living tree branch filled with Life and Negative Energy. The result bordered on religious ecstasy. Zahal and Albu stared at the branch with open emotion and declared that only the true King could accomplish such a thing inside Hell.[2]
Roxanne privately explained the reason to Victor. The Keepers had been created by [[Qliphoth|Earth's Negative World Tree]]. Because Victor carried Roxanne's Energy and that Energy was extremely similar to her sister's, the twins effectively interpreted Victor as a direct descendant or chosen child of their creator.[2]
The reaction confirmed to Victor that their loyalty could become extremely useful.
Victor continued toward the Demon King's throne while the twins remained nearby.
As he approached, the throne physically changed to accommodate him. After Victor sat upon it, the castle itself transformed and information connected to Hell's systems entered his awareness. The reaction demonstrated that Hell itself recognized Victor's authority.[2]
For Zahal and Albu, this removed any remaining uncertainty.
The true King had appeared.
The twins concluded that the Keys still held by [[Diablo]] properly belonged to Victor and should eventually be returned to him.[2]
Victor almost immediately noticed something wrong with the flow of Souls.
Zahal gave the bluntest explanation: Diablo had refused to become a proper Ruler and had damaged Hell's entire system.[2]
Victor explained the consequences to his Generals. Hell was not simply an enormous territory inhabited by Demons. It existed because Reality required an institution capable of receiving corrupted Souls, punishing them and recycling what could eventually re-enter the cycle of Life.
If the Dimension completely ceased performing that role, the Judges of the Abyss and Universal Tree could destroy it and replace the broken component of the system.[2]
This was precisely the kind of failure Zahal and Albu had been created to prevent.
The twins revealed that Diablo had avoided the worst consequences only because of an unknown bargain with the Judges involving billions of Souls. Whatever he had purchased through the transaction was temporary rather than a permanent solution.[2]
Victor eventually asked why the twins regarded him differently from previous Kings.
Once again, the explanation centered upon their Mother.
Victor manifested the living branch, proving that the Energy associated with the [[World Trees]] and genuine Life existed within him. Both twins became nearly overwhelmed by the sight.[2]
For ancient beings who had spent their entire existence inside Hell, the existence of genuine plant life there bordered on impossible.
When Victor dismissed the branch, both Demons cried out in disappointment.
Victor promised them something greater.
One day, he would create a garden for Zahal and Albu to protect.
In return, he wanted their knowledge.[2]
The twins immediately bowed even more deeply and declared themselves completely available for whatever orders he wished to give.
Victor subsequently created a new formal title specifically for the twins:
[[Elders]] of Hell.[2]
Their previous responsibility as Guardians would continue, but Victor expanded their purpose. Zahal and Albu would now preserve the complete historical record of the Dimension and ensure that the events shaping Hell were not lost with the deaths of individual generations.
Victor also recognized the political usefulness of this arrangement. Accurate records documenting the failures of previous Kings and the improvements made under his own reign would naturally shape how future Demons understood their history.[2]
The twins were additionally ordered to educate Helena, Vine and Vepar regarding Hell's ancient history, politics and institutional structure. Victor wanted his three Generals to govern the realm using knowledge that only beings who had personally lived through its earliest eras could provide.
Their position was placed directly beneath Victor himself. No other Demon possessed the authority to order the Elders unless Victor specifically delegated that right.[2]
The brothers responded together:
Victor's [[Will Solace|Will]] was their Will.

#### King Among Kings
Over the following centuries, Zahal and Albu fulfilled the responsibilities [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had assigned to them. They compiled books documenting Hell's history, rulers, wars, territories and physical development.
These texts became important enough that later generations of Demons actively read them. Even senior officials used their records when attempting to understand portions of Hell that predated the current regime.[3]
The value of the records became especially clear as Victor's forces explored increasingly distant regions of the Dimension. Helena observed that Hell appeared substantially larger than older descriptions suggested and used the books written by Zahal and Albu as historical evidence that the realm had continuously expanded over time.[3]
The [[Elders]] consequently serve not merely as historians but as the closest thing Hell possesses to living institutional memory.
Every modern Demon government can theoretically possess documents.
Zahal and Albu personally witnessed the events those documents describe.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Centuries later, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] returned to the Demon King's castle accompanied by [[Heavenly Father]], Ariel, Ophis, [[Nero Elderblood]] and [[Metis]].[1]
Zahal and Albu were still standing before the throne.
The moment they saw Victor, both immediately knelt.
Together they announced the return of:
His Majesty, the Tyrannical Demon King Victor Alucard.[1]
Victor casually asked how the two had been.
Zahal, the grey-skinned twin, answered on behalf of both brothers and attributed their condition to the extraordinary capabilities of their King.[1]
The fanaticism visible in their eyes was unmistakable.
The appearance of the two [[Elders]] immediately increased the caution of the Heavenly Father and Ariel.
Unlike Victor's daughters, the two Heavenly beings did not need introductions.
They already knew exactly what Zahal and Albu were.[1]
The narration states that the twins were as ancient as the Heavenly Father and Ariel, beings who had remained in Hell since it was merely barren terrain without buildings or recognizable civilization.
More importantly, they were regarded as equally dangerous or potentially even more dangerous than the previous Rulers of Hell.[1]
The Heavenly Father and Ariel had simply stopped accounting for them because Zahal and Albu had historically been so withdrawn from ordinary political affairs that they could remain almost completely unnoticed if they wished.
Seeing their complete devotion toward Victor changed the strategic calculation.
Two ancient beings who previously cared only about preserving Hell had become fanatics of Victor's rule.
Victor's internal assessment during Chapter 910 finally establishes a relatively precise level for the Elders.
Like the other Demons serving him, Zahal and Albu had become substantially stronger through Victor's intervention.
At this stage, both had reached the level of:
Fully developed Second Generation [[Primordial Gods]].[1]
Victor intended to strengthen them further in the future by granting them the Blessing of Destruction.
Once that occurred, he expected both Keepers to reach:
First Generation Primordial God-level.[1]
The narration consequently declares that they are, without doubt, the strongest Demons in Hell.[1]
The distinction is important: First Generation Primordial God-level is currently a future state dependent upon Victor giving them the Blessing, while their confirmed present level in Chapter 910 is that of fully developed Second Generation Primordial Gods.
Victor ordered the Elders to introduce themselves to his daughters.
Zahal spoke first, identifying himself by name.
Albu followed.
Together they declared themselves the Elders of Hell and formally welcomed Ophis, [[Nero]] and Metis to the Demon King's castle as Princesses.[1]
The title confused Nero and Metis for different reasons, but the twins considered the matter extremely simple. If Victor recognized someone as his daughter, the political and metaphysical society of Hell would recognize her as a Princess regardless of her race or biological origin.
Albu was the one who directly explained this principle to Nero, but Zahal displayed the same unquestioning acceptance.
Their loyalty had become so absolute that the Heavenly Father was reminded of Michael when looking at them.[1]
Victor then gave Ophis, Nero and Metis an important instruction.
If neither Victor nor their mothers were available, the girls were to seek help from Zahal and Albu.[1]
The order is significant because Victor's daughters are among the people he protects most obsessively in existence. Entrusting the Elders with their safety demonstrates an exceptional degree of personal trust.
Their current Second Generation Primordial God-level power also makes them suitable for that responsibility. Few beings inside ordinary Imperial territory could threaten Victor's daughters while both Keepers were present.
Victor marked his eldest daughters with a magical seal and formally charged them with helping protect their younger sisters, while Zahal and Albu remained among the emergency guardians available to the Imperial Princesses.[1]
Victor's use of the twins extended beyond family protection.
During the Heavenly Father's visit, Zahal and Albu were also positioned so that they could respond if Heaven's Creator suddenly became hostile inside Victor's territory.
Ariel recognized the intention immediately.[1]
The fact that Victor considered the twins viable security during a visit by the Heavenly Father is one of the clearest practical demonstrations of their importance. It does not mean either twin individually equals the Heavenly Father, but it confirms that Victor regards them as powerful enough to meaningfully participate in a contingency involving beings of the highest Biblical order.

### Relationship with Albu
Albu is Zahal's twin brother, fellow Keeper and constant companion. The two have existed together since their creation and have spent essentially the entirety of Hell's history performing the same fundamental duty.
Their supernatural synchronization is unusually strong. They frequently complete one another's explanations, speak the same declarations simultaneously and react almost identically to important events.[2]
Their identities nevertheless remain distinct. Zahal is consistently identified as the grey-skinned twin and commonly speaks first during their appearances, while Albu is red-skinned and sometimes provides the more detailed follow-up explanation.
Both possess the same current power classification and both are intended to receive [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s future Destruction Blessing.[1]
Neither has been canonically established as decisively stronger than the other.

### Relationship with Qliphoth
[[Qliphoth]], Earth's Negative World Tree, is Zahal's creator.[2]
The twins refer to the entity as their Mother and retain an extraordinary emotional attachment to her Energy despite the enormous passage of time since their creation.
When they detected similar Energy within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], the discovery became one of the principal reasons they treated him as fundamentally different from previous Demon Kings.
The manifestation of a living branch containing Negative and Life Energy affected both brothers so strongly that they stared at it with outright fanaticism.[2]
Victor's promise to eventually create a garden associated with their Mother became one of the earliest foundations of their personal devotion to him.

### Relationship with Victor Elderblood
Zahal's loyalty toward [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] represents a fundamental break from the Keepers' historical relationship with Demon Kings.
Before Victor, Zahal's loyalty was conditional.
The King served Hell.
The Keepers served Hell.
If the King endangered Hell, the twins' responsibility to the realm took priority.[2]
Victor earned something more.
His Energy reminded them of [[Qliphoth]], the throne independently recognized his authority, he immediately understood the broken Soul system, and he offered the Keepers both purpose and recognition beyond their ancient custodial role.
Victor did not dismiss them as relics.
He made them [[Elders]].
He gave them responsibility for Hell's history.
He placed them directly beneath himself.
He promised them living nature connected to their Mother.
And later, he made them vastly stronger.[2][1]
By Chapter 910, Zahal and Albu are no longer merely custodians serving a competent King.
They are fanatically loyal to Victor personally.
The [[Heavenly Father]] and Ariel notice the difference immediately.[1]
Victor reciprocates that loyalty with exceptional trust. He identifies the twins as beings his daughters should seek out for protection and intends to raise them even further through his Blessing of Destruction.

### Skills and Abilities
Zahal has not yet participated in a full direct combat scene, so the exact details of his individual fighting style remain largely unknown. Nevertheless, Chapter 674 establishes his superiority over [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s empowered Demon Generals through pressure alone, while Chapter 910 explicitly assigns him the combat level of a fully developed Second Generation Primordial God and identifies him and Albu as the strongest Demons in Hell.[2][1]

#### Ancient Demon Physiology
Ancient Demon Physiology: Zahal is an extraordinarily ancient Demon created directly by a Negative World Tree rather than produced through the conventional processes responsible for later Demonic populations.
- Immense Strength: At his current level, Zahal possesses physical power appropriate for a being comparable to a fully developed Second Generation Primordial God.[1]
- Immense Speed and Reflexes: His present power level implies supernatural movement and reactions vastly beyond ordinary Pillars and most conventional Divine beings.
- Immense Durability: Zahal's ancient body and current power allow him to withstand forces far beyond those survivable by ordinary Demons.
- Immense Stamina: His existence is designed for the permanent guardianship of Hell rather than mortal biological limitations.
- Extreme Longevity: Zahal existed before [[Lucifer]]'s fall and has survived from the barren earliest eras of Hell into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s modern Empire.[2][1]
- Agelessness: Zahal shows no indication of conventional biological aging.
- Miasma Affinity: As one of Hell's native primordial-age guardians, he naturally exists within the most concentrated Miasma environments of the Dimension without difficulty.
- Infernal Environmental Adaptation: Zahal can survive permanently in the deepest regions of Hell regardless of the hostile conditions that prevent most weaker beings from approaching.
- Demonic Regeneration: As one of the strongest Demons in existence, Zahal should possess the fundamental regenerative characteristics of his race to an exceptional degree, although the precise speed and limitations of his individual regeneration have not yet been demonstrated directly.
- Demonic [[Immortality]]: Demons possess unusual survival mechanics connected to Hell and their Souls. Zahal's exact interaction with normal Demonic resurrection has never been tested on-panel because he has not been killed.

#### World Tree-Created Existence
World Tree Creation: Zahal was directly created by [[Qliphoth|Earth's Negative World Tree]].[2]
This provides him with an intimate metaphysical connection to Negative World Tree Energy.
- Negativity Affinity: Zahal possesses an exceptionally strong natural affinity with Negative Energy.
- World Tree Energy Recognition: He immediately recognized similarities between [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Energy and the Energy belonging to his creator.[2]
- Life and Negativity Perception: Zahal could perceive both qualities inside the branch manifested by Victor and reacted specifically to their coexistence.[2]
- Creator Recognition: His connection to Qliphoth is profound enough that the presence of similar Energy inside Victor strongly influenced Zahal's recognition of him as True King.

#### Immense Supernatural Pressure
Supernatural Pressure: Zahal and Albu can release overwhelming pressure merely by opening their eyes and allowing their presence to spread through the surrounding environment.[2]
At their debut, the combined pressure immediately stopped [[Vine]], Helena and Vepar from moving forward despite all three already being among [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s most powerful Demon followers.
- Aura Projection: Zahal can project his supernatural presence across the surrounding area.
- Suppression: High-ranking Demons drastically weaker than him can be forced to halt or retreat beneath the pressure.
- Intimidation: His existence naturally produces extreme fear and caution among beings capable of perceiving the difference in power.
The exact proportion of the Chapter 674 pressure produced individually by Zahal rather than jointly with Albu has not been quantified.

#### Presence Concealment
Presence Suppression: Chapter 910 states that Zahal and Albu can remain extremely discreet whenever they wish.[1]
Their withdrawal from political affairs was so complete that even the [[Heavenly Father]] and Ariel effectively stopped accounting for them despite personally knowing who the twins were.
This should not automatically be interpreted as universal supernatural invisibility. What is confirmed is that two extremely ancient and dangerous Demons possess exceptional ability to remain withdrawn and suppress their relevance or presence enough to avoid the attention of the wider supernatural world.

#### Keeper Authority and Knowledge
Keeper of Hell: Zahal's oldest responsibility is ensuring that Hell remains functional.
- Dimensional Guardianship: Zahal monitors threats capable of damaging or destroying [[Biblical Hell]].[2]
- Protection Against Inept Kings: His duty explicitly includes opposing a Demon King whose actions threaten the existence of the Dimension.[2]
- Ruler Assessment: Zahal understands the distinction between merely possessing the title of King and properly performing the metaphysical duties of a Ruler.
- Soul-System Knowledge: He recognized that [[Diablo]]'s refusal to become Ruler had destabilized Hell's Soul cycle.[2]
- Afterlife Knowledge: Zahal understands Hell's role in Soul punishment and recycling as well as the consequences of failing to perform that role.
- Knowledge of the Regulators: He is aware of the interaction between Hell, [[The Judges of The Abyss]] and [[The Universal Tree]].
- Keys of Hell Knowledge: Zahal understood that the Keys retained by Diablo belonged to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] after the throne acknowledged him.
- Ancient Political Knowledge: Having existed throughout Hell's entire civilized history, Zahal possesses firsthand information concerning rulers and political systems that predate almost every surviving Demon.

#### Historical Record-Keeping
Historical Expertise: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] officially appointed Zahal and Albu as the historians and record-keepers of Hell.[2]
Their written records later became authoritative enough that Helena used them to determine that Hell itself had expanded beyond its historical dimensions.[3]
- Ancient History: Zahal has firsthand memories from eras before [[Lucifer]] entered Hell.
- Governmental History: He lived through the reigns and political changes associated with Lucifer, Lilith, [[Diablo]] and Victor.
- Dimensional Records: His books contain historical information concerning Hell's physical growth and geography.[3]
- Political Instruction: Zahal was ordered to teach [[Vine]], Vepar and Helena about Hell's political structure and history.[2]
- Institutional Memory: Zahal effectively serves as one of the living archives of [[Biblical Hell]].

#### Current Power Level
Second Generation Primordial God-Level: By Chapter 910, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s influence has strengthened Zahal and Albu until they are explicitly described as being at the level of fully developed Second Generation [[Primordial Gods]].[1]
This statement represents their current confirmed level at that point in the story.
The comparison is particularly notable because it occurs after the narration has already described the twins as dangerous enough to concern both the [[Heavenly Father]] and Ariel.
Strongest Demon in Hell: Chapter 910 explicitly states that Zahal and Albu are undoubtedly the strongest Demons in Hell at this stage.[1]
This places them above the ordinary Ars Goetia, Seven Deadly Sins and the surviving conventional Demon hierarchy in overall individual power by that point.

#### Future Destruction Blessing
Blessing of Destruction (Future): [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] intends to grant Zahal and Albu a Blessing associated with Destruction.[1]
According to Victor's assessment, receiving this Blessing will raise the twins from their current fully developed Second Generation Primordial God-level existence to:
First Generation Primordial God-level.[1]
The distinction must remain clear:
- Zahal is not yet First Generation Primordial God-level at the time of Chapter 910.
- The upgrade depends upon Victor granting a Blessing of Destruction in the future.
- His current confirmed level is fully developed Second Generation Primordial God-level.
The exact abilities granted by the Destruction Blessing have not yet been demonstrated for Zahal and should therefore not be invented before the transformation occurs on-page.

#### Combat Experience
Ancient Combat Experience: Zahal has existed for an incomprehensible period and served as one of the guardians responsible for stopping threats to Hell, including theoretically hostile Kings.
Although no extensive direct battle has yet been shown, this role strongly implies immense experience dealing with Demonic and [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]]-level threats.
High-Level Threat Response: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] trusts Zahal and Albu as emergency guardians for Ophis, [[Nero]] and [[Metis]] and keeps them close while potentially dangerous beings such as the [[Heavenly Father]] are present inside Hell.[1]

#### Resistances
Miasma Resistance: Zahal can exist indefinitely within the deepest regions of Hell and extremely concentrated Infernal Energy.
Extreme Environmental Resistance: His ancient physiology allows him to survive conditions lethal to weaker supernatural beings.
Supernatural Pressure Resistance: Zahal can stand normally within the presence of beings such as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and other extremely high-level entities without suffering the disabling effects experienced by weaker Demons.
Longevity Resistance: Time itself has little biological effect upon Zahal. He has survived from the primordial-age history of Hell into the modern era without showing ordinary deterioration.
The exact Soul, Conceptual, Holy and existence-erasure resistances possessed individually by Zahal have not yet been demonstrated sufficiently to assign them without speculation.

### Trivia
- Zahal is the dark-grey-skinned twin; Albu is the red-skinned twin.[2][1]
- Zahal normally introduces himself before Albu when the brothers formally identify themselves.[2][1]
- He and Albu were created by [[Qliphoth|Earth's Negative World Tree]], currently known as Qliphoth.[2]
- The older page incorrectly listed [[Yggdrasil]], Earth's Positive World Tree, as their creator.
- Zahal and Albu existed before [[Lucifer]] Morningstar fell from Heaven.[2]
- They were already present when Hell was still barren and lacked constructed civilization.[1]
- The twins are approximately as ancient as the [[Heavenly Father]] and Ariel.[1]
- Zahal personally called [[Diablo]] a fool for refusing to become a proper Ruler and damaging Hell's Soul system.[2]
- The Keepers' original loyalty belonged primarily to Hell rather than to whichever Demon occupied its throne.[2]
- A King who threatened the existence or proper function of Hell could be considered an inept King and lose their support.[2]
- Zahal was the twin who first told [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] that he was different from previous Kings, while Albu explained that their Mother's Energy flowed through him.[2]
- Both brothers became visibly emotional and fanatical after seeing Victor manifest living vegetation containing Negative and Life Energy inside Hell.[2]
- Victor promised to eventually create a garden for the twins to protect.[2]
- Victor personally created the title [[Elders]] of Hell for Zahal and Albu.[2]
- They are responsible for recording the history of Hell and educating Victor's Generals about the ancient structure of the Dimension.[2]
- Under Victor's system, no one besides Victor or a person explicitly authorized by him can command the Elders.[2]
- The historical books written by Zahal and Albu later became authoritative sources concerning the development and expansion of Hell.[3]
- The Heavenly Father and Ariel had effectively forgotten about the twins because Zahal and Albu historically avoided outside affairs and could remain extremely discreet.[1]
- Seeing the Elders' fanaticism toward Victor caused both Heavenly beings to reassess Victor's forces.[1]
- Their devotion reminded the Heavenly Father of Michael.[1]
- Chapter 910 explicitly states that Zahal and Albu are currently at the level of fully developed Second Generation [[Primordial Gods]].[1]
- The same chapter explicitly calls them the strongest Demons in Hell.[1]
- Victor intends to grant both twins a future Blessing of Destruction.[1]
- According to Victor, receiving that Blessing will raise the twins to First Generation Primordial God-level.[1]
- Consequently, the old infobox was broadly correct about the two power tiers but should distinguish current Second Generation level from future First Generation level.
- Victor instructs Ophis, [[Nero]] and [[Metis]] to seek help from Zahal and Albu if neither he nor their mothers are available.[1]
- This makes the two Elders emergency protectors of Victor's Imperial Princesses in addition to their original responsibility toward Hell.
- Victor also uses the twins as a security measure during the Heavenly Father's visit to his territory.[1]
- Their exact individual fighting techniques remain largely unknown because neither Zahal nor Albu has yet received an extended direct battle scene.
- The project CharacterNav correctly places Zahal and Albu in the dedicated Elders of Hell classification rather than grouping them with the 72 Dukes of Hell.
