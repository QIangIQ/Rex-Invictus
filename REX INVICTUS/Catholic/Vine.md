### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 650
Vine Elderblood, formerly known as King Vine and the 45th Key of Solomon, is a former Duke of Hell, one of [[Victor Elderblood]]'s original Demonic Generals, a former Demon King and later one of his wives. Prior to meeting Victor, Vine ruled thirty-eight Demonic Legions and possessed enough individual power and pride that [[Bael]] considered her one of the most arrogant Demons he had ever known. Her signature supernatural ability was the creation and control of storms, a power capable of devastating entire territories when used without restraint.[1]
Although Vine belonged to the Ars Goetia hierarchy, she was not a loyal member of [[Diablo]]'s faction during the Demonic War. She had instead been associated with [[Lilith Elderblood]] and was considered a "traitor" by Diablo's side, which was why she had not been selected as one of the Pillars responsible for Diablo's new conquests.[2] Her independence ended when Victor was forcibly banished into Hell and crossed her territory. Vine attacked him after initially mistaking him for an extraordinarily attractive High-Level Demon, only to watch Victor annihilate her Legions and several of her own children so completely that she abandoned her legendary arrogance and begged for her life.[1]
Victor spared Vine because she surrendered intelligently, possessed exceptional combat ability and wielded a power he considered useful. He subsequently offered her the one thing capable of appealing simultaneously to both her Pride and Lust: greater power in exchange for absolute allegiance. Vine accepted, swearing herself to Victor body and Soul. Victor then greatly strengthened her, transformed her appearance, nearly quadrupled her Energy reserves and formally abolished her old identity as a Pillar, declaring that she would henceforth be his General and his sword.[2]
Vine rapidly became one of the most fanatically loyal people in Victor's new Demonic order. Together with [[Vepar Elderblood]] and Helena Gremory Elderblood, she rose to the highest authority beneath the new King of Hell, eventually earning the title The [[Destroyer]] from the Demons themselves. While Helena became the principal strategist and Vepar oversaw logistics and administration, Vine became responsible for warfare, military training and the new Demonic armies.[3]
Her relationship with Victor ultimately ceased being merely one of King and General. By Chapter 888, Vine is openly recognized alongside Helena, Vepar and Lily as one of Victor's Demonic wives.[4] She later became a Blood Dragon and True Dragon, with her original Demonic nature and Storm affinity becoming part of her new Draconic existence.[5] Vine is currently a member of Clan Elderblood, [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]] and the [[Elderblood Empire]], remaining one of Victor's senior Demonic military figures long after the destruction of the political system in which she was originally born.

### Physical Description
Before being empowered by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Vine possessed an enormous and intimidating Demonic body standing approximately 2.5 meters tall. She had red skin, long black hair extending to approximately her buttocks, completely black sclerae surrounding blood-red irises, large Demonic horns, a tail and a massive pair of wings. She normally wore red armor and wielded a gigantic battle axe suitable for her unusual height and immense physical strength.[1][2]
Her appearance reflected the exaggerated physicality of a powerful Demon ruler. Vine possessed an extremely muscular body and gave the impression of a giant warrior rather than the more traditionally seductive appearance associated with Succubi. Even after Victor massacred her army, she remained physically imposing despite kneeling before him covered in wounds, several of which were sufficiently severe that her normal Demonic regeneration could not immediately repair them.[1]
Victor's empowerment dramatically changed her body. After accepting him as her Lord, red Energy entered Vine and reconstructed her physical form. Her height fell from approximately 2.5 meters to around 2 meters, but this reduction did not make her physically weaker. Her muscles instead became denser and more compact, her proportions became more refined, her horns, wings and tail became smaller and her overall appearance became considerably more beautiful.[2]
The transformation was therefore a qualitative compression rather than a reduction in power. Vine's enormous body had previously reflected the amount of Demonic Energy she possessed, while Victor's enhancement created a smaller form capable of containing almost four times her former Energy without the same physical bulk.[2]
After becoming a Blood Dragon, Vine's appearance evolved again. Like the other women transformed during Victor's mass creation of [[True Dragons]], her new species retained a small but important portion of her previous racial foundation rather than completely erasing it. Her modern form therefore remains connected to her Demonic characteristics and Storm affinity even though her fundamental race is now Draconic.[5]

### Personality and Traits
Vine was originally defined by overwhelming Pride. [[Bael]] considered her the most arrogant Demon he personally knew, noting that she barely restrained that arrogance even in front of Demons far above her Rank. The confidence was not entirely unjustified: Vine controlled thirty-eight Legions, possessed an unusual Storm ability and held enough personal strength to maintain Rank 45 in Hell's brutal political hierarchy.[1]
Her second dominant sin was Lust. These two impulses heavily influenced her first encounter with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Vine initially attacked because she found him extraordinarily attractive and mistook him for a powerful Demon whose appearance appealed directly to her desires. Once she began analyzing him more carefully, the combination of multiple Elemental abilities, Blood manipulation, Soul consumption, supernatural beauty and overwhelming Energy caused her to realize that the stranger was actually Alucard.[2]
Vine is nevertheless considerably more intelligent than her Pride initially suggests. She regarded herself as one of the more cunning Pillars and knew when continuing a battle would accomplish nothing except her own permanent death. When Victor annihilated her forces, she discarded pride almost immediately and begged for mercy. This was not a permanent collapse of her personality but an accurate recognition of the hierarchy in front of her: Victor had decisively proven himself stronger, and Vine saw no value in dying merely to preserve appearances.[1]
Her reaction to the death of her original army and children also demonstrates the alien morality of old Hell. Vine displayed essentially no grief when looking upon their corpses. Her primary reaction was disappointment at losing resources she had considered useful. This does not necessarily mean Vine is incapable of attachment; rather, the relationships she possessed before joining Victor were shaped by the extremely predatory Demonic culture in which strength and usefulness mattered more than conventional familial affection.[1]
That emotional structure changed significantly after Victor gave her something she valued more strongly than her previous position. When he offered Vine greater power in exchange for permanent service, she did not hesitate long. Her Pride became increasingly centered upon being one of Victor's Generals, while her Lust and attraction became focused almost entirely upon him. Immediately after receiving his power, she detected other Pillars spying upon them and reacted with intense anger that "worms" would dare observe her Lord.[2]
Victor deliberately refused to turn this loyalty into mindless obedience. When Vine swore herself to him, he made clear that he wanted a General capable of thinking rather than a stupid slave who merely followed orders. Vine subsequently demonstrated that she understood the distinction. She obeyed Victor with extreme devotion, but she continued independently judging recruits, developing tactics, identifying talent and making military decisions without waiting for instructions on every detail.[2]
Her encounter with Helena is one of the clearest examples. Vine arrived in the ruined Gremory territory intending to recruit Duke Gremory, discovered that he was dead and immediately reassessed the situation instead of treating the journey as wasted. She noticed Helena's trauma, intelligence, Gremory Bloodline and advanced Succubus Dream [[Magic]], correctly concluding that the young heiress possessed exceptional potential. Vine recruited her and even told Helena that, given enough time, she would probably become Vine's equal.[6]
Vine can therefore be surprisingly generous toward competence. She is enormously proud of her own strength but does not need every capable subordinate to remain beneath her forever. Helena's excessively proper behavior even amused her; Vine internally remarked that the traumatized half-Succubus behaved almost like an Angel despite living in Hell.[6]
What Vine is far less tolerant of is disrespect toward Victor. Her devotion eventually became infamous throughout Hell. The title The [[Destroyer]] was not formally invented by Victor; it emerged from the Demons themselves after observing her actions. In one notorious incident, a Demon openly insulted Victor and Vine personally traveled to his territory and destroyed the entire region with her storms. Such incidents established that criticizing the new King within reach of his Generals could have consequences extending far beyond the person responsible.[3]
Despite the extremity of her loyalty, Vine does not appear embarrassed by it. Victor offered her greater power, recognized her usefulness, elevated her beyond her old Pillar status and eventually treated her as family. From Vine's Demonic perspective, serving the strongest and most rewarding King she had ever encountered was not degradation but one of the greatest sources of Pride in her life.

### History

#### Ancient Hell
Vine's complete early history has not yet been explored in detail. She had nevertheless existed long enough to become one of the recognized Keys of Solomon and acquire the title King Vine. By the time of the Demonic War, she occupied Rank 45 and commanded thirty-eight Legions of Demons.[1]
Unlike many Pillars supporting [[Diablo]]'s expansion, Vine was politically associated with [[Lilith Elderblood]]. Chapter 651 specifically identifies her as a "traitor" allied with Lilith from the perspective of Diablo's faction, explaining why she was not one of the Pillars selected for Diablo's new conquest operations.[2]
This distinction is important to her later history. Vine was a member of the Ars Goetia hierarchy, but membership in the Keys of Solomon did not mean that every Pillar personally considered Diablo their sovereign. She later makes this explicit when Helena assumes they already serve the same King and Vine responds dismissively that Diablo is not her Lord.[6]
At some point before meeting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Vine had also produced multiple children. Their identities, fathers and exact number are never established. Some of these children remained among the forces surrounding her at the time Victor entered her territory.[1]

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s entry into Hell was not voluntary. During the conflict involving the Amazons, he was removed from the Human World and forcibly transported into [[Biblical Hell]] as part of a larger attempt to isolate and eventually kill him. Rather than treating Hell as a prison, Victor immediately began using the realm as a gigantic source of enemies, knowledge, Souls and combat experience.
One of the first major rulers to encounter him was Vine.
Vine entered the confrontation commanding thirty-eight Legions and initially failed to understand what kind of Being had appeared within her territory. Victor destroyed her military forces with overwhelming efficiency, leaving an enormous number of Demonic corpses scattered across the battlefield. Among those killed were several of Vine's own children.[1]
The defeat was so complete that the famously arrogant Demon King was eventually reduced to her knees. Her body was covered in wounds, several of which refused to regenerate properly, and tears ran down her face as she begged the unknown High-Level Demon to spare her life.[1]
When Victor asked for her identity, Vine introduced herself as Rank 45 among the Keys of Solomon and admitted that she had commanded thirty-eight Legions before Victor destroyed them. [[Bael]], observing the confrontation through supernatural means, could barely believe the sight because the woman describing herself as "humble" was normally one of the most arrogantly prideful beings in Hell.[1]
Victor did not spare her out of compassion alone. He identified three practical reasons: Vine possessed considerable combat ability, her power to create and control storms interested him, and she had demonstrated enough intelligence to surrender quickly once continuing resistance became obviously pointless.[1]
Her survival became one of the earliest examples of the policy Victor later applied throughout Hell. Useful Demons willing to submit did not need to die merely because they had initially fought him.
Vine did not immediately understand Victor's identity. While observing his Blood abilities and the protective Blood structure surrounding the battlefield, she gradually connected the evidence: multiple Elemental Powers, Blood manipulation, Soul consumption and supernatural beauty. She finally realized that the "Demon" who had destroyed her forces was actually Alucard.[2]
This realization should have terrified her, but the fear rapidly mixed with fascination. Demons grow by accumulating Energy, often through violently taking it from others, and Victor's presence inside Hell was saturated with an extraordinary quantity of Negative Energy. Vine's Lust and Pride consequently reacted not merely to his appearance but to the sheer potential represented by his existence.[2]
Victor asked Vine what she wanted most.
Her answer was simple: Power.
Victor offered it to her in exchange for complete allegiance—her body, Soul and future service. Vine formally swore herself as King Vine, Rank 45 among the Solomon Keys, to eternal obedience and undying loyalty toward Victor.[2]
Victor accepted the oath and poured his own Energy into her.
The result completely transformed Vine. Her approximately 2.5-meter body shrank to around two meters while becoming denser, more refined and dramatically more powerful. Her horns, tail and wings decreased in size, her beauty increased and the total Energy contained inside her body rose to nearly four times its former level.[2]
The change was so extreme that other Pillars observing the scene were stunned. Vine herself immediately understood that the power Victor had given her exceeded what she could have realistically acquired through ordinary Demonic advancement in any short period.
Victor then completed the symbolic transformation of her status. Using Ice, he created an enormous battle axe specifically suited to Vine's fighting style and physique. Vine judged the weapon sufficiently durable that beings significantly weaker than Victor would have extreme difficulty damaging it.[2]
He informed Vine that she was no longer one of the ordinary Demonic Pillars.
From that moment, she was his General and his sword.
Victor also clarified what kind of service he expected. He did not want blind obedience devoid of thought; Vine was expected to think, adapt, command and make useful decisions for herself while remaining unquestionably loyal to him.[2]
Her first great assignment reflected that expectation. Victor ordered Vine to bring the territories of Pillars Rank 46 through Rank 60 under his authority within less than six months. How she accomplished the mission was largely irrelevant: killing, negotiation, bribery, blackmail, intimidation and political manipulation were all acceptable so long as the territories eventually belonged to him.[2]
Vine departed with enthusiasm.
Vine carried out Victor's order with far greater speed than the six-month limit suggested. The Pride possessed by many ancient Pillars made peaceful submission difficult, forcing her to eliminate numerous rulers rather than recruit them. Their populations and surviving forces were subsequently absorbed into Victor's growing army whenever possible.[6][7]
Her campaign eventually brought her to the territory historically belonging to Duke Gremory. Vine was disgusted by what she found. A city once considered beautiful had deteriorated into near-slum conditions and the palace itself was collapsing, making the conquest feel beneath the glory of the Lord she now represented.[6]
Inside the ruins she encountered Helena, Gremory's surviving daughter. Vine explained that she had come to have Gremory swear allegiance to her Lord, while Helena initially assumed both women already served [[Diablo]]. Vine immediately corrected her: Diablo was not her Lord.[6]
Helena then revealed that Gremory was permanently dead.
Rather than leave, Vine began assessing the heiress. Helena possessed obvious emotional trauma, but Vine quickly recognized intelligence, a powerful Gremory Bloodline capable of manipulating mass and advanced talent with Succubus Dream [[Magic]]. She concluded that Helena had far more future value than the ruined territory surrounding her.[6]
Vine consequently offered Helena a place beneath Victor.
Helena accepted and joined the campaign. Her strategic abilities immediately complemented Vine's overwhelming combat strength. During the conquest of additional Pillar territories, Helena could manipulate the mass of thrown stones to devastate enemies while Vine unleashed her storms, allowing them to repeat successful tactics while minimizing unnecessary losses.[6]
Vine also displayed an unexpectedly encouraging attitude toward her new subordinate. When Helena insisted upon formally addressing her as "Lady Vine", Vine told her that someone with Helena's talent would probably become her equal eventually and did not need to maintain excessive formality forever.[6]
By the time Vine returned to Victor, the territories from Rank 46 through Rank 60 had effectively been eliminated, conquered or brought beneath his control. Helena accompanied her back as one of the most valuable new recruits produced by the campaign.[7]
Victor immediately put Helena's strategic claims to the test, giving her only 50,000 Lesser Demons and Vine while ordering her to capture the territory of the 39th Pillar without losing a single soldier. Vine therefore transitioned from being Helena's recruiter into the principal military asset through which the new strategist could prove herself.[7]
Vine remained at Victor's side as his campaign advanced into increasingly dangerous regions of Biblical Hell. By Chapter 673, she, Helena and Vepar visibly occupied the three highest positions immediately beneath Victor himself.[8]
Their authority was not based solely upon favoritism. Victor had directly empowered all three, raising their combat strength to a level compared with the former Top 10 Pillars, and all three had independently demonstrated competence worthy of the positions they received.[8]
Vine's familiarity with Hell remained useful as Victor approached the territory normally reserved for the highest-ranked Pillars and the King himself. When they discovered that the great gate leading toward the lower levels had been sealed, Vine immediately recognized the action as an unusual defensive measure likely taken by the Top 10 Pillars' subordinates in response to Victor's advance.[8]
The supposedly impossible obstacles protecting the old political hierarchy did not survive Victor. The conquest culminated in his seizure of the throne and recognition as the new King of Biblical Hell, making Vine one of the first high-ranking Demons to have correctly chosen the winning side.
The conquest of Hell did not end Vine's importance. Victor immediately began dismantling the ancient political structure and replacing it with a meritocratic government capable of functioning without relying entirely upon hereditary Pillar Rank.[3]
Vine, Vepar and Helena became the three central Generals beneath the King. The titles attached to them arose organically among the Demonic population:
Vine the [[Destroyer]], Vepar the Proud, and Helena the Strategist.[3]
Vine earned her title through both battlefield performance and her extreme response to threats against Victor's authority. In one of the incidents responsible for her reputation, a Demon insulted Victor. Vine personally visited the offender's territory and unleashed her Storm abilities with such violence that she devastated the region rather than limiting punishment to a single individual.[3]
Her fanaticism became sufficiently notorious that Demons quickly learned to be careful when discussing their new King around his Generals.
Victor nevertheless gave Vine responsibilities extending beyond personal retaliation. Vine and four commanders selected beneath her became responsible for warfare and the training of the new Demonic forces, effectively making her one of the primary military heads of the reformed Hell. Vepar's branch focused upon logistics and administration, while Helena's branch handled internal and external policy.[3]
The division reflected each General's strongest qualities. Vine had spent her old life commanding thirty-eight Legions and personally possessed overwhelming destructive strength; under Victor, those traits were transformed from the assets of an independent Pillar into the foundation of a centralized military institution.
The three Generals also possessed enough standing to advise or question Victor when circumstances required it. Victor wanted competent subordinates rather than silent worshippers, even though the personal loyalty Vine and the others developed often approached fanaticism.[3]
When Victor eventually returned to the main battlefield of the Demonic War, Vine accompanied him alongside the military forces of his newly conquered Hell. The appearance of the transformed former Pillars shocked Diablo and his remaining forces because Vine, Vepar and Helena had all become vastly more powerful than they had been before Victor entered the realm.
Vine's allegiance was no longer ambiguous. She entered the war as one of Victor's Generals and helped dismantle the remaining elite structure of Diablo's army.
During the final collapse of Diablo's faction, Helena and Lily dealt with Bael while Vine and Vepar slaughtered the remaining elite Demons surrounding him. Victor subsequently converted the defeated enemies into spheres of blood so that they could be consumed.[9]
Helena, Vine and Vepar approached Victor together, knelt and presented the recovered blood to their King. Victor praised their work as impeccable, causing all three women to display obvious pride and satisfaction at his approval.[9]
The scene represented the complete reversal of Vine's original position. The Demon King who had once knelt before Victor begging him not to kill her now knelt voluntarily as one of the senior commanders responsible for destroying the political order that had ruled Hell before his arrival.
Following the Demonic War, Vine remained one of the principal figures responsible for transforming Biblical Hell from an ancient collection of competing Demon territories into a centralized civilization beneath Victor.
While Victor himself devoted considerable time to personal training and matters beyond ordinary administration, he repeatedly acknowledged that the enormous changes throughout Hell were produced through the work of his Generals and other subordinates. Vine, Helena, Vepar and later Lily formed the military and governmental leadership capable of translating the King's overwhelming personal authority into functional institutions.[9]
Vine's old experience commanding Legions became increasingly valuable in the process. Rather than controlling thirty-eight Legions primarily for her own status, she now helped organize, train and deploy forces belonging to a much larger Demonic state.
Her political position also changed fundamentally. She was no longer "King Vine" because of the territory belonging to Rank 45; she was General Vine because Victor had personally selected her as one of the people through whom the new Hell would operate.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
By the time [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] expanded [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]] into the territories formerly belonging to Egypt, Vine's relationship with him had become openly personal rather than purely military.
In Chapter 888, a red portal opened into the former Egyptian territory and several Demonic Legions arrived together with Helena, Vine, Lily, Lilith and Vepar. [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood explicitly referred to the women as Victor's Demonic wives and told them that excessive formal behavior toward her was unnecessary because they shared the same husband.[4]
Vine and the other Generals nevertheless had immediate military responsibilities. The former Egyptian territory was receiving enormous numbers of migrants and supernatural populations, creating the possibility of disorder. Vine considered the presence of the Demonic Legions necessary to establish control, reinforce authority and discourage anyone from exploiting the transitional period.[4]
She consequently descended with her commanders and soldiers to begin securing the territory, demonstrating that marriage to Victor did not remove the military role that had originally earned her place beside him.
Her relationship with Victor was further demonstrated around Chapter 898, where Vine belonged to the group of women already treated as part of his intimate household rather than a distant subordinate.[10]
Vine's greatest racial evolution occurred during Victor's mass transformation of compatible members of his family into [[Blood Dragons]].
Victor did not simply erase the original race of everyone undergoing the transformation. A portion of each individual's former racial essence, experience and characteristics was intentionally preserved so that the resulting [[True Dragons]] would remain distinct rather than becoming identical copies of one another.[5]
During the third wave of transformations, Vine entered the process alongside [[Maria Elderblood]], [[Roberta Elderblood]], [[Bruna Elderblood]], [[Eve Elderblood]], [[Mizuki Elderblood]], [[Lily Elderblood]], Helena Gremory Elderblood, [[Vepar Elderblood]] and [[Aline Elderblood Valefar]].[5]
Vine emerged as a True Dragon descended from Victor's Blood Dragon lineage. Her Demonic essence and longstanding affinity with Storms remained part of the foundation of her new existence, explaining her classification as a Demonic/Storm-oriented Dragon within the family rather than a completely generic member of the species.
The transformation massively increased her potential and connected her to the natural qualities of True Dragons, including their extraordinary bodies, immense Energy, instinctive affinity with Creation and ability to understand the language of Dragons.

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Vine remained integrated into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family as his civilization transitioned from [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]] into the much larger [[Elderblood Empire]]. Her original role as one of the rulers of Hell evolved naturally into the Imperial era because the Demonic territories and military forces she had helped reorganize became part of Victor's greater political structure.
By this stage Vine's identity had changed completely from the independent Rank 45 Pillar introduced in Chapter 650. She was simultaneously a True Dragon, Elderblood wife, senior Demon leader and one of the veteran Generals whose loyalty predated Victor's formal assumption of many of his later Imperial titles.
Her relationship with Victor and her place among his wives eventually became incorporated into the metaphysical family structure surrounding Clan Elderblood and its Soul-based marriage bonds.[11]
Vine's military significance also remained symbolically important. She was among the earliest powerful enemies Victor ever turned into a truly loyal commander simply by proving himself stronger, recognizing her usefulness and offering her a path to become more than the position she already occupied.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
Vine's relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] began through violence. She attacked him as an unknown intruder, lost nearly everything she had brought into the battle and eventually begged him for survival. What could have remained a relationship based entirely upon fear changed when Victor recognized that Vine possessed traits worth cultivating rather than simply consuming.[1]
Victor understood Vine's psychology remarkably quickly. Her two dominant sins were Pride and Lust, and instead of attempting to remove those qualities he redirected them. Vine's Pride became attached to the privilege of serving a King she regarded as incomparable, while her Lust became focused upon Victor himself. Most importantly, he gave her the Power she openly admitted she desired more than anything else.[2]
The relationship therefore developed from an explicitly transactional beginning into extreme personal loyalty. Vine did not merely obey Victor because he could kill her. Within a short period she took personal offense when others watched him disrespectfully, later destroyed territory because its ruler insulted him and showed visible happiness whenever Victor praised her performance.[2][3][9]
Victor likewise came to trust Vine with responsibilities that brute strength alone could not justify. He made her a General, placed her over part of Hell's military structure and allowed her to recruit and train commanders beneath herself. His original instruction that she remain capable of independent thought instead of becoming a mindless servant shaped the military leader Vine later became.[2][3]
By Chapter 888 their connection had developed into marriage, with [[Aphrodite]] explicitly recognizing Vine among Victor's Demonic wives.[4] Vine was later transformed into a Blood Dragon and incorporated completely into Clan Elderblood, turning what began as the submission of an enemy ruler into a permanent familial relationship.

#### Helena Gremory Elderblood
Vine personally recruited Helena and was therefore responsible for bringing one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s most important later strategists into his faction. Their first meeting also reveals that Vine was capable of recognizing ability in others without feeling threatened by future competition.
Helena initially maintained extreme formality toward Vine, but Vine believed her intelligence and talents would eventually allow her to become an equal. The prediction proved correct when both women later stood with Vepar as Victor's three highest-ranking original Generals.[6][8]
Their skills also complemented one another particularly well. Helena specialized in strategic planning while Vine provided overwhelming battlefield power and the Storm abilities necessary to execute those strategies on a massive scale.

#### Vepar Elderblood
Vepar became Vine's fellow General and one of the women most consistently associated with her throughout the conquest and reconstruction of Hell. Whereas Vine represented the military arm of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s new state, Vepar became central to logistics and administration.[3]
The pair frequently fought together and later jointly eliminated the remaining elites of [[Diablo]]'s army during the final battle.[9] They subsequently became fellow wives and [[True Dragons]] within Clan Elderblood.

#### Lilith Elderblood
Vine was politically allied with Lilith before meeting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and was consequently considered a traitor by [[Diablo]]'s faction.[2]
The precise details of Vine's original relationship with Lilith remain largely unexplored, but the connection is important because it explains why Vine was not one of Diablo's loyal Pillars despite occupying a recognized position within the Ars Goetia hierarchy.
After both women eventually became members of Victor's faction and family, their previous political distinctions lost most of their significance.

#### Skills and Abilities
Vine is an exceptionally powerful former Demon Pillar who became one of [[Victor Elderblood]]'s original Generals and later evolved into a Blood Dragon and True Dragon. Even before meeting Victor, she was the 45th Key of Solomon, commanded thirty-eight Demonic Legions and possessed an unusual supernatural affinity that allowed her to create and control storms. [[Bael]] specifically attributed much of Vine's legendary arrogance to the considerable power she possessed even among high-ranking Demons.[1]
After Vine swore permanent allegiance to Victor, he directly infused her with his own Energy. The process healed her damaged body, restructured her physique into a smaller but significantly denser form and increased the amount of Energy inside her to nearly four times what she had possessed previously.[2] By the time Victor reached the deepest levels of Hell, Vine, Helena and Vepar were explicitly described as having become comparable in overall power to the former Top 10 Pillars of Hell.[3]
Her later transformation into a Blood Dragon elevated her existence again, granting the physiology, instincts and potential of a True Dragon while retaining characteristics originating from her former Demonic nature and Storm affinity.[4]

##### Blood Dragon / True Dragon Physiology
Blood Dragon Physiology: Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s large-scale transformation of his family, Vine became one of his [[Blood Dragons]] and consequently a genuine True Dragon. Her original Demonic traits were not simply discarded; Victor deliberately allowed aspects of an individual's previous nature to remain incorporated into the resulting Dragon, leaving Vine as a Dragon whose identity remains heavily associated with Demonic Energy and Storms.[4]
As a True Dragon, Vine possesses an existence vastly superior to her former high-ranking Demon physiology. The following characteristics are racial abilities derived from her current species even when the novel has not yet provided Vine with an individual demonstration of every application.
- Immense Strength: [[True Dragons]] possess extraordinary physical power without requiring conventional reinforcement. Vine's physical abilities greatly exceed those she possessed as the 45th Pillar.
- Immense Speed and Reflexes: Her Draconic physiology provides supernatural movement speed, reactions and combat perception.
- Immense Durability: Vine's body is vastly denser and more resistant than that of ordinary supernatural species, allowing her to withstand tremendous physical and Energy-based attacks.
- Enhanced Stamina: Her Draconic body can sustain prolonged combat and extremely high supernatural output.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Blood Dragons possess extremely powerful regeneration. Vine had already possessed Demonic regeneration before her transformation, and becoming a Blood Dragon further elevated the durability and recovery of her entire existence.
- Extreme Longevity / [[Immortality]]: Vine was already an ancient Demon who did not suffer ordinary mortal aging. Her current True Dragon physiology further removes her from normal biological lifespan limitations.
- Enhanced Senses: Vine possesses highly developed supernatural sight, hearing, Energy perception and battlefield awareness. Even during her Demonic era she could sense powerful observers watching her from afar after Victor empowered her.[2]
- Immense Energy Reserves: True Dragons naturally possess enormous quantities of supernatural Energy. This builds upon the nearly fourfold increase Vine had already received from Victor before becoming a Dragon.[2]
- Natural Flight: Vine possessed Demonic wings before becoming a True Dragon and retains aerial capability through her current supernatural physiology.
- Shapeshifting: True Dragons can alter their forms and assume humanoid appearances despite possessing enormously larger true bodies.
- Dragon Form: Vine possesses a genuine True Dragon form corresponding to her Demonic and Storm-based characteristics. The precise maximum dimensions of her individual Dragon Form have not been explicitly stated.
- Draconic Natural Weapons: In her Dragon state, Vine possesses the natural claws, fangs, scales, wings and other biological weapons associated with True Dragons.
- Dragon Breath: True Dragons possess racial breath attacks. Vine therefore possesses the underlying capability, although the exact element or special property of her individual breath has not yet received a detailed canonical demonstration.
- Draconic Presence: True Dragons naturally exert enormous supernatural pressure upon weaker creatures simply through the quality of their existence.
- Superior Soul and Existence: Transformation into a True Dragon modifies more than the physical body. Vine's Soul and overall metaphysical existence were elevated alongside her physiology.[4]

###### Draconic Runes
Draconic Runes: As a True Dragon, Vine possesses the natural connection to the Draconic Language inherited by members of the species. Draconic Runes are not merely a foreign magical writing system to a True Dragon; their meaning is innately comprehensible because the language is fundamentally connected to Draconic existence.
This grants Vine the potential to employ Draconic Runes for supernatural effects, although the novel has not yet shown her using an individually named Runic spell or technique. The page therefore should not assign specific Runes to Vine until she personally demonstrates them.

##### Former High-Ranking Demon Physiology
Demon Physiology: Before becoming a Blood Dragon, Vine was already one of the Ars Goetia and possessed the racial attributes of an ancient High-Level Demon to a far greater degree than ordinary Demonic inhabitants of Hell.[1]
Many of these characteristics form part of the supernatural foundation preserved within her modern Demonic Dragon existence rather than simply disappearing after her racial transformation.
- Superhuman Strength: Vine's enormous original physique and Rank 45 status already granted considerable physical strength before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] empowered her.
- Superhuman Speed and Reflexes: As a Pillar-level Demon, she possessed reaction and movement speed vastly above Lesser and ordinary High-Level Demons.
- Superhuman Durability: Vine survived Victor's initial assault despite suffering extensive injuries severe enough to suppress portions of her normal regeneration.[1]
- Enhanced Stamina: Vine could personally participate in prolonged campaigns involving the conquest of multiple Pillar territories.
- Demonic Regeneration: Her original Demon body could naturally repair severe physical injuries, although Victor demonstrated that sufficiently powerful damage could overwhelm this healing.[1]
- Demonic Longevity: Vine was already an ancient supernatural being before becoming a Dragon.
- Miasma Manipulation: As a native high-ranking Demon of Hell, Vine possessed natural compatibility with Miasma and could use Infernal Energy to reinforce her supernatural abilities.
- Miasma Resistance: Vine can exist normally within regions of Hell containing concentrations of Demonic Energy that would be dangerous or fatal to many non-Demonic races.
- Demonic Wings: Her original body possessed a large pair of wings that later became smaller when Victor condensed and refined her physique.[2]
- Demonic Horns and Tail: Vine naturally possesses the characteristic supernatural appendages associated with her Demonic body.
- Supernatural Energy Perception: Vine can perceive supernatural beings, Energy signatures and hostile observation. Immediately after Victor's empowerment, she became aware of multiple Demon Pillars remotely watching the area.[2]
Demonic Resurrection (Former Racial Trait): Powerful Demons whose bodies are destroyed outside their native Hell can under certain conditions return through the Demonic afterlife system so long as their Souls remain intact. Vine never personally required this process, and her later transformation into a Blood Dragon altered her fundamental species; therefore, it should not be assumed that her current resurrection mechanics remain identical to those of an ordinary Pillar Demon.

##### Victor's Empowerment
Energy Empowerment: Upon accepting Vine's oath of allegiance, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] directly fed her his own Energy and radically evolved her body.[2]
Unlike a temporary combat enhancement, the change permanently altered Vine's baseline.
- Nearly Fourfold Energy Increase: Vine's total Energy reserves became almost four times greater than before the transformation.[2]
- Physical Reconstruction: Victor's Energy healed Vine's existing wounds and rebuilt her body into a more refined state.[2]
- Body Compression: Her height decreased from roughly 2.5 meters to approximately 2 meters while her body and muscles became dramatically denser. The reduction in physical size therefore represented increased efficiency rather than diminished strength.[2]
- Physical Enhancement: Vine's strength, durability, speed and overall supernatural performance increased together with her Energy.
- Ability Enhancement: Her existing Storm and Demonic abilities benefited from the vastly greater quantity and quality of Energy available to her.
- Top-Pillar-Level Growth: By Chapter 673, Vine, Helena and Vepar were directly compared to the former Top 10 Pillars after receiving Victor's power and proving themselves worthy of their positions.[3]
- Evolution: Other Demons explicitly referred to what happened to Vine as an "evolution", indicating that Victor's intervention altered more than her raw Energy quantity.[5]

##### Tempestakinesis
Storm Manipulation: Vine's signature supernatural ability is the power to create and control storms. [[Bael]] specifically identifies this ability as one of the principal reasons Vine was powerful enough to justify her extreme arrogance even before meeting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[1]
The Storm ability operates on a large enough scale to function as both a battlefield-control power and a weapon of territorial destruction.

###### Storm Creation
Vine can generate supernatural storms rather than merely manipulating naturally occurring weather.[1]
The complete environmental requirements have never been stated, but nothing in her demonstrated uses suggests she requires a pre-existing storm before activating the ability.

###### Storm Manipulation
Once a storm exists, Vine can direct and intensify it as an offensive supernatural phenomenon. Her control is sufficient for her storms to overwhelm armies and devastate populated territories.
During her campaigns through Hell, Vine regarded ordinary Demonic armies as insignificant compared with the destructive potential of her Storm ability.[6]

###### Large-Scale Weather Manipulation
Vine's power can affect an entire territorial region rather than being confined to the immediate area surrounding her.
Her most famous demonstration occurred after a Demon openly disrespected [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Vine personally traveled to the offender's domain and submerged the entire territory beneath storms, completely destroying it. The scale and brutality of the retaliation directly produced her title "The [[Destroyer]]".[7]

###### Area Destruction
Vine can use storms as a mass-destruction ability rather than merely atmospheric interference. The destruction of an entire Demon territory demonstrates that her power is suitable for large-scale warfare and the elimination of fortified positions.[7]

###### Battlefield Control
The combination of severe winds, precipitation, atmospheric turbulence and the destructive Energy contained within Vine's supernatural storms allows her to radically alter the conditions of a battlefield.
The novel has not individually demonstrated every possible component of Vine's Storms as a separate technique. Therefore, abilities such as unrestricted independent [[Electrokinesis]], Aerokinesis or Hydrokinesis should not automatically be listed as separate powers unless later chapters explicitly show Vine manipulating those phenomena outside the context of her Storm ability.

###### Storm Affinity
Vine's association with Storms predates [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and represents a fundamental element of her original supernatural nature rather than a technique she learned later.[1]
Because Victor's Blood Dragon transformation preserves significant characteristics from the subject's prior race and abilities, Vine's Storm nature remained an important aspect of her current Draconic identity.[4]

##### Authority Detection Evasion
Metaphysical Concealment / Authority Detection Evasion: Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s first evolution of Vine, [[Bael]] discovered that his unique Rank-1 Authority could no longer determine her location anywhere in Hell.[5]
As the King of the Pillars, Bael possessed the privileged ability to know the location of every other Ars Goetia Pillar. This Authority had previously allowed him to detect [[Sitri]] invading his territory and was known only to Bael and [[Diablo]].[5]
After Vine's evolution, however, she completely disappeared from that supernatural detection system. Bael described the situation as though Vine had been hidden from his senses and confirmed that the phenomenon had persisted ever since Victor altered her.[5]
- Rank-1 Authority Evasion: Bael's supernatural Pillar-location Authority could no longer identify Vine's position.[5]
- Persistent Concealment: The effect continued while Vine carried out Victor's campaign against Pillars Rank 46–60.[5]
- Evolution-Linked Effect: Bael specifically connected the disappearance to Vine's transformation under Victor.[5]
- Unknown Mechanism: The chapter never establishes whether Vine consciously activates this effect, whether Victor intentionally granted it, or whether it emerged automatically because she was no longer metaphysically recognized as an ordinary Pillar.
For that reason, this ability should not be written as unrestricted invisibility or universal concealment. What is canonically demonstrated is that Vine became undetectable to Bael's specific Rank-1 tracking Authority.

##### Enhanced Perception
Supernatural Detection: After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s empowerment, Vine detected powerful entities observing her and Victor remotely even though she had previously been too distracted to notice them.[2]
This indicates enhanced awareness of supernatural observation and Energy signatures, although its absolute range and ability to penetrate deliberate concealment are unknown.
Combat Senses: Vine possesses enough supernatural perception to follow extremely high-level battles, detect changes in enemy pressure and recognize when remaining near a battlefield has become too dangerous.[8][9]

##### Weapons
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Ice Battle Axe: After empowering Vine, Victor personally created an enormous battle axe made from his supernatural Ice and presented it to her as the weapon appropriate for his new General.[2]
Vine continued using the axe during her campaign against the lower-ranked Pillars.[6]
- Extreme Durability: Vine believed the weapon to be sufficiently durable that opponents substantially weaker than Victor would have extreme difficulty damaging it.[6]
- Heavy Weapon Mastery: Vine was already accustomed to wielding gigantic axes before receiving Victor's weapon, indicating considerable proficiency with large two-handed melee weapons.[1]
- Enhanced Striking Power: Combined with Vine's immense physical strength, the axe gives her an extremely powerful close-range offensive option when large-scale Storm attacks are unnecessary or undesirable.

##### Combat Abilities
Close-Quarters Combat: Vine is an experienced ancient Demon combatant who maintained her own Pillar territory and commanded dozens of Legions before joining [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
Axe Mastery: Her preferred physical weapon is a massive battle axe, which she uses in conjunction with her strength and supernatural abilities.[1][6]
Large-Scale Warfare: Vine's combat style is particularly suitable for confronting armies and conquering territories because Storm Manipulation can affect enormous areas simultaneously.
High-Level Demon Combat Experience: Vine personally eliminated or defeated multiple Pillar rulers while conquering Ranks 46–60 on Victor's behalf.[6][10]

##### Military and Leadership Skills
Generalship: Vine commanded 38 Legions of Demons before meeting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and therefore possessed extensive experience directing large military forces even before becoming one of his Generals.[1]
After Victor restructured Hell, she became one of the three principal Generals of his new government and was specifically placed in charge of warfare and the training of Demonic forces.[7]
Army Command: Vine can coordinate enormous numbers of Demons and later maintained authority over commanders and Legions serving the new King of Hell.
Military Training: Her branch of Victor's reformed government became directly responsible for developing and training Hell's new armies.[7]
Territorial Conquest: Victor's first major order to Vine required her to bring Pillars Rank 46–60 under his control through whatever combination of battle, coercion, diplomacy or manipulation proved effective.[2] Vine progressed through the assignment vastly faster than the six-month deadline she had been given.
Threat Assessment: Vine is capable of recognizing when a battlefield has become strategically unsustainable. During Victor's battle with War, she correctly concluded that the increasing level of destruction endangered nearby allies and ordered the surrounding Demons to withdraw and reorganize rather than remain uselessly exposed.[11]
Talent Recognition: Vine quickly recognized Helena as an exceptional strategist and powerful potential recruit instead of dismissing her because of Gremory's fallen political status.[6]

##### Resistances
Miasma Resistance: As a former native Demon of Hell, Vine possesses extremely high resistance to Infernal Miasma and environments saturated with Negative Energy.
Extreme Environmental Resistance: Her current True Dragon body can survive hostile supernatural environments and pressures far beyond the limits of ordinary Demons.
Physical Damage Resistance: Vine's Blood Dragon physiology grants enormous resistance to conventional physical trauma.
Energy Resistance: The exceptional density and Energy of True Dragon bodies grants greater resistance to supernatural Energy attacks than Vine possessed during her Pillar era.
Soul Durability: Her True Dragon transformation elevated the quality of her overall existence, including her Soul, making her spiritually far more difficult to damage than ordinary supernatural creatures.[4]

#### Former Abilities and Characteristics
The following characteristics originated primarily from Vine's former identity as the 45th Pillar. They are historically useful distinctions, but her current Demonic/Storm Dragon existence incorporates much of their underlying supernatural foundation rather than simply losing everything associated with her old race.
- High-Ranking Demon Physiology
- Demonic Miasma Affinity
- Demonic Regeneration
- Demonic Longevity
- Demonic Wings
- Ars Goetia Political Authority (Lost after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] abolished the old Pillar system)
- Command of 38 Personal Legions (Former independent forces)
Her Storm Manipulation should not be considered a former ability. It was one of Vine's defining powers before becoming a Dragon and nothing indicates that her transformation removed it.
