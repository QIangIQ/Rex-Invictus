Diablo, also known as Al'Diabolos, The Incarnation of Evil, The Prime Evil and The Devil, was an ancient Demon, former Demon King and one of the principal antagonists of the Demonic War. He originated from the accumulated sins associated with Humanity and the [[Heavenly Father]]'s creations following the establishment of the Soul Recycling System, becoming an existence fundamentally associated with Human evil rather than an ordinary deceased Human transformed into a Demon.[1]
Although frequently described as a Primordial Demon, Diablo was not a Primordial Entity or Regulator of Existence. The term refers instead to his extreme antiquity and position near the beginning of the modern Demonic system.
Over hundreds of thousands of years, Diablo consumed Souls and accumulated Demonic Energy until he became capable of contending with beings on the level of [[Lilith Elderblood]].[2] He eventually defeated Lilith, dominated [[Biblical Hell]] and launched the Demonic War while pursuing a much larger objective centered upon transforming himself into a complete Nephalem, unifying the major Hells and eventually constructing an infernal Pantheon under his control.[3][4][5]
His plans were ultimately dismantled by Victor Elderblood, who conquered Hell, destroyed Diablo's political and military advantages and finally consumed his Soul and existence after overwhelming his incomplete Nephalem form.[6]

### Physical description
Diablo's original body was that of an enormous monstrous Demon with red skin, glowing red eyes, sharp teeth, powerful claws, horns and a long thorn-covered tail.[7] His exact size was variable because he possessed Size Manipulation; one appearance placed him at approximately ten feet tall, while other manifestations were considerably larger.
His body naturally radiated dense Demonic Miasma. During his final battle against Lilith, the Miasma surrounding him became so concentrated that it produced an overwhelming aura of fear and corrosive pressure capable of threatening even high-level Demons.[8]

#### Nephalem Form
After being cornered by Lilith and realizing that escape was becoming impossible, Diablo prematurely performed the ritual intended to transform him into a Nephalem.[2][3] His resulting body became significantly more humanoid and androgynous, with pale skin, dark markings, horns, a black tail and six wings displaying characteristics associated with both Angels and Fallen Angels. Portions of his body retained Demonic armor-like features.
The transformation fused three major supernatural components—Angel, Fallen Angel and Demon—granting simultaneous access to Holy Light and Demonic Miasma.[3] The form was nevertheless incomplete because Diablo had been forced to perform the ritual directly rather than use the [[Egg of Creation]], the specialized incubator intended to stabilize his full evolution.[4]

### Personality and traits
Diablo was patient, manipulative, paranoid, ambitious and obsessed with control. Unlike Demons who acted according to immediate impulse, he was capable of preparing plans across centuries and maintaining multiple contingencies in case his primary strategy failed.[2] He habitually gathered intelligence concerning other Factions, infiltrated organizations through subordinates and investigated anything whose motives or capabilities he did not completely understand.
This caution was accompanied by extreme paranoia. Diablo considered anything outside his control a potential threat and placed agents inside organizations such as the [[New Dawn]] so that their activities could be monitored from within.[4] His style of leadership likewise depended more heavily upon fear than loyalty, with many subordinates obeying because of his strength and cruelty rather than genuine devotion.
Diablo possessed immense pride and believed that the enormous period he had spent accumulating Souls and power entitled him to stand above most supernatural beings. His hatred of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] intensified because Victor repeatedly destroyed plans that had required centuries of preparation. This pride eventually became one of his greatest weaknesses: after the incomplete Nephalem transformation increased his raw Energy but severely damaged his technique, instincts and mental stability, Diablo became increasingly irrational and relied more heavily upon brute force.[3]
Victor's overwhelming superiority ultimately broke this confidence completely. Faced with an existence he could neither understand, control nor meaningfully oppose, Diablo experienced genuine terror and helplessness shortly before his death.[6]

### Synopsis

#### Origins and Rise
Diablo originated from the accumulated sins of Humanity and the creations associated with the [[Heavenly Father]] following the establishment of the Soul Recycling System.[1] He was therefore considered the personification or incarnation of Human evil rather than an ordinary Human who later became a Demon.
Across an immense period of time, he continuously consumed Souls and accumulated Demonic Energy. This process eventually elevated him to a level where he could contend with [[Lilith Elderblood]], despite her status as a Demon Progenitor and God-King-level existence.[2]
Diablo and Lilith became the two dominant powers of [[Biblical Hell]] after [[Lucifer]] Morningstar abandoned his position as legitimate Ruler. Diablo eventually defeated Lilith, imprisoned and enslaved her, and became the dominant Demon King of Hell.[9]
Despite frequently being described as the ruler of Hell, Diablo deliberately refused the actual Ruler position. Chapter 674 establishes that accepting that role would bind him to Hell's Soul-processing responsibilities and interfere with his ambitions outside the [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]].[10] He instead forced Lilith to function as a temporary Ruler while pursuing his plans elsewhere.

#### Preparation for the Demonic War
Diablo's strategy before the Demonic War relied heavily upon manipulation rather than direct invasion alone. He used alliances, hostages, contracts, infiltrators and staged events to weaken supernatural Factions before open conflict began. Through cooperation with Evie Moriarty, he obtained a ritual capable of enslaving Lilith and turning one of the strongest Demons in existence into a controlled weapon.[11]
Diablo also negotiated directly with [[The Judges of The Abyss]], paying billions of Souls in exchange for an agreement that prevented immediate Primordial interference so long as Hell's Soul-processing structure continued functioning.[11][10] This allowed him to operate dangerously close to the boundary of cosmic Balance without immediately provoking entities such as The Owner of Limbo or [[True Death]].
Once these preparations were complete, Diablo launched large-scale attacks across Earth. His forces invaded China and Russia, damaged major Pantheons and attempted to cripple Factions such as [[Nightingale]] and Samar through hostage-taking and political pressure rather than simple extermination.[12][13]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] increasingly became the greatest obstacle to these plans. After Victor rescued hostages Diablo intended to use as leverage, Diablo arranged multiple attempts to eliminate him and began treating the new Vampire Count as a rapidly escalating threat.

#### Lucifer and the Nephalem Ritual
Diablo later used the enslaved Lilith as bait to lure [[Lucifer]] Morningstar into a trap.[14] He possessed the [[Spears|Spear]] of Longinus, a weapon designed to kill creations of the [[Heavenly Father]], and intended to obtain Lucifer's heart as the high-ranking Fallen Angel component required for his Nephalem transformation.
The plan failed because Lucifer had already evolved into an Elder God. He was no longer a conventional Fallen Angel, rendering both the Spear's intended effect and Lucifer's heart unsuitable for Diablo's ritual.[15]
Diablo was therefore forced to obtain a replacement. He ultimately killed the corrupted [[Gabriel]] and took his heart, securing a high-ranking Angel who had fallen into sin as the Fallen component required for the ritual.[16]

#### Collapse of Diablo's Position
As the Demonic War progressed, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s allies systematically dismantled Diablo's advantages. The [[Greek Underworld]] escaped his influence, his control over Lilith collapsed, the [[Key of Hell]] left his faction and his armies suffered catastrophic losses.[16][17]
Victor eventually recognized Diablo as the Nephalem seen in Violet's future vision, confirming that Diablo's planned transformation was approaching.[18] With his political and military situation collapsing, Diablo was confronted directly by the now-freed Lilith.

#### Battle against Lilith
The battle against Lilith demonstrated the true strength of Diablo's original Demonic form. Even against Lilith in her True Demon Form, wielding Genesis and using Divinities such as Rottenness, Corruption, Despair and Beginning, Diablo remained extraordinarily difficult to kill.[2]
His body possessed extreme regenerative capability, while his red Miasma was intensely corrosive and generated unnatural fear throughout the battlefield.[8] He could project enormous quantities of Demonic Energy and continue fighting despite severe injuries.
Lilith gradually gained the advantage by damaging Diablo faster than he could comfortably recover and using abilities that interfered with his regeneration. After more than an hour of combat, Diablo concluded that remaining would result in his death and began considering escape routes, sacrifices and contingency plans.[2]
The battlefield had been sealed by opposing Positive and Negative Energies, however, leaving Diablo unable to escape conventionally. With every other option collapsing, he was forced to perform the Nephalem transformation before he was properly prepared.

#### Incomplete Nephalem Transformation
Diablo's intended complete transformation required the [[Egg of Creation]], but he was unable to reach it during the battle.[2][4] He therefore consumed three hearts directly:
- His own Demonic heart.
- Michael's exceptionally righteous Angelic heart.
- [[Gabriel]]'s corrupted Angelic heart.[2]
The ritual produced an enormous eruption of Holy Light and Demonic Miasma and transformed Diablo into an incomplete Nephalem.[3] The event immediately attracted the attention of the Primordials responsible for cosmic Balance, with the Owner of Limbo considering intervention while the Judges prevented immediate action because their existing agreement remained valid and the Balance had not yet technically collapsed.
The transformation dramatically increased Diablo's raw Energy and granted simultaneous access to Holy and Demonic forces, but it also created severe disadvantages. His new humanoid body was fundamentally different from the enormous Demonic form he had spent hundreds of thousands of years learning to use, while the rushed evolution damaged much of the technique and instinct accumulated throughout his life.[3][4]

#### Battle against Victor Elderblood
Immediately after the transformation, Diablo confronted [[Victor Elderblood]].[3] Despite Diablo's vastly increased Energy, Victor overwhelmed him almost instantly because combat effectiveness depended upon far more than raw power. Diablo had lost familiarity with his body, lacked refined control over his new abilities and attempted to fight using instincts developed for an entirely different physiology.
Victor repeatedly struck Diablo faster than he could effectively respond and explained that true combat power included physical ability, technique, instinct, Energy control, discipline, [[Martial Arts]], mental state and familiarity with one's own body.[3] Diablo had dramatically improved his Energy while damaging several of the other elements.
His incomplete Nephalem state therefore made him more dangerous against opponents who could simply be overwhelmed by brute force but substantially less effective against a highly skilled fighter capable of exploiting his deficiencies. Diablo could theoretically have adapted to the body given sufficient time, but Victor gave him no opportunity to do so.[6]
As the battle continued, Diablo became increasingly unstable and abandoned much of the patient strategy that had previously defined him. He created weapons from Light and Darkness and launched large-scale attacks, but Victor avoided them effortlessly. Victor then released the pressure of his own Negative Energy, overwhelming Diablo psychologically as well as physically.[6]
Victor eventually transformed partially into his monstrous state and consumed Diablo completely, including his Soul. Diablo ceased to exist as an independent Being.[6]

#### Posthumous Revelations
Because [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] consumed Diablo, he gained access to important portions of the Demon's accumulated memories.[4] These memories revealed hidden bases, intelligence networks, contingency plans and the existence of the [[Egg of Creation]].
The Egg was a Demonic artifact constructed from blood, flesh and Souls and served as both a Soul repository and the intended incubator for Diablo's complete Nephalem transformation.[4] Stored Souls could be used to resurrect destroyed Demons, empower selected subordinates and support the evolution ritual. The complete process was intended to fundamentally transform both Soul and Race while properly stabilizing the resulting Nephalem, unlike Diablo's improvised battlefield evolution.
[[Asmodeus]] stole the Egg during the chaos surrounding Diablo's defeat and escaped.[4] Victor also discovered that [[Baal]] had infiltrated the [[New Dawn]] under Diablo's orders because Diablo considered the organization sufficiently unpredictable to require internal surveillance.
Later revelations established that becoming a Nephalem was only the first stage of Diablo's wider ambition. His eventual objective involved consolidating the major Hells and establishing an infernal Pantheon populated by powerful beings under his influence.[5]

### Skills & Abilities
Diablo was one of the strongest Demons of his era, possessing immense physical power, Demonic Energy, Soul-based abilities and extensive ritual knowledge developed over hundreds of thousands of years.

#### Demon Physiology
Diablo possessed extraordinary strength, speed, durability and stamina. His body could endure prolonged direct combat against Lilith's True Demon Form and regenerate severe injuries at extreme speed.[8][2] He was effectively ageless, possessed natural claws and other Demonic weapons, could alter his size and generated enormous quantities of Demonic Energy and Miasma.
His red Miasma was particularly dangerous. It was highly corrosive and could create an unnatural sensation of fear across the battlefield.[8] Diablo could also project concentrated Demonic Energy over large distances and use his natural body as an extremely destructive close-combat weapon.

#### Soul Manipulation and Consumption
Souls were central to Diablo's growth and long-term plans. He consumed Souls across hundreds of thousands of years to increase his power and stored vast quantities for later use.[2] Through the [[Egg of Creation]], those Souls could be used to strengthen Demons, resurrect destroyed subordinates and support large-scale rituals.[4]
Diablo possessed extensive knowledge of Demonic possession, Soul manipulation and ritual alteration, although the exact limits of every application are not fully shown.

#### Magic and Ritual Knowledge
Diablo possessed advanced knowledge of Demonic [[Magic]], Soul rituals and artificial racial transformation. His greatest project was the Nephalem ritual associated with the [[Egg of Creation]], which was intended to fundamentally alter both the participant's Soul and Race.[4] He also participated in the magical enslavement of Lilith using a ritual developed with assistance from Evie Moriarty.[11]

#### Strategic Intelligence
Before his incomplete transformation, Diablo was an exceptionally patient strategist. He maintained multiple contingency plans, gathered intelligence across Factions, infiltrated organizations, manipulated alliances, used hostages and exploited emotional relationships to force enemies into predictable actions. His ambush of [[Lucifer]] is a clear example: Diablo knew Lucifer would recognize Lilith as bait but correctly predicted that his emotional attachment would cause him to enter the trap regardless.[14]

#### Incomplete Nephalem Physiology
Diablo's incomplete Nephalem form fused Angelic, Fallen Angel and Demonic characteristics.[3] It dramatically increased his raw Energy, granted six wings and allowed him to simultaneously manipulate Holy Light and Demonic Miasma. He could create weapons from Light and Darkness, including swords and numerous projectile spears.
The form also granted hybrid advantages against both Angels and Demons because Diablo could wield supernatural forces normally associated with each opposing side. However, the transformation severely disrupted his combat instincts, body familiarity and technical skill, leaving him unable to properly exploit the enormous Power he had acquired.[3][4]
Given sufficient time, Diablo would likely have adapted progressively to the new body and become substantially more dangerous.[6] [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] killed him before this adaptation could occur.

### Equipment

#### Spear of Longinus
[[The Spear of Longinus]] was one of Diablo's most important weapons and had been created specifically to kill creations of the [[Heavenly Father]].[14] Diablo used it during his ambush of [[Lucifer]], expecting its specialized properties to bypass the former Angel's defenses. The weapon failed to perform as intended because Lucifer had already evolved into an Elder God and no longer qualified as an ordinary Fallen Angel.[15]

#### Key of Hell
Diablo possessed a portion of the [[Key of Hell]] during the period in which [[Biblical Hell]] was divided between himself and Lilith. After defeating Lilith he temporarily controlled the complete Key, but deliberately refused to accept the actual Ruler position because its responsibilities would restrict his wider ambitions.[10] The Key eventually left his faction during the final stages of the Demonic War.

#### Egg of Creation
The [[Egg of Creation]] was the centerpiece of Diablo's long-term evolution plan.[4] Constructed from blood, flesh and Souls, it functioned as a Soul repository, a source of resurrection and empowerment for Demons, and the proper incubator for Diablo's complete Nephalem transformation.
The artifact was intended to stabilize the alteration of Soul and Race that Diablo later performed prematurely on the battlefield. His inability to reach the Egg forced him to evolve without its support, creating the unstable incomplete Nephalem state that contributed heavily to his defeat.

### Trivia
- Diablo's title Primordial Demon does not make him a Primordial Entity.
- He originated from Humanity's accumulated sins rather than being a conventional Human Soul transformed into a Demon.[1]
- Diablo was a Demon King but explicitly refused to become a legitimate Ruler of [[Biblical Hell]].[10]
- His arrangement with the Judges was a transactional agreement involving billions of Souls rather than an ordinary alliance.[11][10]
- Diablo intentionally operated close to the limits of cosmic Balance while avoiding direct violation for as long as possible.
- His incomplete Nephalem transformation increased raw Power while initially making him a substantially worse fighter.[3][4]
- [[Lucifer]] was originally intended to provide the Fallen Angel component of the Nephalem ritual, but his evolution into an Elder God made him unsuitable.[15]
- Diablo ultimately used his own heart, Michael's heart and [[Gabriel]]'s heart for the emergency transformation.[2]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] inherited important memories concerning Diablo's hidden plans after consuming him.[4]
- Becoming a Nephalem was only the first stage of Diablo's larger plan to consolidate the Hells and establish an infernal Pantheon.[5]
