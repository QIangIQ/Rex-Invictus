### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 509
Michael was an Original Seraph, the strongest Seraphim of the Seventh Heaven and the Supreme Chief commanding the armies of the [[Heavenly Father]]. He was one of Heaven's highest-ranking creations and stood immediately below the Heavenly Father within the military hierarchy of the Angels. At the [[Supernatural Gathering]], even the neutral host formally introduced Michael by the title "Strongest Seraphim" and as the supreme commander of the Eternal Father's army.[1]
Michael was also one of the earliest Angels created by the Heavenly Father. His spiritual quality was extraordinary even among Heaven's elites; when [[Diablo]] searched for a replacement for [[Lucifer]] Morningstar's heart, an Elder God messenger explained that only the hearts of the First Three Created Angels possessed comparable quality, causing Diablo to immediately consider Michael and [[Gabriel]] as his viable targets.[2]
During the final stages of the Demonic War, Michael personally commanded Heaven's forces against Diablo's armies. He initially fought [[Lilith Elderblood]] and the Elder-class Demonic Dragon [[Zaladrac Zeovnur Elderblood]] before transferring that confrontation to Gabriel so he could resume directing the wider Angelic army.[3] Diablo ultimately exploited Michael's love for his siblings and responsibilities as commander, weakening him through Sloth and Envy before murdering Gabriel in front of him. Michael lost his composure and charged Diablo in anger, allowing Diablo and his hidden accomplice to create an opening through which Diablo tore out Michael's heart.[4]

### Physical Appearance
Michael appeared as a tall and imposing man with six large white wings, golden hair, golden armor and a western-style sword worn at his waist. His entire presence radiated such intense holiness that supernatural Light covered his face like a veil, preventing observers from properly seeing his facial features. Only his long golden hair remained clearly visible through the radiance.[1]
Michael and [[Gabriel]] appeared extremely similar when first introduced, both wearing golden armor and carrying western swords. Their most immediately identifiable visual difference was their hair, with Michael possessing gold hair while Gabriel's was silver. Michael's supernatural presence was also noticeably stronger than Gabriel's during their introduction.[1]
During active combat, Michael could manifest a golden halo above his head while the [[Divine Energy]] surrounding him increased dramatically. His six wings could expand to their full size when channeling large quantities of [[Holy Energy]] or commanding Heaven's forces.[4]
The gallery identifies an additional True Form, although the chapters directly depicting Michael's final battle do not provide enough detailed narration to catalogue every physical alteration of that state. Consequently, visual details unique to the artwork should not automatically be treated as canon unless supported by another chapter.

### Personality
Michael was righteous, disciplined and intensely devoted to the [[Heavenly Father]]. As commander of Heaven's armies, he normally prioritized order, obedience and the protection of his fellow Angels. He possessed the authoritative disposition expected of Heaven's supreme military commander and could coordinate large numbers of Seraphim, Virtues and ordinary Angels across a chaotic battlefield.[3][4]
He also possessed a degree of fairness unusual for someone raised within Heaven's rigid worldview. Although Michael fundamentally opposed beings and powers Heaven classified as evil, his actions were not portrayed as mindless hatred toward everything outside the [[Biblical Pantheon]]. During the [[Supernatural Gathering]] he behaved with considerably more restraint than many of the representatives surrounding him and participated in negotiations rather than immediately treating supernatural rivals as enemies.[1]
Michael's strongest emotional attachment was toward his Angelic siblings, especially [[Gabriel]]. The two were described as practically inseparable and had spent so long fighting together that either could assume the other's battlefield duties with almost no disruption. When Michael needed to resume overall command of Heaven's army, Gabriel immediately understood what was required and took over the confrontation against Lilith and Zaladrac.[3]
This attachment ultimately became Michael's fatal weakness.
When Gabriel began undergoing forced corruption during the Demonic War, Michael became increasingly distressed. [[Cassiel]] had to remind him that his army still depended upon his leadership because Michael had momentarily focused entirely upon his brother's suffering.[4]
Michael's inability to emotionally detach himself from Gabriel was further demonstrated when [[Thor]] bluntly argued that Gabriel might need to be killed before his corruption completed. Michael angrily rejected the idea because Gabriel was his brother, even when Thor reminded him that Michael bore responsibility for every Angel under his command.[4]
After [[Diablo]] tore out Gabriel's heart, Michael finally abandoned the emotional control expected of the strongest Seraph. His grief became anger—a cardinal Sin for an Angel—and the resulting surge of power explosively threw Sloth and Envy away from him. Michael then charged Diablo seeking vengeance rather than acting as commander.[4]
Diablo immediately mocked the contradiction, pointing out that Heaven's most exalted Angel had allowed anger to dictate his actions. Michael's loss of composure created precisely the opportunity Diablo's plan required.[4]
Michael's death subsequently became a strategic disaster for Heaven. Ariel later criticized his failure as commander, concluding that once Gabriel was struck Michael became too emotionally compromised to continue managing the war effectively. With Michael and Gabriel both dead, Angelic morale collapsed and Ariel was forced to take command herself.[5]

### History

#### Creation and the Original Seraphim
Michael was among the earliest Angels created by the [[Heavenly Father]]. Unlike the majority of later Angels, the earliest creations possessed spiritual structures of exceptional quality and stood at the highest levels of Heaven's hierarchy.
Original Seraphim such as Michael and [[Gabriel]] also possessed Conceptual authority comparable in principle to the Concepts held by Gods. Even members of the Seven Heavenly Virtues, themselves beings associated with Concepts, remained below Michael and Gabriel in authority because the Original Seraphim belonged to an even higher order.[6]
Michael eventually became the strongest Seraphim and Supreme Chief of Heaven's armies.[1]
The novel has not currently revealed the specific Concept personally represented by Michael. Therefore, assigning him a speculative Concept such as Light, War, Judgment or another theological association would be unsupported.

#### Lucifer and Heaven
Michael existed during the ancient period in which [[Lucifer]] Morningstar belonged to Heaven and survived into the modern era long after Lucifer's fall.
Although traditional religious accounts commonly associate Michael with Lucifer's expulsion, the checked novel chapters do not provide enough direct detail about Michael's individual role in Lucifer's rebellion to reproduce the traditional mythology as established MTW canon. What is established is that Michael remained loyal to the [[Heavenly Father]] while Lucifer became the First of the Fallen.

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Michael and [[Gabriel]] first appeared publicly as Heaven's representatives at the historic [[Supernatural Gathering]]. The meeting's mediator introduced Michael as the strongest Seraphim and Supreme Chief of the [[Heavenly Father]]'s army. Michael appeared with Gabriel at the entrance of the neutral meeting realm before silently entering the palace.[1]
Michael represented Heaven during the negotiations alongside some of Earth's greatest supernatural authorities, including [[Shiva]], [[Thor]], [[Zeus]], [[Volk Fenrir]], Evie Moriarty, [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]], [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]] and representatives of [[Nightingale]]. His attendance reflected Heaven's position as one of the world's major supernatural powers.[1]
Years later, the conflict between Heaven and Hell developed into full-scale war.
Michael personally led the Angelic army during the final Demonic War. Heaven fielded ordinary Angels, Seraphim and the Seven Heavenly Virtues, while allied forces including Volk's [[Werewolves]] and Thor assisted against [[Diablo]]'s armies.[3]
Michael initially fought directly against [[Zaladrac Zeovnur Elderblood]], an Elder-class Demonic Dragon serving Diablo, while [[Lilith Elderblood]] rode the Dragon into battle. Michael accelerated toward Zaladrac as a beam of Holy Light and struck the enormous Dragon with his golden sword, producing enough force to stun and visibly injure her.[3]
Michael confronted Lilith immediately afterward, accusing her of once again opposing the Heavenly Father. Lilith, already under Diablo's control, gave no normal emotional response and attacked him with numerous spikes of concentrated Miasma. Michael avoided the attacks while noticing that something was profoundly wrong with her behavior, internally observing that she felt emotionally dead.[3]
Because the broader Angelic army required active command, Michael contacted Gabriel and ordered him to take over the fight against Lilith and Zaladrac. Gabriel accepted immediately. Their exchange showed how accustomed the brothers were to functioning together; Michael and Gabriel served as Heaven's commander and vice-commander and could exchange responsibilities fluidly during battle.[3]
Michael returned to directing the Angels while Gabriel engaged the Demonic Goddess and Dragon.
The situation changed when Diablo's plan to corrupt Gabriel activated. Gabriel suddenly began leaking dark Miasma from his chest, and his [[Holy Energy]] started transforming into Demonic Energy. Michael screamed his brother's name and momentarily lost awareness of the wider battlefield.[4]
[[Cassiel]] forced Michael to focus again, reminding him that countless Angels still depended upon his orders. Realizing the army was beginning to lose ground, Michael expanded his wings, manifested his halo and sought the Heavenly Father's permission to deploy Heaven's hidden reinforcement.[4]
The Heavenly Father granted permission.
Using his own Energy as a channel, Michael called upon [[Azrael]] and opened an enormous golden portal above the battlefield. Hundreds of Angels belonging to Azrael's forces descended through the gateway while the Angel of Death himself entered the war.[4]
Ariel immediately recognized that Michael had deployed one of Heaven's trump cards earlier than originally intended. Michael justified the decision through Gabriel's corruption and ordered Azrael's assistance in exterminating the Demonic forces responsible.[4]
Even after Azrael's arrival, Michael remained preoccupied with Gabriel. Thor eventually confronted him over the worsening situation and argued that Gabriel's fall could make him a danger to the very Angels he had once commanded. Michael angrily rejected the suggestion of killing his own brother.[4]
Thor reminded Michael that Gabriel was not his only brother and that a commander was responsible for the entire Angelic army. Michael remained emotionally conflicted and failed to restore the decisiveness expected from Heaven's Supreme Chief.[4]
Diablo immediately exploited the disorder.
Sloth and Envy, two of the Seven Deadly Sins, suddenly appeared beside Michael. Sloth imposed supernatural exhaustion and drowsiness upon him while Envy rapidly drained Michael's enormous Energy reserves. Envy then released a dark parasitic entity that entered Michael's body and further compromised his condition.[4]
Simultaneously, [[Agares]] ambushed Ariel and another Heavenly Virtue before disappearing into his own dimensional ability. Diablo personally entered the battlefield immediately afterward.[4]
Diablo approached Gabriel while the newly Fallen Angel was paralyzed and drove a claw through his body, ripping out his heart.[4]
Michael witnessed Gabriel's murder.
His grief immediately transformed into extreme rage. The surge of Holy power erupting from Michael was sufficient to blast Sloth and Envy away, temporarily freeing him from their immediate interference.[4]
Rather than regrouping or returning to command, Michael charged directly toward Diablo with vengeance dominating his thoughts.
Diablo had planned for precisely this response.
As Michael approached, Diablo released enormous quantities of red Miasma. Before Michael could properly understand the attack, Diablo pierced his chest and ripped out his heart.[4]
Michael's final question was how Diablo had managed to overcome him so suddenly. Diablo answered that he had not been fighting alone. [[Asmodeus]], whose presence Michael believed had disappeared earlier, was still participating in the trap from concealment.[4]
Michael died immediately after losing his heart.
Contrary to the previous version of this article, Agares did not kill Michael. Agares attacked Ariel and another Virtue elsewhere on the battlefield. Michael's heart was personally removed by Diablo, with the preceding attacks from Sloth, Envy and Asmodeus creating the opening necessary for the Demon King to succeed.[4]
The deaths of Michael and Gabriel devastated Heaven's army. Ariel observed that the Angels had lost their two best frontline leaders at essentially the same moment, severely damaging morale and turning what should have been a favorable war into a defensive struggle.[5]
Ariel assumed command after Michael's death. Azrael, enraged by the deaths of his brothers, abandoned restraint and massacred almost every Pillar-ranked Demon remaining on the battlefield, with only [[Bael]] and Agares surviving his initial rampage.[5]
Michael's importance to Diablo's plan continued even after death.
Long before the battle, Diablo had learned that the quality of [[Lucifer]]'s heart could only be rivaled by the hearts of the first Angels created by the Heavenly Father. Michael therefore became one of the primary ingredients Diablo required for his attempt to transcend the existing Balance.[2]
Diablo preserved Michael's heart after the battle. When later driven into desperation, he produced three hearts: his own heart saturated in the accumulated Evil of Hell, Gabriel's partially corrupted Angelic heart and Michael's overwhelmingly Holy heart.[7]
He consumed all three.
The combination of Demonic Miasma and Angelic Holy Energy violently transformed Diablo and became part of his attempt to break the supernatural Balance itself.[7]

### Relationships

#### Heavenly Father
The [[Heavenly Father]] was Michael's creator, sovereign and the ultimate authority to whom Michael remained completely loyal.
Michael served as the Supreme Chief of his Father's armies and required direct authorization before prematurely summoning [[Azrael]] and his forces during the Demonic War. The Heavenly Father immediately granted the request after [[Gabriel]]'s corruption destabilized the battlefield.[4]

#### Gabriel
[[Gabriel]] was one of Michael's closest siblings and served as his vice-commander within Heaven's army. The two were described as inseparable brothers who had fought together for so long that they could switch military responsibilities almost instinctively.[3]
Michael's love for Gabriel ultimately became the weakness [[Diablo]] exploited. Gabriel's corruption distracted Michael from commanding his army, and Gabriel's murder caused Michael to abandon his discipline entirely and attack Diablo in rage.[4]

#### Azrael
[[Azrael]] was another of Michael's Original Seraphim siblings and one of Heaven's most dangerous combatants.
Michael considered Azrael's intervention important enough that he personally requested the [[Heavenly Father]]'s permission before summoning him. He referred to Azrael as his brother and declared that the Angel of Death's strength had become necessary after [[Gabriel]] was infected.[4]
Michael and Gabriel's deaths subsequently enraged Azrael enough for him to massacre nearly every remaining Pillar-ranked Demon on the battlefield.[5]

#### Ariel Elderblood
Ariel served beneath Michael as one of the Seven Heavenly Virtues. She respected the established chain of command but became increasingly frustrated with Michael's decisions after [[Gabriel]]'s corruption.[4]
Following Michael's death, Ariel concluded that Michael's emotional collapse had contributed significantly to Heaven losing control of the battlefield. She consequently assumed overall command herself.[5]

#### Lucifer Morningstar
[[Lucifer]] was one of the earliest [[Heavenly Father]]'s creations and a former member of the same highest Angelic order as Michael before becoming the First Fallen.
Michael remained loyal to Heaven after Lucifer's rebellion. The exact personal relationship between the two during the ancient rebellion is not extensively explored in the checked chapters.
The supernatural quality of their existence was nevertheless directly compared. [[Diablo]] originally wanted Lucifer's heart for his transformation, but after Lucifer's circumstances made that impossible, an Elder God messenger informed him that the hearts of the First Three Created Angels could possess comparable quality. Michael consequently became one of Diablo's replacement targets.[2]

#### Diablo
[[Diablo]] was directly responsible for Michael's death.
Rather than attempting to defeat the strongest Seraphim through a straightforward duel, Diablo designed a coordinated strategy that attacked Michael's emotional vulnerabilities, Energy reserves and command responsibilities simultaneously.[4]
Sloth exhausted Michael, Envy drained and infected his Energy, [[Gabriel]]'s death provoked him into anger and [[Asmodeus]] remained hidden as an additional component of the ambush. Diablo then used the opening to remove Michael's heart.[4]
Michael's heart was one of the primary resources Diablo had been seeking throughout the conflict and was later consumed during Diablo's transformation.[7]

### Skills & Abilities
Michael was explicitly recognized as the strongest Seraphim and served as Heaven's supreme military commander.[1] As one of the Original Seraphim, his supernatural authority exceeded ordinary Angels and even the Seven Heavenly Virtues.[6]

#### Original Seraph Physiology
- Original Seraph Physiology: Michael belonged to the highest ancient order of Angels created directly by the [[Heavenly Father]]. His physiology possessed [[Holy Energy]], supernatural physical attributes, wings, enhanced senses and the spiritual characteristics associated with Heaven's earliest creations.[1][6]
- Superhuman Strength: Michael could physically engage entities such as the Elder-class Demonic Dragon Zaladrac and strike with enough force to stun the massive creature.[3]
- Superhuman Speed: Michael could transform his movement into a streak or ray of Light during high-speed combat and rapidly intercept aerial threats.[3]
- Superhuman Reflexes: He evaded multiple Miasma projectiles fired by Lilith while simultaneously analyzing her abnormal condition.[3]
- Enhanced Durability: As one of Heaven's highest Seraphim, Michael possessed a supernatural body vastly superior to ordinary Angels.
- Enhanced Stamina: Michael could command a global-scale battlefield while simultaneously participating in combat against elite Demonic entities.
- Extreme Longevity: Michael existed before modern Humanity and survived from Heaven's earliest periods until the Demonic War.
- Regeneration: Like high-ranking Seraphim, Michael possessed supernatural recovery from injuries that would kill ordinary beings. This did not protect him from the removal of his heart.[4]
- Flight: Michael possessed six large wings capable of sustained high-speed aerial combat.[1]
- Holy Presence: Michael naturally radiated intense holiness and Light powerful enough to obscure his facial features from ordinary observation.[1]
- Complete/Exceptional Soul: Michael belonged to the earliest order of Angels rather than later generations built from incomplete spiritual foundations. His Soul possessed enough quality that [[Diablo]] considered Michael's heart a viable replacement for [[Lucifer]]'s in a transformation intended to break the existing supernatural Balance.[2]
- Conceptual Authority: Original Seraphim possess Concepts similarly to the Seven Heavenly Virtues and Gods, granting them greater authority than ordinary Seraphim.[6]
- Michael's specific Concept has not been revealed. No individual Concept should currently be assigned to him without direct chapter evidence.

#### Holy Energy Manipulation
- [[Holy Energy]] Manipulation: Michael possessed immense quantities of Heaven's Holy Energy and could use it both offensively and defensively.
- Photokinesis: His attacks and movement could manifest as concentrated golden Light.[3]
- Holy Aura: Michael could release an intense aura of holiness around himself, particularly when entering combat or invoking greater Angelic power.[1]
- Halo Manifestation: A luminous halo appeared above Michael when he gathered his Energy to request and perform [[Azrael]]'s summoning.[4]
- Holy Weapon Enhancement: Michael could cover his sword in golden Angelic Energy, dramatically increasing its effectiveness against creatures aligned with Demonic Miasma.[3]
- Anti-Demon Attribute: Angelic Holy Energy possesses an inherent advantage against Demonic beings and Miasma. This racial compatibility contributed substantially to Heaven initially holding the upper hand during the Demonic War.[5]

#### Swordsmanship
- Swordsmanship: Michael carried a western sword as his primary weapon and demonstrated sufficient proficiency to fight Elder-class supernatural opponents while moving at extreme speed.[1][3]
- Holy Sword Strike: Michael surrounded his sword in golden Energy and struck Zaladrac with enough power to make the Elder-class Demonic Dragon roar in pain and become momentarily stunned.[3]

#### Heavenly Communication and Summoning
- Telepathic Communication: Michael could communicate directly with [[Gabriel]] across the battlefield without conventional speech, allowing rapid exchange of military orders.[3]
- Communication with the [[Heavenly Father]]: Michael could directly request authorization from his creator during battle.[4]
- Heavenly Portal Creation: After receiving the Heavenly Father's permission, Michael used his Energy as a channel to open an enormous golden portal above the battlefield.[4]
- Angelic Army Summoning: Hundreds of Angels descended through Michael's portal as reinforcements.[4]
- [[Azrael]] Summoning: Michael specifically summoned Azrael, Heaven's Angel of Death, as an emergency trump card after Gabriel began falling.[4]

#### Conceptual and Spiritual Status
- Conceptual Existence: Original Seraphim possess Conceptual authority analogous to high-ranking Divine beings.[6]
- Spiritual Potency: Michael's Heart and Soul were of extraordinary quality even among Angels. [[Diablo]] specifically required such a high-grade Angelic component for his later transformation.[2][7]
- Resistance to Demonic Corruption: As one of Heaven's purest and highest-order Angels, Michael naturally possessed extremely high resistance to Demonic influence. Diablo therefore did not attempt to corrupt Michael in the same fashion as [[Gabriel]] and instead designed a coordinated ambush to physically kill him.
- Resistance to Sin Influence: Michael could initially resist Sloth's supernatural drowsiness despite immediately feeling its effects.[4]
- This resistance was not absolute. Envy's simultaneous Energy drain and parasitic attack substantially weakened him.[4]

#### Emotion-Fueled Power
- Anger Empowerment: After witnessing [[Gabriel]]'s heart being removed, Michael's rage generated a sudden surge of power strong enough to violently throw Sloth and Envy away from him.[4]
- This was not a beneficial ability under normal Angelic doctrine. Michael's power spike came from succumbing to anger, a Sin fundamentally opposed to the spiritual purity expected from an Original Seraph, and the emotional loss of control directly contributed to his death.

### Leadership and Skills
- Supreme Military Command: Michael was the overall commander of Heaven's armies and responsible for coordinating Angels, Seraphim and the Seven Heavenly Virtues during major conflicts.[1][3]
- Battlefield Coordination: Michael could rapidly redistribute responsibilities among Heaven's highest-ranking combatants, demonstrated when he ordered [[Gabriel]] to replace him against Lilith and Zaladrac while he resumed overall command.[3]
- Military Experience: As an Angel older than Humanity and Heaven's Supreme Chief, Michael possessed eons of experience overseeing the Heavenly Host.
- Tactical Communication: His telepathic link with Gabriel allowed extremely efficient command exchanges in active combat.[3]
- Authority over Angels: Michael possessed enough status to command even extremely powerful members of Heaven's hierarchy, although deployment of figures such as [[Azrael]] still required the [[Heavenly Father]]'s approval under exceptional circumstances.[4]

### Weaknesses
- Heart Destruction/Removal: Michael's greatest biological vulnerability was the heart. [[Diablo]]'s removal of it immediately killed him despite his status as the strongest Seraphim.[4]
- Emotional Attachment: Michael's love for [[Gabriel]] severely compromised his effectiveness as commander once Gabriel began falling. His inability to separate personal grief from military responsibility allowed Diablo's plan to progress.[4]
- Anger: Michael's rage after Gabriel's death gave him a temporary surge in power but destroyed his judgment. He charged Diablo without properly assessing the surrounding threats and was killed almost immediately afterward.[4]
- Energy Drain: Envy could rapidly reduce Michael's Energy reserves despite Michael's enormous power.[4]
- Parasitic Debilitation: Envy implanted a dark parasitic influence into Michael, further compromising his combat condition.[4]
- Sloth Inducement: Sloth's power could force supernatural tiredness and sleepiness upon Michael, although he initially resisted the urge.[4]
- Coordinated Ambushes: Michael was vastly more difficult to eliminate in direct combat than through layered attacks. Diablo succeeded by simultaneously exploiting Gabriel, Sloth, Envy, [[Asmodeus]] and the wider battlefield rather than challenging Michael under neutral conditions.[4]

### Death and Legacy
Michael's death was one of the decisive turning points of the Demonic War.
Heaven lost both its commander and vice-commander almost simultaneously when [[Diablo]] removed [[Gabriel]]'s and Michael's hearts. Angelic morale collapsed, forcing Ariel to assume command while [[Azrael]] responded through indiscriminate slaughter of Diablo's elite forces.[4][5]
Michael's death also fulfilled one of Diablo's longest-running objectives. The Demon King required an Angelic heart of a quality comparable to [[Lucifer]]'s for his greater plan, and Michael—one of the earliest and purest [[Heavenly Father]]'s creations—provided exactly that component.[2]
Diablo later consumed Michael's heart together with his own Miasma-saturated heart and Gabriel's corrupted Angelic heart. The union of overwhelmingly Negative and Positive energies triggered a massive transformation and became part of Diablo's attempt to destabilize the Balance between Heaven and Hell.[7]
Michael therefore influenced the conclusion of the war even after his death: the extraordinary spiritual quality that made him Heaven's strongest Seraph also made his heart one of the most valuable supernatural resources Diablo could acquire.
