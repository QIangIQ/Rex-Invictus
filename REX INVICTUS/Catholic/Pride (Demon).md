### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 683
Pride was a powerful Demon, one of the Seven Deadly Sins serving [[Diablo]], and the Demon who represented the Sin of Pride. Although his direct participation in the story was extremely brief, Diablo's own assessment placed Pride among the highest combatants available to his faction. Before War became a [[Demon God]], Pride was one of only four Demons—alongside Wrath, [[Agares]] and [[Bael]]—considered capable of fighting the Horseman evenly.[1]
Pride participated in the final stage of the Demonic War, where he fought the Heavenly forces alongside the remaining Deadly Sins. He, Wrath and Envy were eventually overwhelmed and severely wounded by the Seven Virtues. Shortly afterward, the [[Heavenly Father]] activated Heavenly Judgment, covering the battlefield in Holy Light. Pride was completely evaporated by the attack and, unlike Sloth, did not survive.[2]

### Physical Appearance
Pride's physical appearance is never described in sufficient detail in the novel. His participation during the Demonic War occurs alongside several other high-ranking Demons, but the narration does not establish his height, build, hair color, eye color, clothing, horns, wings or any unique True Form.[2]
Consequently, Pride's current visual depiction on the wiki should be treated as an artistic interpretation rather than a canonical design.

### Personality and Traits
Very little is known about Pride's personality. Unlike Wrath, whose battle-seeking attitude is directly demonstrated, or Sloth, whose behavior is extensively defined by her Sin, Pride receives no individual dialogue or detailed internal characterization before his death.
His identity as the Sin of Pride confirms that Pride occupied the position corresponding to that Deadly Sin within [[Diablo]]'s hierarchy, but the story never explains how the Sin influenced his personality or behavior. Traits such as extreme arrogance, superiority complexes or pride-based psychological effects should therefore not be treated as confirmed solely because of his title.
What can be established is that Pride remained among Diablo's elite forces during the final Demonic War rather than abandoning the battlefield, continuing to fight Heaven until the Seven Virtues rendered him incapable of continuing.[2]

### History

#### Seven Deadly Sins
At some point prior to the Demonic War, Pride became one of the Seven Deadly Sins, the elite Demons serving beneath [[Diablo]]. As the holder of the Pride position, he possessed the titles Prince of Pride and Sin of Pride.
The Seven Deadly Sins occupied an exceptional position within Hell's military hierarchy. Pride's own strength is demonstrated more precisely through Diablo's evaluation of the Horseman War. Before War obtained Divinity and evolved into a [[Demon God]], Diablo identified only Pride, Wrath, [[Agares]] and [[Bael]] as Demons capable of fighting him evenly.[1]
This places Pride far above ordinary Infernal Demons and among Diablo's strongest individual combat assets.
War's later evolution changed that comparison. Once the Horseman attained Demon Godhood during his battle against [[Victor Elderblood]], Diablo's assessment narrowed the Demons capable of matching him to only Agares and Bael. Pride and Wrath were therefore below the level War attained after becoming a Demon God.[1]

#### Demonic War
Pride eventually participated directly in the final stage of [[Diablo]]'s Demonic War against Heaven. By that point, the conflict had escalated beyond ordinary Angelic and Demonic armies, involving the Seven Virtues, the Deadly Sins, high-ranking Angels, Gods and numerous supernatural factions.[2]
After Heaven reorganized its remaining forces, the Seven Virtues aggressively counterattacked Diablo's elites. Pride fought alongside Wrath and Envy, but all three accumulated numerous injuries from the Virtues. Their wounds eventually became severe enough that they could no longer continue fighting and had to be carried away from the front lines by allied Demons.[2]
The effectiveness of the Heavenly attacks was particularly dangerous because [[Holy Energy]] possessed a natural advantage against Demonic beings. Although Pride had previously been regarded as one of Hell's highest-level fighters, the combined pressure of the Seven Virtues left him incapacitated.[2]
Their retreat did not save them. The Heavenly faction had suffered equally catastrophic casualties, prompting Ariel to appeal directly to the [[Heavenly Father]]. Heaven's Creator ordered the remaining Angels to retreat and subsequently activated Heavenly Judgment.[2]
Enormous beams of Holy Light descended across the battlefield, destroying approximately half of the remaining Demonic army. Pride was caught within the attack alongside the other surviving Deadly Sins and was completely evaporated. Sloth became the sole exception among them, surviving by shielding herself beneath an enormous concentration of Miasma.[2]
Pride's destruction by Holy Light prevented the ordinary Demonic process through which sufficiently powerful Demons could eventually reform themselves within Hell after conventional death.[3]

### Skills & Abilities
Pride was one of [[Diablo]]'s strongest individual Demons, but almost none of his personal techniques are shown before his death. His abilities should therefore be limited to powers demonstrated by his position, racial nature or direct statements concerning his combat strength rather than speculative abilities associated with the traditional concept of Pride.
- Demon Physiology: Pride was a high-ranking Infernal Demon and one of the Seven Deadly Sins. As a Demon, his existence was naturally compatible with Hell, Miasma and Demonic Energy.
- Conditional [[Immortality]] / Demonic Reconstitution: Demons destroyed through conventional methods can eventually reconstitute themselves within Hell, with more powerful Demons requiring longer periods to return. Certain methods such as Holy Light, specialized anti-Demon weapons or complete absorption can prevent this process. Pride was permanently destroyed because Heavenly Judgment killed him through overwhelming Holy Light.[2][3]
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics: Pride possessed physical and supernatural parameters far beyond ordinary Demons. Diablo considered him powerful enough to contend evenly with War before the Horseman became a [[Demon God]].[1]
- Extreme Combat Power: Pride belonged to an exceptionally small group within Hell's forces. Before War's Divine evolution, only Pride, Wrath, [[Agares]] and [[Bael]] were explicitly regarded as Demons capable of fighting the Horseman evenly.[1]
- Demonic Energy: As one of the highest-ranking Demons in Diablo's forces, Pride possessed and utilized Demonic supernatural power. The exact nature and applications of his personal Energy were never individually demonstrated.
- Sin of Pride: Pride possessed the supernatural position corresponding to the Sin of Pride. However, unlike Sloth's Sin—which later receives a detailed explanation—the novel never reveals the mechanics of Pride's individual Sin ability before his death. Abilities such as Pride Empowerment, Arrogance Inducement, Ego Manipulation or power amplification through superiority are therefore not confirmed.
- Combat Experience: Pride's status as one of Diablo's elite fighters and his comparison to War indicate extensive combat capability. He remained engaged against Heaven's strongest active forces until being incapacitated by the Seven Virtues.[1][2]
- Demonic Resilience: Pride remained alive despite accumulating numerous injuries during combat with the Seven Virtues. He was ultimately incapacitated rather than immediately killed by those wounds, before Heavenly Judgment permanently destroyed him.[2]

Once War became a [[Demon God]], however, Diablo stated that only Agares and Bael remained capable of matching him. Pride therefore occupied an exceptional level among ordinary Demons while remaining below the stronger Divine state eventually attained by War.[1]
Pride's eventual defeat by the Seven Virtues does not establish that any single Virtue individually surpassed him. #### Weaknesses
- [[Holy Energy]] / Holy Light: Holy power possesses exceptional effectiveness against Demons. Pride, Wrath and Envy were severely wounded by the Seven Virtues' Holy attacks before being incapacitated.[2]
- Permanent Destruction by Holy Light: Although Demons can normally reconstitute after conventional death, Holy Light is capable of preventing this regeneration. Pride was completely evaporated by Heavenly Judgment and consequently did not return.[2][3]
- Higher Divine Power: Pride's status among [[Diablo]]'s strongest Demons did not place him beyond vastly superior Divine attacks. The [[Heavenly Father]]'s battlefield-wide Judgment overwhelmed and erased him completely.[2]

### Relationships

#### Diablo
Pride served [[Diablo]] as one of his Seven Deadly Sins and belonged to the strongest portion of the Demon King's military forces.
Diablo's own evaluation demonstrates the importance of Pride as a combat asset. When evaluating War, he identified Pride among the extremely small number of Demons capable of matching the Horseman before War obtained Godhood.[1]
Pride remained loyal to Diablo's faction throughout the final Demonic War and continued fighting Heaven even after the balance of the conflict had begun collapsing around the former Demon King.[2]

#### Seven Deadly Sins
Pride was one of the Seven Deadly Sins alongside Wrath, Envy, Greed, Gluttony, Lust and Sloth.
During the final Heavenly counterattack, Pride, Wrath and Envy were all severely injured by the Seven Virtues and removed from active combat. Shortly afterward, Heavenly Judgment destroyed the remaining Sins caught within its range. Sloth was the only one to survive.[2]

#### War
Although Pride and War are never shown fighting directly, [[Diablo]]'s evaluation provides the clearest measurement of Pride's combat strength.
Before War became a [[Demon God]], Pride was one of only four Demons explicitly considered capable of fighting him evenly. After War attained Divinity, Pride was removed from that group, leaving only [[Agares]] and [[Bael]] as viable equals.[1]

### Trivia
- Pride is first mentioned before his direct appearance when [[Diablo]] evaluates the Demons capable of fighting War.[1]
- His first direct story involvement occurs during the final stage of the Demonic War.
- Before War became a [[Demon God]], Pride was one of only four Demons capable of fighting him evenly, alongside Wrath, [[Agares]] and [[Bael]].[1]
- After War attained Demon Godhood, Pride was no longer considered capable of matching him.[1]
- Pride, Wrath and Envy were all incapacitated by the Seven Virtues shortly before Heavenly Judgment.[2]
- Pride was permanently killed by Holy Light, preventing the normal Demonic reconstitution process.[2][3]
- Sloth was the only one of Diablo's active Seven Deadly Sins to survive Heavenly Judgment.[2]
- Despite representing the Sin of Pride, the novel never explains the mechanics of Pride's individual Sin ability.
- No canonical physical description of Pride has been provided in the novel, meaning existing artwork is interpretative.
