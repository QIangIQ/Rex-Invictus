### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 509
Gabriel was one of the Original Seraphim, one of the first three Angels created by the [[Heavenly Father]], the Angel who represented the Concept of Justice and the one responsible for announcing the word of his Creator. Alongside his elder brother Michael, Gabriel stood above the ordinary Seraphim and even the Seven Heavenly Virtues within Heaven's spiritual hierarchy.[1][2]
Gabriel served as Michael's vice-commander during the Demonic War. The brothers had fought together for so long that they could exchange battlefield responsibilities almost instinctively, with Gabriel taking command of individual engagements whenever Michael needed to resume control of Heaven's entire army.[3]
Among Heaven's Angels, Gabriel was described as one of its most exalted beings and, immediately before his Fall, as the purest Angel in Heaven.[4] This purity made him critically important to [[Diablo]]'s plans. When Diablo discovered that [[Lucifer]] Morningstar could no longer provide the Angelic heart he required, he learned that only the hearts of the First Three Created Angels possessed comparable spiritual quality, making Michael and Gabriel his primary replacement targets.[5]
Gabriel was ultimately struck by one of the anti-Angel Cursed Bullets prepared by Diablo's faction. The corruption forcibly converted his [[Holy Energy]] into Miasma and transformed the Original Seraph into a Fallen Angel. Before he could recover or adapt to his new state, Diablo immobilized him and personally ripped out his heart.[6][4]

### Physical Appearance
Gabriel originally appeared as a tall and imposing man with six large white wings, silver hair and golden armor. He carried a western-style sword similar to Michael's.[1]
Gabriel and Michael appeared almost identical when they entered the [[Supernatural Gathering]]. Both wore the same general style of golden armor, possessed six wings and radiated immense holiness. The primary visible difference was their hair: Michael possessed golden hair while Gabriel's was silver. Gabriel's supernatural presence was also described as somewhat less intense than Michael's.[1]
Like his brother, Gabriel's face was concealed beneath supernatural Light. His holiness manifested so strongly that the radiance functioned almost like a veil, preventing ordinary observers from clearly perceiving his facial features.[1]

#### Fallen Form
Gabriel's appearance changed radically after being struck by the cursed projectile during the Demonic War. A mixture of Holy Light and black Miasma erupted around his body while the corruption progressed, and his previously Angelic scream gradually became increasingly Demonic.[4]
Once the transformation was completed, Gabriel was explicitly described as looking considerably more Demon-like than before. His original [[Holy Energy]] had been completely corrupted and the Energy inside his body had become pure Miasma.[4]
The chapter does not provide a detailed catalogue of every physical change in his Fallen Form. Consequently, details visible only in artwork should not automatically be treated as canonical characteristics.

### Personality
Gabriel was disciplined, dutiful and deeply committed to the order established by the [[Heavenly Father]]. His very Concept was Justice, and his formal role as the Angel who announced his Creator's word placed him among the clearest representatives of Heaven's laws and authority.[1][2]
Like many Angels, his understanding of right and wrong was strongly influenced by the Heavenly Father's established order. Gabriel reacted with particular hostility toward beings he believed violated that order. His contempt for [[Lilith Elderblood]], for example, extended back to the beginning of Biblical history. When confronting her during the Demonic War, Gabriel stated that the Heavenly Father should have eliminated her during Genesis so the subsequent chaos associated with her existence could never have occurred.[3]
He was nevertheless not portrayed as irrational or needlessly reckless. When Michael transferred responsibility for fighting Lilith and Zaladrac to him, Gabriel calmly accepted the assignment despite recognizing how dangerous the pair were.[3]
Gabriel also displayed genuine affection for Michael. The two were described as inseparable brothers who understood one another exceptionally well after fighting together for countless years. Gabriel's final conversation with Michael before their last battle was characteristically simple: Michael warned him to be careful because Lilith was abnormal, while Gabriel casually replied that she had never been normal before reassuring his brother.[3]
His loyalty to Heaven remained intact until his Fall. Unlike [[Lucifer]] Morningstar, Gabriel did not willingly rebel against the Heavenly Father. His transformation into a Fallen Angel was artificially induced through [[Diablo]]'s cursed weapon, and even while the Miasma consumed him, Gabriel actively struggled against the corruption.[4]
[[Azrael]] immediately recognized that the resistance was futile, but Gabriel continued fighting the corruption until his transformation was complete.[4]

### History

#### One of the First Three Angels
Gabriel belonged to the earliest generation of Angels personally created by the [[Heavenly Father]]. He was one of the First Three Created Angels, a group whose spiritual quality exceeded that of later Heavenly creations.[5]
The other members associated with this first order were Michael and [[Lucifer]] Morningstar. Their Souls and hearts were of such exceptional quality that [[Diablo]] later regarded Michael and Gabriel as the only acceptable replacements when Lucifer's heart became unusable for his planned transformation.[5]
Unlike most later Angels, Original Seraphim possessed complete enough existences to develop or embody Concepts. Gabriel specifically represented Justice.[1][2]
Gabriel eventually became one of Heaven's highest-ranking figures and served directly beneath Michael in the military hierarchy.

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Gabriel first appeared during the great [[Supernatural Gathering]], accompanying Michael as one of Heaven's representatives.[1]
The mediator introduced Michael as the strongest Seraphim and supreme chief of the [[Heavenly Father]]'s army before formally introducing Gabriel as Michael's brother, the Seraph who represented Justice and the one who announced the word of the Eternal Father.[1]
Gabriel and Michael entered the gathering together without unnecessary displays of aggression. Their presence alone demonstrated the importance Heaven placed upon the meeting, where the supernatural world's most influential leaders were attempting to address the rapidly destabilizing balance between major factions.[1]
Gabriel later participated directly in the final conflict between Heaven and [[Diablo]].
As the Demonic War intensified, Michael initially engaged [[Lilith Elderblood]] and the Elder-class Demonic Dragon [[Zaladrac Zeovnur Elderblood]]. Because Michael was simultaneously responsible for directing the entire Heavenly army, he eventually contacted Gabriel telepathically and ordered him to replace him in the confrontation.[3]
Gabriel immediately appeared beside his brother in a flash of golden Light with his sword already prepared.
Michael warned him that Lilith was abnormal.
Gabriel responded that she had always been abnormal.[3]
The exchange illustrated how familiar the brothers were with both one another and their duties. Michael and Gabriel served as commander and vice-commander of the Heavenly forces and had worked together for long enough that transferring battlefield responsibilities required almost no explanation.[3]
Gabriel then confronted Lilith and Zaladrac.
He condemned Lilith's rebellion against the Heavenly Father's order, stating that she should have been eliminated during Genesis. Lilith, whose personality and will were being suppressed by Diablo at the time, did not respond verbally.[3]
Zaladrac answered instead.
The Elder-class Demonic Dragon gathered immense Demonic power in her mouth and released a massive blast of pure Miasma toward Gabriel. Gabriel reacted by opening his wings and rapidly ascending into the sky, avoiding the Dragon's attack while deliberately drawing Lilith and Zaladrac away from Michael and the wider Angelic army.[3]
Gabriel continued occupying both opponents while Michael resumed command.
Diablo's actual plan, however, had already been prepared.
Earlier in the war, [[Asmodeus]] had been involved in the production of specialized Cursed Bullets designed specifically for Heaven's highest-order Angels. Diablo considered the supply already produced sufficient for his objectives.[6]
The weapon was based upon the same type of corruption previously inflicted upon Ariel, but Gabriel represented a far more important target.
Near the end of Chapter 681, an unnaturally loud gunshot echoed across the battlefield.
Michael immediately screamed Gabriel's name.[3]
Gabriel clutched his chest as extremely dark Miasma began leaking from the wound. The Seven Heavenly Virtues instantly understood what had struck him because Ariel had previously experienced the same form of corruption.[4]
Diablo watched with satisfaction while the purest Angel in Heaven became covered in Black Energy.[4]
Michael demanded to know what had happened, and Asmodeus appeared nearby before declaring that the event represented the will of the Demon King.[4]
Gabriel's corruption rapidly intensified.
An enormous pillar composed of Holy Light and Miasma erupted around him, and his formerly Angelic voice progressively became more Demonic as the foreign Energy overwhelmed his nature.[4]
Gabriel attempted to resist the transformation.
When [[Azrael]] later inspected his condition, however, the Angel of Death immediately understood that Gabriel could not win. The Miasma had already corrupted him too extensively and his Fall had become unavoidable.[4]
The pillar surrounding Gabriel eventually became completely consumed by Miasma.
When it disappeared, Gabriel no longer resembled the exalted Seraph who had entered the battle. His physical appearance had become Demonic and the Energy inside his body was now entirely Miasma.[4]
Gabriel had fallen.
The event devastated the Heavenly army. One of its highest and purest Angels had been forcibly transformed into the very type of being Heaven had fought for countless ages.[4]
Gabriel had virtually no opportunity to explore or control his new existence.
While Michael was being attacked by Sloth and Envy, Diablo entered the battlefield personally. Ariel and another Virtue attempted to intervene but were ambushed by [[Agares]], while Zaladrac prevented the remaining Heavenly elites from easily approaching Diablo.[4]
Diablo reached the newly Fallen Gabriel, who discovered that he could no longer move his body.
Gabriel questioned why he was paralyzed, but Diablo refused to explain.
The Demon King's claw pierced Gabriel's chest.
Diablo ripped out his heart.[4]
Gabriel died almost immediately.
His murder drove Michael into overwhelming rage. The strongest Seraph abandoned his normal emotional discipline and attacked Diablo directly, allowing Diablo's wider ambush to culminate in Michael's death as well.[4]
Diablo stood over the battlefield holding both Angelic hearts.
His plan had succeeded.
Gabriel's heart was now the heart of a highest-order Fallen Angel, while Michael's remained one of the purest Holy hearts in existence. Diablo later combined these with his own heart saturated in the Evil of Hell as components for his attempted evolution beyond the established Balance.[4][7]
The loss of Gabriel and Michael simultaneously devastated Heaven's morale. Ariel was forced to take over command while Azrael, enraged by the murder of his brothers, began exterminating Diablo's Pillar-ranked Demons.[8]

### Relationships

#### Michael
Michael was Gabriel's brother, superior officer and closest battlefield partner. Michael served as commander of the Heavenly Host while Gabriel acted as vice-commander.[3]
Their familiarity was extraordinary. The two had fought beside one another for so long that they could exchange responsibilities in the middle of a major supernatural war with minimal explanation.[3]
Gabriel's Fall emotionally destabilized Michael almost immediately. Michael temporarily stopped properly directing Heaven's army and had to be reprimanded by [[Cassiel]] for allowing concern for one brother to distract him from every other Angel depending upon him.[4]
Gabriel's death completely shattered Michael's remaining restraint. After seeing [[Diablo]] tear out Gabriel's heart, Michael succumbed to rage and recklessly attacked the Demon King, creating the opening that led directly to Michael's own death.[4]

#### Heavenly Father
The [[Heavenly Father]] was Gabriel's creator and sovereign.
Gabriel's official role was directly tied to him: he was the one who announced the word of the Eternal Father, serving as one of Heaven's principal representatives of Divine authority.[1]
Gabriel's Concept of Justice likewise reflected his place within the Heavenly Father's order.
Unlike [[Lucifer]], Gabriel never willingly rejected his Creator. His Fall was forced upon him through [[Diablo]]'s cursed weapon.[4]

#### Lucifer Morningstar
[[Lucifer]] was one of Gabriel's fellow earliest Angels and one of the only beings whose spiritual quality could be directly compared with his own.[5]
[[Diablo]] originally intended to use Lucifer's heart for his transformation. Once Lucifer ceased being a suitable Fallen Angel target, Diablo was informed that only the hearts of the First Three Created Angels could rival its quality. This placed Gabriel and Michael directly at the center of Diablo's plans.[5]
Unlike Lucifer's voluntary rebellion against Heaven, Gabriel's Fall occurred through forced corruption.

#### Azrael
[[Azrael]] was another of Gabriel's ancient Angelic siblings.
After Gabriel was struck by the Cursed Bullet, Michael prematurely summoned Azrael and his Angelic legion to the battlefield because Heaven's situation had become critical.[4]
Azrael examined Gabriel's struggle against the Miasma and immediately concluded that his brother's Fall could no longer be stopped.[4]
Gabriel and Michael's deaths later caused Azrael to lose his restraint and massacre nearly every Pillar-ranked Demon remaining in the immediate conflict.[8]

#### Diablo
Gabriel was one of the most important targets in [[Diablo]]'s long-term plan.
After losing access to [[Lucifer]] as the ideal Angelic component for his transformation, Diablo learned that Michael or Gabriel could provide hearts of comparable spiritual quality.[5]
Diablo therefore arranged for [[Asmodeus]]' faction to produce Cursed Bullets capable of affecting even Highest Order Angels.[6]
Gabriel was forcibly corrupted rather than immediately killed because Diablo required the heart of an extremely high-order Fallen Angel for his ritual. After the corruption transformed Gabriel's [[Holy Energy]] completely into Miasma, Diablo personally removed his heart.[4]

#### Lilith Elderblood
Gabriel regarded Lilith as a fundamental enemy of Heaven's order.
During their final confrontation, he declared that the [[Heavenly Father]] should have destroyed her during Genesis, believing that many later disasters could have been avoided if her existence had ended immediately.[3]
Lilith did not respond normally because [[Diablo]]'s control had suppressed much of her personality. Gabriel instead fought both her and Zaladrac simultaneously as part of Michael's broader strategy.[3]

### Skills & Abilities
Gabriel was an Original Seraph and one of the first three Angels created by the [[Heavenly Father]]. His spiritual authority exceeded ordinary Seraphim and the Seven Heavenly Virtues, while his specific Concept was explicitly identified as Justice.[1][2]

#### Original Seraph Physiology
- Original Seraph Physiology: Gabriel possessed the supernatural physiology of one of Heaven's earliest and highest-order creations.[1]
- Superhuman Strength: As one of Heaven's highest-ranking warriors, Gabriel possessed physical power vastly exceeding ordinary Angels and most supernatural species.
- Superhuman Speed: Gabriel could move through the battlefield as golden Light and react quickly enough to evade the attacks of an Elder-class Demonic Dragon.[3]
- Superhuman Reflexes: Gabriel avoided Zaladrac's large-scale Miasma Breath immediately after entering their confrontation.[3]
- Enhanced Durability: His body possessed the supernatural resilience associated with Original Seraphim.
- Enhanced Stamina: Gabriel could participate in the large-scale Heaven–Hell conflict while fighting some of [[Diablo]]'s strongest forces.
- Extreme Longevity: Gabriel existed long before modern Humanity and survived from the earliest period of the Biblical supernatural order until the Demonic War.
- Regeneration: Gabriel possessed the supernatural healing capabilities expected from a highest-order Angel, although heart removal remained fatal.
- Flight: Gabriel possessed six large white wings enabling unrestricted aerial movement and high-speed combat.[1][3]
- Exceptional Soul Quality: Gabriel's Soul and heart were of a quality comparable to those of the First Fallen, [[Lucifer]]. Diablo was explicitly told that only the First Three Created Angels could provide such a substitute.[5]
- Complete/High-Order Spiritual Existence: Unlike the incomplete spiritual foundations associated with many later Angels, Original Seraphim possess sufficient spiritual authority to embody Concepts.[2]

#### Concept of Justice
- Conceptual Authority — Justice: Gabriel explicitly represents Justice. His status as an Original Seraph grants him Conceptual authority analogous in principle to the Concept-bearing powers of Gods and the Seven Heavenly Virtues.[1][2]
- His exact applications of Justice were never demonstrated in sufficient detail before his death.
- The Concept should therefore be indexed as confirmed, but specific speculative abilities such as absolute judgment, law manipulation, punishment inducement or conceptual erasure should not be attributed to Gabriel without chapter evidence.

#### Holy Energy Manipulation
- [[Holy Energy]] Manipulation: As one of Heaven's purest and highest-order Angels, Gabriel possessed immense quantities of Holy Energy.[4]
- Photokinesis: His movement and manifestations could appear as brilliant golden Light.[3]
- Holy Aura: Gabriel naturally emitted intense holiness powerful enough to veil his face from ordinary perception.[1]
- Holy Weapon Enhancement: Gabriel carried a golden western-style sword suitable for channeling Angelic power in combat.[1][3]
- Anti-Demonic Attribute: Angelic Holy Energy possesses a natural advantage against Demonic Miasma and beings of Hell, making Gabriel inherently dangerous to Demon opponents.
- Holy-Demonic Energy Conversion (Forced; Former): The Cursed Bullet converted Gabriel's Holy Energy into Demonic Miasma during his Fall. This was not a voluntary technique but an external corruption inflicted by [[Diablo]]'s faction.[4]

#### Swordsmanship
- Swordsmanship: Gabriel used a western sword as his primary conventional weapon and was entrusted with engaging Lilith and Zaladrac directly.[1][3]
- Combat Proficiency: His position as Michael's vice-commander required him to operate as both a frontline combatant and high-level military officer.

#### Telepathy
- Telepathic Communication: Gabriel and Michael could communicate mentally across an active battlefield, allowing Michael to transfer orders without physically approaching him.[3]

#### Military Command
- Vice-Command of Heaven's Army: Gabriel served immediately beneath Michael in the Heavenly military structure.[3]
- Battlefield Leadership: Michael and Gabriel could exchange command responsibilities smoothly because of their enormous shared experience.[3]
- Strategic Coordination: Gabriel understood Michael's orders immediately and could occupy major enemy threats while his brother redirected attention toward the greater battlefield.[3]

#### Fallen Angel Physiology
Gabriel became a Fallen Angel only shortly before his death.[4]
- Demonic Transformation: The Cursed Bullet forcibly altered Gabriel's spiritual and energetic state from Angelic to Demonic.[4]
- Miasma Generation: Following the completion of his Fall, Gabriel's internal Energy had become pure Miasma.[4]
- Demonic Appearance: His formerly Angelic appearance became significantly more Demonic as the corruption completed.[4]
- Fallen Angel Heart: Gabriel's newly corrupted heart possessed the combined significance of his original high-order Angelic foundation and his Fallen state, making it one of the key ingredients [[Diablo]] required for his later transformation.[4][7]
Gabriel died too quickly after his Fall to demonstrate the complete range of abilities his new Fallen Angel existence might have developed.

### Weaknesses
- Heart Removal: Removing Gabriel's heart resulted in immediate death despite his status as an Original Seraph.[4]
- Cursed Bullets: [[Asmodeus]]' faction created special projectiles capable of corrupting Highest Order Angels. One such projectile successfully transformed Gabriel from an Original Seraph into a Fallen Angel.[6][4]
- Forced Corruption: Once the curse began converting his [[Holy Energy]] into Miasma, Gabriel could not stop the process through his own Angelic power. Even [[Azrael]] immediately determined that his resistance was futile.[4]
- Post-Fall Paralysis: After completing his transformation, Gabriel was unable to move when [[Diablo]] approached him, leaving him defenseless against the fatal attack.[4]
- Demonic Conversion: The corruption fundamentally altered the Energy sustaining Gabriel's existence, bypassing the natural purity and resistance expected from an Original Seraph.[4]

### Death and Legacy
Gabriel's death was one of the central events that caused Heaven's position in the Demonic War to collapse.
His Fall alone shook the morale of the Angels because one of Heaven's most exalted beings had become a Demon before their eyes.[4]
His subsequent murder triggered Michael's fatal emotional breakdown. Within moments, Heaven lost both its commander and vice-commander, forcing Ariel to assume military control while [[Azrael]] retaliated against [[Diablo]]'s elites.[4][8]
Gabriel's heart remained important after his death.
Diablo eventually possessed three hearts required for his attempted evolution: his own heart saturated with the Evil of Hell, Michael's overwhelmingly Holy heart and Gabriel's recently corrupted Fallen Angel heart.[7]
By combining these conflicting spiritual sources, Diablo attempted to create an existence combining Angelic and Demonic characteristics and transcend the supernatural Balance governing Heaven and Hell.[7]
Gabriel himself did not return after his death.
