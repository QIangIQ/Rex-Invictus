Lucifer Morningstar, originally named Samael, was one of the first Seraphim created by the [[Heavenly Father]], the First of the Fallen, former Sin of Pride, first great King of [[Biblical Hell]], and the realm's former legitimate Ruler. Unlike the overwhelming majority of later Angels, Samael belonged to the earliest generation created with a complete Soul, granting him an existential potential deliberately denied to later Angelic generations and ultimately allowing him to transcend the species in which he had been created.[3]
Following his rebellion and fall from Heaven, Lucifer became one of the foundational figures of Demonic civilization. He ruled Biblical Hell, controlled its ancient gateway system, held the authority of its legitimate Ruler and inspired extraordinary loyalty among Fallen Angels and Demons. His charisma remained so powerful long after his retirement that [[Diablo]] believed approximately sixty percent of Hell's forces could immediately abandon him if Lucifer simply returned and asked them to follow their former King.[4]
Lucifer eventually abandoned Hell and disappeared from the known supernatural world. During this absence he underwent a complete existential rebirth as an Elder God, ceasing to metaphysically count as one of the Heavenly Father's creations and gaining a Divine existence centered on Darkness together with the unusual resurrection mechanisms of the Elder Gods.[5] This transformation rendered the special killing property of [[The Spear of Longinus]] ineffective against him, but his apparent immortality contained a fundamental weakness: his continued resurrection ultimately depended upon the Elder God hierarchy and its leader, [[Ikor-Kar-VI]].[5]
Lucifer later fought [[Victor Elderblood]] alongside Ikor and other Elder Gods at the [[Edge of the Cosmos]]. Although his Darkness, Time manipulation and repeated resurrection allowed him to continue returning to an otherwise hopeless battle, Victor defeated him repeatedly. Lucifer's final death came when Ikor decided that continuing to restore his subordinate was inefficient and instead consumed Lucifer through Predation, converting the former Morning Star into fuel for his own power.[6][2]

### Physical Appearance
Lucifer was considered the most handsome Angel in Heaven and possessed exceptional beauty even by the standards of a Race whose members were naturally supernaturally attractive. In his original Angelic appearance he had blackish-blonde hair, sapphire-blue eyes and white skin, and his outward appearance remained largely recognizable after becoming a Fallen Angel and later a Demon.[5]
[[Aphrodite]], who had met Lucifer before his fall, remembered him as exceptionally beautiful and remarkably pure in demeanor. His transformation into an Elder God finally produced a clearly visible alteration: his formerly normal sclera became completely black while his eyes changed from sapphire blue to glowing gold, visually announcing that his existence was no longer Angelic or Demonic.[7][5]
Lucifer retained elements resembling his previous forms even after his rebirth. When preparing to confront [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] under Ikor's orders, he manifested wings resembling those of a Fallen Angel and covered himself in ornate black-and-gold armor.[8]

### Personality and Traits
Lucifer's defining characteristic was overwhelming pride. His history as the Morning Star, the brightest of the ancient Angels, the First Fallen and one of Hell's greatest rulers gave him an enormous sense of personal superiority. Even when [[Diablo]] impaled him through the chest with Longinus, Lucifer responded with amusement rather than fear because he already knew that the weapon's specialized authority could no longer recognize him as a valid target.[9][5]
His arrogance was accompanied by an obsessive desire for independence and self-determination. Lucifer had already rebelled against the [[Heavenly Father]], abandoned Hell when he became tired of its responsibilities and later fundamentally reconstructed his species so that he would no longer remain one of his Father's creations. He took obvious satisfaction in revealing this accomplishment to Diablo because, from Lucifer's perspective, becoming an Elder God proved that he had escaped limitations imposed upon him at creation.[5]
The irony was that his new freedom was incomplete. The resurrection that made Lucifer almost impossible to permanently kill depended upon an external Elder God authority. Diablo immediately recognized the contradiction: Lucifer had escaped dependence upon the Heavenly Father only to attach his continued existence to another superior being.[5] Lucifer's later dealings with Ikor nevertheless show that he did not regard himself as a blindly obedient servant. When ordered to eliminate [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], he immediately asked what he would receive in return, and Ikor had to promise him what he desired before Lucifer agreed to participate.[8]
Lucifer's personality also changed considerably following his fall. [[Aphrodite]] remembered the Angelic Samael as astonishingly pure, displaying little interest in lust even in her presence. After becoming Fallen and Demonic, Lucifer developed a substantially more indulgent and selfish personality and eventually attempted to court Aphrodite himself.[7] His enormous pride, greed and self-centeredness therefore appear to have developed or intensified after the destruction of his original Angelic identity.
Despite this selfishness, Lucifer retained genuine attachments. The most important was his former wife Lilith. Diablo knew that Lucifer still cared enough about her to place himself at risk and deliberately exploited that affection when constructing the Longinus ambush. Lucifer recognized that the situation was suspicious yet came anyway because Lilith was involved, demonstrating that his pride and desire for freedom had not completely eliminated his emotional bonds.[9]
Lucifer was also an exceptional leader. His influence over Hell was not maintained only through fear or legal authority; ancient Demons and Fallen Angels genuinely remembered and revered him. Diablo openly considered Lucifer more naturally charismatic than Lilith and believed his own armies could collapse politically if the Morning Star chose to reclaim their loyalty.[4]

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Lucifer's influence appears in the story long before his physical debut. As the supernatural conflict surrounding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] expanded, information concerning the ancient structure of [[Biblical Hell]] revealed that an important Key once associated with Lucifer had been broken into separate portions. These fragments became deeply connected to the barriers and gates regulating travel between Hell and the Human World, reflecting Lucifer's former responsibility for controlling the passage between the two realms.[10]
Later, during preparations for the coming Demonic War, [[Aphrodite]] discussed Lucifer's ancient personality while speaking about Heaven and Hell. Having personally known him before his fall, she remembered Samael as an extraordinarily beautiful but remarkably pure Angel who showed no meaningful lust toward her despite her position as the Goddess of Love. His later Demonic personality differed so completely that the Fallen Lucifer eventually attempted to pursue Aphrodite romantically himself.[7]

#### Demonic War
Lucifer's historical importance became increasingly relevant as the Demonic War developed. [[Diablo]] openly acknowledged that the former King's charisma remained one of the greatest threats to his own control of Hell. Although Lucifer had abandoned the realm ages earlier, Diablo believed that approximately sixty percent of his existing forces could immediately defect if Lucifer returned and asked for their loyalty, particularly among ancient Demons and Fallen Angels who still remembered the Morning Star's reign.[4]
Lucifer finally appeared directly when Diablo used Lilith as bait to lure him into a prepared trap. Diablo intended to obtain the First Fallen's heart and attacked Lucifer using [[The Spear of Longinus]], whose exceptional property allowed it to kill beings that remained creations of the [[Heavenly Father]]. The weapon pierced Lucifer directly through the chest, and for a moment Diablo believed his plan had succeeded.[9]
Lucifer instead laughed at him. He explained that Longinus would indeed have been fatal to the Angel, Fallen Angel or Demon Lucifer once was, but the weapon's special authority depended upon its target still being one of the Heavenly Father's creations. Lucifer revealed that he had undergone a complete racial and existential rebirth as an Elder God. His sclera turned black, his eyes glowed gold and the metaphysical foundation that had once defined him as an Angel of Heaven no longer applied.[5]
The revelation destroyed the central premise of Diablo's trap. Lucifer could no longer be permanently killed by Longinus' specialized authority because the Elder Gods had remade him into something the Heavenly Father had never created. Diablo further realized that Lucifer possessed the extraordinary resurrection associated with the Elder Gods: destroying his body was insufficient because his existence could return so long as the external authority supporting that immortality remained active.[5]
Lucifer's transformation also changed his political relationship with Hell. Historically he had been the realm's first great Demon King and its true Ruler, but becoming an Elder God meant he no longer belonged biologically or existentially to the Demonic Race. Diablo therefore believed that, despite Lucifer's enormous charisma, his ability to simply return and reclaim Hell through legitimate Demonic authority had been weakened.[5]
Diablo ultimately chose not to attempt permanent imprisonment. Sealing an Elder God risked provoking the civilization responsible for Lucifer's rebirth, and Diablo had no interest in opening another major conflict while already fighting Heaven and numerous other factions. An Elder God representative eventually appeared to retrieve Lucifer, revealing that the entire confrontation also served as a message from the Elder God faction.[5]
Further information concerning Hell later clarified just how important Lucifer's former position had been. Lucifer had not merely been a Demon King controlling territory through military force; he had been the legitimate Ruler of [[Biblical Hell]], recognized by the supernatural system governing the realm. After he abandoned the position, Hell remained without a genuine Ruler for ages. Even Diablo, after reuniting the two portions of the [[Key of Hell]], admitted that possessing the artifact and controlling the dimension did not make him equivalent to Lucifer because he had never completed the process required to become a legitimate Ruler.[11]
Lucifer's physical infrastructure likewise survived long after his departure. One of Hell's enormous gates had personally been created by Lucifer and connected to Hell's own Energy, causing generations of Demons to regard it as effectively indestructible. [[Victor Elderblood]] eventually disproved that assumption during his conquest by cutting through the ancient structure.[12] Lucifer's former authority therefore continued shaping Biblical Hell even after its creator had ceased to be either Angel or Demon.

#### King Among Kings
Lucifer returned to the active storyline during the conflict with the [[Elder Gods Pantheon]]. By this point he operated beneath [[Ikor-Kar-VI]], although their relationship was plainly transactional. When Ikor informed Lucifer that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]—the being described as the one loved by [[Chaos]]—was fighting and ordered him to eliminate the target, Lucifer first demanded to know whether completing the mission would earn the reward he wanted. Ikor promised that defeating one of the specified targets would grant his request and offered his full support if Lucifer personally eliminated Victor.[8]
Lucifer manifested Fallen Angel-like wings, equipped himself in black-and-gold armor and entered battle with several Elder God assets supplied by Ikor, including a Type-6 [[Destroyer]], Type-5 [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] and a Priest.[8] He ultimately joined Ikor, [[Var-Kar-VI]] and another enormous four-armed Elder creature in confronting Victor.
The confrontation eventually moved into the abnormal region surrounding the [[Edge of the Cosmos]], where ordinary concepts such as Space and Time no longer functioned reliably. Victor fought the assembled Elder Gods for an impossible-to-measure period; his perception could no longer determine whether the battle had lasted centuries, millennia or vastly longer, meaning statements estimating one hundred to one hundred thousand years describe distorted subjective experience rather than a reliable chronological timespan.[2]
Lucifer remained a meaningful participant in the battle due to his Elder God abilities and the resurrection mechanisms sustained by Ikor. His Darkness could create Divine-scale defenses and attacks, while his control over Time was useful enough to interfere with the extreme movement advantage Victor generated through his red Lightning.[6] However, none of this allowed Lucifer to actually overcome Victor. Victor repeatedly killed or destroyed the Elder God combatants, only for Ikor's system to restore them and return them to the confrontation.[6][2]
The endless resurrection was not free. Maintaining and repeatedly restoring his allies consumed Ikor's reserves of Positive Energy, and the older Elder God eventually judged the process inefficient. After Victor defeated Lucifer and the four-armed creature again, Ikor refused to resurrect them. He instead activated Predation and consumed both allies, absorbing their accumulated existence to restore and strengthen himself.[2]
Lucifer's final death therefore did not occur because Victor personally discovered a method of permanently bypassing his resurrection. Victor repeatedly defeated him in combat, but Ikor was the one who ultimately removed Lucifer from existence by consuming the subordinate whose immortality depended upon his faction's support. The fate completed the central irony of Lucifer's life: the Angel who rebelled against his Creator and the King who abandoned Hell because he refused to remain imprisoned by duty ultimately became fuel for the very superior being through whom he had pursued greater freedom.[2]
Ikor later consumed Var-Kar-VI as well before Victor ultimately defeated and devoured Ikor himself.[13] Because Ikor had already incorporated Lucifer through Predation, part of the information and supernatural accumulation originating from the former Morning Star ultimately entered Victor indirectly when Victor consumed Ikor.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Important information concerning Lucifer continued emerging after his death. During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later interactions with the [[Heavenly Father]] and Ariel, the unusual nature of the original Angels was explained more clearly. Lucifer and Lilith possessed complete Souls, giving them an existential flexibility and developmental potential that later Angels were deliberately denied.[3]
The consequences of the first Angelic generation had convinced the Heavenly Father that providing such powerful creations with completely unrestricted spiritual potential was dangerous. Later Angels were therefore created with spiritual structures more specifically suited to their intended nature. Lucifer's eventual transformation into a genuine God was not an inexplicable exception to Angelic limitations; his original complete Soul had always given him the potential to transcend what he had initially been.[3]
Victor later discovered another remnant of Lucifer while investigating [[Cain's Dagger]]. The ancient weapon contained supernatural influences originating from both Lucifer and the Heavenly Father together with the metaphysical consequences of Cain's First Murder. The continued coexistence of powers associated with opposing sides of the Biblical supernatural order contributed to Victor's developing understanding that apparently contradictory forces could exist together in a stable Balance rather than automatically annihilating one another.[14]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Lucifer's original identity was finally confirmed explicitly when the [[Heavenly Father]] referred to his fallen creation as Samael.[1] The name established that Lucifer was not the identity with which the Morning Star had originally been created, but the identity he became known by after the events surrounding his rebellion and fall.
By this period, Lucifer had been permanently dead for centuries, but the consequences of his existence remained embedded throughout Heaven, Hell, the Elder God conflict and artifacts such as [[Cain's Dagger]]. His history also became one of the Heavenly Father's clearest examples of why the earliest generation of Angels had been fundamentally different from those created afterward.

### Skills & Abilities
Lucifer possessed three fundamentally different supernatural states over the course of his existence: Original Seraph, Fallen Angel/Demon and Ruler of Hell, and finally Elder God. Abilities belonging specifically to one phase should not automatically be treated as permanently available to every other phase.

#### Original Seraph Physiology
- Original Seraph Physiology: Samael belonged to the first and highest ancient generation of Angels created directly by the [[Heavenly Father]]. He possessed the immense physical, spiritual and supernatural characteristics expected from an Original Seraph and existed long before Humanity.[3]
- Complete Soul: Unlike the restricted spiritual construction of most later Angels, Lucifer possessed a complete Soul. This allowed far greater existential development and was the underlying reason he could eventually transcend Angelhood entirely.[3]
- Godhood Potential: The Heavenly Father and Ariel explicitly recognized that Lucifer and Lilith's complete Souls gave them the potential to become genuine Gods instead of remaining eternally limited to their original created races.[3]
- Divine/Holy Energy (Formerly): As an Original Seraph of Heaven, Samael possessed the sacred supernatural power natural to the highest Angelic ranks. The exact personal techniques he used before his fall are not depicted.
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Samael possessed strength, speed, durability, reflexes, senses and stamina vastly above ordinary mortal beings.
- Flight: Lucifer possessed Angelic wings and later retained the ability to manifest Fallen Angel-like wings even after becoming an Elder God.[8]
- Agelessness: As an Angelic spiritual being, Samael was not subject to ordinary Human aging.
- Exceptional Charisma: Lucifer possessed one of the most extraordinary leadership presences shown among Demons or Angels. [[Diablo]] believed approximately sixty percent of his forces could abandon him instantly if Lucifer returned and demanded their loyalty.[4]

#### Fallen Angel and Demon Abilities
- Fallen Angel Physiology (Formerly): Samael became the First Fallen after rebelling against Heaven, transforming his relationship with the [[Heavenly Father]]'s order while retaining much of his ancient Angelic power.
- Demon Physiology (Formerly): Lucifer later became one of the foundational Demonic powers of [[Biblical Hell]]. His exact racial transition between Fallen Angel and Demon is not explored in complete mechanical detail.
- Miasma / Demonic Energy (Formerly): As a high-ranking Demon and King of Hell, Lucifer possessed access to the negative supernatural Energy characteristic of Demonic beings.
- Sin of Pride (Formerly): Lucifer historically occupied the position associated with Pride before the later Seven Deadly Sins serving [[Diablo]]. The novel does not reveal a named personal Sin technique belonging to Lucifer, so abilities should not be invented solely from the title.
- Demonic Reconstitution (Formerly): As an extremely high-ranking Demon, Lucifer possessed the spiritual persistence characteristic of Biblical Demons before abandoning that Race. His later Elder God immortality was an entirely different mechanism and should not be conflated with ordinary Demonic reformation.

#### Ruler of Hell Authority
- True Ruler Authority (Formerly): Lucifer was the legitimate Ruler of [[Biblical Hell]] rather than merely a political Demon King. His office connected him to the supernatural system through which the dimension administered sinners and Souls.[11]
- Hell System Connection (Formerly): Lucifer was integrated into Biblical Hell's deeper metaphysical functions. [[Diablo]] later admitted that controlling the realm and reuniting the Key did not make him Lucifer's equal as a legitimate Ruler because he had never passed the corresponding qualification.[11]
- [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] Authority (Formerly): As Ruler, Lucifer possessed legitimate authority throughout Biblical Hell and its administrative systems.
- Soul Administration (Formerly): Rulers oversee the local systems responsible for processing Souls and sinners within their Underworld. Lucifer's authority was subordinate to the greater Soul machinery represented by [[The Judges of The Abyss]], not universal ownership of every Soul in Creation.
- Territorial Authority (Formerly): Lucifer's legitimate Rulership granted far greater supernatural authority within Hell than merely commanding Demonic armies through political power.
- Demonic Command (Formerly): Lucifer ruled Fallen Angels and Demons as Hell's King and continued possessing enormous historical influence over its population even after abandoning the realm.[4]
- [[Key of Hell]] Manipulation: Lucifer possessed the ancient Key connected with Hell's gate and authority. Before leaving, he split the Key and left its portions behind, producing long-term consequences for both Lilith and Diablo.[10][11]
- Artifact Creation: Lucifer personally created one of Biblical Hell's enormous gates and connected it to the realm's own Energy, producing a structure considered effectively indestructible until [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] severed it.[12]
- Hell-Energy Integration (Crafting): The gate demonstrates that Lucifer could construct supernatural artifacts capable of drawing directly upon the Energy of the dimension itself.[12]

#### Elder God Physiology
- Elder God Physiology: Lucifer underwent a complete racial and existential rebirth into an Elder God. This was not a temporary transformation or additional power layered onto his old body; he ceased metaphysically being a creation of the [[Heavenly Father]] and became an existence belonging to the Elder God system.[5]
- Divine Physiology: As an Elder God, Lucifer possessed genuine Divine existence, [[Divine Energy]] and a Soul capable of participating in combat against some of the strongest beings within the Sector.
- Superhuman Strength: Lucifer could participate in the prolonged battle against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] alongside Ikor, [[Var-Kar-VI]] and other Elder God combatants.[6][2]
- Superhuman Speed: Lucifer could meaningfully participate against Victor despite Victor's extreme movement speed and Lightning amplification.[6]
- Superhuman Durability: His Elder God body could withstand the environment and attacks involved in the high-level conflict at the [[Edge of the Cosmos]], although Victor remained capable of repeatedly killing him.[2]
- Extreme Regeneration: Elder God restoration allowed Lucifer to recover from otherwise fatal bodily destruction while the external resurrection mechanism remained active.[5][2]
- Agelessness / Divine [[Immortality]]: Lucifer's Elder God body did not deteriorate through ordinary biological age or natural death.
- Massive Soul: Lucifer possessed an exceptionally large and powerful Soul even after becoming an Elder God. Victor's observations placed his spiritual presence far beyond conventional mortals and minor supernatural beings.[15]
- Divine Energy Manipulation: Lucifer wielded Divine-grade Energy throughout his Elder God state and could continue fighting in an environment where ordinary Space-Time had become unreliable.[2]
- Wing Manifestation: Lucifer could reproduce wings resembling his Fallen Angel appearance despite no longer biologically belonging to the Angelic Race.[8]
- Armor Manifestation: He manifested black-and-gold armor around himself immediately before entering combat under Ikor's orders.[8]

#### Darkness Divinity
- Divinity — Darkness: Lucifer's principal demonstrated Divine Concept as an Elder God was Darkness.[6]
- Darkness Manipulation: He could generate and control supernatural Darkness on a Divine scale.
- Darkness Constructs: Lucifer could form defensive structures from Darkness, including barriers capable of intercepting attacks during the battle against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[6]
- Darkness Generation: His Elder God existence could continuously produce Divine Darkness rather than depending upon surrounding natural darkness.
- Divine Defense: Lucifer used his Darkness as an active defense against high-level attacks rather than merely as concealment or an environmental effect.[6]
The story does not establish that Lucifer became the universal source, embodiment or Primordial authority of Darkness. His title Elder God of Darkness refers to his Divine specialization within the [[Elder Gods Pantheon]].

#### Time Manipulation
- Time Manipulation: Lucifer demonstrated authority over Time during his battle against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[6]
- Temporal Interference: He could manipulate the temporal conditions surrounding movement and combat sufficiently to interfere with Victor's Lightning-enhanced speed.[6]
- Speed Suppression (Through Time): Rather than physically matching every acceleration produced by Victor, Lucifer could use Time to reduce the practical advantage generated by that movement.
- Temporal Combat Application: Lucifer's Time control was developed enough to be used responsively during direct battle rather than functioning only as a ritual or environmental ability.
The complete scale and origin of Lucifer's Time authority are not explained, and the novel does not identify Time as his principal Divinity.

#### Elder God Immortality
- Near-Absolute [[Immortality]] (Externally Sustained): [[Diablo]] concluded that ordinary bodily destruction could no longer permanently eliminate Lucifer after his Elder God rebirth. His existence could return even after otherwise lethal damage so long as the Elder God mechanism sustaining him remained available.[5]
- Resurrection: Lucifer repeatedly returned to the battle against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] after being killed or destroyed, demonstrating the practical effectiveness of the Elder God resurrection system.[6][2]
- Soul Persistence: Destruction of Lucifer's current body was insufficient to permanently terminate his existence while the resurrection system retained his spiritual foundation.
- Longinus Immunity: [[The Spear of Longinus]] possessed a special ability to kill beings created by the [[Heavenly Father]], but Lucifer's complete rebirth removed him from that metaphysical category and therefore nullified the weapon's special killing condition against him.[9][5]
- Racial-Origin Severance: Lucifer's Elder God rebirth fundamentally severed the old relationship through which supernatural effects could identify him as one of the Heavenly Father's Angelic creations.[5]

#### Resistances
- Resistance to Holy/Creator-Specific Killing (Elder God): Lucifer's reborn existence invalidated Longinus' specialized authority against the [[Heavenly Father]]'s creations.[5] This does not establish universal immunity to [[Holy Energy]] or every Divine weapon.
- Resistance to Conventional Death: Destroying Lucifer's body was insufficient to permanently kill him while his Elder God resurrection remained supported.[5][2]
- Resistance to Bodily Destruction: Fatal physical damage could be reversed through restoration and resurrection.
- Extreme Spiritual Resilience: Lucifer's complete Soul and later Elder God existence made him exceptionally difficult to remove through ordinary spiritual damage.
- Environmental Resistance: He could continue functioning in the abnormal [[Edge of the Cosmos]] battlefield where ordinary Space-Time had ceased behaving normally.[2]

### Power and Combat Ability
Lucifer was one of the most powerful beings historically associated with the [[Biblical Pantheon]], but his strength changed dramatically across his three major states. His original status as one of the first Seraphim placed him among Heaven's highest ancient creations, while his reign over Hell combined personal power with legitimate Ruler authority. His Elder God rebirth later replaced both of those racial foundations with a genuinely Divine existence.[5][3]
The most relevant direct scaling comes from his Elder God period. Lucifer was powerful enough for Ikor to deploy him as one of the combatants intended to eliminate [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and possessed useful high-order abilities such as Darkness and Time Manipulation.[8][6] His Time control was particularly useful because it could interfere with Victor's Lightning-based speed advantage, while his Darkness provided high-level defensive applications.
Lucifer nevertheless was not individually comparable to Victor by the end of their confrontation. Victor repeatedly defeated the participating Elder Gods, and Lucifer remained in the battle largely because Ikor's resurrection system continually restored him after death.[2] His most dangerous quality was therefore the combination of strong Divine abilities with repeated restoration rather than superiority over Victor in direct martial combat.
His final death also establishes the limitation of this immortality. Ikor did not need to discover a special anti-resurrection weapon; as the superior Elder God controlling the relevant system, he simply chose not to restore Lucifer and consumed him through Predation instead.[2]

### Weaknesses
- Patron-Dependent [[Immortality]]: Lucifer's Elder God resurrection was not truly self-sufficient. His continued restoration depended upon the Elder God system and ultimately upon superior entities such as [[Ikor-Kar-VI]].[5][2]
- Predation: Complete supernatural consumption could bypass the practical advantages of his resurrection. Ikor permanently eliminated Lucifer by consuming him instead of restoring him.[2]
- Superior Authority Within the Elder God System: The hierarchy responsible for Lucifer's rebirth possessed authority over the very mechanism that made him difficult to kill. This allowed Ikor to turn Lucifer from subordinate into fuel.
- Pride and Arrogance: Lucifer's enormous pride affected his judgment and reinforced his belief that transcending his old Race had made him truly free.[5]
- Affection for Lilith: His lingering attachment to [[Lilith Elderblood]] was strong enough for [[Diablo]] to successfully use her as bait despite Lucifer recognizing the possibility of a trap.[9]
- Loss of Demonic Legitimacy: Becoming an Elder God severed Lucifer's existential identity as a Demon. His historical charisma remained enormous, but Diablo believed the transformation weakened Lucifer's direct claim to rulership over a Demonic civilization.[5]
- Inferior Martial Development to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]: Although Lucifer possessed dangerous Divine abilities, Victor's adaptive combat ability and continual technical improvement allowed him to defeat Lucifer repeatedly during the Elder God confrontation.[6][2]

### Relationships

#### Heavenly Father
The [[Heavenly Father]] created Samael as one of the original Seraphim and granted him the complete Soul that would later make his rebellion and transcendence possible. Lucifer eventually rejected his Creator's order and became the First Fallen, but the relationship continued defining both of them long afterward. The Heavenly Father altered the construction of later Angelic generations partly because beings such as Lucifer demonstrated how dangerous complete Souls could become when combined with enormous Angelic power.[3]
Lucifer's eventual Elder God rebirth represented the ultimate expression of his desire to escape that origin. By ceasing to metaphysically qualify as the Heavenly Father's creation, he rendered Longinus' Creator-specific killing property ineffective.[5] Despite everything that occurred, the Heavenly Father continued remembering him by his original name, Samael.[1]

#### Lilith Elderblood
Lilith was Lucifer's former wife and one of the few beings for whom he retained genuine affection after abandoning Hell. Their relationship eventually ended, but Lucifer continued caring enough about her for [[Diablo]] to exploit the bond directly.[9]
Lilith and Lucifer were also similar in one exceptionally important way: both possessed complete Souls with the potential to transcend their original existences and become genuine Gods.[3] Their daughter, [[Lily Elderblood]], survived both parents' ancient conflicts and eventually became closely associated with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family and later infernal administration.

#### Lily Elderblood
[[Lily Elderblood]] is Lucifer and Lilith's daughter. Lucifer's direct relationship with Lily has received comparatively little attention because he had already abandoned Hell and disappeared from its political structure for much of the era relevant to her adult life. His bloodline nevertheless forms part of Lily's origin and connects her directly to two of [[Biblical Hell]]'s most historically important beings.

#### Diablo
[[Diablo]] was one of Lucifer's successors within Hell and one of the figures to whom the former King entrusted part of the broken Key before abandoning the realm. Their later relationship was openly hostile. Diablo admired Lucifer's charisma while simultaneously resenting and fearing the political threat he represented; he knew enormous portions of his own army might abandon him if the Morning Star returned.[4]
During the Demonic War, Diablo exploited Lucifer's affection for Lilith and attempted to kill him with Longinus. Lucifer's Elder God rebirth caused the plan to fail and allowed him to openly mock Diablo's assumptions.[9][5] Diablo subsequently found particular amusement in the revelation that Lucifer's supposedly superior immortality depended upon another master, seeing it as a contradiction of everything the Morning Star claimed to value.

#### Ikor-Kar-VI
[[Ikor-Kar-VI]] was Lucifer's Elder God patron and the superior authority associated with the faction into which Lucifer had been reborn. Their relationship was based on exchange rather than devotion. Lucifer demanded compensation before accepting the order to eliminate [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], and Ikor promised additional support if he succeeded.[8]
The dependency ultimately proved fatal. Ikor controlled resources required to repeatedly resurrect his Elder God combatants and eventually decided Lucifer was more valuable as fuel than as an ally. After Victor defeated Lucifer again, Ikor abandoned resurrection and consumed him through Predation.[2]

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] encountered Lucifer as an enemy during the war against the Elder Gods. Lucifer possessed sufficient power and specialized abilities to participate alongside several other high-level Elder beings, but he never succeeded in defeating Victor. Instead, Victor repeatedly destroyed Lucifer while using the prolonged battle to refine his own martial abilities.[6][2]
Victor was not technically Lucifer's permanent killer; Ikor consumed Lucifer after one of Victor's victories. Victor later killed and consumed Ikor, indirectly absorbing part of the information and power Ikor had already taken from Lucifer.[13]

### Legacy
Lucifer's legacy extends far beyond his death. He was one of the first examples demonstrating that a created supernatural being could transcend the Race and metaphysical category imposed upon it at birth. His complete Soul allowed an Angel to become Fallen, Demonic and ultimately Divine without losing the continuous identity connecting those existences.[3]
Within Hell, his influence was even greater. Lucifer established political and metaphysical structures that survived for ages after his departure, including the great gate connected to Hell's Energy and the divided Key later inherited by Lilith and [[Diablo]].[10][12] His abandonment also created a lasting institutional crisis because Hell lost its legitimate Ruler and remained without a genuine replacement until far later events changed its administration.[11]
Lucifer's charisma became part of Demonic legend, his Elder God transformation exposed important limitations in weapons such as Longinus, his death demonstrated the weakness hidden inside externally maintained immortality, and the lingering influence within [[Cain's Dagger]] later contributed to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s research into the coexistence of opposing supernatural forces.[5][14]

### Trivia
- Lucifer's original Angelic name was Samael. The [[Heavenly Father]] continued using this name long after Samael became known as Lucifer.[1]
- Lucifer belonged to the first generation of Angels created with complete Souls.[3]
- The complete Soul was one of the primary reasons he could eventually transcend Angelhood and genuinely become a God.[3]
- The problems caused by the original Angels contributed to the Heavenly Father creating later Angels with more restricted spiritual structures.[3]
- Lucifer was considered the most handsome Angel in Heaven.[5]
- According to [[Aphrodite]], Samael was remarkably pure before his fall and showed almost no lust even toward the Goddess of Love; after falling, Lucifer later attempted to court her.[7]
- Lucifer's charisma was considered greater than Lilith's. [[Diablo]] believed approximately sixty percent of his own forces could immediately defect if Lucifer returned to Hell and asked them to follow him.[4]
- Lucifer was a genuine Ruler of [[Biblical Hell]] rather than merely its political King.[11]
- Diablo eventually reunited the divided [[Key of Hell]] and controlled the realm, but still admitted that he had not become a legitimate Ruler equivalent to Lucifer.[11]
- Lucifer personally created one of Biblical Hell's enormous gates and linked the structure to Hell's own Energy. It remained effectively indestructible until [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] cut through it.[12]
- Lucifer's Elder God transformation was a complete existential rebirth rather than a simple racial disguise or additional power.[5]
- Because he was no longer metaphysically a creation of the Heavenly Father, [[The Spear of Longinus]] lost its specialized killing advantage against him.[5]
- Longinus failing against Elder God Lucifer does not mean Lucifer possessed universal immunity to Holy weapons; the specific condition targeted creations of the Heavenly Father.
- Lucifer's Elder God immortality was externally sustained and therefore should not be described as genuinely absolute.[5][2]
- Lucifer negotiated with [[Ikor-Kar-VI]] before accepting the order to attack Victor, showing that his allegiance to the Elder Gods was transactional.[8]
- The apparent statement that Lucifer and the other Elder Gods fought Victor for somewhere between 100 and 100,000 years is not reliable chronological time. The battlefield existed where ordinary Space-Time had become effectively meaningless, and Victor himself could no longer determine how long the confrontation had lasted.[2]
- Victor repeatedly defeated Lucifer, but Victor was not the one who permanently ended his resurrection. Ikor-Kar-VI chose not to restore Lucifer and instead consumed him through Predation.[2]
- Lucifer's death is particularly ironic because his life was defined by attempts to free himself from superior authorities, yet his final Divine existence depended upon a superior Elder God who ultimately consumed him.
- Victor later consumed Ikor after Ikor had already consumed Lucifer, allowing portions of Lucifer's accumulated information and power to reach Victor indirectly.[13]
- [[Cain's Dagger]] contains supernatural influence associated with Lucifer alongside power originating from the Heavenly Father and the consequences of Cain's First Murder.[14]
- Lucifer's historical position as the Sin of Pride predates the later Pride who served among Diablo's Seven Deadly Sins. The two should not be treated as the same character.
- No chapter currently establishes Lucifer as a Primordial God. His final confirmed classification is Elder God.
- Although Lucifer possessed Time Manipulation as an Elder God, his principal explicitly associated Divine specialization was Darkness; the complete origin and scope of his temporal authority remain unexplained.
- Lucifer's first direct appearance occurs in Chapter 614, but his historical influence and artifacts are mentioned considerably earlier, including the material surrounding Hell's Key in Chapter 250.[10][9]
