### Appearances

#### First Mention
Chapter 675

#### Debut
Chapter 682
Azrael is an Original Seraph of the [[Biblical Pantheon]], the Angel of Death and one of the most powerful surviving Angels created by the [[Heavenly Father]]. Unlike Angels whose responsibilities revolve around virtues, warfare or Divine messages, Azrael occupies a unique position within Heaven's cosmic order: he is described as the Death of the Biblical Pantheon, simultaneously functioning as its jury and executioner. He is also the only Angel explicitly authorized by the Heavenly Father to accompany mortals during their final moments.[1]
Azrael's reputation alone is sufficient to unsettle other Angels. When Chastity's prophetic vision mentioned the name "Azrael" while describing the newly emerging King of Hell, the Angels present immediately reacted with alarm before [[Cassiel]] clarified that Azrael's name was being used symbolically because it was practically synonymous with Death.[2]
His direct combat portrayal places him among Heaven's greatest warriors. Following the deaths of Michael and [[Gabriel]], an enraged Azrael tore through [[Diablo]]'s forces and killed every remaining Pillar-ranked Demon on the battlefield except [[Bael]] and [[Agares]]. The narration further states that, among the Demonic forces present, only [[Lilith Elderblood]] or Diablo themselves could fight the Angel of Death on approximately equal terms.[3]

### Physical Appearance
Azrael is an imposing male Angel dressed almost entirely in black. He wears black plate armor and a hood that conceals most of his facial features.[1]
Like Heaven's highest-order Angels, Azrael possesses three pairs of wings for a total of six wings. Unlike the completely white wings commonly associated with Seraphim, Azrael's wings are described as translucent with a mixture of white and black shades, immediately distinguishing him even among Heaven's elite.[1]
His unusual appearance visually reflects his position between the ordinary imagery of Heaven and the phenomenon he governs. Although unquestionably an Angel loyal to the [[Heavenly Father]], Azrael embodies Death rather than the purely radiant concepts generally associated with Heaven.

### Personality
Azrael is cold, severe, stubborn and exceptionally direct. He does not waste time on unnecessary conversation when circumstances demand action and expects other Angels to obey when he gives an order.[1]
His authority does not appear to come primarily from a formal position within Heaven's military hierarchy. Rather, the other Angels listen because of Azrael's personal reputation and overwhelming strength. When he ordered the Seven Heavenly Virtues to abandon their current opponents and move elsewhere during the Demonic War, none of them challenged him.[1]
Azrael is also described as someone who only truly respects and listens to the [[Heavenly Father]].[1] This makes him unusually independent even among the highest-ranking Angels. Ariel herself understood that issuing an order to Azrael did not guarantee compliance.
This stubborn independence became particularly evident immediately before the Heavenly Father's large-scale Judgment. Ariel ordered every Angel to retreat from the battlefield. The entire Angelic army obeyed except Azrael, who continued fighting despite the approaching Divine attack. Ariel did not attempt to force the issue because she knew both that Azrael was enormously powerful and that he was unlikely to listen to her anyway.[3]
Although normally emotionally restrained, Azrael is deeply attached to his Angelic siblings. The deaths of Michael and [[Gabriel]] transformed his usual cold demeanor into extreme fury. He responded not with grief or hesitation but by launching himself directly into [[Diablo]]'s elite forces and slaughtering Pillar-ranked Demons in rapid succession.[3]
Azrael's fury did not make him lose his combat effectiveness in the same manner that Michael's anger had. Instead, he became even more dangerous. The narrative describes him cutting through Demon elites like a true executioner, making his title literal on the battlefield.[3]
Despite this ferocity, Azrael remains completely obedient to the Heavenly Father. Centuries later, when his Father ordered him to participate in the international hunt organized by [[Victor Elderblood]], Azrael accepted the assignment without objection.[4]

### History
Azrael is one of Heaven's Original Seraphim, an ancient order of Angels that predates Humanity.
The exact circumstances of his creation have not been shown, but he was personally created by the [[Heavenly Father]] and was entrusted with an authority fundamentally connected to Death.
Unlike ordinary Death Gods belonging to Pantheons, Azrael functions within the Biblical system as a direct servant of its Creator. His duties are not limited to combat. He is the Angel authorized to accompany mortals during the final moments of life, making him directly involved in the transition between Life and Death.[1]
His reputation became so strongly associated with this function that "Azrael" itself became interchangeable with "Death" among the Angels.[2]

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Azrael was first mentioned shortly before Heaven entered the final stages of the Demonic War.
Chastity, one of the Seven Heavenly Virtues, received a prophetic vision concerning the new King of [[Biblical Hell]]. The name appearing within the vision was Azrael.[2]
The reaction among the gathered Angels was immediate. Several believed the vision implied that Azrael himself had betrayed the [[Heavenly Father]] and taken control of Hell, something they considered virtually impossible because of his absolute loyalty.[2]
[[Cassiel]] clarified that the prophecy should not be interpreted literally. Chastity's visions were often symbolic, and Azrael was simply another name for Death. The new King of Hell was therefore someone existing near the boundary between Life and Death rather than Azrael himself.[2]
Azrael eventually entered the war directly after [[Gabriel]] was struck by the supernatural corruption prepared by [[Diablo]]'s forces.
Gabriel's [[Holy Energy]] began changing into Miasma, and Michael quickly realized that Heaven's existing forces were no longer sufficient. He spread his wings, manifested his halo and requested direct permission from the Heavenly Father to summon another of his brothers.[1]
Permission was granted.
Michael used his Energy as a channel and called:
Azrael.
A gigantic golden portal opened above the battlefield. Hundreds of Angels descended first, followed by the Angel of Death himself.[1]
Azrael emerged wearing black plate armor and a hood, with his six translucent black-and-white wings spread behind him. His appearance immediately distinguished him from the more traditionally radiant Angels surrounding him.[1]
Michael explained that Gabriel had been infected and ordered the complete extermination of the Demonic forces responsible. Azrael needed only a single look at Gabriel to understand his condition. Although Gabriel continued desperately resisting the corruption, Azrael immediately determined that the struggle was futile and that his brother would inevitably Fall.[1]
[[Thor]] approached Azrael soon afterward, openly wondering whether the Biblical Angel of Death or the Death-related entity belonging to the [[Norse Pantheon]] would prove stronger. Azrael refused to indulge the comparison, telling Thor that it was something they would never discover.[1]
He then disappeared as a black streak and appeared before the Seven Virtues.
Azrael ordered the Virtues to leave the Pillar-ranked Demons to him and find other opponents. None argued. The narration explained that when Azrael gave an instruction, the other Angels listened because he respected and obeyed virtually no one except the Heavenly Father himself.[1]
The war deteriorated soon afterward.
Sloth and Envy ambushed Michael while [[Agares]] attacked Ariel and another Virtue. Diablo then personally entered the battlefield and tore Gabriel's heart from his body. Michael lost control after witnessing his brother's death and was killed moments later when Diablo tore out his heart as well.[1]
The deaths of Michael and Gabriel shattered the morale of the Heavenly Host.
Azrael reacted very differently.
The Angel of Death became furious.
As the elite combatants abandoned all remaining restraint, Azrael moved to the front of the chaos and began slaughtering Diablo's elite forces and Pillars.[3]
[[Lilith Elderblood]] was forced to descend from [[Zaladrac Zeovnur Elderblood]] and engage Azrael directly. The narration explicitly states that if there were beings on the Demonic side capable of going toe-to-toe with the Angel of Death, they were Lilith or Diablo himself.[3]
Even Lilith's intervention did not undo the damage he had already caused.
By this stage of the battle, every Pillar-ranked Demon except [[Bael]] and Agares had died because of Azrael's rage.[3]
The Heavenly Father eventually decided to intervene personally through Heavenly Judgment.
Ariel ordered every Angel to retreat from the battlefield before the attack.
Every Angel obeyed.
Azrael did not.[3]
Ariel was not particularly worried. She considered Azrael powerful enough to survive and privately noted that the Heavenly Father would not intentionally strike his favorite son. She also knew that attempting to command Azrael away from the battle would probably accomplish nothing.[3]
The Heavenly Father's Judgment then devastated the battlefield and inflicted catastrophic losses upon Diablo's remaining forces.
The conflict changed again when [[Victor Elderblood]], now the true Demon King of Biblical Hell, appeared.
Victor opened the Gates of Hell and released his newly strengthened Demonic Legions. The density of the Miasma pouring from the deepest regions of Hell immediately altered the environment.[5]
The concentration became so extreme that even Azrael was forced to move away.[5]
The Miasma continued spreading until Heaven's forces could no longer safely participate in the central battlefield. Ariel recognized that Victor had effectively removed the Angels from the conflict while replacing them with hundreds of thousands of Demons personally loyal to him.[5]
Azrael subsequently withdrew with Heaven's forces rather than attempt to remain within an environment increasingly saturated by toxic high-density Miasma.

#### King Among Kings
Centuries later, Azrael remained one of the [[Heavenly Father]]'s most powerful active agents.
Following another major Gathering of Supernatural Beings, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] organized a global hunt against hostile Gods and other supernatural targets. The operation produced an unprecedented coalition between factions that historically would rarely cooperate.[4]
The Heavenly Father contributed two representatives:
- Azrael, the Angel of Death
- [[Ariel Elderblood]], Virtue of Generosity and the current commander of Heaven's Angels[4]
They joined a force that also included [[Thor]], [[Taranis]], [[Cernuno]], Susanoo, [[Takemikazuchi]], [[Loki]] and other powerful representatives of Earth's Pantheons.[4]
When Susanoo jokingly questioned Ariel about participating in a hunt targeting supposedly "innocent" Gods, Ariel replied that the Heavenly Father had ordered their participation and therefore they would obey.
Azrael silently nodded in agreement.[4]
The exchange demonstrated that despite his independent personality, Azrael remained absolutely loyal to his Creator.
Victor eventually arrived accompanied by several of his wives, now transformed into [[True Dragons]]. Even the assembled Gods and Angels were visibly intimidated by the concentration of power represented by Victor's group.[4]
Azrael nevertheless remained part of the coalition rather than challenging Victor, reflecting the changing political relationship between Heaven and Victor's expanding faction.

### Relationships

#### Heavenly Father
The [[Heavenly Father]] is Azrael's creator and the only being Azrael is explicitly described as consistently respecting and obeying.[1]
Azrael's unique role as Death was granted directly through Heaven's system. He alone was authorized to accompany mortals during their final moments, acting as the [[Biblical Pantheon]]'s jury and executioner in matters connected with Death.[1]
Their relationship also appears unusually close. Ariel refers to Azrael as the Heavenly Father's favorite son while considering whether he would survive Heavenly Judgment.[3]
Despite Azrael's stubbornness toward other Angels, he continues carrying out the Heavenly Father's commands centuries later, including joining [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s supernatural hunt upon his Father's orders.[4]

#### Michael
Michael was Azrael's brother and Heaven's Supreme Commander.
Michael possessed enough authority to formally request Azrael's intervention during the Demonic War, but still required the [[Heavenly Father]]'s permission before summoning him prematurely.[1]
Azrael answered Michael's request immediately after learning that [[Gabriel]] had been infected.
Michael's later death contributed directly to Azrael's rampage against [[Diablo]]'s Pillars.[3]

#### Gabriel
[[Gabriel]] was another of Azrael's brothers.
Upon entering the Demonic War, Azrael immediately inspected Gabriel's condition. His connection to Death and enormous supernatural experience allowed him to recognize almost instantly that Gabriel would lose his struggle against the Miasma corrupting him.[1]
Gabriel's eventual murder by [[Diablo]], combined with Michael's death moments later, triggered one of Azrael's greatest displays of rage and combat power.[3]

#### Ariel Elderblood
Ariel and Azrael fought on the same side during the Demonic War, although their roles differed considerably.
Ariel was suited to command and later inherited Michael's old leadership position. Azrael, despite possessing enough power and authority to qualify for command, was explicitly described as more of a warrior than a commander.[3]
Ariel also understood his stubborn personality better than most. When the [[Heavenly Father]] ordered the Angels to retreat before Heavenly Judgment, she did not expect Azrael to obey her instructions.[3]
Centuries later, the two were deployed together by the Heavenly Father during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s supernatural hunt.[4]

#### Lilith Elderblood
Lilith was one of the few Demonic beings capable of directly confronting Azrael during the Demonic War.
After Azrael began massacring the remaining Pillars, Lilith personally descended from Zaladrac and fought him. The narration identifies Lilith and [[Diablo]] as the two Demonic beings capable of going toe-to-toe with the Angel of Death at that point in the war.[3]

### Skills & Abilities
Azrael is one of Heaven's most powerful warriors and the metaphysical Death associated with the [[Biblical Pantheon]]. His status is demonstrated primarily through direct feats rather than through an explicitly assigned power tier.

#### Original Seraph Physiology
- Original Seraph Physiology: Azrael is an ancient Original Seraph and possesses the supernatural characteristics of Heaven's highest-order Angels.[1]
- Superhuman Strength: Azrael could fight elite Pillar-ranked Demons continuously and eventually annihilate almost all of them during his rampage.[3]
- Superhuman Speed: Azrael could disappear as a black beam and reappear elsewhere on the battlefield almost instantly.[1]
- Superhuman Reflexes: His combat level allowed him to fight numerous high-ranking Demons in the chaotic conditions of a large-scale supernatural war.
- Enhanced Durability: Azrael remained active on a battlefield containing Heavenly Judgment, high-ranking Demons, Deadly Sins, Gods and Divine-level attacks.[3]
- Enhanced Stamina: He continued fighting after the deaths of Michael and [[Gabriel]] and personally slaughtered numerous elite Demons without showing the exhaustion expected of lesser Angels.[3]
- Extreme Longevity: Azrael has existed since long before modern Humanity.
- Flight: His six wings allow unrestricted supernatural flight.
- [[Holy Energy]]: As an Original Seraph, Azrael possesses enormous quantities of Holy Energy naturally harmful to Demons.
- Six-Winged Seraph Form: Azrael possesses three pairs of translucent white-and-black wings, a configuration associated with Heaven's highest-order Angels.[1]

#### Death Authority
- Conceptual Authority — Death: Azrael is explicitly described as the Death of the [[Biblical Pantheon]]. He is not merely an Angel whose occupation involves killing; Death itself is his designated supernatural role within Heaven's system.[1]
- Jury and Executioner: The narration describes Azrael simultaneously as Heaven's jury and executioner, linking his authority to the judgment and conclusion of mortal life.[1]
- Death Escort: Azrael is the only Angel authorized by the [[Heavenly Father]] to accompany mortals during their final moments.[1]
- Death Perception / Spiritual Insight: Azrael required only a brief examination of [[Gabriel]] to understand that his resistance against Demonic corruption was futile and that his Fall had become unavoidable.[1]
- Death Association: Azrael's name is so fundamentally associated with Death that Chastity's prophecy could use "Azrael" symbolically to represent the boundary between Life and Death rather than Azrael himself.[2]
The full mechanics of Azrael's Death authority have not yet been shown. Abilities such as automatic death inducement, unrestricted Soul destruction or absolute control over the dead should therefore not be assumed without further chapter evidence.

#### Holy Energy Manipulation
- [[Holy Energy]] Manipulation: Azrael possesses the Holy Energy inherent to Original Seraphim. This Energy is naturally highly effective against Demons.
- Anti-Demonic Attribute: During the Demonic War, Angelic attacks were described as particularly lethal to Demons. Azrael's superior Holy nature contributed to his ability to slaughter Pillar-ranked opponents.[3]
- Holy Aura: As a highest-order Angel, Azrael emits a supernatural Angelic presence distinct from ordinary beings.
- Energy Resistance: Azrael remained capable of fighting despite the enormous supernatural pressures present during the Demonic War.
- Miasma Resistance: Azrael can withstand Demonic Miasma far beyond the tolerance of ordinary Angels.
- This resistance has limits. The Miasma [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] released from the deepest regions of Hell became dense enough that even Azrael was forced to retreat from the immediate area.[5]

#### Combat Abilities
- Master Combatant: Azrael is one of Heaven's greatest frontline warriors and was considered a more natural combatant than battlefield commander.[3]
- Executioner Fighting Style: After Michael and [[Gabriel]] died, Azrael tore through [[Diablo]]'s elite army and Pillars with extreme efficiency, being directly compared to an executioner.[3]
- Multiple-Opponent Combat: Azrael killed numerous Pillar-ranked Demons in the middle of an unrestricted battlefield rather than defeating them through isolated duels.[3]
- High-Speed Movement: He can cross significant battlefield distances as a black streak or beam.[1]
- Overwhelming Killing Intent: His enraged state after his brothers' deaths made him the foremost Angelic killer on the battlefield.[3]

#### Authority and Command
- Authority over Angels: Although he was not Heaven's primary military commander, other high-ranking Angels obeyed direct instructions from Azrael without argument.[1]
- Battlefield Presence: His arrival immediately allowed the Seven Virtues to abandon several Pillar-ranked opponents and redistribute themselves across the war.[1]
- Fear Inducement through Reputation: Merely hearing Azrael's name in Chastity's prophecy caused visible alarm among Angels who knew exactly what the name represented.[2]

### Power
Azrael's exact position within a universal numerical hierarchy is never directly assigned in the relevant chapters.
What the novel establishes is considerably more reliable:
- Azrael is one of the highest-order Angels of Heaven.[1]
- He is the [[Biblical Pantheon]]'s designated Death.[1]
- Following Michael and [[Gabriel]]'s deaths, he slaughtered every remaining Pillar-ranked Demon except [[Bael]] and [[Agares]].[3]
- [[Lilith Elderblood]] personally intervened to stop him.[3]
- The narration specifically states that Lilith or [[Diablo]] were capable of going toe-to-toe with Azrael.[3]
- He was powerful enough that Ariel believed the [[Heavenly Father]]'s Judgment would not casually kill him, although she also believed their Father would avoid deliberately targeting his favorite son.[3]
- Even Azrael was unable to remain close to the extraordinarily dense Miasma [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] released from the deepest layers of Hell.[5]
The previous claim that Azrael could definitively fight "a few [[Primordial Gods]]" is not directly established by the checked chapters and should not be treated as a canonical rank. His demonstrated comparison with Lilith, Diablo and Pillar-ranked Demons is the safer basis for evaluating his combat strength.

### Weaknesses
- Dense Demonic Miasma: Although significantly more resistant than ordinary Angels, Azrael remains an Angel and can be forced away by sufficiently concentrated Miasma. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s release of pure high-density Miasma from Hell became intolerable even for him.[5]
- Stubbornness: Azrael is difficult for other Angels to command and may continue fighting despite broader tactical instructions. He ignored Ariel's retreat order before Heavenly Judgment.[3]
- Emotional Rage: The deaths of Michael and [[Gabriel]] caused Azrael to enter an extremely violent state. Unlike Michael, he remained highly effective in combat, but the reaction demonstrates that even Heaven's Angel of Death is not emotionally detached from his siblings.[3]
- Higher Authority: Azrael remains subordinate to the [[Heavenly Father]] and obeys his Creator's commands even when ignoring the authority of other Angels.[1][4]
