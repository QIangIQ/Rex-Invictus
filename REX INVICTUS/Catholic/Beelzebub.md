Beelzebub, also known as the Lord of the Flies, is a high-ranking Demon and supporting antagonist in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]]. He belonged to [[Diablo]]'s faction and was associated with the 72 Pillars of Hell, serving as one of the influential Demonic nobles during Diablo's struggle against Lilith for supremacy over [[Biblical Hell]]. Although notorious for his cruelty, pride and vindictive temperament, Beelzebub distinguished himself through an extensive intelligence network maintained using supernatural flies, allowing him to gather information throughout Hell. His intelligence operations were sufficiently effective that [[Bael]], Diablo's right-hand man, personally acknowledged their reliability.[1]
Beelzebub's most significant personal conflict involved [[Belial]], another powerful Demon serving Diablo. Their feud originated from Belial's sexual involvement with Beelzebub's wife and daughter, an incident that ultimately resulted in Beelzebub personally killing both women and developing an intense hatred toward the Duke. This animosity later became the basis of a deception orchestrated by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] Alucard, who impersonated Beelzebub while attacking Belial's territory in an attempt to provoke internal conflict among Diablo's forces. Rather than becoming hostile toward Victor, Beelzebub considered the possibility of cooperating with the Vampire against their mutual enemy.[2][3][1]

### Physical Appearance
Beelzebub's complete physical appearance has not been described in detail. He is identified as an insect-type Demon and possesses a supernatural connection to flies, although the novel has not established the exact anatomy of his original Demonic form. During his meeting with [[Bael]] and [[Belial]], he displays recognizable humanoid features and expressions, including prominent veins appearing across his face when enraged. He is also capable of displaying a mocking smile while deliberately humiliating Belial over the curse imposed upon him by [[Aphrodite]].[1]
His height, hair color, eye color and clothing remain unspecified, and the available descriptions do not confirm whether his true form possesses wings, additional limbs or other insectoid characteristics. Consequently, depictions of Beelzebub as a monstrous fly-like creature remain artistic interpretations rather than confirmed representations of his appearance.

### Personality
Beelzebub is an arrogant, vindictive, possessive and profoundly selfish Demon whose personal pride frequently takes precedence over his relationships with others. His feud with [[Belial]] provides the clearest demonstration of his cruelty. After learning that Belial had engaged in sexual relations with both his wife and daughter, Beelzebub regarded the women as permanently tainted and personally murdered them. His subsequent remarks reveal that he considered the incident an intolerable humiliation to himself, demonstrating that his sense of ownership and reputation mattered more to him than his family's lives. Even Belial, himself a notoriously depraved Demon, found the justification hypocritical.[1]
Despite his violent temperament, Beelzebub is capable of exercising restraint when confronted with genuinely superior authority. He openly despises Belial and would have attacked him during their meeting if [[Bael]] had not been present, but he understands that [[Diablo]] prohibits unnecessary conflict between his major subordinates. Beelzebub therefore suppresses his desire for revenge rather than risk disobeying the Primordial Demon. However, this restraint does not prevent him from verbally humiliating his rival, particularly when he reveals that [[Aphrodite]] cursed Belial with impotence. He takes considerable pleasure in the revelation and jokingly suggests becoming one of Aphrodite's worshippers because she has finally done something useful for the Demonic world.[1]
Beelzebub also demonstrates notable intelligence, political awareness and opportunism. Unlike Demons who depend entirely upon physical intimidation, he maintains an extensive surveillance network and actively gathers information about events occurring outside his immediate territory. His reaction to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s attack upon Belial illustrates his strategic mentality: instead of treating Victor as an enemy for impersonating him, he recognizes an opportunity to exploit their shared hostility toward Belial. His awareness of the approaching [[Supernatural Gathering]] further demonstrates that he follows developments among other supernatural factions and can anticipate the political significance of major events.[1]

### Synopsis

#### A New Pillar
Beelzebub first becomes relevant to the narrative during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] Alucard's escalating conflict with [[Belial]]. While separated from most of his family following the closure of [[Nightingale]], Victor repeatedly interferes with Demonic activities and eventually kills Belial's son and heir. When the enraged Duke attempts to confront him through a subordinate, Victor responds by destroying the intermediary and preparing an attack upon Belial's territory. Using dangerous supernatural materials, he creates a compressed explosive weapon and arranges for a portal to open directly above the Duke's castle.[2]
Rather than openly claiming responsibility, Victor deliberately impersonates Beelzebub and declares war upon Belial in his name, fabricating an accusation concerning Beelzebub's wife. The deception exploits the existing hostility between the two Demons, making the supposed declaration of war more believable. Victor launches the weapon through the portal, causing a devastating explosion within Belial's territory and attempting to provoke a conflict between two influential members of [[Diablo]]'s faction.[2]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
The consequences of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s attack become apparent when [[Belial]] discovers the destruction inflicted upon his territory. His subordinates initially struggle to understand why Beelzebub would openly attack another Demon belonging to the same faction, but their master's notorious personal history makes the possibility plausible. The incident attracts the attention of [[Diablo]], who recognizes that the voice and behavior associated with the declaration of war do not match the real Beelzebub. Suspecting external interference, Diablo orders [[Bael]] to investigate the incident and determine who is attempting to create disorder within his forces.[3][4]
Bael subsequently summons Beelzebub and Belial to a meeting under Diablo's authority. The investigation takes place while Diablo is preparing for a larger confrontation against Lilith and the other supernatural factions. His organization possesses considerable military strength, controlling approximately sixty-nine percent of Hell's Demonic forces, but its internal stability depends heavily upon absolute obedience to Diablo. Both Beelzebub and Belial understand that Bael's requests effectively carry their master's authority and that refusing to cooperate could result in severe punishment.[1]
The meeting quickly becomes contentious when Beelzebub confronts Belial over his involvement with his wife and daughter. Belial attempts to dismiss the declaration of war as Victor's deception but makes little effort to conceal his enjoyment of Beelzebub's anger. When accused of sleeping with both women, he openly mocks Beelzebub and claims that they participated willingly. The provocation causes Beelzebub to strike the table in fury, with prominent veins appearing across his face. The narration makes clear that he would have attacked Belial immediately if Bael had not been present to enforce Diablo's authority.[1]
Beelzebub retaliates by exposing information acquired through his intelligence network. He reveals that [[Aphrodite]] placed a curse upon Belial, rendering him impotent after his repeated harassment of the Goddess. The revelation surprises Bael, who nearly laughs at Belial's humiliation, while Beelzebub openly celebrates his rival's misfortune. He mockingly expresses gratitude toward Aphrodite and even jokes about becoming one of her believers, reasoning that the Goddess has performed a valuable service by depriving Belial of his sexual capabilities.[1]
When Belial threatens to pursue Beelzebub's wife and daughter again after removing the curse, Beelzebub reveals that both women are already dead. He admits that he personally killed them after discovering their relationships with Belial, explaining that he regarded them as contaminated and could no longer tolerate their existence. The revelation creates an uncomfortable atmosphere, although Belial responds primarily by criticizing Beelzebub's hypocrisy. Despite having already murdered his own family, Beelzebub remains determined to kill Belial, but Diablo's prohibition against unauthorized conflict prevents him from immediately pursuing revenge.[1]
The investigation also reveals the effectiveness of Beelzebub's intelligence network. Through flies distributed throughout Hell, he has already learned that Belial's capital was destroyed and that Victor Alucard was responsible. Unlike Belial, however, Beelzebub does not consider Victor's actions an immediate personal grievance. He instead recognizes that a Vampire willing to attack a Demon of Belial's standing must possess considerable courage and strength, making Victor a potentially valuable ally. Beelzebub privately considers gathering more information about Alucard and contacting him through a Witch to propose cooperation against their common enemy, although no subsequent meeting or formal alliance is confirmed.[1]
Bael eventually redirects the discussion toward Diablo's military preparations, revealing that the Demon King intends to exploit the approaching [[Supernatural Gathering]]. With the leaders of the major supernatural factions occupied, Diablo plans to attack Human territory and establish strategic military positions for a wider invasion. Beelzebub immediately recognizes the significance of the Gathering, prompting Bael to praise the reliability of his information network. Both Beelzebub and Belial receive instructions to concentrate upon their assigned military objectives while Diablo's other subordinates prepare for an all-out confrontation against Lilith, which is intended to conclude before the Gathering begins.[1]

#### Demonic War
[[Diablo]] eventually launches the Demonic War, deploying enormous Demonic armies across Earth and other supernatural territories. His offensive involves the Keys of Solomon, Demonic Legions and other powerful servants, while preparations connected to [[The Judges of The Abyss]] facilitate the movement of Demons and the collection of souls. Beelzebub's earlier participation in the military planning establishes his involvement in the faction's preparations, but his individual assignments, battlefield engagements and accomplishments during the invasion are not specifically documented.[5]
The war ultimately develops against Diablo's interests as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] builds alliances, undermines Demonic operations and eventually claims the throne of Hell. Following his conquest, Victor becomes the new King of Demons, replacing the former political order and establishing authority over the infernal realm. Although numerous Demons are defeated, killed or brought under the new regime, the available accounts do not establish whether Beelzebub survived the transition, submitted to Victor or was killed during the fighting. His last substantial confirmed appearance remains the meeting with [[Bael]] and [[Belial]], and his ultimate fate should therefore remain classified as unknown rather than presumed dead.[6]

### Skills & Abilities
As a high-ranking Demon associated with the 72 Pillars of Hell, Beelzebub possesses the supernatural nature and authority expected of an influential member of [[Diablo]]'s faction. However, his confirmed appearances primarily establish abilities related to intelligence gathering, surveillance and military leadership rather than direct combat. The following abilities distinguish demonstrated characteristics from racial capabilities whose individual applications remain uncertain.

#### Racial Abilities
- Demon Physiology: Beelzebub possesses the supernatural constitution of a higher Demon, existing beyond the limitations of ordinary Human biology. Although members of his race commonly possess enhanced physical capabilities and Demonic Energy, the novel does not individually establish his strength, speed, durability or regeneration level.[1]
- Longevity: Beelzebub is an ancient Demon who has existed for millennia, greatly exceeding the natural lifespan of ordinary Humans. His precise age and the circumstances of his creation have not been revealed.[1]
- Insect Physiology: Beelzebub possesses an insect-associated Demonic nature, reflected in his title and supernatural connection with flies. His exact biological characteristics remain unknown, and abilities such as metamorphosis, insectoid regeneration or natural flight cannot be attributed to him without additional evidence.[1]

#### Supernatural Abilities
- Fly Manipulation: Beelzebub possesses a supernatural association with flies, allowing him to maintain an extensive information network throughout Hell. These flies serve as agents of surveillance, gathering intelligence about events occurring beyond his immediate surroundings.[1]
- Remote Surveillance: His flies provide information concerning events in [[Belial]]'s territory without requiring Beelzebub to be physically present. Through this network, he learns of the destruction of Belial's capital and identifies [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] as the perpetrator.[1]
- Distributed Information Gathering: Beelzebub maintains multiple sources of information across Hell, enabling him to remain informed about political and military developments. [[Bael]] explicitly recognizes the reliability of this network, although its complete range and limitations are not established.[1]
- Enhanced Espionage: Beelzebub acquires sensitive information that other influential Demons do not necessarily possess, including Belial's impotence curse and developments surrounding the approaching [[Supernatural Gathering]]. His intelligence network provides a significant strategic advantage, allowing him to evaluate events and exploit his enemies' vulnerabilities.[1]

#### Non-Combat Skills
- Intelligence Network Management: Beelzebub demonstrates exceptional skill in maintaining and interpreting information gathered through his flies. His ability to obtain accurate intelligence across Hell makes him one of the more effective information-oriented subordinates shown within [[Diablo]]'s faction.[1]
- Political Awareness: He understands the relationships and rivalries among major Demonic figures and recognizes the importance of developments involving outside supernatural factions. His knowledge of the [[Supernatural Gathering]] before [[Bael]] formally explains Diablo's intentions demonstrates this awareness.[1]
- Strategic Thinking: Beelzebub recognizes an opportunity to exploit [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s hostility toward [[Belial]] rather than immediately retaliating against the Vampire for impersonating him. He considers using an intermediary to establish contact and potentially arrange cooperation against their mutual enemy.[1]
- Military Leadership: Beelzebub occupies a sufficiently important position within Diablo's faction to receive independent strategic assignments. Bael treats him as one of the commanders responsible for implementing the Demon King's military objectives rather than an ordinary subordinate.[1]
- Psychological Provocation: Beelzebub deliberately exploits sensitive information to humiliate individuals he dislikes, most notably when he publicly reveals [[Aphrodite]]'s curse upon Belial. This reflects his knowledge of psychological vulnerabilities rather than a demonstrated supernatural mind-manipulation ability.[1]

### Relationships

#### Diablo
[[Diablo]] was Beelzebub's ruler and the supreme authority of the faction to which he belonged. Although Beelzebub possessed influence and military responsibilities, his personal ambitions remained subordinate to the Primordial Demon's commands. Diablo maintained control over his servants through fear, treating disobedience as grounds for execution, and Beelzebub understood that his hatred toward [[Belial]] did not justify violating this rule. His restraint during [[Bael]]'s meeting demonstrates the extent of Diablo's authority over even the most prideful Demonic nobles. Beelzebub's allegiance following Diablo's defeat has not been established.[1]

#### Belial
[[Belial]] was Beelzebub's greatest known personal enemy despite their shared allegiance to [[Diablo]]. Their feud originated when Belial engaged in sexual relations with Beelzebub's wife and daughter, prompting Beelzebub to murder both women and develop an intense desire for revenge. Their hostility became sufficiently notorious that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] could plausibly impersonate Beelzebub while declaring war against Belial. During [[Bael]]'s meeting, Beelzebub repeatedly humiliates the Duke, particularly by revealing the impotence curse imposed upon him by [[Aphrodite]]. Although both Demons openly despise one another, Diablo's prohibition against internal conflict prevents them from pursuing their grievances without risking punishment.[3][1]

#### Bael
[[Bael]] served as [[Diablo]]'s right-hand man and possessed authority over Beelzebub within the faction. He summoned Beelzebub and [[Belial]] to investigate the destruction of Belial's territory, subsequently preventing their confrontation from escalating into violence. Although Bael outwardly maintained control of the meeting, he appeared amused by their personal disputes and nearly laughed when Beelzebub revealed Belial's curse. He nevertheless acknowledged Beelzebub's capabilities, explicitly praising the reliability of his intelligence network when the Demon demonstrated knowledge of the approaching [[Supernatural Gathering]].[1]

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] Alucard indirectly became involved in Beelzebub's affairs by impersonating him during an attack upon [[Belial]]'s territory. The deception was intended to provoke conflict within [[Diablo]]'s faction, but Beelzebub responded with interest rather than anger after discovering Victor's identity. He considered the Vampire courageous and powerful for openly attacking an established Demon Duke and recognized that their mutual hostility toward Belial could provide the foundation for an alliance. Beelzebub contemplated contacting Victor through a Witch intermediary, although the novel has not confirmed that the two ever met or established formal cooperation.[2][1]

#### Aphrodite Elderblood
Beelzebub has no established personal relationship with [[Aphrodite]], but he greatly approves of her decision to curse [[Belial]] with impotence. After learning about the incident through his intelligence network, he deliberately exposes the information during [[Bael]]'s meeting and takes obvious pleasure in his rival's humiliation. His suggestion that he should become one of Aphrodite's worshippers is presented as mockery directed toward Belial rather than evidence of genuine religious devotion.[1]

#### Family
Beelzebub had an unnamed wife and at least one unnamed daughter, both of whom became sexually involved with [[Belial]]. Upon discovering their relationships, he personally killed both women because he considered them contaminated and viewed the incident as an intolerable affront to his pride. His actions demonstrate an extreme possessiveness in which maintaining his own reputation outweighed familial affection. Their names, abilities and individual histories have not been revealed, and no other members of Beelzebub's immediate family have been conclusively identified.[1]

### Trivia
- Beelzebub is known as the Lord of the Flies, a title reflected in his supernatural intelligence network. His association with flies is one of the few personal abilities directly demonstrated in the novel.[1]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] first invokes Beelzebub's identity in Chapter 249 while impersonating him during the attack upon [[Belial]]'s territory. Beelzebub's first substantial physical appearance occurs in Chapter 328.[2][1]
- Beelzebub killed his own wife and daughter after discovering their involvement with Belial. Their deaths were caused by Beelzebub himself rather than the Duke.[1]
- He openly desired Belial's death but refrained from attacking him because [[Diablo]] prohibited unnecessary conflict among his subordinates.[1]
- Beelzebub's information network allowed him to identify Victor as the person responsible for destroying Belial's capital, despite the Vampire's attempt to frame him for the attack.[1]
- [[Bael]] explicitly praised Beelzebub's intelligence-gathering capabilities when the latter demonstrated knowledge of the [[Supernatural Gathering]].[1]
- He considered forming an alliance with Victor against Belial, but no confirmed chapter establishes that the proposal was actually delivered or accepted.[1]
- Beelzebub's precise physical appearance and individual combat capabilities remain largely unknown. His rank should not be used to assign abilities demonstrated by unrelated Demon Dukes.
- In historical religious and demonological traditions, Beelzebub is associated with [[Baal]]-Zebub and later depictions of powerful infernal beings. These traditions are separate from his established history and abilities within the novel.
