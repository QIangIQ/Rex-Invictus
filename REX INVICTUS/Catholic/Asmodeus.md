**[[Asmodeus]]** is the 32nd of the [[Demon Lords of Hell]] of the Ars Goetia, a king of demons associated with lust and wrath. In Rex Invictus he is a present-day power who fights the angel Sariel in his throne room ([[Ch17 - Rex Invictus X|Ch17]]). [[Condottiero]] is his disciple.

## Greek History (IRL)

**IRL tradition.** Asmodeus (Ashmedai) appears in the Book of Tobit, where he kills seven husbands of Sarah until the archangel Raphael binds him. The Talmud makes Ashmedai king of demons who helped build Solomon's temple. The 17th-century *Lesser Key of Solomon* (Ars Goetia) lists him as the 32nd spirit, a king with three heads (bull, man, ram) riding a dragon, who commands 72 legions.

#### Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
There is only one Asmodeus in the story: the present-day demon lord. Two points to keep in mind:
- **Future data.** [[Répertoire du Savoir Athénien|The Répertoire]] (see `B03_AtRep_Repertoire_du_Savoir_Athenien.md`) lists him with a "UOE unified" ranking (2563). Since it ranks him in the [[Undying Olympian Empire]]'s system, he presumably exists, or is at least recorded, in that future era. The text never shows a future Asmodeus.
- **Old-timeline knowledge.** [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] knows from his old timeline that Evergreen spies "would collapse the structure from the inside a few millennia later" ([[Ch04 - Rex Natus II|Ch4]]). By giving Asmodeus the Eye, Percy may have changed that future. Evergreen still exists in the far-future era ([[Ch18 - Rex Invictus XI|Ch18]] "Evergreen pilots"; [[Ch20 - Rex Invictus XIII|Ch20]], where [[Bounty Hunters of Dead Gaia|Asimov]]'s friend is "One of the [[Evergreen Family]] branch managers").
- **Not to be confused with** his servant [[Condottiero]] (`I02_Condottiero.md`), who did the ambush and the killing.

#### Appearance
- **[[Ch04 - Rex Natus II|Ch4]]:** He looks like an ordinary rich man: "Shoulder length blonde hair, sharp nose, and high cheekbones." What gives him away are his eyes, "glowing eyes burning with balefire" (the race's signature), and "a mouthful of spiked teeth." When angered, his face "briefly twisted into an unrecognizable form." His arm makes "strange cracking noises" as it reaches out, and he turns his head "a full 180 degrees" with crunches. His voice has a "grinding timbre."
- **[[Ch08 - Rex Invictus I|Ch8]] and [[Ch17 - Rex Invictus X|Ch17]]:** His eyes are "crimson" and "glowing with intensity," with sharp fangs. He has shrunk the Eye of the Cyclops and set it in his **left eye socket**, where it sits "pulsing faintly." Smoke curls from him, and in [[Ch17 - Rex Invictus X|Ch17]] he has a "toothy grin."

#### Personality
- **Greedy and openly treacherous, at least in what he says.** Offered a bargain, he first threatens to take the Eye "from your cold, dead hands" and sneers that demons "backstab, kill, and plunder" ([[Ch04 - Rex Natus II|Ch4]]).
- **Ruled by fear of his superiors.** One mention of [[Paimon]] makes him freeze and ask how [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] knows her ([[Ch04 - Rex Natus II|Ch4]]).
- **Jealous of rivals.** The name [[Dantalion]] makes him slam his fist on the armrest. He closes the deal at once to get ahead of that rival ([[Ch04 - Rex Natus II|Ch4]]).
- **Territorial.** [[Werewolves]] in his domain outrage him: "You should have started with that first, demigod. I would have wiped them out without the offering" (Ch4).
- **Paranoid.** His first question about Percy is "A spy? Or an assassin?" (Ch4).
- **Cold, calculating revenge.** Through the Eye he finds the Evergreen spies. Instead of flying into a fury he feels "a cold, calculating satisfaction," burns the spies to ash in front of terrified staff, and swears the [[Evergreen Family]] "would be left with nothing beneath the soil they stood on" ([[Ch08 - Rex Invictus I|Ch8]]).
- **Honors contracts in practice.** For all his talk, he keeps the deal: he sends [[Condottiero]] and orders "Spare no expense." Later he rewards Percy with intelligence and safe passage. Percy trusts that his servant "will not break his master's word" ([[Ch17 - Rex Invictus X|Ch17]]).
- **Capable of gratitude.** In [[Ch17 - Rex Invictus X|Ch17]] his satisfaction is "a rare warmth in a being of shadow." He even says it "would be horrible if you perished midway in your quest."
- **Arrogant toward heaven.** He mocks Sariel ("Balance? Child...") and calls the angel "but a flare" ([[Ch17 - Rex Invictus X|Ch17]]).

When he gives orders his phrasing turns formal ("with extreme prejudice"). He doesn't swear in the modern sense, though he does say "bastards" about Evergreen.

#### Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- Commands Disciples of the Lesser Keys. Drinking a drop of a Disciple's Daemonblood teleports the drinker to him ([[Ch04 - Rex Natus II|Ch4]]).
- Catches objects "with a swirl of red energy"; his body can stretch and twist ([[Ch04 - Rex Natus II|Ch4]]).
- **Eye of the Cyclops.** Worn in his left socket, it shows hidden truths. Through it he sees spies as "faceless shadows" along with their "Encrypted messages, dead drops" ([[Ch08 - Rex Invictus I|Ch8]]). He can hide it magically ("magicked away") in the middle of a fight ([[Ch17 - Rex Invictus X|Ch17]]).
- Turns traitors to ash from a distance ([[Ch08 - Rex Invictus I|Ch8]]).
- In combat against an angel: shadows soak up holy light, he reforms his shape in mid-air, strikes with "blackened tendrils," and moves "faster than any mortal eye could track" ([[Ch17 - Rex Invictus X|Ch17]]).
- Controls the **Hellway**, a demonic shortcut road that brought the questers from his fortress to the outskirts of [[San Francisco]] ([[Ch17 - Rex Invictus X|Ch17]]).
- Has a personal castle 0.8 km northwest of the Yosemite church ([[Ch03 - Rex Natus I|Ch3]]), and a fortress "in Hell" with an obsidian throne ([[Ch08 - Rex Invictus I|Ch8]] and Ch17).
- Gathers intelligence across pantheons (Ch17; see Story Arc).

#### Backstory
Very little is given. He is the 32nd demon lord, serves the female demon [[Paimon]], and competes with [[Dantalion]]. His faction is at war with the [[Evergreen Family]], "a power that sought to control both Heaven and Hell" ([[Ch08 - Rex Invictus I|Ch8]]), whose spies had worked their way into the demon lords' ranks. [[Percy Jackson|Percy]]'s old-timeline knowledge says that infiltration would eventually bring the demon hierarchy down. *Canon/myth baseline (not story):* in the Ars Goetia, Asmodeus (Asmoday) is the 32nd spirit, a king. Paimon is traditionally male, while this story makes her female. In the Book of Tobit he is the demon of lust.
- **Author's vault note (not yet in text):** the vault's *Demon Lords of Hell* note lists eight Demon Lords (kings) of Hell under **[[Lucifer]]**: [[Baal]], Paimon, [[Beleth]], [[Purson]], **Asmodeus (5th)**, [[Vine]], [[Balam]] and [[Zagan]]. Ranking him just below Paimon (2nd) fits his service to Paimon; "32nd" is his Goetic spirit number. The vault's own ranking for him matches the text (#2563). The vault's `Asmodeus.md` and `Paimon.md` are otherwise empty.

Asmodeus is an ancient high-ranking Demon, an Ancient Spirit, former 32nd Key of Solomon and 32nd Duke of Hell, and one of the most dangerous occult specialists who served under [[Diablo]].[3][1] Unlike many of Diablo's other elite Demons, Asmodeus specialized less in straightforward warfare and more in supernatural research, Soul-based experimentation, [[Necromancy]], corruption weapons, and the creation of tools capable of harming beings that ordinary Demonic weapons could not easily affect.
His reputation preceded his physical appearance by hundreds of chapters. [[Niklaus Horseman]] specifically warned Theo never to enter into a contract with Asmodeus, internally identifying him as a Demon of the highest rank and judging Theo far too weak-minded to survive contact with such an existence.[3]
During the Demonic War, Asmodeus operated primarily from Diablo's laboratories. He conducted research on unidentified supernatural materials supplied to Diablo, developed corruption-based weapons, mass-produced the Cursed Bullets prepared for the Highest Order Angels, and practiced an extreme form of Necromancy capable of dragging ancient mythological entities back from death by exploiting the Souls of the deceased.[4][5][1][6]
Following Diablo's defeat and death at the hands of [[Victor Elderblood]], Asmodeus did not submit to the new ruler of Hell. Instead, he exploited the chaos surrounding the collapse of Diablo's regime, stole the forbidden [[Egg of Creation]], and disappeared. Victor later learned through Diablo's memories that Asmodeus was responsible for the theft and acknowledged that locating him would be extremely difficult precisely because Asmodeus was a Spirit.[2]
As of his latest confirmed activity, Asmodeus remains alive and at large with the Egg of Creation in his possession.

### Physical Appearance
Asmodeus' true physical appearance has never been clearly revealed.
When he first manifested directly before [[Diablo]], he appeared as a darkened Spirit rather than a conventional humanoid Demon. His form was obscured and spectral, while his voice was distorted and sounded as though several voices were speaking simultaneously.[5]
During his later intervention in the battle involving the Angels, Asmodeus was again described more as a shadow than a conventional physical Being. He possessed Demonic wings and displayed a large, disturbing smile while surrounding himself with the sinister atmosphere characteristic of his Demonic nature.[6]
His tendency to manifest in shadowy and incorporeal forms is consistent with his classification as an Ancient Spirit and later explains why even [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s forces considered him unusually difficult to locate after the fall of Diablo.[1][2]
The multiple voices heard whenever Asmodeus speaks have previously been interpreted as evidence that he contains several Souls. However, the novel only explicitly describes his voice as containing multiple voices; the precise metaphysical reason for this phenomenon has not yet been confirmed.[5]

### Personality
Asmodeus is secretive, intelligent, methodical, cruel, and deeply interested in supernatural experimentation.
Unlike Demons whose primary value to [[Diablo]] came from raw military power, Asmodeus appears most comfortable working behind the scenes. For a substantial portion of the Demonic War, he remained inside a laboratory developing weapons and researching supernatural materials instead of participating openly on the front lines.[5][1]
His obedience toward Diablo initially appeared absolute. When the Demon King requested research results, Asmodeus addressed him respectfully as "My King" and immediately made the products of his work available.[5] Likewise, when Diablo ordered further corruption weapons to be produced from the Souls of Helheim's dead, Asmodeus continued serving as the specialist responsible for such projects.[4]
His loyalty, however, had clear limits. Once Diablo's power structure collapsed and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became the ruler of Hell, Asmodeus did not remain behind to defend his former King's legacy or submit to Victor. Instead, he stole the [[Egg of Creation]]—one of Diablo's most valuable and forbidden artifacts—and escaped with it.[2] This demonstrates a strongly opportunistic and self-preserving side to his personality.
Asmodeus also displays pronounced sadistic tendencies. During the Angelic conflict, his appearance was accompanied by a disturbing laugh, and he showed no hesitation while using corruption, [[Necromancy]], and Soul-based techniques against his enemies.[6]
His Necromancy is particularly revealing of his morality. Rather than merely animating empty corpses, Asmodeus uses the Souls of deceased beings in ways that interfere with the normal processes of death and judgment. [[Morgana Elderblood]] regarded what he had done as considerably worse than conventional Witch Necromancy precisely because the Souls involved were prevented from proceeding normally after death.[6]
Asmodeus also possesses the mentality of a researcher. His value to Diablo came from understanding how Souls, Miasma, corruption, Demonic Energy, and foreign supernatural technologies could be repurposed into practical weapons. His collaboration with James further indicates that he was willing to work with outside researchers when their knowledge could advance his projects.[2]

#### A New Pillar
Asmodeus was first mentioned long before the beginning of the Demonic War.
During a conversation involving Theo and [[Niklaus Horseman]], Niklaus warned Theo that there was one Demon with whom he should never attempt to form a contract: Asmodeus.[3]
Theo questioned the warning, but Niklaus considered the matter far more seriously. In his judgment, Asmodeus was a Demon of the highest rank, while Theo's weak and corrupted mental state made him completely unsuitable for dealing with such an existence. Niklaus believed that allowing Theo to enter a contract with Asmodeus could lead to catastrophic consequences rather than granting Theo the power he desired.[3]
This early warning established Asmodeus as dangerous even among high-ranking Demons before he ever appeared personally.

#### Demonic War
As [[Diablo]]'s invasion expanded, Asmodeus became one of the Demon King's principal occult specialists.
Rather than deploying him immediately as an ordinary battlefield commander, Diablo kept Asmodeus involved in research and weapons development. When Diablo required another weapon of corruption, he instructed Death to contact Asmodeus and specifically ordered that the Souls of those who had died in Helheim be used as material.[4]
The order demonstrated both Asmodeus' specialization and the methods available to him. Souls were not merely sources of Energy in his research; they could be processed into supernatural weapons designed to corrupt beings that ordinary Demonic attacks might not easily defeat.[4]
Later, while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s conquest of Hell continued undermining Diablo's regime, Diablo personally contacted Asmodeus. A darkened Spirit manifested before the Demon King, speaking through a distorted voice containing several voices at once. Asmodeus reported that he was still working inside the laboratory and that the results of research involving a mysterious "guest's gift" were already prepared for Diablo to claim.[5]
This was one of the clearest demonstrations that Asmodeus functioned as more than simply the 32nd Duke. He was effectively one of Diablo's supernatural researchers, trusted with dangerous foreign materials and experiments considered important enough for the Demon King to inspect personally.
By Chapter 667, Diablo was forced to reconsider how his remaining forces should be used. Before one of his subordinates departed, Diablo ordered that Asmodeus be contacted and declared that it was finally time for the Ancient Spirit to leave his laboratory.[1]
The possibility immediately raised concern regarding the production of the Cursed Bullets. Diablo responded that the quantity Asmodeus had already produced was sufficient for their intended targets: the Highest Order Angels.[1]
This establishes Asmodeus as the researcher responsible for one of Diablo's most dangerous anti-Angel weapons.
His most significant direct battlefield activity occurred shortly afterward.
While Victor's allies observed the wider war, the supernatural factions discovered that supposedly dead mythological beings had begun reappearing as Undead. Among those revived were [[Kumbhakarna]] from the Hindu side and [[Yamata-no-Orochi]] from the [[Shinto Pantheon]].[6]
A recording showed a Being covered in Miasma performing the resurrection of Yamata-no-Orochi. Morgana identified the culprit immediately:
Asmodeus.[6]
The discovery horrified those familiar with [[Necromancy]] because Asmodeus was doing far more than simply manipulating dead flesh. The ritual involved Souls themselves. The deceased were being dragged back into the world rather than being allowed to proceed through the ordinary system of death, judgment, and reincarnation.[6]
Asmodeus was therefore effectively turning ancient mythological dead into reusable weapons for Diablo's war.
During the same stage of the conflict, Asmodeus directly participated in Diablo's operation against the highest-ranking Angels. He appeared as a winged shadow with a disturbing smile and used his abilities as part of the plan that corrupted [[Gabriel]], whose Holy nature became contaminated until the Archangel's presence turned Demonic and evil.[6]
Michael immediately recognized Asmodeus and demanded to know what he had done, while Asmodeus simply attributed the operation to the will of the Demon King before disappearing once again.[6]
The attack produced an explosive collision of Miasma and Holy Power, after which Gabriel's Angelic state was catastrophically altered. Later information explicitly identifies the pure-Miasma projectile involved in Gabriel's corruption as part of the same research associated with Asmodeus and his collaborators.[6][2]
Asmodeus later appeared again during the confrontation surrounding Michael. His spiritual and stealth-based nature allowed him to approach unexpectedly, placing a cold hand upon Michael's neck before announcing himself through his characteristic laugh.[6]
After Diablo acquired the components he required from the Angelic battlefield, Asmodeus asked his King for permission to consume what remained. Diablo granted the request.[6]
Although the chapter does not fully explain the precise mechanics of Asmodeus' consumption, the scene further reinforces his association with Souls, spiritual consumption, and the remains of powerful supernatural beings.
The Demonic War ultimately collapsed despite Diablo's preparations. Victor conquered Hell, was accepted by its throne, defeated the forces sent against him, and eventually devoured Diablo himself.[7]
Asmodeus was one of the significant Demonic specialists who escaped the collapse rather than being consumed or forced into Victor's new regime.

#### King Among Kings
Following [[Diablo]]'s death, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] gained access to enormous portions of the former Demon King's memories.
Those memories allowed him to locate Diablo's hidden bases, projects, and conspirators. Victor sent his own Demonic forces to reclaim the resources that now legally belonged to the new ruler of Hell, but one extremely important object was missing:
the [[Egg of Creation]].[2]
From Diablo's memories, Victor learned that Asmodeus had stolen it.[2]
The Egg of Creation was not an ordinary artifact. It was a forbidden Demonic tool constructed from Blood, organs, and Souls. Diablo had used it as a storage vessel for the Souls he collected, allowing those Souls to be used to revive Demons and empower selected individuals. Its greatest intended purpose was to act as part of the ritual through which Diablo planned to reconstruct his Race and Soul into that of a Nephalem.[2]
Asmodeus therefore escaped the Demonic War carrying one of the most dangerous pieces of Demonic supernatural technology in existence.
Victor immediately recognized the difficulty of retrieving it. Because Asmodeus was fundamentally a Spirit, conventional tracking methods were poorly suited to locating him. Victor explicitly described him as exceptionally difficult to track for precisely this reason.[2]
Diablo's memories also revealed another dangerous connection: Asmodeus and James had worked together several times in the past.[2]
This information became particularly alarming once Victor considered the corruption weapon used against [[Gabriel]]. A projectile containing concentrated Miasma had been capable of corrupting one of Heaven's highest-ranking Angels until Gabriel fell. If James possessed Asmodeus' research or had independently learned the same principles through their collaboration, [[New Dawn]] could potentially reproduce weapons based upon that technology.[2]
Victor therefore treated Asmodeus as more than a surviving Demon fugitive. He represented a loose end connecting Diablo's occult research, the Egg of Creation, anti-Angel corruption technology, and individuals already associated with New Dawn.
When Victor later considered how to protect his household from hostile infiltrators, he specifically recognized Spirit-like or dimensional specialists such as Asmodeus as beings capable of avoiding ordinary detection. Zaladrac's exceptional senses were considered useful precisely because someone like Asmodeus would have a much lower chance of slipping past her unnoticed.[2]
As of the latest confirmed information, Asmodeus has not been captured, killed, or forced to return the Egg of Creation.

### Skills & Abilities
Ancient Spirit/Demon Physiology: Asmodeus is both a high-ranking Demon and an explicitly identified Ancient Spirit.[1] His existence therefore differs from conventional flesh-and-blood Demons, granting him an unusually elusive spiritual nature and making conventional tracking considerably less effective.[2]
- Spirit Form: Asmodeus can manifest as a darkened spiritual figure rather than maintaining an ordinary solid body. His first direct appearance consisted of a shadow-like Spirit forming before [[Diablo]].[5]
- Incorporeal/Elusive Nature: Asmodeus' existence as a Spirit makes him extremely difficult to locate or track through ordinary methods. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly cited his spiritual nature as the reason finding him after the theft of the [[Egg of Creation]] would be difficult.[2]
- Stealth and Concealment: Asmodeus repeatedly appears unexpectedly around powerful supernatural beings and disappears after completing his task. His intervention against the Angels demonstrates that he can approach or withdraw without relying upon straightforward physical movement.[6]
- Demonic Wings: When appearing directly during the Angelic battle, Asmodeus manifested Demonic wings as part of his shadow-like form.[6]
- Miasma Manipulation: Asmodeus possesses extensive knowledge and practical control over Demonic Miasma. His Necromantic rituals are associated with large quantities of Miasma, while his laboratory work produced weapons based upon concentrated corruption and Miasma capable of harming extremely powerful Holy beings.[4][6][2]
- Demonic [[Immortality]]: As a high-ranking Demon and Spirit, destroying Asmodeus' temporary manifestation is not necessarily equivalent to destroying his existence. Demons whose Souls remain connected to Hell can eventually return unless their Souls are destroyed, consumed, or otherwise prevented from returning through the normal Demonic cycle.

#### Necromancy
Asmodeus is an exceptionally advanced Necromancer whose methods exceed conventional corpse animation.[6]
- Undead Resurrection: He can resurrect beings who had already died and turn them into Undead combatants. Confirmed examples include [[Kumbhakarna]] and [[Yamata-no-Orochi]].[6]
- Mythological Resurrection: The beings revived by Asmodeus are not limited to ordinary soldiers. He successfully restored major mythological monsters whose original deaths belonged to the histories of established Pantheons.[6]
- Soul-Based [[Necromancy]]: His form of Necromancy utilizes Souls rather than merely animating empty corpses. This prevents affected Souls from proceeding normally through death and judgment while binding them back into physical existence.[6]
- Corpse Reanimation: By returning a Soul to a dead body or otherwise reconnecting the spiritual and physical components, Asmodeus can produce functional Undead servants from long-deceased supernatural beings.[6]
The full limits of his Necromancy have not been shown. The resurrection of beings such as Yamata-no-Orochi nevertheless demonstrates that the age and supernatural significance of a corpse do not automatically prevent him from exploiting it.

#### Occult Research
Asmodeus is one of the most technically knowledgeable Demons shown under [[Diablo]]'s former regime.
- Supernatural Research: He maintained a dedicated laboratory and personally conducted research on supernatural materials supplied to Diablo.[5]
- Soul Research: Much of Asmodeus' work involves using Souls as raw material, whether through [[Necromancy]], corruption weapons, or other experiments.[4][6]
- Demonic Weapon Creation: Diablo directly relied upon Asmodeus when additional corruption weapons were required, establishing him as one of the principal weapons researchers of the former Demonic faction.[4]
- Cross-System Research: Asmodeus worked with James on multiple occasions, demonstrating that his research was not restricted to purely traditional Demonic methods and could incorporate knowledge developed by outsiders.[2]

#### Cursed Bullet Creation
Asmodeus was responsible for the production of the Cursed Bullets used by [[Diablo]]'s faction.[1]
The weapons were sufficiently dangerous that Diablo specifically considered the existing stock adequate for use against the Highest Order Angels.[1]
These bullets and related corruption weapons rely upon Demonic Miasma and Soul-based materials rather than functioning as conventional physical ammunition.
- Holy Being Corruption: Asmodeus' research produced corruption weaponry capable of contaminating extremely powerful Holy existences. A projectile containing concentrated Miasma was involved in the corruption of [[Gabriel]], causing the Archangel's Holy nature to collapse into a Fallen state.[6][2]
- Anti-Angel Weaponry: The Cursed Bullets were specifically stockpiled with Highest Order Angels in mind, establishing their ability to bypass or undermine defenses that ordinary Demonic attacks would struggle to overcome.[1]
- Soul-Fueled Weaponry: Diablo ordered Asmodeus to use the Souls of Helheim's dead as materials for another weapon of corruption, demonstrating that the weapons could be constructed by converting harvested Souls into offensive supernatural effects.[4]

#### Spiritual Consumption
During the battle against Michael, Asmodeus asked [[Diablo]] for permission to consume what remained of the Angel after Diablo had obtained what he required.[6]
Diablo granted the request.
The exact mechanism and benefit of this consumption have not yet been explained, so it remains unclear whether Asmodeus directly absorbs the Soul, Energy, spiritual body, or some combination of the target's remaining essence.

#### Composite Voice
Whenever Asmodeus speaks in his Spirit form, his voice is distorted and appears to contain several voices simultaneously.[5]
The cause has not been explicitly revealed. Although this phenomenon could indicate an unusual spiritual composition, the novel has not confirmed that Asmodeus literally contains multiple Souls within himself.

### Possessions

#### Egg of Creation
Main article: [[Egg of Creation]]
Asmodeus is the current known possessor of the Egg of Creation, having stolen it during the collapse of [[Diablo]]'s regime.[2]
The Egg is an extremely dangerous Demonic artifact composed from Blood, organs, and Souls. It functions as a Soul repository and was used by Diablo to store the Souls of beings he killed, revive Demons, and empower selected Demonic servants.[2]
Its most important purpose was connected to Diablo's intended transformation into a Nephalem. The artifact formed part of a ritual capable of fundamentally changing the Race and Soul of its user through carefully selected Angelic and Demonic components.[2]
Asmodeus' theft therefore gave him access to an artifact capable of:
- Storing large quantities of Souls.
- Supporting Demonic resurrection.
- Empowering chosen Demons.
- Serving as a component in Race and Soul transformation.
- Contributing to the creation of a Nephalem-like existence.[2]
His reason for stealing the Egg has not yet been revealed.

### Relationships

#### Diablo
[[Diablo]] was Asmodeus' former King and master.
During the Demonic War, Diablo trusted Asmodeus with some of his most important supernatural research. Asmodeus worked within a dedicated laboratory, developed corruption weapons, produced Cursed Bullets, researched materials delivered by Diablo's associates, and took part directly in the operations against Heaven's strongest Angels.[4][5][1][6]
Asmodeus consistently addressed Diablo respectfully while the Demon King remained in power and obeyed his commands without visible opposition.[5]
That loyalty did not survive Diablo's defeat. Following Diablo's death, Asmodeus stole the [[Egg of Creation]] and fled rather than defending the old regime or submitting to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[2]

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] became Asmodeus' de facto enemy after overthrowing [[Diablo]] and conquering [[Biblical Hell]].
Asmodeus apparently avoided direct confrontation with Victor during the conquest and survived the destruction of Diablo's faction. Once Victor gained Diablo's memories, he discovered that Asmodeus had escaped with the [[Egg of Creation]].[2]
Victor recognizes Asmodeus as a dangerous fugitive whose Spirit nature makes him difficult to locate. His knowledge, access to forbidden Demonic technology, and history of collaboration with James make him considerably more dangerous than an ordinary surviving Duke.[2]

#### James
James collaborated with Asmodeus several times before the collapse of [[Diablo]]'s faction.[2]
The exact details of every project remain unknown, but [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] considered the relationship particularly dangerous because James may possess research associated with the corruption weapons Asmodeus developed.
The fact that a pure-Miasma projectile was capable of turning [[Gabriel]] into a Fallen Angel caused Victor to consider what James might accomplish if he possessed the same principles or research independently.[2]
This establishes cooperation between the two but does not by itself establish Asmodeus as an official member of [[New Dawn]].

#### Gabriel
[[Gabriel]] was one of the principal victims of the corruption technology connected to Asmodeus.
During the Demonic War, Asmodeus participated in the operation that contaminated Gabriel with Demonic corruption until the Archangel's Holy nature collapsed and his presence became Fallen and Demonic.[6]
The event demonstrated the effectiveness of Asmodeus' anti-Angel research against even Heaven's highest-ranking forces.

#### Michael
Michael recognized Asmodeus immediately during the Angelic battle and reacted with fury upon realizing what the Ancient Spirit had done.[6]
Asmodeus later approached Michael unexpectedly during the conflict and, after [[Diablo]] had taken what he needed, requested permission to consume the Angel's remains.[6]
