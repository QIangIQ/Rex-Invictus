### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 530
Sitri was the 12th Key of Solomon and one of the highest-ranking members of the Ars Goetia serving beneath [[Diablo]]. Unlike the lower-ranked Pillars whose positions could be maintained largely through wealth and political influence, Sitri belonged to the upper twenty, where considerable personal power and a valuable bloodline were required in addition to status. Hilda specifically used Sitri as an example of a Pillar who possessed both political influence and genuine combat ability.[1]
Sitri's position was nevertheless built partly through ruthless political maneuvering. Before becoming Rank 12, he undermined Gremory and seized his higher position, territory and status, contributing to Gremory's fall to Rank 56. His success made Sitri increasingly arrogant, and he later attempted a similar maneuver against [[Bael]], the Rank 1 Pillar. That attempt ended catastrophically: Bael defeated him, Sitri lost his wife, he was effectively confined to his own territory, and after attempting to assault Bael's wife Lily, her Sacred Dark Light permanently mutilated him, leaving him castrated.[2]
During the Demonic War, Diablo deployed Sitri against the [[Snow Clan]] as part of the coordinated invasion of [[Nightingale]]. Sitri's actual mission was deliberately limited: delay the Clan and destroy members of the lower command structure without provoking the Count family strongly enough to turn all Vampires against Diablo. Instead, [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] exploited his pride, repeatedly humiliating him with details of his past until Sitri abandoned Diablo's instructions and threatened her personally. Violet entered her Second [[Vampire Count Form]], prevented him from using his signature technique, overwhelmed him through the Snow bloodline's emotion-amplified Fire and ultimately destroyed him with Supernova.[2][3]
Because Sitri's Soul had simply returned to Hell rather than being permanently destroyed, Diablo used thousands of innocent Souls to bypass the thousands of years normally required for a powerful Demon to resurrect and immediately restored Sitri. His failure nearly cost him his Rank, army and the wealth he had accumulated over millennia. Desperate to preserve everything he possessed, Sitri accepted a final mission against the Vatican despite recognizing that it was effectively suicidal.[3]
Diablo secretly intended Sitri to function as bait. While Sitri and millions of Demons occupied the Vatican and drew the attention of the Angels and Inquisition, War and Death were given the real objective of permanently killing Alexander and his Generals. Sitri survived long enough to confront [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] when the Vampire Progenitor entered the battlefield, but Victor annihilated his army, captured the wounded Duke and completely consumed his Soul. Unlike his death to Violet, this second death severed Sitri's connection to Hell and made resurrection impossible.[4][5]

### Physical Description
Sitri was a tall male Demon with two large Demonic wings and a pair of horns whose tips were dark blue. His eyes possessed completely black sclera surrounding bright blue pupils, immediately distinguishing him from an ordinary Human or Human-possessing lesser Demon. He dressed unusually elegantly for an invading Demon, wearing expensive black clothing with a single dark-blue tie providing the only noticeable color.[2]
His appearance reflected his status as one of Hell's wealthier and more politically powerful Dukes. Sitri had spent thousands of years accumulating possessions, territory, armies and status, and losing those assets frightened him almost as much as losing his life.[3]
When Sitri became serious in battle, his Demonic characteristics intensified. Miasma engulfed his body, he became taller, dark-blue scales spread across his skin, his eyes shone more brightly and his hands developed powerful claws. The transformation increased the density of his Miasma and his physical capabilities, although it proved insufficient against Violet's continuously escalating Fire.[2]
His wings were functional rather than decorative and allowed him to freely fight in the air. During his battle with Violet he repeatedly transitioned between aerial and close-range combat while attempting to create distance necessary for his supernatural techniques.[2]

### Personality
Sitri was arrogant, vindictive and deeply proud of the political position he had accumulated within Hell. His first conversation with Violet demonstrates this clearly: he dismissed more than three hundred Elder Vampires and Royal Guards as mere "extras", treated the invasion of Snow territory as beneath him and openly boasted about the freedom afforded by his Rank 12 status.[2]
His arrogance was paired with considerable insecurity. Sitri could tolerate general insults, but attacks directed at the humiliations that defined his political history rapidly destroyed his self-control. Violet deliberately exploited this flaw by reminding him that his position had originally been stolen from Gremory through schemes, that his attempt to repeat the same strategy against [[Bael]] had failed and that Lily had permanently castrated him when he attempted to violate her.[2]
Sitri initially attempted to pretend the stories did not bother him, describing his actions against Gremory as a legitimate "conquest". His composure deteriorated as Violet continued, particularly when the assembled Vampires began looking at him with ridicule and pity. Once she publicly called him a eunuch, his rage overpowered the strategic purpose of his mission.[2]
This made Sitri dangerously easy to manipulate through pride. [[Diablo]] had specifically warned him before the invasion that the women of the [[Snow Clan]] used emotions as weapons and that he should never allow himself to be provoked by them. Sitri only remembered that warning after Violet had already transformed and begun tearing him apart.[2]
His response to Violet's insults also revealed an exceptionally cruel and sexually predatory personality. After losing his temper, Sitri threatened to destroy the entire Snow territory, force Violet to watch her citizens and Clan members die, and then allow thousands of his Demons to sexually violate her. The threat transformed Violet's anger into the emotion-fueled state that ultimately killed him.[2]
Sitri was nevertheless capable of understanding hierarchy when confronted by someone he truly feared. His attitude toward Diablo was completely different from his behavior around weaker beings. After being resurrected, Sitri immediately knelt and apologized. When Diablo stated that excuses were irrelevant and threatened to strip him of his Rank, Legions and wealth, Sitri visibly panicked and begged for another opportunity.[3]
His attachment to Rank 12 appears to have been one of the central motivations of his life. Sitri had spent thousands of years accumulating his army, fortune and position, and the possibility of losing them was unbearable enough that he accepted Diablo's command to invade the Vatican even after pointing out that the region was protected by Angels.[3]
Sitri was therefore not courageous in the same sense as warriors such as War. His willingness to accept dangerous missions primarily came from fear of losing his possessions and Diablo's favor rather than an enthusiasm for confronting superior opponents.
He was also intensely competitive within Hell's ranking system. During the Vatican campaign, Sitri grew desperate because other Demons were gaining accomplishments elsewhere while he remained trapped in a defensive battle against Ariel. To Sitri, prolonged inaction meant losing opportunities to increase his standing.[4]

### History

#### Rise Through the Ars Goetia
Sitri was not originally the 12th Pillar. At an unknown point in Hell's past, he targeted the higher-ranked Gremory and successfully undermined him through political schemes. Gremory lost territory, influence and status until he eventually fell to Rank 56, while Sitri rose into the upper echelon of the Ars Goetia.[2]
Violet's information described the conflict in especially brutal terms: Sitri took Gremory's territory, violated his wife and destroyed much of his dignity and political standing. Sitri did not deny the broad account and instead described the entire episode as a conquest he had no reason to hide.[2]
The success encouraged him to attempt something even more ambitious. Sitri later tried to use similar schemes against the Rank 1 Pillar [[Bael]]. Unlike Gremory, Bael defeated him decisively. Sitri's wife was taken, his freedom was restricted and he was effectively reduced to a glorified guardian confined to his own territory.[2]
During the same conflict, Sitri attempted to sexually assault Bael's wife, Lily. This proved even more disastrous than challenging Bael himself. Lily possessed [[Lucifer]]'s bloodline and the unusual Dark Light ability, whose Sacred properties could inflict wounds that ordinary Demonic regeneration could not easily correct. She permanently castrated Sitri, giving rise to the humiliating "Eunuch" insult later used by Violet.[2]
Despite these failures, Sitri retained the Rank 12 position and spent thousands of years rebuilding or maintaining his wealth, Legions and influence beneath [[Diablo]].[3]

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Before the main invasion of [[Nightingale]], Sitri's forces were positioned around the [[Snow Clan]] territory. Hilda quickly recognized something strange about the assault. Sitri was a Rank 12 Pillar capable of mobilizing more than two million Demons from his personal forces without significantly weakening either himself or the rest of his army, yet he was deliberately using low-ranking Demons in small-scale attacks against defenders they could not realistically overcome.[1]
This revealed that the invasion was primarily intended to occupy and delay the Snow Clan. [[Diablo]] did not initially want the [[Vampire Counts]] completely committed against Hell; Sitri was supposed to kill non-Snow personnel within the lower command chain, create administrative disruption and buy time for operations occurring elsewhere.[1][3]
Sitri eventually appeared personally outside the Snow territory, where [[Violet Elderblood Snow]], Hilda, members of Clan Blank and more than three hundred Elder Vampires were waiting.[2]
He greeted Violet and Hilda by name but contemptuously dismissed the Royal Guards and surrounding Vampires as "extras". Violet immediately recognized both his arrogance and the fact that he was continuing to waste time rather than seriously attacking.[2]
Rather than allow Sitri to dictate the pace of the confrontation, Violet began attacking his pride. She first reminded him that his Rank 12 position had been acquired through schemes against Gremory and then described the humiliation Sitri suffered after challenging [[Bael]]. When Sitri attempted to preserve his composure, Violet revealed the most embarrassing part of the story: Lily had castrated him with Sacred Dark Light after he attempted to assault her.[2]
Sitri finally snapped. Ignoring Diablo's mission entirely, he announced that he would destroy Snow territory and personally subject Violet to a horrific fate after killing her citizens and family. His Miasma surged around him as he began invoking [[Cocytus]].[2]
He never completed the technique. [[Oda Blank]] emerged from the shadows and drove Darkness-made daggers through Sitri's heart and brain while his attention was consumed by rage. At Violet's direct order, Oda added a third attack specifically intended to further humiliate him.[2]
The injuries were serious but not immediately fatal due to Sitri's Demonic vitality. His wounds began healing and he attempted to retaliate, only for Oda to disappear back into the shadows.[2]
Violet then entered her Second [[Vampire Count Form]]. Her Fire alone evaporated the surrounding Miasma, and the transformation raised her physical abilities, reflexes and control to a level Sitri had not expected from such a young Vampire.[2]
Sitri responded by entering a stronger Demonic combat state. His body increased in size, dark-blue scales appeared across him and his claws became more pronounced while his Miasma intensified. He initially caught one of Violet's punches and compared the heat of her Fire to that of Phoenix.[2]
The balance immediately shifted again. Violet pierced Sitri's chest with her claws, ripped out his still-beating heart and burned it before his eyes. She explicitly stated that the Snow Clan possessed extensive intelligence concerning the 72 Pillars and that she already knew how Sitri's powers worked.[2]
This knowledge was particularly important because she refused to allow Sitri enough time or distance to activate Cocytus. When he created sharpened water around his arm and attempted to attack, Violet's increasing heat destroyed both the technique and his limb.[2]
Sitri repeatedly tried to increase his output, but Violet's Fire grew faster than his Miasma could compensate. Snow Clan Fire becomes stronger through emotion, and Sitri had personally created the anger driving Violet. Every attempt to resist only prolonged the battle and increased the power available to her.[2]
Violet tore off one of Sitri's arms, used it against him, destroyed his left eye and continued beating him while his attempts at defense collapsed. Only then did Sitri remember Diablo's explicit warning that he should never provoke the Snow Clan or allow its members to provoke him.[2]
Violet eventually ripped out his throat and carried him into the sky. Her temperature reached such an extreme level that buildings far from the battle began melting and the Elder Vampires were forced to protect the surrounding city.[2]
She then unleashed Supernova, a technique inherited from the Snow bloodline that concentrated her accumulated Fire into an enormous stellar explosion. Sitri and the remaining invading Demons were completely destroyed, with observers later describing Sitri as having been reduced to atoms with nothing left behind.[2][3]
Sitri's first death was not permanent. Demons killed outside Hell normally return to the Hell where they originated and eventually regenerate, although a powerful Demon's resurrection can require thousands of years.[3][5]
Diablo possessed the authority to circumvent this delay. After War reported Sitri's death, Diablo combined Sitri's returning Demonic Soul with the Souls of thousands of innocent beings, using a forbidden exchange available to a King who completely controlled his Hell. Sitri was immediately reconstructed before him instead of waiting through his natural resurrection period.[3]
The resurrected Sitri immediately fell to his knees and apologized. Diablo refused to hear excuses and reminded him that his single assignment had been to delay the Snow Clan while disrupting the lower command structure without provoking a total war with the Vampires.[3]
Diablo then announced that Sitri would lose his Rank. The threat caused a much stronger reaction than his recent death. Sitri understood that demotion would strip him of the Legions Diablo had granted him, the wealth accumulated across thousands of years and the Rank 12 position he valued above almost everything else.[3]
Desperate, Sitri begged for one final opportunity and promised to obey any order Diablo gave him. Diablo exploited that desperation by commanding him to attack the Vatican, destroy the Inquisition's forces and kill its Generals.[3]
Sitri immediately recognized the danger because the Vatican was effectively Angelic territory. When he hesitated, Diablo reminded him that he had just promised absolute obedience. Sitri accepted.[3]
Diablo provided him with two additional Legions containing approximately two million Demons together with elite forces, but privately revealed to War that Sitri's actual role was expendable. The Vatican forces and Angels were meant to focus upon Sitri while War and Death carried out the true assassination operation against Alexander and the Inquisition leadership.[3]
Diablo explicitly described Sitri as bait.[3]
Sitri arrived at the Vatican expecting a straightforward massacre. His intended strategy was to use his famous Cocytus technique, flood the Vatican and eliminate the enemy he had come to kill.[4]
Instead, the appearance of Ariel, one of the Seven Virtues and an exceptionally powerful Seraphim, completely altered the battle. Ariel's Holy presence and defensive barrier prevented Sitri's Demons from simply overwhelming the Inquisition and transformed what Sitri expected to be a quick extermination into a prolonged defensive conflict.[4]
Sitri became increasingly frustrated. His Demonic spies informed him that battles were occurring across the world and other Demons were accumulating victories, honors and opportunities to rise through Hell's hierarchy while he remained trapped outside the Vatican.[4]
The situation became even worse when Alucard entered the battlefield. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s presence alone temporarily paralyzed both armies. Sitri repeatedly screamed commands at his Demons, but even his own soldiers found themselves unable to ignore the Vampire Progenitor standing before them.[4]
Furious at losing control of the battlefield, Sitri ordered his entire army to attack Victor. A light-red aura spread over the Demons as Sitri's command took effect and their physical power increased. Hell's hierarchy made refusal extremely difficult; when a Duke invoked his authority, subordinate Demons were effectively forced to obey even when they understood that the order was suicidal.[4]
Victor responded by using his shadow-based abilities and the powers of his Maids to massacre the attacking forces. More than seventy percent of the Demons outside the Vatican walls were destroyed almost instantly through a mixture of cutting attacks, telekinesis, corrosive Black Fire and other powers before their remains and Souls were absorbed.[5]
Sitri attempted to exploit the chaos and approach Victor from behind, but Kaguya immediately detected him. [[Maria Elderblood]] restrained Sitri with her threads and [[Eve Elderblood]] ignited those threads with corrosive Black Fire.[5]
Sitri attempted to counter the flames using his Water power, but the fire could not be extinguished. The attack burned deeply enough that Sitri felt as though his Soul itself was being destroyed.[5]
Victor then finished the remaining Demonic army. After gathering the surviving Demons into the sky, he cut the entire horde apart with such precision and speed that even their Souls were fragmented by the attack.[5]
With Sitri's army effectively destroyed, Victor appeared directly in front of the injured Duke. Sitri visibly recoiled and was unable to respond before Victor severed all four of his limbs and both wings.[5]
Victor then asked Sitri whether Demons could cry. Before Sitri could properly answer, Victor manifested a monstrous head from his own body and swallowed the Demon Duke whole.[5]
The consumption was complete enough that Victor immediately accessed Sitri's memories. He witnessed Sitri's first death to Violet and the fragmented transition from annihilation in Nightingale to awakening before Diablo, allowing Victor to investigate the mechanism through which the Demon King had resurrected him.[5]
Victor subsequently confirmed the difference between Sitri's two deaths. Violet had destroyed Sitri's body, causing his Soul to return naturally to Hell. Victor instead consumed the Soul itself. Sitri's connection to Hell was therefore broken permanently, making a second resurrection impossible.[5]
Sitri ceased to exist as an independent individual.

#### Legacy
Although Sitri himself permanently died in Chapter 559, his accumulated memories and combat knowledge remained inside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] after his Soul was consumed. This eventually allowed Victor to reproduce Sitri's techniques rather than merely remembering them.[5]
The clearest example is [[Cocytus]]. During Victor's later battle with War inside Hell, Victor invoked Sitri's technique directly. Torrents of water erupted from the ground while rain formed overhead, producing enough water within seconds to flood an entire city-sized region and eventually create something closer to a sea than a conventional lake.[6]
Vepar, herself an experienced Water user, immediately recognized Cocytus and recalled having witnessed Sitri use it in the past. She remembered Sitri's version flooding and destroying an entire capital, with numerous Demons becoming victims of the Rank 12 Pillar's attack.[6]
Victor's later use therefore confirms why Violet had deliberately prevented Sitri from activating the technique during their battle: Cocytus was not merely a concentrated Water attack but a genuine mass-destruction ability capable of transforming the terrain of an entire city.

### Skills and Abilities
Sitri was a Rank 12 Demon Pillar whose position required more than political influence alone. Hell's Pillar hierarchy allowed wealth and connections to raise a Demon only so far; placement within approximately the upper twenty additionally required genuine power and a sufficiently important lineage. Hilda specifically identified Sitri as possessing all of these traits.[1]
High-Ranking Demon Physiology: As an ancient Duke-class Demon, Sitri possessed all of the standard supernatural properties of powerful Demons at a vastly greater level than ordinary members of his race.
- Superhuman Strength: Sitri could physically intercept attacks from Violet's Second [[Vampire Count Form]] and engage her directly in hand-to-hand combat before her emotion-amplified power surpassed him.[2]
- Superhuman Speed and Reflexes: Sitri could react to combatants operating at Elder Vampire and Vampire Count levels, although Violet's transformed speed eventually surpassed his ability to defend reliably.[2]
- Enhanced Durability: Sitri survived daggers piercing his heart and brain and immediately began regenerating from the wounds.[2]
- Enhanced Stamina: He continued fighting after multiple critical injuries and severe mutilation.
- Regeneration: Sitri rapidly regenerated wounds inflicted by ordinary non-Holy attacks, including damage to vital organs. Oda specifically noted that Sacred weaponry would have been substantially more effective against him.[2]
- [[Immortality]]: Demons do not naturally die from age and powerful Demons can exist for thousands of years or longer.
- Demonic Resurrection: Destroying Sitri's physical body outside Hell did not kill him permanently. His Soul automatically returned to Hell and could eventually reconstruct a new body. [[Diablo]] accelerated the process through the sacrifice of thousands of Souls.[3][5]
- Miasma Manipulation: Sitri generated enormous quantities of Miasma, using it to empower himself, attack opponents and transform his body.[2]
- Miasma Aura: When enraged, Sitri could surround himself in a dense cloud of Miasma powerful enough to resemble a miniature dark sun.[2]
- Flight: Sitri possessed two large Demonic wings and demonstrated natural aerial mobility during his battle against Violet.[2]
- Natural Weaponry: His transformed body possessed sharp claws, scales, horns and wings useful during direct combat.
- Demonic Possession: Like other sufficiently powerful Demons, Sitri could use compatible deceased or damned mortal vessels when operating outside Hell.
- Soul Consumption: As a Demon, Sitri could consume damned Souls for nourishment and power.
- Enhanced Senses: Sitri possessed the supernatural perception expected from an ancient Duke-class Demon.
Demonic Combat Transformation: Sitri could intensify his true Demonic characteristics by surrounding himself in Miasma. His body became taller, dark-blue scales appeared across his skin, his claws lengthened and the quantity of Miasma surrounding him dramatically increased.[2]
This transformation increased his physical power enough to initially contend with Violet's Second Vampire Count Form. However, the improvement was finite, while Violet's Snow Fire continued becoming stronger as her rage fed the ability.[2]

#### Hydrokinesis
Water Manipulation: Sitri possessed an exceptionally powerful affinity for Water and could generate, shape and weaponize it even while fighting at close range.[2][4]
- Water Generation: Sitri could produce Water through supernatural means rather than requiring an existing natural body of water.
- Water Weaponry: During his fight against Violet, Sitri coated his arm in sharpened Water and attempted to use it as a cutting weapon.[2]
- Water Projection: His Water could be released offensively over distance or around his own body.
- Fire Countermeasure: Sitri instinctively attempted to use his Water power to extinguish Eve's Black Fire during his final battle, although that specific supernatural flame could not be extinguished by conventional means.[5]
- Large-Scale Water Generation: Sitri's highest-level techniques could create vastly greater amounts of Water than ordinary Hydrokinesis, enough to alter an entire city's terrain.[6]
[[Cocytus]]: Sitri's signature technique and the most famous expression of his Water Manipulation. Sitri could generate catastrophic quantities of Water over an enormous area, flooding and destroying entire cities.[4][6]
- During the [[Nightingale]] battle, Violet explicitly targeted Sitri before he could complete Cocytus because Clan Snow's intelligence already understood how dangerous the technique was.[2]
- Sitri intended to open his Vatican assault by flooding the entire city with Cocytus.[4]
- After consuming Sitri, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] inherited his knowledge of the technique and later reproduced it during his battle with War.[5][6]
- When Victor invoked Cocytus, violent torrents erupted from the ground while rain formed throughout the sky. Within seconds, enough Water existed to completely flood a city-sized region.[6]
- Vepar recalled Sitri personally using the technique in the past to flood and destroy an entire Demon capital, creating a lake where a city had once existed.[6]
- Cocytus therefore functions as a mass-destruction and battlefield-transformation technique rather than simply an ordinary Water blast.

#### Demonic Army
Mass Demon Summoning / Deployment: As the 12th Pillar, Sitri controlled an enormous personal military force. Hilda stated that he could easily summon more than 2,000,000 Demons from his own army to attack the [[Snow Clan]] without meaningfully diminishing his personal power or exhausting the majority of his forces.[1]
His failure in [[Nightingale]] was therefore not caused by a lack of available soldiers. Sitri had deliberately deployed weak Demons because [[Diablo]]'s initial plan required delay and disruption rather than the immediate destruction of Clan Snow.[1]
After Sitri's resurrection, Diablo supplied him with additional Legions numbering approximately two million Demons and additional elites for the Vatican operation.[3]
Demonic Command Authority: Sitri possessed supernatural authority over lower-ranked Demons. During the Vatican battle, he issued an absolute order for the army to kill [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. A light-red aura appeared around the Demons, their strength increased and the hierarchy of Hell compelled them to obey despite many recognizing that approaching Victor was suicidal.[4]
This ability demonstrates that Sitri's position as a Duke gave him more than conventional military leadership; his commands could interact directly with the supernatural hierarchy governing subordinate Demons.

#### Political and Strategic Abilities
Ars Goetia Authority: Sitri's Rank 12 status granted him control over territory, Legions and resources within Hell's political system. The position represented centuries or millennia of accumulated power and was valuable enough that Sitri risked his life rather than allow [[Diablo]] to strip it from him.[3]
Political Manipulation: Sitri was capable of using schemes and political maneuvering effectively enough to remove Gremory from his former standing and acquire a substantially higher rank for himself.[2]
His success against Gremory demonstrates that Sitri was not unintelligent. His principal strategic flaw was emotional instability when his pride or sexual humiliation became the subject of an opponent's manipulation.
Battlefield Command: Sitri commanded armies numbering in the millions and understood enough large-scale warfare to participate as one of Diablo's principal commanders during the Demonic War.
