Dantalion was a high-ranking Demon, the 71st Key of Solomon and one of the 72 Demonic Pillars of Hell. He belonged to [[Diablo]]'s faction and was infamous as the Lord of the Immortal Army because his unique bloodline allowed him to carry an army of Demons within his own Soul, strengthen those soldiers with his miasma and continually resurrect them after their destruction.[1][2]
Despite his title as a Duke of Hell, Dantalion occupied one of the lowest positions among the 72 Pillars and was considered comparatively weak by the standards of the Ars Goetia. [[Morgana Elderblood]] regarded him as so insignificant that she believed [[Nightingale]]'s Royal Guard could defeat him without her intervention, and later stated that he had always been little more than a nuisance when compared with the far stronger Demonic Dukes she had fought during her time as Lilith's General.[3][1]
Dantalion nevertheless possessed a dangerous specialization rather than exceptional individual power. His personal army, ability to repeatedly restore fallen soldiers and willingness to enhance himself through external curses made him far more troublesome in prolonged warfare than his rank alone suggested. He participated in Diablo's invasion of Nightingale, fought Morgana in the Royal Capital and was ultimately captured after she annihilated his army. He was later interrogated and permanently killed by [[Victor Elderblood]], who consumed his Soul and prevented the Demon from resurrecting through Hell.[2][4]

### Physical Appearance
Dantalion's natural form was that of an enormous humanoid Demon standing approximately four meters tall. He possessed four red eyes, arranged as two pairs, and displayed the large, physically imposing body typical of a high-ranking Demon.[3]
During his battle against Morgana, Dantalion transformed further after activating the enhancements placed upon him before the invasion. Black, letter-like markings spread across his body as a pillar of darkness erupted from him, making his appearance substantially more demonic. His hands developed enormous claws capable of directly clashing against Morgana's energy weapons.[1]
The markings were not a natural Demonic technique. Morgana recognized them as curses produced through Witchcraft, realizing that Dantalion had willingly allowed himself to be experimented upon and modified in exchange for additional combat power.[1]

### Personality
Dantalion was arrogant when surrounded by advantages but considerably less courageous when deprived of them. His reputation as the Lord of the Immortal Army influenced much of his behavior; he preferred fighting while supported by overwhelming numbers and became visibly distressed when circumstances prevented him from summoning his normal forces.[1]
Morgana summarized his greatest weakness directly: Dantalion was nothing without his army. Unlike Dukes such as [[Bael]] who could stand among Hell's greatest combatants through their personal strength, Dantalion's danger came from endlessly throwing soldiers at an enemy while remaining behind his legions.[1]
He was also opportunistic and willing to exploit emotional weaknesses. When Morgana began overpowering him, Dantalion deliberately mocked Lilith, claiming that [[Diablo]] had reduced the former Mother of Demons to an obedient dog. He correctly predicted that the revelation would destabilize Morgana enough to interrupt her technique, then immediately used the opening to summon the army hidden within his Soul.[2]
His arrogance frequently worked against him. Dantalion became increasingly confident after receiving the curses supplied by Diablo's faction and initially believed those enhancements might allow even a Rank 71 Duke to fight Morgana. Once she displayed her full destructive power, however, he rapidly understood the difference between them and regretted confronting her personally rather than remaining behind his army.[1][2]
Dantalion possessed little loyalty to abstract notions of honor. Survival came before pride once defeat became unavoidable. After Morgana mutilated him and demanded information about Lilith, he immediately began explaining everything he knew and repeatedly insisted that he had no further information rather than continue resisting.[2]
He was nevertheless deeply loyal to Diablo's authority during the invasion. When activating the Witch-made enhancements embedded in his body, Dantalion declared that his King had chosen him and granted him an opportunity he refused to waste.[1]
Dantalion was also exceptionally cruel even by Demonic standards. Centuries before his direct appearance, he was identified as the Demon responsible for persecuting the Incubi until the entire race disappeared from the Seven Hells.[5]

### History
Before the events of the Demonic War, Dantalion had existed for millennia as one of the 72 Demonic Pillars. At an unspecified point in Hell's history, he led the persecution of the Incubi. The campaign continued until the Incubus species was effectively exterminated from the Seven Hells.[5]
His bloodline eventually earned him the title Lord of the Immortal Army. Unlike ordinary commanders who depended upon conventional portals and logistics, Dantalion could keep Demonic soldiers inside his Soul, allowing an army to accompany him wherever he travelled.[2]
By the period preceding the invasion of [[Nightingale]], Dantalion belonged firmly to [[Diablo]]'s faction. Diablo also arranged for him to receive powerful Witch-produced curses designed to compensate for his comparatively low standing among the Demonic Dukes.[1]

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Dantalion formally appeared when [[Diablo]] launched his invasion of [[Nightingale]]. Numerous portals opened throughout the Vampire world, releasing Demonic armies across the territories controlled by the major Vampire Clans. Dantalion appeared in the Royal Capital alongside far more dangerous members of the Ars Goetia, including the 1st Key [[Bael]] and the 9th Key [[Paimon]].[3]
[[Jeanne Elderblood]] identified Dantalion as the 71st Key of Solomon. Morgana immediately dismissed him as a weak opponent, remarking that Nightingale's ordinary Royal Guard should have been capable of dealing with him. Her confidence changed only after Bael and Paimon revealed themselves, confirming that Diablo had deployed several considerably stronger figures alongside the lower-ranked Duke.[3]
The Demonic force operating within the Royal Capital was tasked with suppressing Nightingale while Diablo's larger plans unfolded. Dantalion, Bael and Paimon confronted Morgana and Jeanne, with Diablo's faction also expressing interest in reclaiming Morgana and her daughters. Dantalion specifically suggested that if Morgana refused to join them, they could simply take her offspring instead.[6]
That statement immediately drew Morgana's full attention. Her Demonic pressure focused upon Dantalion as she warned that nobody would touch her daughter and survive. Despite being the 9th Key, even Paimon could feel the fear produced by Morgana's presence.[6]
Morgana subsequently trapped Dantalion within the projected dream of her Succubus abilities. Because Jeanne had interfered with the Demons' connection to Hell, Dantalion initially could not call his armies through the conventional magical circles he normally used. Morgana exploited that weakness and reminded him that while she had historically fought Bael and entire armies, Dantalion without his legions had never been anything more than a nuisance to her.[1]
Unwilling to accept the humiliation, Dantalion activated the enhancements granted to him for the invasion. Black cursed markings spread across his body and dramatically increased his physical abilities. Morgana immediately recognized the markings as Witchcraft and realized that Dantalion had allowed Witches to modify him for additional power.[1]
The empowered Dantalion became capable of physically contesting Morgana to a degree. His claws clashed against her energy sword, and his enhanced body could regenerate damage at remarkable speed. When Morgana attempted to cut him apart, another Demonic limb even emerged from his body and seized her, revealing that the curses had altered his body beyond simple physical enhancement.[1]
His greatest advantage remained his bloodline. After exploiting Morgana's emotional reaction to news about Lilith, Dantalion activated a second means of deploying his forces. Instead of summoning Demons from Hell, he released the soldiers already stored inside his Soul. This bypassed the restriction preventing his normal summoning method.[2]
Dantalion released 169 Demons from within himself. The cursed markings on his body then activated alongside his miasma, empowering every soldier until each possessed power stated to be comparable to a Rank 72 Demonic Duke. The army charged Morgana simultaneously, restoring the numerical advantage upon which Dantalion normally relied.[2]
The tactic failed catastrophically. Learning that Lilith had apparently become fanatically loyal to Diablo pushed Morgana into using the destructive Vampire power she had rarely relied upon. Her radiation-based energy engulfed the battlefield in crimson light and annihilated Dantalion's entire legion along with much of the surrounding architecture.[2]
The army's destruction was not permanent. Dantalion's soldiers immediately began resurrecting, confirming the basis of his title as Lord of the Immortal Army. Morgana simply destroyed them again with concentrated beams of energy before attacking Dantalion himself.[2]
Dantalion was completely outmatched once Morgana stopped allowing his army to control the battle. She seized him by the neck and severed his limbs until only his torso remained. The previously arrogant Duke finally recognized the situation clearly, remembering Morgana's history as [[Morgana Elderblood|The Reaper]]—a warrior who had once fought Bael's forces and defeated five Demonic Dukes by herself.[2]
When Morgana demanded information concerning Lilith, Dantalion abandoned his earlier bravado. He explained that Lilith had abruptly begun attending Diablo's meetings with a dramatically different attitude and had become fanatically supportive of his plans. Dantalion claimed that he did not know what had been done to her and that the other Demons had only witnessed the sudden change.[2]
Morgana believed that he was no longer lying but wanted further information. The battle elsewhere in the capital prevented her from continuing the interrogation immediately, so she carried the mutilated Dantalion directly into Vlad's castle. Familiar with Vlad's facilities from her years as his wife, Morgana placed Dantalion inside a magically sealed solitary room used for interrogation and experimentation before returning to assist Jeanne.[2]
Dantalion remained a Vampire prisoner after the invasion. The intelligence he possessed concerning Diablo, Lilith and Hell made him considerably more useful alive than dead for a brief period.[2]
His usefulness eventually ended when [[Victor Elderblood]] became involved. Victor interrogated Dantalion for the information available to him and then killed the Duke permanently by consuming his Soul.[4] Because a Demon's normal resurrection depends upon its Soul surviving and returning to Hell, Victor's consumption prevented Dantalion from undergoing the resurrection that would ordinarily follow the destruction of a Demon's body.

### Relationships

#### Diablo
Dantalion served [[Diablo]] as his King and belonged to Diablo's faction during the struggle for control over Hell. He willingly participated in Diablo's invasion of [[Nightingale]] and accepted dangerous external modifications to his body after being selected for the operation.[1]
Dantalion appeared genuinely proud that Diablo had personally granted him additional power, viewing the curses as proof that his King had given him an opportunity to rise beyond his ordinary Rank 71 status.[1]

#### Morgana Elderblood
Morgana and Dantalion had known of one another long before the [[Nightingale]] invasion. Their reputations, however, existed at drastically different levels. During Morgana's service beneath Lilith she fought the strongest military forces of Hell, while Dantalion was so weak by Duke standards that she normally ignored him.[1]
Dantalion attempted to compensate for the difference through his immortal army and the Witch-made curses embedded into his body. Although these enhancements briefly allowed him to surprise Morgana, they never closed the actual gap between them. Morgana annihilated his legions, mutilated him and personally imprisoned him after extracting information concerning Lilith.[1][2]

#### Lilith Elderblood
Dantalion was not a follower of Lilith and instead served her political enemy, [[Diablo]]. During his battle with Morgana he mocked Lilith's apparent conversion into a fanatical supporter of Diablo, specifically using the information to destabilize Morgana emotionally.[2]
His own knowledge of what had happened to Lilith was limited. He claimed that the other Demons simply witnessed her personality and behavior change before she began appearing in Diablo's meetings as one of his supporters.[2]

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] encountered Dantalion only after the Duke had already been defeated and imprisoned. He used the captive as an information source concerning [[Diablo]]'s faction and the circumstances surrounding Lilith before permanently eliminating him.[4]
Rather than merely destroying Dantalion's body and allowing his Soul to eventually reform in Hell, Victor consumed the Soul itself. Dantalion therefore became one of the notable powerful Souls devoured during Victor's development.[4]

### Skills & Abilities
- Demon Physiology: As a high-ranking Demon and member of the 72 Pillars, Dantalion possessed the supernatural physiology of the Demonic race at a vastly greater level than ordinary Demons. This included immense physical ability, longevity, regeneration, miasma and the capacity to survive injuries fatal to conventional lifeforms.[3][1]
- Supernatural Strength: In his curse-enhanced state, Dantalion could physically clash with Morgana and restrain her through the additional Demonic limb manifested from his body.[1]
- Supernatural Speed and Reflexes: Dantalion could react to Morgana's attacks and counterattack during their close-range exchanges, although she ultimately remained considerably faster and more skilled.[1]
- Supernatural Durability: His large Demonic body could survive extensive supernatural punishment and continue fighting while heavily injured.[1]
- Regeneration: Dantalion possessed unusually powerful regenerative ability. Severe wounds inflicted during his battle with Morgana began repairing themselves visibly, with his curse-enhanced body restoring damage at exceptional speed.[1]
- Dependent [[Immortality]]: Like other Demons, destroying Dantalion's body alone would not necessarily constitute permanent death so long as his Soul could return through Hell's Demonic resurrection cycle. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] bypassed this by consuming his Soul entirely.[4]
- Miasma Manipulation: Dantalion could produce Demonic miasma and spread it across his soldiers. During the battle with Morgana, his cursed markings caused the miasma surrounding his 169 Soul-bound Demons to dramatically enhance their individual power.[2]
- Dantalion Bloodline: Dantalion possessed a specialized Demonic bloodline centered around maintaining and continually deploying an army. This unique ability was the source of his reputation as the Lord of the Immortal Army.[2]
- Soul Storage: Dantalion could house living Demonic soldiers inside his own Soul. These soldiers accompanied him regardless of his physical location and could be released directly into the material world.[2]
- Army Summoning: Under normal circumstances, Dantalion could summon soldiers directly from Hell through Demonic magic circles. Jeanne's interference prevented this method during the invasion of [[Nightingale]].[2]
- Internal Army Manifestation: Even when his connection to Hell was obstructed, Dantalion could summon the Demons already residing within his Soul, allowing him to bypass restrictions placed upon conventional Demonic summoning.[2]
- Resurrection: Soldiers belonging to Dantalion's army could resurrect after being destroyed. Morgana completely annihilated his deployed legion, only for the Demons to begin returning moments later.[2]
- Army Empowerment: By activating the cursed markings on his body and spreading his miasma across his troops, Dantalion empowered 169 Demons until each reached a level described as comparable to a Rank 72 Demonic Duke.[2]
- Demonic Transformation: Dantalion could further transform his already Demonic body, increasing its monstrous traits and physical power.[1]
- Claw Manifestation: His transformed body possessed massive claws strong enough to clash directly against Morgana's energy weapon.[1]
- Additional Limb Manifestation: During his curse-enhanced state, an additional Demonic arm could emerge from his body and physically seize an opponent.[1]
- Witch-Curse Enhancement: Before invading Nightingale, Dantalion received supernatural modifications associated with Witches. Black letter-like markings spread across his body when activated and significantly amplified his combat capabilities.[1]
- Physical Enhancement: The curses allowed Dantalion, despite occupying Rank 71, to fight far above the level Morgana expected from him.[1]
- Regenerative Enhancement: His body healed from otherwise devastating injuries at visibly accelerated rates while the markings were active.[1]
- Demonic [[Magic]]: Dantalion could employ magical circles for large-scale Demon summoning and possessed sufficient supernatural knowledge to command a major Demonic force.[2]
- Military Command: Dantalion had commanded Demonic armies for an extended period and was specifically specialized around legion-based combat rather than individual duels.[2]

### Weaknesses
- Dependence on his Army: Dantalion's greatest weakness was his reliance upon numerical superiority. Morgana explicitly stated that the supposedly formidable Lord of the Immortal Army was "nothing" without his soldiers.[1]
- Low Position among the Ars Goetia: As the 71st Key of Solomon, Dantalion stood near the bottom of the 72 Pillars and was regarded as weak by stronger Demons and veteran combatants such as Morgana.[3][1]
- Emotional Arrogance: The additional strength granted by [[Diablo]]'s faction caused Dantalion to greatly overestimate himself. He later admitted that he should have remained behind his legions rather than personally confronting Morgana.[2]
- Soul Destruction/Consumption: His Demonic resurrection could not save him if his Soul itself was destroyed or consumed. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] permanently killed him through precisely this method.[4]

### Trivia
- Dantalion was the 71st of the 72 Demonic Pillars, making him the second-lowest-ranked Key of Solomon.[3]
- Despite his low personal rank, his unique bloodline earned him the title Lord of the Immortal Army.[2]
- Dantalion carried Demons directly within his Soul rather than relying exclusively upon conventional summoning.[2]
- During his battle against Morgana, he released exactly 169 Demons from his Soul and empowered them to the level of Rank 72 Demonic Dukes.[2]
- His soldiers could resurrect almost immediately after being annihilated, forcing Morgana to destroy them repeatedly.[2]
- The supernatural markings that strengthened Dantalion were identified by Morgana as Witch-produced curses, not a natural power of his Demonic race.[1]
- Morgana had previously fought much stronger Demons than Dantalion, including [[Bael]] and groups of multiple Demonic Dukes. This was why she initially regarded Dantalion himself as a negligible threat.[1][2]
- Dantalion was responsible for the historical persecution that resulted in the extinction of the Incubi from Hell.[5]
- The Incubi should not be confused with the Succubi. Morgana and other Succubi remained extant long after Dantalion's campaign against their male counterparts.
- Dantalion's information concerning Lilith was one of the first direct indications given to Morgana that something was fundamentally wrong with Lilith's sudden loyalty toward [[Diablo]].[2]
- His defeat by Morgana did not immediately kill him. She deliberately preserved him as a prisoner because of the intelligence he possessed.[2]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] ultimately consumed Dantalion's Soul after interrogating him, preventing the normal Demonic resurrection process.[4]
- The traditional Ars Goetia also contains a spirit named Dantalion; however, abilities attributed to the mythological figure should not automatically be treated as abilities possessed by the character in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]].
