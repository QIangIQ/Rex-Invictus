**[[Daniel (Angel)]]** is a high-ranking Angel of the Biblical Pantheon, a six-winged Seraphim, the twin brother of Zaniel, and one of the two personal Seraphim assigned to protect and serve Ariel, the former Heavenly Virtue of Generosity. (Lore adapted from *My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires*.)

### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 558
Daniel is a high-ranking Angel of the [[Biblical Pantheon]], a six-winged Seraphim, the twin brother of [[Zaniel]], and one of the two personal Seraphim assigned to protect and serve Ariel, the former Heavenly Virtue of Generosity. Daniel and Zaniel occupy the rank immediately beneath Ariel within their local command structure; Chapter 558 explains that each of the Seven Heavenly Virtues is entitled to two Seraphim as personal subordinates and guards because Virtues, through their Conceptual authority, stand above ordinary Seraphim in Heaven's hierarchy.[1]
Daniel's limited appearances nevertheless establish him as a considerably calmer and more analytical Angel than his twin. During the crisis surrounding Ariel's near-fatal poisoning, Zaniel immediately focused upon punishing the Humans responsible, whereas Daniel concentrated on diagnosing Ariel's condition, attempting to heal her and determining why ordinary Angelic restoration was failing. He was able to identify the interaction between the Hell-Metal projectile and its absurd concentration of Miasma, but the corruption was powerful enough to injure Daniel merely when he attempted to approach the contaminated wound.[2][3]

### Physical Appearance
Daniel is a handsome, androgynous male Angel with six enormous white wings, blond hair and sapphire-blue eyes. He and [[Zaniel]] are identical twins and are described as nearly impossible to distinguish from one another through their facial appearance alone. Their most obvious physical difference is their hair: Zaniel wears her blond hair long, while Daniel keeps his comparatively short.[1]
His androgynous appearance is not unusual among Angels, whose supernatural beauty frequently reduces conventional masculine and feminine distinctions. Daniel first manifested on the battlefield as a mass of pure white light before assuming his visible Seraphim form beside Ariel.[1]
The novel has not provided a specific height, clothing description or individually unique Angelic transformation for Daniel.

### Personality and Traits
Daniel is disciplined, loyal, calm and considerably more analytical than his twin sister. Both Seraphim obey Ariel immediately and without argument, reflecting the rigid military hierarchy of Heaven, but their personalities become particularly distinct after Ariel is critically wounded. [[Zaniel]] reacts to the betrayal with immediate rage and kills the Human responsible, while Daniel directs his attention toward preserving Ariel's life and understanding the unusual weapon used against her.[2][3]
Even while frustrated by his inability to heal Ariel, Daniel remains focused on solving the problem. He patiently explains that the projectile is still lodged within her body and that the extraordinarily concentrated Miasma is continuously damaging her system. When Zaniel insists that poison should not threaten one of the Seven Virtues, Daniel agrees and recognizes that this is precisely what makes the attack abnormal: the weapon had apparently been deliberately constructed to harm a high-ranking Holy being rather than relying upon conventional poison.[3]
Daniel is also cautious and respectful of command authority. When Alucard approached the critically injured Ariel, Daniel and Zaniel refused to simply allow an unknown Vampire near their superior. They nevertheless avoided attacking him without provocation and only stepped aside after Ariel personally ordered them to permit [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s approach. Daniel subsequently accepted the result when it became obvious that Victor had genuinely removed the corruption and restored Ariel's condition.[3]
His conduct contrasts particularly strongly with Zaniel's impulsiveness. After Ariel was shot, Zaniel immediately executed the attacker, unintentionally destroying the easiest source of information concerning the specialized projectile. Daniel, meanwhile, specifically considered interrogation necessary because understanding the weapon could reveal how it had bypassed Ariel's normal defenses.[2][3]

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Daniel entered the story during the battle surrounding the Vatican and the Inquisition, where Ariel had been deployed as one of Heaven's principal commanders. The conflict had already reached a difficult stalemate because Ariel's presence and the Holy defenses surrounding the Vatican prevented [[Sitri]] and the Demonic army from freely advancing. The situation changed dramatically when Alucard appeared on the battlefield and his overwhelming presence forced Angels, Humans and Demons alike to focus upon him.[1]
Ariel judged that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was not currently hostile toward Heaven and decided against unnecessarily provoking him. After ordering the Human forces outside her defensive position to return, she called for Daniel and [[Zaniel]]. The twins manifested behind her as two masses of white light before revealing their six-winged Seraphim forms. The scene also established their status within Heaven's hierarchy: although Daniel and Zaniel were themselves Seraphim, they served beneath Ariel because the Seven Heavenly Virtues possessed Conceptual authority that elevated them above ordinary Seraphim. Each Virtue was assigned two Seraphim subordinates specifically to assist and protect them. Ariel ordered Daniel and Zaniel to recall the Angelic forces, and both immediately bowed their heads before disappearing to execute the command.[1]
Daniel later witnessed Victor annihilate the majority of the Demonic horde surrounding the battlefield. The raw number of Demons killed was not by itself extraordinary to Ariel and the twins, since powerful Seraphim could cause similarly devastating casualties through Holy Power, but the method disturbed them: Victor's attacks had not merely butchered the Demons' bodies but had damaged or destroyed their Souls as well, preventing the ordinary infernal process through which Demons could eventually return to Hell.[2]
The situation abruptly changed when Ariel was shot in the abdomen by Jonah, a Human infiltrator operating among the Inquisition's forces. Daniel and Zaniel immediately rushed to her side and discovered a wound produced by an unusual projectile made from Hell Metal. Zaniel attacked and killed Jonah in rage before he could be interrogated, while Daniel remained with Ariel and attempted to stabilize her.[2]
By the following moments, Ariel's condition had become critical. The bullet remained lodged inside her while concentrated Miasma spread through her body and corrupted her Holy nature. Daniel attempted to heal her but found that his abilities could not properly reach the wound. He explained to Zaniel that the Miasma concentration was absurdly high and appeared specifically designed to deal with a being such as Ariel. Ordinary poison should have been effectively meaningless against a Heavenly Virtue, yet the combination of Hell Metal and Demonic corruption created a supernatural attack capable of bypassing that natural resistance.[3]
Daniel demonstrated the danger directly when he attempted to approach the contaminated area. The Miasma damaged his hand merely from exposure, leaving it temporarily withered and injured before his Angelic regeneration restored the limb within several seconds. Recognizing that conventional healing would not resolve the problem while the source of the corruption remained inside Ariel, Daniel concluded that interrogating the Human attacker might have provided the information necessary to identify the weapon's construction. Zaniel's earlier execution of Jonah had already eliminated that possibility.[3]
The attempted assassination also created immediate distrust between the Angels and Humans. When General Leonardo tried to approach and insist that Jonah had been a Demonic infiltrator rather than a representative of the Inquisition, the Seraphim refused to simply accept the explanation. Daniel remained focused on Ariel while Zaniel openly warned the Humans to keep their distance. Ariel herself, despite being in severe pain, ordered her guards not to attack indiscriminately because the mission assigned by Michael took priority over personal anger.[3]
Victor eventually approached Ariel. Daniel and Zaniel positioned themselves between him and the injured Virtue, refusing to permit an unknown supernatural being to approach their superior despite Victor deliberately leaving [[Junketsu]] behind. Victor asked whether they intended to allow Ariel to die, but neither Seraphim immediately moved. Only after Ariel personally ordered them to let him pass did Daniel and Zaniel step aside.[3]
Victor determined that the corruption inside Ariel included an exceptionally powerful Demonic influence that his own predatory abilities could consume. He removed it directly from her body, ultimately restoring the Virtue when Daniel's Holy healing had proven ineffective against the specialized attack. Daniel and Zaniel were initially shocked by Victor's extremely unconventional method of healing Ariel, but once the process ended they immediately realized that her condition had genuinely recovered. When Zaniel attempted to retaliate against Victor for what she perceived as him taking advantage of Ariel during the process, Ariel stopped her and confirmed that Victor had actually saved her life.[3]
Daniel survived the incident and remained a Seraphim of Heaven. No later chapter currently provides a major independent storyline, named battle or confirmed death for him.

### Skills & Abilities
Daniel is a six-winged Seraph and consequently belongs to one of the highest conventional ranks of Heaven's Angelic hierarchy. While his limited appearances do not reveal a unique Concept or individually named offensive ability, he demonstrates advanced Holy healing, extremely rapid regeneration and sufficient knowledge of supernatural wounds to diagnose an anti-Angel weapon that had critically injured one of the Seven Heavenly Virtues.

#### Angel Physiology
- Angel Physiology: Daniel is a genuine Angel created within the [[Heavenly Father]]'s supernatural system and possesses the physical, spiritual and energetic qualities inherent to Heaven's soldiers.
- Seraphim Physiology: Daniel possesses six wings and belongs to the Seraphim, one of the highest ranks among conventional Angels. Chapter 558 places ordinary Seraphim below the Seven Heavenly Virtues and Original Seraphim within Heaven's upper hierarchy.[1]
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: As a high-ranking Angel, Daniel possesses strength, speed, durability, stamina, reflexes and senses vastly beyond ordinary Humans.
- Agelessness / [[Immortality]]: Angels are supernatural beings capable of existing indefinitely without ordinary biological aging. Daniel has no known natural lifespan limitation.
- Angelic Regeneration: Daniel possesses rapid supernatural recovery. When concentrated Miasma damaged and withered his hand while he attempted to heal Ariel, the injury restored itself within only several seconds.[3]
- Supernatural Beauty: Like other high-ranking Angels, Daniel possesses the highly symmetrical, androgynous appearance characteristic of the race.[1]

#### Holy Energy
- [[Holy Energy]] Manipulation: Daniel naturally possesses the sacred Energy wielded by Angels. Holy Energy is especially effective against beings aligned with Demonic or otherwise negative supernatural forces.
- Holy Energy Generation: As a Seraphim, Daniel can produce Holy Energy directly through his Angelic nature rather than relying upon an external tool.
- Holy Healing: Daniel can channel his Holy power into injured beings in an attempt to restore their bodies. He personally tried to heal Ariel after she was struck by the specialized Hell-Metal projectile.[3]
- Anti-Demonic Properties: Angelic Holy Energy is naturally opposed to Miasma and Demonic Energy. Daniel's failure to heal Ariel was caused by the exceptional construction and concentration of the attack rather than an inability to use Holy power.[3]
- Holy Light (Racial): Angels can manifest their sacred Energy as supernatural Light. Daniel himself first appears by materializing from pure white light behind Ariel.[1]

#### Healing and Medical Expertise
- Advanced Healing: Daniel was the Seraphim primarily responsible for attempting to stabilize Ariel after her injury rather than [[Zaniel]], establishing healing as one of his directly demonstrated abilities.[3]
- Supernatural Injury Diagnosis: Daniel quickly recognized that Ariel could not be healed normally because the projectile remained embedded inside her and continuously released corruption through her body.[3]
- Miasma Analysis: He correctly identified the extraordinarily high concentration of Miasma affecting Ariel and understood that the amount had been specifically engineered to threaten a being with her level of Holy resistance.[3]
- Hell-Metal Recognition: Daniel and Zaniel immediately recognized the projectile as involving Hell Metal, a material especially dangerous to Angels.[2][3]
- Poison Analysis: Daniel understood that conventional poison could not explain Ariel's condition because a Heavenly Virtue's nature should ordinarily negate such effects. This allowed him to identify the attack as a deliberately specialized supernatural weapon rather than an ordinary toxin.[3]
- Life Preservation: Even though Daniel could not completely cleanse Ariel, he remained focused on preserving her condition and searching for an alternative treatment rather than abandoning the attempt.

#### Flight
- Six-Winged Flight: Daniel possesses six white Angelic wings and can naturally fly through them.[1]
- Aerial Mobility: His Seraphim physiology allows unrestricted three-dimensional movement appropriate for Heaven's high-ranking soldiers.

#### High-Speed Movement
Daniel and [[Zaniel]] can move quickly enough to disappear from ordinary observation. When Ariel ordered them to recall Heaven's Angels, both twins lowered their heads and immediately vanished to carry out the command.[1] The scene does not establish whether this was literal Teleportation or simply extremely high-speed Angelic movement, so it should not automatically be classified as unrestricted teleportation without additional evidence.

#### Military Training
- Angelic Military Discipline: Daniel immediately obeys Ariel's commands and operates efficiently inside Heaven's rigid command structure.[1]
- Protective Detail: His official role beneath Ariel includes protecting a Heavenly Virtue from threats that bypass the ordinary Angelic forces.[1]
- Battlefield Awareness: Daniel remained attentive to Ariel's condition, Human movements and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s approach during a rapidly deteriorating battlefield situation.[2][3]
- Threat Assessment: He correctly recognized that attacking Victor without cause while Ariel was incapacitated would create another dangerous enemy and did not impulsively escalate the confrontation.
- Investigation and Interrogation Awareness: Daniel recognized the tactical value of keeping the attacker alive long enough to determine how the anti-Angel projectile had been constructed.[3]

#### Power and Rank
Daniel is an ordinary Seraphim in the sense of Heaven's upper racial hierarchy, but ordinary Seraphim should not be confused with ordinary Angels. Chapter 558 explicitly establishes that Seraphim are powerful enough to serve as the dedicated guards of the Seven Heavenly Virtues, while the Virtues themselves occupy a higher position because they possess Conceptual authorities comparable in nature to those of Gods.[1]

### Weaknesses
- Miasma / Demonic Energy: Holy and Demonic Energies are naturally opposed. The concentrated Miasma within Ariel's wound damaged Daniel's hand merely when he attempted to approach closely enough to heal her.[3]
- Hell Metal: Hell Metal is specifically dangerous to Angels. The projectile used against Ariel demonstrated that sufficiently powerful weapons constructed from the material can threaten even extremely high-ranking Seraphim and Heavenly Virtues.[2][3]
- Specialized Anti-Angel Weapons: Daniel's healing could not overcome Ariel's wound while the Hell-Metal projectile and concentrated Demonic corruption remained embedded inside her.[3]
- Holy/Demonic Energy Incompatibility: Daniel's sacred nature makes sufficiently concentrated negative Energy actively corrosive to him rather than something he can harmlessly absorb.

### Relationships

#### Ariel Elderblood
Ariel was Daniel's direct superior and the Heavenly Virtue he was assigned to protect. Daniel and [[Zaniel]] served as her two personal Seraphim, an arrangement Chapter 558 identifies as part of the structure surrounding all Seven Heavenly Virtues.[1]
Daniel's loyalty toward Ariel is clearest following her assassination attempt. Rather than pursuing revenge, he immediately attempted to diagnose and heal her, repeatedly exposing himself to concentrated Miasma while searching for a method of saving her. He also refused to allow [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] near Ariel until she personally gave permission, demonstrating that protecting her took priority even when an outsider claimed to possess the solution.[3]

#### Zaniel
[[Zaniel]] is Daniel's twin sister. The two are physically almost identical, sharing blond hair, sapphire-blue eyes, six wings and nearly indistinguishable facial features; Daniel's short hair and Zaniel's long hair are the principal visual difference specifically identified by the narration.[1]
Their personalities differ considerably during crisis. Zaniel is more emotionally explosive and retaliatory, while Daniel remains calmer and more focused upon solving the immediate problem. After Jonah wounded Ariel, Zaniel killed him in anger, whereas Daniel later regretted that the attacker could no longer be interrogated for information concerning the weapon.[2][3]

#### Victor Elderblood
Daniel first encountered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] while serving Ariel during the Demonic War. He witnessed Victor annihilate an enormous number of Demons and saw enough of the attack's spiritual effects to understand that Victor's abilities extended beyond ordinary physical slaughter.[2]
Their most direct interaction occurred after Ariel was critically injured. Daniel initially blocked Victor's access to her, but stepped aside when Ariel ordered him to do so. Victor subsequently succeeded where Daniel's healing had failed by consuming the Demonic corruption responsible for the wound. Although Daniel was visibly disturbed by Victor's unconventional method, he recognized that Ariel had genuinely recovered and did not continue opposing him once she confirmed that Victor had saved her.[3]

#### Heavenly Father
[[Heavenly Father]] is Daniel's Creator and ultimate sovereign. As a member of the Heavenly Host, Daniel belongs to the military and spiritual order established by the Heavenly Father and operates through the hierarchy descending from Heaven's Creator to its commanders and Seraphim.
