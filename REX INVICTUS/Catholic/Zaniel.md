### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 558
Zaniel is an ancient Angel and six-winged Seraph of the [[Biblical Pantheon]]. She is the twin sister of Daniel and originally served directly beneath Ariel, the Heavenly Virtue of Generosity. Zaniel and Daniel functioned as trusted high-ranking subordinates who could be dispatched to relay Ariel's commands throughout Heaven's military forces.[1]
Zaniel is considerably more emotional and aggressive than her twin. This became especially apparent after Ariel was critically wounded by Jonah using a weapon made from Hell's Metal. Zaniel immediately killed Jonah in rage before he could be interrogated and later struggled to restrain herself from attacking [[Victor Elderblood]] when he arrived to treat Ariel.[2][3]

### Physical Appearance
Zaniel is a female Seraph possessing six large white wings. When she and Daniel first appeared in response to Ariel's summons, the twins initially manifested as beings composed almost entirely of brilliant white Light before their Angelic forms became distinguishable.[1]
The novel has not provided an extensive description of Zaniel's facial features, height, hair color or normal clothing. Details appearing exclusively in artwork should therefore not automatically be treated as canonical physical characteristics.

### Personality
Zaniel is fiercely loyal, emotional, impulsive and highly protective of those she considers her superiors and family. Her devotion to Ariel is particularly pronounced; she reacts to threats against Ariel with immediate hostility and has difficulty maintaining the emotional detachment expected from an Angel when Ariel is placed in danger.[2][3]
Her loyalty is accompanied by strong military discipline. When Ariel summoned Zaniel and Daniel and instructed them to order the other Angels to withdraw from the battlefield, neither twin questioned the command. They lowered their heads respectfully and immediately departed to carry out Ariel's instructions.[1]
This obedience is especially important because Ariel's authority over the twins is clearly hierarchical. Zaniel is powerful enough to be a Seraph, yet she consistently accepts Ariel's commands without resistance even when those commands contradict her own emotional desires.[1][3]
Zaniel's greatest weakness is her temper. When she discovered that Jonah had critically wounded Ariel, she reacted before considering the strategic consequences. She immediately attacked and killed him, preventing Heaven from extracting information concerning the conspiracy behind Ariel's assassination attempt.[2]
Her emotional nature contrasts noticeably with Daniel's calmer disposition. Where Daniel focused upon Ariel's condition and attempted to treat her injuries, Zaniel directed her attention toward punishing the person responsible.[2][3]
Zaniel also possesses a strong distrust of beings traditionally classified by Heaven as dangerous or evil. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] arrived after Ariel had been wounded, Zaniel immediately viewed him as a potential threat and wanted to attack him. She restrained herself only because Ariel explicitly ordered her not to interfere.[3]
Her hostility toward Victor was intensified by the circumstances surrounding Ariel's treatment. Zaniel already felt helpless because neither she nor Daniel could resolve Ariel's worsening condition, making Victor's ability to intervene particularly frustrating for the proud Seraph.[3]
Despite this temperament, Zaniel is capable of controlling herself when given a direct command by a superior she respects. Her repeated obedience to Ariel demonstrates that her impulsiveness does not override her loyalty to Heaven's chain of command.

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Zaniel and Daniel first appeared after Ariel summoned the twin Seraphim during the growing conflict involving Heaven and Hell.
The twins manifested before Ariel as two beings composed of brilliant white Light, each possessing six wings. Their appearance and immediate obedience demonstrated that they occupied a high position among Heaven's Angels while still remaining beneath Ariel within the established hierarchy.[1]
Ariel ordered Zaniel and Daniel to spread instructions recalling the Angels from the battlefield. Both twins lowered their heads respectfully in acknowledgment before disappearing to carry out the order.[1]
The twins became considerably more important shortly afterward when Ariel was attacked.
Ariel was struck by a weapon manufactured from Hell's Metal, a material particularly dangerous to Angelic beings. The wound exposed her body to Demonic contamination and placed even a high-ranking Heavenly Virtue in critical condition.[2]
Zaniel rushed to Ariel's side alongside Daniel. Upon examining the injury, the Seraphim recognized the nature of the weapon and understood how serious Ariel's condition had become.[2]
The attacker was revealed to be Jonah.
Zaniel reacted immediately.
Overwhelmed by rage at seeing Ariel injured, she attacked Jonah and cut him in half before the conspirator could be interrogated.[2]
Her decision created an immediate strategic problem. Jonah potentially possessed valuable intelligence concerning the people responsible for the attack and the larger conspiracy operating around Heaven. By killing him instantly, Zaniel eliminated one of the most obvious sources through which that information might have been obtained.[2]
The act demonstrated both her extraordinary loyalty to Ariel and the impulsiveness that accompanies it.
Ariel's condition continued worsening.
Daniel attempted to use his abilities to treat her, but the Demonic contamination and properties of the injury interfered with ordinary Angelic recovery. The Miasma affecting Ariel made the wound extremely difficult for Heaven's own forces to resolve.[3]
Zaniel became increasingly frustrated as she watched her twin fail to stabilize their superior.
The situation became even more tense when [[Victor Elderblood]], still widely known as Alucard among the supernatural factions, arrived.
Zaniel immediately treated Victor as a threat.
Her first instinct was to attack him, but Ariel—despite her condition—directly ordered Zaniel to remain still and allow Victor to approach.[3]
Zaniel obeyed, albeit reluctantly.
Victor then examined Ariel and intervened in her condition using methods considerably different from conventional Angelic healing. His involvement placed Zaniel in an uncomfortable position: an individual she distrusted and regarded as dangerous was accomplishing what the Angels surrounding Ariel had been unable to do themselves.[3]
Zaniel remained visibly hostile throughout the interaction, but Ariel's authority prevented her from attacking Victor.
The incident became one of the earliest direct interactions between Victor and members of Ariel's personal Angelic entourage. It also foreshadowed the gradual collapse of the rigid assumptions separating Heaven's forces from supernatural beings traditionally categorized as evil.

### Relationships

#### Daniel
Daniel is Zaniel's twin brother. The two usually operate together and were introduced simultaneously when Ariel summoned them.[1]
Their personalities appear noticeably different. Zaniel is the more emotionally aggressive twin, immediately attacking Jonah after Ariel was wounded, while Daniel focused upon attempting to treat Ariel's injuries.[2][3]
Despite these differences, the twins demonstrate complete coordination when performing their duties. Both acknowledged Ariel's order simultaneously and departed together to distribute her commands among Heaven's forces.[1]

#### Ariel Elderblood
Ariel was Zaniel's direct superior and someone toward whom she displayed extraordinary loyalty.
Zaniel followed Ariel's instructions without hesitation during military operations and reacted with extreme anger after Ariel was critically wounded.[1][2]
Her killing of Jonah was motivated almost entirely by this emotional attachment. Zaniel considered the attack upon Ariel sufficiently unforgivable that punishment took precedence over interrogation or intelligence gathering.[2]
Ariel also appears to be one of the few individuals capable of reliably restraining Zaniel. When Zaniel wanted to attack [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Ariel's direct command was enough to make her stand down despite her obvious hostility.[3]

#### Victor Elderblood
Zaniel initially regarded [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] with suspicion and hostility.
When Victor approached the wounded Ariel, Zaniel immediately prepared herself for confrontation and was willing to attack him despite Ariel's already critical condition.[3]
Ariel personally ordered Zaniel not to interfere.
Zaniel obeyed but remained visibly displeased as Victor treated Ariel through methods the Angels had been unable to reproduce.[3]
The checked chapters do not establish a later personal relationship between Zaniel and Victor. Therefore, Ariel's eventual incorporation into Victor's family should not automatically be extended into an alliance or friendship between Victor and Zaniel without a later direct appearance.

#### Jonah
Jonah was responsible for critically wounding Ariel with Hell's Metal.
Upon identifying him as the attacker, Zaniel immediately killed him by cutting his body in half.[2]
Although the act satisfied Zaniel's desire for retaliation, it prevented Heaven from interrogating Jonah concerning the larger conspiracy behind Ariel's attack.

### Skills & Abilities
Zaniel is a six-winged Seraph and therefore possesses supernatural capabilities considerably beyond ordinary Angels. However, her brief appearances provide only a limited number of direct feats. Generic abilities not demonstrated or clearly established for her should not be added solely because other Seraphim possess them.

#### Seraph Physiology
- Seraph Physiology: Zaniel is a high-ranking Angel with six wings and possesses the supernatural physical and spiritual characteristics associated with Seraphim.[1]
- Superhuman Strength: Zaniel killed Jonah in a single immediate attack, cutting his body completely in half.[2]
- Superhuman Speed: She was able to attack Jonah before any prolonged interrogation or intervention could occur and could rapidly depart to carry out Ariel's battlefield commands.[1][2]
- Superhuman Reflexes: As a Seraph active within Heaven's military forces, Zaniel possesses reflexes significantly beyond ordinary supernatural combatants.
- Enhanced Durability: Her Seraph body possesses the supernatural resistance characteristic of high-ranking Angels.
- Enhanced Stamina: Zaniel can operate within large-scale supernatural conflicts without the limitations of mortal physiology.
- Extreme Longevity: Zaniel is an ancient Angel whose lifespan extends far beyond Humanity's recorded history.
- Flight: Her six wings allow unrestricted aerial movement.[1]
- [[Holy Energy]]: As a Seraph, Zaniel naturally possesses the Holy Energy of Heaven. The checked chapters do not show a named personal Holy technique.
- Holy Presence: Zaniel and Daniel appeared as intensely luminous Angelic beings during their first manifestation.[1]

#### Light Manifestation
- Photokinetic Manifestation: Zaniel and Daniel initially appeared in forms resembling pure white Light.[1]
- Light-Based Movement: The twins could appear and disappear extremely rapidly while performing Ariel's orders. Whether this represents literal transformation into Light, teleportation or high-speed Angelic movement is not explained in sufficient detail to assign a narrower classification.[1]

#### Combat Abilities
- Combat Proficiency: Zaniel possesses enough combat ability to act as a high-ranking Seraph under Ariel and kill a supernatural opponent almost instantly.[2]
- Bisecting Strike: Zaniel's only clearly demonstrated offensive feat is the attack that severed Jonah's body in half.[2]
- The exact weapon or mechanism used in the strike is not sufficiently established in the checked material and should not be specified without further evidence.

#### Supernatural Knowledge
- Material Recognition: Zaniel and Daniel recognized the supernatural danger presented by Hell's Metal after Ariel was wounded.[2]
- Miasma Awareness: Zaniel understood that Demonic contamination was interfering with Ariel's condition and preventing normal Angelic recovery.[3]

#### Military Skills
- Command Relay: Ariel trusted Zaniel and Daniel to distribute orders throughout Heaven's forces, indicating that the twins held sufficient status to communicate commands to lower-ranking Angels.[1]
- Military Discipline: Zaniel immediately acknowledged Ariel's commands and carried them out without argument even when her personal emotions strongly disagreed with them.[1][3]

### Weaknesses
- Emotional Impulsiveness: Zaniel's greatest demonstrated weakness is her tendency to act in anger before considering strategic consequences. Killing Jonah immediately prevented Heaven from interrogating him for information.[2]
- Protectiveness Toward Ariel: Threats against Ariel provoke unusually intense reactions from Zaniel and can override her normal judgment.[2][3]
- Demonic Materials: As an Angel, Zaniel belongs to a race naturally disadvantaged against certain infernal substances such as Hell's Metal and sufficiently concentrated Miasma. Although Zaniel herself is not shown being wounded by Hell's Metal, she immediately recognized its danger after seeing its effect upon Ariel.[2]

### Power
Zaniel's precise combat level has not been directly established.
Her six wings identify her as a Seraph and therefore place her above ordinary Angels, but the novel has not provided sufficient direct feats to accurately compare her with Original Seraphim such as Michael, [[Gabriel]] or [[Azrael]]. Likewise, her service under Ariel does not automatically mean she possesses power equivalent to a member of the Seven Heavenly Virtues.
Her strongest demonstrated combat feat is killing Jonah instantly, while most of her appearances emphasize loyalty, military discipline and her relationship with Ariel rather than large-scale combat.
