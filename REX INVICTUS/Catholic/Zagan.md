### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 524
Zagan, commonly referred to as King Zagan, Duke Zagan, or simply The Merchant, is a high-ranking Demon, former 61st Key of Solomon, and former 61st Duke of [[Biblical Hell]]. Despite occupying one of the lowest numerical positions among the 72 Dukes of Hell, Zagan possesses wealth, political influence, military forces, and personal danger comparable to or exceeding Demons ranked within the highest twenty positions.[1]
Unlike many of the Dukes who built their reputations primarily through direct violence, Zagan's influence comes from an enormous commercial network, near-unlimited wealth, information, rare commodities, and more than thirty-two Legions of Demons under his authority.[1] His true power, the origin of his wealth, and the methods by which a comparatively low-ranked Duke accumulated so much influence remain largely unknown. Even the Inquisition possessed almost no useful information regarding how to confront him, and it considered the safest strategy simply to avoid attracting his interest.[1]
Zagan served under [[Diablo]] during the Demonic War but was never blindly loyal to him. He disliked Diablo's increasingly disruptive decisions, openly criticized his methods in private, and preferred the earlier political balance created by the rivalry between Diablo and Lilith.[1] When [[Victor Elderblood]] conquered Hell and established himself as the new Demon King, Zagan carefully evaluated both rulers rather than immediately committing himself to either side.[3]
After Victor ultimately emerged as the uncontested ruler of Hell, Zagan voluntarily submitted to him alongside Phoenix, explaining that Victor had won and therefore deserved his support.[2] Unlike most Demons beneath Victor, however, Zagan is deliberately not kept under strict control. Victor considers individuals like Zagan more useful when allowed to operate freely, though the exact means by which Victor ensures his cooperation remains unknown.[2]

### Physical Appearance
Zagan normally appears in a humanoid form as an elegant man wearing an expensive and elaborate suit. During his confrontation with the Scarlett sisters, he maintained a neutral smile almost constantly, regardless of whether he was being threatened or directly attacked.[1]
His presentation strongly reflects his identity as a merchant. Zagan behaves more like an eccentric noble businessman than a conventional Demon Duke, drinking wine from golden cups, sitting upon elaborate golden thrones, and maintaining refined gestures even in the middle of a battlefield.[1]
The apparent luxury of his clothing and accessories is not merely decorative. Zagan can transform the material around him at will, meaning objects such as his golden cup, throne, coins, and weapons can be altered into entirely different shapes or substances whenever he wishes.[1]

### Personality
Zagan is carefree, eccentric, calculating, opportunistic, manipulative, and extraordinarily difficult to predict.
On the surface, he behaves almost absurdly casually for a Demon Duke. During the invasion of the Scarlett territory, the army assigned to him was annihilated, yet Zagan displayed almost no anger or concern. Instead, he sat down, created wine from the Ice surrounding him, transformed a golden cup into a throne, and waited while casually speaking with the Scarlett sisters.[1]
This apparent carelessness hides an extremely observant and intelligent individual. Zagan deliberately studies the personalities, relationships, weaknesses, and desires of people around him before deciding how they can be useful. During his meeting with Ruby, Lacus, and Pepper, he revealed private information about each woman specifically to observe their reactions and verify several theories he had developed about them.[1]
His greatest defining trait is his mentality as a merchant.
Zagan claims that his primary interests are:
- Gold.
- Rare objects.
- Unique collectibles.
- Information.
- Profitable transactions.
- Anything capable of becoming part of an interesting trade.[1]
To Zagan, nearly everything has a price. He openly declared that everything was negotiable and jokingly extended this principle even to his own daughter's virginity, offering to trade her to the Scarlett sisters as though discussing an ordinary commercial transaction.[1]
Despite how ridiculous his behavior can appear, Zagan is not foolish. His humor frequently functions as misdirection, lowering the tension of a situation while allowing him to gather information. Ruby quickly recognized that speaking with him felt similar to negotiating with Esther or a Witch: every sentence could potentially contain another hidden objective.[1]
Zagan is also a highly skilled opportunist. He does not attach himself permanently to rulers because of loyalty or ideology. Instead, he evaluates which side is most beneficial to him.
This became especially apparent when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became the new King of Hell. Zagan acknowledged that Victor was charismatic, manipulative, ruthless, decisive, and unusually capable of understanding how Demons needed to be ruled. He described those characteristics as belonging to a nearly perfect Demon King.[3]
Despite recognizing Victor's potential, Zagan did not immediately betray [[Diablo]]. Diablo had been an established "customer" for thousands of years and promised enormous rewards if his plan succeeded, while Victor represented greater uncertainty but potentially greater future profit. Zagan therefore initially preferred remaining neutral rather than committing himself prematurely.[3]
His friendship with Phoenix is based upon similar principles. The two share a small independent association built around mutual benefit rather than strict loyalty to one another or any sovereign.[3]
Zagan also possesses little respect for Diablo personally. During his conversation with the Scarlett sisters, his internal thoughts reveal that he disliked Diablo's plans and even wished Lilith would somehow regain enough influence to keep him under control.[1]
Nevertheless, Zagan follows orders when doing so remains advantageous. His loyalty is transactional rather than emotional.

### Reputation
Zagan's numerical rank among the 72 Dukes is extremely misleading.
Although officially the 61st Key of Solomon, his influence and military strength were considered comparable to the Demons ranked within the highest twenty positions.[1]
The Inquisition regarded him as one of the most troublesome Demons to confront because virtually nothing reliable was known about his full abilities. Training concerning Zagan did not provide a detailed combat strategy. Instead, [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] were specifically advised:
Do not intervene.[1]
Zagan was classified as a relatively passive Demon who generally would not act unless something attracted his interest or someone attacked him first. The Inquisition therefore considered avoiding his attention safer than attempting to prepare conventional countermeasures against him.[1]
His reputation originated from three major factors:
- His enormous personal wealth.
- His more than thirty-two Legions of Demons.
- The complete lack of reliable information explaining how a Demon ranked sixty-first had acquired either of them.[1]
Even the Scarlett sisters, all highly trained combatants, instinctively recognized that attacking Zagan was dangerous. Ruby repeatedly failed to identify a safe opening despite Zagan simply sitting before them, while Lacus and Pepper experienced the same sensation that a direct confrontation would not end as expected.[1]

### Wealth and Military Power
Zagan is one of the wealthiest Demons ever introduced.
His wealth was described as effectively near-infinite from the perspective of those researching the Dukes, and the source of that fortune was unknown even to the organizations attempting to gather information on him.[1]
Zagan also commands more than thirty-two Legions of Demons, an extraordinary military force for someone occupying such a low official position within the Ars Goetia hierarchy.[1]
The combination of financial power, military strength, information gathering, and unknown supernatural abilities resulted in Zagan receiving the informal title of King despite technically remaining only a Duke.
During the invasion of [[Nightingale]], however, the Demon soldiers attacking Clan Scarlett were specifically described as forces given to Zagan by [[Diablo]], meaning they should not automatically be equated with Zagan's personal Legions.[1]

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Zagan first appeared directly during [[Diablo]]'s coordinated invasion of the three Vampire Count territories in [[Nightingale]].
While Phoenix attacked the Fulger territory and other Demon Dukes targeted the remaining Counts, Zagan was assigned responsibility for invading [[Scarlett Clan]] territory.[4]
Siena Scarlett was informed that the invading commander was the 61st Key of Solomon, King Zagan, accompanied by his Demon forces.[4]
The Scarlett sisters responded by combining their Bloodline abilities into a massive environmental attack. Lacus filled the battlefield with mist, Pepper introduced the required water, Siena froze the atmospheric conditions, and Ruby manipulated the resulting Ice and snow. The combined attack transformed the territory into a frozen battlefield and annihilated the majority of the attacking Demons.[4]
Zagan was not particularly concerned.
When the sisters later confronted him directly, he reached toward one of Ruby's snowflakes and allowed it to freeze his finger. Instead of breaking the Ice normally, he transmuted the Ice into water and then transformed the water into wine. He summoned a golden cup through a portal, poured the wine into it, and calmly drank while complimenting the Scarlett sisters' technique.[1]
Ruby identified him as the 61st Key of Solomon but noted that his influence and military strength rivaled the highest-ranking Dukes despite his position.[1]
Zagan then threw his golden cup toward the ground and transformed the object into an elaborate golden throne. Sitting calmly upon it, he explained that the Demons Diablo had assigned him were already dead and therefore he saw little reason to continue fighting immediately.[1]
[[Liena Walter]], who possessed Inquisition training regarding supernatural threats, explained the organization's limited information concerning Zagan. He was known to command over thirty-two Legions and was considered eccentric, unpredictable, and unusually passive. The Inquisition's recommended strategy was simply to avoid attracting his attention because it possessed no reliable combat countermeasure against him.[1]
Zagan subsequently revealed his identity as a merchant.
He transformed part of his golden throne into a coin, multiplied the coin, liquefied the resulting gold, and returned it to the throne while explaining that a trader should always examine an opportunity carefully before deciding how to profit from it.[1]
He then demonstrated the depth of his information network.
Zagan accurately identified the personal histories of Ruby, Lacus, and Pepper, including information regarding their original Clans and backgrounds that was not widely available. He attempted to manipulate Lacus by claiming that survivors from her extinct Clan might still exist, prompting Ruby to immediately attack him before he could exploit her sister's uncertainty.[1]
Ruby's spear strike was automatically blocked when a golden spike emerged from Zagan's throne. Further golden thorns attacked from beneath Ruby when she attempted to move against him again.[1]
Despite the attack, Zagan remained completely calm.
He then tested another hypothesis by asking whether Lacus and Pepper were interested in joining [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s romantic household. Ruby's immediate fury confirmed to Zagan that her relationship with Victor was far deeper than publicly known.[1]
The Scarlett sisters quickly realized that the real danger of the conversation was not simply Zagan's supernatural abilities. He was actively gathering information from every emotional response they displayed.
Ruby's battle instincts repeatedly warned her against attacking. Although Zagan appeared relaxed and exposed, she could not identify a genuine opening, and both Lacus and Pepper experienced the same danger response.[1]
Rather than demand payment, Zagan eventually offered them information for free after realizing he had provoked Ruby too far. He revealed that Diablo's attacks against the Vampire Count territories were fundamentally a diversion intended to prevent Nightingale from interfering with the larger Demonic invasion of Earth.[1]
The revelation was accurate. Ruby contacted Esther and confirmed that Demonic forces had already attacked multiple Human regions and that entire supernatural powers had been drawn into the conflict.
Zagan mocked Diablo's methods internally and openly demonstrated that he did not follow the Demon King with blind loyalty. He privately wished that Lilith would regain enough influence to restrain Diablo and considered the invasion disruptive to the relative balance he preferred.[1]
Zagan then elaborated upon his merchant philosophy, claiming that he enjoyed gold, unique collectibles, rare items, and a good trade.
He declared that everything was negotiable, even his daughter's virginity.[1]
Zagan produced an image of his daughter through black Miasma. She was a towering red-skinned Half-Succubus standing roughly 220 centimeters tall, with pointed horns, golden eyes, large Demonic wings, and a tail. Zagan casually attempted to offer her as part of a potential transaction, only for Lacus and Pepper to reject him immediately.[1]
The encounter ended when an enormous presence descended upon Nightingale.
Zagan immediately recognized that [[Scathach]] had returned.
Despite his earlier confidence, he showed no interest whatsoever in remaining long enough to confront the Strongest Female Vampire. He converted his throne back into a golden cup, opened a portal behind himself, hurried through it, and escaped moments before Scathach arrived alongside [[Alexios Alioth]].[1]
Ruby, Lacus, and Pepper were relieved when he left and collectively regarded the conversation as more exhausting than an ordinary life-or-death battle.[1]
Zagan later remained in Hell while Victor's conquest of the Realm accelerated.
When Diablo recognized that Victor had become a serious threat, he ordered [[Baal]] to involve Zagan in an operation intended to eliminate Victor. Baal visibly disliked interacting with Zagan and regarded him as an unpredictable joker whose involvement could complicate virtually any situation.
By that point, however, Zagan had already begun observing Victor's rise rather than automatically committing himself to Diablo.
After Victor successfully manipulated Hell's Demons through a combination of fear, reward, public judgment, and absolute authority, Zagan openly admitted that he had underestimated him.[3]
He described Victor as a natural manipulator who correctly understood the Demonic race and knew how to balance reward and punishment. To Zagan, Victor's charisma, ruthlessness, decisiveness, and willingness to make difficult choices made him an ideal Demon King.[3]
Phoenix subsequently joined him.
The two discussed whether they should continue supporting Diablo or move toward the newly established King. Zagan remained undecided because both rulers represented different forms of potential profit. Diablo possessed thousands of years of history with him and promised enormous rewards if his plans succeeded; Victor was younger and less predictable, but demonstrated enormous potential and possessed the unusual ability to strengthen Demons merely through his growing authority over Hell.[3]
Rather than choosing based upon morality or loyalty, Zagan evaluated the matter entirely as a business decision.
Phoenix ultimately suggested that they did not necessarily need to choose only one ruler and could continue dealing with both until circumstances made the superior investment obvious.[3]
The exchange established the independent merchant organization shared by Zagan and Phoenix and demonstrated how little either man cared for the ideological conflict between Diablo and Victor.

#### King Among Kings
The decision was eventually made for Zagan when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] definitively won.
After [[Diablo]]'s death and Victor's consolidation of power over Hell, the new Demon King's forces systematically tracked the remnants of Diablo's faction and either absorbed, eliminated, or subjugated them.
Unlike important fugitives such as [[Asmodeus]] and [[Agares]], Zagan did not attempt to hide indefinitely.
Instead, he and Phoenix voluntarily submitted to Victor.[2]
According to Zagan's own reasoning, the matter was simple:
Victor was the winner, therefore Zagan would support him.[2]
The decision perfectly reflected Zagan's worldview. He did not pretend to have suddenly developed emotional loyalty toward Victor; the new Demon King had simply proven himself the superior ruler and therefore became the more profitable person to support.
Victor accepted the submission under unusual conditions.
Most Demons incorporated into Victor's Hell were placed beneath extremely strict authority because Victor understood that Demons naturally exploited weakness whenever given the opportunity. Zagan, however, was allowed considerably greater freedom.[2]
Victor's senior Demonic subordinates distrusted the decision. None of them considered Zagan reliable, and they recognized that the Merchant remained precisely the sort of Demon capable of changing sides if he believed doing so would benefit him.
When they questioned why Victor permitted such an unpredictable individual to operate without the restrictions imposed upon other Demons, Victor's explanation was simple:
Men like Zagan were more useful when free.[2]
The exact safeguard Victor possesses over Zagan has never been revealed. His subordinates nevertheless concluded that Victor almost certainly had some method of preventing Zagan from becoming a genuine threat even while allowing him to retain operational independence.[2]
Zagan consequently became one of the relatively rare former Dukes of Diablo's regime who remained useful enough to continue operating with considerable autonomy under the Demon King of Tyranny.

### Skills & Abilities
Demon Duke Physiology: Zagan is a high-ranking Demon and former member of the 72 Dukes of Hell. Despite being ranked 61st, his presence, military strength, influence, and instinctive danger were considered comparable to much higher-ranked Demons.[1]
Like other powerful Demons, Zagan possesses the basic supernatural characteristics of his race, including a highly resilient spiritual existence and the ability to return to Hell and eventually reconstitute himself if killed through ordinary means. Demons can only be permanently eliminated through specific methods such as Holy Power, special anti-Demonic weapons, Soul destruction, or absorption.[2]

#### Transmutation
Zagan's primary demonstrated ability is an extremely versatile form of Transmutation.
He can change the physical or energetic properties of matter around him into entirely different substances and configurations.[1]
Demonstrated applications include:
- Ice-to-Water Transmutation: When Ruby's snow froze his finger, Zagan transformed the Ice into liquid water.[1]
- Water-to-Wine Transmutation: Immediately after converting the Ice into water, he transformed the water into wine and drank it.[1]
- Object Transformation: Zagan transformed a golden drinking cup into an enormous throne.[1]
- Gold Reshaping: He can reshape portions of his throne into coins, liquid gold, spikes, thorns, or other objects.[1]
- Material Multiplication: A single golden coin in Zagan's hand transformed into several identical coins before being returned to his throne as liquid gold.[1]
- Defensive Transmutation: His throne automatically produced a golden spike capable of blocking Ruby's spear strike before it reached his head.[1]
- Offensive Transmutation: Golden thorns emerged from the ground beneath Ruby when she attempted to reposition herself, demonstrating that his Transmutation can be weaponized directly.[1]
The full scope of Zagan's Transmutation has never been revealed. Even Ruby was unable to determine whether his manipulation of wine and gold represented separate abilities or different applications of a single deeper Power.[1]

#### Spatial Manipulation
- Portal Creation: Zagan can open spatial gates capable of transporting himself and objects between locations.[1]
He casually summoned a golden cup from another location through a portal and later opened a full gate to escape from [[Nightingale]] when [[Scathach]] returned.[1]

#### Information Gathering
Zagan possesses an exceptional information network.
He knew highly sensitive information concerning:
- Ruby's personality and reputation.
- Pepper's heritage.
- Lacus' original extinct Clan.
- The private relationships surrounding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and the Scarlett sisters.
- [[Diablo]]'s true strategic purpose behind the attacks on [[Nightingale]].[1]
Even the Scarlett sisters were disturbed by how much he knew.
Zagan explicitly treats information as merchandise. His identity as a trader therefore extends far beyond physical goods; secrets, rumors, political intelligence, and personal vulnerabilities are all commodities to him.

#### Psychological Analysis
Zagan is exceptionally skilled at reading people.
During his conversation with the Scarlett sisters, he deliberately introduced emotionally sensitive subjects and studied how each woman reacted. From these reactions he evaluated their personalities, decision-making habits, and connection to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[1]
He correctly identified that Ruby tends to overthink unexpected situations and used that tendency to keep her occupied while he continued gathering information.[1]
As a merchant, he considers reading the emotions of potential buyers one of his core abilities.[1]

#### Danger Concealment
Although Zagan stood almost motionless before Ruby, Lacus, and Pepper, none of them could identify a safe opening through which to attack him.
Their trained combat instincts repeatedly warned them that engaging Zagan directly would be dangerous despite the apparent lack of visible preparation.[1]
The exact cause of this phenomenon has not been explained. It may result from his unknown abilities, concealed strength, Transmutation, or some other unrevealed defensive mechanism.

#### Demonic Immortality
As a high-ranking Demon, Zagan possesses the normal regenerative immortality of his species.
Unless killed through Holy Light, specialized weapons, Soul destruction, absorption, or equivalent methods, a Demon who dies outside Hell can eventually return to Hell and reconstruct their body. More powerful Demons require longer periods to fully reconstitute.[2]

### Possessions and Resources

#### Personal Wealth
Zagan possesses extraordinary wealth even by the standards of powerful Demons.
His fortune was sufficiently vast that outsiders described it as nearly limitless, yet neither the Inquisition nor most other supernatural organizations understood how he acquired it.[1]
His wealth is not limited to conventional currency. Zagan collects:
- Gold.
- Rare supernatural objects.
- Unique artifacts.
- Information.
- Collectibles possessed by nobody else.
- Anything sufficiently unusual to possess trading value.[1]

#### Demon Legions
Zagan possesses more than thirty-two Legions of Demons under his personal authority.[1]
This military strength is one of the reasons his effective influence far exceeds what would normally be expected from the 61st-ranked Key of Solomon.

#### Merchant Network
Zagan operates through a large and largely unexplained commercial and intelligence network capable of obtaining information that even major supernatural organizations struggle to acquire.
The exact structure and size of this network remain unknown.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
Zagan initially regarded [[Victor Elderblood]] as an unpredictable new variable rather than a ruler deserving automatic loyalty.
When Victor conquered much of Hell and began establishing himself as its King, Zagan carefully observed his methods. Victor's ability to understand Demonic psychology impressed him considerably.[3]
Zagan described Victor as:
- Manipulative.
- Charismatic.
- Ruthless.
- Decisive.
- Capable of properly balancing reward and punishment.
- An ideal Demon King.[3]
What interested him most was Victor's ability to increase the Power of Demons simply through his expanding authority over Hell. Zagan immediately recognized the commercial and strategic value of remaining close to someone capable of producing such an effect.[3]
After Victor defeated [[Diablo]], Zagan voluntarily submitted because Victor had proven himself the winner.[2]
Victor does not place Zagan beneath the same strict restrictions used for most other Demons, believing the Merchant is more useful when allowed freedom to operate.[2]

#### Diablo
[[Diablo]] was Zagan's former King.
Zagan obeyed Diablo's military orders during the Demonic War and accepted the assignment to invade Clan Scarlett.[4]
His loyalty was nevertheless purely practical.
Privately, Zagan disliked Diablo's aggressive plans and preferred the older balance in which Lilith remained strong enough to restrain him. He openly mocked aspects of Diablo's strategy and did not display the reverence toward him shown by more fanatical subordinates.[1]
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s rise created uncertainty regarding Hell's future, Zagan treated Diablo as an old customer rather than as a beloved sovereign. Diablo's value lay primarily in the rewards his long-term plans could potentially provide.[3]
Once Diablo lost, Zagan immediately shifted his support toward the victor.[2]

#### Phoenix
Phoenix is one of Zagan's closest known associates and friends.
The two maintained a small independent organization that did not consider itself permanently loyal to either [[Diablo]] or [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[3]
Their relationship appears relaxed and familiar. Phoenix could speak casually with Zagan, challenge his reasoning, and openly suggest exploiting both competing rulers instead of choosing one prematurely.[3]
Following Diablo's fall, Phoenix voluntarily submitted to Victor alongside Zagan.[2]

#### Scarlett Sisters
Zagan first interacted directly with Ruby, Lacus, and Pepper while invading Clan Scarlett.
Rather than seriously attempting to kill them, he treated the encounter as an opportunity to observe the sisters and test several theories regarding their personalities and relationships.[1]
The sisters collectively found Zagan extremely irritating and dangerous. By the time he escaped from [[Scathach]], they considered dealing with him more mentally exhausting than an ordinary battle.[1]

#### Unnamed Daughter
Zagan has at least one daughter with an unnamed Succubus.
His daughter is a Half-Succubus approximately 220 centimeters tall with red skin, pointed horns, golden eyes, wings, and a Demonic tail.[1]
Zagan described her as socially capable at times, highly arrogant, and difficult enough that she had apparently remained single for several millennia.
In typical Zagan fashion, he was perfectly willing to offer his daughter's virginity as part of a trade when speaking with the Scarlett sisters.[1]
Whether his offer was serious or simply another attempt to provoke a reaction is unknown.
