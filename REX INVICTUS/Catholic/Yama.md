### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 579
Yama is the title of the current ruler of the Hindu [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]], an Asura, God and complete Ruler who succeeded his grandfather, the Old Yama. His true personal name has never been revealed. Yama originally ruled Naraka as an independent King of Hell and commanded the Asuras and supernatural infrastructure of the Hindu Underworld through an exceptionally powerful Ruler's Bond.[1]
Despite being relatively young compared with many ancient Divine beings, Yama possesses unusually high potential. Under [[Merlin]]'s instruction, he deliberately concealed the majority of his real strength from [[Diablo]], allowing outsiders to believe that he was only somewhat stronger than powerful High-Level combat Gods such as [[Ares]] or [[Thor]] because of his additional Ruler authority. His battle against [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] revealed that this public image considerably underestimated him, while [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] later judged Yama capable of contending with Vlad and revealed that he possessed another innate power that even Merlin had never completely understood.[2][3]
Yama is defined as much by pragmatism as power. His cooperation with Diablo existed only for as long as their agreement benefited him, and he abandoned the Biblical Demon King immediately after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s conquest invalidated its conditions.[4] Centuries later, Yama voluntarily surrendered his independent political position and joined the [[Elderblood Empire]], accepting that he would begin from the lower levels of its meritocratic system rather than receive privilege simply for having previously been a King. He considered the loss of status preferable to becoming stagnant like his grandfather.[3]

### Physical Appearance
In his most Human-like form, Yama appears as a heavily muscular man approximately two meters tall. He possesses burnt-brown skin, long black hair and dark obsidian eyes. Despite his position as an ancient ruler of the dead, he has been shown wearing a comparatively modern black suit.
When Yama invokes the authority connected to his [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]], a supernatural crown composed of Miasma and Hellfire manifests above his head. Vlad immediately recognized the crown and Yama's Soul as evidence that he possessed an extremely strong connection to the Hindu Underworld and had successfully taken complete control of the realm from his predecessor.[1]
Yama can further manifest his Asura characteristics during combat. An additional pair of arms forms beneath his normal arms, giving him four usable limbs, while thick Miasma can gather around his hands and transform them into large claw-like weapons.[1]
His more pronounced Demonic form possesses significantly less Human characteristics, with his skin taking red and dark-blue tones, elongated fangs and elaborate ceremonial clothing dominated by red and gold.

### Personality
Yama is arrogant, competitive, highly pragmatic and substantially more intelligent than his impulsive behavior initially suggests. [[Merlin]] frequently regarded him as an immature or sadistic "brat" because Yama could abandon carefully constructed plans when confronted by an opponent interesting enough to awaken his desire for battle.[2]
His confrontation with Vlad demonstrated this contradiction particularly well. The original purpose of the encounter was not to defeat the Vampire King but to secretly determine whether previous attempts to weaken him had succeeded. Yama nevertheless became increasingly excited as Vlad displayed more of his abilities and eventually began exposing powers that Merlin had specifically instructed him to conceal from [[Diablo]].[2]
Yama possesses considerable pride as both a King and powerful supernatural being. Vlad's comparison between Yama's narrow worldview and ordinary Human arrogance offended him enough to make him escalate their battle further, culminating in the activation of Yama's Judgment.[2] He similarly regarded the Witches of Arcane as politically weak and became openly irritated when Evie Moriarty repeatedly rejected his proposals.[5]
However, unlike many ancient rulers whose pride prevents retreat, Yama is capable of accepting humiliation whenever the alternative threatens his future. He abandoned Diablo after their agreement became invalid rather than die in a lost war, withdrew from Arcane rather than provoke [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], sacrificed Merlin when the Elderblood faction came for him and eventually knelt before Violet to ask for admission into the Empire.[4][5][3]
Yama considers continuous development more important than preserving appearances. When he asked to join the [[Elderblood Empire]], he openly acknowledged that kneeling injured his pride but argued that pride was worthless if preserving it caused him to stagnate. He specifically cited the Old Yama as an example of what he did not want to become, believing that any being which ceases developing will eventually decay.[3]
Despite his selfish pragmatism, Yama is an effective ruler. Vlad was surprised by the loyalty of Yama's Generals because such devotion was unusual among Demonic factions. Yama credited this to understanding something his grandfather had failed to appreciate: competent and loyal subordinates were valuable assets rather than disposable servants.[1]
His relationship with Merlin demonstrated both sides of his personality. Yama trusted Merlin's intelligence, [[Magic]] and instruction enough to accept him as both teacher and subordinate, but repeatedly acknowledged that Merlin's eccentricity made him unreliable. This concern proved justified when Merlin attempted to sign away enormous concessions to Arcane purely because he wanted access to new knowledge.[5]
Yama eventually betrayed Merlin by providing information to the Elderblood faction. His decision was overwhelmingly pragmatic, although Violet's perception indicated that he still felt some genuine sadness and pity over his teacher's fate. Yama rationalized his participation through Merlin's own philosophy that a student responsible for surpassing and eventually causing his teacher's death had achieved the ultimate proof of advancement.[3]

### History
The current Yama is the grandson of the Old Yama, the previous ruler of the Hindu [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]]. Vlad discovered their relationship by examining Yama's Soul and recognizing the spiritual signature of the ruler he had known previously.[1]
The exact circumstances surrounding the Old Yama's death have not been shown directly. The conversation between Vlad and the current Yama strongly implies a violent succession, with Yama casually discussing how Demonic families frequently kill one another for authority. [[Merlin]] later instructed him to publicly claim that [[Diablo]]'s forces had killed the Old Yama, further implying that the true circumstances of the succession were something they deliberately concealed.[1][2]
Whatever occurred, Yama did considerably more than inherit a political title. His Ruler's Bond showed that he had successfully taken complete metaphysical control of the Hindu Underworld and its inhabitants.[1]
Merlin later became one of his Generals, advisor and teacher. Under Merlin's guidance, Yama developed methods for suppressing the apparent scale of his power, allowing him to cooperate with Diablo while hiding abilities that might otherwise make the Biblical Demon King consider him a greater threat.[2]

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Yama first became relevant to the wider war after separating the Hindu [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] from the ordinary administration of the [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]]. The change interfered with the Pantheon's management of its dead, causing Souls associated with Hindu territory to become displaced while the supernatural world was already destabilizing.[6]
Rather than becoming [[Diablo]]'s subordinate, Yama entered into an agreement with him as another King of Hell. His Asuras therefore participated in Diablo's broader coalition while remaining under Yama's independent authority.
Yama later targeted ancient Vampire populations in South Africa and captured Vampires whose rescue eventually brought [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] directly before him. Upon meeting, Vlad immediately recognized that the Demon was not the Yama he had previously known. His examination of Yama's Soul revealed the Old Yama's spiritual signature, and the current ruler identified himself as the former King's grandson.[1]
Yama's Generals attempted to protect him despite standing against the First Vampire Progenitor, surprising Vlad with their loyalty. At the same time, the crown of Miasma and Hellfire appearing above Yama revealed the strength of his connection to the Hindu Underworld. Vlad recognized that Yama had achieved complete rulership over the realm rather than merely inheriting its throne.[1]
Yama offered Vlad a simple agreement: if Vlad defeated him, the captured Vampires would be released. Vlad immediately suspected that the confrontation existed for another reason because Yama lacked the pure battle-driven mentality of warriors such as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] or [[Scathach]].[1]
The duel nevertheless became increasingly serious. Yama manifested four arms and concentrated Miasma around his limbs while his Generals maintained a large dimensional battlefield around the combatants. Vlad initially overwhelmed Yama by transforming his body into living blood, slipping through his defenses and draining his life so rapidly that he briefly believed the Demon King had died. Yama reconstructed his body almost immediately and manifested his crown again.[1]
Yama increased his power and began displaying abilities he had deliberately concealed. He generated powerful Hellfire and Miasma, created supernatural weapons and eventually activated Yama's Judgment, opening a gigantic red spatial rift from which enormous arms emerged. When one of the hands struck the living blood controlled by Vlad, the Souls contained within the affected portion disappeared entirely.[2]
[[Merlin]] was forced to reveal that the duel had never been intended to progress this far. Their actual objective was determining whether Vlad remained healthy and whether previous plans to weaken or poison him had succeeded. Merlin also explained that Yama had intentionally maintained a public image approximately comparable to a powerful High-Level combat God such as [[Ares]] or [[Thor]], with any unusual strength being attributed to his Ruler authority. Revealing Yama's full arsenal risked alerting Diablo to the deception.[2]
Yama's battle enthusiasm nearly created another disaster when he attempted to directly receive Dragon Breath produced through an Ancient Dragon whose blood Vlad possessed. Merlin intervened and redirected the attack, explaining that Dragon Breath inflicted abnormal damage capable of making conventional regeneration ineffective.[2]
Once their objective was complete and Vlad's forces had recovered the captured Vampires, Merlin forced Yama to withdraw. Yama complained that leaving would make them appear as if they were fleeing, while Merlin privately recognized that fleeing was exactly what they were doing. The duel ended without either King conclusively defeating the other.[2]
Yama later participated in the larger Demonic War alongside Merlin. When the [[Heavenly Father]] unleashed a widespread Divine Judgment across the battlefield, Yama protected himself through an enormous barrier supplemented by Merlin's [[Magic]]. The barrier survived but became heavily cracked, with Yama privately acknowledging that the attack would have broken through without Merlin's assistance.[7]
Even then, Yama deliberately refused to expose all of his power. Maintaining the illusion surrounding his strength remained strategically more important than displaying everything merely to resist an attack that could already be survived with Merlin's support.[7]
The Hindu Underworld also possessed a logistical advantage over [[Biblical Hell]]: Yama's forces could deploy lower Asuras without requiring the same sacrificial summoning process used by Diablo's Demons. Although individually weaker on average, this allowed Yama to mobilize large numbers of soldiers efficiently.[7]
His alliance with Diablo finally ended once Victor Elderblood conquered Biblical Hell. Yama's agreement had been made specifically with Diablo in his capacity as the King of Biblical Hell. With Diablo no longer holding that position, Yama declared the contract invalid and immediately departed through a portal with Merlin and his Generals rather than continue fighting a war they expected to lose.[4]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Centuries later, Yama and [[Merlin]] appeared before [[The Arcane Realm]] after the Witches discovered a portal leading to an unknown world. Yama recognized the potential value of access to an unexplored civilization and personally accompanied Merlin to negotiate with Evie Moriarty.[5]
Yama was already wary of the situation because Arcane existed within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wider sphere of influence. He trusted Merlin's competence but openly stated that he did not trust the Wizard's eccentricity, particularly when unfamiliar knowledge was involved.[5]
His concerns were justified almost immediately. Merlin had written a proposed contract that granted the Witches enormous advantages and effectively obligated Yama's Demons to support Arcane in exchange for access to the portal. Yama realized that Merlin had compromised his own King's interests simply because the Wizard wanted to study the newly discovered world.[5]
Yama discarded Merlin's proposal and produced another agreement he had personally reviewed. His version offered approximately seventy percent of the resulting profits to his faction and thirty percent to Arcane. Evie rejected this as well because Yama's side would control exploration and could theoretically conceal valuable discoveries before profits were calculated.[5]
The second refusal angered Yama. He reminded Evie that Arcane's apparent political security existed largely because other factions were unwilling to risk Victor's retaliation by attacking it. Yama briefly considered simply taking the portal by force but ultimately demonstrated the same pragmatism that characterized his earlier decisions and withdrew rather than create a direct conflict with Victor.[5]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Yama's political position changed fundamentally after the creation and rapid expansion of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. By this period, the difference between [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s civilization and Yama's independent [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] had grown enormous enough that Yama no longer considered remaining separate the best path for his own future.[3]
The decisive opportunity came through [[Merlin]]. When the Elderblood faction moved against the Wizard, Merlin attempted to escape through Yama's Hell but found his Energy, Space and Runes restricted by powers far beyond his own. [[Violet Elderblood Snow]], Hilda and Nyx Elderblood participated in the operation.[3]
Yama had provided the information necessary to locate his former teacher. After Merlin's defeat, Yama personally appeared and thanked Violet for allowing him to witness the end. Violet immediately pointed out the irony of expressing gratitude after selling Merlin out.[3]
Yama did not deny what he had done. He reasoned that Nyx and the Imperial forces would eventually have found Merlin regardless, meaning that voluntarily providing useful intelligence allowed him to gain value from an outcome he considered inevitable. He additionally invoked Merlin's own philosophy that a student who became responsible for his teacher's death could be considered to have surpassed that teacher.[3]
His true objective was then revealed. Yama knelt before Violet and formally requested admission into the Elderblood Empire. He offered his abilities, knowledge, Underworld and future usefulness in exchange for access to the Empire's opportunities for development.[3]
Violet immediately recognized the maneuver as opportunistic, but Yama openly admitted it. Kneeling before another ruler injured his pride, yet he considered remaining an independent King inside a comparatively stagnant realm considerably worse. He explicitly contrasted himself with his grandfather and stated that he refused to stop developing merely to preserve his existing status.[3]
Violet accepted his oath but gave him no automatic position based upon his former kingship. Yama would enter the Empire's meritocratic structure and rise according to his own contributions. His assistance in locating Merlin would count in his favor, but his future authority would need to be earned.[3]
The Hindu Underworld was consequently marked for integration into Imperial territory. Violet stated that if Yama demonstrated competence and loyalty, he could eventually regain administration of his Hell as an Imperial subordinate supported by the Elderblood Empire rather than as an independent sovereign.[3]
Yama accepted the conditions and received an Imperial Dragon seal. After his departure, Violet explained that recruiting him had value beyond obtaining another Underworld. Yama was relatively young by Divine standards yet already capable of fighting Vlad seriously, and he possessed an unidentified power within himself that had interested even Merlin. The Wizard had investigated the phenomenon but never fully understood it beyond determining that it appeared to belong naturally to Yama himself.[3]
Violet considered Yama's selfishness manageable rather than inherently disqualifying. His decisions followed a predictable pattern: he aligned himself with the path offering the greatest survival and development. With the Elderblood Empire overwhelmingly superior to every alternative available to him, loyalty to Victor was therefore compatible with Yama's own interests.[3]

### Skills & Abilities
Yama is an exceptionally powerful Asura, God and complete Ruler of the Hindu [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]]. His true combat ability was deliberately concealed for a significant portion of the story. [[Merlin]] constructed his public image around the strength of powerful High-Level combat Gods, while Yama's Ruler authority provided a convenient explanation whenever his performance exceeded that expectation.[2] By Chapter 1097, Violet considered him capable of contending with Vlad despite Yama still possessing substantial room for further development.[3]

#### Racial and Physical Abilities
- Asura Physiology: Yama is an Asura, a powerful class of Hindu Demon, and possesses physical and supernatural characteristics vastly beyond Lesser Demons.[1]
- Superhuman Strength: Yama can directly exchange close-range attacks with Vlad and generate enormous destructive force through physical strikes and Demonic Energy.[1][2]
- Superhuman Speed and Reflexes: Yama could react to and fight Vlad throughout portions of their duel, although Vlad's experience and unusual liquid Blood form repeatedly allowed him to bypass conventional defenses.[1][2]
- Enhanced Durability: Yama can continue fighting after receiving attacks from extremely powerful supernatural opponents.[1]
- High-Speed Regeneration: Vlad drained Yama's life and damaged his body severely enough to initially believe he had killed him. Yama reconstructed himself almost immediately afterward.[1]
- Longevity/Immortality: As a Demon, God and [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] Ruler, Yama possesses a lifespan far beyond ordinary mortal limitations.
- Additional Limb Manifestation: Yama can manifest a second pair of arms, giving himself four arms in combat.[1]
- Claw Transformation: He can concentrate Miasma around his hands and transform them into large supernatural claws.[1]
- God Physiology: Yama is recognized as a Divine existence in addition to being an Asura and Ruler. His Divine development was still considered comparatively young by the standards surrounding the [[Elderblood Empire]].[3]
- [[Divine Energy]]: Yama possesses and manipulates supernatural Energy on the level expected of major Divine combatants.
- Divine Longevity: His existence is not constrained by normal biological aging.
- Exceptional Potential: Violet considered Yama's current strength notable specifically because he had achieved it while still relatively young by Divine standards.[3]

#### Demonic Energy
- Miasma Manipulation: Yama can generate and manipulate the infernal Miasma associated with the Hindu [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]]. He surrounds his body and limbs with dense Miasma during combat and incorporates it into multiple offensive techniques.[1]
- Hellfire Manipulation: Yama can produce enormous quantities of Hellfire. During his fight with Vlad, his flames tore upward through the bloody battlefield and dispersed the surrounding atmosphere and clouds.[2]
- Energy Weapon Creation: Yama can form weapons through his supernatural power. During the battle against Vlad, he generated and threw a large spear capable of destroying one of Vlad's enormous Blood constructs.[1]
- Energy Suppression: Through training beneath [[Merlin]], Yama became capable of hiding significant portions of his true power and maintaining a deliberately false public assessment of his strength.[2]
- Yama maintained this suppression even while defending against the Heavenly Father's Judgment because exposing more power risked revealing his deception to [[Diablo]].[7]

#### Ruler Authority
- Ruler Physiology: Yama possesses the metaphysical characteristics of a complete [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] Ruler rather than merely being a monarch who politically controls territory.[1]
- Ruler's Bond: Yama possesses an exceptionally powerful connection to the Hindu Underworld and the beings within it. Vlad immediately recognized the strength of this bond and concluded that Yama had taken complete rulership of the realm.[1]
- Underworld Authority: Yama can exercise authority over Naraka's supernatural infrastructure, Asura population and functions associated with its administration of the dead. Separating his Hell from the wider [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]] disrupted the normal movement and administration of Hindu Souls.[6]
- Soul Manipulation Resistance: A Ruler's Soul is exceptionally difficult to damage or erase because Rulers themselves govern and interact directly with Souls. Vlad explained that ordinary Soul attacks and conventional Death-God abilities would have greatly reduced effectiveness against a complete Ruler such as Yama.[1]
- Limitations: This protection is not absolute. Higher authorities over Souls, including entities such as [[The Universal Tree]] and the Judges of the Abyss, can exceed the natural spiritual defenses provided by Ruler status.[1]
- Subordinate Bond: Yama's Ruler connection contributes to the extraordinary loyalty displayed by the Asura Generals directly bound to his rule.[1]
- Asura Deployment: The Hindu Underworld can deploy lower Asuras without relying upon the sacrificial summoning process required by [[Biblical Hell]]. This gives Yama's realm a significant logistical advantage when mobilizing large numbers of troops.[7]

#### Defensive Abilities
- Barrier Manipulation: Yama can generate extremely durable Demonic barriers. During the [[Heavenly Father]]'s widespread Judgment, his barrier survived the attack while becoming extensively cracked.[7]
- [[Merlin]] reinforced the barrier, and Yama admitted that without Merlin's assistance it would have broken. Yama was simultaneously suppressing a considerable portion of his own real power, meaning the scene does not establish the absolute limit of his defensive abilities.[7]

#### Yama's Judgment
Yama's Judgment is Yama's principal revealed trump card and one of the abilities [[Merlin]] specifically intended to keep hidden from [[Diablo]].[2]
Yama releases a massive quantity of Energy into the sky and tears open an enormous rift within a red-colored region of Space. Gigantic arms then emerge from the unknown location beyond the rupture, gripping and expanding the edges of the opening.[2]
At least four hands initially manifested during his duel against Vlad, with additional limbs appearing as the technique progressed. When Yama finally cancelled the technique, six enormous hands were described withdrawing through the rupture.[2]
- Spatial Rift Creation: Yama opens a large breach connected to an unidentified location, realm or dimension.[2]
- Giant Limb Manifestation: Multiple gigantic arms can emerge simultaneously through the rift and physically attack targets.[2]
- Soul Erasure: Contact between one of the hands and Vlad's living Blood caused the Souls stored within the affected section to disappear entirely rather than merely becoming damaged.[2]
- Massive Physical Force: Vlad's instincts considered the hands sufficiently dangerous that he attacked the developing technique rather than allow Yama to finish preparing it.[2]
- Preparation Requirement: Yama's Judgment requires time to fully develop. Merlin explicitly criticized Yama for beginning to expose the technique during what was supposed to be a controlled operation, while its activation period created an opportunity for interruption.[2]

#### Unknown Innate Power
- Unidentified Internal Power: Yama possesses an additional power or phenomenon whose nature has not yet been revealed. By Chapter 1097, Violet stated that [[Merlin]] had studied it without completely understanding what it was.[3]
- The phenomenon appeared to be an expression of Yama's own existence rather than an external blessing or borrowed ability.[3]
- Violet stated that this power manifested during Yama's confrontation with Vlad.[3]
- Its source, mechanics, limits and relationship—if any—to Yama's God, Asura or Ruler nature remain unknown. It should therefore not currently be assigned a speculative Divinity or power classification.

### Skills
- Leadership: Yama successfully established complete authority over the Hindu [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] and inspired unusually strong loyalty from his Generals. Vlad explicitly found this notable for a Demonic ruler.[1]
- Strategic Thinking: Yama participated in a long-term deception intended to hide his real power from [[Diablo]], used the confrontation with Vlad to gather information and repeatedly withdrew from situations when continued escalation offered no strategic benefit.[2][4]
- Political Pragmatism: Yama is willing to abandon alliances, status and personal pride whenever doing so provides a superior long-term position. His voluntary entry into the [[Elderblood Empire]] is the clearest example.[3]
- Contract Knowledge: Yama pays close attention to the exact wording and conditions of supernatural agreements. He exploited the change in ownership of [[Biblical Hell]] to declare his pact with Diablo invalid.[4]
- Negotiation: Yama personally negotiated with Evie over access to Arcane's newly discovered world and recognized the political consequences of attempting to seize the portal by force.[5]
- Combat Experience: Yama possesses extensive experience as both a supernatural ruler and combatant, although [[Merlin]] repeatedly criticized his tendency to become overexcited when facing exceptionally strong opponents.[2]
- Power Concealment: Yama and Merlin successfully maintained a deliberately inaccurate public image of his combat level for an extended period, including while operating directly beside Diablo's faction.[2][7]

### Weaknesses
- Battle Excitement: Yama can allow his desire to test powerful opponents to override strategic objectives. His fight against Vlad escalated far beyond what [[Merlin]] intended because Yama continued revealing additional power.[2]
- Pride: Insults toward his intelligence, authority or worldview can provoke Yama into unnecessarily aggressive behavior.[2]
- Yama's Judgment Preparation: His greatest revealed technique requires time to reach its full state and can potentially be interrupted before completion.[2]
- Limited Knowledge of Dragons: During his confrontation with Vlad, Yama attempted to directly withstand Ancient Dragon Breath without understanding its abnormal ability to create damage difficult to regenerate. Merlin was forced to intervene before the attack reached him.[2]
- Pragmatic Self-Interest: Although this frequently benefits him, Yama's willingness to change sides when circumstances shift makes his loyalty heavily dependent upon his assessment of long-term advantage. Violet considered this predictable enough to work within the Empire because betraying [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] would provide Yama no rational benefit.[3]

### Relationships

#### Old Yama
The previous Yama was the current ruler's grandfather. Their exact personal relationship remains largely unexplored, but the current Yama clearly views his predecessor as an example of stagnation. When requesting entry into the [[Elderblood Empire]], he stated that he refused to remain static and decay as the Old Yama had.[3]
The circumstances surrounding the Old Yama's death remain intentionally obscured. Chapter 669 strongly implies violent succession, while [[Merlin]] later instructed Yama to attribute his grandfather's death publicly to [[Diablo]]'s forces.[1][2]

#### Merlin
[[Merlin]] served simultaneously as Yama's General, advisor and teacher. He trained Yama in concealing his real strength and possessed enough influence to reprimand the King for recklessness during the battle against Vlad.[2]
Their relationship was functional but unstable. Yama respected Merlin's extraordinary competence while distrusting his eccentricity, particularly his obsession with acquiring knowledge. This distrust was vindicated when Merlin created an Arcane contract overwhelmingly favorable to the Witches simply because he wanted access to another world.[5]
Yama ultimately betrayed Merlin by providing the Elderblood faction with information concerning his location. He still possessed some attachment to his teacher, but judged his own development and future within the Empire more important.[3]

#### Vlad Dracula Tepes
Yama and Vlad confronted one another as opposing supernatural Kings during the Demonic War. Yama initially used captured Vampires to draw Vlad into a battle intended to test whether the Progenitor remained healthy after attempts to weaken him.[1][2]
The fight evolved far beyond its original purpose because Yama became increasingly enthusiastic. Vlad repeatedly surprised him with the unusual properties of Progenitor Blood and his ancient True Form, while Yama revealed abilities he had previously concealed from [[Diablo]].[1][2]
Neither achieved a decisive victory before [[Merlin]] ended the confrontation. Centuries later, Violet regarded Yama as capable of contending with Vlad, making the duel an important early demonstration of the younger ruler's abnormal potential.[3]

#### Diablo
Yama was never truly [[Diablo]]'s subordinate. Their relationship was contractual cooperation between two rulers whose objectives temporarily aligned.[4]
Yama and [[Merlin]] actively concealed his full power from Diablo because they did not want the Biblical Demon King to understand the real strength of another potential rival.[2]
Once [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became the legitimate King of [[Biblical Hell]], Diablo ceased satisfying the identity specified in Yama's agreement. Yama immediately declared the pact void and abandoned him on the battlefield.[4]

#### Violet Elderblood Snow
Violet was the Imperial authority before whom Yama formally submitted himself. Yama voluntarily knelt, requested admission into the [[Elderblood Empire]] and acknowledged that he was prepared to surrender his previous status in order to continue developing.[3]
Violet accepted him but refused to automatically restore his royal authority. Yama would begin within the Imperial meritocratic system and prove his usefulness like other subjects. She nevertheless considered his talent worth cultivating and suggested that successful service could eventually result in Yama governing his former [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] again beneath Imperial authority.[3]

### Power
Yama's precise maximum combat level remains deliberately undefined. During the Demonic War, [[Merlin]] concealed his true strength by presenting him publicly as approximately comparable to powerful High-Level combat Gods such as [[Ares]] or [[Thor]], with any excess performance being explained through his additional Ruler authority.[2]
This public estimate was intentionally false. Yama repeatedly suppressed himself, including against the [[Heavenly Father]]'s Judgment, specifically to prevent [[Diablo]] from realizing how powerful he actually was.[7]
The later and more reliable assessment comes from Violet in Chapter 1097, when she considered Yama capable of contending with Vlad despite being relatively young and still developing. This does not establish Yama as a God-King, Primordial God or Second Generation Primordial God; no checked chapter assigns him those classifications.[3]
His unexplained innate power represents an additional unknown variable and is one of the reasons Violet considered him valuable enough to recruit rather than discard.[3]
