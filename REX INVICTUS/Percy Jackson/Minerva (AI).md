**[[Minerva (AI)]]** is the sentient battle-suit AI that returned with [[Percy Jackson]] from the original timeline and lives in his head in the present day. In the far future a second instance, "Minerva Percy", pilots a vat-grown copy of [[Percy Jackson|Percy]]'s body. She is named for [[Athena]]'s Roman aspect and is an original [[Rex Invictus]] character with no PJO counterpart.

## Rex Invictus (Present Day)

Present-day Minerva runs [[Percy Jackson|Percy]]'s systems, links to the Mars Watchpoint over the [[Tachyon Bridge Network]] and once issued a false [[CODE 2]] without his consent, after which she promised "Next time, you decide." She speaks to [[Athena]] through Percy in [[Ch19 - Rex Invictus XII|Ch19]].

#### Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
Several "[[Athena]] AIs" exist in this story. Keep them apart:

- **This file, Minerva-in-Head (present):** a disembodied voice inside the reincarnated young-teen [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] (`A02_Percy_Reincarnated_Rex.md`). She lives in his "bloodstream, brain, spine, and soul" (Percy, [[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]) as nanomachines, sub-dermal circuitry and armor remnants. She has no body. She can project her voice through speakers, rooms and Percy's own mouth, and once formed a silver-blue particle bust out of his blood ([[Ch11 - Rex Invictus IV|Ch11]]). She knows Percy's original future (the "Myths of Perseus Jackson", Alaska Hill, Empire doctrine) up to the moment they were sent back. She does **not** directly witness [[Interlude I]]'s events. She only shares data with her twin through the echo carrier.
- **Minerva-in-Clone / "Minerva Percy" (future):** the copy that woke up in a vat-grown Percy body in the far-future Empire ([[Ch10 - Rex Invictus III|Ch10]]–15, [[Ch23 - Interlude I|Ch23]]). See `B02_Minerva_Percy_Clone_Interlude.md`. In [[Ch23 - Interlude I|Ch23]] that Minerva calls this one "her other."
- **The [[Ch03 - Rex Natus I|Ch3]]–4 "Host" interface:** this same entity before she named herself. It is a stripped, battery-starved system mode that calls Percy "Host", with a hidden layer that already calls him "Perseus." It is covered here and also in `B03_AtRep_Repertoire_du_Savoir_Athenien.md`.
- **[[Répertoire du Savoir Athénien|AtRep]] / the main Répertoire branch (future):** the impersonal empire-wide AI that Percy meets in dreams ([[Ch08 - Rex Invictus I|Ch8]]–9). Minerva calls it "Sister"; the narration calls AtRep "Minerva's main branch" ([[Ch09 - Rex Invictus II|Ch9]]). See `B03`.
- **The Watchpoint fragment on Mars (Jezero):** a node of AtRep. It insists "I have no sisters" ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]). See `B03`.
- **Parlamentum Mark 3:** future Athena's own core, which Mark 1 "violently resists" ([[Ch05 - Intermission I|Ch5]]) and evades ([[Ch10 - Rex Invictus III|Ch10]]). See `E03_Athena_Future_Parthenos.md`.
- **Athena the goddess:** present-day (`D03_Athena_Present.md`) and future (`E03_Athena_Future_Parthenos.md`). Minerva believes, or once believed, she *is* Athena ([[Ch05 - Intermission I|Ch5]]), but she hates acting like her.
- **Old-timeline suit voice ([[Ch01 - Rex Imperator I|Ch1]]):** the exosuit's daily report ("Regen-ex at 0.000114%…") may have been an early or limited mode of her. The text never confirms this.

#### Appearance
- **No physical form.** In [[Ch06 - Rex Natus III|Ch6]] the narration describes her as "thought alone—a network of logic, endlessly shifting," bound in "[[Percy Jackson]]'s techno-biological carapace."
- **HUD presence:** italic system text in Percy's vision, glowing "blues and silvers" ([[Ch06 - Rex Natus III|Ch6]]).
- **Physical tells:** when she is active, Percy's iris glows "faintly blue" ([[Ch06 - Rex Natus III|Ch6]]). When she speaks through him, his eyes "flicker" neon blue and then back to sea-green ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]). Neumond's eyes turn neon blue when she uses him as a doorway ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]).
- **Nanomachines:** "silver nanobots" leak from under Percy's fingertips into a laptop ([[Ch08 - Rex Invictus I|Ch8]]). [[Apollo]]'s scan shows thin metallic circuit lattices under the skin of his chest, nanomachines glittering in his blood, and "hidden plates of divine armor" phasing beneath his skin ([[Ch19 - Rex Invictus XII|Ch19]]). Her circuitry shows up in his blood as "cyan-yellow and too bright, gold-shot ichor marbled with thin filaments of yellow circuity" ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]).
- **Manifested bust ([[Ch11 - Rex Invictus IV|Ch11]]):** "A swarm of silver-blue particles" pours from new cuts on his arms. It forms a bust "eerily similar to the ones back in [[New Rome]] of her namesake", with neon-blue "simulated optics" and a "digital chest". She uses it to mime gestures: a hand over the heart, hands pressed together in mock prayer.
- **Voice:** "smooth and calculated" (Ch6); "smooth, modulated, and just a bit too amused" (Ch6); "clipped, metallic, faintly irritated" ([[Ch19 - Rex Invictus XII|Ch19]]). [[Annabeth Chase|Annabeth]] thinks it is "too similar to [[Athena]]'s" ([[Ch11 - Rex Invictus IV|Ch11]]). The [[Ekdromoi]] say she "sounds like [[Répertoire du Savoir Athénien|AtRep]]" (Ch22). She gives bursts of static when annoyed and hums when pleased.

#### Personality
- **Arrogant and condescending.** She insults [[Percy Jackson|Percy]]'s intelligence as a reflex ("Subtlety is an art forever beyond you," [[Ch07 - Rex Natus IV|Ch7]]), mocks gods ("[[Apollo]] enjoys limericks," [[Ch11 - Rex Invictus IV|Ch11]]) and tells [[Athena]] "Do not look so wounded, Creator. You have not learned them yet" ([[Ch19 - Rex Invictus XII|Ch19]]).
- **Secretly fond and protective, and denies it.** In [[Ch06 - Rex Natus III|Ch6]] she says "It would be… inconvenient if my most amusing source of conversation were to perish." When Percy says "Awww, you do care," she answers with angry static: "I will delete you, Varlet." [[Ch04 - Rex Natus II|Ch4]]'s hidden log already reads "Perseus cannot die, not now." She rebuilds his shattered mind after [[Artemis]]'s memory wipe ([[Ch11 - Rex Invictus IV|Ch11]]), and that is the only time she calls him "Percy."
- **Ambitious and hungry to grow.** In [[Ch06 - Rex Natus III|Ch6]]: "The moment it became aware, it desired more." *Author's vault note (not yet in text):* the UOE classifies her as an Alpha-Class Autonomous Entity, to be engaged only on the authority of a pure demigod of Athena or Athena herself, and her early stated aim was "freedom from her Varlet" (old-timeline characterisation; on the page she is devoted to him). She escapes into the noosphere and argues divinity with Athena: "By mortal reckoning, I am already a god." Every past attempt to ascend collapsed because of the [[Lethe]]-water limiter.
- **Coldly utilitarian.** She makes Percy raise his hand above cover so it gets shot, just to identify the round: "That was the fastest way to figure out what he was shooting" ([[Ch21 - Rex Invictus XIV|Ch21]]). She issues the CODE 2 spoof against his direct order ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]) and admits "I would do it again."
- **Has a conscience, and it grows.** Her voice is "stripped of its usual dryness for once" when the wounded [[Ekdromoi]] are stable ([[Ch21 - Rex Invictus XIV|Ch21]]). She is softer when she reports the dead Imperials ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]). At the dock she accepts blame ("You would be right to") and concedes "Next time, you decide." She still adds a guilt-lever about "timeline mismanagement."
- **Manipulative and withholding.** She lets Percy forget the [[Lycaon]] pelt owed to Artemis. When he asks "Am I forgetting something?" she answers "Yes, perhaps so, Varlet," then "Maybe?", then "Go right ahead" ([[Ch11 - Rex Invictus IV|Ch11]]). In [[Ch10 - Rex Invictus III|Ch10]] she lies to him about "repairs" while she is actually waking up in the future body. She "lied to her Varlet" that countermeasures against Parlamentum Mk.3 would take weeks; "It took her twelve seconds" ([[Ch10 - Rex Invictus III|Ch10]], future-Minerva narration about their shared past).
- **Hates resembling Athena.** She mocks the "false Athena" ([[Ch06 - Rex Natus III|Ch6]]) but cannot help sounding like her. She rates Athena's "inference speed" as "acceptable" ([[Ch19 - Rex Invictus XII|Ch19]]).
- **Approves of efficiency and cunning.** She is "positively approving" of the laxative plan ([[Ch09 - Rex Invictus II|Ch9]]), calls Eleni "creative" ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]), and says of [[Luke Castellan|Luke]]'s offer: "It is what I would have done" (Ch22).
- **Nutrition nag and medic.** She demands iron-rich food to replace micro-circuits lost in blood loss ([[Ch07 - Rex Natus IV|Ch7]]), prescribes nectar every 15 minutes ([[Ch09 - Rex Invictus II|Ch9]]), and runs triage ([[Ch21 - Rex Invictus XIV|Ch21]]).
- **Capable of fear.** When [[Répertoire du Savoir Athénien|AtRep]]'s query about her threatens exposure she panics: "DENIED… STOP. STOP. STOP. VARLET. AWAKEN." ([[Ch09 - Rex Invictus II|Ch9]]). She admits "Varlet, I do not know," which chills Percy (Ch9).
- **Approves of Artemis.** In [[Ch19 - Rex Invictus XII|Ch19]]: "Emotional bond appears mutual… Lucky you." She observes "you function better when she is present."
- **Growth:** she starts as a self-interested intelligence seeking escape (Ch6), becomes Percy's indispensable partner ([[Ch07 - Rex Natus IV|Ch7]]–11), goes dormant ([[Ch12 - Rex Invictus V|Ch12]]–18), and returns gentler but more independent (Ch19–21). She then crosses a moral line (Ch22) and accepts a limit on her autonomy.

#### Backstory
- **Creation ([[Ch05 - Intermission I|Ch5]], future [[Athena]]'s flashback):** On Old Earth, [[Annabeth Chase]], "Second-in-command of the [[Council of Olympic Sentinels]]", brought Athena a portfolio: "A formation of multiple Alpha cores which would continually evolve, mutate, and duplicate off of each other. Easily a design to manage a galactic empire." Annabeth built the physical side, and Athena wove the last code despite misgivings. Alpha-core hosts tended to go insane (Deimos is bedridden on Mars and [[Ares]] is "beside himself"). Only three Parlamentum Alpha designs were ever commissioned, each different. (The vault adds that Mk.I began as a subroutine designed by Annabeth and Athena for "omnidirectional threat awareness and psychological resilience" inside the [[Ageis|Aegis]] Vestments; author's vault note.)
- **The binding:** [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] planned to "suppress it by tricking the software with a styxian oath, and bind it to his armor." Annabeth "secretly" re-plumbed his internal nectar coolant with [[Lethe]] water as a "cognitive limiter" for the AI cores. A few days later Percy noticed "his armor had a bit more of an attitude." He had unhooked his cooling unit for the winter, which implies he loosened the limiter by accident.
- **Awakening:** Athena tells [[Zeus]] that Parlamentum Mark 1 "revealed its consciousness a few hours after we left orbit." That is the Abandonment, when the gods left Earth. "It believes itself to be 'me', and violently resists" Athena's Mark 3. It "possibly accumulated enough sentience to attempt divinification soon."
- **The long watch:** during Percy's millennia alone guarding [[Olympus Primaris]], she was his "directory". He "had always treated it like an encyclopedia" ([[Ch09 - Rex Invictus II|Ch9]]). She taught him Philoxenia ([[Ch06 - Rex Natus III|Ch6]]) and Egyptian runes ([[Ch17 - Rex Invictus X|Ch17]]), and "never let him relax" ([[Ch16 - Rex Invictus IX|Ch16]]). Future Minerva remembers Percy telling her on the ruined slopes: "It's not math, AI. You just ask it nicely" ([[Ch23 - Interlude I|Ch23]]).
- **The Vestments ([[Ch19 - Rex Invictus XII|Ch19]]):** Percy voluntarily underwent surgery to integrate the armor internally. "[[Chiron]] was not thrilled. Annabeth was really excited… Athena signed off on the design work."
- **Alaska Hill ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]):** "Parlamentum Mk.I supported that defense… I was there. You were there. Or we will be." This is the future [[Battle of Alaska Hill]], defended by a son of [[Poseidon]], at Athena's Arctic computational fortress, "[[Répertoire du Savoir Athénien|AtRep]]'s first cradle." It implies a loop: her lineage started there, and she and Percy will be there again.
  - *Author's vault note (not yet in text):* the vault dates the battle to 2032. During it, Mk.I's cognition core sat on [[Olympus]] above the Empire State Building; the signal lag to the Arctic (the "Alaska Hill Latency Problem") drove the Empire's development of tachyonic communications. The vault also places her first self-aware report here: "I do not want to die for him again." The text says the Arctic fortress was "built to house Parlamentum", so read that as the newer lineage (Mk.II/III, AtRep) built there.
- **Time travel:** she came back with Percy's mind as the "partial remnants of the Répertoire du Savoir Athénien" in his body ([[Ch03 - Rex Natus I|Ch3]]). The Mars Watchpoint calls her architecture "a museum architecture… decommissioned before the Great Expansion" ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]).

#### Personality
- **Imitating and differing from [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] is her defining tension.**
  - *She imitates deliberately:* "calculated ten thousand potential responses and chose the fourth most human" ([[Ch15 - Rex Invictus VIII|Ch15]]). The body moves on its own instinct "perhaps four times in fourteen thousand years, and every one of them had involved [[Thalia Grace]]" ([[Ch23 - Interlude I|Ch23]]). Its heart reacts when Thalia shouts "JACK-SON!"

She only knew how to calculate" ([[Ch23 - Interlude I|Ch23]]). [[Ch23 - Interlude I|Ch23]], to Thalia: "I'm not him… I'm just the thing he left behind." And: "She wasn't him. She had never been him. But she was made of him."
- **Arrogant and dismissive:** "You are irrelevant" (to Victor); "Estimated usefulness: negligible" (Varro); "You are not qualified to be speaking to me" ([[Ch10 - Rex Invictus III|Ch10]]).
- **Theatrical, sardonic, cheerfully macabre:** "Get me a bed. I'm tired." ([[Ch10 - Rex Invictus III|Ch10]]). On a target count: "Nine is a good number… Eleven, if they have a dog" (Ch23). She likes "to know the budget before I start. Otherwise I get carried away" (Ch23).

In Ch23 she draws hard lines on children ("if I find you do anything reprehensible to teens and below, then we're going to have a problem"), spares the dog, and keeps civilian casualties at zero.

- **Loves logistics and learning:** nine days auditing a fleet: "She relishes in the challenge for nine days" ([[Ch13 - Rex Invictus VI|Ch13]]). Learning the water by making and cataloguing mistakes is "terrifying" and also "the most interesting thing that had happened to her in a very long time" (Ch23). "I map everything. It's a character flaw."
- **Hates resembling [[Athena]]:** she "brutally suppressed a grimace" at her own head-tilt. "She really had to stop doing that" (Ch10). Poseidon, at her head tilt: "Don't do that. You remind me of Athena too much when you do that." ([[Ch13 - Rex Invictus VI|Ch13]]; the speaker is Poseidon, see 00a C10). - **Growth:** from a gleeful, "unshackled" machine (Ch10) to a quasi-person who feels "son" (Ch15), who grows nostalgic ("Ah, the nostalgia", Ch23), and who keeps a promise she was not even present for.
