**[[Percy Jackson]]** (Perseus Jackson) is a [[Demigod]] son of [[Poseidon]] and [[Sally Jackson]], hero of [[Percy Jackson and the Olympians]] and protagonist of [[Rex Invictus]]. He is named after the hero [[Perseus (Hero)]], wields [[Riptide]] ([[Riptide|Anaklusmos]]) and bears the [[Curse of Achilles]].

In [[Rex Invictus]] he exists in several versions:

- **Lord-Commander of the [[Council of Olympic Sentinels]]**, the millennia-old last guardian of ruined [[Olympus Primaris]] in the original timeline.
- **"Rex" Percy**, that same mind sent back by [[Chaos]] into his young body in the present day, carrying the AI [[Minerva (AI)|Minerva]].
- **The canon self** his return overwrote.
- **The Dreamwalker**, who pilots a vat clone in the far future through dreams.
- **The Legend**, the [[Undying Olympian Empire]]'s venerated but absent **Lord Imperator**, with a metaphysical shard called [[Him within the moon]].

## Greek History (IRL)

**Namesake.** Perseus, son of [[Zeus]] and Danaë, was cast to sea in a chest by his grandfather Acrisius. With gifts from [[Athena]] and [[Hermes]] he beheaded the Gorgon [[Medusa]], rescued Andromeda from the sea monster Cetus and founded Mycenae. He is one of the few Greek heroes with a happy ending; [[Sally Jackson|Sally]] chose the name for that reason. See [[Perseus (Hero)]].

## PJO Canon

**[[The Lightning Thief]].** Twelve-year-old Percy, dyslexic and ADHD, is attacked by Mrs. Dodds (a [[Fury]]) at Yancy Academy. He reaches [[Camp Half-Blood]] after killing the Minotaur, is claimed by [[Poseidon]] and goes west with [[Annabeth Chase]] and [[Grover Underwood]] to recover [[Zeus]]'s [[Master Bolt]]. He defeats [[Ares]], visits the [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] and exposes [[Luke Castellan]] as the thief working for [[Kronos]].

**[[The Sea of Monsters]].** He meets his Cyclops half-brother Tyson, sails the [[Sea of Monsters]], escapes [[Circe]] and Polyphemus, and recovers the [[Golden Fleece]], which revives [[Thalia Grace]].

**[[The Titan's Curse]].** He rescues the di Angelo siblings, joins the quest to save [[Artemis]], holds up the sky for her and is present when [[Zoe Nightshade]] and (in canon) [[Bianca di Angelo]] die.

**[[The Battle of the Labyrinth]].** He explores [[Daedalus]]' [[Labyrinth]], spends time on [[Ogygia]] with [[Calypso]], and helps defend camp.

**[[The Last Olympian]].** At sixteen he bathes in the [[Styx]] to gain the [[Curse of Achilles]], leads the defense of Manhattan against [[Kronos]] and gives [[Luke Castellan|Luke]] the knife that ends the war. He refuses godhood and asks the gods to claim all their children. He and [[Annabeth Chase|Annabeth]] start dating.

**[[The Heroes of Olympus]].** [[Hera]] wipes his memory and sends him to [[Camp Jupiter]] ([[The Son of Neptune]]), where he becomes praetor. He is one of the Seven of the Prophecy, falls into [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] with Annabeth ([[The House of Hades]]) and helps defeat [[Gaia]].

**Later.** In [[The Trials of Apollo]] he helps [[Apollo]] early on. In [[The Chalice of the Gods]] he completes three quests for college recommendation letters.

**Powers.** [[Hydroknesis]], breathing underwater, healing in water, talking to horses and sea creatures, earthquakes and storms (rarely), and swordsmanship with [[Riptide]].

## Rex Invictus (Present Day)

The Rex Percy is a millennia-old mind in a teenage body. His age is a canon ruling issue: see [[00a_CANON_RULINGS]] R1 and project rules. He returns via [[Chaos]]'s gamble ([[Ch03 - Rex Natus I|Ch3]]), carries [[Minerva (AI)|Minerva]], kills the Minotaur again ([[Ch07 - Rex Natus IV|Ch7]]), changes the Titan's Curse quest, gives [[Artemis]] fragments of his [[Curse of Achilles]] ([[Ch18 - Rex Invictus XI|Ch18]]), and leads the defense of Long Island against Imperial forces ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]). He agrees the [[Anabasis Accord]] with the Imperials on Mars. As the Dreamwalker he once possessed a vat clone in the far future ([[Ch10 - Rex Invictus III|Ch10]] to [[Ch11 - Rex Invictus IV|Ch11]]).

#### Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
This is **the protagonist**: the "Rex" Percy of the present-day timeline. He has the original Lord-Commander's memories, soul and personality in a young, underpowered body. He is not:
- **[[Heavenly Father|The Lord]]-Commander of the old timeline** (`A01_Percy_LordCommander_OldTimeline.md`), the rusting sentinel of [[Ch01 - Rex Imperator I|Ch1]] who stood alone at the gate of [[Olympus Primaris]]. That man's consciousness *became* this one when [[Chaos]] sent it back. Everything A01 remembers, A02 remembers. A02 lives in a world where none of it has happened yet.
- **Canon / original-body Percy** (`A03_Percy_Canon_OriginalSelf.md`), the ordinary troubled Yancy kid whose life this mind overwrote. A02 only "faintly" remembers being him and imitates him badly.
- **Percy-the-Dreamwalker / "Psychic"** (`A04_Percy_Dreamwalker_Psychic.md`). This is the same mind, but only while asleep and slipping into future Imperial soldier bodies and the vat clone. A02 is the waking, embodied version.
- **[[Him within the moon|Him Within the Moon]] / the [[Riptide]] fragment** (`A05_Him_Within_the_Moon.md`). This is a shard of future Percy, formed from [[Artemis]]'s energy and Anaklusmos, that calls A02 "My Original" ([[Ch13 - Rex Invictus VI|Ch13]]). After [[Ch14 - Rex Invictus VII|Ch14]] it seems to live inside Riptide, where it speaks as "a sliver cast from the Jackson" ([[Ch16 - Rex Invictus IX|Ch16]]).
- **The Empire's legend** (`A06_Percy_The_Legend_Lord_Imperator.md`): murals, the "Myths of Perseus Jackson", cult hymns, the Imperial key and CODE 15. Imperial soldiers have been "told since childhood that you are a god" ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]). A02 is the real, flawed boy behind that title. He insists "I'm not him yet."
- **The clone body [[Minerva (AI)|Minerva]] pilots in the future** (`B02_Minerva_Percy_Clone_Interlude.md`). It wears his face, but the operator is an AI.

**What A02 knows:** the whole canon PJO arc as he originally lived it (to the extent memory survives), plus millennia of future history: the Great Abandonment, the Great Expansion, foreign pantheons, demon hierarchies, Imperial technology, and Roman law. **What he does not know:** many specifics of the Empire's later era (he did not recognise Eleni's cult lore or the [[Caligo Vale]] marriage, [[Ch15 - Rex Invictus VIII|Ch15]]), what Artemis erased from his memory in [[Ch11 - Rex Invictus IV|Ch11]], what Riptide has become, and the ways "this reality" already differs from his own ([[Ch14 - Rex Invictus VII|Ch14]]: "Something about this reality had changed slightly").

#### Personality
**Core: a veteran soldier in a boy's body.** He thinks like a commander and a survivor. He scouts the monster camp from above before striking ([[Ch03 - Rex Natus I|Ch3]]), wears the Minotaur down methodically ([[Ch07 - Rex Natus IV|Ch7]]), and instantly reads the Othrys monsters' feints as stalling for [[Atlas]] ([[Ch19 - Rex Invictus XII|Ch19]]). His body keeps failing his instincts. In [[Ch07 - Rex Natus IV|Ch7]] his hydrokinesis does not answer: "body and soul were still misaligned."

**Socially rusty, and bad at acting normal.** "Millenia of standing guard and zero social interaction had deteriorated his social skills into nothingness" ([[Ch03 - Rex Natus I|Ch3]]). He tries to mimic other kids, fakes headaches and ignorance ("I thought they were just Greek myths", [[Ch06 - Rex Natus III|Ch6]]), and is seen through by [[Zoe Nightshade|Zoë]], [[Artemis]], [[Dionysus]], [[Athena]], [[Apollo]] and [[Annabeth Chase|Annabeth]]. The Athena cabin knows "you shouldn't be this smart" ([[Ch13 - Rex Invictus VI|Ch13]]).

**Cold and formal when it counts, flippant otherwise.** With [[Sally Jackson|Sally]] in [[Ch03 - Rex Natus I|Ch3]] he is stiff and archaic, and finds her questions "merely a waste of time in his eyes", though he privately thinks "Hoo boy, this is going to be really hard". With gods and officials he can be precise and legalistic, as in the Roman speech of [[Ch17 - Rex Invictus X|Ch17]]. Most of the time he is a sarcastic, deadpan teenager: "Yeah, that's kind of my thing" ([[Ch06 - Rex Natus III|Ch6]]), "I'm so cooked" ([[Ch15 - Rex Invictus VIII|Ch15]]), "Nanomachines, Son" ([[Ch19 - Rex Invictus XII|Ch19]]).

**Cunning and willing to deceive, but with a code.**
- He lies to [[Chiron]] about a lost pencil case to get [[Riptide]] (Ch3).
- He lies to [[Asmodeus]] about the Evergreen family wanting the Eye ([[Ch04 - Rex Natus II|Ch4]]).
- He bluffs and blackmails [[Ares]] with his foreign aspects ([[Ch10 - Rex Invictus III|Ch10]]).
- He gives cover stories on the bus ([[Ch11 - Rex Invictus IV|Ch11]]).
- He tells Artemis the biovirus is safe when [[Minerva (AI)|Minerva]] said "It might" ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]).
- He also says "I usually don't lie, you know" ([[Ch17 - Rex Invictus X|Ch17]]).
- His one hard line is refusing to deceive enemies *in their own voice*: "We don't lie to them in their own voice" ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]).

**Pragmatic to the point of cruelty, early on.** In Ch3 he burns a living dracaena and a whimpering, crippled hellhound alive as offerings to the [[Labyrinth]], with a formal dedication for each. In [[Ch04 - Rex Natus II|Ch4]] he has a demon slaughter [[Lycaon]]'s whole pack, including "the young", so he can harvest the king's heart. Then he cuts out his own heart with an ice mouthguard in his teeth.

**Reckless self-sacrifice.** He climbs Othrys alone and takes the sky from Artemis against her orders ([[Ch19 - Rex Invictus XII|Ch19]]). He pours his blood, divine core and [[Achilles]] curse into a shrine fire for her: "He did not regret it… Good." ([[Ch18 - Rex Invictus XI|Ch18]]). He spends power his body cannot hold; Artemis tells him "You spend power your body is unsuitable for" ([[Ch16 - Rex Invictus IX|Ch16]]).

**Increasingly merciful, by Ch19–22, which complicates the "degradation" arc.**
- He keeps all five [[Ekdromoi]] alive after they nearly killed him ([[Ch21 - Rex Invictus XIV|Ch21]]).
- He argues for them to Artemis: "They're lost… doing their job" ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]).
- He stops Apollo from killing the bound prisoners (Ch22).
- When landers sink he "command[s] the waters to crush their heads to grant them a quick death" (Ch22), which is a mercy killing.
- He confesses Minerva's false CODE 2 to Castellan Oriel and apologises (Ch22).
- His first order as Lord Imperator is "casualty recovery, all parties, no distinction" (Ch22).

**Embarrassed by worship.** "No more titles!" ([[Ch15 - Rex Invictus VIII|Ch15]]). "Don't call me a despot." "Please," … "Get up. I'm not... I'm not him yet." (Ch22).

**Playful provocateur.** After the tent scene he deliberately torments Artemis with a florid, mock-courtly voice in front of [[Hunters of Artemis|the Hunt]] and the camp (Ch19–20). He knows exactly when to stop: he bows his head at her warning "Perseus." He banters crudely with Apollo ("my greatsword's thrusts will be far superior", [[Ch16 - Rex Invictus IX|Ch16]]) and jokes about fishnets and hookers in Vegas ([[Ch08 - Rex Invictus I|Ch8]]).

**Haunted, and grieving under the jokes.** He carries the Abandonment and millennia alone. In [[Ch12 - Rex Invictus V|Ch12]] he describes being "effectively dead" after the wipe, "a hole now, cleanly carved". He cried for the old world in his past life. In Ch22 his hands shake, his voice cracks, and he sits with Apollo beside Michael Yew's shroud.

**Contradictions.** He acts on the timeline aggressively (saving Zoë, rousing Rome, bulldozing "Ancient Laws", [[Ch17 - Rex Invictus X|Ch17]]) while also deferring to the "pattern" (letting Artemis be captured, [[Ch16 - Rex Invictus IX|Ch16]]). He is a hardened killer who cannot bear being knelt to. He hates being used as a weapon, yet happily lets the gods treat him as a "power bank" (Ch19).

##### The moral-degradation arc (author's intent vs. what the text shows so far)
- **Stated intent ([[Ch01 - Rex Imperator I|Ch1]] A/N):** Percy will be "very OOC" with huge knowledge and strength, and will slowly develop "a personality similar to Fang Yuan from Reverend Insanity… a slow degradation of ethics and morals, but still honorable (which is contradictory, I know)." The fic's summary adds: "tearing down the looms of fate… and damn the consequences… Alea Iacta Est."
- **Early evidence of erosion ([[Ch03 - Rex Natus I|Ch3]]–10):**
  - Torturous live burnt offerings.
  - Commissioning a massacre through a demon lord.
  - Manipulating demons by naming their rivals and their masters.
  - Blackmailing a god.
  - Poisoning an army's water supply with laxatives as a war-game tactic.
  - Pocketing [[Dionysus]]'s money card ([[Ch16 - Rex Invictus IX|Ch16]]).
  - Bribing Iris for silence ([[Ch18 - Rex Invictus XI|Ch18]]).
- **Ambiguous seeding of conflict:** he invites the angel Sariel to "trail us… You might encounter something interesting" ([[Ch17 - Rex Invictus X|Ch17]]), and Sariel then attacks [[Asmodeus]]. The text does not say whether he set this up on purpose.
- **His inner voices ([[Ch11 - Rex Invictus IV|Ch11]] mindscape)** show the fork in him. A purgist fragment says "We should have killed them upon their thrones." A guide fragment says "We must guide them, shape them." A moral fragment asks "If we become convicted and executioner both, then what are we?"
- **Counter-movement ([[Ch18 - Rex Invictus XI|Ch18]]–22):** the most ruthless calls are increasingly made by **[[Minerva (AI)|Minerva]]**, not Percy. She makes him get shot through the hand as a test ([[Ch21 - Rex Invictus XIV|Ch21]]). She issues CODE 2 against his direct order: 118 dead by her own count (Landers Eleven and Six, and part of Three), traded against her projection of "between forty and seventy of your campers" ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]; see 00a C8). Percy's own choices trend toward mercy, accountability and restraint. Minerva even "commend[s] the restraint" when he declines to intervene in the [[Labyrinth]] vision of a Russian brute: "Add it to the list" ([[Ch21 - Rex Invictus XIV|Ch21]]). That is also a colder, triage-minded detachment.
- **Reading for roleplay:** as of [[Ch23 - Interlude I|Ch23]] he is *honourable but hardening*. He is a pragmatist who will burn monsters, cut deals with demons and let distant strangers die if they are, as Minerva puts it, "not your problem" ([[Ch21 - Rex Invictus XIV|Ch21]]). He keeps his word, owns his mistakes, and protects the people in front of him. The Fang Yuan endpoint has not arrived; treat it as a slope, not a destination.

#### Voice & Speech Patterns
- **Default:** casual, modern American teen. Short answers ("Yeah." "Yep." "Bad."), sarcasm, deadpan understatement, meme references ("Nanomachines, Son"), the odd Southern-ish "Ya'll" ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]).
- **Greek-flavoured oaths:** "No way in [[Hades]]," "What the Hades," "gods—". Moderate to strong profanity: "Fuck," "Shit," "Fuuuu—". He uses the Greek insult "Valkas!" (vlakas, "idiot") when he realises the [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] are coming ([[Ch04 - Rex Natus II|Ch4]]).
- **Ceremonial / archaic register:** used with demons, angels and old powers ("I hope that this evening finds thee well", [[Ch04 - Rex Natus II|Ch4]]; "I have one for thee, Holy Watcher", [[Ch17 - Rex Invictus X|Ch17]]). He invokes gods by full epithets ("Phoibos Apollon. 'Blessed Symphony', 'Apostle of Fate', 'Undying Light'", [[Ch21 - Rex Invictus XIV|Ch21]]). He speaks a pre-Athenian Greek dialect to the [[Labyrinth]] ([[Ch21 - Rex Invictus XIV|Ch21]]).

#### Backstory
Pieced together from [[Ch01 - Rex Imperator I|Ch1]], [[Ch03 - Rex Natus I|Ch3]] and later references. The order is approximate.

1. **Canon youth.** He arrived at [[Camp Half-Blood]] "barely conscious, slumped over [[Grover Underwood|Grover]]'s shoulder" ([[Ch07 - Rex Natus IV|Ch7]]). He returned [[Zeus]]'s master bolt to "its disgruntled owner" in the original throne room ([[Ch01 - Rex Imperator I|Ch1]]). He met [[Artemis]] outside Westover Hall facing [[Dr. Thorn]] (future Artemis, [[Ch13 - Rex Invictus VI|Ch13]]). He took part in "their subjugation of Lord [[Atlas]]" ([[Ch01 - Rex Imperator I|Ch1]]), where "The Eternal Hunter Princess" (most likely [[Zoe Nightshade|Zoë Nightshade]]) taught him ancient dialects. He fought the defective Talos with [[Thalia Grace|Thalia]] in the junkyard ([[Ch13 - Rex Invictus VI|Ch13]]). He fought the Battle of Manhattan beside [[Annabeth Chase|Annabeth]], and afterwards they sat "on the steps of the Empire State Building" ([[Ch23 - Interlude I|Ch23]]). He was trapped in the Lotus Casino at twelve, which Thalia calls "the worst thing that ever happened to him" ([[Ch23 - Interlude I|Ch23]]). The "Myths of Perseus Jackson" record his duel with [[Ares]] over the bolt ([[Ch09 - Rex Invictus II|Ch9]]).
2. **Adulthood and the space age.** He saw nearly every [[Olympus]] built: "the beautiful marbles of [[Olympus Secundus]]", "the ascension of the Olympus Mons of Mars" (Ch1). The suit was "of his own design" (Ch1). Annabeth, "Second-in-command of the [[Council of Olympic Sentinels]]" ([[Ch05 - Intermission I|Ch5]]), built the hardware of its AI core and secretly switched its nectar cooling to [[Lethe]] water ([[Ch05 - Intermission I|Ch5]]); she later became his "ex" ([[Ch03 - Rex Natus I|Ch3]]). He fought a modern war as commander of the defense of **Alaska Hill**, [[Athena]]'s Arctic computational fortress, "built to house Parlamentum" and "[[Répertoire du Savoir Athénien|AtRep]]'s first cradle", during the 2030s "Arctic Resource War". [[Minerva (AI)|Minerva]]: "I was there. You were there. Or we will be" ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]).
   - *Author's vault note (not yet in text):* the vault dates Alaska Hill to 2032. Percy was appointed operational commander of the demigod defence forces, with Annabeth Chase and [[Malcolm Pace]] as co-commanders, and fought in an experimental combat exoskeleton integrated with Parlamentum Mk.I. He survived an assassination attempt during the campaign. The vault also says Mk.I's cognition core then sat on Olympus above the Empire State Building, and that Minerva's first self-aware report came here: "I do not want to die for him again." He spent "many millennia" at Camp Half-Blood, yet in all that time went up to the Big House staff quarters only five times ([[Ch08 - Rex Invictus I|Ch8]]).
3. **Rammael and the stars.** His half-brother [[Rammael Marccusson]] landed on Cyrax in the Sirius system, which won "genuine pride from every major Olympian". Bound to his post, Percy could only watch the glory go elsewhere (Ch1).
4. **The Great Abandonment.** Once the first atmospheres had been seeded, the gods held "a brief party on [[Olympus Primaris]]" and left on the *Spear of Zeus*. The ship was longer than Olympus Secundus, which itself is 100 nautical miles across. Percy learned of it only on the eve of departure and caught [[Hermes]] alone. Hermes told him "YOU have no right to demand anything from us boy" and swore on "Hermes Clepsiphron" that the gods would return. The launch maimed him, and his last sight of the ship was Aeolus withdrawing "the very clouds from the atmosphere" (Ch1). Other dates: [[Ch12 - Rex Invictus V|Ch12]] ties his abandonment to "the beginning of [[The Great Expansion]]" (Minerva to [[Poseidon]]), so the two are the same event. [[Ch23 - Interlude I|Ch23]] puts his boyhood "A thousand years before the Abandonment". The vault dates the Great Expansion / Abandonment to year 3045, which fits (00a R4).
5. **The worst century.** Every other pantheon fled. Amaterasu's *Royal Blade of Retribution* went first. The stragglers "glass[ed] half the hemisphere with their [[The Crow Goddess|Morrigan]]-pattern dreadnoughts". [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] and "veritable millions of accursed realms" overran humanity. The Sentinels split up: others guarded Olympus Secundus and Tertius with the survivors, and Percy alone held Primaris against everything that "wished to assume the mantle and authority of the old seat of the gods" (Ch1). [[Typhon]] destroyed Poseidon's palace four centuries after the Abandonment. A flight of dragons destroyed Athena's palace weeks after it (Ch1).
6. **"The Great Cleansing."** [[Chaos]] says the council "shattered merely centuries after" the departure, in what Percy "would call The Great Cleansing". He hates "the Divinities that play it" (Ch1). The details are never given.
7. **Things he witnessed on watch.** He saw three time-travel incidents, each followed by pantheons warring over the traveller ([[Ch03 - Rex Natus I|Ch3]]). He heard of a Chinese man who travelled back "through artificial means. Something about an Autumn Cicada?" He also knew that the Greeks once tortured a reincarnator from the future ("the poor kid") until they dominated the western hemisphere by the end of the 5th millennium (Ch3).
8. **The political marriage to Artemis (revealed later; not mentioned in Ch1).** **(Empire era, after the Abandonment and early in the Great Expansion; never before the Abandonment, see 00a R8. The vault places the rite on [[Olympus Tertiae]].)** As the Olympian Court crumbled ("Poseidon would not act. Zeus would not bend"), the Empire needed "a stabilizer". He and Artemis were "bound" at [[Caligo Vale]], a site "chosen by [[Hera]] on a whim". As "Guardian of [[Gaia]]" he "would not intrude as much as any other entity eligible under Poseidon's dominion" (future Artemis, [[Ch13 - Rex Invictus VI|Ch13]]). Present-day Artemis finds this in the memories she rips from Rex Percy in [[Ch11 - Rex Invictus IV|Ch11]]: "They would be politically married in the future?" ([[Ch12 - Rex Invictus V|Ch12]]). The future Minerva calls Artemis "this body's wife", and [[Aphrodite]] calls her "Sister-in-law" (Ch23). The Empire's cult adds the "Silent Pact" and a "black eclipse when he was re-exiled into the Lands of our Birth, Gaia" ([[Ch15 - Rex Invictus VIII|Ch15]]). Rex Percy does not recognize those cult details, though he may simply have lost them to the memory wipe.
   - *Author's vault note (not yet in text):* the rite was invoked by Hera and was "nominal and ritualized under extreme duress". Within hours of the oathbinding the future Percy was mind-wiped and sent back to Gaia. This matches the cult's "re-exiled into the Lands of our Birth, Gaia" ([[Ch15 - Rex Invictus VIII|Ch15]]).
9. **The last day (Ch1).** Regen-ex is nearly gone and the suit is failing. He makes one more patrol, then meets Chaos. See Story Arc below.

His healing fluid, Regen-ex, is almost gone. He is formal and courtly with gods, bitter about their smirks and their broken oaths, and profane in private. He is nostalgic for friends long gone, above all Annabeth, his ex and the engineer who built his armor's AI core. Under all of it he is still loyal to a family that forgot him, and still hopes someone will come back. He knows the far future, its empires and its horrors, better than almost anyone alive.

**Personality keywords:** exhausted, bitter, duty-bound, abandoned, sardonic, courtly, profane, nostalgic, battle-hardened, quietly hopeful, proud, wary of power yet hungry for it.

**Knowledge boundaries:** He knows his whole original life: canon PJO, Annabeth, the space age, Rammael, the Abandonment, the Great Cleansing (which he hates), Unified Rankings, demons and Répertoire data, and Minerva as his armor AI. The text implies (Ch12, Ch13, Ch23) that he was politically married to future Artemis (in the Empire era, after the Abandonment) and that the Empire granted him the Lord Imperator key, but Ch1 never mentions either. Treat both as real but buried and painful, and keep them understated. He knows the suit is his own design and that Annabeth built his AI core's hardware; it was Athena, not Zeus, who called him "that man". He must NOT know anything after his dive into Chaos's abyss: the reincarnation into his young body in the present day, Lycaon's heart, Eleni in the past, Him Within the Moon awakening, the Mars legion, CODE 18, or the Anabasis Accord. He does not know whether the gods will ever return.

#### Continuity Notes
- **Time figures (00a R3):** the Empire era is about 14,000 years after the present (vault: about 14,200). The three figures measure different things and are not rival answers: (a) [[Ch03 - Rex Natus I|Ch3]], Percy's own estimate of his jump, "approximately twenty thousand standard years"; (b) [[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]], "six thousand years old" ([[Minerva (AI)|Minerva]], of the archived key-holder) and "six thousand years in the future" (narration; [[Luke Castellan|Luke]]), a known slip; (c) [[Ch23 - Interlude I|Ch23]], the packet's "APPROXIMATELY FOURTEEN THOUSAND [CORRUPTED] YEARS BEFORE PRESENT".
- **What the gods call him:** "that man" to [[Athena]] and one of "[[Poseidon]]'s children" to [[Zeus]] ([[Ch05 - Intermission I|Ch5]]; 00a C9), but "our savior" and Lord Imperator to the Empire.
- **His age in art:** the Monument depicts both figures at about 17. The Imperials call Rex Percy "sixteen" ([[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]). Profiles describe Rex Percy as a young teen; the text calls him sixteen ([[Ch07 - Rex Natus IV|Ch7]], [[Ch13 - Rex Invictus VI|Ch13]], [[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]]), though the premise blurb says he returned as a twelve-year-old (00a R1).
- **Spelling:** Autokrætor / AUTOKRÆTOR is spelled with "æ" throughout. [[Ch09 - Rex Invictus II|Ch9]] spells [[Olympus]] as "Oylmpus".
- **Code numbering:** the same number "CODE 15" carries two meanings in different code lists ([[Ch14 - Rex Invictus VII|Ch14]] [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] list vs. [[Ch15 - Rex Invictus VIII|Ch15]] Perseus definition).
