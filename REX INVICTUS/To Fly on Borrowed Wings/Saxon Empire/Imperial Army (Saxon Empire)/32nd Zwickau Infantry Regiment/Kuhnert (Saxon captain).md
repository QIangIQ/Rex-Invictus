## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Captain, commander of the 2nd Company in the battalion under [[Major Tobias]] (ch88). Saxon. Small mustache.

---

## Background

Not stated.

---

## Personality

- Playful and resourceful. Admits with suppressed laughter that the company commanders saw [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] training enthusiastically and copied his method (ch88).

---

## Abilities

- Procured shovels and picks from the engineers and quartermaster unit to drill earthwork digging (ch88). Other skills not stated.

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - junior-ranked superior in the training regime who is teased as the source of tactics; friendly, joking (ch88).
- [[Wiesner (Saxon captain)]] - fellow company commander (3rd Company); practised the same drills in secret (ch88).
- [[Major Tobias]] - battalion commander; his officers' relaxed mood before battle is what he wanted (ch88).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch88: At the pre-battle briefing he and [[Wiesner (Saxon captain)]] confess they secretly copied [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s earthwork training. [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] jokes they owe him tuition and promises them Saxon beer after the battle.
- The 2nd and 3rd Companies carry out feint attacks (ch88, ch93).
