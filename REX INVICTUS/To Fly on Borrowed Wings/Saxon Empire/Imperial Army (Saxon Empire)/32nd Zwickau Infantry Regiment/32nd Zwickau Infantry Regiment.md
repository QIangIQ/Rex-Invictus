**[[32nd Zwickau Infantry Regiment]]** is a faction hub of [[To Fly on Borrowed Wings]].

Saxon line regiment of the Aragon expedition (called the 33rd in some early chapters). Gabriel serves in its 1st Battalion as platoon leader and then 1st Company commander (ch1-111).
