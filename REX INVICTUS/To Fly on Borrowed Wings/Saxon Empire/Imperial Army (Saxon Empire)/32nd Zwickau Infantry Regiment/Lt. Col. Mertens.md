## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Lieutenant Colonel, commander of the 2nd Battalion, 32nd Infantry Regiment (Zwickau), Saxon Army, during the Iberian campaign (ch100). Appears once.
- Not the same person as the Gallic "Sentinel Project" mage [[Leopold von Asmuth]] (ch360 onward); the shared surname is coincidence as far as the text shows.
