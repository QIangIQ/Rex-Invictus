## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Tall NCO of 3rd Platoon: commands 5th Squad in the farmhouse assault (ch13), Corporal on the Seville recon team (ch17-31, 77), Sergeant and 1st Platoon Sergeant (ch83-93, 180). One of [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s oldest soldiers. Saxon.

---

## Background

- Veteran of Seville; later in 1st Company, 1st Battalion, 32nd Zwickau Regiment (ch125). Origin not stated.

---

## Personality

- Loyal, dependable and brave: volunteers for the raid (ch56), leads the short-side ambush (ch57). Practical; procures hot water for [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] (ch77).
- Honest about limits: says 400 m is too far for a shot (ch24). Told [[Voigt (Saxon officer)]] that [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] has led every charge since the first battle (ch89).

---

## Abilities

- Rifleman with Gew.98 (ch93); bayonet fighting; did not gain magic (ch65). Tries and fails to speak Aragon to a farmer (ch19).

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - trusted NCO and recon partner; [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] is grieved at his death (ch187).
- [[Anton Kessler]] - fellow veteran NCO; door guard together (ch76); brings news of his death (ch187).
- [[Voigt (Saxon officer)]] - fellow veteran, introduced to Kleist and Manstein as most trusted NCOs (ch125).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch13-15: farmhouse assault and defense. ch17-24: recon team; kills an officer in the Seville house (ch22).
- ch39: tasked with casualty lists for pensions. ch57-58: ambush, bayonets an apprentice mage; ch65 reports Hans died.
- ch83: medal ceremony. ch93: climbs a roof for crossfire in the town fight.
- ch180: leads the 40-man alert unit. ch186: killed when Gallic 75 mm Magic Guided Cannons destroy his three-story building with an MG08 squad inside.
