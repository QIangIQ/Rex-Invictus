## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Major, commander of 1st Battalion, 33rd Infantry Regiment (ch7), later 1st Battalion, 32nd Zwickau Regiment (ch82-100). [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s battalion commander and early champion. Saxon.

---

## Background

- Burly, mustached; owns a horse and a golden pocket watch (ch90). Earlier history not stated.

---

## Personality

- Grim over casualties but promises [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] a commendation (ch15). Open-minded: convinced by the recon but cannot change the plan (ch25-27); later vows "I will not watch my soldiers be mown down like wheat again" (ch88).
- Brave and inspiring: rallies men and leads a counter-charge "For the glory of the Black Eagle banner" (ch33-34). Admires [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]: "That boy... He actually pulled it off" (ch93).

---

## Abilities

- Conventional infantry battalion commander; no magic stated.

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - subordinate he champions: endorses him in the looting incident (ch41), rushes his urban warfare plan to Brigade (ch44), makes him temporary commander of 3rd Company (ch65).
- [[Emil Reinholt]] - company commander under him.
- [[Albrecht Fuhrmann]] - brigade commander above him.

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch12: commits 3rd Company to the San Isidro assault. ch17: assigns the recon mission and approves bicycles.
- ch65: wounded (arm in sling, head bandaged). ch83: receives an award.
- ch86-90: adopts the new tactics, assigns the main assault to 1st Company with two MG08s, H-hour 07:15 after 15 minutes of artillery. ch98-100: jokes about the cannon battalion and chairs the Vallecas meeting.
