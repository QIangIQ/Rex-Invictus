Known in the story as:

- Ernst (Gabriel's first orderly)

## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Young Saxon enlisted soldier serving as the orderly of [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] while the latter is a Second Lieutenant and 3rd Platoon leader (ch18-53). Rank and nationality beyond "Saxon soldier" not stated.
- Chapter 18 is titled after him (the coffee-meme chapter). Other soldiers who share the first name are different characters.

---

## Background

- Not stated. Only the Seville campaign period is shown.

---

## Personality

- Eager, dutiful and a little shy. Jogs off at once when given orders ("Yes, Second Lieutenant!", ch18) and scratches his head, embarrassed, when relaying that the company wanted [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] left to sleep (ch28).
- Looks after his officer's small comforts: brings coffee (ch18), warmed mess kit and black bread (ch28), canteen and bread for the workshop craftsmen (ch47).
- Alert in battle: his shout draws attention to the white Armored Knights approaching (ch53).

---

## Abilities

- No magic or special skills shown. Rides a borrowed bicycle with the party in ch40; carries the platoon leader's food and messages.

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - his officer. His terrible coffee prompts a meme joke in ch18 and [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] downs it in one gulp anyway. His death is what finally makes [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] feel "the cruelty of war" (ch65).
- [[Anton Kessler]] - the platoon sergeant; the two are sent together to question the cathedral priest (ch40).
- [[Matthias Hollerbach]] - the corporal who tells [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] that the orderly died (ch65).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch18 - Named for the first time; serves a burnt, bitter coffee before the bicycle raid party leaves (the meme chapter).
- ch28 - Wakes [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] and relays Captain [[Emil Reinholt]]'s order to let him rest.
- ch40 - Accompanies [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] and [[Anton Kessler]] to the cathedral bell tower in Seville.
- ch47 - Provides food and water to [[West-Lucia chemistry professor]] and craftsmen at the HEAT workshop.
- ch53 - Spots the Britannian Armored Knights from a rooftop.
- ch65 - Revealed to have been killed in the earlier street fighting; [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] calls the orderly out of habit and gets no answer.

---

## Status at ch473

- Dead (killed at Seville, revealed ch65). A different later orderly serves [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] in ch473.
