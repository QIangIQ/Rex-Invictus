## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Captain, commander of 3rd Company, 1st Battalion, 33rd Infantry Regiment, 16th Brigade (ch7-62); direct superior of [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]. Saxon, veteran, "sharp, capable-looking".
- Reports to [[Major Tobias]]. Dies at Seville in ch62; later mentioned as "late 1st Company commander" (ch74, 79, 80).

---

## Background

- Married; his wife ([[Madam Reinholt]]) gave him a new pocket watch (ch10). They and their ten-year-old son [[Benno Reinholt]] live in Zwickau (ch105-106). Earlier history not stated.

---

## Personality

- Welcoming and fatherly: greets [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] on arrival (ch7), hugs him after victory (ch15) and on his return (ch25), bear-hugs him again at Seville (ch59).
- Doctrinally conservative at first: overconfident in the "one charge" doctrine (ch10), but listens, reluctantly approves [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s flank attack (ch12-13), heeds his dispersal warning (ch30) and later calls him "a genius" (ch44).
- Courageous and self-sacrificing; last words "Long Live the Glory of the Saxon Empire!" (ch62).

---

## Abilities

- Conventional infantry commander; no magic stated. Carries a pocket watch and a wristwatch engraved "To My Beloved".

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - mentor and superior; gives him his old pocket watch (ch10), introduces him to [[Konrad von Seeckt]] (ch38), asks him to help command after platoon leaders die (ch59), entrusts his wife's watch to him (ch62-65).
- [[Major Tobias]] - battalion commander above him.
- [[Madam Reinholt]] and [[Benno Reinholt]] - wife and son; [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] returns the watches and the son wants to be a hero like his father (ch105-106).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch7: receives new platoon leader [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]; ch9-10 briefs the San Isidro attack and gives the pocket watch.
- ch14: leads the frontal charge when the machine gun falls silent; ch15 embraces [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] after victory.
- ch59-60: tells [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] the 1st and 2nd platoon leaders died; fights in the buildings.
- ch62: mortally wounded by a wooden splinter in the abdomen; gives [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] a cloth-wrapped package ("You're still young, live on!"), then with two veterans lifts the shaped charge and kills the last St. George, dying.
- ch65: the package holds the wristwatch and a note asking that it go to his wife. ch105-106: [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] delivers it; a pension was paid and the son may enter the military academy.
