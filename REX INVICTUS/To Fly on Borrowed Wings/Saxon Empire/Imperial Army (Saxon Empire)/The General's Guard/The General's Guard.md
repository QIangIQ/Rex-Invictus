## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Group entry: heavily modified soldiers in enchanted full plate, nearly 2 m tall, with explosive speed. General Mackensen's guard (ch2-3, 77); 160+ are massed for the offensive (ch258-259).
- Four are assigned to [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] by the General Staff for close protection (ch321); two guard his confinement tent (ch334).

---

## Background

Equipped by Imperial Magic Guided Technicians (ch178). A guard who rescued Mackensen's party at Seville later sacrifices himself pinning a Sentinel (ch261).

---

## Personality

Loyal and blunt. When [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] is released, the leader says: "We will accompany you, wherever you go." (ch338). Mostly unnamed.

---

## Abilities

Greatswords, war hammers, shields, or MG08-derived machine guns (ch338-339). Buffed with Bull's Strength they defeat Britannian heavy-armor sergeants (ch347-348).

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - protectee.
- [[Rudolf (General's Guard)]] - the one named member, a heavy machine gunner.
- General Mackensen - original commander; releases [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] and sends the guards with him (ch338).

## Lore & Key Events

- ch338-339: cross No Man's Land with [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]].
