## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Member of the four-man [[The General's Guard]] detail assigned to [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]; the heavy machine gunner in plate armor, nearly 2 m tall (ch343).

## Background

Not stated.

---

## Personality

- Reticent; speaks little, answers with a muffled roar (ch343, 347).
- Protective: instinctively turns to shield the commander from bullets (ch343).

---

## Abilities

- MG08 heavy machine gun carried in enchanted plate armor, used for suppressing fire (ch343, 346).
- After his gun is destroyed by [[Gaffer (Spell Jaeger captain)]] (ch347), he switches to a mace, enchanted with Magic Weapon, and a 20-round extended-magazine Mauser pistol.

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - commander he guards; ordered to suppress or watch the rear (ch343, 346-347).
- [[The General's Guard]] - his unit.
- [[Gaffer (Spell Jaeger captain)]] - destroys his weapon.

## Lore & Key Events

- ch343: laying down suppressing fire as the guards cross No Man's Land.
- ch346: slips on a conjured grease patch during a fight.
- ch347: weapon destroyed, rearmed with mace and pistol.
- ch349: pinned by enchanted [[Förster (master sergeant)]] fire less than 20 m from the enemy.
- ch350: several guards are carried off wounded; his own fate is not stated.
