## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Colonel commanding the Saxon First Army Group artillery, at its artillery command post (ch352).

---

## Background

Senior artillery officer; otherwise not stated.

---

## Personality

Cold, hard, decisive, "not an inflexible blockhead" (ch352). Overrides a hesitant staff officer ("What else needs confirming? Look for yourself"). Reasons independently about the abnormal temperature drop on the offensive axis. Pained by ineffective fire.

---

## Abilities

Directs two 150mm howitzers, a battalion of 105mm howitzers and a battalion of 77mm field guns on [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s coordinates, without spotting rounds (ch352); later brings more guns and tries to call armored trains and airships. Surprised by [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s precise infantry-artillery coordinates.

## Relationships

- Artillery staff officer - subordinate who questions the regulations (ch352).
