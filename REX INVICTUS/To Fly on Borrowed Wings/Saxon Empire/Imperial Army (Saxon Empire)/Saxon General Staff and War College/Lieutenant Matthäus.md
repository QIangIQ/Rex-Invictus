## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Lieutenant, liaison officer of the Saxon Army General Staff assigned to [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] during his recovery in Dresden (ch374-377). Has "von" in his name; surname not stated (ch375).

---

## Background

Long stayed in the General Staff (ch374). Otherwise not stated.

---

## Personality

- Nervous and deferential at first, sits on "half of his butt" (ch374); careful in wording.
- Honest about General Staff politics: reports supporters (Western Front observers) and old-school generals opposed to the elite unit plan (ch374).
- Impressionable: moved to "utter worship" by [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s speech about soldiers' welfare (ch375). Helpful, notices the missing "von" in [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s signature (ch375).

---

## Abilities

Courier of documents; passes reviewed private mail (ch375).

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - subordinate-style liaison; relationship warms from nervous to admiring (ch374-377).
- Chief of the General Staff - source of the quoted view that training grounds cost less than battlefield deaths (ch374).
- [[Therese von Falkenstein]] - [[Lieutenant Matthäus]] delivers mail that includes her letter (ch375).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch374: delivers the Stormtrooper/academy plan; says [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] is the natural academy president (titular role).
- ch375: carries [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s supplementary report back; three days later brings the approval order (Koblenz main campus, southern Bavaria Alpine training ground); delivers letters.
- ch377: brings the discharge approval and relays the Chief of the General Staff's request that [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] report soon.
