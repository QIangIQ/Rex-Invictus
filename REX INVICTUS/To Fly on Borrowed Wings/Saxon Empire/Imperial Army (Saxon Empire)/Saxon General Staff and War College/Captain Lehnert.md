## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Captain; liaison officer of the Saxon Army Group Headquarters. An unnamed "old acquaintance" liaison collects [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] in ch321; named in ch333, where he leads military police to arrest him on General Mackensen's order.
- Distinct from [[Major Danneberg]] (Major, 4th Battalion).

---

## Background

Not stated.

---

## Personality

- Embarrassed and apologetic when making the arrest: "Sorry, Lieutenant Colonel" is said with a dry tone and visible discomfort, and a cold sweat on his forehead (ch333).
- Sympathetic and candid in private: tells [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] that "many people actually think privately that what you did wasn't wrong" about the ceasefire (ch333).
- Deferential to authority: stresses Mackensen's strictness about discipline; gives a "good luck" look and slips away after delivering him (ch333).

---

## Abilities

Staff liaison with military police and a headquarters vehicle; no combat or magic shown.

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - old acquaintance; [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] cooperates calmly ("Let's go, Captain ") so as not to embarrass the General, and the captain thanks him (ch333).
- General Mackensen (historical) - his superior, whose order he carries out.
- Manstein (historical) and [[Detlef von Raschke]] - know him as an acquaintance; Manstein challenges the arrest (ch333).

## Lore & Key Events

- ch321: liaison officer who escorts [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] to the Army Group Headquarters (unnamed there).
- ch333: arrests [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] over the ceasefire incident, drives him to Mackensen and delivers him to the door.
