## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Colonel, Deputy Director of Logistics and Supply, Army General Staff; Junker noble from a wealthy family. Nicknamed "Mr. Nice Guy" by the army high echelon; the Britannian-side code name "Colonel K" (ch390-391; restored from the alias list).

---

## Background

Family loyal to the Empire for generations; ancestral wealth lets him live in a three-story white house with garden, better than higher-ranked generals (ch391).

---

## Personality

- Always smiling and polite on the surface (ch391). Panicky when caught, then tries to claim a grand motive: the country is "sick", he wanted to "reshuffle the deck" by engineering a setback so the old elite fall (ch391). Collapses under Major Nicolai's contempt and confesses; [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] finds the speech comically eloquent.

---

## Abilities

Control over army logistics paperwork: forged weapon-transfer documents (ch391).

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - his instruction unit raids and arrests him; listens in from the next room (ch391).
- Major Nicolai (Department III) - interrogator who breaks him (ch391).
- Britannian intelligence - buyer of MG14 light machine guns, Luger P08s, grenades and explosives, used for the opera-house assassination attempt (ch391).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch390: the name "Colonel K" appears repeatedly in recovered material.
- ch391: identified through safe houses, forged documents and a captured agent; arrested in bathrobe at dawn; confesses and names accomplices, triggering a purge.
- ch392: counted among the "big fish" swept up with many smaller spies.

---

## Status at ch473

Arrested and confessed (ch391); fate after that not stated.
