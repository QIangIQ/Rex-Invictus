## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- General, Vice-President (Vice Dean) of the Saxon War College; teaches "Imperial Military Tradition" (ch126). Old, pipe-smoking, handlebar-mustached, traditionalist (ch295).

---

## Background

Taught most Army Department officers. Spelled [[General Wernsdorff]] in some chapters.

---

## Personality

- Conservative but clear-headed: "The greater the success a system achieves through a particular method, the more difficult it is for that system to undergo self-revolution." (ch128).
- Honest and self-mocking: admits he was wrong about [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] (ch295).
