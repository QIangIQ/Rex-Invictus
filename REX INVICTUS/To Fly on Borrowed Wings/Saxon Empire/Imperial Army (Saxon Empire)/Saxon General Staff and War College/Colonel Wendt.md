## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Colonel of the Operations Department at First Army Group headquarters, holding the fort when [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] phones in an emergency (ch351). Steady middle-aged voice. Distinct from the real historical Major General Schmidt von Knobelsdorff and from the messenger Schmidt of ch5-7.

## Background

Not stated.

---

## Personality

Procedure-minded and skeptical: doubts a Tier-8 Weather Control caster is present and objects to using army group artillery without the General's authorization and with limited shell stocks (ch351). Yields when [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] roars at him, and then acts decisively, calling his adjutant to contact artillery (ch351).

---

## Abilities

Operations staff officer; authority to relay fire requests to artillery.

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - caller of ch351; initially resisted, then complies after being pressed.
- [[Colonel Thiele]] - artillery commander who executes the fire mission (ch352).
