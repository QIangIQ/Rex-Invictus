## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

Captain, officer in charge of the Field Post Office sorting center at the Second Army Group headquarters camp (Saxon). Appears in ch206. Not the same man as [[Emil Reinholt]] ([[Emil Reinholt]]).

---

## Background

Not stated.

---

## Personality

Enthusiastic and accommodating toward [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s unit, which General von Bulow had asked staff to treat with "special care". Careful about regulations (insists letters still be censored) yet easily won over by gifts (ch206).

---

## Abilities

Controls postal priority; stamps mailbags with a red "Highest Priority" stamp.

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - treats him as a combat hero of the Second Army Group; speeds the battalion's last mail and [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s three personal letters, receiving captured Gallic cigarettes as thanks (ch206).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch206: Receives a mailbag of over a thousand letters from [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]], stamps it Highest Priority, promises fast processing after mandatory censorship, wishes him luck in battle. The letters lead to the scene of ch240.
