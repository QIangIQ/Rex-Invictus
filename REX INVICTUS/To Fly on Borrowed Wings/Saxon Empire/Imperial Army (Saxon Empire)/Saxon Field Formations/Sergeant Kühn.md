## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Sergeant, a stocky, stubble-faced veteran of the Captain's company on the central salient; leads [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s disguised night patrol (ch325-327). Saxon.
- A separate messenger, [[Möller (messenger)]], who runs a field telephone line (ch96), and the postman [[Old Brendel]] (ch240) are not stated to be the same man.

---

## Background

- Has a wife who sends him sausage from his hometown (ch327). Nothing else stated.

---

## Personality

- Nervous around rank, wiping his hands on his trousers, but steady in the field (ch325).
- Careful and brave: stops the crawl at the right moments and does not flinch under a Britannian patrol (ch326).
- Humble and warm: shares soup with his men, gives bacon to the youngest, and proudly offers sausage to the officer (ch327).

---

## Abilities

- Veteran scout and patrol leader; signals with cooing calls to the trench (ch327).

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - is ordered to lead him as "Private Gabriel" on a night patrol; Kühn is awed by his foresight (ch326) and later receives his stew (ch327).
- The Captain, his company commander - hands the patrol to him (ch325).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch325 - Introduced as the man to obey on the night patrol.
- ch326 - Leads the crawl into No Man's Land; during a standoff with a Britannian patrol, receives a mental message from [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]; afterward asks how he knew they would not shoot.
- ch327 - Shares the stew; shows his wife's sausage, promising not to eat it all before Christmas Eve.

---

## Status at ch473

Not seen after ch327; presumed alive.
