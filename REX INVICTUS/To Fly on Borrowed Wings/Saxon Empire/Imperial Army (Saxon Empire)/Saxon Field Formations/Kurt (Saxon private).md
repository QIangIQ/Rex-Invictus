## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Private of the 42nd Infantry Regiment; a high school geography teacher before the war. Viewpoint soldier for the Creil assaults (ch250-252, 256-259, 264).

---

## Background

- Former geography teacher who notices terrain and the polluted orange sky (ch250-251). Had a fellow townsman in his unit who died in the first charge (ch251).

---

## Personality

- Nervous and observant, driven by "Survive" (ch251). Disciplined enough to follow the Instruction Assault Battalion despite fear, and grateful for harsh training (ch257).
- Resilient: survives the first charge, helps a wounded comrade back to the trench (ch252).

---

## Abilities

- Gew.98 rifle and bayonet; trained 48 hours by an Instruction Assault Battalion sergeant (ch257).

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - sees him as "a lighthouse in the darkness" leading the charge on Sept 6 (ch258); an unnamed young officer of the Instruction Assault Battalion pulls [[Kurt (Saxon private)]] up in ch251.
- Wounded comrade and veterans of the 42nd - fellow survivors.

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch250-251 - Arrives at Creil, finds corpses built into the trench walls; survives the first charge, nearly killed by a Colonial soldier before assault troops save him.
- ch252 - Retreats under cover of the assault soldiers.
- ch256-257 - Selected among the regiment's elite for the first wave and the 48-hour training.
- ch258-259 - Charges behind [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] with the [[The General's Guard]] nearby.
- ch264 - Watches Armored Airship L29's strafing break the Gallic defense.
