## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Brigadier, commander of the 16th Infantry Brigade (ch27-78), stern man in his forties; later Major General commanding the 4th Guard Reserve Infantry Division, the first fully motorised infantry division (ch424-444), age 45. Saxon.

---

## Background

- Burly with a handlebar mustache; lacks promotion seniority (ch425). He was brigade commander when [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] was drawn into the Aragonese Civil War (ch424). Not clearly a real historical person.

---

## Personality

- Stern but fair; praises [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] yet is bound by orders (ch27). Later friendly and joking, calling [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] a "military merit wishing machine" (ch425).

---

## Abilities

- Large-formation commander; motorised division operations.

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - subordinate turned partner: adopts his urban-warfare system (ch45), requests a private meeting and writes a special report on his spellcasting (ch78), detaches units to form the Protagonist Kampfgruppe (ch425). Described as a former superior of higher rank, 12 years older; not in the Kampfgruppe (ch444).
- [[Major Tobias]] - battalion commander under him.

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch27: keeps the attack plan after an Imperial General Staff telegram demands Seville "at any cost" and sends Armored Airship L30.
- ch42: after taking Seville, refuses to retreat and orders a hold until the airship arrives.
- ch424-425: his division completes a secondary penetration, seals the "dumpling wrapper" around the Serbian First Army, and detaches the 12th Guard Reserve Infantry Regiment and the 5th Guard Reserve Cavalry Regiment. ch430: seals the retreat of two Serbian divisions.

---

## Status at ch473

- Alive; commands 4th Guard Reserve Infantry Division.
