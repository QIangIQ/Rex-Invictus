## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Colonel, commander of the heavy Armored Train "Odin", about forty, tall and resolute. Saxon (ch267-288).

---

## Background

Not stated beyond his command.

---

## Personality

- Hearty and enthusiastic; laughs loudly and calls Odin his "baby" (ch267).
- Open admirer of [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]; says his crew hoped to fight alongside the Instruction Assault Battalion after the Liège success (ch267).

---

## Abilities

- Commands Odin, the newest heavy armored train, one of five reinforcing the northern theatre out of eighteen in service.
- Deploys the Gungnir railgun, but cannot fire it himself; only a few Magitech engineers can (ch270).

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - admirer and fellow commander; tours him through Odin and plans the second firing mode with him (ch267, 275-278).
- [[Leona von Fortis]] - present at the first meeting (ch267).
- General Mackensen - reports to him on the Gungnir effort (ch279).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch267: greets [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] and shows him Odin and Gungnir.
- ch268-271: fires Gungnir at Fortress One and anti-air arrays.
- ch275-278: fails to hit the Eiffel Tower in first mode, then plans the second mode with [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]].
- ch283-288: oversees the conversion and gives the final order to fire; Gungnir topples the Eiffel Tower.
