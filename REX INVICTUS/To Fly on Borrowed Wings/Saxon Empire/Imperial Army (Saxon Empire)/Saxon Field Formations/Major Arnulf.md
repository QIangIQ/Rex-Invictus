## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Major, commander of the 1st Battalion, 11th Infantry Regiment. Sent by Ludendorff as reinforcement to the Instruction Assault Battalion at Charleroi (ch196). Addressed as "Captain" by [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] in the translation, but the text gives his rank as Major.

---

## Background

- Had taken part in the siege of Liege Fortress and the battle to interdict the Flanders Fifth Infantry Division (ch196). Otherwise not stated.

---

## Personality

- Composed and perceptive. After the shock of the corpse field he recovers first and offers [[Anton Kessler]] a cigarette to ask how the line was held (ch196).
- Had privately thought the claim that one battalion held off a division was exaggerated, then realised he was wrong; felt awe, sympathy and some fear (ch196).

---

## Abilities

- Arrives with his battalion, captured Liege searchlights and Radiant Crystal generators (ch196).

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - receives and welcomes him; sincere smile, treated as senior in rank (ch196).
- [[Major Dietrich]] - fellow reinforcement battalion commander, arrives and reacts alongside him.
- [[Anton Kessler]] - questions him about how the position was held (ch196).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch196: Introduces himself on arrival, reacts to the smell of corpses and the sea of dead, and asks [[Anton Kessler]] how the Gallic division was beaten.
