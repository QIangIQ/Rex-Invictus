## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Major, commander of the 2nd Battalion, 12th Infantry Regiment. Reinforcement sent by Ludendorff to Charleroi (ch196).

---

## Background

- Fought at the siege of Liege Fortress and the interdiction of the Flanders Fifth Infantry Division (ch196). Otherwise not stated.

---

## Personality

- Visibly squeamish: covers his nose at the smell of corpses (ch196). Like [[Major Arnulf]], was sceptical and then awed by the scale of the dead.

---

## Abilities

- Brings his battalion and supplies, including captured searchlights and Radiant Crystal generators (ch196).

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - greets him on arrival; [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] has his men unload supplies and learn the positions (ch196).
- [[Major Arnulf]] - fellow battalion commander; they exchange solemn glances (ch196).
