## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Colonel, commander of the 93rd Infantry Regiment, Saxon Empire Army (ch339). Nationality Saxon. Appears in a single scene.
- Works alongside the instruction unit commanded by [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] in the First Army Group zone.

## Personality

- Practical and quick to protect his men: when the [[The General's Guard]] advance into fire he curses and orders his adjutant to shout them back (ch339).
- Dry humour: once he recognises the runner he relaxes, and the adjutant's reaction shows the unit's reputation is enough to dispel concern (ch339).
