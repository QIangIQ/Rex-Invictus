## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Major; commander of the 4th Battalion, 12th Guard Reserve Infantry Regiment (Saxon), attached to [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s Kampfgruppe (ch430-439).
- Not to be confused with [[Captain Lehnert]] (Captain, Army Group liaison) or the airship captain [[Siegmund von Lauterbach]] (ch220-221), who are separate people.

---

## Background

Not stated beyond his rank and posting. His men are Guard Reserve troops: adopted the new training syllabus but lack the foundation of the instruction unit (ch433).

---

## Personality

- Cautious and practical. Initially wary of the routed Austro-Hungarians: draws his Mauser pistol and is ready to order fire if they charge (ch431), speaks to them without politeness, then reluctantly lets them in and orders a little bread and thinned soup shared out. Soon regrets the generosity: "If we give it all to them, what do we eat?" (ch431).
- Takes the Austro-Hungarian warning of "monsters" seriously enough to heighten night vigilance and patrol the trenches himself with coffee in hand (ch431).
- Resolute under pressure: with about twenty men left in the third trench he says "We nail ourselves here even if we die" (ch433).
- Humble and willing to learn; admits honestly that his men are exhausted (ch439).

---

## Abilities

Ordinary infantry officer: M1915 Mauser pistol, later a submachine gun, MG08 heavy machine guns in his position, illumination flares (ch431, ch433). No magic shown.

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - his superior. He is stunned by his counter-charge and kill rate against the Vukodlak, and understands the feared nicknames for him (ch434). In ch439 [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] teaches him and his adjutant about logistics and decentralized "resolve locally" command, which he recognizes from the military academy.
- [[Josef Hirschl]] - Austro-Hungarian lieutenant colonel whose remnants join his line; they fight side by side in the third trench (ch431-433).
- [[Kunze (Saxon officer)]] - fellow battalion commander in the same defence (ch433).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch430-431: takes in routed Austro-Hungarians, orders them guarded, and prepares for the "monster" warning; night raid begins and his flares reveal the Vukodlak (ch431).
- ch432-433: position is overrun in the second trench, two of three MG08s are destroyed, he is pulled back to the third trench with about twenty men.
- ch434: [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] arrives, orders a counter-charge; the major witnesses him kill Vukodlak and the [[The General's Guard]] charge.
- ch435-437: exhausted after the all-night fight; relays orders for the next phase.
- ch439: receives the logistics lesson from [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]].

Alive as far as the text shows; last seen ch439. Later fate not stated.
