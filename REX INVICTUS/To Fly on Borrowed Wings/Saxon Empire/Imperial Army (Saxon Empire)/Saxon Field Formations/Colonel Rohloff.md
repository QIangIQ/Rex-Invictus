## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Colonel; commander of the Saxon 52nd Cavalry Regiment with a Horse Artillery Battalion of 12 77mm guns (regiment at 64 percent strength at ch50), operating near Seville.

## Personality

Decisive and brave: spots the exposed enemy battery, raises his lance first and leads the charge with "For the Emperor and the Empire!" (ch52). Quick to read danger and shout a warning when mages appear.

## Abilities

Cavalry lance charge; binoculars; bugle signals (ch52).
