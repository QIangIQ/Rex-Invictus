## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Ex-Imperial Guards officer; Lieutenant and commander of 2nd Company (ch165-245), 2nd Company of the Instruction Assault Battalion; by ch355 commander of 1st Battalion, later Major (ch460). Saxon.

---

## Background

- Described in the roster notes as a former Imperial Guards officer; family and origin otherwise not stated.

---

## Personality

- Reckless, fast-thrusting and enthusiastic (ch231, 245); 8% casualties in 2nd Company (ch231). Was ecstatic to serve under [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] (ch165).
- Prone to overextending: [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] scolds him over the loudspeaker "Are you deaf? I told you to advance!" (ch231-232); nearly overextends again (ch234) and [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] promises extra training (ch235).
- Fierce, aggressive style as battalion commander (ch422); proud: "Our 1st Battalion rolled out of the mountains of corpses and seas of blood in Paris!" (ch433).

---

## Abilities

- Assault infantry leader; praises the Mortar Launchers (ch245); lays mines on the perimeter on order (ch433).

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - commander and trainer; he is eager to impress him (ch165) and is corrected by him (ch231-235).
- [[Voigt (Saxon officer)]] - fellow officer ordered about in the same river-bank operation (ch166).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch165-166: forced march and river-bank deployment under [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s orders.
- ch217: supervises Imperial Guards Division digging. ch231-235: Amiens/South Bank fighting, reprimanded for advancing recklessly.
- ch355: commands 1st Battalion. ch422-427: reports destruction of Second Army HQ. ch433: mines the perimeter.
- ch460-464: breaches the pass, then dismounts his battalion at once on news of the blockage.

---

## Status at ch473

- Alive; Major commanding 1st Battalion.
