## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Executive Officer of Rommel's 1st Company, 2nd Battalion, during the Romanian campaign (ch458-460). Lieutenant. Saxon. Distinct from [[Anton Kessler]] (NCO), despite the shared surname.

## Background

- Not stated.

---

## Personality

- Skeptical but loyal. Questions the untested mortar-guidance tactic ("Are you sure it wasn't just a spontaneous idea [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] came up with?", ch458), yet carries out orders at once.
- Gets carsick on mountain roads; his face turns green (ch459).
- Understands his commander's intentions without being told (ch460).

---

## Abilities

- Leads messengers and communications as company HQ, directs hand-signal orders, interrogates captured Romanian officers (ch459), and leads the main body in a frontal assault (ch460).

---

## Relationships

- Rommel (real historical person) - his company commander and close partner in the field (ch458-460).
- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - Colonel and overall commander; Pfeil doubts but follows his tactics (ch458).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch458: Follows twenty meters behind Rommel; helps read the enemy positions and relays mortar and guidance squad orders.
- ch459: Interrogates captured officers at the taken Romanian HQ; stunned by what he sees through binoculars; gets carsick in the truck.
- ch460: Keeps the main force attacking when Rommel flanks; the Romanian line wavers and surrenders.
