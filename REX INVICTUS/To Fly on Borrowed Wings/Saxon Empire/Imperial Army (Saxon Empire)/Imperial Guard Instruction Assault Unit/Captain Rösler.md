## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Captain (once addressed as "Lieutenant this officer" by a messenger, ch315), commander of the Regimental Supply Company of [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s instruction regiment and the "chief steward" of all regimental vehicles (ch314).
- Among the first Saxon officers to handle Radiant Crystal Trucks since they entered military trials (ch314).

---

## Background

- Not stated beyond long experience with the trucks' faults. Saxon.

---

## Personality

- Anxious, grumbling and conscientious: worried about the 120 km regimental road march, calls the staff planners "bookworms who only know how to draw lines on maps" (ch314); calls his trucks his "babies" (teased by Guderian).
- Hands-on: personally helps swap a leaking fuel connector on the first breakdown (ch315) and shuttles through the convoy checking vehicles.
- Open to good ideas and quick to credit them; sincerely thanks [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] for the pre-exercise tests (ch315).

---

## Abilities

- Vehicle maintenance management: builds the equipment demand list by failure patterns and fuel/material consumption, split into three priority levels; the "two big bags plus several small bags" spare-parts configuration (small bags to drivers, big bags to Forward and Middle Maintenance Teams); 3 trucks reserved for spare parts; he rides at the rear with the maintenance support team (ch314).
- Commands maintenance technicians and drivers; the system keeps most faults under half an hour (ch316).

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - his battalion/regiment commander (Lieutenant Colonel at the time); gives key suggestions such as the Forward and Middle Maintenance Teams, and this officer finds him "increasingly unfathomable" (ch314-315).
- Heinz Wilhelm Guderian (real) - radio platoon leader and fellow mechanical enthusiast; helps with the spare-parts list and checks (ch314-315).
- [[Detlef von Raschke]] - staff officer overseeing the march; [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] cites this officer's work to calm him (ch316).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch314: foresees breakdowns; designs the maintenance support system with [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] and Guderian.
- ch315: pre-exercise tests; personally fixes the first broken truck.
- ch316-317: maintenance system keeps the convoy moving and works more smoothly on the return, though the "fragile" first-generation trucks still break down often.
