## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Sergeant commanding the Reconnaissance Detachment of the 3rd Battalion in the Romanian campaign (ch461). Saxon, instruction unit. The battalion commander is [[Brenner (Saxon officer)]].

## Background

- Not stated.

---

## Personality

- Calm and decisive: gives orders without raising his voice and makes a "split-second decision" to strike first when an encounter is unavoidable (ch461).
- Leads from the front, walking point.

---

## Abilities

- MP14 submachine gun, NCO-issue binoculars (standard only to NCO level in the unit), truck-borne recon with a topographic map. Uses hand signals and trained fire discipline.

---

## Relationships

- [[Brenner (Saxon officer)]] - battalion commander who arrives after the skirmish (ch461).
- His detachment's squad leaders and soldiers - trained and unflinching under contact.

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch461: Heads a recon of two or three squads (twenty men in the strike element) toward a "Suspected Enemy Position". Meets a Romanian patrol on a narrow path, ambushes it, and wipes it out in about a dozen seconds without loss, then confirms the Romanian position ahead.
