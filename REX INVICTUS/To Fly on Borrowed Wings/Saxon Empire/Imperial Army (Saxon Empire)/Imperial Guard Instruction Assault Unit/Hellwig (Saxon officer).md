## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Second Lieutenant, 2nd Platoon Leader of 1st Company (ch80-98, 150); a classmate of [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] from the Central Military Academy. Later commander of 2nd Company, 1st Battalion, Instruction Unit (ch321). Spelled [[Hellwig (Saxon officer)]], [[Hellwig (Saxon officer)]] and [[Hellwig (Saxon officer)]] across translations (the same man).

---

## Background

- Cohort of [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] at the academy, where [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] was seen as a womaniser beyond hope (ch80). Posted as a new platoon leader from a reserve battalion of the 8th Division.

---

## Personality

- Steady and competent but cautious; "a newcomer to the battlefield" who relied on his platoon sergeant at first (ch93). Meek in front of veterans like [[Anton Kessler]] (ch80). Dejected when criticised for not flanking (ch98).
- By ch306 the oldest trio "almost beat the new officers like spinning tops" in simulated exercises.

---

## Abilities

- Platoon and company command; fires the platoon flare gun (ch150). Midfielder who passes to [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] in the Christmas football match (ch332).

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - former classmate turned subordinate who grows awed by him (ch80-89).
- [[Voigt (Saxon officer)]] and [[Kranz (Saxon officer)]] - fellow platoon leaders, later fellow company commanders.
- [[Anton Kessler]] and [[Matthias Hollerbach]] - veteran NCOs who set the company's tone (ch79-87).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch80: reports for duty with the others. ch90: leads 2nd Platoon in night trench digging. ch92-93: attacks beside [[Voigt (Saxon officer)]] and fights in town streets.
- ch98: 5 killed and 8 wounded in his platoon; [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] tells him to flank and infiltrate. ch150: fires a flare in a night fight.
- ch306: veteran trio in the 1st Battalion. ch321: company commander at Koblenz station. ch332: plays the assist in the Christmas match against a Britannian team.
