**[[Imperial Guard Instruction Assault Unit]]** is a faction hub of [[To Fly on Borrowed Wings]].

Gabriel Jagdfeld's own formation: the Imperial Guards First Instruction Assault Battalion (about 1,300 men, the only fully motorized battalion), grown into the Imperial Guard Assault Instruction Unit (about 4,500 men) and then the Jagdfeld Kampfgruppe (20,000+ men by ch464).
