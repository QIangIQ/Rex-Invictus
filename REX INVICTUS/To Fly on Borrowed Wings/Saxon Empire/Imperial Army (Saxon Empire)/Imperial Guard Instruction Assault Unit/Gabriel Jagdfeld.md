**[[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]** is the viewpoint protagonist of [[To Fly on Borrowed Wings]]: a [[Demigod]] cadet of [[The Citadel]] in the far-future [[Undying Olympian Empire]] ([[UOE]]) whose soul wakes in the body of a junior officer of the [[Saxon Empire]] in 1914, and who rises to command the [[Imperial Guard Instruction Assault Unit]] and the Jagdfeld Kampfgruppe.

## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Viewpoint protagonist. A UOE Athenian Demigod Citadel cadet who transmigrated into the body of a junior Saxon officer (with a heavily damaged and lobotomized AtRep).
- Nationality and faction: Saxon Empire, Imperial Army; Junker noble family, declining; a baron from ch375 (title without a fief). Born 26 Dec 1892 in Dresden, so aged 22 in ch295-298.
- Rank and post over time:
  - ch1-64 Second Lieutenant, 3rd Platoon leader, 3rd Company, 1st Battalion; briefly "senior orderly" in Mackensen's observation group (ch5).
  - ch65-78 acting commander of 3rd Company after [[Emil Reinholt]] dies.
  - ch79-111 First Lieutenant (promoted after 21 days, a record), commander of 1st Company, 1st Battalion, 32nd Zwickau Infantry Regiment.
  - ch112-295 Captain, first commander of the Imperial Guard Instruction Assault Battalion (a General Staff direct unit); the notes disagree on the exact timing of the Captain promotion (ch112, ch113, ch122).
  - ch295-392 Lieutenant Colonel (skipping Major), head of a reinforced regiment of about 4,500 (ch310); Blue Max (ch296-297); baron and president of the Assault Infantry Experimental Combat Academy (ch374-375).
  - ch393 Colonel; honorary Austro-Hungarian colonel (ch407); commander of the "Kampfgruppe" from ch425, over 20,000 men by ch464.
- Decorations: Iron Cross 2nd class (ch83) and 1st class (ch99), Pour le Merite (ch296-297), Gold Spellbreaker Medal, Gold Infantry Assault Medal, Close Combat Clasp, Air Assault Medal, Black Wound Badge (ch297), Order of Leopold (ch407).
- Nicknames: ?, ?, ?, ?, ?, ?, ? (ch294), ? (ch389), ? (ch406), "?" (ch418-432). ("?" marks a nickname erased by the draft placeholder pass; restore from the manuscript.)

### UOE Aspect (far future)

- In his first life he was an excellent [[Demigod]] student of [[The Citadel]], the [[UOE]] academy run by [[Chiron]], in the Greco-Roman far future of the [[Undying Olympian Empire]] (profile, 2026-11-26).
- The profile calls him an **Athenian** demigod cadet, which ties him to [[Athena]]'s side of the UOE. Whether Athena is his divine parent is not stated.
- His soul arrived in Europa with a heavily damaged and lobotomized AtRep, the [[Répertoire du Savoir Athénien]], Athena's imperial intelligence. PROPOSAL, not a stated ruling: the broken AtRep is the in-world source of his game-like System (map, unit cards, Technology and Spellbook tabs).
- His knowledge of twentieth-century history and doctrine comes from his Citadel schooling, which is why he recognises Guderian, Rommel, Manstein, Paulus and Model on sight (ch304-305).
- Röntgen's scan finding that "you aren't human at all" (ch410-411) fits his demigod nature under the Sentinel modification.

> [!note] Premise conflict
> The draft roster (section 1) still describes him as a 21st-century cadet from "Blue Star" (modern Earth). The 2026-11-26 profile replaces that with the UOE Citadel origin. The UOE origin is used here. See [[TFBW Section Rules and Decisions]].

## Background

- Original owner: a "famous playboy of Dresden" living on [[Therese von Falkenstein]]'s allowance; once publicly rejected by [[Eugenia von Seeckt]] at a ball (ch107, ch115). Parents died when he was young; last of his line; grandfather tied to Mackensen (ch104).
- Previous life: Excellent Demigod student from The Citadel in the Greco-Roman Far future UOE, knows twentieth-century history and later figures (recognises Guderian, Rommel, Manstein, Paulus, Model, ch304-305).
- Wakes in a Britannian cellar in Seville and is rescued with Mackensen (ch1-3). His private wish is to leave the battlefield (ch103) but he keeps being sent forward. He fears hyperinflation and a later "Bad Ending" purge (ch112, ch391-392) and tries to steer history, for instance by promising a recommendation to a young dispatch rider (ch214-216).

## Personality

- Analytical and teaching-minded: holds the first "post-action review" (ch16), builds the Five Core Infantry Skills and a 400 m obstacle course (ch123-125), writes honest reports listing every failure (ch317).
- Leads from the front and trusts small, well-briefed teams: Misty Step assaults (ch91-94), night raids, airdrop into Liege (ch152-153).
- Cares for subordinates: reduces casualties by dispersal (ch30), shares his stew with [[Sergeant Kühn]] (ch327), refuses to regret the Christmas ceasefire ("I do not regret it", ch334).
- Blunt with superiors: "We are the dagger, not the shield" (ch196-198).
- Humour and appetite: "?" (ch28, nickname erased by the draft placeholder pass), calls himself a "pay-to-win whale" when buying patents (ch313), makes pop-culture jokes (ch317).
- Pragmatic businessman who hides behind a silent-majority shareholding to protect Junker propriety (ch128).

## Abilities

- Abjuration mage with a game-like interface. Progression: Level 0 (ch65), Level 3 / 2nd circle after [[Ainsley (Highland High Mentor)]]'s manual (ch66), Level 4 (ch223), third circle (ch233), Level 7 / Tier 4 (ch335), Level 9 / 5th circle by ch473.
- Spells: Mage Armor (stops about 20 rifle bullets, ch91), Shield, Misty Step, Invisibility, Greater Invisibility, Haste, Counterspell, Blink, Dispel Magic, Mend (recharges enchanted cuirasses, ch179), Mage Hand, Feather Fall, Bull's Strength, Cat's Grace. Arcane Ward and Projected Ward features.
- Equipment: Amulet of the Guardian, Bigby's Hand scroll (read ch370), Krieghoff double-barrelled shotgun from [[Therese von Falkenstein]] (ch320), MP14 submachine gun.
- Skills and inventions: HEAT shaped charge with [[Pavel (International Brigades)]] (ch45-49), urban-warfare system (ch43-44), Tachanka (ch203), assault-group tactics with creeping barrage (ch255-256), spaced and composite armour, two-seat Knights, motorised stress test (ch314-317), tracer rounds (ch319).
- Physically abnormal: 400 m course in 1 min 30 s (ch305); a scan reveals "you aren't human at all" (ch410-411); Sentinel modification plateaus at 40% (ch377); his blood is the base of the Donnerkrieger serum (ch457).
