**[[Jakob (Saxon officer)]]** is a character in [[To Fly on Borrowed Wings]], placed under the [[Imperial Guard Instruction Assault Unit]] of the [[Saxon Empire]].

Known in the story as:

- Jakob
- Major Jakob

## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Instruction unit officer. Shown as "3rd Battalion Commander" in ch350 (Paris). Ch422 explains he was 3rd Company commander and was promoted to command the 2nd Battalion, with [[Brenner (Saxon officer)]] taking the 3rd Battalion. Major by the Romanian campaign (ch460).
- Ch460: leads the "Forward Assault Group" into Romania. The text itself lists the ch350 and ch422 ranks inconsistently.

---

## Background

- Not stated beyond rising through the instruction unit; carries an "Armored Knight phobia" that lasted years (ch429).

---

## Personality

- Relatively steady and "balanced" among the three battalion commanders, combining the aggression of [[Kunze (Saxon officer)]] and the steadiness of [[Brenner (Saxon officer)]] (ch422, 460). Best infantry tactics scores in the unit (ch460).
- Practical and calm: gives subordinates autonomy (Rommel, ch422), chats with gun crews, repeats "Hold firm, and there will be a way" and trusts the Colonel completely (ch429). Smokes in the command post (ch350).

---

## Abilities

- Infantry command; uses telephone-linked command trucks on Hill 1066 (ch459). Applies [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s "Continuous Alternating Search-and-Destroy Attack" with two 170mm mortars, extra MG08 teams, engineers and light mortars attached (ch460). Cured of his fear of Armored Knights by the 50mm RAK 15 anti-armour gun (ch429).

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - his commander and the source of tactics he trusts (ch429, 460).
- [[Brenner (Saxon officer)]] - fellow battalion commander; they dispatch recon teams together (ch470) and force-march after each other (ch464).
- [[Kunze (Saxon officer)]] - 1st Battalion commander.
- Kleist and Rommel (instruction unit officers, Rommel as a detachment commander) - he briefs Kleist in ch350 and gives Rommel wide latitude in ch422.

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch350: rushes into the command post during the Paris campaign and briefs Kleist.
- ch422: promoted to 2nd Battalion commander. ch429: holds a position on a dirt road against two modified Armored Knights using RAK 15 guns.
- ch458-460: scouts forward, takes the third Romanian position below the pass, holds the Hill 1066 command post and is selected for the Forward Assault Group.
- ch464: force-marches his battalion on news of a blockage. ch470: sends recon teams to learn the enemy's plan.

---

## Status at ch473

- Alive, commanding the 2nd Battalion in the Romanian Carpathian campaign.
