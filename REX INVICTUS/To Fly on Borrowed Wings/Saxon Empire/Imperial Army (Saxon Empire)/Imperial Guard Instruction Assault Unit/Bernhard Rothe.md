## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Sergeant Major and acting commander of the Instruction Assault Battalion's Battalion Flame Platoon (ch246), a platoon of sixty, many former professional firemen drawn from First Army Group engineer units. Tall, dark-skinned. Saxon.

---

## Background

Pre-war life not stated beyond his unit being firemen-heavy.

---

## Personality

Disciplined (sharp salute) and eager; "excited" to present the new equipment (ch246). Competent briefer.

---

## Abilities

Fmw14 flamethrower expert: two-man weapon, thickened Glory Crystal Crude Oil, about 35 kg, about 12 m range, twenty bursts per fuel load (ch246). Gives [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] a demonstration.

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - battalion commander; receives the demonstration (ch246).
- Flame Platoon soldiers - commands them as acting platoon leader.

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch246: presents the newly issued flamethrowers to [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]; [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] sees them as ideal for trench and street fighting.
- Later flamethrower use appears (e.g. ch281), but [[Bernhard Rothe]] is not named there.
