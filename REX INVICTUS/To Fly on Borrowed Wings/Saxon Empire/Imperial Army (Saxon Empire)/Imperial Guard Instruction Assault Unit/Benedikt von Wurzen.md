## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Walk-on officer on the command staff of the new assault battalion commanded by [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] (ch123).
- Junker noble ("von" family), transferred from an original Guard unit into the newly formed unit; his previous post is not stated (his introduction is cut off at "Previously served as...").
- Introduced alongside [[Ulrich von Elsterau]], [[Valentin von Hohenfels]], Erich von Manstein (formerly Junior Staff Officer, Operations Department, Army General Staff) and the Executive Officer Ewald von Kleist. Exact rank and role in the battalion: not stated.

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - his new battalion commander. Like the other transferees, he was reluctant to leave his Guard unit for an uncertain new formation, but admiration replaced doubt once he saw the First Class Iron Cross on the commander's chest (ch123).
- [[Ulrich von Elsterau]] and [[Valentin von Hohenfels]] - fellow transferred Junker officers introduced in the same scene (ch123).
