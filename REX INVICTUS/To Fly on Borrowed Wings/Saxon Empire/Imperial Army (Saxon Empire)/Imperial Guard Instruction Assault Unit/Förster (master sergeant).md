## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

---

## Background

- Not stated. Called a veteran.

---

## Personality

- Observant, unhurried and trusted: stays seated smoking while others dismiss the plane, tracking it with his eyes (ch436).
- Brusque with subordinates: kicks a dozing machine gunner awake and tells him to get ready without explanation.

---

## Abilities

- Experienced reading of battlefield cues. Commands light machine gun crews.

---

## Relationships

- 3rd Platoon second lieutenant - consults him and acts on his judgment (ch436).
- Manfred von Richthofen (real) - the low-flying pilot whose repeated pointing northwest [[Förster (master sergeant)]] correctly reads as an alarm.

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch436: Recognizes the pilot's repeated low passes and pointing as a warning about the two blocked Serbian infantry divisions to the northwest, puts his men on alert and has the platoon report upward. His reaction is typical of the unit's veterans across several positions.
