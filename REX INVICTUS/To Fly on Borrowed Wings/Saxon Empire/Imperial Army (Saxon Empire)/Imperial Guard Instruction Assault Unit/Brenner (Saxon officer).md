## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Commander of 4th Company (ch217-231), later commander of the mountain-trained 3rd Battalion (ch422), Major commanding 3rd Battalion of the Deep Strike Group (ch460-471). Saxon.

---

## Background

- His battalion's backbone comes from Bavarian mountain jaeger units (ch446). Personal origin not stated.

---

## Personality

- Steady, disciplined, "nice guy" in manner but rigorous (ch446); embodies [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s emphasis on troop discipline (ch462).
- Decisive under pressure: after a landslide blocks the road he abandons vehicles and heavy gear and says "the time has come for the Colonel and the Empire to test us" (ch462).

---

## Abilities

- Mountain warfare and forced marching: 12 km in 1 h 32 min with about 900 men and two basic ammunition loads (ch464); leaves behind 170 mm mortars.

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - commander; takes his 1st Company to scout Predeal Pass (ch446-447); relays his artillery warnings by messenger (ch422-424).
- Rommel (real historical person) - his company relieves him at the pass (ch466).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch217: supervises 5th Reserve Division digging. ch221: told to hold the breach with 4th Company and battalion HQ. ch231: holds the South Bank bridgehead with zero fatalities.
- ch422-424: attacks the corps supply point.
- ch461-466: convoy spearhead; forced march; builds defenses on high ground at the valley exit; holds against three Romanian waves with fixed bayonets until relieved.

---

## Status at ch473

- Alive; Major, 3rd Battalion, after holding the Prahova Valley exit.
