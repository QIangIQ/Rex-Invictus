## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Second Lieutenant and 3rd Platoon Leader of 1st Company (ch80-98), classmate of [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] from the Central Military Academy. Later commander of 3rd Company, 1st Battalion, Instruction Unit (ch321). Ch86 calls him "2nd Platoon Leader", which the roster treats as a translation inconsistency.

---

## Background

- Same academy cohort and reserve-battalion posting as [[Hellwig (Saxon officer)]] and [[Voigt (Saxon officer)]] (ch80).

---

## Personality

- Eager and "keen for action" (ch93); speaks up in agreement with peers (ch86, 89). Meek amid the veterans' blood aura (ch80).
- Thoughtful when given a task: leads the company reserve and tunnelling work without fuss (ch90, 93).

---

## Abilities

- Platoon and company command; leads digging parallels and reserve charges (ch90, 93). Plays on the Instruction Unit football team (ch332).

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - former classmate who becomes his superior and role model.
- [[Voigt (Saxon officer)]] and [[Hellwig (Saxon officer)]] - fellow platoon leaders and later company commanders.
- [[Anton Kessler]] - veteran company sergeant major who lectures him and the others (ch86).
