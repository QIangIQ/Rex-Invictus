## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Commander of the 3rd Company, Instruction Assault Battalion, under [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] (ch172 onward). Saxon.
- Charleroi: commands the south/southeast sector from a three-story observation post (ch172). Amiens: supervises 5th Division digging (ch217), then flanks the breach with 3rd Company (ch221) and fights in South Amiens (ch231-232).

## Background

Not stated.

---

## Personality

- Cautious about his men's lives: refuses to use a half-platoon as bait ("I cannot gamble with my soldiers' lives", ch172).
- Candid in reports, shows fear and astonishment (ch176); a little proud, quick to answer "Hmph. They have horses; we have trucks" (ch172). Runs over eagerly when the battalion commander arrives (ch178).

---

## Abilities

Company-level infantry command, uses colored signal flares agreed with the battalion (red = powerful enemy, ch172), grenade-led urban assault (ch232).

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - battalion commander; trusts his judgement and receives orders by loudspeaker or direct command (ch176, 217, 231-232).
- [[Kunze (Saxon officer)]], [[Anton Kessler]], [[Brenner (Saxon officer)]] - fellow company commanders (ch217, 221, 231).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch172: Gallic Dragoons threaten a village half-platoon; sends 2nd Platoon out to receive them without engaging.
- ch176: reports over forty casualties from the cavalry fight and that the Cuirassiers shrug off bullets.
- ch178: returns with 46 unbreached cuirasses.
- ch217: sent with 3rd Company to the 5th Division digging.
- ch221: with [[Kunze (Saxon officer)]] stabs the left and right flanks of the Britannian breach.
- ch231-232: ordered to take a four-story red brick building and told to bypass the sniper-covered plaza.
