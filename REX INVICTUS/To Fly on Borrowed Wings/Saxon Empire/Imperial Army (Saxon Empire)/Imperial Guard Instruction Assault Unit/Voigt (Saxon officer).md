## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Academy classmate of [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]. 1st Platoon Leader, 1st Company, 32nd Zwickau Infantry Regiment (ch80-93); 1st Platoon Leader in the Instruction Assault Battalion (ch161-264); commander of 1st Company, 1st Battalion from ch321 (ch354-370 on the Paris raid); a company commander again in ch422.
- Conflict: the notes also give 1st Company command to [[Anton Kessler]] from ch422.

---

## Background

- Veteran of the Aragon campaign. The name is a football-player joke (the Christmas match uses similar names). Earlier life: not stated.

---

## Personality

- Takes after [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s leadership from the front (ch93); decisive, orders grenades at once; acts "like a farmer herding ducks" when loading trucks (ch355).
- Goalkeeper in the Christmas football match (ch332).

---

## Abilities

- Platoon and company tactics, assault teams; leads a "soldier king" company with the highest tactical level in the regiment (ch354-355).
