## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- 16-year veteran NCO, "a family heirloom" (ch7). Rank and post over time:
  - Platoon Sergeant, 3rd Platoon (ch7); acting Company Sergeant Major (ch66).
  - Sergeant First Class and Company Sergeant Major, 1st Company (ch80); Iron Cross 2nd class (ch83).
  - Acting commander of 1st Company, Instruction Assault Battalion (ch149), later called Company Commander (ch178, ch217-232). One slip calls him "4th Company Commander" (ch152).
  - Sent to Potsdam Military Academy for a three-month course (ch296); Second Lieutenant (ch320); adjutant of 1st Battalion (ch355).
  - Commander of 1st Company, 1st Battalion, after refusing a deputy battalion post (ch422); Captain (ch457).
- Saxon. A different officer, [[Lieutenant Pfeil]] ([[Lieutenant Pfeil]]), appears as an executive officer in ch459-460.

---

## Background

- Family man with a wife and children at home (ch103, ch301). Veteran of the Aragon campaign. Earlier life: not stated.

---

## Personality

- Stern, steady, near-blind faith in [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] after the first review (ch16, ch30: "my eyes have truly been opened today").
- Strict "father" of the company; fond of political debates (ch86); weeps for a hometown friend killed (ch72) and at his Iron Cross (ch83).
- Blunt and loyal: "If we didn't kill them, we would be the ones who died" (ch196). Offers to lead the assault in [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s place (ch89); is kept as the company's ballast.
- Refuses a safe staff post to stay at the front (ch422).

---

## Abilities

- Veteran infantry leadership, heavy machine guns, trench and barricade defense; mines the Amiens street with Magic Crystal Bombs (ch227). Enhanced senses and strength after the Donnerkrieger serum (ch457).

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - most trusted NCO and company commander ("closest to the protagonist among company commanders", ch444).
- [[Matthias Hollerbach]] - fellow veteran of the 3rd Platoon; reports at Charleroi (ch180).
- [[Voigt (Saxon officer)]] - 1st Platoon leader under him; the notes both give 1st Company command to [[Voigt (Saxon officer)]] in ch354-370 and to this character from ch422.
- [[Kunze (Saxon officer)]] - battalion commander at the Paris raid (ch355, refuses this character's request to join).
