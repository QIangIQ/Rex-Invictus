## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Head of the Empire's largest radiant (brilliant) crystal supplier and refiner, with mines and refineries overseas; annual sales about 0.9 to 1 billion Marks (ch112). The conglomerate, called "United Industries" in various spellings, absorbs real firms such as Daimler, Benz, Borgward and Demag.
- Age 35, a widow ("mysterious widow", ch292); former mechanical designer with her own studio (ch109). Saxon, belongs to the Court Junker camp (ch379) but avoids taking sides when the royals court her (ch295).
- Styles: Madam, "Big Sister" or "Sister", "Brilliant / [[Therese von Falkenstein]]".
- First seen in ch40 as a telegram that enrages Mackensen; the unnamed noblewoman who asked Mackensen to shelter [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] (ch4) is probably her but never confirmed.

---

## Background

- Close friend of [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s late mother, "like a sister"; after his parents died she supported and watched over him (ch103-104, ch240), indulged his playboy years, and gave him a watch before the war (ch217). Physical description: cold gray-blue eyes, honey-platinum hair in a chignon, vetiver scent (ch103). Family details beyond widowhood: not stated.

---

## Personality

- Composed and teasing in public, with warmth hidden "like light beneath ice" (ch103).
- Ruthless negotiator (ruins a Gallic merchant, ch107) and formidable organiser: gets lathes and a site for Helga Schmeisser in a day (ch121), reorganises supply to the Balkans in five days (ch443).
- Jealous and possessive: reacts to the "Dearest" in a letter to Helga (ch240-241); insists on dressing his wound herself ("I know clearly how many moles you have", ch299).
- Practical about risk: vetoes naming a company after him, since a silent majority stake is safer for a Junker (ch128).

---

## Abilities

- Not a spellcaster. Skills: mechanical design, finance, patent strategy, vehicle development (leaf-spring trucks, tracked and half-tracked vehicles, ch203-204, ch299). Gives a custom Krieghoff over-under shotgun as a farewell gift (ch320).

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - guardian "big sister", lover from ch299-300 ("I will wait for you to come back, forever", ch328). Open secret in upper echelons (ch441). Falkenhayn lists her among his ties (ch417).
- [[Magdalena (Falkenstein head maid)]] - head maid and secretary.
- [[Valdés (Falkenstein butler)]] - butler and convoy chief (ch328).
- Helga Schmeisser - lives at her manor; [[Therese von Falkenstein]] buys the Schmeisser workshop for the protagonist (ch118-119).
- [[Eugenia von Seeckt]] - funds her research "on the protagonist's tab"; encourages [[Eugenia von Seeckt]] and Helga's friendship (ch243).
- Mackensen - cross-pressure over the protagonist (ch40).
