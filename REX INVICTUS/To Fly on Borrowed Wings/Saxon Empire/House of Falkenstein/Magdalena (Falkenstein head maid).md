**[[Magdalena (Falkenstein head maid)]]** is a character in [[To Fly on Borrowed Wings]], placed under the [[House of Falkenstein]] of the [[Saxon Empire]].

Known in the story as:

- Magdalena
- Head Maid Magdalena
- Head Maid Lena

## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Head Maid of Falkenstein Manor and private secretary to [[Therese von Falkenstein]], handling daily clerical work and scheduling (ch111). Highest in the maid hierarchy. Saxon.
- Later takes on business liaison work for the industrial group: manufacturer liaison (ch443) and acts as driver and courier (ch442).

---

## Background

- Middle-class origin with a good education (ch111). Described as an elegant, well-dressed middle-aged lady (ch240). Family not stated. In ch118 the text calls a head maid "Lena" escorting a guest, probably the same woman or another head maid, unresolved.

---

## Personality

- Discreet, perceptive and quietly amused. Smiles knowingly and withdraws when [[Therese von Falkenstein]] and [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] want privacy (ch111-112, 320). Watches scenes with glittering eyes (ch114) and snickers at [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s hunger (ch385).
- Courteous and generous: gives the post office postman Brendel a food basket (ch240). Efficient and steady in a crisis (ch299, 442).
- Calls the protagonist "Young Master" followed by his personal name (ch299, 442).

---

## Abilities

- Secretarial and logistics skills: drafts the Schmeisser Arms acquisition contract and gets daily necessities (ch119), contacts [[Eugenia von Seeckt]] on request (ch241), drives a car (ch442), carries folders into executive meetings (ch443).

---

## Relationships

- [[Therese von Falkenstein]] - employer, trusted aide who is "inseparable" from her (ch299). Serves her baths, breakfast and coats (ch300).
- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - feeds and equips him on returns to the manor (ch386), delivers his farewell shotgun box (ch320), wakes [[Therese von Falkenstein]] for his telegram (ch442).
- [[Eugenia von Seeckt]] - contacted for the letter handoff (ch241).
- Manor guard veterans (disabled instruction unit men such as [[Stahl (manor guard)]] and [[Knorr (manor guard)]]) - share the household (ch443).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch111: introduced; accompanies [[Therese von Falkenstein]] home. ch119: runs the acquisition paperwork. ch240-241: receives [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s letters. ch299-300: attends [[Therese von Falkenstein]] the morning after his return. ch320: brings the shotgun box at the farewell dinner. ch385-386: awaits him with a lantern and orders extra food. ch442-443: delivers his telegram and runs liaison with Borgward and other manufacturers for the Romanian vehicle drive.

## Status at ch473

- Alive, still [[Therese von Falkenstein]]'s head maid, secretary and liaison.
