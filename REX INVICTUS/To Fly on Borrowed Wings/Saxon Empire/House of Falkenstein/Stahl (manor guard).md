**[[Stahl (manor guard)]]** is a character in [[To Fly on Borrowed Wings]], placed under the [[House of Falkenstein]] of the [[Saxon Empire]].

---

## Hierarchical Placement

- [[To Fly on Borrowed Wings]]
    - [[Saxon Empire]]
        - [[House of Falkenstein]]
            - [[Stahl (manor guard)]]

---

## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Disabled veteran of the instruction unit, hired by [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] as one of the head bodyguards of the Falkenstein manor (ch443). First appears ch443.

---

## Background

- Left hand crippled (ch443). Has a family; his wartime disability pension shrank as prices rose, so the manor job gave him needed income (ch443).

---

## Personality

- Deeply grateful and loyal to [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] and [[Therese von Falkenstein]] for looking after him and his family; text rates his loyalty on par with or above active-duty instruction unit men. Does his security work flawlessly; the job is within his comfort zone (ch443).

---

## Abilities

- Trained the manor bodyguards "to the bone" with [[Knorr (manor guard)]], giving them skills ordinary bodyguards lack (ch443). Bodyguards also act as test shooters for the manor's weapons inventor.

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - employer and benefactor who hired him.
- [[Therese von Falkenstein]] - mistress of the manor, whom he is loyal to.
- [[Knorr (manor guard)]] - companion; they discuss opening a security company after the war (ch443).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch443: after [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] leaves, he and [[Knorr (manor guard)]] train the manor guards hard; he chats about founding a professional security firm postwar.
- The in-book name matches that of a corporal in ch31 ([[Corporal Rinke]]); the text does not state they are the same man.

---

## Status at ch473

Still head bodyguard at the manor as of ch443; later not stated.
