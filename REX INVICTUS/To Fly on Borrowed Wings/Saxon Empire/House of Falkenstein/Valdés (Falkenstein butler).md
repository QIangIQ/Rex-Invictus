## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Middle-aged butler and convoy chief in the service of [[Therese von Falkenstein]], who delivers her supply convoy to [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s front (ch328).

## Personality

- Courteous and cheerful, invites [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] to just call him by his name; addresses him as "Young Master" plus [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s name in a playful tone (ch328).
- Careful with secrets: gives [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] the key and operating instructions for the locked box with the confidential items (ch328).
