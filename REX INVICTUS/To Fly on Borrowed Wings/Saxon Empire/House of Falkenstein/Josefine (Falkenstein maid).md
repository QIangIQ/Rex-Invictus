## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Maid in the household of [[Therese von Falkenstein]] at the Falkenstein manor. First appears ch105. A maid by this name is not otherwise located in the text; the roster notes a head maid/aide of [[Therese von Falkenstein]] who calls [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] "[[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]" (ch442-443), but the text does not equate the two.
