## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Head of the State Secret Patent Review Department, Imperial Patent Office (ch110). Lifelong official; has handled almost all state-secret patent compensation (ch110). White-haired but energetic elder.

---

## Background

Not stated beyond a career spent in the Patent Office.

---

## Personality

- Courteous (serves [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] black tea himself, ch110), proud of his experience, cheerful and quick to laugh (ch122), technically sharp: immediately questions the reliability of the mechanical fuse and is drawn to the new Arcane Spark Detonation Fuse (ch122).

---

## Abilities

Technical patent validation and compensation negotiation; reads blueprints with reading glasses.

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - the inventor he evaluates; offers him twenty million Imperial Marks for his state-secret patent (ch110), reviews his updated fuse blueprint (ch122), compensation paid in five installments (first arrives ch133).
- Crown Prince Georg - his instructions led the Patent Office to expect [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] (ch110).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch110: introduces himself and offers 20 million Marks, saying all past state-secret patent compensation combined is less; notes the Patent Office was informed in advance.
- ch122: reviews the Arcane Spark Detonation Fuse; says the Arcane Institute must be negotiated with.
- ch133: first installment of compensation arrives.
