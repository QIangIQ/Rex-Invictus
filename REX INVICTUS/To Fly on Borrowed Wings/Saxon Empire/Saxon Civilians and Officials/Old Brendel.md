## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

Veteran postman of the Central Post Office in Dresden, Saxon Empire; gray-haired, with over thirty years on his route (ch240).

---

## Background

Long-serving civilian carrier who knows every street of his route; war has raised prices and reduced civilian food.

---

## Personality

Sharp-eyed, reliable, discreet, curious but not prying. Delighted and grateful for the gift basket (ch240).

---

## Abilities

Drives a Radiant Crystal-powered post vehicle; trusted by his supervisor with sensitive mail.

---

## Relationships

- Post office supervisor - trusts him most; reduces his other duties for the delivery.
- [[Magdalena (Falkenstein head maid)]] - [[Therese von Falkenstein]]'s head maid; receives the letters and gives him a food basket.
- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - sender of the three letters; Brendel never meets him.
- [[Therese von Falkenstein]] - lady of the manor; Brendel never learns who owns it.

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch240: Notices three finely sealed letters from [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] addressed to three different women at one suburban manor; delivers them personally, passes guard checks and a carriage ride, hands them to [[Magdalena (Falkenstein head maid)]], and goes home with sausage, bread and salted meat.
