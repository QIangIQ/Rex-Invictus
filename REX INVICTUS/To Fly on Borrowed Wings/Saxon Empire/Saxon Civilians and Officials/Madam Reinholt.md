## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Widow of Captain [[Emil Reinholt]], living in Zwickau with her son [[Benno Reinholt]]. Civilian.

---

## Background

- Gave her husband a new pocket watch before he left for war (ch10). First name not stated.

---

## Personality

- Dignified under grief: wears plain black, weary and sorrowful, holds back tears, then weeps while clutching the watches and apologizes for it (ch105). Proud, declining further help: "we cannot impose on you and the military any further" (ch106).

## Relationships

- [[Emil Reinholt]] - late husband.

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - visits to return her husband's wristwatch and pocket watch; she receives him with courtesy (ch105-106).
