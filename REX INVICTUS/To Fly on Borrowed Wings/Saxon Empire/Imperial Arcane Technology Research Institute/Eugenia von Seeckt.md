## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Genius researcher in arcane (magitech) technology at the Imperial Arcane Technology Research Institute near Dresden, with her own office. Not a spellcaster, so excluded from top projects (ch119, ch242, ch412).
- Daughter of General Seeckt, Chief of Staff of an Army Group (ch239, ch381, ch386); sister of [[Konrad von Seeckt]]. Age 20 in ch119.
- Appearance: blonde, tsundere manner; eye colour contradictory (emerald green ch108, ch121; sapphire blue ch242).

### Real History (IRL)

- The real Hans von Seeckt had no daughter (only a stepson), so this family tie is invented.

---

## Background

- Junker noble family. Publicly rejected the original [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s confession at a ball (ch40, ch107, ch115). Described as the "apple of the family's eye" and an "arrogant eldest miss" (ch75, ch299). Earlier history: not stated.

---

## Personality

- Proud "like a peacock" (ch108); keeps up pretences, such as claiming she is resting her feet while joining a technical discussion (ch117).
- Tsundere: calls [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] "that idiot", signs notes "Don't break it" (ch328, ch242); leaves her lab early to fetch his letter (ch242).
- Moved by honest reasoning: affected by "practice is the sole criterion for testing truth" (ch120). Rolls her eyes at court politics (ch120).
- Mild jealousy over Helga Schmeisser (ch116-117).

---

## Abilities

- Explains the Arcane Spark synchronized detonation that completes the shaped-charge fuse (ch117, ch122). Researches "magical ripple" wireless and miniature crystal oscillators (ch121, ch242).
- Builds the Portable Magitech Communication Device: main unit plus four receivers, 5 km range, 15-minute battery, only a mage can drive it (ch328).
- Papers on mana waves and an optimal Shield model (ch412), praised by Röntgen (ch412, ch417). Lends grimoires (ch275). Proposes a joint company (ch386).
