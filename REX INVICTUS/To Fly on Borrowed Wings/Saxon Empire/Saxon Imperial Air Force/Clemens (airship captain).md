**[[Clemens (airship captain)]]** is a character in [[To Fly on Borrowed Wings]], placed under the [[Saxon Imperial Air Force]] of the [[Saxon Empire]].

Known in the story as:

- Clemens
- Lieutenant Colonel Clemens
- Lt. Col. Clemens

## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Lieutenant Colonel, captain of the Saxon Imperial Air Force L15 Armored Transport Assault Airship, "Lilienthal" class (320 m, 12 engines, 8 MG08 turrets, 6 Becker 20mm turrets, spell shields; a makeshift tactical-verification model) (ch450, ch470).
- Reports to [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s "Jagdfeld Kampfgruppe" in the Carpathians as an attached supporting asset (ch450). Executive Officer: Grigor; Chief Gunnery Officer: Lieutenant Gray (ch452).

---

## Background

- Air Force Academy graduate, one class below [[Walther Krell]], who sees him off (ch450). Otherwise not stated.

---

## Personality

- Formal and disciplined, crisp in reports (ch450, ch470). Cautious and tense under risk, cold sweat during the drop (ch458).
- Initially wary of [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s "radical" ideas, warned by [[Walther Krell]]; gradually trusts him implicitly (ch471).
- Professional and keen on research: writes many reports on armored airship use (ch472); candid about the L15's weak firepower (ch470).

---

## Abilities

- Airship command, aerial spotting and logistic lift; carries 1,800 t payload; dropping explosive satchels from the cargo bay (ch470).

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - his tactical commander in the Kampfgruppe; asks the L15 to spot, carry supplies, and air-drop the commander with Armored Knights (ch450); later asked to carry two artillery regiments (ch472).
- [[Walther Krell]] - academy senior and mentor figure (ch450, ch458).
- Grigor (executive officer) - second in command (ch470).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch450: arrives at Predeal staging; learns of the airdrop plan.
- ch452-453: nervous before the airdrop; L15 suppresses ground targets.
- ch458: drops [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] and Armored Knights behind the Romanian line at Predeal; remarks on a Kampfgruppe commander personally jumping.
- ch470-471: bombs Romanian positions with machine guns, autocannons and satchels from 300 tons of captured explosives; locates and hits a division headquarters.
- ch472-473: returns to the rear base to fetch artillery regiments, agreeing to carry two regiments plus supplies.

---

## Status at ch473

- Alive, captain of L15, leaving to ferry artillery regiments.
