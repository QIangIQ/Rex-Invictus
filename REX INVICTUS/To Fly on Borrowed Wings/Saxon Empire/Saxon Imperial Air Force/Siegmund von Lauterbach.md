## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Captain of the Saxon Armored Airship L24, "one of the earliest Saxon Armored Airship Captains" (ch220). L24 is an older, smaller ship with twelve 150mm single-barrel naval cannons, flying at high altitude to screen the heavier L29 at Amiens (ch219).
- Not to be confused with a different officer of the same surname, a battalion commander from ch430 onward (a separate character, [[Major Danneberg]]).

---

## Background

- Transferred from the Navy fourteen years earlier as First Officer of the L24; later trained and was promoted to captain; keeps "the peculiar obsession of a naval officer" (ch221).

---

## Personality

- Calm, decisive under attack: orders interdiction fire and calls the L29 for support (ch220).
- Deeply sentimental about his ship ("his second home"), selfless toward the crew, and loyal to the Empire; stays at the helm after ordering all to bail out (ch221).

---

## Abilities

- Commands L24 with its special magic-reinforced shield, Arcane Tracking tech and 150mm cannons (ch220).

---

## Relationships

- [[Walther Krell]] - captain of the L29 airship; L24 requests his fire support, which destroys four Highland Mages (ch220).
- [[Ellis Whitcombe]] - leader of the Highland Mages whose Disintegrate and Delayed Blast Fireball cripple L24 (ch220).
- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - watches the L24 crash through binoculars and hears the captain's last cry; moved and further motivated (ch221).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch219-220: L24 detects eight Highland Mages; shield disintegrated and gas envelope torn open.
- ch221: orders crew to bail out, steers the burning ship into the Britannian Magic Crystal Cannon positions in South Amiens, shouting "Long live the Saxon Empire!" over the speakers. The crash destroys many cannons and soldiers and the Saxons charge.
