## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Pilot of a Dove Monoplane reconnaissance aircraft, Saxon Air Force, on the Creil front. First and apparently only appearance ch257.

---

## Background

Not stated; had flown the plane in rear training fields but this is his first real battlefield flight (ch257).

---

## Personality

- Nervous but disciplined; keeps the plane steady for his observer, shouts orders in the heat of the moment (ch257).

---

## Abilities

- Flies a Dove Monoplane, which needs runways; it appears on the Creil front only after temporary airfields were built.

---

## Relationships

- [[Bruno (observer)]] - observer and partner; they trade glances and grin with relief (ch257).

## Lore & Key Events

- ch257: scouts Gallic positions along no-man's-land; when a Gallic monoplane appears, orders [[Bruno (observer)]] to return fire in a comic dogfight, then breaks off with the photos taken. The text notes the incident might become an anecdote in future history books.
