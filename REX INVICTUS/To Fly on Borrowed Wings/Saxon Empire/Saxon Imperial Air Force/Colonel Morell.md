## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Colonel; captain of Armored Airship No. L28 (ch286-287, ch289). Gender is never stated.
- Previously coordinated with [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] on the air strike against the Liege Fortress (ch286).

---

## Background

Not stated beyond the earlier Liege cooperation.

---

## Personality

Nervous before results, then exultant: awaits the bombing "nervously" and roars excitedly as the second batch lands (ch286-287). Safety-minded, warning the whole crew by broadcast to hold on to fixed objects against the shockwave (ch286).

---

## Abilities

Commands L28, which climbs to 6,400 m and drops Radiant Crystal Reaction Shells from its bomb bay; coordinates by radio with L29 and the armored train (ch286).

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - coordinated the Liege strike with him; L28 is later asked to keep bombing the fallen Eiffel Tower (ch289).
- [[Walther Krell]] - captain of the sister ship L29; the two captains split roles over Paris (ch286).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch280: L28 hovers over Paris awaiting resupply.
- ch286: L28 climbs to bomb and interfere; first batch of three shells released.
- ch287: second batch dropped as an enemy mage spends mana on defence; Eiffel Tower assault succeeds.
- ch289: L28 continues bombing the fallen tower on request.
