## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Captain of Armored Airship L29, Saxon Imperial Air Force (ch220-236); Colonel by ch263. Later a retired air hero who works on theory (ch450). Saxon.

---

## Background

- Air Force Academy graduate; senior of [[Clemens (airship captain)]] (ch450-452). Fought the Eiffel Tower at Paris, was blasted by the Tower Spirit's chain lightning, then retired to theory (ch450).

---

## Personality

- Bold, calm and a fan of air-ground integration: orders L29 to descend to minimum safe altitude to rescue [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] (ch227-228). Warm and humorous ("You're awake, Mr. Lunatic", ch230). Fiery in crisis (ch274, 288). Warns that [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] is radical "but not a madman, at least not yet" (ch450).

---

## Abilities

- Commands L29 with eight engines, 203 mm guns and magic shield (ch220, 227, 263).

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - rescued him at Amiens, shares ideas of a "forward air fortress" and radio (ch231), lends radio and loudspeaker (ch231-232), promised a visit to Dresden for black beer (ch232).
- [[Clemens (airship captain)]] - junior from the Academy.
- Manstein (real historical person) - contacted him by radio to arrange the rescue (ch230).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch220: orders anti-air fire on Mages. ch227-228: rope-ladder rescue at Amiens. ch236: aerial fire support. ch263-264: green-signal attack on Gallic lines. ch274-288: Paris, rams L29 toward the Eiffel Tower to buy time for "Gungnir" (ch288).

---

## Status at ch473

- Alive; retired from command, advising on air theory.
