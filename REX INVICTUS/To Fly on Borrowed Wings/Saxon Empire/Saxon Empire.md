**[[Saxon Empire]]** is a faction hub of [[To Fly on Borrowed Wings]].

Germany analogue of Europa: a dual-monarchy federal state under the House of Wettin, capital Dresden, imperial seat Sanssouci. A militaristic Junker state that relies on magitech more than on its 392 mages (92% of them 3rd circle or lower). Won the 1870-71 war with Gaul and took Alsace-Lorraine.
