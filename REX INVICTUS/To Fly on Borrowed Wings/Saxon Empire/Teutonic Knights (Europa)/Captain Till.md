## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Captain and Armored Knight pilot of the Teutonic Knights, lead machine of a trio of 'Siegfried I' Armored Knights escorting a Saxon infantry battalion (ch248). Saxon.

---

## Background

- Not stated. Described by his squadron commander as one of his best pilots (ch249).

---

## Personality

- Calm and steady on the external amplifier; cautious, warning the Major that they are far from the nearest allied unit and support would be slow (ch248).
- Responds crisply to orders ("No problem at all", "Received! We're moving!").

---

## Abilities

- Pilots a Siegfried I Armored Knight armed with an armor-piercing war hammer or lance using the shaped-charge head. Leads his two wingmen in a standard three-point assault formation. Squad-level communication is a known weakness (ch248).

---

## Relationships

- Unnamed Saxon Major - infantry commander he covers and takes orders from (ch248).
- [[Ruhland (Teutonic squadron commander)]] - his squadron commander, who mourns him (ch249).
- [[Colonel Reginald]] - the Britannian Ranger Squadron commander whose ambush kills him (ch249).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch248: Covers the Saxon search for a suspected enemy company, then advances into the woods against an Armored Knight that vanishes from his detectors.
- ch249: Ambushed by Ranger Squadron 'Hound Type 4' machines using nets, hammers and shortswords on joints. All three Siegfrieds are crippled and the pilots killed. His cockpit is pried open; the remains are unrecognizable.
