## Personality

- A "typical Knight-type officer" who values the Knights' honor and camaraderie above his own life (ch249).
- Hot-tempered at first (roars "Scoundrels!", slams his fist on the wreck), then regains composure and analyzes the scene methodically.

---

## Abilities

- Pilots an Armored Knight and commands a squadron. Sharp forensic reasoning: counts spent shaped-charge shells against the numbers and deduces the Britannians took an intact weapon set (ch249).

---

## Relationships

- [[Captain Till]] - subordinate and one of his best pilots, killed in the ambush (ch249).
- [[Leona von Fortis]] and General Mackensen - he reports his conclusion upward (roster note, ch249).
- Britannian Garter Knights - the rivals the shaped-charge weapon gives the Teutonic Knights an edge over (ch249).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch249: Rallies the squadron and a nearby infantry regiment after the ambush news, finds the three wrecked Siegfrieds, deduces the capture of an intact Shaped Charge Armor-Piercing Weapon and recognizes the strategic danger, then reports it.
