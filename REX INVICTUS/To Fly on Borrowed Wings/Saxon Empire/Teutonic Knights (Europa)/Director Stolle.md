## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Director of the Teutonic Knights' Armored Knight R&D and Manufacturing Department (ch307); "responsible for the order's Armored Knight design" (ch445). Introduced as a middle-aged person in gold-rimmed glasses with a manager's manner.

---

## Background

Not stated. Evaluated and followed up [[Konrad von Seeckt]]'s dual-pilot control report (ch307).

---

## Personality

- Shrewd, prudent, quickly enthused: calls the dual-seat idea of a noble knight as a "tractor" blasphemous, then lights up at the "Artillery Type" concept, seeing "a door to a new world" (ch308-309).
- Data-minded; demands precise numbers and presents firing-test data soberly (ch308-309).
- Honest about limits: admits the workshop cannot do artillery and bought assault cannons from Krupp (ch309); in ch446 argues the Order must drop duelling pride because Saxon knights lag Britannia's Garter knights in basic magitech.
- Pushes glasses up when thinking; speaks faster as ideas build (ch446).

---

## Abilities

Armored Knight design and manufacturing management; leads reverse analysis of captured Britannian machines (70 percent complete at ch445).

---

## Relationships

- [[Leona von Fortis]] - Grand Master; [[Director Stolle]] reports to her and she orders development in the new direction (ch445-446).
- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - treats him as a fellow enthusiast; adopts spaced and composite armor and shaped-charge tactics from him (ch308, ch446).
- Master Staudinger (Master Magitech Technician) - close co-director; the two finish each other's points (ch446).
- [[Konrad von Seeckt]] - author of the dual-pilot report [[Director Stolle]] assessed (ch307).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch307-309: meets [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]; dual-seat and Artillery Type design discussions; firing tests show shells above 850 m/s cannot be fully dodged.
- ch309: surprised when [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] asks to borrow knights for the instruction regiment.
- ch445-446: reverse-analyzes an elite Britannian Armored Knight (lighter, more mobile, shorter life); proposes high-mobility light knights with camouflage nets and large-caliber anti-armor artillery; ordered to begin R&D.
- ch452: mentioned as having discussed Feather Fall for knights with [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]].

---

## Status at ch473

Working on light, mobile Armored Knight development at the Marienburg base; alive.
