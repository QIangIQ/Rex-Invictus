- Leona
- Fortis
- Grand Master Fortis
- the Golden Lion

## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Grand Master of the Teutonic Knights, holding the rank of General (ch266). Styled "Grand Master Fortis" (restored from the alias list) and "Her Excellency".
- Appearance: about 1.9 m, muscular, golden hair, can bend gun barrels (ch381); eye colour inconsistent (ruby ch253, blue ch266, golden ch313, azure ch385). A text variant "Leonidas" appears in ch249.
- Unnamed in ch106, ch115 and ch207, where she destroys twelve Golden Fleece knights and kills their commander in a duel. Meets [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] in ch253.

### Real History (IRL)

- No real Grand Master of the Teutonic Order matches her. The order's real defeat at Tannenberg/Grunwald (1410) is part of her background.

---

## Background

- Her stubborn sense of honour rests on a deathbed promise to the previous Grand Master and on the Order's shattered honour at Tannenberg in 1410 (ch445-446). Family: not stated.

---

## Personality

- Dazzling, sharp-eyed and direct; smiles at [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] in ch253 while her gaze "seems to peer into the heart".
- Cold anger when faced with bad news (ch253); drives hard bargains (patent access in exchange for R&D participation, ch313).
- Open to evidence: agrees with [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s light-Knight plus camouflage plus large anti-armor artillery proposal (ch445-446).
- Embarrassed about the smell of the cramped cockpit (ch384).

---

## Abilities

- Elite Knight pilot: leads 30 Siegfrieds with 12 consecutive kills at Creil (ch258-259); pilots a gold "Golden Lion" Siegfried; limit about 850 m/s dodge at 400 m (ch309).
- Pilots the two-seat Valkyrie (Projekt Walkure) with [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] as Weapons Officer, through a mind link (ch383-385).

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - ally and admirer, listed among his women by Falkenhayn (ch417); lends Knights, grants patents.
- [[Konrad von Seeckt]] - subordinate Knight.
- Directors [[Director Stolle]] and Staudinger - R&D staff (ch445-446).
- Mackensen - superior at the front (ch253).
