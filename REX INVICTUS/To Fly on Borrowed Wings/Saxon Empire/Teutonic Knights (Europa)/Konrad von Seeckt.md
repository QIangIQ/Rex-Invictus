Known in the story as:

- Lieutenant Colonel Konrad
- Lord Konrad
- young von Seeckt

## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Lieutenant Colonel and Armored Knight pilot of the Teutonic Knight Order, Saxon noble of the von Seeckt family of Dresden. Rank stays Lieutenant Colonel throughout.
- Posts: Teutonic Knights squadron commander at Seville (ch35-65); squadron leader of the 22nd Armored Knight Squad (ch307); leads the three-Knight squad attached to [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s instruction unit (ch310-311, ch393); called a "newly minted ace" in ch438.
- Machine: Siegfried Mark 1 / Type 1, later a modified version. Call signs "Hercules" (ch398) and "Hawkeye" (ch414-415).
- Surname von Seeckt is given only from ch74-78; the name echoes a real general but he is not presented as historical.

### Real History (IRL)

- Borrows the surname of the real Hans von Seeckt (1866-1936), who had no son. In this story Konrad is presented as the general's son.

---

## Background

- Brother of [[Eugenia von Seeckt]] (called both younger sister and eldest miss in different chapters) and, by implication, son of the general of the same name. Physically modified for arcane affinity (ch119). Knew the original [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] from Stuttgart and Dresden noble parties as a "fallen playboy" (ch38, ch40). Other family details: not stated.

---

## Personality

- Handsome, talkative and elegant, with noble arrogance he sets aside: salutes [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] after the Seville rescue (ch38).
- Good-humoured gambler on talent: bets on [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] and "borrows" shells and artillerymen in a brigadier's name (ch44-45).
- Matchmaker and teaser: wants to pair his sister with [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] (ch75, ch108), later jokes he will stop it even as a ghost (ch341, ch452).
- Pent-up fighter, frustrated when left at headquarters (ch438); converts quickly to [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s doctrine (admits the protagonist was right, ch339-340).

---

## Abilities

- Armored Knight piloting; first man to use the shaped-charge weapon in combat (kills [[Colonel Bertram]], ch53). Later uses Armor-Piercing Lance and Hammer, spaced armor, MG08 shoulder guns (ch319), the communicator with two-way link (ch340).
