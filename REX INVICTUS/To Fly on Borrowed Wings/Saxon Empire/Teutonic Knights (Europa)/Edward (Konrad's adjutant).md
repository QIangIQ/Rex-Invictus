## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Saxon Teutonic Knights Armored Knight pilot, comrade and later adjutant of [[Konrad von Seeckt]]. The text spells the in-book name two ways (ch35-40 and ch60-63); both are the same role (the comrade at [[Konrad von Seeckt]]'s side).
- Pilots a Siegfried Type 1 with a battle-axe (ch60). Rank not stated.

---

## Background

- Not stated.

---

## Personality

- Anxious and less patient than [[Konrad von Seeckt]]: urges intervening before the infantry battalion is wiped out and admits he cannot be as calm (ch35).
- Fiercely loyal and hot-tempered in the end: eyes bloodshot, roars "Die! You damned spellcaster!" when his commander and comrades fall (ch61).

---

## Abilities

- Siegfried Type 1 Armored Knight with a battle-axe. Performs an uncapped charge and leap, reaching about the machine's own height, to punch High Mentor [[Ainsley (Highland High Mentor)]] out of the air (ch61).

---

## Relationships

- [[Konrad von Seeckt]] - commander and friend; calls him "Colonel" in battle (ch61). Follows his patient ambush plan (ch35-36).
- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - meets him on the bell tower in ch40 with a surprised look, then goes downstairs at [[Konrad von Seeckt]]'s word.
- [[Ainsley (Highland High Mentor)]] - the Britannian High Mentor who kills him.

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch35 - Watches the battle with [[Konrad von Seeckt]]; both launch their Armored Knights to ambush the Order of Garter.
- ch36-37 - Fights beside [[Konrad von Seeckt]] against the white Armored Knights.
- ch40 - Present at the cathedral bell tower and leaves when told.
- ch60 - Helps kill the last St. George Type 3 with his axe, severing an arm.
- ch61 - Shouts a warning when [[Ainsley (Highland High Mentor)]]'s force-field sword strikes; after the other knights fall he charges the mage and punches him from the sky.
- ch62-63 - Rampages among Britannian infantry; [[Ainsley (Highland High Mentor)]] kills him with a Sixth-Circle Disintegrate scroll, leaving only the machine's legs.
