## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- 5th-circle Transmutation mage of the Kingdom of Bulgaria, one of only nine combat mages in the kingdom; addressed as "Master [[Stoyan Marinov]]" (ch466).
- Serves at the Monastery of Saint Ivan of Rila, the command node of the southern defense line.

---

## Background

Studied at the Saxon Imperial Royal Spellcaster Academy (per the roster notes). Little else stated.

---

## Personality

- Unrefined for a mage: drinks from a canteen and wipes his mouth casually, lacking the elegance of mages from other nations (ch466).
- Reports carefully; admits he could not identify an opponent's school (ch466).

---

## Abilities

- Spells: Transmute Rock to Mud, Stoneskin, Mage Armor. Carries a Mauser pistol; his bullets bounce off the attackers' breastplates (ch466).
- Hidden clasped inner pockets with documents or items, searched after death (ch467).

---

## Relationships

- [[Rupert Ellingham]] - his killer at Rila (ch466-467), who paralyses him and shoots him with an enchanted bullet.
- Bulgarian army staff - a major reports to him (ch466).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch466: returns wounded (left sleeve burned) to the monastery HQ; attack begins, his Mauser fails against armor.
- ch467: held immobile by a spell and killed by an enchanted bullet that breaks his Mage Armor.
