## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Lieutenant Colonel, commander of 1st Battalion, 126th Regiment, 48th Brigade, 24th Infantry Division, Aragon Royal Army, stationed in Seville (ch22).

---

## Background

Northern nobleman who bought his rank hoping to gain credentials for after the war (ch22). Paid General Sanjurjo a large annual sum, so his misconduct was long tolerated (ch24).

---

## Personality

- Cowardly, opportunistic and depraved: preyed on civilians, abducted girls with fellow officers (ch21-22). Terrified when captured, wets himself, answers every question to save his life (ch22). Called a "parasite" and "idiot" by superiors (ch24).

---

## Abilities

None of note; carries a fine revolver (a Britannian-made sidearm) and officer ID and badge, later taken by [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] (ch25).

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - captor and killer (ch22-23).
- [[Matthias Hollerbach]] - helped capture and interrogate him.
- General Sanjurjo - superior who profited from his bribes and sent soldiers to fetch him (ch24).
- [[Ainsley (Highland High Mentor)]] - overhears the scandal with contempt (ch24).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch22: interrogated; reveals the 24th Division garrison, Britannian Northumberland Fusiliers (2,000-3,000) and a dozen mages; stabbed in the chest afterwards.
- ch24: his death-room is found; separating the bodies triggers a booby trap.
- ch25: [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] presents his ID, badge and revolver to [[Major Tobias]] as proof.
