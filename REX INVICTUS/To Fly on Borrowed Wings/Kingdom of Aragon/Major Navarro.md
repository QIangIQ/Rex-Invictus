## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Major, commander of the Magic Crystal Cannon Battalion of the Aragonese Kingdom Army, coordinating the artillery defense of San Lorenzo de la Parrilla (ch 91).
- Captured when [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] takes the town and forced to operate his six Magic Crystal Cannons for the Saxons (ch 96). Speaks Aragonese.

---

## Background

Not stated beyond his post. His battalion was deployed at San Lorenzo only days earlier; the cannons had been assembled a few days before and no firing table existed yet (ch 96).

---

## Personality

- Technical and candid: explains the limits of unguided Magic Crystal Cannons to a fellow major instead of bluffing (ch 91).
- Pragmatic rather than defiant: with Gew.98 rifles aimed at his gunners he sighs, says "Do as he says" and cooperates despite "deep doubts" (ch 96).

---

## Abilities

- Commands a battalion of six Magic Crystal Cannons firing Arcane Shrapnel Shells. Without a Mage observer, accurate fire needs long coordinate calculation and several test rounds (ch 91, 96).
- Notes that Mages used to fly up and guide fire by eye (ch 96).

---

## Relationships

- [[Major Íñigo]] - fellow major (infantry battalion commander); he tells him the cannons cannot hit the Saxon trenches without Mage guidance (ch 91); both are bracketed by the first Saxon salvo and he tries to drag him to cover.
- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - captor and new commander; he answers his questions on guidance and then relays gun-laying status over the field telephone ("30 seconds, sir!") (ch 96).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch 91: the Kingdom Army's only guidance Magic Apprentice and all Mages had been withdrawn to Madrid at Britannian pressure, leaving his guns blind. Saxon calibration shots bracket his position.
- ch 96: captured, surrenders command of the guns under threat; tells [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] the Mage-observer method; operates the cannons on his orders.
- ch 96-97: his captured guns, guided by [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] from the castle tower, shatter two Britannian battalions (see [[Colonel Cyril]]).
