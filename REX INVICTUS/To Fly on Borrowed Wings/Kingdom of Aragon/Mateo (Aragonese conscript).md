## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Aragonese conscript infantryman of the Kingdom Army, a one-chapter viewpoint figure (ch51). No rank, unit or surname stated.

---

## Background

- Farmer from the northern mountains of Aragon, unmarried. Conscripted about three months before ch51, taken from his field by a conscription squad, given an ill-fitting uniform and an old rifle with nearly worn-out rifling.
- Wanted only to return home, farm, and save to marry a girl from the neighboring village (her name appears once in the text; she is not tagged).

---

## Personality

- Unwilling soldier, terrified and instinctive: drops flat, charges for the nearest shelter, hammers on a nailed-shut door and smashes windows with his rifle butt (ch51). His last thoughts are of the wheat fields of home and his sweetheart.

---

## Abilities

- None of note. Old worn rifle.

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - never meets him; his fate results from the Saxon defensive net of house ambushes, MG08 fire and chimney grenades at Seville (ch51).
- General José Sanjurjo (real historical name) - commander of his division, who orders the doomed assault (ch51).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch51: During the Kingdom Army's first assault on Seville, officers promise an easy "armed parade". Ambushed on an open street, he finds ground-floor doors nailed shut, crowds into a building with a stripped chimney, survives a grenade barrage dropped down it, staggers out and is shot in the thigh. He loses consciousness murmuring his sweetheart's name.
