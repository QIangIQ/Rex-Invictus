## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Major, commander of the Kingdom Army Infantry Battalion holding San Lorenzo de la Parrilla, in a regiment led by a Lieutenant Colonel (32nd Infantry Regiment per ch94). Aragonese. Appears ch91, captured ch94.

---

## Background

- Not stated beyond his post. His garrison had lost its mage cannon-spotter when the Britannians pressed Aragon's High Command to pull all mages back to Madrid (ch91).

---

## Personality

- Blunt and anxious; skips pleasantries and thrusts his binoculars at his colleague, demanding the guns flatten the unexpected enemy trenches (ch91). Gets a throbbing headache when told it cannot be done quickly.

---

## Abilities

- Conventional infantry command; no magic. Binoculars.

---

## Relationships

- [[Major Navarro]] - fellow Major, commander of the Magic Crystal Cannon Battalion; they argue over whether the cannons can destroy the trenches and are dragged to cover together when the shelling begins (ch91). Both end up prisoners (ch94, ch96).
- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - his attacker; the trenches [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s company built stun the defenders (ch91), and the town falls with him among the captured (ch94).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch91: Spots the Saxon trenches at dawn, summons the artillery major, and is caught in the 7:00 AM bracketing and salvo of twelve 77mm guns.
- ch94: Counted among the captured senior officers (regimental commander, infantry battalion commander, cannon battalion major) after the town is taken.

---

## Status at ch473

- Prisoner since ch94; later fate not stated.
