## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Role: group of three young girls held in a Seville building and rescued by [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] and his team (ch23). Treated as one tag; individuals are unnamed.
- Faction: Aragon civilians. Nationality: Aragon.

---

## Background

Not stated beyond being very young, held captive in a two-story building in Seville during the civil war. Their families are alive and are later escorted out too (ch25).

---

## Personality

- Terrified after the ordeal, they shrink back from the soldiers (ch23).
- One, slightly bolder, speaks for the group: she asks if the soldiers are the National Army or the foreign International Brigades, then asks if [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] is a good person; she tells him he looks fierce but must be good people. She turns back at the end and manages a smile (ch23).

---

## Abilities

None.

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - rescuer. He refuses the label 'good person', saying he cannot stand to see such things and must live up to the Red Scarf and Party Badge he once wore; his odd earnest reply calms them. He sends them home with instructions to go with their families to safety (ch23).
- [[Matthias Hollerbach]] - silent, imposing corporal in the rescue team who stands behind [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]; the girls cannot be understood by him as he does not follow their language (ch23).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch23: Rescued from a booby-trapped building after [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] kills the Royal Army Lieutenant Colonel [[Rodrigo de Ayala]]; guided through alleys using the system map to a safe district and sent home.
- ch25: Their families' escape route is made unusually smooth and safe by the team's actions.
