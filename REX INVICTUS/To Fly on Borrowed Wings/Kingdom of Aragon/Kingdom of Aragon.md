**[[Kingdom of Aragon]]** is a faction hub of [[To Fly on Borrowed Wings]].

Spain analogue in civil war: the Britannian-backed Royal Army against the Saxon-backed National Army and International Brigades, fought over a vast Luminite (brilliant crystal) vein. Seville is the opening battlefield.
