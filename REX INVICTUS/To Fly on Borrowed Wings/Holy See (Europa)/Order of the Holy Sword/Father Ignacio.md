## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Priest of the Order of the Holy Sword and head of the Papal Neutral Purification Unit in Paris (ch356, 366).

---

## Background

Not stated.

---

## Personality

- Burly, unshaven, fierce-looking; to [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] he resembles a docks enforcer (ch356). Blunt and sardonic ("drag out those rats hiding in the gutters", ch356), but surprisingly kind to a weeping teenage necromancer, jokingly offering him an apprentice deacon post (ch366-367). Decisive: orders highest combat readiness at once when anomalies emerge (ch367).

---

## Abilities

Heavy white full-body plate, dog-faced bascinet, greatsword; also a one-handed war hammer and a rifle (ch356, 366). Commands Knights of the Holy Sword and armed deacons.

---

## Relationships

- [[Archbishop Anselme]] (Archbishop of Paris) - close partner, shares perceptions of rising negative energy (ch366-367).
- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - first meeting ch356 via Major Nicolai; later cooperates against the Paris underground horror.
- Major Nicolai - liaison who introduces them (ch356).
