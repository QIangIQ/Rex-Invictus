## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Unnamed Tier-1 necromancer apprentice, about fifteen or sixteen years old, caught by the Holy See's purification unit in the ruins of Paris's Left Bank (ch366). Comic-relief figure.
- Nationality: not stated; he speaks Gallic and wandered the villages of Gaul. Faction: no allegiance; a wandering apprentice.

---

## Background

- Sole apprentice of a Tier-2 "hedge mage" in his seventies, with whom he wandered Gaul learning magic (ch366).
- After the Battle of Paris, news of the negative energy outburst reached Necromancy School mages across Europa. His teacher brought him into the city to "join the grand event" with other necromancers.
- A few days before capture, his teacher was torn apart by undead that burst out of the ground while collecting materials. Family and origin: not stated.

---

## Personality

- Timid and easily terrified: bursts into tears on seeing [[Father Ignacio]]'s fierce face, sobbing "Don't burn me to death... I'm not a heretic" (ch366).
- Raised on his teacher's "subtle influence" to believe the Holy See burns necromancers for fun; he hid like a rat and scavenged spoiled food until hunger drove him out (ch366).
- Meager vocabulary, honest, and forthcoming once reassured (ch367).

---

## Abilities

- Necromancy, only just at the Tier-1 threshold. Tattered black mage robe. No notable gear.

---

## Relationships

- [[Archbishop Anselme]] - the Archbishop calms him with a handkerchief and gentle questioning, promises he will be escorted out of Paris and recommended to a regular mage organization (ch366).
- [[Father Ignacio]] - frightens him at first, then jokingly offers him a place as apprentice deacon with better meals (ch367).
- His dead teacher - unnamed Tier-2 hedge mage; killed by undead in Paris (ch366).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch366: Captured in the Left Bank ruins after a multi-day Holy See hunt; revealed to be a harmless apprentice.
- ch367: Spared and offered a place. Reports that other necromancers he met said a treasure and the inheritance of an ancient Necromancy School archmage lie in the Paris catacombs, and that negative energy is rising rapidly. This alarms the Archbishop and the Holy Sword priest.
