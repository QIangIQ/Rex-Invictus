## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

Deacon of the Paris Archdiocese, a burly man nearly two meters tall who is usually in charge of the clergy's combat training. Serves under [[Archbishop Anselme]]. Gaulish (Gallic) churchman. Appears only in ch283.

---

## Background

Not stated beyond his role as combat trainer of the armed clergy.

---

## Personality

Brave and physically aggressive: in the Night of the Dead he charges in a wedge with the elite Armed Priests to stop the undead (ch283).

---

## Abilities

Heavy flail, half-plate armor. Strong enough that his blow tears open his own hand, yet only mangles a flesh construct's leg (ch283). No spellcasting shown.

---

## Relationships

- [[Archbishop Anselme]] - his archbishop; recognizes him as the combat-training priest and cries out his name when he falls (ch283).
- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - fights on the same side; [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] calls in 77mm field guns after seeing [[Thibault (deacon)]]'s failure (ch283).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch283: Leads the armed priests against a giant flesh construct, strikes its leg with his flail, is swatted away, his armor caved in, and he spits blood and crashes to the ground, fate unknown. His fall convinces the clergy that manpower is no match and prompts the call for artillery.
