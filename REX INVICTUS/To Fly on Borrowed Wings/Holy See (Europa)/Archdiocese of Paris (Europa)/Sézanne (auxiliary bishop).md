## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Younger auxiliary bishop of the Paris Archdiocese under [[Archbishop Anselme]] (ch277). Fights at the front with armed clergy (ch282, 285).

---

## Background

- Gallic cleric; earlier history not stated.

---

## Personality

- Pious, indignant and brave. Furious at the desecration of resting souls when necromancy is unleashed in Paris (ch277); more emotional than his archbishop, who must calm him. Practical in battle, personally knocking down a ghoul with a heavy crozier (ch285).

---

## Abilities

- Senses necromantic and negative energy (ch277, 285); armoured and plated, with a heavy crozier. Uses Holy Water and Holy Oil to bless guns and bullets.

---

## Relationships

- [[Archbishop Anselme]] - his archbishop; [[Sézanne (auxiliary bishop)]] acts on his order and shares his views.
- [[Detlef von Raschke]] - Saxon company commander whom he reinforces (ch282, 285).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch277: With the archbishop on the bell tower, senses massive necromancy from the Eiffel Tower. ch282: Brings Holy Water and Oil from Notre-Dame to [[Detlef von Raschke]]'s Fourth Company, blesses weapons and soldiers and forms a barricade with the armed priests. ch285: Fights ghouls; senses the necromancy intensify.
