## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Archbishop of the Paris Archdiocese, appointed personally by the Pope; over sixty, lean, with sharp eyes (ch277, 281). Papal States / Holy See.
- Ch277-289: temporary advisor and ally of the Saxon First Army Group during the Battle of Paris. Ch366-367: still in Paris and sends word to the Saxons about rising negative energy.

### Real History (IRL)

- The real Archbishop of Paris in 1914 was Cardinal Léon-Adolphe Amette.

---

## Background

- Studied for many years in his youth in the Holy See's Department of Mysteries in Rome. Not a spellcaster and has no divine arts, but is unusually sensitive to necromantic "blasphemous" power (ch277). Notre-Dame has no Papal spellcasters.
- Originally did not want to interfere in a war between mortal nations (ch277).

---

## Personality

- Direct and serious: "getting straight to the point" with Mackensen (ch279). Fully armoured and carrying a heavy war-hammer crozier that makes a truck sink (ch281).
- Shows a rare kind smile, "like a helpful old man next door", when [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] speaks Gaulish to him (ch281). Fights at the front, roars orders and shields technicians with his own men (ch282, 287-288). Stays grave even after victory (ch282, 289). Comforts a frightened teenage necromancer kindly (ch367).

---

## Abilities

- Detects necromantic energy; martial skill with a hammer-like crozier and heavy armour; commands armed priests with consecrated Berthier M1892 rifles, chainmail, half-plate, kite shields, flails and war hammers from the Notre-Dame armoury (ch277).

---

## Relationships

- Mackensen (real historical figure) - allies at ch279: he asks for help and in turn is asked to get the Holy See to condemn the Gallic Republic publicly (ch279). Advises him in the night command tent (ch285).
- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - fights beside him and seals the catacomb entrance (ch281-282); they later break into Paris together (ch356).
- [[Father Ignacio]] - a Father of the Holy Sword order, fellow churchman who shares his alarm over necromancy (ch366-367).
- [[Sézanne (auxiliary bishop)]] - subordinate, with him in ch277 and ch282.

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch277: senses the necromantic surge, opens the hidden armoury and consecrates weapons. ch279: meets Mackensen and pledges aid. ch281-282: rides out with [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]], smashes skeletons and helps seal the catacombs. ch283: charges a flesh construct.
- ch285-288: rides the armoured train "Odin" with armed priests and protects the Gungnir technicians at Bir-Hakeim Bridge. ch289: says Paris is a dead city and the Church should endorse the action.
- ch366-367: senses new negative-energy anomalies, receives a teenage necromancer and warns the Saxons.
