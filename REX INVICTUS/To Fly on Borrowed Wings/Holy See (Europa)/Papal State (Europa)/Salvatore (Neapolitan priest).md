## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

Priest and leader of the guard detail beneath the Turbolo Chapel (Santa Maria la Nova Church), Naples, Papal State. Has served there exactly seventeen years (ch455).

---

## Background

Knows what lies in the stone sarcophagus (the 【Heart】) and has read the records of its 440 years of sealing.

---

## Personality

Cautious, grave, disciplined; his tone does not relax even when the seal proves intact (ch455). Orders triple guard strength.

---

## Abilities

Commands four Church Knights in white plate with MP14 submachine guns and MG14 machine guns. Personal abilities not stated.

---

## Relationships

- [[Matteo (Neapolitan priest)]] - younger partner priest; [[Salvatore (Neapolitan priest)]] directs him to report to the Vatican and mobilize more guards (ch455).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch455: Writes the prayer log when the chamber ward flickers (as the vampire transformation begins elsewhere); inspects the sarcophagus with the knights, finds the seal intact but the fluctuation real, orders a Vatican report, highest alert and threefold guard until the Holy See's special envoy arrives. Notes it is the first ward fluctuation in 440 years.
