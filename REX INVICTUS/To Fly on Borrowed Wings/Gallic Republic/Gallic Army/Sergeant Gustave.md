**[[Sergeant Gustave]]** is a character in [[To Fly on Borrowed Wings]], placed under the [[Gallic Army]] of the [[Gallic Republic]].

## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Gallic sergeant, a native of Paris or its surroundings, retreating with the army when Paris is abandoned (ch270).

---

## Background

- Family (wife, children, parents) lives in Paris, according to the soldiers around him (ch270). Otherwise not stated.

---

## Personality

- Passionate, patriotic and persuasive: he is red-eyed and bandaged but stops a fleeing group and shouts that they are soldiers of the Republic whose sacred duty is to defend home and country (ch270).

---

## Abilities

- Rifle; arm bandaged; ragged uniform. Rank gives him the standing to rally others.

---

## Relationships

- Leads fellow Gallic soldiers, one a young soldier who first questions him. His action is a small counter-current against the rout after the General and government left (ch270).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch270: After the officers flee, he rallies a group of retreating soldiers to return and die on the barricades of Paris; they answer "For the homeland!" and the group grows (about a third of the retreating men, per the roster notes).
