## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Captain commanding the First Squadron of Gallic Dragoons, under [[Émile Castagnet]] in the cavalry force at Charleroi (ch169-175).

---

## Background

- Not stated.

---

## Personality

- Cautious and observant: before the city assault he says the silence is wrong and wonders why the Saxons left no sentry (ch169). Hesitant about reckless orders; doubts the reconnaissance plan because civilians describe fierce firepower (ch170). Loyal and obedient once a plan is chosen (ch173).

---

## Abilities

- Dragoon squadron with lances (ch174); skilled horseman.

---

## Relationships

- [[Émile Castagnet]] - his commander; [[Fontenaille (Gallic captain)]] agrees to send a dispatch rider with the civilians' report (ch170) and is sent to maneuver along the riverbank (ch173).
- [[Lemercier (Gallic captain)]] - fellow captain with whom he links up on the riverbank (ch174).
- [[Colonel Duvernay]] - his superior, remembered after death as proud and reckless (ch175).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch169: Suspects a trap at the silent city. ch170: Proposes caution. ch173-174: Rides to the riverbank to link with [[Lemercier (Gallic captain)]] and charge the Saxon flank; both squadrons are hit by artillery as they prepare to charge. ch175: The cavalry is routed with heavy losses; he tells [[Émile Castagnet]] that [[Colonel Duvernay]] is dead.

Alive at ch175; later fate not stated.
