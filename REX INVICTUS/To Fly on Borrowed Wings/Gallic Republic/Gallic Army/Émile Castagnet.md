## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Cavalry Captain, commander of the Second Squadron, 11th Dragoon Regiment, Third Cavalry Division (ch 169).
- After [[Colonel Duvernay]] dies he becomes commander of the surviving cavalry and leads the retreat and report (ch 175).

---

## Background

Not stated. The division is one of the Gallic Army's elite, with about 5,400 cavalrymen (ch 169).

---

## Personality

- Disciplined and caring for his men and horses: orders them to feed horses and check saddlery in ten minutes (ch 169).
- Eager for revenge on the Saxons at first, but cautious and sensible: suggests horse artillery fire before any charge (ch 170); realizes the "combat reconnaissance" is suicide when he hears automatic fire (ch 172).
- Learns quickly: "we didn't lose; we ran into an iron wall" (ch 175).

---

## Abilities

- Leads Dragoons with Berthier carbines, lance, saber and revolver (ch 169). Tactically careful: reconnoiters, fights from the woods, keeps a copse for cover (ch 171-172).

---

## Relationships

- [[Colonel Duvernay]] - overbearing superior who ignores his warnings (ch 170).
- [[Fontenaille (Gallic captain)]] and [[Lemercier (Gallic captain)]] - fellow squadron captains, whom he gathers after the rout (ch 175).
- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - opponent whose defenses wreck his command.

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch 169: marches on Charleroi at night.
- ch 170: objects to the charge plan and is overruled.
- ch 173-174: attacks the village and is caught in the Saxon artillery barrage.
- ch 175: second squadron loses about one-third; he sends a dispatch rider to the Division Commander with the first accurate account of Charleroi's firepower, leading the Division to recommend infantry and artillery, not cavalry.

---

## Status at ch473

Alive as of ch 175; later fate not stated.
