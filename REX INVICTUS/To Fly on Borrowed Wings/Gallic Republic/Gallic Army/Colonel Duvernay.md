## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Colonel, commander of the Gallic 4th Cuirassier Regiment (three squadrons, about 450 men) in the Third Cavalry Division, under Lanrezac's army group (ch 170, 175).

---

## Background

Not stated. His ornate cuirass bears his family crest (ch 170-174).

---

## Personality

- Arrogant and scornful: laughs off civilian reports of a Saxon division with ten-plus heavy machine guns as "cowards scared senseless by gunfire" (ch 170).
- Dismissive of caution: rebukes [[Émile Castagnet]] with "Are you scared by those farmers' stories?" and "we are Gallic cavalry... not timid rabbits" (ch 170).
- Stocky with a thick upturned mustache, black warhorse; theatrical, drawing his saber to shout "Let the Saxons taste the might of our Cuirasses!" (ch 170).
- Personally brave to the end, charging on foot (ch 173-174).

---

## Abilities

- Saber, revolver and an Enchanted Cuirass that flashes blue and absorbs several hits before failing (ch 174).

---

## Relationships

- [[Émile Castagnet]] - subordinate Dragoon captain whose warnings he overrides.
- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - opponent (via the Charleroi defense) who destroys his regiment.
- [[Fontenaille (Gallic captain)]] and [[Lemercier (Gallic captain)]] - Dragoon squadron commanders in the same operation.

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch 170: orders a "Combat Reconnaissance" with a cavalry charge.
- ch 172: told by a scout that the enemy is retreating (a ruse), he leads the First Squadron's charge with 150 Cuirassiers.
- ch 173: his horse falls in the fire; he rolls clear and leads the survivors on foot.
- ch 174: reaches the Saxon position and kills two defenders, then is shot down by submachine guns: "For... Gaul."
- ch 175: the regiment suffers over 50 percent casualties, horses nearly all dead. The division commander curses him as "a complete and utter idiot".

Dead (ch 174).
