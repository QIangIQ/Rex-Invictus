## Personality

- More optimistic and credulous than [[Fontenaille (Gallic captain)]]: guesses the Saxons have withdrawn after a feint (ch169), is astonished and skeptical at a civilian's report of a division (ch170). Defers to [[Émile Castagnet]]'s plan (ch170). Despairs after the rout (ch175).

## Abilities

- Dragoon squadron with lances; handles the riverbank maneuver (ch171).

---

## Relationships

- [[Émile Castagnet]] - commanding officer.
- [[Fontenaille (Gallic captain)]] - fellow squadron captain, with whom he links up (ch174-175).

## Lore & Key Events

- ch169-170: Speculates the Saxons are gone; backs the plan. ch171: Leaves with his squadron for the riverbank. ch174: Links up with the other squadron, and both are hit by artillery. ch175: Says "We lost", unable to believe that six elite squadrons were routed without reaching the Saxon main position.
