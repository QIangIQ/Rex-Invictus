## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- General, commander of the Gallic Third Cavalry Division. Appears unnamed after the Charleroi cavalry disaster (ch 175, where he curses [[Colonel Duvernay]]) and is named in ch 192.

### Real History (IRL)

- The real French 3rd Cavalry Division was commanded by Dor de Lastours in 1914.

---

## Background

Not stated. Holds the "Offensive Doctrine" and the belief that Gallic cavalry beat the Saxons in an earlier war (ch 193).

---

## Personality

- Disdainful: calls the Saxon First Imperial Guards Cavalry Division "the Emperor's parade unit" and the Saxons "clumsy imitators" (ch 192-193).
- Driven by wounded pride after Charleroi; sees the encounter as a chance to redeem the division's honor (ch 192).
- Predictable and doctrinaire: plans a simple massed frontal charge (ch 193); mocks dismounted skirmishing as "obsolete" (ch 193).
- Shows tactical competence once, countering a Saxon lancer flank with bicycle infantry and machine guns (ch 193), but panics at the trap (ch 194).

---

## Abilities

- Commands about 5,400 men: Dragoons, Cuirassiers, Hussars, horse artillery, Bicycle Battalion, machine gun platoons (ch 169, 193).

---

## Relationships

- General Ernst von Hoeppner - Saxon opponent, who baits him with a feigned retreat (ch 193-194).
- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - his Charleroi defeat motivates the engagement; [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s warning reaches Hoeppner (ch 190-191).
- [[Émile Castagnet]] - surviving squadron commander from the earlier disaster.

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch 175: hears the Charleroi report and recommends no more cavalry attacks.
- ch 192: ordered to protect the Ninth Division's flank; takes the bait of the Saxon cavalry.
- ch 193-194: orders the main charge; the Saxons "retreat" into prepared heavy machine guns, and he watches "utterly dumbfounded", muttering "Something's wrong".
- ch 195: Saxon Lancers circle behind and destroy the horse artillery; the division breaks. ch 199 records the division completely crippled.
