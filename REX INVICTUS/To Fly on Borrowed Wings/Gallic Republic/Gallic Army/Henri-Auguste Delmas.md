## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- General, commander of the Gallic Ninth Infantry Division, Third Corps: 16,000 men, two brigades, 12 battalions, a 75mm Magic Guided Artillery Regiment of 36 guns and a Dragoon squadron (ch 180-181).
- Assaults Charleroi against the Instruction Assault Battalion led by [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]].

---

## Background

Not stated. A committed believer in the Gallic "Offensive Doctrine" (ch 181, 189).

---

## Personality

- Booming voice; colleagues say he could be an opera singer (ch 181).
- Overconfident and doctrinaire: expects a "fair and square" field battle, thinks hiding in a city is "the style of colonial rebels", and blames the cavalry's losses on arrogance (ch 181).
- Boasts "I want to have lunch in Charleroi's City Hall before noon" (ch 181).
- Responds to failure by deciding "offensive strength was insufficient", not that the tactics were flawed (ch 182); rages at "cowardly" snipers and traps (ch 189).
- Capable of doubt and humanity: first questions the Offensive Doctrine after the second wave (ch 189) and orders recovery of the dead despite the risk (ch 190).

---

## Abilities

- Rides a warhorse; commands massed bayonet assaults, plus 12 guns of the artillery regiment sent into the city (ch 185).

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - unseen opponent whose defense destroys his division.
- Chief of Staff and staff officers (unnamed) - feed his optimism, then report the losses (ch 182, 189).
- [[Colonel Duvernay]] and [[Émile Castagnet]] - Gallic cavalry whose earlier defeat at Charleroi he dismisses (ch 181).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch 181: orders the assault after saturation fire.
- ch 182: the first four battalions (4,000 men, over 80 percent of officers lost) vanish into the city; he sends a second wave of eight battalions.
- ch 185: sends 12 guns into the urban fight.
- ch 189: second wave returns under 5,000 of 8,000+; about 3,900 casualties; field hospital collapses; he orders all offensives halted.
- ch 190: troops sickened by the stench of unburied dead; he suspends attacks and awaits reinforcements from Corps. ch 199 records the Ninth Division routed by the L29 airship.
