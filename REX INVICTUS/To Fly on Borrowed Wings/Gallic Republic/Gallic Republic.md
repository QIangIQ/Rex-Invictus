**[[Gallic Republic]]** is a faction hub of [[To Fly on Borrowed Wings]].

France analogue. Rivals Britannia over Africa and holds Gibraltar and North Africa. Its mage order is the Eye of the Loire (525 mages). Paris becomes a Domain of the Dead; Gaul signs the Sedan armistice on 6 May 1915.
