**[[Lucien (Gallic Sentinel)]]** is a character in [[To Fly on Borrowed Wings]], placed under the [[Sentinel Project]] of the [[Gallic Republic]].

Known in the story as:

- Lucien
- Commander Lucien
- Captain Lucien

## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Commander (called Captain in ch272) of the remaining 17 "Sentinels" in Paris; a Sixth-Circle Evocation-specialist mage. The Sentinels are dark-gold-masked Battlemages selected one in ten thousand, physically modified, created by the Loire River Eye Mage Corps (ch270).

---

## Background

- Survived the Creil Line fighting (8 of 25 Sentinels lost). The main Sentinel body is fighting in the south and headquarters staff left for Bordeaux (ch270). Earlier life not stated.

---

## Personality

- Powerfully built and commanding; his voice silences a angry room (ch270). Level-headed: asks what dying at the gates would actually achieve, and proposes rallying the retreated soldiers (ch270).
- Dutiful and respectful to Master Eiffel, saluting him; steps into the pool first without hesitation (ch272).

---

## Abilities

- Sixth-Circle Evocation, physical modification, potions and Magic Crystal reserves, combat suit and sabre-type close weapons used by Sentinels.

---

## Relationships

- Gustave Eiffel - his "Master"; [[Lucien (Gallic Sentinel)]] and his men accept Eiffel's plan to fuse into the tower (ch270-272).
- The other 16 Sentinels - his subordinates, whom he calls "gentlemen" (ch270).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch270 - Presides over the Paris command room after the government abandons the city; summoned to the Eiffel Tower.
- ch272 - Leads his men to the tower, hears the fusion plan, and is the first into the Magic Core pool.
- ch273 - His soul is part of the tower's collective will, with many more souls than his unit.

---

## Status at ch473

- Dead as an individual; consciousness merged into the Eiffel Tower, which fell at the end of ch288.
