## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Tier-5 Necromancy mage, aged 92 (ch369). Saxon-born. Former senior researcher at the Imperial Royal Academy and one of Emperor Albert II's early "enlightenment" tutors; author of the General Magic textbook that Fritz Haber studied (ch361, 363).
- Officially killed in an accident; Section III always suspected he had defected (ch361). Found by [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s team as Chief Technical Advisor and General Director of the Gallic "Sentinel Project" in the Paris underground research institute (ch360-364).

---

## Background

- His account (ch363-366): the Magical Research Association blacklisted him because his report on spreading magical affinity to ordinary people via alchemical potions and magitech circuit implantation threatened mage privilege. Saxon agents then tried to kill him at the border and the "Eye of the Loire" Mage Order captured him in anti-magic shackles. Given the choice of being drained for experiments or serving the Gauls, he served (ch364).
- Says he gave Chardonnet the idea of turning the Paris Catacombs into a "negative energy battery" and improved the project by combining necromancy with biological modification (ch364).
- Truth (ch370): Britannian intelligence had recruited him, promising a hereditary noble title and ten times better treatment if he brought the Sentinel core technology to Britannia. His plan was a staged laboratory accident plus Feign Death, then retrieval by Highland Mages.

---

## Personality

- Arrogant, leaning on his bone staff and walking ahead without regard for [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] (ch363). Cold, mocking, resentful of Saxony ("returning to that country that once betrayed him", ch368). Hoarse chuckle, rhetorical questions ("what would you choose?", ch364).
- A skilled actor: plays "victim" and "collaborator" until the chaos of battle (ch368). Mocks the Saxon field manual: offense is everything, retreat is "attacking in another direction" (ch365). Lied about the modification potion's lack of side effects (dependency, 7-10 day cycle, ch371).

---

## Abilities

- Tier-5 Necromancy: Dominate Person (ch364), Vampiric Touch, Negative Energy Flood and Ray of Enfeeblement (ch368). Bone staff with a green gem. Feign Death prepared (ch370).
- Created the second-generation "Sentinel" modification potion (no surgery; short-term human enhancement) and the "underlying logic" implanted in the Sentinels (ch363-366, 370).

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - enemy turned captive turned betrayer; offers a deal (protection for research and three potions, ch365), then strikes him with Negative Energy Flood (ch368). Killed by his slap (ch369).
- Fritz Haber (real historical person) - studied his textbook; later analyses his potions (ch361, 371).
- Section III operatives - two assigned to "support" him are killed by his Vampiric Touch (ch368).
- Britannian intelligence / Highland Mages - his secret patrons, whose retrieval force the Sentinels rout (ch370).
- Gallic Republic / "Eye of the Loire" Mage Order - captors and employers (ch364).
- [[Laurence Fenwick]] - Britannian Coldstream commander killed in the same testing area (ch365).
