**[[To Fly on Borrowed Wings]]** is the author's new fic and its story section in the [[Rex Invictus]] vault: a [[Demigod]] cadet of the [[UOE]]'s [[The Citadel]] wakes in 1914 in the body of a Saxon second lieutenant, in a Europa of renamed nations, D&D-style magic and magitech Armored Knights. The draft covers chapters 1-473 (Seville, February 1914, to the siege of Bucharest, May 1915).
