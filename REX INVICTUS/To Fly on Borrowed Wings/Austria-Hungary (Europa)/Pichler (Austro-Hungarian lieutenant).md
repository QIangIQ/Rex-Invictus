### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

## Background

- Not stated beyond serving two years with [[Kovách (Austro-Hungarian officer)]]; had been in Dresden, spending time at the opera house (ch394).

---

## Personality

- Short-sighted, resentful of Saxon arrogance, and naive: grumbles about pressing hot faces against cold backsides for a mere colonel, and has never heard of [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s titles (ch394).
- Excitable and loyal; bursts in red-faced with news and shakes his superior by the shoulders (ch405).
