**[[Austria-Hungary (Europa)]]** is a faction hub of [[To Fly on Borrowed Wings]].

The Saxon Empire's weak ally. The Vienna coup of 17 March 1915 is followed by Conrad's counter-rebellion, which abolishes the 1867 Compromise.
