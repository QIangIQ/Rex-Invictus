## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Major, then Lieutenant Colonel by ch447; Austro-Hungarian rail-transport liaison stationed by Vienna (ch393), later liaison to four mountain regiments (ch449). The name appears as [[Kovách (Austro-Hungarian officer)]] (ch393-405) and [[Kovách (Austro-Hungarian officer)]] (ch447-449).

## Personality

- Smoothly self-serving, leaning on a pillar with a cigarette (ch394); embarrassed and obsequious later (ch447-448).
- Panics when the error is discovered: disheveled, slumped, picturing a firing squad (ch405).
- Acts like a "broadcasting machine" for news (ch449).

---

## Abilities

Railway coordination; supplies trains to the Saxons.

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - supplies his regiment HQ train and sends it to Vienna by error, then becomes his liaison (ch393-449). [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] reaches for his pistol on seeing him again (ch447).
- His adjutant [[Pichler (Austro-Hungarian lieutenant)]] - looked down on by the adjutant's own noble pride (ch394).
