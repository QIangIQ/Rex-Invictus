## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Colonel, commander of the Austro-Hungarian 2nd Kaiserjäger Regiment, in his forties, face tanned and rough from mountain wind (ch448). Serves on the right flank under [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] (then Colonel, commanding the combined force) in the Carpathian mountain campaign against the Kingdom of Romania (ch448-454).

---

## Background

Career mountain-warfare officer ("fought mountain warfare for half his life", ch454). Long experience fighting Romanian Home Defense Brigades along the Carpathians (ch448). Family not stated.

---

## Personality

- Stiffly formal, ramrod straight; honest and conscientious. Arrives to "plead guilty" for the failed right-flank attack, driven by Austro-Hungarian custom of apologizing to a superior (ch448).
- Literal-minded: told why he did not mention the demolition tunnels at the meeting, answers "Commander... you didn't ask" (ch448).
- Sincere and proud of his men: boasts of the regiment's top shooting scores (ch448); thanks [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] for not ordering a continued attack (ch448).
- Notes that previous superiors would not have called a halt even in hail (ch449) and decides to "seriously listen" to [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] (ch449).

---

## Abilities

Expert mountain infantry commander; knows Romanian terrain and tactics; explains pre-dug mountain demolition tunnels ("makeshift airplanes", Tihuta Pass tunnel of 12 tons) (ch448). Pistol, binoculars (ch453).

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - superior; respect grows from his non-punitive questioning (ch448-449) to awe at the pass assault (ch454).
- [[Kovách (Austro-Hungarian officer)]] - explains Austro-Hungarian customs to [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] (ch448).
- Commander of the 27th Mountain Infantry Regiment - peer; shares the view that the Saxon is clearer-minded (ch449).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch448: reports on the failed right-flank attack; reveals mountain blasting tunnels; confirms defenders are Romanian Home Defense Brigade mountain troops; 2nd and 3rd Companies still intact.
- ch449: watches [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] halt the operation in the storm; resolves to follow him.
- ch452-453: pushes regiment forward after the Saxon charge; orders the whole regiment to accelerate and take the pass alongside the Saxons.
- ch454: stands shocked at [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s bloodless-style pass tactic.

## Status at ch473

Alive and serving with the allied force after ch454; later fate not stated.
