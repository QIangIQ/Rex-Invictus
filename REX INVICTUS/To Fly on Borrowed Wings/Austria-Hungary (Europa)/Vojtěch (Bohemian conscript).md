## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

Young conscript of Czech descent from Central Bohemia, drafted less than a year before, serving in the coup forces' infantry attacking Hofburg Palace in Vienna (ch402).

---

## Background

Czech from Central Bohemia; conscripted barely a year earlier. Calls for "Mother" as he dies.

---

## Personality

Terrified, but more afraid of the officers behind him, who shoot retreating men on the spot (ch402). Obeys and runs forward numbly.

---

## Abilities

Mannlicher rifle. No special skills.

---

## Relationships

- Coup officers - feared more than the enemy; their shooting of retreaters drives him forward.
- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s side (instruction unit and Imperial Guard) - the enemy whose fire kills him (indirectly).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch402: Charges across the Hofburg plaza, is hit in the back by mortar-shell fragments, collapses bleeding and dies, one of many young corpses on the plaza.
