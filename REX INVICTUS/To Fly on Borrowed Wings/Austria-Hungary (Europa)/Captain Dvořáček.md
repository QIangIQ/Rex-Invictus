**[[Captain Dvořáček]]** is a character in [[To Fly on Borrowed Wings]], placed under the [[Austria-Hungary (Europa)]] of the [[Austria-Hungary (Europa)]].

## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Czech/Bohemian captain, 2nd Platoon, 1st Company, 2nd Battalion, 35th Infantry Regiment; ordered to control Vienna Westbahnhof during the coup (ch396). Nationality Bohemian in the Austro-Hungarian Army.

---

## Background

Not stated beyond his reputation in the 35th Regiment, where fellow officers say he is famous for looting; he is said to have made a fortune at the station (ch397).

---

## Personality

- Greedy and undisciplined; his men are loot-minded and have lost watch discipline (ch397).
- Arrogant until confronted, then cowardly: scrambles behind a pillar when the attack begins, begs by invoking the Geneva Conventions, and pours out everything in fluent Saxon once threatened (ch396).

---

## Abilities

Carries a Gasser M1898 revolver (standard Austro-Hungarian officer sidearm), later confiscated.

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - captor; interrogates him with a Russian roulette game using Simon's own revolver, with several empty clicks (ch396).
- [[Detlef von Raschke]] - takes down his name, rank and unit at the start of the interrogation (ch396).
- 35th Regiment staff - mock him as useless and lose contact with his detachment (ch397).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch396: station seized by the instruction unit; Simon interrogated, reveals the coup timetable, which units hold which areas, and that nearly a regiment is involved.
- ch397: his regiment loses contact and sends more troops to the station.

## Status at ch473

Captured at the Westbahnhof; later fate not stated.
