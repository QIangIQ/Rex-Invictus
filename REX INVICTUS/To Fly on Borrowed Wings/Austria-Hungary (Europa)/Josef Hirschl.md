## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Lieutenant Colonel, commander of the Austro-Hungarian 79th Infantry Regiment (Fifth Army). Leads more than a thousand routed men into the Saxon lines at the 4th Battalion position (ch 431).

---

## Background

Viennese; his ancestors fought alongside the Habsburgs "to conquer the land". Speaks fairly fluent Saxon (ch 431-432). Described as an elegant Vienna society type (ch 437 notes).

---

## Personality

- Proud but pragmatic: accepts disarming and Saxon reorganization "for the lives of the soldiers" (ch 431).
- Shaken by the Vukodlak ("monsters taller than bears... eyes red"), insisting earnestly "you really must believe me, my friend!" (ch 431).
- Honor-bound: stung by a Saxon second lieutenant's tirade that he is a coward, he picks up a rifle and orders his men to block the enemy (ch 432).
- Fiery in action: personally fires an MG14 and curses in Saxon (ch 437).

---

## Abilities

- Commands a polyglot regiment, relayed through officers in Hungarian, Czech and other languages (ch 431-432). Uses stick grenades and an MG14 light machine gun (ch 434, 437).

---

## Relationships

- [[Major Danneberg]] - Saxon major whose 4th Battalion hosts him; bluntly orders his men disarmed and later fights beside him (ch 431-434).
- Saxon second lieutenant (unnamed) - the officer whose rebuke turns him back to fight (ch 432).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch 431: arrives with the routed regiment after a Serbian night attack and Vukodlak breach; the first witness to describe them.
- ch 432: runners are shot, he rallies the survivors to fight.
- ch 433-434: fights mixed with Saxons in the third trench and blocks a communication trench with grenades, injuring two Vukodlak.
- ch 437: fires an MG14 in the "battle frenzy" of the defense.

---

## Status at ch473

Last seen alive in ch 437; later fate not stated.
