## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Major, Flanders Army; highest-ranking surviving officer of the remnants of the 31st Infantry Regiment (11th Mixed Brigade, Third Infantry Division). His regimental commander had vanished (ch166).
- Defends the Charleroi bridges and commands the rearguard (ch166-167).

---

## Background

Described as an experienced veteran; no family or earlier history stated.

---

## Personality

- Despair at the losses, but tactically sensible: recognises he cannot win a firepower duel and decides to trade space for time by drawing the Saxons into South City for street fighting (ch166).
- Brave: stays to hold the bridgehead himself while the others withdraw (ch166).
- Reacts fast to danger, throwing himself down when he identifies grenades (ch166).

---

## Abilities

Officer's revolver, binoculars; sends dispatch riders to Fifth Army Group (they reach Lanrezac, ch168).

---

## Relationships

- Saxon 2nd Company 1st Platoon ([[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s force) - the enemy who defeat him in about ten seconds with smoke and grenades (ch166-167).
- General Lanrezac - receives [[Major Verhaegen]]'s dispatch riders (ch168).

## Lore & Key Events

- ch166: defends Charleroi bridges, demolition team pinned under the bridge; sends dispatch riders; orders staged retreat into South City; smoke covers the bridge and grenades land among his group.
- ch167: pinned behind sandbags by heavy machine guns, flung into a wall by the final blasts and knocked unconscious.
