**[[Grand Duchy of Flanders]]** is a faction hub of [[To Fly on Borrowed Wings]].

Merged Netherlands, Belgium and Luxembourg, in effect a Britannian protectorate, with the Order of the Golden Fleece Knights. Liège and Charleroi fall in August 1914.
