## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Unnamed leader of a secret society of black-robed members in Bucharest; addressed as "Master" by Ferdinand I of Romania (ch471-472). Romanian (implied).
- Hunched, withered, "chicken-claw" hand, ancient calm voice, face always in shadow.

---

## Background

- Not stated. His pupils are the same eerie scarlet as the king's (ch472), implying a link to the same vampiric source.

---

## Personality

- Calm, flat and manipulative; courteous in form but coldly utilitarian. Gives the same answer to the king's blood-supply question each time (ch471).
- Shows his first emotion when pressing the king to accept sacrificing civilians: "In war, there are never any innocents" (ch472). Appeals to the king as the "Dragon of Wallachia" that has slumbered 440 years, and bows as if to another ruler behind him.

---

## Abilities

- Directs the conversion of "Blood Thralls": soldiers given a drop of the king's blood become fast, strong, tough, with improved shooting, and need fresh blood daily (ch471).

---

## Relationships

- Ferdinand I of Romania - nominal master and puppet; the old man advises and steers him (ch471-472).
- [[Major General Stoica]] - his activities produce the "monster" rumours Stoica reports (ch473).
- Queen Marie - appears with the king in the aftermath (ch471).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch471 - Reports that Blood Thrall conversion is going very well and will continue before the Saxons arrive; refuses to explain the blood source.
- ch472 - Tells the king that stored blood lasts at most two days and that Bucharest, with over 200,000 civilians, is "a massive blood bank"; urges him to choose iron and blood.
- ch472 - His society converts Blood Thralls without rest.

---

## Status at ch473

Active in Bucharest, converting Blood Thralls and feeding on civilians; the king's moral resistance is weakening.
