**[[Kingdom of Romania (Europa)]]** is a faction hub of [[To Fly on Borrowed Wings]].

Part of the Balkan coalition in 1915. Its Predeal Pass, Ploiești and Bucharest are the last battles of the draft (ch450-473); Romanian Blood Thralls appear at the end.
