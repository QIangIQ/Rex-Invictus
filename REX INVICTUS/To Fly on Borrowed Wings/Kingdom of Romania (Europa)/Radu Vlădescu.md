## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Lieutenant Colonel, acting commander of the Romanian 3rd Border Guard Regiment and commander of the defense of Predeal Pass (ch 450, 454). Romanian.

---

## Background

Not stated. Hints of border experience: he knows the Austro-Hungarian attempt to use Armored Knights on the pass failed when two fell off cliffs (ch 453).

---

## Personality

- Calm and analytical under pressure: smokes nervously (three matches to light a cigarette, ch 450) but decides coolly to hold ground rather than waste artillery on the slopes.
- Realistic to the point of fatalism: asks Brigade to plan for "after the fall of the Predeal Pass" and reads from the morning's ambush that the Saxon commander is a careful one (ch 450).
- Brave at the end: refuses to surrender and fires on [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] (ch 454).

---

## Abilities

- Mountain defense with bunkers, tunnels, blasting and a pass artillery battalion whose fields of fire are limited by ridges (ch 450). Carries a pistol.

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - opponent who kills him; [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] bows to the body in respect (ch 454).
- Unnamed old artillery battalion commander - warns him the Saxons spent the bombardment ranging their guns for a night attack (ch 452).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch 450: sees a Saxon armored airship land six kilometers away; his guns cannot reach it. Orders a hold and telegraphs Brigade for heavy artillery.
- ch 452: night bombardment and aerial war beast; realizes the Saxons have ranged the whole position.
- ch 453: three Saxon Armored Knights drop from the sky, defying mountain-defense doctrine ("Holy Mother Mary"); he retreats toward a rear tunnel.
- ch 454: shot dead on the scree by [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] together with his staff; the pass command is decapitated.

Dead (ch 454).
