## Personality

- Calm and analytical when briefing: calculates the 112 km straight-line distance from Predeal Pass to Bucharest and the few defensible terrains (ch452).
- Commanding under stress: slams the table and demands operable suggestions instead of empty talk (ch452).
- Visibly shaken by defeat: veins pop on his hand, his voice goes hoarse, and he sags "like a deflated balloon" at the Predeal collapse (ch454).

- Staff officer and strategist; no magic or combat feats shown.

## Relationships

- Ferdinand I of Romania - his king; the General Staff reports the front situation to him (ch452-454).
- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - the Saxon commander whose forces break the Predeal Pass; [[Matei Mavrocordatos]] never meets him.
- [[Radu Vlădescu]] - Lieutenant Colonel commanding the border regiment at Predeal (killed ch453).

## Lore & Key Events

- ch452 - Assesses that Saxon vanguards could reach Bucharest within a week; orders reinforcement of Predeal Pass and calls for suggestions.
- ch454 - Receives the telegram "Predeal Pass... contact lost", two hours after Saxon armored airships supported the assault; revises the estimate to at most three days before the Saxons reach Bucharest's outskirts.
