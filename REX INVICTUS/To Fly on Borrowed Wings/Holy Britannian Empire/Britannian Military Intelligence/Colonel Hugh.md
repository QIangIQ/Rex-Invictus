**[[Colonel Hugh]]** is a character in [[To Fly on Borrowed Wings]], placed under the [[Britannian Military Intelligence]] of the [[Holy Britannian Empire]].

## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Colonel; senior military advisor stationed in the Balkans by the Holy Britannia Empire's Military Intelligence Bureau/Service, attached to the Balkan Coalition (ch419, 440). Appears with an adjutant (not named).

---

## Background

- Not stated. Speaks fluent Serbian (ch419).

---

## Personality

- Polished in public, contemptuous in private. Wears gold-rimmed glasses and smokes cigars/cigarettes; speaks with a "haughty and caustic tone unique to Britannians" (ch440).
- Manipulative: dangles supplies and a possible expeditionary force to keep coalition delegates fighting, though the aid is described as an empty promise (ch420). Predicts the coalition members will start preserving their own strength (ch440).
- Drops the diplomat's mask when angry at the coalition's failure to crush Bulgaria (ch440).

---

## Abilities

- Intelligence and diplomacy; no magic shown.

---

## Relationships

- Field Marshal Radomir Putnik - coalition commander; Hugh publicly reassures him, privately resents his trying to "order the Highland Mages around," yet admits his analysis is right (ch440).
- His adjutant - confidant for his candid remarks (ch440).
- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - never met on page; Hugh wants "true experts" sent to deal with the Saxon "[[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]" (ch440).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch419 - Briefs the coalition conference that Bulgaria must be eliminated before the Saxons and Austrians reorganize; gratified by the hostility toward Bulgaria.
- ch420 - Promises a convoy of munitions, medicine and canned food and the possible dispatch of an expeditionary force.
- ch439-440 - Asked by Putnik to send Highland Mages to Bulgaria; after a critical remark about the coalition armies he decides to use the dedicated line to contact home and request experts against [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]].
- No later appearance found after ch440.
