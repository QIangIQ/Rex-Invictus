## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Known only by a codename (a real name is not stated): the senior operations director stationed in Vienna by Britannia's General Directorate of Military Intelligence (ch394). Also called "senior director of the Military Intelligence Bureau" (ch401).
- Manages the covert effort to stage a coup against the Austro-Hungarian Empire and tie the Saxon Empire down in Central Europe.

---

## Background

- Not stated. He speaks flawless, accentless Saxon, notable in a multi-ethnic empire (ch394). Long operating in Austria-Hungary (ch400).

---

## Personality

- Cold, calculating, contemptuous of his partners: privately calls the Hungarians "a bunch of greedy fools" and views Czechs and Hungarians alike as pawns on Britannia's chessboard (ch395).
- Manipulative: sets Masaryk a 24-hour ultimatum and hints at framing radical Austrians for an "accident" to Crown Prince Karl (ch394).
- Cautious about red lines: coldly rejects a proposal to assassinate Emperor Franz Joseph I because a trace pointing to Britannia could wreck Balkan diplomacy (ch401).
- Prone to quiet fury when plans fail (smashes his glass, ch400), yet pragmatic: orders destroying documents and evacuation (ch400-401).
- Fair-minded to subordinates: appreciates a gifted commoner subordinate over a noble one but avoids favoritism (ch401). Thrifty humor: downs his drink because it is "paid for by operational funds" (ch395).

---

## Abilities

- Intelligence tradecraft and agent handling; commands a "Special Operations Team" including Armored Knights ("Hound Mark 4 Modified") and a Highland mage embedded in the palace (ch394, ch400-401).

---

## Relationships

- Masaryk (real historical figure; Czech deputy) - conspirator handled by this officer; supplied with funds, weapons and officer-corps channels; deceived about the goal (ch394-395).
- Hungarian envoy from Budapest (unnamed; works for Count István) - ally of convenience; he plans to give Bohemia to Hungary under "temporary military administration" (ch395).
- Archduke Karl (real) - Crown Prince; target of persuasion or threat (ch394).
- Two subordinates, one noble and one commoner (unnamed) - feud with each other; he arbitrates (ch401).
- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - no direct meeting is shown; the "Saxon unit" that "popped out of nowhere" is [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s Kampfgruppe, which wrecks the coup (ch400).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch394: confronts Masaryk and unveils a photo of an Armored Knight as an incentive; sets the 24-hour deadline.
- ch395: the same night, plots with the Hungarian envoy; coup set for 4:30 AM on March 17.
- ch400: coup collapses (General Staff recaptured, Conrad freed, Schoneberg barracks lost); orders evacuation.
- ch401: refuses the plan to kill Franz Joseph I; burns documents; abandons the Czechs and tells the palace mage to withdraw.
- ch410: forced to evacuate Vienna with most intelligence staff.

---

## Status at ch473

- Alive and out of Vienna since ch410; not mentioned afterward.
