## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Director of the Military Intelligence Directorate of the Holy Britannia Empire, also referred to as the head of MI6 (ch345). A middle-aged man who looks shrewd and capable.
- Rose slowly from a military attaché stationed abroad to "intelligence boss" (ch345).

### Real History (IRL)

- The real head of British military intelligence in 1914 was George Macdonogh, and MI6 was led by Mansfield Cumming.

---

## Background

- Career path as above; nothing else stated about family or origin.

---

## Personality

- Pragmatic and politically adept. Lowers his posture to the Highland mage [[Duncan Muirhead]] to preserve cooperation and admits flaws in the intelligence work while insisting Her Majesty's will must be carried out (ch345).
- Cynical about bureaucracy: almost spits out his cigar at the "Queen's Blade Evaluation Committee", and shares a knowing toast with Kitchener about forming committees to decide what is already decided (ch345).
- Eager and impatient when the divination session begins ("Is it [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s weakness? Or the time of his death?").

---

## Abilities

- Non-magical intelligence chief; mobilises espionage assets inside the Saxon Empire to build a profile of [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] (ch345).

---

## Relationships

- [[Duncan Muirhead]] - Highland Mage Order envoy; [[Montague (Britannian intelligence)]] defers to him and negotiates joint operations (ch345).
- [[Master Quarrie]] - Tier-6 Diviner whose divination [[Montague (Britannian intelligence)]] attends; it ends in failure (ch345).
- Field Marshal Kitchener - Secretary of State for War; [[Montague (Britannian intelligence)]] brings him the final operation report and learns Kitchener already knew everything (ch345).
- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - target of Operation "Queen's Blade"; [[Montague (Britannian intelligence)]] receives a thin report with blurry long-range photos of him (ch345).
