## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Major, liaison officer of the Britannian Expeditionary Force; noble, a descendant of the Duke of Devonshire's family, and field supervisor of Project Prometheus (ch153-155, 303, 353).
- Nationality: Britannian.

### Real History (IRL)

- Presented as a member of the real Cavendish (Dukes of Devonshire) family. No real officer of this name is known.

---

## Background

Of noble blood. The text first gives only the surname; the full name appears in ch353 documents.

---

## Personality

Not shown directly; he is knocked out early. Under later interrogation he talks, revealing Project Prometheus (ch353-354).

---

## Abilities

Carries an ID, a crested card and a sealed letter. [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] takes these and uses them as cover.

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - captor; knocks him out, strips and binds him at Liège, then impersonates him (ch154-155).
- Major General Leman - the Saxon-held Liège commander [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] bluffs while posing as the liaison (ch154).
- Queen Victoria - demands he be found (ch303).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch154-155: captured by [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] on the way to Liège; his identity is used to enter the fortress; he is sent to Army Group HQ as an important prisoner.
- ch303: presumed lost; the Queen issues an ultimatum to find him.
- ch353-354: held in the senior-officer area of the First POW Camp; Department III extracts details of Project Prometheus.
- ch357, 360: his information shapes the Paris operation.

---

## Status at ch473

Prisoner of the Saxons as of ch353-360; later fate not stated.
