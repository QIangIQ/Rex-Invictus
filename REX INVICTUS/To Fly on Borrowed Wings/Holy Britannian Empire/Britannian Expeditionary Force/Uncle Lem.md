## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Veteran soldier of the North American Legion, in his forties with graying temples, called "[[Uncle Lem]]" by younger men (ch329). Rank not stated; a roster note calls him a corporal.

---

## Background

Not stated in the chapters beyond being a long-serving veteran; roster notes say he has a daughter across the ocean.

---

## Personality

- Calm, laconic, dutiful: volunteers for a sneak-attack reconnaissance on Christmas Eve ("I'll go... You young pups stay here and enjoy the holiday", ch329) so the younger men are spared.
- Moved by the carol music drifting from the Saxon lines, he stops crawling (ch329). Easily embarrassed: his face turns "the color of pig liver" when caught (ch331).

---

## Abilities

Rifle; crawls through no-man's-land mud as a scout (ch329).

---

## Relationships

- Younger soldiers of his company - protective older figure (ch329).
- Saxon soldiers - captured good-naturedly in a crater and sent back with whistles (ch331).
- [[Prescott (North American Legion)]] and [[Scottish Captain (Christmas truce)]] - officers of the same truce; no direct scene stated.

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch329: volunteers for the recon and crawls into No Man's Land.
- ch330: in a crater about forty meters from the Saxon trench, sees the Christmas tree and thinks the cold is making him hallucinate.
- ch331: found by Saxon soldiers, scurries back to his own side; the moment breaks the last barrier between the two sides.
