## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Colonel; company commander of the Guard Knight Order, piloting a "St. George Type 3" in the Seville street fighting (ch53). Leads eight knights against six Teutonic knights.

## Personality

Cold, arrogant, aristocratic; sneers at the Saxon knights as clumsy "iron lumps" and believes narrow streets and numbers favor him (ch53). Furious at being ignored by the opposing pilot (ch53).
