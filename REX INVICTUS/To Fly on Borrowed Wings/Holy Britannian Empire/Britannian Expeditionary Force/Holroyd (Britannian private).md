## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Private, 20 years old, a Yorkshire farm boy and miner's son in the Britannian Expeditionary Force (ch337-338).

---

## Background

- Son of a Yorkshire miner; furthest he had travelled was Leeds market. Planned to apprentice at a Glasgow shipyard and save to marry. Recruited about two months earlier by a speech promising a medal and a cow-buying allowance (ch337-338).

---

## Personality

- Frightened and homesick, prays "God bless" under his breath, resentful of the recruiting officer (ch337).

---

## Abilities

- Lee-Enfield rifle (ch337-338). Acts as point man in the trench assault.

---

## Relationships

- Bill - fellow townsman since childhood who boasted he could drink a gallon of beer; killed beside him (ch337).
- A sergeant behind him prods him forward with a rifle butt (ch338).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch337: Charges the Saxon line, is hit by Bill's brain matter, and drops into the communication trench.
- ch338: Becomes point man in the zigzag trench. Rounding a corner he faces Saxon MP14 submachine gunners, is shot dead, and the rest of his column is cut down.
