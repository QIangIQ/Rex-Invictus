## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Colonel commanding the Britannian Worcestershire Regiment, one of the first home-based units sent to reinforce the Iberian Peninsula. Leads two battalions (the text also says "three battalions") in the counterattack on San Lorenzo de la Parrilla (ch 96).

---

## Background

Not stated. The Britannian high command had suppressed news of the defeat at Seville, so he enters the fight unaware of what Saxon forces can do (ch 96).

---

## Personality

- Arrogant and contemptuous: calls the Aragonese "farmers" and the Saxons "barbarians", dismisses holding the town as "a fool's dream" (ch 96).
- Confident to the point of complacency: "I intend to be drinking Saxon coffee in the town's castle before lunch" (ch 96).
- Brittle under shock: thrown from his rearing horse, he shrieks that "Saxon Mages have never appeared on the front line!" (ch 97).

---

## Abilities

- Rides a tall thoroughbred; orders company-level dense formations with bounding overwatch (ch 96). No personal magic stated.

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - unwitting opponent whose regiment is destroyed by guns Britannia supplied to Aragon, now guided by [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] (ch 96-97).
- [[Major Navarro]] - the captured gunner whose cannons wreck his regiment (no direct contact).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch 96: orders the advance up the gentle slope toward the town.
- ch 97: two Arcane Shrapnel Shell volleys shatter both battalions; MG08 and captured Vickers guns then cut down the survivors, and Saxon companies counter-charge. He watches stunned and cannot understand how the Saxons use the Empire's Magic Crystal Cannons so accurately.
