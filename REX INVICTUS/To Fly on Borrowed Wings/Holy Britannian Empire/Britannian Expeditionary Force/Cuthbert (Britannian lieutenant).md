## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Second Lieutenant of the Northumberland Fusiliers, Britannian Expeditionary Force; Britannian, from Kent (ch336).
- Appears only in ch336, in the large Britannian counterattack in which fresh units were deliberately placed on the front line. It is his first action on the continent.

---

## Background

- A young gentleman from Kent on the main island; middle-class or gentry background, with polished boots and a Webley revolver (ch336). Earlier history not stated.

---

## Personality

- Arrogant and naively confident in the traditional-nobility manner; tells his men the Saxon trench has been blasted to ashes and that taking it will be "like taking a stroll in Hyde Park" (ch336).
- Freezes under real combat: he is "frightened stupid" by the hand-to-hand slaughter (ch336). Shows a flicker of tactical sense when he understands that they must charge in or be used as targets in No Man's Land.

---

## Abilities

- Shiny silver Webley revolver, a status symbol that marks him as an officer and a target; polished calfskin boots and belt. No magic or special skills stated.

---

## Relationships

- Commands a platoon of equally neat, inexperienced Northumberland Fusiliers (ch336).
- Killed by a young Saxon private, unnamed in the text; the older Saxon sergeant major leading the ambush is also unnamed.

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch336: Leads his men over the top; survives the shelling in a crater; reaches the Saxon first trench and realizes why it is thinly held. Saxons burst from hidden tunnels in a prepared trap; he drops his pistol and is stabbed in the abdomen by the young private and dies.

---

Dead (killed ch336).
