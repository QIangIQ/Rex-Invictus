## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Middle-aged captain of Spell Jaeger Team A, known by a nickname meaning an older man, given by his team. Team A has eleven members: six Spell Jaegers (mages) and five Heavy Armor Sergeants (ch346). Known only by this nickname.
- Part of the Britannian operation to kill [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] (Operation Queen's Blade, set up in ch345).

---

## Background

- The Spell Jaegers are "the eliminated ones, the forgotten ones": low-tier mage apprentices who, after years of harsh training at a remote country estate, became a hybrid of hunter and mage (ch346). His own origin is not stated; the text calls him "an old hunter" (ch350).

---

## Personality

- Cold, professional and observant. Calls the shots by communication stone and picks his own role as sniper ("I'm responsible for calling roll", ch346).
- Respectful of the target's apparent talent ("far stronger than us folks", ch346).
- Analytical: while fighting, he tallies [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s spell slots and deliberately overestimates them by one per tier (ch348).
- Knows when to retreat; follows team doctrine that heavy armor units cover the rear with their lives (ch350). Shows quiet respect for comrades who die buying his escape.

---

## Abilities

- Modified Lee-Enfield short rifle: optical sight, rune-engraved barrel, stave-wood stock; he casts Magic Weapon on it (ch346).
- Spells and kit used by the team: Blade Ward (ch347), Expeditious Retreat and Invisibility scroll (ch350), plus Haste, Grease, Web, Fire Bolt in the squad toolkit.

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - the assigned target; he snipes at him and tracks him as he vanishes into the Ethereal Plane (ch346-349). Never prevails.
- Team A (Spell Jaegers and Heavy Armor Sergeants) - close comrades after years of joint training; most die (ch349-350).
- MI6 / Highland Mage Order - sponsors of the operation (ch345-346).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch346: team in position near the gap; spots [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]; leads an assault and snipes from a crater.
- ch347-348: charges in with Blade Ward, enters a trench, and works out his opponent's spell slots.
- ch349: [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] repeatedly vanishes and reappears, killing two of his people; he cannot get a shot off.
- ch350: pinned down, with only one other [[Förster (master sergeant)]] left; two Heavy Armor Sergeants charge to cover them; he and the survivor flee invisibly.
