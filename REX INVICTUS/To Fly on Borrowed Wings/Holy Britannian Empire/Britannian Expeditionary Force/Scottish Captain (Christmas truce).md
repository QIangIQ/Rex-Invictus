**[[Scottish Captain (Christmas truce)]]** is a character in [[To Fly on Borrowed Wings]], placed under the [[Britannian Expeditionary Force]] of the [[Holy Britannian Empire]].

Known in the story as:

- Scottish Captain
- the Scottish Captain
- red-bearded Scottish Captain

## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Red-bearded Scottish company-level captain in a kilt, in the Britannian trench line facing the Saxon First Army Group at the Christmas truce (ch331-332). Name not stated.
- Faction: Britannia (Scottish regiment). Counterpart of [[Prescott (North American Legion)]] (North American Legion second lieutenant); the two are the "grassroots officers" who meet [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] (then Lieutenant Colonel) in No Man's Land.

---

## Background

Not stated beyond being a Scottish officer. Earlier, Scots are described as "completely fearless" defenders (ch100, as seen by [[Lt. Col. Mertens]]).

---

## Personality

- Blunt, earthy, humorous; swears ("Bloody hell", ch331). Smooths his beard and eyebrows with spit before meeting a Saxon officer (ch331).
- Pragmatic: "My boys just want to drink some wine tonight, not kill people" (ch331); toasts "To staying alive" (ch331); insists on a "sense of ceremony" for the ceasefire.
- Sportsmanlike and good-humoured: takes a cigarette from [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]], says God would not want them fighting on His birthday (ch332), calls [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s dribble "humiliating" (ch332).

---

## Abilities

Kilt, hip flask of whiskey, serves as goalkeeper and captain of the Britannian representative football side, shouting its roster (ch332).

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - enemy officer turned truce partner; negotiates the ceasefire and extension, drinks with him, plays against him (ch331-332).
- [[Prescott (North American Legion)]] - fellow officer, co-proposer of the truce, shares the court-martial fate.
- [[Uncle Lem]] - soldier of the same truce, caught in the crater (ch331); direct interaction not stated.

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch331: meets [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] at the tree stump, agrees to silence on his side, toasts "to staying alive"; salutes solemnly when the truce ends.
- ch332: truce extended; football match, he guards goal and is beaten by [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s chip over his beard; shares whiskey.
- ch332 narration: he and [[Prescott (North American Legion)]] are relieved of duty and sent to the rear for court-martial; troops ordered not to leave trenches.

---

## Status at ch473

Last seen awaiting court-martial sentence after ch332; fate not stated.
