## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Major; commander of the 1st Task Force Company, Coldstream Guards. A Britannian noble officer with golden hair, posted to guard the entrance of the underground research institute (the Paris institute) at the Bois de Boulogne (ch357-358).

---

## Background

- Coldstream officer longing to meet "true powerhouses" to shake off the lethargy of the old regiment in trench warfare (ch358). Family not stated beyond being a noble.

---

## Personality

- Known for calm, but badly rattled when the Saxons arrive: his heartbeat races (ch358). Brave and stubborn: refuses to retreat although the plan allowed it, because the operation team has not come out (ch358).
- Resigned and defiant at the end: tries to shoot himself rather than be taken (ch363).

---

## Abilities

- Webley revolver; commands a company with a Vickers heavy machine gun and Lee-Enfield riflemen. No magic.

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] and the Saxon Imperial Guard Assault Instruction Unit - the enemy that wipes out his company (ch358); in ch362 he shouts "We are not the enemies! Those things are!" in broken Saxon when the Sentinels appear.
- Captain Jensen (2nd Platoon leader, unnamed in the tag list) - reports the collapse of the platoons (ch358).
- [[Voigt (Saxon officer)]] - held captive in the same trench in the testing area (ch364).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch358 - His company is destroyed by the Saxons' assault squads; he retreats underground, leaving wounded to delay the enemy (ch359).
- ch362-363 - Begs the Saxons not to fire on the Britannians; wounded in the left arm and dragged by out-of-control Sentinels to the Practical Combat Testing Area.
- ch364 - Pale with blood loss, bandaged, thrown with Britannian and Gallic survivors into the test trench.
- ch365-366 - Killed by the Sentinels; his severed head is seen in the pile of bodies.
