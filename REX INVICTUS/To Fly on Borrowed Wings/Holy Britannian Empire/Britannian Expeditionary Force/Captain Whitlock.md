## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Britannian captain, temporary commander of two infantry companies sent to delay a Saxon assault unit, attached to the Fifth Infantry Division screen near Arras, 20 Aug (ch210).

---

## Background

- Not stated.

---

## Personality

- Complacent at first: assumes two companies against one is a foregone conclusion, and laughs with his men when the Saxons seem to run (ch210). Instinctive and quick to give way to survival once he sees the odds; orders the retreat to save what is left (ch210).

---

## Abilities

- Binoculars and standard infantry command; no special skills stated.

---

## Relationships

- Opponent of [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]], whose assault company routs him. Also a casualty of the "Saxon-Style Tachankas" built with [[Konrad von Seeckt]] (ch210).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch210: Deploys two companies against [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s unit, hoping to catch up with the main force. Four horse-drawn heavy machine guns are unmasked from behind carriages; he dives for cover, and his companies break when the Saxon automatic-weapon infantry charge. He orders a retreat.
