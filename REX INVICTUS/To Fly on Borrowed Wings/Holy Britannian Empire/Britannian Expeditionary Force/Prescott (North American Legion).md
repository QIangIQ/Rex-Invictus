## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Second Lieutenant in the North American Legion of the Britannian Expeditionary Force, a colonial officer (ch330-332). Young with sunken, exhausted eyes.

---

## Background

- A "country bumpkin" from the colonies; the Legion's soldiers are second-class citizens who fight for first-class citizenship for their families (ch331-332).

---

## Personality

- Earnest and peace-minded: proposes the Christmas ceasefire in broken Saxon and accented Britannian (ch330).
- Easily flustered by rank: snaps a non-standard salute and stammers on meeting a Lieutenant Colonel (ch331).
- Becomes boisterous in the football match, charging in to tackle ([[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]) (ch332).

---

## Abilities

- Ordinary line officer; no magic shown.

## Relationships

- [[Uncle Lem]] - Legion veteran corporal found in a crater (ch329-331).
