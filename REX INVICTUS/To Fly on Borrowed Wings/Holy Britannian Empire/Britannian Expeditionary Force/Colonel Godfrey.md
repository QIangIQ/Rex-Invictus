**[[Colonel Godfrey]]** is a character in [[To Fly on Borrowed Wings]], placed under the [[Britannian Expeditionary Force]] of the [[Holy Britannian Empire]].

## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Colonel of the Britannian Expeditionary Force, commanding a battalion of the Northumberland Musket Regiment at Seville (ch53-55).

---

## Background

- Not stated.

---

## Personality

- Arrogant and contemptuous of allies; says in stiff Aragonese "Watch closely, peasants. Next, you'll witness how a real war is fought" (ch53). Loses his head under pressure, grabbing a messenger's collar (ch54) and refusing retreat: "The army of our Holy Britannian Empire must never retreat in front of these peasants!" (ch54).

---

## Abilities

- Conventional infantry command; no magic noted.

---

## Relationships

- [[Ainsley (Highland High Mentor)]] - fellow expeditionary leader; mildly rebukes his insult to the Aragonese (ch53) and confers with him when the attack collapses (ch54).
- General José Sanjurjo (real historical name) - Aragonese ally he humiliates; the Britannians take over the assault (ch53).
- [[Colonel Bertram]] - fellow Britannian colonel, killed in the first duel; the news stuns him (ch54).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch53: Leads the new Britannian-fronted attack on Seville after Sanjurjo is stripped of command.
- ch54: Learns of five more Armored Knight losses and the colonel's death; orders reserves in and refuses to withdraw while troops are mixed into street fighting.
- ch55: His reinforced Musket Regiment battalion is pushed forward alongside the mage group.
