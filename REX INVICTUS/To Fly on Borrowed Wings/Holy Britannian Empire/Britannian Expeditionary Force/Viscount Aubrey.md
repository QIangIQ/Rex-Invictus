**[[Viscount Aubrey]]** is a character in [[To Fly on Borrowed Wings]], placed under the [[Britannian Expeditionary Force]] of the [[Holy Britannian Empire]].

## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Viscount (noble); captain and leader of an Order of the Garter knight squad flying the "St. George Type 3" (ch341-342). Recognized "Combat Master" of the knighthood and spiritual pillar of his squad.

---

## Background

Noble pilot; the Garter had suffered badly for months against Saxon shaped-charge weapons before Britannia reverse-engineered them (ch341).

---

## Personality

Arrogant and eager: "Come on, Saxon barbarians!" with a cruel smile (ch341). Disdains Saxons' tactics of depleting shields at range, switches off the auto-shield to save mana (ch341). Collapses into disbelief when his weapon fails against spaced armor.

---

## Abilities

St. George Type 3 with tower shield and a copied shaped-charge armor-piercing lance; enchanted breastplate (ch341).

---

## Relationships

- [[Konrad von Seeckt]] - opponent who kills him; George is baffled by the spaced armor (ch341).
- Two Garter squad pilots - demoralized by his death, then destroyed (ch342).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch341: duels [[Konrad von Seeckt]]; his lance has no effect on the spaced armor, and a shaped-charge jet penetrates his cockpit. He dies.
- ch342: his headless machine kneels in the mud; his squad panics and is destroyed within two minutes.

---

## Status at ch473

Dead (ch341).
