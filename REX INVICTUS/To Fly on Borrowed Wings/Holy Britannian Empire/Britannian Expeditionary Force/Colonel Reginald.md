## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Colonel, commander of the Britannian "Ranger" Squadron, an elite Armored Knight hunter unit; described as "the most elite Armored Knight Hunter of Britannia" (ch249). Scarred face, hawk-like eyes, dark green pilot uniform, leather gloves (ch239, ch247).
- Arrives at Rouen and places his squadron under Field Marshal John French by War Office order (ch239). Mission: capture a Saxon prototype Armored Knight intact or obtain the specifications of the new weapon (ch239).
- A subordinate once calls him "Major" (ch247). The ch247 Saxon "Erwin" of the Teutonic Knights is a source naming collision; only the Britannian is this character.

---

## Background

- Not stated beyond his reputation as a specialist hunter of Armored Knights and his squadron's jungle-hunting style ("we Rangers are accustomed to hunting in the jungle", ch239).

---

## Personality

- Calm, flat-voiced, almost without inflection even when discussing the Expeditionary Force's defeats at Arras and Amiens (ch239).
- Patient, cunning predator: smiles "the cunning smile of a hunter" (ch239) and "the smile of a predator" (ch247, ch248).
- Methodical and practical: studies the railway map, plans a bait-and-ambush rather than a pitched battle (ch239); orders crews to disable leg joints and spare cockpits because "our mission is capture, not destruction" (ch247).
- Technically curious and professional; ignores the captured Siegfried machines and goes straight to the unusual weapons, noting the melted, circular holes in his own cockpits (ch249).

---

## Abilities

- Pilots his own Armored Knight with the squadron's ten-odd dark green "Hound Type 4" machines, which use near-silent Magic Guided Engines and a stealth approach (ch239, ch248).
- Uses a Communication Stone to coordinate squads (ch247). Excellent at ambush design and use of spies and local cover.

---

## Relationships

- Field Marshal John French (real) - superior under whose command the squadron is placed; French is tired and resigned, he is confident (ch239).
- [[Ruhland (Teutonic squadron commander)]] - the Teutonic Knight squadron commander whom his trap draws out; arrives too late and is enraged by the ambush (ch249).
- Royal Welsh Fusiliers (two veteran companies disguised as Gallic guerrillas) - supporting force for the capture (ch248).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch238-239: arrives at Rouen, proposes to cut at the Saxon supply line on the Amiens-Paris railway.
- ch247: sets a trap using a planted spy farmer with a Gallic flag; lures a Saxon patrol and three Siegfried I Armored Knights into the copse.
- ch248-249: Rangers cripple three Siegfried I, rout the accompanying infantry battalion, and take an intact Armor-Piercing War Hammer and Lance plus structural samples; withdraw before reinforcements arrive.
