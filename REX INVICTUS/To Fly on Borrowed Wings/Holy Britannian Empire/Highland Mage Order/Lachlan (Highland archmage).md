**[[Lachlan (Highland archmage)]]** is a character in [[To Fly on Borrowed Wings]], placed under the [[Highland Mage Order]] of the [[Holy Britannian Empire]].

Known in the story as:

- Lachlan
- Archmage Lachlan

## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Seventh-Circle Transmutation Archmage of the Highland Mage Corps, Britannian side; called "Archmage" and "Master" (ch225-229). Unnamed "other Seventh-Circle Transmutation Mage" in ch220, first named in ch225.
- Works as the partner of [[Ellis Whitcombe]] during the defence of Amiens (South City counterattack unit, ch225).

---

## Background

Not stated beyond being an older academic mage: years of study in the Mage Tower left him poor at physical exertion (ch227). Described as a pillar of the Highland Mage Corps and a strategic asset to the Empire (ch229).

---

## Personality

- Short-tempered and contemptuous of Saxons: curses [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] as a "Saxon barbarian" who "runs as fast as a rabbit" while panting after him (ch227).
- Reactive rather than leading; follows [[Ellis Whitcombe]]'s decisions and puzzles over the enemy's moves (ch227).

---

## Abilities

- Transmutation: [Disintegrate] (destroyed the shield, ch220), [Bigby's Hand] (controls a giant hand that crushes Armored Knights, ch226).
- Accelerates himself with [Expeditious Retreat], uses a [Fly] scroll and [Shield Spell] (ch227). Wears a communication wristband showing allied life lights (ch229).

---

## Relationships

- [[Ellis Whitcombe]] - fellow Seventh-Circle mage and partner in the counterattack; mourns him as "all dead" (ch229).
- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - hunted him through Amiens; killed him with a street explosion (ch229).
- [[Konrad von Seeckt]] - crushed his Armored Knight with [Bigby's Hand] (ch226).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch220: [Disintegrate] shatters an allied shield (identified later as his).
- ch225: joins the elite counterattack unit in the South City plaza with four St. George III Knights.
- ch226: [Bigby's Hand] buries [[Konrad von Seeckt]]'s Knight and then targets the two remaining Saxon Knights.
- ch227: pursues [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] through the streets, flies up, driven back by the Saxon armored airship's machine guns.
- ch229: killed with three supporting mages (two named apprentices and a Fifth-Circle mage) when [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] triggers a massive explosion.

Dead (ch229).
