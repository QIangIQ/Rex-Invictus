## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Elderly blindfolded Tier-6 Diviner, one of only two Divination mages in the Highland Mage Order (the other is his apprentice) (ch345). Treated with deference: all the mages stand when he enters.

---

## Background

- Becomes a Divination mage only through an innate talent glimpsed on awakening; cannot be retrained (ch345). Otherwise not stated. Possesses an arcane tradition [Comprehend Languages].

---

## Personality

- Proud; refuses to believe repeated failures and tries Scrying twice more, each time with worse backlash until he is bedridden (ch345).
- Dignified but frustrated by the failure and the Intelligence Directorate's doubts about magic.

---

## Abilities

- [Divination] (Tier 4), [Scrying] (Tier 5) with a crystal ball, [Comprehend Languages].

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - target of the divinations; the result is an incomprehensible, angry phrase (Chinese, rendered as "This son of a bitch definitely used cheats!") and Scrying is blocked by fog (ch345).
- [[Montague (Britannian intelligence)]] - Director of Military Intelligence, who requests the work (ch345).
- [[Duncan Muirhead]] - a master of the Highland Mage Order, who stood in the conference (ch345).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch345: Divination fails to decipher, Scrying fails and he vomits blood; two more attempts leave him bedridden. The hunt for [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] stalls.
