## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Middle-aged 6th-tier (6th-circle) Enchantment mage of the Highland Mage Order, Holy Britannia; field codename "Baron" (ch409-410), later addressed as "Lord Baron".
- Ch409-410: field agent in the Vienna operation. Ch410 onward: leader of the new Anti-Jagdfeld Task Force. Ch446: also runs the reorganised "Spell Jäger" unit from a manor in Norfolk. Ch466-468: leads the raid on Rila Monastery in Bulgaria.

---

## Background

- Went to Vienna because the mission promised promotion and personal guidance from an 8th-tier Archmage (ch410). Family and origin otherwise not stated.
- Ch409-410: in the Hofburg coup he infiltrated with a Ring of Invisibility, made the Royal Magic Guards kill each other, helped the coup force reach the depths of the palace, and used Modify Memory scroll MM-23 on the old Emperor.

---

## Personality

- Cautious, self-preserving, and a skilled talker. Argues he cannot be sent against [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] ("If I go again, I'm definitely dead", ch410). Thirsts for power and status despite fear (ch410).
- Angry and sarcastic under Inquisition questioning ("was I supposed to stay for dinner?", ch410).
- Ch446: stalls in comfort by writing ever longer training plans (anti-reconnaissance drills, survival at mana depletion) and soundproofs his study; sees remnants of the Spell Jägers as "perfect human shields".
- Ch467-468: pragmatic and unsentimental; hoards even poor spell components, notes that the runic cloaks could be dyed as camouflage, and looks back at two dead child soldiers before leaving.

---

## Abilities

- Enchantment magic: Ring of Invisibility, Modify Memory scroll (ch410), Hold Person, a mana "force hand" that writes and searches (ch446, 467), Mage Hand.
- Silver-runed Webley revolver with enchanted bullets that crack Mage Armor (ch467). Communication stone, spell component pouches, escort of heavy-armour soldiers ("iron cans") and Spell Jägers (ch467).

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - the "[[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]"; this character came within reach of him in the Hofburg when a grenade was thrown at his pillar, and fled (ch410). Hopes never to meet him again (ch446).
- Highland Mage Order leadership and the Inquisition - superiors who suspect him, then conscript him into the task force with threats and promised promotion (ch410).
- Spell Jägers - subordinate remnants, including an early-forties scarred sniper ("Old Man", ch467-468) and a younger rifleman.
- [[Ambrose Wetherell]] - Chief Archmage of the Order, seen only in the later Council scene (ch468).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch409: reports back to London after the Vienna operation; first Highland Mage to survive a close encounter with [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] unscathed.
- ch410: interrogated by the Inquisition; he reasons that [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] cannot see through Invisibility, only guesses. Assigned to lead the Anti-Jagdfeld Task Force.
- ch446: in Norfolk, takes over the Spell Jägers and delays deployment; a messenger owl ends his idleness and he is sent to Bulgaria.
- ch466-468: kills the Bulgarian 5th-circle mage [[Stoyan Marinov]] with an enchanted bullet at Rila Monastery, seizes a telegraph codebook and withdraws before two Bulgarian battalions close in.

---

## Status at ch473

- Alive, in Bulgaria or retreating from Rila Monastery; still leads the Anti-Jagdfeld Task Force.
