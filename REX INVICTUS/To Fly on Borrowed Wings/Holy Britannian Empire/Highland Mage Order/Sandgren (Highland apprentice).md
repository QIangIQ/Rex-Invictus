## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Mage Apprentice of the Highland Mage Corps, one of two apprentices guarding the Somme railway bridge detonator at Amiens. Named only in ch225 (killed in ch224).

---

## Background

- Not stated. Posted to guard the demolition device for Master [[Ellis Whitcombe]].

---

## Personality

- Not individually shown. Per Corps regulations they keep [Mage Armor] up constantly (ch224); the younger one panics and flees when outmatched.

---

## Abilities

- Apprentice casters using spell slots and scrolls (Mage Armor, Fireball, Expeditious Retreat seen in ch224); carried spell journals and scrolls, looted by [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] (ch225). Tracking wristbands linked to [[Ellis Whitcombe]].

---

## Relationships

- [[Ellis Whitcombe]] - master; senses their wristband lights go out and reports the bridge lost (ch225).
- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - killed the pair with an enchanted-bullet MG14 and a bayonet, took the detonator, scrolls and journals (ch224-225).
- [[Tanner (Highland apprentice)]] - fellow apprentice guarding the bridge; they die within moments of each other (ch224).

- ch225: Named by [[Ellis Whitcombe]] when their lights go out; the Expeditionary Force realizes the bridge is lost.
