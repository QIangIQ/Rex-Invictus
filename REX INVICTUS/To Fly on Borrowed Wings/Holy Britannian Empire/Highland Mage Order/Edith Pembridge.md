## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Archmage of the Highland Mage Order, 9th-circle Conjuration; one of the six archmages on its council, seated second to the Speaker [[Ambrose Wetherell]]'s right (ch469).
- Looks in her early thirties through a "secret art", but is older than the Eternal Queen Victoria.

## Background

Not stated beyond her age.

---

## Personality

Calm and analytical: plays with a crystal ball until the Heart is mentioned, then leans back and tapping the armrest, weighs the political risk of stealing from the Vatican (ch469). Pragmatic; asks [[Ambrose Wetherell]] privately about the Naples mole (ch469).

---

## Abilities

Conjuration (9th circle); a crystal ball; life-extending art (ch469).

---

## Relationships

- [[Ambrose Wetherell]] - Speaker; she addresses him informally by his given name and checks his plan.
- [[Duncan Muirhead]] - fellow archmage; agrees his concerns about the Papal State are valid.
- [[Selwyn (Highland archmage)]] - fellow archmage (Enchantment Master).
- Queen Victoria and [[Pope Clement XV]] (historical-style figures) - the Queen approved the Blood of the Griffin restart; the Pope's reaction is her worry.

## Lore & Key Events
