## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Senior Highland Mage, a middle-aged bearded man, on reconnaissance north of Paris with an escort from the Black Watch Highland Infantry Regiment, covered by a feint ordered by Sir John French (ch289).

---

## Background

- Britannian. Seeks promotion within the Mage Corps rather than a colonial posting as Chief Mage Advisor; career details not stated (ch289).

---

## Personality

- Analytical and ambitious: judges the Saxon energy attack as comparable to a Ninth-Circle spell, and values reports that win high evaluations (ch289). Takes charge of recording evidence.

---

## Abilities

- Mage; uses a monocular-style magitech observation instrument and a magitech recording device. Circle level not stated.

---

## Relationships

- [[Ewan (Highland mage)]] - his junior mage partner; [[Malcolm (Highland mage)]] is the senior (ch289).
- Sir John French - sent a feint attack to cover the mission.

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch289: Watches the Mage Tower (Eiffel Tower) collapse under Saxon attack; notes the Gallic use of the tower to amplify low-circle spells; orders the soldiers to photograph the scene; the group withdraws on horses in the morning mist.
