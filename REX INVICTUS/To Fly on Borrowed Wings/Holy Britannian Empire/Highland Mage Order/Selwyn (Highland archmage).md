## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Enchantment Master and member of the Highland Mage Order's ruling Council of Archmages (ch469 text; the roster notes a first mention at ch467). Nationality: Britannian (Holy Britannia Empire). Rank/position does not change in the text.
- Seated first on [[Ambrose Wetherell]]'s left at the Council table.

## Background

Not stated.

---

## Personality

- Known in the Council for his reticence; he "broke his silence unprecedentedly" to speak (ch469). Otherwise not characterised.

---

## Abilities

- Enchantment school mage, titled Master. The Council reasons that the Empire's enchantment and alchemy techniques cannot synthesise a key component of its super-soldier plan (ch469). No specific spells shown.

---

## Relationships

- [[Ambrose Wetherell]] - presides over the Council he sits on (ch469).
- [[Edith Pembridge]] - fellow Council member, seated across the table (ch469).
- [[Duncan Muirhead]] - fellow member of the Highland Mage Order (ch345, ch469 context).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch469 - In the Council discussion of the 【Heart】, states that obtaining it would complete the final puzzle piece of the "Blood of the Griffin" super-soldier project, prompting a stir among the other Archmages.

## Status at ch473

Alive and a Council member; no further appearance after ch469.
