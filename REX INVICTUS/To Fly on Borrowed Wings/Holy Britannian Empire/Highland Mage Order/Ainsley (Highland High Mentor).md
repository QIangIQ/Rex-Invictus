## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- High Mentor (also "High-Level Mage / Tutor", "Sir") of the Highland (High-Level) Mage Corps, Evocation mage trained at the Scottish Mage Tower two decades earlier (ch37). First major antagonist, at the siege of Seville. Killed in ch64.

---

## Background

- Britannian; apprenticeship twenty years before the story; three apprentices accompany him (ch23): one, [[Clive (Highland apprentice)]], dies in a building trap (ch55), two are killed by [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] (ch58). Family: not stated.

---

## Personality

- Arrogant and humiliating toward allies: strips General Sanjurjo of command (ch53). Furious at the death of an apprentice (ch25).
- Capable of caution: flees at the white retreat flare when his Mage Armor cracks (ch37). In the final charge he draws a saber and shouts "For the glory of Holy Britannia!" (ch62-63).

---

## Abilities

- Senses spiritual energy and [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s gaze at 400 m (ch23-24); Fly, Fireball (ruby ring, ch61), Mage Armor, Life Sense, Turn to Stone, fifth-circle Transmute Rock (ch37), telekinesis against cavalry (ch52), Mordenkainen's Sword and Disintegrate scrolls (ch61-62).
- His Mage Armor withstood hundreds of rifle shots (ch93 reference). A Fireball scroll he requisitioned is later used at Sarajevo (ch138).

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - hunter and hunted; his notebook becomes the protagonist's manual (ch65-66).
- [[Edward (Konrad's adjutant)]] - punched out of the sky by him, then kills him with Disintegrate (ch61-63).
- [[Konrad von Seeckt]] - traps his Knights in mud (ch37).
- [[Colonel Rohloff]] ([[Colonel Rohloff]]) - kills him with telekinesis (ch52).
- General Sanjurjo - commanded by him.
