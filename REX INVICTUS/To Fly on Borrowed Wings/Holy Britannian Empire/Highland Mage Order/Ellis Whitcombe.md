## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- High Mentor and Seventh-Circle Evocation mage, addressed as "Master" by the Britannian staff. Leader of the 18 Highland Mages attached to the Britannian Expeditionary Force at Amiens (ch217). Elderly, white hair and beard, staff of white oak, dark silver-runed robe.

---

## Background

- Mentions 16 years earlier a meteor spell in an African colonial war, referenced by the Field Marshal (ch230). Otherwise not stated.

---

## Personality

- Calm and unreadable on the surface, in reality nervous before the first mage-versus-armoured-airship clash in history (ch220). Fierce and vengeful once his apprentices die (ch225-226). Candid and unsentimental: demands retreat, saying the Mages' lives are "not to be wasted against the Saxon War Machines for this damned piece of Gallic earth" (ch230).

---

## Abilities

- Mordenkainen's Magnificent Mansion scroll (ch217), Chain Lightning, Delayed Blast Fireball that downs airship L24 (ch220), Fly scrolls, Shield Spell, Dimension Door scroll, Bigby's Hand that slams [[Konrad von Seeckt]] into a wall (ch235), tracker bracelet linking his apprentices. Tracks [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] through a mark on a stolen detonator crystal (ch225-226).

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - hunts him for killing his apprentices and recognises him as the "[[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]"; nearly kills him but is wounded by his street bomb (ch225-229).
- [[Lachlan (Highland archmage)]] - fellow Seventh-Circle mage and ally; dies in the explosion.
- Field Marshal John French (real historical person) - commander he pressures to retreat (ch230, 238).
- [[Walther Krell]] - captain of airship L29, whose crew kill most of his eight mages (ch220, 227-228).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch217: war council at Amiens. ch219-220: leads eight mages against two Saxon airships, downs airship L24 but loses most of his group to L29.
- ch225: apprentices [[Tanner (Highland apprentice)]] and [[Sandgren (Highland apprentice)]] die at the railway bridge. ch226-228: chases [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] through Amiens until L29 intervenes.
- ch229: survives the street explosion with Dimension Door, left arm broken, and orders the Highland Mages to withdraw. ch230: tells French "this war was unwinnable" and mentions the Prometheus Project. ch238: sails home to report.
