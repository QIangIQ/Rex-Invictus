## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- 9th-circle Transmutation archmage, one of six on the High Council of Six, the governing body of the Highland Mage Corps, seated in the Mage Tower across the Thames from the Palace of Westminster (ch102-103).
- Later addressed as "Master" and seen in a purple mage robe; the Council member who represents the Mage Order at high-level imperial meetings, with the sharpest political instincts of the six (ch302, 345, 469).
- Nationality: Britannian.

---

## Background

Not stated beyond his council seat.

---

## Personality

- Calm, controlled, political; smooths his expression after a frown (ch102) and keeps it unchanged under the Queen's rebuke (ch302).
- Takes responsibility when pressed (ch302), but shows arrogance toward ordinary people (ch345) and a rare fanatical gleam when pitching the Antarctica Project (ch303).
- Weighs diplomatic consequences before acting; [[Ambrose Wetherell]] praises this (ch469).

---

## Abilities

Transmutation magic at the 9th circle; no specific spells shown. Wears the purple robe of the Mage Order.

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - enemy by proxy: he designates him a High-Value Target (ch302) and sits in the councils that hunt him (ch345).
- [[Ambrose Wetherell]] - council colleague and superior as Speaker; defers to him in ch469.
- [[Ainsley (Highland High Mentor)]] - the tutor whose requisitioned Fireball scroll he traces (ch138).
- Queen Victoria - reports to her and takes the blame for mage losses (ch302-303).
- Chairman (unnamed shadowed figure) - agrees to arrange sending someone to examine Saxon spellcasters (ch103).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch102-103: interviews the traumatized Seville survivor, then argues the Saxons must have gained something new and proposes re-evaluating their spellcasters.
- ch138: confirms scroll AX-734, a Fireball scroll, was checked out by [[Ainsley (Highland High Mentor)]], last seen in Seville; a fragment turned up in Sarajevo at the Archduke's assassination.
- ch302: takes the blame before Queen Victoria and designates [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] a High-Value Target.
- ch303: after the Eiffel Tower data, pushes the long-shelved Antarctica Project, a world-scale Mage Tower.
- ch345: under pressure from the Queen's ultimatum, takes part in the hunt for [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]].
- ch469: raises the Papal State objection to the plan to seize the Heart in Naples.

Alive and active on the High Council; no later fate stated.
