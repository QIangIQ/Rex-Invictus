## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Elderly Tier-8 Transmutation Archmage of the Highland Mage Order; the Order's highest combat power in the Britannian Expeditionary Force (ch350). Wears a deep purple robe and carries a staff with a large sapphire.

---

## Background

- Not stated. Personally chose his two Tier-5 gray-robed assistants and brought them to Gaul (ch352).

---

## Personality

- Reserved and composed; rests silent in the shadow of the command post while shells fall (ch350). Unmoved under bombardment, completely focused (ch352). Willing to rescue the Field Marshal when asked: "I will buy enough time for you" (ch350).

---

## Abilities

- Casts [Weather Control]: a circle about 5 km in diameter, 2.5 km radius, forcing blizzard and cold across the battlefield; he must stay still and concentrate (ch350-352). Two Tier-5 assistants alternate [Wall of Force], carrying scrolls; the barrier lasts about four hours (ch352).

---

## Relationships

- Field Marshal John French (real) - Britannian commander who reluctantly relies on him; credits him with saving the entire expeditionary force (ch350, ch352).
- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - his adversary at long range; the spell halts the Saxon winter assault, and [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] sees the enemy mage as a red dot on his system map (ch350-352).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch350: Saxon assault routs the first line; he volunteers and flies to the front with two mages.
- ch352: casts Weather Control; Saxon artillery cannot break his Walls of Force; Saxon attack halts and the Britannians regroup.
