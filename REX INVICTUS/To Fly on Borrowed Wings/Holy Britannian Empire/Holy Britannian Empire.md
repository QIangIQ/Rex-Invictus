**[[Holy Britannian Empire]]** is a faction hub of [[To Fly on Borrowed Wings]].

Britain analogue and old-world hegemon, ruled from London by the ageless Eternal Queen Victoria. Holds North America (with a North American Colony Conscript Legion) and leads in Armored Knight and mage technology, with 642 mages, 83% of them Evocation.
