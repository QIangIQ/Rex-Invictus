# TFBW Section Rules and Decisions

Up: [[To Fly on Borrowed Wings]]. Names: [[TFBW Name Conversion Bank]]. Roster: [[Character Roster (To Fly on Borrowed Wings)]].

Every rule, decision and open question for this story section, with who set it. "Author" means the vault owner's own instruction; "PROPOSAL" means a choice made while building the section that the author has not confirmed yet.

## Author rules (2026-11-26)

- Build the section from the author's character list and individual profiles and integrate it into the vault.
- "For all new/own names/characters, generate a new name for them, do follow the same naming style of their original names." The draft names are disliked.
- Do not replace any confirmed real people.
- Gabriel, Therese, Leona and Eugenia keep their names.
- Keep a conversion bank, because patching the old names in the manuscript may be a later task: [[TFBW Name Conversion Bank]].
- The protagonist must link to the [[UOE]]: [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]].
- Once the characters are done, sync the vault with the GitHub repository 1 for 1.
- Only this section syncs between the Datavault and the BLACKSTAR vault and GitHub. Datavault material such as Darktide modding or RxDt must never sync to BLACKSTAR or go into the Rex Invictus repository.
- Standing style rules apply: minimise em dashes, negative parallelism, rules of three and common AI writing tells. No vendor or assistant names in anything uploaded.

## Decisions made while building (PROPOSAL unless confirmed)

- **Title and folder (author, 2026-11-26).** The section and the new fic are both called *To Fly on Borrowed Wings* (short form TFBW). The folder is `REX INVICTUS/To Fly on Borrowed Wings/`, because the protagonist is a UOE cadet and so belongs to the Rex Invictus setting.
- **Folders.** One folder per faction and sub-group, with each hub note inside its own folder and the characters beside it (for example `Saxon Empire/Imperial Army (Saxon Empire)/Imperial Guard Instruction Assault Unit/`).
- **Template.** Character notes follow the [[Scathach]] layout: bold intro, aliases, Hierarchical Placement, Identity & Nature with source aspects, then Background, Personality, Abilities, Relationships, Lore & Key Events, Status at ch473 and Classification.
- **Source aspects.** "Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)" for every character, "UOE Aspect (far future)" for the protagonist, and "Real History (IRL)" where a character borrows a real family or replaces a real office holder. Tag `source/tfbw`.
- **Names.** New names keep the nationality, era and shape of the old one (surname only stays surname only, rank prefixes and "von" kept, spelling-variant pairs kept as pairs) and avoid exact matches to real 1910s leaders, the UOE name banks and existing vault notes. Football, celebrity and fiction joke names (Kahn/Kane, Ballack, Lahm, Hummels, Khedira, Klose, Gerrard, Rowan Atkinson, Anne Hathaway, Ollivander) became plain period names.
- **Real family surnames.** Where a surname ties an original character to a real family or to a kept character, only the given name changed: Konrad von Seeckt (son of the real Hans von Seeckt, brother of Eugenia), Edmund Charles Cavendish, Matei Mavrocordatos. Jürgen von Kleist was renamed in full (Detlef von Raschke) so he is not confused with the real Ewald von Kleist in the cast.
- **Families stay together.** Karl Hauser, Madam Hauser and their son became Emil Reinholt, Madam Reinholt and Benno Reinholt.
- **Unnamed characters keep their labels (author confirmed 2026-11-26: leave them unnamed for now)**: The General's Guard, the West-Lucia chemistry professor, the black-robed old man, the Scottish Captain, the teenage necromancer and the three Aragon girls. Groups and nameless walk-ons were left unnamed rather than given invented names.
- **File names.** Bare single names get a short role in brackets so links stay unique in the vault, for example [[Kunze (Saxon officer)]]. Slash pairs use the first spelling as the file name and keep the second as an alias.
- **Real people** (Mackensen, Ludendorff, Rommel, Haber and the rest) stay as plain text for now. No notes were made for them, so no empty link targets were added.
- **Placeholder damage.** The draft's placeholder pass had overwritten some real names and words. Restored: Otto Moritz Walter Model, Hans-Jürgen von Arnim, Georges Cuvier, Primo de Rivera, Nicolas Sarkozy, the footballer names, Saint Ivan of Rila, "Spell Jaeger" (a Britannian unit type). Konrad's call signs were restored as "Hercules" (ch398) and "Hawkeye" (ch414-415) in alias-list order. Colonel K (Kaspar von Ritzow's code name) and "Grand Master Fortis" (Leona's style) were restored from the alias lists.
- **Lost nicknames.** Gabriel's nicknames that the placeholder pass erased are shown as "?" with their chapter numbers, to be restored from the manuscript.
- **Pet name (author, 2026-11-26).** Therese's old private name for the protagonist ("Fritz", for Friedrich) is removed for now, with no replacement. Servants' "Friedrich" became "Gabriel".
- **The System.** PROPOSAL: the protagonist's game-like System is the broken AtRep ([[Répertoire du Savoir Athénien]]) he carried over from the UOE.
- **Premise.** The roster's premise section still says the protagonist came from "Blue Star" (modern Earth). The profile's UOE Citadel origin replaces it.
