## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Bearded commander in the International Brigades ("Eastern Europa Brigade, 2nd Regiment, 1st Battalion"), a volunteer fighting alongside Saxon forces in Aragon (ch33-50). Not Saxon; from the "West-Lucia Provisional National Government".

---

## Background

- From a Russian-like region; explains its civil war to [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] (ch47-48). The roster notes that another passage implies a Gallic origin but the text does not say; nationality is not firmly stated.

---

## Personality

- Straightforward, warm and idealistic: fights "for a world without oppression, where technology serves everyone" and against Britannian monopoly over Magical Energy (ch47). Laughs readily, offers strong liquor (ch47). Affectionate: hugs [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] farewell hoping "we don't meet on the battlefield" (ch77).

---

## Abilities

- Improvised weapons: makes Molotov cocktails (ch46); builds the HEAT / shaped-charge anti-armor warhead with [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] and the volunteer group (ch49).

---

## Relationships

- [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]] - respectful comrade; shakes hands (ch35), discusses ideals (ch47-48); his brigade's combat report corroborates [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s (ch78).
- [[West-Lucia chemistry professor]] - chemistry professor in his group who helped make the warhead (ch77-78).

---

## Lore & Key Events

- ch33-35: meets [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]. ch46-48: Molotovs, ideological talk. ch49: successful HEAT test on Armored Knight armor. ch77: farewell at Seville rail station.
