## Identity & Nature

### Europa Aspect (To Fly on Borrowed Wings, 1914-1915)

- Unnamed chemistry professor from a West-Lucia university; technical consultant of the International Brigades at the makeshift workshop beside the Teutonic Knights' camp (ch46-49). Also a member of the group led by [[Pavel (International Brigades)]].

---

## Background

Fled West-Lucia after publishing anti-war statements (ch46). Opposes reactionary rulers; part of the revolutionary camp.

---

## Personality

- Scholarly, bespectacled, tired and melancholic (ch46); polite, drinks delicately (ch47). Initially sceptical ("this completely violates traditional demolition theory"), then absorbed by calculations (ch46). Pragmatic gradualist: "Revolution is a tall ladder, not a single leap" (ch47). Humble: "Learning truly is boundless" (ch49). Laughs and accepts [[Gabriel Jagdfeld]]'s offer of Saxon beer (ch77).

---

## Abilities

Chemistry and metallurgy knowledge; calculates the explosive equivalent and optimal cone angle for the hollow charge; advises on zinc loss when melting brass (ch46).

---

- [[Pavel (International Brigades)]] - International Brigades commander and companion.
- [[Konrad von Seeckt]] - observer of the workshop (ch46, 49).
