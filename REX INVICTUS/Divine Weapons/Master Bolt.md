### Ownership

#### Creator
[[Zeus]]
The Master Bolt was the personal Divine Weapon and Symbol of Power of Zeus, the former God-King of [[Olympus|Mount Olympus]] and ruler of the [[Greek Pantheon]]. Rather than functioning merely as a weapon used to produce lightning, [[Poseidon|the Master]] Bolt was directly connected to Zeus' position and Divine authority as King of Olympus. This relationship became most apparent during the destruction of the Greek Pantheon, when Zeus deliberately broke the Master Bolt and sacrificed his Symbol of Power in exchange for enough Influence to temporarily impose the Territory of Olympus over a portion of the [[Greek Underworld]].[1][2]
The destruction of the Master Bolt was treated as an act of extreme desperation. [[Amon]], [[Erebus]] and [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] were shocked that Zeus had willingly destroyed his own Symbol of Power, and the resulting loss contributed to their fear that the Greek Pantheon was becoming so weakened that its continued survival had to take priority over their original plans.[2] The Master Bolt was therefore not simply one of Zeus' weapons; it was an artifact intrinsically connected to his authority and to the political and metaphysical strength of Olympus.

### Description
The Master Bolt manifests as a concentrated bolt of blue lightning. During the final war of the [[Greek Pantheon]], [[Zeus]] raised his hand toward the sky and a blue lightning bolt descended directly into his grasp, where it remained physically contained as [[Poseidon|the Master]] Bolt.[1]
The weapon's exact material composition, creator and original forging process have not been directly explained in the novel. Information originating solely from external Greek mythology regarding its construction should therefore not be added to the article unless independently confirmed by an MTW chapter.

### History

#### Zeus and Olympus
The Master Bolt belonged to [[Zeus]] and functioned as his personal Symbol of Power as the God-King of [[Olympus]].[1][2] The precise point at which Zeus obtained the weapon has not yet been directly narrated.
As a Symbol of Power, [[Poseidon|the Master]] Bolt possessed significance beyond its use as conventional armament. Its connection to Zeus represented part of his authority as King of Olympus, making its voluntary destruction an extremely serious act for both Zeus and the Pantheon.[2]

#### Fall of the Greek Pantheon
The Master Bolt's most important demonstrated use occurred during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s destruction of the [[Greek Pantheon]]. By Chapter 796, Victor Elderblood and his forces were permanently killing the armies of both [[Zeus]] and Kronus, while the Greek Gods were fighting inside the [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]], a region where the natural Influence of [[Olympus]] did not reach them. The Gods were additionally suppressed by the Negative Energy filling the battlefield and by [[Persephone Elderblood]], the true Ruler of the Greek Underworld.[1]
Realizing that the combined Olympian and Titan forces were losing, [[Ares]] pressured Zeus into taking more drastic action. Zeus then summoned [[Poseidon|the Master]] Bolt into his hand and invoked his authority as King of Olympus. Despite being visibly reluctant, he broke the weapon in half and declared the surrounding region the Territory of Olympus.[1]
The Master Bolt was explicitly described as being sacrificed in exchange for Influence. Immediately afterward, enormous quantities of lightning descended across the battlefield and an explosion of Zeus' power spread outward from his position.[1]
The act effectively imposed the Influence of Olympus upon territory that actually belonged to [[Persephone]]'s Underworld.

#### Persephone's retaliation
[[Zeus]]' use of [[Poseidon|the Master]] Bolt was considered a direct attack on [[Persephone]]'s authority as the Ruler of the [[Greek Underworld]]. Persephone immediately became furious and ordered [[Thanatos]] to prepare her army for war against the Olympian faction.[1]
The resulting Territory was powerful enough that Persephone could not simply dismiss it instantaneously despite remaining the legitimate Ruler of the Underworld. Chapter 800 explains that Zeus had used a Word of Power fueled by the Master Bolt to impose the declaration upon the region. Persephone retained superior fundamental authority because they were still technically inside her Underworld, but removing Zeus' imposed Territory required considerable Energy and time.[3]
Persephone consequently focused her Ruler authority on dismantling Zeus' Influence while her Underworld Gods fought the Olympians.[3]

#### Destruction
The Master Bolt was permanently broken by [[Zeus]] himself in Chapter 796.[1]
Its destruction immediately alarmed several of the beings overseeing the Greek conflict. [[Amon]] described a God intentionally breaking their own Symbol of Power as madness, while [[Erebus]] and [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] concluded that the [[Greek Pantheon]] was rapidly becoming too weak to survive if the conflict continued.[2]
There is currently no indication that [[Poseidon|the Master]] Bolt was repaired, reconstructed or recovered after the destruction of [[Olympus]].

### Symbol of Power
The Master Bolt is one of the clearest examples of a God's Symbol of Power shown in the story. The term should not be reduced to "favorite weapon"; destroying [[Poseidon|the Master]] Bolt carried consequences serious enough that ancient Gods such as [[Erebus]] and [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] immediately reconsidered their broader plans for the [[Greek Pantheon]].[2]
The precise universal mechanics governing every God's Symbol of Power have not been completely described, so the effects demonstrated by the Master Bolt should not automatically be applied identically to every Divine Weapon.

### Limitations
The Master Bolt's greatest demonstrated territorial effect required the destruction of the weapon itself.[1] [[Zeus]] therefore permanently sacrificed his Symbol of Power to obtain the necessary Influence.
The imposed [[Olympus]] Territory also did not permanently overwrite the [[Greek Underworld]]. [[Persephone]] remained the legitimate Ruler of the realm and was capable of removing Zeus' imposed Influence, although doing so required significant Energy and time.[3]
Consequently, [[Poseidon|the Master]] Bolt's sacrifice should be understood as a powerful temporary imposition of Zeus' authority rather than permanent ownership of Persephone's territory.
