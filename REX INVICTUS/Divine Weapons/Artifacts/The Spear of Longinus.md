The Spear of Longinus is a supernatural weapon associated with the death of Jesus, the most beloved son of the Heavenly Father. Originally an ordinary spear wielded by a Human, the weapon acquired an extraordinary killing property after taking Jesus' life, becoming capable of killing beings created by the Heavenly Father. Its effectiveness is determined by the nature of its target's existence rather than conventional physical strength, racial characteristics or Divine status.[1][2]
The Spear eventually came into the possession of [[Diablo]], the Primordial Demon who formerly ruled [[Biblical Hell]]. During the Demonic War, he used the weapon to ambush [[Lucifer]] Morningstar and later attempted to kill [[Lilith Elderblood]] with it. Both confrontations demonstrated that its specialized killing property could be circumvented through fundamental transformations of the target's existence. Lucifer's rebirth as an Elder God and Lilith's ascension to Godhood had severed the metaphysical conditions that originally made them vulnerable to the weapon.[2][3]

### Description
The Spear of Longinus possesses an ordinary and unremarkable appearance despite its exceptional supernatural properties. During [[Diablo]]'s confrontation with Lilith, [[Scathach]] recognized it as a simple spear without the luxurious decoration or elaborate construction commonly associated with powerful Divine Artifacts. Its significance originates from its historical connection to Jesus rather than any remarkable feature of its physical design.[3]
The weapon was originally wielded by a Human whom [[Lucifer]] described as mediocre. It was not presented as an artifact forged by a God, a weapon constructed through Divine craftsmanship or an instrument deliberately imbued with supernatural authority. Instead, it became special because it took the life of Jesus, an event that established its extraordinary relationship with the [[Heavenly Father]]'s creations.[1][2]
Its material composition, dimensions, original creator and precise supernatural mechanism have not been identified. The narrative establishes the event responsible for the [[Spears|Spear]]'s transformation without explaining the complete metaphysical process through which its killing property developed.[2]

### Origin and History

#### The Death of Jesus
Approximately two thousand years before the events of the Demonic War, the [[Spears|Spear]] of Longinus was involved in the death of Jesus. According to [[Lucifer]], the weapon was originally an ordinary Human spear and only acquired its extraordinary nature after killing the son most loved by the [[Heavenly Father]]. The event transformed the Spear into a weapon uniquely capable of killing the creations of the Biblical Creator, granting it an effectiveness that could not be explained by its ordinary material construction.[1][2]
The weapon's historical significance extends beyond its supernatural property. When [[Scathach]] encountered the Spear during [[Diablo]]'s battle with Lilith, its appearance brought back memories of events approximately two thousand years earlier. She remembered an exceptionally talented young man whom she had wanted to train, believing that he possessed the potential to become a formidable warrior. Her recollection carried a sense of melancholy because the young man's goodness had ultimately contributed to his unfortunate fate. The scene establishes Scathach's personal connection to the period surrounding the Spear's history, although it does not provide a complete account of her involvement in those events.[3]
Following Jesus' death, the Spear disappeared from Lucifer's knowledge. He became interested in acquiring it for his personal collection and attempted to locate it, but his search was unsuccessful. The weapon remained outside his possession until Diablo unexpectedly revealed that he had acquired it. Neither the identity of its subsequent custodians nor the circumstances through which Diablo obtained it have been established.[1]

#### Diablo's Ambush of Lucifer
During the Demonic War, [[Diablo]] arranged a confrontation with [[Lucifer]] by exploiting the latter's lingering attachment to his former wife, [[Lilith Elderblood]]. Although Lilith had been placed under Diablo's control, her emotions had not completely disappeared. She remained conscious of what was happening while her body obeyed Diablo's commands, making her an effective instrument for drawing Lucifer into a trap he was unwilling to ignore.[1]
When the confrontation was revealed, Lucifer had already been impaled through the chest by the [[Spears|Spear]] of Longinus. Diablo expected the weapon to be particularly effective against him because Lucifer had originally been created directly by the [[Heavenly Father]]. His subsequent transformation into a Fallen Angel and then a Demon did not, in Diablo's initial assessment, erase that original relationship. Diablo similarly regarded Lilith as a creation of the Heavenly Father and believed the Spear capable of killing her.[1][2]
Diablo's objective extended beyond killing his predecessor. He intended to obtain the heart of the First of the Fallen as an ingredient for his larger plans, making Lucifer's death valuable both strategically and materially. However, Lucifer had already recognized the trap and possessed information that fundamentally changed the confrontation. He initially attempted to convince Diablo that the Spear could no longer affect him because he was not the same kind of existence he had once been.[2]
Lucifer claimed that he had undergone a complete rebirth and become an Elder God. His transformation had replaced the original racial existence created by the Heavenly Father with something fundamentally different. Consequently, the weapon's specialized killing property could no longer recognize him as a valid target, allowing Lucifer to survive an injury that Diablo had expected to prove fatal.[2]
The confrontation also revealed that Lucifer had attempted to manipulate Diablo in return. He allowed the Spear to remain embedded in his chest and hoped Diablo would attempt to remove it himself. Lucifer subsequently expressed disappointment that Diablo had recognized something suspicious and abandoned the attempt, indicating that Lucifer had intended to turn the apparently successful ambush against its architect. The exact technique Lucifer intended to employ was not demonstrated.[2]
After Diablo refused to continue the attempted execution, Lucifer removed the Spear from his own chest and openly revealed his new Elder God nature. Diablo recognized that Lucifer's racial transformation had invalidated the conditions necessary for the weapon to kill him. More importantly, the transformation also rendered Lucifer's heart unsuitable for Diablo's intended purpose, forcing the Primordial Demon to seek an alternative ingredient.[2]
Lucifer briefly retained the weapon, but Diablo recovered it from his grasp before departing. This recovery was demonstrated when Lucifer attempted to hold the Spear again and discovered that it had already returned to Diablo's possession. Although the encounter failed to produce the death Diablo originally intended, he retained ownership of the weapon and subsequently employed it again during the final stages of the war.[2]

#### The Final Battle Against Lilith
The [[Spears|Spear]] reappeared during the concluding stages of the Demonic War, after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had liberated Lilith from [[Diablo]]'s control. With her free will restored, the Mother of Demons attacked her former captor using her true Demonic form, her Divinities and the God-Slaying sword Genesis. Her position as both a Demon Progenitor and a Goddess provided her with advantages that Diablo could not easily reproduce through physical strength or accumulated Energy alone.[3]
Diablo understood the dangers presented by Lilith's Divinities of Rottenness, Corruption, Despair and Beginning. Despite possessing enormous physical durability and Energy reserves, he lacked the same range of specialized supernatural abilities. Recognizing that an extended confrontation favored Lilith, he resorted to the Spear of Longinus as a weapon specifically intended to exploit her origin as a creation of the [[Heavenly Father]].[3]
The Spear's appearance provoked immediate reactions among the observers. Ariel condemned the presence of such a weapon in the hands of a Demon, while [[Scathach]] recognized it and recalled the events surrounding its historical significance. Its outward simplicity contrasted sharply with its reputation as a weapon capable of killing the Heavenly Father's creations.[3]
Diablo covered the Spear in Miasma before throwing it directly toward Lilith. However, both Victor Elderblood and the Heavenly Father immediately recognized that the attack would fail. Lilith intercepted the weapon with Genesis, producing a powerful explosion when the two weapons collided. She subsequently caught the Spear with her free hand and threw it back at Diablo, who avoided the returning weapon rather than risk being struck by it.[3]
The Spear's failure initially confused Natashia, who understood that Lilith had originally been created by the Heavenly Father. Victor explained that the crucial distinction was Lilith's ascension to Godhood. When an individual becomes a genuine God, the Soul undergoes a metamorphosis in which the characteristics of the former mortal existence are merged and transformed into an entirely new Divine existence. This process resembles a fundamental rebirth rather than an ordinary increase in supernatural power.[3]
Lilith's transformation had consequently placed her beyond the influence that the Heavenly Father originally possessed over her existence. Although she remained historically connected to him through her creation, her present Divine nature was no longer subject to the same metaphysical conditions. The Spear therefore failed against her for a reason comparable to its failure against [[Lucifer]], despite the two having followed different paths of transformation.[3]
The confrontation demonstrated that the Spear's exceptional killing property was not unrestricted. An individual could retain their historical identity, memories and relationship with the Heavenly Father while nevertheless ceasing to qualify as one of his creations for the purpose of the weapon's supernatural effect.[2][3]

### Powers and Abilities
The Spear of Longinus possesses a specialized supernatural killing property associated with the creations of the [[Heavenly Father]]. Its documented abilities and characteristics are significantly narrower than those of weapons designed to destroy Gods or supernatural beings indiscriminately.
- Creation-Specific Lethality: The [[Spears|Spear]] possesses the supernatural ability to kill beings created by the Heavenly Father. [[Lucifer]] identifies it as the only weapon capable of killing his Father's creations. Its effectiveness is tied to the target's continued metaphysical relationship with the Heavenly Father rather than merely their current racial classification.[2][3]
- Existence-Based Targeting: The weapon's special property distinguishes between a being who remains one of the Heavenly Father's creations and an individual whose existence has been fundamentally reconstructed. Lucifer's transformation into an Elder God and Lilith's Divine metamorphosis demonstrate that the target's current state of existence determines whether the specialized effect can operate.[2][3]
- Supernatural Transformation: The Spear acquired its extraordinary characteristics after taking Jesus' life. Its origin establishes that an otherwise ordinary Human weapon can acquire a supernatural property through an exceptional event without having been deliberately forged as a Divine Artifact.[1][2]
- Exceptional Durability: The weapon withstood direct contact with Genesis during Lilith's confrontation with [[Diablo]]. Although the collision released a significant explosion of power, the Spear remained intact and could immediately be caught and thrown back at its wielder.[3]
- Demonic Energy Compatibility: Diablo successfully enveloped the Spear in his Miasma during his attack against Lilith. The weapon remained functional and intact while carrying the additional Demonic Energy. This demonstrates compatibility with Diablo's particular method of empowering a physical attack, although no broader Energy absorption or transformation ability has been established.[3]

### Conditions and Limitations
The [[Spears|Spear]]'s extraordinary property operates according to specific conditions, several of which became apparent during [[Diablo]]'s unsuccessful attempts to kill [[Lucifer]] and Lilith.
Condition
Effect
Creation by the Heavenly Father
The Spear's special killing property is directed against beings who remain creations of the Heavenly Father. This is the fundamental condition established through Lucifer's explanation.[2]
Fundamental Racial Rebirth
A sufficiently complete transformation into another form of existence can invalidate the original relationship. Lucifer's rebirth as an Elder God prevented the Spear from killing him despite his historical origin as an Angel.[2]
Divine Soul Metamorphosis
Ascension to Godhood can reconstruct an individual's Soul into a new Divine existence. Lilith consequently escaped the weapon's special influence after becoming a Goddess.[3]
Physical [[Radar Warning Receiver (RWR)|Interception]]
The weapon is not demonstrated to be unblockable. Lilith intercepted Diablo's thrown Spear using Genesis before catching and returning it.[3]
Restricted Specialization
The Spear has not demonstrated the ability to universally kill every God, immortal, Demon or supernatural creature. Its extraordinary lethality is associated specifically with the Heavenly Father's creations.[2][3]

#### Metaphysical Rebirth
[[Lucifer]] provides the clearest demonstration that the [[Spears|Spear]]'s specialized effect is not determined exclusively by a target's history. Although Lucifer had originally existed as one of the [[Heavenly Father]]'s most prominent Angels, his transition into an Elder God fundamentally replaced the racial existence upon which the Spear's killing property depended. His immunity did not arise from conventional armor, physical durability or an ordinary resistance to piercing attacks; it resulted from the fact that he was no longer considered the same creation.[2]
This distinction is especially important because changing one's outward appearance or merely possessing greater physical strength is not established as sufficient. Lucifer specifically emphasized that he had been completely reborn into a different existence. The novel does not establish that every ordinary racial transformation produces the same result.[2]

#### Ascension to Godhood
Lilith's immunity originated from a different form of fundamental transformation. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explained that genuine Godhood involves the reconstruction of the Soul, incorporating the characteristics of the prior mortal state into a newly formed Divine existence. This metamorphosis changes the individual's relationship with the forces that originally governed them and, in Lilith's particular case, removed her from the [[Heavenly Father]]'s influence.[3]
The event demonstrates that becoming a Goddess can provide protection from the [[Spears|Spear]]'s special property without requiring the individual to become an Elder God. However, this should not be interpreted as proof that every form of Divine power, Blessing or temporary supernatural empowerment grants the same protection. Lilith's immunity was explicitly connected to her completed Soul metamorphosis.[3]

#### Unconfirmed Capabilities
The [[Spears|Spear]] has not demonstrated unrestricted Conceptual Erasure, automatic Soul Destruction, universal [[Immortality]] Negation or the ability to bypass every supernatural defense. Its documented supernatural property is capable of killing creations of the [[Heavenly Father]], but the precise mechanism through which that effect operates has not been explained in sufficient detail to assign these additional abilities.[2][3]
Likewise, the novel has not established whether the weapon's specialized effect applies equally to distant descendants of the Heavenly Father's creations, individuals possessing only partial ancestry derived from them or beings whose Souls have been modified without undergoing a complete rebirth. These circumstances should not be treated as equivalent to the directly demonstrated cases involving [[Lucifer]] and Lilith.

### Relationship with Genesis
The Spear of Longinus and Genesis are both supernatural weapons possessing highly specialized killing properties, but their demonstrated functions are different. Genesis is explicitly identified as a God-Slaying sword, while the [[Spears|Spear]] is distinguished by its ability to kill the [[Heavenly Father]]'s creations. The distinction is particularly important because Lilith wielded Genesis during the same battle in which [[Diablo]] attempted to kill her with Longinus.[2][3]

### Current Status
The [[Spears|Spear]]'s present location and ownership remain unconfirmed. Its last directly established use occurred during [[Diablo]]'s battle against Lilith, when he threw it at her after covering it in Miasma. Lilith intercepted the attack, caught the weapon and returned it toward Diablo, who avoided the returning Spear.[3]
Diablo subsequently continued fighting and was ultimately defeated by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], but the available account of the Spear's final appearance does not establish who recovered it afterward. There is no confirmed transfer of ownership to Victor, Lilith, the [[Heavenly Father]] or another member of the victorious factions. Accordingly, Diablo remains its last established long-term possessor rather than its confirmed current owner.[3][4]

### Trivia
- The Spear of Longinus originated as an ordinary Human weapon and acquired its extraordinary killing property through the death of Jesus, rather than being described as a deliberately forged Divine Artifact.[1][2]
- [[Lucifer]] had previously attempted to locate the weapon because he wanted to add it to his personal collection, but it had disappeared before he could acquire it.[1]
- [[Diablo]] sought to use the [[Spears|Spear]] against Lucifer to obtain his heart for a separate supernatural undertaking. Lucifer's Elder God transformation invalidated both the Spear's special killing property and the usefulness of his heart for Diablo's intended purpose.[2]
- Lucifer deliberately attempted to exploit the situation by encouraging Diablo to remove the Spear from his chest. Diablo recognized that something was wrong and refused to complete the action Lucifer wanted.[2]
- Diablo managed to recover the Spear from Lucifer's grasp without the latter immediately noticing, demonstrating that Lucifer's temporary possession did not prevent Diablo from reclaiming the weapon.[2]
- Ariel reacted with outrage upon seeing the Spear in Diablo's possession, regarding its use by a Demon as blasphemous.[3]
- [[Scathach]] recognized the weapon and recalled memories from approximately two thousand years earlier, including a talented young man whom she had wished to train.[3]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and the [[Heavenly Father]] simultaneously recognized that the Spear would fail against Lilith before the thrown weapon reached her.[3]
- Unlike Lucifer, whose immunity resulted from rebirth into the Elder God race, Lilith became immune through the Soul metamorphosis associated with her ascension to Godhood.[2][3]
- The historical wielder's identity has not been explicitly established in the relevant novel passages. The Spear's traditional association with the name Longinus does not independently confirm every detail of its real-world religious history within the novel's continuity.
