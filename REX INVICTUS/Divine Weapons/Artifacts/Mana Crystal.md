Mana Crystals, also called Mana Stones, are solid magical resources containing highly condensed Mana. Their principal function is to act as independent reservoirs of magical Energy. Rather than requiring a Witch, Rune user or other supernatural being to remain physically present and continuously expend their own power, a sufficiently powerful Mana Crystal can be connected to a supernatural system and function as its external Energy supply.[1]
Mana Crystals are particularly associated with Witches, whose technological products frequently depend upon them. This relationship gives the Witches considerable economic influence: purchasing Witch technology does not necessarily remove the customer's dependence upon the seller because the device may continue requiring Mana-based resources to remain operational.[1][2]
Their value varies enormously according to grade, size and Mana concentration. Known examples range from low-level Mana Stones normally costing approximately $10,000 to high-level Mana Crystals whose prices can reach billions of dollars.[1][2] Their usefulness extends beyond ordinary Witch products. [[Alexios Alioth]] demonstrated that Mana Crystals could sustain his spatial Runes, allowing enlarged buildings and later portable dimensional-storage objects to maintain internal spaces vastly greater than their external dimensions.[1][3]

### Terminology
The novel uses both Mana Crystal and Mana Stone when referring to the resource. Both terms appear in discussions concerning condensed Mana, Witch technology, grades, prices and supernatural Energy supplies, with no canonical distinction currently established between them.[1][2]
"Mana Crystal" is used during Alexios' explanation of their function as Energy sources, while "Mana Stone" is also used when discussing grades, pricing and Witch commerce.[1][2]

### Nature
Mana Crystals are explicitly described as stones containing condensed Mana.[1] Their defining characteristic is therefore the magical Energy contained within them rather than any confirmed physical composition.
Unlike a supernatural being directly sustaining a technique with personal Energy, a Mana Crystal can remain connected to a magical or Runic system after its creator leaves. Alexios directly compared this role to a battery while explaining how spatially enlarged buildings could remain operational without him continuously supplying Energy.[1]
This distinction is important. Mana Crystals are not shown independently producing the supernatural phenomenon they power. Instead, they supply the Energy required by another ability, Rune, magical construct or technological system.
The exact process by which Mana Crystals naturally form or are artificially produced has never been explained in the material covering their introduction. Although Witches possess enormous commercial control over them and commonly use them in their technology, the story does not establish that Witches personally create every Mana Crystal.[1]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] already possessed some knowledge of Mana Crystals before Alexios explained them. When the subject was raised, Victor searched through the memories he had previously acquired and recalled information concerning the stones dating from roughly five hundred years earlier.[1]

### Properties and Applications
- Mana Storage: Mana Crystals contain condensed Mana and act as reservoirs of magical Energy.[1]
- External Energy Supply: A Mana Crystal can supply Energy to a supernatural system without requiring its creator to remain present and continuously expend personal power.[1]
- Magical Battery: Alexios directly compared their role to a battery. He could create the spatial phenomenon himself, while the Mana Crystal supplied the Energy needed to keep it active afterward.[1]
- Witch Technology Power Source: Mana Crystals are commonly required by technological products produced and sold by Witches.[1]
- Runic Energy Supply: They can power supernatural structures maintained through Runes. Alexios specifically intended to combine Mana Crystals with his spatial Runes.[1]
- Spatial Expansion Maintenance: A mid-level Mana Crystal around 30 cm in size could maintain approximately 50 m² of expanded internal Space.[1]
- Large-Scale Spatial Infrastructure: With sufficient Energy, Alexios stated that creating approximately 1 km of enlarged Space was easy for him, while several high-level Mana Stones could sustain an internal region comparable to an entire country within a house.[1]
- Dimensional Storage: The same principle was adapted for portable storage. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] commissioned large suitcases and smaller bags whose interiors were enormously greater than their exterior dimensions.[1]
- Portable Infrastructure: Completed spatial luggage allowed enormous quantities of material to be transported through an object small enough to carry conventionally. Ruby later possessed one of the large suitcases, while June used one of the smaller 500 m² bags.[3][4]
- Persistent Magical Infrastructure: Because an external reservoir can replace continuous personal Energy expenditure, Mana Crystals are particularly useful for supernatural systems intended to remain operational for long periods without their creator remaining nearby.[1]

### Spatial Expansion
One of the most detailed demonstrations of Mana Crystals occurred during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Vlad's plans for a new city beside Clan Snow's territory.
Victor proposed using [[Alexios Alioth]]'s control over Space and Runes to construct buildings whose interiors were much larger than their physical exteriors. Alexios confirmed that he could create such structures but immediately identified Energy supply as the primary problem.[1]
When Alexios previously created a portal in Japan, he could personally provide the Energy necessary to sustain his technique. A city containing numerous spatially enlarged buildings was fundamentally different: Alexios could create the Runes, but he could not remain permanently beside every structure and continuously power them himself.[1]
Mana Crystals provided an external solution.
For an internal expansion of approximately 50 square metres, Alexios estimated that one mid-level Mana Crystal approximately 30 cm in size would be sufficient.[1]
The price immediately demonstrated the difficulty of using the method on a city-wide scale. A crystal of that level was valued at approximately $500 million, while high-level crystals with greater Mana concentration could cost billions. Agnes concluded that even extremely wealthy Vampire families possessed financial limits and that powering an enormous number of structures in this manner could become economically unsustainable.[1]
The limitation came from Energy supply rather than Alexios' ability to manipulate Space. Alexios stated that if sufficient Energy were available, creating approximately 1 km of expanded Space would be easy for him. With several high-level Mana Stones, he could theoretically create an internal region comparable to an entire country within the physical Space of a house.[1]
The Mana Crystals themselves do not perform this spatial manipulation. Alexios creates and defines the enlarged Space through his own supernatural power and Runes; the crystals function as the Energy reservoirs that allow the resulting structure to continue operating.
Victor immediately recognized the commercial implications. A supernatural faction possessing both spatial Runecraft and reliable access to Mana Crystals could sell or rent enlarged properties while charging recurring fees for the Energy infrastructure necessary to maintain them.[1]

### Dimensional Storage
The discussion surrounding spatially enlarged houses quickly expanded into portable storage.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] realized that the same principle could be applied to luggage: rather than physically transporting the contents of an entire residence, a person could place those possessions within an object whose interior Space had already been expanded through Alexios' Runes.[1]
Alexios estimated that, once the method was established and taught to Natalia, they could potentially produce approximately 10–20 spatial bags per day. He nevertheless recognized that large-scale production could not continue indefinitely because the underlying Energy problem remained. Creating the Runic structure was only one part of the process; every finished item still required sufficient power to maintain its expanded interior.[1]
Victor subsequently commissioned:
- four large reinforced suitcases whose openings were large enough for a person to pass through and whose interiors were designed around an approximately 2 km-scale expanded space;[1]
- seven smaller bags possessing approximately 500 m² of internal Space.[1]
Victor intended the large suitcases for himself, [[Scathach]], Agnes and Natashia, while Vlad separately ordered an even larger version through the Royal Treasury.[1]
The project was successfully realized. Ruby later possessed one of the large suitcases and explicitly praised the enormous portable interior provided by Alexios' spatial work.[3] June likewise possessed one of the 500 m² bags during the Demonic War and considered the capacity barely sufficient for her accumulated possessions, while envying the much larger door-sized suitcase.[4]
These objects demonstrate that Mana Crystal-supported spatial technology was not merely theoretical. The system progressed from a proposal for enlarged buildings into functioning portable dimensional storage within the same general period.[1][3][4]

### Witch Technology
Mana Crystals form an important part of the commercial ecosystem surrounding Witch technology. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] compared the arrangement to purchasing a product only to discover that the customer must continue purchasing the resource required to make that product function.[1]
This creates two interconnected economic advantages for the Witches:
- they manufacture and sell highly desirable supernatural technology;
- many of those products depend upon Mana-based resources that must continue being acquired after the original purchase.[1]
Consequently, the sale of a Witch-made artifact or device does not necessarily end the economic relationship between the customer and the Witches.
This dependence contributes to the unusual position occupied by Witches within supernatural society. Unlike many supernatural races whose influence primarily derives from military strength or territorial rule, Witches possess technology, magical expertise, products and resources wanted by numerous other factions.
Vampires and [[Werewolves]] were described as being among the major consumers of Witch products, while Gods also conducted extensive trade with Witches because each side possessed rare materials and supernatural products desired by the other.[2]
Mana Stones represent one part of that larger commercial system.

### Economics
Mana Crystals are an exceptionally valuable supernatural commodity whose price varies according to grade, Mana concentration, physical size, purchase volume and the behavior of the Witch conducting the sale.[1][2]
Their pricing is sufficiently extreme that even some of the wealthiest supernatural rulers consider large-scale Mana Crystal consumption financially problematic.[1]

#### Witch Monopoly
The Witches possess enormous influence over Mana Crystal commerce. When Alexios explained that Witch technology commonly used Mana Crystals, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] immediately recognized the arrangement as a form of monopoly: customers purchased the technological product and could subsequently remain dependent upon Witch-controlled supplies for the Energy required to operate it.[1]
Victor considered the monopoly valuable enough that he briefly contemplated recruiting independent or "rogue" Witches and establishing a competing supernatural industry. The central obstacle was access to Mana Crystals themselves. Victor explicitly noted that easy access to the stones would allow his faction to compete much more effectively within that market.[1]
The discussion demonstrates that technical knowledge alone is insufficient to reproduce the Witches' economic position. A competing faction would also require reliable access to the resources necessary to sustain the technology.

#### Bulk Purchases and Price Manipulation
The Witches do not necessarily maintain fixed prices.
During the [[Supernatural Gathering]], [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and [[Scathach]] discussed a typical example of Witch commerce. A low-level Mana Stone could normally be purchased for approximately $10,000. If the Witch seller discovered that a customer needed a large number of stones for an important project, the same Mana Stone could suddenly be priced at approximately $100,000.[2]
Nothing in the discussion indicates that the stone itself became stronger. The increased price resulted from the seller recognizing the buyer's dependence and exploiting the scale of the purchase.
The same problem had already been anticipated during the spatial-city project. Once the Witches realized that Vlad and Victor needed large quantities of Mana Crystals, the people involved expected the price to increase further.[1]
This made large-scale Mana Crystal infrastructure considerably more expensive than calculating the ordinary unit price alone would suggest.

#### Supernatural Consumers
The high prices do not prevent supernatural civilizations from purchasing Witch products.
Vampires, Gods and [[Werewolves]] were specifically discussed as sufficiently wealthy to continue paying Witch prices, even while resenting them.[2] Vampires and Werewolves were among the largest consumers of Witch products, while Gods possessed their own Divine artifacts, materials and treasures and therefore maintained a major trade relationship with the Witches rather than depending entirely upon them.[2]
The Mana Stone market consequently exists inside a much broader supernatural economy involving magical technology, rare materials, Divine products, artifacts and specialized services.

#### Strategic Importance
Mana Crystals possess value beyond their direct price because access to them can determine whether certain supernatural technologies are economically viable.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Vlad possessed Alexios, an individual capable of creating extraordinary spatial infrastructure, yet even his abilities could not remove the need for a sustainable Energy supply. The cost and accessibility of Mana Crystals therefore became the principal bottleneck separating a powerful supernatural technique from practical mass deployment.[1]
A faction capable of securing a large independent Mana Crystal supply could consequently reduce its dependence upon the Witches and exploit technologies that would otherwise be prohibitively expensive.

### Known Limitations
- Finite External Reservoir: Mana Crystals function as Energy supplies rather than as the supernatural effect itself. The system being powered still requires the correct technology, Runes, [[Magic]] or supernatural ability to make use of that Energy.[1]
- Extremely High Cost: Mid-Level and High-Level crystals can be extraordinarily expensive. Even wealthy Vampire rulers considered the cost of powering an entire spatially expanded city difficult to sustain.[1]
- Supply Access: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s comments demonstrate that obtaining large quantities of Mana Crystals is not simple despite their commercial availability.[1]
- Bulk Price Inflation: Witches may increase the price when they discover that a customer requires large quantities.[1][2]
- Infrastructure Bottleneck: Alexios could potentially produce many spatial bags through Runecraft, especially if Natalia assisted him, but the continuing need for Energy prevented effortless unlimited mass production.[1]
- Power Requirement Scales with Effect: Larger supernatural systems require correspondingly greater Energy supplies. Approximately 50 m² could be maintained by one mid-level crystal, whereas a country-sized internal Space required several high-level stones.[1]
- No Independent Spatial Manipulation: Mana Crystals do not themselves enlarge Space. They supply Energy to Alexios' spatial Runes and abilities.[1]
- Economic Dependence: A device powered through Mana Crystals may continue imposing costs long after the initial technology has been purchased.[1]
