The Dragon Heart is a living Divine Artifact and one of the central technological foundations of the [[Elderblood Empire]], functioning as an extraordinarily powerful Energy generator and the primary source of power for the Empire's integrated Energy infrastructure. Created through the combined scientific expertise of Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II and [[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]], and subsequently transformed by Victor Elderblood, the Dragon Heart is an artificial construct designed to reproduce the Energy-generating characteristics of a Dragon's heart through Divine Materials, Draconic Runes, [[Junketsu]] and Victor's own Soul.[1]
Originally developed as the prototype for a future infinite battery, the construct underwent a fundamental transformation when Victor incorporated the properties of Junketsu, infused it with his Energy, inserted a fragment of his Soul and altered its underlying Reality. The result was a living artificial Dragon Heart capable of producing immense quantities of pure, unaltered Energy without being restricted to any particular elemental, Divine, Demonic or Draconic attribute. Its initial measured production increased from the prototype's baseline of 100% to more than 10,000%, with the output continuing to rise after its transformation.[1]
Unlike conventional Energy generators, the Dragon Heart can produce Energy without predetermined characteristics, allowing the resulting power to be converted into whatever form is required by the connected technology or supernatural system. This versatility made it a foundation for strategic weapons, artificial Souls, clone production, advanced Imperial research and infrastructure operating on a planetary or greater scale. Its development subsequently expanded beyond ordinary generation when it became integrated with World Cores and [[World Trees]], establishing an interconnected system through which Energy could be gathered, circulated, regulated and distributed throughout the Empire.[1][2]
The Dragon Heart is not identical to the biological Dragon Heart within Victor's body. Although the artificial construct was modeled after the principles governing his Draconic physiology and contains a fragment of his Soul, it remains a separate artifact rather than a replacement for his natural heart. Victor's own biological Dragon Heart was subsequently described as possessing superior efficiency to the artificial counterpart serving his Empire.[1][3]

### History

#### Development of the Original Prototype
The Dragon Heart originated from the technological research of Velnorah and her daughter Velina during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s preparations for the future expansion of his Empire. Both women possessed extraordinary scientific knowledge, with Velnorah drawing upon the technological foundations of the former [[Thaloria Empire]] and Velina demonstrating exceptional aptitude for research, engineering, Technomancy and the analysis of complex supernatural systems.[1]
Their original construct was a floating spherical artifact created through the combination of Technomancy, Runes and Divine smithing. The device represented an extremely advanced technological achievement and was intended to become the foundation of a future Energy system capable of supplying Victor's growing civilization. Although the prototype was already functional, its designers continued examining possible failures, refining its stability and considering how its existing technology could be improved.[1]
Velina identified more than three million possible failure scenarios associated with the device and wanted to eliminate every potential weakness. Velnorah recognized that absolute reliability could not be guaranteed when the researchers lacked complete knowledge of the Universe, explaining that improvements would necessarily continue as additional information became available. Their approach demonstrated that the original project was not merely concerned with generating enormous quantities of power; it also required a stable technological foundation capable of operating safely within an expanding civilization.[1]
The two researchers spent approximately two weeks stabilizing the artifact before Victor intervened. Despite their considerable investment, neither objected when Victor began modifying it, since the information obtained during development would allow them to reconstruct the original prototype if necessary. Their work provided the scientific foundation upon which Victor subsequently built the Dragon Heart.[1]

#### Creation of the Dragon Heart
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] arrived at the laboratory after examining the technological developments associated with his expanding Empire. Having studied the information contained within the prototype, he recognized that its existing structure could be fundamentally improved rather than merely supplied with greater quantities of Energy. His decision was influenced by his responsibilities as Emperor and his growing awareness of the dangers represented by civilizations beyond the familiar supernatural territories of Earth and [[Nightingale]].[1]
Manifesting [[Junketsu]] around his body, Victor adopted an armored form approximately five meters tall and extended the Soul Weapon's dark-violet substance toward the spherical artifact. Upon contact, the prototype began transforming as the properties of Junketsu altered and reinforced its structure. The sphere expanded into a massive icosahedron approximately ten meters tall, with Draconic Runes appearing across its surface.[1]
Victor subsequently incorporated his Energy and a fragment of his Soul into the construct while using his supernatural authority to alter its Reality. During the process, Draconic wings manifested from his back and began pulsating with violet power as the sound of an enormous heartbeat echoed throughout the laboratory. When the transformation stabilized, the spherical prototype had disappeared, replaced by a dark-violet icosahedral artifact constructed from Divine Materials and covered in Draconic Runes. Its rhythmic pulsations resembled those of a living Dragon's heart rather than the mechanical operation of a conventional reactor.[1]
Velnorah immediately recognized the extraordinary nature of the transformation. Rather than simply improving the original technological design, Victor had created an entirely different class of artifact. The previous construct had been intended to serve as the prototype for a future infinite battery, whereas the completed Dragon Heart had become the living infinite battery itself. Its structure combined Divine engineering with the supernatural characteristics of Victor's Soul, the properties of Junketsu and the Energy-producing principles associated with Draconic physiology.[1]
Before departing, Victor entrusted the artifact to Velnorah and declared that it would henceforth function as the core of his Empire. The two researchers subsequently examined its capabilities and chose to name the finished construct the Dragon Heart, although Velnorah initially referred to it using the plural name Dragon Hearts.[1]

#### The First Energy Measurements
Immediately following its creation, the researchers examined the artifact's output and discovered that its Energy production had increased beyond the expected limits of the original design. Where the prototype's previous output had registered at 100%, the transformed Dragon Heart exceeded 10,000% and continued increasing. The measurement represented an initial comparison against the prototype rather than a definitive statement of the artifact's maximum capacity.[1]
Even more significant was the nature of the Energy being produced. Velina discovered that the output lacked the usual characteristics of Negative Energy, Positive Energy and [[Draconic Energy]]. Instead, the artifact generated completely pure, unaltered Energy that could subsequently be transformed into whichever attribute its user desired. This meant that the same central source could theoretically support a variety of technologies and supernatural systems without requiring each to possess an entirely separate type of generator.[1]
Velnorah immediately recognized the implications for Imperial weapons development. The availability of pure convertible Energy made it possible to plan weapons based upon concentrated Negative Energy and potentially combine their effects with Divine Concepts such as Destruction. At the same time, the vast output simplified the development of battle clones and other advanced technological projects whose Energy requirements would ordinarily limit their production.[1]

#### The Pillar Project
The creation of the Dragon Heart also contributed to the beginning of the Pillar Project, an undertaking intended to develop a genuinely living artificial intelligence capable of serving as the mind of the Empire. Unlike conventional artificial intelligences that merely processed information according to programmed instructions, the proposed entity would possess genuine life while retaining the computational advantages of advanced Imperial technology.[1]
During their examination of the Dragon Heart, Velina questioned whether artificial beings created through its Energy would possess a connection to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] because the artifact contained a fragment of his Soul. Velnorah concluded that sentient creations whose existence originated from the Dragon Heart's Energy would fall within Victor's Domain, giving him a potential method of controlling or eliminating artificial beings that rebelled against the Empire. She also reasoned that the connection could extend to descendants of artificially created beings if the same Energy formed the foundation of their Souls.[1]
This conclusion represented an important proposed safeguard for artificial armies, particularly because Velnorah believed that no amount of genetic conditioning or technological preparation could absolutely eliminate the possibility of rebellion. However, the precise limits of this authority were discussed as part of the researchers' understanding of the artifact and should not be interpreted as proof that every machine or individual receiving electricity from the Imperial network automatically becomes subject to Victor's direct control.[1]
The Pillar Project eventually developed alongside the work that produced [[Akasha Elderblood]]. Although the original proposal concerned a living artificial intelligence, Akasha herself was created directly by Victor through his own flesh, Blood, Soul and Eldritch Divinity. She consequently became a genuine living daughter of Victor rather than an ordinary artificial intelligence that had simply achieved consciousness through technological development.[4][5]

#### Akasha and the Imperial System
Upon her creation, Akasha immediately recognized a connection to the enormous Energy source associated with her father. Her existence was linked to the Dragon Heart and the technological systems of the Empire, allowing her to interact with Imperial infrastructure and process information on a scale far beyond conventional artificial intelligence.[5]
Akasha described herself as the Overseer of the Empire and demonstrated that the Imperial System was connected to her through the Dragon Heart. The artifact therefore became more than a power source for ordinary technology: it also supported the infrastructure through which the Empire's central living intelligence could access, supervise and coordinate the systems under her authority.[5]
Despite this connection, Akasha and the Dragon Heart remain distinct existences. Akasha possesses her own Soul, identity and Eldritch authority over Control, while the Dragon Heart functions as an artificial Energy-producing artifact connected to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] through a fragment of his Soul. Their relationship is therefore best understood as an integration between the Empire's central intelligence and its primary Energy infrastructure rather than the Dragon Heart itself becoming Akasha.[4][5]

#### Expansion of Imperial Infrastructure
As the [[Elderblood Empire]] expanded beyond the territories where the Dragon Heart was originally created, the need for advanced Energy infrastructure increased dramatically. The availability of enormous quantities of power enabled researchers to undertake projects that would previously have been impractical, but it also introduced the challenge of distributing that Energy across increasingly distant territories.[2]
The Dragon Heart consequently became integrated with the development of World Cores, artificial planetary systems designed to regulate and distribute Energy throughout worlds under Imperial authority. Rather than supplying every individual facility directly from the original artifact, the Empire began constructing a hierarchical network in which World Cores acted as intermediary power centers.[2]
These developments represented a transition from an extraordinarily powerful individual artifact into an interconnected Imperial Energy system capable of supporting planetary civilizations. Its relationship with [[World Trees]] also allowed the network to function through a reciprocal exchange of power instead of depending exclusively upon Energy flowing outward from a single central generator.[2]

#### The World Core Network
The structure of the Imperial Energy network was explained by Akasha during the introduction of the [[World Core]] system. The Dragon Heart occupied the highest position within the proposed Energy hierarchy, supplying World Cores responsible for the maintenance, supervision and nourishment of individual Imperial planets.[2]
At the time of this explanation, Velnorah had already upgraded the World Cores and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had connected them to the Dragon Heart. Each World Core could then transmit Energy throughout its planet using channels formed through the Root systems of [[World Trees]]. However, the complete infrastructure necessary to handle such enormous quantities of power across the entire Empire had not yet been constructed, and only certain facilities were sufficiently developed to utilize the available output efficiently.[2]
The system also revealed that the Dragon Heart's relationship with World Trees extended beyond planetary transmission. Akasha explained that the artifact not only produced enormous quantities of Energy passively but also channeled the Energy supplied by World Trees connected to Victor. Those World Trees could contribute power to the Dragon Heart while continuing to sustain their respective planets, allowing the Heart to redistribute Energy through the same planetary network.[2]
This arrangement established a reciprocal Energy cycle in which the Dragon Heart and World Trees reinforced one another. The World Trees supplied additional Energy to the central network, while the Dragon Heart redistributed its combined output through World Cores and planetary channels. The result was described as an effectively endless Energy cycle, although the existence of that cycle did not eliminate the need for the physical and supernatural infrastructure required to transmit its power.[2]

#### Development of the Time-Acceleration Project
The Dragon Heart became particularly important during the preparations for the Empire's enormous Time-acceleration project, which sought to provide Imperial civilization with extended periods of development while comparatively little Time passed outside the affected region.[6][7]
The undertaking required an immense quantity of Energy alongside advanced Runes, planetary structures and the Divine authorities necessary to manipulate Time. Natalia recognized the Dragon Heart as an important potential Energy source because of its practically infinite capacity. Violet nevertheless noted that the existing artifact had not been designed specifically to discharge the exceptionally large quantities of power required by the proposed ritual in the intended manner. The original plan relied upon [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Jeanne personally acting as its principal batteries rather than expecting the Dragon Heart alone to sustain the entire undertaking.[7]
During the ensuing discussion, Victor revealed an important feature that had not previously been fully explained: the Dragon Heart was designed to be upgraded without requiring the reconstruction of the entire Energy network connected to it. Any improvements made to the artifact could affect the connected system without damaging the infrastructure already dependent upon its output. This eliminated the necessity of creating another Dragon Heart merely to obtain greater production capacity and demonstrated that Victor had anticipated the need for future expansion when he designed the finished artifact.[7]
Although the possibility of upgrading the Dragon Heart was established, Victor did not consider such an improvement necessary for that particular undertaking. The Time-acceleration ritual continued developing around Victor, Natalia, Jeanne and the other Divine participants, supported by the Empire's growing technological and supernatural infrastructure.[7]

#### Two Hundred Thousand Years of Imperial Development
The Dragon Heart remained part of the Empire's broader Energy infrastructure as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] initiated the temporal ritual intended to grant Imperial civilization approximately two hundred thousand years of internal development over a much shorter external period. The undertaking placed extraordinary demands upon the Empire's Energy sources because every affected world continued undergoing the processes associated with planetary rotation, biological development, technological production and the passage of Time.[8][9]
Victor personally served as the principal Energy source sustaining the ritual, drawing upon the extraordinary reserves available through his own existence and his connections to [[World Trees]]. Even those reserves were subjected to unprecedented pressure by the enormous scale of the undertaking. The Dragon Heart remained an important component of the Empire's established infrastructure, but the ritual itself should not be attributed exclusively to the artifact or interpreted as a demonstration that it could independently sustain every process involved.[8][9]
The Empire's accelerated development further emphasized the distinction between possessing enormous quantities of Energy and being capable of using that Energy effectively. Continued technological advancement required distribution systems, suitable materials, planetary infrastructure and intelligent supervision, all of which remained essential regardless of the Dragon Heart's enormous production capacity.[2][9]

#### Later Developments
As the [[Elderblood Empire]] continued expanding, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s enormous Energy reserves and control over the forces sustaining his civilization became increasingly important. During the later conflict involving the barriers separating Sectors, he was capable of supplying power to his territories, maintaining the temporal ritual and supporting the Empire while resisting enormous external pressure upon the Sector's boundaries.[3]
During these events, Victor's biological Dragon Heart was distinguished from the artificial Dragon Heart belonging to the Empire. His physical heart was described as more efficient than the Imperial artifact, providing another source of tremendous Energy alongside the [[World Trees]] connected to him. This distinction demonstrates that the artificial Dragon Heart reproduces extraordinary aspects of [[Draconic Energy]] generation without necessarily matching the capabilities of the original biological organ from which its fundamental principles were derived.[3]

### Appearance and Structure
The Dragon Heart takes the form of an enormous dark-violet icosahedron approximately ten meters tall. Its exterior consists of Divine Materials covered in Draconic Runes, while its interior incorporates the supernatural properties of [[Junketsu]] and a fragment of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul. Despite its artificial construction and geometric appearance, the artifact behaves like a living organ, emitting powerful heartbeats during its creation and continuing to pulsate afterward.[1]
Its appearance reflects the fusion of several fundamentally different supernatural systems. Divine Materials provide the artifact's physical structure, Draconic Runes contribute to its supernatural architecture, Junketsu allows the construct to endure and accommodate Victor's power, and the Soul fragment establishes a permanent connection with its creator. Victor's alteration of Reality transformed these components into a unified living Energy source rather than leaving them as independent enhancements applied to a conventional machine.[1]

### Energy System

#### Infinite Energy Generation
The Dragon Heart's defining characteristic is its ability to function as a living infinite battery. Velnorah explicitly distinguished the artifact from its original prototype, explaining that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had transformed a device intended for future development into the completed Energy source itself. The artifact consequently produces enormous quantities of power without functioning merely as a conventional battery that depends upon a previously stored finite reserve.[1]
The novel nevertheless distinguishes between the concept of infinite Energy generation and the practical amount of power that a particular system can transmit or discharge at once. During the Time-acceleration project's planning, the Dragon Heart was described as possessing practically infinite capacity, but its existing design was not considered automatically suitable for every possible form of Energy expenditure. Its generation capacity therefore should not be confused with unlimited instantaneous output through every connected device.[7]

#### Pure Energy Generation
Unlike many supernatural generators, the Dragon Heart produces Energy without an inherent attribute. Its output was specifically observed to lack the characteristics of Positive Energy, Negative Energy and [[Draconic Energy]], instead existing as pure, unaltered power that could subsequently be transformed according to the requirements of its user.[1]
This characteristic makes the artifact extraordinarily versatile because it does not require a different fundamental generator for each type of supernatural application. Technologies capable of processing the output can assign the appropriate properties to the Energy after generation, allowing a shared source to support numerous independent systems. The chapter does not, however, establish that the artifact automatically grants every possible Divine Concept or supernatural ability to anything powered by it.[1]

#### Energy Conversion and Attribution
The Dragon Heart's unaltered Energy can be transformed into other forms of power after generation. Velnorah immediately considered applications involving concentrated Negative Energy and weapons potentially enhanced through Concepts such as Destruction, demonstrating that the artifact's usefulness depends not only upon the amount of Energy it produces but also upon the properties that connected technologies can impose upon that Energy.[1]
Consequently, the Dragon Heart should be classified as a highly versatile Energy source rather than a generator restricted to producing [[Draconic Energy]]. Its name originates from its construction, behavior and relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s existence, not from an intrinsic requirement that every unit of Energy it generates possess a Draconic attribute.[1]

#### World Tree Energy Channeling
The Dragon Heart's relationship with [[World Trees]] underwent a significant development following the establishment of the [[World Core]] network. Akasha explained that the Imperial artifact not only produces enormous quantities of Energy passively but also channels Energy from World Trees connected to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Because Dragons possess an exceptional relationship with Nature and the Dragon Heart represents an important aspect of their Energy physiology, the connection allows the Imperial artifact to operate in conjunction with the vast supernatural resources available through Victor's World Tree network.[2]
World Trees maintain their respective planets and can simultaneously contribute Energy to Victor or systems associated with him. In the Imperial network, this permits the Dragon Heart to receive additional power through the connected World Trees while distributing Energy back into planetary infrastructure through World Cores. The World Trees therefore function as both contributors to the broader Energy system and essential elements of its planetary distribution network.[2]
This additional contribution does not mean that the Dragon Heart originally depended upon World Trees to become an infinite battery. Its extraordinary generation capacity was already established at the moment of its creation, before the later planetary network was explained. The World Tree connection expanded the system's functionality and established a reciprocal Energy cycle rather than constituting the original source of its artificial infinite-battery nature.[1][2]

#### Reciprocal Energy Cycle
The integration of the Dragon Heart, World Cores and [[World Trees]] established an interconnected Energy system in which power could flow between central and planetary infrastructure. The World Trees connected to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] could contribute Energy to the Dragon Heart, while the Dragon Heart supplied the World Cores responsible for individual planets. Those World Cores subsequently distributed Energy through channels associated with the Root systems of World Trees.[2]

#### System-Wide Upgradability
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] specifically designed the Dragon Heart to support future upgrades without requiring the complete reconstruction of the Energy network already connected to it. During preparations for the Time-acceleration project, he explained that improvements made to the artifact could affect its connected systems without damaging them, demonstrating that the original design anticipated further expansion.[7]
This feature is particularly important for an Empire whose technological requirements continually increase. Rather than treating the existing artifact as an unchangeable endpoint, the Dragon Heart can theoretically receive improvements while preserving the integrity of its broader infrastructure. The capability was directly confirmed by Victor, although the chapter did not establish that any particular subsequent upgrade had already been completed.[7]

### Powers and Abilities
The Dragon Heart possesses several extraordinary properties arising from its construction, its connection to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and its integration with the Empire's technological infrastructure.
- Infinite Energy Generation: The artifact functions as a living infinite battery, continuously producing enormous quantities of Energy. Its existence represents a fundamental improvement over the original technological prototype rather than merely an enlarged conventional Energy reserve.[1]
- Pure Energy Generation: Produces unaltered Energy without inherent Positive, Negative or Draconic characteristics, allowing its output to serve as the foundation of multiple specialized systems.[1]
- Energy Conversion: Its generated Energy can be assigned different properties according to the requirements of connected technologies. Velnorah immediately considered applications involving concentrated Negative Energy and Concept-enhanced strategic weaponry.[1]
- Energy Channeling: Channels Energy supplied by [[World Trees]] connected to Victor, expanding its function beyond independent Energy generation.[2]
- Energy Distribution: Supplies connected World Cores, which redistribute power throughout individual planets using channels associated with World Tree Roots.[2]
- Energy Synchronization: Supports an interconnected Energy network in which generation, planetary regulation and distribution operate through compatible Imperial systems.[2]
- Soul Connection: Contains a fragment of Victor's Soul, providing stabilization and establishing a direct supernatural relationship between the artifact and its creator.[1]
- Artificial Life Support: Its Energy was identified as a foundation for artificial Souls and sentient creations. Velnorah further theorized that artificial beings created through this Energy would remain within Victor's Domain.[1]
- Strategic Weapons Support: Supplies the Energy necessary for the development of advanced weapons, including proposed cannons based upon concentrated Negative Energy and potential Concept-enhanced attacks.[1]
- Clone Production Support: Greatly reduces the Energy limitations associated with producing battle clones and other artificial beings intended for Imperial military and technological development.[1]
- System Integration: Functions as a connection point between the Empire's Energy infrastructure and Akasha, who demonstrated the ability to access the Imperial System through her connection with the Dragon Heart.[5]
- System-Wide Upgradability: Can be upgraded in ways that improve the connected Energy network without automatically damaging or requiring the complete reconstruction of existing infrastructure.[7]
- Exceptional Durability: Its structure is reinforced through Divine Materials, Draconic Runes, [[Junketsu]] and Victor's power. Velnorah considered it extraordinarily difficult to destroy, although absolute indestructibility was not established.[1]

### Applications

#### Imperial Energy Infrastructure
The Dragon Heart functions as the central Energy source of the Empire's integrated planetary network. Through World Cores and the Root systems of [[World Trees]], its output can be transmitted to worlds requiring supernatural power for the maintenance of cities, technological infrastructure and other Imperial facilities. This system reduces the need for every individual location to possess an independent generator of comparable capability.[2]

#### Strategic Weapons
Velnorah recognized that the Dragon Heart's enormous output would accelerate the development of strategic-class weapons. Because its Energy is unaltered, researchers can potentially use it as the foundation for weapons employing specific attributes rather than being restricted to ordinary Draconic power. Her immediate proposals included cannons based upon concentrated Negative Energy and the possibility of incorporating Concepts such as Destruction.[1]
These applications should be distinguished from abilities personally demonstrated by the Dragon Heart. The artifact serves primarily as the source of Energy that advanced weapons can utilize; it is not itself shown directly firing every proposed type of attack.[1]

#### Artificial Souls and Clone Production
The availability of enormous quantities of pure Energy made the creation of battle clones and artificial Souls significantly more practical. Velnorah and Velina intended to use this advantage when developing future armies and sentient synthetic beings, while the Dragon Heart's connection to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] offered a potential safeguard against rebellion among those whose existence originated from its Energy.[1]
The later creation of Akasha demonstrates the relationship between technological planning and Victor's direct supernatural authority, although Akasha herself was created through Victor's flesh, Blood, Soul and Eldritch Divinity rather than being produced solely from the Dragon Heart's Energy.[4][5]

#### Imperial System and Administration
The Dragon Heart also supports the systems through which Akasha supervises the Empire. Following her creation, Akasha demonstrated access to Imperial technology through her connection with the artifact, allowing her to monitor and manage systems connected to the central infrastructure.[5]
The Dragon Heart therefore possesses strategic significance beyond its output alone. By combining extraordinary Energy generation with the Empire's living central intelligence, it contributes to a civilization in which enormous technological systems can be coordinated without relying entirely upon ordinary administrators or disconnected mechanical controls.[5]

#### Planetary Development
The [[World Core]] network allows the Dragon Heart's Energy to be incorporated into the maintenance and development of planets. World Cores regulate the supply distributed to their assigned worlds, while World Tree Root channels provide pathways through which that power can reach infrastructure requiring it. This arrangement is particularly valuable to a civilization expanding across multiple planetary systems because it establishes a shared framework for Energy production and distribution.[2]

#### Large-Scale Imperial Projects
The Dragon Heart's immense reserves and capacity for further upgrades made it an important consideration during preparations for the Empire's Time-acceleration project. However, the completed ritual depended primarily upon [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and other powerful Divine participants rather than the Dragon Heart alone, demonstrating that extraordinary Energy generation remained only one component of the Empire's most ambitious undertakings.[7][8][9]

### Durability and Limitations
The Dragon Heart possesses extraordinary durability due to its construction from Divine Materials, the properties of [[Junketsu]], its Draconic Runes and the fragment of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul incorporated into its structure. Velnorah considered its physical and supernatural defenses so formidable that she questioned whether destroying the artifact was realistically possible.[1]
However, the Dragon Heart has not been established as absolutely indestructible. Velnorah's assessment reflects the extraordinary nature of the artifact relative to her knowledge and capabilities rather than a confirmed metaphysical rule guaranteeing that no force could ever damage or destroy it.[1]
The artifact also possesses operational limitations that distinguish its generation capacity from the performance of the technological network surrounding it. These limitations concern transmission, discharge requirements, infrastructure compatibility and the distinction between an enormous available Energy reserve and the amount of power that can be safely utilized by a particular system.[7][2]

### Relationship with Victor's Biological Dragon Heart
The artificial Dragon Heart derives its fundamental inspiration from the supernatural characteristics associated with a Dragon's heart, particularly its role as an extraordinary Energy-producing organ. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s own biological Dragon Heart serves as part of his Draconic physiology, whereas the Imperial artifact reproduces comparable principles through Divine engineering, Soul manipulation and Reality alteration.[1]
The relationship between the two does not make them interchangeable. Velnorah explicitly explained that the artificial Dragon Heart was connected to Victor through a fragment of his Soul but was not part of his physical body. Destroying it would therefore not cause Victor to die, and its continued existence was not established as a necessary condition for his survival.[1]
Later developments further distinguished their capabilities. During the enormous Energy expenditure associated with Victor's defense of his Sector, his own biological Dragon Heart was described as more efficient than the artificial construct belonging to the Empire. This distinction is consistent with the artifact's original purpose: it was designed to reproduce extraordinary [[Draconic Energy]] generation on behalf of an entire civilization rather than replace the actual heart of the Blood Dragon Primogenitor.[3]

### Strategic Importance
The Dragon Heart represents one of the most important technological developments in the history of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Before its creation, Velnorah and Velina were attempting to develop a sufficiently powerful and reliable Energy core through conventional Divine engineering and Technomancy. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s intervention transformed that project into a living Energy source capable of providing the foundation for weapons, synthetic life, planetary infrastructure and technologies whose demands would otherwise exceed the resources of ordinary civilizations.[1]
Its strategic importance increased as the Empire expanded. The [[World Core]] network transformed the Dragon Heart from an individual artifact into the central component of a reciprocal Energy system connected to [[World Trees]] and planetary infrastructure, while its integration with Akasha allowed the Empire's living intelligence to interact directly with systems supported by the artifact. Victor's confirmation that the Dragon Heart could be upgraded without damaging connected systems further established its suitability for a civilization expected to continue advancing technologically and geographically.[5][7][2]
The artifact ultimately demonstrates the relationship between the extraordinary personal abilities of Victor and the scientific expertise of his family. Its original structure originated from Velnorah and Velina's research, while its final transformation depended upon Victor's Soul, Draconic nature and authority over Reality. The resulting construct provided the Empire with something considerably more valuable than an exceptionally powerful individual weapon: a common Energy foundation capable of supporting the continuing development of an interstellar civilization.[1][2]

### Trivia
- The Dragon Heart was originally a spherical prototype created by Velnorah and Velina, who spent approximately two weeks stabilizing it before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s intervention.[1]
- Velina identified more than three million possible failure scenarios associated with the original device, reflecting the researchers' concern with long-term stability rather than Energy production alone.[1]
- The completed artifact is approximately ten meters tall, dark violet in color and shaped like an icosahedron covered in Draconic Runes.[1]
- The Dragon Heart's initial measured Energy production exceeded 10,000% of the prototype's original output and was still increasing when the researchers examined it.[1]
- Although the artifact is called the Dragon Heart, its generated Energy does not inherently possess Draconic attributes.[1]
- Velnorah initially proposed the plural name "The Dragon Hearts" for the artifact.[1]
- The fragment of Victor's Soul incorporated into the Dragon Heart does not make the artifact one of his biological organs or an automatic weakness capable of killing him if destroyed.[1]
- The artifact's connection to Victor led Velnorah to propose that sentient artificial beings created using its Energy would fall within his Domain, potentially allowing him to eliminate rebellious synthetic armies.[1]
- The Dragon Heart was connected to the development of the Pillar Project, which sought to create the first genuinely living artificial intelligence for the Empire.[1]
- Akasha later became connected to the Imperial System through the Dragon Heart, but she was created directly by Victor through his own existence rather than merely evolving from ordinary artificial intelligence.[4][5]
- The [[World Core]] network established that [[World Trees]] can both contribute Energy to the Dragon Heart and serve as part of the channels through which the Heart's output is redistributed to planets.[2]
- Victor designed the Dragon Heart so that future upgrades could affect its connected systems without necessarily damaging the existing Imperial Energy infrastructure.[7]
- Victor's biological Dragon Heart was later described as more efficient than the artificial Dragon Heart serving the Empire.[3]
