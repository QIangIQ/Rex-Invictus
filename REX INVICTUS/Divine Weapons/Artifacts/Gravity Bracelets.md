Gravity Bracelets are advanced gravity-based training devices used by the Elderblood Clan during the physical conditioning of its exceptionally powerful members. The bracelets impose adjustable gravitational pressure directly upon the wearer's body, allowing even Dragon Gods to train under loads far beyond anything achievable through conventional weights.[1]
Rather than measuring their output through conventional numerical multipliers, the bracelets use stellar gravitational references. Their demonstrated range extends from the gravity associated with ordinary stars toward neutron stars, while their highest stated setting corresponds to the gravity associated with a black-hole singularity.[1]

### Overview
The Gravity Bracelets were directly shown during [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]]'s retraining of [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]]. Albedo wore four bracelets simultaneously, with one attached to each wrist and ankle. Despite already possessing the physiology of a Dragon Goddess, the gravitational pressure produced by the devices slowed her body considerably.[1]
The bracelets form part of [[Scathach]]'s method of returning powerful fighters to their physical foundations. Instead of allowing Albedo to depend entirely upon [[Magic]], Dragon Runes and increasingly complicated supernatural techniques, Scathach forced her to strengthen the body and fundamentals supporting those abilities.[1]
The technology's inventor, internal construction, Energy source and exact method of generating its gravitational field have not yet been explained.

### Functions
The bracelets generate artificial gravitational pressure centered upon the wearer. Increasing their setting forces the user's entire body to operate beneath a progressively greater gravitational load, turning basic movement into physical resistance training.[1]
Because the pressure acts upon the wearer rather than merely adding conventional weight to the limbs, the devices remain useful to beings whose natural physical strength has already rendered ordinary training equipment essentially meaningless.

#### Gravity Manipulation
The primary function of the bracelets is the generation and adjustment of extreme gravity. Their output can be progressively increased according to the physical condition of the trainee.[1]

#### Physical Conditioning
The increased gravity forces the wearer's muscles, skeletal structure and overall physiology to continuously resist compression and increased effective weight. Albedo's bracelets were powerful enough to drastically restrict her movement despite her Dragon-Goddess body.[1]

#### Strength Training
Every physical movement becomes progressively more difficult as the gravitational setting increases. Walking, moving the arms or performing ordinary combat motions therefore become strength exercises without requiring physically larger weights.[1]

#### Durability Training
The trainee must personally endure the gravitational pressure being generated. The bracelets do not grant automatic protection from their own effect; [[Scathach]] explicitly stated that activating the maximum setting on Albedo at her current level would crush her.[1]

#### Movement Restriction
The bracelets can also function as extreme physical restraints during training. Albedo's active setting significantly slowed her body, requiring her to learn to move normally despite the additional gravitational burden.[1]

### Elderblood Training
The Gravity Bracelets are incorporated into the basic training used by [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] for members of the Elderblood family whose bodies and powers have reached extreme levels.[1]
Despite the severity of the equipment, [[Scathach]] regarded Albedo's stellar-level training as insufficient. When Albedo reacted to the prospect of surviving even greater gravitational pressure, Scathach pointed toward [[Siren Elderblood]] as an example of the standard she expected her to eventually reach.[1]
Siren had already endured the gravitational pressure associated with a singularity during training. Her achievement demonstrated that the bracelets' highest stated benchmark was not merely a theoretical maximum; sufficiently developed members of the Elderblood family could actually be trained under such conditions.[1]
Scathach considered Albedo's belief that such an environment should be impossible to survive a weakness in mentality. In her view, the extreme physiology of Dragon Gods meant that apparently impossible physical conditions should be tested and overcome rather than automatically accepted as absolute limitations.[1]
[[Anna Elderblood]] explained that this approach forms part of the family's broader training philosophy for [[True Dragons]]. The objective is not merely to teach newly developed Dragons how to use their powers, but also to reshape their mentality so they properly understand the capabilities and potential of their new existence.[1]

[[Tyche]] is expected to undergo the same general training after her transformation into a True Dragon. Anna explained that the family's basic training would be used both to teach Tyche about her new powers and to develop the mentality expected of a Dragon.[1]
The chapter does not explicitly state that Tyche will personally wear the Gravity Bracelets, although her planned training is discussed directly alongside Albedo and Siren's gravity training.[1]

### Limitations
- Physical tolerance: The bracelets can generate gravitational pressure greater than the wearer can survive. [[Scathach]] stated that Albedo would be crushed if immediately exposed to the maximum setting.[1]
- Progressive training: The user must develop sufficiently before advancing to higher gravitational benchmarks. Albedo was being gradually moved from stellar gravity toward neutron-star gravity rather than immediately receiving the maximum output.[1]
- No automatic resistance: Nothing indicates that the bracelets protect the user from their generated gravity. Their training value specifically comes from requiring the wearer's own body to resist it.[1]
- Unknown numerical output: The exact acceleration or force produced by each level has never been given. The devices are described through astronomical reference points instead.[1]
- Unknown construction: Their materials, Energy source, internal technology and creator remain unrevealed.
- Imperial distribution unknown: Although the devices exist within the civilization of the [[Elderblood Empire]], the novel has currently only demonstrated them within Elderblood family training. It has not confirmed that Gravity Bracelets are standard equipment issued to ordinary Imperial soldiers.

### Trivia
- A demonstrated training set consists of four bracelets: two worn on the wrists and two on the ankles.[1]
- Their calibration system uses stars and other compact stellar objects rather than conventional numerical gravity multipliers.[1]
- Albedo was already a Dragon Goddess when stellar-level gravity was able to heavily restrict her movement.[1]
- [[Siren Elderblood]] had progressed far enough to survive singularity-level gravitational training before Albedo began undergoing the same level of physical reconditioning.[1]
- [[Scathach]] considers the ability to eventually endure such extreme conditions part of the physical standard expected from sufficiently developed Elderblood Dragon Gods.[1]
