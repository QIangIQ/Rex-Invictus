Stellar Arsenal is the Fourth Legion of the [[Elderblood Empire]] and its principal organization dedicated to scientific research, technological development, superweapon engineering and the study of forbidden technologies. Unlike the Empire's conventional military Legions, whose responsibilities primarily involve combat, defense or law enforcement, the Stellar Arsenal concentrates upon developing the scientific and technological foundations necessary to sustain the Empire's continued expansion and military superiority. Its research encompasses weapons capable of destroying entire solar systems and greater astronomical structures, genetic engineering, temporal manipulation, alien technology and other experimental fields involving exceptionally dangerous supernatural or technological phenomena.[1]
The Legion was established in Year 50 of the [[Imperial Calendar]] through the combined efforts of [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]], [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]], [[Natalia Elderblood née Alioth]] and [[Aline Elderblood Valefar]]. Its creation consolidated the Empire's scientific knowledge and research resources into a unified organization responsible for technological advancement. The founders also established the acquisition and examination of knowledge originating from extraterrestrial civilizations as one of the Legion's principal responsibilities, ensuring that discoveries made beyond Imperial territory could contribute to the Empire's development.[1]
The organization is commanded by Ruby Elderblood Scarlett, with Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II and Aline Elderblood Valefar serving as substitute commanders whenever Ruby is unavailable. Its personnel include scientists, engineers, witches and automatons, with the latter particularly useful when conducting experiments too dangerous for conventional researchers. The Legion's responsibilities extend across numerous scientific disciplines, combining advanced technology with [[Magic]], Runes, biological engineering and other supernatural systems.[1]
Although the Stellar Arsenal was formally established during the Empire's accelerated development, its principal researchers had already exerted considerable influence over Imperial science. Ruby, Velnorah and Aline possessed authority over the Empire's technological development before the formation of the Seven Legions, with existing research departments on [[Yggdrasil]] already employing cloned scientists and other specialized personnel. The Stellar Arsenal transformed this broader scientific establishment into a formal Imperial Legion, providing an organizational structure through which the Empire could consolidate its accumulated knowledge and pursue increasingly ambitious projects.[2][1]
As the Empire developed, its scientific establishment expanded beyond ordinary weapons manufacturing into fields involving galactic engineering, sophisticated biological modification and the manipulation of fundamental aspects of Reality. Ruby, Velnorah and Aline remained among the most influential researchers in the Empire, while the next generation of the Imperial Family increasingly contributed to their work. Their projects reflected the Empire's unusual combination of scientific knowledge, advanced supernatural powers and access to resources unavailable to ordinary civilizations.[3]

The Stellar Arsenal functions as the Empire's principal scientific and technological Legion, combining the expertise of several of its greatest researchers with a large supporting workforce. Its mandate includes the development of new technologies and the study of existing phenomena, making the organization important to both civilian progress and military preparation.[1]
The Legion's scientific responsibilities distinguish it from organizations whose principal functions concern direct warfare. Although the First Legion and Second Legion possess access to extraordinarily destructive weapons, their established specializations concern offensive and defensive military operations. The Stellar Arsenal instead specializes in the research, development and control of such technologies, including experimental weapons whose effects may extend far beyond ordinary planetary warfare.[1]
Its importance also derives from the Empire's policy of integrating different technological and supernatural traditions. Technologies originating from alien civilizations, Runes, Witchcraft, genetic engineering and advanced Space-Time manipulation can all become relevant to the same research undertaking. This multidisciplinary approach allows Imperial researchers to pursue solutions unavailable to civilizations whose scientific knowledge remains restricted to a single technological or supernatural system.[2][1]

### History

#### Volume 9: Those Who Rule Reality
The formal establishment of the Stellar Arsenal occurred during the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s extraordinary period of accelerated development. [[Victor Elderblood]] intended to strengthen his civilization sufficiently to confront the advanced empires and supernatural authorities inhabiting the Upper Sectors. To accomplish this, he initiated a massive temporal ritual capable of granting the Empire approximately 200,000 years of internal development while only one year elapsed outside the affected region.[4]
The ritual allowed the Empire to develop its population, scientific infrastructure, military resources and supernatural capabilities over an enormous period. Victor and his Empresses remained actively involved through their Avatars, supervising the civilization's progress and intervening in important decisions while their principal bodies sustained the ritual. The Empire's administration was reorganized around specialized military and institutional branches, eventually producing the Seven Imperial Legions.[4][5]
The Empire already possessed considerable scientific resources before the accelerated era. Ruby, Velnorah and Aline had established themselves as its principal technological authorities, and their research extended across several departments responsible for different areas of development. The rapid expansion of Imperial territory and the acquisition of knowledge from numerous civilizations nevertheless created the need for a more centralized system through which discoveries could be preserved, shared and developed into practical applications.[2][1]
During Year 50 of the [[Imperial Calendar]], Ruby, Velnorah, Natalia and Aline descended through their Avatars and undertook the unification of the Empire's accumulated knowledge. Their efforts resulted in the establishment of the Fourth Legion, Stellar Arsenal, which assumed responsibility for research, development and the control of superweapons and forbidden technology. The organization was also tasked with acquiring and studying knowledge originating from civilizations beyond the Empire, allowing future discoveries to become part of its scientific foundation.[1]
The Legion's original responsibilities encompassed an unusually broad range of subjects. Its scientific establishment was authorized to pursue weapons of mass destruction capable of devastating solar systems and larger astronomical regions, genetic experiments, temporal manipulation and the study of advanced technologies considered too dangerous or restricted for ordinary use. Scientists, engineers, witches and automatons formed its commonly identified personnel, supplying the organization with both intellectual resources and practical experimental capabilities.[1]
Ruby became the Legion's principal commander, with Velnorah and Aline designated as her substitutes. This structure reflected their pre-existing importance within the Empire's scientific establishment. Although they possessed nominal departmental responsibilities, the three women already exercised broad influence over technological development, and their scientific authority extended beyond the limits of any single specialized laboratory.[2][1]
The Stellar Arsenal subsequently became one of the central institutions supporting the Empire's military and technological expansion. The Fifth Legion was expanded to protect the Imperial Family, while the First Legion and Second Legion were later established around Year 1000 to assume the principal offensive and defensive responsibilities of the conventional Imperial military. The scientific advancements pursued by the Empire provided the technological environment in which these increasingly powerful military organizations could operate.[1][6]
The growth of Imperial research continued as the Empire acquired additional knowledge and developed its existing technologies. Ruby, Velnorah and Aline remained the foremost authorities associated with scientific advancement, while [[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]] became increasingly involved in the research undertaken by the older generation. Their combined expertise allowed the Empire to address increasingly complex technological problems, including genetic manipulation and the development of specialized scientific personnel.[3]
The expansion of the Empire's technological capabilities also produced projects whose danger and strategic value required extraordinary secrecy. Among the most significant was Project Polaris, a classified undertaking begun during the 100,000th millennium of the Imperial Calendar. The project involved constructing a gigantic Divine warship capable of employing Reality manipulation, Space-Time distortion, destructive conceptual powers and [[Eldritch Energy]]. Its most dangerous planned armament involved channeling the Concept of END through the vessel, allowing it to threaten targets through an effect far more fundamental than conventional destructive weaponry.[7]
The construction and development of Polaris involved Ruby, Velnorah and Aline, whose scientific expertise was essential to addressing the dangers associated with its weapon systems. The project was sufficiently classified that even many members of the Imperial Family possessed little information concerning its existence or capabilities. Rather than permitting knowledge of the Empire's most important strategic weapons to circulate freely, Victor restricted access to individuals directly involved and those possessing the appropriate Imperial authorization.[7][8]
Further scientific developments included the creation of specialized technomantic organisms and cloned researchers. Velnorah and Velina investigated genetic combinations intended to produce personnel with the adaptability necessary to perform advanced scientific work and enough personal power to survive dangerous environments. Their efforts reflected a growing emphasis upon the development of dedicated scientific workforces rather than relying entirely upon the personal involvement of the Imperial Family in every research undertaking.[3]
The Empire's researchers also investigated the possibility of creating contradictions within the fundamental rules of Reality. Ruby, Aline, Velnorah and Velina pursued theories concerning the creation of an absolute paradox, intending to discover a means of threatening the Primordial authorities whose existence and influence restricted the native Universe. Their experiments encountered severe limitations because Reality continually corrected contradictions arising within its own structure. The research remained experimental, with the actual production of a stable paradox proving far more difficult than conventional manipulation of physical or supernatural phenomena.[9]
The ongoing research establishment also received new material originating from Victor's encounters with powerful Divine beings. Among these developments was a fragment of Divinity that Victor entrusted to Ruby for investigation. The material represented another potential subject for the Empire's scientific researchers, illustrating the continued exchange between the Emperor's supernatural discoveries and the experimental work undertaken by his family.[10]

### Foundation and Development

#### Unification of Imperial Knowledge
The central purpose behind the Stellar Arsenal's foundation was the unification of the Empire's scientific knowledge. By consolidating the discoveries and expertise accumulated across Imperial territory, its founders created an organization capable of coordinating research involving many different civilizations and supernatural traditions.[1]
The Empire's rapid expansion had produced access to numerous forms of knowledge. Advanced engineering, [[Magic]], Runes, supernatural biology and technologies originating from previously independent civilizations all contributed to its growing scientific foundation. Organizing these resources under a dedicated institution allowed the Empire to pursue projects requiring expertise from several disciplines simultaneously.
The Stellar Arsenal was also responsible for acquiring and studying alien knowledge, ensuring that technologies encountered during future expansion would not remain isolated discoveries. Such knowledge could be examined, compared with existing Imperial research and potentially incorporated into new developments.[1]
This responsibility made the Legion important beyond the immediate production of weapons. Its mandate concerned the preservation, accumulation and practical exploitation of scientific knowledge, supporting the Empire's ambition to become a civilization capable of competing with the greatest powers of the Cosmos.

#### Earlier Imperial Research Departments
The research establishment preceding the Stellar Arsenal already contained several specialized departments. On [[Yggdrasil]], the Empire maintained a Weapons Technology Department and a Biological Department, both associated with the research activities of Ruby, Aline and Velnorah.[2]
The Biological Department was formally headed by Ruby, while Aline maintained authority over personnel associated with weapons technology. Each department employed hundreds of researchers, many of whom were clones specifically created to undertake scientific work. These arrangements provided the Empire with a workforce capable of assisting the Imperial Family in numerous technical disciplines.[2]
Despite these formal departmental assignments, Ruby, Aline and Velnorah exercised authority extending across the broader scientific establishment. Their positions within the Imperial Family and their extraordinary technical expertise meant that their responsibilities were not confined to the departments nominally assigned to them.[2]
The later establishment of the Stellar Arsenal continued this approach on a larger organizational scale. Its creation did not mark the beginning of Imperial science, but the formal consolidation of an already influential research establishment into a dedicated Legion.

#### Research and Development Doctrine
The Stellar Arsenal's guiding principle is expressed through its slogan, "Progress at any cost." Its established responsibilities include several fields whose experimentation can create dangers extending far beyond conventional technological development.[1]
The Legion is authorized to investigate technologies capable of destroying entire solar systems and greater astronomical structures, manipulate biological systems through genetic engineering and conduct research involving Time and other fundamental properties of Reality. Such projects require extraordinary scientific resources and methods capable of containing the consequences of experimental failure.
The inclusion of automatons among its common personnel reflects the danger of certain research environments. Artificial workers can undertake tasks that would expose conventional researchers to unacceptable risks, allowing the Legion to investigate dangerous technology without requiring living scientists to perform every hazardous operation directly.[1]
Nevertheless, the organization's slogan should not be interpreted as proof that every experiment is conducted without oversight or that the Empire has abandoned all concern for safety. Some of its most dangerous projects are subject to strict secrecy, and Imperial researchers employ containment measures and specialized personnel where necessary.

#### Superweapon Development
The development and control of superweapons constitutes one of the Stellar Arsenal's defining responsibilities. Its mandate includes the production of weapons of mass destruction capable of operating on the scale of entire solar systems and beyond.[1]
These capabilities place its technological objectives far above conventional planetary military engineering. The Empire's most powerful enemies possess abilities capable of threatening astronomical structures, making weapons designed only for ordinary terrestrial warfare insufficient for future conflicts.
The Stellar Arsenal consequently pursues technologies capable of supporting the Empire's confrontations with advanced civilizations and powerful supernatural beings. Its responsibilities encompass both destructive capability and the control of technologies whose effects may be dangerous even to the civilization developing them.
The confirmed existence of solar-system-scale and greater weapons within the Legion's mandate does not establish that every weapon under its control possesses equivalent power. The destructive capabilities of individual devices depend upon their design, resources and intended function.

#### Forbidden Technology
The Stellar Arsenal is expressly responsible for the control of forbidden technology. This mandate recognizes that certain technologies may possess strategic value while also presenting dangers requiring specialized oversight.[1]
Within the [[Elderblood Empire]], technological development can involve supernatural materials, Divine Energies, biological modification and powers capable of affecting Space, Time or the structure of Reality. Technologies involving these principles may produce consequences that ordinary researchers or military institutions are unprepared to address.
The Legion's role therefore extends beyond inventing new devices. It also concerns the investigation and control of existing technologies considered too dangerous for unrestricted use.
The exact classification system through which the Empire determines whether a technology is forbidden has not been fully established. Likewise, the existence of this mandate does not automatically place every dangerous artifact or supernatural weapon beneath the Stellar Arsenal's exclusive authority.

#### Genetic Engineering
Genetic experimentation is one of the Legion's principal established research fields. The Empire possesses extensive expertise in supernatural biology, artificial organisms and the combination of genetic characteristics originating from different races.[1][3]
Velnorah is particularly accomplished in this field, having developed extraordinary knowledge of advanced biological engineering and technomancy. Her research extends beyond the production of conventional clones to the creation of organisms possessing carefully selected traits and abilities.
The Empire's access to the biology of Dragons, Bloodhunters, supernatural hybrids and numerous other species provides researchers with an enormous range of potential genetic reference points. Such knowledge can be employed to develop more effective organisms, strengthen existing biological systems or produce specialized personnel adapted to particular tasks.[3]
However, the existence of genetic research within the Stellar Arsenal does not establish that every supernatural race created or modified by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] belongs to a Fourth Legion project. The Emperor possesses independent Divine and biological abilities, and the involvement of his researchers must be distinguished from projects carried out solely through his own Authority.

#### Temporal Technology
The manipulation of Time is another established area of research within the Stellar Arsenal's scientific mandate.[1]
The [[Elderblood Empire]] possesses access to extraordinarily advanced temporal powers through individuals such as [[Natalia Elderblood née Alioth]], whose Authority over Space and Time makes her an important figure in the development of technologies and supernatural mechanisms involving temporal manipulation.
Temporal research can involve principles far beyond conventional engineering because changes in the passage of Time can affect the operation of entire civilizations and astronomical regions. The Empire's accelerated-development ritual demonstrates the immense potential of combining supernatural temporal control with large-scale scientific and magical infrastructure.[4]
Nevertheless, the original ritual was a major Imperial undertaking involving numerous powerful participants. It should not be treated as a project developed exclusively by the Stellar Arsenal, whose formal establishment occurred during the period of accelerated development.
The Legion's confirmed mandate includes temporal manipulation, but the exact list of completed temporal devices under its control remains unidentified.

#### Alien Knowledge and Technology
The acquisition and study of alien knowledge is an explicit responsibility of the Stellar Arsenal. As the [[Elderblood Empire]] expands beyond the civilizations originally incorporated into its government, encounters with technologically advanced species provide opportunities to obtain discoveries unavailable within its existing scientific traditions.[1]
Such knowledge can include unfamiliar engineering principles, advanced materials, technological systems and information concerning phenomena not previously investigated by Imperial researchers.
The organization's role is to bring this knowledge into the Empire's wider scientific establishment, allowing it to be studied and compared with existing developments.
The precise procedures governing the acquisition, storage and distribution of alien research have not been described. The mandate confirms the Legion's responsibility for studying such knowledge, but does not establish a particular method of acquisition for every alien civilization encountered.

#### Technomancy
Technomancy represents an important component of the Empire's scientific development. It combines technological knowledge with supernatural abilities, allowing researchers to manipulate advanced systems through powers that ordinary technological civilizations may lack.[3]
Velnorah's knowledge of technomancy originates partly from her previous civilization, whose technological development provided numerous techniques and biological characteristics suitable for further experimentation.
The integration of technomancy with genetic engineering permits the creation of specialized organisms designed to understand and control complex technological systems. Such beings can perform research, operate advanced equipment and contribute to projects requiring both scientific knowledge and supernatural compatibility.[3]

#### Scientists and Engineers
Scientists and engineers form the principal technical workforce of the Stellar Arsenal. Their responsibilities include conducting experiments, investigating unfamiliar phenomena and developing the practical technologies required by the Empire.[1]
The Empire's extraordinary technological capabilities allow research to operate across disciplines that ordinary civilizations might treat as incompatible. Engineers can work alongside individuals possessing supernatural abilities, while scientists may investigate biological, magical or conceptual phenomena through technological methods.
The exact professional ranks, qualifications and internal divisions separating scientists from engineers have not been identified.

#### Witches
Witches are among the Stellar Arsenal's commonly employed personnel. Their inclusion reflects the Empire's use of [[Magic]] as a component of its scientific and technological research.[1]
Magical expertise can provide methods of manipulating Energy, materials and supernatural phenomena that are not available through conventional engineering alone. The Empire's scientific establishment therefore incorporates Witchcraft as one of the disciplines contributing to its technological development.
The individual magical specializations possessed by the Legion's witches have not been fully documented, and no complete roster of named Witch researchers has been established.

#### Automatons
Automatons are artificial personnel employed by the Stellar Arsenal, particularly when conducting experiments too dangerous for ordinary researchers.[1]
Their use reflects the hazards associated with forbidden technology, advanced superweapons and experimental systems whose effects may be difficult to predict or contain. Artificial personnel can undertake dangerous operations without necessarily exposing valuable living researchers to the same degree of risk.
The precise nature of the Legion's automatons, including their autonomy, intelligence, physical durability and individual combat capabilities, remains unspecified.
The use of automatons should not automatically be equated with complete immunity to supernatural corruption or hostile manipulation. The Empire's leadership recognizes that sufficiently unusual supernatural powers may compromise artificial soldiers or other controlled organisms, making additional safeguards necessary.[7]

#### Cloned Scientific Personnel
The Empire employed cloned researchers even before the formal establishment of the Stellar Arsenal. Its specialized departments contained hundreds of personnel, many of whom were artificial organisms specifically produced to perform scientific work.[2]
These personnel provided researchers with a supporting workforce capable of handling tasks that would otherwise require the continuous personal attention of the Imperial Family.
As the Empire expanded, cloning and genetic engineering continued to contribute to scientific development. The use of artificial personnel became particularly relevant to projects requiring large amounts of experimentation, information processing or work within hazardous environments.[3]

#### Ruby Elderblood Scarlett
Main article: [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]]
Ruby Elderblood Scarlett is the principal commander and one of the founders of the Stellar Arsenal. She possesses an exceptional scientific intellect and extensive experience with supernatural research, particularly biological experimentation and the integration of scientific knowledge with the powers available to the Elderblood family.
Ruby's scientific influence predates the formal organization of the Fourth Legion. She was associated with the Empire's Biological Department, where she served as its nominal head, although her authority and expertise extended throughout the broader scientific establishment rather than remaining confined to biological research.[2]
Her command of the Stellar Arsenal places her at the head of the Empire's principal research Legion, whose responsibilities encompass advanced weapons, forbidden technologies, genetic experimentation and the acquisition of alien knowledge.[1]
Ruby continues participating directly in important research undertaken by the Imperial Family. Alongside Aline, Velnorah and Velina, she investigates the limits of scientific and supernatural manipulation, including experimental attempts to influence the fundamental rules of Reality.[9]
Her position as commander does not make her the sole inventor of every technology developed by the Empire. The scientific establishment depends upon numerous researchers, engineers and specialized personnel, while individual members of the Imperial Family may undertake separate projects outside the Legion's ordinary administrative structure.

#### Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II
Main article: [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]]
Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II is a founder and substitute commander of the Stellar Arsenal. She is among the Empire's foremost technological authorities and possesses extraordinary knowledge of advanced engineering, technomancy and genetic manipulation.[1]
Velnorah's scientific background originates from an advanced extraterrestrial civilization whose technology greatly exceeded that available to most of the Empire's earlier members. Her expertise was subsequently combined with the resources and supernatural abilities of the Elderblood family, allowing her to participate in developments involving advanced biological systems and technologies of immense destructive potential.
Her influence extends beyond the design of individual weapons. Velnorah investigates methods of producing specialized organisms, improving genetic compatibility and creating scientific workforces capable of handling dangerous research without requiring constant personal supervision.[3]
She also participates in the development of projects involving extremely dangerous conceptual powers, including Polaris and the Imperial Family's attempts to produce an absolute paradox.[7][9]

#### Aline Elderblood Valefar
Main article: [[Aline Elderblood Valefar]]
Aline Elderblood Valefar is a founder and substitute commander of the Stellar Arsenal. She is a highly accomplished Demon scientist whose expertise in technological research and development contributed substantially to the growth of the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s scientific establishment.[2][1]
Before the establishment of the Fourth Legion, Aline was associated with Imperial weapons research and possessed subordinates working within the technological departments on [[Yggdrasil]]. Her authority, like that of Ruby and Velnorah, extended beyond the limits of a single nominal research assignment.[2]
Aline's previous experience in Hell also gave her expertise in the manipulation of Demonic technology and supernatural systems. Her scientific capabilities allowed her to contribute to projects requiring knowledge beyond the methods available to conventional mortal researchers.
She remained one of the principal scientists involved in the development and improvement of Polaris and participated in research concerning the creation of contradictions within the fundamental rules of Reality.[7][9]
Aline Elderblood Valefar should be distinguished from Aline Elderblood Fulger, the daughter of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Carmila. The scientist and Fourth Legion substitute commander is the former Demon researcher Aline Valefar, not the younger Imperial Princess.

#### Natalia Elderblood née Alioth
Main article: [[Natalia Elderblood née Alioth]]
Natalia Elderblood née Alioth is one of the founders of the Stellar Arsenal. She participated alongside Ruby, Velnorah and Aline in the consolidation of the Empire's accumulated knowledge and the creation of the Fourth Legion.[1]
Natalia possesses extraordinary mastery over Space and Time, making her one of the most important supernatural specialists associated with the Empire's large-scale temporal and spatial projects. Her powers provide knowledge and capabilities particularly relevant to technologies involving the manipulation of Space-Time.
Her involvement in the Legion's foundation establishes her importance to the development of the Empire's scientific establishment. Nevertheless, her participation should not be confused with the permanent operational command positions assigned to Ruby, Velnorah and Aline.
Natalia is a confirmed founder, but she is not identified among the Stellar Arsenal's designated substitute commanders.[1]

### Advanced Research and Development
The Stellar Arsenal's scientific establishment benefits from the exceptional abilities of its founders and the continued development of the Imperial Family's technological knowledge. Some of the Empire's most ambitious projects involve technologies and supernatural principles that cannot be reproduced through conventional engineering alone.
The following undertakings are associated with the principal researchers of the Stellar Arsenal. Their involvement establishes an important relationship with the Legion's scientific leadership, but does not automatically prove that every project was formally assigned to the Fourth Legion rather than conducted as a separate classified Imperial undertaking.

#### Project Polaris
Project Polaris is one of the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s most dangerous classified technological undertakings. Begun during the 100,000th millennium of the [[Imperial Calendar]], it produced a gigantic warship functioning as a Divine Artifact possessing galaxy-destroyer-class capabilities.[7]
Polaris incorporates several exceptionally advanced technological and supernatural systems. Its capabilities include the manipulation of Reality, the distortion of Space and Time, the employment of destructive conceptual powers and compatibility with Eldritch transformations. Its most dangerous weapon allows the vessel to channel the Concept of END, a power capable of producing effects far beyond conventional physical destruction.[7]
The ship's END-related armament employs [[Fenrir]], [[Typhon]] and Jormungand as conductors, allowing the conceptual power to be directed into a devastating attack. The principal engineering difficulty involved the inability of an ordinary weapon barrel to withstand the destructive influence of END after firing.[7]
Rather than attempting indefinitely to construct an indestructible cannon, the researchers adopted a replaceable-weapon design. The vessel was equipped with hundreds of disposable cannons, each capable of delivering a single attack before being destroyed by the conceptual power passing through it.[7]

After the original vessel's construction, Victor instructed Ruby, Velnorah and Aline to continue investigating methods of increasing Polaris's destructive capabilities. Polaris represents a particularly important example of the Empire's combination of advanced technology with fundamental conceptual powers. However, its status as an Imperial classified project must be distinguished from a confirmed statement that it constitutes the standard equipment of the Stellar Arsenal. Its development involved the Legion's principal scientific leaders, while its operational assignment and secrecy were controlled directly by the Emperor.

#### Specialized Technomantic Workforce
Velnorah pursued the development of a specialized scientific workforce through genetic engineering and the combination of desirable characteristics originating from different races.[3]
The research involved cloned organisms possessing exceptional adaptability and the technological characteristics associated with Velnorah's former civilization. Rather than developing another race intended primarily for direct combat, she sought to create researchers naturally suited to technomancy and sophisticated technological work.
The proposed organisms would possess advanced scientific knowledge from the beginning of their existence, reducing the need for lengthy conventional education. Velnorah also intended them to possess enough personal combat power to survive dangerous environments, considering planetary destruction a reasonable standard of strength for advanced Imperial scientific personnel.[3]
Her objective was not to reproduce the Empire's strongest combatants. The new personnel were intended to function as capable researchers and assistants, possessing sufficient supernatural power to protect themselves while concentrating upon scientific tasks.
The project also addressed the limitations of relying entirely upon a small number of extraordinary researchers. Although Ruby, Velnorah and Aline were capable of accomplishing enormous amounts of work independently, the continued expansion of Imperial research produced vast quantities of information and experimental tasks that could be delegated to specialized personnel.[3]

#### Research Delegation and Automation
The development of specialized personnel reflected a broader principle within the Empire's scientific establishment: even exceptionally powerful researchers benefit from delegation.
Ruby, Velnorah and Aline possess extraordinary processing capabilities and scientific knowledge, but the continued expansion of the Empire generates enormous quantities of experimental information. Requiring the same individuals to personally examine every minor result would reduce the attention available for projects demanding their highest levels of expertise.[3]
By delegating routine operations to subordinates, clones and specialized researchers, the Empire's principal scientists can concentrate upon the most important questions while continuing to receive information from subordinate research activities.
Velnorah also recognized the value of alternative perspectives. Individuals possessing different experiences and limitations may identify useful solutions that members of the Imperial Family overlook because of their extraordinary capabilities and unfamiliarity with the problems experienced by ordinary citizens.[3]
This approach allows the Empire's scientific establishment to operate through a combination of exceptional central researchers and a much larger supporting workforce.

#### Absolute Paradox Research
Another advanced scientific undertaking involved attempts to produce an absolute paradox capable of threatening the fundamental stability of the native Universe.
Ruby, Velnorah, Aline and Velina investigated methods of generating a contradiction that Reality could not automatically resolve. Their objective was connected to the problem posed by the [[Primordial Entities]], whose relationship with the Universe allowed them to maintain influence over its fundamental structure.[9]
The research presented extraordinary difficulties because the native Universe behaves as a system capable of correcting inconsistencies within its own rules. Attempts to produce contradictory effects through native powers generally resulted in Reality resolving the contradiction rather than allowing a permanent paradox to form.
The researchers considered whether foreign supernatural powers might provide a means of circumventing this limitation. [[Eldritch Energy]] originates outside the native Universe and can distort local Reality according to foreign principles, making it comparatively easier to produce abnormal effects than to force the native system to violate its own laws.[9]
Even so, using foreign Energy was not equivalent to creating a genuine contradiction that completely bypassed the Universe's fundamental rules. The research team therefore continued investigating methods of producing an effect capable of undermining the protection enjoyed by the Primordial authorities.
The experiments remained highly dangerous. A successful paradox could provoke a response from the Universe itself, making uncontrolled testing potentially catastrophic.
No completed or successfully deployed absolute-paradox weapon has been established through the documented research. The undertaking remained experimental, with its participants continuing to investigate the theoretical obstacles preventing its completion.[9]

#### Research into Divine Fragments
The Stellar Arsenal's principal researchers also possess access to materials originating from exceptionally powerful supernatural beings.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] entrusted Ruby with a fragment of Divinity for examination, offering the material as the foundation of a new scientific undertaking. Ruby intended to bring the fragment to the other researchers so that its properties and potential applications could be investigated.[10]
The material represented another example of the interaction between the Emperor's supernatural activities and the Empire's scientific establishment. Rather than treating every Divine object exclusively as a religious or metaphysical phenomenon, Imperial researchers can examine such materials as subjects of systematic experimentation.
The precise outcome of the fragment's investigation remains unknown. Its acquisition establishes the beginning of a research opportunity, not the completion of a new weapon or technological discovery.

### Classified Research and Security
The Stellar Arsenal's scientific responsibilities include technologies whose destructive potential makes uncontrolled disclosure dangerous. The Empire consequently maintains strict restrictions surrounding its most important projects, with access determined by Imperial authorization rather than ordinary membership in the ruling family.[7][8]
The Empire possesses an extensive information network capable of preserving enormous quantities of knowledge, but that does not mean every individual can examine every classified project.
Even the Imperial Princesses, who are generally permitted access to considerable amounts of restricted information, do not possess unrestricted knowledge of the Empire's most important strategic secrets. Certain projects are deliberately excluded from the databases ordinarily accessible to them, and information is limited to individuals whose involvement is necessary.[8]

#### Project Compartmentalization
The secrecy surrounding Polaris illustrates the Empire's approach to protecting its most dangerous technological developments.
The vessel represents an exceptional strategic asset whose effectiveness depends partly upon enemies remaining unaware of its existence and operational capabilities. Consequently, detailed knowledge of the project is restricted even among members of the Imperial Family.[8]
The principle is straightforward: the more individuals who know the characteristics of a strategic weapon, the greater the possibility that an enemy might obtain information concerning it.
This concern is especially important in a setting where supernatural beings possess extraordinary methods of perception, information acquisition and mental manipulation.

#### Hazardous Experimental Personnel
The Stellar Arsenal's use of automatons and artificially produced personnel is particularly important for dangerous research.
Certain experiments may involve Energy, biological materials or supernatural technologies capable of causing catastrophic damage if mishandled. Artificial personnel can be employed to undertake operations that would otherwise expose important researchers to extreme danger.[1]
However, artificial organisms and machines are not automatically immune to hostile supernatural influence. The Empire's leadership recognizes the possibility that unusual powers might corrupt or manipulate such personnel, making protective safeguards and the continued development of more secure systems important.[7]

#### Independent Imperial Research
Not every classified experiment conducted by a member of the Imperial Family is necessarily a project formally assigned to the Stellar Arsenal.
[[Pepper Scarlett Elderblood]], for example, maintains private scientific facilities and undertakes independent research involving artificial stars, advanced Runes and technologies inspired by fiction. Her laboratory contains sophisticated equipment and extensive experimental records, demonstrating that major scientific discoveries can originate outside the publicly recognized research departments.[11]
Pepper's experiments include the development of an artificial star intended to produce an explosion equivalent to approximately one billion suns. The undertaking requires advanced containment and spatial isolation to prevent experimental failure from threatening surrounding inhabited regions.[11]
Her later involvement in Polaris connects her creativity with an official Imperial strategic project. Nevertheless, her private research laboratory and independently developed inventions should not automatically be classified as direct Fourth Legion assets without a confirmed administrative relationship.

#### The Spartans
Main article: [[The Spartans]]
The First Legion is the Empire's principal offensive military organization. Its forces include war dragons, armored soldiers, siege mages and advanced military spaceships capable of devastating planets or entire solar systems.[1]
The Stellar Arsenal's responsibilities concern the development and control of advanced military technologies, while the Spartans specialize in conducting offensive operations using the resources available to the Empire.
This distinction separates the design and advancement of military technology from the direct command of armies in battle.
However, the existence of a shared Imperial technological system does not establish that every Spartan weapon was individually invented by the Fourth Legion.

#### Order of the Iron Veil
Main article: [[Order of the Iron Veil]]
The Order of the Iron Veil is the Empire's principal defensive Legion. It specializes in planetary shields, fortified zones, bastions, defensive rings and the protection of established Imperial territory.[6]
Its military establishment possesses access to extraordinarily powerful armaments, but its principal responsibilities concern defensive operations rather than scientific research.
The Stellar Arsenal's technological mandate is consequently distinct from the [[Iron]] Veil's role in operating military infrastructure.
The specific research teams responsible for developing each Iron Veil defensive system have not been identified, and no complete administrative relationship between the two organizations has been established.

#### Akasha Elderblood and the Imperial Information Network
Main article: [[Akasha Elderblood]]
Akasha Elderblood forms a central component of the Empire's broader information infrastructure. Her network supports communication, administration and the processing of information across Imperial territory.
Such capabilities are particularly relevant to an advanced civilization possessing numerous scientific departments and enormous quantities of accumulated research.
Nevertheless, Akasha's information network is not identical to the Stellar Arsenal. The Fourth Legion possesses a scientific and technological mandate, while Akasha's broader responsibilities concern the Empire's information and administrative systems.
The exact mechanisms through which the Stellar Arsenal exchanges classified research with Akasha's network have not been fully established.

### Strategic Importance
The Stellar Arsenal represents one of the Empire's principal means of reducing its dependence upon the personal intervention of its most powerful supernatural beings.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his family possess abilities capable of accomplishing tasks far beyond the capabilities of ordinary civilizations. Nevertheless, the administration and defense of a vast interstellar empire require systems that can operate through established technology and organized personnel rather than demanding the Emperor's direct participation in every undertaking.
The Stellar Arsenal contributes to this requirement by developing technologies that can be researched, constructed, maintained and employed through the Empire's broader institutional structure.[1]
Its importance also extends to the acquisition of knowledge. The Empire's continued expansion brings it into contact with civilizations possessing unfamiliar technologies and supernatural systems. By collecting and studying their discoveries, the Stellar Arsenal can expand the Empire's existing knowledge rather than remaining dependent upon the techniques already developed by its own inhabitants.
The organization is especially important during preparations for conflict against the advanced civilizations of the Upper Sectors. Conventional military strength alone may be insufficient against enemies possessing extraordinary supernatural Authorities, sophisticated technology and an understanding of Reality exceeding that of the older Pantheons.
The Stellar Arsenal's mandate therefore includes the development of increasingly powerful weapons and the investigation of scientific principles capable of improving Imperial survival.
Its research into genetic engineering, conceptual technologies and fundamental contradictions within Reality further demonstrates the extraordinary scope of its ambitions. The organization's work is not limited to improving existing weapons; it also concerns the discovery of entirely new methods of manipulating the physical and supernatural world.
