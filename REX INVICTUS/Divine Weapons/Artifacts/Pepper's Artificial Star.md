Pepper's Artificial Star, also called the Artificial Sun, is an experimental stellar construct created by Pepper Elderblood. It is one of Pepper's most dangerous and ambitious private projects, designed around the idea of creating a supernova in the palm of her hand.[1]
The project was discovered by Priscilla Elderblood, Rosemary Elderblood and [[Sylvia Elderblood]] while they were searching through Pepper's private research spaces. Inside a heavily protected inner dimension, the three found a huge white sphere of energy floating among advanced scientific equipment. Using [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s eyes, Rosemary perceived thousands of runes within the small dimension, while the project records explained that Pepper had already stabilized the star with hundreds of runes and moved the experiment into its next phase.[1]
According to Pepper's own workflow notes, the project had already reached Phase 4. The star had been stabilized and was "ready", but still needed to be fed so that it could eventually achieve the power of a billion exploding suns.[1]

### Description
Pepper's Artificial Star appears as a huge white sphere of energy hovering within a specialized research dimension.[1]
The object is surrounded by scientific equipment and supported by an extremely complex network of Runes. To outside observers, it resembles a miniature star or artificial sun suspended within a laboratory environment.
Unlike a naturally occurring celestial body, this star is an artificially constructed energy object maintained through a combination of Pepper's intelligence, advanced technology, magical engineering and runic control.[1]

### Origin
The project originated as one of Pepper's personal "fun projects", though the scale of the experiment makes the label somewhat deceptive.[1]
Pepper's recorded project notes identify the experiment with the phrase:
"Fun project: Creating a supernova in the palm of my hand."
[1]
This makes it clear that Pepper's objective was not merely to create a harmless light source, but to produce a controllable artificial star with catastrophic destructive potential.
The experiment was carried out in complete secrecy inside one of Pepper's private research dimensions, hidden within her already massively expanded personal room.[1]

### Development
Only the later phases of the project are currently known.

#### Phase 3
By Phase 3, Pepper had succeeded in stabilizing the star through the use of hundreds of runes.[1]
Her notes also reveal that she received assistance from:
a Sun Goddess she had recently promoted
After obtaining that help, Pepper erased the goddess's memory.[1]
Pepper additionally noted that her sister [[Amaterasu]] would have been much better suited for this kind of work, but that the assistance she did obtain was enough to get the project started properly.[1]

#### Phase 4
By the time Priscilla and her sisters discovered the experiment, Pepper had already entered Phase 4.[1]
According to her records:
- the star was already ready;
- it no longer needed initial stabilization;
- it now needed to be fed;
- and Pepper intended for it to eventually reach the power of a billion exploding suns.[1]
This stage suggests that the core structure of the star had already been completed and that the remaining task involved increasing its stored power or output.

### Structure and Containment
The Artificial Star is not kept in ordinary physical space.
It is housed within its own specially protected dimension, nested inside additional layers of spatial protection.[1]
Rosemary explained the system as a form of multi-layered protection:
- if the star explodes, the inner dimension is meant to absorb the released energy;
- if any energy leaks beyond that layer, an outer dimension exists to ensure that the planet is spared.[1]
This makes the containment system one of the most important parts of the project.

### Runic Framework
Runes are central to the Artificial Star's creation and stabilization.
Pepper's own notes state that she stabilized the star with hundreds of runes.[1]
At the same time, Rosemary perceived thousands of runes in the dimension when examining it through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s magical eyes.[1]
This is not necessarily contradictory. The hundreds of runes mentioned in Pepper's notes may refer specifically to the runes required to stabilize the star itself, while the thousands observed by Rosemary may include:
- additional stabilization systems;
- laboratory support mechanisms;
- containment arrays;
- dimensional reinforcement;
- and safety barriers.
The exact function of each individual Rune has not been revealed.

### Functions

#### Artificial Stellar Creation
The fundamental purpose of the project is the creation of a stable artificial star.[1]
This alone makes the experiment extraordinary, as Pepper was not merely imitating solar light or heat but constructing a compact stellar energy mass within a controlled environment.

#### Massive Energy Generation
The Artificial Star serves as a source of enormous power.
Its energy output is already great enough to be used in other projects, most notably Pepper's experimental plasma swords.[1]
This confirms that the star is not a decorative experiment, but an active energy source capable of powering advanced weaponry.

#### Supernova Potential
Pepper's stated objective is to turn the project into something with the power of:
a billion exploding suns
[1]
This clearly indicates an intended supernova-scale or greater destructive application.
However, this should be understood as Pepper's goal for the project rather than a fully demonstrated current output.

#### Weapon Power Source
Later in the same chapter, Rosemary identifies Pepper's "lightsabers" as plasma swords whose documentation states that they harness the energy of the star the sisters had just seen.[1]
This establishes the Artificial Star as a foundational energy source for Pepper's other experimental technology.

### Relationship with Plasma Swords
One of the most important confirmed applications of the Artificial Star is its connection to Pepper's plasma swords.
When Priscilla discovered a collection of glowing swords in Pepper's "Room for Fun Projects", Rosemary checked their documentation and explained that they were:
plasma swords that harness the energy of the star we just saw
[1]
This confirms that the Artificial Star is already functional enough to support at least some secondary technological applications.
It also demonstrates that the star's purpose is not purely theoretical or apocalyptic; it has practical use as an advanced power source.

### Strategic Importance
Pepper's Artificial Star is strategically significant for several reasons.

#### Energy Infrastructure
As a stable high-output energy source, the star could support advanced weapons, experiments or other magical-technological systems.[1]

#### Weaponization Potential
If Pepper successfully completes her intended feeding process, the Artificial Star could become one of the most destructive constructs in the setting, at least in terms of raw Energy release.[1]

#### Scientific Value
The project demonstrates a level of engineering, runic mastery and magical science far beyond ordinary artifact creation. It is one of the clearest examples of Pepper's genius and the Elderblood family's ability to combine supernatural power with research and technological development.

#### Containment Implications
The elaborate containment measures imply that even an incomplete or unstable version of the star poses catastrophic danger. This alone elevates it from a simple experiment into a project with potentially world-threatening consequences.[1]

### History

#### Discovery by Victor's Daughters
During Chapter 1162, Priscilla secretly entered Pepper's private room while searching for information related to another project. She was later joined by Rosemary and Sylvia.
While investigating Pepper's expanded private spaces, the three discovered a small hidden dimension containing a huge white sphere of energy and large amounts of specialized equipment.[1]
Rosemary, using [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s magical sight, perceived vast numbers of runes around the object. Sylvia then located Pepper's workflow records and read the project notes, revealing the true nature of the experiment.[1]
This was the first direct narrative reveal of the Artificial Star.

#### Connection to Later Experiments
After leaving the star chamber, the sisters entered another hidden project room containing Pepper's collection of exotic weapons and inventions.
There, Rosemary identified plasma swords whose documentation explicitly linked them to the Artificial Star as their power source.[1]
This established the project as an active research foundation rather than a standalone curiosity.

### Limitations

#### Still in Development
The project is not complete.
Pepper's own notes state that the star still needs to be fed.[1]
Its final intended output therefore remains unrealized.

#### Extreme Danger
The need for multi-layered dimensional shielding proves that the project is highly dangerous.[1]
Even a containment failure could have catastrophic consequences if the energy were not successfully absorbed.

#### Output Not Fully Demonstrated
Although Pepper intends to reach the power of a billion exploding suns, the story has not yet shown the Artificial Star actually releasing energy on that level.[1]
This statement should therefore be treated as Pepper's project goal rather than a demonstrated feat.

#### Unknown Feeding Method
The exact meaning of "feeding" the star has not yet been explained.
It is unknown whether Pepper intends to feed it:
- raw energy;
- fuel;
- sacrificial materials;
- magical resources;
- or another unknown power source.

#### Unknown Long-Term Stability
Although the star has been stabilized, the long-term reliability of the project is still unclear.
The existence of extensive containment measures suggests that Pepper does not fully trust an uncontrolled release.
