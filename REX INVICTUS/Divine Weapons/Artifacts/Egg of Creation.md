The Egg of Creation is a forbidden Demonic artifact formerly possessed by [[Diablo]] and one of the most important components of his plan to transcend the limitations of an ordinary Demon. Despite its name, the Egg is not merely a vessel for creating life. It serves simultaneously as a Soul repository, a source of power for Demonic resurrection and empowerment, and an incubator capable of fundamentally altering the Soul and Race of a being through an unholy ritual.[1]
Diablo's ultimate intention was to use the Egg of Creation to undergo a controlled rebirth as a Nephalem, combining fundamentally opposing Angelic and Demonic characteristics within a single existence. He successfully acquired the necessary components during the Demonic War, but circumstances prevented him from returning to the Egg and completing the transformation correctly.[2][3] Forced to perform the ritual hastily without the artifact, Diablo achieved an incomplete Nephalem state whose immense raw Energy was not matched by equally developed technique and instinct.[4][1]
Following Diablo's defeat and absorption by [[Victor Elderblood]], Victor attempted to recover the Egg while reclaiming Diablo's remaining bases. He discovered through Diablo's memories that [[Asmodeus]] had stolen the artifact during the chaos surrounding Diablo's fall. Asmodeus' spiritual nature subsequently made locating both him and the Egg extremely difficult.[1]

### Appearance
The exact physical appearance of the Egg of Creation has never been directly described in detail.
Although the artifact is consistently called an Egg, the known chapters do not establish its exact size, color, external material, ornamentation, or whether its appearance literally resembles an ordinary biological egg. Its identification as an Egg appears primarily connected to its role as an incubator used to reconstruct and rebirth a being into another Race.[1]
Any visual depiction beyond this should therefore be treated as an artistic interpretation rather than a confirmed canonical appearance.

### Nature
The Egg of Creation is described by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] as a Demonic Tool made from Blood, guts, and Souls, to such an extreme degree that Victor considered its construction evil even by Demon standards.[1]
Its functions revolve heavily around Souls and biological or metaphysical transformation. Unlike conventional artifacts that merely provide additional offensive power, the Egg directly interacts with the fundamental existence of beings placed within its processes.
The artifact was capable of:
- storing the Souls of beings killed by [[Diablo]];
- using stored Souls to revive Demons;
- using stored Souls to strengthen selected Demons;
- functioning as an incubator for an unholy evolutionary ritual;
- altering both the Soul and Race of the ritual subject;
- and enabling the complete transformation of a compatible subject into a Nephalem.[1]
These functions make the Egg simultaneously an evolutionary artifact, Soul-based artifact, resurrection mechanism, and Demonic biological/metaphysical incubator.

### Abilities and Effects

#### Soul Storage
One of the Egg's primary functions is the storage of Souls collected by [[Diablo]].
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] learned from Diablo's absorbed memories that Diablo used the Egg as an instrument for keeping the Souls of those he killed.[1]
The Egg therefore acts as a supernatural Soul repository rather than merely storing conventional Energy or physical materials.

#### Demonic Resurrection
Souls stored within the Egg could be consumed or utilized to revive Demons.[1]
This gave [[Diablo]] access to a renewable strategic resource so long as he continued accumulating sufficient Souls. Rather than allowing useful Demonic subordinates to remain permanently lost, the Egg could contribute to restoring them through its Soul-based functions.
The exact limitations of this resurrection process are not completely explained. The chapter does not establish whether every Demon can be restored regardless of Soul damage, nor whether the process reconstructs a destroyed Soul. It should therefore not be interpreted as unrestricted resurrection of anything without limitation.

#### Demonic Empowerment
The stored Souls could also be used to give additional strength to selected Demons.[1]
This made the artifact useful even outside [[Diablo]]'s personal evolutionary ritual. Souls accumulated through warfare and slaughter could effectively be converted into military power by strengthening important members of Diablo's forces.
The exact amount of power granted by the Egg varies or remains unstated.

#### Incubation
The Egg serves as a supernatural incubator for the Nephalem transformation ritual.[1]
This appears to be one of its most important functions. [[Diablo]] did not merely require the three ritual hearts; he intended to return to the Egg after obtaining them and remain within the process for enough time to allow his ascension to be completed properly.[2]
The Egg therefore provides more than raw Energy. It facilitates the controlled transformation of the subject's existence while the conflicting components are integrated.

#### Soul Transformation
The ritual performed through the Egg can fundamentally change the Soul of the subject.[1]
This distinguishes the process from ordinary biological transformation. The Egg does not simply modify the physical body or grant temporary Angelic and Demonic powers; the resulting evolution reaches the spiritual foundation of the being undergoing the procedure.

#### Racial Transformation
Alongside the Soul, the ritual is capable of changing the subject's Race.[1]
In [[Diablo]]'s intended case, the result was the transformation from Demon into a Nephalem possessing characteristics belonging to:
- Demons;
- Angels;
- and Fallen Angels.[3][4]
This racial reconstruction was profound enough for Diablo's resulting existence to possess both Demonic Miasma and Holy Angelic power simultaneously.[4]

#### Nephalem Creation
The Egg's greatest demonstrated purpose is the creation of a Nephalem through the controlled fusion of opposing supernatural components.
According to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], the complete ritual requires:
- the heart of the most righteous / pure Angel;
- the heart of the most evil Demon;
- and the heart of a high-ranking Angel who fell into sin and became a Fallen Angel.[1]
With these components and the Egg acting as an incubator, the ritual alters the Soul and Race of its subject and produces a Nephalem.[1]
The resulting existence was described as one capable of breaking the Balance because it simultaneously contained characteristics belonging to opposing sides of the established supernatural order.[1]

### Ritual Components

#### Demonic Heart
[[Diablo]] used his own heart as the Demonic component of the transformation.
During his emergency ritual, it was described as a heart nourished by the evils of the world and saturated in Miasma.[3]
This fulfilled the requirement for the heart representing the extreme Demonic side of the ritual.

#### Michael's Heart
The Angelic component was the heart of Michael, whom [[Diablo]] regarded as the fairest and holiest Angel available for the ritual.[2][3]
Diablo personally removed Michael's heart during the Demonic War after weakening and ambushing him amid the collapse of the Angelic forces.[2]

#### Fallen Angel Heart
The third component required the heart of an extremely high-ranking Fallen Angel.
[[Diablo]] originally intended to use [[Lucifer]] as the ideal component. However, Lucifer's transformation into a different form of existence made him unsuitable for Diablo's original plan.[3]
Diablo consequently used the heart of [[Gabriel]], who had recently been corrupted by [[Asmodeus]] and transformed into a Fallen Angel.[2][3]
Diablo himself acknowledged that Gabriel was not the perfect ingredient he originally intended to acquire, but considered his heart sufficient to proceed.[3]

### History

#### Diablo's Preparations
The Egg of Creation formed one of the final pieces of [[Diablo]]'s long-term plan.
His schemes during the Demonic War were not solely directed toward conquering Earth, destroying Heaven, or maintaining control over [[Biblical Hell]]. A major objective was obtaining the ingredients necessary to transcend his existing Race and become a being that combined both sides of the supernatural Balance.[2]
During the war, Diablo succeeded in acquiring the hearts he required. [[Gabriel]] was corrupted into a Fallen Angel through [[Asmodeus]]' actions, while Diablo subsequently extracted both Gabriel's and Michael's hearts.[2]
After obtaining them, Diablo considered his necessary ingredients complete. His intended next step was to return to the Egg of Creation and begin the transformation process.[2]

#### Intended Perfect Ascension
[[Diablo]] specifically understood that the Egg-based evolution could not be rushed.
After obtaining the required hearts, he planned to return to the Egg only after winning the war because the ascension required time for the process to be perfected.[2]
This is important because the Egg was not merely an optional container. Diablo's later failure establishes that the incubation period was an integral part of achieving the transformation correctly.
Had the ritual proceeded as intended, Diablo would have entered the Egg with the necessary components and undergone a controlled rebirth rather than forcing the contradictory powers into himself during active combat.

#### Emergency Nephalem Transformation
[[Diablo]] never received the opportunity to complete his intended ritual.
During his confrontation with [[Lilith Elderblood]], he became trapped inside a barrier maintained through opposing Positive and Negative Energies. Escape became increasingly impossible, and Lilith's attacks severely damaged his body while interfering with his regeneration.[3]
Realizing he was going to die, Diablo abandoned his original plan.
Although he explicitly wanted to perform the transformation within the Egg of Creation in his hideout, survival forced him to attempt the evolution immediately.[3]
Diablo produced the three required hearts:
- his own Demonic heart;
- Michael's Holy heart;
- [[Gabriel]]'s Fallen Angel heart;
and consumed them directly.[3]
The resulting collision of Holy and Demonic power transformed his body and Soul, producing an Angelic-Demonic hybrid existence recognizable as a Nephalem.[4]

#### Incomplete Evolution
[[Diablo]]'s emergency transformation succeeded only partially.
His resulting body displayed characteristics belonging to Demons, Angels and Fallen Angels simultaneously, including mixed wings, Demonic traits, Miasma and Holy Light.[4]
However, Diablo himself recognized that he had not completed his evolution with the Egg of Creation.[4]
The absence of proper incubation and the rushed nature of the transformation severely compromised the result. Although Diablo possessed immense Energy and had successfully reconstructed himself into a hybrid existence, the transition shattered or disrupted much of the fighting technique and instinct he had previously developed.[1]
[[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] later explained that raw Energy alone did not determine combat effectiveness. Diablo's hurried transformation left him possessing enormous power without a correspondingly stable foundation of technique and instinct, allowing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to overwhelm him despite Diablo's expectations that his new Race would make him superior.[1]

#### Diablo's Death
[[Diablo]]'s incomplete evolution failed to reverse the course of the war.
Although he believed becoming a Nephalem placed him above ordinary beings, [[Victor Elderblood]] demonstrated an overwhelming superiority in practical combat ability, speed, technique, Energy control and overall foundation.[4]
Diablo was ultimately defeated and absorbed by Victor.
Through this absorption, Victor inherited access to Diablo's memories and learned the full purpose and operating principles of the Egg of Creation.[1]

#### Theft by Asmodeus
After the war, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] returned his attention to [[Diablo]]'s remaining possessions and hidden bases.
He dispatched his Demonic forces to reclaim the former King's installations and resources, successfully recovering much of what Diablo had left behind.[1]
The Egg of Creation, however, was missing.
By searching Diablo's memories, Victor discovered that [[Asmodeus]] had stolen the artifact during the chaos surrounding Diablo's defeat.[1]
Because Asmodeus is a spiritual existence and exceptionally difficult to track conventionally, Victor was unable to immediately locate either him or the stolen Egg.[1]
As of its last confirmed mention, Asmodeus remains the last known possessor of the Egg of Creation. The novel has not demonstrated him activating the Egg or completing Diablo's Nephalem ritual with it.

### Relationship with Balance
The Egg of Creation is particularly dangerous because its primary evolutionary function allows artificially opposed supernatural natures to coexist within one reconstructed existence.
The completed ritual produces a Nephalem combining aspects of forces that normally occupy opposing sides of the supernatural order.[1]
[[Diablo]] believed this transformation would produce a being that broke the existing Balance and grant him a fundamental advantage over ordinary Demons and Angels.[4]
However, Diablo's incomplete transformation demonstrated that merely combining opposing powers does not automatically grant absolute superiority. His failure resulted partly from attempting the transition without the controlled Egg-based incubation process and partly from neglecting the martial and technical foundation necessary to properly wield his new power.[4][1]

### Limitations
- The complete Nephalem ritual requires exceptionally specific components rather than simply placing any being inside the Egg.[1]
- [[Diablo]] required the heart of an extreme Demonic being, the heart of an exceptionally pure Angel and the heart of a high-ranking Fallen Angel.[1]
- The ascension requires sufficient time to stabilize and perfect the transformation. Diablo specifically avoided beginning the ritual immediately after obtaining the ingredients because he knew the process could not be completed correctly under battlefield conditions.[2]
- Performing the Nephalem transformation without the Egg can produce an incomplete evolution.[3][4]
- An increase in raw Energy does not automatically provide the subject with the technique, instinct or experience required to use the resulting power effectively.[1]
- No chapter currently establishes that the Egg can resurrect absolutely any type of being or restore a completely erased Soul. Its confirmed resurrection function concerns Demons.[1]

### Trivia
- The Egg is considered an extraordinarily evil artifact even by Demonic standards because its construction incorporates Blood, organs and Souls.[1]
- Despite its importance to [[Diablo]]'s plan, he never successfully performed the complete Egg-assisted Nephalem transformation.
- Diablo already understood before beginning the ritual that proper ascension required time. His decision to transform immediately was made solely because Lilith was about to kill him.[2][3]
- [[Gabriel]] was not Diablo's ideal Fallen Angel component. He originally intended to use [[Lucifer]], but Lucifer's change in Race made him unsuitable for the role Diablo had planned.[3]
- Diablo's incomplete Nephalem state retained both Holy Light and Demonic Miasma simultaneously.[4]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] never recovered the Egg after killing Diablo. The last confirmed owner is [[Asmodeus]].[1]
- The story has not yet shown Asmodeus using the Egg, meaning any theory that he intends to become a Nephalem himself remains speculative.
- Although the current artifact is called an "Egg", the novel has not yet provided a detailed physical description of its exterior.
