Polaris is a highly classified Galaxy Destroyer-class Divine Artifact developed by the [[Elderblood Empire]] in the form of a gigantic strategic spacecraft. It is among the most powerful artificial weapons confirmed within the Empire and represents the integration of advanced technology, Divinity, supernatural Concepts, Reality Manipulation, Space Manipulation, Time Manipulation and systems compatible with [[Eldritch Energy]]. Its principal distinguishing characteristic is the ability to channel the Concept of <END> into an offensive discharge intended to erase its target from Reality, making Polaris considerably more dangerous than an ordinary warship whose destructive capabilities depend exclusively upon physical force or supernatural Energy.[1]
Polaris was developed during the enormous period of accelerated Imperial advancement preceding the Empire's anticipated confrontation with civilizations from the Upper Sectors. Its construction was associated primarily with [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]] and [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]], whose accumulated knowledge allowed the Empire to combine extraordinary supernatural principles with advanced engineering. Rather than relying upon a single type of technology, the vessel incorporates the results of the Empire's increasingly sophisticated understanding of Reality and the forces capable of altering it. Its systems can bend Space and Time through Destruction-related Concepts and are sufficiently adaptable to accommodate Eldritch powers, demonstrating a level of technological development beyond conventional Divine Artifacts and ordinary spacecraft.[1]
The vessel's ultimate weapon presented a problem even the Empire's greatest researchers could not fully overcome. Although Polaris could channel <END>, no cannon constructed for the project could survive a complete discharge of the Concept more than once. The problem was not merely excessive heat, recoil or ordinary structural damage; the firing mechanism disappeared after transmitting the attack. Instead of continuing to pursue an indestructible cannon, the researchers adopted a different solution and manufactured hundreds of replacement cannons, allowing Polaris to fire again after installing another mechanism. The resulting design accepts the destruction of an individual cannon as an expected consequence of deploying the weapon rather than treating it as a catastrophic failure of the entire spacecraft.[1]
The first confirmed completed Polaris was assigned to [[Hela Elderblood]], whose relationship with <END> made her particularly suitable for operating a vessel centered upon its destructive properties. [[Victor Elderblood]] also possessed resistance to <END> through his access to the opposing Concept of <BEGIN>, and initially expressed his intention to maintain another Polaris as a personal reserve. The decision established that Polaris was not intended to remain a unique artifact that could never be reproduced, but the foundation of an exceptionally restricted class of Imperial superweapons.[1]
Construction continued after the first vessel. During preparations for the conflict approaching from the Upper Sectors, Victor confirmed that a second Polaris was ready and ordered that it be handed over to Hela so she could become familiar with its operation. At least two vessels therefore exist. No complete inventory of the class, and no final assignment of every constructed hull, has been stated. Polaris remained part of the Empire's active military preparations rather than an abandoned experiment.[2]
The class later received a confirmed combat deployment against a biomass weapon of Darkness. Hela's vessel, a gigantic ship on the same scale as the flagships already present, transformed in battle. A huge cannon grew from its bow. She charged that cannon, through her authority over <END>, with the exact energy quantity calculated by Velnorah and fired a beam of pure existential erasure. A second, full-power discharge followed after a new cannon was ready. [[Fenrir]] and Jormungand charged that cannon with their own power, and the beam erased both the containing dimension and the enemy. A trump card of Darkness was erased. Leaders of the opposing great factions immediately recognized the attack as <END> joined to technology.[3]

### Description
Polaris is a gigantic spaceship whose structure is itself a Divine Artifact. Although it possesses the appearance and basic identity of a spacecraft, its capabilities are not limited to ordinary technological propulsion, conventional weapons or Energy shields. The vessel's supernatural construction allows Concepts to be integrated directly into its functional systems, permitting it to manipulate aspects of Reality ordinarily associated with Divine authorities rather than mechanical equipment.[1]
Its principal systems are associated with Destruction, which provides the conceptual foundation for bending Reality, Space and Time. The narration further establishes that the artifact is malleable enough to be transformed through Eldritch powers, creating the potential for interactions with supernatural forces that do not necessarily obey the ordinary laws governing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s native Universe. The exact materials, dimensions, propulsion mechanisms, internal layout and defensive systems of the spacecraft have not been individually described, so they should not be inferred from its classification or the abilities of its creators.
Polaris is classified as Galaxy [[Destroyer]]-class, placing it among the greatest artificial military weapons explicitly identified within the [[Elderblood Empire]]. This designation establishes the strategic level assigned to the artifact, but it does not provide a measured attack radius for every weapon installed upon it. The <END> cannon has now been fired in battle. Those discharges erased what the beam struck, opened a precise hole through a galaxy-threatening organism, and later removed a held target together with the dimension imprisoning it. They do not establish that one shot erases an entire Universe, a Sector, or every higher-order structure merely because the weapon employs <END>.[1][3]
The vessel's design also differs from that of conventional warships through its use of expendable conceptual weapon components. While ordinary military engineering generally attempts to ensure that its primary armaments remain intact after firing, Polaris employs a weapon whose own supernatural effect destroys the mechanism through which it is discharged. Its researchers therefore designed the system around rapid replacement and continued use rather than the impossible requirement of making every component permanently resistant to <END>. Combat use confirmed that a new cannon could be brought to readiness for a second discharge in the same engagement.[1][3]
In battle the hull is not a static gun platform. Hela's ship transformed, and the erasure cannon grew from the bow before firing. The exact external appearance beyond its identity as a gigantic spaceship has not otherwise been described. Its illustration represents the vessel's appearance on the wiki, but visual details not established by the novel should not be treated as independently confirmed specifications.[3]

### Development

#### Those Who Rule Reality
The origins of Polaris are connected to the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s rapid development under the Great Acceleration. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] intended to provide his civilization with approximately two hundred thousand years of internal progress so its military, technological and supernatural institutions could prepare for contact with the advanced civilizations occupying the higher regions of the Cosmos. This effort transformed the Empire from a powerful supernatural government into a civilization capable of developing technologies that manipulated planets, Galactic structures and the underlying principles governing Reality.
Polaris emerged from this environment as one of the Empire's most secret military projects. The beginning of the project is placed around the 100,000 mark of the [[Imperial Calendar]], although the wording uses the expression 100,000th Millennium, which cannot be interpreted literally without conflicting with the established two-hundred-thousand-year developmental period. The precise starting year is therefore less certain than the broader fact that construction occurred during the Empire's accelerated development.[1]
Ruby and Velnorah were already among the greatest scientific authorities within the Empire. Ruby possessed extraordinary knowledge of supernatural biology, [[Magic]], Bloodlines and the technological systems developed through Victor's faction, while Velnorah contributed experience originating from an advanced extraterrestrial civilization. Their collaboration allowed the Empire to investigate technologies and supernatural principles that could not easily be understood through a single scientific tradition.
The resulting project was extraordinary even by Imperial standards. Ordinary artificial weapons could channel large quantities of Energy or reproduce techniques associated with Gods, but Polaris attempted to integrate Destruction-related Concepts and the ability to channel <END> into a functional spacecraft. This required its researchers to confront a fundamental problem: how could an artificial mechanism transmit a power capable of ending the existence of the very materials forming the weapon?
The difficulty remained unresolved throughout the construction process. Although the researchers succeeded in creating the ship and establishing a mechanism capable of channeling <END>, attempts to produce a reusable cannon failed because every mechanism disappeared after one full discharge. The effect demonstrated that the Concept could not be treated as an ordinary destructive force against which increasingly durable materials would necessarily provide a satisfactory defense.
Instead of abandoning the weapon, the researchers changed their approach. They accepted that the cannon would be consumed whenever <END> was released and manufactured hundreds of replacements. This eliminated the need for the individual firing mechanism to survive indefinitely and allowed the completed vessel to retain the capability of deploying its ultimate weapon repeatedly, provided replacement mechanisms remained available.[1]
The solution was ultimately accepted by Victor. When he gathered the senior members of his family after investigating the foreign influence connected with the [[Black Eclipse Pantheon]], he questioned Ruby and Velnorah concerning the status of Polaris. The researchers confirmed that the vessel had been completed and explained the decision to replace the original ambition of an indestructible cannon with a stock of disposable firing mechanisms.
Victor approved the arrangement and assigned the completed Polaris to Hela, instructing her to become accustomed to the spacecraft. Her suitability arose from her resistance to <END>, a characteristic that distinguished her from most of the powerful Gods and Dragon Goddesses surrounding the Emperor. Victor likewise possessed resistance through <BEGIN>, the opposing Concept, giving him a means of interacting with the weapon's ultimate destructive principle without depending exclusively upon ordinary defenses.[1]
However, Victor did not regard completion of the first vessel as the conclusion of the project. He instructed Velnorah, Ruby and Aline to continue investigating ways of increasing Polaris' lethality, emphasizing that useful improvements should be incorporated rather than treating the existing construction as a permanent technological ceiling.
Victor also ordered [[Pepper Scarlett Elderblood]] to participate in subsequent development. Pepper possessed extraordinary creativity and a tendency to consider possibilities that more conventional researchers might disregard, but her ideas frequently created problems because she did not always distinguish between an interesting experiment and a potentially disastrous one. Ruby and Velnorah had already developed the habit of recording Pepper's unusual proposals while preventing her from implementing particularly dangerous suggestions without appropriate oversight.
Victor deliberately permitted Pepper greater freedom because he believed unconventional thinking might produce improvements that the original development team would overlook. Nevertheless, he reminded her that he intended to operate another Polaris himself, ensuring that she understood the consequences if one of her experiments endangered the person the entire family most wanted to protect.
[[Scathach]] subsequently volunteered to join the development team. Her decision originated largely from concern that Pepper's enthusiasm would become uncontrollable if the researchers allowed her unrestricted freedom. Although Scathach was not identified as one of Polaris' principal engineers, her exceptional experience, authority and familiarity with Pepper made her an appropriate participant in supervising the development of a weapon capable of causing catastrophic destruction.[1]
The expansion of the project therefore combined several different approaches. Ruby and Velnorah provided established scientific leadership, Aline contributed additional supernatural research, Pepper introduced unconventional proposals, and Scathach supplied an experienced authority capable of challenging reckless decisions. Victor remained responsible for the project's overall direction, determining which objectives the researchers should pursue and how the completed vessels would be assigned.
The decision also established that Polaris was intended to remain reproducible. Victor explicitly wanted another vessel held in reserve, indicating that the Empire possessed sufficient knowledge and resources to construct additional examples rather than relying forever upon the first completed artifact.

#### Imperial Secrecy
Polaris' importance became clearer during the events surrounding Pepper's secret research and the younger Imperial Princesses' curiosity about the projects being conducted throughout the Empire.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] ordinarily allowed his daughters access to exceptionally large quantities of classified Imperial information. Many of them were expected to become future administrators, scientists or military commanders, and the Emperor considered knowledge of the civilization they would eventually serve an important part of their education. The Imperial database consequently contained information that would have been restricted to the highest authorities in most ordinary governments.
Nevertheless, access to Polaris remained substantially narrower. The project was explicitly identified as an Imperial trump card whose details were hidden even from most members of Victor's immediate family. The daughters could not simply discover its construction by searching through the usual Imperial records, and even many of Victor's wives did not possess complete information about the spacecraft.
The authorized group was described as consisting principally of the people directly involved in the project and the two Empresses referred to at the time. Membership in the Imperial family does not automatically grant unrestricted access to every military secret.[4]
The restriction was deliberate rather than simply a consequence of administrative inconvenience. Victor considered the value of a strategic trump card partially dependent upon how little an enemy knew about its mechanisms, operational limits and weaknesses. Even if the Empire possessed overwhelming military strength, allowing sensitive information to circulate freely could enable hostile civilizations to devise countermeasures before Polaris was deployed.
This policy also explains why the existence of [[Stellar Arsenal]] and the involvement of its principal researchers do not establish that every scientist or official connected with the Legion possessed complete access to Polaris. The project was restricted according to operational necessity rather than ordinary institutional seniority.

#### The Second Polaris
Further development continued after the completion of the original spacecraft. When the Empire prepared for the impending confrontation with the Upper Sectors, Polaris had advanced beyond the existence of one confirmed completed vessel.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] questioned whether the second Polaris was ready. After receiving confirmation, he ordered the spacecraft handed over to Hela and instructed that she become familiar with its operation as quickly as possible.[2]
The exchange establishes that the Empire had successfully reproduced Polaris, confirms that construction of the second vessel had reached a usable state, and demonstrates that the project remained part of the Empire's immediate military preparations. The second spacecraft was not merely an unapproved proposal or an incomplete design existing only within a research laboratory. Victor received a direct confirmation of its readiness.
The assignment also creates a distinction between Victor's earlier intentions and the later confirmed arrangement. When the first vessel was completed, Victor expressed a desire to maintain another Polaris as a reserve for himself. When the second vessel was ready, he instead ordered it handed over to Hela. No passage explains whether this was a change in the intended distribution, a temporary operational arrangement, or another stage of Hela's preparation. The later instruction should be recorded without assuming that Victor permanently surrendered every claim to a personal reserve.
The exchange occurred during the same period that Victor dispatched Krieg, his Herald of War, toward the Upper Sectors. Although the Emperor was preparing a large-scale Imperial conflict, he intended to deploy his Legions and gather practical experience rather than immediately commit every strategic reserve. His decision to have Hela familiarize herself with the second Polaris fits this broader preparation. No passage establishes that either vessel accompanied Krieg through the barrier or fired upon the enemy during his initial operation.[2]

### Technology and Construction
Polaris combines several supernatural disciplines into a functional warship rather than relying upon a conventional technological framework supplemented by simple enchantments. Its identity as a Divine Artifact is especially significant because Concepts form part of the mechanisms through which the spacecraft operates. These are not merely decorative inscriptions or enhancements comparable to ordinary enchanted equipment; they are functional aspects of the vessel's ability to interact with Reality.
The original explanation establishes that Polaris can bend Reality, Space and Time through Destruction-related Concepts. The novel does not describe the individual components responsible for each function or provide a complete technical diagram, but the stated capabilities demonstrate that the vessel is designed around more than raw destructive output. Space-Time manipulation and Reality alteration provide a foundation for military applications that ordinary projectile-based weapons would struggle to reproduce.[1]
Its compatibility with Eldritch powers is another distinguishing feature. Such forces can operate according to principles different from those governing the native Universe, making them particularly difficult to incorporate into ordinary supernatural technology. Polaris was described as sufficiently malleable to be transformed through Eldritch powers, demonstrating that its design could accommodate modifications beyond conventional Divine systems. However, this does not establish that every possible Eldritch ability is already installed upon the completed vessels.
The most unusual component remains the <END> weapon. Rather than attempting to produce a conventional Energy attack imitating the effects of END, Polaris was designed to channel the Concept itself. The resulting weapon creates a major contradiction for engineering: if the discharge can erase what it affects from Reality, then the firing mechanism must transmit a power capable of destroying the mechanism itself.
The Empire addressed the contradiction pragmatically. Instead of requiring the cannon to resist its own effect indefinitely, the designers manufactured replacements and accepted that each individual mechanism would be consumed. The arrangement demonstrates how the Empire's technological development increasingly involves understanding the limitations imposed by supernatural principles and designing around them when direct resistance is impossible.
Battle subsequently showed the practical form of that architecture. The hull transformed, the cannon grew from the bow, the first discharge was loaded to a calculated energy quantity, and a new cannon was ready for a second, full-power shot in the same action. [[Fenrir]] and Jormungand functioned as conductors by charging that second cannon with their power. [[Typhon]] remains a confirmed conductor of the system, but was not named in that engagement.[1][3]

### Powers and Abilities
Polaris' confirmed abilities originate from its construction as a Divine Artifact, its Destruction-based Conceptual systems, its ability to channel <END>, and the discharges observed when Hela employed the vessel against Darkness. Some broader capabilities remain known through description rather than through a measured limit, and those limits should not be invented from the classification alone.

#### Divine Artifact Systems
- Divine Artifact: Polaris is explicitly classified as a Divine Artifact despite its technological appearance, allowing supernatural Concepts to form part of its functional construction.[1]
- Conceptual Manipulation: The vessel employs Destruction-related Concepts to influence Reality and other fundamental aspects of existence.[1]
- Reality Manipulation: Polaris can bend Reality through its conceptual systems. The statement confirms the general capability but does not establish unlimited authority over every possible law or structure.[1]
- Space Manipulation: The spacecraft can bend Space through its Destruction-related systems. Its precise spatial applications have not been individually demonstrated.[1]
- Time Manipulation: Polaris can bend Time through the same general conceptual framework. No specific temporal techniques, such as universal Time reversal or unrestricted temporal travel, have been confirmed.[1]
- Conceptual Destruction: The vessel employs destructive Concepts rather than relying exclusively upon physical or ordinary Energy-based attacks. Its strongest application is the channeling of <END>.[1]
- Eldritch Compatibility: Polaris' construction can accommodate transformation through Eldritch forces. This establishes compatibility, not automatic possession of every ability associated with Eldritch Beings.[1]
- Combat Transformation: The hull can reshape itself so that the erasure cannon grows from the bow.[3]

#### Offensive Capabilities
- Galaxy [[Destroyer]]-Class Weaponry: Polaris is explicitly assigned a Galaxy Destroyer classification. Battle has shown existential erasure of a target and of a region of space. It has not shown the complete destruction of a named galaxy as a measured result.[1][3]
- <END> Channeling: The spacecraft channels the Concept of END through a specialized weapon. Hela loads the cannon through her own authority over END.[1][3]
- Calculated Discharge: The first battle shot was loaded with the exact energy quantity specified by Velnorah. That beam erased everything it touched and tore a precise hole through the target. The organism could not resist the beam.[3]
- Full-Power Discharge: A second shot, after a new cannon was charged by [[Fenrir]] and Jormungand, erased a gigantic square dimension and the enemy held inside it.[3]
- Reality Erasure: Whatever the beam strikes is removed from existence rather than merely broken into debris. Observers felt an existential shudder and recognized the attack as the erasure beam of Death.[3]
- Regional Void: The area where pure END was used was left with nothing remaining, compared to a portion of the universe rubbed out by an eraser. Imperial forces were ordered to stay clear until the region recovered. The erased zone later began to refill, and the primordial beings registered the damage.[3]
- Conceptual Weaponization: Polaris converts an abstract destructive principle into a functional military attack through its Divine Artifact construction and dedicated cannon.[1]
- Conducted Conceptual Discharge: Fenrir, [[Typhon]] and Jormungand function as conductors supporting the channeling process. In the confirmed battle, Fenrir and Jormungand charged the second cannon. Their exact individual technical roles beyond that support have not been itemized.[1][3]
- Repeatable Ultimate-Weapon Deployment: Polaris fired twice in one engagement by bringing a new cannon to readiness after the first discharge.[1][3]

#### Engineering Characteristics
- Replaceable Cannon System: The researchers manufactured hundreds of cannons after determining that no known design could survive more than one complete <END> discharge.[1]
- Modular Weapon Architecture: The ultimate weapon can be replaced without treating the disappearance of the original cannon as the end of the spacecraft's operational usefulness. The battle used a new cannon for the second shot.[1][3]
- Expendable Conceptual Components: The cannon is deliberately consumed during normal use of the ultimate weapon, separating the destruction of the firing mechanism from the continued existence of the vessel.[1]
- Replicable Construction: At least two completed Polaris vessels exist, confirming that the artifact can be constructed more than once.[2]
- Continuing Development: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] ordered further work on the vessel's offensive capabilities after the original construction was completed.[1]
The presence of Reality, Space-Time and Conceptual systems does not automatically establish unrelated abilities such as unrestricted Soul Manipulation, complete Causality Control, unconditional [[Immortality]] Negation or universal-level destruction. Those capabilities require independent demonstrations. Soul recovery during the same battle was performed by Rose and [[Persephone]], not by the erasure beam. The beam's role was existential erasure of matter and of the imprisoned enemy.[3]

### <END> Weapon System
The <END> cannon is the defining component of Polaris and the principal reason the spacecraft possesses strategic significance beyond its Galaxy [[Destroyer]] classification. Although the Empire had developed extraordinary weapons during the Great Acceleration, channeling a Concept directly associated with ending existence created a problem fundamentally different from producing larger explosions or concentrating more supernatural Energy.
The weapon operates by using [[Fenrir]], [[Typhon]] and Jormungand as conductors through which Polaris can channel <END> into its final attack. They are not identified exclusively as ordinary ammunition. Their confirmed role is to participate in the process that allows the spacecraft to access and release the Concept. When the weapon was used at full power, Fenrir and Jormungand charged the new cannon directly.[1][3]
The attack erases what its discharge affects. This distinguishes it from ordinary physical destruction, where a target may be reduced to debris while its constituent matter remains part of the Universe. The observed scope was a precise hole through a single organism, followed by the complete erasure of that organism together with the dimension holding it, and a void in the surrounding region that afterward began to refill. The effect should not be extended to an unlimited number of Universes, Sectors or higher-order cosmological structures.[3]

#### Cannon Consumption
The major technical difficulty is that the cannon itself cannot endure a full release of <END>. Even after the researchers successfully completed Polaris, they could not produce a firing mechanism capable of surviving repeated use. Every cannon disappeared after transmitting one complete discharge, meaning that the ultimate weapon could not be operated indefinitely through a single barrel or permanently installed mechanism.
This limitation was not resolved by creating a sufficiently durable substance. The research team eventually abandoned the attempt to produce an indestructible cannon and instead manufactured hundreds of replacements. Each cannon is intended to be used once, after which another mechanism can be installed for the next discharge.[1]
The resulting system represents a deliberate engineering compromise. Polaris itself does not become a single-use artifact merely because its ultimate cannon is destroyed. The spacecraft remains useful because the vulnerable component has been designed to be replaced, while the general vessel and the means of channeling <END> continue supporting subsequent deployments. The battle demonstrated that replacement in practice: the second discharge waited on a new cannon, which [[Fenrir]] and Jormungand then loaded.[3]
The number of attacks available through this arrangement depends upon the stock of usable cannons and the conditions required to operate the system. Hundreds of replacements were built. No passage states that they can be recreated instantly, that the process consumes no resources, or that Polaris can fire the weapon continuously without interruption.

#### Operational Significance
The <END> system matters because the [[Elderblood Empire]] is preparing for conflicts involving enemies whose supernatural foundations may exceed the destructive power of ordinary Galactic military forces. A weapon capable of employing END provides a different kind of threat from conventional attacks, since its purpose concerns the termination of the target's existence rather than merely overwhelming physical defenses.
The ability to channel <END> does not automatically establish that Polaris can eliminate every possible conceptual or primordial opponent. The relative authority of the Concept, the target's nature and the limitations of the artifact remain relevant. What the opposing leaders themselves concluded is narrower and explicit: END can be answered only by BEGIN. [[Vaelthar]], the lost Throne of Origin, no longer possessed his BEGIN-Throne, and <Kaiser> had no subordinate able to provide that answer. Both leaders treated a faction able to join a bastard's END to technology as a reason for the other side to disappear, or at minimum as a reason to stop holding their main forces back.[3]
Hela's assignment follows from the same principle. Her resistance to END makes her particularly suitable for a vessel whose principal weapon incorporates a force that destroys even its own firing mechanism. She does not merely sit aboard the ship. She loads the cannon through her authority over END, and she ordered the full-power shot. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] possesses comparable protection through his access to BEGIN, but that does not establish that every other member of the Imperial family can safely operate the same system without appropriate safeguards.[1][3]

### Combat Deployment
Darkness answered the imperial invasion by releasing a predatory biomass organism into a conquered solar system. The creature had no fixed shape. Overlapping organic plates, distorted metal and dark tissue continually rearranged its body, its limbs changed form, and slits along its mass swallowed matter. It began absorbing the entire solar system, including the fleets and soldiers stationed there, and spawned smaller monsters as a swarm. Imperial command raised the threat from a level capable of endangering multiple solar systems to a level capable of endangering a galaxy, and ordered every imperial unit to leave without attacking it.[3]
Swallowing the soldiers was a mistake. [[Abhoth]]'s protection held their souls, but those souls could not return to the Emperor. The failure drew [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s attention at once. [[Akasha Elderblood]] confirmed that he already knew. Velnorah, Hela and Rose arrived in succession, each in a ship on the scale of John's, and Rose temporarily took command. Their presence was understood as the Emperor keeping his promise the moment imperial souls were captured.[3]
Hela's ship transformed. A huge cannon grew from the bow. Velnorah had already calculated the shot and marked the point of impact. Hela loaded the cannon, through her power over END, with exactly that quantity of energy and fired. A beam of pure existential erasure left the weapon. Everything the beam touched was removed from existence. The monster could not resist. Every observer felt an existential shudder and recognized the erasure beam of Death. The shot tore a precise hole through the creature and did not by itself finish the action.[3]
Rose went next. She appeared as a true dragon, tore the organism open, and had six Valkyries pull it apart while dragon plasma poured into its interior. The scream carried across multiple galaxies because the attack struck the soul as well as the flesh. Rose drew the trapped soldiers' souls out, and [[Persephone]] took them into her realm at once. Velnorah then formed a gigantic square dimension that fixed the monster in place and called for Hela at full power.[3]
[[Fenrir]] and Jormungand supported their sister. As soon as the new cannon was ready they loaded it with their power. The resulting beam erased the dimension and the enemy together. A trump card of Darkness was erased without difficulty. Velnorah departed with samples. Hela ordered the Spartans to remain clear of that part of the galaxy until it recovered, because nothing existed there any longer, as though part of the universe had been rubbed out. She then left with the ship, and Rose returned command to John.[3]
The political result was immediate. Darkness was told the weapon was lost and answered without drama. A flaming skull, the manifestation of <Kaiser>, appeared beside [[Vaelthar]] in full seriousness and said the faction had to disappear: someone who could use the END of a bastard and join that power to technology. Both leaders understood that END is answered only by BEGIN, that Vaelthar's BEGIN-Throne was already lost, and that Kaiser had no subordinate who could supply the counter. Kaiser proposed alliance and total commitment of Thrones and Enforcers. Vaelthar refused a blind great offensive and chose to keep studying the enemy. The skull left the choice to him and withdrew. Vaelthar watched the space END had erased. The zone was already beginning to fill again, and the primordial beings were already reporting the damage.[3]
The engagement therefore confirms Polaris as a used weapon, not only a completed design. It also shows the limits of what one action proved. The organism was not deleted by the first calibrated hole alone. Soul rescue, physical tearing, dimensional restraint and a second full-power shot were all part of the kill. The void was local, and it began to recover. Nearby enemy fleets were not swept away by the shot. They halted, contacted their own Kaiser, and withdrew.[3]

### Operational Security
Polaris is maintained under extraordinary security because its value as a strategic weapon depends partly upon preventing enemies from learning how it functions. The project is an exception to the broad access normally granted to members of the Imperial family.[4]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] allows his daughters to consult large quantities of restricted information because many are expected to serve as future leaders within the Empire. They possess extensive educational and security support, while Imperial Guards and the Emperor's personal bodyguards protect them when they travel outside the family residence. Under ordinary circumstances, this makes allowing them access to sensitive information less dangerous than it would be for conventional government personnel.
Polaris is different. The project is concealed from the usual Imperial databases available to the Princesses, and most members of the family do not possess complete knowledge of its construction. Its secrets are concentrated among the individuals directly participating in development and the highest specifically authorized authorities.
The reasoning is straightforward: an enemy that understands a weapon's capabilities, weaknesses and activation conditions can prepare countermeasures before confronting it. Even if Polaris possesses enough destructive power to threaten Galactic targets, revealing the limitations of its <END> cannon or the requirements for operating the vessel could reduce its value as an unexpected strategic deterrent.
The arrangement is an example of need-to-know compartmentalization. Possessing an Imperial title, extraordinary supernatural strength or a familial relationship with Victor does not automatically grant access to every classified aspect of Polaris.
This restriction also prevents the assumption that every researcher serving beneath Ruby or Velnorah understands the complete project. Their broader scientific institutions may contribute personnel or resources, but the novel does not establish unrestricted distribution of Polaris' design among all Imperial scientists.
The battle reduced part of that secrecy in practice. Darkness and Kaiser saw the result, named it as END bound to technology, and correctly identified BEGIN as the only counter. They did not display knowledge of the disposable cannons, the conductor arrangement, the hull's internal layout, or how many such ships exist.[3]

### Ownership and Operators

#### Hela Elderblood
[[Hela Elderblood]] is the primary confirmed assigned operator of Polaris. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] directly instructed her to become accustomed to the first completed spacecraft after Ruby and Velnorah confirmed that the original construction had been finished.[1]
Her assignment is especially significant because END is closely associated with her supernatural existence. During the original discussion, Hela and Victor were identified as the only individuals present possessing resistance to the Concept, making her particularly suited to operating a weapon whose ultimate attack employs it directly.
When the second completed Polaris was ready, Victor ordered that vessel handed over to Hela as well, emphasizing that she needed to understand its operation quickly in preparation for the approaching conflict.[2]
She has since used the weapon in battle. Her ship transformed, the bow cannon grew, she loaded the first shot through her power over END, and she fired the full-power shot after [[Fenrir]] and Jormungand charged the replacement cannon. She also set the exclusion zone around the erased region and withdrew with the ship once the action was finished.[3]
The later assignment confirms her continuing importance to the program but does not establish whether she was intended to personally command multiple ships simultaneously, whether the second vessel represented a replacement for an earlier assignment, or how the broader Polaris force would ultimately be organized. The battle account identifies one ship under her command. It does not identify which of the completed hulls that ship was.

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] possesses supreme authority over the Polaris project as Emperor of the civilization responsible for its construction.
He reviewed the original vessel's development, accepted the disposable-cannon solution, assigned Hela to operate the completed spacecraft and ordered continued improvements to its destructive capabilities. He also personally authorized the addition of Pepper and [[Scathach]] to the project, demonstrating that even the Empire's most dangerous research programs remain subject to his strategic decisions.[1]
Victor possesses resistance to <END> through his access to the opposing Concept of <BEGIN>. During the original discussion, he expressed his desire to keep another Polaris in reserve for himself, providing a reason for the researchers to ensure that future designs would also be suitable for his use.
Confirmation that a second Polaris was ready demonstrates that the project successfully produced additional vessels. His instruction to hand that spacecraft to Hela does not conclusively establish the final fate of his intended personal reserve.[2]
Victor's connection with <BEGIN> should not be confused with an independent ability possessed by Polaris. BEGIN belongs to Victor's supernatural authority and explains his resistance to the power used by the spacecraft. It has not been established as a standard offensive or defensive system installed upon every Polaris vessel. Enemy leaders later treated BEGIN as the only known answer to the weapon Hela fired, which is a statement about the Concept, not a system mounted on the ship.[3]

#### Supporting participants in the confirmed battle
These roles belong to the engagement, not to ownership of the class.
- [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]]: Calculated the first shot, marked the impact point, and afterward imprisoned the damaged organism in a square dimension so the full-power beam had a fixed target. She left with samples.[3]
- [[Fenrir]] and Jormungand: Charged the new cannon for the full-power discharge.[3]
- Rose: Took temporary command, tore the organism open with the Valkyries, and pulled the captured souls free before the final erasure. She did not fire Polaris.[3]
- [[Persephone]]: Received the freed souls into her realm. She did not fire Polaris.[3]

### Strategic Role
Polaris is intended to provide the [[Elderblood Empire]] with a destructive capability beyond that of conventional military spacecraft. Its classification, Conceptual systems and ability to channel <END> make it a potential strategic deterrent against enemies whose strength would render ordinary weapons ineffective.
The project emerged during a period when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] recognized that the Empire's extraordinary development had not eliminated every danger posed by the higher regions of the Cosmos. Foreign civilizations possessed millennia or millions of years of accumulated experience, and some were governed by beings whose supernatural power operated on a scale beyond the Empire's ordinary military hierarchy.
Rather than rely exclusively upon Victor's personal strength, the Empire developed powerful artificial weapons that could potentially give other senior commanders access to attacks capable of threatening exceptional opponents. Polaris exemplifies this approach because it allows a technological platform to channel a Concept associated with the end of existence while remaining under the command of an authorized member of the Imperial family.
The vessel also provides a form of military redundancy. Its <END> cannon cannot survive repeated firing, but the replacement system allows additional attacks without requiring the entire weapon to be reconstructed. The confirmation of a second Polaris further demonstrates that the Empire can reproduce the platform rather than depending upon one irreplaceable artifact.
The first known use was not a war-opening barrage against a great faction's main fleet. It was the deletion of a single galactic-level trump card after that weapon had swallowed imperial souls, under the direct command of Hela, with Velnorah setting the shot and Rose recovering the souls first. The result was still strategic. Darkness lost the weapon. Kaiser treated the joining of END to technology as intolerable. Both sides identified BEGIN as the only counter and found that neither of them still had it available. Kaiser moved to press for open war. [[Vaelthar]] refused to spend his remaining forces on an unexamined offensive.[3]

#### Relationship to the Universal Deterrent
Polaris must be distinguished from the later project concerning an independent universal-level deterrent.
Following the crisis involving [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]] and the Primordial order, Violet and Sasha concluded that the Empire needed a means of threatening higher-order enemies without requiring [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to personally initiate universal catastrophe. The resulting research involved Ruby, Aline, Velnorah and Velina, who investigated an experimental weapon based upon the creation of an [[Absolute Paradox]] capable of forcing mutually contradictory states to coexist within Reality.[5]
The proposed mechanism differs substantially from Polaris' <END> cannon. Polaris employs a destructive Concept through a completed Divine Artifact spacecraft, while the Absolute Paradox research attempts to manipulate the fundamental Rules of Reality in a way that could threaten Beings whose existence cannot be adequately addressed through ordinary destruction.
The researchers encountered serious difficulties because creating an internal contradiction within the Universe's own laws proved far more complicated than manipulating Reality through external supernatural powers. The Absolute Paradox project remained unresolved, with Ruby refusing to abandon it simply because the initial approaches had failed.[6]
The novel has not established that Polaris was redesigned into the Absolute Paradox weapon or that the two programs became identical. They should remain separately classified unless a later passage explicitly confirms their connection.
Likewise, Polaris' Galaxy [[Destroyer]] classification should not be automatically upgraded to Universal level merely because some of the same scientists are investigating a different weapon intended to threaten universal-scale entities. The confirmed erasure, however severe inside its area, left a zone that began to refill and was reported to the primordial beings as damage, not as the end of the Universe.[3]

### Limitations
Despite its extraordinary capabilities, Polaris possesses several confirmed restrictions and numerous technical characteristics that have not yet been revealed.
- Single-Use <END> Cannon: Every confirmed cannon capable of channeling a complete <END> discharge disappears after one use. The researchers have not demonstrated a mechanism that can survive repeated full discharges. The battle required a new cannon for the second shot.[1][3]
- Finite Replacement Supply: The original design compensates through hundreds of replacement cannons, but no passage establishes an unlimited supply or instantaneous reconstruction of destroyed mechanisms.[1]
- No Confirmed Permanent Firing Mechanism: The researchers abandoned attempts to create an indestructible <END> cannon after failing to solve the underlying durability problem.[1]
- Specialized Operator Resistance: Hela's resistance to <END> is specifically relevant to her assignment and to her ability to load the cannon. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] possesses resistance through <BEGIN>. No passage establishes that every other member of the family can safely operate the same weapon without additional protection.[1]
- Calibrated shots are not automatically total: The first discharge, loaded to Velnorah's specified quantity, erased what it touched and opened a precise hole. It did not by itself remove the entire organism. Full erasure of that target followed containment and a second shot.[3]
- Dependent on the rest of the action: Soul extraction, physical dismemberment and dimensional restraint were separate acts by Rose, the Valkyries, [[Persephone]] and Velnorah. Those feats are not systems of the ship.[3]
- The void is not shown to be permanent: The erased region contained nothing immediately afterward and had to be avoided until it recovered. The same zone was already beginning to refill, and the primordial beings reported the damage.[3]
- Unspecified maximum erasure scope: The ultimate attack erases what it affects. No measurement establishes a fixed radius, a galaxy-wide guarantee, or a universal guarantee.[1][3]
- Undemonstrated destruction of a whole galaxy: The Galaxy [[Destroyer]] designation is stated. The confirmed target was a galaxy-threatening organism and the space and dimension around it, not a fully depicted galaxy wiped from the map.
- BEGIN remains the stated counter: Opposing leaders treated BEGIN as the only answer. Absence of that counter among their own subordinates is not the same as proof that no other defense can ever exist.[3]
- Incomplete Technical Specifications: The precise materials, dimensions, propulsion mechanisms, internal structure, ordinary armaments and defensive systems have not been revealed.
- Unconfirmed Independent Conceptual Authority: The spacecraft can channel <END> and manipulate Reality through Concepts, but this does not establish independent unlimited authority over all possible supernatural principles.
- Unspecified Eldritch Modifications: Compatibility with Eldritch powers is confirmed, but the exact additional systems implemented through that compatibility have not been individually identified.[1]
- Restricted Knowledge: Complete information is confined primarily to the authorized development personnel and highest relevant Imperial authorities. The battle revealed the effect to the enemy without revealing the design.[4][3]
- Continuing Development: Victor ordered further improvements after the first spacecraft had already been completed. The existing design was not treated as the final stage of Imperial weapons engineering.[1]
- Unidentified hull in battle: One ship under Hela fired. Which of the completed vessels that was, and whether more than one was present, is not stated.[2][3]
The absence of a demonstrated maximum does not mean Polaris is incapable of reaching the destructive scope implied by its classification. It means that confirmed results stay separate from extrapolations about an attack that has not been shown.

### Current Status
At least two Polaris vessels have been completed. Hela remains the principal confirmed operator, and the weapon has been fired in battle.
The first Polaris was reported complete when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] accepted the disposable-cannon solution and instructed Hela to become accustomed to the vessel. Its research team remained active afterward, and Victor authorized continued development intended to make the design more dangerous.[1]
A second Polaris was later confirmed ready. Victor ordered it handed to Hela during preparations for the conflict with the Upper Sectors, establishing that the Empire had reproduced the original artifact and was preparing the class for operational use.[2]
Hela has since taken one such ship into battle, transformed it, grown the bow cannon, fired a calculated END beam, and fired a full-power beam from a new cannon charged by [[Fenrir]] and Jormungand. The second beam erased a Darkness trump card and the dimension holding it. The erased region began to refill. Development of the class was not described as finished, and no complete count of hulls has been given.[3]

### Trivia
- Polaris is first explicitly revealed when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] reviews the finished vessel with Ruby and Velnorah.[1]
- It is described as both a gigantic spaceship and a Divine Artifact rather than an ordinary technological warship.
- Its explicit classification is Galaxy [[Destroyer]]-class. An actual galaxy, named and wholly destroyed, has not been depicted.[1]
- Polaris originated during the Empire's accelerated development. The stated starting mark uses the expression 100,000th Millennium, which does not reconcile literally with the established two-hundred-thousand-year acceleration. The exact construction date should be treated cautiously.
- Its Conceptual systems can bend Reality, Space and Time through Destruction-related principles.
- The artifact is compatible with transformation through Eldritch powers, but its complete list of possible Eldritch modifications remains unrevealed.[1]
- Polaris' ultimate attack employs the Concept of <END>.
- [[Fenrir]], [[Typhon]] and Jormungand are conductors for channeling <END>. Fenrir and Jormungand charged the cannon in battle. Typhon was not named there.[1][3]
- The original researchers could not construct a cannon that survived more than one complete discharge.
- Instead of creating an indestructible firing mechanism, the Empire manufactured hundreds of replacement cannons.[1]
- Polaris itself is not confirmed to be destroyed when its <END> cannon fires. The component identified as disappearing is the cannon.
- [[Hela Elderblood]] was assigned the first completed Polaris because of her resistance to <END>, and she fires it through that same authority.[3]
- Victor Elderblood possesses resistance to <END> through access to its opposing Concept, <BEGIN>.[1]
- Victor originally expressed his intention to possess another Polaris as a personal reserve.
- [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]] and [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] were the principal researchers Victor questioned when reviewing the vessel's completion.
- [[Aline Elderblood Valefar]] was assigned to assist with continued development after the initial spacecraft had already been completed.
- [[Pepper Scarlett Elderblood]] joined the project because Victor valued her unconventional creativity and willingness to investigate unusual ideas.[1]
- [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] volunteered to participate after Pepper received greater freedom, largely because she wanted to supervise her daughter's potentially dangerous experimentation.
- Polaris is one of the Empire's most closely guarded trump cards. Many members of the Imperial family are excluded from its complete technical information.[4]
- Its secrecy is intended to prevent enemies from understanding the vessel's capabilities and weaknesses before confronting it. Darkness and Kaiser nevertheless recognized the live attack as END joined to technology, and they named BEGIN as the only counter.[3]
- The existence of the project is not normally disclosed through the Imperial databases accessible to the younger Princesses.
- A second Polaris was completed and handed to Hela before the Upper-Sector conflict.[2]
- The second confirmed vessel demonstrates that Polaris is a reproducible design rather than an intrinsically unique Divine Artifact.
- In battle the ship transforms and the cannon grows from the bow.[3]
- The first live shot was calibrated to Velnorah's figure and opened a precise hole. The second, at full power, erased the target and the dimension around it.[3]

- A trump card of Darkness was erased. The shot did not destroy the enemy fleets watching from nearby. Those fleets withdrew after contacting their Kaiser.[3]
- The later [[Absolute Paradox]] universal-deterrent research is a distinct project and should not be treated as an upgrade already installed upon Polaris.[6]
- Polaris has no confirmed V-Series threat rating in the verified passages. Its Galaxy Destroyer classification is a separate designation. The organism it erased had been raised to a galaxy-level threat code before the shot.
- The complete external dimensions and internal specifications of the spacecraft remain unknown.
- The exact maximum erasure area of the ultimate weapon has not been revealed. Galaxy Destroyer classification should not be equated with confirmed unrestricted universal destruction.
