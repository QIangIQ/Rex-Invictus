Shiro-Hime is a Strategic Continental Spaceship and complete Divine Artifact belonging personally to [[Violet Elderblood Snow]]. Designed principally by [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]], the vessel is one of the earliest demonstrations of the technological direction later adopted by Dragon's Nest, combining extremely advanced science, Runes, Divine craftsmanship and stellar-scale Energy engineering within a single spacecraft.[1][2]
Shiro-Hime is powered by an actual yellow star that Velnorah successfully isolated and stabilized for use as the vessel's core. The stellar core supplies the ship with infinite Energy, allowing Shiro-Hime to operate as an entirely self-sustaining spacecraft without depending upon conventional external Energy or refueling infrastructure.[1] The vessel is capable of traveling through the vastness of Space and possesses a primary cannon powerful enough to completely disintegrate an entire planet into cosmic dust.[1]
Beyond its raw capabilities, Shiro-Hime is significant because the entire spacecraft itself is a Divine Artifact. This revelation shocked the [[God-Kings]] present when Violet displayed a miniaturized version of the ship during the negotiations over the former [[Norse Pantheon]], as technological constructions of this scale possessing Divine Artifact status were outside what most of Earth's Pantheons considered normal supernatural craftsmanship.[2]

### Development
Shiro-Hime originated from Velnorah's rapidly developing technological projects after joining [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction. Velnorah's status as a Technomancer allows her to directly manipulate and construct technology at speeds impossible for conventional engineers. Victor noted that, provided sufficient materials were available, she was capable of constructing several vessels through direct technological manipulation rather than relying upon ordinary industrial assembly methods.[1]
Even Victor was surprised by the speed at which Velnorah produced Shiro-Hime's first prototype. The vessel nevertheless took considerably longer to complete than its basic structure would suggest because several of its most important systems required solutions beyond straightforward technological construction.[1]

#### Construction Time
The complete development process took approximately 45 days.[1]
The physical hull was one of the easiest parts of the project. Through the combined abilities of Velnorah and [[Hephaestus]], the ship's hull itself was completed in approximately three days.[1]
The majority of the development period was consumed by more complicated supernatural engineering problems, particularly:
- isolating and stabilizing an actual yellow star for use as the vessel's core;
- preventing the stellar core's gravitational effects from interfering with the spacecraft;
- integrating Runes throughout a technological structure of this complexity;
- and Velnorah personally completing much of the finishing work because Shiro-Hime was intended as a special vessel for the Empress rather than an ordinary mass-produced spacecraft.[1]
Velnorah's relative lack of expertise with Runes was one of the primary bottlenecks. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] believed that if she had requested direct assistance from experts such as [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] or [[Dun Scaith Elderblood]], Shiro-Hime could have been completed significantly faster.[1]
With sufficient assistance, Victor estimated that a vessel of this kind could potentially have been completed in approximately one week. He also expected automation of Velnorah's construction methods to reduce production time further.[1]

### Design
The novel gives relatively little information regarding Shiro-Hime's exact external appearance. It is described as an advanced science-fiction-style spaceship, with its extraordinary nature primarily coming from the systems contained within it rather than a detailed canonical description of its exterior shape.[1]
Its construction combines two normally separate approaches:
- extremely advanced technological engineering;
- and supernatural systems based upon Runes and Divine craftsmanship.[1]
Violet consequently described Shiro-Hime as a technological marvel combining science and Runes.[1]
Although the vessel is normally a large spacecraft, Violet was able to present Shiro-Hime at Hela's negotiation table in a miniaturized form. The gathered Gods initially observed what appeared to be a miniature science-fiction spaceship before learning that the entire object was itself a Divine Artifact.[1][2]
The precise mechanism responsible for reducing and restoring the vessel's size has not been explained.

### Divine Artifact
Shiro-Hime is not merely a technological spaceship containing Divine equipment. The entire ship is classified as a Divine Artifact.[2]
This distinction was particularly shocking to the [[God-Kings]] attending Hela's negotiations. They were familiar with Divine weapons, enchanted structures and supernatural artifacts, but an entire advanced interstellar spacecraft simultaneously functioning as a Divine Artifact represented an unusual fusion of supernatural and technological development.[2]
The reaction to Shiro-Hime therefore reflected more than fear of its planetary weapon. It demonstrated how far Dragon's Nest had begun moving beyond the traditional separation between science, [[Magic]], Divine craftsmanship and supernatural technology.[1][2]

### Stellar Core
The central power source of Shiro-Hime is an actual yellow star.[1]
Transforming a star into a usable spacecraft core was one of Velnorah's greatest technical challenges. A normal star possesses tremendous gravity and Energy, meaning it could not simply be placed inside the ship without affecting the vessel and its surroundings. Velnorah therefore had to isolate and stabilize the star so that its gravitational influence would not interfere with Shiro-Hime's normal operation.[1]
Once successfully stabilized, the stellar core supplied Shiro-Hime with infinite Energy.[1]

#### Infinite Energy
Shiro-Hime's yellow-star core provides the vessel with an infinite Energy supply. This is explicitly presented as one of the ship's defining technological achievements and is the foundation of its status as a completely self-sustaining spacecraft.[1]
Because its principal Energy source does not run out, Shiro-Hime does not depend upon the conventional fuel limitations that would normally restrict a spacecraft during extremely long journeys or extended military operations.
The infinite-energy core also demonstrates the scale of Velnorah's engineering. Rather than simply constructing a powerful reactor, she successfully converted and stabilized an astronomical object into the permanent Energy foundation of a mobile Divine Artifact.[1]

### Runic Systems
Shiro-Hime extensively integrates Runes with Velnorah's technology.[1]
The Runic component was sufficiently important that it became one of the principal factors slowing construction. Although Velnorah possessed extraordinary command over technology, she was not comparably specialized in Runecraft and therefore experienced significantly greater difficulty integrating the required supernatural inscriptions than she did constructing the technological portions of the vessel.[1]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] specifically considered [[Scathach]] and [[Dún Scáith|Dun Scaith]] capable of substantially accelerating this part of the project if Velnorah had requested their assistance.[1]
The novel does not yet provide a complete list of every Rune incorporated into Shiro-Hime, so individual defensive, offensive or utility Runic systems should not be assigned unless separately demonstrated.

### Propulsion and Interstellar Travel
Shiro-Hime is explicitly capable of traveling through the vastness of Space.[1]
Its exact propulsion mechanism and maximum velocity have not been individually explained. What is established is that the ship was designed for genuine interstellar travel rather than functioning merely as an atmospheric or orbital warship.
Its infinite stellar Energy supply and self-sustaining design allow Shiro-Hime to operate across astronomical distances without the conventional Energy and refueling limitations of ordinary spacecraft.[1]

### Armament

#### Planetary Disintegration Cannon
Shiro-Hime's primary known weapon is an unnamed cannon capable of completely destroying a planet.[1]
According to Violet, activating the weapon requires little more than pressing the appropriate button, after which the cannon possesses enough destructive output to reduce an entire planet to cosmic dust.[1]

### Self-Sustaining Operation
Violet explicitly describes Shiro-Hime as entirely self-sustaining.[1]
Its infinite stellar Energy supply is the central component of this independence. The spacecraft does not require conventional external Energy or refueling infrastructure to continue functioning during interstellar operation.
This makes Shiro-Hime especially suitable for missions across vast regions of Space, where conventional logistics would become increasingly impractical.
The chapter does not individually describe every component included within its self-sustaining systems, so specific claims concerning food synthesis, biological resources, ammunition manufacture or crew requirements remain unknown unless later material establishes them.

### Classification
Shiro-Hime is classified as a Strategic Continental Ship.[1]
The novel does not provide a formal technical definition for the "Strategic Continental" designation when Shiro-Hime is introduced. It clearly does not represent the vessel's maximum destructive capacity, since its main weapon is explicitly capable of completely destroying a planet.[1]
The term therefore represents the ship's stated strategic classification rather than a limit upon its offensive output.
Later technological development within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s civilization produced numerous [[Continental Spaceships]] and progressively more powerful interstellar vessels, making Shiro-Hime one of the earliest major examples of the technological direction that would eventually support the [[Elderblood Empire]].

### Upgrades
The Shiro-Hime presented during the [[Norse Pantheon]] negotiations was not merely its original prototype. Violet specifically stated that her personal vessel possessed the latest updates available from their faction.[1]
This confirms that Shiro-Hime continued receiving technological and supernatural improvements after its initial completion.
Chapter 968 does not provide a complete comparison between the original vessel and the updated model, meaning the exact systems added during those upgrades remain unidentified.
The existence of continuing upgrades nevertheless establishes Shiro-Hime as an evolving technological platform rather than a static Divine Artifact whose capabilities were permanently fixed at the moment of construction.

### History

#### Creation
Following Velnorah's integration into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction, her advanced technological knowledge began being combined with the craftsmen, Divine materials and supernatural systems available within Dragon's Nest.
Shiro-Hime became one of the clearest early products of this cooperation. Velnorah acted as the project's principal engineer while [[Hephaestus]] assisted in creating the hull. The completed vessel combined advanced science, Runes, Divine Artifact construction and a stabilized stellar Energy core within a single interstellar spacecraft.[1]
Its complete construction required approximately 45 days, although the hull itself was finished within only three days.[1]
Victor was particularly impressed by the achievement because Velnorah had created the ship rapidly despite working with supernatural systems that had not existed within her original technological civilization.

#### Violet's Personal Ship
Shiro-Hime became the personal Strategic Continental Ship of [[Violet Elderblood Snow]].[1]
Velnorah personally handled much of the finishing work because the ship was intended for one of the Empire's principal Empresses rather than being an ordinary mass-production vessel.[1]
By the time Violet displayed Shiro-Hime publicly, the ship had already received the latest updates developed by their faction.[1]

#### Norse Pantheon Auction
Shiro-Hime made its most prominent known appearance during Hela's auction of the remnants of the [[Norse Pantheon]].[1]
Instead of conducting a conventional monetary auction, Hela requested treasures, Divine materials and other valuables that personally interested her. Violet participated as one of Dragon's Nest's representatives and began presenting increasingly extraordinary assets from their faction.[1]
Violet eventually produced Shiro-Hime upon the negotiation table in its miniaturized form.[1]
She introduced the vessel as:
“Strategic continental ship, Shiro-Hime.”[1]
Violet then explained that the ship:
- could travel through the vastness of Space;
- was entirely self-sustaining;
- possessed infinite Energy from a yellow star used as its core;
- and carried a cannon capable of turning an entire planet into cosmic dust at the push of a button.[1]
The gathered rulers were shocked by the vessel's specifications.
Their surprise increased further when they learned that the entire spaceship was itself a Divine Artifact.[2]
Shiro-Hime consequently became a demonstration not merely of destructive power but of the technological gap beginning to form between Dragon's Nest and the old supernatural civilizations of Earth.
Hela ultimately did not acquire Shiro-Hime, leaving the vessel in Violet's possession.[1][2]

### Strategic Significance
Shiro-Hime represents an important transition in the military development of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s civilization.
Powerful individuals capable of planetary destruction already existed throughout supernatural society, but Shiro-Hime demonstrated that comparable destructive capability could be transferred into an engineered technological platform.[1]
This distinction dramatically increases the strategic value of Velnorah's technology.
The ship was not a unique object requiring thousands of years of Divine craftsmanship. Velnorah produced the original vessel in approximately 45 days, Victor believed expert assistance could reduce construction to roughly one week, and further automation was expected to make production still more efficient.[1]
The implication was that sufficiently developed infrastructure could eventually reproduce vessels possessing:
- infinite stellar Energy;
- interstellar independence;
- Divine Artifact status;
- and planetary-scale weaponry.
Victor understood the significance immediately and gained a deeper appreciation for how Velnorah's original civilization and race had once risen to dominance within their own Sector.[1]
Shiro-Hime therefore represents one of the foundations of the technological doctrine eventually developed by the Elderblood civilization: rather than forcing science, [[Magic]], Divinity and Runes to remain separate disciplines, they could be combined into a single technological system.
