The Blade of Fafnir, also known as [[Fafnir]]'s Blade, is a Divine-Class sword and the greatest ancestral treasure of the [[Snow Clan]], one of the original Noble Vampire Count families of [[Nightingale]]. The weapon was forged from the remains of Fafnir, an ancient Dragon King known as the King of All Fire Dragons, whose overwhelming power made him a calamity capable of destroying entire countries. Constructed from Fafnir's scales and immersed in the Blood of his [[Dragon Heart]], the sword retains a portion of his original power and possesses the ability to channel and amplify the supernatural Fire of the Snow Clan's leader. Its extraordinary properties made it the foundation of a traditional Martial Art passed down through the Clan's ruling lineage.[1]
Although originally introduced as an ancient Draconic weapon, the Blade of Fafnir is not merely an artifact containing residual Dragon Energy. A living portion of Fafnir's Soul continues to exist within the sword, possessing sufficient awareness to react to its wielder, display preferences and acknowledge an individual as its Master. This unusual characteristic distinguishes it from conventional Divine weapons, whose supernatural effects generally originate from enchantments, accumulated Energy or the Divine authority of their creators. Fafnir's continued existence makes the sword simultaneously an instrument of combat and the vessel of an ancient Draconic consciousness.[2][3]
The weapon is principally associated with [[Agnes Elderblood Snow]], the former Countess of Clan Snow and one of [[Victor Elderblood]]'s wives. Although Agnes inherited the sword through her position as Clan leader, she initially relied so heavily upon her natural Fire that she neglected the full martial tradition associated with Fafnir. Following a personal confrontation with her daughter [[Violet Elderblood Snow]], Agnes retrieved the ancestral treasure and entered seclusion to recover her former combat proficiency. Her decision represented a major turning point in the history of Clan Snow, whose traditions warned that whenever its leader wielded the Blade of Fafnir again, a period of great chaos would follow.[1]
Agnes' relationship with Fafnir subsequently developed through her increasing strength and the influence of Victor's Blood. During the Demonic War, the Dragon consciousness within the weapon fully acknowledged Agnes as its Master, allowing her to employ the legendary Flames of Fafnir against Divine opponents. The sword's spiritual existence was later altered directly by Victor, who transformed the originally male Dragon remnant into a female state through his supernatural power. This intervention eventually produced an even greater transformation: Fafnir developed into a Fire Elemental weapon capable of merging with Agnes, surrounding her in a full suit of living flames, and being reshaped into different weapons while retaining its characteristic material properties.[4][5]
By its latest explicitly documented evolution, the Blade of Fafnir had progressed beyond the limitations of an ordinary physical sword. It could behave as an independently responsive Fire Elemental, enter Agnes' body, manifest armor, and provide the material foundation for other weapon forms. The development attracted Victor's interest because its properties partially resembled those of [[Junketsu]], his own living weapon, although important differences remained between their supernatural foundations.

### Description

#### Original Appearance
The Blade of Fafnir was originally described as a completely red Western-style sword. Its shape resembled the conventional swords commonly used in earlier periods of supernatural history, without requiring an unusually elaborate silhouette to distinguish it from ordinary weapons. Its true value originated from the supernatural materials used in its construction and the Draconic power preserved within them.
The sword was forged using the scales of [[Fafnir]], an ancient Dragon King whose original physical existence possessed extraordinary durability and an affinity for Fire. The completed weapon was also bathed in the Blood of the Dragon King's heart, incorporating another source of his supernatural characteristics into its construction. These materials allowed the sword to retain a portion of Fafnir's original power and provided a foundation capable of channeling the intense Fire of Clan Snow's leaders.[1]
The weapon's red appearance is therefore associated with its Draconic origin rather than simply representing the colors of its owning Clan. Its supernatural construction allows the sword to support flames that would exceed the tolerances of ordinary materials, making it especially compatible with a Bloodline whose principal ability involves generating and manipulating Fire.
Agnes commonly manifests the weapon through her own flames. During its introduction, the sword appeared in her hand as Fire gathered around her, revealing the completely red blade. Later appearances similarly demonstrate that she can summon it without carrying it continuously in an ordinary sheath, although the precise mechanism through which the sword is stored has not been independently explained.
When Agnes returned from her renewed training, the weapon was described as a red Western sword decorated with Dragon-scale details. She carried it close to her body, emphasizing its significance as the ancestral treasure of Clan Snow and the weapon through which she intended to recover her former martial strength.

#### Living Dragon Presence
The sword possesses a genuine Draconic spiritual presence originating from [[Fafnir]]. Although the Dragon King's original body had long since become the source of materials used in the artifact, his existence had not disappeared completely. His Soul continued inhabiting the weapon and retained enough consciousness to interact with those attempting to wield it.
This characteristic became particularly apparent when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] handled the sword during a conversation with Agnes. The Dragon consciousness was capable of reacting to an individual other than its customary wielder, but Victor's overwhelming supernatural nature allowed him to suppress its resistance without difficulty. The encounter demonstrated that the weapon was not merely a collection of materials holding a fixed amount of ancient Energy; something capable of perceiving and responding to external circumstances remained within it.[2]
Fafnir's consciousness was later observed directly through Divine perception. When [[Artemis]] examined the weapon during a confrontation with Agnes, she perceived the presence of an enormous Dragon watching through the blade. The discovery revealed that the sword remained spiritually alive and that the will contained within it could respond to a possible enemy rather than remaining completely passive.[3]
The consciousness initially possessed an independent and somewhat troublesome disposition. Nyx later observed that Fafnir had previously teased its host and resisted fully accepting Agnes. Following Agnes' evolution through Victor's Blood and the Clan Initiation Ritual, however, that resistance disappeared. The Dragon recognized Agnes as its legitimate Master, establishing a substantially more cooperative relationship between the wielder and the spiritual existence contained within the weapon.

#### Evolved Elemental Appearance
The sword's appearance and fundamental nature changed considerably following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later intervention.
By Chapter 1070, [[Fafnir]] had developed characteristics resembling those of an actual Fire Elemental. Rather than existing exclusively as a solid weapon containing Draconic power, the sword could manifest as a fiery existence capable of responding to Agnes' intentions and integrating itself into her body.
When Agnes summoned the evolved weapon before Victor and Velnorah, its unusual state immediately attracted their attention. Velnorah recognized that the sword had apparently become a Fire Elemental, while Victor considered the possibility that Fafnir had developed into an Elemental Dragon. The novel does not formally establish a new racial classification for Fafnir, but the transformation into an elementalized living weapon is explicitly demonstrated.[5]
The evolved sword glowed softly with Fire while floating near Agnes. When commanded, it entered her body and manifested flames across her form, producing a complete suit of Fire Armor. Agnes could also manipulate those flames into different weapons while reproducing the appearance and material characteristics associated with Fafnir.
The evolved appearance therefore no longer remains restricted to the original red Western sword. Its physical form can become part of Agnes' equipment and elemental techniques, reflecting a much deeper connection between the wielder and the living artifact.

### History

#### Origins and Creation
The Blade of Fafnir originated from the remains of the ancient Dragon King [[Fafnir]], a supernatural Being whose reputation was associated with overwhelming destruction.
Fafnir was remembered as the King of All Fire Dragons, a title establishing his position among the Dragons associated with the Element of Fire. His power was sufficient to destroy countries with relative ease, and his name inspired despair among those who understood the consequences of confronting him.
At an unspecified point in supernatural history, Fafnir's original body ceased to exist in its former state, and portions of his remains were used to create a Divine-Class sword. The weapon incorporated the scales of the Dragon King and was immersed in the Blood of his heart, allowing it to preserve a portion of his original supernatural power.[1]
The exact circumstances surrounding Fafnir's death or the loss of his original body have not been established. The novel does not identify the individual who defeated him, the craftsman who constructed the sword, or the event through which the [[Snow Clan]] initially acquired his remains. Consequently, the weapon's confirmed history begins with its construction from the Dragon King's remains rather than a documented account of Fafnir's final battle.
Although the weapon's physical materials originated from the Dragon King's body, the surviving Soul demonstrates that Fafnir's existence was not entirely extinguished. His consciousness persisted within the completed sword, eventually becoming part of the ancestral inheritance of Clan Snow.
The identity and original intentions of the sword's creator remain unknown. It is therefore not established whether the preservation of Fafnir's Soul was deliberately engineered or arose naturally from the exceptional characteristics of a Dragon King.

#### The Snow Clan's Ancestral Treasure
The Blade of Fafnir eventually became the greatest treasure of the [[Snow Clan]], one of the ancient Noble Vampire families established beneath the authority of [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]].
The Clan's hereditary Fire made the weapon particularly valuable to its ruling lineage. Rather than simply providing a powerful blade, [[Fafnir]] could channel the full Fire output of the Clan leader and strengthen it further. This allowed a sufficiently skilled wielder to combine the characteristics of the [[Snow Bloodline]] with power preserved from an ancient Dragon King.
A dedicated Martial Art was developed around the weapon and became part of the traditions practiced by successive leaders of Clan Snow. Its continued transmission demonstrates that the sword was intended for practical combat rather than existing solely as a symbol of political authority or a ceremonial inheritance.[1]
The importance of the Blade of Fafnir also reflects the relationship among [[Nightingale]]'s three ancient Countess families. Clan Scarlett possessed [[Gae Bolg]], the Runic spear created by [[Scathach]], while Clan Fulger possessed [[Taranis]], the ancestral Spirit Daggers associated with Carmila Fulger. Each treasure represented the distinctive supernatural tradition of its respective family, with Fafnir embodying the Snow Clan's connection to overwhelming Fire.
Unlike Gae Bolg, which Scathach personally constructed using her own knowledge of Runes, the Blade of Fafnir was an inherited weapon whose origin extended into the distant history of Dragons and Noble Vampires.
The Clan also preserved a superstition concerning the sword's use. According to the saying passed down through successive generations, whenever a Snow Clan leader wielded the Blade of Fafnir, great chaos would arise in the near future.
The belief was not established as a supernatural prophecy or a confirmed power possessed by the artifact. Instead, it reflected the historical association between the Clan's greatest weapon and periods when its leaders abandoned ordinary restraint to confront extraordinary dangers.
The superstition nevertheless remained important to the family's collective memory, particularly among those old enough to recognize the significance of a Clan Head returning to the weapon after a long period of neglect.

#### Agnes' Return to Training
One of the sword's most important appearances occurred when [[Agnes Elderblood Snow]] decided to abandon her previous complacency and recover her neglected martial abilities.
For much of her life, Agnes had possessed extraordinary natural potential but had depended primarily upon the overwhelming Fire inherited through Clan Snow. Because her elemental power was sufficient to defeat most opponents, she had rarely considered it necessary to refine her abilities beyond the family's basic [[Martial Arts]].
Her behavior changed following an emotionally significant confrontation with Violet. Agnes recognized that her daughter had begun developing into someone whose determination and strength of character demanded greater effort from the woman leading Clan Snow.
Rather than remain satisfied with her inherited status as a Countess, Agnes decided to strengthen herself through dedicated training. She instructed Hilda to mobilize the Clan's resources, cancel her existing appointments and prepare for an extended period of seclusion.
Agnes then raised her hand and summoned the Blade of Fafnir through a manifestation of Fire, revealing the completely red Western sword that represented the Clan's greatest martial inheritance.[1]
The decision immediately attracted Hilda's attention. Having served Agnes for an extraordinary period, Hilda understood that the Countess rarely displayed such determination unless her emotions had been affected profoundly.
She recognized an intensity resembling the temperament Agnes had possessed before her comparatively peaceful years with [[Adonis Snow]], although the renewed determination was accompanied by a different emotional foundation.
Agnes declared that she had been foolish to neglect the training necessary to remain strong and resolved to correct the mistake. Her decision marked the return of an active Clan Head wielding the ancestral weapon rather than relying exclusively upon natural Fire.
Hilda also remembered the superstition associated with the sword. Although she did not ordinarily believe such traditions predicted the future, Agnes' decision gave her an ominous feeling that the return of [[Fafnir]]'s Blade might accompany a period of extraordinary upheaval.
The subsequent training became part of Agnes' development as a more complete supernatural warrior, laying the foundation for the increased mastery she would later demonstrate against Gods and other powerful opponents.

#### Recognition as a Clan Treasure
The Blade of Fafnir later appeared during the preparations for the Meeting of Supernatural Beings, when the three great Vampire Countesses assembled alongside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and the other representatives of [[Nightingale]].
Agnes arrived carrying the sword close to her chest, treating it as a precious possession rather than an ordinary weapon. Its red blade and Draconic origin contrasted with the distinct supernatural characteristics of the weapons carried by the other Countesses.
[[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] appeared with [[Gae Bolg]], an extraordinarily sophisticated spear whose thousands of Runes reflected her mastery of supernatural weapon construction. [[Annasthashia Elderblood Fulger]] carried [[Taranis]], the golden ancestral daggers left to Clan Fulger by Carmila.
The gathering consequently presented the three principal treasures of Nightingale's ancient Countess families together, demonstrating how their different supernatural traditions had been preserved through their ancestral weapons.[6]
The Blade of Fafnir remained distinctive because its materials and spiritual presence originated from an ancient Dragon King rather than an exclusively Vampiric system of weapon creation.

#### Interaction with Victor
The sword's living nature became evident during an earlier conversation between Agnes and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
The two had been discussing the consequences of Victor's extraordinary Bloodline and the possibility of combining supernatural Elements in ways that ordinary Vampires could not reproduce. Agnes was gradually becoming more comfortable around Victor, whose presence affected her emotions in ways she had not experienced for a considerable period.
During their interaction, Victor handled [[Fafnir]]'s Blade despite not being its established Master. The Dragon consciousness within the weapon possessed enough independent will to react to his presence, but Victor's supernatural power allowed him to suppress its resistance without difficulty.[2]
The interaction demonstrated that ownership and physical possession were not the only factors governing the weapon's behavior. Fafnir remained capable of responding to the supernatural nature of an individual attempting to wield it, and the power of that individual could affect the relationship between the artifact and its wielder.
Victor's ability to suppress the Dragon consciousness foreshadowed his later capacity to modify its spiritual existence directly.
However, the encounter does not establish that Victor permanently became the sword's Master or that he took ownership from Agnes. His interaction demonstrated his superiority over the consciousness inhabiting the weapon, while Agnes remained its established wielder.

#### The Flames of Fafnir
The sword's combat significance became particularly apparent during the Demonic War, when Agnes, [[Scathach]] and Natashia confronted several Greek Goddesses while negotiating the future of the Amazons.
The meeting deteriorated after [[Athena]], [[Hera]] and [[Artemis]] repeatedly displayed hostility toward the Vampire Countesses. Although the three women initially restrained themselves out of consideration for [[Hestia]], the continued arrogance of the Olympian Goddesses eventually exhausted their patience.
Scathach activated the Runes of [[Gae Bolg]], while Natashia demonstrated the overwhelming speed inherited through Clan Fulger. Agnes subsequently advanced with the Blade of Fafnir in her hand, allowing its flames to surround the weapon as she confronted Artemis.
When Artemis aimed her Divine Bow toward Natashia, Agnes threatened to incinerate the Goddess with the Flames of [[Fafnir]] before she could successfully intervene.[3]
The threat immediately attracted Artemis' attention. Although she initially regarded the Countesses as mortals beneath the dignity of [[Olympus]], the mention of Fafnir forced her to consider the true nature of the sword.
Using her Divine Senses, Artemis examined the weapon and perceived an enormous Dragon watching through the blade. The Draconic presence appeared to be waiting for an opportunity to attack, revealing that the weapon remained spiritually alive and capable of responding to the hostility surrounding its wielder.
The discovery caused Artemis to recognize that Agnes was not merely carrying an ordinary Fire weapon. The sword contained the surviving presence of one of the ancient Dragon Kings, and the Flames being channeled through it represented a genuine supernatural threat.
The confrontation also demonstrated that Fafnir's influence was not limited to improving the physical sharpness or durability of the sword. Its Fire could be employed as an offensive power, allowing Agnes to threaten Divine opponents through the combination of her own abilities and the artifact's Draconic inheritance.
Nevertheless, Agnes did not actually strike Artemis with the weapon during the confrontation. The encounter establishes that the Goddess recognized the danger and that Agnes was prepared to employ the Flames of Fafnir, but it does not constitute a demonstrated permanent elimination of Artemis or another God.

#### Fafnir Accepts Agnes
Another important development occurred when Nyx examined the three Countesses through her Divine Senses.
Nyx recognized that the Progenitor Blood flowing through the women had become considerably stronger and concluded that they had completed the Clan Initiation Ritual. The resulting increase in power affected not only their personal supernatural characteristics but also their relationships with the equipment they carried.
When Nyx examined Agnes, she discovered that [[Fafnir]]'s behavior had changed substantially. The Dragon consciousness was no longer teasing or resisting its host and had fully acknowledged Agnes as its Master.[3]
This observation established a progression in the relationship between the Countess and the weapon. Although Agnes had inherited the sword through Clan Snow, Fafnir had not previously behaved as though it accepted every aspect of her authority without objection.
Her enhanced Bloodline and increased supernatural strength apparently satisfied the Dragon consciousness, allowing the sword to acknowledge her more completely.
The change was especially significant because the weapon contained a living spiritual remnant rather than a purely mechanical enchantment. Its cooperation depended at least partly upon a relationship between its consciousness and its wielder, making personal development relevant to the artifact's effectiveness.
The novel does not provide a detailed conversation between Agnes and Fafnir explaining the specific conditions of this acceptance. Nyx's observation nevertheless confirms that the Dragon's attitude changed and that Agnes acquired its full recognition.

#### Victor Alters Fafnir's Soul
The Blade of Fafnir underwent another extraordinary transformation following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s development into the Blood Dragon Primogenitor.
During a confrontation involving Agnes and [[Persephone]] in the [[Greek Underworld]], Agnes summoned the sword through her flames while preparing to confront the Goddess responsible for the suffering associated with Adonis.
Upon retrieving the weapon, Agnes noticed that its spiritual presence felt unusually docile. She turned toward Victor, recognizing that her husband's extraordinary perception and supernatural authority might explain the change.
Victor subsequently summoned the sword from Agnes' hand, causing it to appear before him. He then blew upon the weapon, covering its entire body in violet flames.
Addressing the consciousness within the sword as his daughter, Victor altered the spiritual nature of [[Fafnir]] before returning the blade to Agnes.[4]
Agnes immediately recognized the difference. The presence within the weapon now felt more comfortable and distinctly feminine, leading her to question whether Victor had changed the sword's gender.
Victor confirmed that he had altered the Dragon Soul because he disliked the thought of another male remaining in such close proximity to one of his wives, even if that male existed only as a spiritual remnant inhabiting a weapon.
The incident was treated humorously by the surrounding family, but its supernatural implications were considerable. Victor had directly modified the identity of an ancient Dragon consciousness without destroying the sword or eliminating the remaining portion of Fafnir's existence.
The transformation also demonstrates that Fafnir's original biological sex and the later condition of the surviving Soul are no longer identical. Historically, Fafnir had been a male Dragon King; following Victor's intervention, the spiritual existence inhabiting the sword possessed a female state.
The novel does not establish that this operation recreated Fafnir's complete original Dragon body or restored the entirety of the ancient Dragon King's lost power. The confirmed alteration concerns the spirit inhabiting the sword and its subsequent relationship with Agnes.

#### Evolution into a Fire Elemental
The most substantial documented evolution of the Blade of Fafnir occurred during the later Imperial Era, after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had developed considerably more advanced Draconic and supernatural abilities.
While Victor was spending time near [[Jupiter]] alongside [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]], Agnes approached him and revealed that the sword had begun changing in ways she did not fully understand.
She summoned [[Fafnir]] as a fiery sword, immediately attracting Victor's attention. Velnorah stopped her work and examined the weapon, recognizing that its supernatural composition had apparently developed into that of a Fire Elemental.[5]
Agnes explained that the change had not occurred immediately after Victor modified the sword. For several years, Fafnir had continued functioning in much the same manner as before, only beginning to display its new characteristics afterward.
Victor examined the weapon and considered whether the ancient Dragon remnant had become something resembling an Elemental Dragon. The sword responded to his attention through a soft glow of Fire, demonstrating that its consciousness remained active despite the transformation.
Agnes then revealed that the elemental evolution involved more than a change in appearance.
She commanded the floating sword to approach, and Fafnir immediately understood her intentions. Rather than simply returning to Agnes' hand, the weapon entered her body.
Flames subsequently spread across Agnes, enveloping her within a complete suit of Fire Armor.
The heat produced during the transformation briefly reached approximately 6,000 degrees, forcing Victor to isolate the surrounding area to prevent the enormous temperature from affecting Velnorah or the nearby environment.[5]
Agnes also revealed that she could manipulate the flames into other weapons whose appearance and material characteristics resembled those of Fafnir. This allowed the sword's substance to become the foundation for different weapon configurations rather than remaining restricted to its original Western-style form.
Finally, Agnes demonstrated that she could transform herself completely into a Fire Elemental, converting her body into a manifestation of flames.
The combined abilities demonstrated a major progression in the relationship between Agnes and the weapon. Fafnir was no longer merely an external sword whose supernatural power could be channeled through ordinary attacks. Its elemental existence could merge directly with Agnes, manifest as protective armor and participate in transformations involving her body and equipment.
Victor recognized the similarity between Fafnir's new characteristics and those of [[Junketsu]], his own living weapon. However, he also identified important differences. Junketsu possessed the ability to assume the properties of different materials, while Fafnir's demonstrated transformations remained associated with its own elemental and Draconic substance.
The incident attracted Victor's interest because it suggested that his intervention might have fundamentally altered the developmental potential of the original artifact. He considered whether similar transformations could be induced in other weapons, potentially allowing the Empire to create additional extraordinary artifacts through the same principles.
No subsequent chapter has established that this exact process was successfully reproduced across an entire class of weapons.
The Blade of Fafnir therefore remained a distinctive example of a Divine-Class artifact whose spiritual existence had evolved through Victor's interference and its continued synchronization with Agnes.

### Powers and Abilities
The Blade of Fafnir possesses abilities originating from three principal sources: the supernatural materials used in its creation, the surviving Soul of [[Fafnir]], and the later transformations produced through Agnes' development and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s intervention.
Its original characteristics center upon Fire amplification and Draconic power, while its evolved form introduces elemental transformations, direct fusion and the ability to manifest different weapons.

#### Original Divine Weapon Abilities
- Divine Weapon: The Blade of Fafnir is explicitly identified as a Divine-Class weapon, making it one of the greatest ancestral artifacts possessed by the Noble Vampire families of [[Nightingale]].[1]
- Draconic Weapon Physiology: Its construction incorporates the scales and [[Dragon Heart]] Blood of [[Fafnir]], preserving supernatural characteristics originating from an ancient Dragon King.[1]
- Draconic Power: The weapon retains a portion of Fafnir's original strength and supernatural Energy despite the loss of his complete physical body.[1]
- Fire Manipulation: The sword can channel supernatural flames associated with the Fire Dragon King and the elemental inheritance of Clan Snow.[1][3]
- Fire Amplification: Its principal original function is to channel the full Fire output of the [[Snow Clan]]'s leader and increase the power of those flames.[1]
- Energy Channeling: The weapon serves as a medium through which Agnes can concentrate and release her Fire, allowing her own supernatural power to be expressed through the sword.[1]
- Supernatural Flame Empowerment: Agnes can envelop the blade in flames and employ the resulting weapon as part of her offensive combat techniques.[3]
- [[Draconic Energy]] Retention: The sword preserves a portion of its original Dragon King's power within the materials from which it was constructed.[1]
- Weapon-Based [[Martial Arts]]: A traditional Martial Art developed from the sword and became part of the combat inheritance practiced by successive leaders of Clan Snow.[1]
- Supernatural Weapon Manifestation: Agnes can summon the blade through her flames rather than continuously carrying it in an ordinary sheath.[1][4]

#### Living Dragon Soul
- Sentient Weapon: The Blade of Fafnir contains a living Draconic consciousness capable of reacting independently to individuals interacting with it.[2][3]
- Soul Preservation: A portion of [[Fafnir]]'s Soul survived the loss of his original body and continues existing within the artifact.[3]
- Spiritual Awareness: The Dragon consciousness can perceive circumstances surrounding the weapon, as demonstrated when [[Artemis]] observed it watching her through the blade.[3]
- Independent [[Will Solace|Will]]: Fafnir initially displayed its own disposition toward Agnes, teasing or resisting its host before later accepting her as Master.[3]
- Wielder Recognition: The Dragon consciousness can acknowledge an individual as its legitimate Master, with Agnes receiving this recognition following her supernatural development.[3]
- Spiritual Presence: The surviving Dragon can be perceived through sufficiently advanced supernatural senses, including the Divine perception employed by Artemis.[3]
- Draconic Intimidation: The consciousness of the ancient Dragon King contributes to the danger perceived by opponents capable of recognizing its supernatural nature.[3]
- Soul Alteration Susceptibility: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] demonstrated that the Dragon consciousness could be directly altered through superior supernatural authority, changing its gender while preserving the weapon's continued existence.[4]

#### Fire and Offensive Techniques
- Flames of [[Fafnir]]: Agnes can employ flames associated directly with the Dragon King through the sword. Their power was sufficient for her to threaten [[Artemis]] during the confrontation with the Greek Goddesses.[3]
- Enhanced Fire Attacks: The sword improves the effectiveness of the wielder's Fire, allowing Agnes to combine the supernatural strength of Clan Snow with the power preserved from Fafnir.[1]
- Elemental Weapon Enhancement: Fire can surround the blade during combat, increasing its supernatural offensive properties beyond those of an ordinary sword.[3]
- Draconic Flame Channeling: The weapon acts as the medium through which Agnes can employ the flames associated with its ancient Dragon consciousness.[3]
- Supernatural Damage: The weapon's Divine-Class status and Draconic power allow it to threaten opponents far beyond ordinary physical combatants.[1][3]
The precise nature of the Flames of Fafnir has not been fully explained. It remains unclear how much of their power originates independently from Fafnir's surviving consciousness and how much represents Agnes' existing Fire being amplified through the sword.
The confrontation with Artemis confirms the technique's name and the threat it posed, but does not establish that the flames automatically erase the existence of every God they strike.

#### Elemental Evolution
The following abilities became explicitly demonstrated after the sword developed into a Fire Elemental-like existence.
- Elemental Weapon Physiology: [[Fafnir]]'s sword developed characteristics recognized by Velnorah as those of a Fire Elemental, fundamentally changing its previous condition as a solid Divine weapon containing a Dragon Soul.[5]
- Fire Elemental Transformation: The weapon can manifest through a fiery elemental state while retaining its consciousness and connection with Agnes.[5]
- Living Fire Manifestation: Fafnir's evolved existence is capable of appearing as supernatural Fire rather than remaining restricted to the original solid blade.[5]
- Elemental Integration: The sword can enter Agnes' body and combine its elemental state with her own supernatural existence.[5]
- Weapon Fusion: Agnes can merge with Fafnir instead of wielding the sword exclusively as a separate physical object.[5]
- Fire Armor: Fusion produces a complete suit of armor made from flames, enveloping Agnes and providing a new method of employing the weapon's power.[5]
- Heat Generation: The Fire Armor transformation briefly produced temperatures of approximately 6,000 degrees, requiring [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to isolate the surrounding area.[5]
- Elemental Weapon Transformation: Agnes can reshape the flames associated with Fafnir into different weapons whose appearance and material properties resemble those of the original artifact.[5]
- Weapon Material Replication: The resulting weapon forms reproduce the appearance and characteristics associated with Fafnir's materials rather than functioning merely as ordinary unstructured flames.[5]
- Multiple Weapon Forms: Fafnir's evolved state is not restricted to the shape of its original Western-style sword, allowing Agnes to produce other weapon configurations through its elemental substance.[5]
- Fire Elemental Body: Agnes demonstrated the ability to become completely composed of flames while employing her evolved Fire abilities. This is a capability demonstrated by Agnes rather than a confirmed independent humanoid transformation of the sword itself.[5]
- Enhanced Wielder Synchronization: The evolved weapon responds to Agnes' intentions and can merge directly with her body, demonstrating a deeper relationship than ordinary equipment usage.[5]
- Elemental Responsiveness: Fafnir can understand Agnes' commands and adjust its form accordingly, as demonstrated when she instructed the floating sword to approach her.[5]

#### Wielder-Dependent Abilities
Several techniques associated with [[Fafnir]] require the active participation of Agnes. These should not automatically be treated as abilities the sword can reproduce independently without a compatible wielder.
- Fire Elemental Transformation: Agnes demonstrated the ability to transform her own body completely into flames while revealing Fafnir's evolved capabilities.[5]
- [[Snow Clan]] [[Martial Arts]]: The specialized techniques developed around Fafnir require martial proficiency rather than depending exclusively upon the weapon's stored power.[1]
- Advanced Fire Control: Agnes supplies her own supernatural Fire and technical understanding, allowing the weapon's amplification to be used effectively.[1]
- Elemental Weapon Shaping: The creation of different weapons through Fafnir's flames is demonstrated through Agnes' control and her integration with the evolved artifact.[5]
- Fire Armor Manifestation: The armor is produced when Agnes merges with the sword. The novel has not demonstrated Fafnir independently placing identical armor upon any individual regardless of compatibility.[5]

### Martial Arts
The Blade of Fafnir is associated with a traditional Martial Art developed by Clan Snow and practiced by successive leaders of the family.
Its existence was confirmed during Agnes' decision to resume serious training. The narration explained that Clan Snow, like Clan Scarlett and Clan Fulger, possessed a principal ancestral weapon around which its martial inheritance had developed.[1]
The tradition allowed the Clan's leader to combine swordsmanship with the extraordinary Fire inherited through the [[Snow Bloodline]]. Because [[Fafnir]] can channel and amplify that Fire, the Martial Art provides a means of employing both the wielder's personal abilities and the power preserved within the Dragon King's remains.
However, Agnes had neglected much of this inheritance during her earlier life. Her extraordinary natural talent allowed her to defeat most opponents through Fire alone, reducing her incentive to develop the technical foundation possessed by more disciplined martial practitioners.
Her decision to retrieve the Blade of Fafnir and train in seclusion consequently represented more than simply obtaining a stronger weapon. It was an attempt to recover the martial knowledge she had neglected and improve her ability to use the sword properly.
The novel does not provide a complete list of named techniques belonging to the traditional Blade of Fafnir Martial Art. Although the Flames of Fafnir are explicitly named, the available passages do not establish that this is the formal title of an individual stance or numbered technique within the ancestral system.
No additional named techniques should be assigned to the tradition without direct confirmation from the novel.

### Fire Armor
Fire Armor is an evolved application of the relationship between Agnes and the Blade of Fafnir, first demonstrated in Chapter 1070.
The technique became possible after [[Fafnir]] developed into a Fire Elemental-like existence following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s earlier intervention. Agnes discovered that the sword could enter her body and merge with her rather than remaining restricted to its original external form.
When activated, flames spread across Agnes and create a complete suit of armor made from Fire. The transformation differs from ordinary flames surrounding a physical body because the weapon itself becomes incorporated into Agnes' existence, producing a combination of supernatural equipment and elemental transformation.[5]
The heat generated during the initial demonstration briefly reached approximately 6,000 degrees. Victor immediately isolated the surrounding area to prevent the temperature from affecting the environment or Velnorah, who was present during the demonstration.
The chapter does not specify a temperature scale for the stated figure. It should therefore be recorded as 6,000 degrees without automatically converting it into Celsius, Fahrenheit or another unit.
The armor also demonstrates a major change in the weapon's relationship with its wielder. Previously, Agnes channeled her Fire through a sword containing the power of an ancient Dragon King. After the elemental evolution, she could incorporate the sword directly into her body, allowing the weapon and wielder to function through a more closely integrated supernatural state.
The exact durability of the armor has not been measured independently, and no confirmed passage establishes complete immunity to every attack while Agnes is wearing it.
Its principal confirmed characteristics are the direct fusion with Fafnir, manifestation of complete Fire Armor and production of extraordinary heat.

### Weapon Transformation
The elemental evolution also granted Agnes the ability to reshape [[Fafnir]]'s flames into different weapons.
During the demonstration before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Velnorah, Agnes explained that she could mold the flames into weapons whose appearance and material characteristics corresponded to Fafnir's original substance.[5]
This differs from conventional Fire Manipulation, in which a practitioner might simply create a vaguely weapon-shaped concentration of flames. Fafnir's evolved forms reproduce characteristics associated with its material existence, allowing its elemental body to act as the foundation of an actual supernatural weapon configuration.
The ability is important because the Blade of Fafnir had historically been associated with one physical form: a completely red Western sword. After the transformation, Agnes could employ the same living artifact through other shapes without abandoning the Draconic source of its power.
Nevertheless, this does not establish unrestricted material transformation. Victor specifically distinguished Fafnir from [[Junketsu]], which possesses the ability to assume the properties of other materials.
Fafnir's demonstrated transformations are connected with its own elemental and material foundation. Its ability to become weapons made from entirely unrelated substances has not been established.
The novel also does not identify a complete inventory of specific weapon forms Agnes can create through this ability. It confirms the general transformation mechanism but does not establish that every possible weapon design has already been demonstrated in combat.

### Wielders and Compatibility

#### Agnes Elderblood Snow
[[Agnes Elderblood Snow]] is the principal confirmed wielder of the Blade of Fafnir and the individual most closely associated with its modern history.
As the leader of Clan Snow, Agnes inherited responsibility for the weapon alongside the family's other ancestral traditions. Her natural Fire made her particularly compatible with its original amplification properties, while her subsequent development allowed her to master its Draconic consciousness more completely.
Following the Clan Initiation Ritual and her strengthening through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood, [[Fafnir]] fully acknowledged Agnes as its Master. This recognition established a more cooperative relationship between the ancient Dragon consciousness and the Countess.[3]
Victor's later modification of Fafnir's Soul and its eventual development into a Fire Elemental deepened that relationship further, allowing Agnes to fuse with the weapon and manifest abilities unavailable during its original introduction.

#### Previous Snow Clan Leaders
The weapon's traditional Martial Art was practiced by successive leaders of Clan Snow, demonstrating that the ancestral sword formed part of the family's historical combat inheritance.
However, the names of earlier individual wielders and the circumstances surrounding their use of [[Fafnir]] have not been completely documented.
The existence of a Clan tradition does not establish that every historical Clan Head permanently possessed the sword, nor does it provide sufficient information to reconstruct each previous wielder's personal techniques.

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] has directly handled and modified the Blade of Fafnir, but he is not its established owner.
His earliest important interaction demonstrated that his supernatural power could suppress the Dragon consciousness residing within the weapon. Later, he directly altered that consciousness through his violet flames, changing [[Fafnir]]'s gender and influencing the sword's subsequent development.[2][4]
These interactions demonstrate Victor's ability to affect the artifact through powers beyond those ordinarily available to its wielder.
They do not establish that he permanently replaced Agnes as Fafnir's Master or that the sword became one of his personal weapons.

### Limitations
Despite its extraordinary development, the Blade of Fafnir is not confirmed to possess unlimited abilities or unrestricted superiority over other Divine Artifacts.
- Wielder Dependence: Its original amplification function is specifically associated with the Fire of the [[Snow Clan]]'s leader, while its evolved fusion techniques have been demonstrated through Agnes.[1][5]
- Independent Consciousness: [[Fafnir]] possesses its own spiritual existence and initially displayed resistance toward its host. The sword did not always behave as a completely obedient object.[3]
- Master Recognition: Fafnir fully accepted Agnes only after her subsequent supernatural development. The novel does not establish that every individual can command its consciousness without resistance.[3]
- Limited Original Description: The initial sword possesses confirmed Fire amplification and Draconic power, but its complete original enchantment system has not been revealed.
- Elemental Transformation Restrictions: The evolved weapon can reproduce forms associated with Fafnir's material characteristics, but has not been shown freely adopting every unrelated substance.[5]
- No Confirmed Universal Material Adaptation: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] distinguished Fafnir's evolved abilities from [[Junketsu]]'s more extensive material-property transformations.[5]
- Unmeasured Fire Armor Durability: The armor's heat output was demonstrated, but its precise defensive limits have not been established.
- No Confirmed Absolute Fire: The Flames of Fafnir threatened [[Artemis]], but no demonstrated event establishes that they automatically eliminate every God or bypass every supernatural resistance.[3]
- No Confirmed Complete Dragon Resurrection: The sword contains Fafnir's surviving Soul and later develops elemental properties, but the novel does not establish that his complete original Dragon King existence has been restored.
- Unspecified Transformation Conditions: The exact mechanism through which Victor's intervention eventually produced the Fire Elemental evolution has not been fully explained.[5]
- Unconfirmed Additional Forms: The weapon can be shaped into different weapons, but the complete range of specific forms and their individual characteristics has not been documented.
- Unknown Maximum Power: The sword's precise upper limit following its evolution remains undemonstrated.
The Blade's classification as Divine-Class also should not be confused with an automatic ability to wield every Divine Concept. Its documented supernatural powers remain principally Draconic, elemental and associated with the surviving Soul of Fafnir.

### Current Status
The Blade of Fafnir remains active and is principally associated with [[Agnes Elderblood Snow]].
Its latest explicitly documented evolution occurred in Chapter 1070, when Agnes demonstrated that [[Fafnir]] had developed into a Fire Elemental-like weapon capable of merging with her body, manifesting Fire Armor and being reshaped into different weapons.[5]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] recognized that his earlier intervention had likely contributed to the transformation and considered whether similar effects could be reproduced in other living weapons. However, no confirmed event establishes that Fafnir was subsequently destroyed, permanently separated from Agnes or replaced by another artifact.
The complete capabilities of its evolved state remain unknown, particularly because the transformation occurred after its original Draconic power and living consciousness had already undergone substantial changes.
