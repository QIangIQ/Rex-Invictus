This page catalogs notable weapons, Divine Weapons, supernatural artifacts, magical items, technological constructs, consumables, Divine resources, cores, vehicles and other important objects appearing in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]].
Only objects possessing a specific name, unique supernatural property, recurring narrative significance, unusual function or sufficient importance to the setting are included. Ordinary unnamed weapons and equipment are generally omitted unless their technology, properties or role make them individually notable.
Objects known exclusively from real-world mythology are not automatically considered canonical equipment. A mythological character appearing in the novel does not establish every weapon traditionally associated with them unless the novel itself names, shows or describes the object.

### Excluded or Insufficiently Established Objects
The following are deliberately not treated as full independent entries until the novel provides sufficient direct information:
- Trishula is associated with [[Shiva]] in secondary wiki material, but a sufficiently direct novel passage establishing its MTW-specific weapon properties was not located during this audit.
- [[Poseidon]]'s Trident should not be assigned a complete MTW abilities profile purely from mythology without direct novel evidence.
- [[Hermes]]' footwear and [[Sun Wukong]]'s cloud have been discussed as examples of transportation artifacts, but the current material does not provide enough individual MTW-specific information for complete entries.
- [[Hera]]'s unnamed Divine weapon during the Greek conflict lacks enough individual description to justify a separate artifact profile.
- Albedo's Chapter 1172 sword is not indexed as a permanent artifact because she materialized it during battle and Chapter 1173 establishes that she was primarily using it as a magical catalyst rather than demonstrating a unique independent weapon.
- Violet's rotating crown is not currently separated from her Divine regalia because Chapter 1164 does not establish whether it is an independently constructed artifact, an authority manifestation or part of her projected Divine appearance.[68]
- The many pieces of alien machinery, experimental armor, crystals and fictional-inspired prototypes in Pepper's private collection are not separately listed unless the chapter provides a specific functioning design.[92]
- Generic Imperial firearms, orbital cannons and ordinary equipment are not individually indexed unless sufficient unique information is provided.
- Ordinary manifestations of Energy shaped into swords, spears or other weapons are generally abilities rather than Items unless the object demonstrates independent persistence, construction or artifact status.

### Notes
- The index is intended to catalog notable objects, not every weapon physically held by a character.
- Malleus [[Hephaestus]] remains separate from the genuine [[Hammer of Hephaestus]] because Chapter 181 explicitly establishes the copied hammer and demonstrates its individual Runic abilities.[149]
- The [[Callisto's Armor Set]] qualifies independently because the armor possesses a specific Energy-absorption and adaptive-transformation mechanism.[150]
- The artificial Imperial [[Dragon Heart]] created in Chapter 1004 must not be confused with the biological True Dragon Heart.[151]
- The [[World Core]] is Imperial technology evolved from the [[City Core]] rather than a naturally occurring planetary metaphysical core.[152]
- The Family Tower of Nightmare, the Imperial Nightmare Tower and Babylon are related but distinct systems. Properties demonstrated by one should not automatically be assigned to the others.[153][154]
- [[Shiro-Hime]] appears only in the spacecraft section rather than being duplicated throughout the technology tables.
- The Chapter 971 Dream, Madness, Storm and Destruction Essences are retained because the story treats them as discrete supernatural resources with individual developmental value.[155]
- The Chapter 971 black sphere remains deliberately unnamed beyond Unidentified Black Sphere. Its actual nature has not yet been revealed.[156]
- [[Pepper's Artificial Star]], plasma weapons and Basilisk-based wrist weapon are individually cataloged because Chapter 1162 provides enough information to distinguish them from the rest of her enormous prototype collection.[92]
- The [[Gravity Bracelets]] are not ordinary weighted bracelets. Chapter 1173 explicitly calibrates their effects through stellar gravitational benchmarks and establishes progression from stellar conditions toward neutron-star and singularity-level gravity.[106]
- The late Imperial source creates an apparent continuity issue concerning [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s [[Magic]] Restriction Gloves. Chapter 598 previously described the gloves as destroyed, while Chapter 1150 explicitly identifies Kassandra's treasured gloves as the ones Victor wore when young. Both statements are retained rather than silently replacing one with the other.[157][108]
- Nyx's Black Scythe and Violet's Order Staff are listed with descriptive names because their objects are directly shown, but the novel has not supplied confirmed individual canonical names for them.[65][68]
- Fred's original Sacred [[Spears|Spear]] is included because obtaining it directly caused his entrance into supernatural society despite the weapon's individual canonical name remaining unidentified.[158]
- The [[Veillon Empire]]'s Positive-Energy network source is cataloged separately from the Elderblood Imperial Cores. It is foreign infrastructure whose integration also functions as a method of eliminating independent will.[137]
- The World Tree teleportation network should be treated as Imperial infrastructure rather than a single portable Artifact.[94]
- Mythological abilities or properties should never be added solely because an item shares the name of a real-world mythological artifact.
