### Ownership

#### Creator
[[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]]
Gae Bolg is the signature Runic spear of Scathach Elderblood Scarlett, the former Strongest Female Vampire and current Ancestor of the Red Dragons. It is the principal weapon treasure of Clan Scarlett, personally designed and constructed by [[Scathach]] through her mastery of Runes and spearmanship. Its entirely red body is engraved with thousands of supernatural inscriptions, forming an interconnected system capable of enhancing the weapon, modifying its attacks and imposing multiple debilitating effects upon enemies.[1][2]
Unlike the ancestral treasures inherited by the Snow and Fulger Clans, Gae Bolg was constructed specifically for its current wielder. Scathach designed it around her own martial specialization, combining the fundamental properties of a spear with the versatility of advanced Runecraft. The weapon possesses exceptionally demanding requirements: according to the novel, properly wielding it requires reaching [[Poseidon|the Master]] Realm in both spearmanship and Runes.[1]
Gae Bolg's construction reflects one of the most extraordinary demonstrations of conventional Runic craftsmanship in the series. During her instruction of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and the Scarlett sisters, Scathach revealed that the spear contained precisely 2,669 interconnected Runes, arranged into a synchronized system of major, secondary and tertiary inscriptions. Its creation required several years of work because even a minor error within a complicated Runic structure could compromise the weapon's intended functions.[3][2]
The spear combines these inscriptions with Scathach's exceptional martial ability. Its demonstrated functions include supernatural multiplication, weapon recall, tracking, poisoning, bleeding, organ failure, bone destruction, weakening curses and Anti-God enchantments. Some of these effects were confirmed during Scathach's confrontation with the Greek Death God [[Thanatos]], whose unusual Divine existence demonstrated both the effectiveness and the limitations of Gae Bolg's original anti-Divine properties.[4][5]
During the destruction of the [[Greek Pantheon]], Victor Elderblood further enhanced Scathach's spear by covering it in his Progenitor Blood, granting it the ability to sever Souls. The modification addressed the fundamental limitation revealed by Thanatos: while the original Anti-God enchantments could permanently kill ordinary Gods, certain Divine beings possessed survival mechanisms that required direct interference with their Souls or deeper existence.[6]
Scathach continued employing Gae Bolg after becoming a Blood Dragon. By Chapter 1038, she could channel enormous quantities of supernatural Energy through its Runes while performing advanced Grandmaster-level techniques capable of distorting Reality. One such technique, Pierce to Infinity — Gae Bolg, forced Victor to prepare a defense using [[Junketsu]].[7]
The later Imperial Era introduced an additional distinction. During Scathach's confrontation with [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]], she employed an advanced Divine spear created for her by Victor through Draconic and Sinister Runes. Although this weapon belongs to Scathach's later arsenal, the available account does not explicitly establish whether it is a further evolution of Gae Bolg or a separate spear. The two descriptions must therefore be distinguished rather than automatically treated as identical.[8]

### Description
Gae Bolg is a completely red spear whose body is engraved with thousands of Runes. Chapter 506 describes the weapon as a personal creation of [[Scathach]], specifically constructed through her Runic knowledge and designed exclusively for her own use. Its crimson appearance complements her traditional combat equipment and reflects its longstanding association with the [[Scarlett Clan]].[1]
The engravings are not merely decorative or symbolic. They constitute a functional supernatural system in which numerous inscriptions interact to regulate the weapon's characteristics and the effects it can produce. Scathach's explanation of Runecraft demonstrates that the complexity of Gae Bolg originates not simply from the number of inscriptions it contains, but from the precision required to make different instructions function together without producing incompatible or unstable results.[3][2]
The spear is also exceptionally durable by the standards of conventional Runic equipment. Scathach explained that inscriptions impose supernatural instructions upon Reality and that repeated activation produces wear upon the material containing them. She therefore required sufficiently resilient materials to support the weapon's complicated enchantments and spent several years completing its construction. The exact substance from which Gae Bolg was forged has not been identified in the verified chapters.[3]
Gae Bolg's practical appearance changes during combat when Scathach activates its supernatural functions. Its Runes glow intensely, while enormous quantities of crimson Energy can gather around its shaft and spearhead. During the battle against Inari, the weapon's engravings activated to reproduce the original spear into thousands of attacking copies, while a later attack enveloped it in concentrated red Energy powerful enough to threaten the Goddess severely.[4]
Its supernatural characteristics are closely connected to Scathach's skill as a Grandmaster spearwoman. The weapon does not replace the necessity for [[Martial Arts]] or technical knowledge; instead, it allows her to express principles of piercing, acceleration, precision and Runic interference through a weapon capable of supporting extraordinarily complicated effects.

### Origin and Name
Gae Bolg was personally created by [[Scathach]] rather than inherited as a completed treasure from an earlier Clan leader. Chapter 506 explicitly identifies the spear as her own design, constructed through her knowledge of Runes and intended for her exclusive use.[1]
The name is connected to [[Dun Scaith Elderblood]], Scathach's former master. The novel associates Gae Bolg's naming with the legendary name given by [[Dún Scáith|Dun Scaith]], establishing a connection between Scathach's present weapon and the martial tradition represented by her teacher.[1]
The name also recalls the legendary spear Gáe Bolg associated with the Irish hero Cú Chulainn. However, the spear wielded by Scathach in the main story is explicitly a modern personal construction rather than a confirmed surviving original from the historical legend. The older legendary weapon and Scathach's own creation should not be treated as the same physical object without additional evidence.[1]
Gae Bolg must also be distinguished from the older spear Scathach later gave to Ruby. Chapter 524 reveals that Scathach possessed another Runic spear during her youth, originally given to her by her mother and subsequently improved through her own Runecraft. That earlier weapon carried her childhood ambition of becoming the strongest, but it was not the same spear as her later personal masterpiece.[9][2]

### History

#### Creation and Early Development
The exact date of Gae Bolg's creation has not been established. However, the weapon was already a recognized treasure of Clan Scarlett by the events surrounding the Meeting of Supernatural Beings. Chapter 506 identifies it as a completely red spear containing thousands of Runes, personally created by [[Scathach]] after developing the Runic knowledge necessary to construct an artifact of such complexity.[1]
Scathach's earlier life was characterized by her continuous pursuit of martial perfection. Although spearmanship became her principal specialization, she also developed extensive knowledge of Runic construction under [[Dún Scáith|Dun Scaith]] and through subsequent independent study. Gae Bolg united those disciplines in a single weapon, transforming the ordinary advantages of a spear into a complicated supernatural system capable of producing effects that conventional martial technique alone could not achieve.
The construction required considerable effort. Scathach eventually explained that preparing a complex enchanted weapon was fundamentally different from inscribing a simple Rune upon an ordinary object. Supernatural instructions place stress upon the materials containing them, and repeated activation can damage or erase an inscription if its construction is unsuitable. Creating a weapon intended for long-term use therefore demanded precise engraving, durable materials and compatibility among the various functions.[3]
By Chapter 715, Gae Bolg contained 2,669 interconnected Runes. Their arrangement was sophisticated enough that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Ruby, Siena, Lacus and Pepper immediately recognized the extraordinary accomplishment after Scathach explained the principles involved. Rather than merely engraving thousands of independent effects, she had created a synchronized system in which numerous instructions complemented and supported one another.[2]
The time required to construct the spear was also significant. Scathach spent several years completing its Runic structure, an accomplishment reflecting both her technical proficiency and her willingness to refine individual details until the resulting weapon satisfied her standards.[3][2]

#### Clan Scarlett's Treasure
Gae Bolg eventually became the principal weapon treasure of Clan Scarlett and was recognized among the three famous Clan treasures carried by [[Nightingale]]'s ancient Vampire Countesses.[1]
The other two principal treasures were the [[Blade of Fafnir]], carried by [[Agnes Elderblood Snow]], and [[Taranis]], the ancestral daggers associated with [[Annasthashia Elderblood Fulger]] and the [[Fulger Clan]].
Unlike those weapons, Gae Bolg was personally created by the Countess who wielded it. Its importance therefore reflected [[Scathach]]'s individual accomplishments as much as the standing of her Clan.
When the three Countesses assembled at Vlad's castle before the Meeting of Supernatural Beings, Scathach appeared carrying the entirely red spear behind her. The narration identified its creator, Runic construction and extraordinary wielding requirements while placing it alongside the treasures of the Snow and Fulger Clans.[1]
The weapon became a visible representation of Clan Scarlett's military tradition. Whereas the other Count Clans possessed their own ancestral symbols, Gae Bolg represented the authority of a matriarch who had earned her reputation through warfare and martial development rather than inheritance alone.

#### Battle against Inari Ōmikami
One of Gae Bolg's earliest major combat demonstrations occurred during [[Scathach]]'s confrontation with Inari Ōmikami in Japan.
Scathach initially attacked the Goddess through direct martial techniques before preparing to use her spear. She covered the weapon with supernatural Ice, took a throwing stance and launched it at extraordinary speed, generating several sonic booms as it crossed the distance toward Inari.[4]
Inari reacted by releasing Fire and preparing additional elemental attacks. Scathach responded by activating a Runic command:
"Multiply."
The Runes engraved upon the thrown spear immediately began glowing, causing the weapon to reproduce itself into numerous copies. Within moments, thousands of spears were flying toward Inari simultaneously.[4]
The attack demonstrated that Gae Bolg's enchantments were not restricted to passive reinforcement. Scathach could activate predetermined supernatural instructions during the weapon's flight, changing the scale and behavior of an attack after its initial release.
Inari attempted to escape through a spatial opening, but Scathach froze the surrounding Space, obstructing the Goddess's movement. The original spear continued toward her and narrowly missed a direct strike to the head, leaving a superficial cut that began freezing her body. The interaction demonstrated Scathach's ability to combine Runic weapon functions with her own advanced Cryokinesis.[4]
After Inari escaped the remaining barrage, Scathach raised her hand and recalled the spear. Its Runes glowed red, the weapon reversed its movement and returned from the upper atmosphere to her hand.[4]
Scathach subsequently prepared a more concentrated attack. The spear's engravings glowed intensely while an enormous quantity of crimson Energy gathered around it and spread across her body. She compressed the resulting aura, giving her appearance the characteristics of a red comet as she accelerated toward Inari.
The Goddess recognized that a direct hit could cause injuries severe enough to leave her incapacitated for an enormous period. The attack therefore represented the combination of Gae Bolg's Runic construction with a specialized martial technique Scathach had originally developed while attempting to pierce the exceptionally durable body of a Behemoth.[4]
The novel explicitly states that the piercing technique had not been formally named. It should not be confused with the later named technique Pierce to Infinity — Gae Bolg unless an explicit connection is established.

#### The Gift of Scathach's Former Spear
During the Demonic War, Ruby received a different spear from [[Scathach]]. This incident is important when distinguishing Gae Bolg from the other weapons historically associated with its creator.
Chapter 524 reveals that the older weapon had originally belonged to Scathach during her youth and was given to her by her own mother when she became an adult. Scathach subsequently improved the weapon after becoming a Runemaster, using it throughout a substantial portion of her life.[9]
Scathach described the older spear as a representation of her youthful dream of becoming the strongest. She entrusted it to Ruby because her daughter was preparing to confront dangers that Scathach could not always personally prevent.
Ruby later carried the weapon while defending Clan Scarlett's territory against the Demon Duke [[Zagan]] and his army. Its Runes responded to Ruby's intentions, demonstrating that the spear remained a functional supernatural weapon in the hands of its new owner.[9]
Chapter 715 explicitly distinguishes Ruby's inherited spear from Scathach's current weapon, identifying the former as the spear Scathach had used for most of her life and describing it as second only to her current spear in Runic complexity.[2]
Gae Bolg therefore represents Scathach's later achievement as a Runemaster, while Ruby's inherited spear represents an earlier period of Scathach's development and her personal history.

#### Runic Structure Revealed
[[Scathach]] later provided a formal explanation of Runecraft to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Ruby, Pepper, Siena and Lacus. Rather than simply demonstrate how to inscribe symbols, she explained the principles determining whether an enchantment could function, how its language affected Energy expenditure and why complex Runic equipment demanded enormous preparation.[3]
The discussion established that Runes require three fundamental components: a language possessing supernatural significance, the intention of the practitioner and sufficient Energy to impose that intention upon Reality.
Scathach also demonstrated the problems associated with employing Divine and Demonic languages despite possessing a different racial foundation. Such inscriptions could produce powerful effects but consumed substantially greater Energy when the user's existence lacked natural compatibility with their source.[3]
Gae Bolg served as the principal example of a weapon designed to overcome the limitations of improvised Runic combat. Its supernatural instructions were prepared and engraved in advance, allowing Scathach to activate selected functions during battle instead of constructing every effect from the beginning.
The complexity of the weapon became clearer during the following lesson. Scathach explained that functional Rune systems could contain primary instructions, supporting instructions and additional tertiary instructions modifying the behavior of those supporting effects. Gae Bolg's 2,669 Runes represented an advanced application of this structure, arranged to work together in perfect synchronization.[2]
The explanation also established the significance of Ruby's inherited spear. Scathach's earlier weapon already possessed extraordinary Runic complexity, yet it remained inferior to Gae Bolg's later construction. Her current weapon therefore represented years of additional experience and improvement rather than simply an older treasure retained without modification.[2]

#### Confrontation with Thanatos
Gae Bolg's anti-Divine properties became particularly important during [[Scathach]]'s expedition into the [[Greek Underworld]] alongside Nyx Elderblood, [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood and [[Morgana Elderblood]].
Scathach detected the presence of [[Thanatos]], [[Hypnos]] and [[Nemesis]] before the other members of her group fully recognized the concealed Gods. She then approached their location and prepared a Runic spear technique while entering her perfected Vampire Count Transformation.[5]
Scathach invoked Celestial Piercing and threw her spear. Its Runes began glowing and rotating at extraordinary speed before the weapon was launched toward Thanatos's head.
The attack occurred within milliseconds. Thanatos was unable to respond before the spear penetrated his skull, destroying his manifested body. The resulting release of force devastated the surrounding terrain and produced an enormous mountain of Ice within the Greek Underworld.[5]
The attack initially appeared to have eliminated the Death God. However, Nyx warned that Thanatos possessed an unusual existence that could not be permanently destroyed through conventional anti-Divine attacks.
She explained that the Anti-God Enchantments installed upon Scathach's spear would have been sufficient to permanently kill an ordinary God, but that the Personification of Death required a different method. Permanently eliminating Thanatos would require the Concept of END or direct destruction of his Soul through an appropriate Soul-related authority.[5]
Thanatos subsequently reconstructed his body and returned to the confrontation. Scathach discovered that the various curses applied through her spear had also disappeared, including its poisoning, bleeding, organ failure, bone destruction, weakening and tracking effects.
The incident demonstrated both the potency of Gae Bolg's Runic system and the limitations of relying exclusively upon ordinary Anti-God enchantments. The weapon could permanently eliminate conventional Divine opponents, but beings possessing more exceptional survival mechanisms could require interference with the deeper foundations supporting their existence.[5]

#### Victor's Soul-Severing Enhancement
During the final conflict against the [[Greek Pantheon]], [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] recognized that [[Scathach]] possessed the necessary strength and experience to confront Gods but lacked the appropriate method for bypassing the extraordinary survival mechanisms of certain Divine beings.
He approached Scathach and allowed his Blood to emerge from his hand, spreading across her spear until the entire weapon was covered in his Progenitor Blood.[6]
Victor then confirmed that the weapon could now sever Souls. His Vampire Progenitor existence possessed an inherent connection with Souls, allowing him to grant the spear a function that Scathach's earlier Anti-God enchantments could not independently reproduce.
The modification represented a significant improvement. Gae Bolg no longer depended exclusively upon causing sufficient physical or supernatural damage to eliminate a Divine body; its attacks could now interfere directly with the Soul supporting that existence.
Scathach reacted enthusiastically to the enhancement and immediately prepared to enter the battlefield. She activated her perfected Vampire Count Transformation and confronted the Greek Gods, selecting [[Helios]] as one of her opponents.[6]
The new capability addressed the specific limitation demonstrated during the encounter with [[Thanatos]]. However, the novel does not provide a detailed explanation of how long Victor's Blood infusion remained active, nor does it establish that every later appearance of Gae Bolg necessarily retained precisely the same configuration.
The Soul-severing enhancement is therefore a confirmed capability of the weapon during the Greek Pantheon conflict. Its permanence and subsequent modification history should not be assumed without additional evidence.

#### Further Development
[[Scathach]]'s transformation into a Blood Dragon fundamentally changed her body and supernatural Energy, but her accumulated knowledge of spearmanship and Runecraft remained part of her existence.[10]
Her later training with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] continued incorporating advanced spear techniques, including applications capable of damaging or distorting Space and Reality.
By Chapter 1038, Scathach was still explicitly using Gae Bolg during a training confrontation with Victor. The spear's Runes glowed as she gathered enormous quantities of crimson Energy, allowing her to employ a powerful piercing technique whose activation distorted Reality.[7]
The demonstration confirmed that Gae Bolg remained relevant after Scathach's transformation into a True Dragon and her continued development beyond the limitations of her original Vampire existence.

#### Pierce to Infinity
During the training surrounding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s research into [[Martial Arts]] beyond the conventional Grandmaster foundation, [[Scathach]] employed a series of rapid spear attacks designed to exploit the limitations imposed upon him during the exercise.
After maneuvering into a favorable position, she grasped the spear in midair and concentrated an enormous amount of crimson Energy through its engraved Runes.[11][7]
Scathach then invoked Pierce to Infinity — Gae Bolg, bending Reality around the technique before launching the weapon toward Victor.
Victor responded by transforming his right arm through [[Junketsu]] and preparing to endure the attack directly.[7]
The exchange demonstrated the continued significance of Gae Bolg as a medium for Scathach's Grandmaster spearmanship. Its Runic system could support enormous concentrations of supernatural Energy, while Scathach's accumulated understanding of piercing allowed her to apply those resources through techniques affecting Reality.
The distortion of Reality must nevertheless be distinguished from an independent passive property of the spear. The demonstrated effect occurred through Scathach's own advanced martial technique, with Gae Bolg serving as the weapon through which it was expressed.

#### Imperial Era
During the later Imperial Era, [[Scathach]]'s combat equipment developed beyond the specifications established during her Vampire existence. She became a Dragon Goddess and the Supreme Commander of the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s Armed Forces, accumulating extraordinary experience through accelerated periods of training and military development.
Chapter 1172 introduces an advanced Divine spear crafted for her by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] using Draconic and Sinister Runes. Scathach summoned the weapon when she began seriously confronting Albedo, and its construction was described as a masterpiece suited to her current combat capabilities.[8]
A single controlled sweep destroyed thousands of Albedo's [[Magic]] Circles and their accompanying attacks, demonstrating the effectiveness of Scathach's modern spear combat against another Dragon Goddess.
However, this later weapon is not explicitly named Gae Bolg in the available chapter. The account distinguishes a weaker version from the Divine spear, but does not provide sufficient information to determine whether these are different versions of the original Gae Bolg or separately constructed weapons.
The modern Divine spear must therefore remain separately identified until the novel directly establishes its relationship with Scathach's original weapon.

### Runic Construction

#### Fundamental Principles
Gae Bolg's supernatural abilities originate primarily from the Runic knowledge [[Scathach]] developed throughout her life. Conventional Runecraft operates through the imposition of meaningful instructions upon Reality using an appropriate language, intention and Energy.[3]
Scathach explained that the choice of language directly influences the cost and effectiveness of a Rune. Ancient languages possess different supernatural foundations, and their compatibility with the practitioner's existence determines how efficiently Energy can be converted into the intended result.
The same principle applies to the complexity of the resulting effect. A simple inscription may require comparatively little preparation, while a complicated enchantment involving numerous interacting instructions can become exceptionally difficult to construct or maintain.
Gae Bolg was designed to solve several of these problems through advance preparation. Rather than create every Rune during battle, Scathach engraved a vast network of supernatural instructions upon the spear and arranged them so they could be activated according to the requirements of a particular attack.[3][2]
The weapon's construction also required sufficiently durable materials. Scathach explained that activating a Rune imposes wear upon the inscription and the material carrying it. Repeated activation can therefore compromise ordinary equipment, while suitable materials and carefully prepared inscriptions allow more reliable use.

#### The 2,669-Rune System
[[Scathach]]'s explanation of Runecraft revealed that Gae Bolg contained exactly 2,669 synchronized Runes. This is the most precise confirmed numerical description of the spear's original enchantment system.[2]
The inscriptions were not independent effects placed together without organization. They formed a coordinated structure in which major Runes established principal functions, secondary Runes supported those functions and further inscriptions modified the operation of the supporting effects.
Scathach emphasized that this hierarchy did not possess a strict limit of only three layers. More complex arrangements could employ additional tiers, provided the creator possessed the knowledge and patience necessary to maintain compatibility among the instructions.
The resulting system represents an extraordinary achievement because a complicated Rune array must remain functional despite the relationships between different supernatural commands. A weapon containing thousands of improperly combined instructions would not automatically become more powerful; its effectiveness depends upon the precision with which those instructions interact.
Gae Bolg's 2,669-Rune configuration therefore demonstrates Scathach's capacity for large-scale Runic synchronization and complicated supernatural equipment design.

#### Examples of Possible Runic Enchantments
During Chapter 715, [[Scathach]] explained how a practitioner could create an enchanted spear by combining various inscriptions. Her examples included Piercing, Return, Recovery, Pursuit, Security and Curse Runes.[2]
These examples are relevant to understanding Gae Bolg's construction but must not automatically be treated as an exhaustive or literal list of every Rune installed on the weapon.
- Piercing Rune: Scathach described a primary inscription that could increase a spear's penetrating effectiveness. This was part of her instructional example; Gae Bolg's actual piercing properties are demonstrated through its use in combat.[2][5]
- Return Rune: A primary inscription capable of recalling a weapon to its wielder. Gae Bolg demonstrably possesses a supernatural recall function during the battles against Inari and [[Thanatos]].[4][2][5]
- Recovery Rune: An example of an enchantment capable of restoring a damaged weapon toward its previous condition. Its presence as a specific installed Rune on Gae Bolg has not been independently confirmed.[2]
- Pursuit Rune: An example of an effect causing a weapon to follow the intended target. Gae Bolg's tracking enchantments are independently confirmed during the confrontation with Thanatos.[2][5]
- Security Rune: An example of an inscription restricting use by unauthorized individuals. The novel does not establish that this particular example is installed on Gae Bolg.[2]
- Curse Rune: An example of a tertiary inscription capable of punishing unauthorized users when combined with a security effect. Gae Bolg possesses harmful curses, but a specific unauthorized-user curse has not been confirmed.[2][5]
The distinction is important because Scathach's lessons describe the principles of Runecraft generally. The precise nature of Gae Bolg's actual enchantments must be established through statements concerning the weapon itself or demonstrations of its effects.

### Powers and Abilities
Gae Bolg's confirmed supernatural characteristics originate from its Runic construction, [[Scathach]]'s later enhancements and the temporary or potentially enduring modifications applied by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Several effects require activation or the application of its wielder's Energy rather than functioning independently at all times.

#### Runic and Weapon Abilities
- Runic Weapon Physiology: Gae Bolg is a supernatural spear constructed around an extensive network of engraved Runes.[1][2]
- Rune-Based Empowerment: Its supernatural inscriptions enhance the weapon's effectiveness beyond the properties of an ordinary spear.[2]
- Multi-Layered Enchantment: The spear possesses numerous primary, secondary and tertiary Runic instructions operating together.[2]
- Runic Synchronization: Its 2,669 documented inscriptions are arranged into a mutually complementary system.[2]
- Supernatural Weapon Multiplication: The command Multiply can reproduce the spear into thousands of attacking copies.[4]
- Projectile Duplication: Gae Bolg's multiplication occurs while the weapon is already traveling toward an opponent.[4]
- Weapon Recall: The spear can return to [[Scathach]]'s hand after being thrown.[4][5]
- Remote Weapon Control: Scathach can trigger the weapon's return without physically retrieving it.[4]
- Runic Activation: The engravings respond to supernatural commands and Energy supplied by their wielder.[4][7]
- Energy Channeling: Gae Bolg can serve as the medium for enormous quantities of supernatural Energy.[4][7]
- Energy Concentration: Scathach can gather and concentrate power around the spear's point or shaft before launching an attack.[7]
- Runic Projectile Enhancement: The weapon's inscriptions can modify the scale and behavior of attacks after they have been launched.[4]
- Supernatural Tracking: Gae Bolg possesses tracking enchantments intended to pursue or identify designated targets.[5]
- Weapon-Based Elemental Integration: Scathach can combine Gae Bolg with her Cryokinesis, including attacks capable of freezing the target after contact.[4]
- Grandmaster Weapon Compatibility: Its construction supports the advanced spearmanship developed by Scathach.[5][7]
- Reality-Affecting Technique Compatibility: Gae Bolg can serve as the medium through which Scathach performs advanced techniques capable of distorting Reality.[7]

#### Curses and Debilitating Effects
The confrontation with [[Thanatos]] establishes that Gae Bolg possesses several harmful Runic enchantments. [[Scathach]] explicitly identified these effects after Thanatos restored himself and removed the supernatural conditions applied through the weapon.[5]
- Poisoning: The spear possesses enchantments intended to inflict supernatural poisoning upon struck targets.[5]
- Bleeding Inducement: Its Runes can inflict or aggravate bleeding through supernatural effects.[5]
- Organ Failure Inducement: The weapon contains enchantments designed to cause the failure of internal organs.[5]
- Bone Destruction: Gae Bolg possesses Runic effects intended to damage or destroy skeletal structures.[5]
- Weakening Curse: Its inscriptions can apply debilitating supernatural effects that reduce an opponent's ability to fight effectively.[5]
- Tracking Curse: Scathach identified tracking among the supernatural effects removed when Thanatos reconstructed his existence.[5]
- Multiple Curse Application: The spear can impose several different debilitating effects during combat.[5]
- Runic Status Effects: Gae Bolg's supernatural enchantments can produce consequences beyond the physical damage caused by a spear wound.[5]
These effects are distinct from the ordinary physical consequences of a spear strike. They are supernatural conditions produced through the weapon's enchantments.
Their effectiveness nevertheless depends upon the target's defenses, survival mechanisms and the comparative supernatural authority involved. Thanatos demonstrated that certain Divine existences can restore themselves in a manner that removes accumulated Runic effects.[5]

#### Anti-Divine Properties
- Anti-God Enchantment: Gae Bolg possesses supernatural inscriptions specifically designed to kill Gods.[5]
- Divine Body Destruction: The weapon can destroy the manifested bodies of Divine beings, as demonstrated during Celestial Piercing against [[Thanatos]].[5]
- God-Killing Capability: Nyx confirmed that the weapon's Anti-God enchantments could permanently kill ordinary Gods.[5]
- Divine [[Immortality]] Bypassing (Conditional): The Anti-God enchantments can overcome the conventional survival mechanisms available to Gods who do not possess the more exceptional characteristics demonstrated by Thanatos.[5]
- Divine Defensive Penetration: [[Scathach]]'s techniques allow the enchanted spear to threaten opponents possessing advanced Divine bodies and supernatural defenses.[5]
- Anti-Divine Curse Application: The weapon's harmful enchantments can be applied in encounters involving Divine beings.[5]
- Anti-God Combat Specialization: Its advanced Runic construction makes it suitable for Scathach's confrontations with Gods and other exceptionally powerful supernatural opponents.[5]
Limitation: Gae Bolg's original Anti-God enchantments did not permanently eliminate Thanatos. His existence as the Personification of Death allowed him to reconstruct his body and remove the effects inflicted by the spear. This does not mean the enchantments were ineffective against ordinary Gods; Nyx specifically identified Thanatos as an exceptional case.[5]

#### Soul-Severing Enhancement
During the [[Greek Pantheon]] conflict, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] covered the weapon in his Progenitor Blood and directly granted it the ability to sever Souls.[6]
- Soul Severing (Victor's Blood enhancement): Gae Bolg gained the ability to sever Souls through Victor's intervention.[6]
- Soul Damage: The enhancement allows successful attacks to affect the target's Soul rather than merely its physical body.[6]
- Divine Soul Interference: The modified weapon can threaten the spiritual foundation supporting a God's continued existence.[6]
- Soul-Based [[Immortality]] Negation (Conditional): The enhancement addresses survival mechanisms that depend upon the continued existence of the target's Soul.[5][6]
- Progenitor Blood Empowerment: Victor's Blood served as the source of the new Soul-affecting property.[6]
- Anti-Divine Enhancement: The modification improved the spear's effectiveness against Divine opponents possessing exceptional survival mechanisms.[6]
Enhancement status: The Soul-severing effect is directly confirmed during Chapter 796. The novel does not explicitly establish whether the coating remained permanently incorporated into the weapon or whether subsequent upgrades changed its operation. Its confirmed application should not be extended automatically into unrestricted Soul Destruction against every possible Being.

### Techniques

#### Multiply
Multiply is a confirmed Runic command that causes Gae Bolg to reproduce itself into numerous attacking spears. [[Scathach]] demonstrated it during her confrontation with Inari Ōmikami.[4]
After throwing the spear toward Inari, Scathach brought her palms together and activated the command. The original weapon's inscriptions glowed intensely, and it immediately produced multiple copies of itself. Within moments, thousands of red spears were moving toward the Goddess.
The technique does not require Scathach to throw each copy independently. Instead, the Runic effect modifies the existing projectile after it has already been launched, creating an enormous barrage from one original attack.
- Weapon Multiplication: Produces numerous copies of the spear.[4]
- Mass Projectile Creation: Generates a large-scale barrage from one thrown weapon.[4]
- Runic Activation: The effect is triggered through the engraved Runes and Scathach's command.[4]
- Mid-Flight Transformation: The copies are produced while the original spear is traveling toward its target.[4]
- Area Saturation: Thousands of projectiles create a difficult environment for opponents attempting to evade the attack.[4]
The chapter demonstrates thousands of copies but does not establish an unlimited number of simultaneous reproductions.

#### Weapon Recall
Gae Bolg possesses a supernatural recall function that allows [[Scathach]] to retrieve the weapon after throwing it.
During the battle against Inari, Scathach raised her hand while the spear was traveling toward the upper atmosphere. Its engravings glowed red, and the weapon reversed its movement before returning to her grasp.[4]
The same general effect appeared during the confrontation with [[Thanatos]], when Scathach recalled her spear after delivering Celestial Piercing.[5]
- Supernatural Weapon Recall: Returns the spear to its wielder after being thrown.[4]
- Remote Activation: The return effect can be triggered without Scathach physically contacting the weapon.[4]
- Runic Movement Control: The spear can change its direction under the influence of its supernatural inscriptions.[4]
- Repeated Weapon Use: Recall permits Scathach to reuse the same spear immediately after a ranged attack.[5]
Scathach later explained the general principle of Return Runes during her Runic lessons, providing a technical foundation for understanding the weapon's demonstrated behavior.[2]

#### Unnamed High-Speed Piercing Technique
During the battle against Inari, [[Scathach]] employed a specialized piercing technique whose appearance resembled a red comet. The chapter explicitly states that she had not formally named the technique.[4]
Scathach concentrated an enormous quantity of crimson Energy through her spear, with the resulting aura spreading across her body. She then compressed that aura to reduce resistance during movement, allowing her to accelerate toward the Goddess with extraordinary speed.
The technique had originated from Scathach's attempt to develop an attack capable of penetrating the unusually durable body of a Behemoth. Through continued refinement, it became powerful enough to threaten the body of a Goddess.
- High-Speed Piercing: Concentrates force into a direct spear attack.[4]
- Runic Energy Channeling: Uses the spear's engraved Runes to support the concentrated attack.[4]
- Crimson Aura Manifestation: Surrounds Scathach and her weapon in a red supernatural aura.[4]
- Air-Resistance Reduction: The compressed aura reduces atmospheric interference with her movement.[4]
- Momentum Accumulation: Increases the force applied to the spear as Scathach accelerates.[4]
- Extreme Durability Penetration: Developed to threaten opponents possessing exceptionally resistant physical bodies.[4]
Name: The technique is unnamed. Its resemblance to a red comet is a visual description, not an established canonical name.

#### Celestial Piercing
Celestial Piercing is a named Runic spear technique demonstrated during [[Scathach]]'s confrontation with [[Thanatos]] in the [[Greek Underworld]].[5]
Scathach activated her perfected Vampire Count Transformation, took a throwing stance and invoked the technique. The Runes engraved upon the spear began rotating and glowing intensely before she launched it toward the concealed Death God.
The attack occurred within milliseconds. Thanatos was unable to react before Gae Bolg penetrated his head and destroyed his manifested body. The resulting force split the surrounding ground and generated an enormous Ice mountain, substantially altering the environment of the Greek Underworld.
- Celestial Piercing: A named Grandmaster spear attack employing Gae Bolg's Runic system.[5]
- High-Speed Attack: The spear travels quickly enough to overwhelm Thanatos's immediate reactions.[5]
- Anti-God Enchantment: The weapon's existing Runic properties make the attack dangerous to ordinary Gods.[5]
- Divine Body Destruction: The demonstrated strike destroyed Thanatos's physical manifestation.[5]
- Runic Energy Concentration: The spear's inscriptions activate intensely during the attack.[5]
- Delayed Destructive Force: The attack continued damaging the surrounding environment after the initial impact.[5]
- Large-Scale Environmental Destruction: The strike devastated the Greek Underworld's terrain and created an enormous Ice formation.[5]
Limitation: Thanatos restored himself through his unusual Divine existence. Although Celestial Piercing destroyed his manifested body, its original enchantments could not permanently eliminate the Personification of Death.[5]

#### Soul-Severing Strike
Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood enhancement, [[Scathach]] could employ Gae Bolg in attacks capable of severing Souls.[6]
The enhancement did not require Scathach to abandon her existing martial techniques. Instead, Victor added a property to the spear that allowed its successful strikes to threaten the Soul supporting an opponent's physical or Divine existence.
- Soul Severing: Adds direct spiritual damage to the weapon's attacks.[6]
- Divine Soul Damage: Allows the spear to threaten Gods through their Souls.[6]
- Enhanced Anti-Divine Lethality: Addresses the limitation demonstrated against Divine beings capable of reconstructing their manifested bodies.[5][6]
Naming distinction: Soul-Severing Strike is a descriptive designation for attacks using Victor's enhancement. The chapter confirms the ability but does not introduce it as a separate formally named technique.

#### Pierce to Infinity — Gae Bolg
Pierce to Infinity — Gae Bolg is one of the most advanced named techniques demonstrated through the spear. [[Scathach]] employed it during her later martial training against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], at a time when both were investigating methods of progressing beyond conventional Grandmaster combat.[7]
During the exchange, Scathach exploited the restrictions placed upon Victor and maneuvered into a position from which she could launch an extraordinarily powerful attack. She grasped her spear in midair as its Runes began glowing, gathering an enormous concentration of crimson Energy.
She then invoked the technique, bending Reality around the attack before throwing the spear toward Victor like a projectile of concentrated red power.
Victor responded by transforming his right arm through [[Junketsu]] and preparing to intercept the attack directly.
- Pierce to Infinity: A named advanced spear technique employed by Scathach.[7]
- Grandmaster Spearmanship: The technique incorporates her exceptional understanding of piercing and weapon control.[7]
- Runic Weapon Empowerment: The spear's engravings activate while Scathach concentrates enormous power.[7]
- Crimson Energy Concentration: Enormous quantities of red supernatural Energy gather around the weapon.[7]
- Reality Distortion: Scathach bends Reality while activating the technique.[7]
- High-Speed [[Spears|Spear]] Projection: The weapon is launched toward Victor with extraordinary force.[7]
- Defensive Pressure: Victor prepared an active defense through Junketsu rather than remaining completely passive.[7]
The ability to distort Reality occurs through Scathach's combined martial understanding, supernatural power and weapon use. It should not be classified as a separate, permanently active Reality-Manipulation enchantment of Gae Bolg without further evidence.

### Wielding Requirements
Gae Bolg possesses unusually demanding requirements compared with conventional Runic weapons. Chapter 506 explicitly states that someone must attain [[Poseidon|the Master]] Realm in both spearmanship and Runes to wield the weapon properly.[1]
The condition reflects the complexity of its construction. A wielder must possess sufficient knowledge to understand advanced martial techniques while also managing a weapon containing thousands of interconnected supernatural instructions.
- Master-Level Spearmanship: The wielder requires a high degree of technical proficiency with spears.[1]
- Master-Level Runecraft: The wielder must possess sufficient knowledge of Runes to operate the weapon's complicated supernatural structure.[1]
- Energy Control: The weapon's advanced applications require appropriate control over the Energy supplied to its engravings.[3][7]
- Runic Compatibility: Effective use of the weapon depends upon the wielder's understanding of its supernatural instructions.[2]
- Technical Precision: The complexity of the weapon makes incorrect or inefficient use a significant limitation.[1][2]
The novel describes a proficiency requirement rather than explicitly establishing a biological Scarlett-only restriction. No confirmed passage states that every person outside Clan Scarlett is physically incapable of touching the spear.
It is also important to distinguish Master Realm from [[Scathach]]'s personal achievement of Grandmastery. The weapon's minimum stated requirement does not mean every potential wielder must already possess Scathach's complete martial proficiency.

### Limitations
Despite its extraordinary capabilities, Gae Bolg is not an unrestricted weapon capable of bypassing every defense or killing every opponent automatically.
- Runic Energy Cost: Activating supernatural instructions requires Energy, and complicated enchantments can become extremely expensive when poorly optimized.[3]
- Runic Wear: Repeated activation can damage inscriptions or the material containing them if the equipment is not sufficiently durable.[3]
- Material Dependence: Conventional Runic equipment requires suitable materials capable of sustaining its supernatural functions.[3]
- Construction Complexity: The synchronization of thousands of inscriptions demands exceptional precision and extensive preparation.[2]
- Master-Level Wielding Requirement: Proper use requires proficiency in both spearmanship and Runecraft.[1]
- Comparative Divine Resistance: The weapon's enchantments can be overcome by exceptional opponents whose survival mechanisms exceed their effects.[5]
- [[Thanatos]]'s Restoration: The original Anti-God enchantments failed to permanently eliminate Thanatos because his Divine existence reconstructed his body and removed the applied curses.[5]
- Soul-Severing Enhancement Source: The Soul-affecting improvement was supplied by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Progenitor Blood rather than independently developed through the spear's original enchantments.[6]
- Unconfirmed Enhancement Permanence: The novel does not establish the exact duration or subsequent configuration of Victor's Blood coating.[6]
- Wielder-Dependent Reality Distortion: The Reality-bending effect demonstrated through Pierce to Infinity results from [[Scathach]]'s technique, not a separately confirmed passive property possessed by the spear without its wielder.[7]
- Unrevealed Complete Rune Inventory: The weapon's 2,669 inscriptions have not all been individually identified. The existence of an enormous Runic system does not establish every conceivable enchantment.[2]
- No Confirmed Absolute Hit Guarantee: The Pursuit Rune described in Scathach's lessons is capable of directing attacks toward targets, but this does not establish that Gae Bolg is incapable of missing or being countered regardless of the opponent's abilities.[4][2]
- No Confirmed Universal [[Immortality]] Negation: The spear's Anti-God and Soul-severing properties do not establish unconditional superiority over every Divine, Spiritual, Conceptual or higher-order survival mechanism.

### Related Weapons

#### Scathach's Former Spear
Before completing Gae Bolg, [[Scathach]] possessed another powerful Runic spear that she used throughout much of her earlier life. Chapter 524 establishes that her mother originally gave her the weapon when she became an adult.[9]
Scathach subsequently refined the spear after becoming a Runemaster. She associated it with the dreams and ambitions of her youth, especially her desire to become the strongest warrior.
The weapon eventually became a gift to Ruby, who received it shortly before the conflict involving [[Zagan]]'s invasion of Clan Scarlett.
Chapter 715 explicitly distinguishes this inherited weapon from Gae Bolg, identifying it as second only to Scathach's current spear in Runic complexity.[2]
The former spear should therefore not be listed as a previous name or alternate appearance of Gae Bolg. It is a separate weapon possessing its own construction history and ownership.

#### Divine Spear
During the later Imperial Era, [[Scathach]] employed a Divine spear constructed for her by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] using Draconic and Sinister Runes.[8]
The weapon was powerful enough to support combat against Albedo, a mature Dragon Goddess. When Scathach released an Energy wave through a controlled spear movement, thousands of powerful [[Magic]] Circles were destroyed.
The chapter identifies the Divine spear as a separate grade or version of weapon from a weaker configuration but does not explicitly identify it by the name Gae Bolg.
Its precise relationship to Scathach's original Runic masterpiece consequently remains undetermined. It may represent a later upgrade, an evolved configuration or a separate weapon, but no single interpretation should be presented as confirmed without direct textual evidence.

### Current Status
Last explicitly confirmed status: Active, owned and wielded by [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]].
Gae Bolg was still named and used by [[Scathach]] during her advanced martial confrontation with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] in Chapter 1038, confirming its continued relevance following her transformation into a True Dragon.[7]
Scathach's later use of a Divine spear is established in Chapter 1172, but the identity of that weapon has not been conclusively connected with the original Gae Bolg. There is no confirmed statement that Gae Bolg was destroyed, permanently retired or replaced beyond recovery.
