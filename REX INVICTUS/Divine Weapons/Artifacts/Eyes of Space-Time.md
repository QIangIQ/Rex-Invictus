Eyes of Space-Time is the commonly used name for the mysterious hereditary ocular power associated with the leadership of [[Clan Alioth]]. The Eyes grant their possessor an extraordinarily intimate relationship with the fundamental properties of Space and Time, allowing their user to perform feats ranging from portals and dimensional expansion to the manipulation of temporal flow and, at sufficiently advanced levels, interaction with the Past itself.[1][2]
Despite the name used for this article, "Eyes of Space-Time" is not a formal technique name established by Clan Alioth. After inheriting and developing the Eyes, Natalia briefly considered naming them the "Eyes of Space and Time", but dismissed the name as overly cliché and continued referring to them simply as the Eyes of the Alioth Clan Leader.[3]
The Eyes are closely related to Clan Alioth's unique bloodline, but should not be confused with the Clan's entire collection of Space-Time techniques. Every known Alioth possesses an abnormal hereditary affinity exclusively directed toward Space and Time, while the special galaxy-like Eyes appear to be an inheritance associated specifically with the Clan's successor and leadership.[4][1][5]
Their true origin remains one of the unresolved mysteries surrounding Clan Alioth. Even [[Victor Elderblood]], Alexios Alioth, Natalia and Albedo were unable to determine exactly where the Eyes originally came from. Victor suspected that they could have belonged to an extraordinarily powerful deceased God of Time or to an existence originating from the Upper Sectors, but explicitly possessed no proof for either hypothesis.[2]

### Nature

#### The Alioth Bloodline
[[Clan Alioth]] is an irregular Human lineage whose supernatural abilities originated from [[Magic]], yet developed into something distinct enough that other knowledgeable practitioners no longer considered their powers ordinary Witchcraft.
The Clan possesses magical power within its bloodline, but unlike conventional Witches, its members are fundamentally restricted to techniques associated with Space and Time. They cannot simply study unrelated branches of Magic. Their lineage behaves as though its supernatural development is permanently locked onto those two aspects of existence.[4]
As generations passed, this original Space-Time Magic evolved into the unique supernatural system associated with the Alioth family. The greatest manifestation of that lineage is represented by the Eyes carried by its Clan Leader.[4]

#### Clan Leader Inheritance
The Eyes are not shown as something possessed simultaneously by every member of [[Clan Alioth]].
[[Alexios Alioth]] explained that a special ritual is performed when the successor of Clan Alioth inherits the Eyes.[1] Much later, Natalia explicitly stated that she expected to receive the Eyes from her father in the future, further establishing that the Eyes pass between generations of Clan leadership rather than simply awakening independently within every Alioth.[5]
The exact mechanics of this transfer have not been explained.
It is presently unknown whether the Eyes are:
- physically transplanted;
- spiritually inherited;
- transferred through a Clan ritual;
- awakened in the successor when the previous holder relinquishes them;
- or transmitted through another supernatural mechanism.
What is confirmed is that Alexios possessed them as Clan Leader and Natalia later became their next known holder.

### Appearance
The Eyes possess one of the most distinctive visual appearances among supernatural ocular abilities in the series.
When [[Alexios Alioth]] opened them in front of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], his ordinary eyes disappeared completely. There were no visible pupils or sclera; instead, each eye appeared to contain an entire galaxy filled with stars.[1]
Natalia's evolved Eyes retain the same fundamental characteristic but are considerably more elaborate. After becoming a True Dragon and Dragon Goddess, her activated Eyes were described as an ever-changing cosmos, as though entire galaxies had been placed inside them.[2]
During her later use of high-level temporal manipulation, Natalia's normal Draconic eyes were completely replaced by these cosmic Eyes.[6]

### Abilities
The Eyes fundamentally enhance the user's interaction with Space and Time. Their exact performance depends heavily upon the user's body, Energy reserves, understanding and level of existence.

#### Space Manipulation
Space is the most naturally demonstrated aspect of [[Clan Alioth]]'s power.
Even before Natalia inherited the Eyes, both she and Alexios could create portals and perform Space-related techniques through their bloodline. Alexios' mastery was considered so extraordinary that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] regarded him as one of the most valuable supernatural specialists available to [[Nightingale]].[1]

##### Portal Creation
[[Clan Alioth]] can connect two separated points in Space, creating portals through which people and objects can travel.
These portals can span:
- rooms;
- cities;
- countries;
- continents;
- worlds;
- outer space;
- and sufficiently distant planetary locations.
The principal limitation is information. An Alioth cannot normally open a portal to a completely unknown destination. Precise positional information or coordinates are required to establish the connection.[5]

##### Spatial Expansion
Alexios can enlarge the interior Space of physical structures far beyond their external dimensions.
He explained that maintaining such an expanded Space requires a continuous source of Energy. With sufficient Mana crystals, Alexios believed he could turn the interior of an ordinary structure into an area comparable to an entire country.[1]
This principle was subsequently used in the development of spatial-storage technology and expanded interior structures.

##### Pocket-Space Creation
Alexios can produce isolated or expanded spaces that exist independently from the object's external dimensions.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] immediately recognized the enormous practical potential of the ability and commissioned portable containers whose interiors were vastly larger than their physical bodies.[1]

##### Dimensional Manipulation
[[Clan Alioth]]'s Space techniques are not restricted to ordinary distances.
Their power can manipulate the boundaries between dimensions and worlds, allowing them to function as some of the most effective dimensional navigators in the known supernatural world.
Alexios has also been used as a bridge between worlds and can assist with isolating or restricting access to dimensional regions.

##### Spatial Observation
Alioth portals do not always need to be large enough for physical travel.
Small openings can be used to observe distant places or establish communication through Space, allowing the user to effectively monitor locations from afar.

#### Time Manipulation
Time is the more dangerous and demanding half of the Eyes.
Alexios specifically explained that manipulating Space was comparatively straightforward for him, while serious manipulation of Time required activation of the special Eyes and carried an enormous cost because of his Mortal nature.[1]
Basic temporal applications include:
- slowing Time;
- accelerating Time;
- isolating a temporal region;
- changing the Time Flow affecting an object or location;
- extending biological lifespan;
- and maintaining temporal effects through Runes or continuous Energy.
At more advanced levels, the Eyes allow their user to interact directly with the Past.[2]

#### Temporal Deceleration
The Alioth Clan can slow the passage of Time affecting a specific target.
This ability forms the basis of the ritual used to extend the lifespan of the Clan Leader. Rather than making the successor biologically immortal, the ritual causes the Time of the user's body to move more slowly.[1]
The result grants approximately another five hundred years of potential lifespan under ordinary circumstances.[1]

#### Temporal Acceleration
The Eyes can dramatically accelerate the passage of Time for a selected target or area.
Natalia eventually demonstrated this application on a planetary scale. She selected an immature mass of rock and accelerated its natural development by billions of years, transforming it into a fully developed planet in an extremely short external interval.[7]
Her Eyes became cosmic in appearance while she performed the feat, directly linking the high-level manipulation to her evolved Alioth ocular ability.[7]

#### Temporal Isolation
An advanced user can isolate a particular moment from the normal progression of Time.
Natalia used this principle while treating Alexios. Rather than simply reversing his entire history, she navigated through her father's personal Time, selected a period when he was healthy and isolated that temporal state.[6]

#### Past Travel
Natalia's version of the Eyes can access the Past directly.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] noted that high-level Time manipulation could theoretically involve traveling into the Past and altering an entire timeline, but the process would normally require an absurd quantity of resources and control even for him.[2]
Natalia's Eyes drastically simplify this process. According to Victor, she could essentially open a door into the Past rather than brute-forcing the same phenomenon with raw Divine power.[2]
This is one of the clearest demonstrations that the Eyes are more than an ordinary Time Divinity.

#### Temporal Retrieval
Natalia can isolate a past version of a Being and bring that state into the Present.
She demonstrated this while restoring Alexios. His body had deteriorated because centuries of manipulating Time had transformed his existence until he was essentially composed of Time Energy rather than ordinary flesh.[2]
Natalia entered her father's temporal history, found a point where he was healthy and brought that version of his condition into the Present.[6]
This was considerably more complex than ordinary rejuvenation.

#### Time Cocoon
Time Cocoon is one of Natalia's most advanced applications.
She placed Alexios within a blue temporal cocoon that caused his vitality and youthful appearance to gradually return.[6]
The technique did not simply heal him. Natalia isolated the healthy Alexios from his Past and attempted to establish that state as his valid Present condition.

#### Reality Warping Through Space-Time
Natalia's highest applications blur the distinction between conventional Time manipulation and Reality Warping.
Vlad correctly observed that simply turning Alexios backward in Time would not permanently solve the problem because Time naturally attempts to return to its proper flow. Natalia therefore went further: she used her connection to Space and Time to bring Alexios' healthy temporal state into the Present and bend Reality so that this state remained valid.[6]
The process effectively created a singular temporal condition in which the healthy version of Alexios could exist in a Present that should normally contain his deteriorated form.
Vlad considered the feat comparable to the work of [[Primordial Entities]], particularly because it interfered with normal Causality.[6]

#### Singularity Creation
The Alexios restoration effectively required the creation of a temporal singularity: a condition that should not naturally exist according to the ordinary flow of Time.
The healthy state of Alexios was extracted from one temporal point and stabilized in another.
This application required:
- extremely precise temporal perception;
- the ability to access the Past;
- isolation of a specific temporal state;
- enormous Energy;
- manipulation of Space and Time simultaneously;
- and sufficient Reality-altering force to stop normal Time from immediately correcting the anomaly.[6]

#### Space-Time Perception
The Eyes do not merely produce Space-Time phenomena; they also help their user perceive and control them.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] specifically noted that Natalia's Eyes assisted her in controlling her extraordinarily developed Space and Time abilities after her evolution into a Dragon Goddess.[2]
This becomes increasingly important as the scale of manipulation grows. Space and Time are interconnected systems, and altering either one on a massive scale can produce unpredictable consequences.

#### Space-Time Runes
[[Clan Alioth]] can encode elements of their Space-Time power through Runes.
Alexios used spatial Runes to create expanded spaces that could continue functioning without his constant presence so long as an appropriate Energy source was supplied.[1]
Natalia later advanced this field considerably and created sophisticated Space-Time Runes used throughout the Elderblood civilization.
The Runes are related applications of Alioth power rather than a separate function of the Eyes themselves.

### Users

#### Alexios Alioth
[[Alexios Alioth]] was the long-standing Clan Leader and the first directly demonstrated wielder of the Eyes.
His extraordinary mastery of Space and Time made him [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]]' principal dimensional specialist and one of the foundational figures supporting [[Nightingale]].
Alexios commonly kept his eyes closed. When genuine Time manipulation became necessary, however, he opened them and revealed the galaxy-like ocular structure beneath.[1]
His major demonstrated applications include:
- large-scale portal creation;
- dimensional travel;
- spatial expansion;
- spatial Runes;
- extended-space structures;
- Time manipulation;
- lifespan extension;
- large-scale temporal isolation;
- and maintaining dimensional restrictions.
However, Alexios' Human body prevented him from safely exploiting the full ability of the Eyes.

#### Natalia Elderblood née Alioth
Natalia inherited the Eyes from Alexios and eventually became their most powerful known user.
Even before receiving the Eyes, Natalia possessed the hereditary Space-Time [[Magic]] of [[Clan Alioth]] and was already an accomplished spatial specialist. She knew that, as Alexios' successor, the Eyes would eventually pass to her.[5]
Her later transformations completely changed the level at which the ability could operate.
After becoming a True Dragon and acquiring Divine authority over Space and Time, Natalia no longer possessed the primary limitation that had restrained Alexios: Mortality.[2]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly contrasted the two users. Alexios was restricted by his Human existence, while Natalia's Dragon physiology allowed her to draw vastly more potential from the Eyes.[2]
Even then, Natalia had not reached their full potential.[2]

### Alexios' Mortal Limitation
The Eyes themselves are not stated to inherently require lifespan as fuel.
This distinction is important.
Alexios' problem arose because he was a Mortal Human attempting to wield extremely high-level Time power.[2]
Members of [[Clan Alioth]] naturally possess unusually strong vitality and longer lives than ordinary Humans, but even they normally cannot pass approximately five hundred years.[1]
When the Clan successor inherits the Eyes, a ritual slows the Time of their body and extends their potential lifespan by approximately another five hundred years.[1]
However, Alexios could convert that accumulated Time back into power.
Every large-scale temporal technique therefore effectively consumed part of the lifespan he had gained.

### Natalia's Evolution
Natalia's development represents the first known case in which the Eyes were placed within a body capable of supporting their deeper potential.

#### Human Natalia
As a Human, Natalia inherited [[Clan Alioth]]'s exclusive Space-Time affinity and specialized heavily in spatial techniques.
She remained aware that the Eyes belonged to the succession of Clan leadership and expected Alexios to eventually transfer them to her.[5]

#### Dragon Goddess Natalia
After joining [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family and becoming a True Dragon, Natalia's relationship with Space and Time became vastly more powerful.
Her Draconic physiology granted her:
- vastly greater Energy reserves;
- a body capable of surviving extreme Space-Time manipulation;
- Divine authority over Space and Time;
- extraordinarily improved perception;
- greater conceptual understanding;
- and freedom from the normal Human lifespan restrictions that crippled Alexios.[2]
When she activates the Eyes in this state, her ordinary Dragon eyes disappear and are replaced by structures resembling entire moving cosmoses.[2][6]
Victor considered Natalia uniquely suited to Time-related problems. Even though he himself possessed a Time Divinity, his mastery and efficiency were significantly inferior to hers for highly specialized temporal work.[2]

#### Planetary Time Acceleration
Natalia eventually demonstrated the ability to manipulate billions of years of planetary history.
Upon finding an immature celestial body that was effectively little more than newly gathered rock, she activated the Eyes and accelerated the entire planet's development.
Billions of years passed for the target while the outside observer experienced only a brief period, producing a mature planet from the original formation.[7]
This became one of the largest confirmed practical demonstrations of the Eyes.

The Eyes and the Divine Concepts of Space and Time are related but not identical.
Alexios possessed the Eyes while remaining Human. Therefore, the Eyes clearly do not require the user to already be a God of Space or Time.[1]

### Relationship with Causality
High-level Time manipulation inevitably interacts with Causality.
Natalia's restoration of Alexios demonstrated this directly. Vlad argued that simply reversing Alexios would violate the natural movement of Time and that the timeline would eventually correct itself.[6]
Natalia's solution was not ordinary reversal. She isolated a valid historical version of Alexios and forced that past state to exist in the Present, while simultaneously bending Reality to maintain it.
The technique therefore did not merely move backward through Time; it imposed an abnormal causal state.
[[Scathach]] summarized the situation by questioning what the Laws of Causality meant to someone capable of bending Reality itself.[6]

### Origin
The true origin of the Eyes remains unknown.
[[Clan Alioth]] traditionally believed that its unusual abilities originated when the First Alioth interacted with [[Magic]]. Even beings familiar with the family assumed the result had simply been an extremely unusual mutation.[2]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later concluded that this explanation was almost certainly incomplete.
The Eyes displayed properties far beyond what he expected from an ordinary Human magical mutation. More importantly, Natalia—despite becoming a True Dragon and Dragon Goddess of Space and Time—still could not access their complete potential.[2]
Victor considered two broad possibilities:
- the Eyes may once have belonged to an extraordinarily powerful deceased God of Time, potentially approaching the level of a Primordial Being;
- or they may originate from an unknown existence connected to the Upper Sectors.[2]
These are only Victor's hypotheses.
Neither explanation has been confirmed.
Even Albedo, Alexios, Natalia and Victor lacked definitive knowledge concerning how the First Alioth acquired the power. [[Jeanne Elderblood]], despite her immense age, was likewise unable to provide an explanation.[2]

### Relationship with the First Alioth
The First Alioth appears to have been the beginning of the modern Clan's abnormal Space-Time lineage.
At some point in the past, Albedo became sufficiently interested in the family that she attempted to take an Alioth for herself in order to study the mutation. [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] intervened and protected the First Alioth from her.[2]
This indicates that the Clan's unusual characteristics were recognized as abnormal from near the beginning of its history.
Exactly how the First Alioth acquired the Eyes remains unrevealed.

### Limitations

#### Unknown Destinations
[[Clan Alioth]] cannot normally open a portal to a destination for which they possess no usable spatial information.
Precise information or coordinates are necessary to connect the two points.[5]
This is especially important for interplanetary and inter-Sector exploration because knowing that another civilization exists is not enough to instantly create a portal to its territory.

#### Energy Consumption
Large-scale Space-Time manipulation can require enormous quantities of Energy.
Even relatively mundane expanded-space structures need a continuous external Energy source if Alexios is not personally maintaining them.[1]
Natalia's later Dragon physiology and her connection to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] give her access to vastly greater reserves, substantially reducing this problem without making the underlying techniques literally costless.

#### Mortal Lifespan
Alexios' greatest restriction was his Humanity.
Large-scale use of Time directly consumed the temporal lifespan sustaining his body.[1]
This weakness is associated with Alexios' Mortal condition, not necessarily with the Eyes themselves.

#### Causal Consequences
Changing Time on a large scale can produce consequences that even extremely powerful Beings cannot easily predict.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] specifically avoided carelessly using Reality manipulation to repair Alexios because changing his past temporal condition could theoretically alter actions he had already performed.[2]
Time manipulation therefore requires far more than sufficient raw Energy.

#### Incomplete Mastery
Neither Alexios nor Natalia has demonstrated the complete potential of the Eyes.
Alexios was fundamentally limited by Mortality, while even Natalia—despite being a True Dragon and Goddess specializing in Space and Time—had not completely unlocked them.[2]

#### Spatial Knowledge Requirement
Spatial power does not provide automatic omniscience.
Even an extraordinarily powerful Alioth must know where the destination is before opening a conventional portal there.[5]

### Current Status
The Eyes are currently associated primarily with [[Natalia Elderblood née Alioth]], who inherited the Alioth lineage and pushed its powers far beyond the limitations demonstrated by her father.
Her current physiology combines:
- the hereditary Space-Time affinity of [[Clan Alioth]];
- the Eyes of the Clan Leader;
- True Dragon physiology;
- extremely large Energy reserves;
- Divine authority over Space;
- Divine authority over Time;
- and thousands of years of specialized training.
This combination has made Natalia the foremost known specialist in Time and Space within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s immediate civilization, particularly when problems require precision rather than merely overwhelming Energy.[2]
Even so, the Eyes remain incompletely understood.
Their maximum capabilities, original owner and true method of entering the Alioth bloodline remain unresolved.

### Trivia

- Natalia preferred the simple description "Eyes of the Alioth Clan Leader" and had no desire to announce the name of her technique to opponents.[3]

- Natalia's evolved version resembles an ever-changing cosmos rather than ordinary Dragon eyes.[2]

- The successor ritual can extend an Alioth Clan Leader's lifespan by slowing the personal passage of Time, but using large-scale Time abilities can consume that extension.[1]
- Alexios' repeated attempts to escape Mortal death ultimately transformed his body until it consisted largely of Time Energy.[2]
- Natalia can exploit the Eyes far more effectively because she is no longer restricted by a Mortal Human body.[2]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] believed Natalia could access the Past through the Eyes much more efficiently than he could reproduce the same result through brute-force Divine manipulation.[2]
- Even Natalia had not unlocked the complete potential of the Eyes after becoming a True Dragon and Dragon Goddess.[2]
- Victor suspected that the Eyes may have originated from a deceased high-level Time God or an Upper-Sector existence, but the novel explicitly leaves this as speculation rather than confirmed history.[2]
- The original explanation that [[Clan Alioth]]'s power was merely an unusual mutation produced through contact with [[Magic]] was eventually considered insufficient.[2]
- The Eyes are among the few known hereditary Human abilities whose later development allowed their user to perform phenomena comparable to the work of Primordial Beings.[6]
- Natalia used their evolved power to accelerate billions of years of natural planetary formation.[7]
