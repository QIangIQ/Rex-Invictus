### Ownership

#### Owner
[[Hela Elderblood]]
The Staff of Death and End is a powerful Divine and Conceptual artifact wielded by Hela Elderblood, the Goddess of Death and former Ruler of Helheim. The weapon acts as Hela's primary medium for channeling the power of END, an Energy and Concept that she was not naturally born possessing. Unlike natural END Beings such as [[Fenrir]], [[Typhon]] and Jormungand, whose bodies are intrinsically saturated with END, Hela externally channels the Energy through her staff, allowing her to manipulate it with substantially greater flexibility.[1][2]
Although Hela herself possesses exceptionally powerful authority over Death, the most unusual component of the Staff is its connection to END. The artifact was created using the fangs of one of Hela's brothers who possessed END, allowing the otherwise non-END Goddess to wield that Energy without making END an intrinsic component of her own body.[1]

### Description
The Staff of Death and End is Hela's personal staff and one of the principal supernatural artifacts associated with her. Its complete physical design has not been extensively described in the novel, although Hela can summon and wield it directly when preparing to use its powers.[1]
The artifact is fundamentally different from an ordinary Divine Weapon. It contains or channels the Energy of END through material originating from an actual END Being, allowing Hela to interact with a power that would normally be extremely dangerous for beings lacking a connection to BEGIN or resistance to END.[1]

### History

#### Creation
The exact circumstances surrounding the Staff's original construction have not yet been fully explained. By the time [[Victor Elderblood]] personally met Hela in Helheim, the artifact was already in her possession.[1]
After inspecting it, Victor recognized that the Staff incorporated the fangs of one of Hela's brothers. These materials allowed the artifact to contain or channel END Energy despite Hela herself not being an END Goddess.[1]
Victor was initially interested in how Hela could safely touch and wield such an object. He determined that the answer was connected to Hela's mother, [[Angrboða]]. Having carried and given birth to children who naturally emitted END Energy, Angrboða had been continually exposed to small quantities of END. Hela, having developed in the same womb as her END siblings, consequently inherited or developed a limited resistance to the Energy.[1]
This resistance does not make Hela an END Being. Instead, it merely allows her to survive contact with and make use of END through an external medium such as the Staff.[1][2]

#### Hela's Use
The Staff remained Hela's primary method of accessing END even after her later development and entry into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction. This distinction became especially important when Hela explained the difference between her own method of using END and that of natural END Beings.[2]
[[Fenrir]], [[Typhon]] and Jormungand channel END directly through their bodies. Their claws, fangs, skin and other physical characteristics are intrinsically imbued with its Energy. As embodiments or natural users of END, this state also prevents them from properly initiating Life and fundamentally affects their biological existence.[2]
Hela avoids this problem because END is not naturally integrated into her body. Instead, she channels it through the Staff, effectively using the weapon as an intermediary between herself and the Energy.[2]
This external method gives Hela considerably more freedom in how END is applied. Rather than merely using END to destroy or terminate whatever it touches, she can manipulate the Energy in a more technical and creative fashion.[2]

#### Accessing the Domain of End
One of the Staff's most significant demonstrated applications occurred when Hela explained the region occupied by the [[Creatures of the Abyss]]. These creatures inhabited the remains of galaxies that had reached their genuine End, regions where conventional Life, Time, Space and even the Concept of Void no longer existed.[2]
Because these dead regions fall under the influence or supervision of [[True Death]] and exist in a condition related to END, Hela was capable of accessing them through the Staff.[2]
Hela pointed the weapon forward and emitted dark Energy from it. The Energy struck the area in front of her and produced a hole in Reality, creating an opening through which an Abyssal creature could interact with their location.[2]
The feat demonstrated that the Staff's END power is not restricted to direct destruction. It can manipulate the boundaries separating ordinary Creation from locations associated with END and establish passages into otherwise extraordinarily inaccessible regions.[2]

### Powers and Abilities

#### END Energy Channeling
The Staff's principal special property is its ability to serve as a medium through which Hela can channel END Energy.[1][2]
Hela does not generate END naturally in the same manner as her END siblings. The artifact instead allows her to draw upon and direct the Energy without permanently saturating her body with it.[2]

#### External END Conduit
Unlike natural END Beings, Hela channels END externally through the Staff. This arrangement allows her to maintain her ordinary existence while using a power whose natural wielders are fundamentally altered by their connection to the End of Everything.[2]
This distinction is one of the Staff's most important properties because Hela's versatility with END specifically originates from the fact that the Energy is being manipulated through a tool rather than through an END-transformed body.[2]

#### Creative END Manipulation
Hela can manipulate END through the Staff in ways that natural END Beings generally do not. Rather than relying only upon claws, fangs or physical attacks imbued with END, she can treat END as an Energy source and shape it into different supernatural applications.[2]
Her handling of the Energy was compared to the manner in which [[True Death]] can manipulate such forces rather than merely allowing them to indiscriminately terminate whatever they contact.[2]

#### Reality Breaching
The Staff can project END Energy against Reality itself and create openings through otherwise inaccessible boundaries.[2]
Hela demonstrated this by directing dark Energy from the Staff into the space before her. The affected portion of Reality opened into a hole connected to a region occupied by Abyssal creatures.[2]

#### Access to Regions of END
Through her connection to END and the Staff's ability to channel it, Hela can locate and access regions associated with the genuine End of cosmic structures.[2]
This includes areas remaining after galaxies have reached their final End, where Life, Time, Space and Void are absent.[2]

#### Existence Termination
END is fundamentally associated with bringing phenomena to their final conclusion. Consequently, offensive applications of the Staff can apply the destructive characteristics of END rather than relying upon conventional physical damage.[1][2]
This property should be distinguished from an independently established ability of the Staff to erase absolutely anything. The effectiveness of END is dependent upon the hierarchy and nature of the target, and beings or powers appropriately resistant to END can interact with it without immediately ceasing to exist.[1]

#### Death Authority
Hela possesses exceptionally powerful authority over Death, and the Staff functions as her principal magical weapon while exercising her Death- and END-related abilities. However, Hela's authority over Death is primarily an inherent part of her own Divinity rather than something originally granted to her by the Staff.[1]
For this reason, Death Manipulation performed while wielding the Staff should not automatically be treated as an independent property of the artifact unless a chapter specifically establishes the Staff itself as the source of that particular application.

### Limitations
- END Resistance Required: Hela can safely wield the artifact because she possesses resistance to END resulting from her unusual birth circumstances. An ordinary being should not be assumed capable of safely wielding the Staff.[1]
- Hela is not an END Being: The Staff gives Hela access to END, but it does not transform her into the same kind of existence as [[Fenrir]], [[Typhon]] or Jormungand.[2]
- Not absolute: END possesses an extraordinarily high metaphysical hierarchy, but resistance and opposing powers capable of interacting with END exist. The Staff should therefore not be described as automatically erasing every possible target regardless of circumstance.

### Trivia
- The artifact is one of the reasons Hela can be considered unusually versatile compared with natural END Beings; she uses END as a manipulable Energy rather than having it permanently incorporated into her physiology.[2]
- Hela's ability to handle the Staff is tied to the unusual circumstances of [[Angrboða]] giving birth to multiple children connected to END.[1]
- The exact brother whose fang or fangs were used to construct the Staff is not explicitly identified by name in the relevant Chapter 942 explanation. It should therefore not be definitively attributed to a particular sibling without additional confirmation.[1]
- Hela's innate powers over Death and Time should not automatically be listed as properties bestowed by the Staff. The artifact's clearest unique contribution is her access to END.
