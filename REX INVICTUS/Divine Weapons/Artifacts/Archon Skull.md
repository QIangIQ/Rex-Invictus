**[[Archon Skull]]** is a figure of Rex Invictus lore. (Lore adapted from *The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** Flamel material is adapted into the Rex Invictus present (c. 2021). Rex Invictus wins any conflict; [[Robespierre]] fills [[Dr. John Dee]]'s role and hunts the Philosopher's Stone rather than [[The Codex]]. See [[Flamel Series#Integration rules]].

> Archon skulls are powerful magical artifacts which can use auric power to see into the minds of others. In [[The Necromancer]], an Archon skull was used by Nicholas and Perenelle Flamel to spy on [[Josh Newman]] while he was being controlled in his sleep by [[Mars Ultor]].

### Trivia
- One of the only ways to destroy an Archon skull is to throw it in an active volcano.
- An Archon skull in the [[Nameless City]] during one of [[Sophie Newman|Sophie]]'s flashbacks in [[Zephaniah]]'s memories.
- Zephaniah states that [[Elders]] are forbidden to touch an Archon skull.
