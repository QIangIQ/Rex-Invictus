### Origin & Nature
Forged by [[Hephaestus]] as an act of vengeance against [[Aphrodite]] after she rejected their forced marriage. The chains were designed to humiliate her by exposing her affair with [[Ares]], trapping both gods in a compromised state. Aphrodite later claimed ownership ("If he made it for me, then it’s mine"), repurposing it as a defensive weapon. Its power stems from Hephaestus’s divine craftsmanship and the conceptual weight of "exposing divine hypocrisy."[1][2]

### Physical Manifestation
- Appears as glowing golden chains observed when materialized in [[Aphrodite]]’s hand).
- Bonds soul-deep when activated ([[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] feels it "inside him" after Aphrodite’s kiss).
- Leaves no visible trace when dormant ([[Zeus]] detects it only through divine sense).

#### Powers & Mechanics
1. God-King Level Nullification: Severely weakens deities to the level of an "Adult Vampire". This would reduce [[Zeus]], God-King—to near-powerlessness.
2. Soul-Bound Activation: Functions as an extension of the wielder’s will. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] can deploy it mentally against hostile gods ("If one of the Gods attacks you, use my chain").
3. Divine Authority Bypass: Ignores conventional divine resistances. Only beings with absolute-level Divinities (e.g., [[Shiva]]’s Destruction) are immune.

#### Limitations
- Ineffective Against Primal Concepts: Useless against [[Shiva]]’s Destruction Divinity.
- Wielder-Dependent Vulnerability: If [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is overpowered before activation, the chains cannot be deployed.
