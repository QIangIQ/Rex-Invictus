Taranis, the Daggers of the Ancestral Spirit, commonly shortened to Taranis, are a pair of Divine Runic daggers and the principal ancestral treasure of the Fulger Clan. Originally belonging to the Clan's founder and first matriarch, [[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]], the weapons were inherited through the [[Fulger Bloodline]] and eventually passed through [[Annasthashia Elderblood Fulger]] to Sasha Elderblood Fulger.[1][2]
The daggers were forged by supernatural Dwarven craftsmen associated with the Norse Gods, with Annasthashia specifically stating that they were created by the same Dwarves responsible for forging Mjolnir. Their Runic enchantments recognize the Fulger Bloodline, reject unauthorized wielders with their own Lightning, and assist legitimate wielders in controlling the Fulger Clan's Lightning abilities.[1][3][2]

### Description
[[Taranis]] consists of two golden daggers engraved with numerous small Runes. The Runes themselves contain considerable supernatural power, making the weapons visibly distinct from ordinary daggers even before their Lightning-related enchantments are activated.[4]
The pair function together as a single Clan treasure and are generally referred to collectively as Taranis, the Daggers of the Ancestral Spirit.[2]

### History

#### Carmila Fulger
[[Taranis]] originally belonged to [[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]], the first matriarch and founder of the [[Fulger Clan]]. The daggers subsequently became one of the Clan's most important inheritances and were passed through its leadership after Carmila's era.[1][2]
According to Annasthashia, Carmila was almost never separated from Taranis during her lifetime. Throughout Annasthashia's childhood, wherever Carmila went, the two daggers accompanied her. The weapons were particularly useful because they helped Carmila control her extraordinary Lightning abilities.[2]
The exact circumstances under which Carmila originally obtained the weapons have not been fully described. What is established is that they were created by supernatural Dwarven craftsmen associated with Norse mythology. Annasthashia later identified their creators as the same Dwarves responsible for the creation of [[Thor]]'s Divine hammer, Mjolnir.[1][2]

#### Annasthashia Fulger
Following Carmila's death, the Clan treasure came into the possession of her daughter, [[Annasthashia Elderblood Fulger]]. During Annasthashia's attempt to reclaim the position of Vampire Count from [[Niklaus Horseman]], she declared that she would place her Clan's Divine Treasure as her stake in the match. She described the treasure as having been created by the Dwarves of Norse mythology and acknowledged that it had originally belonged to her mother, although as Carmila's heir she had inherited ownership of it.[1]
The daggers were subsequently seen in [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]]'s possession. While exploring an ancient underground location with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Kaguya, Sasha noticed similarities between the Runes carved upon a trap and those engraved into [[Taranis]]. When Victor attempted to take the daggers from Sasha, Taranis independently released golden Lightning and rejected him before he could properly wield them.[3]
This incident demonstrated that merely possessing the [[Fulger Clan]]'s Lightning ability is insufficient to satisfy Taranis' enchantment. Victor could manipulate the same Lightning characteristic after his Blood absorbed properties from Sasha's Blood, but he was not a genuine descendant of the Fulger Clan. Taranis therefore continued to identify him as an outsider and refused him.[3]

#### Sasha Fulger
Annasthashia later formally introduced Sasha to the history and name of the weapons during her daughter's training. She summoned the two golden daggers, explained that Carmila had carried them everywhere and revealed that they helped their wielder control Lightning.[2]
Annasthashia then revealed their complete name as [[Taranis]], the Daggers of the Ancestral Spirit and handed them to Sasha. The blades visibly emitted their own Lightning, but this Lightning did not harm Annasthashia. After explaining their Dwarven origin and connection to the makers of Mjolnir, Annasthashia ordered Sasha to begin training with Taranis from that day onward.[2]
Taranis therefore became part of Sasha's training as the heiress of Clan Fulger rather than remaining solely a ceremonial object belonging to the current Clan Head.[2]

#### Clan Treasure
[[Taranis]] continued to be recognized as one of [[Nightingale]]'s principal Clan treasures. During the gathering of the Countesses, Annasthashia carried the two golden daggers at her waist. Their Runic engravings were described as containing great power, and the weapons were identified as the treasure left behind by Carmila for the [[Fulger Clan]].[4]
At the same gathering, [[Agnes Elderblood Snow]] carried the [[Blade of Fafnir]] and [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] carried [[Gae Bolg]], establishing Taranis alongside them as one of the ancestral weapons associated with the three original Countess families.[4]

### Powers and Abilities

#### Fulger Bloodline Recognition
[[Taranis]] possesses an enchantment that distinguishes legitimate members of the [[Fulger Clan]] from outsiders. The requirement is based upon the wielder's actual [[Fulger Bloodline]] rather than simply possession of the Clan's Lightning ability.[3]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] demonstrated this distinction directly. Although he had absorbed the characteristics necessary to use Fulger Lightning, Taranis did not recognize him as a Fulger descendant and rejected his attempt to take the weapons.[3]

#### Automatic Rejection
When an unauthorized individual attempts to touch or wield [[Taranis]], the daggers can independently generate golden Lightning to repel them. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] triggered this defense merely by reaching toward the weapons.[3]
The response appears to be automatic and tied to the Runic Bloodline enchantment rather than requiring the current wielder to consciously command the daggers.[3]

#### Lightning Generation
[[Taranis]] can emit its own Lightning. When Annasthashia revealed the weapons' name to Sasha, the blades discharged Lightning without harming Annasthashia, consistent with their recognition of [[Fulger Bloodline]] members.[2]

#### Lightning Control
One of [[Taranis]]' principal functions is improving a legitimate user's control over the [[Fulger Clan]]'s Lightning abilities. Annasthashia specifically explained that the daggers helped Carmila control her powers and later placed Sasha in training with the weapons for the same reason.[2]
Because control rather than simple destructive amplification is the only explicitly established enhancement, Taranis should not presently be described as multiplying a Fulger's Lightning output unless a later chapter directly establishes such an effect.

#### Runic Enchantments
Both daggers are covered in Runes carrying significant supernatural power.[4]
The best demonstrated application of these Runes is their Bloodline-recognition enchantment, which allows the weapons to determine whether a person belongs to the Fulger family and activate their Lightning rejection against unauthorized individuals.[3]
The identities and functions of every Rune engraved upon [[Taranis]] have not yet been individually explained.

### Wielders
- [[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]] — Original known owner. Carmila carried the weapons almost everywhere and used them to assist in controlling her Lightning.[2]
- [[Annasthashia Elderblood Fulger]] — Inherited [[Taranis]] from Carmila and possessed the weapons as Clan Fulger's leader.[1][4]
- [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] — Received the daggers from Annasthashia as part of her training as the Clan's heiress.[2]

### Limitations
- Bloodline Requirement: [[Taranis]] refuses individuals who do not possess the legitimate [[Fulger Bloodline]], even if they can reproduce the Clan's Lightning ability.[3]
- Known functions are limited: Although the daggers contain powerful Runes and possess Divine-level craftsmanship, the story has not identified every individual Rune or demonstrated a large catalog of offensive abilities. Their best-established functions are Bloodline recognition, automatic Lightning rejection and assisting with Lightning control.[3][2][4]

### Trivia
- [[Taranis]] was forged by the same group of Dwarven craftsmen that Annasthashia identifies as the makers of Mjolnir.[2]
- This shared origin does not establish that Taranis possesses Mjolnir's enchantments or abilities. No such equivalence has been demonstrated.
- Despite the title Daggers of the Ancestral Spirit, the novel has not established that Carmila's Soul resides inside the weapons. Carmila's surviving spiritual existence is separately associated with Annasthashia, so the title should not by itself be interpreted as proof that Taranis contains an ancestral Soul.
- The weapons share the name Taranis with Taranis, the Celtic God introduced much later in the story. No canonical relationship between the Celtic God and the [[Fulger Clan]]'s daggers has currently been established.
- Taranis is one of the principal ancestral treasures of [[Nightingale]]'s Countess families alongside the [[Snow Clan]]'s [[Blade of Fafnir]] and the [[Scarlett Clan]]'s [[Gae Bolg]].[4]
- Mythological characteristics associated with the real-world name Taranis should not automatically be attributed to the daggers unless the novel establishes them.
