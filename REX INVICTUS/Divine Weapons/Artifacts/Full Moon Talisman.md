The Full Moon Talisman is a consumable magical artifact created and commercially distributed by Witches for Werewolves. Its primary function is to reproduce the supernatural effect of a Full Moon even when the natural Moon is not in the required phase, allowing affected Werewolves to access their complete transformations and the corresponding increase in power.[1][2]
Unlike an ordinary personal enhancement item, the talisman produces an environmental effect. When activated, a light rises into the sky and forms a gigantic white magical circle bearing the symbol of the Moon. Werewolves within the affected area respond as though they were being exposed to an actual Full Moon, allowing multiple individuals to transform simultaneously.[1]
The talismans are especially important to Werewolves who cannot naturally access their complete transformations at will. [[Volk Fenrir]] and his children, as powerful Alphas, were stated not to require them, whereas other Werewolves—particularly Betas—depend upon the talismans to reproduce the conditions necessary for their full transformations. This dependency made the artifacts one of the most strategically and economically important products sold by Witch society to the Werewolves.[2]

### Description
A Full Moon Talisman is a relatively small, round magical object bearing the symbol of a wolf.[1]
The artifact is small enough to be carried in one hand and transported as ordinary personal equipment.
Despite its unimposing appearance, activating it produces a magical phenomenon on a considerably larger scale.
When the talisman demonstrated in Chapter 179 was destroyed, a small light emerged from the broken artifact and ascended into the sky. The light then expanded into an enormous white magical circle containing a Moon symbol.[1]
The manifested phenomenon was powerful enough to immediately affect the [[Werewolves]] throughout the battlefield.

### Nature
The Full Moon Talisman does not appear to directly transform a Werewolf through physical contact.
Instead, it artificially reproduces the external supernatural condition created by a natural Full Moon.
This distinction is important.
The artifact essentially changes the local environment into one capable of triggering the same racial response that [[Werewolves]] ordinarily experience beneath the appropriate Moon.

### Creation
Full Moon Talismans are created by Witches.[2]
They form part of the larger supernatural artifact industry maintained by Witch society.
Witches regularly create and sell:
- Talismans;
- contracts;
- magical tools;
- supernatural services;
- dimensional artifacts;
- and other specialized products
to races lacking the knowledge or resources necessary to reproduce those effects themselves.
The Full Moon Talisman is particularly valuable because it reproduces an environmental condition directly connected to Werewolf racial biology.
The identity of the Witch who originally invented the talisman has not been revealed.
Likewise, the exact:
- materials;
- spells;
- Runes;
- Mana requirements;
- construction time;
- and manufacturing process
remain unknown.

### Activation
The demonstrated Full Moon Talisman is a single-use consumable.
To activate it, the Werewolf Elder in Chapter 179:
1. raised the talisman;
2. shattered it;
3. and released its magical effect into the air.[1]
A small light immediately emerged from the broken object and flew upward.
The light then formed an enormous magical circle in the sky.
The physical talisman itself was therefore consumed during activation.

#### Single Use
The Elder specifically referred to the artifact as his last talisman and was reluctant to use it because doing so would permanently consume the item.[1]
This indicates that conventional Full Moon Talismans are not reusable artifacts.
Once activated, another talisman is required for a later artificial Full Moon.

### Artificial Full Moon
The primary ability of the talisman is the creation of a false Full Moon condition.[2]
Chapter 539 directly describes the artifacts as vital tools capable of bringing about a false Full Moon and thereby allowing [[Werewolves]] to access their complete transformations.[2]
The artifact does not physically create another Moon.
Instead, its [[Magic]] reproduces the supernatural influence necessary to trigger the Werewolf racial response.
During its first demonstrated use, the sky displayed a gigantic white magical circle rather than a literal celestial body.[1]
The Werewolves nevertheless responded as though appropriate lunar conditions were present.

### Effects on Werewolves

#### Complete Transformation
The most important function of the Full Moon Talisman is enabling [[Werewolves]] to access their complete transformation.[2]
Before the talisman was activated in Chapter 179, several of the Werewolves possessed only partial transformations.
After the magical circle appeared:
- their bodies increased in size;
- their heads became more wolf-like;
- tails appeared;
- their eyes glowed golden;
- and the group transformed into enormous wolf-like humanoids exceeding roughly 2.5 meters in height.[1]
The transformation occurred across the surviving Werewolf group rather than only affecting the Elder who activated the artifact.

#### Racial Empowerment
The transformation created by the talisman is not merely cosmetic.
The Full Moon is one of the principal environmental factors empowering Werewolf physiology, and the talisman reproduces the condition necessary for weaker [[Werewolves]] to access their more powerful state.[2]
It therefore indirectly improves:
- physical strength;
- speed;
- durability;
- combat capability;
- and other characteristics associated with the Werewolf's complete transformation.
The talisman itself should not be described as granting entirely new supernatural abilities.
Its purpose is to unlock capabilities already inherent within Werewolf physiology.

### Area of Effect
A single Full Moon Talisman can affect multiple [[Werewolves]] simultaneously.
Chapter 179 demonstrates one Elder activating a single talisman and causing all surviving Werewolves in the surrounding battlefield to begin transforming.[1]
This establishes that its [[Magic]] operates across an area rather than exclusively affecting the individual who physically activates it.
The exact maximum range has never been established.
It is therefore unknown whether a single talisman could affect:
- an entire city;
- an army spread across several kilometers;
- Werewolves behind dimensional barriers;
- or individuals outside the manifested magical circle's effective region.
Only its demonstrated battlefield-scale group effect is currently confirmed.

### Relationship with the Natural Full Moon
The Full Moon Talisman exists because many [[Werewolves]] cannot freely reproduce the benefits provided by a natural Full Moon.
When the Elder used the artifact in Chapter 179, he first checked the Moon and recognized that:

He consequently resorted to the talisman.
This demonstrates that the artifact functions as a substitute when natural lunar conditions are unavailable.

### Alpha Werewolves
Not every Werewolf requires a Full Moon Talisman.
Chapter 539 specifically states that [[Volk Fenrir]] and his children did not require the artifact.[2]
They were powerful Alphas capable of accessing their transformations without depending upon an artificial Full Moon.
This demonstrates that reliance upon the talisman depends upon the Werewolf's:
- rank;
- Bloodline;
- natural power;
- and degree of control over their racial transformation.
The Full Moon Talisman should therefore not be described as a universal requirement for all [[Werewolves]].

### Beta Werewolves
The artifacts are particularly important for Beta [[Werewolves]].
Chapter 539 explicitly identifies Betas among those who require Full Moon Talismans in order to reproduce the lunar conditions needed for complete transformation.[2]
This makes the artifact especially important to the majority of Werewolf military forces that do not possess the natural advantages of powerful Alphas.
For such Werewolves, possessing a talisman can represent the difference between fighting in:
- a partial transformation;
- and their complete combat form.

### Use in Warfare
The Full Moon Talisman has considerable military value.
[[Werewolves]] who would normally require specific lunar conditions can carry a talisman and activate their complete transformations whenever necessary.
This allows Werewolf commanders to:
- fight during unfavorable lunar phases;
- launch attacks without waiting for a natural Full Moon;
- empower groups of subordinate Werewolves;
- respond to unexpected supernatural threats;
- and temporarily remove one of their race's environmental limitations.
The Elder in Chapter 179 used his final talisman precisely because his Werewolf group was being overwhelmed by [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]] and [[Bruna Elderblood]].[1]
Although he had wanted to preserve the artifact, the possibility of losing his forces made using it worthwhile.

### Economic Importance
Full Moon Talismans are not merely battlefield items.
They form part of the long-standing commercial relationship between Witches and [[Werewolves]].
Chapter 539 describes the artifacts as vital tools regularly purchased from Witch society.[2]
[[Volk Fenrir]] frequently conducted business with the Witches specifically regarding Full Moon Talismans even though he personally did not require one.
This was because large portions of his people still depended upon them.
The artifact therefore became a strategically important imported resource for Werewolf society.

### Witch Monopoly
The production of Full Moon Talismans gave the Witches considerable economic leverage over the [[Werewolves]].
During a confrontation between Volk and Evie, Evie threatened to increase the base price of Full Moon Talismans sold to Werewolves to:
Three times their previous price.
[2]
When Volk objected that she could not do so, Evie responded that she could because of the Witches':
Monopoly.
[2]
This exchange establishes that the Werewolves lacked an equivalent independent source capable of supplying the same product on comparable terms.
The Witches could therefore use access to Full Moon Talismans as political and economic leverage.

### Trade with Samar
The talisman trade forms part of the broader economic relationship between Witch and Werewolf society.
Because Alpha [[Werewolves]] do not necessarily require the product, its greatest strategic importance lies in supporting the wider Werewolf population and military rather than their strongest leaders personally.
Volk's regular purchases demonstrate that maintaining a reliable supply was important enough to require direct interaction between the highest levels of Werewolf and Witch leadership.[2]
The exact:
- normal price;
- currency;
- quantity purchased;
- production cost;
- and annual trade volume
have never been revealed.
Consequently, the `buy` and `sell` values of the artifact should not use invented numerical prices.

### History

#### Early Demonstration
The first major demonstration of a Full Moon Talisman occurred during the conflict involving [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]] and [[Bruna Elderblood]] against a group of [[Werewolves]].
Kaguya had already killed or incapacitated numerous members of the enemy force when an Elder entered the battle.[1]
The Elder carried:
- a gigantic hammer;
- and a round talisman marked with the symbol of a wolf.
He observed that the night did not possess a natural Full Moon.
Although reluctant to consume his final talisman, he decided that preserving it was less important than preventing the destruction of his forces.[1]
The Elder shattered the object.
A light emerged from it, flew into the sky and transformed into a gigantic white lunar [[Magic]] Circle.
The surrounding Werewolves immediately reacted.
Individuals previously displaying incomplete or partial transformations rapidly developed into their full wolf-like combat forms.[1]
The scene established both the talisman's single-use nature and its ability to affect multiple Werewolves simultaneously.

#### Witch–Werewolf Trade
Much later, the larger significance of the talisman was revealed during a political discussion involving the leaders of the major supernatural races.
The artifact was described as a vital tool that Witches sold to [[Werewolves]] because it reproduced a false Full Moon.[2]
The discussion revealed that:
- powerful Alphas such as Volk and his children did not require it;
- other Werewolves did;
- Betas were particularly dependent upon it;
- Volk regularly purchased the product;
- and the Witches possessed a monopoly over its supply.[2]
The artifact therefore evolved from appearing to be specialized combat equipment into an important component of supernatural international trade.

### Strategic Importance
The Full Moon Talisman compensates for one of the fundamental limitations affecting many [[Werewolves]]:
Dependence upon lunar conditions.
Rather than restructuring the Werewolf's Bloodline, the artifact allows Witch [[Magic]] to artificially create the circumstances necessary for the Bloodline to express its stronger form.
This makes it valuable for:
- military deployments;
- emergency battles;
- covert operations;
- campaigns scheduled outside Full Moon periods;
- defending Werewolf settlements;
- and empowering large numbers of non-Alpha combatants.
Its importance is amplified by the fact that the Witches possess control over its production.
The Talismans therefore have simultaneous:
- military;
- economic;
- magical;
- political;
- and racial importance.

### Limitations

#### Single Use
The demonstrated talisman is destroyed during activation.[1]
It must therefore be replaced after use.

#### Supply Dependency
[[Werewolves]] rely upon Witches to obtain the artifacts.[2]
This leaves their supply vulnerable to:
- price increases;
- political disputes;
- interrupted trade;
- Witch refusal;
- or shortages.

#### Not Required by Powerful Alphas
The artifact provides little necessity to [[Werewolves]] already capable of accessing their complete transformation without lunar assistance.
Volk and his children are explicitly given as examples.[2]

#### Does Not Create Werewolf Power
The talisman does not transform an ordinary Human or unrelated supernatural Being into a Werewolf.
Its demonstrated function activates or enhances capabilities already inherent within Werewolf physiology.

#### Range Unknown
Although one talisman can affect multiple nearby [[Werewolves]], its maximum effective area has not been stated.

#### Duration Unknown
The story has not established exactly how long the false Full Moon effect remains active after the talisman is broken.

#### Manufacturing Process Unknown
The exact Witchcraft used to reproduce the Moon's effect has never been revealed.
It should therefore not be assigned specific:
- Runes;
- spells;
- Mana costs;
- lunar materials;
- or ritual ingredients
without additional evidence.

### Relationship with Witch Society
The Full Moon Talisman is one of the clearest examples of how Witch society maintains influence despite lacking the raw racial military power of factions such as Vampires and [[Werewolves]].
Rather than depending exclusively upon direct combat strength, Witches produce services and artifacts other supernatural races need.
The talisman grants Witches leverage because they control access to an artificial version of a condition directly important to Werewolf combat capability.
Alongside:
- [[Witch Contracts]];
- dimensional artifacts;
- magical services;
- communication tools;
- and other Talismans,
the Full Moon Talisman demonstrates the economic power created by Witch specialization.
