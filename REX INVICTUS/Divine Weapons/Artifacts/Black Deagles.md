### Overview
[[Nero]]'s Deagle is one of the more distinctive modern-style weapons in the series, reflecting Nero's preference for straightforward, lethal ranged combat. During the events in Japan, Nero used her Deagles to fend off supernatural pursuers while escorting Ophis, repeatedly relying on precise gunfire to kill enemies and keep their route clear.[1] After the original pair was destroyed, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] arranged for them to be rebuilt as a present for Nero, preserving their familiar form while improving them through the use of monster materials.[2]

### Appearance
The restored weapons are described as a pair of pure white twin Deagles.[2] They are a matched set meant to be used together, fitting [[Nero]]'s dual-wielding combat style already seen during the Japan arc, where she carried and fired a Deagle in each hand.[1]

### History

#### Original Deagles
[[Nero]] was first shown carrying twin Deagles during the seven-day escape through Tokyo alongside Ophis. While various groups of supernatural beings pursued them, Nero used the weapons as her main method of offense, shooting enemies in the head and fighting while constantly retreating to keep Ophis safe.[1] The chapter establishes the Deagles as Nero's signature weapons long before she was formally gifted the reconstructed pair by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[1]

#### Destruction and Reconstruction
By the time [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later returned to the [[Snow Clan]] mansion, [[Nero]] believed her Deagles had been destroyed in the fight with the yōkai.[2] Victor revealed that he had recovered the broken pieces and asked an acquaintance from Eleonor's fire squad, a weapons expert, to remake them using monster materials.[2] He then presented the rebuilt pair to Nero as a gift, causing her to react emotionally upon seeing the familiar weapons restored.[2]

#### Use Under Victor's Guidance
After giving [[Nero]] the remade Deagles, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] immediately began instructing her on how to use them properly. He told her to focus her energy as if turning her hands into claws, then transfer that energy into the Deagle instead. When Nero followed his instructions and pulled the trigger, the weapon released a beam of energy, demonstrating that the reconstructed Deagles could act as conduits for supernatural power in addition to functioning as firearms.[2] Victor also used the moment to teach Nero that she should not rely entirely on weapons and should continue developing her martial arts so she could still fight even if disarmed.[2]

#### Later Comparison
Much later, when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] gifted customized weapons to the Valkyries, Judy received two black Deagles. [[Nero]] immediately noticed that their power was similar to her own weapon, and Victor confirmed that they were similar in concept even if tailored differently.[3] This reinforces Nero's Deagles as part of the line of special weapons Victor had begun arranging or helping create for people close to him.[3]

### Abilities
- Dual-wield firearm combat: [[Nero]] uses the weapons as a matched pair, allowing her to fire from both hands and engage multiple enemies while moving.[1]
- High lethality: During the Japan incident, Nero repeatedly killed pursuers with precise headshots, showing that the Deagles were highly effective in actual combat.[1]
- Monster-material construction: After the originals were broken, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had them remade with monster materials, improving them beyond the initial pair.[2]
- Energy channeling: The restored Deagles can channel Nero's energy. Under Victor's instruction, one of the Deagles emitted an energy beam when Nero infused it with power and pulled the trigger.[2]
- Suited to Nero's fighting style: Nero immediately recognized later custom Deagles gifted by Victor as being similar to her own weapon, indicating that her Deagles belong to a distinct weapon type or combat style associated with her.[3]
