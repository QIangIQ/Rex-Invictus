> Virginia Dare's Flute is one of the Artifacts of Power. It has been mentioned as the technology of a race predating the [[Elders]], at least according to [[Osiris]], who speculated it was either Archon or Earthlord in origin.[citation needed] As a result, Elders are not able to touch the flute or artifacts like it. Seeing as Osiris himself was actually an Earthlord, the Flute may more likely be of Archon Origin. The flute is bound to Virginia. As mentioned in [[The Enchantress]], she found it in a cave that is situated in the Grand Canyon.[citation needed] The flute was put there by [[Marethyu]] for Virginia to find.

### Abilities
With her flute Virginia is able to create sounds that can:
- Put people and creatures to sleep
- Awake people and creatures from a somnus spell
- Play a note so high that it can cause anyone who hears it to pass out
- Create severe pain
- Mesmerize anyone who hears it to obey the player of the flute
- Kill[citation needed]
- Flute is unable to affect those creatures which are deaf, such as the [[Lotan]]
- Flute is unable to affect [[Marethyu]]. Most likely this is due to his lack of aura.
- If stolen and played by anyone other than Virginia, the Flute was stated to be capable of stealing the thief’s voice. As stated by Virginia herself when one of the [[Nereids]]’s took it from her. [citation needed]
