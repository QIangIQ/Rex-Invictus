[[Valeria Alekerth]]
[[True Death|The Death]] Staff is a powerful Divine artifact created specifically for Valeria Alekerth, Supreme Priestess of the [[Blood God Religion]] and disciple of [[Victor Elderblood]]. It was jointly created through the power and craftsmanship of Victor, [[Thanatos]] and [[Hephaestus]] following Victor's conquest of the [[Greek Pantheon]].[1]
The Staff grants Valeria access to a fraction of Victor's authority over Death, allowing a Human who normally possesses no natural Divine authority over Souls or Death to perceive Souls, communicate with the dead, summon Souls as servants, store Souls, command death-related supernatural phenomena and more easily summon Demons from Hell. It also possesses powerful Runic safeguards that prevent the artifact from easily being stolen or separated from its owner.[1]

### Description
[[True Death|The Death]] Staff is a black staff decorated with golden details and covered with numerous supernatural Runes. At its upper portion floats a violet gemstone, which functions as both a supernatural component of the artifact and a storage vessel for Souls.[1]
The artifact can float independently behind Valeria when she is not actively carrying it. By later periods it had become recognized as Valeria's personal symbol of power, representing her position as Supreme Priestess and representative of the God Emperor.[2]
When Valeria takes hold of the Staff, supernatural power immediately flows through her body.[2]

### History

#### Creation
Following the conquest of the [[Greek Pantheon]], [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] acquired additional authorities and powers from the defeated Pantheon. One of the Domains incorporated into his expanding authority was Death.[1]
Victor subsequently decided to provide his disciple and Supreme Priestess, [[Valeria Alekerth]], with a weapon that would allow her to make practical use of a fraction of this authority while acting as the leader of the [[Blood God Religion]].[1]
The Staff was produced through the combined work of three exceptionally powerful beings: Victor, whose power and authority formed the foundation of the artifact; [[Thanatos]], a Greek God directly associated with Death; and [[Hephaestus]], one of the greatest Divine craftsmen of the Greek Pantheon.[1]
The completed artifact was delivered to Valeria by Aline. Because of the power contained within it and the beings responsible for its construction, Aline considered the Staff an artifact that would be coveted even among Gods.[1]

#### Valeria Alekerth
When Aline presented the Staff to Valeria, she explained that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had acquired Death among his Domains and that the artifact allowed Valeria to use a small portion of that power.[1]
Upon touching the Staff for the first time, Valeria's eyes glowed neon violet and her perception immediately expanded. She became capable of perceiving Souls and experienced an intoxicating surge of power strong enough that Aline warned her to control herself rather than allow the artifact's Energy to overwhelm her.[1]
Aline then explained the Staff's numerous additional functions, including direct communication with the dead, Soul interaction, Soul storage, summoning deceased beings as servants, easier Demon summoning and the ability to impose Death upon people connected to the [[Blood God Religion]].[1]
After receiving the Staff, Valeria immediately began using its supernatural abilities as part of her role in expanding Victor's religion. By later years, the artifact remained continuously associated with her and had become her recognized symbol of authority.[1][2]

### Powers and Abilities

#### Death Authority
The Staff grants Valeria access to a portion of the Death-related authority possessed by [[Victor Elderblood]].[1]
This allows a Human who ordinarily lacks direct authority over Death to exercise supernatural effects involving death and Souls through the artifact.[1]

#### Death Bestowal
Valeria can use the Staff to give Death to individuals connected to the [[Blood God Religion]]. When such a person dies through this authority, their Soul falls directly under [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s control rather than being left to an unrelated afterlife system.[1]
This function connects the Staff directly to Victor's role as the God worshipped by the religion and allows its Death authority to participate in the spiritual administration of his followers.[1]

#### Spiritual Vision
One of the Staff's passive effects is granting its wielder spiritual vision. When Valeria first touched the artifact, her eyes changed to a neon-violet glow and she became capable of perceiving aspects of the world normally hidden from ordinary Human senses.[1]
The effect specifically allows Valeria to see Souls.[1]
This perception is not equivalent to the much greater visual abilities possessed by Gods or Dragons, but it provides a Human wielder with access to spiritual phenomena normally perceptible only to particular supernatural species.[1]

#### Communication with the Dead
While holding the Staff, Valeria can function as a medium between the living and the dead. She can directly communicate with deceased Souls and interact with them rather than merely sensing their presence.[1]

#### Soul Interaction
[[True Death|The Death]] Staff allows Valeria to physically or supernaturally interact with Souls through its authority.[1]
This makes Souls tangible to her supernatural abilities despite her Human nature and forms the basis for several of the Staff's other functions.

#### Soul Storage
The violet gemstone floating above the Staff can contain Souls. Several Souls had already been placed inside the gemstone when the artifact was first given to Valeria.[1]
The gemstone can also absorb the Souls of beings killed by Valeria, increasing the number of spiritual entities available through the artifact.[1]

#### Soul Summoning
Souls stored within the Staff can be summoned into the physical world as servants or minions under Valeria's control.[1]
Valeria can use these summoned Souls as extensions of herself, including making use of them as additional senses.[1]
Shortly after receiving the artifact, Valeria struck the Staff against the ground and summoned humanoid entities composed of violet flames. These beings consisted primarily of Souls made capable of existing within the physical realm through the properties of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Draconic Fire.[1]

#### Necromantic Command
Because the Staff permits Valeria to store, summon, perceive and command deceased Souls, it effectively provides her with a powerful form of necromantic authority.[1]
Unlike conventional necromancy based upon manipulating corpses, the Staff's demonstrated abilities primarily operate upon the Soul itself.[1]

#### Demon Summoning
The Staff can function as a medium that allows Valeria to summon Demons from Hell substantially more easily.[1]
This ability complements the authority Valeria already possessed as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s representative and Supreme Priestess, giving her a direct supernatural tool through which the forces under Victor's rule can be called into action.[1]

#### Automatic Return
[[True Death|The Death]] Staff possesses an enchantment preventing it from being easily separated from Valeria.[1]
When Aline deliberately took the Staff from Valeria's hands, the artifact immediately disappeared and returned to its owner's possession.[1]
This makes conventional theft or disarmament ineffective against the weapon.

#### Draconic Runic Protection
The automatic-return function and other safeguards are maintained through powerful Runes associated with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[1]
The enchantments are extremely difficult to overcome due to their Draconic nature. However, Aline explicitly warned Valeria that they were not absolutely invincible; sufficiently powerful beings may be capable of preventing the Runes from functioning.[1]

#### Power Enhancement
The Staff functions as more than a collection of individual abilities. Simply taking hold of it causes supernatural power to surge throughout Valeria's body.[2]
The artifact therefore acts as a major personal amplifier and supernatural focus for the Supreme Priestess in addition to granting its specialized Death and Soul abilities.

### Relationship with the Blood God Religion
[[True Death|The Death]] Staff is closely tied to [[Victor Elderblood]] and the [[Blood God Religion]]. It was specifically created for Victor's Supreme Priestess rather than as a general-purpose Divine Weapon.[1]
Its ability to direct the Souls of deceased followers toward Victor makes it part of the religion's supernatural infrastructure, while its Demon-summoning and Soul-related properties allow Valeria to exercise Victor's authority directly while acting in his name.[1]
As the religion expanded, the Staff became one of Valeria's defining possessions and her personal symbol of power.[2]

### Wielder
- [[Valeria Alekerth]] — The Staff was personally created for Valeria by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], [[Thanatos]] and [[Hephaestus]]. Its enchantments recognize her as its owner and automatically return it to her if removed.[1]

### Limitations
- Fraction of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Authority: The Staff grants Valeria only a portion of Victor's Death-related power rather than making her equivalent to Victor or a genuine God of Death.[1]
- Runic Defenses Are Not Absolute: Extremely powerful beings can potentially interfere with or suppress the Runes responsible for the Staff's automatic-return enchantment.[1]
- Spiritual Vision Has Limits: The perception granted by the artifact allows Valeria to see Souls, but it is explicitly inferior to the extraordinary visual perception possessed by Gods and Dragons.[1]
- Stored Souls: Some of its Soul-summoning functions rely upon Souls contained within the Staff's violet gemstone.[1]

### Trivia
- The Staff is one of the few known artifacts in the series jointly created through the abilities of a Dragon Progenitor, a God associated directly with Death and a Divine blacksmith.[1]
- Although commonly describable as a weapon, much of its utility is spiritual, religious and administrative rather than purely offensive.
- [[True Death|The Death]] Staff is specifically identified as Valeria's symbol of power by Chapter 1022.[2]
- Valeria commonly allows the Staff to float behind her rather than continuously holding it.[2]
- The Staff does not grant flawless resurrection. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ability to fully resurrect Valeria's daughter involved separate powers and negotiations concerning her Soul and should not be attributed to the Staff itself.
