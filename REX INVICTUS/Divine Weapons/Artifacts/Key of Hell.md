The Key of Hell, also called the Key to Hell, Key to the Gates of Hell or [[Lucifer]]'s Key, is an ancient Divine Artifact connected to the supernatural authority governing [[Biblical Hell]]. Originally associated with Lucifer Morningstar, the first Demon King, the Key functions as an intermediary between its holder and the metaphysical mechanisms responsible for regulating Hell, administering its inhabitants and maintaining the dimensional boundary separating the infernal realm from the World of the Living. Its principal importance lies in its ability to provide access to the authority of Hell's legitimate Ruler rather than functioning as an ordinary physical key or conventional weapon.[1][2]
The Key was originally entrusted to Lucifer as part of the system through which Biblical Hell was governed. However, when Lucifer abandoned his responsibilities, he performed an act that fundamentally destabilized the infernal realm: he broke the Key into two pieces and distributed them between Lilith, the Mother of Demons, and Diablo, the Demon who would later dominate the majority of Hell. The division disrupted the integrity of the Gates of Hell, creating gaps through which lesser Demons could enter the Human World and forcing more powerful Demons to employ alternative methods, particularly possession of recently deceased Humans, to bypass the restrictions imposed upon their Race.[3]
The separation of the artifact also had consequences for Hell's government. Although Lilith and Diablo each controlled substantial portions of the infernal realm, neither possessed the complete authority of its legitimate Ruler. They could perform certain administrative functions through their respective halves of the Key, but the realm remained without an individual properly recognized as its true Ruler. Diablo eventually defeated Lilith and reunited the two pieces, yet even possessing the complete Key did not grant him the position previously held by Lucifer. Instead, he continued exercising partial authority while deliberately avoiding the responsibilities and restrictions attached to genuine rulership.[2]
The distinction between possessing the Key and being recognized as Hell's Ruler became particularly important during the Demonic War. After conquering Biblical Hell, [[Victor Elderblood]] entered the Demon King's Castle and was recognized by the ancient Keepers of Hell, Albu and [[Zahal]], and by the Throne itself. The realm accepted him as its true King before he possessed the Key, but the authority transmitted to him remained incomplete while Diablo retained the artifact. Victor consequently ordered his wives and allies to recover it, recognizing that obtaining the Key was necessary both to complete his connection with Hell and to escape the dimensional restrictions imposed upon its King.[4]
The Key was eventually stolen from Diablo during the conflict between the Demonic forces and the armies of Heaven. Its recovery enabled Victor to establish a more complete connection with the realm he had conquered and travel between Biblical Hell and the World of the Living without remaining trapped within the infernal dimension. However, the artifact's later history became less clear after Victor fundamentally restructured the afterlife, merging the former Biblical and Greek Hells into a new realm governed under his authority. The novel confirms the establishment of new Rulers but does not explicitly explain whether the original Key retained all its previous functions within the reorganized system.[5][6]

### Description

#### Physical Appearance
The exact physical appearance of the Key of Hell has not been described in sufficient detail to establish its original shape, dimensions, materials or decorative characteristics. Although it is repeatedly identified as a Key, the novel places considerably greater emphasis upon its supernatural function than its external form. Its ability to be divided into two independently held portions confirms that it possesses an existence capable of being separated, but the text does not provide a detailed description of the resulting fragments.
The Key should also be distinguished from the Gates of Hell themselves. These are separate aspects of the infernal system: the Key is the artifact through which authority and access may be exercised, while the Gate represents the boundary restricting passage between [[Biblical Hell]] and the World of the Living.
The Gate's appearance was described more directly during [[Diablo]]'s early introduction. Looking toward the blood-red sky of Hell, Diablo perceived an enormous black door that appeared to be bleeding. His ability to observe the Gate originated from his possession of one half of [[Lucifer]]'s Key, but that incomplete connection did not allow him to touch or interact with the structure. Diablo believed that acquiring the complete artifact would enable him to exercise the authority necessary to manipulate it.[3]
The description establishes that the Key provides access to a supernatural structure beyond an ordinary physical doorway. However, it does not demonstrate that the Key itself possesses the same appearance as the Gate or that the artifact can transform into the Gate.

#### Nature
The Key of Hell is fundamentally an Authority Artifact. Its supernatural properties are inseparable from the system governing [[Biblical Hell]], particularly the responsibilities associated with the realm's legitimate Ruler.
[[Diablo]] directly explained that the artifact served as a connection point for Hell's Ruler, comparing it with the Helm of [[Hades]], which performs a related function within the [[Greek Underworld]]. This comparison establishes that certain afterlives possess unique artifacts through which qualified individuals can access or exercise authority over their respective realms. The Key is therefore better understood as a means of interacting with Hell's governing mechanisms than as the source from which the entire dimension derives its existence.[2]
The distinction is demonstrated by the history of its owners. [[Lucifer]] was recognized as the legitimate Ruler while possessing the Key, whereas Lilith and Diablo could exercise portions of the associated authority without acquiring the same status. Later, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became Hell's recognized King while the complete artifact remained in Diablo's possession. His authority was genuine but incomplete until the missing connection could be recovered.
Consequently, three separate aspects of the infernal system must be distinguished: the legitimacy of the individual recognized by Hell, the authority accessible through the Key, and the practical ability to administer or control the realm. These aspects may belong to the same individual, as they did under Lucifer, but they are not automatically identical.
The Key's importance is further demonstrated by its relationship with the Gates of Hell. Its division caused the dimensional boundary to become unstable, while its incomplete state prevented Diablo from fully interacting with the Gate despite his possession of one of its fragments. These circumstances indicate that the artifact's condition directly influences access to certain mechanisms regulating Hell.

#### Connection to Hell's Authority
[[Biblical Hell]] is not merely an enormous dimension inhabited by Demons. It is also part of the wider cosmic system responsible for receiving, judging and recycling Souls. Its existence therefore carries responsibilities that extend beyond the political ambitions of whichever Demon happens to control the greatest territory.
As [[Diablo]] explained, the position of Ruler is connected to the judgment of sinners and the administration of the realm's supernatural processes. A recognized Ruler is responsible for ensuring that Hell continues performing the functions required by the broader system of Life, Death and Reincarnation. Although the Key provides access to aspects of this authority, it does not independently make an unqualified holder the individual legitimately responsible for the entire system.[2]
This distinction became particularly important after [[Lucifer]]'s departure. Lilith and Diablo maintained portions of the infernal administration through their divided ownership, but neither completed the process necessary to become the true Ruler. Diablo later acknowledged that he had deliberately avoided the position because the obligations and restrictions associated with Hell conflicted with his ambitions beyond the infernal realm.
The Key therefore allows an individual to interact with certain powers associated with rulership without necessarily granting the full recognition, knowledge or responsibilities possessed by a legitimate Ruler.

### History

#### Lucifer's Rule
[[Lucifer]] Morningstar was the original known holder of the Key of Hell and the first Demon King responsible for governing [[Biblical Hell]]. His position included the guardianship of the dimensional boundary restricting Demons from freely entering the Human World, a responsibility connected with the Divine rules established by the [[Heavenly Father]].
The existence of this restriction was particularly important because Demons possessed extraordinary numbers and the capacity to influence the Mortal World through direct violence, contracts and possession. Without a functioning barrier, powerful Demonic armies could potentially enter Earth in numbers far greater than those permitted through the normal supernatural system.
Lucifer served as the guardian of this boundary while also occupying the position associated with Hell's highest legitimate authority. The Key formed part of the system through which that authority could be exercised, allowing its owner to maintain the connection between the infernal realm and the mechanisms governing passage beyond it.[1]
However, Lucifer eventually abandoned his responsibilities for reasons connected with his own desires and refusal to remain permanently confined to the role assigned to him. Before leaving, he deliberately broke the Key into two portions and distributed them to two powerful Demonic figures who would subsequently dominate the political development of Biblical Hell.
The act was extraordinary because the Key had been regarded as something that should not ordinarily be breakable. [[Bael]], reflecting upon Lucifer's departure, recognized that the division of an artifact intended to remain intact had disrupted the supposedly orderly system established by the Heavenly Father.[3]
Lucifer's actions consequently created both a political division and a supernatural malfunction. The former Ruler was gone, the authority formerly concentrated in his position had been divided, and the Gate restricting passage from Hell could no longer operate in its original condition.

#### Division Between Lilith and Diablo
[[Lucifer]] entrusted one half of the Key to [[Lilith Elderblood]], the Mother of Demons, and the other to [[Diablo]], the Demon associated with the incarnation of Evil. The division accompanied the fragmentation of [[Biblical Hell]] into competing political factions, each governed by one of the two individuals who had inherited a portion of Lucifer's authority.
Lilith and Diablo possessed different objectives. Lilith did not share Diablo's desire to dominate Humanity through an extensive military invasion, while Diablo sought to expand his influence beyond Hell and establish control over the Mortal World. Their disagreements eventually developed into a struggle for the political and military future of the Demonic Race.
The divided Key reflected this situation. Neither faction possessed the complete artifact or the full connection previously available to Lucifer, leaving Hell without a single individual capable of exercising its legitimate Ruler's complete authority.[1]
Nevertheless, possession of the fragments was not meaningless. Diablo later explained that both he and Lilith had been performing portions of the Ruler's work through their respective connections, allowing the infernal realm to continue functioning despite the absence of an individual properly recognized by its governing system.
This arrangement was imperfect, but it prevented the immediate collapse of the administration associated with the dead. The two Demon leaders exercised practical control through the powers accessible to them while the cosmic system continued operating without a legitimate successor to Lucifer.[2]

#### Instability of the Gates of Hell
[[Lucifer]]'s decision to break the Key directly affected the Gate separating [[Biblical Hell]] from the World of the Living. The division caused the boundary to become unstable, producing gaps through which Demons could attempt to escape the restrictions imposed upon their Race.
These breaches did not simply eliminate the [[Heavenly Father]]'s rules. Although lesser Demons could pass through the openings, more powerful Demons remained unable to cross freely without adopting additional measures. The resulting restrictions contributed directly to the methods employed by [[Diablo]]'s faction when sending supernatural agents to Earth.[1]
One possibility involved suppressing a Demon's power until its strength resembled that of a Low-Level Demon. This approach allowed sufficiently powerful individuals to reduce themselves to a level compatible with the weakened boundary, but it created considerable risks because a Demon temporarily deprived of most of its strength could become vulnerable to enemies or betrayal.
The alternative involved Demonic Possession. Powerful Demons could occupy the bodies of recently deceased Humans and employ the remaining Human Soul as a means of passing the boundary. Because this method did not require voluntarily abandoning most of their supernatural power, it became particularly attractive to Demons unwilling to risk entering the Mortal World in a weakened state.
The broken Key therefore contributed indirectly to the continued presence of Demonic agents on Earth. Its damaged condition created opportunities for infiltration while simultaneously restricting the direct movement of the strongest inhabitants of Biblical Hell.
However, the gaps should not be interpreted as evidence that the Gate had ceased existing. The boundary remained active, and higher-ranking Demons continued confronting substantial restrictions upon direct passage.

#### Diablo's Incomplete Connection
[[Diablo]]'s own experience demonstrated the limitations imposed by possessing only one part of the Key.
During his early appearance, he observed the enormous black Gate suspended against the blood-red sky of Hell. The structure appeared to be bleeding, reflecting the abnormal condition of the dimensional boundary after [[Lucifer]] divided the artifact.
Diablo understood that his fragment allowed him to perceive the Gate, but he could not touch or interact with it. He believed acquiring the complete Key would remove this limitation and allow him to exercise a greater degree of control over the structure.[3]
This distinction is important because it establishes that the two fragments did not simply provide identical unrestricted copies of the original artifact. Diablo's possession granted a connection, but certain interactions remained inaccessible while the Key was incomplete.
The narration presents Diablo's expectation concerning the complete artifact as his own understanding of its operation. It does not independently describe every mechanism through which the Gate would respond once both halves were reunited.
The Key's fragments nevertheless represented important supernatural resources, and obtaining Lilith's portion became valuable to Diablo's ambition of consolidating his rule.

#### Diablo Reunites the Key
[[Diablo]] eventually defeated Lilith and reunited the two halves of [[Lucifer]]'s Key. His victory also consolidated much of [[Biblical Hell]] beneath a single political authority, ending the prolonged division between the two principal Demonic factions.
However, restoring the artifact did not automatically restore the original administrative system. Diablo subsequently acknowledged that although he had defeated Lilith and united the fragments, he had never completely become the realm's true Ruler.[2]
The reason was not simply a lack of personal strength or the failure of the complete Key to respond. Diablo deliberately avoided accepting the restrictions associated with legitimate infernal rulership because his ambitions extended beyond Hell. He wanted to conduct operations in the Mortal World and pursue objectives that would have been complicated by the obligations attached to supervising the realm.
Instead, Diablo continued exercising authority through the Key while employing Lilith as a substitute administrator. He temporarily transformed her into something resembling a Ruler so the system governing Souls would continue operating, but she had not completed the required trial and was not legitimately recognized by the higher authorities responsible for maintaining the afterlife.
Diablo himself regarded the arrangement as a practical workaround rather than genuine succession. Lilith functioned as part of the administrative system without obtaining the complete standing associated with a legitimate Ruler.
The distinction mattered because the system governing Souls was supervised by [[The Judges of The Abyss]], whose responsibilities extended beyond the political arrangements of Biblical Hell. If Diablo's activities interfered too severely with the processing of Souls, the cosmic authorities could intervene regardless of how many Demons acknowledged him as King.
Diablo consequently negotiated with the Judges, offering enormous numbers of Souls in exchange for a period during which they would not interfere directly with his plans. The arrangement allowed him to continue exploiting the system for his military ambitions while postponing the consequences of his disruption to the established Balance.[2]
Thus, even while Diablo possessed the complete Key and controlled the largest political faction within Hell, his position remained fundamentally different from that of Lucifer.

#### Victor's Recognition as King
The Key acquired even greater strategic importance after [[Victor Elderblood]] entered [[Biblical Hell]] during the Demonic War.
Victor's invasion transformed into a prolonged conquest during which he defeated Demonic commanders, accumulated followers and established himself as an authority capable of challenging the existing infernal hierarchy. His supernatural nature allowed him to survive conditions that ordinary Vampires and many other living beings could not endure, while his growing command over Demonic forces made him a genuine threat to [[Diablo]]'s political position.
The decisive development occurred when Victor reached the Castle of the Demon King and entered its Throne Room alongside [[Vine]], Vepar and Helena. There he encountered Albu and [[Zahal]], the ancient Keepers of Hell who had existed since before [[Lucifer]]'s fall from Heaven.
The Keepers were not ordinary guards appointed by Diablo. Their responsibility was to protect the continued existence of Hell, including preventing an incompetent King from damaging or destroying the realm through their actions. Their loyalty consequently belonged first to Hell itself rather than unconditionally to whichever Demon currently occupied its Throne.
When Victor approached, the Keepers recognized the extraordinary characteristics of his existence and knelt before him, acknowledging him as the true King of Hell. Victor subsequently approached the Throne, which altered its dimensions and appearance to accommodate him, before accepting his presence as its legitimate sovereign.[4]
The recognition transmitted extensive knowledge concerning Hell's administration and the duties associated with its rulership directly into Victor's mind. He became aware of the mechanisms supporting the realm and recognized that the infernal Soul-processing system had been seriously disrupted by Diablo's activities.
However, Victor also detected an important deficiency in his newly acquired authority. Although the Keepers and Throne had accepted him, his connection with Hell remained incomplete because Diablo still possessed the Key.
Albu explained that the missing portion could only be restored once the artifact was returned from the former King's possession.
This event established the most important distinction governing the Key of Hell: Victor was recognized as the legitimate King without possessing the Key, while Diablo possessed the complete Key without being recognized as the legitimate Ruler.
The artifact therefore did not independently determine rightful succession. Instead, it provided access to functions whose proper exercise depended upon the relationship between the holder and Hell's governing system.

#### The Problem of Leaving Hell
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s recognition as King introduced another major complication. Although he had conquered [[Biblical Hell]] and established his authority over its inhabitants, the dimension's supernatural rules prevented its King from leaving through ordinary methods.
This restriction differed from the circumstances governing certain other Pantheons. Victor explained that the Rulers of some Greek, Norse and Hindu Underworlds could travel to the Human World, whereas Biblical Hell possessed unusually severe restrictions associated with its governing system.
He also recognized that the realm itself protected its King. When others attempted to interfere with his position or remove him forcibly, Hell could oppose their actions through the supernatural structure governing the dimension.
Victor understood that his condition differed from [[Lucifer]]'s and [[Diablo]]'s historical circumstances, since the former Demon Kings had experienced additional restrictions connected with the [[Heavenly Father]]. Nevertheless, the natural limitations of Biblical Hell still prevented him from returning to his family through ordinary passage.[5]
The Key offered a solution. Victor explained that possessing it would allow him to travel between Hell and the World of the Living, making the artifact necessary not only for completing his Authority but also for restoring his personal freedom of movement.
This introduced an important second function of the Key. Beyond its administrative relationship with Hell's Ruler, it could provide the means of interacting with the dimensional boundary that otherwise prevented the King from leaving.
Victor consequently made recovering the artifact a priority. Although his conquest had placed an enormous Demonic army under his command, the immediate problem could not be resolved merely by possessing more soldiers or defeating additional Demonic commanders. The missing connection had to be recovered from Diablo himself.

#### Operation to Recover the Key
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] established contact with his wives and allies through a connection maintained by [[Alexios Alioth]], who possessed extraordinary abilities involving Space. The gathering included several of the strongest women associated with Victor's family, all of whom were deeply concerned about his continued confinement within [[Biblical Hell]].
Victor explained that his position as King was genuine but that the Key remained in [[Diablo]]'s possession. He emphasized that obtaining the artifact would allow him to return to the living world and exercise the authority he had acquired more completely.
He subsequently ordered the group to recover the Key without entering an unnecessary confrontation with Diablo. The distinction was deliberate: although several of Victor's wives possessed extraordinary strength, fighting the Demon King directly risked substantial casualties and could complicate the operation.[5]
The principal recovery team included Natashia, [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]], Natalia, [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] and [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood. Their different supernatural specialties offered the possibility of locating, approaching and removing the artifact without relying upon a direct contest of overwhelming destructive power.
[[Scathach]] emphasized the need to discover the Key's location before determining the exact method of retrieval. This was especially important because the artifact was under Diablo's control, and simply knowing that he possessed it did not establish whether it was carried openly, concealed through supernatural powers or stored somewhere inaccessible to ordinary thieves.
The operation subsequently became connected with the enormous battle involving the Demonic armies, the forces of Heaven and several other supernatural factions.
During the conflict, Victor's allies approached Diablo while much of his attention was occupied by the increasingly chaotic battlefield. Natashia's extraordinary speed and the group's coordinated efforts enabled the Key to be taken from his possession, depriving him of the connection previously maintained through the artifact.
By Chapter 683, Diablo explicitly recognized that he had lost both the Key of Hell and his former position within the realm. The loss was especially humiliating because he had spent enormous periods preparing his military campaign, only to have Victor conquer Hell while his most important authority artifact was removed during the struggle.[7][8]
Diablo's defeat concerning the Key was therefore not simply the loss of a valuable object. It deprived him of the remaining administrative connection through which he had exercised significant influence over Biblical Hell after [[Lucifer]]'s departure.

#### Recovery and Victor's Return
The successful recovery of the Key resolved the principal obstacle preventing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] from leaving [[Biblical Hell]] through its normal supernatural restrictions.
By the end of the Demonic War, Victor could return to the World of the Living while retaining his position as King. He subsequently acknowledged the assistance provided by his wives and confirmed that possession of the Key made travel between the two realms considerably easier.
The development completed the requirement identified by Albu and [[Zahal]] during Victor's recognition by the Throne. Whereas [[Diablo]] had possessed the artifact without legitimate rulership, Victor now combined the authority acknowledged by Hell with the connection previously withheld through Diablo's possession.
The event also marked an important political transformation. [[Lucifer]]'s departure had left Hell divided between competing rulers, Diablo had reunited the artifact while refusing its responsibilities, and Victor had conquered the realm before obtaining the Key from its former holder. The artifact's return consequently accompanied the establishment of a more complete infernal government beneath the new King.
However, the novel does not provide a complete technical description of the Key's appearance or the precise actions Victor performed to activate it after its recovery. Its significance is established through the resulting restoration of his access and the completion of the missing authority rather than through an extensively described activation ceremony.

#### Reorganization of Hell
The Key's later history became more complicated after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] developed beyond his original Vampire Progenitor existence and began restructuring the supernatural territories under his authority.
Following the collapse of the [[Greek Pantheon]]'s former political system, Victor obtained the opportunity to reorganize the [[Greek Underworld]] alongside [[Biblical Hell]]. Rather than preserve every original infernal boundary indefinitely, he pursued the creation of a more unified afterlife capable of functioning under a common sovereign authority.
The project eventually resulted in the merger of the former Biblical and Greek Hells into a new realm associated with Victor's expanding Personal Universe.
During Chapter 827, Victor summoned [[Aline Elderblood Valefar]] and [[Persephone Elderblood]], both of whom had previously possessed Ruler Authority connected with the original infernal systems. He placed his fingers against their foreheads and strengthened their authority directly before declaring them the Rulers of the new Hell.[6]
[[Persephone]] initially questioned why another declaration was necessary when she had already been a Ruler. Victor explained that the earlier Biblical and Greek Hells had ceased existing as separate systems following their merger. Consequently, the authority associated with their original jurisdictions needed to be adapted to the new realm.
This development is important to the Key of Hell because the artifact's confirmed original functions were tied specifically to Biblical Hell. The new infernal realm possessed a different political and metaphysical structure, and Victor could directly confer authority upon its Rulers through powers associated with his own existence.
The chapter does not state that the Key of Hell was destroyed, nor does it explicitly describe the artifact being transferred to Aline or Persephone as part of the new succession. It also does not establish that the Key automatically gained authority over every afterlife incorporated into the merger.
Its precise position within the reorganized system therefore remains uncertain. The original Key was recovered for Victor, but its subsequent physical custody and continued functions have not been individually documented.

### Powers and Abilities
The Key of Hell possesses abilities associated primarily with Ruler Authority, dimensional passage and interaction with the supernatural mechanisms of [[Biblical Hell]]. Unlike an ordinary combat artifact, its importance depends upon the connection it provides to an existing system of authority rather than the direct generation of offensive techniques.

#### Ruler Authority
- Divine Artifact: The Key is an ancient supernatural artifact connected with the authority of [[Biblical Hell]]'s legitimate Ruler. Its importance originates from its relationship with the realm's governing system rather than conventional weapon enchantments.[2]
- Authority Connection: [[Diablo]] explicitly identifies the Key as a connection point for the Ruler of Hell, comparing its function to that of the Helm of [[Hades]].[2]
- [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] Authority: Possession of the Key allows access to portions of the supernatural authority associated with Biblical Hell. Lilith and Diablo exercised administrative functions through separate halves of the artifact while neither was fully recognized as the legitimate Ruler.[2]
- Authority Completion: The artifact is necessary to complete the connection of an individual already recognized by the realm. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became the legitimate King before recovering the Key, but his authority remained incomplete until the missing artifact could be returned.[4]
- Partial Rulership: An incomplete or otherwise insufficient connection can still permit access to certain administrative functions. Lilith and Diablo maintained aspects of Hell's operation through their divided possession after [[Lucifer]]'s departure.[2]
- Rulership Empowerment: The Key contributes to the exercise of legitimate Ruler Authority by supplying access to the functions unavailable without it. This should not be confused with independently granting the entire status of King or Ruler.[4]
- Supernatural System Access: The artifact provides a means of interacting with the mechanisms governing Hell, including the authority necessary to administer certain aspects of the realm's supernatural structure.[2]

#### Dimensional Abilities
- Dimensional Travel: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] identified possession of the Key as the means through which he could travel between [[Biblical Hell]] and the World of the Living despite the restrictions ordinarily imposed upon Hell's King.[5]
- Dimensional Passage: The Key provides access through the dimensional boundary separating Biblical Hell from the Mortal World, allowing a properly connected holder to overcome restrictions associated with that realm.[5]
- Dimensional Gate Interaction: Possession of a fragment enabled [[Diablo]] to perceive the enormous Gate of Hell, although the incomplete artifact did not allow him to touch or manipulate it.[3]
- Gate Authority: The complete Key is associated with the authority governing Hell's boundary. Its division destabilized the Gate, demonstrating the relationship between the artifact's condition and the dimensional restriction imposed upon Demons.[1]
- Dimensional Restriction Bypass: The Key allowed Victor to overcome the special condition preventing the King of Biblical Hell from departing freely through conventional means.[5]
- Interdimensional Access: Its confirmed travel function connects Biblical Hell with the living world. The novel does not establish unrestricted travel to every supernatural dimension, Sector or Universe.[5]

#### Administrative Functions
- Hell Administration: The Key permits access to portions of the authority required to administer [[Biblical Hell]]. Lilith and [[Diablo]] used their fragments to perform some of the responsibilities left vacant following [[Lucifer]]'s departure.[2]
- Soul System Connection: Its authority is associated with the system through which Hell processes Souls, a responsibility supervised by the Judges of the Abyss. This is an administrative connection rather than demonstrated unrestricted control over every Soul in the Cosmos.[2]
- Afterlife Regulation: The Key supports the proper exercise of Hell's administrative authority, whose responsibilities include maintaining the realm's participation in the broader system of judging and recycling Souls.[4]
- Rulership Function Access: Individuals who are not legitimate Rulers may exercise certain functions through the artifact without acquiring the full status of the position, as demonstrated by Diablo and Lilith.[2]

#### Artifact Properties
- Divisible Artifact: [[Lucifer]] deliberately broke the original Key into two portions, each of which could be possessed independently by a different individual.[1]
- Independent Fragment Functionality: The fragments retained enough supernatural properties to support portions of Hell's administration and allow [[Diablo]] to perceive the Gate.[3][2]
- Reunification: Diablo eventually obtained both fragments and restored the complete Key after defeating Lilith.[2]
- Exceptional Construction: [[Bael]] regarded the original Key as an artifact that should have been unbreakable, making Lucifer's division of it an extraordinary event within the established Divine system.[3]
- Authority Transfer: Possession of the complete artifact could pass from one holder to another, as demonstrated when Diablo lost the Key during the Demonic War and it entered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s control.[8]
The Key has not been shown generating destructive attacks, transforming into a combat weapon, creating independent Souls or granting universal authority over every afterlife. Such abilities should not be assigned to it merely because other artifacts associated with [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] rulers possess different supernatural functions.

One of the most important revelations concerning the Key is that the individual possessing Hell's authority artifact is not necessarily the individual legitimately recognized as its Ruler.
This distinction was demonstrated repeatedly through the different individuals who held the artifact after [[Lucifer]]'s departure.

The artifact therefore acts as an essential component of Hell's original rulership system without independently determining the legitimacy of every individual attempting to govern the realm.

### Relationship with the Gates of Hell
The connection between the Key and Hell's dimensional boundary is one of its most important established characteristics.
[[Biblical Hell]] possesses a Gate separating it from the Mortal World. The boundary was associated with the Divine restrictions imposed upon Demons, preventing them from freely entering Earth and interfering with Humanity.
[[Lucifer]] originally served as the guardian responsible for maintaining this restriction. His decision to abandon the position and divide the Key caused the Gate to become unstable, producing gaps through which Demons could attempt passage.[1]
The resulting instability did not completely eliminate the boundary. Lesser Demons could exploit the openings, while stronger Demons remained restricted and required alternative methods to enter the Mortal World.
[[Diablo]]'s early perception of the Gate further clarifies the connection. Possessing one fragment allowed him to see the enormous black structure, but not to touch or interact with it. His belief that he could exercise greater authority after reuniting the Key establishes that its condition affected more than simply the number of administrators capable of exercising its power.[3]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later use revealed another application: the Key allowed a recognized King to travel between Hell and the World of the Living even when the realm's ordinary laws would otherwise prevent his departure.
The artifact therefore functions as both an administrative authority connection and a means of interacting with the dimensional access system associated with Biblical Hell.
Nevertheless, the exact method through which the Key opens or regulates the Gate has not been fully described. The novel does not establish that the physical artifact must be inserted into a conventional lock or that every use requires an identical ritual. Its demonstrated effects concern authority, access and the consequences of its separation.

### Relationship with the Soul System
[[Biblical Hell]] is one of the supernatural realms responsible for processing Souls within the broader cosmic structure governing Life, Death and Reincarnation.
The duties associated with Hell's Ruler are consequently more extensive than commanding Demons or controlling territory. A legitimate Ruler must ensure that the realm continues functioning as part of the system through which Souls are judged, processed and eventually returned to the broader cycle.
[[Diablo]]'s explanation in Chapter 667 confirms that the absence of a legitimate Ruler caused problems serious enough to attract the attention of the Judges of the Abyss. His temporary use of Lilith as an administrative substitute was intended to prevent the system from deteriorating to the point where Souls would be lost or their processing would cease entirely.[2]
The Key contributed to the provisional administration because both Diablo and Lilith had been able to perform portions of the Ruler's work through their fragments. However, this did not grant either of them the same standing as an individual who had legitimately inherited the position.
The problem became even clearer when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] acquired the Throne. The knowledge transmitted through his recognition allowed him to perceive that the flow of Souls through Biblical Hell had been seriously disturbed by the previous administration.
Albu and [[Zahal]] explained that the realm's survival depended upon continuing to perform its responsibilities. A Hell that ceased functioning properly could face intervention from the cosmic authorities responsible for the broader system, regardless of the strength of its political ruler.[4]
The Key therefore possesses importance beyond the inhabitants of Biblical Hell. Its connection contributes to the maintenance of a supernatural structure whose malfunction can affect the wider process through which Souls are administered.
However, the artifact should not be confused with the Judges of the Abyss themselves. The Judges possess superior cosmic responsibilities concerning Souls and their processing, while the Key provides access to administrative authority within a particular infernal jurisdiction.

### Relationship with the Helm of Hades
The Key of Hell is directly compared with the Helm of [[Hades]], the Divine Artifact associated with the legitimate Ruler of the [[Greek Underworld]].
[[Diablo]] described both objects as connection points through which the Rulers of their respective Hells could access the authority associated with their realms.[2]
Their functions are similar in principle, but their documented applications and succession mechanisms are not identical.

The comparison establishes that Underworld artifacts can perform related administrative functions without possessing identical supernatural abilities. The Key's documented importance concerns the Ruler's connection, Hell's Gate and dimensional travel, while the Helm possesses a more extensively demonstrated succession trial and later weapon manifestations.
The similarities also demonstrate the importance of distinguishing individual afterlives from the wider cosmic system responsible for their existence. Neither artifact independently governs every Hell or replaces the higher authorities overseeing the processing of Souls.

### Political and Strategic Importance
The Key of Hell influenced the political history of [[Biblical Hell]] for an enormous period following [[Lucifer]]'s departure.
Its division reflected the separation of authority between Lilith and [[Diablo]], while the resulting instability of the Gate affected the Demons' ability to intervene in the Human World. The artifact consequently became connected with both the internal governance of Hell and the external ambitions of the factions attempting to expand beyond its boundaries.
For Diablo, recovering the complete Key represented an important stage in consolidating political power. Its reunification allowed him to exercise a more complete connection with Hell than either fragment could provide independently, although he continued refusing the obligations of legitimate rulership.
For [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], the artifact possessed a different significance. He did not require it to conquer Hell or receive recognition from its Throne, but needed it to complete his authority and return to the living world without abandoning his newly acquired position.
The recovery mission therefore became one of the most important objectives assigned to his wives during the Demonic War. Although Diablo was surrounded by powerful Demons and operating amid an enormous supernatural conflict, Victor specifically ordered the recovery team to avoid unnecessary fighting and concentrate upon securing the artifact.[5]
Its eventual loss represented a strategic defeat for Diablo, who recognized that his former political position had been taken by Victor while the Key itself was no longer in his possession.
The artifact's recovery also demonstrated that destroying an enemy's military forces was not always necessary to eliminate an important part of their authority. Removing the Key from Diablo accomplished something that ordinary battlefield victories could not: it transferred control of an essential connection to Biblical Hell's governing system.

### Current Status
The Key of Hell continues to be treated as an existing artifact, but its precise location and modern function remain unconfirmed.
Its last major confirmed transfer occurred during the Demonic War, when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s allies recovered it from [[Diablo]]. The artifact was essential to completing the missing portion of his authority over [[Biblical Hell]] and allowing him to travel between the realm and the World of the Living.
However, the later restructuring of Hell fundamentally changed the system to which the Key had originally been connected. Victor merged the former Biblical and Greek Hells into a new infernal realm and directly strengthened the authority of Aline and [[Persephone]] as its Rulers.
No verified passage establishes that the Key was destroyed, permanently transferred to another individual or modified to govern every part of the new afterlife. Accordingly, its original functions are confirmed, while its continuing role within the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s reorganized infernal system remains unresolved.

### Trivia
- The Key was originally associated with [[Lucifer]] Morningstar, the first known legitimate King of [[Biblical Hell]].[1]
- Lucifer deliberately broke it into two pieces before abandoning the realm, distributing the fragments between Lilith and [[Diablo]].
- [[Bael]] considered the artifact something that should not ordinarily have been breakable, making Lucifer's actions especially disruptive to the established Divine system.[3]
- The division destabilized the Gate between Hell and the Human World, creating gaps through which lesser Demons could pass.[1]
- Stronger Demons generally needed to suppress their powers or possess Human bodies to exploit the unstable Gate.
- Possessing one fragment allowed Diablo to perceive the Gate, which appeared as an enormous black door that seemed to be bleeding.[3]
- Diablo could not touch or interact with the Gate while possessing only his original half of the artifact.
- Diablo eventually defeated Lilith and reunited both halves, but did not become Hell's legitimate Ruler.[2]
- Diablo deliberately avoided accepting legitimate rulership because he did not want the responsibilities of governing Hell to obstruct his wider ambitions.
- The Key is explicitly compared with the Helm of [[Hades]], another artifact functioning as a connection point for an [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]]'s Ruler.[2]
- Lilith and Diablo performed portions of Hell's administrative responsibilities through their fragments while the realm lacked a properly recognized Ruler.
- Diablo subsequently employed Lilith as a provisional administrator without her completing the succession trial associated with genuine rulership.[2]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was recognized as the true King of Hell by Albu, [[Zahal]] and the Throne before recovering the Key.[4]
- The Keepers informed Victor that his authority remained incomplete while the Key was in the former King's possession.
- Victor needed the artifact to bypass the restrictions preventing Biblical Hell's King from freely returning to the World of the Living.[5]
- The mission to recover the Key involved Natashia, Kaguya, Natalia, [[Scathach]] and [[Aphrodite]].
- Victor ordered the team to prioritize retrieval rather than engaging Diablo in unnecessary combat.
- By Chapter 683, Diablo recognized that he had lost both the Key and his former position within Hell.[8]
- The Key's recovery formed part of Victor's establishment as the legitimate King of Biblical Hell.
- The artifact should not be confused with the 72 Keys of Solomon, which is a designation associated with the Demonic hierarchy rather than the authority vessel governing Hell.
- Chapter 827 confirms that the former Biblical and Greek Hells were merged into a new infernal realm beneath Victor's authority.[6]
- Victor subsequently appointed Aline and [[Persephone]] as Rulers of the new Hell without a separately depicted transfer of Lucifer's original Key.
- The creator, material composition and complete physical appearance of the Key remain unknown.
- No confirmed event establishes that the Key was destroyed after the merger or that it acquired unrestricted authority over all afterlives.
