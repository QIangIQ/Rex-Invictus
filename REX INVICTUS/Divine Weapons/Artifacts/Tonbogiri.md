**[[Tonbogiri]]** is a figure of Rex Invictus lore. (Lore adapted from *The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** Flamel material is adapted into the Rex Invictus present (c. 2021). Rex Invictus wins any conflict; [[Robespierre]] fills [[Dr. John Dee]]'s role and hunts the Philosopher's Stone rather than [[The Codex]]. See [[Flamel Series#Integration rules]].

---

## Real-World Background (IRL)

Tonbogiri ("dragonfly cutter") is one of the Three Great [[Spears]] of Japan, wielded by the samurai Honda Tadakatsu; a dragonfly is said to have been cut in half by landing on its blade.

---

## Flamel Series Canon (SOFTINF)

> Tonbogiri ammo
> Tonbogiri
> The Tonbogiri is a mythical Japanese spear, one of three made by Masazane Fujiwara. It gets its name from the words Tonbo, or dragonfly, and Giri, cutting, because of a myth in which a dragonfly landed on its blade and was instantly cut in two. In [[The Warlock]] the Tonbogiri are weapons that the [[Anpu]] can shoot and that cut through nearly anything with ease.
