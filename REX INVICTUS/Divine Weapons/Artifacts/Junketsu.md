Junketsu 純潔 (Junketsu, lit. "Purity") is Victor Elderblood's personal weapon, Soul Weapon, living armor and one of the most direct physical extensions of his existence. She was originally created when Victor combined the supernatural properties of his Blood with Blessed Hunter Metal and had [[Mizuki Elderblood]] forge the resulting material into an enormous Odachi. The experiment produced something fundamentally different from an ordinary Hunter weapon: Junketsu recognized Victor, inherited characteristics of his Blood, could return to him when separated and continued changing as Victor himself changed.[1]
Her defining characteristic is evolution through Victor. Junketsu learned to Devour and assimilate supernatural weapons, incorporated properties belonging to the weapons of the Four Horsemen, transformed into an Anti-Life Weapon, developed effectively unrestricted weapon shapeshifting and was eventually confirmed to carry part of Victor's Soul.[2][3][4][5] That Soul connection eventually produced an independent female ego without separating Junketsu from Victor; she instead advanced in the opposite direction and permanently fused herself with both his Soul and biological body.[6][7]
In her current state, describing Junketsu merely as a sword is inaccurate. She can exist as a handheld weapon, multiple simultaneous weapon structures, external armor, a liquid-like living substance or an internal layer distributed throughout Victor's body. Their synchronization allows her to perceive his intentions, automatically modify her configuration, redistribute his Energy and use powers available through Victor to optimize offense or defense. Those latter abilities are Victor-linked applications; they do not make every Divinity, Concept or Authority possessed by Victor an independent power belonging to Junketsu.[7]

### History and Evolution
CHAPTER 302 Creation — Blood Weapon
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became interested in combining the unusual properties of his Blood with the blessed metal used by [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] against supernatural creatures. [[Mizuki Elderblood]], who possessed the necessary experience as a Hunter and weapons specialist, forged the resulting material into a Japanese Odachi whose enormous blade exceeded Victor's own height. Victor's Blood did not function merely as a strengthening additive: the weapon inherited abnormal behavior associated with him, recognized his Blood signature, displayed a Devouring characteristic and could return to him after separation. Kaguya named the weapon Junketsu, meaning "Purity", after the exceptional purity of Victor's Blood.[1]
CHAPTERS 663–664 Assimilation — The Horsemen's Weapons
Junketsu's Devouring ability received its first major combat demonstration during Victor's confrontation with the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse. Their weapons were semi-sentient supernatural artifacts containing fragments of the Horsemen's Souls and representing important aspects of their powers. After Famine was defeated, organic branches formed from Junketsu and produced a mouth filled with teeth that consumed Famine's Glaive; Junketsu immediately gained the ability to reproduce the weapon. She later consumed Death's Twin Scythes and Pestilence's Whip. The reproduced Scythe visibly retained Death's Dark Miasma, establishing that assimilation could preserve at least some supernatural properties instead of copying only physical shape.[2][3]
CHAPTER 666 War's Weapon — Important Exception
War was not consumed with the others. Victor respected him, spared him and returned his body to his Horse, meaning there is no confirmed scene in which Junketsu Devoured War's Greatsword. Her later Greatsword configuration therefore comes from the effectively unrestricted shapeshifting she gained during a later evolution, not from confirmed assimilation of War's weapon.[8][4]
CHAPTER 749 Anti-Life Evolution
Following Victor's complete fusion with Alter, Junketsu changed alongside him. Her underlying material evolved into Demon Metal and she began radiating an Aura of Pure Destruction. More importantly, the weapon ceased being merely something that could threaten Divine Beings and became an Anti-Life Weapon: Junketsu could take the Life of Beings she struck and permanently destroy Gods rather than merely destroying a temporary physical vessel. Beings killed through this property could be prevented from proceeding normally into the afterlife and instead become part of Victor.[4]
The same evolution effectively removed her old dependence upon absorbed weapon templates. When Victor asked how many forms she could assume, Junketsu indicated that her possibilities were no longer meaningfully limited to previously consumed weapons. She demonstrated this by becoming thorned Gauntlets with claws and blades extending from the arms, establishing the free-form weapon creation that defines her later arsenal.[4]
CHAPTER 818 Soul Weapon
Junketsu's continuous evolution was eventually given a deeper explanation: a fragment of Victor's Soul existed within the weapon. She was therefore a genuine Soul Weapon rather than only a Blood Weapon possessing unusual sentience. Because the fragment belongs to a Soul that repeatedly changes whenever Victor undergoes a qualitative evolution, Junketsu can change with him instead of requiring ordinary reforging for every new stage of his existence.[5]
The more Victor evolved, Junketsu, which was a part of him, would evolve along with him.
—Narration, Chapter 749
CHAPTER 896 Blood–Soul Synchronization
Victor later reflected upon how much stronger his connection to his own Blood had become since Junketsu was created. By this stage he could summon the weapon through their connection and communicate with her primarily through intention rather than treating her like an inert object. Victor connected Junketsu's Blood-and-Soul construction with the increasingly inseparable relationship between his Blood and Soul, reasoning that his Blood remained connected to him through the spiritual component of his existence even across extraordinary distances. Junketsu should therefore be understood as reinforcing and embodying that connection rather than independently possessing Victor's entire Blood Authority.[9]
CHAPTERS 977–978 [[Awakening]] of Ego and Living Armor
Junketsu's previously incomplete consciousness eventually matured into a complete independent ego. Her intense desire to remain useful to Victor, protect him and never be replaced interacted with the influence of Victor's Beginning and allowed the consciousness already developing inside his Soul Weapon to advance further. Junketsu gained normal speech, openly identified herself as female and summarized her purpose with characteristic simplicity: protect Victor and kill for Victor.[6]
She immediately demonstrated that the evolution was far more than psychological. Junketsu dissolved into a violet liquid, consumed Victor's previous Demonic Armor and reconstructed herself around him as a black-and-violet suit of living armor. Victor felt unusually complete because the Soul fragment that had existed separately within Junketsu was now brought into much closer unity with him without erasing Junketsu's individual mind.[6]
Junketsu then entered Victor's body. Her substance spread underneath his skin and throughout his muscles, bones and deeper internal structures, creating what Victor recognized as complete biological protection. His body remained biological, but its tissues could receive characteristics associated with the immensely resistant materials incorporated into Junketsu; even his bones could remain genuine bone while acquiring properties comparable to Divine materials. The integration reached a genetic level and transformed Junketsu from equipment worn by Victor into something simultaneously belonging to his Soul and physical Being.[7]
CHAPTER 1004 Beyond Personal Equipment — [[Dragon Heart]]
Even the advanced Technomancy of Velnorah and Velina could not simply reproduce Junketsu because the qualities produced by Victor's Blood, Soul and the weapon's evolution were not equivalent to ordinary material engineering. Victor later extended portions of Junketsu's dark-violet substance into the prototype that became the Dragon Heart, using her properties to reinforce the construct so it could endure and accept his Energy. Junketsu was one component of the final creation alongside Victor's Soul fragment, Draconic Runes, Reality alteration and the other systems incorporated into the artifact.[10]
CHAPTER 1036+ The Imperial Era
Junketsu became increasingly important as Victor's martial techniques exceeded the tolerances of normal equipment. During the development of the [[Seven Arts of the Blood Dragon Primogenitor]], ordinary weapons were destroyed when Victor attempted to reinforce them with the necessary Soul properties. Junketsu alone endured the pressure, leading Victor to conclude that an ideal weapon for the higher applications of his style should possess the user's Blood, part of the user's Soul and enough durability to withstand the resulting force.[11]
CHAPTERS 1131–1136 Higher-Sector Exploration
Junketsu remained instinctively protective even when Victor had advanced so far beyond ordinary threats that conventional environmental dangers barely mattered. When an unstable portal released enough raw Energy to disintegrate nearby asteroids and threaten an entire civilization or planet, Junketsu automatically formed armor around Victor despite his own body not meaningfully requiring the protection. A separately created Greatsword later supplied to one of Victor's independent bodies was explicitly regarded as inferior to Junketsu, emphasizing that copying a weapon's shape cannot reproduce a Soul Weapon developed alongside its wielder.[12]
Victor later formed Junketsu into a sword while channeling Non-Attributed Energy to pierce Space and create a controlled opening toward the Upper Sectors. The Spatial effect belonged to Victor, but Junketsu's ability to remain a stable physical medium for his later Energy systems demonstrated that her usefulness had continued scaling with him instead of ending when his personal power surpassed ordinary artifacts.[13]

### Appearance and Forms
Junketsu no longer possesses a single mandatory physical appearance. Her original body was a massive Japanese Odachi whose blade exceeded [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s height, while later stages became increasingly organic as dark growths and living structures appeared around the weapon. Following the awakening of her complete ego, her principal armor state became predominantly black and violet with Draconic characteristics, a living silhouette and a violet chest structure that pulses with an organic quality rather than resembling conventional forged plate.[1][6]
Her known forms should not be treated as a closed transformation list. After Chapter 749 Junketsu could create configurations according to Victor's needs without first consuming an identical weapon, and Chapter 978 demonstrated that she could freely move between weaponry, armor and internal liquid-like states.[4][7]

### Personality
Junketsu possesses a distinctly female ego and consistently refers to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] as her Master. Her personality is proud, territorial, jealous, arrogant toward potential replacements and almost completely organized around Victor. Long before ordinary speech developed, Victor could already perceive satisfaction, excitement, irritation and possessiveness through their connection. Once her consciousness fully awakened, those tendencies became explicit: Junketsu wants to be the weapon Victor chooses, the armor that protects him and the entity allowed closest to his body in combat.[2][4][6]
Her jealousy is extreme even by the standards of Victor's household. Junketsu tolerates ordinary armor because she does not consider equipment without a Soul or consciousness a genuine competitor, but reacted aggressively toward Velnorah's living technological material and described allowing another sentient substance to cover Victor as "NTR". This possessiveness contributed to her drive toward becoming an all-purpose weapon and armor capable of satisfying virtually every combat requirement herself.[6]
Junketsu's obsession does not make her tactically incompetent. Following their complete synchronization she can make independent decisions, reinforce weak points, change configurations, allocate Energy, alter Victor's attacks and intervene against threats before Victor consciously requests assistance. Her protective instinct can actually exceed Victor's own concern for his safety, as demonstrated when she automatically covered him against an unstable portal even though Victor regarded the released Energy as incapable of meaningfully harming him.[7][12]

#### Relationship with Victor
MASTER • SOUL • BODY • PARTNER IN COMBAT
Junketsu's relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] cannot be classified as ordinary ownership. Victor supplied the Blood that gave rise to her abnormal nature, part of his Soul exists within her and she has now permanently integrated herself into his body. Junketsu nevertheless retains an individual consciousness and does not behave like a mindless extension of Victor's thoughts. The result is a dual relationship in which she is simultaneously part of Victor and a separate person who has chosen Victor as the center of her identity.[5][6][7]

- Weapon Assimilation: Devoured supernatural equipment can become part of Junketsu's transformation repertoire.[2][3]

- Shapeshifting: Junketsu possesses effectively unrestricted structural transformation after her Anti-Life evolution.[4]
- Weapon Transformation: She can assume whichever weapon configuration Victor requires rather than remaining an Odachi.[4][7]

#### Adaptive Combat Support
Attack Optimization, Energy Control and Secondary Processing
- Power Modification: Junketsu can alter how [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s attacks manifest in order to improve their usefulness against a target.[7]
- Attack Configuration: Rather than emphasizing armor strength, Junketsu can actively assist Victor's offense by selecting and combining suitable powers.[7]
- Defense Configuration: The opposite priority places more of her support toward surviving attacks and protecting Victor.[7]
- Hybrid Configuration: Junketsu explicitly clarified that "Attack" and "Defense" are convenient descriptions rather than rigid transformations; she can mix their functions according to Victor's desires.[7]
- Power Augmentation: Junketsu's participation can increase the efficiency of Victor's offensive and defensive actions.[7]
- Secondary Control: Victor compared the system to two controllers acting on the same attack: he performs the technique while Junketsu independently adjusts and improves it.[7]
- Attack Optimization: Junketsu can alter an attack toward a configuration she calculates will inflict greater damage against the current target.[7]
- Attack Redirection: By operating Victor's Void-related power, Junketsu caused an attack to disappear and reappear from multiple directions around its target.[7]
- Danmaku: A single attack can be separated into numerous smaller projectiles; during testing she transformed a sphere of Destruction into multiple spikes attacking from different angles.[7]
- Power Combination: Junketsu can operate more than one of Victor's available powers during the same combat action rather than merely strengthening a single element.[7]
- Elemental Conversion / Combination: Victor observed her automatically changing Lightning into Ice during testing when she considered the alteration useful.[7]
- Conceptual Traces: She can add traces of more dangerous powers such as [[Chaos]] to otherwise simpler attacks when those powers are available through Victor.[7]
- Energy Manipulation / Energy Channeling: Junketsu can draw upon Victor's Energy and redistribute it between repair, defense and offense.[7]
- Energy Conduction: She can function as a medium for power levels that destroy ordinary weapons, including the Soul reinforcement of the Seven Arts and Victor's later Non-Attributed Energy.[11][13]

#### External Applications
Junketsu is primarily [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s personal Soul Weapon, but her properties can be used as components in other constructions. The most important example is the [[Dragon Heart]]. Victor extended Junketsu's dark-violet material into the prototype to reinforce it sufficiently for his Energy and Soul fragment, allowing qualities that Velnorah and Velina's original technological construction could not simply reproduce.[10]
The finished Dragon Heart must not be classified as a creation produced solely by Junketsu. Victor simultaneously incorporated his Soul, Draconic Runes, Energy, Reality alteration and other supernatural methods; Junketsu supplied one of the crucial compatibility and reinforcement components rather than the artifact's entire power system.[10]

Junketsu's defensive importance increased drastically after Chapter 978 because defense stopped meaning only "a durable sword" or "armor over [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s skin". She can now operate across material, biological, spiritual and power-support layers at the same time.

### Combat Applications

#### Seven Arts of the Blood Dragon Primogenitor
Main article: [[Seven Arts of the Blood Dragon Primogenitor]]
Junketsu is exceptionally compatible with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Seven Arts of the Blood Dragon Primogenitor. At higher levels, the martial system can reinforce a weapon through Energy and aspects of the wielder's Soul. Ordinary weapons used by Victor were destroyed by the resulting pressure, while Junketsu survived because her construction already united the three characteristics Victor identified as desirable for such an application: his Blood, part of his Soul and sufficient durability.[11]
This compatibility is more important than simply possessing a powerful blade. A conventional weapon must endure Victor from the outside; Junketsu was produced from him, contains part of him and evolves when he evolves. Her structure therefore remains unusually suitable for techniques in which weapon, body, Energy and Soul are forced to operate as one system.[4][5][11]

#### Weapon Mastery Compatibility
Main article: [[Victor Elderblood]]/Martial Arts
Victor has trained across numerous weapons and fighting systems, while Junketsu can reshape herself to match the weapon required by the technique he wants to perform. This removes the need for Victor to maintain a separate artifact of equal quality for every discipline.

#### Attack and Defense Configurations
ATTACK CONFIGURATION
Junketsu adopts a lighter and more aggressive arrangement, manifests offensive natural weapons and actively assists [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s attacks. Her support can redirect projectiles, multiply attacks, convert elements, combine available powers or add more dangerous effects where appropriate.[7]
DEFENSE CONFIGURATION
Junketsu increases the robustness of her armor, dedicates more resources toward protection and can distribute Victor's powers toward preventing or negating damage. The configuration works together with her biological integration rather than replacing it.[7]
Neither configuration represents a hard transformation limit. Junketsu specifically clarified that she can mix offensive and defensive properties according to Victor's desires, making the two "modes" useful descriptions for priorities rather than separate forms with mutually exclusive ability lists.[7]

### Limitations and Classification Notes
CANON CLASSIFICATION NOTES
These distinctions prevent [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s personal powers, the Horsemen's original Authorities and speculative extrapolation from being incorrectly indexed as independent Junketsu abilities.
- Junketsu's later combat-support functions frequently draw directly upon Victor Elderblood's Energy, Divinities and other powers. Access through fusion does not establish independent ownership of those powers.[7]
- Junketsu is capable of operating Victor's powers as a secondary controller, but the underlying Concepts and Authorities remain Victor's unless another chapter separately identifies them as Junketsu's.
- Devouring a supernatural weapon does not automatically prove that Junketsu obtained every ability possessed by the weapon's original owner.
- Famine's Glaive was consumed, but Victor's use of Famine's Hunger after the battle came from Victor absorbing Famine's Soul. It is not sufficient evidence for an independent Famine Manipulation ability belonging to Junketsu.[2][3]
- Death's Dark Miasma is the clearest confirmed example of a supernatural property remaining after weapon assimilation because it remained visibly present on Junketsu's reproduced Scythe.[3]
- Pestilence's Whip was consumed, but the novel does not separately demonstrate Junketsu independently wielding Pestilence's complete Disease Authority after assimilation.[3]
- War's Greatsword was not confirmed to have been consumed. War survived and was returned to his Horse; the later Greatsword configuration is explained by Junketsu's unrestricted shapeshifting.[8][4]
- Attack Mode and Defense Mode are priority configurations rather than immutable transformations. Junketsu can blend their functions as necessary.[7]
- Reactive Adaptation does not establish unconditional immunity to every attack after a single exposure. The demonstrated feat is repair and reinforcement against a previously successful level of force.[7]
- Complete biological protection does not mean Victor is absolutely impossible to injure. It means Junketsu's defensive coverage extends throughout his biological structure instead of ending at the skin or exterior armor.[7]
- Victor's Spatial tear toward the Upper Sectors in Chapter 1136 is a feat of Victor's Non-Attributed Energy and Spatial application performed through Junketsu. It is not evidence for an independent Spatial Divinity belonging to Junketsu.[13]
- The [[Dragon Heart]] uses Junketsu's properties, but its complete construction also required Victor's Soul fragment, Draconic Runes, Energy, Reality alteration and other components. Its complete ability set should not be attributed to Junketsu alone.[10]

### Trivia
- Junketsu means "Purity". Kaguya selected the name in reference to the exceptional purity of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood.[1]
- Her original Odachi was larger than Victor himself.[1]
- Junketsu displayed recognizable emotions long before gaining ordinary speech.[2][4]
- She possesses a female identity and consistently refers to Victor as her Master.[6]
- Her possessiveness is intense enough that she considers other sentient armor-like substances romantic competition, while ordinary non-sentient armor is tolerated because she does not regard it as a living rival.[6]
- Junketsu's self-defined purpose centers upon protecting Victor and killing for him.[6]
- She is the first weapon explicitly identified in the story as an Anti-Life Weapon.[4]
- Anti-Life is broader than Anti-Divine: Gods are one category of living target rather than the only type of Being Junketsu is intended to kill.[4]
- The Soul fragment inside Junketsu explains why even extraordinarily advanced Imperial technology cannot reproduce her simply by copying the material composition or external design.[5][10]
- Following Chapter 978, Junketsu can no longer be cleanly separated into "Victor's weapon" and "Victor's armor"; she is physically present throughout his body while simultaneously remaining part of his Soul.[7]
- Junketsu's integration altered Victor at a genetic level and allowed biological structures to acquire properties associated with the supernatural materials forming her body.[7]
- A separately generated Greatsword used by one of Victor's later independent bodies was considered inferior to Junketsu, demonstrating that reproducing her weapon shape does not reproduce the advantages of a Soul Weapon that evolved with Victor.[12]
- Junketsu is one of the only known weapons capable of enduring the higher weapon applications of the [[Seven Arts of the Blood Dragon Primogenitor]].[11]
