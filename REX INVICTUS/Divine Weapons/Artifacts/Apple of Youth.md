The Apple of Youth, also referred to as the Apples of Youth, [[Golden Apples]] and [[Idun]]'s Apples, is a powerful Divine fruit originating from the [[Norse Pantheon]] and traditionally protected and cultivated by Idun, the Goddess of Spring and Eternal Youth. Far more than a simple fruit that preserves youth, the Apples function as Divine empowerment resources capable of improving a God's physical condition, supporting their understanding of their Divinity and protecting their body from several forms of supernatural contamination.[1]
The Apples were among the most strategically important resources maintained by [[Asgard]]. During the preparations for Ragnarök, [[Odin]] ordered increasingly large quantities to be distributed among the Norse forces, eventually forcing Idun to warn that continued artificial production would permanently damage the vitality of the entire Norse dimension.[2] During the same period, Nyx Elderblood secretly stole a portion of Asgard's stock and transferred it to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction through War. This allowed Velnorah and the other Dragon Goddesses to personally examine the fruit and recognize its potential for further development.[2]
Following Victor's intervention in the problems affecting Asgard, the Apples were no longer restricted to their original Norse cultivation. Victor's faction discussed using [[Gaia]] to accelerate the production of new batches, and the fruit was eventually planted within Victor Elderblood's own Domain.[3] By Chapter 1098, Apples cultivated under the extraordinarily life-rich conditions of Victor's Domain had become explicitly more potent than those originally produced in the Norse Pantheon, turning the former Asgardian resource into another enhanced product of the [[Elderblood Empire]].[4]

### Description
The original Apples of Youth appear as golden apples and are grown through the power of [[Idun]]. Idun is not merely their keeper; her Divine Power is capable of forcing the trees responsible for the Apples to produce additional fruit beyond their natural rate. The process depends upon the surrounding vitality of the Norse dimension, effectively drawing Life Energy from the environment and transferring it into the fruit.[2]
Because of this relationship, the Apples cannot be treated as an infinitely renewable resource. Under normal circumstances, Idun could accelerate production without causing lasting damage because the vitality consumed by the trees would naturally replenish itself. During the preparations for Ragnarök, however, Idun discovered that [[Asgard]]'s environmental vitality was already declining and was no longer recovering normally. Continuing to force additional harvests would therefore have transformed the land into a barren environment even if [[Odin]] ultimately won the war.[2]
Although originally associated almost exclusively with Asgard and its Gods, the Apples proved capable of benefiting considerably stronger species. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Dragon Goddesses consumed stolen Apples, [[Scathach]] was still capable of perceiving an increase in her Energy, although the effect was comparatively small by the standards of a True Dragon Goddess.[2] This demonstrated that the fruit's empowering properties were not restricted exclusively to ordinary Norse Gods.

### Abilities and Effects

#### Divinity Comprehension
One of the Apple of Youth's most important abilities is its capacity to improve a Divine Being's understanding of their own Divinity. [[Aphrodite]] specifically described the Apples as an Artifact that allowed Norse Gods to obtain a better understanding of their Divinities, making them useful not merely for maintaining health but for strengthening a God's ability to comprehend and develop the Conceptual authority forming the basis of their power.[1]
This property attracted particular attention from Velnorah after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction acquired samples. Her analysis concluded that the Apples could potentially be refined alongside materials belonging to the Elderblood faction to create an Elixir capable of further increasing Divinity comprehension.[2]

#### Physical Reinforcement
The Apples provide vitality and improve the physical condition of whoever consumes them. Their original Norse application allowed Gods to maintain an exceptionally healthy body, while [[Scathach]]—despite already possessing the vastly superior physiology of a True Dragon Goddess—could still perceive a small increase in her Energy after eating one.[1][2]
Velnorah further theorized that combining the fruit with herbs cultivated near [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Dragon Energy-rich volcanic regions could produce an enhanced preparation capable of substantially reinforcing the body. The resulting complete product would contain enough Dragon Energy that ordinary beings could not safely consume it without modification, although diluted versions could potentially be distributed to armies.[2]

#### Youth and Longevity
As its name implies, the fruit possesses powerful properties associated with vitality, youth and longevity. This aspect became much clearer after the species was cultivated within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Domain. The enhanced Elderblood-grown version was powerful enough that an ordinary Mortal consuming a single Apple—or even drinking only a small quantity of its juice—would gain several additional years of life.[4]
The same consumption would also strengthen the Mortal's body, meaning the improved Apple does not merely delay aging but directly enhances biological vitality.[4]

#### Resistance to Harmful Conditions
The original Apples protect the body against numerous harmful supernatural conditions. [[Aphrodite]] specifically identified their healthy-body effect as capable of preventing conditions produced by exceptionally dangerous substances such as:
- Hydra Poison
- Dragon Poison
- Demonic Miasma
- Other comparable supernatural toxins and adverse bodily conditions
The precise upper limit of this protection has never been established, and it should therefore not be interpreted as unconditional immunity against every poison or corruptive substance in existence. Nevertheless, the examples given demonstrate that the fruit can protect against afflictions that would be extremely dangerous even to supernatural beings.[1]

#### Energy Enhancement
Consumption can directly increase the consumer's Energy. This was demonstrated when [[Scathach]] ate one of the Apples stolen from [[Asgard]] and immediately detected an increase in her Energy, although she emphasized that the increase was small by the standards of herself and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s other Dragon Goddesses.[2]
Because an already enormously powerful True Dragon Goddess could still perceive its effect, the increase would logically be considerably more valuable when used by weaker Gods or supernatural soldiers. [[Odin]] consequently reserved the remaining Apples for Asgard's highest-ranking elites during Ragnarök.[2]

### Production

#### Norse Apples
The original Apples were produced from trees controlled by [[Idun]]. Their creation required enormous quantities of environmental vitality drawn from the Norse dimension. Idun could accelerate their growth through her Divine Power, but the process remained dependent on the health of the surrounding world rather than creating the fruit's vitality from nothing.[2]
During Ragnarök's preparation, [[Odin]]'s continuous demand for additional Apples caused Idun's reserves to fall rapidly. When he demanded that every available resource be committed to the war, Idun warned that further forced production could permanently exhaust the land because the vitality taken by the trees was no longer regenerating.[1][2]
At the time, [[Asgard]] possessed 722 Apples in reserve. Nyx secretly removed part of the stock while remaining concealed inside Odin's war room. By the time Odin asked for the number again, Nyx privately remarked that only 666 remained, having already transferred the stolen Apples to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction through War.[2]

#### Elderblood Cultivation
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] stabilized the problem affecting the Norse dimension, his family considered increasing Apple production without repeating [[Odin]]'s environmentally destructive method. Velnorah proposed using [[Gaia]], whose authority as the Mother of Earth could stimulate the trees naturally enough to accelerate two additional harvests. Her estimate placed those two batches at approximately 2,500 Apples.[3]
This demonstrates an important difference between the original Norse production method and Victor's later approach: rather than relying exclusively upon [[Idun]] forcibly extracting greater quantities of vitality from an already weakened environment, Victor's faction possessed multiple Nature-based Divine authorities capable of assisting the cultivation process.

#### Enhanced Elderblood Apple
By Chapter 1098, the fruit had been successfully planted and cultivated inside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s own Domain. The environment surrounding Dragons Nest possessed an extraordinarily high concentration of Nature-aligned species and supernatural vitality, further amplified by Victor's own existence. Consequently, naturally grown food within the Domain developed effects far beyond equivalent produce cultivated elsewhere.[4]
The Apple cultivated there was explicitly stated to be much more potent than the Apples previously produced in the [[Norse Pantheon]]. Its juice possessed a golden appearance and was sufficiently concentrated that even a single drop could meaningfully affect an ordinary Mortal.[4]
Its demonstrated effects include:
- Granting a Mortal several additional years of lifespan.
- Strengthening the Mortal's body.
- Retaining enough vitality for its processed juice to possess the fruit's supernatural effects.
- Possessing substantially greater potency than the original Norse-grown fruit.[4]
Despite this improvement, the enhanced Apple had effectively no meaningful empowering effect on Victor himself. By this stage Victor had become so biologically and metaphysically advanced that he regarded the supernatural juice as little more than ordinary apple juice, consuming it for its flavor rather than its enhancement properties.[4]

### History

#### Norse Pantheon
The Apple of Youth was originally one of [[Idun]]'s responsibilities within the [[Norse Pantheon]]. Idun's status as Goddess of Spring and Eternal Youth made her the protector and producer of the Apples, and their ability to improve Divinity comprehension and maintain the health of [[Asgard]]'s Gods made her strategically important to the entire Pantheon.[1]
Immediately before Ragnarök, [[Odin]] ordered Idun to use her stock to strengthen Asgard's principal forces. The demand became severe enough that Idun interrupted Odin's war council to warn him that her supplies were being consumed too quickly.[1] Odin initially intended to exhaust the available resources regardless of the consequences, believing that even losing future harvests for a million years would be preferable to losing Ragnarök. Idun corrected him, explaining that the danger was considerably greater: the trees were consuming the remaining vitality of Asgard itself, and continued forced production could leave the dimension permanently barren.[2]

#### Theft by Nyx
While secretly observing [[Odin]]'s war council through her Divinity, Nyx Elderblood began taking the [[Golden Apples]] without the Norse Gods noticing. The stolen fruit was transferred between dimensions by War, allowing Violet, [[Scathach]], Velnorah, Eleonor and others within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction to eat and study them while [[Idun]] and Odin remained completely unaware of the theft.[2]
The group immediately recognized the fruit's strategic value. Velnorah's analysis concluded that it could be combined with resources from Victor's world to create superior elixirs for Divinity comprehension and physical reinforcement, while [[Aphrodite]] believed Idun's abilities could become considerably more valuable when combined with those of [[Gaia]], [[Demeter]] and other Nature-oriented members of their faction. These discoveries strengthened the group's decision that Idun herself was worth bringing into their sphere.[2]

#### Use by Victor's Faction
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction did not merely hoard the stolen Apples. They consumed them directly, experimented with their possible applications and processed them into drinks. During Victor's later vacation, [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] was shown casually drinking golden apple juice, demonstrating how a resource once carefully controlled by [[Asgard]] had already become available within Victor's household.[5]
The faction also discussed deliberately expanding the supply. Once the vitality problem affecting Asgard was stabilized, Velnorah proposed allowing [[Gaia]] to accelerate new harvests, potentially producing another 2,500 Apples while simultaneously keeping Asgard strong enough for Ragnarök to continue.[3]

#### Cultivation in Victor's Domain
At some point following the Norse conflict, the Apple was successfully transplanted into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s own Domain. Rather than remaining dependent upon [[Asgard]], the Elderblood family began producing the fruit within their own territory.[4]
The transplantation also caused a natural evolution in quality. The immense vitality of Victor's world, its concentration of Nature-based species and Victor's own presence resulted in an Apple considerably more powerful than its Norse predecessor. Victor was later shown drinking its golden juice while noting that the fruit had become substantially more potent than the ones produced in the [[Norse Pantheon]].[4]
The Apple of Youth therefore progressed from an exclusive Divine resource controlled by Asgard into one of the many supernatural resources assimilated, cultivated and ultimately improved by the [[Elderblood Empire]].

### Limitations
- The original Apple requires enormous quantities of environmental vitality to produce. Excessive artificial harvesting can drain the surrounding land and potentially cause permanent ecological damage.[2]
- Its effectiveness depends greatly upon the strength of the consumer. While extremely valuable to ordinary Gods and Mortals, the original fruit provided only a comparatively minor Energy increase to a True Dragon Goddess such as [[Scathach]].[2]
- The enhanced fruit cultivated in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Domain still had no meaningful strengthening effect on Victor himself by Chapter 1098 because his existence had already surpassed what the Apple could meaningfully improve.[4]
- The Dragon-Energy-enhanced Elixir proposed by Velnorah could not safely be consumed at full concentration by incompatible weaker species; according to her analysis, such beings could physically explode from the incompatibility, requiring diluted versions instead.[2]

### Trivia
- Although the fruit is introduced as the Apple of Youth, Chapter 944 repeatedly describes [[Idun]]'s harvested fruits as [[Golden Apples]] and Idun's Apples, indicating that these terms refer to the same resource.[2]
- Nyx's theft was unusually extensive. Idun reported a stock of 722 Apples, while Nyx privately corrected the number to 666 after her Heralds had already removed several batches.[2]
- Violet's initial reaction upon learning about Idun and the Apples was to suggest kidnapping the Goddess because of how useful her ability would be to their faction.[1]
- Velnorah later reached essentially the same conclusion after analyzing the fruit, believing that combining Idun's abilities with those of [[Gaia]], [[Demeter]] and other Nature specialists could produce resources unavailable anywhere else.[2]
- Chapter 1098 renders the later cultivated fruit as a "Tounn Apple" in the available chapter text. The same passage immediately identifies it as an Apple planted in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Domain and compares its potency directly with those produced in the [[Norse Pantheon]]. Due to the inconsistent spelling compared with the previously established Idun/Idunn terminology, this article treats it as the later Elderblood-cultivated form of the Apple rather than establishing "Tounn Apple" as a separate confirmed species.[4]
- Unlike the original Norse Apples, the Elderblood-grown version benefits from the extraordinary ambient vitality of Victor's planet, causing the fruit to improve naturally simply by being cultivated there.[4]
