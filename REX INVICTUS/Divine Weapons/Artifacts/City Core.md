The City Core is a Divine Artifact created by [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]] to monitor and secure a defined territory. It represents one of the earliest examples of the large-scale administrative technology later adopted by the [[Elderblood Empire]], combining surveillance, information recording and territorial access control within a single autonomous artifact.[1]
Two principal versions are known. The complete violet-and-black City Core contains the full set of territorial-security functions, including monitoring activity throughout its jurisdiction and preventing unauthorized or hostile Beings from entering. A downgraded red-and-black City Core removes most of these functions and specializes almost entirely in recording everything occurring within its territory, allowing it to operate at a dramatically lower Energy cost.[1]
The City Core first appeared when [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] provided the [[Blood God Religion]] with Core technology for its Earth base. Rather than turning the location into a fortress protected by Divine-level defenses, [[Valeria Alekerth]] deliberately requested that the base remain only as secure as highly prepared Mortals could realistically make it. This allowed hostile observers and agents of rival Pantheons to believe infiltration remained possible, while the installed City Core could record their activity without revealing the full extent of Imperial security technology.[1]
The technology later served as the direct conceptual and technological predecessor of the [[World Core]]. Once [[Akasha Elderblood]] made large-scale calculation and Core management significantly more efficient, Velnorah expanded the City Core principle from individual cities and territories to entire planets.[2]

### Appearance
Known City Cores possess a simple cubic form.
When Violet introduced the technology to Valeria, she produced two cubes:
- a violet cube with black tones;
- and a red cube with black tones.[1]
The color identifies the version and therefore the functions available to the artifact.
No precise measurements have been given for either model.
The downgraded red Core is accompanied by a separate bracelet that provides the Energy necessary for its operation.[1]

### Creation
The City Core was created by [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]].[1]
Its original purpose was straightforward:
To keep watch over an entire territory.
Unlike conventional surveillance devices that observe a limited number of cameras, sensors or entryways, the City Core treats the territory assigned to it as a unified area under observation.
Violet's explanation establishes that the technology was already mature enough to perform several complex tasks simultaneously:
- record everything occurring within its territory;
- monitor potential betrayal;
- identify unauthorized individuals;
- distinguish hostile intruders;
- and enforce territorial access rules.[1]
The Core therefore combines information-gathering and security rather than operating as a simple recording device.

### Variants
Two principal models have been directly demonstrated.

#### Violet City Core
The violet-and-black City Core is the complete version of the artifact.[1]
Violet described it as the model possessing:
all functions
Its known capabilities include:
- complete territorial recording;
- monitoring the thoughts of traitors;
- identifying unauthorized individuals;
- identifying hostile Beings;
- preventing unauthorized entry;
- preventing hostile invasion;
- and maintaining a direct connection to another artifact located within Velnorah's territory.[1]
These extensive functions come at a cost.
The violet model consumes significantly more Energy than the downgraded red version.[1]

##### Connection to Velnorah's Artifact
The complete City Core is directly connected to another artifact located within Velnorah's territory.[1]
The identity, appearance and exact purpose of this superior or linked artifact have not been explained.
Because of this, it should not automatically be identified as:
- the [[Dragon Heart]];
- a [[World Core]];
- Akasha;
- or another later Imperial system
without additional evidence.
What is confirmed is simply that the complete City Core does not necessarily function as an isolated artifact and can participate in a wider connected system.[1]

#### Red City Core
The red-and-black City Core is a deliberately downgraded model.[1]
Its principal confirmed function is:
Recording everything occurring within its territory.
[1]
It lacks the full defensive and access-control systems of the violet version.
This reduction produces a major advantage:
Far lower Energy consumption.
[1]
Violet specifically selected this version for the Earth base of the [[Blood God Religion]] because the objective there was observation rather than creating an obviously impossible Divine fortress.

##### Bracelet Power System
The red City Core is supplied with a bracelet used to power the artifact.[1]
Violet explained that the Core itself does not require a large amount of Energy.
Instead, the bracelet consumes Energy from its bearer and uses that power to maintain the Core's operation.[1]
The system can therefore be represented as:

The exact amount of Energy required and the maximum duration one bearer can sustain the artifact have not been stated.

### Functions

#### Territory Surveillance
The City Core's primary function is monitoring an assigned territory.[1]
Rather than observing only a specific building or doorway, the artifact is intended to keep watch across the entire area under its jurisdiction.
The maximum size of a standard City Core territory has not been directly quantified.
Its name and later evolution into the [[World Core]] suggest that the technology was originally conceived around city-scale or comparable territories, but Chapter 1023 only directly refers to an entire territory.

#### Event Recording
Both known versions can record activity occurring within their operating area.[1]
The red model is specifically reduced almost entirely to this one function.
This makes the artifact useful for:
- intelligence gathering;
- reconstructing incidents;
- monitoring personnel;
- investigating infiltration;
- and identifying actions occurring without direct witnesses.
The exact storage capacity and duration of recorded information have not been explained.

#### Thought Monitoring
The complete City Core can record information extending even to:
the thoughts of traitors
[1]
This is one of the artifact's most intrusive known capabilities.
The wording demonstrates that City Core surveillance is not limited to ordinary visual or auditory observation.
However, the exact mechanism remains unknown.
The novel has not explained whether the Core:
- continuously reads every thought inside its territory;
- only detects thoughts associated with betrayal;
- identifies hostile intent;
- or records information through another metaphysical system.
Therefore, the artifact should not automatically be described as possessing unrestricted omniscient telepathy over everyone within its territory.

#### Unauthorized Entry Prevention
The complete violet City Core can prevent unauthorized Beings from entering its territory.[1]
This implies some form of permission or recognition system distinguishing acceptable individuals from unauthorized ones.
The chapter does not explain:
- how authorization is registered;
- whether permission is based upon identity, Energy signature or another criterion;
- who possesses administrative authority over the Core;
- or how the system reacts to someone whose identity cannot be recognized.

#### Hostile Entry Prevention
The full City Core can also prevent hostile Beings from invading the protected territory.[1]
This is distinct from simple authorization.
An individual may therefore theoretically be treated differently according to whether they are merely unregistered or actively hostile.
The exact method through which hostility is determined has not been explained.
Given that the same Core can detect the thoughts of traitors, its security functions may involve some level of intent analysis, but the story has not explicitly confirmed the exact connection.

#### Territorial Security
The combination of:
- surveillance;
- recording;
- access control;
- thought monitoring;
- and hostile-entry prevention
allows the full City Core to function as a compact territorial-security system.
It can therefore reduce the amount of manpower necessary to constantly patrol every entrance and monitor every individual inside a protected area.

### Energy Consumption
Energy requirements differ dramatically between the two known models.

#### Full Version
The violet City Core consumes the greatest amount of Energy because it maintains all available functions simultaneously.[1]
These functions include active territorial protection and connection to another artifact inside Velnorah's territory.
The chapter does not state the full version's actual Energy source.

#### Downgraded Version
The red model is significantly more economical.[1]
Because it primarily records events rather than maintaining the complete protective system, its Energy requirements are low enough to be supplied through an individual bearer's bracelet.[1]
This demonstrates that Velnorah designed City Core technology with different operational requirements in mind rather than forcing every location to support the most expensive configuration.

### Blood God Religion Deployment
The first directly shown deployment of City Core technology occurred at a [[Blood God Religion]] base on Earth.

#### Reason for Deployment
[[Valeria Alekerth]] recovered a suspicious artifact linked to activity involving the Celts and other Gods.
She correctly refused to touch it without appropriate protection because supernatural objects belonging to unknown Pantheons could contain dangerous curses.[1]
[[Violet Elderblood Snow]] later confirmed that the artifact was indeed cursed and discussed increasing the security of the [[Blood God Religion]]'s Earth base.
Violet initially suggested asking Velnorah to reinforce the base to the same level as the primary headquarters.[1]
Valeria disagreed.

#### Deliberately Limited Security
Valeria argued that the Earth base should remain defended only at the level expected from:
prepared Mortals
[1]
Her reasoning was strategic.
If the base suddenly possessed obviously Divine defenses, hostile Pantheons would recognize that infiltration was impossible and change their methods.
By leaving apparent weaknesses in place, the Religion could:
- attract spies;
- observe who was investigating them;
- identify hostile factions;
- and collect intelligence on enemies willing to infiltrate the base.[1]
Violet accepted Valeria's argument.
She therefore provided both City Core variants but ordered the red version installed at the base.[1]
The red model could record everything that occurred while avoiding the much stronger access-denial functions of the full Core.
This deployment demonstrates that City Core variants can be selected according to strategic purpose rather than simply installing the most powerful available model.

### Strategic Use
The [[Blood God Religion]] deployment reveals an important aspect of City Core technology:
More security is not always strategically superior.
The violet version would have made the base significantly harder to infiltrate.
However, that would also have eliminated an opportunity to identify hostile agents.
The red version therefore functioned as an intelligence tool.
Instead of preventing enemy movement, it allowed enemy movement to occur within a territory whose activity was being recorded.
This makes City Cores useful for two fundamentally different approaches:

This flexibility is one of the defining advantages of the system.

### Administration
The exact interface used to configure a City Core has not been shown.
Violet was able to:
- produce the Cores;
- explain their functions;
- assign one to the [[Blood God Religion]];
- and allow Valeria to determine who would monitor the installed artifact.[1]
This establishes that information gathered by the Core can be observed by designated personnel rather than being accessible exclusively to Velnorah.
However, the exact administrative hierarchy, command interface and permission system remain unknown.

### Relationship with Akasha
[[Akasha Elderblood]] later became important to the continued development of Core technology.
By Chapter 1100, Akasha was capable of managing the Empire's City Cores far more efficiently and without wasting excessive quantities of Energy.[2]
This improvement in large-scale calculation and coordination was one of the factors that encouraged Velnorah to develop the next generation of the technology.
The City Core itself predates this development and does not depend upon Akasha for its basic functions.

### Development into the World Core
Main article: [[World Core]]
The City Core eventually became the technological basis for the World Core.[2]
The distinction is primarily one of intended operational scale.
The City Core was built to supervise a local territory.
The later World Core expanded the same basic principle to the terrain of an entire planet.[2]
This article does not duplicate the functions of the World Core, as the planetary model later incorporated infrastructure and Energy-distribution systems that go substantially beyond what has been directly demonstrated for the original City Core.
The important developmental relationship is simply:
CITY CORE

The City Core therefore represents the foundational stage of Velnorah's Core technology rather than merely a weaker World Core.

### Limitations

#### Territorial Scope
The City Core is designed around a limited defined territory.
It was not originally intended to govern an entire planet.
This limitation is precisely what later distinguished it from the [[World Core]].[2]

#### Energy Consumption
The complete version consumes a considerable amount of Energy.[1]
The red model solves this problem partly by removing most functions.

#### Reduced Model Lacks Full Protection
The red City Core primarily records information.[1]
It should not be attributed the violet model's full access-control and anti-invasion functions.

#### Bearer Dependency
The downgraded version depends upon a bracelet drawing Energy from its bearer.[1]
If the bearer cannot continue supplying Energy, continued operation may become impossible, although the exact failure conditions have not been shown.

#### Unknown Resistance Ceiling
The story has not established the maximum strength of a Being the full City Core can prevent from entering.
Therefore, its ability to stop unauthorized or hostile targets should not be interpreted as absolute protection against:
- [[Primordial Gods]];
- [[True Dragons]];
- Overlords;
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]];
- or arbitrarily superior Beings.

#### Unknown Thought-Monitoring Limits
The Core can monitor the thoughts of traitors, but its complete mental-surveillance mechanics remain unexplained.
It should not be treated as universal unrestricted mind-reading without additional evidence.
