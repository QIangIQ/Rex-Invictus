Cain's Dagger is a unique Conceptual artifact associated with the First Murder, the killing committed by Cain against his brother. The dagger carries the accumulated supernatural consequences of that event: the Sin of Man produced by the first act of murder, traces of the Holiness of [[Lucifer]] Morningstar, Divine power supplied by the Heavenly Father, a record of the First Murder itself, and a fragment of Cain's Soul that remained within the weapon when the act was committed.[1][2]
Its most unusual property is not simple destructive power but the natural coexistence of opposing supernatural principles. Holiness and Sin exist inside the dagger in harmony rather than consuming or destabilizing one another, making it one of the clearest known examples of an object existing simultaneously through both sides of Balance.[1][2] This characteristic made the artifact valuable enough that the Heavenly Father kept and studied it for millennia without discovering the principle responsible for its stability, while Victor Elderblood recognized its significance almost immediately after obtaining it.[1]

### Description
Cain's Dagger is an ancient weapon whose outward form is far less important than the supernatural phenomena contained within it. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] first examined the artifact, his senses immediately detected an Aura containing both Holiness and Sin. He also perceived traces associated with Angels, eventually identifying the connection to [[Lucifer]].[1]
The dagger's apparently contradictory supernatural characteristics do not exist in an unstable fusion. Its Holy and Sinful aspects are naturally balanced, allowing both forces to remain present without either destroying the other.[1][2]
Because of this unusual condition, the [[Heavenly Father]] had retained the dagger for an immense period specifically to study how two apparently opposing energies could coexist in one artifact without producing the type of imbalance normally associated with unnatural combinations of opposite forces.[1]

### Origin
Cain's Dagger is the weapon associated with the First Murder. Cain used it when committing the first recorded act of murder, causing the weapon to become permanently connected to that event.[1][2]
The act did more than stain the weapon with ordinary Blood. The first Sin of Murder became recorded within the dagger itself, while part of Cain's Soul remained attached to the weapon at the moment the murder was committed.[2]
Cain had also been raised by [[Lucifer]] Morningstar, one of the [[Heavenly Father]]'s children. Although Lucifer had Fallen, he retained his inherent Holiness, and the dagger was consequently exposed to Lucifer's Holy power.[1]
The Heavenly Father later placed his own Energy into the dagger. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] determined that this additional Divine influence was responsible for strengthening the artifact's Positive aspect until it existed in equilibrium with its Sinful and Negative characteristics.[2]
The artifact therefore developed through several naturally occurring supernatural events rather than through one deliberate process of artificial construction:
- Cain committed the First Murder with the dagger, imprinting the first Sin of Murder upon it.[2]
- A fragment of Cain's Soul remained within the artifact at the moment of the murder.[2]
- Cain's connection to Lucifer exposed the dagger to the Holiness of the Fallen Brightest Angel.[1]
- The Heavenly Father later infused the artifact with his own [[Divine Energy]], reinforcing its Positive side.[2]
- These influences eventually produced an artifact in which forces associated with opposite sides of Balance could coexist naturally.[2]

### Nature and Composition

#### First Murder
The dagger contains a supernatural record of Cain's murder. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Divinity associated with Assassination immediately reacted when he examined the artifact, allowing him to recognize that the First Murder had become embedded within the weapon rather than existing merely as a historical association.[2]
The dagger is consequently more than the physical object Cain happened to use. The event itself left a lasting metaphysical imprint upon it.

#### Cain's Soul Fragment
A fragment of Cain's Soul remained inside the dagger when he committed the First Murder.[2]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] initially detected this through the reaction of his Divinity, while [[Amara Elderblood]] and [[Roxanne Elderblood]] were able to examine the artifact more deeply after their Positive and Negative Energies synchronized. Amara confirmed the presence of the Soul fragment, while Roxanne identified additional emotional residue attached to it.[2]
The fragment does not mean Cain's complete Soul is imprisoned inside the dagger. Only a portion of him remained behind as a consequence of the act.

#### Repressed Emotions
The Soul fragment retains Cain's repressed emotions.[2]
These emotional remnants form part of the Negative aspect contained within the artifact and are inseparable from the event that produced its metaphysical nature.

#### Sin of Man
The dagger was permanently tainted by the Sin of Man through its use in the First Murder.[1]
This Sin is one of the principal forces contributing to the artifact's Negative side. It is not merely symbolic guilt assigned retrospectively to Cain's actions; [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and other supernatural beings can directly perceive the resulting supernatural characteristic within the weapon.

#### Lucifer's Holiness
Cain was raised by [[Lucifer]] Morningstar, and traces of Lucifer's power remained upon the dagger.[1]
Lucifer's Fall did not completely remove the Holiness inherent to his existence. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was already familiar with this phenomenon through other Fallen Angels and recognized that the power on the dagger retained Holy characteristics despite originating from Lucifer.[1]

#### Heavenly Father's Energy
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] determined that the dagger had also been directly imbued with Energy from the [[Heavenly Father]].[2]
This additional Divine influence strengthened the Positive aspect of the artifact sufficiently for it to exist in equilibrium with its Sinful qualities. Victor specifically identified the Heavenly Father's contribution as the reason the Positive side remained balanced.[2]

#### Natural Duality
Cain's Dagger was not produced by forcibly combining incompatible powers through an artificial process. Its contradictory characteristics accumulated through the events surrounding Cain, [[Lucifer]], the First Murder and the [[Heavenly Father]] until the artifact naturally became something belonging to both sides of Balance.[2]
This distinction became extremely important to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] because it separated the dagger from beings such as [[Diablo]], whose combination of opposite supernatural forces had been produced through abnormal artificial circumstances and threatened the surrounding Balance.

### Powers and Abilities
- Conceptual Artifact: Cain's Dagger possesses metaphysical properties produced by one of Humanity's foundational supernatural events. The First Murder is recorded within the weapon itself rather than merely being part of its history.[2]
- Sin Imbuement: The dagger is permanently stained by the Sin produced through the First Murder, giving it a powerful Negative supernatural aspect.[1][2]
- Holiness: Despite carrying the First Murder and Sin, the dagger simultaneously contains powerful Holiness originating from [[Lucifer]] and the [[Heavenly Father]].[1][2]
- Positive and Negative Energy: The artifact possesses characteristics belonging to both sides of the cosmic duality. The Heavenly Father's Divine power contributes to its Positive aspect, while the First Murder, Sin and Cain's emotional residue contribute to the opposing side.[2]
- Natural Balance: The opposing supernatural characteristics within the dagger coexist without either side consuming the other. This makes Cain's Dagger a naturally balanced artifact rather than an unstable artificial fusion.[1][2]
- Dual-Nature Existence: Cain's Dagger can contain forces traditionally regarded as contradictory without automatically becoming an existence that destroys Balance. Its existence demonstrated that possessing characteristics from both sides of duality is not inherently prohibited; the true danger arises when one side overwhelms the other and causes the balanced structure to collapse.[2]
- Soul Containment: A fragment of Cain's Soul remains within the artifact as a consequence of the First Murder.[2] The dagger has not been shown independently absorbing additional Souls, so this property should not be interpreted as general Soul Absorption.
- Emotional Imprint: Cain's repressed emotions remain attached to the Soul fragment contained within the dagger.[2] These emotions can be perceived by sufficiently capable beings examining the artifact.
- Event Recording: The dagger preserves a supernatural record of the First Murder itself. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] distinguished the recorded Sin from the Soul fragment, confirming that both the event and a portion of Cain remained imprinted within the weapon.[2]
- Divinity Resonance: Victor's Divinity associated with Assassination reacted directly to Cain's Dagger because of its connection to the First Murder.[2] This allowed him to perceive properties of the artifact that were not immediately apparent through ordinary observation.
- Conceptual Resonance: The dagger can resonate with Concepts related to killing, murder and assassination because the First Murder became metaphysically recorded within it.[2] The artifact has not yet been shown automatically amplifying every such Divinity, so its demonstrated effect is best treated as resonance rather than unrestricted enhancement.
- Supernatural Energy Signature: Victor immediately perceived both Holiness and Sin when the Heavenly Father manifested the weapon, while deeper examination revealed its Angelic, Divine, Soul and Balance-related components.[1][2]
- Balance Manifestation: When Victor simultaneously used the Positive Energy supplied by Amara and Negative Energy supplied by Roxanne while studying the principles revealed through the dagger, the two forces synchronized into the form of a Yin-Yang symbol instead of annihilating one another.[2] The manifestation was produced by Victor using the two [[World Trees]]' Energies rather than the dagger independently generating a Yin-Yang construct.
- Conceptual Insight: Cain's Dagger functions as an unusually valuable object of study for beings attempting to understand Balance, Positivity, Negativity and Creation. The Heavenly Father retained it for millennia for this purpose, while Victor's analysis directly advanced his understanding of these principles.[1][2]

### Relationship with Balance
Cain's Dagger became important because it contradicted the simplified assumption that opposite cosmic forces cannot coexist inside one existence without violating Balance.
The [[Heavenly Father]] had already recognized this anomaly. Holiness and Sin existed in complete harmony within the same artifact, making the dagger appear to violate the ordinary understanding of the world's dual structure. He consequently preserved and studied it for millennia but was unable to determine why the System tolerated its existence.[1]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] reached a different conclusion after studying the dagger alongside Roxanne and Amara. Both [[World Trees]] represented complementary sides of a naturally occurring planetary cycle. A Positive World Tree nurtures a developing world and its Life, while a corresponding Negative World Tree eventually arises alongside sentient death and powerful Negative emotions, becoming responsible for the world's spiritual and infernal processes. The two opposing structures are not automatically enemies; together they form part of the same functioning cycle.[2]
The dagger followed a comparable principle. It had not been created by forcibly making one side consume or dominate the other. Holiness, Sin, Cain's Soul, [[Lucifer]]'s influence and the Heavenly Father's Energy had accumulated through natural supernatural events until the weapon existed through both sides simultaneously.[2]
Victor therefore realized that duality itself exists to support Creation, while Balance prevents either half of that duality from completely consuming the other.[2]
He demonstrated the principle by synchronizing Amara's Positive Energy with Roxanne's Negative Energy. The two forces formed a balanced Yin-Yang structure. When Victor deliberately increased the Negative side, it began consuming the Positive side; the Positive force resisted, destabilizing the entire structure until it collapsed.[2]
The experiment clarified that possessing both opposing qualities is not automatically what makes an existence a threat to Balance. The greater danger is allowing one side to erase the other and destroy the dual structure Creation depends upon.[2]
Victor further concluded that the System's judgment cannot depend solely upon the raw quantity of opposing Energy within an individual. His own existence combines powers associated with multiple sides of Balance without receiving the same treatment as artificial abnormalities such as [[Diablo]], causing him to suspect that the nature, development and personality of the individual are also relevant to whether the System regards that existence as a threat.[2]

### Importance to Victor
Cain's Dagger was particularly valuable to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] because its nature closely reflected his own developing existence.
Victor already possessed powers associated with both sides of the cosmic Balance. Unlike [[Diablo]], however, his condition developed naturally through his evolution, relationships and accumulated powers rather than through an artificial forced fusion. The dagger gave him a second major example demonstrating that natural coexistence between opposing forces was possible.[2]
Studying it also caused a measurable increase in Victor's understanding of his deeper Divinities. His proficiency with Beginning and Negativity increased slightly after his realization concerning Creation and Balance, leading him to conclude that advancement in such fundamental Divinities depends heavily upon comprehension rather than merely accumulating additional Energy.[2]
The discovery also raised additional questions concerning the purpose of the System, the [[Akashic Records]] and the Primordial Beings' extreme concern with maintaining Balance. Victor concluded that the System appeared to observe possible threats and record Creation, but he did not yet understand the ultimate reason the Akashic Records preserve everything or why Creation requires such aggressive protection.[2]

### Acquisition
Cain's Dagger originally belonged to the [[Heavenly Father]], who had kept the artifact for millennia in an unsuccessful attempt to understand the equilibrium between its opposing supernatural energies.[1]
During the Heavenly Father's visit to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Hell, Victor offered his guests a black Imperial card containing 1,000,000 Knull so they could experience Hell's attractions without lacking local currency. The Heavenly Father was uncomfortable accepting what would effectively be another gift and instead offered to exchange an artifact.[1]
He manifested Cain's Dagger and explained its connection to the First Murder, Sin, Divine Holiness and [[Lucifer]]. Victor immediately noticed that its opposing forces existed in unusual harmony and accepted the exchange.[1]
Recognizing that the artifact was dramatically more valuable than the original gift, Victor increased the balance of the card by three additional zeroes, raising its value from 1,000,000 to 1,000,000,000 Knull.[1]
The transaction therefore valued Cain's Dagger at one billion Knull, although the exchange was not a normal marketplace purchase. Victor assigned that amount because he considered the artifact's metaphysical properties worth the extraordinary price.[1]
The Heavenly Father immediately realized Victor had perceived something in the dagger that he himself had failed to understand and asked Victor to share whatever he discovered. Victor made no promise, noting that the information might not be useful to the Heavenly Father even if explained.[1]

### Current Status
After completing his initial examination, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] created a display case from Divine metal and placed Cain's Dagger inside it.[2]
He then sealed the artifact through Draconic Language, declaring that only individuals authorized by him could remove the dagger from its display.[2]
The weapon therefore remains one of Victor's protected possessions rather than an artifact available for unrestricted use by the wider [[Elderblood Empire]].

### Limitations
- Not a Demonstrated Ultimate Combat Weapon: Despite its extraordinary conceptual history, Cain's Dagger has not yet been shown performing attacks comparable to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s strongest weapons. Its primary demonstrated value lies in its metaphysical composition and the information it contains.[2]
- Soul Fragment Only: The artifact contains only a fragment of Cain's Soul, not Cain's entire spiritual existence.[2]
- No Demonstrated Automatic Divinity Amplification: Victor's Assassination-related Divinity reacts to the First Murder recorded within the dagger, but the artifact has not been explicitly shown automatically increasing the raw power of Murder or Assassination Divinities.[2]
- Balance Manifestation Requires External Energies: The Yin-Yang manifestation observed during Victor's discovery was produced through Roxanne's and Amara's Negative and Positive Energies. Cain's Dagger served as the object whose nature led to the discovery, not as an independently demonstrated producer of the Yin-Yang construct.[2]
- Restricted Access: Victor sealed the dagger so that only people personally authorized by him can remove it from its display.[2]
