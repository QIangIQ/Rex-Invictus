Ambrosia is a rare Divine fruit historically cultivated on [[Olympus|Mount Olympus]] and accumulated by the [[Greek Pantheon]]. It possesses powerful life-extending properties and can be further enhanced through a God's [[Magic]] to dramatically increase its effects on Mortals. A sufficiently empowered Ambrosia can keep a Human's physical body alive indefinitely unless they are killed by an external cause, effectively granting a form of physical immortality.[1]
Ambrosia does not, however, transform its consumer into a supernatural species or grant the complete immortality possessed by beings such as Vampires. It does not provide extraordinary regenerative abilities, new supernatural Powers or corresponding Spiritual Longevity. Because the Human Soul is not naturally designed to exist indefinitely, extending the physical body's lifespan far beyond its natural limits can eventually produce serious spiritual and psychological consequences.[1]
The fruit also has an unusual effect upon already supernatural beings. When used on a Noble Vampire through the same Divine enhancement method, Ambrosia can temporarily negate the regular necessity of consuming Blood. [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood estimated that a Noble Vampire consuming a properly prepared fruit could remain without Blood for approximately three to five years, with the duration potentially increasing according to the amount of Energy invested into the fruit.[1]

### History
Ambrosia was one of the many Divine resources accumulated by the Greek Gods throughout the long history of [[Olympus|Mount Olympus]]. The exact origin of the fruit, the identity of the first Being to cultivate it and the period in which it was discovered have not been revealed.
During the collapse of Olympus, [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood escaped with a large group of Greek Goddesses and used one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s dimensional storage bags to remove a substantial quantity of valuable material from the Olympian treasury. When the recovered resources were inventoried, Victor Elderblood was surprised by the amount of Ambrosia Aphrodite had managed to bring with her alongside numerous Divine Weapons and other treasures.[1]
Aphrodite explained that she had deliberately prioritized items that would remain useful to Victor's faction. Despite the considerable amount she recovered, even her storage capacity had been insufficient to empty the complete treasury of Olympus.[1]
The recovered Ambrosia became particularly valuable because the group no longer possessed access to the unique environmental conditions responsible for cultivating the fruit on Mount Olympus. Aphrodite therefore treated the surviving supply as an emergency strategic resource rather than ordinary food.[1]

### Description
Ambrosia is described as a fruit possessing Divine life-extending properties. The novel has not yet provided a detailed canonical description of its size, color, taste, internal structure or exact botanical species.
Its extraordinary nature is primarily demonstrated through its supernatural effects rather than its physical appearance.
Unlike an ordinary fruit, Ambrosia can also be deliberately enhanced through Godly [[Magic]] or [[Divine Energy]]. This enhancement allows Gods to increase the life-preserving properties already contained within the fruit and adapt its effect according to the intended consumer.[1]

### Properties and Effects

#### Lifespan Extension
The fundamental property of Ambrosia is the ability to extend the natural lifespan of a Mortal.[1]
The exact amount of lifespan granted by an unenhanced fruit has not been stated. Consequently, Ambrosia should not automatically be described as granting complete immortality merely through ordinary consumption.
The more extreme immortality-like effect discussed by [[Aphrodite]] requires the fruit to be enhanced through the [[Magic]] of a God.[1]

#### Physical Immortality
When Ambrosia is sufficiently empowered through Divine [[Magic]], a Human can be made to remain physically alive until an external force kills them.[1]
This condition is described as a form of physical immortality.
It prevents ordinary death through aging but does not make the individual invulnerable. A Human affected by Ambrosia can still be killed.

#### No Regenerative Immortality
Ambrosia's immortality is fundamentally different from that of Vampires.
When [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] asked whether an Ambrosia-enhanced Human would possess immortality comparable to a Vampire, [[Aphrodite]] explicitly rejected the comparison. Vampire immortality is supported by extremely powerful regeneration, whereas the Human affected by Ambrosia merely possesses a body capable of continuing to live without dying naturally from age.[1]
The Human therefore does not automatically gain:
- Vampire-level regeneration;
- supernatural durability;
- enhanced physical attributes;
- racial Powers;
- or immunity to lethal injury.[1]

#### No Automatic Supernatural Powers
Ambrosia does not convert a Human into a God, Vampire or other supernatural race.
A Human receiving its life-extension effect gains no additional Powers simply because their physical lifespan has been extended.[1]
This separates Ambrosia from genuine forms of supernatural ascension. Transforming a Human into a God, for example, requires a significantly more complicated process involving Divinity and refinement of the Soul rather than simply extending the body's lifespan.[1]

### Spiritual Longevity
The greatest limitation of Ambrosia's immortality is that physical longevity and Spiritual Longevity are not the same thing.
A Human body can be sustained far beyond its normal lifespan, but this does not automatically change the natural limitations of the Human Soul.[1]
[[Jeanne Elderblood]] explained that certain species were simply not intended to live for such extreme periods of Time. Humans were one of the examples she provided. Even if a Human's body remained completely healthy, the Soul could deteriorate when forced to exist far beyond the lifespan for which it was naturally suited.[1]
Potential consequences include:
- madness;
- mental imbalance;
- spiritual deterioration;
- and eventually spiritual death.[1]
For this reason, Ambrosia should not be treated as a perfect method of making ordinary Humans truly immortal.

#### Soul Refinement
Jeanne stated that a Soul can be strengthened and refined through exceptionally powerful willpower, allowing the individual to better withstand an unnaturally prolonged existence.[1]
However, she also explained that the underlying imbalance would remain.
A Human continuing to live far beyond the lifespan naturally assigned to their existence represents an abnormality within the natural order, meaning that simply maintaining the physical body does not fully solve the metaphysical consequences of immortality.[1]

### Effect on Vampires

#### Suppression of Blood Requirement
Ambrosia possesses a particularly useful interaction with Noble Vampires.
[[Aphrodite]] explained that if the fruit is applied to a supernatural Being using the same Divine-enhancement method, its life-sustaining properties can interact with the Being's already high vitality.[1]
In the case of Noble Vampires, this allows Ambrosia to temporarily negate the need to regularly drink Blood.[1]
This effect surprised even [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], despite her enormous age and experience.

#### Duration
[[Aphrodite]] estimated that a Noble Vampire consuming Ambrosia could remain without needing Blood for roughly three to five years.[1]
The estimate was not presented as a rigid universal limit.
The duration depends upon factors including:
- how much Energy is invested into the Ambrosia;
- how much Energy the Vampire expends afterward;
- and presumably the manner in which the fruit is prepared.[1]
Aphrodite specifically stated that investing a greater quantity of Energy could increase the duration of the effect.[1]
The three-to-five-year period should therefore be treated as an approximate baseline rather than an absolute maximum.

#### Emergency Resource
The primary value of this property was logistical rather than permanently replacing Blood.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wives had become accustomed to drinking Victor's Blood, creating a potential vulnerability if they were separated from him for a sufficiently long period. Ambrosia provided an emergency alternative that could suppress their blood requirement during extended separation.[1]
[[Aphrodite]] recommended that each relevant Vampire carry approximately ten or more fruits for emergencies.[1]
She emphasized that they were not intended for casual use because the faction possessed only a finite stockpile.

### Cultivation

#### Mount Olympus
The environment of [[Olympus|Mount Olympus]] possessed unusually fertile soil capable of producing Ambrosia.[1]
This environmental requirement appears to be central to the fruit's rarity.
After leaving Olympus, [[Aphrodite]] explicitly warned that the surviving fruits could not simply be replaced in their new territory because the local soil lacked the required properties.[1]

#### Demeter's Limitations
Even [[Demeter]], the Greek Goddess whose authorities are heavily associated with agriculture and cultivation, was not expected to be capable of reproducing Ambrosia outside the appropriate Olympian environment at the time.[1]
This establishes that Ambrosia is not merely an ordinary crop that becomes Divine because a God cultivates it.
Its growth depends upon special environmental conditions associated with [[Olympus]] itself.

### Scarcity
Although [[Aphrodite]] recovered a large quantity of Ambrosia, the supply available to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction was explicitly limited.[1]
The significance of this scarcity was increased by the loss of easy access to [[Olympus|Mount Olympus]].
Aphrodite warned Victor's Vampires not to waste the fruits because they were no longer residing within the environment where Ambrosia could naturally be cultivated. Even assistance from [[Demeter]] was not considered sufficient to reproduce the fruit under their existing conditions.[1]
For this reason, Ambrosia was treated primarily as a strategic reserve.

### Strategic Importance
Ambrosia possesses several properties that make it significantly more useful than an ordinary Divine luxury.
For Mortals, it can greatly extend physical lifespan and potentially create an individual who will not naturally die from aging when properly empowered.[1]
For Noble Vampires, its ability to suppress blood dependence makes it valuable in situations involving:
- prolonged separation from a preferred Blood source;
- extended military campaigns;
- imprisonment or isolation;
- exploration of distant territories;
- emergencies where Blood supplies are unavailable;
- and operations in environments where feeding would be dangerous or impractical.[1]
Its strategic value to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family was immediately recognized precisely because several of the Vampires around him had become heavily accustomed to feeding upon his Blood.
However, because the supply recovered from [[Olympus]] was finite, [[Aphrodite]] insisted upon preserving it for situations where its benefits were genuinely necessary.[1]

### Relationship with Godhood
Ambrosia should not be confused with a method for directly transforming Humans into Gods.
During the same discussion, [[Aphrodite]] explained that Human ascension into Godhood was a substantially more complicated process. A God-King could place a spark of Divinity inside a Human to help them develop toward a Concept and refine their Soul sufficiently to contain Divine Power.[1]
Ambrosia only addresses the problem of the physical lifespan.
It does not by itself refine the Human Soul into one capable of existing indefinitely as a Divine Being.
This distinction explains why the Greek Gods possessed methods for producing physically immortal Mortals while still requiring a separate process to genuinely elevate a Human into Godhood.[1]

### Known Uses
No individual character has yet been explicitly shown on-page receiving Ambrosia and subsequently demonstrating its complete physical-immortality effect.
Most of its known mechanics were explained by [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood when she presented the resources removed from [[Olympus]] to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his family.[1]
The fruit's demonstrated narrative importance therefore currently comes primarily from the properties confirmed during that discussion rather than from a large number of individual consumption scenes.

### Notes
- Ambrosia was one of the most useful resources [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood deliberately recovered from the treasury of [[Olympus]].[1]
- Aphrodite brought a sufficiently large quantity that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was surprised by the size of the stockpile.[1]
- The exact creator of Ambrosia has not been revealed. The previous description of the fruit as having been "created by Ambrosia" is unsupported and circular.
- "The Fruit of Life" has not been established as an explicit alternate name in Chapter 627 and should not be treated as a confirmed canonical alias without another chapter reference.
- The fruit naturally extends Mortal lifespan, while the stronger physical-immortality effect requires enhancement through a God's [[Magic]].[1]
- The physical immortality granted to Humans is specifically distinguished from Vampire immortality because it does not provide the same regenerative capabilities.[1]
- An Ambrosia-enhanced Human receives no automatic supernatural Powers.[1]
- Physical longevity does not guarantee Spiritual Longevity; the Human Soul can still deteriorate despite the body remaining alive.[1]
- A Noble Vampire can remain without regularly drinking Blood for approximately three to five years after receiving Ambrosia's effect, although the exact period depends upon Energy usage and the amount of Energy invested into the fruit.[1]
- Aphrodite recommended keeping ten or more fruits available for emergencies because the stockpile was finite.[1]
- Mount Olympus possessed the fertile environment required to cultivate Ambrosia, while the territory occupied by Victor's group at the time did not. Even [[Demeter]] was not expected to overcome this limitation under those conditions.[1]
- Ambrosia should not be confused with [[Idun]]'s [[Apple of Youth]], another Divine fruit possessing a different set of supernatural effects.
