Eyes of Causality are a supernatural ocular ability centered on the perception of cause and effect across Time. The power first manifested in Adonis Snow after his transformation into a Vampire, was later acquired and integrated into Victor Elderblood after Victor absorbed Adonis' Soul, and was eventually inherited and awakened naturally by Adonis' daughter, Violet Elderblood Snow.[1][2][3]
Although its earliest manifestation appeared to be simple Clairvoyance or Precognition, later developments revealed that the Eyes are considerably broader. Their fundamental principle is Causality: actions performed in the Past and Present create consequences from which possible Futures emerge. The Eyes can perceive those relationships and, depending upon the user's development, observe the Past, predict future events, examine causal connections, perceive the flow of Time and analyze enormous amounts of temporal information.[4][5][6]
The Eyes should not be confused with direct manipulation of Destiny. Victor explicitly distinguished the two principles, reasoning that an independent conscious force called "Destiny" did not need to exist for future consequences to be observable. Causality itself was sufficient: an action produces a reaction, that reaction creates additional effects and those effects generate future possibilities. Consequently, the future shown by the Eyes is not necessarily an immutable predetermined timeline.[4]

### Nature

#### Causality Rather Than Destiny
The Eyes function through Causality, the relationship between causes and the consequences produced by them. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later came to understand his future sight through this principle rather than treating his visions as messages sent by a predetermined Fate.[4]
A simplified representation is:
CAUSE
│
▼
ACTION
│
▼
CONSEQUENCE
│
├──────────────┐
▼              ▼
POSSIBILITY A    POSSIBILITY B
│              │
▼              ▼
FUTURE A         FUTURE B
Changing one of the causes can consequently alter the resulting Future.
Victor compared this phenomenon to the Butterfly Effect. Even an apparently insignificant action can create increasingly large changes as its consequences propagate forward through Time.[4]
This is why advanced users do not interpret every vision as absolute prophecy. The Eyes may show a Future that is perfectly possible according to the existing chain of causes, but once the observer learns about that Future and begins acting differently, the causal conditions themselves have changed.[3][4]

#### The Observer Becomes Part of the Cause
One of the most dangerous characteristics of the Eyes is that seeing the Future can itself become a cause influencing that Future.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] recognized this danger while examining Adonis' experiences. Knowledge of an event can alter a person's behavior until their attempts to prevent or produce that event inadvertently push circumstances toward it. He therefore repeatedly warned Violet against casually observing distant futures.[3][4]
This creates a paradoxical relationship:
User observes Future X
│
▼
User learns Future X exists
│
▼
User changes their behavior
│
▼
New causes are introduced
│
├── Future X is avoided
├── Future X is altered
└── Attempts to avoid X cause X to occur
For this reason, Victor generally considers extremely short-range foresight safer and more practical than treating distant future visions as guaranteed prophecy.

#### Past and Future
The Eyes interact very differently with the Past and the Future.
The Past has already occurred. Its causal events have been completed and therefore form a stable informational history. After Violet's evolution into a Blood Dragon, she discovered that looking into the Past was considerably easier and safer than looking deeply into the Future because she was examining something that had already been "written."[5]
The Future, by comparison, remains dependent upon decisions and changing conditions. The farther the Eyes look ahead, the more possible branches may emerge.[4][6]
By the later Imperial era, Violet explained that she normally limited deliberate future sight to several seconds and, at most, approximately a minute. Beyond that point she could perceive the Future beginning to fragment into numerous possibilities created by different choices.[6]

### Origin

#### Adonis Snow
[[Adonis Snow]] is the earliest known possessor of the ability.
Adonis was originally a Human and did not possess supernatural foresight before becoming a Vampire. His Clairvoyance manifested after Agnes Snow transformed him. The exact reason for its appearance was never conclusively established. Agnes herself did not understand why a Human transformed into a Vampire had acquired an ability of such an abnormal nature, and [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] reportedly regarded it as something resembling the Power of a God.[1]
Unlike the later versions possessed by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Violet, Adonis' ability was extremely unstable. He could not freely determine when a vision would occur and activating the power inflicted severe damage upon both his body and Soul.[1]
Nevertheless, his visions possessed extraordinary accuracy. Before his death, every future he had observed had occurred as foreseen.[1]
Victor later theorized that this apparent perfection did not mean Adonis was seeing an immutable Destiny. Rather, Adonis usually lacked the personal power necessary to significantly alter the enormous causal chains he witnessed. Consequently, the Futures he observed continued progressing toward their predicted results.[3]
Adonis' most important known visions included:
- Victor eventually ascending to an extraordinary position while surrounded by six trusted figures.[1]
- Violet destroying or burning the Vampire Royal Capital while consumed by overwhelming hatred.[1]
- circumstances connected to Adonis' own death.[1]
- later catastrophic developments surrounding [[Nightingale]] and Violet that influenced Adonis' decision to entrust his legacy to Victor.
Adonis ultimately used the information obtained through his visions to arrange his actions around Victor, ensuring that his death would contribute toward the development of someone capable of changing the futures Adonis himself lacked the strength to prevent.

### Acquisition by Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] did not inherit the Eyes biologically. He acquired their underlying ability after consuming and assimilating [[Adonis Snow]]'s Soul and memories.[2]
The assimilation transformed both Victor's physical eyes and his pre-existing supernatural perception.
Before absorbing Adonis, Victor possessed a special Vampire vision that caused the world to appear predominantly red and allowed him to:
- see through walls and other physical obstructions;
- perceive living beings at considerable distances;
- recognize supernatural races through their distinctive Energy or aura;
- and analyze surroundings beyond ordinary visual limitations.
After Adonis merged with him, this perception underwent a fundamental change.[2]

#### Violet World
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s normal eye color changed to violet, resembling Adonis' eyes. When he activated Adonis' future sight, his eyes temporarily became blood-red before returning to violet once the vision ended.[2]
His previous "Red World" also transformed. Instead of perceiving everything through a red filter, Victor began seeing the environment predominantly in violet while people and important objects retained substantially clearer natural detail.[2]

#### Causal Lines
The most significant change was the appearance of numerous red lines spread throughout [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s supernatural field of vision.
The lines varied drastically in thickness. Some were thin and barely noticeable while others were enormous and intertwined like knots. Particularly important relationships produced substantially stronger connections; Victor's connection with [[Agnes Elderblood Snow]], for example, appeared exceptionally thick.[2]
At this stage Victor could perceive the lines but could not physically interact with or manipulate them.[2]
The chapter did not initially explain the phenomenon in full, but Victor's later understanding that his future sight operated through Causality provides the context for the strange connections his evolved eyes were displaying.[4]

#### Enhanced Clairvoyance
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] immediately inherited Adonis' ability to observe events several seconds before they occurred.[2]
Unlike Adonis, however, Victor possessed an enormously stronger body, Soul and supernatural processing capacity. The ability therefore did not produce the same catastrophic physical deterioration when he used it normally.
Victor eventually demonstrated the capacity to look far beyond combat-scale intervals. He has observed possibilities extending years or even centuries into the Future, including glimpses involving daughters he had not yet conceived.[7]
Despite this capability, Victor deliberately avoids depending on distant prophecy. His preferred use of future sight is normally extremely short-range because immediate causal conditions are much less likely to change before the predicted event occurs.

### Inheritance by Violet Elderblood Snow
[[Violet Elderblood Snow]] inherited the potential for the Eyes directly from her father, Adonis.
The ability remained dormant for much of her early life. It awakened after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] performed a major bloodline-evolution ritual upon Violet and several members of his family. During the process, Violet unexpectedly entered a future vision that Victor immediately recognized as the same dangerous ability once possessed by Adonis.[3]
Victor forcibly interrupted the vision before Violet could observe too deeply. Both he and Agnes were alarmed because they understood the danger Adonis' ability had represented.[3]
Unlike her father, however, Violet inherited the Eyes within a dramatically stronger supernatural constitution. She did not possess Adonis' fragile Human-origin body and Soul, and her version consequently possessed far greater growth potential.[3]

### Powers and Applications

#### Causality Perception
The fundamental function underlying the Eyes is the ability to perceive consequences produced by existing causes.[4]
The Eyes do not need to "create" a Future in order to observe it. Rather, they read how present conditions can develop and perceive the resulting temporal information.
The greater the user's perception, cognitive ability and supernatural development, the more information can be extracted from those relationships.

#### Precognition
The most basic and earliest demonstrated ability of the Eyes is Precognition: observing events before they physically occur.[1][2][3]

##### Short-Range Foresight
Both [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Violet can deliberately observe the immediate Future.
During combat training inside the Tower of Nightmare, Violet intentionally looked five seconds ahead. She saw an incoming attack before the enemy physically performed it and moved out of its path in advance.[8]
This is one of the most effective combat applications because only a small amount of Time separates the vision from the event, leaving comparatively little opportunity for the causal chain to change.

##### Future-Based Evasion
Future sight can effectively make an opponent appear predictable.
Rather than reacting to an attack after movement begins, the user can begin moving before the attack has been performed.[8]
This allows:
- pre-emptive evasion;
- counterattack preparation;
- prediction of attack trajectories;
- anticipation of feints;
- optimal positioning;
- and avoidance of otherwise unexpected attacks.
The Eyes do not automatically increase the user's physical speed. A user who sees an attack but lacks sufficient speed or power to avoid it can still be struck.

##### Long-Range Future Vision
The Eyes can observe events much farther into the Future than several seconds.
Adonis experienced visions concerning events months or years beyond his present circumstances.[1] [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later perceived possible events extending extremely far into his own future.[7] Violet likewise deliberately forced her sight deep into a distant possible future.[9]
Long-range perception is considerably more dangerous because countless additional causes can arise between the observer's present and the observed event.

#### Possible Future Perception
A sufficiently advanced user can perceive that the Future is composed of possibilities rather than a single permanently fixed sequence.[4][6]
Violet eventually became capable of identifying the point where a relatively stable immediate Future fractured into numerous alternate possibilities. By Chapter 1056, she deliberately restricted ordinary foresight to short intervals because predictions extending beyond approximately one minute became increasingly fragmented according to the choices made by different Beings.[6]
Thus:
PRESENT
│
▼
Highly Probable Immediate Future
│
├──── Choice A ──── Future A
│
├──── Choice B ──── Future B
│
├──── Choice C ──── Future C
│
└──── Unknown Cause ── Future D
The farther the observation extends, the more severe this branching can become.

#### Retrocognition
Violet's Eyes evolved beyond future sight after her transformation into a Blood Dragon.
She discovered that she could now look backward through the causal history of a person or object and observe the Past.[5]
Unlike future observation, Retrocognition is comparatively stable because the observed events have already happened.

##### Personal History Observation
By looking at Eleonor, Violet examined her previous experiences and obtained detailed knowledge regarding her behavior, preferences, combat habits and psychological tendencies.[5]
She was therefore not merely seeing isolated historical images. The Eyes could provide enough contextual information for Violet to construct an extremely comprehensive profile of the individual.

##### Behavioral Analysis
Past observation allows the user to determine behavioral patterns.
If a target has reacted to similar situations hundreds or thousands of times, knowledge of those reactions can be used to estimate what the target will probably do again.[5]
This creates a second form of prediction independent of direct future sight:
Observe Target's Past
│
▼
Identify Repeated Habits
│
▼
Understand Personality
│
▼
Predict Likely Decision
│
▼
Prepare Countermeasure
Violet used this method against Eleonor rather than continuously looking into the Future.[5]

##### Weakness Analysis
An individual's complete history can reveal:
- physical weaknesses;
- preferred techniques;
- combat habits;
- emotional vulnerabilities;
- personal secrets;
- previous injuries;
- fears;
- relationships;
- psychological triggers;
- and recurring tactical decisions.[5]
The Eyes can therefore function as an extremely powerful information-gathering ability even when direct temporal prediction is unnecessary.

#### Akashic Retrocognition
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually developed an even broader application by combining his extraordinary Eyes and perception with the [[Akashic Records]].
By focusing upon a location, Victor could access the information recorded there and observe previous events almost as though he were watching a recording of what had occurred.[10]
This should not automatically be treated as a basic function available to every possessor of the Eyes. Victor's version had already been altered by numerous evolutions, his connection to supernatural knowledge systems and his own extraordinarily developed perception.

#### Temporal Perception
After Violet became a True Dragon, her inherited ability fused with the perceptive capabilities of her Draconic eyes.
This allowed her to perceive Time itself rather than merely isolated past or future events.[6]
She can observe differences in temporal flow between locations and recognize when one region is experiencing Time differently from another. She demonstrated this while examining the difference between the outside world and a Dream environment in which years could pass internally while only minutes elapsed externally.[6]

#### Cross-Temporal Perception
One of Violet's most extraordinary demonstrations occurred when she forced her vision extremely far into the Future.
Instead of merely watching an unaware future scene, Violet encountered an older version of herself who noticed that her past self was observing her.[9]
Future Violet consciously turned toward the observer and spoke directly to her younger self.
This demonstrated that an adequately developed Eyes of Causality user can potentially perceive an observer located at another temporal point rather than functioning as a passive image inside a vision.

#### Cross-Temporal Communication
Future Violet successfully transmitted information to her younger self through their causal connection.[9]
However, the younger Violet could not properly communicate back in the same manner. Future Violet implied that her younger counterpart simply had not yet developed sufficient understanding or mastery of their inherited Eyes.[9]
The exchange also demonstrated the danger of revealing excessive future information. Future Violet deliberately limited what she said because providing too much knowledge could alter the chain of events responsible for producing her own Future.[9]

#### Causal Connection Perception
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s version of the Eyes can visually reveal supernatural connections between individuals as red lines or threads.[2]
The thickness and complexity of these connections appear to correspond to the significance of the relationship. Particularly powerful bonds can resemble enormous interconnected knots.
Known visual characteristics include:
- thin red threads representing weaker connections;
- thicker lines representing stronger causal relationships;
- multiple lines converging upon important individuals;
- extraordinarily thick connections between deeply linked Beings;
- and lines remaining visible through ordinary physical obstructions.[2]
Victor initially possessed no demonstrated ability to alter these threads directly.

#### Enhanced Visual Perception
Because [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Eyes of Causality fused with his pre-existing Vampire vision, his version retained and enhanced several unrelated perceptual functions.[2]
These include:
- seeing through walls and physical obstructions;
- viewing distant individuals;
- identifying supernatural races;
- perceiving Energy or aura signatures;
- and obtaining substantially greater visual clarity.
These abilities are specifically part of Victor's merged version and should not automatically be attributed to Adonis or Violet.

### Evolution by User

#### Adonis' Eyes
Adonis possessed the original and least stable known form.
His ability could reveal extremely important future events with remarkable accuracy, but the cost was severe. Every activation damaged his body and Soul, contributing toward the gradual destruction of his already fragile existence.[1]
He also lacked complete activation control. Visions could occur independently rather than only when he deliberately requested them.[1]
The ability was consequently both a blessing and a curse: Adonis could see disasters approaching but often lacked either the physical ability or political power required to prevent them.

#### Victor's Eyes
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s absorption of Adonis fundamentally altered the ability.
Rather than inheriting an isolated Clairvoyance technique, Victor fused Adonis' foresight with his own special Vampire vision. This produced a new visual state capable of simultaneously perceiving physical surroundings, supernatural information and strange red connections between people.[2]
Victor's vastly superior body and Soul also removed many of Adonis' biological limitations.
As Victor continued evolving through Vampire Progenitor, Blood Dragon, God and later states, the original ability became only one component of a far larger perceptual system. His later access to the [[Akashic Records]] allowed him to reconstruct historical events on a scale never demonstrated by Adonis.[10]

#### Violet's Eyes
Violet represents the natural hereditary evolution of Adonis' ability.
Her Eyes initially awakened as future sight, but unlike Adonis she possessed a powerful Noble Vampire body, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s evolving Blood and eventually the physiology of a Blood Dragon.[3]
After becoming a Dragon, the Eyes underwent their greatest confirmed evolution. Violet gained:
- Past perception;
- detailed historical analysis;
- improved causal processing;
- Draconic cognitive capacity;
- temporal-flow perception;
- and considerably greater control over the duration and target of her observations.[5][6]
Her later Eyes can therefore be considered far more versatile than Adonis' original Clairvoyance.

### Combat Applications
Although the Eyes can function as a prophetic power, Violet demonstrated that their safest and most consistent combat applications involve combining temporal information with ordinary skill.
- Precognition: Observing an attack before it occurs.[8]
- Pre-Emptive Evasion: Moving away from a future attack before the enemy physically begins it.[8]
- Counter Preparation: Preparing the appropriate defense or counterattack according to an opponent's predicted action.
- Behavior Prediction: Using a target's past behavior to infer their likely present action without directly viewing the Future.[5]
- Weakness Detection: Studying a target's history to identify exploitable habits and vulnerabilities.[5]
- Psychological Warfare: Revealing private knowledge obtained from the target's Past to destabilize them emotionally.[5]
- Environmental Analysis: Determining how a location or situation reached its current state through Retrocognition.
- Temporal Distortion Detection: Recognizing regions where the flow of Time differs from the surrounding Reality.[6]

### Limitations

#### Future Sight Is Not Absolute
The most important limitation is that the Future is mutable.
A vision represents an outcome that can emerge from the causes existing when the observation occurs. New actions can create new consequences and therefore change the result.[4]

#### Branching Futures
The farther the Eyes look into the Future, the greater the number of variables involved.
Violet eventually explained that predictions extending beyond relatively short intervals begin splitting into numerous possible Futures according to the decisions made by the people involved.[6]

#### Causal Feedback
Knowledge of a Future can affect the Future itself.
An observer attempting to prevent a vision may accidentally create the conditions necessary for it to happen. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] considered this one of the most dangerous properties of the ability.[3][4]

#### Energy Consumption
Extremely deep Future observation requires enormous Energy.
After Violet forced herself to look far enough forward to encounter her future counterpart, she emerged drenched in sweat, exhausted and effectively depleted by the experience.[9]

#### Physical and Spiritual Backlash
Adonis' original version actively harmed him.
Repeated activation damaged his body and Soul and contributed to the deterioration of his health.[1]
This weakness was largely mitigated in later users because [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Violet possessed supernatural bodies enormously superior to Adonis' original constitution.

#### Lack of Activation Control
Adonis could not fully control when his visions occurred. Important visions could activate involuntarily.[1]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Violet later demonstrated substantially greater conscious control.

#### Information Does Not Equal Power
Knowing what will happen does not automatically provide the ability to prevent it.
Violet acknowledged this while analyzing powerful opponents. Even if the Eyes revealed precisely how someone such as [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] would attack, Violet would still be struck if the difference in speed, strength or skill exceeded her ability to respond.[5]

#### Unpredictable Opponents
Retrocognitive behavioral prediction becomes less reliable against opponents whose actions vary drastically from previous behavior.
A fighter with enormous versatility, intentional randomness or superior tactical adaptability can reduce the effectiveness of predictions based exclusively upon historical patterns.[5]

#### Cross-Temporal Communication Restrictions
Young Violet was able to hear her future counterpart but could not properly communicate back.
Future Violet implied that this was a matter of insufficient mastery rather than an absolute impossibility.[9]

#### Causal Lines Were Initially Intangible
Although [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] could perceive red connections after acquiring Adonis' power, he could not simply touch, sever or manipulate them when the ability first evolved.[2]
Therefore, Causality Perception should not automatically be treated as Causality Manipulation.

#### Abnormal Reality Conditions
Future sight can lose usefulness when the ordinary progression of Reality or Time is disrupted. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] consequently prefers to treat foresight as supplementary information rather than an infallible combat system.

### Relationship with the Akashic Records
The Eyes of Causality and the [[Akashic Records]] are closely compatible but should not be treated as identical abilities.
The Eyes inherently allow users such as Violet to perceive historical causal information without requiring explicit access to the Akashic Records.[5]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later form of Retrocognition is more advanced. His evolved perception can interface with the Akashic Records and retrieve the history of events associated with a location, effectively allowing him to observe the Past like recorded information.[10]

### Relationship with Dragon Eyes
Violet's transformation into a Blood Dragon did not replace the Eyes of Causality. Instead, the ability fused with the superior perceptive and cognitive properties of her new Draconic existence.
Before becoming a Dragon, Violet primarily demonstrated future sight.[3][8]
Afterward, she demonstrated:
- perception of the Past;[5]
- massively improved information processing;[5]
- historical behavioral analysis;[5]
- perception of Time-flow differences;[6]
- and better understanding of how future possibilities branch.[6]
The Draconic evolution therefore expanded the Eyes from specialized Clairvoyance into a much broader temporal-perception system.

### Relationship with Violet's Order
The Eyes of Causality are separate from Violet's Order Divinity.
The Eyes provide information concerning Past, Present and possible Futures, while Order allows Violet to directly impose conditions upon Reality.
However, the two abilities naturally complement each other. By observing how events developed or how a target is likely to respond, Violet can make substantially more informed decisions regarding how and where to impose her Order.
Her later position as the Dragon Goddess of Order therefore makes the information supplied by the Eyes considerably more dangerous than it was when she possessed Clairvoyance alone.
