# Brainstorm: Bianca di Angelo and the Order of the Hollow Crescent

# PART ONE: BIANCA DI ANGELO

## Where she stands at the end of Ch20

- **The era:** about 30,000 years after the Empire rose, on a quarantined, glassed Earth, which puts it roughly 16,000 years after Percy's Shear.
- **What the scan found:** a weak, human-sized lifeform in a man-made structure under Everest.
- **What Octavia found instead:** dozens of downed Imperial soldiers who got there before her crew.
- **How Bianca talks:** in a haughty, archaic register ("my esteemed self," "third-rate fools… bizarre contraptions") that reads like a cultivator coming out of closed-door seclusion.
- **Her tell:** pale blue fire in her eyes, and power that feels like "a natural disaster given human form."
- **Why she flares:** the words "Undying Olympian Empire" are the trigger.
- **The present-day seeds:**
  - She joined the Hunt.
  - Flamel smelled sulfur on the "timid girl" (Ch17).
  - Nico dropped out of the story after Westover.

## What happened to her: backstory options

These can be mixed. Each one explains how she's alive, why she's under Everest, and why she hates the Empire.

**A. The Hunter who stayed behind.**
- **The Abandonment:** at the Great Abandonment (3045), Artemis took the Hunt to the stars. Bianca refused to go, because the dead of Earth weren't going anywhere and her father's realm was still rooted there.
- **The glassing:** when the Empire glassed Earth to deny it to whatever came after, she was underground with them.
- **The Himalayas:** the Jade Court remnants who never left the mountains taught her to survive on qi instead of ambrosia. That's where the voice comes from.
- **The grudge:** the Empire burned her home with her dead still in it.

**B. The Warden of a door.**
- **The door:** Everest sits on one of the few hidden entry points into Tartarus. Call it the Roof Gate, a back door Hades cut at the top of the world, where the sky is closest to the pit's opposite.
- **The duty:** Bianca is its warden, and has been for millennia.
- **Why she's the "greatest weapon":** AtRep lists her that way because whoever holds her holds a route into Tartarus that doesn't pass Watchpoint Hades.
- **Tie-in:** this links straight to the Hollow Crescent plot in Part Two.

**C. Something wearing her.**
- **The sulfur:** Flamel smelled it in Ch17 because something was already hitching a ride.
- **What it is:** a demon (Kin of the 32nd, Asmodai's line?) or a Chthonic entity, which grew with her across the ages.
- **What's left of her:** the haughty voice belongs to the passenger. The real Bianca surfaces in fragments.

**D. Percy's contingency.**
- **The request:** before the Shear, Percy asked her to guard something he couldn't take with him. It could be the Everest door, Nico, or a piece of himself.
- **Keeping it:** she has kept the promise for sixteen thousand years longer than he did.
- **The grudge:** she hates the Empire because it built itself on his name after he left, and she knew him before the name.

**E. The Nico answer.**
- **The dead boy:** Nico died in the present-day timeline, or later.
- **The bargain:** Bianca bargained with Hades, or someone older, to hold his soul out of the Empire's Wall system and away from the Répertoire's harvesting of the dead (Mind-Shard Ascension, Ch14).
- **Under the mountain:** the "weak lifeform" isn't Bianca. It's her brother, kept barely alive, and she is the thing standing guard over him.

## Five ways to continue after "I am Bianca Di'Angelo."

### 1. The name on the Wall

Octavia knows the name. Every Hunter does, because Bianca di Angelo is on the Hunt's Wall, marked the way Zoë's record is marked: MISSING. NOT DEAD.

- **The reflex:** before Octavia can stop herself, she drops into the Hunt's salute, fist to sternum and bow lowered.
- **Recognition:** Bianca notices at once, and something in her face goes from disaster to person.
- **The question and the answer:** she asks the only question she cares about: *"Does the Lady still hunt?"* Octavia, who left the Hunt for reasons she hasn't told anyone, has to answer honestly. *"She's… having one of her nights. Most nights, lately."*
- **The contempt:** Bianca laughs, and it's not a nice laugh. "Then she has become exactly what she swore she would never be. She waits for a man."
- **The hook:** Bianca doesn't want to be captured. She wants to be *carried*. She hires them, or commandeers them, to get her off Earth past the defense lattice. Octavia's crew becomes a ferry for the most dangerous passenger in the Sol System, and the bounty becomes the thing Octavia keeps not collecting.
- **Best for:** a long road-trip arc. It uses Octavia's ex-Hunter wound and lets Lauren and Asimov stay comic relief with real stakes.

### 2. The Warden and the door

The Imperial soldiers weren't there by accident. AtRep sent them to open something, and Bianca is standing in front of it.

- **The reveal:** behind her, carved into the mountain's root, is a door older than the Empire. It is black stone with a helm of darkness worked over the lintel. Asimov's scanner reads nothing behind it, and the nothing goes down forever.
- **The fight:** the surviving Roman legacy triggers a failsafe charge meant to crack the door. Bianca has to choose between killing him and stopping the charge, and she can't do both.
- **Octavia's choice:** she can grab the detonator and save the door, or grab the legacy and save the bounty.
- **Consequences:** the door is damaged either way. Bianca, furious, tells them what they've done: "You have opened a crack in a wall that has held since before your Empire learned to walk. Now everything that has been waiting for a crack will come."
- **Tie-in:** that sets up the Hollow Crescent arriving at Everest (Part Two, Option 1).
- **Best for:** linking Bianca directly to the Tartarus time-road plot and giving the cult a destination.

### 3. The lattice wakes up

The downed squad's distress beacons have been screaming the whole time. Sol's orbital defense lattice finally notices the fight.

- **The alarm:** Lauren's voice comes over comms, perfectly calm: "Big sis. Every platform in the northern hemisphere just turned toward you."
- **The strike:** kinetic rods start falling. Bianca raises a hand and the shadows of the Himalayas stand up to meet them. The mountain's shadow goes from lying on the ground to standing between them and the sky. It holds, barely.
- **The bounty:** mid-bombardment, Lauren reads out the fresh AtRep bounty that just posted for Bianca. The number is so large Asimov starts laughing and can't stop.
- **The escape:** Bianca grabs Asimov by the collar as a porter or hostage and orders them to "Take me to your ship. Now. Or be buried with them."
- **Best for:** a big action set piece and a fast pivot into a chase across the Sol System, with Octavia now hunted by the Empire for harboring the target she came to catch.

### 4. The bluff

The scan was right. Her life force *is* extremely weak.

- **The collapse:** the display of power was the last of her reserves, spent to make the Imperials kneel. The moment the Roman legacy bows his head, the blue fire gutters out. She takes two steps and folds.
- **The question:** Octavia is now standing over the Empire's greatest weapon, unconscious, with forty wounded Imperial witnesses who saw her face.
- **What Octavia does with them:** she can finish the witnesses, bribe them or run.
- **What Bianca needs:** Lauren's medical scan of Bianca shows no metabolism at all. Nothing in her body works the way a body should. She has been sustaining herself on something else. When the nearest wounded soldier dies, Bianca's color improves.
- **The implications:** she eats death. The sulfur Flamel smelled was the first sign of it.
- **Best for:** a quieter, horror-tinged turn. It sets up a moral problem for Octavia's crew, since keeping Bianca alive means feeding her.

### 5. Two voices

The thing that answered the Roman was not Bianca.

- **The switch:** Octavia says, half to herself, the first line of the Hunt's oath: *"I turn my back on the company of men…"* The woman stops mid-sentence. The haughty voice cuts off, and a smaller, frightened one comes through, a girl's voice with a 1930s Washington accent: *"…Is that the Lady? Is she here? Please. Is Nico here?"*
- **The passenger returns:** the blue fire comes back. The first voice returns, annoyed. "Ignore her. She talks in her sleep."
- **What's inside:** Bianca has been sharing her body for millennia with something older. It could be a Jade Court immortal, a Kin of Asmodai or a fragment of her father's realm. The cultivator voice belongs to the passenger. The real Bianca is the "extremely weak lifeform" the scanner found.
- **Octavia's new target:** not a bounty. It's getting the girl out.
- **Best for:** a creepier, character-driven mystery, and a payoff for the Flamel sulfur detail.

**My pick** is a mix of 2 and 5. Bianca is the Warden of the Everest door, and what guards the door is only partly her. That gives the Hollow Crescent a destination, gives Octavia a reason to stay, and pays off the sulfur. Option 3 works as the exit from the scene in any version.

# PART TWO: THE ORDER OF THE HOLLOW CRESCENT

## What's established

- **The Order:** a Luna-based cult that worships Percy and Him Within the Moon, a shard of Percy tied to Artemis. It formed an alliance with the Sunborne Cult. Its chants come from Ch13:
  - "Slough off your mortal suffering and be reborn…"
  - "Rise, Perseus Jackson. Let our blood be thine…"
  - "Speak the names, The One Son. The Tidebreaker. The Gentle Storm. The Not-God-Who-Walked."
  - "The Moonbound waits with arms wide."
- **The Arenberg twins:** Orran (elder, seven feet, rapier *Severance*) and Virre (younger, bayonet knife *Backstab*, 500 m/s).
  - **Standing:** they're Percy's half-siblings, ranked 74 and 75, and Alpha-Class Traitors.
  - **Powers and habits:** Power Leech, they speak in tandem or not at all, and they're heavily implied cannibals ("good eating in Virre Arenberg's barracks").
  - **The Order's claim:** Percy's soul was never meant to be bound to Luna.
  - **The twins' real goal:** to leech the shard.
- **The Eye of Light:** they captured it 32 years before Ch15, and it was recovered from Luna.

## Tone notes from Darktide's Admonition

- **The ritualists** speak in stiff archaic grammar ("thou art," "thy failing") with cold contempt for their own allies. Their chants run on repeated closing refrains ("The darkness arises. The darkness arises.").
- **The twin captains:** Rinda and Jakel are the best model for the Arenbergs. They trade sibling needling in the middle of a fight ("Try not to embarrass me"). They share a food obsession: meat for the cauldron, the flavor of desperation. They laugh through defeat and promise to come back.
- **Other habits:** faithful dedications of each kill to their god, and the belief that the cult is the future ("We are without number").
- **The Hollow Crescent's version:** swap the Grandfather for the Moonbound, rot for the tide, and the cauldron for the Drowning. The Order's god is a drowned, patient king under silver light, and its theology is about being *pulled under and remade*.

## Cult vocabulary

- **The Moonbound, the One Son, the Tidebreaker:** Percy. He is never "Jackson" to the faithful. Saying his family name aloud is for outsiders and heretics.
- **The Undertow:** Tartarus, reinterpreted as the current that carries the faithful back to Him.
- **The Low Road:** the route through Tartarus into the past.
- **Drowning:** conversion. Also a euphemism for murder.
- **Salt:** blood of the faithful. "Give salt" means bleed for the cause.
- **Tideless:** the unfaithful, and especially Olympian loyalists.
- **The Chorus:** the Order's ritualists, who always speak in the plural ("We").
- **Heralds of the Tide:** field captains, each with an epithet (see below).
- **The Hollow:** the empty space in the moon where He waits, and the matching emptiness the faithful carve into themselves.

**Herald names** (in the style of Admonition captains):
- Sennet the Unsalted
- Mother Esk the Tideless-Once
- Halvard the Drowned Twice
- Coriander the Faithful Wound
- Ysolde the Listening
- Brannoch the Hull-Breaker
- Tamsin of the Low Road
- Vey the Unburied

## How they learn Percy can be reached

Pick one, or layer them:

1. **The leak from the Wound.**
   - **What gets out:** the CODE 18 packet recovered at the Shear (Interlude I) confirms the Lord Imperator is alive and verified at a date about 14,200 years in the past.
   - **How:** someone on Aphrodite's bridge, or an Aldra watcher, sells it.
   - **How the Chorus reads it:** He did not die. He went *down and back*.
2. **The Evergreen hack.** The same Evergreen branch manager who hacked AtRep for Asimov (Ch20) sells a second packet to the Order. It carries Minerva's Watchpoint queries about Tartarus as a covert route, and the existence of the single sanctioned rift (Ch15).
3. **Luke's offer, remembered.** In XV, Luke offered the Imperials "Kronos's road home through Tartarus." If a survivor of Orpheus comes back through the Mars rift, the story spreads through the ranks. The cult hears there is a road, and that it ends where He is.
4. **The shard speaks.** Him Within the Moon stirs when the present-day Artemis bond ignites (Ch18). The Moonbound's silverlight apparitions begin whispering one word to the Chorus: *Below.*

Recommended: 1 plus 4. Evidence and a vision arrive at the same time, which is what turns a cult into a crusade.

## Where the hidden entry points are

- **Watchpoint Hades' rift:** the sanctioned rift (Ch15). It is too heavily guarded, and it's the obvious decoy.
- **The Roof Gate under Everest:** on quarantined Gaia (tie to Bianca).
- **Taenarum:** the old mouth at Cape Matapan, Greece, which is canon. It lies under glass now and is watched by the orbital lattice.
- **Lake Avernus:** Italy, the Roman door, flooded and forgotten.
- **Orpheus's Door:** a collapsed descent on Luna itself. This explains *why the Order was on Luna in the first place.* They weren't only worshipping the shard. They were digging.

**Strong option:** the Order has spent decades digging the Luna door and the Hunt has been holding them off (Ch12–13). The new intel tells them the Luna door is shallow and leads only to the upper pit. The real Low Road starts from Gaia. So they have to *leave* Luna and cut across the Empire to Sol, the most fortified system there is.

## The slaughter-march: beats

1. **The Revelation Mass.** In the Hollow, the Chorus reads the leaked verdict aloud as scripture. The twins listen from the back and don't kneel.
2. **The covenant.** The twins agree to lead the crusade. The Order believes they do it out of faith. Their real reason is that anything that can reach Percy can also leech him. There is tension from the first scene: the faithful want to be drowned *into* Him, and the twins want to *eat* Him.
3. **The first cut, Luna Secundus again.** They break out through the Hunt's cordon. Virre takes the Hunters personally, since Backstab still carries Artemis's blessing.
4. **Recruiting the desperate.** The march passes through the Tithe worlds. In the Callistus Undercity (Ch21), the people who lost sons to Legio XX's unnamed front don't need much convincing. The cult promises a king who comes back *for them*. Petra's boy could turn up here.
5. **Stealing a Helmsman.** To run the Sol lattice they need a ship with a living relay. They take a Poseidon legacy off a Waves of Poseidon vessel. This drags the Pyrrhus family in (Marien, Stephanos).
6. **The Director's hunt.** Future Artemis learns the Order is going for Percy. Her relapse becomes a war-footing. This collides with Thalia's report from Voss.
7. **Sol.** They punch through the lattice with heavy losses and land at Everest. They meet Bianca and Octavia's crew.
8. **The Low Road.** They enter Tartarus and come out behind the Imperial front, at Thaleia's Lethe Hold (Ch14), from the wrong side.
9. **The endgame.** Some of them reach the present. Percy faces a crowd that knows his future titles and wants to die for him, which is exactly what he doesn't want. The twins face present-day Artemis, who isn't yet the goddess who fought them on Luna.

## Open questions for you

- Do the twins believe *anything*, or is this pure appetite? A crack of real longing for a brother would make Virre far more interesting than Orran.
- Does the Order know the twins plan to leech Percy? A mid-arc betrayal (the Chorus tries to sacrifice the twins at the Low Road's mouth) is available.
- Which Percy do they reach: the present-day kid, or does the Low Road deposit them somewhere else in the timeline, such as the Great Abandonment?
