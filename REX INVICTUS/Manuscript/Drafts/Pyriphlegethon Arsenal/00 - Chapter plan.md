## House rules applied to every chapter
- No em dashes, no "the way a", no "not X but Y", no negative parallelism, no rhetorical Q then A, few triplets, minimal "very", no "because" tails, no button lines, no simile piles, no echo/call-and-response dialogue, no short-sentence stacks in action.
- Blank line between every paragraph and dialogue line. Straight quotes.
- Base-doc voice: close third with attitude, dialogue-led, profanity and dry bureaucratic humour, AtRep log lines as texture.
- UOE unit numbers as numerals with only the numeral italic (*3rd* Siege Army, *44th* Trench Regiment, *6th* Cohort). Rebel, Hunt, Order and Cohort-of-Nemesis names keep their own forms.
- Technical magic gets a plain-language line beside it.
- Minerva is clipped and impersonal to everyone except Percy and Artemis.
- No romantic or sexual content involving Percy's body.
