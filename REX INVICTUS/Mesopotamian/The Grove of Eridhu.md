**[[The Grove of Eridhu]]** is a figure of Rex Invictus lore. (Lore adapted from *The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** Flamel material is adapted into the Rex Invictus present (c. 2021). Rex Invictus wins any conflict; [[Robespierre]] fills [[Dr. John Dee]]'s role and hunts the Philosopher's Stone rather than [[The Codex]]. See [[Flamel Series#Integration rules]].

---

## Real-World Background (IRL)

Eridu, in southern Mesopotamia, was regarded by the Sumerians as the first city, seat of the god Enki.

---

## Flamel Series Canon (SOFTINF)

> The Grove of Eridhu is the shadowrealm once inhabited by [[Tammuz]]. It is one giant forest that Tammuz has been repairing, and he plans to make it the home of all the nature spirits remaining on Earth.
