## Real-World Background (IRL)

Inanna (Akkadian Ishtar) is the Sumerian goddess of love, war and the planet Venus. Her descent into the underworld is one of the oldest surviving myths.

Inanna is a powerful Elder and former consort of Tammuz. She was worshipped by the Sumerians as a goddess of lust, fertility, and warfare. Inanna was widely respected by the [[Elders]] on [[Danu Talis]], and wielded great power. She supported the rise of the humani.[1]
Inanna briefly appears in [[The Enchantress]] amidst a crowd of Elders on Danu Talis.

### Appearance
Following the effects of [[The Change]], Inanna has the wings and feet of a bird. She kept lions on [[Danu Talis]], and smelled of cats, straw, and dung.[2]

### Abilities
- [[Immortality]]: Inanna is Immortal and does not age. Though an immortal may be poisoned or slain in battle, they may live for thousands or millions of years.
