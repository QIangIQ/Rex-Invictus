#### Contents
- 1 References
True Dragon Physiology: As an True Dragon Tiamat is immensely powerful being. She possesses all of dragons racial abilities to much higher degree like Immense Strength, Superhuman Speed and Reflex, Immense Durability, Enhanced Agility, Enhanced Sense, Regenerative Healing Factor, Breath, Shapeshifting, Huge Size,....,etc.
- Draconic Runes: As a True Dragon, Draconic Language comes naturally to her and gives her mastery over that set of powerful runes.
- Draconic Vision: [[True Dragons]]' eyes are special by nature. It allows them to see the world as it really is which means they can't be fooled by illusions, tricks, concealment,...,etc.
- [[Draconic Energy]]: True Dragons can manipulate and generate their own brand of energy, which is superior to all Mortal races energy.
- True [[Dragon Heart]]: As a True Dragon, Tiamat also has a Dragon Heart that act as a nuclear reactor for her energy.
God (Primordial) Physiology: As a Primordial Goddess, Tiamat is an immensely powerful being that has existed prior to or since the beginning of time. She possesses all of the [[Primordial Gods]] abilities like, Superhuman Strength, Superhuman Speed and Reflex, Enhanced Durability, Enhanced Agility, Enhanced Sense, Regeneration, Divine Power, High Divinity, Blessing and Curse Bestowal, True [[Immortality]], Concept Manipulation, Resurrection,...,etc.
