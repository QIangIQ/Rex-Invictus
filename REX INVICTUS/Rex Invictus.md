**[[Rex Invictus]]** is a [[Percy Jackson and the Olympians|Percy Jackson]] AU by the vault's author (FanFiction.net story 14337399). A millennia-old [[Percy Jackson]], the last guardian of ruined [[Olympus Primaris]], is sent back by [[Chaos]] into his young body in the present day with the AI [[Minerva (AI)|Minerva]]. The story cuts between that present and the far-future [[Undying Olympian Empire]] about 14,000 years later.

This note is the vault's home page. Every folder has an entry point linked below.
