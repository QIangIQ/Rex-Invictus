The Solar System Destroyer-Class Spaceship, commonly referred to as a Solar System [[Destroyer]], is a class of colossal strategic spacecraft used by the Empire of Light. Rather than functioning like a conventional warship, it operates as an enormous spaceborne fortress capable of annihilating an entire stellar system from outside its boundaries. The first vessel encountered by [[Victor Elderblood]] was positioned far outside the Solar System in which his reconnaissance clone was fighting, was itself described as much larger than an entire Solar System, possessed several massive cannons, and destroyed the complete system with a single bombardment.[1]
The class was formally identified later by Velnorah from the information Victor obtained during the reconnaissance mission. Ariel Elderblood explained Velnorah's findings to the [[Heavenly Father]]: Solar System Destroyers remain in the empty regions between Solar Systems and attack the core of the target system's star, with the resulting attack decimating everything around it.[2] Its demonstrated destructive capability corresponds directly to the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s later CODE: V-05 — Solar System Destroyer Level threat category.[3]

### Description
The Solar System [[Destroyer]] encountered during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s first direct confrontation with the Empire of Light was an artificial structure on a scale far beyond the ordinary spacecraft previously encountered by the [[Elderblood Empire]]. After Victor absorbed [[Solus 969]] and gained access to his memories, he became aware of a structure located extremely far outside the Solar System. The construction was explicitly described as being much larger than an entire Solar System and resembling a massive fortress rather than a normal ship. Several enormous cannons were built into the structure and could be turned toward a distant target system.[1]
Its size does not constitute its primary threat. Solar System Destroyers are strategic stellar weapons designed to attack from the empty Space separating Solar Systems rather than entering the system they intend to eliminate. Velnorah's analysis established that the class targets the very core of a system's central star and uses the resulting attack to destroy the surrounding Solar System.[2] The vessel that attacked Victor demonstrated this capability directly when its cannons turned toward him, Space was overwhelmed by the resulting release of power and Victor's clone was vaporized together with the entire Solar System.[1]

### Appearance
Only one Solar System [[Destroyer]] has been directly described in detail. It appeared as a gigantic artificial construction standing motionless outside its target Solar System, at such an enormous distance that it could engage the system without entering it. Despite being classified as a spaceship, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] perceived it as something closer to a colossal space fortress. Its overall structure exceeded the scale of the Solar System it targeted and incorporated several immense cannons capable of turning toward a target across inter-system distances.[1]
The novel has not provided a precise numerical length, width or mass for the vessel, nor has its interior layout, crew complement, armor composition or propulsion system been shown. Its only definitive size comparison is nevertheless extraordinary: the observed vessel was larger than an entire Solar System.[1]

### Operational Method
Solar System Destroyers employ a form of warfare centered on eliminating the astronomical foundation of the enemy's territory instead of fighting through each planet individually. According to Velnorah, the vessel remains within the empty Space between Solar Systems and attacks the core of the target star. The attack then decimates everything associated with that stellar system.[2]

### Armament

#### Solar System Destruction Cannons
The observed Solar System [[Destroyer]] possessed several massive cannons integrated into its fortress-like structure. After [[Solus 969]] died and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] learned of the vessel through his memories, the fortress rotated these cannons toward Victor's Solar System and discharged its weapons.[1]
The resulting firepower erased the entire Solar System. Victor's clone, the battlefield, the local Empire of Light infrastructure and the rest of the system were destroyed together rather than the weapon merely striking one planet or military position.[1] Victor reacted to the scale of the attack with excitement, explicitly recognizing it as the power to destroy an entire Solar System and praising the weapon's firepower immediately before he was vaporized.[1]
The exact relationship between the multiple cannons has not yet been explained. The story does not establish whether each individual cannon possesses Solar-System-destroying output by itself or whether the combined battery is required to produce the demonstrated attack.

#### Stellar-Core Targeting
Chapter 1149 gives additional technical context absent from the initial destruction scene. Velnorah determined that Solar System [[Destroyer]]-Class Spaceships attack the very core of a star. The resulting stellar destruction then decimates the surrounding Solar System.[2]
This means the vessel does not need to individually target every astronomical object inside the system. The central star itself becomes the focal point through which system-wide annihilation is achieved.

#### Extreme-Range Bombardment
The vessel does not need to approach the target star before firing. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] observed the fortress outside the Solar System, in a place very far away, while Velnorah later generalized this behavior by explaining that the class remains in the empty Space between Solar Systems.[1][2]
Its weapons therefore possess effective ranges spanning inter-system distances. The precise maximum distance has not been quantified, but the first demonstrated attack proves that the Solar System [[Destroyer]] can eliminate a system while remaining far beyond its normal planetary and stellar boundaries.

### Relationship to the Empire of Light's Stellar Infrastructure
The Solar System [[Destroyer]] was not the only method through which the Empire of Light attempted to destroy the Solar System during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s reconnaissance mission. Before the external fortress fired, Victor sensed that the enemy was already doing something to the system's sun and was surprised that the Empire was willing to casually erase an entire Solar System simply to deal with him.[1]
One of Victor's clones subsequently infiltrated the Empire of Light's base of operations on the star. At its center was an Artifact containing pure Positive Energy, which served as the central power source for the Empire's infrastructure throughout that Solar System. Victor extracted the system's information before destroying the Artifact. The local network immediately reported that its central power source had been destroyed, all activated systems throughout the Solar System had gone offline, its existing countermeasure had been deactivated and the destabilization of the star could no longer proceed.[1]
The same warning system then announced that an attack against the Empire's main property had been detected and that additional countermeasures were being activated. Shortly afterward Victor killed [[Solus 969]], obtained his memories and discovered the enormous fortress waiting outside the Solar System.[1] The sequence demonstrates that the Empire of Light possessed more than one method of destroying or sacrificing the system: its local infrastructure was already attempting to destabilize the star, while the external Solar System Destroyer remained available as a vastly larger strategic response after the local system failed.
These mechanisms should not be conflated. The Positive-Energy Artifact and its local star-destabilization system were destroyed by Victor before they could complete their function; the Solar System Destroyer was the separate external fortress whose later bombardment actually erased the Solar System.[1]

### Capabilities
- Solar System Destruction: The defining capability of the class. Its first demonstrated attack completely erased the Solar System containing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s clone.[1]
- Stellar-Core Bombardment: Velnorah identified the vessel's operational method as attacking the core of the target star and decimating everything around it.[2]
- Inter-System Bombardment: Solar System Destroyers remain in the empty Space between stellar systems rather than needing to enter the target Solar System.[2]
- Extreme Attack Range: The first vessel was positioned extremely far outside Victor's Solar System yet could accurately direct its weapons toward the system and destroy it.[1]
- Multiple Massive Cannons: The observed fortress possessed several enormous cannons that turned toward Victor before the system-destroying attack was released.[1]
- Solar-System-Scale Construction: The observed Solar System [[Destroyer]] was physically larger than the complete Solar System it targeted.[1]
- Strategic Extermination: By destroying the target system as a whole, the vessel can bypass conventional planetary warfare, defensive campaigns and occupation entirely.[1][2]
- Threat to High-Level Gods: After studying the attack, the [[Heavenly Father]] concluded that he could not withstand it head-on without injury and would at minimum be severely wounded.[2]

### Power
The Solar System [[Destroyer]]'s destructive power is directly demonstrated rather than based solely upon statements. Chapter 1134 depicts the vessel attacking once and vaporizing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s reconnaissance clone together with the entire Solar System.[1] The narration further states that defending against the attack was impossible for the restricted clone and that only Victor's Original self could accomplish such a defense.[1]
This is an important distinction when evaluating the feat. The clone was intentionally much weaker and more limited than Victor's true existence, but it still retained extraordinary combat knowledge, Grandmaster martial ability, Soul-related powers and access to Negativity-derived abilities. The Solar System Destroyer's attack exceeded what that incarnation could defend against outright, while the narration explicitly placed the original Victor above that defensive requirement.[1]

### History

#### First Encounter
Near the end of Volume 8, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] sent an intentionally restricted independent clone into unknown territory so he could investigate civilizations existing beyond the regions he already controlled without exposing his original body, family or Empire. The clone eventually encountered the Empire of Light and engaged increasingly advanced soldiers, technological systems and Solus units while the original Victor and his wives observed the encounter and collected information.[1]
During the battle Victor sensed that the Empire was manipulating the Solar System's star. A secondary clone infiltrated the stellar base, eliminated the personnel there, discovered a sphere of pure Positive Energy serving as the system's central power source and copied the Empire's local information. Victor transferred the captured data back to his original body, where Velnorah isolated it inside a separate Dimension rather than allowing potentially hostile foreign information direct access to the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s networks.[1]
Victor then destroyed the Positive-Energy Artifact. The Empire's systems reported the collapse of their local power infrastructure and the failure of the attempt to destabilize the star, but also announced that additional countermeasures had been activated in response to the destruction of major Imperial property.[1] Victor subsequently stopped prolonging his fight, slaughtered the remaining forces and killed [[Solus 969]]. Only after absorbing his enemy and reading his memories did Victor understand why 969 had remained calm even while dying.
Outside the Solar System, at an enormous distance, stood the Solar System [[Destroyer]]. The fortress was larger than the entire system and already possessed several massive cannons capable of turning toward Victor's location. The vessel fired shortly afterward, and the resulting attack vaporized Victor's clone together with the complete Solar System.[1]

#### Aftermath
Although the clone was destroyed, the reconnaissance mission was strategically successful. The original [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] remained safe, the enemy had gained no direct path toward his family and the [[Elderblood Empire]] now possessed captured information concerning the military technology, ideology and operational methods of an Upper-Sector civilization. The Solar System [[Destroyer]]'s attack nevertheless provided undeniable evidence that the scale of warfare beyond Victor's Sector was radically greater than the conflicts his civilization had previously fought.[1]
At the beginning of Volume 9, Victor treated the encounter as a warning rather than immediately crossing into enemy territory. The Empire began consolidating its Sector, mapping its galaxies, expanding military infrastructure, reinforcing allied Pantheons and preparing for wars against civilizations capable of treating astronomical destruction as conventional military strategy.[4] The experience fed directly into the development of the Empire's new threat-classification system and its shift toward weapons and military forces intended to remain relevant on Solar-System, Galactic and eventually greater scales.[3]

### Heavenly Father's Assessment
The most important later analysis of the Solar System [[Destroyer]] occurred when [[Ariel Elderblood]] visited the [[Heavenly Father]] during Volume 9. By this point the [[Elderblood Empire]] had undergone an enormous period of accelerated development and was actively preparing its allies for the eventual Upper-Sector war. Ariel warned that combatants classified around CODE: V-03 — Continental Level could appear merely as expendable foot soldiers among the forces they expected to face.[2]
Ariel then gave the Heavenly Father the Empire's detailed reports concerning its first encounters with hostile external factions. After examining the recording of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s clone and the destruction of the Solar System, the Heavenly Father immediately focused on the final shot and concluded that he could not withstand the attack head-on without consequences. He estimated that a direct hit would, at minimum, leave him severely injured.[2]
Ariel identified the weapon using Velnorah's analysis and explained that it was a Solar System Destroyer-Class Spaceship operating from the empty regions between stellar systems and attacking the core of a star to decimate everything around it.[2] The Heavenly Father's reaction made clear how abnormal this military technology remained even from the perspective of one of Earth's oldest and most powerful Divine Beings.

### Strategic Significance
The Solar System [[Destroyer]] represents one of the clearest examples of the difference between warfare in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s original supernatural civilization and warfare among major powers beyond his Sector. Planetary destruction had previously been associated primarily with extremely powerful Gods, Dragons or singular supernatural beings. The Empire of Light had transformed destruction on an even greater astronomical scale into a technological military asset that could be positioned outside a Solar System and ordered to erase it.[1][2]
Ariel explained that the Upper Sectors possess no comparable territorial limitation to the smaller protected Sectors and that the surrounding Cosmos continues expanding. Civilizations operating there could therefore afford losses on a scale that would be unthinkable to Earth's old Pantheons; she stated that they could potentially afford to lose a few galaxies if necessary.[2] Both Ariel and the [[Heavenly Father]] regarded such a mentality with disgust because a single galaxy could contain trillions of lives, but the statement illustrates the scale on which Upper-Sector powers could consider military loss and territorial destruction.
The Heavenly Father and Ariel also reasoned that the greater Cosmos could not simply be completely lawless. If civilizations possessing this kind of firepower were free to invade weaker Sectors without restriction, Lower-Sector civilizations would have little chance to develop naturally. They therefore concluded that some greater order imposed by the [[Primordial Entities]] must regulate interactions between the different levels of the Cosmos, although Ariel noted that they would only understand the full political structure once they actually entered those regions.[2]

### Limitations of Current Information
The class has only received limited direct technical exposition. Its exact dimensions beyond the Solar-System-scale comparison, construction materials, Energy source, crew requirements, propulsion system, movement speed, defensive systems, recharge period and production cost remain unknown. The number of Solar System Destroyers possessed by the Empire of Light has likewise not been stated, and the vessel that destroyed [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s clone has never received an individual name.
It is also unknown whether every cannon mounted upon a Solar System [[Destroyer]] independently possesses Solar-System-destroying output or whether the demonstrated attack requires several of them to operate together. What is canonically established is the result of the complete weapon platform's attack: the entire Solar System was erased.[1]
