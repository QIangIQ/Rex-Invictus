A Continental Spaceship is a class of enormous interstellar vessel developed by the [[Elderblood Empire]] during its transition from a predominantly planetary civilization into an expanding interstellar power. The defining characteristic established for the class is its extraordinary capacity: a Continental Spaceship is large enough to house an entire country. By Chapter 1068, the Empire's Continental Spaceships had already been completed alongside its dedicated military Warships, forming part of the logistical and strategic infrastructure intended to support [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s rapidly expanding civilization.[1]
Unlike the Empire's Warships, which are identified separately as combat vessels, Continental Spaceships are presented primarily as enormous strategic platforms capable of housing populations and supporting Imperial command operations. Victor Elderblood personally possessed an unnamed vessel of the class whose Command Room he used while overseeing the development of the Emperor's Soldiers. Victor explicitly designated this ship as one of his external Command Centers, contrasting it with the significantly safer personal Dimensions contained within his Soul.[1]

### Development
Continental Spaceships emerged during the period in which [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his family were converting the [[Elderblood Empire]] into a civilization capable of operating beyond individual worlds. Earlier Imperial development had already introduced increasingly sophisticated spacecraft, artificial intelligence, Runic technology and large-scale supernatural engineering, but the creation of an organized interstellar fleet required vessels capable of carrying far more than ordinary crews or military detachments.
By Chapter 1068, Velnorah informed Victor that both the Continental Spaceships and the Empire's Warships were ready.[1] Their completion occurred alongside the development of the Emperor's new soldiers and the broader military infrastructure required to support Imperial expansion. This places Continental Spaceships among the foundational large-scale vessels of the early interstellar Elderblood Empire, preceding the later accelerated era in which Imperial spacecraft would progress toward planetary, Solar-System and eventually Galactic levels of military engineering.
The novel does not identify a single individual as the sole inventor of the class. They are therefore best attributed to the Elderblood Empire and its technological establishment rather than specifically to Velnorah alone. Velnorah was directly involved in supervising the military and technological projects discussed during their introduction, but the exact distribution of engineers, Runemasters, Divine craftsmen and other specialists involved in construction has not been stated.[1]

### Design and Scale
The full external dimensions of a standard Continental Spaceship have never been provided. The principal canonical measurement of the class is functional rather than numerical: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s personal Continental Spaceship was explicitly described as being capable of housing an entire country.[1]
This description establishes a scale far beyond conventional transport spacecraft. A vessel of this class can contain enough internal capacity for a country-scale population or equivalent occupation, although the chapter does not quantify the number of inhabitants involved, the internal land area or whether the vessel achieves this capacity primarily through enormous physical construction, spatial expansion, or a combination of Imperial technologies.
Continental Spaceships are confirmed to contain specialized command infrastructure. Victor's personal ship possessed a dedicated Command Room, from which he observed and coordinated the development of the Emperor's Soldiers with Velnorah.[1] This demonstrates that at least some vessels of the class are intended to serve as strategic administrative platforms in addition to functioning as immense transportation or habitation vessels.

### Country-Scale Capacity
The defining known capability of a Continental Spaceship is its ability to house an entire country.[1] The statement is important because the novel does not merely compare the ship's size to a large building or city; it directly establishes national-scale capacity within a mobile interstellar vessel.
The precise interpretation of "house an entire country" has not been further quantified. The chapter does not provide a population figure, usable internal area or a complete list of infrastructure contained within a standard vessel. It therefore cannot currently be stated that every Continental Spaceship automatically contains every city, industry, ecosystem or governmental structure belonging to a conventional nation. What is directly established is that the class possesses sufficient overall capacity to accommodate something on the scale of an entire country.
This capacity makes Continental Spaceships useful for large-scale population movement, military deployment, colonial expansion and strategic relocation even where the story has not individually demonstrated each possible application.

### Fleet Control
The Imperial fleet was initially intended to operate through distributed control. Separate beings would have been assigned to control the individual Continental Spaceships and Warships. The creation of [[Akasha Elderblood]] fundamentally altered this arrangement.[1]
Akasha's connection to the Empire's systems allows her to simultaneously control all of the completed spaceships and Warships by herself. Because of this, the original requirement for an independent controller assigned to every ship ceased to be technologically necessary.[1]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] nevertheless rejected complete dependence upon Akasha.
Even after being informed that Akasha could control the entire fleet alone, Victor immediately stated that operators were still necessary. He therefore planned another Legion of Clones focused primarily upon support functions, using Laura's memories and Blood as the foundation for those personnel.[1]
The resulting structure demonstrates an important principle of Imperial military organization: centralized intelligence can coordinate the fleet, but human or supernatural operators remain part of the system rather than being eliminated entirely.

### Command-Center Function
Continental Spaceships are capable of functioning as high-level command vessels. The clearest example is [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s personal ship, which he explicitly intended to use as one of the Emperor's external Command Centers.[1]
Victor distinguished these external command facilities from his safest possible headquarters. His personal Dimensions remained inside his Soul and therefore possessed considerably greater security, but relying exclusively upon inaccessible internal territory would not be practical for every Imperial operation. Continental Spaceships offered a mobile physical location from which Victor could personally supervise military and governmental activities while remaining connected to the wider Empire.
His ship's Command Room was already functional when the vessel itself remained otherwise unstaffed, demonstrating that a Continental Spaceship could begin operating through Victor, Velnorah and Akasha's systems before receiving its permanent personnel.[1]

### Victor's Personal Continental Spaceship
The only individually described standard Continental Spaceship belongs personally to [[Victor Elderblood]]. At the time of its introduction, the vessel had no name and no personnel, but Victor was already using its Command Room while coordinating the training and creation of his future soldiers.[1]
The ship was explicitly described as:
- a Continental Spaceship;[1]
- capable of housing an entire country;[1]
- equipped with a dedicated Command Room;[1]
- intended to function as one of Victor's external Command Centers;[1]
- unnamed at the time of introduction;[1]
- and still awaiting permanent personnel.[1]
Victor used the vessel during the final preparations surrounding the Emperor's Soldiers. From its Command Room he discussed fleet readiness, the new Clone Legions, support personnel and the accelerated training process with Velnorah.[1]

### Operators and Support Personnel
Although Akasha removed the absolute need for individual pilots or controllers assigned to each ship, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] deliberately preserved a distributed personnel structure. Immediately after Velnorah explained that Akasha could control the complete fleet, Victor responded that operators were still required and proposed the creation of an additional Clone Legion specifically focused on support.[1]
Laura was selected as the biological and memory foundation for this support force. Victor did not publicly explain the complete reason for choosing her, claiming that his instincts told him it was the correct decision, although Velnorah immediately suspected that he possessed information regarding Laura that he had not disclosed.[1]
The support Legion was intended to complement rather than replace Akasha. This distinction became increasingly important as the Empire expanded: Akasha could coordinate enormous technological systems, but Victor did not want the civilization to become helpless if one central intelligence became unavailable. Continental Spaceships therefore belong to an Imperial infrastructure built around both centralized coordination and distributed personnel.

### Strategic Role
Continental Spaceships provide the [[Elderblood Empire]] with mobile infrastructure suitable for operations extending far beyond a single planetary system. Their country-scale capacity allows an enormous quantity of personnel to remain aboard a single vessel, while their command facilities allow at least certain ships to function as mobile administrative centers.[1]
Their confirmed or directly implied strategic roles include:
- Large-Scale Habitation: A Continental Spaceship can house an entire country.[1]
- Strategic Transportation: As interstellar spaceships possessing country-scale capacity, they provide the Empire with a platform suitable for moving enormous populations and military resources between worlds.
- Mobile Command: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] designated his personal vessel as one of his external Command Centers and operated from its Command Room.[1]
- Fleet Operations: Continental Spaceships form part of the same Imperial fleet architecture as dedicated Warships and can be controlled through Akasha.[1]
- Distributed Operation: The Empire maintains operators and support personnel despite Akasha's ability to control the fleet centrally.[1]
- Imperial Expansion: The class appeared during the development of the infrastructure required for Victor's civilization to expand beyond its original planetary territories.

### Relationship with Warships
Chapter 1068 explicitly distinguishes Continental Spaceships from Warships. Velnorah reported that both classes had been completed, and both were incorporated into the fleet Akasha could control.[1]
Continental Spaceships are associated with country-scale accommodation and command functions, whereas Warships are separately identified military vessels. The available chapter does not establish the precise armament of standard Continental Spaceships and does not state that the class possesses the same combat specialization as dedicated Warships.
This distinction should therefore be preserved. Although a Continental Spaceship may logically possess defensive systems as an Imperial interstellar vessel, no specific weapon, attack potency or defensive rating has been provided for the standard class.
Later Elderblood military development produced dedicated spacecraft capable of destroying planets and complete Solar Systems, demonstrating how far Imperial fleet technology eventually progressed.[2] Those later weapon specifications should not automatically be retroactively assigned to the original Continental Spaceship class without direct evidence.

### Relationship with Shiro-Hime
[[Shiro-Hime]] predates the mass-produced Continental Spaceships and was described as a Strategic Continental Ship. It was a unique personal vessel belonging to [[Violet Elderblood Snow]], constructed through Velnorah's technology, Divine craftsmanship and Runes, powered by a stabilized yellow star and equipped with a cannon capable of disintegrating a planet.[3]
The similarity in terminology suggests a relationship between Shiro-Hime's strategic design philosophy and later large Imperial spacecraft, but Chapter 1068 does not explicitly identify Shiro-Hime as a standard vessel of the later Continental Spaceship class. Shiro-Hime should therefore remain treated as a unique Strategic Continental Ship rather than having every property of the later class automatically applied to it.

### Later Imperial Development
Continental Spaceships represent an early stage of the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s transition into a mature Galactic civilization. By the accelerated Imperial era, the scale of Imperial spacecraft had increased dramatically. Super spaceships could contain hundreds of thousands of square kilometres of Runically expanded interior territory and carry tens of millions of occupants, while military vessels could possess enough firepower to destroy entire Solar Systems.[2]
[[Aphrodite]] Elderblood eventually received a super spaceship measuring approximately 60 km × 15 km externally, containing more than 300,000 km² of Runically expanded internal area, capable of accommodating approximately 50 million beings and possessing Solar-System-destroying firepower.[2] Such vessels demonstrate the later technological evolution of the same broad Imperial principle first embodied by Continental Spaceships: spacecraft could function simultaneously as transportation systems, strategic platforms, population centers and military infrastructure.
This later development does not mean every original Continental Spaceship possessed equivalent weapons or spatial dimensions. Rather, it illustrates how the Empire continued expanding the concept of civilization-scale spacecraft after the class was introduced.

### Unknown Specifications
The novel has not provided the standard external dimensions, internal area, maximum population figure, construction time, Energy source, propulsion method, travel speed, armor composition, defensive systems or armament of the Continental Spaceship class. It has likewise not stated how many vessels were produced or whether all ships possess the same internal configuration as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s personal vessel.
The statement that a Continental Spaceship can house an entire country should not be converted into an arbitrary numerical population or land-area figure until the novel supplies one. Similarly, later Solar-System-destroying Imperial warships and super spaceships should not be used to assign Solar-System-level firepower to the original Continental Spaceship class without explicit evidence.
