Elderblood Warships are the dedicated military spacecraft of the [[Elderblood Empire]], forming the combat-oriented counterpart to the Empire's [[Continental Spaceships]] and other large-scale civilian, command and expeditionary vessels. The first generation was completed by Chapter 1068 as part of [[Victor Elderblood]] and Velnorah's preparation of a professional Imperial military, with [[Akasha Elderblood]] capable of simultaneously controlling the entire fleet.[1]
Warship development continued alongside the Empire's expansion from an interplanetary civilization into a Galactic power. Early fleets already carried tens of thousands of God-comparable soldiers and maintained orbital bombardment systems, while later military vessels included Capital Ships assigned to Imperial Empresses and spacecraft capable of destroying planets or complete Solar Systems.[2][3][4] The term Elderblood Warships therefore refers to a broad military vessel category rather than one single standardized model.

### Development
The first explicit confirmation of the Imperial warship fleet occurs while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Velnorah were completing the infrastructure required for the Emperor's Soldiers. When Victor asked for an update on preparations, Velnorah reported that both the [[Continental Spaceships]] and the Warships were ready.[1] This establishes the two as separate categories from their introduction: Continental Spaceships fulfilled enormous habitation and command roles, while Warships formed the military component of the developing Imperial fleet.
The original plan required separate Beings to control each vessel individually. The creation of [[Akasha Elderblood]] rendered this technically unnecessary because she could control all of the spaceships and Warships simultaneously. Victor nevertheless refused to eliminate independent personnel. He immediately insisted that operators were still necessary and ordered the creation of another Clone Legion specialized in support functions, using [[Laura Bloodhunter]]'s Blood and memories as the foundation.[1] This established one of the core principles of Elderblood fleet doctrine: Akasha can centralize and optimize the fleet, but Imperial ships are not intended to become completely useless without her.
Warship technology continued improving throughout the following expansion. By Chapter 1083, Imperial fleets were already conducting independent planetary conquest operations, while by Chapter 1136 specialized Capital Ships were ready for deployment under senior Empresses. During the Great Acceleration, military shipbuilding progressed still further until the Empire's premier invasion forces routinely included vessels capable of destroying planets and Solar Systems.[2][3][4]

### Fleet Control

#### Akasha Network
[[Akasha Elderblood]] is capable of simultaneously controlling the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s entire early fleet of [[Continental Spaceships]] and Warships.[1] Her presence removes the technological necessity for every spacecraft to possess a completely independent primary controller, allowing information, navigation, tactical coordination and fleet movements to be managed through a civilization-scale intelligence.
This does not mean Imperial warships operate exclusively through Akasha. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] deliberately retained independent operators, commanders and support personnel because complete reliance upon one intelligence would create an unnecessary strategic vulnerability. The support Clone Legion planned in Chapter 1068 was intended specifically to provide personnel capable of operating alongside the centralized system.[1]
This distributed structure remained visible in later fleets. Operators aboard Imperial vessels continued controlling local systems, monitoring commanders, executing orbital-fire orders and maintaining mission records even though Akasha and other artificial intelligences could provide higher-level coordination.[2]

#### Operators
Operators form an important part of Imperial naval organization. During the Chapter 1083 Hive operation, [[Clare Bloodhunter]] explicitly entrusted her ships to their operators while she personally descended into combat. The operators remained aboard the fleet and coordinated orbital positioning, weapons and communications.[2]
The mature fleet continued using professional operators centuries later. The [[Explorer-class Starship]] deployed in Andromeda possessed a lead operator capable of coordinating Spartan deployments, orbital cannons and mission records, demonstrating that the Empire never abandoned [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s original insistence upon distributed personnel despite the continued growth of Akasha's capabilities.[5]

### Early Fleet Operations

#### Hive Campaign
One of the clearest early demonstrations of Elderblood fleet warfare occurred during the campaign against the Hive organism encountered during Imperial expansion. [[Clare Bloodhunter]] commanded a force of ten spaceships carrying approximately 50,000 soldiers.[2]
The force was already extraordinary by the standards of the old supernatural world. Each of the approximately 50,000 soldiers was described as capable of contending with a low-level God and carried plasma weaponry powerful enough to melt hostile organisms. The fleet possessed operators, tactical simulations, inter-ship communications and orbital cannons, allowing Clare to coordinate planetary operations without personally commanding every individual unit.[2]
Clare ordered the ten vessels to remain in strategically important positions around the planet and keep their orbital cannons ready to fire. Her commanders then descended to eliminate the central Hive while the ships remained in orbit, preventing the greater Hive intelligence from simply escaping through another organism elsewhere on the world.[2]
The operation demonstrated that Elderblood warships function as more than troop transports. Even before the Empire's massive accelerated development, fleets already operated as coordinated orbital weapons platforms capable of supporting tens of thousands of enhanced soldiers during planetary conquest.

### Orbital Warfare
Orbital bombardment is one of the earliest directly demonstrated functions associated with Imperial fleets. During Clare's campaign, all ten vessels were ordered into important orbital positions with their cannons prepared to fire at any moment.[2]
The chapter does not provide the exact destructive output of those early orbital cannons, but their role was strategic rather than merely defensive. The ships were positioned around the planet so that the Imperial force could maximize destruction against hostile organisms while preventing the Hive from transferring its central intelligence elsewhere.
Later Imperial expeditions continued using orbital weapons. In Chapter 1163, the fleet surrounding an infected world in Andromeda possessed orbital cannons that could be prepared immediately if [[Frederick Winter]] and [[Andrew Walter]] required support.[5] Whether every such cannon was mounted upon a conventional Warship, an [[Explorer-class Starship]], or specialized orbital platforms is not individually specified, but orbital fire remained a standard component of Imperial fleet operations.

### Capital Ships
By Chapter 1136, the [[Elderblood Empire]] possessed a formally identified category of Capital Ships. [[Akasha Elderblood]] informed [[Agnes Elderblood Snow]], [[Amaterasu]] Elderblood and [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]] that their Capital Ships were ready for deployment and that mission destinations had already been selected.[3]
Their troops were simultaneously placed on standby, establishing the Capital Ships as major military assets directly associated with large Imperial formations rather than ordinary personal transportation. Agnes, Amaterasu and Leona immediately changed into Imperial military uniforms and departed to begin their assignments.[3]
The chapter does not provide dimensions, individual names, armaments or detailed internal layouts for these vessels. It nevertheless confirms that the mature pre-Acceleration Imperial fleet already possessed a hierarchy in which important commanders or Empresses could be assigned dedicated Capital Ships for major deployments.

### Great Acceleration
The approximately 200,000-year Great Acceleration transformed the scale of Imperial military engineering. The Empire's scientists, engineers, Witches, Runemasters, Divine Beings and artificial personnel were given enormous subjective spans of Time to develop technology while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his Empresses continuously supported and supervised the civilization.
By Imperial Year 1000, the First Legion: Spartan Legion had been established as the Empire's primary direct-attack and invasion force. Its responsibilities included conquering planets, breaking stellar defenses and leading campaigns against external enemies. Among its ordinary listed military units were spaceships capable of exploding planets and Solar Systems.[4]
This marks a major technological progression from the Chapter 1083 fleet. Early Warships already supported planet-wide warfare through powerful soldiers and orbital weapons, but later Imperial military spacecraft themselves could carry weaponry reaching Planetary and Solar-System scales.
The wording of Chapter 1139 does not establish that every vessel in the Elderblood fleet possesses Solar-System-destroying firepower. Instead, it demonstrates that spacecraft at both planetary and Solar-System destructive levels existed as recurring assets within the mature Spartan Legion.

### Planet-Destroying Warships
Planet-destroying spacecraft became part of the mature Imperial military rather than remaining unique artifacts. The Spartan Legion's standard asset list explicitly included spaceships capable of destroying planets alongside Solar-System-class vessels.[4]
Planetary destructive technology was not unprecedented within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s civilization. [[Shiro-Hime]], created much earlier for [[Violet Elderblood Snow]], already possessed a cannon capable of reducing an entire planet to cosmic dust.[6] Shiro-Hime was a specialized Divine Artifact and personal strategic vessel, however, while Chapter 1139 demonstrates the later incorporation of similar or greater destructive scales into formal Imperial military formations.

### Solar-System-Destroying Warships
The highest explicitly stated destructive category of ordinary Imperial Legion spacecraft is the ability to destroy an entire Solar System.[4] The Spartan Legion fields spaceships capable of operating at this scale, placing its heaviest regular vessels within the Empire's CODE: V-05 — Solar System [[Destroyer]] Level threat category in terms of destructive potential.[3]
The exact method used by these vessels has not been described. They should not automatically be assumed to operate like the [[Solar System Destroyer-Class Spaceship]] of the [[Veillon Empire]], whose weapons specifically attack the core of a star from the empty Space between stellar systems. Elderblood vessels are independently stated to possess Solar-System-destroying capability, but no chapter has yet explained whether they destroy a central star, directly bombard the complete system, manipulate Space-Time, employ Runic weapons or use another mechanism.

#### Aphrodite's Super Spaceship
The clearest individually described Elderblood vessel possessing Solar-System-level firepower belongs to [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood. Her unnamed Super Spaceship measures approximately 60 km in length and 15 km in width externally while containing more than 300,000 km² of Runically expanded internal Space and enough capacity for approximately 50 million beings. It is explicitly capable of destroying an entire Solar System.[4]
The vessel demonstrates that the Empire could combine enormous habitation capacity with strategic weaponry in a ship whose physical hull remained vastly smaller than the internal territory it contained.

### Spartan Legion Fleet
The Spartan Legion is the Imperial military branch most directly associated with large-scale offensive warship use. Created by [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood, [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] and [[Rose Adrasteia Elderblood]], the Legion exists to invade, conquer and secure territory for the Empire.[4]
Its operational responsibilities explicitly include breaking stellar defenses and leading campaigns against external civilizations. War Dragons, armored soldiers, siege mages and Planetary-to-Solar-System-destroying spaceships are listed among its common assets.[4]
This indicates that mature Elderblood fleet warfare is not based upon ships operating independently. Warships support supernatural infantry, Dragon units, mages and commanders whose individual power can itself reach planetary or greater levels. The fleet therefore functions as one component of a combined-arms military rather than replacing supernatural combatants.

### Explorer Fleets
By Chapter 1163, large Imperial fleets had become normal enough that more than fifty ships could be dispatched to a regional biological crisis within the Andromeda Galaxy. The largest vessel in that operation was an [[Explorer-class Starship]], which functioned as the expedition's command platform while accompanying ships supported planetary operations.[5]
The Andromeda deployment involved orbital cannons, Spartan forces, scientific acquisition orders from the Fourth Legion and professional fleet operators. The event was treated as a regional Imperial response rather than complete military mobilization, demonstrating the enormous expansion of fleet capacity by the late accelerated era.
Explorer-class vessels should not be considered synonymous with Warships. They are expeditionary command ships, while Warships are the Empire's dedicated military combat spacecraft. Both vessel types can nevertheless operate inside the same Imperial fleet.

### Strategic Superweapons

#### Polaris
The ultimate confirmed development of Elderblood warship engineering is Polaris, although the vessel exists far above conventional Imperial Warships. Polaris is a classified Galaxy [[Destroyer]]-class Divine Artifact built in the form of a gigantic spaceship and developed as one of the Empire's principal strategic trump cards.[7]
The vessel can bend Reality, Space and Time through destructive Concepts, is compatible with Eldritch forces and possesses a weapon capable of channeling the Concept of <END>. Because no artificial cannon could permanently withstand END, the engineers instead created hundreds of replaceable firing mechanisms, each designed to survive only one complete discharge before disappearing.[7]
[[Hela Elderblood]] was assigned the first confirmed operational Polaris, while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] ordered a reserve vessel for himself. [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]], [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]], [[Aline Elderblood Valefar]] and later [[Pepper Scarlett Elderblood]] were ordered to continue making the design even more dangerous.[7]
Polaris should therefore be viewed as the extreme endpoint of Imperial strategic shipbuilding rather than a representative example of an ordinary Elderblood Warship.

### Military Doctrine
Elderblood fleet doctrine reflects [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s broader approach to Imperial warfare: overwhelming power is useful, but it should be combined with competent personnel, redundancy and independent decision-making. The Empire does not treat its Warships as autonomous weapons that eliminate the need for soldiers, nor does it reduce its soldiers to passive passengers transported by ships.
A typical Imperial operation can combine:
- Warships or other fleet vessels holding orbital positions;
- operators and artificial intelligences coordinating battlefield data;
- orbital cannons prepared for strategic bombardment;
- Emperor's Soldiers acting as commanders;
- mass-produced or conventional Imperial troops;
- Spartans serving as assault forces;
- War Dragons and supernatural specialists;
- Witches and siege mages;
- scientific personnel from [[Stellar Arsenal]];
- and higher commanders or Empresses capable of personally intervening if necessary.[2][4][5]
[[The Hive]] campaign provides an early example of this structure. Clare did not attempt to destroy over a trillion hostile organisms personally despite possessing overwhelming individual power. She used specialized commanders, fifty thousand soldiers, ten orbital ships, biological research and tactical planning to attack the Hive efficiently.[2] This mentality remained part of Imperial doctrine even as its technology became capable of destroying astronomical structures.

### Relationship with Akasha
Akasha acts as the central intelligence capable of integrating the Empire's warships into one enormous information and command network. Her processing capacity permits simultaneous control on a scale that would normally require countless independent artificial intelligences.[1]
The Empire nevertheless preserves local command. Operators remain aboard vessels, commanders possess independent authority and fleets can execute missions through established military hierarchies. This prevents Akasha from becoming a single point whose absence would completely paralyze Imperial military operations.
As Imperial territory eventually extended through numerous Solar Systems and galaxies, this mixed model became increasingly important. Centralized information could flow through Akasha while individual fleets, Legions and commanders continued acting autonomously within their assigned missions.

### Relationship with the Dragon Heart
The [[Dragon Heart]] predates the first confirmed Imperial Warship fleet and became one of the foundational Energy systems of the civilization. Created from Velnorah and Velina's prototype and transformed by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] through Draconic Runes, Divine materials, [[Junketsu]] and a fragment of his Soul, the Dragon Heart continuously generates enormous quantities of pure, unaltered Energy that can be converted into other forms.[8]
The novel has not stated that every Elderblood Warship carries an individual Dragon Heart. The artifact instead forms part of the broader Imperial Energy infrastructure that made increasingly extreme technological projects possible. Specific power cores used by standard Warships remain unidentified.

### Distinction from Other Spaceships

#### Continental Spaceships
[[Continental Spaceships]] and Warships were explicitly listed separately when both fleets became operational.[1] Continental Spaceships are enormous strategic vessels capable of housing an entire country and can function as mobile Command Centers, while Warships are the dedicated military combat component of the fleet.

#### Shiro-Hime
[[Shiro-Hime]] is a unique Strategic Continental Spaceship and complete Divine Artifact belonging to [[Violet Elderblood Snow]]. Its stabilized yellow-star core provides infinite Energy, and its principal cannon can disintegrate an entire planet into cosmic dust.[6] Although Shiro-Hime influenced the technological direction of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s civilization, the novel does not identify it as a standard Elderblood Warship.

#### Explorer-class Starships
Explorer-class Starships serve expeditionary and fleet-command functions. The first confirmed example was the largest vessel in a fleet of more than fifty ships operating in Andromeda.[5] They may operate alongside Warships but are not explicitly identified as the same class.

#### Polaris
Polaris is a specialized Galaxy [[Destroyer]]-class Divine Artifact and strategic superweapon rather than an ordinary fleet vessel.[7] Its Reality-, Space-, Time- and <END>-based systems place it far beyond the specifications confirmed for conventional Imperial Warships.
