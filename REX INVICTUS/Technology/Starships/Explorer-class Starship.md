An Explorer-class Starship is a class of large expeditionary spacecraft operated by the [[Elderblood Empire]] during its mature Galactic era. The class is designed for long-range operations throughout Imperial territory and can act as the central command vessel of a multi-ship exploration or intervention fleet. The first confirmed Explorer-class Starship appeared in the Andromeda Galaxy during an Imperial response to an aggressive newly emerged alien species, where it was explicitly described as the largest vessel in a fleet of more than fifty ships.[1]
The vessel served as the operational center of the expedition, carrying senior Imperial personnel, a lead operator and the systems necessary to coordinate planetary analysis, Spartan deployment, orbital weapons and communications between the various forces involved in the mission.[1] Unlike specialized strategic weapons such as Polaris or the [[Solar System Destroyer-Class Spaceship]], the Explorer-class is presented primarily as an expeditionary and command-oriented vessel rather than a dedicated astronomical destruction platform.

### Description
By the late accelerated era, the [[Elderblood Empire]] possessed territory extending across multiple galaxies and routinely maintained fleets capable of responding to incidents far from its original worlds. Explorer-class Starships formed part of this mature interstellar infrastructure. The only directly observed vessel of the class was deployed to a World Tree-bearing Imperial planet in Andromeda as the largest ship in a fleet consisting of more than fifty spacecraft.[1]
The chapter does not provide the vessel's numerical dimensions, but its designation as the fleet's largest ship establishes it as substantially more important than the smaller vessels accompanying it. Important personnel operated from aboard the ship, commands affecting the wider fleet were issued through its operators and planetary military support was coordinated from the vessel.[1]
Its interior included at least a launch platform from which high-level Imperial personnel could descend toward a planetary battlefield. The vessel also maintained communication with personnel operating on the surface and remained involved in the mission after the initial deployment.[1]

### Role
Explorer-class Starships function as major expeditionary vessels used when the Empire investigates, secures or responds to events occurring across its enormous territory. Their observed role combines exploration, command, military deployment and scientific support rather than focusing exclusively on direct ship-to-ship warfare.
During the Andromeda operation, the Explorer-class vessel coordinated a mission involving a previously unregistered species whose characteristics included extremely rapid reproduction, high adaptability and enough collective potential to threaten an entire planet. The ship's systems or associated Imperial network identified the targets, analyzed their properties, confirmed the safety of the planet's World Tree and processed a request from the Fourth Legion for biological samples.[1]
The mission demonstrates that Explorer-class vessels can participate in operations that simultaneously involve:
- planetary reconnaissance and threat assessment;
- scientific acquisition;
- military deployment;
- Spartan operations;
- orbital fire support;
- surface communications;
- and command coordination between different Imperial institutions.[1]
This makes the class particularly suited to the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s expansion model, in which newly encountered planets are not automatically destroyed or conquered without investigation. Military, scientific and administrative organizations can all participate in the same expedition depending upon the situation.

### Fleet Command
The Explorer-class Starship observed in Chapter 1163 functioned as the largest and operationally most important vessel within a fleet of more than fifty ships.[1] Although the chapter does not explicitly use the formal word "flagship", the ship effectively performed a command-vessel role during the planetary operation.
A lead operator aboard the vessel communicated directly with senior Imperial personnel and issued or relayed orders concerning the expedition. When [[Frederick Winter]] and [[Andrew Walter]] decided to personally descend toward the planet, the operator objected because their unnecessary exposure to danger could create serious consequences for the personnel responsible for the mission.[1]
After failing to convince them to remain aboard, the lead operator ordered that the incident be recorded and instructed the fleet to prepare both the orbital cannons and the Spartans to ensure that nothing happened to the two men.[1]
This scene demonstrates that the Explorer-class vessel is not simply a transport. It operates as an organized military command platform with professional operators capable of coordinating multiple assets across an active planetary operation.

### Planetary Analysis
The Andromeda expedition demonstrated the vessel's access to advanced planetary and biological intelligence systems. Imperial personnel aboard the fleet confirmed that the target planet's World Tree was safe before proceeding with broader military operations and identified the newly emerged species as an unregistered life-form.[1]
The species was analyzed as possessing:
- a high reproductive rate;
- significant adaptability;
- and enough potential to eventually destroy an entire planet.[1]
The information was processed rapidly enough that the Fourth Legion could request living biological specimens during the operation rather than waiting until the planetary threat had already been exterminated. The request was accepted and the Spartans were deployed accordingly.[1]
Whether these analyses were performed exclusively by systems physically aboard the Explorer-class vessel, by Akasha and the wider Imperial network, or through cooperation between both has not been specified. The observed fleet nevertheless possessed immediate access to the information while operating in Andromeda.

### Military Deployment

#### Spartan Deployment
The Explorer-class vessel participated in the deployment of [[The Spartans]], the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s principal large-scale invasion and combat forces. When the Fourth Legion requested samples of the hostile alien species, Spartan deployment was approved as part of the response.[1]
After Frederick and Andrew chose to descend personally, the lead operator ordered the Spartans prepared specifically as additional protection for them.[1] Unidentified objects subsequently entered the atmosphere after the two men, corresponding with the wider military deployment toward the surface.
The exact number of Spartans carried by the Explorer-class ship itself is not stated, nor is it established whether the entire force was embarked aboard the large vessel or distributed throughout the fleet.

#### Launch Platform
The observed Explorer-class Starship possesses a launch platform. Frederick and Andrew moved toward this area before beginning their descent to the planetary surface.[1]
A countdown preceded their deployment, after which both men descended from orbit toward the planet as crimson trails and impacted at separate locations.[1]
The scene confirms that the vessel supports direct orbital-to-surface deployment of powerful Imperial personnel. The technology responsible for the descent is not individually named.

#### Orbital Cannons
The expedition possessed orbital cannons that could be prepared to support the surface operation. After Frederick and Andrew descended, the lead operator ordered the cannons readied alongside the Spartans so that immediate support could be provided if the two men encountered serious danger.[1]
The chapter does not explicitly state whether the orbital cannons were physically mounted upon the Explorer-class vessel, distributed among the accompanying fleet or existed as separate orbital weapon platforms. They should therefore be treated as assets under the expedition's command rather than automatically identified as the Explorer-class ship's personal weapons.
No destructive output is given for these cannons.

### Communications
The Explorer-class expedition maintained real-time communication between the fleet and personnel operating on the planetary surface. The lead operator remained capable of speaking with Frederick and Andrew after they descended, and later observed the manifestation of [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] through the communications network.[1][2]
When Violet projected a portion of her power onto the planet and imposed Order over the hostile species, climate and atmosphere, the lead operator witnessed the event remotely and expressed disbelief at the strength displayed by an Imperial Empress.[2]
The event shows that the command vessel remained connected to planetary operations even after its principal combatants left orbit.

### Andromeda Expedition
The first known Explorer-class Starship appears during an Imperial operation in the Andromeda Galaxy. A planet already belonging to the [[Elderblood Empire]] had developed a previously unregistered species that rapidly overran the environment and became classified as a potential planetary threat.[1]
A fleet of more than fifty Imperial vessels arrived around the affected world. The largest vessel was identified as an Explorer-class Starship, aboard which [[Frederick Winter]] and [[Andrew Walter]] were present.[1] Both men had become enormously more powerful during the accelerated development of the Empire: Frederick had matured into one of the civilization's foremost Onmyō practitioners, while Andrew operated through the reorganized Imperial Inquisition and the supernatural systems connected to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Pantheon.
The fleet confirmed that the planet's World Tree was secure before analyzing the hostile species. Its reproductive capacity and adaptability were considered dangerous enough that the species had the potential to destroy the planet if left uncontrolled. At the same time, the Fourth Legion recognized scientific value in the organisms and requested biological samples rather than simply ordering complete extermination.[1]
[[The Spartans]] were selected for the operation, but Frederick became bored with remaining aboard the ship and announced that he would descend personally. The lead operator advised against the decision, only for Andrew to join Frederick as well. Unable to stop them, the operator ordered the objection entered into the official record and prepared the orbital cannons and Spartans as protection.[1]
Frederick and Andrew then launched from the ship and entered the atmosphere separately, creating enormous impact craters when they landed. They began fighting the alien organisms directly while Imperial forces continued descending behind them.[1]
The conflict eventually escalated into the invocation of Violet's Divine power. Violet projected herself onto the planet, examined the situation and imposed Order upon the hostile species and environment. The creatures became docile, the planetary climate stabilized and the atmosphere itself improved.[2] The lead operator remained connected to the operation through the fleet's communication system and witnessed the event.
After Violet departed, Andrew ordered the Imperial personnel back to work capturing the creatures and finishing the Fourth Legion's mission rather than exterminating the newly discovered species.[2]

### Scientific Operations
The Andromeda incident illustrates how Explorer-class expeditions support the scientific objectives of the [[Elderblood Empire]] alongside military operations. The Fourth Legion did not regard a dangerous newly emerged species purely as something to eliminate. Its adaptability and unusual biology made it valuable enough that biological samples were requested while the infestation was still active.[1]
This type of mission reflects the mature Empire's division of responsibilities. Combat forces such as the Spartans can suppress threats, exploration fleets provide transportation and command infrastructure, operators coordinate the battlefield and organizations such as the Fourth Legion collect technologies, organisms and information that may benefit Imperial research.
The Explorer-class vessel's presence within such an operation makes it particularly useful for exploration of unknown or recently altered regions of Imperial territory.

### Relationship with Other Imperial Vessels

#### Continental Spaceships
[[Continental Spaceships]] were already operational long before the first known Explorer-class Starship appeared. They possess enough capacity to house an entire country and can function as mobile strategic or command platforms.[3]
The Explorer-class appears considerably later, after tens of thousands of years of accelerated Imperial development, and is specifically shown leading a multi-vessel expedition in another galaxy. Canon has not stated that Explorer-class Starships evolved directly from Continental Spaceships or that the two classes share identical internal designs.

#### Imperial Warships
The [[Elderblood Empire]] maintains dedicated Warships separately from its larger transport and command vessels. Imperial Warships were already completed by Chapter 1068 and could be centrally controlled by Akasha while still retaining dedicated operators.[3]
The more-than-fifty-vessel fleet accompanying the Explorer-class Starship in Chapter 1163 likely included multiple specialized ship types, but the chapter does not individually classify the remaining vessels. They should therefore remain identified simply as Imperial ships unless additional canon information specifies their classes.

#### Aphrodite's Super Spaceship
[[Aphrodite's Super Spaceship]] represents another mature Imperial vessel. It measures approximately 60 km by 15 km externally, possesses more than 300,000 km² of Runically expanded interior area, can house approximately 50 million beings and carries enough firepower to destroy an entire Solar System.[4]
The Explorer-class is not given comparable measurements or offensive specifications. Its canonical importance instead comes from its expeditionary role, fleet-command position and deployment during routine Galactic operations.

#### Polaris
Polaris occupies a fundamentally different category. It is a classified Galaxy [[Destroyer]]-class Divine Artifact built specifically as an Imperial strategic superweapon. Explorer-class Starships operate as conventional components of mature Imperial fleets, whereas Polaris exists as one of the Empire's highest-level trump cards.[5]

### Strategic Significance
The appearance of the Explorer-class Starship illustrates how completely ordinary interstellar operations had changed within the [[Elderblood Empire]] by the late accelerated era. Earlier in Imperial history, the completion of a handful of large spaceships represented a major technological achievement. By Chapter 1163, a fleet consisting of more than fifty ships could be dispatched to a single alien outbreak in Andromeda without the event being treated as a civilization-wide military emergency.[1]
The Explorer-class vessel at the center of that fleet combined the functions required for this new scale of administration: it transported important personnel, coordinated military assets, maintained communications, supported scientific requests and operated as the largest command platform in a multi-ship expedition.
The incident also demonstrates that the Empire no longer relied upon [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] or his wives to personally resolve every planetary problem. Professional operators, fleet personnel, Spartans, scientists and other Imperial organizations could handle ordinary Galactic incidents independently, while members of the Imperial Family intervened only when desired or when exceptional circumstances justified their attention.
