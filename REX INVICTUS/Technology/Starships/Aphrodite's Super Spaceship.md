Aphrodite's Super Spaceship is an unnamed colossal super spaceship of the [[Elderblood Empire]] personally given to [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood during the Empire's accelerated era. Externally, the vessel measures approximately 60 km in length and 15 km in width, yet extensive Runic spatial expansion increases its usable interior to more than 300,000 km², enough to accommodate approximately 50 million beings. Despite this immense habitation capacity, the ship is also a military vessel possessing enough destructive power to destroy an entire Solar System.[1]
The vessel was granted to Aphrodite in Imperial Year 1000, during the same period in which she, [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] and [[Rose Adrasteia Elderblood]] established the First Legion: Spartan Legion. The gift formed part of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s broader recognition of Aphrodite and the surviving Greek members of his family: on the same day, the ancient city of Aphrodisias was recreated on [[Yggdrasil]] as the First Legion's headquarters, while Aphrodite also received an entire planet upon which she could rebuild her Greek temple.[1] The ship consequently appeared at a major point in the transformation of the Elderblood Empire from an interplanetary civilization into a mature Galactic military power.

### Development
Aphrodite's Super Spaceship was created during the enormous period of accelerated Imperial development initiated by [[Victor Elderblood]] and his Empresses in preparation for future warfare against the civilizations of the Upper Sectors. During this era, the Empire spent tens of thousands of subjective years refining its technology, [[Magic]], Runes, biotechnology, Space-Time engineering, military organization and supernatural sciences while only a comparatively short period passed externally.[1]
By Chapter 1139, interplanetary civilization had already become normal within the Empire. Populations had spread throughout the mother galaxy, worlds containing [[World Trees]] were being settled and ordinary citizens could travel between planets through teleportation networks and spaceships employing advanced Space-Time principles. The Empire's understanding of Space had progressed to the point that astronomical distance was becoming a logistical problem rather than an absolute barrier.[1]
Military technology evolved alongside civilian infrastructure. The Empire reorganized its armed forces into specialized Legions, while the Fourth Legion: [[Stellar Arsenal]] was explicitly established to research and create superweapons, forbidden technologies and weapons of mass destruction operating on Solar-System scales and beyond.[1] [[Aphrodite]]'s vessel was created within this technological environment, when spacecraft capable of threatening planets and Solar Systems were becoming part of the Empire's military doctrine rather than singular experimental achievements.

### Design
The vessel is described as a super spaceship with external dimensions of approximately 60 km × 15 km.[1] Its exact height, shape, armor configuration, internal layout and external appearance have not yet been described in canon.
Although enormous by conventional standards, its physical hull represents only a small fraction of the total usable volume available to those inside. Imperial Runecraft dramatically expands the internal Space beyond the vessel's external measurements, allowing the ship to contain territory comparable to a major terrestrial region while maintaining a hull measured in tens of kilometres.[1]

### Runic Spatial Expansion
One of the vessel's defining technologies is its enormous discrepancy between external and internal size. While the hull measures approximately 60 km by 15 km, its interior contains more than 300,000 square kilometres of usable area.[1] The novel explicitly attributes this difference to Runes, which greatly expand the Space contained inside the vessel.
This represents a mature application of the spatial principles that had appeared much earlier in the story through dimensional bags, expanded buildings and other supernatural storage technologies. By the accelerated Imperial era, the [[Elderblood Empire]] was no longer using spatial expansion merely to increase storage capacity; it could apply the technique to enormous military spacecraft and create mobile environments capable of supporting populations numbering in the tens of millions.
The stated 300,000 km² is an area measurement rather than the ship's physical exterior size. The vessel therefore possesses an internal usable region many times greater than the footprint implied by its outer hull.[1]

### Capacity
Aphrodite's Super Spaceship can accommodate approximately 50 million beings.[1] This population capacity places it well beyond the role of an ordinary transport vessel. A ship capable of supporting tens of millions of occupants can potentially function as a mobile military base, population transport, staging ground or large-scale strategic habitat during Imperial expansion.
The chapter does not specify whether the 50-million figure represents a strict absolute maximum, an intended standard population or simply the number that can be comfortably housed within the expanded interior. It likewise does not detail how the internal territory is divided between habitation, military facilities, storage, engineering systems and other infrastructure. What is explicitly established is that the vessel's Runic expansion provides enough internal Space to house a population on the scale of a major terrestrial nation.[1]

### Armament

#### Solar System Destruction
The vessel possesses sufficient firepower to destroy an entire Solar System.[1] Chapter 1139 describes the ship as a cruiser capable of Solar-System destruction while giving its 60 km × 15 km external dimensions, establishing that its astronomical-scale offensive power is an inherent property of the vessel rather than merely a consequence of carrying [[Aphrodite]] herself.
The precise method through which the ship destroys a Solar System has not been revealed. No individual cannon, missile, beam, Divine Artifact or stellar-destruction mechanism has yet been named, and the novel has not shown Aphrodite's vessel performing a Solar-System-destroying attack directly. Its destructive rating is therefore an explicit canonical specification of the ship rather than a capability inferred from its owner.
This distinguishes it from the [[Solar System Destroyer-Class Spaceship]] encountered during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s reconnaissance of the [[Veillon Empire]]. The Veillon weapon's mechanism is explained as an attack against a star's core from inter-system Space, whereas no equivalent technical explanation has yet been given for Aphrodite's ship. Both possess Solar-System-scale destructive capability, but their weapons should not automatically be assumed to function identically.[1]

### Relationship to the Spartan Legion
[[Aphrodite]] received the vessel on the same day the First Legion: Spartan Legion was formally established in Imperial Year 1000.[1] The Legion was created by Aphrodite alongside [[Scathach]] and Rose as the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s principal large-scale offensive force. It was designed to invade, conquer and secure enemy territory, break stellar defenses and lead major external military campaigns.
The Spartan Legion's common military assets explicitly include spaceships capable of destroying planets and Solar Systems, demonstrating that Solar-System-scale vessels were incorporated into the broader technological standard of the Empire's premier invasion force by this era.[1] The Legion's role was summarized through its own doctrine: it was the Empire's spear, intended to be among the first forces committed whenever large-scale battle began.

### History

#### Imperial Year 1000
The ship was introduced during the accelerated development of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. By Imperial Year 1000, several specialized military Legions had already appeared, and [[Aphrodite]], [[Scathach]] and Rose created the First Legion as a dedicated invasion and conquest force. Aphrodite chose the name Spartan Legion in honor of the martial symbolism associated with ancient Sparta, arguing that she wanted to preserve the spirit and history rather than glorify the individual Gods of the destroyed [[Greek Pantheon]].[1]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] initially disliked the Greek association but accepted Aphrodite's reasoning. The other Greek Goddesses quickly supported her, making clear that even though the political structure of their former Pantheon had disappeared, they still wished to preserve parts of their cultural history. Victor ultimately recognized that his Greek wives possessed very little remaining within the Empire that directly referenced their former civilization.[1]
He therefore restored Aphrodisias in the heart of [[Yggdrasil]] and designated it the headquarters of the First Legion. During the same celebration, Aphrodite received her super spaceship and an entire planet she could shape according to her wishes and use to rebuild her temple.[1]
The gifts affected Aphrodite so strongly that her happiness caused an explosion of Divinity powerful enough to influence even nearby Dragon Gods. She immediately embraced Victor and repeatedly declared her love for him.[1]

#### Greek World
The consequences of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s gift continued immediately afterward. [[Aphrodite]]'s personal planet caused the other Greek women in Victor's family to become jealous, including normally restrained figures such as [[Hestia]] Elderblood. Victor responded by expanding the world until it became slightly larger than old Earth, giving the Greek Goddesses enough territory to rebuild their respective temples and cultural regions.[2]
The spaceship therefore formed one part of a larger Imperial recognition of Aphrodite's cultural and military contributions: she gained a personal Solar-System-destroying vessel, the restoration of Aphrodisias, and access to a dedicated world for the preservation of Greek architecture and Divine traditions.[1][2]

### Strategic Role
Aphrodite's Super Spaceship combines three functions that the mature [[Elderblood Empire]] increasingly merged within its largest spacecraft: transportation, habitation and strategic warfare. Its 300,000+ km² internal territory allows it to carry a massive population, while its Solar-System-destroying firepower ensures that the ship does not require a separate escort merely to threaten astronomical-scale targets.[1]
Its existence also demonstrates the enormous technological advancement achieved since earlier Imperial spacecraft. During the Empire's initial interstellar expansion, large ships already functioned as mobile command centers and transports; after thousands of years of accelerated development, Runic spatial engineering and weapons research had reached a point where a vessel only tens of kilometres long could contain hundreds of thousands of square kilometres internally and carry enough weaponry to destroy a complete stellar system.
This technological standard is further supported by the composition of the Spartan Legion itself, whose common units include war dragons, armored soldiers, siege mages and spacecraft capable of destroying planets or Solar Systems.[1] Solar-System-scale warships were therefore no longer treated solely as irreplaceable experimental superweapons by this stage of the Empire's development.
