A **[[Long-Stroke Gas Piston]]** is a firearm operating system where the piston is fixed to the bolt carrier and travels the full length of the carrier's stroke. Propellant gas tapped from the barrel drives the piston rearward, unlocks the bolt and cycles the action.

## IRL

- **Examples.** The AK-47 family, the M1 Garand, the FN Minimi and the Bren.
- **Strengths.** Tolerates dirt and fouling, and keeps hot gas out of the receiver.
- **Weaknesses.** The heavy moving mass shifts the rifle's balance and raises felt recoil compared with a short-stroke piston (such as the AR-18 or HK416) or direct impingement (the AR-15).
