**[[Melee]]** is hand-to-hand combat, and melee weapons are the arms used for it. This note covers the hand weapons in [[Weapon types]] that have no note of their own.

## Swords
[[Xiphos]] (Greek), [[Gladius]] (Roman), and the Titan-scale chainswords of the far future.

## Spears and lances
See [[Spears]].

## Warhammers
A war hammer pairs a hammer face with a spike. It appeared in late medieval Europe when plate armor made blades less effective, since a blow transfers force through armor that a cut cannot pierce.

## Bows
Bows store energy in a bent limb. Composite bows of horn, wood and sinew powered steppe armies; the English longbow decided Crécy (1346) and Agincourt (1415). In PJO canon [[Artemis]], [[Apollo]] and the [[Hunters of Artemis]] fight with bows.

## Shields
Greek hoplites carried the round aspis, and Roman legionaries the curved scutum. In PJO canon [[Thalia Grace]] carries [[Ageis|Aegis]].
