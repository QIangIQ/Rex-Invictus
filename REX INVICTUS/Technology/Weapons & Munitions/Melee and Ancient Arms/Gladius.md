The **[[Gladius]]** is the short stabbing sword of the Roman legionary, about 60–70 cm long, used with the large scutum shield in close formation. Variants include the Hispaniensis, Mainz and Pompeii patterns. In PJO canon [[Camp Jupiter]] legionaries carry [[Imperial Gold]] gladii, and [[Jason Grace]] fights with one. Compare the Greek [[Xiphos]].
