**[[Spears]]** are the oldest and most common war weapon: a pole with a pointed head. The Greek hoplite's dory was about 2–3 m long; the Macedonian sarissa reached about 6 m. The Roman pilum was a heavy javelin whose soft shank bent on impact so it could not be thrown back. A lance is a cavalry spear used couched under the arm, from medieval knights to 19th-century lancers. In PJO canon [[Clarisse]] fights with her electric spear Maimer, and [[Reyna Avila Ramírez-Arellano|Reyna]] carries an [[Imperial Gold]] spear.
