The **[[Xiphos]]** is the ancient Greek double-edged, single-handed straight sword, usually with a leaf-shaped blade about 45–60 cm long. Hoplites carried it as a backup to the spear. In PJO canon the Greek demigod's standard sword is a [[Celestial Bronze]] xiphos; [[Percy Jackson|Percy]]'s [[Riptide]] (Anaklusmos) is one. Compare the Roman [[Gladius]].
