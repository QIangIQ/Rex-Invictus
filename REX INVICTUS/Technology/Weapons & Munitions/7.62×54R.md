**[[7.62×54R]]** is a rimmed rifle cartridge introduced by the Russian Empire in 1891 for the Mosin-Nagant rifle.

---

## Ballistics (IRL)

It fires a 7.62 mm (.30 calibre) bullet, usually 9 to 13 g, at about 800 to 870 m/s, with energy similar to the .30-06 and the 7.62×51 NATO. The "R" marks the rim, an old design kept in service for over 130 years. It feeds the SVD Dragunov, the PK machine gun family and many other Soviet and Russian weapons.

## UOE (far future)

The [[VA-RF-762 “MORROW-IX”]] battle rifle of [[Vanguard Armament Solutions]] is chambered for an imperial descendant of the round. See [[Battle Rifle]], [[Imperial Ammunition Codes]] and [[Divine Material Ammunition]].
