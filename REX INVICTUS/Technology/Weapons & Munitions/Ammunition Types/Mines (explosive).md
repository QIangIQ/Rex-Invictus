**[[Mines (explosive)]]** are explosive devices left in place to detonate when a target comes near or touches them.

- **Land mines.** Anti-personnel and anti-tank types, triggered by pressure, tripwire, tilt rod or magnetic influence. The 1997 Ottawa Treaty bans anti-personnel mines for its 160-plus member states.
- **Naval mines.** Contact, magnetic, acoustic and pressure types, moored or laid on the seabed.
- **Use.** Mines shape movement rather than kill directly: they deny ground and sea lanes and channel enemies into kill zones, and clearing them is slow.
