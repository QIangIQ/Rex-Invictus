A **[[Swarm Missile]]** attack saturates defenses with many small missiles or submunitions at once, so [[Point-Defence]] cannot engage them all.

## IRL

Multiple-launch rocket systems (the BM-21 Grad, M270 and HIMARS), cluster munitions, and networked drone swarms that coordinate their attack. Mass is cheaper than defeating an interceptor one-on-one.

## UOE (Far Future)

In [[All Titan-Based Fire Solutions]] the SWRM code covers clustered or multi-submunition systems that scatter micro-munitions over an area. [[Titans]] also carry HS (heat-seeking) and DF (direct-fire) missile racks; see [[Weapon types]].
