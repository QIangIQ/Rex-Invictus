**[[Divine Material Ammunition]]** is the UOE family of anti-pantheonic rounds. Each type takes a conventional design and adds trace [[Celestial Bronze]], [[Imperial Gold]] and [[Stygian Iron]] through [[Divine-Metallurgical Binding Impurities (DMBI)]], so the round can harm [[Monsters (Greek)|monsters]], demigods and legacies as well as hardware. The full list is in [[All Titan-Based Fire Solutions]].

## DMAP
Divine Material Armor-Piercing. The divine form of [[Armor-Piercing (AP)]].

## DMAPL
Divine Material Armor-Piercing, Lead-Core. The divine form of APL.

## DMAPFSDS
Divine Material Armor-Piercing Fin-Stabilized Discarding Sabot. A long-rod penetrator ([[Armor-Piercing Fin-Stabilized Discarding Sabot (APFSDS)]]) with divine-metal micro-phases, still relying mainly on kinetic energy. Small-arms example: [[VAS-762R APDS “QVARTUS”]].

## DMHE
Divine Material High-Explosive. [[High-Explosive (HE)]] with divine-metal particulates. Example: [[VAS-762R HE “PHOEBUS-3”]].

## DMEMSF
Divine Material Electro-Magnetic Shaped Fragmentation. The divine form of [[Imperial Ammunition Codes#EMSF|EMSF]].

## DMRIP
Divine Material Radically Invasive Projectile. A frangible multi-segment round that splits into several wound tracks. Example: [[VAS-762R RIP ”NAIIL”]]. Its IRL ancestor is the G2 R.I.P. (Radically Invasive Projectile) hollow point, which breaks into trocar petals.
