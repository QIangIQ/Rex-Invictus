An **[[Anti-Tank Guided Missile (ATGM)]]** is a guided missile built to destroy armored vehicles, usually with a [[High-Explosive Anti-Tank (HEAT)|HEAT]] or tandem shaped-charge warhead.

- **First generation.** Manual command to line of sight (MCLOS), such as the AT-3 Sagger, which was flown by joystick.
- **Second generation.** SACLOS: the gunner keeps the crosshair on the target and the launcher steers the missile by wire or beam (TOW, Kornet).
- **Third generation.** Fire-and-forget seekers (Javelin, Spike), many with top-attack profiles that strike the thinner roof armor.
- **Counters.** Active protection systems such as Trophy, [[Reactive Armor]] and smoke.
