**[[High-Explosive (HE)]]** ammunition carries an explosive fill that detonates on impact, after a delay, or near the target with a proximity fuze. Its damage comes from blast overpressure and fragments of the casing (HE-FRAG). Common fills include TNT, Composition B and RDX/HMX mixes, which the vault's [[VAS-762R HE “PHOEBUS-3”]] uses. HE is the main round against infantry, light vehicles and structures. Its anti-pantheonic form is [[Divine Material Ammunition#DMHE|DMHE]].
