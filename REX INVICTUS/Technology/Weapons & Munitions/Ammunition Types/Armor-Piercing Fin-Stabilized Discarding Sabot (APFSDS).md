**[[Armor-Piercing Fin-Stabilized Discarding Sabot (APFSDS)]]** is the standard modern tank kinetic round: a long, thin rod of tungsten alloy or [[Depleted Uranium]] carried down the barrel by a sabot that falls away after the muzzle. Fins stabilize the rod, which is too long to spin-stabilize.

## IRL

- **Performance.** Muzzle velocities run about 1,550–1,800 m/s. Length-to-diameter ratios above 20:1 concentrate energy on a tiny area.
- **Examples.** The U.S. M829 series (depleted uranium, 120 mm) and the German DM53 (tungsten).
- **APDS.** The earlier armor-piercing discarding sabot used a short spin-stabilized core; the vault's [[VAS-762R APDS “QVARTUS”]] borrows the name.
- **Defeat.** Heavy composite armor and some heavy [[Reactive Armor]] such as Kontakt-5.
