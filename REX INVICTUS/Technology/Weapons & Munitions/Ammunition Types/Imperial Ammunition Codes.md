**[[Imperial Ammunition Codes]]** are the short codes used for UOE munition types in [[All Titan-Based Fire Solutions]] and [[Weapon types]]. Conventional types have their own notes: [[Armor-Piercing (AP)|AP and APL]], [[Armor-Piercing Composite Rigid (APCR)|APCR]], [[Armor-Piercing Fin-Stabilized Discarding Sabot (APFSDS)|APFSDS]], [[High-Explosive (HE)|HE]], [[High-Explosive Anti-Tank (HEAT)|HEAT]], [[High-Explosive Squash Head (HESH)|HESH]], [[Anti-Tank Guided Missile (ATGM)|ATGM]], [[Smoke (SMK)|SMK]] and [[Swarm Missile|SWRM]]. Divine types are under [[Divine Material Ammunition]].

## EMSF
Electro-Magnetic Shaped Fragmentation. Uses electromagnetic pulse shaping to control how fragments disperse.

## KNTC
Kinetic. An inert mass-driver or high-velocity penetrator that relies only on kinetic energy, as in railguns. The IRL U.S. Navy railgun program (cancelled 2021) fired hypervelocity projectiles at about Mach 6.

## GRV
Gravitic. Uses localized gravitational distortion for structural disruption. Related to [[Kugelblitz]].

## THMNCLR
Thermonuclear. A fission-fusion device with strategic yield. IRL, the first thermonuclear test was Ivy Mike (1952); the largest was the Soviet Tsar Bomba (about 50 megatons, 1961). Example: [[IA-762R THMNCLR “PROMETHEUS-IX”]].
