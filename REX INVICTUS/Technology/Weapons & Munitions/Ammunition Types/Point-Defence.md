**[[Point-Defence]]** systems protect a single ship or site from incoming missiles, aircraft and shells at short range.

## IRL

Close-in weapon systems (CIWS) such as the U.S. Phalanx (a radar-directed 20 mm Gatling) and the Dutch Goalkeeper, short-range missiles such as RAM, and land C-RAM systems against rockets and mortars. Newer systems add lasers, such as the U.S. Navy's LaWS and the British DragonFire.

## UOE (Far Future)

In the [[War of Princes]] the Voss defenders lacked point-defence because [[Solon Themis]] had sent the Icarus corvettes and Mastodon frigates to colonize [[Voss Primaris]] and [[Voss Secundus]]. See [[Flak]] and [[Kármán Line Skeleton]].
