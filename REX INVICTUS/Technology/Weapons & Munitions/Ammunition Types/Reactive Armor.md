**[[Reactive Armor]]** reacts to a hit. Explosive reactive armor (ERA) puts a thin layer of explosive between two metal plates. When a [[High-Explosive Anti-Tank (HEAT)|shaped-charge]] jet strikes, the explosive drives the plates into the jet's path and breaks it up. The Soviet Kontakt-1 (1982) was the first widely fielded ERA. Heavy ERA such as Kontakt-5 and Relikt also degrades [[Armor-Piercing Fin-Stabilized Discarding Sabot (APFSDS)|APFSDS]] rods. Tandem warheads were developed to defeat it with a small precursor charge. Non-explosive and electric reactive armors are later variants.
