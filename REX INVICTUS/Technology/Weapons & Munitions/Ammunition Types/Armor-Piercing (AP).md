**[[Armor-Piercing (AP)]]** ammunition defeats armor with a hard, dense projectile that relies on kinetic energy rather than explosives.

## IRL

- **Full-caliber shot.** Early AP shot was solid hardened steel. Later shells added a soft cap (APC) to stop shattering and a ballistic cap (APCBC) for streamlining.
- **Small arms.** Rifle AP rounds use a hardened steel or tungsten-carbide core inside a jacket, like the U.S. M2 AP (.30-06) and M995 (5.56 mm). A lead-core round (the vault's APL) is the cheaper ordinary bullet that the AP core replaces.
- **Successors.** Sub-caliber designs ([[Armor-Piercing Composite Rigid (APCR)]] and [[Armor-Piercing Fin-Stabilized Discarding Sabot (APFSDS)]]) put a dense core on a lighter carrier for higher velocity.

## UOE (Far Future)

AP and APL are the conventional baselines in [[All Titan-Based Fire Solutions]]. Their anti-pantheonic forms are [[Divine Material Ammunition#DMAP|DMAP and DMAPL]].
