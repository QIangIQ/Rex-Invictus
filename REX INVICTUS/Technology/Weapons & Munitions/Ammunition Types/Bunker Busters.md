**[[Bunker Busters]]** are munitions built to penetrate earth or concrete before detonating, to destroy hardened or buried targets. They use heavy, narrow casings of high-strength steel and delayed or void-sensing fuzes. Examples include the GBU-28 (built in weeks for the 1991 Gulf War from surplus gun barrels) and the 13,600 kg GBU-57 Massive Ordnance Penetrator. Britain's Tallboy and Grand Slam "earthquake bombs" were Second World War forerunners.
