**[[Smoke (SMK)]]** munitions create obscurant clouds to block observation and targeting. White phosphorus and hexachloroethane screen the visible band; modern multispectral smokes add brass flakes or graphite to block infrared and millimetre-wave sensors. Tanks carry smoke grenade dischargers that can fire automatically when a laser warning receiver detects a rangefinder. The vault's [[DIFM-CFT Smoke]] adds IR frequency-memory deception and divine-band chaff.
