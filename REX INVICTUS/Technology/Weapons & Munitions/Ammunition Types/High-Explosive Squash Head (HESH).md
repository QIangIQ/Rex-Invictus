**[[High-Explosive Squash Head (HESH)]]** (U.S. term: HEP) has a thin shell filled with plastic explosive that flattens against armor before a base fuze detonates it. The shock wave travels through the plate and knocks a scab of metal off the inside face (spalling), which wrecks the crew compartment without penetrating. It was a British specialty, used by the L7 105 mm and Challenger's 120 mm rifled guns, and works well against buildings. Spaced armor and spall liners defeat it.
