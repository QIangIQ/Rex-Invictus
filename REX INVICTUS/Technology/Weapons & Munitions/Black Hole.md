A **[[Black Hole]]** is a region where gravity is so strong that nothing, not even light, can escape.

- **Event horizon.** The boundary of no return. For a non-rotating hole its radius is the Schwarzschild radius, 2*GM*/*c*², about 3 km per solar mass.
- **Singularity.** General relativity predicts infinite density at the center, a sign that the theory breaks down there.
- **Hawking radiation.** Stephen Hawking showed in 1974 that quantum effects make black holes emit thermal radiation. Small holes are hotter and evaporate faster.
- **Gravitational lensing.** Mass bends light, so a black hole distorts and magnifies what lies behind it. The Event Horizon Telescope imaged the shadow of M87* in 2019 and Sagittarius A* in 2022.
- **[[Kugelblitz]].** A black hole formed from concentrated light rather than matter, the basis of the vault's [[Kugelblitz]] weapon and the [[Institute Academy]] black-hole launchers.
