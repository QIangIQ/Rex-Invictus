**[[Battle Rifle]]** is a rifle firing a full-power cartridge such as the 7.62×51 NATO or [[7.62×54R]], as opposed to the lighter intermediate round of an assault rifle.

---

## Weapons History (IRL)

Classic examples are the M1 Garand, M14, FN FAL, Heckler & Koch G3 and the Soviet SVD. They hit harder and reach further than assault rifles, at the cost of heavier ammunition and more recoil in automatic fire. Most armies replaced them with 5.56 mm rifles from the 1960s on, but they returned as designated marksman rifles.
