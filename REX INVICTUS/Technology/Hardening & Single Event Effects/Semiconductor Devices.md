**[[Semiconductor Devices]]** are electronic components built from semiconductors, mainly silicon: diodes, transistors, integrated circuits, microprocessors and memory. Their behavior depends on carefully placed charge in very thin layers, which is why [[Ionizing Radiation]] can upset or damage them ([[Single Event Effects (SEE)]], [[Radiation Hardening]]). Power transistors switch large currents, logic elements implement Boolean functions, and memory stores bits as charge (DRAM, flash) or in latches (SRAM).
