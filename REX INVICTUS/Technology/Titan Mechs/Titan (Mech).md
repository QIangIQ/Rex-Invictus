## Honour Titans (Pyrrhus Command), added 2026-11-16

**Role (user ruling 2026-11-16):** bleeding-edge "sixth-generation fighter" counterparts to the [[Behemoth|Behemoths]]. ### Pilots and the capsule (XXI "THE WARBRINGER FURY, THE TITAN DECK. 0616")
- Pilots are heavily augmented humans: spinal port rows from skull to tailbone, wrist and arm ports, bone lacing, a second heart pump, direct neural handshake, and as many as eleven rebuild surgeries.
- They sit in a bus-sized capsule filled with the [[Perfluoro-tert-butyl-perfluorodecalin - Perfluorohexyl-perfluoroisopropyl Ether - Fluorinated Silica Shear-Thickening Oxygenated Suspension Medium|oxygenated suspension medium]] (the same fluid the [[Ekdromoi]] breathe). It fills every cavity and goes rigid within ~1 ms of a pinwheel, so the pilot feels ~7 g.
- **Pinwheel:** the machine kills ~2 km/s of momentum and rebuilds it on three axes. At 400,000 t, with 5/6 lost as heat, that is ~30 PJ per turn, about a year's output of a full-size Old Terra fission plant.
- 191,000 line-replaceable units in a Seneschal-class. The reactor alone has ~11,000 parts.
- Fire control reads the spinal ports and puts the rifle on target ~40 ms before the pilot decides to shoot.
- 1,200 vector nozzles and 35 ms leg muscle bundles.
- Named here: TW-SP-PKAX "Tau Seneschal" (reserve pilot Rudolf Würger). Chief Engineer Mihail Popescu-Vrancea and Apprentice Ines Bauer of the Forge Cohort.
