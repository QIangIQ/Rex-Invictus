**[[Signal Analysis]]** in [[Electronic Intelligence (ELINT)]] measures an intercepted emission's technical characteristics so it can be identified.

- **[[Electromagnetic Spectrum (EMS)|Frequency]] analysis.** Carrier frequency, agility and bandwidth.
- **Time domain analysis.** Pulse timing: [[Pulse Width]], [[Pulse Repetition Frequency (PRF)|PRI and PRF]], stagger and jitter.
- **Spectral analysis.** Fourier transforms reveal modulation such as [[Linear Frequency Modulation (LFM)]] or [[Phase Modulation (PM)]] codes.
- **Pulse analysis.** Rise time, intrapulse modulation and unintentional modulation on pulse (UMOP), the tiny imperfections that let analysts tell one transmitter from another.
- **Output.** A pulse descriptor word per pulse, which deinterleaving sorts into separate emitters before [[Emitter Identification]].
