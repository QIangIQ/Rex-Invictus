**[[Emitter Identification]]** matches an intercepted signal to a known emitter type, and at its finest to one specific transmitter.

- **Libraries.** [[Electronic Intelligence (ELINT)]] builds emitter libraries (mission data files) that list parameter ranges for every known radar. [[Radar Warning Receiver (RWR)|RWRs]] and [[Electronic Support (ES)]] systems compare live measurements against them.
- **Signature.** An emitter's signature is its set of parameters from [[Signal Analysis]]: frequency, [[Pulse Width]], [[Pulse Repetition Frequency (PRF)|PRF]], scan and modulation.
- **Fingerprinting.** Specific emitter identification (SEI) uses unintentional modulation to tell individual transmitters of the same model apart, which lets analysts track one ship or battery.
- **Pattern recognition.** Modern systems use statistical classifiers and machine learning, which matters against software-defined radars whose waveforms change.
