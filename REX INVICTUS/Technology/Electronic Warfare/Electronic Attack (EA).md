**[[Electronic Attack (EA)]]** is the division of [[Electronic Warfare (EW)]] that uses electromagnetic energy, directed energy or anti-radiation weapons to degrade, neutralize or destroy enemy capability. It includes what older doctrine called [[Electronic Countermeasures (ECM)]].

## IRL techniques

- **Noise jamming.** Barrage jamming spreads power across a wide band; spot jamming concentrates on one frequency; swept jamming moves a narrow jammer across a band.
- **Deception jamming.** Range-gate and velocity-gate pull-off walk a tracking radar off the true target. Modern deception uses digital radio frequency memory (DRFM) to record and replay the radar's own pulses with false delay or Doppler.
- **Expendables.** [[Chaff]] clouds and towed or launched decoys.
- **Destructive EA.** Anti-radiation missiles such as the AGM-88 HARM home on radar emissions; high-power microwave and laser weapons attack electronics directly.
- **Platforms.** Escort and stand-off jammers such as the [[Airborne ELINT Platforms|EA-18G Growler]], self-protection pods, and ground and naval jammers.
