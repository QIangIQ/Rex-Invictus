**[[Geolocation]]** in [[Electronic Warfare (EW)]] fixes an emitter's position.

- **Triangulation.** Crosses two or more [[Direction Finding (DF)]] bearings taken from different places or from one moving platform.
- **Time difference of arrival (TDOA).** Compares when one pulse reaches separated receivers. Each pair defines a hyperbola, and intersections fix the emitter. This is called multilateration.
- **[[Electromagnetic Spectrum (EMS)|Frequency]] difference of arrival (FDOA).** Uses Doppler differences between moving receivers, often combined with TDOA by satellites and aircraft.
- **Use.** A precise fix lets anti-radiation weapons, artillery or strike aircraft engage the emitter. That threat is why radars and command posts practice emission control and move after transmitting.
