**[[Range Resolution]]** is a radar's ability to separate two targets at nearly the same range. It is set by the signal's bandwidth *B*: Δ*R* ≈ *c* / (2*B*).

- An unmodulated pulse has bandwidth about 1/τ, so resolution depends on [[Pulse Width]].
- [[Pulse Compression]] widens bandwidth inside a long pulse. The **compression ratio** (time-bandwidth product τ*B*) is how much shorter the pulse becomes after the [[Matched Filter]].
- In a [[Linear Frequency Modulation (LFM)]] chirp, the **chirp rate** is the bandwidth swept per unit time (*B*/τ).
