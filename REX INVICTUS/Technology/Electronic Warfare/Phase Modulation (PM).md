**[[Phase Modulation (PM)]]** encodes a signal in the carrier's phase. In radar [[Pulse Compression]], a long pulse is divided into short chips whose phase is switched by a code.

- **Binary phase shift keying (BPSK)** uses two phases, 0° and 180°.
- **Barker codes** are the classic BPSK codes; the longest known has 13 chips and gives a peak-to-sidelobe ratio of 13:1.
- **Polyphase codes** (Frank, P1–P4) use more phases for lower sidelobes.
- **Contrast with chirps.** Phase codes are less tolerant of Doppler than [[Linear Frequency Modulation (LFM)]] but harder for an interceptor to recognize, which helps low-probability-of-intercept radars.
