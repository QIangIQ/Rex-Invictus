A **[[Fire Control Radar (FCR)]]** tracks a single target precisely enough to aim guns or guide missiles. Compared with search radars it uses narrow beams, high update rates and usually higher frequencies (X band and above).

- **Guidance modes.** Command guidance, semi-active homing (the radar illuminates the target and the missile homes on the reflection) and track-via-missile.
- **Lock.** A lock-on or illumination is the strongest warning a [[Radar Warning Receiver (RWR)]] gives.
- **Examples.** Engagement radars in SAM systems like the S-300 and Patriot, and naval fire-control directors.
- **Countermeasures.** Deception jamming and [[Chaff]].

See [[Radar Systems]] and [[Integrated Air Defense System (IADS)]].
