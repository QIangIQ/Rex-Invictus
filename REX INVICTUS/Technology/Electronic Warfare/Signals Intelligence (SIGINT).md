**[[Signals Intelligence (SIGINT)]]** is intelligence gathered by intercepting signals. It has two main branches: [[Communications Intelligence (COMINT)]], from messages between people, and [[Electronic Intelligence (ELINT)]], from non-communication emitters such as radars. Some taxonomies add FISINT (foreign instrumentation signals, such as missile telemetry).

## IRL

- **History.** [[Radar Warning Receiver (RWR)|Interception]] of telegraph and radio grew through the First World War (Room 40's reading of the Zimmermann Telegram in 1917) and peaked in the Second (Bletchley Park and Ultra; the U.S. breaking of Japanese naval codes before Midway).
- **Agencies.** The U.S. National Security Agency, Britain's GCHQ, and the "Five Eyes" sharing arrangement among the U.S., UK, Canada, Australia and New Zealand.
- **Relation to EW.** SIGINT is processed for analysis over time. The same interception used for immediate threat warning belongs to [[Electronic Support (ES)]] within [[Electronic Warfare (EW)]].
