**[[Direction Finding (DF)]]** measures the bearing to an emitter.

- **Amplitude comparison.** Compares signal strength across several squinted antennas. It is simple and used in [[Radar Warning Receiver (RWR)|RWRs]], but accurate only to several degrees.
- **Phase interferometry.** Measures the phase difference between spaced antennas, accurate to about a degree or better.
- **Time difference of arrival.** [[Geolocation|TDOA]] between widely separated receivers.
- **History.** High-frequency DF ("Huff-Duff") let Allied escorts locate U-boats by their radio transmissions in the Battle of the Atlantic.

Bearings from several sites combine into a fix by [[Geolocation]].
