An **[[Integrated Air Defense System (IADS)]]** links early-warning [[Radar Systems|radars]], command centers, fighters, surface-to-air missiles and guns into one network, so a target detected by one sensor can be engaged by any weapon. Suppression of enemy air defenses (SEAD) targets it with [[Electronic Attack (EA)]], decoys and anti-radiation missiles. Mapping it is a main purpose of the [[Electronic Order of Battle (EOB)]].
