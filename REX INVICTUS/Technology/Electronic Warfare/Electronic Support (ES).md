**[[Electronic Support (ES)]]**, formerly Electronic Support Measures (ESM), is the division of [[Electronic Warfare (EW)]] that searches for, intercepts, identifies and locates sources of electromagnetic energy for immediate use: threat warning, targeting and avoidance.

## IRL

- **[[Radar Warning Receiver (RWR)|Interception]].** Wideband and channelized receivers watch the [[Electromagnetic Spectrum (EMS)]]. A [[Radar Warning Receiver (RWR)]] is the simplest ES system.
- **Analysis.** [[Signal Analysis]] measures parameters such as [[Pulse Repetition Frequency (PRF)]] and [[Pulse Width]].
- **Identification.** [[Emitter Identification]] matches those parameters against libraries built by [[Electronic Intelligence (ELINT)]].
- **Location.** [[Direction Finding (DF)]] gives bearing; [[Geolocation]] fixes a position.
- **ES vs ELINT.** The hardware overlaps. ES serves the operator now; ELINT is collection processed for longer-term intelligence and the [[Electronic Order of Battle (EOB)]].

## UOE (Far Future)

Imperial ES feeds the [[Institute Academy]] IA-ELINT and IA-ECCM systems and the anti-divine sensors of [[Tochter des Elysium R&D]].
