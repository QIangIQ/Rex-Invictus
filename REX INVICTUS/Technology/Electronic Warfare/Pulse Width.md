**[[Pulse Width]]** (PW, or pulse duration) is how long each radar pulse lasts, typically from fractions of a microsecond to hundreds of microseconds.

- A longer pulse carries more energy, so detection range rises.
- A shorter pulse gives finer range resolution, about *c*τ/2 for a simple pulse of width τ (1 µs gives about 150 m).
- [[Pulse Compression]] breaks that trade by modulating a long pulse and compressing it on receive.
- Duty cycle is PW × [[Pulse Repetition Frequency (PRF)|PRF]].
- ELINT measures PW for [[Emitter Identification]].
