An **[[Electronic Order of Battle (EOB)]]** is a database of an adversary's emitters: what each radar, jammer and data link is, where it sits, which unit owns it and what it is doing. It is built from [[Electronic Intelligence (ELINT)]] collection, and it feeds mission planning, [[Electronic Support (ES)]] threat libraries and suppression of an [[Integrated Air Defense System (IADS)]]. An emitter that appears or moves can reveal a unit's deployment before anything is seen.
