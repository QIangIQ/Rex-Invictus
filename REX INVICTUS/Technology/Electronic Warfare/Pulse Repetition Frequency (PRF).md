**[[Pulse Repetition Frequency (PRF)]]** is the number of pulses a pulsed radar transmits per second. Its inverse is the pulse repetition interval (PRI).

- **Low PRF** gives long unambiguous range but ambiguous velocity.
- **High PRF** gives unambiguous Doppler velocity but ambiguous range.
- **Medium PRF** compromises, and modern airborne radars switch among several PRFs to resolve ambiguities.
- **Unambiguous range** is *c* / (2 × PRF): at 1 kHz it is 150 km.
- **For ELINT.** PRF, PRI patterns (stagger, jitter, dwell-and-switch) and [[Pulse Width]] are among the strongest fingerprints for [[Emitter Identification]].

See [[Radar Systems]] and [[Doppler Filtering]].
