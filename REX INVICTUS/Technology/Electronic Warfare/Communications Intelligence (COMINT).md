**[[Communications Intelligence (COMINT)]]** is the branch of [[Signals Intelligence (SIGINT)]] that targets communication signals: voice, data, text and the traffic patterns around them. It covers interception, decryption and traffic analysis (who talks to whom, when and how often, even when content cannot be read). Its counterpart is [[Electronic Intelligence (ELINT)]], which targets non-communication emitters such as [[Radar Systems|radars]].

Classic examples are the breaking of the German Enigma at Bletchley Park and the U.S. reading of the Japanese JN-25 naval code before Midway in 1942.
