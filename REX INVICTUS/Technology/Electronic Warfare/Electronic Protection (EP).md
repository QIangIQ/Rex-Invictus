**[[Electronic Protection (EP)]]** is the division of [[Electronic Warfare (EW)]] that protects friendly people, facilities and equipment from enemy [[Electronic Attack (EA)]] or friendly interference. It includes what older doctrine called [[Electronic Counter-Countermeasures (ECCM)]].

## IRL techniques

- [[Electromagnetic Spectrum (EMS)|Frequency]] agility and spread-spectrum waveforms that make jamming expensive.
- [[Pulse Compression]], which gives processing gain against noise.
- Sidelobe blanking and cancellation, low-sidelobe antennas and adaptive nulling.
- [[Rex Invictus|Home]]-on-jam modes that turn the jammer into a target.
- Emission control (EMCON) and low-probability-of-intercept radars that deny the enemy [[Electronic Support (ES)]].
