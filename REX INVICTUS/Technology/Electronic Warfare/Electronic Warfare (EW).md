**[[Electronic Warfare (EW)]]** is military action that uses electromagnetic energy to sense, attack or protect within the [[Electromagnetic Spectrum (EMS)]]. Current U.S. and NATO doctrine splits it into three divisions: [[Electronic Support (ES)]], [[Electronic Attack (EA)]] and [[Electronic Protection (EP)]]. This note is the hub for the vault's EW folder.

---

## IRL

| Division | Older name | Purpose | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[Electronic Support (ES)]] | [[Electronic Support (ES)\|Electronic Support Measures (ESM)]] | Search, intercept, identify and locate emitters for immediate use | [[Radar Warning Receiver (RWR)]], [[Direction Finding (DF)]] |
| [[Electronic Attack (EA)]] | [[Electronic Countermeasures (ECM)]] | Degrade or deceive enemy sensors and links | [[Electronic Attack (EA)\|Jamming]], [[Chaff]], decoys, DRFM deception |
| [[Electronic Protection (EP)]] | [[Electronic Counter-Countermeasures (ECCM)]] | Keep friendly systems working under attack | [[Electromagnetic Spectrum (EMS)\|Frequency]] agility, [[Pulse Compression]], sidelobe blanking |

- **Relation to intelligence.** ES serves the commander in real time. The same collection, processed for long-term analysis, is [[Signals Intelligence (SIGINT)]], split into [[Electronic Intelligence (ELINT)]] and [[Communications Intelligence (COMINT)]]. ELINT feeds the [[Electronic Order of Battle (EOB)]] and the emitter libraries that ES receivers use.
- **History.** Radio jamming and intercept began in the Russo-Japanese War (1904–1905) and the First World War. The Second World War brought radar, Window (chaff) and the "Battle of the Beams" over Britain. Modern EW centers on suppression of [[Integrated Air Defense System (IADS)|integrated air defenses]] with aircraft like the [[Airborne ELINT Platforms|EA-18G Growler]].

## UOE (Far Future)

- The vault extends the IRL ladder with [[Electronic Counter-Counter-Countermeasures (ECCCM)]] and divine-band systems such as [[DIFM-CFT Smoke]] and [[Flux Attenuation Scanning]].
- [[Institute Academy]] is the main EW designer (the IA-ECM, IA-ECCM, IA-ELINT and IA-[[Signals Intelligence (SIGINT)|SIGINT]] series). [[Tochter des Elysium R&D]] builds advanced sensors.
- EW is one of the threats that made the [[Helmsman]] program standard across the [[UOE Navy]].
- Hardening against EW and divine effects: [[Radiation Hardening]], [[Divine Hardening]], [[Single Event Effects (SEE)]].
