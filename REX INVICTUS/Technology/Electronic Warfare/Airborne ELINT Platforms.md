**[[Airborne ELINT Platforms]]** collect [[Electronic Intelligence (ELINT)]] and conduct [[Electronic Warfare (EW)]] from the air and space.

- **RC-135V/W Rivet Joint.** A U.S. Air Force reconnaissance aircraft based on the C-135 airframe that collects ELINT and [[Communications Intelligence (COMINT)|COMINT]] in near real time.
- **EP-3E Aries II.** A U.S. Navy signals reconnaissance version of the P-3 Orion. One collided with a Chinese J-8 fighter off Hainan in 2001.
- **EA-18G Growler.** A U.S. Navy electronic attack version of the F/A-18F Super Hornet, carrying jamming pods and anti-radiation missiles for [[Electronic Attack (EA)]].
- **Space-based ELINT.** Satellites from the 1960 GRAB onward map radars from orbit.
