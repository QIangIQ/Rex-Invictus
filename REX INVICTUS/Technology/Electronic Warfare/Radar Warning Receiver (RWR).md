A **[[Radar Warning Receiver (RWR)]]** detects radar emissions illuminating its platform, identifies the threat type against a library, and shows its bearing and mode to the crew. It warns when a [[Radar Systems|radar]] moves from search to track or a missile guidance link appears. RWRs are the simplest [[Electronic Support (ES)]] systems and often cue self-protection [[Electronic Attack (EA)|jammers]] and [[Chaff]] dispensers. Examples include the U.S. AN/ALR-56 and AN/ALR-67 families.
