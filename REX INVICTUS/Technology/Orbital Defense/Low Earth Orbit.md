**[[Low Earth Orbit]]** (LEO) runs from about 160 km to 2,000 km altitude, with periods of about 90 to 127 minutes. The International Space Station (about 400 km) and constellations like Starlink fly there. Spacecraft there cross the South Atlantic Anomaly, where the inner radiation belt dips low, so they need [[Radiation Hardening]]. Below LEO lies the [[Kármán Line]]. In the vault, LEO is the zone guarded by the [[Kármán Line Skeleton]] and the [[Zeus-Wrath Hunter Killer Satellites]].
