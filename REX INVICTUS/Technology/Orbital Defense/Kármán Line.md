The **[[Kármán Line]]** is the conventional boundary of space at 100 km altitude, used by the Fédération Aéronautique Internationale. It is named for Theodore von Kármán, who estimated the height where a wing would need orbital speed to stay aloft. The U.S. military and NASA use 50 miles (about 80 km) instead. The vault's [[Kármán Line Skeleton]] is the orbital defense shell named after it. See [[Low Earth Orbit]].
