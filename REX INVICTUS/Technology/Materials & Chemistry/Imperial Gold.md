**[[Imperial Gold]]** is the Roman counterpart of [[Celestial Bronze]] and one of the three magical weapon metals, with [[Stygian Iron]]. It was consecrated in ancient Rome, and its making was a closely kept secret of the emperors. Sources in canon disagree on the site: [[King Midas]] says the Temple of [[Jupiter]], while [[Reyna Avila Ramírez-Arellano|Reyna]] says the Pantheon.

---

## PJO Canon

- **Properties.** It harms monsters, immortals and their descendants, and works especially well on wind spirits (venti). A broken Imperial Gold weapon explodes: when [[Jason Grace]]'s weapon Ivlivs breaks in *The Lost Hero* it carves a crater into Mount [[Diablo]]. It is rarer than [[Celestial Bronze]] and [[Camp Half-Blood]] never uses it.
- **Notable weapons.** [[Jason Grace|Jason]]'s coin that becomes a sword or lance (Ivlivs), and later his gladius. [[Reyna Avila Ramírez-Arellano|Reyna]]'s dagger and spear, Hazel's spatha and [[Frank Zhang|Frank]]'s spear. Most weapons at [[Camp Jupiter]] are Imperial Gold, and new gold is forged in the camp's sacred temple.
- **[[Alaska]].** After the quest of *The Son of Neptune*, [[Percy Jackson|Percy]], Frank and Hazel recover lost Legion weapons and the eagle standard from the Hubbard Glacier in [[Alaska]].

## UOE (Far Future)

- Produced on [[Vulcanis-Prime]] with [[Celestial Bronze]] and [[Stygian Iron|Stygian iron]].
- Added in trace amounts to Imperial ammunition through [[Divine-Metallurgical Binding Impurities (DMBI)]].
- The [[DIFM-CFT Smoke]] table rates it an excellent conductor with "violent discharge on catastrophic fracture", which matches the canon explosion on breaking.
- [[Roman Legions]] and the far-future [[Legio XII Fulminata]] inherit the Roman tradition of gold arms.
