**[[Depleted Uranium]]** (DU) is uranium with less of the fissile isotope U-235 than natural uranium, a by-product of enrichment. It is about 1.7 times as dense as lead and is used in kinetic penetrators and tank armor.

- **Self-sharpening.** DU fails by adiabatic shear: the rod's nose sheds material in bands and stays pointed, while tungsten tends to mushroom. This gives DU rods an edge in penetration.
- **Pyrophoric.** Fragments ignite on impact, adding incendiary effect.
- **Use.** The U.S. M829 [[Armor-Piercing Fin-Stabilized Discarding Sabot (APFSDS)|APFSDS]] series, the 30 mm GAU-8 rounds of the A-10, and the armor package of later M1 Abrams tanks.
- **Concerns.** Its chemical toxicity and low radioactivity raise health and environmental questions in former battle zones.
