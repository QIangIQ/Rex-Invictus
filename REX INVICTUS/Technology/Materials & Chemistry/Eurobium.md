**[[Eurobium]]** is a strategic far-future material used for [[Warp Shielding]].

## UOE (Far Future)

- In the [[War of Princes]] the Voss defenders ran short of Eurobium for their warp shields and scrapped the void-shielded wrecks of Vanguard and Mastodon patterns (see [[Foreign Ship Model Names]]) to make up the gap.
- [[Annex 13287]] records the fight over the largest Eurobium mining plant of the war. A reactor breach incinerated 72% of its reserves, and Eurobium futures fell 16% within the hour.
- [[Aspina Aggregates]] is the easiest source of Eurobium, [[Hyperium]], Teshlack and Projinite outside the [[UOE]], and ships it along the [[Tachyon Bridge Network]].
