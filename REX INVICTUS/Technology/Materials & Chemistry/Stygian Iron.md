**[[Stygian Iron]]** is one of the three magical weapon metals of the Riordan setting, alongside [[Celestial Bronze]] and [[Imperial Gold]]. It is mined and forged in the [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] and cooled in the River [[Styx]].

---

## PJO Canon

- **Properties.** Unlike [[Celestial Bronze]] and [[Imperial Gold]], Stygian iron can harm mortals, monsters and immortals alike. Instead of only breaking a monster's body, it absorbs the monster's essence, which can stop it re-forming in [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]]. It drains life force, so it also affects ghosts, shades and the undead. A wielder can channel [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] power through it, such as commanding the dead or opening fissures. It does not harm lycanthropes, because it is not silver.
- **Who uses it.** Children of [[Hades]] ([[Pluto]]) and of other Underworld deities, plus some powerful gods.
- **Notable items.** [[Nico di Angelo]]'s black sword, which he carries from *[[The Battle of the Labyrinth]]* onward. [[Hades]]' sword, commissioned by [[Persephone]] as a Symbol of Power and seen in *[[The Last Olympian]]*. [[Hades]]' staff and chariot, [[Thanatos]]' scythe, and the frame of the Doors of Death are also Stygian iron.

---

## Rex Invictus (Present Day)

Present-day use follows PJO canon. [[Nico di Angelo]] and [[Bianca di Angelo]] are the children of [[Hades]] closest to the metal.

---

## UOE (Far Future)

- [[Vulcanis-Prime]] is one of the few forge-worlds that produces all three metals, and one of the only planets that can bear the presence of [[Styx]] herself.
- Imperial alloys carry trace Stygian iron through [[Divine-Metallurgical Binding Impurities (DMBI)]], as in the [[VAS-762R APDS “QVARTUS”]], [[VAS-762R HE “PHOEBUS-3”]] and [[VAS-762R RIP ”NAIIL”]] cartridges.
- The [[DIFM-CFT Smoke]] metal table lists it as the metal that prevents monster re-formation.
- The [[Apollonian Hospitalier Complex]] is built of Hephestian ferrocrete reinforced with polymerized Stygian mesh.
