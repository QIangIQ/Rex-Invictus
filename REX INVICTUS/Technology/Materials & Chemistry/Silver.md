**[[Silver]]** (Ag, atomic number 47) is a soft precious metal with the highest electrical and thermal conductivity of any element.

---

## Chemistry (IRL)

It tarnishes black from sulfur compounds in air, and its ions are antimicrobial, which is why it is used in wound dressings and water filters. Most silver is mined as a by-product of lead, zinc and copper ores. It is used in electronics, solar cells, mirrors, coins and, historically, photographic film.

## Myth and Folklore

Silver is the metal of the moon and of [[Artemis]] and [[Selene]]. Folklore holds it harmful to [[Werewolves]], [[Vampire|vampires]] and other creatures of the night. In [[Percy Jackson and the Olympians|PJO]] the [[Hunters of Artemis]] use silver arrows and knives.
