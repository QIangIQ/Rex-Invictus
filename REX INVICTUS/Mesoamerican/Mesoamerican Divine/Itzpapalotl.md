**[[Itzpapalotl]]**, the "Obsidian Butterfly", is a fearsome Aztec goddess who ruled Tamoanchan, the paradise of the gods.

---

## Mesoamerican Myth (IRL)

She is drawn as a skeletal woman with butterfly or bat wings edged in obsidian blades, and jaguar claws on her hands and feet. She leads the tzitzimimeh, star demons who were believed to descend and devour people during solar eclipses. She was a patron of women who died in childbirth and of the Chichimec peoples, and she is linked to the tenth day-sign and the thirteen-day period ruled from Tamoanchan in the ritual calendar. Compare [[Coatlicue]], [[Quetzalcoatl]] and [[Xolotl]].
