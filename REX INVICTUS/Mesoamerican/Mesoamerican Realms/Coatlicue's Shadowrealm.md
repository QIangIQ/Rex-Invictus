### Description
Coatlicue's Shadowrealm is a dark, empty, and completely flat Shadowrealm designed to imprison the Mother of all Gods, [[Coatlicue]]. Desolate without a fraction of light or sound, the [[Elders]] designed the most distant, unforgiving Shadowrealm imaginable to banish the destroyer. [1] In a place without substance, all that could be done was awaken from dreams to nightmares without end.

### History
[[Coatlicue]] was stranded in the Shadowrealm for millennium as punishment for her horrific stampede against the various [[Elders]] in time past. Her perpetual hatred and isolation drover Coatlicue insane as her experimented body.[2]
She escaped the realm when [[Dr. John Dee|John Dee]] used the Four [[Swords of Power]] to create a Leygate into the Shadowrealm. Reciting her name released four dim pinpricks of light into the darkness, acting as an anchor to follow.
Coatlicue was lured out with the promise of [[Josh Newman]]'s powerful and rare Gold Aura, and she nearly got her reward.  [[Aoife]], [[Niten]], and [[Sophie Newman]] attacked her relentlessly, wrestling her away from her prize. Aoife eventually managed to trap Coatlicue back into her Shadowrealm, but she was forced in as well, and remained there, trapped in eternal battle and endless darkness.
It is assumed that Niten later entered Coatlicue's Shadowrealm to rescue Aoife, and succeeded.[3] It is also presumed that Coatlicue remains there, trapped until there is another window of opportunity to escape. However, it could be that [[Marethyu]] helped Niten to go there and probably he fought the Mother of All Gods so Niten could flee with Aoife safely and if there was a fight, it is clear that Marethyu had won, leaving her either trapped or killed.
