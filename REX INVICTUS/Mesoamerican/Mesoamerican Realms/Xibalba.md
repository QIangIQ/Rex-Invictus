## Real-World Background (IRL)

Xibalba ("place of fear") is the Maya underworld ruled by the Lords of Death, where the Hero Twins of the *Popol Vuh* defeat them in a ball game.

### Description
Xibalba is a Shadowrealm that connects with nine other realms. Roughly translated, the name means: place of fear. Known as The Crossroads for the high amount of leygates located within. Each entrance Is surrounded by archaic symbols carved into the rock. [1]
The realm is entirely one expansive cave with few inhabitants. The cave has many stalagmites and stalactites, remaining geologically active. Bubbling pits of lava belched noxious sulfur gases into the air. Luminescent fungi coat the walls.

#### Connected Shadowrealms
An astounding nine total shadowrealms connect directly with Xibalba. Those explicitly mentioned or seen include: Earth (via the Parisian Notre Dame leygate) and the [[Pleistocene Era Shadowrealm]] (via henge gate) [2].

### History
It is historically considered neutral territory by all Elder factions. In particular, the [[Dark Elders]] use it to send messengers to other [[Shadowrealms]] to guarantee every remote loyalist receives impending news.
The [[Anpu]] cross it.
It is also the place where [[Dr. John Dee]] chose his distinctively caustic aura scent of sulfur, brimstone and rotten eggs. Ironically, these same pathways became the vehicle for declaring Dee utlaga by his masters, sending his disavowal to allies in all realms.
