## MTWABV Canon

The Fulger Bloodline is the ancestral supernatural lineage of the [[Fulger Clan]], originating from [[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]]'s existence as a powerful Lightning Spirit before her transformation into a Noble Vampire. Its defining ability is an extraordinarily powerful affinity with Lightning, accompanied by immense speed potential and a residual spiritual connection to the elemental existence from which the family originated.[1]
Unlike most Noble Vampire Bloodlines, Fulger power did not originate as an ordinary hereditary Vampire ability. Carmila was originally an elemental life-form whose body fundamentally consisted of Lightning. When [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] transformed her into a Vampire, her natural power was converted into a hereditary Bloodline that could be transmitted to descendants and, in weaker form, granted to initiated members of her Clan.[1]
This conversion also created the Bloodline's greatest historical weakness: a Lightning Spirit naturally exists as its element, while Carmila's Vampire descendants were forced to channel enormous electrical power through physical flesh. Generations of Fulgers therefore possessed powers that could injure their own bodies if pushed beyond what their physiology could withstand.[1]

### Origin
[[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]] was originally a high-order Lightning Spirit.
Her existence fundamentally differed from ordinary biological life. Lightning was not simply something Carmila controlled—it was part of what she was.
Through an agreement with [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]], Carmila became a Vampire.
The transformation gave her Vampire physiology but caused her elemental nature to become restricted by physical flesh. Natashia later summarized the result by explaining that the family had effectively become weaker because the power that once existed naturally as Lightning was now limited by a Vampire body.[1]
Carmila nevertheless passed her power to her descendants, creating the hereditary ability now recognized as the Fulger Bloodline.

### Main Bloodline
Direct descendants of Carmila possess something that ordinary initiated Fulger members do not: a remnant of their ancestor's original Lightning Spirit nature.
Natashia Fulger described this as approximately a one-percent trace of Lightning Spirit ancestry residing within the Soul of the main family.[2]
This residual Spirit nature is not simply the source of their Lightning manipulation.
Natashia explicitly distinguished between Lightning power and potential.
An initiated Clan member can acquire the potential to use Lightning, but Carmila's direct descendants inherit the deeper spiritual characteristic that gives them the possibility of reaching substantially greater heights.[2]
Known direct descendants include Natashia, Victoria, Sasha and [[Tatsuya Rider]].

### Clan Initiation
Like the other ancient Count families, Clan Fulger can transmit part of its Bloodline through a Clan Initiation Ritual.
A Noble Vampire accepted into the Clan receives a drop of the Clan Leader's Blood and Essence.[2]
Successful initiation produces several changes:
- the Vampire develops characteristic blonde hair;
- their Bloodline becomes connected to Fulger;
- their loyalty toward the main family is strengthened;
- and they acquire the potential to develop Lightning.[2]
The ritual is not equivalent to enslavement. It strengthens an existing desire to serve the main family rather than destroying personality or transforming the recipient into a mindless puppet.
Initiated members also remain below the natural potential of Carmila's main descendants. Natashia explicitly explained that ordinary Clan members cannot awaken a stronger version of the inherited potential than the main Bloodline from which the ritual originates.[2]

### Core Powers and Abilities
- Electricity Manipulation ([[Electrokinesis]]): The fundamental supernatural ability of the Fulger Bloodline. Users generate, control and direct electricity and Lightning.[3][4]
- Electricity Generation: Fulger carriers naturally produce enormous quantities of electrical Energy rather than requiring an external power source.
- Lightning Manipulation: Users can manipulate Lightning as a combat element, including large bolts and atmospheric strikes.[5]
- Electrical Attacks: Lightning can be projected as bolts, concentrated blasts, arcs and large discharges.
- Electric Aura: Fulger users can surround their entire bodies in Lightning. Sasha and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Fulger Count Forms both manifest as bodies heavily covered in electrical Energy.[6]
- Electrical Augmentation: Lightning can be circulated through the body to improve physical performance.
- Electrically Enhanced Speed: The Bloodline's most characteristic combat application. Users propel and accelerate themselves through Lightning, reaching speeds far above what their normal Vampire bodies would produce. Sasha used Fulger Lightning to escape the Inquisition at supersonic speed, while Victor repeatedly used the ability as his primary acceleration power.[7][8]
- Electrically Enhanced Movement: Sudden changes in direction and explosive bursts of acceleration can be produced by concentrating Lightning through the limbs.
- Lightning Concentration: Users can concentrate an unusually large amount of Lightning in particular portions of the body. Victor's Fulger Count Form placed especially heavy concentrations of power in his legs because the transformation specialized in speed.[9]
- Electricity Infusion: Lightning can be channeled into weapons. [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] commonly combines Fulger Lightning with [[Taranis]].[10]
- Lightning Weaponry: Concentrated Lightning can extend around or beyond physical weapons, effectively forming larger energy blades and similar offensive shapes.[11]
- Electrical Constructs: Skilled users can shape Lightning into complex forms rather than only firing unstructured bolts.
- Atmospheric Electricity Manipulation: Fulger users can interact with Lightning in the surrounding atmosphere and storm clouds instead of relying exclusively upon electricity produced directly from their own bodies.
- Lightning Calling: Powerful users can call down Lightning from above over a battlefield.[12]
- Electricity Resistance: Fulger carriers possess considerable tolerance toward electrical Energy because their bodies naturally generate and conduct it. This is not complete immunity; the Bloodline can produce enough Lightning to damage its own Vampire host.[13]

### Electromagnetic Applications
Advanced Fulger users discovered that Lightning could be used not merely offensively but as a sensory system.
- Electromagnetic Field Generation: Natashia and Sasha developed a technique in which electrical/static Energy is emitted around their bodies as a controlled field.
- Electromagnetic Detection: Disturbances entering the generated field can be detected, allowing the user to perceive movement around themselves without relying solely upon sight or hearing.
- Omnidirectional Detection: Because the electrical field surrounds the user, threats can be detected from multiple directions.
- Enhanced Spatial Awareness: Information gathered from the field provides a detailed understanding of nearby movement and positioning.

#### Red Static Field
Red Static Field is the most advanced known Fulger-derived sensory technique.
Natashia and Sasha originally developed the principle by releasing controlled static around themselves and stabilizing the resulting electrical overload through electromagnetic effects. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later massively expanded the concept, producing a detection field extending approximately 15.693 kilometers around himself.[14]
Victor's version could also feed information directly into his extremely developed combat processing, allowing detected threats to be met with nearly immediate responses.

### Lightning Spirit Applications
The Fulger Bloodline contains a deeper elemental nature inherited from Carmila.

#### Electricity Mimicry
Rather than merely coating themselves in Lightning, advanced carriers can progressively make their physical existence behave like Lightning itself.[15]

#### Elemental Transformation
Carmila's Secret [[Martial Arts]] were designed around returning her descendants toward their ancestral Lightning Spirit state.
The central principle is that the user stops treating Lightning as something external and instead synchronizes the body with the element:
"I am the Lightning... and the Lightning is me."[16]
At higher levels, the user's body becomes increasingly elemental.

#### Lightning Physiology
The ultimate direction of this development is a body capable of existing as Lightning rather than merely manipulating it.
The Fulger women's later evolution into Dragons magnified this characteristic rather than destroying it; their previous Lightning abilities were incorporated into their new bodies and developed into increasingly extreme elemental states.[17]

#### Electrical Intangibility / Elemental Body
When a carrier enters a sufficiently developed Lightning-like state, conventional physical interaction becomes increasingly difficult because the user's form behaves less like ordinary flesh and more like electrical Energy. This application should be limited to users actually demonstrating the advanced elemental state rather than attributed automatically to every Fulger Vampire.

### Fulger Vampire Count Form
The Fulger [[Vampire Count Form]] is the Count-class transformation associated with the Bloodline.
Sasha demonstrated a Count-class Lightning transformation during the battle against the Inquisition, dramatically increasing her speed.[18]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later deliberately manifested his own Fulger Count Form.[19]
Known Bloodline-related effects include:
- Electrokinetic Empowerment: Greatly increases Lightning output.
- Extreme Speed: The transformation is heavily specialized toward acceleration and movement speed.[20]
- Lightning Body Coating: The user's body becomes completely covered by Lightning.
- Lightning Concentration in Limbs: Victor's transformation particularly concentrated power in his legs.[21]
- Enhanced Acceleration: The user can reach maximum combat speed substantially faster.
- Enhanced Physical Abilities: The transformation amplifies Vampire physical characteristics together with the Bloodline. This is a Count-form property rather than something exclusive to Fulger.[22]
- Greater Electrical Tolerance: The transformed body can sustain larger quantities of Lightning than the user's ordinary Vampire state.

### Bloodline-Derived Combat Techniques
These are techniques built from the Fulger Bloodline rather than separate innate powers.

#### One Hit, One Million Hits
A Fulger-derived speed principle adapted by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] into swordsmanship. By using extreme acceleration, Victor performs an enormous number of attacks so quickly and along such similar trajectories that they effectively become one catastrophic strike.[23]

#### One Hit, One Million Punches
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s unarmed variation of the same Fulger-derived principle, replacing the sword strike with an enormous number of nearly simultaneous punches.[24]

#### One Billion Hits
After his speed increased enormously, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] evolved the technique from millions of strikes into approximately one billion hits. During his battle with [[Fenrir]], the resulting attack produced an enormous red plasma-like discharge.[25]

#### One Cut, One Million Cuts
A later greatsword adaptation in which the Fulger multi-strike principle is applied through enormous numbers of cuts concentrated against the same area.[26]

#### Kirin
A [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]-created technique derived from Fulger [[Electrokinesis]]. Lightning is gathered through the atmosphere and concentrated until it manifests as a gigantic Lightning beast that descends upon the target.[27]
As Victor's control developed, the technique evolved from producing a single Lightning creature into creating large numbers of them simultaneously.[28]

#### Lynet
A Fulger martial form associated with Carmila's Lightning Spirit tradition. Sasha used the Clan's advanced elemental principles during the Demonic invasion of Fulger territory, entering a Lightning-dominated state and annihilating enemies through extreme speed and widespread Lightning strikes.[29]

### Carrier-Specific Bloodline Evolutions
The following powers genuinely evolved from Fulger Lightning but are not automatically possessed by every Fulger.

#### Plasma Manipulation
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually developed his inherited Lightning to such a degree that it produced and controlled plasma.[30]
This represents an evolved high-energy application of his Fulger-derived [[Electrokinesis]] rather than an original standard ability of Clan Fulger.
Known classifications include:
- Plasma Generation
- Plasma Manipulation
- Plasma Attacks
It should not be used to claim every Fulger Vampire naturally possesses Plasma Manipulation.

#### Red Lightning
After approximately seven hundred years of development within Hell's altered temporal environment, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s inherited Fulger Lightning mutated into destructive crimson/red Lightning.[31]
[[Scathach]] recognized this as a mutation/evolution of his pre-existing Lightning rather than an unrelated new elemental power.
This is therefore a legitimate Fulger-derived evolution but remains specific to Victor's developmental path.
Later strengthening from consuming [[Zeus]] should not be classified as part of the Fulger Bloodline because Zeus' experience and Divine Lightning were external additions.

#### Electrical Flight
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] developed the ability to use extreme electrical acceleration for aerial movement and later demonstrated Lightning-based high-speed travel. This should be considered a carrier-developed extension rather than a guaranteed Fulger ability.

### Bloodline Weaknesses

#### Self-Damage
The Fulger Bloodline historically possessed a severe physiological flaw.
Carmila's original elemental power was inherited by descendants possessing physical Vampire bodies. Consequently, a carrier could generate more Lightning than their own flesh was capable of safely conducting.
Natashia's Bloodline became powerful enough to threaten the integrity of her mortal body and played a central role in the Soul crisis surrounding Carmila and Natasha.[32]
Sasha was deliberately bred from a paternal lineage whose physical characteristics gave her better tolerance toward this problem.

#### Energy Consumption
Extreme acceleration and large-scale Lightning techniques require correspondingly greater amounts of Energy.

#### Control Requirement
Because very high speeds dramatically reduce the amount of time available for decision-making, poor control can make Fulger acceleration dangerous even when the body itself survives it.

### Abilities Not Produced by the Fulger Bloodline
- Enhanced Perception / Time Difference: Sasha's exceptional perception and complementary physical traits are connected to [[William Salvatore Florence]]'s paternal lineage rather than Carmila's original Lightning Bloodline. They synergize extremely well with Fulger speed but should remain classified separately.[33]
- William's Enhanced Physical Endurance: Likewise paternal rather than Fulger.
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Demonic Beast Fulger Form: Victor later modified the Fulger Count Form using characteristics taken from Demonic Beasts. The resulting claws, tail, wings and beast-like traits came from those external creatures, not the Fulger Bloodline itself.[34]
- [[Zeus]]' Lightning Authority: Victor's Lightning was later enhanced by Zeus' experience and power, but Divine Lightning originating from Zeus is not an intrinsic Fulger trait.
- Dragon Abilities: Powers acquired through later Blood Dragon physiology are separate unless they directly represent the continued evolution of Fulger Lightning.

### Physical Limitation
The original Fulger weakness originates directly from Carmila's transformation into a Vampire.
A Lightning Spirit naturally possesses an elemental body and therefore cannot be injured simply because too much Lightning passes through ordinary flesh.
Carmila's descendants are different.
Their power became hereditary, but their Vampire bodies remained physical. Excessive output could consequently damage the user's own body.[1]
Natashia suffered this problem extensively.
The weakness became important enough that she deliberately selected [[William Salvatore Florence]] as the father of her child partly because his hereditary physical characteristics could complement the Fulger lineage.
Sasha inherited significantly greater bodily endurance from her paternal Bloodline and could therefore withstand Fulger Lightning considerably better than previous generations.[1][35]
This paternal endurance is not itself part of the Fulger Bloodline.

### Perception and William's Bloodline
Sasha's extraordinary ability to perceive high-speed movement is sometimes incorrectly grouped with Fulger Lightning.
Chapter 635 clarifies that the ability originated from William's lineage.[35]
William's family possessed a low-level Bloodline that heightened perception, causing events to appear slower and allowing the user to observe fine details more easily.
Natashia deliberately chose this lineage because such perception synergized perfectly with Fulger speed.
Sasha therefore possesses:
Fulger Lightning + William's enhanced perception/endurance traits.
The two Bloodlines complement one another but should remain separately identified.

### Secret Martial Arts
The Secret [[Martial Arts]] of Clan Fulger were personally created by [[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]] to overcome the limitations created when her descendants became Vampires.[1]
The foundation of the system is simple:
Return to the origin of the Bloodline as a Lightning Spirit.
Rather than treating Lightning as an external power, the practitioner synchronizes their body and element until the distinction begins disappearing.
The fundamental principle is expressed as:
"I am the Lightning... and the Lightning is me."[1]
At sufficient mastery, Lightning completely covers the user's body and forces the Vampire toward an increasingly elemental state.
This is not a separate Bloodline ability in itself, but rather a martial methodology specifically designed to exploit the Bloodline's hidden Spirit potential.

#### First Form: Lynet
Lynet is a named form within Carmila's martial tradition.
Sasha used it during the defense of Fulger territory.
After entering the Lightning Spirit-like state, she accelerated through the battlefield while enormous numbers of Lightning strikes descended around her, killing Demons across a vast region within seconds.[1]
Only the Clan heir and particularly talented members were considered capable of fully learning Carmila's original martial arts.

### Vampire Count Form
The Fulger Bloodline possesses a specialized Vampire Count transformation.
In high-level users, the form dramatically increases:
- Lightning output;
- speed;
- physical capability;
- elemental control;
- and the user's ability to approach a Lightning Spirit-like state.
Sasha's transformation completely surrounded her body in Lightning.
Natashia eventually developed the form even further. Through mastery and the strengthening of her Bloodline, her body could become increasingly similar to living Lightning rather than merely a Vampire surrounded by electricity.[36]
Carmila's martial system becomes particularly effective when combined with the completed Fulger Count Form because the transformation makes the user's physiology more compatible with its elemental origin.[1]

### Bloodline Recognition
The Divine weapon [[Taranis]], the Daggers of the Ancestral Spirit contains an enchantment capable of distinguishing legitimate Fulger Blood.
The weapon rejects unauthorized users through Lightning.[37]
This is particularly important because merely possessing Fulger Lightning is not sufficient.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] inherited the ability to manipulate Lightning through Sasha, but this did not originally make him a biological descendant of Carmila and therefore did not automatically give him the same recognition from the ancestral weapon.
Taranis consequently provides direct evidence that the Fulger Bloodline contains an identifiable hereditary component beyond simple [[Electrokinesis]].

### Dormant Inheritance
A direct descendant can carry Fulger potential without initially manifesting Lightning.
[[Victoria Fulger Elderblood]] is the clearest example.
Victoria belonged to Carmila's direct lineage despite historically failing to display the signature Lightning power.
The ancestry nevertheless remained within her and was transmitted into later generations, including her son [[Tatsuya Rider]].
After the family's later evolution, Victoria herself eventually awakened the ancestral Lightning potential.[38]
Fulger inheritance should therefore not be measured solely by whether an individual is currently capable of firing Lightning.

### Strengthening by Victor's Blood
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] incorporated Sasha and Natashia into his personal lineage, his Blood did not replace their existing Bloodlines.
Instead, it strengthened what was already present.[35]
Sasha's paternal endurance became greater, while the women's Fulger Lightning simultaneously increased in power.
Victor concluded from this that existing Noble Vampires respond differently from beings who are transformed into entirely new Vampires: his Blood reinforces their established hereditary characteristics rather than simply creating unrelated abilities.[35]
This principle is extremely important to the later evolution of Fulger.

### Blood Dragon Evolution
The Bloodline eventually reached something close to its original form after the main Fulger women became [[Blood Dragons]].
Dragon physiology removed many of the physical limitations that had restricted the family while simultaneously magnifying their elemental nature.
By Chapter 920, the main Fulger women were capable of completely transforming into Lightning Spirits, effectively reproducing the form from which Carmila's Bloodline originally descended.[39]
Sasha's Lightning evolved beyond conventional electrical manipulation until she could be described as existing as Lightning itself rather than merely controlling it.[40]
Victoria likewise awakened the Bloodline after previously failing to express it.
The evolution therefore completed a cycle spanning the family's entire history:
Lightning Spirit → Vampire Bloodline → Lightning-Spirit Vampire → Blood Dragon capable of becoming Lightning itself.

### Trivia
- Fulger means "Lightning", directly reflecting Carmila's elemental nature.[42]
- Carmila was genuinely a Lightning Spirit before becoming a Vampire; this is directly confirmed rather than merely being Clan folklore.[1]
- Transforming into a Vampire initially made Carmila's elemental power more restricted because it became dependent upon physical flesh.[1]
- Direct descendants preserve a residual trace of Carmila's Lightning Spirit ancestry within their Souls.[2]
- An initiated Fulger can acquire Lightning without becoming a biological descendant of Carmila.
- The Clan's Secret [[Martial Arts]] exist specifically to reverse the limitations caused by the family's Vampire physiology.[1]
- Sasha's enhanced perception and unusual bodily endurance are partly inherited through William and should not be mislabeled as original Fulger traits.[35]
- The ancestral weapon [[Taranis]] can distinguish legitimate Fulger Blood from an outsider who merely possesses Lightning.[43]
- Victoria demonstrates that the Bloodline can remain dormant and later awaken.
- After the Blood Dragon transformation, the main family regained the ability to completely enter Lightning Spirit states.[44]
