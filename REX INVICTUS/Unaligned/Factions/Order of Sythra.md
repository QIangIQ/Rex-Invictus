The Order of Sythra is a political order in the Upper Sectors, made up of former rulers and independent representatives. It treats the spread of Victor Elderblood's Empire as a threat to the balance of cosmic powers, and it answers by feeding separatist movements on worlds the Empire has not finished absorbing.[1]

### Overview
The Order does not land an army to seize planets, and it does not pirate cargo. Its tools are envoys, money, and recognition. Leaders who are not named decide to back separatists specifically where administrative incorporation is still open.[1]
The envoys negotiate with local authorities. The offer is resources and political recognition, in exchange for support of independence. On some worlds the representatives persuade the rulers to turn away from Imperial administration. On others the Order pays groups that organize protests, occupy public buildings, and demand that the garrisons leave.[1]
No great faction is named as the Order's patron. The chapter's later judgment is that most of these small powers act shortsightedly, and that only a few truly act on the commission of the higher factions. Sythra is not placed in either list by name.[1]

### History

#### The incorporated worlds
The campaign runs through conquered worlds at different stages of annexation. Where the envoys succeed, local rulers leave the Imperial administration. Where they do not, financed crowds carry the same demand into the streets: protests, occupied public buildings, and calls for the garrisons to withdraw.[1]

#### The industrial planet
The gravest crisis is an industrial planet whose administration has only just passed to Imperial representatives. The local authorities declare independence and try to take the production installations under their own control. Part of the population stays loyal to the Empire. Clashes between the two sides threaten to stop the planet's economy.[1]
Regional command sends mediators to negotiate a settlement. The armed forces protect the strategic facilities. The investigation finds Sythra agents, and finds that their money financed the rising. Once that evidence is laid before the local authorities, the separatists lose part of their support.[1]
The conflict ends with the arrest of the principal organizers and the withdrawal of the foreign agents. The Imperial administration still has to rebuild the government if it wants to avoid another rising.[1]

### Relationship with the Elderblood Empire
Sythra fights the annexation in the administration, not in the fleet. The Empire answers with mediators, garrisons on the strategic sites, and evidence, not with the solar-system elites used against the [[Varkhess League]].[1]
As with the other operations in the chapter, the losses the Empire appears to take are not the point. Those dead are clones, already returned to the Emperor's galaxy to recover. [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]]'s closing decision is to make an example of powers that attack because they expect a greater faction to shield them. Kaguya gives that work to the Third Legion, [[The Black Hand of the Emperor]], together with the First, [[The Spartans]]. The chapter does not show that order carried out against Sythra, and it does not say Sythra was acting under such a shield.[1]

### Current Status
On the industrial planet, the rising's organizers are under arrest, the foreign agents have withdrawn, and the separatists have lost part of their support. The planetary government still has to be restructured.[1]
The Order's leaders are not named, captured, or stated to be dead. Other worlds where the envoys succeeded are not given a separate ending. The planet itself is not named.[1]

### Trivia
- "Sythra agents" and "foreign agents" are the same intervention in this chapter. The agents withdraw. The Order is not said to disband.[1]
- The Order is the third small faction treated in Chapter 1203. It follows the [[Nharok Consortium]] and is followed by an unnamed alliance of space pirates.[1]
- No earlier chapter on this wiki names the Order of Sythra. This article uses only Chapter 1203.
