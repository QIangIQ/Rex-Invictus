The Heralds of Chaos are a supernatural species and elite Imperial organization descended from War, the first Progenitor deliberately created by Victor Elderblood after obtaining the Divinity of Beginning. Victor established the lineage by reconstructing War's existence through an extraordinary combination of Dragon Fire, Positive Energy, Negative Energy, the characteristics of the original Four Horsemen and a portion of his own Essence. Rather than merely empowering an existing Demon, the transformation produced a new kind of Being capable of passing on a [[Chaos]]-derived supernatural nature to later generations. War became the Progenitor of the species, while Victor remained its foundational creator and absolute sovereign.[1][2]
The Heralds combine the characteristics of a developing racial lineage with those of an Imperial service whose members exist to carry out assignments requiring power, discretion and unquestionable loyalty. They serve as messengers, bodyguards, scouts, investigators and executioners rather than conventional front-line troops. Their history begins with the transformations following Victor's judgment of former enemies, continues through specialized operations against ancient Dragons and rival Pantheons, and expands into a substantial organization whose members develop individual Eldritch Forms. Despite their purpose and extreme devotion, Heralds possess thoughts and emotions and are explicitly treated as living Beings rather than disposable machines.[3][4][5][6]

### Origin and Nature

#### The Birth of a Progenitor
The lineage originated after War completed a final assignment for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] during the latter's development as the Dragon God of [[Chaos]]. Impressed by War's unshakable loyalty and competence, Victor abandoned his initial intention of merely strengthening a Demonic servant and chose to create a new species. He gathered an Energy sphere consisting of approximately 1% Positive Energy, 1% Negative Energy and 98% Dragon Fire, then incorporated the characteristics acquired from the other original Horsemen and a portion of his own true Essence. His Divinity of Beginning reacted to the transformation, giving rise to a new Progenitor whose existence combined influences that would ordinarily be considered incompatible. The proportions describe War's original transformation rather than a confirmed universal formula for transforming every subsequent Herald.[1]
War emerged with a face of Darkness, six wings made of dark Energy, genuine Holy characteristics and Dragon Fire replacing the Hellfire associated with his original Demonic body. Even the [[Heavenly Father]] found the result disturbing because an apparently infernal creature carried properties ordinarily associated with Positive Energy and Angels. [[Roxanne Elderblood]] recognized that Victor had created an unprecedented Progenitor, explaining that an existence could embody properties from opposing sides without automatically destroying the balance of Creation. War acknowledged Victor as his King, Father and Creator, establishing a relationship based upon personal gratitude and loyalty as well as the direct supernatural link created through his new Essence.[1][2]

#### Racial Propagation and the Nature of Chaos
The distinction between War and later Heralds is fundamental. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] created the first member and Progenitor, but War himself possesses the ability to create additional individuals belonging to that species. The new Heralds derive their racial identity from War, while their ultimate allegiance is directed toward Victor, whose Essence and Divinity made the founding transformation possible. The lineage therefore operates as both a genuine supernatural race and an Imperial institution built around a shared purpose. Its members are not required to undergo War's original transformation independently or possess the entire combination of powers accumulated through his previous existence as a Horseman and [[Demon God]].[2][4][5]
The species' [[Chaos]]-derived foundation makes it unusual within the normal divisions of Creation, but it should not be equated automatically with every other type of Chaos Being. War's initial existence is described as predominantly dark while still possessing Holy and Positive properties, demonstrating a controlled supernatural contradiction rather than the uncontrolled destruction of Balance. As the lineage develops, some members begin making contact with personal Eldritch Forms, indicating that their relationship with Chaos is not limited to the conventional powers of Gods or Demons. This progression does not prove that all Heralds possess the complete abilities, ontological nature or cosmic authority of mature [[Outer Gods]].[2][5]

#### Individuality, Loyalty and Residence
Heralds possess extreme loyalty to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and instinctively recognize the hierarchy established through War, but the transformation does not automatically remove their minds. The first explicitly transformed recruits demonstrate recognizable consciousness and internal thought after their new bodies are formed, and Victor later observes genuine happiness and purpose when he praises a group of Heralds for completing an operation. Their Imperial duties are narrow and often severe, yet the narrative directly distinguishes them from machines or beings incapable of emotion. Their supernatural obedience should therefore be described alongside their continuing individuality rather than treated as evidence that they are empty avatars.[4][6]
The species also possesses an unusual residence within Victor's own existence. As his Soul developed into a vast internal Cosmos containing planets, dimensions and progressively deeper regions, an innermost hidden Core associated with his Eldritch Form remained inaccessible to ordinary visitors. Chapter 1124 identifies this secluded region as the place where the Heralds usually reside, demonstrating that their everyday existence is tied as closely to their creator's innermost supernatural domain as to the geographical territory of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Their presence there does not establish that every member can independently access all of Victor's protected Soul regions or understand his unrestricted Eldritch nature.[7]

### Physical Characteristics
The first fully depicted ordinary Heralds retain humanoid proportions but acquire an unmistakable supernatural appearance. During their transformation, their faces become featureless Darkness, a pair of wings formed from pure Darkness appears behind them, and their clothing changes into armor resembling that of War, although less massive. These characteristics form the clearest demonstrated baseline for the first generation. Their later appearances in hooded armor preserve the difficulty of seeing their faces, making them immediately recognizable as members of the same lineage even when their individual identities are concealed.[4][6]
War's own form is substantially more imposing and must not be mistaken for the default appearance of every Herald. When first transformed, he manifested six dark wings, while Chapter 986 later describes him at approximately 2.5 meters tall with twelve wings that appeared to absorb the surrounding light. Observers who stared into the Darkness of his hood could perceive numerous disturbing eyes, revealing a further distinction between the matured Progenitor and the initially transformed recruits. The later twelve-winged manifestation is confirmed for War, not for the species generally, and the individual appearance of other Heralds may vary as they grow and approach their Eldritch Forms.[1][2][8]

Victor occupies the supreme position within this hierarchy because the entire lineage originates from his creation of War. War serves as the Progenitor, military leader and direct representative of Victor's will, transforming qualified individuals into Heralds and employing them in specialized assignments. Ordinary members belong to War's racial lineage while operating under the Emperor's authority. This organizational structure does not mean that every Herald has exactly the same power, experience or function; some carry out reconnaissance and concealment, while others serve as escorts, guards, messengers or members of an investigative team.[4][9][5]

### History

#### Dragon God of Chaos
After his rise as the Dragon God of [[Chaos]], [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] rewarded War for completing a final test by reconstructing his supernatural existence. Rather than strengthening an existing Hell-Fire Demon, the Emperor combined opposite Energies, Dragon Fire and supernatural characteristics associated with the other Horsemen before introducing a portion of his own Essence. The Divinity of Beginning helped produce an entirely new Progenitor who could carry dark and Holy characteristics simultaneously. War acknowledged Victor as his father and creator, while Roxanne recognized that a new racial lineage had been born. The first Herald therefore did not arise from conventional recruitment, but from the deliberate creation of a new foundation for supernatural life.[1][2]
The earliest confirmed induction occurred during the judgment of individuals who had previously opposed Victor. War gathered several former enemies and brought the former Human exorcists Jimmy and Thomas before him. He explained that their years of service as informants had become genuine contributions to Victor's interests and that the Emperor rewarded loyalty, competence and sustained improvement. After both accepted his offer, War used Darkness to transform them and proclaimed the emergence of the Heralds of Chaos. The two men developed dark wings, featureless faces and armor resembling their Progenitor's own equipment before instinctively traveling to the region where Victor's vast supernatural manifestation could be observed. Their retained thoughts establish that the transformation did not simply replace their consciousness with obedient artificial constructs.[3][4]
Lucy and Karen were present among the other former enemies gathered for judgment, alongside figures such as [[Kurama]], [[Luan Davis]] and [[Corneliu Funar]]. Later secondary member listings associate Lucy and Karen with the Heralds, but the specifically depicted transformation at the end of Chapter 933 and beginning of Chapter 934 concerns Jimmy and Thomas. For this reason, the founding scene should not be presented as an explicitly depicted simultaneous transformation of all four named individuals. The fate and individual conversion sequences of the other captives must be distinguished from the pair whose induction is directly narrated.[3][4]
War's new subordinates were soon employed on operations requiring specialized supernatural techniques. During the investigation of the ancient Dragon Nidhoggr, a female Herald accompanying War and Nyx Elderblood gathered Energy from the surrounding Dimension together with Energy supplied by Nyx. She combined these sources into a concealment field intended to obscure supernatural activity from a True Dragon's extraordinary awareness of Nature and Creation. War explained that ordinary concealment could fail because [[True Dragons]] perceive changes in their environment, whereas the contradictory character of Chaos could interfere with that compatibility. The operation demonstrated that Heralds were capable of advanced reconnaissance, deliberate Energy combination and specialized sensory evasion rather than existing only to destroy enemies through brute force.[9]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
After the beginning of the Imperial Era, War increasingly served as a direct messenger whose appearance represented [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s personal authority. During the dispute with the [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]], War stood before [[Shiva]] in heavy armor with a darkened face and twelve immense wings, delivering a warning from the Emperor. His supernatural presence unsettled the assembled Gods, and his arrival communicated that further defiance could have catastrophic consequences. The scene established how the Progenitor's appearance could serve diplomatic and punitive purposes without requiring the Emperor to personally conduct every confrontation.[8]
The Heralds subsequently participated in unusually dangerous operations involving Victor's deeper Eldritch existence. When part of his true nature became perceptible in Chapter 1059, War and other Heralds helped restrict the resulting disturbance, opening their wings and assisting the protective measures established around the affected area. Their ability to operate near such a manifestation supports an exceptional degree of tolerance for supernatural conditions that would endanger ordinary beings. However, it does not demonstrate unrestricted immunity to every type of Eldritch corruption, nor does it establish that each ordinary Herald could perceive the Emperor's complete nature without consequences.[10]
The organization's military importance became unmistakable during Victor's confrontation with Shiva and the Hindu Pantheon. As the Emperor displayed overwhelming Imperial force, Heralds emerged from the Void and took positions around him. The narration identified War as stronger than Shiva and the other Heralds present as approximately on his level, placing these established members among the strongest direct servants of the Empire. The assessment concerned the combatants available in that scene and should not be applied as a guaranteed minimum to every new recruit immediately after conversion.[11]
As the Empire expanded, Victor arranged for additional Heralds to be created and intended them to accompany his daughters alongside the Leviathans when the Imperial Princesses eventually traveled beyond protected territory. War performed the actual racial propagation because he was the species' Progenitor. Victor could sense their population growing and new members beginning to contact their own Eldritch Forms. The narration characterized the Heralds as some of the strongest Beings in the Empire, including members whose strength challenged even some of Victor's wives, while only a small number of Imperial combatants could properly face War. At the same time, their purpose was described as the removal of targets Victor ordered eliminated, distinguishing them from forces created primarily for conquest and occupation.[5]
Their integration into the Emperor's innermost existence was also established during this period. Chapter 1124 explained that the hidden Eldritch Core within Victor's already complex Soul served as the normal residence of his Heralds, even though access to Victor's Soul Core was otherwise heavily restricted. The arrangement underscored the unusual relationship between the lineage and its creator and provided a concealed home for an Imperial force whose members were not permanently stationed among ordinary citizens or conventional Legions.[7]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
By the accelerated Imperial Era, War was recognized as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s strongest Herald and commanded a powerful organization capable of undertaking sensitive assignments involving foreign supernatural threats. During the investigation of [[Nikolai Francis Nichev]], a [[Demigod]] compromised by powers from the Upper Sectors, War personally confronted the suspected traitor while four subordinate Heralds—two men and two women—completed the capture of those involved and delivered the prisoners to the Third Legion. The subordinates appeared in dark hooded armor with faces hidden by their supernatural Darkness. Their actions demonstrated a coordinated command structure in which War dealt with the principal danger while other Heralds performed arrests and secured the surrounding operation.[6]
War compressed the compromised moon into a small object and reconstructed an identical replacement to preserve the planet's astronomical conditions. This extraordinary feat belonged specifically to War, not automatically to the four ordinary Heralds accompanying him. He subsequently brought Nikolai to an isolated laboratory created by Victor for interrogating the foreign presence operating through the Demigod. When the hostile entity manifested, the Heralds spread out to contain it and War positioned himself protectively before the Emperor. Victor thanked his subordinates, and their unmistakable emotional response confirmed that even such intensely devoted Eldritch-associated servants remained living Beings capable of happiness, purpose and personal recognition.[6]
As the barrier separating the Emperor's territory from the Upper Sectors weakened, hostile powers attempted to penetrate the Empire through objects containing fragments of foreign Divinities. The Imperial response developed into preparations for a major war, culminating in Victor's decision to deploy War beyond the barrier ahead of the Legions. Victor created the sentient Eldritch greatsword [[Gol-goroth]] specifically for this mission, and War entered the Upper Sectors as a singular high-level combatant carrying the Emperor's judgment. The weapon's special properties allowed War to manifest Eldritch incarnations corresponding to Death, Famine and Pestilence, but these manifestations were products of War and Gol-goroth's later combat powers, not independently confirmed recruits or ordinary members of the Heralds of Chaos lineage.[12][13][14]
War's confrontation with the [[Black Eclipse Pantheon]] reached a new stage when four of its Chief Gods opposed him and the three Eldritch incarnations. In the fighting that followed, War recalled his brothers and revealed powers he had previously concealed. Gol-goroth established an isolated Eldritch domain, while War transferred power and temporarily synchronized the senses of all four combatants. Death, Famine and Pestilence restrained and weakened [[Azrakar]] before War personally decapitated him and pierced his heart; the four together then erased the God of the Void from existence. The defeat demonstrated the extraordinary potential of the Progenitor at his most advanced, but it must not be attributed to the ordinary species as a universal racial ability. The Great Darkness ordered the Horsemen brought before him alive or dead, leaving the wider war unresolved.[15]

### Powers and Abilities
The Heralds possess a genuine supernatural racial foundation and numerous skills suited to the Emperor's service, but their confirmed characteristics must be distinguished from the techniques of War himself and from the talents of individual recruits. The abilities indexed below are based on demonstrated racial transformations, ordinary Herald feats and later collective descriptions. They are not an assertion that every Herald possesses the same proficiency, maximum Energy reserves or specialized Divinities.[4][9][5]

#### Racial Characteristics
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Established Heralds possess extraordinarily advanced strength and supernatural power, with members deployed beside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] during the [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]] confrontation described as approximately comparable to [[Shiva]]. This statement concerns those combatants rather than an established universal starting level for new Heralds.[11]
- [[Chaos]] Physiology: The lineage descends from War, a Progenitor created through the coexistence of Positive Energy, Negative Energy, Dragon Fire and Victor's Essence. The exact relative proportions of these influences in ordinary members have not been specified.[1][2][4]
- Darkness Manipulation and Darkness Physiology: The first inducted Heralds developed faces composed of apparently impenetrable Darkness, together with wings of dark Energy. Their bodies retained a recognizable humanoid structure.[4]
- Wing Manifestation and Flight: Jimmy and Thomas manifested a pair of wings made from pure Darkness after transformation, while later Heralds operated in open space and used their wings during protective and concealment operations.[4][9][10]
- Spatial Mobility: The newly transformed Heralds disappeared from War's location and reappeared within a cosmic environment before Victor's manifestation. The precise method and limits of the movement have not been independently explained.[4]
- Eldritch Physiology (Developing): Members of the expanding lineage began to make contact with their Eldritch Forms as Victor ordered additional Heralds created. This confirms progression toward deeper Eldritch characteristics, not the automatic completion of a mature Outer God state for every individual.[5]
- Space Survival: Heralds can operate in outer space and other supernatural environments without displaying the ordinary environmental vulnerabilities of unprotected Humans.[4][6]
- Enhanced Senses (Limited evidence): Heralds are capable of coordinating surveillance and supernatural containment around immensely powerful beings, although no single universal sensory range or exhaustive suite of special senses has been established for all members.[9][6]

#### Demonstrated Specialist Techniques
- Energy Manipulation: During the surveillance of Nidhoggr, an ordinary female Herald gathered supernatural Energy from both the surrounding Dimension and Nyx, demonstrating direct manipulation of distinct Energy sources.[9]
- Dimensional Energy Manipulation: The same Herald collected Energy from the Dimension itself and employed it in the construction of a sophisticated concealment field.[9]
- Energy Combination: She blended Dimensional Energy with Nyx's Energy to create an effect suited to masking supernatural disturbances from a True Dragon's perception.[9]
- Concealment and Stealth Mastery: The resulting field was designed to hide the observers by using the difficulty [[True Dragons]] experience when sensing [[Chaos]]-derived irregularities within Nature. The technique required preparation and specific Energy sources, rather than proving universal undetectability.[9]
- Perception Manipulation (Anti-Draconic concealment): The unusual properties of the concealment field interfered with the environmental perception of a True Dragon. Its demonstrated application was evasion of perception, not automatic control of the Dragon's mind or senses.[9]
- Barrier Creation (Concealment field): The female Herald formed a dome from the combined Energies during the reconnaissance mission. Its confirmed function was concealment rather than general-purpose invulnerability.[9]
- Battlefield Containment: During the exposure of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s deeper Eldritch existence, the Heralds helped isolate the affected area by spreading their wings and supporting other protective measures. This was a coordinated operation rather than the feat of any single Herald acting alone.[10]
- Dimensional Travel (Demonstrated movement): Members operate between distant locations and supernatural regions, though the exact mechanisms and limits of their transportation powers remain insufficiently defined.[4][6]
- Regeneration (Not universally established): No uniform regeneration level has been directly confirmed for ordinary Heralds. War's exceptional restorative capabilities cannot automatically be assigned to all of his descendants.

#### Resistances and Adaptation
- Resistance to Eldritch Influence (Operational tolerance): Heralds operate near [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Eldritch manifestations and occupy regions associated with his hidden Eldritch Core. The episodes support considerable tolerance but not unlimited protection against every form of Eldritch corruption.[10][7]
- Resistance to Environmental Hazards: Confirmed Herald operations include vacuum environments, dimensional boundaries and extraordinary supernatural disturbances that would be dangerous to ordinary mortals.[4][10][6]
- Mental Resistance (Unquantified): Heralds endure exposure to threatening manifestations of their Emperor that can overwhelm other beings. This does not establish universal resistance to mind manipulation or unrestricted comprehension of his deepest true form.[4][6]

#### War-Exclusive and Weapon-Dependent Abilities
War possesses powers inherited from his former existence as the Horseman of War, his ascension into Demon Godhood, and the distinctive circumstances of his creation as a [[Chaos]] Progenitor. His abilities include specialized combat mastery, the supernatural characteristics of the original Horsemen, extraordinary destructive power and a level of martial experience exceeding that of ordinary Heralds. His later use of [[Gol-goroth]] adds weapon-granted Eldritch manifestations, an isolated domain and the temporary synchronization and empowerment of the Four Horsemen. These abilities are indexed on War/Abilities and must not be presented as automatic properties of each ordinary Herald, particularly the collective existential erasure of [[Azrakar]] in Chapter 1194.[1][14][15]

### Military Role
The Heralds are an elite force intended for assignments whose danger or sensitivity makes ordinary military deployment unsuitable. Their principal purpose is the execution of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s decisions, including the destruction of enemies judged unworthy of conquest. However, their recorded missions are more varied than extermination alone: they carry Imperial messages, conduct surveillance, capture hostile agents, assist in cosmic-scale containment and protect members of the Imperial household. They therefore operate beside rather than replace conventional Imperial Legions, elite soldiers and the Leviathans.[9][8][5][6]

### Power and Limitations
The Heralds are counted among the most powerful servants of the [[Elderblood Empire]], but the level attained by an individual member depends upon their development and available supernatural abilities. During the [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]] confrontation, War stood above [[Shiva]] while the Heralds accompanying [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] were described as comparable to the God-King. Later narration confirms that some Heralds could challenge even powerful members of Victor's family and that only a few Imperial Beings were able to confront War properly. These statements place established Heralds among the Empire's elite without providing an exact universal measure of attack potency, lifting strength, speed or durability for the entire species.[11][5]
War remains the clearest example of the extreme upper limit demonstrated by the lineage's Progenitor. He possesses the unique experiences and Divine development of his original existence as a Horseman and [[Demon God]], together with Victor's personally bestowed Essence and later Eldritch armament. His advanced feats against the Gods of Black Eclipse, including the combined erasure of [[Azrakar]] in Chapter 1194, should therefore be distinguished from the powers directly demonstrated by lower-ranking members. Conversely, the female Herald's specialist concealment technique establishes that ordinary members can possess useful capabilities unrelated to War's personal destructive attacks.[9][15]
The species' limitations remain incompletely understood. There is no demonstrated guarantee that all members possess identical wing counts, Energy reserves, specialized skills or completed Eldritch Forms, and their ability to operate near Victor's deeper nature is not evidence of absolute resistance to every supernatural attack. The requirements, limitations and possible failure conditions of War's conversion process have not been fully explained. The lineage's evolution into mature Eldritch forms also does not independently establish that each ordinary Herald is equivalent to the independent [[Outer Gods]] whose Divinities originate from an entirely different cosmic source.[1][4][10][5]

### Relationship with Outer Gods
The Heralds originate within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s own Cosmos as descendants of a Progenitor produced by the combination of [[Chaos]]-related powers, Positive and Negative Energy and his Divinity of Beginning. Their supernatural development later introduces explicit Eldritch Forms, and their normal residence lies near Victor's hidden Eldritch Core, establishing a direct connection with his deeper existence. Nevertheless, ordinary Heralds have not been shown to possess the complete conceptual inheritance, universal authority or mature physiology associated with independent [[Outer Gods]]. The supported classification is therefore a Chaos-derived Progenitor species with developing Eldritch characteristics, with War's later transformations and weapon-granted powers representing abilities that require their own evidence.[2][5][7][15]
