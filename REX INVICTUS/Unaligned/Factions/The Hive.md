### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 1083
The Hive is the designation given by the [[Elderblood Empire]] to an extraterrestrial parasitic hive-mind species first encountered during the Empire's interstellar expansion. Rather than functioning as an ordinary civilization, the species operates through enormous reproductive Hives and innumerable specialized organisms connected to a distributed central consciousness. The first known Hive colony had completely overwhelmed a yellow-colored planet and numbered at least 1,025,654,564,879 hostile organisms by the time [[Clare Bloodhunter]] and her siblings arrived.[1]
Initial biological analysis revealed that the Hive was not native to the planet. It consumed enormous amounts of the world's [[Natural Energy]] to maintain its population, slowly destroying the planetary ecosystem and preventing the local Positive World Tree from developing properly. The species was consequently classified as a parasitic extraterrestrial life-form, with [[Akasha Elderblood]] immediately considering the possibility that it had originated from forces operating in the Higher Sectors.[1]
The Yellow Planet infestation was only the first discovered colony. Centuries of Imperial expansion eventually revealed genetically and energetically similar signatures on multiple worlds containing immature [[World Trees]]. The Empire concluded that the Hive represented part of a long-term colonization strategy employed by unknown Higher-Sector powers: low-level organisms or subordinate Heralds were inserted into weaker Sectors, attached themselves to developing World Trees and manipulated the natural evolution of entire planets until those worlds became suitable for conquest.[2]
After determining that the remaining Hive organisms possessed no useful information within their Souls, [[Victor Elderblood]] personally eliminated every detected Hive signature throughout his galaxy, declaring that parasites had no place within his territory.[3]

### Description
The Hive is not a conventional singular organism. The term describes both the colossal reproductive organism acting as the central structure of a colony and the innumerable beings produced by it. All members are linked through a shared hive consciousness, allowing information and biological responses to propagate throughout the colony.[1]
The central Hive encountered by Clare was a gigantic pulsating organic structure continuously generating new organisms even before the Elderblood forces openly attacked it. [[Kaleb Hunter]] initially questioned why it was wasting resources creating such enormous numbers of soldiers in the absence of visible enemies, but he and Conan concluded that the Hive had likely sensed [[Victor Elderblood]] when the Emperor previously observed the planet. Perceiving Victor himself as an existential threat, the Hive had apparently accelerated reproduction in preparation for the coming invasion.[1]
The interior of the central structure was filled with corrosive material and contained a smaller pulsating core. The reproductive Hive was capable of generating radically different biological castes rather than simply duplicating a single organism. Some soldiers possessed humanoid bodies composed almost entirely of acid, while stronger organisms generated during the battle possessed enough [[Natural Energy]] to be classified by Imperial scanners as High-Level Combat God threats.[1][4]
The species drastically alters the environment of worlds it occupies. The Hive prefers cold conditions and releases toxic gases into the atmosphere while consuming the planet's Natural Energy. This enormous Energy expenditure feeds continuous reproduction while simultaneously starving the developing World Tree and the larger planetary ecosystem.[1]

### Biology
The Hive's biology is constructed around survival through numbers, rapid reproduction and distributed consciousness rather than dependence upon a single irreplaceable individual. Destroying the visible central Hive does not necessarily eliminate the species because its primary consciousness can migrate into surviving members of the colony.[1]
This ability makes complete extermination considerably more difficult than killing an ordinary collective species. Imperial biological records supplied by Velnorah warned that hive organisms are especially troublesome because the "main" consciousness can transfer itself into another individual if the current central host is threatened or destroyed. Clare's strategy consequently required simultaneous suppression of the primary Hive and extermination of the surrounding population so that the mind would have nowhere left to escape.[1]
The Hive also possesses an extremely dangerous method of biological adaptation. When a pathogen begins successfully affecting the species, newly produced generations rapidly develop resistance to that threat. [[Laura Bloodhunter]] immediately identified this property as the greatest weakness in Kaleb's initial proposal to attack the infestation with a biological weapon: a simple virus would become useless once a resistant generation appeared, while an endlessly mutating virus could itself become uncontrollable.[1]
For this reason, Kaleb engineered a temporary virus intended only to force the central Hive into biological dormancy. Once injected into its core, the entire Hive redirected its biological resources toward combating the infection and temporarily ceased normal activity. Kaleb estimated the first dose would remain effective for approximately twenty-four terrestrial hours and that repeated variants could extend the suppression period to roughly seven days before the Hive became completely immune to every available version.[4]

### Relationship with World Trees
The Hive's planetary colonization strategy revolves around immature [[World Trees]]. Early examination of the Yellow Planet showed that the species was consuming such enormous quantities of [[Natural Energy]] that the local Positive World Tree could not complete its development.[1]
Later Imperial investigations revealed that this behavior was not an accident but part of a much larger method of conquest. Hive organisms were detected in several Solar Systems containing World Trees still in their infant stages. Rather than immediately destroying the Tree, the species establishes a parasitic or superficially symbiotic relationship with it, receiving nutrients and Natural Energy while occupying the role that would normally belong to the world's emerging sentient species.[2]
This artificially accelerates the world's spiritual development. Because the Hive functions as the planet's dominant sentient population, its activity eventually helps fulfill the conditions necessary for the corresponding Negative World Tree to appear. Once the positive and negative sides of the planetary World Tree system are established and the planet has matured into a valuable world, the territory becomes suitable for eventual conquest by the forces responsible for planting the Hive organisms there.[2]
The strategy is exceptionally slow by mortal standards and can require thousands of years. To the ancient and effectively immortal forces of the Higher Sectors, however, such waiting periods are negligible. The Hive therefore functions less like a conventional invading army and more like a biological seed planted centuries or millennia before the actual conquerors arrive.[2]

### History

#### Growing Imperial Domain
The [[Elderblood Empire]] first encountered the Hive while the seven Original Emperor's Soldiers were conducting independent planetary-conquest missions designed to expand the Empire and gain practical experience as Imperial Commanders.[1]
[[Clare Bloodhunter]] commanded the operation against the Hive-infested Yellow Planet. After studying the available intelligence, Clare declared that the planet would be conquered within two days and immediately focused upon the enemy's greatest advantage: its extraordinary reproductive capability.[1]
[[Kaleb Hunter]] suggested producing a virus capable of incapacitating the Hive, but [[Laura Bloodhunter]] warned that the species could simply create descendants resistant to whatever pathogen threatened it. Rather than attempting biological extermination, Kaleb redesigned the plan around temporarily incapacitating the central organism while the Imperial military eliminated every potential secondary host on the planet.[1]
This precision was necessary because destroying only the central body would not permanently kill the collective. According to Velnorah's biological records, the central consciousness of a hive-type species could simply transfer into another surviving member. Complete extermination therefore required the Empire to prevent the Hive from moving its consciousness into any surviving organism.[1]
Clare organized ten Imperial warships containing approximately 50,000 Emperor's Soldiers around the planet. Every soldier possessed plasma weaponry powerful enough to melt the alien organisms, while the ships positioned their orbital cannons around predetermined firing zones. Clare, Conan and Kaleb would personally infiltrate the main Hive, while Kiana and Caelus were assigned to lead the ground extermination once the central organism had been neutralized.[1]
Clare, Conan and Kaleb were then launched directly from orbit. Upon entering the atmosphere, Clare's combat system detected exactly 1,025,654,564,879 hostile presences. Despite the astronomical population, the Imperial AI evaluated completion of the mission as only moderately difficult for the forces deployed.[1]
The three Commanders penetrated the Hive's territory using Dragon Fire. Clare encountered numerous humanoid organisms composed of pure acid, but the creatures were extremely vulnerable to her flames and were vaporized before the acid could meaningfully harm her. Inside the enormous reproductive structure, she discovered a smaller pulsating core and extracted biological samples using the artificial manipulators built into her armor rather than touching the unknown organism directly.[1]
Akasha immediately analyzed the samples. She confirmed the species' enormous reproductive capacity, consumption of planetary [[Natural Energy]], preference for cold environments, atmospheric toxic-gas production and severe vulnerability to Fire and extreme temperatures. More importantly, the analysis strongly suggested that the species had originated outside the planet and might have been inserted from the Higher Sectors. Akasha immediately classified the information as an Imperial G-7 secret because of the potential involvement of hostile extra-Sector civilizations.[1]
During the sample extraction, the Hive responded to the Imperial intrusion by producing five dramatically stronger biological combat units. Imperial scanners detected enormous concentrations of Natural Energy within them and classified each as approximately comparable to a High-Level Combat God. Clare immediately killed four with a concentrated Dragon Fire breath while Conan eliminated the fifth.[4]
Kaleb completed the anti-Hive pathogen shortly afterward. Clare, Conan and Kaleb entered the organism's core and injected the virus directly into it. The effect was immediate: the Hive screamed across the entire planet, ceased pulsating and redirected essentially all of its biological activity toward resisting the infection.[4]
With the central Hive incapacitated, Clare ordered the Imperial fleet to bathe the planet in Imperial Fire. Ten ships bombarded the surface continuously for approximately five minutes, transforming the planetary surface into a burning landscape and killing nearly every exposed organism.[4]
Despite the bombardment, more than 50 million organisms survived underground. Kiana and Caelus consequently began the second phase of the extermination operation, deploying approximately 18,000 Emperor's Soldiers directly from orbit. The soldiers separated into squads and systematically hunted the remaining organisms beneath the burning surface while the Hive remained incapable of transferring its central consciousness safely.[4]
The campaign successfully destroyed the known Yellow Planet infestation and secured the local World Tree from further predation. The biological data recovered during the mission, however, ultimately proved more important than the planet itself.

#### Higher-Sector Infiltration
Long after the original infestation had been eliminated, [[Akasha Elderblood]] continued analyzing the signatures discovered by Clare's expedition. The Empire placed surveillance systems throughout the Solar Systems encountered during expansion, particularly around planets containing immature [[World Trees]].[2]
This surveillance discovered organisms possessing biological signatures similar to the original Hive across multiple different Solar Systems. The pattern immediately proved that the Yellow Planet had not hosted an isolated mutation or naturally evolved species.[2]
More detailed observation revealed the actual colonization method. Hive organisms deliberately attached themselves to planets whose World Trees remained in infant stages, exploited those Trees for nutrients and operated as artificial sentient populations. By accelerating the conditions required for the emergence of a Negative World Tree, the parasites gradually transformed immature worlds into fully developed planetary territories suitable for conquest.[2]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Akasha concluded that such a strategy only made sense for extraordinarily long-lived civilizations. Spending thousands of years preparing a world would be completely impractical for mortal empires, but Higher-Sector beings possessed both the lifespan and resources to regard such preparations as insignificant investments.[2]
The discovery also helped Victor understand the larger political structure beyond his Sector. Powerful beings could not simply descend personally into lower regions without violating the System's Sector restrictions. Instead, Higher-Ranked entities could send weaker servants, Heralds, parasites or subordinate civilizations capable of operating beneath those restrictions and gradually expanding their master's influence.[2][3]
Victor theorized that what initially looked like countless unrelated invasions might actually represent only three or four enormous Higher-Sector factions controlling thousands of worlds through subordinate forces. The Hive was therefore potentially one small component of a vastly larger interstellar expansion system rather than an independent galactic civilization.[3]

#### Eradication
After reviewing every planet displaying the foreign biological signature, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] personally examined the organisms in an attempt to obtain information regarding whoever had sent them. The effort failed because their Souls contained no useful intelligence concerning their masters or mission beyond what the Empire had already deduced.[3]
Victor consequently saw no reason to preserve them. While looking across the affected worlds, his eyes briefly shone crimson-violet. Akasha's planetary scanners immediately began reporting that the foreign signatures were dying simultaneously.[3]
Victor dismissed the organisms as parasites unworthy of remaining within his galaxy and erased the detected infestation across the territory under his influence. Unlike the first Yellow Planet campaign—which required Imperial soldiers, orbital bombardment, a specially engineered virus and systematic extermination—Victor's later action removed the remaining detected Hive populations remotely and essentially instantaneously.[3]
This galactic purge marks the destruction of the known Hive presence inside Victor's territory. However, because the species was strongly suspected of originating from Higher-Sector powers and similar infiltration methods later appeared outside Victor's home galaxy, it should not automatically be assumed that Victor exterminated every member of the species throughout the entire Universe.[2][3]

#### Legacy
The Hive discovery became one of the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s earliest concrete indications that civilizations from the Higher Sectors were already interfering with worlds far below them. The encounter directly influenced [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s preparations for the future conflict that would begin once his Sector's protective barriers disappeared.[2][3]
Similar evidence appeared again during Imperial operations in the Andromeda Galaxy. There, the Empire discovered the [[Skaarith Empire]], whose rulers possessed Energy and physiological characteristics resembling the earlier Hive influence. Unlike the Yellow Planet, where the Hive had apparently consumed the entire civilization, the Skaarith operation suggested a more subtle infiltration model in which the ruling class acted as outside agents while the native population was genetically altered and infected to accelerate development through continuous warfare.[5]
The Skaarith discovery reinforced the Empire's conclusion that the Hive was part of a broader pattern of Upper-Sector powers manipulating weaker civilizations through biological proxies, engineered conflict and World Tree exploitation rather than always invading directly.[5]

### Skills & Abilities
Hive-Mind Physiology: The Hive is a distributed collective organism composed of enormous numbers of biologically distinct units connected to a shared central consciousness. Individual organisms function as components of the larger entity rather than completely independent beings.[1]
- Hive Mind: All Hive organisms are connected through a shared mental network, allowing the collective to coordinate its enormous population as parts of a single greater organism.[1]
- Distributed Consciousness: The true "main" mind is not permanently restricted to the gigantic central Hive. If necessary, the consciousness can migrate into another surviving member of the species.[1]
- Consciousness Transfer: The primary mind can transfer itself into another organism if its current central host is threatened, making the species exceptionally difficult to permanently eradicate while even one viable host remains.[1]
- Information Sharing: Members of the colony function through the same collective network, enabling discoveries and threats encountered by portions of the Hive to contribute to the responses of the whole.
- Planetary Coordination: A single central Hive can coordinate populations numbering more than one trillion individuals across an entire planet.[1]
Extreme Reproduction: The species possesses an extraordinary reproductive capacity. The primary Hive continuously generates additional organisms and can increase production massively when confronted with an existential threat.[1]
- Mass Production: The Yellow Planet infestation contained at least 1,025,654,564,879 active hostile organisms during Clare's arrival.[1]
- Threat-Responsive Reproduction: The central Hive accelerated production after apparently perceiving [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s observation of the planet as a danger to its existence.[1]
- Biological Resource Conversion: The Hive converts absorbed planetary [[Natural Energy]] into the resources necessary to sustain and continuously expand its enormous population.[1]
Reproductive Adaptation: New generations can rapidly develop biological resistance to previously effective threats. This adaptation occurs quickly enough that the Imperial Commanders considered conventional biological warfare incapable of permanently eliminating the species.[1]
- Pathogen Resistance: Once exposed to an effective disease, the Hive can create descendants resistant to that pathogen.[1]
- Rapid Immunity Development: Kaleb calculated that his anti-Hive virus would function effectively for approximately one day and that repeated variants would become progressively weaker. After roughly seven days, the Hive would become completely immune to every available variant.[4]
- Generational Counter-Evolution: The species does not necessarily require existing organisms to individually evolve against a danger; it can instead produce a new generation already adapted to the problem.[1]
Biological Specialization: The Hive can manufacture multiple biological castes possessing radically different structures and combat capabilities rather than merely reproducing identical drones.[1][4]
- Acidic Life-Forms: Some Hive soldiers possess humanoid bodies composed almost entirely of highly corrosive acid.[1]
- Acid Generation: The central structure itself contains enough corrosive material to completely cover an intruder entering its interior.[1]
- God-Level Combat Organisms: When threatened by Clare, Conan and Kaleb, the Hive produced five specialized organisms containing enough Natural Energy to receive a High-Level Combat God threat classification from Imperial scanners.[4]
- Variable Morphology: Hive organisms are not restricted to a single body plan; demonstrated units include humanoid acidic creatures, enormous slimy biological combatants and the massive reproductive central Hive.[1][4]
Natural Energy Absorption: The Hive feeds directly upon the Natural Energy of occupied planets. This Energy supports its reproduction and enormous biological infrastructure.[1]
- Planetary Energy Drain: Sustaining a sufficiently large Hive can consume enough Natural Energy to gradually damage the entire planet.[1]
- World Tree Drain: The Yellow Planet infestation consumed enough Energy to prevent its Positive World Tree from fully developing.[1]
- World Tree Parasitism: Other populations were discovered attaching themselves to infant [[World Trees]] and obtaining nutrients from them as part of a long-term planetary colonization strategy.[2]
Artificial Ecosystem Manipulation: By becoming the dominant sentient population surrounding an immature World Tree, Hive colonies can manipulate a planet's natural developmental cycle.[2]
- Accelerated Negative World Tree Formation: Their existence as an artificial sentient population helps accelerate the conditions required for the emergence of the planet's Negative World Tree.[2]
- Planetary Preparation: Once both sides of the World Tree system have developed, the world becomes a more valuable and structurally complete target for eventual Higher-Sector conquest.[2]
Atmospheric Contamination: The central Hive releases toxic gases into the atmosphere of occupied worlds, altering planetary environmental conditions in favor of the species.[1]
- Toxic Gas Generation: The Hive continuously emits poisonous atmospheric substances.[1]
- Environmental Alteration: Large infestations can gradually transform the surrounding planetary environment through toxic emissions and Natural Energy depletion.[1]
Extreme Population Survivability: The enormous size and underground distribution of a mature Hive colony make complete extermination extremely difficult. Even after five continuous minutes of planetary orbital bombardment had burned virtually the entire surface of the Yellow Planet, more than 50 million organisms remained alive underground.[4]
Possible Long-Range Threat Perception: The Yellow Planet Hive dramatically accelerated reproduction after Victor remotely observed the planet. Kaleb theorized that the organism had sensed Victor's presence and recognized him as a threat. This is strongly implied but not explained sufficiently to determine the exact mechanism involved.[1]

### Weaknesses
- Fire: Biological analysis explicitly identified Fire as one of the Hive's greatest natural weaknesses. Dragon Fire rapidly vaporized the acidic organisms and destroyed specialized units almost instantly.[1][4]
- High Temperatures: The species strongly prefers cold environments and is unusually vulnerable to extreme heat.[1]
- Temporary Biological Suppression: Although pathogens cannot permanently defeat the Hive because of its adaptation, a sufficiently specialized virus can temporarily incapacitate the central organism and force all of its resources toward resisting infection.[4]
- Dependence on Surviving Hosts: Consciousness Transfer protects the Hive only while viable organisms remain. Simultaneously incapacitating the central Hive and exterminating every available host prevents the primary mind from escaping.[1]
- Resource Dependence: Its extreme reproductive strategy requires immense amounts of [[Natural Energy]]. The Hive consequently depends upon suitable planets and [[World Trees]] to maintain large populations.[1][2]
- Inferior Individual Units: The overwhelming majority of its organisms were individually weak enough to be destroyed easily by Emperor's Soldiers and Dragon Fire. Its true danger came from numbers, adaptation and collective survival rather than the strength of ordinary drones.[1][4]
