The Elderblood Bloodline is the personal supernatural bloodline originating from Victor Elderblood and inherited by his descendants. It represents the hereditary continuation of Victor's existence, combining the exceptional properties of his original [[Walker Bloodline]], his evolution as a Vampire Progenitor, his development into the Blood Dragon Primogenitor, and the increasingly extraordinary Draconic, Divine and Eldritch characteristics incorporated into his existence. The bloodline eventually became one of the defining hereditary possessions of the [[Elderblood Imperial Family]], distinguished by its capacity to strengthen existing bloodlines, assimilate supernatural characteristics, preserve inherited abilities and transmit aspects of Victor's existence to subsequent generations.[1][2][3][4]
Unlike the [[Snow Bloodline]], [[Scarlett Bloodline]] or [[Fulger Bloodline]], which are associated with particular hereditary abilities, the Elderblood Bloodline is not defined by one elemental power. Its most important characteristic is the unusually dominant and assimilative nature of Victor's inheritance. His blood can strengthen pre-existing ancestry rather than simply replacing it, while his biological descendants may inherit supernatural characteristics from both parents without suffering the incompatibilities ordinarily associated with combining different species. This became increasingly pronounced following Victor's evolution into a True Dragon, whose physiology possesses exceptional compatibility with supernatural systems and whose inherited predatory characteristics can assimilate other powers into its own foundation.[2][5][6]
The bloodline also extends beyond conventional biological inheritance. Victor's earlier Progenitor Blood could incorporate selected individuals into his personal supernatural lineage, changing their ancestry and strengthening their inherited characteristics. Such transformations were demonstrated long before the Elderblood surname became the designation of the Imperial Dynasty. However, membership in Victor's supernatural lineage, biological descent from Victor, possession of True Dragon physiology and membership in the Elderblood family are related but distinct classifications. A person bearing the Elderblood surname does not necessarily possess every hereditary characteristic of Victor's direct biological descendants.[1][3][4]
The distinction became politically significant during the Imperial era. Victor deliberately restricted the distribution of his Blood because the Elderblood Bloodline and the True Dragon race represented the sovereignty, identity and hereditary authority of the Imperial Family. Although he possessed the power to create additional Dragons and strengthen other species, he did not intend to distribute the highest inheritance of his Dynasty indiscriminately throughout the Empire.[4]

### Nature

#### A Predatory and Assimilative Bloodline
The Elderblood Bloodline is fundamentally characterized by the unusual dominance of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s inherited supernatural characteristics. This dominance became apparent long before his eventual evolution into a Dragon God. Following his transformation from a Human into a Vampire, Victor possessed the hereditary powers of three separate Noble Vampire Clans despite the fact that transformed Humans ordinarily did not inherit Noble Vampire abilities in this manner. His Blood also contained the characteristics of [[Sanguis Noctis Regis]], the exceptionally rare blood associated with Vampire Progenitors. The interaction between Victor's original physiology and the abnormal marriage ritual involving [[Violet Elderblood Snow]], [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] and [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] produced an existence that violated the conventional limitations of Vampire inheritance.[7][8][9]
Victor's ability to incorporate different bloodlines eventually developed into a means of modifying other individuals. Unlike an ordinary Progenitor who transmits selected characteristics to descendants, Victor could actively strengthen existing ancestral traits, including those that had remained dormant or comparatively insignificant for generations. His blood did not necessarily erase the recipient's previous identity or inherited powers. Instead, those traits could become more pronounced and develop alongside the characteristics introduced through Victor's lineage.[2][5]
This tendency reached a much more advanced state following Victor's Draconic evolution. As the Primogenitor of the [[Blood Dragons]], his predatory inheritance was supported by True Dragon physiology, which possessed extraordinary compatibility with supernatural powers. Chapter 1168 clarified that Victor's blood carried an unusually aggressive tendency to devour, incorporate and assimilate other supernatural characteristics rather than allowing incompatible inherited traits to remain separate and weaken the resulting individual.[10][6]
Consequently, the descendants of Victor's mature Draconic lineage can inherit characteristics from their mothers while retaining their fundamental True Dragon existence. Maternal traits are not necessarily discarded, but may instead become part of the descendant's Draconic biology, Divine abilities or individual supernatural development. This process helps explain why Victor's daughters possess extraordinarily different powers while remaining members of the same paternal lineage.[11][12][6]

#### Biological and Metaphysical Inheritance
Ordinary supernatural bloodlines usually transmit traits through ancestry, with the resulting inheritance influenced by the individual's parents and racial characteristics. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s lineage operates at a considerably deeper level because his blood and Soul became increasingly interconnected throughout his evolution.
During his Progenitor era, Victor demonstrated the ability to modify the ancestry of existing Vampires. These changes affected powers that were already part of the recipient's bloodline, including secondary and previously dormant traits. The transformation of [[Morgana Elderblood]] additionally demonstrated that his Blood could influence the relationship between supernatural racial characteristics and the individual's Soul Core.[2][5][13][3]
After Victor became the Dragon God of [[Chaos]], his offspring could also inherit characteristics derived from his Divine and Eldritch nature. These traits are not limited to conventional physical heredity. Some descendants were born possessing exceptional relationships with Concepts, Divine Authorities or powers originating from the Eldritch side of Victor's existence. The development of [[Nero Elderblood]] demonstrated that his foreign Eldritch characteristics could be transmitted to descendants, allowing their Divine powers to develop differently from those belonging to ordinary native Gods.[14][15]
Nevertheless, Conceptual inheritance is not identical to hereditary transmission of an ordinary supernatural ability. A descendant may inherit an affinity for a Concept, possess a portion of a parent's conceptual characteristics, or develop a new conceptual manifestation through the interaction of both parents. Such inheritance does not mean that the child automatically occupies the same cosmic position or exercises identical authority to the original bearer.[12][6]

#### Inheritance and Individuality
Although [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s bloodline is extraordinarily dominant, it does not produce identical descendants. His children inherit characteristics from him while retaining influences originating from their mothers, and their individual powers can develop in substantially different directions.
Some daughters closely resemble their mothers physically while possessing Victor's eyes and Draconic characteristics. Others display combinations of both parents' physical features, while certain descendants inherit unique Divine or Eldritch powers whose manifestations differ from anything directly demonstrated by either parent.[11][16][17]
Therefore, the Elderblood Bloodline is not a standardized set of abilities copied into every descendant. It is an exceptionally powerful hereditary foundation capable of producing different expressions according to the inherited traits, racial characteristics, Divine potential and individual development of its members.

### Origin and Development

#### The Walker Bloodline
The earliest known hereditary foundation of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s eventual Elderblood lineage is the [[Walker Bloodline]], his original Human ancestry. Long before becoming a Vampire, Victor possessed a rare blood type designated RH-null, also known as Golden Blood. His condition was accompanied by severe anemia and chronic physical weakness, preventing him from performing ordinary physical activities without risking serious medical complications.[18][19]
The deeper significance of his Human ancestry was revealed much later. In Chapter 1152, Victor explained that his original bloodline had accumulated potential through successive generations. Rather than passing down one conventional supernatural ability, the lineage progressively improved the potential available to its descendants. Over many generations, this accumulation eventually culminated in the abnormal individual who became Victor Walker.[20]
Victor reached this conclusion after investigating why his biological daughters possessed such extraordinary potential. Although their talents could partly be explained through their True Dragon physiology and Divine inheritance, their consistently abnormal development suggested another contributing factor. Research undertaken with Ruby and Aline revealed the unusual accumulation of potential within Victor's original Human ancestry.[20]
The investigation also introduced [[Veylan Valentin Walker]], an ancestor who had lived during the eighteenth century. [[Shiva]] attempted to exploit the Walker lineage by obtaining a reconstructed version of this ancestor through a transaction involving Primordial powers. However, the experiment failed to reproduce Victor's full hereditary circumstances because the reconstructed ancestor did not possess the countless subsequent generations of accumulated development that had eventually culminated in Victor.[20]
The Walker Bloodline therefore represents an important ancestral foundation of Victor's existence. Nevertheless, the novel does not explicitly state that the Walker Bloodline was renamed the Elderblood Bloodline or that its original hereditary identity ceased to exist. The two remain distinguishable: Walker refers to Victor's original Human ancestry, while Elderblood denotes the supernatural lineage associated with his evolved existence and Imperial descendants.

#### The Abnormal Vampire Transformation
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s transformation into a Vampire introduced three Noble Vampire bloodlines into his existing Human physiology. Under ordinary circumstances, a Human transformed into a Vampire would become a Plebeian Vampire rather than inheriting the hereditary abilities reserved for naturally born Noble Vampires. Victor instead inherited the [[Snow Clan]]'s Fire, the [[Scarlett Clan]]'s Ice and the [[Fulger Clan]]'s Lightning through an abnormal marriage ritual involving three Noble Vampire heiresses.[7][8][9]
This abnormal inheritance demonstrated that Victor's physiology could accommodate supernatural characteristics that conventional Vampire biology would not ordinarily permit. The presence of [[Sanguis Noctis Regis]] further complicated his existence, eventually contributing to his recognition as the Second Vampire Progenitor.[21]
His development should not be interpreted as the immediate creation of the modern Elderblood Bloodline. At this stage, Victor was still known as Victor Walker and later Victor Alucard, while his developing supernatural traits were associated with his abnormal transformation and Progenitor potential.

#### The First Alucard Lineage
Following his recognition as a Vampire Progenitor, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] acquired the ability to establish a direct Vampire lineage beneath himself. This was the earliest practical manifestation of his power to pass supernatural characteristics to individuals belonging to his Clan.
Eve Alucard became one of the first clear demonstrations of this ability. In Chapter 260, Eve manifested a distinctive form of black Fire originating from the Fire-related power she inherited through Victor. The narrative explained that when a Progenitor created a Vampire, the creator could choose to pass a characteristic to the newly created descendant.[22]
Eve's flames were visibly different from Victor's ordinary Fire, reflecting her own nature rather than reproducing her creator's power in an identical form. Her development established that membership in Victor's direct Vampire lineage could involve the inheritance of one of his abilities and that the resulting manifestation could differ between individuals.[22]
The significance of Eve's transformation was both hereditary and political. Until then, Clan Alucard had been a newly founded Count Clan centered primarily upon Victor himself. Eve's inheritance demonstrated that the Clan could develop into an actual supernatural lineage whose members possessed characteristics originating from its founder.

#### The Transformation of the Battle Maids
A major advancement in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s understanding of his own lineage occurred when he began incorporating his Battle Maids into his Clan more completely. [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]], [[Bruna Elderblood]], [[Roberta Elderblood]], [[Maria Elderblood]] and Eve had repeatedly demonstrated their loyalty and were offered permanent places within Victor's personal family and supernatural lineage.[23][24]
Using his Progenitor Blood, Victor deepened their relationship with his lineage and increased their potential. Unlike a conventional Vampire conversion, the process could alter existing ancestry and strengthen traits that the recipient already possessed.
Kaguya became a particularly important example. As a member of Clan Blank, she possessed inherited Shadow abilities, but her ancestry also contained characteristics associated with Clan Snow. Victor's Blood strengthened this previously secondary inheritance sufficiently for Kaguya to manifest the [[Snow Clan]]'s Fire while retaining the Shadow abilities of Clan Blank.[1][2]
This development provided the basis for the more extensive ancestry modifications Victor would perform upon his wives.

#### Strengthening the Noble Vampire Bloodlines
Chapters 634–636 established some of the clearest demonstrated properties of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Progenitor lineage. During a series of transformations, Victor deliberately manipulated the Blood already present within several members of his family and used it to modify their ancestry.
The recipients included Violet, Agnes, Sasha, Natashia, Ruby, Jeanne and Morgana. Their transformations were not identical because each possessed a different supernatural foundation.[2][5][13]
Violet and Agnes
The [[Snow Clan]]'s Fire became considerably more powerful during their transformation. Their bodies released enormous quantities of Fire that required Victor and [[Scathach]] to establish protective Ice barriers around the training area. The process also refined their existing physical characteristics and strengthened their connection to Victor's lineage.[2]
Violet experienced an especially important change when the precognitive characteristics inherited through [[Adonis Snow]] awakened more completely. Her Eyes began displaying a violet glow associated with Foresight, prompting Victor to intervene before she became overly absorbed in a vision of the future. The development showed that Victor's Blood could strengthen ancestral qualities that were not part of the recipient's primary elemental inheritance.[2]
Sasha and Natashia
The Fulger family's Lightning became significantly stronger after Victor manipulated their ancestry. Both women experienced increased power output, while additional inherited qualities associated with their familial ancestry became more pronounced.[5]
Sasha's physical resilience improved considerably. Victor and Scathach demonstrated the change by testing how her body responded to a physical attack that would ordinarily have displaced or injured a Vampire of her previous level. The transformation also strengthened the abilities associated with Sasha's paternal ancestry, including the heightened perception of Time that allowed its inheritors to observe movement and events in far greater detail.[5]
Natashia likewise experienced improved perception and substantially greater Lightning output. These changes demonstrated that Victor's Blood could enhance existing abilities without simply replacing the individual's original bloodline.
Ruby, Jeanne and Morgana
Ruby, Jeanne and Morgana underwent further transformations as Victor incorporated them into his lineage. Ruby's inherited abilities were strengthened, while Jeanne's already extraordinary supernatural foundation became more closely connected to Victor's Blood.[13]
Morgana experienced the most unusual transformation because her existence already contained both Demonic and Vampiric characteristics. Rather than merely increasing their strength independently, Victor's Blood caused a fundamental change in the relationship between the two racial components.[13][3]

#### Creation of the Demonic Vampire Subspecies
Morgana's transformation demonstrated that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Progenitor Blood could assimilate racial characteristics as well as strengthen ordinary hereditary abilities.
Following her transformation, Victor initially believed that Morgana had become a completely new race because her Demonic and Vampiric traits had merged into one existence. Her Soul had changed significantly, and her body displayed an integrated combination of previously separate supernatural properties.[13]
However, Chapter 637 clarified the correct classification. Rather than becoming the independent Progenitor of a new race, Morgana had become a Demonic Vampire, a subspecies of Noble Vampire. Her Vampiric nature had assimilated her Demonic characteristics, incorporating those traits into one unified supernatural foundation.[3]
The distinction was important because an ordinary Hybrid possesses characteristics originating from different races, which may coexist without completely integrating. Morgana's transformation instead caused her Vampire side to incorporate the properties of her Demon side without leaving them as two incompatible components. Her individual Soul Core remained unified throughout the process.[3]
This demonstrated one of the most important properties of Victor's bloodline: Racial Assimilation. Under suitable circumstances, his lineage could incorporate the characteristics of another species into an existing supernatural foundation, preserving useful traits while producing an evolved form of the original race.

#### The Blood Dragon Primogenitor
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s evolution during the conflict surrounding [[Olympus]] fundamentally changed the racial foundation supporting his lineage.
After his body underwent repeated destruction and regeneration during combat, his developing Dragon characteristics became fully integrated into his physiology. His previous Vampire Progenitor existence fused with his Draconic nature, eventually producing the supernatural race recognized as the [[Blood Dragons]]. Victor became the Blood Dragon Primogenitor, the origin of this new racial lineage.[25][10]
This evolution should not be confused with the original creation of the Elderblood Bloodline. A Blood Dragon is a racial classification, while the Elderblood Bloodline represents Victor's hereditary lineage. However, the two became increasingly interconnected because Victor himself was the origin of both.
The change was especially significant for his future descendants. The properties of his Blood were no longer supported exclusively by Vampire Progenitor physiology, but by a fundamentally Draconic existence possessing far greater potential for racial and supernatural integration.

#### True Dragonhood and Divine Evolution
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ascension as the Dragon God of [[Chaos]], he began developing the ability to transform selected members of his family into genuine Dragons. His influence could reconstruct existing racial characteristics while preserving important powers and aspects of the recipient's previous existence.[26][27]
The transformations demonstrated that Victor's Draconic lineage could absorb and elevate a wide variety of supernatural foundations. The resulting Dragons were not interchangeable copies of their Progenitor. Members of the Fulger family retained their Lightning characteristics, while other recipients preserved distinct combinations of elemental, spiritual, magical, racial and Divine powers.[28]
These transformations provided an important precursor to the inheritance displayed by Victor's later biological children. Instead of producing descendants whose racial characteristics weakened one another, the True Dragon foundation could incorporate supernatural qualities into a more unified existence.
The novel nevertheless distinguishes transformation through Victor's power from biological inheritance through Victor's bloodline. Both can establish direct relationships with his lineage, but they do not necessarily follow identical rules.

#### The Second Elderblood Generation
The birth of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s biological children demonstrated the most extensive development of his hereditary lineage.
By Chapter 987, Victor's descendants included daughters born from mothers belonging to radically different supernatural races and possessing different Divine, elemental and hereditary characteristics. Nevertheless, the children consistently displayed characteristics originating from Victor, combined with powers and traits inherited from their mothers.[11]
Some daughters inherited familiar powers in exceptionally strengthened forms, while others displayed entirely new abilities created through the interaction of paternal and maternal inheritance. Their development established that the Elderblood lineage was not restricted to the reproduction of existing abilities. It could provide the foundation for supernatural characteristics that had not previously existed in precisely the same form.[11][29][30]
The extraordinary variety of the second generation eventually became one of the defining characteristics of the [[Elderblood Imperial Family]]. Its members possessed a shared hereditary origin while developing widely different powers, affinities and specialized abilities.

#### Eldritch Inheritance
An additional development occurred when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters began awakening powers associated with his Eldritch existence.
[[Nero Elderblood]] demonstrated that the inherited characteristics of Victor's descendants extended beyond ordinary Draconic physiology and native Divinity. Her developing power over Apocalypse possessed Eldritch characteristics because it originated through Victor's existence rather than following only the conventional structures governing Gods within his Cosmos.[31][14][15][32]
Victor's Eldritch development allowed supernatural characteristics foreign to the ordinary cosmic framework to become part of his descendants' inheritance. Such characteristics could alter the way Divine powers developed and interacted with the established Rules of Existence.
However, the degree of Eldritch inheritance differs between individuals. The presence of Eldritch characteristics within the family does not establish that every descendant manifests the same Eldritch powers or the complete abilities of an independent Outer God.

#### The Imperial Era
The Elderblood Bloodline eventually became a matter of Imperial sovereignty.
By Chapter 1164, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] deliberately limited the distribution of his Blood and the creation of additional [[True Dragons]]. Although the [[Elderblood Empire]] possessed the scientific, magical and Divine capabilities to improve the biology of ordinary citizens on an enormous scale, the Imperial Family's most exceptional hereditary characteristics remained restricted.[4]
The distinction was not simply a practical limitation upon resources. Victor could create or transform supernatural beings on a scale far beyond the capabilities of ordinary Progenitors, yet he considered the Elderblood Bloodline and True Dragon race part of the identity and sovereignty of his Dynasty.[4]
The later discussion of Dragon inheritance also established that membership in the extended Imperial Family did not automatically entitle an individual to transformation into one of Victor's Dragon lineages. His wives considered the privilege something that needed to be earned rather than a benefit granted solely through distant blood relations or family connections.[33]

### Hereditary Properties

#### Bloodline Manipulation
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] can alter the ancestry of individuals incorporated into his supernatural lineage. This power was demonstrated during the transformation of his Battle Maids and later used deliberately upon his wives. Rather than merely creating an ordinary Vampire, Victor could manipulate the blood already present within the recipient and change how its inherited supernatural characteristics developed.[1][2]

#### Bloodline Empowerment
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood can increase the potential and potency of existing bloodlines. [[Scathach]] explicitly recognized this property after observing Kaguya manifest the [[Snow Clan]]'s Fire despite previously relying primarily upon the Shadow abilities of Clan Blank.[2]
The same principle occurred during the transformations of Violet, Agnes, Sasha and Natashia. Their inherited abilities became significantly stronger without requiring them to abandon their original ancestral characteristics.[2][5]

#### Dormant Power Activation
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood can strengthen hereditary traits that are dormant, weak or overshadowed by more dominant characteristics.
Kaguya's Snow inheritance became sufficiently powerful for her to use Fire alongside Shadows, while Violet's precognitive abilities developed more completely after her ancestry was modified.[2]
This property is particularly significant for individuals possessing multiple hereditary backgrounds because it allows characteristics that would ordinarily remain secondary to become useful supernatural abilities.

#### Power Inheritance
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] can transmit selected supernatural characteristics through his Progenitor lineage. Eve Alucard demonstrated this ability when she inherited a Fire-related characteristic and developed her own black flames.[22]
The power inherited by a descendant does not necessarily possess precisely the same manifestation as the original ability. Eve's Fire reflected her individual nature despite originating from Victor.

#### Racial Trait Assimilation
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s bloodline can assimilate supernatural characteristics originating from another race into an existing racial foundation. Morgana's transformation into a Demonic Vampire demonstrated this process when her Vampiric nature incorporated her Demonic traits, producing a unified Vampire subspecies rather than an ordinary Hybrid.[13][3]
The property later became especially important in Victor's True Dragon descendants, whose dominant Draconic foundation could incorporate characteristics inherited through their mothers.[6]

#### Bloodline Integration
Multiple hereditary characteristics can coexist within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s lineage without necessarily replacing one another. This was demonstrated in Kaguya's strengthened Snow and Blank inheritance, as well as the enhancements experienced by Sasha through her Fulger and paternal ancestral characteristics.[2][5]
Among Victor's biological descendants, the process operates on a much larger scale because maternal characteristics may be incorporated directly into the child's True Dragon physiology and supernatural development.[6]

#### Hybridization and Racial Stabilization
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s lineage possesses an unusual capacity to produce stable combinations of supernatural characteristics. This includes the assimilation of racial powers into one coherent existence, as demonstrated by Morgana, and the inheritance of maternal supernatural traits by his Draconic children.[3][11]
However, not every unusual species created through Victor's powers should automatically be classified as a biological Hybrid. Morgana's Demonic Vampire transformation was explicitly classified as the creation of a Vampire subspecies, while Victor's mature True Dragon offspring retain a dominant Draconic racial foundation.

#### Enhanced Hereditary Potential
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s descendants frequently possess exceptional potential from birth. This development is partly associated with their Draconic physiology, Divine inheritance and the unusual hereditary qualities contributed by Victor.[11][20]
The [[Walker Bloodline]]'s accumulated potential provides additional context for this development. Nevertheless, the novel does not establish a numerical multiplier by which every Elderblood descendant is guaranteed to exceed the potential of the previous generation.

#### Conceptual Inheritance
Certain Elderblood descendants inherit aspects of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s conceptual and Divine characteristics rather than only conventional supernatural powers.
[[Nigredo Elderblood]], daughter of Victor and [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]], inherited a relationship with Victor's Concept of Darkness. Her resulting Divinity developed into an Eldritch manifestation of Darkness associated with black holes and the relationship between Being and Non-Being.[34]
[[Kassandra Kashvi Elderblood]], daughter of Victor and [[Kali Elderblood]], was born as an incarnation of Destruction. Her Draconic nature assimilated the Concept into her biology, allowing her powers and Dragon Language to express Destruction directly. She additionally inherited Victor's Eyes and traces of his relationship with Negativity.[12]
These examples demonstrate that conceptual inheritance can produce descendants whose powers differ substantially from the original parental manifestations.

#### Eldritch Trait Inheritance
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Eldritch characteristics can pass to his descendants and influence their supernatural development. [[Nero]]'s awakening of an Eldritch form of Apocalypse provided an early demonstration of this property.[14][15][32]
The existence of descendants such as Nigredo and [[Yog-Sothoth]] Elderblood further demonstrates the diversity of Eldritch characteristics present within the Imperial Family, although Yog's inheritance also directly involves the extraordinary nature of her mother, [[Azathoth Elderblood]].[35][36][34]

#### Draconic Trait Inheritance
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s mature biological descendants inherit True Dragon characteristics rather than merely reproducing the ordinary racial state of the other parent.
Chapter 1168 explained that his dominant Draconic lineage assimilates compatible maternal traits while preserving the child's True Dragon foundation. The extraordinary compatibility of True Dragon physiology allows the offspring to incorporate supernatural characteristics that would ordinarily create difficulties for conventional Hybrids.[6]
This inheritance includes Draconic biological characteristics and the potential for the descendants to develop powers associated with Dragon Energy, Dragon Language and other aspects of their racial existence. However, individual mastery still varies between descendants.

#### Hereditary Perception
One of the clearest universally demonstrated hereditary characteristics of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s biological daughters is their violet Dragon Eyes and the associated ability to perceive deeper aspects of Reality.
Chapter 1146 explicitly explained that all of Victor's daughters inherit some form of this perception. Its strength differs between individuals. Certain daughters possess exceptionally powerful manifestations from birth, while others begin with weaker expressions and must develop the ability through training.[16]
The eyes therefore provide an example of a hereditary characteristic shared across the second generation without requiring every descendant to possess an identical level of power or perception.

#### Individualized Power Development
The Elderblood lineage permits substantial individual variation despite its dominant hereditary foundation.
The powers inherited through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] may combine with maternal abilities to produce manifestations unlike either parent's original powers. This is especially apparent among daughters born with unique Divinities and extraordinary combinations of Draconic, Divine and Eldritch characteristics.[11][12][17]
Consequently, the bloodline does not predetermine every power a descendant will eventually possess. Training, experience, personal understanding and the individual's development remain important factors.

### Inheritance Mechanics

#### Paternal and Maternal Contributions
The hereditary characteristics of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s descendants originate from the interaction between his bloodline and the inherited properties of the other parent.
Victor contributes the dominant foundation associated with his mature Draconic lineage, alongside other characteristics belonging to his supernatural existence. The mother contributes her own biological, racial, Divine and hereditary traits. The resulting descendant may therefore possess an integrated combination rather than an unstable mixture of separate racial components.[11][6]
The exact manifestation depends upon the inherited qualities involved. Some children express their mother's most important Divinity in a strengthened form, while others acquire powers associated with both parents or develop a new variation of an inherited Concept.

#### Dominance of the True Dragon Foundation
Chapter 1168 provided a particularly important explanation of the inheritance rules governing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s mature True Dragon descendants.
[[True Dragons]] possess extraordinary compatibility with Reality and supernatural systems, while Victor's own bloodline possesses powerful predatory and assimilative characteristics. In the offspring of his mature Draconic lineage, these qualities allow maternal supernatural traits to be incorporated into the child's Draconic existence rather than producing an inferior racial combination.[6]
Victor explained that a child directly descending from his mature Dragon lineage would not simply be born as an inferior non-Dragon because his dominant Blood rejects that kind of regression. The inheritance preserves the superior Draconic foundation while assimilating useful characteristics from the mother.[6]
This rule specifically concerns the mature lineage described in that chapter. It should not automatically be applied retroactively to every Vampire Victor created before becoming a True Dragon or to every unrelated individual later incorporated into his family.

#### Predatory Assimilation of Concepts
The interaction between [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s lineage and maternal Concepts became particularly clear in the case of Kassandra.
Born from Victor and Kali, Kassandra possessed a direct relationship with Destruction. Her True Dragon nature did not merely allow the Concept to coexist with her racial physiology. The predatory characteristics of her Blood assimilated Destruction into her biology, making the Concept part of her Draconic existence and supernatural abilities.[12]
As a result, her Dragon Language could carry destructive authority directly through her commands. The process demonstrated that the inheritance of a Concept could affect more than the child's Divinity alone; it could influence the nature and behavior of the descendant's racial abilities.

#### Inheritance of the Father's Eyes
The violet eyes shared by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s biological daughters are one of the most consistently demonstrated characteristics of his lineage.
Their supernatural perception originates from Victor, but its strength is not identical among the descendants. Each daughter possesses an individual starting level, and the inherited ability can develop further through training and experience.[16]
This provides a useful distinction between possessing a hereditary ability and possessing complete mastery of that ability. Inheritance establishes the foundation, but it does not automatically guarantee the same level of development as Victor.

#### Inheritance Without Conventional Reproduction
Not every descendant connected to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Essence was created through conventional biological reproduction.
[[Yog-Sothoth]] Elderblood originated through the extraordinary interaction between Victor and Azathoth's Essences. Her existence demonstrates that their supernatural characteristics could contribute to the creation of offspring through processes beyond ordinary physical reproduction.[35][36]
Such cases should be distinguished from the ordinary biological inheritance displayed by most of Victor's daughters. The presence of Victor's Essence establishes a hereditary or metaphysical connection, but the exact mechanisms may differ considerably according to the circumstances of the descendant's creation.

### The Second Generation
The biological descendants of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] possess a shared hereditary foundation despite the enormous variation between their mothers and individual supernatural powers. The following examples demonstrate the diversity of the Elderblood lineage.

Although the Elderblood Bloodline is closely connected to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s current existence, several different classifications must be distinguished.

### Relationship with Other Bloodlines

#### Walker Bloodline
The [[Walker Bloodline]] predates all of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s supernatural transformations. Its most unusual property is the progressive accumulation of potential over many generations. Chapter 1152 establishes that this ancestry contributed to the circumstances surrounding Victor's exceptional birth and development.[20]
The Walker lineage therefore forms part of Victor's ancestral history, but the novel does not explicitly state that its original hereditary properties ceased existing or were completely absorbed into a single new bloodline.

#### Sanguis Noctis Regis
[[Sanguis Noctis Regis]] is associated with the extraordinary Blood possessed by Vampire Progenitors. Its relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s original physiology was central to his abnormal development after becoming a Vampire and his later recognition as the Second Progenitor.[21]
Although its characteristics contributed to Victor's supernatural existence, the term Sanguis Noctis Regis has its own identity and should not be used interchangeably with Elderblood Bloodline.

#### Snow Bloodline
The [[Snow Bloodline]] is one of the hereditary Vampire lineages incorporated into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] during his original transformation. Its primary elemental inheritance is Fire, while other ancestral traits may exist within particular branches of the family.
Victor's Progenitor Blood demonstrated the ability to strengthen this inheritance within Violet, Agnes and Kaguya without removing their other supernatural characteristics.[2]

#### Scarlett Bloodline
The [[Scarlett Bloodline]] is associated with the extraordinary Ice-related abilities of Clan Scarlett. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] inherited these characteristics during his original Vampire transformation and later used his own Progenitor powers to strengthen members of the Scarlett family.[9][13]
The Scarlett lineage continues to possess its own hereditary identity despite its close relationship with Victor and the Elderblood family.

#### Fulger Bloodline
The [[Fulger Bloodline]] is associated with Lightning and the extraordinary physical and perceptual development of the Fulger family.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] inherited Lightning through the original transformation ritual. His later manipulation of Sasha and Natashia's ancestry substantially strengthened their inherited powers, while also enhancing additional ancestral characteristics.[9][5]

#### Blood Dragon Lineage
The Blood Dragon race originated through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s fusion of Vampire Progenitor characteristics with his developing Draconic existence. The resulting racial foundation became central to the physiology of the subsequent Elderblood generation.[25][10]
However, a distinction remains between the racial classification of Blood Dragon and the hereditary identity of the Elderblood Bloodline. The latter concerns Victor's personal lineage, while the former identifies a race originating from his evolution.

#### True Dragon Lineage
True Dragonhood represents a racial state distinct from the Elderblood Bloodline, although both are closely connected through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
The separation was explicitly established during the Imperial era, when the Elderblood Bloodline and True Dragon race were discussed as separate hereditary possessions associated with the Imperial Family.[4]
Chapter 1168 further clarified that the dominance of Victor's mature True Dragon foundation allows his biological descendants to assimilate maternal characteristics while retaining their Draconic nature.[6]

### Imperial Restrictions and Distribution

#### The Sovereignty of the Dynasty
The Elderblood Bloodline became one of the hereditary foundations of the Imperial Family's sovereignty. Unlike ordinary supernatural abilities, which [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] could study, reproduce or improve for the benefit of his civilization, his personal bloodline was not intended for unrestricted distribution.[4]
The restriction concerned both the exceptional nature of Victor's Blood and the superior racial state possessed by his direct Draconic family. Although the Empire possessed the means to strengthen ordinary citizens through genetics, technology, [[Magic]] and Divine intervention, the highest hereditary characteristics of the Imperial Dynasty remained comparatively exclusive.

#### Restrictions on Dragon Transformation
The later Dragon hierarchy included [[True Dragons]], ordinary Dragons and Lesser Dragons. Although [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] possessed the capacity to create members of these lineages, transformation into one of them was not automatically granted to every person connected to his family.[33]
Even relatives of Victor's wives were not guaranteed entry into the superior Dragon lineages simply because of their family connections. The women of the Imperial Family considered such privileges responsibilities requiring merit, loyalty and suitability rather than automatic hereditary rewards.[33]
Victor accepted the possibility of qualified individuals entering lesser Dragon branches, but the direct True Dragon foundation of the Imperial Family remained substantially more restricted.

#### Bloodline Membership and Family Membership
The distinction between belonging to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family and possessing his Bloodline is especially important.
An individual may become part of the Elderblood family through marriage, adoption or personal recognition without necessarily possessing the same biological inheritance as Victor's direct descendants.
Likewise, an individual transformed through Victor's Progenitor powers may possess characteristics of his supernatural lineage without having been born as one of his biological children.
The Elderblood surname is therefore a marker of familial identity and political belonging, but it should not be treated as sufficient evidence that every individual carrying it possesses an identical hereditary structure.
