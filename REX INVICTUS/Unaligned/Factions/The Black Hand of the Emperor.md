Emperor's Black Hand, officially called The Black Hand of the Emperor and abbreviated as B.H.E., is the Third Legion of the [[Elderblood Empire]] and one of the seven principal military and administrative organizations established under [[Victor Elderblood]]. Specializing in intelligence, espionage, infiltration, psychological warfare and covert operations, the Black Hand functions as the Empire's clandestine intelligence and security service. It operates primarily beyond public observation, performing sensitive assignments that require secrecy, deception, surveillance or the elimination of threats without the open deployment of conventional Imperial military forces.[1]
The Black Hand was established around Year 20 of the [[Imperial Calendar]] by Nyx Elderblood, [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]] and [[Persephone Elderblood]], shortly after the formation of the Sixth Legion, [[The Silent Judgment]]. Its creation formed part of the Emperor's effort to establish a comprehensive security structure capable of maintaining Imperial authority across an expanding civilization. Whereas the Silent Judgment performs the Empire's public judicial and enforcement duties, the Black Hand operates through intelligence gathering, covert intervention and the suppression of threats before they require open military action.[1]
The Legion is commanded by Kaguya Elderblood Blank, with Nyx Elderblood assuming command whenever Kaguya is unavailable. Its personnel include shadow assassins, telepaths, shapeshifters and shadow demons, providing the organization with a variety of supernatural abilities suited to clandestine operations. Its responsibilities extend beyond conventional espionage to hunting traitors, destabilizing hostile factions and controlling the information and narratives affecting the Empire's political stability.[1]
The Black Hand maintains a particularly close operational relationship with the Silent Judgment. Together, the Third and Sixth Legions fulfill functions comparable to an intelligence service and a police or judicial enforcement organization, creating complementary branches of Imperial security. The distinction between their responsibilities is commonly represented by the contrast between the Black Hand's justice in the shadows and the Silent Judgment's justice in the light.[1]

The Black Hand's existence reflects the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s separation of specialized military and administrative responsibilities. Rather than assigning intelligence gathering, criminal investigation, political security and military conquest to a single undifferentiated organization, the Empire maintains separate Legions capable of performing these functions while remaining subordinate to the Imperial command structure.[2][1]
Within this arrangement, the Black Hand concentrates upon threats that cannot always be effectively addressed through conventional military intervention. Its operations include gathering information, penetrating hostile organizations, identifying individuals working against Imperial interests and undermining enemy stability. Such responsibilities make the Legion particularly important in situations where public intervention would reveal Imperial intentions or prematurely alert an opponent.[1]
The Black Hand is not an independent political faction. Although Kaguya possesses operational command, the organization ultimately serves the Emperor and functions within the unified military hierarchy of the Elderblood Empire.

### History

#### Volume 9: Those Who Rule Reality
The foundations of the Black Hand emerged during the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s preparations for its enormous period of accelerated development. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] intended to transform his civilization into a power capable of competing with the advanced empires and supernatural organizations existing beyond his own Sector. To accomplish this, he initiated a Time-acceleration ritual intended to provide the Empire with 200,000 years of internal development while only a single year passed for those remaining outside the affected region.[2]
The Emperor also established a broader plan for reorganizing his military and administrative forces into Seven Imperial Legions. Each Legion would fulfill a particular strategic function while operating beneath a unified Imperial command structure. This organization was intended to prevent competing Pantheons, military branches and subordinate civilizations from fragmenting the Empire's leadership during future large-scale conflicts.[2]
Victor and his Empresses remained involved in the Empire's development through their Divine Avatars, participating in major decisions and supervising the expansion of Imperial infrastructure. As the civilization grew, its military and administrative systems were reorganized to accommodate enormous increases in population, supernatural power and territorial influence.[1]
The first two newly established Legions appeared around Year 20 of the [[Imperial Calendar]]. The Sixth Legion, [[The Silent Judgment]] was founded through the efforts of Ariel, Kaguya, Nyx, Leona and Tasha. It was designed to apply Imperial law throughout the Empire's territories, particularly newly acquired worlds requiring the establishment of Imperial authority and the suppression of corruption or rebellion.[1]
Shortly afterward, Kaguya, Nyx and [[Persephone]] founded the Third Legion, the Black Hand of the Emperor. Its creation addressed the need for an organization capable of operating beyond the public judicial and military structures already being established. Rather than enforcing Imperial law openly, the Black Hand would undertake the Empire's clandestine assignments, including espionage, infiltration, psychological warfare and activities requiring secrecy.[1]
The organization was established with Kaguya as its principal commander and Nyx as her acting replacement. Its personnel were selected from supernatural specializations suited to covert activity, including assassins capable of operating through shadows, telepaths, shapeshifters and shadow demons. These capabilities allowed the Legion to address threats through methods that differed considerably from those employed by conventional military formations.[1]
Together, the Black Hand and the Silent Judgment established the Empire's early internal security framework. The Silent Judgment possessed authority to publicly enforce Imperial law, conduct punishment and cleanse rebellious or corrupted territories, while the Black Hand specialized in intelligence gathering, clandestine intervention and the identification of threats operating beyond public scrutiny.[1]
The usefulness of covert personnel was particularly apparent during the Empire's expansion. The Imperial Family possessed overwhelming military power, but open confrontation was not always necessary or desirable when addressing defiant individuals or subordinate Gods. Assassins operating under Kaguya represented one of the methods available to the Imperial leadership for dealing with particularly troublesome opponents without mobilizing entire military formations.[3]
The establishment of the Black Hand and Silent Judgment preceded the creation of several other major Legions. The [[Stellar Arsenal]] was subsequently established around Year 50, while the Empire's principal offensive and defensive Legions were formalized as its military infrastructure continued to develop. The Black Hand nevertheless retained its specialized identity as the Third Legion and the primary organization dedicated to intelligence and covert operations.[1][4]

### Purpose and Responsibilities
The Black Hand exists to protect the [[Elderblood Empire]] through intelligence dominance, clandestine intervention and the disruption of hostile activities. Its responsibilities are separate from conventional territorial conquest or public military defense, focusing instead upon the collection and exploitation of information and the suppression of threats that could undermine Imperial stability.[1]

#### Intelligence Gathering
The acquisition of information concerning hostile individuals and organizations is one of the Black Hand's principal responsibilities. Intelligence allows the Empire to identify potential enemies, uncover hidden conspiracies and understand the activities of factions whose intentions may not be apparent through public observation.[1]
The Legion's combination of stealth specialists, telepaths and infiltration personnel provides methods suited to gathering information without necessarily confronting a target openly. Such capabilities are particularly valuable when investigating organizations protected by political influence, supernatural concealment or conventional military security.

#### Espionage
Espionage constitutes a core specialization of the Black Hand. Its agents are suited to operating within hostile environments, obtaining intelligence and carrying out missions in which revealing Imperial involvement would compromise the intended objective.[1]
The Legion's emphasis on secrecy distinguishes its operations from those of the Empire's openly deployed military forces. Instead of relying primarily upon visible displays of overwhelming power, espionage allows Imperial intelligence personnel to gather information and undermine hostile operations before conventional intervention becomes necessary.

#### Infiltration
Infiltration is among the Black Hand's established functions. The presence of shapeshifters, shadow assassins and other stealth-oriented personnel provides the organization with agents capable of penetrating locations or organizations where ordinary Imperial soldiers would be readily identified.[1]
The Legion's infiltration responsibilities are especially relevant to enemy factions attempting to conceal their activities or personnel. Successful intelligence penetration can reveal an opponent's intentions, internal vulnerabilities and potential sources of resistance.
The complete range of infiltration methods employed by Black Hand operatives has not been individually documented. Its known personnel types nevertheless establish a clear specialization in concealment and clandestine access rather than direct battlefield engagements.

#### Counterintelligence and Hunting Traitors
The identification and pursuit of traitors are explicit responsibilities of the Black Hand. Within the [[Elderblood Empire]], treason represents a direct threat to the authority of the Emperor and the security of the civilization under his protection.[1]
The Legion's intelligence and infiltration capabilities are suited to locating individuals who conceal hostile intentions, secretly cooperate with enemy organizations or attempt to undermine Imperial rule. Its ability to investigate such threats complements the public enforcement authority of the Silent Judgment.
The distinction between identifying a traitor and conducting formal judicial punishment remains important. The Black Hand specializes in discovering and pursuing threats through clandestine methods, whereas the Silent Judgment possesses an explicit mandate to judge, punish and enforce Imperial law.[1]

#### Psychological Warfare
Psychological warfare forms another major responsibility of the Black Hand. The Legion can employ covert methods intended to affect an enemy's perceptions, confidence and willingness to resist Imperial authority.[1]
This specialization extends beyond direct physical attacks. The organization's role in destabilizing enemy factions makes psychological operations relevant to undermining hostile unity and weakening resistance before open conflict. The exact techniques and individual operations employed by its psychological-warfare specialists have not been comprehensively described.

#### Enemy Destabilization
Destabilizing hostile organizations is one of the Black Hand's explicitly identified objectives. Whereas the Empire's offensive Legions are structured to defeat enemy forces through direct military action, the Black Hand can undermine the stability of an opposing faction through clandestine activity.[1]
Such operations fall within the Legion's broader intelligence and psychological-warfare specialization. The organization is therefore particularly suited to conflicts in which direct military superiority is already assured but the removal of hidden resistance, hostile leadership or internal conspiracies remains necessary.

#### Imperial Narrative Control
The Black Hand is also responsible for controlling the Imperial narrative, making information management an established element of its duties. This responsibility concerns the information environment surrounding Imperial authority and the political narratives capable of influencing the Empire's population or its enemies.[1]
Narrative control distinguishes the Black Hand from an organization concerned exclusively with gathering secret intelligence. Its responsibilities include influencing the wider political and informational environment in which the Empire operates, alongside more conventional intelligence and counterintelligence activities.
The specific institutions, communication systems and procedures employed for this responsibility remain unidentified. The Black Hand's narrative-control function should therefore not be confused with proof that it personally administers every public communication, news service or information network within the Empire.

#### Covert Elimination
The Black Hand maintains personnel suited to assassination and other clandestine forms of hostile-target removal. Shadow assassins are among its common units, while assassins associated with Kaguya are recognized as a potential means of dealing with powerful individuals whose behavior threatens the Empire's authority.[3][1]
Such personnel provide the Imperial leadership with options distinct from deploying Dragons, Leviathans or conventional military formations. They can be used where discretion is strategically preferable to an open demonstration of force.
Although assassination is compatible with the Legion's composition and responsibilities, a complete list of its successful eliminations, operational methods and individual targets has not been established.

### Command Structure
The Black Hand operates within the unified hierarchy governing the Seven Imperial Legions. Its supreme political authority belongs to Emperor [[Victor Elderblood]], while the Empire's overall military command is exercised through [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]]. The Legion's everyday leadership is entrusted to Kaguya, with Nyx assuming command when she is unavailable.[2][1]

#### Kaguya Elderblood Blank
Main article: [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]]
Kaguya Elderblood Blank is the principal commander of the Black Hand and one of its three founders. Her position places her in charge of the Empire's specialized intelligence and covert-operations Legion, an organization responsible for missions involving espionage, infiltration, psychological warfare and the pursuit of traitors.[1]
Kaguya's personal background makes her particularly suited to the role. Long before the Black Hand's establishment, she possessed extensive experience acting discreetly in service to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], combining extraordinary loyalty with the ability to conceal her actions and accomplish assignments without unnecessarily attracting attention.
Her command of the Black Hand represents an extension of those qualities into a formal Imperial organization. Rather than operating exclusively as Victor's personal maid and subordinate, she exercises leadership over an entire Legion dedicated to protecting the Empire through intelligence and covert intervention.[1]
Assassins associated with Kaguya are among the clandestine assets available to the Imperial leadership, demonstrating that her influence extends beyond ordinary administrative responsibilities.[3]

#### Nyx Elderblood
Main article: Nyx Elderblood
Nyx Elderblood is a co-founder of the Black Hand and its designated acting commander. She assumes command whenever Kaguya is occupied or otherwise unavailable, ensuring continuity in the Legion's leadership.[1]
As a powerful Goddess associated with Night and Darkness, Nyx possesses a supernatural nature compatible with the Legion's focus on concealed activity and shadow-oriented personnel. Her involvement in establishing the organization also gives her a direct connection to its original purpose and command structure.
Nyx's role is not merely ceremonial. Her authority to replace Kaguya establishes her as the Legion's recognized secondary commander, capable of assuming operational responsibility when required.[1]

#### Persephone Elderblood
Main article: [[Persephone Elderblood]]
Persephone Elderblood is one of the three founders responsible for establishing the Black Hand. She participated in its creation alongside Kaguya and Nyx during the early development of the Imperial Legions.[1]
[[Persephone]]'s founding role is confirmed, but no separate permanent command position, operational subdivision or specific intelligence assignment has been established for her. Her involvement should therefore be distinguished from Kaguya's position as Legion Commander and Nyx's position as acting commander.

### Organization and Personnel
The Black Hand's known personnel are concentrated around supernatural abilities suited to secrecy, intelligence gathering and clandestine intervention. Its composition differs from the conventional armies of the [[Elderblood Empire]] because its principal responsibilities do not require every member to function as a frontline soldier.[1]
The Legion's most commonly identified units are shadow assassins, telepaths, shapeshifters and shadow demons. These categories give the organization access to a combination of stealth, mental abilities, disguise and shadow-oriented supernatural powers.[1]

### Relationship with the Silent Judgment
Main article: [[The Silent Judgment]]
The Black Hand maintains a particularly close relationship with The Silent Judgment, the Sixth Imperial Legion responsible for the public enforcement of Imperial law. The two organizations were founded during the same early period of Imperial development and were designed to perform complementary functions.[1]
The Silent Judgment acts through overt authority, applying Imperial law within conquered or newly incorporated territories and punishing individuals or populations identified as corrupt, rebellious or heretical. Its personnel include elite soldiers, executioners, inquisitors, mind readers and empathic beings capable of detecting emotions.[1]
The Black Hand instead specializes in covert activity. Its responsibilities include intelligence gathering, espionage, infiltration, psychological warfare and the pursuit of traitors. These functions allow it to identify or interfere with hostile activities that may not be immediately visible to public enforcement organizations.[1]

### Relationship with Imperial Intelligence Infrastructure

#### Akasha Elderblood and the Imperial Network
Main article: [[Akasha Elderblood]]
The [[Elderblood Empire]] possesses an extensive information infrastructure coordinated through Akasha Elderblood, one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters and the central intelligence responsible for maintaining communication and information flow throughout the Empire.[5]
Akasha's network includes numerous subordinate artificial intelligences and Operators capable of supporting Imperial administration and transmitting information between widely separated individuals and institutions. This infrastructure allows the Emperor's government to maintain awareness across its expanding territories and coordinate its military and administrative responsibilities.[5]
The Black Hand operates within the same civilization but represents a different organizational function. Akasha is responsible for the Empire's broader information and communication infrastructure, while the Third Legion is formally dedicated to intelligence, espionage, covert operations and internal security.[5][1]
The existence of Akasha's network does not establish that every Operator or subordinate artificial intelligence is a member of the Black Hand. The precise arrangements through which the Legion accesses, exchanges or coordinates information with Akasha's systems have not been individually described.

### Operational Doctrine
The Black Hand's operational identity is built around the use of secrecy rather than overt military intimidation. Its established responsibilities emphasize information, infiltration, destabilization and the pursuit of concealed enemies, allowing the Empire to confront threats through means other than direct force.[1]
This specialization is particularly relevant to the [[Elderblood Empire]] because of the enormous disparity between its military capabilities and those of many lesser civilizations. A hostile individual or organization does not necessarily need to possess the strength to defeat an Imperial army to create problems. Espionage, treachery, political instability and the concealment of enemy activities can threaten an expanding civilization in ways that conventional military superiority alone does not automatically eliminate.
The Black Hand addresses these vulnerabilities through covert personnel and intelligence-oriented operations. Its use of assassins, telepaths, shapeshifters and shadow demons gives it a distinct operational character, emphasizing concealment and the ability to act within environments where the public presence of an Imperial army would be conspicuous.[1]
The Legion also performs assignments falling outside the responsibilities normally associated with public law enforcement. Its reputation for carrying out the Empire's clandestine or undesirable work reflects a deliberate separation between openly acknowledged Imperial authority and operations intended to remain outside public observation.[1]
The precise rules governing operational secrecy, authorization, interrogation, detention, assassination and the handling of classified intelligence have not been established. Likewise, no complete record of named missions undertaken exclusively by the Black Hand is available.
