The Arachne Clan is a Japanese Yokai clan composed of supernatural beings possessing spider-like characteristics. Although relatively minor compared with major Japanese factions such as the Kitsune clans, the Arachne Clan became involved in [[Nightingale]]'s affairs after attempting to manipulate Agnes Snow and use her political position to establish influence within Nightingale.[1]
The plan ultimately failed when the clan's unnamed leader personally provoked Agnes during an earlier supernatural meeting. After learning what had happened, [[Victor Elderblood]] economically pressured the Japanese Yokai until [[Hashimoto Genji]] surrendered the leader to him. Victor killed and consumed the clan leader, learning the true purpose behind the Arachne Clan's actions directly from the information he obtained.[2][1]

### Overview
The Arachne Clan is one of the numerous Yokai clans operating within Japan. Its members are identified as Spider Yokai, and its former clan leader visibly possessed arachnid characteristics.[2][1]
Unlike Yokai factions primarily characterized through military strength, the Arachne Clan's only extensively documented operation relied upon indirect influence. The clan intended to exploit Agnes Snow's position as the head of one of [[Nightingale]]'s three great Vampire Count families by gradually altering her perception of them through a supernatural talisman.[1]
Their intended objective was not simply to gain Agnes' personal friendship. The clan hoped that favorable treatment from Agnes would eventually translate into political support for the Yokai and consequently give them a degree of influence inside Nightingale.[1]
The operation became a disastrous political miscalculation when the clan leader deviated from the plan and attempted to force himself upon Agnes during a supernatural meeting. Agnes retaliated violently, burning the meeting place and leaving him near death. The incident effectively destroyed negotiations between the parties and turned the Arachne Clan into a direct concern for Clan Snow.[2]

### History

#### Attempt to Influence Agnes Snow
Before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became involved, representatives of the Yokai had participated in a supernatural meeting connected to establishing relations with [[Nightingale]]. The Arachne Clan's leader was among the Yokai present.[2]
During the meeting, the leader attempted to take Agnes Snow to bed by force. Agnes responded by burning the location and severely injuring him, leaving him close to death. The meeting consequently ended in failure and damaged relations between the Yokai and Clan Snow.[2]
The incident initially appeared to have resulted merely from the leader's personal behavior. The true circumstances were more extensive. The Arachne Clan had already devised a plan to "approach" Agnes through questionable means. Their original strategy involved applying a supernatural "good impression" talisman to Agnes so that she would develop an artificially favorable opinion of their clan.[1]
The effect was intended to be developed gradually. Through repeated encounters, the Arachne Clan planned to continue influencing Agnes until she became a reliable supporter of Yokai interests. Because Agnes was a Vampire Countess with enormous influence inside Nightingale, successfully manipulating her would have provided the Yokai with a political foothold within the realm. The clan leader's attempt to personally force himself upon Agnes was not part of this original strategy; it was his own mistake and caused the operation to collapse before it could properly develop.[1]

#### Victor's Intervention
After the death of [[Adonis Snow]], [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] temporarily assumed responsibility for reorganizing portions of Clan Snow's administration. While reviewing records, he discovered documentation concerning the failed Japanese supernatural meeting and questioned Hilda about what had happened.[2]
When Hilda explained that a Spider Yokai had attempted to force himself upon Agnes, Victor immediately became interested in locating him. After confirming that the individual belonged to a clan known as the Arachne Clan, Victor began applying indirect pressure rather than personally travelling across Japan to find him.[2]
Victor restricted trade entering and leaving [[Nightingale]] for the Yokai and organizations associated with them. Because Nightingale represented an important supernatural market, the decision placed economic pressure not only upon the Arachne Clan but upon numerous factions whose interests depended upon continued access to Nightingale.[1]
The Witches understood Victor's intention almost immediately. Rather than attempting to negotiate the restrictions away, they investigated the reason behind the embargo, discovered the incident involving Agnes and pressured the Yokai to surrender the responsible individual. From their perspective, maintaining favorable relations with Victor and the Vampire Count families was considerably more valuable than protecting one Yokai who had already created a major political incident.[1]

#### Fall of the Clan Leader
[[Hashimoto Genji]] eventually arrived before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] accompanied by other Yokai and delivered the captured leader of the Arachne Clan. Genji intended to negotiate for the survival of the remainder of the clan after surrendering its leader.[1]
Victor already anticipated this request. He informed Genji that he did not intend to exterminate an entire clan merely because of the actions of a single member. However, Victor immediately added an important qualification: the situation would be different if the rest of the clan had knowingly supported that individual's actions.[1]
The threat was particularly dangerous because Victor subsequently consumed the Arachne Clan's leader. By doing so, he gained access to information concerning the conspiracy and discovered that the Arachne Clan's involvement extended beyond the leader's personal misconduct. The clan itself had intended to influence Agnes through the "good impression" talisman and gradually turn her into a supporter of Yokai interests.[1]
Genji nevertheless insisted that the remainder of the Arachne Clan knew nothing and that its leader had acted independently. Victor's knowledge made the statement extremely questionable, and Genji's own reaction demonstrated that he feared Victor had discovered considerably more than he should have known. The other Yokai accompanying Genji were similarly terrified when Victor examined them individually.[1]
Rather than immediately expose the contradiction and begin massacring the clan, Victor accepted the explanation presented to him, remarking that it was good news. In practice, the exchange functioned as an unmistakable warning: the Arachne Clan was permitted to survive, but Victor had demonstrated that he could uncover their hidden actions and would not extend the same restraint if they again targeted someone under his protection.[1]

### Characteristics
Members of the Arachne Clan are Spider Yokai. Their exact racial biology has not been extensively explored in the novel, but the clan leader possessed obvious arachnid features that visibly distinguished him from humanoid Yokai.[1]
The clan's known behavior indicates a preference for covert political manipulation over direct confrontation. Their operation involving Agnes was designed to slowly change the attitude of an influential supernatural ruler rather than defeat or openly coerce her. This would have allowed them to gain political influence while leaving the target unaware that her favorable opinion had been artificially induced.[1]

### Abilities and Techniques
Only a limited number of abilities have been directly demonstrated or described for members of the Arachne Clan. Abilities commonly associated with fictional or mythological spider creatures, such as venom production, supernatural webbing or wall-crawling, have not been explicitly demonstrated by the clan in the novel and should not be treated as confirmed abilities.
- Yokai Physiology: As members of the Yokai race, the [[Arachne]] possess supernatural physiology rather than being ordinary biological spiders or Humans.[1]
- Arachnid Physiology: The clan possesses visible spider characteristics. The former clan leader displayed additional spider-like limbs extending from his body, establishing that at least some members possess pronounced arachnid features.[1]
- Supernatural Talismans: The Arachne Clan possessed or had access to supernatural talismans capable of influencing other beings.[1]
- "Good Impression" Talisman: The clan planned to use this talisman on Agnes Snow. Its purpose was to artificially cause Agnes to develop a favorable impression of the Arachne Clan. The effect was intended to serve as the first stage of a longer manipulation campaign rather than instantly placing Agnes under complete control.[1]
- Psychological Influence: The clan's plan involved repeatedly exposing Agnes to supernatural techniques after establishing the initial favorable impression, gradually manipulating her until she actively supported Yokai interests. The exact mechanics, limits and resistance requirements of these techniques were never explained.[1]
- Political Manipulation: Rather than attempting to invade [[Nightingale]] or overpower a Vampire Countess directly, the clan designed a long-term infiltration strategy centered upon manipulating an influential political figure and using her position to create Yokai influence within the realm.[1]

### Political Relations

#### Clan Snow
The Arachne Clan's most significant known interaction with [[Nightingale]] involved Clan Snow. The clan attempted to manipulate its leader, Agnes Snow, because her political status would have allowed them to gain influence far beyond what the Arachne Clan could achieve independently.[1]
The relationship collapsed when the [[Arachne]] leader attempted to force himself upon Agnes. Agnes nearly killed him and destroyed the meeting place, ending any possibility of normal diplomatic relations between the factions.[2]

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became the clan's greatest threat after learning what its leader had done to Agnes. Rather than immediately attacking Japan, he used [[Nightingale]]'s economic importance to pressure every faction associated with the Yokai until the [[Arachne]] leader was surrendered to him.[1]
After consuming the leader and discovering the clan's hidden plan, Victor made clear that he would have exterminated the entire Arachne Clan if he chose to hold all of its members responsible. He ultimately allowed them to survive, but only after Genji formally presented the conspiracy as the independent actions of the deceased leader.[1]

#### Hashimoto Genji
[[Hashimoto Genji]] acted as the Yokai representative who delivered the [[Arachne]] leader to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. His primary objective following the surrender was preventing Victor from extending his retaliation to the remainder of the clan.[1]
Genji claimed that the clan itself had known nothing about the conspiracy and that the leader had acted entirely independently. This statement conflicted with the information Victor obtained after consuming the leader, which identified the attempt to manipulate Agnes as a plan belonging to the Arachne Clan rather than merely an impulsive action by one individual. Victor nevertheless accepted Genji's explanation rather than pursue extermination.[1]

#### Witches
The Witches were instrumental in forcing the resolution of the conflict. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s trade restrictions threatened their economic interests, and they quickly determined that handing over the [[Arachne]] leader was more advantageous than defending him. They therefore pressured the Yokai into complying with Victor's unstated demand.[1]

### Current Status
The Arachne Clan remains extant, but its former leader is dead. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] deliberately chose not to annihilate the entire clan after Genji surrendered its leader and claimed that the remaining members had not participated in the conspiracy.[1]
The incident represented a major political defeat for the clan. Their attempt to secretly gain influence inside [[Nightingale]] instead resulted in the death of their leader, the exposure of their manipulation strategy and a demonstration that Victor was willing to economically pressure unrelated supernatural factions until the people responsible for threatening his family were delivered to him.[1]
No later event has established that the Arachne Clan attempted retaliation against Victor, Agnes or Nightingale. Likewise, the novel has not shown the identity of a successor to the deceased clan leader or provided substantial details concerning the clan's later political position.

### Trivia
- Hilda initially struggled to remember the clan's exact name, recalling it only as something resembling "[[Arachne]]", "Arcne" or "Arai", because Japan contained numerous Yokai clans and she considered relatively few of them important.[2]
- The clan leader's attempt to force himself upon Agnes was not part of the clan's original plan. Their intended strategy was considerably more subtle and centered upon the "good impression" talisman.[1]
- The purpose of the talisman was not explicitly described as complete mind control. It was intended to give Agnes a favorable impression of the clan so that further manipulation could occur over repeated meetings.[1]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] discovered the true conspiracy only after consuming the clan leader and acquiring his information.[1]
- Genji attempted to protect the surviving clan by claiming that only its leader was responsible. Victor knew enough about the underlying plot to recognize the inconsistency but chose not to pursue the issue further.[1]
- Contrary to the clan's previous article, the novel has not demonstrated the Arachne Clan using spider silk, webs, venom, wall-crawling or venom-induced hallucinations. Those abilities therefore should not be indexed as canon unless they are demonstrated in a later chapter.
