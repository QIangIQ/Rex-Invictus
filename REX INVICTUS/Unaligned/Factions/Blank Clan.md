Clan Blank, commonly called the Blank Clan and historically descended from the Japanese [[Kuroyami Clan]], is an ancient Noble Vampire assassin lineage originally subordinate to the [[Snow Clan]] and later incorporated into the wider Elderblood Clan and [[Elderblood Empire]]. Traditionally specializing in assassination, espionage, infiltration and Shadow Manipulation, the Clan served for generations as the hidden intelligence and execution arm of the Snow family.[1]
Blank's unusual Bloodline originated through the union of the ancient Japanese Kuroyami assassin Clan with a member of the Snow main family. This created descendants who combined Kuroyami Shadow abilities with several inherited Snow traits, particularly immunity to sunlight and resistance to Fire.[1] The Clan's relationship with Snow gradually changed from political subordination into hereditary servitude, eventually producing generations of elite maids, assassins and intelligence agents such as [[Oda Blank]] and Kaguya Elderblood Blank.
Following Kaguya's marriage to [[Victor Elderblood]] and the rise of the Elderblood Empire, Clan Blank underwent another fundamental transformation. Its members were converted from Noble Vampires into Dragonoids and became part of Victor's Imperial intelligence and enforcement apparatus. By the Imperial era, the Blanks function as the shadows of the Emperor, investigating treason, gathering intelligence and eliminating threats throughout his domains.[2][3]

### History

#### Kuroyami Origins
Before becoming Clan Blank, the family's Shadow-manipulating ancestors belonged to the [[Kuroyami Clan]], an ancient Japanese Clan of Vampire assassins.[1]
The connection with Clan Snow began when the uncle of Agnes Snow traveled throughout the world and eventually reached Japan. There he had a child with a woman belonging to Clan Kuroyami.[1]
Their son produced an unusual Bloodline combination. He did not initially inherit the Snow family's Flames, but he did inherit their extremely rare immunity to sunlight while retaining the Kuroyami lineage's connection to Darkness and Shadows.[1]
At the time, Clan Kuroyami was experiencing severe difficulties. Agnes' mother, who was then Leader of Clan Snow, proposed an alliance: the Kuroyami lineage would enter Snow as a subordinate Clan, while Snow would provide them with protection, territory, resources, purpose and the ability to operate beneath the Sun through their mixed descendants.[1]
This arrangement produced what eventually became known as Clan Blank.

#### From Subordinates to Servants
The original arrangement did not make Clan Blank servants.
They were subordinate allies of Clan Snow, with their own leadership and internal authority.[1]
The relationship changed after the death of the Clan's heiress.
Certain Blank [[Elders]] exploited the resulting political instability and, without the approval of the [[Snow Clan]] Leader, arranged for the entire family to sign a lifetime contract of servitude.[1]
Agnes' mother regarded this as insubordination and executed the responsible Elders, burning them to death. However, the contract itself had already been completed.
By that point, Agnes' uncle—the Snow Vampire responsible for the original connection between the families—had also died, leaving Clan Blank without two important political figures who could have contested the arrangement.[1]

#### The Snow–Blank Heir
The mixed descendant born from Snow and Kuroyami eventually grew into an extraordinarily powerful Vampire.
Unlike the earlier generation, he inherited:
- the [[Snow Clan]]'s Fire;
- the Snow Clan's sunlight immunity;
- and Clan Blank's Darkness.[1]
He became powerful enough that he could likely have challenged the servitude contract and restored Blank's independence.
Instead, he voluntarily chose to uphold it.
The Snow Clan had protected and supported his family when Kuroyami was vulnerable, and he considered rejecting the relationship after benefiting from Snow's aid dishonorable. His choice converted what had begun through political manipulation into a genuinely accepted bond of loyalty.[1]
From that generation onward, Clan Blank became known as the most trusted servants of Clan Snow, answering directly to the Snow Clan Leader.[1]
[[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]] is a direct descendant of this powerful mixed Snow–Blank ancestor.[1]

#### Service to Clan Snow
For generations, Clan Blank served as Snow's hidden intelligence, assassination and security force.
While the [[Snow Clan]] managed [[Nightingale]]'s economic and diplomatic affairs, Blank dealt with problems that required secrecy:
- information gathering;
- infiltration;
- assassination;
- counterespionage;
- surveillance;
- protection of Snow leaders and heiresses;
- and elimination of internal threats.
Its Shadow abilities made members exceptionally suited to this work.
[[Oda Blank]] eventually became Clan Leader and served directly beneath [[Agnes Elderblood Snow]], functioning as her butler, assassin and source of intelligence.
Kaguya was raised from childhood according to the same traditions and assigned to [[Violet Elderblood Snow]], where she was trained to function as an exceptionally capable maid, bodyguard, spy and assassin.[4]

### The Snow Clan Purge
Clan Blank played a central role during the discovery of traitors within [[Snow Clan]].
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] learned that members of Snow and Fulger had been conspiring to remove Agnes and Natashia from power while intending to exploit Violet and Sasha as reproductive assets for future Noble Vampire Bloodlines.[5][6]
After Victor assumed temporary authority over Snow, Blank was mobilized as his principal covert force.
Under orders from Victor, Oda, Kaguya and other Blank operatives hunted the conspirators throughout Snow territory and systematically eliminated them.[7]
The purge demonstrated precisely why the Clan had been maintained as Snow's hidden weapon for generations: conspirators who had infiltrated normal administrative positions found it considerably harder to escape assassins capable of moving through Shadows and striking without warning.
Following the purge, Victor reorganized Snow's finances, internal security and administration while continuing to use Clan Blank as one of the principal mechanisms for preventing another infiltration.[8][9][10]

### Kaguya and Victor Elderblood
Clan Blank's political position changed radically after Kaguya became permanently attached to [[Victor Elderblood]].
After Victor transformed Kaguya into a member of his personal lineage and publicly acknowledged her as his wife, the consequences extended beyond their private relationship.
Kaguya was a descendant of the powerful Snow–Blank heir and a recognized senior member of Clan Blank. As the wife of the Second Vampire Progenitor, her political status technically rose above even the traditional Vampire Count houses.[1]
This created a significant political problem inside [[Nightingale]].
At the time, Victor's other marriages to members of the Count families were not all publicly understood in political terms. Kaguya's publicly known marriage could therefore be interpreted as Clan Blank—and indirectly Snow—formally aligning itself with the new Progenitor against [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]].[1]
Agnes explicitly feared that if the Count houses appeared to unite openly beneath Victor, Vampire society could interpret the development as rejection of the old Progenitor and descend into civil conflict.[1]
The incident illustrates that Clan Blank, despite historically being considered a subordinate family, possessed far greater political significance than its small public presence suggested.

### Elderblood Era
As [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction expanded beyond [[Nightingale]], Clan Blank gradually moved from being exclusively a Snow intelligence organization into an important covert arm of the Elderblood faction.
Kaguya simultaneously retained her Blank heritage while becoming:
- Victor's wife;
- a member of Elderblood Clan;
- leader of the [[Maid Squad]];
- and one of Victor's principal intelligence and enforcement authorities.
The Clan's traditional expertise in Shadows, assassination and espionage made it naturally suited for the covert requirements of Victor's rapidly expanding civilization.

### Dragonoid Transformation
By Chapter 986, Clan Blank had undergone a complete racial upgrade.
The operatives accompanying Kaguya were no longer ordinary Noble Vampires but Dragonoids, beings described as possessing potential second only to [[True Dragons]].[2]
This transformation greatly increased the Clan's combat potential while retaining its traditional specialization in Darkness and stealth.
The Blanks subsequently appeared publicly alongside the True Dragon Kaguya when Gods attempted to take credit for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s restoration of Earth.
Rather than Victor personally dealing with such comparatively minor offenses, Clan Blank acted in his name.[2]
Kaguya publicly executed one offending God with Black Dragon Fire while her Dragonoid operatives surrounded the [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]], establishing Blank not merely as assassins of the Snow family but as an Imperial force capable of threatening Divine Beings.[2]
This marked the Clan's transition from:
[[Snow Clan]] covert operatives → Elderblood Imperial covert operatives.

### Imperial Intelligence Service
By the mature Imperial era, Clan Blank functions as one of the Emperor's principal hidden security organizations.
Chapter 1065 explicitly depicts the Blanks as the shadows of the Emperor.[3]
Their duties now include:
- counterintelligence;
- identifying internal traitors;
- investigating foreign espionage;
- interrogation;
- covert arrests;
- rehabilitation of offenders;
- intelligence gathering;
- and removing threats to Imperial security.
Kaguya personally supervises this work.
Oda remains one of the Clan's senior leaders and acts with considerable independence whenever Kaguya is occupied elsewhere.[3]
Other operatives use codenames rather than revealing their identities. One such agent, Mifune, worked directly beneath Oda and Kaguya during an investigation involving the leaking of Imperial information.[3]
This demonstrates that the Clan possesses considerably more members than the few individually named characters shown in the story.

### Hierarchy

#### Historical Leadership
Traditionally, Clan Blank possessed its own Leader and formerly also possessed a council of [[Elders]].[1]
The Elders were intended to act as a safeguard capable of removing a Leader deemed evil or unfit.
This structure ended disastrously when those Elders abused the death of the Clan heiress to impose the lifetime servitude contract without Snow's authorization. Agnes' mother subsequently executed them.[1]

#### Snow Era
During the modern pre-Elderblood period:
- [[Oda Blank]] — Clan Leader.
- [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]] — Second/Vice Leader.
Kaguya was elevated after the [[Snow Clan]] purge so that she could access information and authority useful in serving [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].

#### Imperial Era
The exact formal titles of every Blank leader are not completely explained in the later chapters.
However, Chapter 1065 shows Kaguya exercising supreme operational authority over the Clan's Imperial activities while describing Oda as one of the leaders who assumes responsibility when Kaguya is occupied.[3]
The modern structure is therefore better represented as:
- [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]] — principal Imperial commander / senior authority.
- [[Oda Blank]] — senior Clan leader and operational commander.
- other unidentified Blank leaders and agents.
- field operatives, many of whom use codenames.
This is more accurate than treating Oda as the only meaningful leader throughout every era.

### Abilities

#### Umbrakinesis
The signature ability of Clan Blank is Shadow Manipulation.
Members can manipulate Darkness and Shadows, use them for concealment and merge their bodies with existing Shadows.
This provides exceptional utility for:
- assassination;
- infiltration;
- surveillance;
- covert movement;
- ambushes;
- escaping detection;
- and protecting their masters.
Experienced Blank Vampires can approach targets without being noticed even by individuals possessing enhanced supernatural senses.
The ability historically made them particularly dangerous to Witches, whose vulnerability to unexpected physical attacks made assassins capable of emerging directly from Shadows extremely difficult to counter.

#### Shadow Movement
Blank operatives can use Shadows as pathways for movement and concealment.
Kaguya became the most prominent example, routinely remaining inside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Shadow for prolonged periods and emerging only when required.
The technique allows a Blank assassin to remain physically close to their protected target without visibly accompanying them.

#### Sunlight Immunity
Because of the Clan's Snow ancestry, Blank Vampires inherited the ability to withstand sunlight.[1]
This is particularly useful for assassins because it removes one of the primary limitations that would normally prevent Vampires from operating freely in Human society during the day.

#### Fire Resistance
Blank descendants also inherited a high degree of resistance to the [[Snow Clan]]'s Fire.
Unlike the main [[Snow Bloodline]], most Blank members did not retain [[Pyrokinesis]], but the defensive traits associated with Snow ancestry remained.
A particularly powerful ancestral heir possessed both Snow Fire and Blank Darkness, demonstrating that the loss of Fire was not necessarily absolute throughout every branch of the lineage.[1]

#### Assassination Arts
Clan Blank possesses generations of specialized training derived from its Kuroyami assassin ancestry.
Members are trained to prioritize efficient killing rather than honorable direct confrontation.
Their combat methodology emphasizes:
- surprise attacks;
- exploiting blind spots;
- concealment;
- precision;
- silent movement;
- rapidly incapacitating the target;
- and completing an assignment before conventional combat becomes necessary.

#### Espionage and Intelligence
Blank operatives are trained to observe, gather and transmit information without revealing their presence.
This role became increasingly important after the Clan entered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Imperial structure, where intelligence operations expanded from protecting one Vampire Count family to monitoring threats across an interplanetary Empire.[3]

#### Dragonoid Physiology
Following their transformation during the Elderblood era, the active Blanks became Dragonoids.[2]
This granted them significantly greater physical and supernatural potential than their former Noble Vampire bodies while preserving their specialized Shadow abilities.
Their exact individual Draconic traits have not been sufficiently described to justify assigning every ability demonstrated by Kaguya herself to ordinary Blank Dragonoids.

### Relationship with Snow Clan
Clan Blank's relationship with Snow is considerably more complex than simple enslavement.
It began as an alliance of necessity.
Snow offered the struggling [[Kuroyami Clan]]:
- political protection;
- resources;
- territory;
- purpose;
- and connection to a Bloodline capable of walking in sunlight.[1]
Blank in return became Snow's specialized subordinate intelligence family.
The later lifetime servitude contract was created through political abuse by Blank [[Elders]] rather than the original terms offered by Agnes' mother.[1]
Nevertheless, the mixed Snow–Blank heir voluntarily chose to preserve the relationship even after becoming strong enough to potentially invalidate the contract.
The result was a relationship that became genuinely loyal despite originating through questionable political circumstances.
This distinction should be preserved on the page: Clan Blank was not originally created as a slave/servant Clan.

### Relationship with Elderblood Clan
Kaguya's marriage to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] permanently connected Blank to the Elderblood Clan.
Unlike its historical relationship with Snow, this affiliation gradually became greater than simple political subordination.
The Blank Clan's skills were incorporated into Victor's expanding government, and its members eventually received Dragonoid transformations that elevated them far above their former Noble Vampire status.[2]
By the Imperial era, the Clan acts directly in the Emperor's interest and protects the Elderblood family's security and reputation.
For this reason, modern Blank should be considered affiliated with both its ancestral Snow family and the wider Elderblood order.

### Current Status
Clan Blank remains active.
It has undergone three broad historical phases:
1. Kuroyami Era — Ancient Japanese Vampire assassin Clan.
2. Snow Era — Subordinate and later hereditary servant family functioning as Clan Snow's assassins and intelligence agents.
3. Elderblood Imperial Era — Dragonoid intelligence and enforcement organization operating as the shadows of Emperor [[Victor Elderblood]].
The Clan has therefore not disappeared after Snow's integration into the [[Elderblood Empire]].
Instead, its original profession became substantially more important because the organization now performs covert security work for an Empire rather than a single Vampire Count house.
