The Kolo Empire was a galactic power ruled by [[Galactic Emperor Kolo]] and one of the principal rivals faced by Thaloria Xyphora during the expansion of the Thaloria Empire. Its most important known military asset was the Yuurakyo, an extraordinarily dangerous warrior race whose members were renowned in their Sector as the assassins of Galactic Emperor Kolo. Unlike the technological supremacy associated with [[Eldoria]], the threat presented by Kolo's forces came largely from the exceptional biology, combat aptitude and adaptability of the Yuurakyo, whose individual warriors were capable of breaking through defenses that had previously allowed Thaloria's civilization to dominate conventional opponents.[1]
Very little has been revealed about the Empire's founding, civil government, territorial administration or everyday culture. Most information concerning it originates from [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]]'s account of her mother's rise, where Kolo and his Yuurakyo forces appear primarily as the greatest military obstacle preventing the Thaloria Dynasty from obtaining uncontested control of their galaxy.[1]

### History

#### Conflict with the Thaloria Dynasty
[[Thaloria Xyphora]] rose from the technologically gifted [[Xyrra]] race and transformed [[Eldoria]] into an increasingly advanced civilization. In less than two thousand years, her dynasty had brought the entire planet under its control and begun expanding into space. As Eldoria encountered other worlds and races, talented outsiders were incorporated into the growing Empire, bringing Thaloria into contact with increasingly dangerous extraterrestrial powers.[1]
Among these powers was [[Galactic Emperor Kolo]]. Velnorah identified the Yuurakyo as a race associated with Kolo and described them as being known within her Sector as the assassins of Galactic Emperor Kolo. Kolo would become one of Thaloria's major enemies as their respective powers expanded into conflict.[1]
The war was significant because the Yuurakyo represented something Thaloria's technological advantage could not casually overcome. Eldoria could defeat conventional civilizations through superior technology, planning and weaponry, but Yuurakyo warriors possessed a biological and martial superiority that allowed them to directly contest forces which ordinarily dominated their opponents through technology.[1]

### The Yuurakyo
The Empire's Greatest Military Asset
The Yuurakyo were not dangerous merely because they were physically strong. Their biology, natural combat talent, environmental adaptability and later artificial enhancement collectively produced warriors capable of inflicting major losses upon an advanced interstellar civilization.
The Yuurakyo were a race fundamentally shaped around warfare. Their natural bodies were superior to those of most other races encountered by Velnorah's civilization, while their perception and innate talent for combat were likewise unusually developed. They were also highly adaptable, requiring only several weeks to acclimate themselves to unfamiliar and hostile environments.[1]
Their combat aptitude was demonstrated when a single male Yuurakyo infiltrated Thaloria's territory. Thaloria discovered the infiltration and, rather than immediately eliminating the intruder, created a plan to capture him alive. Even with her technology and some of her best generals supporting the operation, the cost was considerable. The warrior fought through multiple technological restraints and force fields using his body alone, inflicted heavy losses and even damaged Thaloria's own armor before finally being subdued.[1]
The incident convinced Thaloria that the Yuurakyo possessed something her Empire lacked. Instead of being satisfied with defeating the infiltrator, she attempted to understand and reproduce the race's extraordinary physical power so it could be incorporated into her own civilization.[1]

#### Biological Characteristics
Characteristic
Description
Superior Physiology
Yuurakyo were naturally born with bodies superior to those of many other races. Even before their later enhancement, they possessed exceptional physical capabilities.[1]
Combat Aptitude
Their perception and talent for fighting were naturally superior, reflecting a race whose development and culture were fundamentally centered upon combat.[1]
Environmental Adaptation
They could acclimate to new and hostile environments within only a few weeks, making them exceptionally suitable for interplanetary warfare and infiltration.[1]
Reproductive Condition
Successful reproduction possessed an unusual behavioral condition: the male needed to defeat the female and obtain her submission. Attempts to produce viable offspring while bypassing this condition repeatedly failed.[1]
Coming-of-Age Enhancement
Yuurakyo elders administered a special serum during a coming-of-age ceremony. This serum dramatically enhanced their physical abilities and was responsible for much of the instability detected in the genetic material of mature Yuurakyo.[1]
Non-Hereditary Enhancement
The modifications caused by the serum were not naturally inherited by offspring. Every new generation therefore needed to undergo the ceremony independently.[1]

### Thaloria's Yuurakyo Research

#### The Captured Warrior
Thaloria's first objective was not extermination. She wanted to obtain the Yuurakyo's strength for herself. The captured male consequently became a test subject in a long series of experiments designed to reproduce or transfer the characteristics responsible for the race's combat superiority.[1]
Her first approach was cloning. This failed because the warrior's genetic code was extraordinarily unstable. At the time, Thaloria did not yet understand that this instability was not entirely natural to the species; it was connected to the enhancement serum administered later in a Yuurakyo's development.[1]
She next attempted to produce Yuurakyo offspring through artificial insemination using females from other species. This also failed catastrophically. The developing children placed such extreme nutritional and physical demands upon their hosts that females belonging to other races could not sustain the pregnancy.[1]
Thaloria then explored whether another species could simply be transformed into Yuurakyo. Research involving Progenitors had taught her an important principle: changing a body alone was insufficient to truly transform a being's race. The Soul itself needed to be altered before the body would properly respond to the racial transformation. Despite understanding the principle, Thaloria was unable to reproduce the authority of a genuine Progenitor and eventually abandoned this approach.[1]

#### Capture of a Female Yuurakyo
While the war against Kolo's power continued and Thaloria's Empire was struggling, a female Yuurakyo infiltrated the facility in an attempt to rescue the captive male. By this point Thaloria possessed extensive information from studying the first prisoner and did not repeat her earlier mistake of underestimating the species. She successfully anticipated the rescue attempt and captured the female with considerably greater efficiency.[1]
Possessing both a male and female appeared to solve the reproductive problem, but artificial insemination between the two still failed. The resulting child could not survive because Yuurakyo reproduction was connected to the race's martial nature. The male was required to defeat the female and cause her to submit to him before viable reproduction could properly occur.[1]
After numerous failures, Thaloria resorted to administering powerful aphrodisiacs to both prisoners. The crude experiment unexpectedly succeeded and produced a living Yuurakyo child. The child was unquestionably powerful when compared with ordinary species, but remained substantially weaker than his parents even after maturing.[1]

#### The Coming-of-Age Serum
The child's comparatively stable genetics finally exposed the missing variable. Unlike his parents, he had not grown up within Yuurakyo society and had never undergone their traditional coming-of-age ceremony.[1]
During this ceremony, an elder of the race injected young Yuurakyo with a specialized serum that greatly strengthened their physical abilities. The serum was responsible for the genetic instability that had prevented Thaloria from successfully cloning the captured adults. Because the alterations produced by the serum were not inherited naturally, each generation required a new administration of the enhancement.[1]
This discovery radically changed the direction of Thaloria's research. She had originally been attempting to reproduce the Yuurakyo's strength; the existence of an unenhanced specimen instead gave her the biological baseline necessary to discover how to kill them.[1]

### War with the Thaloria Empire
Turning Point of the War
Thaloria did not overcome the Yuurakyo by creating stronger soldiers. She used the knowledge obtained from their biology to remove the race itself from the conflict.
By the time Thaloria completed her research, the conflict against Kolo's power had demonstrated that technological superiority by itself was not sufficient while the Yuurakyo remained active. They could directly contest [[Eldoria]]'s military forces and inflict losses that other civilizations could not reproduce.[1]
Using information obtained from the Yuurakyo child and his parents, Thaloria developed a race-specific biological weapon. The resulting virus attacked the brains of Yuurakyo and killed them almost immediately while being designed to affect their race specifically.[1]
Thaloria deployed the weapon and exterminated the Yuurakyo as a species. In doing so, she destroyed what Velnorah described as the greatest power of Kolo's Galactic Empire. Once that force disappeared, the balance of the war changed completely. No conventional opponent remaining in the galaxy could compete with Thaloria in a purely technological conflict, allowing her to eliminate the remaining resistance and continue expanding her dominion.[1]

### Defeat of Kolo's Power
The destruction of the Yuurakyo effectively ended Kolo's ability to oppose Thaloria on equal terms. His greatest known military force was gone, while the Thaloria Dynasty retained its technological infrastructure, research capabilities and overwhelming advantage against more conventional enemies. Thaloria subsequently brought the galaxy under her rule and rose to the position later associated with an Overlord.[1]
The original account provides very little information about the internal collapse of Kolo's government itself. It does not describe an administrative surrender, mass annexation process, succession crisis or detailed occupation of individual Kolo territories. What is established is that the extermination of the Yuurakyo removed the Galactic Empire's greatest source of military power and eliminated the principal opposition preventing Thaloria from achieving galactic supremacy.[1]
For this reason, descriptions of the Kolo Empire as possessing a documented strength-based caste system, formal warrior challenges, specific laws of honor, war-beast armies or a government where every position was decided by personal combat are unsupported by the currently revealed canon. The known information centers overwhelmingly upon Kolo's conflict with Thaloria and the Yuurakyo forces associated with him.

### Military

#### Yuurakyo Assassins
The only extensively documented military arm associated with Kolo is the Yuurakyo. Their reputation as the assassins of [[Galactic Emperor Kolo]] indicates that they served as one of his most feared military assets rather than simply being an unrelated species caught in the conflict.[1]
Their value came from qualities that were extremely difficult for advanced technology to imitate:
- Physical Superiority: Mature Yuurakyo possessed exceptional natural bodies further strengthened through their coming-of-age serum.[1]
- Natural Combat Talent: Their perception, instincts and talent for battle were superior to those of ordinary species encountered by the Thaloria Dynasty.[1]
- Rapid Adaptation: They could become accustomed to dangerous and unfamiliar environments within weeks.[1]
- Anti-Technological Effectiveness: An individual warrior demonstrated the ability to smash through advanced force fields physically and inflict severe losses upon forces supported by Thaloria's technology.[1]
- Infiltration Capability: At least two Yuurakyo successfully penetrated deeply enough into Thaloria's territory to conduct infiltration or rescue operations, reinforcing their value as more than conventional front-line troops.[1]

#### Technology
Contrary to the earlier version of this article, canon does not establish that Kolo's civilization rejected advanced technology, artificial intelligence, cybernetics or other forms of interstellar infrastructure. Kolo was a Galactic Emperor commanding an interstellar power, but Chapter 892 primarily focuses upon the biological superiority of his Yuurakyo forces and does not provide sufficient information to define the technological level or technological philosophy of the wider Empire.[1]
Likewise, there is no confirmed statement that Kolo's fleets were deliberately primitive, that his soldiers fought without equipment as a cultural rule, or that the Empire considered technology dishonorable. The contrast established in Velnorah's history is specifically between Thaloria's overwhelming technological specialty and the Yuurakyo's extraordinary biological and martial strength.[1]

### Government
The internal political organization of the Kolo Empire remains largely unknown. Kolo held the title of Galactic Emperor and possessed enough authority to have a major interstellar power associated with him, but the story has not described a cabinet, nobility, military governorships, succession system or formal social hierarchy beneath him.[1]
Accordingly, the Empire should not currently be described as a confirmed meritocracy, warrior caste system or society where rank was determined exclusively by combat. Those concepts may fit the martial nature of the Yuurakyo themselves, but they have not been established as the governing structure of Kolo's entire civilization.

### Aftermath and Historical Consequences
The extermination of the Yuurakyo gave Thaloria the victory she required in the immediate conflict, but Velnorah later considered it one of her mother's most catastrophic strategic decisions.[1]
Thaloria's subsequent galactic dominance caused her civilization to encounter enemies operating on a vastly greater scale. Against those beings, Thaloria and Velnorah themselves were capable of fighting, but the overwhelming majority of their subordinates were not. The Yuurakyo, whose entire racial development revolved around combat and whose warriors had already proven capable of threatening [[Eldoria]]'s most advanced forces, would have been the Thaloria Dynasty's best available military population for confronting those future enemies.[1]
Velnorah therefore concluded that exterminating them had ultimately helped doom her own civilization. Thaloria had destroyed the greatest military obstacle to her rise, only to later discover that the same warriors could have become an invaluable defense against the much greater threats encountered after her ascension.[1]
Historical Irony
The Yuurakyo were simultaneously the greatest obstacle to Thaloria's galactic conquest and, in Velnorah's assessment, the greatest military asset her civilization could have possessed against the enemies that came afterward.[1]

### Legacy
Although comparatively little information has been given about Kolo's civilization itself, the Empire occupies an important place in the history of [[Eldoria]] because it exposed the first major limitation of Thaloria's approach to conquest. Technology could grant overwhelming superiority against conventional societies, but it did not automatically invalidate races whose biology and martial potential had developed to comparable cosmic scales.[1]
The conflict also demonstrated the lengths to which Thaloria was willing to go when confronted with an obstacle she could not defeat efficiently. Instead of continuing an increasingly costly conventional war, she studied the enemy species until she understood enough of its biology to attack the race at its weakest systemic point. The result simultaneously destroyed Kolo's greatest military advantage, enabled Thaloria's rise to galactic supremacy and planted the conditions for the future vulnerability of her own Empire.[1]

### Trivia
- The vast majority of information about the Kolo Empire is provided retrospectively by [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]] in Chapter 892 rather than through scenes directly depicting Kolo's reign.[1]
- The Yuurakyo were referred to as the assassins of [[Galactic Emperor Kolo]] within Velnorah's Sector.[1]
- One Yuurakyo warrior was dangerous enough that capturing him alive cost Thaloria several high-ranking soldiers and resulted in damage to her own armor despite extensive preparation and technological support.[1]
- The extreme genetic instability observed in adult Yuurakyo was not completely natural. It resulted from a specialized enhancement serum administered during their coming-of-age ceremony.[1]
- The serum's effects were not hereditary, explaining why the Yuurakyo child produced in Thaloria's laboratory was considerably weaker than his parents before receiving any equivalent enhancement.[1]
- Thaloria initially wanted to reproduce the Yuurakyo's power rather than exterminate them. Their destruction became possible only after years of failed cloning, reproductive and racial-transformation experiments revealed a species-specific vulnerability.[1]
- Velnorah later regarded the extermination as strategically disastrous because the Yuurakyo could have provided the combat force necessary to oppose the much stronger enemies encountered after the Thaloria Dynasty expanded beyond its original galactic environment.[1]
