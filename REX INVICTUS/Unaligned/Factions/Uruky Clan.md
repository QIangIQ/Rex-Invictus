Uruky Clan is one of the oldest, wealthiest and most influential Werewolf Clans of Samar. It is a hereditary family of Alpha [[Werewolves]] led by its ancient Patriarch, [[Leônidas Uruky]], who spent centuries expanding the Clan's wealth, numbers and reputation until it became powerful enough to directly rival the considerably older [[Lykos Clan]].[2]
Unlike the Lykos Clan, whose extraordinary status is heavily associated with its unusually powerful ancestral Bloodline, Clan Uruky's prominence was largely built through Leônidas' personal leadership and the development of generations of powerful Alpha descendants.[2] By the time [[Victor Elderblood]] visited Samar, the Uruky family had become so large and secure that Leônidas had effectively retired from day-to-day leadership and devoted himself primarily to training his descendants.
The Clan briefly supported Fenir, the First Prince of Samar, during the Royal succession struggle. This allegiance ended after Zaion Uruky's actions nearly caused Victor to destroy the entire family. Leônidas subsequently withdrew all Uruky support from the First Prince, resumed direct leadership of the Clan and ordered the family to remain as far away from Victor as possible.[1]

### Overview
Clan Uruky is described as a long family of Alpha [[Werewolves]].[2] Through Leônidas' efforts, the family accumulated enough wealth, political influence and military strength to become one of Samar's most renowned Alpha Clans.
Its growth was particularly notable because Leônidas consciously wanted to build a family capable of standing alongside the [[Lykos Clan]]. In his youth, he had been attracted to both [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] and the exceptional strength of her lineage, even briefly considering becoming one of her partners. His pride ultimately prevented him from accepting a position as merely another member of Maya's enormous group of men, and he instead devoted himself to strengthening his own Clan.[2]
His efforts succeeded. By the modern era, Clan Uruky's wealth and reputation were considered sufficient to directly rival Clan Lykos, despite Lykos possessing an older and more exceptional hereditary lineage famous for producing Generals for the [[Lycaon|Werewolf King]].[2]

### Hierarchy
Clan Uruky follows the instinctive Alpha–Beta hierarchy common to Werewolf society, but the authority of its Patriarch is considerably more extensive than that of an ordinary pack leader.

#### Patriarch
[[Leônidas Uruky]] is the Patriarch and ultimate Alpha of the Clan.
Every Werewolf belonging to his family is considered one of his Betas in relation to him, even individuals who were naturally born as Alpha [[Werewolves]].[2] This creates a hierarchy in which a member can simultaneously be an Alpha by birth while remaining subordinate to the Clan Patriarch.
The enormous size of the Uruky family directly contributes to Leônidas' strength. The number of subordinate Wolves connected to him increases the power available to the Patriarch, making Clan growth beneficial both politically and supernaturally.[2]
Leônidas also maintains a supernatural connection to the Wolves operating beneath his authority. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] isolated the Uruky mansion and caused all sounds from the surrounding family members to disappear, Leônidas realized that they had not been killed because his connections to his Betas remained intact and his own power had not diminished.[2]

#### Clan Leader
By Chapter 737, Leônidas had largely retired from active administration and left the leadership of Clan Uruky to a younger generation while he concentrated on training descendants.[2]
The identity of this temporary Clan Leader was not revealed.
Following the Zaion incident, Leônidas concluded that the current leadership had become too incompetent and announced that he would personally resume the position of Clan Leader. He ordered the incumbent leader to immediately step down and declared that anyone wishing to contest his return could challenge him directly for the title.[1]
This effectively reunited the roles of Patriarch and active Clan Leader beneath Leônidas.

#### Alpha Bloodline
Clan Uruky descends from a long hereditary line of Alpha [[Werewolves]].[2]
Numerous members of the family are therefore naturally born as Alphas. These individuals retain the biological advantages of Alpha Werewolves but remain beneath Leônidas within the Clan's internal pack hierarchy.
The Clan's Alpha lineage is one of the principal reasons it became an important political and military family within Samar.

### History

#### Rise of Clan Uruky
[[Leônidas Uruky]] was born into an already established family of Alpha [[Werewolves]], but the Clan had not yet achieved the reputation it possessed during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s visit to Samar.[2]
Through centuries of effort, Leônidas dramatically expanded the family's wealth, reputation and strength. His goal was ambitious enough that Clan Uruky eventually became capable of directly rivaling the prestigious [[Lykos Clan]].[2]
By the time Leônidas reached more than a thousand years of age, the Clan had become large and powerful enough that few forces on Samar could casually threaten it. Satisfied with the family's stability, he gradually withdrew from ordinary administrative work and allowed younger Werewolves to manage its daily affairs while he concentrated on training his descendants.[2]

#### Alliance with the First Prince
At some point before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s visit to Samar, Clan Uruky supported Fenir, the First Prince, during the political competition surrounding succession to the Werewolf throne.[1]
The full terms of this relationship were never explained. However, [[Zaion Uruky]] believed that delivering [[Natalia Elderblood née Alioth]] to the First Prince would benefit his plans, indicating that at least some members of the Clan were actively attempting to advance Fenir's interests.[2]
The alliance ultimately ended because of Zaion's actions.

#### Zaion Incident
Shortly after [[Victor Elderblood]] entered Samar as a protected guest of Anderson, [[Zaion Uruky]] observed Victor's group from a distance and became interested in Natalia.[3]
Zaion intended to capture her and eventually deliver her to the First Prince, while also wanting Natalia personally and intending to exploit the valuable abilities of [[Clan Alioth]]. Unknown to Zaion, Natalia was Victor's wife.[2]
[[Icarus Uruky]] quickly recognized the danger. Because Clan Alioth had historically been protected by [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]], [[Icarus]] initially believed that the mysterious man accompanying Natalia might be a shapeshifted Vampire King and warned Zaion that his actions could provoke a war.[2]
Leônidas overheard the conversation and experienced an instinctive premonition that something was seriously wrong.
Before the Clan could resolve the matter internally, Victor personally entered the Uruky residence.
At approximately the same time, [[Volk Fenrir]] publicly announced throughout Eclipse Ventus that Victor Alucard, King of Hell, was a protected guest of Second Prince Anderson and ordered Samar's [[Werewolves]] not to antagonize him.[2]
Victor isolated the room containing Leônidas, Zaion and Icarus from the rest of the Clan and revealed what he had perceived from Zaion's intentions toward Natalia.
Victor originally intended to annihilate Zaion and everyone closely connected to him. His decision changed after examining Leônidas' character. Despite understanding the enormous difference in strength between them, Leônidas remained willing to fight Victor and sacrifice himself if doing so became necessary to protect his family.[2]
Victor respected this determination and offered the Patriarch a deal: surrender Zaion and the rest of Clan Uruky would be spared.
Leônidas accepted.
Although the decision violated his strong Werewolf belief that Wolves should remain united, he refused to sacrifice an entire Clan for one descendant responsible for creating the crisis.[2][1]
Before surrendering Zaion, Leônidas demanded assurance that Victor would leave the remainder of the family unharmed. Victor created a Demonic Contract intended to guarantee the Clan's safety so long as its members did not attack his family.[2]
Leônidas signed the contract with his Blood and removed Icarus from the room, leaving Zaion behind.

#### Abandonment of the Uruky Mansion
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] subjected Zaion to an extremely prolonged punishment inside the Clan residence.
His screams echoed through the mansion strongly enough that Leônidas could no longer tolerate remaining there. He ordered every member of Clan Uruky to evacuate immediately and instructed them to remove everything valuable to the family before abandoning the building permanently.[1]
Leônidas refused to continue living in a residence stained by the blood and suffering of his grandson.
The Clan relocated toward its Eastern Mansion in the capital, where Leônidas intended to gather all members of the family and their allies for a major meeting.[1]
The former main residence was consequently abandoned.

#### Political Reorganization
The Zaion incident caused Leônidas to reassess the entire direction of his Clan.
When [[Icarus]] asked whether they intended to retaliate against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Leônidas immediately rejected the idea and ordered every Uruky Werewolf to remain as far away from Victor as possible.[1]
He then ended Clan Uruky's political support for the First Prince:
"As of today, the Uruky Clan no longer supports the First Prince."[1]
The decision was particularly significant because Fenir was later revealed to have connections with [[New Dawn]], using the organization as part of his attempt to strengthen his position within Samar's succession struggle.[4]
Leônidas also determined that the younger generation of leaders had failed to protect the family from reckless political decisions. He therefore resumed direct command of Clan Uruky and ordered the current Clan Leader to step down immediately.[1]
When Icarus reacted with shock, Leônidas made his position clear: he would not allow Clan Uruky to be destroyed because of a spoiled Prince and incompetent leaders. Any Werewolf who wanted the position of Clan Leader would first have to defeat him personally.[1]

### Political Standing
Clan Uruky is one of Samar's major Alpha Werewolf families.
Its influence comes from a combination of:
- A long hereditary Alpha Werewolf lineage.[2]
- A large number of family members.
- The supernatural pack structure surrounding Leônidas.
- Considerable accumulated wealth.
- A reputation comparable to Clan Lykos in modern Samar.[2]
- Political connections within the Werewolf Kingdom.
- A large network of allies, important enough that Leônidas ordered both Clan members and allied groups to attend the post-Zaion meeting.[1]
Despite this standing, the encounter with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] demonstrated that Clan Uruky's influence remained limited when confronted by rulers operating far above Samar's ordinary Alpha hierarchy.
Victor himself compared exterminating Clan Uruky to destroying one of [[Nightingale]]'s major Noble Vampire Clans, such as [[Snow Clan]] or [[Fulger Clan]]. Such an action would represent the destruction of an important portion of the Werewolf Kingdom's national strength and could provoke a war between rulers.[1]

### Relationship with Clan Lykos
The [[Lykos Clan]] is the family most frequently used to contextualize Clan Uruky's status.
Clan Lykos possesses an exceptionally powerful ancestral Bloodline and historically produced numerous distinguished Generals for the [[Lycaon|Werewolf King]].[2] Leônidas was fascinated enough by the lineage that he once considered joining [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]]'s group of partners.
His pride prevented him from doing so.
Rather than joining Clan Lykos, Leônidas spent centuries strengthening his own family until Clan Uruky's wealth and reputation could directly rival the Lykos Clan.[2]
This rivalry should primarily be understood in terms of Clan prestige, wealth and reputation. The novel does not establish that an average Uruky Werewolf possesses the same hereditary potential as the exceptional descendants of [[Elizabeth Lykos]].

### Pack Structure and Abilities
Clan Uruky's supernatural structure is centered on its Patriarch.
- Patriarchal Pack Bond: Every member of Leônidas' family functions as one of his Betas within the Clan hierarchy, including natural Alpha [[Werewolves]].[2]
- Collective Empowerment: The number of subordinate Werewolves under the Patriarch directly increases Leônidas' personal power.[2]
- Pack Connection: Leônidas can perceive whether his connections to subordinate family members remain intact, allowing him to determine whether members of his Clan are still alive under certain circumstances.[2]
- Hereditary Alpha Werewolves: Clan Uruky possesses a long lineage capable of naturally producing Alpha Werewolves.[2]
- Werewolf Physiology: Members possess the standard racial characteristics of Werewolves, including superhuman physical ability, enhanced senses, regeneration, longevity and Werewolf transformation.
- Pack Hierarchy: The Patriarch's authority supersedes the individual Alpha status of younger family members.[2]
No unique mutation comparable to the Elizabeth Trait of Clan Lykos has been revealed for Clan Uruky.

### Former Affiliations

#### First Prince's Faction
Clan Uruky formerly supported Fenir, the First Prince of Samar.[1]
The alliance ended immediately after Zaion's actions nearly caused the family's destruction. Leônidas concluded that continued involvement with a reckless Prince and incompetent internal leadership presented an unacceptable risk to the Clan and formally withdrew all support.[1]

### Locations

#### Former Uruky Mansion
Clan Uruky's original primary residence in Eclipse Ventus was the location of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s confrontation with Leônidas, [[Icarus]] and Zaion.[2]
After Zaion's punishment filled the residence with his screams and blood, Leônidas ordered the entire building abandoned.[1]

#### Eastern Mansion
Following the abandonment of the original residence, Leônidas ordered Clan Uruky to relocate to its Eastern Mansion in the capital.[1]
The location was intended to serve as the Clan's immediate base while Leônidas reestablished direct control and summoned the family and its allies for a meeting.
