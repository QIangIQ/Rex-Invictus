#### Origin
[[Dragons Nest Pantheon]]
Elite warriors forged by the Dragon God Emperor himself, designed to serve as the ultimate instruments of war.
The Emperor's Soldiers are an artificial military race personally designed and created by [[Victor Elderblood]] to serve as the elite soldiers of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Rather than mass-producing a large number of comparatively weak troops, Victor deliberately chose to develop a smaller but vastly superior military species whose individual members could function as weapons of mass destruction, adapt to different battlefields and continue developing through accumulated experience.[1]
Although they outwardly resemble powerful Dragonoids, the Emperor's Soldiers are considerably more complex than ordinary members of that race. Victor constructed them as artificially stabilized chimeric Beings using a Dragonoid body as their foundation while incorporating selected characteristics from Humans, Angels, Demons, Faeries and Vampires. The decisive component allowing these otherwise incompatible traits to coexist is a minute fragment of Victor's own Soul, together with his Blessings and Essence.[1] Their first generation consisted of seven unique individuals personally created by Victor, later referred to as the Seven Originals or Imperial Commanders. Their Souls, experiences and development became the foundation from which the mass-produced Emperor's Soldiers were subsequently created.[2][3]
The Emperor's Soldiers should not be confused with the entirety of the Elderblood Empire's military. They form an exceptionally powerful elite military species and appear throughout the Empire's security and armed forces, but the later Imperial military also includes Dragons, Demons, Vampires, Gods, Leviathans, warships, artificial intelligences and numerous other races and specialized units. Likewise, Victor's original plan for the Seven Originals to become the basis of seven future Legions predates the much later formal Seven Legions, whose final organization developed through centuries of Imperial history and was not simply a one-to-one assignment of one Original to each Legion.[3][4]

### History

#### Creation
The project that eventually produced the Emperor's Soldiers began when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] sought to create military clones capable of carrying part of his power. He quickly encountered a fundamental problem: Victor's Soul, Blood and physical Essence had become so abnormal that ordinary Beings could not safely contain them. His wives could accept portions of him because their existences were already deeply connected to his own, while his daughters had been born directly from his Essence, but an unrelated artificial soldier attempting to contain Victor's Soul would normally die from the incompatibility.[1]
Victor therefore abandoned the idea of simply placing his power inside an ordinary artificial body and instead began designing a species specifically capable of supporting it. He considered whether his Empire required enormous numbers of merely competent soldiers or fewer specialized elites. Since ordinary military personnel could be recruited from the many races already serving the Empire, Victor chose the latter approach and decided that soldiers personally representing him would be the best of the best.[1]
Human adaptability became one of the key components of the design. Victor recognized the extraordinary adaptability bestowed upon Humanity by [[Heavenly Father]], although he intentionally avoided making the trait excessively powerful because an uncontrolled rate of adaptation could itself overwhelm the artificial body. Human adaptability would instead assist the integration of the otherwise incompatible racial components and, most importantly, help the soldier's Soul gradually accommodate Victor's own Soul fragment.[1]
A Dragonoid body became the physical foundation. Its connection to Victor made spiritual compatibility easier, while its naturally powerful physiology provided the robust body necessary for an elite soldier. Victor then incorporated carefully selected Angelic, Demonic and Faerie characteristics, followed by the regenerative and metamorphic properties of Vampires. Once the basic design was complete, Victor inserted 0.0000001% of his own Soul into each prototype. The fragment was intended to grow together with the soldier rather than remaining permanently at its original size. Victor then personally bestowed his Blessings upon the newly created Souls, completing the basic structure of the Emperor's Soldiers.[1]
The first two Souls placed into creation chambers were [[Clare Bloodhunter]] and John. Five additional Souls were created immediately afterward, producing the Seven Originals. Victor specifically engineered their DNA so that it could later be replicated by authorized members of the Empire while including an unspecified security measure against unauthorized attempts to steal or reproduce their genetic material. He also ordered that their future clone-descendants possess different appearances, ensuring that mass production would not reduce them to visually identical copies.[1]

#### Awakening of the Seven Originals
The Seven Originals spent approximately ten years developing inside an accelerated environment while receiving enormous quantities of implanted knowledge. When they finally awakened, they possessed physically mature bodies despite being only ten years old from their own perspective. At that stage they stood roughly two meters tall in their restrained humanoid forms and possessed enough raw power that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] internally considered even an accidental mistake from one of them potentially capable of erasing a city.[2]
The implanted memories consisted of information taken from historical heroes and previous wars. The Seven experienced military campaigns, supernatural conflicts, tactics, strategies and accumulated knowledge without confusing those memories with their own identities. Victor considered this distinction extremely important: possessing the memories of heroes did not automatically make his creations heroes or competent warriors. The memories provided information and theoretical experience, while genuine combat, individual decision-making and practical training were still necessary.[2]
Upon awakening, the Seven immediately recognized Victor as their Creator and Emperor. Victor, however, did not intend them to exist as mindless tools. He considered them his Creations and Children as well as his soldiers, telling them that they were intended to become his [[Spears|Spear]] and Shield and that his [[Will Solace|Will]] would be exercised through them. For that reason he believed they deserved the best training and developmental resources available within the Empire.[3]

#### Tower of Nightmare Training
Main article: Tower of Nightmare
[[Scathach]] subsequently brought the Seven into the Elderblood family's personal Tower of Nightmare. Rather than placing all of them together, the Tower generated separate versions of the same large-scale war scenario for each soldier. The scenario involved two rival Empires—one technologically advanced but physically weak and another possessing enormous physical strength but inferior intelligence—being simultaneously invaded by creatures from another Dimension. Each Original was independently ordered to pacify the two warring civilizations and eliminate the interdimensional threat.[5]
The exercise deliberately provided almost no conventional preparation. Scathach believed that possessing implanted memories of warfare was not equivalent to experiencing genuine danger. She therefore immediately placed them into war so that they could experience injury, survival pressure, uncertainty, tactical decision-making and the consequences of their choices. The ordinary forces of the [[Elderblood Empire]] possessed their own alternate Nightmare Tower for training, but [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] ordered that the Seven receive access to the family's superior personal Tower because he wanted his prototypes trained with the best resources available.[5]
The scenario also demonstrated that the Seven were not mentally identical despite originating from the same program. Conan immediately entered battle and attacked whatever stood in his path; Laura approached the technologically advanced civilization and attempted interaction rather than immediately beginning a massacre; Kiana waited while apparently developing her own plan before disappearing at extreme speed; Caelus chose to observe before acting; Kaleb moved directly toward the ruler of the strength-based Empire; John initially prioritized reconnaissance before abandoning that plan to save a child; and Clare approached the conflict as a conqueror. These differences demonstrated the independent personalities Victor intentionally preserved in his soldiers.[5]
Years of subjective combat experience accumulated within the Tower while considerably less time passed outside. Velnorah continuously analyzed the memories generated by the Seven and used them to improve the later soldiers. The Originals therefore served not merely as commanders but as a living prototype program: every mistake, successful tactic, physical adaptation and battlefield experience could be studied and selectively transferred to later generations.[6]

#### Dragonoid Foundation
Despite the number of racial components involved, the Soldiers remain fundamentally Dragonoid-based. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Essence mixes their characteristics together around this foundation, causing an uninformed observer to identify them simply as Dragonoids.[1] Their natural Draconic features are not uniform. Depending upon the development of the individual Soul, a Soldier may possess Draconic eyes, scales, horns, teeth or other traits, while another member of the same race may display entirely different characteristics.
Their intended unrestricted combat body can reach approximately four meters in height. Victor considered this larger physiology necessary because of the enormous number of supernatural characteristics contained within them, while their agility remained extremely high despite the increased mass.[1] Their Vampire-derived metamorphosis prevents this size from becoming a practical weakness, allowing them to assume smaller forms where necessary.

#### Soul Fragment
The defining component of an Emperor's Soldier is the portion of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul incorporated into its existence. Victor initially placed approximately 0.0000001% of his Soul into each design and intended the fragment to increase as the Soldier developed.[1] This was not merely an Energy source. Victor's Soul acted as part of the mechanism that allowed the numerous racial factors to coexist without the artificial Being collapsing from incompatibility.
The effects became clearer during the first Tower training. [[Clare Bloodhunter]] unexpectedly developed characteristics beyond those of an ordinary Dragonoid, including functional Draconic wings and a violet Fire breath carrying traces of Angelic Light and Demonic Darkness. [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] concluded that Victor's Soul fragment—particularly the Eldritch component of his existence—was producing mutations among the Soldiers. Rather than behaving like simple Dragonoid hybrids, they resembled chimeras capable of expressing beneficial traits from the races incorporated into their design. Victor's Blessings simultaneously assisted in stabilizing those mutations.[5]
These mutations are individual rather than a fixed universal powerset. Clare demonstrating wings and mixed Light/Dark Fire does not establish that every Emperor's Soldier possesses precisely the same manifestation; Chapter 1066 explicitly establishes that their supernatural and Draconic characteristics can differ according to the development of each Soul.[1][5]

#### Regeneration and Metamorphosis
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] intentionally incorporated Vampire regeneration and metamorphosis rather than relying exclusively upon Dragonoid durability. Their regenerative properties make them significantly harder to permanently incapacitate, while metamorphosis provides strategic flexibility by allowing a Soldier to change its physical dimensions.[1]
Victor considered this essential because a four-meter-tall elite soldier would be intimidating but impractical during infiltration, diplomacy and other delicate operations. He wanted a military race capable of functioning in virtually any scenario rather than one designed exclusively for open warfare.[1]

#### Adaptability
Human adaptability forms another central component of their design. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] deliberately selected a controlled version of Humanity's adaptive potential because it could help the artificial bodies accommodate changes without becoming unstable.[1] This quality is reinforced by their training system: experiences gathered by the Seven Originals are analyzed, refined and used to improve future generations, causing the race itself to develop through a continuous cycle rather than remaining identical to its first production model.[6]

### The Seven Originals
The Seven Originals are the first complete Emperor's Soldiers personally created by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Unlike the later mass-produced soldiers, they possess individually constructed Souls and served as the prototypes from which the clone generations were derived.[1][2] Victor later called them his seven Imperial Commanders and initially intended future Legions to develop from the experiences they accumulated.[3]
The Seven are not canonically numbered from one to seven by rank. Clare possesses the designation C-000 and John was designated [[John-000]], but the narrative does not establish the commonly used numbered list of the Seven as a formal hierarchy.[1]

#### John's Designation
Unlike Clare, Laura, Kiana, Caelus and Kaleb, John and Conan were not given surnames. John's designation became [[John-000]]. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] established that each time John died and returned, the numerical portion of his designation would increase, simultaneously recording the number of deaths and functioning as a symbol of service and honor to the Emperor.[1]
This mechanic should not be interpreted as evidence that John alone possesses resurrection. Later chapters establish that death is not necessarily permanent for the mass-produced soldiers either; John's numbered designation is simply a unique method of recording his reincarnations.[1][6]

### Abilities and Characteristics

#### Enhanced Physical Abilities
All Emperor's Soldiers possess extraordinary physical characteristics derived primarily from their Dragonoid foundation. Even before completing their advanced training, the Seven possessed enormous strength, durability, speed and agility.[2] Their large natural bodies do not make them slow; [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly designed their four-meter forms to retain agility comparable to much smaller supernatural races.[1]
There are nevertheless substantial individual differences. Conan, for example, was physically stronger than the other Originals during their early development, while John compensated through a combination of strength and technique and Clare through technical superiority.[6] The race should therefore not be presented as possessing completely identical statistics.

#### Supernatural Senses and Draconic Traits
The Soldier's Dragonoid foundation can produce Draconic eyes, horns, scales, teeth and other physical characteristics. The manifestation depends heavily upon the individual Soul rather than following a mandatory template.[1] Their supernatural perception is correspondingly advanced, and later soldiers were capable of performing large-scale security and military duties against Divine-level opponents.[7]

#### Regeneration
The Emperor's Soldiers possess Vampire-derived regeneration deliberately retained by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] during their creation.[1] This regenerative ability operates alongside their naturally durable Dragonoid bodies, making the race substantially harder to incapacitate than ordinary humanoid soldiers.

#### Metamorphosis / Shapeshifting
Their Vampire component also grants metamorphosis. The Soldiers can reduce or otherwise alter their natural physical size, allowing them to function in environments where their approximately four-meter unrestricted forms would be impractical.[1]

#### Racial Adaptation
Human adaptability allows the Soldiers to adjust to changes in their bodies and supports the continued integration and evolution of their mixed racial characteristics.[1] Their future generations are additionally improved through accumulated battlefield data, making adaptation both an individual ability and a feature of the species' production system.[6]

#### Mixed Racial Manifestations
Because of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul fragment, the racial components within the Soldiers may combine in unexpected ways. Clare provides the clearest early example: despite being based upon a Dragonoid body, she manifested functioning wings and produced a violet Fire breath containing detectable traces of Angelic Light and Demonic Darkness.[5]
[[Scathach]] concluded that the Soldiers were closer to stabilized chimeras than ordinary Dragonoids because Victor's Eldritch Soul fragment allowed beneficial aspects of several races to manifest without the catastrophic instability such combinations would normally produce.[5]
Individual manifestations vary. Consequently, a power demonstrated by one Original should not automatically be attributed to every member unless later chapters establish it as a universal racial ability.

#### Blessings
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] personally placed his Blessings upon the Souls of the Seven Originals during their creation.[1] These Blessings contribute to their development and stabilization and were important enough that [[Scathach]] specifically considered being Blessed by the Emperor an advantage when beginning their Tower training.[5]

#### Implanted Knowledge
The Originals awakened possessing enormous quantities of artificial memories based upon heroes and wars from the past. These memories contain tactics, strategy, intelligence and practical information concerning conflicts of numerous scales.[2] Importantly, the recipients recognize that these experiences are not their own and therefore retain their individual identities.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] subsequently ordered the Seven's own experiences to be recorded so that later clones could inherit carefully selected sets of combat experience, political expertise and wisdom. He explicitly rejected the idea of creating soldiers incapable of thinking independently.[3]

#### Resurrection / Reincarnation
Death is not necessarily permanent for the Emperor's Soldiers. John was specifically created with a designation that increases every time he dies and returns, while Velnorah later discusses the deaths of mass-produced clones as events that are generally not permanent.[1][6]
Their connection to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s system of artificial Souls therefore allows the Empire to preserve valuable soldiers and the experience gathered through their deaths rather than treating every casualty as the permanent loss of an elite unit.

### Training and Artificial Experience
The Emperor's Soldiers are deliberately created with an enormous informational advantage but are not considered combat-ready merely because they possess inherited memories. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] distinguished between knowing the experiences of historical warriors and personally developing the instincts necessary to use them.[2]
The Seven Originals therefore functioned as the prototype training generation. Their years inside the Tower were recorded and analyzed by Velnorah, who separated useful memories and experiences for inheritance by the later clones. Their experiences using Draconic bodies were considered particularly valuable because subsequent generations could use that information as a developmental reference.[6]
Velnorah initially estimated that approximately fifty years could produce elite soldiers suitable for almost any situation, but the extreme Time manipulation and resources available to the [[Elderblood Empire]] could reduce the effective preparation period to approximately three years or potentially less.[6] This process was designed as an ongoing system rather than a single finished generation: each new group could generate additional information that would then improve those created afterward.

### Mass Production
The Seven Originals were created as templates rather than as the final numerical strength of the program. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] engineered their DNA for authorized replication and specifically instructed the researchers to vary the appearances of future soldiers so that the resulting Beings would possess visual individuality despite sharing prototype genetics.[1]
The Originals' experiences were recorded into an Orb and separated into appropriate memory packages. Later soldiers could therefore awaken with selected experience in combat, politics and decision-making while continuing to develop their own personalities.[3] Velnorah regarded this as a continuous refinement process: experiences produced by one generation would reveal weaknesses or useful adaptations that could improve the next.[6]
By Chapter 1077, 25,000 Imperial Soldiers had already been produced and deployed throughout [[Nightingale]] as a security perimeter.[7] This figure is a confirmed population snapshot from that particular period and should not be treated as their final number. Victor later expected his Soldier population to increase into the millions after several more years of production.[8]

### Operators
Operators are support-oriented derivatives developed alongside the frontline Emperor's Soldiers. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] recognized that an army of extremely powerful combatants still required personnel capable of coordinating them, monitoring operations and handling more support-focused responsibilities.[3]
[[Laura Bloodhunter]] was selected as the principal foundation for the Operator project. Victor initially ordered that Laura's Blood and memories be used as the base for a separate support-oriented clone population.[3] Velnorah later concluded that Laura's natural kindness and ability to calmly understand others made her particularly appropriate for the role. She proposed adding characteristics from her own technological people as well as additional Elven and Faerie traits. Victor agreed with strengthening the calmer elements while retaining enough of Laura's influence to prevent the Operators themselves from becoming excessively aggressive or addicted to warfare.[6]
Operators were designed to care sufficiently about their Soldiers to coordinate them effectively without becoming psychologically incapable of accepting combat losses. Later Imperial field deployments demonstrate that local officers could defer larger decisions to Operators monitoring the operational environment.[7]

### Command Structure
The Emperor's Soldiers possess a structured military hierarchy rather than functioning as independent mobs.
At the top is [[Victor Elderblood]], their Creator and Emperor, whose orders possess absolute institutional authority over the force. [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] became the principal military authority beneath him. Victor originally stated that the Seven Originals would serve as Imperial Commanders directly under [[Scathach]],[3] while later troops explicitly address her as Supreme General.[7]
Ordinary Soldiers are nevertheless capable of exercising limited command authority. During the [[Nightingale]] deployment, the highest-ranking officer in a local area could issue minor orders provided those commands did not conflict with Victor's standing instructions. More consequential decisions could be referred to Operators observing the operation.[7]

#### Relationship with the Seven Legions
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s initial statement that the Seven Originals would become the first of seven future Legions should be understood as an early military plan.[3] The [[Elderblood Empire]] subsequently developed for thousands of years and eventually formalized seven large military organizations with distinct histories, creators, functions and command structures.[4]
The mature Seven Legions therefore should not simply be assigned to Clare, Laura, Kiana, Conan, Caelus, Kaleb and John in order. The clearest confirmed later connection is John, who serves as the current commander of the First Legion, the Spartan Legion, with Rose, Eleonor or Kali capable of replacing him depending upon circumstances.[4]
The later Legions also contain many units that are not Emperor's Soldiers, including Dragons, assassins, telepaths, scientists, Witches, automatons, executioners, spaceships and numerous other specialized forces. The Emperor's Soldiers are therefore an elite military race operating within the broader Imperial military rather than being synonymous with all seven Legions.[4]

Their equipment, discipline and ability to conceal their presence further increased the threat presented by large formations.

#### Threat in Numbers
The Soldiers become substantially more dangerous when operating in organized groups. Vlad initially regarded two guards as opponents that would require some seriousness from him and became increasingly disturbed when he discovered larger formations possessing virtually identical levels of strength.[7] The existence of tens of thousands of Divine-level artificial soldiers consequently represented a military force fundamentally different from traditional supernatural armies composed mostly of weaker inhabitants commanded by a small number of Gods.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] nevertheless remained dissatisfied. He designed the Soldiers with the eventual Upper Sectors in mind, where Primordial God-level existences were significantly less exceptional, and therefore continued planning ways to further evolve both the ordinary units and the Seven Originals.[9]

### Loyalty and Individuality
The Emperor's Soldiers possess exceptional loyalty toward [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], whom the Seven Originals recognized from birth as both their Creator and Emperor.[2] Their connection is reinforced by Victor's Soul fragment, their inherited memories, their education and the culture deliberately established around their creation.
This loyalty does not make them unintelligent automatons. Victor explicitly rejected soldiers unable to think for themselves and ordered that future generations inherit combat experience, political expertise and wisdom rather than only obedience.[3] Their first Tower test confirms substantial psychological variation between the Seven Originals, whose approaches ranged from observation and diplomacy to conquest and immediate aggression.[5]
The mass-produced Soldiers likewise possess stable thoughts and Souls rather than behaving as empty puppets. By Chapter 1079, the clone project was considered successful precisely because their Souls, Bloodlines and thought processes were functioning normally.[9]
Their loyalty can nevertheless appear extreme to outsiders. Soldiers deployed around [[Nightingale]] treated Vlad, the First Vampire Progenitor and King of Nightingale, only with the minimum respect required by Victor's orders. Their primary allegiance remained with the Emperor and the Elderblood military hierarchy rather than the authority of a foreign allied monarch.[7]

### Military Purpose
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] created the Emperor's Soldiers for situations requiring powerful, intelligent and controllable elite personnel rather than indiscriminate destruction. This distinction became clearer as his military expanded. Massive creatures such as the Leviathans could be used as enormous invasion weapons against armies or planetary targets, whereas the Emperor's Soldiers were intended for precise operations requiring judgment, discipline and individual combat ability.[8]
Their known functions include direct combat, planetary security, elite protection, conquest, reconnaissance, infiltration and command. The adaptive nature of their bodies and equipment allows the same underlying race to operate in multiple environments rather than being restricted to a single military specialty.[1][5]
By the mature [[Elderblood Empire]], the Soldiers had become one element of a far larger military machine rather than the Empire's only armed force. Elite Soldiers appear among the kinds of units serving later Imperial Legions, while John himself eventually commands the First Legion.[4]

### Notes
- The Emperor's Soldiers are commonly described as Dragonoids because the Dragonoid Race forms their physical foundation and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Essence blends their other characteristics into it. However, their internal composition is considerably more complicated and [[Scathach]] later compares them more closely to stabilized chimeras.[1][5]
- The main racial formula described by Victor consists of the Dragonoid body and pride, Human adaptability and ingenuity, Angelic obedience and kindness, Demonic aggression and cruelty, and Faerie spirit/Nature affinity. Vampire regeneration and metamorphosis are explicitly added as further abilities rather than being listed as one of Victor's initial five principal ingredients.[1]
- The statement that each Soldier contains 0.0000001% of Victor's Soul refers to the fragment inserted during creation. Victor specifies that the fragment can grow over time.[1]
- The Seven Originals are not merely seven visually identical clones. They possess individual Souls, appearances, personalities, natural mutations and approaches to combat.[2][5]
- The later mass-produced Soldiers were intentionally given different appearances despite deriving from the Original templates.[1]
- The fixed specializations sometimes assigned to every Original should not be treated as established racial canon unless individually demonstrated. The chapters establish differences in personality and talent—such as Conan's physical strength, Clare's technique, Laura's kindness and John's balanced ability—but do not give every Original a rigid RPG-style combat class.[5][6]
- Laura is genuinely exceptional among the Seven because Victor used the Essence of [[Lucifer]], an Archangel, in her creation instead of the ordinary Angel Essence used for the other six Originals.[2]
- Clare's wings and mixed Light/Dark violet Fire demonstrate the potential of the Soldiers' chimeric biology, but should not automatically be treated as universal abilities possessed in precisely the same form by every Soldier.[5]
- The 25,000 Soldier figure comes from Chapter 1077, where they are explicitly stated to be deployed throughout [[Nightingale]]. Chapter 1080 instead contains Victor's projection that their numbers would eventually rise into the millions.[7][8]
- The ordinary Soldiers being High-Level God-level and the Commanders being capable of defeating a Primordial God are directly stated in Chapter 1079. Victor's desire to eventually make ordinary Soldiers Primordial God-level and awaken Divinities in them is a future developmental goal in that chapter, not their already completed state.[9]
- Victor's statement in Chapter 1068 concerning seven future Legions reflects his original plan for the Seven Originals. It should not be retroactively interpreted as meaning that each Original automatically commands one of the mature Seven Legions described thousands of years later. The later Legions developed separately through Imperial history and possess their own creators, commanders and specialized personnel.[3][4]
- John is the clearest confirmed connection between the Originals and the mature Legion system, eventually serving as commander of the Spartan Legion.[4]
- Although the term "clone" is frequently used during development, the Emperor's Soldiers are not literal copies of Victor. Their bodies and Souls were purpose-built by Victor, while later clones were replicated from the Seven Original templates.[1]
- Their extreme loyalty should not be confused with an inability to think independently. Victor explicitly wanted intelligent soldiers capable of judgment, and their earliest battlefield simulation demonstrates substantially different decisions and personalities among the Seven.[3][5]
