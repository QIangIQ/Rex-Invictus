Article Scope — This page documents the Blood God Religion itself: its faith, history, clergy, doctrines, laws, rites, membership, temples, education, religious military forces and development into the official religion of the Elderblood Empire. [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]], the Elderblood Empire, the Imperial Inquisition and other organizations are discussed only where they directly affect the Religion.
The Blood God Religion, originally referred to as the Blood God Cult, is the organized religion centered upon the worship of Victor Elderblood, first revered by its followers as the Blood God and later increasingly addressed as the God Emperor or Emperor God. The faith began without Victor deliberately founding a religion around himself. Devotion initially emerged among people personally saved, inspired or transformed by him and was subsequently organized by his family and followers into a formal multiracial religious institution.[1][2]
The Religion originated from the spontaneous devotion of [[Bruna Elderblood]], whom Victor later recognized as his earliest genuine worshiper. Her faith influenced several of Victor's maids, while [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]] eventually organized the expanding movement after it became too large to remain an informal collection of devotees.[2] [[Roxanne Elderblood]], [[Aline Elderblood Valefar]], Victor's wives and several other followers subsequently transformed that movement into an institution possessing clergy, laws, educational systems, intelligence networks, temples, military personnel and an extensive reward structure.[3][2]
Victor initially remained largely outside the Religion's administration. That changed when he personally appeared before its Acolytes, formally accepted their worship, declared the movement a home and family, appointed [[Valeria Alekerth]] as his High Priestess and created Victorius Aeternus as a sanctuary for the faithful.[4][5]
Over subsequent centuries the Religion expanded far beyond its original Earth-based cult. Its congregations reached [[Nightingale]], Hell and other worlds under Victor's authority; its clergy gained access to supernatural transportation and Imperial resources; its temple network became openly public; and its military arms became capable of operating on a planetary scale.[6][7] By Chapter 1163, the Religion is explicitly described as the official religion of the Elderblood Empire, with Valeria remaining its head and religious combat institutions operating beneath her authority.[8]

### History
ORIGIN
Bruna's Devotion
The earliest foundation of the faith came from [[Bruna Elderblood]]. A former nun, Bruna redirected the intense religious devotion that had defined her old life toward [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] after he rescued and transformed her. Her devotion spread to [[Maria Elderblood]], [[Eve Elderblood]] and [[Roberta Elderblood]], eventually influencing members of the Noble Vampire families surrounding Victor.[2]
EARLY ERA
Organization of the Faith
As Victor's reputation grew, worship spread beyond his immediate maids. [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]] began organizing the movement, while Ruby, Violet, Sasha, Roxanne, Aline, [[Vine]], Helena and Lily became involved in its administration, recruitment or expansion.[2]
CH. 709
The Blood God Cult
The faith became publicly recognizable following the worldwide Demon invasion. Human attitudes toward Victor were sharply divided: some blamed the new King of Hell for the catastrophe, while others regarded him as the Being who had helped save Humanity.[1]
CH. 709
Valeria's Cell
One early English cell included [[Valeria Alekerth]] and followers associated with Roxanne. The group used condemned criminals in a blood ritual that summoned Aline Elderblood Valefar from Hell. Among the sacrifices was [[Jonathan Eric Gonzales]], the murderer of Valeria's daughter Vanessa.[1]
CH. 710
Expansion Across Earth
Roxanne and Aline systematically expanded the Religion through regions with limited Divine influence. Its network grew into an enormous intelligence and social-influence system with followers and sympathizers spread across multiple countries and even within organizations associated with the Vatican.[3]
CH. 710
Seven Archbishops
Seven Archbishops belonging to different races were selected to administer the growing faith. The separate position of High Priestess remained vacant, with Roxanne identifying Valeria as the preferred candidate.[3]
CH. 711–712
Victor Accepts the Religion
Victor visited the Religion personally, examined its doctrines, laws, education and reward systems, and accepted the organization rather than dismantling it. He appeared before the assembled faithful and reinforced the principle that effort, loyalty and useful work would be rewarded.[2][4]
CH. 712
Appointment of the High Priestess
Victor personally selected Valeria Alekerth as High Priestess. He formed a supernatural contract with her, strengthened her body, maintained her youth and granted her the ability to access power and Demonic servants associated with him.[4]
CH. 712–713
Victorius Aeternus
Victor rejected the idea that his Religion should merely be a gathering place. He created an entire settlement around the faithful and declared the institution a home and family. The city was subsequently named Victorius Aeternus and became the Religion's holy capital.[4][5]
CH. 826
Staff of the High Priestess
Valeria eventually received a powerful Staff produced through work involving Victor, [[Thanatos]] and [[Hephaestus]]. The artifact became both a weapon and a symbol of her office.[9]
CH. 876–877
Paradise and the Faithful Dead
Victor established afterlife territories associated with those serving him. His most exclusive Paradise was intended for individuals displaying exceptional loyalty, while broader afterlife arrangements were created for other faithful and allied populations.[10][11]
CH. 986
Outer and Inner Circles
The enormous Religion became divided into an Outer Circle containing the majority of ordinary worshipers and an Inner Circle composed of highly devoted supernatural followers. The Outer Circle was approximately 70% female and 30% male at the time described, while the Inner Circle consisted entirely of women from multiple races.[12]
CH. 1021
Valeria's Reward
Victor resurrected [[Vanessa Alekerth]] as a reward for Valeria's extraordinary service, demonstrating the extreme end of the Religion's merit doctrine: loyalty and contribution could be rewarded with far more than wealth or rank.[13]
CH. 1022–1024
Transworld Church
The Religion possessed an administrative headquarters on a floating island above Velnorah, supernatural security systems and interworld mobility. Victor's enormous public Dragon manifestation then triggered an unprecedented missionary campaign across Earth using television, radio, the internet and conventional preaching.[6][14]
CH. 1085
Imperial Church and Battle Sisters
The Religion had become an openly established global institution possessing monumental temples, Bishops, approximately one thousand mobilized Battle Sisters in one major operation and clergy drawn from multiple races. Its temples publicly represented recognized Dragon Goddesses while maintaining Victor as the supreme focus of worship.[7]
CH. 1163+
Official Religion of the Empire
The Blood God Religion is explicitly identified as the official religion of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. High Priestess Valeria remains its head, and religious or Faith-based Imperial combat institutions—including the Imperial Inquisition and associated warriors—operate under her authority or coordination.[8]

[[Bruna Elderblood]] is the earliest identifiable source of the faith that eventually became the Blood God Religion.
Before meeting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Bruna had devoted herself to the Church. Her previous faith failed to protect her when she was abducted and abused, while Victor personally rescued her, allowed her to take revenge upon those responsible and offered her an entirely new existence.
The strength of Bruna's religious personality did not disappear after becoming a Vampire. It changed its object.
Her devotion shifted toward Victor.[2]
Victor later identified Bruna as his first true devotee, making her the closest thing the Religion possesses to a foundational saint or first believer even though she did not administratively create the later church herself.

### The Blood God Cult
The Religion's first major public incarnation developed in the aftermath of the worldwide Demonic invasion.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had become the new King of [[Biblical Hell]], creating a severe public-relations problem. Although Victor helped end the crisis, many Humans associated him with the destruction simply because he had become ruler of the same realm from which the invading Demons originated.[1]
Others interpreted events very differently.
To them, Victor was a supernatural Being who had fought directly against the forces threatening Humanity.
These people began treating him as a savior and deity.

#### The English Cell
One early group in England included [[Valeria Alekerth]] and women connected to Roxanne.
The group captured criminals judged irredeemable and used their blood in a ritual that summoned Aline from Hell.[1]
Among the condemned was [[Jonathan Eric Gonzales]], who had raped, murdered and dismembered Valeria's daughter Vanessa.
The early ritual established several motifs that continued appearing within the Religion:
BloodRitual medium associated with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
JudgmentSevere punishment of individuals considered irredeemably wicked.
ProtectionVictor as protector and avenger of his followers.
HellReligious access to Demons serving Victor.
The practice should not be interpreted as evidence that human sacrifice became mandatory daily worship.
The later Religion uses prayers, offerings, temples, ritual initiations, incense, Blessings and conventional clergy alongside supernatural blood rites.[7]

### Expansion on Earth
Roxanne and Aline transformed the small religious movement into a global network.
Regions lacking strong direct influence from established Gods proved particularly receptive. In some countries described during this period, more than ninety percent of women were said to be directly or indirectly influenced by the Religion.[3]
When supernatural women were included, Roxanne described the organization as possessing eyes and ears throughout the planet.[3]

#### Intelligence Network
The early Religion was simultaneously a faith network and intelligence system.
Its followers provided information from:
- ordinary Human governments;
- supernatural communities;
- religious organizations;
- political institutions;
- and even circles connected to the Vatican.[3]
Some female Angels were willing to provide information despite their association with Heaven.[3]
The Religion's intelligence function became more sophisticated with time rather than disappearing after its public expansion.

#### Public Image Campaign
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family understood that portraying him as an entirely benevolent God would not be credible.
He was:
- King of Hell;
- a Vampire Progenitor;
- openly violent toward enemies;
- associated with Blood and destruction;
- and increasingly feared throughout supernatural society.
Roxanne and Aline therefore sought to change the narrative rather than falsify it.
Victor would be presented as a Chaotic God containing both destructive and protective characteristics.[3]

#### Early Female Recruitment Strategy
The Religion's Earth expansion focused heavily upon women.
Aline argued that recruiting large numbers of women would also indirectly influence the men in their families and social circles.[3]
This was a recruitment strategy rather than a permanent prohibition against male believers.
By Chapter 986, the Religion's Outer Circle was approximately 70% female and 30% male.[12]

### Formal Establishment
By Chapter 711, the Blood God Religion had already spread throughout Hell, [[Nightingale]] and large portions of Earth.[2]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually visited one of its temples personally.
Hundreds of highly devoted worshipers gathered inside a hidden forest temple dominated by a gigantic silver statue depicting Victor in armor.[2]
Victor examined the Religion instead of automatically approving everything done in his name.
He learned about:
- its laws;
- its education;
- its reward system;
- its clergy;
- its racial policies;
- its internal discipline;
- and its conception of him as a deity.
Victor ultimately accepted the institution.

### The High Priestess

#### Selection of Valeria
Roxanne originally argued that the High Priestess should be Human.
She selected [[Valeria Alekerth]] because Valeria combined extraordinary devotion with discipline, work ethic and an uncompromising attitude toward individuals she considered sinners.[3]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] ultimately agreed with the choice but emphasized another trait: humility.
When the possibility of being appointed High Priestess was raised, Valeria did not assume she deserved the position. She believed other candidates might be more qualified.[4]
Victor interpreted that reaction positively.
The person speaking in his name needed to prioritize the Religion over personal vanity.

#### Appointment
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] formally appointed Valeria in Chapter 712.[4]

### Victorius Aeternus
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] rejected the idea that the Religion should exist only as an organization whose members gathered periodically to pray.
He declared it a home and family.[4]
Using his control over Nature, Victor created a large settlement around the assembled faithful.
That settlement became Victorius Aeternus.[5]

The city was intended as:
- the residence of the High Priestess;
- a home for Archbishops and official members;
- a sanctuary for the faithful;
- a religious administrative center;

#### A Present God

### Doctrine

#### Merit and Reward
One of the Religion's strongest doctrines is:
FOUNDATIONAL PRINCIPLE
EFFORT MUST PRODUCE REWARD.
Members are expected to work, improve and produce useful results.
In return, the Religion can provide:
- knowledge;
- training;
- resources;
- strength;
- equipment;
- influence;
- rank;
- social advancement;
- protection;
- housing;
- and access to higher opportunities.[2]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] personally reinforced this doctrine when he rewarded Roxanne in front of the assembled faithful and told those who envied her reward to use that feeling as motivation to improve themselves.[4]
Envy and ambition are not automatically treated as sins.
What matters is what an individual does with them.

#### Capability Matters
Faith alone does not make someone qualified to hold power.
By Chapter 1030, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s expectations for senior religious positions explicitly include capability, discipline, loyalty and the ability to actually perform the responsibilities attached to the office.[16]
An incompetent worshiper does not become a competent Archbishop merely by possessing extreme devotion.

#### Family
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly declared the Religion a home and family.[4]
Official members are therefore expected to protect one another.
This concept is not merely symbolic; failure to help another official member in genuine mortal danger can result in extreme punishment.[2]

#### Protection
Followers who enter the institution receive more than theological teaching.
The Religion can provide:
- education;
- physical security;
- supernatural protection;
- community;
- shelter;
- opportunities;
- healing;
- training;
- and advancement.
In exchange, official members accept obligations toward the institution and one another.

#### Racial Equality
The Religion prohibits racial and ethnic discrimination among official members.[2]
Humans, Vampires, Demons, Angels, [[Werewolves]], Witches, Youkai and other intelligent species are treated as individuals rather than judged solely through species.
A member harmed by a specific person is permitted to hate or seek justice against that individual.
The Religion rejects transforming that grievance into blanket hatred toward an entire species.[2]
Where trauma prevents a member from interacting normally with another race, treatment and intervention are preferred over simply demanding that the victim suppress their experiences.

#### Other Religions
The Blood God Religion is highly devoted to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], and figures such as Valeria personally regard him as superior to other Gods.
However, the Religion does not possess a universal doctrine requiring the extermination of every deity outside Dragon's Nest.[14][7]
Other Pantheons can coexist with the Religion when they coexist with Victor's political order.
Military action is directed toward hostile powers rather than every foreign God simply for being foreign.

### Four Foundational Laws
The Religion possesses four foundational laws applying specifically to official initiated members.[2]
I
Internal Conflict
Official members may not attack one another maliciously. Disputes should be taken to a neutral mediator. Persistent violation can lead to expulsion and removal of memories relating to institutional secrets.[2]
II
Mutual Protection
Members are expected to protect one another. Deliberately abandoning another member to die without legitimate justification can result in execution after investigation.[2]
III
Equality
Racial and ethnic discrimination is prohibited. Official members are judged according to conduct and individual responsibility rather than species.[2]
IV
Official Membership
The complete institutional obligations apply to formally initiated Acolytes. Ordinary believers may worship [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] without joining the active organization.[2]

#### Departure and Retirement
Official members can leave.
Leaving and betraying the Religion are not the same.
A person who voluntarily departs can have sensitive information or relevant memories removed.[2]
Members can also retire from ordinary active service, although severe emergencies may still create an obligation to assist the Religion.

#### Betrayal
Betrayal receives far harsher treatment.
Traitors can be:
- marked with a supernatural curse;
- identified to other Acolytes;
- stripped of resources gained from the Religion;
- hunted;
- and executed.[2]

A person does not need to become an Acolyte simply to worship [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
Common believers may pray, attend temples and participate in ordinary religious practice without assuming the obligations imposed upon official members.[2]

### Worship and Rituals

#### Prayer
Prayer existed before formal institutionalization.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is capable of perceiving devotion directed toward him, strengthening the belief that prayer is not simply symbolic communication with an absent God.[4]

#### Blood Rituals
Blood possesses both symbolic and practical ritual significance.
Early followers used blood to summon Demons associated with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Hell.[1]
Valeria later used her own blood during supernatural communication rituals connecting her with Victor's wider organization.[6]
Blood rites therefore remained one component of the faith but should not be treated as its only form of worship.

#### Rite of Passage
A believer becomes an official Acolyte by completing a Rite of Passage administered by an Archbishop.[2]
The rite separates:
WORSHIPER
Prays to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] but remains outside formal institutional service.
→
RITE OF PASSAGE
Acceptance of the Religion's laws and obligations.
→
ACOLYTE
Official member receiving institutional rights, protection and responsibilities.

#### Offerings
Imperial-era temples possess conventional altars where offerings such as fruit and grain can be presented.[7]
Incense and other conventional religious practices also exist.

#### Statues
Statues serve an important educational and devotional function.
Early temples prominently displayed [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] alone.
Later temples also displayed publicly recognized Dragon Goddesses belonging to Dragon's Nest.[7]

#### Recorded Revelation
Statements made directly by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] during his first major appearance before the Religion were recorded for later followers.[5]
This helped prevent later generations from inventing a completely unrelated theological version of the Blood God.

### Afterlife
The Religion's teachings eventually incorporated [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s growing authority over death, Souls and afterlife territories.
During the worldwide missionary campaign, followers openly preached that death did not necessarily end the believer's relationship with the Blood God.[14]

#### Paradise
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] developed multiple afterlife arrangements.
His most exclusive personal Paradise was intended for people demonstrating exceptional loyalty.[10]
A wider Paradise associated with territory taken from the [[Egyptian Pantheon]] was intended for additional faithful and allied populations.[11]

### Activities

#### Missionary Work
Spreading knowledge of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] remains one of the Religion's principal responsibilities.
Early methods relied upon:
- direct recruitment;
- interpersonal networks;
- religious cells;
- supernatural influence;
- and local communities.
By Chapter 1024 the Religion could simultaneously use:
- television;
- radio;
- internet;
- public preaching;
- temples;
- and Imperial communications infrastructure.[14]

#### Public Relations
The organized faith was partly created to counter the simplistic image of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] as merely an evil ruler of Hell.[3]
Rather than deny his destructive side, the Religion contextualized it beside his role as:
- protector;
- father;
- ruler;
- warrior;
- provider;
- and defender of his people.

#### Intelligence
The Religion's enormous social network naturally became an intelligence asset.
Early followers passed information from numerous countries and supernatural organizations.[3]
Later the High Priestess personally inspected branches and supervised more formal security systems.[6]

#### Counter-Infiltration
The Religion treats infiltration by hostile Divine powers as a major threat.
Valeria has identified individuals carrying foreign Divine influence and immediately ordered suspects detained while affected territory was investigated and purified.[6]
The Religion can employ:
- Valeria's Staff;
- supernatural senses;
- Shadow Demons;
- [[City Core]] technology;
- interrogation;
- and magical purification.

#### Anti-Corruption
Religious authority is not intended to protect corrupt clergy.
Valeria considers corruption particularly offensive because every official acts publicly in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s name.[6]
Abusing religious power therefore damages both the institution and the reputation of the deity it represents.

### Temples and Holy Sites

#### Early Forest Temple
One of the first formal temples appeared hidden within a dense forest.
Its most striking feature was a massive statue of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] made entirely from silver and depicting him in full armor.[2]
Hundreds of dedicated followers gathered there in white hooded clothing.

#### Victorius Aeternus
Victorius Aeternus became the Religion's holy capital after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] personally created the settlement.[5]
Its religious significance is unique because its construction itself is treated as a direct act of the God worshiped by its inhabitants.

By Chapter 1022, the Religion possessed a major headquarters on a floating island above Velnorah.[6]

### Dragon Goddesses
The mature Blood God Religion does not publicly represent [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] as the only Divine Being belonging to his Pantheon.
Recognized Dragon Goddesses may receive:
- statues;
- offerings;
- temple space;
- public descriptions;
- identification of known Divine Domains;

Victor nevertheless remains overwhelmingly the primary object of worship.

#### Voluntary Public Worship
Not every Divine woman in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family wishes to become a public religious figure.
Some explicitly avoid religious publicity.[7]
The Religion therefore does not automatically construct a cult around every Dragon Goddess merely because she belongs to Victor's family.
When a previously unknown Goddess becomes publicly recognized, clergy can update religious records and educate worshipers concerning her known Domains.

### Transworld Administration
By Chapter 1022, the Religion is no longer merely an Earth church.

This allowed the Religion's supreme administrator to:
- inspect distant branches;
- move between territories;
- investigate corruption;
- coordinate clergy;
- and respond personally to serious religious problems.

#### Communication
The Religion uses conventional and supernatural communication.
Valeria can perform blood-based rituals capable of establishing contact with members of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family such as Violet.[6]
Kaguya and Roxanne also served as important points of communication between the Religion and Victor's household.

### Symbols and Aesthetics

#### Blood
Blood functions as the Religion's most obvious symbolic element because it connects directly to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Vampire origin and early title as the Blood God.
It also possesses literal supernatural utility in several rituals.[1][6]

#### White
The earliest major temple congregation wore white hooded garments.[2]
White subsequently remained closely associated with the formal clergy and Battle Sisters, creating a visual contrast with the Religion's association with Blood.

#### Dragon
As [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s identity evolved, Draconic imagery became increasingly important.
Battle Sisters display a Dragon-head emblem upon their uniforms.[7]
The Religion's later temples also depict the Dragon Gods and Goddesses belonging to Victor's Pantheon.

#### Silver
One of the earliest major statues of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was constructed entirely from pure silver.[2]

#### Imperial Religious Art
Imperial temples use architecture, mosaics and friezes to preserve major events from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s history.[7]
The temples therefore function partly as religious archives.

### Trivia
- The faith began before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] personally authorized or administered it.[2]
- [[Bruna Elderblood]] is the earliest known genuine devotee and the foundational source of the movement.[2]
- [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]] was one of the earliest major organizers rather than the sole religious founder.[2]
- The name Blood God Cult primarily describes the movement's early developmental period.
- Victor initially considered the Religion something his followers had created on their own but later accepted direct responsibility for it.[4]
- The Religion's early focus on women was a recruitment strategy rather than a prohibition on male followers.[3][12]
- The original seven Archbishops were women belonging to different races.[3]
- Valeria was selected partly because she did not immediately assume herself superior to the other candidates.[4]
- Victor personally created Victorius Aeternus as a home for the faithful.[4][5]
- The Religion possesses a formal system for voluntary departure, retirement, expulsion and punishment of traitors.[2]
- Official membership and ordinary belief are separate statuses.
- The Religion does not possess a universal commandment requiring the death of every non-Victor God.[14][7]
- The Outer Circle was approximately 70% female and 30% male when its demographics were described.[12]
- The Inner Circle was entirely female at that same point despite containing members of several supernatural races.[12]
- Valeria's greatest known reward was the resurrection of her daughter Vanessa.[13]
- Victor temporarily handled portions of the Religion's administration while Valeria spent time with her resurrected daughter.[17]
- The Battle Sisters can receive Blessings from multiple Dragon Goddesses.[7]
- Approximately one thousand Battle Sisters assembled under Valeria during one major Imperial operation, while additional branches mobilized elsewhere.[7]
- Not every Dragon Goddess allows herself to be publicly worshiped.[7]
- [[Frederick Winter]] and [[Andrew Walter]] are popularly regarded as unofficial Popes but do not replace Valeria's formal position as High Priestess.[8]
- By Chapter 1163, the Blood God Religion is explicitly the official religion of the [[Elderblood Empire]].[8]
