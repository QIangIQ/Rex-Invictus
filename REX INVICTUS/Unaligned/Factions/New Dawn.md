New Dawn was an extremely secretive multi-racial supernatural organization and conspiratorial network composed of influential individuals from numerous [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] and communities. Originally founded by [[Niklaus Horseman]], former Inquisition General James, and Fanir, the First Prince of the [[Werewolves]], the organization initially developed around the common problem represented by the rapidly growing Victor Elderblood. As Victor repeatedly interfered with the plans of its founders and became progressively more powerful, New Dawn expanded through alliances with Demons, Gods, Elder Gods, supernatural researchers and other factions whose goals overlapped with its own.[1][2]
By Chapter 761, New Dawn had evolved far beyond its original three-person conspiracy into an organization encompassing numerous Races and communities and governed by a Council of Seven. Each Council seat represented an influential individual belonging to a particular Race or community, and all seven positions possessed equal authority regardless of their numerical designation. Fanir held Seat #2 and represented the Werewolves, but the identities of the other six Council representatives have not been fully revealed.[3]
New Dawn became one of the most dangerous hidden networks operating during Victor's early rise because it combined political infiltration, scientific research, biological weapons, supernatural alliances, dimensional concealment and information gathering rather than relying upon one military force. [[Merlin]] considered its reach sufficiently extensive that he listed it among the principal forces capable of observing major supernatural activity that powerful factions could not realistically conceal.[4]
The organization's structure became even more complicated after its alliance with Diablo. New Dawn initially gained contacts throughout the Demonic Army and Infernal factions, but following Diablo's defeat it sought other supernatural powers capable of opposing Victor.[2] Chapter 804 later revealed that New Dawn had become supported by, and apparently merged with, an older organization composed largely of Gods associated with the Negative side of Balance, although some Positive-aligned Gods were also involved. Even [[Erebus]] did not possess complete knowledge of New Dawn itself, demonstrating that the later network extended beyond the information held by many of the powerful Gods participating in it.[5]
The Earth-Sector core of New Dawn was effectively dismantled during Victor's campaign in Chapters 870–871. Fanir had already been exposed and imprisoned, the organization's local headquarters was assaulted by Clan Blank, James was captured along with his research, and Niklaus escaped through an unknown portal into another Sector after revealing his true identity as Radu Tepes.[3][6][7] Because Radu survived outside the Sector and New Dawn had previously become connected to a considerably larger organization of Gods, the destruction of its Earth-based leadership should not automatically be interpreted as proof that every remnant of the wider network ceased to exist.

### Description
New Dawn functioned less like a conventional nation or military faction and more like a clandestine coalition built from individuals whose personal objectives temporarily aligned. Its members included representatives of different supernatural [[Races (Flamel)|Races]], researchers, political infiltrators and beings with access to resources from multiple Pantheons. Chapter 657 identifies its known racial reach as including [[Werewolves]], Noble Vampires, Vampire-Human Hybrids, Vampire-Werewolf Hybrids, Demons and Hellish Beings, with later information establishing connections to Gods, Elder Gods and their servants.[4]
The organization did not require every participant to share an identical ideology. Radu Tepes primarily sought revenge against [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]], James was obsessed with supernatural experimentation and understanding exceptional beings such as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Fanir possessed his own political ambitions within Samar, while [[Diablo]] and the Gods associated with the larger network pursued their respective plans for new Pantheons, supernatural power and changes to the existing world order.[1][2][5] What unified these interests was the belief that the existing balance of power obstructed their objectives and, increasingly, that Victor's abnormal rise represented an existential danger to their plans.
New Dawn therefore preferred indirect warfare. Rather than confront Victor, Vlad or the great supernatural factions in straightforward battles, its members relied upon toxins, experimental species, spies, hidden bases, magical privacy measures, Monster attacks, political manipulation and alliances with stronger powers. Victor repeatedly recognized that the organization was dangerous precisely because he did not possess enough information to know what additional countermeasures it might have developed.[8]

#### Secrecy
Secrecy was one of New Dawn's greatest advantages. Its influence extended across multiple [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] while even powerful supernatural factions possessed little reliable information concerning its complete membership or structure. [[Merlin]] warned that New Dawn's information network was capable of detecting major supernatural events even when the participants attempted to remain hidden.[4]
The organization also used supernatural methods to protect information. When Fanir was captured, a powerful Privacy Spell prevented him from revealing New Dawn's most sensitive secrets.[3] [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] concluded that forcibly breaking the protection through conventional interrogation was impractical without taking extreme measures, demonstrating that high-ranking members were protected against ordinary intelligence extraction.
New Dawn maintained multiple concealed facilities and evacuation routes rather than relying upon a single headquarters. Victor's forces repeatedly discovered bases only after the occupants had already evacuated, while the Bermuda Triangle facility existed within an artificially constructed pocket dimension possessing numerous escape routes and underground infrastructure.[8][9][10]

### Goals
New Dawn did not possess one completely uniform objective because its structure incorporated representatives and allies with different personal ambitions. Nevertheless, several recurring strategic goals can be identified throughout its history.
The elimination of [[Victor Elderblood]] became one of the organization's highest priorities. During the Chapter 595 meeting, Niklaus explicitly argued that Victor was dangerous because his actions were unpredictable, his growth rate was anomalous and his increasing tendency to act proactively threatened every long-term plan involving him.[1] The organization's founders believed he had to be eliminated before his development reached a level at which no existing countermeasure could stop him.
Radu simultaneously retained his personal objective of destroying [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]]. Even after New Dawn's Earth-based organization collapsed, Radu's final words before escaping in Chapter 870 concerned returning to take revenge upon Vlad.[6] James pursued supernatural experimentation and attempted to understand, reproduce or exploit the biological mechanisms behind exceptional supernatural beings. Fanir's motivations were closely connected to Werewolf politics and dissatisfaction with the structure maintained by [[Volk Fenrir]].
The broader organization also pursued the development of new supernatural powers capable of changing the existing balance. Its research included artificial Hybrids, anti-Vampire poisons, Monster-derived weapons and a controlled Faith project designed to reproduce the ancient conditions through which powerful new Gods were born.[11][9][12] After New Dawn became associated with the older organization of Gods, its activities increasingly overlapped with plans for a new Pantheon composed of supernatural powers dissatisfied with the current world order.[5]

### History

#### Origins
The exact date on which the name New Dawn first began being used has not been specified, but Chapter 762 identifies [[Niklaus Horseman]], James and Fanir as the organization's original three members.[2] The group formed as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s rise increasingly destabilized the plans of established supernatural conspirators. Victor had progressed from a newly transformed Vampire into a Count-level threat within an extraordinarily short period, interfered with Niklaus' political schemes, became hostile toward James' experimental network and repeatedly demonstrated abilities that could not be explained through conventional Vampire development.
The founders gradually concluded that none of them could reliably deal with him independently. New Dawn therefore evolved around shared intelligence, resources and mutual support against threats that individual members could not handle alone.

#### Early Research and Artificial Hybrids
One of the most important capabilities later associated with New Dawn came from James. While still connected to the Inquisition, James participated in research intended to artificially combine Human and Vampire traits.[13] The resulting artificial Vampire-Human Hybrids possessed enhanced physical abilities and could temporarily utilize Faith Energy, although their unstable biology imposed severe limitations.
Later investigation established that the successful research had depended heavily upon individuals who already contained traces of Vampire ancestry, particularly [[Zandriel]].[14] James subsequently carried his scientific expertise and accumulated data into New Dawn.
For this reason, the artificial Hybrid project is better described as a precursor technology brought into New Dawn through James and his associates rather than automatically attributing every early Inquisition experiment to an organization whose formal involvement had not yet been established.

#### Anti-Progenitor Poison
Niklaus and James later developed one of the most dangerous anti-Vampire weapons shown during the early story: a compound intended to exploit several biological weaknesses simultaneously.[11] The poison incorporated materials dangerous to Vampires, including Elder-God-derived substances, Monster material, Hybrid-related toxins and Werewolf components.
[[Theo Tepes]] successfully exposed [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] to the poison, weakening even the Vampire Progenitor.[11] Chapter 595 establishes that the effects remained significant enough that Niklaus estimated Vlad would require approximately two years to fully recover his unrestricted Progenitor strength.[1]
This achievement demonstrated New Dawn's general methodology: rather than attempt to overpower an ancient Progenitor directly, its researchers combined multiple known weaknesses into a weapon capable of bypassing advantages that would normally make such a Being almost impossible for them to confront.

#### The Eye
Older versions of this page attributed the purge of infiltrators inside the Snow and Fulger Clans in Chapter 290 to New Dawn. The chapter itself identifies the organization involved as The Eye, a group whose objective was to infiltrate the Vampire Count Clans, replace the existing Countesses and acquire political control over [[Nightingale]].[15]
No direct statement in Chapter 290 identifies The Eye as New Dawn. The incident therefore should not be treated as a confirmed New Dawn operation unless another chapter explicitly establishes that The Eye was a branch, predecessor or subordinate organization of New Dawn.

#### Alliance with Diablo
As [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] continued growing, New Dawn sought support beyond its original members. This eventually brought the organization into contact with [[Bael]] and [[Diablo]], giving it access to the influence and resources of the Demonic Army.[2] Diablo was pursuing his own plans to create a Pantheon composed of powerful Infernal Beings from the Seven Hells, allowing the two groups to cooperate temporarily because Victor threatened both of their objectives.
During Chapter 595, Niklaus convened a meeting focused specifically upon eliminating Victor. He argued that Victor's unpredictability and extraordinary growth made killing him the highest priority, while James presented research concerning Victor's Human identity, RH Null Blood and transformation into a Vampire Progenitor.[1] James had already attempted to reproduce the conditions that created Victor but repeatedly produced only weak Vampires, leading him to conclude that Golden Blood alone could not explain Victor's existence.
Bael participated in the organization during this period, but Chapter 701 later reveals that his position was more complicated than that of an ordinary New Dawn loyalist. Diablo had deliberately infiltrated Bael into the organization to observe it.[8] He therefore functioned simultaneously as an ally, liaison and spy serving Diablo's interests.

#### Elder Gods and Monster Weapons
New Dawn also developed relationships with the [[Elder Gods Pantheon]] and its messengers. After absorbing [[Bael]]'s memories, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] learned that James and Niklaus had repeatedly met representatives connected to the Elder Gods.[8] This relationship was especially dangerous because the Elder Gods possessed extensive biological knowledge involving [[Nightingale]]'s Monsters and materials intrinsically poisonous to Vampires.
Victor consequently considered the possibility that New Dawn had access to several separate anti-Vampire weapons, including Hybrid toxins, Monster poison and technology originating from Nightingale's native civilization.[8] He deliberately refused to attack blindly because he did not know what additional weapons James and the Elder-God messengers may have developed.
The Monster ambush in Chapters 646–647 demonstrated the effectiveness of these methods. Victor and his companions fought multiple types of Nightingale Monsters, and Victor was struck by a toxin that caused his body to repeatedly deteriorate and regenerate.[16] The attackers also successfully displaced Victor into [[Biblical Hell]], demonstrating access to a method capable of forcibly transporting a living Being into an [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] dimension.
Victor killed the attacking Monsters, meaning the operation failed to eliminate him, but the successful poisoning and dimensional removal demonstrated that his enemies possessed methods capable of affecting even his rapidly developing Progenitor body.

#### Fall of Diablo and Victor's Investigation
After [[Diablo]]'s defeat, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] consumed numerous elite Demons, including [[Bael]], and inherited their memories.[8] Bael's memories revealed his infiltration of New Dawn and provided the location of one of its bases. Victor immediately prioritized investigating the organization because Niklaus, James and Fanir were all enemies with histories of threatening his Family.
The discovered base had already been evacuated by the time Victor's forces reached it. Rather than conclude that New Dawn was harmless, Victor became more cautious. The information obtained through Bael confirmed that New Dawn possessed connections to Elder-God messengers and potentially several anti-Vampire technologies that even Victor had not yet encountered.[8]
Victor therefore began systematically tracing its remaining facilities rather than launching a reckless frontal assault.

#### Bermuda Triangle Facility
One of the most important New Dawn-associated facilities was concealed within an artificial pocket dimension above the Bermuda Triangle.[9] [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett|Scathach Scarlett]], [[Jeanne Elderblood]], Morgana and [[Oda Blank]] located the dimension after tracking information gathered from previously abandoned enemy bases.
Inside existed an artificially constructed island containing an isolated Human community. Every household possessed a shrine dedicated to a supposed God, while the population had been deliberately cut off from reliable information concerning the outside world.[12] The inhabitants believed Humanity had effectively been devastated by Monsters and that their continued survival depended upon supernatural protectors.
The entire environment was designed as a controlled Faith-generation experiment. Monster attacks were intentionally staged against the community, after which the supposed deity would appear to save the inhabitants. Fear, uncertainty and repeated salvation increased the Humans' Faith while recreating the conditions under which ancient myths and Gods had naturally developed.[12][10]
The central artificial deity was represented as a heroic, spiky-haired man wielding an axe and called Thorron. Jeanne concluded that Thorron did not yet truly exist as an established God; instead, generations of controlled belief, mythology and Faith were intended to eventually cause such a God to be born.[12]
The project used specialized Divine Items capable of gathering and storing Faith far more efficiently than the naturally developed temples of ancient civilizations.[10] This potentially allowed the organization to manufacture powerful Gods over sufficiently long periods instead of waiting for unpredictable natural mythological development.
The island was also a sophisticated research installation containing laboratories, underground passages and multiple escape routes. Its pocket dimension had been created from scratch rather than being ordinary magically isolated land, implying the involvement of powerful Gods with exceptional dimensional capabilities.[10]
Victor evacuated the manipulated Human population, confiscated useful materials and destroyed the island and its pocket dimension with his Draconic Breath.[17]

#### Associated Gods
The aftermath of the Bermuda facility exposed part of the divine network connected to the project. [[Taranis]] and [[Anubis]] arrived after the island's destruction and reacted as individuals whose work had been destroyed, while Taranis explicitly referred to hiding their other research fields.[18] [[Erebus]] also appeared, identified [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Dragon Breath and discussed the situation with them.
This scene establishes a direct connection among these Gods and the research network, but it should not automatically be interpreted as proof that all three occupied formal seats on New Dawn's Council. Later information demonstrates that New Dawn had become connected to a much larger and older organization of Gods, making the exact organizational boundary increasingly difficult to determine.[5]
The same distinction should be maintained when discussing other Gods such as [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]], Seth, Ra, [[Amon]] and [[Nocturnus]]. Several participated in the larger anti-Victor coalition or shared allies with New Dawn, but shared objectives do not by themselves establish formal Council membership.

#### Fanir and Samar
New Dawn developed significant influence inside Samar through Fanir, the First Prince of the [[Werewolves]]. Fanir's position gave the organization access to a member of Samar's Royal Family and made him the Council's representative for the Werewolf Race.[3]
His faction was eventually dismantled after [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]] and [[Volk Fenrir]] uncovered his betrayal. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] participated in the interrogation, but a powerful Privacy Spell prevented Fanir from exposing New Dawn's most sensitive information.[3] What could be recovered nevertheless revealed the Council of Seven and allowed Tasha to reconstruct a substantial portion of the organization's structure.
Chapter 762 further clarified New Dawn's origin and expansion, confirming that the organization began with Niklaus, James and Fanir before gaining support from [[Diablo]]'s faction and later seeking other powers capable of opposing Victor.[2] Fanir was ultimately imprisoned, causing New Dawn to lose one of its most valuable sources of political influence within Samar.

#### The Greater Organization of Gods
The destruction of the [[Greek Pantheon]]'s hostile coalition in Chapter 804 revealed that New Dawn was only part of a much larger conspiracy.[5] After consuming [[Erebus]], [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] examined his memories and discovered that Erebus had been recruited by an organization of Gods involved with [[Diablo]]'s plan to construct a new Pantheon consisting primarily of Negative-aligned Beings from different Pantheons.
Victor realized that even Erebus did not possess detailed information concerning New Dawn itself. From the evidence available, he concluded that New Dawn had apparently merged with or become supported by an already existing organization created by Gods.[5] Most of these Gods were associated with the Negative side of Balance, although Erebus had also interacted with Positive-aligned Gods and at least one foreign Goddess originating outside Earth's known Pantheons.
The resulting network resembled the early structure of a completely new Pantheon more than a conventional Earth-based secret society. This revelation explains why New Dawn's later list of apparent allies spans numerous otherwise unrelated Pantheons and why the organization was capable of accessing resources far beyond those possessed by its original three founders.
The larger God organization has not been given a confirmed formal name in the relevant chapter. It should therefore not be assigned an invented name such as "Negative Pantheon" unless later canon explicitly identifies it.

#### Victor's Declaration of War
By Chapter 870, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] possessed enough intelligence to move openly against New Dawn. During a major gathering of supernatural powers, he publicly declared war upon the [[Egyptian Pantheon]], the [[Elder Gods Pantheon]] and New Dawn, directly naming [[Niklaus Horseman]] and James among his targets.[6]
The declaration was synchronized with an immediate assault rather than serving merely as a future warning. Clan Blank and allied forces attacked New Dawn's Earth-Sector headquarters while Victor confronted its divine allies elsewhere.
Niklaus rapidly concluded that remaining at the base would result in his capture or death and initiated a strategic retreat. He arranged for his daughter [[Jessica Horseman Snow]] to escape toward the [[Snow Clan]] before attempting to flee himself.[6]
[[Oda Blank]] and Clan Blank assassins surrounded Niklaus and inflicted otherwise fatal damage, but he survived through previously prepared measures. Niklaus then activated an unusual black egg-like object that created a dark-red portal. Tentacles belonging to an unidentified entity emerged from the other side and pulled him through despite Oda's attempts to stop the escape.[6]
Before leaving, Niklaus publicly revealed his true identity:
Radu Tepes.
He ordered Oda to tell [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] that Radu would eventually return for revenge and disappeared into another Sector.[6]

#### Capture of James
Radu's escape did not save the rest of New Dawn's Earth-based leadership. James was captured by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s forces during the operation, and Victor specifically ordered that the researcher's accumulated work be seized rather than destroyed.[7]
Clan Blank confiscated the available research while James was placed in captivity. With Fanir already imprisoned, James captured and Radu outside the Sector, the three original founders no longer possessed an operational organization capable of functioning normally on Earth.
The assault therefore marked the practical destruction of New Dawn's Earth-Sector core.

### Artificial God Project
The artificial God project represents one of New Dawn's most ambitious demonstrated programs. Rather than attempting to directly transform an existing mortal into a God, the research attempted to reproduce the social and supernatural conditions responsible for the birth of ancient mythological Gods.[12]
The Bermuda population was deliberately deprived of outside information and made to believe that monstrous forces had devastated Humanity. Artificial crises were periodically created, and the inhabitants were then saved by beings presented as divine protectors. Every home contained a shrine, while specialized Divine Items efficiently captured the Faith generated by the population.[12][10]
The process was intended to produce a self-reinforcing mythology. Repeated stories of divine salvation would spread across generations, the supposed God's legend would gain complexity, and the accumulated Faith could eventually result in the birth of an actual powerful God. Thorron was the first directly shown candidate, but Jeanne concluded that the infrastructure could potentially be used to create multiple Gods over sufficient time.[12]
The project demonstrates that New Dawn was not merely attempting to acquire existing allies. It was developing methods through which entirely new supernatural assets could be manufactured.

### Relationship with Diablo
New Dawn and [[Diablo]] possessed a temporary alliance rather than a simple master-subordinate relationship. The original founders required additional military and supernatural support against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], while Diablo was attempting to construct a new infernal Pantheon and wanted Victor eliminated.[1][2]
Through Diablo and [[Bael]], New Dawn gained access to Demonic influence and contacts with other Infernal Entities. Diablo nevertheless distrusted the organization enough to place Bael inside it specifically as an observer.[8] [[Agares]] was also involved in intelligence activity surrounding the wider network, although his exact formal New Dawn status is unclear.
After Diablo's death, New Dawn did not collapse. Instead, its surviving founders sought replacement allies among Gods, Elder Gods and the older divine organization later uncovered through [[Erebus]]' memories.[2][5]

### Relationship with the Elder Gods
New Dawn's relationship with the [[Elder Gods Pantheon]] was based upon mutual interests, particularly hostility toward Vampires and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. [[Bael]]'s memories showed James and Niklaus repeatedly meeting messengers of the Elder Gods.[8] Victor considered these meetings particularly dangerous because Elder-God resources potentially gave New Dawn access to Monster poisons, [[Nightingale]] technology and biological weapons specifically effective against Vampires.
The Elder Gods did not regard most outsiders as genuine allies in the ordinary sense, so their cooperation with New Dawn should be understood primarily as a strategic exchange against common enemies. Their ultimate objectives remained distinct.

### Relationship with the Greater God Organization
Chapter 804 establishes that New Dawn's later activities cannot be understood solely through its original Earth-based membership. The organization had become intertwined with an older network of Gods whose objectives resembled [[Diablo]]'s project for a new Pantheon.[5]
Most known participants were associated with Negative Concepts or the Negative side of Balance, but the network was not exclusively Negative. [[Erebus]]' memories included meetings with Positive-aligned Gods and a Goddess from outside Earth's local Pantheons.[5]
This distinction explains several apparent contradictions in New Dawn's membership records. Characters such as Erebus, [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]], [[Anubis]], [[Taranis]], Seth and other Gods may be closely associated with the same conspiracy without necessarily being formal members of New Dawn's seven-seat Council.

### Major Successes
New Dawn and its founders achieved several meaningful successes despite ultimately failing to eliminate [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] or overthrow the established supernatural order. Its researchers developed anti-Vampire weapons capable of affecting beings vastly stronger than themselves, and the poison used against Vlad demonstrated that even a Progenitor could be weakened through sufficiently specialized preparation.[11][1]
The organization and its associated forces also successfully poisoned Victor and forcibly transported him into [[Biblical Hell]] in Chapter 647, although the attempt failed to kill him.[16] Its connections extended across Werewolf royalty, the Demonic Army, Elder Gods and multiple Pantheons, while the artificial Faith project demonstrated a viable method for accelerating the creation of powerful mythological Gods.[12][10]
Perhaps its greatest strategic success was surviving and expanding long enough to become part of a conspiracy significantly larger than its original founders. By Chapter 804, New Dawn had ceased being merely three enemies of Victor cooperating in secret and had become interconnected with a cross-Pantheon network resembling the foundations of a new Pantheon.[5]

### Failures
The organization's central strategic objective—eliminating [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] before his growth became uncontrollable—failed completely. Chapter 595 specifically identifies his accelerating development as the reason he had to be killed early, but New Dawn's repeated attempts instead gave Victor additional information, enemies to absorb and reasons to strengthen his intelligence network.[1]
The Bermuda artificial-God project was destroyed before Thorron could fully emerge, Fanir's exposure removed New Dawn's influence within Samar, several of its most powerful divine associates were killed during the destruction of the [[Greek Pantheon]]'s hostile coalition, and Victor eventually launched a coordinated war that dismantled its Earth-Sector headquarters.[17][3][5][6]
By Chapter 871, James had been captured, Fanir remained imprisoned and Radu was the only original founder confirmed to have escaped, leaving the organization's original local structure effectively destroyed.[7]

### Current Status
New Dawn should no longer be listed simply as Extant (Secretive). Its known Earth-Sector core was dismantled during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s campaign in Chapters 870–871. Fanir had already been captured in Samar, James was imprisoned and his research confiscated, and the principal headquarters was overrun by Clan Blank.[3][6][7]
However, describing New Dawn as completely extinct would also be too absolute. Radu escaped to another Sector through a portal connected to an unidentified entity, and Chapter 804 establishes that New Dawn had previously become connected to or merged with a much broader cross-Pantheon organization of Gods.[5][6] The present condition of every external remnant of that wider organization has not been fully revealed.
The most accurate current classification is therefore:
Dismantled within Earth's Sector; external remnants and the status of the greater affiliated God organization remain uncertain.

### Trivia
- New Dawn was originally founded by [[Niklaus Horseman]], James and Fanir.[2]
- The organization was not originally an enormous Pantheon-level conspiracy; it expanded progressively as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s enemies searched for greater support.[2]
- By Chapter 761, it was governed by an equal Council of Seven representing multiple [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] and communities.[3]
- Fanir occupied Seat #2 and represented the [[Werewolves]]; the seat number did not make him the second-highest-ranking member.[3]
- The identities of the other six Council representatives have not been completely revealed.
- [[Merlin]] regarded New Dawn as one of the principal sources capable of detecting otherwise hidden major supernatural activity.[4]
- [[Bael]] was not merely a loyal New Dawn member; [[Diablo]] deliberately infiltrated him into the organization to observe it.[8]
- James attempted to reproduce the conditions responsible for Victor becoming a Vampire Progenitor after discovering his Human history and RH Null Blood, but every attempt failed to create anything comparable to Victor.[1]
- New Dawn's connections to Elder-God messengers caused Victor to suspect that it possessed multiple undiscovered anti-Vampire technologies.[8]
- The Bermuda Triangle project deliberately recreated the fear, uncertainty, mythology and Faith conditions associated with the natural birth of ancient Gods.[12]
- Thorron was not yet an established God when Victor discovered the project.[12]
- Specialized Divine Items were used to collect Faith far more efficiently than ordinary worship.[10]
- The Bermuda facility was only one research site; [[Taranis]] explicitly referred to other research fields after its destruction.[18]
- Older versions of the page incorrectly treated the organization called The Eye in Chapter 290 as definitively being New Dawn. Chapter 290 itself identifies it separately.[15]
- Chapter 804 reveals that New Dawn had apparently merged with or become supported by a pre-existing organization of Gods.[5]
- Most of the Gods in that larger organization were associated with the Negative side of Balance, but some Positive-aligned Gods were also involved.[5]
- Even [[Erebus]] did not possess complete information concerning New Dawn itself.[5]
- Victor considered the resulting network similar to the foundations of an entirely new Pantheon.[5]
- Victor publicly declared war on New Dawn in Chapter 870 and coordinated the declaration with an immediate attack upon its headquarters.[6]
- Radu escaped the destruction of the Earth-Sector organization by travelling through an unknown portal into another Sector.[6]
- James was captured and his research confiscated in Chapter 871.[7]
- New Dawn's Earth-Sector core is therefore dismantled, but the survival of Radu and the existence of the greater God organization prevent its entire wider network from being confidently classified as extinct.
