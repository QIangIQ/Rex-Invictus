## MTWABV Canon

The Lost Club is a supernatural nightclub, bar, information brokerage, and mercenary hub located in Brooklyn, United States. It is owned and operated by the clandestine Witch Esther, better known to many of its patrons as "Madam".[1][2]
The establishment is one of the few locations in Brooklyn recognized as a neutral zone for the supernatural world. Members of different supernatural races are permitted to enter, meet, conduct business, purchase information, and socialize so long as they do not cause problems inside the establishment.[2] Despite this designation, the club's neutrality is not an absolute law. Rather, it is an arrangement upheld by Esther, her reputation, and the numerous magical protections placed throughout her territory.[3]
Beyond its public function as a nightclub, The Lost Club acts as the headquarters of Esther's information network. Through the club, Esther and her associates gather and sell information concerning numerous supernatural races and factions. The establishment also serves as a workplace and meeting point for supernatural mercenaries, most notably [[Johnny Lykos]].[4][2]
Following [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]]'s negotiations with Esther, the latter's information network was brought into Ruby's sphere of influence and began supporting Ruby and [[Victor Elderblood]]'s plans. The club later also became a protected refuge and operational base for multiple clandestine Witches affiliated with Esther, including Brown and White.[5][6][7]

### Overview
The Lost Club outwardly operates as a nightclub and bar in Brooklyn, but its importance comes primarily from the supernatural activities conducted behind its ordinary appearance. Supernatural beings of various races use the club to drink, socialize, meet contacts, purchase information, and arrange work. Because of the variety of races that gather there, the establishment is regarded as one of Brooklyn's few neutral zones.[2]
The concept of "neutrality" at The Lost Club is largely practical rather than absolute. Natalia Alioth explained that a true neutral zone does not really exist within the supernatural world, since a dispute between different supernatural races can erupt at any time. The events surrounding Ruby and Johnny later demonstrated this directly when the two nearly fought to the death inside the club.[2][3]
What allows the establishment to continue functioning is primarily Esther's authority. As the owner and resident Witch, Esther considers the building her territory and personally intervenes when conflicts threaten the club. Numerous magical circles and barriers have also been constructed throughout the establishment, making the interior particularly dangerous for anyone attempting to directly oppose Esther on her own ground.[3][6]

### Functions

#### Supernatural Neutral Zone
The Lost Club serves as a meeting place where members of otherwise hostile supernatural races can occupy the same establishment without immediately entering into conflict. Vampires, [[Werewolves]], Witches, and other supernatural beings are capable of using the club provided that they respect its rules and refrain from causing unnecessary disturbances.[2]
This neutrality does not mean that the patrons are allies or that racial and political conflicts disappear upon entering. It instead functions as an agreed-upon form of temporary restraint. Esther is willing to interfere when necessary to preserve the establishment and prevent disputes from escalating into battles capable of destroying the club.[3]

#### Information Brokerage
One of The Lost Club's most important functions is as the center of Esther's information business. Esther possesses a network capable of collecting information from multiple supernatural races and factions, while Eddy also acts as an intermediary who can provide information to clients through the club.[4][2]
The appearance of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] Alucard as the Fifth Vampire Count dramatically increased demand for information. Supernatural forces became interested in learning about the newly emerged Count, creating considerably more business for Esther's network. Access to reliable information concerning powerful figures could provide the network not only with money, but also greater prestige and influence within supernatural society.[2]
Ruby recognized the strategic value of this network. Rather than repeatedly purchasing information from independent Witches, she wanted direct access to a dedicated intelligence network already connected to different races. This became one of the principal reasons behind her approach to Esther.[2]

#### Mercenary Hub
The Lost Club is also associated with supernatural mercenary activity. [[Johnny Lykos]], an Alpha Werewolf known as the White Wolf, regularly worked as a mercenary through the establishment.[1][4]
The club consequently acts as more than a place where information is simply purchased. Individuals looking for work, contracts, contacts, or hired supernatural assistance can also use the establishment as an intermediary point. At least one Werewolf involved in the attempt to capture [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] was stated to have obtained his job through The Lost Club.[8]

#### Clandestine Witch Base
The Lost Club eventually became a base and refuge for several clandestine Witches connected to Esther. These Witches do not openly occupy the public areas of the nightclub; instead, a concealed section beneath the building allows them to remain hidden from ordinary patrons and unwanted visitors.[6][7]
Among the known Witches residing or operating there are Brown and White. Both are directly affiliated with The Lost Club and recognize Esther as their superior.[6][7]

### Layout

#### Main Club
The publicly accessible portion of The Lost Club resembles an ordinary nightclub and bar. A large sign displaying the establishment's name is positioned outside the building. Access to the club is monitored, and at least during Ruby's visit, a security guard was stationed at the entrance.[2]
The bar is operated by Eddy, who works directly beneath Esther. In addition to serving drinks, Eddy participates in the information business and has been shown providing clients with documents and intelligence from behind the counter.[4]
The main club floor is large enough to accommodate numerous supernatural patrons and has repeatedly become the scene of confrontations, including Ruby's altercation with Johnny and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later visit.[3][9]

#### Esther's Office
Esther possesses a private office within the establishment. Important business negotiations and sensitive information exchanges are normally moved away from the public club floor and conducted there.[10]
Ruby's negotiations with Esther concerning her intelligence network and the information surrounding Clan Horseman were conducted in this office.[10][5]
Natalia is also capable of using her Space abilities to create portals directly into Esther's office, allowing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his companions to bypass the public entrance during later visits.[6]

#### Reserved Room and Secret Passage
A room within the club is specifically reserved for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Ruby. Although it can function as a normal room, it conceals an additional purpose.[6]
A wall inside the room contains a hidden magical mechanism. When activated, a red magic circle appears and the wall disappears, revealing a staircase leading beneath the establishment. This passage provides access to the concealed area occupied by Esther's clandestine Witches.[6]
The Witches hidden beyond the passage are capable of observing approaching visitors and determining whether they wish to reveal themselves. Consequently, the lower area serves as both a protected residence and a concealed meeting place.[6]

### Magical Defenses
As the personal territory of an experienced Witch, The Lost Club possesses extensive magical protection. Esther has placed numerous magical circles and barriers throughout the building, including additional defenses surrounding private areas such as her room.[3][6]
These defenses allow Esther to exercise a significant degree of control over events occurring inside her establishment. During Ruby's confrontation with Johnny, Natalia prepared to intervene through her Space abilities, but Esther activated the magic established throughout the club and forced the confrontation to stop before it escalated further.[3]
Fighting a Witch inside territory that she has personally prepared is considered extremely disadvantageous, and the club's defenses are one of the primary reasons Esther is capable of maintaining authority despite regularly hosting powerful supernatural beings.[3]
The magical barriers also provide privacy. During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later visit, he noticed numerous barriers protecting Esther's private areas. His enhanced perception allowed him to examine defenses that would normally prevent others from seeing what was hidden behind them.[6]

### History

#### The White Wolf
The Lost Club first appears when [[Johnny Lykos]], known as the White Wolf, enters the establishment in Brooklyn. Johnny was already familiar with the club and with Eddy, demonstrating that he had been using it as a place of business well before its introduction in the story.[1]
Through Eddy, Johnny received information concerning his family and the developing supernatural situation surrounding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Eddy treated the exchange as business, demonstrating the transactional nature of information inside the club. Johnny's activities also established the location's connection with supernatural mercenary work.[4]
Johnny was frequently accompanied by several women associated with him, including Jinsei, Judy, Jessica Horseman, and later Roberta. Roberta in particular frequently appeared at The Lost Club while presenting herself as one of Johnny's lovers, although it was eventually revealed that their supposed relationship had been created through manipulated memories.[11][12]

#### Ruby Scarlett's Visit
After becoming concerned with the rapidly changing supernatural situation surrounding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] decided that she required an independent intelligence network. Accompanied by Natalia, she travelled to The Lost Club to negotiate directly with Esther.[2]
Upon arriving, Natalia identified the club as one of Brooklyn's few supernatural neutral zones. Ruby nevertheless understood that such neutrality was only maintained so long as those inside respected it. Her actual objective was Esther, whose information network had contacts extending across different supernatural races.[2]
Inside the club, Ruby learned that Victor's emergence as a new Vampire Count had caused an enormous increase in demand for information. Nearly every relevant supernatural power wanted information concerning him, giving Esther's business a sudden opportunity to increase its money, prestige, and influence.[2]
Ruby proposed an arrangement in which she would provide valuable information while Esther's intelligence network would give Ruby access to everything it discovered. Ruby was specifically interested in possessing a network that already interacted with multiple races rather than continuing to independently purchase individual pieces of information from Witches.[2]
The negotiation was temporarily interrupted when Ruby encountered [[Johnny Lykos]]. Johnny eventually attacked Ruby after being provoked, only to discover that he was considerably outmatched. Ruby froze him and nearly continued the confrontation before Esther personally intervened.[3]
Esther declared that The Lost Club was neutral territory and demanded that any battle be taken elsewhere. The incident demonstrated both the limitations of the club's neutrality and Esther's willingness to personally enforce it when her establishment was threatened.[3]
After the disturbance ended, Ruby and Esther moved to the latter's office and continued their negotiations. Ruby presented information concerning suspicious activity within Clan Horseman and used both the information and Esther's previous dealings with her to place herself in the superior negotiating position.[10]

#### Alliance with Ruby
Ruby ultimately revealed a much larger ambition than simply purchasing information. She intended to gather sufficient intelligence, influence, and resources to reshape the unstable supernatural world into an environment in which [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and their family could live without constantly being threatened by hostile factions.[5]
Esther concluded that supporting Ruby was the more advantageous choice. She accepted the proposed partnership and provided Ruby with all of the information she currently possessed concerning Clan Horseman as a gesture of good faith toward what she expected to become a long-term arrangement.[5]
Ruby, however, was unwilling to rely on Esther's word or on a conventional Witch contract. Due to her existing distrust of Witches and her previous experience with Esther, Ruby used an improved form of Vampiric Charm on her. The technique acted as insurance against betrayal and placed Esther firmly under Ruby's control.[5]
From that point onward, Esther's information agency operated in support of Ruby's plans and, by extension, Victor. Esther continued to manage The Lost Club and conduct her normal business, but her intelligence resources had effectively become part of Ruby's growing information apparatus.[5]

#### Victor's First Visit
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] personally arrived at The Lost Club much later and under unusual circumstances. After using one of Natalia's portals, he unexpectedly found himself inside Esther's establishment.[9]
Esther explained that he was inside her bar, The Lost Club, and described it as a neutral establishment where members of multiple races could gather. Victor immediately became interested in the concept of an establishment containing many different supernatural species.[9]
Victor's presence caused considerable tension among the club's patrons due to his rapidly growing reputation as the Fifth Vampire Count. His arrival also brought him into direct contact with Esther, Eddy, Johnny, and Roberta.[9][13]
Victor eventually became suspicious of Roberta. Her past appeared strangely uncertain, and the people around her were unable to properly explain where she had come from. After investigating, Victor discovered that Roberta had manipulated Johnny's memories, causing him to believe that she was his lover and allowing her to hide in plain sight inside the club.[14][12]
After confronting Roberta and discovering the mysterious entity associated with her, Victor became interested in her potential and decided to take her with him rather than treat her as an immediate enemy. He eventually established a magical contract restricting her ability to manipulate certain people.[12][15]
Victor's departure caused visible relief among many of the club's patrons. The supernatural beings present had feared that the presence of a Vampire Count, particularly one with Victor's reputation, could cause the entire establishment to be destroyed if another major confrontation occurred.[15]

#### Base of the Clandestine Witches
The Lost Club later gained additional importance during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s period on Earth. When Victor wanted to contact Esther's Witches, Natalia opened a portal directly into Esther's office.[6]
While moving through the establishment, Victor discovered the large number of magical barriers protecting its private areas. He then entered the room reserved for himself and Ruby and activated the concealed passage leading beneath the club.[6]
The hidden section housed multiple clandestine Witches associated with Esther. Brown immediately greeted Victor, while other Witches remained concealed because they were wary of unfamiliar visitors such as Natalia and Mizuki.[6]
Among the Witches working from this hidden area was White, an exceptionally talented specialist in memory magic. Victor later used her abilities to convert memories into magical recordings and to better organize the enormous amount of information contained within the memories he had acquired.[7]
The presence of these Witches establishes The Lost Club as more than Esther's commercial business. By this period it had also become a secure enclave for clandestine Witches living outside the primary Witch hierarchy, many of whom were protected through Esther's connection to Ruby and Victor.[6][7]

### Relationships

#### Ruby Elderblood Scarlett
Although Esther remains the owner and day-to-day operator of The Lost Club, Ruby effectively gained control over its information network after their agreement. Ruby's Vampiric Charm ensured that Esther could not safely betray her, transforming what began as a commercial partnership into a subordinate relationship.[5]

#### Victor Elderblood
Because Esther became one of Ruby's intelligence assets, The Lost Club indirectly became part of the support structure surrounding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Victor himself later developed a friendly relationship with several of the clandestine Witches residing there and was allowed unrestricted access to protected portions of the establishment.[6][7]

#### Clandestine Witches
The hidden Witch community beneath The Lost Club demonstrates Esther's continued connections with Witches who live outside conventional Witch society. The club provides these individuals with secrecy, magical protection, contacts, and—following Esther's alignment with Ruby and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]—the indirect protection of considerably more powerful supernatural figures.[6][7]

- Esther's private magical defenses extend throughout the establishment and include multiple barriers protecting restricted sections of the building.[6]
- A room is specifically reserved for Victor and Ruby and also conceals the entrance to the clandestine Witches' underground area.[6]
