The Spartans, officially designated the Spartan Legion, are the First Legion of the Elderblood Empire and its principal offensive military force. Specializing in direct assault, planetary invasion, siege warfare and large-scale military conquest, the Spartans constitute the spearhead of the Empire's conventional military establishment. Their primary responsibility is to confront hostile forces directly, break through planetary and stellar defenses, and lead campaigns intended to expand Imperial territory or eliminate external threats.[1]
The Legion was founded in Year 1000 of the [[Imperial Calendar]] by [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood, [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] and [[Rose Adrasteia Elderblood]]. Its creation formed part of the Emperor's reorganization of his civilization into Seven Imperial Legions, each possessing specialized responsibilities within the Empire's military, intelligence, research and security infrastructure. The Spartans were established as the Empire's primary assault formation, with [[Scathach]] and Rose personally overseeing their training to ensure that the Legion could invade, conquer and secure territories on behalf of [[Victor Elderblood]].[2][1]
The organization is commanded by John, one of the Emperor's powerful subordinates. When circumstances require an alternative commander, Rose Adrasteia Elderblood, [[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]] or [[Kali Elderblood]] can assume responsibility for the Legion. Its military personnel include war dragons, heavily armored soldiers, siege mages and advanced warships capable of devastating planets or entire solar systems, making the Spartans one of the Empire's foremost instruments of large-scale conventional warfare.[1]
The Legion's name was chosen by Aphrodite in honor of the warriors of ancient Sparta. The designation served as a tribute to the cultural heritage of the former [[Greek Pantheon]], preserving a connection to Greek history within the rapidly expanding civilization governed by Victor. The Emperor accepted the name despite his unfavorable history with the old Greek Gods, recognizing that the surviving Greek Goddesses wished to preserve part of their former civilization. The founding of the Spartans was consequently accompanied by the reconstruction of Aphrodisias on [[Yggdrasil]], which became the headquarters of the First Legion.[1]
Together with the [[Order of the Iron Veil]], the Second Legion responsible for territorial defense, the Spartans form the Empire's principal offensive and defensive military partnership. The First Legion is designed to seize and overcome hostile positions, while the Second Legion specializes in securing Imperial territory, maintaining fortifications and protecting conquered worlds. Both belong to the most numerous military branches of the Seven Legions and possess access to extraordinarily powerful weapons suitable for warfare on a stellar scale.[1][3]

The Spartans occupy a central position within the Empire's offensive military doctrine. Unlike Legions dedicated to intelligence, law enforcement, research or the protection of the Imperial Family, the First Legion is organized primarily around direct military confrontation. Its forces are intended to enter combat ahead of other conventional formations, overwhelm hostile defenses and establish Imperial control over strategically important territories.[1]
The Legion's offensive specialization does not prevent its forces from securing captured territory. Its founding purpose explicitly includes the ability to invade, conquer and secure everything necessary for the Empire, allowing Spartan formations to participate in the consolidation of military victories rather than restricting them exclusively to the opening stages of an invasion.[1]
Its designation as the First Legion reflects its official place within the Seven Legions rather than the chronological order in which those organizations were established. Several other Imperial Legions existed before the Spartans were formally founded, with the Third and Sixth Legions appearing around Year 20 and the Fourth Legion being established around Year 50 of the Imperial Calendar.[1]

### History

#### Those Who Rule Reality
The foundations of the Spartan Legion emerged during the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s preparations for conflicts against civilizations existing beyond its own Sector. [[Victor Elderblood]] recognized that his civilization required an enormous period of development before confronting the advanced empires and supreme supernatural authorities inhabiting the greater Cosmos. He consequently initiated an extraordinary Time-acceleration ritual intended to provide the Empire with 200,000 years of internal development while only one year passed outside the affected region.[2]
The ritual encompassed the Empire's developing galactic territory and allowed its inhabitants to experience prolonged periods of technological, military and supernatural advancement. Victor and his Empresses remained actively involved through their Avatars, supervising important developments while their principal bodies sustained the ritual. The Emperor also established a plan to organize the Empire into seven specialized Legions under a unified command structure, ensuring that the various Pantheons, military organizations and subordinate civilizations could function as components of a single Imperial power.[2][4]
As the accelerated years passed, the Empire underwent substantial changes. Its population expanded through Divine blessings associated with Life, Fertility, Marriage and Motherhood, while the combined efforts of Victor and [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]] improved the biological foundations of numerous races. Bloodlines became stronger, previously incompatible species could produce increasingly stable descendants, and the Empire's technological infrastructure permitted the widespread development of interplanetary civilization.[1]
The construction and expansion of the Tower of Challenges, Babylon also contributed to the development of a widespread warrior culture. Copies of the Tower appeared on numerous inhabited planets, allowing individuals separated by astronomical distances to participate in the same interconnected training system. Its existence encouraged the population to continually improve their abilities, producing an environment particularly favorable to the recruitment and development of future Imperial soldiers.[1]
The Empire's initial Legions were created to address the administrative and security requirements of this expanding civilization. The Sixth Legion, [[The Silent Judgment]], and the Third Legion, the Black Hand of the Emperor, were established around Year 20 to handle public enforcement and clandestine intelligence operations. The [[Stellar Arsenal]] followed around Year 50, centralizing advanced research, military technology and the development of increasingly powerful weapons. [[The Imperial Guard]] was likewise expanded into the Fifth Legion to provide specialized protection for the Imperial Family and sacred Imperial sites.[1]
The Empire's conventional military establishment eventually required dedicated organizations capable of conducting offensive and defensive warfare on an enormous scale. In Year 1000 of the [[Imperial Calendar]], [[Aphrodite]], [[Scathach]] and Rose established the First Legion, the Spartans, while the [[Order of the Iron Veil]] was created as the Second Legion responsible primarily for territorial defense.[1][3]
Aphrodite selected the name Spartans in tribute to the warriors of ancient Greece. The decision was intended to preserve a recognizable element of Greek civilization within the Empire, whose development had gradually reduced the influence of its former independent Pantheons. Victor had little personal attachment to the old Greek military tradition and understood that the Legion's future accomplishments would ultimately become associated with his Empire rather than ancient Greece. Nevertheless, he accepted Aphrodite's proposal because it held considerable meaning for the Greek Goddesses who had joined his family.[1]
The Legion's training was entrusted personally to Scathach and Rose, two of the Empire's most accomplished martial specialists. Its soldiers were prepared for direct assault, invasion, conquest and the securing of hostile territory, with the expectation that Spartan formations would be the first conventional Imperial troops to engage an enemy when a major battle occurred.[1]
The organization's founding also carried considerable cultural significance. Recognizing Aphrodite's desire to preserve something of the former Greek civilization, Victor ordered the recreation of Aphrodisias in the heart of the Empire on [[Yggdrasil]]. The city became the headquarters of the First Legion and established a permanent connection between the Spartans' Imperial military identity and the heritage that inspired their name.[1]
Victor further rewarded Aphrodite with a powerful personal spaceship and an entire planet where she could restore her Greek temple. The gifts delighted Aphrodite, whose emotional reaction and release of Divine power were sufficiently intense to affect even the Dragon Gods. The other Greek Goddesses subsequently became interested in possessing their own places upon the planet, leading Victor to expand it sufficiently for their temples and associated territories.[1][3]
The founding of the Spartans therefore represented both the formal organization of the Empire's offensive military forces and the preservation of part of the cultural identity belonging to its Greek Divine members. The Legion thereafter occupied its established position as the First Imperial Legion, commanded by John and supported by the training and expertise of Scathach and Rose.[1]

### Founding and Greek Heritage

#### Origin of the Name
The name Spartans honors the warriors of ancient Sparta, whose military culture became associated with discipline, courage, endurance and a willingness to engage powerful enemies directly. Within the [[Elderblood Empire]], the designation was adopted to represent its principal offensive military force rather than to restore the political independence of ancient Greece.[1]
The choice was primarily associated with [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood, who wished to preserve a recognizable part of the civilization from which she and several other Goddesses originated. Although the Empire had incorporated numerous supernatural Pantheons and civilizations, many of their former political identities had become increasingly subordinate to the Emperor's unified system.[1]
For the surviving Greek Goddesses, the Legion's name represented a means of preserving cultural memory without challenging the authority of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] or establishing an independent Greek military faction. The Spartans remained an Imperial Legion, serving the Emperor under the same command structure governing the other military organizations.[1]
Victor accepted the designation despite his dislike of the former [[Greek Pantheon]]'s political and Divine leadership. He understood that the name would eventually be associated with the military accomplishments of his own civilization, while its original historical meaning would remain important to the Goddesses who proposed it.[1]

#### Aphrodisias
Aphrodisias is the established headquarters of the Spartan Legion, located on [[Yggdrasil]], one of the principal worlds at the heart of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. The city was recreated through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s intervention in connection with the founding of the First Legion.[1]
Its construction reinforced the relationship between the Legion's military identity and the Greek heritage preserved by [[Aphrodite]]. Rather than functioning only as an arbitrary designation, the name Spartans became associated with a physical Imperial location established to honor the civilization that inspired it.

### Military Purpose
The Spartans serve as the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s primary offensive Legion. Their responsibilities concentrate upon confronting external enemies, overcoming military defenses and expanding Imperial territory through organized campaigns.[1]

#### Direct Assault
Direct assault is the Legion's foremost military specialization. Spartan forces are trained to engage hostile military formations openly rather than depend exclusively upon infiltration, intelligence gathering or prolonged defensive operations.[1]
Their organization emphasizes the ability to take the initiative during warfare. When an enemy must be confronted directly, the Spartans are intended to form the initial offensive force, employing their soldiers, war dragons, siege specialists and warships to break resistance and open a path for subsequent Imperial operations.[1]
The Legion's reputation as the Empire's spear reflects this responsibility. Its function is to strike hostile positions and overcome the barriers preventing Imperial expansion, leaving other military organizations free to concentrate upon defensive consolidation or their own specialized duties.

#### Planetary Invasion
Planetary conquest is among the Spartans' explicitly established responsibilities. The Legion is organized to invade hostile worlds, defeat their defenders and establish the military conditions necessary for Imperial control.[1]
Its common military units provide capabilities relevant to warfare against advanced planetary civilizations. Heavily armored soldiers can participate in direct engagements, war dragons supply powerful supernatural forces, siege mages can support attacks against fortified positions, and advanced spacecraft permit offensive operations beyond the limitations of conventional ground armies.[1]
The Empire's ability to transport military forces across astronomical distances further supports this function. Its advanced Space-related technology, transportation networks and powerful vessels allow it to operate across multiple planetary systems without depending entirely upon ordinary interstellar travel.[1]
The exact structure of a Spartan planetary invasion, including its individual stages of deployment and occupation, has not been exhaustively documented.

#### Stellar Defense Breaching
The Spartans are responsible for breaking stellar defenses, allowing the Empire to confront civilizations protected by military infrastructure extending beyond an individual planet.[1]
This function distinguishes the Legion from an army designed only for conventional surface warfare. The First Legion possesses warships with destructive capabilities sufficient to threaten planets and solar systems, providing a means of confronting extremely large defensive formations and fortified celestial territories.[1]
The existence of such armaments does not establish that every Spartan soldier or vessel possesses equivalent destructive power. These capabilities belong to particular classes of military spacecraft and weapons rather than representing the baseline strength of the entire Legion.

#### Large-Scale Offensive Warfare
The Spartans are intended to lead major military campaigns against external enemies of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Their role encompasses direct confrontation, invasion, the destruction of hostile defenses and the securing of strategically important territories.[1]
Unlike the Emperor's Black Hand, which specializes in clandestine operations and intelligence, the First Legion is structured around visible military force. Its operations are therefore suited to conflicts in which the Empire intends to defeat an opposing military establishment through large-scale combat rather than covert interference.
The Legion's offensive responsibilities also differ from those of the Silent Judgment, whose principal mandate concerns public law enforcement and the punishment of corruption, rebellion and heresy within territories subject to Imperial authority.[1]

#### Territorial Conquest and Consolidation
The Spartan Legion was trained not merely to invade territories but also to conquer and secure them for the Empire. This establishes a role extending beyond the initial destruction of an enemy's forces.[1]
The consolidation of conquered territory may require the cooperation of other Imperial organizations, particularly the Second Legion's defensive forces and the Sixth Legion's enforcement personnel. Nevertheless, the Spartans themselves possess a founding mandate to secure the results of their military campaigns.[1][3]
The complete administrative procedures governing conquered worlds, including the division of responsibilities between military commanders and civilian governors, have not been specifically established for the First Legion.

### Command Structure
The Spartans operate within the unified command hierarchy of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Their supreme sovereign is [[Victor Elderblood]], while the overall military authority of the Imperial Legions belongs to [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]]. The First Legion's principal commander is John, with Rose, Eleanor and Kali available to assume command according to the circumstances.[2][1]

#### John
Main article: John
John is the principal commander of the Spartan Legion. His appointment places him in charge of the Empire's foremost offensive military organization, whose responsibilities include planetary conquest, direct assault and large-scale military campaigns.[1]
As Legion Commander, John occupies an important position within the Empire's military hierarchy. The Spartans' purpose requires leadership capable of directing enormous formations of conventional and supernatural forces, including war dragons, siege specialists, armored soldiers and advanced warships.
His authority is nevertheless subordinate to the Emperor and the Supreme Military Commander, while Rose, Eleanor and Kali are recognized as alternative commanders when the circumstances require their involvement.[1]

#### Scathach Elderblood Scarlett
Main article: [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]]
Scathach Elderblood Scarlett is one of the Spartan Legion's three founders and one of the principal individuals responsible for its training. Her involvement is especially important because of her exceptional experience as a martial artist, teacher and military commander.[1]
[[Scathach]] personally trained the Legion alongside Rose, establishing a direct connection between the First Legion's combat development and the standards upheld by some of the Empire's most accomplished warriors. Her participation ensured that the Spartans would be prepared for offensive warfare rather than simply assembled as an enormous force without the corresponding training.[1]
Her role also extends beyond the First Legion. As the Supreme Military Commander, she occupies a position overseeing the broader Imperial military establishment, allowing the Spartans to remain integrated within the unified command structure established by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].

#### Rose Adrasteia Elderblood
Main article: [[Rose Adrasteia Elderblood]]
Rose Adrasteia Elderblood is a founder, principal trainer and designated substitute commander of the Spartan Legion. She participated directly in its creation alongside [[Aphrodite]] and [[Scathach]], and personally trained its soldiers for the responsibilities associated with large-scale conquest and direct warfare.[1]
Rose's extraordinary martial skill makes her particularly suited to overseeing the development of elite combatants and soldiers expected to confront powerful supernatural enemies. Her responsibilities combine the training of the Legion with the ability to assume operational command when required.[1]
She also holds importance within the wider Imperial military establishment, where her combat abilities and experience make her a valuable commander independently of her founding relationship with the Spartans.

#### Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia
Main article: [[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]]
Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia is one of the individuals authorized to assume command of the Spartans when circumstances require an alternative to John. Her association with the First Legion is particularly notable because she also occupies an important leadership position within the Empire's defensive military establishment.[1][3]
This overlapping command responsibility demonstrates the operational relationship between the First and Second Legions. Although their principal functions differ, their leadership structures permit experienced members of the Imperial Family to assume responsibilities across the two organizations when necessary.
Eleanor's substitute-command position does not make her the permanent commander of the Spartans. John remains their established principal leader.[1]

#### Kali Elderblood
Main article: [[Kali Elderblood]]
Kali Elderblood is another member of the Imperial Family capable of assuming command of the Spartans. Her inclusion among its recognized substitute commanders reflects the importance of exceptionally powerful Divine Beings within the Empire's military leadership.[1]
Kali's position provides the Legion with access to an alternative commander possessing immense supernatural strength and considerable experience with Divine conflict. Her involvement is circumstantial rather than that of a permanent commander, allowing the First Legion to remain under John's normal leadership.[1]

#### Aphrodite Elderblood
Main article: [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood
Aphrodite Elderblood is one of the Legion's founders and the principal figure responsible for the adoption of its Spartan identity. Her involvement was motivated partly by the military requirements of the Empire and partly by a desire to preserve the cultural heritage of ancient Greece.[1]
Aphrodite's role differs from that of [[Scathach]] and Rose, who are explicitly identified as the Legion's personal trainers. Her principal documented contributions concern its foundation, name and historical symbolism. She is not listed among the established substitute commanders, and her cultural patronage should not be confused with permanent operational command of the First Legion.[1]

### Military Composition
The Spartan Legion includes several categories of conventional and supernatural combatants designed for large-scale offensive warfare. Its commonly identified units are war dragons, armored soldiers, siege mages and advanced military spaceships.[1]
These categories provide the Legion with a combination of direct combat strength, specialized supernatural techniques and large-scale technological firepower. Their existence allows the Spartans to confront threats ranging from conventional planetary armies to civilizations possessing powerful stellar defenses.

#### War Dragons
War dragons are among the Spartan Legion's commonly identified military forces. Their inclusion provides the organization with access to Draconic combatants capable of operating alongside conventional soldiers and advanced military technology.[1]
Dragons possess extraordinary supernatural potential and are among the most formidable categories of beings associated with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s civilization. Their participation in the First Legion reflects the Empire's ability to incorporate immensely powerful races into conventional military organizations rather than relying exclusively upon separate personal armies.[1]
The exact species, individual identities, number and personal destructive capabilities of the war dragons assigned to the Spartans remain unspecified. Their inclusion should not automatically be interpreted as meaning that every Dragon or Blood Dragon created by Victor belongs to the First Legion.

#### Armored Soldiers
Heavily armored soldiers form one of the conventional combat categories associated with the Spartans. Their role is consistent with the Legion's specialization in direct assault, invasion and offensive warfare against established enemy forces.[1]
The Empire's technological advancement and development of stronger races provide a favorable environment for maintaining powerful conventional military personnel. However, the precise equipment, armor classifications, weapons and individual ranks employed by Spartan infantry have not been separately identified.

#### Siege Mages
Siege mages are supernatural specialists incorporated into the Legion's offensive forces. Their inclusion reflects the importance of [[Magic]] within Imperial military operations, particularly when confronting heavily defended positions or enemies possessing supernatural fortifications.[1]
Their presence allows the First Legion to combine conventional military formations with specialized magical capabilities. The exact spells, schools of Magic and individual destructive techniques used by Spartan siege mages remain unidentified.

#### Military Spaceships
The Spartans possess advanced military spaceships capable of devastating planets and solar systems. These vessels constitute one of the Legion's most significant sources of large-scale destructive power and allow its offensive capabilities to extend well beyond planetary surface warfare.[1]
The Empire possesses technology incorporating advanced Space manipulation, teleportation and Runes, allowing the creation of structures whose internal capacities can greatly exceed their external dimensions. Such developments are particularly relevant to a civilization maintaining military forces on an interplanetary or greater scale.[1]
Nevertheless, the precise ship classes, fleet numbers, internal capacities and standard armaments of the Spartans have not been comprehensively documented. The extraordinary vessel gifted personally to [[Aphrodite]] during the Legion's founding demonstrates the Empire's technological capabilities but is not explicitly identified as the standard design of the First Legion's warships.

### Military Strength
The Spartans constitute one of the largest and most important conventional military organizations within the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Their strategic importance results from their combination of enormous military formations, highly trained combatants, powerful supernatural units and advanced military technology.[1][3]
The First and Second Legions serve as the Empire's principal offensive and defensive forces, respectively, and are among its most numerous military organizations. This distinguishes them from specialized groups such as the Imperial Guard, whose composition emphasizes exceptional individual combatants rather than the maintenance of enormous conventional armies.[1][3]
The Spartans' exact personnel strength, number of war dragons, fleet size and distribution of combatants across different military ranks have not been individually established. The existence of enormous Imperial populations or billions of soldiers within the broader military system does not provide an exact numerical count for the First Legion alone.

#### Training and Combat Readiness
The Spartans were personally trained by [[Scathach]] and Rose, two of the Empire's most accomplished martial specialists. This training was intended to prepare the Legion for invasion, conquest and the securing of territories rather than simply providing its members with ordinary military instruction.[1]
The wider Empire's warrior culture also contributes to the development of its military personnel. The interconnected Tower of Challenges encourages individuals to continually improve their abilities, while the Empire's advanced technology and Divine support permit increasingly powerful races and supernatural combatants to develop.[1]
The Legion's exact training curriculum, progression requirements and individual military academies have not been established, but its founding under Scathach and Rose confirms that direct personal instruction from exceptionally powerful combat specialists formed part of its initial development.[1]

### Relationship with the Order of the Iron Veil
Main article: [[Order of the Iron Veil]]
The Order of the Iron Veil is the Second Imperial Legion and the principal defensive counterpart to the Spartans. Both Legions were established around Year 1000 of the [[Imperial Calendar]] as the Empire developed a more comprehensive conventional military structure.[1][3]
Their responsibilities are complementary. The First Legion specializes in direct assault, invasion and the conquest of hostile territories, while the Second Legion concentrates upon planetary defense, fortifications, defensive rings, shields and the protection of established Imperial possessions.[1][3]

The distinction between the Spartans and the other Legions does not make its military responsibilities completely isolated. The Empire maintains a unified hierarchy through which forces and resources can be coordinated under the Emperor and his Supreme Military Commander.[2]
The First Legion's offensive mandate is especially relevant to the larger military system because major campaigns can involve multiple categories of Imperial resources. However, the specific procedures governing combined operations, the assignment of auxiliary forces and individual inter-Legion campaigns have not been comprehensively documented.

### Strategic Importance
The Spartans provide the [[Elderblood Empire]] with a dedicated organization for large-scale offensive warfare, allowing the Emperor to employ conventional military forces against hostile civilizations without depending entirely upon the personal intervention of his strongest family members.[1]
This distinction is important because [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Empire contains numerous individuals whose personal combat capabilities greatly exceed those of ordinary military units. The Emperor, his wives, his daughters and various elite supernatural beings possess extraordinary powers, but the existence of such individuals does not eliminate the need for organized armies capable of conducting prolonged campaigns and securing conquered territories.
The Spartans address this requirement through a structured military formation combining enormous numbers of troops, supernatural combatants, specialized magical personnel and technologically advanced spacecraft. Their purpose is to provide a conventional offensive framework capable of operating across the Empire's expanding territories.[1]
The Legion also plays an important role in the strategic separation of Imperial responsibilities. Intelligence organizations can identify threats, technological organizations can develop military assets and defensive organizations can secure existing territory, while the Spartans remain responsible for the principal offensive operations required to overcome external resistance.
Its cultural identity further distinguishes it from other military organizations. The preservation of the Spartan name and the establishment of Aphrodisias create a direct connection between Imperial expansion and the historical traditions of one of the civilizations incorporated into Victor's Empire.[1]

- [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] — Supreme Military Commander, co-founder and principal trainer of the Spartans.[1]

- [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood — Co-founder and principal proponent of the Legion's Spartan name, preserving an element of Greek cultural heritage within the Empire.[1]

- John — Principal commander of the First Legion.[1]

- War Dragons — Draconic combatants incorporated into the Legion's offensive forces.[1]

- Warship Personnel — Personnel associated with the Legion's advanced military spaceships, including vessels capable of planetary and solar-system destruction. Their individual identities and ranks have not been established.[1]

### Trivia
- The Spartans are the First Legion of the [[Elderblood Empire]], but they were not the first Imperial Legion to be created chronologically. The Third and Sixth Legions appeared much earlier in the [[Imperial Calendar]].[1]
- The Legion was established in Year 1000 of the Imperial Calendar alongside the [[Order of the Iron Veil]].[1]
- Its name honors the warriors of ancient Sparta and was proposed by [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood as a means of preserving Greek cultural heritage.[1]
- [[Victor Elderblood]] accepted the name despite his unfavorable history with the former [[Greek Pantheon]], recognizing its importance to Aphrodite and the other Greek Goddesses.[1]
- The city of Aphrodisias was recreated on [[Yggdrasil]] and became the headquarters of the First Legion.[1]
- Aphrodite received a personal spaceship with more than 300,000 km² of internal space and room for approximately 50 million beings. Its external dimensions were only about 60 km by 15 km, made possible through Rune-based spatial expansion.[1]
- The ship gifted to Aphrodite was capable of destroying a solar system, but its personal ownership should be distinguished from the First Legion's general military fleet.[1]
- Victor also granted Aphrodite an entire planet upon which she could reconstruct her Greek temple. The other Greek Goddesses subsequently became involved in restoring their own religious and cultural traditions there.[1][3]
- The Spartans were personally trained by [[Scathach]] and Rose, rather than being placed under the exclusive military tutelage of Aphrodite.[1]
- John is the Legion's principal commander, while Rose, Eleanor and Kali are recognized as possible substitutes according to the circumstances.[1]
- The Legion's established personnel include war dragons, armored soldiers, siege mages and advanced military spaceships.[1]
- Certain Spartan warships possess sufficient destructive capabilities to annihilate entire solar systems.[1]
- The First Legion is intended to enter combat before other conventional Imperial assault formations during major battles.[1]
- The Spartans and the Order of the Iron Veil form the Empire's principal offensive and defensive military partnership, commonly represented by the imagery of a spear and a shield.[1][3]
- The Legion's internal slogan is "We are the spear of the Empire, For the Emperor!"[1]
- The precise number of Spartan soldiers, war dragons and spaceships has not been independently specified, despite the Legion's status as one of the Empire's most numerous military organizations.
