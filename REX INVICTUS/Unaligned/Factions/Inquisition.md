The Inquisition is an ancient Human supernatural military organization created by the Vatican Church to train individuals capable of defending Humanity against dangerous supernatural beings.[1] Its combatants, commonly called [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] or Exorcists, traditionally use Holy/Faith Energy, blessed weapons, Holy Water, religious scripture, prayers and sacred artifacts to compensate for the enormous physical difference between ordinary Humans and supernatural races such as Vampires and Demons.[2]
Although the Inquisition's original intended purpose was to serve as a defense for vulnerable people against supernatural predators, the organization became increasingly corrupted under the leadership of Pope Alexander. By the modern era, sections of the old Inquisition had adopted an openly supremacist ideology that treated supernatural existence itself as a crime, attacked otherwise non-hostile supernatural families for political advantage, conducted experiments upon Humans and children, created artificial Human-Vampire Hybrids, and secretly cooperated with individuals and factions that directly contradicted the religious principles the organization claimed to represent.[3][4][5][6]
This corruption culminated during the Demonic War. After the Demonic assault upon the Vatican and the assassination of Alexander, the Heavenly Father openly condemned the Inquisition's crimes, withdrew the autonomy he had historically allowed it through his philosophy of Free [[Will Solace|Will]], and ordered the entire organization investigated.[7] Following the destruction of Vatican City, the Inquisition survived but was placed under direct Angelic leadership. Its doctrine was subsequently rebuilt around strict anti-discrimination rules and the protection of innocent beings regardless of whether they were Human, Vampire, Werewolf, Witch, Oni, Spirit or another Race.[8]

### Overview
The Inquisition historically operated as the Vatican's principal hidden military force within the supernatural world. Its existence was largely concealed from ordinary Human society before the Demonic War exposed supernatural civilization to the general population. The organization trained Humans capable of fighting creatures whose natural physical abilities vastly exceeded those of ordinary people and maintained personnel, intelligence networks and operational facilities in multiple countries.[1][9][10]
The organization should not be confused with the Angels themselves. The Vatican Church created the Inquisition, while direct Angelic administration only began after the organization's corruption was exposed during the Demonic War.[1][7][8] Historically, the [[Heavenly Father]] allowed considerable autonomy to Human religious institutions because of his belief in Free [[Will Solace|Will]]; this lack of direct interference ultimately allowed individuals such as Alexander and General James to distort the organization far beyond its intended function.
[[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] varied enormously in strength. Ordinary members such as Jimmy and Thomas relied heavily upon prayers, Holy Water, scripture and blessed weapons, while senior figures such as the Generals could wield vastly greater quantities of Faith Energy and perform complex supernatural techniques.[2][9][11] The organization additionally supplemented its natural Hunters through scientific and supernatural experimentation, culminating in artificial Hybrid soldiers capable of combining Human Hunter [[Magic]] with portions of Vampire physiology.[12][5]

### History

#### Foundation and Original Purpose
The Inquisition was established by the Vatican Church as a Human response to the dangers presented by the hidden supernatural world. When the organization is first explained in [[Ch05 - Intermission I|Chapter 5]], its purpose is described as training Humans to hunt Vampires who cause trouble in the Human world.[1] Chapter 708 later clarifies the broader principle behind its intended function: the Inquisition was supposed to be a protective force for vulnerable people and a weapon against individuals who preyed upon or exploited them.[8]
The original mission therefore should not be confused with Alexander's later objective of eliminating every supernatural species. Hunting a Vampire who murdered or abducted Humans was consistent with the organization's intended function; attacking a supernatural individual solely because of their Race was the corruption that eventually became institutionalized within significant portions of the old Inquisition.
[[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] were equipped specifically to overcome supernatural advantages. Holy Water, scripture, prayers and Faith-based weapons allowed even relatively inexperienced Hunters to threaten ordinary Vampires. [[Ch14 - Rex Invictus VII|Chapter 14]] demonstrates Jimmy invoking protection through Biblical scripture to produce a golden barrier and a giant sword of golden Energy, while Thomas transforms a crucifix into a blessed sword and receives a physical blessing increasing his body and stamina.[2] Thomas' poorly controlled attack was nevertheless stated to possess enough [[Holy Energy]] to cause permanent damage to an ordinary Vampire, demonstrating why even physically weaker Human Hunters remained dangerous to the supernatural population.

#### Early Organization
Before the Demonic War, the Inquisition's central headquarters was located in Vatican City.[10] Pope Alexander acted as the supreme head of the organization, with several Generals serving directly beneath him. [[Ch15 - Rex Invictus VIII|Chapter 15]] establishes that the Inquisition possessed four Generals during the early story, while Chapter 30 identifies the senior [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] present around Alexander as James, Kurtz and Leonardo in addition to the younger Japanese General Aikyo Mizuki.[9][10]
According to Jimmy's explanation, the four Generals were considered capable of fighting extremely old Vampires, and he credited Mizuki with exterminating ten Vampire clans in Japan.[9] Jimmy also described the Generals with fanatical language such as being chosen by God; because [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] openly questioned his religious exaggeration, those statements should not automatically be treated as neutral measurements of every General's actual capabilities.
The Vatican also possessed an internal threat-response system. When [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett|Scathach Scarlett]] arrived at the Vatican in Chapter 30 after Mizuki's attack on Ruby, Alexander immediately ordered General Kurtz to activate a Level 6 alert.[10] The meanings of the lower alert levels have never been explained and should not be invented.

#### Corruption Under Alexander
Over time, the old Inquisition's mission was distorted from opposing dangerous individuals into hostility toward supernatural beings as a whole. This development was not uniform—different [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] possessed different motivations and levels of fanaticism—but by the period of Alexander's leadership the organization had repeatedly demonstrated willingness to attack supernatural individuals who had committed no crime against Humanity.
One of the earliest examples occurred in [[Ch16 - Rex Invictus IX|Chapter 16]]. After learning that the leader of Clan Fulger had lost the title of Vampire Count, the Inquisition attempted to exploit the Clan's temporary political weakness by kidnapping its heiress, [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]], while she was living in the United States.[3] [[Carlos Reiss]] and Maria attacked Sasha's household, killed her servants—including Julia—and intended to use Sasha as a bargaining chip to further weaken the [[Fulger Clan]]. The operation was political rather than defensive and directly contradicts the organization's later-stated original purpose of protecting vulnerable people.
The organization also developed increasingly extreme racial prejudice. Many Hunters came to describe Vampires and other supernatural races indiscriminately as monsters, demons or beings requiring purification, regardless of an individual's actual behavior. Chapter 366 eventually makes the ideological corruption explicit: Alexander's objective is identified as the elimination of all Supernatural Beings, a goal Mizuki and [[Abe-no-Seimei]] condemn as the ideology of a tyrannical supremacist rather than a legitimate religious duty.[6]

#### Internal Opposition
Not every Hunter accepted the direction taken by Alexander and the senior leadership. Mizuki eventually left the Inquisition while continuing to maintain intelligence contacts throughout its worldwide network. By Chapter 312 she regarded the organization as deeply corrupted and was investigating evidence that at least one faction of [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] was cooperating with Demons.[4]
Mizuki also discovered one of General James' secret laboratories. The facility contained children who had been used as experimental subjects and psychologically reduced to what she described as broken puppets.[6] Mizuki burned the laboratory, rescued the surviving children and subsequently cooperated with [[Victor Elderblood]] in locating and destroying additional facilities connected to James.
Other individuals abandoned the organization for similar reasons. [[Liena Walter]], once a famous Hunter, eventually retired after discovering what the Inquisition was doing.[13][14] Maria also ultimately separated from the Church after her transformation and exposure to the consequences of its actions.
The old organization was therefore not ideologically uniform. Its corruption was institutional and severe, but individual Hunters could oppose, expose, abandon or secretly undermine it.

#### Infiltration and Espionage
By Chapter 312 the Inquisition's internal security had deteriorated enough that Jimmy and Thomas, two ordinary [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] originally encountered by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], were secretly functioning as intelligence assets for Victor, Ruby and Mizuki.[4] Information obtained through them allowed outside factions to monitor developments inside the organization despite Mizuki having formally left.
The organization was simultaneously being penetrated from other directions. During the Demonic War, a traitor within the Human forces supplied the Demons with an opportunity to undermine Angelic protection at the Vatican. Jonah, an assistant associated with General Leonardo, attacked the Seraphim Ariel using a projectile made from Hell-associated material and saturated with destructive Miasma, severely compromising her ability to defend the Humans around her.[15] Jonah was immediately killed by [[Zaniel]], but the betrayal damaged the trust between the Angels and the Inquisition during an active Demonic invasion.

#### Demonic War
The Vatican became a direct battlefield during the Demonic War. [[Sitri]], the 12th Duke of Hell, led an enormous Demonic force against the Inquisition and intended to destroy its leadership.[16] The Angels intervened in defense of the Human forces, with Ariel functioning as one of the principal celestial protectors of the Vatican.
Despite the survival of the organization itself, Vatican City suffered catastrophic destruction. Chapter 708 later states that the Vatican had been wiped off the map because of Sitri's attack.[8] Vatican City should therefore be listed as the Inquisition's former headquarters, not as its confirmed current base.
The Demons also targeted Alexander directly. War and the [[Horseman of Death]] attacked the Pope after Ariel had been temporarily disabled. During their confrontation the [[Heavenly Father]] withdrew the Power and support sustaining Alexander, causing the ancient Hunter's body to rapidly deteriorate.[7] Alexander realized that his God had abandoned him immediately before War and Death completed their attack.

#### Purge
Alexander's death did not destroy the Inquisition. Instead, the [[Heavenly Father]] used the crisis to purge the corrupted leadership.
In Chapter 561, the Heavenly Father directly condemns Alexander for allowing the organization to abuse the Free [[Will Solace|Will]] it had been granted and declares that its sins were severe enough to horrify even Demons.[7] Following Alexander's death, Ariel publicly announced the Heavenly Father's judgment: the Inquisition would lose its autonomy, the entire organization would be investigated, and those responsible for its atrocities would have their Souls sent to Limbo.
This moment represents the beginning of the modern Inquisition's reformation. War and Death themselves recognized that the Heavenly Father had effectively allowed the Demonic attack to eliminate the organization's most influential corrupted figure so that he could "clean his house".[7]
The purge also explains why General James no longer belonged to the Inquisition afterward. By Chapter 702, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly identifies James as no longer being a member of the organization.[17] James subsequently became associated with [[New Dawn]] and other hostile factions rather than the reformed Church.

#### Angelic Reformation
By Chapter 708, the Inquisition had survived the destruction of the Vatican and undergone a fundamental ideological transformation. It was now being run by a literal Angel, placing the organization under direct celestial oversight for the first time.[8]
The Angels simultaneously became far more active in Human religious affairs. Because Humans were recording Angelic statements and immediately treating them as religious doctrine, the Angels separately assigned a Seraphim to supervise what was being written so their Creator's teachings could not once again be distorted by Human institutions.[8] The Seraphim responsible for supervising religious records should not automatically be identified as the same unnamed Angel who leads the Inquisition; the chapter describes the two functions separately.
The [[Heavenly Father]] did not completely abandon his belief in Free [[Will Solace|Will]], but he decided that allowing atrocities to continue unchecked in organizations claiming to represent him was no longer acceptable. The Angels were granted greater freedom to operate in the Human world, supervise religious institutions and punish individuals committing atrocities in the Heavenly Father's name.[8]
The reformed Inquisition instituted a strict anti-discrimination policy. Protection was extended not only to Humans but to innocent Vampires, [[Werewolves]], Oni, Witches, Spirits and other supernatural beings. Individuals who remained prejudiced against Non-Humans could not become or remain members, and Chapter 708 explicitly states that this requirement even applied to Angels.[8]
The modern purpose of the organization was consequently restored to what the narrative describes as its intended function: a protective shield for vulnerable beings and a weapon against those who exploit them, regardless of Race.[8]

#### Current Hierarchy
The exact internal hierarchy following the Angelic purge has not been revealed. Chapter 708 confirms only that the surviving Inquisition is being run by an unnamed Angel and operates beneath much stricter Heavenly supervision.[8] It is therefore unsafe to assume that the old positions, Generals, laboratories or Papal chain of command continued unchanged after the reformation.
Likewise, the post-reformation status of surviving old Generals such as Kurtz and Leonardo has not been explicitly revisited in sufficient detail. Because the new Inquisition prohibits anti-supernatural racial prejudice, any surviving old member would necessarily have to comply with the new doctrine in order to remain part of the organization.[8]

#### Threat Levels
The historical organization possessed numbered security or threat alerts. The only explicitly shown example is Level 6, activated by Alexander when [[Scathach]] arrived at the Vatican in Chapter 30.[10] No canonical explanation has been given for Levels 1–5 or whether levels above 6 existed.

### Recruitment and Training
The majority of historical Inquisition combatants were Humans trained to compensate for their species' natural physical disadvantage through Faith, supernatural techniques, teamwork and specialized weaponry. Recruitment methods varied. Maria and [[Carlos Reiss]] were orphaned street children who survived through petty crime until Carlos was recruited by a priest while they were teenagers; Maria joined him, and the pair were subsequently trained to hunt whatever supernatural targets the Church assigned them.[19]
This history demonstrates that the Inquisition did not recruit exclusively from children raised within Church institutions. However, the organization clearly valued early indoctrination. During Ruby's later analysis of the Hybrid project, she suspected that many experimental [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] were likely orphans taken in and raised by the Church because training an individual from childhood made it easier to shape them into completely loyal weapons.[5] This was Ruby's reconstruction of the organization's methods rather than confirmation that every Hunter or experimental subject possessed the same background.
Chapter 366 independently confirms that General James actually did experiment upon children in at least some of his facilities.[6] The children rescued by Mizuki had been psychologically devastated by what was done to them, establishing that child exploitation was not merely a theory derived from the Texas laboratory.
The reformed Inquisition applies a fundamentally different ideological requirement to recruitment. Chapter 708 states that individuals prejudiced against Non-Humans are forbidden from integrating into or remaining within the organization, with the rule extending even to Angels.[8]

### Hunters
[[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] are the trained supernatural combatants historically forming the military core of the Inquisition. They remain Human in origin unless modified through experiments and rely upon Faith, sacred equipment and specialized combat training to confront creatures naturally stronger than themselves.
Ordinary Hunters can still pose a serious threat to supernatural opponents because Holy/Faith Energy interacts particularly effectively with beings traditionally vulnerable to sacred power. [[Ch14 - Rex Invictus VII|Chapter 14]] demonstrates two relatively ordinary Hunters producing barriers, conjured weapons and physical enhancement powerful enough to concern Noble Vampires.[2]
The enormous difference between ordinary Hunters and senior Generals should nevertheless be maintained. Jimmy and Thomas exhausted large portions of their available Energy using relatively simple attacks, whereas elite Hunters such as Leonardo could manipulate vast amounts of golden Energy directly without relying upon conventional incantations.[11]

### Hunter Magic and Faith Energy
The Inquisition's principal standardized supernatural system is generally described through Faith, Holy Power or golden [[Divine Energy]]. [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] channel this Power through sincere belief, prayers, scripture, sacred symbols and blessed objects. The exact relationship between personal Faith, Angelic/Divine authority and the greater Faith Energy system is more complex than a conventional [[Magic]] spell system, but [[Ch14 - Rex Invictus VII|Chapter 14]] demonstrates that even ordinary Hunters can invoke supernatural effects through religious practice.[2]

#### Holy Water
Holy Water is standard anti-supernatural equipment among [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]]. Jimmy carried it during his confrontation with Violet and used it while preparing his Faith-based defense.[2] Its effectiveness varies according to the nature and strength of the target.

#### Blessed Weapons
[[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] use sacred or blessed weapons capable of conducting Faith Energy. These can range from conventional physical weapons empowered by golden Energy to supernatural manifestations created through religious symbols. Because the Power is especially dangerous to ordinary Vampires, an experienced Hunter can threaten an opponent possessing greater raw physical ability if the sacred attack connects properly.[2]

#### Incantationless Faith
Incantations are not an absolute requirement for every Hunter. Leonardo explicitly states in Chapter 345 that he does not need them because his Faith comes directly from his heart.[11] He could immediately produce enormous quantities of golden Energy and later demonstrated Energy weapons, defensive shields, projections, flight-related constructs and extensive bodily enhancement across Chapters 345–347.[11][20][21]
Leonardo should be treated as an example of an exceptionally advanced Hunter rather than the baseline for ordinary Inquisition personnel.

#### Non-Standard Techniques
Not every high-ranking member relied exclusively upon standardized Biblical Hunter techniques. Mizuki, while serving as one of the four Generals, possessed extensive abilities derived from her independent Japanese supernatural heritage and training. Her presence demonstrates that the old Inquisition was willing to employ powerful specialists whose personal supernatural systems differed from the standard methods used by ordinary Vatican [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]].[9]

### High-Level Support
The pre-reformation Inquisition possessed methods capable of restoring at least some high-ranking personnel after death. In Chapter 173, [[Scathach]] remembers having previously killed Generals Leonardo and Kurtz, despite both later appearing alive again.[22] Alexander is associated with a resurrection ability in material concerning his powers, but the exact mechanics of how the two Generals returned are not completely explained in the relevant chapter. It is therefore safer to describe this as historical resurrection/restoration support rather than a normal ability available to all [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]].

### Hybrid Experimentation
Main article: Hybrids
One of the darkest projects of the old Inquisition was the attempt to artificially create Human-Vampire Hybrid [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]]. These experiments were intended to reduce the enormous biological gap between Human Hunters and supernatural species by combining the adaptability and Faith-based abilities of Humans with selected characteristics of Noble Vampires.[12][5]
The project was associated particularly strongly with General James, who maintained secret laboratories and personal experimental soldiers. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction discovered one major facility in Texas containing numerous failed specimens, while Mizuki independently located another James-controlled laboratory containing experimented-on children and later cooperated with Victor in destroying additional facilities.[12][6]

#### Objective
The Hybrid project attempted to create soldiers capable of combining:
- Human physiology and adaptability.
- Hunter [[Magic]] / Faith Energy.
- Enhanced Vampire physical characteristics.
- Vampire regeneration.
- Greater strength, speed and durability than ordinary Humans.
- Extreme loyalty to the Inquisition.
The desired result was effectively a Human Hunter capable of competing with supernatural beings without completely losing access to Humanity's Faith-based abilities.
The project never achieved a truly stable ideal result.

#### Experimental Facilities
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s group discovered a hidden Inquisition installation containing rows of Human-Vampire experimental subjects suspended in tubes filled with green liquid.[12] Specimens were identified through alphanumeric codes such as F-061, F-068 and F-039, while surviving experimental records classified different subjects according to their mutation and potential.
Many of the bodies were technically being maintained by the liquid but could not survive independently. Ruby concluded that awakening some of them would immediately result in death.[12] The substance used in the tanks possessed properties associated with healing and pain suppression, allowing severely damaged experimental bodies to remain functional long enough for further procedures.
The laboratory and its research material were later studied extensively by [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]], whose expertise allowed her to reconstruct much of the program's biological methodology.[5]

#### Selection of Subjects
The experiments began with Human subjects, but not every Human could successfully undergo the procedure. Records indicated that only individuals meeting certain unknown conditions were selected for more advanced Hybridization.[5]
Ruby suspected that many of the [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] used by the organization were orphans raised under Church control because the Inquisition historically preferred training loyal soldiers from an early age. This should remain identified as Ruby's inference rather than a confirmed universal recruitment method.[5]
What is directly confirmed is that James used children in experimental facilities. Mizuki personally discovered and destroyed one such laboratory and rescued the surviving children.[6]

#### Scientific Knowledge
The Hybrid program demonstrated that Human researchers had accumulated surprisingly sophisticated knowledge of Vampire biology. The procedures required extremely precise manipulation of Human and Vampire genetic material, and Ruby was impressed that ordinary Human scientists had managed to understand enough of Vampire physiology to produce partially viable artificial Hybrids.[5]
The enormous number of failures demonstrates the limits of that knowledge. Many experimental bodies possessed DNA so extensively damaged that they could not biologically remain alive without artificial or supernatural assistance.

#### Zandriel — Patient Zero
[[Zandriel]] was officially documented as:
Experiment Z-000 — Patient Zero.[12][5]
He was the first documented Human-Vampire subject whose genetic structure could be considered genuinely complete rather than catastrophically damaged. Unlike many later artificial Hybrids, Zandriel had already possessed Vampire ancestry before the Inquisition altered him. Ruby determined that he had originally been approximately one-quarter Vampire, most likely through a Noble Vampire ancestor, giving him superior physical characteristics compared with an ordinary Human while still allowing him access to Hunter [[Magic]].[5]
The experiments increased his Vampire ancestry from approximately 1/4 to 1/2. Genetically, Ruby considered him an unusually successful Hybrid because his DNA was structurally complete, but the result remained functionally defective. Zandriel could not properly access the full powers of his Vampire side, could not naturally become stronger with age like a true Vampire, retained a finite lifespan, and prolonged use of Hunter Magic damaged the Vampire component of his body.[5]
Ruby estimated that had Zandriel been left at his natural one-quarter Vampire ancestry rather than subjected to further experiments, he might have lived approximately 200–300 years.[5]

#### Bruno
[[Father Bruno]] represented a much less stable result. Ruby described his DNA as an organized mess with important portions entirely missing.[5] Under normal biological conditions he should not have remained functional, causing Ruby to conclude that some external mechanism—possibly supernatural or magical—must have been sustaining his body.
This possibility should not be generalized into a confirmed mechanism for every Hybrid. Chapter 319 specifically presents it as Ruby's conclusion while examining Bruno.

#### Carlos Reiss
[[Carlos Reiss]] underwent an even more extreme transformation. Ruby found approximately half of his genetic structure missing or inaccessible, leaving his condition more unstable than Bruno's.[5] Comparison with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s constantly changing Progenitor DNA produced an unusually high correspondence with the missing component, leading Ruby to theorize that Carlos' experiment involved genetic material connected to [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] or one of Vlad's descendants.
This is specifically a clue concerning Carlos. It should not be rewritten as confirmation that the entire Inquisition Hybrid program universally depended upon Progenitor blood.
Carlos could simultaneously use powers associated with his Human Hunter side and abnormal Vampire-derived abilities, but his body remained fundamentally unstable.

#### Later Experimental Soldiers
General James continued developing modified [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] beyond the earliest Z-series experiments. By the Japan conflict he possessed soldiers such as August and Sylvie, designated respectively A-000 and S-000.[18][11] They were significantly more developed combatants than many earlier failures and operated as trusted members of James' personal forces, but they remained vulnerable to the underlying limitations of artificial Hybrid physiology.
August had been promoted to Commander following Carlos' death and arrived in Japan with Sylvie while commanding part of James' personal army.[18]

#### Limitations
The Inquisition never solved the fundamental incompatibility between Human and Vampire biology. Known problems included incomplete or collapsing DNA, limited lifespans, inability to naturally gain strength with age as true Vampires do, interference between Faith/Hunter [[Magic]] and the Vampire portion of the body, dependence upon external support in severely unstable specimens, and extremely high fatality rates.[12][5]
The experiments therefore produced powerful individual soldiers but failed to create the perfectly stable Human-Vampire warrior originally sought by the organization.

### Doctrine
The ideological history of the Inquisition can be divided into three distinct periods.

#### Original Doctrine
The original Inquisition was intended to provide Humans—physically one of the weakest major intelligent species—with a method of defending themselves against predatory supernatural entities. This basic principle is why [[Ch05 - Intermission I|Chapter 5]] initially describes its mission in terms of hunting Vampires causing trouble within Human society.[1]

#### Alexander's Doctrine
Alexander gradually transformed the organization's defensive purpose into supernatural extermination. By Chapter 366 his objective is explicitly characterized as eliminating all Supernatural Beings.[6] This worldview ignored the enormous differences between individual supernatural beings and treated Race itself as sufficient justification for persecution.
This doctrine also created an obvious contradiction: the Inquisition claimed to act for Humanity while cooperating with supernatural criminals, Demonic factions and other organizations whenever doing so served the ambitions of its leadership. Mizuki's investigation in Chapter 312 specifically uncovered evidence that a faction within the [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] was working with Demons.[4]

#### Current Doctrine
The Angel-led Inquisition explicitly rejects collective racial guilt. Chapter 708 states that the organization protects weak and innocent Humans, Vampires, [[Werewolves]], Oni, Witches and Spirits without distinguishing whether they are Human or supernatural.[8] Membership likewise requires abandoning anti-Non-Human prejudice, and the rule applies even to Angels themselves.
The reformed organization should therefore not list Vampires, Werewolves, Witches or Yokai as blanket current enemies. Its enemies are individuals and groups who threaten or exploit the vulnerable, regardless of Race.

### Relationship with the Heavenly Father
Main article: [[Heavenly Father]]
The Inquisition historically claimed to act in the name of the Heavenly Father, but for most of its existence he did not personally direct its ordinary decisions. His philosophy emphasized Free [[Will Solace|Will]], and that freedom extended to the Human organizations acting under his religion.[7]
The old leadership abused this autonomy. Alexander and other senior figures permitted acts that fundamentally contradicted the principles the organization claimed to defend, eventually causing the Heavenly Father to intervene directly. During Alexander's assassination in Chapter 561, he stripped the Pope of his supernatural support, condemned the organization's crimes and abolished its institutional autonomy.[7]
Following the war, the Heavenly Father became significantly more active in reforming Human religious institutions. The Inquisition was placed under Angelic leadership and strict oversight, while Angels were empowered to punish atrocities committed in their Creator's name.[8]

### Relationship with the Angels
Main article: Angels
The Angels historically functioned as allies and religious authorities rather than the founders of the Inquisition. During the Demonic War, Angels directly defended the Vatican and its Human population, with Ariel serving as one of the major celestial commanders protecting the Inquisition.[16][15]
The relationship deteriorated temporarily when Jonah betrayed Ariel, demonstrating that the Humans and Angels could no longer automatically trust one another during the conflict.[15] Alexander's death and the [[Heavenly Father]]'s purge subsequently transformed their relationship entirely: instead of merely assisting the Human organization from outside, Angels began directly supervising and leading it.
Chapter 708 establishes both an unnamed Angel operating the Inquisition and a separate Seraphim appointed to supervise the religious records produced from Angelic teachings.[8] Unless a later chapter identifies them as the same individual, those two positions should remain distinct.

### Relationship with Supernatural Races
The Inquisition's relationship with supernatural species changed more radically than almost any other part of the organization. Historically, many [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] treated Vampires, [[Werewolves]], Demons, Witches, Yokai and related beings as inherent enemies. Under Alexander, this prejudice was institutionalized into the objective of eliminating supernatural life itself.[6]
The modern Inquisition instead judges individuals according to their actions. A peaceful Vampire or Werewolf is eligible for protection, while a Human exploiting innocent people can become an enemy of the organization.[8] The policy represents a complete inversion of the old racial doctrine and prevents members from treating an entire species as guilty for crimes committed by individual members.
This does not mean dangerous supernatural beings ceased being targets. Vampires, Demons, Werewolves, Spirits, Yokai and other entities remain potential threats when they prey upon innocents; what changed is that supernatural Race alone is no longer treated as sufficient evidence of guilt.

### Current Purpose and Status
The Inquisition remains active following the Demonic War, but the institution operating after Chapter 708 differs fundamentally from the organization led by Alexander. Vatican City can no longer be treated as its current headquarters because the old Vatican was destroyed during [[Sitri]]'s attack.[8] The location of the reformed organization's primary headquarters has not been revealed.
Its current explicitly established principles are the protection of vulnerable and innocent beings regardless of Race, opposition to individuals who exploit those weaker than themselves, direct accountability for abuses committed under religious authority, and strict rejection of racial prejudice within its membership.[8] The old objective of eradicating supernatural species has been abolished.
The organization remains associated with the [[Biblical Pantheon]] and its Angelic hierarchy. By the later Imperial Era, the surviving supernatural political structure of Earth—including the Biblical Pantheon—was incorporated into the [[Elderblood Empire]].[25] However, no later chapter presently gives a detailed independent description of the Inquisition's headquarters, modern ranks or exact chain of command within the Empire, so those details should remain listed as unknown rather than inferred.
