Chapter 1203 describes an alliance of space pirates and never names it. The title of this article is a description, not a name from the text. The alliance appears at the edge of a scarcely explored sector, takes an abandoned mining complex as its base, and loses that base to the Imperial navy.[1]

### Overview
The pirates are the last of the small threats in the chapter. They are not rulers, not a trading consortium, and not a political order. They do not want planets. They want ships, the equipment on those ships, and payment from companies that would rather buy a safe passage.[1]
The base is an ancient mining complex, already abandoned when they find it. From it they can hit a route used by ships moving between colonies and industrial installations, and they can fall back into nearby asteroids.[1]
No patron is named. Nothing in the chapter ties this alliance to <Emperor>, <Darkness>, Light, or Law.[1]

### History
Once the base is in use, attacks on that route begin. Several firms stop using it. The cost of supplying the neighboring systems goes up.[1]
The Imperial navy answers. A fleet blocks the main approaches to the complex. Smaller units go in through the asteroid field. The pirates run in groups and find the escape routes already watched. The base is taken. The confiscated ships are inspected so the buyers and the accomplices can be identified. With the main installation gone, the alliance loses control of the region.[1]
[[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], reading the report, calls them a pack of vermin that scents an opportunity everywhere.[1]

### Relationship with the Elderblood Empire
This is the one incident in the chapter answered by the navy as a fleet action: a blockade, an advance through the rocks, and a captured base. It is still part of the same trap. The losses the Empire seems to take across these operations are clones, already returned to the Emperor's galaxy to recover. The politics of the conquered worlds, and the cargo, are things the Empire is willing to spend.[1]
The closing order is wider than the pirates. [[Scathach]] intends to make an example of those who attack under the belief that a greater faction will protect them. Kaguya commits the Third Legion, [[The Black Hand of the Emperor]], to work with the First, [[The Spartans]]. The pirate alliance has already lost the region before that order is given. The chapter does not show a second strike against whoever escaped.[1]

### Current Status
The mining complex is no longer theirs. The alliance no longer controls the region. Buyers and accomplices are being identified from the captured ships.[1]
The chapter does not name the alliance, the complex, the sector, the route, or any captain. It does not say the fleeing groups were destroyed.[1]

### Trivia
- This is the only small faction in Chapter 1203 left without a proper name. The [[Varkhess League]], the [[Nharok Consortium]], and the [[Order of Sythra]] are named.[1]
- [[Scathach]]'s "pack of vermin" line is spoken over the report that includes this alliance. The same report is where she treats all of the apparent losses as one Imperial trap.[1]
- No earlier chapter on this wiki describes this alliance. This article uses only Chapter 1203.
