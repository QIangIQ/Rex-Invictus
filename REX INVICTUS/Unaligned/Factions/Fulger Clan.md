The Fulger Clan, also known as Clan Fulger, is one of the oldest Noble Vampire families of [[Nightingale]], one of the original four Vampire Count houses recognized beneath [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]], and one of the major dynastic branches incorporated into the modern Elderblood Clan. Founded by [[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]], the Clan historically ruled Nightingale's eastern territory and was responsible for domestic affairs, manufacturing, food production and the distribution of resources required by Vampire society, including Blood-based products such as Blood Crystals.[1]
What distinguishes Fulger from most Noble Vampire houses is the origin of its Bloodline. Carmila was not born a Vampire. She was originally a high-level Lightning Spirit who became a Vampire through an agreement involving Vlad. The elemental nature that once constituted Carmila's existence was subsequently encoded into her Vampiric descendants as a hereditary Lightning Bloodline.[2]
That conversion created both Fulger's greatest strength and its oldest weakness. A genuine Lightning Spirit could exist as elemental Lightning without being restricted by ordinary flesh, whereas Carmila's descendants possessed increasingly powerful Lightning inside physical Vampire bodies. Excessive output could consequently damage the user from within.[2] Much of the family's martial history revolves around overcoming that contradiction.
The main Fulger women eventually transcended the limitation almost completely. Their integration into [[Victor Elderblood]]'s Bloodline and subsequent evolution into [[Blood Dragons]] allowed Carmila's descendants to combine their ancient Lightning Spirit Essence with True Dragon physiology, producing a modern family dramatically closer to its elemental origin than the Vampiric generations that came before it.[3][4]

### Overview
Fulger is the surviving Bloodline of an elemental Being forced into flesh. Its strongest traditions, marriages and martial arts were all shaped by a single ancestral problem: how could descendants of Lightning recover the freedom their founder possessed before becoming a Vampire?
Clan Fulger historically possessed hundreds of members, making it structurally very different from the tiny [[Scarlett Clan]]. Despite this population, only a small proportion were direct biological descendants of Carmila. Most were unrelated Noble Vampires incorporated through the Clan's ancient Initiation Ritual.[5]
The difference between these groups was important. Initiated Vampires could inherit Fulger characteristics and potentially awaken Lightning, but the main Bloodline contained a trace of Carmila's original Lightning Spirit nature within the Soul itself. This gave direct descendants a level of intrinsic potential unavailable to ordinary initiated members.[5]
The Clan's history is consequently not merely a political chronology. Its development includes three successive biological stages:
LIGHTNING SPIRIT → NOBLE VAMPIRE → TRUE DRAGON
Carmila began as an elemental existence, converted that nature into a hereditary Vampire Bloodline, and later saw her descendants recover an even more complete elemental state through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Draconic lineage.

### Creation

#### Carmila, the First Fulger
Clan Fulger began with [[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]], a high-order Lightning Spirit whose existence was intrinsically connected to Lightning.[2]
Carmila eventually entered an agreement with [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] and became a Vampire. The exact significance of this event extends beyond an ordinary racial transformation: her original Lightning Spirit nature was forced into the biological and spiritual framework of a Noble Vampire, creating the ancestral Bloodline from which Fulger later developed.[2]
Carmila subsequently became one of [[Nightingale]]'s original Vampire Countesses. Like the other first-generation Counts, her position was rooted heavily in her personal strength rather than merely inheriting an already established title.
Origin of the Name
The Clan's ancestral family record explains that "Fulger" means "Lightning", directly reflecting Carmila's original nature and the power inherited by her descendants.[6]

#### The First Paradox
Carmila gained the benefits of Vampire existence, but the conversion also weakened one aspect of her original nature.
A Lightning Spirit did not need to force immense electrical Energy through flesh, bones and organs. A Vampire did.
From that point onward, Fulger inherited a fundamental contradiction: increasingly powerful elemental output paired with a body that could be damaged by the very power it was born to wield.[2]
Later Fulgers spent generations solving this problem through:
- selective reproduction;
- martial training;
- improved bodily reinforcement;
- Bloodline evolution;
- complete or partial transformation toward Lightning;
- and ultimately Draconic evolution.

### Territory
⚡ Eastern [[Nightingale]]
A productive, comparatively mild territory associated with internal infrastructure, food production and the material needs of Vampire civilization.
Clan Fulger historically governed the eastern territory of Nightingale. The region is comparatively temperate and has often been described as possessing an atmosphere reminiscent of perpetual autumn.
Unlike the more militarized identity of Clan Scarlett, Fulger's political importance lay heavily in sustaining everyday Vampire civilization. Its lands supported production, processing and distribution systems required by Nightingale's population.[1]
Among the Clan's most important products were Blood-based resources such as Blood Crystals, allowing the territory to play a central role in supplying a species biologically dependent upon Blood.[1]
After Natashia lost the Count title, control of much of the political authority associated with the eastern territory passed into the hands of [[Horseman Clan]]. Fulger later recovered its Count position after Natashia challenged [[Niklaus Horseman]].[7][8]
The territory continues to exist in the modern Imperial era even though the political meaning of the old Count system has largely disappeared. Thousands of years later, Nightingale still contains recognizable Snow, Fulger, Adrasteia and Scarlett territories, now functioning within the immensely larger [[Elderblood Empire]].[9]

### Historical Responsibilities
⚡ THE DOMESTIC PILLAR
Mission: maintain the internal resources and material infrastructure necessary for [[Nightingale]]'s Vampire population.[1]
Clan Fulger historically handled:
- Domestic Affairs: internal matters related to the functioning of Vampire society.[1]
- Food Production: large-scale provision of resources suitable for Vampiric consumption.
- Blood Resources: production and distribution of Blood-based products such as Blood Crystals.
- Manufacturing: material production required throughout Nightingale.
- Internal Infrastructure: responsibilities necessary for the kingdom to function away from its military and foreign-policy apparatus.
The assignment was less spectacular than Clan Scarlett's role as Nightingale's offensive spear, but it was essential. Armies, nobles and ordinary citizens could not function without a stable internal economy and food supply.
The old responsibility was eventually superseded by the enormous industrial systems of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Fulger's senior members consequently shifted from regional Count administration toward Imperial government, military command and independent assignments.[10]

### Government and Clan Structure
Clan Fulger historically possessed two distinct layers:
MAIN BLOODLINE
Direct descendants of Carmila carrying residual Lightning Spirit Essence within their Souls. They possess the highest natural potential of the Clan.[5]
INITIATED MEMBERS
Unrelated Noble Vampires accepted into Fulger through an ancient Blood-and-Essence ritual. They can acquire Clan characteristics and potentially awaken Lightning.[5]

#### Leadership
Carmila was the founder and First Countess.
Her daughter Natashia eventually inherited leadership and became the Clan's principal modern Countess. [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]], Natashia's daughter, became the recognized heiress.
Victoria, Carmila's other biological daughter, remained part of the main Bloodline despite failing to manifest Lightning during her earlier Vampire life. Her son [[Tatsuya Rider]] subsequently inherited the potential that had remained dormant in Victoria, demonstrating that an inactive expression in one generation did not erase Carmila's lineage.

#### Population
By the Demonic War era, Fulger possessed approximately five hundred members.[5]
Such a population could not have been produced entirely through direct reproduction. Noble Vampires possess extremely low fertility, and the vast majority of those members were not Carmila's descendants.
The Clan Initiation Ritual explains how Fulger could function as a large noble institution while maintaining an extremely small biological core.

### Main Bloodline
The main Fulger lineage begins with Carmila.
Natashia explained that her direct descendants retained approximately a one-percent trace of Lightning Spirit nature within their Souls.[5] The numerical description should not be treated merely as ordinary genetics; the important distinction is that direct descendants inherited something spiritual from Carmila's pre-Vampire existence.
This trace gave the main family:
- greater affinity with Fulger Lightning;
- superior ultimate potential;
- deeper compatibility with the Clan's Secret [[Martial Arts]];
- stronger capacity to approach a genuine Lightning Spirit state;
- and recognition by Bloodline-sensitive artifacts such as [[Taranis]].
An initiated member could become extremely powerful through personal talent, but the ritual itself could not grant an intrinsic Bloodline exceeding the main line's inherited potential.[5]

### Clan Initiation Ritual
The Fulger Clan was not five hundred biological relatives. Most of its members were Noble Vampires deliberately incorporated into the house.
The Clan Initiation Ritual is an old ritual known among the most ancient Noble Vampire families.[5]
Before initiation, the Clan Leader personally evaluates a prospective member. Acceptance is therefore not automatic.
Once accepted, the leader provides a portion of their Blood and Essence, altering the recipient's existing Noble Vampire Bloodline.[5]
Unlike the transformation of a dead Human or supernatural creature into a Vampiric servant, the Clan ritual does not erase individuality. It instead amplifies an existing psychological inclination to serve and remain loyal to the Clan Leader's main Bloodline.[5]
It is not conventional mindless enslavement. Fulger sought competent and willing Clan members, not empty puppets incapable of independent action.[5]
For a Noble Vampire accepted into Clan Fulger, initiation could produce:
- Blonde Hair: one of the recognizable physical characteristics propagated through the Clan.
- Fulger Essence Compatibility: the recipient becomes spiritually and biologically more compatible with the house.
- Potential [[Electrokinesis]]: initiated members can gain the possibility of awakening Lightning.[5]
- Strengthened Loyalty: service toward the central family becomes psychologically reinforced.
- Bloodline Limitation: the artificial inheritance does not automatically reproduce the full ancestral potential of Carmila's descendants.[5]

### Bloodline

#### Lightning Spirit Ancestry
THE BLOODLINE IS A CONVERTED SPIRIT ESSENCE
Fulger Lightning did not originate as an ordinary Vampire elemental mutation. It descends directly from Carmila's existence as a Lightning Spirit.[2]
When Carmila became a Vampire, the Lightning that had once been her natural elemental existence became a reproducible hereditary characteristic.
This explains several unusual aspects of the Bloodline:
- Lightning is extraordinarily integrated with the user's body and Soul.
- The strongest techniques revolve around becoming Lightning rather than simply producing it.
- Direct descendants possess residual Spirit qualities even when they do not actively manifest [[Electrokinesis]].
- The Clan's greatest historical weakness originated from forcing Spirit-level output through flesh.
- Draconic evolution disproportionately benefited Fulger because True Dragon physiology removed many of those physical limitations.

#### Electrokinesis
Fulger members capable of awakening the family's active Bloodline can generate, manipulate and shape electricity and Lightning.
Common applications include:
- lightning projection;
- electrical reinforcement of the body;
- explosive movement;
- enhanced reflexes;
- lightning weapons;
- ranged attacks;
- concentrated discharge;
- battlefield-wide lightning;
- and speed-oriented combat.
Fulger Lightning is particularly suited for mobility. Members frequently cover their bodies in electrical Energy to dramatically accelerate movement and reaction speed.
At high mastery, the distinction between the user controlling Lightning and the user existing as Lightning begins to disappear.

#### The Flesh Limitation
For generations, the most important weakness of Fulger was the body itself.
A genuine Spirit is fundamentally more compatible with elemental transformation than a physical Noble Vampire. Descendants such as Natashia possessed enough Lightning to damage their own flesh when output exceeded what the body could tolerate.[2]
This weakness influenced the family's reproduction strategy.
Natashia deliberately selected [[William Salvatore Florence]] because his Bloodline possessed physical characteristics useful for producing a child capable of withstanding Fulger Lightning.[2]
The result was [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]].
Sasha inherited:
- Carmila's Lightning Spirit-descended Bloodline;
- a stronger physical foundation from William;
- and a paternal perceptual ability especially useful for combat at extreme speed.[11]
The result was a Fulger descendant who could use levels of Lightning that would have produced severe backlash in earlier generations.

#### Enhanced Perception
William's lineage possessed a sensory characteristic allowing its wielder to perceive events in drastically greater detail, producing an effect similar to observing rapidly moving phenomena in slow motion.[11]
Natashia specifically considered this desirable because extreme speed becomes dangerous if the user's mind and senses cannot process the world quickly enough.
This power became incorporated into Sasha's personal Bloodline and was later amplified through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
It should not be treated as one of Carmila's original Fulger abilities. It entered the family through William.

### Secret Martial Arts of Clan Fulger
“I am the Lightning... and the Lightning is me.”
— Core principle of the Fulger Secret [[Martial Arts]][2]
The Secret Martial Arts of Clan Fulger were personally created by [[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]].[2]
Their objective is not merely to improve [[Electrokinesis]]. The art exists to reverse the fundamental limitation introduced when Carmila became a Vampire.
The practitioner progressively returns toward the family's origin as a Lightning Spirit.
Training emphasizes:
- synchronization between body and Lightning;
- conversion of the physical body toward an elemental state;
- extreme-speed perception and movement;
- Lightning-enhanced weapon combat;
- explosive acceleration;
- control sufficient to prevent internal damage;
- and preservation of consciousness while the body increasingly behaves like elemental Energy.
The techniques were difficult enough that full instruction was traditionally associated with the Clan's heir and its most talented members.[2]

#### Lightning Spirit State
When the foundational state is activated, Lightning completely envelops the practitioner until the distinction between physical body and electrical phenomenon begins to blur.[2]
Natashia explained that using the Technique in an ordinary state trained the user for the much more difficult application within a complete Vampire Count transformation.
The ultimate objective was simple in principle and extremely difficult in practice:
Become what Carmila originally was.

#### First Form: Lynet
During the Demonic War, Sasha demonstrated one of the Clan's Secret [[Martial Arts]] after remembering Natashia's instruction.
First Form — Lynet[2]
Sasha declared herself one with Lightning, entered the Fulger elemental state and moved throughout the territory as a trail of golden electrical Energy. Lightning simultaneously descended across the battlefield while she cut through Demonic forces personally.[2]
Within roughly ten seconds, large numbers of Demons distributed across Fulger territory had been exterminated.[2]
The display reminded observers why the Clan had retained its place among [[Nightingale]]'s ruling houses for generations despite periods of political decline.

### Vampire Count Transformation
As one of [[Nightingale]]'s ancient Count Bloodlines, Fulger possessed a specialized Vampire Count state.
The transformation dramatically amplified:
- Lightning output;
- movement speed;
- reaction speed;
- physical ability;
- and compatibility with the Secret [[Martial Arts]].
In the perfected use envisioned by Carmila's martial tradition, the Count Form could be combined with Lightning Spirit transformation, allowing the wielder to approach the family's ancestral elemental state much more closely.[2]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] inherited a Fulger-derived Count transformation through Sasha. His version concentrated immense Lightning around the body—particularly the limbs—and emphasized extreme acceleration over the brute-force characteristics of some of his other Count-derived forms.[12]
Victor's later modifications should not automatically be attributed to native Fulger members.

### Clan Treasures

#### Taranis
⚡ TARANIS
Paired ancestral daggers that recognize legitimate Fulger Blood rather than simple possession of Lightning.
[[Taranis]], the Daggers of the Ancestral Spirit are Clan Fulger's principal ancestral weapons.
They are a pair of golden daggers covered in supernatural inscriptions and historically associated with Carmila.[13]
The weapons were forged by Dwarves connected to the legendary tradition that produced artifacts such as Mjolnir.[14]
Their most important property for Clan identity is Bloodline recognition.
Taranis can distinguish a legitimate Fulger from someone who merely possesses an equivalent Lightning ability.[15]
Anyone rejected by the artifact can be violently struck by its Lightning.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] provided the most important example. Through Sasha, he could use Fulger Lightning, yet the daggers did not originally recognize him as a genuine biological member of Carmila's Bloodline.[15]
This demonstrates that:
Fulger Lightning ≠ Fulger Blood.
Taranis also assists the wielder in controlling the family's Lightning and is deeply connected to its secret martial traditions.
Natashia eventually entrusted the daggers to Sasha so the heiress could train properly with the ancestral weapons.[16]

#### Ancestral Family Record
Clan Fulger also possesses a supernatural ancestral family record created by an earlier member of the family.[6]
The artifact takes the form of a genealogical book, but it is considerably more than a written family tree.
Its supernatural function allows it to identify and display legitimate members of the main Fulger family.
When examined during the earlier story, the record showed:
Carmila Fulger
↳ Annasthashia Fulger → Sasha Fulger
↳ Victoria Fulger → [[Tatsuya Rider]]
The record was treated as an artifact of significance comparable to [[Taranis]].[6]
Its importance also demonstrated that Fulger family membership was not defined solely through biological genetics.
After Julia, Sasha's deceased Human Head Maid and maternal figure, was formally honored by Natashia, her name was added to the main family record despite Julia possessing no biological Fulger Blood.[6]
The decision represented one of the most meaningful changes in Natashia's attitude toward Clan tradition: the supernatural family archive itself recognized someone whose claim to the family came through love and motherhood rather than genetics.

### History

#### Carmila and the First Generation
FOUNDATION ERAA Spirit became a Vampire, and Lightning became a Bloodline.
Carmila founded Fulger after her transformation from Lightning Spirit into Vampire.[2]
She became one of Vlad's first Countesses and developed many of the traditions that later defined the Clan, including the Secret [[Martial Arts]] designed around returning a physical Vampire toward a Spirit state.
Her control over Lightning became the standard by which later descendants judged themselves. Natashia required approximately fifteen centuries of development to approach the precision she remembered from Carmila.
Carmila also became deeply associated with the Clan's ancestral daggers, [[Taranis]].

#### Natashia and Victoria
Carmila had two major daughters in the original direct line: Natashia and Victoria.
Natashia inherited enormous active Lightning potential and eventually succeeded Carmila as Countess.
Victoria remained a genuine daughter of Carmila but did not originally manifest Fulger Lightning herself.
That absence did not mean the Bloodline had vanished. Victoria later produced [[Tatsuya Rider]], in whom the ancestral potential reappeared strongly.
The contrast became important to understanding Fulger heredity: a direct descendant can carry Spirit-derived potential without personally expressing every active characteristic.

#### The Fracturing of Natashia
Natashia's inherited Lightning eventually became dangerous to her own existence.
During a severe crisis in her youth, the enormous power passing through her body and Soul threatened to destroy her.
Carmila attempted to save her daughter through direct spiritual intervention. Her effort succeeded in keeping Natashia alive but had consequences that remained hidden for centuries.[17]
A remnant of Carmila's Soul became attached within Natashia, while damage and fragmentation within Natashia's developing Soul eventually contributed to the emergence of another personality—the woman later known independently as [[Natasha Elderblood Fulger]].[17]
This meant that several phenomena originally interpreted as psychological irregularities were actually connected to an extraordinary spiritual accident involving:
- Natashia's damaged Soul;
- an emerging independent fragment/personality;
- and the remaining Soul of the Clan's supposedly dead founder.

#### Sasha's Birth
Natashia understood that Fulger's greatest biological weakness was the inability of Vampiric flesh to perfectly withstand its own ancestral Lightning.
She therefore approached reproduction strategically.
[[William Salvatore Florence]] possessed a Bloodline providing superior physical characteristics and highly useful enhanced perception. Natashia selected him partly because those characteristics could complement Fulger's weaknesses.[2][11]
Their daughter, [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]], inherited both sides.
Sasha possessed:
- Fulger Lightning;
- the main family's Spirit potential;
- a body considerably better suited to surviving its output;
- and enhanced perception capable of keeping pace with extraordinary speed.[11]
Unfortunately, Sasha's childhood became dominated by the harsher personality associated with Natashia's fractured state. Her extraordinary potential resulted in severe training and experimentation rather than a healthy upbringing.
The person Sasha came to regard most strongly as a mother was instead the Human Head Maid Julia.[18]

#### Loss of the Count Title
Shortly before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became a Vampire, [[Niklaus Horseman]] manipulated circumstances that resulted in Natashia wagering the Fulger Count title.
She lost.
The defeat temporarily removed Fulger from its historical position among [[Nightingale]]'s active ruling houses and transferred the Count status to [[Horseman Clan]].
The political weakness had immediate consequences.
Enemies understood that Fulger no longer possessed the same protection attached to one of Vlad's ruling Pillars. The Inquisition consequently targeted Sasha during a major attack.[18]
The attack devastated the mansion's household and killed Julia while Sasha was being pursued.
Julia's death remained one of the defining traumas of Sasha's life.

#### Victor and Sasha
Sasha became one of the three Noble Vampires involved in [[Victor Elderblood]]'s transformation, permanently linking Victor's earliest supernatural existence to Fulger.
Through the ritual and Blood bond, Victor inherited Fulger Lightning while Sasha became one of his original wives.
Victor eventually encountered Natashia and William directly and learned the extent of Sasha's childhood experiences.
His confrontation with her parents escalated into violence.[19][20]
William ultimately died, while the internal disruption surrounding Natashia allowed the warmer original personality that had long been suppressed beneath the harsher state to re-emerge.
Natashia subsequently attempted to rebuild her relationship with Sasha rather than simply demand maternal authority she had failed to earn.

#### Restoration of Clan Fulger
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually challenged Natashia's acceptance of the Clan's political decline and encouraged her to reclaim what had been lost.
Natashia challenged [[Niklaus Horseman]] and placed Fulger's inheritance into the resulting wager.[7]
The battle forced Natashia to confront both Niklaus and the weaknesses that had accumulated throughout her own life. Niklaus relied upon experience, preparation and poison, while Natashia increasingly returned to the identity of a Fulger Countess rather than the woman who had accepted defeat.[21][22]
Niklaus eventually forfeited.
Natashia reclaimed the Count title and restored Fulger to its traditional political place.[8]
Clan Fulger fell once, but it did not disappear. Its restoration became one of the earliest signs that the old balance of [[Nightingale]] was beginning to shift around Victor.

#### The Internal Betrayal
Fulger's restoration did not mean its internal problems were over.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later discovered a hidden faction containing traitors embedded within both Fulger and [[Snow Clan]]. The conspiracy had operated for years and intended to exploit moments of weakness to replace existing leadership.[23][24]
The conspirators' plans included treating younger women such as Sasha and Violet as reproductive resources through which powerful new Noble Vampire generations could be produced.
Victor infiltrated their gathering and eliminated much of the organization before traveling directly into Fulger territory.
Additional traitors were exposed inside the household, including Maid Joan, and Natashia began a full internal purge.[24]
The cleansing continued alongside the corresponding purge inside Clan Snow.[25][26]

#### The Demonic War
CHAPTER 523Sasha returned the [[Fulger Bloodline]] toward its original form.
Before the Demonic War, Natashia formally taught Sasha the higher-level Secret [[Martial Arts]] created by Carmila.
When Demons invaded Fulger territory, Sasha assumed responsibility for organizing its defenders despite acknowledging that leadership did not come naturally to her.[2]
Her charisma nevertheless allowed her to organize Adult and Elder Vampires throughout the region and direct inexperienced members toward safety.
Sasha then invoked the principles Natashia had taught her.
She declared herself Lightning, entered the Clan's ancestral elemental state and used First Form: Lynet to cross the territory as golden electrical Energy while Lightning rained around her.[2]
The attack slaughtered large numbers of Demonic invaders distributed throughout the region within seconds.
Even Tatsuya—himself an exceptional direct descendant—was surprised by the display.
The battle proved that Fulger's greatest inheritance was not merely the ability to generate electricity. Its highest martial tradition was the art of returning its wielder toward the state of existence its founder had possessed before the Clan technically existed.

#### Natashia and Victor
Natashia's relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually developed beyond their original connection as mother-in-law and son-in-law.
The pair later performed a formal Vampire marriage ritual in the Fulger mansion located in [[WarFall]], exchanging Blood and vows and establishing a supernatural marital bond.[27]
This relationship further pulled Fulger away from dependence upon Vlad's political system and toward the family developing around Victor.

#### Julia's Recognition
Natashia eventually confronted one of the greatest failures of her relationship with Sasha: another woman had been Sasha's true mother when Natashia herself was unable to fill that role.
Rather than erase or minimize Julia's importance, Natashia formally recognized it.
She added Julia to Fulger's supernatural ancestral family record as part of the main family.[6]
Julia had no Fulger Blood, no Spirit ancestry and was already dead.
The significance was therefore entirely familial.
The Clan artifact itself now recorded a Human maid as family because the people who mattered accepted that she had earned the position through the life she lived.

#### The Truth of the Main Bloodline
During the Demonic War era, Natashia explained the Clan Initiation Ritual and Fulger's true genealogy to Tatsuya.[5]
The revelation established that:
- Fulger possessed around five hundred members;
- the vast majority were not related to Carmila;
- initiated members could awaken Lightning;
- the main Bloodline retained Spirit traces inside the Soul;
- and merely possessing Lightning did not make someone equivalent to Carmila's descendants.[5]
This distinction explains both the size of the historical Clan and the much smaller genealogy represented by its ancestral record.

#### Victor's Progenitor Blood
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later strengthened Sasha and Natashia through his own Progenitor lineage.
The process did not simply replace their previous Bloodlines.
Instead, his Blood amplified what was already present.
Natashia's Lightning became stronger, while Sasha's Fulger traits, physical durability and paternal sensory inheritance were reinforced together.[11]
The event foreshadowed the method Victor would later use during Dragonization: rather than homogenizing everyone around himself, he increasingly evolved existing characteristics into stronger individualized forms.

#### Carmila and Natasha Return
THREE GENERATIONS REUNITED
The founder thought dead for centuries, the daughter carrying her Soul, and the granddaughter inheriting her Bloodline eventually stood together as living individuals.
Shortly before the later Meeting of Supernatural Beings, Natashia suddenly entered severe spiritual distress.[17]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] investigated the deepest layers of her Soul and discovered two additional presences:
- [[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]] — the remaining Soul of the Clan founder.
- [[Natasha Elderblood Fulger]] — an existence produced from Natashia's damaged and fragmented spiritual structure.[17]
Carmila explained that her presence originated from the desperate attempt to save Natashia when Fulger Lightning threatened her daughter's life.
Victor repaired Natashia's damaged spiritual foundation.
Once Natashia no longer depended upon the foreign spiritual structures to remain stable, Carmila and Natasha could be separated rather than destroyed.[28][29]
Both received independent existence.
For the first time:
- Carmila existed independently again;
- Natashia possessed a repaired Soul;
- Natasha possessed her own existence rather than remaining an internal personality;
- Victoria could reunite with Carmila;
- and Sasha could physically interact with multiple generations of her maternal family simultaneously.
The event radically altered the Clan's genealogy. Fulger no longer possessed merely the memory of its founder. Carmila had returned.

#### The Blood Dragon Era
The Fulger women subsequently entered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Draconic Bloodline.
Dragonization did not erase Carmila's Spirit ancestry.
It enhanced it.
By Chapters 918–920, Fulger's main women could approach a much purer Lightning-Spirit state while simultaneously possessing the physical and spiritual foundation of [[True Dragons]].[3][4]

This evolution solved much of the contradiction that had afflicted Fulger for generations.
The old family possessed immense Lightning but bodies capable of being destroyed by it.
The modern family possesses Dragon physiology designed to survive and adapt to forms of Energy vastly beyond ordinary Vampire flesh.
The transformation therefore brought Fulger closer to Carmila's original nature than almost any development since she accepted Vlad's offer.

### Elderblood Imperial Era
Clan Fulger survived the disappearance of [[Nightingale]]'s old political order.
Its role changed.
The central Fulger women—Carmila, Natashia, Natasha, Victoria and Sasha—became simultaneously:
- members of Fulger;
- wives and family of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]];
- members of Elderblood Clan;
- [[True Dragons]];
- senior figures of the [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]];
- and Imperial authorities within the [[Elderblood Empire]].
By Chapter 1143, the five women were operating together on the opposite side of the Imperial galaxy while discussing Victor's increasingly decentralized method of government.[10]
Carmila asked her daughters and granddaughter to evaluate the policy. Naty and Natasha supported it, while Victoria observed that although delegation might appear less efficient than Victor personally controlling every detail, an Empire containing so many extraordinary Beings did not require its Emperor to micromanage everything.[10]
Sasha was considerably less philosophical and complained that Victor had sent her away from his side.
The scene demonstrated an important difference between early Fulger and the Imperial family.
Historically, the Clan depended heavily upon one Countess and a rigid hereditary institution.
By the Imperial era, its strongest members were trusted to operate independently across Galactic distances while remaining part of one interconnected family.
Sasha later became one of the principal figures responsible for the Empire's policing and internal security, preserving Fulger's long association with internal administration on a vastly greater scale.
Later Imperial-era observations confirm that the Fulger territory on Nightingale remains prosperous and continues to be led by members of Victor's family even after the old Vampire Count hierarchy became politically obsolete.[9]

### The Modern Fulger Dynasty
THE BLOODLINE EXPANDED AGAIN
The accelerated Elderblood era produced a new generation through the women of Clan Fulger, turning Carmila's once-small biological line into one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s major dynastic branches.
Several Fulger-descended daughters appeared during the centuries following Victor's rise as God Emperor:
- [[Silvia Elderblood]] — daughter of [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] and Victor.[30]
- [[Angelina Elderblood]] — daughter of the independent [[Natasha Elderblood Fulger]] and Victor.
- Aline Elderblood Fulger — daughter of [[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]] and Victor.
- [[Julia Elderblood]] — daughter of Naty/Natashia and Victor.
- [[Svetlana Veronika Elderblood]] — daughter of [[Victoria Fulger Elderblood]] and Victor.[31]
- [[Alisa Sasha Elderblood]] — daughter of Carmila Fulger Elderblood and Victor.[31]
The later Fulger daughters prominently retain the visual identity of the family. Chapter 1165 describes the blonde Imperial Princesses appearing together with the violet Dragon Eyes inherited from Victor. Some, particularly Alisa and Svetlana Veronika, resemble Victor more strongly, while daughters such as Julia and Silvia strongly resemble their Fulger mothers.[31]
Genealogical clarification: Natasha and Natashia/Naty originated from the same damaged spiritual existence but became physically and spiritually independent after Victor repaired Natashia's Soul.[17][28] Their daughters are therefore separate branches of the modern Fulger line: Angelina Elderblood belongs to Natasha, while Julia Elderblood belongs to Naty/Natashia.

### Fulger Characteristics

#### Blonde Hair
Blonde hair became strongly associated with Fulger.
The trait can appear in both biological descendants and Vampires accepted through the Clan Initiation Ritual.[5]
Its presence is therefore a visual Clan characteristic rather than definitive proof of direct descent from Carmila.
The later Elderblood princesses born through the Fulger branch likewise commonly possess blonde hair combined with the violet Dragon Eyes characteristic of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s descendants.[31]

#### Lightning Identity
The family remained culturally associated with Lightning even after becoming Dragons.
In the late Imperial household, Natasha explicitly described the Fulger family's supernatural nature as electric, contrasting it with the warmer nature of the Snow branch and colder character of the Scarlett branch.[32]
The statement reflects how completely ancestral Lightning remained part of the family's identity despite the enormous number of additional Draconic, Divine and Imperial abilities accumulated over time.

### Affiliations

#### Nightingale
Fulger began as one of [[Nightingale]]'s four original Count houses and historically governed the eastern territory beneath Vlad's kingdom.[1]
Its formal Count role has become largely historical, but the territory and Fulger identity remain recognizable within modern Nightingale.

#### Snow Clan and Scarlett Clan
Fulger possesses particularly close historical ties to the [[Snow Clan]] and [[Scarlett Clan]].
Sasha, Violet and Ruby were childhood friends before becoming [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s first three wives.
Their marriages gradually transformed the relationship between the three houses from ordinary alliance into a network of direct family relationships.
When the old political system began collapsing, Snow, Fulger and Scarlett formally allied themselves with Clan Alucard.[33]
These houses should nevertheless remain distinct genealogical branches. Political integration did not erase the Snow, Fulger or Scarlett identities.

#### Elderblood Clan
Fulger is among the houses most thoroughly incorporated into the Elderblood Clan.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually formed relationships with multiple generations of Fulger women, while their daughters became Imperial Princesses.
This creates an unusual dynasty in which the founder, her descendants and the next generation are all integrated into one much larger supernatural family.

#### Dragons Nest Pantheon
The principal Fulger women joined the [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]] during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s rise.
Their later transformation into [[True Dragons]] changed their Race without erasing the historical Clan to which they belonged.

#### Elderblood Empire
By the Imperial era, Fulger no longer exists principally as a semi-independent regional power beneath Vlad.
It instead functions as a dynastic house within the Elderblood ruling family.
Its territory remains wealthy, its senior women receive Imperial responsibilities and its descendants form a growing branch of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s second generation.[10][31][9]

### Legacy
Clan Fulger's history is fundamentally a story of adaptation.
Carmila sacrificed the freedom of a Spirit body to become a Vampire and founded one of [[Nightingale]]'s ruling houses. Her descendants inherited extraordinary Lightning together with the physical weakness created by that decision.
Natashia attempted to correct the problem through selective reproduction.
Carmila's martial arts attempted to correct it through technique.
Sasha became the most successful early example of combining those solutions.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood strengthened the existing lineage rather than replacing it.
Finally, True Dragon evolution resolved the oldest contradiction almost entirely by giving the family bodies and Souls capable of supporting an elemental existence far beyond the limitations of ordinary Vampiric flesh.
Fulger began with a Lightning Spirit who became flesh. Its modern descendants possess the ability to become Lightning again—without surrendering the advantages gained during every generation between.
