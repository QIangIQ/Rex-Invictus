**[[Scarlett Bloodline]]**: The Scarlett Bloodline is the hereditary and ritually transmissible Noble Vampire Bloodline associated with the [[Scarlett Clan]]. (Lore adapted from *My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** This material is adapted into the Rex Invictus world at the lowest priority: Rex Invictus wins any conflict, then the Flamel material, then MTWABV. Events of the MTWABV timeline (Victor's conquests, the fall of the pantheons, the [[Elderblood Empire]]) are distant background or rumor unless a chapter puts them on the page. ## MTWABV Canon

The Scarlett Bloodline is the hereditary and ritually transmissible Noble Vampire Bloodline associated with the [[Scarlett Clan]]. Its primary supernatural characteristic is an affinity for Ice, allowing compatible members to develop Cryokinesis of increasingly greater scale and sophistication. The Bloodline originates from the main Scarlett lineage headed by [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] and was naturally inherited by her biological daughter [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]], while unrelated Noble Vampires could acquire a lesser expression of the lineage through the ancient Clan Initiation Ritual.[1]
The Scarlett Bloodline should not be confused with every supernatural ability displayed by members of Clan Scarlett. Siena, Lacus and Pepper were adopted into the Clan rather than being [[Scathach]]'s biological daughters, and each retained characteristics originating from their own original Bloodlines. The Clan ritual specifically gives an initiate the potential to acquire Ice, while abilities such as Pepper's Water manipulation and Lacus' [[The Mist|Mist]]/Smoke abilities belong to their individual development rather than being universal Scarlett powers.[2]

### Origin
The precise ancient origin of the Scarlett Bloodline before [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] has not been fully explained. In the modern story, [[Scathach]] represents the main lineage and highest known natural expression of the Bloodline.
Her biological daughter [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] directly inherited the lineage and consequently possesses an exceptionally strong natural affinity for Ice.
The difference between a member of the main Bloodline and someone initiated into the Clan became explicitly explained when Natashia Fulger described the Clan Initiation Ritual. A Noble Vampire accepted by Scathach could receive part of her Blood and Essence, causing the initiate to acquire Scarlett characteristics such as red hair and the potential to awaken Ice manipulation.[3]
The ritual does not make the recipient genetically identical to Scathach or Ruby, nor does it automatically erase the recipient's original Bloodline. Instead, the Scarlett traits are added to the Vampire's existing potential.

### Inheritance
There are two confirmed methods through which the Scarlett Bloodline can be obtained.

#### Natural Inheritance
Direct descendants of the main Scarlett lineage can inherit the Bloodline biologically.
[[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] is the clearest example, being [[Scathach]]'s biological daughter and inheriting her mother's powerful affinity for Ice.
Ruby's Blood was also one of the three Noble Vampire Bloodlines incorporated into [[Victor Elderblood]] during the abnormal ritual that transformed him into a Vampire. Victor subsequently demonstrated the ability to generate and manipulate Ice despite never having belonged naturally to Clan Scarlett.[4]
This demonstrates that the supernatural information of the Scarlett Bloodline is contained within the Blood and can be transferred under sufficiently abnormal circumstances.

#### Clan Initiation
Ancient Noble Vampire houses possess a ritual through which an unrelated Noble Vampire can become a genuine Clan member.
For Clan Scarlett, [[Scathach]]'s Blood and Essence can cause the recipient to develop:
- characteristic red hair;
- compatibility with the Scarlett lineage;
- and the potential to awaken Ice manipulation.[5]
Siena, Lacus and Pepper are examples of Vampires incorporated into Scathach's family rather than naturally born from her.
The acquired Bloodline does not automatically reach the same potential as the main lineage. Natashia explicitly explained that the ultimate strength of an initiated Vampire depends upon the individual and that an acquired Clan Bloodline will normally not possess greater natural potential than that of the Clan's main family.[6]
Siena is considered an exceptional case because of the extraordinary strength she developed with Ice despite being an adopted daughter.[7]

### Core Powers and Abilities
- Ice Manipulation (Cryokinesis): The fundamental ability of the Scarlett Bloodline. Users can create, shape, move and control Ice through supernatural Energy. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] inherited this ability through [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] during the abnormal Vampire transformation ritual and demonstrated control over it almost immediately.[8]
- Ice Generation: Users can create Ice directly without requiring naturally occurring frozen material. Victor, Ruby and other Scarlett users have generated enormous quantities of Ice in environments where none previously existed.[9][10]
- Cold Generation: Scarlett Cryokinesis can rapidly lower the temperature of the surrounding environment, allowing users to freeze targets, moisture and terrain.
- Freezing: Users can freeze physical objects and living targets directly. At sufficiently high output this can immobilize entire groups rather than merely covering a target in surface Ice.[11][12]
- Environmental Freezing: High-level users can spread their Cryokinesis over large portions of the battlefield, transforming the surrounding environment into frozen terrain. Victor's attacks have converted forests and large combat zones into snowy or frozen landscapes.[13][14]
- Area Freezing: Scarlett power can be released indiscriminately throughout a surrounding area instead of targeting individuals one at a time. Victor demonstrated this against the Monsters surrounding [[WarFall]].[15]
- Ice Construction: Users can create complex solid objects from Ice rather than merely forming crude masses. Known applications include weapons, walls, platforms, thrones, giant hands, spikes and battlefield structures.[16][17]
- Ice Weaponry: Ice can be shaped into swords, spears, blades, spikes and enormous numbers of other weapons. Victor's Ice World application caused weapons to emerge throughout a completely frozen battlefield.[18]
- Ice Armor: Ice can be formed defensively over the user's body. Victor's Scarlett Count Form manifested pale Ice/scales across his body like armor.[19]
- Ice Claw Manifestation: Victor's Scarlett Count Form produced Ice claws over his hands and feet, allowing the Bloodline to be used as a close-combat extension of his transformed body.[20]
- Ice Barrier Generation: Users can construct walls, domes and other defensive obstructions from Ice.
- Ice Infusion: Ice can be layered around weapons or limbs to reinforce attacks. Victor has coated his hands, fingers and weapons in Scarlett Ice while fighting.[21]
- Ice Imprisonment: Users can surround enemies or entire regions with enormous Ice structures in order to prevent escape. Victor's Cocoon technique created a frozen enclosure extending around and underneath the affected region, preventing conventional escape even underground.[22]
- Ice Restraint: Smaller applications can immobilize individual limbs or bodies by freezing them in place.
- Hail Generation: Victor demonstrated sufficiently precise Cryokinesis to convert enormous falling masses of dangerous Ice debris into harmless hail before they struck [[Nightingale]]'s capital.[23]
- Snow Generation: By freezing atmospheric moisture and manipulating environmental conditions, the Bloodline can create snow across affected terrain.
- Snow Manipulation: Existing frozen precipitation can be incorporated into Scarlett Cryokinesis. The Scarlett sisters exploited snow and frozen atmospheric conditions during Nightingale's Demonic invasion.[24]
- Terrain Manipulation (via Ice): Large-scale Cryokinesis can radically alter the shape and usability of a battlefield by covering structures, ground and obstacles in Ice.[25]

### Advanced Cryokinetic Applications
The following abilities represent unusually advanced uses of Scarlett Cryokinesis and should not be assumed to be automatically possessed by every carrier.
- Absolute Zero: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] developed an advanced Cryokinetic technique referred to as Absolute Zero, rapidly dropping the temperature across a wide region and instantly freezing the Monsters present there.[26] The name is the technique's in-story designation; it should not automatically be interpreted as proof that every Scarlett user can casually produce literal thermodynamic absolute zero.
- Mass Freezing: Victor's advanced Scarlett transformation allowed him to freeze enormous quantities of material essentially simultaneously rather than spreading Ice progressively from one target to another.[27]
- Battlefield Conversion: Through A World of Weapons — A World of Ice / Ice World, Victor converted the surrounding buildings, vegetation, Monsters and terrain into an enormous frozen environment before causing numerous Ice weapons to emerge throughout it.[28]
- Ice World Creation: A sufficiently powerful user can effectively convert the entire immediate battlefield into a Cryokinetic domain where Ice is present everywhere and can subsequently be used as material for attacks.[29]
- Ice Mirror Creation: Advanced Scarlett Cryokinesis has been used to form reflective Ice structures capable of functioning as part of a more complex combat setup.[30]
- Ice Clone Creation: Victor demonstrated the ability to form Ice replicas as part of his advanced Scarlett-style combat applications.[31]
- Cryokinetic Weapon Barrage: By combining large-scale Ice generation with construction, enormous numbers of frozen weapons can be produced throughout the battlefield rather than individually.
- Large-Scale Ice Imprisonment: Victor's Cocoon can seal a region hundreds of meters across, demonstrating that Scarlett restraint techniques can be expanded from individual targets to settlements and large enemy groups.[32]

### Scarlett Vampire Count Form
The Scarlett [[Vampire Count Form]] represents a higher evolutionary expression of the Bloodline. Count transformations amplify a Noble Vampire's racial abilities and Bloodline output while also improving the body's ability to endure and control that increased power.[33]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] manifested his Scarlett transformation during the [[WarFall]] expedition.[34]
Known Bloodline-related effects include:
- Cryokinetic Empowerment: Greatly increases the amount, scale and intensity of Ice the user can create.
- Enhanced Freezing: The transformation allows substantially greater areas and stronger opponents to be frozen.
- Ice Armor: Victor's body became covered in white scale-like frozen armor.[35]
- Ice Claws: Frozen claws manifested on his hands and feet.[36]
- Enhanced Ice Construction: Victor could create structures and weapons on a much greater scale while transformed.
- Environmental Conversion: The Count Form enabled the battlefield-scale Ice World application.[37]
- Bloodline Output Amplification: Like other Count transformations, it increases the quantity of elemental power the user can safely output.[38]
- Enhanced Physical Abilities: Strength, speed, durability and other Vampire characteristics increase alongside the elemental Bloodline. This is a general Count-form characteristic rather than a power unique specifically to Scarlett.[39]

### Bloodline-Derived Techniques
These are techniques created through Scarlett Cryokinesis rather than separate inherited powers.

#### Cocoon
Cold spreads throughout the surrounding region before forming an enormous enclosed structure of Ice. The technique can close the battlefield from above, around the sides and underground, preventing large numbers of opponents from escaping.[40]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later incorporated swordsmanship into the same concept, releasing Cryokinetic slashes whose paths formed the walls of the enclosure.

#### Absolute Zero
A wide-area freezing technique that rapidly drops environmental temperature and freezes essentially every hostile target inside its effective range.[41]

#### A World of Weapons — A World of Ice
An advanced Scarlett Count Form technique in which [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] converts the surrounding environment into Ice before creating huge numbers of frozen weapons throughout the transformed battlefield.[42]

### Bloodline Evolution and Later Carriers
The Scarlett Bloodline continued developing after its users reached levels far beyond ordinary Noble Vampires.
- Bloodline Enhancement: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s abnormal transformation and later Progenitor power substantially increased the quality and quantity of his inherited Scarlett Cryokinesis.
- Draconic Integration: After members of the Scarlett family became [[Blood Dragons]], their original abilities were not erased. Their existing powers were integrated and magnified through their new Dragon physiology.[43]
These later developments do not mean that every Draconic or Divine ability gained by Ruby, [[Scathach]] or Victor should retroactively be classified as part of the Scarlett Bloodline.

### Limitations
- Cryokinetic output depends upon the user's Energy, training and control.
- The ability to manipulate Ice does not automatically grant every advanced application demonstrated by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] or [[Scathach]].
- The [[Vampire Count Form]] requires sufficient development of the Bloodline and Vampire physiology.
- Techniques such as Absolute Zero, Cocoon and Ice World represent learned or developed applications rather than passive powers possessed automatically by every carrier.
- Later Blood Dragon, Divine and conceptual abilities are separate evolutionary systems unless explicitly derived from Scarlett Cryokinesis.

### Abilities Not Produced by the Scarlett Bloodline
The following abilities have appeared among [[Scarlett Clan]] members but should not be classified as fundamental Scarlett Bloodline powers:
- Water Manipulation (Hydrokinesis): Ruby possesses Water manipulation, but this is associated with her paternal inheritance rather than the principal Scarlett Ice Bloodline.
- Underwater Breathing: A derivative of Ruby/Victor's Water manipulation rather than Scarlett Cryokinesis.
- [[The Mist|Mist]] Manipulation: Primarily associated with [[Lacus Scarlett Elderblood]]'s individual/original Bloodline.
- Smoke Manipulation: Likewise associated with Lacus rather than the Scarlett Ice lineage.
- Pepper's Hydrokinesis: Pepper Scarlett Elderblood's Water affinity should not be used as evidence that every Scarlett possesses Water.
- [[Scathach]]'s [[Martial Arts]]: Scathach's martial mastery is learned skill, not a supernatural effect automatically produced by the Bloodline.
- Scathach's later Draconic abilities: Powers acquired after becoming a Dragon are not automatically Scarlett Bloodline abilities.

### Individual Expression
The Scarlett Bloodline does not manifest identically in every member.

#### Ruby Scarlett
As [[Scathach]]'s biological daughter, Ruby possesses one of the purest known natural expressions of the modern Scarlett Bloodline.
Ruby combines powerful Cryokinesis with exceptional control and analytical ability. Her fighting style often emphasizes controlling the battlefield rather than relying exclusively upon destructive output.

#### Siena Scarlett
Siena acquired the Scarlett Bloodline through [[Scathach]] rather than natural birth.
Despite this, her affinity with Ice became exceptionally strong. Natashia specifically described Siena as an anomaly among initiated members because of how powerful her acquired Ice became.[44]
Siena specializes heavily in large-scale ranged attacks, Ice constructs and battlefield control.[45]

#### Lacus Scarlett
Lacus received Scarlett Blood through Clan initiation and consequently possesses the potential for Ice, but generally developed abilities better suited to assassination, including [[The Mist|Mist]] and Smoke manipulation.
Natashia specifically noted that an initiated member may choose to train the Bloodline they possessed from birth because developing a lifelong ability is easier than mastering something newly acquired.[46]

#### Pepper Scarlett
Pepper likewise carries Scarlett lineage through [[Scathach]]'s initiation but specializes primarily in Water manipulation and overwhelming physical attacks rather than Ice.
Her Hydrokinesis should therefore remain listed as a personal/original ability unless a chapter explicitly identifies it as part of the Scarlett Bloodline.

### Vampire Count Evolution
As one of the ancient Count Bloodlines, Scarlett power can be pushed into the transformation associated with high-level Noble Vampires.
[[Victor Elderblood]], after inheriting Scarlett power through Ruby, eventually displayed an extreme Ice-oriented transformation during the [[WarFall]] expedition. His physiology changed considerably while his ability to generate and manipulate Ice increased to a level suitable for fighting the powerful Monsters beyond WarFall.[47]
Because Victor possesses several mixed Bloodlines and an abnormal physiology, the exact visual characteristics of his transformation should not automatically be assumed to represent the universal Scarlett Count Form.
The important point is that the Scarlett Bloodline possesses a sufficiently advanced evolutionary state to support Count-class elemental transformations.

### Interaction with Other Bloodlines
A Vampire initiated into Clan Scarlett does not necessarily lose their original abilities.
Chapter 577 explicitly establishes that Siena, Lacus and Pepper possessed or potentially possessed abilities separate from the Ice acquired through [[Scathach]].[48]
The Scarlett Bloodline can therefore coexist with another Noble Vampire Bloodline inside the same individual.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] represents an extreme version of the same principle. His transformation allowed him to simultaneously possess:
- Snow Fire;
- Fulger Lightning;
- and Scarlett Ice.
Rather than interfering with one another, he eventually became capable of switching between or combining the three Count Bloodlines.[49]

### Blood Dragon Evolution
The Bloodline continued existing after the principal Scarlett women entered [[Victor Elderblood]]'s Draconic lineage.
Becoming [[Blood Dragons]] did not simply erase the abilities the women possessed as Vampires. Instead, their pre-existing characteristics were incorporated into their new Dragon existences and continued developing alongside their individual Draconic powers.[50]
Consequently, the Scarlett Bloodline should now be considered an ancestral component of the modern Elderblood descendants originating from [[Scathach]] and Ruby rather than exclusively a Noble Vampire ability.

### Limitations
The Scarlett Bloodline is not automatically powerful merely because it is acquired.
Its effectiveness depends upon:
- individual compatibility;
- training;
- supernatural Energy;
- experience;
- and the strength of the inherited expression.
Chapter 577 explicitly establishes that an initiated member does not automatically obtain power equal to the main Bloodline.[54]
The lineage also should not be used as an umbrella explanation for every ability possessed by the Scarlett sisters. Their original Bloodlines and later Dragon evolutions introduced abilities unrelated to Scarlett Ice.

### Trivia
- The [[Scarlett Clan]] Initiation Ritual changes the recipient's hair to red and gives them the potential to awaken Ice.[55]
- Ruby is [[Scathach]]'s biological daughter, while Siena, Lacus and Pepper entered the lineage through adoption and supernatural Clan initiation.
- Siena developed unusually powerful Ice despite not belonging to Scathach's original biological lineage.[56]
- Pepper's Water manipulation and Lacus' [[The Mist|Mist]]/Smoke abilities should not automatically be classified as Scarlett Bloodline powers.
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was capable of using Scarlett Ice despite never undergoing Scathach's normal Clan Initiation Ritual because of the abnormal ritual connecting him to Ruby.
- The Bloodline can coexist with unrelated supernatural lineages rather than automatically replacing them.
