The Blank Bloodline is the hereditary supernatural lineage possessed by members of the Blank Clan. It originated from the union between a member of the [[Snow Clan]]'s Main Family and a Japanese Noble Vampire belonging to the ancient [[Kuroyami Clan]], a lineage of assassins whose characteristic supernatural ability was the manipulation of Darkness and Shadows.[1][2]
Unlike a completely independent elemental bloodline, the modern Blank Bloodline is a composite lineage containing hereditary traits from both the Kuroyami and Snow families. The Kuroyami side provides the Clan's signature ability to manipulate Shadows, while the Snow ancestry introduced traits such as immunity to sunlight and the potential to inherit the Snow Clan's Fire. The strength and expression of these inherited characteristics vary between descendants; some members manifest primarily the Kuroyami Shadow ability, while rare individuals can awaken both the Shadow and Snow components simultaneously.[1][2]
The most prominent modern example is Kaguya Elderblood Blank. Kaguya naturally possessed the Blank Clan's Shadow power and inherited the Snow Clan's immunity to sunlight, but initially lacked its [[Pyrokinesis]]. After [[Victor Elderblood]] incorporated her into his own Progenitor lineage, the dormant Snow component of her ancestry was strengthened sufficiently for her to awaken Fire Manipulation as well, demonstrating that the [[Snow Bloodline]] had remained present within the Blank lineage despite not being expressed in every generation.[3]

### Origin

#### The Kuroyami Clan
The maternal foundation of the Blank Bloodline originated from the [[Kuroyami Clan]], an ancient Japanese Clan of assassins. Its members were Noble Vampires associated with Japan and possessed a hereditary ability to manipulate Shadows and Darkness.[2]
The lineage's specialization naturally complemented the methods of assassins and shinobi. The ability to hide within, manipulate and travel through Shadows eventually became the defining supernatural characteristic of Clan Blank and remained central to the family's role as spies and assassins even after their relocation to [[Nightingale]].
The Kuroyami lineage should not be confused with the later [[Blank Clan]] as a completely separate modern bloodline. The Blank lineage developed from the descendants produced after Kuroyami ancestry became mixed with that of Clan Snow.

#### Union with Clan Snow
More than a thousand years before the beginning of the series, a male Noble Vampire belonging to the Main Family of Clan Snow traveled outside [[Nightingale]] and became involved with a Japanese Noble Vampire capable of controlling Shadows.[1]
Chapter 604 later identifies the Japanese family as the ancient [[Kuroyami Clan]] and identifies the Snow Vampire as an uncle of Agnes Snow. The two produced a child, creating a new hereditary branch combining both lineages.[2]
The earliest known child of this union did not initially inherit the [[Snow Clan]]'s Fire. He did, however, inherit the Snow family's immunity to sunlight together with his Japanese ancestry.[2]
The resulting lineage therefore contained traits from both parents even when all of those traits were not simultaneously expressed.

### Formation of Clan Blank
The circumstances surrounding the new family eventually transformed what had begun as a mixed Japanese-Snow lineage into the Clan later known as Clan Blank.
The [[Kuroyami Clan]] was experiencing political difficulties, while the descendants created through their connection to Clan Snow lacked sufficient independent support. Agnes' mother, who served as Head of Clan Snow at the time, consequently offered the family protection and resources in exchange for becoming subordinate to Clan Snow.[2]
The agreement provided them with:
- territory;
- political protection;
- resources;
- a powerful Count Clan as their patron;
- a defined purpose within [[Nightingale]];
- and continued association with the family from which part of their bloodline originated.[2]
The family subsequently became one of Clan Snow's most trusted subordinate lineages and specialized in espionage, intelligence gathering and assassination.
The Clan's later role as servants was not originally the fundamental nature of the relationship. Clan Blank began as a subordinate Clan rather than a hereditary servant caste. Political manipulation following the deaths of important family members eventually resulted in a lifetime servitude contract being imposed upon the Clan, changing the relationship between the two families.[2]

### Kuroyami Inheritance

#### Shadow Manipulation
Shadow Manipulation is the primary and most recognizable supernatural ability of the Blank Bloodline. It originates from the Japanese Vampire ancestry inherited from the [[Kuroyami Clan]].[1][2]
Members can manipulate Darkness and Shadows as supernatural extensions of themselves. The power became particularly suited to Clan Blank's culture of assassination and espionage, allowing its warriors to hide their presence, attack unexpectedly and develop specialized techniques based upon Shadow manipulation.
Kaguya and [[Oda Blank]] are both confirmed modern users of this hereditary ability.
The bloodline provides the underlying Shadow affinity; the numerous assassination techniques developed by Clan Blank are applications created through training and should not all be treated as separate hereditary powers.

#### Shadow Affinity
Blank descendants demonstrate an exceptionally natural affinity toward Shadows. For experienced users, Shadows function as far more than areas where light is absent. They can become weapons, concealment, pathways and spaces through which specialized Clan techniques operate.
This affinity eventually allowed members such as Kaguya to develop abilities including Shadow weapons, Shadow movement and considerably more advanced techniques. These applications depend upon skill and training rather than automatically appearing in every newborn member of the bloodline.

### Snow Inheritance

#### Sunlight Immunity
The most consistently inherited Snow characteristic within Clan Blank is the ability to withstand sunlight.
Kaguya explicitly explains that although she did not inherit the [[Snow Clan]]'s Flames, she inherited their immunity to the Sun because of the blood connection between Clan Blank and Clan Snow.[1]
The trait existed from the earliest known generation of the mixed lineage. Agnes later explained that her uncle's first child with the Kuroyami family similarly failed to manifest Snow Fire while still inheriting sunlight immunity.[2]
Sunlight immunity therefore appears to be considerably more consistently expressed among the known Blank descendants than active [[Pyrokinesis]].

#### Latent Pyrokinesis
The [[Snow Clan]]'s Fire did not disappear from the mixed lineage merely because many Blank Vampires failed to manifest it.
A later historical heir of Clan Blank inherited:
- the [[Blank Clan]]'s Darkness;
- the Snow Clan's immunity to sunlight;
- and the Snow Clan's Fire.[2]
This individual provides direct evidence that [[Pyrokinesis]] can remain genetically or metaphysically latent within the Blank Bloodline and reappear in later descendants.

### Dual-Bloodline Expression
Rare Blank descendants can simultaneously express the primary abilities of both ancestral lineages.
The unnamed historical heir described by Agnes was an exceptional example. He wielded the Darkness of Clan Blank together with the Fire and sunlight immunity of Clan Snow. Agnes described him as extraordinarily powerful and strong enough that he could have challenged the political contract binding his family to Clan Snow had he wished to do so.[2]
Rather than reject the Clan that had protected his family, he willingly accepted its relationship with Clan Snow. His decision helped establish Clan Blank as one of the Count family's most trusted subordinate forces.[2]
[[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]] later became the second prominent example of dual expression after her dormant Snow inheritance was awakened through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood.[3]

### Kaguya's Bloodline Awakening
Kaguya provides the clearest modern demonstration of how the Blank Bloodline actually functions.
Before joining [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s direct lineage, Kaguya possessed:
- the [[Blank Clan]]'s natural Shadow power;
- Snow-derived immunity to sunlight;
- but no active Snow Fire.[1]
When Victor transformed Kaguya into a member of his own Progenitor lineage, the process behaved differently from the transformation of an ordinary Human into a Plebeian Vampire. Kaguya was already a Noble Vampire and therefore already possessed established hereditary bloodlines.[3]
Rather than giving her an entirely unrelated personal ability, Victor's Blood strengthened what was already present within her ancestry.
Her Shadow power became considerably stronger and easier to control, while she unexpectedly manifested Fire. Victor concluded that she had inherited the [[Snow Bloodline]] present within him, while Kaguya immediately recognized that more testing would be required to determine how closely her newly awakened Fire matched the conventional Snow inheritance.[3]
Victor reasoned that because his transformation method resembled the Noble Vampire ritual used to transmit bloodlines between generations, Kaguya had received a bloodline rather than developing the personality-derived ability usually observed in transformed Plebeian Vampires.[3]
The event demonstrates that Kaguya's Snow inheritance was not merely a historical genealogical connection. The relevant hereditary information was still sufficiently present within her existence to be activated and strengthened.

### Historical Heir
One of the most important known members of the Blank lineage was an unnamed descendant of the original Snow-Kuroyami union.
Unlike the first mixed descendant, this later heir successfully manifested all of the major ancestral powers discussed by Agnes:
- Snow Fire;
- Snow sunlight immunity;
- and Blank Darkness.[2]
His existence is important because it establishes that the Blank Bloodline did not simply inherit one Snow passive trait while becoming an otherwise independent Shadow lineage. The complete supernatural potential of both families remained present within the ancestry and could manifest together in a sufficiently compatible descendant.
The heir also played a major role in determining Clan Blank's political future. Although he became powerful enough that he could potentially have contested the lifetime contract imposed upon his Clan, he chose to honor Clan Snow because of the support the Count family had given his people during their period of weakness.[2]
His decision helped transform what could have become a hostile relationship into generations of extreme loyalty between the two families.

### Relationship with the Kuroyami Clan
The exact relationship between the names Kuroyami and Blank is somewhat complicated by how the Clan's history is presented throughout the novel.
Early material establishes the family as descendants of a Japanese Noble Vampire capable of Shadow manipulation.[1] Chapter 604 later explicitly identifies the Japanese family involved as the [[Kuroyami Clan]], an Ancient Clan of Assassins.[2]
The modern family is consistently known as Clan Blank.
For encyclopedia purposes, the safest distinction is:
- Kuroyami Clan — the ancient Japanese ancestral Clan from which the Shadow inheritance originated.
- [[Blank Clan]] — the later mixed Snow-Kuroyami lineage established in [[Nightingale]].
- Blank Bloodline — the composite hereditary lineage carried by modern Clan Blank.
Because very little independent information is presently known about the original Kuroyami Clan outside its assassin tradition and Shadow ability, its ancestry is most appropriately documented as part of the Blank Bloodline rather than assuming that every modern Blank characteristic existed in the original Japanese lineage.

#### Later Descendants
[[Yuno Elderblood]] is the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]]. She is therefore genealogically descended from the Blank lineage through Kaguya.
However, Yuno's individual abilities have not been sufficiently detailed to determine which specific Blank traits she inherited. She should therefore not presently be used as evidence that every Elderblood descendant of Kaguya automatically manifests Shadow Manipulation or Snow Fire.

### Abilities and Traits
The following list contains the hereditary characteristics directly supported for the Blank Bloodline rather than trained Clan techniques.
- Shadow Manipulation: The primary supernatural ability inherited from the ancient Kuroyami lineage. Blank Vampires possess an innate affinity for controlling Darkness and Shadows, which forms the foundation of most of the Clan's later assassination and espionage techniques.[1][2]
- Darkness Manipulation: Blank Shadow power can manifest as the manipulation and generation of supernatural Darkness. The novel frequently uses Darkness and Shadow terminology interchangeably when discussing the Clan's inherited power.[2]
- Sunlight Immunity: Descendants inherited the [[Snow Clan]]'s ability to withstand sunlight. This characteristic can manifest even when the individual does not inherit active Snow Fire.[1][2]
- Hereditary Power Inheritance: Traits originating from both the Kuroyami and Snow families remain transmissible across generations rather than one bloodline completely replacing the other.[2]
- Latent Power: An ancestral trait can remain inactive within a descendant while still existing within the bloodline. Snow Fire is the clearest demonstrated example.[3][2]
- [[Pyrokinesis]]: Rare Blank descendants can inherit and actively manifest the Fire of Clan Snow. This was demonstrated by the historical Blank heir and later by Kaguya after the dormant Snow component of her bloodline was strengthened through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[3][2]
- Dual-Bloodline Expression: Exceptional descendants can simultaneously use the supernatural characteristics of both ancestral families, wielding Kuroyami Darkness together with Snow Fire.[2]
- Bloodline Empowerment Compatibility: Dormant Blank/Snow characteristics can be strengthened through sufficiently powerful Bloodline alteration. Victor's transformation of Kaguya dramatically strengthened her Shadow power and awakened her previously inaccessible Fire inheritance.[3]

### Applications
The following are applications developed from the Blank Bloodline's Shadow power rather than separate inherited abilities. Their availability depends upon the user's skill, training and individual development.
- Shadow Concealment: Using manipulated Shadows to hide the user's body and supernatural presence.
- Shadow Merging: Entering or merging with Shadows for concealment and movement.
- Shadow Weapon Creation: Forming weapons from Darkness or Shadow.
- Shadow Movement: Using connected Shadows to approach targets and reposition without conventional movement.
- Assassination Support: The Shadow affinity greatly enhances stealth-based combat and forms the supernatural foundation for Clan Blank's assassination style.
- Espionage Support: Skilled users can use Shadows to observe, infiltrate and remain concealed near important targets.
These applications should not be interpreted as powers that every Blank child automatically knows how to perform. Clan Blank's effectiveness comes from combining its hereditary Shadow affinity with generations of specialized training.

### Limitations
- The Snow component does not manifest identically in every descendant. Several members can possess sunlight immunity without being capable of using Fire.[1][2]
- Active Snow [[Pyrokinesis]] appears to be comparatively rare within Clan Blank.
- The novel has not established that every Blank Vampire inherits every passive ability possessed by members of the Snow Main Family.
- Kaguya herself questioned whether she had inherited the [[Snow Bloodline]]'s emotion-based amplification after awakening Fire; the scene does not confirm that she possessed it.[3]
- Advanced Shadow techniques are learned skills and should not automatically be attributed to the bloodline itself.
- Kaguya's later powers as a Blood Dragon, Goddess and member of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s lineage are not inherent abilities of the original Blank Bloodline.
- Kaguya's extremely advanced Darkness techniques should not be used as the baseline capability of ordinary Blank Vampires.
- The exact proportion of Snow and Kuroyami inheritance within individual members has never been quantified.

### Notes
- Clan Blank is a genuine biological branch of Clan Snow rather than merely an unrelated servant family; [[Ch12 - Rex Invictus V|Chapter 12]] explicitly states that the two families possess a blood bond originating from one of Snow's Main Family members.[1]
- The modern lineage nevertheless possesses its own supernatural identity because its signature Shadow power originates from the Japanese Kuroyami ancestry rather than Clan Snow.[2]
- The earliest known mixed descendant inherited sunlight immunity without Snow Fire, demonstrating that different components of a Noble Vampire bloodline can be inherited separately.[2]
- A later Blank heir inherited Shadow Manipulation, Snow Fire and sunlight immunity simultaneously, proving that the Snow Fire remained within the lineage despite disappearing from some generations.[2]
- Kaguya's awakening in Chapter 592 provides a modern example of dormant ancestral power becoming active after the underlying bloodline was strengthened.[3]
- Assassination Arts and espionage are part of Clan Blank's culture and training rather than hereditary supernatural abilities.
- The original [[Kuroyami Clan]] currently lacks enough independently documented information to require a separate bloodline article; most confirmed information about its hereditary power directly concerns its contribution to the formation of Clan Blank.
- The surname Blank and the hereditary Blank Bloodline should not be confused with the Clan's former subordinate status. The lineage continued to exist independently of the political contract binding it to Clan Snow.
- After Kaguya married [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Clan Blank's political relationship with Clan Snow changed, but this did not alter the historical origin of its bloodline.
