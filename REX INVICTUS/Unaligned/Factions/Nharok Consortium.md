The Nharok Consortium is an association of armed merchants and private fleet owners in the Upper Sectors. It does not try to conquer the [[Elderblood Empire]]. It tries to make money from the disorder of the conquest.[1]

### Overview
Where the [[Varkhess League]] takes worlds, the Consortium takes cargo. Its members have no intention of occupying territory. They want the profit in the instability [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s expansion creates.[1]
The ships fly on forged documents and on proofs of ownership that belong to legitimate companies. That is why the people responsible are hard to identify. Part of the goods is sold to independent governments. The rest reaches groups that are planning a confrontation with the Empire.[1]

### History
The Consortium intercepts deliveries for newly incorporated systems and turns industrial equipment, military components, and minerals onto black markets.[1]
The Empire lets the losses show. For weeks, vanished deliveries appear to delay Imperial projects. They do not. The Empire has never lacked resources. The whole pattern is a trap.[1]
The investigation is opened by the B.H.E. authorities. In this chapter that name is "Black Hand The Emperor," the Third Legion under [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]], recorded on this wiki as [[The Black Hand of the Emperor]]. The investigation uncovers a network of middlemen operating over several trade routes. The network is connected with hundreds of smaller factions, and those smaller factions in turn stand in relation to all the great factions except the Empire. Imperial agents do not answer with a blind strike. They identify the main buyers and follow the resources to a Consortium depot.[1]
The depot is seized in a swift operation. Documents, business records, and equipment are secured, and those records expose further connected storehouses. Cut off from the depots, and unable to keep hiding the work, the Consortium's leaders lose a great part of their economic influence in the adjoining territories.[1]

### Relationship with the Elderblood Empire
The Consortium believes it is robbing a war economy that cannot spare the cargo. The cargo is bait. Imperial dead in these operations are clones, already sent back to the Emperor's galaxy to recover. What the Empire keeps is the map of who is buying, and which small factions are tied to the great ones.[1]
[[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] judges the small powers that snap at the expansion, the pirates among them in so many words, as vermin that scent an opportunity everywhere. The decision at the end of the chapter is to make an example of anyone who strikes the Empire on the belief that a greater faction's protection will excuse it. Kaguya assigns that work to the Third Legion together with the First, [[The Spartans]]. The chapter does not show the Consortium's remaining leaders destroyed.[1]

### Current Status
The known depot is in Imperial hands. Further storehouses have been found from the papers. The leaders' economic influence in the adjoining territories is largely gone.[1]
The chapter does not name those leaders, does not give the depot's location, and does not say the Consortium has been abolished. It also does not pin the Consortium itself to one patron. The hundreds of smaller factions in the middleman network are the ones said to stand in relation to every great faction but the Empire.[1]

### Trivia

- "Hundreds of smaller factions" is the reach of the middleman network, not a headcount of the Consortium's members.[1]
- No earlier chapter on this wiki names the Nharok Consortium. This article uses only Chapter 1203.
