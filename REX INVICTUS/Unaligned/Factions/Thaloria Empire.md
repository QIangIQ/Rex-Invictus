#### First Appearance
Chapter 892
The Thaloria Empire was an extraordinarily advanced interstellar and later galactic civilization founded upon the planet [[Eldoria]] by the [[Xyrra]] ruler [[Thaloria Xyphora]], the woman who would become known as the First Empress Thaloria. Beginning as the civilization of the technologically gifted Xyrra, the Empire expanded from a single planet into a dominant galactic power and eventually reached the position of an Overlord civilization. Its technology became advanced enough that its rulers could contend with entities and civilizations operating on a galactic scale, while the Xyrra's natural affinity for machines caused their scientific development to advance at a rate abnormal even by extraterrestrial standards.[1]
Unlike the ecologically destructive machine civilization implied by older versions of this article, Eldoria under the Thaloria Dynasty was explicitly shown as a futuristic world where extremely advanced technology existed in harmony with nature rather than destroying it.[1] The Empire's technological supremacy nevertheless possessed a ruthless side. Thaloria pursued scientific advancement, military dominance and biological research with very few ethical restraints when she believed the survival or future supremacy of her civilization was at stake. The most extreme example was her research into the [[Yuurakyos]], which eventually resulted in a biological weapon specifically engineered to exterminate their entire race.[1]
The extermination of the Yuurakyo removed the principal warrior race serving [[Galactic Emperor Kolo]] and allowed the Thaloria Empire to dominate its galactic rivals and rise into an Overlord-level power. However, the same extermination later became one of the Empire's greatest strategic mistakes. When vastly more powerful Galactic Emperors emerged after the circumstances of their Sector changed, Thaloria and her daughter were personally capable of opposing them, but the Empire's ordinary forces lacked warriors capable of meeting such beings directly. Velnorah later concluded that the extinct Yuurakyo would have represented their civilization's greatest opportunity to resist those enemies.[1]
The Empire was eventually conquered and its ruling dynasty displaced, with [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]] fleeing beyond her former galactic territory and eventually reaching [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s irregular Mid-Level Sector. Despite the loss of their original territory, the dynasty itself did not culturally consider the Thaloria Empire completely dead. Long afterward, Velnorah deliberately continued the ancient royal naming tradition through her daughter [[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]], treating the survival and prosperity of the royal family as proof that the legacy of the Empire had not disappeared.[2]

### History

#### The Xyrra of Eldoria
The Thaloria Empire originated on [[Eldoria]], the homeworld of the [[Xyrra]]. The Xyrra are enormous humanoid beings, with ordinary members shown standing over three meters tall, and possess an extraordinary natural affinity toward technology. Their relationship with machinery is sufficiently intuitive that members of the species can manipulate and manufacture highly advanced devices with ease, with one demonstration showing a Xyrra constructing a futuristic weapon through little more than a movement of his hand.[1]
Even among this naturally gifted species, [[Thaloria Xyphora]] was exceptional. She would eventually become known as the First Empress of Eldoria and the founder of the dynasty that ruled the planet and later the Empire.[1]
Thaloria was physically disadvantaged compared with ordinary Xyrra. While members of her race normally exceeded three meters in height, she was considerably smaller and physically weaker during her early life. Rather than accepting this weakness, Thaloria compensated through technology and developed an advanced suit that enhanced her body and maintained it in ideal condition.[1]
The construction principles behind this equipment eventually became part of the knowledge transmitted through the Thaloria royal family. Over time, the suit and Thaloria's technological development allowed her to overcome the physical disadvantages that had separated her from ordinary Xyrra and ultimately rival or surpass members of her own species physically.[1]

#### Unification of Eldoria
Thaloria's technological genius was not restricted to personal equipment. With her own abilities and the assistance of other members of the [[Xyrra]] race, she gradually dominated the other races inhabiting [[Eldoria]]. The technological difference became so extreme that the Xyrra effectively appeared godlike compared with less developed species on the planet.[1]
From this planetary conquest emerged the Thaloria Empire. Because an entire race of beings already possessed extraordinary instinctive technological talent, the civilization developed at an enormous rate once brought beneath a single political order.[1]
In less than 2,000 years, the Empire had completely unified Eldoria and had already begun exploring outer space. This places its transition from planetary unification to interstellar expansion within an extraordinarily short period when compared with ordinary civilizations.[1]
The Empire's expansion did not remain exclusively Xyrra. As Thaloria's influence grew, exceptional beings from other races and other planets entered her service. The Thaloria Empire therefore developed from a racial planetary state into a multi-species interstellar power centered around the authority of the Thaloria Dynasty.[1]

#### Conflict with the Yuurakyo
One of the most important civilizations encountered during the Empire's expansion was the [[Yuurakyos]], a naturally warlike alien race associated with [[Galactic Emperor Kolo]]. They were commonly known within Velnorah's Sector as the Assassins of Galactic Emperor Kolo.[1]
The Yuurakyo possessed extraordinary natural bodies, combat perception and instincts superior to most ordinary races. They were also remarkably adaptable, requiring only weeks to adjust biologically to unfamiliar and hostile environments.[1]
A Yuurakyo infiltrator eventually entered the growing group of exceptional alien individuals surrounding Thaloria. Because the infiltrator was operating within territory controlled by the Empress, Thaloria discovered him and chose to capture him rather than simply kill him.[1]
The operation immediately demonstrated the difference between the Yuurakyo and Thaloria's ordinary enemies. Even after constructing a calculated trap and deploying some of her best generals alongside advanced technology, the Empire suffered serious casualties before successfully capturing a single warrior. Several generals were killed and even Thaloria's personal armor was damaged.[1]
Instead of being discouraged, Thaloria became fascinated by the species' biological superiority. She concluded that possessing comparable physical power could be essential for confronting the enemies her Empire would eventually face and began experimenting upon the captured male.[1]

#### The Yuurakyo Experiments
Thaloria's first objective was to reproduce the Yuurakyo's natural advantages for use by her own civilization. She initially attempted conventional cloning, but the species' genetic structure was too unstable to reproduce successfully.[1]
She next attempted to produce Yuurakyo offspring using females of other species. These attempts also failed catastrophically. The nutritional and biological demands of developing Yuurakyo embryos were so extreme that mothers from other races could not survive the pregnancy.[1]
Thaloria then attempted something considerably more ambitious: converting another race into Yuurakyo. Through research involving the Progenitor associated with [[Eldoria]], she learned that genuine racial transformation required changes to the Soul rather than merely reconstructing the physical body. However, the fundamental power that defined the race could not simply be stolen or reproduced through laboratory modification, causing this line of experimentation to fail as well.[1]
During the same period, the Thaloria Empire was already involved in a large-scale war and was beginning to lose ground. An opportunity appeared when a female Yuurakyo infiltrated the facility in an attempt to rescue the captured male. Unlike the first encounter, Thaloria now possessed extensive biological and combat data concerning the species and successfully captured the second warrior with far greater efficiency.[1]
With both a male and female available, Thaloria attempted to produce a Yuurakyo child through artificial insemination. Even this failed. She eventually discovered that Yuurakyo reproduction itself was deeply connected to their warrior culture and biology: the male had to defeat the female and obtain her submission before viable offspring could successfully develop.[1]
Thaloria eventually forced the circumstances necessary for reproduction through the use of powerful aphrodisiacs. The resulting child survived, but was dramatically weaker than either parent.[1]
That weakness ultimately provided the breakthrough Thaloria needed.

#### Coming-of-Age Serum
Through studying the child, the Thaloria researchers discovered that a Yuurakyo's enormous power was not entirely hereditary. Members of the species underwent a coming-of-age ceremony in which their elders injected them with a specialized serum that enormously enhanced their physical capabilities.[1]
The serum was responsible for much of the instability observed within mature Yuurakyo genetics. Because those modifications were not naturally transmitted to descendants, the laboratory-born child possessed a comparatively stable genetic structure and could be studied far more effectively than its parents.[1]
This discovery finally allowed Thaloria to isolate a decisive weakness within Yuurakyo biology.

#### Extermination of the Yuurakyo
Using the information accumulated across years of experimentation, Thaloria developed a specialized biological weapon: a virus designed exclusively to attack members of the Yuurakyo race. The virus targeted their brains and caused effectively immediate death while remaining specialized toward their racial biology.[1]
Thaloria deployed the weapon on a species-wide scale.
The result was the complete extermination of the Yuurakyo race.[1]
The strategic consequences were enormous. The Yuurakyo had represented the strongest military force associated with [[Galactic Emperor Kolo]] and one of the only powers capable of seriously threatening Thaloria's technologically dominant forces. Their disappearance fundamentally altered the balance of power throughout the galaxy.[1]
With the Empire's greatest martial obstacle removed, few civilizations remained capable of opposing the Thaloria Dynasty. Thaloria continued expanding until her authority covered the galaxy and she rose to the position of an Overlord.[1]

### Galactic Empire
At its height, the Thaloria Empire was no longer merely a spacefaring extension of [[Eldoria]]. It had become a galactic civilization ruled by an Empress whose personal and political authority qualified her as an Overlord.[1]
Its expansion incorporated exceptional beings and subordinate races from beyond Eldoria, meaning the Empire should not be understood as an exclusively [[Xyrra]] state. The Xyrra formed its original technological core and ruling civilization, but its territory eventually included numerous alien peoples.[1]
Thaloria's supremacy was built upon several advantages: the Xyrra's inherent technological affinity, her own extreme genius, accumulated research from conquered species, and an increasingly powerful military-industrial civilization capable of competing across interstellar and galactic distances.
The Empire's rise also demonstrates that Thaloria did not depend upon technology because she was personally incapable of fighting. By the later stages of her reign, her technological development had allowed her to become an extraordinarily dangerous combatant in her own right. Velnorah's later recollection indicates that she and her mother were personally among the very few beings within their Empire capable of directly contending with the Galactic Emperors who later threatened them.[1]

### Government
The Thaloria Empire was an hereditary Imperial Dynasty rather than the purely meritocratic technocracy described by the older version of this page. The ruling line originated with [[Thaloria Xyphora]], and descendants of the royal family inherited the name of their mother together with the surname of the First Empress.[2]
Thaloria became the First Empress, while her daughter [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]] became the [[Anna Elderblood|Second Empress]] Thaloria.[1]
Velnorah eventually inherited not merely the throne but the enormous accumulated technological and scientific legacy created by her mother. By her own later era she had become an Overlord and ruler capable of expanding the civilization beyond the limitations of a single planetary or ordinary galactic state.[1][3]
The Empire unquestionably valued technological ability and scientific knowledge because these qualities formed the greatest natural advantage of the [[Xyrra]]. However, Chapter 892 does not establish the detailed social structure claimed by the previous article—such as scientists automatically possessing the highest social rank, a central artificial intelligence governing all commerce and law, or every citizen receiving status based upon technological contributions.

### Royal Dynasty

#### Thaloria Xyphora
[[Thaloria Xyphora]] was the founder of the Empire, conqueror of [[Eldoria]], First Empress and the figure whose dynasty gave the civilization its name.[1]
Born physically weaker and smaller than other [[Xyrra]], she compensated through technological ingenuity, eventually developing equipment capable of eliminating her disadvantages. Her personal rise mirrored the rise of the Empire itself: weakness was treated not as an immutable limitation but as a problem to be solved through science.[1]
Thaloria's reign produced both the civilization's greatest accomplishments and one of its most consequential mistakes. Her eradication of the Yuurakyo eliminated a dangerous enemy and helped secure galactic supremacy, but also destroyed the single warrior race Velnorah later believed could have helped defend the Empire when much stronger opponents appeared.[1]

#### Velnorah Xyphora Thaloria II
[[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]] inherited the title of [[Anna Elderblood|Second Empress]] and the scientific legacy of her mother. She eventually became an Overlord in her own right and continued expanding the Empire's influence across multiple territories and Sectors.[1][4]
Velnorah possessed essentially the accumulated knowledge, technology and research of her civilization when she later fled to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Sector, explaining why even the technological systems of Gods and advanced supernatural civilizations frequently appeared primitive to her.
Her later civilization-building project, Elvenorah, retained her preference for highly technological societies while also reflecting lessons learned from the destruction of her original Empire. Elvenorah deliberately combined technology, magic, supernatural species and distributed administration rather than depending upon a single irreplaceable ruler to personally manage every aspect of civilization.[5]

#### Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III
Long after the territorial destruction of the original Empire, Velnorah named her daughter with [[Victor Elderblood]] [[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]].[2]
The name continued an ancient custom of the Thaloria royal family: descendants inherited their mother's name and the surname of the First Empress. Velnorah deliberately preserved that tradition as a tribute to the destroyed Empire and as a declaration that its royal line still existed.[2]
For Velnorah, the Thaloria Empire therefore remained culturally alive so long as the descendants of its dynasty continued to survive and prosper.[2]

### Society
The original culture of [[Eldoria]] was built around a species for whom technology was practically instinctive. Consequently, scientific and technological development occupied a central position within [[Xyrra]] civilization.[1]
At the same time, Eldoria was not depicted as a sterile machine world. Velnorah's holographic reconstruction specifically showed a futuristic planet where a high-level technological society existed alongside preserved nature in a sustainable balance.[1]
This distinction is important. The Thaloria Empire's technological advancement did not inherently require destroying biological ecosystems or replacing its inhabitants with machinery.
The Empire also became increasingly multi-species as it expanded. Exceptional individuals belonging to other races and originating from other planets became Thaloria's subordinates, indicating that advancement within the Empire was not automatically restricted to Xyrra.[1]
How ordinary conquered populations were governed is not described in sufficient detail to establish whether the Empire operated through direct administration, autonomous planetary governments, forced assimilation or another model.

### Technology

#### Xyrra Technological Affinity
The foundation of Thalorian technology was the innate affinity the [[Xyrra]] possessed toward machines. They did not merely have a technologically advanced culture; individual members of the race were naturally talented at understanding, constructing and manipulating sophisticated technological systems.[1]
A holographic example shown by Velnorah depicted a Xyrra producing an advanced weapon with extraordinary ease, demonstrating how deeply technology was integrated into the species' natural talents.[1]

#### Advanced Power Suits
One of the earliest signature technologies of the Thaloria Dynasty was Thaloria's personal suit. Designed to compensate for her physically inferior [[Xyrra]] body, it enhanced her physical capabilities and maintained her body in ideal condition.[1]
The technology associated with its construction was passed through the royal family across generations.[1]
The suit was sufficiently durable and powerful that damaging it required the efforts of a Yuurakyo warrior who simultaneously killed several of the Empire's greatest generals.[1]

#### Biological Engineering
The Empire possessed extremely sophisticated biotechnology even before attaining complete galactic supremacy. Thaloria's scientists could perform advanced genetic analysis, cloning attempts, artificial insemination, interspecies reproductive experimentation and research into racial transformation.[1]
Their attempts to transform another race into Yuurakyo also produced important discoveries concerning the metaphysical relationship between the body and Soul. Thaloria learned that genuine transformation into another species required altering the Soul rather than merely reconstructing biological tissue.[1]

#### Species-Specific Biological Weapons
The most infamous achievement of Thalorian biotechnology was the biological weapon created to destroy the Yuurakyo. After isolating their unique genetic vulnerabilities, Thaloria produced a virus that targeted their brains and killed members of that race virtually immediately.[1]
The weapon was specific enough to be deployed against the Yuurakyo as a civilization-ending weapon and resulted in their extinction.[1]

#### Interstellar and Galactic Technology
By less than two thousand years after planetary unification, the Empire was already exploring outer space.[1]
Its technological capabilities continued growing until the Thaloria Dynasty could operate on the scale of an entire galaxy. Exact ship classes, propulsion systems and infrastructure from this period have not yet been explained in detail.
Consequently, claims from the previous article regarding specific orbital battle stations, universal planetary shields, limited or unlimited FTL capability, cybernetic armies or every planet being controlled by a centralized AI should not be presented as confirmed unless later chapters explicitly establish them.

### Military
The Thaloria Empire combined the natural technological abilities of the [[Xyrra]] with soldiers and exceptional beings drawn from numerous conquered or subordinate species.[1]
During Thaloria's attempt to capture the first Yuurakyo infiltrator, she deployed some of the Empire's greatest generals together with advanced technology and carefully prepared traps. Despite these preparations, a single Yuurakyo killed several generals and damaged Thaloria's equipment before being subdued.[1]
This defeat strongly influenced later Thalorian military policy. Thaloria recognized that technological superiority alone did not automatically solve encounters with beings whose personal physical power exceeded the assumptions upon which conventional armies were built.
Her attempt to reproduce Yuurakyo power was partly motivated by the desire to supplement the technological strength of her Empire with a species capable of matching extraordinary enemies physically.[1]
After genetic replication proved impossible, Thaloria chose the opposite solution: rather than obtaining Yuurakyo strength for herself, she engineered a method capable of eliminating them completely.

#### Galactic Warfare
By the later period of Thaloria's reign, conflicts surrounding her Empire had escalated beyond conventional planetary war. Velnorah's holographic history showed warfare occurring at scales she considered too large to properly describe as ordinary "battles".[1]
The eventual Galactic Emperors represented a completely different category of opponent from the civilizations Thaloria had previously defeated. Thaloria and Velnorah could fight such beings personally, but ordinary Thalorian forces could not reliably contend with them.[1]
This imbalance between the extraordinary strength of the ruling Overlords and the weaker military beneath them became one of the Empire's decisive vulnerabilities.

### The Yuurakyo Mistake
The extermination of the Yuurakyo was initially one of the greatest strategic victories in Thaloria's history. It removed [[Galactic Emperor Kolo]]'s feared assassin race and helped eliminate the greatest conventional military opposition to the Thaloria Dynasty.[1]
In the short term, Thaloria's decision worked perfectly.
In the long term, it proved disastrous.
When stronger Galactic Emperors appeared, the Empire possessed technology capable of competing at absurd scales, and Thaloria and Velnorah themselves remained capable of fighting them. However, their ordinary subordinates lacked equivalent personal strength.[1]
Velnorah later concluded that the Yuurakyo had been the Empire's best chance at fielding warriors capable of confronting those enemies. By eradicating them rather than eventually integrating or exploiting them as military allies, Thaloria had unknowingly destroyed the force her civilization would later need most.[1]
The fall of the Empire therefore should not be simplified into "technology lost to raw strength." Its technology remained extremely dangerous. The actual failure was a strategic imbalance: the rulers could operate at Overlord-level power, but their civilization lacked sufficient numbers of individual combatants capable of surviving against the new class of enemies.

### Convergence and Fall
At some point after the Thaloria Dynasty had established its supremacy, the strategic environment of its Sector changed dramatically. More powerful Galactic Emperors appeared, placing the Empire against opponents operating on a level vastly above the civilizations it had previously dominated.[1]
Thaloria and Velnorah themselves were capable of opposing these Galactic Emperors, but the same could not be said for most of their subordinates. The Empire consequently entered a war it could no longer win simply through the technological superiority that had secured its earlier conquests.[1]
The eradication of the Yuurakyo now became catastrophic. The warrior race that Thaloria had eliminated would have represented their greatest source of soldiers capable of confronting enemies whose individual power rendered conventional forces increasingly ineffective.[1]
The war progressively destroyed Thalorian control. Velnorah eventually crossed the border of her galaxy while fleeing the conflict and ultimately arrived in the Mid-Level Sector containing Earth, where she encountered a completely irregular concentration of powerful beings, including [[Primordial Entities]], Kali, Apocalyptic Beasts and eventually [[Victor Elderblood]].[1]
The original Thaloria Empire and its territory were subsequently lost to stronger forces. [[Eldoria]] was no longer controlled by its original royal dynasty, and Velnorah became a former Overlord of a ruined Empire.[1][2]
The available chapters do not establish that the Empire fell because its AI systems malfunctioned, because its automated defenses could not adapt, or because Eldoria's machines were inherently inferior to raw physical power. Those explanations appeared in older versions of this page but are not stated in the source material.

### Legacy
The destruction of the Empire did not erase its influence. Velnorah escaped carrying the accumulated scientific knowledge, technological traditions and memories of her civilization, later introducing those principles into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s rapidly developing faction.
Her expertise became foundational to the technological development of Elvenorah, the [[Elderblood Empire]], advanced artificial intelligences, military technology and projects combining technology with [[Magic]], Runes and Divinity.
Velnorah did not simply reproduce her original civilization unchanged. When developing Elvenorah, she created a society organized around a Technocracy where citizens were given stability and essential resources so that education, research and productive specialization could flourish.[5]
Automation and artificial intelligence performed large amounts of ordinary labor, monitoring and security, but Velnorah deliberately avoided making the civilization completely dependent upon machines or one irreplaceable individual. Important responsibilities were distributed among trusted people and capable deputies.[5]
This later design is important because it should not be retroactively imposed upon the ancient Thaloria Empire. Elvenorah represents Velnorah creating a new civilization using the experience gained from the successes and failures of her former one.
The strongest cultural survival of the old Empire remains its royal dynasty. Thousands of years after its destruction, Velnorah continued the Thaloria naming tradition through [[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]].[2]
Velnorah's reasoning was explicit: if the royal family of the supposedly dead Empire still lived and flourished, then in at least one meaningful sense, the Thaloria Empire was still alive.[2]
