The Elderblood Imperial Family is the recognized sovereign household centered around Victor Elderblood, his confirmed wives and his direct, adopted, created and otherwise recognized children. The wider family registry also records established step-relations created through those marriages without treating every relative of a wife as a member of the core dynasty. It is simultaneously a family, a supernatural lineage, a political dynasty and the ruling household of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. It should not be confused with Victor's complete harem, wider love-interest network, Pantheon, military, World Tree network or Imperial citizenry: service to Victor, worship of him, a Soul-level alliance or membership in one of his institutions does not by itself make someone a member of the Elderblood Imperial Family.
The dynasty developed from Victor's Human family, the abnormal Vampire marriage ritual joining him to Violet Elderblood Snow, [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] and [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]], his emergence as a Progenitor, his evolution into the Primogenitor of the [[Blood Dragons]], and the later admission of Vampires, Humans, Gods, Dragons, Demons, [[Werewolves]], Witches, Angels, [[Gorgons]], the individually established World Tree wives [[Roxanne Elderblood]] and [[Amara Elderblood]], extraterrestrial beings and Eldritch entities into his immediate household. Once the Elderblood Empire was established, many wives and children became permanent parts of its command structure as Empresses, military commanders, intelligence officers, scientists, educators, regional rulers, afterlife administrators, cultural authorities and strategic specialists.
The family is held together by more than genealogy. Blood, Soul bonds, Divine Blessings, Victor's recognition, the protected Elderblood identity, shared spiritual safeguards and the restricted True Dragon lineage create an actual supernatural dynasty. By the late Imperial era, however, its internal hierarchies remain deliberately distinct: marital seniority, domestic authority, Imperial office, military command, combat power and public popularity are not the same thing.

### Identity and Scope
Imperial Family
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s recognized wives, biological children, adopted children, created or reconstructed children and other directly admitted family members. This is the sovereign household.
Elderblood Clan
The supernatural Clan and Bloodline descended from Victor's earlier Alucard lineage. It is a major component of the dynasty, but is not synonymous with every institution of the Empire.
[[Dragons Nest Pantheon]]
Victor's Pantheon and supernatural political structure. Many members serve Victor without being members of his immediate family.
[[Elderblood Empire]]
The civilization ruled by the dynasty. Its citizens are subjects of the Crown rather than members of the Elderblood family.
The distinction became increasingly important as Victor's authority expanded from a Vampire Clan into a civilization spanning planets, dimensions and Galactic territory. Victor deliberately created professional military, scientific, administrative, religious and intelligence institutions instead of transforming the entire population into members of his personal Bloodline.[1][2]

### Dynastic Evolution
I
The Walker Family
Before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s [[Awakening]]
Victor began life as Victor Walker, the Human son of [[Leon Walker]] and Anna Walker. His Human upbringing and attachment to his parents remained fundamental to the family-centered worldview he developed after entering supernatural society.[3]
II
The Founding Three
Chapters 2–32
[[Violet Elderblood Snow]] transformed Victor through the Noble Vampire marriage ritual to prevent his death. The simultaneous involvement of [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] and [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] caused the ritual to behave abnormally, connecting all three women to Victor and allowing him to inherit characteristics originating from the Snow, Scarlett and Fulger Bloodlines.[4][5]
III
Clan Alucard
Chapter 131 onward
[[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] established Victor as the head of the Fifth Pillar of Vampire Society and recognized Clan Alucard. Victor abandoned the Walker surname in supernatural society and began constructing a household and political Clan independent from the older Count families.[6]
IV
The Progenitor Lineage
Chapter 260 onward
The creation of [[Eve Elderblood]] demonstrated that Victor had begun developing an independent hereditary lineage. Limbo subsequently recognized him as the Second Vampire Progenitor.[7][8]
V
The Soul-Bound Household
Chapter 550 onward
Victor's relationship with [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood introduced genuine [[Soul Marriage]] into the family. Unlike an ordinary marriage ritual, Soul Marriage directly binds the participants' Souls and later becomes an important component of the communication, recovery and defensive infrastructure surrounding Victor's wives.[9]
VI
The Blood Dragon Dynasty
Chapters 802–920
Victor's Vampiric and Draconic foundations fused into the new Blood Dragon Race. He subsequently reconstructed numerous members of the family as genuine [[True Dragons]] while preserving important characteristics of their previous [[Races (Flamel)|Races]], Bloodlines and Divinities rather than reducing the entire household to identical copies of himself.[10][11][12][13]
VII
The Imperial House
Imperial Era
After Victor founded the [[Elderblood Empire]], his household became a formal Imperial dynasty. His wives assumed military, scientific, administrative, intelligence, policing, educational and planetary responsibilities, while the rapidly expanding second generation developed into Dragon Goddesses, scientists, warriors, administrators and Conceptual specialists.[2][14]
VIII
The Accelerated Imperial Dynasty
Chapters 1137–1183
The civilization-wide temporal ritual was designed to provide approximately 200,000 years of internal development while roughly one year elapsed outside the accelerated region. By Chapter 1183, approximately 190,000 internal years had explicitly passed and the ritual was still active, meaning the planned two-hundred-thousand-year endpoint had not yet been reached.[15][16] During those millennia the Elderbloods became a mature cosmic dynasty possessing dozens of children, specialized Imperial institutions, restricted Draconic lineages, enormous private dimensional estates and a family security system capable of mobilizing against Universal-scale threats.
IX
The Daughter-Wife Generation
Chapters 1155–1165
The family structure eventually expanded beyond ordinary marriage categories. [[Nero Elderblood]] and [[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]] are explicitly described as sister-wives in Chapter 1165, while [[Metis]] Elderblood, [[Akasha Elderblood]] and [[Melida Charlotte Elderblood]] are likewise established in the project chronology as Victor's daughters and wives by the Chapter 1155 period.[17][18] Eve Elderblood also possesses the older dual status of adopted daughter and wife. These relationships are therefore indexed in both the wife and child branches of the dynasty rather than forcing them into only one category.

### Principles of Membership
The Elderblood name is a supernatural identity, not merely a surname.
The mature dynasty distinguishes between Imperial citizenship, subordinate Draconic lineages and genuine membership in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s immediate family.

#### Recognition Before Biology
The Imperial Family contains biological children, adopted daughters, directly created children, reconstructed Souls, Soul-Married wives and other abnormal forms of kinship. Genetics alone therefore cannot define membership.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s recognition is one of the central requirements. By Chapter 1155, the surname Elderblood itself was protected through an Eldritch order created by Victor. Merely claiming or writing the surname does not make an outsider a legitimate member of the dynasty; the deeper supernatural recognition associated with the name depends upon Victor acknowledging the person as family.[17]

#### Admission and Secrecy
Highly trusted outsiders approaching the inner family may be placed beneath temporary security restrictions before complete admission. [[Tyche]] Elderblood, for example, signed an absolute secrecy agreement before receiving sensitive information concerning [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his family. The contract was intended to remain until her eventual [[Soul Marriage]] formally integrated her into the family.[17]

#### Restricted Sovereign Bloodline
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] does not distribute the highest form of his Bloodline indiscriminately. By Chapter 1164, his Blood, the name Elderblood and the True Dragon Race are explicitly treated as marks of Imperial sovereignty and of the Imperial Family itself.[19]
Victor is physically capable of increasing the True Dragon population, but deliberately refuses to turn the Empire's entire population into [[True Dragons]]. The exclusivity is intentional.

#### Worthiness Before Extended Bloodline Access
Blood relation alone does not entitle an extended relative to become a Dragon of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s line. By Chapter 1171, Violet, Ruby, Sasha, Kaguya, Anna and [[Scathach]] support mandatory trials for relatives wishing to enter even the lesser Draconic branches. Their position is that family connection alone does not excuse weakness of character, disloyalty or unworthiness.[20]

#### Maternal Heritage
Joining [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s lineage does not automatically erase the mother's heritage. Blood Dragon transformation repeatedly preserves and integrates previous Racial Essence, Bloodlines, Divinities and supernatural characteristics.[11][13]
This principle allows Victor's descendants to combine his True Dragon and Eldritch inheritance with traits originating from Noble Vampires, Gods, [[Werewolves]], Demons, [[World Trees]], [[Xyrra]], Youkai and other maternal lineages.

#### No Political Wife Factions
Friendships and informal cliques exist naturally inside an enormous household, but [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly rejects political factions among his wives. [[Tyche]] initially expected the Imperial harem to operate through competing factions centered around powerful senior wives, only for Victor's perspective to clarify that such behavior was forbidden. The women could form friendships and social circles, but not hostile political blocs struggling for influence over him.[17]

### Dynastic Reproduction Policy
Reproduction inside the Elderblood dynasty is heavily controlled because the children being produced are not ordinary supernatural beings.

#### Power and Fertility
The greater a supernatural being becomes, the more difficult reproduction generally becomes. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] avoids much of this limitation because he is a Progenitor and possesses unusually complete control over his own body and fertility.[17]
By Chapter 1156, Victor was explicitly preventing additional children during the accelerated Imperial development period through direct control over his fertility.[21]

#### The One-Child Policy
Chapter 1164 establishes that most wives having one child is a deliberate policy, not evidence that Dragon Goddesses are biologically incapable of multiple births.[19]
- Dragon Goddesses can produce more than one child.
- Twins and triplets are biologically possible.
- Producing multiple children simultaneously requires enormous Energy.
- A multiple pregnancy could temporarily leave even an extremely powerful mother severely weakened.
- The wives are nevertheless powerful enough that such pregnancies would not automatically kill them.
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his wives deliberately restricted reproduction because the True Dragon population was already attracting cosmic attention.
- Victor had been warned by Primordial powers to restrain the expansion of his Race.[19]
By this period, only approximately 40–50 [[True Dragons]] existed throughout the entire Sector, including the family.[19]
The scarcity of True Dragons is intentional.
Their limited population acts as one of the natural restraints on a Race whose individual members possess extraordinary compatibility, power and developmental potential. Victor could increase their numbers, but doing so recklessly would upset the balance he was not yet prepared to challenge directly.[19]

#### Multiple Births
The one-child policy does not mean that twins or triplets are impossible. Chapter 1164 explicitly states that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Dragon Goddess wives are physically capable of multiple births; the limitation is the extraordinary Energy cost imposed by carrying several children of Victor's level simultaneously.[19]
[[Hela Elderblood]] is associated with a known twin pregnancy, demonstrating that the household's policy and its biological possibilities are separate matters. Unnamed or unresolved children are not given invented identities in this registry; only individually established family members are counted in the named-child totals.

#### Inherited Power Does Not Erase Individuality
The common foundation does not make [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters identical. Their individual powers can originate from maternal Bloodlines, maternal [[Races (Flamel)|Races]], maternal Divinities, Victor's Blood Dragon lineage, Victor's Eldritch inheritance, personal mutations, independently developed Concepts, Draconic Language, Runes, training and accumulated experience.
[[Norabell Elderblood]], for example, combines Victor's Dragon Eyes and Eldritch inheritance with [[Natalia Elderblood née Alioth]]'s Space-Time lineage.[31] [[Julia Elderblood]] possesses the Divinity of Cultivation, while [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]] possesses an anomalous relationship with Luck and Reality inherited through the interaction of Victor and [[Tyche]] Elderblood.[32][33]

#### Created and Adopted Children
Not every daughter originates biologically from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. [[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]], [[Nero Elderblood]], [[Metis]] Elderblood, [[Akasha Elderblood]] and [[Melida Charlotte Elderblood]] entered the family through adoption, recognition, reconstruction, direct creation, reincarnation or other metaphysical processes.
These daughters can share family-wide protection and recognition, but should not automatically be assigned every biological inheritance listed above unless their individual origin or later transformation confirms it.

#### Powers That Are Not Universal
Do not automatically assign these powers to every Elderblood.
- A wife's personal Divinity is not automatically shared by the other wives.
- Maternal Bloodline powers are not universal family abilities.
- Outer God abilities are not automatically possessed by every wife merely because [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is Eldritch.
- Victor's Primogenitor abilities are not ordinary Blood Dragon racial powers.
- A daughter's maternal abilities should only be indexed when inherited or demonstrated.
- Blood Dragon physiology is widespread among the wives but is not safely universal to every wife.
- [[Aphrodite]]'s fertility support exists within the household but is not confirmed as one identical passive Blessing possessed by every wife.
- Individually bestowed Concepts remain personal powers rather than household-wide inheritance.

#### Why a Full 1–58 Combat List Is Not Used
- Raw Energy is not battle skill. Jeanne has more Energy than [[Scathach]], yet Scathach is explicitly preferred in combat.[42]
- Conceptual matchups matter. Luck, Order, Soul, Space-Time, Life, Death, Darkness and Destruction do not behave like interchangeable quantities.
- Training matters. [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]] possesses the body and Energy of a Dragon Goddess but was criticized for wasting that potential through poor combat fundamentals.[41]
- Preparation changes threat. Ruby, Velnorah, Aline and Pepper can create technology whose destructive output is far greater than their normal personal fighting style suggests.
- Institutional power is not personal power. Commanding a Legion or Pantheon increases strategic importance without automatically determining one-on-one strength.

#### Unidentified and Unnamed Children
Several wives are known to possess children whose personal names have not yet been established in the main story. These remain indexed generically rather than receiving invented names. The Elderblood family registry therefore distinguishes named children from the family's larger total population and does not treat the current count of forty-nine named direct/recognized children as a statement that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] has only forty-nine children.
[[Hela Elderblood]] also possesses the established multiple-pregnancy material associated with her branch of the family. Multiple True Dragon births are biologically possible; the later Imperial policy of usually limiting each wife to approximately one child reflects Energy cost, dynastic restraint and concern over rapidly multiplying [[True Dragons]] rather than biological infertility.[17][19]

### Education and Development of the Imperial Children
The Imperial Princesses are not left to rely only upon inherited power.
Chapter 1161 establishes that the daughters receive a broad Royal education intended to take advantage of their Draconic cognitive abilities. Even daughters with little personal interest in science can rapidly process extremely complicated information because of their supernatural brains; specialization is therefore primarily a matter of interest rather than a lack of basic capability.[46]

[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] deliberately exposes his daughters to much of the Empire's information because he expects them eventually to participate in its operation.[46]
Victor does not grant unrestricted access to every secret simply because someone is his daughter. Projects classified as genuine Imperial trump cards remain compartmentalized even from members of the Imperial Family who lack the necessary authorization.[46]

#### Training
[[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] remains one of the household's greatest martial instructors and rarely refuses a daughter who genuinely wishes to train. The daughters' extraordinary starting potential therefore does not exempt them from technical training, combat education and discipline.[46]

#### Participation in the Empire
The younger generation is encouraged to eventually contribute according to individual talent. Some daughters gravitate toward research, others toward military Legions, while others pursue administrative or Conceptual roles.
The expectation is not that every daughter becomes identical, but that extraordinary inherited potential should eventually be directed toward something productive for herself, the family or the Empire.

### Family Culture and Internal Rules
Despite the enormous scale of the dynasty, later chapters establish several consistent rules governing daily family life.

#### No Unrestricted Power Use Against Sisters
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] forbids his daughters from using their supernatural abilities against one another during ordinary family disputes. The rule is especially important because many daughters possess Divine, Conceptual, Draconic or Reality-altering powers capable of turning an ordinary argument into a catastrophic confrontation.[42]
Passive abilities are treated differently. Stella's inherited Truth-related perception, for example, remains active because it functions naturally rather than as a deliberate attack upon her sisters.[42]

#### Sibling Solidarity
Competition for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s attention, teasing, pranks and arguments are normal, but the daughters possess unusually strong collective loyalty.
When Priscillia prepared to enter an area she was not permitted to access, Rosemary and Sylvia followed because they refused to leave their sister to face the consequences alone. The narration directly establishes that this is characteristic of the family: the sisters may possess difficult personalities, but abandoning one another is not how the household operates.[46]
An attack on one sister becomes a problem for the entire family.
Sibling rivalry remains internal. Genuine external danger rapidly overrides ordinary competition.

#### Privacy
Family members possess supernatural senses powerful enough to make ordinary privacy almost impossible. The castle therefore contains additional barriers and personal security around private areas, particularly the territories belonging to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his wives.[46]
Entering a mother's private quarters without permission can result in severe punishment. One of the harsher threatened consequences is extended "re-education" and training beneath [[Scathach]].[46][38]

#### Discipline
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is famously indulgent toward his daughters, while other mothers compensate through stricter discipline.
Anna commonly represents domestic authority and responsibility. [[Scathach]] represents intensive training and punishment. Individual mothers retain authority over their own daughters, while Victor intervenes when a genuine boundary is crossed.

#### Family Participation
Scientific achievement or Imperial responsibility does not excuse permanent social isolation.
Chapter 1172 uses [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]] as the clearest example. Her tendency to disappear completely into laboratory work became a family problem because Ruby, Velnorah and Aline also possessed consuming scientific responsibilities while still making time for the household.[41]
The expectation is therefore:
Imperial responsibility does not replace family participation.

### Family Traditions
The Imperial Family continues preserving ordinary traditions despite the cosmic scale of its members.

#### Family Gatherings
Chapter 1165 develops into a gathering of more than thirty-five daughters, followed by the arrival of numerous wives and other members of the household.[18]
The size of the gathering itself demonstrates how rapidly the second generation expanded, but the more important cultural point is that the daughters still gather as sisters rather than existing as isolated princesses governing separate domains.

#### Meals
Family meals remain an important tradition. Even after the family acquired servants, Divine beings and technology capable of automating nearly any domestic task, the wives still participate directly in preparing and organizing gatherings.

#### Celestial Banquets
By the late Imperial era, the scale of these traditions becomes absurdly large. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] can construct furniture and scenery from stars, nebulae and cosmic structures while the family treats the resulting celestial banquet as an ordinary gathering.
The contrast is deliberate: the family can manipulate astronomical structures, yet still places value on eating, arguing, laughing and spending time together.

#### Victor's Presence
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters consistently become dramatically more affectionate and childish around their father regardless of their normal rank or intelligence. Even daughters who function as powerful Imperial authorities can revert immediately to seeking Victor's attention when he appears.[47]

### Imperial Residence
The mature Elderblood residence is not a conventional palace.
Chapter 1170 establishes that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s castle is approximately the size of a small country, compared with ancient Japan, while Runic systems allow the structure to expand further when necessary.[48]
The entire castle is designed around the possibility that residents may assume enormous Dragon Forms.
Country-Scale Castle
The physical complex is already comparable to a small country before additional dimensional expansion.[48]
Dragon-Sized Architecture
Entrances in the wives' section can accommodate Dragons hundreds of metres in length.[48]
Runic Expansion
The structure can expand to accommodate the transformations and needs of multiple [[True Dragons]].[48]
Private Worlds
Many personal "rooms" are actually pocket dimensions or private territories vastly larger than conventional architecture.
[[Tyche]]'s residence, for example, is a pocket dimension approximately comparable in area to South America. It contains forests, animals, hundreds of Fairy Spirits, waterfalls, a large sea and a Temple specifically designed around her preferences.[48]
[[Scathach]]'s personal territory is even larger and approaches planetary scale.[48]
These private Dimensions allow wives and daughters to live according to supernatural requirements impossible to support inside an ordinary Humanoid residence.

### Family Governance Doctrine

#### Family Before Political Convenience
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] consistently rejects treating his relationships exclusively as political acquisitions. During the [[Tyche]] situation, he objected to Violet attempting to systematically collect powerful women merely because their powers would benefit the Empire. He preferred relationships to develop through genuine attraction, compatibility and shared experience.[21]

#### Family Before Internal Factions
Friendships and cliques are accepted. Political factions attempting to compete for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] or control the household are not.[17]

#### Authority Follows Competence
Being [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wife does not automatically make someone qualified to command every institution. Victor uses competent non-family specialists alongside wives whose powers and personalities suit particular responsibilities.[2][14]

#### Delegated Imperial Authority
The Empire is far too large for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to personally administer every problem. Mature wives therefore govern enormous portions of military, administrative, scientific and intelligence infrastructure without requiring Victor to personally approve every minor action.

#### Family Matters Are Not Automatically Imperial Matters
Even Violet's status as Empress does not give her unilateral authority over [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s personal relationships or the private choices of every family member. The confrontation surrounding her attempts to expand the harem demonstrates that the daughters and wives can challenge her decisions when she oversteps domestic boundaries.[36]

#### Family Participation Is Expected
Being a brilliant scientist, commander or administrator does not excuse abandoning the household entirely. The family expects its members to remain socially connected despite their individual projects.[41]
