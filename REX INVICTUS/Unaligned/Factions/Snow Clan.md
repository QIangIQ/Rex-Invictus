The Snow Clan, also known as Clan Snow, is one of the oldest Noble Vampire houses of [[Nightingale]], one of the original four Vampire Count Clans that formed the principal Pillars of [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]]' kingdom, and one of the major dynastic branches incorporated into the modern Elderblood Clan. Historically ruled by Agnes Snow, the Clan governed Nightingale's southern territory and was responsible for economic affairs, domestic commerce and international relations.[1]
Unlike the more directly military responsibilities of the [[Scarlett Clan]] and [[Adrasteia Clan]], Snow's influence extended through markets, diplomacy, resource circulation and the economic dependence of other supernatural factions. The Clan possessed enough control over Nightingale's commercial network to damage foreign powers without deploying an army, a capability later demonstrated when [[Victor Elderblood]] used Snow's economic authority to pressure supernatural groups associated with the Youkai.[2][3]
The [[Snow Bloodline]] is unusual even among Noble Vampires. Its earliest known form possessed only minor Fire manipulation together with the extraordinarily rare ability to withstand sunlight. Successive generations deliberately trained and refined that inheritance until modern descendants such as Agnes and Violet could produce Fire vastly beyond the original Bloodline's capabilities.[4]
The decline of Nightingale's old Count system did not destroy the Snow family. Agnes, Violet and several of the Clan's closest members became integrated into Victor's faction, later evolving into [[Blood Dragons]], Dragon Goddesses and Blood Dragonoids while retaining the Snow identity. In the Imperial era, Clan Snow remains recognizable as one of Nightingale's immensely wealthy dynastic houses and continues to be led by members of the Emperor's family.[5]

### Overview
Clan Snow is a Bloodline improved by inheritance rather than defined entirely by its founder. The family began with weak Fire manipulation and sunlight immunity; generations of Snow Vampires intentionally trained the inherited ability until the flames carried by Agnes and Violet became almost unrecognizable compared to those of their ancestor.[4]
Clan Snow's importance to [[Nightingale]] cannot be understood through combat strength alone. While its leaders were unquestionably powerful Vampires, the house's traditional function was primarily economic and diplomatic. Snow governed much of Nightingale's commercial system, assisted smaller Noble families, maintained trade with outside supernatural communities and represented Vampire interests beyond the boundaries of Nightingale.[1]
Its immunity to sunlight was perfectly suited to this purpose. Ordinary Vampires were severely restricted when operating during daytime in the Human World, whereas members carrying the relevant Snow traits could conduct negotiations, business and travel without suffering one of the Race's principal weaknesses.[1]
Under Agnes, much of the Clan's practical government was handled by her husband [[Adonis Snow]]. Agnes remained the actual Countess and strongest authority, while Adonis supplied the administrative, financial and political expertise necessary for the territory's day-to-day functioning. Their daughter Violet was the recognized heiress.
The structure changed drastically after Adonis' death. His absence exposed administrative weakness, corruption and an internal conspiracy. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] temporarily assumed control, exterminated the traitorous faction, reorganized the Clan's finances, expanded the practical role of Hilda and forced Violet to begin taking her position as heiress more seriously.[6][7][2][8]
By the later story, Snow's position had moved far beyond that of a regional Noble Vampire house. Agnes and Violet became members of Victor's Draconic family, Snow's territory remained prosperous beneath the [[Elderblood Empire]] and the lineage continued through a new generation of Imperial Princesses.

### Creation

#### The First Snow
Clan Snow was founded by an unnamed Noble Vampire identified as Violet's great-great-grandfather. He was both the founder and First Leader of the family.[4]
This detail is important because later Snow leadership became strongly associated with powerful women such as Agnes and Violet. The Clan should therefore not be described as having been matriarchal from its foundation. Its earliest confirmed leader was male.[4]
The founder's Bloodline was considerably less destructive than that possessed by his modern descendants.
Its principal characteristics were:
- Sunlight Immunity
- Minor Fire Manipulation[4]
The First Snow refused to treat those inherited characteristics as fixed limitations.
He continuously trained his Fire, strengthening and refining the Bloodline throughout his life. The improved inheritance passed to the next generation, whose members repeated the process. Generation after generation, descendants pushed the same hereditary ability further until the family's once-weak flames evolved into one of [[Nightingale]]'s defining elemental Bloodlines.[4]
Generational Bloodline Evolution
Snow's modern Fire is not simply the untouched power of its founder. It is the accumulated result of multiple generations deliberately improving the same hereditary ability before transmitting it to their descendants.[4]
The Clan eventually gained enough influence and strength to become one of the four principal Count houses serving Vlad's kingdom.

### Territory
☀ Southern [[Nightingale]]
Warm coastal territory, major commercial infrastructure and one of the principal economic centers of Vampire civilization.
Clan Snow historically governed the southern region of Nightingale.[1]
The southern territory differs strongly from the colder northern lands associated with the Scarlett family. Its climate is warmer, with tropical characteristics, large coastal areas and beaches.
Its geographical character matched the Clan's political function. Snow territory developed into one of Nightingale's most important centers of:
- commerce;
- administration;
- domestic trade;
- foreign exchange;
- wealth management;
- and support for other Noble families.[1]
Because Snow controlled a significant portion of Vampire economic infrastructure, its authority extended far beyond citizens physically living in its territory.
The Clan's unusual ability to operate under sunlight further strengthened its position. Representatives could travel through the Human World during hours that would incapacitate or kill ordinary Vampires, making Snow especially useful for international transactions and diplomacy.[1]
By the late Imperial era, the old territorial distinctions of Nightingale had not disappeared. [[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]] visited the regions associated with Snow, Fulger, Adrasteia and Scarlett and observed extraordinary prosperity throughout them, explicitly noting that the territories were led by wives of the Emperor.[5]
The Snow territory therefore survives geographically and culturally even though the old Count relationship beneath Vlad no longer defines its political status.

### Historical Responsibilities
☀ THE ECONOMIC PILLAR
Mission: maintain [[Nightingale]]'s economy, commercial relationships and interaction with supernatural powers beyond the kingdom.[1]
Clan Snow historically handled several interconnected responsibilities.

#### Economy
Snow possessed authority over major portions of Vampire commerce and the internal economic structure of [[Nightingale]].[1]
This included the ability to support developing Noble houses and influence which organizations could participate freely in the Vampire market.

#### Domestic Trade
The Clan managed commercial relationships between territories and families inside Vampire society.
Unlike Fulger, which historically concentrated more heavily upon domestic production and material resources, Snow's role emphasized the movement, regulation and economic organization of those resources.

#### International Relations
Snow also represented [[Nightingale]] in commercial and diplomatic interactions with outside supernatural communities.[1]
Its sunlight immunity made the Clan uniquely useful for this responsibility because representatives did not need to organize their activities around the ordinary Vampire weakness to daylight.

#### Support of Noble Families
Smaller Noble Vampire houses could depend upon the economic system overseen by Snow for continued development.[1]
The stability of Clan Snow therefore affected considerably more people than its direct members.

#### Economic Warfare
Snow could attack without drawing a weapon.
One of the clearest demonstrations of Snow's political power occurred after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] learned that a Youkai associated with an [[Arachne]]-like faction had attempted to force himself upon Agnes.
Rather than immediately invade another supernatural nation, Victor weaponized Snow's economic authority.
He declared that any faction continuing business with the offending group would lose access to the Vampire market.[2][3]
Hilda initially questioned whether such a broad economic decision would require consultation with the other Count houses, but Victor recognized that Snow's traditional jurisdiction specifically included domestic and international commercial policy.[2]
The strategy worked.
Outside supernatural powers valued access to [[Nightingale]]'s markets more than they valued protecting the offending Youkai. Instead of Victor personally searching for the target, multiple factions applied pressure until the Japanese supernatural leadership was forced to respond.[3]
The incident demonstrated that Snow's real geopolitical strength was not merely Agnes' Fire.
It was the ability to make other supernatural powers act because refusing Snow's terms was economically more damaging than cooperation.

### Government

#### Countess and Administrator
During the modern Count era, [[Agnes Elderblood Snow]] was the formal ruler of Clan Snow.
Her husband [[Adonis Snow]] served as the house's principal administrator, political adviser and financial manager.
This division of responsibilities suited both individuals:
- Agnes represented overwhelming personal power, Bloodline authority and the formal Count title.
- Adonis possessed approximately seventeen centuries of political experience and handled much of the bureaucracy, economic management and long-term planning required by the territory.[9][2]
Their daughter, [[Violet Elderblood Snow]], was the recognized heiress.

#### Hilda's Authority
[[Hilda Snow Elderblood]] served as Agnes' Head Maid and one of the most trusted senior figures in the household.
Her position extended well beyond domestic service.
Hilda possessed enough administrative and political experience to operate the Clan when Agnes was unavailable. After Adonis' death and Agnes' emotional collapse, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] placed significant practical responsibility in Hilda's hands while reorganizing Snow's government.[2][10]

#### Violet as Heiress
Violet initially possessed relatively little enthusiasm for Clan administration.
Her priorities centered overwhelmingly around [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
Adonis' death, the internal betrayal and Victor's reforms eventually made it impossible for her to ignore the responsibilities attached to her inheritance.
Violet began studying reports, reviewing Clan affairs and participating more directly in the political structure she was expected to inherit.[8]
Her later transformation into an Empress and one of the principal rulers of Victor's civilization radically exceeded the political scale for which Clan Snow originally intended to prepare her.

### Laws
Snow territory historically followed the female-favoring political culture found throughout several of [[Nightingale]]'s Count regions during the era of the Countesses.[1]
Its criminal system could be severe.
Under Agnes, individuals convicted of sufficiently serious crimes could be sentenced to effectively indefinite forced labor. Prisoners received enough Blood to remain capable of working but were otherwise denied normal freedom.[1]
Those unwilling to submit could simply be executed.
Agnes' Fire made the latter option extremely straightforward.[1]
This distinguished her somewhat from [[Scathach]]. Where Scathach might prefer to destroy a sufficiently irritating enemy immediately, Agnes could view a criminal as a resource whose labor still possessed practical value.

### Bloodline

#### Pyrokinesis
FIRE REFINED ACROSS GENERATIONS
The Snow Clan's famous modern flames began as a weak inherited Fire ability and became progressively stronger through repeated Bloodline development.[4]
The signature offensive ability of Clan Snow is Fire Manipulation.
Members can generate, control and shape supernatural Fire whose effectiveness depends upon:
- Bloodline quality;
- age;
- training;
- control;
- physical and spiritual development;
- and emotional state.
The modern Bloodline is vastly stronger than its ancestral version.[4]
Agnes became one of its highest historical expressions among Noble Vampires, while Violet inherited the same potential and continued developing it through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood, Divine growth and eventual Draconic evolution.

#### Sunlight Immunity
One of the oldest confirmed Snow traits is immunity to sunlight.[4]
Unlike the family's Fire power, this characteristic was already highly valuable in its ancestral form.
Ordinary Vampires are strongly restricted by sunlight. Snow descendants carrying the immunity can operate during daytime without suffering the same racial weakness.
This characteristic helped shape the entire political identity of the Clan.
A Vampire house capable of functioning during the day was naturally better suited to:
- international commerce;
- Human-world travel;
- diplomacy;
- negotiations;
- and monitoring economic activity beyond [[Nightingale]].[1]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] inherited this characteristic through Violet during his own abnormal transformation, contributing to the fact that sunlight failed to harm him even immediately after becoming a Vampire.[11][12]

#### Fire Resistance and Immunity
A Bloodline built around increasingly powerful Fire necessarily requires the ability to survive its own output.
Snow descendants possess strong resistance to heat and Fire, with highly developed users effectively functioning as immune to ordinary manifestations that would severely harm other Vampires.
The defensive inheritance could survive even when the offensive Fire ability did not.
The [[Blank Clan]], whose ancestry includes Snow Blood, lost the main family's [[Pyrokinesis]] after its Bloodline became centered upon Shadow manipulation but preserved important Snow-derived resistances and compatibility with sunlight.[13]

#### Emotion Empowerment
The [[Snow Bloodline]] reacts strongly to emotion.[14]
Intense emotion—particularly anger—can dramatically increase Fire output and the pressure generated by a Snow Vampire.
This characteristic is especially apparent in Agnes and Violet, both of whom possess extremely volatile emotional states when people they love are threatened.
The connection has advantages and dangers.
A furious Snow Vampire can become considerably more destructive, but emotional amplification without sufficient control can also encourage wasteful or indiscriminate use of power.

#### Vampire Count Transformation
Powerful members of the Snow line could manifest the specialized transformation associated with [[Nightingale]]'s [[Vampire Counts]].
The Snow Count Form massively amplifies:
- physical strength;
- speed;
- durability;
- Vampire instincts;
- Fire generation;
- Fire compression;
- and Bloodline output.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] manifested a transformation derived from Snow unusually early after inheriting Violet's Blood.[15]
In this state, Fire enveloped his body, his sclera darkened, his irises became blood-red and increasingly bestial physical characteristics emerged.
Victor's later mastery produced much greater control and enormous wings composed of Fire.[16]
Agnes possessed considerably greater experience with the authentic Snow transformation during the same period, showing how far a genuine ancient member of the Bloodline could develop the state.[16]
Victor's personal modifications and later evolved forms should not automatically be treated as universal Snow abilities.

The process described in Chapter 643 indicates that the founder strengthened his Fire before passing the improved inheritance onward. His descendants continued doing the same.[4]
Snow's Bloodline therefore represents accumulated ancestral development rather than a single mutation remaining unchanged across centuries.
This principle later became dramatically more pronounced after members entered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s lineage.
Victor's Blood strengthened inherited characteristics rather than simply erasing them, and Draconic reconstruction allowed Snow's Fire to continue existing within vastly superior physical and spiritual foundations.

### Clan Treasure

#### Blade of Fafnir
🔥 BLADE OF FAFNIR
The ancestral Divine weapon of Clan Snow, created from the remains of a legendary Fire Dragon King.
The Snow Clan's greatest known ancestral weapon is the [[Blade of Fafnir]].[17]
The weapon was created from materials belonging to [[Fafnir]], a legendary King of Fire Dragons.
Its construction incorporated:
- Fafnir's scales;
- material associated with the Dragon King's body;
- and Blood from Fafnir's heart.[17]
The resulting sword retained characteristics of the Fire Dragon King and possessed exceptional compatibility with Snow's elemental Bloodline.
When used by the Clan's leader, the weapon can channel and amplify Snow Fire, allowing the wielder to produce output considerably beyond ordinary unarmed Bloodline usage.[17]
A martial tradition developed around the sword and passed between successive leaders.
The Clan also maintained a superstition concerning the weapon:
Whenever the leader of Clan Snow was forced to seriously draw upon the Blade of Fafnir, great chaos was believed to follow.[17]

### Subordinate Clan

#### Blank Clan
The [[Blank Clan]] historically functioned as Clan Snow's hidden intelligence and assassination house.
Its ancestry combined Snow Blood with an old Japanese Noble Vampire lineage associated with Shadow abilities.[13]
The resulting descendants developed primarily around Shadow Manipulation rather than Snow Fire, but retained important characteristics inherited from their Snow ancestry, including:
- resistance to Fire;
- compatibility with sunlight;
- and portions of Snow's unusual Vampire physiology.[13]
This made Blank an ideal complementary institution.
Snow handled visible commerce, government and diplomacy.
Blank operated where visibility was undesirable.
Its members served as:
- spies;
- assassins;
- scouts;
- information gatherers;
- covert bodyguards;
- and internal security personnel.
[[Oda Blank]] served as one of the principal historical leaders and attendants connected to Agnes.
[[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]] eventually became the Clan's second leader while simultaneously transferring her personal loyalty increasingly toward [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[18]
During the betrayal inside Clan Snow, Victor relied extensively upon Blank's intelligence network and assassins to identify, isolate and eliminate the conspirators.[6][7]
By the Elderblood era, Blank's old subordinate relationship became secondary to the fact that both families were deeply incorporated into Victor's larger dynasty.

### History

#### Early Snow Clan
FOUNDATION ERAA weak flame became a hereditary inferno.
Clan Snow originated from the Noble Vampire family founded by Violet's unnamed great-great-grandfather.[4]
The founder possessed sunlight immunity and comparatively weak Fire manipulation.
Rather than accept the limitations of the initial Bloodline, he strengthened it through training and passed a more developed inheritance to his descendants. Each generation continued the process.[4]
Over time, the family accumulated enough wealth, combat power and social influence to become one of [[Nightingale]]'s four principal Count houses.
Its unusual ability to operate freely beneath sunlight naturally pushed the family toward responsibilities involving economics and interaction with the outside world.

#### Agnes and Adonis
By the modern era, [[Agnes Elderblood Snow]] held the Count title.
Agnes possessed tremendous Fire power but had little enthusiasm for much of the bureaucracy involved in running a major economic territory.
Her husband [[Adonis Snow]] became the practical administrative backbone of the Clan.
Adonis possessed approximately seventeen centuries of accumulated experience and handled:
- finances;
- diplomacy;
- political planning;
- Noble-family relationships;
- internal administration;
- and numerous matters Agnes preferred not to manage personally.[9][2]
Their daughter [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] was recognized as heiress.
The combination created a relatively functional leadership structure for many years:
- Agnes supplied authority and power.
- Adonis supplied administration.
- Hilda supplied trusted household management.
- Violet represented the next generation.
The problem was that the system had become excessively dependent upon Adonis.

#### Violet and Victor
Violet spent substantial portions of her youth in the Human World and became intensely attached to the Human [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] Walker long before he understood that Vampires existed.
When Victor suffered an injury that would otherwise have killed him, Violet attempted to save him through a ritual designed to transform a Human into her Vampire spouse.
The ritual did not proceed normally.
Victor became an unprecedented type of Vampire and inherited characteristics from not only Violet but also Sasha and Ruby, whose Blood had become involved in the same event.[11][12]
Through Violet, Victor inherited:
- Snow Fire;
- sunlight immunity;
- Fire compatibility;
- and eventually the capacity to reproduce a Snow-derived Vampire Count state.[15]
Violet's relationship with Victor consequently connected Clan Snow to the man who would later become the Second Vampire Progenitor and ultimately the center of the [[Elderblood Empire]].

#### The Death of Adonis
Adonis' health and spiritual condition deteriorated due partly to the circumstances surrounding [[Persephone]] and the Curse attached to his Soul.
He eventually concluded that ordinary death would risk returning his Soul to Persephone.
Rather than allow this, Adonis deliberately arranged for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to consume and absorb him.[19][20][9]
Victor initially resisted because Adonis was Violet's father and Agnes' husband.
Adonis nevertheless insisted.
When Victor finally absorbed him, the process transferred:
- memories;
- accumulated experience;
- political knowledge;
- economic expertise;
- portions of his abilities;
- and approximately seventeen centuries of lived experience.[9]
Adonis himself remained dead.
Victor possessed his memories but did not become the same continuous individual.
Agnes was devastated by the loss.
Her emotional collapse created precisely the weakness enemies inside and outside Clan Snow had been waiting for.

#### The Snow Clan Betrayal
CHAPTERS 289–294Adonis died. The traitors moved. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] answered first.
A conspiracy had developed within Clan Snow over many years.[21][22]
The conspirators expected Adonis' death to leave Agnes incapable of properly governing.
Their plans extended beyond simple political replacement.
Younger Count heiresses such as Violet were viewed as valuable reproductive assets through which powerful Noble Vampire descendants could be produced.
Victor regarded the plan as an unforgivable betrayal.
Adonis' inherited memories allowed him to understand the Clan's internal structure immediately.
He requested temporary operational control over [[Blank Clan]] from Oda and sealed the territory.[6][7]
Experienced assassins were stationed at exits.
Snow forces protected Agnes and civilians.
Kaguya and other Blank agents were deployed against the conspiracy.
Victor's objective was not negotiation.
It was extermination.[7]
Through memories and interrogation, he distinguished principal conspirators from lesser followers and systematically dismantled the faction.
At least one traitor was publicly displayed impaled and burning in the central square as a deliberate warning to anyone considering another internal betrayal.[7]
The purge re-established control over the territory but also exposed the administrative decay hidden beneath Adonis' personal competence.

#### Victor's Reforms
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] temporarily took over Adonis' former office.
The Clan he inherited operational control over possessed enormous wealth and economic influence, but portions of its administration had become inefficient.
Agnes' concentration on Adonis' deteriorating condition had allowed many responsibilities to accumulate.[2]
Victor found:
- bureaucratic inefficiency;
- excessive paper-based record keeping;
- corruption;
- wasteful expenditure;
- members living extravagantly through Clan funds;
- and poor use of Blank's intelligence capabilities.[2][10]
He began restructuring almost immediately.
Victor:
- ordered information systems modernized;
- reduced unnecessary paper administration;
- confiscated property purchased through misused Clan money;
- ordered unnecessary valuables sold;
- cut excessive allowances;
- demanded that capable members contribute useful work;
- reorganized security;
- strengthened coordination with Clan Blank;
- and placed Hilda in a central practical administrative position.[2][10]
Violet herself was not exempt.
Victor reduced even his wife's excessive personal spending when he considered it economically irresponsible.[2]
The reforms were intended to make Snow capable of functioning without requiring another Adonis to personally hold the entire administration together.

#### Economic Sanctions and the Youkai
The Youkai dispute became the first major demonstration of the reformed Clan's economic power.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] refused to solve the problem through immediate military violence.
Instead, Snow restricted commercial access.
The threat of losing entry to [[Nightingale]]'s Vampire market caused supernatural factions with no emotional investment in the dispute to pressure the relevant Youkai themselves.[3]
It was a lesson Victor inherited partly from Adonis:
Economic dependence could sometimes accomplish what an army would otherwise need to do.

#### Violet Accepts Her Position
The crisis finally forced Violet to confront the reality that being heiress involved more than possessing Agnes' Bloodline.
She began reviewing Snow documents and participating in administrative work rather than assuming the Clan could permanently be left to older relatives and servants.[8]
The development became increasingly important because Violet's future political responsibilities expanded far beyond anything the Count house could originally have predicted.

#### Demonic War
During the Demonic War, Clan Snow became one of the [[Nightingale]] territories attacked by [[Diablo]]'s forces.
Violet and Hilda participated in defending the region as the Count houses mobilized against the invasion.[23][14][24]
The conflict demonstrated the destructive value of Snow Fire against large enemy formations.
It also reinforced the importance of emotion within the Bloodline. Violet's output could increase dramatically when her emotional state intensified.[14]
By this stage, however, Snow was already becoming less politically isolated.
Agnes, Violet, Hilda and the other principal members increasingly operated as part of the family coalition surrounding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] rather than as servants whose highest loyalty belonged to Vlad's throne.

#### Integration into Victor's Bloodline
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later strengthened Agnes and Violet through his own Progenitor Blood.[25]
As with the Fulger and Scarlett women, the transformation did not simply discard their ancestral characteristics.
Snow Fire remained Snow Fire.
The inherited Bloodline was instead amplified through Victor's vastly superior foundation.
This marked another stage in the family's transition away from ordinary Noble Vampire biology.

#### End of the Old Count Order
The political system built around Vlad's Count houses gradually ceased to define [[Nightingale]]'s strongest families.
Snow, Fulger and Scarlett formally announced their alliance with Clan Alucard during the expansion of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction.[26]
Clan Scarlett had by this point effectively abandoned its conventional territorial administration. Large numbers of Noble Vampires formerly associated with Scarlett lands were absorbed principally into Snow and Fulger, strengthening both surviving administrative houses.[26]
This development should not be interpreted as Snow absorbing the Scarlett family itself.
The Scarlett dynasty remained distinct.
Snow merely absorbed a substantial portion of the Noble population previously living beneath Scarlett's territorial government.
The alliance publicly confirmed what the supernatural world increasingly understood: the old Count families were becoming politically centered around Victor.

#### Blood Dragon Era
The transformation of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household into a Draconic family changed Clan Snow more radically than any ordinary Bloodline improvement.
Agnes and Violet eventually became [[Blood Dragons]] and True Dragon Goddesses.
Their Vampiric ancestry and Snow Fire remained part of their identities, but their current existence could no longer be described accurately as merely Noble Vampire.
[[Hilda Snow Elderblood]] later entered Victor's Draconic lineage as well, while [[Yuki Snow]] developed into a Blood Dragonoid.
VAMPIRE FIRE → DRACONIC FIRE
The modern Snow house preserves the Bloodline created by its Vampire ancestors, but its principal members now wield that inheritance from the vastly superior biological and spiritual foundation of Dragons.

#### The Imperial Era
The rise of the [[Elderblood Empire]] transformed Snow from one of several regional Vampire governments into a branch of a civilization operating across planets, star systems and galaxies.
Violet became the Empress beside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
Her responsibilities therefore expanded far beyond the original commercial jurisdiction of Clan Snow.
Agnes remained the family's matriarch and continued to be treated as the leader of Snow even after the Count title lost much of its old meaning.
The lineage also expanded through Victor's children.
The most important confirmed Snow-descended Imperial Princesses include:
- [[Valentina Victoria Elderblood]] — daughter of Victor and Violet.
- [[Agatha Agnessa Elderblood]] — daughter of Victor and Agnes.
Valentina is additionally associated with the name Valentina Victoria Snow, reflecting her potential position as a future inheritor of Violet's family line.[27]
This is significant because the Elderblood surname did not automatically erase the older Clans.
A daughter could simultaneously belong to Victor's Imperial dynasty and preserve the heritage of her mother's house.
By the late Imperial era, [[Nightingale]] itself had transformed drastically. The territories of Snow, Fulger, Adrasteia and Scarlett were still recognizable and possessed immense wealth beneath leaders belonging to Victor's family.[5]
The old Count system had ended.
The great houses had survived it.

### Modern Dynastic House
FROM COUNT HOUSE TO IMPERIAL DYNASTY
The Snow name remains attached to its Bloodline, territory, retainers and descendants even though its strongest modern members simultaneously belong to the Elderblood ruling family.
Clan Snow now exists as a house within the Elderblood order.
Its principal surviving identity is maintained through:
- Agnes' continued position as matriarch;
- Violet's status as heiress and Empress;
- Hilda and Yuki's enduring connection to the family;
- the surviving Snow territory;
- Clan Blank's ancestral relationship with Snow;
- the [[Blade of Fafnir]];
- descendants carrying Snow Blood;
- and the cultural memory of one of [[Nightingale]]'s original four Pillars.
The Clan should therefore not be described as disbanded.
What disappeared was its old constitutional position beneath Vlad.
The family did not disappear with it.

### Affiliations

#### Nightingale
Snow originated as one of the four great Count houses of [[Nightingale]].
Its old duties included economics, trade and international relations.[1]
Although Nightingale itself later became integrated into the [[Elderblood Empire]], Snow territory remains recognizable and extraordinarily wealthy.[5]

#### Blank Clan
[[Blank Clan]] was historically subordinate to Snow and supplied covert intelligence, assassination and security capabilities.
The relationship originated through ancestry as well as politics, because Blank retains traits inherited through Snow Blood.[13]

#### Fulger Clan and Scarlett Clan
Snow maintained close political and personal ties with [[Fulger Clan]] and [[Scarlett Clan]].
Violet, Sasha and Ruby were childhood friends long before their marriages to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] connected the three families even more deeply.
The three Clans later formally allied with Clan Alucard.[26]
Snow and Fulger also absorbed many Noble Vampires from the territory abandoned by Clan Scarlett, but this did not erase Scarlett as a family.

#### Elderblood Clan
The modern Snow family is deeply integrated into the Elderblood Clan.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is married to both Violet and Agnes, and their daughters continue the [[Snow Bloodline]] within the Imperial Family.
The relationship therefore extends beyond ordinary political alliance.
Snow is now one of the hereditary family branches connected directly to the Emperor.

#### Dragons Nest Pantheon
The principal Snow women entered the [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]] as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s coalition evolved into a formal supernatural civilization.
Their later Draconic transformations altered their Race without erasing the family history from which their abilities originated.

#### Elderblood Empire
By the Imperial era, Snow's political importance exists inside a system vastly larger than [[Nightingale]].
Violet stands at the center of the Empire as Empress.
Agnes remains a senior member of the Imperial Family.
Hilda and Yuki remain closely attached to the household.
Snow territory itself remains prosperous, while its younger descendants belong to the ruling dynasty.[5]

### Current Status
ACTIVE DYNASTIC HOUSE
The Count house changed governments. The Snow family survived.
Clan Snow remains active.
Historically, it was:
- one of [[Nightingale]]'s four original Count houses;
- ruler of the southern territory;
- the economic and commercial Pillar of Vampire society;
- a principal diplomatic house;
- the main family above Clan Blank;

In the modern era:

The most accurate modern description is therefore:
Former Vampire Count Clan • Active Fire-Blooded Elderblood Dynastic House

### Legacy
Clan Snow's greatest achievement is not that its founder possessed an overwhelming power.
He did not.
Its legacy comes from what the family did with what it inherited.
A weak Fire ability was refined until descendants could incinerate powerful supernatural opponents.
Sunlight immunity became the foundation for an entire diplomatic and commercial institution.
A regional economy became powerful enough to influence foreign supernatural governments without conventional warfare.
A subordinate Shadow family became one of the most effective intelligence networks in [[Nightingale]].
An heiress obsessed with one Human eventually became Empress of a Galactic civilization.
Clan Snow was built on inherited potential—but defined by improvement. Each generation received something from the one before it, strengthened it and passed a greater inheritance forward.
That principle survived [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s rise.
The modern Snow family no longer measures progress merely by the flames of its Vampire ancestors. Its principal members are Dragons and Goddesses, its descendants belong to an Imperial dynasty and its former regional government exists within a civilization whose scale would have been unimaginable to the Clan's founder.
Yet the old inheritance remains identifiable.
Fire.
Sunlight.
Family.
And the expectation that the next generation should surpass the last.

### Trivia
- Clan Snow was one of [[Nightingale]]'s four original Vampire Count houses. Clan Alucard became the Fifth Pillar much later.
- The founder and First Leader of Clan Snow was a man, specifically Violet's great-great-grandfather.[4]
- The Clan was therefore not matriarchal from the moment of its foundation, despite its later association with [[Agnes Elderblood Snow|Countess Agnes]] and heiress Violet.
- The original Snow Fire ability was comparatively weak.[4]
- Its modern destructive [[Pyrokinesis]] resulted from multiple generations deliberately strengthening the same inherited Bloodline.[4]
- Sunlight immunity existed before Snow's Fire became overwhelmingly powerful.[4]
- The Clan's ability to operate under sunlight contributed directly to its usefulness in commerce and international diplomacy.[1]
- Snow historically governed Nightingale's southern territory.[1]
- The Clan's traditional political responsibilities centered upon economic affairs, commerce and international relations rather than direct military administration.[1]
- Adonis handled much of Clan Snow's practical administration despite Agnes holding the actual Count title.
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] inherited approximately seventeen centuries of Adonis' memories and political experience after absorbing him.[9]
- Adonis' death exposed how dependent the Clan's administration had become upon him.[2]
- Victor temporarily took control of Snow's internal administration after Adonis' death and carried out a large-scale purge of traitors.[6][7]
- Victor reduced Violet's allowance during his financial reforms despite being her husband.[2]
- Clan Snow's economic power was sufficient for Victor to pressure foreign supernatural factions without directly invading them.[3]
- The [[Blank Clan]] historically served as Snow's espionage and assassination family.
- Blank's ancestry preserved Snow-derived sunlight and Fire resistances despite its principal Bloodline becoming associated with Shadows.[13]
- Members of the [[Snow Bloodline]] can become stronger when their emotions intensify, especially through anger.[14]
- Victor's earliest recognizable Vampire Count transformation was derived from the Snow characteristics inherited through Violet.[15]
- The [[Blade of Fafnir]] is Clan Snow's principal ancestral Divine weapon and was created from the remains of a Fire Dragon King.[17]
- The Snow family possessed a superstition that major chaos followed whenever its leader was forced to seriously wield the Blade of Fafnir.[17]
- Violet was eventually forced to begin studying Snow's government rather than relying entirely upon Adonis, Agnes and Hilda.[8]
- Snow and Fulger absorbed many Noble Vampires formerly living under Scarlett territorial administration after Clan Scarlett abandoned its traditional governing role.[26]
- This population transfer did not merge or erase Clan Scarlett itself.
- Agnes and Violet eventually ceased being ordinary Noble Vampires and entered Victor's True Dragon lineage.
- Hilda also later entered Victor's Draconic family, while Yuki became a Blood Dragonoid.
- [[Valentina Victoria Elderblood]] is also associated with the name Valentina Victoria Snow, reflecting her possible future inheritance of the Snow family.[27]
- [[Agatha Agnessa Elderblood]] continues the principal Snow Bloodline through Agnes and Victor.
- The Snow, Fulger, Adrasteia and Scarlett territories still exist in late Imperial Nightingale and are described as possessing enormous wealth under women of Victor's family.[5]
- By the late Imperial era, Violet was still interested in locating additional surviving Snow ancestry, demonstrating that the older family identity remained relevant even after hundreds of thousands of subjective years.
- Clan Snow's old Count-house position ended, but the family itself was never disbanded.
