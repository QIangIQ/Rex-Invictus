The Tepes Clan is the Royal Vampire Clan of Nightingale, headed by Vlad Dracula Tepes, the ancient Vampire Progenitor and King of the Vampires. Unlike the great Count Clans, which traditionally functioned as the military and territorial Pillars supporting Nightingale, Clan Tepes occupies the position of the Royal House above that political structure. Its core family consists of Vlad, his Queens, his direct children and the Royal descendants produced through his Bloodline.[1][2]
For most of recorded Vampire history, Clan Tepes represented the center of Nightingale's monarchy. Vlad ruled the Vampire territories for approximately three thousand years and possessed authority over the Royal Family, Royal Guards, the great Count Clans and the Noble Vampires living beneath the Crown.[3] The Clan's extraordinary political standing ultimately derives from Vlad himself: he is a Progenitor rather than merely an exceptionally old Noble Vampire, making his Bloodline fundamentally different from the hereditary Bloodlines of the Count families.
The dynasty was once considerably larger. Vlad had six wives before his current Queen and at least seven biological children—Theo Tepes, Lucas Tepes, Saul Tepes, Adam Tepes, Lilith Tepes, Elizabeth Tepes and Ophis Tepes. The Royal Family was devastated by the death of [[Otsuki Hana]], internal betrayal among Vlad's wives and children, the execution of Theo and three Queens, the departure of Jeanne d'Arc and Anna, and the later movement of Ophis toward [[Victor Elderblood]]'s family.[4][5]
Despite these losses, the Tepes Clan remains active. Vlad continues to rule Nightingale, remarried for political reasons, retained his surviving children and Royal military forces, and later began investing more seriously in Adam Tepes as a possible future successor.[6][7]

### Origin
The precise date on which the modern Tepes Clan formally became a Royal Clan has never been stated.
Its modern form is inseparable from [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]].
However, an important distinction exists between:
- the current Vampire Progenitor known as Vlad Dracula Tepes;
- and [[Vlad III Tepes]], the historical individual whose identity became deeply connected with him.
The Vampire Progenitor is not literally the original mortal Vlad III in the ordinary sense. He consumed the original Vlad and absorbed his Soul, experiences and characteristics, much as other beings consumed by sufficiently powerful Soul-eating abilities can become incorporated into the victor's existence. The Progenitor retained enough of Vlad's identity that the name and history became one of his principal identities.[8][9]
The Tepes name therefore possesses both:
Historical significance
Through Vlad III Tepes and his family.
Progenitor significance
Through the Vampire Progenitor who inherited and continued the identity while becoming the ruler of modern Vampire society.
This distinction also explains the complicated position of Radu Tepes.
The original Vlad III possessed a younger brother named Radu. The Vampire Progenitor later transformed Radu into a Vampire at Vlad III's request.[8]
The individual eventually associated with the identity [[Niklaus Horseman]] therefore possesses a genuine connection to the old Tepes lineage, but his relationship with the modern Royal Clan is highly abnormal and should not be treated as equivalent to that of Vlad's biological children.

His authority historically extended over nearly the complete political structure of Vampire society.
He controlled or commanded:
- the Royal Family;
- the Royal Guards;
- the Noble Vampire population;
- the [[Vampire Counts]];
- and the political institutions of Nightingale.
This authority did not make every Vampire a member of Clan Tepes.

### Bloodline

#### Progenitor Blood
The greatest biological advantage possessed by the Royal Family is direct descent from a Vampire Progenitor.
Vlad's children do not merely descend from an old Noble Vampire family; they directly carry Blood originating from the Progenitor himself.
The significance of this Bloodline is repeatedly demonstrated throughout the story.
[[Theo Tepes]], as Vlad's firstborn son, possessed Blood close enough to his father's that Stella could survive by drinking from him after centuries of exposure had caused her body to reject ordinary Vampire blood.[4]
[[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]] provides an even more extreme example. Her existence contains approximately half of Vlad's Progenitor heritage, including characteristics tied not only to his Blood but also to the special spiritual nature of a Progenitor.[10]
This direct connection gives the Royal Bloodline an exceptional biological foundation even when individual members fail to properly develop it.

#### Royal Blood Does Not Guarantee Strength
The history of Clan Tepes repeatedly demonstrates that powerful lineage does not automatically produce exceptional individuals.
Vlad openly criticized his sons because centuries or millennia of security had caused several of them to become complacent.[11]
Theo was approximately three thousand years old but had neglected combat development.
Lucas trained considerably more seriously and was therefore explicitly stronger despite being younger.
Saul possessed useful perception but lacked discipline.
Adam possessed considerable potential but initially lacked the broader worldview and experience required to become an effective ruler.[11]
The Royal Bloodline therefore provides:
potential, not guaranteed supremacy.

#### Hana's Death
[[Otsuki Hana]] occupied a unique position among Vlad's wives.
She was the woman Vlad loved most deeply.
Her death caused a major psychological shift in the Vampire King and contributed heavily to the deterioration of his remaining marriages and relationship with his children.
Hana was killed by the Elder God Shur, an event that caused Vlad to develop an obsessive hatred toward [[Nightingale]]'s ancient Elder Gods. Centuries later, he personally fought Shur alongside Hana's younger sister [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]].[12]
Hana's death also left Ophis without her biological mother and indirectly helped create the emotional isolation that defined much of the young Princess' childhood.

#### Collapse of the Original Household
The Royal household eventually suffered a catastrophic internal collapse.
Vlad had neglected several of his wives for extremely long periods. Three eventually became unfaithful:
- Stella with her own son, Theo;
- Lucas' mother with a Werewolf;
- Saul's mother with [[Eros]].[4]
Vlad killed all three.
Theo was also executed after his relationship with Stella became public and because of his betrayal of the Crown.[4]
[[Jeanne Elderblood]] and [[Morgana Elderblood]] subsequently separated from Vlad completely.
The result was that in an extremely short period Vlad lost:
- his eldest son;
- three Queens through execution;
- two Queens through divorce;
- and had already lost Hana centuries earlier.
Chapter 335 explicitly describes Vlad as becoming even more isolated after these events.[5]

### Princes

#### Theo Tepes
[[Theo Tepes]] was the First Prince and Vlad's firstborn son.
Theo was intelligent, manipulative and politically ambitious but significantly weaker than his age suggested because he devoted himself to conspiracies rather than martial development.[11]
He eventually participated in [[New Dawn]] and planned to overthrow Vlad.
His relationship with his mother Stella became public during the collapse of the Royal Family.
Knowing Vlad would kill him, Theo deliberately consumed a specialized poison intended to enter Vlad's body when the Progenitor absorbed him after death.
Vlad killed and consumed Theo, inadvertently completing that portion of Theo's plan.[4][13]

#### Lucas Tepes
[[Lucas Tepes]] is the Second Prince and currently the eldest surviving biological son of Vlad.
Unlike Theo, Lucas consistently trained and was explicitly considered substantially stronger by their father.[11]
Lucas nevertheless lacked independence and frequently followed Theo.
Chapter 335 reveals that he had participated in Theo's plan to overthrow Vlad. After witnessing Vlad kill Theo and his own mother, Lucas became terrified that his involvement would be exposed and abandoned the conspiracy.[5]
He later participated in the Royal Castle's defense against [[Bael]].[14]

#### Saul Tepes
[[Saul Tepes]] is the Third Prince.
Vlad described Saul as occasionally perceptive but lazy and inconsistent. Saul remained outside Theo's confirmed [[New Dawn]] conspiracy and survived the collapse of the household.
His mother was executed for betraying Vlad with [[Eros]].
Saul privately believed Vlad bore part of the responsibility for the breakdown of their marriage but remained silent because he feared his father.[5]
He later fought [[Bael]] alongside his siblings during the invasion of [[Nightingale]].[14]

#### Adam Tepes
[[Adam Tepes]] is Vlad's youngest known son and Fourth Prince.
Among the princes, Adam eventually received greater investment from Vlad.
During the Third [[Supernatural Gathering]], Vlad personally brought Adam with him in order to expose his son to the true scale of the supernatural world and broaden the worldview necessary for a possible future ruler.[7]
This suggests that, despite Lucas being the eldest surviving prince, traditional seniority alone no longer determines Vlad's preferred successor.

### Princesses

#### Lilith Tepes
[[Lilith Tepes]] is Vlad and Morgana's eldest daughter and the First Princess.
Although born as a complete Vampire because Vlad's Progenitor Bloodline dominated her racial development, Lilith inherited the ability to manipulate Miasma from Morgana's original existence as a high-level Demon.[5]
She was later captured during the Demonic War and rescued by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].

#### Elizabeth Tepes
[[Elizabeth Tepes]] is Vlad and Morgana's younger daughter and the Second Princess.
Her birth is extremely unusual.
Morgana used her own Dream-based abilities and Essence together with Vlad's Essence to create Elizabeth as genuine independent life rather than through ordinary biological reproduction.
Elizabeth therefore resembles a younger spiritual version of Morgana while remaining her own person.

#### Ophis Tepes Elderblood
[[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]] is Vlad and Hana's daughter.
Among Vlad's children, Ophis possesses one of the strongest demonstrated direct connections to the Progenitor Bloodline. Her supernatural constitution contains approximately half of Vlad's Progenitor nature.[10]
Ophis became increasingly attached to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and eventually regarded him as another father.
After Victor became the Blood Dragon Primogenitor, Ophis increasingly identified herself with the Elderblood family rather than the political Royal household.
She remains biologically Tepes but is presently much more closely affiliated with Clan Elderblood.

### Current Queen
After the destruction of his previous marriages, Vlad eventually chose to marry again.
Unlike his relationship with Hana, the new marriage was explicitly political.
His seventh wife, presently known simply as the [[Vampire Queen]], descends from the Bloodline of another ancient Progenitor that had apparently disappeared thousands of years earlier.[6]
Vlad deliberately approached the new marriage differently from his previous relationships.
Having experienced the catastrophic consequences created by making his wives dependent upon his Progenitor Blood, he did not intend to repeatedly feed his new Queen in the same manner.[6]
The Queen already possessed a daughter before or through circumstances surrounding the marriage, making Vlad the girl's stepfather.[15]
Ophis interpreted the arrival of the new child negatively and believed Vlad had replaced her with another daughter.[15]

### Royal Guard
Clan Tepes is protected by a dedicated Royal Guard serving Vlad and his household.
The Royal Guard is distinct from ordinary Clan membership.
Its members are military retainers rather than biological members of the Tepes Bloodline.
Their strength is considerable.
During [[Bael]]'s assault upon the Royal Castle, six Royal Guards released transformations producing pillars of power significant enough to draw the attention of Jeanne and Morgana from elsewhere in the Capital.[14]
The Guards fought alongside:
- Lucas;
- Saul;
- Adam;
- and Elizabeth
against Bael.
Although the First Duke ultimately defeated the defenders, the combined resistance left him missing:
- one hand;
- one leg;
- and one eye.[14]
The exact portion of the damage caused by individual members of the Royal Family is not identified, so the injuries should be treated as a collective achievement of the castle's defenders.

Major families included:

- [[Snow Clan]];
- [[Fulger Clan]];
- [[Adrasteia Clan]];
- [[Blank Clan]].
A Vampire Count was not simply an aristocratic title.
The Counts functioned as territorial rulers and military Pillars whose power stabilized [[Nightingale]] beneath the King.[2]

### Radu Tepes
Radu Tepes occupies an unusual place in the Clan's history.
The original mortal [[Vlad III Tepes]] possessed a younger brother named Radu. Radu was sickly and eventually transformed into a Vampire through the Progenitor at Vlad III's request.[8]
The later individual known as [[Niklaus Horseman]] carried this identity and became associated with the Horseman experiments and [[New Dawn]].
Radu should therefore not be listed casually among the present Royal Family.
His relationship is better described as:
a historical Tepes Bloodline connection complicated by later identity, experimentation and betrayal.

### The Demonic War
The invasion of [[Nightingale]] during the Demonic War became one of the greatest direct assaults ever made against Clan Tepes.
[[Diablo]]'s forces deliberately targeted Vlad's family because the Royal children could be used as hostages or political leverage against the Vampire King.
[[Bael]], the First Duke of Hell, attacked the Royal Castle directly.[14]
Lucas, Saul, Adam, Elizabeth and six Royal Guards fought him.
Lilith was captured.
Although Bael defeated the defenders, the attack demonstrated that the Royal Family was not merely ceremonial. Several of Vlad's children were active combatants capable of participating in the defense of Nightingale against high-ranking Demons.[14]
The invasion also reinforced the increasingly interconnected relationship between the Tepes and Elderblood families because [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction became directly involved in retrieving captured members such as Lilith.

### Hana and the Elder Gods
Hana's death transformed the long-term foreign policy of Clan Tepes.
Vlad's hatred of the Elder Gods was not merely a political conflict.
It was personal.
Shur had killed the woman Vlad loved most, and the Vampire King spent centuries carrying that loss.
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later organized a wider campaign against the ancient entities threatening the supernatural world, Vlad immediately possessed his own reason to participate.
He and [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]] personally confronted Shur.[12]
The battle represented:
- Vlad avenging his wife;
- Haruna avenging her sister;
- and Ophis' surviving family finally confronting the Being responsible for the defining tragedy of their bloodline.

### Succession
Succession within Clan Tepes has never functioned purely through age.
Theo was the First Prince but Vlad did not consider him worthy of becoming King.
Lucas became the eldest surviving prince after Theo's death but likewise possessed flaws Vlad considered severe.
Saul lacked sufficient discipline.
Adam was younger than all three but possessed qualities Vlad considered more promising.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s first appearance before the Royal Family forced Vlad to recognize just how complacent his sons had become. He explicitly used Victor as an example of the mentality they lacked.[11]
Later, Vlad began personally investing in Adam's growth.
He brought Adam to the Third [[Supernatural Gathering]] so his son could:
- observe genuinely powerful entities;
- understand the scale of supernatural politics;
- and broaden his worldview.[7]
This indicates that the Tepes throne is not automatically inherited by the oldest living son.
Vlad appears to prioritize:
- strength;
- judgment;
- mentality;
- political competence;
- and the ability to survive among the world's greatest powers.

### Military Power
The strength of Clan Tepes comes from several overlapping sources.

#### Vlad Dracula Tepes
The overwhelming majority of the Clan's strategic deterrence historically came from Vlad himself.
He is an ancient Vampire Progenitor whose full power stands dramatically above ordinary Noble Vampires.

#### Royal Children
Vlad's children possess direct Progenitor Blood and thousands of years of potential development.
However, their actual strength varies enormously according to training and individual inheritance.

#### Royal Guard
The dedicated Royal Guard provides [[Nightingale]]'s central palace defense.

#### Remaining Noble Vampires
As King, Vlad retains influence over Noble Vampires who continue serving the Crown.

#### Political Authority
The Tepes name historically commanded the institutions of [[Nightingale]] even when individual family members were personally weaker than the great [[Vampire Counts]].

### Current Status
Clan Tepes remains an active Vampire dynasty under Vlad's leadership.
However, its place in the supernatural world is radically different from the period before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s rise.
Originally:
Tepes stood unquestionably at the apex of [[Nightingale]]'s Vampire hierarchy.
The traditional Count Clans served beneath Vlad, and no competing Vampire Progenitor existed inside modern Vampire civilization.
That situation changed completely with Victor.
Most of the important old Count lineages became connected to Clan Elderblood, while Ophis, Jeanne and Morgana likewise entered Victor's family.
Vlad nevertheless remains:
- King of Nightingale;
- head of Clan Tepes;
- an ancient Vampire Progenitor;
- and one of the oldest surviving supernatural rulers.
The Clan therefore remains politically important even though it is no longer the uncontested center around which every major Vampire power revolves.
The later relationship between Tepes and Elderblood is better described as a complicated alliance between two powerful dynasties connected through:
- family;
- politics;
- shared enemies;
- mutual respect;
- and the increasingly interconnected structure of the modern supernatural world.
