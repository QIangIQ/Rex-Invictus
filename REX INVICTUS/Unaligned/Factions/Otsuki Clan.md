Clan Otsuki is an ancient and highly unique lineage of nine-tailed fox Youkai, known for their deep supernatural bonds and extraordinary powers. Unlike ordinary Youkai, their strength is amplified when they are together, making them a collective force rather than just individuals. This shared power grants them exceptional influence over supernatural forces, making them one of the most feared and respected fox clans in existence.

### Bond and Strength in Unity
One of the most defining characteristics of the Otsuki clan is their unique connection to each other. Their power is greatly enhanced when they are together, making them comparable to a pack of [[Werewolves]] in terms of how their abilities scale in numbers. However, unlike Werewolves, the Otsuki do not follow an Alpha hierarchy—instead, their strength is an extension of their familial bond, making them an almost hive-like force when gathered.
This bond is so strong that it even works on an instinctual level, guiding Otsuki foxes toward one another without conscious effort. Ophis, a being with connections to the Otsuki bloodline, was naturally drawn to Haruna, proving that this bond transcends space and time.

### Notable Members
- [[Otsuki Yoichi]] – Former leader of the clan.
- [[Otsuki Hana]] – A former pillar of the clan, she was a woman of immense power and charisma. Despite being emotionless and quiet, she possessed a natural magnetism that made people follow her. She was deeply connected to [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]], and her death was a major catalyst in his shift toward vengeance and destruction.
- Otsuki Haruna – A black-furred nine-tailed fox of great power. She demonstrates incredible combat ability and is one of the few remaining active members of the Otsuki.

##### Legacy and Influence
Despite their dwindling numbers, the Otsuki clan remains a formidable force in the supernatural world. Their strength in unity, rare lineage, and deep-rooted mystical connections make them an unpredictable and powerful presence. Their history is filled with mystery, tragedy, and influence, ensuring that their legacy will continue to shape the supernatural world for generations to come​.
