### Appearances

#### First Mention
Chapter 892
Galactic Emperor Kolo was the ruler of the [[Kolo Empire]] and one of the major galactic powers encountered during the expansion of [[Thaloria Xyphora]]'s original civilization. His most notable known military asset was the Yuurakyo, an alien warrior race described within Velnorah's Sector as the Assassins of Galactic Emperor Kolo. Kolo was identified as a future enemy of Thaloria at a time when her civilization was still expanding beyond [[Eldoria]], and the subsequent war became one of the greatest obstacles to the First Empress' conquest of the galaxy.[1]
Very little has been directly revealed about Kolo himself. He never appears personally in the historical account shown by Velnorah, and no dialogue, physical description, Divinity or personal combat feat has yet been attributed to him. Most information concerning Kolo is instead reconstructed through the war between his power and Thaloria's Empire, particularly the role played by the Yuurakyo under his authority.[1]

### Physical Appearance
Kolo's canonical physical appearance has not been revealed.
The tall man with completely blue eyes and dark-blue skin shown during Velnorah's holographic account in Chapter 892 was not Kolo. He was an unnamed member of the Yuurakyo race who had infiltrated territory controlled by Thaloria. The description previously attributed to Kolo therefore belonged to one of his Yuurakyo operatives and cannot presently be used as Kolo's own appearance.[1]

### Personality
Kolo's personality remains largely unknown because he has not appeared directly and has not been given dialogue or a point-of-view scene. Descriptions of him as ruthless, honorable, calculating or personally obsessed with combat are therefore speculative.
What can be established is that Kolo possessed enough political and military power for [[Thaloria Xyphora]] to regard him as a future enemy while her civilization was undergoing rapid interplanetary expansion. His Empire possessed the Yuurakyo assassins, and the military forces connected to his power eventually placed Thaloria's growing Empire in a losing position before she developed a biological countermeasure against the warrior race.[1]

### Synopsis

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Kolo's existence was revealed through [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]] while she explained the history of [[Eldoria]] and the rise of her mother, [[Thaloria Xyphora]]. Thaloria belonged to the technologically gifted [[Xyrra]] race and had transformed her planet into an extremely advanced civilization. In less than two thousand years her dynasty had conquered Eldoria and begun exploring space, gradually attracting exceptional individuals from other races and worlds into its expanding territory.[1]
Among the extraterrestrial races encountered during this period were the [[Yuurakyos]]. Velnorah identified them as a warrior race bred around warfare and explained that they were commonly known in her Sector as the Assassins of Galactic Emperor Kolo. Kolo himself was identified as an enemy whom Thaloria would eventually be forced to confront.[1]
The Yuurakyo represented a kind of military power fundamentally different from Thaloria's technological specialization. Their natural bodies were superior to those of most races, their perception and talent for battle were exceptionally high and they could adapt to dangerous foreign environments within only several weeks. One Yuurakyo infiltrated individuals operating within Thaloria's growing sphere of influence, but Thaloria detected him and attempted to capture him alive.[1]
Even with some of her best generals and the support of Eldoria's advanced technology, the capture was extraordinarily costly. The Yuurakyo physically destroyed multiple force fields with his bare hands, fought through the forces surrounding him and caused the deaths of several generals. Thaloria's own armor was damaged during the operation before the infiltrator was finally subdued.[1]
The incident convinced Thaloria that the Yuurakyo possessed a form of strength her civilization lacked. More importantly, she believed that obtaining such power would help her confront her future enemy. The captured warrior was consequently taken into a laboratory and subjected to a series of experiments intended to reproduce the Yuurakyo's biological advantages.[1]
Thaloria first attempted to clone the warrior, but his genetic structure proved too unstable. Artificial insemination with females belonging to other species also failed because the developing Yuurakyo offspring consumed more resources than the host body could sustain. Attempts to transform members of other species into Yuurakyo similarly failed when Thaloria learned from her research into Progenitors that genuine racial transformation required alteration of the Soul rather than merely reconstructing the physical body.[1]
While these experiments continued, the larger war was already underway and Thaloria's Empire was losing. An opportunity emerged when a female Yuurakyo infiltrated the facility in an attempt to rescue the captured male. Unlike the first encounter, Thaloria now possessed extensive biological and combat information concerning the race and prepared accordingly, successfully capturing the female.[1]
Possessing both sexes did not immediately solve the problem of reproduction. The Yuurakyo had developed so completely around conflict that viable reproduction depended upon a male defeating a female and obtaining her submission. Artificial reproduction continued to produce offspring incapable of surviving until Thaloria eventually forced the conditions necessary for conception through chemical intervention.[1]
The resulting child exposed the secret behind the extraordinary physical superiority of mature Yuurakyo. The race possessed a coming-of-age ceremony during which an elder injected the younger generation with a specialized serum that greatly increased their physical abilities. The alterations produced by this serum were not hereditary, explaining both the instability of adult Yuurakyo genetics and why the artificially raised child was considerably weaker than his parents.[1]
This discovery ultimately became catastrophic for Kolo's military power. Instead of successfully reproducing the Yuurakyo's strength, Thaloria used the new biological information to engineer a race-specific virus capable of attacking the Yuurakyo brain and killing members of the species almost immediately. She deployed the biological weapon and eradicated the Yuurakyo population.[1]
Velnorah explicitly described the Yuurakyo as the greatest power of the Galactic Empire. Their extermination therefore removed the greatest known military asset associated with Kolo. Once they were gone, no remaining opponent in the galaxy could match Thaloria in a technological war. She proceeded to subjugate the entire galaxy and established herself as its Overlord.[1]
The historical account does not directly depict a final battle between Kolo and Thaloria. It also does not explicitly show Kolo being killed. What is established is that the greatest power belonging to his Galactic Empire was destroyed, the resistance against Thaloria collapsed afterward and Thaloria successfully conquered the galaxy.[1]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Kolo himself has not reappeared in the modern era, but the legacy of his Yuurakyo forces survived through the data and research preserved by Velnorah. Approximately two hundred thousand years into the development of the [[Elderblood Empire]], Velnorah produced cloned Yuurakyo while working with [[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]].[2]
Velnorah admitted that she had once regarded the Yuurakyo as close to an ideal warrior race. Her standards had changed dramatically after living among [[True Dragons]], [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s hybrid creations and the other exceptional beings of the Elderblood Empire. Compared with those later creations, she now considered the Yuurakyo heavily flawed as pure combatants. Their extraordinary adaptability nevertheless remained valuable enough that she planned to incorporate the characteristic into a new population of technomages.[2]
The development provides a distant legacy for Kolo's former military power: a race that had once been important enough to threaten Thaloria's expansion was eventually reconstructed generations later as biological material for a civilization operating on a vastly greater scale.[2]

### Power and Status
Kolo's personal combat capabilities have never been directly demonstrated. Although his title establishes him as a Galactic Emperor, Chapter 892 does not reveal his Divinities, race, fighting style, weapons or physical statistics. Consequently, the powers of the Yuurakyo should not automatically be assigned to Kolo himself.[1]
The available information nevertheless establishes the scale of the political and military power under his authority.
- Galactic Sovereignty: Kolo possessed the title of Galactic Emperor and ruled the [[Kolo Empire]], a power important enough to become one of the principal obstacles to Thaloria's continued expansion.[1]
- Command of the Yuurakyo: The [[Yuurakyos]] were known throughout Velnorah's Sector as the Assassins of Galactic Emperor Kolo, directly associating the warrior race with his military authority.[1]
- Interstellar Military Power: During Thaloria's experimentation upon captured Yuurakyo, her Empire was already engaged in war and was described as losing. This demonstrates that the forces opposing her were capable of threatening a civilization whose greatest specialty was extremely advanced technology.[1]
- Imperial Military Resources: Velnorah described the Yuurakyo as the greatest power of the Galactic Empire. Their extermination was the decisive strategic event that removed the greatest known obstacle standing between Thaloria and complete galactic domination.[1]
- Unknown Personal Power: No direct feat has been shown for Kolo himself. His placement as an Emperor indicates extraordinary status within his historical civilization, but assigning specific levels of strength, durability, Divinity or martial ability would presently be speculative.[1]

### Relationship with the Yuurakyo
The precise political relationship between Kolo and the entire Yuurakyo species has not been completely explained. They are specifically called his assassins, demonstrating that Yuurakyo warriors served as a military force associated directly with him.[1]
Their biology made them exceptionally valuable for this role. A single operative was capable of infiltrating Thaloria's expanding civilization and required her best personnel and advanced technology to capture. Another member later penetrated deeply enough into the same territory to attempt a rescue operation. These incidents demonstrate that Kolo's Yuurakyo were useful not merely as front-line soldiers but also as infiltrators capable of operating inside highly advanced enemy territory.[1]
The destruction of their species ultimately removed what Velnorah identified as the Galactic Empire's greatest power and caused the larger strategic balance to collapse in Thaloria's favor.[1]

### Relationship with Thaloria
Kolo and [[Thaloria Xyphora]] were opposing Galactic rulers during Thaloria's original expansion. Velnorah specifically introduced Kolo as an enemy her mother would confront in the future, and Thaloria's desire to acquire Yuurakyo power was partially motivated by the belief that she needed such strength to defeat that enemy.[1]
Their conflict became a demonstration of two very different forms of military superiority. Kolo possessed access to a warrior race whose individual members could overcome technological defenses through exceptional biology and combat capability, while Thaloria specialized in research, preparation and technological adaptation. Unable to simply reproduce the Yuurakyo's strength, Thaloria eventually attacked the problem at the biological level and exterminated the race entirely.[1]
The story has not shown the final confrontation between Kolo and Thaloria personally. Therefore, while Thaloria ultimately conquered the galaxy previously contested by Kolo's power, claims that she personally killed Kolo remain unconfirmed by the currently available account.[1]

### Formerly Attributed Abilities
The following characteristics were previously associated with Kolo through the abilities of the Yuurakyo but are not confirmed as powers belonging to Kolo himself:
- Superhuman physical strength derived from Yuurakyo physiology.
- Yuurakyo combat perception and instinct.
- Rapid adaptation to hostile environments.
- Yuurakyo biological durability and stamina.
- Enhancement through the Yuurakyo coming-of-age serum.
- The blue eyes and dark-blue skin of the unnamed Yuurakyo infiltrator.
Unless Kolo is later confirmed to belong to the Yuurakyo race, these characteristics should remain indexed under [[Yuurakyos]] rather than treated as personal abilities of Kolo.[1]

### Trivia
- Kolo is first mentioned in Chapter 892 during Velnorah's account of the history of her original Sector.[1]
- Despite the importance of his title, Kolo himself is never shown in the holographic sequence. The only individual shown when his name is introduced is an unnamed Yuurakyo operative.[1]
- The tall figure with entirely blue eyes and dark-blue skin previously identified as Kolo was actually that unnamed Yuurakyo.[1]
- Kolo's race has not been canonically identified in Chapter 892. There is therefore currently no direct basis for listing him as a Yuurakyo.[1]
- Kolo has not been explicitly identified in the revealed source material as a Primordial God, nor have any personal Divinities been named for him.
- The title Galactic Emperor should not by itself be treated as proof of a specific Divine power level. Velnorah uses Imperial and Overlord terminology to describe positions of cosmic political authority whose exact individual capabilities can vary.[1]
- The Yuurakyo were considered so important to Kolo's power that Velnorah described them as the greatest military power of his Galactic Empire.[1]
- Ironically, Velnorah later believed exterminating the Yuurakyo had harmed her own civilization in the long term. After her Sector underwent its transformation and much stronger Galactic Emperors appeared, she considered the Yuurakyo the best military force her Empire could have possessed against those new enemies.[1]
- In Chapter 1164, Velnorah reconstructed Yuurakyo through cloning. By then she considered them inferior to the strongest races and hybrid warriors of the [[Elderblood Empire]], although she still valued their adaptability.[2]
