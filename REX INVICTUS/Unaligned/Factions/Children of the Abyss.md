Children of the Abyss is the Seventh Legion of the [[Elderblood Empire]] and its most secretive specialized military organization. Personally established by [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Violet Elderblood Snow]], the Legion is responsible for defending the Empire against threats originating beyond conventional dimensional boundaries, including hostile cosmic entities, primordial horrors and interdimensional invaders. Unlike the other six Imperial Legions, whose principal functions concern conventional warfare, territorial security, intelligence, scientific development and law enforcement, the Children of the Abyss exist to confront dangers capable of threatening the fundamental stability of Reality itself.[1]
The Seventh Legion was established during the early millennia of the [[Imperial Calendar]], around the period in which the Empire completed the organization of its principal offensive and defensive military forces. Its existence is concealed from ordinary public knowledge, with its membership, operational hierarchy and combat capabilities maintained under exceptional secrecy. Although recognized as one of the Seven Legions, the organization does not publicly disclose the identities of its officers or the supernatural beings serving within its ranks.[1]
The Legion's command restrictions are particularly severe. Victor Elderblood, Violet Elderblood Snow and Azathoth are the only individuals identified as capable of directly commanding its forces. [[Hela Elderblood]] possesses the potential to exercise similar authority in the future, but doing so would require her to become sufficiently powerful to withstand the dangerous nature of the organization without suffering mental deterioration.[1]
Its personnel are described as extraordinarily dangerous beings whose appearances can threaten the sanity of unprepared observers. The Legion is associated with an eye-shaped insignia, while its concealed commander is described as a hooded figure possessing wings that absorb surrounding light. Even the appearance of its members represents a potential hazard, reinforcing the necessity of concealing their identities and restricting their deployment.[1]
The Children of the Abyss represent the Empire's ultimate specialized response to threats that cannot be adequately confronted through ordinary military methods. Its existence ensures that Victor possesses a dedicated institution for dealing with dangers extending beyond conventional planetary, stellar or even dimensional warfare, while the complete nature of its members and capabilities remains undisclosed.

The Children of the Abyss occupy a distinctive position within the Empire's military hierarchy. Their responsibilities concern threats whose nature differs fundamentally from that of ordinary armies, hostile civilizations or political organizations. An invading civilization can threaten planets and territories through military force, but an extradimensional or existential entity may endanger the very conditions that permit those territories to exist.[1]
The Seventh Legion is intended to respond to this higher category of threat. Its establishment reflects Victor's recognition that military strength alone is insufficient when confronting opponents capable of operating beyond familiar supernatural and physical limitations.
The organization's hidden status further distinguishes it from the other Legions. Its existence is acknowledged within the Imperial military structure, but its actual composition and operational capabilities are withheld from public knowledge. This secrecy serves both to protect sensitive military information and to prevent unprepared individuals from encountering beings whose supernatural nature may itself be dangerous.

### History

#### Those Who Rule Reality
The Children of the Abyss emerged during the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s preparations for conflict against the powerful civilizations and supreme supernatural authorities inhabiting the greater Cosmos. After discovering the technological and military capabilities of civilizations existing beyond his own Sector, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] recognized that his Empire required considerable further development before confronting such opponents.
He consequently initiated an extraordinary temporal ritual intended to provide approximately 200,000 years of internal development while only a single year elapsed outside the affected region. The accelerated period allowed Imperial civilization to undergo enormous military, technological, political and supernatural changes, with Victor and his Empresses continuing to supervise major developments through their Avatars.[2]
The Empire's expansion created the need for a unified military structure capable of performing numerous specialized responsibilities. Victor reorganized its forces into Seven Imperial Legions, establishing separate organizations for military conquest, territorial defense, intelligence, technological development, elite protection, public law enforcement and the suppression of extraordinary existential threats.[2][3][1]
The Sixth Legion, [[The Silent Judgment]], and the Third Legion, the Black Hand of the Emperor, were established during the Empire's early development to maintain public order and address clandestine threats. The [[Stellar Arsenal]] followed as the Fourth Legion, concentrating upon research, forbidden technology and superweapons. The Fifth Legion was expanded into an elite protective force, while the First Legion and Second Legion became the principal offensive and defensive formations of the Imperial military.[3][1]
The responsibilities assigned to these organizations addressed the requirements of a rapidly expanding interstellar civilization. Nevertheless, Victor understood that the dangers awaiting his Empire were not limited to conventional military conflict.
The greater Cosmos contained beings and phenomena capable of affecting dimensions and Reality through powers that ordinary supernatural civilizations could not necessarily comprehend or resist. Such threats required a response distinct from the deployment of enormous conventional armies or increasingly powerful military spacecraft.
The Children of the Abyss were consequently established as the Seventh Legion through the personal involvement of Victor and Violet. Their organization was concealed from the general population, and their identities and capabilities were placed under exceptional restrictions.[1]
The Legion's intended purpose was to defend the Empire against extradimensional threats, hostile cosmic entities and primordial horrors. Its mandate extended to protecting the boundaries of Reality and eliminating hostile forces whose existence or actions threatened those boundaries.[1]
The supernatural nature of its members made unrestricted deployment dangerous. Their appearances could overwhelm the minds of ordinary observers, and the ability to command them required exceptional strength and resistance. Rather than placing the organization beneath an ordinary military commander, Victor restricted its direct command to himself, Violet and Azathoth.[1]
Hela was also identified as possessing the potential to command the organization in the future. However, her eventual qualification depended upon becoming sufficiently powerful to withstand the supernatural dangers associated with the Legion, making her candidacy conditional rather than an established appointment.[1]
The Legion's ordinary operational commander remained classified. A hooded figure with wings capable of absorbing surrounding light was associated with its concealed leadership, but the individual's personal identity was not publicly revealed.[1]
The Children of the Abyss thereafter remained one of the most heavily concealed institutions within the Empire's military structure. Their secrecy contrasted with the other Legions, whose commanders, general responsibilities and organizational identities were comparatively accessible.
The formal establishment of the Seventh Legion thus completed an essential element of Victor's military preparations: a force reserved for enemies whose capabilities could threaten not merely Imperial territory, but the fundamental integrity of the Reality containing it.

### Purpose and Responsibilities
The Children of the Abyss possess a mandate centered upon threats of an existential or extradimensional nature. Their responsibilities are distinct from those of the Empire's conventional Legions because their intended opponents may not be limited by ordinary physical boundaries, military capabilities or the supernatural systems familiar to Imperial civilization.[1]

#### Extradimensional Defense
Extradimensional defense is one of the Legion's principal responsibilities. The organization exists to protect the Empire against hostile forces capable of originating from or entering through dimensions outside those ordinarily occupied by its population.[1]
Such threats may pose problems for conventional territorial defense because dimensional movement does not necessarily follow the same limitations as ordinary interplanetary or interstellar travel. An enemy possessing sufficient dimensional capabilities may threaten locations that would otherwise be protected by astronomical distances or conventional military infrastructure.
The Seventh Legion's specialization provides the Empire with an organization specifically intended to confront this category of danger.
The precise methods used to detect, intercept or eliminate extradimensional invaders remain classified. No complete inventory of detection systems, dimensional barriers or specialized weapons assigned to the Legion has been disclosed.

#### Defense Against Cosmic Entities
The Legion is responsible for confronting hostile cosmic entities whose capabilities may exceed those of ordinary supernatural combatants.[1]
Within the greater cosmology, powerful entities can possess extraordinary authority over supernatural phenomena, fundamental Concepts and aspects of Reality itself. Such beings may threaten their surroundings through more than conventional destructive power.
The Children of the Abyss are intended to provide a military response to these threats, allowing the Empire to deploy forces suited to opponents whose supernatural nature may render ordinary weapons or defensive measures ineffective.
The Legion's mandate does not establish that its personnel can automatically defeat every cosmic entity. Its purpose is to confront such enemies, while the exact limits of its combat capabilities remain undisclosed.

#### Defense Against Primordial Horrors
The suppression of primordial horrors forms another important aspect of the Legion's responsibilities.[1]
These threats are associated with supernatural dangers of extraordinary scale or nature. Their existence may create circumstances in which conventional distinctions between military offense, defense and territorial control become insufficient.
The Seventh Legion's role is to protect the Empire against such beings and prevent their continued activity from endangering Imperial civilization.
The term primordial horrors should not automatically be interpreted as referring exclusively to the [[Primordial Entities]] responsible for maintaining the fundamental structure of the native Universe. The Legion's mandate identifies a category of extraordinary threats rather than a confirmed declaration of war against every Primordial Entity.
The exact beings classified as primordial horrors by the Empire remain unidentified.

#### Protection of Reality's Boundaries
The Children of the Abyss are charged with protecting the boundaries of Reality, distinguishing their responsibilities from conventional territorial defense.[1]
The Empire exists within a vast cosmological structure containing numerous dimensions and supernatural systems. Threats capable of destabilizing the boundaries separating these environments can create dangers extending beyond the destruction of individual planets or civilizations.
The Seventh Legion exists to oppose hostile incursions that threaten those boundaries.
Its responsibility concerns the preservation of the Empire's security against extradimensional dangers, but the specific mechanisms through which it maintains or restores dimensional stability have not been revealed.
The Legion should therefore not be credited with universal control over all dimensional barriers or the fundamental laws governing the native Universe.

#### Existential Threat Elimination
The Children of the Abyss are intended to eliminate threats that ordinary military forces may be unable to neutralize.[1]
Its establishment recognizes that certain enemies cannot be adequately confronted through conventional military strategies alone. An opponent capable of surviving extraordinary destruction, bypassing dimensional restrictions or threatening Reality itself may require a specialized form of intervention.
The Legion's response to such threats is characterized by its extreme nature and the dangerous forces placed under its authority.
However, the organization's mission of existential threat elimination does not independently establish that every member possesses abilities such as unrestricted Existence Erasure, Conceptual Manipulation or immunity to all supernatural attacks.
Its exact offensive capabilities remain classified.

#### Interdimensional Invasion Response
The prevention of interdimensional invasion forms part of the Legion's greater defensive mandate.[1]
Whereas the [[Order of the Iron Veil]] maintains conventional planetary and territorial defenses, the Seventh Legion is intended to address dangers approaching through conditions that may bypass ordinary military infrastructure.
This specialization gives the Empire a dedicated response to incursions whose source, nature or method of entry differs from conventional warfare.
The precise procedures governing deployments against such invasions have not been described.

### Secrecy and Classification
The Children of the Abyss operate under an exceptional level of secrecy, surpassing that ordinarily associated with the Empire's other military organizations.[1]
Although the Seven Legions possess specialized responsibilities, most have established commanders and publicly identifiable functions. The Seventh Legion is different. Its membership, internal organization, operational commander and methods remain concealed.
This secrecy is particularly significant because the Legion's own personnel may represent a danger to individuals lacking the ability to withstand their supernatural nature.

#### Hidden Existence
The Seventh Legion is deliberately concealed from the wider population. It exists as part of the Empire's official military structure without exposing its complete organization or personnel to ordinary Imperial society.[1]
The distinction between being officially recognized and publicly understood is important. The Legion's existence does not require the Empire to disclose the identities of its members or the nature of its capabilities.
Its hidden status also prevents hostile civilizations from obtaining a complete understanding of the Empire's specialized response to existential threats.
The precise administrative methods through which the Empire preserves this secrecy have not been established.

#### Classified Personnel
The identities of the Legion's members remain undisclosed.[1]
Its personnel are associated with supernatural characteristics sufficiently abnormal to threaten ordinary observers, but the complete list of races, entities or individual beings comprising the organization has not been revealed.
The Legion's exact numerical strength is similarly unknown.
No confirmed list of ordinary soldiers, subordinate officers, specialized military divisions or individual operatives is available.

#### Classified Operations
The Legion's missions are subject to restrictions corresponding to its extraordinary strategic role.
Because it exists to confront threats capable of endangering Reality itself, its deployment is associated with circumstances far beyond routine military engagements.
However, no comprehensive public record of specific operations conducted under the formal designation Children of the Abyss has been established.
Its confirmed founding and stated responsibilities should not be expanded into invented campaigns, battles or victories involving unidentified personnel.

#### Victor Elderblood
Main article: [[Victor Elderblood]]
Victor Elderblood is the founder and supreme authority of the Children of the Abyss. Its establishment reflects his determination to prepare the Empire for threats capable of exceeding the military and technological standards familiar to his civilization.[1]
Victor possesses extraordinary powers associated with [[Chaos]], the fundamental structure of Reality and his evolution into an Outer God. His supernatural development grants him the capacity to confront phenomena that would overwhelm ordinary Divine or Demonic beings.
His authority over the Seventh Legion is therefore supported by both his position as Emperor and his exceptional level of existence.
The organization's classified nature ensures that its deployment remains subject to his direct authority rather than ordinary public military administration.

#### Violet Elderblood Snow
Main article: [[Violet Elderblood Snow]]
Violet Elderblood Snow is the co-founder of the Children of the Abyss and one of the individuals authorized to command its forces.[1]
As one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s principal Empresses, Violet possesses exceptional authority within the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Her supernatural development also places her far beyond the capabilities of ordinary supernatural rulers.
Her participation in establishing the Seventh Legion demonstrates the direct involvement of the Imperial Family in preparing for threats whose nature exceeds conventional military concerns.
Violet's authorized command position should not be confused with the undisclosed identity of the Legion's ordinary operational commander.

#### Azathoth
Main article: Azathoth
Azathoth is one of the three individuals capable of exercising command over the Children of the Abyss.[1]
Her supernatural nature is fundamentally different from that of ordinary native Gods. As an immensely powerful Eldritch Being, she possesses an exceptional relationship with forms of existence originating outside the native Universe.
This makes her particularly suited to commanding a force whose personnel may possess dangerous or incomprehensible supernatural characteristics.
Azathoth's authority to command the Legion does not establish that she personally founded it or created every being serving within its ranks. Its confirmed founders remain [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Violet.

#### Hela Elderblood
Main article: [[Hela Elderblood]]
Hela Elderblood has been identified as a potential future commander of the Children of the Abyss.[1]
Her candidacy depends upon the possibility that she may eventually develop enough power to withstand the Legion's dangerous supernatural nature.
The ability to command the organization is not solely a matter of military experience. A prospective commander must also possess the necessary strength and resistance to avoid suffering mental deterioration from exposure to the forces under their authority.
Hela has not been established as a current commander. Her position remains a future possibility rather than a confirmed operational appointment.

#### Classified Operational Commander
The identity of the Legion's operational commander remains concealed.[1]
The associated description concerns a hooded figure wearing armor and possessing wings capable of absorbing surrounding light.
No confirmed personal name, racial classification or complete set of abilities has been publicly established for this individual.
The figure's unusual appearance is consistent with the secrecy and supernatural dangers surrounding the Legion, but physical descriptions alone do not establish an individual's identity.

### Appearance and Insignia
The Children of the Abyss possess an appearance and visual identity that differ considerably from those of the Empire's conventional military organizations.
Their uniforms are associated with an eye-shaped symbol, while their personnel possess characteristics capable of causing severe psychological harm to observers who cannot withstand their supernatural nature.[1]

#### The Eye Insignia
The eye serves as the recognizable symbol associated with the Seventh Legion.
Its presence is connected to the disturbing nature of the organization's concealed personnel.
The exact visual design of the insignia, the method through which it is applied to uniforms and whether the emblem possesses independent supernatural abilities have not been comprehensively documented.
The danger associated with seeing a member of the Legion should not automatically be attributed to the symbol alone. The confirmed concern involves exposure to beings whose appearance may be incomprehensible or mentally destructive.

#### Concealed Faces
The Legion's personnel are associated with faces that should not be observed directly by unprepared individuals.[1]
This restriction distinguishes them from ordinary supernatural soldiers, whose appearances generally do not represent an independent threat to the observer's sanity.
The precise reason for this danger remains undisclosed. It may involve the supernatural characteristics of their bodies or the inability of weaker beings to comprehend what they are witnessing, but no complete universal mechanism has been established for every member.
The exact appearances of individual operatives remain classified.

#### Hooded Armor and Wings
The Legion's concealed leadership is associated with hooded armor and wings that absorb surrounding light.[1]
This appearance reflects the organization’s abnormal supernatural nature and its separation from conventional Imperial military formations.
No confirmed personal identity can be established solely through these characteristics.
The material composition of the armor, the biological or supernatural nature of the wings and the powers possessed by their bearer remain unspecified.

#### Mental and Perceptual Hazards
The Legion's most clearly identified supernatural danger concerns the effect that observing its personnel may have upon an unprepared mind.
Their appearance can cause madness, making direct visual exposure potentially dangerous even without an ordinary physical confrontation.[1]
This characteristic reinforces the importance of secrecy and the restrictions imposed upon individuals attempting to command or interact with the organization.
However, the precise limits of the effect remain unidentified. The information available does not establish that every observer is guaranteed to become insane regardless of supernatural protection, mental resistance or level of existence.

#### Combat Capabilities
The Children of the Abyss are intended to confront enemies capable of threatening the Empire through extraordinary dimensional and existential powers.
This mandate establishes the intended scale and specialization of the organization's operations, but it does not provide an exact numerical measure of its members' destructive capabilities.
No definitive baseline has been established for the individual strength of ordinary Seventh Legion personnel.
Their ability to confront existential threats should not be interpreted as proof that every operative possesses all forms of Conceptual Manipulation, Existence Erasure, Reality Manipulation or resistance to every supernatural phenomenon.
The Legion's actual combat methods remain concealed.

#### Resistance and Survival
The severe restrictions placed upon the Legion's command suggest that its personnel possess a supernatural nature requiring exceptional resistance or compatibility from those who interact with them.
The potential danger of exposure is explicitly recognized through the requirements imposed upon Hela before she could become a commander.[1]
Nevertheless, no complete list of resistances possessed by every member has been established.
The Legion's classified nature prevents a definitive assessment of its members' defensive abilities, individual immunities or survival mechanisms.

### Strategic Importance
The Children of the Abyss represent an essential part of the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s preparations for conflicts extending beyond the scale of its original supernatural civilization.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Empire possesses powerful armies, extraordinary technological resources, elite combatants and numerous Divine beings. Nevertheless, the existence of hostile forces capable of operating beyond conventional dimensions creates dangers that cannot necessarily be addressed through ordinary military procedures.
The Seventh Legion exists to confront these threats.
Its importance is not based upon a publicly disclosed number of soldiers or an inventory of destructive weapons. Instead, its strategic value derives from its specialized purpose and the exceptional restrictions placed upon its personnel and command.
The organization gives the Empire a dedicated response to enemies whose capabilities may threaten the boundaries separating dimensions or the fundamental structure of Reality.
Its secrecy also prevents hostile civilizations from obtaining a complete understanding of the forces Victor has prepared for the most dangerous categories of supernatural conflict.
By maintaining the Children of the Abyss separately from the conventional Legions, the Emperor ensures that the military establishment possesses an organization reserved for threats of an entirely different nature.
