Victor's Faction, frequently referred to as the Alucard Faction, was the independent multiracial political and military coalition founded under Victor Alucard during the Demonic War. It represented Victor's first deliberate attempt to create a political power whose loyalty did not flow through Vlad, the Vampire Crown or any pre-existing Pantheon. Its foundations had developed gradually around Victor's household and allied Vampire families, but the faction itself became a distinct organization when Victor's associated groups formally agreed to unite beneath a new coalition in Chapter 547.[1][2]
Unlike a conventional Vampire Clan or single-race kingdom, the faction was designed from the beginning to accommodate independent peoples with different governments, cultures and supernatural systems. Noble Vampires eventually stood beside Youkai, Humans, Fairies, Amazons, Goddesses, Demons, [[Gorgons]] and other populations without requiring those groups to abandon their original racial identities.[3][4]
At its most developed stage, the faction possessed a central government, common laws, representatives for its major populations and a network of semi-independent rulers whose territories remained locally autonomous while cooperating beneath Victor's supreme authority.[4] This structure became one of the principal administrative foundations of the later Dragon's Nest.
The faction did not collapse through defeat or internal dissolution. Its independent identity became unnecessary once Victor acquired Pantheon-level territory and transformed the coalition into something larger. The creation of Pantheon 7475909777 began that transition, while the formal registration of Dragon's Nest as Victor's multiracial Pantheon marked the institutional successor to the older coalition.[5][6] The older faction structure continued to be referenced during the subsequent consolidation of territories and populations before becoming primarily a historical term.[7]

The Alucard Faction occupied a transitional period in Victor's political development. Before its formation, Victor possessed a powerful Clan, wives from several Noble Vampire families, personal followers and numerous allies, but these relationships did not yet constitute a single independent government. After the faction's disappearance, those same peoples existed within the much larger political framework of Dragon's Nest and, eventually, the [[Elderblood Empire]].
Its historical importance therefore lies less in any single territory than in the political model it created: multiple races could maintain their own rulers and identities while participating in one greater organization governed by common laws and ultimate loyalty to Victor.

Its governments, military groups, populations and alliances became constituent parts of Dragon's Nest. The phrase "Victor's Faction" remained usable informally for Victor's side, but no longer represented the earlier standalone government.

### Foundation

#### Pre-Faction Political Bloc
The faction's roots preceded its official founding. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household already united individuals belonging to multiple supernatural traditions, while his marriages to [[Violet Elderblood Snow]], [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] and [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] permanently connected him to three of [[Nightingale]]'s most important Noble Vampire families.
His rise as the Second Vampire Progenitor made those personal relationships politically significant. [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett|Scathach Scarlett]], Agnes Snow, Annasthashia Fulger, Kaguya, Jeanne, Morgana, Victor's maids and numerous other followers increasingly operated as one political bloc even before that bloc possessed a formal name.
The Demonic War accelerated this development. Vlad's traditional political system repeatedly proved too slow or too restrictive for Victor's objectives, while Victor's own family and allies increasingly followed his decisions independently of the Vampire Crown.[17][18]

#### Proposal of an Independent Faction
By Chapter 543, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was no longer thinking merely in terms of strengthening Clan Alucard. He explicitly wanted to create a new Faction capable of incorporating several races rather than reproducing [[Nightingale]]'s Vampire-centered system.[8]
The proposal also exposed an important weakness: Victor possessed powerful individuals but lacked a true independent country. His coalition still relied heavily upon territories and infrastructure belonging to existing organizations. Building the faction therefore required administrative and territorial development in addition to military strength.

#### Formal Founding
The faction became an independent political organization during Chapters 546–547.[1][2]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] gathered the powers willing to follow him and proposed that they formally unite beneath one faction. The decision represented a direct break from the assumption that Victor's political authority existed only because Vlad recognized Clan Alucard as a Vampire Count Clan.
Clan Adrasteia and [[Clan Alioth]] could not initially become ordinary constituent members because of their obligations elsewhere. Victor refused to solve that issue by forcing contracts upon people he considered genuine allies. Cooperation built through trust was more valuable to him than magically enforced loyalty.[2]
The newly established organization therefore began as a coalition rather than a conventional centralized nation.

### Political Philosophy
“
The faction's defining political idea was not that every race should become the same, but that different peoples could remain themselves while accepting a shared political order.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Faction was deliberately constructed around populations that had historically viewed one another as enemies. Vampires, Youkai, Humans, Demons and Divine beings were not required to erase their cultures or racial systems merely to participate in the same government.
Local rulers retained authority over their own peoples. Haruna remained ruler of the Youkai, [[Viviane]] retained her relationship with the Fairies, [[Aphrodite]] remained an important Divine authority and the Vampire Clans continued maintaining their own internal structures.
The faction instead created an additional political layer above those governments. Matters internal to one population could remain local; matters affecting multiple constituent peoples could be handled through the faction's shared leadership.
This decentralized design allowed the organization to expand quickly without destroying the institutions that made newly associated populations functional.

### Government

#### Faction Leader
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] Alucard was the founder, Faction Leader and supreme authority of the organization.[4]
The faction was not a democracy in which the Council could overrule Victor. Representatives existed because Victor did not want or need to personally administer every dispute involving every race. Ultimate sovereignty remained with him.
Victor nevertheless preferred delegation to micromanagement. Competent leaders were expected to make decisions within their own responsibilities, and only matters requiring wider authority needed to rise toward Victor or the collective leadership.

#### Government Mansion
Chapter 730 established a dedicated mansion intended to function as the center of government for the Faction.[4]
The building served as the faction's clearest formal political headquarters during its independent existence. Snow and Fulger political operations also became closely associated with the new governmental center.

### Early Legal Charter
The earliest clearly described common laws came from negotiations surrounding the alliance between [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s side and Haruna's Youkai.[3]
I
Internal Non-Aggression
Members and allied populations were prohibited from maliciously attacking one another. Severe violations could lead to execution or expulsion from the offender's organization.[3]
II
Unforgivable Crimes
Rape, exploitation of children and experimentation upon other species were among the offenses severe enough to warrant death.[3]
III
Dispute Resolution
Conflicts between allied populations were to be judged by an available senior authority. Disputed judgments could be escalated toward the faction leadership and trusted representatives for review.[3]
These rules were not a complete legal code. Their purpose was to establish a minimum common standard that prevented disputes between individuals from escalating into conflicts between entire species.
The same political principle later became increasingly important throughout Victor's larger civilization: individuals were judged primarily through their actions and allegiance rather than being treated automatically as enemies because of race.

Clan Alucard and Victor's Faction were closely connected but should never be treated as synonymous.

### Relationship with Parallel Organizations

#### Blood God Religion
The [[Blood God Religion]] and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Faction shared Victor as their central figure but fulfilled different functions.
The Religion was fundamentally a religious and social institution built around worship of Victor. Its organization involved faith, temples, clergy, education and the administration of believers.[13][14]
Victor's Faction was a political and military government.
A person could therefore belong to Victor's political side without being defined primarily as a member of the Blood God Religion.

#### Biblical Hell
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became its King, [[Biblical Hell]] represented one of his greatest territorial, economic and military assets, but the realm retained its own society and administration.
Hell should therefore be understood as a parallel kingdom under the same sovereign, not as a city or headquarters belonging to the Alucard Faction.
Abbadon, Alcántara and other Infernal cities were territories of Hell. They should not be listed as original bases of Victor's Faction merely because Victor controlled both organizations.

#### Victorius Aeternus
Victorius Aeternus was principally associated with the [[Blood God Religion]]. Its connection to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] naturally made it useful to his wider political system, but it should not be presented as though it was established specifically as the Faction's national capital.

#### Independent Allies
The faction also cooperated with powers that never became constituent members.
Vlad and the forces directly loyal to [[Nightingale]]'s Crown remained politically separate despite repeatedly fighting beside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
The same distinction applies to various Witches and, during the later transition period, Pantheons that cooperated with Victor against mutual enemies.
Alliance did not automatically equal membership.

### Territories and Political Centers

#### Government Mansion
The Government Mansion established in Chapter 730 was the faction's formal administrative center and the most accurate location to identify as its headquarters.[4]

#### Snow Territory
Snow territory in [[Nightingale]] developed into one of the coalition's major early population centers. Both Vampires and Humans lived within the expanding region, contributing to the need for dedicated Human representation in the later Council.[4]

#### Youkai Territory
Haruna's Youkai retained their own territory and ruler after forming an alliance with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Their membership in the greater coalition did not require annexation of their homeland.

#### Distributed Territory
The faction remained geographically decentralized for most of its history. Its members occupied [[Nightingale]], Earth, Youkai lands and other supernatural territories, while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] simultaneously ruled institutions that were not themselves identical to the Faction.
This lack of one national territory was one of the reasons the later acquisition of genuine Pantheon land became so politically significant.

### Major Conflicts

#### Demonic War
The Alucard Faction was born directly from the pressures of the Demonic War. [[Diablo]]'s invasion demonstrated that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] could no longer depend upon pre-existing governments to protect everyone associated with him.
The war drove the Snow, Fulger, Scarlett and other associated powers closer together, forced Victor to recruit outside [[Nightingale]] and transformed his personal household into a recognizable independent political bloc.[1][2]

#### Conflict with New Dawn
[[New Dawn]] had repeatedly targeted [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], his family and allied supernatural groups long before the Faction existed.
The organization's experiments, conspiracies and manipulation of multiple races made it one of the coalition's longest-running external enemies. Its activities continued influencing Victor's foreign policy long after the Demonic War.

#### Greek Pantheon War
The conflict with the Greek leadership represented the greatest territorial turning point in the faction's history.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s victory over [[Olympus]] destroyed an ancient Pantheon's previous political structure and left him with Divine territory capable of supporting something far larger than a decentralized coalition.[15][5]
The resulting political opportunity directly accelerated the transformation of Victor's Faction into a true Pantheon.

#### War against the Elder Gods
By the final period of the archive, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] no longer fought through the Alucard Faction alone. His diplomatic network included independent Pantheons and civilizations powerful enough to participate in large-scale joint campaigns.
The defeat of hostile Elder Gods and acquisition of additional territories in Chapters 873–887 created the conditions for mass consolidation beneath Dragon's Nest.[20][16]

### Transition into Dragon's Nest
THE FACTION WAS NOT DESTROYED.
It was superseded by the civilization it helped create.

#### Pantheon 7475909777
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s conquest of the Greek political order gave his coalition what it previously lacked: a genuine Divine territorial framework.
Following the war, the Akashic system recognized Victor's reconstruction of the conquered domain as Pantheon 7475909777.[5]
This was an important institutional milestone, but it should not be treated as the exact moment Victor's Faction vanished. The coalition continued functioning during the following political transformation.

#### Formal Recognition of Dragon's Nest
The more decisive change occurred around Chapter 868 when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] formally named the emerging Pantheon Dragon's Nest.[6]
The System recognized it as a genuine Pantheon whose membership was not restricted to one race or religious culture. Dragons, Vampires, Demons, Humans, Youkai, [[Werewolves]] and other peoples could exist beneath the same greater structure.
This was effectively the faction's founding philosophy elevated to the scale of an official Pantheon.

#### Final Consolidation
The later conquest of hostile Elder-God territories allowed [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to move from building an alliance to reorganizing entire populations and worlds.
Chapter 888 still refers to Victor's Faction while describing the relocation of allied populations lacking suitable homelands into newly acquired territories.[7] This makes the transition best understood as a process rather than a single instantaneous dissolution.
Once Dragon's Nest became the normal government for those peoples, the older Faction's independent political purpose disappeared.

### Legacy
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Faction was Victor's first successful experiment in governing a civilization rather than merely leading a Clan or commanding a group of exceptionally powerful individuals.
Its most important contribution was institutional rather than military. It demonstrated that multiple supernatural races with incompatible histories could exist beneath one government without every constituent population being culturally absorbed into a single race.
The Council of [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] established the principle of recognized representation. The alliance charter established basic laws applicable across racial boundaries. The decentralized governmental model allowed established rulers to keep their authority while participating in a larger political order. The faction's military demonstrated the advantages of combining radically different supernatural systems beneath one command structure.
These ideas survived the organization itself.
Dragon's Nest expanded them to a Pantheon-wide civilization. Later Imperial institutions extended the same philosophy across worlds, races and eventually Galactic territories.
