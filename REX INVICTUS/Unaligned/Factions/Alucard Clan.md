Clan Alucard was the Vampire Clan founded by Victor Alucard after his appointment as [[Nightingale]]'s Fifth Vampire Count. Unlike Victor's Faction, which later became a multiracial political coalition encompassing entire races and organizations, Clan Alucard was fundamentally Victor's personal Vampire Clan, household and developing Bloodline.[1]
The Clan was formally created when [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] elevated Victor to the position of Vampire Count. Before accepting the title, Victor was required to establish a supernatural Clan name distinct from his Human surname, Walker. After learning Vlad's complete name, Victor reversed Dracula and adopted the name Alucard.[1] The choice deliberately symbolized Victor's intention to become Dracula's opposite rather than merely another subordinate beneath him.
Vlad subsequently recognized Clan Alucard as the Fifth Pillar of Vampire Society, with responsibility for exploration and the discovery of new lands.[1] Victor was only twenty-one years old and had existed as a Vampire for a fraction of the time normally associated with a Vampire Count, making the creation of an entirely new Pillar highly abnormal.
At first the "Clan" was primarily political. Victor himself was the only actual Alucard. That changed as he learned to create Vampires through his own Blood, accepted individuals into his family and ultimately developed the unique Vampire lineage that became the foundation of the much broader [[Elderblood Bloodline]].[2][3]

### Creation

#### The Fifth Vampire Count
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s rise into [[Nightingale]]'s aristocracy occurred shortly after his confrontation with Vlad.
Vlad and [[Alexios Alioth]] presented Victor with the same type of supernatural contract used by Nightingale's other [[Vampire Counts]]. The agreement granted Victor the authority and privileges of a Count while binding him to secrecy concerning information restricted to the highest levels of Vampire society.[1]
Before signing, Victor required a formal Clan identity.
"Walker" represented his Human family. Vlad's system required the newly appointed Count to possess a supernatural surname appropriate to the Noble Clan he would establish.
DRACULA
⇄
ALUCARD
A reversed name for a Vampire who intended to become Dracula's equal and opposite.
Victor selected Alucard by reversing Dracula.[1]
To Victor, the decision represented the type of ruler and family head he intended to become. He did not want to reproduce Vlad's distrust toward those closest to him. To the older Vampires watching the exchange, however, the symbolism was much more provocative: Victor had effectively created a name declaring that he intended eventually to stand opposite Dracula as an equal.
Vlad understood the implication and laughed rather than objecting.[1]

#### Recognition as the Fifth Pillar
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] signed the Count contract, Vlad announced the creation of a new Pillar throughout [[Nightingale]].

Exploration · Discovery · Expansion
Clan Alucard was assigned responsibility for exploring and discovering new lands.[1][4]
The appointment was extraordinary because Victor possessed none of the conventional foundations expected from a new Count Clan. He had:
- no ancient Noble lineage;
- no generations of descendants;
- no centuries-old territorial administration;
- no inherited army;
- no established treasury comparable to the ancient Counts;
- and only months of experience as a Vampire.
What he possessed instead was overwhelming individual potential, the support of several extraordinarily influential women and Vlad's recognition that Victor was becoming something Vampire society had not produced in millennia.

### Original Role
Clan Alucard was initially one of [[Nightingale]]'s five Vampire Count Clans.
Its official role was exploration and the discovery of new territory, making it different from the older Pillars whose identities had developed around pre-existing lands, populations and military traditions.[1]
The role suited [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] particularly well. Even before his political network became enormous, his life repeatedly pulled him into unknown supernatural territories, races, techniques and civilizations.
The Clan consequently developed around several recurring principles:

Victor's Clan never developed according to a conventional feudal model in which hundreds of ordinary Vampires existed merely to increase the Count's numbers.
Victor repeatedly preferred a smaller group of powerful, trusted and personally connected individuals.

### Bloodline

#### From Political Clan to True Bloodline
When Clan Alucard was created, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was its only true member.
The political title existed before the Bloodline.[2]
This changed when Victor asked [[Scathach]] how Noble Vampires normally created descendants. She explained that conventional Vampire transformation was heavily regulated because direct conversion carried a substantial risk of killing the Human subject.[2]
Ordinary Noble transformation generally required:
- Blood belonging to a Noble Clan leader;
- alchemical or magical ingredients;
- a Witch;
- a transformation circle;
- and a compatible Human candidate.[2]
Victor was an exception.
Because of Blood of the Night King, Victor could directly transform a compatible virgin Human through his bite with a success rate described as effectively complete.[2]
血
THE ALUCARD BLOODLINE
Victor's Clan ceased being only a political title once his own Blood began producing genuine descendants.

#### Bruna
Bruna became one of the first Humans directly transformed by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
After Victor rescued her from a group involved in trafficking captive women, Bruna chose to follow him and was transformed into a Vampire.[5][6]
Bruna immediately demonstrated unusually rapid adaptation to her Vampiric body, providing Victor with some of his earliest practical information about the characteristics of Vampires directly created from his Blood.[7][8]
At this stage, however, service to Victor and direct transformation did not automatically mean the individual had formally become an Alucard.

#### Eve Alucard
Eve Alucard became the decisive turning point.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] encountered Eve after years of severe abuse and offered her freedom together with the option of becoming a Vampire. Eve chose both.[9][3]
Victor renamed her Eve Alucard, made her his personal maid and transformed her through his Blood.[3]
Eve became the first explicitly recognized member of Clan Alucard besides Victor himself.[3]

#### Effects of Victor's Blood
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood did considerably more than create ordinary Vampires.
Early observations showed that individuals repeatedly exposed to it underwent abnormal physical and supernatural development. Kaguya grew physically after consuming Victor's Blood, while Eve's malnourished body rapidly reconstructed itself during her transformation.[3]
Later developments demonstrated far greater effects.
Victor's Progenitor Blood could:
- strengthen an existing Vampire Bloodline;
- awaken or amplify dormant hereditary characteristics;
- integrate multiple Bloodline traits without automatically erasing the original lineage;
- restructure incompatible racial characteristics;
- and create new Vampire subspecies.[10][11][12]
The historical Alucard Bloodline was therefore the developmental ancestor of what eventually became the much more advanced [[Elderblood Bloodline]].

Their relationships with Victor did not initially erase those identities.

The situation changed dramatically after Victor became the Second Progenitor and deliberately began incorporating members of his family into his personal Bloodline.

#### Snow Branch
[[Agnes Elderblood Snow]] and [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] were among the first wives [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] deliberately brought into his personal lineage.[11]
Victor's Blood did not erase the [[Snow Bloodline]]. Instead, it strengthened and incorporated its properties while establishing a deeper ancestral connection to Victor.

#### Fulger Branch
[[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] and Natashia followed.[18]
Their Fulger Lightning and ancestral characteristics were strengthened rather than removed.

#### Scarlett Branch
[[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] underwent the same process.
Her various Bloodline characteristics became increasingly unified within one existence rather than requiring the careful balancing that had previously defined her biology.[19][20]

#### Jeanne and Morgana
[[Jeanne Elderblood]] and [[Morgana Elderblood]] were also incorporated during the same Bloodline expansion.[19]
Morgana demonstrated the most extreme property of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s lineage.
Her Demonic and Vampiric components were assimilated into one stable existence, producing a true Demonic Vampire rather than an unstable hybrid.[12]
This proved that Victor's Bloodline could create entirely new branches rather than merely reproducing conventional Vampires.

### Household
Clan Alucard was family-centered from its earliest period.
The distinction between family, servant and subordinate was often much less rigid inside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household than within conventional aristocratic Clans.

#### Victor
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was the unquestioned Clan Head.
His authority did not derive from an inherited family structure. The Clan existed because he founded it, his Blood formed its lineage and its political position originated from his Count title.

#### Violet
Violet increasingly acted as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s [[Violet Elderblood Snow|First Wife]] in practice, coordinating members of the household and representing Victor when necessary.[21]
Her position inside the family therefore exceeded the authority implied by simply being a foreign Countess' daughter married to the Clan Head.

#### Kaguya
Kaguya was effectively the senior operational maid during the Clan's early growth.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] distinguished Kaguya's practical seniority from Eve's formal significance. Eve carried the Alucard name, but she lacked Kaguya's experience and therefore did not automatically command the household merely because of her surname.[22]
This demonstrates an important principle of Victor's household:
status within the family did not automatically replace competence.

#### Maid Squad
Main article: [[Maid Squad]]
The Maid Squad developed into Clan Alucard's principal internal service, security and specialist organization.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly rejected the idea of accumulating hundreds of mediocre attendants. He preferred a small force of extremely capable women who could function as:
- servants;
- bodyguards;
- assassins;
- intelligence agents;
- battlefield operatives;
- logistical personnel;
- and members of his family.[23]
Kaguya represented the original standard toward which Eve, Bruna and the later maids were expected to develop.

### Political Role

#### A Pillar of Nightingale
Clan Alucard was legally established within the political order created by Vlad.
This made [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] one of the [[Vampire Counts]] and therefore theoretically part of [[Nightingale]]'s existing aristocratic system.
In practice, Victor's relationship with Vlad quickly became unusual.
Victor did not behave like a conventional subordinate aristocrat, and after becoming the Second Vampire Progenitor the political fiction that his authority existed solely because Vlad had made him a Count became increasingly untenable.[16]
The two eventually interacted more as rulers possessing separate power bases than as King and ordinary Count.

#### No Ancient Territory
Unlike the older Count Clans, Clan Alucard did not begin with an established ancestral territory in [[Nightingale]].
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household instead operated through several locations connected to himself and his wives.
His Human residence on Earth became a genuine family home, while the properties and territories of Snow, Fulger and Scarlett remained institutionally connected to their original Clans.
By Chapter 445, Victor and Ruby were still actively discussing the future development of dedicated territory for Clan Alucard.[27]
This distinction is important when cataloguing the Clan's historical bases.
Snow City was not simply "Clan Alucard's city".
Scarlett territory was not simply "Clan Alucard territory".
Hell was not a Clan Alucard estate.
Those locations belonged to other organizations that later became politically connected to Victor.

### Clan Culture

#### Family Over Numbers
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] never showed much interest in building a massive Clan simply for prestige.
His preference was for a relatively small number of exceptional people whom he trusted personally.
This was most clearly expressed through his approach to the [[Maid Squad]], where quality, loyalty and growth mattered far more than possessing hundreds of servants.[23]

#### Protection
The protection of family was one of the Clan's defining principles.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] consistently treated deliberate attacks upon his wives, children, maids or household as attacks upon himself.
[[Scathach]] reinforced this mindset after Kaguya was nearly killed. As the head of his own Clan, Victor was expected to defend what belonged beneath his protection rather than rely on unrelated authorities to punish attackers for him.[24]

#### Greed
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] openly associated Vampiric nature with greed.
When Eve asked whether she could choose both freedom and transformation rather than selecting only one future, Victor approved of the desire to take both when nothing required her to surrender either.[3]
This philosophy repeatedly influenced the Clan's behavior:
- acquire useful knowledge rather than destroy it;
- take enemy resources after victory;
- seek greater strength rather than accepting stagnation;
- pursue desired relationships honestly;
- and exploit opportunities instead of abandoning them unnecessarily.

#### Merit
Clan status alone did not make someone competent.
Eve's formal position as the first Alucard descendant did not place her above Kaguya operationally because Kaguya possessed vastly greater experience.[22]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] consistently valued the ability to perform a role over symbolic rank.

#### Enemies as Resources
The raids against the Human hybrid laboratories helped establish another recurring Alucard practice.
When Violet wanted to destroy an enemy laboratory, Ruby recognized that its information and technology were valuable.
Clan Alucard removed the useful equipment and research before destroying the remainder.[25][26]
The Clan therefore increasingly followed the logic:
DEFEAT
→
ANALYZE
→
TAKE WHAT IS USEFUL
→
DESTROY THE REST
The same philosophy later appeared on vastly larger scales throughout [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Empire.

This meant the Clan was no longer merely "the Fifth Count Clan".
It was the beginning of another root within Vampire evolution.

### Relationship with Nightingale
Clan Alucard originated as a legally recognized Pillar of [[Nightingale]].
Its creation therefore initially placed [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] inside Vlad's political structure.
However, Victor's rapid rise altered this relationship almost immediately.
By the time Victor became the Second Progenitor, Vlad could no longer realistically treat him as simply another Count whose importance existed because of Royal appointment.[16]
Clan Alucard increasingly developed:
- its own Bloodline;
- its own household forces;
- independent allies;
- external racial relationships;
- research and intelligence networks;
- and eventually a political faction whose loyalty centered on Victor rather than the Vampire Crown.
The Clan therefore began as a Pillar inside Nightingale and ended as the dynastic core of a power that had outgrown Nightingale's original Clan system.

### Legacy
Clan Alucard provided several foundations that remained visible long after the name itself ceased to define [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family.

#### The Family Model
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] increasingly reserved direct Clan membership for individuals he personally considered family.[13]
This principle later became even stronger under the Elderblood name.

#### The Bloodline
The Alucard Bloodline demonstrated that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood could assimilate, strengthen and evolve pre-existing racial information.[10][11][12]
The modern [[Elderblood Bloodline]] is the vastly evolved continuation of this process.

#### The Maid Squad
The Clan's Battle Maids remained part of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household long after the Count Clan system ceased to define their place.

#### The Noble Clan Alliance
The political relationships between Alucard, Snow, Fulger, Scarlett, Blank and later Adrasteia became the Vampire foundation of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wider political order.

#### Victor's Faction
Clan Alucard became the dynastic center around which [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Faction formed.
The Faction in turn became one of the principal predecessors of Dragon's Nest.

#### The Elderblood Dynasty
Most importantly, the Clan established the principle that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family was not defined solely by biological descent.
Eve entered through transformation.
[[Nero]] entered through adoption.
The maids entered through service transformed into family.
Victor's wives entered his lineage without losing every inherited characteristic from their original Clans.
That combination of Blood, choice, loyalty and recognition became one of the defining characteristics of the later Elderblood family.

### Trivia
- Alucard is Dracula spelled backwards.[1]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] selected the name only after Vlad required him to establish a supernatural surname for his new Count Clan.[1]
- Clan Alucard was officially recognized as the Fifth Pillar of Vampire Society.[1]
- Its original responsibility was exploration and the discovery of new lands.[1]
- Victor was initially the only actual Alucard despite already possessing the title of Clan Head.[2]
- Eve Alucard was the first individual besides Victor explicitly identified as an actual member of the newly founded Clan.[3]
- Bruna was transformed by Victor before Eve but was not described as the first actual Alucard member.[6][3]
- Victor originally intended the [[Maid Squad]] to remain comparatively small and elite rather than filling his estate with hundreds of ordinary servants.[23]
- Kaguya possessed greater operational authority than Eve despite Eve's formal importance as the first direct Alucard descendant.[22]
- [[Nero]] became an Alucard through Victor accepting her as his daughter rather than through biological descent.[16][17]
- Clan Alucard did not automatically absorb allied races. Victor explicitly rejected making the entire Gorgon Race part of his Clan simply because they pledged themselves to him.[13]
- By Chapter 633, direct Clan membership was increasingly reserved for people Victor regarded as genuine family.[13]
- Victor's Blood could strengthen existing family Bloodlines without automatically erasing them.[11][18][19]
- Morgana's transformation demonstrated that Victor's lineage could assimilate incompatible racial characteristics into a stable Vampire subspecies.[12]
- Snow, Fulger and Scarlett remained distinct Noble Clans even after formally allying themselves with Clan Alucard.[30]
- Clan Alucard and Victor's Faction were never the same organization.
- Later Hell cities, [[Etherea]], Elvenorah and Imperial worlds should not be retroactively listed as historical Clan Alucard territories.
- The modern Elderblood Clan is the successor dynasty and should contain the current Imperial-family information rather than this archival page.
