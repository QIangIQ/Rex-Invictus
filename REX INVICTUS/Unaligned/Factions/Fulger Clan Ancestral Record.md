The Fulger Clan Ancestral Record is a supernatural book of power belonging to the [[Fulger Clan]]. It contains the recorded genealogy and beginning of the Fulger family and serves as the official supernatural registry of the Clan's Main Bloodline. The book was created by [[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]], Natashia's grandmother and one of the foundational figures of the Fulger lineage.[1]
Natashia described the Record as an Artifact of approximately the same level as the ancestral daggers she wielded, referring to [[Taranis]], the Daggers of the Ancestral Spirit. Unlike Taranis, which functions primarily as a Bloodline-bound weapon, the Ancestral Record preserves the Fulger Clan's family history and determines who is formally recognized within its most important genealogical line.[1]
The Artifact normally appears to contain no visible writing. When activated, however, the book illuminates and manifests the names of Clan Fulger's recorded members in genealogical order. It is also capable of being altered by the recognized Clan Leader: Natashia used her authority and Lightning to formally add [[Julia Fulger]] to the Primary Family Bloodline, granting the former maid one of the greatest honors Clan Fulger could bestow.[1]

### Name
The novel has not provided the book with a clearly established unique proper name.
Natashia refers to it descriptively as the book in which:
the family tree of Clan Fulger is written
and explicitly calls it:
a book of power
[1]
The title Fulger Clan Ancestral Record is therefore a descriptive encyclopedia name used to distinguish the Artifact from ordinary genealogical records.
Other descriptive terms used on this page, such as Primary Family Bloodline Records and Clan Fulger Registry, refer to functions or records contained within the same Artifact rather than confirmed separate objects.

### Appearance
The Ancestral Record appears as a magical book.
When Natashia first revealed it to [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]], Lightning gathered around her hand before the book manifested.[1]
At first, the pages appeared:
completely blank.
[1]
Only after Natashia began explaining the Fulger lineage did the Artifact begin glowing and names become visible across its pages.
The book therefore does not appear to function as an ordinary permanently written genealogy.
Instead, its information can remain supernaturally concealed until activated.
The exact:
- cover material;
- dimensions;
- page count;
- age;
- inscriptions;
- and physical construction
have never been described in detail.

### Origin
The Artifact was created by Natashia's grandmother, [[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]].[1]
Natashia describes its purpose simply:
The beginning of our family is recorded in this text.
[1]
Its creation is closely connected to the origins of Clan Fulger itself.
While explaining the Artifact to Sasha, Natashia revealed that her grandmother was originally:
a Lightning Spirit of the highest order.
[1]
Natashia further explained that the Clan's name was not coincidental. In Latin, Fulger means Lightning, and her grandmother represented the very heritage from which the Clan's supernatural Lightning descended.[1]
The Ancestral Record therefore preserves more than names.
It functions as a surviving record of the supernatural origin and inherited identity of Clan Fulger.

### Nature
The Ancestral Record is explicitly described as both:
- a book of power;
- and an Artifact.[1]
Natashia compared its level directly with the ancestral daggers she used.
This establishes that the book is not simply:
- a handwritten history;
- an administrative ledger;
- or a mundane family tree.
It possesses supernatural functions tied to the [[Fulger Clan]] and its Main Bloodline.

### Functions

#### Genealogical Record
The primary function of the Artifact is recording the family tree of Clan Fulger.[1]
Natashia explicitly stated that:
the beginning of our family is recorded in this text.
The Artifact therefore preserves the genealogical development of the Clan from its origins through later descendants.

#### Dynamic Name Manifestation
The book does not permanently display all of its contents.
When Sasha first saw it, nothing appeared written on the pages.[1]
Shortly afterward:
- the book began glowing;
- names appeared;
- and its genealogical structure became visible.[1]
This indicates that its records are stored magically rather than simply through physical ink.

#### Bloodline Organization
The names are displayed according to familial descent.
During Sasha's observation, the Record displayed a succession including:
- Fulger;
- Carmila Fulger;
- Natashia Fulger;
- Victoria Fulger;
- Sasha Fulger;
- and [[Tatsuya Rider]].[1]
The exact layout reflected parent-child relationships.
Sasha appeared beneath Natashia, while Tatsuya appeared beneath Victoria.[1]
The Artifact therefore functions as a true supernatural family tree rather than merely displaying an unordered list of Clan members.

#### Main Bloodline Registry
The Record distinguishes the Primary Family or Main Bloodline of Clan Fulger from individuals who merely possess traces of Fulger Blood.[1]
This distinction is important.
Natashia's family maintained a memorial area restricted specifically to members of the Main Bloodline, with even Clan members carrying remnants of their Bloodline ordinarily forbidden from entering.[1]
Being written into the Ancestral Record's Primary Family Bloodline therefore represents more than recognition as an ordinary Clan member.
It establishes a person's place within the most honored genealogical line of Clan Fulger.

#### Formal Registration
The Artifact can be deliberately updated.
Natashia demonstrated this when she decided to formally recognize [[Julia Fulger]] as a member of the Main Family.[1]
After declaring:
Julia Fulger shall be remembered as a member of the Main Family and will be within the Clan's registry as of today.
Natashia entered her evolved Vampire Count transformation and commanded:
Obey me.
A small bolt of Lightning then descended and struck the book.
Immediately afterward, Julia's name appeared beside Natashia and Victoria within the Primary Family Bloodline records.[1]
This demonstrates that the Registry is not immutable.
A recognized authority within Clan Fulger can add an individual under appropriate circumstances.

### Clan Leader Authority
The Ancestral Record appears to possess a special relationship with the leader of Clan Fulger.
When Sasha expressed surprise that she had never known about such an important Artifact, Natashia answered:
You're not the Clan leader yet.
[1]
This strongly indicates that knowledge, access or administrative authority over the Artifact is traditionally restricted according to Clan leadership.
The exact rule has not been explained.
It remains unknown whether:
- only the Clan Leader can summon the Artifact;
- only the Clan Leader can modify its records;
- an heir can view it with permission;
- the book automatically recognizes the current leader;
- or leadership authority is passed through a ritual.
What is directly demonstrated is that Natashia, as current Clan Leader, could command the Artifact and alter the Main Family Registry.

#### Manifestation
Natashia did not retrieve the book from a shelf or container.
She opened her hand, Lightning formed around it, and the book appeared.[1]
This suggests that the Artifact can be:
- summoned;
- manifested;
- stored supernaturally;
- or called through Fulger Lightning.
The exact mechanism has not been explained.

#### Command Recognition
Natashia's command of:
Obey me.
was immediately followed by the Artifact accepting Julia's registration.[1]
This indicates that the book recognizes some form of authority associated with Natashia.
Whether that authority comes from:
- her status as Clan Leader;
- her Fulger Blood;
- her Lightning;
- her Vampire Count status;
- or a combination of these factors
has not been explicitly established.

### Julia Fulger
The most important demonstrated use of the Ancestral Record was the registration of [[Julia Fulger]].

#### Background
Julia had served Clan Fulger as a maid and became the principal maternal figure in Sasha's childhood while Natashia was unable to properly care for her daughter.[1]
Natashia later came to deeply regret her failures during that period and regarded Julia's treatment of Sasha with profound gratitude.
After rebuilding her life and marrying [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Natashia decided that she could not move forward without properly honoring Julia.[1]

#### Memorial
Natashia created a memorial containing statues of:
- Carmila;
- and Julia.[1]
The location was ordinarily reserved for the Main Bloodline of Clan Fulger.
Sasha initially questioned whether including Julia there violated Clan tradition.
Natashia rejected the objection on the basis that she was the current Clan Leader and therefore possessed the authority to alter those traditions.[1]

#### Highest Clan Honor
Natashia declared that caring for Sasha was sufficient reason to give Julia:
the highest honor that our Clan and I can bestow.
[1]
She then summoned the Ancestral Record and added Julia to the Main Bloodline.
The narration explicitly describes this act as:
an honor of the highest order
for someone who had originally been only a maid.[1]
Julia's registration therefore demonstrates that membership in the Main Bloodline Registry can, under exceptional circumstances, be granted through the authority of Clan leadership rather than being determined only by biological descent.

### Adoption into the Main Family
Julia's inclusion establishes an important property of the Artifact and the Clan system.
The Record does not merely detect biological Bloodline automatically and refuse all exceptions.
The legitimate Clan Leader can apparently use it to formally recognize a non-blood relative as belonging to the Main Family.[1]
This means the artifact records not only:
- biological genealogy;
- but also formally recognized familial identity.
However, the exact supernatural consequences of such registration are unknown.
There is no evidence that being written into the book automatically grants:
- the [[Fulger Bloodline]];
- Lightning abilities;
- [[Taranis]] recognition;
- biological kinship;
- or inherited supernatural traits.
Julia's inclusion is explicitly framed as an act of familial recognition and honor.

### Relationship with Fulger Bloodline
The book is deeply connected to Clan Fulger's identity.
Natashia used its reveal to explain the origin of their name and Lightning inheritance to Sasha.[1]
She stated that her grandmother was a Lightning Spirit of the highest order and tied the word Fulger directly to Lightning.
The Artifact therefore functions as one of the surviving links between:
- Clan Fulger's supernatural origin;
- its Bloodline;
- its founders;
- and its later descendants.
Unlike [[Taranis]], however, the book has not been demonstrated directly enhancing Fulger Lightning.
Its importance is genealogical and symbolic rather than martial.

### Relationship with Clan Tradition
Knowledge of the Ancestral Record appears to be part of information reserved for Clan leadership.
Sasha—the acknowledged Heiress—had never seen or been told about the Artifact before Chapter 473.[1]
Natashia specifically justified this by pointing out that Sasha was:
not the Clan leader yet.
[1]
This suggests that assuming leadership of Clan Fulger involves inheriting knowledge inaccessible even to ordinary members of the Main Family.
The Ancestral Record may therefore be part of a larger body of:
- leadership secrets;
- Clan traditions;
- Bloodline knowledge;
- and inherited artifacts
passed from one Fulger leader to the next.

### Relationship with the Fulger Memorial
The Ancestral Record was revealed inside a hidden Fulger memorial accessible through Natashia's private room.[1]
The memorial contained a statue of Carmila and, by Natashia's decision, a statue honoring Julia.
Natashia described the location as:
a resting place for members of the main house of Clan Fulger.
[1]
Even members carrying remnants of Fulger Blood were normally prohibited from entering.

### Julia's Biography
Following Julia's recognition, Sasha also personally wrote a biography of her life with assistance from [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]].[1]
The biography is separate from the Ancestral Record.
It preserves:
- Julia's life;
- teachings;
- service;
- and influence upon [[Fulger Clan]] maids.
Her teachings were intended to become part of future Fulger maid training.[1]
The Ancestral Record therefore records Julia's familial status, while the biography preserves the details of her individual life.
The two should not be treated as the same object.

### Historical Importance
The Ancestral Record is one of the clearest surviving repositories of early [[Fulger Clan]] history.
Natashia explicitly states that:
the beginning of our family is recorded in this text.
[1]
This makes the Artifact potentially one of the oldest surviving internal historical sources belonging to the Clan.
However, only a small portion of its records has been directly shown.
The article should therefore not invent:
- the complete Fulger genealogy;
- unknown Clan leaders;
- dates;
- marriages;
- wars;
- or Bloodline changes
that have not appeared in the story.

### Strategic Importance
Unlike [[Taranis]], the Ancestral Record has no demonstrated battlefield function.
Its significance lies in:
- preserving the legitimacy of the Main Bloodline;
- recording succession;
- maintaining Clan identity;
- preserving historical genealogy;
- and allowing Clan leadership to formally recognize exceptional individuals.
Because Clan Fulger historically served as one of [[Nightingale]]'s great Vampire Count families, maintaining an authoritative record of its primary lineage would also have political significance.
The story has not demonstrated the book being used in a legal dispute or succession crisis, however.

### Limitations

#### No Demonstrated Combat Abilities
Despite being called a book of power and compared to [[Taranis]] in artifact level, the Record has never demonstrated an offensive or defensive combat ability.[1]
It should not be assigned Lightning attacks simply because it belongs to Clan Fulger.

#### Unknown Bloodline Effects
Being entered into the book does not demonstrably grant [[Fulger Bloodline]] powers.
Julia's registration was an honor and recognition of familial status.

#### Unknown Modification Requirements
Natashia successfully altered the registry, but the exact requirements remain unclear.
It is unknown whether another Clan Leader without Natashia's level of power could perform the same action as easily.

#### Unknown Security Mechanisms
The story does not establish what happens if:
- an outsider steals the Artifact;
- a non-Fulger attempts to open it;
- someone tries to erase a recorded name;
- or an unauthorized person attempts to alter the genealogy.

#### Unknown Full Contents
Only part of the genealogy was displayed on-page.
The statement that the beginning of the family is recorded within suggests substantially more information may exist than what Sasha saw.[1]
