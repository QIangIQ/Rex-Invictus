Clan Scarlett, commonly known as the Scarlett Clan, is the ancient noble house founded and historically ruled by Scathach Elderblood Scarlett. It was one of the original four great Vampire Count houses of [[Nightingale]] and was responsible for warfare, military response and counteroffensive operations. Where Clan Adrasteia acted as the King's Shield, Clan Scarlett served as the King's [[Spears|Spear]]: during a major invasion or war, [[Scathach]] was expected to assume the role of general and destroy the enemy.[1]
Clan Scarlett was radically different from the other Count families. Scathach refused to create a conventional military or surround herself with thousands of subordinates, considering the time required to train mediocre Vampires an unacceptable waste. Her territory consequently revolved around an exceptionally small family and household rather than a large hereditary aristocracy.[1] This same structure unintentionally made the Clan extraordinarily resistant to infiltration; there were almost no ordinary servants, soldiers or lesser nobles among whom a spy could disappear.[1]
The Clan's political form eventually ceased to exist when Scathach effectively abandoned the administration of her old territory and the Noble Vampires living there were largely absorbed into the Snow and Fulger territories.[2] The Scarlett family itself was never erased. Scathach, Ruby, Siena, Lacus and Pepper entered [[Victor Elderblood]]'s family, became integral members of the Elderblood Clan, and later evolved beyond ordinary Noble Vampires into the Draconic lineage surrounding Victor.[3][4][5][6][7]

### Overview
Clan Scarlett was never an ordinary Vampire Clan. At its height as a Count house, its military strength was concentrated almost completely into one woman and the daughters she personally raised. [[Scathach]]'s philosophy rejected quantity in favor of exceptional individual quality, creating a house that possessed very little conventional infrastructure but produced some of [[Nightingale]]'s most dangerous combatants.
The Clan historically controlled territory in northern Nightingale, but Scathach showed little interest in the conventional duties associated with feudal government. She did not maintain a capital comparable to the Snow or Fulger domains, disliked political administration and generally considered her personal strength sufficient to satisfy the military obligations Vlad had assigned her.[1]
Her mansion consequently functioned more as a family residence, fortress and training ground than the seat of a conventional state. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] first arrived there, the household was conspicuously empty despite its enormous size. The principal residents were Scathach, Siena, Lacus, Pepper, Ruby and Ruby's maid Luna.[8]
The Clan's small size concealed an unusually complicated family structure. Siena, Lacus and Pepper were not Scathach's biological children; all three were adopted and ultimately incorporated into her Blood through Vampire ritual. Ruby was originally Scathach's only biological daughter.[9] In practice, Scathach never considered the distinction important. Once accepted into her Blood and household, they were her daughters.
That philosophy survived long after the political Count house disappeared. The Scarlett identity is now best understood as a family and dynastic branch within Clan Elderblood, preserving Scathach's martial tradition, Bloodline heritage and descendants rather than governing an independent Vampire state.

### Territory
❄ Northern [[Nightingale]]
Cold forests, isolated settlements and a private estate built around the needs of [[Scathach]]'s family rather than the requirements of a traditional capital.
Clan Scarlett's historical territory occupied the northern region of Nightingale, where the environment was cooler and heavily forested. Like Clan Adrasteia's lands, the area was comparatively isolated from Nightingale's denser centers of civilization.[1]
Scathach deliberately avoided constructing or personally administering a major capital. Her reasoning was practical from her own perspective: she had no interest in accumulating citizens and soldiers merely to imitate the structure of other Count houses. The Countess responsible for war believed she herself was sufficient to answer most military emergencies.[1]
This left the territory with an unusual division between land theoretically belonging to the Count house and the very small family organization actually surrounding Scathach. There were Noble Vampires living throughout the greater territory even though they were not all intimate members of Scathach's household. When Clan Scarlett later abandoned its territorial rule, many of these Nobles were absorbed into Clan Snow and Clan Fulger rather than accompanying Scathach as a conventional population migration.[2]

### History

#### Foundation and the King's Spear
FOUNDATION ERAOne warrior became an entire military institution.
[[Scathach]] was born during an age dominated by warfare and spent thousands of years pursuing combat and self-improvement. Her extraordinary military ability eventually brought her into the circle surrounding [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]], who recognized her as one of the original [[Vampire Counts]] and placed responsibility for war upon the house she founded.[1]
The four original Count houses were not interchangeable noble titles. Each served a strategic purpose inside [[Nightingale]]. Adrasteia protected the kingdom as its Shield, while Scarlett represented the corresponding offensive function. If Nightingale suffered an invasion or required a major counterattack, Scathach was expected to become its general.[1]
The title did not turn her into an obedient bureaucrat. Scathach remained highly independent and never developed the temperament expected from an administrator. Her usefulness to Vlad came from military capability rather than enthusiasm for royal procedure.
Most importantly, she rejected the assumption that a Count responsible for warfare required a conventional army. Scathach believed training untalented soldiers consumed time that could be spent improving herself or genuinely promising disciples. As a result, the Clan responsible for Nightingale's wars possessed virtually no standing military institution of its own.[1]
The Scarlett Paradox: the Count Clan officially responsible for war possessed one of the smallest organizations in Nightingale, yet remained one of the kingdom's most dangerous military assets because Scathach herself could perform the role normally assigned to an army.

#### Creating the Scarlett Family
[[Scathach]] gradually built a family around herself rather than a conventional aristocratic dynasty.
[[Siena Scarlett Elderblood]] became her eldest adopted daughter and developed into the person most capable of handling the responsibilities Scathach disliked. Siena eventually managed much of the territory's ordinary political and administrative work and functioned as Scathach's practical right hand.[8]
[[Lacus Scarlett Elderblood]] was an exiled princess of another Noble Vampire Clan. She had been rejected as someone supposedly lacking the talent expected from her original lineage, survived the disappearance of that house and was eventually adopted and trained by Scathach. Her personal abilities later developed around mist and extreme mobility.
[[Pepper Scarlett Elderblood]] came from another powerful Noble Vampire lineage. Her original Clan possessed exceptionally potent Water abilities; Pepper later explained that sufficiently capable members could create tidal waves capable of destroying countries.[10] Extremist [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] infiltrated [[Nightingale]] and devastated the Clan when Pepper was young. Scathach arrived after much of the destruction had already occurred, exterminated the attackers and rescued Pepper together with several survivors.[10]
The remnants of Pepper's original Clan did not all become Scarletts. Some remained under Scathach's protection while living comparatively peaceful lives in her territory. One surviving member later became Ruby's biological father, directly connecting Ruby's Water affinity to the lineage from which Pepper herself originated.[10]
Scathach eventually decided she wanted a biological daughter. She selected a Vampire whose Bloodline complemented her own and produced [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]], who became the recognized heiress of Clan Scarlett. Ruby inherited enormous affinity for both Ice and Water, creating a particularly complete expression of the powers surrounding the house.
Blood mattered — but acceptance mattered more.
Siena, Lacus and Pepper were later explicitly described by Scathach as daughters she had adopted into her Blood through Ritual, not merely foster children living beneath her roof.[9] The resulting family was therefore built through both birth and deliberate supernatural adoption.

#### Victor Elderblood and the Scarlett Household
The Clan became central to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s development almost immediately after his transformation into a Vampire.
Ruby was one of the three women whose Blood participated in the ritual that created Victor's unusual Noble Vampire existence. Through her, Victor inherited access to the Scarlett side of his elemental foundation, including Ice and Water-related characteristics.[11][12][13]
When Ruby was injured during the early conflict involving the Inquisition, [[Scathach]] personally retaliated. The incident reinforced one of the most consistent rules surrounding the Scarlett family: attacking one daughter meant attracting the attention of the matriarch herself.[14][15]
Scathach soon encountered Victor directly and recognized the extraordinary potential created by his blood, instincts and abnormal rate of adaptation. She took him into [[Nightingale]] and began the brutal training that would become the foundation of Victor's later martial development.[16][17]
Victor's first stay at the Scarlett estate also brought him into regular contact with Lacus, Pepper and Siena.[8] The house that initially represented his wife's intimidating maternal family gradually became one of the most important training environments of his early supernatural life.
After Victor later returned to the Human World, Scathach brought Siena, Lacus and Pepper with her and effectively moved much of the tiny Scarlett household into the underground complex beneath the Walker residence.[18][19]
There Victor fought all four Scarlett sisters as a coordinated team. The experience showed that their family was not simply a collection of interchangeable Ice users. Each sister had been trained to exploit a different foundation and fight in a way that complemented the others.[20][21]

#### Training and Independent Growth
As [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s influence over the family expanded, he began identifying weaknesses in the Scarlett sisters' development.
Lacus and Pepper in particular had stagnated because their lives contained too few opponents capable of forcing them to improve. Victor began personally training them and later involved Siena as well.[23]
The objective was not to erase [[Scathach]]'s teaching. Victor instead pushed them to move beyond overdependence on the specialization they had originally received. Speed without endurance, raw Strength without refinement, Power without control and defensive ability without adaptability could each become exploitable weaknesses against stronger enemies.
Scathach later taught Victor, Ruby, Siena, Lacus and Pepper the principles of Runes, further expanding the family's education beyond elemental combat and martial arts.[24] By this period the Scarlett household had become much more than an ancient Vampire fighting family: its members were increasingly learning systems that would later become foundational within the Elderblood civilization.

#### The Demonic War
CHAPTERS 523–524The daughters defended the [[Spears|Spear]] without [[Scathach]].
The Demonic War provided one of the clearest demonstrations of how far Scathach's daughters had progressed. Demonic portals opened throughout [[Nightingale]], including the Scarlett territory, while many of the most powerful leaders were occupied elsewhere.[25][26]
The attacking force assigned to Clan Scarlett was commanded by [[Zagan]], the 61st Key of Solomon.[26]
Siena was already handling the Clan's administrative affairs when the invasion began and immediately organized the response. Ruby returned to defend her home, joining Siena, Pepper and Lacus rather than assuming Scathach would eventually solve the problem for them.[26]
The sisters turned the battlefield itself into their weapon. Their different applications of Water, Ice, mist and environmental control were combined to create conditions where the entire group could operate as one system. What one sister produced, another could amplify or weaponize.

#### The End of the Independent Count House
Clan Scarlett's transformation from political state into family house became explicit by Chapter 730.
[[Scathach]] had effectively abandoned conventional rule over the Scarlett territory. The Snow and Fulger Clans subsequently absorbed most of the Noble Vampires who had lived within the former Scarlett domain, dramatically strengthening both neighboring Count houses.[2]
At the same time, the remaining Scarlett family did not disappear. Clans Scarlett, Snow and Fulger formally declared their alliance with Clan Alucard, making public a political reality that [[Nightingale]]'s upper class had already largely understood.[2]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s marriages transformed that alliance into blood and family. Ruby, Siena, Lacus and Pepper were publicly included among the principal Scarlett women entering Victor's household, while the Snow and Fulger lines underwent similar integration.[2]
Because a Vampire Progenitor represented the purest level of Vampiric Blood, Clans directly intermarrying with Victor began to be treated socially like a form of royalty within Vampire society.[2]
Important distinction: Clan Scarlett was not simply "merged into Snow and Fulger." Its territorial government ended and the Nobles residing in the old domain were largely absorbed by Snow and Fulger. The Scarlett family itself continued separately through Scathach, her daughters and their descendants inside the Elderblood dynasty.[2]

#### The Red Dragon Ancestor
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became the progenitor of a new Draconic lineage, [[Scathach]] became one of the first members of his family to undergo a complete transformation.
In Chapter 818, Scathach's existing characteristics—her affinities, martial talent, experience and individual identity—were preserved while her existence was rebuilt through a genuine Draconic foundation.[3]
The transformation made Scathach a Dragon Ancestor and established the foundation associated with the family's Red Dragon branch.[3]
This represented a fundamental break with Clan Scarlett's original biology. The woman who had founded the house as a Noble Vampire was no longer merely a stronger Vampire Count; she had become the ancestor of a Draconic lineage.

#### The Scarlett Sisters Become Dragons
Ruby, Siena, Lacus and Pepper later followed during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s mass Dragonization of his family.[4][5][6][7]
The transformation did not erase the abilities or identities they possessed as Scarletts. Victor's method incorporated existing traits into each woman's new Draconic foundation, producing distinct Dragons rather than standardized copies.[7]
Their former Noble Vampire classification therefore ceased to describe their current existence completely. They remained Scarletts by family and historical Bloodline, but their present bodies, Souls and potential belonged to the True Dragon civilization Victor was creating.

The modern Scarlett branch preserves the culture and martial identity of the old Count Clan while its principal members have biologically and spiritually evolved beyond the Race that originally founded it.

#### The Imperial Era
The rise of the [[Elderblood Empire]] transformed the scale at which members of the former Clan operated.
[[Scathach]] expanded her ancient role as a martial instructor into an Imperial institution. During the Empire's accelerated development she maintained an entire training world containing fortifications, arenas, logistical infrastructure and approximately one hundred million recruits.[29] What had once been a training philosophy applied to a handful of daughters and disciples had become part of the martial foundation of a Galactic civilization.
Ruby became one of the leading minds of the Empire's Fourth Legion, [[Stellar Arsenal]], working alongside [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]] and [[Aline Elderblood Valefar]] on scientific, magical and technological systems designed for threats operating far beyond the scale of Earth's old supernatural conflicts.[30]
Pepper developed into a far more dangerous researcher than her formerly carefree behavior suggested. By Chapter 1159 she was participating in the development of classified Imperial weapons such as Polaris, with Scathach becoming involved partly to ensure Pepper's creativity remained under supervision.[31]
Pepper's own private research went even further. Her isolated laboratory contained an artificial star surrounded by scientific equipment and thousands of Runes. The project, casually titled "Creating a supernova in the palm of my hand", had already reached a stage where Pepper intended to continue feeding the star until the resulting explosion could reach a projected output comparable to roughly one billion exploding suns.[32][33]
This Imperial development illustrates how far the old Scarlett family had evolved. The same house once famous because one woman refused to maintain an army eventually produced generals, scientists, Dragon Goddesses, Imperial researchers and an entire second generation of princesses.

#### Matriarchal Government
Like the other ancient Count houses, Clan Scarlett historically existed within [[Nightingale]]'s strongly matriarchal noble structure. [[Scathach]] was founder, Countess and permanent head of the Clan for its entire existence as an independent political house.[1]
The surrounding laws and social customs tended to favor women more strongly than comparable Human governments, a characteristic shared by several of Nightingale's female-led Count territories.[1]
Even so, Clan Scarlett was unusual because its matriarchal structure was not simply political tradition. The Clan genuinely consisted almost entirely of Scathach and women she regarded as daughters.

### Responsibilities
⚔ THE KING'S SPEAR
Mission: destroy external threats to [[Nightingale]] and lead offensive military operations.[1]
Historically, Clan Scarlett was responsible for:
- War: responding when Nightingale entered major armed conflict.
- Counteroffensive Operations: attacking forces that had breached or threatened the kingdom.
- Generalship: [[Scathach]] was expected to assume command during a major invasion.[1]
- Elite Combat: the Clan's strategic value came from exceptional individual warriors rather than numerical forces.
- Training: although Scathach refused to train ordinary soldiers, she devoted extraordinary attention to disciples and family members whom she considered genuinely talented.
The old responsibility ended when the Scarlett territory ceased operating as an independent Count domain. Its martial legacy did not. Scathach's role eventually expanded from Nightingale's general into one of the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s most important instructors and warriors.[29]

### Bloodline

#### Scarlett Ice
The traditional Scarlett identity is strongly associated with Ice Manipulation. Members can generate, shape and control enormous quantities of Ice, create weapons and structures, alter the battlefield and combine Cryokinesis with martial combat.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] inherited Scarlett Ice through Ruby during his transformation and spent a significant portion of his early Vampire training learning to treat the element as a natural part of himself rather than an external ability.[34][35][36][37]

#### Water
Water is equally important to understanding the family.
Pepper originated from a destroyed Noble Vampire Clan whose most capable members possessed Water abilities strong enough to generate devastating tidal waves.[10] One survivor of that Clan later became Ruby's biological father, meaning Ruby inherited [[Scathach]]'s lineage together with the Water-related potential of Pepper's ancestral family.[10]
Ruby later demonstrated exceptionally precise control over Water, including the ability to use it as part of the process through which her own body could be transformed into Ice and restored safely.[22]
Pepper retained particularly strong Water manipulation herself, while the sisters' cooperation during the Demonic War demonstrated how Water could be converted into battlefield-scale Ice and environmental control.[26]

#### Inherited and Adopted Blood
Clan Scarlett's Bloodline is unusual because three of [[Scathach]]'s first four daughters entered the house through adoption rather than biological descent.
Siena, Lacus and Pepper retained aspects of their original Bloodlines even after becoming Scathach's daughters. This explains why the family developed such dramatically different expressions:
- Lacus retained an innate mist ability that complemented her Speed specialization.[22]
- Pepper retained the powerful Water affinity inherited from her original Clan.[10]
- Siena developed toward Power and large-scale Ice manipulation.
- Ruby biologically united Scathach's line with a survivor of Pepper's original Water lineage.[10]
The later Blood Ritual connecting the adopted daughters to Scathach further blurred the distinction between biological descent and supernatural kinship.[9]

### Martial Tradition
The true inheritance of Clan Scarlett was never merely Ice. [[Scathach]]'s most important legacy was a method of thinking about combat: build the foundations, eliminate weakness, seek Control and never allow power to replace skill.
Scathach spent approximately two thousand years continuously improving herself rather than settling into the complacency common among ancient supernatural beings.[22]
Although capable of using numerous martial disciplines and weapons, her principal traditional weapon style is Sojutsu, Japanese spearmanship.[22] Her broader combat expertise eventually became so extensive that different portions could be given to separate daughters without exhausting the system.
The four foundational principles taught to her original daughters were:
1. Strength — developed most heavily in Pepper.
2. Speed — developed most heavily in Lacus.
3. Resistance — developed most heavily in Ruby.
4. Power — developed most heavily in Siena.[22]
These foundations were intended to correct weaknesses and create balance. Scathach did not believe the correct training method was simply to amplify whatever someone already did best.
A deeper principle eventually became increasingly important throughout both [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s and the Scarlett sisters' training: Control. Enormous quantities of power were meaningless if the wielder could not apply precisely the amount required, adapt to different opponents or transition naturally between abilities.

### Draconic Evolution

#### Scathach — Red Dragon Ancestor
[[Scathach]]'s transformation in Chapter 818 preserved the martial identity that defined her while rebuilding her existence through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Draconic lineage.[3]
Her evolution established her as an ancestor of the branch later associated with Red Dragons.

#### Ruby, Siena, Lacus and Pepper
The four sisters underwent their own complete Dragonization during Chapters 917–920.[4][5][6][7]
The process preserved individual characteristics rather than replacing the sisters with generic Dragons. Their old Bloodline abilities and accumulated training became foundations upon which the new Draconic traits were built.
Consequently, describing the modern Scarlett women simply as Noble Vampires is obsolete. Their history is Vampiric; their present existence is Draconic.

### Affiliations

#### Nightingale
Clan Scarlett began as one of the principal military houses of [[Nightingale]]. Its original legitimacy and responsibilities were defined through [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] and the Count system.[1]
That relationship is now historical rather than the Clan's primary identity.

#### Snow Clan and Fulger Clan
The Scarlett, Snow and Fulger families possessed close personal connections long before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction became dominant. Ruby, Violet and Sasha were childhood friends and later became Victor's original three wives.
After Scarlett abandoned its old territorial government, Snow and Fulger absorbed much of the Noble population formerly living in Scarlett's lands.[2]
This did not erase the Scarlett bloodline or household.

#### Elderblood Clan
The modern Scarlett family is deeply incorporated into the Elderblood Clan. [[Scathach]], Ruby, Siena, Lacus and Pepper all became members of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family, while the next generation consists of Elderblood princesses descended directly from Scarlett women.
Clan Scarlett therefore survives today primarily as a house within a house—an identifiable family branch inside the much larger Elderblood dynasty.

#### Dragons Nest Pantheon
As [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s coalition transformed from an alliance into a new supernatural civilization, the Scarlett family became part of the [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]] and ceased functioning as a purely Vampiric political entity.

#### Elderblood Empire
By the Imperial era, individual Scarlett women occupied positions reaching far beyond the scale of their original [[Nightingale]] responsibilities. [[Scathach]] trained planetary-scale armies, Ruby worked inside the Empire's weapons research structure and Pepper participated in classified projects capable of producing astronomical-scale destruction.[30][29][31][33]

### Current Status
ACTIVE DYNASTIC HOUSE
The Count Clan ended. The Scarlett family did not.
Clan Scarlett should no longer be classified simply as Disbanded.
Its independent territorial government is defunct. [[Scathach]] abandoned normal administration, its former resident Nobles were largely incorporated into Snow and Fulger territory, and the original Count system ceased to define the family.[2]
However:
- Scathach remains alive and continues to act as the family's matriarch.
- Ruby, Siena, Lacus and Pepper remain identifiable members of the Scarlett family.
- All principal Scarlett women entered the Elderblood family.
- The house now possesses multiple direct Elderblood descendants.
- The family's martial tradition continues through Scathach's Imperial training.
- Its original Vampiric lineage has evolved into several Draconic branches.
The most accurate modern description is therefore:
Former Vampire Count Clan • Current Elderblood Dynastic House

### Legacy
Clan Scarlett's historical importance extends far beyond the territory it once governed.
It helped establish [[Nightingale]]'s original military structure as the King's [[Spears|Spear]].[1] [[Scathach]]'s training created much of the martial foundation upon which [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later built his combat style. Ruby's Blood became one of the three principal Vampire lineages involved in Victor's original awakening. Siena, Lacus, Pepper and Ruby later demonstrated that Scathach's teachings could produce radically different warriors rather than copies of their mother.
The Clan's transformation also mirrors the broader transformation of the setting. It began as an isolated Noble Vampire house whose military doctrine revolved around one terrifying Countess. It later abandoned territory, joined a Progenitor's family, evolved into [[True Dragons]] and became part of a Galactic civilization where its founder trained armies on an entire planet and her daughters helped build weapons intended for wars between cosmic powers.
Clan Scarlett's greatest inheritance was never its land. Its enduring legacy is Scathach's family, her training philosophy and the expectation that anyone carrying the Scarlett name must continue improving regardless of how powerful they have already become.

### Trivia
- Clan Scarlett was historically called the King's [[Spears|Spear]], while Clan Adrasteia served as the King's Shield.[1]
- Despite being [[Nightingale]]'s Clan responsible for war, [[Scathach]] refused to maintain a conventional Vampire army because she believed the available candidates lacked enough talent to justify her time.[1]
- The Clan's tiny membership made conventional espionage unusually difficult because outsiders had almost no ordinary subordinate population into which they could infiltrate.[1]
- Scathach was never replaced as Clan leader during its existence as an independent Count house.
- Ruby was the formal heiress, but Siena performed much of the territory's everyday administration.
- Siena, Lacus and Pepper were incorporated into Scathach's Blood through supernatural adoption rather than simple legal adoption.[9]
- Pepper's original Clan possessed powerful Water abilities, and one of its surviving members eventually became Ruby's father.[10]
- Ruby was therefore biologically connected to both Scathach and the Water lineage from which Pepper originated.[10]
- Scathach's martial system for her daughters centered around four foundations: Strength, Speed, Resistance and Power.[22]
- Pepper was assigned Strength, Lacus Speed, Ruby Resistance and Siena Power.[22]
- Scathach's preferred principal weapon art was Sojutsu despite her mastery of numerous martial disciplines.[22]
- The sisters' different specialties were intentionally complementary and became particularly effective when they fought together.[20][26]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] learned enough from fighting the Scarlett sisters to recognize that his early combat instincts were better suited to individual opponents than coordinated groups.[21]
- Clan Scarlett effectively relinquished territorial rule by Chapter 730, with Snow and Fulger absorbing most Nobles living in its former territory.[2]
- The Scarlett, Snow and Fulger Clans formally declared an alliance with Clan Alucard at the same time.[2]
- Scathach became a genuine Dragon Ancestor in Chapter 818, establishing a Draconic branch associated with Red Dragons.[3]
- Ruby, Siena, Lacus and Pepper later became genuine Dragons during Victor's mass Dragonization.[4][5][6][7]
- [[Siren Elderblood]] is Victor and Scathach's daughter and was named after the identity associated with Scathach's earlier life.[38]
- [[Rosemary Lavina Elderblood]], [[Priscillia Penny Elderblood]], [[Sylvia Elderblood]] and [[Lucrecia Lena Elderblood]] continue the Scarlett branch through Ruby, Pepper, Siena and Lacus respectively.[32][40]
- Pepper's later Imperial research included an artificial-star project intended to culminate in a controlled supernova with a projected output of roughly one billion exploding suns.[33]
- Even thousands of years into the Imperial era, Scathach remained obsessed with training and continued subjecting younger Elderbloods—including Siren—to increasingly extreme combat conditioning.[39]
