## MTWABV Canon

### Kuroyami Clan
The Kuroyami Clan is a secretive and ancient organization of vampire assassins that has existed in the shadows of history, shaping events through precision, deception, and calculated bloodshed. Their legacy is woven into Japan’s darkest conflicts, serving as unseen executioners for those who seek power and dominance. Unlike warrior clans that engage in open conflict, the Kuroyami Clan’s strength lies in its ability to eliminate threats before they become problems, ensuring their influence remains absolute while their presence remains unknown.[1]
They were later became a subserviant clan under the [[Snow Clan]] and renamed to [[Blank Clan]].[2]

### Origins and History
The Kuroyami Clan’s origins date back to feudal Japan, where they initially served as silent enforcers for powerful warlords, ensuring political stability through assassinations and sabotage. Their skills in stealth, infiltration, and blood magic made them invaluable assets in the turbulent era of warring states. As the supernatural world intertwined with mortal affairs, the Kuroyami adapted, becoming one of the most feared vampire assassin clans, capable of eliminating both human and non-human targets with the same ruthless efficiency.
Their most notable historical involvement was during [[Oda Nobunaga]]’s campaign for unification, where they played a pivotal role in eliminating his enemies, allowing his forces to march unopposed. However, true to their pragmatic nature, they saw the tides shifting and sided with [[Akechi Mitsuhide]], [[Toyotomi Hideyoshi]], and [[Kurama]] during the Honnō-ji Incident, ensuring Nobunaga’s demise by executing his remaining loyalists before they could retaliate. This betrayal solidified their reputation as an organization that served power, not individuals.

### Structure and Organization
The Kuroyami Clan is not a single family but a network of highly trained vampire assassins, bound by strict rules and absolute discipline. They operate as a mercenary guild, taking contracts from various supernatural factions, noble houses, and even gods who require their services. Their ranks are structured as follows:
- The Grandmaster – The unseen leader of the clan, a figure shrouded in myth, known only to the highest-ranked assassins.
- The [[Elders]] – A council of ancient vampires who dictate the clan’s long-term goals and enforce the traditions that have kept them in power for centuries.
- The Handlers – Strategists who manage contracts, assigning missions to assassins based on skill and risk levels.
- The Executioners – The elite assassins of the clan, specializing in different forms of silent death, including poison, swordsmanship, and blood magic.
- The Shadows – The lowest-ranked operatives, still undergoing training and proving their worth before being granted full status as assassins.

### Abilities and Combat Style
The Kuroyami Clan combines vampiric abilities with specialized assassination techniques, making them one of the most dangerous forces in the supernatural underworld. Unlike warriors who rely on brute strength, they eliminate targets before a fight even begins.
- Blood [[Magic]] Mastery – By using the blood of their victims, they can curse, track, and manipulate their targets from a distance.
- Shadow Concealment – A unique ability that allows them to blend into darkness, rendering them undetectable even by beings with heightened senses.
- Curse Engravings – Assassins inscribe runes onto their weapons and bodies, granting them enhanced speed, silent movement, and resistance to detection magic.
- Stealth and Infiltration – Their training in silent movement allows them to bypass even the most sophisticated defenses, entering and exiting enemy territory unseen.

### Philosophy and Code
The Kuroyami Clan follows a code of absolute discretion, believing that a true assassin is one who never leaves a trace. Contracts are honored without question, and those who betray the clan are hunted down and erased from existence. They do not kill out of personal grudges or emotion—only for profit, influence, or the greater interests of the clan.
Unlike warrior factions that value honor in combat, the Kuroyami believe that victory is the only honor that matters. They despise arrogance and recklessness, seeing open battle as a failure in planning. A mission that ends without a single trace of their involvement is considered their greatest success.

### Legacy and Modern Presence
Despite centuries of bloodshed, the Kuroyami Clan remains active in the modern supernatural world, though their numbers are fewer than in the past. They continue to take contracts from powerful Vampire houses, Gods, and ancient entities, ensuring that their influence remains strong. However, with the rise of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] Alucard and the shifting power dynamics in the supernatural world, the clan finds itself at a crossroads—either adapt or face irrelevance.
They are no longer the only masters of the night, and for the first time in centuries, the shadows they once ruled are no longer safe.
