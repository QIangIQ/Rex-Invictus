## MTWABV Canon

The Snow Bloodline is the hereditary Noble Vampire Bloodline of the [[Snow Clan]], distinguished primarily by Sunlight Immunity, exceptional affinity with Fire and an unusual relationship between supernatural power and emotional intensity. Unlike many ancient Vampire Bloodlines whose modern abilities closely resemble those of their founders, the Snow Bloodline is explicitly known to have been strengthened across successive generations through deliberate training.[1]
The original lineage created by Violet Snow's great-great-grandfather possessed only minor Fire manipulation together with immunity to sunlight. The founder systematically developed that ability and transmitted his improved Bloodline to his descendants, who repeated the process generation after generation until the comparatively weak ancestral power evolved into the devastating [[Pyrokinesis]] demonstrated by modern members such as [[Agnes Elderblood Snow]] and Violet.[1]
Because of this history, the Snow Bloodline is one of the clearest examples in the series that hereditary supernatural abilities are not permanently fixed: a Bloodline can be trained, refined and strengthened until its descendants inherit a superior starting point.

### Origin
The Snow Bloodline was established by the great-great-grandfather of [[Violet Elderblood Snow]], who was both the creator and First Leader of the [[Snow Clan]].[1]
His original Bloodline was comparatively unimpressive offensively.
It possessed two principal characteristics:
- Immunity to Sunlight;
- and minor control over Fire.[1]
Sunlight Immunity was extraordinarily valuable for a Vampire, but [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly described the original Fire ability as weak enough that, without further development, the Bloodline was not particularly impressive in terms of combat power.[1]
The founder refused to leave the ability in that condition.
He continuously trained and developed his inherited Fire, forcing the Bloodline beyond the level at which it originally manifested.
The improvement became hereditary.
His descendants inherited a stronger version of the ability and repeated the same process.
Over successive generations, accumulated development transformed the lineage into the modern Snow Bloodline.[1]

### Bloodline Evolution
The development of the Snow Bloodline demonstrates an important supernatural principle:
Training can permanently improve a hereditary Bloodline.
The improvement is not restricted to the individual who performs the training.
When the user successfully develops the Bloodline itself rather than merely becoming more skilled at using it, later descendants can inherit the improved version.[1]
The sequence can therefore be represented as:
Weak Sunlight/Fire Bloodline → Founder develops it → Improved inheritance → descendants develop it further → modern Snow [[Pyrokinesis]].
By the era of Agnes and Violet, the family's Fire had become powerful enough to represent one of the signature Count-class elemental abilities of [[Nightingale]].

### Core Powers and Abilities
- Fire Manipulation ([[Pyrokinesis]]): The fundamental offensive ability of the modern Snow Bloodline. Users can create, shape, move and control supernatural Fire.[2][3]
- Fire Generation: Users generate Fire directly from supernatural Energy without requiring an external flame source.
- Fire Attacks: Snow Fire can be projected offensively as blasts, waves, spheres and large-scale eruptions.
- Fireball Projection: Concentrated Fire can be formed into spheres and launched or detonated. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] has created Fire masses sufficiently large and bright to be compared visually to miniature suns.[4][5]
- Fire Wave Emission: Flames can be released across broad areas rather than concentrated into individual projectiles.
- Fire Aura: High-level users can surround their bodies in Fire, causing nearby objects and weaker targets to be exposed constantly to extreme heat.
- Fire Body: The Snow Count Form can cover essentially the user's entire body in supernatural flames.[6]
- Fire Infusion: Fire can be channeled through weapons and physical attacks, allowing the user's martial techniques to carry extreme heat and incendiary effects.
- Heat Generation: Snow Fire can raise environmental temperatures dramatically even before an attack is released.
- Heat Manipulation: Skilled users can regulate the temperature and concentration of their Fire instead of merely increasing its quantity.
- Flame Temperature Manipulation: Victor's training allowed him to vary his Fire through increasingly hot states, including ordinary red/orange flames, blue flames and eventually extremely hot white flames.[7]
- Blue Fire Generation: Highly compressed Snow Fire can reach a blue state considerably hotter and more destructive than ordinary flames. Victor compressed an enormous quantity of Fire into a basketball-sized blue sphere while using the Snow Count Form.[8]
- White Fire Generation: Further refinement allowed Victor to create still hotter white Fire.[9] This is an advanced development and should not automatically be attributed to every Snow carrier.
- Large-Scale Incineration: Advanced users can completely burn large structures, settlements or battlefields rather than merely injure individual targets. Victor's compressed blue flame destroyed the structures of an entire Monster-occupied city and left the region heavily devastated.[10]

### Defensive and Racial Traits
- Sunlight Immunity: One of the oldest and most important Snow Bloodline traits. Carriers can remain beneath direct sunlight without suffering the normal destructive weakness experienced by Vampires. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] displayed this immediately after his transformation because he inherited the Snow Bloodline through [[Violet Elderblood Snow]].[11]
- Fire Resistance: Snow carriers possess extremely high natural resistance to Fire because their bodies are adapted to generating and controlling it.
- Heat Resistance: The same adaptation allows the user to survive temperatures that would destroy ordinary Vampires.
- Fire Immunity: Sufficiently developed members can progress from resistance toward true immunity. Complete mastery of the Snow Count transformation is associated with a degree of Fire immunity beyond the tolerance shown by less developed users; [[Agnes Elderblood Snow]] had achieved the complete Level 3 transformation while Victor had not yet reached that level during the [[WarFall]] period.[12]

### Emotion-Based Powers
The Snow Bloodline possesses one of the most unusual relationships between emotion and supernatural power among the Count families.

#### Emotion Empowerment
The user's power increases with the intensity of their emotions, particularly powerful states such as anger and rage.[13]
The stronger the emotional state, the more violently the user's Fire and overall combat output can rise.
This does not mean Snow users require anger to use Fire. Emotion functions as an amplifier for a power that already exists.
Known classifications include:
- Emotion Empowerment
- Rage Empowerment
- Power Amplification through Emotion

#### Emotional Amplification
The relationship works in the opposite direction as well.
Snow Fire can intensify emotions that the user is already experiencing. Anger becomes more intense, jealousy becomes harder to control and predatory impulses can grow stronger when the user releases large amounts of the Bloodline's power.[14]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] experienced this during his earliest Snow transformation, when his growing Fire was accompanied by escalating anger, sadistic impulses and predatory behavior.[15]
This creates a potential feedback loop:
Strong Emotion → Stronger Snow Fire → Intensified Emotion → Still Greater Power
Experienced users can control this effect through training.[16]

### Snow Vampire Count Form
The Snow [[Vampire Count Form]] represents an advanced evolutionary manifestation of the Bloodline and was the first foreign Count transformation awakened by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[17]
Known Bloodline-related abilities include:
- Pyrokinetic Empowerment: Massively increases the amount of Fire the user can generate and control.[18]
- Fire Aura: The transformed body becomes enveloped by intense Fire.
- Fire Body: Victor's entire body became covered in flames, with sufficient heat to destroy ordinary clothing through proximity.[19]
- Fire Wing Manifestation: With greater mastery of the first transformation, Victor developed two large wings composed entirely of Fire.[20]
- Enhanced Flame Temperature: Greater Bloodline output allows Fire to be compressed into hotter states such as blue flame.[21]
- Large-Scale Incineration: The transformed user can release Fire over a dramatically larger area.
- Enhanced Physical Abilities: Strength, speed and durability increase alongside Bloodline output. This is a generic Vampire Count property rather than a Snow-exclusive elemental power.[22]
- Enhanced Senses: Victor's first Snow transformation sharply increased his sight, hearing and general perception.[23] This should likewise be treated partly as a Count/Vampire transformation trait rather than a unique Fire ability.
- Enhanced Fire Resistance: The transformed body is capable of containing substantially greater quantities and temperatures of Fire.
- Fire Immunity at Complete Mastery: The fully mastered Level 3 Snow transformation can reach complete resistance/immunity against Fire beyond what lesser users can tolerate.[24]

### Advanced Pyrokinetic Applications

#### All of Creation Turns to Ashes
A large-scale Fire technique created by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] from his inherited Snow [[Pyrokinesis]].
Victor produces an enormous concentration of Fire and unleashes it across the surrounding battlefield. An early form was used during his attack on Clan [[Amon]], while a drastically more developed version used around [[WarFall]] produced an enormous miniature-sun-like mass of flames several kilometers in scale.[25][26]
SPW-style classifications involved include:
- Large-Scale Fire Generation
- Area Incineration
- Fireball Projection
- Heat Generation

#### Compressed Blue Fire
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] demonstrated sufficiently high control to gather an enormous amount of Fire and continuously compress it until it became a comparatively small sphere of extremely intense blue flame.[27]
This dramatically increased the amount of destructive Fire concentrated inside a small volume.

#### Purifying Fire
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] discovered that his Fire possessed useful purifying properties and used it while confronting the Miasma and violent impurities accumulating inside his existence.[28]
In SPW-style terminology this can be classified as:
- Purification (via Fire)
- Miasma Purification
- Corruption Cleansing
However, this should presently be treated as an advanced Victor-developed application of Snow-derived Fire, not proof that every Snow Vampire automatically possesses supernatural purification.

### Hereditary Bloodline Properties

#### Power Evolution
The Snow Bloodline is explicitly capable of improving across generations.[29]
The original Bloodline had sunlight immunity and relatively weak Fire manipulation. Its founder repeatedly trained that Fire until the improvement became part of the lineage transmitted to descendants.
Successive generations continued the same process.
In Superpower Wiki-style terminology, this can be described as:
- Hereditary Power Evolution
- Power Development
- Enhanced Power Inheritance
These terms describe the Bloodline's evolutionary mechanism rather than a combat attack.

#### Dormant Power Activation
Snow characteristics can remain latent within collateral descendants.
[[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]] possessed ancestral Snow lineage through the connection between Clan Snow and [[Blank Clan]]. After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] strengthened the pre-existing Bloodlines within his maids, Kaguya manifested more of the Snow characteristics already present in her heritage.[30]
This demonstrates:
- Atavism
- Dormant Power Activation
- Latent Genetic Ability
It does not mean Victor simply gave Kaguya an unrelated Fire power from nothing.

### Bloodline Integration
Snow Fire can coexist with other Noble Vampire powers.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] simultaneously carried:
- Snow Fire;
- Scarlett Ice;
- Fulger Lightning;
- and his own Blood-related abilities.
The three elemental Bloodlines did not reject one another and could even be combined into the same combat sequence.[31]
This compatibility should not be classified as an intrinsic Snow ability by itself. It is primarily a consequence of Victor's abnormal physiology.

### Limitations
- The Bloodline's Fire can amplify negative emotional states and make inexperienced users lose control.[32]
- Higher-temperature Fire requires greater Energy and control.
- Blue and white Fire are advanced expressions rather than default output for every carrier.
- Fire resistance does not necessarily equal complete Fire immunity in an undeveloped user.
- Sunlight immunity is a specific inherited Snow trait and should not be generalized to all Noble Vampires.
- The Count Form requires sufficient Bloodline development and Vampire mastery.
- Later Divine and Draconic powers possessed by Violet or Agnes should not automatically be attributed to the Snow Bloodline.

### Abilities Not Produced by the Snow Bloodline
- Foresight: Associated with [[Adonis Snow]] and his own supernatural circumstances, not the basic Snow Fire Bloodline.
- Order Manipulation: A later Divine/Conceptual power associated with Violet rather than ancestral Snow Fire.
- Obsession Manipulation: Likewise part of Violet's later Divine development.
- Dragon Abilities: Acquired through later transformation into [[Blood Dragons]], not through the ancestral Vampire Bloodline.
- [[Blade of Fafnir]] Powers: The Blade of Fafnir amplifies and channels Snow Fire, but its Dragon-derived properties belong to the weapon itself rather than the Bloodline.
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later Solar/Star abilities: Fire techniques compared visually to miniature suns are not automatically Solar Manipulation. Victor's actual later stellar abilities come from separate sources.

### Vampire Count Transformation
The Snow Bloodline possesses several advanced manifestations associated with the traditional Vampire Count transformation.

#### First Form
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] displayed an extremely early Snow-derived transformation shortly after becoming a Vampire.
His body became surrounded in Fire, his Vampire characteristics intensified and his physical and elemental abilities increased dramatically.[33]
Corneliu immediately recognized the state as something that should normally belong to far older and stronger Vampires.
Victor's abnormal biology allowed him to awaken this transformation almost immediately after acquiring the Bloodline.

#### Advanced Count Form
Violet later displayed a much more developed Count transformation.
In her advanced state:
- Fire erupted around her entire body;
- her physical Vampire traits became more pronounced;
- her hair became Fire-like;
- wings manifested;
- her overall physical capability increased;
- her ability to control her power improved;
- and the amount of Fire she could safely output rose substantially.[34]
The transformation demonstrates that the Count state does not merely increase raw Firepower. It also increases the user's ability to withstand and control the Bloodline's own output.

### Emotional Regulation
Because emotional intensity can directly influence Snow power, emotional discipline is particularly important for advanced users.
This creates an unusual contradiction.
A Snow Vampire benefits from extremely intense emotions but can simultaneously become less rational while experiencing them.
The strongest user is therefore not simply the person capable of becoming the angriest, but someone capable of exploiting intense emotion without surrendering completely to it.
This issue became particularly important for Violet, whose already possessive personality was further intensified by Vampire psychology and the nature of her Bloodline.

### Latent Inheritance
The Snow Bloodline can remain hidden or partially expressed within collateral descendants.
The most important example is [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]].
Blank originated partly from Snow ancestry, and descendants retained characteristics derived from that connection even after Shadow abilities became the defining power of their family.[35]
After Kaguya joined [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s personal lineage, his Blood strengthened the inherited supernatural information already present inside her.
Kaguya consequently awakened Snow Fire while retaining the Shadow abilities of Clan Blank.[36]
This confirmed that a Bloodline trait can remain latent for generations and later be strengthened or reactivated under suitable conditions.

### Interaction with Other Bloodlines
The Snow Bloodline can coexist with unrelated hereditary abilities.
The Snow–Blank lineage demonstrates this naturally: one historical descendant possessed both Snow Fire and Blank Darkness.[37]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] represents a much more extreme example.
Through the abnormal ritual involving Violet, Ruby and Sasha, he acquired:
- Snow Fire;
- Scarlett Ice;
- and Fulger Lightning.
Instead of one Bloodline automatically destroying the others, all three existed simultaneously and could later be combined.
This compatibility became one of the foundations of Victor's eventual development far beyond ordinary Vampire limitations.

### Strengthening through Victor's Blood
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later incorporated Agnes and Violet into his personal Vampire lineage.
Rather than replacing the Snow Bloodline, his Progenitor Blood increased its existing potential.
Both women immediately released enormous amounts of Fire as the change spread through their bodies.[36]
The same process awakened Kaguya's dormant Snow ancestry.
Victor concluded that his Blood behaved differently when interacting with established Noble Vampires: rather than simply creating an entirely new power set, it strengthened the Bloodlines they already possessed.[36]
The event represented another major evolutionary stage in the Snow lineage.

### Blood Dragon Evolution
The Bloodline remained part of Agnes and Violet after their later evolution into [[Blood Dragons]].
Dragon physiology provided a substantially greater foundation through which their previously developed Fire could operate, while the women continued advancing beyond the limitations that had applied to Noble Vampires.
The resulting abilities should not all be classified as pure Snow Bloodline powers, however.
Violet and Agnes eventually acquired Draconic, Divine and individual abilities unrelated to their original lineage. The Snow Bloodline is specifically the ancestral component responsible for their Fire affinity, Sunlight/Fire resistance and emotion-linked heritage.

### Relationship with Blank Clan
The [[Blank Clan]] preserves an important collateral branch of Snow ancestry.
The family originated when a relative of the Snow main family produced descendants with the Japanese Kuroyami assassin lineage.[38]
Those descendants inherited Shadow-related abilities while preserving several Snow characteristics.
A particularly powerful historical descendant eventually manifested:
Snow Fire + Sunlight Immunity + Blank Darkness.[39]
Kaguya descends from this mixed lineage.
The Snow and Blank Bloodlines therefore provide direct evidence that supernatural hereditary abilities can coexist and remain latent through later generations rather than simply replacing one another.

### Importance
The Snow Bloodline is particularly important to the study of supernatural genetics because its history explicitly proves three principles:
1. Bloodlines can evolve through training.
2. Improvements can become hereditary.
3. Latent ancestral traits can remain within descendants and later reawaken.[1][36]
Few other Noble Vampire lineages have their developmental history explained as directly.
The modern power of Snow is therefore not simply something the family received at its creation. It is the accumulated product of multiple generations deliberately improving what they inherited.

### Trivia
- The original Snow Bloodline possessed only Sunlight Immunity and weak Fire manipulation.[1]
- Violet's great-great-grandfather was both the First Leader and creator of the [[Snow Clan]].[1]
- Successive generations repeatedly improved the Bloodline, causing their descendants to inherit progressively stronger Fire.[1]
- Sunlight Immunity is older than the Bloodline's modern high-level [[Pyrokinesis]].
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] inherited Sunlight Immunity immediately after becoming a Vampire despite not being naturally born into the family.[40]
- The Bloodline's strength can increase with emotional intensity, particularly anger.[34]
- Clan Blank preserves collateral Snow ancestry.[41]
- Kaguya's dormant Snow Fire was reactivated after Victor strengthened her existing Bloodlines.[36]
- Snow Fire can coexist with completely different Bloodlines rather than necessarily replacing them.
- The modern Snow lineage is one of the clearest canonical examples that inherited supernatural powers can permanently improve across generations.
