The Seven Legions, also known as the Imperial Legions or Elderblood Legions, are the seven principal military, intelligence, scientific and enforcement organizations of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Established under the authority of Emperor [[Victor Elderblood]], they constitute the specialized institutional foundation of the Empire's military administration. Each Legion possesses its own command structure, personnel and strategic responsibilities while remaining subordinate to the Emperor and the unified Imperial military leadership.[1][2][3][4]
The Legions were progressively organized during the Empire's early [[Imperial Calendar]], with the first formal institutions appearing around Year 20. Their principal functions were established by approximately Year 1,000, although the development of their personnel, technologies and military capabilities continued throughout the Empire's accelerated era and subsequent expansion. Their numerical designations do not represent their chronological order of creation: the Sixth and Third Legions appeared before the First and Second Legions were formally established.[3][4]
The Seven Legions were created to address the demands of governing a civilization whose territory, population and military capabilities had expanded far beyond those of the former supernatural powers of Earth and [[Nightingale]]. Rather than allowing the armies and institutions of individual Pantheons to operate independently, Victor sought to establish a unified system in which each organization fulfilled a specialized function while remaining accountable to the Imperial command structure.[2]
The First Legion serves as the Empire's offensive spearhead, while the Second Legion specializes in territorial defense. The Third Legion handles clandestine intelligence and espionage, and the Fourth Legion concentrates upon technological development and superweapons. The Fifth Legion protects the Imperial Family and sacred locations, while the Sixth Legion enforces Imperial law. The Seventh Legion is a classified organization created to confront extraordinary extradimensional and existential threats.[3][4]
The Legions operate within a civilization possessing extremely advanced military technology, supernatural warriors, vast interplanetary transportation systems and powerful Divine authorities. Their personnel benefit from the Empire's continually evolving scientific infrastructure, improved racial and biological foundations, sophisticated training programs and access to the extraordinary resources controlled by the Imperial Family.[3][5]
Although the Legions are central to the Empire's military organization, they do not encompass every force serving Victor. His personally created soldiers, Leviathans, Dragons, Demonic armies, independent Divine allies, subordinate Pantheons and [[Akasha Elderblood]]'s information network coexist with the seven formal organizations. Their separate existence allows the Empire to draw upon forces and resources that do not necessarily belong to any one Legion.[1][6]

### History

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
The foundations of the Seven Legions predate their formal organization during the [[Imperial Calendar]]. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had already begun constructing a professional military establishment capable of operating independently of the armies personally maintained by his wives and allied supernatural factions.
A significant development occurred through the creation and training of seven exceptional Imperial soldiers: John, [[Clare Bloodhunter]], [[Kiana Bloodhunter]], [[Laura Bloodhunter]], Conan, [[Kaleb Hunter]] and [[Caelus Hunter]]. These individuals were among Victor's earliest specially developed Imperial commanders and possessed the potential to lead powerful forces created through his expanding military and biological technologies.[7][8][1]
Victor intended them to serve as the foundations for future military Legions. Their purpose was not merely to function as powerful personal attendants, but to develop into commanders whose experience and abilities would contribute to the establishment of a professional Imperial army.
They were informed that the Emperor intended to create seven Legions through their future experiences, placing them beneath the command of his General, [[Scathach]]. Their training was intended to prepare them for increasingly demanding military responsibilities, although Victor emphasized that they would first need to become capable of meeting his expectations.[1]
This early plan is an important stage in the development of the Imperial military establishment. However, it should be distinguished from the later finalized command arrangements of the Seven Legions. The seven soldiers represented an initial military-command project, while the eventual Legions acquired different specialized responsibilities and included commanders drawn from the Imperial Family and other trusted individuals.
The establishment of [[Akasha Elderblood]] further strengthened the developing Imperial command system. Created by Victor and connected to the Empire's central systems, Akasha became an important intelligence responsible for maintaining information flow, communication and coordination throughout the growing civilization.[9][10]
Victor's military ambitions also became increasingly practical as the Empire began undertaking operations beyond its original inhabited worlds. Imperial soldiers could conduct planetary investigations, confront hostile forces and establish control over newly discovered territories without requiring the Emperor to personally participate in every operation.[11]
The creation of professional soldiers, the development of powerful military technologies and the establishment of Akasha's information infrastructure provided the foundations upon which the later Seven Legions would be formally organized.

#### Those Who Rule Reality
The formal development of the Seven Legions became one of the Empire's most important institutional changes during its preparations for conflict against the civilizations of the greater Cosmos.
Following the destruction of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s exploratory clone by a civilization associated with the Empire of Light, the Emperor recognized the enormous disparity between his own Sector and the advanced powers occupying the higher regions of Reality. His response was to strengthen the Empire rather than immediately initiate a war against opponents whose capabilities remained incompletely understood.[12][13]
Victor initiated an extraordinary ritual accelerating the passage of Time within his territory. The undertaking was intended to provide approximately 200,000 years of internal development while roughly one year passed outside the affected region. Natalia controlled the temporal component, while Victor and Jeanne supplied the principal Energy required to sustain the enormous undertaking. The other Empresses participated through a rotating arrangement, assisting with the ritual while continuing to supervise the Empire through their Avatars.[2][6]
The accelerated era produced enormous changes in the Empire's population, technological advancement, biological development and military capabilities. The Imperial Family actively participated in major historical events, supervising the development of new technologies, the improvement of supernatural races and the creation of increasingly sophisticated institutions.[3]
The earliest formally established Legions emerged around Year 20 of the [[Imperial Calendar]].
The Sixth Legion, [[The Silent Judgment]] was established by Ariel, Kaguya, Nyx, Leona and Tasha to enforce Imperial law and maintain order within newly incorporated territories. Shortly afterward, Kaguya, Nyx and [[Persephone]] founded the Third Legion, the Black Hand of the Emperor, whose duties concerned intelligence gathering, espionage, infiltration and clandestine security.[3]
The two organizations developed complementary responsibilities comparable to those of a public enforcement authority and a secret intelligence service. Their formation provided the Empire with means of addressing corruption, rebellion, treason and hostile political activity without depending exclusively upon conventional military intervention.[3]
In Year 50, Ruby, Velnorah, Natalia and Aline consolidated the Empire's accumulated scientific knowledge and established the Fourth Legion, [[Stellar Arsenal]]. The new organization became responsible for advanced technological research, the study of alien knowledge, forbidden technologies, genetic experimentation and the development of increasingly destructive military weapons.[3]
The Fifth Legion developed through the expansion of an existing Imperial Guard organization. Victor personally strengthened it into a dedicated elite protective force responsible for the security of the Imperial Family, sacred temples, relics and particularly devoted followers. [[Nero]] and Ophis became its principal commanders, with Rose serving as their substitute.[3]
The establishment of these early Legions occurred during a period of extraordinary demographic and technological development. The Empire's population increased through Divine blessings associated with Life, Fertility, Marriage and Motherhood, while Victor and Velnorah improved the biological foundations of numerous races. Stronger bloodlines, improved reproductive compatibility and new supernatural hybrids contributed to the emergence of increasingly capable Imperial citizens.[3]
The development of the Tower of Challenges into a vast interconnected training system reinforced this transformation. Towers were established on numerous planets, allowing warriors separated by astronomical distances to train within the same greater system. The resulting culture encouraged constant improvement while preserving the importance of scientific, administrative and other noncombat professions.[3]
Around Year 1,000, the Empire formalized the two principal conventional military Legions.
The First Legion, the Spartans was founded by [[Aphrodite]], [[Scathach]] and Rose. Its purpose was to lead offensive military campaigns, invade hostile territories and overcome enemy defenses. Scathach and Rose personally trained its personnel, while John became its established commander.[3]
The Second Legion, the [[Order of the Iron Veil]] was created by Eleanor, Violet, Haruna and Hela. It became responsible for planetary defense, fortifications, defensive rings and the protection of established Imperial territories. Eleanor assumed its principal command, supported by Haruna and Hela as substitute commanders.[4]
The Seventh Legion, [[Children of the Abyss]] was also established during the early Imperial era. Personally created by Victor and Violet, it became the Empire's most secretive military organization, specializing in threats originating beyond conventional dimensional boundaries. Its internal commander and personnel remained classified, with direct command restricted to Victor, Violet and Azathoth.[4]
The creation of these organizations provided the Empire with specialized institutions for every principal category of military and security responsibility. Their development continued over subsequent millennia rather than ending with their original foundations.

#### Expansion of Military Training
The growth of the Imperial military establishment required enormous training resources.
[[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] devoted an entire planet to the training of new soldiers. Its facilities contained vast military training fields, arenas, fortresses and logistical infrastructure. Approximately 100 million recruits were present during one training cycle, demonstrating the enormous scale of the Empire's developing military manpower.[5]
The training planet reflected the transformation of [[Scathach]]'s role. Whereas she had previously been renowned for personally training a small number of exceptionally talented disciples, she now possessed the resources necessary to influence the development of armies on an interstellar scale.
The Empire's training infrastructure was supplemented by the Tower of Challenges, improved racial biology and advanced technological support, ensuring that new generations of soldiers could continually surpass the capabilities of the supernatural armies that had existed before the Empire's expansion.[3][5]

#### Mature Imperial Military Operations
As the accelerated era progressed, the Legions developed beyond their initial organizational descriptions.
The Empire's conventional forces gained experience operating across numerous planets, while its scientists continued examining new species, technological discoveries and unusual supernatural phenomena.
A documented operation involving the First and Fourth Legions demonstrates this development. An Imperial fleet consisting of more than fifty vessels investigated a planet affected by a rapidly reproducing hostile species. The World Tree belonging to the planet had already been secured, but the emergence of an unidentified life-form created both a military problem and a potential scientific opportunity.[14]
The Fourth Legion requested a biological sample of the newly discovered species. The request was accepted, and the Spartans were dispatched to the planet to secure the area and support extraction.[14]
The operation involved powerful Imperial combatants, a fleet possessing orbital weaponry and conventional Spartan troops. The soldiers who landed on the planet were physically formidable, with ordinary personnel exceeding two meters in height and elite Bloodhunter soldiers such as John and his brothers reaching approximately three to four meters.[14]
The participating combatants also demonstrated the increasing strength of Imperial personnel. [[Frederick Winter]] and Andrew were classified within the Empire's V-05 threat category, reflecting their ability to threaten an entire Solar System through the combined use of their powers and techniques.[14]
The situation ultimately required the intervention of [[Violet Elderblood Snow]], whose Authority over Order allowed her to stabilize the hostile species and restore order to its environment. The incident demonstrated how the Empire could combine military operations, scientific research and the supernatural Authority of the Imperial Family when confronting unusual threats.[14][15]
The Fourth Legion's research establishment continued developing specialized personnel capable of assisting its leading scientists. Ruby, Velnorah, Aline and Velina remained among the Empire's foremost scientific authorities, while the creation of artificial researchers and technomantic organisms allowed increasingly complex work to be delegated to specialized subordinates.[15]
The protection of the Imperial Family also became increasingly sophisticated. The Imperial Princesses were accompanied by Imperial Guards when leaving the castle, demonstrating the Fifth Legion's continued operational relevance despite the extraordinary personal powers possessed by the Emperor's daughters.[16]
The Legions remained active institutions throughout the Empire's continued development, maintaining the specialized responsibilities established during their original formation while adapting to the changing military, technological and political conditions of Imperial civilization.

### Command Hierarchy
The Seven Legions operate beneath a centralized Imperial military hierarchy designed to prevent the fragmentation of authority between separate military organizations, Pantheons and subordinate factions.
At the highest level stands Emperor [[Victor Elderblood]], whose authority supersedes that of every subordinate commander. The unified military establishment is directed through [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], Victor's General and the Empire's Supreme Military Commander.[1][2]
Each Legion possesses its own commander or commanders responsible for fulfilling its specialized mandate. Many also possess designated substitute commanders capable of assuming leadership when their principal commanders are unavailable.

#### Victor Elderblood
Main article: [[Victor Elderblood]]
Victor is the creator of the Imperial Legion system and its supreme political and military authority. His commands take precedence over those of subordinate military officers, regardless of their personal power or previous positions within independent supernatural factions.
The establishment of the Legions reflects Victor's desire to create institutions capable of operating without his constant personal supervision. Although he possesses the strength to confront extraordinary threats directly, the administration of a civilization extending across enormous astronomical distances requires reliable commanders and specialized institutions.
Victor personally supervises important strategic decisions while delegating ordinary military, scientific and administrative responsibilities to his wives, daughters and trusted subordinates.

#### Scathach Elderblood Scarlett
Main article: [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]]
[[Scathach]] is [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s General and the supreme commander of the Imperial military establishment. The seven Imperial commanders envisioned during Victor's earlier military reforms were explicitly intended to serve beneath her authority.[1]
Her position reflects her extraordinary experience as a martial artist, military leader and instructor. She is responsible for ensuring that the Empire's military forces remain capable of confronting enemies whose strength continues increasing alongside Imperial development.
Scathach's influence also extends to the training of Imperial soldiers. Her dedicated training planet contains facilities capable of accommodating enormous numbers of recruits, with approximately one hundred million soldiers participating during one recorded training period.[5]
Her authority is particularly important in situations involving multiple Legions, whose commanders must remain part of a unified military system rather than competing for independent control of Imperial resources.

#### Legion Commanders
Each Legion's principal commander is responsible for the organization assigned to them.
The Legion commanders include members of the Imperial Family, trusted personal subordinates of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and individuals possessing exceptional experience within their designated fields.
Some organizations possess joint leadership, such as the Fifth Legion under [[Nero]] and Ophis. Others possess a principal commander with several designated substitutes, as seen in the First, Second and Fourth Legions.
The Seventh Legion possesses additional restrictions because its personnel and supernatural nature require command from individuals capable of safely exercising authority over its classified forces.

#### Akasha's Information Network
Main article: [[Akasha Elderblood]]
Akasha Elderblood provides important information and administrative capabilities throughout the Empire. Her systems support communication, information processing and the coordination of Imperial activities across enormous distances.[9][10]
Akasha's network functions alongside the military command structure rather than constituting an eighth official Legion.
Her information infrastructure can support the exchange of reports, requests and operational data between different Imperial organizations, while her own responsibilities extend beyond ordinary military administration.
The network's extraordinary capabilities do not establish that every message is incapable of being intercepted or that all communications are automatically immune to every form of dimensional interference. The exact limits of individual systems must be distinguished from Akasha's broader supernatural Authority.

### The Seven Legions

#### First Legion — The Spartans
Main article: [[The Spartans]]
The Spartans
THE SPEAR OF THE EMPIRE
"We are the spear of the Empire, For the Emperor!"
The Spartans are the Empire's principal offensive Legion, responsible for invasion, direct assault, territorial conquest and large-scale military campaigns. Founded in Year 1,000 by [[Aphrodite]], [[Scathach]] and Rose, the Legion was personally trained by Scathach and Rose to confront hostile forces and secure territories for the Empire.[3]
Its personnel include war dragons, heavily armored soldiers, siege mages and powerful military spacecraft. Its warships possess destructive capabilities extending from planetary warfare to the destruction of entire Solar Systems.[3]
John serves as its principal commander, with Rose, Eleanor and Kali capable of assuming command when required. The Legion's name was selected by Aphrodite to preserve a part of ancient Greek military heritage, while its headquarters were established at the reconstructed city of Aphrodisias on [[Yggdrasil]].[3]
The Spartans constitute one of the Empire's largest conventional military organizations and operate in close partnership with the Second Legion.

#### Second Legion — Order of the Iron Veil
Main article: [[Order of the Iron Veil]]
Order of the Iron Veil
THE SHIELD OF THE EMPIRE
"We protect the Empire until the last breath."
The Order of the Iron Veil is the Empire's principal defensive Legion, created around Year 1,000 by Eleanor, Violet, Haruna and Hela. Its responsibilities include planetary defense, fortified zones, bastions, defensive rings and the protection of established Imperial territories.[4]
Its commonly identified assets include stationary knights, climate controllers and orbital defense towers. The Legion possesses access to weapons capable of destroying entire Solar Systems, making it a formidable conventional military organization despite its defensive specialization.[4]
[[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]] serves as principal commander, with Haruna and Hela authorized to replace her when necessary.
Although primarily responsible for defense, the [[Iron]] Veil can undertake offensive operations when required. Together with the Spartans, it forms the Empire's principal offensive and defensive military partnership.

#### Third Legion — Emperor's Black Hand
Main article: Emperor's Black Hand
Emperor's Black Hand
JUSTICE IN THE SHADOWS
"No one sees what bleeds in the dark."
[[The Black Hand of the Emperor]], abbreviated B.H.E., is the Empire's principal clandestine intelligence organization. Founded around Year 20 by Kaguya, Nyx and [[Persephone]], its responsibilities include espionage, infiltration, counterintelligence, psychological warfare, hunting traitors and controlling the Imperial narrative.[3]
The Legion employs shadow assassins, telepaths, shapeshifters and shadow demons, allowing it to address threats through methods that differ from conventional military intervention.
[[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]] serves as its principal commander, with Nyx acting in her place when necessary.
The Black Hand operates particularly closely with the Sixth Legion, forming the clandestine counterpart to the Empire's public law-enforcement organization.

#### Fourth Legion — Stellar Arsenal
Main article: [[Stellar Arsenal]]
Stellar Arsenal
THE EMPIRE'S SCIENTIFIC ARSENAL
"Progress at any cost."
The Stellar Arsenal is the Empire's primary scientific and technological Legion. Founded in Year 50 by Ruby, Velnorah, Natalia and Aline, it was established to consolidate the Empire's accumulated knowledge and develop technologies capable of supporting its continued expansion.[3]
Its mandate includes superweapons, forbidden technologies, advanced engineering, genetic experimentation, temporal manipulation and the acquisition of alien scientific knowledge.
The Legion's personnel include scientists, engineers, witches and automatons. It is responsible for research into weapons capable of destroying entire Solar Systems and greater structures.[3]
[[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] commands the organization, with Velnorah and Aline serving as substitutes.
The Legion's scientific importance continued increasing as its researchers developed sophisticated biological systems, artificial personnel and experimental technologies capable of manipulating extraordinary supernatural phenomena. Its principal researchers also participated in the development of classified Imperial projects, including the Divine warship Polaris.[17][15]

#### Fifth Legion — The Imperial Guard
Main article: [[The Imperial Guard]]
The Imperial Guard
THE IMPERIAL FAMILY'S GUARDIANS
"We protect the hope of the Empire."
The Imperial Guard is the Empire's principal elite protection force. An existing organization expanded into the Fifth Legion under [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s authority, it is responsible for protecting the Emperor, his wives, his daughters, sacred relics and the Empire's most devoted followers.[3]
Its personnel consist primarily of exceptional warriors personally selected by the Emperor or an Empress. Sacred temples normally possess three to five guarding units, whose members are known among the faithful as Sacred Guards.[3]
The Legion is jointly commanded by [[Nero Elderblood]] and [[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]], with Rose serving as their substitute.
The Imperial Guard concentrates its resources upon exceptionally powerful combatants rather than the enormous conventional formations maintained by the First and Second Legions. The personnel protecting the royal family became capable of fighting beings comparable to First Generation [[Primordial Gods]], demonstrating the extraordinary strength of the Emperor's elite protective forces.[18]
Its members can also reinforce other Imperial Legions when additional elite combat strength is required.

Its responsibilities encompass judgment, punishment, execution, the suppression of rebellion and the cleansing of corrupted or rebellious planets. Its personnel include elite soldiers, executioners, inquisitors, mind readers and empathic beings.
[[Jeanne Elderblood]] serves as its principal commander, with Leona authorized to assume command when necessary.

#### Seventh Legion — Children of the Abyss

Only Victor, Violet and Azathoth are identified as capable of commanding the Legion directly. Hela possesses the potential to become a commander in the future if she develops sufficient strength to withstand the organization’s dangerous supernatural nature.[4]

### Military Strength and Scale
The Seven Legions operate within an Empire whose military resources grew enormously during the accelerated [[Imperial Calendar]]. Their scale reflects the demographic expansion of Imperial civilization, the improvement of supernatural races and the technological infrastructure developed to support planetary and interstellar military operations.[3][5]

#### Personnel Strength
The First and Second Legions constitute the Empire's principal large-scale conventional military forces and are described as among its most numerous organizations. The Fifth Legion differs because it emphasizes elite warriors and maintains fewer common units, while the Seventh Legion's personnel strength remains classified.[3][4]
The organizational estimates traditionally associated with the Seven Legions place most of the conventional branches in the billions of personnel, while the Imperial Guard is generally described as numbering in the millions. These are broad military-scale estimates rather than a complete numerical census for a specified Imperial year. They should be distinguished from directly documented troop concentrations, such as the approximately one hundred million recruits present on [[Scathach]]'s training planet.[5]

#### Military Training
Military training is a fundamental part of the Empire's development.
[[Scathach]]'s dedicated training planet provides one of the clearest demonstrations of Imperial military scale. The facilities were designed to accommodate immense training populations, including a documented concentration of approximately 100 million recruits.[5]
The Tower of Challenges further contributes to military development by encouraging continuous improvement among Imperial citizens. Its interconnected planetary copies allow individuals separated by astronomical distances to participate in a shared system of training and competition.[3]
The Empire's military development also benefits from the strengthening of its races. Advances in biological engineering, bloodline compatibility and supernatural evolution allow new generations to possess far greater physical potential than their predecessors.
By the mature Imperial era, powerful combatants capable of threatening planets and Solar Systems no longer occupied the same exceptional position they would have held during the earlier history of Earth or [[Nightingale]].[14][15]

#### Threat Classifications
The Empire employs a threat classification system capable of distinguishing between different scales of military and supernatural danger.
During the later development of Imperial civilization, personnel such as Andrew and [[Frederick Winter]] were classified as V-05, representing beings whose capabilities could threaten an entire Solar System under suitable conditions.[14]
The higher V-06 classification concerns Galactic-scale threats, although the system recognizes that beings within the same general category can differ enormously in their individual powers and practical destructive capabilities.[15]
These classifications concern potential threat rather than providing a universal military rank.
An ordinary soldier's membership in a Legion does not automatically grant them the same destructive capabilities as the Legion's strongest officers, specialized weapons or elite combatants.
Likewise, the existence of a Galaxy-class individual within the Imperial Family does not establish that every conventional military formation possesses equivalent strength.

### Military Technology and Armaments
The Legions benefit from the extraordinary technological development achieved by the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Advanced military spacecraft, interplanetary transportation networks, Runic systems and supernatural engineering allow its forces to operate across vast astronomical distances.[3]

#### Interplanetary Transportation
Imperial civilization employs advanced transportation technologies incorporating the manipulation of Space and Time.
Transportation systems exist on numerous populated planets, while military and civilian spacecraft can employ related principles to travel between distant locations. Such technology greatly reduces the practical significance of ordinary astronomical distances.[3]
These developments support the Empire's ability to maintain military forces across multiple worlds and rapidly deploy troops or resources where required.
The existence of advanced transportation infrastructure does not establish that every military vessel possesses identical movement capabilities or that every form of dimensional interference can be bypassed automatically.

#### Rune-Expanded Structures
The Empire possesses advanced Runic technology capable of expanding the internal dimensions of physical structures.
A prominent example is the extraordinary spaceship given to [[Aphrodite]] during the establishment of the Spartan Legion. Although externally measuring approximately 60 kilometers long and 15 kilometers wide, the vessel possessed more than 300,000 square kilometers of internal space and could accommodate approximately 50 million beings.[3]
Its capabilities demonstrate the scale of Imperial technological engineering and the integration of Runes with advanced spacecraft.
However, Aphrodite's vessel was a personal Imperial gift rather than a confirmed standard warship assigned to every Spartan formation.

#### Strategic Weapons
The Empire possesses military weapons capable of destroying planets, Solar Systems and greater astronomical structures.
The First Legion employs advanced military spacecraft capable of planetary and Solar-System destruction, while the Second Legion possesses access to comparable strategic armaments for the defense of Imperial territory.[3][4]
The Fourth Legion's responsibilities explicitly include the research, development and control of weapons capable of operating on Solar-System scales and beyond.[3]

The distinction between developing a weapon and fielding it is significant.
The [[Stellar Arsenal]] possesses responsibility for the development and control of experimental superweapons, while the Spartans and [[Iron]] Veil are the principal conventional Legions associated with the routine employment of extremely powerful offensive and defensive military armaments.
The broader Imperial military system remains subject to the Emperor and the Supreme Military Commander. Strategic weapons can therefore be placed under centralized military authority, but a universal rule requiring simultaneous authorization from both [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and [[Scathach]] for every transfer of Solar-System-class weaponry has not been independently established as a formal regulation.

#### Classified Technologies
Certain Imperial technologies remain subject to exceptional restrictions because of their military importance.
The classified Polaris project is a prominent example. Its development involved the Empire's principal scientists and produced a Divine warship whose planned capabilities included extraordinarily destructive conceptual attacks.[17]
Knowledge concerning Polaris was restricted even among members of the Imperial Family, reflecting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s determination to prevent information about important strategic weapons from becoming available to potential enemies.[16]
Such secrecy demonstrates that the Empire's military establishment possesses multiple levels of access to technological information. Membership in a Legion does not necessarily provide unrestricted knowledge of every classified project.

### Operational Cooperation
Although each Legion possesses a specialized mandate, the Empire's military structure permits cooperation between different organizations.
Their functions are intended to complement one another rather than create entirely isolated military establishments.

#### Offense and Defense
The First and Second Legions form the Empire's principal conventional military partnership.
[[The Spartans]] are responsible for direct assault, conquest and the destruction of hostile military defenses, while the [[Iron]] Veil specializes in fortifying and protecting established Imperial territory.[3][4]

The distinction does not make either organization incapable of fulfilling the other's responsibilities. The Iron Veil can undertake offensive operations when necessary, while the Spartans' original mandate includes securing conquered territories.
Their specialized functions provide the Empire with an organizational system capable of supporting both territorial expansion and long-term security.

#### Intelligence and Enforcement
The Third and Sixth Legions constitute the Empire's principal clandestine and public security partnership.
The Black Hand handles intelligence gathering, infiltration and covert intervention, while the Silent Judgment applies Imperial law through public enforcement, judgment and punishment.[3]

Their relationship resembles that of an intelligence service and a public enforcement authority, providing complementary means of preserving Imperial order.

#### Research and Military Deployment
The Fourth Legion's relationship with the conventional military organizations is demonstrated by its request for an alien biological specimen during an operation involving an Imperial-controlled planet.
The scientific request was accepted and Spartan troops were deployed to secure the target and facilitate extraction. The operation involved advanced naval support, orbital weaponry and specialized Imperial personnel.[14]
This incident establishes practical cooperation between the Empire's scientific and offensive military institutions.
The mission also illustrates the Empire's preference for studying unusual beings and phenomena when their properties may prove valuable. Rather than automatically destroying every unfamiliar organism, Imperial researchers may request specimens for further examination.

#### Elite Reinforcement
The Fifth Legion can temporarily deploy its elite personnel to reinforce other military organizations.[3]
This arrangement permits the Empire to concentrate exceptional individual combat strength where required without permanently transferring the Guard's personnel away from their principal protective responsibilities.
The specific circumstances governing each reinforcement deployment remain subject to Imperial command.

#### Existential Threat Response
The Seventh Legion possesses responsibilities that differ considerably from ordinary military operations.
Its purpose concerns threats originating beyond conventional dimensional boundaries, including hostile cosmic entities and primordial horrors.[4]
Because the identity and capabilities of its personnel remain classified, the complete operational procedures through which it coordinates with the other Legions have not been disclosed.
Its special status should not be used to assign unconfirmed members, commanders or military victories to the organization.

### Supporting Imperial Structures
The Seven Legions operate within a broader military and administrative system containing additional organizations, supernatural forces and infrastructure.
These forces may support the Legions or serve the Emperor directly, but their existence does not automatically establish formal membership within any of the seven organizations.

#### Akasha Elderblood's Network
Main article: [[Akasha Elderblood]]
Akasha's information network is one of the Empire's principal systems for communication, coordination and the processing of administrative information.
Created through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s power and connected to the Empire's central infrastructure, Akasha possesses enormous computational and supernatural capabilities. Her subordinate systems and operators support the management of information across distant worlds.[9][10]
The network operates alongside the Imperial military system rather than possessing an officially established numerical designation as one of the Seven Legions.
Its exact total number of artificial intelligences and Operators should not be confused with the troop counts of the Legions.

#### Leviathans
Main article: Leviathans
The Leviathans constitute a separate category of extraordinarily powerful creatures serving the Empire.
Their development reached an immense scale during the accelerated Imperial era. Billions inhabited their designated world, and the largest were capable of swallowing planets comparable in size to Earth.[19]
Their numbers and capabilities demonstrate the enormous biological and military resources available to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
However, their population should not automatically be counted as the personnel strength of a particular Legion.

#### Dragons and Draconic Forces
Main article: Dragons
The Empire maintains numerous Draconic forces whose powers and responsibilities extend beyond conventional military organization.
War dragons are among the common units associated with the Spartans, while other Dragons serve [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], the Imperial Family or the wider Empire through different relationships.
The existence of Dragon troops within the First Legion does not make every Dragon or Blood Dragon a member of that organization.

#### Demonic Forces
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s authority over Hell provides the Empire with enormous Demonic armies and supernatural military resources.
These forces have served the Emperor in major conflicts and can be placed under Imperial military leadership when necessary.
However, the Demonic armies originating from Hell possess their own historical structures and should not automatically be counted as members of the Seven Legions.

#### Allied Pantheons
The Empire's relationship with the remaining supernatural Pantheons provides additional military, political and Divine resources.
The Pantheons can retain aspects of their cultural and religious identities while operating within the Emperor's broader political and military order.
Their allied status does not necessarily make every God, [[Demigod]] or military formation belonging to those Pantheons a permanent member of one of the Seven Legions.

#### Personal Imperial Forces
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] possesses additional servants, creations and military assets whose allegiance is directed personally toward him.
Such forces may operate independently of conventional Imperial military institutions, particularly where their nature or assignments require direct Imperial authority.
Their exact relationship with individual Legions must be established through explicit organizational statements rather than assumed through shared loyalty to the Emperor.

### Imperial Development and Military Doctrine
The Seven Legions reflect the Empire's transition from a supernatural faction centered upon a small number of extraordinarily powerful individuals into a civilization possessing permanent military and administrative institutions.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s personal power remains its ultimate foundation, but the Legions allow the Empire to undertake ordinary military and administrative operations without requiring the Emperor to directly supervise every detail.
This approach reflects the increasing importance of delegation as the Empire expands across numerous planets and galaxies.

#### Specialization
Each Legion possesses a principal mandate, preventing every branch of the military establishment from becoming responsible for the same functions.
The division allows scientists to concentrate upon technological development, intelligence agents upon clandestine operations, conventional armies upon warfare and the Imperial Guard upon protection.
The arrangement does not eliminate cooperation between branches, but establishes specialized institutions whose personnel can develop expertise suited to their particular responsibilities.

#### Unified Authority
The Legions remain subordinate to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and the Empire's Supreme Military Commander.
This hierarchy prevents the creation of competing independent military powers under commanders who might otherwise possess significant personal or political influence.
The original military reforms anticipated commanders operating directly beneath [[Scathach]], while the subsequent Legion organization incorporated the Imperial Family and trusted subordinates into those command positions.[1][2]

#### Supernatural Development
The Empire's military strength benefits from the evolution of its population and the enhancement of its supernatural races.
Advances in genetic engineering, bloodline compatibility, Divine blessings and technological development continually improve the potential of Imperial citizens.
The interconnected Tower of Challenges provides another means of training, allowing individuals across the Empire to improve their abilities through shared challenges.[3]
These systems support the long-term development of the military by continuously increasing the pool of individuals capable of joining its specialized organizations.

#### Scientific Advancement
The Empire's scientific establishment develops technologies suited to increasingly powerful opponents.
The [[Stellar Arsenal]] plays an important role in this process through research involving forbidden technology, superweapons, genetic engineering and the acquisition of alien knowledge.
The development of specialized scientific personnel further allows the Empire to maintain large research institutions without requiring its leading scientists to personally complete every task.[15]

#### Imperial Faith
The Empire's religious and reincarnation systems also contribute to its long-term stability.
Divine blessings associated with reproduction and the development of the afterlife support demographic continuity, while the reincarnation system allows Souls to return to mortal life under the Empire's greater supernatural administration.[6][3]
These developments support Imperial civilization as a whole rather than constituting military recruitment programs exclusive to any single Legion.

### Trivia
- The Seven Legions are the Empire's principal specialized military, intelligence, scientific and enforcement organizations rather than seven identical conventional armies.[3][4]
- Their numerical designations do not reflect their chronological order of creation. The Sixth and Third Legions were among the first to appear, while the First and Second Legions were formally established later.[3]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s original military project involved seven soldiers—John, Clare, Kiana, Laura, Conan, Kaleb and Caelus—who were intended to develop into Imperial commanders beneath [[Scathach]]. The later Legion leadership structure incorporated different commanders and specialized institutions.[1]
- The first formally established Legions appeared around Year 20 of the [[Imperial Calendar]].[3]
- The [[Stellar Arsenal]] was established in Year 50 through the efforts of Ruby, Velnorah, Natalia and Aline.[3]
- [[The Imperial Guard]] existed before its formal expansion into the Fifth Legion.[3]
- The First and Second Legions were established around Year 1,000 and became the Empire's principal conventional offensive and defensive forces.[3][4]
- The First Legion's name was chosen by [[Aphrodite]] in honor of ancient Sparta. Its headquarters were established in the recreated city of Aphrodisias on [[Yggdrasil]].[3]
- The Third and Sixth Legions possess closely related security functions, with the Black Hand handling clandestine operations and the Silent Judgment enforcing Imperial law publicly.[3]
- The Imperial Guard's sacred temple personnel are called Sacred Guards, with three to five guarding units normally assigned to a temple.[3]
- [[Nero]] and Ophis jointly command the Fifth Legion, rather than one serving solely as the other's substitute.[3]
- The Seventh Legion's operational commander remains classified, and only Victor, Violet and Azathoth are identified as capable of exercising direct command over its forces.[4]
- [[Hela Elderblood]] may eventually become capable of commanding the Seventh Legion after sufficient supernatural development.[4]
- Scathach maintains a dedicated military training planet, with approximately 100 million recruits present during one recorded training period.[5]
- The First and Second Legions possess access to Solar-System-destruction-class weapons, while the Fourth Legion develops and controls even more advanced military technologies.[3][4]
- During a later operation, the Fourth Legion requested a biological sample from an alien species and the Spartans were deployed to secure the target, confirming direct cooperation between Imperial research and military forces.[14]
- Ordinary Spartan soldiers were described as exceeding two meters in height, while certain elite Bloodhunter personnel reached approximately three to four meters.[14]
- The mature Empire employs military threat classifications such as V-05 and V-06, reflecting the potential danger posed by exceptionally powerful supernatural combatants.[14][15]
- The Empire's wider military establishment includes additional forces such as Leviathans, Dragons, Demonic armies and the personal creations of Victor, which should not automatically be counted as permanent members of one of the Seven Legions.
- [[Akasha Elderblood]]'s information network supports the Empire's administration and military coordination but is not officially designated as an eighth Legion.
- The precise numerical strength of every Legion remains incompletely documented, and broad organizational estimates should not be mistaken for an exact census.
- No confirmed connection establishes War or the entire [[Heralds of Chaos]] race as members of the Seventh Legion. The [[Children of the Abyss]] retain their classified personnel and command structure.
