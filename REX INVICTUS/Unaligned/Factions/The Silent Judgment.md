The Silent Judgment is the Sixth Legion of the [[Elderblood Empire]] and its principal organization responsible for the public enforcement of Imperial law. Specializing in judgment, execution, punishment, the suppression of rebellion and the cleansing of corrupted territories, the Legion serves as the Empire's overt instrument of judicial and coercive authority. Its mandate extends throughout Imperial territory, particularly newly acquired worlds where the laws and authority of [[Victor Elderblood]] must be established and maintained.[1]
The Silent Judgment was founded around Year 20 of the [[Imperial Calendar]] through the combined efforts of [[Ariel Elderblood]], [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]], Nyx Elderblood, [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]] and [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]]. It was one of the first two Imperial Legions established during the Empire's accelerated development, preceding the formation of several organizations bearing lower numerical designations. Its creation provided the rapidly expanding Empire with a centralized institution capable of imposing Imperial law upon newly incorporated territories and responding to threats against domestic stability.[1]
The Legion is commanded by [[Jeanne Elderblood]], with Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos serving as her designated substitute whenever Jeanne is unavailable. Its personnel include elite soldiers, executioners, inquisitors, mind readers and empathic beings capable of perceiving emotions. These specialists collectively support the Legion's responsibilities in investigating misconduct, identifying threats, applying judgment and enforcing the consequences of violating Imperial authority.[1]
The Silent Judgment maintains a particularly close relationship with the Emperor's Black Hand, the Third Imperial Legion responsible for intelligence gathering, espionage, infiltration and clandestine operations. Together, the two organizations provide complementary forms of Imperial security. The Black Hand operates beyond public observation, while the Silent Judgment carries out visible enforcement of Imperial law. Their relationship is commonly characterized as the distinction between justice in the shadows and justice in the light.[1]
Unlike the First Legion or the Second Legion, whose principal responsibilities concern offensive and defensive military operations, the Silent Judgment is primarily an institution of internal security, territorial administration and public enforcement. Its military capabilities support the establishment of Imperial authority, the punishment of hostile individuals and the restoration of order in territories affected by corruption or rebellion.[1]

The Silent Judgment occupies a central position within the Elderblood Empire's internal security structure. Its purpose is to ensure that territories incorporated into the Empire remain subject to the Emperor's laws, that individuals violating Imperial authority can be punished and that populations or factions engaging in rebellion can be brought under control.[1]
The Legion's designation as an instrument of justice reflects its role in enforcing the legal and political order established by Victor. Its authority is exercised through the Empire's own system of government rather than through an independent legal institution possessing sovereignty separate from the Emperor.
The organization's functions extend beyond conventional policing. Its mandate includes the judgment of civilizations, the application of absolute Imperial laws and the cleansing of entire planets considered corrupted or rebellious. These responsibilities distinguish it from ordinary local law-enforcement organizations and demonstrate the enormous territorial and political scope of its authority.[1]
The Silent Judgment's powers should nevertheless be distinguished from those of the [[Regulators of Existence]] or the Judges of the Abyss. The Sixth Legion enforces the laws of the Elderblood Empire upon living civilizations and their territories; it is not the cosmic institution responsible for the universal judgment, administration and distribution of Souls after death.

### History

#### Volume 9: Those Who Rule Reality
The foundations of the Silent Judgment emerged during the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s preparations for large-scale conflict against civilizations and supreme supernatural authorities inhabiting the greater Cosmos. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] recognized that the Empire required far greater technological, military and administrative development before confronting the civilizations existing beyond his own Sector. To provide sufficient time for this development, he initiated a vast temporal ritual intended to grant the Empire approximately 200,000 years of internal development while only one year elapsed beyond the affected region.[2]
The ritual accelerated the development of an entire galactic civilization rather than merely the passage of subjective Time experienced by its inhabitants. Planets continued evolving, populations expanded, infrastructure was constructed and supernatural beings developed their abilities under the altered temporal conditions. Victor and his Empresses remained actively involved through their Avatars, supervising major political, technological and military decisions while their principal bodies supported the ritual.[2][3]
Among Victor's principal reforms was the creation of Seven Imperial Legions, each assigned a different responsibility within the Empire's expanding military and administrative establishment. The arrangement was intended to eliminate conflicts arising from competing command structures while preserving the cultural identities of the various Pantheons and civilizations incorporated into the Empire. Independent organizations could continue maintaining their traditions, but military authority ultimately belonged to the Emperor and the unified Imperial leadership.[2]
The need for institutions capable of enforcing Imperial authority became particularly significant as the Empire's territory expanded. The integration of new populations and previously independent civilizations required more than military conquest. Laws had to be established, political stability maintained and threats involving corruption, rebellion or hostile factions addressed before they could undermine the Empire's larger objectives.
The Sixth Legion, the Silent Judgment, became one of the first major institutions created to fulfill these responsibilities. Founded around Year 20 of the [[Imperial Calendar]] by Ariel, Kaguya, Nyx, Leona and Tasha, it was responsible for applying Imperial law throughout newly acquired territories and maintaining order within the Empire. Its establishment provided the Emperor's government with a dedicated organization possessing both judicial and military enforcement functions.[1]
The Silent Judgment's founding doctrine emphasized execution, punishment and the cleansing of corrupted or rebellious planets. Its responsibilities included judging offenders and civilizations, applying absolute Imperial laws and eradicating heresy. The organization was therefore intended to enforce Imperial authority decisively rather than functioning merely as an advisory legal institution.[1]
Its personnel were drawn from several specialized categories suited to public enforcement and investigation. Elite soldiers supplied military strength, executioners performed punitive functions, inquisitors supported the identification and suppression of prohibited activities, and mind readers and empathic beings provided supernatural methods of obtaining information or assessing individuals.[1]
Although the Legion's five founders established its purpose and initial structure, [[Jeanne Elderblood]] assumed its principal command. [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]] became the designated substitute commander, responsible for assuming leadership whenever Jeanne was occupied or otherwise unavailable.[1]
Shortly after the Silent Judgment's foundation, Kaguya, Nyx and [[Persephone Elderblood]] established the Third Legion, the Black Hand of the Emperor. The new organization was designed to operate clandestinely, handling intelligence gathering, espionage, infiltration, psychological warfare and other activities requiring secrecy. Its creation complemented the Silent Judgment by providing the Empire with a means of identifying and disrupting threats beyond the reach of public enforcement.[1]
The two Legions subsequently became the central counterparts of the Empire's early internal security system. Their relationship resembled that between a public police or enforcement organization and a secret intelligence service. The Black Hand could operate in the shadows, while the Silent Judgment publicly enforced the authority of the Empire.[1]
The creation of these organizations preceded the establishment of several other Legions. Around Year 50, the [[Stellar Arsenal]] emerged as the Fourth Legion, consolidating advanced scientific, magical and technological research. [[The Imperial Guard]] was expanded into the Fifth Legion to provide specialized protection for the Imperial Family and important sacred locations. The First Legion and Second Legion were later established around Year 1000 to fulfill the Empire's primary offensive and defensive military responsibilities.[1]
The Silent Judgment consequently retained its place as one of the Empire's earliest specialized organizations. Although it bears the Sixth numerical designation, its foundation predates the formal establishment of several other Imperial Legions.

### Purpose and Responsibilities
The Silent Judgment exists to enforce Imperial law and preserve the political and social order established under [[Victor Elderblood]]. Its responsibilities encompass public judgment, punishment, execution, the suppression of rebellious territories and the elimination of corruption or heresy threatening Imperial authority.[1]
The Legion's mandate is particularly important within newly acquired territories. Military conquest or incorporation into the Empire does not automatically ensure that a population will obey Imperial law, abandon hostile political structures or accept the authority of its new sovereign. The Silent Judgment provides an institution responsible for addressing these problems through the application of Imperial law and punitive enforcement.

#### Imperial Law Enforcement
Imperial law enforcement is the Silent Judgment's principal responsibility. The Legion applies the Emperor's laws to territories under Imperial jurisdiction, including worlds newly incorporated into the [[Elderblood Empire]].[1]
Its authority is tied directly to the Empire's centralized system of government. The laws it enforces originate from the political and legal structure established beneath [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] rather than from the independent traditions of every previously sovereign civilization.
This responsibility makes the Legion an instrument of administrative consolidation as well as military security. Its purpose is not simply to defeat individuals who resist the Empire, but to ensure that the legal order imposed upon a territory remains effective after Imperial authority has been established.
The complete contents of the Imperial legal code, its administrative subdivisions and the procedures through which local law is reconciled with Imperial law have not been individually described. The Legion's responsibility for applying those laws, however, is explicitly established.[1]

#### Judgment
The judgment of offenders and civilizations constitutes one of the Silent Judgment's principal functions. Its mandate extends beyond the prosecution of individual crimes to situations involving populations or political organizations considered corrupted, rebellious or otherwise opposed to Imperial authority.[1]
This broad responsibility reflects the scale of the Empire. A civilization possessing immense territorial resources and supernatural capabilities requires institutions capable of addressing threats at several levels, from individual offenders to entire worlds.
The Silent Judgment possesses personnel suited to supporting investigative and judicial activities, including inquisitors, mind readers and empathic beings. These specialists provide supernatural means of obtaining information or examining individuals, complementing the Legion's more conventional military personnel.[1]
The exact proceedings through which judgment is issued remain unspecified. The existence of judicial responsibilities and specialized personnel does not independently establish a particular tribunal structure, legal appeal system or standardized sentencing procedure.

#### Execution
Execution is an explicitly established function of the Silent Judgment. The Legion possesses executioners among its common personnel and is authorized to carry out punishments associated with violations of Imperial law.[1]
Its execution function distinguishes it from organizations limited to intelligence gathering or ordinary military confrontation. Whereas the Emperor's Black Hand specializes in clandestine operations, the Silent Judgment is equipped to carry out visible punitive action under the authority of the Empire.
The precise conditions under which execution is imposed have not been comprehensively documented. Its mandate confirms the existence of capital punishment as one of its enforcement functions, but does not establish that every legal violation automatically results in execution.

#### Punishment
The application of punishment forms another central aspect of the Legion's responsibilities. The Silent Judgment is authorized to enforce consequences against offenders and hostile factions that violate Imperial law or resist the authority of the Empire.[1]
Punishment in this context extends beyond conventional battlefield defeat. It concerns the consequences imposed after conduct has been judged incompatible with Imperial law, including offenses involving corruption, rebellion and heresy.
The organization therefore combines military enforcement with a punitive mandate. Elite soldiers and executioners provide the means to carry out such decisions, while investigative specialists support the identification of individuals or groups subjected to judgment.
The complete range of punishments available to the Legion remains unidentified. Execution is explicitly established, but other possible sanctions should not be treated as confirmed without direct evidence.

#### Suppression of Rebellion
The suppression of rebellion is one of the principal responsibilities of the Silent Judgment. The Legion is tasked with cleansing planets or territories where rebellion threatens Imperial authority, allowing the Empire to respond to resistance within regions already subject to its rule.[1]
This responsibility differs from the military conquest of previously independent civilizations. [[The Spartans]] specialize in defeating external enemies and acquiring territory, while the Silent Judgment possesses a mandate to restore order when Imperial authority is challenged within territory under its jurisdiction.
The Legion's military personnel allow it to address rebellion through more than investigative or administrative measures. Its responsibilities explicitly include punitive intervention against rebellious planets, giving the organization a role in large-scale internal security operations.[1]
The precise distinction between rebellion, dissent and other violations of Imperial law has not been defined through a complete legal code. Consequently, the Legion's anti-rebellion mandate should not be expanded into an invented classification of offenses or punishments.

#### Cleansing of Corrupted Territories
The cleansing of corrupted planets is among the Silent Judgment's explicitly established functions. Its mandate encompasses territories considered sufficiently corrupted to require punitive or coercive intervention by Imperial authorities.[1]
The term cleansing identifies one of the Legion's authorized enforcement activities rather than a single fully described supernatural technique. The exact actions involved may depend upon the threat being addressed, but the available description does not establish one universal procedure employed against every corrupted world.
The distinction is important because corruption within the setting can take numerous forms, including political misconduct, hostile supernatural influence and the destabilization of established authority. The Legion's mandate refers to corrupted territories in the context of Imperial law enforcement, but does not identify every possible category of corruption as an identical offense.
The Silent Judgment's role should therefore be understood as a confirmed responsibility for dealing with corrupted territories, without assuming that all such operations necessarily involve the destruction of an entire planet or population.

#### Eradication of Heresy
The eradication of heresy is a defining responsibility of the Silent Judgment and the subject of its internal slogan. The Legion is charged with identifying and eliminating heresy under the authority of Imperial law.[1]
This responsibility gives the organization an explicitly inquisitorial character. Inquisitors are among its commonly identified personnel, while its mandate includes the application of absolute laws and the eradication of prohibited beliefs or conduct classified as heretical by Imperial authority.
However, the existence of a heresy-eradication mandate does not establish that the Silent Judgment possesses exclusive authority over every religious institution in the Empire. Nor does it automatically make the Legion identical to the historic Inquisition or the Empire's other religious organizations.
The exact definition of heresy under Imperial law, including the distinction between prohibited religious conduct and other forms of political or supernatural disobedience, has not been completely established.

#### Maintenance of Imperial Order
The Silent Judgment's responsibilities ultimately serve the preservation of order throughout the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Its mandate expressly includes maintaining stability at whatever cost the Imperial enforcement system considers necessary.[1]
This purpose connects the Legion's individual functions into a single institutional mission. Judgment identifies offenses, investigative personnel support the discovery of threats, punishment enforces the law and military forces provide the means to restore Imperial authority where it has been challenged.
The organization therefore serves as more than a conventional police force. It is a specialized Imperial Legion empowered to act against threats ranging from individual misconduct to widespread rebellion affecting entire planets.

### Jurisdiction and Authority
The Silent Judgment possesses an Empire-wide enforcement mandate, with particular responsibility for territories newly incorporated into the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Its authority is derived from the Emperor's political and military sovereignty rather than from an independent Divine office.[2][1]
Its jurisdiction encompasses the application of Imperial law, the punishment of offenders and the suppression of conduct considered incompatible with the authority of the Empire. Unlike the cosmic judgment exercised by the [[Regulators of Existence]], its functions are directed primarily toward the governance and security of living civilizations.

#### Newly Acquired Territories
Newly acquired territories are a central concern of the Silent Judgment. Its establishment was specifically connected to the need to apply Imperial law wherever the Empire extended its authority.[1]
The incorporation of a civilization into the Empire creates political and legal responsibilities extending beyond the initial military campaign. The new territory must be brought under Imperial authority, and resistance or corruption threatening that authority must be addressed.
The Silent Judgment supplies the enforcement capability necessary for this process. Its combination of elite soldiers, investigative specialists and punitive personnel allows it to operate as a visible representative of Imperial law within such regions.
Its exact procedures for assuming responsibility over newly incorporated planets remain unknown. No complete sequence of occupation, inspection, prosecution or administrative transfer has been established.

#### Absolute Imperial Laws
The Legion is responsible for applying absolute laws, reflecting the supreme authority attributed to the Empire's legal system.[1]
Within the [[Elderblood Empire]], political authority is centralized beneath [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and the Imperial Family. Previously independent Pantheons and civilizations may preserve their cultural traditions, but their leadership must remain subject to the Emperor's commands whenever the unified Imperial system requires their cooperation.[2]
The Silent Judgment enforces this centralized legal order through its established public enforcement responsibilities.
The description of Imperial laws as absolute concerns their binding authority within the Empire. It should not be confused with metaphysical Laws of Existence or the Divine Authority governing the fundamental structure of Reality. The Legion's legal mandate does not independently grant its members unlimited Reality Manipulation.

#### Relationship with Imperial Sovereignty
The Silent Judgment ultimately serves [[Victor Elderblood]], whose status as Emperor provides the political foundation for its authority. Its command structure exists beneath the Imperial military establishment, with Jeanne exercising direct leadership and Leona authorized to assume command when necessary.[2][1]
The Legion therefore possesses considerable enforcement authority without functioning as an independent state or Pantheon. Its jurisdiction exists within the Empire's larger system of centralized government.

### Organization and Personnel
The Silent Judgment's personnel combine conventional military capabilities with investigative, judicial and supernatural specializations. Its most commonly identified units are elite soldiers, executioners, inquisitors, mind readers and empathic beings capable of perceiving emotions.[1]
This composition reflects the organization's broader responsibilities. Military personnel provide the force necessary to impose Imperial authority, executioners fulfill punitive duties, inquisitors support enforcement against heresy and prohibited conduct, and mentally oriented specialists supply additional methods of obtaining information.

#### Elite Soldiers
Elite soldiers form the conventional military component of the Silent Judgment. Their presence gives the organization the means to enforce Imperial law against threats that cannot be addressed through investigation or judicial authority alone.[1]
Unlike the Spartans, whose principal military responsibility concerns offensive campaigns against external enemies, the Silent Judgment's soldiers serve an enforcement organization. Their combat capabilities support punishment, public security and the suppression of rebellious or corrupted territories.
The exact racial composition, equipment, destructive capabilities and recruitment standards of these soldiers have not been individually established.
Their designation as elite personnel confirms a specialized military role, but does not establish that every member possesses the same individual combat strength as the Imperial Guard or other exceptionally powerful Imperial formations.

#### Executioners
Executioners are specialists responsible for carrying out the Legion's punitive functions. Their inclusion is consistent with the Silent Judgment's mandate to impose judgment and punishment under Imperial law.[1]
The organization possesses authority to undertake executions, distinguishing its enforcement responsibilities from those of ordinary administrative institutions.
The precise weapons, supernatural abilities and methods employed by its executioners remain unknown. Their role should therefore not be expanded into specific Soul-destruction techniques or other metaphysical punishments without direct confirmation.

#### Inquisitors
Inquisitors constitute one of the Silent Judgment's principal specialist categories. Their presence corresponds to the Legion's responsibility for identifying and eradicating heresy, investigating prohibited conduct and enforcing Imperial authority.[1]
The title reflects an inquisitorial function within the Empire's enforcement structure. However, the Silent Judgment's inquisitors should not automatically be treated as identical to every member of the historic Inquisition or the later Imperial religious organizations.
The exact relationship between the Sixth Legion's inquisitors and those separate religious institutions has not been fully established. Their common designation alone does not confirm identical training, leadership or organizational membership.

#### Mind Readers
Mind readers are supernatural specialists incorporated into the Silent Judgment's enforcement establishment. Their abilities allow them to examine thoughts or otherwise acquire mental information through supernatural means.[1]
Such abilities are relevant to an organization responsible for investigating offenses and identifying individuals whose intentions may be concealed. Mental information can supplement conventional investigative methods, providing the Legion with capabilities unavailable to ordinary mortal enforcement institutions.
The precise limits of the mind-reading abilities possessed by Silent Judgment personnel remain unspecified. Their inclusion does not establish that every member can perceive all thoughts, bypass every mental defense or obtain infallible knowledge of a person's guilt.

#### Empathic Beings
Empathic beings are another specialist category employed by the Legion. Their defining ability concerns the perception of emotions, distinguishing them from personnel whose powers involve directly examining thoughts.[1]
Emotional perception can provide information about an individual's psychological condition and reactions. In the context of Imperial enforcement, such information may be useful when examining people whose behavior requires investigation.
Nevertheless, sensing emotions is not necessarily equivalent to possessing perfect lie detection, omniscience or knowledge of every concealed intention. The exact strength and reliability of the Silent Judgment's empathic specialists have not been established.

#### Combined Enforcement Capabilities
The Legion's combination of conventional soldiers and supernatural specialists gives it capabilities extending beyond those of an ordinary police organization. Its personnel can employ combat power, investigative methods and punitive authority within the same institutional structure.[1]
The organization can therefore address different aspects of an enforcement operation through specialized personnel without relying entirely upon one category of supernatural ability.
Its exact methods of coordinating these units, including whether they operate through permanent mixed formations or separate departments, have not been documented.

#### Jeanne Elderblood
Main article: [[Jeanne Elderblood]]
Jeanne Elderblood is the principal commander of the Silent Judgment. Her position places her at the head of the Empire's public law-enforcement Legion, whose responsibilities include judgment, punishment, execution and the suppression of corrupted or rebellious territories.[1]
Jeanne possesses exceptional supernatural power and an extraordinary relationship with the greater structure of the native Universe. Her status within the Imperial Family makes her one of the most powerful individuals available to the Empire's centralized administration.
Her responsibilities as Legion Commander concern an organization whose authority extends across Imperial territory rather than a conventional military formation dedicated solely to battlefield conquest. The Silent Judgment's mandate requires the enforcement of laws, the maintenance of order and the application of punishment against individuals or civilizations violating Imperial authority.
Although Jeanne did not belong to the five named founders, she holds the highest established operational position within the Legion. Her appointment distinguishes the organization's initial creation from the subsequent arrangement of its command structure.[1]
The precise manner in which Jeanne directs individual investigations, judgments or executions has not been described. Her appointment establishes her command authority but should not be interpreted as confirmation that she personally presides over every proceeding conducted by the Sixth Legion.

#### Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos
Main article: [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]]
Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos is one of the Silent Judgment's five founders and its designated substitute commander. She possesses the authority to assume leadership whenever Jeanne is occupied or otherwise unable to exercise direct command.[1]
Leona's responsibilities place her in a particularly important position within the Empire's enforcement structure. Unlike the other founders who possess no separately identified permanent command office, Leona is explicitly recognized as the individual responsible for maintaining continuity in the Sixth Legion's leadership.
Her appointment reflects the Empire's practice of providing major Legions with trusted alternative commanders. This arrangement ensures that the organization can continue fulfilling its responsibilities even when its principal commander is engaged in another Imperial duty.
Leona's founding and substitute-command roles are both confirmed. However, no separate judicial title, specialized inquisitorial rank or exclusive enforcement department has been established for her beyond those responsibilities.[1]

#### Ariel Elderblood
Main article: [[Ariel Elderblood]]
Ariel Elderblood is one of the five founders responsible for establishing the Silent Judgment. Her participation reflects the involvement of powerful Divine members of the Imperial Family in the development of its legal and military institutions.[1]
Ariel possesses extensive experience with organized supernatural military structures through her former position among the highest-ranking Angels of the [[Biblical Pantheon]]. Her background includes leadership responsibilities and a longstanding association with forces concerned with Divine authority and religious order.
These experiences provide a relevant background for her participation in establishing a Legion dedicated to public enforcement. Nevertheless, her personal history should be distinguished from the Silent Judgment's documented personnel and procedures.
Ariel's confirmed position within the Sixth Legion is that of a founder. She is not identified as its principal commander or designated substitute.[1]

#### Kaguya Elderblood Blank
Main article: [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]]
Kaguya Elderblood Blank participated in the creation of the Silent Judgment alongside Ariel, Nyx, Leona and Tasha. Her involvement is especially significant because she subsequently became one of the founders and the principal commander of the Emperor's Black Hand.[1]
Kaguya's participation in both organizations creates a direct historical connection between the Empire's public enforcement and clandestine intelligence institutions. The Silent Judgment operates openly, while the Black Hand handles espionage, infiltration and other covert responsibilities.
Although Kaguya was involved in establishing the Sixth Legion, her confirmed permanent command position belongs to the Third Legion. She should therefore be identified as a founder of the Silent Judgment rather than as its current operational commander.[1]

#### Nyx Elderblood
Main article: Nyx Elderblood
Nyx Elderblood is another founding member of the Silent Judgment who also participated in the establishment of the Black Hand. Her involvement in both organizations contributed to the creation of the Empire's complementary public and clandestine security institutions.[1]
Nyx subsequently became the designated substitute commander of the Third Legion, assuming its command whenever Kaguya is unavailable. Her established command responsibilities therefore concern the Black Hand rather than the Silent Judgment.[1]
Her presence among the founders of the Sixth Legion nevertheless confirms her involvement in the earliest development of the Empire's centralized law-enforcement system.

#### Tasha Fenrir Elderblood
Main article: [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]]
Tasha Fenrir Elderblood is one of the Silent Judgment's five founders. Her participation connects the Legion to a member of the Imperial Family possessing extensive experience with supernatural politics, leadership and the administration of Werewolf society.[1]
Tasha's background as a former Egyptian Goddess and leader of powerful Werewolf factions provides her with considerable experience in matters involving authority, loyalty and political stability. Her participation in the creation of a Legion responsible for enforcing Imperial law is consistent with those broader responsibilities.
Her confirmed association with the Sixth Legion is its foundation. No permanent command office, specialized judicial subdivision or individual enforcement rank has been established for her.[1]

### Relationship with the Emperor's Black Hand
Main article: Emperor's Black Hand
The Emperor's Black Hand is the Third Imperial Legion and the principal clandestine counterpart to the Silent Judgment. Both organizations were established during the early development of the [[Elderblood Empire]], with the Silent Judgment appearing first and the Black Hand being founded shortly afterward.[1]
Their respective mandates establish two complementary approaches to maintaining Imperial security. The Silent Judgment openly enforces Imperial law through judgment, punishment and the suppression of prohibited activities, while the Black Hand operates covertly through intelligence gathering, espionage, infiltration and psychological warfare.[1]

#### Cooperative Enforcement
The cooperation between the two Legions allows the Empire to address threats requiring both intelligence and public enforcement. Covert information gathering can reveal hostile activities, while public enforcement can impose Imperial authority after a threat has been identified.
Their association is reinforced by their founding histories. Kaguya and Nyx participated in establishing the Silent Judgment before joining [[Persephone]] in founding the Black Hand, creating a direct organizational connection between the two institutions.[1]
Despite this relationship, the two Legions remain separate organizations. Jeanne commands the Silent Judgment, while Kaguya commands the Black Hand. Their complementary responsibilities do not place one Legion permanently beneath the authority of the other.

#### The Imperial Guard
Main article: [[The Imperial Guard]]
The Imperial Guard is the Fifth Legion and the Empire's principal force dedicated to the immediate protection of the Imperial Family and important sacred locations. Its personnel are selected for exceptional individual strength, and its organization differs considerably from the general enforcement duties of the Silent Judgment.[1]
The Sixth Legion's responsibility is broader in legal and territorial scope. It applies Imperial law to civilizations and territories, while the Guard concentrates upon protecting individuals and locations whose security is of exceptional importance to the Emperor.
The existence of separate Legions for these duties prevents the Empire from depending upon its elite household protectors for every routine or large-scale enforcement responsibility.

#### The Spartans
Main article: [[The Spartans]]
The First Legion constitutes the Empire's primary offensive military force. Its responsibilities include direct assault, invasion, planetary conquest and the destruction of hostile military defenses.[1]
The Silent Judgment's mandate begins from a different organizational purpose. Rather than functioning as the principal army responsible for acquiring territory, it applies Imperial law and suppresses corruption or rebellion within territories subject to Imperial authority.
These responsibilities are complementary. Military conquest establishes or expands Imperial control, while public enforcement addresses the preservation of the legal order governing the resulting territories.
The exact procedures through which a conquered planet is transferred between military and administrative authorities remain unspecified.

#### The Order of the Iron Veil
Main article: [[Order of the Iron Veil]]
The Order of the Iron Veil is the Second Legion and the Empire's principal conventional defensive organization. Its responsibilities include planetary defense, fortified zones, bastions, shields and the protection of established Imperial territories.[4]
The distinction between the [[Iron]] Veil and the Silent Judgment concerns the difference between military defense and legal enforcement. The Iron Veil protects territory against hostile military incursions, while the Silent Judgment enforces Imperial law and suppresses threats involving corruption, rebellion or heresy.
The two organizations therefore address different aspects of the Empire's security requirements, although both contribute to the preservation of Imperial authority.

### Relationship with Religious Institutions
The Silent Judgment possesses an explicitly inquisitorial mandate through its responsibility for eradicating heresy and employing inquisitors among its common personnel.[1]
This relationship with religious enforcement should be understood within the broader structure of the [[Elderblood Empire]], which incorporates numerous former Pantheons and supernatural religious traditions beneath the sovereignty of [[Victor Elderblood]].
The preservation of cultural and religious identities does not make those organizations politically independent from the Emperor. Their leaders remain subject to Imperial authority when the unified command structure requires their cooperation.[2]

#### Imperial Inquisition
Main article: Inquisition
The Silent Judgment should be distinguished from the broader Inquisition and its Imperial successors. Although both employ inquisitorial terminology and possess functions associated with religious authority, the Sixth Legion is specifically established as an Imperial law-enforcement organization.[1]
Its responsibility for eradicating heresy confirms an inquisitorial function, but does not establish that every historical or Imperial inquisitor automatically belongs to the Silent Judgment.
Likewise, the existence of a separate religious hierarchy does not by itself place the Sixth Legion under the command of that hierarchy. The confirmed operational commander of the Silent Judgment is [[Jeanne Elderblood]], with Leona serving as her substitute.[1]
Any more detailed institutional relationship between the Legion and the Empire's religious organizations remains unidentified.

#### Judgment of Souls
Main article: [[The Judges of The Abyss]]
The Silent Judgment's name and judicial responsibilities do not make it equivalent to the Judges of the Abyss, whose cosmic function concerns the administration and judgment of Souls.
The distinction lies in jurisdiction. The Silent Judgment exercises Imperial legal authority over living individuals, organizations and civilizations, while the Judges of the Abyss belong to the greater supernatural administration of the afterlife.
The Sixth Legion's power to sentence or execute offenders should therefore not automatically be interpreted as direct authority over the complete reincarnation system or the metaphysical destination of every Soul.

#### Scale of Operations
The Legion's responsibilities extend across Imperial territory, including entire planets affected by corruption or rebellion. This establishes an operational mandate far broader than the policing of a single city or region.[1]
The organization's authority to intervene against planetary threats does not establish that every member possesses planet-destroying powers. Its enforcement capabilities arise from the combined military and supernatural resources of the Legion rather than one uniform destructive level shared by its personnel.
Similarly, the exceptional power of Jeanne and the Imperial Family does not establish an identical combat level for the Legion's ordinary soldiers or inquisitors.

#### Personnel Strength
The precise numerical strength of the Silent Judgment remains unknown. Its original organization includes several specialized categories of personnel, but no confirmed count has been established for the total number of soldiers, executioners, inquisitors, mind readers or empaths belonging to the Sixth Legion.[1]
The broader [[Elderblood Empire]] possesses enormous military resources, but its combined population and armed forces should not be automatically treated as the personnel strength of any single Legion.

#### Headquarters and Facilities
No definitive headquarters has been identified for the Silent Judgment. Its Empire-wide mandate confirms that it operates within the Imperial administrative and military structure, but the locations of its principal command facilities, judicial chambers, training installations and detention facilities remain unspecified.
The existence of executioners and inquisitors does not independently establish a particular prison system, named tribunal or dedicated execution world under its control.

### Operational Identity
The Silent Judgment represents the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s visible capacity to impose and enforce its laws. Its institutional identity rests upon the principle that Imperial authority must be supported by organizations capable of making judgments and carrying out their consequences.[1]
Its position complements the Empire's powerful military forces. While the Spartans can conquer territory and the [[Iron]] Veil can defend it, neither organization's primary purpose is the public judgment and punishment of individuals violating Imperial law. The Silent Judgment fills that role through specialized enforcement personnel and an explicitly judicial mandate.
The Legion also complements the clandestine intelligence capabilities of the Black Hand. Hidden threats may require investigation or covert interference, but an Empire exercising public sovereignty must also possess a visible institution through which its laws are applied.
The distinction is especially important within a civilization consisting of numerous races, Pantheons and previously independent societies. Imperial government requires a means of establishing a common legal authority without forcing every subordinate organization to perform identical responsibilities.
The Silent Judgment therefore functions as one of the Empire's central mechanisms of political and legal consolidation, ensuring that the expansion of Imperial territory is accompanied by the enforcement of the Emperor's laws.

- The Silent Judgment is the Sixth Imperial Legion but was one of the first two Legions established chronologically, appearing around Year 20 of the [[Imperial Calendar]].[1]
- It was founded before the Third Legion, the Black Hand, despite possessing a higher numerical designation.[1]
- The Legion has five confirmed founders: Ariel, Kaguya, Nyx, Leona and Tasha.[1]
- [[Jeanne Elderblood]] commands the organization but is not among its five named founders.[1]
- [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]] is both a founding member and the designated substitute commander.[1]
- Kaguya and Nyx participated in founding both the Sixth Legion and the Third Legion, establishing a direct historical relationship between the Empire's public enforcement and covert intelligence organizations.[1]
- The Silent Judgment's original purpose was to apply Imperial law within newly acquired territories.[1]
- Its principal functions include execution, punishment, the cleansing of corrupted or rebellious planets and the maintenance of Imperial order.[1]
- The Legion is explicitly responsible for judging, applying absolute laws and eradicating heresy.[1]
- Its known personnel include elite soldiers, executioners, inquisitors, mind readers and empathic beings capable of sensing emotions.[1]
- The organization usually operates alongside the Emperor's Black Hand, functioning as its visible public-enforcement counterpart.[1]
- The relationship between the two Legions is compared to that between a police force and a secret service.[1]
- The Silent Judgment's responsibility for eradicating heresy does not establish that it is identical to the historic Inquisition or that every Imperial inquisitor belongs to the Sixth Legion.
- Its name should not be confused with the cosmic judgment exercised by the Judges of the Abyss, whose responsibilities concern the administration of Souls.
- The precise personnel strength, headquarters, internal subdivisions and formal legal procedures of the Legion remain unidentified.
- The Legion's internal slogan is "Heresy will not be tolerated."[1]
