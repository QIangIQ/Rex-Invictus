The Varkhess League is a minor alliance of rulers in the Upper Sectors. It holds a number of systems near a border of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. In Chapter 1203 it is the first of the small powers to move against territory [[Victor Elderblood]] has just taken.[1]

### Overview
The League is not one of the four great factions. It is one of the minor powers scattered through the Upper Sectors that lack the means to face [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] directly. Like others of that kind, it acts from its own interest and looks for the shelter of a greater faction.[1]
Its own rulers are not named. The one leader the chapter puts on the page is a white-skinned alien with octopus-like eyes and six arms. That description is this captive's body. It is not stated as the species of the League.[1]
The money behind the seizure comes from [[Auralion]], a god. [[Kaleb Hunter]] identifies him as one of the chief gods of one of the four enemy factions. That faction is the one the System calls <Emperor>. The captives are being used. Their database has nothing on that faction beyond the public names of ten chief gods, each tied to an aspect of justice. Kaleb's own judgment is that the justice they preach depends on who is speaking, and that in the end these people are only enemies of the Emperor.[1]

### History

#### The three worlds
During the Imperial reorganization of conquered ground, the League's leaders decide to take three worlds only recently brought into the Empire. The planets have considerable mineral deposits and industrial installations. Full production has not been reached. From the Imperial side the ground is still empty. The Empire is not working the industries. It has only driven off the forces of <Darkness>.[1]
The League begins by taking the local administrative centers. It deposes the Imperial authorities and puts its own representatives in their place. It does not smash the infrastructure. The plant is to be kept and used for the occupation.[1]
Regional command answers with an expeditionary force. The invaders fight with the planetary defense installations and still cannot stop the landing. After they lose the main cities and industrial centers, the League's soldiers abandon their positions. The three planets are taken back. The League's leaders are left facing Imperial retaliation.[1]

#### The retaliatory strike
The retaliation comes two days later. Conan leads a troop of several elite soldiers, each capable of annihilating entire solar systems. They storm the system and take everything in less than thirty minutes. There is hardly any resistance. The narration calls the League's choice foolish and driven by fear, and says that by it they lost everything.[1]
In the ruins of a destroyed capital, Conan and Kaleb look at the captive leader. Conan names [[Auralion]] as the one who financed them and throws the leader down before Kaleb. He then tells Kaleb to send the captive to "the girls," the unit of the B.H.E. that handles delicate matters. The chapter gives that unit's name as "Black Hand The Emperor" and places it as the Third Legion under [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]]. On this wiki that legion is [[The Black Hand of the Emperor]]. Kaleb tells Conan not to say that aloud. The chapter does not show the handover.[1]

### Relationship with the Elderblood Empire
The League tries to steal worlds the Empire has not yet begun to use. The answer is conventional reconquest, then a decapitation strike by solar-system-class elites under Conan.[1]
The wider chapter frames every loss the Empire shows in these operations as bait. The dead among the Imperial side are clones, already returned to the Emperor's galaxy to recover. The point of letting the small powers move is to learn who will actually stand against the Dragon Emperor. A small faction does not do that without considerable confidence in itself. Most act shortsightedly. Few are truly on the commission of the higher factions, and those few tend to have real influence. The League is one of the cases where the patron can be named.[1]
At the end of the chapter [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] decides to make an example of powers that strike the Empire because they think a greater faction's protection will cover them. She intends their worlds laid in rubble and ash. Kaguya says the Third Legion will work with the First Legion, [[The Spartans]]. The chapter ends on that order. It does not show the punishment carried out.[1]

### Current Status
The three worlds are back in Imperial hands. A system has been stormed, a capital is in ruins, and one leader is a captive with a weak mind, identified as such by Conan. The League is treated in the narration as having lost everything by that decision.[1]
What is not stated is the abolition of the League, the fate of its other rulers, the names of the three worlds, or the name of the system Conan takes. [[Auralion]]'s rank as a chief god of <Emperor> is stated. His presence at the battle is not.[1]

### Trivia
- The League is the first small faction in Chapter 1203 to act, ahead of the [[Nharok Consortium]], the [[Order of Sythra]], and an unnamed alliance of space pirates.[1]
- [[Auralion]] is the financier. He is not the six-armed captive, and he is not the envoy later called Justice.[1]
- The ten chief gods, each an aspect of justice, are what the League's side is said to know of <Emperor>. The chapter does not give the other nine names.[1]
- No earlier chapter on this wiki names the Varkhess League. This article uses only Chapter 1203.
