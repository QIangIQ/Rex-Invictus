#### First Appearance
Chapter 1126
The Skaarith Empire was a militaristic planetary civilization located in the southern region of the Andromeda Galaxy. Outwardly, the Empire appeared to be a highly disciplined warrior civilization ruled by a genetically superior Emperor whose people believed themselves to have transcended weakness, fear and ordinary mortality. In reality, the civilization had been extensively infiltrated and biologically altered by agents associated with the Upper Sectors, turning the entire planet into a long-term experiment designed around forced evolution through continuous warfare.[1]
The Empire's population was not itself originally an invading Hive species. The inhabitants were the natural natives of the planet, but their genetics had been altered so extensively by an engineered infection that the [[Elderblood Empire]] could no longer easily reconstruct their original biology. The ruling Emperor possessed Energy and physiology resembling the Hive-Mind organisms previously encountered in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s home galaxy, leading Imperial researchers to conclude that the planet's leadership consisted of foreign infiltrators manipulating the native population from within.[1]
The Skaarith civilization was ultimately destroyed during an Elderblood Imperial operation commanded by [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]]. Because the infection had irreversibly altered virtually the entire planetary population and represented an extreme biological threat, the Empire elected to strategically eliminate the infected civilization while preserving its dying World Tree. By saving the World Tree and the biological Records stored within it, Velnorah intended to preserve the possibility that the planet's original inhabitants could someday be recreated or naturally re-emerge without the corruption imposed upon them by the Upper-Sector faction.[1]

### Overview
The Skaarith Empire controlled most of its home planet before the Elderblood invasion. Its people lived under the absolute authority of a figure known simply as the Emperor, whom they revered as their Supreme Leader. Every morning included rituals performed in his honor, and the population credited him with genetically evolving their species beyond its original limitations.[1]
The Empire's official ideology claimed that its people had surpassed fear and mortality through centuries of biological improvement and warfare. Soldiers believed themselves superior to conventional beings and considered constant combat a natural part of existence. One soldier, [[Faharah-Imo]], described his people as warriors bred specifically to kill and wage war and stated that battle fervor was effectively the only strong emotion he regularly experienced.[1]
This confidence proved largely artificial. When Elderblood Dragonoids descended directly from orbit and effortlessly broke through the Empire's defenses, Skaarith soldiers experienced genuine fear despite claiming their civilization had eliminated the emotion. Their supposed evolutionary supremacy had been maintained largely because they had never encountered an outside civilization operating on the level of the [[Elderblood Empire]].[1]
The Empire was also not truly autonomous. Imperial investigation revealed that its entire civilization was effectively one component of a controlled experiment conducted by forces originating in the Upper Sectors. The Skaarith Emperor and at least one opposing ruler were apparently foreign agents deliberately forcing the planetary natives into continuous warfare so that conflict would accelerate their biological and technological development.[1]

### Planet
The Skaarith homeworld was a relatively young inhabited planet located within the southern region of the Andromeda Galaxy. Despite its youth, by the time the [[Elderblood Empire]] discovered it the planet was already approaching ecological and spiritual death.[1]
From orbit, Aline Valefar Elderblood noted that the world appeared unnaturally lifeless. Imperial scans subsequently discovered that the civilization was drawing Energy directly from the planetary core to power itself. This extraction disrupted the natural Energy cycle between the planet and its World Tree, gradually starving both systems.[1]
Normally, a World Tree initially nourishes its developing planet before the mature world begins returning and storing Energy in a mutually beneficial cycle. The Skaarith rulers were continuously removing Energy from the planetary core without allowing that Energy to return. As a result, the World Tree's vital signature had become extraordinarily weak and the planet itself was entering its final stages of life.[1]
Velnorah concluded that even if the Elderblood Empire had never intervened, the civilization would eventually have died alongside its planet once the Upper-Sector faction completed whatever "harvest" it had planned.[1]

### Inhabitants
The original inhabitants of the Skaarith homeworld were an unnamed extraterrestrial humanoid species possessing four arms. They were significantly smaller than the Dragonoid soldiers of the [[Elderblood Empire]] but were nevertheless physically formidable compared with ordinary mortal species.[1]
By the time of the Empire's arrival, however, virtually the entire population had been biologically altered. An engineered virus created or introduced by the foreign rulers had spread throughout the inhabitants and extensively rewritten their genetics. The virus increased their strength and contributed to their artificial evolution, but it was simultaneously extremely dangerous to both living organisms and the planet itself.[1]
The degree of genetic alteration was severe enough that Imperial artificial intelligences working with biological data from [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] and Velnorah could not simply reverse the mutations. Without preserved genetic information belonging to the species' ancestors, restoring the original population through ordinary Imperial biotechnology was considered impractical.[1]
Theoretically, [[Victor Elderblood]] could have reconstructed the species through his direct authority over Life and Souls even without complete genetic data, but he was occupied elsewhere at the time of the operation. The Empire therefore focused upon preserving the World Tree, whose Records contained information concerning every native race it had helped develop.[1]

#### Physical Characteristics
The known Skaarith inhabitants possessed a humanoid body plan but had four arms rather than two. Their bodies had been genetically enhanced for warfare and were durable enough that individuals could survive considerable blunt-force trauma from much larger opponents, although they remained overwhelmingly inferior to Imperial Dragonoids.[1]
Skaarith soldiers were trained extensively in close-quarters combat and ranged warfare. [[Faharah-Imo]] considered himself highly proficient in hand-to-hand combat and immediately attempted to close distance with an Imperial soldier after opening fire with his weapon.[1]
Their precise natural physiology before the Upper-Sector genetic modifications is unknown.

### Society and Culture

#### Warrior Civilization
Skaarith society was constructed almost entirely around warfare. Its soldiers explicitly described themselves as beings bred to kill and wage war. Daily life included ritual devotion to the Emperor followed by physical and military training intended to continually improve the individual.[1]
The population considered combat not merely an occupation but its primary cultural purpose. This mentality was not entirely natural, however; Imperial intelligence later revealed that their society had been deliberately manipulated into permanent conflict by the rulers overseeing the planetary experiment.[1]
The purpose of this endless warfare was accelerated development. The Upper-Sector infiltrators exploited the basic principle that sustained conflict forces civilizations to develop new weapons, biological adaptations and social structures more rapidly than civilizations experiencing prolonged peace.[1]

#### Emperor Worship
The Skaarith population treated their Emperor as the undisputed Supreme Leader of civilization. Soldiers performed daily rituals in his honor and credited him personally with the genetic advancement of their species.[1]
The Emperor was believed to be the strongest being within their civilization and represented the ideal toward which the Skaarith were expected to evolve. That belief collapsed when the Elderblood invasion demonstrated that ordinary Imperial Dragonoids already operated vastly beyond the standards of the Skaarith military.[1]
More importantly, the Emperor was not simply an exceptionally evolved member of the native species. Imperial scans determined that he possessed Energy and physiology resembling the Hive-Mind organisms previously discovered in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s galaxy. This strongly indicated that the ruler was one of the Upper-Sector spies responsible for corrupting the planet rather than an ordinary Skaarith native.[1]

#### The Northern Heretics
The Skaarith Empire did not control the entire planet. A rival population occupied the northern continent and was officially described by Skaarith soldiers as "Heretics".[1]
The rivalry was far more important than the inhabitants understood. Imperial investigators discovered evidence of two Emperors deliberately maintaining the planet in a permanent state of war. Rather than representing a genuine ideological conflict between independent civilizations, the struggle was most likely another component of the Upper-Sector experiment, with both leadership structures pushing the native inhabitants toward faster development through warfare.[1]
The exact identity, title and political organization of the northern ruler remain unknown.

### Genetic Experimentation
The Skaarith population represented an enormous planetary-scale biological experiment. Rather than allowing the inhabitants to evolve naturally, the infiltrating rulers infected the population with an artificial pathogen capable of altering their genetics and granting greater physical power.[1]
The resulting mutations were not simple enhancements. By the time the [[Elderblood Empire]] examined the species, their genes had diverged so severely from their ancestral state that even the Empire's advanced biological technology could not safely reconstruct the original species without additional data.[1]
This was especially significant because the Elderblood Empire was already capable of cloning living beings, modifying races and creating soldiers capable of retaining information obtained from previous cloned lives. The inability to easily repair the Skaarith demonstrated just how extensively the Upper-Sector faction had altered them.[1]
The infection was also not benign. Imperial analysis described it as extraordinarily dangerous to living beings and to planetary ecosystems. The Skaarith therefore gained strength at the cost of becoming genetically dependent upon an experiment that was ultimately destroying both themselves and their world.[1]

### Technology
The Skaarith Empire possessed technology that Velnorah's Operators described as simultaneously strangely advanced and extremely primitive. This contradiction was another indication that the civilization's technological development had been deliberately manipulated rather than progressing naturally.[1]

#### Weaponry
Skaarith soldiers possessed compact ranged weapons known as Skivart. These weapons could fire projectiles or Energy attacks and were standard equipment among Imperial soldiers such as [[Faharah-Imo]].[1]
The weapons were powerful by the standards of the native civilization but largely ineffective against the armor used by Elderblood Dragonoids. During the invasion, Skaarith projectiles simply bounced from Imperial armor or failed to inflict meaningful damage.[1]

#### Planetary Defenses
The Empire possessed automated planetary or military-base defenses capable of detecting descending enemies and firing multiple Energy beams against them. These systems successfully struck Imperial soldiers during orbital descent but were unable to seriously damage the attackers.[1]

#### Communications
Skaarith military units possessed equipment capable of rapidly relaying information and coordinating responses during attacks. The exact principles behind their communication technology are not explained.

#### Civilization Level
The Skaarith Empire is sometimes classified as a possible Type I Civilization due to its large-scale control and exploitation of planetary Energy. However, this classification remains uncertain. Its civilization had been artificially accelerated and was irresponsibly extracting Energy directly from its planetary core rather than demonstrating the stable and comprehensive planetary Energy management normally associated with a mature Type I civilization.[1]

### Upper-Sector Experiment
The [[Elderblood Empire]] encountered the Skaarith after scanners detected a foreign "trace" leading from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s galaxy toward the Andromeda Galaxy. The signal formed part of the Empire's investigation into agents and biological organisms apparently originating from the Upper Sectors.[1]
When Velnorah's fleet reached the planet, Imperial Operators concluded that the entire world was an experiment. Their strongest evidence came from the ruling Emperor, whose Energy signature and physiology closely resembled the Hive-Mind organisms previously discovered in the Milky Way.[1]
Unlike the earlier Hive planet, where the invading species had effectively taken over the entire world directly, the Skaarith case represented a more sophisticated infiltration strategy. The native population remained physically present and continued believing itself independent, while only the leadership appeared to consist of foreign spies.[1]
The infiltrators then used genetic manipulation, planetary resource exploitation and manufactured warfare to accelerate the civilization's development. Velnorah interpreted their strategy as a form of selective harvesting: stronger individuals produced by centuries of conflict could be preserved as valuable "products" when the Sector barriers eventually opened, while weaker inhabitants could simply be discarded or consumed.[1]
This made the Skaarith Empire fundamentally different from a conventional sovereign state. Its rulers were cultivating the civilization for the benefit of an outside faction rather than for the native population.

### Elderblood Invasion
After detecting the Upper-Sector trail, [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]] personally commanded an Elderblood expeditionary fleet into the southern region of Andromeda.[1]
Hundreds of Imperial Dragonoids descended from orbit directly onto Skaarith military installations. Their arrival immediately shattered the natives' belief that they had evolved beyond fear. [[Faharah-Imo]] and the warriors around him experienced genuine terror for the first time after hearing the roar of the descending Dragonoids and witnessing their defenses fail.[1]
The technological and biological difference between the two civilizations was overwhelming. Skaarith soldiers opened fire, attempted close combat and coordinated attacks against the invaders, but their weapons generally failed to penetrate Imperial armor. Dragonoids moved directly through Skaarith formations, killing soldiers through physical force and advanced Imperial weaponry with little meaningful resistance.[1]
Faharah-Imo survived only because Imperial scanners detected a foreign "trace" upon him. Rather than execute him with the other soldiers, the Dragonoids placed him into containment for study.[1]
The battle was not intended as an ordinary territorial conquest. Velnorah's primary objectives were to locate Upper-Sector infiltrators, capture individuals displaying foreign traces and prevent the hostile biological experiment from spreading beyond the planet.[1]
When Imperial biological analysis determined that the general population had been mutated beyond what could be safely repaired with the data available, Velnorah ordered the Dragonoids to capture all useful "traces" and strategically eliminate the remaining infected population. Operators were authorized to employ orbital bombardment while Imperial artificial intelligences synchronized with soldiers on the surface to prevent friendly casualties.[1]

### The World Tree
While the military extermination proceeded, Velnorah focused personally upon locating the planet's World Tree. Imperial scanners detected an extraordinarily weak vital signature, initially surprising both Velnorah and Aline because the planet was far too young to be dying naturally.[1]
Further examination revealed that the Skaarith civilization was extracting Energy directly from the planet's core. This process destroyed the normal symbiotic Energy cycle between World Tree and planet, leaving the Tree increasingly unable to sustain either itself or the world surrounding it.[1]
Velnorah refused to allow the World Tree to die even though she had authorized the destruction of the infected civilization. Her decision was partly strategic and partly humanitarian. [[World Trees]] preserve the Records of the races and civilizations they help nurture; as long as the Tree survived, the true ancestral inhabitants of the planet could theoretically return someday.[1]
Under natural conditions, restoring an extinct planetary population from a World Tree's Records could require thousands of years. With [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s assistance, Velnorah believed the process could potentially be accelerated dramatically.[1]
The preservation of the Tree therefore gave the Skaarith homeworld a possible future even after the extermination of its corrupted civilization.

### The Truth of the Skaarith Empire
The Skaarith Empire was ultimately neither a naturally evolved warrior civilization nor a genuine independent galactic power. It was a planetary laboratory whose inhabitants had been manipulated by outsiders they worshiped as rulers.[1]
The experiment operated through several interconnected mechanisms:
- Foreign Leadership: The Skaarith Emperor displayed the same type of Energy and physiology associated with the earlier Hive-Mind incursion, strongly suggesting that he was an Upper-Sector infiltrator rather than a native ruler.[1]
- Planetary Genetic Infection: An engineered virus had spread across the population, granting strength while drastically rewriting their biology.[1]
- Artificial Permanent Warfare: Two opposing rulers deliberately maintained constant conflict between the Skaarith Empire and the northern population because warfare accelerated technological and biological development.[1]
- Selective Harvesting: The strongest individuals were apparently being identified and preserved for a future period when the Sector barriers opened, while the weaker majority were considered expendable.[1]
- Planetary Exploitation: Energy was extracted directly from the planetary core even though this process was slowly killing the World Tree and the world itself.[1]
- Disposable Civilization: Velnorah concluded that the faction responsible did not care whether the planet or its people survived after fulfilling their purpose, comparing its behavior to an endlessly consuming glutton.[1]
The Skaarith case became one of the clearest pieces of evidence available to the [[Elderblood Empire]] that Upper-Sector factions were already planting agents, experiments and proxy civilizations throughout lower regions long before direct contact between Sectors became possible.

### Trivia
- The Skaarith Empire was located in the southern region of the Andromeda Galaxy.[1]
- [[Faharah-Imo]] had served the Empire for approximately 400 Cycles, although Chapter 1126 never establishes how long one Skaarith Cycle lasts.[1]
- The previous claim that Skaarith lived approximately 500 Earth years is therefore currently unsupported.
- Native Skaarith soldiers possessed four arms.[1]
- They referred to the invading Imperial Dragonoids as "Akajaa". The exact meaning of this native term is unknown.[1]
- Despite claiming their civilization had eliminated fear, Faha and his fellow soldiers immediately experienced terror when confronted with the vastly superior Dragonoids.[1]
- The Skaarith Emperor possessed Energy and physiology similar to the Hive-Mind organism previously found in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s home galaxy.[1]
- The chapter does not state that the entire native species itself was a Hive Mind. Only the leadership was believed to consist of foreign infiltrators.[1]
- The exact Upper-Sector faction controlling the planet is currently unnamed.
- The old page's association with a God named Ion is not supported by Chapter 1126 and should not be included unless another chapter explicitly establishes the connection.
- Likewise, Chapter 1126 does not identify the native four-armed species by the name [[Ahishahu]]. Until that name can be tied to an explicit source, the inhabitants should be described generically.
- The planet's technology was described as simultaneously advanced and primitive, suggesting artificial technological uplift rather than natural progression.[1]
- The virus infecting the population was considered extremely dangerous to other living beings and even to the planet itself.[1]
- The Skaarith civilization was extracting Energy directly from its planetary core, gradually killing both the planet and its World Tree.[1]
- Velnorah prioritized saving the World Tree because it retained the Records of the planet's original races and could allow them to be restored in the future.[1]
- The Elderblood operation was commanded by [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]], with Aline Valefar Elderblood temporarily assuming command while Velnorah searched for the World Tree.[1]
- The population extermination was not carried out simply because the [[Elderblood Empire]] considered the Skaarith hostile. Their genes had been catastrophically altered by an Upper-Sector pathogen, and the infection was considered a major biological threat.[1]
- The Skaarith incident strongly resembled the earlier Hive-Mind infestation but differed in one critical respect: on the earlier planet, the foreign organism directly dominated the world, whereas the Skaarith planet retained its native population while foreign agents controlled its leadership.[1]
