Clan Horseman was a powerful Noble Vampire family formerly led by Niklaus Horseman. For a brief period, the Clan rose to the highest echelon of [[Nightingale]]'s Vampire aristocracy when Niklaus defeated Annasthashia "Natashia" Fulger and took the [[Fulger Clan]]'s position as a Vampire Count. The Clan's rise was therefore unusually rapid and politically controversial, as its Count status was not inherited through an ancient Count bloodline but acquired through Nightingale's formal game system.
Behind its public status, Clan Horseman was also involved in considerably more dangerous activities. Niklaus possessed extensive knowledge of artificial Hybrids, maintained experimental subjects and cooperated with individuals such as Lucy and Karen, whose attempt to forcibly transform themselves from Plebeian Vampires into Noble Vampires brought them into direct contact with Niklaus' sons [[Einer Horseman]] and [[Zwei Horseman]].[1][2]
The Clan's political ascent lasted only a relatively short period. Its possession of the Count title produced conflict with both the remnants of Clan Fulger and [[Victoria Fulger Elderblood|Victoria Rider]], culminating first in the highly publicized Game Between Clan Rider and Clan Horseman and later in Natashia's direct rematch against Niklaus. The second confrontation ended with Niklaus relinquishing his Count status, restoring Clan Fulger to its former place among Nightingale's highest Vampire families.[3][4][5]
Although the Horseman bloodline itself survived through Einer, Zwei and Jessica, the Horseman Clan as a major independent political power effectively collapsed after Niklaus' defeat, evacuation of its personnel and eventual abandonment of its position in Nightingale. Its former leader was later revealed to possess a much more complicated identity and history as Radu Tepes, making Clan Horseman only one portion of a far older conspiracy surrounding him.

### Overview
Clan Horseman was not one of [[Nightingale]]'s ancient traditional Count families. Its political significance came primarily from the actions of [[Niklaus Horseman]], who deliberately challenged Clan Fulger and used Nightingale's game system to acquire its Count title.
The Clan therefore passed through three broad political stages:

↓

↓

↓

↓

↓

Unlike older Clans whose identities revolved around one well-established inherited Power, the Horseman family members shown in the novel possess noticeably different abilities. Niklaus' Powers, Einer's black thorn-based ability and the still-unrevealed personal abilities of Zwei should therefore not be compressed into a single generic "Horseman Power" unless the novel explicitly establishes a common hereditary source.

### History

#### Before Victor Elderblood
The exact date of Clan Horseman's formation has not been revealed. What is known is that the family existed under the leadership of [[Niklaus Horseman]] before [[Victor Elderblood]] became a Vampire and had already accumulated enough resources, personnel and political influence to challenge one of [[Nightingale]]'s three [[Vampire Counts]].
Niklaus' family included his twin sons [[Zwei Horseman]] and [[Einer Horseman]] and his daughter Jessica Horseman. [[Alienova Constantine]] is also associated with the family through her relationship to Niklaus and their children.
Niklaus was considerably more dangerous than an ordinary ambitious Noble Vampire. His public identity concealed a much older history, while his Clan maintained knowledge regarding subjects that were considered taboo or exceptionally difficult even within supernatural society, including the artificial creation of Hybrids.[2]

#### Rise to Count Status
Shortly before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s transformation into a Vampire, Niklaus challenged Natashia Fulger through [[Nightingale]]'s formal game system.
The challenge carried unusually high stakes: Natashia's position as a Vampire Count was placed on the line. Niklaus also deliberately attracted her interest by revealing that he possessed access to a Hybrid, knowing that a stable combination of supernatural races was an extraordinarily rare and controversial subject.
Natashia accepted.
The circumstances surrounding the original match favored Niklaus, and he successfully defeated the Fulger leader. As a consequence, Natashia lost the political title that had defined Clan Fulger as one of Nightingale's three ruling Count families, while Niklaus inherited that position.
Clan Horseman consequently became a Vampire Count Clan.
This was a significant disruption to Nightingale's political balance. The ancient structure built around the Snow, Scarlett and Fulger families had been broken, while a comparatively new political power now occupied Fulger's former seat.

#### Lucy and Karen
During Clan Horseman's period of increased influence, Lucy and Karen were attempting to transcend their status as Plebeian Vampires.
The pair had spent centuries pursuing a ritual which they believed could transform them into Noble Vampires. Their process required a magical blood ritual involving large quantities of innocent Human blood, Noble Vampire blood and specialized Witchcraft.[1]
The Horseman Clan became directly involved in the project.
When Einer and Zwei arrived at Lucy's hidden base, Karen had already become impatient and entered the ritual without waiting for Lucy. The ritual activated and increased Karen's physical strength, but failed to reconstruct her into a Noble Vampire.[1]
Einer revealed the reason.
The ritual itself was not entirely incorrect, but an ordinary Noble Vampire's blood was incapable of fundamentally reconstructing the biological difference between a Plebeian and a Noble. The procedure required something vastly more powerful: the blood of a Vampire Progenitor.[1]
The Horseman brothers then revealed that their family possessed exactly such a resource.
A servant brought out a sealed container holding blood belonging to [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]], the First Vampire Progenitor and King of [[Nightingale]]. The mere sight of the blood produced an overwhelming physical reaction in Lucy and Karen, who simultaneously desired it and instinctively feared it.[1]
Einer deliberately exploited their reaction, displaying the blood and allowing them to believe they could obtain it before withholding it again.
The incident demonstrated that Clan Horseman possessed resources far beyond what ordinary Noble Vampires could reasonably acquire, including actual blood from the Vampire King.

#### Hybrid Research
Clan Horseman's activities were not limited to Lucy and Karen's racial transformation experiment.
Niklaus was deeply involved in research concerning Hybrids, Beings combining multiple supernatural races. This research was important enough that possession of a Hybrid had originally been used as leverage to entice Natashia into accepting his first challenge.
The Horseman family continued maintaining at least one experimental Hybrid afterward.
Before the Rider–Horseman game, Niklaus questioned [[Zwei Horseman]] about the condition of a boy associated with the Clan's experiments. Zwei reported that the subject was sick, dying and did not have much time remaining. Niklaus reacted negatively because producing a Hybrid had required considerable effort and losing the subject before the experiment reached completion would represent a serious failure.[2]
The exchange confirms that:
- Clan Horseman possessed one or more Hybrid experimental subjects;
- Niklaus personally supervised the program;
- Zwei knew enough about the project to report directly on a subject's condition;
- and the Clan regarded stable Hybrid creation as an important objective.[2]
The exact methods used during these experiments have not been completely revealed in the early chapters and should not be expanded through speculation.

#### Conflict with Clan Rider
Clan Horseman's acquisition of the Fulger Count title did not remain uncontested.
[[Victoria Fulger Elderblood|Victoria Rider]], who maintained extremely close ties to Natashia and Clan Fulger, challenged Clan Horseman's growing political position. Their dispute eventually became an official game sanctioned through [[Nightingale]]'s legal system.[6][7]
The game became one of the largest public Vampire events [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had witnessed since entering Nightingale.
Clan Horseman was represented by [[Einer Horseman]], who was publicly introduced as the heir of the Clan. Clan Rider was represented by [[Tatsuya Rider]], officially presented as a mercenary but secretly Victoria's son.[2][8]
The stakes were enormous.
Clan Horseman placed its newly acquired Count title on the line, while Victoria wagered her enormous business empire and associated resources. The fight therefore represented far more than a personal duel; it could reshape the political and economic balance surrounding both families.

##### Einer Horseman vs. Tatsuya Rider
Einer and Tatsuya immediately demonstrated why they had been selected.
Einer possessed a strange black supernatural Power capable of creating weapons, binding substances and enormous black thorns, while Tatsuya used a highly unusual perceptual ability combined with swordsmanship and lightning.[8][9]
The fight escalated until Einer activated the [[Vampire Count Form]] at only approximately 105 years of age. The achievement surprised experienced Vampires observing the battle and established Einer as an exceptional young member of the Horseman bloodline.[10]
Einer's transformation also indirectly demonstrated why the Clan had entrusted him with its political future. Even [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett|Scathach Scarlett]] later acknowledged his talent and the degree of control he possessed over the form despite his age.[3]

##### Victor's Intervention
The formal Rider–Horseman duel was interrupted by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s desire to participate.
With [[Scathach]]'s intervention, Victor entered a separate confrontation against both combatants. Einer and Tatsuya were quickly forced to abandon their rivalry and cooperate simply to survive Victor's escalating elemental attacks.[11][12]
Einer demonstrated another application of his Power by transferring it to Tatsuya, temporarily increasing the swordsman's strength and changing his normally golden lightning into black lightning. The pair then combined their abilities to destroy one of Victor's largest attacks.[13]
They were ultimately exhausted and defeated by Victor.[3]
Because Victor's participation was considered a separate game, his victory did not technically resolve the original wager between Clan Rider and Clan Horseman. With the surrounding political situation becoming increasingly unstable, Victoria and Niklaus ultimately agreed to treat their own dispute as a draw.[3]
Clan Horseman therefore retained its Count title for the time being.

#### Natashia's Rematch
Clan Horseman's position changed permanently several months later.
After regaining her motivation and resolving to reclaim what she had lost, Natashia Fulger personally challenged Niklaus to another formal game.[4]
The match attracted enormous attention throughout [[Nightingale]] because it represented a direct rematch between the former Countess and the man who had taken her title.
The stakes were once again exceptional.
Niklaus possessed the Vampire Count title formerly belonging to the Fulger family, while Natashia wagered one of Clan Fulger's most valuable inherited treasures, a Divine-class artifact of comparable political importance.[4]
The arrangement ensured that neither side could casually refuse.

#### Evacuation of Clan Horseman
Niklaus did not enter the rematch carelessly.
Before the battle began, he became increasingly suspicious of the circumstances surrounding Natashia's sudden confidence and the involvement of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and the other Count families. Believing that something larger might be occurring, he ordered Jessica to begin moving Horseman personnel and resources away from [[Nightingale]].
The decision demonstrated one of Niklaus' defining traits: unlike many arrogant supernatural rulers, he prepared an escape route before committing himself completely.
Clan Horseman's evacuation meant that even if Niklaus lost the political battle, the entire family structure would not automatically be destroyed with him.

#### Natashia Fulger vs. Niklaus Horseman
Natashia and Niklaus fought within a specially reinforced arena capable of surviving the destructive output of Count-level Vampires.[14][15]
Natashia unleashed the lightning abilities of Clan Fulger at a level far beyond that normally displayed by younger Vampires, while Niklaus relied upon his own supernatural abilities, experience and willingness to exploit every weakness available.[16][17]
The fight demonstrated why Niklaus had originally been capable of taking the Count title. He was not merely a political schemer; he was an ancient and highly experienced Vampire capable of fighting a former Countess directly.
Niklaus eventually succeeded in poisoning Natashia and moved into a position from which he appeared capable of ending the battle.
However, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s intervention and the increasingly suspicious circumstances surrounding the game convinced Niklaus that remaining would expose him to greater danger. He recognized that he was no longer dealing only with Natashia but with a political environment in which Victor, the Vampire King and several powerful families had interests directly opposed to his own.
Rather than risk revealing further secrets connected to himself and Clan Horseman, Niklaus abandoned the match.[17][5]
His decision effectively conceded the Count title.

#### Loss of Count Status
Natashia's victory restored Clan Fulger to its former position as one of [[Nightingale]]'s highest Noble families.[5][18]
Clan Horseman's brief period as a Count Clan consequently ended.

#### Political Collapse
Following the loss of the Count title, Niklaus no longer possessed the authority that had allowed Clan Horseman to stand alongside [[Nightingale]]'s ruling families.
The earlier evacuation meant that surviving Clan members were not automatically eliminated, but the Clan ceased functioning as a major Count-level political power.
This distinction is important:
Clan Horseman did not lose its bloodline. It lost its political position.
Einer, Zwei and Jessica survived, while Niklaus himself escaped the immediate consequences of the game. However, the organized Horseman faction largely disappeared from Nightingale's central political structure after the defeat.

#### Niklaus' True Identity
The history of Clan Horseman became considerably more complicated once Niklaus' true background was revealed.
[[Niklaus Horseman]] was not merely an ambitious Noble Vampire who had created a new Clan. His identity was connected to Radu Tepes, a far older Vampire with direct ties to the history of the Tepes bloodline.
This revelation recontextualized the Clan.
Rather than being understood simply as a recently ambitious family attempting to become one of [[Nightingale]]'s ruling Clans, Clan Horseman can also be viewed as one of the identities and political structures constructed around Radu's much longer plans.
For detailed information concerning Niklaus' true history, see Radu Tepes.

### Known Associates

#### Lucy and Karen
Lucy and Karen were not formal members of Clan Horseman, but the family became involved in their attempt to transform themselves into Noble Vampires.
The Horseman Clan possessed knowledge they lacked regarding the ritual and, more importantly, possessed [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]]' Progenitor blood—the ingredient required to potentially complete it.[1]
Their relationship appears to have been transactional and manipulative rather than a genuine alliance.

#### Hybrid Subjects
Clan Horseman maintained experimental subjects connected to Niklaus' research into supernatural Hybrids.[2]
These individuals should not automatically be categorized as Clan members because the story presents at least some of them as experimental assets rather than willing members of the family.

### Abilities
Clan Horseman does not possess one fully established universal Power comparable to the clearly defined bloodline abilities of Clans such as Fulger, Snow or Scarlett.
The previous version of this article listed Corruption as a universal Clan ability. This is misleading. Corruption-related abilities are strongly associated with Niklaus/Radu, while [[Einer Horseman]] demonstrates a different black thorn-based Power. [[Zwei Horseman]]'s personal Power has not yet been revealed.
Abilities should therefore be separated between racial traits and individual members.

#### Vampire Physiology
All confirmed blood members of Clan Horseman are Noble Vampires and possess the supernatural characteristics associated with that race.
- Superhuman Strength
- Superhuman Speed
- Superhuman Durability
- Enhanced Senses
- Regeneration
- Longevity
- Blood Consumption
See Vampires for the full racial physiology.

#### Vampire Count Transformation
Qualified members of the Horseman line can potentially access the Vampire Count Transformation, but it should not be listed as though every member has demonstrated the ability.
[[Einer Horseman]] is the clearest confirmed example. At approximately 105 years old, he was already capable of entering the form, something that surprised observers and earned praise from [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett|Scathach Scarlett]].[10][3]
Niklaus, as an actual Count-level Vampire, naturally possessed power at or beyond the level associated with the transformation.
Zwei's possession of the form has not been directly demonstrated.

### Research and Forbidden Knowledge
One of the Horseman Clan's most distinctive characteristics was not a single combat Power but the breadth of dangerous supernatural research available to its leader.

#### Hybrid Research
Niklaus possessed enough knowledge regarding Hybrids to create or maintain experimental Hybrid subjects.[2]
Creating a stable Hybrid was considered extraordinarily difficult, and Niklaus regarded the possible death of one experimental subject as a major loss because reproducing the result was not simple.

#### Racial Transformation Knowledge
Through its interactions with Lucy and Karen, the Clan demonstrated knowledge regarding the differences between:
- Human-born Plebeian Vampires;
- naturally born Noble Vampires;
- the role of Progenitor blood;
- and the possibility of reconstructing Vampire physiology through ritual methods.[1]

#### Access to Progenitor Blood
Clan Horseman possessed a vial of [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]]' blood.[1]
The exact circumstances by which Niklaus obtained the blood were not explained during the early storyline, but possession of such a resource demonstrates the extraordinary level of access and planning behind the Clan.

### Political Relationships

#### Fulger Clan
[[Fulger Clan]] was Clan Horseman's principal political rival.
Niklaus' greatest public achievement came from defeating Natashia and taking the Fulger Count title. The entire political existence of Clan Horseman as a Count family was consequently built upon a title formerly belonging to Clan Fulger.
Natashia's rematch ultimately reversed that result and restored her Clan's position.[4][5]

#### Rider Clan
Rider Clan opposed Clan Horseman's control of the Fulger title and eventually challenged the family through a formal game.
Einer and Tatsuya represented the two sides, although [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s intervention prevented the original dispute from reaching a conventional conclusion.[8][3]

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] initially had no personal interest in Clan Horseman's internal politics.
That changed rapidly.
Victor befriended and became increasingly attached to Natashia, while his involvement in the Rider–Horseman game brought him into direct conflict with Einer. Victor later became one of the primary motivations behind Natashia regaining the confidence necessary to challenge Niklaus again.
After Niklaus escaped the consequences Victor wanted him to suffer, Victor's attitude became openly hostile.[5]

#### Tepes Clan
The relationship between Clan Horseman and [[Tepes Clan]] is complicated by Niklaus' true identity as Radu Tepes.
This does not mean Clan Horseman functioned as an ordinary branch of the Tepes family within [[Nightingale]]'s political hierarchy. Publicly, it existed as a separate Noble Clan under [[Niklaus Horseman]].

### Former Count Status
Clan Horseman should be distinguished from [[Nightingale]]'s ancient Count bloodlines.
The family became a Count Clan because Niklaus legally acquired the title through a game against Natashia Fulger. This political achievement did not retroactively make the Horseman lineage equivalent in age or inherited history to the ancient Scarlett, Snow or Fulger lines.
Its Count status was therefore:
- Acquired rather than ancient;
- Political rather than a separate species;
- dependent upon Niklaus holding the title;
- and lost when Natashia successfully reclaimed it.

### Current Status
Clan Horseman's exact modern organizational status has not been fully detailed.
What can be stated safely is:
- Niklaus lost the Vampire Count title;
- Clan Fulger reclaimed its former position;
- the Horseman political faction withdrew from [[Nightingale]];
- the family bloodline survived through Einer, Zwei and Jessica;
- and Niklaus/Radu's later story became increasingly separated from his former public identity as the leader of Clan Horseman.
For this reason, the Clan should not be described as an active Vampire Count Clan in the present era.
The safest classification is that the Horseman bloodline survives, while the former Count-level political organization has collapsed or become inactive.

### Trivia
- Clan Horseman held the Vampire Count title for only a relatively short period compared with [[Nightingale]]'s ancient Count families.
- The Count position originally belonged to [[Fulger Clan]] and was obtained after Niklaus defeated Natashia in a formal game.
- [[Einer Horseman]] was explicitly introduced as the heir of Clan Horseman during the Rider–Horseman game.[8]
- [[Zwei Horseman]], despite being Einer's older twin, was not publicly identified as the heir.
- Niklaus stated before the Rider–Horseman game that Zwei could not participate, but the novel did not explain why.[2]
- Jessica was also not selected because Niklaus considered the game too important to entrust to her at the time.[2]
- Einer was already capable of using the Vampire Count Transformation at only 105 years old.[10]
- Clan Horseman possessed actual blood belonging to [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]], which Einer used to manipulate Lucy and Karen.[1]
- Karen's attempted racial-transformation ritual was strengthened by Horseman knowledge but failed because she initially lacked Progenitor blood.[1]
- The Clan maintained at least one dying Hybrid experimental subject during the events preceding the Rider–Horseman game.[2]
- The earlier wiki description listing the Inquisition, Demons and rogue [[Werewolves]] as direct Clan affiliations should not be retained without specific chapter evidence showing a formal Clan-level alliance. Individual conspiracies or dealings should instead be documented in the relevant history sections.
- Likewise, Corruption should not be presented as a universal Horseman bloodline ability unless a later chapter explicitly establishes it. The demonstrated abilities of Niklaus and Einer differ considerably.
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s victory over Einer and Tatsuya did not directly strip Clan Horseman of the Count title. Victor was participating in a separate game; the Rider–Horseman dispute itself was treated as a draw.[3]
- Clan Horseman finally lost the Count position when Natashia directly challenged Niklaus again in Chapters 224–232.[4][5]
- Niklaus' later revelation as Radu Tepes substantially changes the interpretation of Clan Horseman's history, as the apparent Clan leader was operating under an identity concealing a much older Vampire lineage.
