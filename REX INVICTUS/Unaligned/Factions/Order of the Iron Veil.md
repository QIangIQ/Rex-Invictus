The Order of the Iron Veil, commonly known as the [[Iron]] Veil, is the Second Legion of the [[Elderblood Empire]] and its principal defensive military organization. Specializing in planetary defense, fortified territories, defensive rings, shields and the protection of Imperial possessions, the Iron Veil forms the defensive counterpart to [[The Spartans]], the Empire's First Legion. Its purpose is to prevent hostile forces from conquering or destroying Imperial territory while maintaining the military infrastructure necessary to protect the Empire's expanding civilization.[1][2]
The Legion was founded around Year 1000 of the [[Imperial Calendar]] by [[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]], [[Violet Elderblood Snow]], [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]] and [[Hela Elderblood]]. Its establishment formed part of the Emperor's military reforms, through which the armed forces of the Elderblood Empire were reorganized into Seven Imperial Legions possessing distinct strategic responsibilities. Whereas the Spartans were established to invade and conquer hostile territories, the Iron Veil was created to defend those territories, establish powerful fortifications and ensure that Imperial worlds could withstand external attacks.[3][1][2]
The Order is commanded by Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia, with Otsuki Elderblood Haruna and Hela Elderblood serving as designated substitutes whenever circumstances require another commander. Its military establishment includes stationary knights, climate controllers, orbital defense towers and other defensive assets intended to protect Imperial territory. Despite its principal specialization in defensive warfare, the Legion is also capable of participating in offensive operations when necessary.[2]
The Iron Veil is one of the Empire's two most numerous conventional military Legions, alongside the Spartans. Its forces possess access to extraordinarily advanced military technology, including weapons capable of destroying entire solar systems. These capabilities allow the organization to defend against threats far beyond ordinary planetary civilizations, while its large military population provides the manpower necessary to protect the Empire's extensive territories.[1][2]
The relationship between the First and Second Legions is commonly represented by the imagery of a spear and a shield. The Spartans constitute the offensive spearhead responsible for breaking enemy defenses and capturing territory, while the Iron Veil serves as the shield protecting established Imperial possessions. Their closely related military responsibilities make cooperation between the two organizations an important element of the Elderblood Empire's conventional military structure.[2]

The Iron Veil's primary responsibility is the preservation of Imperial territory against military aggression. Its forces are organized around maintaining defensive infrastructure and preventing hostile armies from penetrating worlds, fortifications and strategic positions under the authority of the [[Elderblood Empire]].[2]
This specialization distinguishes the Second Legion from organizations dedicated to military conquest, intelligence gathering, law enforcement or the direct protection of the Imperial Family. Rather than functioning exclusively as a conventional army, the Iron Veil combines military personnel with planetary and orbital defensive systems, giving it responsibility for both direct combat and the protection of established Imperial positions.[1][2]
The Legion's defensive mandate does not prevent it from engaging in offensive combat. Its commanders can employ offensive operations when necessary, and its armaments possess sufficient destructive power to threaten military targets on a stellar scale. The distinction between the Iron Veil and the Spartans therefore concerns their principal responsibilities rather than an absolute prohibition against performing each other's functions.[2]

### History

#### Volume 9: Those Who Rule Reality
The Order of the Iron Veil originated during the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s extraordinary period of accelerated development. Following the consolidation of his civilization and the expansion of its galactic territory, [[Victor Elderblood]] recognized that the Empire required considerably greater military and technological development before confronting the powerful civilizations existing beyond his own Sector.[3]
To accomplish this, Victor initiated an enormous ritual designed to accelerate the passage of Time within his territory. The ritual provided the Empire with approximately 200,000 years of internal development while only a single year elapsed outside the affected region. Victor and his Empresses remained involved in the civilization's development through their Avatars, overseeing political, technological, military and supernatural progress throughout the accelerated period.[3][4]
The Empire's rapid expansion produced the need for a more comprehensive military administration. Its population increased enormously, its technology advanced and numerous civilizations were incorporated into the Emperor's territories. The military structures that had previously been sufficient for a much smaller supernatural faction required reorganization to accommodate a civilization operating across vast astronomical distances.[1]
Victor established a plan to divide the Imperial military establishment into Seven Legions, each possessing specialized responsibilities under a unified command structure. The arrangement was intended to provide the Empire with separate organizations capable of conducting military conquest, defending territory, gathering intelligence, enforcing Imperial law, developing military technology and protecting its most important individuals and locations.[3][1]
Several Legions were established during the earliest stages of the [[Imperial Calendar]]. The Sixth Legion, [[The Silent Judgment]], and the Third Legion, the Black Hand of the Emperor, appeared around Year 20 and assumed responsibility for public law enforcement and clandestine intelligence operations. The [[Stellar Arsenal]] subsequently emerged as the Fourth Legion, centralizing advanced technological research, experimental development and the creation of increasingly powerful military equipment.[1]
The Empire's conventional military establishment continued developing as its territory and population expanded. The creation of enormous armies, advanced weapons and powerful military spacecraft made it possible to conduct warfare on scales far exceeding the capabilities of traditional supernatural civilizations. However, the Emperor also required organizations capable of protecting conquered worlds and preventing hostile powers from undermining Imperial control after a successful campaign.[1][2]
Around Year 1000 of the Imperial Calendar, the Empire established the First and Second Legions as its principal conventional military forces. [[The Spartans]], the First Legion, were organized around direct assault, invasion and territorial conquest. The Order of the Iron Veil, the Second Legion, was founded shortly afterward to provide the complementary defensive functions required by an expanding interstellar empire.[1][2]
The [[Iron]] Veil's creation was undertaken by [[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]], [[Violet Elderblood Snow]], [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]] and [[Hela Elderblood]]. The organization was designed to protect Imperial territories through planetary shields, fortified zones, bastions, defensive rings and specialized defensive forces. Its military composition included stationary knights, climate controllers and orbital defense towers, reflecting a focus upon the protection and preservation of established positions rather than exclusively mobile assault operations.[2]
Eleanor became the Legion's principal commander, while Haruna and Hela were designated as individuals capable of assuming responsibility when she was unavailable. This leadership arrangement placed the Second Legion under members of the Imperial Family whose authority and supernatural capabilities could support the organization in both defensive and offensive situations.[2]
The Iron Veil and Spartans developed into the Empire's two most numerous conventional Legions. Their respective military responsibilities permitted the Empire to maintain a large offensive force while simultaneously developing the defensive infrastructure necessary to protect its expanding territory. The relationship between the organizations became associated with the imagery of a spear and shield, representing the complementary requirements of conquest and defense.[2]
As the Empire continued developing, the destructive capabilities available to both Legions expanded alongside its technological and supernatural advancement. The Iron Veil obtained access to military armaments capable of destroying entire solar systems, ensuring that its defenders possessed the means to confront powerful enemies rather than relying exclusively upon static fortifications.[2]

### Foundation and Purpose
The Order of the Iron Veil was created to fulfill a military responsibility distinct from the conquest-oriented operations of the Spartans. An expanding civilization requires more than the ability to defeat hostile armies; it must also maintain control over acquired territories, protect its population and prevent enemies from destroying important infrastructure or retaking strategically valuable locations.
The [[Iron]] Veil addresses these responsibilities through an organized system of defensive forces and fortifications. Its mandate encompasses planetary protection, the construction and maintenance of defensive positions, and the use of powerful military assets against enemies attempting to penetrate Imperial territory.[2]

#### Protection of Imperial Territory
The protection of Imperial territory constitutes the Legion's foremost responsibility. Its forces are intended to prevent enemy incursions into planets, fortified zones and other locations belonging to the [[Elderblood Empire]].[2]
This responsibility applies to a civilization possessing territories distributed across enormous astronomical distances. Defensive operations must therefore account for threats arriving through conventional interstellar approaches as well as supernatural powers capable of bypassing ordinary physical barriers.
The Legion's established military infrastructure includes defenses operating at planetary and orbital levels. These provide the organization with the means to protect worlds against external attack while maintaining a military presence capable of responding to direct assaults.[2]
The precise distribution of [[Iron]] Veil forces across the Empire's planets has not been established. Its mandate concerns Imperial territorial protection generally, but there is no confirmed numerical allocation of troops or defensive installations to every individual world.

#### Planetary Defense
Planetary defense is one of the Order's principal military specializations. Its defensive systems include planetary shields and associated fortifications intended to protect Imperial worlds from hostile attack.[2]
The strategic importance of planetary defense extends beyond the survival of individual armies. A successful attack against a developed Imperial world could threaten its population, infrastructure and the resources necessary for the Empire's continued military development.
The [[Iron]] Veil's defensive responsibilities consequently concern the preservation of established territory rather than merely the defeat of an attacking enemy. Its forces are intended to maintain protection even when faced with opponents possessing powerful supernatural abilities or advanced military technology.

#### Fortification
The construction and maintenance of fortified zones, bastions and defensive rings form another essential part of the [[Iron]] Veil's responsibilities.[2]
Fortifications allow the Empire to establish defensible positions around important territories and locations. The presence of orbital defense towers, shields and stationary military forces demonstrates that the Second Legion employs a combination of fixed infrastructure and stationed personnel to protect its assigned regions.
Unlike the Spartans, whose military doctrine emphasizes rapid offensive action, the Iron Veil's primary function is to maintain positions that an enemy must overcome before threatening Imperial territory.

#### Defensive Military Doctrine
The [[Iron]] Veil's doctrine emphasizes the use of fortifications, military personnel and powerful defensive weapons to resist hostile forces. Its organization is intended to preserve the Empire's ability to withstand attacks while maintaining the conditions necessary for continued military operations.[2]
Nevertheless, the Legion is not restricted to passive defense. Its members can undertake offensive operations when the circumstances demand, allowing a defender to eliminate threats before they overwhelm established fortifications.
This flexibility is important because the defense of Imperial territory may require both holding existing positions and directly confronting the forces attempting to capture them. A purely stationary military establishment would lack some of the capabilities necessary to respond to threats outside its immediate defensive perimeter.
The Iron Veil therefore retains an offensive capability alongside its principal defensive specialization.

#### Planetary Shields
Planetary shields are among the most important defensive systems associated with the Order of the Iron Veil. Their inclusion reflects the Empire's ability to employ large-scale supernatural or technological barriers to protect inhabited worlds.[2]
Such defenses are especially relevant to warfare involving powerful military spacecraft and supernatural combatants capable of inflicting destruction far beyond conventional weapons. Protecting a planet against external attack requires defensive measures capable of operating over an enormous area rather than relying exclusively upon individual soldiers.
The [[Iron]] Veil's mandate explicitly includes planetary shields, although the precise construction materials, Energy sources, operating mechanisms and durability of its standard shielding systems have not been individually established.
The existence of planetary shields should therefore not be interpreted as proof that every Imperial planet possesses an identical barrier or that the shields automatically resist every conceivable Divine or Eldritch attack.

Fortified zones are established defensive areas intended to strengthen the protection of Imperial territory. Within the Empire's greater military structure, they contribute to the preservation of strategically important positions and the ability to resist hostile forces attempting to establish control over Imperial territory.
The exact size, construction, military complement and individual designations of the Legion's fortified zones remain unknown.

#### Bastions
Bastions form another category of defensive position associated with the Order. They are incorporated into the Legion's broader responsibility for establishing fortifications and protecting Imperial territory.[2]
The term identifies fortified defensive positions rather than a separately established military rank or class of soldier. The specific appearance, armaments and defensive capabilities of individual [[Iron]] Veil bastions have not been described.
Their importance lies in the Legion's broader military emphasis upon maintaining positions capable of resisting attacks and preserving Imperial control.

#### Defense Rings
Defense rings are among the defensive structures associated with the [[Iron]] Veil. They form part of the Empire's protective infrastructure and are relevant to the defense of important territories or celestial locations.[2]
Their inclusion alongside planetary shields, bastions and orbital towers indicates that the Order possesses a variety of defensive installations rather than depending upon a single form of protection.
The precise construction and operational mechanisms of the Legion's defense rings remain unspecified. They should not automatically be equated with a particular planetary shield, orbital station or larger Imperial defensive project without direct identification.

#### Orbital Defense Towers
Orbital defense towers are among the commonly identified assets belonging to the [[Iron]] Veil. Their role is connected to the protection of Imperial territory through defensive installations operating beyond the surface of a planet.[2]
Their orbital deployment places them within the broader field of interplanetary defensive warfare, where threats may originate from hostile spacecraft or supernatural forces approaching a world from Space.
The exact weapon systems, operating personnel and individual power levels of these towers are not established. However, their presence confirms that the Legion's defensive infrastructure extends beyond conventional ground fortifications.

### Military Organization
The Order of the Iron Veil combines conventional military personnel with specialized defensive forces and large-scale technological or supernatural installations. Its organization reflects its responsibility for protecting established territory rather than functioning solely as a mobile assault army.[2]
The Legion's commonly identified units include stationary knights, climate controllers and orbital defense towers. These categories establish the general nature of its military composition, although the complete internal organization, individual ranks and specialized subdivisions of the Second Legion remain unknown.

#### Stationary Knights
Stationary knights are one of the Order's principal identified personnel categories. Their inclusion reflects the importance of stationed military forces within the Empire's defensive establishment.[2]
Unlike the mobile assault formations associated with the Spartans, these personnel belong to an organization primarily responsible for holding and protecting Imperial positions. The exact equipment, supernatural abilities and training requirements of stationary knights have not been separately documented.
The designation does not establish that its members are literally incapable of movement. Rather, it identifies a category of military personnel associated with the Legion's defensive forces.

#### Climate Controllers
Climate controllers constitute another specialized category associated with the [[Iron]] Veil. Their designation indicates a relationship with the control or regulation of climatic conditions, distinguishing them from conventional armored soldiers and fixed defensive structures.[2]
Within a military organization responsible for planetary defense, environmental or climatic control represents a potentially valuable specialization. However, the precise supernatural or technological methods employed by the Iron Veil's climate controllers remain unspecified.
No individual climate controller has been identified by name, and their exact operational responsibilities, personal combat abilities and relationship with planetary defense systems have not been established.

#### Orbital Defense Personnel
The presence of orbital defense towers establishes the importance of defenses operating beyond a planet's surface. These installations belong to the known defensive assets of the [[Iron]] Veil and contribute to its responsibility for protecting Imperial territory.[2]
The personnel responsible for operating, maintaining and protecting such installations have not been individually classified. Consequently, the existence of orbital towers should not be used to invent specific officer ranks, engineering divisions or named military units.

#### Additional Military Forces
The Order possesses access to weapons capable of destroying entire solar systems, demonstrating that its military establishment extends beyond planetary fortifications and conventional stationed soldiers.[2]
These armaments provide the Legion with exceptional offensive and defensive potential, but the precise vessels, installations or weapon platforms through which they are deployed have not been comprehensively described.
The number of individual combatants and defensive assets maintained by the [[Iron]] Veil is likewise unknown, although the Legion is identified as one of the Empire's most numerous military organizations.

#### Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia
Main article: [[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]]
Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia is the principal commander of the Order of the Iron Veil and one of the four individuals responsible for its foundation. Her position places her at the head of the Empire's largest dedicated defensive organization, whose responsibilities include planetary protection, fortified territories and the maintenance of defensive military infrastructure.[2]
As Legion Commander, Eleanor oversees an organization possessing both conventional defensive forces and weapons capable of operating on a stellar scale. Her responsibilities consequently extend beyond directing individual combatants to the broader protection of Imperial territory.
Eleanor is also recognized as one of the possible substitute commanders of the First Legion, establishing a direct relationship between the leadership structures of the Empire's principal offensive and defensive organizations.[1][2]
This overlap reinforces the complementary nature of the two Legions, allowing an experienced Imperial commander to participate in both defensive and offensive military responsibilities according to the situation.
Eleanor's position as principal commander does not eliminate the authority of the Emperor or Supreme Military Commander, under whom the Seven Legions ultimately operate.

#### Violet Elderblood Snow
Main article: [[Violet Elderblood Snow]]
Violet Elderblood Snow is one of the founding members responsible for the establishment of the [[Iron]] Veil. Her involvement connects the organization directly to one of the Emperor's principal Empresses and reflects the participation of the Imperial Family in constructing the military institutions necessary for the Empire's continued development.[2]
Violet possesses extraordinary Divine and supernatural powers, making her an exceptionally important figure within the larger Imperial hierarchy. However, her involvement in founding the Legion should not automatically be treated as evidence that she serves as its permanent battlefield commander.
The established command structure identifies Eleanor as principal commander and Haruna and Hela as her designated substitutes. Violet's confirmed relationship with the Order is therefore that of a founder, distinct from the operational positions assigned to the other three women.[2]

#### Otsuki Elderblood Haruna
Main article: [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]]
Otsuki Elderblood Haruna is a co-founder of the [[Iron]] Veil and one of its two designated substitute commanders. She possesses the authority to assume leadership whenever Eleanor is unavailable or the circumstances require another commander.[2]
Haruna's background as a powerful supernatural warrior and member of the Elderblood family makes her a valuable figure within the Empire's military leadership. Her position connects her directly to the organization responsible for maintaining planetary defenses and protecting established Imperial territories.
Her substitute-command role is separate from the office of principal Legion Commander, which remains assigned to Eleanor.

#### Hela Elderblood
Main article: [[Hela Elderblood]]
Hela Elderblood is a co-founder of the Order and its other designated substitute commander. She can assume responsibility for the Legion when Eleanor is unavailable or a different commander is required.[2]
Hela's immense Divine powers and experience as a Goddess associated with Death and the [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] provide her with a supernatural background distinct from those of the Legion's other founders. Her participation in the [[Iron]] Veil places an exceptionally powerful Divine Being among the leaders available to defend Imperial territory.
Her status as a substitute commander does not establish that every soldier under the Iron Veil possesses Death-related powers or that the Legion as a whole operates exclusively through Hela's Divinity.

### Military Strength
The Order of the Iron Veil is one of the two most numerous Legions of the [[Elderblood Empire]], alongside the Spartans. Its enormous military establishment is intended to protect Imperial territory on the same broad scale that the First Legion is expected to conduct offensive warfare.[2]
This makes the [[Iron]] Veil considerably different from the Fifth Legion, whose personnel are selected primarily for exceptional individual strength and the direct protection of the Imperial Family. The Second Legion instead maintains an immense conventional military and defensive infrastructure capable of protecting territories distributed across the Empire.

#### Personnel Strength
The exact number of military personnel assigned to the [[Iron]] Veil has not been individually established. Nevertheless, its identification as one of the Empire's two most numerous Legions confirms that its forces represent a substantial portion of the conventional Imperial military establishment.[2]
The organization must also be distinguished from the total population of the [[Elderblood Empire]] or the combined military personnel belonging to all Seven Legions. Neither provides a confirmed exact figure for the number of stationary knights, climate controllers or other personnel serving within the Second Legion.

#### Solar-System-Destruction-Class Weaponry
The [[Iron]] Veil possesses access to weapons capable of destroying entire solar systems, demonstrating that its defensive specialization does not restrict its armaments to ordinary barriers or planetary fortifications.[2]
Such weapons provide the Order with the ability to confront hostile military forces possessing tremendous destructive capabilities. Their presence also reflects the technological and supernatural advancements achieved by the [[Elderblood Empire]] during its accelerated development.

#### Defensive Capabilities
The [[Iron]] Veil's defensive capabilities encompass more than individual combat strength. Its effectiveness also depends upon the military infrastructure, fortifications and protective systems placed under its responsibility.
Planetary shields, bastions, defensive rings and orbital towers represent different components of that infrastructure. Their combination permits the Empire to establish defensive positions operating across several military environments, from planetary surfaces to surrounding orbital regions.[2]
However, the precise durability of each defensive system has not been quantified. The Order's access to powerful weapons does not automatically establish that every barrier it operates can withstand an attack possessing equivalent destructive power.

### Offensive Capabilities
Although established primarily for defense, the [[Iron]] Veil is capable of conducting offensive military operations when required.[2]
Its founders did not intend the Legion to become incapable of attacking simply because its principal specialization concerned protecting territory. The organization maintains access to powerful combatants and armaments, allowing it to confront enemies beyond its established defensive positions when circumstances demand.
This flexibility creates an important distinction between the Iron Veil's primary purpose and the complete range of actions available to its forces. The Legion's primary function is to defend Imperial territory, but it can employ offensive force where doing so supports its objectives.
The exact circumstances under which the Order transitions from defense to offense have not been defined through a separate formal doctrine. Nevertheless, its ability to perform both functions is an established part of its military identity.[2]

This overlap demonstrates that the Empire's specialized Legions remain part of a unified military system. Individual commanders can possess responsibilities extending across organizational boundaries without eliminating the separate purposes for which those organizations were established.

### Relationship with Imperial Defensive Development
The [[Iron]] Veil's creation was closely connected to the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s development into an advanced interstellar civilization. The extraordinary passage of Time within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ritual allowed the Empire to accumulate military resources, improve its technological capabilities and construct infrastructure far beyond what had been available during its earlier existence.[3][1]
These developments created the conditions necessary for a military organization specializing in large-scale planetary and territorial defense. The Legion's established use of shields, bastions, orbital installations and solar-system-class weaponry reflects the resources available to the Empire after extensive technological and supernatural advancement.[2]
However, the Iron Veil should be distinguished from the Empire's complete defensive infrastructure. Victor and other members of the Imperial Family developed additional methods of protecting their territory, including larger-scale military projects and supernatural defenses that were not necessarily placed under the Second Legion's direct authority.
The Legion is therefore the principal conventional defensive force of the Empire rather than the sole organization responsible for every protective system, World Tree, barrier or supernatural guardian associated with Imperial territory.

#### Relationship with the Stellar Arsenal
Main article: [[Stellar Arsenal]]
The Stellar Arsenal serves as the Empire's Fourth Legion and specializes in research, technological development and advanced military equipment. Its responsibilities differ from the [[Iron]] Veil's established duties, which concern the protection of Imperial territory through military forces and defensive installations.[1]
The distinction is relevant because advanced military technology can serve both offensive and defensive purposes. The Iron Veil possesses powerful armaments and installations, while the Stellar Arsenal is responsible for the broader research and development of advanced technology.
The existence of these complementary specializations does not establish that every Iron Veil shield, bastion or orbital tower was personally designed by the Stellar Arsenal. The specific development and deployment arrangements for individual defensive systems remain undocumented.

#### Relationship with the Imperial Guard
Main article: [[The Imperial Guard]]
The Imperial Guard, the Fifth Legion, is responsible for the specialized protection of the Imperial Family and locations considered sacred or exceptionally important. Its membership is characterized by powerful elite combatants rather than the enormous conventional military population associated with the First and Second Legions.[1]
The [[Iron]] Veil's responsibility is broader in territorial scope. Instead of concentrating primarily upon the immediate protection of the Emperor and his family, it maintains the military defenses of the Empire's planets and established possessions.
The two organizations therefore fulfill different aspects of Imperial security, with the Guard providing elite protection and the Iron Veil serving as the conventional defensive military establishment.

### Strategic Importance
The Order of the Iron Veil provides the [[Elderblood Empire]] with a dedicated military organization capable of defending enormous territories without requiring the Emperor or his most powerful family members to personally confront every hostile incursion.[2]
The Empire possesses numerous individuals whose personal abilities greatly exceed those of ordinary supernatural soldiers. Nevertheless, the maintenance of an interstellar civilization requires extensive defensive infrastructure and military personnel capable of responding to threats across many worlds simultaneously.
The [[Iron]] Veil addresses this requirement by combining enormous military numbers with defensive installations, stationed personnel and powerful strategic weapons. Its forces can protect important locations, reinforce fortified positions and undertake military action when hostile forces threaten Imperial territory.[2]
The Legion's importance is also connected to the Empire's continued expansion. Military conquest without the ability to protect acquired territories would leave an expanding civilization vulnerable to repeated invasions and the destruction of infrastructure. The establishment of a specialized defensive Legion ensures that the Empire possesses an organization whose principal purpose is preserving those gains.
Its relationship with the Spartans reinforces this strategic balance. The First Legion provides the means to overcome enemy defenses and seize territory, while the Second Legion maintains the protective infrastructure necessary to preserve Imperial authority after military success.[1][2]
The Iron Veil consequently represents a central component of the Empire's conventional military organization, ensuring that its defensive responsibilities remain supported by an institution dedicated specifically to their fulfillment.

### Trivia
- The Order of the Iron Veil is the Second Legion of the [[Elderblood Empire]] and was established around Year 1000 of the [[Imperial Calendar]].[2]
- Its establishment followed the creation of [[The Spartans]], the First Legion, although several other Imperial Legions had been established earlier in the Empire's history.[1][2]
- The Legion possesses four founders: Eleanor, Violet, Haruna and Hela. All four participated in its establishment, despite only three appearing in some abbreviated founder listings.[2]
- [[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]] is its principal commander, while [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]] and [[Hela Elderblood]] are designated substitutes.[2]
- Violet is a confirmed founder but is not listed among the Order's permanent or substitute operational commanders.[2]
- The Legion's principal responsibilities include planetary defense, fortified zones, bastions, defense rings and the protection of Imperial territories.[2]
- Its known military assets include stationary knights, climate controllers and orbital defense towers.[2]
- Although the [[Iron]] Veil is the Empire's primary defensive Legion, it can undertake offensive operations when necessary.[2]
- The Iron Veil and Spartans constitute the Empire's two most numerous conventional military Legions.[2]
- The two organizations possess complementary responsibilities commonly represented by the relationship between a spear and a shield.[2]
- The Iron Veil possesses access to solar-system-destruction-class weaponry, demonstrating that its military capabilities extend beyond planetary shields and conventional fortifications.[2]
- Eleanor's position as principal commander of the Iron Veil overlaps with her status as a possible substitute commander of the Spartans.[1][2]
- The organization operates beneath the Emperor and the Empire's Supreme Military Commander rather than possessing independent sovereignty.[3]
- Its exact personnel count, headquarters, internal subdivisions and the technical specifications of its defensive installations remain unidentified.
- The Legion's slogan is "We protect the Empire until the last breath."[2]
