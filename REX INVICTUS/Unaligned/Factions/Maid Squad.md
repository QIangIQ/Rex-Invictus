The Maid Squad, more commonly called Victor's Battle Maids after their military development, is an elite group of personal attendants, bodyguards, assassins, intelligence operatives and combatants serving Victor Elderblood. Victor created the group after realizing that increasing his own strength could not solve one fundamental problem: he could not physically remain beside every member of his family at all times. The Maid Squad was therefore intended to become an independent extension of his ability to protect the people closest to him.[1][2]
The organization is led by [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]], Victor's first personally chosen maid and the professional standard against which the others are measured. Victor originally envisioned seven extremely powerful personal maids, but his concept later evolved into eight leader maids symbolically associated with the seven Deadly Sins and the older eighth Cardinal Sin, Melancholy. These leaders were intended to eventually command subordinate maid lineages of their own rather than forcing Victor to personally create every future maid through his Blood.[3]
Six leader positions were explicitly assigned in the novel: Pride, Lust, Wrath, Gluttony, Envy and Sloth. The remaining positions, Greed and Melancholy, were unfilled when the system was introduced. Later author commentary associated [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]] with Greed and Kali with Melancholy, but neither assignment should be presented as a novel-confirmed appointment unless the story itself formally establishes them.
The Maid Squad eventually grew far beyond the level Victor imagined when the group was founded. Its members entered his family and Bloodline, several became [[Blood Dragons]], and the Battle Maids were trusted to fight directly against Divine enemies during the destruction of the [[Greek Pantheon]].[4][5]

Despite their title, the Battle Maids were never intended to function as ordinary domestic servants.
Victor wanted individuals capable of operating independently in dangerous environments. A member could be expected to protect a wife, assassinate an enemy, gather information, manage household affairs and enter combat within the same general role.[2]

#### Quality over numbers
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] initially considered collecting a much larger number of maids but abandoned that idea.
He decided that seven women capable of reaching extraordinary levels of strength would be more useful than a large household of comparatively weak attendants.[2]

Victor did not know whether every woman he selected would actually reach his imagined standard.

Bruna's courage and Eve's enormous suppressed rage were among the characteristics that convinced him they could grow far beyond their current strength.[2]

### Formation

#### Kaguya
Kaguya is the foundation upon which the later Maid Squad was modeled.
She originated from the [[Blank Clan]], a lineage of servants, assassins and spies historically tied to the Snow family.
Her training covered:
- assassination;
- espionage;
- infiltration;
- etiquette;
- household management;
- supernatural combat;
- intelligence gathering;
- and covert movement.
Although service had been imposed upon her by birth, Kaguya desired to become the "perfect maid" serving a master she personally considered worthy.
Her relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] gradually became that choice.[6]
When Violet formally allowed Kaguya to choose Victor as her master, she became his first personal maid.

#### Bruna
[[Bruna Elderblood]] became [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s second core maid.
A former nun rescued from horrific circumstances, Bruna displayed enough courage under pressure that Victor immediately considered her unusually promising.[8][2]
Her later religious devotion toward Victor became one of the foundations of the [[Blood God Religion]].
Within the Maid Squad she occupies the symbolic seat of Lust.[3]

#### Eve
[[Eve Elderblood]] became the third maid.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] rescued her after a childhood defined by extreme abuse, gave her the name Eve and recognized the immense anger suppressed beneath her quiet behavior.
Victor considered that anger potential rather than merely emotional damage.[2]
Eve consequently received the Sin position of Wrath.[3]

### Expansion of the Core

#### Maria
[[Maria Elderblood]] originally served as a Human Vampire Hunter and later became a Ghoul connected to Sasha.
Following the collapse of her former loyalties and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s intervention, Maria accepted her supernatural existence and eventually entered Victor's household.
She became the fourth maid and was later associated with Gluttony.[3]
Her Ghoul nature made the symbolism particularly appropriate.

#### Roberta
[[Roberta Elderblood]] joined before Roxanne and became one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s principal personal maids.
Her position is complicated by her connection with [[Medusa]], whose existence can manifest through Roberta.
Victor assigned Roberta the position of Envy.[3]
Medusa should not therefore be counted as a separate Sin leader simply because she is associated with the Squad.

#### Roxanne
[[Roxanne Elderblood]] is explicitly [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s sixth maid.[10]
Roxanne differs fundamentally from the rest of the original group because she is a World Tree.
Her primary value is not merely conventional combat.
She is:
- spiritually integrated with Victor;
- capable of supporting and nourishing his Soul;
- connected to planetary and World Tree systems;
- a major Nature-based entity;
- and one of the most important supernatural beings associated with his internal existence.
During the [[Greek Pantheon]] conflict, Roxanne accompanied Victor through their Soul connection but remained concealed instead of publicly deploying beside the other Battle Maids.[4]

#### Victor
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] possesses final authority over the Maid Squad.
He:
- selects its central members;
- determines its overall purpose;
- provides Bloodline empowerment;
- issues high-level missions;
- equips or enhances members;
- and decides which individuals belong to his immediate household.

#### Head Maid
Kaguya is the professional commander.
Her authority originates from both seniority and competence.
She was [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s first maid, was already an experienced assassin and servant before the other members were recruited, and repeatedly assumes operational command when several Battle Maids are deployed together.[4]
The others frequently refer to her as Boss.

#### Leader Maids
The eight leader positions represent the apex of the future maid organization.
The leaders are not simply senior household workers.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] intended them to be the origin points from which later maid branches would develop.[3]

#### Subordinate Maids
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] planned for future subordinate maids to descend from or be organized beneath the eight leaders rather than all being directly created through his own Blood.[3]
This creates an important distinction between:
1. Victor's direct Battle Maids;
2. the eight planned leaders;
3. their future subordinate lines;
4. ordinary household maids.

#### Kaguya Elderblood Blank
Kaguya is [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s first personal maid, Head Maid and operational commander.
Her position does not come merely from joining first.
She possessed the strongest professional maid training among the original group and spent centuries developing assassination, espionage and household skills before Victor became a Vampire.
Her Pride title corresponds naturally to her perfectionism and immense pride in the profession.[3]
Victor repeatedly entrusts her with command during serious operations.
During the [[Greek Pantheon]] conflict he explicitly ordered the Battle Maids to work together beneath Kaguya.[4]
Kaguya retained her identity as a maid even after entering Victor's family at far higher status.
Chapter 1166, long into the Imperial era, still describes her as the perfect maid and shows her naturally taking responsibility for organizing the family's meal.[20]

#### Bruna Elderblood
Bruna is [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s second core maid and representative of Lust.
Victor selected her after observing courage that exceeded what he expected from an ordinary victim trapped in a hopeless situation.[8][2]
She later became:
- a member of Victor's direct family;
- an important early devotee of Victor;
- foundational to the [[Blood God Religion]];
- and a far more powerful supernatural being than the young Vampire Victor originally recruited.

#### Eve Elderblood
Eve is [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s third maid and representative of Wrath.
Victor's relationship with Eve has always possessed a strongly paternal component.
He rescued her, gave her an identity and repeatedly emphasized that she retained the right to choose what she wanted rather than being obligated to remain his servant simply because he saved her.[13]
After her Dragon evolution, her mutant flames improved dramatically and Alter Eve became capable of manifesting outside Eve's body.[5]

#### Maria Elderblood
Maria is [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s fourth maid and representative of Gluttony.
She began as a Human Vampire Hunter and later became a Ghoul.
Her powers eventually evolved around that origin rather than being erased when she entered Victor's Bloodline.
Following her Draconic development, Maria became capable of producing Ghouls directly from corpses and exercising far greater control over the species.[5]

#### Roberta Elderblood
Roberta occupies the Envy position.
She possesses abilities involving memory manipulation and shares a unique relationship with [[Medusa]].
Later evolution allowed Roberta to use a controlled form of Medusa's petrifying gaze while her own mental and memory abilities increased considerably.[5]

#### Roxanne Elderblood
Roxanne occupies Sloth and is the sixth explicit maid.
Her existence as a World Tree means she should not be classified simply as another Blood Dragon in the infobox.
She is simultaneously:
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s maid;
- family;
- World Tree;
- spiritual partner;
- and a major component of his internal cosmic ecosystem.

### Medusa's Status
[[Medusa]] is associated with the Maid Squad because of her shared existence with Roberta.
She can:
- speak through Roberta;
- manifest through their shared body;
- contribute Gorgon powers;
- and interact directly with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and the Squad.
However, Medusa is not an independent ninth Sin leader.
Chapter 470 assigns the Envy position to Roberta, and the intended organization contains eight leader positions total.[3]
During the [[Greek Pantheon]] conflict, Medusa's presence became particularly relevant when she noticed [[Apollo]] and demanded the opportunity to confront him through Roberta's position.[4]

### Duties

#### Personal Service
The core members remain [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s personal maids.
Their duties can include:
- accompanying Victor;
- organizing household activities;
- managing personal matters;
- preparing or overseeing family events;
- carrying messages;
- and coordinating other attendants.
The enormous political status eventually accumulated by the women does not make this role obsolete.

#### Family Protection
Protecting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family is the reason the organization exists.[2]
Victor recognized that enemies could attack people he loved while he was occupied elsewhere.
The maids therefore became a mobile security layer capable of acting independently.

#### Assassination
Kaguya's [[Blank Clan]] training gives the Squad a strong assassination tradition.
The Battle Maids are suited to eliminating:
- infiltrators;
- spies;
- hostile supernatural beings;
- assassins;
- and targets too dangerous for ordinary servants or soldiers.

#### Intelligence
Kaguya frequently serves as one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s direct information conduits.
Her skills include:
- surveillance;
- infiltration;
- investigation;
- shadow-based movement;
- information gathering;
- and coordination with other intelligence networks.

#### Combat
All core maids are expected to become combat-capable.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] never intended repeated consumption of his Blood to replace genuine training.
The original plan explicitly included difficult missions and continuous combat development.[2]

#### Battlefield Command
Kaguya commands the group when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] does not personally micromanage the operation.
Chapter 796 provides the clearest example, with Victor explicitly ordering the maids to operate together under Kaguya.[4]

#### Representing Victor
As [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became increasingly powerful, it became inefficient for him to personally respond to every minor enemy, dispute or threat.
The Battle Maids, wives and other senior family members increasingly handle lesser problems in his place.

The distinction between maid and family gradually disappeared.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually gathered Kaguya, Eve, Bruna, Maria, Roberta and Roxanne and discussed bringing them permanently into his Clan and Bloodline.[13]
The process was not presented as merely another promotion.
Victor wanted them to belong to the family he considered his.

Kaguya required special treatment because her identity remained tied to the [[Blank Clan]].
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] did not force her to abandon that heritage.
The resulting ritual removed the ancestral Vampire influence inherited through Vlad while preserving the Blank lineage Kaguya personally valued.[14][15]
She consequently became Kaguya Alucard-Blank, later [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]].

### Battle Maids
The term Battle Maid reflects the point at which the military side of the Squad became impossible to ignore.
The women still preserve the appearance and identity of maids while operating as fully armed supernatural combatants.

#### Greek Pantheon War
The [[Greek Pantheon]] battle represents the clearest demonstration of the organization's military development.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] summoned the Battle Maids onto the battlefield and immediately informed them that their enemies were Gods.[4]
The group responded seriously rather than questioning the order.
Victor instructed them:
- to work together;
- to obey Kaguya's battlefield command;
- to kill hostile Gods;
- and to spare designated targets such as [[Poseidon]] and [[Athena]].[4]

#### God-Killing Weapons
The maids' ordinary equipment did not inherently possess the properties required to permanently kill Gods.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] solved the problem directly.
His Blood covered their weapons and granted them the ability to kill Divine targets permanently.[4]
CHAPTER 796
BLOOD-EMPOWERED GOD-KILLING ARMAMENT
Victor temporarily altered the properties of the Battle Maids' weapons so that Divine enemies could be killed permanently.
This did not mean the maids independently possessed Victor's Soul-severing capability at all times.
The feat depended upon Victor empowering their equipment.

### Bloodline Evolution

#### Early Vampiric Development
Several maids originally developed primarily as Vampires or Vampire-adjacent beings.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood repeatedly improved their physical and supernatural potential.

#### Blood Dragons
Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Draconic evolution, several Battle Maids became [[Blood Dragons]].[5]
This should not be generalized to every associated member.
Roxanne remains fundamentally a World Tree, while [[Medusa]] remains a Gorgon-associated existence through Roberta.

#### Kaguya
Kaguya's Dragon transformation greatly strengthened her Shadow and Darkness abilities.[5]
She subsequently displayed emerging Divinity.[19]

#### Eve
Eve's mutant Fire evolved considerably.
Alter Eve also became capable of separating from Eve's body and acting independently.[5]

#### Maria
Maria's Ghoul abilities expanded from personal racial traits into the ability to create and control Ghouls on a much greater level.[5]

#### Roberta
Roberta gained stronger memory-related powers and controlled access to [[Medusa]]'s Petrification.[5]

#### Bruna
Bruna later shares the wider Draconic development of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s transformed family, although the Chapter 920 scene focuses more heavily upon Kaguya, Eve, Maria and Roberta.

#### Roxanne
Roxanne follows an entirely different evolutionary path as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s World Tree and should not be forced into the same racial classification as the others.

### Power and Combat Ability
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s original claim that he wanted maids capable of destroying the world was an objective, not a description of their strength in Chapter 185.[2]
At the time:
- Bruna was still inexperienced;
- Eve had only recently entered Victor's household;
- and even Kaguya was vastly below the level she would later attain.
Their growth came through years and eventually immense periods of:
- Bloodline development;
- physical training;
- martial training;
- dangerous missions;
- supernatural evolution;
- Draconic transformation;
- and exposure to increasingly powerful enemies.
By Chapter 796, Victor considered the active Battle Maids useful in a direct war against Gods provided they coordinated under Kaguya and possessed suitable anti-Divine weaponry.[4]
By Chapter 920, their racial powers had undergone another qualitative transformation.[5]
The Maid Squad therefore should not be assigned one permanent collective power level across the entire story.

### Equipment
The Squad does not use a universal standardized weapon.
Equipment is selected according to the individual.
Common characteristics include:
- equipment suitable for supernatural combat;
- weapons compatible with each maid's preferred fighting style;
- magical or Blood-based enhancement;
- combat clothing maintaining a maid aesthetic;
- and specialized gear for covert operations.
During the [[Greek Pantheon]] conflict, the Battle Maids arrived armed and armored while retaining their characteristic maid appearance.[4]

### Relationship with Victor
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s relationship with his personal maids is fundamentally different from an ordinary employer-servant hierarchy.

#### Possessiveness
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] considers the maids part of the people personally belonging beneath his protection.
Kaguya's near death demonstrated this very early.
Victor was not merely upset because a useful servant had almost been lost.
He was terrified because someone he loved had nearly died.[1]

#### Their opinions matter
The maids are allowed to disagree and express opinions.
Roberta explicitly reminded Kaguya that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] listens to what the maids think and does not regard them as mindless tools.[9]

#### Love
By Chapter 491, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s feelings toward the six maids were openly beyond ordinary employment.
He loved them.[12]
The exact nature of each individual relationship differed, but they all occupied family-level emotional importance.

#### Freedom
Eve demonstrates [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s approach particularly clearly.
When discussing permanent family and Bloodline membership, Victor refused to treat Eve's rescue as something obligating her to accept whatever relationship he wanted.
Her choice remained hers.[13]

### Relationship Between Members
The Squad combines hierarchy with sibling-like familiarity.
Kaguya remains the unquestioned senior professional member.
The others can tease her and behave casually around one another outside operations, but battlefield situations produce an immediate change in behavior.
Chapter 796 shows the group becoming serious the moment they realize the scale of the threat and following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Kaguya without hesitation.[4]

### Current Status
The Maid Squad remains active conceptually and socially within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household.
The original institution was not dissolved when:
- Clan Alucard became the Elderblood Clan;
- Victor founded Dragon's Nest;
- the family became Draconic;
- or the [[Elderblood Empire]] expanded across galaxies.
Instead, the position evolved alongside its members.
By Chapter 1166, Kaguya is still described as the perfect maid and naturally takes charge of household organization despite simultaneously possessing vastly greater familial and Divine importance than she did during the Squad's early years.[20]
Bruna, Eve, Maria and Roberta likewise remain present as major members of Victor's family in the late Imperial era.
The Maid Squad's modern importance is therefore less about whether the original six spend every day performing domestic chores and more about what the title represents:

ProtectionGuardians of the Elderblood family.

IdentityA role maintained even after enormous personal evolution.

### Trivia
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s original plan consisted of seven maids.[2]
- The later eight refers to the Sin-associated leader structure introduced in Chapter 470.[3]
- The eight-seat structure includes Melancholy, which Victor identifies as an older Cardinal Sin removed from later classifications.[3]
- Six Sin positions are directly assigned in the novel.
- Greed and Melancholy were unfilled when the structure was introduced.[3]
- Albedo's Greed and Kali's Melancholy assignments originate from author commentary unless later novel material explicitly formalizes them.
- Kaguya is the Head Maid regardless of the later organizational expansion.
- Roxanne is explicitly called Victor's sixth maid.[10]
- [[Medusa]] is associated with the Squad through Roberta but does not possess a separate ninth Sin position.
- Victor intentionally chose a female maid organization partly because he preferred women surrounding his wives and enjoyed the maid aesthetic.
- The Battle Maids' combat armor preserves their maid appearance.[4]
- Victor temporarily empowered their weapons to permanently kill Gods during the [[Greek Pantheon]] war.[4]
- Kaguya, Eve, Maria and Roberta are explicitly examined after their Draconic transformations in Chapter 920.[5]
- Kaguya displays developing Divinity in Chapter 921.[19]
- The group is racially mixed in the modern era; describing every Maid Squad member simply as a Blood Dragon is inaccurate because Roxanne is fundamentally a World Tree and Medusa remains an associated Gorgon existence.
- Kaguya is still described as the perfect maid thousands of years into the Imperial era.[20]
