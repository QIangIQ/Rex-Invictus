The Empire of Light, also known as Veillon, is an extremely advanced civilization of the Upper Sectors ruled by the Entity known as <Light>, the Emperor of Light. The Empire was first encountered when [[Victor Elderblood]] sent an independent reconnaissance clone beyond the barrier of his native Sector. The Clone initially learned the Empire's name by devouring the Soul of one of its soldiers, identifying both the Empire of Light and the name Veillon.[1]
Veillon is a heavily militarized expansionist civilization combining extremely advanced technology with Divinity and pure Light Energy. Even its ordinary frontline soldiers possess Power comparable to [[Primordial Gods]], while its military can field tens of thousands of such troops, fleets of warships, specialized anti-ability technologies, elite combatants known as the Solus and strategic weapons capable of erasing an entire Solar System.[1][2][3]
Despite presenting itself as a civilization of purity and righteousness, the Empire operates according to an absolutist ideology in which anything rejected by its Light can be classified as Evil and therefore subject to purification or eradication. Its dependence upon Light extends far beyond religion. The Empire establishes enormous sources of pure Positive Energy within occupied Solar Systems, allowing <Light>'s influence to permeate its forces and infrastructure. Examination of one such Artifact revealed that prolonged integration with the system gradually strips affected Beings of their independence until their thoughts and actions become subordinate to the will of Light.[1][3]
After analyzing [[Vik-Ir-loah]]'s memories, [[Solus 969]]'s memories, the technological information stolen from a Veillon installation and the Solar-System-destroying weapon used against [[Victor Walker (Clone)|Victor's Clone]], Velnorah estimated Veillon to be a Type IV Civilization within the upper echelons of that category. The classification was presented as an informed estimate rather than an absolute measurement of the Empire's full capabilities.[4]

### Characteristics

#### Civilization
The true size of Veillon remains unknown. [[Scathach]] concluded from the Empire's military presence and organization that it must extend through an unknown number of Solar Systems, and warned against judging its complete military strength from the single force [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] encountered. The Eleventh Frontline represented only one formation within a civilization expected to possess additional elites, weapons, technologies and hidden resources.[2]
Veillon's expansion is at least partially based around the acquisition of new territory and resources. [[Vik-Ir-loah]] initially regarded the operation in which Victor appeared as a routine process of acquiring resources for the Empire. Powerful outsiders can also apparently be assimilated rather than immediately destroyed; after recognizing Victor's Grandmaster status, Vik-Ir-loah attempted to recruit him and offered the possibility of joining the Solus after the Empire had "purified" his Evil.[2]
The Empire considers itself intrinsically superior to civilizations and Beings outside its system. Its soldiers repeatedly described Victor as an inferior Being from the Lower Sectors and treated the authority of their Light as an objective moral standard. Vik-Ir-loah summarized the ideology directly: anything their Light could not accept was considered Evil and Evil was to be eradicated.[1]

#### Artificial Gods
The ordinary soldiers encountered by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] were highly abnormal Divine Beings. Upon consuming one of their Souls, Victor discovered that the soldier possessed no Race and that his body was composed from pure Energy. Even the Soul itself appeared unnatural. Victor compared the process to something interfering while the individual was becoming a God, leaving behind a Divine existence containing fundamental flaws.[1]
Victor consequently regarded them as incomplete or "false" Gods. They were neither conventional Gods nor Demigods, yet their raw combat ability remained extremely high. The ordinary soldiers encountered by the Clone were individually comparable in Power Level to [[Primordial Gods]] and possessed sufficient training and equipment that Victor believed an ordinary Primordial God such as [[Erebus]] would be annihilated against the army.[1]
Their weakness was therefore not a lack of raw Power, but a lack of the depth possessed by naturally developed high-level Beings. Victor's family repeatedly distinguished the Empire's large numbers of powerful artificial soldiers from combatants who had accumulated thousands of years of personal experience, unique abilities and true martial development.[2]
The soldiers were not completely incapable of emotion. Although they initially displayed remarkably little reaction even when allies died, Victor successfully provoked anger by insulting their Empire and Emperor. They later displayed fear when confronted by Victor's Negative Divinities. Their individuality was therefore suppressed and heavily conditioned rather than portrayed as completely nonexistent in every circumstance.[1][2]

#### Light and Free Will
The Empire's greatest defining characteristic is its dependence upon Light.
Within the Solar System investigated by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], a large Artifact composed from pure Light was installed within the stellar base. Victor Elderblood and [[Amara Elderblood]] identified the substance as pure Positive Energy. Victor's family interpreted the structure as a form of battery through which the Emperor could extend his influence and observation into Solar Systems controlled by the Empire.[3]
Further inspection revealed that the Artifact was considerably more invasive than a conventional Energy source. Its influence gradually corrupted affected Beings until their entire existences functioned as tools of Light. Victor specifically observed that the process could eventually remove even the freedom to think or perform basic actions independently if Light did not permit them.[3]
This distinguished Veillon's system from ordinary Faith-based loyalty. Its subjects could ultimately become extensions of the Emperor's will rather than merely worshippers choosing to obey him.[3]

### Military
Veillon possesses an enormous professional military structured around rigid hierarchy, overwhelming numbers, technological support and the authority of the Emperor.
During the first encounter, the Eleventh Frontline under [[Vik-Ir-loah]] fielded more than 20,000 soldiers. After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was reclassified as a serious threat, every available soldier stationed within the Solar System was summoned, increasing the force to approximately 100,000 warriors supported by 50 warships, five of which were substantially larger than the others.[1][2]
The Empire's troops displayed absolute respect for military hierarchy. During combat, thousands of soldiers refused to independently alter their tactics without receiving instructions from their Commander. Orders, reinforcements, countermeasures and escalation protocols were coordinated through the Empire's artificial intelligence and command network.[2]

#### Commanders
Frontline forces are commanded by officers such as [[Vik-Ir-loah]], Commander of the Eleventh Frontline. Commanders possess authority to mobilize troops, request specialized countermeasures, contact the Solus and activate large-scale emergency protocols such as the Stellar Protocol.[1][2]
The Empire also appears capable of remotely controlling or terminating compromised personnel. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] began corrupting Vik-Ir-loah's Soul, the Empire's AI attempted several countermeasures before deciding that the risk of information leakage justified deleting the Commander's "program". Victor prevented the process through Soul Manipulation.[3]

#### Solus
The Solus are Veillon's elite warriors and occupy a military status far above ordinary soldiers.
Their hierarchy is numbered from 1000 to 1. Lower numerical rankings indicate greater status and strength, while single-digit Solus are described as the strongest and closest to the [[Aurethys Vaelthion|Emperor of Light]].[2]
The normal requirements for becoming a Solus include:
- possessing Primordial-God-level Divinity, meaning the highest possible Divinity within the applicant's respective Concept;
- performing conquests or achievements for the Empire;
- and undergoing whatever purification or integration Veillon requires of its members.[2]
Grandmasters are valued so highly that [[Vik-Ir-loah]] believed [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] could bypass the ordinary progression requirements and enter the organization after being purified.[2]
[[Solus 969]] was the only numbered Solus directly encountered during Victor's reconnaissance. He was dramatically more capable than the Empire's ordinary troops, possessed enough combat experience to recognize that Victor was intentionally prolonging their fight for intelligence, and could exchange attacks with the Clone after he entered his Demon King of Tyranny form. However, even 969 was ultimately overwhelmed once Victor stopped gathering information and fought to end the confrontation.[3]

#### Grandmasters
True Grandmasters are extremely rare even within Veillon.
After recognizing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s martial mastery, [[Vik-Ir-loah]] stated internally that the number of Grandmasters serving his Empire could be counted on one hand. The revelation immediately forced him to reevaluate Victor from an allegedly inferior Lower-Sector Being into a military threat worthy of exceptional attention.[2]
Veillon's respect for Grandmasters indicates that its advanced technology and enormous numbers have not made genuine mastery obsolete. Personal experience and martial development remain strategically valuable even within one of the Upper Sectors' strongest civilizations.

### Technology
Veillon's technology is deeply integrated with Divinity. Its equipment cannot be accurately described as ordinary machinery; technological systems interact directly with supernatural abilities, Souls, Energy and Divine phenomena.[1][3]

#### Combat Analysis
The Empire possesses automated systems capable of analyzing hostile Beings during battle. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was progressively identified as a speed-based combatant, a [[Ares|God of War]], a Grandmaster and a user of several supernatural abilities. The system was capable of detecting new abilities and immediately attempting specialized countermeasures.[1][3]
During Chapter 1134, for example, the Empire's AI directly detected both Cloning and Umbrakinesis when another Victor appeared behind [[Vik-Ir-loah]] and immediately activated the appropriate countermeasure systems.[3]

#### Suppression Fields
One of Veillon's most important technologies consists of enormous pillar-like Artifacts capable of creating specialized suppression zones.
After analyzing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s fighting style, the Empire deployed devices intended to counter:
- extreme physical speed;
- Spatial movement;
- and Reality manipulation.[1]
The technology was effective. Victor became noticeably slower, his normal movement methods became restricted and even using Lightning to increase his speed produced greater pressure upon his body. The field did not simply fail because Victor was stronger; he was forced to change his combat approach and rely upon his Grandmaster martial skill to destroy one of the generators.[1]
The Empire also demonstrated redundancy. Damaged technology could be replaced as the conflict continued, forcing Victor to repeatedly account for newly deployed countermeasures.

#### Ability Countermeasures
Veillon possesses additional automated responses to unusual supernatural abilities. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] used Cloning and Umbrakinesis, the Empire immediately attempted countermeasures against both powers. Its anti-cloning system failed against Victor and requested additional data, demonstrating that the Empire's countermeasures are advanced but not universally effective against every unfamiliar ability.[3]
The Souls of ordinary soldiers likewise contained protections against information-based predation. Even after Victor consumed an entire soldier's Soul, only basic information could initially be extracted because the Empire had deliberately designed countermeasures against Beings capable of stealing information through Soul consumption.[1]

#### Artificial Intelligence
A centralized Empire AI coordinates much of Veillon's military infrastructure.
The AI can:
- identify supernatural abilities;
- activate appropriate countermeasures;
- track Empire property;
- report system failures;
- coordinate battlefield protocols;
- process requests from commanding officers;
- determine when information leakage has become dangerous;
- and attempt to terminate compromised personnel.[1][2][3]
Its integration extends deeply enough that [[Vik-Ir-loah]] was treated partly as Empire "property", with the system attempting to delete his program once [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s corruption threatened classified information.[3]

#### Warships
Veillon maintains technologically and Divinely advanced fleets capable of operating throughout interplanetary space.
During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s encounter, approximately fifty ships were mobilized within a single Solar System. Five were substantially larger capital vessels, while the remaining ships were smaller combat craft. Their weapon systems could focus large quantities of Energy upon individual targets and coordinate fire with the Empire's soldiers.[2]
The exact number of fleets possessed by the complete Empire is unknown.

#### Light Batteries
Veillon establishes massive sources of pure Light or Positive Energy inside controlled Solar Systems.
The Artifact investigated by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] functioned as the central power source for the Empire's infrastructure throughout that system. Victor's family compared the structure to a battery planted in a Solar System through which <Light> could observe and spread his influence.[3]
The structure supported:
- Empire military installations;
- suppression systems;
- Light-based soldiers;
- the process used to destabilize the local star;
- and the wider local network of Empire technology.[3]
When Victor destroyed it, every active Empire system inside the Solar System lost power, the suppression field shut down, the stellar destabilization process stopped and the Light surrounding the Empire's soldiers visibly weakened.[3]

#### Stellar Protocol
The Stellar Protocol is a military emergency measure authorized against threats the Empire considers too dangerous to eliminate conventionally.
After witnessing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s numerous Negative Divinities, [[Vik-Ir-loah]] ordered the protocol activated even though doing so risked destroying the entire Solar System. The local system immediately began the process of destabilizing its star.[2]
The protocol demonstrates that Veillon is willing to sacrifice complete Solar Systems and its own personnel if doing so is considered necessary to eliminate a sufficiently dangerous enemy.
The process was interrupted when Victor destroyed the Solar System's central Light Artifact.[3]

#### Strategic Solar-System Weaponry
Veillon's true strategic military power extended far beyond the forces deployed directly around [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
After absorbing [[Solus 969]], Victor discovered through his memories an artificial fortress positioned extremely far outside the local Solar System. The structure was described as being larger than an entire Solar System and possessed multiple enormous cannons.[3]
When fired, its attack erased the entire Solar System containing [[Victor Walker (Clone)|Victor's Clone]]. The restricted Clone was incapable of defending against the weapon; the narration stated that only the original Victor possessed the means to deal with an attack of that magnitude.[3]
This fortress provided the clearest direct demonstration of Veillon's strategic destructive capability.

### Methods of Expansion
Veillon appears to expand through a combination of resource acquisition, military conquest, Light infrastructure and ideological assimilation.
A general pattern demonstrated or implied by the first encounter is:
Expansion into a Solar System
↓
Establishment of Veillon Infrastructure
↓
Installation of a Positive-Energy / Light Power Source
↓
Military and Technological Integration
↓
Observation and Influence through Light
↓
Acquisition of Resources and Useful Beings
↓
Purification / Assimilation of Acceptable Targets
↓
Eradication of Beings Classified as Evil or Unusable
The exact procedure may differ between regions, and the novel has not yet shown Veillon conquering a complete civilization from beginning to end.

#### Resource Acquisition
[[Vik-Ir-loah]] described the operation occurring before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s appearance as a normal process of acquiring resources for the Empire. This indicates that Upper-Sector expansion is not motivated exclusively by ideology; Veillon also actively exploits foreign territories for material or strategic benefit.[2]

#### Assimilation
Powerful outsiders are not automatically destroyed. [[Vik-Ir-loah]] attempted to recruit [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] after recognizing his Divinity and Grandmaster status, suggesting that useful foreign Beings may be incorporated into Veillon after undergoing purification.[2]

#### Purification
The Empire repeatedly distinguishes between Light and Evil. A Being considered sinful or incompatible with Veillon can apparently be "purified", after which their previous crimes may be forgiven if they become useful to the Empire.[2]
Considering the properties of the Light Artifact discovered in Chapter 1134, the long-term result of Veillon's integration can extend much further than ideological conversion and eventually erode genuine free will.[3]

### Encounter with the Elderblood Empire

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Veillon first became known to the [[Elderblood Empire]] when [[Victor Elderblood]] sent an independent Clone beyond his Mid-Level Sector as a reconnaissance measure.
The Clone encountered [[Vik-Ir-loah]] and the Eleventh Frontline and rapidly turned the encounter into open warfare. More than twenty thousand Primordial-God-level soldiers initially engaged him, followed by escalating reinforcements, specialized suppression technologies, approximately one hundred thousand total warriors, fifty warships and eventually [[Solus 969]].[1][2][3]
Although [[Victor Walker (Clone)|Victor's Clone]] had been intentionally stripped of several of the original's greatest powers, its Grandmaster martial skill, Soul abilities, access to Negative Divinities and combat experience allowed it to repeatedly outperform Veillon's ordinary troops. It killed thousands of soldiers, destroyed major warships, corrupted Vik-Ir-loah and infiltrated the Empire's stellar installation.[1][2][3]
Before destroying the local Light infrastructure, Victor extracted the facility's technological database and transmitted it back to the original Victor. Velnorah isolated the stolen information inside a separate Dimension rather than connecting potentially hostile foreign data directly to the Elderblood network.[3]
After destroying the Positive-Energy core, Victor eliminated the remaining local forces and absorbed Solus 969. Veillon responded from an enormous distant fortress by firing a weapon that erased the entire Solar System together with Victor's Clone.[3]
The reconnaissance nevertheless succeeded. Veillon revealed substantial portions of its military structure, technology, ideology and strategic capabilities while the original Victor and his civilization remained protected behind their Sector barrier.

#### Those Who Rule Reality
The information stolen from Veillon became one of the principal sources used by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family to evaluate the powers of the Upper Sectors.
After examining [[Vik-Ir-loah]]'s memories, [[Solus 969]]'s memories, the stolen database and the final Solar-System-level attack, Velnorah estimated that the Empire of Light was most likely a Type IV Civilization within the upper echelons of that category.[4]
The evidence also strongly connected the Empire's ruler to the System-ranked Entity <Light>, previously identified as the chosen representative of Positivity. Victor's family concluded that Veillon was likely one of the strongest Factions of the Upper Sectors and that <Light> was one of the major rulers operating there.[2][4]
The Empire of Light's demonstrated capabilities became one of the immediate reasons Victor accelerated the military, technological and territorial development of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Rather than risk entering the Upper Sectors unprepared, Victor began consolidating his entire native Sector and preparing his civilization for eventual contact with powers operating on Veillon's scale.[4][5]

### Strengths
- Enormous Military Numbers: A single Solar-System deployment contained approximately one hundred thousand soldiers with individual Power comparable to [[Primordial Gods]].[1][2]
- High Baseline Power: Even ordinary soldiers possess raw strength that would make them major threats in less-developed Sectors.[1]
- Divine Technology: Veillon seamlessly combines technology with Divinity, Souls and supernatural Energy.[1][3]
- Rapid Threat Analysis: Empire systems analyze enemy abilities and deploy specialized countermeasures during battle.[1][3]
- Military Redundancy: Veillon can replace destroyed equipment, summon reinforcements and continually escalate the resources committed against a threat.[1][2]
- Elite Combatants: The Solus exist above ordinary military personnel, while the Empire also possesses a small number of true Grandmasters.[2]
- Information Security: Soldier Souls contain safeguards against memory and information theft, and compromised personnel can be remotely terminated to protect classified data.[1][3]
- Solar-System Infrastructure: Central Light Artifacts allow Veillon to sustain military and technological control throughout entire Solar Systems.[3]
- Strategic Destructive Power: Veillon possesses fortresses and weapon systems capable of erasing entire Solar Systems.[3]
- Unknown Full Scale: The Eleventh Frontline represented only a small observed portion of the complete civilization, leaving its greatest military assets and highest-ranked Solus largely unknown.[2]

### Trivia
- The Empire's formal name is Veillon, while Empire of Light is the title by which it repeatedly identifies itself.[1]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] mocked both names as being excessively simplistic and accused the Emperor of Light of having no creativity.[1]
- Veillon's ordinary soldiers can individually reach a raw Power Level comparable to [[Primordial Gods]], but the Elderblood family considered their lack of depth and experience a major distinction between them and naturally developed powerful Beings.[1][2]
- The Empire's dependence on hierarchy is so extensive that its soldiers remained stationary during moments of uncertainty until [[Vik-Ir-loah]] issued direct orders.[2]
- Veillon considers Grandmasters exceptionally valuable. Vik-Ir-loah believed the Empire possessed so few that their total could be counted on one hand.[2]
- The Solus hierarchy extends from 1000 to 1, with lower numbers representing greater status and single-digit members being the strongest and closest to <Light>.[2]
- The Empire's initial attempt to destroy Victor through the Stellar Protocol involved destabilizing the local star. This is separate from the far more powerful external fortress that eventually erased the Solar System outright.[2][3]
- Victor's destruction of the central Light Artifact demonstrated that at least some Veillon Solar Systems rely upon highly centralized Energy infrastructure.[3]
- Velnorah's Type IV classification was explicitly an estimate based upon the intelligence available after Victor's reconnaissance; the Empire's complete military strength has not yet been directly shown.[4]
