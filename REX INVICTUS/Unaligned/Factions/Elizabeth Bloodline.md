The Elizabeth Trait/Gene is an ultra-rare mutation within the [[Lykos Clan]], occurring only once every three to four generations. Named after Elizabeth Lykos, the most powerful wife of the Werewolf Progenitor, this genetic mutation significantly enhances the instinctual, physical, and combat abilities of its inheritor. Unlike standard [[Werewolves]], those with this mutation awaken at a much younger age, exhibit superior battle instincts, and possess a level of dominance and power unmatched by others of their race.
This trait is exclusive to female Werewolves and cannot be inherited by males​[1] which was later on proven otherwise due to beings like [[Victor Elderblood]] who could control souls to such a high degree that some restriction due to genetics isn't even a concern for him and he could grant this power to anyone related to Lykos as their soul inherently have this power inside there soul records.

### Powers & Abilities
- Supernatural Strength: Far surpassing that of regular [[Werewolves]], allowing for devastating physical attacks​.[1]
- Enhanced Speed & Reflexes: The user moves significantly faster than normal Werewolves, with reaction speeds that allow them to evade and counter attacks effortlessly​.[1]
- Heightened Senses: Includes superior eyesight, hearing, and smell, enabling the user to track enemies or prey over vast distances​.[1]
- Enhanced Durability & Endurance: Capable of withstanding massive amounts of damage and fighting for long durations without tiring​.[1]
- Early Werewolf [[Awakening]]: Unlike regular Werewolves, who awaken between 18-21 years old, those with the Elizabeth Trait awaken as early as 3-6 years old, resulting in a significant early power advantage​.[1]
- Perfect Werewolf Transformation: The user gains access to a superior Werewolf form, far surpassing ordinary Werewolves in raw power, size, and ferocity​.[1]
- Primal Dominance Effect: The user naturally emits an aura of supremacy, compelling lesser Werewolves and weaker beings to submit instinctively​.[1]
- Instinctive Combat Mastery: The user instinctively understands combat, allowing them to fight at a master level even without formal training​.[1]
- Unparalleled Battle Adaptation: Gains strength rapidly from combat experiences, evolving in real-time during battles​.[1]
- Genetic Lineage Enhancement: Offspring born from a mother with the Elizabeth Gene have 1.5x to 3x more potential and strength than the previous generation​.[2]
- Exclusive to Female Werewolves: This genetic trait is never inherited by males, further solidifying the matriarchal dominance within the [[Lykos Clan]]​.[1]

### Side Effects
- Emotional Volatility: The user experiences more intense emotions, making them prone to aggression and impulsivity​.[2]
- Instinct-Driven Decision Making: The enhanced instincts can override rational thought, leading to more primal, reactionary behavior​.[2]
- Stronger Desire for an Alpha Mate: The user is instinctually drawn to powerful partners and may experience agitation or restlessness if this need is unfulfilled​.[2]
- Instinctual Satisfaction Requirement: Without proper training and discipline, an Elizabeth Gene user may feel frustration, agitation, or instability if their battle, dominance, or survival instincts are not met​.[2]

### Trivia
- Every Matriarch of the [[Lykos Clan]] throughout history has possessed the Elizabeth Trait, making it a symbol of leadership and power​.[2]
- All Generals of the [[Lycaon|Werewolf King]] have historically come from the Lykos Clan, reinforcing the legendary status of the Elizabeth Trait​.[2]
- First Patriarch of the Lykos Clan, [[Edward Jonathan Elizabeth Lykos]] initially didn't had Elizabeth trait, but was granted by [[Victor Elderblood]].[3]
