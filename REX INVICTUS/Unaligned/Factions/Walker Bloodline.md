The Walker Bloodline is the original human hereditary lineage of Victor Elderblood, who was born Victor Walker. Despite originating from an apparently ordinary Human family with no known supernatural ancestry, the Walker lineage possesses an abnormal hereditary characteristic: the potential carried by the bloodline progressively increases through successive generations. Rather than representing an ability suddenly appearing in Victor, the phenomenon accumulated through his ancestry until Victor was eventually born as the culmination of countless generations of gradual development.[1]
The bloodline was only properly understood extremely late in Victor's life. [[Shiva]], after observing Victor and the extraordinary potential displayed throughout his descendants, reasoned that at least part of Victor's abnormal talent had to be hereditary. His attempt to exploit that inheritance by obtaining one of Victor's ancestors ultimately caused Victor to investigate the lineage directly, revealing that the Walker family's unusual potential was not concentrated equally throughout its history. Instead, each generation inherited the accumulated development of those preceding it and added another step to the progression. Consequently, a distant Walker ancestor could be biologically related to Victor while possessing only a fraction of the potential eventually concentrated within Victor himself.[1]

### Overview

Unlike a conventional supernatural bloodline in which descendants inherit a fixed ability—such as the [[Snow Bloodline]]'s Fire, the [[Scarlett Bloodline]]'s Ice or the [[Fulger Bloodline]]'s Lightning—the Walker inheritance appears to improve the capacity of the lineage itself. A later Walker is not automatically stronger than every earlier ancestor in terms of actual combat ability, since training, species, lifespan, experiences and other inherited traits remain important. What increases through the bloodline is the descendant's underlying potential.[1]

### Characteristics

#### Generational Potential
The defining property of the Walker Bloodline is a cumulative increase in potential across generations. Every successive link in the lineage inherits what had already been developed before it, with that hereditary foundation continuing to improve as the family proceeds into later generations.[1]

The important distinction is that the bloodline does not appear to instantly empower every newly born descendant. Its effect concerns the quality and developmental ceiling inherited by later generations. A Walker living centuries before Victor therefore would not necessarily display anything obviously supernatural even though that person formed part of the same hereditary progression.[1]

#### Cumulative Inheritance
The Walker Bloodline's development is cumulative rather than resetting with each child. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] determined that his own potential represented the accumulated result of a very long biological chain instead of a random mutation existing exclusively within himself.[1]
This distinction became essential when examining [[Veylan Valentin Walker]]. [[Shiva]] expected an ancestor from Victor's family to possess a meaningful percentage of Victor's potential merely because they shared the same lineage. Victor's later analysis demonstrated why that assumption was flawed: an earlier ancestor existed at a much earlier point in the generational progression and therefore had not inherited the countless subsequent generations of accumulated potential that eventually produced Victor.[1]
In practical terms, the bloodline functions less like a single inherited superpower and more like a continually developing hereditary foundation.

#### Victor as the Culmination
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] Walker represents the most extreme known expression of the original Walker Bloodline. By the time of his birth, the progression had continued through an enormous number of Human generations, allowing the accumulated hereditary potential of the lineage to reach an unprecedented point.[1]
Victor's original Human condition initially concealed this potential. He was born physically frail and suffered from severe health problems associated with his extraordinarily rare Rh-null blood.[2] His body's weakness therefore should not be confused with a lack of potential. The Walker Bloodline concerns what an individual is capable of eventually becoming, not necessarily the immediate strength of their original body.
Victor's later transformation into a Vampire, his absorption of several Noble Vampire bloodlines and his eventual evolution into the Blood Dragon Primogenitor provided increasingly powerful bodies and supernatural systems through which that potential could express itself. The Walker Bloodline consequently became one component of an existence that later incorporated numerous unrelated bloodlines, species and conceptual powers.

#### Inheritance by Victor's Descendants
The existence of the Walker Bloodline became particularly relevant when considering [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s descendants. Victor's children consistently displayed exceptional potential even when born from radically different mothers and combinations of supernatural species. [[Shiva]] regarded this repeated pattern as strong evidence that Victor himself possessed a hereditary quality worth investigating.[1]
Because the Walker characteristic is biological and hereditary, Victor's biological descendants inherit the lineage through him alongside the much more powerful supernatural characteristics of the modern Elderblood Clan. The Walker inheritance therefore continues beyond Victor even though his descendants no longer belong to an ordinary Human Walker family.
Its exact contribution to each individual child cannot presently be isolated numerically. Victor's children commonly inherit combinations involving True Dragon physiology, Divine traits, Progenitor characteristics, their mother's bloodline and Victor's later [[Elderblood Bloodline]], making it impossible to state how much of any individual daughter's overall potential originates specifically from the ancient Walker inheritance.

### Relationship with Sanguis Noctis Regis
The Walker Bloodline and [[Sanguis Noctis Regis]] should not be treated as the same bloodline.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was born with Rh-null blood, later identified within supernatural society as Sanguis Noctis Regis or the Blood of the Night King. That blood possesses its own documented effects, including enhanced learning, adaptation, efficient growth, resistance against mental interference and special authority among creatures of the night.[3]
The Walker Bloodline revealed in Chapter 1152 concerns the separate generational inheritance carried through Victor's Human ancestry.[1] Consequently, Victor possesses both:

### History

#### Human Lineage
For most of its existence, the Walker Bloodline belonged to ordinary Humans. Its abnormal property did not manifest as an obvious supernatural power, allowing generation after generation to live without understanding that their descendants were progressively inheriting greater potential.[1]
One confirmed historical member was [[Veylan Valentin Walker]], a distant ancestor of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Veylan lived and died long before Victor's birth and formed one of the many ancestral links through which the Walker inheritance eventually reached Victor. Although biologically important to the lineage, Veylan existed at a significantly earlier stage in its hereditary development and therefore did not possess the accumulated potential of his distant descendant.[1]
The line eventually continued through [[Leon Walker]] and culminated, within the original Human Walker family, in Leon's son Victor Walker.

#### Victor Walker
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was born into the family as an apparently ordinary Human suffering from an extremely weak constitution. Nothing about the Walker family was initially understood to be supernatural, and Victor himself had no knowledge that his ancestry carried an unusual hereditary progression.[2]
His transformation into a Vampire radically changed the circumstances surrounding his development. After being freed from the limitations of his original Human body, Victor demonstrated extraordinary rates of learning, adaptation and growth. However, many of those traits were also directly associated with [[Sanguis Noctis Regis]] and his later supernatural evolution, meaning they should not all be retrospectively attributed to the Walker Bloodline alone.[3]
The true nature of the Walker lineage would remain unidentified until vastly later in the story.

### Shiva's Experiment

#### Interest in Victor's Heredity
During the Imperial era, [[Shiva]] became interested in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s genetics after observing the extraordinary talent surrounding the Emperor and his children. Rather than interfering directly with Victor's daughters, Shiva attempted to obtain an individual belonging to an earlier generation of the same family.[4][1]
The result was a young individual identified as [[Veylan Valentin Walker]], one of Victor's distant Human ancestors. His resemblance to Victor was sufficiently strong to immediately attract suspicion from Victor's family.[4][1]
Shiva admitted that he had dealt with a Primordial Being capable of interfering with information preserved through the [[Akashic Records]]. Souls were effectively the resource Shiva could offer in such a transaction, with the circumstances leading Kali to associate the arrangement with an authority such as the Judge of the Abyss.[1]

#### Adoption and Demigod Transformation
[[Shiva]]'s objective was not merely to study Veylan. Convinced that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s talent possessed a hereditary component, Shiva hoped that even an ancient Walker ancestor might carry approximately a fraction of the potential displayed by Victor. He therefore performed a ritual accepting Veylan as his own blood, effectively making the reconstructed ancestor his son and transforming him into a [[Demigod]].[1]
The experiment nevertheless failed to produce what Shiva expected.

#### The Reconstruction Problem
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] personally examined Veylan, he discovered that the person produced through [[Shiva]]'s arrangement was not a genuine biological resurrection of the original ancestor. The original Veylan had already lived and died. What stood before them was an extraordinarily accurate reconstruction created from information preserved within the [[Akashic Records]] and physically manifested through Shiva's Divine power.[1]
This distinction was decisive.
The reconstructed Veylan possessed the ancestor's information and identity, but he did not possess the authentic physical body that had once carried the Walker Bloodline. Consequently, Shiva's creation could not simply reproduce the hereditary biological potential belonging to the original lineage.[1]
Even an authentic Veylan would not have possessed Victor's full potential. Victor determined that the Walker Bloodline had continued developing throughout every subsequent generation after Veylan's lifetime. Victor was therefore not powerful merely because he belonged to the same family as Veylan; he represented the later culmination of the entire lineage's accumulated progression.[1]
The experiment therefore failed for two separate reasons:
1. The Veylan produced by Shiva was an Akashic reconstruction rather than the original biological body carrying the authentic Walker inheritance.
2. Veylan belonged to an earlier generation and therefore predated the generations of accumulated hereditary development that eventually culminated in Victor.[1]

#### Aftermath
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] regarded [[Shiva]]'s actions as extremely serious, particularly because he had interfered with Victor's ancestry and entered an arrangement involving Primordial authorities without informing the Imperial Family. Shiva was consequently stripped of his leadership position over the [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]], with Kali assuming authority, while Shiva was assigned to fight on dangerous front lines during the coming conflict.[1]
Victor ultimately chose not to preserve Veylan's artificial existence indefinitely. After acknowledging the ancestor whose original life had eventually contributed to Victor's own birth, he allowed the reconstruction to finally rest.[1]
The incident nevertheless gave Victor something significantly more valuable than Shiva intended: an explanation for the abnormal potential hidden within his original Human ancestry.

### Abilities and Traits
The Walker Bloodline does not currently possess a large catalogue of independent supernatural abilities. Its known properties are primarily hereditary.
- Potential Enhancement: The defining characteristic of the Walker Bloodline. The inherent potential transmitted through the lineage increases across successive generations, causing later descendants to possess a progressively greater developmental foundation than earlier members.[1]
- Power Inheritance: Descendants inherit the accumulated hereditary development of preceding Walker generations instead of the bloodline returning to an identical baseline with every birth.[1]
- Generational Accelerated Development: Rather than improving through an instantaneous transformation, the bloodline develops across generations over extremely long periods. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] compared the phenomenon to a gradual generational version of a Zenkai-like increase, with the accumulated result becoming increasingly significant as the lineage continues.[1]
- Enhanced Potential: Later descendants can possess a superior ceiling for growth, development and eventual power compared with earlier members of the lineage. This does not mean every descendant is automatically born physically stronger than their ancestors.[1]
- Hereditary Compatibility: The Walker characteristic remained relevant even after Victor ceased being Human and produced descendants with numerous supernatural species. His biological children continue to inherit his hereditary potential together with other racial and bloodline characteristics.[1]

### Limitations
- The Walker Bloodline increases potential; it has not been shown to automatically provide equivalent immediate combat strength.[1]
- Earlier members of the family can possess significantly less potential than later descendants because they existed before subsequent generations were added to the hereditary progression.[1]
- A reconstruction based only on information from the [[Akashic Records]] does not automatically reproduce the authentic biological bloodline. [[Shiva]]'s reconstructed Veylan is the demonstrated example.[1]
- No exact numerical rate for the increase between generations has been given.
- It is unknown when the bloodline's unusual generational property first appeared.
- It is unknown whether there is an upper limit to the amount of potential the lineage can accumulate.
- The novel has not established that every unusual trait possessed by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] originates from the Walker Bloodline. Victor simultaneously possesses [[Sanguis Noctis Regis]], multiple Vampire bloodlines, Blood Dragon characteristics, Divine powers and numerous other acquired traits.

### Notes
- The Walker Bloodline predates [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s supernatural transformation and originates from his Human ancestry.[1]
- Rh-null/Golden Blood should not presently be described as something created by the Walker Bloodline. It is separately identified as the Blood of the Night King and possesses its own powers and inheritance.[3]
- The historical Veylan and the individual encountered by Victor are not physically identical existences. The latter was a reconstruction derived from information in the [[Akashic Records]].[1]
- The Walker lineage is therefore important less because of what any single ancient Walker could do and more because of what thousands of successive generations could eventually produce.
- Victor's later [[Elderblood Bloodline]] should likewise be distinguished from the Walker Bloodline. Walker represents his original Human ancestry, whereas Elderblood represents the supernatural Imperial lineage centered upon Victor after his later evolution.
