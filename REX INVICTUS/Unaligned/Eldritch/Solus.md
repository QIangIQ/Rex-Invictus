The Solus are the elite warriors of the [[Veillon Empire]], occupying a position far above its standard military forces. They are numbered from 1000 to 1, with lower numerical designations corresponding to greater standing within the organization. The single-digit Solus are explicitly identified as the strongest members and those closest to the Emperor of Light.[1]
Membership is normally reserved for exceptional Divine Beings. According to [[Vik-Ir-loah]], a conventional candidate must be a Primordial God in Divinity—possessing the highest possible Divinity available within their respective Concept—and must perform conquests for the Empire. Grandmasters are considered sufficiently rare and valuable that the normal requirements can apparently be bypassed; Vik-Ir-loah believed [[Victor Walker (Clone)|Victor's Clone]] could easily become a Solus after being "purified" by the Empire.[1]
Only one Solus, [[Solus 969]], has been directly shown. Despite carrying the relatively high numerical designation of 969, he was dramatically more capable than ordinary Veillon soldiers and was deployed after approximately one hundred thousand standard warriors, dozens of warships and specialized countermeasures proved insufficient to deal with Victor's Clone.[1][2]

### Hierarchy
The Solus use a numerical hierarchy extending from 1000 to 1. Lower numbers signify higher placement, while members holding single-digit numbers occupy the pinnacle of the organization and stand closest to the [[Aurethys Vaelthion|Emperor of Light]].[1]

### Membership Requirements
The normal route into the Solus requires two explicitly stated qualifications:
- Primordial God-Level Divinity: A candidate must possess the highest possible level of Divinity within their respective Concept. [[Vik-Ir-loah]] described this as being a Primordial God in Divinity.[1]
- Conquest for the Empire: Candidates must perform conquests or comparable service for Veillon before entering the Solus through the normal route.[1]
Grandmasters represent an important exception. After discovering that [[Victor Walker (Clone)|Victor's Clone]] was a Grandmaster, Vik-Ir-loah immediately considered him valuable enough to potentially enter the Solus without following the conventional route, provided the Empire first "purified" what it considered his Evil.[1]
This exception reflects the extraordinary rarity of genuine Grandmasters in Veillon. Vik-Ir-loah believed that even throughout the Empire, the number of Beings who had achieved Grandmaster-level martial mastery could be counted on one hand.[1]

### Characteristics

#### Elite Status
The Solus stand distinctly above ordinary Veillon military personnel. Standard Empire soldiers already possess raw Power comparable to [[Primordial Gods]], yet the Empire still treats the Solus as a separate elite class reserved for exceptional combatants.[1]
[[Vik-Ir-loah]] confidently described the Solus as Beings beyond what he believed [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] could defeat. While this claim was disproven when Victor eventually defeated [[Solus 969]], it demonstrates the enormous prestige the organization possesses within Veillon itself.[1][2]

#### Individuality and Experience
Ordinary Veillon soldiers possessed enormous strength but were repeatedly criticized for lacking the depth and individual combat experience of naturally developed high-level Beings. [[Solus 969]] was immediately recognized as different. [[Scathach]] specifically noted that the ordinary soldiers lacked depth before identifying 969 as someone worth observing separately.[2]
969 demonstrated significantly greater tactical awareness than the common troops. He recognized the danger [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] represented, adapted his defensive responses, ordered allied soldiers away from a battle they could not meaningfully contribute to and continued analyzing Victor rather than relying solely upon raw Power.[2]
This suggests that at least some Solus possess substantially greater personal development and combat experience than Veillon's mass-produced frontline forces.

#### Loyalty
[[Solus 969]] demonstrated unwavering loyalty to Veillon and the [[Aurethys Vaelthion|Emperor of Light]] even when facing death. He was deeply disturbed when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] transformed [[Vik-Ir-loah]] from a Representative of Light into a Representative of Darkness and regarded Victor's ability to perform such a transformation as something that should belong exclusively to the Emperor.[2]
Even after being defeated, 969 remained calm and warned Victor that his "brothers" would avenge him. His confidence was partially explained when Victor absorbed his memories and discovered the colossal Veillon fortress positioned beyond the Solar System, whose weapons subsequently erased the entire system containing [[Victor Walker (Clone)|Victor's Clone]].[2]
The precise meaning of 969's use of "brothers" has not been clarified. It most naturally refers to his fellow Solus or comrades and should not currently be interpreted as proof that all Solus are biologically related.

### Combat Capabilities
The complete range of abilities available to the Solus is unknown because only [[Solus 969]] has fought on-screen. Consequently, abilities demonstrated exclusively by 969 should not automatically be assumed to belong identically to every Solus.

#### Divinity
Normal Solus candidates must possess the highest possible Divinity within their respective Concept before being accepted. The organization is therefore composed of Beings whose Divine development has already reached the Primordial level within their particular authority.[1]
This requirement concerns Divinity, not necessarily species. The novel does not state that every Solus belongs to one common Race or that every member was artificially created in the same manner as Veillon's ordinary soldiers.

#### Physical Power
[[Solus 969]] was significantly larger than ordinary Veillon soldiers and could directly exchange attacks with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s [[Victor Walker (Clone)|Independent Clone]] after Victor entered his Demon King of Tyranny transformation.[2]
During their opening clash, 969 attacked Victor with what appeared to surrounding soldiers to be a single punch. In reality, both combatants struck numerous times at effectively the same moment, and the collision generated an enormous explosion that illuminated the vacuum of space.[2]
969 was also capable of physically injuring Victor during their prolonged battle, although the Clone's regeneration rapidly repaired the resulting wounds.[2]

#### Light-Based Power
[[Solus 969]] was covered in Light substantially more powerful than that surrounding Veillon's standard soldiers. Immediately before his first clash with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], the Light surrounding his body was described as stronger than that of all nearby ordinary soldiers combined.[2]
The exact relationship between a Solus and the Empire's Light infrastructure has not been fully explained. Although 969 clearly wielded exceptionally powerful Light-based Energy, the novel does not yet establish that every Solus is literally composed from Light or that all members possess identical Light abilities.

#### Speed and Reactions
969 possessed enough speed and combat perception to engage [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Grandmaster-level Clone directly. His attacks appeared as single motions to the perception of ordinary Veillon soldiers despite actually consisting of numerous strikes.[2]
When Victor enhanced his greatsword with the Miasma of the Abyss, 969 chose to evade rather than block and simultaneously removed allied soldiers from the attack's path, demonstrating both high-speed reactions and battlefield awareness.[2]

#### Enhanced Senses
969 could monitor events elsewhere on the battlefield through his senses while maintaining his attention on [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. When another Victor manifestation reached [[Vik-Ir-loah]], 969 perceived what was happening to the Commander without needing to turn his attention completely away from his principal opponent.[2]

#### Combat Skill
[[Solus 969]]'s most important distinction from Veillon's standard troops was not simply greater raw Power. He demonstrated actual combat judgment.
During his battle with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], he:
- recognized that ordinary soldiers were incapable of contributing safely;
- ordered their withdrawal rather than waste them;
- changed from directly contesting Victor's attacks to evading more dangerous techniques;
- monitored the wider battlefield while maintaining pressure on his opponent;
- recognized that Victor was deliberately prolonging events;
- and continued fighting effectively despite the Clone's increasing adaptation.[2]
However, the chapter never identifies 969 as a Grandmaster. Grandmasters were treated as a separate rarity within Veillon, and his ability to fight Victor should not be used to assign him that martial classification without direct confirmation.

#### Advanced Equipment
[[Solus 969]] wore equipment visibly different from that used by normal Veillon soldiers. His armor was more technologically advanced and his overall physical presentation was substantially larger than that of the ordinary forces surrounding him.[2]
The exact functions of this equipment have not been individually described. Claims that it specifically grants regeneration, enhanced senses, defensive amplification or communication abilities should therefore remain excluded unless later chapters establish them.

### Combat Role
The Solus function as Veillon's elite military combatants and can be requested when ordinary Imperial resources are insufficient.
Their first demonstrated deployment occurred only after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had:
- killed thousands of Veillon soldiers;
- destroyed major suppression infrastructure;
- been identified as a Grandmaster;
- forced the Empire to increase its deployment from roughly twenty thousand to one hundred thousand soldiers;
- destroyed five major warships with a single Energy attack;
- and continued overwhelming every measure deployed against him.[3][1]
[[Vik-Ir-loah]] then explicitly ordered the Empire to contact the Solus, after which [[Solus 969]] arrived as reinforcement.[1][2]
This confirms the Solus as a higher-level military response than standard soldiers, fleets and conventional battlefield countermeasures. However, the text does not establish that they are deployed only against existential threats or that every appearance of a Solus automatically represents Veillon's final military option.
The Empire possesses weapons and assets vastly beyond Solus 969, including the enormous fortress responsible for erasing the entire Solar System after his defeat.[2]

### Solus 969
[[Solus 969]] was the first and currently only named Solus encountered directly. He responded to [[Vik-Ir-loah]]'s request for assistance after [[Victor Walker (Clone)|Victor's Clone]] had inflicted catastrophic losses upon the Eleventh Frontline.[2]
Upon arriving, 969 was immediately distinguished from the surrounding troops by his much larger stature, different armor and substantially more advanced-looking equipment. A far greater concentration of Light covered his body than that of ordinary soldiers. When Vik-Ir-loah explained the situation, 969 accepted the mission without hesitation and moved directly to eliminate [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[2]
His first exchange demonstrated the gap between him and the Empire's common troops. Victor used his transformed Demon King body and the Grandmaster technique One Cut, One Million Cuts, while 969 answered with a punch that similarly contained numerous attacks within what appeared to lesser observers as one motion. Their collision produced an explosion powerful enough to illuminate the surrounding vacuum.[2]
When Victor began combining his martial arts with the Miasma of the Abyss, 969 avoided the attack rather than attempting to directly resist it and removed nearby allied soldiers from danger. Recognizing that they could not meaningfully participate, he ordered Vik-Ir-loah to withdraw the standard troops.[2]
The battle continued even after Victor corrupted the Commander and created additional clones. 969 repeatedly clashed with the original manifestation, and although he successfully injured Victor, the Clone regenerated each time and continued evolving throughout the battle.[2]
The turning point came when another Victor clone infiltrated Veillon's stellar installation and destroyed the pure Positive-Energy Artifact powering the Solar System's Imperial infrastructure. Veillon's systems shut down, the Light surrounding its soldiers weakened and Victor ended the remaining battle with a massive Blood-based attack. Solus 969 was eventually reduced to his upper body before Victor seized him.[2]
Even while dying, 969 remained confident that Victor would be destroyed and warned him that his brothers would take revenge. Victor absorbed 969 into his Legion and read his memories, discovering the existence of the colossal Veillon fortress stationed far beyond the Solar System. The fortress immediately fired and erased the entire Solar System together with Victor's Clone, explaining 969's confidence even in defeat.[2]

### Limitations
- Unknown Upper Limit: Only [[Solus 969]] has been shown. Nothing demonstrated by #969 establishes the capabilities of higher-ranked members, particularly the single-digit Solus.[1][2]
- Rank Difference: Because lower numerical designations represent higher standing, 969 cannot be used as a reliable representation of the strongest Solus.[1]
- Not All Are Confirmed Grandmasters: Grandmasters are extraordinarily rare within Veillon and can apparently receive exceptional entry treatment, but the novel does not state that Solus membership itself grants or requires Grandmaster-level martial mastery.[1]
- Dependence on Veillon Infrastructure: Solus 969 remained connected to the wider Veillon battlefield system. The destruction of the Solar System's central Positive-Energy source visibly weakened the Empire's Light-based forces and shut down its active technology.[2]
- Can Be Overwhelmed by Superior Mastery: Despite being substantially stronger and more experienced than ordinary Veillon soldiers, Solus 969 was ultimately unable to overcome [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s restricted Clone, whose Grandmaster martial mastery, evolution, versatility and broader arsenal allowed him to dominate the prolonged engagement.[2]

### Trivia
- The Solus hierarchy runs from 1000 to 1; members with single-digit numbers are explicitly the strongest and closest to the [[Aurethys Vaelthion|Emperor of Light]].[1]
- Entry through the normal route requires Primordial God-level Divinity within one's Concept and conquest performed for the Empire.[1]
- A Grandmaster can apparently receive exceptional treatment when entering the Solus because genuine Grandmasters are extraordinarily rare even within Veillon.[1]
- [[Vik-Ir-loah]] initially claimed that the Solus were Beings even [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] could not fight. The subsequent defeat of [[Solus 969]] demonstrated that this statement reflected the Commander's confidence in the organization rather than an absolute rule.[1][2]
- Solus 969 is the only member whose abilities have been directly demonstrated. The Powers of the single-digit Solus remain completely unknown.[1][2]
- 969's armor and equipment were visibly more advanced than those of Veillon's standard soldiers, but their specific functions were never explained.[2]
- Solus 969 was capable of injuring [[Victor Walker (Clone)|Victor's Clone]], although the damage was rapidly repaired through Victor's regeneration.[2]
- 969 remained calm even when he was about to die because he knew Veillon possessed a distant fortress capable of erasing the entire Solar System.[2]
- The term "brothers", used by 969 when threatening Victor with retaliation, has not been explained and should not presently be interpreted as proof that the Solus are literal siblings or clones of one another.[2]
