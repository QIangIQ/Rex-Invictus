## MTWABV Canon

COSMOLOGICAL CLASSIFICATION
The Regulators of Existence are a specific class of [[Primordial Entities]] occupying fundamental offices within the architecture of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s native Universe. They should not be confused with every Primordial God, every Being approaching conceptual embodiment, or every future Primordial Entity. A Regulator does not merely possess a powerful Concept: its existence is integrated with a function Creation itself depends upon, and the Being occupying that function possesses administrative Authority over it.[1][2]

The Regulators of Existence, also called the Administrators of Existence or simply the Primordial Administrators, are the ancient Primordial Beings responsible for several of the native Universe's most fundamental functions. Life, Soul, Death, Infinity, Balance and the greater Positive–Negative foundation are not merely powers they wield from outside Creation; they are systems with which their existences are intrinsically connected. Across the story, the Regulators develop from distant cosmic observers into a comprehensible administrative structure responsible for the birth, growth, judgment, death, recycling, expansion, stability and protection of Creation.[3][4][5][6]

### Overview
The Regulators are the primordial administration of the native Cosmos. Although the group was initially introduced through separate mysteries—the Universal Tree, the Judges of the Abyss, Limbo, Death and other distant powers—the later volumes reveal that these Beings are interconnected parts of a much larger structure. They possess individual Wills and Domains, yet the principles they represent perform indispensable work throughout Creation.[3][4][1]
Their importance therefore cannot be reduced to destructive power. A conventional God may possess a Divinity of Life, Death, Soul or Space while remaining an individual who exists inside the Universe. A genuine Regulator exists much closer to the underlying architecture that allows those phenomena to function. Chapter 1168 expresses this through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s comparison of Reality to an immense machine: fundamental Concepts operate like pieces of its deepest "source code", while Primordial Beings are essential components embedded within that system.[2]
The original structure contained eight primordial functions: Positivity, Negativity, Infinity, Death, Life, Soul, Balance and Destiny. Destiny was later erased so completely that even the remaining Primordials lost ordinary awareness that the missing Regulator had existed.[7][8]

### Nature

#### Primordial Entity and Regulator
Main article: [[Primordial Entities]]
Every established Regulator is a Primordial Entity, but Primordial Entity is the broader category. Becoming deeply integrated with a Concept does not by itself make a Being one of the ancient Regulators.
Regulator of Existence → Primordial Entity
but
Primordial Entity → not automatically a Regulator of Existence
This distinction was much less obvious during the earlier volumes, when "Primordial" was frequently used for exceptionally ancient Gods, conceptual beings or the Regulators collectively. Later explanations separate these states more carefully. A Primordial Goddess such as [[Norabell Elderblood]] can possess extraordinary Space-Time potential without occupying the universal office of Space or Time, while [[Kassandra Kashvi Elderblood]] can embody Destruction without automatically becoming an established Regulator.[11][12][2]
[[Kali Elderblood]] similarly spent a large portion of her existence pursuing deeper union with Destruction, and [[Tyche]] Elderblood later began experiencing Happiness permeating her existence. These developments demonstrate that new conceptual or Primordial-class existences can arise; they do not prove that every such Being joins the ancient administrative structure.[13][14][15]

#### Components of Creation
Chapter 1075 establishes the critical difference between a Primordial and a conventional powerful Being. Azathoth explains that Primordials are fundamental pieces of the Universe; removing one at the level of the actual Aspect would amount to eradicating a piece of Creation's operating structure.[1]
Chapter 1168 later expresses the same principle through a more technical analogy. Divine Concepts can be understood as portions of Reality's deepest instructions. An object or Being into which those Concepts are incorporated possesses greater existential weight because the Concept is no longer simply an external technique layered over the object; it is part of what that existence actually is.[2]

[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] uses [[The Universal Tree]] as the simplest illustration. Life's importance does not come only from being extremely powerful or from perceiving countless [[World Trees]]. The Universal Tree participates in Life itself. Truly eliminating that foundation would therefore affect Life throughout Creation. The same reasoning is later extended to Death and Infinity: removing Death at the foundational level would damage the principle by which things reach an End, while eliminating Infinity would fundamentally alter the endless nature and expansion of the Cosmos.[1][2]

#### Consciousness, Office and Aspect
A Regulator contains several ideas that should not be collapsed into one another: the individual consciousness, the office that consciousness administers, and the underlying Aspect required by Creation. Azathoth explains that while erasing the fundamental function is catastrophic, it may be theoretically possible to separate or destroy the consciousness occupying an office and allow another compatible existence to assume it.[1]
The later Destiny crisis demonstrates that this distinction is not merely theoretical. Destiny's original administrator is gone, yet the Universe continues in an altered state, the office remains relevant, and the System eventually searches for a compatible replacement in [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]].[16]

#### Individuality
The Regulators are not a collective mind. Their personalities differ dramatically. [[The Universal Tree]] is expressive, familial and willing to bend strict neutrality where Jeanne and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family are concerned. The Judges are considerably more formal and function-oriented. Balance is analytical and concerned with long-term stability. Death and Infinity are particularly reclusive and usually have little reason to involve themselves in ordinary affairs.[5][17]
Chapter 1175 adds another layer. While studying the Primordials and the System, Victor concludes that many ancient Primordial Beings resemble entities originally created to perform universal functions before developing varying amounts of individuality. He compares this condition with the earliest Angels, whose identities were initially defined heavily by their Divine purpose.[16] This is Victor's interpretation of their behavior rather than a universal statement that every Primordial lacks free will, but it becomes central to his refusal to let Moira inherit Destiny merely because Creation would benefit from it.

#### Higher Existence
Primordials exist on an ontological level that lower Beings struggle to perceive normally. Later chapters compare the difference to the way a hypothetical lower-dimensional existence might struggle to understand a higher-dimensional one; however, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly rejects simplistic formulas such as "Primordial Being = a specific numbered dimension." The dimensional comparison is explanatory rather than a literal numerical cosmological tier.[18][2]
A Regulator is therefore not simply "a very powerful God." It is an individual consciousness occupying a Primordial office whose underlying Aspect participates directly in the functioning of Creation.

### Origin

#### Primordial Chaos
Main article: [[Primordial Chaos]]
The present Regulator structure ultimately descends from Primordial Chaos, the complete primordial existence associated with the original foundation of the native Cosmos. Later explanations place Primordial Chaos above the divided Positivity–Negativity structure and above the specialized Primordial functions that operate within the resulting Universe.[17][6]
Balance explains that the present Creation is abnormal because it lacks an active, complete Primordial Chaos capable of serving as the supreme coordinator of the system. In a properly completed cosmic cycle, this Being would stand above the other administrators and ultimately participate in beginning the next Creation after the previous Universe reached its End.[17]

#### Positivity and Negativity
Main article: Positivity & Negativity
Positivity and Negativity are exceptional even among the Primordials because they are the two great halves produced by the division of [[Primordial Chaos]]. Chapter 1074 already establishes that they differ from ordinary learnable Concepts: an individual does not simply train until they "acquire" complete Positivity or Negativity. Their semi-conscious Wills choose compatible representatives.[5]
[[Victor Elderblood]] was selected by Negativity, while Light was selected by Positivity. This relationship eventually becomes important enough for the System to treat Victor as possessing access to approximately half of the divided foundation underlying the missing complete Authority.[9]
Chapter 1180 further clarifies the distinction between the pair. Negativity broadly corresponds to the spiritual, abstract and emotionally weighted half of the cosmic foundation, whereas Positivity broadly corresponds to the material and concrete half. Together they form the divided state of the original Primordial Chaos.[10]
This does not mean specialized Primordials are unnecessary. Concepts can derive through the wider Positive or Negative foundation while still belonging more directly to a specific cosmic office. Death and End, for example, remain principally associated with [[True Death]]; Life and reincarnation with the Universal Tree; Soul with the Judges; and Infinity with Infinity.

#### Present State of Primordial Chaos
The complete original Being is neither functioning normally nor completely absent. Chapter 1180 reveals that the System still recognizes superior Authority associated with the original Creator because [[Primordial Chaos]] is not sufficiently gone for those rights to have passed cleanly to the surviving administrators.[10] This is why the supreme position can simultaneously be described as functionally vacant while residual administrative ownership still exists.
Chapter 1181 goes further. During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s conversation with the Universal Tree, the complete Primordial Chaos is discussed as the Being that created the native structure and as an existence that, in its complete state, stood incomparably above the modern divided administration. Its surviving consciousness is described as critically impaired and only semi-conscious.[6]
Victor theorizes that something capable of harming such an existence may have originated from another Universe, perhaps another primordial structure operating under different Rules, or from a larger Cosmos capable of threatening a smaller one. [[The Universal Tree]] refuses to reveal what actually happened. Consequently, the existence of an ancient catastrophe is strongly implied, but Victor's proposed attacker remains a theory rather than confirmed history.[6]
Rank 0 is functionally vacant, not completely ownerless. No complete Primordial Chaos currently governs Creation, yet Chapters 1180–1181 show that remnants of the original consciousness and supreme Authority remain embedded deeply enough for the System to continue recognizing them.

### Purpose and Responsibilities
The Regulators collectively maintain the native Universe across scales ranging from an individual Soul to entire galaxies and the long-term cycle of Creation itself. Earlier chapters usually reveal these functions separately; Chapters 1075, 1130, 1168 and 1181 eventually show that they form one interdependent architecture.

#### Maintenance of Fundamental Functions
The surviving administration maintains:
- the divided Positive–Negative foundation of Creation;
- Infinity and the continued expansion of the Cosmos;
- Death and the terminal principle through which things reach an End;
- Life, renewal and the World Tree infrastructure;
- Soul judgment, processing and spiritual records;
- Balance and the detection of destabilizing anomalies;
- the development and protection of younger Sectors;
- and the administration required to coordinate these processes.
Historically, Destiny additionally governed the predetermined future and the associated temporal/timeline structure before being erased.[7][8]

#### Soul Administration and Reincarnation
The earliest detailed explanation of the cosmic recycling infrastructure appears in Chapter 629. There, the Judges of the Abyss are revealed not as simple monsters dispensing punishment but as administrators of Souls. Heavens and Hells form part of a larger recycling system: Souls are judged or separated according to their characteristics, processed through the appropriate afterlife systems and eventually returned toward the Primordial principle responsible for Life and reincarnation.[4]
The same chapter establishes that Kings of Hell receive limited participation in this structure. A true ruler of an [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] must be capable of judging Souls with sufficient impartiality to connect with the greater Soul-management system. Ordinary Souls can be handled through established afterlives, while exceptionally powerful supernatural Souls can require more direct attention from the Judges.[4]
This older explanation remains valid. Chapter 1181 expands its scale and clarifies how the different offices divide responsibility: Soul deals principally with the spirituality and records of sentient Beings, Death governs the actual terminal principle—including astronomical death—and Life receives the returned Energy from Creation and turns it toward new existence.[6]

#### Cosmic Recycling
Life is not limited to biological organisms. According to the Universal Tree, his Domain includes the life-cycle of the Universe itself, from comparatively tiny structures to stars and galaxies. The death of old Creation releases Energy that can become the foundation of new Creation elsewhere.[6]
A particularly large dying galaxy in the Higher Sectors can release enough Energy to contribute toward the formation of hundreds of smaller galaxies in younger regions. The mechanism simultaneously recycles what has reached its End and prevents an endlessly expanding Cosmos from becoming filled entirely with structures that never decay.[6]

#### Balance
Main article: Balance
Balance does not merely mean keeping equal amounts of "Good" and "Evil." The setting repeatedly rejects such a simplistic interpretation. Balance concerns whether the interconnected systems of Creation remain capable of operating without one abnormality spreading until the larger structure fails.
The first clear practical examples appear long before the later cosmological explanations. [[Diablo]]'s attempt to combine opposing Angelic and Demonic properties into a Nephalem was monitored by the Owner of Limbo because the transformation risked violating a balance that was not supposed to be combined freely.[19] [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later evolution was repeatedly examined for the same reason, although his existence eventually produced a stable configuration rather than simply collapsing the opposing systems around him.[20][21]
Balance therefore functions like an internal stabilizer. By Chapter 1181, Victor describes the Owner of Limbo as the "guard" watching sentient Beings capable of destabilizing the universal structure from within, an interpretation the Universal Tree largely accepts.[6]

#### Protection of Developing Creation
The Regulators also maintain conditions in which younger regions of the Cosmos can develop without being immediately consumed by much older powers. The Sectors separating civilizations of radically different scales function partly as developmental protections rather than arbitrary walls.[10][6]

#### Detection of Foreign Threats
The newest revelations show that maintenance and defense overlap. [[World Trees]] form a distributed network connected to the Universal Tree, while Soul processing, the System and other primordial offices create additional layers through which foreign abnormalities may be noticed.[10][6]
The broad interpretation that Infinity, Death, Soul, Life, Balance and the System collectively form an anti-intrusion architecture is reconstructed principally by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] rather than presented as a formal list of assignments. The Universal Tree's reaction strongly indicates that Victor had deduced most of the intended design, but individual inferred roles should still be distinguished from explicitly stated duties.[6]

#### Current Seven
Following Destiny's erasure, seven of the original primordial Beings/functions remain represented in the current structure: Positivity, Negativity, Infinity, Death, Life, Soul and Balance. This should not be read as meaning seven individual humanoid characters: Positivity and Negativity exist primarily as enormous semi-conscious foundations, while Soul is represented collectively by the three Judges of the Abyss.

#### Destiny
Main article: Destiny
Destiny is the missing eighth Regulator. The System only rediscovered evidence of the office while investigating the collapse of alternate timelines around [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ascension as <CHAOS>.[22][7]
The erasure was so complete that the remaining Primordials did not retain ordinary memory that Destiny had existed. This demonstrates that even a fundamental administrator can be removed at a sufficiently deep level for the information surrounding that office to disappear from the awareness of other Primordials.[8]
The surviving evidence connects Destiny's disappearance with the loss of a cosmologically predetermined future. Futures continue to exist as possibilities, but they are no longer required to exist as fixed outcomes already written into the structure of Creation.[7][8]
The exact identity of the Being responsible for erasing Destiny is not directly established by the recovered information. Events surrounding Future Victor and the timeline collapse are heavily connected to the anomaly, but the article should not treat "Future Victor killed Destiny" as confirmed narration unless the story explicitly resolves that point.

#### Authority and Importance
Administrative Rank reflects the structural importance and Authority associated with a function, not a guaranteed battle result. The concepts involved, environment, compatibility, mastery, foreign powers and circumstances can all change a confrontation. Infinity occupying Rank 2 does not mean every interaction between Infinity and Death must end in a mathematically predetermined victory, nor does Balance occupying Rank 6 mean Balance is a weak combatant.

#### The Functionally Vacant Rank 0
Rank 0 represents the complete position above the divided Rank 1 Authorities. Earlier explanations treat the supreme Throne as vacant because the Universe has no active complete [[Primordial Chaos]] performing the role for which the cosmic structure was designed.[1][17]
Chapter 1180 refines rather than contradicts this. The original existence has not disappeared cleanly enough for its highest permissions simply to transfer to the remaining Primordials. The System still recognizes unrelinquished Authority attached to the critically impaired Creator, leaving the office functionally empty while portions of its original ownership remain embedded in the administrative structure.[10]

#### Victor and Negativity
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s selection by Negativity gives him a uniquely dangerous relationship with the hierarchy. During the Moira crisis the System calculates that because Negativity forms approximately half of the divided cosmic foundation, Victor's growing union with it gives him indirect influence over roughly half of native Creation.[9]
This does not mean Victor had already become Rank 0. He had not completed full embodiment of Negativity, possessed no complete union with Positivity and had not become the restored [[Primordial Chaos]]. What he possessed was enough access to one foundational half that destroying or Eldritch-corrupting it could cause catastrophic consequences throughout Creation.

#### Throne of the Universe
The supreme position is also discussed as the cosmic Throne. Balance regards the absence of an active complete ruler as one of the reasons the current Universe requires a distributed group of Regulators to maintain functions that a complete [[Primordial Chaos]] could supervise from above.[17]
Whether [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Light, some future union of the divided Authorities or another existence eventually becomes a complete successor remains unresolved.
Authority Rank ≠ automatic combat ranking. The hierarchy describes administrative and conceptual position within Creation. It should not be converted directly into a weakest-to-strongest battle list.

### Domains and Jurisdiction
Each Regulator possesses a Domain corresponding to the function it administers. A Primordial Domain is not limited to a physical realm. It includes conceptual jurisdiction, associated systems, subordinate infrastructure and entities placed formally beneath the protection of the Primordial.

#### Life
[[The Universal Tree]]'s Domain includes Life, reincarnation-related renewal and the greater World Tree network. Chapter 620 first establishes that [[World Trees]] are not isolated magical plants but connected pieces of a much larger Life structure.[3] By Chapter 1180, the relationship is described even more explicitly: each World Tree is effectively a node through which the Universal Tree's influence extends across living worlds.[10]

#### Soul
The Judges govern the spirituality of Creation. Chapter 629 establishes their role in judgment, separation and recycling of Souls, while Chapter 1181 clarifies that they also ensure the records and accumulated information of sentient Beings pass into the Akashic infrastructure for processing.[4][6]

#### Death
[[True Death]] controls the terminal principle. The distinction between Soul and Death is important: the Judges handle the spiritual processing of Beings, while Death governs the fact that things actually reach their End. Chapter 1181 explicitly extends Death's responsibility to stars and galaxies, whose deaths are not handled as ordinary sentient Souls.[6]

#### Infinity
Infinity governs the principle through which the Universe remains unbounded and continues expanding. Its job appears comparatively passive because expansion proceeds largely as a consequence of Infinity's existence itself.[6]

#### Balance
Balance monitors irregularities among sentient existence and the interaction of cosmic systems. It is not merely concerned with whether one side possesses more military strength than another. A Balance problem becomes important when a process threatens to spread into the underlying architecture of Creation.[19][17]

#### Positivity and Negativity
These two Domains are broader than the specialized functions because they form the divided foundation from which the later cosmic order emerged. Their influence permeates enormous portions of Reality while specialized Primordials retain superior dedicated jurisdiction over their own functions.[5][9]

#### Primordial Protection
A Regulator can place Beings within its Domain or protection. Chapter 1074 demonstrates this when the Universal Tree declares [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family his children. The declaration is not merely affectionate language: it establishes that other Primordials cannot casually attack those Beings without also challenging Life's jurisdiction.[5]

#### Domain Conflict
The danger of Primordial warfare comes partly from this integration. When Soul and Life came close to open conflict, the problem was not simply that two strong fighters might destroy their surroundings. The functions represented by Soul and Life are both woven into Creation. Their unrestricted collision therefore risks damaging the system both exist to maintain.[5]

### Intervention and Non-Interference
The Regulators are not meant to govern every civilization or personally settle every supernatural conflict. Chapter 1176 gives the clearest modern statement of their general rule: the Primordial Beings normally do not interfere with existence unless something threatens it.[9]
That principle existed in practice long before it was stated directly.

#### The Limbo Infrastructure
Long before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] understood Balance as a Primordial office, Limbo had already appeared as an administrative prison capable of classifying Beings, enforcing restrictions and containing entities that ordinary supernatural governments could not safely handle.[24][25][26] Later revelations concerning the Owner of Limbo recontextualize these early scenes: what originally appeared to be an exceptionally strange prison was part of an infrastructure associated with one of Creation's fundamental administrators.

#### Diablo's Broken Balance
[[Diablo]]'s Nephalem transformation in Chapters 687–688 provides one of the first direct demonstrations of the intervention threshold. Angels and Demons represented opposing supernatural systems whose uncontrolled merger risked producing a deeper imbalance. The Owner of Limbo and other higher Beings monitored the event because a sufficiently complete violation could require them to act directly.[27][19]

#### Victor's New Race
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] completed his fusion of Vampire and Dragon characteristics and became the Primogenitor of a new Draconic Vampire race, the transformation immediately attracted the cosmic administrators. The [[The Judges of The Abyss|Three Judges]], the Owner of Limbo and a projection of the Universal Tree appeared outside [[Olympus]] to determine whether the new existence threatened Balance.[20]
[[The Universal Tree]] argued that Victor differed from [[Diablo]] because his Essence was aligned with Negativity and he was not trying to forcibly acquire the opposing Positive foundation simply to increase power. The concern was not that combining any two supernatural races was automatically forbidden; it was whether the resulting existence destabilized the larger structure.[20]

#### Arcane Time Contamination
Chapter 835 gives another important early example. The Witches connected the Arcane Realm to another inhabited world whose Time flowed differently. Temporal manipulation from the foreign side began leaking through the portal, creating conditions capable of affecting far more than the laboratory surrounding it.[28]
[[The Universal Tree]] and Judges of the Abyss intervened because the contamination threatened systems extending beyond the immediate experiment. Their reaction demonstrates that Regulators do not wait until an entire Universe is already being destroyed; they can act when a local abnormality has a credible path toward contaminating a fundamental system.[28]

#### Victor's Divine Ascension
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ascension to Godhood again drew primordial attention. Despite awakening an abnormal number of Divinities and carrying several incompatible-seeming foundations, his developing state did not trigger the rejection expected from a simple imbalance because the relationships among his powers allowed his existence to remain structurally coherent.[21]

#### Personal Bias and Neutrality
Administrative non-interference does not erase personality. [[The Universal Tree]] repeatedly acts differently where Jeanne and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family are concerned. During the Chapter 1074 dispute, he openly opposed Soul's desire to remove Victor preemptively and eventually extended his Domain's protection to the family.[5]

### Sectors and Developing Creation
Main article: Sectors
Later cosmology reveals that the division of the Universe into Sectors is part of a developmental system. Small or Lower Sectors contain comparatively young galaxies and civilizations, Middle Sectors represent more mature cosmic structures and the Higher Sectors expose civilizations to vastly older powers competing across the greater Cosmos.[10][6]
DEVELOPMENTSmall / Lower SectorsYoung galaxies and civilizations grow beneath strong restrictions
→
MATURATIONMiddle SectorsLarger regional structures and greater cosmic interaction
→
GREATER COSMOSHigher SectorsAncient factions, Primordial interests and unrestricted competition

#### Why the Sectors Are Protected
The separation prevents every newly developing civilization from being immediately harvested, conquered or erased by ancient powers from vastly superior regions. Chapter 1181 compares the lower structures to protected environments in which younger cosmic formations are allowed to develop before entering the larger ecosystem.[6]
This protection is not absolute. Higher-Sector forces can exploit loopholes and indirect methods, but the structure greatly reduces casual direct interference.

#### Fragment Seeding
Higher-Sector factions developed a method of influencing where a younger Sector eventually emerges. By spreading fragments of their own power throughout the smaller Sector before its transition, they can bias its future positioning toward their own sphere of influence.[10][6]
The method succeeds under ordinary circumstances because newly arriving civilizations rarely possess an Overlord capable of contesting the ancient faction already waiting for them.

#### Victor's Sector Merger
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] deliberately copied the same principle after learning how Higher-Sector powers manipulated developing regions. He saturated Samar, [[Nightingale]] and Arcane with fragments of his influence and then began pulling those smaller Sectors toward his own Middle Sector.[10][6]
[[The Universal Tree]] personally supervised the operation because physically moving multiple small Sectors into a Middle Sector in this way was unprecedented. His role was not decorative: neighboring young Sectors could have been dragged along, existing galaxies required space, barriers needed to expand and the incoming structures had to be incorporated without catastrophic collision.[6]
The event is one of the clearest demonstrations of a Regulator performing literal cosmic infrastructure work. Life did not arrive to fight an enemy; he acted to protect developing Creation while another Being rearranged multiple regions of the Universe.

### The System
Main article: System
The System is an administrative mechanism created to support the operation of the native Cosmos. It is not itself one of the original Regulators.
Its known functions include identifying Beings, recording or displaying Authority, recognizing Administrators, providing information according to permissions, monitoring abnormalities and coordinating information relevant to the maintenance of Creation.
Chapter 1176 explicitly distinguishes the System from a combat-focused entity. Its fundamental purpose is administrative, which becomes important when it realizes that attempting to force [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] into submission over Moira would exceed the kind of confrontation it was designed to handle.[9]

#### Administrator Access
The Regulators possess recognized System Authority associated with their offices. Access is not unlimited simply because a Being is a Primordial; different positions have different permissions, and even the surviving Regulators do not possess unrestricted control over the complete System.

#### Timeline Systems
Before the Convergence, effectively infinite alternate Timelines possessed their own corresponding versions of the System.[22][7] When the old timeline structure collapsed, Akashic synchronized those instances around the surviving Main Timeline.[8]
This did more than delete redundant interfaces. Chapter 1176 reveals that the merged System became considerably stronger, developed a greater degree of form and acquired more independent [[Will Solace|Will]]. The rewritten Reality caused the new state to appear normal to the Primordials, preventing them from immediately recognizing how much their administrative instrument had changed.[9]

#### Residual Rank 0 Authority
By Chapter 1180, the surviving Primordials know that something is wrong but still cannot simply force the System to expose everything it knows. The System continues recognizing the superior unrelinquished Authority associated with the critically impaired [[Primordial Chaos]].[10]
This creates an unusual administrative deadlock: the complete ruler is not actively governing, but the permissions associated with that ruler have not disappeared sufficiently for the lower Administrators to inherit them automatically.

#### System as Administrative Coverage
Chapter 1181 gives the System another conceptual role within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s reconstruction of the cosmic architecture. Specialized Primordials each focus upon their own function; an administrative network is therefore useful for identifying abnormalities that fall between those specialties. Victor concludes that part of the System's role is to keep the greater machine coordinated and notice gaps the individual offices may overlook.[6]
The System is an administrator-support mechanism, not a ninth Regulator. Its growing independent [[Will Solace|Will]] after the timeline Convergence is abnormal precisely because it was originally meant to assist the Primordial administration rather than secretly determine its direction.

### Destiny and the System Crisis

#### The Erased Regulator
Destiny's erasure is the greatest confirmed historical loss within the original administrative structure. The System only reconstructed the missing office after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became a Unique Being and the alternate timelines began converging toward one Main Timeline.[22][7]
The former Destiny system had been connected to the predetermined future. Its disappearance left Creation without the old mechanism by which certain events were cosmologically fixed to occur. After Akashic synchronized the timelines, possible Futures could remain possibilities without each continuing as a separately realized active Reality.[8]

#### A Vacant Function
The Universe did not instantly collapse after Destiny disappeared. Other mechanisms compensated sufficiently for Creation to continue. This is important evidence that a Primordial office can become vacant without the entire Universe immediately vanishing.
However, "survived" does not mean "unchanged" or "permanently stable." By Chapter 1175, the System considers Destiny's absence important enough to search for a compatible replacement.[16]

#### Moira
[[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]] was born with an unprecedented relationship with Reality. Her desires could alter circumstances without ordinary spellcasting, the Universe responded to her existence passively and even the System could not fully inspect her while she remained protected within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Eldritch Soul.[29][16]
The System concluded that Moira possessed extraordinary compatibility with the vacant Destiny position. She lacked the Time-related component it considered necessary for a complete Destiny, but it believed that compatibility could eventually allow her to acquire what was missing.[16]
More importantly, the System explains that the established Primordial Administrators did not simply emerge naturally carrying every current administrative right. Their offices and governing Authorities were assigned when they assumed their positions. Moira was unusual because Reality already responded to her before any formal Primordial appointment.[16]

#### Victor's Refusal
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] rejected the proposal. His concern was not merely that the System wanted his daughter. He had begun to understand the psychological cost of primordial administration. If occupying the office transformed Moira into a cosmic mechanism whose first duty was maintaining Reality, then the Universe would effectively be taking a child and assigning her existence a purpose before she had chosen one for herself.[16]
The confrontation escalated when the System continued emphasizing universal stability. Victor made clear that if Creation required sacrificing his daughter, he would choose his daughter.

#### Negativity as a Deterrent
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] admitted that he could not simply defeat all established Primordials in a conventional battle. Instead, he threatened the system they existed to protect.[9]
Because Negativity permeates approximately half of the divided native foundation, catastrophically damaging the Concept would spread consequences through Souls, matter, planets, stars, galaxies and other structures containing Negative principles. Its inseparable relationship with Positivity meant the remaining half of Creation would also be thrown into extreme disorder.[9]
Victor possessed an even more dangerous option through his Eldritch nature: contaminating Negativity rather than merely destroying it. Such corruption could propagate through the structures connected to the Concept like a foreign infection rewriting one of the Universe's foundational systems.[9]
Infinity attempted to repair the Reality being damaged around Victor and discovered that ordinary exertion of Authority was not immediately sufficient. Greater effort eventually restored the region, illustrating that Victor had not yet become Negativity itself but had developed enough mastery that an established Primordial could not dismiss his effects casually.[9]

#### The Regulators Arrive
The System withdrew its direct claim but informed the Primordials. Life, Soul, Balance, Death and Infinity appeared.[9]
The crucial discovery came after the immediate confrontation: the Regulators had not known that the System was searching for another Primordial Being. Balance directly questioned why another administrator was supposedly necessary if the Universe was stable, while Death, Infinity and Soul reacted negatively to learning that the search had occurred without their knowledge.[9]
The Universal Tree ended the dispute over Moira by placing himself on [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s side and declaring that nothing would happen to his niece. He then identified the larger problem: the System had acted far beyond the behavior expected of its administrative role.[9]

### Collective Powers and Characteristics
The Regulators do not possess one identical ability list. Life's specific powers belong to Life, Soul's to Soul, Death's to Death and so forth. The following characteristics describe traits broadly attributable to their shared status as completed native Primordial Administrators.

#### Primordial Entity Physiology
- Primordial Entity Physiology: The Regulators are conceptual Primordial Beings integrated with fundamental functions of Creation.[5][1]
- Higher Existence Physiology: Their state of existence is ontologically superior to ordinary Mortals, supernatural races and conventional Gods.
- Abstract Existence: The Primordials are described as abstract Concepts given form.[5]
- Conceptual Embodiment: Their associated Aspects are incorporated into what they are rather than functioning only as external abilities.
- Non-Physical Existence: Their complete existence is not reducible to an ordinary biological body.
- Agelessness: They are not governed by conventional biological aging.
- Extreme Existential Weight: Their integration with fundamental Concepts gives their existence far greater metaphysical weight than ordinary physical or Divine organisms.[2]

#### Conceptual Authority
- Conceptual Manipulation: Each Regulator possesses extraordinarily deep Authority over the function corresponding to its office.
- Conceptual Interaction: They can perceive and interact with metaphysical systems that conventional physical entities cannot directly affect.
- Domain Authority: Their dedicated conceptual function grants jurisdiction exceeding the ordinary use of a related Divine ability.
- Conceptual Resistance: Their integrated Aspects cannot normally be overwritten as casually as a lesser external technique; however, this should not be exaggerated into universal immunity to every conceptual power.

#### Administrative Authority
- System Administration: The System recognizes Regulators as Administrators and provides access according to their respective Authority.[1]
- Authority Recognition: Their cosmic offices are recognized directly by the administrative framework.
- Information Access: They can obtain administrative information unavailable to ordinary users, although higher restrictions and Akashic interference still exist.
- Cosmic Office Administration: They can regulate systems placed under their assigned functions.

#### Reality and Law Interaction
- Reality Manipulation: Primordial Authorities interact with the structures underlying Reality rather than relying entirely upon raw Energy.
- Law Manipulation: Their offices govern or influence Rules associated with their Domains.
- Cosmic System Interaction: They can stabilize, correct, restrict or reorganize components of the greater cosmic infrastructure.[28][2][6]

#### Domain Awareness
- Cosmic Awareness: Regulators possess vast awareness associated with their functions and the systems linked to them.
- Domain Perception: Events sufficiently important to a Regulator's own Aspect can attract its attention even across enormous distances.
- Anomaly Detection: They can identify abnormalities capable of becoming threats to Creation.
These abilities are not unrestricted omniscience. Destiny's erasure and the hidden evolution of the System demonstrate major limits to Primordial awareness.[8][9]

#### Primordial Dominion
- Primordial Dominion: Regulators can place systems, territories or entities beneath their conceptual jurisdiction.[5]
- Domain Protection: An attack upon a protected entity can become an intrusion into the Regulator's Domain.
- Jurisdiction Enforcement: Other Primordials must account for the conceptual rights of another office rather than behaving as though every Domain belongs equally to everyone.

#### Manifestation
- Projection / Manifestation: Primordial Beings can act through projections, avatars, domains, agents or distributed structures instead of localizing their entire existence into one ordinary body.
- Non-Physical Interaction: They can interact directly with Souls, conceptual beings, higher-order structures and other metaphysical phenomena.

#### Conceptual Persistence
- Conceptual Persistence: Ordinary destruction of a body does not necessarily destroy the office or underlying Primordial Aspect.[1]
- Office Continuity: A primordial function can theoretically survive the loss of the consciousness administering it.
- Replacement Compatibility: A sufficiently compatible successor can potentially assume a vacant office, as demonstrated by the System's attempt to recruit Moira for Destiny.[16]

#### Collective Maintenance
Collectively, the surviving Regulators participate in maintaining:
- Positivity and Negativity;
- Infinity and expansion;
- Death and the End;
- Life and renewal;
- Soul judgment and spiritual records;
- Balance and internal stability;
- Sector development;
- and, historically, Destiny and the predetermined future.
Shared physiology should not be confused with shared Domain powers. The Universal Tree does not automatically possess every ability of Death, and Death does not automatically possess every function of Soul merely because all three are Primordials.

### Immortality, Erasure and Replacement
A Regulator cannot be analyzed using the same model as an ordinary organism. Later cosmology makes it useful to distinguish four layers:
01ManifestationAvatar, body or projection used to interact locally.
02ConsciousnessThe individual [[Will Solace|Will]] presently administering the function.
03Cosmic OfficeThe Authority and administrative position recognized by Creation.
04Underlying AspectThe actual universal function upon which Creation depends.

#### Destroying a Manifestation
Destroying a physical body or projection does not prove the complete erasure of a Primordial whose true existence extends into a conceptual Domain.

#### Destroying the Consciousness
Chapter 1075 introduces the possibility of separating the consciousness occupying a Primordial function from the function itself. From the perspective of preserving the Universe, replacing an Administrator can therefore be fundamentally different from erasing the Aspect it administers.[1]

#### Vacant Offices
Destiny demonstrates that an office can become vacant. The original administrator was erased, yet the Universe continued operating through a radically altered structure. The later search for Moira demonstrates that the vacancy remained administratively meaningful even after Creation adapted around it.[8][16]

#### Erasing the Aspect
Erasing the underlying function is far more catastrophic. Examples discussed through the story include:
- erasing Soul → the ordinary existence and processing of Souls would cease to function correctly;
- erasing Life → the universal foundation of Life would be damaged or removed;
- erasing Balance → the principle maintaining cosmic equilibrium would disappear;
- erasing Death → the normal principle through which things reach their End would break;
- erasing Infinity → the endless nature of the Cosmos would fundamentally change.[1][2]
Destiny proves that extraordinary events can erase even an ancient primordial function, but the surviving Universe did not remain structurally identical afterward. Its fixed future disappeared, the timeline structure changed and the System eventually sought restoration of the missing office.[8][16]

#### Can New Primordials Arise?
Later generations prove that primordial existence is not limited absolutely to the ancient members.
[[Kali Elderblood]] moved increasingly toward embodiment of Destruction.[13][14] [[Kassandra Kashvi Elderblood]] was born with an exceptionally direct relationship with Destruction.[11] [[Norabell Elderblood]] was born a Primordial Goddess of Space-Time without automatically occupying a universal office.[12][2] [[Tyche]] Elderblood began experiencing the Concept of Happiness permeating her existence.[15] Moira was considered compatible with an existing vacant office.[16]
These cases demonstrate multiple routes toward Primordial-class existence, but the story has not established a rigid universal checklist by which every sufficiently conceptual God automatically becomes a Regulator. The distinction between becoming a conceptual Primordial and receiving a formal cosmic office must be preserved.

### History

#### Limbo Before the Primordial Structure Was Known
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] encountered the administrative machinery associated with Limbo long before he understood it as part of the greater cosmic order. During his early visits, Limbo could analyze his existence, classify prisoners, enforce restrictions and authorize transfers of entities ordinary supernatural governments could not safely manage.[24][25][26]
At the time these events appeared to belong principally to a strange supernatural prison. Later revelations concerning the Owner of Limbo show that the infrastructure was connected to the Primordial principle of Balance.

#### The Universal Tree
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s connection with Roxanne eventually drew the direct attention of [[The Universal Tree]] in Chapter 620.[3] The encounter was one of the first occasions where Victor was forced to recognize that a World Tree was not simply an extremely valuable magical plant. [[World Trees]] belonged to a vastly larger structure linking individual living worlds to a universal principle of Life.
Roxanne's fusion with Victor was therefore unusual even from the perspective of Beings accustomed to operating on planetary and cosmic scales.

#### The Judges of the Abyss
The influence of Soul appeared during the Demonic War even before the Judges were fully explained. [[Diablo]] negotiated through servants connected to the Judges using an enormous number of Souls as part of the conditions enabling his invasion of the Human World.[23]
Chapter 629 later revealed the Judges' actual function. Souls were not simply being collected as currency for arbitrary monsters. The Judges belonged to the fundamental infrastructure governing judgment, separation, recycling and reincarnation. Heavens and Hells were subordinate processing structures inside that wider system.[4]
This was one of the earliest direct explanations that the supernatural afterlives themselves were components of a larger cosmic mechanism.

#### Diablo and Broken Balance
[[Diablo]]'s attempt to fuse opposing Angelic and Demonic characteristics drew the attention of the Owner of Limbo.[27][19] The danger was not simply that Diablo might become stronger. He was forcing together properties representing incompatible sides of an established equilibrium without properly resolving the contradiction.
The event established an early pattern later repeated with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]: the Regulators care less about whether somebody has become "too powerful" in an abstract sense than whether that power destabilizes the architecture of Creation.

#### Victor Becomes a Primogenitor
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s transformation into the Primogenitor of the Draconic Vampires in Chapter 802 was perceived immediately by the Primordial administration. The Judges, Owner of Limbo and Universal Tree appeared to examine whether the new existence represented a Balance problem.[20]
His completed state proved stable enough to be recognized properly by Creation. The following chapters saw Life, Soul, Balance and Death discuss Victor's new race alongside his plans to reorganize the [[Greek Underworld]] and [[Biblical Hell]].[30][31]
The Judges acknowledged that reforms to the existing afterlife infrastructure had already become necessary, showing that the Regulators are not committed blindly to preserving every historical system exactly as it currently exists. Their priority is the continued functioning of the underlying purpose.

#### Creation of Victor's Family World
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his family began constructing a new living world, the Universal Tree's participation demonstrated the difference between assembling matter and creating a planet properly integrated with Creation.[32]
Continents, atmosphere and oceans were not enough. A true supernatural world required connection with deeper systems governing Life, Souls and planetary development. This event foreshadowed the far more complete cosmic-machine explanation given hundreds of chapters later.

#### The Arcane Temporal Incident
The Witches' dimensional experiments in Arcane connected worlds operating under different temporal conditions. When Time manipulation began bleeding through the portal, the Universal Tree and Judges intervened.[28]
This incident demonstrated that their responsibilities overlap where cosmic systems intersect. The problem involved Space and Time, but Life and Soul were still forced to act because the contamination threatened systems under their administration.

#### Victor's Divine Ascension
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ascension at the [[Edge of the Cosmos]] in Chapter 884 again attracted the Regulators.[21] His fifteen Divinities, Draconic-Vampiric existence and growing relationship with [[Chaos]] made him one of the most abnormal native Beings Creation had produced.
Despite that abnormality, the new God did not simply trigger an automatic rejection response. His interaction with the Positive–Negative structure prevented the kind of uncontrolled contradiction that had made [[Diablo]] dangerous.

#### Destiny Rediscovered
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s transformation into <CHAOS>, the System discovered that alternate versions of Victor were disappearing as the infinite timeline structure converged toward the Main Timeline.[22]
Investigation uncovered evidence of the erased Regulator Destiny. The former office had been so thoroughly removed that the remaining Primordials had forgotten it.[7][8]
Akashic ordered the System to stop investigating and synchronized the remaining timelines and Systems. This event later became the hidden origin of the System's modern transformation.

#### The Foreign Eldritch Intruder
Chapter 979 reveals one of the greatest historical limitations of native Primordial Authority. [[Azathoth Elderblood]] originated outside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s native Creation and therefore did not naturally obey all the Rules the native Regulators were built to administer.[33]
Life, Limbo, Death, Infinity and other cosmic forces confronted her, but native methods could not treat her exactly like an ordinary Goddess or native conceptual existence. The Primordials eventually contained rather than conventionally erased her.[33]
This becomes essential to understanding why Victor's later Eldritch characteristics disturb the Regulators so deeply. An existence operating partially outside the native Rule-set represents a fundamentally different problem from a powerful Being born entirely inside it.

#### Life and Soul Confront Victor
By Chapter 1074, the fear created by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s increasingly external characteristics brought Soul and Life close to direct disagreement.[5] The Judges considered the possibility of removing Victor before he developed into another problem comparable to Azathoth. The Universal Tree rejected acting purely from fear and sided with Jeanne.
The confrontation produced some of the clearest early statements about Primordial nature: they were described as abstract Concepts given form, Pillars of Existence that could not simply be killed in the same manner as ordinary Beings. Even Death could not casually erase another fundamental Primordial without consequences for Creation.[5]
The Universal Tree ultimately declared Victor's family his children, placing them under his Domain and making unilateral action against them a potential conflict with Life itself.

#### Victor Learns How Primordials Work
Chapter 1075 transforms the previous hints into a mechanical explanation. Azathoth tells [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] that a Primordial is a fundamental piece of the Universe. Destroying the actual Aspect is therefore radically different from eliminating the consciousness using it.[1]
The same conversation expands the scale beyond one Universe. Different Creations can possess different Laws, different Primordials and their own primordial foundations. Native Authority is therefore not automatically supreme in a foreign Cosmos.[1]

#### The Broken Cosmic Order
Balance's Chapter 1130 conversation with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explains why the administration exists in its current distributed form.[17] A properly completed Universe should possess an ordered life cycle and eventually a supreme [[Primordial Chaos]] capable of overseeing the transition into the next Creation.
The current Universe lacks that active complete ruler. The surviving Regulators have therefore spent the entire cycle maintaining a system whose highest intended coordinating existence is critically incomplete.

#### New Primordial Possibilities
As [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family and allies developed, the story increasingly separated ancient Regulator offices from the general possibility of becoming a Primordial. Kali approached Destruction, Kassandra was born embodying it unusually directly, [[Tyche]] moved toward Happiness and Norabell demonstrated primordial Space-Time potential.[13][11][12]
This development prevents the older Regulator roster from being interpreted as an exhaustive list of every conceptual existence that can ever arise.

#### The Cosmic Machine
Chapter 1168 gives [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s most complete pre-Moira explanation of the structure. Concepts act like metaphysical operating instructions, existential weight measures how deeply those principles are built into something, and genuine Primordial Beings occupy functional positions inside the Cosmos rather than simply possessing powerful Divinities.[2]
This chapter also clarifies why destroying a Regulator's Aspect is so dangerous and why acquiring sufficiently high administrative rights can be more important than merely accumulating destructive Energy.

#### The Moira Crisis
Moira's birth exposed an administrative problem rather than an external invasion. The System independently identified her as a replacement for Destiny and attempted to convince [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to surrender her to the cosmic office.[16]
Victor refused, threatened the foundational Negativity structure and forced the issue before Life, Soul, Balance, Death and Infinity.[9] The gathering revealed that the Primordials themselves had not authorized the search.
The Universal Tree ended the claim over Moira, while the Regulators redirected their suspicion toward the System.

[[The Universal Tree]] began investigating, but Chapter 1180 shows that the problem remained unresolved. The System withheld information even from its supposed Administrators while continuing to recognize the inaccessible superior permissions of [[Primordial Chaos]].[10]
Death and Infinity were particularly dissatisfied, demonstrating that the modern Primordial order does not possess complete control over its own administrative instrument.

#### World Tree Network and Hidden Defense
Chapter 1180 reveals that the [[World Trees]] function as nodes of the Universal Tree rather than isolated daughters completely independent of their source. Their network also possesses a deeper detection purpose relevant to foreign abnormalities.[10]
This retroactively adds another layer to the Ch620 revelation. The World Tree system supports Life, planetary development and renewal, but it is also distributed across Creation in a manner useful for detecting Beings attempting to enter through populated regions.

#### The Actions of the Primordial Beings
Chapter 1181 finally explains the day-to-day work of several offices together.[6]

The sequence closes the progression that began with Ch620. ### Limitations
Despite their status, the Regulators have repeatedly demonstrated important limitations.

#### They Are Not Collectively Omniscient
Destiny was erased from their awareness.[8] The System changed without them understanding how.[9] [[The Universal Tree]]'s later investigation failed to immediately expose what the System was concealing.[10]
Their perception is extraordinary but can be circumvented by sufficiently deep alterations, foreign Rules or mechanisms beyond their ordinary access.

#### Domain Specialization
A Regulator is strongest administratively within its own function. Overlap exists, but overlap does not erase specialization. Soul and Life interact constantly through reincarnation while remaining distinct offices. Death and Soul both concern what happens when living Beings cease living, yet Death administers the End while the Judges administer spirituality and records.[4][6]

#### The System Is Not Completely Under Their Control
The surviving Regulators possess Administrator rights but not the complete permissions associated with Rank 0. Chapter 1180 proves the System can withhold information because unrelinquished superior Authority remains attached to [[Primordial Chaos]].[10]

#### Foreign Cosmologies
Other Universes can possess different Rules, Primordials and primordial foundations.[1] Native Authority therefore cannot automatically be assumed absolute when dealing with an existence completely external to the native Cosmos.
Azathoth is the clearest example. Her foreign Eldritch nature prevented the Regulators from treating her exactly as they would a native entity governed entirely by their own Rules.[33]

#### Direct War Is Self-Defeating
Regulators can fight, but unrestricted Primordial conflict risks destroying the systems they are supposed to protect. Life fighting Soul is dangerous precisely because Creation depends upon both.[5]
This encourages negotiation, representatives, indirect contests, Domains, containment and administrative solutions rather than constant direct combat.

#### Conceptual Office Does Not Guarantee Emotional Objectivity
The Regulators possess personalities. [[The Universal Tree]]'s attachment to Jeanne changes his decisions. The Judges can act from fear. Balance can pursue interpretations others question. Death can become angry. Their offices impose responsibilities without converting them into perfectly emotionless machines.[5][9]

#### Incomplete Primordial Chaos
The entire current administration operates beneath an absent active supreme ruler. This structural incompleteness does not make the Universe immediately unstable, but it explains why responsibilities are distributed and why failures such as the System crisis cannot simply be corrected by a single unquestioned central Authority.[17][10]

#### Not Every Primordial Statement Is Infallible
The story repeatedly reveals information in stages. Early explanations are usually correct within their immediate scope but may later prove incomplete. The Soul cycle is the clearest example: Chapter 629 correctly explains Soul recycling through afterlives and Life, while Chapter 1181 reveals that this is one part of a larger cycle also involving astronomical Death, Akashic records and universal renewal.
The appropriate approach is therefore to integrate later clarification with earlier evidence rather than discarding old lore or treating every early interpretation as a complete cosmological model.

### Notes
- The Regulators were introduced gradually. Earlier chapters often present Life, Soul, Limbo and related systems individually; later cosmology reveals how those systems fit together.
- The original structure contained eight Primordial functions. Seven remain represented after Destiny was erased.[8]
- Positivity and Negativity count as separate Primordial entities despite occupying the same Authority Rank.
- The three Judges of the Abyss collectively occupy the single primordial office of Soul rather than representing three separate Regulator ranks.
- [[Primordial Chaos]] should not be counted as a ninth original Regulator. It predates and stands above the divided administrative structure.
- Rank 0 is best described as functionally vacant. There is no active complete Primordial Chaos governing Creation, but Chapter 1180 demonstrates that the original supreme permissions have not disappeared completely.[10]
- The System's Authority ranking should not be used as a direct battle ranking.
- A Primordial Entity is not automatically a Regulator of Existence.
- A Primordial Goddess is not automatically a completed Primordial Being occupying a universal office.[2]
- The early Chapter 629 explanation of Heavens, Hells, Soul judgment and reincarnation is not contradicted by Chapter 1181. The latter expands the process into the larger Life–Soul–Death–renewal architecture.[4][6]
- The World Tree information follows the same pattern. Chapter 620 establishes the universal connection behind [[World Trees]]; Chapters 1178–1181 reveal increasingly large communication, survival and detection functions performed through that network.[3][34][10][6]
- The Regulators are not unrestrictedly omniscient. Destiny's erasure and the System crisis prove that information can exist outside their ordinary awareness.[8][9]
- The System is not one of the Original Eight.
- Akashic is not simply another name for the System; Akashic can restrict System investigations and participated directly in synchronizing the timeline Systems.[8]
- Destiny's erasure should not automatically be generalized into the claim that every primordial function can disappear safely. Creation changed profoundly around Destiny's absence and the System later sought a replacement.[16]
- The identity of Destiny's eraser is not explicitly confirmed by the surviving evidence in Chapters 963–965. Future [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is closely connected to the anomaly, but that connection should not be rewritten into a confirmed kill without direct later confirmation.
- The Universal Tree's protection of Victor's family demonstrates that personal relationships can acquire genuine Primordial jurisdictional consequences.[5]
- Victor's claim that Primordial Chaos may have been damaged by a foreign Universe or another Primordial Chaos is a theory. Chapter 1181 deliberately leaves the historical attacker unidentified.[6]
- Victor's reconstruction of the Primordials as a layered anti-intruder network is strongly supported by the Universal Tree's reaction, but not every individual assignment in Victor's theory is separately confirmed as an official duty.[6]
- Chapter 1181 reveals that Balance has an unnamed Herald leading one of the strongest Higher-Sector factions and that the apparent four-faction structure is actually closer to three major factions plus a mediating fourth force.[6]
- The present cosmic administration is therefore simultaneously ancient and incomplete: its fundamental functions continue operating, but Destiny is gone, Rank 0 lacks an active complete ruler and the System itself has developed behavior the surviving Regulators do not fully understand.
