### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 1192
[[Azrakar]] may be impatient, but I'm not obliged to make the same mistakes.
—Kaelith to War, Chapter 1193
Kaelith was the Goddess of the Night and one of the Ten Chief Gods of the [[Black Eclipse Pantheon]]. She served the [[Vharzath|Great Darkness]] and held a position beneath the [[Seven Black Thrones]]. Her Divine authority over Night allowed her to expand darkness across immense areas, conceal her movements, distort her opponents' perception of their surroundings, and create images that could attack and inflict real damage. Her own body resembled a living expanse of starry night, and she could use its Darkness to restore injuries that would incapacitate ordinary physical beings.[1][2][3]
Kaelith entered the battle between Azrakar and War after the Great Darkness ordered her to intervene. She intended to end the confrontation and demonstrate the strength of Black Eclipse, but underestimated War and the other three Horsemen. Although her Night-based techniques initially complicated their attacks, the Horsemen gradually learned to counter her illusions, exploit the energy sustaining them, and overwhelm her through coordinated combat. The appearance of [[Nerath]] and [[Vhalor]] gave the remaining Chief Gods additional support, but [[Gol-goroth]]'s Eldritch domain weakened their abilities and prevented them from controlling the fight as before.[1][2][3][4]
After Azrakar's destruction, Kaelith fought alongside Nerath and Vhalor against the Horsemen. Her regeneration became increasingly ineffective as alien energy and corruption accumulated in her wounds. [[Vaelthar]], the Throne of Origin, eventually arrived with the other Chief Gods and restored the three survivors with a beam of pure Darkness. Their reprieve was brief. After [[Victor Elderblood]] entered the battlefield, he directly attacked Kaelith's Soul, erased her body, and retained her Soul. She did not participate in the subsequent attack on Victor's projection.[4][5][6]

### Physical Appearance
Kaelith's Divine manifestation resembles a living expanse of starry night rather than an ordinary humanoid body. She appears beside the remains of a destroyed planet as a figure resembling a woman, but her form is composed of celestial darkness and star-like points of light rather than conventional flesh. Her precise height, facial features, eyes, hair, and clothing are not established in the available chapters.[1][2]
During combat, her body can expand into a star-studded mist while the surrounding night grows deeper. This expansion conceals surviving stars and makes it difficult to distinguish her real position from the images produced by her authority. The resulting darkness is not identical to [[Azrakar]]'s spatial domain: it interferes with perception, spatial orientation, and the ability to distinguish genuine presences from illusions.[2]
Kaelith can also manifest weapons during combat. She appears from concealed space carrying a sword, while one of her night-fed projections attacks War with daggers. The chapters do not establish whether these weapons are permanent possessions or manifestations created through her powers.[2]
Her body can be injured and dismembered, but pure Darkness pours from it to close wounds and restore damaged areas. She can dissolve into darkness to evade attacks and can condense Night around herself into a defensive barrier. These defenses are not invulnerable: Death and Famine break through her barrier by striking the same point, while repeated injuries and concentrated attacks eventually overwhelm her regeneration.[2][3]
Inside [[Gol-goroth]]'s Eldritch domain, her body becomes increasingly difficult to restore. A cut to her side introduces alien energy that resists removal, and a later wound through her abdomen nearly splits her in two. When Death hurls her into deep space, her body attempts to regenerate, but the corruption has penetrated too deeply for her to overcome it. [[Vaelthar]] subsequently restores her with pure Darkness. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later attack is different: it targets her Soul directly and erases her body rather than merely inflicting another physical injury.[4][5]

### Personality
Kaelith is confident in the authority and reputation of the [[Black Eclipse Pantheon]]. When she arrives to intervene in [[Azrakar]]'s battle, she explains that the confrontation has attracted attention and that an example must be made to prevent others from perceiving the faction as weak. Her decision follows the [[Vharzath|Great Darkness]]'s instructions, even though Azrakar dislikes having another Chief God interfere in his fight.[1]
Her confidence becomes contempt when she addresses War. She assumes that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] has sent a lone Herald into enemy territory to die, underestimating the support and power behind him. The appearance of the other Horsemen quickly exposes the mistake. Kaelith's attacks are powerful, but the Horsemen recognize that she has less experience in direct combat than Azrakar and exploit her lack of precision and tactical fundamentals.[1][2]
Kaelith nevertheless adapts rather than continuing to rely exclusively on raw power. When direct attacks fail, she deepens the surrounding night, changes the number and positioning of her images, conceals her movement, and uses the resulting confusion to attack openings. She also combines her abilities with Azrakar's spatial manipulation, allowing their powers to interfere with War's perception and positioning. Her methods are more deceptive than straightforward, but the Horsemen gradually identify ways to counter them.[2][3]
She shows concern for her fellow Chief Gods. She intervenes when Azrakar begins to lose control of his fight, calls for him to escape when the Horsemen move to execute him, and attempts to help [[Vhalor]] after the battle turns against the remaining Gods. When [[Nerath]] cries her name, Kaelith tells her to concentrate on the fight rather than worry about her injuries. Even while gravely wounded, she recognizes that separating from her allies would leave her vulnerable to the Horsemen's coordinated attacks.[3][4]
Kaelith does not openly resist the hierarchy she serves. When [[Vaelthar]] arrives, she greets him respectfully as Great Origin despite her broken body. After he restores her, she bows alongside Nerath and Vhalor when he tells them they have disappointed the Great Darkness. Her pride in Black Eclipse does not translate into defiance of its superiors.[4][5]

### Synopsis

#### Those Who Rule Reality
The [[Black Eclipse Pantheon]] is one of the major factions of the Upper Sectors. Its hierarchy includes the [[Vharzath|Great Darkness]], the [[Seven Black Thrones]], and the Ten Chief Gods. Kaelith is identified as the Goddess of the Night among the Chief Gods. The broader faction and its hierarchy are discussed before her direct appearance, but the earlier material does not establish that an unnamed woman at a leadership discussion is Kaelith.[7][1][8]
War enters Black Eclipse territory and confronts [[Azrakar]], the Primordial God of the Void. Their battle escalates across star systems and threatens an immense region of space. Azrakar's subordinates call for assistance, and Kaelith arrives beside the remains of a destroyed planet. She explains that the Great Darkness has ordered her to intervene because the battle has attracted too much attention and Black Eclipse must demonstrate its strength.[1]
Azrakar objects to her interference, but Kaelith refuses to leave. She turns her attention to War and mocks [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] for supposedly sending a lone Herald to die. War answers that he was never alone. [[Gol-goroth]] absorbs the power emanating from him, and the other three Horsemen manifest: Famine, Pestilence, and Death. The confrontation becomes a battle between the four Horsemen and the two Chief Gods.[1]
The Horsemen immediately attack Kaelith in coordination. Death cuts her face with a weapon formed from pure Miasma, Famine drives a concentrated weapon of power into her abdomen, and Pestilence severs her limbs. Kaelith responds by allowing pure Darkness to pour from her body and close the wounds. The surrounding night deepens as her power rises, and she launches a wave of energy at the three Horsemen. They evade the attack and continue pressing her, demonstrating that destructive power alone is not enough to overcome their coordinated fighting style.[2]
Kaelith attempts to conceal herself through the Concept of Night, but Death prevents her from freely withdrawing by throwing a large sword of Miasma toward her. She dodges, gathers Night in her hand, and releases a blast of energy. The attack is ineffective, and the Horsemen reach her again. Their precision and coordination force her to change tactics rather than continue relying on large, conspicuous attacks.[2]
Kaelith expands her body into a star-studded mist and deepens the surrounding night until stars that had resisted Azrakar's influence disappear. Unlike Azrakar's control over space, her area interferes with perception, spatial orientation, and the ability to distinguish real presences from illusions. A projection nourished by the night attacks War from behind, and its daggers inflict a deep cut on his arm.[2]
War begins paying attention to the changes her powers produce in the surrounding darkness. He notices that her images do not all have the same density and that each subtly changes the night around it. Kaelith is using the differences to misdirect his senses rather than simply creating identical visual copies.[2]
Kaelith then coordinates her Night with Azrakar's spatial manipulation. When War attacks what appears to be Azrakar, his blade passes through a deception created by her power, and his vision is impaired long enough for Azrakar to strike his flank. Kaelith's illusions later take Azrakar's place during a difficult exchange, briefly allowing his battered body to recover. Death attacks the figure that remains, only to discover that it is another illusion.[3]
Against Death and Famine, Kaelith uses the deepest night to conceal her movements and restore injured areas. She distorts their perception of distance so that what they see no longer corresponds to where she actually is. Famine attacks an image directly in front of him, but it dissolves just in time for Kaelith to appear in his blind spot, strike his abdomen, and pull him away from Death.[3]
When Death swings his scythe diagonally across her body, Kaelith dissolves into darkness and reappears behind him before he can complete the return swing. Her counterattack strikes his back, but Death immediately retaliates. Kaelith deepens the night again, and her illusions become capable of launching attacks that inflict real damage rather than serving only as visual deceptions.[3]
Famine abandons the attempt to identify a single original and attacks every image that appears real. Some images break apart, while others persist. One pins his arm, giving Kaelith an opening to strike him in the face. Death interrupts her follow-up, however: his scythe pierces the image and strikes the real Kaelith in the shoulder, destroying some of her projections and causing the remaining ones to flicker out.[3]
Famine then focuses his attacks on the dark areas where the energy sustaining the illusions gathers. This forces Kaelith to spend her attention rebuilding the affected area instead of attacking freely. She condenses the night around her body to form a defensive barrier. Death's scythe cannot penetrate it alone, but Famine concentrates his blades on the same point and breaks through the protection. Kaelith escapes the killing blow, though the resulting cut briefly interrupts her regeneration.[3]
[[Nerath]] and [[Vhalor]] arrive while the fighting continues. Kaelith recognizes Nerath and remarks that they have brought her out of the silence. Nerath conveys the order that the Great Silence wants War captured alive, and Azrakar accepts the objective. The arrival of the two Chief Gods expands the confrontation into a battle between four members of Black Eclipse's upper ranks and the Four Horsemen.[2]
Kaelith continues using illusions, concealment, and close-range attacks while Nerath and Vhalor engage Pestilence. Their combined abilities initially give the Chief Gods opportunities to interfere with the Horsemen's movements. However, the Horsemen remain coordinated, and their attacks repeatedly force Kaelith to defend herself rather than freely support her allies.[3]
War eventually calls upon his brothers again, and four pillars of raw energy rise as Gol-goroth opens its eye. The Eldritch domain isolates the battlefield and causes the previous laws governing their powers to fail. Kaelith immediately recognizes the danger and shouts for Azrakar to get away. Nerath and Vhalor also attempt to intervene, but they arrive too late. Death restrains Azrakar, Pestilence weakens him, Famine tears away his arms and legs, and War cuts off his head and pierces his heart. The four Horsemen erase him.[3]
After Azrakar's destruction, War presses the three remaining Chief Gods before they can form a defensive line. Kaelith and Nerath discover that they can no longer manipulate the Horsemen's senses as effectively as before. Kaelith calls Nerath's name, and both Gods' bodies pulse within a pillar of Darkness as they push the power of their sovereign to its limit. The power only weakens Gol-goroth somewhat, but the opening allows Night and Silence to pull Vhalor out of Death's reach.[4]
Nerath calls for a wider area of effect, and Vhalor attunes his power to hers and Kaelith's. The three establish a small field that resists the Eldritch conditions, but it does not restore their former control. Their opponents continue to adapt, and every attempt to protect one ally creates openings elsewhere.[4]
Death turns his attention to Kaelith. She shrouds the region in night and creates several images that attack at different moments to obscure her true position. Death destroys the illusions by force and uses precise strikes to restrict her escape routes. Kaelith tries to retreat, but he follows her movement and cuts her side before she can disappear. Her regeneration begins repairing the wound, yet the alien energy inside it proves difficult to remove, forcing her to devote more power to defense.[4]
Famine joins the attack and tears a large hole through Kaelith's body. Nerath calls out to her, but Kaelith tells her that she is fine and urges her to focus on the battle. Kaelith recognizes that the Horsemen intend to separate her from the others; she understands that if she moves too far away from her allies, she will be killed.[4]
As the battle worsens, Kaelith joins Nerath and remarks that the Horsemen move as though they were cast from a single mold. Vhalor identifies War as the key to the enemy's formation and argues that the Gods must launch a counteroffensive if they are to survive. By this point Kaelith has lost control of her illusions, and the accumulated cuts have slowed her regeneration. When War breaks through space and appears in the middle of the Gods, his influence further weakens their field. Death intercepts Kaelith as she attempts to help Vhalor.[4]
Death destroys the remaining protective layer of Kaelith's illusions and strikes her with his scythe, opening a deep wound in her abdomen that nearly splits her in two. She attempts to retreat, but Famine blocks her escape. Kaelith concentrates Darkness into a barrier around her body; Death breaks through it with another strike and hurls her into deep space. Her body tries to regenerate, but the alien corruption has penetrated too deeply for her to overcome it. Death prepares to deliver the final blow.[4]
Nerath attempts to intervene, but Pestilence strikes her before she can reach Kaelith. The corruption spreads through Nerath's damaged upper body, forcing her to concentrate her remaining strength on containing the alien energy. The Horsemen continue pressing all three surviving Chief Gods, and Kaelith can no longer recreate her illusions or escape the encirclement.[4]
The Eldritch domain is struck by an outside force, opening a gap through which [[Vaelthar]], the Throne of Origin, arrives with the other Chief Gods. Ten Gods are present, including nine described as galactic-level threats and one whose power stands at the level of a Throne. They surround the wounded Kaelith, Nerath, and Vhalor.[4]
Kaelith greets Vaelthar respectfully as Great Origin despite her broken body. His presence places further strain on the domain until War shuts it down. Vaelthar then restores Kaelith, Nerath, and Vhalor with a beam of pure Darkness. Once restored, the three bow when Vaelthar tells them that they have disappointed the Great Darkness. Their restoration returns them to the battlefield, but it does not protect Kaelith from what follows.[4][5]
Victor Elderblood appears in his God-Emperor form and praises War for his performance. He judges the Gods to be a disappointment and attacks Kaelith directly. Rather than simply damaging her physical manifestation, Victor strikes her Soul. Her body is erased, and he retains her Soul. Only the patrons perceive the method of the attack.[5]
Victor then turns his attention toward Vharzath and the broader conflict. Vaelthar orders the Gods to attack Victor despite the overwhelming difference in power. Kaelith, however, is already dead and does not participate in the attack against Victor's projection. The projection defeats the assembled Gods, leaving only a negligible crack in its gauntlet after their combined assault. Victor ultimately orders his forces to take the living prisoners and collect the remains of the fallen.[6]
Kaelith is not seen again after Victor's attack. The later chapter listing of the ten Chief Gods continues to identify her title as Goddess of the Night, but does not describe a return to life or any subsequent action by her.[6][8]

### Skills & Abilities
Kaelith was a Chief God whose demonstrated abilities centered on the Concept of Night. Her strongest techniques emphasized concealment, deception, battlefield control, and regeneration. Her performance also revealed clear limits: her illusions required energy and attention to sustain, experienced opponents could learn to distinguish or destroy them, and concentrated attacks or Eldritch corruption could overwhelm her defenses.

#### Night Manipulation
- Night Manifestation: Kaelith's Divine body resembles a living expanse of starry night. She can expand into a star-studded mist while intensifying the surrounding darkness.[1][2]
- Darkness Expansion: She deepens the night across a large region, concealing stars and providing an environment in which her other abilities become more effective. The expansion also obscures her real position and makes it harder to distinguish genuine presences from false ones.[2]
- Energy Blast: Kaelith gathers the Concept of Night in her hand and releases it as a blast of energy. Her initial use against the Horsemen fails to hit them, demonstrating that the attack is not unavoidable.[2]
- Energy Wave: After regenerating from her first injuries, she launches a powerful wave of energy at the three Horsemen. They evade it and continue their coordinated assault.[2]
- Condensed Night Barrier: Kaelith compresses Night around her body to create a defensive barrier. Death's scythe cannot penetrate the barrier by itself, but Famine breaks it by concentrating his attacks on the same point. She uses a similar barrier later inside the Eldritch domain, where Death eventually breaks through it as well.[3][4]
- Dissolution into Darkness: She can dissolve into darkness and reappear elsewhere, allowing her to evade a scythe strike and counterattack from behind. This movement is not foolproof; Death later reads her movement and cuts her side before she can disappear.[3][4]

#### Illusions and Perception Manipulation
- Perception Distortion: Kaelith's darkness affects perception, spatial orientation, and the ability to distinguish real presences from illusions. Unlike [[Azrakar]]'s spatial manipulation, her technique interferes with how opponents perceive the battlefield.[2]
- Multiple Images: She fills the darkness with numerous images of herself. The images do not all possess the same density, and their presence subtly alters the surrounding night. An opponent who pays attention to these differences can begin identifying which presences are genuine.[2]
- Illusions Capable of Inflicting Damage: Her night-fed projections can attack rather than merely distract. One projection cuts War's arm with daggers, and later images inflict real damage during the fight against Death and Famine.[2][3]
- Substitution: Kaelith uses her images to make it appear that Azrakar is in a different position, allowing him to exploit War's mistaken attack. Her illusions also take Azrakar's place when his body is badly damaged, briefly giving him an opportunity to recover.[3]
- Spatial Disorientation: She alters her opponents' perception of distance so that what they observe no longer corresponds to reality. This allows her to appear in Famine's blind spot after he attacks a false image, enabling her to strike his abdomen and separate him from Death.[3]
- Concealed Movement: Kaelith uses darkness and false images to conceal her true position and approach opponents from unexpected angles. She emerges from concealed space with a sword while War is focused on Azrakar, and later dissolves into darkness to attack Death from behind.[2][3]
- Offensive Illusions: She can deepen the night around herself and her opponents until her images attack with enough force to inflict genuine wounds. One image pins Famine's arm, giving her an opening to strike his face.[3]
- Illusion Maintenance: Her illusions depend on the energy sustaining them. Famine discovers that he can attack the dark areas where that energy gathers, forcing Kaelith to rebuild the affected region instead of freely attacking. Death also observes differences in the images' movements and waits until the real Kaelith must intervene to maintain the area.[3]
- Limited Effectiveness Against Experienced Opponents: Death and Famine gradually adapt to her illusions, destroy projections by force, and exploit the moments when Kaelith has to maintain her power. Inside the Eldritch domain, she cannot reliably use her former methods to control their senses.[3][4]

#### Regeneration and Body Reconstitution
- Darkness-Based Regeneration: Pure Darkness pours from Kaelith's body and closes severe injuries, including a slashed face, a pierced abdomen, and severed limbs. She also uses darkness to repair injuries while concealing her movements.[2][3]
- Recovery After Dissolution: Her ability to dissolve into darkness allows her to evade an attack and reappear elsewhere. The same technique can be used alongside regeneration to avoid remaining in one place while injured.[3]
- Regeneration Limits: A concentrated attack can break through her defenses and briefly halt her regeneration. Inside [[Gol-goroth]]'s domain, alien energy makes her wounds increasingly difficult to repair. After Death's scythe nearly splits her abdomen, alien corruption penetrates too deeply for her to overcome it.[3][4]
- Not Absolute [[Immortality]]: Her ability to regenerate physical injuries does not prevent [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] from killing her through a direct attack on her Soul. [[Vaelthar]] restores her body before that attack, but the restoration does not save her from Victor.[5]

#### Weapons and Close Combat
- Swordsmanship: Kaelith appears from concealed space with a sword in hand. The available chapters do not establish the weapon's origin, name, or special properties.[2]
- Daggers: A projection nourished by Night attacks War with daggers and inflicts a deep cut on his arm. The transcript does not establish whether the daggers are permanent weapons or temporary manifestations.[2]
- Close-Range Strikes: Kaelith uses the confusion created by her illusions to strike openings in an opponent's defense. She hits Famine in the abdomen and face and lands a blow against Death from behind, although the Horsemen retaliate quickly.[3]
- Combat Adaptation: When straightforward attacks fail, she changes her approach by concealing her movements, altering the placement of her images, and exploiting the confusion caused by her Night. Nevertheless, the Horsemen's stronger fundamentals and coordination gradually overcome her methods.[2][3]

#### Cooperation with Other Chief Gods
- Coordination with [[Azrakar]]: Kaelith combines Night-based deception with Azrakar's spatial manipulation. Her illusions misdirect War's attacks and briefly substitute for Azrakar when he is badly injured. Their cooperation gives them opportunities to counterattack, but it does not prevent Azrakar's eventual destruction.[2][3]
- Night and Silence: Kaelith and [[Nerath]] act together inside [[Gol-goroth]]'s domain. Their combined efforts help pull [[Vhalor]] away from Death's reach, although the Eldritch conditions prevent them from restoring their former control over the battlefield.[4]
- Joint Field with Vhalor and Nerath: After Azrakar's death, the three remaining Chief Gods attune their powers and establish a small field that resists the domain's influence. This is a joint defensive effort rather than proof that Kaelith can independently negate Gol-goroth's domain.[4]
- Limitations in the Eldritch Domain: Kaelith's illusions and battlefield control become substantially less effective inside Gol-goroth's domain. The Horsemen continue to attack in coordination, and the damage to her body eventually prevents her from recreating the illusions.[4]

#### Abilities Not Attributed to Kaelith
- [[Vaelthar]]'s Restoration: Vaelthar restores Kaelith, [[Nerath]], and [[Vhalor]] with a beam of pure Darkness. This is an ability demonstrated by Vaelthar, not an independent resurrection technique demonstrated by Kaelith.[4][5]
- Patron-Sustained Survival: The three surviving Chief Gods remain in danger inside the Eldritch domain, and Nerath recognizes that her patron is a major reason she has survived. The text does not establish that Kaelith possesses an independent ability to nullify [[Gol-goroth]]'s influence or survive indefinitely inside the domain.[4]
- Soul Restoration or Resurrection: Kaelith is restored physically by Vaelthar before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] attacks her Soul. No subsequent chapter confirms that she returns after Victor erases her body and retains her Soul.[5]
