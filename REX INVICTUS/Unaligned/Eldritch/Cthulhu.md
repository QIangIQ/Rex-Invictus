Cthulhu Elderblood is an Outer God and one of the Eldritch daughters born from the union of Victor Elderblood and [[Azathoth Elderblood]]. She exists among the cosmic beings stationed around the Main Timeline and the [[Akashic Records]], where Victor uses his Eldritch family as part of an extreme defensive system against entities attempting to intrude upon their Reality.[1]
Unlike Victor's Blood Dragon descendants, Cthulhu belongs to the Eldritch side of the Elderblood bloodline. Her apparently humanoid body conceals a vastly more dangerous Nightmare Form, and her demonstrated destructive capacity is great enough for an emotional outburst to destroy multiple universes.[1]

### Physical Appearance
In her humanoid manifestation, Cthulhu is a pale-skinned woman with long black hair and black eyes. Her general appearance strongly resembles the Elderblood family and particularly [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], although she lacks the characteristic violet Dragon Eyes normally associated with his Blood Dragon daughters.
This humanoid appearance is not the full representation of her existence. Cthulhu possesses a Nightmare Form, an incomprehensible Eldritch manifestation vastly different in scale from her ordinary body. When Victor moved her away from the Records during one of her emotional outbursts, this form could be seen in the distance destroying several of the living universes surrounding the Main Timeline.[1]

### Personality
Cthulhu possesses the extreme emotions characteristic of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family, but unlike many of his more mature daughters, she has difficulty preventing those emotions from manifesting through her enormous Eldritch power. She is highly affectionate and possessive toward Victor, becoming openly jealous when she believes another member of the family is receiving more of his attention.[1]
Her jealousy can be intensely childish despite the cosmic scale of her existence. Upon discovering that Victor had produced additional daughters with Azathoth, Cthulhu complained about him repeatedly creating new "monstrosities" before becoming upset that he was supposedly paying too much attention to Azathoth. After sensing Victor's Essence within her newly created sisters, her emotional state deteriorated so rapidly that Victor immediately removed her from the vicinity of the Records before she lost control.[1]
Cthulhu also has a particularly hostile relationship with [[Shub-Niggurath]]. Before Shub's creation, [[Nyarlathotep]] remarked that the two had never been able to get along and that Victor's presence was effectively the reason the Eldritch sisters could coexist at all. Victor's influence acts as an emotional anchor for these beings, suppressing enough of their inherent madness and hostility for them to behave as a family rather than immediately turning upon one another.[1]
Despite her volatility, Cthulhu is not portrayed as malicious toward Victor or the Elderblood family. Her most destructive behavior originates from jealousy, emotional instability and the difficulty of controlling an existence whose natural scale is fundamentally incompatible with ordinary environments.

### Synopsis

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Within the future events shown around the [[Akashic Records]], [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] radically redesigned the defenses surrounding the Main Timeline. Rather than attempting to protect an effectively limitless region uniformly, he reinforced the surrounding structure and intentionally left a controlled entrance through which foreign entities would be forced to pass. The space surrounding the Records was then populated with Eldritch beings and other lethal defenses so that intruders who somehow penetrated the first barriers would enter an environment intrinsically hostile to conventional existence.[1]
While discussing further expansion of these defenses, Victor decided that [[Shub-Niggurath]] would be one of the next daughters produced through himself and [[Azathoth Elderblood]]. [[Yog-Sothoth]] Elderblood immediately warned that her sister would dislike this decision, while [[Nyarlathotep]] clarified that she was referring to Cthulhu. According to Nyarlathotep, Cthulhu and Shub had historically been almost incapable of getting along without Victor present to restrain the madness inherent to their existences.[1]
Victor nevertheless proceeded. Shub's natural ability to produce abnormal Life was particularly valuable to his defensive strategy because her existence could generate entire living universes, galaxies, planets and stars. These creations were not ordinary ecosystems but hostile cosmic organisms capable of consuming other things and even one another. Victor intended to place them around the Main Timeline as false worlds and additional layers of protection, forcing invaders to pass through universes filled with cosmic horrors before ever reaching the Records themselves.[1]
After Shub's arrival, Cthulhu sensed her presence and appeared near the group, immediately recognizing the source of the disturbance. She complained to Victor that he had once again created more "monstrosities", but was unable to provide an answer when her father simply asked why he should not. Her actual frustration soon became obvious when she turned toward Azathoth and accused Victor of giving their mother too much attention.[1]
Yog attempted to tease Cthulhu about the circumstances surrounding the new daughters, only for Nyarlathotep to stop her. Cthulhu then used her senses to inspect her sisters and recognized Victor's Essence within them. The realization pushed her toward an emotional crisis. Her hair fell across her eyes, her body began trembling and Victor immediately understood what was about to happen.[1]
Rather than permit an uncontrolled Eldritch manifestation beside the Records, Victor threw Cthulhu far away into the surrounding void. Cthulhu screamed that the situation was unfair before manifesting her Nightmare Form. Her tantrum immediately escalated to a cosmic scale, with Cthulhu destroying several of the living universes that had only recently appeared through Shub-Niggurath's existence. Azathoth reacted with amusement, while Akashic could only sigh at what had become a literal cosmic tantrum.[1]
Victor prepared to personally calm his daughter while ordering Azathoth and the others to continue watching for intruders. The incident demonstrated both Cthulhu's tremendous destructive capacity and the reason Victor considered his own presence essential when dealing with his Eldritch children: emotions that would amount to an ordinary family argument among conventional beings could become universe-destroying events when expressed by an Outer God.[1]

### Skills & Abilities
As one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Azathoth's Eldritch daughters, Cthulhu's capabilities operate on a fundamentally different scale from conventional Gods and supernatural races. Only a limited portion of her individual abilities has been directly demonstrated, and abilities belonging to other [[Outer Gods]] should not automatically be attributed to her without evidence.
- Outer God Physiology: Cthulhu is an Eldritch cosmic existence born from Victor and Azathoth rather than a conventional member of Creation. She can naturally exist in the Nothingness surrounding the [[Akashic Records]], an environment where conventional concepts and the normal progression of Time cease functioning consistently.[1]
- Eldritch Physiology: Her true nature is fundamentally incompatible with ordinary existence. The Eldritch beings Victor placed around the Records are described as beings of indescribable appearance whose mere existence can drive conventional observers insane, which is one of the reasons they are used as deterrents against foreign invaders.[1]
- Transformation: Cthulhu can abandon her humanoid manifestation and enter her Nightmare Form, revealing a vastly greater Eldritch body suited to operating on a cosmic scale.[1]
- Cosmic-Scale Destructive Power: During a single uncontrolled tantrum, Cthulhu's Nightmare Form destroyed several universes that had been created around the Records following [[Shub-Niggurath]]'s manifestation.[1]
- Universal Destruction: Cthulhu is therefore directly demonstrated to possess sufficient offensive power to destroy complete universes rather than merely damaging individual planets, stars or galaxies.[1]
- Supernatural Perception: Cthulhu sensed Shub-Niggurath's "dreadful presence" before appearing beside the family, allowing her to recognize who had manifested without first seeing her.[1]
- Essence Perception: By examining her sisters, Cthulhu could perceive Victor's Essence within them. The discovery immediately revealed to her the nature of their connection to their father.[1]
- Survival in Nothingness: Cthulhu functions normally outside the protected Records in the surrounding Nothingness, despite that region lacking the conventional structure that gives Space, Time and other Concepts ordinary coherence.[1]
- Resistance to Nonexistent Environmental Conditions: The absence or instability of ordinary cosmic laws outside the Records does not prevent Cthulhu from existing, perceiving her surroundings or using her powers.[1]
- Eldritch Presence: Cthulhu's genuine existence belongs to the same category of cosmic horrors Victor deliberately uses to make the exterior of the Main Timeline effectively uninhabitable to ordinary intruders. Such beings are dangerous even before consciously attacking because beings incapable of comprehending them can suffer madness or catastrophic physical consequences simply from exposure.[1]
- Emotion-Linked Power Manifestation: Cthulhu's emotional control is directly connected to how safely she can express her power. Severe jealousy caused her to enter her Nightmare Form and begin destroying universes, illustrating why younger Eldritch beings are expected to suppress their emotions around sensitive structures such as the Akashic Records.[1]

#### Weaknesses
- Emotional Instability: Cthulhu's greatest demonstrated weakness is her limited control over her emotions. Jealousy can escalate extremely quickly and cause her Eldritch nature to manifest destructively.[1]
- Limited Self-Control: Unlike the more experienced Azathoth, who can express strong emotions without automatically causing cosmic phenomena, younger Eldritch beings such as Cthulhu require substantially greater restraint around important locations.[1]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Authority: Despite her enormous power, Cthulhu remains responsive to Victor as her father. His presence is specifically described as one of the primary reasons the Eldritch sisters can coexist without their inherent madness causing constant conflict.[1]

### Trivia
- [[Yog-Sothoth]] Elderblood refers to her by the shortened nickname "Cthu".[1]
- Cthulhu and [[Shub-Niggurath]] have an exceptionally poor relationship. [[Nyarlathotep]] states that without [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], the two would have difficulty peacefully coexisting.[1]
- Her first directly shown feat is one of the largest introductory destructive feats among Victor's daughters: while merely throwing a tantrum, her Nightmare Form destroyed several universes.[1]
- Cthulhu's jealousy is directed primarily toward the amount of attention Victor gives other members of the Eldritch branch of the family rather than toward conventional political authority or material possessions.[1]
- The previous version of this article listed several epithets associated with the character's Lovecraftian namesake, such as "The Sleeper of R'lyeh" and "Lord of the Watery Abyss". These titles have not been attributed to Cthulhu within [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]] and are therefore not treated as canonical MTW titles.
- Although inspired by the name of the famous cosmic entity from the Cthulhu Mythos, MTW's Cthulhu is a distinct character within the Elderblood family and should not automatically inherit abilities, history, relationships or titles belonging to other fictional versions of Cthulhu.
