I will call you... Yog-Sothoth, my first daughter, a girl who is my hope to escape from this accursed prison and find my companion. Now go, my daughter, go find your father.
—[[Azathoth Elderblood]] to Yog-Sothoth, Chapter 980
Yog-Sothoth Elderblood, usually called Yog, is an Outer God, Eldritch Primordial Goddess, Dragon Goddess and Imperial Princess of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. She is the first directly created daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and Azathoth Elderblood, born when the imprisoned Azathoth combined a portion of her own foreign Eldritch Essence with a minute quantity of Victor's Essence. Although Azathoth performed the act that produced her, Yog inherited the overwhelming majority of the characteristics forming her identity from Victor while simultaneously receiving the alien conceptual foundation that made her a genuine Outer God.[3][4]
Yog was created partly because Azathoth desired a child and companion connected to Victor and partly because a daughter sharing both parents' characteristics could eventually become the bridge necessary to escape her imprisonment. Victor nevertheless rejected the idea that this purpose defined Yog's existence. From the moment she reached him, he regarded her as his daughter before regarding her as a possible solution to any cosmic problem, and the rest of the Elderblood household eventually adopted the same approach.[4][5][6]
Her existence is unusual even among Victor's children. Victor and Azathoth occupy the foundational position of Eldritch [[Chaos]], whereas Yog began as an Eldritch Primordial Goddess possessing the foreign Concepts of Space, Intangibility and Nullity. These Concepts originate outside the conceptual framework of Victor's native Creation and therefore do not possess the same predetermined native limitations as conventional Divinities. By the late Imperial era, Yog had matured into one of the most extraordinary members of Victor's second generation, with her original Nullity developing into the broader expressions described as Unreality and Nothingness.[5][7][8]
Despite her cosmic nature, Yog grew into a recognizable member of the Elderblood family rather than an emotionally detached force of nature. She participates in sibling rivalries, seeks praise, follows household rules with varying degrees of technical interpretation and is regarded by Anna as one of the better-behaved daughters. At the same time, her instinctive comfort around cosmic horrors, her accessible Eldritch Form and her later experiments with Nigredo demonstrate that the domestic woman raised by Victor remains fundamentally alien by the standards of ordinary Reality.[8][9][10]

### Physical Appearance
Yog first appeared before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] in the humanoid form of a very young girl, approximately one year old in appearance. She possessed pale skin, black hair, black eyes and facial features strongly resembling Victor. Her resemblance to him reflected the large influence his Essence had upon her formation, although her eyes remained black instead of possessing the violet coloration characteristic of many of Victor's later daughters.[3][4]
The infant body was never the complete measure of Yog's existence. She was an immature Outer God attempting to express herself in a form suitable for interaction with Victor and his family, and the difference between that manageable body and what she actually was became apparent through the distortions surrounding her. Her powers affected Space and Reality passively because she had not yet learned to regulate herself, while Victor discovered that his ordinary Draconic authority was far less effective at controlling the phenomenon than his own Eldritch nature.[4]
By the late Imperial era Yog normally manifests as an adult woman with a slim figure, pale skin and long black hair. Even this deliberately ordinary presentation can appear subtly unnatural; when she joined Hinata and Irene in Chapter 1160, her arrival was described as though Reality itself had barely registered her presence.[8]
Her genuine Eldritch manifestation is far more disturbing. By Chapter 1188, Yog can deliberately assume a grotesquely distorted humanoid form covered with numerous eyes staring in different directions. The Elderblood daughters can tolerate the sight because of their own Divine and Eldritch heritage, but the unrestricted perception of such beings is dangerous to ordinary life. Earlier material specifically uses Victor and Yog as examples of existences whose unrestrained presence can overwhelm weaker observers with more information than their minds and bodies can process, with their Eldritch Forms worsening the effect considerably.[11][10]

### Personality
Yog's earliest behavior was genuinely childlike despite the scale of her existence. She recognized [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] as her father, repeated information Azathoth had given her and openly admitted that she was too young and weak to accomplish the task for which her mother hoped she would eventually be useful. She lacked even basic knowledge of how to restrain her own presence, asking Victor how she was supposed to "control herself" when he instructed her to stop distorting the surroundings.[4]
Victor's refusal to raise her as a weapon became central to her development. Her ability to interfere with fundamental phenomena made it easy for the adults to imagine future uses for her, but [[Scathach]], Jeanne, Anna, [[Hestia]] and Victor firmly rejected burdening a newborn with responsibilities simply because of what she might eventually become. Yog was instead allowed to mature as one of Victor's daughters, building familial attachments and developing judgment alongside her extraordinary nature.[5][6]
As an adult, Yog is intelligent, composed and comparatively well behaved, although she has inherited the Elderblood family's love of mischief. She is perfectly willing to exploit technicalities in Victor's household rules and possesses enough pride to be manipulated through praise. Hinata and Irene successfully flatter her by calling her their strongest, best and most beautiful sister, causing Yog to smugly demand further compliments; she only becomes annoyed when they add "most terrifying" to the list.[8]
Her reputation for good behavior is nevertheless genuine. When her sisters struggle to acquire protected photographs of Victor as a Human child, Yog bypasses their complicated plans by simply approaching Anna and asking for copies. Anna agrees because Yog causes fewer problems than many of the others, leaving Hinata and Irene stunned that ordinary manners worked. Yog subsequently raises her chin in triumph and declares this to be her "power".[8]
Beneath that domestic personality, Yog retains instincts fundamentally different from most native Beings. Victor's artificial Eldritch galaxy causes almost every daughter examining it to recoil from the enormous cosmic horrors hidden among its stars, yet Yog and Nigredo regard the creatures as beautiful. Yog also becomes increasingly enthusiastic about creating bizarre forms of Eldritch Life herself. By Chapter 1188, she and Nigredo are experimenting with Eldritch organisms and Leviathans near the family castle with an attitude closer to children showing off unusual pets than Gods handling potentially catastrophic cosmic organisms.[12][9][10]
Yog is proud of her work and occasionally possessive of credit. When Velina discovers the Eldritch fish produced by Yog and Nigredo, Yog initially calls them her creations until Nigredo silently becomes irritated, forcing her to amend the statement and acknowledge that the project belongs to both sisters. The exchange reflects how thoroughly Yog's alien nature has been integrated into ordinary sibling behavior: the two cosmic horrors can argue over creative credit in essentially the same way their less Eldritch sisters argue about anything else.[10]

### Origin and Nature
Yog is neither a conventional biological child nor an avatar of either parent. Azathoth created an independent existence by combining her own Eldritch Essence with a minute quantity of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Essence obtained through their distant connection. Yog consequently possesses both parents as genuine metaphysical parents without functioning as a detachable extension of Azathoth.[3][4]
Her formation also explains why she resembles Victor so strongly. Although Azathoth was the parent who consciously created her, the structure composing Yog inherited most of its recognizable characteristics from Victor. At the same time, Azathoth supplied the foreign Eldritch foundation that allowed Yog to emerge as an Outer God rather than simply another Dragon-related daughter of Victor.[3][4]
At birth, Yog was extremely immature by the standards of [[Outer Gods]]. Her visible humanoid body was tiny relative to the true scale implied by her nature, and her lack of control meant that an existence capable of affecting Reality could still possess the mentality and experience of an infant. The contradiction between those two conditions defined her introduction: she was dangerous because of what she was, not because she possessed malicious intent.[3][4]
Victor's later study of the [[Eldritch Pantheon]] provided a more precise classification. He and Azathoth represent [[Chaos]], the source position from which their Eldritch Divine hierarchy develops. Yog occupies the next major stage as an Eldritch Primordial Goddess, while daughters such as [[Nero]] and Ophis began as ordinary Eldritch Goddesses drawing from Victor's developing Pantheon. Victor emphasized that these were starting points rather than immutable castes; Nero or Ophis could eventually develop toward the same stage at which Yog began.[7]
Yog also possesses a Draconic side through Victor. Chapter 1160 explicitly identifies her as a Dragon Goddess and describes her as carrying Victor's powers through the Essence used in her creation. Her identity is therefore not cleanly divided between "Outer God" and "Dragon": she is one of the clearest examples of the supernatural overlap created by Victor's existence, simultaneously belonging to his family of Dragon Gods and to the Eldritch hierarchy generated with Azathoth.[8]
Her relationship with the Eldritch Pantheon becomes increasingly important in the late story. Yog is described as a fully fledged Eldritch Goddess while Nigredo possesses a Deity strongly connected to the same system. Victor later explains that all of his daughters possess [[Eldritch Energy]] to varying degrees, but Yog and Nigredo are singled out as particularly strong examples. By the time the Great Chaos hierarchy is articulated, Yog stands among the true Outer Gods directly beneath the two Creators and above the Great Old Ones generated by the next generation.[9][13][10]

### Synopsis

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Yog's story began long before she personally met [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], through the history of [[Azathoth Elderblood]]. Azathoth originated outside Victor's native Creation and entered a Reality whose normal Rules were not designed to contain her. As she expanded, learned and consumed information, she eventually attracted the attention of the native cosmic administrators. Their usual methods could not simply erase an Outsider according to the Rules governing native Beings, leading instead to her confinement.[14]
Azathoth remained capable of observing distant regions through separated portions of her Essence. One of those connections eventually exposed Victor, and beneath the Vampire, Dragon and Divine characteristics composing him she recognized something she had never expected to encounter inside the native Cosmos: Victor was independently becoming the same fundamental category of Eldritch existence as herself. For a Being whose development had been dominated by isolation, discovering another potential [[Chaos]] capable of becoming a genuine companion fundamentally changed her priorities.[14][3]
Unable to reach Victor physically, Azathoth used the connection between them to acquire a minute portion of his Essence and combined it with her own. The result became Yog-Sothoth, whom Azathoth immediately regarded as her first daughter and as the hope through which she might eventually escape imprisonment and reach Victor.[3]
Yog traveled through that connection and appeared before Victor as a small child. She recognized him as her father immediately and identified Azathoth as her mother, leaving Victor, Amara and Roxanne attempting to comprehend how his curiosity regarding a mysterious connection had somehow resulted in a daughter with an Outer God. Victor could feel the relationship himself and accepted Yog despite having had no involvement in planning her birth.[4]
Her condition immediately demonstrated the problem created by placing cosmic power inside an infant. Yog's presence distorted Space and Reality without conscious intent. When Victor ordered the surrounding Space to return to normal using the Dragon Language, his command was ignored. Roxanne correctly suspected that an Outer God could not be controlled so easily through a native Draconic mechanism, and Victor shifted into his own Nightmare/Eldritch state. Yog reacted to the grotesque appearance by enthusiastically calling her father handsome, while Victor's Eldritch authority finally interacted naturally enough with her foreign nature to restrain the leaking effects.[4]
Victor then identified the three Concepts forming the core of Yog's newly born existence: Space, Intangibility and Nullity. Their implications greatly increased his concern because they operated outside the native conceptual framework and could interact with phenomena that conventional powers treated as absolute.[4]
Yog also explained the purpose Azathoth had given her. She knew her mother was imprisoned, knew enough to identify that she should eventually help free her and knew the direction of the connection leading back toward Azathoth, but openly admitted that she was currently too weak and too young to accomplish anything significant. Victor accepted that freeing Azathoth could become a future objective but refused to treat Yog as a disposable mechanism designed solely for that purpose.[4]
Introducing Yog to the rest of the family produced jealousy and confusion before acceptance. Ophis and [[Nero]] were particularly unhappy about another daughter suddenly appearing and receiving Victor's attention, while Violet, Anna, Leona and the others attempted to understand the circumstances surrounding a child whose mother existed somewhere beyond the known civilizations of their Cosmos.[15]
The discussion eventually shifted from fear of Yog's capabilities toward concern for Yog herself. The adults realized that her unusual Concepts could tempt people to rely upon her against problems involving Space, Infinity, Death and other fundamental phenomena. [[Scathach]], Jeanne, Anna and [[Hestia]] rejected exploiting her in this way, arguing that she was still a newborn regardless of how terrifying her future potential might become. Victor agreed completely and chose to raise her alongside the rest of his daughters rather than accelerate her development simply because the family possessed cosmic problems she might someday solve.[5][6]
Analysis of Yog also widened the family's understanding of the Cosmos. Her Concepts did not originate from the native Creation and therefore were not limited by exactly the same structure governing ordinary Divinities. Victor concluded that her practical limit would be determined more by how much her own existence could endure and develop than by a predefined ceiling belonging to the native conceptual system.[5]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
The development of [[Nero]] and Ophis later gave [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] enough information to place Yog within a broader Eldritch hierarchy. His own Nightmare existence and Azathoth occupied the foundational position of Eldritch [[Chaos]], while Yog could be understood as an Eldritch Primordial Goddess created from those sources. Nero and Ophis represented Eldritch Goddesses beginning from a lower stage, although Victor emphasized that the difference concerned their starting point rather than an absolute limit preventing them from eventually reaching Yog's condition.[7]
This realization also established the basic shape of the [[Eldritch Pantheon]]. Victor recognized that his Eldritch family was beginning to reproduce a structure comparable in principle to conventional Pantheons without depending upon the same cosmological source: Chaos at the beginning, Eldritch [[Primordial Gods]] beneath that foundation and developing Eldritch Gods drawing power from the system.[7]
Yog matured throughout the enormous development of the Elderblood civilization rather than remaining the uncontrolled infant of her introduction. Her humanoid appearance became adult, her control stabilized and the conceptual identity surrounding Nullity deepened into later descriptions involving Unreality and Nothingness. She simultaneously became a clearer example of the principle Victor had begun understanding through himself and Azathoth: an Outer God's humanoid body is a chosen manifestation of something far less conventional underneath.[7][8]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
By the mature Imperial era, Yog had reached a qualitative state of existence exceptional even among [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s monstrous family. During Victor's observation of [[Tyche]], the narration used a deliberately simplified numerical analogy to explain existential differences: Tyche served as one example, True Dragon Goddesses such as Anna, [[Aphrodite]] and [[Scathach]] another, while Victor, Jeanne, Azathoth and Yog were grouped at a still higher qualitative level. The narration explicitly warned that the numbers used in the comparison were illustrative rather than literal cosmological ranks.[11]
The same explanation established why Yog's true presence is dangerous even without deliberate hostility. Sufficiently weak observers attempting to perceive beings of her class without those beings limiting the information being presented can die from informational overload. Exposing Yog's Eldritch Form would make the problem substantially worse.[11]
Yog's first substantial domestic appearance of the era involved Hinata and Irene attempting to obtain photographs of Victor from his Human childhood. Her arrival prompted the narration to describe the mature state of her Nullity-related nature through Unreality and Nothingness, establishing that she could interfere with the validity of effects, Concepts and realities rather than relying solely upon conventional destruction. The same passage identifies her as a Dragon Goddess created through Victor's Essence.[8]
Victor's rules forbid the daughters from turning ordinary sibling disagreements into battles involving their most dangerous supernatural authorities. Yog immediately looked for a loophole, asking who had said she actually intended to use her abilities. In practice she needed none of them. She entered Anna's office, politely asked for copies of the photographs and received them because Anna considered her one of the daughters least likely to cause unnecessary trouble. Yog emerged extremely pleased with herself and accepted increasingly extravagant praise from Hinata and Irene until being called terrifying finally offended her.[8]
Yog later joined Victor's lessons about dimensions, Reality and the deeper meaning of Divine Concepts. Victor produced a miniature artificial galaxy using Beginning, Negativity and Eldritch influence. When the daughters examined its apparent stars with their Divine senses, they discovered enormous cosmic horrors. Everyone except Yog and Nigredo instinctively moved away from the sight, while the two Eldritch-oriented sisters simply described the creatures as beautiful.[12]
Victor identified Yog as a fully fledged Eldritch Goddess and explained why she felt so little discomfort inside an environment formed from Eldritch principles. Nigredo possessed a Deity strongly associated with the Eldritch, while Akasha was another compatible sister because Victor had created her through his Eldritch Divinity.[9]
Yog's understanding of the artificial galaxy extended beyond aesthetic appreciation. When Victor asked the daughters to identify what was wrong with it, Yog realized that the apparent Life lacked something fundamental, describing the missing quality as Life itself or an "anchor". Victor confirmed that the galaxy contained extraordinarily convincing replicas rather than truly independent organisms. He summoned genuine Eldritch Beings, including [[Abhoth]], allowing the girls to compare authentic Life against the empty imitations and demonstrating that a stable autonomous Cosmos required deeper Conceptual Rules rather than matter and Energy alone.[9]
Victor privately acknowledged that directly combining himself with Azathoth could create something much closer to a genuine Eldritch galaxy, but refused to perform the experiment because such an event could advertise their location to external cosmic powers. Yog's reaction nevertheless demonstrated that she possessed an intuitive sensitivity toward Eldritch and conceptual structures that many of her sisters needed Victor to explain explicitly.[9]
The original reason Azathoth created Yog finally became an active objective again after the crisis surrounding [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]]. Victor demanded to know where Azathoth's actual body had been imprisoned, and Azathoth revealed that the forces responsible for containing her had moved her into a dimension outside the Universe, beyond the ordinary region governed by the Rules of their native Creator and where conventional Time held little useful meaning.[16]
Yog retained a connection to Azathoth despite this separation. Azathoth confirmed that their daughter could guide Victor toward the prison, making the relationship between mother and daughter one of the few known paths to a location inaccessible through conventional travel. Victor consequently identified Yog and Moira as the two daughters most important to the future rescue: Yog could lead him to Azathoth, while Moira's abnormal relationship with Reality might allow the family to bypass restrictions that even Victor could not solve through native authority alone.[16]
During Victor's later lessons concerning Energy, he explained that [[Eldritch Energy]] effectively originates from outside their Universe and distorts local Reality according to foreign logic. All of his daughters possess varying traces of that inheritance, but Yog and Nigredo are specifically highlighted as two whose Eldritch nature is particularly pronounced.[13]
By Chapter 1188, Yog had moved from simply understanding Eldritch Life to creating it. She and Nigredo appeared near the family castle in their grotesque Eldritch Forms, accompanied by lesser cosmic organisms and Leviathans. Their river contained hybrid Eldritch fish created jointly by the sisters. Yog contributed the spatial side of their nature, allowing them to move through Space, while Nigredo gave them the capacity to survive within black holes. Leone later expanded their adaptability.[10]
The project quickly became a family experiment. Valentina, Hinata, Silvia, Rosemary, Stella, Velina, Leone and the others began adding their own supernatural characteristics while using one of Victor's scales as an additional Draconic foundation. What emerged was not another true Outer God but an independently living Divine entity. Victor classified the creature as [[Cthugha]], a Great Old One, and welcomed him into the Great [[Chaos]].[10]
Cthugha's creation also clarified Yog's place in the larger Eldritch hierarchy. Victor and Azathoth stand at the top as Creators. Beneath them are true [[Outer Gods]] or Eldritch Gods, including their daughters and Abhoth; direct descendants possess higher internal status than other created Gods. Beneath the Outer Gods are Divine creations called Great Old Ones, followed by non-Divine Servants of the Outer Gods. Yog therefore belongs to the first generation directly beneath the two Creators while also retaining her more specific developmental classification as an Eldritch Primordial Goddess.[10]
Her involvement in Cthugha's birth represents a significant change from the infant created to rescue Azathoth. Yog is no longer merely a daughter whose parents speculate about what she might eventually become; she has begun producing subordinate Eldritch Life of her own and participating in the expansion of the Great Chaos alongside the rest of Victor's second generation.[10]

### Role in the Eldritch Pantheon
Main article: [[Eldritch Pantheon]]
Yog occupies one of the highest positions within the Divine hierarchy created around [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Azathoth. The Pantheon's earlier developmental model places Victor and Azathoth as Eldritch [[Chaos]], Yog as an Eldritch Primordial Goddess and developing daughters such as [[Nero]] and Ophis as Eldritch Goddesses. The distinction represents differing starting conditions and degrees of development rather than an absolute prohibition against lower members advancing further.[7]
The later Great Chaos hierarchy describes a different aspect of the same supernatural family. There, Victor and Azathoth are the two Creators, their true [[Outer Gods]] occupy the next tier, Divine subordinate creations are Great Old Ones and lesser non-Divine creations are Servants of the Outer Gods. Yog is therefore simultaneously an Outer God within the Great Chaos creation hierarchy and an Eldritch Primordial Goddess within the developmental structure of the Pantheon.[7][10]
Her direct descent from both Creators gives her especially high internal standing. [[Abhoth]] is also a genuine Eldritch God created by Victor and Azathoth, but the later hierarchy explicitly places the direct daughters above him in Pantheon status because they are descendants of the Creators rather than specialized Gods produced later.[10]
Yog's importance is not solely ceremonial. She is one of the daughters with the strongest foreign nature, can recognize deficiencies in artificial Eldritch Life, remains comfortable in environments other Elderbloods find instinctively disturbing and has progressed far enough to create subordinate Eldritch organisms with Nigredo. The emergence of [[Cthugha]] shows that the generation beneath Victor and Azathoth has begun creating its own Divine descendants, turning the Pantheon into a reproducing cosmological hierarchy rather than a collection of isolated Gods.[9][10]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is Yog's father, the dominant source of the characteristics forming her existence and the person responsible for turning what could have been an isolated cosmic weapon into a daughter raised inside a family. Yog recognized him immediately after meeting him, while Victor accepted the relationship as soon as he confirmed what had happened. His response remained consistent throughout her childhood: Azathoth's prison mattered, but not enough to justify sacrificing Yog's development or treating her as equipment.[4][6]
Victor also became the first person capable of teaching Yog control over her foreign nature. His ordinary Dragon Language initially failed against the distortions produced by her, but his own Nightmare/Eldritch state could interact with her naturally enough to suppress the leakage. The scene reinforced both their biological connection and the deeper similarity between their Eldritch existences.[4]
Their relationship remains visibly affectionate after Yog reaches adulthood. The future material makes Victor's role even more important: the mature [[Outer Gods]] surrounding him possess personalities and instincts that could make peaceful coexistence extremely difficult without a central figure they all genuinely recognize. Yog explicitly values having a present father, and Victor functions as the familial anchor preventing a collection of cosmic horrors from behaving like unrelated hostile entities.[2]
Future/Extra material also depicts Yog as one of Victor's daughter-wives. This belongs to the enormously later Extra chronology and should not be retroactively treated as the status of the infant or current main-story Yog.[2]

#### Azathoth Elderblood
Azathoth is Yog's mother and the Being who actively performed her creation. Yog is the first explicitly identified child created through the union of Azathoth's Essence with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s, making her birth the first concrete proof that the two Eldritch [[Chaos]] existences could produce a genuine descendant together.[3]
Azathoth's motives combined affection, loneliness and practicality. She wanted a daughter connected to the companion she had discovered, but she also recognized that a child carrying both their characteristics could eventually cross barriers that prevented Azathoth herself from reaching Victor. Yog's name and original mission were therefore given directly by her mother.[3]
Their bond survives a level of separation that would make conventional family contact impossible. Even after Azathoth's true body is moved into a dimension outside the Universe, Yog remains sufficiently connected to her that Azathoth can identify her daughter as the guide Victor will need to find the prison.[16]

#### Nigredo Elderblood
[[Nigredo Elderblood]] is Yog's most prominent current Eldritch collaborator. The two stand out even among [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters because of how naturally they react to cosmic horrors and foreign Eldritch environments. Both can manifest accessible Eldritch Forms by Chapter 1188 and are treated as the two high-ranking [[Outer Gods]] involved in the experiment surrounding the hybrid creatures near the family castle.[9][10]
Their abilities and personalities complement one another during creation. Yog contributes spatial characteristics to their hybrid organisms, Nigredo contributes black-hole survival and the two jointly claim authorship over the bizarre lifeforms they produce. Their argument over Yog initially taking sole credit also shows that their relationship remains that of sisters rather than two impersonal cosmic entities cooperating solely because their abilities match.[10]

#### Ophis Tepes Elderblood and Nero Elderblood
Ophis and [[Nero]] were among the first daughters to react emotionally to Yog's arrival. Both became jealous when the unknown infant immediately received [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s attention and briefly treated her existence as evidence that their father might continue returning home with previously unknown daughters from increasingly absurd origins.[15]
The initial jealousy did not prevent Yog from becoming fully integrated into the same sibling group. By the mature Imperial era, the difference between the three is primarily metaphysical development rather than familial legitimacy: Yog began as an Eldritch Primordial Goddess, while Nero and Ophis developed their Eldritch Divinities later through Victor's Pantheon.[7]

#### Anna Elderblood
Anna is one of the maternal figures Yog openly accepts as her mother. Her willingness to provide Yog with protected photographs of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] reveals an unusually high degree of trust compared with the suspicion she directs toward several more troublesome daughters. Yog responds with obvious affection, thanking her and promising not to cause problems.[8]

### Future / Extra
The Future/Extra chapters depict a much later version of Yog and should be kept separate from the capabilities, responsibilities and relationships of her current main-story incarnation. Her Extra appearance actually predates her chronological main-story birth from the reader's perspective, meaning readers encountered an enormously developed future Yog before Chapters 980–985 explained where she came from.[17]
In this future, Yog belongs to a mature Eldritch family operating on a scale far beyond ordinary Imperial administration. Yog and [[Nyarlathotep]] are associated with the defense of separate cosmological Layers, while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] reorganizes the surrounding barriers so that hostile entities attempting to enter the protected Creation are forced toward controlled points rather than being free to invade anywhere.[17][2]
The future [[Outer Gods]] can expose far greater Eldritch manifestations than those normally shown in the current story. Yog, Azathoth, Nyarlathotep and related members exert enough existential pressure that ordinary Reality struggles with their simultaneous manifestation, making emotional control and restraint necessary even when they are not deliberately attacking anything.[2]
Their family structure becomes equally important. Independent Outer Gods naturally possess enough madness, contradiction and incompatibility to make coexistence unstable, but Victor's presence provides a shared emotional center. Yog values having a father who is actually present in their lives, while the broader family recognizes that Victor's involvement prevents their extraordinary household from collapsing into the kind of disorder expected from unrelated cosmic horrors.[2]
The Extra chronology also depicts Yog in the future daughter-wife structure surrounding Victor. The precise point at which that relationship formally changes is unrevealed, so it should remain labeled as a Future/Extra status rather than applied to the current main-story chronology.[2]

### Abilities

Yog-Sothoth Elderblood is a fully-fledged Outer God, an Eldritch Primordial Goddess and a later-described Dragon Goddess created from the Essences of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Azathoth Elderblood]]. Her individual supernatural foundation is centered upon the foreign Eldritch Concepts <Space>, <Intangibility> and <Nullity>, Concepts originating outside the native Creation and therefore not possessing the same predetermined developmental limits as ordinary native Divinities.[1][2][3]
Yog's danger is not based solely upon destructive force. Her Eldritch existence interacts with Reality through foreign principles, while Nullity governs the unreal and non-existent rather than merely destroying conventional matter. At birth, Victor already identified applications involving impossible phenomena, complete erasure, interaction with Non-Existence and reversal of erasure; after more than one hundred thousand subjective years of development, her authority had expanded into the manipulation of the validity of effects, Concepts, realities and Laws themselves.[2][4] She additionally possesses Draconic inheritance and, according to Chapter 1160, the powers inherited through Victor's Essence, although inheritance should not be equated with Victor's experience, skill or present mastery of every individual ability.[4]

#### Skills & Abilities

##### Eldritch Primordial Goddess Physiology
- Outer God Physiology: Yog is a genuine Eldritch existence rather than a native Goddess altered by [[Eldritch Energy]]. She was produced through the combined Essences of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Azathoth and possesses a fundamental nature partially external to the framework governing conventional native Gods and Divinities.[1][2]
- Eldritch Primordial Goddess: Victor later places Yog at the Eldritch equivalent of the Primordial-Goddess stage. Victor and Azathoth occupy the foundational state of Eldritch [[Chaos]], while Yog began existence from the higher-order Eldritch Primordial position rather than developing upward from ordinary Eldritch Godhood.[5]
- Fully-Fledged Eldritch Goddess: By Chapter 1168, Yog is explicitly distinguished from daughters who merely carry Eldritch traits and is called a fully-fledged Eldritch Goddess.[6]
- Outsider Nature: Yog's personal Concepts were not generated by the native Creation. Her supernatural foundation consequently operates through foreign conceptual principles rather than being wholly subordinate to the native Divine hierarchy.[3]
- Foreign Conceptual Authority: Because her Concepts originate outside the native cosmological framework, they do not possess the same predetermined upper limit as ordinary native Divinities.[3]
- Existence-Limited Growth: The practical limit upon Yog's Concepts is described in terms of how much development her own existence can sustain rather than a fixed ceiling imposed by the native Universe.[3]
- Higher Starting Point: Yog began at a substantially higher Eldritch stage than [[Nero]] and Ophis. Victor nevertheless treats this as a difference in starting position rather than an absolute barrier preventing other Eldritch Goddesses from eventually reaching a comparable stage.[5]
- Existential Similarity to Victor: When analyzing Yog after her arrival, Victor regarded her as approximately 99% similar to himself in existential terms, with the remaining difference produced by the combination of his Essence with Azathoth's. He instinctively recognized Yog as fundamentally close to his own developing type of existence.[2]
- Conceptual Existence: Mature Eldritch Goddesses are not adequately described through ordinary physical biology. Yog's deeper existence carries conceptual and informational weight capable of becoming dangerous merely through perception.[5][7]
- Eldritch Energy: Yog naturally possesses foreign Eldritch Energy. When Victor later explains that all of his daughters inherit some quantity of it, Yog and Nigredo are specifically highlighted as daughters possessing particularly strong Eldritch characteristics.[8]
- Passive Reality Distortion: Immediately after birth, Yog distorted Reality around herself without deliberately activating an ability because she had no control over the influence leaking from her existence.[2]
- Passive Spatial Distortion: The Space surrounding newborn Yog continuously destabilized as her innate Concepts leaked outward.[2]
- Ontological Corruption: Phenomena rewritten through Yog's foreign Eldritch authority can become fundamentally distorted rather than remaining ordinary native phenomena with additional Energy applied to them.[4]
- Existential Pressure: By the mature Imperial era, Yog belongs to a class of existence whose unrestricted manifestation is itself hazardous to vastly weaker beings.[7]
- Information Overload: A sufficiently weak observer attempting to perceive Yog without her existence being suppressed can receive more information than their mind and body are capable of processing, potentially resulting in death.[7]
- Madness Inducement: Victor specifically uses Eldritch Goddesses such as Yog as examples of beings whose true state can drive ordinary mortals insane merely through perception.[5]

##### True Eldritch Form
- Nightmare Form / Eldritch Form: Yog possesses a genuine Eldritch manifestation fundamentally different from her humanoid appearance. Unlike older versions of this page which treated the form almost entirely as a future ability, Yog explicitly manifests her Eldritch Form in the current main-story chronology by Chapter 1188.[9]
- Grotesque Eldritch Manifestation: Yog's manifested Eldritch state retains a roughly humanoid configuration while becoming heavily distorted and covered with numerous eyes facing different directions.[9]
- Formless Nature: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s explanation of developed Eldritch existences establishes that their true nature is not fundamentally restricted to one biological shape. Humanoid bodies are usable manifestations rather than absolute anatomical limits.[5]
- Variable Manifestation: Yog can interact with her family through a stable humanoid body despite possessing a deeper Eldritch state with radically different characteristics.[5][9]
- True-Form Information Hazard: The danger produced by perceiving Yog becomes substantially greater when she exposes her Eldritch manifestation instead of suppressing herself through a humanoid form.[7]
- Galactic-Scale Destructive Potential: Victor explicitly identifies Yog as one of his strongest daughters and states that, in her Eldritch Form, she is capable of creating destruction on a Galactic scale.[10]
- Extreme Threat Without Transformation: Victor further notes that Yog remains exceptionally dangerous without using her Eldritch Form because of the combination of Space, Intangibility, Nullity and her overall Eldritch nature.[10]
- Reality Destabilization (Future/Extra): In the Future/Extra material, several fully developed [[Outer Gods]] manifesting their greater forms simultaneously place severe pressure upon ordinary Reality.[11][12]

##### Eldritch Concepts
Yog was created with three explicitly identified personal Concepts: <Space>, <Intangibility> and <Nullity>. Azathoth deliberately selected their combination because their interaction could eventually produce a means of bypassing the prison separating her from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[1][2]

###### Space
- Space Manipulation: <Space> is one of Yog's three innate foreign Concepts.[1][2]
- Conceptual Spatial Authority: Yog's Space is an Eldritch Concept originating outside the native Creation rather than a conventional native Space Divinity.[3]
- Spatial Distortion: Yog distorted surrounding Space passively as a newborn because she lacked control over the Concept.[2]
- Spatial Interaction: Her authority can interact with spatial phenomena at a level relevant even to powers derived from Infinity.[2]
- Spatial Obstruction Bypass: Space naturally complements Intangibility, allowing Yog to circumvent restrictions dependent upon conventional spatial separation and ordinary physical positioning.[2]
- Infinity Countermeasure: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly identifies Yog's combination of Space and Intangibility as a potential answer to Infinity's ordinarily overwhelming spatial defense.[2]
- Spatial Trait Bestowal (Collaborative Creation): Yog and Nigredo later create hybrid Eldritch-Leviathan organisms capable of moving through Space. The creatures combine properties derived from the sisters' respective Eldritch natures rather than being ordinary biological animals.[9]

###### Intangibility
- Intangibility: <Intangibility> is Yog's second innate foreign Concept and a fundamental part of her conceptual makeup.[1][2]
- Conceptual Phasing: Yog can exist in an intangible state in which ordinary material interaction does not function against her conventionally.[2]
- Physical Attack Bypass: Attacks that depend entirely upon conventional physical contact can fail to interact normally with her intangible existence.[2]
- Spatial Obstruction Bypass: Intangibility works together with Space against restrictions that assume the target occupies a conventional material position.[2]
- Infinity Countermeasure: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] specifically cites the interaction between Space and Intangibility when considering Yog a countermeasure against Infinity.[2]
- Non-Physical Existence: Because Intangibility is a Concept rather than a simple biological ability, it forms part of Yog's existential structure rather than functioning only as temporary bodily phasing.[2]

###### Nullity
<Nullity> is Yog's most unusual personal Concept. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] describes it as the functional inversion of conventional Reality Manipulation: Reality Manipulation governs what is real, whereas Nullity governs the unreal, the non-existent and phenomena that ordinarily possess no valid place within Reality.[2]

###### Non-Existence and Nothingness
- Nullity Manipulation: Yog possesses direct conceptual authority over Nullity.[1][2]
- Unreality Manipulation: She can interact with and manipulate states defined as unreal rather than being limited to already-existing phenomena.[2][4]
- Nothingness Manipulation: By Chapter 1160, her developed authority is explicitly described through Unreality and Nothingness.[4]
- Non-Existence Manipulation: Yog can manipulate Non-Existence as though it were an operative state capable of direct interaction.[2]
- Non-Existence Interaction: Beings or phenomena lacking conventional existence remain interactable to Nullity because the Concept directly governs the state they occupy.[2]
- Non-Existential Phenomena Manifestation: Nullity can allow phenomena possessing no ordinary valid existence to operate within conditions governed by Yog.[2]

###### Null Space and Impossible Phenomena
- Null Space Manipulation: Chapter 981 describes Nullity as capable of controlling a non-existent "Space" whose behavior does not need to follow the assumptions of ordinary Reality.[2]
- Field Control: Yog can govern phenomena operating inside conditions dominated by Nullity.[2]
- Rule Establishment (Null Space): Within the example given for Nullity, the user can dictate operative "rules" governing unreal phenomena inside the non-existent region.[2]
- Impossible Phenomena Manifestation: Nullity can give functional expression to things that should not exist according to conventional Reality.[2]
- Unreal Phenomena Creation and Control: The source explanation describes the user as being capable of producing and controlling unreal things within Nullity.[2]
- Logic-Defying Phenomena: Nullity can support effects that ordinary Reality would consider impossible. This should be understood through the specific mechanics given for Nullity rather than as unrestricted control over all logic.[2]

###### Existence Erasure
- Existence Erasure: Nullity can erase a target from existence rather than merely inflicting conventional destruction.[2]
- Matter Erasure: The erasing function explicitly extends from ordinary matter upward.[2]
- Metaphysical Erasure: The source description states that the erasure can reach metaphysical Aspects rather than stopping at physical structures.[2]
- Abstract Erasure: Abstract Concepts are included among the levels that Nullity can theoretically erase.[2]
- Conceptual Erasure: Conceptual structures can consequently be targeted by Nullity's erasing function.[2]
- Multi-Layered Erasure: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s explanation describes the theoretical range as extending from physical matter through the metaphysical and Abstract components of Reality.[2]

###### Erasure Reversal and Restoration
- Erasure Reversal: Nullity possesses a reversal function capable of bringing back that which has been erased.[2]
- Existence Restoration: A removed existence can theoretically be returned when Yog possesses sufficient information concerning what was erased.[2]
- Non-Existence Reversal: The ability can operate after a target has entered a non-existent state rather than requiring a conventional surviving body.[2]
- High-Level Restoration (Theoretical): Because the original erasure reaches metaphysical and Abstract levels, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] treats the inverse function as correspondingly capable of restoring extremely deeply erased phenomena when the necessary information exists.[2]
- Primordial Death Countermeasure (Theoretical): Victor specifically concludes that Yog could theoretically return things erased by Primordial Death, counteracting the result of the Concept of the End under the correct conditions.[2]
- Knowledge Requirement: Restoration is not unconditional. Yog must know or retain sufficient information concerning the erased target before she can properly reverse its removal.[2]

###### Validity Manipulation
By the late Imperial era, Yog's mastery has progressed beyond the basic examples [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] identified at her birth. Chapter 1160 describes her as capable of manipulating whether effects, Concepts, realities and Laws remain valid within the area governed by her power.[4]
- Effect Manipulation: Yog can attack the validity sustaining a supernatural effect rather than necessarily overpowering the effect through greater raw Energy.[4]
- Effect Nullification: An effect can cease operating after its validity is removed.[4]
- Effect Dissolution: The ability can render a phenomenon non-operative through invalidation rather than physical destruction.[4]
- Conceptual Manipulation: Yog can apply developed Nullity directly to Concepts.[2][4]
- Conceptual Nullification: A Concept can cease functioning as a valid principle inside the affected sphere.[4]
- Conceptual Dissolution: She can attack the validity maintaining a Concept rather than merely resisting one of its manifestations.[4]
- Reality Manipulation (Through Nullity): Yog's mature Nullity can interfere with whether conditions recognized as real remain valid.[4]
- Reality Nullification: A condition of Reality can have the basis of its validity removed within the range of her authority.[4]
- Reality Override: Foreign conditions created through Yog's Unreality can replace native assumptions within the affected region.[4]
- Law Manipulation: Yog can interfere directly with Laws determining which phenomena Reality permits.[4]
- Law Nullification: A Law can cease applying within the affected sphere.[4]
- Law Dissolution: Rather than simply violating a Law, Yog can dissolve the validity maintaining it.[4]
- Native-Rule Bypass: Her foreign authority can invalidate a restriction instead of obtaining permission through the native cosmological system.[4]
- [[Primordial Chaos]] Law Override: Chapter 1160 explicitly states that her developed Nullity can override Laws originating from Primordial Chaos within the scope of her influence.[4]
- Cosmological Requirement Negation: If Reality requires a particular condition before a phenomenon is allowed to occur, Yog can remove the Law enforcing that requirement.[4]
- Life-Requirement Negation: The narrative example states that Yog could permit Life to exist on a planet without a World Tree by erasing the native Law requiring one rather than by acquiring the Universal Tree's own Authority over Life.[4]

###### Eldritch Corruption
- Eldritch Corruption: Yog's foreign influence can corrupt phenomena altered through Nullity, causing the result to conform increasingly to Eldritch logic instead of remaining completely native to the original Creation.[4]
- Ontological Corruption: The corruption changes the nature of what is affected rather than behaving like ordinary poison or contamination.[4]
- Conceptual Corruption: Phenomena validated through foreign conceptual authority can acquire characteristics incompatible with their original native framework.[4]
- Biological Corruption: The World Tree example specifically warns that Life made possible through Yog's altered Laws could emerge distorted into cosmic-horror-like forms rather than ordinary native organisms.[4]
- Reality Corruption: Regions modified through her foreign authority can begin functioning according to conditions differing from unaltered native Reality.[4]
Note: Eldritch corruption is not equivalent to Demonic Miasma, ordinary disease, moral corruption or conventional mind control. It is the ontological distortion produced when foreign Eldritch principles are imposed upon a native cosmological framework.

##### Dragon Goddess Physiology
- Dragon Goddess Physiology: Chapter 1160 explicitly states that Yog is a Dragon Goddess because she was created from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Essence.[4]
- Draconic Inheritance: Yog possesses characteristics inherited from Victor's Draconic existence in addition to her Outer God foundation.[4]
- Divine-Draconic Existence: Her modern existence therefore combines her Eldritch Primordial nature with the inheritance of a Dragon God.[4]
- Variable Physical Manifestation: Her Dragon-Goddess status coexists with an Eldritch conceptual existence whose underlying form is not restricted to ordinary biological anatomy.[5][4]
Canon Clarification: The novel explicitly calls Yog a Dragon Goddess, but the reviewed material does not require her primary racial classification to be replaced with conventional Blood Dragon. Her fundamental confirmed race remains an Outer God with Draconic inheritance.

##### Paternal Power Inheritance
- Complete Paternal Power Inheritance (By Statement): Chapter 1160 explicitly states that because Yog was created using [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Essence, she possesses all of Victor's powers.[4] Her available arsenal is therefore broader than Space, Intangibility and Nullity alone.
- Inherited Draconic Powers: Victor's Draconic characteristics are confirmed strongly enough for Yog to be identified as a Dragon Goddess.[4]
- Inherited Physical and Regenerative Abilities: The broad inheritance statement encompasses Victor's supernatural physical and regenerative powers in principle.[4]
- Inherited Energy Manipulation: Victor's Energy-related powers fall within the broad inheritance statement.[4]
- Inherited Blood and Elemental Powers: His Blood, Fire, Ice, Lightning and related powers are likewise encompassed by the statement unless contradicted by a more specific limitation.[4]
- Inherited Soul-Related Powers: Victor's Soul-based powers are included by the same literal inheritance statement.[4]
- Inherited Divine and Eldritch Powers: Yog possesses her own Eldritch foundation while additionally inheriting supernatural characteristics carried by Victor's Essence.[4]
For the complete documented catalogue of the paternal arsenal referenced by this statement, see Victor Elderblood/Abilities.
Important: The statement that Yog possesses Victor's powers does not establish Victor-equivalent proficiency, [[Martial Arts]], experience, conceptual understanding or current maximum output with every inherited ability. Individual mastery should be treated as confirmed only when Yog demonstrates it or receives a specific statement.

##### Inherited Reality Perception
- Truth / Reality Perception (Inherited): [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later explains that all of his biological daughters inherit variations of his ability to perceive the deeper truth of Reality.[13]
- Source-Code Perception: Victor describes the ability as seeing the "source code of Reality", allowing sufficiently developed daughters to perceive the deeper Rules from which dimensional structures are constructed.[13]
- Dimensional Perception: Levels of dimensions created according to the Rules of the native Universe can become perceptible through this inherited faculty.[13]
- Potential Reality Editing: Victor states that the daughters can eventually alter what they perceive once they understand what they are doing. This is an inherited developmental potential and should not be treated as proof that Yog has independently demonstrated every possible application.[13]
- Divine Senses: Yog participates with her sisters in examining Victor's artificial galaxy through Divine perception and can perceive the Eldritch organisms hidden within its stellar structures.[13]

##### Eldritch Perception and Compatibility
- Eldritch Perception: Yog can perceive Eldritch environments and Beings without suffering the instinctive rejection displayed by sisters whose Eldritch nature is less developed.[6]
- Cosmic-Horror Recognition: When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] created an artificial Eldritch galaxy, nearly every daughter instinctively recoiled after seeing the cosmic horrors inside it. Yog and Nigredo instead called them beautiful.[13][6]
- Conceptual Structural Perception: Yog notices that Victor's artificial Eldritch galaxy lacks something deeper despite possessing convincing matter, Energy and simulated creatures, describing the missing quality as Life or an "anchor". Victor confirms that her observation is partially correct.[6]
- Authentic-Life Differentiation: After Victor summons genuine Eldritch Beings such as [[Abhoth]], Yog participates in comparing them against the artificial replicas and can perceive the distinction between authentic autonomous Life and a convincing simulation lacking sufficient conceptual foundation.[6]
- Eldritch Reality Compatibility: Yog is naturally comfortable in environments shaped through foreign Eldritch principles that would psychologically or existentially disturb ordinary native beings.[6]

##### Life Creation and Eldritch Biological Engineering
- Life Creation (Collaborative): By Chapter 1188, Yog and Nigredo are actively creating hybrid Eldritch organisms instead of merely observing or manipulating pre-existing Life.[9]
- Eldritch Creature Creation: The sisters populate a river with functional Eldritch fish possessing abnormal cosmic characteristics.[9]
- Hybridization: Their creations combine Eldritch organisms with characteristics derived from Leviathans, producing hybrid Life capable of surviving environments ordinary creatures could not endure.[9]
- Supernatural Trait Integration: Yog and Nigredo successfully incorporate their respective Eldritch characteristics into the created organisms. The resulting fish can traverse Space and survive inside black holes.[9]
- Further Adaptive Modification (Collaborative): Leone subsequently contributes extreme adaptability to the creatures, demonstrating that Yog's creations can accept additional supernatural traits from other Elderblood daughters.[9]

##### Great Old One Co-Creation
- Divine Life Creation (Collaborative): Yog participates in the family experiment that ultimately produces [[Cthugha]].[9]
- Power Contribution: Yog, Nigredo, Leone, Valentina, Silvia, Rosemary, Hinata, Stella, Velina and other sisters each contribute supernatural characteristics to the developing organism while one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s scales supplies an additional Draconic foundation.[9]
- Great Old One Creation (Collaborative): The resulting Being develops genuine Divinity and is formally classified by Victor as a Great Old One. Yog is therefore one of Cthugha's co-creators.[9]
- Subordinate Eldritch Creation: Cthugha's birth establishes that Victor's Eldritch daughters can collectively produce Divine subordinate entities even though Victor states that only himself and Azathoth can create true Eldritch Gods/Outer Gods of the higher tier.[9]
Limitation: Yog did not independently create Cthugha, and his complete characteristics cannot be attributed solely to her. The feat resulted from the combined powers of multiple sisters, Eldritch organisms, Leviathan characteristics and Victor's scale.

##### Connection to Azathoth
- Maternal Eldritch Connection: Yog retains an extraordinarily deep connection to [[Azathoth Elderblood]] despite the extreme separation between them.[14]
- Extra-Universal Resonance: Azathoth reveals that her true body is imprisoned inside a dimension outside the Universe, where the normal Rules of their native Creator do not apply, yet Yog remains connected to her.[14]
- Extra-Universal Guidance: Azathoth specifically states that Yog can guide [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] toward the location of her prison.[14]
- Prison-Locating Function: Yog therefore acts as a living metaphysical connection to a location otherwise inaccessible through ordinary native cosmological navigation.[14]
- Key to Azathoth's Liberation: Victor identifies Yog and Moira as the daughters central to the future rescue plan. Yog supplies the path to Azathoth, while Moira may provide a means of bypassing the restrictions surrounding the prison.[14]

##### Power and Existential Scale
- One of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Strongest Daughters: Victor explicitly considers Yog one of the strongest members of his enormous second generation because of her combination of Nullity, Space, Intangibility and Eldritch nature.[10]
- Galactic Threat (Eldritch Form): Victor states that Yog can wreak havoc on a Galactic scale while using her Eldritch Form.[10]
- High Threat Without Transformation: He further states that she remains extremely dangerous without transforming because Space and Intangibility make conventional engagement with her exceptionally difficult.[10]
- Primordial-Killing Potential (Statement / Intended Role): Victor considers Yog part of a future solution intended not only to free Azathoth but potentially to kill a Primordial-level opponent. This is a statement of intended potential and matchup value rather than a feat showing Yog independently defeating a completed Primordial Being.[10]
- Exceptional Existential Quality: Chapter 1155 uses a deliberately simplified numerical analogy in which [[Tyche]] occupies one qualitative level, True Dragon Goddesses such as Anna, [[Aphrodite]] and [[Scathach]] occupy another and the abnormal group containing Victor, Jeanne, Azathoth and Yog occupies a still higher level.[7]
- Below Completed Primordial Beings (Illustrative Comparison): The same analogy places established Primordial Beings above the group containing Yog and Creator-level existences higher still.[7]
- Non-Literal Ranking: The numbers used in the analogy are explicitly stated to possess no literal meaning and should not be treated as an official numerical power system.[7]

##### Native Authority Resistance
- Resistance to Native Reality Enforcement: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] initially attempts to stop newborn Yog's spatial disturbance with the Dragon Language, issuing a command that would normally impose his [[Will Solace|Will]] directly upon Reality. The command fails to control her foreign power.[2]
- Dragon Language Resistance: Native Draconic reality-enforcement cannot simply command Yog's foreign Concepts in the same manner that it commands conventional native phenomena.[2]
- Foreign-Law Independence: Roxanne and Amara connect the failure directly to Yog being an Outer God whose power does not originate from the native framework Victor's normal authority was addressing.[2]
- Eldritch Authority Interaction: Victor succeeds only after entering his own Nightmare Form and using the Eldritch side of his existence, at which point his authority can properly interact with Yog's leaking power.[2]

##### Resistances and Countermeasures
- Resistance to Eldritch Madness: Yog looks directly at [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] after he assumes a grotesque Nightmare manifestation capable of overwhelming ordinary minds and instead embraces him and calls him more handsome.[2]
- Resistance to Eldritch Information Overload: Yog possesses the existential compatibility required to perceive high-order [[Outer Gods]] without suffering the cognitive collapse expected of ordinary observers.[2][7]
- [[Eldritch Energy]] Compatibility: Foreign Eldritch environments do not produce the instinctive rejection they cause in conventional native Beings.[6]
- Reality-Distortion Compatibility: Yog can exist within Reality destabilized by her own nature and by related Eldritch manifestations.[2]
- Foreign Conceptual Independence: Space, Intangibility and Nullity are not wholly dependent upon the native conceptual framework and therefore should not be treated as conventional native Divinities possessing identical restrictions.[3]
- Physical Attack Countermeasure: Intangibility provides a direct answer to attacks dependent upon conventional material interaction.[2]
- Spatial Restriction Countermeasure: Space and Intangibility together provide methods of bypassing restrictions dependent upon conventional spatial separation.[2]
- Infinity Countermeasure: Victor explicitly identifies the combination of Space and Intangibility as a countermeasure against Infinity.[2]
- Non-Existence Countermeasure: Nullity permits Yog to interact directly with phenomena occupying non-existent states.[2]
- Existence-Erasure Countermeasure: Nullity's reversal can theoretically restore something erased from existence if Yog possesses sufficient information about the erased target.[2]
- End / Primordial Death Countermeasure (Theoretical): Victor uses [[True Death]] as the extreme example of an erasure Yog could potentially reverse under the correct conditions.[2]
- Native Law Countermeasure: Mature Nullity can invalidate native Laws within the sphere of Yog's authority rather than requiring her to obey every local restriction.[4]
- [[Primordial Chaos]] Law Countermeasure: Her developed authority is specifically stated to be capable of overriding Laws established through Primordial Chaos within its affected range.[4]
Note: A countermeasure is not the same as unconditional immunity. Yog possessing a method of opposing Infinity, End, native Laws or physical attacks does not mean every application of those powers from a sufficiently superior Being automatically fails against her.

##### Future / Extra Abilities
The following material belongs to a substantially later Yog and should not be retroactively assigned to her inexperienced newborn or current main-story incarnations.
- Layer Protection: Future Yog is one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s developed Eldritch daughters entrusted with protecting a major cosmological Layer.[11][12]
- Cosmic Defense Reinforcement (Collaborative): Yog contributes her power alongside Victor, Azathoth, [[Nyarlathotep]] and other Eldritch Beings to strengthen the defenses surrounding protected Creation.[11]
- Nightmare Form Deployment: Victor specifically orders the participating [[Outer Gods]] to assume their Nightmare Forms while contributing to the defensive process.[11]
- Reality-Layer Reinforcement: Yog's Eldritch influence contributes to strengthening the boundaries separating the protected region from hostile outsiders.[11]
- Controlled Entry-Point Support: Victor restructures the larger defense so that external invaders are directed toward controlled access points rather than being capable of entering Creation arbitrarily. Yog contributes power to the system but does not independently construct the entire framework.[11]
- High-Order Eldritch Coexistence: Future Yog possesses enough control to coexist with several immensely developed Outer Gods whose unrestricted manifestations and emotions could otherwise destabilize surrounding Reality.[12]
- Existential Self-Control: The mature Elderblood Outer Gods can suppress the catastrophic environmental consequences their deeper forms could otherwise impose upon surrounding existence.[12]
