Gol-goroth is an extraordinarily powerful sentient Eldritch-Draconic greatsword created by Victor Elderblood and entrusted to War, his strongest Herald of [[Chaos]], during the preparations for the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s offensive against the Upper Sectors. Manifested from pure purple Dragon Power and awakened through an Eldritch declaration, Gol-goroth possesses its own consciousness, supernatural perception and the ability to empower a wielder recognized by the Emperor. Its principal function is to transform its approved wielder into War personified, granting access to an unpredictable manifestation of chaotic Eldritch War that transcends the conventional understanding of organized warfare.[1]
Unlike an ordinary enchanted weapon, Gol-goroth is a conscious supernatural existence whose powers are directly connected to the extraordinary authority of its creator. During its awakening, the greatsword developed an enormous eye and acknowledged Victor as Great Chaos, demonstrating awareness of both its origin and the purpose for which it had been created. After receiving its name, the weapon briefly merged with War's arm before returning to its greatsword form, immediately expanding the Herald's existing supernatural abilities. The resulting empowerment granted War access to a form of warfare characterized by unpredictability, unrestrained conflict and the absence of conventional battlefield rules. Its extraordinary nature made it particularly suitable for a solitary combatant confronting overwhelming enemy numbers, since unleashing chaotic Eldritch War near friendly forces could place them at risk alongside the intended enemies.[1]
Gol-goroth's importance extends beyond its ability to strengthen War's individual combat capabilities. During his confrontation with the [[Black Eclipse Pantheon]], the weapon absorbed Energy emanating from its wielder and opened its enormous eye, enabling the manifestation of three additional Eldritch beings corresponding to Famine, Pestilence and Death. These manifestations emerged from War's body and possessed independent combat capabilities, effectively restoring the apocalyptic roles of the original Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse through a new supernatural existence. Their appearance demonstrated that Victor had provided War with an extraordinary means of reinforcement, allowing his Herald to confront multiple powerful Gods without depending upon an ordinary Imperial military formation.[2]
Gol-goroth also serves as an exceptionally effective weapon against powerful Divine beings. War employs it to break through Void-based spatial imprisonment, destroy supernatural barriers, redirect concentrated Divine attacks and exploit structural weaknesses within complex spatial constructions. During his extended battle against [[Azrakar]], the Primordial God of the Void, its supernatural durability and ability to channel the Herald's Energy allow War to withstand and overcome several techniques capable of devastating astronomical regions. Nevertheless, the effectiveness of these applications depends considerably upon War's personal strength, martial experience and precise control, rather than every result originating from an independent ability of the sword.[2][3]
The weapon's consciousness remains active throughout the confrontation. When the [[Vharzath|Great Darkness]] personally intervenes to prevent the battle from devastating his territory, Gol-goroth detects an unfamiliar presence and warns War that another powerful Being has arrived. This establishes that its sentience is not limited to obeying its original creator, as it can independently perceive supernatural changes and communicate useful information to its wielder during combat.[3]
Its largest shown function comes later, when the four Horsemen are losing the open fight. Gol-goroth opens its eye again, and the narration calls that opening the reveal of its true purpose. The sword raises an Eldritch domain that isolates the space completely. Inside it, Eldritch beings have the advantage, and the laws of the universe cease to apply. The knights' dormant Eldritch aspects wake. The Gods' hold on that space ends. Azrakar's void and [[Vhalor]]'s shadows do not work inside it. The four erase Azrakar there. The domain is still up afterward. Gol-goroth breaks Vhalor's shadow ward and opens his shoulder, and his realm no longer affects the knights. When [[Vaelthar]] comes through, his presence strains the domain. War judges that leader stronger than himself, sees no way out, and shuts the domain down. The sword is not shown destroyed, and it is not shown leaving War's hand.[4][5][6]

### Description
Gol-goroth is an Eldritch-Draconic weapon whose existence is inseparably connected to [[Victor Elderblood]]'s supernatural authority. It was created specifically for War, whose previous existence as one of the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse and subsequent transformation into a [[Chaos]] Progenitor made him an exceptionally compatible wielder. Rather than functioning merely as a conduit through which supernatural Energy is released, the sword establishes a direct conceptual relationship between its bearer and the nature of warfare, allowing the approved individual to embody an Eldritch manifestation of War itself.[1]
The weapon's creation differs fundamentally from the manufacture of ordinary Divine Artifacts. Victor did not require an existing physical sword, conventional forging materials or an established enchantment process. Instead, he manifested an enormous greatsword directly from his Dragon Power and awakened its consciousness through words spoken in the Eldritch language. The declaration imposed a condition upon the resulting existence, linking the ability to become War personified with the recognition of the Emperor. Gol-goroth subsequently acknowledged Victor's authority, received its name and became the personal companion of his selected Herald.[1]
Its supernatural foundation combines characteristics associated with Dragon Power and Eldritch existence. Although the original manifestation was composed of pure [[Draconic Energy]], the awakening process introduced an extraordinary Eldritch nature that distinguished the weapon from conventional Draconic armaments. An enormous eye emerged from the blade, accompanied by a brief release of uncanny Energy that was immediately suppressed. The weapon's recognition of Victor as Great Chaos established that its existence was directly associated with the authority of its creator rather than simply containing an impersonal magical effect.[1]
Gol-goroth's principal function is to empower an Emperor-recognized wielder through the personification of War. In War's hands, this effect interacts with abilities and experiences already accumulated throughout his previous existence as a Hell-Fire Demon, naturally ascended [[Demon God]] and Chaos Progenitor. The weapon therefore does not create the original Horseman's identity or replace the supernatural characteristics obtained through Victor's earlier reconstruction. Instead, it grants an additional Eldritch manifestation of warfare, expanding his existing powers into a more chaotic and unpredictable form.[1]
The weapon possesses genuine consciousness and is capable of independently recognizing supernatural presences, communicating with its wielder and activating extraordinary manifestations through their shared power. Its awakening of the Eldritch Horsemen demonstrates that the relationship between Gol-goroth and War extends beyond ordinary physical weapon use. The sword absorbs the Energy emanating from its bearer, opens its enormous eye and participates in a supernatural process through which additional apocalyptic combatants emerge from War's body. Although the exact metaphysical mechanism remains unidentified, the resulting incarnations possess independent mobility, supernatural attacks and the capacity to confront powerful Gods without being directly controlled through every movement of the sword.[2][3]
Gol-goroth is particularly suited to warfare involving large numbers of enemies and limited friendly support. The chaotic nature of its power makes ordinary distinctions between organized armies, carefully regulated engagements and unpredictable supernatural conflict less meaningful. War immediately recognizes that such an ability should not be unleashed carelessly near Imperial allies, preferring circumstances in which every surrounding combatant is an enemy. This characteristic aligns with Victor's decision to deploy War into hostile territory before committing the main Imperial Legions, allowing the Herald to employ his new powers without requiring the Emperor to expose ordinary soldiers to the consequences of chaotic Eldritch warfare.[1]

#### Appearance
Gol-goroth initially manifests as an enormous purple greatsword composed of pure Dragon Power. Its size is appropriate to War's extraordinary physique, and its supernatural construction allows it to withstand combat involving Divine beings capable of devastating entire star systems. During its creation, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] holds the weapon before his face and speaks in the Eldritch language, causing the blade to undergo an immediate transformation. Its form changes as an enormous eye opens within the weapon, accompanied by an uncanny Energy that briefly manifests before being suppressed.[1]
The gigantic eye constitutes Gol-goroth's most distinctive visible characteristic. Immediately after awakening, it turns toward Victor and acknowledges him as Great [[Chaos]], demonstrating that the eye is associated with the newly created consciousness rather than functioning as a purely decorative feature. The weapon subsequently returns to a usable greatsword form and is entrusted to War, briefly integrating with his arm before reappearing as a separate object. Its appearance remains sufficiently recognizable that the Herald can carry it over his shoulder and employ it through conventional sword movements, despite the extraordinary supernatural abilities contained within its structure.[1]
The eye appears again during the confrontation against Black Eclipse. As Gol-goroth absorbs the Energy emanating from War, the enormous eye opens upon the blade and accompanies the manifestation of the Eldritch incarnations of the other Horsemen. It opens a third time when the four Horsemen are pressed. That opening is the one the narration calls the reveal of its true purpose, and the domain follows it. The eye is not restricted to the initial awakening. The precise conditions that make it open, and whether it stays visible in ordinary combat, are still not given.[2][4]
Gol-goroth can also temporarily integrate with War's body, changing its physical manifestation without losing its identity as a separate sentient weapon. Its initial fusion occurs when the Herald grasps the hilt, after which the sword immediately reappears in its usual form. The transformation does not prevent War from subsequently throwing the weapon, using it to strike the surrounding space or employing its hilt in close combat. During his battle against [[Azrakar]], the weapon returns to War's hand after being thrown, demonstrating that the wielder is not necessarily required to physically retrieve it through ordinary movement.[1][2]
Its exact dimensions, weight, complete internal structure and potential alternate forms remain unidentified. The weapon possesses the appearance of a greatsword, an awakened eye-bearing manifestation and the ability to integrate temporarily with its bearer, but a complete inventory of additional transformations has not been established.

#### Sentience and Personality
Gol-goroth possesses genuine consciousness and an awareness of its supernatural purpose. Upon awakening, the weapon turns its eye toward [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and acknowledges him as Great [[Chaos]], recognizing its creator without requiring an external command to identify him. When Victor asks whether it understands its destiny, Gol-goroth responds affirmatively and declares that it will fulfill the purpose of its existence. Its immediate recognition of Victor and acceptance of its assigned function demonstrate an intelligence capable of understanding instructions and communicating through speech.[1]
The weapon's consciousness remains active after being entrusted to War. During the confrontation against Black Eclipse, Gol-goroth responds to the Herald's declaration that he has never been alone by absorbing his supernatural Energy and opening its enormous eye. The process enables the appearance of the three Eldritch Horsemen, providing the first substantial demonstration of the weapon's independent supernatural functionality beyond its creation and initial integration with its wielder.[2]
Gol-goroth also possesses supernatural awareness extending beyond the immediate physical movements of the combatants. When the [[Vharzath|Great Darkness]] intervenes within his territory, the sword recognizes that an unfamiliar presence has entered the battlefield and warns War. Although the Herald can also sense the increased supernatural pressure, Gol-goroth communicates its observation independently, demonstrating the practical value of its consciousness during combat. The weapon consequently functions as both a supernatural armament and a companion capable of providing information concerning changes in the surrounding environment.[3]
Its individual personality remains less developed than that of its wielder. Gol-goroth has demonstrated obedience toward Victor, acceptance of its purpose, responsiveness to War's actions and the ability to warn him of supernatural danger. However, its personal preferences, emotions, ambitions and potential capacity to act entirely without a wielder remain unknown. Its consciousness should not be confused with that of the original apocalyptic Horsemen, whose Eldritch manifestations operate as separate combatants associated with War's empowered existence.

### Creation
Gol-goroth was created during the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s preparations for war against civilizations occupying the Upper Sectors. The protective barrier separating [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Sector from the higher regions of the Cosmos was weakening beneath repeated foreign interference, while fragments of Divine Concepts continued appearing throughout Imperial territory. Victor recognized that the invading forces intended to exploit the instability and establish supernatural footholds before their principal military forces could cross the barrier directly. Maintaining the enormous ritual supporting the Empire while resisting these attacks placed considerable strain upon his Soul and Energy reserves, prompting him to prepare a direct response rather than allowing the enemy to dictate the circumstances of the approaching conflict.[1]
After receiving confirmation that the Empire's experimental weapons project had not yet achieved a breakthrough, Victor ordered his civilization to prepare for war within approximately one year. He subsequently summoned War, his strongest Herald, and explained that the foreign Gods had mistaken the Empire for vulnerable prey. The Emperor intended to demonstrate that attacking his territories would provoke overwhelming retaliation, selecting the Herald as the spear through which his judgment would be delivered. War accepted the assignment without hesitation, reaffirming that his will belonged entirely to his master.[1]
Victor then manifested an enormous purple greatsword from pure Dragon Power and raised it before his face. Speaking in the Eldritch language, he established the condition that whoever wielded the weapon with the Emperor's recognition would become War personified. The greatsword immediately changed its shape, and an enormous eye opened upon its surface as uncanny Energy briefly manifested from within it. The newly awakened consciousness gazed toward Victor and acknowledged him as Great [[Chaos]], confirming that it understood the purpose for which it had been created.[1]
Victor named the weapon Gol-goroth and entrusted it to War, declaring that it would become his companion. Upon grasping the hilt, the Herald experienced a brief fusion between the sword and his arm before it returned to its conventional greatsword form. War immediately became aware of new supernatural abilities connected to warfare, recognizing that the power he had received differed fundamentally from ordinary Divine War. It was chaotic, Eldritch and unrestricted by the conventional structure of organized conflict, possessing an unpredictable nature that made it unsuitable for careless use near friendly forces.[1]
The Herald welcomed the transformation and prepared to confront the enemies of the Empire. Carrying Gol-goroth over his shoulder, he unfolded his enormous wings and accelerated toward the Sector barrier, transforming into a comet of Darkness moving faster than light. Victor opened a portal into the Upper Sectors, allowing War to pass through and begin the Imperial offensive before the main army had completed its mobilization. When Violet questioned whether even such a powerful Herald should be sent into hostile territory alone, Victor reassured her that War possessed extraordinary strength and additional support. The true significance of that reassurance would become apparent when Gol-goroth manifested the other three Eldritch Horsemen.[1][2]

### Powers and Abilities
Gol-goroth possesses extraordinary supernatural abilities derived from its Draconic origin, Eldritch awakening and conceptual connection to warfare. Its powers include sentience, bodily integration, supernatural Energy interaction, empowerment of an approved wielder, the manifestation of Eldritch beings associated with the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse, and the Eldritch domain that isolates a battlefield. As a weapon, it is also capable of channeling the destructive Energy and martial techniques of War, allowing its bearer to directly contest powerful Divine authorities and attack abnormal spatial structures. However, the combat capabilities of the sword must be distinguished from those originating from the Herald's own physique, experience and connection to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[1][2][3][4]

#### Intrinsic Abilities
- Sentience: Gol-goroth possesses genuine consciousness and can independently recognize, understand and communicate with other beings. It acknowledges [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] during its awakening and later warns War of an unfamiliar presence entering the battlefield.[1][3]
- Self-Awareness: The weapon understands that it possesses a distinct purpose and voluntarily affirms its intention to fulfill the destiny established by its creator.[1]
- Supernatural Communication: Gol-goroth can speak and convey information without possessing conventional biological vocal structures. It communicates with both its creator and its wielder.[1][3]
- Shapeshifting: Its physical form changes during awakening, and it can temporarily integrate with War's arm before returning to its recognizable greatsword form.[1]
- Biological Integration: Gol-goroth briefly fuses with War's body when accepted, demonstrating a supernatural compatibility between the weapon and its wielder.[1]
- [[Draconic Energy]] Constitution: The weapon originates from Victor's pure Dragon Power, establishing a supernatural foundation derived from the extraordinary characteristics of its creator.[1]
- [[Eldritch Energy]]: Gol-goroth possesses an Eldritch nature awakened through Victor's declaration. Uncanny Energy manifests from its enormous eye, while its principal empowerment is associated with chaotic Eldritch War.[1]
- Energy Absorption and Channeling: The weapon absorbs supernatural Energy emanating from War during the activation of the Eldritch Horsemen, demonstrating an ability to interact directly with the power of its recognized wielder.[2]
- Eye Manifestation: An enormous supernatural eye appears during the sword's awakening, again during the activation of its Horseman-related powers, and a third time when the domain is raised. The third opening is the one called its true purpose.[1][2][4]
- Eldritch Domain: When the open fight turns against the Horsemen, Gol-goroth opens its eye and isolates the space. The narration calls this its true purpose. Inside the domain, Eldritch beings have the advantage and the laws of the universe cease to apply. The knights' dormant Eldritch aspects awaken. The Gods' influence over that space ends. [[Azrakar]]'s void is wasted there, and [[Vhalor]]'s shadows are useless. War later shuts the domain down himself, after judging [[Vaelthar]] stronger.[4][5][6]
- Supernatural Perception: Gol-goroth independently detects the presence of an unfamiliar powerful Being when Darkness intervenes in the confrontation, warning War of the change in the surrounding environment.[3]
- Supernatural Weapon Return: After War throws his weapon during the confrontation with Azrakar, Gol-goroth returns to his hand without requiring him to retrieve it through ordinary physical movement. The exact mechanism remains unspecified.[2]

#### Conceptual Empowerment
- Power Bestowal: Gol-goroth grants additional supernatural abilities associated with War to an individual recognized by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], expanding the wielder's existing powers rather than replacing them.[1]
- Personification of War: The weapon's central condition allows an Emperor-recognized wielder to become War personified, establishing a supernatural relationship between its bearer and warfare itself.[1]
- Chaotic Eldritch War: Gol-goroth grants access to an Eldritch manifestation of warfare characterized by unpredictability, unrestricted conflict and the absence of conventional battlefield rules.[1]
- Eldritch War Empowerment: War experiences a substantial expansion of his existing supernatural abilities after obtaining Gol-goroth, enabling him to employ additional forms of warfare against powerful Divine opponents.[1]
- Eldritch Manifestation: Through its interaction with War's Energy and existence, Gol-goroth enables the manifestation of three powerful Eldritch incarnations corresponding to Famine, Pestilence and Death.[2]
- Apocalyptic Reinforcement: The three manifested Horsemen function as independent combatants capable of fighting alongside War, allowing the wielder to confront multiple powerful enemies without requiring additional Imperial personnel.[2][3]
- Warfare Adaptability: The chaotic nature of Gol-goroth's empowerment complements War's ability to operate within unpredictable combat situations, particularly when confronting opponents whose supernatural powers distort the surrounding battlefield.[1][3]

#### Combat Applications
- Supernatural Weapon Combat: Gol-goroth functions as an enormous greatsword capable of withstanding direct combat against a Primordial God whose attacks devastate enormous astronomical regions.[2]
- Energy Channeling: War can concentrate his supernatural Energy through the weapon and release powerful attacks against Divine defenses and abnormal spatial structures.[2]
- Spatial Prison Destruction: War channels power through Gol-goroth to shatter a Void-based spatial prison created by [[Azrakar]], restoring his ability to advance through the affected region.[2]
- Spatial Distortion Breaking: The weapon is used to strike and break supernatural distortions that interfere with conventional movement and spatial relationships.[2]
- Void Energy Dispersal: War repeatedly employs Gol-goroth to cut through concentrated regions of Azrakar's supernatural Darkness, temporarily dispersing the affected Energy and creating openings through which he can advance.[2]
- Projectile Deflection: War uses a precisely angled swing of Gol-goroth to redirect a concentrated Void projectile rather than attempting to overpower its total Energy. The attack subsequently causes thousands of distant stars to collapse, but its destructive power originates from Azrakar's projectile.[3]
- Divine Barrier Destruction: War redirects his sword strike toward a structurally unstable region of Azrakar's defensive barrier, breaking through the protection and creating an opening for a close-range attack.[3]
- Convergence-Point Severing: War concentrates power at Gol-goroth's tip and severs a connection supporting Azrakar's enormous spatial prison, destabilizing the structure without needing to destroy the entire affected region through brute force.[3]
- Close-Quarters Weapon Techniques: War employs both the blade and hilt of Gol-goroth during close-range combat, including a precisely timed strike that damages Azrakar's ribs and interrupts his concentration.[3]
- Weapon Throwing: War throws Gol-goroth toward Azrakar during their confrontation, forcing the Primordial God to evade before the sword returns to its wielder's hand.[2]
- Decapitating Strike: Inside the domain, War cuts off Azrakar's head and pierces his heart while the four Horsemen strike together. The erasure that follows is the four raising their hands. It is not a separate technique of the blade alone.[4]
- Shadow-Ward Break: After Azrakar is erased, Gol-goroth comes down in a vertical blow on [[Vhalor]]. The shadow structure holds for a moment, then shatters. The blade opens his shoulder. His realm no longer affects the knights.[5]

#### Personification of War
Gol-goroth's primary supernatural function is the transformation of its recognized wielder into War personified. The condition was established directly by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] during the weapon's creation, requiring the individual to wield Gol-goroth with the Emperor's recognition. This requirement distinguishes its empowerment from ordinary magical equipment whose abilities are automatically available to anyone capable of carrying or activating the object.[1]
War had already possessed a profound relationship with warfare before obtaining the greatsword. During his original existence, he served as one of the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse and later naturally ascended into Demon Godhood through his determination during battle against Victor. His subsequent reconstruction as a [[Chaos]] Progenitor granted him extraordinary physical characteristics and a direct connection to the Dragon Emperor's Essence. Gol-goroth therefore did not create his identity as the Horseman of War or establish his original supernatural lineage. Instead, it granted an additional Eldritch manifestation capable of expanding and transforming the powers he already possessed.[1]
The resulting empowerment differs fundamentally from ordinary warfare. War perceives the new power as a form of conflict in which established rules lose their significance, conventional distinctions between life and death become meaningless and only unpredictable Chaos remains. This description reflects the extraordinary conceptual nature of the empowerment rather than establishing that the weapon independently possesses unrestricted authority over Life, Death, Resurrection or every possible form of warfare.[1]
Gol-goroth's personification effect becomes especially significant during the confrontation against [[Azrakar]] and [[Kaelith]], when War demonstrates the ability to remain effective amid enormous spatial distortions, deceptive Night manifestations and constantly changing battlefield conditions. His experience and martial precision remain essential to this performance, but the Eldritch empowerment granted by Gol-goroth forms part of the supernatural foundation supporting his transformation into a combatant capable of confronting several powerful Divine beings.[2][3]

#### Chaotic Eldritch War
The chaotic Eldritch War granted by Gol-goroth is an extraordinary manifestation of warfare associated with unrestricted and unpredictable conflict. Unlike conventional military techniques, which depend upon organized formations, recognizable objectives and established relationships between allies and enemies, this power is most appropriate when its wielder operates alone against entirely hostile forces. War immediately recognizes that unleashing the ability near friendly personnel could be dangerous, making it particularly suitable for his deployment into enemy territory before the arrival of the Imperial Legions.[1]
The power's Eldritch characteristics are not limited to visual effects or the presence of unusual Energy. Gol-goroth interacts with War's supernatural existence and provides access to manifestations that would ordinarily be impossible through conventional weapon techniques. Its most significant demonstrated application is the emergence of the other three Eldritch Horsemen, allowing War to bring powerful independent combatants into a confrontation without requiring them to travel from another location through ordinary means.[2]
Despite the conceptual nature of this empowerment, Gol-goroth has not demonstrated unrestricted control over all conflict throughout the Cosmos. Its abilities remain associated with its recognized wielder and the supernatural processes activated through their connection. The chaotic characteristics of its power also do not establish that it automatically neutralizes every opposing Divine technique or prevents enemies from injuring War, who continues sustaining damage during combat against Black Eclipse's Chief Gods.[3]

#### Manifestation of the Eldritch Horsemen
Gol-goroth's most extraordinary demonstrated ability, before the domain, is its role in the manifestation of the remaining Three Horsemen of the Apocalypse. During War's confrontation against [[Azrakar]], [[Kaelith]] arrives as reinforcement and mocks [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] for apparently sending his strongest Herald into enemy territory without support. War rejects her assumption, declaring that his brothers and Emperor have always remained with him. Gol-goroth responds by absorbing the Energy emanating from War and opening its gigantic eye, after which three figures emerge directly from the Herald's body.[2]
The beings identify with the apocalyptic roles of Eldritch Famine incarnate, Eldritch Pestilence incarnate and Eldritch Death incarnate. Their appearance effectively restores the Four Horsemen as a group of powerful Eldritch combatants, with War functioning as the fourth member through the personification granted by Gol-goroth. The manifestations are familiar to the Herald but possess supernatural characteristics distinct from those of their original Demonic existences. Although they correspond to War's deceased brothers, the process does not establish that the original Souls of Famine, Pestilence and Death have been resurrected intact.[2]
The three incarnations are not merely illusions or independent images produced by the blade. They possess distinct physical manifestations, extraordinary supernatural power and the ability to operate as separate combatants. Their appearance allows War to confront multiple Chief Gods without dividing his attention among every opponent, while Gol-goroth remains available as his personal greatsword. Their combined presence turns the battle into a confrontation involving four apocalyptic warriors rather than an isolated Herald facing the overwhelming military strength of Black Eclipse.[2]
During the subsequent engagement, the Eldritch incarnations demonstrate extraordinary combat precision and coordination. Death creates an enormous sword of pure Miasma and attacks Kaelith, while Famine employs a concentrated Energy knife and Pestilence uses cutting attacks capable of severing the Goddess's limbs. Their coordinated assault inflicts severe injuries despite Kaelith's extraordinary Divine power, forcing her to regenerate through supernatural Darkness. The incarnations subsequently return to War when Azrakar and Kaelith coordinate their respective abilities, restoring the balance of the confrontation against the two Chief Gods.[3]
The individual techniques performed by Famine, Pestilence and Death belong to those manifested combatants rather than automatically constituting independent abilities directly exercised by Gol-goroth. The weapon's confirmed function is to enable their manifestation through its interaction with War's Energy and supernatural existence. Their later change of form, the transfer of power between them, and the brief sharing of senses are War's acts once the domain is already up. They are not a second power of the blade.

#### Eldritch Domain
The domain is the function the narration calls Gol-goroth's true purpose. It is not the same act as the first opening of the eye, which brought out Famine, Pestilence and Death. This opening comes when the four are losing the fight in the open and War calls them back to him. The sword isolates the space completely. Eldritch beings have the advantage inside it. The laws of the universe cease to apply there, and only there. The knights' dormant Eldritch aspects awaken. The Gods' influence over that space ends. [[Azrakar]]'s void is wasted. [[Vhalor]]'s shadows are useless. The four corrupt the space and erase Azrakar inside it.[4]
The domain does not end with that death. The fight against [[Kaelith]], [[Nerath]] and Vhalor continues inside it. Gol-goroth is still a sword there: the vertical blow breaks Vhalor's shadow ward and wounds him, and his realm no longer reaches the knights. Pressure from outside then hits the isolated space. The domain holds the first wave. The second starts to break the barrier, and War drops the killing stroke. A gap opens. [[Vaelthar]] comes through it, and his presence strains what is left. War judges that leader stronger than himself, sees no escape and fully fresh enemies, and deactivates the domain himself. Nothing in the text says the sword is broken by that choice.[5][6]

#### Supernatural Awareness
Gol-goroth possesses independent supernatural perception sufficient to recognize the arrival of an unfamiliar powerful presence. When the [[Vharzath|Great Darkness]] intervenes to contain the devastation caused by the confrontation, his Authority spreads throughout the surrounding region and forcibly stabilizes the affected Solar Systems and galaxies. War notices the changed conditions of the battlefield, while Gol-goroth separately warns him that something has entered the surrounding space.[3]
The warning demonstrates that Gol-goroth can communicate useful combat information without requiring its wielder to explicitly request an assessment. However, its perception has not been established as omniscient or unrestricted. The weapon recognizes an unusual presence but does not identify the Great Darkness by name or reveal the complete nature of the Authority responsible for the environmental changes.[3]

### History

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Gol-goroth was created during the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s preparations for war against the Upper Sectors. The barrier protecting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s territory was weakening beneath foreign attacks, and fragments of Divine Concepts had begun appearing throughout Imperial civilization. Victor recognized that the continuing infiltration could compromise his subjects and eventually expose the Empire to a direct invasion. Rather than exhaust his resources indefinitely maintaining the barrier while his enemies determined the pace of the conflict, he ordered the Empire to prepare for war and selected War to initiate a retaliatory offensive.[1]
Victor summoned his Herald and declared that the foreign Gods threatening his civilization would regret treating the Empire as vulnerable prey. He manifested Gol-goroth from pure purple Dragon Power and awakened it through an Eldritch declaration establishing the condition of War personified. The newly conscious weapon developed an enormous eye, acknowledged Victor as Great [[Chaos]] and affirmed its intention to fulfill its purpose. After receiving its name, Gol-goroth was entrusted to War and briefly fused with his arm before returning to its greatsword form. The Herald immediately recognized the emergence of new supernatural abilities associated with chaotic Eldritch warfare.[1]
War departed for the Upper Sectors carrying Gol-goroth over his shoulder, passing through a portal opened by Victor before the arrival of the main Imperial army. Violet questioned the decision to send a single Herald into hostile territory against potentially enormous enemy forces, but Victor remained confident in War's extraordinary strength and his connection to the Emperor. He also implied that the Herald would not truly be alone, suggesting that his preparations included resources extending beyond the personal combat capabilities of War and the newly created sword.[1]
Gol-goroth's first major deployment occurred when War infiltrated the living vessel transporting [[Azrakar]], one of the Ten Chief Gods serving Black Eclipse. The Herald destroyed the vessel and engaged the Primordial God of the Void in a prolonged confrontation involving enormous physical strength, spatial manipulation and destructive [[Divine Energy]]. Azrakar repeatedly attempted to imprison his opponent through abnormal spatial structures, but War concentrated Energy through Gol-goroth and shattered the affected regions, allowing him to continue advancing despite the God of the Void's efforts to control the battlefield.[2]
As the battle escalated, Gol-goroth was employed against concentrated masses of supernatural Darkness and increasingly powerful spatial distortions. War used the greatsword to cut openings through Azrakar's attacks, escape spatial restraints and force the Primordial God into repeated close-range exchanges. When Azrakar attempted to prevent his approach through large-scale Void manifestations, War continued concentrating his power through the weapon and striking the surrounding space until the abnormal structures collapsed. The resulting confrontation devastated star systems and affected neighboring galaxies, demonstrating the extraordinary strength of the combatants and the durability of Gol-goroth as a weapon employed in Galactic-scale warfare.[2]
Azrakar eventually demanded to know the identity of the warrior capable of opposing him despite the extraordinary power of his Divinity. War identified himself as the Herald of an Emperor who had already determined the Primordial God's fate, confirming that the attack originated from Victor's Sector. Their confrontation continued until [[Kaelith]], the Goddess of the Night and another Chief God of Black Eclipse, arrived to reinforce Azrakar. She mocked Victor for apparently sending War to his death without support, only for the Herald to reveal the true significance of Gol-goroth's extraordinary powers.[2]
Gol-goroth absorbed the Energy emanating from War and opened its enormous eye, allowing three Eldritch incarnations to emerge from the Herald's body. These manifestations embodied Famine, Pestilence and Death, restoring the apocalyptic roles of the other Horsemen through a new supernatural existence. Their arrival transformed the confrontation into a battle involving four powerful apocalyptic combatants against the Chief Gods, fulfilling Victor's earlier assurance that War would not truly be alone. The incarnations subsequently engaged Kaelith while War continued confronting Azrakar, allowing the Herald to focus upon the Primordial God of the Void without immediately being overwhelmed by additional enemies.[2]
During the continuing battle, Azrakar compressed an enormous quantity of Void Energy into a black sphere whose supernatural density distorted nearby light and space. War used Gol-goroth to intercept the projectile at a carefully calculated angle, redirecting its trajectory rather than attempting to overpower its total Energy. The attack passed him and entered a distant region containing numerous star systems, where it caused thousands of stars to undergo gravitational collapse. The destruction originated from Azrakar's projectile, while War's successful deflection demonstrated the precision with which he could employ Gol-goroth against attacks possessing extraordinary destructive potential.[3]
War subsequently exploited Gol-goroth in a series of carefully controlled martial exchanges. When Azrakar erected a defensive barrier of Darkness, the Herald altered the angle of his strike and targeted a structurally unstable region created by the God's own movement. The resulting opening allowed War to close the distance and strike Azrakar's ribs with the weapon's hilt, disrupting his concentration and causing him to lose part of the Energy he had gathered. The effectiveness of the technique demonstrated that Gol-goroth could be employed with extraordinary precision rather than functioning exclusively as an instrument of overwhelming destructive force.[3]
Azrakar attempted to regain control of the battle by constructing an enormous spatial prison supported by multiple convergence points. Instead of attempting to destroy the entire affected region, War concentrated power at Gol-goroth's tip and examined the distribution of the surrounding distortions. Recognizing that severing the connection between two supporting points would be considerably easier than overcoming the entire structure, he directed his attack toward the appropriate location and destabilized the prison. The resulting fracture allowed him to escape and resume his offensive against the Primordial God.[3]
The continuing destruction eventually attracted the attention of Equilibrium and the Judge of the Abyss, who recognized that additional galaxies could be lost if the confrontation continued without restraint. Before the cosmic authorities intervened directly, the Great Darkness appeared within his territory and imposed his Authority upon the surrounding Solar Systems and galaxies, forcibly stabilizing their movements and preventing further uncontrolled destruction. He also agreed to surrender the Souls of those killed during the battle to Soul, avoiding additional interference by the [[Primordial Entities]].[3]
The changed conditions of the battlefield attracted Gol-goroth's attention. The weapon independently detected an unfamiliar presence and warned War that something had entered the region. Although the Herald could also sense the increased supernatural pressure, the sword's warning demonstrated that its consciousness remained active during combat and could provide information concerning changes beyond the immediate movements of his opponent. War subsequently resumed fighting as Kaelith began combining her Night-based perception manipulation with Azrakar's spatial techniques, while the three Eldritch Horsemen returned to support him.[3]
The confrontation escalated further when [[Nerath]], Goddess of Silence, and [[Vhalor]], God of Shadows, arrived as additional reinforcements. Their presence brought the number of opposing Chief Gods to four, while Nerath announced that an unidentified authority known as the Great Silence wanted War captured alive. Azrakar accepted the instruction, and the assembled combatants prepared to continue fighting. Gol-goroth remained in War's possession, while the Eldritch incarnations of Famine, Pestilence and Death continued accompanying the Herald.[3]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Gol-goroth's creator and the authority responsible for establishing the conditions governing its supernatural existence. The weapon was formed directly through Victor's Dragon Power and awakened by an Eldritch declaration, immediately recognizing the Emperor as Great [[Chaos]]. Its first words and subsequent affirmation of its purpose demonstrate a direct relationship with the Being responsible for its creation. Victor also assigned its name and selected War as its recognized wielder, establishing the weapon's initial function within the military structure of the [[Elderblood Empire]].[1]
The requirement for Imperial recognition is particularly significant because Gol-goroth's principal empowerment is not established as a power automatically available to anyone who possesses the sword. Its ability to transform a wielder into War personified is connected to the approval of its creator, making Victor's authority an essential component of the weapon's original condition. Whether another individual could receive recognition, whether Victor could revoke an existing authorization and whether Gol-goroth can independently reject a recognized wielder remain unknown.[1]

#### War
War is Gol-goroth's first and only confirmed wielder, as well as the individual for whom [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] created the weapon. His original existence as the Horseman of War, natural ascension into Demon Godhood and subsequent reconstruction into a [[Chaos]] Progenitor provide a supernatural foundation particularly compatible with the powers associated with Gol-goroth. The weapon expands his existing relationship with warfare into an Eldritch manifestation characterized by unpredictability and the ability to operate against overwhelming enemy forces.[1]
Their relationship extends beyond the ordinary use of a physical armament. Gol-goroth can temporarily integrate with War's body, interact directly with his supernatural Energy and enable the manifestation of the other three Eldritch Horsemen. The sword also possesses sufficient independent awareness to warn him of unfamiliar presences, allowing it to function as a sentient companion during combat. War's extraordinary martial experience enables him to exploit the weapon's capabilities with precision, while Gol-goroth provides access to supernatural functions unavailable through conventional greatsword techniques. War is also the one who shuts the domain down. That choice is his, made because he judges [[Vaelthar]] stronger.[2][3][6]
War's separate connection to Victor allows the Emperor to restore him if he is killed under the circumstances described during his deployment. This protection originates from the Herald's relationship with his creator and has not been established as an independent resurrection ability possessed by Gol-goroth.[1]

#### Eldritch Horsemen
The Eldritch manifestations of Death, Famine and Pestilence are directly associated with Gol-goroth's principal empowerment. Their appearance occurs when the weapon absorbs War's Energy and opens its enormous eye, enabling the three beings to emerge from the Herald's body. They possess independent supernatural capabilities and are capable of coordinating their attacks against powerful Divine opponents. Inside the later domain, their dormant Eldritch aspects awaken, and the four erase [[Azrakar]] together.[2][3][4]
Although the incarnations correspond to the apocalyptic roles of War's original brothers, their manifestation should not be interpreted as proof that Gol-goroth possesses unrestricted resurrection powers or has restored the deceased Demons' original Souls. The weapon enables the emergence of Eldritch beings associated with those roles, while their individual identities and supernatural constitutions remain distinct from an independently verified restoration of their previous existences.[2]

### Trivia
- Gol-goroth was created directly by [[Victor Elderblood]] from pure purple Dragon Power rather than being forged from an existing physical weapon.[1]
- The weapon's consciousness was awakened through an Eldritch declaration establishing the condition that an Emperor-recognized wielder would become War personified.[1]
- Gol-goroth recognized Victor as Great [[Chaos]] immediately after awakening and affirmed its intention to fulfill the purpose of its existence.[1]
- Its most distinctive visible feature is an enormous supernatural eye that appears during its awakening, the activation of its Eldritch Horseman-related powers, and the raising of the domain.[1][2][4]
- Gol-goroth briefly merged with War's arm when the Herald first accepted it, demonstrating supernatural compatibility between the sword and its wielder.[1]
- Victor referred to Gol-goroth as War's companion, establishing that he intended the weapon to function as more than ordinary military equipment.[1]
- Gol-goroth's chaotic Eldritch War differs from conventional organized warfare and is particularly suitable for a warrior fighting alone against surrounding enemies.[1]
- The weapon can absorb Energy emanating from War and enable the manifestation of Eldritch incarnations corresponding to Famine, Pestilence and Death.[2]
- The three Eldritch Horsemen emerge from War's body rather than directly from the blade, although their manifestation is enabled through Gol-goroth's supernatural activation.[2]
- The appearance of the Eldritch Horsemen does not establish that the original Souls of War's deceased brothers have been resurrected intact.[2]
- Gol-goroth returns to War's hand after being thrown during his confrontation with [[Azrakar]], although the mechanism responsible has not been explained.[2]
- War uses Gol-goroth to redirect a concentrated Void projectile created by Azrakar. The projectile subsequently causes thousands of distant stars to collapse, but the destructive Energy originates from Azrakar's attack.[3]
- The weapon is used to exploit weaknesses within Divine barriers and sever the convergence points supporting an enormous spatial prison.[3]
- Gol-goroth independently detects an unfamiliar presence when the [[Vharzath|Great Darkness]] intervenes and warns War that another powerful Being has entered the battlefield.[3]
- The sword's supernatural perception has not been established as omniscient, and it does not identify the Great Darkness by name during its warning.[3]
- The eye opens again in chapter 1194. The narration calls that opening the reveal of its true purpose: an Eldritch domain that isolates the space.[4]
- Inside the domain, Eldritch beings have the advantage and the laws of the universe cease to apply. The effect is on that isolated space, not a claim over the whole Cosmos.[4]
- Azrakar is erased inside the domain. The erasure is the four Horsemen together. The blade is War's weapon in the beheading and the heart-strike. It is not a separate erasure technique of the sword alone.[4]
- After that death, Gol-goroth breaks [[Vhalor]]'s shadow ward. His realm no longer affects the knights while the domain is up.[5]
- The domain holds the first outside wave. The second starts to break the barrier. War drops the killing stroke, and [[Vaelthar]] comes through the gap.[5]
- Vaelthar's presence strains the domain. War deactivates it himself. The sword is not shown breaking.[6]
- War's ability to return to Victor for restoration after death originates from the Herald's separate connection to the Emperor and is not a confirmed ability granted by Gol-goroth.[1]
- Gol-goroth possesses a distinct Eldritch nature but remains fundamentally different from [[Junketsu]], the sentient weapon associated with Victor's own Blood and Soul.
- No individual other than War has been confirmed as an authorized wielder of Gol-goroth.
- The weapon is still extant after the domain is shut. What it does after that is not shown.[6]
