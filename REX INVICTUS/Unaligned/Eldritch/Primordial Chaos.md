#### Debut
Auxiliary Chapter — Explanations of the novel's mechanics and power rankings. Read if you wanted.
Primordial Chaos, also known as Primal Chaos, [[Chaos]] and the Creator, is the original Creator and complete Rank 0 existence of the native Universe in which the principal events of [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]] take place. Rather than simply being the strongest individual born inside that Reality, Primordial Chaos occupies a fundamentally different cosmological position: a normal Primordial Being incarnates a fundamental Concept or universal function, whereas a complete Primordial Chaos is the incarnation of the Universe itself. The entirety of its native Creation exists within the Reality represented by it.[1][2]
Primordial Chaos established the native Universe and the underlying Rules through which its Reality functions. The later Primordial order does not stand beside the Creator as a collection of equal independent cosmic powers: the System explicitly states that the Authorities presently possessed by the Primordial Beings were originally given to them by Primordial Chaos, including the System's own Authority.[3] The Primordial Beings therefore operate as fundamental administrators and incarnations of specific functions inside a greater cosmological structure whose original supreme position belonged to the Creator.
The complete Creator is no longer active as one unified Being. Later revelations establish that <Positivity> and <Negativity> are not unrelated supreme entities but the two divided halves of the original Primordial Chaos. Its consciousness survived that division, although its present condition is severely damaged and described as semi-conscious. The complete Rank 0 position consequently remains unoccupied even though the Being that once held it has not truly died.[4][5][6]
Chapter 1185 gives the clearest explanation of the Creator's nature. If a Primordial Being of Destruction is the incarnation of Destruction, then Primordial Chaos is the incarnation of the entire Universe. All native Creation is contained within its cosmological existence, and within the realm that it embodies a complete Primordial Chaos is described as omnipotent, omniscient and omnipresent.[2] These descriptions are specifically contextualized by the novel as sovereignty over the Universe represented by that Creator; they should not be interpreted as proof that one Primordial Chaos automatically possesses unrestricted supremacy over every independent Universe, foreign Creator or cosmological system.
Current condition: Primordial Chaos is alive but divided. Its former complete Rank 0 existence survives through the separated foundations of <Positivity> and <Negativity>, while Light and [[Victor Elderblood]] have respectively been selected as their present successors.[4][5][2]

### Physical Appearance
The original complete appearance of Primordial Chaos has never been canonically shown. Existing visual representations are therefore symbolic depictions rather than confirmed portraits of the Creator's unified body.
Primordial Chaos is consistently treated as a conscious individual and referred to with masculine pronouns, but its later classification makes ordinary assumptions concerning anatomy unreliable. A complete Primordial Chaos is not merely a humanoid God occupying physical space; it is the cosmological embodiment of an entire Universe.[2] Humanoid manifestations associated with <Negativity> or <Positivity> consequently should not be treated automatically as the historical physical form of the complete Creator.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s description of the Creator as a Painter is similarly metaphorical. The analogy was introduced while Victor attempted to understand the relationship between the conscious Creator, the undefined foundation beneath differentiated supernatural Energies and the completed Reality constructed from that foundation.[7]

### Personality
Very little is known about Primordial Chaos as a person. For most of the story, the Creator is understood indirectly through the Universe it left behind, the Rules embedded into Reality, the Primordial offices, the System and the testimony of beings old enough to remember the earlier cosmic order.
[[The Universal Tree]] provides the clearest surviving description of its temperament. He remembers that, had Primordial Chaos remained complete, many problems affecting the current Universe could theoretically have been resolved through the Creator's intervention with little difficulty. Despite possessing such overwhelming authority, however, Primordial Chaos apparently was not inclined to interfere with every event merely because it could. The Universal Tree recalls that the Creator preferred to observe events and weigh circumstances before acting.[5]
This behavior is consistent with the structure left behind after the Creator's disappearance. Rather than personally performing every universal function, Primordial Chaos established an ordered Reality containing specialized Primordial administrators, a System responsible for administration and distributed structures capable of maintaining Creation without requiring continuous direct intervention.
Its present mental state is far less clear. Chapter 1176 confirms that the Creator's consciousness survived its division, yet [[True Death]] repeatedly fails to obtain an ordinary response when attempting to address it as "My Creator".[4] The Universal Tree later describes the divided consciousness as semi-conscious and states that its condition is too critical for normal communication.[6] [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] nevertheless appears capable of receiving limited reactions through his connection with <Negativity>; most notably, <Negativity> directly corrected his mistaken conclusion concerning the ancient conflict that caused the Creator's present condition.[2] The exact degree of awareness retained by the original personality remains unknown.

### Nature and Cosmological Position
Primordial Chaos is best understood as a cosmological state of existence, not simply as another stage in an ordinary Divine power hierarchy. Its defining distinction is the scale of Reality with which it is identified.

#### Gods, Primordials and a Primordial Chaos
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later lessons establish a direct distinction between Gods, completed Primordial Beings and a Creator. A God possesses and uses one or more Divine Concepts. A completed Primordial Being goes further and becomes the incarnation of a fundamental Concept or universal function, becoming one of the components through which the native Universe operates. Primordial Chaos exists above that relationship because the object it embodies is not one individual Concept but the Universe containing the entire system.[8][2]

#### The Native Universe
The novel consistently limits the Creator's confirmed jurisdiction to the native Universe. Separate Creations can possess their own Rules, Primordials and corresponding Creator-class existences. This became explicit when [[Azathoth Elderblood]] explained the distinction between independent cosmological systems and the beings produced within them.[9]
This distinction is essential because the word "[[Chaos]]" is used for multiple related but non-identical ideas throughout the story. Native Primordial Chaos is the conscious Creator and universal embodiment responsible for the order of the main Universe. It is not interchangeable with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Eldritch nature, Azathoth's foreign cosmological existence or the ordinary use of "chaos" to describe disorder.
Chapter 1185 further explains why a Universe requires such an existence. According to Victor, a Universe without a complete Primordial Chaos is unprotected.[2] The current native Universe is therefore functioning without its complete supreme cosmological protector even though the lower Primordial mechanisms established beneath that position continue operating.

#### The Present Sector Structure
The fact that Primordial Chaos created the native Universe does not mean every feature of its present political and cosmological arrangement has been confirmed as the Creator's personal design. In Chapter 1186, [[Hinata Elderblood]] asks [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] whether the Creator itself divided the Universe into the modern Low, Middle and High Sectors or whether the surviving Primordial Beings established that structure afterward. Victor explicitly answers that he does not know.[10]
Victor's current hypothesis is that the ancient conflict removed Primordial Chaos from active Creation too early for it to have personally established every part of the modern Sector system. He therefore suspects the surviving Primordials constructed or completed much of the present arrangement after the Creator's division. He further explains that separating developing regions into Low, Middle and High Sectors protects younger civilizations from immediately confronting vastly older powers, but also creates a severe knowledge vacuum that gives Higher-Sector civilizations an enormous informational advantage.[10]
This remains an in-universe deduction, not confirmed history. The native Universe and its foundational Rules are directly attributed to Primordial Chaos, while the authorship of the modern Sector hierarchy remains unresolved.

#### Primordial Chaos and Pantheon "Primordial Chaos"
Chapter 1186 also clarifies that the title "Primordial Chaos" has historically been used at more than one scale. [[Gina Elderblood]] asks whether the original Creator also created the beings that older Pantheons called their own "primordial chaos". [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] answers that the original Creator created the greater Reality from which everything ultimately arose, but probably did not personally create those later Pantheon-level beings because it had already ceased active participation by the time many such Pantheons developed.[10]
Victor attributes the repeated terminology to limited cosmological knowledge. Civilizations and Pantheons that did not know a Creator existed above their own primordial founders naturally used language such as "primordial chaos" for the oldest source within the cosmology they understood. The universal Primordial Chaos discussed on this page is therefore not automatically interchangeable with every mythological or Pantheon-level entity historically given a similar title.[10]
This distinction is especially important when comparing the native Creator to Victor Elderblood or [[Azathoth Elderblood]]. Victor and Azathoth can function as creator-class or "Primordial Chaos"-equivalent existences for their own Eldritch lineages, but that usage describes their position relative to those lineages rather than making them retroactively identical to the original Rank 0 Creator of the native Universe.

#### Creation and the Painter Analogy
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s study of Creation eventually allowed him to understand the original Creator through the analogy of a painter. Ordinary supernatural Energies possess characteristics that determine what they are: Fire behaves as Fire, [[Divine Energy]] possesses Divine characteristics and other forms of Energy follow their respective definitions. Beneath those differentiated expressions, Victor discovered a state of Attributeless Energy lacking a predetermined characteristic.[11]
The analogy distinguishes the Creator from the medium used in Creation. Primordial Chaos is the Painter; the undefined energetic foundation is the material upon which the Painter acts; and the finished Universe is the work produced after characteristics, Rules and definitions are imposed.[7][11] Chapter 1185 explicitly resolves the hierarchy between the two: the foundational Energy is not above the Being that created and defined it. Primordial Chaos created that Energy and presently survives as the divided incarnation of Positivity and Negativity.[2]
This also explains why Victor's artificial galaxy in Chapter 1168, despite containing stars, apparently living cosmic organisms and highly sophisticated internal structures, was still considered an imitation rather than a genuinely autonomous Universe. Its physical and energetic contents were insufficient without the complete conceptual foundations and self-sustaining Rules required for independent Reality.[8]

#### Positivity and Negativity
Main article: Primordial Chaos/Positivity & Negativity
The deepest confirmed structure of native Creation is the duality produced by the Creator's division. <Negativity> principally represents the spiritual, emotional and nonphysical side of existence, while <Positivity> principally represents the material and physical side. [[The Universal Tree]] describes them as two halves of one Being from which everything arose.[5]
Chapter 1185 expands this explanation by establishing that every native existence contains both principles. Even ordinary matter, galaxies and fundamental Divine Concepts such as Death, Destruction, Time and Space contain Positive and Negative aspects. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] uses an approximately equal underlying balance as the general model, while strongly spiritual or emotional Concepts can lean around seventy percent toward Negativity and strongly physical Concepts can lean around seventy percent toward Positivity.[2]
Neither half functions as an entirely isolated Reality. Chapter 1176 explicitly states that removing Negativity would catastrophically damage the Universe because Positivity and Negativity are ultimately portions of one greater foundation rather than self-sufficient cosmologies.[4]

#### Divine-Concept Energy Hierarchy
The Auxiliary mechanics explanation gives a direct hierarchy for how Divine Concepts are supported inside the native cosmology. Primordial Chaos is first; from its division came <Positivity> and <Negativity>; and the Divine Concepts used by Gods ultimately draw their compatible power through these divided foundations and the specialized Primordials beneath them.[1]
This does not mean every God draws Energy directly from the complete consciousness of Primordial Chaos. The hierarchy is distributed. A fear-related Divinity is naturally compatible with the Negative side, while kindness is compatible with the Positive side. More specialized fundamental Concepts are additionally linked to the corresponding Primordial administrators: Death, End and portions of Time are associated with [[True Death]]; Life, Nature and Reincarnation are associated with The Universal Tree; Soul is associated with [[The Judges of The Abyss]]; Infinity and Space are associated with Infinity; and Balance belongs to The Owner of Limbo.[1]
Primordial Chaos therefore stands at the root of the hierarchy without replacing every specialized office. The complete Creator represents the whole universal structure, Positivity and Negativity form the two supreme divided foundations, and the later Primordials distribute and incarnate narrower universal functions.
Chapter 1186 gives the same system a second explanation from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s current understanding. When Stella asks what actually nourishes Divine Concepts, Victor answers that the immediate source is the Universe itself. Concepts exist throughout Creation even when no God has yet claimed them, while different Concepts vary enormously in significance. Fundamental ideas such as Life, Death, Time, Space and Nature possess far greater structural importance than highly specific or culturally derived Concepts, even though the latter can still genuinely exist.[10]
The two explanations are complementary rather than contradictory. The Universe contains and sustains the conceptual possibilities, while its supreme Positive/Negative foundations and specialized Primordial offices provide the deeper cosmological architecture through which those Concepts are expressed and administered.

#### The Primordial Order
The modern [[Regulators of Existence]] are fundamental components of the native Universe, but their administrative sovereignty originated beneath the Creator. During the System's attempt to claim [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]], it stated that every Authority possessed by the Primordial Beings had been given to them by Primordial Chaos and explicitly included its own Authority in that statement.[3]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] consequently interpreted the Primordial order as a deliberately constructed administrative framework. Some Primordials developed broad personalities and highly individual behavior, while others remain much more tightly constrained by the functions they represent. Victor theorized that these differences were consequences of how the Creator designed or limited the administrators responsible for maintaining Reality.[3] The origin of their Authority is directly confirmed; Victor's explanation of exactly why individual Primordials possess different psychological limitations remains his interpretation.
Moira's existence further demonstrates how extraordinary the Creator's position is. The System describes her natural relationship with Reality as unprecedented among beings born after Primordial Chaos: the Universe passively rearranges circumstances in accordance with her genuine wishes despite her not yet possessing a formal Primordial office. Even then, the System states that in governing Authority she has only Primordial Chaos above her.[3]

#### The System
Main article: System
The System is an administrative mechanism associated with the maintenance of the native cosmological order rather than an independent supreme Creator. Its Authority was granted by Primordial Chaos alongside the Authorities belonging to the Primordial Beings.[3]
This relationship remains active even after the Creator's division. In Chapter 1180, the Universal Tree explains that the System still serves the "[[Diablo|Big Guy]]" and that Primordial Chaos is not sufficiently absent to have relinquished its Authority completely. Death, Infinity and the other Primordials therefore cannot simply seize unrestricted control of the System when they disagree with its decisions.[5]
The continuing hierarchy is especially significant because the Universal Tree describes the established Primordial Beings as the greatest native beings in the Cosmos while immediately qualifying that statement: apart from the Big Guy, no one is above them.[5] The Creator's sovereignty consequently remains recognized by the machinery of Reality even while its unified form is absent.

#### Authority Over Cosmological Rules
Primordial Chaos' supreme position is not limited to possessing greater Energy than the specialized Primordials. The Creator is associated with the Rules defining how the native Reality is permitted to function. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explains in Chapter 1185 that the current restriction allowing only one complete Primordial Being for a given universal Concept is itself a rule rather than an absolutely immutable truth: if the Creator wished, that rule could be changed.[2]
This provides one of the clearest examples of the qualitative difference between a Primordial administrator and complete Rank 0. A Primordial embodies and administers a function within the existing cosmological architecture; the complete Creator possesses authority over the architecture that determines how those offices can exist in the first place.
The statement should remain contextualized to the native Universe. It demonstrates Creator-level Rule/Law alteration over the cosmology it embodies, not unrestricted authority over the independent Rules belonging to foreign Universes.

#### The World Tree Network
Main article: [[World Trees]]
The World Tree network is explicitly part of the architecture created by Primordial Chaos. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] questioned why individual worlds required World Trees despite the Universal Tree already incarnating Life throughout the Cosmos, the Universal Tree told him not to question the Creator's creation.[5]
Individual World Trees function as distributed nodes through which the Universal Tree's existence and influence extend into living worlds. Each node belongs to a greater interconnected structure, and when one disappears another can eventually arise to continue the planetary function. The arrangement allows the universal incarnation of Life to operate throughout an incomprehensibly large Cosmos without physically manifesting as one localized body on every inhabited planet.[5]
The network appears to possess an additional defensive value. While reconstructing the logic of the Primordial order, Victor reasoned that World Trees, Soul administration, Death, Infinity and Balance collectively create several overlapping systems capable of noticing foreign abnormalities entering native Reality.[6] The Universal Tree's own thoughts support the World Tree network having a detection function, but Victor's larger reconstruction of the entire Primordial structure as a coordinated anti-intrusion defense has not yet been fully confirmed.

#### The Akashic Records
Main article: [[Akashic Records]]
The exact chronological relationship between Primordial Chaos and the Akashic Records remains one of the few fundamental creation questions that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] cannot yet answer. In Chapter 1186, [[Stella Elderblood]] directly asks whether Akashic is older or younger than the Creator. Victor admits that he does not know.[10]
He nevertheless considers one explanation overwhelmingly logical. Because Positivity and Negativity originated from the Creator and the native Universe itself ultimately arose from that same source, Victor reasons that the Akashic Records most likely also originated through Primordial Chaos, in the same broad manner as the other foundational cosmic structures.[10]
Victor immediately qualifies this conclusion: it is a deduction, not confirmed knowledge. Akashic should therefore not currently be listed as a definitively proven creation of Primordial Chaos in the same manner as the native Universe or the Authorities directly stated by the System to have come from the Creator. The relationship is presently best indexed as probable creation / unconfirmed origin.[10]
The hierarchy implied by Victor's reasoning is nevertheless significant. If everything native ultimately originates from Primordial Chaos, then Akashic would exist within the Creator's cosmological framework rather than above the complete Rank 0 existence. This remains consistent with the distinction between the Creator as the embodiment of the whole Universe and Akashic as the sentient Record preserving the information of that Universe.

### The Ancient Division
The exact event that reduced Primordial Chaos from a complete Rank 0 Creator into <Positivity> and <Negativity> remains one of the major unresolved events in the cosmology.
What is confirmed is that Primordial Chaos originally existed as one conscious Primordial Being.[6] At some point it was split into the two halves now known as Positivity and Negativity. Chapter 1176 states that the Creator is not dead and that its consciousness survived the division, while Chapter 1185 states that the separation occurred for reasons connected to keeping the Universe stable.[4][2] The precise chronological sequence between the original creation of the Universe, the ancient conflict and the final division has not yet been fully explained.
[[The Universal Tree]]'s comments make clear that the division was not an ordinary voluntary retirement. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] asked how an existence described as all-powerful, omnipresent and omniscient within its own Universe could have reached such a condition, the Universal Tree compared the answer to his own need for a protector while young.[6] Victor immediately inferred the involvement of an enemy and proposed several possibilities, including another Primordial Chaos from an independent Universe, a Creator operating under different Rules or a larger Universe attempting to consume a smaller one. The Universal Tree refused to confirm the details, but his reaction strongly indicated that Victor had approached information he was deliberately withholding.[6]
[[Jeanne Elderblood]] could not provide the missing history. She explained that she had been created to protect the Universal Tree and that Primordial Chaos never appeared before her again after giving her that purpose.[6] Victor subsequently manifested his connection with <Negativity> and attempted to question the divided Creator directly. No conventional verbal answer appeared, and the Universal Tree described the Creator's condition as too critical for normal communication.[6]
Chapter 1185 provides the strongest correction to the assumption that Primordial Chaos was simply defeated. Victor initially concluded that the native Creator must have lost the ancient confrontation. <Negativity> immediately reacted and communicated that this conclusion was wrong: the enemy had been left in an even worse condition than Primordial Chaos.[2] The ancient event should therefore not be summarized as a straightforward defeat. The identity, origin, nature and current state of the opposing existence remain unknown.
Confirmed outcome of the ancient event: the native Creator survived, its consciousness remains present, the unified Rank 0 state was lost, Positivity and Negativity became its two principal halves, and whatever opposed it was left in a condition even worse than its own.[4][6][2]

### Foreign Universes and Other Creators
Primordial Chaos' supremacy is cosmologically contextual. Chapter 1075 establishes that independent Creations can possess their own Rules, fundamental beings and corresponding [[Chaos]] structures.[9] Native Primordial Chaos is therefore the Creator of the main Universe, not the universally confirmed Creator of every possible Reality.
This distinction becomes especially important in relation to [[Azathoth Elderblood]]. Azathoth represents a foreign Eldritch Creator-class existence rather than a native descendant or subordinate of Primordial Chaos. The existence of foreign Creators demonstrates that the highest native cosmological position can have counterparts belonging to independent universal systems.

#### Jurisdiction of the Creator's Rules
The Rules established by native Primordial Chaos govern the Reality belonging to its Universe, but the story provides a direct example of their jurisdiction ending. Azathoth reveals that her true body was moved into a dimension outside the native Universe, where the normal Rules of their Creator do not apply.[4]
The limitation does not diminish Primordial Chaos' position inside its own Creation. Instead, it establishes that separate complete cosmological systems are not simply subordinate territories inside one universal rule set.

#### Conflict Between Primordial Chaoses
Chapter 1185 describes a confrontation between two complete Primordial Chaoses as something fundamentally different from an ordinary battle. Because each Creator embodies a Universe, such a conflict is effectively a collision between the embodiments of two independent universal Realities.[2]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly states that familiar measurements such as conventional power levels, martial skill or ordinary tactical advantages cease to provide a meaningful way of evaluating a conflict at that scale. Even with his accumulated knowledge, he does not yet know the actual prerequisites by which one complete Primordial Chaos defeats another.[2]
The ancient condition of the native Creator demonstrates that such conflicts are nevertheless possible. What occurred during that forgotten confrontation, and what ultimately determines victory between universal embodiments, remain unrevealed.

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
The existence of the higher universal administration first became directly relevant to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] when his evolution began producing consequences beyond the political systems of ordinary Pantheons. His transformation into the Primogenitor of the Draconic Vampires attracted the attention of the [[Regulators of Existence]], establishing that the Cosmos possessed beings whose responsibilities extended to fundamental functions of Reality rather than individual civilizations.[14]
The greater connection appeared during Victor's ascension to Godhood. Rather than merely developing another ordinary Divinity, Victor was accepted by <Negativity>, one of the supreme foundations later revealed to be half of Primordial Chaos.[15] At the time neither Victor nor those around him understood the full implication. What appeared to be an extraordinarily high Divine Concept was actually a direct connection to one half of the Authority once unified at Rank 0.
The same period made clear that supreme Concepts could possess [[Will Solace|Will]] rather than functioning as completely inert powers. Victor had not manufactured Negativity through ordinary Divine advancement; the Concept had accepted him. The true reason would only become explicit hundreds of chapters later when the divided Creator's surviving consciousness and succession mechanism were finally revealed.[4]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ascension as the Dragon God of [[Chaos]], the distinction between his own increasingly Eldritch existence and the native cosmological order became progressively more important. His vision of the incomprehensible entity existing within the deepest portion of himself established that his developing True Self could not be reduced to an ordinary native God or Dragon.[16]
This distinction prevented later uses of the word "Chaos" from being treated as interchangeable. Victor's Eldritch nature originated from a path increasingly foreign to the normal Rules of the native Universe, whereas Primordial Chaos was the conscious Creator that established those Rules. The difference became essential once Victor began encountering information concerning independent cosmologies and foreign Creator-class beings.

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
The most substantial early explanation arrived after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s civilization expanded beyond its original planetary framework. Through Azathoth, he learned that separate Creations could possess different laws, Primordial structures and corresponding [[Chaos]] existences.[9] During the same explanation, the System revealed an Authority hierarchy with an unidentified Rank 0 above <Negativity> and <Positivity>, followed by the specialized Primordial functions of Infinity, Death, Life, Soul and Balance.
This knowledge changed how Victor understood the [[Regulators of Existence]]. A true Primordial was not simply an exceptionally powerful God; it was an incarnation of a function whose complete removal without replacement could cripple the Universe depending upon it.[9] The question of who or what occupied the unidentified position above those administrators consequently became much more important than an ordinary question of combat strength.
Victor's research into the foundations of Energy later produced another piece of the puzzle. While studying the principles eventually identified as Attributeless Energy, he realized that differentiated supernatural Energies could be understood as an undefined foundation upon which characteristics had been imposed. This led to the Painter analogy: the medium itself was not the Creator; the Creator was the conscious Being capable of defining and organizing that medium into a functioning Reality.[7][11]
The Creator's personal connection to [[Jeanne Elderblood]] was also uncovered. Primordial Chaos had directly intervened in Jeanne's existence and assigned her the purpose of protecting The Universal Tree.[17] Jeanne would later explain that the Creator never appeared before her again after giving her that role.[6]
Victor's meeting with Balance finally established that the current condition of the Universe was abnormal. The Cosmos was functioning without a complete Primordial Chaos, while the simultaneous existence of representatives connected to the divided supreme foundations suggested the possibility that a new complete universal leader might eventually arise.[13] Balance also explained the Creator's place in the final cosmic cycle: Death concludes Creation and gathers what it accumulated; a complete Primordial Chaos provides the beginning from which another Creation can arise.

#### Those Who Rule Reality
By the Imperial era, the scattered clues surrounding Rank 0 began converging into a coherent model. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s encounter with a foreign Upper-Sector operative in Chapter 1158 reinforced the political importance of <Negativity>, <Positivity> and the vacant supreme position. The foreign entity recognized Victor as an emerging force connected to <[[Chaos]]>, while external powers such as <Light> were already interested in the Authority developing around him.[18]
During his lessons to his daughters, Victor demonstrated the distinction between simulating a Cosmos and establishing a truly independent Reality. His artificial galaxy contained stars and convincing imitations of cosmic Life yet lacked the complete conceptual foundation required to exist autonomously. He explained that a genuine Universe requires sufficiently deep Rules and a supreme Creator capable of establishing them—a Primordial Chaos.[8]
The same lesson produced Victor's "great machine" analogy. Divine Concepts were compared to the deepest instructions defining Reality, while completed Primordial Beings were essential components responsible for universal functions. Acquiring the supreme administrative position would be fundamentally different from individually overpowering every component because complete Rank 0 represents Authority over the larger structure in which those components operate.[8]
Victor also proposed that <Negativity> and <Positivity> retained remnants of the Creator's consciousness and selected compatible successors themselves. At that point he treated the conclusion as a theory.[8] The theory was soon vindicated.
When the System attempted to claim [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]] as a new Primordial force, it revealed that no being born after Primordial Chaos had naturally possessed governing Authority comparable to hers and declared that only Primordial Chaos stood above Moira's extraordinary relationship with the Universe.[3] More importantly, the System admitted that the Authorities of the existing Primordials—including its own—had originally been granted by the Creator.
Victor refused to surrender his daughter. The resulting confrontation with Death, Infinity, the Universal Tree, the Judges of the Abyss and Balance's Primordial exposed the true significance of his connection to Negativity. The narration states that Victor possessed half the Authority of Rank 0 as its chosen successor, while the destruction or corruption of Negativity would damage approximately half of the universal foundation because every part of Creation contains it.[4]
During the confrontation Primordial Chaos was explicitly identified as the only Divine Concept transcending Negativity. Death attempted to contact the Creator and received no ordinary answer, after which the narration clarified the critical fact that Primordial Chaos was not dead: it had divided, and its consciousness continued to exist.[4] The inability of ordinary beings to simply obtain Positivity or Negativity was a consequence of that surviving [[Will Solace|Will]]. Negativity had chosen Victor, while Positivity had chosen Light.
Chapter 1180 subsequently established that the old sovereignty had not vanished. The System still served the original Creator, whose incomplete condition had not caused it to relinquish enough Authority for the other Primordials to assume unrestricted control.[5] The Universal Tree also provided the most concise explanation of the two supreme halves: Negativity represented the spiritual side of Creation, Positivity its material counterpart and together they were the two halves of one Being from which everything in the Universe arose.
The Universal Tree then revealed more of the original personality behind that structure. He remembered Primordial Chaos as a Being capable of resolving enormous universal problems with a thought while nevertheless preferring to observe and deliberate rather than constantly interfere.[5]
In Chapter 1181, the Universal Tree finally stated directly that Negativity and Positivity were originally one conscious Primordial Being which had been split into two halves for specific reasons.[6] Victor immediately deduced that an external enemy was probably involved and reconstructed much of the Primordial architecture as a defensive system against possible intrusions from beyond the Universe. The Universal Tree refused to reveal the ancient history but reacted strongly enough to make clear that Victor had approached information he was deliberately withholding.
The decisive explanation came in Chapter 1185. Victor taught his daughters that the Being which created the foundational Energy used in Creation was presently incarnated through Positivity and Negativity, and that the two halves stood above the Energy precisely because they were the divided state of the Creator that had produced it.[2] He further explained that every part of native Reality contains both halves and that complete Primordial Chaos is to the Universe what a normal Primordial Being is to its Concept: its incarnation.
Victor then explicitly described complete Primordial Chaos as the embodiment containing all native Creation and as omnipotent, omniscient and omnipresent within its corresponding universal realm.[2] A Universe without such an existence is unprotected. When Victor assumed the native Creator had simply lost the ancient confrontation that caused its division, Negativity immediately corrected him by revealing that the unknown enemy had suffered an even worse fate. The ancient conflict therefore remains unresolved rather than representing a confirmed defeat of the original Creator.
Chapter 1186 resolves several terminology questions while opening new historical ones. Stella asks whether the [[Akashic Records]] predate the Creator; Victor admits that he does not know and only theorizes that Primordial Chaos probably created Akashic because the Records exist inside the greater Reality originating from the Creator.[10] Hinata then asks whether Primordial Chaos personally divided the modern Universe into Low, Middle and High Sectors. Victor again admits uncertainty and suspects that the surviving Primordials completed the present Sector structure after the ancient conflict removed the Creator from active Creation.[10]
Gina's question further distinguishes the universal Creator from the many historical entities that Pantheons called their own "primordial chaos". Victor explains that such terminology emerged from incomplete knowledge: many civilizations knew their local primordial founders without understanding that a greater Creator existed above the entire native cosmic structure. The original Primordial Chaos therefore remains the singular complete Rank 0 Creator of the native Universe rather than merely another Pantheon founder.[10]

#### Powers and Abilities

##### Creator Physiology
- Universal Embodiment: Primordial Chaos is the living incarnation of the native Universe rather than merely its ruler. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explains the relationship by comparing a completed Primordial Being with its Concept: just as a Primordial of Destruction would be Destruction itself, Primordial Chaos is the corresponding embodiment of the entire Universe. Every native Concept, Energy, cosmic mechanism and layer of Creation therefore exists inside the greater Reality represented by him.[5]
- Abstract Existence: Primordial Chaos does not depend upon an ordinary physical body to exist. His complete state represents an entire cosmological structure, while his present incomplete existence survives through the abstract universal principles of <Positivity> and <Negativity>. Even after the unified Creator was divided, his consciousness continued existing through these two cosmic Wills.[2][3][4]
- Conceptual Embodiment: Primordial Chaos simultaneously represents the complete state from which <Positivity> and <Negativity> originate. The narration explicitly identifies Primordial Chaos as the only Divine Concept transcending Negativity, establishing that the complete Creator exists conceptually above either individual Rank 1 half.[2]
- Distributed Existence: The current Creator exists through Positivity and Negativity, which permeate native Creation. Chapter 1185 explains that everything from the smallest particles to enormous galaxies contains both Positive and Negative components; consequently, even while divided, the Creator's present existence is distributed throughout the Universe rather than localized in one conventional vessel.[2][3][5]
- [[Immortality]]: The loss of Primordial Chaos' original unified state did not destroy him. His consciousness survived an ancient Creator-class conflict severe enough to leave him divided into two universal principles. The System still recognizes his surviving sovereignty, demonstrating that the current Creator is damaged and incomplete rather than dead.[2][3]
- Agelessness: Primordial Chaos existed before the present universal order and created the framework in which ordinary biological Life later developed. His existence is cosmological and conceptual rather than biological, so the normal aging process of mortal organisms does not apply to him.[1][4]
- Pre-Creation Existence: Primordial Chaos predates the completed Universe, the modern Primordial order and the Rules he established for native Creation. The Creator therefore did not originate as an ordinary product of the cosmology whose structure he later created.[1][10]
- Consciousness Persistence: The destruction of the Creator's unified state did not erase the mind associated with it. Chapter 1176 explicitly establishes that Primordial Chaos' consciousness continued living after the division into Positivity and Negativity, explaining why the two supreme halves can still display [[Will Solace|Will]] and select successors.[2]
- Persistent Authority: Primordial Chaos' sovereignty continues to be recognized even while the Creator remains divided. The System still serves the original "[[Diablo|Big Guy]]", and the existing Primordials cannot simply take unrestricted control of the System because the higher Creator Authority has not been relinquished.[3]
- Self-Division: Primordial Chaos separated his complete existence into the two supreme halves known as <Positivity> and <Negativity>. The division preserved the underlying Creator-consciousness while allowing the two halves to function as semi-conscious universal principles. Chapter 1185 states that this division occurred for reasons connected with preserving the stability of the Universe.[2][3][5]

##### Omnipotence
- Omnipotence: Complete Primordial Chaos is explicitly stated to be omnipotent within the Universe he embodies. This is not merely a statement that he possesses greater raw power than ordinary Primordials; the Universe itself is his realm and all native Creation exists within his greater existence. His complete Authority therefore represents the highest possible native control over the Reality he created.[5]
- Absolute Native Authority: Rank 0 represents the complete administrative Authority of the native Universe. The System places Positivity and Negativity at Rank 1 beneath an otherwise unidentified Rank 0, while later chapters reveal that the two Rank 1 Authorities are the divided halves of Primordial Chaos. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s inheritance of Negativity consequently grants him half of Rank 0, confirming that the completed Creator possesses the entire supreme administrative structure.[1][2]
- Universal Reality Control: Complete Primordial Chaos can directly change the Reality he created rather than being restricted to manipulating individual phenomena inside it. When Victor explains the structure of Primordial Concepts to his daughters, he states that even restrictions governing the existence of Primordial Beings could simply be changed if the Creator decided to alter the Rules.[9][5]
- Universal Correction: [[The Universal Tree]] remembers that if Primordial Chaos had remained complete, many of the modern problems affecting the Universe could have been solved by him with a single thought. This demonstrates the difference between Rank 0 Authority and the specialized Primordials, who must individually operate through their respective functions.[3]

##### Omniscience
- Omniscience: Primordial Chaos is explicitly described as omniscient within the native Universe. Because the Universe constitutes his own cosmological existence, the complete Creator possesses awareness of the Reality, Concepts and cosmic structures existing within that realm.[5]
- Universal Awareness: The completed Creator's awareness extends throughout the native material and spiritual structure rather than being restricted to a single planet, dimension or Divine Domain. Positivity represents the material side of Creation while Negativity represents its spiritual and nonphysical counterpart, and complete Primordial Chaos contains both.[3][5]
- Conceptual Awareness: Primordial Chaos possesses awareness of the Concepts forming his Universe. Chapter 1168 describes Divine Concepts as fundamental pieces of Reality's deepest structure and completed Primordials as functional components within the machine established by Primordial Chaos. As the Creator and complete administrator of that structure, Rank 0 possesses the corresponding supreme understanding of its native conceptual framework.[9]
- Cosmic-System Awareness: Life, Death, Infinity, Soul, Balance, the System and the Positive-Negative duality all operate within the Universe created by Primordial Chaos. The System's Authority and the governing Authorities of the Primordials were themselves granted by the Creator, placing their administrative information beneath the complete Rank 0 structure.[8]

##### Omnipresence
- Omnipresence: Complete Primordial Chaos is explicitly stated to be omnipresent throughout his own Universe. The statement is explained through his status as the embodiment of the Universe itself rather than through conventional teleportation or speed.[5]
- Universal Presence: The Creator's complete existence is inseparable from the Reality he embodies. This means his presence is not restricted to one physical body occupying one coordinate inside Space-Time; native Creation exists within the cosmological existence represented by Primordial Chaos.[5]
- Conceptual Omnipresence: The Creator's divided halves remain present throughout native Reality. Negativity permeates Creation at every level, while Positivity does the same for the complementary half. Chapter 1176 establishes that damaging Negativity would affect everything from microscopic structures to stars, Souls, galaxies and universal mechanisms because the Concept exists throughout the Universe.[2]
- Material and Spiritual Omnipresence: Chapter 1180 defines Positivity as principally representing the material side of Creation and Negativity as the spiritual side. Chapter 1185 then clarifies that every native phenomenon contains both, allowing the complete Creator to encompass both physical and nonphysical existence simultaneously.[3][5]

##### Creation
- Universe Creation: Primordial Chaos created the native Universe and the conceptual framework required for it to exist as a genuine autonomous Reality. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] demonstrates the difference in Chapter 1168 when he produces an extremely convincing artificial galaxy containing stars and apparently living organisms but admits that it is not a true independent Universe because it lacks the complete network of conceptual Rules that only a Creator-level existence can establish.[9]
- Reality Creation: Primordial Chaos can create a complete Reality rather than merely produce objects inside an existing one. A true Universe requires physical structure, Energy, Space, metaphysical laws, Divine Concepts and the interconnected systems that allow those components to function without borrowing the Rules of a larger Reality.[9]
- Matter Creation: Material existence forms part of the Positive side of Creation, while Primordial Chaos contains complete Positivity in his unified state. His act of universal Creation necessarily produced the physical substance from which planets, stars, galaxies and ordinary material structures developed.[3][5]
- Energy Creation: Primordial Chaos is associated with the creation of the foundational Attributeless Energy used to bring differentiated existence forth. Victor specifically corrects the idea that this Energy could stand above the Creator by pointing out that Primordial Chaos was the Being who created it in the first place.[11][5]
- Dimensional Creation: Dimensions exist as subordinate structures inside the greater Universe created by Primordial Chaos. Chapter 1168 explains that a normal dimension can borrow its deeper Rules from the parent Universe, while constructing a truly independent Reality requires the full conceptual framework belonging to Creator-level Creation.[9]
- Concept Creation: Primordial Chaos established the conceptual foundations underlying native Reality. Divine Concepts are described as the deepest instructions through which the Universe functions, and the original Creator built the system containing ordinary Divinities, supreme Concepts and the completed Primordial offices.[9][8]
- Law Creation: The Rules governing the native Universe originate from Primordial Chaos. Victor describes the original Creator's system as deceptively simple precisely because its Rules are not superficial magical restrictions—they are definitions built directly into how Reality itself works.[9]
- Cosmic Infrastructure Creation: Primordial Chaos created the administrative architecture through which the Universe continues functioning. This includes the Primordial offices, the Authorities governing those offices and the System's own administrative Authority.[8]
- World Tree Network Creation: The distributed World Tree system forms part of the Creator's design. When Victor questions why individual planets require [[World Trees]] despite the Universal Tree already embodying Life, the Universal Tree explicitly tells him not to question the Creator's creation. The countless planetary World Trees act as nodes through which Life's influence extends across the Universe.[3]

##### Reality Warping
- Reality Warping: As the Being who created and embodies the native Universe, Primordial Chaos can alter native Reality at its foundational level rather than merely applying temporary supernatural effects to it. The existence of Rank 0 represents administrative control over the system from which lower Reality-manipulating powers operate.[9][5]
- Reality Restructuring: Primordial Chaos can alter the structure of his Universe because its laws and conceptual Rules originate from his own Creator Authority. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly uses the possibility of changing the one-Primordial-per-Concept limitation as an example of something the Creator could rewrite.[5]
- Reality Stabilization: Chapter 1185 connects the ancient division of Primordial Chaos into Positivity and Negativity with maintaining universal stability. The two halves now permeate native Reality as complementary foundations, demonstrating that the Creator can restructure his own existence in a manner that affects the stability of the Universe itself.[5]
- Reality Restoration: [[The Universal Tree]]'s statement that complete Primordial Chaos could resolve major universal problems through a thought establishes that Rank 0 possesses corrective control over damaged or malfunctioning portions of native Reality far beyond ordinary restorative abilities.[3]
- Reality Definition: Primordial Chaos did not merely reshape an already completed Reality; the Creator assigned the fundamental characteristics from which differentiated Energy, matter and the ordered native Universe emerged. The Painter analogy treats Attributeless Energy as the undefined medium and Primordial Chaos as the intelligence that gave that medium its properties.[11][10]
- Fundamental Property Manipulation: Because the Creator formed differentiated existence by imposing characteristics upon an undefined foundation, Rank 0 operates at the level at which properties defining native phenomena are assigned rather than being limited to manipulating the completed phenomena afterward.[11][6]

##### Conceptual Manipulation
- Conceptual Manipulation: Primordial Chaos possesses supreme Authority over the native conceptual architecture. Divine Concepts function as the deepest operating instructions of the Universe, while completed Primordial Beings incarnate fundamental Concepts as functional components. Primordial Chaos created the greater system in which those Concepts exist and granted the Authorities through which its administrators govern them.[9][8]
- Concept Creation: The Creator can establish new conceptual structures as part of universal Creation. A true independent Reality requires its own Conceptual foundation rather than merely copying the appearance of matter and living beings.[9]
- Concept Definition: Primordial Chaos determines how fundamental native Concepts function inside his Universe. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s explanation that the Creator could alter the rule governing how many Primordial embodiments a Concept may possess demonstrates control over the definitions and restrictions imposed upon those Concepts.[5]
- Concept Modification: Because the Creator can change the Rules governing Primordial Concepts, he possesses the Authority necessary to modify how native conceptual systems behave rather than being permanently bound by the structure he originally established.[5]
- Conceptual Hierarchy Manipulation: The native cosmology contains ordinary Divine Concepts, higher Concepts, specialized Primordial embodiments, Positivity and Negativity at Rank 1 and complete Primordial Chaos at Rank 0. The Creator's Authority stands above this hierarchy and is responsible for the administrative structure beneath it.[1][8][2]
- Conceptual Authority Bestowal: Primordial Chaos granted the governing Authorities possessed by the native Primordial Beings. These are not independent powers that developed above him; the System explicitly states that every Authority held by the Primordials was originally given to them by the Creator.[8]
- Conceptual Destruction: The destruction of a sufficiently fundamental Concept can damage the Reality built around it. Victor demonstrates this principle through Negativity when he threatens to destroy the Concept itself rather than fight the assembled Primordials directly. Because Negativity forms one half of the Creator's divided structure, its destruction would devastate approximately half the Universe and destabilize the rest.[2]

##### Law Manipulation
- Law Manipulation: Primordial Chaos possesses control over the fundamental Rules defining his Universe. These Rules determine how native Concepts, Primordial offices and cosmological functions behave and are integrated directly into Reality rather than functioning as ordinary supernatural restrictions.[9]
- Law Creation: The original Creator established the fundamental native Rules when creating the Universe. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] repeatedly refers to the structure as the predecessor's system and acknowledges how difficult its principles are to circumvent because they constitute the basic operating conditions of Reality itself.[9]
- Law Modification: Complete Rank 0 can change fundamental universal Rules. Victor explicitly explains that if the Creator wished to permit multiple completed Primordial embodiments of a Concept, the current restriction could be changed.[5]
- Law Replacement: The Creator's ability to rewrite the operating Rules of the native cosmology means an existing metaphysical restriction is not absolute in relation to Rank 0; it remains valid only while the Creator's system continues defining Reality that way.[5]
- Reality Law Administration: The specialized Primordial administrators operate within the Rule structure established by Primordial Chaos. Their Authorities let them administer individual mechanisms, whereas Rank 0 governs the framework that determines what those mechanisms are permitted to do.[9][8]

##### Energy Manipulation
- Attributeless Energy Manipulation: Primordial Chaos possesses complete mastery of the undefined energetic foundation associated with Creation. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] describes ordinary Energies as differentiated states produced by assigning characteristics to a deeper Attributeless medium and connects full mastery of this foundation with the capability required to become a Primordial Chaos capable of creating an entire Universe.[11][7]
- Energy Generation: Creator's Energy can be produced as an undefined energetic state before any specialized characteristics are imposed upon it. [[Scathach]]'s later training confirms that Attributeless Energy itself can be generated independently rather than existing only as a theoretical substrate.[6]
- Energy Characterization / Property Imposition: The blank energetic medium can be assigned characteristics that determine what type of Energy it becomes. Fire is Fire because the corresponding properties are imposed upon the Energy; the same principle applies to Divine, Demonic, Primordial and other differentiated Energies.[11][6]
- Energy Transmutation: Changing the characteristics imposed upon Energy changes what that Energy becomes. Victor and Charlotte's discoveries demonstrate that apparently incompatible supernatural Energies share a deeper common substrate rather than existing as completely disconnected substances.[11][6]
- Energy Conversion / De-Characterization: Differentiated Energy can be stripped of the characteristics defining its specialized state and returned toward its underlying Attributeless condition.[6]
- Specialized Energy Generation: By applying the appropriate characteristics, the foundational medium can reproduce differentiated native Energies. [[Primordial Energy]] is directly demonstrated as one possible characterized state, while Chapter 1185 identifies [[Divine Energy]], Demonic Energy, Spirit Energy, Mana, Ki and other supernatural Energies as expressions of the same deeper Energy principle.[6][5]
- Primordial Energy Generation: Creator's Energy can specifically be given the characteristics of Primordial Energy, establishing that Primordial Energy is not identical to the Attributeless state from which the Creator worked.[6]
- Energy Amplification: Small quantities of Attributeless Energy can be incorporated into existing techniques to produce effects far beyond what the same amount of ordinary specialized Energy would achieve. Victor demonstrates the principle while teaching Scathach, greatly magnifying an Ice technique through only a minute quantity of Creator's Energy.[7]
- Energy Efficiency Enhancement: Creator's Energy can serve as a superior foundation for an existing supernatural technique, allowing substantially greater results from much smaller conventional Energy expenditure.[7]
- Extreme Energy Control: Manipulating Attributeless Energy safely requires control exceeding ordinary Grandmaster standards. Victor explicitly connects complete Master-level compatibility with this medium to the state of a genuine Primordial Chaos capable of creating a Universe.[7]
- Primordial Energy Manipulation: Primordial Energy remains a defined Energy despite its extreme quality and can therefore be understood as another characterized state derived from the deeper Attributeless foundation. Chapter 1185 distinguishes the functions directly: Attributeless Energy brings things into existence, while Primordial Energy makes established Creation function.[6][5]
- Positive Energy Manipulation: Complete Primordial Chaos contains the full Positive half of his original existence. Positive Energy is the energetic expression of the material and physical side of Creation, and Positivity's Rank 1 Authority constitutes one half of Rank 0.[1][2][3]
- Negative Energy Manipulation: Complete Primordial Chaos likewise contains the full Negative half. Negative Energy permeates native Creation and is especially prominent within spiritual, emotional and abstract phenomena. Victor's inheritance demonstrates that even an incomplete successor can draw enough of this universal Energy to threaten the entire cosmological structure.[12][2][3]

##### Return to Origin
- Existence Manipulation — Return to Origin: Attributeless Energy can return a differentiated target toward the undefined source-state preceding its present characteristics. This is not ordinary destruction or END: END concludes an existence, whereas Return to Origin regresses the definitions that make the affected phenomenon what it presently is.[6]
- Existence Deconstruction: Creator's Energy can strip away the characteristics defining matter or Energy until the affected structure is no longer properly recognized in its former state.[6]
- Property Removal: Defining characteristics can be removed from an affected phenomenon rather than merely damaged, changing what Reality recognizes that phenomenon to be.[6]
- Energy Regression: Specialized Energy can be reduced toward the Attributeless condition preceding its differentiated identity.[6]
- Matter Regression: Physical structures can lose the characteristics defining their material state and regress toward the underlying energetic foundation from which differentiated matter was produced.[6]
- Pre-Existence Regression: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] describes the process as returning something toward its "Beginning", reducing the target toward a condition preceding the definition of its current created form.[6]
- Record Manipulation: When [[Scathach]] was injured by improperly controlled Attributeless Energy, the missing portion of her body was no longer correctly preserved in the information/Record identifying her original state. Life, Creation, Dream and Nightmare-based restoration consequently failed to reproduce the missing tissue normally.[6]
- Soul-Record Manipulation: Victor warns that a sufficiently severe failure with Creator's Energy could affect the Record of the Soul itself. Damage at that level could cause even a later resurrection to reconstruct an incomplete version of the affected person because part of the information defining them would no longer be correctly present.[6]
- Regeneration Negation: Ordinary regeneration can fail against Return to Origin because there may no longer be a correctly defined damaged structure for regeneration to reconstruct. The problem is not simply that the wound is severe—the underlying characteristics required to identify the original form have been stripped away.[6]
- Restoration Negation: Multiple high-level restorative methods failed to naturally restore the portion of Scathach affected by Creator's Energy because the missing part was no longer properly defined within her existing Record.[6]

Benevolent emotion still sits on the spiritual side, because the classification follows the kind of existence, not the ethics.[1][2]
The distinction is also not absolute. Everything native to the Universe contains both Positivity and Negativity: matter, Souls, galaxies and even fundamental Concepts such as Death, Destruction, Time and Space contain the two principles in varying proportions. They cannot function as two isolated Universes because they are ultimately the separated components of the same greater totality.[4][2]

Before Positivity and Negativity existed separately, both formed the complete existence known as Primordial Chaos, the Creator and cosmological embodiment of the native Universe. They are not descendants created by Primordial Chaos, and they are not two unrelated Primordial Beings set beneath him. They are the surviving halves of the Creator himself.[5][6][2]
The full cause of the division is not given. Primordial Chaos did not die. #### Nature

##### The Universal Duality
Positivity and Negativity are the broadest native dualistic principles beneath complete Primordial Chaos. They exist throughout Creation rather than being localized Divine Domains maintained in one location. Both Energies are distributed everywhere throughout the Universe, and the two Wills interact with an immense amount of universal information through them.[1]
Their relationship is complementary rather than competitive. Neither side constitutes complete Reality independently. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] threatened Negativity itself, the assembled Primordials treated the threat as universal. Eliminating or catastrophically damaging the Negative half would not leave a functioning Positive Universe. Because Positivity and Negativity are two components of a single greater structure, removing one would throw the entire system into universal disorder.[4]

##### Not Good and Evil
The terminology used for the duality can be misleading. Positive does not mean morally Good, while Negative does not mean morally Evil.
Negativity does not make its representative evil. It is the other side of a cosmic scale, with different functions.[1] [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] places spiritual and emotional phenomena, including emotions which can be benevolent, toward Negativity, because the determining factor is their nonphysical nature rather than moral alignment.[2]
A cruel physical act can still occur through material systems dominated by Positivity, while kindness, love, emotion or spiritual phenomena can possess a stronger Negative composition. The terms describe metaphysical characteristics, not ethics.

#### Development of the Duality in the Story

##### Demonic War
When a God dies and the Divine Concept leaves its holder, that Divinity returns to the primordial framework of Positivity and Negativity.[10] The pair is the infrastructure under Godhood, not another pair of Gods.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s earlier relationship with [[Roxanne Elderblood]] had also exposed him to the Negative side without giving him the Concept of <Negativity>. As Roxanne's Keeper, he could draw upon the enormous quantity of Negative Energy generated and processed through a Negative World Tree. During his rule in Hell, Victor became capable of deliberately channeling exceptionally pure Negative Energy through their connection.[11][12]
This distinction is important: using Negative Energy through a World Tree is not the same as possessing the supreme Divine Concept <Negativity>. Victor accessed the Energy centuries before Negativity herself selected him as her Representative.

##### King Among Kings
The same duality reproduces itself on a planetary scale. On Samar, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] met [[Aurora Seraphina Evergreen]], the Positive World Tree, and her newly developed Negative counterpart Aira Seraphina Evergreen.[13]
Aurora explained that the first World Tree to arise upon a developing life-bearing planet is ordinarily the Positive World Tree. Across immense periods of time, its existence supports the development of the material environment and the emergence of Life. Once sentient beings and a sufficiently complex spiritual ecosystem appear, a corresponding Negative World Tree begins to form.[13]
The two planetary Trees are simultaneously separate and interconnected. They possess independent personalities and bodies while representing complementary aspects of one larger planetary supernatural system. The pair is the same complementarity the universal halves have, on the scale of one world.
The transformation of Victor into the Primogenitor of the Draconic Vampires subsequently drew direct attention from the [[Regulators of Existence]].[14] Their discussions increasingly framed Victor through the Balance of Creation because his existence contained an enormous Negative alignment without yet possessing a comparably developed Positive counterpart.
Victor refers to the semi-conscious Positive–Negative regulatory principle as "The Balance". When he attempted to understand why [[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]] could not simply become another Vampire Progenitor while [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] remained alive, Victor concluded that the greater cosmic structure enforced the rule that one Progenitor of a given Race could occupy that position within an era.[15]
That use of The Balance is not The Owner of Limbo. Limbo is a separate Primordial office inside the system. The older name was the wider Positive–Negative equilibrium itself.

##### The Duality of Divine Energy
Positive and Negative Energy sit above ordinary Divine Energies, because the duality contains the structures from which specialized Concepts are expressed. Even extreme Concepts such as BEGIN and END remained connected to the greater universal framework despite possessing their own higher-order relationships.[16]
Attributeless Energy, the divided Creator, and foreign cosmologies sit around that duality. They do not replace it.

Begin was revealed to exist directly beneath Positivity in the Positive conceptual hierarchy and, like Negativity, could not simply be manufactured through ordinary Divine development. #### Negativity
<Negativity> is the female half of the divided Creator associated principally with spirituality, emotion, abstraction and nonphysical existence.[5][2]
Her domain encompasses far more than hostile emotions or Demonic phenomena. Fear, hatred, terror, corruption and similar Concepts possess strong Negative characteristics, but so can non-hostile spiritual or emotional Concepts. What matters is their metaphysical nature rather than morality.[2]
Negativity likewise should not be confused with ordinary Negative Energy. Negative Energy is an energetic expression of the principle throughout Creation; <Negativity> is the supreme Conceptual [[Will Solace|Will]] and Rank 1 Authority from which the greater Negative framework derives.

##### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Negativity's first known Representative and current chosen successor.[7][1][4]
Negativity's choice was unprecedented precisely because she had apparently never accepted a Representative before him. Victor's personality, existence and repeated affinity with spiritual and Negative phenomena made him extraordinarily compatible, but compatibility alone did not create the Authority—the semi-conscious [[Will Solace|Will]] had to choose him.[7][1]
Holding the Divinity is not the same as understanding it. After those two thousand years the understanding was still around 3%. Recognition is not embodiment.[18]
In the Imperial era the Primordials acknowledge him as approaching full embodiment of the Negative framework. He has not become Negativity herself.[23][4]

#### Positive and Negative Energy
Positive and Negative Energy are the universal energetic expressions associated with the two halves. [[World Trees]] and Gods already circulate both Energies through planets, afterlives, living beings and Divine systems.
They should not be treated as ordinary elemental Energies. Fire, Ice or Lightning describe particular phenomena; Positive and Negative Energy describe which side of the fundamental universal duality an expression is aligned with.
The same phenomenon can manifest differently depending upon its conceptual foundation. A Fire tied to a Sun or to Creation can be Positive-aligned. Hellfire, or a destructive spiritual Fire, can be Negative-aligned without ceasing to be Fire.[19]
The flexibility exists because everything native contains both halves. The distinction is predominance, not exclusive ownership.[2]

##### Synchronization
A true Representative can theoretically achieve Synchronization, temporarily drawing upon the corresponding universal Concept with a degree of access comparable to a Primordial state.[1] Compatibility is insufficient by itself; profound understanding of the Concept is also required.
The display in front of the Primordials was not Synchronization.
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] began moving inside a domain occupied by Primordial Beings while his body appeared to become a mass of Negative Energy, the Universal Tree and Judges believed he had synchronized with Negativity. Victor himself had not even known Synchronization existed.[1] His actual conceptual understanding was still too incomplete to attain the state naturally.
Victor instead used his Eldritch/Outer God nature to force an enormous quantity of Negative Energy through himself, imitating the external characteristics of Synchronization closely enough to deceive the observers. He then exploited Negativity's connection with universal information and the [[Akashic Records]] to reproduce historical images as part of the deception.[1]
Synchronization is a real state. Victor had not reached it.

#### Relationship with the Akashic Records
The universal spread of Positivity and Negativity is tied to the [[Akashic Records]].[1]
Because both Energies permeate Creation, they continuously interact with information generated throughout Reality. They collect universal information and contribute it to the Records. During the false display, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] used that tie and pulled images of past events without the Akashic access a normal God would have needed.[1]
The Akashic Records remain their own cosmic structure. Positivity and Negativity are not simply alternate names for Akasha, nor are the Records merely a subordinate Divine Concept owned exclusively by one side. Their relationship is based upon the ubiquitous presence of both universal Energies and the information carried through them.

#### The Primordial Machine
A normal dimension can borrow its underlying Rules from the greater Universe. Creating a truly autonomous Reality requires considerably more: Concepts, laws and fundamental functions must be established deeply enough for the new Reality to sustain itself independently.[28]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] compared the Universe to a gigantic machine. Ordinary Gods interact with individual systems. Completed Primordial Beings become essential components of the machine. Positivity and Negativity represent the divided supreme structure inherited from the Creator who designed the greater system.[28]
This led Victor to the idea of administrative rights. Destroying every individual component of a Universe would be disastrously inefficient; obtaining the highest Authority over the system could theoretically allow the Rules themselves to be rewritten. The vacant Rank 0 and the eventual reunification of the two Rank 1 halves therefore became much more important than simply obtaining additional combat power.
Nobody has independently developed a complete <Positivity> or <Negativity>, because the Concepts retain the consciousness of Primordial Chaos and select their own bearers.[28] The Moira crisis shows that selection in practice.

He appears in Velion, before [[Aurethys Vaelthion]], as a being of pure energy, and he calls the man an old friend. She, he says, has always been rebellious. It lies in her nature. What stops Positivity is the energy. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is using attributeless energy. Aurethys asks whether the other half taught it, the way Positivity taught it to him. The answer is that this is impossible. Only he inherited the knowledge of the former self. She received only instincts. Attributeless energy was a property of that former self. He knows every method of using it, and the primordial beings do not. He does not believe it is an energy one simply learns. Aurethys's conclusion, from the fact in front of them, is that the Dragon Emperor learned it alone.[8]
His question after that is practical. If her Chosen has her temperament, he will not give up his Authority without a fight. Aurethys calls the question pointless. He will take back what rightfully belongs to him, and he does not have to be the one who moves. Darkness and Emperor will not stand idle. The two of them wait, and let the others test the strength.[8]
She comes to Victor after the supergalaxy is finished. A feminine body of pure red energy. Her first words are an assessment, and an arrogant one. There is still room for improvement. The precision is not high enough, because it is the first time. Violet is the one who names her. Kaguya confirms it. Asked whether she has gained more consciousness, she says yes. The moment he entered the Upper Sectors, she woke further, probably because the two Chosen Ones are close to each other. Her other self, as always, chose someone uninteresting. That was to be expected. He inherited the knowledge and the reason of her former self.[9]
She states the division in her own words. She is the instinct of Primordial Chaos. He embodies the reason. He inherited the knowledge of how to use these powers. She inherited the instincts. It stands to reason that he also has attributeless energy, because that was a property of the former self. Victor's answer is that this changes nothing. The goal remains the same. Her smile widens. That is why she chose him. Not only strength and talent. His mentality matched hers.[9]
She will not teach him. He only has to feel, as he has done. She is instinct. His instinct. Trust it, and the answers are there. She embraces him from behind, dissolves into the red energy, and presses into his body. He hears her laughing inside him. The sentence he keeps is the first one. The two Chosen Ones stand close to each other. The key, in his reading, is Positivity. He has other work in front of that.[9]
The title is already a fact to the rulers who are not her. When the dragon appears holding the galaxy, Darkness is overjoyed to have found Negativity. At his first appearance in the Upper Sectors, the Chosen One of Negativity stands level with the leaders who hold galaxies. <Emperor>, offered twenty percent to make an enemy of him, calls the price far too little. And the attempt to manufacture another Chosen fails. [[Vaelthar]] was made for the office, given a portion of Vharzath's power, and left unchosen, with a <BEGIN> stained by that Darkness.[29][30][31]
