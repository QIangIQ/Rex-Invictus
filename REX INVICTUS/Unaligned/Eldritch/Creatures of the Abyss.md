Creatures of the Abyss, commonly called Abyss Creatures or Abyssal Creatures, are a vast population of native Low-Level Eldritch beings inhabiting The Abyss Realm, the dead regions of The Universe produced when galaxies reach their true End. They are adapted to an environment in which ordinary Life, Time, Space and even the familiar Concept of Void have ceased to exist, surviving within the True Darkness and residual cosmic Energy left behind by ended Creation.[1]
Although Eldritch in nature, the Creatures of the Abyss should not be confused with conventional [[Outer Gods]] originating beyond the native cosmological system. Their existence developed inside the current Creation after fragments and influences of [[Azathoth Elderblood]] spread through the Universe even while she remained imprisoned. Because the creatures themselves were born within Creation, The System accepted them as legitimate native existences rather than treating them as completely foreign intruders. Their Eldritch nature is therefore derived from Azathoth, but their cosmological origin is fundamentally different from Azathoth herself.[1]
The creatures also perform an important ecological function. Residual and aged Energy remaining inside dead galaxies is consumed by the Abyssal population instead of leaking toward active Sectors, causing Amara to compare them to a natural cosmic waste-disposal system. Their usefulness is one reason the Primordials did not simply exterminate the species after discovering what had developed in the abandoned portions of Creation. Instead, the Abyss was placed under the supervision of True Death, who regulates their feeding, prevents dangerous specimens from freely entering living regions and destroys them when their continued existence becomes an unacceptable threat.[1][2]

### Nature and Classification
The Creatures of the Abyss demonstrate that Eldritch and Outer God are not interchangeable classifications. Azathoth is an existence originating from a foreign cosmological system and consequently entered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s native Creation from outside its natural Rules. The Abyssal creatures, by contrast, arose within the native Universe after fragments of Azathoth's Eldritch Power and influence became involved in their development. They inherited qualities recognizable as Eldritch without sharing her foreign origin.[1]
This distinction became obvious to Victor Elderblood. Long before he understood their complete history, encountering one of Hela's contracted creatures produced a weak but unmistakable sense of familiarity comparable to the instinctive recognition Vampires may experience toward beings belonging to the same lineage. After investigating the Abyss directly, Victor confirmed that the creatures belonged to the same extremely broad Eldritch family of existence represented at vastly higher levels by himself and Azathoth, but the difference in existential hierarchy was enormous.[3][1]
Roxanne and Amara emphasized that comparing an ordinary Creature of the Abyss to Victor or Azathoth was effectively comparing a microscopic organism to something incomparably greater. Their common Eldritch nature therefore does not imply equality in Power, authority, complexity or status. The creatures are most accurately treated as native Low-Level Eldritch beings whose ancestry was influenced by a vastly superior foreign Eldritch existence.[1]
Their integration into the native cosmology is equally important. Rather than remaining errors which Reality continuously rejects, the creatures became recognized by The System and developed a functional role within the Universe. Their existence does not distort surrounding Reality on the same scale as vastly higher Eldritch beings such as Victor and Azathoth, although exposure to them can still be extremely harmful to ordinary minds and even affect beings from considerably more advanced Sectors.[1][2]

### Habitat
Main article: The Abyss Realm
The natural habitat of the species is The Abyss Realm, consisting of regions where galaxies have experienced their true End. A conventionally destroyed galaxy may still contain stars, matter, radiation, black holes and material capable of eventually contributing to new celestial formation. A true End produces a qualitatively different state. Hela explained that within such dead galactic regions ordinary Life no longer exists, Time no longer exists, Space no longer exists and even the Concept of Void is absent, leaving True Darkness and the remnants associated with a completed cosmic End.[1]
The creatures are consequently not merely organisms capable of surviving in extremely hostile outer space. Their native environment is one in which several of the normal foundations required by conventional life have already ceased to operate. This should not automatically be interpreted as universal immunity to every ability involving Time, Space, Life or Void; canon directly establishes their ability to naturally exist within an environment where these components are absent, but does not state that every member automatically negates all supernatural manipulation of those Concepts.[1]
The Primordials originally paid little attention to these regions when Creation was divided into Sectors. Dead galaxies appeared useless to ordinary civilizations, and there seemed to be little reason for powerful species to deliberately inhabit them. That neglect allowed the strange ecosystem developing within True Darkness to remain largely isolated until beings such as Hela began establishing connections to it.[4][1]

#### Relationship with End
The Creatures of the Abyss are strongly associated with End, but they are not themselves automatically Gods of End or embodiments of the Concept. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s understanding during his earlier investigation was that such creatures exist as consequences of what remains after a galaxy experiences its End. An End-aligned being can therefore contribute to the conditions creating their habitat without the resulting creatures themselves inheriting the Divinity responsible for the original destruction.[3]
This connection allows [[Hela Elderblood]] to use End Energy as a guide toward their home. Hela channels End through her staff rather than embodying it in exactly the same manner as some of her brothers, giving her enough flexibility to use the Energy as a navigational connection toward regions whose normal spatial framework has already ceased to exist. Her method is effective but conspicuous because using End to intrude upon a region supervised by [[True Death]] can immediately attract his attention.[1][2]

### Origin
The species originated indirectly through [[Azathoth Elderblood]]. Even while imprisoned by the native cosmic authorities, Azathoth remained capable of influencing Creation. Fragments of her Eldritch Power had spread throughout the Universe, and beings searching for Power could enter her sphere of influence. Over time, organisms carrying these influences developed within the dead galaxies and became what are now known as the Creatures of the Abyss.[1]
Azathoth should therefore be associated with the Eldritch origin of the species, not with the creation of the Abyss itself. The dead regions already arise when galaxies reach their true End; Azathoth's influence produced or shaped many of the abnormal beings capable of thriving there. Likewise, the creatures are not fragments of Azathoth in the sense of being extensions of her consciousness. They possess their own instincts, individual abilities, rivalries and varying degrees of independent mentality.[1][2]
Because their formation occurred inside the native Universe, they were eventually accepted by The System. This transformed what might otherwise have remained an anomalous infestation into a naturally incorporated component of Creation's lifecycle. The species consumes the remains of dead Creation, remains restricted primarily to regions living civilizations cannot normally occupy and is ultimately regulated by a native Primordial responsible for Death.[1]

### Appearance
There is no single fixed anatomy shared by every Creature of the Abyss. Specimens observed by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] possessed strongly amorphous and Eldritch characteristics, appearing as unstable masses of darkness or viscous material from which tentacles, eyes, mouths and other structures could continuously emerge. Their bodies could possess a gelatinous or partially translucent quality covered by an unnatural iridescent sheen resembling oil across water, producing visual characteristics that appeared paradoxical inside an environment otherwise defined by True Darkness.[1]
Numerous eyes of radically different sizes can develop across their bodies and move independently, while mouths containing sharp teeth may emerge between them without following ordinary anatomical organization. Older creatures can display increasingly exaggerated Eldritch characteristics, with Amara noting that age causes some specimens to become progressively more reminiscent of existences such as Victor and Azathoth. This resemblance reflects Eldritch development rather than proof that the creatures eventually become equal to either of them.[1]
One particularly important scene initially appeared to show Victor facing an enormous entity possessing hundreds of thousands of distorted eyes. The apparent organism was actually a congregation of numerous separate Abyssal creatures. Individual eyes corresponded to different beings which had temporarily suppressed their usual competition because Victor represented an overwhelmingly valuable concentration of Energy. Their ability to cluster together should therefore not be mistaken for evidence that every visible Abyssal mass is automatically a single organism.[1]
The species is also capable of changing significantly after leaving its natural environment or being modified. By the Imperial era, the creature Victor selected from the Abyss—the Little One—could appear as a tiny elf-like being. In Chapter 1168, Victor summoned Little One alongside [[Abhoth]] into his hand while teaching his daughters about genuine Eldritch life. Both appeared as small elf-like creatures capable of greeting the girls normally. The story does not establish whether this appearance developed naturally through Little One's own mutable physiology or resulted from later evolution and alterations under Victor, so it should not be treated as the standard appearance of the species.[5]

### Ecology
The Creatures of the Abyss have become an unintended but useful component of the Universe's lifecycle. Even after a galaxy reaches its End, considerable quantities of old or residual Energy can remain. Abyssal creatures consume this Energy, preventing it from freely escaping dead regions and spreading into active Sectors. Amara consequently compared their collective function to a cosmic trash depot: they inhabit environments almost no ordinary species wants and consume what remains after Creation has finished using those regions.[1]
This ecological role explains why the Primordials tolerate a species that would otherwise be extraordinarily dangerous. Destroying every Abyssal creature would remove a population naturally disposing of residual Energy from dead Creation, while allowing them completely unrestricted movement would create the opposite problem and expose living celestial structures to predatory Eldritch organisms. The solution adopted by the Primordials was regulation rather than extermination, with [[True Death]] becoming responsible for maintaining the boundary between the useful function of the species and the danger created by uncontrolled feeding.[1][2]

#### Feeding
Hunger is the most fundamental behavioral drive displayed by the species. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] entered their territory, the surrounding creatures initially attempted to consume him because his existence contained an overwhelming concentration of Energy. Victor quickly realized that their behavior was not driven by personal hatred or hostility. To them he was an extraordinarily valuable food source, and their response was fundamentally predatory.[1]
Ordinary Energy can be consumed, but major natural growth requires considerably more specific nourishment. Chapter 1060 establishes that significant Abyssal development depends upon Energy associated with a star approaching death, a dying planet or a galaxy approaching or experiencing its End. These sources allow specimens to increase their size and strength in ways ordinary feeding cannot indefinitely reproduce.[2]
[[True Death]] prevents the species from simply roaming through healthy Creation in search of such resources. Energy produced by dead galaxies is itself heavily regulated, with much of it being filtered through Death as part of his cosmic responsibilities. Dying stars and planets are similarly inaccessible under ordinary circumstances because the creatures are intentionally kept inside their assigned regions. Their natural development is therefore shaped as much by access to food as by age.[2]

#### Competition
Competition between individual creatures is severe. Feeding opportunities capable of producing meaningful growth are limited, and stronger organisms can monopolize them before weaker ones have any opportunity to feed. This creates a hierarchy in which age and raw Power are not directly proportional. A being may survive for an enormous period without becoming exceptionally strong if it repeatedly loses every valuable feeding opportunity to more dominant competitors.[2]
The Little One selected by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is the clearest example. It was one of the oldest creatures he encountered while simultaneously being one of the smallest and weakest. Its age had not translated into raw strength because stronger Abyssal organisms continually reached available nourishment first. [[True Death]] even regarded its natural prospects as effectively exhausted before Victor became interested in it.[2]

### Behavior and Intelligence
Most naturally observed Creatures of the Abyss possess relatively primitive mentalities centered upon hunger, desire and fear. Their ecosystem rewards competition for limited Energy rather than cooperation or civilization-building, and nothing currently establishes the existence of a unified Abyssal government, culture, religion or conventional society. They are nevertheless not mindless. They can assess opportunities, react to threats, communicate through Eldritch means, recognize agreements and possess individually developing supernatural abilities.[1][2]
They can also temporarily cooperate when a sufficiently valuable incentive overrides competition. The enormous congregation surrounding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] did exactly this, gathering so densely that many individuals appeared to constitute a single greater organism. When Victor subsequently offered a contract, the population was capable of understanding the opportunity and expressing intense interest rather than merely attacking without thought.[1]
Individual psychological development varies. The Little One possessed something Victor considered exceptionally valuable precisely because it differed from most of its species: ambition. Having spent an immense period near the bottom of an ecosystem in which stronger creatures repeatedly stole every meaningful opportunity, it developed the desire to become something greater rather than existing only through immediate hunger and fear. Victor valued this mentality more than the raw strength of the much stronger creatures surrounding it.[2]

### Eldritch Hierarchy
Abyssal creatures instinctively respond to overwhelming differences in Eldritch existence. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] first entered their territory, the population behaved like predators attempting to consume an extraordinary source of Energy. Victor then exposed his complete Eldritch nature for only an infinitesimal period—approximately a fraction of a nanosecond. The response was immediate. Creatures whose behavior had previously been dominated by hunger experienced overwhelming fear and respect, withdrew from their attacks and began observing Victor as though awaiting the commands of a fundamentally superior existence.[1]
Victor was then capable of communicating with them through the instinctive language associated with Eldritch beings. This communication was distinct from ordinary spoken languages, Draconic speech or the mechanisms commonly used by native Gods. The surrounding creatures understood his intention and cooperated when he demanded access to their home, using enormous numbers of appendages to widen Hela's connection until Victor could pass through.[1]
Their reaction should not be interpreted as evidence that all Creatures of the Abyss are naturally Victor's servants. They obeyed because his revealed nature demonstrated an overwhelming difference in hierarchy, after which Victor offered an actual agreement rather than simply claiming ownership of the species. Likewise, their Azathoth-derived characteristics do not automatically make them direct servants of Azathoth's consciousness.[1]
[[True Death]]'s observations further demonstrate that their presence remains dangerous despite their low position on the greater Eldritch hierarchy. Even beings originating from Higher Sectors could not necessarily remain completely unaffected by Abyssal organisms while inside their territory. Death considered only the strongest individuals from High-Level Sectors capable of enduring their presence without being seriously influenced, placing the species considerably above the impression created by Victor casually standing among millions of them.[2]

### Relationship with True Death
Main article: [[True Death]]
True Death supervises the Creatures of the Abyss because their habitat exists within the terminal side of the universal lifecycle. After the Primordials discovered what had developed in the dead regions, the Abyss became part of Death's jurisdiction. His relationship with the creatures is regulatory rather than biological or religious: he did not create the species, is not established as their racial progenitor and is not worshipped as a conventional king.[1][2]
Death effectively keeps the species "on a leash". He prevents them from freely entering active Creation, controls their access to dying celestial Energy, filters much of the Energy released by completely ended galaxies and possesses the authority to destroy creatures whose development threatens the stability of living regions. He can also permit lower organisms to interact with beings carrying suitable connections to End.[2]
The Abyss itself was not created through Death's Power, which means his awareness of the region differs from the absolute domain-awareness available to entities controlling realms they personally created. He nevertheless watches important disturbances and became aware of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s intrusion when Hela's portal appeared. Victor consequently recognized that repeatedly reaching the Abyss through conspicuous End Energy would effectively announce his activities to Death every time.[2]

### Relationship with Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s relationship with the species developed from instinctive familiarity into direct investigation and recruitment. He first noticed the strange resonance surrounding Hela's contractor during the period following Ragnarök, eventually comparing the sensation to recognition between related Vampires. His later understanding of Azathoth explained the source of that familiarity: Victor had developed into a vastly higher Eldritch existence while the creatures represented primitive native descendants of influence originating from the same broader type of existence.[3][1]
When Victor entered the Abyss, he did not attempt to exterminate the population. After demonstrating his superiority, he offered a contract and examined the creatures willing to respond. The strongest specimens were the most obvious candidates, but Victor deliberately ignored raw Power and selected one of the weakest organisms in the region. He valued potential, mentality and unusual characteristics more than obtaining another servant whose only advantage was present strength.[1][2]
The chosen creature possessed an unexplained direct connection to the Abyss itself. Even the creature apparently did not understand the nature of this connection. Victor immediately recognized its strategic significance because Hela's End-based access method was effectively equivalent to announcing an intrusion to [[True Death]]. A creature intrinsically connected to the Abyss might eventually provide a far less conspicuous means of reaching the region without requiring Hela to repeatedly expose herself to Death's attention.[2]

#### The Little One
The Little One is currently the most extensively examined member of the species. Before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s arrival, its enormous age had given it little practical advantage. Stronger creatures continually took the limited cosmic Energy required for meaningful growth, leaving the ancient organism exceptionally small and weak. [[True Death]] saw little value in Victor's choice because, from the Primordial's perspective, the creature had already failed to develop throughout an immense portion of its natural existence.[2]
Victor evaluated it differently. The Little One had developed ambition despite living among organisms overwhelmingly governed by hunger, desire and fear, and its unexplained connection to the Abyss offered strategic possibilities none of the visibly stronger creatures could immediately provide. Victor consequently regarded its future potential as considerably more important than its present statistics.[2]
The Little One remained part of Victor's Eldritch household long after the expedition. In Chapter 1168, Victor summoned it alongside [[Abhoth]] while teaching several daughters about Eldritch creation. The two appeared as tiny elf-like beings standing in Victor's hand. Victor specifically used Little One and Abhoth as examples of genuinely living Eldritch beings in contrast with the technically successful but spiritually empty organisms he had reproduced inside an artificial Eldritch galaxy. Their mere nature remained dangerous enough that Victor noted ordinary beings could lose their sanity from observing them directly, while his daughters could withstand the sight because of their own Eldritch characteristics.[5]

### Growth and Evolution
Natural Abyssal development does not operate through age alone. The primary limiting resource is suitable Energy from celestial structures approaching death. A creature that continually obtains such nourishment can become substantially larger and stronger, while one deprived of it can remain comparatively insignificant despite surviving for an enormous period.[2]

Age nevertheless affects the species in ways separate from raw strength. Older specimens can develop increasingly pronounced Eldritch characteristics resembling those of higher beings such as Azathoth and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. This means two different developmental processes operate simultaneously: feeding determines much of their natural increase in size and Power, while prolonged existence can deepen their Eldritch characteristics and individuality.[1][2]
External intervention can bypass at least some of the restrictions governing natural growth. Victor explicitly regarded the Little One's apparent natural limitations as temporary rather than absolute, and much later he and Azathoth altered Eldritch creatures for Imperial use on scales impossible for naturally starved specimens. These enhanced organisms are therefore examples of artificial evolution rather than evidence that ordinary Abyssal creatures naturally possess the same capabilities.[2][6]

### Powers and Abilities
The following abilities represent characteristics directly demonstrated by the species or repeatedly associated with Abyssal physiology. Individual Creatures of the Abyss can develop additional powers, meaning the racial list should not be interpreted as the complete arsenal of every specimen.

#### Common Racial Characteristics
- Eldritch Physiology: The creatures possess native Eldritch existences formed under the influence of fragments of Azathoth's Power. Their existence differs fundamentally from conventional biological species and ordinary native Gods, while remaining recognized by the current Creation because they were born inside it.[1]
- Amorphous Physiology: Many specimens lack conventional fixed anatomy, manifesting as shifting masses of darkness or viscous matter containing tentacles, eyes, mouths and other structures whose arrangement can continuously change.[1]
- Body Control: Their unstable anatomy allows appendages, eyes and mouths to emerge, withdraw and reposition throughout their bodies without obeying conventional biological structure.[1]
- Extreme Environment Adaptation: The species naturally exists within true-dead galactic regions where ordinary Life, Time, Space and even the Concept of Void are absent. This is a demonstrated environmental property and should not automatically be expanded into universal immunity against every Conceptual attack involving those forces.[1]
- Energy Absorption: Abyssal creatures consume residual Energy remaining within dead Creation and can feed on Energy-rich beings or celestial phenomena.[1]
- Conditional Evolution / Statistics Amplification: Consuming the appropriate Energy of dying stars, planets or galaxies allows individual creatures to increase their size and Power substantially.[2]
- Eldritch Presence / Madness Inducement: The presence and appearance of Abyssal organisms can overwhelm ordinary minds. A massive congregation observed by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] possessed hundreds of thousands of distorted eyes whose exposure was capable of driving insufficiently protected observers insane; [[True Death]] further noted that even beings from Higher Sectors could be affected by these organisms inside their territory.[1][2]
- Eldritch Communication: The creatures can understand and communicate through the instinctive form of language shared by Eldritch beings. Victor successfully addressed them through this method despite having no conventional linguistic relationship with the species.[1]
- Instinctive Existential Recognition: Abyssal organisms can perceive overwhelming differences in Eldritch hierarchy. After Victor briefly revealed his complete nature, the population immediately experienced fear and respect and abandoned its attempt to consume him.[1]
- Portal Interaction: Abyssal beings are capable of physically interacting with connections established to their territory. After Victor ordered them to reveal their home, numerous creatures used their appendages to expand Hela's passage until he could enter the Abyss.[1]
- Existence within System: Unlike truly foreign [[Outer Gods]], Abyssal creatures are accepted as part of the native Creation and function within The System despite their Eldritch characteristics.[1]
- Aged Energy Consumption: Their feeding removes old residual Energy from ended galaxies and prevents it from spreading freely into active Sectors, making the species part of the Universe's natural disposal process.[1]

#### Individual Ability Development
Individual members can develop supernatural powers not shared by the entire species. Chapter 1060 explicitly establishes that Abyssal creatures possess personal abilities of differing complexity; some can possess similar abilities while others develop highly individualized effects.[2]
- Duplication / Division (Certain Individuals): Some creatures can divide or split themselves into multiple portions or independent manifestations. This ability is not established as universal to the species.[2]
- Unique Ability Development: Individual creatures can naturally manifest abilities unrelated to the baseline physiology of neighboring specimens, making the species extremely variable in actual combat.[2]

##### Impregnation
Impregnation is the unique known ability of the Little One. Despite its name, it has nothing to do with biological reproduction. The ability allows the user to soak or saturate a target with a chosen corruptive negative effect, effectively imposing a detrimental condition throughout the victim.[2]
- Status Effect Inducement: The user can fill a target with a selected harmful condition.[2]
- Corruption Inducement: The imposed effect behaves as a corruptive negative influence saturating the affected being.[2]
- Mental Exhaustion Inducement: The Little One's most commonly described application produces extreme mental exhaustion.[2]
- Death Inducement (Conditional): Continuous mental exhaustion can become severe enough to kill targets too weak to withstand the accumulated effect.[2]
Hela and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] both regarded Impregnation as comparatively basic when measured against the abilities possessed by Victor's family. Its importance lies less in overwhelming destructive force than in demonstrating that even an exceptionally weak Abyssal creature can possess an individualized supernatural mechanism unrelated to the standard traits of its species.[2]

### Limitations
- Specialized Growth Requirement: Consuming arbitrary Energy does not allow unlimited natural development. Major growth requires Energy associated with celestial structures approaching death.[2]
- Restricted Feeding: [[True Death]] deliberately prevents the species from freely reaching dying stars, planets and living galaxies, greatly restricting the resources needed for their evolution.[2]
- Severe Competition: Stronger creatures can monopolize limited feeding opportunities for immense periods, preventing old but weaker specimens from developing. The Little One remained among the weakest despite being one of the oldest known individuals.[2]
- Primitive Instincts (Most Natural Specimens): Many creatures are overwhelmingly governed by hunger, desire and fear, reducing their behavioral complexity compared with more developed Eldritch beings. Exceptions such as Little One demonstrate that greater individuality can emerge.[1][2]
- Eldritch Hierarchy: Their own Eldritch nature does not protect them from vastly superior members of the broader category. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s complete presence immediately overwhelmed their predatory instincts and produced fear and submission.[1]
- True Death's Regulation: Their habitat lies under the supervision of one of the Universe's Primordial regulators. Death can restrict feeding, prevent expansion and eliminate individuals considered too dangerous to Creation.[1][2]
- Natural Development Ceiling (Individual): Extremely old specimens deprived of proper Energy can reach a point where natural development appears effectively stalled. Victor considered external modification capable of changing this situation, meaning the limitation is not necessarily absolute when beings of his level interfere.[2]

### Imperial Modification and Use
By the later Imperial Era, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] no longer regarded Eldritch organisms merely as curiosities from an inaccessible dead region. During the enormous developmental period produced by the Empire's temporal ritual, organisms connected to Hela's previous wars were incorporated into a Galactic defensive project and further altered through the combined work of Victor and [[Azathoth Elderblood]].[6]
Beginning around the seventieth millennium of accelerated development, the Imperial galaxy itself was transformed into a defensive structure. Modified Eldritch organisms were distributed through strategically valuable astronomical environments, including asteroids, stellar regions and even black holes. Their purpose was to turn apparently empty areas of space into concealed living defenses capable of attacking invaders unfamiliar with Imperial territory.[6]
Victor specifically altered these organisms so they would not require the same conventional feeding pattern while lying dormant. This prevented Imperial defenses from consuming surrounding ecosystems merely to sustain themselves and allowed creatures to remain hidden for immense periods until required. These specimens therefore possess modifications beyond the natural baseline established in Chapters 1059–1060 and should not be used to retroactively grant every wild Creature of the Abyss the same independence from feeding.[6]

### History

#### Ragnarök
The species first became directly relevant to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] through [[Hela Elderblood]]. During the conflict surrounding Ragnarök, Hela established contact with a Creature of the Abyss and used it as part of her war against [[Odin]]. The creature represented an unfamiliar form of existence to most observers and prompted the first substantial explanations concerning the dead regions located outside the normal territories occupied by living civilizations.[4]
Victor later learned that Hela's contractor was unimpressive by the standards of its own race. It was not even considered a fully mature adult and stood close to the bottom of the Abyssal food chain. Nevertheless, Victor felt an unusual resonance with its Eldritch nature, leaving him interested in eventually discovering exactly what the creature was and where Hela had obtained it.[3]

#### Investigation of the Abyss
Much later, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] questioned Hela directly and asked her to demonstrate the connection. Hela explained the distinction between conventionally destroyed galaxies and those which had truly reached their End before using End Energy through her staff to open a passage toward the Abyss. The opening immediately attracted enormous numbers of creatures, including a congregation whose innumerable eyes initially made it appear to be one gigantic organism.[1]
Victor recognized the influence of Azathoth within their existence and briefly displayed his complete Eldritch nature. The creatures immediately abandoned their attempt to consume him, experiencing fear and respect before responding to his Eldritch communication. At Victor's command they widened the portal, allowing him to physically enter their territory.[1]
Inside the Abyss, Victor expanded his senses and discovered millions of extremely Low-Level Eldritch organisms, several hundred stronger specimens and approximately seven powerful enough to attract his attention. Rather than begin a battle, he offered a contract. The response demonstrated that the supposedly primitive beings were capable of understanding opportunity and agreement when presented with something valuable enough.[1]

#### Recruitment of the Little One
[[True Death]] watched [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s actions and was surprised when the Emperor ignored the obvious stronger candidates. Victor selected one of the smallest and weakest creatures present: an ancient specimen which had spent most of its existence losing every significant feeding opportunity to stronger rivals.[2]
The creature's weakness concealed the traits Victor actually valued. It possessed ambition, an individual ability called Impregnation and an unexplained direct connection to the Abyss. Victor recognized that its connection might eventually provide an alternative to Hela's conspicuous End-based access method and therefore took the creature with him for future development.[2]

#### Imperial Era
The principles discovered through the Abyss later contributed to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s use of Eldritch organisms as part of Imperial infrastructure. Modified creatures were placed throughout the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s Galactic defenses, where Victor and Azathoth altered them into dormant living weapons capable of occupying environments such as asteroids, stellar regions and black holes without consuming surrounding life merely to survive.[6]
The Little One itself remained alive and connected to Victor. In Chapter 1168 it appeared alongside [[Abhoth]] during Victor's lesson on Eldritch cosmology. Victor contrasted the two genuine living Eldritch beings with artificial replicas he had created inside a simulated Eldritch galaxy, establishing that Little One had developed far beyond its role as an anonymous weak organism collected during his first expedition.[5]

### Relationship with Outer Gods
Main article: [[Outer Gods]]
The Creatures of the Abyss belong to the broader category of Eldritch beings, but describing them simply as Outer Gods obscures an important cosmological distinction. A true Outer God such as Azathoth originates outside the native Creation and consequently possesses an existence that did not develop beneath its normal Rules. Abyssal creatures originated inside the native Universe and were accepted by The System despite inheriting Eldritch characteristics from an outside influence.[1]
The species therefore demonstrates that Eldritch existence contains multiple levels and origins. Millions of primitive Abyssal organisms can exist at the lowest observed end of the spectrum, while enormously greater beings such as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Azathoth occupy existential hierarchies so far beyond them that sharing the word "Eldritch" does not imply meaningful equality. Origin, development, authority and existential weight remain more important distinctions than appearance alone.[1]

### Canon Clarifications
- They are not automatically Gods of End. The species inhabits the aftermath of true Ends and can be reached through End Energy, but the creatures themselves are not stated to universally possess the Divinity or Authority of End.[3]
- They are not conventional foreign [[Outer Gods]]. Their Eldritch traits originate through Azathoth's influence, but the creatures themselves were formed inside the native Creation and recognized by The System.[1]
- Azathoth did not create the Abyss. Her fragments and influence are associated with the formation of its Eldritch inhabitants. The Abyss itself consists of galaxies that have reached their true End.[1]
- [[True Death]] is not their creator. Death supervises their habitat and controls their growth because the Abyss falls within his jurisdiction, but he did not create the region or species through his Power.[2]
- The enormous many-eyed being encountered in Chapter 1059 was not necessarily one creature. The apparent organism was a congregation of separate Abyssal beings which had gathered around [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] because of the Energy contained within him.[1]
- The approximately seven powerful creatures are not confirmed to be the seven strongest Abyssal beings in existence. They were simply the specimens powerful enough to catch Victor's attention within the population he perceived during that expedition.[1]
- Surviving where Time, Space, Life and Void are absent does not automatically establish universal immunity to every manipulation of those Concepts. The demonstrated racial feat is natural existence within the true-dead environment of the Abyss.[1]
- The Imperial organisms are modified specimens. Their ability to remain dormant without conventional feeding and function as Galactic weapons resulted from later alterations by Victor and Azathoth rather than being established natural properties of every wild Abyssal creature.[6]

### Trivia
- The Creatures of the Abyss are among the clearest known examples of Eldritch life being integrated into the native System rather than remaining foreign to Creation.[1]
- Their existence gives dead galaxies a continuing function in the universal ecosystem because they consume residual Energy that might otherwise spread into living Sectors.[1]
- The species' low standing relative to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] should not be confused with weakness by ordinary standards; [[True Death]] noted that their presence could affect even beings from Higher Sectors inside Abyssal territory.[2]
- Age and strength are not directly proportional. Little One was one of the oldest creatures Victor encountered while simultaneously ranking among the weakest because it continually lost access to suitable food.[2]
- The strongest immediately observed creatures were not the ones Victor wanted. He deliberately selected Little One because of ambition and its mysterious connection to the Abyss rather than present combat capability.[2]
- Little One's later elf-like appearance should not be treated as the default racial form because its origin—natural transformation, evolution or modification under Victor—has not been established.[5]
- Victor eventually used genuine Little One and [[Abhoth]] as examples to teach his daughters the difference between truly living Eldritch beings and technically convincing but spiritually empty replicas.[5]
