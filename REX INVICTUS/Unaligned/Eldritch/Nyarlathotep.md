### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 962
Nyarlathotep Elderblood is an Outer God and one of the Eldritch daughters of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Azathoth Elderblood]]. She first appears during a glimpse into the future surrounding Victor's defense of the Main Timeline, where she serves alongside Azathoth and [[Yog-Sothoth]] Elderblood as one of the cosmic beings protecting the layers surrounding the [[Akashic Records]].[1]
Nyarlathotep is one of the more composed members of Victor's Eldritch family. Although playful and willing to tease her sisters, she possesses enough restraint to understand when their emotions could become catastrophically dangerous. This is demonstrated when she prevents Yog-Sothoth from further provoking [[Cthulhu]] immediately before Cthulhu loses control and begins destroying the living universes surrounding the Records.[2]

### Physical Appearance
In her humanoid manifestation, Nyarlathotep appears as a pale-skinned woman with long black hair and black eyes. She possesses features similar to the other daughters born from the union of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Azathoth, reflecting the Elderblood side of their existence while remaining visibly distinct from Victor's Blood Dragon descendants.
Unlike the majority of Victor's biological Dragon daughters, Nyarlathotep is an Eldritch existence rather than a conventional True Dragon and therefore does not possess the characteristic violet Dragon Eyes associated with his Draconic bloodline.
Her humanoid body is only a manageable manifestation. Nyarlathotep possesses a vastly more incomprehensible Nightmare Form appropriate to her true nature as a cosmic horror. When she, Azathoth and [[Yog-Sothoth]] simultaneously returned to these forms, their presence alone was sufficient to push the surrounding Reality toward instability.[1]

### Personality
Nyarlathotep is considerably more restrained than several of her Eldritch sisters. She displays a mischievous and quietly amused temperament, but unlike [[Cthulhu]] or [[Shub-Niggurath]], she has not been shown losing emotional control to the point of accidentally producing cosmic destruction. Her interactions instead suggest that she observes the behavior of those around her and recognizes when amusement should give way to restraint.[2]
She is also well aware of the abnormal family dynamics surrounding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Azathoth. When Azathoth affectionately continued calling Victor "Father" despite simultaneously being the mother of Nyarlathotep and her sisters, Nyarlathotep and [[Yog-Sothoth]] merely looked at her with narrowed eyes. Both understood perfectly well that their mother's insistence upon maintaining the title was simply one of Azathoth's peculiar preferences rather than genuine confusion over their family relationship.[2]
Nyarlathotep nevertheless shares the strong attachment toward Victor common among his Eldritch daughters. She openly acknowledges that Victor's presence has an unusual calming effect upon them. According to Nyarlathotep, beings such as herself, Cthulhu and Shub-Niggurath would ordinarily find peaceful coexistence virtually impossible because of the madness inherent to their nature. Victor's presence suppresses that instability enough for them to function as a family.[2]
This awareness makes Nyarlathotep unexpectedly pragmatic. When Yog-Sothoth began to tease Cthulhu about what Victor and Azathoth had been doing, Nyarlathotep immediately covered her sister's mouth and warned her not to provoke Cthulhu. Her caution proved justified almost immediately when Cthulhu discovered Victor's Essence within the newly created daughters and suffered a jealousy-driven breakdown severe enough for Victor to throw her away from the Records before she transformed.[2]
Despite her restraint, Nyarlathotep is not humorless. Her behavior around her siblings carries a recurring undertone of amusement, particularly when observing the absurd situations produced by their family's relationships. Her composure therefore appears to originate from greater emotional control rather than emotional detachment.

### Synopsis

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Nyarlathotep first appears during an extraordinary glimpse into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s future. After [[Hela Elderblood]] attempted to use her authority over Time to observe or interfere with Victor's past, her consciousness was unexpectedly dragged into a region filled with countless enormous spheres representing galaxies or universes. There she encountered a future Victor whose existence had become radically different from the Dragon God Hela knew.[1]
This future Victor explained through his actions that attempts to interfere with his past had become a recurring problem. Foreign entities possessing unusual abilities or powers granted by outside systems repeatedly attempted to enter his Reality, manipulate history or approach the people he considered his family. Victor responded with overwhelming retaliation, including destroying the universe of an intruder rather than permitting another uncontrolled attack upon his home.[1]
Realizing that simply destroying individual invaders was inefficient, Victor decided to reinforce the barriers separating his Reality from foreign universes. His strategy was not to make entry absolutely impossible. Instead, he intended to manipulate the defenses so that any successful intrusion would be forced through a single controlled route—a funnel where Victor and the entities protecting his Reality could immediately identify and eliminate the intruder.[1]
Victor summoned Azathoth and ordered her to bring Nyarlathotep and [[Yog-Sothoth]]. Azathoth initially noted that the two were already responsible for protecting other layers, confirming that Nyarlathotep possessed an established defensive role elsewhere in the structure surrounding their Reality. Victor nevertheless required their presence because the defenses protecting Creation were about to undergo another major reinforcement.[1]
Nyarlathotep and Yog-Sothoth arrived with Azathoth, all three appearing through humanoid forms possessing similar characteristics. Victor then ordered his daughters to return to their Nightmare Forms. The three transformed simultaneously, and their presence alone altered the environment so severely that Reality could barely endure the existence of the cosmic horrors occupying the same region. Victor subsequently returned to his own Nightmare Form and worked together with them to strengthen the defenses surrounding his home and control the routes through which outsiders could enter.[1]
Nyarlathotep later appeared around the [[Akashic Records]] while Victor expanded those defenses even further. Victor's solution to foreign intrusion had evolved beyond conventional barriers: he intended to transform the empty region surrounding the Main Timeline into an environment inhabited by Eldritch horrors. Any outsider capable of surviving the initial barriers would therefore find themselves surrounded by beings whose existence was itself hostile to ordinary minds and conventional Reality.[2]
When Victor announced that [[Shub-Niggurath]] would become another of his daughters with Azathoth, Yog-Sothoth remarked that one of their sisters would not be pleased. Nyarlathotep immediately recognized that she meant [[Cthulhu]], explaining that Cthulhu and Shub had never been able to get along properly. She added that, without Victor, peaceful coexistence among the Eldritch sisters themselves would be nearly impossible. Victor's presence naturally calmed the madness inherent to their existence, allowing beings that might otherwise behave as mutually hostile cosmic horrors to function as members of the same family.[2]
Shub-Niggurath's ability became central to the next stage of Victor's defenses. Her existence produced abnormal forms of Life on astronomical scales, including entire living planets, stars, galaxies and universes. These creations were not ordinary inhabited worlds but colossal living organisms capable of consuming other things and even devouring one another. Victor intended to use them as false universes surrounding the Records, forcing any successful invader to cross hostile living cosmologies before approaching the Main Timeline itself.[2]
Nyarlathotep and Yog-Sothoth watched with narrowed eyes as Azathoth embraced Victor and once again called him "Father". Despite Azathoth technically being their mother, both daughters understood that her continued insistence upon presenting herself as Victor's daughter was simply part of her preferred dynamic with him. Rather than questioning it further, they merely reacted with the weary familiarity of children who had witnessed their mother's behavior many times before.[2]
The newly manifested Shub-Niggurath soon became upset because she wanted Victor's attention after effectively being reborn. Her emotions caused enormous creatures to begin appearing around her until Azathoth ordered her to control herself. Shub immediately obeyed, demonstrating the difference between Azathoth's mature control and the instability still affecting the younger Eldritch daughters. Victor warned Shub not to lose control around the Records, while Azathoth promised that she would supervise her younger sisters.[2]
Cthulhu arrived soon afterward after sensing Shub's presence. She immediately complained that Victor had once again created more Eldritch daughters and accused him of giving Azathoth too much attention. Yog-Sothoth nearly made the situation worse by teasing Cthulhu, but Nyarlathotep reacted immediately and covered Yog's mouth, warning her not to provoke their already jealous sister.[2]
Nyarlathotep's intervention was insufficient to prevent the eventual disaster. Cthulhu examined her sisters and sensed Victor's Essence within them, causing her emotional state to collapse. Victor recognized the danger and threw her away from the Records moments before she transformed into her Nightmare Form. Cthulhu's resulting tantrum destroyed several of the living universes generated through Shub-Niggurath's existence.[2]
Victor departed to calm Cthulhu while ordering Azathoth and the remaining daughters to watch for possible intruders. Their defenses were tested almost immediately when an outsider successfully entered the region. The intruder was unable to comprehend what stood before him; his head exploded and his existence began transforming into something distorted merely from encountering the environment Victor had created. Victor captured and consumed the resulting creature, confirming that the defensive system built with the assistance of Nyarlathotep and her sisters functioned exactly as intended.[2]

### Skills & Abilities
Nyarlathotep is an Eldritch daughter produced from the union of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Azathoth and consequently belongs to a category of existence fundamentally different from conventional Gods, Dragons and native supernatural races. Her complete individual power set has not yet been revealed; the abilities below are limited to characteristics directly demonstrated or established through her appearances.
- Outer God Physiology: Nyarlathotep is an Eldritch cosmic existence born through Victor and Azathoth. Her nature allows her to exist within regions surrounding the [[Akashic Records]] and other environments where conventional Reality becomes unstable or ceases to operate normally.[1][2]
- Eldritch Primordial Physiology: Nyarlathotep belongs to the first generation of Eldritch entities directly descended from Victor and Azathoth, placing her among the primordial members of the Outer God lineage rather than among later derivative Eldritch species.
- Nightmare Form: Nyarlathotep possesses a true Eldritch manifestation referred to as a Nightmare Form. Victor can order her to abandon her humanoid manifestation and return to this state when her complete nature is required.[1]
- Reality Distortion: The combined presence of Nyarlathotep, [[Yog-Sothoth]] and Azathoth in their Nightmare Forms caused the surrounding Reality to struggle to remain stable. This occurred passively from their existence rather than through a deliberate attack.[1]
- Cosmic Existence: Nyarlathotep can operate on scales where galaxies, universes and the barriers separating entire realities are treated as structures that can be protected, manipulated or surrounded by defensive systems.[1][2]
- Existence in Nothingness: Nyarlathotep can remain and function outside the ordinary cosmological structure surrounding the Akashic Records, including regions where normal Reality and its familiar laws cannot be assumed to function conventionally.[1][2]
- Cosmic Awareness: Nyarlathotep is capable of recognizing the condition of the layers she protects and participating in surveillance against entities attempting to penetrate Victor's Reality. She was already assigned to the protection of another layer when Victor summoned her to reinforce the central defenses.[1]
- Dimensional Barrier Manipulation: Nyarlathotep assisted Victor, Azathoth and Yog-Sothoth in reinforcing the boundaries separating their Reality from outside universes. Their work contributed to Victor's plan to control inter-universal intrusion by forcing successful invaders toward predetermined entry points.[1]
- Dimensional Defense: Nyarlathotep serves as one of the beings responsible for defending the layers surrounding Victor's home. Her role is not limited to direct combat; she participates in maintaining the enormous defensive structure designed to prevent interference from foreign realities.[1]
- Eldritch Presence: Nyarlathotep's true form possesses the characteristic incomprehensibility of an Eldritch horror. Beings lacking sufficient resistance may be incapable of safely perceiving entities of her nature. Victor deliberately populated the regions surrounding the Main Timeline with such beings because their presence alone could render the environment lethal or maddening to intruders.[2]
- Supernatural Perception: Nyarlathotep can perceive and distinguish other Eldritch family members and understand disturbances occurring within the region surrounding the Records. She immediately understood which sister Yog-Sothoth was referring to when discussing [[Shub-Niggurath]] and [[Cthulhu]]'s incompatibility.[2]
- Emotional Resistance and Self-Control: Compared with younger sisters such as Cthulhu and Shub-Niggurath, Nyarlathotep demonstrates substantially greater control over her emotions. She can remain composed around important cosmic structures and recognize when another Eldritch being is approaching an emotionally dangerous state.[2]
- Madness Suppression through Victor: The inherent instability and madness of Nyarlathotep and her sisters are significantly calmed by Victor's presence. Nyarlathotep herself states that without their father it would be essentially impossible for several of the Eldritch sisters to peacefully coexist.[2]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is Nyarlathotep's father and the central stabilizing influence within her existence. Nyarlathotep follows his orders during the defense of the Main Timeline and acknowledges that his presence has a direct calming effect upon the madness inherent to herself and her sisters. This effect is important enough that she believes peaceful coexistence between several members of the Eldritch family would otherwise be impossible.[1][2]

#### Azathoth Elderblood
Azathoth is Nyarlathotep's mother and one of the most mature Eldritch beings in the family. Nyarlathotep clearly recognizes Azathoth's unusual habit of calling [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] "Father" despite their relationship and reacts to it with amused exasperation rather than confusion. Azathoth also possesses greater control over her Eldritch nature and is trusted by Victor to supervise Nyarlathotep and her younger sisters around sensitive locations such as the Records.[2]

#### Yog-Sothoth Elderblood
[[Yog-Sothoth]] is one of the sisters most frequently shown beside Nyarlathotep. The two protect different portions of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s cosmic defenses and were summoned together when Victor strengthened the barriers surrounding his Reality. Their personalities contrast slightly: Yog is more willing to casually tease other family members, while Nyarlathotep demonstrates enough caution to stop her when the target of the joke is an emotionally unstable Eldritch sister.[1][2]

#### Cthulhu
Nyarlathotep understands [[Cthulhu]]'s emotional instability particularly well. She already knew that Cthulhu and [[Shub-Niggurath]] were unable to get along naturally and immediately prevented [[Yog-Sothoth]] from deliberately provoking Cthulhu when the latter became jealous over [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s new daughters. Her caution proved justified when Cthulhu transformed and destroyed several universes shortly afterward.[2]

#### Shub-Niggurath
Nyarlathotep identifies [[Shub-Niggurath]] as one of the sisters [[Cthulhu]] historically cannot tolerate. Despite this conflict, both remain part of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s defensive Eldritch family because his presence suppresses enough of their natural madness for coexistence to become possible.[2]

### Trivia
- Nyarlathotep's original article contained numerous titles such as "The Crawling [[Chaos]]", "The Bloody Tongue", "The Bloated Woman", "The Faceless God" and "Mother of All Bats". These are names associated with other fictional interpretations of Nyarlathotep and have not been attributed to Nyarlathotep Elderblood within [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]]; they are therefore not treated as canonical titles on this article.
- Her first appearance occurs during the future sequence in Chapter 962 rather than during the contemporary chronology of the story.[1]
- When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] first summons her on-page, Nyarlathotep is already protecting another layer of his cosmic defenses alongside [[Yog-Sothoth]], implying that her defensive responsibilities predate the scene shown to the reader.[1]
- Nyarlathotep, Azathoth and Yog-Sothoth transforming simultaneously was enough for Reality itself to struggle under their presence.[1]
- Nyarlathotep is considerably more cautious around [[Cthulhu]] than Yog-Sothoth, immediately stopping Yog from teasing their sister because she understood how dangerous Cthulhu's emotional reaction could become.[2]
- She openly states that Victor's presence calms the madness inherent to the Eldritch sisters and allows them to coexist as a family.[2]
- Nyarlathotep and Yog-Sothoth both understand that Azathoth referring to Victor as "Father" is intentional despite Azathoth simultaneously being their mother.[2]
- Unlike Cthulhu and [[Shub-Niggurath]], Nyarlathotep has not yet displayed a unique personal Concept or specialized individual ability in the novel. Her confirmed feats primarily concern her Outer God nature, Nightmare Form and participation in Victor's cosmic defenses.
