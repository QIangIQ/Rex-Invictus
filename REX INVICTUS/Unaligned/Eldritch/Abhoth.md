Abhoth is an Outer God and Eldritch God created through the combined powers of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Azathoth Elderblood]]. He is a member of the Great [[Chaos]], the Eldritch order associated with Victor and Azathoth, and serves as a supernatural guardian of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. His existence embodies an extraordinarily dangerous form of corruption that Victor can control and direct, allowing Abhoth to function as both an independent Eldritch being and the foundation of a distributed security system protecting the Emperor's civilization.[1]
Abhoth was created to counter supernatural corruption, possession, hostile manipulation and attempts to turn Imperial personnel against their ruler. Described by Victor as living corruption, Abhoth possesses an innate corruptive property through which everything he touches becomes subject to corruption, while Victor determines what that corruption entails. By distributing fragments of Abhoth among his soldiers and citizens, Victor can transform attempts at hostile infiltration into opportunities to detect, corrupt and potentially seize control of the aggressor through the very connection used to attack his people.[1]
Although produced through the combined Eldritch powers of Victor and Azathoth, Abhoth does not carry Victor's Essence in the same manner as [[Yog-Sothoth]] Elderblood. Instead, his existence possesses a particularly direct connection to the Emperor. This relationship allows his fragments to act as supernatural anchors through which Victor can observe hostile interference and project his own Authority. His unusual relationship with Victor makes him especially suited to operating throughout the Empire without requiring the Emperor to remain personally present wherever a protected individual encounters danger.[1]
Abhoth is not merely an artificial security organism. He possesses genuine Eldritch life and occupies the rank of an Outer God within the Great Chaos. As one of the Eldritch Gods created directly by Victor and Azathoth, he stands above the Great Old Ones and the lesser servants produced by higher Eldritch beings. The numerous Eldritch creatures Victor distributed throughout the galaxies are explicitly subordinate to Abhoth in both power and importance, establishing him as a high-ranking member of the Eldritch hierarchy rather than an expendable creation.[2]

### Physical Appearance
Abhoth originally manifested as a shapeless, slime-like creature covered in hundreds of eyes. His body possessed no recognizable humanoid anatomy, appearing instead as an amorphous Eldritch organism whose unnatural appearance reflected the combined cosmic powers involved in his creation. Despite his grotesque physical characteristics, Azathoth treated the newborn affectionately, approaching and caressing him immediately after his formation.[1]
Abhoth can also manifest in a considerably smaller and less grotesque form. When summoned by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] during a demonstration of genuine Eldritch life, Abhoth appeared alongside another creature in the Emperor's left hand. Both possessed the appearance of small elf-like beings, raised their hands in greeting and later waved before disappearing. The contrast between Abhoth's original amorphous appearance and his later diminutive manifestation demonstrates that his outward form is not restricted to the physical characteristics displayed at his birth.[3]
The comparatively harmless appearance of his smaller manifestation does not diminish the danger associated with his Eldritch existence. His presence remains fundamentally hazardous to beings lacking sufficient supernatural compatibility. Victor's daughters could safely observe Abhoth and the other summoned creature because they possessed Eldritch characteristics inherited from their father. Ordinary beings without those characteristics would have lost their minds merely from looking at them.[3]
The complete extent of Abhoth's physical transformations, his natural dimensions and the relationship between his primary body and his distributed fragments have not been established. His original appearance and smaller manifestation are the only directly described forms.

### Personality
Abhoth possesses a comparatively limited amount of established personal characterization, having been created primarily to fulfill a protective function within the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Nevertheless, his behavior demonstrates that he is a conscious living being rather than a mindless supernatural construct. He responds to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s summons, acknowledges the people around him and displays simple social behavior, including greeting others and waving farewell.[3]
His earliest interactions were characterized by the affection of his creators rather than hostility. [[Azathoth Elderblood]] welcomed him immediately after his birth, caressing the newborn and treating him as a member of her Pantheon. When [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] recognized the name Abhoth and associated it with the mythological being described as the source of impurity and the progenitor of evil, Azathoth defended him. She considered it unreasonable to regard him as inherently evil merely because of his name, emphasizing that he had only just been born.[1]
Although Abhoth's powers are extraordinarily dangerous, he has not demonstrated a personal desire to spread uncontrolled destruction or harm Victor's family. His corruptive nature is employed under Victor's direction for the protection of Imperial personnel, and his known appearances have been peaceful. His precise desires, individual ambitions and capacity for independent judgment beyond the behavior already demonstrated remain unknown.

### Synopsis

#### Volume 9: Those Who Rule Reality
Abhoth's creation occurred during the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s preparations for future conflicts against civilizations originating from the Higher Sectors. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had obtained intelligence concerning the [[Black Eclipse Pantheon]], a Divine organization ruled by the Overlord <Darkness> and supported by an enormous military establishment. Many of its lower- and middle-ranking soldiers were mindless beings functioning similarly to organic machines, allowing the faction to maintain immense numbers of combatants without depending entirely upon conventional supernatural soldiers.[1]
The discovery attracted the interest of [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]], who considered expanding the Empire's use of artificial soldiers. Its existing clones were useful, but she recognized that additional purpose-built combatants could strengthen Imperial forces. She contemplated cloning members of her former race and producing an army of beings capable of controlling advanced technology. [[Amaterasu]] Elderblood, however, identified a dangerous weakness in the proposal. The countless supernatural powers existing throughout Reality made it impossible to guarantee that mindless soldiers, clones or machines would remain immune to corruption and external manipulation. A military system entirely dependent upon such troops could potentially be turned against its own creators.[1]
The Elderblood Empire already possessed powerful and diverse military forces, including clones, Dragons, Leviathans, Eldritch organisms and thousands of Gods. Furthermore, Victor's existing clones benefited from supernatural safeguards established through their connection to his Authority over Souls. Even if a clone were corrupted or forced into betrayal, its body could destroy itself and its Soul could return to Victor. Nevertheless, the Emperor refused to depend entirely upon any single protective mechanism, recognizing that unknown enemies might possess abilities capable of circumventing conventional defenses.[1]
Victor consequently devised another form of protection. Gathering several supernatural powers within his right hand, he requested Azathoth's assistance. She placed her hand above his and added her own power to the formation. Their combined influence was dangerous enough that the women surrounding them instinctively withdrew, understanding that the cooperation of two immensely powerful Eldritch beings could produce unpredictable results. The gathered powers subsequently formed a strange, shapeless organism resembling a slime covered in hundreds of eyes.[1]
The newborn immediately attracted Azathoth's affection. She caressed the creature and welcomed it as a member of her Pantheon, while Victor introduced its purpose to the others. Rather than creating another conventional soldier, he had produced an existence intended to function as living corruption. He named the creature Abhoth, prompting Ruby to recognize the name's association with the mythological source of impurity and progenitor of evil. Azathoth rejected the implication that their newborn creation was already evil, pointing out that he had done nothing to deserve such a characterization.[1]
Abhoth's supernatural function was far more sophisticated than his appearance suggested. Everything coming into contact with him could become corrupted, but the effects of that corruption were subject to Victor's direction. The Emperor could determine its characteristics rather than allowing the process to spread indiscriminately. He intended to distribute fragments of Abhoth among Imperial soldiers, creating an additional protective layer against abilities capable of corrupting or manipulating them.[1]
The principal advantage of Abhoth's protection was its capacity to reverse the danger posed by hostile interference. An enemy attempting to corrupt an Imperial soldier would encounter Abhoth's corruptive influence, potentially becoming corrupted in return. Because Abhoth was directly connected to Victor, the Emperor could also become aware of the attack or an act of betrayal. The fragments could then function as anchors through which Victor reached the opposing party, turning the enemy's attempted intrusion into an opportunity for counter-infiltration and control.[1]
This arrangement effectively established two interconnected defenses. Abhoth's corruption could retaliate against the aggressor, while his connection to Victor provided a means of alerting the Emperor and allowing him to intervene. Victor considered the system extraordinarily difficult to counter because an enemy attempting to compromise his forces could instead expose itself to the attention of a vastly more powerful cosmic being. The Empire's enormous network of [[World Trees]] could support the system by monitoring protected individuals, while Victor's parallel thought processes provided another means of supervising its operation without requiring his full attention.[1]
The defensive implications disturbed several members of Victor's family. Velnorah considered him an exceptionally troublesome enemy, recognizing how easily his abnormal powers could undermine conventional military strategies. [[Scathach]] shared that assessment, while Ruby jokingly compared the arrangement to stories in which an enormously powerful elder appeared whenever the descendants of a sect were threatened. She even wondered whether Abhoth should be addressed as a young master, a humorous suggestion rather than an actual title.[1]
Violet's primary concern was the safety of the people Abhoth would protect. She directly questioned whether incorporating the newborn entity into Imperial personnel posed any danger. Victor confirmed that it was safe, having no intention of recklessly endangering soldiers who fought for him. His existing protections were already designed to prevent their Souls from falling into enemy hands, and Abhoth would supplement those safeguards rather than replace them. Satisfied with the explanation, Violet expanded the proposal beyond military personnel, deciding that Abhoth's protection should be provided to every citizen of the Elderblood Empire.[1]
The resulting design transformed Abhoth from a specialized countermeasure against corrupted soldiers into a potential Empire-wide security system. Instead of existing solely as a powerful individual Eldritch God, his fragments could become components of a vast protective network linked to Victor's consciousness, Soul-related Authority and existing Imperial infrastructure. The full extent of the proposed deployment was not described, but its intended purpose was to protect the Empire's population against hostile corruption, manipulation and betrayal.[1]
Abhoth subsequently appeared during Victor's instruction of his daughters concerning Divinity, Divine Concepts and the conceptual foundations required for a genuine Universe. Victor had created a miniature Eldritch galaxy using his powers associated with Negativity, Beginning and Eldritch existence. Although the construct contained stars and apparently living cosmic horrors, its underlying structure remained incomplete. Without the proper conceptual foundations and genuine Life, the creatures inhabiting the artificial galaxy were convincing replicas rather than independent living beings.[4][3]
To illustrate the distinction, Victor summoned Abhoth and another genuine Eldritch creature into his hand. Both appeared as small elf-like beings and greeted the gathered daughters. Their seemingly harmless manifestations concealed an existential danger that would have driven ordinary observers insane; only the Eldritch characteristics possessed by Victor's daughters allowed them to perceive the creatures safely.[3]
The comparison revealed an essential distinction between Abhoth and the organisms inhabiting Victor's artificial galaxy. Although those replicas possessed recognizable forms and apparently functioned as cosmic organisms, they lacked the deeper foundation necessary for authentic Life. Abhoth, by contrast, possessed genuine Eldritch existence. His status as a truly living being demonstrated that producing a convincing physical or supernatural imitation was not equivalent to creating an independent Life sustained by the appropriate conceptual laws.[3]
The limitations of Victor's artificial galaxy were connected to the greater structure of Creation itself. A stable Universe required conceptual rules established through the authority of a supreme creator, rather than merely the construction of physical objects and the introduction of immense quantities of Energy. Victor understood that combining his powers more directly with Azathoth could potentially produce something much closer to a genuine Eldritch galaxy, but doing so risked attracting the attention of powerful beings from outside their immediate Reality. Abhoth's genuine Life therefore served as an example of the difference between a completed Eldritch existence and a sophisticated but incomplete artificial creation.[3]
After the demonstration, Abhoth and the other summoned creature waved farewell and disappeared. Their brief appearance established that Abhoth was capable of responding directly to Victor, manifesting in a small humanoid-like form and interacting with others without displaying the grotesque features of his original body.[3]
Abhoth's position within the Great [[Chaos]] was further established during the creation of [[Cthugha]], an Eldritch being produced through the experiments of Victor's daughters. Unlike Abhoth, Cthugha was not created directly by Victor and Azathoth. The daughters combined their own powers, Leviathan characteristics, Eldritch influences and the power contained within one of Victor's scales to produce a new divine organism. The resulting existence was classified as a Great Old One rather than an Outer God.[2]
The distinction reflected the hierarchy governing Victor and Azathoth's Eldritch order. Victor and Azathoth occupied the highest position as its creators, followed by genuine [[Outer Gods]] such as their daughters and Abhoth. Lesser Divine creations possessing Divinity belonged to the Great Old Ones, while non-Divine Eldritch organisms formed the servant class beneath them. As one of the genuine Outer Gods produced by the supreme creators, Abhoth occupied a higher level of existence than the newly created Cthugha.[2]
Victor's daughters possessed greater standing than Abhoth within the Pantheon's internal hierarchy because they were direct descendants of the supreme creators. Nevertheless, Abhoth remained a genuine Eldritch God whose power and importance exceeded those of the lesser Eldritch beings Victor had scattered throughout the galaxies. His position established him as a member of the higher Divine establishment of the Great Chaos, distinct from the numerous organisms created for military, environmental and experimental purposes.[2]

### Nature and Classification
Abhoth is a genuine Outer God, an Eldritch existence belonging to the highest Divine class beneath the supreme creators of the Great [[Chaos]]. His classification is determined by the nature of his creation and his position within the Eldritch hierarchy, rather than solely by his physical appearance or the destructive effects of his powers.[2]
The distinction between [[Outer Gods]] and lower Eldritch organisms is fundamental. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Azathoth possess the ability to create genuine Eldritch Gods, whereas lesser Divine beings produced by their descendants are classified as Great Old Ones. Creatures lacking Divinity occupy the servant class. Abhoth belongs to the former category, having been created through the direct combined involvement of Victor and Azathoth.[1][2]
His existence also possesses several unusual characteristics that distinguish him from other creations within the Elderblood family. Although he does not inherit Victor's Essence in precisely the same manner as [[Yog-Sothoth]], his direct connection to the Emperor allows him to act as an extension of Imperial security infrastructure. This connection does not reduce him to a mindless construct, as Abhoth has demonstrated independent responsiveness and possesses genuine Eldritch Life.[1][3]
Abhoth's defining power is innate corruption, but his status as a genuine Outer God does not automatically establish that Corruption is a separately acquired Divine Concept under the native cosmological system. His corruptive property is directly demonstrated, while the precise relationship between that property and an independent Divinity remains unspecified. Likewise, his membership in the same broad class as Victor's Eldritch daughters does not establish that he possesses every ability inherited by those daughters or that his personal combat strength equals theirs.[1][2]

### Eldritch Hierarchy
Abhoth occupies the upper Divine level of the Great [[Chaos]]'s hierarchy, beneath [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Azathoth and below their direct descendants in internal Pantheon precedence. His position is superior to that of the Great Old Ones and lesser Eldritch servants, while the numerous creatures Victor distributed throughout the galaxies are explicitly subordinate to him in power and importance.[2]

### Powers and Abilities
Abhoth's known powers revolve around his Eldritch physiology, innate corruption and direct connection to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Several aspects of the Imperial security system are performed through cooperation between Abhoth and Victor rather than being abilities that Abhoth has independently demonstrated without his creator's involvement.
- Outer God Physiology: Abhoth is a genuine Eldritch God created through the combined powers of Victor and Azathoth. He belongs to the same broad Divine classification as their Eldritch descendants and occupies a superior position to the Great Old Ones and lesser servants of the Great [[Chaos]].[1][2]
- Eldritch Existence: Abhoth possesses an alien form of existence fundamentally different from ordinary native living beings. His creation required the combined Eldritch powers of Victor and Azathoth, and his presence possesses properties dangerous to observers lacking sufficient Eldritch compatibility.[1][3]
- True Eldritch Life: Abhoth possesses genuine Life rather than existing as a simulated organism or an artificial replica lacking conceptual foundations. Victor used his existence as a direct example of authentic Eldritch Life when comparing him with the creatures inhabiting an incomplete artificial galaxy.[3]
- Abstract / Unconventional Physiology: His original body was a shapeless slime-like mass covered in hundreds of eyes, lacking an ordinary humanoid anatomy. This establishes an unconventional Eldritch physiology rather than the physical structure of an ordinary biological organism.[1]
- Variable Manifestation: Abhoth can appear in significantly different physical forms, including his original amorphous manifestation and a much smaller elf-like appearance. His full shapeshifting range has not been established.[1][3]
- Passive Madness Inducement: Abhoth's Eldritch presence is mentally hazardous to ordinary beings. Individuals lacking sufficient Eldritch characteristics can lose their minds merely from perceiving him, while Victor's daughters resist the effect through their inherited Eldritch nature.[3]
- Supernatural Perception Compatibility: Abhoth can be perceived safely by beings possessing sufficient Eldritch characteristics. Victor's daughters were able to examine him directly without suffering the mental consequences that would affect ordinary observers.[3]
- Summoning Response: Abhoth can respond to Victor's call and manifest directly in his presence. He appeared within Victor's hand alongside another Eldritch creature and departed after the demonstration concluded.[3]
- Corruption Manipulation: Abhoth's defining ability is the corruption of whatever comes into contact with him. Victor describes his existence as living corruption, identifying corruption as an innate supernatural property rather than merely a technique learned after his creation.[1]
- Corruption Generation: Abhoth naturally produces a corruptive influence capable of affecting other beings or targets through contact with his existence.[1]
- Contact-Based Corruption: Everything Abhoth touches can become corrupted. This property forms the foundation of his role as an anti-corruption guardian and the security system constructed around his fragments.[1]
- Selective Corruption: The nature of Abhoth's corruption can be configured by Victor, allowing different corruptive effects to be applied according to the requirements of the Empire's defenses.[1]
- Corruption Customization: Victor determines what Abhoth's corruption entails, meaning the corruptive influence is not restricted to one fixed effect. The complete range of possible configurations has not been demonstrated.[1]
- Counter-Corruption: Abhoth's influence can retaliate against attempts to corrupt protected individuals, causing the aggressor to encounter corruption instead of simply overcoming the target's defenses.[1]
- Corruption Redirection: Within Victor's protective arrangement, an enemy's attempt to compromise a protected individual can become the means through which corruptive influence is directed back toward the hostile party.[1]
- Fragmentation / Body Separation: Abhoth's existence can be distributed through fragments capable of retaining the properties required for the Imperial security system. These fragments provide a means of extending his protective influence across enormous numbers of individuals.[1]
- Distributed Presence: Abhoth's fragments are intended to exist within separate protected individuals throughout the Empire, allowing his influence to operate across a vast population without requiring his primary manifestation to remain beside every host.[1]
- Internal Implantation: His fragments can be incorporated into Imperial soldiers and citizens as components of a supernatural defensive system designed to respond to hostile corruption and manipulation.[1]
- Distributed Corruption: Individual fragments can serve as points through which Abhoth's corruptive property participates in the Empire's anti-corruption defenses.[1]
- Supernatural Anchoring: Abhoth's fragments act as anchors through which Victor can maintain a connection to protected individuals and potentially exploit hostile attempts to establish supernatural influence over them.[1]
- Direct Creator Connection: Abhoth possesses a direct connection to Victor Elderblood that differs from the manner in which [[Yog-Sothoth]] carries her father's Essence. This connection is central to his intended role throughout Imperial territory.[1]
- Remote Information Transmission: Through the connection between Abhoth and Victor, hostile attempts to compromise a protected individual can become known to the Emperor without requiring his physical presence.[1]
- Corruption Detection: The security system allows Victor to become aware when an enemy attempts to corrupt one of his protected soldiers.[1]
- Betrayal Detection: The connection is also intended to alert Victor when an individual protected through Abhoth betrays him, extending its usefulness beyond attacks originating from outside the Empire.[1]
- Counter-Infiltration: Abhoth's fragments can convert an attempted hostile connection into a route through which Victor can reach the opposing party, reversing the intended direction of the intrusion.[1]
- Remote Control Conduit: Victor can potentially use Abhoth's fragments to control the party on the opposite side of a hostile connection. The controlling Authority belongs to Victor, while Abhoth supplies the supernatural anchor enabling its application.[1]

- Defensive Corruption / Countermeasure Resistance: Victor regards the system created around Abhoth as exceptionally difficult to counter because attempts to corrupt protected individuals can reveal and expose the aggressor to retaliation. ### Imperial Security System
Abhoth's most important known function is his participation in the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s anti-corruption and counter-infiltration security network. The system was developed to address the possibility that advanced enemies could compromise Imperial personnel through abilities involving corruption, manipulation or other supernatural interference without needing to defeat those individuals in conventional combat.[1]
Unlike ordinary defensive barriers, Abhoth's system is designed not only to resist hostile interference but also to identify and retaliate against the source. Its effectiveness depends upon the interaction between Abhoth's innate corruption, the fragments distributed among protected individuals and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s direct connection to the Eldritch entity.[1]

#### Corruption and Hostile Control
Abhoth's system possesses an important distinction from conventional methods of preventing possession or mind control. Rather than relying entirely upon preventing a hostile supernatural connection from forming, it can make that connection dangerous to the attacker. The aggressor's attempt to compromise a protected individual can consequently become the route through which [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s retaliatory influence reaches them.[1]
This property is particularly important against enemies whose abilities bypass ordinary defenses or manipulate soldiers without directly confronting them. However, the precise range of hostile powers that Abhoth's system can counter has not been exhaustively demonstrated. Its effectiveness against every possible form of conceptual manipulation, possession or corruption remains untested in direct combat within the available record.[1]

#### Connection to Victor
The security network relies upon Abhoth's direct connection to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], who possesses the Authority necessary to determine the effects of its corruption and exploit supernatural connections established through its fragments. Abhoth therefore functions as both an independent living deity and an intermediary through which Victor can extend the reach of his own abilities.[1]
This relationship is distinct from ordinary bloodline inheritance or the direct transmission of Victor's Essence to his descendants. Abhoth does not carry Victor's Essence in the same manner as [[Yog-Sothoth]], but the connection between the two is sufficiently direct to support the Emperor's remote detection and intervention.[1]

#### World Tree Integration
The [[World Trees]] distributed throughout the Empire provide an additional means of supporting Abhoth's protective functions. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] maintains connections with an enormous number of World Trees, allowing their awareness and supernatural infrastructure to contribute to the supervision of Imperial territory.[1]
By combining these connections with portions of his parallel thought processes, Victor can distribute the monitoring workload rather than relying upon one continuous conscious act of observation. The World Trees function as supporting guardians within the proposed security architecture rather than replacing Abhoth's intrinsic corruption or the direct connection to Victor.[1]

#### Deployment and Scope
Abhoth's original intended recipients were Imperial soldiers, particularly those whose corruption or betrayal could create serious military vulnerabilities. Violet subsequently expanded the objective to encompass the entire Imperial population after confirming that the entity would not endanger those receiving his protection.[1]
This decision established an intended Empire-wide deployment, but the precise extent of completed distribution has not been documented. The existence of a plan to integrate Abhoth's fragments into every citizen should therefore be distinguished from a confirmed statement that every individual within the Empire already carries such a fragment.[1]

### Role in the Great Chaos
Abhoth belongs to the highest Divine stratum of the Great [[Chaos]] beneath its supreme creators. His position reflects both the nature of his creation and the importance of his duties within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Azathoth's Eldritch civilization.[2]
The Great Chaos distinguishes genuine [[Outer Gods]] from lesser Divine and non-Divine creations. Victor and Azathoth stand at the summit as the supreme creators, while the Outer Gods produced through their powers form the next major class. Abhoth occupies this class alongside the Emperor's Eldritch descendants, although the daughters possess greater precedence because of their direct familial relationship to the supreme creators.[2]
Beneath this class are the Great Old Ones, such as [[Cthugha]], whose Divine existence resulted from the combined powers of Victor's daughters. The lower Eldritch servants lack Divinity and perform functions assigned by their superior creators. Abhoth's authority and importance extend above these lesser beings, with the Eldritch creatures Victor distributed throughout the galaxies expressly recognized as subordinate to him.[2]
Abhoth's practical role is also distinct from that of many other Eldritch creations. Whereas certain organisms serve as military weapons, guardians of celestial structures or experimental life-forms, Abhoth was specifically designed to protect the Empire against hostile corruption and supernatural infiltration. His existence allows an Outer God to function as the central component of a vast protective network rather than simply operating as an individual combatant.[1]
The arrangement reflects the unique relationship between the Great Chaos and the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Eldritch powers originating outside the ordinary metaphysical framework of the native Cosmos can be incorporated into an organized civilization governed by Victor, allowing the Empire to combine conventional technological development with supernatural protections derived from an entirely different order of existence.[1][2]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Abhoth's co-creator, supreme ruler and the individual to whom his existence possesses a particularly direct supernatural connection. Victor designed Abhoth as a countermeasure against corruption and hostile manipulation, determining the characteristics of his corruptive influence and incorporating his fragments into the Empire's protective infrastructure.[1]
Their relationship extends beyond the ordinary obedience of a created servant. Abhoth's direct connection to Victor allows the Emperor to become aware of hostile interference affecting protected individuals and potentially turn an enemy's own supernatural connection against them. Abhoth's fragments consequently serve as extensions of Imperial security through which Victor can employ his own Authority at locations far removed from his physical body.[1]
Victor also treats Abhoth as a genuine living being rather than merely a disposable construct. He can summon the Eldritch God directly, and Abhoth responds to him through recognizable social gestures. His status as a true Outer God further establishes him among the important creations belonging to Victor and Azathoth's Eldritch order.[3][2]

#### Azathoth
[[Azathoth Elderblood]] is Abhoth's co-creator and one of the two supreme authorities of the Great [[Chaos]]. She directly participated in his formation by combining her powers with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s, producing the newborn Eldritch God through their shared cosmic influence.[1]
Azathoth immediately displayed affection toward Abhoth. She caressed him, welcomed him as a member of her Pantheon and defended him when Ruby reacted negatively to the mythological implications of his name. From Azathoth's perspective, Abhoth's newborn status meant that he should not be judged according to the reputation of a similarly named being from fiction.[1]
Their relationship also establishes his origin within the Great Chaos. Abhoth is a genuine Eldritch God belonging to the class of beings created through the powers of Victor and Azathoth rather than a lesser organism lacking Divinity.[2]

#### Yog-Sothoth
[[Yog-Sothoth]] is one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Azathoth's Eldritch daughters and occupies a higher position than Abhoth within the Great [[Chaos]]'s internal family hierarchy. Unlike Abhoth, Yog carries Victor's Essence through her creation, giving her a fundamentally different relationship with her father's powers.[1][2]
The contrast between the two illustrates that beings created through the same supreme Eldritch powers need not possess identical metaphysical characteristics. Yog carries her father's Essence, whereas Abhoth is distinguished by his direct connection to Victor and his specialization in controlled corruption. Both belong to the genuine Outer-God class, but their individual functions and origins differ considerably.[1]

#### Victor's Eldritch Daughters
The Eldritch daughters of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Azathoth occupy a privileged position within the Great [[Chaos]] because they are direct descendants of its supreme creators. Abhoth belongs to the same general Outer-God class but possesses lower internal precedence within the Pantheon's hierarchy.[2]
This relationship places Abhoth within the upper Divine establishment of the Great Chaos while distinguishing him from the Emperor's direct descendants. The hierarchy concerns their status within the Pantheon and does not independently establish that every daughter possesses greater individual combat capability than Abhoth.[2]

#### Ruby Elderblood Scarlett
[[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] immediately recognized the mythological significance of Abhoth's name and reacted with disbelief when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] revealed his identity. She associated Abhoth with the source of impurity and the progenitor of evil, prompting Azathoth to object to the characterization of their newborn creation.[1]
Ruby also recognized the absurdity of Abhoth's defensive purpose. The prospect of an immensely powerful Eldritch being retaliating whenever Victor's soldiers were threatened reminded her of stories involving powerful ancestral guardians protecting their descendants. She jokingly asked whether Abhoth should be addressed as a young master, a remark that reflected her familiarity with popular fiction rather than an official title bestowed upon him.[1]

#### Violet Elderblood Snow
[[Violet Elderblood Snow]] played an important role in determining the intended scope of Abhoth's protective function. While [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] initially conceived the system as a safeguard for his soldiers, Violet questioned whether the entity could be safely integrated into Imperial personnel.[1]
Satisfied by Victor's assurance that the protection posed no danger to those receiving it, she decided that Abhoth's fragments should be distributed throughout the entire Imperial population. Her decision transformed the security proposal from a military countermeasure into a potential civilization-wide protection system.[1]

#### Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II
[[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]] indirectly contributed to the circumstances surrounding Abhoth's creation through her proposal to expand the Empire's artificially engineered military forces. Her consideration of mindless soldiers and technologically capable clones prompted discussion of the dangers posed by hostile corruption and supernatural manipulation.[1]
Although Velnorah intended to continue developing artificial forces, Abhoth provided a different form of protection. His existence complemented the Empire's technological and military projects by offering an Eldritch countermeasure against forms of interference that conventional machines or programmed safeguards might fail to prevent.[1]

#### Amaterasu Elderblood
[[Amaterasu]] Elderblood identified the vulnerability that made Abhoth's protective function especially valuable. She recognized that artificially produced or mindless soldiers could not be guaranteed absolute immunity against the enormous variety of supernatural powers existing throughout Reality.[1]
Her objections encouraged the Empire to avoid depending exclusively upon mechanically controlled forces and instead make greater use of protections connected to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s exceptional spiritual and Soul-related Authorities. Abhoth subsequently became an important component of that defensive approach.[1]

### Titles and Epithets
- Living Corruption — [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s description of Abhoth's innate supernatural nature and principal function within the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s security infrastructure.[1]
- The Source of All Impurity — A description associated with the mythological being whose name inspired Abhoth's identification. Ruby invoked the association when reacting to his creation; it has not been established as a literal cosmic office belonging to Abhoth.[1]
- The Progenitor of Evil — Another mythological description associated with his name. Azathoth rejected the implication that Abhoth was inherently evil merely because he resembled or shared the name of such a being.[1]
- Little One — An affectionate address used by Azathoth toward Abhoth upon his creation. It is not established as an exclusive personal name; a separate Eldritch creature is also addressed as the Little One.[1][3]
- Young Master — A humorous designation suggested by Ruby after learning that Abhoth could function as a guardian connected to Victor. It is not an established formal rank or title.[1]

### Trivia
- Abhoth was created directly through the combined powers of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Azathoth Elderblood]], making him one of the genuine Eldritch Gods belonging to the Great [[Chaos]].[1][2]
- His original body resembles an amorphous slime covered in hundreds of eyes, but he can also appear as a small elf-like creature.[1][3]
- Abhoth's name references the mythological entity associated with impurity and evil, although Azathoth objected to judging the newborn according to that reputation.[1]
- His defining ability is innate corruption, and Victor can determine the characteristics of the corruption produced through him.[1]
- Unlike [[Yog-Sothoth]] Elderblood, Abhoth does not carry Victor's Essence in the same manner and is instead directly connected to the Emperor.[1]
- Abhoth's fragments can serve as anchors that allow Victor to detect hostile corruption and potentially reverse an attempted infiltration against its source.[1]
- The proposed security network can incorporate the countless [[World Trees]] connected to Victor and portions of his parallel thought processes.[1]
- [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] expanded Abhoth's intended protection from Imperial soldiers to every citizen of the [[Elderblood Empire]].[1]
- Victor considers the defensive arrangement extraordinarily difficult to counter, although an absolute immunity to every conceivable supernatural power has not been independently demonstrated.[1]
- Abhoth's genuine Eldritch Life was used to distinguish authentic living beings from the incomplete replicas inhabiting Victor's artificial Eldritch galaxy.[3]
- His presence can cause madness in beings lacking sufficient Eldritch characteristics, even when he appears in a small and comparatively harmless form.[3]
- During Victor's lesson, the Emperor summoned the Little One and Abhoth as two separate beings. Consequently, the Little One appearing beside Abhoth should not be mistaken for another manifestation of Abhoth himself.[3]
- Abhoth belongs to the Outer-God class, above Great Old Ones such as [[Cthugha]] and the lesser servants of the Great Chaos.[2]
- Victor's Eldritch daughters possess higher internal Pantheon precedence than Abhoth because they are direct descendants of the supreme creators.[2]
- The numerous Eldritch creatures Victor distributed throughout the galaxies are subordinate to Abhoth in both power and importance.[2]
- Abhoth's exact personal destructive capability has not been demonstrated, despite his confirmed status as a genuine Outer God.[2]
- Abhoth's corruption is an established innate power, but a separately named native Divine Concept of Corruption has not been confirmed for him.[1]
