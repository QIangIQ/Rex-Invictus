**[[Outer Gods]]**, also known as Eldritch Gods and External Gods, are Divine Eldritch existences whose nature, Energy and Concepts can originate outside the ordinary conceptual framework of a local Cosmology. (Lore adapted from *My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires*.)

Events of the MTWABV timeline (Victor's conquests, the fall of the pantheons, the [[Elderblood Empire]]) are distant background or rumor unless a chapter puts them on the page. Outer Gods, also known as Eldritch Gods and External Gods, are Divine Eldritch existences whose nature, Energy and Concepts can originate outside the ordinary conceptual framework of a local Cosmology. They are not simply unusually powerful Gods. Their existence can operate according to a foreign metaphysical foundation, allowing their powers to interact with Reality differently from ordinary native Divinities.[1][2][3]
The category includes the foundational Eldritch [[Chaos]] represented by Victor Elderblood and [[Azathoth Elderblood]], Eldritch [[Primordial Gods]] such as [[Yog-Sothoth]] Elderblood and [[Nigredo Elderblood]], and other Eldritch Gods whose Concepts are sustained through Victor's Eldritch structure, such as [[Nero Elderblood]], [[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]] and [[Akasha Elderblood]]. [[Abhoth]] is another true Outer God, created jointly by Victor and Azathoth as an embodiment of controllable living corruption.[4][5][6][7][8][9]
Outer God describes a nature and class of existence rather than a single fixed level of strength. Outer Gods can begin at different stages, can develop through different methods and do not possess an identical set of personal Concepts. An infant Eldritch God, an Eldritch Primordial Goddess and a fully matured Chaos are therefore all Eldritch existences while differing enormously in development, authority and raw power.[1][2][5]
Later developments also establish that not every Eldritch creature is an Outer God. The Great Chaos possesses a broader creation hierarchy. Victor and Azathoth stand at its summit as creators; beneath them are the true Outer Gods or Eldritch Gods; beneath those are Great Old Ones, Divine Eldritch creations; and beneath them are Servants of the Outer Gods, lesser creations without Divinity. [[Cthugha]] is the first explicitly identified Great Old One produced by Victor's daughters.[9]

#### Outer God as an Existential Category
Being an Outer God is not synonymous with merely possessing an Eldritch-flavored ability. Chapter 1171 distinguishes between [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s descendants carrying Eldritch inheritance and the smaller number whose actual Divinities pertain directly to his [[Eldritch Pantheon]]. The daughters are described as possessing mixed Eldritch and Primordial characteristics, but only particular daughters have developed Divinities explicitly belonging to that system.[8]
This distinction becomes even more important by Chapter 1188. Almost all of Victor's daughters possess the potential for an Eldritch Form, although only some can currently access it. Nevertheless, the hierarchy still distinguishes true Outer Gods from lower Eldritch creations. An Eldritch appearance or partial heritage therefore does not, by itself, establish the same rank as a fully recognized Eldritch God.[9]

#### Eldritch Energy
[[Eldritch Energy]] is the fundamental foreign Energy underlying much of Outer-God existence. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later gives one of the clearest explanations of its nature while teaching his daughters: Eldritch Energy comes from outside their universe and can be thought of as something analogous to the attributeless Energy of another universe. Because it does not belong to their native Reality and carries the distorted characteristics of that foreign foundation, using it produces corruption and changes phenomena according to the logic of another universe.[3]
This explains why Eldritch corruption is not simply poison, darkness or uncontrolled mutation. The affected target is being exposed to principles whose foundation does not originate from the same local cosmological system. The resulting distortion can affect matter, Souls, Concepts, biological forms, Reality and the rules according to which those things operate.[15][3]
Victor's daughters all possess some degree of this Energy, although not equally. During his explanation, he specifically notes that some possess more than others, directing particular attention toward Yog and Nigredo.[3]

#### Foreign and Native Existence
Azathoth originates from another Cosmos, making her a genuinely foreign [[Chaos]]. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is fundamentally different: he was born within the native Creation while simultaneously developing an Eldritch existence that increasingly transcends its normal conceptual limitations. This combination gives him an unusual relationship with both systems rather than making him merely another invading foreign Chaos.[16][15]
Yog demonstrates another form of foreign conceptual existence. Her original Concepts of Space, Intangibility and Nullity do not derive their foundation from Victor's native Creation. Consequently, the local Cosmos does not establish their predetermined maximum in the same way that it regulates ordinary native Concepts. Yog herself remains limited by what her own existence can support, but the Concepts are not naturally capped by the foreign universe in which she currently resides.[1]
Foreign origin and acceptance by the local Reality are not mutually exclusive. The Eldritch creatures living in dead galaxies are accepted by the System as legitimate inhabitants of the Cosmos despite the connection of their origin to Azathoth's influence. They participate in the recycling of residual cosmic Energy rather than automatically existing as invaders rejected by Reality.[13]

#### Corruption, Distortion and Contradiction
Corruption is one of the most characteristic manifestations of Eldritch power. Azathoth explains that pure [[Chaos]] does not naturally create ordinary ordered Life. Its creations inherit distorted properties from the power responsible for producing them. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later clarifies the underlying reason: [[Eldritch Energy]] carries the logic of another universe into the local one.[15][3]
This foreign quality makes Eldritch power unusually effective at interfering with the native Cosmos. During research into creating an absolute paradox, Velnorah concludes that affecting the Cosmos with an external force is considerably easier than attempting to violate its Rules through those Rules themselves. Eldritch Force is given as the principal example. Even then, however, the effect is more accurately a distortion than a true destruction of the universe's underlying Rules.[9]
The distinction is important. Eldritch power can bypass or deform systems in ways native abilities struggle to reproduce, but it does not automatically mean that every Eldritch technique completely overrides the entire Cosmos. The universe can still correct contradictions produced inside itself, and sufficiently fundamental structures can resist or adapt to attempted interference.[9]
Contradictory manifestations repeatedly appear within the [[Eldritch Pantheon]]. Akasha possesses a Domain of Control but can manifest its opposite, loss of control. [[Nero]]'s Apocalypse does not interact with Order as a perfectly ordinary native Divinity would. [[Cthugha]], although a Great Old One rather than an Outer God, later demonstrates an especially extreme result: a Being simultaneously carrying a native Star Concept, foreign Eldritch Space, Draconic influence and recognition as the progenitor of Fire Vampires.[17][6][9]
Cthugha is explicitly described as a living contradiction and a hybrid of two universes. Despite this, the native universe does not immediately react against his existence. His creation consequently becomes an important clue for Victor's family while researching whether a sufficiently absolute paradox could threaten higher cosmic structures.[9]

#### Bodies, Souls and Eldritch Forms
Outer Gods possess no universal biological anatomy. Azathoth originally existed as an unidentified mass of [[Chaos]]. [[Abhoth]] was born as a malformed slime-like creature covered in hundreds of eyes. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] possesses a deeper cosmic-horror Self beneath his conventional body, while other members can retain comparatively humanoid forms.[18][5][7]
An Eldritch Form, Nightmare Form or cosmic-horror manifestation is a deeper expression of the Being's Eldritch existence rather than a standardized biological transformation shared identically by every member. Victor's Soul can manifest enormous incomprehensible structures, while Yog and Nigredo later appear in distorted humanoid forms covered in eyes.[5][9]
Chapter 1188 further establishes that Eldritch Forms are broadly inherited among Victor's daughters. Velina recognizes that she possesses one despite not yet knowing how to use it, and the narration states that almost all of Victor's daughters possess such forms while only a limited number can currently access them.[9]
Outer-God appearance is therefore highly variable. An individual may possess a humanoid body for ordinary interaction while their deeper existence expresses itself through masses of eyes, darkness, impossible anatomy, cosmic phenomena or forms that ordinary observers cannot mentally process.[19][20][9]

#### True Eldritch Life
Chapter 1168 provides an explicit distinction between genuine Eldritch Life and a sufficiently convincing artificial reproduction.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] creates an Eldritch galaxy-shaped construct containing organisms that appear and function like cosmic horrors. However, Yog notices that something is fundamentally absent from the construct. Victor confirms that the galaxy and its inhabitants lack the complete conceptual foundation necessary to make them truly independent Life.[21][20]
To demonstrate the distinction, Victor summons the Little One and [[Abhoth]]. Unlike the replicas, these two are actually alive. Their existence contains something deeper than visual form, behavioral simulation or Energy structure. The difference is linked to the conceptual anchoring required for a stable Reality and authentic Life.[20]
Victor states that a stable universe requires conceptual Rules established by a supreme Being comparable to a [[Primordial Chaos]]. His experimental galaxy is therefore a highly realistic imitation rather than an independently complete Reality. He privately acknowledges that combining or merging his power with Azathoth would allow the creation of a genuine Eldritch galaxy, but doing so would be conspicuous enough to attract unwanted attention.[20]

#### Madness and Incomprehensibility
Eldritch Beings can be dangerous to perceive even when they are not attacking. In Chapter 1168, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters can safely observe [[Abhoth]] and the Little One because they are Divine descendants possessing Eldritch traits. The narration states that without those characteristics, simply seeing the two genuine Eldritch creatures could cause them to lose their minds.[20]
Victor's unrestricted presence demonstrates this principle at a much higher level. Chapter 1155 describes an ordinary Mortal encountering his unsuppressed existence as receiving more information than their body and mind can comprehend. The difference is compared to a higher-dimensional Being appearing before a lower-dimensional existence, with an even more severe effect if Victor or Yog fully manifests an Eldritch Form.[19]
This is not merely fear produced by grotesque appearance. The danger comes from the information and existential complexity contained in what the observer is attempting to comprehend.[19][20]

#### Abstract and Higher Existence
An Outer God's real existence is not necessarily identical to the physical body through which it interacts with ordinary Reality. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] recognizes the incomprehensible entity within himself as his True Self, while his humanoid and Draconic bodies remain expressions through which that deeper existence acts. Azathoth likewise begins as [[Chaos]] rather than a conventional biological organism.[22][23][18]
The narrative repeatedly uses dimensional comparisons to communicate these existential differences. These comparisons should not be reduced to a simple numbered-dimensional statistic. Chapter 1168 treats dimensions, pure Energy states, conceptual existence and cosmic consciousness as increasingly abstract examples for teaching how Reality possesses different existential weights and foundations.[20]
Azathoth describes the advanced maturation of Eldritch Chaos as eventually producing an existence its original Reality can no longer properly contain. In simplified terms this is described as becoming a higher-dimensional or Multiversal Being. This transcendent state is an advanced result of maturation, not a baseline automatically possessed by every newly born Outer God.[2][14]

This hierarchy should not be confused with the older terms Eldritch Chaos, Eldritch Primordial God and Eldritch God. Those terms describe differences within the Outer-God side of the system, including developmental stage and conceptual condition. The Chapter 1188 hierarchy instead describes the relationship between the creators, true Outer Gods and their subordinate creations.[5][9]

#### Creators
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Azathoth occupy a unique position above their created Pantheon. When Victor observes his daughters creating [[Cthugha]], he explicitly explains that the newborn is not an Eldritch God because only Victor and Azathoth can create beings of that class.[9]
This statement concerns direct creation of genuine Eldritch Gods. Earlier chapters demonstrate that existing Beings can also be transformed into Eldritch Goddesses through changes to their conceptual foundation, as occurred with [[Nero]] and Ophis. Direct creation and transformation therefore represent distinct routes into Eldritch existence.[4][2][9]

#### Outer Gods / Eldritch Gods
The true Outer Gods form the highest created level beneath [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Azathoth. Chapter 1188 includes Victor's daughters and [[Abhoth]] within this layer. Although Abhoth is a true Outer God, Victor's daughters possess a higher position inside the Pantheon's family hierarchy because they are direct descendants of the creators.[9]
The classification does not mean every daughter possesses the same maturity or specialized Eldritch Divinity. Chapter 1171 distinguishes their universal Eldritch heritage from those daughters whose actual Deities directly pertain to the [[Eldritch Pantheon]].[8]

#### Great Old Ones
Great Old Ones are Divine Eldritch creations below the Outer Gods. The defining distinction provided in Chapter 1188 is the possession of Divinity without reaching the status of a true Eldritch God.[9]
[[Cthugha]] is the first named example. He was collectively created by several of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters using Eldritch creatures, Leviathan characteristics, Victor's scale and portions of their own powers. Despite the enormous amount of supernatural influence involved, Victor explicitly classified the resulting Being as a Great Old One rather than an Outer God.[9]
Cthugha immediately demonstrates that the class can possess extraordinary conceptual complexity. He simultaneously embodies the native Concept of a star, foreign Eldritch Space, Draconic influence and the status of progenitor of a new Fire Vampire race. As a newborn he already possesses enough power to destroy a Solar System, with further growth expected as he ages.[9]

#### Servants of the Outer Gods
Below the Great Old Ones are the non-Divine Servants of the Outer Gods. These are lesser beings produced by superior Eldritch existences without receiving Divinity.[9]
This formal category should not automatically be applied retroactively to every previously encountered Eldritch organism. Creatures from the dead galaxies, altered Imperial cosmic organisms and the Little One were introduced before the formal hierarchy and can be described safely as lesser Eldritch creatures unless their exact hierarchical status is established.[13][24][9]

### Origin and Development

#### Natural Formation and Self-Understanding
Azathoth emerged from tangled [[Chaos]] without an initially developed identity. Her Essence spread as she sought Energy and information, and lesser Beings exposed to it could descend into madness. The accumulation of memories and experiences gradually produced self-awareness and personality.[18][16]
Contact with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] greatly accelerated her psychological and spiritual development. This illustrates that the maturation of an Outer God is not purely a matter of accumulating Energy. Physical growth, spiritual maturity, comprehension, identity and control can develop at different rates.[16]

#### Creation through Combined Essence
Azathoth combined a fragment of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Essence with her own in order to create [[Yog-Sothoth]]. Yog therefore emerged as a direct child of two [[Chaos]] Beings and possessed a much deeper similarity to Victor than the lesser Eldritch organisms he had previously encountered.[16][25]
Her foreign Concepts manifested immediately and reacted to her emotions. Victor had to interact with her using his deeper Eldritch nature in order to properly calm and understand the newborn.[25][1]
[[Abhoth]] represents another direct creation. Victor gathered several powers together while Azathoth amplified the process with her own power, creating a true External God whose nature was living corruption. Unlike Yog, Abhoth does not carry Victor's Essence in the same manner; instead, he possesses a direct connection to Victor that makes him especially useful as an Imperial security network.[7]

#### Direct Creation
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] can independently create Beings whose existence is connected to his conceptual structure. [[Akasha Elderblood]] was created as his daughter and as the guardian of his civilization, possessing an Eldritch Domain of Control connected directly to Victor rather than an ordinary native Primordial source.[26][6]
Chapter 1188 later establishes an important limit to subordinate creation. Victor's daughters can produce Divine Eldritch Beings such as [[Cthugha]], but Victor identifies those creations as Great Old Ones rather than true Outer Gods. Direct production of an Eldritch God remains associated with Victor and Azathoth.[9]

#### Transformation and Conceptual Source Replacement
An individual does not necessarily need to be born as an Outer God. Existing Divine Beings can have the source supporting their Concepts replaced or transformed.
During [[Nero]]'s awakening, her Soul fills with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Nightmare Essence and the emerging Concept of Apocalypse becomes tied to him rather than to the native conceptual system. Ophis's awakening provides an even more deliberate example: Victor severs the native Primordial of Infinity's influence and replaces the Concept's Energy source with his own Eldritch foundation.[4]
This process is easier during the birth or awakening of a Divinity than after a mature God has already become deeply integrated with Creation. Azathoth explains that an established Goddess continually receives immense conceptual Energy from the Cosmos. Replacing the foundation means assuming responsibility for the continuing Energy demand of the entire Divine existence, not merely paying a one-time transformation cost.[2][27]
Victor's family is unusually compatible with such development because his descendants already carry his Essence or Eldritch characteristics. His wives also gradually accumulate [[Eldritch Energy]] through their connection with him. Chapter 1188's narration states that Victor's wives eventually become Eldritch Goddesses like his daughters.[2][15][9]

#### Inherited Eldritch Nature
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s descendants naturally inherit varying amounts of Eldritch characteristics. Chapter 1171 describes his daughters as possessing both Eldritch and Primordial aspects, although only some have developed Deities directly connected with the [[Eldritch Pantheon]].[8]
This inheritance affects both body and Soul. By Chapter 1188, almost all of his daughters are stated to possess Eldritch Forms, even though only a smaller number currently know how to manifest them.[9]
Inherited characteristics can combine with other lineages rather than replacing them. Victor's daughters may simultaneously possess True Dragon physiology, native Divinities, Primordial potential and Eldritch traits. This produces hybrid existences whose powers cannot always be described through a single species classification.[28][8][9]

#### Eldritch Primordial Development
The existence of native Primordial offices does not appear to prevent an Eldritch equivalent from developing through a foreign conceptual structure.
While explaining Primordial Beings to his daughters, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] states that a descendant could theoretically become an Eldritch Primordial embodiment of a Concept rather than occupying the corresponding native Primordial office. The result would essentially perform a comparable role while belonging to a different universal foundation.[3]
This is particularly important when a native universe already possesses a Primordial embodiment of the same Concept. The normal native restriction against two Primordial Beings occupying the identical cosmic office does not necessarily prevent the existence of an Eldritch equivalent belonging to another cosmological framework.[3]
Victor further explains that this path is easier for descendants already possessing Eldritch characteristics in both Soul and body. The Soul is identified as the crucial factor.[3]

#### Consumption and Maturation
Outer Gods can continue developing through the acquisition of immense quantities of Energy. Azathoth describes Divinities, stars, black holes, planets and other cosmic sources as nourishment relevant to the growth of a developing [[Chaos]].[2][15]
At a sufficiently advanced stage, a Chaos can consume other Cosmoses and enlarge its own existence. The scale of a Chaos is tied to the scale and development of the reality associated with it.[15]
The author's auxiliary explanation describes Outer Gods as having no conventional fixed ceiling comparable to ordinary native species. A mature Outer God can progress beyond the level at which the native Primordials can readily stop it and can approach or attain Multiversal existence.[14]
This potential must not be confused with the strength of every newborn member. [[Abhoth]], [[Nero]] during her transformation and Yog at birth all demonstrate that developmental stage remains important even among beings possessing extraordinary potential.[25][5][7]

### Eldritch Creation and Reality

#### Conceptual Anchoring
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s experiment with an artificial Eldritch galaxy demonstrates that producing matter, Energy, stars and apparently living organisms is not sufficient to create a completely independent Reality.
A stable universe requires conceptual Rules and an underlying foundation established by a sufficiently high existence. Victor's artificial galaxy therefore resembles a genuine one but remains an extraordinarily sophisticated imitation because its Life and Reality lack the complete anchor necessary to exist independently.[21][20]
Victor privately acknowledges that a merger of his power with Azathoth could produce a genuine Eldritch galaxy. He avoids doing so because such an act would effectively announce their activity to higher external powers.[20]

#### Genuine and Simulated Life
The artificial cosmic horrors within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s galaxy can reproduce the visible and functional characteristics of Eldritch organisms but remain fundamentally different from genuine entities such as [[Abhoth]] and the Little One.[20]
This establishes multiple levels of creation:
- Simulation / Replica: A construct can imitate the form and behavior of Eldritch Life without possessing complete independent Life.[20]
- Lesser Genuine Eldritch Life: Creatures such as the Little One are genuinely alive and possess their own supernatural nature without necessarily possessing Divinity.[24][20]
- Great Old One: A genuinely Divine subordinate Eldritch creation such as [[Cthugha]].[9]
- Outer God / Eldritch God: A true Divine Eldritch existence occupying the rank above Great Old Ones.[9]
- Eldritch [[Chaos]] / Creator: The foundational existence capable of creating true Eldritch Gods and supporting the larger Pantheon.[2][9]

#### Influencing Native Reality
Because [[Eldritch Energy]] originates from outside the native universal framework, it is unusually effective at changing local Reality. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] describes it as analogous to the attributeless Energy of another universe, while Velnorah independently observes that external power can affect the Cosmos more easily than native systems attempting to break themselves from within.[3][9]
Nevertheless, the native universe possesses corrective behavior. Contradictions generated entirely through its own Rules are automatically reconciled rather than developing into absolute paradoxes. Even Eldritch Energy generally causes distortion rather than effortlessly deleting the entire system's foundations.[9]

#### Cross-Cosmological Hybridization
[[Cthugha]] demonstrates that foreign and native conceptual principles can coexist within one created existence. He carries the native Concept of a star and foreign Eldritch Space while also inheriting Draconic characteristics through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s scale and being recognized as the progenitor of Fire Vampires.[9]
Victor describes this mixture as a hybrid of two universes and a walking contradiction. The native universe nevertheless fails to produce an obvious reaction against him, making Cthugha an important example of successful cross-cosmological conceptual integration.[9]

### Eldritch Language and Runes
The Eldritch Language is distinct from Draconic, Divine and System languages. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] uses it instinctively when communicating with compatible Eldritch organisms. Its information and sound are foreign to ordinary listeners and can carry mentally hazardous characteristics.[33][13]
Eldritch Runes provide a written and structural expression of the same foreign system. Victor manifests unknown violet symbols associated with dangerous information and later incorporates Eldritch writing into secrecy agreements, mental restrictions and the supernatural recognition of the Elderblood surname.[33][19][7]
Eldritch and Draconic systems can also be combined. [[Scathach]]'s Heptagrammakos uses a Draconic-Eldritch Runic structure that transforms the surrounding arena into darkness filled with eyes and mouths, suppresses movement and spells, and places considerable mental strain on observers.[34]

### Skills & Abilities
The abilities below are indexed from demonstrated feats and statements belonging to individual Outer Gods, Eldritch [[Chaos]], transformed Goddesses and closely related descendants. An ability possessed by one member is not automatically a universal racial ability.

#### Common Eldritch Characteristics
- Eldritch Physiology: Outer Gods possess an existence shaped by foreign, chaotic or extracosmological principles. Their bodies and manifestations vary enormously and need not obey ordinary biological anatomy.[18][5][7]
- [[Eldritch Energy]] Manipulation: Their power can utilize Energy originating outside the native universe. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] compares it to the attributeless Energy of another universe whose foreign logic produces corruption and distortion when applied locally.[3]
- Abstract / Conceptual Existence: Victor's True Self, Azathoth's [[Chaos]] and Yog's foreign Concepts demonstrate existences whose fundamental identity is deeper than ordinary physical anatomy.[22][23][18][1]
- Conceptual Manipulation: Eldritch Gods possess or sustain Concepts such as Apocalypse, Infinity, Control, Space, Nullity and living Corruption.[1][4][6][7]
- Reality Warping: Eldritch presence and Concepts can distort the functioning of Reality. Yog affects Space and Reality through emotion, Victor creates Eldritch cosmic structures and Moira's inherited Eldritch Luck forces Reality to reorganize itself in her favor.[25][21][31]
- Corruption Inducement: Eldritch Energy can distort the nature of whatever it influences according to foreign metaphysical logic. [[Abhoth]] represents a directly controlled form of this principle.[15][7][3]
- Madness Inducement: The existence or information contained within an Eldritch Being can destabilize observers incapable of comprehending it. Genuine Eldritch creatures can induce madness merely by being seen, while greater manifestations can kill through information overload.[19][20]
- Fear Inducement: Weaker Beings can experience instinctive terror before vastly superior Eldritch existences such as Victor or War's Chaos-derived presence.[10][12]
- Aura: Eldritch influence can act passively through presence. Yog's emotions distort the surroundings, [[Nero]]'s newly awakened Apocalypse must be suppressed and Azathoth's anger can reduce the mental stability of nearby observers.[25][5][35]
- Shapeshifting / Form Variation: Outer Gods need not possess one fixed anatomy. Victor possesses humanoid, Draconic and cosmic-horror expressions; Azathoth is inherently formless; Abhoth changes from a malformed many-eyed slime to a small elf-like manifestation.[5][7][20]
- Eldritch Form: Deeper cosmic-horror forms reveal a greater expression of the Being's true nature. Almost all of Victor's daughters are later stated to possess such forms even though only some can currently manifest them.[9]
- Supernatural Perception: Eldritch Beings can recognize Essence, conceptual structure and differences between genuine and simulated supernatural existence.[25][20]
- Cross-Cosmological Compatibility: Eldritch principles can coexist with native Draconic, Divine and conceptual systems, producing mixed Beings such as Victor's daughters and hybrid creations such as [[Cthugha]].[8][9]
- Resistance to Madness: Compatible Eldritch Beings can safely perceive forms that would destroy the sanity of ordinary observers.[25][20]
- Potential for Higher-Dimensional Development: Mature Chaos can progress toward a state beyond the ordinary framework of its original Reality and eventually become Multiversal.[2][14]

- Supernatural Perception: Azathoth can perceive the conceptual Energy exchange sustaining native Divinity, while Victor examines Souls, Concepts and the deeper construction of Reality.[27][21]

### Lesser Eldritch Creatures
Not every cosmic horror produced by Eldritch influence is an Outer God. The dead-galaxy ecosystem establishes a broad population of lesser organisms whose shapes, feeding habits and powers are Eldritch without possessing Divine status.[13][24]
- Amorphous Physiology: These creatures can appear as liquid darkness, collections of tentacles, mouths or eyes rather than conventional organisms.[13]
- Shapeshifting: Their bodies can change shape or divide into multiple independent forms.[13][24]
- Energy Consumption: Many feed upon residual Energy produced by dying planets, stars and galaxies.[13][24]
- Spatial Rupture: Creatures answering [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s command physically tear open a portal to increase its size.[13]
- Fragmentation: Individual organisms can divide themselves into multiple bodies or components.[24]
- Corruption Inducement — the Little One: Its ability, Impregnation, saturates targets with a selected corruptive negative effect, commonly mental exhaustion.[24]
- Environmental Connection — the Little One: Victor selects it partly because its age has given it a meaningful connection with the dead-galaxy environment.[24]
- Astronomical Dormancy: Victor later enhances Eldritch creatures for Imperial defense and removes their need for continuous ordinary feeding, allowing them to remain hidden in asteroids, stellar environments and black holes.[39]
- Hybridization: Yog, Nigredo and their sisters later experiment with combining lesser Eldritch Life with Leviathan characteristics and other powers.[9]
- Environmental Adaptation: The resulting experimental fish can survive Space, black holes and other extreme environments through the abilities contributed by Victor's daughters.[9]
The formal term Servant of the Outer Gods is introduced later for non-Divine creations beneath the Great Old Ones. Previously introduced natural or altered Eldritch creatures should remain under the broader lesser-creature category unless the story explicitly places them within that formal hierarchy.[9]

### Great Old Ones
Great Old Ones are a newly established Divine rank beneath the true Outer Gods. Unlike Servants of the Outer Gods, they possess Divinity; unlike true Outer Gods, they are subordinate creations and do not occupy the same existential rank as the Eldritch Gods directly associated with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Azathoth.[9]
[[Cthugha]] is the first explicitly named Great Old One. He was created collectively by several of Victor's daughters using Eldritch organisms, Leviathan characteristics, Victor's Draconic scale and portions of the daughters' own powers.[9]
His creation demonstrates that Great Old Ones can become extremely complex independent supernatural life. Cthugha possesses the Concept of a star native to the current universe together with Eldritch Space belonging to a foreign universal foundation. He is simultaneously recognized as the progenitor of Fire Vampires and possesses Draconic influence through Victor's scale.[9]
As a newborn, Cthugha already possesses enough power to destroy a Solar System. Victor expects his capabilities to become substantially more extreme as he matures.[9]
His first stated desire is to serve the Great [[Chaos]], and Victor assigns him a task worthy of his position directed toward future enemies attempting to invade Victor's galaxy.[9]

### Limitations and Vulnerabilities
- Developmental Stage: Outer Gods begin at different levels of maturity. Vast potential does not make a newborn member equal to a completed [[Chaos]].[1][2]
- Energy Requirements: Growth can require enormous amounts of Energy, including Divinities and astronomical sources.[2][15]
- Self-Understanding: Accumulating Energy does not replace the need for identity, comprehension and control.[16]
- Conceptual Source Requirements: Transforming a mature native Goddess requires taking responsibility for the continuing conceptual Energy that sustains her existence.[27]
- Uncontrolled Influence: Eldritch powers can activate through emotion and endanger allies or the environment. [[Nero]], Yog, Azathoth and future members all demonstrate the importance of restraint.[25][5][35][32]
- Compatibility Differences: Possessing some Eldritch heritage does not make every descendant equally resistant to every stronger Eldritch manifestation.[10][8]
- Superior Counterforce: Eldritch powers can resist ordinary counters but can still be suppressed by sufficiently superior opposing power.[17]
- Native Reality Still Reacts: Foreign [[Eldritch Energy]] makes interference easier but generally distorts the native system rather than automatically deleting its fundamental Rules.[9]
- Universal Self-Correction: Contradictions generated within the native universe can be automatically reconciled by its underlying structure, complicating attempts to create an absolute paradox.[9]
- Imprisonment: Azathoth demonstrates that an Eldritch Chaos can still be sealed by sufficiently powerful native Primordial forces.[2][15]
- Assimilation: Another Chaos can potentially consume and incorporate a Chaos rather than killing it through conventional means.[2]
- Immature Ego Vulnerability: Destroying or damaging a developing consciousness remains distinct from destroying the deeper cosmic phenomenon supporting it.[2]
- Danger to Allies: An Outer God's true presence can be mentally or physically lethal to beings lacking sufficient compatibility.[19][20]
- Extracosmological Hazards: Travel between Cosmoses exposes a Being to other foreign powers and requires sufficient development to survive.[15]

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s conflict with War begins during the conquest of Hell. War's willingness to confront him as a warrior eventually develops into an unusual relationship, and the former Horseman later becomes the Progenitor of Victor's [[Chaos]]-derived Herald race.[47][10]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s deeper Nightmare existence becomes increasingly visible after his Divine advancement. War gathers former enemies and useful servants for judgment, transforming Thomas, Jimmy, Lucy and Karen into Heralds. Their confrontation with Victor's cosmic-horror manifestation reveals an existence whose presence can consume, distort and create while forcing allies to protect less compatible observers from its effects.[37][10]
The future chapters reveal that Victor's completed self has already developed far beyond this stage. He operates outside ordinary history, protects his own records and later fights foreign beings alongside an Eldritch family containing Azathoth, Yog, [[Nyarlathotep]], [[Shub-Niggurath]] and [[Cthulhu]].[44][43][32]
At the conclusion of the volume, Azathoth's history and true relationship with Victor are revealed. She recognizes that he is developing into the same fundamental class of [[Chaos]] existence as herself. Unable to physically reach him, she combines their Essences to create [[Yog-Sothoth]]. Yog arrives before Victor carrying foreign Concepts whose foundation does not belong to his native Creation.[18][16][25][1]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
[[Nero]] and Ophis awaken Divinities connected to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Eldritch structure. Nero's Apocalypse naturally fills with Victor's Nightmare Essence, while Victor deliberately removes the native source from Ophis's Infinity and replaces it with his own power. Azathoth explains that Victor is becoming an Eldritch [[Chaos]] capable of sustaining an independent conceptual Pantheon.[4][2][27]
Nero's completed transformation allows Victor to examine the difference between a relatively defined Eldritch Goddess and his own formless Chaos. The family begins distinguishing Eldritch Gods, Eldritch Primordials and the broader developmental potential of the Pantheon. Victor subsequently creates Akasha as an independent daughter possessing the contradictory Eldritch Domain of Control.[5][26][6]
Hela later introduces Victor to the ecosystem of Eldritch creatures inhabiting dead galaxies. Victor's unrestricted presence forces the organisms to recognize him as a superior existence. He selects the Little One because of its ambition, corruptive ability and deep connection to that environment.[13][24]
Azathoth later explains foreign Cosmoses, the spaces separating them, the development of Chaos and the possibility that Victor's unique native origin may allow him to occupy the vacant Chaos position of his own Creation rather than behaving solely as an external predator.[15]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
The [[Elderblood Empire]] begins integrating altered Eldritch creatures into its astronomical defense system. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Azathoth strengthen the organisms while Victor modifies their feeding requirements so that they can remain dormant throughout Imperial territory without threatening its population.[39]
Victor's unrestricted presence increasingly resembles an existence beyond ordinary comprehension. Mortal observation can cause information overload, while his Eldritch manifestation becomes more dangerous than his already overwhelming higher-dimensional presence.[19]
After obtaining intelligence concerning the [[Black Eclipse Pantheon]], Victor and Azathoth create [[Abhoth]]. The newborn Outer God functions as living corruption. Victor intends to distribute fragments of Abhoth throughout his soldiers and citizens so that corruption, betrayal or infiltration automatically reveals the hostile actor and can become a route for counterattack.[7]
Yog later demonstrates Unreality and Nothingness capable of invalidating local Rules, while the younger Elderblood generation reveals increasingly complex mixtures of Draconic, Primordial and Eldritch inheritance.[28][21]
Victor creates an artificial Eldritch galaxy while teaching his daughters. The construct successfully reproduces appearances and functions but lacks genuine Life and the complete conceptual anchor necessary for an independent universe. Abhoth and the Little One are summoned as examples of genuine Eldritch Life. Victor privately acknowledges that combining himself with Azathoth would allow them to create a true Eldritch galaxy.[21][20]
Chapter 1171 further distinguishes inherited Eldritch nature from an actual [[Eldritch Pantheon]] Divinity. Victor's daughters possess mixed Eldritch and Primordial characteristics, but only certain daughters have developed Deities explicitly belonging to the Eldritch side of their lineage. Nigredo is identified as an Eldritch Primordial Goddess.[8]
Moira's birth demonstrates another form of inherited Eldritch abnormality. Her Eldritch Luck causes Reality to aggressively favor her through passive alterations, spontaneous creation of Eldritch defenders and direct removal of threats. The System later discovers that it cannot penetrate Victor's Eldritch Soul to observe her.[31][35]
During later instruction, Victor finally gives a direct explanation of [[Eldritch Energy]]. He describes it as a foreign force from outside the universe, comparable to the attributeless Energy of another universe. Because it belongs to a different universal foundation, it corrupts and distorts local Reality according to foreign logic. All of his daughters possess some quantity of this Energy, although the concentration varies between individuals.[3]
Victor additionally explains that Eldritch development can produce equivalents to native Primordial states. A descendant may become an Eldritch Primordial embodiment of a Concept without occupying the native universe's corresponding Primordial office, allowing conceptual structures belonging to different cosmologies to coexist.[3]
By Chapter 1188, Victor's daughters have begun deliberately experimenting with Eldritch Life. Yog and Nigredo create hybrid fish capable of surviving Space and black holes, while Leone contributes extraordinary adaptability. Several sisters subsequently combine their powers with Eldritch organisms, Leviathan traits and one of Victor's scales.[9]
The experiment produces [[Cthugha]]. Victor uses the event to explain the hierarchy of the Great [[Chaos]] for the first time. Victor and Azathoth stand at the summit as creators. Beneath them are true Outer Gods or Eldritch Gods. Beneath those are Great Old Ones possessing Divinity, followed by Servants of the Outer Gods that lack Divinity.[9]
Victor explicitly states that his daughters did not create an Eldritch God because only he and Azathoth can directly create such beings. Cthugha is instead classified as a Great Old One.[9]
Cthugha emerges as a living contradiction combining the Concept of a star from the native universe with Eldritch Space belonging to an alien universal foundation, Draconic influence from Victor's scale and recognition as the progenitor of Fire Vampires. Despite this cross-cosmological contradiction, the universe does not react against him.[9]
The event also reveals further information concerning Victor's family. Yog and Nigredo can openly manifest grotesque Eldritch Forms, almost all of Victor's daughters possess latent forms of their own, and the narration states that Victor's wives eventually become Eldritch Goddesses as well.[9]

### Trivia
- Outer God, Eldritch God and External God are used as overlapping terms, but the story also employs more specific internal descriptions such as Eldritch [[Chaos]] and Eldritch Primordial God.[2][5]
- Outer God is not a fixed power level. Individual members can begin at radically different developmental stages.[5]
- [[Eldritch Energy]] is explicitly described as a force from outside the native universe and compared to the attributeless Energy of another universe.[3]
- The corruption associated with Eldritch power is linked to foreign universal logic rather than merely ordinary biological contamination.[3]
- Almost all of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters eventually possess Eldritch Forms, although only some can currently manifest them.[9]
- Possessing Eldritch heritage does not automatically mean that every Elderblood daughter has developed a Divinity belonging specifically to the [[Eldritch Pantheon]].[8]
- Genuine Eldritch Life possesses a deeper conceptual foundation than a convincing artificial replica.[20]
- Victor can simulate an Eldritch galaxy by himself, but states internally that a genuine one could be created through the combined power of himself and Azathoth.[20]
- [[Abhoth]] and the Little One are separate Beings and appear together during Victor's lesson concerning genuine Eldritch Life.[20]
- Chapter 1188 resolves Abhoth's previously unclear hierarchy by explicitly placing him among the true Eldritch Gods beneath Victor and Azathoth.[9]
- Victor's daughters possess greater internal Pantheon standing than Abhoth because they are direct descendants of the creators.[9]
- The lesser Eldritch beings Victor has scattered throughout the galaxies are explicitly subordinate to Abhoth in power and importance.[9]
- Chapter 1188 introduces Great Old Ones as a distinct Divine rank below true Outer Gods.[9]
- [[Cthugha]] is the first explicitly named Great Old One and the first major creation collectively produced by Victor's daughters.[9]
- Great Old Ones possess Divinity; Servants of the Outer Gods are defined as lesser creations without Divinity.[9]
- Cthugha demonstrates that native and Eldritch Concepts from different universal foundations can coexist within one created Being.[9]
- Cthugha possesses Solar-System-destroying power immediately after birth despite being hierarchically below an Outer God.[9]
- The Great Chaos hierarchy and developmental classifications should not be conflated: Eldritch Primordial God describes a form of Outer-God development, while Great Old One describes a subordinate class beneath Outer Gods.[5][9]
- The names of several Eldritch Beings are inspired by Lovecraftian fiction, but their actual powers, relationships and cosmological positions are determined by the novel's own setting.[7][32]
- Azathoth's future habit of addressing Victor as her father does not replace her established role as the mother of their shared Eldritch descendants.[32]
- The future defensive structure surrounding the [[Akashic Records]] contains multiple generations and classes of Eldritch Life rather than consisting exclusively of Outer Gods.[32][9]
