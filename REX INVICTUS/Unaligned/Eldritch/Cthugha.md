Cthugha is a newly created Great Old One belonging to the developing Eldritch hierarchy surrounding [[Victor Elderblood]] and his family. It was accidentally produced by several of Victor's daughters during an experiment combining their individual powers with Eldritch influences, Leviathan characteristics and the Draconic power contained within one of Victor's scales.[1]
Unlike a true Outer God, Cthugha is classified as a Great Old One: a Divine Eldritch being created by an Outer God or by beings descended from one. Victor described Cthugha as a walking contradiction because its existence combines properties originating from two different universes. It simultaneously possesses the native Concept of a Star, an alien form of Eldritch Space, Draconic influence derived from Victor and recognition by the current universe as the Progenitor of an entirely new Vampire race — the Fire Vampires.[1]
Despite having only just been born, Cthugha already possessed sufficient power to destroy an entire Solar System, with the narration explicitly stating that this strength would become even more extreme as it matured.[1]

### Appearance
Cthugha's initial form is completely inhuman. Upon emerging from the experimental river in which it was created, it appeared as a sphere of pure plasma covered in numerous eyes. No humanoid or alternative form has currently been shown.[1]
Its appearance reflects the contradictory mixture involved in its birth. Although its body visually resembles an astronomical or stellar phenomenon, its existence simultaneously carries an unmistakably Eldritch nature inherited from the Outer God influences used during its creation.[1]

### Personality
Very little of Cthugha's individual personality has currently been revealed because it was introduced immediately after its creation. It was nevertheless born fully sapient and capable of communicating with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] without needing to be educated in basic language or social understanding.[1]
Its first independently stated desire was to serve the Great [[Chaos]] in the best possible way. This immediately established Cthugha as loyal to Victor and the greater Eldritch hierarchy surrounding him rather than as an uncontrolled monster created by the experiment.[1]

### Synopsis

#### Those Who Rule Reality
During the later portion of the great Imperial temporal ritual, [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]], [[Aline Elderblood Valefar]], [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]] and [[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]] were attempting to discover a method of creating an absolute paradox capable of threatening the Primordial Beings. Their experiments repeatedly failed because contradictions created using the laws of the universe were simply corrected by the universe itself. Even isolated dimensions remained subject to the fundamental structure of the same Reality.[1]
Velina eventually returned home and discovered [[Yog-Sothoth]] Elderblood and [[Nigredo Elderblood]] experimenting with new Eldritch life forms. The two had already produced bizarre fish-like organisms combining Eldritch characteristics with those of the Leviathans. These creatures could survive environments that should ordinarily be impossible for conventional life: they could move through Space and survive within black holes.[1]
Several of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s other daughters soon became involved. Yog-Sothoth Elderblood, Nigredo Elderblood, [[Valentina Victoria Elderblood]], Leone Luna Elena Elderblood, [[Hinata Elderblood]], [[Silvia Elderblood]], [[Rosemary Lavina Elderblood]], [[Stella Elderblood]] and Velina gathered around the experiment and began discussing what additional properties they could place within the developing life form. Rather than stopping her sisters, Velina eventually became just as interested as they were.[1]
Valentina proposed introducing Dragon Fire, Silvia contributed Dragon Lightning, Rosemary offered Ice, Hinata introduced the power of the Sun, Stella contributed her power associated with Truth, and Velina deliberately supplied Intelligence. Leone had already contributed abilities centered upon extreme adaptability and existing in hostile environments, while Yog and Nigredo supplied the experiment with its powerful Eldritch foundation. The sisters ultimately agreed to contribute portions of their respective powers together.[1]
The experiment was further empowered by one of Victor's scales. The scale contained enough of his Draconic power to become an extraordinarily potent source material and was combined with the various Eldritch and Leviathan influences already present within the experiment.[1]
[[Aphrodite]] Elderblood returned while the experiment was underway and immediately compared the sight to watching the creation of Pandora during ancient Greece. Before she could properly stop her daughters, they fled with the remaining Leviathans, Victor's scales and the lesser Eldritch creature they had been using. The river nevertheless began boiling after their departure, indicating that their combined experiment had already successfully created something new.[1]
Victor had deliberately allowed the experiment to continue. He explained to Aphrodite that he had been observing everything and would have intervened if the daughters had actually placed themselves in danger. He also clarified that they had not created another true Outer God. According to Victor, only foundational beings such as himself and [[Azathoth Elderblood]] possessed the capacity to directly produce existences at that level.[1]
The event consequently revealed a more detailed hierarchy for Victor's developing [[Eldritch Pantheon]]:

When the water finally stopped boiling, Cthugha emerged as a sphere of living plasma covered in eyes. Victor formally welcomed the newborn Great Old One into the Great Chaos and immediately became fascinated by what the daughters had produced.[1]
Upon examining Cthugha, Victor discovered that the creature was far more abnormal than its appearance suggested. Its existence incorporated properties from two separate universes. It possessed the Concept of a Star belonging to Victor's native universe while simultaneously carrying Eldritch Space originating from an alien cosmological system. The Draconic properties inherited through Victor's scale further complicated its structure.[1]
The greatest contradiction was its relationship with Vampirism. Cthugha had somehow been recognized by Reality as the Progenitor of a new Vampire race called the Fire Vampires. This directly contradicted one of the traditional weaknesses of Vampires, as most Noble Vampire lineages outside the [[Snow Clan]] were naturally vulnerable to Fire, while Cthugha had become the origin of a Vampiric race fundamentally associated with it.[1]
Cthugha was already capable of destroying an entire Solar System immediately after birth. Victor additionally recognized that its power would continue becoming more extreme as the Great Old One aged.[1]
More importantly for the research being conducted by Ruby, Aline, Velnorah and Velina, Cthugha demonstrated that an existence containing apparently contradictory principles from different cosmological systems could be created without the universe immediately rejecting or correcting it. Victor therefore pointed Cthugha out to Velina as a potential clue toward the paradox project her mothers had been pursuing.[1]
When Victor asked what the newborn wished to do, Cthugha answered that it wanted to serve the Great Chaos as effectively as possible. Victor accepted its loyalty and, identifying Cthugha as the first creation produced by his daughters, assigned it a task worthy of its unusual status, with the narration indicating that this assignment was intended for future enemies foolish enough to invade Victor's galaxy.[1]

### Skills & Abilities
Cthugha is an extremely unusual composite existence whose complete capabilities have not yet been demonstrated. Chapter 1188 explicitly describes its abilities and Concepts as a "wild jumble" created through the interaction of powers originating from multiple Elderblood daughters, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s scale, Eldritch beings and Leviathan characteristics.[1]

#### Great Old One Physiology
- Great Old One Physiology: Cthugha is explicitly classified as a Great Old One rather than an Outer God. Within the hierarchy established by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Great Old Ones are Divine Eldritch beings created by Victor, an Outer God or their descendants that possess their own Divinity.[1]
- Eldritch Physiology: Its existence contains genuine Eldritch properties originating from forces outside the ordinary cosmological framework of Victor's native universe.[1]
- Divinity: Cthugha possesses Divinity of its own; this is the characteristic separating Great Old Ones from the lesser servants beneath them in Victor's Eldritch hierarchy.[1]
- Paradoxical Existence: Victor describes Cthugha as a walking contradiction. Properties that would ordinarily belong to incompatible systems coexist within one stable being without the universe immediately correcting or destroying it.[1]
- Dual-Cosmology Nature: Cthugha simultaneously possesses a Concept originating from the native universe and Eldritch Space belonging to an alien universe, making it an explicit hybrid of two cosmological systems.[1]
- Draconic Influence: One of Victor's scales was used as a major power source during Cthugha's creation, and Victor specifically identifies the Dragon power contained within the scale as part of what produced Cthugha's contradictory existence.[1]
- Plasma Physiology: Cthugha initially manifests as a sphere composed of pure plasma rather than conventional organic matter.[1]
- Non-Humanoid Physiology: Its natural body does not resemble an ordinary humanoid species and is instead a stellar-looking plasma sphere covered by numerous eyes.[1]
- Multiple Eyes: Numerous eyes are distributed across Cthugha's body.[1]
- Natural Sapience: Cthugha was capable of speech, self-expression and understanding Victor immediately after being born.[1]

#### Conceptual Powers
- Conceptual Manipulation: As a Great Old One possessing its own Divinity, Cthugha contains genuine conceptual properties rather than functioning solely through physical or energetic abilities.[1]
- Star Concept: Cthugha explicitly possesses the Concept of a Star belonging to the native universe. The full applications of this Concept have not yet been demonstrated.[1]
- Eldritch Space: Cthugha simultaneously possesses an Eldritch form of Space originating from an alien universe. This is one of the primary characteristics [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] used to identify Cthugha as a hybrid of two universes.[1]
- Cross-Cosmological Concept Integration: Native and foreign conceptual properties coexist within Cthugha without one automatically erasing the other.[1]

#### Fire Vampire Progenitor
- Vampire Progenitor Physiology: Reality recognizes Cthugha as the Progenitor of a completely new Vampire race.[1]
- Fire Vampire Progenitor: The race originating from Cthugha is identified as the Fire Vampires.[1]
- Vampire-Fire Paradox: Cthugha's existence fundamentally contradicts traditional Vampiric biology. Fire historically functions as a major weakness for Noble Vampires outside specialized lineages such as the [[Snow Clan]], yet Cthugha became the Progenitor of Vampires whose very identity is associated with Fire.[1]
- Independent Vampiric Lineage: The Fire Vampires constitute a distinct Vampiric race originating from Cthugha rather than one of the old Noble Vampire bloodlines descended from [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]].[1]
The exact method through which Cthugha creates Fire Vampires, their biological characteristics and which abilities they inherit from their Progenitor have not yet been revealed.

#### Power
- Solar-System Destruction: Cthugha possessed enough power to destroy an entire Solar System immediately after being born.[1]
- Extraordinary Starting Power: Its Solar-System-level destructive capability was present while Cthugha was still a newborn, without requiring centuries or millennia of development.[1]
- Progressive Growth: Cthugha's power is explicitly stated to become increasingly extreme as it grows older.[1]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
Cthugha recognizes [[Victor Elderblood]] as the central authority of the Great [[Chaos]]. Victor did not personally assemble Cthugha, but his scale was one of the most important materials used during the creation process and supplied the experiment with his Draconic power.[1]
Victor reacted to Cthugha with fascination rather than fear or hostility. He formally welcomed the Great Old One into the Great Chaos, analyzed its contradictory structure and immediately recognized its usefulness both as a clue for Imperial research and as a potential asset against future invaders.[1]
Cthugha's first declared objective was to serve the Great Chaos as effectively as possible. Victor subsequently gave it a task befitting its status as the first major creation of his daughters.[1]

#### The Elderblood Daughters
Cthugha is the collective creation of several of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters rather than the offspring of one specific individual. Yog, Nigredo, Valentina, Leone, Hinata, Silvia, Rosemary, Stella and Velina each participated in the experiment and supplied different qualities or powers to the developing being.[1]
Victor explicitly referred to Cthugha as the first creation of his daughters, giving the Great Old One a unique position within the rapidly developing second generation of the Elderblood Eldritch hierarchy.[1]

### Nature as a Contradiction
Cthugha is particularly important because its existence challenges several previously understood distinctions between different supernatural systems.
First, it combines concepts originating from different universes. Its Star Concept belongs to the native Creation, whereas its Eldritch Space originates from an external cosmological system. These forces coexist within one stable entity rather than causing the universe to automatically remove the contradiction.[1]
Second, its Vampiric nature overturns one of the traditional assumptions surrounding Vampires. Fire has historically been one of the race's most important vulnerabilities outside special lineages such as Clan Snow, yet Cthugha is simultaneously an entity composed of stellar plasma and the Progenitor of the Fire Vampires.[1]
Finally, Cthugha demonstrated that an apparently impossible combination of native Rules and external Eldritch properties could remain functional inside the universe. This immediately attracted Velina's attention because Imperial researchers were attempting to determine whether contradictions could be weaponized against the Primordial structures governing Reality.[1]
Cthugha therefore serves not only as a powerful Great Old One but as direct evidence that the boundary between incompatible cosmological systems can be crossed without necessarily causing immediate rejection by Reality.
