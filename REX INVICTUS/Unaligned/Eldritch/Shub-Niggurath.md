### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 965
Shub-Niggurath Elderblood is an Outer God and one of the Eldritch daughters of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Azathoth Elderblood]]. She appears within the future surrounding the [[Akashic Records]], where Victor creates her as part of the increasingly extreme defense system protecting the Main Timeline from beings attempting to invade or interfere with his Reality.[1]
Shub's defining ability is an extraordinary manifestation of Life on a cosmic scale. Rather than merely creating individual organisms, she can produce living celestial and cosmological structures, including planets, stars, galaxies and entire universes. These creations are themselves living organisms capable of interacting with, consuming and attacking other cosmic structures. Victor consequently uses Shub and her offspring as another defensive layer surrounding the Main Timeline, transforming the space outside the Records into an ecosystem of hostile living universes.[1]

### Physical Appearance
In her humanoid manifestation, Shub-Niggurath appears as a pale-skinned woman with long black hair and black eyes. Like the other Eldritch daughters created through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Azathoth, her humanoid appearance resembles Victor more strongly than Azathoth, reflecting the unusual manner in which Victor's Essence manifests within their offspring.
Despite this resemblance, Shub is not one of Victor's conventional [[Blood Dragons]]. She does not possess the characteristic violet Dragon Eyes associated with most of Victor's Draconic daughters. Her existence instead belongs to the Eldritch side of the Elderblood bloodline.
Even while humanoid, Shub's connection to Life is reflected through unnatural biological characteristics. Her body and hair can display living traits inconsistent with ordinary anatomy, including eye-like structures and mouths lined with sharp teeth. These characteristics foreshadow the incomprehensible biology expressed much more completely through her true form.
Shub also possesses a Nightmare Form, representing the vastly more monstrous Eldritch state hidden beneath her manageable humanoid appearance. Her true existence is not restricted to normal Human proportions or conventional anatomy, allowing her to function comfortably among cosmic structures and the Nothingness surrounding the [[Akashic Records]].[1]

### Personality
Shub-Niggurath is emotionally expressive, highly attached to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and comparatively immature when contrasted with Azathoth. Shortly after manifesting, she immediately wanted her father's attention and became upset when she felt she was not receiving it. Because her emotions directly influence her Eldritch abilities, even a childish outburst from Shub can have consequences on an astronomical scale.[1]
Her behavior demonstrates that she does not yet possess Azathoth's degree of self-control. When Shub became agitated, enormous Eldritch creatures began spontaneously appearing around her in response to her emotional state. Azathoth merely needed to give her a stern look for Shub to recognize the danger and regain control, showing that she respects and responds to her mother despite possessing tremendous power herself.[1]
Shub is nevertheless not portrayed as deliberately destructive or malicious. Her dangerous manifestations are primarily the result of emotional instability combined with an ability whose natural scale is absurdly large. Victor consequently treats her as a daughter requiring guidance rather than a weapon that merely needs to be aimed at an enemy.
She is especially incompatible with [[Cthulhu]]. [[Nyarlathotep]] explains that Cthulhu and Shub have difficulty peacefully coexisting because of the madness inherent to Eldritch beings. Victor acts as an unusual stabilizing influence upon his daughters, suppressing enough of their natural instability that beings who would otherwise struggle to tolerate one another can function as members of the same family.[1]

### Synopsis

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Shub-Niggurath appeared during a glimpse of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s future surrounding the [[Akashic Records]]. By this point, Victor had become increasingly obsessed with preventing outside entities from entering his Reality, manipulating its history or approaching his family through powers capable of bypassing ordinary dimensional barriers.[1]
Instead of relying upon a single conventional barrier, Victor developed a multilayered defense around the Main Timeline. The empty region outside the Records would itself become hostile territory populated by Eldritch creatures and other cosmic horrors, while any entity capable of penetrating the external defenses would be guided toward predetermined areas where Victor and his family could respond immediately.[1]
Victor decided that the next stage of this defense required another daughter from himself and Azathoth. The result was Shub-Niggurath. Her particular nature was exceptionally useful because Victor required something capable of populating the enormous Nothingness surrounding the Records without forcing him to personally manufacture every defensive organism.[1]
Shub's emergence immediately demonstrated why Victor had chosen her. Her influence over Life was not restricted to biological creatures of conventional size. Planets could be alive. Stars could be alive. Galaxies could be alive. Even complete universes could exist as individual living organisms produced through Shub's power.[1]
These entities were fundamentally different from ordinary universes merely containing life. The celestial structure itself was the organism. A living planet was not simply a world covered in organisms; the planet was alive. A living galaxy was itself a creature. A living universe similarly constituted an enormous cosmic life-form rather than a conventional universe populated by unrelated species.[1]
Their behavior made them ideal for Victor's purposes. Shub's creations were predatory and could consume other entities, including one another. Victor therefore planned to fill the territory outside the Main Timeline with them, creating an entire hostile cosmic ecosystem between potential invaders and the Akashic Records. An outsider who penetrated the primary defenses would not find empty space but universes, galaxies and other astronomical structures that were themselves enormous monsters.[1]
Shub's first moments also revealed the disadvantage of connecting such an ability to a comparatively immature personality. Wanting Victor's attention, she became emotionally agitated and colossal Eldritch creatures began appearing around her. Victor and Azathoth immediately warned her that losing control near the Records was unacceptable. Azathoth's stern reaction was enough to make Shub suppress the uncontrolled manifestations, after which she remained more restrained.[1]
Her manifestation also created another family problem. [[Yog-Sothoth]] Elderblood predicted that one of their sisters would be particularly unhappy with Shub's arrival. [[Nyarlathotep]] immediately understood that she meant [[Cthulhu]], explaining that Shub and Cthulhu were naturally incompatible and historically struggled to coexist peacefully without Victor's presence stabilizing them.[1]
Cthulhu soon sensed Shub's presence and arrived to confront Victor. She immediately complained that he had once again created more Eldritch daughters and became increasingly jealous of the attention he was giving Azathoth and the new members of the family. Yog nearly teased Cthulhu about the circumstances surrounding their creation, only for Nyarlathotep to silence her before she made the unstable situation even worse.[1]
Cthulhu then examined Shub and the others more closely and perceived Victor's Essence within them. The discovery pushed her jealousy beyond what she could safely control. Victor immediately recognized the approaching disaster and threw Cthulhu far away from the Records before she could transform beside them.[1]
Cthulhu entered her Nightmare Form and began venting her frustration upon the surrounding environment. Several of the living universes generated through Shub's existence were destroyed during the resulting tantrum, directly demonstrating both the scale of Shub's creations and the absurdity of conflicts among Victor's Eldritch children. What would have been an ordinary disagreement between siblings became the destruction of multiple universes.[1]
Victor left to calm Cthulhu while ordering Azathoth and the remaining daughters to continue watching for intruders. Shub's offspring remained throughout the region as living defensive structures, forming part of the environment specifically designed to make approaching the Main Timeline extraordinarily dangerous.[1]
The defensive concept was soon validated when a foreign intruder managed to enter the region. The entity encountered an environment so fundamentally hostile and incomprehensible that its body and existence began breaking down before Victor ultimately captured it. Shub's living cosmic ecosystem consequently formed one component of a larger system in which even successfully crossing the external barriers did not bring an enemy meaningfully closer to Victor's family.[1]

### Skills & Abilities
Shub-Niggurath is an Outer God whose known abilities revolve overwhelmingly around Life and the creation of living entities on increasingly absurd scales. Her complete abilities have not yet been revealed, so the following list is restricted to powers directly demonstrated or established through her appearance.
- Outer God Physiology: Shub is an Eldritch cosmic existence born from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Azathoth. Her nature fundamentally differs from conventional Gods, Dragons and native supernatural species, allowing her to naturally exist in the regions outside the ordinary Reality surrounding the [[Akashic Records]].[1]
- Eldritch Physiology: Shub's body does not obey conventional biological standards. Even her humanoid manifestation displays unnatural living characteristics, while her genuine existence is represented through a far more monstrous Nightmare Form.
- Nightmare Form: Like Victor's other Eldritch daughters, Shub possesses an incomprehensible true form beyond her humanoid manifestation. This state more completely expresses her nature as a cosmic horror.[1]
- Life Manipulation: Life is the defining phenomenon surrounding Shub's existence. She can generate living organisms without being restricted to conventional biological scales, allowing her influence to encompass organisms ranging from Eldritch creatures to celestial and universal structures.[1]
- Life Creation: Shub can spontaneously produce new living beings through her power. Her emotional agitation alone was sufficient to cause enormous Eldritch organisms to begin appearing around her.[1]
- Cosmic Life Creation: Shub can create organisms on astronomical scales. Her creations include living planets, stars and other celestial structures whose entire physical forms function as individual organisms rather than merely containing conventional life.[1]
- Living Galaxy Creation: Entire galaxies can manifest as living entities through Shub's power. These structures are not simply galaxies populated by life; the galaxy itself functions as the organism.[1]
- Living Universe Creation: Shub's most extraordinary demonstrated ability is the production of complete living universes. The entire universe constitutes a single cosmic organism capable of existing independently outside the Akashic Records.[1]
- Mass Creation: Shub is not limited to maintaining a single cosmic organism. Sufficient numbers of her creations appeared around the Main Timeline for Victor to use them as an environmental defensive layer rather than as isolated weapons.[1]
- Autonomous Creation: The organisms produced through Shub possess their own behavior rather than functioning exclusively as puppets requiring constant commands. They can interact with and attack surrounding entities independently.[1]
- Predatory Cosmic Life: Shub's astronomical organisms can consume other things, including other members of their own cosmic ecosystem. This transforms the region surrounding the Records into an environment where planets, galaxies and universes themselves can behave as predators.[1]
- Biological Manifestation: Shub can manifest abnormal biological structures directly through her power, producing Eldritch creatures with bodies that do not follow the anatomy of ordinary life.[1]
- Emotion-Linked Creation: Her powers react directly to her emotional state. When Shub became upset while seeking Victor's attention, large Eldritch creatures began appearing around her without any deliberate strategic purpose.[1]
- Cosmic Environmental Engineering: Victor uses Shub's Life Creation as a method of transforming the otherwise empty regions outside the Main Timeline into an inhabited defensive environment filled with predatory cosmic organisms.[1]
- Dimensional Defense: Shub and her offspring form part of the multilayered defenses protecting the Akashic Records. Her function is particularly valuable because enemies who pass conventional barriers must still cross an ecosystem composed of hostile living astronomical structures.[1]
- Existence in Nothingness: Shub can function normally in the Nothingness surrounding the Akashic Records, despite the absence or abnormal condition of the ordinary cosmic structures required by conventional organisms.[1]
- Eldritch Presence: As an Outer God, Shub possesses an existence fundamentally alien to conventional beings. Victor deliberately uses beings of this nature around the Records because entities unable to comprehend what they encounter may suffer catastrophic consequences merely by entering the region.[1]
- Variable Scale: Shub is not fundamentally restricted to the dimensions of her humanoid manifestation. Her true nature and the organisms she produces operate comfortably on planetary, stellar, Galactic and universal scales.[1]

### Weaknesses
- Emotional Instability: Shub has difficulty completely separating her emotions from the activation of her powers. Becoming upset can cause dangerous creatures to manifest without deliberate intent.[1]
- Limited Control: Compared with Azathoth, Shub is still inexperienced at controlling an Eldritch existence whose natural abilities operate on astronomical and universal scales. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] specifically warns against losing control near the Records.[1]
- Attachment to Victor: Much of Shub's demonstrated emotional instability revolves around wanting Victor's attention. Although not a weakness in conventional combat, this attachment can provoke uncontrolled manifestations when she feels neglected.[1]
- Azathoth's Authority: Shub responds immediately to Azathoth's discipline. A stern look from her mother is sufficient to make her recognize her mistake and restrain her power.[1]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is Shub's father and the principal reason for her creation. He specifically desired an Eldritch daughter whose power could fill the Nothingness surrounding the Main Timeline with living cosmic defenses. Shub immediately becomes strongly attached to him and seeks his attention after manifesting, to the point that feeling ignored can trigger involuntary creations.[1]
Victor nevertheless treats her as a daughter rather than merely as a source of weapons. When her emotions begin affecting the surrounding environment, he corrects her behavior instead of simply exploiting the creatures she produces. His presence also suppresses part of the madness inherent to his Eldritch daughters, allowing Shub to coexist with sisters she would naturally struggle to tolerate.[1]

#### Azathoth Elderblood
Azathoth is Shub's mother and acts as the more experienced Eldritch authority responsible for helping control the younger daughters. Shub respects her enough that a single stern reaction from Azathoth causes her to immediately restrain an uncontrolled manifestation of Eldritch creatures.[1]

#### Cthulhu
[[Cthulhu]] and Shub possess an especially incompatible relationship. [[Nyarlathotep]] states that the two naturally have extreme difficulty getting along and that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s stabilizing influence is one of the few reasons peaceful coexistence between them is possible.[1]
Shub's emergence immediately triggered Cthulhu's jealousy. After Cthulhu discovered Victor's Essence within Shub and her sisters, she entered her Nightmare Form and destroyed several of the living universes associated with Shub's power.[1]

#### Nyarlathotep
[[Nyarlathotep]] understands the danger surrounding Shub and [[Cthulhu]]'s relationship and immediately recognizes which sister [[Yog-Sothoth]] means when she predicts Shub's arrival will cause trouble. Her greater emotional restraint makes her one of the sisters capable of recognizing situations likely to escalate into an Eldritch disaster.[1]

#### Yog-Sothoth Elderblood
[[Yog-Sothoth]] was present during Shub's manifestation and quickly realized that [[Cthulhu]] would react badly to the existence of another Eldritch daughter. She nearly provoked Cthulhu further before [[Nyarlathotep]] stopped her.[1]

### Trivia
- Shub-Niggurath's first appearance is in Chapter 965, during a future sequence involving [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s increasingly extreme protection of the Main Timeline.[1]
- Her defining characteristic is not simply creating organisms within a universe. Shub can make the universe itself alive.[1]
- Her creations scale downward from living universes and galaxies to stars, planets and individual Eldritch creatures.[1]
- Shub's cosmic offspring can prey upon one another, turning the territory surrounding the [[Akashic Records]] into an actual Eldritch ecosystem rather than a static wall of monsters.[1]
- Several of Shub's living universes were destroyed by [[Cthulhu]] during the latter's jealousy-driven tantrum.[1]
- Her power is dangerous even when she has no intention of fighting, since sufficiently strong emotions can cause creatures to manifest around her automatically.[1]
- The previous version of this article listed titles such as "The Black Goat with a Thousand Young", "All-Mother", "Mother Goddess", "Lord of the Woods", "The Mighty Mother", "Shupnikkurat" and "Quake". These titles have not been attributed to Shub-Niggurath Elderblood within [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]] and are therefore not treated as canonical MTW titles.
- Although her name references Shub-Niggurath from cosmic-horror fiction, MTW's Shub-Niggurath is a separate character and should not automatically inherit abilities, history, titles or relationships from unrelated versions of the character.
