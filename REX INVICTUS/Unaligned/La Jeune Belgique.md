**[[La Jeune Belgique]]** ("Young Belgium") was a Belgian literary society and review founded in Brussels in 1881. It championed "art for art's sake" and published French-language Belgian writers who later led the Symbolist movement. Listed under [[Unaligned]].

## IRL

- **The review.** *La Jeune Belgique* ran from 1881 to 1897, with Max Waller as its first driving force.
- **Contributors.** Georges Rodenbach (*Bruges-la-Morte*), Eugène Demolder, Émile Verhaeren, Maurice Maeterlinck (Nobel Prize in Literature, 1911), Charles Van Lerberghe, Albert Giraud (*Pierrot lunaire*, later set by Schoenberg), Georges Eekhoud and Camille Lemonnier.
