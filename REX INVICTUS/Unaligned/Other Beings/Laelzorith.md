### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 1207
My name is Laelzorith, and I represent the Law of my Emperor.
—Laelzorith
Laelzorith is a male god and Grandmaster who serves Aetheryon, one of the four great leaders of the Upper Sector. His own domain is Law. He represents that Law for his Emperor. He does not serve <Law>, and Law is not the name of the leader he answers to.
<Law> is a different power. That faction is the herald of the Primordial of Balance, and it moves only when that Primordial allows it. Laelzorith's master is Aetheryon, the one the System records as <Emperor> and also as <Kaiser>. When that leader shows himself, he is a gigantic skull of pure fire. Beside him, the other rulers of that tier are <Darkness> and <Light>. <Darkness> made his principal galaxy into his own body. <Emperor>, <Light> and <Law> soaked a galaxy in their power and made that galaxy their domain.[1][2][3][4][5]
The Emperor's public face is justice. His ten chief gods are known by name outside his faction because of that preaching, and each of them stands for one aspect of justice. Laelzorith is not named in that count. Aetheryon calls him one of his judges, elite of that empire. What he claims for himself is the domain of Law, held for his Emperor. With him are [[Seraphel]], whose domain is Truth, and [[Dravakar]], whose domain is Retribution.[3][6][5]
He meets [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] outside the galaxy where [[Kali Elderblood]] and [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]] are fighting the Thrones [[Aetherys]] and [[Oryndal]]. The three come at galactic scale and attack as one formation. Their Emperor orders a retreat they cannot make. The attack that follows unmakes the body he is wearing. The life is kept by the [[World Trees]] Aetheryon spends on his elite.[6][7][5][8]

### Physical Appearance
His face is never seen. The helmet is smooth, with no eyes and no openings, and its elongated shape runs into a rigid crown. Height, eyes, hair, skin, species and race are unknown. The narration treats him as a god.[7]
The armor is gold and black metal plate, with geometric lines across the chest, shoulders and arms. The suit is cut to look precise and to show the wearer's power. His spear is dark metal with gold detail, symmetrical and exact.[7]
[[Dravakar]], beside him in the fight, wears black and silver armor cut for speed and uses two dark-bladed swords. [[Seraphel]] wears silver and gray plate with purple detail. His helmet also hides his head. His scythe has a long handle and a curved blade covered in geometric markings and strange characters, and the same characters are on his armor.[7]
That armor, that spear, and the body under them are what the battle leaves behind. [[Scathach]]'s Big Bang takes the suit apart before he can react. The body follows, down to atoms and then to elementary particles. When the blast subsides there is no armor, no weapon, and not a fragment from which that body could be put back together. Only residual energy of creation remains where he stood.[5]

### Personality
He gives his name, names his Emperor, and names Law. He does not add a speech. He is not the one who says they only came to watch. He is not the one who tells [[Seraphel]] that [[Scathach]] will listen to no one because her existence screams for blood, or who says the mission will go forward in the name of the Great One.[6]
In the fight he takes the first thrust and keeps trying to put the formation back together. A hit on the forearm is enough to make him give ground and cover the arm. When the attack fails, he is the one who sets himself at the front, with [[Dravakar]] at his side. He steps in to take the pressure off Dravakar, and he is the one who orders Dravakar to heal by using their Emperor's power. Dravakar answers, "Not yet."[7]
He does not take being handled lightly. Through their link he growls, "She treats us like children!" After Scathach has already driven him off his footing, she tells him he lacks initiative and is too used to subduing his enemies.[7]

### Synopsis

#### Volume 9: Those Who Rule Reality
The Upper Sector is held by four factions, and none of them will let a fifth stand. [[Vharzath]] offers [[Aetheryon]] twenty percent of the spoils if he will pull [[Victor Elderblood]] aside. Aetheryon calls the price too low to make an enemy of Negativity's Chosen. Vharzath tells him he will never spend his main fleet, and that smaller factions are already being used to test the new Empire. Aetheryon answers with fifty percent, and with his main fleet only if Darkness sends the Abominations. Vharzath agrees, and takes the first choice of what he receives. Neither of them means to keep the bargain if a better chance appears.[2]
After [[Hela Elderblood]] erases one of Darkness's weapons with END, the same leader comes back as the flaming skull, serious this time. END can be warded off only by BEGIN. Darkness has lost that Throne, and Aetheryon has no subordinate who can answer it. He tells Vharzath to deploy his Thrones while he deploys his own Enforcers. Vharzath refuses a blind large-scale attack. Aetheryon leaves the method to him and keeps his own. The later order is narrower. He will hit the Elderblood forces, and he will not send his elites into Victor's galaxy. That place is the domain of Negativity's Chosen, and only he himself would enter it. Once enough elites are gone, the two will attack that galaxy together. His answer is that he handles things his own way.[9][4]
That is the war Laelzorith walks into. He is not sent into the supergalaxy. He comes to the legions, outside the galaxy where the fight with Darkness is already happening. [[Kali Elderblood]] and [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]] are there against [[Aetherys]], Throne of Time, and [[Oryndal]], Throne of Space. [[Scathach]] watches them and is satisfied. They are not trading beams. They are using the martial foundation she and Victor taught, and the Thrones have power with almost none of that foundation.[10][11][6]
Victor has already told Eleanor and Rose to accompany Scathach and take command if they must. When she feels the three arrivals, she tells those two she is counting on them. Rose asks Victor if he foresaw it. Scathach does not wait for the three to look the field over.[10][6]
She meets them as a pillar of pure red Dragon power, dense enough to make its own gravity and pull at the nearest galaxies. She calls three against one a fair fight and manifests a spear covered in uncanny runes. The runes make them waver. Her face grows wilder and more draconic. She names herself Scathach Elderblood, wife of Emperor Victor Elderblood and Supreme Commander of the Empire, and tells them to entertain her or die.[6]
One of them says they only came to watch. She calls it a lie and stops the power from streaming outward, gathering it around her body like armor. The pressure nearly triples. Another tells [[Seraphel]] that she will listen to no one, because her existence screams for blood. Their mission, he says, will be carried out in the name of the Great One. Capturing her is useful because she is the Dragon Emperor's confidante. That Emperor is Victor. It is not the leader Laelzorith serves.[6]
Laelzorith then gives his name and says he represents the Law of his Emperor. A spear appears in his hand. Seraphel names himself and says he represents Truth. Two swords appear in his hands. [[Dravakar]] names himself and says he represents Retribution. A scythe appears in his hand. Scathach's smile widens. She calls the three Grandmasters a jackpot.[6]
Victor sees them. His eyes narrow. He orders [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]] to go with Nyx Elderblood and step in only if it is necessary. Kaguya says Scathach will not like it. Victor says he will deal with her later, and that her safety outranks her pride. Kaguya bows and vanishes into the shadows.[6]
Scathach reads their spacing before the first blow. This group is far better organized than the others she has been fighting. They attack together. Laelzorith opens. The spear goes for her chest, testing the reaction and forcing a choice. She twists, and the point passes her shoulder. Dravakar is already there with both swords, one at the lower abdomen and one cutting the escape. She gives half a step, lets the first blade pass, and knocks the second aside. She does not counter. Seraphel's scythe is already coming in behind Dravakar. She leaves the spot before the blade finishes its arc.[7]
She uses the gap to advance on Laelzorith. He has already put the spear back between them. She stops short of the next blow. The formation does not need every weapon to land. Each of them only has to close the space the others leave open.[7]
His second thrust aims at her waist. She flicks the point off without turning the weapon all the way around, shoves the shaft outward, and forces him to fix his grip. As he realigns, she hits his forearm with her own shaft. He is not dropped. The next move is gone. He backs off and protects the arm.[7]
Dravakar fills the empty place. His swords come in at different heights. She parries the first. The second scrapes her armor. She steps inside his reach and hits his wrist, then his shoulder, so he has to pull the arm in. Seraphel tries to take the gap. The scythe comes down on a diagonal and makes her let go of Dravakar. She knocks the handle off with her forearm and falls back with all three still in sight. The gods stop and reset.[7]
Laelzorith holds the spear lower and covers his legs. Dravakar waits for her to commit to defense before he closes. Seraphel stops striking and uses the scythe to threaten the exits. Scathach calls them worthy. Teamwork, decisions and strength are all there. She says they deserve the title of Grandmaster, and that they are competent in every respect.[7]
He thrusts again. She slips the point and tries to walk in along the shaft. Dravakar hits the flank before she reaches him. She has to break off, catch one sword and duck the other. Seraphel closes the way out. For a moment all three weapons box her in. She does not smash through. She presses into the sword that caught her and shifts toward Dravakar, so he has to drop his guard or cut his own companion. She swings under the sword, hits his knee, and is already gone before the scythe finishes. Laelzorith tries to cut her off. The spearhead only grazes her, and her spear hits his shoulder before he can pull it back. All three retreat.[7]
Dravakar will not put weight on the bad leg. Laelzorith keeps one arm in against his body. Seraphel can no longer swing as freely, because she has started to read the rhythm. The wounds are not grave, but they stack, and every pause makes the formation worse. Dravakar realizes her spear is blocking his natural regeneration.[7]
They stop coming all at once. Laelzorith starts a thrust and cuts it off early, trying to make her move too soon. She waits. When the spear comes again, she turns the tip just far enough to leave its line. He is open. She does not take it. Dravakar is already closing, and Seraphel has the scythe ready to shut the gap. She steps back and makes all three come to her.[7]
Dravakar arrives first. She turns the right-hand sword, hits the hand on the left, and he has to yank the arm back or stab himself. She uses that for a short thrust through the chest plate. He is driven back, hand on the wound. Seraphel attacks and she parries the scythe as if it costs her nothing.[7]
Laelzorith tries the opening. The spear goes for her back. She feels the shift in his body and turns before the point arrives. She pushes the shaft off the center line and walks in. One hit takes the arm. The next takes the ribs of the armor and throws him out of position. She tells him he lacks initiative, and that he is too used to subduing his enemies. She does not chase. Seraphel opens the distance and starts a wide cut to keep her off him.[7]
She ducks into the blade's radius. The long handle is slow to change direction. She hits Seraphel's forearm, then his shoulder, parries the handle when he tries to shove her with it, and drives him back with a thrust to the torso. She is gone before Dravakar can close the circle. Whenever Laelzorith tries to wall off her movement, she slips toward Dravakar and makes him drop the attack. When Seraphel threatens the flank, she backs into a spot where his own scythe also stops the other two. They spend more time fixing their places than landing what they planned. Seraphel cannot understand it. He knows they should not be losing ground, and they still are not getting anywhere.[7]
Laelzorith growls that she is treating them like children. Dravakar says they need to slow her momentum. Laelzorith orders him to heal the wound with their Emperor's power. Dravakar refuses for the moment and goes in again. She clears both swords, hits the wrist, then the face. Laelzorith advances so the pressure comes off his companion. She lets Dravakar go and meets the spear with her own. Both shafts are knocked aside. She steps in and hits Laelzorith in the abdomen with her fist. He loses his footing and has to retreat or take the spearhead in the neck. Seraphel comes from the flank. She does not attack at once. She lets the sweep turn him, gets inside the blade, and takes the arm. The arm is gone.[7]
The three fall back. None of them is beaten, and the formation can still be dangerous if she makes a mistake, but the first offensive is over. Laelzorith takes the front. Dravakar stays beside him.[7]
An order, "Insert them here!", is answered with "Yes." The speaker is not named. Golden power rises, and the healing that should have been instant takes seconds that matter. The wounded god demands to know what kind of spear can keep even their Emperor's strength from closing the wound quickly. The answer, also unnamed, is that the weapon seems mixed from different divine concepts, and that this is what blocks the regeneration. That wound is Dravakar's. Scathach takes the healing enemy's arm, calls it repulsive, and burns it away in Dragonfire. She ignores the healing and gives the three an A+ for combined performance and teamwork.[7]
She tells Laelzorith, Dravakar and Seraphel to rejoice. Outside her family, they are among the strongest she has met. Ranked inside it, they could challenge her sisters Rose and Eleanor, though only with great difficulty. Then she stops. Beside Victor the same technique is flawless and still useless to her: no aggression, too methodical, none of his flexibility, and no understanding of how to use their strength against an equal. She calls them boring next to him.[7]
The fight is what makes her own level obvious to her. She is the wife who challenges Victor whenever she can. He stands with the leaders of the largest factions in the Upper Sector, and she is the woman who fights him most often. She had been holding back with her sisters, so she had not noticed. She is the second strongest woman in the family. Azathoth is stronger, and only because Azathoth surpasses her in everything except technology and martial arts. In technology, warfare and hand-to-hand combat, she places herself first behind Victor. Opponents who can rival Rose and Eleanor are not close to that.[7]
She tightens her grip. Crimson power bursts from the hand. Dragon runes and Eldritch runes light along the spear. As thanks for making that clear, she offers them a masterpiece. The space between the galaxies goes black, a primeval dark in which nothing can be seen. Their Emperor's voice reaches them: retreat, then retreat now. They cannot move. Scathach spreads her arms and speaks the name Heptagrammakos. She tells them to look, because creation is beautiful, and everything begins with an explosion.[7]
The name is the start of the attack. The faction leaders move as soon as she speaks it. Darkness orders the nearby Thrones who were about to attack her to pull back. Light and Law watch. Aetheryon is the one who shouts for his soldiers to withdraw. He calls the three his judges, the elite of his empire. Losing all three would completely weaken his influence. He calls Darkness a coward for retreating. He knows the attack will destroy souls, and that a resurrection afterward will be impossible.[5]
He rises from his throne to leave his galaxy and save them himself. Victor's gaze, from the enemy galaxy, stops him. An empty homeland is an opening. The choice is the elite or the homeland. He spends a trump card he knows will draw the wrath of the primal force of life. A being who can threaten a galaxy is not replaced. He sends hundreds of [[World Trees]]. They materialize before him, bow, and answer, "Yes, my Emperor." Their eyes are empty. The reason is already gone.[5]
Scathach's confined creation becomes a local Big Bang. The first wave hits the three at once. The armor of Laelzorith, Dravakar, and Seraphel comes apart before they can react. The bodies follow, broken down to atoms and then to elementary particles. There is no time to resist or to run, and no matter left from which those bodies could be rebuilt. When it subsides, the place where they stood holds no armor, no weapon, and not a fragment of them. Only the residual energy of creation is there. Left alone, the expansion could have reached distant galaxies. [[The Limbo Guy|The Limbo]] type appears with Infinity and holds it in.[5]
The lives are what the trees were sent to buy. Where the three vanished, new beings begin to form. Scathach sees the life force at once and says that hundreds of worlds were sacrificed just for her. Seven rays of light open, and the enemy elite comes out, each one an aspect of justice. Laelzorith is not named in that number, and he does not speak again. Victor understands that the elite lived because of the World Trees.[5]
Aetheryon orders an immediate withdrawal. The name his elite answer with is Emperor Aetheryon. The price of the trees comes after. [[The Universal Tree]], the Progenitor of Life, forbids every World Tree in that territory from helping him and drives them off their posts. What remains to Aetheryon is his own divinity. The planets of the faction die. He orders the evacuation into space. A World Tree that has spent all of its energy dies with the planet it was feeding, and hundreds of them were spent so the elites would live. After that, anyone marked by him who approaches a World Tree makes that tree leave at once. Those trees stand under the Dragon Emperor.[5][8]

### Skills & Abilities
His domain is Law, held for the Emperor he serves. He never uses that Law as an attack. What he fights with is the spear, the formation, and the authority to call on his Emperor's power for a companion's healing. None of that stops Heptagrammakos. The body he fights in is unmade. The life that remains is not a technique of his.[6][7][5][8]
- Domain of Law: He says he represents the Law of his Emperor. That is his domain under that leader, in the same sense that [[Seraphel]]'s is Truth and [[Dravakar]]'s is Retribution. No effect of the Law itself is shown. This is not the authority of the <Law> faction.[4][6]
- Weapon Creation: A spear appears in his hand when he names himself. In the battle it is a weapon in his grip, dark metal with gold detail, used for thrusts, feints and to wall off ground. No separate technique of the spear is named. The Big Bang leaves neither the spear nor the hand that held it.[6][7][5]
- [[Spears|Spear]] mastery: He opens on the chest to test a reaction, resets the spear between them before [[Scathach]] can enter, aims the waist, breaks a thrust off early to draw a flinch, and later goes for the back when she is occupied. She turns every one of those lines aside. He is hit in the forearm, the shoulder, the arm, the ribs of the armor and the abdomen, and he has to give ground to keep her spearhead out of his neck. He is not knocked down by the first blow. The formation fight does not defeat him. The blast that follows leaves no body.[7][5]
- Formation combat: The three strike together, and each one exists to cover what the others leave open. After the first clash he drops the spear to guard his legs. On defense he takes the front. He will leave an opening to go help Dravakar. Scathach's answer is to make that formation expensive: she never stays on the man who just attacked, and she uses their need to avoid hitting each other. They are still in that formation when the retreat order arrives and they cannot move.[7]
- His Emperor's power: He orders Dravakar to heal through their Emperor's power. The healing that follows is golden, and it does not finish at once. Their Emperor's strength is not enough to close Scathach's spear-wound quickly. He also hears that Emperor when the order to retreat arrives. He cannot obey it. What keeps the life is a different card. Aetheryon spends the energy of hundreds of [[World Trees]] from his own territory, and those trees save the lives of his elites.[7][5][8]

#### Power and Threat Level
- Grandmaster: [[Scathach]] names all three Grandmasters before the fight, and after the first exchange she says they deserve it. Teamwork, decision-making and strength are all exceptional. She later gives the three an A+ for combined performance and teamwork.[6][7]
- Galactic scale: The three arrive as beings of galactic scale. [[Aetheryon]] treats beings who can threaten a galaxy as priceless, and that is why he spends the [[World Trees]] rather than let the three die. No separate feat of Laelzorith's is given a wider measure than the arrival. He is not shown at the level of the four leaders, and he is not one of Darkness's Thrones.[6][5]
- Ranked by Scathach: Outside her family, she calls the three among the strongest she has met. Inside it, she says they could challenge Rose and Eleanor only with great difficulty. Set beside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], the same technique is flawless and still too rigid. She calls them boring next to him. Once she measures herself against that gap, opponents of Rose and Eleanor's level are no longer close to her.[7]
The gravity-dense Dragon power, the pressure she triples around her body, the runes that make the three waver, the spear that stops natural regeneration, and Heptagrammakos are hers.
