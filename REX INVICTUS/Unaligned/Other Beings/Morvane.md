### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 1205
Morvane is the Chief God of Fear and one of the Ten Chief Gods of the [[Black Eclipse Pantheon]], in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]]. The name is a title. <Darkness>, whom [[Victor Elderblood]] addresses as Vharzath, bestows it. The charge, sixth in the order Lily gives, is fear.[1]
Fear is already among the concepts the first Imperial report ties to the pantheon, with Darkness, Night, Void, Silence, Shadows, Death, Eclipse, and Oblivion. The report does not give fear a God. The extraction does. The title is Morvane.[2][1]
No body, armor, face, or pronoun is given. The name is not spoken on a battlefield.[1]

### The Office
The ten places are not won. A God who has reached an elite is given the title, the realm, and the blessing, and the blessing is what makes a galactic threat. The first years are an untrained hand on a weapon meant to end a world. The captured generation had held the offices for hundreds of years before the new holders were expected to match them, and Lily does not expect the match. The force they use is Darkness. Fear is the realm. The patron's power is not fear's own, and it is alien to the realm it is set into.[3][1]
Violet hears the ten charges as concepts bound to negativity. Fear belongs in that hearing without a stretch. Spiritual and emotional things already carry more of it than stone and starlight do. The office is the pantheon's seat for that concept. No spreading of dread, no breaking of a mind, and no victim are shown.[1]
The seat stands under the [[Seven Black Thrones]]. Esteem is not promotion. [[Nerath]] is the Chief God the Thrones hold highest, and she is still a Chief God. Morvane is not given even that esteem on the page. A fallen holder is replaced. Vharzath is already raising the next void, and nine further offices were still waiting when that successor left the palace.[4][5]

### Among the Ten
Lily's order puts fear after soul and before darkness. The titles around it are [[Khariel]] and [[Azelith]]. Four titles have bodies on the early field. [[Azrakar]], [[Kaelith]], [[Nerath]], and [[Vhalor]]. Morvane is not one of them. Nine Chief Gods are emptied with [[Vaelthar]], and the bodies are ordered burned after Ruby takes her samples. The nine are not named. This title is spoken over that table and not attached to one corpse.[6][1]
