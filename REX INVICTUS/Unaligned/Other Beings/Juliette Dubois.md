### Synopsis

#### Those Who Rule Reality
During approximately the 170,000th year of the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s accelerated development, Juliette was identified as the mother of [[Nikolai Francis Nichev]], an Imperial [[Demigod]] operating within the Sagittarius Dwarf Elliptical Galaxy, approximately 80,000 light-years from [[Yggdrasil]].[1]
Nikolai had secretly established connections with a foreign God originating from the higher regions of the Cosmos. His activities involved investigating [[Victor Elderblood]]'s extraordinary Bloodline, attempting to reproduce its characteristics and discovering the source of the Emperor's unusual existence. These actions attracted the attention of War, Victor's most powerful Herald, who personally intervened to apprehend Nikolai and investigate the foreign influence.[1]
Although Juliette was identified through Nikolai's family background, she did not personally appear during the confrontation. Her knowledge of her son's activities, any involvement in his conspiracy and her reaction to his capture remain unknown.[1]

### Skills & Abilities
Juliette's race, supernatural abilities and combat capabilities have not been revealed. Her son's [[Demigod]] status originates from his father's descent from [[Dagda]], and does not establish that Juliette herself possesses Divine ancestry.[1]

### Trivia

- She is first mentioned in Chapter 1158, without making a direct appearance.
