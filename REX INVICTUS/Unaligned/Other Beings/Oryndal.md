Oryndal is the Throne of Space and one of the [[Seven Black Thrones]] of the [[Black Eclipse Pantheon]], in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]]. The seat stands under <Darkness>, the Great Darkness, whom [[Victor Elderblood]] addresses as Vharzath, and above the Ten Chief Gods. Distance and dimension are the charge. Through them the galaxies Darkness has conquered are joined, and Oryndal is the bridge. He advances with [[Aetherys]] at the head of the reinforcement, and he is met there by [[Kali Elderblood]] and Haruna Elderblood. The fight ends with both Thrones broken, returned to its beginning by a stored point they could not have reached alone, and then taken. He is unconscious in the Dragon Emperor's galaxy, under an order to be learned and then erased. The erasure is not carried out on the page.[1][2][3]

### Physical Appearance
The body has no recognizable anatomy and no traits of any known species. Black plates are set around a central core. Long extensions leave the sides and bend at angles that change as he moves. The tips form geometric structures that open and close, slowly.
Enormous blue-violet rings turn on different planes around the core. Their surfaces carry patterns like maps of galaxies: points of light, joined by lines that wander without settling. From time to time, small openings appear in the rings. Through them, regions are visible that are not where he is.
He does not cross the distance in front of him. The space between him and the goal arranges itself so that a distant region becomes adjacent, and he advances without covering the gap. His voice carries through that space. No height, face, eyes, hair, or skin is given.[2]
The plates and the rings are the structure the concept is carried on, and they can be broken. A blow from Kali tears a fissure across several black plates. A main ring, once struck, loses its stability and collapses inward. Later the crack runs through the whole structure, the dimensional rings fall apart, and pieces of the black armor are torn off the body. The extensions can be sent through an opening. They can also be cut off. Protective layers sit over the core, and they can be penetrated. No height is added.[4]
The plates can also be thrown out of the body by the pressure of a fold he made and then lost. A deeper rift can be torn in the central plate until the black core lies bare, with fragments whirling around it. That plate can be pulled out of its anchoring. The core can be dragged free, fall into pieces that no longer connect, and then lose its form entirely, until nothing is left but wreckage. The stored return puts the body back whole. When he and [[Aetherys]] burn lifespan to pull a future potential into the present, his form is the one that warps first.[3]

### Personality
The fleet battle does not hold him. He and [[Aetherys]] move at the head of the formation with no interest in the fighting, and the ships of Darkness hold their lines and clear a path.
He names what he is looking at. Kali is a high-ranking Goddess of Destruction, elite, and one step from a Primordial Being. Haruna's presence is less crushing, and the same instinct still places her at least as dangerous. He asks whether Kali is one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s elite fighters, and he answers her name with his office.
A duel with the two of them is not what he came to spend himself on. Once they have been identified, Aetherys synchronizes with him and the pair try to leave. The aim is losses, not the elites in front of them. The thought he shares with Aetherys is that the Dragon Emperor is sending his own family into the battle, and that taking the two wives would be a decisive advantage. He does not know that taking them is harder than fighting them.
What everyone in Darkness despises, he despises with them. Everything bound to Primordial Death is repulsive to that side, and an insult aimed at their leader makes the anger worse. He has to force his concentration to say so.[2]
In the fight he spends the concept on getting Aetherys out and on putting Kali out of the combat area, so the two of them have the numbers again. When he sees an opening he attacks Haruna from behind, through a passage, rather than standing in front of her. Kali's answer puts him on his own survival, and after the link with Aetherys breaks he cannot get a distance maneuver finished.[4]
He does not meet Kali's arm head-on once the plates are open. He lengthens the road in front of her without moving his own core, and he means that road to have no end. When that fails he tries to shut her into smaller and smaller spaces, and when the core is bare he tries to crush the whole field into the core he intends to rebuild. After the stored return he is the one who speaks. They have adapted to the way the wives fight, to their powers, and to their existence. In the cosmos, he says, time and space go hand in hand. His form warps before Aetherys's does.[3]

### Synopsis

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Space is the road between the conquests. Oryndal rules distance and dimension, and that rule is what connects the enemy's galaxies. The work is named as a bridge. [[Aetherys]], the Throne of Time, is the seat this one works with. Aetherys keeps the flow of time in those same galaxies and shifts it when commanded. On the field the pair do more than divide a map. Aetherys synchronizes with Oryndal, and space and time fuse.[1][2]
The office is not a Chief God's title. Those places are bestowed by [[Vharzath]], blessed up until the holder can threaten a galaxy, and filled again when the holder falls. A Throne is the part of the host that is not replaced that way. Black Eclipse has seven beings who threaten a galaxy in their own right, and the seven are these seats. Lily separates the captured Origin from the rest of the order in one judgment. [[Vaelthar]] was the experiment, made as a possible Chosen One of Negativity and not chosen. The others are much more stable, and they are the true elites of Darkness. Oryndal is one of those others.[5][6][1]
Velnorah reads the seven titles as an attempt at a self-contained universe, with the Beginning Vaelthar carried as its starting point. Ruby will not leave them as thrones in that design. Taken as potential Primordial Beings of a new universe, they cover almost every essential function of a stable cosmos. Jeanne counts what is missing. Life, death, and infinity are absent. Space is not one of the absences. The reading belongs to the wives. It is not a System rank, and Oryndal is not entered as a Primordial God on any Authority list. The name [[Scathach]] calls arrogant is [[Chaos]], and that seat is [[Zhaeroth]]'s.[1]
Vaelthar is the only Throne who has fought [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. A projection broke him. Lily emptied what was left, and the body was ordered burned. [[Xytherion]] is the deputy, the most trusted, and the supreme commander, and the one sent to deploy the [[Nihilvore]]. The bridge is not those offices. The right hand by charge, the seat that holds everything below Darkness, is Zhaeroth, and that charge is not this one.[7][6][8][1]
The conquests were already coming apart when Vharzath released two of the Thrones and told them to put their own safety first. The two who answered are not named. What follows is the counteroffensive Violet had already expected. A reinforcement of thousands leaves the galaxy of Darkness: cruisers, battleships, carriers, interceptors, and units made for fighting in space. The battle reforms around them. The advance is not numbers alone. Aetherys and Oryndal manifest at its head.[9][1][2]
John does not wait. Both are V-06. The imperial fleets leave the combat zone together, and the order is to leave the galaxy. The Thrones try to stop that retreat. An unnamed protective power cancels the attempt. Whenever those powers are activated, destruction rises and annihilates them before they take effect. Kali is waiting in the wreckage of an imperial ship, and Haruna with her. Oryndal identifies Kali before either of them has given a name.[2]
They introduce themselves so the Thrones will look only at them. The delay is also the retreat. Natalia is taking the imperial forces out of the galaxy. Once the real fight begins, the region closes. The effects of that duel would reach several distant solar systems, and could threaten the whole galaxy. The wives wait until their own forces are clear.[2]
Aetherys calls the Dragon Emperor a fool for sending his family, and he promises them a capture, a reception in the galaxy of Darkness, and a death in front of Victor when the emperor comes to beg. Kali and Haruna drop the restraint they had been keeping. Oryndal wraps himself in Space as Aetherys's Time covers the solar system, and the joined rise breaks the region into a place where space no longer means anything. Natalia gives the clear. The two wives take their forms, and Haruna draws Ikari. Oryndal's answer, through the waver in his clarity, is the hatred of that side for Primordial Death, and the anger at what was said about their leader.[2]
The two concepts then expand together and close around both Thrones. Distant stars lose their apparent places. The coordinates of the imperial fleets no longer agree. Time swings between speeding up and slowing down. Distances fold inward, and stretches appear in which light seems to take impossible paths.[4]
Haruna reaches Aetherys before he can follow her. Oryndal opens several dimensional passages around his companion, into other regions of space, as ways out. At the same time he changes the distance between Kali and himself and drives her out of the combat area. The separation is meant to give the two of them the numbers again. Kali does not stay out. The Destruction around her swallows the dimensional structures before they stabilize. Distance stops being an obstacle, and she crosses the spatial layers he put up to hold her.[4]
Her first blow takes a black plate and tears a fissure across several of them. He tries to reorder the space around his body. She keeps on, alternating between the dimensional rings and the central core. Those structures are what carry his abilities, and the blows are concentrated enough to endanger them. He tries to shift his own core into another dimension and separate it from the damaged part of the body. Violet energy is already through the protective layers. Kali strikes a main ring, the transfer breaks off, and the ring loses its stability and collapses inward. Each of her attacks erases part of his defense on the conceptual level. Had he not put that spatial defense up at the first clash, the blow would have removed him from existence at once.[4]
While Haruna is on Aetherys, Oryndal tries to take her from behind. An opening appears above her. Several of his black extensions come through and go for her body. Kali sees it, appears in front of him, and destroys the dimensional structure with the arm she has wrapped in Destruction. The passage collapses, the extensions are cut off, and he has to withdraw. She advances until his attention is on his own survival.[4]
The pair synchronize again. Aetherys widens the temporal area. Oryndal reorders the space around all four. Distances change at every moment, and events repeat before they can finish. For a few moments the goddesses are held apart by distances that keep growing, and their movements sit under the temporal distortion. The aim is to separate them and break the attacks. It does not last. Haruna cuts through one of the dimensional structures and opens a passage of her own. Kali comes through it and hits Oryndal before he can reorder the space. Haruna, in the same moment, is on Aetherys's damaged core. The synchronization breaks, and the two abilities no longer work together. Without Oryndal's spatial stability, Aetherys cannot keep the temporal distortions up. Without Aetherys's temporal control, Oryndal cannot predict their movements with the same precision.[4]
Kali gathers Destruction into her right fist. Oryndal tries to raise a barrier of several overlapping dimensions. She hits it before it closes. The impact goes through the layers and into the core. The crack runs through the whole structure. The dimensional rings start to fall apart, and pieces of the black armor are torn off. He tries to repair what was hit. The Destruction spreads through those areas before they regain their form.[4]
Aetherys tries to withdraw. Kali stands in his way, and Haruna throws him into the place where Oryndal is trying to reconstruct himself. The two Thrones collide. The impact scatters wreckage and armor plate. They cannot raise a joint defense after that. Kali destroys the dimensional structures Oryndal tries to form. He tries to intervene. His own structure is weakened, and every attempt to manipulate distance is interrupted by the Destruction spreading around her.[4]
The fight is still going on among the stars, far from any planet, when the narration leaves the four of them. The fleets of Darkness use the chaos to leave the region. Scathach, watching, judges the pair against the training she and Victor gave the wives. Beams were available. They were not the point. Her reading is that beings who lean on power lose to an equal who is better in close combat, and that these Thrones, set beside wives who have mastered both the foundations and their powers, are playing with the concepts. She calls the lack of commitment pitiful.[4]
The domains do not hold. The Thrones still have enough left to keep the goddesses from ending the fight, and the cracks in both structures flare, warp space, and rock time in short waves. Haruna reads that the damage has weakened them and made the ground impossible to predict. Aetherys spends that instability on futures. Oryndal spends Haruna's hesitation. He stretches the space between her and the Throne at the moment she strikes, and the blow loses its reach. She does not chase it. She stops. He then draws the Throne aside before she can reach him, and space opens into successive corridors, each one leading another way. She chooses none of them. The flat of Ikari hits the edge of the distortion. She feels the tension holding the fold and cuts the junction. Every corridor closes at once, and the pressure throws fragments of black plate out of his body.[3]
Kali comes through the opening. The Destruction around her arms sweeps the edges of the dimensional structures away before he can put the space back. He does not block the arm. He lengthens the distance between them piece by piece, and he does it without moving his core. With every step she looks smaller in front of the Throne, though she is still coming forward. The approach is meant to become a journey with no end. She does not attack the distance. She sets Destruction on the connections between the spaces he made. The dividing lines lose their continuity. Space breaks into segments, like a cracked surface. She crosses every break without letting him decide where she is, appears in front of him, and her fist tears a new, deeper rift in the central plate.[3]
Aetherys tries to set him back to the state before the wound. Time draws tight around the Throne of Space, and the plates start to close. Haruna throws Ikari between the two cores, and Aetherys has to drop the restoration. The rift stays open. Kali puts her arm into it and drives Destruction against the inner layers. Oryndal lays dimensional rings around her, to hold her in spaces that grow smaller and smaller. She draws herself in and keeps the arm where it is. The rings close one after another. Her Destruction eats them from the inside. When the last layer gives, she pulls the arm back. The black core lies bare, and the fragments whirl around it.[3]
He answers by compressing the whole area. Every possible strand of the future gathers at a single point. Debris, streams of energy, and even the light of the explosions are drawn toward the Throne. The pressure takes both goddesses at once. Haruna feels Ikari pulled to the center. Kali feels the layers of space closing on her shoulders. If the contraction had finished, both of them would have been caught in the core he meant to rebuild. Haruna drives Ikari into the edge of the distortion and tears a line through the compressed surface. That alone is not enough. Kali eats the space away around the sword. The blade marks a way out. The Destruction holds the passage open. The contraction loses its symmetry. One side splits, and they get clear.[3]
They do not separate. Haruna shoots past him and forces the damaged core to follow her. On the other side Kali smashes the connections of the black plates, and a gap opens between the outer defense and the exposed core. Aetherys tries to freeze that moment. What he has left then goes into one golden wave, meant to restore both Thrones, and Oryndal's cracks close with it, because a connection to Aetherys is still there. Kali does not cross the wave. She hits the bond that lets Aetherys regenerate Oryndal with himself. The first blow tears it. The second makes the golden light flicker. Ikari goes into the rift she opened and cuts the synchronous flow of time that joins the two. The wave of restoration splits, and each half turns on its own core. With no temporal cover left, he cannot hide the core before she reaches it.[3]
He tries again to shift the core into another dimension. Kali sets her hand on the central plate, holds the structure where it is, and eats the edges of the passage before it can open. Aetherys falls, and the fall throws a shockwave. Oryndal tries to leave on it. The rifts no longer make a continuous path. Kali tears the central plate out of its anchoring and drags the core free. There is no explosion. The black structure falls to pieces. Each fragment loses its connection to the others and vanishes in the Destruction in her hands. He holds for one moment more, braced by the last fragments of space. She cuts the lines that join them. The core loses its form and vanishes. Only an aimless field of wreckage remains.[3]
The two weakened Thrones go up in a pillar of pure darkness. Time and space collapse into one place. The withdrawal that had failed succeeds, and on a greater scale. They return to a stored moment, the beginning of the battle against the elites of the Dragon Emperor, and they are whole. Haruna had felt, through the whole fight, that it was too easy. Kali names the difference. Putting time back on a stored point takes an immense amount of energy. It is not the same thing as rewinding the damage of a blow. They could not have done it alone.[3]
He is the one who speaks when they stand in front of the wives again. Their way of fighting, their powers, their existence: the pair has adapted. In the cosmos, time and space go hand in hand. His form warps. Kali reads it. Time around the two is accelerating. They are burning their lifespan to pull a future potential into the present. Aetherys warps with him and claims that the two concepts make them invincible. Haruna refuses to hold back, and Kali's Destruction tightens on the same refusal. Victor stops both of them. Scathach has already shown too much today. The trump cards stay hidden. The voice of their leader orders the retreat at once. Aetherys does not hesitate. He seizes his partner and tries to leave. A dragon's eye opens over them. Victor's projection tells them they will not escape.[3]
Eleanor, Rose, Kaguya, and Nyx come out of four distortions of space. Eleanor runs Aetherys through. Rose does the same to Oryndal. Kaguya and Nyx set their divine concepts on the pair and hold them. The four take Kali, Haruna, and the prisoners back into their own galaxy. The capture was the lure, meant to draw the enemy leader out of his territory. Darkness comes for the subordinates in a body of pure darkness, larger than a galaxy, with hundreds of arms, and the Emperor comes with him as a body of golden energy. Victor meets that size and refuses to give the subordinates back. Neither leader attacks. Oryndal is already unconscious when Victor looks down at the two and tells Ruby to learn everything about them, and then to erase them. She takes the order. The erasure is not on the page.[3]

### Skills & Abilities
Oryndal wields the divine concept of Space. His demonstrated powers are the manipulation of spatial relationships, distance, dimensions, corridors, compression, and the structures those powers are carried on. Time is not an individual concept under his authority. It enters the paired work when [[Aetherys]] synchronizes with him, and it is what covers him once that bond is cut. A return of both Thrones to a stored moment is also shown. Kali says the two of them could not have done that return alone.[1][2][4][3]
- Spatial Manipulation: Also called Spatiokinesis. Oryndal manipulates space and can wrap his body in the divine concept of Space. When his spatial authority expands alongside Aetherys's Time, the surrounding region becomes subject to combined spatial and temporal distortions. Oryndal can reorder the space around his own body and, while synchronized with Aetherys, manipulate the space surrounding all four combatants. His dimensional rings and central core serve as the structures through which these abilities operate. Kali's Destruction attacks progressively damage those structures on the conceptual level, undermining his spatial defenses. Once the protective armor is torn apart and cracks spread through the structure, his attempts to restore it are repeatedly interrupted before the damaged sections can recover. Later, the connections between the spaces he has made can themselves be eaten. When those lines lose their continuity, the space breaks into segments, and he no longer decides where Kali is.[2][4][3]
- Distance Manipulation: Oryndal controls the distance between locations rather than needing to physically cross the intervening space. He rearranges the space between himself and his destination so that a distant region becomes immediately adjacent, allowing him to advance without traversing the gap. His movements make stars appear to shift and interfere with navigation systems: ships that are physically far apart may register as barely separated, while others disappear from sensors because their positions no longer correspond to consistent spatial references. In combat, he alters the distance between himself and Kali to drive her away from the immediate battle. During synchronization, he helps create continuously changing distances between the goddesses. He can also stretch the gap between Haruna and a Throne at the moment she strikes, so the blow loses its reach. His manipulation can be interrupted when his spatial structures are sufficiently damaged, and Kali can overcome the distance-based defenses by crossing the spatial layers he creates to restrain her. Attacking the distance itself is not the only way through it. She beats the later stretch by striking the connections between the spaces, not the stretch.[1][2][4][3]
- Infinite Approach: Without moving his core, he increases the distance in front of an advancing enemy piece by piece and pushes her back. With every step she appears smaller before the Throne, though she is still going forward. The approach is meant to become a journey with no end. Kali does not chase the receding core. She breaks the joins between the intermediate spaces, crosses the segments, and arrives in front of him.[3]
- Dimensional Manipulation: Dimension is another aspect of Oryndal's authority. He connects the galaxies conquered by Darkness, functioning as a bridge between them through his control over distance and dimensions. During combat, he creates spatial layers to restrain Kali and attempts to shift his central core into another dimension, separating it from the damaged portion of his body. The first transfer fails when Kali strikes one of his main rings and destabilizes the structure supporting the technique. He attempts the shift again after the synchronous bond with Aetherys is cut. Kali holds the central plate in place and eats the edges of the passage before it can open. Oryndal can also create barriers composed of multiple overlapping dimensions, although Kali breaks through his attempted defense before it can fully close.[1][4][3]
- Intergalactic Spatial Connection: Oryndal's responsibilities include connecting the galaxies conquered by Darkness, allowing him to serve as a bridge between them. This is part of his stated role as the Throne of Space. The scene does not establish that this connection is a separate dimension created by Oryndal, nor does it show him transporting an entire fleet through it.[1]
- Portal Creation: Small openings appear in the rings surrounding Oryndal's core, revealing regions of space that are not physically adjacent to his current location. During the battle, he opens several dimensional passages around Aetherys to provide escape routes into other regions of space. He later opens another passage above Haruna, allowing black extensions to emerge and attack her. These scenes establish his ability to create dimensional openings and use them to access or affect distant locations. They do not show him transporting a fleet through a portal.[2][4]
- Spatial Corridors: When he dodges, space can open into successive corridors, each leading a different way. The defense depends on the target choosing one of them. Haruna chooses none. She strikes the edge of the distortion with the flat of Ikari, feels the tension holding the fold, and cuts the junction. Every corridor closes at once, and the pressure throws fragments of black plate out of his body. A later attempt to leave on the shockwave of Aetherys's fall fails for the same class of reason. The dimensional rifts no longer form a continuous path.[3]
- Spatial Extension Projection: Oryndal can attack through a dimensional opening by sending black extensions through it toward a target. He uses this technique against Haruna, opening a passage above her and directing several extensions toward her body. Kali intervenes and destroys the dimensional structure, severing the attack and forcing Oryndal to withdraw. No full range is given, and the extensions are not shown acting once they have been cut off.[4]
- Force-Field Generation: Oryndal can form a defensive barrier from several overlapping dimensions. He attempts to raise this barrier against Kali's concentrated Destruction attack, but she strikes before the structure finishes closing. Her blow penetrates the dimensional layers and damages his central core. The incomplete barrier does not withstand the attack.[4]
- Dimensional Confinement: He can lay dimensional rings around an enemy who has an arm inside his rift, to hold her in spaces that grow smaller and smaller. The rings close one after another. Kali's Destruction eats them from the inside. When the last layer gives, the black core is left bare.[3]
- Spatial Compression: He can compress an entire area into the core he means to rebuild. Debris, streams of energy, the light of the explosions, and every possible strand of the future are drawn to a single point. The pressure takes both goddesses at once and pulls a weapon toward the center while the layers of space close on the other. A finished contraction would have trapped them in that core. It does not finish. One blade marks an exit, Destruction holds the passage open, and the contraction loses its symmetry.[3]
- Structural Self-Repair: Oryndal attempts to repair damage to the structures forming his body and supporting his spatial abilities. During Kali's assault, he tries to restore damaged sections while she continues attacking the dimensional rings and central core. His attempts are repeatedly interrupted, and the spread of Destruction prevents the damaged areas from recovering their previous condition. He also fails to restore the space she has already swept before she is through the opening. A later closing of his cracks is not this repair. It is Aetherys's golden wave, working through a connection that is still there. Once Kali drags the core free, the black structure falls to pieces, each fragment loses its connection to the others, and what remains of him loses its form. This is not unlimited regeneration, and it is not survival of the core by itself.[4][3]
- Space-Time Manipulation: Oryndal does not independently wield Time. When Aetherys synchronizes with him, their divine concepts of Space and Time expand together and combine into a shared battlefield effect. Aetherys supplies the temporal manipulation, while Oryndal provides the spatial component that stabilizes and reshapes the affected region. Their synchronized power distorts the surrounding solar system: distant stars lose their apparent positions, fleet coordinates become inconsistent, distances fold, and light follows seemingly impossible paths. As their synchronization intensifies, distances change continuously and events appear to repeat before reaching their consequences. Oryndal's spatial control allows the pair to separate their opponents and interfere with their movements, while the combined effect enables more precise prediction of the goddesses' actions. When their synchronization breaks, Aetherys loses the spatial stability needed to maintain his temporal distortions, and Oryndal loses the temporal assistance that had improved his ability to predict his opponents' movements. A residual connection still lets Aetherys close Oryndal's cracks and regenerate him together with himself. Cutting that synchronous flow strips the temporal cover, and Oryndal cannot hide his core. After the stored return he states the pairing himself: in the cosmos, time and space go hand in hand. The invincibility claimed for those two concepts is not tested.[1][2][4][3]
- Lifespan Burning: After the stored return, his form warps first. Time around both Thrones accelerates. They burn lifespan to pull a future potential into the present. Kali is the one who reads the mechanism. Oryndal does not explain it. What he says, before the warp, is that the pair has adapted, and that time and space go hand in hand. A measure of how much life is spent is not given.[3]
- Stored-Point Return: The two weakened Thrones explode into a pillar of pure darkness. Time and space collapse into one place, and they return to a stored moment: the beginning of their battle against the elites of the Dragon Emperor. He reappears whole. Kali separates this from a rewind of battle damage. Resetting time to a stored point costs an immense amount of energy, and she says they could not have done it alone. The helper is not named. What the return buys is the fight they have already seen. He is the one who says they have adapted to the wives' way of fighting, to their powers, and to their existence.[3]
- Power Detection: Oryndal can assess the apparent power and status of other divine beings through their presence. Upon sensing Kali, he identifies her as a high-ranking Goddess of Destruction and recognizes her as a member of the enemy's elite. The rank he senses places her one step below a Primordial Being. He also recognizes the divine energy of War emanating from Haruna and instinctively judges her to be at least as dangerous as Kali, despite her presence being less overwhelming.[2]

### Trivia
- Throne of Space. One of the seven Gods directly under the [[Vharzath|Great Darkness]], and above the Ten Chief Gods. The personal name is not in the earlier count of seven generals. It comes out of Lily's extraction.[10][1]
- The charge is distance and dimension. The conquered galaxies are connected through it, and Oryndal is the bridge.[1]
- Often works with [[Aetherys]]. On the field, Aetherys synchronizes with him, and space and time fuse. Without Oryndal's spatial stability, Aetherys cannot keep the temporal distortions up. Without Aetherys's temporal control, Oryndal cannot predict the fight with the same precision.
