### Synopsis

#### Those Who Rule Reality
During the accelerated development of the [[Elderblood Empire]], approximately 170,000 years into the temporal ritual, Ivankov was identified as the father of [[Nikolai Francis Nichev]], a [[Demigod]] whose lineage originated from the Celtic God [[Dagda]]. Dagda was described as an ancestor several generations removed from Nikolai rather than his direct father. Ivankov was specifically identified as the parent responsible for transmitting that Divine ancestry.[1]
Nikolai became involved in a conspiracy connected to powerful foreign Gods originating from the higher regions of the Cosmos. Operating from a facility within the Sagittarius Dwarf Elliptical Galaxy, approximately 80,000 light-years from [[Yggdrasil]], Nikolai attempted to investigate and reproduce the extraordinary Bloodline of [[Victor Elderblood]]. His actions eventually attracted the personal intervention of War, the Emperor's most powerful Herald.[1]
Ivankov himself did not appear during these events. Whether he knew about Nikolai's activities, maintained any relationship with the foreign Gods or faced consequences following his son's capture has not been established.[1]

### Skills & Abilities
Ivankov possesses ancestral ties to the Divine Bloodline of [[Dagda]], establishing his connection to the [[Celtic Pantheon]]'s lineage. However, his own supernatural classification, level of Divine inheritance and combat abilities have not been revealed. His son's status as a [[Demigod]] does not independently establish that Ivankov possesses equivalent Divine powers.[1]

### Trivia
- Ivankov is a descendant of the Celtic God [[Dagda]], although his exact generational relationship to the deity remains unspecified.[1]
- His son, [[Nikolai Francis Nichev]], inherited Divine ancestry through Ivankov's family line.[1]
- [[Juliette Dubois]] is the mother of Ivankov's son. Their marital status has not been confirmed.
- Ivankov was first mentioned in Chapter 1158 and has not made a confirmed direct appearance in the available material.
