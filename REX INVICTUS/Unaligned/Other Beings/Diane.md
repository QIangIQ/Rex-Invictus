Events of the MTWABV timeline (Victor's conquests, the fall of the pantheons, the [[Elderblood Empire]]) are distant background or rumor unless a chapter puts them on the page. ### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 1111
Diane is a Negative World Tree, the twin sister and negative counterpart of [[Diana]], and one of the [[World Trees]] who became connected to [[Victor Elderblood]]. She originally maintained the same unnamed inhabited planet as Diana before both sisters absorbed Energy released by Victor and abandoned their previous position in favor of joining his faction.[2]
Although Diane receives less dialogue than Diana during their debut, she performs one of the most important supernatural feats in the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s planetary infiltration. Her Negative World Tree abilities masked the Imperial operation from Beings capable of sensing the Spectrums of Life, covering a weakness in the Empire's Mark I concealment technology and allowing the mission to conclude with every target captured and no escapees.[2]

### Physical Appearance
Diane's precise physical appearance has not been separately described.
Chapter 1111 states only that she is similar to [[Diana]], but with the color themes reversed.[2]
The chapter does not specify Diane's hair color, eye color, skin color, exact height or clothing. Therefore, black hair, red eyes, purple eyes or any other specific palette used in artwork should be treated as artistic interpretation unless a later chapter provides an explicit description.

### Personality
Diane is considerably quieter than [[Diana]] during their introduction.
After absorbing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Energy, she shared her twin's overwhelming desire to establish a connection with him. When Diana announced that they were leaving, Diane immediately agreed and accompanied her.[2]
Diane was also completely willing to participate in Ion's execution. A dagger similar to Diana's appeared in her hand, and the twins walked toward the wounded Primordial God together before Rose and Eleanor stopped them.[2]
Unlike Diana, who had been Ion's greatest confidant and directly confronted him, Diane showed little interest in arguing with the Primordial God. When Rose explained that Ion was more useful alive, Diane simply accepted the decision and walked away.[2]
This makes her behavior after absorbing Victor's Energy appear calm but no less extreme than her sister's. Diane remained capable of following strategic reasoning and changing course when Victor's allies requested it, indicating that the [[World Trees]]' altered mentality did not reduce them to mindless puppets.[2]

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Diane's first appearance occurred during the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s operation against the planet she maintained with [[Diana]].
Before the operation, a massive quantity of Energy released by [[Victor Elderblood]] had escaped into the Galaxy. [[World Trees]] on numerous planets absorbed portions of it, resulting in accelerated development and significant behavioral changes.[2]
The narration compares the effect to addiction. Victor's Energy was highly natural and therefore compatible with the World Trees, but it also carried the characteristics of his Eldritch nature and his Yandere Divinity. World Trees absorbing the Energy without a filter became increasingly dependent upon it.[2]
Diana and Diane were directly identified as a pair deeply affected by this contradiction.
Before the twins formally joined Victor, [[Rose Adrasteia Elderblood]] and [[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]] had already been negotiating with the World Trees concerning a future alliance. Those discussions had stalled until Victor's Energy reached their planet.[2]
Diana subsequently betrayed Ion, the Primordial God leading the planet's Pantheon.
After Diana wounded Ion through the heart, stabbed one of his eyes with a Cursed Dagger and rejected him in favor of Victor, Diane appeared beside her twin. The chapter describes Diane as similar to Diana with their color themes reversed.[2]
Diana told Diane that it was time to leave.
Diane agreed.
The twins held hands and approached Ion. A dagger similar to Diana's manifested in Diane's hand, and both prepared to kill the Primordial God.[2]
Rose and Eleanor stopped them because Ion was more useful alive.
Diane immediately accepted Rose's instruction and withdrew. Diana then demanded the promised contract. Both sisters signed, and the twins disappeared from the planet and appeared within Victor's Soul.[2]
The operation then revealed that Diane had already made another major contribution.
The Elderblood Empire's Mark I concealment armor could hide appearance, sound and scent, but its designers had overlooked supernatural Beings capable of sensing the Spectrums of Life. This weakness had nearly compromised the invasion.[2]
Diane's Negative World Tree powers concealed the operation from those senses.
Rose explicitly described the World Tree of Negativity's work in masking their actions as impeccable. Without Diane's intervention, the Imperial infiltration could have been discovered.[2]
The experience was immediately incorporated into Imperial development, with Eleanor noting that later generations of armor would correct the weakness exposed during the campaign.

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Chapter 1116 establishes that conventional [[World Trees]] were created female by [[The Universal Tree]], are as old as the planets they maintain and ordinarily have no interest in mortal-style reproduction because their primary role is planetary nourishment.[1]
Diane is not individually named during the later World Tree Network scenes, but she had already entered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul in Chapter 1111.
In Chapter 1136, Victor called World Trees from across his entire Sector. Millions of connections responded, and the arriving Trees entered Victor while Roxanne and Amara attempted to organize them.[3]
Chapter 1178 later describes all World Trees connected to Victor collectively emerging from him as light and planting roots in the reconstructed Mars. Their true existences remained within Victor's inner world, where they were protected even if the planets connected to them were destroyed.[4]
The resulting World Tree Network allowed effectively immediate Galactic communication and transportation across the Empire.
Chapter 1180 additionally establishes that every conventional World Tree is a node in the Universal Tree's greater network of Life. The Trees under Victor represent less than one percent of the World Trees existing throughout the entire Universe.[5]
These are collective developments of Victor's World Tree population; Diane is not given a separate individual scene during them.

### Skills & Abilities
- World Tree Physiology: Diane is a mature Negative World Tree and a daughter of [[The Universal Tree]].[2][1]
- Extreme Longevity: Conventional [[World Trees]] are as old as their associated planets.[1]
- Planetary-Scale Soul: World Tree Souls are essentially planetary in scale.[1]
- Planetary Nourishment: The natural purpose of a World Tree is to nourish the planet connected to her.[1]
- Inter-Connection: World Trees form nodes within the larger Life-system created by the Universal Tree.[5]
- Negative World Tree Nature: Diane is directly identified as [[Diana]]'s Negative counterpart and as the World Tree of Negativity.[2]
This classification does not by itself prove that Diane possesses unrestricted control over the complete Primordial Concept <Negativity>.
- Life-Spectrum Concealment: Diane's most important directly demonstrated ability. Her powers masked the Elderblood forces from supernatural detection methods capable of sensing the Spectrums of Life.[2]
- Presence Concealment: She concealed the actions of the Imperial infiltration force.
- Supernatural Detection Interference: Diane compensated for a flaw in Imperial stealth technology that otherwise left soldiers vulnerable to Life-based perception.
- Large-Scale Concealment: The effect was extensive enough to protect the operation until all objectives were completed.
- Military Support: Rose explicitly credited the World Tree of Negativity's masking as a major reason the operation was not compromised.[2]
- Planetary Semi-Omnipresence (Racial Ability): Mature World Trees can exist through the Nature of their connected planets and possess a form of semi-omnipresence within that sphere.[6]
- Planetary Awareness: They can observe activity throughout their planetary environment.
- Multi-Sensory Observation: Their perception is not restricted to ordinary sight.
- Contractor Perception: World Trees can observe through Beings bearing their connection or contract.[6]
These abilities are explained through [[Aurora Seraphina Evergreen]] as World Tree racial mechanics. Diane does not independently demonstrate their maximum range.
- Energy Absorption: Diane absorbed [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s leaked Energy after it reached her world.[2]
- Compatibility with [[Natural Energy]]: Victor's unusual Energy remained natural enough for World Trees to absorb despite its corruptive Eldritch characteristics.
- Energy-Induced Alteration: The absorbed Energy dramatically changed Diane's priorities and attachment toward Victor.[2]
- Weapon Usage: Diane manifested a dagger similar to Diana's when the twins prepared to execute Ion.[2]
- Soul/Inner-World Integration: Diane entered Victor's Soul immediately after signing the Imperial contract.[2]
The scene does not establish whether entering Victor's Soul is Diane's independent ability or a function of their contract and Victor's World Tree connections.

### Relationships

#### Diana
[[Diana]] is Diane's twin sister and Positive counterpart.
The two [[World Trees]] maintained the same planet as complementary Positive and Negative Trees and were simultaneously affected by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Energy.[2]
Diane followed Diana during the betrayal of Ion, attempted to execute him with her and entered Victor's Soul beside her.

#### Ion
Ion belonged to the Divine order associated with the world maintained by the twins.
Although [[Diana]]'s personal relationship with Ion is described in much greater detail, Diane was sufficiently involved with the same planetary structure to join Diana in turning against him and was prepared to kill him.[2]

#### Victor Elderblood
Diane developed an overwhelming desire for [[Victor Elderblood]]'s Energy after absorbing the uncontrolled Energy released into the Galaxy.[2]
She signed the Imperial contract and entered Victor's Soul with [[Diana]].
The exact romantic status of Diane and Victor is not explored in a dedicated Diane-focused scene. Chapter 1116 explains that [[World Trees]] can consciously develop attraction despite their unusual reproductive nature, but Diane's direct canonical material remains centered upon Chapter 1111 and her entry into Victor's World Tree population.[1]

### Trivia
- Diane is the Negative counterpart of [[Diana]].[2]
- Her canonical hair and eye colors have not been stated.
- Chapter 1111 only establishes that she looks similar to Diana with their color themes reversed.
- Diane's direct specialty is much better established than Diana's: she successfully masked the entire Imperial operation against Life-based supernatural perception.[2]
- The Empire discovered through this incident that its Mark I stealth armor did not sufficiently account for Beings capable of sensing the Spectrums of Life.
- The flaw was scheduled to be corrected in later armor generations.
- Diane was prepared to kill the Primordial God Ion despite showing almost no emotional reaction to the decision.
- When Rose ordered the twins not to kill Ion, Diane accepted immediately.
- Diane signed the Imperial contract without reading it and entered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul alongside Diana.[2]
- Black hair, red eyes and other specific visual details used in artwork are artistic interpretations rather than confirmed novel descriptions.
- Generic claims such as unrestricted Soul Manipulation, omniscience or complete Primordial Negativity control should not be assigned to Diane without an applicable chapter source.
