The Yuurakyos were an extraterrestrial warrior race originating from the former Mid-Level Sector of [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]]. Within that Sector they were commonly known as the "Assassins of [[Galactic Emperor Kolo]]", connecting them directly to Galactic Emperor Kolo and the military power of the [[Kolo Empire]]. Their species was described as having been bred for war, possessing naturally superior bodies, exceptional battle perception, extraordinary fighting talent and the ability to acclimate to unfamiliar hostile environments within only a few weeks.[1]
The original Yuurakyo population was eventually exterminated by Thaloria Xyphora, who spent years studying captured members of the species before engineering a race-specific biological weapon that attacked their brains and killed them almost immediately. Their extinction removed the greatest military power associated with Kolo's Galactic Empire and helped the Thaloria Dynasty establish galactic supremacy.[1]
Despite the destruction of the original population, the species was not permanently lost. Approximately two hundred thousand years into the later development of the [[Elderblood Empire]], Velnorah recreated Yuurakyo specimens through cloning and began extracting their most valuable biological characteristic—their exceptional adaptability—for use in a new engineered technomage population.[2]
Status Clarification
The Yuurakyo are historically extinct because Thaloria eradicated their original civilization and natural population. However, Chapter 1164 establishes that Velnorah retained enough biological information to recreate members of the species as clones. Therefore, "Extinct" refers to the original population rather than the complete destruction of their genetic lineage.[1][2]

### Physical Appearance
Known Yuurakyo were tall humanoids. The male infiltrator displayed in Velnorah's historical recording possessed dark-blue skin and eyes that were entirely blue, without the ordinary distinction in coloration seen in Human eyes.[1]
Because only a limited number of members have been visually described, it is unknown how much physical variation existed throughout the species or whether all Yuurakyo possessed exactly the same coloration.

### Biology
The Yuurakyo were naturally adapted toward warfare even before undergoing the artificial enhancements used by their civilization. Velnorah described their natural bodies as superior to those of other races encountered by the Thaloria Dynasty, while their perception and innate talent in battle were likewise naturally elevated.[1]
Their physiology was therefore divided between two important components: natural racial traits and the additional enhancement produced by their coming-of-age serum.

#### Natural Strength
Yuurakyo physiology was powerful enough to challenge a civilization whose principal advantage was highly advanced technology. When the first known Yuurakyo infiltrator was discovered inside Thaloria's territory, she attempted to capture him alive rather than kill him. Even supported by some of her best generals and advanced technological restraints, the operation suffered severe casualties.[1]
The warrior physically broke through several containment measures and force fields, killed members of Thaloria's forces and even damaged equipment used personally by the First Empress before finally being subdued. The incident was significant enough that Thaloria immediately became interested in reproducing the species' physical advantages for her own civilization.[1]

#### Adaptability
One of the Yuurakyo's most valuable innate characteristics was their ability to adapt rapidly to environmental conditions. A member could require only a few weeks to become acclimated to a new and hostile environment.[1]
This characteristic remained valuable even after Velnorah's standards for warrior races had changed dramatically. During the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s later era, she considered the original Yuurakyo heavily flawed compared with [[True Dragons]] and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s engineered hybrid populations, yet specifically chose to retain their adaptation genes while designing a new artificial race.[2]

### Coming-of-Age Enhancement
The greatest physical abilities displayed by mature Yuurakyo were not entirely natural. Their civilization possessed a coming-of-age ceremony during which an elder injected younger Yuurakyo with a specialized physical-enhancement serum.[1]
The serum dramatically increased their physical capabilities but produced an important biological complication: the resulting modifications were not hereditary. Children did not naturally inherit the full enhanced state of their parents and consequently needed to undergo the same coming-of-age procedure themselves.[1]
The serum also explained what Thaloria initially interpreted as extreme instability within Yuurakyo genetics. Her earliest attempts to clone a captured adult repeatedly failed because she was working with genetic material that had already undergone artificial modification. An experimentally produced child who had never received the serum possessed considerably more stable biology but was also much weaker than his enhanced parents.[1]
Genetic Instability — Corrected
The Yuurakyo should not be described as inherently impossible to clone. Thaloria's original cloning attempts failed because she was studying artificially enhanced adults whose genetics had become unstable after receiving the coming-of-age serum. Velnorah later successfully recreated Yuurakyo clones once her technology and biological knowledge had advanced sufficiently.[1][2]

### Reproduction
Yuurakyo reproduction was unusually demanding and closely connected to the race's combat-oriented nature.
Thaloria first attempted to reproduce a captured male Yuurakyo using females belonging to other races. The experiments failed because developing Yuurakyo offspring required such enormous quantities of nutrition and biological resources that the surrogate mothers could not sustain them.[1]
Believing that only another Yuurakyo could carry the child, Thaloria attempted to convert members of other species into Yuurakyo. These experiments led her to discover that genuine racial transformation could not be accomplished through physical alteration alone: the Soul also needed to be transformed before the body would completely accept the new racial state. Unable to reproduce the authority of a genuine Progenitor, she abandoned that approach.[1]
A second opportunity arose when a female Yuurakyo infiltrated Thaloria's territory in an attempt to rescue the imprisoned male. Because Thaloria had already gathered extensive information from the first captive, she was able to prepare a countermeasure and capture the female much more efficiently.[1]
Even artificial insemination between two members of the species continued to fail. Thaloria eventually discovered that the Yuurakyo reproductive process possessed a behavioral condition derived from the race's warrior nature: the male had to defeat the female and cause her to submit before successful reproduction could occur.[1]
Thaloria ultimately bypassed the normal process through chemical stimulation, administering powerful aphrodisiacs to the two captives. A viable male child was successfully produced, giving her the first unenhanced Yuurakyo specimen available for direct study.[1]

### Relationship with Galactic Emperor Kolo
The Yuurakyo were known within Velnorah's original Sector as the "Assassins of [[Galactic Emperor Kolo]]". Their exact political structure and the precise relationship between the entire species and Galactic Emperor Kolo have not been completely explained, but the designation establishes that Yuurakyo warriors functioned as one of the most important military forces associated with Kolo's Galactic Empire.[1]
Their importance became especially clear during Kolo's conflict with the Thaloria Dynasty. While Thaloria's civilization possessed overwhelming technological advantages against conventional opponents, Yuurakyo warriors could directly break through sophisticated defenses using their own bodies and combat abilities.[1]
Velnorah later referred to the Yuurakyo as the greatest power of the Galactic Empire. Their removal therefore represented far more than the extinction of a dangerous species; it destroyed Kolo's most important known military advantage.[1]

### Conflict with the Thaloria Dynasty

#### The First Captive
During the expansion of the [[Thaloria Empire]], a male Yuurakyo infiltrated individuals operating within Thaloria's sphere of influence. [[Thaloria Xyphora]] detected him and prepared a trap intended to capture the infiltrator alive.[1]
The operation demonstrated why the species had acquired such a reputation. Despite facing some of [[Eldoria]]'s best generals and advanced technology, the Yuurakyo fought through the containment operation, inflicted heavy casualties and damaged Thaloria's equipment before eventually being captured.[1]
Thaloria became fascinated with the species rather than simply executing the prisoner. Her civilization already dominated through technology, while the Yuurakyo possessed biological combat power her people could not easily reproduce. She consequently turned the captive into the center of an extensive research project.[1]

#### Failed Replication
Thaloria initially attempted to clone the warrior, but the genetic material of the enhanced adult was too unstable. She proceeded through multiple alternative approaches:
- Cloning: Failed because of the instability present in the enhanced adult's genetics.[1]
- Cross-Species Artificial Reproduction: Failed because foreign females could not sustain the enormous nutritional requirements of Yuurakyo offspring.[1]
- Racial Conversion: Failed after Thaloria discovered that true transformation required changing the Soul rather than merely restructuring the body.[1]
- Yuurakyo-to-Yuurakyo Artificial Insemination: Initially failed because the necessary dominance/submission condition between the parents had not been fulfilled.[1]
- Forced Reproductive Conditions: Chemical stimulation finally enabled the captured male and female to produce a viable child.[1]
The surviving child ultimately gave Thaloria the information she needed to understand the difference between a natural Yuurakyo and an enhanced adult.

### Extinction
EXTINCTION EVENT
Thaloria's research began as an attempt to obtain Yuurakyo strength for herself. Once she understood their biology well enough, the same research was converted into a weapon capable of eliminating the entire species.[1]
The unenhanced Yuurakyo child revealed that the extreme genetic instability of the captured adults came from their coming-of-age enhancement rather than the species' natural genome. With an unmodified specimen available for comparison, Thaloria gained a much clearer understanding of Yuurakyo biology.[1]
She used this knowledge to engineer a biological weapon specifically designed to exploit the race's physiology. The resulting virus targeted the Yuurakyo brain and killed members of the species almost immediately.[1]
Thaloria deployed the weapon across the Yuurakyo population and successfully exterminated the species. Their disappearance removed what Velnorah described as the greatest military power of Kolo's Galactic Empire. With the Yuurakyo gone, Thaloria's technological civilization faced no comparable obstacle within its original galaxy and eventually achieved galactic supremacy.[1]

### Strategic Consequences
The extermination was an overwhelming short-term victory and a disastrous long-term decision.
Following Velnorah's later rise as an Overlord, her Mid-Level Sector underwent a Convergence and became exposed to the far more dangerous civilizations of the Higher Sectors. New Galactic Emperors appeared whose individual capabilities vastly exceeded the opponents [[Eldoria]] had previously fought. Velnorah and Thaloria themselves could resist such enemies, but the majority of their military could not operate effectively at the same level.[1]
Velnorah therefore concluded that exterminating the Yuurakyo had helped doom the old Thaloria civilization. The race that had once represented its greatest enemy was also one of the few populations with enough natural combat potential and adaptability to have served as a viable mass military force against the new Galactic Emperors.[1]
The historical irony became one of the lessons Velnorah carried with her after losing her Empire: destroying a dangerous enemy could be less useful than understanding how that enemy might be incorporated into a civilization facing even greater future threats.[1]

### Recreation in the Elderblood Empire

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Approximately two hundred thousand years into the development of the [[Elderblood Empire]], Velnorah revisited the Yuurakyo species while working alongside her daughter, [[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]]. By this point she possessed preserved biological information and technology advanced enough to produce cloned Yuurakyo specimens.[2]
Her opinion of the species had changed dramatically.
In her original civilization, Velnorah had considered the Yuurakyo close to her conception of a perfect warrior race. After becoming a True Dragon, living among [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s descendants and studying the hybrid soldiers produced by the Elderblood Empire, she now regarded the Yuurakyo as highly flawed and considerably inferior to the biological standards available to her.[2]
Velina reached a similar conclusion after examining one of the recreated specimens, finding the race far less impressive than the beings to which the younger Imperial generation had become accustomed.[2]
Despite this reevaluation, Velnorah did not dismiss them as useless. She specifically identified their adaptation genes as worth preserving.[2]

#### Technomage Project
Velnorah's objective was no longer to resurrect the Yuurakyo as her ideal soldiers. Instead, she planned to combine:
- the adaptation genes of the Yuurakyo;
- the technomancy genes inherited from her own original race;
- and selected advantageous traits from additional species.[2]
The result was intended to become a population of specialized technomages rather than another warrior race. Velnorah wanted scientists who possessed natural technological abilities, could function in dangerous environments and were personally powerful enough to defend themselves without requiring constant military protection.[2]
She specifically rejected using extremely polarized Angelic and Demonic characteristics because she wanted a comparatively neutral mentality rather than soldiers defined primarily by combat. Knowledge would be implanted directly into the engineered clones so that they could begin functioning without spending years acquiring elementary scientific education.[2]
Velnorah summarized her objective simply: she did not need another collection of supreme elites because the [[Elderblood Empire]] already possessed more than enough. She wanted reliable technomages who could support her research and survive if attacked.[2]
Modern Legacy
The original Yuurakyo civilization remains extinct, but their most valuable biological trait survived. In the Elderblood era, their adaptability became genetic material for an entirely new engineered population rather than the basis for restoring the old warrior society.[2]

### Skills & Abilities

#### Natural Abilities
- Enhanced Condition: Yuurakyo were naturally born with superior bodies compared with many other extraterrestrial races encountered by Velnorah's civilization.[1]
- Enhanced Strength: A captured warrior physically broke through multiple technological restraints and force fields while fighting Thaloria's forces.[1]
- Enhanced Durability: Their bodies allowed them to continue fighting through advanced containment operations and combat against technologically superior forces.[1]
- Natural Combat Aptitude: The species possessed exceptional innate talent for battle and was specifically described as having been bred for warfare.[1]
- Enhanced Combat Perception: Their battle perception was naturally superior to that of ordinary races.[1]
- Environmental Adaptation: They could acclimate to unfamiliar and hostile environments within only several weeks, a characteristic Velnorah still regarded as valuable hundreds of thousands of years later.[1][2]
- Survival Adaptation: Their rapid adjustment to hostile environments made them naturally suitable for deployment beyond their native worlds.[1]

#### Artificial Enhancement
- Coming-of-Age Serum: Young Yuurakyo underwent a ceremony in which an elder injected them with a specialized serum that dramatically strengthened their physical capabilities.[1]
- Physical Enhancement: The serum elevated an adult Yuurakyo significantly beyond an unenhanced member of the species.[1]
- Durability Enhancement: Their artificially strengthened physiology formed part of the enormous difference between the captured adults and the laboratory-born child.[1]
- Non-Hereditary Enhancement: The changes produced by the serum were not genetically passed to offspring, requiring each generation to undergo the ceremony separately.[1]

### Weaknesses
- Species-Specific Biological Vulnerability: Once Thaloria understood Yuurakyo biology, she could engineer a virus that attacked their brains and killed them almost immediately.[1]
- Enhancement-Induced Genetic Instability: The coming-of-age serum destabilized the genetics of enhanced adults enough that Thaloria's original attempts to clone them failed.[1]
- Reproductive Restrictions: Their reproduction depended upon specific dominance and submission conditions between male and female, making conventional artificial reproduction ineffective without recreating or bypassing those conditions.[1]
- Extreme Gestational Demand: Yuurakyo offspring required such large quantities of biological resources that females from other species could not safely sustain them.[1]
- Reliance on External Enhancement: Much of the exceptional power of mature warriors depended upon a serum that was not naturally inherited, meaning offspring began substantially weaker without the traditional coming-of-age process.[1]
- Inferiority to Later Imperial Hybrids: By Chapter 1164, Velnorah considered them severely flawed compared with [[True Dragons]] and the engineered hybrid populations available to the [[Elderblood Empire]].[2]

### Culture
Only a very limited amount of Yuurakyo culture has been directly revealed.
The species was described as a warrior race bred for war, and at least two confirmed traditions reflected that martial orientation:
- Their reproductive process was tied to combat, requiring a male to defeat a female and obtain her submission before successful reproduction.[1]
- Their young participated in a coming-of-age ceremony where elders administered the enhancement serum responsible for the extraordinary physiques of mature warriors.[1]
Beyond these details, the novel has not established a complete Yuurakyo political structure, social caste system, honor code, religion, economy or technological philosophy. Previous claims that every rank in their civilization was decided through combat or that they were explicitly technologically primitive are therefore unsupported and should not be treated as canon.

### Legacy
The Yuurakyo occupy an unusual place in Velnorah's history. They began as enemies whose combat potential threatened the technological supremacy of the Thaloria Dynasty, became experimental subjects in Thaloria's pursuit of stronger biology, and were finally exterminated once their weaknesses became understood.[1]
Their destruction helped create the conditions for the Thaloria Dynasty's short-term galactic victory but contributed to its eventual inability to field enough powerful combatants when much greater enemies arrived after the Sector Convergence. Velnorah consequently came to regard them simultaneously as a dangerous former enemy and a lost strategic resource.[1]
Much later, the enormous difference between the old and new eras became obvious when Velnorah reconstructed the race inside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Empire. Beings she had once considered nearly ideal warriors were now judged inadequate by the standards of a civilization populated by [[True Dragons]], powerful hybrids and artificially engineered combat species. Only their adaptability remained exceptional enough to be deliberately preserved in the next generation of Velnorah's biological research.[2]

### Trivia
- The Yuurakyo first appear in Velnorah's historical explanation in Chapter 892.[1]
- They were commonly known within Velnorah's original Sector as the Assassins of [[Galactic Emperor Kolo]].[1]
- The first shown Yuurakyo was a tall male with entirely blue eyes and dark-blue skin.[1]
- Their extraordinary combat ability was partly natural and partly the result of an artificial coming-of-age serum.[1]
- The coming-of-age enhancement was not hereditary.[1]
- Their genetic instability was therefore not purely a natural flaw of the species; it was heavily connected to the serum used to enhance mature members.[1]
- Although Thaloria's original cloning attempts failed, Velnorah later successfully recreated Yuurakyo specimens, proving that the species was not fundamentally impossible to clone.[1][2]
- Thaloria originally researched the Yuurakyo because she wanted their power for her own civilization. Extermination became the final solution only after her research uncovered a reliable biological weakness.[1]
- Velnorah once considered them close to her ideal warrior race. By Chapter 1164, after centuries of experience with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family and approximately two hundred thousand years of Imperial development, she described them as significantly flawed.[2]
- Despite that criticism, their adaptation genes remained valuable enough to become one of the foundations of Velnorah's proposed technomage clones.[2]
- The original Yuurakyo society remains extinct even though cloned biological descendants now exist.[2]
