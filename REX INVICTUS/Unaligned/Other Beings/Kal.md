### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 458
Kal is an Alpha belonging to the original native population of [[Nightingale]] and the younger brother of Ken. He is one of the few named members of the civilization that inhabited Nightingale before the modern Vampire population came to dominate the planet.
Kal was 418 years old when first introduced, yet his age was only then considered sufficient for him to be treated as an adult by his people.[1] Following the death of his father at the hands of [[Rose Adrasteia Elderblood]], Ken inherited leadership of their village according to native custom and immediately designated Kal as his successor in the event of Ken's own death.[2][1]
Although historically grouped together with the creatures called Monsters, Kal and the other Alphas are distinct from the mindless or artificially modified Monster types encountered by Clan Adrasteia. Alphas were members of Nightingale's original intelligent population and served as leaders of their settlements, while many of the creatures collectively called Monsters were later created, altered or exploited as weapons.[3][4][5]

### Personality
Kal receives very little direct characterization, but his introduction establishes him as considerably less experienced than his older brother Ken.
When Kal entered the control room in Chapter 458, he had no understanding of the crisis occurring outside and innocently asked Ken what was wrong. Ken initially attempted to hide their father's death from him before reconsidering after remembering that Kal was already 418 years old and therefore an adult by their people's standards.[1]
Kal was visibly confused when Ken abruptly informed him not only that their father had died but also that Ken was now the village's new leader and Kal would be trained as his successor. His reaction suggests that, despite his age, Kal had not previously been heavily involved in the leadership, warfare or strategic responsibilities handled by his father and older brother.[1]
Ken specifically told Kal that he would soon learn the true abilities of their enemies, indicating that Kal still lacked practical knowledge concerning the forces threatening their people at the time.[1]
There is not enough material to assign Kal more specific traits such as cowardice, aggression or hatred toward Vampires.

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Kal first appeared during the conflict between Clan Adrasteia and the native forces occupying the wilderness of [[Nightingale]].
His father was an Alpha and leader of their village. While the Adrasteia expedition fought through increasingly powerful Monster forces, Kal's older brother Ken remained inside the native settlement monitoring the battlefield and controlling several of the creatures deployed against the invading Vampires.[2]
Their father personally entered the battle and confronted [[Rose Adrasteia Elderblood]]. Despite the Alpha's confidence in the native forces, Rose overwhelmed the battlefield through the sword techniques of Clan Adrasteia. Her final technique, Nightfall, erased the Alpha together with the remaining Monster forces surrounding him.[2]
Ken witnessed his father's death through the village's surveillance system.
Overcome by anger, he nearly released a Type 7 Monster—the highest-level creature available to the village and one that Ken did not possess sufficient authorization to deploy. Before he could break the rules, Kal unexpectedly entered the room and asked what was happening.[1]
Kal's arrival caused Ken to stop.
For a moment, Ken attempted to hide the situation from his younger brother, but after looking at Kal he reconsidered. Kal was already 418 years old, and Ken decided that he was old enough to understand the responsibilities now being placed upon their family.[1]
Ken told him directly that their father had died while fighting the invaders.
Kal initially responded only with confusion and disbelief.
Ken then explained one of their people's succession customs: when the leader of a village died, his eldest son inherited the position. Their father's death therefore made Ken the new leader of the settlement.[1]
Ken immediately continued:
Kal would be prepared to succeed him if Ken himself died.
[1]
Kal did not understand why such responsibility was suddenly being placed upon him, but Ken told him that he would understand soon and would have to learn the true strength of the enemies threatening their people.[1]
The scene establishes Kal as the next person in the village's line of succession after Ken.

#### King Among Kings
Much later, the true history of [[Nightingale]]'s surviving native population was uncovered during [[Victor Elderblood]]'s final conflict against the Elder Gods.
The beings commonly referred to collectively as "Monsters" were revealed to have a much more complicated history than the Vampires previously understood. [[Ikor-Kar-VI]] and the Elder Gods had systematically exploited Nightingale's native population, using them as experimental material and consuming useful abilities through Predation. Many of the grotesque Monster forms encountered across the planet were products of experimentation involving the natives, Alphas and other supernatural biological material.[5]
By the end of the conflict, only approximately 1,700 surviving natives remained hidden underground, including families and children.[5]
Ken was among those survivors and had become their active leader. When Victor opened the underground settlement, Ken attempted to defend his people despite the enormous difference in power between them. His willingness to confront Victor earned Victor's respect through his Divinity of Martial Honor, and Victor ultimately healed him rather than treating the surviving natives as enemies.[5]
Victor subsequently placed the surviving native population under the supervision of [[Rose Adrasteia Elderblood]] and [[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]], allowing Clan Adrasteia to determine how the remaining Alphas and natives would be integrated into Nightingale's new order.[5]
Kal is not individually named during the Chapter 886 scene. Because Ken and approximately 1,700 members of their people survived, Kal may have been among the remaining population, but the chapter does not explicitly identify him. His individual activities between Chapter 458 and the Elder Gods' defeat remain unknown.

### Skills & Abilities
Kal has never received a personal battle scene, so his exact fighting ability and any unique powers remain unknown.
His known supernatural characteristics derive primarily from his status as an Alpha and member of [[Nightingale]]'s original native population.

#### Alpha Physiology
Alphas are the intelligent leadership class among [[Nightingale]]'s native beings. Unlike many of the more bestial Monster classes encountered by Clan Adrasteia, Alphas possess humanoid forms, intelligence, organized settlements and social hierarchies.[3][4]
As an Alpha, Kal possesses the general supernatural characteristics associated with his people.
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Alphas are supernatural beings physically superior to ordinary Humans.
- Enhanced Durability: Native beings of Nightingale possess bodies adapted to the supernatural environment of the planet.
- Enhanced Stamina: Kal possesses the supernatural stamina expected from his Race.
- Enhanced Senses: Nightingale's native supernatural beings possess senses beyond ordinary Humans.
- Longevity: Kal was already 418 years old while only recently being regarded as sufficiently mature to assume adult responsibilities.[1]
- Humanoid Physiology: Unlike many of the artificially modified or animalistic Monster types, Alphas possess intelligent humanoid bodies.[3]
The novel has not shown Kal personally demonstrating the more extreme regenerative or resurrection properties associated with some engineered Monster classes, so those abilities should not automatically be attributed to him.

#### Intelligence
Unlike the numerous Monster types created or manipulated as weapons, Alphas possess full sapience and organized intelligence.[3][4]
Kal can speak normally, understand social relationships and participate in the succession system governing his village.

#### Leadership Potential
Kal is part of his village's ruling bloodline.
Following his father's death, Ken became leader under the custom that the eldest son succeeds the previous leader. Ken then immediately declared that he would train Kal as his own successor if he died.[1]
This establishes Kal as:
- Heir to the Village Leadership
- Potential Alpha Leader
- Successor to Ken
Kal had not yet completed the necessary training at the time of his debut.

### Relationships

#### Ken
Ken is Kal's older brother and the person with whom he has the strongest demonstrated relationship.
Ken's normally furious demeanor visibly softened when Kal entered the room during Chapter 458. Even while overwhelmed by anger over their father's death, his first instinct was to protect Kal from the truth.[1]
Ken ultimately reconsidered because Kal was already considered an adult.
After becoming village leader, Ken immediately designated Kal as his successor and intended to personally prepare him for leadership in case he died fighting their enemies.[1]
This demonstrates both considerable trust and the expectation that Kal would eventually inherit responsibility for their people if Ken could no longer lead them.
Ken later survived the Elder Gods' destruction and became the leader representing the approximately 1,700 remaining natives before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[5]
Kal's individual relationship with Ken during this later period has not been shown.

#### Unknown Alpha
Kal and Ken's father was an unnamed Alpha who served as leader of their village.
He personally fought against [[Rose Adrasteia Elderblood]] and the other invaders during Clan Adrasteia's expedition through [[Nightingale]]'s wilderness.[2]
Rose ultimately killed him with her sword technique Nightfall.[2]
His death triggered the village's succession customs, making Ken leader and Kal the next designated successor.[1]

#### Rose Adrasteia Elderblood
[[Rose Adrasteia Elderblood]] killed Kal's father during the conflict between Clan Adrasteia and the [[Nightingale]] natives.[2]
Kal himself is not shown confronting Rose and does not personally express hatred toward her on-page.
Later, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] placed the surviving natives under the supervision of Rose and [[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]] after learning the true circumstances behind their exploitation by the Elder Gods.[5]
If Kal survived among the remaining natives, this would place him under the authority of the same woman responsible for killing his father, although no direct reaction from Kal has been shown.

### Power
Kal's individual combat strength is unknown.
He belongs to the Alpha population, which is significantly more intelligent and socially organized than the ordinary Monster classes, but Kal has never participated in a direct fight.
His father was capable of commanding large numbers of Monsters and personally fighting Clan Adrasteia, while Ken possessed enough authority to access the village's advanced Monster containment systems. Kal was still being prepared to inherit those responsibilities when he first appeared.[2][1]
Consequently, Kal should not currently be given:
- a specific Monster Type;
- a combat tier;
- a unique supernatural ability;
- or scaling equal to his father or Ken
without an individual feat.
