### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Endless was mentioned in Chapter 1074 when [[Victor Elderblood]] exposed records from the past while confronting [[The Judges of The Abyss]]. The records demonstrated that the Judge had repeatedly reacted with hostility or suspicion whenever particularly exceptional Beings appeared throughout history.[1]
Among the examples shown were <Light>, <Order> and <Endless>. When the record concerning Endless appeared, the Judge complained:
"Why doesn't Death keep <Endless> under control?"[1]
The statement establishes some form of jurisdiction or responsibility between [[True Death]] and Endless, although the exact nature of their relationship has not been explained. It is unknown whether Endless is a subordinate, creation, servant, inhabitant of Death's domain or simply a Being whose nature falls under True Death's Authority.
No direct appearance or further information concerning Endless was provided during the scene.

### Skills & Abilities
Very little is known about Endless' abilities.
- Overlord: Endless is classified as an Overlord, placing him among the exceptional cosmic rulers known to exist beyond ordinary planetary and Pantheon-level powers.
- Unknown Abilities: Endless' name, powers, Race, Energy source and supernatural nature have not yet been explained.
- Connection to [[True Death]]: The Judge of the Abyss expected True Death to be capable of keeping Endless "under control", indicating that Endless falls under some form of Death-related jurisdiction or responsibility.[1] The exact mechanics of this relationship are unknown.
