**[[Sanguis Noctis Regis]]**, more commonly known as the Blood of the Night King and also referred to as RH Null or the Golden Blood, is an exceptionally rare bloodline associated with the highest hierarchy of certain Creatures of the Night. (Lore adapted from *My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** This material is adapted into the Rex Invictus world at the lowest priority: Rex Invictus wins any conflict, then the Flamel material, then MTWABV. Events of the MTWABV timeline (Victor's conquests, the fall of the pantheons, the [[Elderblood Empire]]) are distant background or rumor unless a chapter puts them on the page. Sanguis Noctis Regis, more commonly known as the Blood of the Night King and also referred to as RH Null or the Golden Blood, is an exceptionally rare bloodline associated with the highest hierarchy of certain Creatures of the Night.[1][2]
The bloodline possesses several extraordinary characteristics, including enhanced learning speed, superior adaptability, more efficient growth, resistance to mind-related interference, inherited knowledge, and the potential to place its bearer at the top of the hierarchy of whichever compatible Creature of the Night they become.[1][3]
Rather than belonging exclusively to Vampires, the Blood of the Night King is associated with the broader concept of a Night King. Its exact manifestation depends upon the race of its bearer. [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] explained that if someone possessing the blood became a Werewolf instead of a Vampire, they would not become an ordinary subordinate Werewolf but would immediately possess the potential of a True Alpha capable of eventually becoming the King of the race.[1]
The bloodline is also closely connected to the creation of Progenitors among certain Creatures of the Night. Chapter 849 later establishes that RH-null was normally a prerequisite for both Vampire and Werewolf Progenitors, although exceptional outside interference can bypass this requirement.[2]

### Description
To ordinary Humans, the blood appears as the extraordinarily rare RH-null blood type. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] possessed this blood from birth, long before becoming a Vampire.[1]
Among supernatural beings, however, the blood possesses a much greater significance.
Thousands of years before the events of the story, ancient Vampires referred to it as:
Sanguis Noctis Regis
or:
The Blood of the Night King.[1]
[[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] possesses the same blood and personally explained its nature to Victor Elderblood after recognizing that the young Vampire possessed the same bloodline.[1]
The blood is sometimes called Golden Blood, particularly in later portions of the story.[2][4]

### Nature
The fundamental property of Sanguis Noctis Regis is not simply that it makes its bearer physically stronger.
Its greater importance is the potential contained within the blood.
Vlad explains that the race into which a bearer of Sanguis Noctis Regis is born or transformed determines how that potential manifests.[1]
For a compatible Creature of the Night, the blood naturally pushes its bearer toward the highest hierarchy of that species.
For example, Vlad explains that if [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had been transformed into a Werewolf rather than a Vampire, he would not have become an ordinary Beta.
Instead, he would have become a:
True Alpha
with the potential to eventually become:
King of the [[Werewolves]].[1]
This principle is later reinforced in Chapter 963, where Victor observes the existence of an alternate Timeline in which Leona reached him before Violet and Victor subsequently became the Progenitor of Werewolves rather than the Vampire Progenitor.[4]

#### Race-Dependent Expression
Sanguis Noctis Regis should therefore not be treated as a Vampire-exclusive bloodline.
Its manifestations can depend upon the Creature of the Night possessing it.
A Vampire Night King and Werewolf Night King would possess different racial characteristics because the blood amplifies and develops the hierarchy of the race into which the individual belongs.
The relationship can broadly be understood as:
Sanguis Noctis Regis
↓
Compatible Creature of the Night
↓
Highest Racial Potential
↓
Potential Night King / Progenitor
The exact racial abilities obtained afterward depend upon the species.
For this reason, abilities belonging specifically to Vampire Night Kings should not automatically be attributed to every theoretical bearer of Sanguis Noctis Regis.

### Known Bearers
[[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] is a natural-born Vampire who possesses the complete Blood of the Night King.[1][5]
[[Victor Elderblood]] was born as a Human possessing RH-null blood. Unlike Vlad, Victor was not naturally born as a Creature of the Night; he was later transformed into a Vampire through a ritual.[1]
[[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]] inherited the blood incompletely from Vlad. Chapter 129 explicitly states that if Vlad and Victor possess 100% of the Blood of the Night King, Ophis possesses approximately 50%.[5]

#### Victor's Exceptional Case
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s case is considered unusual even among bearers of the Blood of the Night King.
Vlad was:
born with the blood as a Vampire.
Victor was:
born with the blood as a Human and later transformed into a Vampire.[1]
The Vampire Ritual did not create Victor's RH-null blood.
Victor already possessed it.
What the ritual did was interact with his existing abnormal blood in an unusual manner.
During Victor's transformation, the blood of [[Violet Elderblood Snow]], [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]], and [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] was involved in the ritual. Victor subsequently acquired the elemental Vampire powers associated with all three heiresses.[1]
Vlad explicitly points out that Victor's case differs from his own because Victor began as an ordinary Human before being transformed.
Therefore, the correct progression is:
Victor is born Human with RH-null
↓
Vampire Ritual
↓
Sanguis Noctis Regis reacts abnormally to the ritual
↓
Victor inherits characteristics from the three Vampire Bloodlines
rather than:
Vampire Ritual creates Sanguis Noctis Regis

### Abilities and Characteristics

#### Enhanced Learning
One of the first characteristics explicitly attributed to Sanguis Noctis Regis is enhanced learning speed.[1]
The bearer can learn and understand new abilities considerably faster than an ordinary member of their race.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s extraordinary rate of development after becoming a Vampire is partially attributed to this characteristic.
This should not be interpreted as automatic omniscience or instant mastery.
Training, experience and understanding are still required, but the bloodline greatly increases the rate at which its bearer can develop.

#### Superior Adaptability
Sanguis Noctis Regis grants superior adaptation capabilities.[1]
This characteristic allows the bearer to adjust to new abilities, conditions and changes more efficiently than normal members of their race.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s adaptability became one of his most important characteristics throughout the story, although his later adaptability is further enhanced by numerous additional factors beyond Sanguis Noctis Regis.
Therefore, not every later adaptation feat performed by Victor should automatically be attributed solely to the Blood of the Night King.

#### Efficient Growth
The blood allows its bearer to grow stronger more efficiently.[1]
This does not simply mean the bearer receives an immediate fixed increase in physical strength.
Instead, the blood improves the efficiency with which the bearer develops.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s rapid development as a young Vampire was one of the reasons Vlad immediately connected many of his abnormalities to Sanguis Noctis Regis.[1]

#### Hierarchical Potential
The greatest characteristic revealed by Vlad is the bloodline's relationship with racial hierarchy.[1]
Depending upon the Creature of the Night the bearer becomes, the potential contained within the blood places them near or at the highest level of that race's natural hierarchy.
Vlad uses [[Werewolves]] as his example.
Had [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] become a Werewolf:
- he would not have become a Beta;
- he would have become a True Alpha;
- and he would possess the potential to become the King of the Werewolves.[1]
Vlad consequently explains that whoever possesses the Blood of the Night King possesses the potential to become the King of an entire race.[1]

#### Enhanced Pride
Sanguis Noctis Regis also appears to influence the instincts and personality of its bearer.
Vlad directly connects [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s unusual pride and refusal to lower his head to others with the qualities of his blood.[1]
According to Vlad, it would be unnatural for someone whose blood inherently drives them toward the top of the hierarchy to instinctively behave like a subordinate.
This characteristic should not be interpreted as an absolute supernatural rule preventing the bearer from ever bowing, submitting, respecting another person or accepting authority.
It is more accurately described as an unusually powerful hierarchical pride and dominance instinct.
[[Scathach]] deliberately withheld information concerning Victor's blood partly because she did not want knowledge of his potential to make him excessively arrogant.[1]

#### Mental Resistance
Vlad states that the Blood of the Night King makes its bearer immune to mind-related damage.[1]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] immediately connects this explanation to several earlier incidents involving mental and empathic abilities.
However, this protection is not completely absolute against every higher-order supernatural effect.
During Victor's meeting with [[Aphrodite]], his natural resistance as a Night King protected him from ordinary forms of manipulation, but Victor recognized that resistance of this type was insufficient against beings who embody the relevant Concept itself.[6]
Therefore, the ability is better understood as extraordinarily high natural resistance or immunity to conventional mental interference rather than unrestricted immunity to all possible conceptual effects.

#### Enhanced Charm
For a Vampire bearer, Sanguis Noctis Regis enhances the effectiveness of Vampiric Charm.
Vlad explains that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] can control certain special individuals using his Vampiric Charm because of the properties of his blood.[1]
Victor connects this explanation to his previous ability to influence [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] and even other Vampires.
Because Vlad's explanation is specifically given to two Vampire Night Kings, the exact equivalent ability of a non-Vampire bearer may differ according to their race.

### Inherited Knowledge
Those who inherit the Blood of the Night King receive a form of inherited ancestral knowledge.[3]
This ability is compared to the inherited knowledge of Dragons.
Dragons can be born possessing magical information inherited from their predecessors.
Night Kings possess a similar phenomenon, but their inherited information primarily consists of general knowledge or ancient common sense rather than purely magical techniques.[3]
Examples include basic information concerning:
- supernatural races;
- Dragons;
- Gods;
- Angels;
- Demons;
- Blood;
- supernatural customs;
- and other information that would have been considered ordinary knowledge in ancient eras.[3]
Alter [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explains that some of this information became outdated or ceased being considered common knowledge as civilizations changed.
The inherited knowledge therefore functions somewhat like an internal repository of ancient supernatural information.

#### Accessibility
Possessing these inherited memories does not necessarily mean the bearer can immediately consciously access all of them.
In [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s case, Alter Victor could access information contained within his Night King inheritance that Victor himself had not yet consciously unlocked.[3]
Alter explained that Victor would eventually gain access to everything after becoming sufficiently powerful and fully assimilating his Progenitor existence.[3]

### Effects on Other Creatures
The blood of a Night King is exceptionally valuable to other Creatures of the Night.

#### Strengthening
Vlad states that when Creatures of the Night drink Blood of the Night King, they will slowly become stronger.[1]
This characteristic explains part of the abnormal development experienced by individuals who repeatedly consumed [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s blood during his early Vampire life.
Victor himself immediately connects Vlad's explanation to the increased strength of:
- his wives;
- [[Scathach]];
- and other Vampires who had regularly consumed his blood.[1]
The effect is progressive rather than an instantaneous transformation into the same level as the Night King.

#### Vampire Satiation
Vampires who consume Blood of the Night King are satiated much more effectively than when drinking ordinary blood.[1]
Vlad specifically connects this property to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ability to help stabilize [[Scathach]]'s long-standing Bloodlust problem.
The exceptionally high quality of the blood allows Vampires to satisfy their hunger using considerably less blood than would normally be required.

#### Reciprocal Strengthening
The relationship is not entirely one-sided.
Vlad explains that when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] drinks the blood of powerful individuals, Victor himself can also become stronger and gain greater control over the abilities associated with that blood.[1]
Victor had already experienced this phenomenon without understanding its cause.
After drinking [[Scathach]]'s blood, controlling his powers became easier.
After drinking Natashia's blood, Victor's control over Lightning improved.[1]
This interaction helped Victor understand why exchanging blood with powerful Vampires was repeatedly accelerating the development of both sides.

### Vampire Night King Abilities
Certain abilities associated with Sanguis Noctis Regis are specifically described as abilities of Vampire Night Kings rather than universal traits of every bearer.

#### Existence Absorption and Memory Acquisition
Vampire Night Kings possess a passive ability connected to the absorption of beings they kill.[7]
[[Scathach]] explains that after a Vampire Night King kills and absorbs an individual, a process involving the target's existence and Soul fragments can occur.
Through those fragments, the Night King can obtain the memories of the victim.[7]
Under normal circumstances, this ability was expected to awaken only after the Vampire Night King had undergone significantly greater maturation, roughly around the period of becoming a thousand-year-old Vampire.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] awakened the ability extraordinarily early.[7]

#### Predatory Identity Protection
When Violet and Sasha expressed concern that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] might lose himself inside the enormous number of foreign memories he absorbed, [[Scathach]] explained that this should not occur normally.
The Night King is the predator consuming the individual.
According to Scathach, Victor's own blood would not allow him to become lost inside the memories of his prey.[7]
This protection is specifically discussed in relation to the Vampire Night King's memory-absorption ability.
It should therefore not automatically be treated as universal immunity against every form of Soul or memory manipulation.

### Progenitor Blood
Sanguis Noctis Regis is closely related to Progenitors, particularly Progenitors belonging to Creatures of the Night.
However, the bloodline and every ability possessed by a Progenitor should not be treated as exactly the same thing.
Chapter 849 later explains that RH-null was normally a requirement for both Vampire and Werewolf Progenitors.[2]
This supports the connection:
Blood of the Night King → Potential for Progenitor status
among those races.
However, different Progenitor species possess their own racial authorities and abilities.
For example, Soul authority associated with Vampire Progenitors is a characteristic of the Vampire Progenitor rather than simply a generic ability automatically granted to every RH-null Human.

#### Blood Control
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually demonstrates near-absolute control over his own blood.
In Chapter 313, Victor explains that as a Progenitor, he can control his blood as naturally as moving one of his own hands.[8]
If someone consumes Victor's blood without his permission, Victor can deliberately activate that blood inside the target and cause enormous internal damage.
This ability should therefore be classified primarily as an expression of:
Vampire Progenitor Blood Control
rather than one of the initial universal characteristics directly listed by Vlad for Sanguis Noctis Regis.

### Victor's Blood
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s blood developed several exceptional properties beyond the basic characteristics Vlad described in Chapter 125.
These later effects result from the combination of:
- Sanguis Noctis Regis;
- his status as a Vampire Progenitor;
- his personal Devouring property;
- his Soul;
- and his later evolution.
They should therefore not automatically be attributed to every bearer of the Blood of the Night King.

#### Devouring
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s blood possesses an extremely aggressive Devouring characteristic.[8]
When Mizuki consumed Victor's blood while suffering from severe Miasma contamination, his blood behaved like a hungry predator and completely consumed the Miasma inside her body.[8]
Victor states that if someone consumed his blood without permission, the blood itself could devour that individual.
This Devouring characteristic later became important to several abilities and objects connected to Victor, including [[Junketsu]].

#### Healing
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s blood possesses extraordinary healing properties.
When he deliberately allowed the critically injured [[Mizuki Elderblood]] to drink it, even a single drop immediately began affecting her body.[8]
Continued consumption:
- removed the Miasma contaminating her;
- healed her internal injuries;
- removed old scars;
- repaired years of accumulated damage;
- and restored her physical condition.[8]
Victor himself was surprised by the extent of the healing.
This feat should be described specifically as a demonstrated property of Victor's Progenitor blood rather than automatically claiming every incomplete bearer of Sanguis Noctis Regis possesses identical regenerative blood.

#### Temporary Vampiric Characteristics
After consuming [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s blood, Mizuki temporarily developed several Vampire-like characteristics, including blood-red eyes.[8]
Victor determined that she had not permanently become a Vampire.
Instead, she had temporarily become something resembling a Human-Vampire hybrid while his blood remained active inside her body.[8]
Had she continued consuming sufficiently large quantities, Victor believed she would eventually have transformed completely into a Vampire.[8]

### Relationship with Progenitor Creation
Chapter 849 provides important later clarification concerning the relationship between RH-null and Progenitors.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explains that the original requirement for becoming a Vampire or Werewolf Progenitor was:
RH Null / Golden Blood.[2]
He finds this particularly strange because Vampires and [[Werewolves]] are opposing races, yet both share the same fundamental requirement for becoming their respective Progenitors.
Victor consequently theorizes that the similarity may indicate a deeper common origin between the two races.[2]
The exact origin of this connection remains unresolved.

#### Tasha's Exception
[[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]] became the Werewolf Progenitor despite not originally possessing RH-null blood.[2]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly recognizes this as an exception to the normal requirement.
He determines that some form of external interference—possibly the System, the Balance, or another Primordial influence—was involved in allowing Tasha to complete the transformation.[2]
For this reason, Tasha should not automatically be listed as a confirmed natural bearer of Sanguis Noctis Regis merely because she became the Werewolf Progenitor.
Unless later material explicitly states that her blood itself transformed into Sanguis Noctis Regis, the more accurate classification is:
Werewolf Progenitor who bypassed the normal RH-null requirement through external intervention.

### Victor's Werewolf Possibility
The versatility of Sanguis Noctis Regis is further demonstrated through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s alternate possibilities.
Chapter 963 establishes that among the infinite alternate Timelines recorded within Creation, there exists a Timeline where Leona reached Victor before Violet did.[4]
In that Timeline, Victor did not become the Vampire Progenitor.
He became the:
Progenitor of [[Werewolves]].[4]
This confirms the fundamental principle Vlad introduced in Chapter 125: Victor's special blood was not inherently limited to producing a Vampire ruler.
The race into which he was transformed determined the path his Night King potential followed.

### Confirmed Bearers
- [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] — Possesses the complete Blood of the Night King and was born as a natural Vampire.[1][5]
- [[Victor Elderblood]] — Possessed RH-null from birth as a Human and retained its supernatural potential after becoming a Vampire.[1]
- [[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]] — Inherited approximately 50% of Vlad's Blood of the Night King.[5]

### Related Cases
- [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]] — Did not naturally possess RH-null but nevertheless became the Werewolf Progenitor through an abnormal transformation involving external interference.[2]
- Alternate [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] — A possible Victor from another Timeline became the Werewolf Progenitor after Leona transformed him instead of Violet.[4]

### Limitations and Clarifications
Several characteristics commonly attributed to Sanguis Noctis Regis require clarification.

#### It Does Not Automatically Make the Bearer a Progenitor
Possessing RH-null provides the required potential and places compatible Creatures of the Night toward the top of their racial hierarchy, but merely possessing the blood should not automatically be equated with having completed Progenitor evolution.
Ophis is the clearest example.
She possesses 50% of Vlad's Blood of the Night King but was not automatically transformed into another Vampire Progenitor simply by inheriting it.[5]

#### It Does Not Grant Every Progenitor Ability
Soul Manipulation, complete Progenitor Blood Control, racial creation abilities and other Progenitor authorities should remain classified under Progenitors or the corresponding character's abilities.
Sanguis Noctis Regis is strongly connected to Progenitor potential among Creatures of the Night, but it is not synonymous with every power of every Progenitor species.

#### Mind Protection Is Not Absolutely Unbreakable
Vlad initially describes the bearer as immune to mind-related damage.[1]
However, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later encounters conceptual beings capable of bypassing ordinary racial resistance. [[Aphrodite]]'s Conceptual authority could affect him despite his natural Night King protection.[6]

#### Victor Is Not the Standard Bearer
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later blood possesses numerous abilities that were not established as universal properties of Sanguis Noctis Regis.
His blood is additionally influenced by:
- Vampire Progenitor authority;
- Devouring;
- three inherited Count Bloodlines;
- Roxanne;
- Dragon evolution;
- Divinity;
- and later Eldritch characteristics.
Victor's late-story blood feats should therefore not automatically be assigned to Vlad, Ophis or hypothetical Night Kings without supporting evidence.

### Misconceptions
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] gained RH-null from Violet's Vampire Ritual
False. Victor possessed RH-null while he was still Human. The ritual interacted abnormally with his pre-existing blood.[1]
Every RH-null bearer automatically becomes a Progenitor
Not established. The blood provides the potential and normally acts as an important requirement, but other conditions are involved.[1][2]
The Blood of the Night King is Vampire-exclusive
False. Vlad explicitly explains its potential using [[Werewolves]] as an example, and Chapter 963 confirms an alternate Victor who became the Werewolf Progenitor.[1][4]
Every ability Victor's blood possesses is an ability of Sanguis Noctis Regis
False. Victor's blood also contains his personal Progenitor, Devouring, Draconic and other evolved characteristics.
Sanguis Noctis Regis grants absolute inability to bow or submit
Overstated. Vlad connects the blood to Victor's powerful pride and hierarchical instincts, but does not establish a literal unrestricted compulsion that makes voluntary submission impossible.[1]
Tasha was born with Sanguis Noctis Regis
False. Chapter 849 explicitly establishes that she lacked the normal RH-null requirement and became a Progenitor through an abnormal exception.[2]

### Trivia
- Sanguis Noctis Regis can be translated approximately as Blood of the Night King.
- Ancient Vampires had already used the name thousands of years before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] learned of it.[1]
- Vlad and Victor both possess the complete bloodline, while Ophis inherited approximately 50%.[5]
- [[Scathach]] deliberately avoided explaining Victor's blood to him because she feared knowledge of his potential would make him excessively arrogant.[1]
- The blood is exceptionally attractive and delicious to Creatures of the Night.[1]
- Vampires are more easily satiated by the Blood of the Night King than by ordinary blood.[1]
- Creatures of the Night can gradually become stronger by repeatedly consuming the blood.[1]
- Night Kings inherit ancient common knowledge from their predecessors in a manner conceptually similar to the inherited magical knowledge of Dragons.[3]
- Vampire Night Kings possess a passive ability allowing them to obtain memories through the absorption of their victims' existence and Soul fragments.[7]
- Victor awakened several Night King abilities much earlier than Scathach believed should normally have been possible.[7]
- Chapter 849 establishes RH-null as the normal historical requirement for both Vampire and Werewolf Progenitors.[2]
- Chapter 963 confirms an alternate Timeline where Victor became the Werewolf Progenitor rather than the Vampire Progenitor.[4]
