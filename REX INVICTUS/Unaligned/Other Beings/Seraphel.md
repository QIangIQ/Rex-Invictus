### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 1207
Seraphel. I represent Truth.
—Seraphel
Seraphel is a male god and Grandmaster who serves [[Aetheryon]], one of the four great leaders of the Upper Sector. His own domain is Truth. He represents that Truth for his Emperor. He does not serve <Law>, and Truth is not the name of the leader he answers to.
<Law> is a different power. That faction is the herald of the Primordial of Balance, and it moves only when that Primordial allows it. Seraphel's master is Aetheryon, the one the System records as <Emperor> and also as <Kaiser>. When that leader shows himself, he is a gigantic skull of pure fire. Beside him, the other rulers of that tier are <Darkness> and <Light>. <Darkness> made his principal galaxy into his own body. <Emperor>, <Light> and <Law> soaked a galaxy in their power and made that galaxy their domain.[1][2][3][4][5]
The Emperor's public face is justice. His ten chief gods are known by name outside his faction because of that preaching, and each of them stands for one aspect of justice. Seraphel is not named in that count. Aetheryon calls him one of his judges, elite of that empire. What he claims for himself is the domain of Truth, held for his Emperor. With him are [[Laelzorith]], whose domain is Law, and [[Dravakar]], whose domain is Retribution.[3][6][5]
He meets [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] outside the galaxy where [[Kali Elderblood]] and [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]] are fighting the Thrones [[Aetherys]] and [[Oryndal]]. In the formation he is the reach: the scythe covers the exits and the flanks while the other two press. [[Scathach]] takes one of his arms. The three are still fighting when their Emperor orders a retreat they cannot make. The attack that follows unmakes the body he is wearing. The life is kept by the [[World Trees]] Aetheryon spends on his elite.[6][7][5][8]

### Physical Appearance
His face is never seen. The helmet is narrow and elongated, and its smooth surface hides the head completely. Height, eyes, hair, skin, species and race are unknown. The narration treats him as a god.[7]
The armor is silver and gray plate with purple detail across the chest and the joints. In the fight he carries a scythe. The handle is long metal. The blade is curved, and geometric markings and strange characters cover the blade, the handle and the armor.[7]
When he first names himself, two swords appear in his hands. Once the fight is underway, the weapon in his hands is the scythe, and [[Dravakar]] is the one with two swords.[6][7]
That armor, that scythe, and the body under them are what the battle leaves behind. [[Scathach]]'s Big Bang takes the suit apart before he can react. The body follows, down to atoms and then to elementary particles. When the blast subsides there is no armor, no weapon, and not a fragment from which that body could be put back together. The arm she had already taken goes with the rest. Only residual energy of creation remains where he stood.[5]

### Personality
He gives his name and names Truth. He does not add a speech. He is the one being spoken to when another of the three says [[Scathach]] will listen to no one, that her existence screams for blood, and that the mission will go forward in the name of the Great One. He does not say those lines, and he is not shown answering them. He is also not the one who claims they only came to watch.[6]
In the fight he holds the outside of the formation. He threatens the ways out instead of standing in the press, and he comes off the flank when one of the other two is about to be finished. He is the one who says, aloud, that he does not understand what is happening. He knows they should not be losing ground, and they still are not getting anywhere against her.[7]

### Synopsis

#### Volume 9: Those Who Rule Reality
The Upper Sector is held by four factions, and none of them will let a fifth stand. [[Vharzath]] offers [[Aetheryon]] twenty percent of the spoils if he will pull [[Victor Elderblood]] aside. Aetheryon calls the price too low to make an enemy of Negativity's Chosen. Vharzath tells him he will never spend his main fleet, and that smaller factions are already being used to test the new Empire. Aetheryon answers with fifty percent, and with his main fleet only if Darkness sends the Abominations. Vharzath agrees, and takes the first choice of what he receives. Neither of them means to keep the bargain if a better chance appears.[2]
After [[Hela Elderblood]] erases one of Darkness's weapons with END, the same leader comes back as the flaming skull, serious this time. END can be warded off only by BEGIN. Darkness has lost that Throne, and Aetheryon has no subordinate who can answer it. He tells Vharzath to deploy his Thrones while he deploys his own Enforcers. Vharzath refuses a blind large-scale attack. Aetheryon leaves the method to him and keeps his own. The later order is narrower. He will hit the Elderblood forces, and he will not send his elites into Victor's galaxy. That place is the domain of Negativity's Chosen, and only he himself would enter it. Once enough elites are gone, the two will attack that galaxy together. His answer is that he handles things his own way.[9][4]
That is the war Seraphel walks into. He is not sent into the supergalaxy. He comes to the legions, outside the galaxy where the fight with Darkness is already happening. [[Kali Elderblood]] and [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]] are there against [[Aetherys]], Throne of Time, and [[Oryndal]], Throne of Space. [[Scathach]] watches them and is satisfied. They are not trading beams. They are using the martial foundation she and Victor taught, and the Thrones have power with almost none of that foundation.[10][11][6]
Victor has already told Eleanor and Rose to accompany Scathach and take command if they must. When she feels the three arrivals, she tells those two she is counting on them. Rose asks Victor if he foresaw it. Scathach does not wait for the three to look the field over.[10][6]
She meets them as a pillar of pure red Dragon power, dense enough to make its own gravity and pull at the nearest galaxies. She calls three against one a fair fight and manifests a spear covered in uncanny runes. The runes make them waver. Her face grows wilder and more draconic. She names herself Scathach Elderblood, wife of Emperor Victor Elderblood and Supreme Commander of the Empire, and tells them to entertain her or die.[6]
One of them says they only came to watch. She calls it a lie and stops the power from streaming outward, gathering it around her body like armor. The pressure nearly triples. Another of them speaks to Seraphel by name. She will listen to no one, because her existence screams for blood. Their mission will be carried out in the name of the Great One. Capturing her is useful because she is the Dragon Emperor's confidante. That Emperor is Victor. It is not the leader Seraphel serves.[6]
[[Laelzorith]] gives his name and says he represents the Law of his Emperor. A spear appears in his hand. Seraphel names himself and says he represents Truth. Two swords appear in his hands. [[Dravakar]] names himself and says he represents Retribution. A scythe appears in Dravakar's hand. Scathach's smile widens. She calls the three Grandmasters a jackpot.[6]
Victor sees them. His eyes narrow. He orders [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]] to go with Nyx Elderblood and step in only if it is necessary. Kaguya says Scathach will not like it. Victor says he will deal with her later, and that her safety outranks her pride. Kaguya bows and vanishes into the shadows.[6]
Scathach reads their spacing before the first blow. This group is far better organized than the others she has been fighting. They attack together. Laelzorith opens with the spear. Dravakar comes from the other side with both swords. She gives half a step, lets one blade pass and knocks the other aside. She does not counter. Seraphel is already trailing the scythe in behind Dravakar, and the blow would have reached her in a moment. She leaves the spot before the blade finishes its arc. The scythe cuts empty space. He has to stop the swing and pull the weapon back.[7]
She uses that gap on Laelzorith and is stopped by the spear. The formation does not need every weapon to land. Each of them only has to close the space the others leave open. Laelzorith is hit on the forearm and gives ground. Dravakar fills the empty place, and Scathach steps inside his reach and hits the wrist, then the shoulder. Seraphel tries to take that gap. The scythe comes down on a diagonal and makes her let go of Dravakar. She tilts, knocks the handle off with her forearm, and cannot answer without turning her back to the other two. She falls back with all three still in sight. The gods stop and reset.[7]
Laelzorith holds the spear low and covers his legs. Dravakar waits for her to commit to defense before he closes. Seraphel stops striking. He sets the scythe to threaten the exits instead. Scathach calls them worthy. Teamwork, decisions and strength are all there. She says they deserve the title of Grandmaster, and that they are competent in every respect.[7]
Laelzorith thrusts again. She slips the point and tries to walk in along the shaft. Dravakar hits the flank before she reaches him. She catches one sword and ducks the other. Seraphel uses that duck to shut the way out. For a moment all three weapons box her in. She does not smash through. She presses into the sword that caught her and shifts toward Dravakar, so he has to drop his guard or cut his own companion. She swings under the sword, hits his knee, and is already clear of the scythe before Seraphel can finish the blow. All three retreat. Seraphel can no longer swing with the freedom he had. She has started to read the rhythm.[7]
The wounds are not grave, but they stack, and every pause makes the formation worse. Dravakar realizes her spear is blocking his natural regeneration. They stop coming all at once. When Laelzorith's feint leaves him open, she does not take it, because Dravakar is already closing and Seraphel has the scythe ready to shut the gap. She steps back and makes all three come to her.[7]
Dravakar arrives first and takes a thrust through the chest plate. He is driven back, hand on the wound. Seraphel is in front of him. An unnamed order, "Retreat.", is answered with "Yes." Seraphel attacks anyway. She parries the scythe as if it costs her nothing. Laelzorith tries her back and is driven off his ground. She does not chase him. Seraphel has already opened the distance and started a wide sweep to keep her from getting back to Laelzorith.[7]
She ducks into the blade's radius. He tries to cut the corner. The long handle is slow to change direction at that distance. She hits his forearm, then his shoulder, before he has his footing. He tries to shove her off with the handle itself. She parries that and drives him back with a thrust to the torso. He keeps the scythe, but he needs a moment to stand properly. She is gone before Dravakar can close the circle.[7]
They stop wasting blows, but the formation still needs space and timing, and she uses that. When Seraphel threatens the flank, she backs into a spot where his own scythe also stops the other two from coming in. They spend more time fixing their places than landing what they planned. Seraphel says it aloud. He asks what is going on. The fight feels like a constant disadvantage, and he knows that should not be true. They are simply not getting anywhere against her. Laelzorith growls that she is treating them like children. Dravakar says they need to slow her momentum.[7]
Dravakar goes in again and is hit in the wrist and the face. Laelzorith steps in to take the pressure off him, meets her spear, and takes a fist in the abdomen. He loses his footing. Her spearhead is going for his neck. Seraphel comes off the flank to save him. She does not attack at once. She lets the sweep turn him as he recovers the weapon, and by the time the blade is aimed at her she is too close to use it. Her spear takes the arm. The arm is gone.[7]
The three fall back, resetting weapons and cover. None of them is beaten, and the formation can still be dangerous if she makes a mistake, but the first offensive is over. Laelzorith takes the front. Dravakar stays beside him. Seraphel is no longer swinging with both arms.[7]
An order, "Insert them here!", is answered with "Yes." The speaker is not named. Golden power rises, and the healing that should have been instant takes seconds that matter. The wounded god demands to know what kind of spear can keep even their Emperor's strength from closing the wound quickly. The answer, also unnamed, is that the weapon seems mixed from different divine concepts, and that this is what blocks the regeneration. That wound is Dravakar's. Scathach takes the healing enemy's arm, calls it repulsive, and burns it away in Dragonfire. She ignores the healing and gives the three an A+ for combined performance and teamwork.[7]
She tells Laelzorith, Dravakar and Seraphel to rejoice. Outside her family, they are among the strongest she has met. Ranked inside it, they could challenge her sisters Rose and Eleanor, though only with great difficulty. Then she stops. Beside Victor the same technique is flawless and still useless to her: no aggression, too methodical, none of his flexibility, and no understanding of how to use their strength against an equal. She calls them boring next to him.[7]
The fight is what makes her own level obvious to her. She is the wife who challenges Victor whenever she can. He stands with the leaders of the largest factions in the Upper Sector, and she is the woman who fights him most often. She had been holding back with her sisters, so she had not noticed. She is the second strongest woman in the family. Azathoth is stronger, and only because Azathoth surpasses her in everything except technology and martial arts. In technology, warfare and hand-to-hand combat, she places herself first behind Victor. Opponents who can rival Rose and Eleanor are not close to that.[7]
She tightens her grip. Crimson power bursts from the hand. Dragon runes and Eldritch runes light along the spear. As thanks for making that clear, she offers them a masterpiece. The space between the galaxies goes black, a primeval dark in which nothing can be seen. Their Emperor's voice reaches them: retreat, then retreat now. They cannot move. Scathach spreads her arms and speaks the name Heptagrammakos. She tells them to look, because creation is beautiful, and everything begins with an explosion.[7]
The name is the start of the attack. The faction leaders move as soon as she speaks it. Darkness orders the nearby Thrones who were about to attack her to pull back. Light and Law watch. Aetheryon is the one who shouts for his soldiers to withdraw. He calls the three his judges, the elite of his empire. Losing all three would completely weaken his influence. He calls Darkness a coward for retreating. He knows the attack will destroy souls, and that a resurrection afterward will be impossible.[5]
He rises from his throne to leave his galaxy and save them himself. Victor's gaze, from the enemy galaxy, stops him. An empty homeland is an opening. The choice is the elite or the homeland. He spends a trump card he knows will draw the wrath of the primal force of life. A being who can threaten a galaxy is not replaced. He sends hundreds of [[World Trees]]. They materialize before him, bow, and answer, "Yes, my Emperor." Their eyes are empty. The reason is already gone.[5]
Scathach's confined creation becomes a local Big Bang. The first wave hits the three at once. The armor of Laelzorith, Dravakar, and Seraphel comes apart before they can react. The bodies follow, broken down to atoms and then to elementary particles. There is no time to resist or to run, and no matter left from which those bodies could be rebuilt. When it subsides, the place where they stood holds no armor, no weapon, and not a fragment of them. Only the residual energy of creation is there. Left alone, the expansion could have reached distant galaxies. [[The Limbo Guy|The Limbo]] type appears with Infinity and holds it in.[5]
The lives are what the trees were sent to buy. Where the three vanished, new beings begin to form. Scathach sees the life force at once and says that hundreds of worlds were sacrificed just for her. Seven rays of light open, and the enemy elite comes out, each one an aspect of justice. Seraphel is not named in that number, and he does not speak again. Victor understands that the elite lived because of the World Trees.[5]
Aetheryon orders an immediate withdrawal. The name his elite answer with is Emperor Aetheryon. The price of the trees comes after. [[The Universal Tree]], the Progenitor of Life, forbids every World Tree in that territory from helping him and drives them off their posts. What remains to Aetheryon is his own divinity. The planets of the faction die. He orders the evacuation into space. A World Tree that has spent all of its energy dies with the planet it was feeding, and hundreds of them were spent so the elites would live. After that, anyone marked by him who approaches a World Tree makes that tree leave at once. Those trees stand under the Dragon Emperor.[5][8]

### Skills & Abilities
His domain is Truth, held for the Emperor he serves. He never uses that Truth as an attack. What he fights with is the scythe's reach, and the place he holds on the outside of the formation. None of that stops Heptagrammakos. The body he fights in is unmade. The life that remains is not a technique of his.[6][7][5][8]
- Domain of Truth: He says he represents Truth. That is his domain under Aetheryon, in the same sense that [[Laelzorith]]'s is Law and [[Dravakar]]'s is Retribution. No effect of the Truth itself is shown. This is not the authority of the <Law> faction, and it is not [[Anna Elderblood]]'s Concept of Truth.[4][6]
- Weapon Creation: Two swords appear in his hands when he names himself. In the battle the weapon he uses is a long scythe, with geometric characters on the blade and the handle. No separate technique of either weapon is named. The Big Bang leaves neither the scythe nor the hands that held it.[6][7][5]
- Scythe combat: He trails the cut in behind Dravakar, swings down on a diagonal to break [[Scathach]] off a companion, and sets the blade over the exits instead of trading in the press. The reach is also the weakness. She steps inside the arc, the long handle is slow to change direction, and a parry of the shaft lets her drive him back. She takes the forearm, the shoulder, a thrust to the torso, and then the arm itself. He keeps the scythe after the first of those hits. After the last, the arm is gone. The formation fight does not defeat him. The blast that follows leaves no body.[7][5]
- Formation combat: He is the flank and the net. When Laelzorith is open, the scythe is what stops her from taking the opening. When she would walk back onto Laelzorith, Seraphel is already at a distance, cutting the approach off. When Laelzorith is about to take the spearhead in the neck, Seraphel is the one who comes off the flank. She uses that same reach against the formation: backing into his arc also stops the other two. They are still in that formation when the retreat order arrives and they cannot move.[7]

#### Power and Threat Level
- Grandmaster: [[Scathach]] names all three Grandmasters before the fight, and after the first exchange she says they deserve it. Teamwork, decision-making and strength are all exceptional. She later gives the three an A+ for combined performance and teamwork.[6][7]
- Galactic scale: The three arrive as beings of galactic scale. [[Aetheryon]] treats beings who can threaten a galaxy as priceless, and that is why he spends the [[World Trees]] rather than let the three die. No separate feat of Seraphel's is given a wider measure than the arrival. He is not shown at the level of the four leaders, and he is not one of Darkness's Thrones.[6][5]
- Ranked by Scathach: Outside her family, she calls the three among the strongest she has met. Inside it, she says they could challenge Rose and Eleanor only with great difficulty. Set beside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], the same technique is flawless and still too rigid. She calls them boring next to him. Once she measures herself against that gap, opponents of Rose and Eleanor's level are no longer close to her.[7]
The gravity-dense Dragon power, the pressure she triples around her body, the runes that make the three waver, the spear that stops natural regeneration, and Heptagrammakos are hers.
