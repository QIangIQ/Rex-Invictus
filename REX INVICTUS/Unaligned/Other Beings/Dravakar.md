### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 1207
Dravakar. I represent Retribution.
—Dravakar
Dravakar is a male god and Grandmaster who serves [[Aetheryon]], one of the four great leaders of the Upper Sector. His own domain is Retribution. He represents that Retribution for his Emperor. He does not serve <Law>, and Retribution is not the name of the leader he answers to.
<Law> is a different power. That faction is the herald of the Primordial of Balance, and it moves only when that Primordial allows it. Dravakar's master is Aetheryon, the one the System records as <Emperor> and also as <Kaiser>. When that leader shows himself, he is a gigantic skull of pure fire. Beside him, the other rulers of that tier are <Darkness> and <Light>. <Darkness> made his principal galaxy into his own body. <Emperor>, <Light> and <Law> soaked a galaxy in their power and made that galaxy their domain.[1][2][3][4][5]
The Emperor's public face is justice. His ten chief gods are known by name outside his faction because of that preaching, and each of them stands for one aspect of justice. Dravakar is not named in that count. Aetheryon calls him one of his judges, elite of that empire. What he claims for himself is the domain of Retribution, held for his Emperor. With him are [[Laelzorith]], whose domain is Law, and [[Seraphel]], whose domain is Truth.[3][6][5]
He meets [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] outside the galaxy where [[Kali Elderblood]] and [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]] are fighting the Thrones [[Aetherys]] and [[Oryndal]]. In the formation he is the close fighter. The swords come in pairs, at different heights, and he is the one who steps into a gap the moment it opens. Her spear goes through his chest and stops his regeneration. Their Emperor's power cannot close that wound at once. The three are still fighting when that Emperor orders a retreat they cannot make. The attack that follows unmakes the body he is wearing. The life is kept by the [[World Trees]] Aetheryon spends on his elite.[6][7][5][8]

### Physical Appearance
His face is never seen. The helmet has an angular silhouette and a crest that runs backward. Height, eyes, hair, skin, species and race are unknown. The narration treats him as a god.[7]
The armor is cut for speed. Black and silver plates, shoulder pieces, and overlapping segments cover the arms, the legs and the torso. In the fight he carries two swords. The blades are dark. The crossguards are narrow. They are built for blows that follow each other without a pause.[7]
When he first names himself, a scythe appears in his hand. Once the fight is underway, the weapons in his hands are the two swords, and [[Seraphel]] is the one with the scythe.[6][7]
That armor, those swords, and the body under them are what the battle leaves behind. [[Scathach]]'s Big Bang takes the suit apart before he can react. The body follows, down to atoms and then to elementary particles. The chest wound and the burned arm go with the rest. When the blast subsides there is no armor, no weapon, and not a fragment from which that body could be put back together. Only residual energy of creation remains where he stood.[5]

### Personality
He gives his name and names Retribution. He does not add a speech. He is not the one who says they only came to watch. He is not the one who tells [[Seraphel]] that [[Scathach]] will listen to no one, or who says the mission will go forward in the name of the Great One.[6]
In the fight he does not wait. When [[Laelzorith]] gives ground, Dravakar is already in the empty place. He will drop his own guard rather than cut a companion. He is also the one who says they need to slow her momentum, and the one who refuses, for the moment, when Laelzorith orders him to heal with their Emperor's power. The answer is "Not yet." He goes back in.[7]

### Synopsis

#### Volume 9: Those Who Rule Reality
The Upper Sector is held by four factions, and none of them will let a fifth stand. [[Vharzath]] offers [[Aetheryon]] twenty percent of the spoils if he will pull [[Victor Elderblood]] aside. Aetheryon calls the price too low to make an enemy of Negativity's Chosen. Vharzath tells him he will never spend his main fleet, and that smaller factions are already being used to test the new Empire. Aetheryon answers with fifty percent, and with his main fleet only if Darkness sends the Abominations. Vharzath agrees, and takes the first choice of what he receives. Neither of them means to keep the bargain if a better chance appears.[2]
After [[Hela Elderblood]] erases one of Darkness's weapons with END, the same leader comes back as the flaming skull, serious this time. END can be warded off only by BEGIN. Darkness has lost that Throne, and Aetheryon has no subordinate who can answer it. He tells Vharzath to deploy his Thrones while he deploys his own Enforcers. Vharzath refuses a blind large-scale attack. Aetheryon leaves the method to him and keeps his own. The later order is narrower. He will hit the Elderblood forces, and he will not send his elites into Victor's galaxy. That place is the domain of Negativity's Chosen, and only he himself would enter it. Once enough elites are gone, the two will attack that galaxy together. His answer is that he handles things his own way.[9][4]
That is the war Dravakar walks into. He is not sent into the supergalaxy. He comes to the legions, outside the galaxy where the fight with Darkness is already happening. [[Kali Elderblood]] and [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]] are there against [[Aetherys]], Throne of Time, and [[Oryndal]], Throne of Space. [[Scathach]] watches them and is satisfied. They are not trading beams. They are using the martial foundation she and Victor taught, and the Thrones have power with almost none of that foundation.[10][11][6]
Victor has already told Eleanor and Rose to accompany Scathach and take command if they must. When she feels the three arrivals, she tells those two she is counting on them. Rose asks Victor if he foresaw it. Scathach does not wait for the three to look the field over.[10][6]
She meets them as a pillar of pure red Dragon power, dense enough to make its own gravity and pull at the nearest galaxies. She calls three against one a fair fight and manifests a spear covered in uncanny runes. The runes make them waver. Her face grows wilder and more draconic. She names herself Scathach Elderblood, wife of Emperor Victor Elderblood and Supreme Commander of the Empire, and tells them to entertain her or die.[6]
One of them says they only came to watch. She calls it a lie and stops the power from streaming outward, gathering it around her body like armor. The pressure nearly triples. Another tells [[Seraphel]] that she will listen to no one, because her existence screams for blood. Their mission will be carried out in the name of the Great One. Capturing her is useful because she is the Dragon Emperor's confidante. That Emperor is Victor. It is not the leader Dravakar serves.[6]
[[Laelzorith]] gives his name and says he represents the Law of his Emperor. A spear appears in his hand. Seraphel names himself and says he represents Truth. Two swords appear in Seraphel's hands. Dravakar names himself and says he represents Retribution. A scythe appears in his hand. Scathach's smile widens. She calls the three Grandmasters a jackpot.[6]
Victor sees them. His eyes narrow. He orders [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]] to go with Nyx Elderblood and step in only if it is necessary. Kaguya says Scathach will not like it. Victor says he will deal with her later, and that her safety outranks her pride. Kaguya bows and vanishes into the shadows.[6]
Scathach reads their spacing before the first blow. This group is far better organized than the others she has been fighting. They attack together. Laelzorith opens. The spear goes for her chest. She twists, and the point passes her shoulder. Dravakar is already coming from the other side. The two swords follow at once, one at the lower abdomen and one cutting the escape. She gives half a step. The first blade passes. She knocks the second aside with the shaft. She does not counter. Seraphel's scythe is already coming in behind Dravakar. She leaves the spot before that blade finishes.[7]
She uses the gap on Laelzorith and is stopped by the spear. The formation does not need every weapon to land. Each of them only has to close the space the others leave open. Laelzorith aims at her waist, is hit on the forearm, and gives ground to cover the arm. Dravakar fills that place at once. The swords come in at different heights, so one parry cannot take both. She turns the first. The second scrapes her armor before she can get her body out of the line. She does not stay between the blades. She steps inside his reach, where the wide cuts are harder, and hits his wrist with the lower end of the spear. The sword loses its lead. The next hit takes the shoulder, and he has to pull the arm in to get cover back. Seraphel's scythe comes down on a diagonal and makes her let him go.[7]
They reset. Laelzorith holds the spear low and covers his legs. Dravakar waits. He does not close until she commits to defense. Seraphel stops striking and uses the scythe to threaten the exits. Scathach calls them worthy. Teamwork, decisions and strength are all there. She says they deserve the title of Grandmaster, and that they are competent in every respect.[7]
Laelzorith thrusts again. She slips the point and tries to walk in along the shaft. Dravakar hits the flank before she reaches him. She has to break off, catch one sword, and duck the other as it passes her head. Seraphel shuts the way out. For a moment all three weapons box her in. She does not smash through. She presses into the sword that caught her and shifts her body toward Dravakar, so he cannot swing the second blade without cutting his own companion. He drops the guard. That is enough. She swings under the sword and hits his knee, and she is already gone before Seraphel's scythe finishes. All three retreat.[7]
He will not put weight on the bad leg. The sword in the right hand stays steady. The left hand has to be reset after the hits on the wrist. The wounds are not grave, but they stack, and every pause makes the formation worse. At this speed there is no time for it. He asks himself what kind of spear that is. Her weapon is blocking his natural regeneration.[7]
They stop coming all at once. Laelzorith feints a thrust and leaves himself open. She does not take it, because Dravakar is already closing and Seraphel has the scythe ready to shut the gap. She steps back and makes all three come to her. He is the first to arrive. The right sword leads. The left is meant to finish it. She turns the first blade and hits the hand on the second. The sword stays in his fingers, but the path changes, and he has to yank the arm back or put the point through his own body. She uses that hesitation. A short thrust goes through the chest plate. The impact stops him and drives him back. His hand goes to the wound. Seraphel is in front of him.[7]
An unnamed order, "Retreat.", is answered with "Yes." Seraphel attacks and is parried. Laelzorith tries her back and is driven off his ground. She does not chase. Whenever Laelzorith tries to wall off her movement after that, she slips toward Dravakar and makes him drop the attack. Seraphel says aloud that he does not understand it. They should not be losing ground, and they still are not getting anywhere. Laelzorith growls that she is treating them like children. Dravakar's answer is that they need to slow her momentum. Laelzorith orders him to heal the wound with their Emperor's power. He says not yet.[7]
He goes in fast instead. Both swords follow, one on the torso and one at the legs. She clears the first, pulls the leg out of the second, and shoves his arm off with the shaft. Before he can reverse the sequence she hits the wrist, then the face. Laelzorith advances so the pressure comes off him. She lets Dravakar go, meets the spear, and puts a fist in Laelzorith's abdomen. Seraphel comes off the flank to save Laelzorith and loses the arm. The three fall back. None of them is beaten, and the formation can still be dangerous if she makes a mistake, but the first offensive is over. Laelzorith takes the front. Dravakar stays beside him.[7]
An order, "Insert them here!", is answered with "Yes." The speaker is not named. Golden power rises from the wounded body, and the healing that should have been instant takes seconds that matter. The wounded god demands to know what kind of spear can keep even their Emperor's strength from closing the wound quickly. The answer, also unnamed, is that the weapon seems mixed from different divine concepts, and that this is what blocks the regeneration. That wound is the chest thrust. Scathach takes the healing enemy's arm, calls it repulsive, and burns it away in Dragonfire. She ignores the healing and gives the three an A+ for combined performance and teamwork.[7]
She tells Laelzorith, Dravakar and Seraphel to rejoice. Outside her family, they are among the strongest she has met. Ranked inside it, they could challenge her sisters Rose and Eleanor, though only with great difficulty. Then she stops. Beside Victor the same technique is flawless and still useless to her: no aggression, too methodical, none of his flexibility, and no understanding of how to use their strength against an equal. She calls them boring next to him.[7]
The fight is what makes her own level obvious to her. She is the wife who challenges Victor whenever she can. He stands with the leaders of the largest factions in the Upper Sector, and she is the woman who fights him most often. She had been holding back with her sisters, so she had not noticed. She is the second strongest woman in the family. Azathoth is stronger, and only because Azathoth surpasses her in everything except technology and martial arts. In technology, warfare and hand-to-hand combat, she places herself first behind Victor. Opponents who can rival Rose and Eleanor are not close to that.[7]
She tightens her grip. Crimson power bursts from the hand. Dragon runes and Eldritch runes light along the spear. As thanks for making that clear, she offers them a masterpiece. The space between the galaxies goes black, a primeval dark in which nothing can be seen. Their Emperor's voice reaches them: retreat, then retreat now. They cannot move. Scathach spreads her arms and speaks the name Heptagrammakos. She tells them to look, because creation is beautiful, and everything begins with an explosion.[7]
The name is the start of the attack. The faction leaders move as soon as she speaks it. Darkness orders the nearby Thrones who were about to attack her to pull back. Light and Law watch. Aetheryon is the one who shouts for his soldiers to withdraw. He calls the three his judges, the elite of his empire. Losing all three would completely weaken his influence. He calls Darkness a coward for retreating. He knows the attack will destroy souls, and that a resurrection afterward will be impossible.[5]
He rises from his throne to leave his galaxy and save them himself. Victor's gaze, from the enemy galaxy, stops him. An empty homeland is an opening. The choice is the elite or the homeland. He spends a trump card he knows will draw the wrath of the primal force of life. A being who can threaten a galaxy is not replaced. He sends hundreds of [[World Trees]]. They materialize before him, bow, and answer, "Yes, my Emperor." Their eyes are empty. The reason is already gone.[5]
Scathach's confined creation becomes a local Big Bang. The first wave hits the three at once. The armor of Laelzorith, Dravakar, and Seraphel comes apart before they can react. The bodies follow, broken down to atoms and then to elementary particles. There is no time to resist or to run, and no matter left from which those bodies could be rebuilt. When it subsides, the place where they stood holds no armor, no weapon, and not a fragment of them. Only the residual energy of creation is there. Left alone, the expansion could have reached distant galaxies. [[The Limbo Guy|The Limbo]] type appears with Infinity and holds it in.[5]
The lives are what the trees were sent to buy. Where the three vanished, new beings begin to form. Scathach sees the life force at once and says that hundreds of worlds were sacrificed just for her. Seven rays of light open, and the enemy elite comes out, each one an aspect of justice. Dravakar is not named in that number, and he does not speak again. Victor understands that the elite lived because of the World Trees.[5]
Aetheryon orders an immediate withdrawal. The name his elite answer with is Emperor Aetheryon. The price of the trees comes after. [[The Universal Tree]], the Progenitor of Life, forbids every World Tree in that territory from helping him and drives them off their posts. What remains to Aetheryon is his own divinity. The planets of the faction die. He orders the evacuation into space. A World Tree that has spent all of its energy dies with the planet it was feeding, and hundreds of them were spent so the elites would live. After that, anyone marked by him who approaches a World Tree makes that tree leave at once. Those trees stand under the Dragon Emperor.[5][8]

### Skills & Abilities
His domain is Retribution, held for the Emperor he serves. He never uses that Retribution as an attack. What he fights with is a pair of swords, the habit of filling a gap the moment it opens, and a regeneration her spear shuts off. None of that stops Heptagrammakos. The body he fights in is unmade. The life that remains is not a technique of his.[6][7][5][8]
- Domain of Retribution: He says he represents Retribution. That is his domain under Aetheryon, in the same sense that [[Laelzorith]]'s is Law and [[Seraphel]]'s is Truth. No effect of the Retribution itself is shown. This is not the authority of the <Law> faction.[4][6]
- Weapon Creation: A scythe appears in his hand when he names himself. In the battle the weapons he uses are two swords, dark blades and narrow crossguards, built for blows that follow each other. No separate technique of either weapon is named. The Big Bang leaves neither the swords nor the hands that held them.[6][7][5]
- Dual-sword combat: The pair does not come on one line. One sword takes the abdomen while the other cuts the escape. The next exchange puts them at different heights, so one parry cannot cover both, and the second scrapes her armor. He hits the flank when she tries to walk in on Laelzorith, and he is the first to reach her when they stop attacking all at once. She still gets inside the wide cuts. The wrist, the shoulder, the knee, the hand on the second sword, a thrust through the chest plate, the wrist again, and the face. One of his own blades nearly goes into his body when she changes its path. The formation fight does not defeat him. The blast that follows leaves no body.[7][5]
- Formation combat: He is the one who occupies ground the moment a companion gives it up. He waits for her to commit, then closes. He will drop his guard rather than cut Laelzorith or Seraphel. She uses that. Pressing into him makes the second sword a danger to his own side, and slipping toward him makes him break off whenever Laelzorith tries to wall her in. After the offensive fails he stays at Laelzorith's side. They are still in that formation when the retreat order arrives and they cannot move.[7]
- Regeneration: The hits stack, but they are not what stops him. Her spear blocks his natural regeneration. Laelzorith orders him to heal through their Emperor's power. He refuses at first. When the healing does start, it is golden, and it does not finish at once. Their Emperor's strength is not enough to close that wound quickly. While it is still closing, [[Scathach]] burns the arm. What keeps the life afterward is not that healing. Aetheryon spends the energy of hundreds of [[World Trees]], and those trees save the lives of his elites.[7][5][8]

#### Power and Threat Level
- Grandmaster: [[Scathach]] names all three Grandmasters before the fight, and after the first exchange she says they deserve it. Teamwork, decision-making and strength are all exceptional. She later gives the three an A+ for combined performance and teamwork.[6][7]
- Galactic scale: The three arrive as beings of galactic scale. [[Aetheryon]] treats beings who can threaten a galaxy as priceless, and that is why he spends the [[World Trees]] rather than let the three die. No separate feat of Dravakar's is given a wider measure than the arrival. He is not shown at the level of the four leaders, and he is not one of Darkness's Thrones.[6][5]
- Ranked by Scathach: Outside her family, she calls the three among the strongest she has met. Inside it, she says they could challenge Rose and Eleanor only with great difficulty. Set beside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], the same technique is flawless and still too rigid. She calls them boring next to him. Once she measures herself against that gap, opponents of Rose and Eleanor's level are no longer close to her.[7]
The gravity-dense Dragon power, the pressure she triples around her body, the runes that make the three waver, the spear that stops natural regeneration, and Heptagrammakos are hers.
