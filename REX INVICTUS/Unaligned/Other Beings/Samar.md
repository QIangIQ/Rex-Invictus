Samar is the ancestral planetary homeland of the [[Werewolves]] and the principal center of their civilization. Werewolves historically call it their promised land, while the ancient [[Fenrir]] considers the planet part of his territory through his primal territorial instincts.[1][2] Samar is not the name of the Werewolf capital; the planet's Royal Capital is Eclipse Ventus.[3]
Samar possesses stronger gravity than Earth, a highly unusual day-night cycle and a paired World Tree system consisting of [[Aurora Seraphina Evergreen]], the mature [[Amara Elderblood|World Tree of Positivity]], and [[Aria Seraphina Evergreen]], the much younger World Tree of Negativity.[4][5] The Werewolf civilization that later developed around them was historically ruled by [[Volk Fenrir]] and [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]], until Tasha evolved into the modern Werewolf Progenitor and displaced Volk as the supreme racial ruler of the Werewolves.[6][7]
Samar later became directly connected to [[Victor Elderblood]]. Victor resonated his Positive and Negative Energies with Aurora and Aria and claimed the planet, creating a supernatural connection between himself, its [[World Trees]] and Samar as a whole.[8] By Chapter 986, Victor was described as effectively owning Samar alongside [[Nightingale]] and Earth through his planetary connections.[9] This broader sovereignty does not replace Tasha's position as the local Queen and Progenitor of the Werewolves; Victor stands above Samar as Emperor and planetary sovereign while Tasha administers its native civilization.
By Chapter 1179, Samar had undergone an enormous Imperial transformation. The planet had been enlarged until it was described as three times the size of old Earth, Eclipse Ventus had expanded beyond the scale of South America, and the Werewolf bloodline had become so pure and powerful that it had nearly returned to its ancestral condition by older standards. Samar nevertheless possessed only fifty individuals with planetary-level strength, all fifty of whom were deliberately developed hybrids.[10] At the same time, Samar's Lower Sector had reached its present developmental limit and became one of the external Sectors selected for physical merger with Victor's own Sector.[10]

### Overview
Samar developed independently from Earth and [[Nightingale]] as the major planetary civilization of the Werewolf race. Its society possesses a Royal Family, powerful Alpha bloodlines, a distinct political hierarchy, supernatural commerce, its own capital and a culture heavily shaped by physical strength and Werewolf instincts.[3][11]
The planet itself is far older than the Werewolf civilization inhabiting it. Aurora existed during Samar's earliest biological development and, as a Positive World Tree, participated in the creation and maintenance of Life over a process lasting millions of years. Aria only began forming much later, after the appearance of sentient beings generated sufficient Negative Energy for a complementary Negative World Tree to emerge.[5]

### Cosmological Position

#### Lower Sector
Samar is associated with an unnamed Lower Sector. Earlier explanations use the terminology Low-Level Sector, while later cosmological discussions refer to regions of this class simply as Lower Sectors.[12][10]
Chapter 835 explains that Sectors develop through different stages and that Samar's region was encountered by the ancient Werewolf Progenitor during an unusually favorable transitional period. The region was naturally moving from an inhospitable developmental state toward one capable of sustaining advanced Life, allowing the Werewolf population to establish itself without the type of external correction later encountered in less mature Sectors.[12]
The [[Werewolves]] did not create Samar or personally produce the planetary environment. The planet's biological foundation developed through the World Tree system long before their civilization existed.[5]

#### Relation to Earth and Nightingale
Samar is not located in Earth's Solar System. When Aurora explained the interplanetary World Tree network, Samar and [[Nightingale]] were both explicitly treated as examples of inhabited worlds existing in completely different stellar systems from Earth.[15]
Later cosmological observation makes the separation even clearer. In Chapter 1020, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] extended his perception throughout the Sector containing Earth until he reached its surrounding barriers. He detected smaller connections leading toward other Sectors and identified them as likely pathways toward Nightingale, Samar and the Witches' homeworld.[16]
The novel has not provided a confirmed numerical distance between Earth and Samar or identified Samar's original galaxy by name. Samar should therefore not be assigned to a specific galaxy unless a later chapter establishes one.

#### Protected Development
Lower Sectors function as comparatively protected environments in which developing civilizations can mature without immediately facing the dangers present in higher cosmic regions. Chapter 1179 compares them to smaller aquariums deliberately left separated so that Life can safely develop, with larger Sectors representing progressively less protected environments.[10]
This isolation was never absolute. [[World Trees]] on separate planets could communicate with one another, dimensional travel was possible and sufficiently advanced civilizations could threaten weaker worlds. Aurora's concern over Samar's defenses originated partly from learning through other World Trees that extraterrestrial civilizations capable of invading planets already existed.[15]

#### Development Limit
By Chapter 1179, Samar's Sector had reached its current development limit through its long connection with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s civilization.[10] Rather than leaving the mature Lower Sector isolated indefinitely, the Elderblood leadership began drawing it toward Victor's own Sector as part of a controlled cosmological merger.

### Planetary Characteristics

#### Planetary Scale
Originally, Samar was simply treated as a large inhabited supernatural planet. Its dimensions changed drastically during the Imperial era.
By Chapter 1179, Samar had been multiplied at Tasha's request until it was approximately three times the size of the old Earth.[10] The chapter does not specify whether "size" refers technically to diameter, area, volume or another measurement, so no exact numerical dimensions can presently be established from the statement.
The enlarged world supplied substantially more space for Samar's growing population and allowed Eclipse Ventus itself to expand to continental proportions.[10]

#### Day-Night Cycle
Samar possesses an extreme natural day-night cycle. Daylight lasts approximately 38 hours, while night lasts only around 4 hours.[1]
Despite [[Werewolves]] being culturally associated with the Moon and night, their ancestral homeland therefore spends most of each cycle beneath daylight.

#### Climate
Samar was historically described as possessing hostile and rapidly changing weather. When its short night begins, temperatures can fall below freezing extremely quickly.[1]
This does not make Samar an uninhabitable frozen world. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later observed that its overall environment was considerably more Earth-like than [[Nightingale]]'s permanently dark and colder planetary conditions.[4]

#### Gravity
Samar possesses substantially stronger gravity than Earth.[4] Its gravitational intensity also varies between regions. Some areas possess conditions harsh enough that insufficiently developed [[Werewolves]] can suffer serious physical consequences merely from entering them.
Powerful Werewolves can instead use such environments as natural training grounds, making Samar's extreme physical conditions particularly suitable for a species whose culture and biology strongly emphasize bodily power.[4]

#### Aurora Seraphina Evergreen
[[Aurora Seraphina Evergreen]] is the mature [[Amara Elderblood|World Tree of Positivity]] belonging to Samar. Positive [[World Trees]] are the first part of the paired system to arise on developing living worlds. Through processes extending across millions of years, they assist in creating and maintaining the conditions through which planetary Life develops.[5]
Aurora is deeply integrated into Samar's natural environment. Her perception is not limited to a humanoid avatar or a single physical tree. While on Samar she can observe through the Nature connected to her, allowing the planet's land, vegetation, water and natural systems to function as extensions of her awareness.[15]
This makes hiding ordinary activity from Aurora extremely difficult while remaining on her world. Isolating a room or concealing oneself through conventional means does not automatically prevent the World Tree from perceiving events occurring through the planet to which she is connected.[15]
Aurora's priorities differ from those of a conventional Werewolf ruler. Her primary concern is the survival of Samar itself and the continuation of planetary Life. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly noted that even if Samar's existing civilization disappeared, Life could eventually develop again so long as the planet and its underlying biological system survived.[2]

#### Aria Seraphina Evergreen
[[Aria Seraphina Evergreen]] is Samar's World Tree of Negativity and Aurora's younger counterpart. When introduced she manifested as a small girl and had developed so recently that she did not normally communicate through speech.[5]
Her age is a consequence of the World Tree life cycle. The Positive World Tree first establishes the conditions required for Life; the corresponding Negative World Tree only begins developing after sentient beings arise and generate the Negative Energy required for its existence.[5]
Aria therefore did not participate equally with Aurora in Samar's earliest biological formation and should not be described as one of the planet's ancient co-founders.

#### World Tree Network
Aurora does not exist in complete isolation from other [[World Trees]]. Positive World Trees can exchange information with their counterparts on other inhabited planets. By Chapter 777, Aurora maintained contact with [[Yggdrasil]] on Earth, [[Nightingale]]'s World Tree and four additional World Trees located on distant inhabited worlds.[15]
This network gave Aurora knowledge of events far beyond Samar despite her normally secluded lifestyle. It was through these exchanges that she became increasingly aware of stronger civilizations capable of interplanetary aggression and concluded that Samar's defenses were inadequate.[15]

#### Protection Agreement
Aurora ultimately requested [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s protection because Samar lacked a sufficiently strong defensive structure for the changing supernatural environment.[15]
Victor agreed, but his original promise was deliberately specific: he would protect Samar the planet from invasion. He did not initially promise unconditional preservation of every Werewolf living upon it while he considered the existing leadership inadequate.[2]
The distinction was important because Aurora's principal interest was the planet and continuation of Life, not preservation of a particular political dynasty.

#### Victor's Connection
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] fundamentally changed Samar's status in Chapter 887. After his evolution, he appeared before Aurora and Aria and caused his own Positive and Negative Energies to resonate directly with the respective [[World Trees]].[8]
The connection effectively linked Victor to Samar. Aurora subsequently described an energetic symbiosis in which she supplied Victor with Positive Energy while receiving Energy of superior quality in return, strengthening both sides of the relationship.[13]
This link allows Victor to interact with Samar at a level far beyond conventional political ownership. By Chapter 986, he was explicitly described as being connected to Samar, [[Nightingale]] and Earth and as effectively owning all three planets.[9]

### Werewolf Civilization

#### Ancestral Homeland
Samar is the principal historical homeland of the [[Werewolves]]. Werewolves also lived on Earth and later spread throughout the Elderblood civilization, but Samar developed the largest known independent society specifically organized around their race.
Its social structure historically revolved around:
- the Werewolf Royal Family;
- Alpha bloodlines and Clans;
- instinctive hierarchy and strength;
- Eclipse Ventus;
- traditional Werewolf customs;
- trade with other supernatural races;
- and, in the Imperial era, advanced biotechnology and controlled hybridization.

#### Ancient Progenitor
The ancient First Werewolf Progenitor is identified as Licael.[17] The Werewolf lineage associated with him originated outside Samar's later native population and entered the region during its early cosmic development.[12]
The precise chronology between Licael's arrival, the earliest Werewolf communities and the later construction of Eclipse Ventus has not been fully described.

#### Alpha Clans
Samar's most influential Werewolf families historically functioned through powerful Alpha lineages. In political and social importance, these families were comparable in broad principle to [[Nightingale]]'s ancient Noble Vampire families.[3]
Known major lineages include:
- [[Lykos Clan]]
- [[Uruky Clan]]
- the [[Fenrir]] Royal lineage
- other ancient Alpha families
The Lykos Clan extends back to the earliest periods of Werewolf civilization and contains the bloodline associated with [[Elizabeth Lykos]], whose rare mutation continued appearing among her descendants.[18][19][20][21]
The Uruky family was another extremely influential lineage possessing significant status, wealth and military power during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s first visit.[22]

#### Bloodline Development
The average quality of Samar's Werewolf bloodline increased enormously after Tasha's rise and the integration of the planet into the Elderblood civilization.
By Chapter 1179, Samar's [[Werewolves]] were described as more powerful than ever, with a bloodline so pure and strong that they had nearly returned to their origins by the standards of the old era.[10]
This statement does not mean that every ordinary modern Werewolf became equal to Licael or Tasha. It describes the overall improvement of the Werewolf population and its bloodline compared with the historical civilization.

### Government

#### Historical Monarchy
Samar historically functioned as an absolute monarchy under [[Volk Fenrir]] and [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]].[11] The monarchy possessed genuine governing authority rather than merely ceremonial status, while powerful Alpha families controlled their own estates, businesses, forces and social influence beneath the Royal Family.
Tasha handled much of the difficult political and administrative work required to maintain Samar even while Volk publicly occupied the position of King. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] quickly recognized her as the more capable ruler and repeatedly described her nature as that of an Alpha among Alphas.[23][4][24]

#### New Dawn Crisis
Samar's political weaknesses became particularly visible during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s visit when Fenir, the First Prince, was exposed as cooperating with [[New Dawn]] in pursuit of greater power.[25]
Competition for succession was not inherently unacceptable within Werewolf culture, but allowing an outside conspiracy to manipulate Samar and its people crossed a different line. Once the extent of Fenir's involvement became clear, Tasha and Volk dismantled his faction.[25]
The incident demonstrated the weakness of Samar's previous political structure and reinforced Victor's conclusion that Tasha was the ruler most capable of leading its people.

#### Tasha Fenrir Elderblood
[[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]] is the current Werewolf Queen and modern Werewolf Progenitor.
Her transformation in Chapter 849 first awakened her as a Wolf Goddess whose Divinity extended across the Wolf Race and its subspecies.[6] Her development then crossed into Progenitor authority, placing her at the head of the Werewolf racial hierarchy.[7]
Tasha's new authority was immediately demonstrated against Volk. She withdrew the accumulated Werewolf power supporting him and reduced the former King to an Omega, decisively ending his position as Samar's dominant Alpha.[7]
Her present roles therefore include:
- Werewolf Queen;
- Werewolf Progenitor;
- Alpha among Alphas;
- supreme racial authority over Samar's [[Werewolves]];
- local administrator of Samar;
- Empress within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wider civilization.
Tasha's own species later evolved further through her relationship with Victor. Chapter 1179 describes her as a Dragon hybrid, then immediately clarifies that she is more accurately a Dragon with Werewolf traits. Victor's Draconic Blood is so dominant that it does not submit to the Werewolf side of the combination.[10]

### Eclipse Ventus
Eclipse Ventus is the Royal Capital of the [[Werewolves]] and the principal named city on Samar.[3]

#### Historical Appearance
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s party first entered the city, Eclipse Ventus looked like several different historical periods had been merged together. Medieval and tribal elements existed beside modern architecture, vehicles, clothing, technology and supernatural infrastructure.[3]
The unusual appearance came from the collision of three major influences: ancient [[Werewolves]] more than a thousand years old who preferred the culture they had known for centuries, younger Werewolves who had lived on Earth and wanted modern lifestyles, and Samar's former commercial partnership with the Witches.[11]
Rather than forcing every district and family to follow one cultural standard, the monarchy permitted different estates to develop according to the preferences of their owners. The result was a functioning but visually chaotic city in which traditional and modern lifestyles coexisted.[11]

#### Imperial Expansion
Eclipse Ventus expanded dramatically during the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s accelerated era.
By Chapter 1179, the capital was described as having become as large as an entire country had once been, with its sheer scale surpassing South America.[10]
This growth occurred alongside Samar's own planetary expansion and the enormous increase in population, infrastructure and supernatural development produced during the Imperial era.

### Society and Culture

#### Traditionalists and Modernists
Historical Samar contained a strong cultural divide between ancient [[Werewolves]] and younger generations influenced by Earth.[11]
Older inhabitants frequently preferred customs, architecture and lifestyles that had remained familiar to them for centuries. Younger Werewolves arriving from Earth wanted modern entertainment, technology and social infrastructure. Eclipse Ventus became a physical expression of that generational conflict rather than forcing one side to completely replace the other.[11]

#### Earth-Born Werewolves
Werewolf ancestry did not automatically make an individual culturally Samar-born. Leona, despite belonging to one of the planet's important bloodlines, had never lived in Samar before accompanying [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s group and initially mistook Samar for the name of the capital city.[3]
This demonstrates a distinction between belonging to the Werewolf race and being raised within Samar's native civilization.

#### Witch Influence
The Witches historically maintained a substantial commercial presence on Samar. Their products, magical services and investment contributed directly to Eclipse Ventus' mixture of supernatural and technological development.[11]
During the crisis surrounding the Arcane Realm, the Witches abruptly terminated their arrangements and withdrew not merely from the capital but from Samar entirely.[11]
That withdrawal represents a historical period rather than Samar's modern political relationship with Witch civilization. Both groups later became incorporated into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s much larger Imperial network.

#### Strength and Hierarchy
Traditional Werewolf society places considerable cultural importance upon physical ability, instinct and Alpha hierarchy. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s interactions with Samar repeatedly demonstrated that visible dominance could have immediate social and instinctive consequences even when no formal political title changed hands.[26]
This instinctive culture also influenced Samar's succession politics. Competition between powerful heirs or Alphas was not automatically considered unacceptable; what made Fenir's conduct dangerous was his willingness to involve an external organization against his own people.[25]

### Military and Defenses

#### Historical Position
During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s original visit, Samar possessed formidable individual [[Werewolves]] but lacked the strategic depth required to compete comfortably with the strongest supernatural civilizations.
Aurora explicitly considered the planet's defensive line inadequate. The Noble Vampires had undergone rapid military development, major Pantheons possessed Divine armies, [[Fenrir]] had been incapacitated and Samar lacked the infrastructure necessary to confidently repel a sufficiently advanced planetary invasion.[15]
This historical assessment should not be treated as Samar's modern strength.

#### Fenrir
[[Fenrir]] historically served as Samar's greatest natural deterrent, but the [[Werewolves]] misunderstood the exact nature of his protection.
Fenrir is not Samar's legally appointed guardian and does not defend every Werewolf out of abstract racial loyalty. He regards Samar as his territory and developed personal companionship with Tasha, Volk and members of their family after they aided him.[2]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explained that Fenrir's relationship to Samar is primal rather than political. He protects the planet because it belongs to his perceived territory and protects specific individuals because they are companions. His behavior should therefore not be interpreted as equivalent to a monarch assuming responsibility for every citizen.[2]

#### Aurora
Aurora constitutes another enormous defensive and intelligence advantage. Because her awareness extends throughout the natural systems of Samar, ordinary infiltration on the planet can be extremely difficult to conceal from her.[15]
The limitation is that surveillance is not identical to military strength. Aurora's ability to perceive activity did not remove her concern that Samar lacked sufficient combat forces to resist powerful extraterrestrial invaders.[15]

#### Modern Werewolf Strength
By Chapter 1179, the [[Werewolves]] were explicitly described as more powerful than ever. Their general bloodline quality and racial development had increased enormously during the Imperial era.[10]
The principal remaining weakness identified by Tasha was the number of truly exceptional elites rather than the strength of the average Werewolf population.

#### The Fifty Elites
As of Chapter 1179, Samar possesses fifty individuals with planetary strength.[10]
Although fifty beings of this level would have represented an extraordinary force by the standards of old Samar, Tasha considered the number inadequate for the scale of the modern [[Elderblood Empire]].
Producing additional beings at this level through pure Werewolf bloodlines was difficult. Tasha therefore used the Empire's advances in supernatural genetics and hybrid stabilization to develop a different path.
All fifty of Samar's current top elites are stated to be hybrids.[10]

#### Imperial Hybridization
Historically, hybrids between incompatible supernatural species frequently suffered from instability, disease, sterility or other biological weaknesses. The [[Elderblood Empire]]'s later biological sciences solved many of these problems, allowing stable hybridization to become an intentional method of advancement.
Chapter 1179 notes especially strong results when more spiritual races—such as Gods, Angels, Demons or Spirits—are combined with more materially oriented mortal races. The spiritual component can greatly amplify the mortal foundation.[10]
Tasha deliberately used this system when developing Samar's elite population, making hybridization one of the defining differences between the old Werewolf military and the current Imperial-era civilization.

### History

#### Formation of Samar's Biosphere
Aurora existed on Samar before its modern intelligent civilizations and participated in the development of planetary Life over millions of years. Aria developed only after sentient Life began producing sufficient Negative Energy.[5]

#### Werewolf Settlement
The ancient Werewolf Progenitor encountered Samar's region while its surrounding Sector was naturally transitioning toward a more developed living state.[12] The [[Werewolves]] eventually established Samar as their promised land and created a civilization based around powerful Alpha bloodlines.

#### Development of the Monarchy
The Werewolf Royal Family and powerful Alpha families became the political foundation of the planet, with Eclipse Ventus developing as the Royal Capital. By the modern era, Volk and Tasha jointly ruled the [[Werewolves]], although Tasha handled many of the practical responsibilities necessary to maintain the nation.[3][11][23]

#### Victor's First Visit
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] traveled to Samar with members of his family and Werewolf allies during the events surrounding [[Fenrir]].[27][28]
His presence rapidly destabilized the existing political balance. He entered disputes involving the Alpha Clans, confronted Volk, uncovered [[New Dawn]]'s influence through Fenir and became increasingly involved with Tasha and the future leadership of the planet.[22][25][26]
Victor also met Fenrir personally and learned that the ancient Wolf was an intelligent primal Being whose relationship with Samar had been widely misunderstood.[29][30][31]

#### World Tree Revelation
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s investigation eventually brought him directly to Aurora and Aria, revealing the deeper structure of Samar's planetary development and the paired Positive/Negative World Tree system.[5]
Aurora then requested Victor's protection after explaining that Samar's military development had fallen behind the threats becoming visible across the wider supernatural universe.[15]
Victor accepted responsibility for protecting the planet but refused to extend unconditional political protection to the existing leadership, openly declaring that Samar required a more appropriate Alpha to lead its people.[2]

#### Rise of Tasha
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s earlier judgment of Tasha as an Alpha among Alphas was eventually realized. Her Divine and racial development culminated in her becoming the current Werewolf Progenitor.[6]
She then used that authority to remove Volk's power and reduce him to an Omega, ending the old balance of the monarchy and leaving Tasha as the undisputed leader of Samar's [[Werewolves]].[7]

#### Victor Claims Samar
Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later evolution, he began consolidating the planets and territories connected to his growing civilization.
In Chapter 887, Victor arrived on Samar and resonated his Positive and Negative Energies with Aurora and Aria, directly establishing his supernatural claim over the planet.[8]
The resulting connection was not a conventional territorial declaration alone. Samar's [[World Trees]] became energetically linked to Victor, and Aurora described herself as simultaneously supplying him with Energy and receiving improved Energy through the connection.[13]

#### Integration with Dragon's Nest
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction later reorganized around the former [[Egyptian Pantheon]] and the civilization that would develop into the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Allies without homelands were relocated into Victor's territories, while Samar was specifically treated differently because the [[Werewolves]] already possessed their own world.[13]
Tasha instead established representation within Victor's territory, publicly tying Samar to the power protecting it without abandoning the planet itself.[13]

#### Imperial Era
Samar's relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually advanced from protected allied world to direct component of his Imperial civilization.
By Chapter 1177, Samar, [[Nightingale]] and [[Yggdrasil]] had become enormous economic and population centers inhabited by billions of beings.[14]
Samar's territory, population and racial development continued expanding. Tasha had the planet enlarged, Eclipse Ventus developed on a scale greater than South America and the Werewolf bloodline approached its ancestral condition by the standards of earlier eras.[10]

### Merger of Samar's Sector
Chapter 1179 begins the largest geographical change in Samar's history: the integration of its entire Lower Sector with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s own Sector.

#### Anchor Sphere
Tasha received one of the spheres designed to facilitate the operation. Equivalent anchors were entrusted to Ophis for [[Nightingale]] and [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]] for another external Sector involved in the process.[10]
Tasha herself did not need to move galaxies or manipulate the Sector directly. Her role was to keep the sphere with her and order Samar's population to remain calm while the greater ritual was performed.[10]

#### Sector Attraction
Ruby explained that the participating external Sectors had reached their present developmental limits because of their connection with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s civilization. Rather than waiting for the process to continue naturally, the Empire would accelerate their development and draw the structures toward Victor's Sector.[10]
The spheres held by Tasha, Ophis and Albedo function as anchors through which those Sectors are pulled toward the primary Imperial region.

#### Effect on Victor's Sector
The merger does not itself advance [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Sector into a higher classification.
When Anna specifically asked whether the merger counted as advancement, Ruby answered that it did not. Instead, new galaxies are created as the smaller Sector structures are integrated into the larger one.[10]
Velnorah compared the process to moving smaller aquariums to the edge of a larger aquarium. Victor is responsible for maintaining the cosmic arrangement and ensuring the newly integrated Galactic structures do not collide during the process.[10]
The merger therefore changes Samar from a comparatively isolated Lower-Sector civilization into a directly integrated part of Victor's larger cosmic territory without automatically promoting the primary Sector itself.

### External Relations

#### Elderblood Empire
Samar is now directly integrated into the [[Elderblood Empire]]. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] possesses the overarching planetary authority, Tasha is one of his Empresses and remains Samar's local Werewolf ruler, Imperial science supports its racial development and the planet's entire Lower Sector is being cosmologically incorporated into Victor's larger territory.[8][9][10]
This relationship extends far beyond Aurora's original protection agreement.

#### Nightingale
Samar and [[Nightingale]] possess several parallel characteristics. Both are supernatural racial homelands outside Earth's immediate stellar system, both possess [[World Trees]] and both eventually became directly linked to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[15][9]
Chapter 1020 further indicates that Samar and Nightingale are accessed through distinct Sector connections outside Victor's original Sector.[16]
By the late Imperial era, both worlds had become major population and economic centers and both were included in the Lower-Sector merger operation.[14][10]

#### Witches
Samar historically maintained a strong commercial partnership with the Witches. Their involvement was an important factor in the development of Eclipse Ventus before they suddenly withdrew during the crisis within Arcane.[11]
The historical withdrawal should not be treated as Samar's permanent relationship with Witch civilization, as both groups later became components of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s larger political order.

#### Celtic Pantheon
During Aurora's discussion of Samar's vulnerability in Chapter 777, the [[Werewolves]] possessed a minor conflict or tension involving the [[Celtic Pantheon]].[15]
This was a historical strategic issue and should not be used to list the Celtic Pantheon as a permanent modern enemy of Samar.

### Trivia
- Samar is the planet; Eclipse Ventus is its Royal Capital.[3]
- [[Werewolves]] historically call Samar their promised land.[1]
- Samar's original galaxy and exact distance from Earth have not been numerically established.
- Chapter 777 places Samar in an entirely different stellar system from Earth, while Chapter 1020 later indicates a distinct Sector connection leading toward Samar.[15][16]
- Samar experiences approximately 38 hours of daylight and around 4 hours of night.[1]
- Temperatures can rapidly fall below freezing after night begins.[1]
- Samar's gravity is substantially stronger than Earth's.[4]
- Aurora predates Aria by millions of years of planetary development.[5]
- [[Fenrir]]'s claim over Samar is territorial and instinctive rather than a formal legal kingship.[2]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] first promised to protect the planet itself rather than guaranteeing the survival of its existing political order.[2]
- Victor directly claimed Samar through Aurora and Aria in Chapter 887.[8]
- Victor was later described as effectively owning Samar, [[Nightingale]] and Earth.[9]
- Tasha remains the local ruler of the Werewolves despite Victor's broader Imperial sovereignty.
- Modern Tasha is described as fundamentally a Dragon with Werewolf traits, rather than an evenly balanced Dragon-Werewolf hybrid.[10]
- Samar is currently approximately three times the size of old Earth, although the specific geometric measurement represented by "size" is not stated.[10]
- Eclipse Ventus has grown beyond the scale of South America.[10]
- Samar possesses fifty planetary-strength elites, all of whom are hybrids.[10]
- Samar's Sector has reached its present developmental limit and is being merged with Victor's Sector.[10]
- The merger does not automatically increase Victor's Sector classification; it instead produces or incorporates additional Galactic space.[10]
