Big Guy is the currently unnamed Guardian of [[Roxanne Elderblood]], a World Tree belonging to Victor Elderblood and one of the central pillars of the [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]]. Although he originally appeared to be an enormous Demonic Gorilla inhabiting the former [[Horseman Clan]] territory, Roxanne later revealed that Big Guy is fundamentally a Spirit born as her Guardian rather than an ordinary Demonic Beast.[1]
His existence is intrinsically linked to Roxanne. As she develops, matures and changes species, Big Guy undergoes corresponding evolution. When Roxanne became a Dryad and acquired Vampiric characteristics through Victor's blood, Big Guy likewise evolved, becoming capable of ordinary speech and gaining Vampiric characteristics of his own.[2][3]
His growth continued alongside Roxanne until his enormous gorilla body was no longer his only usable manifestation. Roxanne deliberately modified her Guardian during one of her later evolutionary periods so that he could function more effectively outside his original role, eventually giving him the humanoid appearance of a muscular older man with gray hair.[4][5]
Although Roxanne remains the absolute center of his existence and the person he refers to as "[[Persephone Elderblood|My Queen]]", Big Guy has also developed a genuine friendship with Victor. He eventually became one of the trusted operatives of Clan Elderblood, carrying out scouting, infiltration and protection assignments on Victor's behalf while continuing to fulfill his primary duty of protecting Roxanne.[5][6][7]

### Physical Description

#### Gorilla Form
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] first encountered him, [[Diablo|Big Guy]] resembled an extraordinarily large gorilla standing more than 15 meters tall. His entire body was covered in thick black fur, he possessed blood-red eyes, and his physique was extremely muscular even in proportion to his enormous size. Despite his intimidating appearance, Victor initially found him sitting peacefully beneath the moonlight near the mysterious tree that would later become known as Roxanne.[8]
His physical appearance is that of a Demonic Beast, but this form should not be mistaken for his fundamental species. Roxanne later explained that Big Guy is essentially a Spirit whose physical body functions as the manifestation of her Guardian rather than a conventional biological creature.[1]
When fighting seriously, Big Guy can cover his enormous body in a strange black substance that forms a protective armor over his fur. This material can also be extended onto objects he holds, such as when he covered an uprooted tree in the same black substance and wielded it as a weapon against Victor.[9]

#### Humanoid Form
As Roxanne continued evolving, she deliberately altered her Guardian so that he would no longer be restricted to existing as a gigantic gorilla. She explained that because [[Diablo|Big Guy]] is technically a part of her Soul and exists through their symbiotic relationship, she could modify certain characteristics of his form whenever she underwent another major evolution.[4]
The result was eventually revealed inside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s inner world. Big Guy gained the appearance of an older man with gray hair and an extremely muscular body.[5]
Unlike his original form, the humanoid body allows him to move through populated environments without attracting the enormous amount of attention that would naturally follow a 15-meter-tall gorilla. He later accompanied Victor's forces and performed missions while maintaining this human appearance.[5][10]

### Personality and Traits
Despite his frightening appearance and tremendous strength, [[Diablo|Big Guy]] is generally easygoing when neither Roxanne nor himself is threatened. He does not normally seek conflict without reason and can spend considerable periods simply resting, eating or observing the people around him.
His relaxed personality disappears immediately when Roxanne is endangered. Protecting her is not merely a matter of loyalty or employment: Big Guy was literally created for that purpose. He refers to Roxanne almost exclusively as "[[Persephone Elderblood|My Queen]]" and considers her safety the defining responsibility of his existence.[2][3]
Big Guy is exceptionally loyal but is not mindlessly obedient. He possesses an independent personality, his own opinions and enough confidence to openly disagree with Roxanne over comparatively harmless matters. The clearest example is his continuing refusal to accept the name "Snyde-Clatlo", which Roxanne attempted to give him. Big Guy considered the name terrible and continued demanding that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually give him something better.[6]
He possesses a notably vulgar manner of speaking. Once his evolution allowed ordinary people to understand him, one of the first things he did was insult Victor for calling him a "circus gorilla".[2] This coarse speech contrasts heavily with the dignified appearance of his later humanoid form.
Big Guy and Victor have developed a relationship resembling that of close friends who regularly insult one another. Their conversations frequently consist of sarcasm, profanity and mutual teasing, but Big Guy willingly accepts missions from Victor and Victor trusts him with people he considers irreplaceable.[11][7]
He is also highly mischievous and enjoys embarrassing other people whenever he notices something they would prefer kept private. His extraordinary sense of smell makes this particularly troublesome, as he can identify intimate activities through scent and has no hesitation about reacting to what he discovers.[12]
Big Guy has become increasingly intelligent as Roxanne has matured. This is not simply ordinary learning. Because knowledge passes both ways through their Guardian–World Tree connection, everything Roxanne learns can become available to Big Guy and everything Big Guy learns can likewise become available to Roxanne. Roxanne's habit of reading large numbers of books therefore directly contributed to her Guardian becoming noticeably more knowledgeable and articulate.[11]
Despite joking constantly, Big Guy can become serious immediately when carrying out his responsibilities. During Victor's operations on Samar, he performed covert reconnaissance, located a hidden sub-space, assisted with interference in a portal, and protected Leona without his usual laziness compromising the mission.[6][7]

### Synopsis

#### Vampire Society
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] first encountered [[Diablo|Big Guy]] while exploring the eastern territories formerly associated with the [[Horseman Clan]]. Victor had entered a region inhabited by supernatural beasts and, characteristically, became interested in trying to befriend or tame them rather than avoiding them.[8]
He eventually encountered an enormous black gorilla sitting near a strange tree and enjoying the moonlight. Rather than immediately attacking him, Victor attempted to communicate and offered the creature black bananas. His unusual connection with animals allowed him to understand the gorilla sufficiently well for the two to communicate despite Big Guy being unable to speak in a manner ordinary people could comprehend.[8]
Big Guy eventually offered Victor one of the strange fruits produced by the tree he guarded. Victor's curiosity subsequently escalated into a physical confrontation between the two. Despite Victor already being vastly stronger than ordinary Vampires of his age, Big Guy proved capable of matching him sufficiently well to turn their confrontation into an extended battle.[8][9]
Big Guy demonstrated tremendous physical power, extraordinary speed for something of his size and the ability to surround himself with black armor. He uprooted a tree, stripped away its branches and covered the improvised weapon in the same black material protecting his body, while Victor responded by creating an enormous Ice Greatsword and using his elemental powers.[9]
Their confrontation continued for several hours without either combatant decisively defeating the other. At one point, an enormous Demonic Fox and Demonic Wolf interfered with the fight. Victor and Big Guy immediately stopped attacking one another, instinctively cooperated to kill the intruding beasts, and then returned to their own confrontation afterward. Eventually both became exhausted enough that the fight ended without a clear victor.[9]
The encounter established an unusual friendship between Victor and the Guardian long before Victor understood what Big Guy or the strange tree beside him actually were.

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later returned to [[Diablo|Big Guy]]'s territory after becoming significantly stronger. His return was accompanied by members of his family and allies, and Big Guy immediately recognized his former opponent.[12]
The reunion demonstrated that Big Guy's senses were far beyond those of ordinary Demonic Beasts. He could identify people through scent and detect extremely subtle changes in their bodies and behavior, including information that embarrassed several of Victor's companions.[12]
Victor then turned his attention toward the mysterious tree Big Guy had protected since their first meeting. Big Guy communicated that the tree was female, something Victor had never previously considered, and reacted with amusement when Victor struggled to explain how he was somehow expected to know the gender of a tree.[13]
The tree had previously been targeted by enemies connected to the Horseman faction. Big Guy remained its sole constant protector and destroyed hostile forces sent into the area rather than abandoning his charge.[13]
Victor eventually discovered that the tree could develop through the absorption of sufficiently powerful blood. He began providing enormous quantities of Vampire blood acquired through his access to [[The Limbo Realm]], causing the dying or immature tree to recover rapidly. Its damaged roots healed, new leaves appeared and its supernatural presence intensified.[14][15]
Big Guy immediately benefited from the same development. Those observing the process noticed that as the tree's strength increased, the Guardian's power also increased, providing one of the earliest demonstrations that his existence and development were intrinsically connected to hers.[14]
The tree eventually matured and manifested a humanoid Dryad, revealing herself as the being later named [[Roxanne Elderblood]]. Victor's Blood had not merely accelerated her growth; it altered her existence, causing Roxanne to develop Vampiric characteristics and become connected directly to Victor's lineage.[16][2]
Because Big Guy exists as Roxanne's Guardian, the change affected him automatically. He explained that whenever his Queen evolves, he evolves alongside her. Her maturation consequently caused Big Guy to mature as well, and the Guardian obtained Vampiric characteristics through their connection.[2][3]
One immediately obvious result was communication. Victor had always been capable of understanding Big Guy due to his unusual connection with animals, but following the evolution everyone around them could understand the Guardian normally. Big Guy insisted that he had always been speaking and that the real difference was simply that other people could finally understand him.[2]
Victor briefly joked about turning him into a maid after realizing he had become a Vampire, an idea Big Guy immediately rejected on the grounds that his only true occupation was protecting his Queen.[3]
From that point onward Big Guy formally became part of the growing household surrounding Victor and Roxanne while still identifying his primary allegiance as belonging to his Queen.

#### Demonic War
As [[Diablo|Big Guy]] spent more time around Clan Alucard, his intelligence and knowledge continued increasing. The maids eventually noticed that the formerly simple gorilla had become substantially more articulate and knowledgeable.[11]
Roxanne explained the reason: the bond between a World Tree and its Guardian operates as a genuine symbiotic relationship. Knowledge acquired by Roxanne is transmitted to Big Guy, while experiences and knowledge acquired by Big Guy are likewise transmitted back to her. Because Roxanne had spent considerable time reading books and learning about the world, Big Guy had indirectly acquired that information as well.[11]
Everything I learn, my Queen learns, and everything my Queen learns, I learn too. That's how a symbiotic relationship between a Guardian and a World Tree works.
—Big Guy explaining his connection to Roxanne Elderblood, Chapter 513
This connection meant that neither member of the pair developed entirely independently. Roxanne's intellectual development strengthened Big Guy's mind, while Big Guy's direct experience of the outside world could become knowledge available to Roxanne.[11]
Big Guy later disappeared from ordinary activity for an extended period because another major evolution was occurring. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] asked Roxanne why he had not seen the gorilla, she explained that Big Guy was still changing.[4]
Roxanne further revealed that a Guardian is technically part of the World Tree's Soul, although not completely merged with it. Because Big Guy was born specifically to remain beside and protect her, Roxanne possessed significant control over his evolutionary development. With each major evolution she could alter portions of his body and nature.[4]
Deciding that an enormous gorilla was impractical for many situations, Roxanne deliberately modified his next form so that he could operate more effectively as her Guardian. She refused to tell Victor what appearance she had chosen until the transformation was completed.[4]

#### King Among Kings
[[Diablo|Big Guy]]'s evolved appearance was eventually revealed during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s time on Samar. Inside Victor's inner world, Roxanne was shown standing beside an older man with gray hair and an extraordinarily muscular physique—the evolved humanoid form of her Guardian.[5]
His responsibilities had likewise expanded. Rather than merely remaining physically beside Roxanne, Big Guy began carrying out operations directly for Victor. Victor could communicate with him mentally and issue instructions without needing to be physically near him.[5]
During Victor's political operation on Samar, Big Guy was used as a covert scout. He successfully located [[Fenrir]], who had been concealed behind the Royal Castle within a hidden sub-space that could only be entered through a portal.[6]
Big Guy and Roxanne then interfered with the portal leading into the hidden space. Although their actions were detected by Samar's native World Tree, the operation demonstrated that Big Guy could cooperate with his Queen in manipulating or disrupting supernatural structures that would normally be inaccessible to him alone.[6]
During the same operation, Big Guy complained to Victor about continuing to be called "Big Guy" and demanded a proper name. Roxanne had already attempted to call him Snyde-Clatlo, but he hated the name and refused to accept it. Victor therefore continued using his old nickname.[6]
His role as Guardian also expanded beyond Roxanne herself. Victor trusted Big Guy to protect members of his family during dangerous operations. When Victor divided his forces on Samar, Big Guy and [[Roberta Elderblood]] were assigned to remain with [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]], with Big Guy confidently promising that nobody would touch her while he was present.[7]
Big Guy later participated alongside Victor's other elite allies during the conflict surrounding the [[Greek Pantheon]]. He was explicitly present in his humanoid form among the forces accompanying [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], demonstrating that he was no longer restricted to defending Roxanne's immediate location and could function as one of Clan Elderblood's high-level combatants.[10]

### Skills and Abilities
[[Diablo|Big Guy]] is the natural Guardian of a World Tree and therefore fundamentally different from an ordinary Demonic Beast. His initial enormous physical power was already sufficient to battle the early Vampire Count [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] for several hours without a decisive victor, and his strength has continued increasing alongside Roxanne's successive evolutions.[8][9][14]

#### World Tree Guardian Physiology
- World Tree Guardian: [[Diablo|Big Guy]] was born specifically to protect [[Roxanne Elderblood]]. His existence, development and supernatural nature are directly linked to his Queen rather than belonging solely to himself.[2][4]
- Spiritual Existence: Big Guy is fundamentally a Spirit rather than an ordinary biological gorilla. Roxanne explicitly explained that he does not possess a conventional "real" physical body in the same sense as ordinary creatures.[1]
- Physical Manifestation: Despite his spiritual nature, Big Guy can manifest a fully tangible body capable of physically interacting with the world, receiving attacks and fighting supernatural beings.[8][1]
- Guardian Manifestation: Roxanne can call Big Guy to herself because his existence is intrinsically connected to her. His physical location therefore does not permanently separate the Guardian from his World Tree.[1]
- Soul Connection: Big Guy is technically part of Roxanne's Soul while still remaining an independent consciousness rather than being completely merged with her.[4]
- Dependent Existence: Big Guy's existence is dependent upon Roxanne and the Guardian bond connecting them. He exists specifically as an extension and protector of her existence rather than as an unrelated Spirit.[4]
- Evolutionary Synchronization: Whenever Roxanne undergoes a sufficiently important evolution, Big Guy evolves alongside her. He explicitly explained this after Roxanne's first major maturation.[2]
- Power Synchronization: An increase in Roxanne's power can directly strengthen Big Guy. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] accelerated the young World Tree's recovery with blood, observers noticed Big Guy becoming stronger as the tree developed.[14]
- Racial Synchronization: Roxanne acquiring Vampiric characteristics also caused Big Guy to become a Vampire through their Guardian connection.[2][3]
- Adaptive Evolution: Roxanne can deliberately influence certain details of Big Guy's development whenever their bond enters another evolutionary phase.[4]
- Humanoid Evolution: Using that influence, Roxanne developed a humanoid form for Big Guy so that he would no longer be forced to operate as a 15-meter gorilla in every situation.[4][5]
- Symbiotic Knowledge Sharing: Big Guy and Roxanne automatically exchange learned information through their bond.[11]
- Knowledge Assimilation: Information Roxanne learns through reading, observation or experience can become available to Big Guy without him independently studying the same subject.[11]
- Reciprocal Knowledge Transfer: The process functions in both directions. Things Big Guy learns can likewise become knowledge available to Roxanne.[11]
- Accelerated Intellectual Development: Big Guy's intelligence increased substantially as Roxanne accumulated knowledge, explaining the dramatic difference between the largely gesture-based gorilla Victor originally encountered and the articulate individual seen later.[11]
- Psychic Link: Big Guy and Roxanne possess an intrinsic mental connection created by their Guardian bond.
- Telepathy: Big Guy can communicate with Roxanne without conventional speech.
- Empathic Connection: Their intimate spiritual bond allows Big Guy to remain aware of Roxanne and respond immediately when his Queen requires him.
- Experience Sharing: Experiences can be transmitted through their symbiosis alongside ordinary learned information.[11]

#### Spiritual Demonic Beast Physiology
[[Diablo|Big Guy]]'s original manifested body resembles a gigantic Demonic Gorilla. Despite the terminology, Demonic Beast does not mean that he is a Demon or originates from [[Biblical Hell]]. It describes the supernatural beast-like nature of his physical manifestation.
- Superhuman Strength: Big Guy possesses tremendous physical strength. During his first encounter with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], he could exchange direct physical attacks with him and inflict meaningful damage despite Victor already possessing the physiology of an abnormally powerful Noble Vampire.[8][9]
- Giant Strength: His enormous body allows him to casually manipulate objects such as full-grown trees and use them as improvised weapons.[9]
- Superhuman Durability: Big Guy possesses extraordinary resistance to physical and supernatural attacks. He endured Victor's elemental attacks and continued fighting him for hours.[8][9]
- Superhuman Speed and Reflexes: Despite his enormous size and weight, Big Guy can move at extremely high supernatural speeds and repeatedly react to the early Victor's attacks.[8][9]
- Enhanced Agility: Big Guy's gigantic frame does not prevent him from performing rapid combat movements or responding to much smaller supernatural opponents.
- Enhanced Stamina: He fought Victor for several hours and remained capable of continuing even after the two temporarily interrupted their duel to kill other Demonic Beasts.[9]
- Exceptional Senses: Big Guy possesses extremely developed sensory abilities appropriate to a high-level supernatural beast.
- Enhanced Hearing: His hearing is sensitive enough to perceive distant or extremely subtle sounds.
- Enhanced Smell: Big Guy possesses an extraordinary sense of smell and can identify extremely subtle information about individuals through their scent alone.[12]
- Intimate Scent Detection: His sense of smell is sensitive enough to determine whether someone has recently engaged in sexual activity, much to the embarrassment of Victor's companions.[12]
- Flight / Levitation: Big Guy can remain suspended in the air and move without relying solely upon conventional physical support.[8]

#### Black Armor
- Black Armor Generation: [[Diablo|Big Guy]] can generate a mysterious black substance around his body that functions as supernatural armor, significantly increasing the protection of his already durable physical form.[8][9]
- External Object Coating: The black material is not restricted to Big Guy's body. He can extend it across objects he holds.[9]
- Weapon Reinforcement: Big Guy coated an entire uprooted tree in the black material and used it as a reinforced weapon against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[9]
The exact nature and source of the black substance have not yet been fully explained.

#### Vampire Physiology
Following Roxanne's maturation through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood, [[Diablo|Big Guy]] inherited Vampiric characteristics through his bond with her.[2][3]
- Vampiric Evolution: Big Guy's pre-existing spiritual and Guardian physiology was enhanced rather than replaced by his Vampiric characteristics.
- Enhanced Physical Characteristics: His already exceptional physical abilities increased further following his evolution alongside Roxanne.[2]
- Enhanced Regeneration: His Vampiric characteristics supplement the abnormal survivability already granted by his spiritual nature.
- Enhanced Senses: His existing supernatural senses benefit from the sensory characteristics associated with Vampires.
- Longevity / [[Immortality]]: Big Guy's natural existence was already tied to the life of a World Tree, while his later Vampiric evolution further removed him from conventional biological aging.
Because Big Guy obtained these traits indirectly through Roxanne's evolution rather than through an ordinary Vampire transformation ritual, he should not be treated as a conventional Noble Vampire whose physiology functions identically to naturally born members of [[Nightingale]]'s Clans.

#### Communication Abilities
- Animal Communication: Before his evolution, [[Diablo|Big Guy]] communicated naturally through vocalizations and gestures. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] could understand him because of Victor's own ability to communicate with supernatural animals.[8][13]
- Universal Speech (Post-Evolution): Following Roxanne's maturation, Big Guy became intelligible to ordinary people. According to Big Guy himself, he had always been speaking; his evolution simply resulted in everyone being capable of understanding him normally.[2]
- Long-Range Mental Communication: Big Guy can communicate mentally with Victor across considerable distances while carrying out assignments.[5][6][7]
The exact mechanism connecting Big Guy directly to Victor has not been fully explained, although Roxanne's existence inside Victor and Big Guy's intrinsic bond with her provide an obvious supernatural connection between the three.

#### Scouting and Infiltration
- Reconnaissance: [[Diablo|Big Guy]] is capable of independently carrying out covert scouting missions for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] rather than functioning exclusively as a frontline combatant.[6]
- Hidden Space Discovery: During the operation on Samar, Big Guy successfully located [[Fenrir]] inside a completely concealed sub-space behind the Royal Castle.[6]
- Portal Interference (With Roxanne): Big Guy and Roxanne successfully interfered with the portal controlling access to Fenrir's hidden prison. This should be treated as a cooperative ability rather than evidence that Big Guy independently possesses unrestricted Space Manipulation.[6]
- Covert Operations: His humanoid form allows Big Guy to operate in civilized environments where his original gigantic appearance would make secrecy impossible.[5][6]

#### Combat Skills
- Expert Hand-to-Hand Combatant: Even during his first appearance, [[Diablo|Big Guy]] was sufficiently skilled to engage [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] in an extended close-range battle instead of relying exclusively upon his superior size.[8][9]
- Improvised Weapon Mastery: Big Guy can immediately turn environmental objects into effective weapons. His use of an uprooted tree as a reinforced spear or club is the clearest example.[9]
- Combat Adaptation: During his fight with Victor, Big Guy altered his approach in response to Victor's elemental abilities and weapon creation rather than mindlessly continuing the same attacks.[9]
- Cooperative Combat: Despite fighting Victor moments earlier, Big Guy immediately coordinated with him when outside Demonic Beasts intruded upon their battle, demonstrating strong instinctive battlefield awareness.[9]
- Guardian Combat Instinct: Big Guy's existence was created around defending Roxanne, granting him an extremely strong instinct toward identifying and eliminating threats to his Queen.
- Elite Battlefield Capability: After completing his later evolution, Big Guy was trusted enough to participate alongside members of Victor's elite forces during conflicts involving Divine-level combatants.[10]

#### Protection and Guardian Skills
- Absolute Loyalty: [[Diablo|Big Guy]]'s highest priority is Roxanne's survival and safety. His loyalty is reinforced by the metaphysical reason for his existence rather than merely personal devotion.
- Bodyguard Expertise: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] trusts Big Guy with protecting members of his family during operations in which Victor himself cannot remain beside them.[7]
- Threat Response: Big Guy immediately becomes hostile when Roxanne is threatened, regardless of the identity or political position of the aggressor.
- Protective Coordination: He can operate alongside other Elderblood agents such as [[Roberta Elderblood]] rather than requiring Roxanne to personally direct every action.[7]

### Weaknesses
- Dependence on Roxanne: [[Diablo|Big Guy]]'s existence is fundamentally linked to [[Roxanne Elderblood]]. He was created from and remains connected to her existence as her Guardian. This relationship is the source of many of his greatest advantages but also means he is not an entirely independent supernatural existence.[4]
- Evolutionary Dependence: Big Guy's largest transformations have occurred as consequences of Roxanne's own development. Although he can train and acquire experience independently, his fundamental racial evolution remains closely tied to his Queen.[2][4]
