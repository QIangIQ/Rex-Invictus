### Appearances

#### First Mention
Chapter 892
Thaloria Xyphora was the First Empress of [[Eldoria]], founder of the dynasty later inherited by her daughter [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]], and one of the greatest technological minds produced by the [[Xyrra]]. Although born physically inferior by the standards of her already superhuman race, Thaloria possessed such an extraordinary affinity for technology that Velnorah described her as effectively a goddess within a technological world. She compensated for the limitations of her body through technology, unified Eldoria, expanded her civilization beyond its homeworld and ultimately helped establish a galactic empire whose technological superiority remained relevant even against civilizations far beyond ordinary planetary powers.[1]
Thaloria is particularly infamous for her experiments upon the [[Yuurakyos]], the warrior race serving [[Galactic Emperor Kolo]]. After conventional technological superiority proved insufficient against them, she spent years studying their biology, reproduction and artificial enhancement before developing a race-specific biological weapon that exterminated the species. The act removed the greatest military obstacle facing her civilization and helped the Thaloria Dynasty dominate its galaxy, but Velnorah later considered the extermination a catastrophic strategic mistake: the Yuurakyo would have been among the few military forces capable of helping Eldoria survive the vastly stronger Galactic Emperors that appeared after their Sector evolved.[1]

### Physical Appearance
Thaloria was a member of the [[Xyrra]], a race of enormous humanoids whose men and women normally exceeded approximately three meters in height. Thaloria was an abnormal exception. She stood only around 2.5 meters tall and possessed a noticeably thinner and physically weaker body than ordinary members of her species.[1]
Her appearance strongly resembled that of her daughter, Velnorah, with the historical hologram showing the two as visibly related. Her reduced stature was not considered an advantage among the Xyrra; their species naturally possessed both superior physiques and an affinity for technology, while Thaloria appeared to have received an extreme technological talent at the expense of the physical gifts common to her people.[1]
Rather than accepting this deficiency, Thaloria developed advanced technological equipment and specialized suits to compensate for her weaker body. Her technological designs later became part of the foundation inherited by Velnorah, whose own futuristic equipment was descended from the technological tradition created by the First Empress.[1]

### Personality
Thaloria was defined primarily by extraordinary intelligence, ambition, curiosity and extreme pragmatism. Being born physically inferior within a race that possessed naturally powerful bodies did not cause her to accept a subordinate role. Instead, she used the field in which her talent was unrivaled—technology—to eliminate the disadvantage entirely and eventually rose above the very people who had been born physically superior to her.[1]
Her methods demonstrate that Thaloria placed results considerably above conventional morality. When she encountered the [[Yuurakyos]], she did not simply regard their superior biology as an enemy advantage; she immediately became interested in understanding, reproducing and ultimately weaponizing it. Captured members of the species were treated as experimental subjects through cloning attempts, reproductive experimentation and biological analysis, all in pursuit of determining how their abilities functioned.[1]
Thaloria was also extremely adaptable as a researcher. Every failed experiment became additional information. When cloning failed because of unstable genetic material, she moved to reproduction. When other species could not carry Yuurakyo offspring, she investigated racial transformation. When physical conversion proved insufficient, she discovered the importance of the Soul to racial identity. When conventional artificial reproduction between two Yuurakyo still failed, she continued examining their biology until she discovered the behavioral conditions governing reproduction. Ultimately, the same research originally intended to reproduce their strength was redirected toward discovering a method of exterminating them.[1]
This willingness to completely change strategies made Thaloria far more dangerous than a scientist who depended upon one method. She could approach a military problem as a technological, biological or strategic problem and was prepared to pursue whatever solution produced victory. However, her greatest success also produced one of the greatest long-term failures of her civilization. By destroying the Yuurakyo instead of eventually integrating or exploiting them as a military population, Thaloria removed a warrior race that Velnorah later believed could have helped defend [[Eldoria]] against the much stronger beings waiting in the Higher Sectors.[1]

### Synopsis

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Thaloria's history was revealed when [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]] showed [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Amaterasu]] Elderblood records of her original civilization. [[Eldoria]] was presented as an extraordinarily advanced world where futuristic technology had developed without requiring the destruction of the natural environment. Velnorah explained that its dominant people, the [[Xyrra]], were giant humanoids possessing an inherent affinity for technology. Even among that highly gifted race, Thaloria was exceptional. Velnorah described her mother as someone who could effectively accomplish anything through technology, despite possessing a body considered unusually frail by Xyrra standards.[1]
Thaloria eventually became known as the First Empress of the Planet Eldoria. Under her rule, the dynasty expanded at extraordinary speed. In less than approximately two thousand years, the entire planet had come under its control and Eldoria had already begun exploring space. Expansion brought exceptional individuals and other species into contact with Thaloria's civilization, and her Empire gradually transitioned from a planetary state into an interstellar power.[1]
Among the most dangerous forces encountered during that expansion were the [[Yuurakyos]], an alien warrior race known within Velnorah's Sector as the Assassins of [[Galactic Emperor Kolo]]. Their biology presented a direct challenge to Thaloria's normal approach to warfare. The Yuurakyo possessed naturally superior bodies, exceptional battle perception, great martial aptitude and rapid environmental adaptability. A single infiltrating Yuurakyo was dangerous enough that capturing him alive required Thaloria to employ some of her best personnel and technology; several of her generals were killed, multiple containment measures were broken and even Thaloria's equipment suffered damage before the warrior could finally be subdued.[1]
Rather than merely execute the prisoner, Thaloria became interested in acquiring the properties that made his race so powerful. She first attempted to clone him, only to discover that mature Yuurakyo possessed highly unstable genetic material. She then experimented with artificial insemination using women belonging to other races. These attempts also failed because the developing Yuurakyo required such enormous quantities of nutrients and biological resources that unrelated host bodies could not successfully sustain them.[1]
Thaloria next investigated whether an existing individual could simply be transformed into a Yuurakyo. This research exposed a much deeper principle concerning supernatural racial transformation: reconstructing the physical body alone was insufficient. A true racial transformation also required modification of the Soul, something Thaloria could understand theoretically but could not reproduce with the authority of a genuine Progenitor.[1]
The war surrounding her civilization continued while these experiments were underway, and Thaloria's forces were struggling against their enemies. A female Yuurakyo eventually infiltrated the facility in an attempt to rescue the captured male. By then Thaloria had already accumulated extensive information regarding the species, allowing her to prepare specifically for another Yuurakyo and capture the female considerably more efficiently than the first prisoner.[1]
Possessing both a male and female still did not immediately allow Thaloria to reproduce the species. Artificial insemination between them continued to fail because Yuurakyo reproduction contained a condition tied to their warrior society: a male was required to defeat the female and obtain her submission before healthy reproduction could occur. Thaloria eventually bypassed the normal process through artificial stimulation and successfully produced a living male child.[1]
The result created another mystery. Although the child was naturally powerful compared with many species, he was dramatically weaker than his parents. Because he had grown within Thaloria's laboratories rather than Yuurakyo society, he lacked something every mature member of the race normally possessed. Continued investigation revealed the answer: during a Yuurakyo coming-of-age ceremony, elders injected younger members with a specialized physical enhancement serum. The serum dramatically strengthened their bodies but was not genetically hereditary. It was also responsible for much of the instability Thaloria had detected in mature Yuurakyo genetic material.[1]
The discovery gave Thaloria the biological baseline she had been missing. Her objective gradually shifted from reproducing the Yuurakyo's power toward exploiting their vulnerability. Using the accumulated genetic and biological information, she engineered a biological weapon designed specifically against their species. The resulting virus attacked the Yuurakyo brain and killed infected members almost immediately.[1]
Thaloria deployed the weapon on a racial scale and exterminated the Yuurakyo. The consequences transformed the war. The Yuurakyo had represented the greatest military power associated with Galactic Emperor Kolo's Empire; once they disappeared, no remaining opponent could defeat Thaloria's civilization in a conventional technological contest. Her dynasty continued expanding until its authority extended throughout the galaxy.[1]
Thaloria later had a daughter, Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II, who inherited the scientific traditions, accumulated technology and Imperial legacy of her mother. Velnorah eventually defeated Thaloria and succeeded her as the [[Anna Elderblood|Second Empress]] Thaloria. It was Velnorah, rather than Thaloria, who subsequently achieved the position of Overlord over their Sector. That ascension triggered a Convergence, causing their former Mid-Level Sector to evolve into a much more dangerous High-Level Sector.[1]
The transition completely changed the scale of the enemies surrounding Eldoria. Galactic Emperors from other regions appeared, including beings whose individual power and technology greatly exceeded the civilizations Thaloria's dynasty had previously defeated. Some could control technology on a level comparable to Thaloria herself, while others possessed enough raw supernatural power to erase planets through individual attacks. Despite everything the Thaloria Dynasty had accumulated, only Velnorah and Thaloria were capable of meaningfully confronting several of these highest-level invaders.[1]
Velnorah came to view her mother's extermination of the Yuurakyo as a disastrous mistake. The race had once been the greatest obstacle to Thaloria's conquest, but precisely because they possessed such exceptional combat biology, they could also have become the Empire's strongest mass-produced answer to the Galactic Emperors. With them extinct, the Thaloria Dynasty possessed extraordinary technology but lacked sufficient numbers of biological warriors capable of surviving against opponents operating at that new level.[1]
The situation ultimately became unsustainable. Velnorah was forced away from Eldoria and eventually crossed into the Sector containing Earth while searching for allies capable of helping her reclaim her original civilization. The exact fate of Thaloria following the destruction of the old Imperial order has not been directly shown.[1]
Victor later analyzed records of the forces responsible for attacking Velnorah's original civilization and noticed that one of the beings who had fought Thaloria possessed authorities resembling Night and Darkness. This was particularly important because Nyx Elderblood had never sensed that foreign being competing with her for dominance over the same Concepts, helping Victor understand that apparently identical Divinities could exist independently in different Sectors before those cosmic regions were merged.[2]

### Skills & Abilities
Thaloria's known abilities are primarily technological rather than conventional Divine powers. The description of her as a "goddess" of technology in Velnorah's history refers to the absurd level of her technological mastery; Chapter 892 does not establish that Thaloria was a Primordial Goddess or grant her a conventional native Divinity of Technology.[1]
- Enhanced Intelligence / Genius Intelligence: Thaloria was exceptional even among the [[Xyrra]], a species naturally gifted in technological fields. Her intelligence allowed her to build an interstellar civilization, compensate for her physical limitations and repeatedly reverse-engineer phenomena belonging to completely foreign species.[1]
- Technomancy: Technology was Thaloria's defining specialty. Velnorah described her mother as effectively a goddess within a technological civilization and stated that there was almost nothing she could not accomplish through technology.[1]
- Technology Manipulation: Xyrra possessed an innate affinity for technological systems, with members of the race capable of producing highly advanced devices with extraordinary ease. Thaloria's talent exceeded the already exceptional baseline of her species.[1]
- Invention / Advanced Engineering: Thaloria developed technologies capable of compensating for her own biological shortcomings and became the technological foundation from which the later Thaloria Dynasty developed. The advanced suit and related systems inherited by Velnorah originated from this technological tradition.[1]
- Power Suit Engineering: Because Thaloria possessed a weak body by Xyrra standards, she created advanced equipment to compensate for that weakness rather than attempting to rely upon her natural physique. Her technology allowed her to fight and rule despite being physically inferior to ordinary members of her species.[1]
- Enhanced Physical Capability via Technology: Thaloria's equipment compensated for her naturally frail Xyrra body and allowed her to participate in conflicts involving opponents vastly beyond what her unassisted physiology should have been able to confront.[1]
- Biotechnology: Her research on the Yuurakyo extended far beyond ordinary medicine. She studied their genetics, gestation, reproduction, enhancement process and racial structure until she could deliberately exploit the species biologically.[1]
- Genetic Engineering: Thaloria attempted to clone Yuurakyo, studied the instability introduced by their enhancement process and eventually isolated the biological differences between enhanced adults and an unenhanced child.[1]
- Cloning Research: She attempted to reproduce Yuurakyo through cloning. The original attempts failed because the modified genetic code of mature Yuurakyo was too unstable, but the research contributed to her later understanding of the race.[1]
- Artificial Reproduction: Thaloria performed extensive reproductive experimentation on the captured Yuurakyo, testing cross-species gestation, artificial insemination and eventually the conditions required for successful Yuurakyo reproduction.[1]
- Biological Manipulation: She was capable of manipulating biological processes sufficiently to force conditions that allowed a viable Yuurakyo child to be produced despite repeated reproductive failures.[1]
- Soul Research: Her attempts at racial transformation allowed Thaloria to determine that a true change of species required alteration of the Soul in addition to reconstruction of the physical body. She was unable to reproduce the full racial authority of a Progenitor, but correctly identified the metaphysical requirement involved.[1]
- Biological Weaponry: After mapping the Yuurakyo's biological weaknesses, Thaloria engineered a virus that specifically attacked their brains and killed them with extreme speed.[1]
- Species-Specific Targeting: Her anti-Yuurakyo weapon was engineered to exploit characteristics unique to that race rather than functioning as an indiscriminate plague, allowing the biological attack to be deployed specifically against Kolo's greatest military population.[1]
- Preparation: The first Yuurakyo infiltrator inflicted severe losses before being captured, but Thaloria used the data acquired from him to prepare against the second infiltrator and capture the female considerably more efficiently.[1]
- Strategy Adaptation: Thaloria repeatedly altered her methods when confronted with failure. Attempts involving cloning, cross-species reproduction, racial transformation and artificial insemination each produced information that was incorporated into the next stage of her research.[1]
- Leadership: Thaloria rose from a physically disadvantaged Xyrra to the First Empress of [[Eldoria]] and presided over the dynasty while it unified its home planet and expanded into interstellar civilization.[1]
- Scientific Warfare: Rather than attempt to defeat the Yuurakyo exclusively through larger weapons or stronger soldiers, Thaloria converted scientific research directly into a strategic weapon capable of eliminating the opponent's principal military race.[1]

#### Xyrra Physiology
Thaloria retained the fundamental characteristics of the [[Xyrra]], an extraterrestrial race naturally adapted toward technology and possessing bodies significantly larger and stronger than ordinary Humans. However, she was specifically described as an abnormal and physically inferior member of the species.[1]
- Large Size: Even as an unusually small Xyrra, Thaloria stood approximately 2.5 meters tall.[1]
- Natural Technological Affinity: Xyrra were born with an extraordinary intuitive relationship with technology, allowing technological development to occur almost as naturally as supernatural races manipulate their inherent powers. Thaloria possessed this racial characteristic to an exceptional degree.[1]
- Below-Average Xyrra Physiology: Unlike most members of her species, Thaloria possessed a noticeably frail body. Her weakness was sufficiently pronounced that Velnorah compared it to the Universe balancing her unrivaled Technomancy by reducing her physical potential.[1]

### Legacy
Thaloria's influence continued long after Velnorah succeeded her. The royal naming tradition of [[Eldoria]] required descendants to inherit their mother's name together with the surname of the First Empress. Velnorah consequently retained Thaloria II as part of her original Imperial name and later passed the same tradition to her daughter, [[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]]. Even after the destruction of their old Empire, Velnorah preserved the name as a deliberate reminder that the dynasty and its history had not been erased.[3]
Thaloria's technological legacy also survived through Velnorah. Much of the knowledge that made Velnorah one of the greatest scientists of [[Victor Elderblood]]'s Empire was inherited from or developed upon the scientific civilization established by her mother. The later Elderblood technological revolution therefore indirectly preserved a portion of Thaloria's work even after Eldoria ceased functioning as the center of the old dynasty.[1][3]
Her treatment of the Yuurakyo became the most important cautionary aspect of that legacy. At the time, exterminating them was an extraordinarily successful military solution; it removed the greatest immediate obstacle to the Thaloria Dynasty. From the perspective of the later High-Level Sector war, however, it was strategically disastrous. Velnorah explicitly recognized that a population of enhanced Yuurakyo could have provided the mass combat power necessary to support herself and Thaloria against the invading Galactic Emperors.[1]
Thousands of years later, Velnorah continued remembering the Yuurakyo as a race once considered close to her ideal of a warrior species. After experiencing [[True Dragons]] and the engineered beings of the [[Elderblood Empire]], her standards changed considerably, but she still considered their exceptional adaptability useful enough to reconstruct Yuurakyo through cloning for future technological projects.[4]

### Trivia
- Thaloria's designation as a technological "goddess" in Chapter 892 describes the extraordinary degree of her technological talent. The chapter does not explicitly identify her as a native God, a Primordial God or a Primordial Entity.[1]
- The Overlord whose ascension triggered the Sector Convergence was Velnorah, after she succeeded and defeated Thaloria. Thaloria should therefore not automatically be given Velnorah's later Overlord classification.[1]
- Thaloria's exact age is unknown. "Eons" has not been established for her in the currently cited material.
- Her current fate is also unknown. She remained relevant during the invasion of the High-Level Sector and fought the incoming Galactic Emperors alongside Velnorah, but the story has not directly shown what happened to her after Velnorah fled [[Eldoria]].[1]
- The attacker associated with Thaloria in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later analysis possessed authorities over Night and Darkness. Nyx had never sensed this Being competing for her own Concepts, helping Victor discover that apparently identical Divine Concepts could exist separately between Sectors.[2]
- Despite being physically weak by the standards of her race, Thaloria became the First Empress of a civilization populated by [[Xyrra]] who were both larger and naturally stronger than her.[1]
- Her greatest military success—the extinction of the Yuurakyo—was later considered one of the greatest strategic mistakes in the history of the Thaloria Dynasty.[1]
- The surname Thaloria survived through three generations of the Imperial family: Thaloria Xyphora, Velnorah Xyphora Thaloria II and [[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]].[3]
