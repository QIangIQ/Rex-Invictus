Nharzuel is the Throne of Existence and one of the [[Seven Black Thrones]] of the [[Black Eclipse Pantheon]], in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]]. He stands under <Darkness>, the Great Darkness, whom [[Victor Elderblood]] addresses as Vharzath, and above the Ten Chief Gods. He embodies the essence of being. His domain takes in the existence of beings, objects, worlds, and concepts.[1][2]
The name comes out of what Lily took from [[Vaelthar]] and the nine Chief Gods. No body, armor, or face is given. He has no line of his own.[1]

### The Office
Existence, in this seat, is the fact of being, not a Beginning and not a clock. [[Vaelthar]] held Origin, and the stained <BEGIN> that gave [[Vharzath]]'s design its starting point. [[Aetherys]] keeps the time of the conquered galaxies. [[Oryndal]] is the bridge of distance and dimension. [[Elyndra]] maintains the reality those galaxies already have. Nharzuel is the one whose charge is that any of it exists at all. Beings, objects, worlds, and concepts sit inside the one domain.[3][1]
The report gives the reach and stops. No erasing of a being, no founding of a world, and no contest with another God are shown. The domain is the office. It is not a feat performed on a field.[1]
[[Zhaeroth]], the Throne of [[Chaos]], is charged with everything beneath Darkness, and that charge is what got him called a right hand. It is not this domain. Existence is not the deputy's office either. That station belongs to [[Xytherion]].[1]
The seat is not a Chief God's title. Those places are bestowed, blessed up until the holder can threaten a galaxy, and filled again when the holder falls. A Throne is not replaced that way. Black Eclipse has seven beings who threaten a galaxy in their own right, and the seven are these seats. Lily's judgment leaves Vaelthar as the experiment, made for a Chosen of Negativity and not chosen. The others are much more stable, and they are the true elites of Darkness. Nharzuel is one of those others.[4][3][1]

### Among the Seven
Velnorah reads the seven titles as an attempt at a self-contained universe. Ruby will not leave them as thrones inside that design. Taken as potential Primordial Beings of a new universe, they cover almost every essential function of a stable cosmos. Jeanne counts what is still absent. Life, death, and infinity. Existence is not among the absences. A cosmos can miss a god of life and still have a god whose domain is the being of what is already there. The reading is the wives'. It is not a System rank. Nharzuel is not entered as a Primordial God. The name [[Scathach]] calls arrogant is [[Chaos]].[1]
[[Vaelthar]] is the only Throne who has fought [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. A projection broke him. What remained was emptied, and the body was ordered burned. [[Xytherion]] is the right hand and the one sent to deploy the [[Nihilvore]]. This seat is given neither act.[5][3][6][1]
When the imperial offensive was taking the conquered galaxies apart, [[Vharzath]] loosed two of the six who remained and told them to put their own safety first. Two answered. They are not named. Nharzuel is not identified as either of them.[7]
