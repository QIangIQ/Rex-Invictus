True Death, registered by the System as <Death>, is one of the original [[Regulators of Existence]] and the primordial existence governing Death, END and part of the universal characteristics associated with Time. He is not merely a God possessing a Death-related Divinity; Death is one of the fundamental Primordial Aspects upon which the current Creation operates, while the Concepts of Death and END used by lesser Gods and Apocalypse Beasts ultimately derive from his primordial Authority.[1][2]
Death represents the true conclusion of Creation. Unlike ordinary destruction, which merely destroys or transforms existing structures, END is the terminal principle capable of bringing something to its actual conclusion. Beings such as [[Fenrir]] and [[Typhon]] possess derivative forms of END because their power originates from True Death himself.[3][4][5]
His purpose extends considerably beyond killing individual beings. Death supervises the terminal side of the Universe, regulates creatures connected to dead galaxies and END, filters much of the Energy left behind by galaxies that have reached their end, and ultimately serves as one of the final mechanisms through which the current cosmic cycle concludes. When the Universe eventually reaches its natural End, Death and the eventual [[Primordial Chaos]] are intended to be the final remaining participants. The Essence accumulated by Death throughout that process is then used by Primordial Chaos to establish the next cosmic cycle.[6][7]

### Physical Appearance
True Death's actual primordial form has never been completely described. During his first on-screen appearance, he manifested as a Being entirely composed of Darkness, possessing no conventionally described biological features. His presence was immediately recognized by the other Regulators without requiring an introduction, indicating that the dark humanoid manifestation is at least one form through which he normally interacts with Creation.[1]
The exact relationship between this manifestation and Death's complete conceptual existence remains unknown. As with the other true Primordials, the visible form through which Death communicates should not be assumed to encompass the entirety of the universal Aspect represented by him.[8]

### Personality
Death is exceptionally reclusive, detached and uninterested in most ordinary events occurring throughout Creation. His appearance during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s evolution surprised even the other Regulators because Death normally possesses little interest in what they consider mundane affairs. When he does speak, he generally maintains a neutral and impersonal demeanor, observing unusual phenomena more from curiosity than emotional investment.[1]
He also possesses an openly reluctant attitude toward unnecessary work. When Victor displaced [[Typhon]] into the [[Dimensional Void]] and the other Primordials turned toward Death to resolve the resulting problem, Death initially grumbled about being forced to act before eventually intervening. His behavior does not mean that he neglects his cosmic function; later chapters establish that Death continues supervising END-related beings, restricting dangerous creatures, processing Energy from dead galaxies and performing the work necessary for the terminal side of Creation to continue functioning.[4][6]
Death and Infinity are consequently among the least publicly active Regulators. According to The Owner of Limbo, both are secretive and generally intervene only when something directly affects their respective Domains. Death's apparent inactivity is therefore largely the result of the nature of his responsibility: compared with Life, Soul and Balance, many of his most important duties concern phenomena that occur only when worlds, galaxies or Creation itself approach their End.[7]
His perspective on existence is correspondingly patient and impersonal. Death knows that everything within the current Creation will eventually reach him. From his point of view, immediate events are often insignificant because he is the Being who will eventually close the curtains when the cosmic cycle reaches its conclusion.[6]

### Nature and Cosmic Role
True Death is one of the fundamental administrative Aspects produced within the structure of Creation. The author's auxiliary explanation identifies Death as one of the seven principal Primordial Regulators and specifically describes him as the true Death, the END of everything and the origin from which beings such as [[Fenrir]] and [[Typhon]] receive the END Concept.[3]
Later explanations refine this relationship further. Death, END and part of Time are linked to Primordial Death, meaning Divine Concepts corresponding to these characteristics ultimately draw their power from his primordial Aspect. His relationship with Time overlaps with Infinity, who governs Infinity and Space while sharing part of the characteristics associated with Time.[2]
Death should therefore be distinguished from ordinary Death Gods. A Death God possesses and develops a Divine Concept associated with death; True Death is part of the primordial structure from which that category of Concept receives its underlying Authority. Likewise, Apocalypse Beasts wielding END are not equivalent to True Death. They possess derivative manifestations of his Concept and remain subject to the source from which their END originates.[4][5]

#### END and the Beginning
Despite representing the ultimate End, Death's function is not opposed to the continuation of Creation. The End is one of the mechanisms that makes renewal possible. Galaxies die, old structures disappear and accumulated Essence is eventually preserved for another cycle. In this sense, Death represents the terminal condition necessary before another Beginning can occur.[7]
This should not be confused with possession of the positive Concept of <Begin>. Death does not replace the creative function of Positivity, Progenitors or [[Primordial Chaos]]. Rather, his END concludes the existing cycle so that what remains can subsequently be used to establish another one.[5][7]

### Synopsis

#### King among Kings
True Death first appeared directly during the enormous disturbance surrounding [[Victor Elderblood]]'s evolution into the Primogenitor of the Vampyric Dragons. Outside [[Olympus]], several Regulators gathered to observe the anomaly. Death's arrival surprised the others because of how rarely he concerned himself with ordinary affairs. Appearing as a Being entirely made of Darkness, he observed Victor and questioned why the other Regulators had allowed the existence of another Vampire Progenitor when one already existed. After learning that Victor's development had not been deliberately created by the Regulators, Death remained to observe the anomaly alongside them.[1]
Death became directly involved shortly afterward when Victor removed [[Typhon]] from the battlefield by sending the Apocalypse Beast into the [[Dimensional Void]]. Leaving an uncontrolled Beast of END there was unacceptable because Typhon's Concept would eventually erase the very Void separating Dimensions. [[The Universal Tree]], the Judges and Limbo consequently turned toward Death, since END originated from him. Although visibly reluctant to work, Death appeared beside Typhon, touched the Beast and temporarily sealed its END Concept. He then covered Typhon with his own Power so the Beast would survive while drifting through the hostile Dimensional Void.[4]
Once the larger crisis had passed, Death quickly lost interest in remaining with the other Primordials and returned to his own affairs, reinforcing his reputation as one of the least socially active Regulators.[9]
Death's relationship with the END Beasts became clearer during Victor's confrontation with [[Fenrir]]. Fenrir acknowledged that Death communicated with him and treated the Primordial as the superior authority responsible for END. The destruction caused by Fenrir's END also demonstrated one of Death's most unusual abilities: damage that had effectively been removed by END could be returned through Death's greater source-level Authority. Rather than ordinary reconstruction, the erased surroundings were restored despite having been affected by a power whose ordinary function was to bring things to their terminal state.[10]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
As [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s understanding of Divine Concepts increased, he learned the deeper distinction between Gods and true Primordials. A Being becomes a Primordial when it ceases merely wielding a Concept and instead merges with it sufficiently to become a fundamental part of Creation. During this explanation, END was identified as an exceptional Concept: unlike the enormous number of Divinities derived through Positivity and Negativity, END derives exclusively from True Death.[5]
This established Death not simply as the strongest user of END but as its primordial source within the current Creation. The later auxiliary explanation similarly states that Death, END and part of Time are connected directly to Primordial Death and that corresponding Divine Concepts draw power through his Aspect.[2]
Further information concerning END Beasts reinforced the extent of his jurisdiction. Velnorah considered the presence of multiple Apocalypse Beasts of END freely roaming one Sector extremely abnormal because such beings normally belong within the regions associated with Death rather than wandering Creation without supervision.[11]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later expansion into the deepest regions of Creation revealed more of what Death actually supervises. [[Hela Elderblood]] explained that creatures associated with the Abyss inhabit regions where galaxies have reached their true End. These dead regions are centered around the remains of destroyed Galactic systems and contain forms of existence almost entirely ignored by ordinary civilizations.[12]
When Victor entered one such region, Death immediately noticed the portal opening within the territory under his supervision and began observing him. The chapter clarified an important distinction: although the region was considered Death's Domain or territory, Death had not personally created it in the manner that the Owner of Limbo created and controls his own personal Realm. Consequently, Death did not possess the same automatic passive awareness over every event occurring there. Instead, he was responsible for watching the region, keeping its dangerous inhabitants under control and deciding when certain lesser creatures could interact with beings touched by END.[6]
Death's responsibilities also included regulating how the creatures of these dead regions developed. Such beings could grow by consuming the Energy produced by dying stars, planets and particularly dead galaxies. Death kept them sufficiently restricted that they could not simply consume whatever dying celestial bodies they desired, while the Primordials controlled access to the Energy of dead galaxies. Most of that Galactic Energy was additionally filtered by Death as part of his normal work.[6]
The existence of Endless provided another example of beings expected to fall within his jurisdiction. During the confrontation involving Victor and the Primordials, the Judges specifically complained about Death failing to keep Endless under control, demonstrating that even exceptional END-associated entities remained regarded as Death's responsibility.[13]
Victor subsequently questioned Azathoth about whether a true Primordial could actually be killed. Her answer established the deeper reason Death's existence is fundamentally different from that of an ordinary God. Primordials are pieces of the Universe itself: attempting to eliminate one completely means attempting to eliminate the Aspect of Creation represented by that Primordial. Destroying enough of those Aspects would cause Creation itself to fail. The System's administrative hierarchy consequently registered <Death> at Authority Rank 3, below only the greater primordial positions represented above him.[8]
The long-term purpose of Death was finally explained in substantially greater detail by The Owner of Limbo. Infinity and Death were described as exceptionally secretive Primordials who normally intervene only when events affect their own Domains. The Primordials maintain the current Universe until its natural cycle reaches the inevitable END. At that terminal point, only two ultimate participants are intended to remain: the eventual [[Primordial Chaos]] and Death. The Essence accumulated by Death at the End is then intended to be used by Primordial Chaos to create a new cosmic cycle.[7]
Thus, True Death is not simply the destroyer waiting at the conclusion of the Universe. He is one of the mechanisms ensuring that Creation can actually complete its cycle: governing END, restricting the beings produced around cosmic death, preserving or filtering what remains and eventually delivering the accumulated Essence from one finished Creation into the conditions required for the next.[6][7]

### Authority and Hierarchy
The author's original importance scale places Death beneath Infinity and above [[The Universal Tree]], [[The Judges of The Abyss]] and The Owner of Limbo. This list represents cosmological importance rather than a conventional combat ranking; the same explanation explicitly states that the principal Primordials do not possess a normal hierarchy among themselves because each is supreme within the function they administer.[3]
The later System Authority structure similarly registers:
Rank 0 — Unknown / vacant supreme position
Rank 1 — Positivity & Negativity
Rank 2 — Infinity
Rank 3 — <Death>
Rank 4 — Life
Rank 5 — Soul
Rank 6 — Balance[8]
Death's Rank therefore reflects the importance and administrative position of the Aspect he represents, not merely the amount of destructive Energy he can release.

### Relationship with The Abyss
True Death is deeply associated with The Abyss Realm and the dead regions of Creation, but the exact terminology requires distinction.
The Abyssal regions explored in Chapters 1059–1060 contain Galactic structures that have reached their true End and creatures capable of growing through the Energy released by dying celestial structures. Death is responsible for observing and regulating these beings, preventing unrestricted feeding and filtering much of the Energy left by dead galaxies.[12][6]
However, Chapter 1060 explicitly clarifies that the region [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] entered was not personally created by Death. It was considered Death's territory because he had been assigned responsibility for watching it, not because every part of the Abyss was a personal Reality produced from his own Power. This distinction explains why Death could immediately notice a portal and observe Victor while still lacking the complete passive awareness that a Primordial such as the Owner of Limbo possesses within a Realm personally created from his Authority.[6]
Thus, the most accurate description is:
The Abyss / dead-galaxy regions → terminal regions of Creation.
True Death → primordial supervisor and regulator of those regions and their END-associated inhabitants.

### Notes
- True Death's importance ranking and System Authority Rank are not identical systems. The former was an author-provided explanation of the relative importance of the Primordial functions, while the latter is the administrative ranking observed through the System.[3][8]
- The author explicitly describes the seven original Regulators as possessing infinite power and complete omnipotence within their own realms.[3]
- Chapter 1060 does not contradict Death's primordial omniscience. It establishes that the dead-galaxy territory [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] entered was under Death's supervision rather than a personal Realm created by him; using deeper omniscient observation there would also have alerted Victor.[6]
- END is not ordinary Destruction. Destruction can leave material, Energy or conditions from which something else can arise, whereas END represents the actual terminal conclusion of the affected existence.[3][4]
- END derives exclusively from True Death, whereas many other Divine Concepts ultimately arise through the broader Positivity/Negativity structure.[5]
- The later author explanation clarifies that Death, END and some characteristics of Time are related to Primordial Death, while Infinity separately governs Infinity, Space and shares influence over Time.[2]
- True Death is not responsible for judging Souls after ordinary death. That function belongs to [[The Judges of The Abyss]].
- Death's ability to restore END-erased damage does not necessarily give him the positive Concept of BEGIN; it is better understood as superior control over the terminal Concept from which the erasure originated.[10]
- Despite his apparent laziness and lack of interest in ordinary affairs, Death is continuously performing cosmic work involving END entities and the Energy of dead galaxies.[6][7]
- Death and Infinity are among the most secretive Primordials and generally intervene only when events affect their Domains.[7]

### Trivia
- Death's direct appearance in Chapter 802 surprised the other Regulators because of how rarely he involves himself in mundane events.[1]
- When the other Primordials forced the problem of [[Typhon]] onto him, Death audibly complained about having to work before nevertheless resolving the danger almost immediately.[4]
- Death's relationship with End Beasts resembles that of an administrator responsible for dangerous offspring or subordinates: their END originates from him, and other Primordials expect him to restrain them when they threaten unrelated parts of Creation.[4][13]
- Death refers to the conclusion of Creation metaphorically as the moment when he will "close the curtains".[6]
- Despite representing Death and END, his ultimate cosmic purpose is necessary for renewal rather than meaningless annihilation; without an End, the completed Essence of one Creation could not pass into the next cycle.[7]
- Of the major Regulators explained by the author, Death occupies one of the highest positions of cosmological importance, beneath Infinity but above Life, Soul and Balance in the original importance scale.[3]
