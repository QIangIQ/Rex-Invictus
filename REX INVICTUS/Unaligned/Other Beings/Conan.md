Conan is one of the seven Original Emperor's Soldiers personally created by [[Victor Elderblood]] to form the first generation of elite Imperial Commanders of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Like his six siblings, Conan was created as a hybrid Dragonoid possessing characteristics from several supernatural races, a minute fragment of Victor's own Soul and knowledge derived from the memories of historical warriors. Among the Originals, Conan is distinguished by his exceptionally muscular physique, direct combat mentality and instinctive preference for confronting enemies head-on.[1][2][3]
He does not stop to understand the tool. In the Nightmare Tower the armor had no helmet he could see, and he put one on anyway, because what mattered was that it worked.[3] On the hive he is the one Clare puts between Kaleb and whatever is coming, and the narration classes him with Caelus and Kiana as the berserkers who destroy everything with the fury of the Emperor in their hearts.[4][5] On the offensive against The Black Eclipse he assembles the troops that occupy and hold what has been taken.[6] A mass eating a solar system is, in his mouth, a pile of flesh.[7] Two days after the [[Varkhess League]] lost the worlds it had seized, he led the elites who took its system in less than thirty minutes, threw the leader down in front of Kaleb, and named the god who had paid them.[8]

### Physical Appearance
Conan possesses one of the most physically imposing appearances among the seven Original Emperor's Soldiers. He is a dark-skinned man with black hair, brown eyes, noticeably sharp teeth and a pair of black horns. His body is exceptionally broad and muscular, to the point that [[Clare Bloodhunter]] immediately compared his appearance to the barbarians contained within her inherited memories. Everything about his build was described as strongly masculine.[2]
Conan's physique is used as one of the physical extremes when describing his brothers. [[Caelus Hunter]] possesses a much leaner swimmer-like build, while [[Kaleb Hunter]] and John possess bodies that fall between Caelus' slender physique and Conan's enormous muscular frame.[2]
Like other Emperor's Soldiers, Conan normally wears the Imperial black uniform bearing dark-violet and red details and the Dragon crest of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Empire over the right side of the chest. During combat he is equipped with highly advanced adaptive Imperial armor created under Victor's technological system. The armor can alter itself according to the requirements of its wearer and is capable of generating protective components such as a helmet when necessary.[2][3]
As an Emperor's Soldier, Conan also possesses metamorphic characteristics derived from the Vampiric components incorporated into his race. The Emperor's Soldiers were deliberately designed so that their naturally enormous Dragonoid bodies would not prevent them from infiltrating settlements or performing missions requiring more conventional proportions. The design height is about four meters. The visible height is not fixed.[1]

### Personality
Conan is a direct, instinctive and highly aggressive warrior. His first reaction when placed inside an active battlefield by [[Scathach]] was not to stop and investigate the political situation, examine his equipment or devise a complicated strategy. Conan immediately put on his helmet and set out to kill the monsters, the soldiers, or anything that got in his way.[3]
The helmet is the telling detail. The armor did not have one he could see. He did not know how it appeared. He did not care. What mattered was that it worked.[3]
Despite this behavior, Conan is explicitly not stupid. Scathach's judgment, word for word, is that he was the kind of warrior who acted before thinking, but that didn't mean he was stupid. His name was Conan for a reason. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] knew his nature very well. His behavior was exactly what she expected from him.[3]
Conan therefore operates according to an extremely primal understanding of battle. Rather than approaching warfare through the careful observation favored by John or [[Caelus Hunter]], he understands combat by entering it, and the narration later names that entry for what it is. He, Caelus, and Kiana are the berserkers who destroy everything with the fury of the Emperor in their hearts.[3][4]
The same habit is still there when the alarm is real. On the hive world the scanners in his helmet and Clare's went off. The two of them ignored the alarm and looked at the hive. When the number of enemies had to be explained, his answer was short. "Yes, there are enemies... Ours." Then, "More specifically, the Emperor."[4][5]
The bluntness does not leave him when the thing in front of him is not a hive. A mass eating a solar system is, in his mouth, a pile of flesh. John is the one who adds that a pile of flesh can spread. In a ruined capital the same directness has a use. He throws the captured leader down in front of Kaleb, names the god who paid them, and calls the rest a recurring pattern. They know the financier only because the mind is weak. His answer for that mind is Kaguya's girls, and the fun they will have squeezing it. Kaleb tells him not to say that aloud.[7][8]
Like the other Originals, Conan possesses absolute loyalty toward Victor. From the moment he awakened, he instinctively recognized Victor as his Emperor and Creator and joined his siblings in kneeling before him without hesitation. Victor had deliberately created the seven to become his own professional military leadership rather than servants belonging to any of his wives, making service to the Emperor and the Empire one of the fundamental purposes embedded within Conan's existence.[2][9]
Although Conan was created for warfare, he was not designed as a mindless biological weapon. Victor deliberately gave the seven Originals individual Souls, personalities and the capacity to continue developing through experience. Their implanted memories were intended to provide knowledge and lessons rather than completely dictate the people they would eventually become.[1][2]

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Conan was created during [[Victor Elderblood]]'s attempts to establish a professional military force directly subordinate to the Emperor. Victor did not want the future Empire to depend exclusively upon the armies, followers and personal forces belonging to his wives. At the same time, experiments involving clones demonstrated that ordinary bodies could not simply contain meaningful portions of Victor's existence without encountering compatibility problems. Victor and the scientists assisting him therefore developed an entirely new race specifically intended for Imperial warfare.[1]
The foundation of the resulting species was the Dragonoid body, chosen because of its physical robustness and compatibility with Victor's Draconic existence. Victor supplemented that foundation with several other racial characteristics: Human adaptability and ingenuity, Angelic obedience and benevolent tendencies, Demonic aggression and cruelty, Faerie characteristics providing spiritual and natural affinity, and Vampiric regeneration and metamorphosis. He further Blessed the soldiers with abilities associated with combat and incorporated an extremely small portion of his Soul into their construction, allowing the otherwise contradictory racial components to coexist as a stable new species.[1]
The first seven successful Originals were [[Clare Bloodhunter]], [[Laura Bloodhunter]], [[Kiana Bloodhunter]], Conan, [[Caelus Hunter]], [[Kaleb Hunter]] and John. Their genetics were deliberately constructed so that future soldiers could be replicated from their templates, although Victor placed safeguards upon their genetic material to prevent unauthorized reproduction. The seven were consequently not merely individual soldiers; they were intended to become the genetic, spiritual and military foundation from which a much larger Imperial army could eventually be developed.[1]
Unlike most of the Originals, Conan was not given a surname. Only Conan and John possessed simple single names among the initial seven, something Clare herself noticed upon awakening but did not question because she trusted Victor's intentions.[2]
Conan and his siblings spent approximately ten subjective years developing and receiving instruction before leaving their creation chambers. Victor supplied them with memories and experiences belonging to warriors and heroes of the past, exposing the children to warfare, tactics, strategy and the consequences of combat long before they personally entered a battlefield. The memories were intended as lessons rather than substitutes for genuine experience, something Victor emphasized after the seven finally awakened.[2]
At ten years old, Conan emerged from his chamber with an already mature supernatural body. Alongside his brothers and sisters, he immediately recognized the enormous armored figure before him as Victor Elderblood—their Father, Creator and Emperor—and knelt before him. Victor was pleased by their development but immediately warned them that memories of heroes did not automatically make them heroes. Knowledge could provide a foundation, but practical experience and specialized training were still necessary if the seven were going to fulfill the roles he had created them for.[2]
Victor subsequently brought the seven before four extraordinary instructors. Hassan-i Sabbah was tasked with teaching them assassination and the countless methods through which a life could be taken; [[Julius Caesar]] would educate them in politics and rulership; [[Abe-no-Seimei]] would teach philosophy and wisdom; while [[Scathach]], the Teacher of Heroes and Victor's own former master, would personally develop their combat capabilities.[9]
Scathach initially regarded the children as unimpressive because her standards had become distorted after years of comparing talented people to Victor and their daughters. Victor told her to judge the seven against the great Heroes and Gods of the past rather than the abnormal members of their family. After examining them properly, Scathach changed her opinion. Although the seven did not possess the absurd level of talent belonging to Victor's daughters, they were far from mediocre and possessed potential comparable to exceptional Heroes and Gods.[9]
Victor also explained the purpose he envisioned for them. The seven were not disposable servants created simply to die for him. They were his creations, future military leaders and the foundation of an independent Imperial force directly serving the Emperor. Conan consequently became one of the seven original Imperial Commanders, with later generations of Emperor's Soldiers being created from the genetic and experiential foundations established by him and his siblings.[1][9]
Their first serious practical lesson occurred inside the Elderblood family's personal Nightmare Tower. Scathach placed each soldier into an independent version of a large-scale war scenario involving two technologically and supernaturally developed Empires already fighting one another when interdimensional monsters began invading their world. The seven were ordered to pacify both Empires and eliminate the invading monsters rather than merely survive the battlefield.[3]
Scathach deliberately provided them with only a small amount of equipment: state-of-the-art Imperial armor, a plasma firearm and a sword forged from metals taken from the deepest regions of Hell, where the concentration of Miasma was particularly dense and dangerous. When Clare questioned why they had been thrown into such an extreme situation before receiving conventional combat lessons, Scathach explained that their inherited memories already gave them enough theoretical knowledge. What they lacked was firsthand experience of fear, injury, duty and survival. Only after seeing how each behaved in genuine warfare could she determine the most efficient way to train them. There are no rules in war. Only those who win are considered just, and the strong are what dictate history.[3]
The seven reacted very differently once Scathach disappeared. While John inspected his equipment, Caelus observed the battlefield, Laura approached one of the Empires and Kaleb deliberately sought its strongest ruler, Conan required no such preparation. He wasted no time. He put on his helmet and set out to kill the monsters, the soldiers, or anything that got in his way. The armor had not shown him a helmet. He did not know how it was there. He did not care. What mattered was that it worked.[3]
Scathach was unsurprised. Their actions were expected, just like his. He was the kind of warrior who acted before thinking, but that didn't mean he was stupid. His name was Conan for a reason. Victor knew his nature very well.[3]
That division is how the seven are described once the wars are real. Clare and John are the natural leaders. Conan, Caelus, and Kiana are the berserkers who destroy everything with the fury of the Emperor in their hearts. Kaleb and Laura are the minds. Set inside the group, Laura and Kaleb can look ordinary beside John, Conan, and Clare. Outside it, they are just as abnormal, and the place they match those three is battle intelligence.[4]
On the hive, Clare told Conan and Kaleb to get ready for battle. The answer from them was "Yes!" Her order for the samples was specific. She would collect them. Kaleb would prepare his equipment. Conan would ensure that no one approached Kaleb. The two of them stayed in the air to keep the advantage of height. In that confined space the advantage was thin, because the hostiles could climb the walls and jump, and the air was still safer than the ground. When the number of enemies had to be named, Conan said, "Yes, there are enemies... Ours." Then, "More specifically, the Emperor." Kaleb's reading of that was that the hive had felt the danger when the Emperor looked at the planet, and had started producing more beings.[4]
Akasha cut the line once the samples were in and the data sent. Clare carried the sample to Kaleb and told him to prepare the virus, then joined Conan to hold the hostiles off. She drew her sword, and Dragon Fire lit the area while she killed hundreds. An earthquake started. The scanners in Clare's helmet and in Conan's began to sound. The two of them ignored the scanners and looked at the hive. It was releasing slimy monsters. The alert that followed reported massive life signs, an abundance of [[Natural Energy]], a threat at the level of a High-Level Combat God, and five hostiles. Kaleb finished the vial. Clare, Conan, and Kaleb flew at the hive together, their bodies covered in Dragon Fire, and went straight into the core. Kaleb and Conan were there to keep Clare safe. She injected the virus. The hive screamed, the pulsing stopped, and what remained of its effort turned to defending against what she had put in it.[5]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
The Emperor approved an offensive on several fronts against the sectors nearest The Black Eclipse, with priority on the systems that held the faction's transit and supply routes. The work was divided. John took the reconnaissance of the border. Kaleb coordinated the attacks on military installations. Conan assembled the troops charged with occupying and securing the positions that had been taken. Kiana and Caelus watched the movement between the fronts, so the advance would not be a single push. The First Legion's victory on that field, which had no super-elite on it, is John's, and the principal battlefield was [[Scathach]]'s.[6]
A weapon of Darkness then appeared in Solar System 1-1, eating the matter of the region and making smaller monsters that spread as a swarm. Conan's line was, "It's just a pile of flesh." John's eyes narrowed. A pile of flesh that can spread. He raised the threat and cleared the system. The reading of what the organism was, and of whose weapon it was, went to Kaleb.[7]
The [[Varkhess League]] used the reorganization on an Imperial border to take three newly incorporated worlds. An expeditionary force took them back. The retaliation was Conan's. Two days later, under his leadership, a troop of elite soldiers capable of annihilating entire solar systems stormed the League's system and took everything in less than thirty minutes. There was hardly any resistance. The elites were simply too far above what was there. A foolish decision, driven by fear, had cost the League everything.[8]
In the ruins of the capital he stood with Kaleb over a white-skinned alien with octopus-like eyes and six arms. He threw the leader of the faction down in front of Kaleb. "The one who financed them is a god named [[Auralion]]." Kaleb placed the office. A chief god of the being known as <Emperor>. Conan's judgment of the rest was disparaging. A recurring pattern. They knew who had paid only because the mind was weak. The alien was already unconscious at his feet. "Send him to the girls. They will have their fun squeezing the information out of him." The girls are the B.H.E., Black Hand The Emperor, the third legion under Kaguya, who work in secret and take the dirtier tasks. Kaleb told him not to say that aloud, or he would get into trouble.[8]

### Skills & Abilities
The headers follow Superpower Wiki terminology. Each entry is what Conan is built as, or what he does with it.

#### Hybrid Physiology
- Dragonoid Foundation: The body is a Dragonoid, chosen for robustness and for compatibility with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Draconic existence. The black horns, the sharp teeth, and the barbarian build are the frame Clare sees when he comes out of the chamber.[1][2]
- Racial Composite: Human adaptability and ingenuity, Angelic obedience and benevolent tendencies, Demonic aggression and cruelty, Faerie affinity with Nature, and Vampiric regeneration and metamorphosis, fused by Victor's Essence into one species that still reads as Dragonoid.[1]
- Soul Fragment: An extremely small portion of Victor's Soul is built into the Originals. It is what lets the contradictory pieces coexist, and it grows with the soldier.[1]
- Combat Blessing: Victor Blessed the race with abilities associated with combat.[1]
- Stable Chimera: [[Scathach]]'s later reading of the seven is that they are stable supernatural chimeras, not ordinary Dragonoids. Inside the group, John, Conan, and Clare are the standard the others are set against.[3][4]

#### Supernatural Condition
- Supernatural Strength: He is the muscular extreme of the seven. John and Kaleb are described as the builds between him and the lean [[Caelus Hunter]]. At ten, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] already treats a mistake from one of them as something that can erase a city.[1][2]
- Supernatural Agility: The finished design stands about four meters and is still as agile as an Elf. The size was not bought by giving up movement.[1]
- Supernatural Durability: The Dragonoid frame is the durable one, built to survive the modifications and then the wars the race was made for.[1]

#### Regenerative Healing Factor
- Vampiric Regeneration: Regeneration was kept in the design on top of the Dragonoid durability, so a wound does not take the soldier out of the kind of war he was made for.[1]

#### Shapeshifting
- Size Shifting: Vampiric metamorphosis lets him leave the four-meter frame. The change exists so an enormous Dragonoid body does not make infiltration, or work among ordinary people, impossible.[1]

#### Berserker Mode
- Fury of the Emperor: Conan, Caelus, and Kiana are the berserkers who destroy everything with the fury of the Emperor in their hearts. Clare and John are the leaders. Kaleb and Laura are the minds.[4]
- Action Before Thought: Dropped into a war, he puts the helmet on and goes for the monsters, the soldiers, or anything in the way. [[Scathach]]'s judgment is that he acts before thinking, and that this does not make him stupid. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] named him for that nature.[3]

#### Instinctive Aptitude
- Unexamined Equipment: The armor had not shown him a helmet. He did not know how one formed. He put it on anyway, because what mattered was that it worked, and the suit produced it.[3]
- Blunt Reading: Confronted with the biomass weapon, his line is that it is just a pile of flesh. The spread, and the divinity in it, are read by John and by Kaleb.[7]

#### Flight
- Aerial Combat: Over the hive he and Kaleb hold the air while Clare takes the samples. The hostiles can climb the walls and jump, and the air is still safer than the ground. When the virus is ready, he flies into the core with Clare and Kaleb, the three of them covered in Dragon Fire.[4][5]

#### Supernatural Combat
- Screening: Clare's order is that no one approaches Kaleb while the samples are collected and the equipment is prepared. That post is his.[4]
- Line Holding: While Kaleb builds the virus, Conan holds the hostiles with Clare. On the entry he and Kaleb are the ones charged with keeping her alive until the core is injected.[5]

#### Weapon Proficiency
- Plasma Firearm: The sidearm issued for the first war. The batteries recharge through sunlight or other Energy.[3]
- Miasma Sword: Forged from metals of the deepest Hell, where the Miasma is dense and toxic. A cut poisons as well as wounds.[3]
- Assassination: Hassan-i Sabbah teaches the ways of taking a life.[9]
- Martial Training: [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] builds the combat instruction on how he actually entered the first war.[9][3]

#### Power Suit
- Adaptive Armor: Imperial armor that changes to the wearer, with an assistance system based on [[Akasha Elderblood]] for the body, the vitals, and the equipment.[3]
- Helmet Generation: The suit produces a helmet it had not been showing.[3]
- Scanner: The helmet reads the field. On the hive it reports massive life signs, an abundance of [[Natural Energy]], a threat at the level of a High-Level Combat God, and five hostiles. He and Clare look at the hive instead of the alarm.[5]

#### Leadership
- Imperial Commander: One of the seven Original commanders, under [[Scathach]], and a template later soldiers are developed from.[9]
- Occupation: On the Black Eclipse border he assembles the troops who occupy and secure the positions already taken.[6]
- System Assault: He leads the retaliation against the [[Varkhess League]]. The troop under him can annihilate solar systems. They storm the League's system and take it in less than thirty minutes. Hardly any resistance.[8]
- Prisoner Handling: In the ruined capital he throws the six-armed leader down in front of Kaleb, names [[Auralion]] as the financier, and judges the captive's mind too weak to have hidden who paid. He wants the man sent to the B.H.E. under Kaguya.[8]

#### Enhanced Intelligence
- Inherited Memory: Ten years of other warriors' wars, tactics, strategy, and consequences, kept as lessons and not as his own life.[2]
- Politics: [[Julius Caesar]], on rule.[9]
- Philosophy: [[Abe-no-Seimei]], on judgment.[9]
- God-Class Talent: Judged against the heroes and Gods [[Scathach]] had trained, rather than against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters, the seven sit in that company.[9]
- Pattern: He calls the League's sale of itself a recurring pattern. The financier is known only because the mind is weak.[8]
