### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 1205
Draveth is the Chief God of Inevitability and one of the Ten Chief Gods of the [[Black Eclipse Pantheon]], in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]]. The name is a title. <Darkness>, whom [[Victor Elderblood]] addresses as Vharzath, bestows it. The charge, ninth in the order Lily gives, is inevitability.[1]
The first Imperial report ties Darkness, Night, Void, Silence, Shadows, Death, Fear, Eclipse, and Oblivion to the pantheon. Inevitability is not in that list. The extraction adds it, and gives it this title. No earlier God is identified as the same office.[2][1]
No body, armor, face, or pronoun is given. The name is not spoken on a battlefield.[1]

### The Office
A Chief God does not take the chair by strength. The ruler chooses a God who has reached an elite, gives the title and the realm, and blesses the holder up to a galactic threat. Mastery is later. The weapon, at the start, is the patron's Darkness, alien to the realm it has been laid on. Inevitability is the realm. Nothing is shown of how that realm is used. No fate is closed, no future is forced, and no battle is decided by this seat.[3][1]
The office can be filled again. The predecessor of the void kept the same charge, and the God raised after him can threaten a galaxy without yet being the equal of the one who died. Violet hears the ten as concepts bound to negativity. Inevitability is one of the words she has in front of her when she says it. The seat stands under the [[Seven Black Thrones]]. Those seven are the pantheon's own galaxy-level fighters. This chair is lifted to their tier and does not stay there by itself.[4][1]

### Among the Ten
Ninth. Oblivion is before it. The last item is not another concept. [[Nihrael]] holds oblivion. [[Vaelzara]] is the tenth title, and the tenth charge in the same order is [[Vaelthar]] himself. Draveth is the last of the charges that is still a concept-word. The four who fight War are not this God. Nine Chief Gods are emptied with Vaelthar. The nine are not named, and this title is not pointed at one of them.[5][1]
