### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 155
Zandriel was an Inquisition Hunter, artificial Human-Vampire Hybrid, and longtime companion of [[Father Julian]]. He participated in the Inquisition operation targeting [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] at her former family mansion and became the primary combatant responsible for critically injuring [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]].[1][2]
Although initially presented simply as an abnormally powerful Human Hunter with extraordinary regeneration, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later discovered that Zandriel possessed both a Human heart and unmistakable signs of Vampiric alteration. Subsequent research by [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] revealed that Zandriel was far more significant than the other experimental [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]]: he was the first successful Human-Vampire Hybrid produced by the Inquisition's program and was officially documented as Patient Zero.[3][4]
Unlike later artificial Hybrids such as [[Father Bruno]] and [[Carlos Reiss]], Zandriel possessed a complete and comparatively stable genetic structure. Ruby discovered that he had already been born with approximately one-quarter Vampire ancestry, most likely inherited from a Noble Vampire grandparent. The Inquisition's experiments subsequently increased the Vampiric proportion of his biology from approximately one-quarter to one-half.[4]
Despite being considered a theoretically "perfect" Hybrid, Zandriel remained fundamentally inferior to either a true Vampire or a sufficiently powerful Human Hunter. He could not naturally develop stronger Vampiric powers over time, and excessive use of Hunter magic actively harmed the Vampire component of his body.[4]
Zandriel was ultimately captured by Victor Elderblood following the Fulger Mansion incident. After torture left him mentally and physically broken, Victor determined that further interrogation was useless and killed him by piercing both his heart and brain with a sword of ice. His corpse was deliberately preserved for further research, eventually allowing Ruby to uncover the origins of the Inquisition's Hybrid program.[5][4]

### Physical Appearance
The novel provides relatively little detailed information regarding Zandriel's ordinary physical appearance.
He was a male Hunter who fought using two swords simultaneously. When channeling Faith, his entire body became surrounded by a brilliant golden glow while supernatural power altered and reinforced his weapons.[2]
During his confrontation with [[Victor Elderblood]], Zandriel's eyes became visibly blood-red like those of a Vampire, exposing one of the clearest external signs of his Hybrid physiology.[3]
Victor's supernatural vision revealed further abnormalities hidden beneath his otherwise Human appearance. Zandriel possessed a Human heart while simultaneously carrying unmistakable evidence of Vampiric alteration, including what appeared to be a Vampire bite mark upon his neck.[3]

### Personality
Zandriel was an extremely fanatical and violent Hunter whose religious devotion had developed into an obsessive desire to exterminate supernatural beings.
He repeatedly described Vampires as demons and abominations that needed to be punished for their crimes against Humanity. During his battle with Kaguya, Zandriel loudly declared himself a "chosen instrument of God" before attacking her beneath a golden aura of Faith.[2]
His behavior was sufficiently extreme that Maria remembered rumors describing him as almost demonic despite his angelic-sounding name. According to those stories, [[Father Julian]] regularly traveled with Zandriel even though many [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] regarded Zandriel's behavior as completely insane.[2]
Julian disagreed with this assessment and repeatedly insisted that his companion was perfectly normal. Their interactions demonstrate that the two possessed a genuine friendship beyond simply belonging to the same operation; Julian praised Zandriel's fighting ability and became visibly concerned whenever he was critically injured.[2][3]
Zandriel's fanaticism was extreme enough that even receiving a normally fatal wound did not discourage him. When Kaguya slit his throat, Zandriel briefly wondered whether he would finally enter Heaven. After his regeneration restored the wound, he enthusiastically concluded that God had refused to allow him into Heaven because he still needed to eliminate more sinners.[2]
He also possessed an unusually strong enjoyment of combat. Rather than focusing efficiently upon the mission to capture Sasha, Zandriel became absorbed in attempting to defeat Kaguya, forcing Julian to repeatedly remind him to stop playing around and complete his objective.[2]
Despite his religious obsession, Zandriel was an experienced combatant capable of adapting during a fight. Kaguya's assassination techniques and Shadow movement repeatedly frustrated him, but he continued tracking her movements and releasing increasingly large quantities of Faith in an attempt to overwhelm the battlefield.[2]
His mind ultimately proved considerably less resilient than his body. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s prolonged torture completely destroyed Zandriel's psychological stability. By the time Victor attempted to interrogate him, Zandriel could no longer provide coherent information and simply repeated fragmented statements about God.[5]

### Synopsis

#### A New Pillar
Zandriel first appeared as part of the large Hunter force secretly stationed within [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]]'s former mansion.
Sasha had returned alongside [[Maria Elderblood]] to recover an item hidden within a secret Fulger chamber. After detecting more than twenty [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] inside the mansion, she ordered Maria and Kaguya to capture the intruders while she entered the hidden room.[1]
Among the force were numerous ordinary Hunters, a mercenary Werewolf, [[Father Julian]] and Zandriel. Julian acted as the field leader and specifically instructed Zandriel that Maria and Kaguya could be killed, but Sasha needed to be captured alive because obtaining the Fulger heiress remained their principal mission.[1]
Once combat began, Zandriel immediately proved to be one of the most dangerous individuals present.
He rushed Kaguya with such speed that she could barely respond, forcing her to defend using her Shadow abilities. Despite managing to block partially, Kaguya still received a deep wound across her abdomen.[2]
Zandriel pointed his twin swords at her and declared that demons needed to be punished for their crimes against Humanity. Golden Faith enveloped his body while supernatural energy altered the shape and power of his swords.[2]
Kaguya disappeared into the shadows and used her skills as an assassin rather than confronting Zandriel directly. She emerged unexpectedly from the ceiling's shadow and sliced his throat open before he could properly react.[2]
The wound appeared fatal. Zandriel collapsed while choking on his own blood and briefly wondered whether he would finally be allowed into Heaven.[2]
Instead, brilliant golden energy appeared around the wound and his throat regenerated at extremely high speed.
Rather than reacting with surprise, Zandriel enthusiastically interpreted his survival as proof that God still had work for him to perform, declaring that Heaven had refused him because more sinners remained to be destroyed.[2]
Zandriel resumed fighting Kaguya. His two swords were continually reinforced by golden supernatural energy while his Hybrid physiology repaired the injuries inflicted upon him.[2]
The fight gradually became increasingly destructive. Kaguya repeatedly attacked from the shadows while Zandriel attempted to force her into direct confrontation. Even after accumulating wounds across his entire body, Zandriel continued regenerating them rapidly enough that Kaguya compared the experience to fighting a Vampire rather than a Human.[2]
Julian eventually grew frustrated by Zandriel's inability to finish Kaguya and ordered him to stop playing around.
Responding seriously, Zandriel released an enormous quantity of golden power and raised both swords while invoking the Father, Son and Holy Spirit. He then prepared a much stronger Faith-enhanced attack intended to purge Kaguya.[2]
The prolonged confrontation ultimately ended with Kaguya suffering catastrophic injuries. By the time Sasha Elderblood Fulger returned from the secret chamber, Kaguya had lost her arms and legs, suffered extensive abdominal damage, lost an eye and sustained severe burns while being restrained by golden chains.[3]
Although the Hunters never explicitly attributed every individual wound to Zandriel, he had been Kaguya's primary opponent throughout the battle and was directly responsible for a significant portion of her condition.[2][3]
When Sasha demanded to know who had harmed Kaguya, Julian refused to provide a meaningful answer and instead ordered Zandriel to attack Sasha from behind.[3]
Zandriel attempted the ambush but Sasha entered her Lightning state and avoided his swords before violently kicking him through the mansion wall.[3]
The situation became hopeless for the Hunters when [[Victor Elderblood]] arrived after sensing Sasha's distress.
Victor ignored the Hunters initially and concentrated entirely upon Kaguya. After stabilizing her with his own blood, his attention shifted toward the people responsible for what had happened.[3]
Zandriel became one of his first targets.
Victor overwhelmed him with virtually no difficulty and began examining his unusual physiology. His eyes had become blood-red like those of a Vampire, yet Victor's supernatural vision still revealed a Human heart inside his body.[3]
Victor additionally noticed what appeared to be a Vampire bite mark and realized he had never encountered a being constructed in such a way before.[3]
Curious about Zandriel's regenerative power, Victor crushed both of his eyes before observing his previously destroyed throat restoring itself. Victor then tore his throat open again and froze Zandriel's body before the regeneration could immediately repair the damage.[3]
During Julian's subsequent interrogation, Victor learned that General James intended to use Sasha's superior Noble Vampire blood to create additional experimental "faithful". The stronger the Vampire bloodline involved, the greater the expected quality of the resulting subjects.[6]
Victor immediately connected this information to Zandriel. His combination of a Human heart, Vampire characteristics and extreme regeneration strongly suggested that he was one of the products created through the Inquisition's Hybrid experiments.[6]
After finishing with Julian, Victor attempted to interrogate Zandriel personally.
The attempt was useless.
Victor's torture had reduced Zandriel's body to a mutilated mass of blood and flesh, while the Hunter's mind had completely collapsed. Zandriel could no longer communicate coherently and merely repeated fragmented statements concerning God.[5]
Determining that continuing the interrogation was pointless, Victor created a sword of ice and simultaneously pierced Zandriel's heart and brain, killing him permanently despite his regeneration.[5]
Victor deliberately preserved Zandriel's corpse instead of destroying it. He believed that the unusual body itself could reveal more information than its former owner and intended to have someone experienced investigate it.[5]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Zandriel remained relevant long after his death because his corpse became an important source of information regarding the Inquisition's Hybrid program.
While analyzing samples and records obtained from the Church's laboratories, [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] compared the genetic structures of several artificial Human-Vampire Hybrids, including [[Father Bruno]], [[Carlos Reiss]] and Zandriel.[4]
The results immediately distinguished Zandriel from the others.
Church records identified him as "Codename Zandriel" and explicitly classified him as the first Hybrid Hunter—Patient Zero of the experimental program.[4]
Unlike Bruno, whose genetic structure contained numerous missing components and apparently required supernatural intervention simply to remain functional, Zandriel's DNA was complete. Ruby therefore described him as a "perfect Hybrid" from a purely biological perspective.[4]
The reason for this stability was subsequently discovered.
Zandriel had not originally been a completely ordinary Human. Even before the Inquisition's experiments, approximately one-quarter of his ancestry was already Vampiric. Ruby theorized that one of his grandparents—most likely his grandfather—had been a Noble Vampire.[4]
This inherited blood made Zandriel naturally stronger and more physically developed than an ordinary Human while still allowing him to access Hunter magic.[4]
The Inquisition then used Zandriel as the foundation of its experiments and successfully increased the Vampiric portion of his biology from approximately 1/4 to 1/2.[4]
From a genetic perspective, the process succeeded far better than it did with later experimental subjects.
However, Ruby concluded that even Zandriel represented a fundamentally flawed result.
He inherited the strengths and weaknesses of a Hybrid but could not continuously increase his power with age in the manner of a Vampire. At the same time, extensive use of Hunter magic damaged the Vampiric side of his physiology, preventing him from endlessly strengthening himself through Human methods either.[4]
The experiments had also failed to grant him access to genuine active Vampire powers despite increasing his Vampiric ancestry. His Vampire component primarily provided enhanced physical characteristics and regeneration rather than the complete supernatural abilities of a true Vampire.[4]
Ruby estimated that had Zandriel never been subjected to the experiments and simply remained a natural quarter-Vampire descendant, he could theoretically have lived approximately 200-300 years.[4]
His corpse therefore provided one of the clearest explanations for why the Inquisition's attempts to manufacture ideal Human-Vampire soldiers continued producing compromised results.

### Skills & Abilities
Zandriel was an unusually powerful Hunter whose abilities combined Human Faith with inherited and artificially enhanced Vampire physiology. As Patient Zero, he was considerably more genetically stable than many of the artificial Hybrids developed after him.[4]

#### Hunter Abilities
- Faith Manipulation: Zandriel possessed access to the supernatural power utilized by Human [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]]. His Faith manifested as brilliant golden energy surrounding his body and weapons.[2]
- Prayer-Based Empowerment: Zandriel invoked religious prayers while channeling his Faith, including appeals to God and the Holy Trinity. These invocations increased the quantity of golden supernatural energy surrounding him and his weapons.[2]
- Weapon Empowerment: Zandriel could channel Faith directly into both of his swords, altering their appearance and substantially increasing their supernatural power.[2]
- Dual Swordsmanship: Zandriel was highly proficient with two swords simultaneously and used them as his principal fighting style. His speed and skill were sufficient to injure Kaguya before she could completely defend herself.[2]
- Faith Aura: When fighting seriously, Zandriel released enormous quantities of golden supernatural power from his body, surrounding himself in a radiant aura.[2]

#### Hybrid Abilities
- Human-Vampire Hybrid Physiology: Zandriel possessed a combination of Human and Vampire biology. Unlike many later artificial Hybrids, his genetic structure was complete and biologically stable enough for Ruby to describe him as a theoretically perfect Hybrid.[4]
- Natural Vampire Ancestry: Even before experimentation, approximately one-quarter of Zandriel's ancestry originated from a Noble Vampire. This heritage made him naturally stronger and more developed than an ordinary Human.[4]
- Enhanced Vampire Ancestry: Inquisition experiments increased the Vampiric portion of Zandriel's genetic structure from approximately one-quarter to one-half.[4]
- Superhuman Strength: Zandriel possessed physical power considerably exceeding normal Humans. He was capable of directly fighting Kaguya and inflicting severe injuries upon an experienced Noble Vampire assassin.[2]
- Superhuman Speed: Zandriel moved quickly enough to attack Kaguya before she could completely respond, forcing her to use Shadow abilities merely to limit the damage.[2]
- Superhuman Durability: Zandriel survived numerous injuries which would have been lethal to ordinary Humans, including extensive wounds inflicted throughout his battle with Kaguya.[2]
- Regeneration: Zandriel possessed extremely powerful Vampire-derived regeneration. After Kaguya sliced open his throat, the wound rapidly closed within moments. Even repeated injuries across his body continually healed during their battle.[2]
- Regeneration from Normally Fatal Injuries: A completely severed throat was insufficient to kill Zandriel, and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later observed the same tissue restoring itself after tearing his throat open a second time.[2][3]
- Enhanced Longevity: Zandriel's Vampire heritage granted him greater natural longevity than an ordinary Human. Ruby estimated that his unmodified quarter-Vampire body could theoretically have lived approximately 200-300 years.[4]
- Blood-Red Eyes: Zandriel's eyes could take on the characteristic blood-red coloration associated with Vampires when his altered nature became prominent.[3]

### Weaknesses
- Limited Lifespan: Despite possessing considerable Vampire ancestry, Zandriel remained mortal and lacked the effectively indefinite lifespan possessed by true Vampires.[4]
- No Continuous Vampiric Growth: Unlike true Vampires, Zandriel could not simply continue becoming stronger as centuries passed. His Hybrid physiology placed a natural limit upon his development.[4]
- Faith/Vampire Incompatibility: Although he retained access to Hunter magic, using it excessively harmed the Vampire side of his body. This prevented him from developing without consequence through the same methods available to ordinary Human [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]].[4]
- Lack of Active Vampire Powers: Despite possessing approximately half-Vampire genetics following experimentation, Zandriel was unable to access genuine active Vampire racial powers. The experiments intended to unlock those abilities ultimately failed.[4]
- Brain and Heart Destruction: His regeneration did not make him immortal. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] permanently killed Zandriel by piercing both his heart and brain simultaneously with an ice sword.[5]
- Religious Fanaticism: Zandriel's obsession with killing beings he considered sinners frequently interfered with the actual mission. He became so absorbed in fighting Kaguya that Julian repeatedly had to order him to stop playing around and complete the objective.[2]

### Trivia
- Zandriel was officially documented by the Inquisition as Patient Zero, the first successful Hunter used as the foundation of its Human-Vampire Hybrid experiments.[4]
- He was not originally a completely ordinary Human. Approximately 25% of his genetic ancestry was already Vampiric before experimentation.[4]
- Ruby theorized that Zandriel's Vampire ancestry came from a Noble Vampire grandparent.[4]
- The Inquisition's experiments increased his Vampiric ancestry from roughly 25% to 50%.[4]
- Ruby regarded Zandriel as a genetically "perfect Hybrid" because, unlike Bruno and Carlos, his DNA was complete and did not contain major missing sections.[4]
- Despite that genetic stability, Ruby still considered the overall experiment a failure because Zandriel inherited limitations from both Humans and Vampires without gaining the complete development potential of either race.[4]
- Had he never undergone further experimentation, Ruby estimated that Zandriel's natural quarter-Vampire physiology could have allowed him to live approximately 200-300 years.[4]
- Zandriel and [[Father Julian]] were longtime companions and were sufficiently well known as a pair that Maria recognized Julian partly because she remembered rumors concerning Zandriel.[2]
- Maria described the rumors surrounding Zandriel as depicting a man who, despite possessing an angelic name, was "as crazy as a demon".[2]
- After Kaguya slit his throat, Zandriel interpreted his regeneration as evidence that God was refusing to let him enter Heaven because he still had sinners left to kill.[2]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] preserved Zandriel's corpse after killing him specifically because he believed the body could provide more information than Zandriel himself.[5]
- That decision ultimately proved correct: Ruby's later examination of Zandriel's remains and Inquisition records exposed his identity as Patient Zero and clarified major details regarding the Church's Hybrid experiments.[4]
