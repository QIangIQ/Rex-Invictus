#### Debut
Chapter 1134
You will regret killing me, Monster. I will die, but my brothers will avenge me.
—Solus 969 to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Chapter 1134
Solus 969, commonly referred to simply as Number 969, was an elite warrior of the Empire of Light and a member of the specialized group known as the Solus. The Solus represent one of the highest known combat castes beneath the Emperor of Light, organized through numerical ranks extending from 1000 to 1; lower numbers denote increasingly important and powerful members, while single-digit Solus stand closest to the Emperor himself.[1]
The normal requirement for entering the Solus is extraordinarily high even by Upper-Sector standards. A candidate must possess a Divinity comparable to a Primordial God—described as possessing the highest possible Divinity within their respective Concept—and must prove themselves through conquest performed for the Empire. Grandmasters are so rare and valuable that the Empire is willing to relax this normal path for one after their supposed "Evil" has been purified.[1] As a recognized Solus, Number 969 therefore belonged to a military institution positioned far above the already formidable rank-and-file forces of the Empire of Light, although the story never identifies him as a First Generation Primordial God and never reveals what Concept, if any, belonged specifically to him.
969 was deployed against an independent combat clone created by Victor Elderblood during the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s first serious reconnaissance of an Upper-Sector civilization. While ordinary Veillon soldiers were individually powerful enough to be treated as threats comparable to conventional [[Primordial Gods]], Victor and his family repeatedly observed that those troops lacked the experience, individuality and conceptual "depth" expected of naturally developed high-level Gods. Solus 969 immediately stood out as an exception. [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] specifically noticed that 969 possessed substantially greater depth than the surrounding soldiers, while Victor himself concluded that he appeared to possess an actual independent consciousness rather than functioning as another obedient extension of Light's [[Will Solace|Will]].[2]
Although Number 969 ultimately lost, the battle demonstrated that he was an extremely dangerous combatant. He could directly exchange attacks with Victor's combat clone, reproduce a rapid multi-hit technique similar in principle to Victor's own Grandmaster attack, injure the clone through its defenses and continue monitoring the wider battlefield while simultaneously fighting at extreme speed. Victor's wives noted that the clone possessed only a somewhat higher raw Power Level than the Empire's elite soldiers; the overwhelming difference came from Victor's Grandmaster martial skill, enormous experience, strategic manipulation, Soul abilities and access to numerous Divinities rather than a simple disparity in raw Energy.[2]
Solus 969 was eventually killed once Victor ceased prolonging the battle for intelligence. The clone absorbed 969 into his Legion and accessed his memories, immediately discovering the true reason for the Solus' calm acceptance of death: an enormous Veillon military structure positioned far outside the Solar System was already preparing to erase the entire system. The fortress subsequently fired and annihilated the Solar System together with Victor's expendable clone, turning 969's final warning that his "brothers" would avenge him into an accurate prediction of the immediate military retaliation awaiting Victor.[2]

### Physical Appearance
Solus 969 was a large humanoid male whose natural appearance remained almost entirely hidden beneath the advanced armor of the Empire of Light. When the Solus reinforcements arrived, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] immediately noticed that they were significantly larger than the ordinary soldiers present and that their equipment was visibly more advanced and technologically sophisticated.[2]
His helmet obscured most of his face, leaving details such as his hair color, skin color and natural eye color unknown. However, his eyes remained visible enough that Victor could observe them widen dramatically when the corrupted Empire Commander transformed from a Representative of Light into a Representative of Darkness.[2]
When 969 entered battle, his body became enveloped in an extraordinary concentration of Light. The Light surrounding him was described as stronger than that produced by all of the ordinary soldiers around him combined, immediately distinguishing the Solus from the Empire's conventional troops.[2]
The chapter never reveals whether 969 possessed a completely biological body beneath his armor, an Energy-based body like many of the Empire's lesser soldiers, or some more advanced hybrid existence. Consequently, descriptions treating him as a confirmed Human, artificial God, pure Energy Being or another specific species remain speculative.

### Personality
Solus 969 was disciplined, loyal, analytical and considerably more independently minded than the lower-ranked Empire soldiers surrounding him. When called to reinforce the Eleventh Frontline, he first requested a report from the Commander, assessed the situation and then accepted responsibility for eliminating [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] in the name of the Empire rather than immediately rushing into battle without understanding the threat.[2]
His behavior consistently demonstrated practical battlefield judgment. After directly clashing with Victor's first major attack, 969 recognized the increased danger when Victor incorporated Abyssal Miasma into the next strike. Instead of attempting to overpower the altered attack through pride, he dodged, removed allied soldiers from its trajectory and ordered the conventional troops to withdraw because they could no longer safely contribute to the battle.[2]
969 also displayed significant concern for the battlefield as a whole rather than becoming completely absorbed in his duel. Even while facing Victor directly, he monitored the Commander through his senses and recognized when Victor began corrupting the man's Soul. He later noticed one of Victor's Energy clones moving toward the system's star and correctly recognized that the clone's departure was strategically important, although Victor's primary body prevented him from intervening.[2]
Unlike the ordinary Light soldiers, whose personalities appeared heavily suppressed by the Empire's system, 969 showed genuine irritation, surprise and independent reasoning. Victor openly described the Commander and lower soldiers as puppets without meaningful consciousness before questioning whether 969 was different. [[Scathach]] reached a similar conclusion while observing the battle, stating that the ordinary soldiers lacked depth while Number 969 clearly did not.[2]
His worldview nevertheless remained deeply shaped by the Empire's ideology. When Victor inverted the Commander's Light-aligned existence through Darkness, Abyssal Miasma and Soul Manipulation, 969 reacted with genuine disbelief and insisted that only the Emperor should possess the ability to perform something of that nature. Victor mocked this reaction as evidence of the [[Veillon Empire]]'s arrogance and isolation, arguing that its citizens assumed powers demonstrated by Light must necessarily belong exclusively to him.[2]
969 was also extremely composed in defeat. Even after Victor finally overwhelmed him, he neither begged for mercy nor abandoned his allegiance. He warned Victor that his brothers would avenge him and maintained a tranquil smile as his body dissolved into Victor's Legion. Only after reading his memories did Victor understand that 969 knew an Empire fortress was already preparing an attack capable of erasing the complete Solar System.[2]

### History
Very little is known about Solus 969 before his encounter with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], but Chapter 1133 provides important context concerning the military organization to which he belonged. During Victor's earlier confrontation with Commander [[Vik-Ir-loah]], the Commander attempted to recruit the apparent [[Ares|God of War]] into the [[Veillon Empire]] and explained that exceptionally valuable warriors could eventually become Solus.[1]
The Solus were described as the Empire's true elite, ranked from 1000 to 1. The lower the number, the greater the individual's position within the hierarchy, with single-digit Solus serving as the strongest and closest to the [[Aurethys Vaelthion|Emperor of Light]]. Under normal circumstances, entry required a candidate to possess Primordial God-level Divinity—the highest Divinity possible within their own Concept—and to prove themselves through conquest for the Empire.[1]
This hierarchy places 969 among the lower-numbered half of the known Solus ranking structure, though still far from the single-digit elites closest to Light. His number should therefore not be interpreted as meaning he was the 969th ordinary soldier or a clone designation; it was his recognized position within an elite hierarchy containing only one thousand numbered members.[1]
The precise circumstances through which he earned the position are unknown. The story never reveals his original world, species, Concept, family, age, previous conquests or whether "Solus 969" was the only identity he possessed. His reference to other Solus as his "brothers" suggests a strong collective relationship within the order, but the novel does not establish that they were literal biological brothers or clones.

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Solus 969 entered the conflict after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s expendable reconnaissance clone had already transformed what began as an encounter with several Empire soldiers into a Solar System-wide military crisis. Victor had deliberately entered unknown Upper-Sector territory through an independent clone rather than risk exposing his real body, Soul connections, family or Eldritch nature to civilizations he knew almost nothing about. The clone contained Victor's modern combat knowledge and Grandmaster-level experience but was deliberately restricted in several other respects, allowing the real Victor and his wives to observe the Empire's military response from safety.[3][4]
The clone initially encountered the Eleventh Frontline of the Empire of Light under Commander [[Vik-Ir-loah]]. The soldiers possessed extraordinary baseline Power, advanced Divine technology and artificial countermeasures against abilities such as extreme speed, Spatial movement and Reality manipulation. Victor discovered that many of the ordinary soldiers lacked conventional races entirely, being formed from pure Energy with unnatural Souls and existing as incomplete Gods rather than normally developed Divine Beings. Despite these abnormalities, their base combat strength was enormous; more than twenty thousand such soldiers were present during the original encounter, with later reinforcements increasing the battlefield to approximately one hundred thousand troops.[4][1]
The Empire's greatest weakness was not raw Power but uniformity. Victor's family observed that the soldiers used similar techniques, possessed limited personalities and depended heavily upon technology and the Power associated with their Emperor. Against ordinary Gods, Vampires, [[Werewolves]], Demons or supernatural armies, the force would have been devastating; against a Grandmaster possessing Victor's range of experience and abilities, their predictability became exploitable.[1]
Commander Vik-Ir eventually contacted the Solus after recognizing that conventional forces could not reliably defeat Victor. Shortly afterward, Number 969 arrived at the head of more advanced reinforcements. Victor immediately recognized them as different: their bodies were larger, their equipment was more advanced and their presence represented a clear escalation beyond the soldiers he had previously fought.[2]
After receiving the Commander's report, 969 declared that the target would be eliminated in the name of the Empire. Victor responded by transforming into his Demon King of Tyranny form and using a greatsword variation of the Fulger martial principle commonly summarized as "One Punch, one million hits". Victor's version condensed an immense number of cuts into what appeared externally to be a single sword stroke.[2]
969 met the technique directly. Light stronger than that of every ordinary soldier around him combined enveloped his body, and he launched what appeared to observers as a single punch. In reality, 969 had performed the same general principle as Victor and struck the same point repeatedly at extreme speed. Their attacks collided and created an explosion powerful enough to illuminate the surrounding vacuum of space for several seconds.[2]
Victor immediately tested his adaptability by covering his weapon with the Miasma of the Abyss and performing another attack. 969 recognized that the properties had changed and refused to meet it in the same manner. He dodged while simultaneously removing nearby allies from danger and ordered the standard troops to withdraw because Victor was no longer an enemy they could handle safely.[2]
Victor prevented the retreat by deploying another clone behind Vik-Ir. The Empire's systems recognized the use of cloning and Umbrakinesis and attempted to activate countermeasures, but the anti-cloning system failed because it lacked sufficient information concerning Victor's methods. Victor subsequently invaded the Commander's Soul while the Empire's artificial intelligence attempted to destroy its own officer before classified information could be compromised.[2]
Using Soul Manipulation together with [[Erebus]]' Darkness and the Miasma of the Abyss, Victor inverted the Commander's nature from Light to Darkness. The transformation horrified 969, who demanded to know how an outsider could possess a Power he believed belonged exclusively to the Emperor. Victor dismissed the idea and accused the Empire's inhabitants of being so completely conditioned by Light's ideology that they could not imagine similar or greater abilities existing elsewhere.[2]
Victor then directly questioned 969's nature. He described the normal Commander and surrounding soldiers as puppets lacking real consciousness but suggested that Number 969 possessed something they did not: an actual conscience and individual identity. From the observation room, [[Scathach]] independently noticed the same distinction, describing the ordinary soldiers as lacking depth while identifying 969 as different enough to deserve further observation.[2]
Victor created ten additional Energy clones from strands of hair and distributed them across the battlefield. Although these secondary copies lacked the substance of the primary combat clone and could be destroyed by sufficiently strong attacks, they retained considerable offensive Power and exploded violently when defeated. With the corrupted Commander supporting him and the additional Victors attacking the surrounding forces, the numerical advantage of the Veillon army rapidly became far less useful.[2]
Number 969 continued fighting Victor directly. Their repeated collisions released waves of Power that destroyed combatants indiscriminately around them. 969 succeeded in injuring Victor, establishing that his attacks possessed enough strength to penetrate the clone's defenses, but those victories produced little lasting effect because Victor immediately regenerated his wounds and repaired his armor.[2]
The battle also demonstrated why raw Power alone could not explain Victor's dominance. [[Rose Adrasteia Elderblood]] noted that the combat clone possessed only a slightly higher Power Level than the soldiers fighting it. Victor's real advantages were his Grandmaster-level technique, understanding of warfare, ability to manipulate morale, enormous arsenal of Divinities, Soul abilities, adaptability and experience fighting alone against superior numbers. Scathach consequently described the clone as a combat monster rather than merely a stronger opponent.[2]
While 969 remained occupied, one of Victor's secondary clones moved toward the system's star. Solus noticed the movement but could not disengage because the primary Victor continued forcing him into combat. The secondary clone reached the Empire's installation surrounding the star and slaughtered its defenders in less than a second, discovering an enormous sphere composed of pure Positive Energy.[2]
The Artifact functioned as the central Power source supporting the Empire's local Solar System infrastructure. Through it, Light's influence permeated the soldiers and the surrounding territory, functioning in a manner conceptually similar to the network Victor's own Empire had begun constructing through [[World Trees]] and the [[Dragon Heart]]. Victor's family also realized that the Empire's soldiers could effectively function as extensions of their Emperor's [[Will Solace|Will]] because their bodies themselves were built from the same Light-associated Energy.[2]
Victor's clone copied the information available from the installation and transmitted it back to the original Victor before destroying the sphere. [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]] isolated the stolen information rather than connecting unknown Upper-Sector technology directly to the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s network. Once the Pure Positive Energy source was destroyed, the Empire's active systems throughout the Solar System shut down, the ordinary soldiers' Light visibly weakened and the attempt to destabilize the star failed.[2]
With the reconnaissance mission already successful, Victor stopped prolonging the battle. Number 969 was ultimately overwhelmed and reduced to a state in which Victor could absorb him into his Legion. Even while dying, 969 remained defiant and warned Victor that his brothers would avenge him. He smiled peacefully as his body dissolved.[2]
Victor immediately accessed the memories obtained from the fallen Solus and discovered the reason behind his confidence. Far outside the Solar System was an artificial Veillon structure larger than the Solar System itself. The construct resembled a gigantic mobile fortress equipped with numerous enormous cannons already aimed at Victor's location. The fortress opened fire and erased the entire Solar System, annihilating Victor's restricted clone together with everything remaining inside it.[2]
The clone regarded the weapon with excitement rather than fear, recognizing that surviving such concentrated firepower was beyond its deliberately limited capabilities and that only Victor's true body could realistically attempt such a feat. More importantly, survival had never been the objective. Through the confrontation with the Empire's troops, the corrupted Commander, the destroyed Light infrastructure and the memories of Solus 969, Victor obtained the intelligence he had created the clone to acquire.[2]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
The consequences of Solus 969's battle extended beyond his death. The destruction of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s clone and the complete Solar System demonstrated that Upper-Sector civilizations could treat stellar systems as disposable military resources and possessed strategic technology operating on a scale dramatically beyond the civilizations Victor had previously conquered. The encounter became one of the primary pieces of evidence used by Victor and his family when reassessing the scale of the wider cosmic powers awaiting them outside their developing Sector.[5]
Victor's faction subsequently connected the civilization encountered by the clone to the larger Upper-Sector power structure. System rankings and the information available to them indicated that entities designated <Light>, <Darkness>, <Emperor> and <Law> occupied positions among the dominant authorities beyond Victor's local Reality, with Light understood as the ruler associated with the civilization Victor had just investigated. Rather than dismiss the encounter as an isolated battle, Victor concluded that future conflict with these powers was effectively inevitable.[5]
The intelligence gained from the reconnaissance therefore helped trigger a fundamental change in Elderblood strategy. Victor stopped treating expansion as ordinary conquest and began preparing his entire Sector for eventual warfare against civilizations capable of universal-scale development, initiating complete Sector mapping, greater fortification, technological acceleration and the enormous time-acceleration project that would give the [[Elderblood Empire]] hundreds of thousands of years of internal development before the barriers separating it from more advanced powers disappeared.[5][6][7]
Although 969 himself did not survive into this period, the battle against him became one of the first concrete demonstrations of the level Victor's Empire would eventually need to surpass.

### Skills and Abilities
Solus 969 belonged to an organization whose normal admission requirements demanded Divinity at the level of a Primordial God within the candidate's respective Concept.[1] However, the novel never reveals 969's Concept, natural race or complete ability set. His confirmed powers are therefore restricted to those demonstrated during Chapter 1134.
- Solus Physiology / Status: Number 969 was a member of the Solus, the Empire of Light's elite military order. Solus are ranked from 1000 to 1, with lower numbers representing stronger members and single-digit members standing closest to Light.[1]
- Primordial God-Level Divinity: The normal qualification for becoming a Solus is possessing the highest possible level of Divinity within one's respective Concept, described by the Empire as being a "Primordial God in Divinity".[1] The chapter does not reveal 969's personal Concept or identify him as a naturally born First Generation Primordial God.
- Superior Depth: [[Scathach]] specifically distinguished 969 from ordinary Empire soldiers because he possessed a greater sense of depth and individuality than their mass-produced forces.[2]
- Light Manipulation: Solus 969 could surround and empower himself with an enormous concentration of Light. When he began fighting seriously, the Light covering his body was described as stronger than that emitted by all nearby ordinary soldiers combined.[2]
- Light Reinforcement: He concentrated this Energy around his body while physically engaging [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], allowing his attacks to clash against the clone's Grandmaster-level sword techniques.[2]
- Extreme Physical Strength: 969's punches possessed sufficient force to directly contest Victor's greatsword attacks and generate destructive Power waves through repeated clashes.[2]
- Victor-Clone Damage: He successfully injured Victor's combat clone during their fight, proving that his attacks could penetrate its defenses even though Victor's regeneration immediately repaired the damage.[2]
- Extreme Speed and Reflexes: 969 could fight Victor at speeds where ordinary soldiers were incapable of accurately perceiving the true number of attacks being exchanged.[2]
- Rapid Multi-Hit Strikes: His first displayed punch was actually an enormous number of repeated strikes delivered into the same location, appearing as a singular attack to weaker observers. This allowed him to directly answer Victor's "One Cut, one million cuts" technique.[2]
- Space Flight: Solus 969 freely maneuvered and fought within the vacuum of space throughout his confrontation with Victor without displaying any difficulty surviving or moving in that environment.[2]
- Enhanced Senses: 969 could monitor events outside his direct line of sight while simultaneously fighting Victor. He observed the corruption of the Commander through his senses despite focusing upon Victor's main body and later detected a secondary Victor clone moving toward the Solar System's star.[2]
- Combat Intelligence: 969 demonstrated the ability to evaluate attacks and immediately change tactics when their properties changed. After initially meeting Victor's multi-hit technique directly, he recognized that the addition of Abyssal Miasma made repeating the same defense dangerous and instead dodged the attack.[2]
- Threat Assessment: He recognized that the standard soldiers were incapable of safely continuing the fight and ordered them to withdraw.[2]
- Battlefield Awareness: 969 continuously monitored the Commander, Victor's additional clones, allied troops and changes to the Solar System while personally fighting Victor.[2]
- Protection of Allies: When avoiding Victor's Miasma-infused attack, 969 simultaneously removed allied soldiers from its path rather than simply protecting himself.[2]
- Advanced Empire Equipment: Number 969 wore equipment visibly more technologically advanced than that of normal Veillon soldiers. His armor formed part of the Empire's larger fusion of advanced technology and Divinity.[2] The chapter does not individually explain every function provided by 969's suit, so specific technologies should not be attributed to it without further evidence.
- Independent Consciousness: Unlike many ordinary Empire soldiers and the corrupted Commander, Solus 969 appeared to possess a genuine individual consciousness. Victor explicitly questioned whether 969 was different from the "puppets" surrounding him, while Scathach independently observed that he possessed greater depth.[2]
- Resistance and Endurance: 969 remained capable of prolonged direct combat against Victor's clone despite repeated high-level clashes and massive collateral destruction across the battlefield.[2] The novel does not show 969 regenerating injuries, so Regeneration should not be listed as one of his confirmed abilities.

### Equipment
- Solus Combat Armor: An advanced suit of Veillon technology visually distinct from standard Imperial equipment. The armor completely covered most of 969's body and accompanied him throughout his space battle with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[2]
- Empire Network Access: As a Solus deployed through an Imperial reinforcement request, 969 operated as part of the wider Veillon military network and coordinated with its Commander and soldiers. However, the exact degree to which his personal armor interfaced with the Empire's artificial intelligence is not separately explained.

### Relationships

#### Light
Light was Solus 969's Emperor and the supreme authority of the civilization he served. 969's reaction to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] demonstrated that his understanding of supernatural Power had been deeply shaped by the belief that Light occupied a uniquely elevated position. When Victor inverted the Commander's Light into Darkness, 969 was genuinely shocked because he believed only his Emperor should possess such an ability.[2]
The Solus hierarchy itself is structured around proximity to Light, with the lowest numerical ranks representing the strongest individuals and the single-digit Solus serving closest to the Emperor.[1]

#### Victor
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was the only enemy Solus 969 personally fought on-page.
969 initially treated Victor as an Upper-Sector military target that needed to be eliminated for the Empire. Their first direct clash demonstrated enough similarity in combat execution that 969 could answer Victor's extreme-speed multi-hit sword technique with his own repeated punches.[2]
As the fight continued, 969 became increasingly aware that Victor's danger did not come solely from raw Power. Victor used clones, Miasma, Darkness, Soul Manipulation, [[Martial Arts]], psychological pressure and deliberate battlefield manipulation simultaneously, eventually overwhelming the entire Imperial response despite fighting alone.[2]
Victor was sufficiently interested in 969's individuality to directly distinguish him from the Empire's conventional soldiers and continued fighting him while other clones dismantled the Solar System's supporting infrastructure.[2]
Once the Light Artifact was destroyed, Victor stopped gathering information and killed 969. He absorbed the Solus into his Legion and read his memories, discovering the Empire's enormous Solar System-erasing fortress.[2]

#### Other Solus
969 referred to the remaining Solus as his brothers shortly before his death, confidently telling [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] that they would avenge him.[2]
The story has not clarified whether "brothers" indicates literal biological kinship, common artificial origin, ideological brotherhood or simply the relationship shared between members of the Solus order. It should therefore not be treated as proof that the numbered Solus are clones.
