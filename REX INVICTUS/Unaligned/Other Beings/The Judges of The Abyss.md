The Judges of The Abyss, also known as the Three Judges of the Abyss, are one of the [[Regulators of Existence]] and the Primordial Entity representing Soul. Although they normally manifest as three separate figures—The Hand, The Eye and The Mouth—the three are fundamentally manifestations of a single existence.[1][2]
Within The System, their Concept is registered as <Soul>, possessing Administrator-level authority at Rank 5.[3][4]
Despite their name, judging the dead is only one portion of their function. The Judges are responsible for the creation and administration of Souls, the judgment and classification of deceased beings, the authorization of Rulers who govern recognized Underworlds, and the maintenance of the Soul-related portion of the universal afterlife system.[1][5][6]
Their work is directly connected to The Universal Tree. The Judges themselves explained the division of responsibility between Soul and Life: Life creates the form into which existence is placed, while Soul supplies that form with a Soul. Because of this, the Judges and Universal Tree cooperate in maintaining the cycle of Life, Death and Reincarnation throughout Creation.[5]

### Physical Appearance
The Judges normally appear as three colossal, incomprehensible entities representing different aspects of their singular existence.
They are associated with a gigantic golden eye, multiple enormous golden hands and a mouth within which an entire galaxy-like expanse can be seen.[7]
When [[Persephone]] encountered them directly, the three individual manifestations were identified simply according to their most prominent aspects:
- The Eye
- The Hand
- The Mouth[1][5]
The three manifestations speak individually but frequently complete one another's sentences or speak simultaneously, reinforcing that they are not three unrelated Primordials but three expressions of the same Being.[1][3]
Their colossal appearance should not necessarily be interpreted as a fixed biological body. Like the other Primordials, their true existence is conceptual rather than conventional, with their manifested forms serving as expressions of the Aspect they administer.[2][4]

### Personality
The Judges are generally calm, rational, detached and extremely focused on their responsibilities.
They have existed for an immeasurable period and normally show little concern for the personal desires of individuals. Their primary concern is whether the system of Souls, afterlives and cosmic regulation continues functioning properly.
They are also highly pragmatic.
The Judges are willing to bargain with beings they would otherwise have little reason to associate with if the exchange benefits the Soul system. One example was their agreement with [[Diablo]], in which billions of Souls were involved in exchange for temporary concessions surrounding Hell.[8][6]
They similarly accepted [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s offer of 1,000 supernatural Souls in exchange for the separated positive and negative remnants of [[Vanessa Alekerth]]'s Soul.[3]
Despite their apparent emotional detachment, the Judges are not completely without personal biases.
Their encounters with Azathoth left an extraordinary psychological impression upon them. During Victor's confrontation with the Primordials, he accused the Judges of repeatedly interfering with exceptionally powerful beings not purely because of duty, but because those beings reminded them of the danger represented by Azathoth.[2]
The Judges' reaction demonstrated that Azathoth had genuinely left a lasting fear upon them.[2]
This fear eventually caused them to misjudge Victor.
When Victor's nature developed beyond their expectations, the Judges initially classified him as an unacceptable danger. After examining him more carefully and being confronted by the Universal Tree, however, they recognized that Victor's manifestation of Negativity was not itself an abnormal corruption threatening the Balance and withdrew their interference.[2]
They did not apologize, but they accepted the Universal Tree's decision.

### Origin
The exact circumstances of the Judges' individual birth have not been shown directly.
Early information often associated the Primordials generally with [[Primordial Chaos]], but later explanations refined this origin.
According to Balance, the Primordials now known as Positivity and Negativity originated from what should originally have been a single Being. Their separation gave rise to the current structure of Creation, and the other Primordials—including Soul—were subsequently born from that division.[9]
The Judges are therefore part of the generation of foundational cosmic entities that arose after the primordial division that established the present Universe.
Their existence eventually became inseparable from the function of Souls themselves.
By Chapter 1075, it is explicitly explained that removing the Primordial <Soul> would mean that Souls as a universal Aspect would cease to exist.[4]

### Nature

#### One Being, Three Manifestations
Although called the Three Judges, the Judges of the Abyss are fundamentally one Primordial Being possessing three manifestations.[1][2]
The three bodies frequently communicate sequentially:
The first begins the statement, the second continues it, and the third completes it.
They can also speak simultaneously.
This unusual existence is reflected in their servants, the [[Abyss Watchers]], who also appear as a group of three and refer to the Judges collectively as their Master.[8]

#### Primordial of Soul
The Judges do not merely possess a Divine Concept related to Souls.
They are the Primordial Aspect of Soul itself.
As one of the Regulators, their existence is integrated into the Universe's functioning. The Concept they represent is therefore not equivalent to an ordinary God possessing a Soul-related Divinity.[2][4]
Azathoth later explained the consequence directly:
If <Soul> were somehow removed from the Universe, Souls would no longer exist.[4]
This establishes the Judges as one of the fundamental Pillars upon which Creation operates.

One of the Judges' most important responsibilities is the creation of Souls.
While testing [[Persephone]], they explicitly stated:
"We are the Entity that judges Souls, but one of our responsibilities is to create Souls."[5]
They further explained their relationship with the Universal Tree:
Life creates a form, and Soul supports that form with a Soul.[5]
This establishes the fundamental cooperation between the two Primordials.

#### Judgment of Souls
The Judges are the supreme universal authorities responsible for the judgment of Souls.
When a being dies and reaches the appropriate stage of the afterlife cycle, its Soul is processed according to its nature, karma, power and circumstances.[10][1]
For ordinary mortal Souls, this process does not always resemble a conventional courtroom judgment.
The Judges' function frequently involves separating the positive and negative portions of the Soul and sending them to their corresponding destinations.[10][1]

#### Karma Evaluation
The Judges evaluate the actions and karma accumulated by a Soul.
Positive and negative actions influence the treatment and destination of the Soul after death.[10][1]
This does not necessarily mean every deceased person receives the same individualized trial witnessed during [[Persephone]]'s test.
Ordinary Souls can be processed through the greater afterlife system, whereas powerful supernatural beings and Gods receive more direct or consequential judgment.[1]

#### Judgment of Gods and Supernatural Beings
The Judges' authority is not limited to Humans.
Powerful supernatural beings and Gods can also be judged after death.[1][11]
A sufficiently powerful Soul may receive a much more decisive judgment than an ordinary mortal. Depending on the circumstances, it may be:
- Sent to an appropriate afterlife.
- Returned to the Cycle of Reincarnation.
- Stripped of its previous identity.
- Recycled into a new existence.
- Or prevented from continuing in its previous state.[1]
After becoming the Ruler of the [[Greek Underworld]], [[Persephone]] explicitly stated that her authority included the judgment of the Souls of Gods.[11]

#### Soul Separation
The Judges are capable of separating different aspects of a Soul.
The classic example is the division between the Soul's positive and negative portions during the afterlife process.[10][1]
Chapter 1021 provides an even more direct example.
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] sought to restore [[Vanessa Alekerth]], the Judges possessed her remaining positive and negative Soul fragments as separate entities and traded both portions to Victor.[3]
Victor subsequently reunited the fragments.

#### Soul Recycling
The afterlife does not exist solely to reward or punish the dead.
Both Heaven and Hell also function as parts of the universal Soul Recycling system.[1]
A positive Soul may remain within an appropriate Heaven until it has obtained sufficient satisfaction or completed the experience associated with that afterlife, after which it eventually returns to the reincarnation cycle.[1]
Negative Souls are processed through Hell, where punishment and purification eventually allow recyclable portions of the Soul to return to the greater Cycle.[1]
The exact mechanics differ between Pantheons.
For example, Norse afterlife systems do not operate identically to Biblical or Greek ones, but they remain part of the same broader function.[1]

#### Relationship with Demons
The Judges' Soul-processing system is directly connected to the origin of certain Demons.
When a Soul contains extreme levels of corruption or sin, its positive portion can be separated and preserved while the remaining mass of concentrated negativity becomes an empty shell of evil.[7]
This process is associated with the formation of traditional Biblical Demons.
The resulting Demon is therefore not necessarily the complete original mortal Soul, but the negative remainder left after the redeemable portion was extracted.

### Administration of Heaven and Hell
The Judges are among the entities responsible for the universal infrastructure connecting Souls to Heavens, Hells and Underworlds.[6]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] described them as the entities responsible for the administration of:
- Hell
- Paradise / Heaven
- Souls[6]
However, the Judges do not personally micromanage every [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] in every Pantheon.
Instead, much of the local administration is delegated to Rulers, Hell Kings and other authorized beings.

#### Rulers
A Ruler is an individual granted authority to administer an [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] and participate in the judgment and processing of Souls.
This authority originates from the greater Soul system governed by the Judges.[1][11]
Rulers possess enormous authority within their Underworlds. Their position can be compared to that of a God-King governing a Pantheon, although their responsibilities are specifically connected to the dead and the afterlife.[11][6]
A Ruler is expected to remain impartial.
Because they possess the authority to judge Souls—including the Souls of exceptionally powerful beings—the Judges do not simply grant the position based on raw strength.[1][5]

#### Ruler's Trial
The Judges directly tested [[Persephone]] before allowing her to become the true Ruler of the [[Greek Underworld]].[1]
The Trial took place within the [[Three Judges Realm]].
The first portion tested her ability to evaluate morally complicated Souls and determine whether they belonged in Heaven or Hell.
The second portion was significantly harsher.
Persephone was forced to make decisions involving the Souls of people personally important to her, demonstrating whether she could remain impartial even when her own emotions were involved.[5]
The Judges emphasized:
Favoritism is forbidden; impartiality is necessary.[5]
The Souls involved were real creations of the Judges rather than simple illusions.[5]
After Persephone completed the test, she received the memories, information and authority necessary to operate as a genuine Ruler.[11]
Her authority became so integrated with the Greek Underworld that the dimension itself supported her position.[11]

### Three Judges Realm
The Judges possess their own Domain commonly referred to as the [[Three Judges Realm]].
[[Persephone]] was transported there after activating the authority contained within [[Hades]]' Helm.[1]
She appeared upon a stone platform surrounded by a cosmic landscape composed of innumerable galaxies.
The Realm is used as a location where the Judges evaluate candidates attempting to receive the authority necessary to become Rulers.[1][5]
As the Primordial Domain of <Soul>, it exists at a level fundamentally different from an ordinary Pantheon dimension.

### Abyss Watchers
The [[Abyss Watchers]] are direct servants of the Judges.
Like their masters, the known Watchers appear as a group of three.[8]
They act on the Judges' behalf when direct Primordial intervention is unnecessary.
During the negotiations surrounding [[Diablo]], the Abyss Watchers appeared to enforce and finalize the agreement made between Diablo and their Master.[8]
This allowed the Judges to influence events while remaining removed from ordinary political affairs.

### Relationship with the Universal Tree
The Judges possess one of the closest functional relationships among the Regulators with [[The Universal Tree]].
The reason is straightforward:
Soul and Life are interdependent.
The Universal Tree controls Life and the creation/maintenance of form.
The Judges create and administer the Soul placed into that form.[5]
After death, the Soul is processed through the afterlife system and eventually returned to the cycle governed by Life and Reincarnation.
The two therefore maintain different sections of the same universal cycle.

### Relationship with True Death
The Judges govern the Soul after Death, but they are not the Primordial authority of Death itself.
That position belongs to [[True Death]].[13][4]
This distinction separates two important stages:
- Death ends a living existence.
- Soul governs what happens to the resulting Soul afterward.
The two Primordials therefore possess closely related but fundamentally separate domains.
When the abnormal Norse dimension began producing the Soul of a new Primordial God using a chaotic mixture of Death, Nature, Positive, Negative and other energies, the Judges required cooperation from both the Universal Tree and True Death to properly correct the problem.[14][12]

### Relationship with the Owner of Limbo
The Judges frequently cooperate with the Owner of Limbo, whose Concept is Balance.
Both are among the more active Regulators.[13][2]
Their priorities can nevertheless conflict.
During [[Diablo]]'s plans, the Owner of Limbo wanted to intervene against Diablo's transformation, but the Judges opposed immediate interference because their agreement and actions were considered beneficial to the Soul system.[15]
This demonstrates that the Regulators are not a perfectly unified collective. Each prioritizes the Aspect of Creation placed under their own jurisdiction.

### Relationship with Victor Elderblood
The relationship between the Judges and [[Victor Elderblood]] changed considerably over time.

#### Observation
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s conflict with the [[Greek Pantheon]] reached a level capable of affecting the larger cosmic structure, the Judges appeared alongside the Universal Tree and Owner of Limbo to observe the aftermath.[13]
Their primary concern was maintaining the Balance.

#### Victor's Ascension
During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ascension into true Godhood, the Judges initially believed his existence represented an even greater Balance violation than [[Diablo]].[16]
After examining him more closely, they discovered the opposite.
Victor's existence simultaneously generated [[Chaos]] and Order while forcibly maintaining Balance around itself.
The Judges were genuinely confused by this contradiction.[16]

#### The Norse Anomaly
In Chapter 946, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] discovered a corrupted dimension in the Norse cosmological structure where various energies had accumulated and begun producing the Soul of a new Primordial God.[14]
Victor reported the anomaly to the Judges.
The Judges recognized the danger immediately and began correcting the situation, eventually bringing in the Universal Tree because World Tree-related aspects were outside their sole jurisdiction.[14][12]

#### Bargain for Vanessa's Soul
By Chapter 1021, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was sufficiently familiar with the System's higher functions to directly call the Judges using the language of The System.[3]
The System responded:
Administrator <Soul> will encounter <[[Chaos]]>.[3]
Victor offered 1,000 Souls belonging to supernatural beings in exchange for the positive and negative remnants of [[Vanessa Alekerth]]'s Soul.
The Judges accepted.[3]
They believed resurrecting Vanessa as the same individual was impossible because her Soul had already progressed through Life and Death and its former information had largely been erased.
Victor nevertheless took the two fragments and reconstructed her using his own methods.[3]
The event demonstrates that the Judges are capable of:
- Retaining separated Soul fragments.
- Evaluating the informational condition of Souls.
- Transferring ownership/control of Souls.
- Conducting direct exchanges involving Souls.

#### Conflict over Victor
Their most important confrontation occurred in Chapter 1074.
The Judges became increasingly disturbed by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s abnormal nature and considered him an extreme threat.[2]
Victor responded by using the information recorded throughout Creation to confront them with their history of eliminating particularly exceptional beings.
He accused the Judges of acting not merely from responsibility but from fear.
Specifically, he claimed that their past encounter with Azathoth had traumatized them so severely that every sufficiently extraordinary being reminded them of the Outer God.[2]
The accusation visibly affected them.
Victor's danger level in their perception subsequently reached the highest possible point as his presence reminded them directly of Azathoth.[2]
The conflict was prevented from escalating when the Universal Tree intervened.
[[The Universal Tree]] declared Victor's family to be under his own Primordial Domain and told the Judges not to interfere with them.[2]
Because a direct conflict between Soul and Life would cause the Universe itself to suffer, the Judges accepted the Universal Tree's declaration and withdrew.[2]
After examining Victor more carefully, they also realized that the darkness surrounding his Negativity did not represent the abnormal corruption they had initially suspected.

### Relationship with Azathoth
Azathoth is one of the few known entities to have caused genuine fear in the Judges.
At some point in the distant past, the Primordials were forced to cooperate against the Outer God.[2]
The event left a lasting psychological effect upon the Judges.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later exploited this fear by forcing them to confront the similarity between their reaction to him and their memories of Azathoth.[2]
The Judges themselves were unable to dismiss the comparison.
This is particularly significant because Primordials normally exist as detached cosmic entities that ordinary beings cannot meaningfully threaten.

### Powers and Abilities
- Transcendent Physiology: The Judges of The Abyss are one of the [[Regulators of Existence]], primordial entities whose existence transcends ordinary Gods and conventional supernatural beings. Their System-registered Concept is <Soul>, making them the primordial authority responsible for the administration, creation and judgment of Souls throughout Creation.[17][18][19]
- Abstract Existence / Conceptual Embodiment: Like the other Regulators, the Judges are an abstract cosmic function given consciousness and form. Their existence is fundamentally linked to the Concept of Soul rather than being limited to an ordinary physical body.[18][19]
- Conceptual [[Immortality]]: Primordials cannot be killed in the same manner as ordinary Gods because their functions are part of Creation itself. Even [[True Death]] cannot simply kill them while their fundamental cosmic function continues to exist.[18][19]
- Transcendent Immortality: The Judges possess effectively limitless natural lifespan and are not subject to ordinary aging or conventional mortality.[19]
- Multiple Bodies / Distributed Consciousness: The Judges are one entity existing through three bodies, commonly represented as the Eyes, Hands and Mouth. All three speak and act as different expressions of the same Primordial Being.[17][20]
- Collective Consciousness: Although the three bodies can speak independently, they function as one consciousness and reach judgments collectively.[17][20]
- Non-Physical Interaction: As the Primordial of Souls, the Judges can directly interact with Souls, Divine Spirits and other non-physical existences without requiring a physical vessel.[17][20]
- [[Primordial Energy]] Manipulation: As Regulators created from the original cosmic order, the Judges possess and can manipulate Primordial-level Energy.[18][19]
- Soul Energy Manipulation: Their entire Authority centers around Souls, allowing them to manipulate spiritual Energy and Soul structures at a level far exceeding normal Gods or Progenitors.[17][20]
- Soul Manipulation: The Judges possess the highest known native Authority over Souls within the Universe. They create Souls, divide them, judge them, direct them through the afterlife and administer the systems responsible for processing them.[17][20][21]
- Soul Creation: Creating Souls is explicitly stated to be one of their responsibilities. The Judges explain that Life creates the form while they provide the Soul, meaning living forms normally enter Creation through cooperation between the Life and Soul systems.[20]
- Soul Generation: The Judges can generate new Souls directly rather than merely manipulating pre-existing ones.[20]
- Mass Soul Creation: Their responsibility applies to the greater cosmic cycle rather than isolated individuals, allowing them to provide Souls for enormous numbers of living beings across Creation.[20]
- Soul Replication / Soul Mimicry: During [[Persephone]]'s test, the Judges created real Souls modeled after people personally important to her, including Adonis, [[Demeter]], [[Macaria]] and [[Melinoë]]. These were explicitly stated to be genuine Souls rather than simple illusions.[20]
- Soul Division: The Judges can separate a Soul into distinct components, most notably dividing its positive/good aspects from its negative/evil aspects.[22][17]
- Soul Separation: They can physically/metaphysically remove one portion of a Soul while preserving the remaining component for a different destination.[22][17]
- Evil Extraction / Spiritual Purification: When processing ordinary Souls, the Judges can extract the "evil" portion and send it to Hell while the remaining positive portion proceeds toward Paradise or eventual reincarnation.[22][17]
- Positive/Negative Soul Separation: The Judges can distinguish and separate opposing spiritual aspects of a Soul. Chapter 1021 shows them possessing and transferring both the white and dark portions remaining from [[Vanessa Alekerth]]'s previous Soul.[23]
- Soul Fragment Preservation: The Judges can preserve separated portions of Souls after death and maintain them within the afterlife system until they are processed, traded or otherwise used.[23]
- Soul Fragment Transfer: They can transfer preserved Soul components to another being. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] received Vanessa's remaining positive and negative Soul aspects directly from them in exchange for one thousand supernatural Souls.[23]
- Soul Recycling Administration: The Judges oversee the processing through which dead Souls are stripped of portions of their former identity and prepared to re-enter the greater reincarnation cycle.[17][21]
- Soul Information Erasure / Identity Erasure: Souls passing through the death-and-recycling system lose information from their previous existence. Supernatural Souls are particularly prioritized for removal of previous-life identity before being returned to the reincarnation cycle.[17][23]
Note: The final act of reincarnation belongs principally to The Universal Tree. The Judges process, judge and prepare Souls; they should not be given sole credit for universal reincarnation itself.[20][21]
- Soul Judgment: The Judges determine the fate of deceased Souls based upon the circumstances of their lives, actions and spiritual condition. Their judgment is not based on simplistic mortal morality but on contextual evaluation of the individual's actions and motives.[17]
- Karma Perception / Karma Assessment: They can perceive and calculate the positive and negative karmic value accumulated by a Soul and use that information when determining its destination after death.[22][17]
- Sin Detection: The Judges can determine whether actions constitute genuine Sin within the cosmological soul system rather than merely following cultural or mortal definitions of morality.[17]
- Intent Perception: Judgment considers the motive and circumstances surrounding an action. During Persephone's trial, the Judges distinguish murder committed through cruelty from killing in self-defense or survival.[17]
- Contextual Judgment: They can evaluate surrounding circumstances that ordinary observers may overlook. Their tests explicitly punish simplistic judgment made without requesting sufficient context.[17]
- Moral Alignment Perception: The Judges can determine the relative good and evil contained within a Soul and separate those elements accordingly.[22][17]
- Afterlife Manipulation: The Judges administer the movement of Souls between death, Paradise, Hell, purification and the reincarnation system.[17][21]
- Soul Routing: They can determine where a deceased Soul is sent after judgment.[17]
- Paradise Banishment / Heaven Transportation: Souls judged positively can be sent to the Paradise or Heaven corresponding to their spiritual/religious system.[22][17]
- Hell Banishment / [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] Transportation: Souls judged negatively can be sent directly into Hell or another appropriate Underworld.[22][17]
- Selective Soul Routing: Ordinary Souls may be divided into positive and negative portions, while exceptionally powerful supernatural Souls can be sent whole to Heaven or Hell depending upon the judgment.[17]
- Soul Condemnation: The Judges possess authority to condemn Souls to punishment within the Hells.[22][17]
- Spiritual Salvation / Rehabilitation Administration: Souls that undergo punishment and purification can later be returned toward the reincarnation process rather than remaining eternally trapped.[17][21]
- Soul Destruction / Spiritual Erasure — Judgment Dependent: For sufficiently powerful beings such as Gods, the Judges' verdict can result in the Soul ceasing to continue as the same existence rather than receiving another reincarnation opportunity.[17] (Note: This should not automatically be generalized into unrestricted Existence Erasure usable freely in combat; the demonstrated context is Primordial judgment of the dead.)
- Demon Creation (Indirect): The Judges' Soul-processing system contributes directly to the creation of Demons. When the positive aspects of sufficiently corrupted Souls are removed and recycled, the remaining accumulation of negative/evil spiritual matter can become a Demon.[22]
- Spiritual Corruption Separation: They isolate the corrupted portion of the Soul from the redeemable portion, leaving the former to be processed through Hell.[22]
Note: The Judges do not necessarily "design" individual Demons. Demons arise as a consequence of the Soul-separation and Hell-recycling system they administer.
- Dimensional Creation / Realm Creation: The Judges created the [[Three Judges Realm]], an enormous personal domain used for Soul administration and the testing of candidates for Ruler authority.[17]
- Cosmic Realm Creation: The Three Judges Realm contains countless galaxies and exists as a special administrative space separate from ordinary planetary afterlives.[17]
- Domain Manipulation: Within their own Realm, the Judges possess overwhelmingly superior control over the environment, Souls and events occurring inside it.[17][20]
- Authority-Limited Omnipotence / Absolute Domain Authority: Within matters concerning Souls and their own domain, the Judges are treated as effectively absolute administrators capable of creating Souls, determining death outcomes and controlling the trials imposed upon Ruler candidates.[17][20]
- Domain Omniscience / Soul Omniscience: Their awareness extends throughout the Soul-management system and their own territory, allowing them to know the histories and contexts necessary to judge Souls accurately.[17]
Note: Their omnipotence and omniscience should be qualified as domain/concept-limited, not unrestricted omnipotence over every aspect of the entire cosmology.
- Reality Warping — Domain-Limited: Within the Three Judges Realm, the Judges can construct highly realistic judgment scenarios and cause consequences to manifest according to the choices made during the trial.[20]
- Scenario Creation: They can construct complex situations designed to test impartiality, wisdom and judgment.[17][20]
- Real-Soul Scenario Creation: The Souls used within the tests are genuine rather than illusory, making the trials metaphysically real even when based on recreated versions of people known to the candidate.[20]
- Death Inducement — Domain-Limited: During the second phase of Persephone's test, the Judges declared that one Soul would die depending upon her choice and that both would die if she refused to choose; the chosen consequences immediately manifested within the test.[20]
- Paralysis Inducement / Movement Restriction: Persephone attempted to intervene physically during one of the tests but was unable to move, forcing her to witness the consequence of her judgment.[20]

#### Resistances
- Resistance to Soul Manipulation: As the Primordial embodiment/administrator of Souls, ordinary Soul-based powers are extremely unlikely to function against the Judges normally.[17][19]
- Resistance to Conceptual Manipulation: The Judges are themselves an abstract Conceptual existence rather than ordinary users of a Divinity.[19]
- Resistance to Existence Erasure: Destroying a manifested Primordial is not equivalent to erasing the universal function it embodies. Their continued cosmic role makes permanent elimination vastly more difficult than killing a God.[18][19]
- Resistance to Death Manipulation: Even [[True Death]] cannot simply kill a Regulator while its Conceptual function remains part of Creation.[18]
- Resistance to Time Manipulation: As entities capable of operating within stopped Primordial territory and themselves interfering with Time, lesser temporal powers should not affect them normally.[29][18]
- Resistance to Spatial/Dimensional Manipulation: Their existence and personal Realm operate on an administrative level beyond ordinary dimensional spaces.[17][18]

### Limitations
Despite being the Primordial of Soul, the Judges are not literally unrestricted.
Their specialized authority concerns Soul, while other Primordials possess equally fundamental jurisdiction over their own Aspects.
For example:
- [[The Universal Tree]] possesses primary authority over Life and reincarnation.
- [[True Death]] possesses primary authority over Death.
- The Owner of Limbo possesses primary authority over Balance.
- Infinity possesses primary authority over Infinity and Space.
This means complex events frequently require cooperation between multiple Primordials.
The Norse anomaly is a direct example. The Judges could address the Soul-related component, but they explicitly stated that they could not solve the entire problem alone because the [[World Trees]] belonged to Life's Domain.[12]
The Judges' authority also should not be interpreted as proof that every Soul that can possibly exist was personally created by them.
Certain abnormal or primordial existences can develop outside the normal processes they administer. The Norse anomaly itself involved a primordial Soul beginning to form under highly unusual conditions.[14]
Likewise, the Universal Tree possesses sufficient authority within Life and reincarnation to perform extremely deep Soul modifications when those functions overlap with his own Domain.
Primordial jurisdiction is therefore specialized but can overlap where fundamental systems intersect.
