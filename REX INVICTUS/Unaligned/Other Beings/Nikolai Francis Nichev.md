### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 1158
Nikolai Francis Nichev was an Imperial [[Demigod]] and a distant descendant of the Celtic God [[Dagda]]. Born to the Imperial citizens [[Juliette Dubois]] and [[Ivankov Nichev]], Nikolai secretly betrayed [[Victor Elderblood]] and the [[Elderblood Empire]] after becoming involved with Gods originating from the Upper Sectors. His foreign patron used him as both an operative and a vessel, modifying his Soul so extensively that his existence was already undergoing continuous conceptual decomposition by the time the Empire discovered him.[1]
Nikolai became one of the first direct links through which Victor obtained meaningful information about the powers operating beyond his Sector. His betrayal drew the personal intervention of War, the strongest of the Emperor's Heralds, and ultimately resulted in Victor using Nikolai's body to forcibly manifest and interrogate the Upper-Sector consciousness hidden within him.[1]

### Physical Appearance
Nikolai's normal physical appearance is not described in sufficient detail to establish his height, hair color, eye color, complexion or other identifying characteristics.[1]
By the time War interrogated him, however, Nikolai's body visibly reflected the deterioration of his existence. After being forced to reveal the objective of his foreign patron, black tears ran from his eyes and he violently coughed up foul, Divinely infused blood. His condition continued worsening as War's presence and the foreign influence within him eroded his body, mind and Soul.[1]
Later, after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Runic ritual violently altered his existence, Nikolai's body was left severely damaged and became the physical vessel through which the Upper-Sector consciousness possessing him manifested.[1]

### Personality
Nikolai was willing to betray the civilization in which his family lived in exchange for cooperation with foreign Gods. War explicitly branded him a heretic who had abandoned the Emperor in favor of outside powers, while Nikolai himself admitted that the operation targeting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] involved attacking the Emperor's Bloodline, obtaining and cloning his Blood and uncovering the secret behind his anomalous existence.[1]
His actions indicate considerable ambition and recklessness, but the chapter does not establish his original motivation clearly enough to determine whether he acted primarily for personal power, knowledge, ideological reasons or promises made by his patron. What is certain is that he knowingly participated in an operation directed against Victor despite understanding that discovery would attract Imperial retaliation.[1]
Nikolai nevertheless dramatically underestimated the response. He expected the Empire to notice his actions, but the appearance of War immediately terrified him because he had never expected Victor to consider the matter important enough to personally dispatch one of his Heralds. His fear intensified rapidly beneath War's presence, and he was ultimately incapable of resisting War's command to reveal what his patron wanted.[1]
Nikolai's greatest mistake was his lack of understanding regarding the powers with which he had involved himself. The foreign modifications affecting his Soul were not empowering him safely; they were gradually destroying him. By the time Victor examined him directly, Nikolai's existence had become a mixture of foreign influences that continuously consumed and corrupted him from within.[1]

### Synopsis

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Around the late stage of the Empire's accelerated development project, after approximately 170,000 years of internal Imperial history, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] finally discovered one of the clues he had been searching for since beginning the ritual. The incident occurred within the Sagittarius Dwarf Elliptical Galaxy, approximately 80,000 light-years from [[Yggdrasil]], on a moon belonging to a planet already controlled by the [[Elderblood Empire]].[1]
Nikolai Francis Nichev was identified as a [[Demigod]] and one of the numerous distant descendants of the Celtic God [[Dagda]]. Dagda was explicitly not Nikolai's father; his relationship to Nikolai was compared more closely to that of a distant great-great-grandfather. Nikolai's mother, [[Juliette Dubois]], was an Imperial citizen born on Yggdrasil, while his father, [[Ivankov Nichev]], carried Dagda's bloodline.[1]
At some point before Chapter 1158, Nikolai entered into contact with a powerful foreign patron associated with the Upper Sectors. The relationship exposed him to information that an ordinary Imperial citizen should not have possessed, including knowledge concerning the Emperor's Heralds. Very few people knew precise information about them because those who encountered the Heralds as enemies normally did not survive to report what they had seen.[1]
Nikolai's activities eventually attracted Imperial attention. Victor did not respond with ordinary law enforcement or a conventional Legion. He instead dispatched War, the strongest of his Heralds and the Progenitor of a species recreated specifically to exist in service of the Emperor.[1]
War normally considered an individual of Nikolai's level beneath his personal attention and could have delegated the capture to his subordinate Heralds. Nikolai was different because of what existed inside him. War immediately recognized that the Demigod had been directly touched by Gods of the Upper Sectors, making the situation important enough for Victor himself to observe.[1]
War confronted Nikolai while the Demigod was already kneeling before him. Nikolai immediately panicked upon recognizing the Emperor's Herald. War's mere presence should ordinarily have been sufficient to destroy Nikolai's sanity because the Demigod was incapable of properly comprehending the Being standing before him, yet Nikolai remained functional longer than expected. Rather than reassuring War, this resistance confirmed that foreign Gods had altered him.[1]
War declared Nikolai a heretic who had betrayed Victor for foreign Gods and ordered him to explain his objective. The command imposed such overwhelming supernatural pressure that Reality itself distorted around Nikolai and forced him to speak. Nikolai confessed that the operation sought to attack the Emperor's Bloodline, clone Victor's Blood and uncover the secret behind Victor's anomalous existence for his master.[1]
Before Nikolai could complete the explanation, the foreign influence within him reacted violently. He screamed, black tears poured from his eyes and corrupted, Divinely infused blood spilled from his mouth. War's presence was gradually driving him insane, while his very existence was simultaneously beginning to disappear beneath the combined pressure of War and the powers already inhabiting him.[1]
Through War, Victor inspected Nikolai directly. As Victor's Herald, War could function as his Emperor's eyes, ears and hands, allowing Victor to extend his perception through him when necessary. What Victor discovered inside Nikolai was considerably worse than ordinary possession.[1]
Nikolai's Soul had become an abnormal composite of several foreign influences that were continuously consuming and corrupting him. Victor explicitly distinguished the condition from conventional Eldritch corruption. The process operated at a deeper level through conceptual decomposition, causing Nikolai to die progressively with every second that he remained alive.[1]
Victor recognized similarities between the phenomenon and powers previously observed among Niklaus' descendants, although Nikolai's condition was considerably more severe. From this, Victor inferred that Niklaus had either maintained contact with Upper-Sector entities for a long period or had acquired some remnant they had previously thrown into the lower Sectors. Victor already knew that higher powers sometimes deposited fragments, artifacts or influences inside lower Sectors in order to manipulate their civilizations and eventually bring them under control.[1]
Realizing Nikolai's value as evidence, Victor ordered War to bring him alive. Victor's power briefly enveloped Nikolai in violet and dark Energy and tore him away from ordinary Reality, preparing him for transfer rather than allowing the corruption to destroy him before he could be examined.[1]
War's four subordinate Heralds subsequently reported that everyone involved with Nikolai's operation had been captured and transferred to the Third Legion. War considered the moon upon which the operation had taken place contaminated by a heretic's presence. He and the other Heralds moved away from it, after which War compressed the entire moon into a small stone in less than two seconds without disturbing the planet below. He then created an identical replacement and restored it to the same position.[1]
Victor refused to take Nikolai anywhere near his family, Yggdrasil or the Empire's principal worlds. Instead, he temporarily left the great Time ritual and entered an expendable dimension he had created near the edge of his Sector specifically so that the entire location could be abandoned or destroyed if necessary.[1]
Inside the dimension, Victor transformed a laboratory into a ritual chamber. He separated the location from normal Reality, established multiple anti-escape safeguards and converted a stone bed into an altar covered by hundreds of Draconic and Eldritch Runes. Even War, despite his supernatural nature, experienced a headache merely from looking at the completed Runic structure.[1]
When War delivered Nikolai, Victor placed the Demigod upon the altar and activated the ritual. The complexity of Victor's language had reached such a level that even his Heralds could not understand him; to them, it resembled communication from a higher-dimensional existence attempting to speak to a lower-dimensional one.[1]
The Runes entered Nikolai's body and forcibly restructured his existence until the hidden foreign consciousness could no longer remain concealed. Nikolai's ruined body rose from the altar under the control of the Being inside him.[1]
The manifested existence immediately recognized that Victor had forcibly pulled its consciousness down from the Upper Sectors. It identified Victor as the new <[[Chaos]]>, the wielder of <Negativity> and an anomaly whose existence had already attracted considerable attention among the rulers above his Sector. The entity further described Victor as the potential spark for an approaching war for the Throne and revealed that <Light> was obsessively searching for him in order to obtain his Negativity.[1]
Victor quickly determined that the Being was not physically present. Like one of Victor's own remote clones, only part of its consciousness had been projected through Nikolai. Nevertheless, Victor's ritual had trapped that fragment completely and compelled it to reveal far more than it ordinarily would have volunteered.[1]
The foreign Being began revealing that other powerful factions wished to kill Victor, absorb him or take the powers he possessed. However, an extraordinarily powerful existential Soul Contract activated whenever it approached information it was forbidden to disclose. The contract did not simply kill Nikolai's body; it destroyed the manifested fragment's body and Soul in an attempt to prevent the information from being extracted.[1]
Victor refused to allow the contract to end the interrogation. He intercepted the fragment before it could disappear completely into the [[Akashic Records]] and reconstructed its existence. Within approximately thirty seconds the destroyed Being was once again alive as though its death had never occurred.[1]
The cycle repeated. Every time the contract killed the fragment, Victor restored its body, Soul and mind and continued questioning it. When the entity's mind collapsed from repeated death and exposure to Victor's cosmic nature, Victor restored that as well. Victor explicitly informed it that no matter how many times it died, he would bring it back until it surrendered everything it knew.[1]
Ultimately, the trapped consciousness experienced 623,596,777,174 deaths over years of subjective Time inside the isolated dimension. Victor eventually exhausted all useful information contained within the fragment and learned more about the God behind it and the wider Upper-Sector political environment.[1]
The source was not an important ruler. Victor classified the foreign Being as an elite warrior—a somewhat larger "fish" rather than a genuine member of the highest leadership. It nevertheless possessed enough curiosity and competence to have accumulated useful information and rumors concerning its surroundings.[1]
Among the intelligence Victor extracted was confirmation connecting the faction associated with Niklaus to the ruler known as <Darkness>, a Being whose influence extended across hundreds of galaxies. The interrogation also provided information concerning the wider struggle involving <Light>, <Darkness> and the powers competing for the supreme Throne.[1]
The foreign God operating through Nikolai's body ultimately died as well. Extreme and prolonged exposure to Victor's unrestricted cosmic-horror existence corrupted it beyond recovery. Victor then erased Nikolai's remaining corpse rather than preserve or absorb a body saturated with foreign and Eldritch corruption. He departed with his Heralds and completely destroyed the isolated dimension afterward.[1]
Nikolai's betrayal therefore ended with both himself and the foreign existence possessing him destroyed, but the information obtained from his capture became one of the Empire's first substantial sources of intelligence concerning the powers of the Upper Sectors.[1]

### Skills & Abilities
Nikolai was a [[Demigod]] possessing genuine Divine ancestry, but Chapter 1158 does not provide enough evidence to construct a large independent combat profile for him. Most extraordinary phenomena surrounding him originated from foreign Upper-Sector modifications rather than powers Nikolai personally controlled.[1]
- Demigod Physiology: Nikolai was explicitly identified as a Demigod descended from [[Dagda]]. His Divine lineage came through his father, [[Ivankov Nichev]], who was himself one of Dagda's descendants.[1]
- Divine Blood: Nikolai's blood contained unmistakable Divine influence inherited through his ancestry. When his corrupted blood fell from his body, War immediately recognized the Divinity within it while examining Nikolai under [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s perception.[1]
- Enhanced Mental Resistance (Foreign-Enhanced): War's presence alone should have been sufficient to drive Nikolai insane because his mind could not properly comprehend the Herald's existence. Nikolai nevertheless remained conscious and resisted the effect for longer than War expected. This was specifically treated as evidence that Upper-Sector Gods had modified or "touched" him rather than as an ordinary feature of his Demigod lineage.[1]
- Resistance to Madness Inducement (Limited; Foreign-Enhanced): Nikolai temporarily remained functional beneath War's cognitively overwhelming aura, although the resistance was incomplete. Continued exposure gradually drove him insane and contributed to the deterioration of his existence.[1]
- Upper-Sector Empowerment / Existential Modification: Nikolai had been directly altered by foreign Gods from the Upper Sectors. These modifications changed his Soul and allowed him to endure supernatural phenomena beyond what War expected from an ordinary Demigod, but the enhancements were fundamentally unstable and ultimately lethal.[1]
- Forbidden Intelligence: Through his foreign patron, Nikolai possessed knowledge that ordinary Imperial citizens should not have had, including information concerning the existence and role of the Emperor's Heralds despite the extreme secrecy surrounding them.[1]

### Foreign Modifications and Conditions
The following were properties imposed upon Nikolai by outside Beings rather than powers he was shown deliberately controlling.
- Conceptual Corruption / Conceptual Decomposition: Nikolai's Soul had become a mixture of several foreign influences that continuously consumed and corrupted his existence. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly distinguished this condition from normal Eldritch corruption, describing it as a deeper form of conceptual decomposition. Nikolai was effectively dying every second even before Victor began the interrogation.[1]
- Divine Possession / Vessel Physiology: Nikolai's body housed the consciousness of an Upper-Sector Being. Victor's ritual forcibly drew the foreign consciousness into manifestation, after which Nikolai's damaged body functioned as its physical vessel.[1]
- Remote Consciousness Vessel: The entity inside Nikolai was not the complete foreign God. Victor determined that only a fragment or projection of its consciousness had been sent through Nikolai while the true existence remained elsewhere in the Upper Sectors.[1]
- Foreign Divine Connection: Nikolai remained metaphysically linked to an unknown patron or master from the Upper Sectors. This connection provided him with forbidden knowledge and made him part of an operation specifically directed toward Victor's Bloodline and anomalous existence.[1]
- Existential Soul Contract (Foreign Entity): The consciousness possessing Nikolai was bound by a high-order contract that automatically destroyed its body and Soul whenever it attempted to reveal forbidden information. The safeguard was powerful enough to kill the manifested fragment completely but ultimately failed because Victor repeatedly reconstructed it.[1]

### Weaknesses
- Unstable Foreign Modification: Nikolai's Upper-Sector modifications were killing rather than sustainably empowering him. His Soul and existence continuously decomposed even before his capture.[1]
- Incomplete Madness Resistance: Although he endured War's presence longer than expected, Nikolai was still gradually driven insane by prolonged exposure.[1]
- Susceptibility to Superior Compulsion: Nikolai could not resist War's command to reveal his objective. War's order warped the surrounding Reality and compelled him to answer.[1]
- Foreign Possession: Nikolai had lost meaningful control over his own body by the time [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] activated the ritual. The Upper-Sector consciousness inside him was capable of rising and speaking through his ruined vessel.[1]
- Conceptual Instability: The foreign forces mixed into Nikolai's Soul were mutually destructive enough that Victor described him as dying with every passing second.[1]

### Relationships

#### Juliette Dubois
[[Juliette Dubois]] was Nikolai's mother and an Imperial citizen born on [[Yggdrasil]]. Nothing further is currently revealed about their relationship.[1]

#### Ivankov Nichev
[[Ivankov Nichev]] was Nikolai's father and a descendant of the Celtic God [[Dagda]]. Nikolai inherited his Divine ancestry through this side of the family.[1]

#### Dagda
[[Dagda]] was a distant ancestor rather than Nikolai's direct father. The narration compares the relationship to that of a great-great-grandfather and emphasizes that Nikolai was merely one among many descendants of the Celtic God.[1]

#### Victor Elderblood
Nikolai was born within the civilization ruled by [[Victor Elderblood]] but secretly betrayed him in favor of foreign Gods. His operation specifically targeted Victor's Bloodline and sought to obtain, clone and analyze the Emperor's Blood in order to discover the source of Victor's anomalous existence.[1]
Victor ultimately regarded Nikolai primarily as a useful lead toward the powers of the Upper Sectors. After extracting everything useful from the foreign consciousness inhabiting Nikolai, Victor erased the contaminated corpse and destroyed the interrogation dimension.[1]

#### War
War was personally dispatched to capture Nikolai after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] detected the Upper-Sector influence within him. Nikolai was immediately terrified upon recognizing War because he had never expected an operation of his scale to attract one of the Emperor's Heralds.[1]
War considered Nikolai personally insignificant, referring to him effectively as a small target, but recognized that the foreign powers inhabiting his Soul made him valuable enough to capture alive.[1]

### Trivia
- Nikolai is not a direct son of [[Dagda]]. Dagda is a distant ancestor, compared in Chapter 1158 to something closer to a great-great-grandfather.[1]
- The novel explicitly states that Nikolai's mother [[Juliette Dubois]] was born on [[Yggdrasil]]. It does not explicitly state that Nikolai himself was born there, so describing Yggdrasil as his confirmed birthplace is unsupported.[1]
- Nikolai knew about the Emperor's Heralds despite the fact that ordinary enemies who encountered them did not survive to spread information about them. The narration attributes this unusual knowledge to his foreign patron.[1]
- Nikolai's ability to temporarily withstand War's presence was one of the first clues confirming that Upper-Sector Gods had directly modified him.[1]
- The corruption consuming Nikolai was explicitly not ordinary Eldritch corruption. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] described it as something deeper involving conceptual decomposition.[1]
- Nikolai's body became the vessel through which Victor forcibly manifested an Upper-Sector consciousness, making his capture one of the Empire's first direct opportunities to interrogate an agent connected to the powers beyond the Sector.[1]
- The 623,596,777,174 deaths during Victor's interrogation primarily concern the manifested foreign fragment using Nikolai's body as its vessel; they should not be presented as Nikolai independently possessing resurrection or repeatedly resurrecting through his own power.[1]
- Victor ultimately erased Nikolai's corpse because it had become saturated with corruption from both the foreign entity and prolonged exposure to Victor's Eldritch existence.[1]
