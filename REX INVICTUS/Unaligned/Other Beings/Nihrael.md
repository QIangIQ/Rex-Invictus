### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 1205
Nihrael is the Chief God of Oblivion and one of the Ten Chief Gods of the [[Black Eclipse Pantheon]], in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]]. The name is a title. <Darkness>, whom [[Victor Elderblood]] addresses as Vharzath, bestows it. The charge, eighth in the order Lily gives, is oblivion.[1]
Oblivion is already among the concepts the first Imperial report ties to the pantheon. The report names no God for it. The extraction does. The title is Nihrael. The name is not the [[Nihilvore]]. That is the Abomination [[Xytherion]] is sent to deploy, and Hela erases it with <END>. A Chief God of oblivion is not that weapon, and is not shown beside it.[2][3][4][1]
No body, armor, face, or pronoun is given.[1]

### The Office
The place is bestowed on a God who has already reached an elite. The blessing makes a galactic threat. The first years do not include mastery. What is wielded is the patron's Darkness, a force alien to the realm, and the realm in this chair is oblivion. The captured holders had kept their offices for hundreds of years. The next generation is not expected to equal them. When they fall, another is raised. The Thrones are the line that is not replaced that way.[5][3][1]
Violet takes the charges as concepts bound to negativity. Oblivion sits in that set with void, night, silence, fear, and the rest. No erasing of a memory, a name, or a world is shown in this God's hands. The charge is the office.[1]

### Among the Ten
Eighth. Darkness is before it, inevitability after. [[Azelith]] and [[Draveth]] are the neighboring titles. The four who meet War do not include this name. Nine Chief Gods are emptied with [[Vaelthar]], and dragonfire is ordered for the bodies once Ruby has her samples. The nine are not named. Nihrael is a title in the list, not a body that is pointed out.[6][1]
