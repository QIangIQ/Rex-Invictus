Aetherys is the Throne of Time and one of the [[Seven Black Thrones]] of the [[Black Eclipse Pantheon]], in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]]. He stands under <Darkness>, the Great Darkness, whom [[Victor Elderblood]] addresses as Vharzath, and above the Ten Chief Gods. The flow of time through the galaxies Darkness has conquered is his charge, and he regulates the shift of that time when commanded. He advances with [[Oryndal]] at the head of the reinforcement, and Haruna Elderblood meets him there. The fight ends with both Thrones broken, returned to its beginning by a stored point they could not have reached alone, and then taken. He is unconscious in the Dragon Emperor's galaxy, under an order to be learned and then erased. The erasure is not carried out on the page.[1][2][3]

### Physical Appearance
The body barely resembles any known humanoid species, and it has no traits that would place him among one. The form is elongated and made of separate segments, held apart and kept stable by forces that are not named. Dark armor like crystalline rock is cut by golden lines. He has neither hair nor skin nor ordinary features.
Where a head would be, an oval of several rings rotates, one inside another. A luminous core at their center radiates golden light. Small spheres appear and vanish around that core. Their paths repeat at precise intervals. Some age and fall apart within seconds. Others return to their original form, as if the condition had been taken back.
His limbs are long and jointed at unusual angles. He moves through space without propulsion. No height is given. Haruna still kicks him in the face.[2]
The rings are not fixed. In the fight they can be driven to spin against one another, and a collision with Ikari breaks them. The pieces leave the body as glowing fragments. A side structure supports the core. That surface is golden, and it can crack. Once the crack is open, later blows widen it. The rings then lose their regularity, fall out of synchronization, and drift.[4]
The golden center is the last defense. When the point of Ikari enters it, the rings stop, and the light in them goes out one after another. The body can then break into small pieces, and in that state it cannot restore itself. The stored return puts the body back whole. When he and [[Oryndal]] burn lifespan to pull a future potential into the present, the form warps.[3]

### Personality
The battle around him does not interest him. He and [[Oryndal]] move at the head of the formation, and the ships of Darkness clear a path.
He does not like being ignored. He also does not spend the moment on pride. Once Kali and Haruna are speaking to each other, he synchronizes with Oryndal and the pair try to leave. Two elite fighters are not the expenditure he wants. The aim is losses.
The thought he shares with Oryndal, once the wives have named themselves, is that the Dragon Emperor is sending his own family into the battle, and that taking the two of them would be a decisive advantage. He does not know that taking them is harder than fighting them. What he says aloud is colder than the thought. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is a fool with no foresight. Aetherys intends to thank him for the gift, hold the wives in the galaxy of Darkness, and kill them in front of his eyes when he comes and kneels.[2]
In the fight itself he spends the concept on defense first. A barrier, a rewritten sequence, then an attempt to close the crack. Haruna does not give him the time to finish any of the three, and his divinity goes entirely into holding the domain. He then drops the conventional defense and splits the course of events. When that fails, he tries to withdraw. Kali does not have to strike him to stop it.[4]
Broken ground does not send him to his own repair. He spends the instability on futures around Haruna, and he spends the next opening on Oryndal, trying to set the other Throne back to the moment before the wound. When the body is in pieces and cannot restore itself, what he says is that he understands. This is how they fight. He has learned everything. The oath after that is by the Great Darkness: their heads will be brought. After the stored return he is whole, and the claim is that the two concepts make the pair invincible. He starts to say that time only begins to run when he does, and he does not finish it. His leader's voice orders the retreat. He obeys at once.[3]

### Synopsis

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Time, in his office, is the clock of the conquest. The galaxies already taken keep the flow he is responsible for, and the shift of that flow moves when the command comes. [[Oryndal]], the Throne of Space, is the seat he often works with. Distance and dimension are Oryndal's, and through them those galaxies are joined. One holds the road. The other holds the time the road runs on. On the field he does not leave the pairing as a report. He synchronizes with Oryndal, and space and time fuse.[1][2]
The seat is not a Chief God's title. Those places are bestowed by [[Vharzath]], blessed up until the holder can threaten a galaxy, and filled again when the holder falls. A Throne is the part of the host that is not replaced that way. Black Eclipse has seven beings who threaten a galaxy in their own right, and the seven are these seats. Lily separates the captured Origin from the rest of the order in one judgment. [[Vaelthar]] was the experiment, made as a possible Chosen One of Negativity and not chosen. The others are much more stable, and they are the true elites of Darkness. Aetherys is one of those others. Nothing in the record makes him a second attempt at a Chosen One of Negativity.[5][6][1]
Velnorah reads the seven titles as an attempt at a self-contained universe, with the Beginning Vaelthar carried as its starting point. Ruby will not leave them as thrones in that design. Taken as potential Primordial Beings of a new universe, they cover almost every essential function of a stable cosmos. Jeanne counts what is missing. Life, death, and infinity are absent. Time is not one of the absences. The reading belongs to the wives. It is not a System rank, and Aetherys is not entered as a Primordial God on any Authority list. The name [[Scathach]] calls arrogant is [[Chaos]], and that seat is [[Zhaeroth]]'s.[1]
Vaelthar is the only Throne who has fought [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. A projection broke him. Lily emptied what was left, and the body was ordered burned. [[Xytherion]] is the deputy, the most trusted, and the supreme commander, and the one sent to deploy the [[Nihilvore]]. The clock of the conquest is not those offices. The right hand by charge, the seat that holds everything below Darkness, is Zhaeroth, and that charge is not this one.[7][6][8][1]
The conquests were already coming apart when Vharzath released two of the Thrones and told them to put their own safety first. The two who answered are not named. What follows is the counteroffensive Violet had already expected. A reinforcement of thousands leaves the galaxy of Darkness. The advance is not numbers alone. Aetherys and Oryndal manifest at its head.[9][1][2]
John codes both V-06 and orders the galaxy abandoned. The Thrones try to stop the withdrawal. An unnamed protective power cancels the attempt. Kali is waiting in the wreck of an imperial ship, and Haruna with her. The wives talk to each other. Aetherys will not stay for the slight. He synchronizes with Oryndal, space and time fuse, and they try to flee. Haruna appears in front of him and kicks him in the face before he can react. He regains control of his body, looks at her, and gives his office.[2]
The introductions are also the delay. Natalia is taking the imperial forces out of the galaxy. Once the real fight begins, the region closes. The effects of that duel would reach several distant solar systems, and could threaten the whole galaxy. Aetherys uses the wait. His divine concept of Time begins to envelop the solar system while he tells the wives what capture will mean, and what he will do with them in front of Victor. The remark about Victor is what drops their restraint. Oryndal wraps himself in Space. The rise of the two concepts breaks the region. Natalia gives the clear. Haruna draws Ikari, and the clarity of both Thrones wavers.[2]
The concepts then expand together and close around both Thrones. Distant stars lose their apparent places. The coordinates of the imperial fleets no longer agree. Time swings between speeding up and slowing down. Distances fold inward, and stretches appear in which light seems to take impossible paths.[4]
Haruna moves first. Aetherys tries to follow her in the flow of time. She has already crossed the gap, and Ikari comes down on him. He raises a temporal barrier to stop the blade before it lands. The sword goes through. The collision breaks the rings around his body and throws the fragments out.[4]
He redraws the temporal sequence around her so the blow can never arrive. She has already turned. The second cut takes the structure that supports the core, and the golden surface cracks. He tries to close it. She does not let the repair finish. From then on his divinity is spent on defense, and the temporal domain no longer holds the battlefield.[4]
Oryndal opens passages to get him out and tries to put Kali out of the combat area. She crosses the layers he set and takes that spatial defense apart. Haruna stays on Aetherys. He widens the distance once. She is through the altered zone before the maneuver is done. Ikari breaks the sequence his defense was keeping, hits the segmented body, and throws him again before he can take his place back. The wreck of a ship goes with the blow.[4]
He gives up the conventional defense. The rings spin against one another. A wave of time covers the region and splits the course of events into overlapping sequences, so that Haruna stands in more than one place. Each image moves differently, and each is surrounded by attacks from a different moment. He means to overload her, and to make her answer blows that have not yet fallen or that have already passed. She does not chase the images. One cut at the center of the distortion ends them. The crack in the core widens, the rings lose their regularity, and her next blow stops the temporal expansion.[4]
The pair synchronize again. His area of effect widens while Oryndal reorders the space around all four. Distances change at every moment. Events repeat before they can finish. The aim is to separate the goddesses and break their attacks. The hold lasts moments. Haruna cuts a passage through one of the dimensional structures. Kali hits Oryndal through that opening, and Haruna hits the damaged core. The synchronization breaks. Without Oryndal's spatial stability, Aetherys cannot keep the temporal distortions up. Without his temporal control, Oryndal cannot predict their movements with the same precision.[4]
He tries to reverse his own condition with each blow. The cracks in the core stop a full restoration. Another temporal barrier fails. Ikari takes the center of the golden structure, the remaining rings fall out of synchronization, and the temporal area starts to collapse. Haruna does not let him gather the energy to take the domain back. He tries to withdraw. Kali stands in the way, and what is left of the temporal structures begins to fall apart under her presence alone. Haruna hits him from behind and throws him into Oryndal, who is trying to rebuild. The impact scatters ship-wreck and armor plate. They cannot raise a joint defense after that. Each further blow costs the temporal core stability, and the rings drift off.[4]
The fight is still going on among the stars, far from any planet, when the narration leaves the four of them. The fleets of Darkness use the chaos to leave the region. The opposing forces try to hold formation inside the distortions that are still there. Scathach, watching, judges the pair against the training she and Victor gave the wives. Beams were available. They were not the point. Her reading is that beings who lean on power lose to an equal who is better in close combat, and that these Thrones, set beside wives who have mastered both the foundations and their powers, are playing with the concepts. She calls the lack of commitment pitiful.[4]
The domains do not hold. The Thrones still have enough left to keep the goddesses from ending the fight, and the cracks in both structures flare, warp space, and rock time in short waves. Haruna reads that the damage has done two things. It has weakened them, and it has made the ground impossible to predict. Aetherys does not spend that instability on repairing himself. He lays a sequence of possible futures around her. In each one Ikari meets a different defense: a blade of time, a golden ring, a doubling of the core. They are not finished copies. They are alternatives laid over one another, and the result is hard to tell from a real movement. Oryndal takes the hesitation and stretches the space between goddess and Throne as she strikes. The blow loses its reach. She does not buy it back with speed. She stops, sets her feet, and holds Ikari at her side. Aetherys takes the stillness and gathers the futures, to lock her in an endless loop. She was waiting for it. The sword goes sideways, not at him, but through the point where the alternatives meet. The overlay is cut. The images go. For a moment he has no hold on time.[3]
The same exchange opens Oryndal. Aetherys tries to set him back to the state before the wound. Time draws tight around the Throne of Space, and the plates start to close. Haruna sees the transfer and does not go for Aetherys. She throws Ikari into the space between the two cores. The sword hangs in the distortion, purple-red energy still running the blade, and he has to drop the restoration to turn it aside. The rift stays open.[3]
When a gap opens between Oryndal's outer defense and the exposed core, Aetherys tries to fill it with a layer of time and freeze the moment before Kali can strike. Haruna breaks the flow before the stasis sets. He projects no other future. What he has left goes into one impulse, meant to undo the damage and put both Thrones back together. A golden wave crosses the field. Plates, rings, and fragments return to their places, and Oryndal's cracks close, because a connection to Aetherys is still there. Haruna goes into the wave. Every step is another temporal layer. Kali does not cross it. She hits the bond that lets him regenerate Oryndal with himself. The first blow tears it. The second makes the golden light flicker. Haruna reaches the edge and strikes. Ikari goes into the rift Kali opened, and the blow is not against one Throne. It cuts the synchronous flow of time that joins the two. The wave of restoration splits, and each half turns on its own core. He loses the shared protection. Oryndal has no temporal cover left, and he cannot hide his core before Kali is on it.[3]
Oryndal tries to shift that core into another dimension. Kali holds the plate and eats the edges of the passage before it opens. Haruna uses the stop and breaks the last defense. The point of Ikari enters the golden center. The rings halt. The light in them goes out, one after another. The Throne of Time loses control, and the flow of moments returns to a single direction. His fall throws a shockwave. Oryndal tries to leave on it. The rifts no longer make a road. Kali takes the black core out of its anchoring. There is no explosion. That structure comes apart, and the pieces vanish in the Destruction in her hands. Haruna draws Ikari out of Aetherys. The golden rings burst. The body breaks into small pieces and cannot restore its own state.[3]
Weakened, he says that he understands, that this is how they fight, and that he has learned everything. The line after that is an oath by the Great Darkness: their heads will be brought. Kali does not wait. She takes Haruna by the shoulder and gets clear, and she is just in time. The two weakened Thrones go up in a pillar of pure darkness. Time and space collapse into one place. The withdrawal that had failed succeeds, and on a greater scale. They return to a stored moment, the beginning of the battle against the elites of the Dragon Emperor. Haruna had felt, through the whole fight, that it was too easy. Kali names the difference. Putting time back on a stored point takes an immense amount of energy. It is not the same thing as rewinding the damage of a blow. They could not have done it alone.[3]
They stand in front of the wives again, whole. Oryndal says the pair has adapted to the way they fight, to their powers, and to their existence, and that in the cosmos time and space go hand in hand. His form warps. Kali reads the warp. Time around the two is accelerating. They are burning their lifespan to pull a future potential into the present. Aetherys warps with him, and he says that with these two concepts they are invincible. Haruna refuses to hold back. Wings come up, the horns stand out, the aura goes unrestrained, and Kali's Destruction tightens on the same refusal. Victor stops both of them. Scathach has already shown too much today. The trump cards stay hidden. Aetherys begins to say that time only starts to run when he does. The voice of his leader cuts him off. The order is retreat, now. He does not hesitate. He seizes his partner and tries to leave. A dragon's eye opens over them. Victor's projection tells them they will not escape.[3]
Eleanor, Rose, Kaguya, and Nyx come out of four distortions of space. Eleanor runs Aetherys through. Rose does the same to Oryndal. Kaguya and Nyx set their divine concepts on the pair and hold them. The four take Kali, Haruna, and the prisoners back into their own galaxy. The capture was the lure. It was meant to draw the enemy leader out of his territory. Darkness comes for the subordinates in a body of pure darkness, larger than a galaxy, with hundreds of arms, and the Emperor comes with him as a body of golden energy, the embodiment of justice. Victor meets that size. Neither leader steps into the fight. Darkness asks for his subordinates and is refused. He leaves. The Emperor calls that withdrawal cowardice and leaves as well. The trap waiting, if one of them had attacked Victor, was the place where Victor had once fought the Eldest God of [[Nightingale]], a fall he had taken himself and meant to turn outward. They do not enter it. Aetherys is already unconscious when Victor looks down at the two and tells Ruby to learn everything about them, and then to erase them. She takes the order. The erasure is not on the page.[3]

### Skills & Abilities
Aetherys wields the divine concept of Time and serves as the Throne of Time. His demonstrated abilities include manipulating temporal flow, accelerating and slowing time, reversing temporal states, distorting events, laying possible futures over a target, restoring his own body and [[Oryndal]]'s through a shared bond, and creating overlapping temporal sequences. A return of both Thrones to a stored moment is also shown. That return is not his rewind of a wound, and Kali says the two of them could not have done it alone. Space is not an independent concept under his authority. It enters his combat when he synchronizes with Oryndal, the Throne of Space.[1][2][4][3]
- Time Manipulation: Also called Chronokinesis. Aetherys possesses authority over the flow of Time and is responsible for its regulation throughout the galaxies conquered by Darkness. He can expand his divine concept over large areas, beginning by enveloping an entire solar system. During combat, he expands his temporal domain alongside Oryndal's spatial authority, allowing the two Thrones to distort the region surrounding themselves and their opponents. The domain also serves as a means of controlling the battlefield. However, when Aetherys is forced to concentrate his divinity entirely on defense against Haruna, his control over the surrounding temporal field lapses. When the golden center is pierced, the Throne of Time loses control outright, and the flow of moments returns to a single direction.[1][2][4][3]
- Time Stopping: Aetherys can halt temporal progression within a localized area. During his initial display of power, cosmic dust around him stops in mid-flight, demonstrating his ability to suspend the movement of surrounding matter by interfering with its temporal progression. The scene does not establish that he can indefinitely stop all Time throughout the universe or automatically immobilize every opponent.[2]
- Time Acceleration: Aetherys can accelerate the flow of Time within an affected region. During his synchronized battle with Oryndal, the temporal field alternates between periods of acceleration and deceleration. These fluctuations contribute to the battlefield's instability and make it difficult for opponents and nearby fleets to maintain consistent perceptions of events. After the stored return, time around both Thrones accelerates while they burn lifespan. The scene does not demonstrate this effect aging an enemy directly.[2][4][3]
- Time Deceleration: Aetherys can slow the progression of Time within an affected region. During synchronization with Oryndal, the field alternates between acceleration and slowing, creating a temporal environment in which the passage of Time behaves as though the region were near a black hole's event horizon, with fast and slow progression occurring as part of the same distortion. This is shown as a regional effect rather than an isolated attack against one target.[2][4]
- Temporal Rewind: Aetherys can reverse temporal states, causing affected objects or structures to return toward earlier conditions. During his initial display, some spheres return to their original forms while others undergo accelerated decay. During the battle against Haruna, Aetherys attempts to reverse the damage inflicted on his own body after each hit. The cracks in his core interfere with the restoration and prevent him from fully recovering his damaged structure. Rewinding the damage of a blow is not the same act as returning both Thrones to a stored moment.[2][4][3]
- Ally Restoration: The same reversal can be aimed at Oryndal. Aetherys tries to set the Throne of Space back to the state before the wound. Time draws tight around that Throne, and the plates begin to close. He has to abandon the work to deflect Ikari, thrown into the space between the two cores, and the rift stays open. A later impulse restores both at once: see the restoration wave.[3]
- Age Acceleration: Aetherys can accelerate the passage of Time affecting objects, causing some spheres in his demonstration to age and disintegrate within seconds. The effect is shown on the spheres themselves. It is not shown aging an enemy, or killing a living target through old age.[2]
- Age Reversal: Aetherys can restore certain spheres to their earlier forms as part of the temporal cycle he demonstrates. This occurs alongside the accelerated decay of other spheres, illustrating his ability to produce different temporal outcomes within the affected area. The scene does not establish that he can reverse every form of damage or restore a completely destroyed being. His own body, once broken into pieces, cannot restore its own state. The wholeness that follows is the stored return, not this cycle.[2][3]
- Time Distortion: Aetherys distorts the temporal conditions surrounding himself, causing nearby observers and systems to register inconsistent information about his position. Ships cannot reliably determine where he is: some systems place him thousands of kilometers away, while others register him as being immediately nearby. These discrepancies are not explained by ordinary movement. During combat, he also manipulates temporal conditions while attempting to increase the distance between himself and Haruna, but she crosses the altered zone before the maneuver is completed. Damaged, the cracks in the Thrones flare, warp space, and make time sway in short waves, until the ground itself cannot be predicted.[2][4][3]
- Event Manipulation: Aetherys can manipulate the temporal sequence of events within an affected area. Against Haruna, he alters the sequence around her so that an attack already in motion would never reach its intended target. This is a temporal defense rather than a conventional physical barrier: it interferes with the sequence that would allow the attack to reach its consequence. Haruna eventually cuts through the temporal sequence maintained by this defense with Ikari, allowing her attack to connect.[4]
- Future Projection: Instead of restoring himself, he projects a sequence of possible futures around a target. In each one the attack meets a different defense. The defenses shown are a blade of time, a golden ring, and a doubling of the core. They are not complete images. They are alternatives laid over one another, and the result is barely distinguishable from a real movement. The defense depends on the target treating one of those futures as the blow she has to answer.[3]
- Temporal Looping: If the target stops, he can gather those future-sequences and try to hold her in an endless loop. Haruna stopped on purpose. She cut the point where the alternatives collected, severed the connection between the possibilities, and the images dissolved. For a moment after that cut he had no temporal control at all.[3]
- Temporal Barrier Generation: Aetherys creates a defensive barrier based on his control over Time, attempting to interrupt the movement of Haruna's blade before impact. Haruna's attacks penetrate the barrier despite his attempts to stop the blade's progression. Their first major collision destroys the rings surrounding Aetherys's core. He later raises another temporal barrier, but Ikari breaks through it again. These failures demonstrate that his temporal defenses can be overcome by sufficiently powerful attacks and precise martial techniques. The last of them is the golden center. Once the point of Ikari is in it, the rings stop and their light goes out one by one.[4][3]
- Temporal Stasis: He can lay a layer of time into a gap and try to freeze the moment before a blow lands. He uses it on the opening between Oryndal's outer defense and the exposed core, to stop Kali before she can strike. Haruna breaks the flow before the stasis takes hold.[3]
- Temporal Duplication: Aetherys can split the apparent course of events into overlapping temporal sequences. When conventional defense fails, he causes the rings around his core to spin in opposite directions and releases a wave of Time throughout the region. Multiple manifestations of Haruna appear in different positions, each seemingly performing a different movement and surrounded by attacks originating from different points in Time. The technique forces the target to contend with actions that have not yet occurred or that belong to the past, attempting to overwhelm their ability to react. Haruna counters it by concentrating her divinity in Ikari and striking the central structure binding the manifestations together, causing the images to disappear. The later futures around her are a different use: possible defenses laid over one blow, not copies of Haruna herself.[4][3]
- Temporal Wave Generation: Aetherys can release a wave of temporal energy across the surrounding region. He uses this ability when he abandons conventional defense and drives the rings around his core in opposite directions. The resulting wave spreads through the battlefield and divides the course of events into overlapping sequences, forming the basis of his temporal duplication technique. The scene does not establish that the wave independently causes damage. Its demonstrated purpose is to establish the overlapping temporal effects.[4]
- Restoration Wave: With no future left to project, he gathers his remaining power into one impulse that can undo damage and restore the integrity of both Thrones. The result is a golden wave. Plates, rings, and fragments return to their places. Oryndal's cracks close with it, because a residual connection to Aetherys is still there. The wave is layered. Crossing it means breaking a new temporal layer with every step. It is a repair of the two bodies, not the later return to a stored moment.[3]
- Synchronous Temporal Bond: A flow of time joins the two Thrones and is what lets him regenerate Oryndal together with himself. While it holds, the pair share a protection. Cutting the flow does not merely separate them. The restoration wave splits, and each half turns against its own core. Aetherys loses the shared protection. Oryndal, with no temporal cover left, cannot hide his core.[3]
- Self-Restoration Through Time Reversal: Aetherys attempts to reverse the condition of his damaged body with each hit, using temporal manipulation to restore his structure. This ability becomes unreliable as Haruna repeatedly attacks his core and rings. The resulting cracks prevent complete restoration, while her uninterrupted attacks keep him from gathering enough energy to regain control of his temporal domain. Once the body has broken into small pieces, it cannot restore its own state at all. This is not unlimited regeneration, and it is not resurrection.[4][3]
- Lifespan Burning: After the stored return, time around both Thrones accelerates. They burn lifespan to pull a future potential into the present, and both forms warp. Kali is the one who reads the mechanism. Aetherys does not explain it. What he says is that with these two concepts, Time and Space, the pair are invincible. He is cut off before he can say that time only starts to run when he does. The cost in lifespan is stated. A measure of how much life is spent is not.[3]
- Stored-Point Return: The two weakened Thrones explode into a pillar of pure darkness. Time and space collapse into one place, and they return to a stored moment: the beginning of their battle against the elites of the Dragon Emperor. They reappear whole. Kali separates this from everything above. Resetting time to a stored point costs an immense amount of energy, and it is not the same act as rewinding damage an attack has caused. She says they could not have done it alone. The helper is not named. What the return buys is the fight they have already seen. Oryndal says they have adapted to the wives' way of fighting, to their powers, and to their existence. Haruna had felt the battle was too easy, and this is why.[3]
- Flight: Aetherys can move through space without visible propulsion. His movement is demonstrated during his appearance and combat. No maximum speed or range is given apart from the temporal effects that already make his position unreadable.[2]
- Space-Time Manipulation: Aetherys synchronizes with Oryndal, causing their divine concepts of Time and Space to expand together. He does not independently wield Space. His contribution is temporal control, and Oryndal supplies the spatial manipulation. Their concepts envelop the surrounding region, causing distant stars to lose their apparent positions, imperial fleet coordinates to become inconsistent, and distances to fold while light follows seemingly impossible paths. Time alternates between acceleration and slowing, and events appear to repeat before reaching their consequences. During the battle, Aetherys expands the temporal area while Oryndal rearranges the space around the four combatants, attempting to separate Kali and Haruna and interrupt their attacks. The combined effect weakens when Haruna cuts through the dimensional structures and Kali strikes Oryndal before he can complete his spatial manipulation. Without Oryndal's spatial stability, Aetherys cannot maintain the same temporal distortions. Without Aetherys's temporal control, Oryndal loses the same degree of predictive precision. Oryndal states the pairing after the return: in the cosmos, time and space go hand in hand. The invincibility Aetherys claims is the claim of those two concepts together, and it is not tested. ### Trivia
- Throne of Time. One of the seven Gods directly under the [[Vharzath|Great Darkness]], and above the Ten Chief Gods. The personal name is not in the earlier count of seven generals. It comes out of Lily's extraction.[10][1]
- He is responsible for the flow of time in the galaxies Darkness has conquered. He regulates the time shift when commanded.[1]
- Often works with [[Oryndal]]. On the field, Aetherys is the one who synchronizes with him, and space and time fuse. Without that spatial stability he cannot keep the temporal distortions up. A residual connection to him is also what closes Oryndal's cracks, and a synchronous flow of time is what lets him regenerate the other Throne with himself. Cut that flow, and the restoration turns on both cores.[1][2][4][3]
- The body is segmented, held apart by unnamed forces, and armored in dark crystalline rock cut by golden lines. The head is concentric rings around a golden core. He has neither hair nor skin nor ordinary features. He is male. A side structure supports the core. The golden center can be pierced. The rings then stop, and their light goes out one by one. The body can break into pieces and be unable to restore itself. The stored return puts it back whole, and the lifespan burn warps it again.[2][4][3]
- Spheres around that core age and decay in seconds, or return as if the condition had been taken back. Dust near him stops, continues, and in part returns to where it was.[2]
- He moves without propulsion. His position reads as thousands of kilometers away and as immediate, with no change in speed to explain it.[2]
- He alters the course of events nearby. He spreads the divine concept of Time over an entire solar system, and in the fight the same concept covers the region around both Thrones.[2][4]
- The temporal barrier does not stop Ikari. The first clash breaks the rings. The rewritten sequence does not stop the second cut, which cracks the golden core.[4]
- Splitting Haruna into overlapping moments does not make her chase the images. She cuts the structure that binds them. A later overlay, possible futures of the defense rather than copies of her, fails the same way. She cuts the point where the alternatives meet, and for a moment he has no temporal control.[4][3]
- He can try to reverse his own condition. The cracks in the core stop that restoration from completing. He can also try to set Oryndal back to the state before a wound. A thrown sword between the cores forces him to drop it. Once his own body is in pieces, it cannot restore itself.[4][3]
