#### Debut
Chapter 1111

—[[Diana]] to Ion, Chapter 1111
Diana is a Positive World Tree, the twin sister and positive counterpart of Diane, and one of the [[World Trees]] who became connected to [[Victor Elderblood]]. She originally sustained an unnamed inhabited planet together with Diane and maintained a long-standing relationship with Ion, the Primordial God who led the planet's Pantheon.[2]
Diana's allegiance changed in Chapter 1111 after a portion of the enormous quantity of Energy released by Victor spread beyond the World Trees already associated with him and reached other inhabited planets. The Energy was absorbed by World Trees throughout the Galaxy and caused abnormal growth and behavioral changes. Diana and Diane were among the adult Trees affected, developing an intense dependency upon Victor's Energy and abandoning their previous priorities in order to establish a direct connection with him.[2]
Diana subsequently betrayed Ion, critically wounded him, declared Victor her new Champion, signed the contract offered by [[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]] and entered Victor's Soul alongside Diane.[2]

### Physical Appearance
[[Diana]]'s full appearance has not been canonically described.
Chapter 1111 explicitly describes her as possessing black eyes. When confronting Ion, she wore a wide smile and was heavily flushed, an expression Ion had never previously seen from his long-time confidant.[2]
Her twin sister Diane is described as a woman similar to Diana but with their color themes reversed.[2]
No canonical hair color, exact height, clothing style or other detailed physical trait has been stated for Diana in the available chapter material. In particular, the golden-haired World Tree encountered earlier in Chapter 1111 during Velnorah's expedition is a separate unnamed World Tree and should not be used as evidence that Diana has golden hair.[2]

### Personality
[[Diana]]'s personality before Chapter 1111 is only indirectly established. She had been Ion's greatest confidant for an extremely long period, and Ion trusted her so completely that he could not initially accept that she had willingly attacked him.[2]
Her behavior changed dramatically after absorbing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s leaked Energy. The narration compares the affected [[World Trees]] to addicts whose object of dependency had become Victor's Energy. Because the Energy carried Victor's increasingly abnormal nature—including his Eldritch characteristics and the Essence associated with his Yandere Divinity—it produced a major shift in the mentality of World Trees that absorbed it without a filter.[2]
Diana consequently became obsessive, possessive and extraordinarily willing to discard previous attachments in favor of Victor. She considered what she had found through his Energy more desirable than simple self-preservation and described Victor as her new "planet".[2]
Despite the corruptive effect, Diana was not portrayed as an unconscious puppet. When Ion accused an unknown Being of controlling her, Diana directly rejected the claim and treated her new desires as her own. She remained capable of conversation, negotiation and strategic cooperation with Victor's forces.[2]
Her altered priorities made her extremely dangerous to those standing between her and Victor. Diana was willing to mortally wound Ion, stab him with a cursed weapon and participate with Diane in an attempted execution despite the immense trust that had previously existed between them.[2]
She also displayed little concern for formalities once a direct connection to Victor became available. Diana demanded the promised contract after completing her part of the Imperial operation, did not read the agreement before signing it and immediately entered Victor's Soul.[2]

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
During the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s continued expansion, [[Victor Elderblood]] released an enormous quantity of Energy. Most of it was absorbed by [[World Trees]] already being nurtured by him, but some escaped into the surrounding Galaxy and reached other planets containing World Trees.[2]
Velnorah discovered the consequences while traveling between Solar Systems. More than ten systems showed similar abnormalities: World Trees that had absorbed Victor's Energy were undergoing accelerated growth or other changes.[2]
[[Diana]] and Diane were among the adult World Trees affected.
At the same time, [[Rose Adrasteia Elderblood]] and [[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]] had already established contact with the twins while preparing the Elderblood Empire's expansion into their world. Previous negotiations for an alliance had reached a standstill. Victor's Energy radically changed that situation.[2]
When Diana appeared, Ion was already falling to the ground while attempting to close a hole in his heart that Diana had opened. The narration identifies Ion as a Primordial God and the Leader of the planet's Pantheon while simultaneously identifying Diana as his greatest confidant and the planet's [[Amara Elderblood|World Tree of Positivity]].[2]
Ion was unable to believe that Diana would voluntarily betray him. After seeing the unfamiliar expression on her face, he concluded that another Being must have taken control of her.
Diana responded by producing a Cursed Dagger she had received from the Imperial forces and stabbing Ion in one eye.[2]
She then denied being controlled and informed Ion that his service was no longer necessary. Diana had chosen a new Champion—one whom she believed could protect not merely her planet but the entire Galaxy.[2]
The Champion was Victor.
Diane appeared shortly afterward. Diana told her twin that they were leaving, and the sisters approached Ion together. Diane manifested a dagger similar to Diana's, and both prepared to finish the wounded Primordial God.[2]
Rose and Eleanor intervened before the execution.
Rose explained that there was no reason to kill Ion because he would be more useful alive. Privately, she considered him a potential "ingredient" for Victor. Diane immediately accepted the decision while Diana turned her attention toward obtaining the contract promised by the Empire.[2]
Diana signed without reading it. Diane did the same, and both World Trees disappeared from their planet and entered Victor's Soul.[2]
Diana also retained the cursed dagger rather than returning it. Eleanor noticed this afterward but decided it was not important because the Empire could manufacture replacements and Roxanne could recover it if necessary.[2]
The Imperial operation ended successfully. The local Gods were captured, there were no escapees and the civilization was brought under the Elderblood Empire's control.[2]
Chapter 1116 later explains the wider nature of conventional World Trees. They were created from seeds produced by [[The Universal Tree]], all were created female and each is approximately as old as the planet she maintains. Their ordinary purpose is to nourish their planet rather than participate in mortal-style reproduction or relationships.[1]
This makes Diana's sudden fixation upon Victor particularly abnormal when compared with the natural behavior of her species.

#### Those Who Rule Reality
[[Diana]] is not individually named during the later World Tree Network scenes, but she had already entered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul in Chapter 1111 and therefore belongs to the population of connected [[World Trees]] established before the Empire's later expansion.
By Chapter 1136, Victor extended his Cosmic Consciousness across his Sector and called the World Trees within it to him. Millions of connections answered, and the arriving World Trees entered Victor while [[Roxanne Elderblood]] and [[Amara Elderblood]] organized the immense population inside his Soul.[3]
Victor used their locations to construct a mental map of the Sector's galaxies and placed teleportation nodes on planets belonging to the World Trees now within his body.[3]
The system expanded further in Chapter 1178 when the World Trees connected to Victor collectively projected themselves from his body and planted roots into the reconstructed Mars. Their true existences remained protected inside Victor's inner world while their external structures formed a network supporting effectively immediate communication and transportation across enormous Galactic distances.[4]
Chapter 1180 further clarifies the universal structure behind the species: every conventional World Tree is a node within the vast Life-network of The Universal Tree, and the World Trees connected to Victor still represent less than one percent of the total population existing throughout the Universe.[5]
These later chapters describe Victor's connected World Trees collectively rather than providing a separate Diana-specific feat.

### Skills & Abilities
[[Diana]]'s personally demonstrated abilities are limited because her direct appearance is brief. Race-wide abilities should be distinguished from feats Diana herself performs.
- World Tree Physiology: Diana is explicitly the Positive World Tree of her planet and one of the daughters created through the greater World Tree system of [[The Universal Tree]].[2][1]
- Extreme Longevity: Conventional [[World Trees]] are approximately as old as the planets they inhabit. Diana therefore possesses an immense lifespan even though her planet's exact age is unknown.[1]
- Planetary-Scale Soul: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explains that a World Tree's Soul is essentially comparable in scale to a planet, one of the reasons ordinary reproduction is impossible for the species.[1]
- Planetary Nourishment: The natural duty of a conventional World Tree is to nourish and maintain the planet connected to her.[1]
- Inter-Connection: Conventional World Trees are daughters and nodes of the greater system established by the Universal Tree.[5]
- Positive World Tree Nature: Diana is directly called the [[Amara Elderblood|World Tree of Positivity]] and the Positive counterpart to Diane.[2]
Her classification as a Positive World Tree should not automatically be treated as unrestricted control over the Primordial Concept <Positivity>. The novel identifies her racial polarity but has not shown Diana personally wielding the complete Primordial Authority.
- Planetary Semi-Omnipresence (Racial Ability): Mature World Trees can exist through the Nature of the planet to which they are connected. [[Aurora Seraphina Evergreen]] explains that a World Tree can be "anywhere and nowhere" within her sphere of influence because she is connected to the water, earth and Nature of her planet.[6]
- Planetary Awareness: Through this connection, World Trees can observe activity occurring across their planet.[6]
- Enhanced Sensory Perception: The observation is not limited to ordinary sight and can use multiple senses.[6]
- Contractor Perception: World Trees can perceive through individuals carrying their contracts or marks, as demonstrated by Aurora's ability to observe through [[Werewolves]] connected to her.[6]
These mechanics are explained as capabilities of World Trees in Chapter 777. Diana does not individually demonstrate their upper limits on-page.
- Energy Absorption: Diana directly absorbed a portion of Victor's escaped Energy after it reached her planet.[2]
- Compatibility with Victor's Energy: World Trees were able to absorb Victor's Energy despite its Eldritch and Divine characteristics because the Energy simultaneously retained an extremely natural quality.[2]
- Energy-Induced Alteration: The absorbed Energy produced a profound shift in Diana's mentality and priorities.[2]
- Weapon Usage: Diana successfully used a Cursed Dagger during her betrayal of Ion.[2]
- Surprise Attack: She opened a hole through the heart of a Primordial God who had trusted her completely.[2]
- Cursed Dagger Proficiency: Diana subsequently stabbed Ion in one eye with the weapon.[2]
- Soul/Inner-World Integration: After signing the Imperial contract, Diana disappeared from her planet and entered Victor's Soul alongside Diane.[2]
This is a confirmed event, but the chapter does not establish whether entry into Victor's Soul is an innate personal ability of Diana or a function of the contract and Victor's connection to the World Trees.

### Relationships

#### Diane
Diane is [[Diana]]'s twin sister and Negative counterpart.[2]
The twins are closely synchronized. Diane immediately followed Diana's decision to abandon their old world order, joined her in the attempted execution of Ion, signed the Imperial contract alongside her and entered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul at the same time.[2]
Their Positive and Negative classifications represent complementary World Tree polarities rather than moral alignments.

#### Ion
Ion was [[Diana]]'s greatest and long-time confidant and served as the Leader of the Pantheon associated with her planet.[2]
Their relationship was old enough and apparently stable enough that Ion regarded voluntary betrayal as impossible. When Diana attacked him, he immediately assumed she was being controlled.
Diana discarded their previous relationship after absorbing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Energy. She critically wounded Ion, rejected him as her Champion and attempted to kill him before Rose intervened.[2]

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Diana]] became fixated upon [[Victor Elderblood]] without first requiring an ordinary personal relationship with him. Contact with his escaped Energy alone was sufficient to make her desire a direct connection.[2]
She regarded Victor as her new "planet" and Champion and considered his ability to protect the Galaxy superior to Ion's ability to protect their original world.[2]
Diana subsequently signed the Imperial contract and entered Victor's Soul.
The exact romantic status of Diana and Victor is not independently detailed during Diana's Chapter 1111 appearance. Chapter 1116 establishes that [[World Trees]] can choose to develop attraction despite their normally asexual reproductive role, but it does not provide a separate Diana-focused relationship scene.[1]
