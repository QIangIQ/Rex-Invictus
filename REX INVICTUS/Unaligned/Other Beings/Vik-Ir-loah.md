### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 1132
Anything our light cannot accept is evil. And evil must be eradicated.
—Vik-Ir-Loah to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Chapter 1132
Vik-Ir-Loah was the Commander of the Eleventh Frontline of the [[Veillon Empire]], more commonly described by its inhabitants as the Empire of Light. He was one of the first ranking officers encountered by the [[Elderblood Empire]] during Victor Elderblood's reconnaissance of the civilizations existing beyond his own Sector, commanding more than twenty thousand Light-aligned soldiers when Victor's independent scouting clone arrived within Veillon territory.[1]
Although initially appearing similar to the Empire's mass-produced soldiers, Vik-Ir was later revealed to be fundamentally different from them. Ordinary Veillon soldiers possessed no conventional race and were composed almost entirely from Light-based Energy with abnormal Souls that Victor considered incomplete products of a disrupted process of Divine development. Vik-Ir, by contrast, possessed an actual flesh-and-blood body beneath his armor. When Victor's Grandmaster attack destroyed the Commander's armor and wounded him severely, Vik-Ir spat blood before the surrounding Light began repairing both his body and portions of his damaged equipment.[1]
Vik-Ir nevertheless shared one of the most important characteristics of the soldiers serving Light: the erosion of meaningful personal freedom. Later investigation of the Empire's stellar infrastructure revealed that the Pure Positive Energy distributed throughout its territories gradually altered beings until even basic thought and action became dependent upon the Light. Victor explicitly described the Commander's personality as essentially that of a puppet, contrasting him with [[Solus 969]], whom both Victor and [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] recognized as possessing considerably greater individual depth and consciousness.[2]
As Commander of the Eleventh Frontline, Vik-Ir possessed authority over enormous numbers of soldiers, military vessels, battlefield countermeasure systems and communications with the Empire's higher military castes. He proved tactically competent enough to identify Victor's abilities, withdraw his soldiers when their current approach became ineffective and deploy specialized technology capable of suppressing extreme speed, Spatial movement and Reality manipulation. However, the difference between artificially supported combat power and Victor's Grandmaster experience became increasingly apparent as the battle progressed.[1][3]
Vik-Ir's ultimate fate was particularly ironic. After the Empire attempted to erase him to prevent Victor from extracting military information, Victor stopped the deletion process and directly corrupted the Commander's Soul. By combining Abyssal Miasma, [[Erebus]]' Darkness and Soul Manipulation, Victor inverted Vik-Ir from a Representative of Light into a Representative of Darkness. The same man who had declared everything rejected by the Light to be evil immediately knelt before Victor and called him "Master", demonstrating the extent to which his obedience was rooted in external control rather than independent conviction.[2]
Vik-Ir subsequently assisted Victor against his former comrades. His individual death was not separately depicted, but the entire Solar System was ultimately erased by the Empire's own distant superweapon after Victor completed his reconnaissance mission. As Vik-Ir remained within the battlefield after his corruption and the attack destroyed everything within the Solar System, he is considered deceased.[2]

### Physical Appearance
Vik-Ir-Loah initially appeared visually similar to the other warriors of the Empire of Light. He wore brilliant advanced armor associated with the golden Light permeating Veillon military forces and could survive and operate freely within the vacuum of space.[1]
The similarity was superficial. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] used a Grandmaster sword technique capable of cutting through the surrounding battlefield, the attack destroyed Vik-Ir's armor and severely wounded him. He coughed blood, revealing that unlike the surrounding soldiers—many of whom appeared to consist predominantly or entirely of Light—Vik-Ir possessed a genuine organic body beneath his equipment.[1]
Immediately afterward, golden Light began restoring his injuries while sections of his destroyed armor simultaneously reconstructed themselves around his body. The chapter does not clarify whether Vik-Ir was naturally born as an organic species and subsequently altered by the Empire, artificially constructed around a biological core, or converted from another race through prolonged exposure to Light. His exact race should therefore remain Unknown rather than being classified as Human or a Light Being without direct evidence.[1]
After Victor corrupted him in Chapter 1134, Vik-Ir's Light-aligned nature was forcibly inverted into its opposite. The narration identifies the transformed Commander as a Representative of Darkness, although no detailed physical description of this corrupted form is given beyond the change in his supernatural alignment.[2]

### Personality
Vik-Ir-Loah presented himself as calm, authoritarian and utterly convinced of the moral superiority of the [[Veillon Empire]]. His worldview was defined by a simple absolute: whatever the Empire's Light accepted was righteous, while anything that could not be accepted by that Light was Evil and therefore deserved eradication. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] questioned who possessed the right to define Good and Evil, Vik-Ir did not appeal to universal morality or independent principles; he simply answered that anything rejected by "our Light" was Evil.[1]
This conviction initially made Vik-Ir appear unusually composed. Victor deliberately attempted to provoke the Veillon soldiers through mockery and insults but received almost no emotional reaction until he insulted the Empire and its Emperor directly. Vik-Ir similarly showed little visible concern when individual subordinates were killed, treating the confrontation primarily as a problem requiring efficient elimination rather than a personal tragedy.[1]
Later information fundamentally changes how this apparent stoicism should be interpreted. Victor discovered that the Empire's Pure Positive Energy infrastructure gradually suppressed the free will of those incorporated into its system, eventually making even basic actions dependent upon Light's permission. When Victor later compared Vik-Ir with [[Solus 969]], he directly described the Commander and ordinary soldiers as puppets lacking genuine consciousness, while suggesting that 969 possessed something closer to a true independent conscience.[2]
Vik-Ir was nevertheless capable of tactical decision-making within the boundaries permitted by the system. He observed Victor's fighting methods rather than continuously throwing soldiers into ineffective attacks, ordered his troops to withdraw and disperse once enough information had been collected, activated countermeasures designed specifically for unusual abilities and escalated the Solar System's military alert when Victor proved more dangerous than expected.[1]
He was also extremely confident in the Empire's technological superiority. After deploying the suppression pillars around Victor, Vik-Ir openly explained that Speedsters, Space Gods, Reality Benders and even beings capable of moving at Light Speed were not unprecedented within the Upper Sectors. In his view, Victor was simply another unusual enemy whose abilities could be catalogued, suppressed and contained through the countermeasures developed by the Empire.[1]
That confidence became arrogance when Vik-Ir ordered Victor to surrender after the suppression field reduced several of his movement abilities. Victor responded by demonstrating the enormous difference between restricting supernatural abilities and overcoming a genuine Grandmaster. A single sword attack cut through the battlefield, killed more than five thousand soldiers, destroyed one of the suppression devices and left Vik-Ir coughing blood with his armor destroyed.[1]
Vik-Ir's final loss of autonomy exposed the weakness at the center of his ideology. When the Empire's artificial intelligence detected Victor corrupting his Soul, it immediately attempted to delete the "Commander program" rather than risk classified information falling into enemy hands. Vik-Ir's supposed individuality was treated by his own civilization as expendable software attached to military property.[2]
Once Victor stopped the deletion and inverted Vik-Ir's nature, the Commander immediately recognized Victor as his new Master. His loyalty therefore proved capable of being redirected almost instantaneously once the metaphysical Power controlling him changed, strongly supporting Victor's conclusion that Vik-Ir's obedience to Light had never represented genuine free choice.[2]

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Vik-Ir-Loah first encountered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] during the latter's investigation of the Upper Sectors. After speaking with The Owner of Limbo and realizing that unknown cosmic powers had begun observing his rapidly developing Empire, Victor refused to expose his real body, Soul, [[World Trees]], Eldritch connections or family to an unfamiliar civilization. Instead, he created an independent scouting clone composed almost entirely of Energy, severed from most dangerous metaphysical connections to the original. The clone possessed Victor's combat experience and modern Grandmaster skill while deliberately inheriting a younger, more arrogant and battle-hungry personality.[4]
The clone crossed into unfamiliar territory and immediately encountered a massive military presence belonging to the Empire of Light. The force was commanded by Vik-Ir-Loah, Commander of the Eleventh Frontline. Vik-Ir initially regarded the lone visitor as a potentially useful but inferior lifeform from a Lower Sector, noting that Victor possessed an unusual Bloodline and sufficiently powerful Divinity to survive naturally in space. When Victor mocked the Empire's claims of righteousness and questioned its definition of Evil, Vik-Ir explained the ideological principle underlying Veillon society: whatever their Light could not accept was Evil and therefore needed to be eradicated.[1]
Victor deliberately lied that he had slaughtered his own people and consumed his homeworld in order to determine how the soldiers would react. Vik-Ir accepted the story with disappointment rather than horror and ordered a subordinate to kill him while the remaining forces investigated whether anything useful could still be recovered from the supposedly destroyed civilization.[1]
Victor instantly killed the attacking soldier and consumed his head and Soul, acquiring limited information despite safeguards protecting Veillon personnel from memory extraction. Through those memories he discovered the identity of the commanding officer, learning that Vik-Ir was the Commander of the Eleventh Frontline and that their civilization was the Empire of Light, also associated with the name Veillon. Victor also discovered that ordinary soldiers possessed no identifiable race, consisted of pure Energy and carried abnormal Souls that appeared to have been interfered with during their development into Gods. He considered them incomplete Gods rather than proper natural [[Primordial Gods]], despite each ordinary soldier possessing enough raw Power to fight beings on that general level.[1]
Vik-Ir ordered his forces to attack. More than twenty thousand soldiers initially surrounded Victor, each using technologically advanced weapons and Light-based Energy. Victor reflected their combined ranged attacks before entering their formation and exploiting the three-dimensional environment of space, repeatedly using enemies as shields and killing soldiers faster than the formation could properly adjust.[1]
Rather than continue wasting troops, Vik-Ir eventually ordered the surviving forces to retreat and disperse once his systems had obtained enough combat information. The Empire's artificial intelligence had already identified Victor as possessing extreme speed, Spatial movement, Reality manipulation and the Divinity of War, classifying his body as a valuable research asset that should be captured rather than destroyed if possible.[1]
Vik-Ir then activated specialized countermeasures. Enormous pillars emerged through portals of Light and arranged themselves throughout the surrounding space while the Commander released golden Energy to activate the network. The resulting isolated zone interfered with several of Victor's abilities, reducing his speed and placing increasing pressure upon powers involving Lightning, Space and Reality manipulation. Victor's family immediately recognized the significance of the technology: unlike conventional seals requiring highly specific supernatural knowledge, the [[Veillon Empire]] possessed reusable technological-Divine systems built to suppress entire classes of problematic abilities.[1]
Vik-Ir confidently explained that Speedsters, Space Gods, Reality Benders and beings capable of moving at the speed of Light were nothing new within such a vast civilization. The major powers of the Upper Sectors had encountered similar enemies often enough to create technological countermeasures against them. He then ordered Victor to surrender, believing the suppression field had removed the advantages that made him dangerous.[1]
Victor answered by entering a Grandmaster stance. Vik-Ir sensed the danger and immediately covered his entire body with Power before shouting a warning to the surrounding soldiers. Victor's following sword swing cut everything in its direction, instantly killing more than five thousand Veillon soldiers and slicing through one of the suppression devices. The Empire replaced the destroyed device almost immediately, demonstrating enormous logistical redundancy, but Vik-Ir himself was caught in the attack. His armor was destroyed, he coughed blood and the battlefield finally revealed that the Commander possessed flesh beneath his Light-aligned equipment. Golden Light immediately began healing his injuries while simultaneously reconstructing portions of his armor.[1]
The Empire elevated Victor's criminal classification, placed the entire Solar System on alert and deployed enormous futuristic warships with Light-based engines. The system simultaneously recognized the true nature of its opponent and warned Vik-Ir that he had entered combat with a Grandmaster. Victor responded by mocking the Commander's demand for surrender and telling him that Vik-Ir had fundamentally misunderstood their circumstances: Victor was not trapped with the Empire; the Empire was trapped with him.[1]
The confrontation continued as the Veillon Empire progressively escalated its forces. Victor's wives observed that although the ordinary soldiers possessed extraordinary raw Power and extremely advanced technology, their fighting styles were repetitive, their personalities lacked individuality and their abilities possessed far less conceptual depth than the naturally developed Gods of their own civilization. Even an intentionally weakened clone of Victor was able to exploit those flaws because thousands of years of accumulated warfare, Grandmaster martial knowledge and genuine battlefield experience could not be reproduced solely by increasing numerical strength.[3]
Vik-Ir remained an important part of the defensive command as increasingly dangerous resources were committed to the battle. The Empire eventually called upon the Solus, its specialized elite order whose members stood significantly above ordinary military forces. [[Solus 969|Number 969]] responded to Vik-Ir's request and entered the battlefield, immediately demonstrating far greater Power, combat awareness and individual depth than the common soldiers.[2]
After exchanging attacks with Victor, 969 recognized that ordinary troops could no longer safely contribute and ordered them withdrawn. Vik-Ir obeyed and attempted to retreat, only for another Victor to appear directly behind him. The Empire immediately detected cloning and Umbrakinesis and activated anti-cloning technology, but the countermeasure failed because the system possessed insufficient information about Victor's specific technique.[2]
Victor seized Vik-Ir before he could escape and began interfering directly with his Soul. The Empire's artificial intelligence instantly detected the corruption and initiated multiple countermeasures, all of which failed. Realizing that classified information might be extracted, the system changed priorities and attempted to delete the Commander program, revealing that even a ranking officer such as Vik-Ir could be discarded the moment his continued existence became a security liability.[2]
Victor broke Vik-Ir's helmet, prevented the deletion through Soul Manipulation and deliberately attacked the metaphysical foundation connecting him to Light. He mocked the Empire's obsessive worship of Light and argued that Light possessed meaning only because Darkness also existed. Using the Miasma of the Abyss, [[Erebus]]' Darkness and his authority over Souls, Victor "painted" the Light-aligned portions of Vik-Ir's existence with their opposite attribute until the Commander was completely corrupted.[2]
When the process ended, the former Representative of Light had become a Representative of Darkness. Vik-Ir immediately knelt before Victor and addressed him as "Master". Victor used the transformation to illustrate his criticism of Veillon society to 969, pointing out that the Commander and ordinary soldiers were essentially puppets whose allegiance could be rewritten once the Power governing their existence was replaced. Victor specifically contrasted them with 969, whom he suspected possessed an actual conscience.[2]
Vik-Ir then fought on Victor's side. While Victor's main body occupied Solus 969, the corrupted Commander supported the additional Energy clones Victor released across the battlefield. Their combined assault pushed the Veillon forces increasingly toward collapse while another Victor clone travelled directly toward the star housing the Empire's central local infrastructure.[2]
The clone infiltrated the stellar installation and discovered an enormous sphere composed of Pure Positive Energy. Victor and his family realized that these objects functioned as local extensions of Light's Power, allowing the Emperor's influence to spread throughout entire Solar Systems. More disturbing was the system's effect upon living beings: prolonged integration with the Light gradually stripped away free will until individuals became dependent upon it even for basic thought and action. This discovery retroactively explained much of Vik-Ir's behavior and why Victor considered the Commander fundamentally less independent than Solus 969.[2]
After stealing the installation's information and transmitting it safely back to the original Victor, the clone destroyed the Positive Energy source. The effect was immediate: active systems throughout the Solar System shut down, the suppression technology ceased functioning, attempts to destabilize the star failed and the Light surrounding ordinary soldiers visibly weakened. The Empire's enormous military presence had therefore depended upon a centralized infrastructure ultimately connected to Light.[2]
Victor subsequently ended the reconnaissance battle, killing and absorbing Solus 969 before discovering through his memories that a colossal Veillon fortress far beyond the Solar System was already preparing a retaliatory strike. The structure fired weapons capable of erasing the complete Solar System, destroying Victor's scouting clone and everything remaining within the region.[2]
Vik-Ir's individual final moments are not separately described after his corruption. However, he remained within the battlefield as Victor's newly converted subordinate when the Solar System was destroyed. With no later appearance or indication of escape, and with later records treating him as deceased, his death is best attributed to the destruction of the Solar System rather than to Victor personally killing him.
The encounter nevertheless gave the [[Elderblood Empire]] exactly what Victor wanted. The information obtained from Vik-Ir's forces, Veillon technology, the Positive Energy infrastructure and Solus 969's memories proved that civilizations beyond Victor's Mid-Level Sector possessed immense Divine-industrial capacity, large populations of Primordial God-level soldiers, ability-suppression technology and weapons capable of casually erasing complete Solar Systems. These discoveries directly influenced Victor's preparations for the Upper-Sector powers introduced immediately afterward, particularly Light, Darkness, Emperor and Law.[5]

### Skills and Abilities
Vik-Ir-Loah was substantially more developed than an ordinary Veillon soldier and possessed command authority over the Eleventh Frontline. However, the novel does not establish that he was a Solus, a Grandmaster, a naturally evolved Primordial God or a conventional member of any known race. His confirmed abilities are therefore restricted to what was directly demonstrated during Chapters 1132–1134.
- Light-Aligned Existence: Vik-Ir was heavily integrated with the Light permeating the [[Veillon Empire]]. Although he retained an organic body, his Power, armor, regeneration and military systems all operated through the Empire's Light-based infrastructure.[1][2]
- Representative of Light (Formerly): [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly refers to Vik-Ir after his corruption as the man who had previously been a Representative of Light, establishing that his existence functioned as one of Light's representatives within the Empire.[2]
- Light Manipulation: Vik-Ir could release substantial quantities of golden Light Energy from his body. He used this Energy when activating the suppression network and destroying the Ice structures Victor created around the battlefield.[1]
- Light Empowerment: The Light flowing through Vik-Ir enhanced and sustained both his body and equipment.[1]
- Regeneration: After Victor's Grandmaster attack destroyed Vik-Ir's armor and injured his organic body badly enough to make him cough blood, golden Light immediately began healing his wounds.[1]
- Armor Reconstruction: The same Light simultaneously began restoring parts of his destroyed armor, demonstrating direct integration between his supernatural Power and Veillon equipment.[1]
- Enhanced Durability: Vik-Ir survived Victor's massive sword attack despite the same strike instantly killing more than five thousand surrounding soldiers and cutting through one of the Empire's suppression devices.[1] This establishes significantly greater survivability than the ordinary forces under his command, although it should not be interpreted as immunity to Grandmaster-level attacks.
- Space Survival: Vik-Ir operated normally within open space throughout the conflict without any visible need for conventional life-support equipment.[1]
- Enhanced Senses: As a high-ranking Empire combatant integrated into its military systems, Vik-Ir could follow Victor's movements sufficiently well to coordinate responses and detect imminent danger before Victor's Grandmaster slash.[1]
- Military Command: Vik-Ir commanded the Eleventh Frontline and possessed authority over tens of thousands of Veillon soldiers and the associated military resources within the Solar System.[1]
- Formation Control: He could order thousands of soldiers to simultaneously attack, withdraw, disperse or regroup as battlefield conditions changed.[1]
- Threat Escalation: Vik-Ir coordinated with the Empire's larger military system as Victor's threat classification increased and reinforcements entered the Solar System.[1]
- Elite Reinforcement Request: His position allowed him to request assistance from the Solus, resulting in [[Solus 969]] responding directly to the battlefield.[2]
- Tactical Analysis: Vik-Ir did not continue using failed methods indefinitely. Once Victor had killed enough soldiers for the Empire's systems to analyze his behavior, Vik-Ir ordered the army to retreat and activated technology specifically designed to counter the abilities identified during the battle.[1]
- Empire Combat Analysis System: Vik-Ir's armor and command network communicated continuously with an artificial intelligence capable of analyzing enemies and identifying supernatural abilities.[1]
- Ability Detection: The system identified Victor as possessing extreme speed, Spatial manipulation, Reality manipulation and the Divinity of War.[1]
- Threat Classification: It continually updated Victor's danger and criminal classifications based upon his actions and eventually identified him as a Grandmaster.[1]
- Adaptive Countermeasure Selection: The Empire automatically selected and deployed systems intended to counter abilities it had identified.[1]
- Security Countermeasures: When Victor began corrupting Vik-Ir's Soul, the system attempted multiple defensive measures and ultimately tried to delete the Commander entirely to prevent classified information from being compromised.[2]
- Darkness-Aligned Existence (After Corruption): Victor forcibly transformed Vik-Ir from a Representative of Light into a Representative of Darkness through the combination of Abyssal Miasma, [[Erebus]]' Darkness and Soul Manipulation.[2]
- Forced Allegiance Conversion: Once corrupted, Vik-Ir immediately recognized Victor as his Master and began supporting him against the Empire.[2]
- Corrupted Light Nature: Victor directly altered the Light-aligned portions of Vik-Ir's existence into their opposing attribute rather than merely convincing him to defect psychologically.[2]

### Equipment and Command Systems
The [[Veillon Empire]] relied heavily upon a fusion of Divinity and advanced technology. Vik-Ir possessed command access to several systems during the battle, but these devices belonged to the Empire rather than being innate personal abilities.
- Veillon Combat Armor: Vik-Ir wore technologically advanced Light-aligned armor capable of operating in space and integrating directly with the Empire's support network. After being damaged, portions of the suit reconstructed themselves through the same Light restoring Vik-Ir's body.[1]
- Ability-Suppression Pillars: Massive pillars combining unknown technology and Divinity were deployed through portals around [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Once activated by Vik-Ir's golden Energy, they created an isolated region that interfered with extreme speed, Spatial movement and Reality manipulation.[1]
- Power Suppression: Victor immediately felt himself becoming unnaturally slower inside the field, with Lightning-based acceleration producing an even stronger sensation of restriction.[1]
- Spatial Suppression: The devices interfered with Victor's ability to freely move through Space.[1]
- Reality Manipulation Suppression: Reality Bending was among the abilities the system had been designed to restrain.[1]
- Automatic Replacement: When Victor destroyed one pillar with his sword attack, another replacement appeared almost immediately, revealing the Empire's enormous logistical preparation.[1]
- Veillon Warships: Once Victor's threat level rose, several massive futuristic military vessels entered the Solar System. Their engines and systems were constructed around the Empire's Light technology.[1]
- Reinforcement Network: Vik-Ir could call for additional military support through the Empire's interstellar infrastructure, including forces significantly more dangerous than his original soldiers.[1][2]
- Stellar Infrastructure: The Eleventh Frontline operated within a Solar System containing a central Pure Positive Energy source embedded near its star. The infrastructure powered the Empire's active systems throughout the region and allowed the civilization to interfere with the star itself.[2] The system belonged to the wider Empire rather than Vik-Ir personally and should not be listed as an innate ability.

### Relationships

#### Light
Light was Vik-Ir-Loah's Emperor, source of authority and the central object around which his worldview had been constructed.
Vik-Ir believed the Empire's Light possessed the right to define Good and Evil throughout Reality, declaring that anything rejected by it was automatically Evil and therefore needed to be destroyed.[1]
Later discoveries cast this loyalty in a significantly darker context. The Positive Energy infrastructure maintained throughout the Empire gradually eroded the free will of beings connected to it until even basic independent actions could become impossible without the Light's permission. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] consequently regarded Vik-Ir less as a willing religious follower and more as an extension of Light's [[Will Solace|Will]].[2]
The Empire's willingness to erase Vik-Ir once his Soul became compromised further demonstrated the utilitarian nature of the relationship. Its artificial intelligence identified him as "Empire property" and attempted to delete the Commander rather than risk losing information to Victor.[2]

#### Victor
Vik-Ir initially regarded [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] as an inferior outsider from a Lower Sector and ordered him eliminated once he classified his supposed actions as Evil.[1]
Victor rapidly dismantled this assumption by killing the soldiers surrounding him, reflecting their attacks and exploiting the limitations of their standardized fighting methods. Vik-Ir responded intelligently by analyzing Victor rather than relying entirely upon brute force and successfully deployed technology capable of suppressing several of Victor's abilities.[1]
The Commander's confidence collapsed once Victor demonstrated the qualitative difference created by true Grandmaster skill. A single serious attack killed thousands of soldiers, destroyed one of the supposedly controlling devices and severely injured Vik-Ir himself.[1]
Their relationship ultimately became one of forced master and servant. Victor prevented the Veillon systems from deleting Vik-Ir, corrupted his Soul and inverted his Light-based existence into Darkness. Vik-Ir immediately knelt and recognized Victor as his Master before assisting him against his former allies.[2]

#### Solus 969
Vik-Ir requested assistance from the Solus after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s continued escalation overwhelmed the conventional forces of the Eleventh Frontline. [[Solus 969|Number 969]] answered the call and treated Vik-Ir as the ranking local Commander responsible for explaining the battlefield situation.[2]
Their differences illustrate the internal hierarchy of the Empire of Light. Vik-Ir possessed substantial military authority and access to enormous resources, but 969 stood on an entirely different individual combat level and possessed far greater personal depth. After assessing Victor directly, 969 ordered Vik-Ir and the standard forces to withdraw because the enemy had become too dangerous for them to handle.[2]
969 was also horrified when Victor corrupted Vik-Ir, particularly because the transformation demonstrated that the Empire's Representatives of Light could be forcibly inverted by an outsider.[2]

### Current Status
Vik-Ir-Loah is considered deceased, but his exact individual moment of death was not shown.
The last direct depiction of him shows Vik-Ir successfully corrupted into a Representative of Darkness and supporting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s forces against the Empire. The final Veillon response subsequently erased the complete Solar System, including Victor's clone and everything remaining within it.[2]
No chapter depicts Vik-Ir escaping before the attack, and later records classify him as dead. His cause of death should therefore be described as the Solar System's destruction rather than incorrectly stating that Victor directly killed him.
