Elyndra is the Throne of Reality and one of the [[Seven Black Thrones]] of the [[Black Eclipse Pantheon]], in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]]. The seat stands under <Darkness>, the Great Darkness, whom [[Victor Elderblood]] addresses as Vharzath, and above the Ten Chief Gods. The charge is maintenance. Elyndra is responsible for the reality of all the hostile galaxies.[1][2]
The name comes out of what Lily took from [[Vaelthar]] and the nine Chief Gods. No body, armor, or face is given, and no pronoun is attached to it. There is no spoken line.[1]

### The Office
What the seat holds up is the reality those galaxies already have. The duty is to maintain it, across all of them. [[Aetherys]] keeps their time and shifts it on command. [[Oryndal]] is the bridge of distance and dimension between them. Elyndra is the third hold on the same conquest: the reality the clock and the bridge are set into. No weapon and no private showing of that maintenance are given.[1]
The work is not a rewriting of the universal laws. Jeanne sets that limit on the whole design. Those laws are too strong, and a fundamental change takes Negativity and Positivity. Maintenance sits under that limit. It keeps a reality. It is not the act of founding a different one.[1]
The same title is used elsewhere, and the two are not identified as one office. Aurethys, the [[Aurethys Vaelthion|Emperor of Light]], will not move in a rush merely because a place upon the Throne of Reality has offered itself. That place is something he might take. Elyndra's seat is already filled. It answers to [[Vharzath]], and the galaxies it maintains are the hostile ones.[3][1]
The office is not a Chief God's title. Those places are bestowed, blessed up until the holder can threaten a galaxy, and filled again when the holder falls. A Throne is not replaced that way. Black Eclipse has seven beings who threaten a galaxy in their own right, and the seven are these seats. Lily's judgment on the order leaves [[Vaelthar]] as the experiment, made for a Chosen of Negativity and not chosen. The others are much more stable, and they are the true elites of Darkness. Elyndra is one of those others.[4][5][1]

### Among the Seven
Velnorah reads the seven titles as an attempt at a self-contained universe, with [[Vaelthar]]'s Beginning as the starting point. Ruby will not leave them as thrones inside that design. Taken as potential Primordial Beings of a new universe, they cover almost every essential function of a stable cosmos. Jeanne counts the functions still absent. Life, death, and infinity. Reality is not among them. The reading is the wives'. It is not a System rank. Elyndra is not entered as a Primordial God. The name [[Scathach]] calls arrogant is [[Chaos]], and that seat is [[Zhaeroth]]'s.[1]
Vaelthar is the only Throne who has fought [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. A projection broke him. What remained was emptied, and the body was ordered burned. [[Xytherion]] is the right hand, the deputy, and the supreme commander, and the one sent to deploy the [[Nihilvore]]. This seat is given neither act.[6][5][7][1]
When the imperial offensive was taking the conquered galaxies apart, [[Vharzath]] loosed two of the six who remained and told them to put their own safety first. Two answered. They are not named. Elyndra is not identified as either of them.[8]
