Ken is an Alpha and one of the surviving members of [[Nightingale]]'s original native population. He is the eldest son of an unnamed Alpha who once ruled a native village and the older brother of Kal. Following his father's death at the hands of [[Rose Adrasteia Elderblood]], Ken inherited leadership of their settlement according to the customs of his people and began preparing Kal as his own successor.[1][2]
Ken initially appeared as one of the native commanders responsible for controlling the increasingly powerful Monsters deployed against Clan Adrasteia. He possessed enough authorization to release Type 4 Predators, Type 5 [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] and a Type 6 [[Destroyer]], although the village's Type 7 unit required Commander-level authority beyond his own access at the time.[1][2]
Centuries later in the story, Ken remained alive and had become a considerably more mature leader. During the final war against the [[Elder Gods Pantheon]], he was shown protecting Kal, directing other Alphas to evacuate their families and personally preparing to enter the conflict.[3] After [[Victor Elderblood]] defeated the Elder Gods, Ken emerged as the principal defender of the approximately 1,700 surviving Nightingale natives. Despite being terrified by Victor's existence, he stood between the new Dragon God and his people and charged him with a spear, an act of courage that deeply impressed Victor's newly awakened Divinity of Martial Honor.[4]
Victor healed Ken after his own overwhelming passive pressure nearly killed him and placed the surviving native population under the supervision of Rose Adrasteia Elderblood and [[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]], allowing the faction that had suffered most from the ancient Monster war to determine how the surviving natives would be treated.[4]

### Physical Appearance
Ken's physical appearance has never been described in sufficient detail to establish a canonical design.
He is an adult male member of [[Nightingale]]'s original native population and possesses a humanoid body. During his Chapter 886 appearance he wielded a spear while confronting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[4]

### Personality
Ken is fiercely protective, proud, responsible and strongly devoted to his family and people. Although his early appearances show considerable hatred toward the Vampire invaders, his anger is repeatedly tempered by his sense of responsibility as an older brother and leader.
Ken's first major emotional crisis occurred when he witnessed [[Rose Adrasteia Elderblood]] kill his father. Enraged, he began preparing to release the village's Type 7 Monster despite lacking the Commander-level authorization required to do so and knowing that he would be breaking their rules.[2]
Kal's arrival stopped him.
Ken's demeanor immediately softened when he saw his younger brother. He initially attempted to hide their father's death from Kal, but after remembering that Kal was already 418 years old and considered an adult by their people, Ken decided that shielding him from reality would no longer be appropriate.[2]
Despite the rage he felt toward Clan Adrasteia, Ken forced himself to calm down because he understood that anger alone would accomplish nothing. His father's death had made him the village's leader, giving him responsibilities that now took precedence over personal grief.[2]
This sense of duty became increasingly prominent with age. During the Elder Gods' final war, Ken immediately protected Kal when Divine-level attacks began shaking the region, even though the attack was not actually directed toward them. He then recognized that their village was becoming part of an active battlefield and ordered the other Alphas to evacuate their families.[3]
Ken himself intended to remain and fight.
When Kal questioned the decision, Ken reminded him of the reason he had spent years training his younger brother. Kal had been prepared as Ken's successor so that their people would still possess a leader if Ken died.[3]
His strongest display of courage occurred after the Elder Gods were defeated. Ken was visibly trembling in terror before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and the collection of Gods, Dragons and supernatural beings surrounding him, yet he refused to move away from the entrance protecting his people.[4]
When Victor approached, Ken repeatedly warned him to stop.
Victor ignored him.
Ken attacked.
The effort was completely futile. His spear shattered before touching Victor and the passive pressure generated by Victor's impossibly dense new body caused Ken to cough blood and collapse.[4]
Victor nevertheless respected him immensely for trying.
To Victor, the difference in strength was irrelevant. Ken knew he could not win and still placed himself between an incomprehensibly superior Being and his people. Victor's Divinity of Martial Honor reacted naturally to that courage.[4]

### Background
Ken was born among [[Nightingale]]'s original native civilization long before the truth concerning the [[Elder Gods Pantheon]] and the Monsters became known to the Vampires.
His father was an Alpha and leader of their village, making Ken the eldest son within a hereditary leadership structure.
The title Alpha should not be treated as Ken's biological species. Chapter 886 clarifies that among the surviving native population, adult men and family leaders could receive the title of Alpha and gain the authority to command Monsters.[4]
Earlier Vampire knowledge had incorrectly grouped Alphas together with the Monsters they commanded. In reality, the situation was more complicated. The Elder Gods had experimented extensively upon Nightingale's native population, using native biology, Divine abilities and Noble Vampire material to create many of the Monster strains that later fought Clan Adrasteia.[4]
Ken grew up within this system and consequently viewed the Vampires of Nightingale as invaders threatening his people.
By the time Clan Adrasteia's forces crossed deep into the native territories, Ken was already trusted with access to high-level Monster containment and deployment systems.

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Ken first appeared indirectly as the eldest son of the Alpha leading one of [[Nightingale]]'s native settlements. While [[Rose Adrasteia Elderblood]], [[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]], [[Victor Elderblood]] and the Adrasteia warriors pushed deeper into the wilderness beyond Warfall, the native forces began deploying increasingly dangerous Monsters against them.[5]
From inside the village, Ken monitored the conflict through a strange technological control system combining advanced and archaic aesthetics. When the invading Vampires continued approaching despite earlier traps, Ken concluded that he had no choice but to escalate the village's response.[1]
He authorized the release of three increasingly dangerous Monster classes:

Their father's death therefore made Ken the new leader.
He immediately told Kal that he would begin preparing him as the next successor in case Ken also died.[2]
Although Kal was confused by how abruptly his life had changed, Ken promised that his younger brother would soon learn the true capabilities of their enemies.

#### King Among Kings
Ken and Kal survived the earlier conflict and remained among the native population when the ancient war over [[Nightingale]] finally escalated into open confrontation between the [[Elder Gods Pantheon]], the Vampires and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s multinational supernatural alliance.
During the battle in Chapter 878, Ken and Kal watched [[Ikor-Kar-VI]], whom they regarded as their God, empower the native population. The blessing dramatically strengthened their bodies, demonstrating the Elder Gods' direct influence over the Alphas and their settlements.[3]
Their attention was soon drawn toward the sky as Victor entered the battlefield in his Draconic form and began fighting Ikor. The scale of the confrontation was completely beyond anything the Alphas had previously experienced.
When Victor unleashed an enormous Dragon Breath toward Ikor, Ken instinctively threw himself over Kal to protect him even though the attack was not actually aimed toward their location.[3]
The brothers subsequently watched Victor and Ikor exchange abilities on a scale that left the surrounding Alphas questioning whether this was truly how Gods fought.
Ken quickly understood the danger.
Once it became clear that their village was about to become part of the battlefield, he ordered the nearby Alphas to gather their families and evacuate.[3]
Ken then told Kal to accompany him.
Kal asked whether Ken intended to participate directly in the war.
Ken answered:
Yes.
When Kal attempted to object, Ken reminded him why he had spent years preparing him.
Kal had been trained so that the native population would still possess a successor if Ken died.[3]
The brothers subsequently moved to perform their responsibilities as the war intensified.
The conflict eventually revealed the true nature of the relationship between the Elder Gods, the Monsters and Nightingale's native population. The Alphas and common natives had not been the true architects of the biological horrors that had plagued Clan Adrasteia for millennia. Ikor-Kar-VI and the Elder Gods had used their own people as experimental material, combining native biology with Divine abilities and Noble Vampire characteristics to create many of the Monster strains used throughout the war.[4]
Ikor's Predation also allowed him to consume useful traits that emerged among the native population, meaning members of his own civilization could effectively become resources whenever they developed something he desired.[4]
By the time the Elder Gods were defeated, the consequences were catastrophic.
Only approximately 1,700 members of the original native population remained hidden underground. Most were not warriors but ordinary civilians: children, babies, women, elders and families attempting to survive the destruction above them.[4]
Ken stood at their entrance.
When Victor opened the earth surrounding the hidden settlement, screams erupted among the civilians. Ken immediately stepped forward and demanded to know why the outsiders had come.[4]
A woman attempted to stop him.
Ken ordered her to remain silent and continued standing between the strangers and his people despite his body visibly trembling in fear.
Rose initially wanted the surviving natives exterminated because of the immense losses Clan Adrasteia had suffered throughout the ancient war. Victor disagreed. Through Ikor's absorbed memories, he now understood that most of the survivors were innocent civilians and victims of the Elder Gods rather than the beings responsible for the experiments.[4]
Victor explained that the Monster species were experiments produced from the Elder Gods' work involving their Divinities, the natives and Noble Vampires.
He also clarified the actual social structure surrounding the Alphas: adult males and heads of families could receive the title, allowing them to command Monsters during the war.[4]
Victor then approached Ken.
Ken warned him not to come closer.
Victor continued.
Ken repeated the warning and finally attacked with his spear.
The weapon never touched Victor.
Victor's newly evolved humanoid Dragon body possessed such extreme density and spiritual weight that it naturally produced invisible pressure around him. Ken's spear shattered against that pressure before reaching Victor's body, and the backlash caused Ken to cough blood as injuries spread throughout him.[4]
[[Thor]] explained that Victor's body had become so dense and his Aura so heavy that sufficiently weak beings could no longer physically approach him without suffering damage.
Ken collapsed.
Victor looked at the dying Alpha and praised him.
“Your pride is something noble. Few people can stand before me to defend their people.”
Victor told Ken never to allow that pride to be broken.[4]
The words possessed supernatural weight.
Victor had only recently awakened as a God, and his speech now unconsciously carried his Divinities. His statement activated his Divinity of Martial Honor, causing the words to become deeply engraved within Ken.[4]
Victor then lifted Ken's body, touched him with his Draconic hand and healed every injury as though they had never occurred.
After returning the unconscious Ken to the ground, Victor made his decision regarding the survivors.
The native population would not be exterminated.
Instead, he placed them under the supervision of Clan Adrasteia, specifically giving [[Rose Adrasteia Elderblood]] and [[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]] authority to determine their future because their Clan had suffered more than anyone else from the ancient conflict with the Monsters.[4]
Ken therefore survived the final destruction of the Elder Gods and remained with the last known population of Nightingale's original native civilization.
No later chapter has provided a substantial update concerning his individual activities.

### Skills & Abilities
Ken is an Alpha of [[Nightingale]]'s native population. The Alpha designation is primarily a leadership and command title rather than a confirmed separate species.[4]
His individual supernatural abilities have received relatively little direct explanation.

#### Nightingale Native Physiology
As one of [[Nightingale]]'s original supernatural natives, Ken possesses physical capabilities beyond those of ordinary Humans.
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Ken possesses supernatural physical strength, endurance and durability as a native Alpha.
- Enhanced Longevity: Ken is the older brother of Kal, who was already 418 years old when considered an adult, demonstrating that their people possess extraordinarily long lifespans.[2]
- Supernatural Physiology: Ken belongs to a non-Human native population capable of surviving within Nightingale's supernatural environment.
- Enhanced Endurance: Ken remained conscious long enough to confront [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] despite the overwhelming pressure surrounding the newly ascended Dragon God.[4]
The novel does not directly establish that unmodified Alphas possess the extreme immortality or resurrection demonstrated by several engineered Monster types.

#### Alpha Authority
The title Alpha is granted to adult male leaders and heads of families among the [[Nightingale]] natives.[4]
Alphas possessed authority over the Monster forces used against Warfall and Clan Adrasteia.
Known applications include:
- Monster Command: Alphas could command the Monsters deployed against the Vampire territories.[4]
- Military Authority: Ken possessed authority over Monster deployments even before inheriting his father's village.
- Leadership Authority: Other Alphas obeyed Ken when he ordered them to evacuate their families during the Elder Gods' war.[3]
- Village Leadership: After his father's death, Ken inherited leadership according to native custom.[2]
The exact supernatural mechanism behind an Alpha's control of Monsters has not been fully explained. Ken is shown using technological control systems to deploy Monster units, while later chapters establish the Alphas themselves as recognized commanders of those creatures.

#### Spear Proficiency
Ken uses a spear as his personal weapon.
During Chapter 886 he immediately attacked [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] with the weapon when he believed the Dragon God was threatening his people.[4]
The spear shattered before touching Victor because of Victor's passive pressure.
Ken has not demonstrated enough actual combat with the weapon to classify him as a master spearman.

#### Leadership
Ken possesses substantial leadership ability developed after inheriting his father's village.
During the Elder Gods' war, he immediately recognized that the Divine battlefield would engulf the nearby settlement and ordered the other Alphas to evacuate their families.[3]
He also prepared Kal for succession and understood that preserving the continuity of leadership was more important than his own survival.[2][3]
Known leadership skills include:
- Crisis Management
- Civilian Evacuation
- Military Command
- Succession Planning
- Monster Force Coordination

#### Exceptional Courage
Ken's greatest demonstrated quality is courage.
Even while visibly terrified by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s presence, Ken remained standing between Victor and approximately 1,700 civilians.[4]
He understood that the beings before him were vastly stronger than himself but refused to retreat because doing so would leave his people undefended.
This display was impressive enough to directly resonate with Victor's Divinity of Martial Honor.

#### Martial Honor Imprint
After witnessing Ken's willingness to protect his people, [[Victor Elderblood]] told him:
“Do not let that pride be broken.”
[4]
Because Victor had recently become a God, his words unconsciously carried Divine Power. His statement activated the Divinity of Martial Honor and became deeply engraved within Ken.[4]
This can be treated as a form of:
- Divine Blessing / Conceptual Imprint (Granted by Victor)
- Martial Honor Empowerment (Potential)
The exact long-term effects upon Ken have not yet been demonstrated, so the blessing should not be assigned a detailed power set beyond what Chapter 886 establishes.

#### External Divine Empowerment
During the Elder Gods' final war, [[Ikor-Kar-VI]] raised his hand and strengthened the native Alphas and surrounding population to a level Ken had never experienced before.[3]
This was an external blessing from the Elder God rather than Ken's personal ability.

#### External Healing
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] completely healed Ken after his body was damaged by attempting to approach and attack him.[4]
This healing belongs to Victor and should not be indexed as Ken possessing Regeneration.

### Power
Ken's exact power level is unknown.
He was important enough within the native military structure to possess access to Type 4–6 Monster units before becoming village leader, but he was not authorized to release the Type 7 Monster without breaking their rules.[1][2]
By Chapter 878, he had matured into a leader capable of directing other Alphas and intended to personally participate in the Elder Gods' war.[3]
However, Chapter 886 demonstrates the enormous difference between Ken and higher supernatural beings.
He could not physically reach [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
The passive density and Aura surrounding Victor's newly ascended humanoid Dragon body shattered Ken's spear and caused Ken's own body to begin failing before Victor deliberately attacked him.[4]
Ken's significance therefore lies primarily in his leadership, Monster-command authority and courage rather than exceptional individual combat power.
No chapter establishes him as comparable to:
- Elder Vampires;
- [[Vampire Counts]];
- Gods;
- [[Combat Gods]];
- or Elder Gods.

### Relationships

#### Kal
Kal is Ken's younger brother and designated successor.
Ken is highly protective of him. When Kal entered the control room shortly after their father's death, Ken's otherwise furious expression immediately softened.[2]
Although he initially wanted to hide their father's death, Ken ultimately decided that Kal was already an adult and deserved to know the truth.
After inheriting leadership, Ken immediately told Kal that he would train him to become the village's next leader if Ken died.[2]
Years later, this training had clearly continued. During Chapter 878, Kal questioned whether Ken intended to fight in the Elder Gods' war, and Ken reminded him why he had been trained in the first place.[3]
Ken also instinctively threw himself over Kal when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s battle with Ikor began producing attacks on a Divine scale.[3]

#### Unknown Alpha
Ken and Kal's father was an unnamed Alpha who served as leader of their village.
Ken respected his father and was devastated when he watched [[Rose Adrasteia Elderblood]] kill him.[1][2]
His father's death caused Ken to nearly release the forbidden Type 7 Monster in a moment of rage.
Native succession law subsequently transferred leadership to Ken as the eldest son.[2]

#### Rose Adrasteia Elderblood
[[Rose Adrasteia Elderblood]] killed Ken's father during Clan Adrasteia's expedition through [[Nightingale]]'s wilderness.[1]
Ken initially developed intense hatred toward Rose and the [[Adrasteia Bloodline]] after witnessing the death.
Their later circumstances became considerably more complicated.
After learning that the surviving natives had themselves been exploited by the Elder Gods, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] refused Rose's initial desire to simply exterminate them. Instead, he placed Ken and the approximately 1,700 survivors under the supervision of Rose and Eleanor, reasoning that Clan Adrasteia had suffered the most from the ancient Monster war and therefore deserved authority over the natives' future.[4]
No direct later interaction between Ken and Rose has been shown.

#### Victor Elderblood
Ken originally knew [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] as Alucard, one of the powerful Vampire invaders threatening the native population.
During the Elder Gods' final war, Ken witnessed Victor fighting [[Ikor-Kar-VI]] on a scale that left the surrounding Alphas horrified.[3]
Their first meaningful personal interaction occurred after the Elder Gods' defeat.
When Victor discovered the surviving native population underground, Ken stood between him and the civilians and refused to move despite being visibly terrified.[4]
Ken eventually charged Victor with a spear.
Victor's passive pressure nearly killed him without Victor needing to counterattack.
Rather than viewing the attack as an insult, Victor admired Ken's courage and declared that his pride was noble. His Divinity of Martial Honor unconsciously infused the statement with Divine Power and left a conceptual impression upon Ken.[4]
Victor then completely healed him.
The encounter transformed Ken from someone Victor could have regarded as another enemy into a warrior whose courage he personally respected.

#### Ikor-Kar-VI
[[Ikor-Kar-VI]] was the leader of the Elder Gods and one of the beings Ken and the other Alphas regarded as their God.[3]
During the final war, Ikor directly strengthened the Alphas and native population through his Power.
However, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later learned the full extent of Ikor's crimes against his own people.
The Elder Gods had used the native population as experimental material, and Ikor's Predation allowed him to exploit useful abilities that appeared among them.[4]
Many of the Monsters commanded by Alphas were themselves products of this experimentation.
Ken and his people were therefore simultaneously followers, soldiers and victims of Ikor's civilization.
