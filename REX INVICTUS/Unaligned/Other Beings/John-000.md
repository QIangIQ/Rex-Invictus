John, designation John-000, is an Original Emperor's Soldier and the primary commander of the Spartan Legion, the First Legion of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. [[Victor Elderblood]] created him without a surname and raised him on the memories of past commanders, which John never mistook for his own life.[1][2] [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] marked him, together with [[Clare Bloodhunter]], as one of the two natural leaders among the seven Originals.[3] He later carried the Empire's principal offensive Legion from selective planetary work to the border war against The Black Eclipse, where he found the enemy concentration on the transit route and led the First Legion to victory on a field that had no super-elite.[4][5]

### Physical Appearance
John possesses a comparatively simple appearance when contrasted with several of the other original Emperor's Soldiers. He has black hair and black eyes, with a refined but otherwise relatively plain face. His physique falls between the extremes represented by [[Caelus Hunter]] and Conan; he is considerably broader and more muscular than Caelus, while lacking Conan's heavily built barbarian-like frame.[2]
Despite being only ten years old when he first emerged from his creation chamber, John already possessed a completely mature body and stood approximately two meters tall.[2] The bodies designed for the Emperor's Soldiers were originally constructed around a much larger Dragonoid frame and possess metamorphic capabilities derived from their Vampire characteristics, allowing them to alter their size when required. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] deliberately included this ability because permanently operating as four-meter-tall soldiers would make infiltration and other delicate assignments unnecessarily difficult.[1] By the time the Spartans were being committed to Imperial worlds, that larger frame was the ordinary one for their elites. Common soldiers of the Legion already stood easily above two meters. Elite soldiers, John and his Bloodhunter brothers named among them, measured between three and four meters.[6]
Like the other Imperial Soldiers, John normally wears advanced black Imperial equipment bearing dark violet and red details, with a Dragon crest displayed over the right side of the chest. His specialized combat armor can reform itself according to the requirements of its user. In John's preferred Hunter Mode, his head is enclosed by a completely black helmet marked by violet lines, while the armor adopts a configuration designed specifically for covert warfare and assassination.[3]

### Personality
John is calm, observant and strongly duty-oriented. Although [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] implanted within him the memories and experiences of numerous historical warriors, John never confused those experiences with his own identity. He understood from childhood that the memories were lessons deliberately provided by his Creator, allowing him to study wars, tactics, strategy and the decisions of past heroes without being psychologically overwhelmed by experiences that did not personally belong to him. Having effectively witnessed conventional wars, supernatural conflicts and apocalyptic battles before entering the world himself, John became unusually comfortable with violence and death without becoming needlessly bloodthirsty.[2]
This distinction is particularly important to John's character. He accepts killing as a natural reality of warfare, yet he dislikes innocent people becoming casualties of conflicts between greater powers. Even before receiving practical battlefield experience, he resolved that he would protect such people whenever possible, provided doing so did not compromise his duty. This combination of discipline and empathy later became one of the reasons [[Scathach]] recognized him as a natural leader.[2][3]
John is also highly analytical and inquisitive. When Victor introduced the seven Originals to the greatest instructors available to the Empire, John did not simply accept the privilege without question; he asked why such enormous resources were being invested in them. During his first practical combat test, while some of his siblings immediately entered the battlefield, John instead examined every piece of equipment he had been given and attempted to understand the capabilities of his armor before acting. He quickly determined that the armor and its integrated artificial intelligence were considerably more important than the obvious sword and plasma firearm accompanying it.[7][3]
Despite his caution, John is capable of abandoning a carefully formulated strategy when circumstances demand immediate action. During his first test within the Nightmare Tower, his original intention was to observe the battlefield before choosing his approach. Upon seeing a child about to be killed, however, he immediately crossed the distance, killed the monster and ordered the child toward safety. Scathach openly praised this reaction and concluded that John possessed genuine heart in addition to intelligence and combat ability.[3]
John's loyalty to Victor eventually matured into profound Faith in the God Emperor. However, his devotion does not prevent independent judgment. During later Imperial operations he demonstrated restraint regarding civilians, identified potentially useful survivors for future recruitment, demanded precise orbital strikes instead of unnecessary widespread destruction and prioritized the lives of his soldiers when an unexpected enemy appeared. He therefore embodies Victor's intention for his commanders: soldiers absolutely loyal to the Empire while still possessing enough intelligence, initiative and judgment to operate without constant supervision.[8] That same judgment is what he used on the Black Eclipse border. After a defended world was broken under the guns of his fleet, he did not spend the Legion repeating that destruction. He ordered the living worlds that had no defense taken first.[5]

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
John's existence began as part of [[Victor Elderblood]]'s attempt to create a military force personally belonging to the Emperor rather than depending upon the private armies and followers of his wives. After studying the problems surrounding conventional cloning, Victor determined that ordinary beings could not safely endure meaningful portions of his Soul because they lacked sufficient compatibility with his existence. Rather than producing massive numbers of merely competent soldiers, Victor chose to pursue the more expensive and difficult alternative: a smaller number of elite soldiers designed to become living weapons capable of growing alongside his future Empire.[1]
To create these soldiers, Victor constructed a new hybrid race using the body of a Dragonoid as its foundation. He combined Human adaptability and ingenuity, Angelic obedience and kindness, Demonic aggression and cruelty, the spiritual qualities of Faeries and their connection with Nature, Vampire regeneration and metamorphosis, Divine Blessings associated with combat and a minute fragment of his own Soul. The result was a stable chimera whose many racial characteristics were fused together by Victor's Essence, causing outsiders to primarily perceive them as Dragonoids while concealing the far more complicated nature of their existence. Victor designated the resulting race the Emperor's Soldiers.[1]
John was among the first two Souls personally placed into the creation chambers by Victor. Unlike [[Clare Bloodhunter]], whose name followed the more elaborate naming convention Victor was considering for his soldiers, Victor deliberately gave John no surname. The creation system therefore registered him as John-000. The numerical suffix was designed to increase whenever John died and was recreated, functioning simultaneously as a record of his deaths, reincarnations, service and accumulated honor before the Emperor. John became one of seven Originals alongside Clare Bloodhunter, [[Laura Bloodhunter]], [[Kiana Bloodhunter]], Conan, [[Kaleb Hunter]] and [[Caelus Hunter]], whose genetic templates and accumulated experiences would later serve as foundations for the larger military population.[1]
John spent approximately ten subjective years developing before leaving his chamber. Throughout that period Victor remained present within his perception, teaching and preparing him while exposing him to the memories of historical heroes and warriors. John experienced the strategies, tactics and lessons of conflicts ranging from national and world wars to supernatural and apocalyptic battles, emerging with an enormous theoretical knowledge of warfare despite never having personally fought. His first conscious understanding of Victor was consequently not merely as Emperor, but as his Father, Creator and the being responsible for giving his life purpose.[2]
When the seven Originals finally awakened, they instinctively recognized Victor and immediately knelt before him. Victor warned them that inheriting the memories of heroes did not automatically make them heroes and that knowledge without practical development was insufficient. He therefore brought the seven before four extraordinary instructors: Hassan-i Sabbah, who would teach assassination and methods of taking life; [[Julius Caesar]], who would teach politics and rulership; [[Abe-no-Seimei]], who would teach philosophy and wisdom; and [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], Victor's former master and the Teacher of Heroes, who would develop their combat talents.[2][7]
John questioned why Victor was willing to expend such extraordinary resources on seven newly created soldiers. Victor explained that mediocrity among his subordinates was one of the things he despised most and that the seven were not disposable servants. They were his creations, his children, his spear and shield, and the soldiers through whom his [[Will Solace|Will]] would eventually be exercised. Victor then revealed his intended future for them: John, Clare, Kiana, Laura, Conan, Kaleb and Caelus would become the first seven Imperial Commanders, with seven future Legions eventually growing from their experiences while they operated beneath [[Scathach]]'s command.[7]
Their first major practical evaluation occurred within the Elderblood family's personal Nightmare Tower. Scathach separated each Original into an individual version of the same simulated conflict involving two warring Empires and an interdimensional invasion. Their objective was not simply to survive but to pacify both civilizations and eliminate the invaders using only advanced Imperial armor, a plasma pistol and a sword forged from materials taken from the Miasma-rich depths of Hell. The exercise was designed to reveal their instinctive methods of warfare so that Scathach could determine how each should subsequently be trained.[3]
Unlike several of his siblings, John did not immediately rush into combat. He first examined his weapons and then turned his attention to the armor, discovering an integrated artificial intelligence designated Akasha-001, a subsystem based upon [[Akasha Elderblood]]. After requesting equipment suitable for eliminating enemies covertly, the armor activated Hunter Mode. His standard sword separated into two daggers, his plasma pistol reconfigured into a plasma bow and his helmet activated a battlefield H.U.D. John initially intended to observe the battlefield and gather information before committing himself, but abandoned that approach when he saw a child about to be killed. He moved with such speed that he resembled a violet shadow, bisected the attacking creature and directed the child toward a safe route. Scathach, observing from afar, praised his heart and considered John together with Clare to be the two natural leaders among the seven Originals.[3]
John eventually matured from a promising trainee into an operational commander of the growing [[Elderblood Empire]]. During an expedition to another inhabited world, he commanded several Imperial squadrons through a selective extermination operation against hostile military and political targets. Using advanced armor capable of suppressing his appearance, scent and sound, John and his soldiers infiltrated enemy facilities without triggering alarms and eliminated designated targets within seconds while leaving uninvolved personnel alive. John even memorized several surviving civilians whom he considered intelligent or adaptable enough to potentially deserve recruitment into the Empire later.[8]
During the same campaign, John located a planetary structure combining runes and advanced technology and coordinated directly with orbital forces. Rather than simply destroying the facility with overwhelming firepower, he requested Energy-disrupting ammunition and carefully selected coordinates so that the structure would be disabled without creating unnecessary widespread destruction. When the strike unexpectedly awakened an enormous creature whose power John judged comparable to a First-Generation Primordial God, he immediately ordered his soldiers to withdraw, transmitted the discovery to higher command and refused to sacrifice his brothers and sisters merely to continue an operation that had changed beyond its original parameters. The event demonstrated that John's leadership had developed into the mixture of tactical aggression, caution and responsibility Victor originally intended.[8]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
As the [[Elderblood Empire]] expanded across the [[Imperial Calendar]], [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s original plan for specialized Legions eventually became reality. In the thousandth year of the Imperial Calendar, the First Legion, known as the Spartan Legion, was formally established as the Empire's principal offensive spearhead. Created for direct warfare, planetary invasion, the destruction of stellar defenses and large-scale conquest, the Legion was trained under figures such as [[Scathach]] and Rose Elderblood. John was appointed its primary Legion Commander, with Rose, Eleonor or Kali capable of replacing him when circumstances required it.[4]
By this point, the purpose Victor had envisioned at John's creation had been fulfilled. John was no longer merely an experimental Dragonoid or one of seven promising soldiers. He had become the commander of the Elderblood Empire's foremost offensive Legion and one of the principal military instruments through which the God Emperor's [[Will Solace|Will]] was carried into newly encountered worlds.[4]
The same Legion was later committed against the sectors nearest The Black Eclipse. The Emperor had approved an offensive on several fronts and had given priority to the systems that controlled that faction's transit and supply routes. The work was divided. John took the reconnaissance of the border regions. Kaleb coordinated the attacks on military installations, Conan assembled the troops who would occupy and hold what was taken, and Kiana and Caelus watched the movement between the fronts so that reinforcement arrived where it was needed instead of the whole force pushing in one direction.[5]
John was the first to find a real concentration of Black Eclipse ships. They were spread around a system that controlled an important passage and was covered by armed platforms and surveillance stations. His reports said the defenders had already seen the Imperial movement and were forming to keep the fleet out. The commander of that approach held part of the ships beyond the reach of those defenses and sent smaller detachments against the secondary entrances, so the defenders would have to divide themselves before the main battle. Black Eclipse did not wait. Its squadrons left their positions and struck a flanking formation in order to destroy the military transports. The attack opened with a concentrated barrage on the escorts. The transports changed course, and the combat ships moved between them and the attackers. The first exchange cost both sides, and the fighting spread along the whole route. The defenders tried to break the Imperial formations before reinforcements arrived, and a second line of ships stopped them. Their commander ordered the retreat before his squadrons were surrounded. The Imperial fleet did not chase. It held its place, then advanced on the approaches to the system. The enemy force had not been destroyed, but the Empire now held a military presence on a route The Black Eclipse had been using to move its units.[5]
Kaleb used that result to propose an attack on a military station in a neighboring solar system. The installation was a maintenance and supply base for the ships of the region. Taking it would narrow the room enemy reinforcements had to move through, and it would give the Empire an outpost. John looked at the system in front of him and said, "For the Emperor." The force answered him in the same words. The ships loaded their cannons and fired on the planets. The planetary defenses resisted, but the fire was simply stronger, and a planet exploded. Kaleb then reported that the neighboring worlds were still active. John ordered that the ones without a defense be conquered first. On that front the Imperial rule was already plain. Enemies who had to die were killed. Those without a defense could be taken, and on them the Empire would learn how a people that had lived for a long time under a faction could be brought the glory of the Emperor. It was a battlefield with no super-elite on it. John, as commander of the Spartan Legion, led the First Legion to the victory there. The principal battlefield was a different field, and Scathach stood at the head of that one.[5]

### Skills & Abilities
John is an Original Emperor's Soldier and the commander of the First Legion. What follows is what he has.

#### Physiology
- Dragonoid Foundation: A Dragonoid body is the frame. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] chose it for strength, robustness, and compatibility with himself. To an ordinary observer John reads as an exceptionally powerful Dragonoid.[1]
- Hybrid Characteristics: Human adaptability and ingenuity, Angelic obedience and kindness, Demonic aggression and cruelty, and a diluted Faerie connection with Nature are in the same body. Victor's Essence and Eldritch nature are what keep those traits from tearing the design apart.[1]
- Soul Fragment: Approximately 0.0000001% of Victor's Soul was built into the Original design. The fragment was meant to grow with the soldier, and it is one of the reasons the incompatible racial traits can coexist.[1]
- Combat Blessings: Victor Blessed the race with powers drawn from his Divinities related to combat.[1]
- Regeneration: Vampire characteristics were included so John would not have to rely on Dragonoid durability alone.[1]
- Metamorphosis: He can change the size and proportions of his body. The ability exists so a soldier built on a huge frame can still infiltrate. The elite measure later used for him and his Bloodhunter brothers is three to four meters. The smaller body remains available when the work requires it.[1][6]
- Physical Strength: At ten he already had a finished body two meters tall. Victor regarded the seven children as strong enough that a mistake could erase a city. In the Nightmare Tower he crossed the distance like a violet shadow and cut a monster in half with one movement.[2][3]

#### Knowledge and Training
- Inherited Memories: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] implanted the tactics, strategy, and intelligence of historical heroes and commanders, including world wars, wars between nations, supernatural conflicts, and apocalyptic battles. John keeps those memories separate from his own life and uses them as lessons.[2]
- Assassination: Hassan-i Sabbah was assigned to teach the taking of life. The training fits the covert method John already preferred.[7]
- Politics: [[Julius Caesar]] was assigned to teach politics, so a future commander would not be limited to the battlefield.[7]
- Philosophy: [[Abe-no-Seimei]] was assigned to teach judgment. Victor treated intelligence and raw strength as insufficient without it.[7]
- Martial Training: [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] was charged with polishing each Original and building the training around what the first test showed.[7][3]
- Leadership: [[Scathach]] named John and Clare the two natural leaders of the seven. His method is to look first, keep the people who can be used, and then apply decisive violence. That is what later let him command independent squadrons and, after that, the First Legion.[3][8][4]
- Battlefield Analysis: He examines the tools and the ground before he commits. On the Black Eclipse border that habit was the reconnaissance. He was the first to identify the concentration holding the transit system, and his report established that the defenders had already seen the fleet.[3][8][5]

#### Armor
- Imperial Armor: The adaptive suit is the important piece. It handles combat support, body analysis, vital signs, and tactical work. The sword and the firearm issued with it are secondary.[3]
- Akasha-001: An artificial intelligence based on [[Akasha Elderblood]]. It assists in combat, reads his condition, monitors vitals, scans the equipment and the field, and reconfigures the gear to the role he asks for.[3]
- H.U.D.: The helmet projects his condition, the state of the equipment, and the surrounding battlefield.[3]
- Adaptive Equipment: Armor and weapons change form according to the role. They are not fixed to one shape.[3]
- Hunter Mode: His preferred configuration. A black helmet with violet lines, the sword split into two daggers, and the plasma firearm turned into a plasma bow. Built for stealth, hunting, and silent kills.[3]
- Invisibility: By the later campaigns the armor hid appearance, sound, and scent, enough to move through an alien military installation without ordinary detection.[8]

#### Weapons
- Plasma Firearm: The issued pistol carries batteries of about 20,000 shots. They recharge from sunlight or other Energy, and he carries replacements. In Hunter Mode the same weapon becomes a plasma bow.[3]
- Miasma Sword: Forged from metals taken from the deepest regions of Hell, where Miasma was concentrated and toxic. A wound from it poisons as well as cuts. In Hunter Mode the sword separates into two daggers.[3]

#### Movement and Sense
- Speed: The frame is enormous, and the movement is not. In the first evaluation he left his position like a violet shadow and was above the monster before the cut. Soldiers made from the same template later moved silently at speeds that did not match bodies of several meters.[3][8]
- Perception: He can feel a threat before the Imperial systems have finished identifying it. After an orbital strike he sensed something under the ground and was already on it when the creature came up.[8]

#### Command
- Squadrons and Orbit: He coordinates separate ground squadrons, speaks to the ships, designates targets, and authorizes precision bombardment. He prefers a controlled strike when wiping the area is unnecessary, and he drops an operation when the conditions change. Faced with a creature he judged comparable to a First-Generation Primordial God, he pulled his soldiers out and sent the discovery up rather than spend them.[8]
- The Border: On the Black Eclipse offensive his part of the command was the reconnaissance of a multi-front invasion. He gave the call that sent the fleet in. After a defended planet had already been broken, he ordered the worlds without a defense conquered rather than destroyed. The victory on that field, which had no super-elite on it, was the First Legion's under his command. The principal battlefield was [[Scathach]]'s.[5]

### Trivia
- John and Conan are the only two members of the seven Original Emperor's Soldiers who were not given surnames.[2]
- John's original designation is John-000. The numerical portion was designed to increase whenever he died and was recreated, serving both as a death counter and as a record of honor and service to the Emperor.[1]
- John was the second Original shown being placed into a creation chamber after [[Clare Bloodhunter]], although all seven were created as part of the same first generation.[1]
- John and Clare were the two Originals whom [[Scathach]] immediately identified as natural leaders during their first practical combat evaluation.[3]
- John eventually became the primary commander of the Spartan Legion, the First Legion and principal offensive spearhead of the [[Elderblood Empire]].[4]
- As an elite of that Legion he measures between three and four meters. Ordinary Spartans of the same period stand above two meters.[6]
- He was the first to locate the Black Eclipse concentration holding the transit system, and he led the First Legion to victory on a field with no super-elite. The principal battlefield was Scathach's.[5]
