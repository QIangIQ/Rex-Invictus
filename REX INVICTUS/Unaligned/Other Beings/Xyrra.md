Xyrra are an extraterrestrial race native to [[Eldoria]] and the original race of [[Thaloria Xyphora]] and [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]]. They are enormous humanoids possessing naturally superior bodies alongside an extraordinary hereditary affinity for technology. Rather than technology being merely a learned discipline, Xyrra possess an instinctive aptitude for understanding, manipulating and creating advanced technology; Velnorah later explicitly identifies this characteristic as the technomancy genes of her people.[1][2]
Their collective aptitude allowed Xyrra civilization to advance at an exceptional rate. Under Thaloria, Eldoria was unified and its civilization reached space exploration in less than approximately two thousand years. The resulting [[Thaloria Empire]] continued expanding until its rulers reached Overlord status and dominated territories far beyond their homeworld.[1]
Despite this extraordinary growth, the old civilization eventually collapsed after encountering the far greater scale of Higher-Level Sectors. Balance later specifically cited Velnorah's former race as a species that possessed the potential to progress extremely far but failed to fully realize that potential because its civilization underestimated the Higher-Level Sectors after spending too long in a position of superiority.[3]

### Physical Characteristics
Xyrra are giant humanoids, with ordinary adult men and women generally standing over three meters tall. Their unusual stature is accompanied by naturally superior physiques; Velnorah directly describes her race as being blessed with powerful bodies in addition to their technological affinity.[1]
[[Thaloria Xyphora]] was an abnormal exception. Despite becoming the greatest technological genius of her civilization, she was born only approximately 2.5 meters tall and possessed an unusually weak body by Xyrra standards. Velnorah interpreted this as though existence itself had balanced her mother's abnormal technological talent by depriving her of the superior physique normally possessed by their species.[1]
Thaloria compensated through technology, developing a specialized suit capable of overcoming her physical deficiencies and allowing her to operate beyond the limitations of her natural body. The technology also maintained her body in ideal condition and was later inherited by Velnorah.[1]

### Technological Affinity
The defining racial characteristic of the Xyrra is their innate affinity for technology. Velnorah describes the race as naturally talented with technology, and while demonstrating her people's abilities to [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Amaterasu]] Elderblood, an ordinary Xyrra was shown creating a futuristic weapon with little more than a movement of his hand.[1]
Their aptitude is therefore substantially different from ordinary scientific knowledge acquired only through education. Technology is sufficiently natural to the species that entire generations can produce engineers, inventors and technomancers at a rate unavailable to conventional civilizations.
This does not mean all Xyrra possess equal technological ability. Thaloria was considered extraordinary even among her already technologically gifted race. According to Velnorah, her mother was effectively a "Goddess" within the technological world, capable of accomplishing almost anything when technology was involved.[1]

#### Technomancy Genes
The technological aptitude of the Xyrra is confirmed to possess a hereditary biological component. Long after the collapse of [[Eldoria]], Velnorah referred to the technomancy genes of her own race while designing an artificial species with [[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]].[2]
This confirms that Xyrra technomancy is not merely the product of their old culture or education. The characteristic exists within their genetics and can be isolated, preserved and deliberately incorporated into engineered organisms.[2]

### Civilization

#### Eldoria
Main article: [[Eldoria]]
The Xyrra originated on Eldoria, a planet eventually dominated by the Thaloria Dynasty. At its height, Eldoria possessed an extremely advanced futuristic civilization while simultaneously preserving its natural environment. Velnorah's holographic records showed advanced cities and technology existing harmoniously alongside nature rather than industrial development simply destroying the planet's ecosystem.[1]
The civilization's rapid development was strongly connected to Xyrra physiology. Because technological talent existed throughout the race instead of being restricted to isolated geniuses, scientific advancement could occur throughout the population simultaneously.

#### Rise of Thaloria
[[Thaloria Xyphora]] became the First Empress of [[Eldoria]] and transformed the Xyrra from one of the world's races into the technological foundation of a planetary and later interstellar civilization.
In less than approximately 2,000 years, Thaloria had conquered the other powers inhabiting Eldoria, unified the planet beneath her authority and already begun exploring outer space.[1]
Her Empire did not remain exclusively Xyrra. Exceptionally talented individuals from conquered races and later from other worlds were incorporated into her forces. The [[Thaloria Empire]] therefore developed into a multi-species civilization whose original technological foundation remained Xyrra.[1]

### Conflict with the Yuurakyos
Main article: [[Yuurakyos]]
One of the most important events in Xyrra history was the conflict between the [[Thaloria Empire]] and the Yuurakyos, an extraordinarily powerful warrior race associated with [[Galactic Emperor Kolo]].[1]
Unlike the Xyrra, whose principal racial advantage centered upon technology, Yuurakyos possessed overwhelmingly powerful natural bodies, combat instincts, perception and extreme adaptability. A single Yuurakyo infiltrator caused major casualties among Thaloria's forces despite her generals being supported by advanced technology. Thaloria nevertheless succeeded in capturing him alive and began studying his biology.[1]

#### Genetic Research
Thaloria initially attempted to clone the captured Yuurakyo, but the experiments failed because the genetic code of an adult member of the species had become highly unstable.[1]
She subsequently attempted artificial reproduction using females from other races. This also failed because developing Yuurakyo offspring demanded such extreme biological resources that ordinary mothers could not sustain the pregnancy.[1]
Thaloria then investigated whether another race could simply be transformed into a Yuurakyo. These experiments produced a much broader discovery concerning racial transformation: changing the physical body alone was insufficient. The Soul itself had to undergo the corresponding racial transformation before the body could properly become another species.[1]
Even with this discovery, Thaloria could not simply reproduce the ancestral power responsible for genuine racial transformation. The power of a Progenitor or equivalent racial origin could not simply be stolen and replicated through conventional technological experimentation.[1]
The conflict later gave Thaloria access to a female Yuurakyo as well. With both sexes available, she attempted to naturally reproduce the species, only to discover another complication: Yuurakyo reproduction was intertwined with the race's warrior instincts. A viable offspring required the male to defeat the female and obtain her submission. After several failed methods, Thaloria eventually succeeded in producing a child.[1]
The resulting child, however, was substantially weaker than its parents. By studying its development, Thaloria discovered the reason. Adult [[Yuurakyos]] underwent a coming-of-age ceremony in which their elders injected them with a special serum that dramatically enhanced their physical abilities. The serum also caused the genetic instability that had made cloning the original adult specimen so difficult, and its changes were not naturally inherited by later generations.[1]

#### Extermination of the Yuurakyos
The data gathered from the naturally born Yuurakyo allowed Thaloria to identify a lethal biological vulnerability within the race. She created a species-specific virus that attacked the Yuurakyo brain and killed infected members almost immediately.[1]
Thaloria deployed this biological weapon against the race and exterminated the [[Yuurakyos]], eliminating one of the greatest military obstacles facing her expansion.[1]
At the time, their destruction represented an enormous strategic victory. It eventually became one of the most consequential mistakes in Thalorian history.

### Galactic Expansion
Following the destruction of the [[Yuurakyos]], few surrounding civilizations could oppose Thaloria's technological superiority. Her Empire continued expanding until she had subjugated her surrounding galaxy and achieved the position of an Overlord.[1]
[[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]] was eventually born as Thaloria's daughter and inherited her mother's accumulated technology, research and position. She later became the [[Anna Elderblood|Second Empress]] Thaloria and an Overlord in her own right.[1]
The civilization at this stage was no longer dependent solely upon Xyrra citizens. It possessed conquered territories, subordinate races, advanced military technology and exceptional individuals gathered during its expansion. Nevertheless, the technological culture and development responsible for the Empire's original rise remained rooted in the Xyrra.

### Convergence and Downfall
After Velnorah inherited the civilization, the Sector containing [[Eldoria]] underwent a Convergence, transitioning from a Mid-Level Sector into a High-Level Sector.[1]
This completely changed the scale of opposition available to the [[Thaloria Empire]]. Galactic Emperors and other civilizations began appearing whose rulers and elite forces possessed tremendous individual supernatural power. Velnorah described beings capable of manipulating technology like Thaloria as well as entities capable of destroying a planet through their personal power alone.[1]
Thaloria and Velnorah themselves were powerful enough to fight some of these enemies, but their civilization as a whole was not prepared for the enormous increase in individual power represented by Higher-Level Sector forces.[1]
The extermination of the [[Yuurakyos]] now became catastrophic. Their overwhelming natural combat ability and adaptability would have made them one of the Empire's greatest possible military assets against the newly arriving enemies, but the race had already been destroyed.[1]
The Thaloria Empire eventually lost its supremacy. Velnorah was forced to abandon her former civilization and flee across the boundaries separating cosmic regions, eventually arriving within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Sector.[1]
The records of Eldoria that she later showed Victor became one of his first detailed demonstrations of how dramatically the standards of civilization, Divinity and combat increased beyond the isolated supernatural societies with which he was familiar.[1][4]

### Potential
The fall of their civilization did not mean that the Xyrra possessed poor racial potential.
During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later conversation with Balance, Velnorah's former race was specifically referenced as a powerful species that had the potential to go very far. Their primary failure was not a lack of potential but their civilization's inability to properly appreciate the danger of Higher-Level Sectors after spending so long as the dominant power in its previous environment.[3]
The Xyrra should not, however, be directly equated with [[Blood Dragons]] or [[True Dragons]]. Balance used Victor's Blood Dragons as an example of a species whose unrestricted proliferation could itself affect cosmic Balance, while the Xyrra were separately discussed for their exceptional developmental potential.[3]
Their established racial advantages are their naturally superior bodies, hereditary technomancy and extremely high technological development ceiling.

### Powers and Abilities
Xyrra Physiology: The Xyrra combine physically superior giant bodies with an instinctive hereditary affinity for technology.

#### Racial Abilities
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Xyrra are naturally born with superior physiques. Thaloria's weak body was explicitly considered abnormal by the standards of her species.[1]
- Large Size: Adult Xyrra men and women normally exceed three meters in height.[1]
- Technology Manipulation (Technomancy): Xyrra possess an innate affinity allowing them to interact with and manipulate technology with extraordinary ease.[1][2]
- Technological Intuition: Technology can be understood and utilized instinctively by members of the species rather than exclusively through conventional education.[1]
- Technological Creation: Xyrra can rapidly create sophisticated technological constructs. One member was demonstrated creating a futuristic weapon through a simple movement while Velnorah explained her race.[1]
- Weapon Creation: Their technomancy can directly produce advanced technological weaponry.[1]
- Engineering Mastery: The widespread technological aptitude of the species allows extremely rapid advancement in engineering, infrastructure and scientific development.[1]
- Genetic Technomancy: The technological aptitude of the Xyrra is encoded genetically and can be extracted for use in artificial biological engineering.[2]

#### Technological Applications
The following are demonstrated products of Xyrra/Thalorian science rather than passive abilities automatically possessed by every Xyrra.
- Technology-Based Enhancement: Thaloria developed technology capable of compensating for her weak natural body and increasing her physical performance beyond her original biological limitations.[1]
- Biological Maintenance: Thaloria's suit could continuously maintain its user's body in optimal condition.[1]
- Advanced Weaponry: Xyrra civilization developed futuristic weapon systems sufficient to support planetary and interstellar conquest.[1]
- Cloning: Their science was sufficiently developed for Thaloria to attempt cloning alien species and identify the genetic factors causing those attempts to fail.[1]
- Genetic Engineering: Thaloria studied, manipulated and reproduced alien genetic traits, while Velnorah later continued using genetic engineering on a substantially more advanced scale.[1][2]
- Biological Manipulation: Xyrra-derived research could alter biological characteristics, reproduction and species traits.[1][2]
- Biological Weaponry: Thaloria created a virus specifically capable of exterminating the Yuurakyo race.[1]
- Soul Research: Their research established that true racial transformation requires alteration of the Soul in addition to the body.[1]
- Racial Transformation Research: Thaloria attempted to transform other races into [[Yuurakyos]], although she was unable to reproduce the ancestral power required to complete such a transformation.[1]
- Space Travel: Xyrra civilization reached space exploration less than approximately two thousand years after Thaloria began consolidating [[Eldoria]] and later expanded throughout its surrounding galaxy.[1]
- Environmental Technology: Eldoria combined advanced futuristic infrastructure with the preservation of the planet's natural environment.[1]

### Limitations
No universal supernatural weakness inherent to Xyrra biology has been revealed.
- Individual Variation: Although technological aptitude is racial, its degree varies greatly between individuals. Thaloria was exceptional even among other Xyrra and should not be used as the baseline for every member of the species.[1]
- Physical Variation: Superior physiques are normal but not absolute. Thaloria was born significantly weaker and smaller than other Xyrra.[1]
- Technological Superiority Is Not Absolute: Their affinity with technology does not automatically place them above every supernatural race or civilization. Higher-Level Sectors contained individual entities whose personal power could surpass the conventional forces of the [[Thaloria Empire]].[1]
- Civilizational Dependency: Many of the Xyrra's greatest achievements resulted from accumulated research, infrastructure and specialized equipment rather than biological powers available to every individual.
- Strategic Complacency (Historical): The old civilization's prolonged dominance caused it to underestimate Higher-Level Sectors, something Balance later identified as the primary reason it failed to realize its full potential.[3]

### Legacy
Although the fate of the wider original Xyrra population has not been conclusively established, their technological and genetic legacy survived through Velnorah.

#### Velnorah's Transformation
Velnorah eventually reached the limits of her original race and later accepted [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s transformation into a Blood Dragon. Because her abilities were fundamentally centered upon technology rather than a conventional natural Element, her development demonstrated that technology itself remains compatible with the broader principles of Creation represented by [[True Dragons]].[5][6]
She consequently ceased to be biologically Xyrra while retaining the technological knowledge, talents and research accumulated through her former civilization.

#### Elvenorah
Velnorah later used the experience accumulated from [[Eldoria]] while constructing Elvenorah. Instead of simply recreating the old [[Thaloria Empire]], she developed a technocratic civilization in which basic necessities were provided while education, research and productive specialization were heavily encouraged.[7]
Automation and artificial intelligence were intended to assume large amounts of ordinary labor and administration without making the entire civilization completely dependent upon machines. Velnorah's experience with the rise and collapse of Eldoria heavily influenced this new approach to civilization-building.[7][8]

#### Dragon Heart
Velnorah and her daughter [[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]] later created the technological foundation for what became the [[Dragon Heart]]. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] transformed their experimental power source through Draconic Runes, [[Junketsu]], his own knowledge and a fragment of his Soul, creating a living energy source capable of supporting the enormous technological infrastructure of the future [[Elderblood Empire]].[9]
Although the final Dragon Heart was no longer simply Xyrra technology, the project demonstrates the continued technological lineage descending from Velnorah's original civilization.

#### Technomage Project
The most direct later use of Xyrra racial traits occurred in Chapter 1164, when Velnorah and Velina investigated cloned [[Yuurakyos]].[2]
After spending extensive time among [[True Dragons]], Bloodhunters and the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s engineered species, Velnorah no longer considered the original Yuurakyos as impressive as she once had. She nevertheless retained interest in their extraordinary adaptability and decided to preserve that useful characteristic.[2]
Velnorah's solution was to combine the adaptation genes of the Yuurakyos with the technomancy genes of her own race.[2]
Her objective was not to create another dedicated warrior race. She wanted a neutral population of technomages designed primarily to function as researchers and scientists while possessing sufficient personal strength to protect themselves. Their necessary scientific knowledge would be implanted directly into their minds at birth, preventing the enormous amount of time normally required to educate highly specialized researchers.[2]
This project demonstrates that, even after the destruction of the old Thaloria civilization and Velnorah's transformation into a Blood Dragon, the defining genetic characteristic of the Xyrra remained preserved and usable within Elderblood genetic technology.

### Notable Xyrra

#### Thaloria Xyphora
[[Thaloria Xyphora]] was the First Empress of [[Eldoria]] and the most technologically talented known member of the Xyrra race. Despite possessing an abnormally weak Xyrra body, her technomancy surpassed that of her peers to such a degree that Velnorah described her as effectively a Goddess within the technological world.[1]
She unified Eldoria, created the foundation of the [[Thaloria Empire]], expanded into space, studied the [[Yuurakyos]] and ultimately developed the biological weapon responsible for their extermination.[1]

#### Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II
[[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]] was born a Xyrra and succeeded Thaloria as the [[Anna Elderblood|Second Empress]]. She inherited her mother's technology and research, became an Overlord and survived the eventual collapse of her original civilization.[1]
Velnorah later joined [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], became his wife and transformed into a Blood Dragon, ending her status as a biological Xyrra.[5][6]
Her knowledge of Xyrra technology continued to influence Elvenorah, the [[Dragon Heart]], Imperial scientific infrastructure and the later Technomage Project.[7][9][2]

### Trivia
- Normal Xyrra men and women stand over three meters tall.[1]
- Thaloria's approximately 2.5-meter height was considered unusually small for the race.[1]
- The Xyrra possess both superior natural physiques and innate technological aptitude rather than being a physically weak species dependent entirely upon technology.[1]
- Their affinity for technology was later confirmed to have a genetic component when Velnorah referred to the "technomancy genes" of her people.[2]
- Thaloria's research into the [[Yuurakyos]] established that altering a body is insufficient for genuine racial transformation if the Soul itself remains unchanged.[1]
- The extermination of the Yuurakyos was initially one of the greatest victories of the [[Thaloria Empire]] but became a major strategic disadvantage after the civilization encountered Higher-Level Sector opponents.[1]
- Balance considered the Xyrra capable of progressing much further than their old civilization ultimately achieved.[3]
- The current survival status and population of the original Xyrra race have not been conclusively established. What is confirmed is that their genetic technomancy and technological knowledge remain preserved through Velnorah's research.[2]
