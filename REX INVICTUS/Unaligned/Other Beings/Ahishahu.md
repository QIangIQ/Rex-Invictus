Ahishahus are an ancient species of supernatural entities discovered by the [[Elderblood Empire]] during the conquest of the unnamed planet formerly maintained by Diana and Diane. Their nature was confirmed through the memories of one of the local Gods: Ahishahus are manifestations of Nature itself that took physical form.[1]
The planet's Gods had exploited the Ahishahus for several millennia, using their enormous Energy reserves as living batteries to power the civilization. Nine Ahishahu remains were discovered beneath the world's nine major Energy foci. They appeared dead while connected to the system, but at least one immediately awakened after the structure harvesting its Energy was disabled.[1]
[[Rose Adrasteia Elderblood]] and [[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]] classified the creatures as being closer to Divine Spirits than Dragons, with Eleanor suggesting that they were Nature Spirits that had acquired Divinity. After learning their actual origin, Rose ordered that they be captured alive for possible use by [[Victor Elderblood]].[1]

### Overview
The Ahishahus are not confirmed Dragons despite possessing reptilian and Dragon-like characteristics.
The first awakened specimen was initially detected by Imperial scanners as an organism biologically similar to a Dragon. After seeing it directly, John refused to classify the creature as belonging to the same species as the God-Emperor, considering such a comparison insulting to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[1]
Rose independently reached a similar conclusion. She believed the creature was closer to a Divine Spirit, while Eleanor proposed the more specific interpretation of a Nature Spirit that gained Divinity.[1]
The later memory extraction confirmed that the creature's actual origin was Nature itself rather than the Dragon race.

### Biology and Physiology

#### Appearance
The first Ahishahu encountered directly by the [[Elderblood Empire]] stood approximately 30 meters tall.[1]
Its confirmed characteristics included:
- glowing blue eyes filled with Power;
- large horns;
- stone-like scales around the head;
- a mouth filled with extremely sharp teeth;
- and a massive reptilian appearance resembling a fusion between a Komodo Dragon and a crocodile.[1]
Although its biological structure caused Imperial scanners to initially compare it with Dragons, neither Rose nor John considered the Ahishahu a true member of the Dragon race.
It is unknown whether all Ahishahus possess identical appearances or whether their forms vary according to age, environment or the Nature from which each manifested.

#### Nature Manifestation
The Ahishahus' most important biological characteristic is that they are not conventional animals.
Eleanor obtained their origin directly from a captured God's memories: the Ahishahus were manifestations of Nature that had taken shape.[1]
This supports Rose and Eleanor's earlier Divine Spirit/Nature Spirit classification.
The exact process through which Nature produces an Ahishahu has not yet been explained.

#### Dormant State
The Ahishahus connected to the local civilization's Energy network existed in an extraordinary death-like state.
Imperial records and scanners detected only what appeared to be skeletal remains beneath the first Energy facility. The organism did not register as living until the exact moment the structure drawing Energy from it was shut down.[1]
The creature then awakened immediately and erupted from underground.
All nine major Energy foci on the planet possessed similar remains beneath them.[1]
The chapter does not specify whether this state was:
- a natural form of hibernation;
- artificially imposed by the Gods;
- caused by millennia of Energy extraction;
- or a special biological characteristic of the species.

### Power
The first awakened Ahishahu possessed an enormous Energy signature and was immediately treated as a serious threat by the Imperial forces.
John estimated that the specimen was on the same level as a First Generation Primordial God.[1]
This statement applies directly to the creature John encountered. It should not automatically be treated as proof that every Ahishahu in existence possesses identical power.

### Skills & Abilities
Because the Ahishahus appear only briefly, their complete racial ability set remains unknown.
- Nature Spirit Physiology: Ahishahus are manifestations of Nature itself that acquired physical form.[1]
- Physical Manifestation: Despite their supernatural origin, they possess enormous tangible bodies capable of physically interacting with and destroying their surroundings.
- Divine Characteristics: Rose considered them closer to Divine Spirits, while Eleanor suggested that they were Nature Spirits which had gained Divinity.[1]
- Immense Energy Reserves: The Ahishahus possess enough supernatural Energy for the planet's Gods to use them as long-term power sources.[1]
- Civilization-Scale Energy Supply: Their Energy was distributed through structures composed of Runes and advanced technology and directed toward the planet's cities.
- Millennia-Long Energy Production: The Gods exploited the Ahishahus as batteries for several millennia.[1]
- Primordial God-Class Power (Observed Specimen): John estimated the first awakened Ahishahu to be on the same level as a First-Generation Primordial God.[1]
This was John's battlefield assessment of the individual specimen rather than an explicit universal racial minimum.
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics: The first awakened specimen possessed a colossal body and immediately destroyed the surrounding area when emerging from underground.[1]
- Colossal Size: Approximately 30 meters tall.
- Natural Weaponry: The creature possessed large horns and a mouth filled with sharp teeth.
- Stone-Like Scales: Its head was protected by scales described as resembling stone.[1]
- Dormancy: Ahishahus can exist in a state so inactive that advanced Imperial scanners may identify them as dead skeletal remains.[1]
- Reactivation: The first specimen became alive immediately when the Energy-extraction structure connected to it was disabled.
- Extreme Endurance: The species survived several millennia of forced use as energy sources and remained capable of awakening afterward.[1]

#### Unsupported Former Claims
The following abilities appeared in older versions of the article but are not demonstrated or stated in Chapter 1112:
- Earthquake Generation as an independent supernatural power;
- passive technological disruption;
- passive absorption of surrounding [[Magic]] or technology;
- semi-ethereal physiology;
- immunity to conventional weapons;
- automatic Dragon-slaying resistance;
- unrestricted [[Divine Energy]] blasts;
- or any confirmed species-wide invulnerability.
Environmental destruction caused by the first Ahishahu's awakening should not be converted into a separate Earthquake Generation ability without further evidence.

### History

#### Origins
The exact age and original population history of the Ahishahus remain unknown.
At some point in the distant past of their planet, Nature manifested into physical entities known as Ahishahus.[1]
Their existence predates the several-millennia period during which the local Gods used them as energy sources, but the chapter does not explain when or how the species originally formed.

#### Enslavement by the Gods
The local Gods discovered that the Ahishahus contained immense quantities of usable Energy.
They subsequently transformed the creatures into batteries for their civilization.[1]
Nine major Energy foci existed across the planet. Imperial scans revealed that all nine possessed Ahishahu remains beneath them.
The extracted Energy was routed through large structures combining Runes and advanced technology and then distributed toward the cities of the planet.[1]
The system had operated for several millennia.
Whether the Ahishahus had originally been defeated in battle, deliberately placed into dormancy or discovered already dormant is not revealed.

#### Discovery by the Elderblood Empire
The [[Elderblood Empire]] discovered the species accidentally during the conquest of the planet.
John was leading an operation targeting military and governmental forces that could resist Imperial occupation. Useful high-ranking targets were captured, while others were eliminated.[1]
Near dawn, John discovered a large facility containing an Artifact pulsing with blue Energy.
The Imperial A.I. scanned the structure and determined that it combined Runes with advanced technology. Similar Energy signatures existed at nine locations across the planet, and the Energy produced by the network was being directed toward every city.[1]
Because the Artifact's systems were archaic and difficult to control directly, John authorized an orbital strike using Energy-disruptor munitions.
The strike disabled the structure without destroying it.
Immediately afterward, the Imperial scanners detected an enormous concentration of Energy underground.
The A.I. announced that an organism biologically similar to a Dragon had awakened from its slumber.[1]
The Ahishahu erupted from beneath the ground.
John ordered his soldiers to retreat while the creature destroyed the surrounding area.
When he questioned why it had not been detected earlier, the A.I. explained that previous scans had shown only skeletal remains. The organism had technically been dead until the moment the power was cut off.[1]
The A.I. then confirmed that all nine Energy foci possessed similar remains.
John transmitted the information to command.

#### Identification
[[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]] and [[Rose Adrasteia Elderblood]] examined the information sent by John.
Eleanor noted the creatures' resemblance to Dragons.
Rose rejected that classification and suggested they were closer to Divine Spirits.
Eleanor proposed that they might be Nature Spirits that had gained Divinity, and Rose agreed.[1]
To obtain a definite answer, Eleanor used her Divine Soul and Draconic perception to examine the Soul and memories of a captured local God.
She discovered the species' name:
Ahishahu.
The captured God's memories also revealed their true origin and the history of their exploitation. They were manifestations of Nature that had taken shape, and the Gods had been using them as batteries for several millennia.[1]

#### The Imperial Hunt
After learning what the Ahishahus were, Rose proposed capturing them and feeding them to [[Victor Elderblood]].[1]
Eleanor treated the situation as a return to the sisters' old Monster Slayer work.
Rose responded that they had never truly stopped being Monster Slayers and had merely taken an extended vacation.
Eleanor then ordered the Valkyries to complete their current missions and proceed toward the Ahishahu locations.[1]
John was ordered to capture the creature near him.
When Eleanor asked whether he could accomplish the task, John confidently answered yes. She authorized him to release his full Power because the Empire already controlled the planet's critical locations and no longer needed to preserve operational secrecy.[1]
The chapter ends without showing the final capture of every Ahishahu.
Their ultimate fate therefore remains unconfirmed in the currently indexed material.

### Relationship with the Gods
The Ahishahus were not established as willing members of the local Pantheon.
The confirmed relationship was one of exploitation.
The Gods used them as batteries to power their civilization for several millennia.[1]
For that reason, listing the Ahishahus as ordinary members, allies or willing subordinates of Ion's Pantheon would go beyond what Chapter 1112 actually establishes.

### Relationship with the Elderblood Empire
The Empire did not originally invade the planet in search of the Ahishahus.
Their existence was discovered only after John sabotaged one of the world's Energy facilities.
Once their Nature Spirit origin and power became known, they immediately became valuable targets.
Rose and Eleanor ordered them to be captured rather than exterminated, while Rose proposed that they could eventually be given to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] for consumption.[1]
The chapter does not show that proposal being completed.
It is therefore inaccurate to state as fact that Victor consumed the Ahishahus, that they permanently joined the Empire or that every one of the nine was successfully captured unless a later chapter explicitly confirms those events.
