Faharah-Imo, commonly called Faha by his comrades-in-arms, is a genetically evolved soldier of the [[Skaarith Empire]] introduced during the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s first operation against the civilization. Having served the Skaarith Emperor for 400 cycles, Faha was raised within a militarized society whose warriors were genetically altered and bred for warfare. During the invasion led by [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]], he became one of the few Skaarith soldiers deliberately captured rather than exterminated after the invading Dragonoids detected an unidentified "trace" within him.[1]

### Physical Appearance
Faha is a humanoid member of the native population of the Skaarith-controlled world. Like the other warriors of his race, he possesses four arms and is considerably smaller than the Dragonoids deployed against his civilization. His exact height, coloration and other biological characteristics have not been specified. Despite the enormous physical disparity between himself and the Dragonoids, his genetically modified body was resilient enough to survive several direct strikes from them without being killed.[1]

### Personality
Faha was raised almost entirely around warfare, obedience and devotion to the Skaarith Emperor. He describes his people as warriors "bred to kill" and claims that battle was essentially all he had ever known. Having served for 400 cycles, he took considerable pride in his military service and began every ordinary day by performing rituals dedicated to the Emperor before eating and training.[1]
The ideology surrounding Faha taught him that his civilization had overcome ordinary weaknesses, including fear. Faha consequently believed that he no longer possessed such an emotion until the arrival of the Dragonoids shattered that assumption. Their roar, overwhelming physical presence and effortless destruction of Skaarith soldiers caused his body to tremble uncontrollably, allowing him to consciously recognize fear for the first time. Even then, Faha did not flee. He identified the attackers, warned his comrades and repeatedly charged opponents vastly superior to himself, continuing to fight after being struck and thrown through the air. His behavior demonstrates that beneath the indoctrinated belief in his civilization's superiority, Faha possesses genuine courage, aggression and a deeply ingrained refusal to abandon combat.[1]
Faha is also intensely hostile toward enemies of the Empire. His immediate assumption upon hearing of an attack was that the northern "heretics" were responsible, and he continued insulting and attacking the Dragonoids even after witnessing them slaughter his comrades. His loyalty and military conditioning were therefore strong enough to override self-preservation even after his worldview concerning the [[Skaarith Empire]]'s supposed superiority had begun collapsing.[1]

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Faha lived within the southern region of the Andromeda Galaxy as a soldier of the [[Skaarith Empire]]. His race had been genetically modified under the rule of its Emperor, and Skaarith society presented this development as proof that they had transcended the weaknesses of their ancestors. Faha himself believed his people had overcome fear and mortality, regarding their Emperor as the greatest existence within their civilization. His daily life revolved around rituals to the Emperor, meals, military training and preparation for the continuing conflict with the inhabitants of the northern continent. By the time of his introduction, he had already served for 400 cycles.[1]
This routine ended when an enormous noise erupted across the area and Skaarith warriors began mobilizing. Faha initially assumed the northern forces were attacking, only to learn that the assault was coming from above. Hundreds of figures descended from the sky with their bodies engulfed in flames while the planet's defensive systems fired Energy beams at them. The bombardment failed to stop the newcomers, and a tremendous roar from the descending warriors caused many Skaarith soldiers to collapse. Faha remained standing but discovered his arms trembling uncontrollably, finally realizing that the unfamiliar sensation spreading throughout his body was fear.[1]
When one of the invaders landed directly before his unit, Faha recognized it as an Akajaa, the designation used by his people for the invading Dragonoids. The enormous warrior towered over the Skaarith soldiers and wore unfamiliar armor equipped with weapons far beyond what Faha expected. Nevertheless, Faha immediately warned his comrades, raised his own weapon and charged while firing, attempting to close the distance because close-quarters combat was his specialty.[1]
The Dragonoid deployed a weapon against him, but Faha sensed the danger and evaded the initial shot by throwing himself to the side. A soldier behind him was struck instead. When Faha attempted another evasion, the Dragonoid unexpectedly kicked him directly in the face and launched him through the air. Faha managed to rotate his body while airborne and counterattack, but the Dragonoid defended with little more than an arm before redirecting its attention toward the surrounding soldiers. Faha then witnessed the invader charge through his comrades, crushing some beneath its massive body while cutting down or destroying others with its weapon.[1]
Enraged, Faha charged again despite having already witnessed how ineffective the Skaarith forces were. He failed to notice a second Dragonoid and was struck in the face once more, temporarily losing consciousness. Even while disoriented, Faha remained aware enough to hear the Dragonoids discussing his fate. One intended to execute him according to the order to eliminate the planet's alien population, but another stopped the killing after noticing something unusual about Faha.[1]
A scan revealed an unidentified "trace" attached to or present within him. After communicating with their commander, the Dragonoids received instructions to take Faha with them and imprison him rather than kill him. He was subsequently knocked unconscious and taken into Elderblood custody while the soldiers surrounding him were exterminated.[1]
The larger operation revealed that Faha's civilization was not what its population had been taught to believe. Velnorah and the Elderblood forces determined that the planet was effectively an experiment conducted by forces connected to the Upper Sectors. The Skaarith leadership displayed Energy and physiology resembling the previously encountered Hive Mind, while the native population had been infected with an extremely dangerous virus that granted them strength while drastically altering their genetics. The opposing rulers had deliberately maintained warfare between populations in order to accelerate their development.[1]
Because certain individuals possessed the mysterious traces being investigated by the [[Elderblood Empire]], Dragonoid units were specifically ordered to capture those targets for examination while strategically eliminating the remainder of the compromised population. Faha's survival was therefore not the result of defeating or convincing his attackers; he survived because the unexplained trace detected within him made him potentially valuable to the ongoing investigation into the Upper-Sector forces manipulating his world.[1]

### Skills & Abilities
- Genetic Enhancement / Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Faha belongs to a population deliberately altered through genetic engineering under the [[Skaarith Empire]]. Its warriors possess physical capabilities considerably beyond ordinary unmodified humanoids and were specifically bred for warfare.[1]
- Enhanced Strength: Although completely outclassed by a Dragonoid, Faha could launch himself directly into close combat, attack while airborne and continue attempting to physically engage soldiers substantially larger and stronger than members of his own race.[1]
- Enhanced Durability: Faha survived multiple direct kicks to the face from Dragonoid soldiers. The first launched him through the air without preventing an immediate counterattack, while another only rendered him unconscious temporarily rather than killing him.[1]
- Enhanced Endurance: Even after being struck by a vastly superior opponent and witnessing the systematic slaughter of his comrades, Faha repeatedly returned to the battle instead of retreating.[1]
- Enhanced Agility and Reflexes: Faha sensed danger when a Dragonoid deployed its weapon and immediately evaded the attack. After being kicked into the air, he was capable of rotating his body and launching a counterattack before landing.[1]
- Four-Armed Physiology: Faha's race possesses four arms, granting its members additional limbs that can be utilized for equipment handling and combat.[1]
- Combat Training: Faha is a professional warrior who spent centuries of service training for warfare. His civilization routinely trained after eating, and Faha specifically identified close-quarters combat as his specialty.[1]
- Close-Quarters Combat: Faha deliberately attempted to close the distance against a Dragonoid despite possessing a ranged weapon, indicating that melee combat was the field in which he considered himself most proficient.[1]
- Weapon Proficiency: Faha could fire his weapon while simultaneously advancing into melee range and remained capable of fighting while rapidly responding to unfamiliar enemy equipment.[1]
- Battlefield Awareness: He rapidly recognized the invading soldiers as Akajaa, warned his comrades, perceived danger from unfamiliar weaponry and attempted evasive and counterattacking maneuvers during the brief engagement.[1]
- Military Conditioning: Faha was bred and trained from birth for warfare and had completed 400 cycles of military service by his introduction. His conditioning allowed him to continue functioning even after experiencing genuine fear for the first time.[1]

#### Weaknesses
- Technological and Physical Inferiority: Despite being formidable by the standards of his civilization, Faha was completely outclassed by Elderblood Dragonoids. His attacks failed to meaningfully injure their armor, while individual Dragonoids could incapacitate him with ordinary physical strikes.[1]
- Skaarith Indoctrination: Faha's understanding of himself and the wider universe was heavily shaped by the ideology of the [[Skaarith Empire]]. He believed his race had eliminated fear and possessed no weaknesses until the Dragonoid invasion directly disproved those assumptions.[1]

### Equipment
- Unnamed Skaarith Weapon: Faha carried an unidentified weapon as part of his standard military equipment. It was capable of being fired while he advanced toward an opponent, although its attacks were ineffective against Dragonoid armor.[1]
- Skaarith Military Gear: Faha immediately retrieved his combat equipment once the attack began. The complete functions and specifications of his equipment have not been revealed.[1]

### Trivia
- Faha is the shortened name used for Faharah-Imo by his comrades-in-arms.[1]
- Faha had served the [[Skaarith Empire]] for 400 cycles before the Elderblood invasion.[1]
- He initially believed that his race had completely overcome fear. The arrival of the Dragonoids caused him to experience and consciously identify the emotion for the first time.[1]
- Faha's current survival is directly connected to the unidentified "trace" detected by the Dragonoids. The exact nature of this trace was not explained during his introductory scene.[1]
- The original inhabitants of Faha's world had been extensively genetically altered by the Skaarith leadership and infected with a virus connected to the Upper-Sector experiment being investigated by the [[Elderblood Empire]].[1]
