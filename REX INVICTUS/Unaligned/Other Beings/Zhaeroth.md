Zhaeroth is the Throne of [[Chaos]] and one of the [[Seven Black Thrones]] of the [[Black Eclipse Pantheon]], in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]]. He stands under <Darkness>, the Great Darkness, whom [[Victor Elderblood]] addresses as Vharzath, and above the Ten Chief Gods. Everything beneath that ruler is his responsibility. For that charge he was considered the leader's right-hand man. He is also the seat the captured enemy knew least.[1][2]
The name comes out of what Lily took from [[Vaelthar]] and the nine Chief Gods. No body, armor, or face is given. He has no line of his own. The file on him is the thin one, and the thinness is the fact. Of the seven, the prisoner had the least to give about this seat.[1]

### The Office
The charge is not a concept demonstrated on a field. It is a span of command. Everything below Darkness sits with him, and that is why the report calls him the leader's right hand. It does not make him the deputy.[1]
[[Xytherion]], Throne of the End, is the other man called a right hand, and the words are not the same office. Xytherion is Darkness's deputy in his absence, supreme commander of the enemy forces, and the most trusted man he has, set in that station beside [[Scathach]] and Violet. He is the one who was sent to deploy the [[Nihilvore]]. Zhaeroth's right hand is the charge over what lies beneath the ruler. The deputy's right hand is trust and command. The prisoner could describe the second. He could barely describe the first.[3][1]
[[Nharzuel]]'s domain is the existence of beings, objects, worlds, and concepts. That is a dominion. It is not this responsibility. [[Aetherys]] keeps time, [[Oryndal]] is the bridge, and [[Elyndra]] maintains the reality of the hostile galaxies. [[Chaos]], in the list, is the seat set over the rest of what belongs to Darkness.[1]
The office is not a Chief God's title. Those places are bestowed, blessed up until the holder can threaten a galaxy, and filled again when the holder falls. A Throne is not replaced that way. Black Eclipse has seven beings who threaten a galaxy in their own right, and the seven are these seats. Lily's judgment leaves [[Vaelthar]] as the experiment, made for a Chosen of Negativity and not chosen. The others are much more stable, and they are the true elites of Darkness. Zhaeroth is one of those others. The stability is asserted of the six as a class. It is not a power that was watched.[4][3][1]

### The Name
He is last in the list, and the name is the one the wives stop on. [[Scathach]] calls it arrogant, and the last of them in particular. Jeanne asks how he dares to call himself [[Chaos]] when he knows what he knows. An ignorant creator, of the kind the Greek gods were, may take a word like that because he thinks he knows everything. Beings at this height know how the cosmos works. Wearing the name anyway is, to her, madness.[1]
The word is already occupied, and not by a Throne. Negativity names herself the instinct of the [[Primordial Chaos]], and the other half, reason, is the inheritance that taught Aurethys. <Chaos> is a separate System designation. Niklaus warns a court that already serves the [[Vharzath|Great Darkness]] that the man they call by that designation will destroy them and devour the faction, and that a sector is not taken off him by a rush of traps. The Great Chaos is the being [[Cthugha]] asks to serve. None of the three is identified as Zhaeroth. Jeanne's anger is the distance between a Throne's title and a name that already belongs higher than Vharzath's court.[5][6][7][1]

### Among the Seven
Velnorah reads the seven titles as an attempt at a self-contained universe, with [[Vaelthar]]'s Beginning as its starting point. Ruby will not leave them as thrones inside that design. Taken as potential Primordial Beings of a new universe, they cover almost every essential function of a stable cosmos. Jeanne counts what is still absent. Life, death, and infinity. [[Chaos]] is not among the absences. The reading is the wives'. It does not enter him as a Primordial God, and it does not repair the name.[1]
Vaelthar is the only Throne who has fought [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. A projection broke him. What remained was emptied, and the body was ordered burned. This seat is not given that battle, and it is not given the [[Nihilvore]].[8][9][1]
When the imperial offensive was taking the conquered galaxies apart, [[Vharzath]] loosed two of the six who remained and told them to put their own safety first. Two answered. They are not named. Zhaeroth is not identified as either of them.[10]
