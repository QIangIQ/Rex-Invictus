#### Debut
Chapter 1202
Nihilvore is a biomass-based Abomination deployed by [[Vharzath]], the Great Darkness against the [[Elderblood Empire]]. After reaching a temporary agreement with the leader of the Kaiser faction, Vharzath ordered [[Xytherion]], one of the Seven Thrones, to deploy the creature. The Nihilvore appeared in a solar system already conquered by the Empire and began consuming matter, Imperial ships, and soldiers. It converted the biomass it absorbed into additional creatures, allowing the threat to expand beyond a single organism.[1][2]
The Imperial command initially classified the creature as V-05, a threat to several solar systems. After its ability to produce additional monsters became apparent, John upgraded its classification to V-06, indicating a threat capable of endangering an entire galaxy. Imperial troops were ordered to withdraw rather than engage it directly. [[Hela Elderblood]], Rose, and Velnorah ultimately destroyed the Nihilvore. Hela's first beam of <END> opened a passage through its body, allowing Rose to recover the trapped souls. Velnorah then confined the creature within a separate dimension, which Hela erased along with the Nihilvore using a second, full-power attack.[2]
The creature's destruction deprived Vharzath of one of his faction's trump cards. Although the surrounding region began recovering after the attack, the Nihilvore itself was not restored.[2]

### Physical Appearance
The Nihilvore is presented as a grotesque, biomass-based creature. Its appearance is associated with the mass of organic material it accumulates while consuming its surroundings. The organism grows as it feeds, and its ability to generate smaller creatures makes it more than a single stationary mass.[2]
The available account does not establish a definitive species, natural anatomy, original form, or fixed set of physical features. Its appearance should therefore not be treated as proof of a particular biological origin or classification beyond its demonstrated nature as an Abomination and biomass-based weapon.[2]

### Personality
The Nihilvore is not shown communicating or negotiating with other beings. Its demonstrated behavior is predatory: it consumes matter, absorbs ships and soldiers, and produces smaller creatures from the biomass it acquires.[2]
Its actions are consistent with the function of a weapon deployed to overwhelm an enemy through continued consumption and growth. However, the available events do not establish whether it possesses independent intelligence, self-awareness, or motives beyond this behavior.[2]

### Synopsis

#### Those Who Rule Reality
[[Vharzath]] sought assistance from the leader of the Kaiser faction while the [[Elderblood Empire]] continued its advance. The two leaders negotiated a temporary alliance. The Kaiser faction would commit its main fleet when Vharzath deployed his Abominations, while the Kaiser leader would receive half of the spoils and the right to choose first. Both sides understood that the agreement might be abandoned if circumstances made betrayal more advantageous.[1]
After securing the agreement, Vharzath ordered [[Xytherion]] to deploy the Nihilvore. Xytherion accepted the command and left to carry it out.[1]
The Nihilvore appeared in a solar system already under Imperial control. It began consuming the system's matter, including Imperial ships and soldiers. As it absorbed more material, its biomass increased, and it produced smaller creatures that spread as a swarm.[2]
The disappearance of Imperial soldiers presented a problem beyond their physical losses: their souls remained trapped inside the creature. [[Abhoth]]'s protection prevented those souls from being used or destroyed, but they also could not return to the Emperor while they remained trapped. The situation brought the threat to the attention of the Empire's leadership.[2]
Imperial command initially designated the organism V-05. After observing its growth and the creation of additional monsters, John raised the classification to V-06. The updated assessment reflected the possibility that continued feeding would allow the threat to endanger an entire galaxy. Imperial units were ordered to evacuate the affected system rather than attack the creature directly.[2]
[[Kaleb Hunter]] assessed the Nihilvore as a predatory organism that consumed biomass and used the strange divinity associated with Darkness to produce more beings. He warned that allowing it to continue feeding could make it uncontrollable. [[Akasha Elderblood]] subsequently confirmed that the Emperor was already aware of the situation.[2]
Hela, Rose, and Velnorah arrived to deal with the creature. Their priority was not simply to destroy the organism but to recover the souls of the Imperial soldiers trapped inside it. Rose assumed temporary command of the operation, while the surrounding Imperial forces were ordered to remain clear of the affected area.[2]
Hela's ship transformed, producing a massive cannon. Using her power over <END>, she fired a precisely calculated beam that erased the matter it touched. The attack opened a passage through the Nihilvore but did not destroy the entire organism.[2]
Rose then entered through the opening in the creature and attacked its interior in her true-dragon form. Six Valkyries, described as smaller dragons rather than true dragons, helped tear the organism apart while dragon plasma was released inside it. Rose recovered the trapped souls, and [[Persephone Elderblood]] received the freed souls into her realm. Rose also secured samples of the creature.[2]
After the souls had been recovered, Velnorah created a separate dimension and confined the remaining Nihilvore inside it. Hela prepared another attack, with [[Fenrir]] and [[Jormungandr]] charging the cannon. Hela then unleashed a full-power beam of <END>, erasing both the dimension and the creature contained within it.[2]
The operation succeeded, but the attack left the surrounding region of the galaxy temporarily empty. Hela ordered Imperial forces to avoid the area while it recovered. The region subsequently began to refill as the primordial beings repaired the damage caused by the attack; the Nihilvore itself did not return.[2]
When informed of the weapon's loss, Vharzath acknowledged the outcome. Xytherion was present when the report arrived. The Kaiser faction's leader then advocated a larger commitment of forces, with the Kaiser faction deploying its Enforcers and Vharzath committing his Thrones. Vharzath declined to launch a blind offensive, preferring to continue assessing the Empire's capabilities before escalating the conflict.[2]

### Skills & Abilities
The Nihilvore's demonstrated capabilities center on consuming matter, converting what it absorbs into additional biomass, and trapping the souls of those swallowed by its body. Its classification as a dangerous weapon comes from the potential for this process to continue and expand if it is not stopped.[2]
- Absorption: The Nihilvore consumes matter from its surroundings, including the matter of an Imperial-controlled solar system. It also swallows ships and soldiers, increasing its biomass as it feeds.[2]
- Assimilation: Material absorbed by the creature contributes to its growing biomass. Its consumption is therefore also a means of increasing the organism's mass and capabilities.[2]
- Entity Creation: The Nihilvore produces smaller monsters from the biomass it consumes. These creatures spread as a swarm, increasing the scale of the threat. Kaleb attributes this process to the strange divinity associated with Darkness, although the precise mechanism is not fully explained.[2]
- Soul Trapping: The souls of swallowed Imperial soldiers remain trapped inside the Nihilvore. [[Abhoth]]'s protection prevents the souls from being used or destroyed, but also prevents them from returning to the Emperor while they remain contained within the organism.[2]
- Biomass-Based Growth: The creature grows as it consumes additional matter. Kaleb warns that continued feeding could make it uncontrollable, explaining why the Empire treats the organism as a threat requiring immediate containment or destruction.[2]

#### Unconfirmed Abilities

### Power
The Empire initially classified the Nihilvore as V-05, a threat to several solar systems. Its classification was raised to V-06 after its biomass growth and production of smaller monsters became apparent. The revised assessment reflected the danger that continued consumption could pose to an entire galaxy.[2]
The V-06 designation is a threat classification, not proof that the Nihilvore demonstrated the ability to destroy an entire galaxy in a single attack. The creature was destroyed before it could complete its feeding process or spread its swarm across the galaxy. Its full potential if left unchecked was therefore not demonstrated.[2]
The Nihilvore was nevertheless dangerous enough that Imperial command ordered its ordinary forces to evacuate rather than engage it directly. Its ability to consume ships and soldiers, increase its biomass, produce additional creatures, and trap souls made it a serious strategic threat. Its ultimate defeat demonstrated that Hela's full-power <END> attack could erase it once Rose had recovered the trapped souls and Velnorah had confined it within a separate dimension.[2]

### Relationships

#### Vharzath
[[Vharzath]], the Great Darkness, deployed the Nihilvore as part of his campaign against the [[Elderblood Empire]]. The creature was one of the Abominations committed under the temporary agreement with the Kaiser faction. Its destruction represented the loss of a weapon Vharzath considered a trump card.[1][2]

#### Xytherion
[[Xytherion]] received [[Vharzath]]'s order to deploy the Nihilvore and left to carry it out. He was later present when Vharzath was informed that the weapon had been destroyed. Xytherion is not shown personally fighting alongside the creature.[1][2]

#### The Kaiser Faction's Leader
The leader of the Kaiser faction negotiated with [[Vharzath]] for the commitment of the faction's main fleet in exchange for half of the spoils, with the Kaiser leader choosing first. The agreement required Vharzath to commit his Abominations. The Nihilvore was deployed after the deal was made, but the Kaiser leader did not command the creature directly.[1][2]

#### The Elderblood Empire
The Nihilvore attacked Imperial-controlled territory by consuming matter, ships, and soldiers and producing smaller monsters. The trapping of Imperial souls drew the Empire's attention and led to the intervention of Hela, Rose, and Velnorah. John handled the threat classification, while Kaleb assessed the creature's behavior and potential for uncontrolled growth.[2]

#### Abhoth
[[Abhoth]]'s protection affected the souls swallowed by the Nihilvore. The protection prevented those souls from being used or destroyed, but also kept them from returning to the Emperor while trapped inside the organism. Their continued confinement became a central reason for the Empire's response.[2]

#### Hela, Rose, and Velnorah
Hela, Rose, and Velnorah carried out the operation that destroyed the Nihilvore. Hela used <END> to open the creature and then erase it. Rose entered the organism, helped dismantle it, recovered the trapped souls, and secured samples. Velnorah confined the remaining creature within a separate dimension so that Hela's final attack could erase both the target and its prison.[2]

#### Persephone
[[Persephone Elderblood]] received the souls recovered by Rose into her realm after they were freed from the Nihilvore.[2]

#### Fenrir and Jormungandr
[[Fenrir]] and [[Jormungandr]] helped charge the cannon used for Hela's final, full-power <END> attack against the Nihilvore and the dimension containing it.[2]

### Current Status
The Nihilvore was destroyed by Hela's full-power <END> beam, which erased both the creature and the dimension Velnorah had created to contain it. [[Vharzath]] was informed of the loss, and no replacement Nihilvore is shown in the available account of the event.[2]
Although the Kaiser faction's agreement referred to Abominations in the plural, the deployment described in Chapter 1202 concerns one Nihilvore. The available events do not establish whether additional creatures of the same type exist elsewhere.[1][2]
