### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 1209
My name is Orynthas. Remember it. We will make you pay—
—Orynthas, Chapter 1209
Orynthas is a male god of order who serves [[Aetheryon]], one of the four great leaders of the Upper Sector. The System records that leader as <Emperor> and also as <Kaiser>. Orynthas comes out with the elite whose lives the [[World Trees]] were spent to keep. He says his own name. He calls no one else to witness it. Nyx Elderblood is the one who reads the ground he is holding and names what he is.[1]
He does not serve <Law>. That faction is a different power, the herald of the Primordial of Balance, and it moves only when that Primordial allows it. Order, in his mouth, is not that office. He is not [[Auralion]], whose name is one of the ten public chief gods and whose aspect is never given. He is not the envoy Justice. He is not [[Laelzorith]], [[Seraphel]], or [[Dravakar]]. Those three are the judges who were already on the field, and each of them had named a domain of his own before the blast. He is not [[Oryndal]], the Throne of Space who serves <Darkness>.[2][3][4][5][6][1]
What he does on the page is short. A zone of order stands around Nyx, and he is the elite holding it. He begins a threat and does not finish it. Eleanor's greatsword breaks the territory and impales him. He is still pierced when he answers his Emperor's name.[1]

### Physical Appearance
Nothing of his face, height, eyes, hair, skin, or armor is given. The narration calls him a god and one of the elite.
The only body the scene puts on him is the one the sword goes through. Eleanor's greatsword breaks his territory and impales him. He is still that body when [[Aetheryon]] pulls the blade out. He hisses. He does not scream. The sword then detonates, and the fragments pierce the gods around their Emperor. He is among that elite. No separate wound of his is listed after the blast.[1]

### Personality
He gives his name and tells them to remember it. The rest is a threat, and the threat is cut off. "We will make you pay—" is as far as he gets.
He does not argue the retreat. [[Aetheryon]] has already ordered it. When the order is repeated inside the galaxy, everyone answers, and the god of order answers with them, still pierced. Pain gets a hiss out of him and nothing louder. He is not the one who curses the Dragon Emperor afterward. That line is his master's.[1]

### Synopsis

#### Volume 9: Those Who Rule Reality
[[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] has already fought [[Laelzorith]], [[Seraphel]], and [[Dravakar]] between the galaxies. She names the technique Heptagrammakos. Their Emperor orders the retreat twice. They cannot move.[7]
The leaders move as soon as she speaks the name. Darkness pulls back the nearby Thrones who were about to attack her. Light and Law watch. Aetheryon is the one who shouts for his soldiers to withdraw. He calls the three his judges, the elite of his empire. Losing all three would completely weaken his influence. He calls Darkness a coward for retreating. He knows the attack will destroy souls, and that a resurrection afterward will be impossible.[1]
He rises to leave his galaxy and save them himself. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s gaze, from the enemy galaxy, stops him. An empty homeland is an opening. He spends a trump card he knows will draw the wrath of the primal force of life. A being who can threaten a galaxy is not replaced. He sends hundreds of [[World Trees]]. They materialize, bow, and answer, "Yes, my Emperor." Their eyes are empty. The reason is already gone.[1]
[[Scathach]]'s confined creation becomes a local Big Bang. The armor of the three comes apart. The bodies follow, down to atoms and then to elementary particles. Nothing is left from which those bodies could be rebuilt. [[The Limbo Guy|The Limbo]] type appears with Infinity and holds the expansion in. Where the three vanished, new beings begin to form. Scathach sees the life force and says that hundreds of worlds were sacrificed just for her.[1]
Seven rays of light open, and the enemy elite comes out, each one an aspect of justice. Eleanor, Rose, Kaguya, and Nyx are on Scathach's side. The opponents outnumber the women five to one, and the women are the ones who feel the pressure. Scathach alone is worth three or four of that elite, perhaps more. The attack has not weakened her. What the new elite can do is not known yet.[1]
Aetheryon orders an immediate withdrawal. Scathach stops Nyx from taking the opening. She tells her to look closer and to use the gifts their husband gave her. Nyx narrows her eyes. A zone of order has formed around her, a territory held by one of that elite. She could break it. She cannot break it while the enemy Emperor is supporting the one who holds it.[1]
She has the name of the work before she has the name of the man. A god of order. He answers for himself. "My name is Orynthas. Remember it. We will make you pay—"[1]
Eleanor throws her sword. The greatsword goes through the territory and impales him. Victor's voice reaches the women: kill them. His energy is in their bodies, and they attack in less than a millisecond. The attack does not land. Aetheryon pulls them into his galaxy.[1]
He looks at his elite. When he orders a retreat, they obey at once. They answer "Yes, Emperor Aetheryon." Orynthas answers with them, the sword still in him. Aetheryon pulls the blade out with a flick of the wrist. Orynthas hisses and does not scream.[1]
Aetheryon starts to speak about the material. "Impressive material. I will—" The sword glows and detonates like a grenade. Fragments fly and pierce the gods. Aetheryon is not hurt. What is hurt is his dignity. The elite are battered. He curses the Dragon Emperor, tells them to tend their wounds, and tells them to prepare the counterattack. Then he turns on the primal beings of life. Orynthas has no further line.[1]
The price of those lives comes due in the same hour. [[The Universal Tree]] forbids every World Tree in that territory from helping Aetheryon and drives them off their posts. The planets die. The faction is ordered into space. A World Tree that spends all of its energy dies with the planet it was feeding, and hundreds of them were spent so this elite would live. After that, anyone marked by Aetheryon who approaches a World Tree makes that tree leave. Those trees stand under the Dragon Emperor. Orynthas is not named again.[1][8]

### Skills & Abilities
He is not shown using a weapon of his own. What he holds is a territory of order, and what stops him is a sword thrown through that territory.[1]
- Zone of order: Nyx finds it with the senses [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] gave her. The zone is around her, and one of the elite is maintaining it. She could destroy it. [[Aetheryon]]'s support is what stops her. The territory does not stop Eleanor. The greatsword breaks it and impales the god holding it.[1]
- God of order: That is what Nyx calls him, and it is the work she felt before he spoke. He does not name a domain the way the three judges did. He names himself, and he tells them to remember the name. No second use of the order is shown after the sword goes through him.[1]

#### Power and Threat Level
- Elite: He comes out with the elite [[Aetheryon]] spent the [[World Trees]] to keep. The narration calls that elite aspects of justice. His own word for himself is order. No separate feat of his is given a wider measure than the zone.[1]
- The zone without his Emperor: Nyx's reading is that she could break the territory easily if Aetheryon were not supporting the one who holds it. With that support, she does not.[1]
- The sword: Eleanor's greatsword is enough to cross the zone and impale him. He is still alive, still answering, when the blade is pulled out. The detonation that follows batters the elite. He is not given a death.[1]
He is not shown at the level of the four leaders. He is not one of Darkness's Thrones. The gravity, the Big Bang, and the greatsword are not his.

### Trivia
- The name on the page is Orynthas. He is the one who says it. Nyx has already called him a god of order.[1]
- He serves [[Aetheryon]]. The System tag for that leader is <Emperor>, also written <Kaiser>. The personal name is the one the elite answer, and he answers it with them: Emperor Aetheryon.[1]
- <Law> is another leader's faction, the herald of the Primordial of Balance. A god of order under Aetheryon is not that faction.[4][1]
- The ten chief gods are public, and each stands for one aspect of the justice that faction preaches. Orynthas is not given as one of those ten names. [[Auralion]] is the chief god named in that count. He is not Orynthas.[3]
- The envoy Justice serves the same patron and is not given this name.[2]
- [[Laelzorith]], [[Seraphel]], and [[Dravakar]] are the three judges the blast unmakes. Orynthas is not named with them. He is named after the elite comes back out of the life the trees spent.[6][7][1]
- [[Oryndal]] is the Throne of Space under Darkness. The names are not the same man, and the factions are not the same.[5][1]
- Seven rays open, and that elite is called aspects of justice. The women on [[Scathach]]'s side are outnumbered five to one. Orynthas is the only one of that elite who speaks his name.[1]
- "We will make you pay—" is his, and it is not finished. Eleanor's throw is what cuts it off.[1]
- He hisses when the sword is pulled. He does not scream. "Impressive material" is Aetheryon's, and the detonation cuts that line off too. "Damned Dragon Emperor!" is not spoken under Orynthas's name.[1]
- He is not called an Enforcer. He is not a subordinate who can answer END with BEGIN. Aetheryon has no such subordinate.[9]
- He is not named in the reckoning that follows. The trees, the dead planets, and the evacuation are the price of the elite he stood in.[8]
