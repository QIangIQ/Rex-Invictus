### Appearances

#### First Mention
Chapter 1201

#### Debut
Chapter 1201
Xytherion, the Throne of the End, is one of the Seven Thrones of the Black Eclipse and a subordinate of [[Vharzath]], the Great Darkness. The Thrones are the faction's genuine galaxy-level beings, in contrast to its Chief Gods, whose ability to threaten an entire galaxy depends on Vharzath's blessing. Xytherion's personal combat abilities, weapon, and individual divinity have not been revealed.[1][2]
Xytherion first appears in Vharzath's palace after the newly created [[Azrakar]], God of the Void, leaves. He recognizes that the ritual used to create Azrakar was successful. Vharzath then orders him to deploy the [[Nihilvore]], and Xytherion immediately leaves to carry out the command. The Nihilvore subsequently attacks the Empire's conquered systems, consuming matter and trapping the souls of Imperial soldiers within its body.[2][3]
After the Nihilvore is destroyed by Hela, Rose, and Velnorah, Vharzath acknowledges the loss of his weapon. Xytherion falls silent as he observes his master's unusually serious reaction. The conversation that follows concerns the danger posed by the Empire, the possibility of a larger war, and whether the Black Eclipse should deploy its Thrones. Vharzath chooses caution, arguing that he needs to understand the Empire's capabilities before committing to a large-scale attack. Xytherion's own actions after this discussion are not shown.[3]

### Physical Appearance
Xytherion wears completely black armor with red accents. The narration compares him to a medieval warrior from a fantasy world, contrasting him with other members of the Black Eclipse who look like extraterrestrials. No confirmed description of his face, eyes, hair, height, age, or biological species has been provided.[2]
His appearance distinguishes him from [[Vaelthar]], the former Throne of Origin, who was described as a nearly three-meter-tall humanoid with four golden eyes, four arms, reverse-jointed legs, and ivory armor veined with violet. Xytherion is not stated to share Vaelthar's appearance, artificial origin, or divinity.[2]

### Personality
Xytherion demonstrates formal obedience toward [[Vharzath]]. He greets his master respectfully, makes a brief observation about the success of the [[Azrakar]] ritual, and accepts the order to deploy the [[Nihilvore]] without questioning it. His response, "Your wish is my command," emphasizes his willingness to carry out Vharzath's instructions.[2]
When the Nihilvore is destroyed, Xytherion falls silent as Vharzath reacts to the loss. He does not openly challenge his master's decisions or offer a strategy of his own in the recorded scene. The text does not establish whether his silence reflects fear, concern, or another personal emotion.[3]

### Synopsis

#### Those Who Rule Reality
The imperial invasion under John has begun penetrating the territories controlled by [[Vharzath]]. The Empire advances through multiple systems, breaking defensive lines and establishing bases while keeping its elite forces and major trump cards in reserve. The Black Eclipse has likewise not yet committed its highest-ranking fighters to the conflict.[2]
Inside his palace, Vharzath creates a replacement for the dead [[Azrakar]], God of the Void. He orders another god to abandon his former name and assume Azrakar's identity. Darkness enters the god's body, producing a being resembling the original Azrakar and possessing enough power to threaten an entire galaxy. Vharzath sends the new Azrakar away to await further orders.[2]
Shortly afterward, Xytherion enters the palace. Wearing black armor with red accents, he greets Vharzath and identifies the ritual as successful. Vharzath confirms the result but explains that the new Azrakar is not yet as strong as his predecessor and that nine more replacements must be created. He also reflects on the death of [[Vaelthar]], the Throne of Origin, whom he regards as a loss that cannot be replaced. Vaelthar had been artificially created as a possible Chosen One of Negativity and given a portion of Vharzath's power, but he had not been chosen.[2]
Vharzath then contacts the leader of the Kaiser faction through his cosmic senses. The two discuss the threat posed by the Dragon Emperor and negotiate a temporary alliance. They agree that the Kaiser faction will send its main fleet when Vharzath deploys his Abominations, with the Kaiser faction receiving half the spoils and choosing its share first. Both leaders understand that they may abandon the agreement if circumstances favor them.[2]
After the contact ends, Vharzath calls Xytherion by name. Xytherion answers, "Yes, Great Darkness." Vharzath orders him to deploy the [[Nihilvore]]. Xytherion responds, "Your wish is my command," and immediately leaves to carry out the order.[2]
The Nihilvore appears in a conquered Imperial solar system through spatial distortions. The creature has a shapeless black body made of overlapping organic plates, shifting limbs, and openings that act as intake organs. It begins consuming the matter of the solar system, including Imperial ships and soldiers. Its biomass grows as it absorbs more material, and it produces smaller creatures that spread like a swarm. The souls of the swallowed soldiers remain trapped inside it, drawing the Empire's attention to the attack.[3]
The Empire classifies the creature as a threat capable of endangering an entire galaxy and orders its troops to evacuate rather than attack it directly. John recognizes the danger of allowing the organism to consume more matter, while Kaleb identifies it as a predatory organism that uses the divinity of Darkness to create additional biomass-based creatures.[3]
Rose, Hela, and Velnorah intervene. Hela uses the power of <END> to fire an annihilation beam that erases part of the creature. Rose then tears it open in her true dragon form and extracts the trapped souls, which [[Persephone]] receives into her realm. Velnorah creates a dimension to contain the creature, after which Hela uses a second, more powerful beam to erase the dimension and the Nihilvore together.[3]
The Nihilvore is completely destroyed. Vharzath acknowledges the loss of his weapon in a neutral tone, and Xytherion falls silent. A flaming skull appears near Vharzath, representing the Kaiser faction's leader, who reacts seriously to the Empire's ability to combine technology with the power of <END>. The two leaders discuss the threat and the possibility of total war. The Kaiser leader proposes that Vharzath deploy his Thrones while the Kaiser faction deploys its Enforcers. Vharzath refuses to commit immediately to a large-scale attack, arguing that the Empire may possess further secrets that must first be understood. Xytherion's reaction beyond his silence is not described, and the chapter does not show him deploying another weapon or entering combat.[3]

### Skills & Abilities
No personal combat ability belonging specifically to Xytherion has been demonstrated in the supplied chapters. His confirmed information concerns his position as the Throne of the End, his appearance, his obedience to [[Vharzath]], and the order he receives to deploy the [[Nihilvore]]. The Nihilvore's abilities belong to the creature itself and should not be attributed to Xytherion merely because he was ordered to deploy it.[2][3]

#### Rank and Position
- Throne of the End: Xytherion holds the title of Throne of the End and is one of the Seven Thrones of the Black Eclipse. The Thrones are identified as the faction's genuine galaxy-level beings, while its Chief Gods depend on [[Vharzath]]'s blessing to reach that level of threat. This establishes Xytherion's rank, but no specific feat demonstrates the scale or nature of his individual attacks.[1][2]
- Service to the Great Darkness: Xytherion serves Vharzath and accepts his commands without objection in the scenes shown. This is evidence of his position and obedience, not a separate supernatural ability.[2][3]

#### Deployment and Orders
- [[Nihilvore]] Deployment: [[Vharzath]] orders Xytherion to deploy the Nihilvore, and Xytherion leaves to carry out the instruction. The chapters establish that he is entrusted with the deployment, but they do not show him creating, controlling, or personally empowering the organism. The Nihilvore's consumption of matter, biomass growth, production of smaller creatures, and use of the divinity of Darkness are properties of the creature itself.[2][3]
- Execution of Orders: Xytherion immediately obeys Vharzath's command to deploy the Nihilvore. No additional command-based power or ability to control other Gods is demonstrated.[2]

#### Unconfirmed Abilities
The following have not been demonstrated or confirmed for Xytherion:
- A personal weapon or fighting style.
- A specific divine concept or individual technique.
- Direct control over Darkness or the [[Nihilvore]]'s abilities.
- Creation of the Nihilvore or other Abominations.
- Independent spatial, temporal, dimensional, or existential manipulation.
- The ability to destroy, create, or defend an entire galaxy through a demonstrated personal feat.
- Authority to create replacement Chief Gods or reproduce the ritual that created [[Azrakar]].
- Any specific healing, regeneration, immortality, or resurrection ability.
His position as a Throne indicates his high standing within the Black Eclipse, but it does not establish the details of his personal powers. The unnamed Throne who earlier answered Vharzath and summoned the remaining Chief Gods is not identified as Xytherion; that role should not be assigned to him without further evidence.[4][1][2]

### Power
Xytherion belongs to the highest-ranking group of the Black Eclipse. The Seven Thrones are described as genuine galaxy-level beings, unlike the ten Chief Gods whose galaxy-level threat depends on [[Vharzath]]'s blessing. This classification places Xytherion within the Thrones' rank, but the supplied chapters do not show him fighting or provide a direct comparison between his individual strength and that of another Throne.[1]
The new [[Azrakar]] is described as capable of threatening an entire galaxy, although Vharzath states that he is weaker than the original Azrakar. No equivalent statement establishes Xytherion's precise strength relative to either version of Azrakar or to [[Vaelthar]]. His title is confirmed, but his personal feats remain unknown.[2]
The destruction of the [[Nihilvore]] also does not establish that Xytherion himself was defeated. He was ordered to deploy the creature, but he was not shown fighting alongside it or present at the site of its destruction. His reaction is shown only after Vharzath acknowledges that the weapon has been lost.[3]

### Relationships

#### Vharzath
[[Vharzath]], the Great Darkness, is Xytherion's master. Xytherion addresses him formally, reports that the [[Azrakar]] ritual appears successful, and accepts the order to deploy the [[Nihilvore]]. Vharzath later discusses the loss of the weapon in Xytherion's presence. Xytherion's silence during the exchange is recorded, but no further personal reaction is specified.[2][3]

#### Azrakar
The new [[Azrakar]], God of the Void, leaves [[Vharzath]]'s palace shortly before Xytherion enters. Xytherion observes that the ritual succeeded, and Vharzath explains that the new Azrakar is not yet as strong as his predecessor. No personal friendship, rivalry, or direct chain of command between Xytherion and Azrakar is established.[2]

#### Vaelthar
[[Vaelthar]], the former Throne of Origin, is discussed by [[Vharzath]] while Xytherion is present. Vaelthar was artificially created as a possible Chosen One of Negativity and given a portion of Vharzath's power, but he was not chosen. His divinity of <BEGIN> was stained by Darkness. Xytherion is not stated to share Vaelthar's origin, divinity, or failed candidacy.[2]

#### The Nihilvore
Xytherion receives the order to deploy the [[Nihilvore]] and leaves to carry it out. The creature later attacks the Empire and is destroyed by Hela, Rose, and Velnorah. No passage identifies Xytherion as its creator, companion, or direct controller, and the text does not show what he does after its destruction.[2][3]

#### The Kaiser Faction's Leader
Xytherion is present in [[Vharzath]]'s palace during the aftermath of the failed [[Nihilvore]] deployment and the discussion involving the Kaiser faction's leader. The negotiations and proposed alliance are between Vharzath and the other faction leader. Xytherion is not shown participating in the negotiations or making decisions about the proposed war.[2][3]

### Current Status
Alive; last seen in [[Vharzath]]'s presence after the [[Nihilvore]]'s destruction.
Xytherion is not shown being injured, killed, or replaced. He leaves the palace after receiving the order to deploy the Nihilvore, but the text later places him with Vharzath when the loss of the creature is acknowledged. His exact actions and location after the discussion about a possible large-scale war are not provided.[2][3]
