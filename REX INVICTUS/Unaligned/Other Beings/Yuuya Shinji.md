### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 351
Yuuya Shinji is a Spirit belonging to the extremely rare classification known as a Living Ghost and one of the supernatural operatives serving [[Victor Elderblood]]. He was encountered during Victor's first major involvement in Japan, where [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]] captured him despite the unusual intangibility of his body. After investigating him, Kaguya determined that Yuuya was not an enemy at all, but simply an extraordinarily unlucky man who had been caught in the wrong place at the wrong time.[1]
Unlike a conventional Ghost, Yuuya is neither an ordinary deceased Soul nor a living person possessing a conventional physical body. He is described as a being who is simultaneously alive and ghost-like: his existing "body" is his Soul itself. Ordinarily, a Soul requires some kind of vessel or supporting structure to continue existing outside the normal cycle of death, otherwise the beings responsible for judging the dead would eventually draw it away. Yuuya is an abnormal exception because his Soul is powerful enough to serve as its own vessel and sustain his existence independently. This unusual constitution was considered so extraordinary that the entity explaining his nature to Victor compared him, in one particular sense, to an existence approaching the fundamental structure of a God.[1]
Yuuya's unusual physiology makes him exceptionally useful for infiltration and reconnaissance. He can remain intangible, phase through ordinary matter and is difficult to perceive through conventional supernatural senses. Victor ultimately decided that both Yuuya and his companion [[Watanabe Gintoki]] were far too useful to simply release after the events in Japan and brought them into his growing faction.[2]
By the later Demonic War, Yuuya and Gintoki had become paid servants operating under Victor and were living in [[Nightingale]]. Their unusual abilities were valuable enough that Victor's household temporarily loaned them to Haruna for covert operations in Japan, where they helped locate Demonic positions and other concealed threats. Despite this utility, Yuuya was explicitly distinguished from Victor's frontline combat personnel; his value lies principally in stealth, reconnaissance and his bizarre spiritual physiology rather than direct warfare.[3]

### Physical Appearance
Yuuya outwardly resembles a relatively young Japanese man with white hair, black eyes and extremely pale skin. His complexion is so unnaturally pale that it makes him appear as though he is already dead, an appearance particularly fitting for someone whose physical existence is essentially that of a living Soul. He is also described as relatively thin and considerably shorter than [[Victor Elderblood]], although Victor's own unusual height makes that comparison somewhat misleading.[1][4]
Despite appearing solid to the eye, Yuuya's body does not behave like ordinary flesh. When Victor initially attempted to touch him without using any supernatural medium, his hand simply passed through Yuuya's body. Yuuya was likewise capable of sinking directly into the ground while remaining conscious, demonstrating that his human appearance is effectively the visible manifestation of his living Soul rather than a conventional material body.[1]
Yuuya can nevertheless interact physically when appropriate. His intangibility is not simply a permanently uncontrollable condition; Chapter 352 reveals that he can normally determine whether another person is permitted to physically interact with him. This made Victor's ability to grab him while Yuuya was deliberately remaining intangible particularly alarming to him.[4]

### Personality
Yuuya is comparatively pragmatic, cautious and grounded, particularly when contrasted with the many battle-obsessed supernatural beings surrounding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. After awakening in what Victor had deliberately arranged to resemble Hell and being given the supposed "choice" between helping Victor for money or remaining there indefinitely, Yuuya immediately recognized that he did not possess much of a genuine choice and chose cooperation rather than pointlessly resisting someone who had already demonstrated the ability to bypass his greatest defense.[4]
He is also perceptive enough to recognize danger very quickly. Kaguya left a particularly terrifying impression upon him after his capture; Yuuya remembered her as a figure covered in darkness with hair resembling razors, and the experience made it obvious that his intangibility did not make him untouchable to every supernatural being. Victor produced a similar response after casually grabbing Yuuya while the latter had intentionally remained intangible. From that point onward, the idea of attempting to escape Victor effectively disappeared from his mind.[1][4]
Despite these circumstances, Yuuya is not portrayed as bitter toward Victor. He gradually accepted his position among Victor's people and, together with Gintoki, eventually became a genuinely loyal servant. Morgana later explained that both men remained with Victor because he had changed their lives, not simply because they were being coerced into service.[3]
Yuuya also possesses a dry and somewhat resigned sense of humor. His reaction to Victor's theatrical introduction to "Hell" was considerably more exhausted than terrified, immediately recognizing how absurd his situation had become. He commonly plays the more cautious participant when accompanying Victor's people into bizarre situations, particularly beside Gintoki.[4]
Yuuya's adopted name also indicates that he retains meaningful attachments to his past. When questioned about it, he explained that Yuuya and Shinji were the names of two people who had been important to him and that he had chosen to combine them into his current identity. His original birth name and the identities of those two individuals have never been revealed.[4]

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War

##### Capture in Japan
Yuuya first became involved with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction during the crisis surrounding Ophis in Japan. While Victor's group dealt with the many supernatural individuals caught in the developing conflict, Kaguya discovered and captured Yuuya. This was an unusual accomplishment because Yuuya's body normally allowed ordinary physical objects and people to pass straight through him.[1]
Following an interrogation, Kaguya brought the unconscious Yuuya before Victor. She immediately clarified that Yuuya had nothing to do with the [[Snow Clan]] despite possessing white hair and had no hostile connection to the events Victor was investigating. According to Kaguya, he was simply an innocent man who had been unfortunate enough to become involved in the situation.[1]
What interested Victor was Yuuya himself. Kaguya identified him as a Living Ghost, a phenomenon Victor had never previously encountered. When Victor examined Yuuya with his supernatural vision, the results were equally strange. Living beings normally possessed recognizable internal characteristics, Blood or other signs that Victor could perceive, but Yuuya registered almost like an inanimate object such as a wall or piece of furniture. Victor could perceive practically nothing inside him except for a peculiar thin "wire" extending from the region of his head.[1]
Victor attempted to touch him and found that his hand passed directly through Yuuya's body. As the unconscious Yuuya began sinking into the floor, Victor experimented by covering his glove with his own Blood. The Blood-coated hand successfully made physical contact and allowed Victor to pull Yuuya back upward. The interaction confirmed that Yuuya's intangibility was not absolute and could be bypassed through sufficiently appropriate supernatural means.[1]
Victor subsequently received a more detailed explanation of what Yuuya actually was. Yuuya was not merely a dead person refusing to pass on. He was alive in Soul form. Normally a Soul required a vessel or some other supernatural support to prevent it from being taken for judgment, with existences such as Elemental Spirits and [[World Trees]] surviving through their connection to the planet. Yuuya possessed no such conventional support. Instead, his own Soul functioned as the vessel supporting his existence.[1]
The strength of this Soul prevented the normal forces associated with the judgment of the dead from simply pulling him away. The structure was considered unusual enough to be described as, in one respect, relatively close to how higher spiritual existences such as Gods functioned. This did not mean Yuuya possessed God-level combat strength or a Divinity; the comparison concerned the unusual self-sustaining nature of his Soul.[1]
When Yuuya finally regained consciousness, Victor decided to amuse himself by arranging the surrounding situation as though the man had awakened in Hell. Yuuya quickly realized how terrible his circumstances appeared and reacted accordingly.[1][4]

##### Victor's Offer
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] presented Yuuya with two possibilities: assist him with a task, receive a large amount of money and eventually leave, or remain in the supposed Hell Victor had constructed around him. Yuuya immediately pointed out that this was not particularly different from being given no choice at all, but pragmatically accepted the first option.[4]
When Victor offered his hand and physically pulled him upward, Yuuya became considerably more alarmed than he had been by the theatrical environment. Yuuya had intentionally remained intangible and had not granted Victor permission to touch him, yet Victor was still capable of making physical contact. Realizing that one of his principal supernatural advantages could be bypassed by the man standing in front of him, Yuuya decided that attempting to flee would be a very poor idea.[4]
During their introduction Yuuya explained the structure of his name. Yuuya was his family name and Shinji his given name, but neither represented his original identity. He had selected both names himself from two individuals who had once been important to him. No further information regarding those people or Yuuya's life before becoming a Living Ghost was provided.[4]
Victor later instructed Kaguya to bring both Yuuya and [[Watanabe Gintoki]] to him after their immediate work was complete because he already had future plans for the pair.[4]

##### Joining Victor's Faction
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s interest in Yuuya was ultimately permanent rather than temporary. After the immediate conflict in Japan began settling down, Victor openly stated that he had no intention of simply allowing Yuuya and Gintoki to disappear. Their abilities were too useful to waste, and he intended to provide what they wanted while requiring them to remain associated with him in return.[2]
Yuuya consequently became one of the comparatively few male supernatural operatives directly associated with Victor's household. Rather than functioning as one of Victor's principal warriors, he was useful precisely because his physiology allowed him to perform tasks that ordinary Vampires, Humans and even many other supernatural species could not perform as discreetly.
Yuuya and Gintoki continued accompanying Victor's faction during its operations in Japan. By Chapter 384 they were still moving alongside Victor's people during investigations involving Japanese supernatural territory, demonstrating that their involvement had developed beyond the single job originally offered to them.[5]
During Victor's investigation of a hidden Youkai settlement, Yuuya's unusual nature was specifically treated as one of the group's strongest options for covert searching. Nevertheless, even Yuuya failed to discover the mysterious fox Victor was investigating, demonstrating that his ghostly physiology made him an exceptional scout but did not grant perfect knowledge or the ability to penetrate every form of supernatural concealment.[6]

#### Demonic War
By the period preceding the final stages of the Demonic War, Yuuya and Gintoki had become established servants of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Both were living in [[Nightingale]] and receiving proper payment for the dangerous work they performed rather than continuing as temporary captives from Japan.[3]
Their abilities remained useful to the Japanese faction, leading Morgana to temporarily lend the pair to Haruna. Yuuya and Gintoki contributed significantly to the search for the Demonic forces operating throughout Japan and helped identify locations that conventional forces had difficulty uncovering. Their work allowed Haruna's faction to direct its actual fighters toward confirmed targets rather than blindly searching the country.[3]
[[Mizuki Elderblood]] was particularly interested in Yuuya because even with her extensive experience involving Souls, Spirits, Onmyō techniques and supernatural phenomena, she had never personally encountered another case quite like him. She referred to Yuuya as an irregular and emphasized the uniqueness of a Living Ghost.[3]
Morgana nevertheless warned Haruna not to treat Yuuya or Gintoki as disposable soldiers. Despite their exceptional utility, neither man was considered significant war potential. Haruna agreed and explained that she only assigned them missions appropriate to their abilities. This distinction accurately summarizes Yuuya's role within Victor's faction: he is a specialist whose unusual existence makes him tremendously useful for covert work, but he is not one of the faction's primary frontline combatants.[3]

### Skills and Abilities
Yuuya is not primarily distinguished by destructive power. His value comes from the extremely abnormal metaphysical structure of a Living Ghost, which gives him properties that ordinary living beings and conventional [[Ghosts]] do not possess. His abilities make him particularly suited to infiltration, observation, reconnaissance and survival rather than direct combat.[1][3]
- Living Ghost Physiology: Yuuya exists as a living Soul rather than possessing a conventional material body. He is alive despite existing in a state ordinarily associated with the dead, creating the paradoxical condition described as simultaneously existing and not existing in the conventional sense.[1]
- Living Soul: Yuuya is explicitly alive despite his body being his Soul. He should therefore not be treated as an ordinary deceased Ghost simply because of his appearance or intangibility.[1]
- Soul as Vessel: Most independent Souls require some kind of external receptacle, spiritual structure or supernatural support to continue existing. Yuuya's own Soul performs that function for itself, effectively making his Soul both the individual and the vessel sustaining that individual.[1]
- Self-Sustenance: Because his Soul serves as its own vessel, Yuuya can continue existing without possessing a normal biological body or depending upon another object to anchor him to the living world.[1]
- Exceptional Soul Strength: Yuuya's Soul is extraordinarily strong by ordinary standards. Its strength is sufficient to maintain his existence independently and prevent the normal supernatural forces responsible for the dead from simply dragging him away for judgment.[1]
- Resistance to Soul Collection: Yuuya is not automatically taken by the entities responsible for judging ordinary wandering Souls. His self-sustaining spiritual structure allows him to remain in the living world despite existing directly as a Soul.[1]
- Near-Divine Spiritual Structure: The supernatural entity explaining Yuuya's physiology stated that, in one sense, his existence was comparatively close to a God because of the way his Soul independently sustained itself.[1] This statement concerns the nature of his existence and should not be interpreted as evidence that Yuuya possesses [[Divine Energy]], a Divinity, God-level destructive power or combat ability comparable to actual Gods.
- Ageless Spiritual Existence: Yuuya lacks the ordinary biological body through which conventional aging occurs. The exact limits of his lifespan have never been established, however, so this should not automatically be treated as proof of absolute immortality.
- Intangibility: Yuuya's most immediately recognizable ability is the capacity to remain intangible to ordinary physical matter. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s hand initially passed straight through his body, and Yuuya was capable of sinking through the ground rather than remaining on its surface.[1]
- Selective Intangibility: Yuuya possesses at least some conscious control over whether other people are capable of physically interacting with him. When Victor grabbed him in Chapter 352, Yuuya was shocked specifically because he had not permitted physical contact and knew that he was still intangible at the time.[4]
- Selective Tangibility: Conversely, Yuuya can manifest sufficient physical interaction to move normally through the world and participate in ordinary activities when he wishes. His state is therefore more complex than permanently existing as an untouchable apparition.[4]
- Phasing: Yuuya can pass through solid matter rather than being restrained by normal physical barriers. His body began naturally sinking through the floor while he was unconscious, demonstrating that this property does not always require elaborate preparation.[1]
- Underground Movement: Because he can phase through solid surfaces, Yuuya can enter the ground itself. This characteristic greatly expands the number of routes available to him during infiltration or escape.[1]
- Conventional Restraint Evasion: Ordinary physical methods of grabbing or restraining Yuuya are unreliable while he is intangible. Specialized supernatural powers or substances capable of interacting with Souls are required to overcome this advantage.[1]
- Abnormal Detection Profile: Victor's supernatural vision, which normally identifies living beings through their internal characteristics, initially failed to interpret Yuuya as a conventional person. To Victor's perception, Yuuya appeared more like an inanimate wall or piece of furniture than an ordinary living creature, with virtually nothing recognizable inside his body.[1]
- Lack of Conventional Biological Signature: Because Yuuya does not possess an ordinary material body, abilities searching for conventional internal organs, Blood or biological signs may not perceive him normally.[1]
- Spiritual Anomaly: Victor could perceive only a strange thread-like feature extending from Yuuya rather than the normal internal structures he expected from a living creature. The exact nature and function of this "wire" have never been fully explained.[1]
- Stealth: Yuuya's intangible, Soul-based existence makes him naturally suited to remaining unnoticed. Victor's household considered this characteristic sufficiently useful that Victor refused to simply release him after Japan.[2]
- Infiltration: By combining intangibility, phasing and an abnormal supernatural signature, Yuuya can enter locations that would be considerably more difficult for conventional operatives to penetrate. He continued being employed for covert operations after entering Victor's service.[5][3]
- Reconnaissance: Yuuya became a specialist scout within Victor's faction. His abilities were valuable enough that Haruna borrowed him and Gintoki specifically to help locate hidden Demonic forces throughout Japan.[3]
- Tracking and Search Operations: Yuuya participated in searches for hidden supernatural individuals and locations. Although his abilities are highly effective, they are not infallible; even Yuuya was unable to locate the mysterious fox Victor investigated within the Japanese settlement.[6]
- Supernatural Perception: As a spiritual existence capable of functioning as a covert operative among supernatural factions, Yuuya can investigate phenomena inaccessible to ordinary Humans. The precise mechanics and range of his perception have not been individually explained.
- Operational Experience: After joining Victor, Yuuya repeatedly participated in covert supernatural missions alongside Gintoki and Victor's maids. By the Demonic War he had enough practical experience to be trusted with locating enemy positions across Japan.[3]

### Weaknesses and Limitations
- Intangibility is not absolute: Yuuya cannot assume that being intangible makes him invulnerable. Kaguya successfully captured him despite his nature, proving that appropriate supernatural abilities can interact with or restrain him.[1]
- Blood can interact with him: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] discovered that coating his glove in his own Blood allowed him to physically grab Yuuya even though an ordinary touch had passed harmlessly through him.[1]
- Superior supernatural beings can bypass his defenses: Yuuya was particularly alarmed when Victor touched him while he was deliberately remaining intangible. This demonstrated that his primary defensive trait becomes far less reliable against individuals possessing suitable supernatural properties.[4]
- Not a frontline combatant: Despite his unusual physiology and tremendous usefulness as a scout, Yuuya was explicitly stated to not constitute meaningful "war potential" by the standards of Victor's faction. His missions are selected around his specialist capabilities rather than overwhelming enemies through direct force.[3]
- Reconnaissance is not infallible: Certain sufficiently concealed or unusual supernatural beings can escape even Yuuya's investigation, as demonstrated by his inability to discover the mysterious fox being sought during Victor's Japanese operations.[6]
- Unknown offensive capability: Yuuya has never demonstrated a significant personal offensive technique. Any attempt to assign him Ghost-based possession, Soul destruction, telekinesis or similar abilities would presently be speculative.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is Yuuya's master and the person responsible for transforming what began as an accidental capture into a permanent place within a much larger supernatural faction. Victor's first reaction to Yuuya was intense curiosity rather than hostility. Once Kaguya explained that the strange man was innocent, Victor focused almost entirely on understanding what a Living Ghost actually was.[1]
Yuuya initially had little real ability to refuse Victor's offer. The situation became even clearer when Victor demonstrated that he could touch Yuuya despite his intangibility. Nevertheless, their relationship eventually became more stable than simple coercion. Victor paid Yuuya for his work, gave him a place within his organization and allowed him to build a new life within [[Nightingale]]. By Chapter 671, members of Victor's household openly regarded Yuuya and Gintoki as loyal servants whose lives had been changed by Victor.[4][3]
Victor also understands Yuuya's actual value. Rather than attempting to force him into frontline warfare simply because his physiology is unusual, Yuuya is primarily used for missions where stealth, infiltration and reconnaissance matter more than destructive strength.[3]

#### Kaguya Elderblood Blank
Kaguya was Yuuya's original captor and one of the first people he encountered from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household. Her ability to capture him despite his intangible state immediately established that Yuuya's unusual physiology was not an absolute defense against sufficiently skilled supernatural beings.[1]
Yuuya remembered the encounter vividly. His perspective of Kaguya during the capture was that of a terrifying darkness-covered woman whose hair resembled razor blades, making her one of the principal reasons he immediately understood that attempting to flee Victor's people would be dangerous.[4]
Despite the frightening introduction, Kaguya also defended Yuuya's innocence. After interrogating him she explicitly informed Victor that Yuuya was merely an unlucky person caught in the wrong place at the wrong time rather than an enemy who deserved punishment.[1]

#### Watanabe Gintoki
Gintoki is Yuuya's closest known companion within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction. The two were drawn into Victor's affairs during the same period in Japan and were subsequently treated as a pair because their unusual abilities made them valuable for different kinds of specialized work.[4]
Victor ultimately decided to retain both men rather than allow them to disappear after their original tasks were complete.[2] They later continued accompanying his faction together and eventually settled in [[Nightingale]] as paid servants.[3]
During the Demonic War, Haruna borrowed Yuuya and Gintoki together for covert operations in Japan. Their combined abilities were instrumental in locating Demonic positions without forcing Haruna's main forces to blindly search the country.[3]

#### Otsuki Elderblood Haruna
Haruna temporarily received Yuuya and Gintoki from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household during the Demonic War because their specialist abilities could considerably improve Japanese reconnaissance operations. Yuuya's ghostly nature made him particularly valuable when searching areas where conventional forces could be detected or obstructed.[3]
Haruna understood that neither man was intended to serve as a major combatant. When warned not to unnecessarily endanger them, she made clear that she only assigned missions they were realistically capable of completing.[3]

#### Mizuki Elderblood
Mizuki regarded Yuuya as an extraordinarily rare supernatural anomaly. Despite her extensive knowledge of Souls, Spirits and Japanese supernatural techniques, she stated that she had never encountered another case like him and specifically identified him as an irregular Living Ghost.[3]

### Notes
- Yuuya is not an ordinary dead Ghost. He is explicitly alive despite existing directly as a Soul.[1]
- His Soul serves as its own vessel, allowing him to continue existing independently without a conventional biological body.[1]
- Yuuya's ability to remain in the living world without being automatically taken for judgment derives from the abnormal strength and self-sustaining nature of his Soul.[1]
- The comparison between Yuuya and a God concerns the structure of his spiritual existence. It does not establish that he possesses a Divinity or combat strength comparable to Gods.[1]
- His intangibility possesses a degree of conscious control. Chapter 352 specifically establishes that Yuuya had not allowed [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to touch him and was still intangible when Victor nevertheless grabbed him.[4]
- Yuuya's current name is self-selected. Yuuya is his family name and Shinji his given name, with both being taken from two people who were important to him. His original name remains unknown.[4]
- By Chapter 671, Yuuya was living in [[Nightingale]] and earning a proper wage from the dangerous work performed for Victor's faction.[3]
- Although his abilities are highly valuable, he is specifically stated to not be one of Victor's frontline "war potential" combatants.[3]
- Yuuya is one of the few known supernatural beings whose existence caused even [[Mizuki Elderblood]] to admit that she had never encountered anything comparable before.[3]
