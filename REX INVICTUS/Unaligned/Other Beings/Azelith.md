Azelith is the title of the Chief God of Darkness, one of the ten Chief God positions within the [[Black Eclipse Pantheon]] in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]]. The title is identified in the information obtained by [[Lily Elderblood]], which lists the responsibilities of the ten Chief Gods. However, no individual bearing the title is shown, and the available information does not establish the holder's appearance, gender, personality, or personal abilities.[1]
Azelith's office must be distinguished from the being who rules the Black Eclipse Pantheon. The Great Darkness occupies the position above the [[Seven Black Thrones]] and the Ten Chief Gods. [[Victor Elderblood]] addresses the Great Darkness as Vharzath; that name does not identify the holder of the Azelith title.[2][3][1]

### The Office of Darkness
Azelith is listed as the Chief God associated with Darkness. The title belongs to the hierarchy of the [[Black Eclipse Pantheon]], beneath the [[Seven Black Thrones]] and under the authority of the Great Darkness. The available account identifies the office and its place among the ten positions, but does not show the title's holder exercising its authority in a scene.[1]
The information about the Chief Gods distinguishes their assigned offices from the supreme ruler of the faction. The Great Darkness occupies the highest position described in the Black Eclipse hierarchy, while the Seven Black Thrones and the Ten Chief Gods hold positions beneath it. The title Azelith therefore should not be confused with the Great Darkness itself or treated as another name for Vharzath.[2][1]
The available information does not establish whether the holder of Azelith possesses an innate connection to Darkness, receives the authority through the ruler's blessing, or has mastered the office's power. It also does not demonstrate a unique technique, weapon, transformation, or feat belonging specifically to Azelith. Such details cannot be confirmed from the title alone.[1]

### Among the Ten Chief Gods
Azelith is included in the list of ten Chief God offices. The other named titles include [[Azrakar]], associated with the Void; [[Kaelith]], associated with Night; [[Nerath]], associated with Silence; and [[Vhalor]], associated with Shadows. The list also identifies offices associated with Soul, Fear, Oblivion, and Inevitability, as well as the remaining position in the hierarchy.[3][4][1]
Azelith is not identified as one of the Chief Gods who personally fought War during the confrontation with Azrakar and Kaelith. The battle features Azrakar, Kaelith, Nerath, and Vhalor among the Gods opposing the Four Horsemen. The absence of Azelith from that scene does not establish whether the title's holder was absent, elsewhere, or had another role; the available text does not specify this.[3][4]
The later account of the Chief Gods' offices establishes the title's existence, but it does not identify Azelith as one of the specific individuals whose body or Soul was destroyed during the battle against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. The holder's present condition therefore remains unknown.[1]

### Abilities and Powers
No abilities have been individually demonstrated by a character identified as Azelith. The following information is all that can currently be established:
- Chief God Status: Azelith is listed among the Ten Chief Gods of the [[Black Eclipse Pantheon]], a group positioned beneath the [[Seven Black Thrones]].[1]
- Office of Darkness: The title identifies Darkness as Azelith's designated responsibility. The available information does not demonstrate how the holder uses this authority or what specific techniques it grants.[1]
- Power Level: The Chief Gods are described as beings capable of threatening entire galaxies. This is a general description of the faction's Chief God positions and does not provide a specific feat or measured power level for Azelith's unidentified holder.[3]
