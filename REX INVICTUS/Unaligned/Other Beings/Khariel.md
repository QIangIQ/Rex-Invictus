Khariel is the Chief God of the Soul and one of the Ten Chief Gods of the [[Black Eclipse Pantheon]], in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]]. The name is a title. <Darkness>, whom [[Victor Elderblood]] addresses as Vharzath, bestows it. The charge, fifth in the order Lily gives, is soul.[1]
No body, armor, face, or pronoun is given. The title is not spoken on a battlefield. It comes out of what Lily took from [[Vaelthar]] and the nine Chief Gods beside him.[1]

### The Office
The ten places are not won. A God who has already reached an elite is given the title, the realm, and the blessing. The blessing lifts the holder to a threat against a galaxy. At the start the power is not mastered. Years in the chair are what bring it under control, and the captured generation had held these offices for hundreds of years. What they swing, while they learn it, is Darkness. The realm they were given is someone else's. Soul is Khariel's. The force laid over it is the patron's, and it was alien to the realm before the gift.[2][1]
Soul, here, is the office in the ten. It is not Soul, the being Vharzath pays with the dead of War's battle so that Equilibrium and a Judge of the Abyss stay out of a war between Gods. It is not the soul-work [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] uses to kill [[Kaelith]]. The word is the charge. No taking of a soul, no binding, and no bargain is shown in Khariel's hands.[3][4][1]
The seat sits under the [[Seven Black Thrones]] and over the Greater Gods. A Throne threatens a galaxy without being lifted. A Chief God threatens one because the ruler forces the blessing up, and can be replaced when the holder falls. Violet hears the whole list as concepts bound to negativity. Soul is one of them.[5][1]

### Among the Ten
Lily's order is emptiness, night, silence, shadow, soul, fear, darkness, oblivion, inevitability, and [[Vaelthar]] himself. The titles in that order are [[Azrakar]], [[Kaelith]], [[Nerath]], [[Vhalor]], Khariel, [[Morvane]], [[Azelith]], [[Nihrael]], [[Draveth]], and [[Vaelzara]]. Four of those titles have been seen. Azrakar is erased and raised again, weaker. Kaelith is killed by the Soul. Nerath and Vhalor are restored, rebuked, and then lost into the capture. Khariel is not among the names on that field.[6][4][1]
Nine Chief Gods are in the collection with Vaelthar. Memories, blood, existence, and soul are taken. The bodies are ordered burned once Ruby has her samples. The nine are not named one by one. The new Azrakar is not among them. Khariel's title is in the list Lily speaks over that table. The name is not pointed at a single body.[7][1]
