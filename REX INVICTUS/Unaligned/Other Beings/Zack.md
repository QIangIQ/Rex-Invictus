### Appearances

#### Debut
[[Ch20 - Rex Invictus XIII|Chapter 20]]
Zack is [[Victor Elderblood]]'s longtime companion and the cat raised by the Walker family since long before Victor entered the supernatural world. Although he originally appeared to be an unusually intelligent Maine Coon from Earth, Zack displayed abnormal characteristics even before receiving any supernatural transformation, including exceptional intelligence and a lifespan exceeding twenty years.[3][1]
After being brought to the world that eventually became Dragons Nest, Zack began naturally evolving under the influence of the planet's extraordinarily rich Energy. His formerly overweight body became leaner, muscular and considerably more predatory without Victor directly interfering with his development.[4]
Zack eventually decided that this natural evolution was insufficient and personally requested that Victor transform him further. Victor gave Zack a single drop of his Blood, causing the cat to undergo an extreme supernatural metamorphosis into an unnamed feline existence directly influenced by Victor's blood and Power.[4]
Following this evolution, Zack became vastly stronger than his harmless appearance suggests. He can freely choose the species and size of feline body he displays, normally resides within Victor's shadow, possesses senses capable of penetrating the concealment of powerful dimensional beings such as [[Agares]], and possesses a true form powerful enough to be regarded as a threat capable of destroying an entire world.[5][6][7]
Despite his enormous growth, Zack's relationship with Victor has fundamentally remained unchanged. Victor continues treating him as the cat and companion who grew up beside him, while Zack displays extraordinary loyalty toward Victor and the Elderblood family.

### Physical Appearance

#### Original Form
Zack was originally a large Maine Coon with predominantly black fur. A patch of pinkish-white fur covers his chest and neck, while the fur surrounding his head is unusually thick and somewhat spiky. His ears are relatively short, with portions of dark-brown fur mixed into the predominantly black coloration. Zack possesses pool-blue eyes with narrow black feline pupils and long white whiskers extending from either side of his face.[3]
Zack was originally notably overweight and lazy, although his large Maine Coon frame still gave him a naturally imposing appearance compared to ordinary domestic cats.
Even before becoming supernatural, Zack's body was abnormal. By the time [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] returned from [[Biblical Hell]], Zack had already lived for more than twenty years, substantially exceeding the normal lifespan expected of an ordinary Maine Coon. [[Leon Walker]] admitted that the family had noticed how strange Zack's longevity was but had effectively chosen not to question it.[1]

#### Natural Evolution
After Zack began living on [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s newly created world, its environment and Energy naturally affected his body. His excessive fat disappeared, his muscles became significantly more developed and his general physique became that of a genuine predator rather than a domesticated house cat.[4]
Victor was surprised by how dramatically Zack had changed without receiving any deliberate enhancement. Zack had effectively gone from an overweight house cat to something resembling a miniature lion purely through adaptation to the planet.[4]

#### Evolved Form
Zack's greatest physical transformation occurred after he voluntarily consumed a drop of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood. Crimson Power engulfed his entire body as he rapidly grew and began evolving into an entirely new supernatural existence.[4]
Following the transformation, Zack is no longer restricted to a single fixed appearance. He normally retains his original cat form because he apparently prefers it, but Victor later explained that Zack could just as easily assume the appearance of a tiger, lion or other feline creature.[7]
His larger combat manifestation resembles an enormous predatory feline with characteristics similar to both a panther and tiger. His body becomes covered by Dark Energy and his eyes become blood-red, giving him a considerably more Demonic appearance despite not actually being a Demon.[5][6]
Victor further revealed that none of these ordinary feline appearances necessarily represent Zack's absolute form. His genuine combat body is an enormous creature whose very existence possesses enough power to threaten an entire world.[7]

### Personality
Zack possesses intelligence far beyond that of an ordinary animal. Even before obtaining supernatural power, he could understand Human speech, interpret situations, communicate complex intentions and make independent decisions.[3][8]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] has lived beside Zack for sufficiently long that he can distinguish subtle differences between his various meows and accurately interpret what Zack intends to communicate. Their conversations therefore function almost like ordinary dialogue despite Zack usually refusing to speak in anything other than feline vocalizations.[3][8]
Zack is extremely proud and territorial. Even as an apparently mundane cat, he considered the neighborhood surrounding Victor's home his personal territory and carried himself with such confidence that the narration compared his demeanor to that of a king.[2]
This dominance was demonstrated when Nyatan, another male cat, entered Zack's territory intending to challenge him and take his female companions. Zack barely needed to fight before Nyatan recognized the difference between them, submitted and eventually asked to become Zack's disciple.[2]
Much like Victor, Zack possesses considerable success with the opposite sex. He accumulated numerous female companions while still living on Earth, and this behavior continued after his supernatural transformation. By the later eras, Zack had more than fifty feline partners living within Dragons Nest.[2][7]
Zack nevertheless possesses a peculiar sense of loyalty toward Victor even in this area. Once the number of his own partners roughly reached the number of Victor's wives, Zack deliberately stopped pursuing additional females. Victor understood the reason immediately, explaining that Zack was his "bro" and would not deliberately surpass him.[7]
His loyalty toward Victor's family is considerably more serious. Zack instinctively protects people Victor cares about without requiring direct commands and reacts violently whenever he identifies an actual threat.[6]
His attachment to [[Anna Elderblood]] is particularly evident. Zack willingly rests with her like an ordinary pet, but the instant he detected [[Agares]] approaching, his demeanor changed completely. He transformed, positioned himself beside Anna and attacked the concealed Demon before most of the surrounding defenders had even understood what was occurring.[6]
Despite becoming a supernatural entity capable of destroying worlds, Zack remains fundamentally a cat in personality. He sleeps when he wishes, enjoys being pampered by the family, dislikes baths and continues meowing simply because he apparently prefers communicating that way.[8][7]

### Synopsis

#### The Awakening
Zack was already the Walker family's longtime pet when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became a Vampire. Victor first properly encountered him after his transformation when he visited the veterinary clinic to collect him.[3]
Zack immediately demonstrated unusual intelligence. Victor could converse with him through years of familiarity with his behavior and vocalizations, while Zack clearly understood what Victor said and responded appropriately.[3][8]
Despite being treated by outsiders as an ordinary house cat, Zack's behavior regularly suggested something unusual about him even at this early stage.

#### A New Pillar
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] returned to the Human World, Zack was shown wandering through the neighborhood surrounding the Walker residence. The area effectively functioned as Zack's personal territory, where he had established himself as the dominant male cat.[2]
A yellow cat named Nyatan entered the territory after hearing stories concerning Zack's females and reputation. Nyatan initially intended to challenge Zack and steal both his territory and harem.[2]
Zack responded with overwhelming confidence and intimidated Nyatan into submission without requiring a serious battle. Nyatan abandoned his original goal and instead asked Zack to teach him how to become similarly successful with female cats.[2]
Zack effectively accepted Nyatan as his disciple.
Shortly afterward, Zack heard Victor speaking inside the Walker residence. Upon recognizing his longtime companion's voice, he immediately abandoned the confrontation and rushed inside, reuniting with Victor.[2]

#### King Among Kings
Centuries later, Zack remained alive despite having originally been an ordinary house cat.
Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s return from Hell, his parents finally acknowledged just how abnormal Zack's lifespan had become. Zack had lived for more than twenty years even before receiving a supernatural transformation, something that should have been highly unusual for a Maine Coon.[1]
His first confirmed supernatural evolution occurred after moving to Victor's newly created world.
The extraordinary concentration of Energy and the unique environment of the planet began affecting living creatures naturally. Zack's body responded especially strongly. His fat disappeared, muscle mass increased and his formerly lazy appearance became considerably more predatory.[4]
Victor compared the change to Zack effectively becoming a lion overnight and realized that his world's environment could cause ordinary animals to evolve simply through prolonged exposure.[4]
Zack was not satisfied with the natural transformation.
He communicated to Victor that he wanted Victor to personally alter him further. Victor repeatedly confirmed whether Zack genuinely understood the consequences and whether the decision was his own. Zack maintained his request.[4]
Victor eventually agreed. Rather than performing an elaborate ritual, he produced a single drop of his Blood and allowed it to fall into Zack's mouth.[4]
For several seconds nothing happened.
Then Zack's entire body became covered in pure crimson Power, began glowing and started rapidly increasing in size as an entirely new transformation began.[4]
The resulting species has never been formally named.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Zack's new capabilities became clearer after his transformation.
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] executed a Demon who had violated his laws, he called Zack from his own shadow. Zack emerged as an enormous dark feline resembling a combination of a tiger and panther, possessing glowing blood-red eyes.[5]
Victor threw the remains of the condemned Demon toward Zack. The transformed feline swallowed the Demon whole before casually returning to Victor's shadow.[5]
The scene established that Zack had somehow developed the ability to reside inside Victor's shadow and emerge from it whenever required.[5]
His combat abilities became substantially clearer during the operation involving [[Agares]].
While [[Anna Elderblood]] and [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] were working in Samar, Zack rested comfortably with Anna. He suddenly detected something approaching the room and immediately became alert.[6]
Dark Energy surrounded Zack's body as he grew into his enormous predatory form. Before the people surrounding Anna could properly identify the threat, Zack launched himself at the entrance and attacked.[6]
The target apparently disappeared, but Zack immediately retreated and remained beside Anna rather than relaxing. When Agares attempted to attack again through his dimensional ability, Zack suddenly attacked what appeared to everyone else to be empty space.[6]
His attack connected.
Agares was forcibly revealed and reacted with disbelief. The Demon had deliberately concealed his scent, aura and other detectable characteristics, yet Zack had still known exactly where he was.[6]
Agares himself could not understand how the "Cat Demon" had detected him.[6]
This incident demonstrated that Zack's senses after his transformation operated far beyond ordinary biological smell or hearing and were capable of perceiving threats concealed through supernatural and dimensional means.[6]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Zack's position within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction eventually expanded beyond that of a family guardian.
He began operating alongside War as one of Victor's direct messengers, accompanying the Herald when the Emperor wished to deliver declarations or warnings to other supernatural powers.[9]
Despite possessing enormous power, Zack continued spending most of his time in his smaller feline appearance.
By Chapter 1057, Victor explicitly explained just how misleading Zack's normal form had become. The formerly harmless house cat had evolved into an existence powerful enough to threaten an entire world.[7]
[[Anna Elderblood]] questioned why Zack continued communicating through ordinary meows despite no longer being remotely comparable to a normal cat.
Victor explained that Zack simply preferred behaving that way.
His current form was entirely voluntary. Zack could appear as an ordinary cat, tiger, lion or another feline shape whenever he wished, while his true supernatural body was an enormous beast whose power reached the level of a World [[Destroyer]].[7]
Zack's evolution had also begun affecting feline life throughout Dragons Nest.
According to Victor, practically every feline creature on the planet had started undergoing supernatural development under Zack's influence. These creatures were becoming increasingly dangerous, and the largest among them eventually developed enough power to destroy planets.[7]
Zack therefore ceased being merely an individually evolved animal. His existence had begun functioning as an evolutionary influence over feline creatures within Victor's world.[7]
Even after reaching this state, Zack remained beside Victor and his family exactly as he had since Victor's Human childhood.

### Skills and Abilities
Zack's exact species has not been formally identified. His current existence originated through two major evolutionary processes: prolonged exposure to the Energy-rich environment of Dragons Nest, followed by direct transformation through a drop of [[Victor Elderblood]]'s Blood.[4]
By the later era, Victor explicitly described Zack as an entity capable of threatening or destroying an entire world.[7]

#### Supernatural Feline Physiology
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Zack's evolved body possesses strength, speed, durability, agility and reflexes vastly beyond ordinary animals and most conventional supernatural creatures.[6][7]
- World-Destroying Power: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly describes Zack's true form as a threat powerful enough to destroy an entire world.[7]
- Immense Strength: Zack was capable of physically intercepting and knocking away [[Agares]], one of the strongest surviving Pillar Demons, despite Agares attacking through his dimensional ability.[6]
- Immense Speed and Reflexes: Zack detected Agares and reacted before the surrounding defenders could properly determine that an attack was occurring.[6]
- Immense Durability: As an existence possessing World-[[Destroyer]] levels of power, Zack's evolved body possesses durability correspondingly beyond ordinary supernatural beasts.[7]
- Abnormal Longevity: Zack had already exceeded twenty years of age while still apparently existing as an ordinary Maine Coon, demonstrating abnormal longevity even before his supernatural evolution.[1]
- Zack's current maximum lifespan has never been explicitly established, so complete [[Immortality]] should not presently be listed as confirmed.

#### Evolution
- Environmental Evolution: Zack's body naturally evolves in response to sufficiently powerful environments. Exposure to the Energy and ecosystem of Dragons Nest transformed him from an overweight house cat into a lean and muscular predator without deliberate external modification.[4]
- Energy Adaptation: Zack was capable of adapting to the extremely Energy-rich environment of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s planet and using those conditions to improve his physical existence.[4]
- Blood-Induced Evolution: Consuming a single drop of Victor's Blood triggered a drastic second evolution, surrounding Zack in crimson Power and transforming him into an unknown supernatural feline species.[4]
- Accelerated Growth: Zack's power continued increasing dramatically after his transformation, eventually reaching a level where he was considered capable of destroying a world.[4][7]

#### Shapeshifting
- Feline Shapeshifting: Zack can freely alter his physical appearance between different feline forms.[7]
- Cat Form: Zack normally chooses to retain the appearance of the Maine Coon he possessed before his evolution.[7]
- Tiger Form: He can assume the appearance of a significantly larger tiger-like creature.[7]
- Lion Form: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly states that Zack can become a lion if he wishes.[7]
- Predatory Beast Form: Zack possesses an enormous combat form resembling a mixture between a panther and tiger.[5][6]
- True Form: Zack's true supernatural form is an enormous feline beast possessing enough raw power to threaten an entire world.[7]
- Size Manipulation: Zack's transformation allows him to change his physical size alongside his species appearance, shifting from an ordinary domestic cat into an enormous predatory beast.[5][6][7]
The exact limits of Zack's transformation ability have not been explained. Victor's statement indicates extensive freedom among feline forms but does not establish unrestricted Shapeshifting into unrelated species.[7]

#### Darkness Abilities
- Dark Energy Manipulation: Zack can surround his body with supernatural Dark Energy when transforming into his combat form.[6]
- Darkness Manifestation: In his larger supernatural form, Zack has been depicted as an enormous dark feline whose body and presence are heavily associated with Darkness.[5][6]
- Shadow Dwelling: Zack can reside inside [[Victor Elderblood]]'s shadow for extended periods.[5][7]
- Shadow Emergence: Zack can emerge directly from Victor's shadow whenever summoned or when circumstances require his presence.[5][7]
- Shadow Concealment: While residing within Victor's shadow, Zack is effectively removed from ordinary observation until he chooses to emerge.[5]
There is currently insufficient evidence to claim that Zack possesses unrestricted Shadow Manipulation or can independently travel through any shadow in existence. What is explicitly demonstrated is his ability to inhabit and emerge from Victor's shadow.

#### Supernatural Senses
- Enhanced Senses: Zack possesses sensory abilities enormously superior to ordinary animals following his evolution.[6]
- Threat Detection: Zack can instinctively recognize concealed threats directed toward [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family. He detected [[Agares]] before the surrounding people understood that an attack was occurring.[6]
- Concealment Negation (Limited): Agares had concealed his scent, aura and other detectable traces, yet Zack continued accurately perceiving his position.[6]
- Dimensional Perception (Limited): Zack could perceive and physically intercept Agares while the Demon was utilizing an ability based around shifting between different planes or dimensions.[6]
- Zack attacked apparently empty space at exactly the position Agares was using to approach, successfully forcing the hidden Demon to react.[6]
- Predatory Awareness: Zack possesses extremely developed combat instincts, reacting automatically to threats without waiting for commands from Victor or Anna.[6]
The precise mechanism behind Zack's ability to detect Agares has not been explained. It therefore should not be exaggerated into universal omniscience or unrestricted dimensional awareness.

#### Feline Evolutionary Influence
- Feline Evolutionary Influence: Zack's supernatural existence passively influences other feline creatures living within Dragons Nest, causing them to undergo increasingly extreme supernatural evolution.[7]
- Species Empowerment: Felines affected by Zack's influence became dramatically more powerful than ordinary animals.[7]
- The strongest and largest feline creatures developing under this influence eventually became powerful enough to destroy planets.[7]
- Feline Progenitor-Like Influence (Unconfirmed Classification): Zack's effect on Dragons Nest's feline population resembles the manner in which a powerful source-being can influence the development of related creatures. However, the novel has not formally identified Zack as a Progenitor, so he should not currently be given that racial title.[7]

#### Intelligence and Communication
- Superhuman Intelligence: Zack possessed near-Human or Human-comparable intelligence even before his supernatural transformation. He understands complex speech, social situations and abstract requests.[3][8][4]
- Human Speech Comprehension: Zack can fully understand spoken Human language despite normally responding through meows.[3][8]
- Complex Communication: Zack can express sufficiently complicated concepts through his behavior and vocalizations that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] understood his request to be transformed into another species.[4]
- Independent Decision-Making: Zack is not simply an animal following orders. He independently chose to undergo supernatural evolution, protects Victor's relatives without prompting and determines how he wishes to use his abilities.[4][6]

#### Combat Abilities
- Predatory Combat: Zack fights primarily through the natural weapons and instincts of an enormous feline predator.
- Claws and Fangs: His combat forms possess proportionally enormous claws and fangs capable of damaging high-ranking supernatural beings.[6]
- Ambush [[Radar Warning Receiver (RWR)|Interception]]: Zack is particularly effective at countering surprise attacks because of his extraordinary threat detection. [[Agares]]' attempt to exploit dimensional concealment against Anna failed because Zack intercepted him immediately.[6]
- Protective Combat Instinct: Zack automatically positions himself between threats and members of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family when danger appears.[6]
- High-Ranking Demon Combat: Zack could engage Agares sufficiently effectively to surprise and disrupt him. Agares was a top-ranking Pillar Demon rather than an ordinary Demonic creature.[6]
The Chapter 894 encounter does not establish that Zack completely defeated Agares alone, as Agares was subsequently pursued and captured by a larger force. It does establish that Zack could detect, intercept and physically affect him.

#### Consumption
- Supernatural Consumption: Zack can swallow supernatural creatures considerably larger and more powerful than ordinary prey. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] once fed him the remains of an executed Demon, which Zack consumed whole before returning to Victor's shadow.[5]
There is currently no confirmation that consuming supernatural beings directly grants Zack their powers or Energy; therefore Power Absorption should not be listed.

#### Imperial Functions
- Imperial Messenger: Zack operates alongside War as one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s messengers and can accompany missions intended to deliver the Emperor's will to other supernatural factions.[9]
- Family Guardian: Zack functions as an informal protector of Victor's relatives and reacts automatically to threats against them.[6]

### Weaknesses
Zack currently possesses no explicitly identified supernatural weakness unique to his evolved species.
His original nature as an ordinary domestic cat has been fundamentally altered, and the novel has not established whether conventional Vampire weaknesses apply to his current existence. His transformation occurred directly through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood rather than through the standard Vampire transformation methods used on Humans, so ordinary assumptions concerning Noble or Plebian Vampire physiology should not automatically be applied to him.
