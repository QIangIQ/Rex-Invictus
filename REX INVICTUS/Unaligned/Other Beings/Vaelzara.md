### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 1205
Vaelzara is the tenth of the Ten Chief Gods of the [[Black Eclipse Pantheon]], in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]]. The name is a title. <Darkness>, whom [[Victor Elderblood]] addresses as Vharzath, bestows the ten, and this is the last of them. The charges, in the order Lily gives, are emptiness, night, silence, shadow, soul, fear, darkness, oblivion, inevitability, and [[Vaelthar]] himself. The tenth title is Vaelzara. The tenth charge is not a concept. It is Vaelthar.[1]
Vaelthar is not this God. He is the Throne of Origin, the Great Origin, above the ten, made as a possible Chosen One of Negativity and not chosen. He holds a Darkness-stained <BEGIN>. He is the loss Vharzath cannot replace, and by the time the titles are read he is already an empty shell. The list sets his name where the other nine seats have a concept. It does not say Vaelzara is him, wears him, or serves as his priest. The charge of the office is his name.[2][1]
No body, armor, face, or pronoun is given. The name is not spoken on a battlefield.[1]

### The Office
The other nine charges can be said in one word each. Void, night, silence, shadow, soul, fear, darkness, oblivion, inevitability. Violet calls the set concepts bound to negativity, with that last item still in the list she has just heard. A Chief God's place is not won. An elite is given the title, the realm, and a blessing that lifts a galaxy into reach, and then spends years learning a power that belongs to Darkness and not to the realm. The same rule is what the extraction states for the ten. It is not given a separate exception for the tenth, and it is not shown in use.[3][1]
The seat is under the [[Seven Black Thrones]], and [[Vaelthar]] is one of those seven. A Chief God can be replaced. He cannot. When the void dies, another [[Azrakar]] is raised, weaker, and nine more offices are still to be filled. Vaelzara stands on the replaceable side of that line. The name the charge points at does not.[2]

### Among the Ten
Last. [[Draveth]], inevitability, is the charge before it. The four Chief Gods on the field against War are [[Azrakar]], [[Kaelith]], [[Nerath]], and [[Vhalor]]. Vaelzara is not a fifth. Nine Chief Gods are emptied beside the Throne the charge names. The nine are not called by title. This name is in the list Lily speaks. It is not attached to one of the bodies, and it is not attached to [[Vaelthar]]'s.[4][1]
