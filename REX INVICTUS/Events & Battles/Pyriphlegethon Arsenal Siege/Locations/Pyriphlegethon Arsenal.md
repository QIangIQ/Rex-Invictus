## UOE (far future)

Forge-hold of the Hephaestean orders at Depth 40 of Tartarus, eight thousand years old, on a basalt spur over the bend of the Phlegethon. It made eighty-one percent of the theatre's keyed divine mortar shells and held the Katabasion. Destroyed at the end of the siege.
