## UOE (far future)

Archflamen of the Tartarean theatre, a legacy of Apollo and Hestia raised to the office at forty-one. His preaching tour of the holds made him a holy figure to the soldiers and thralls of the deep, and after the failed CODE 31 erasure at the Pilgrim Stair he declared the Pyriphlegethon Arsenal the Imperator's Hold and led it into revolt.
