## UOE (far future)

Lord-General of the Third Siege Army of Tartarus for the first eight rotations of the siege. A Pyrrhus Command nobleman and Athenian of the Academy on Callistus, he commanded from Quarterdeck Nineteen and was relieved after the failures of the second belt.
