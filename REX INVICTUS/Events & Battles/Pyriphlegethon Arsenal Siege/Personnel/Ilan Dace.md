## UOE (far future)

Haruspex of the Archflamen's household, a legacy of Hecate and Hermes who had drunk from the Lethe in measured doses since his youth. He built the Sworn of Orrhae, turned the Hecatean strays into the instrument of the Echo Mast and called the river factions to the Arsenal's aid, and his Kindlers' creed justified the harvest of the Third's uploads.
