## UOE (far future)

Death and re-embodiment of an implanted Imperial soldier: the implant uploads the soldier's pattern at death to Watchpoint Hades, where it is written into a body grown in nectar. Costs 400 litres of nectar, eleven seconds of bandwidth and nine days of vat time.
