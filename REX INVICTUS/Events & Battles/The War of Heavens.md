**[[The War of Heavens]]** is a far-future war between the Greek gods and the Shinto [[Japanese Pantheon]], in which "Blessed King" [[Percy Jackson]] nearly wiped out the Shinto. It broke Shinto-Greek relations and lies behind the [[Amaterasu Dominion]]'s wariness in the [[War of Princes]].

**Status in the text.** This comes from the author's vault notes. On the page only the [[Ch23 - Interlude I|Ch23]] monument's "Perseus the Blessed King" appears.
