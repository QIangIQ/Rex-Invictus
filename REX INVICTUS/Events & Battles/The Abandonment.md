**[[The Abandonment]]** is the old-timeline departure of the gods that left [[Olympus Primaris]] in ruins and [[Percy Jackson]] as its last guardian ([[Ch01 - Rex Imperator I]]). [[Demeter]]'s fields were left salted and empty, and [[Hermes]] swore to return but never did. See [[A01_Percy_LordCommander_OldTimeline]].
