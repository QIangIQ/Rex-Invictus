The first **[[Panpantheonic Summit]]** (3047.01.3.6, during [[The Great Expansion]]) was a meeting of Greek, Norse and Egyptian delegates that ended in chaos when [[Ares]] challenged [[Thor]] to a duel. [[Athena]] and [[Freyja]] prevented war, but tensions grew; [[Zeus]] considered preemptive strikes until [[Hera]] stopped him. The failure pushed [[Tochter des Elysium R&D]] and the Greek military-industrial colonies toward militarization in 3047–3048.
