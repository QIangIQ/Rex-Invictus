WarFall is a heavily fortified military city located in the western territory of [[Nightingale]] and the principal stronghold of the Adrasteia Clan. Situated beyond the [[Gate of Tartar]], WarFall serves as one of the most important defensive positions of Vampire civilization, separating the comparatively stable territories inhabited by Vampires from the immense unexplored western wilderness dominated by extraordinarily dangerous Monsters.[1][2]
Unlike the political, commercial and residential territories governed by Nightingale's other former Vampire Count Clans, WarFall developed almost entirely around warfare, survival, Monster hunting and territorial defense. Monster attacks are sufficiently common that the city's inhabitants regard large-scale battles as part of ordinary life, while the warriors of Clan Adrasteia are raised with considerably more practical combat experience than most Noble Vampires.[3][4]
WarFall also functions as a major center for the study and exploitation of Nightingale's Monsters. Clan Adrasteia specializes in technology developed from Monster materials and in the biology of Monsters, using the creatures surrounding the city not merely as enemies but as sources of weapons, knowledge and evolutionary resources.[5]

### Location
WarFall is located within the immense western territory controlled historically by the [[Adrasteia Clan]]. Clan Adrasteia possessed the largest territorial domain among the old Vampire Count families, although much of this territory remained uninhabited or undeveloped because of the constant presence of Monsters.[6]
The approach to Adrasteia territory differs considerably from the populated territories controlled by families such as the [[Snow Clan]], [[Scarlett Clan]] and [[Fulger Clan]]. Large portions of the surrounding land are deserted, covered primarily by vegetation rather than towns or conventional Noble Vampire settlements.[7]
Access to the dangerous western region is controlled through the [[Gate of Tartar]], an enormous gate constructed between two colossal mountain ranges. The mountains form a gigantic corridor separating western [[Nightingale]] from the safer territories further east. Travelers passing through the Gate effectively enter the frontier surrounding WarFall and the territories in which Clan Adrasteia continuously battles Monsters.[8]
The name and location are appropriate to the region's reputation: old stories warned that passing through the Gate of Tartar meant that only death awaited beyond it. Although such legends could initially be dismissed as exaggeration, the enormous concentration of hostile Monsters throughout WarFall's frontier demonstrates that the warning was founded upon genuine danger.[9]

### Description
WarFall is best understood as a fortress-city built for permanent warfare. Virtually every aspect of the settlement and surrounding Clan territory was developed around defending against Monster attacks, supporting combatants and allowing Clan Adrasteia to continuously expand or reclaim land from the creatures of western [[Nightingale]].[10][11]
The city's culture is consequently considerably different from conventional Nightingale society. Adrasteia warriors are accustomed to seeing bloodshed, casualties and dangerous supernatural creatures on a regular basis, resulting in a population whose military discipline and practical battlefield experience are considerably greater than those of ordinary Vampires raised primarily in protected territories.[12]
[[Victor Elderblood]] immediately noticed this distinction when he began operating with Eleanor and the Valkyries. Rather than viewing Clan Adrasteia's warriors as pampered aristocrats whose strength existed mostly through inherited Bloodlines, Victor respected them as true soldiers shaped by an environment where failing to fight properly could result in the destruction of their home.[13][14]
WarFall is also highly isolated from the rest of Nightingale. Visitors are uncommon enough that its automated security systems were designed primarily around recognizing members of Clan Adrasteia, while outsiders are required to carry special identification to avoid being attacked by the city's defenses.[15]

### Defenses
WarFall possesses one of the most extensive defensive systems demonstrated in old [[Nightingale]]. The city's defenses combine natural geography, Clan Adrasteia's supernatural abilities, conventional fortifications and automated weapon systems.

#### Gate of Tartar
The [[Gate of Tartar]] forms the principal entrance toward WarFall's western territory. It stands between two enormous and unnaturally shaped mountain ranges that create a narrow corridor leading toward the Gate.[16]
The structure is decorated by two gigantic hooded skeletal figures carrying enormous scythes, visually reinforcing the warning associated with entering the western territories.[17]
Because of its position between the mountain ranges, the Gate functions as a natural and artificial choke point, restricting movement between the Monster-infested frontier and the rest of Vampire-controlled [[Nightingale]].[18]

#### Mountain Barrier
The enormous mountains surrounding the Gate act as part of WarFall's larger defensive structure. Even Vampires familiar with the region have commented upon how unnatural the mountain formations appear, leading to speculation that an ancient member of Clan Adrasteia may have created or altered them through the Clan's Earth-related powers.[19]
The mountains also prevent visitors from simply bypassing the Gate through ordinary travel. Attempting to fly directly over the region is extremely dangerous because WarFall possesses dedicated anti-aircraft defenses.[20]

#### Automated Defenses
Clan Adrasteia developed automated weapon systems capable of distinguishing authorized Clan members from unidentified intruders.[21]
Because essentially all permanent residents living beyond the mountain barrier originally belonged to Clan Adrasteia, the weapons could automatically recognize members of the family while targeting anyone lacking authorization.[22]

#### Visitor Identification
Outsiders entering WarFall receive special black identification symbols. The visitor places a drop of blood upon the object, registering themselves within the city's security system.[23]
Visitors are specifically warned not to lose these identifiers while inside Adrasteia territory, as doing so could result in WarFall's automatic defensive weapons identifying them as hostile targets.[24]

#### City Walls
WarFall is surrounded by enormous fortified walls capable of resisting the continual Monster assaults occurring throughout the region. Clan Adrasteia's soldiers consider these defenses extraordinarily difficult to breach, with the Valkyries later describing WarFall's fortifications as effectively impenetrable to ordinary hostile Vampires.[25]
The walls contain multiple defensive sectors, including a western wall used during one of the largest Monster invasions in WarFall's history.[26]

### Military
WarFall is the center of Clan Adrasteia's military power. Unlike the [[Scarlett Clan]], whose reputation is primarily built around exceptional individual warriors and martial training, Clan Adrasteia operates an entire society permanently adapted to frontier warfare.
Its forces include ordinary Clan soldiers, Monster hunters and elite combat groups such as the Valkyries, who serve directly beneath Eleanor and [[Rose Adrasteia Elderblood]].[27][28]
The inhabitants receive enough regular combat experience that Monster attacks are treated more like military operations than extraordinary disasters. When hordes approach the walls, the city's forces organize defensive deployments, identify the classes and command structure of the attacking creatures and mobilize without the widespread panic that would occur elsewhere.[29][30]
The city's military doctrine also emphasizes withdrawal when necessary. WarFall's warriors understand that attempting to kill every Monster encountered is strategically foolish, particularly when fighting far from established support positions. During the major expedition led by Eleanor, the group retreated after encountering twenty [[Titans]] rather than waste their strength in an unnecessary battle.[25]

### Monster Research and Technology
WarFall is one of [[Nightingale]]'s principal centers for the study of Monsters.
The city specializes in technology derived from Monsters and in the biological study of Monster species.[5] This specialization naturally developed from Clan Adrasteia's environment: the Clan has spent generations fighting creatures possessing unusual physiology, regeneration, poisons, supernatural abilities and radically different evolutionary characteristics.
Monster materials obtained during hunts can consequently become technological resources rather than simply corpses to be discarded.
WarFall's expertise does have limits. During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s expedition, even Clan Adrasteia possessed relatively little reliable information concerning the mysterious Alphas commanding several Monster populations, forcing Eleanor, Rose and the Valkyries to investigate their behavior directly.[5]

### Monster Ecology
The territories surrounding WarFall contain one of the most dangerous concentrations of supernatural life shown on [[Nightingale]]. The strength and variety of Monsters generally increase as expeditions travel farther from the city and established military outposts.[31]
Known Monster categories encountered around WarFall include:
- Minions — Numerous lower-ranked Monsters commonly forming the bulk of large hordes.[32]
- Ogres — Large humanoid Monsters possessing considerable physical power and regeneration.[33]
- Predators — Highly dangerous specialized Monsters encountered deeper within the western territory.[34]
- [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] — Higher-ranked creatures used by the Alpha-controlled Monster factions.[35]
- Destroyers — Extremely dangerous Monster types deployed during larger conflicts.[36]
- Behemoths — Gigantic Monsters powerful enough to threaten major supernatural combatants and influence lesser Monsters around them.[37]
- Wyverns — Flying Monsters used both independently and as mounts by more intelligent creatures.[38]
- Onis — Among the numerous creatures participating in later attacks on WarFall.[39]
- [[Titans]] — Monsters classified above Behemoths. Twenty Titans exceeding fifty meters in height were encountered approximately 42,000 kilometers from WarFall.[25]
- Alphas — Intelligent high-ranking Monsters capable of commanding other species, strengthening Monster hordes, organizing ambushes and developing military strategies against Clan Adrasteia.[40][5]
The region is therefore not a conventional wilderness inhabited by isolated magical animals. It supports entire Monster populations, hierarchies and intelligent factions capable of coordinating attacks against Vampire civilization.

### Western Frontier
WarFall represents the border between controlled Vampire territory and a western region whose true scale remains largely unexplored.
Clan Adrasteia continuously sends hunting and exploration parties away from the city, creating temporary camps and advancing progressively deeper into Monster territory.[41]
The extraordinary distances involved demonstrate the scale of the region. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Eleanor's expedition reached approximately 25,597 kilometers from WarFall while still encountering active Monster populations and continued much farther afterward.[5]
At approximately 42,000 kilometers from WarFall, the group encountered twenty [[Titans]] larger than fifty meters in height and concluded that continuing without a larger military force and an established outpost would be strategically irresponsible.[25]
The expedition consequently demonstrated that WarFall is not simply defending one nearby Monster nest. It is positioned against an enormous frontier whose hostile ecosystem extends for tens of thousands of kilometers beyond established Vampire civilization.

### Culture
The permanent danger surrounding WarFall fundamentally shaped the culture of Clan Adrasteia.

#### Warrior Society
WarFall's population values practical strength, courage, teamwork and proven battlefield experience considerably more than ordinary Vampire aristocratic etiquette.[42]
The Clan consequently possesses comparatively little patience for sheltered Noble Vampires who speak arrogantly without possessing equivalent combat experience.

#### Isolationism
Clan Adrasteia historically remained socially isolated from much of [[Nightingale]]. Visitors to WarFall were uncommon, while essentially everyone permanently residing within the western defensive territory belonged to the Clan.[43]
This isolation also allowed the Clan to hide several of its greatest secrets, most notably the ritual through which members incorporated Monster bloodlines into themselves.[44][45]

#### Attitude Toward Nightingale Nobility
The warriors of WarFall possess little automatic reverence for [[Nightingale]]'s aristocracy. Their respect is generally based more upon demonstrated strength and character than titles.[25]
Even the Vampire Royal Family did not automatically receive the near-worshipful treatment common elsewhere in Nightingale, while only a comparatively small number of Vampires and Clans earned genuine respect from the Adrasteia warriors.[25]

### Economy and Self-Sufficiency
WarFall's constant access to Monsters gives it an unusual economic foundation.
The city is largely self-sustaining and derives substantial resources from the creatures killed during the regular defense of its territory. Monster corpses provide biological material, research subjects, weapon resources and other products that can be exploited by Clan Adrasteia.[46][5]
This relationship creates a cycle unique within [[Nightingale]]: Monsters threaten WarFall's survival, but fighting those same creatures provides the Clan with many of the resources necessary to improve its defenses and warriors.

### History

#### Establishment
The exact date of WarFall's construction has not been stated. The city was already an established military stronghold long before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] entered Vampire society and developed alongside Clan Adrasteia's responsibility for protecting [[Nightingale]]'s dangerous western frontier.
Its defenses, Monster-based culture and geographical isolation indicate that WarFall evolved over generations of warfare rather than being constructed as a conventional Noble Vampire settlement.[47][48]

#### Victor's First Visit
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] first properly entered the WarFall region while traveling with [[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]], Siena Scarlett, Lacus Scarlett, Pepper Scarlett and others.[49]
As they approached the [[Gate of Tartar]], a Monster horde began advancing toward the city walls. Rather than remain inside the carriage, Victor immediately rushed toward the fighting, forcing Eleanor to warn him not to fly above the mountain defenses because WarFall's anti-aircraft weapons could identify him as an intruder.[50]
The confrontation eventually brought Victor against a large Monster force and a Behemoth capable of strengthening other creatures around itself. Victor's unusual enthusiasm for the battlefield immediately demonstrated why he felt unusually comfortable in WarFall's environment.[51][52]

#### WarFall Expedition
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later returned to WarFall to fulfill his agreement with Eleanor and joined the Valkyries on a major expedition beyond the secure territory surrounding the city.[53]
The operation traveled progressively farther west and encountered increasingly dangerous opponents. Victor and the Valkyries fought Ogres, Predators, Behemoths and several unusual Monster variants while learning more about the Alphas commanding the creatures.[54]
At 25,597 kilometers from WarFall, Eleanor's group took refuge within a cave after surviving an Alpha's trap and continued analyzing the biology and command structure of the enemy.[5]
The expedition eventually reached approximately 42,000 kilometers from the city, where twenty [[Titans]] were discovered. Recognizing that fighting all twenty without a proper forward base would waste excessive resources, the group ended the expedition and returned toward WarFall.[25]
The journey became one of the clearest demonstrations of both the enormous scale of WarFall's frontier and the level of danger Clan Adrasteia confronts as part of normal military life.:contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

#### The Great Monster Invasion
During the later Demonic War period, WarFall experienced what was described as an unprecedented invasion, something that had not occurred since the city's founding.[55]
Hundreds of thousands of Monsters attacked WarFall in waves, including Behemoths, Onis, Predators, Wyverns, Minions and at least two Alphas. Clan Adrasteia's defensive systems operated at maximum capacity while its warriors fought across the city walls.[55]
Despite the unprecedented scale of the attack, WarFall did not immediately collapse. Its defenses and the presence of numerous elite combatants transformed the surrounding territory into a massive battlefield.
Eleanor fought directly alongside [[Violet Elderblood Snow]], [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]], [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]], Siena, Lacus, Pepper and [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]], while [[Mizuki Elderblood]] defended the western wall using large-scale Onmyō techniques.[55]
The battle demonstrated that WarFall's fortifications were designed to withstand precisely the kind of enormous supernatural warfare that would devastate ordinary settlements.

#### Elder God Invasion
WarFall later became one of the principal battlefields during the war against the [[Elder Gods Pantheon]]. Enemy forces flooded the region with enormous Monster armies, many of which were Behemoth Class or above.[56]
The invasion was particularly dangerous because the attacking forces were effectively immortal and had been strengthened beyond the Monsters normally encountered by Clan Adrasteia. Reports indicated that thousands to hundreds of thousands of enemies were active throughout the WarFall region.[56]
[[Scathach]] assumed responsibility for WarFall's defense alongside Eleanor, Rose, Ariel and the Valkyries.[56]
The scale of the fighting forced Eleanor to use her Creation abilities over enormous portions of the Adrasteia territory, while other members of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s coalition defended [[Nightingale]] simultaneously against the Elder Gods' armies.[56]
WarFall's survival during this invasion reinforced its historic role as Nightingale's primary fortress against threats emerging from the western territories.

### Notable Locations

#### Adrasteia Castle
The main castle of [[Adrasteia Clan]] is located within WarFall and serves as the administrative and military center of Eleanor's territory.[56]
Important military meetings, strategic planning and command decisions concerning WarFall's defenses are conducted within the castle.

#### Western Wall
One of WarFall's principal defensive walls. During the unprecedented Monster invasion, Mizuki positioned herself above the western wall and used large-scale weather and Onmyō techniques against the invading forces.[55]

#### Gate of Tartar
Main article: [[Gate of Tartar]]
The monumental gateway connecting the western Adrasteia territory with the rest of [[Nightingale]]. The Gate, surrounding mountains and automated defenses form the outer defensive approach toward WarFall.[57]

#### Western Wilderness
The enormous Monster-dominated territory beyond WarFall. The region remains incompletely explored and contains increasingly powerful creatures the farther expeditions travel from the city.[58]

### Trivia
- WarFall's name reflects its identity as a city whose entire history is dominated by warfare and the constant fall of Monsters around its territory.
- Clan Adrasteia historically controlled the largest territory among [[Nightingale]]'s former Vampire Count Clans, although much of it remained unused because of the Monster population.[59]
- Most permanent residents behind the [[Gate of Tartar]] originally belonged to Clan Adrasteia, allowing the city's automated weapons to identify outsiders relatively easily.[60]
- Visitors receive blood-activated identification symbols so WarFall's automated defenses do not attack them.[61]
- WarFall possesses anti-aircraft weaponry powerful enough that even Eleanor specifically warned [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] not to fly over the mountain defenses without authorization.[62]
- WarFall specializes in technology derived from Monsters and Monster biology.[5]
- The expedition led by Eleanor traveled at least 42,000 kilometers away from WarFall before turning back.[25]
- Twenty [[Titans]] larger than fifty meters were encountered at that distance, and fighting all of them without a larger force and an established forward outpost was considered reckless even by Victor.[25]
- WarFall's warriors considered the city's defenses sufficiently formidable that even hostile Elder Vampires from Nightingale would be unable to simply invade the city.[25]
- Chapter 653 describes a Monster assault on WarFall as the first invasion of that magnitude since the city's founding.
- WarFall later became a primary Nightingale battlefield during the Elder God invasion in Chapter 880.
