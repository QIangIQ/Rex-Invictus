The Supernatural Gathering, also called the Supernatural Meeting or Gathering of Supernatural Beings, is a recurring diplomatic summit hosted by The Owner of Limbo. It brings together sovereign rulers, [[God-Kings]] and officially recognized representatives of the major supernatural civilizations associated with Earth. Rather than functioning as a single world government, the Gathering provides neutral ground where independent factions can negotiate issues whose consequences extend beyond the jurisdiction of any one race, Pantheon or supernatural kingdom.[1][2]
The institution is politically significant because agreements recognized there can affect multiple major supernatural powers at once. Rules can be proposed or rejected, inter-faction disputes can be presented publicly, wars can be declared before the international supernatural community and changes in the political structure of Earth can receive recognition from the other sovereign powers.[3][4][5][6]
The Gathering nevertheless possesses no permanent sovereign authority over all of its participants. The rulers attending it retain control over their own nations and Pantheons unless they voluntarily accept a rule, agreement or obligation recognized through the meeting.

### Purpose and Function
The supernatural societies connected to Earth are divided between numerous races, Pantheons, kingdoms, hidden dimensions and independent factions. Many possess enough military or metaphysical power that their decisions can affect populations far outside their own territories.
The Gathering exists to provide a common diplomatic framework before disagreements escalate directly into supernatural warfare.
International Rules
Representatives can propose, maintain, alter or reject rules intended to affect more than one supernatural civilization.[3][4]
Neutral Diplomacy
Historically hostile factions can negotiate without the meeting itself immediately becoming a battlefield because violence inside the venue is prohibited.[7]
International Recognition
Declarations, agreements, newly established political entities and major changes can be announced before Earth's other supernatural rulers.
Crisis Management
Problems involving Soul systems, Pantheon territories, inter-racial warfare or planetary reconstruction can be discussed when no single faction possesses sufficient jurisdiction to decide the matter alone.[8][6]
Subjects addressed through the Gathering have included:
- Interference between supernatural factions.
- Protection and non-interference agreements.
- Rules affecting Divine Artifacts.
- Recognition of new Pantheons.
- The legal and political treatment of supernatural races.
- Changes to Earth's Heaven and Hell systems.
- War declarations.
- Coordination between military allies.
- Major changes to Earth's geography and civilization.
- Transfer of ownership over an entire Pantheon dimension.[2][3][4][8][5][6][9][10]

### Host and Neutrality
The Owner of Limbo serves as the recurring host and mediator of the Supernatural Gathering.[1]
His position differs from the rulers seated around the meeting table. He does not represent a conventional Earth faction seeking political advantage. Instead, he owns the venue, directs the proceedings and possesses sufficient authority to enforce the rules governing behavior inside his territory.
This distinction is essential to the Gathering's function. Gods, Vampires, Demons, [[Werewolves]], Angels, Witches and other beings with centuries or millennia of hostility can occupy the same room because attacking one another inside the Owner's domain would mean challenging the authority protecting the summit itself.
During the First Gathering, the Owner directly warned those present that aggression would not be tolerated and that offenders could become residents of his prison.[7]
During the Third Gathering, the same principle remained intact when tension between [[Hela Elderblood]] and [[Shiva]] escalated enough that both began releasing their powers. The Owner immediately reminded them that fighting would not be permitted within his territory.[11]
Neutrality does not mean enforced peace
The Owner prevents the Gathering itself from becoming a battlefield. He does not prevent sovereign factions from rejecting proposals, threatening retaliation, declaring wars or fighting one another after leaving his territory.

### Location
The Supernatural Gathering is primarily associated with [[Limbo's Island]], an anomalous floating domain controlled by the Owner of Limbo.[1]
The formal summit is held inside a large palace containing a round meeting table. Official Representatives occupy the principal seats while companions, advisers and other members of their delegations remain behind or near them.[2]
The environment surrounding the arrivals can be altered by the Owner. During the ceremonial introductions of the First Gathering, different settings were displayed for incoming factions, including a Japanese-style environment during the presentation of the Youkai delegation.[12]

### Representation and Procedure

#### Representatives and companions
A distinction exists between a faction's Representative and the other individuals attending as part of the delegation.
The Representative acts as the recognized political voice of their faction during formal proceedings. They present requests, respond to other proposals and participate in collective decisions.[2][4]
The position is commonly held by a ruler or exceptionally trusted authority, but it does not necessarily mean that individual is the single strongest member of their entire civilization.
During the First Gathering, for example, [[Shiva]] represented the Gods even though other major Divine rulers such as [[Zeus]] and [[Thor]] were also present.[4]
Companions can include:
- Advisers.
- Heirs.
- Military leaders.
- Spouses.
- Important political figures.
- Powerful warriors.
- Individuals personally invited by the Representative.
They may participate in surrounding conversations, but they do not automatically possess the formal political authority of the Representative.

#### Ceremonial arrival
The First Gathering demonstrates a public presentation period in which major factions and their attendees are formally introduced. Portions of these arrivals were broadcast through supernatural communication systems, allowing outside supernatural populations to observe the identities and presentation of the attending powers.[1][12][13]
The ceremony is therefore more than spectacle. The manner in which a ruler arrives, the companions standing beside them and the power demonstrated by their delegation can communicate political strength before formal negotiation begins.

#### Informal diplomacy
Not every important agreement is created around the formal table.
Participants can negotiate privately before or between official discussions. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s conversations with Otsuki Haruna during the First Gathering eventually influenced the protection she requested for the Youkai when formal proceedings began.[14][15][2]
The Gathering therefore functions simultaneously as a formal international summit and a rare opportunity for rulers who normally inhabit different dimensions or civilizations to speak directly.

#### Formal proceedings
Once formal proceedings begin, the Representatives sit around the central table and the Owner directs the discussion.[2]
Requests and disputes can be presented before the assembled powers. Depending upon the issue, the other Representatives may approve, reject, negotiate or simply recognize what has been announced.
The Gathering is not governed by one universal form of decision-making. A new shared rule can require collective approval, while a unilateral declaration of war may simply be announced and subsequently judged politically by the other factions.

### The Rule Book
One of the most important mechanisms associated with the Gathering is the Rule Book.[3]
The Rule Book contains rules accepted through the Gathering and functions as considerably more than an ordinary written legal document. Chapter 518 describes it as a high-level contractual mechanism encompassing the powerful beings participating in the system.
Unlike a conventional Soul Contract between two private parties, the Rule Book represents the collective rules recognized by the meeting.
Violating a recognized rule can result in punishment according to the severity of the offense, including consequences capable of affecting the offender at the level of the Soul.[3]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Haruna both requested personal copies during the First Gathering.[3]
Example — Youkai Non-Interference Rule
Haruna successfully obtained a rule granting her people 500 years of non-interference. The representatives approved the request, and the Owner formally declared that any faction violating the protection would be punished according to the Rule Book.[2][3]

#### Haruna's request
As one of the new guests, Haruna was allowed to present her request early in the formal meeting.
She asked that no supernatural faction interfere with the Youkai for 500 years.[2]
The request reflected the vulnerable condition of the Youkai after their previous political and military crises. Haruna wanted enough time for her people to rebuild without stronger Pantheons exploiting the instability surrounding them.
[[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] attempted to sponsor the arrangement on the condition that the Youkai become permanent allies of [[Nightingale]], but Haruna rejected his proposal.[2]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] instead offered support without demanding that Haruna surrender the future political independence of her people. He also opposed the idea of basing their relationship primarily upon a Soul Contract, regarding an alliance built entirely around enforced distrust as inherently weak.[2]
The other Representatives ultimately approved the non-interference period.
The Owner formally recognized the decision through the Gathering's system, granting Haruna and her people 500 years of protection from external interference.[3]
Result — 500 Years of Non-Interference
The agreement became one of the clearest early demonstrations that the Gathering could create enforceable international supernatural rules rather than merely host conversation.[3]

#### The Divine Artifact proposal
When the Divine Representative was invited to speak, [[Shiva]] presented a request originating from the Greek side of the Divine community.
A Greek Divine Artifact had previously been stolen and copied. Although the original Artifact had been recovered, several copies remained missing.[4]
The Gods proposed a new rule requiring Divine Artifacts belonging to a Pantheon to be returned when recovered by another recognized faction.
The proposal was put before the Representatives.
It failed by majority vote.[4]
The incident demonstrated that even requests made by major Pantheons were not automatically accepted simply because powerful Gods supported them.

#### Arrival of Lilith and Diablo
The Demonic delegation had initially been uncertain because Lilith was believed to be unavailable.
During Chapter 519, the Owner abruptly announced that circumstances had changed and introduced a sixth major guest: the representative of Hell.[4]
Lilith entered as the Mother and Progenitor of Demonkind.
[[Diablo]] accompanied her.
His appearance immediately raised the tension within the room. Unlike Lilith's formal status as Representative, Diablo had arrived with an entirely different objective.

#### Outbreak of the Demonic War
By Chapter 520, [[Diablo]] revealed that his forces were already moving throughout the Human World.[7]
The timing exposed one of the central limitations of the Gathering.
The Owner could prevent violence inside Limbo's territory, but the neutrality of the meeting did not freeze the armies of sovereign powers operating elsewhere.
While some of Earth's greatest rulers were gathered around the same table, Diablo's Legions were already attacking territories outside [[Limbo's Island]].
The First Gathering therefore transitioned directly into the opening of the large-scale Demonic War.[7][16]
Historical Turning Point — Diplomacy Gives Way to War
The first major Gathering shown in the story ended by demonstrating both the importance and the limitations of supernatural diplomacy: the summit could create rules inside a shared political framework, but it could not prevent a sovereign power from beginning a war outside the venue.

#### Victor's arrival
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s entrance immediately revealed how much his position had changed.
His presence placed enormous pressure upon the meeting chamber, and even the Owner of Limbo recognized in him a characteristic associated with beings capable of becoming Overlords: the ability of their very presence to demand recognition from other rulers.[18][8]
The political seating that followed visually reflected Earth's changing alliances.
Tasha and Haruna moved closer to Victor, while [[Amaterasu]] likewise seated herself near his side.[8]
Without a formal declaration, the room itself displayed which rulers were beginning to gravitate toward Victor's political sphere.

#### Velnorah's presence
The Owner was accompanied by Velnorah Xyphora Thaloria II, who was introduced as his assistant during the meeting.[8]
Her appearance immediately attracted [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s attention because her biology, Energy and technological characteristics did not resemble the conventional supernatural beings of Earth.
Velnorah likewise analyzed Victor and recognized his unusual fusion of Dragon and Vampire characteristics, his Progenitor status and the enormous concentration of Pure Negative Energy surrounding him.[19]
Her presence represented one of the earliest direct indications during the Gathering that Earth's politics existed inside a much larger interstellar and cosmic environment.

#### Restructuring the afterlife
One of the Owner's first agenda items concerned the structure of the afterlife.
Recent decisions by the Primordial authorities had reduced the number of major Hells associated with the flow of Souls to three primary Soul Zones.[8]
The Heavens had not initially undergone the same consolidation, creating an imbalance in the movement of Souls.
At the request of the [[The Judges of The Abyss|Three Judges]] of the Abyss, the corresponding Heavenly structure was reorganized as well.
The number of major Heavens receiving the positive portion of Souls was reduced to three.[8]
This suddenly placed previously separated Pantheon territories in much closer metaphysical proximity.
The Owner established barriers intended to remain for approximately one year, giving the affected Pantheons time to negotiate how Authority and responsibility over the new neighboring territories would be divided.[8]
Cosmic Administrative Change
The Second Gathering was not limited to Earthly politics. It became the venue through which the affected rulers were informed that the basic Heaven-and-Hell infrastructure processing their Souls had been reorganized by authorities above ordinary Pantheon governments.[8]

#### Recognition of Dragon's Nest
The meeting subsequently addressed [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s destruction of the old Greek political structure and the creation of the civilization developing beneath him.
When asked the name of his Pantheon, Victor selected Dragon's Nest.[20]
The name was immediately registered by the greater System, formally recognizing the faction as a Pantheon.
Dragon's Nest differed from conventional Pantheons because Victor did not intend it to belong exclusively to Gods or a single cultural species.
He openly stated that Dragons, Vampires, Demons, Angels, [[Werewolves]], Youkai, Humans, extraterrestrial races and other beings could belong to his civilization so long as they accepted its laws and his leadership.[20]

#### Recognition of Demons as citizens
The political treatment of Demons became one of the Second Gathering's major disputes.
Many supernatural civilizations historically classified Demons collectively as monsters to be exterminated.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] rejected that approach.
His argument was not that Demons were harmless. Instead, he argued that brutality was not unique to their species and that intelligent Demons capable of following laws should be judged as citizens rather than condemned solely because of their race.[20][21]
Victor demonstrated the developed civilization operating within Hell and introduced a Runic identification system used on ordinary Demons under his government.[21]
The Runes allowed outsiders to identify Demons belonging to his civilization and made it possible for Victor's government to trace and punish citizens who committed crimes outside its territory.
His most sensitive intelligence and assassination forces were deliberately excluded from the publicly identifiable version of the system.[21]
Political Shift — Demons as a Civilization
The proposal reframed the international question surrounding Demons. Instead of asking whether every Demon should automatically be exterminated, the discussion increasingly became whether citizens of Victor's Hell obeyed the same political responsibilities expected from other supernatural civilizations.

#### Evidence against the Egyptian Pantheon
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] then presented evidence involving the conspiracy against himself and his family.
Recordings showed members of the [[Egyptian Pantheon]], including conversations involving Ra and Seth, cooperating with forces connected to the Elder Gods.[5]
Ra initially dismissed the recordings as fabricated and argued that infiltrating a Divine Pantheon sufficiently to obtain such intelligence should have been impossible.
Victor revealed that Nyx Elderblood had been responsible for the surveillance.
Her stealth had been improved sufficiently through Victor's support that even Divine defenses had failed to detect her.
Victor demanded the Egyptian leadership's submission.
They refused.

#### Declaration of war
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] responded publicly:
He declared war upon the [[Egyptian Pantheon]], the Elder Gods of [[Nightingale]] and [[New Dawn]].[5]
The declaration immediately shocked the wider supernatural community, particularly because Earth had only recently survived the previous Demonic War.
Victor distinguished his campaign from [[Diablo]]'s earlier invasion. He was not declaring a war of indiscriminate extermination. His stated targets were the individuals and organizations responsible for attacks upon his family and faction.
Those uninvolved in the conspiracy were not intended to be slaughtered merely for belonging to the same civilization.[22]

#### International support
The reaction around the table demonstrated how dramatically [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s diplomatic position had changed.
Several factions publicly supported him, including:
- The [[Shinto Pantheon]] under [[Amaterasu]].
- The Youkai under Haruna.
- The [[Werewolves]] under Tasha.
- [[Nightingale]]'s Noble Vampires under Vlad.
- The faction represented by [[Sucellus]].
- The [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]] under [[Shiva]].
- The Angels under the [[Heavenly Father]].[22]
Shiva's support followed Victor's clarification that surrendering enemies and innocents unrelated to the conspiracy would be spared.[22]
Even Heaven ultimately supported the Demon King.
The political symbolism was considerable: one year after [[Diablo]]'s invasion, representatives of Angels, Demons, Vampires, Werewolves, Youkai and multiple Pantheons were preparing to participate in the same retaliatory campaign.

#### Restructuring Earth
The final major issue involved the future of Earth itself.
The [[Second Genesis War]] had left enormous regions depopulated and severely damaged.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] proposed using supernatural power to repair the planet rather than allowing ecological and geographical damage to persist for thousands of years.[6]
His proposal included merging Earth's fragmented landmasses into a massive supercontinent, restoring environmental damage and creating conditions under which Human civilization could rebuild in a more coordinated manner.[6]
Victor argued that he possessed enough Energy to perform much of the physical reconstruction himself, but deliberately proposed it as a collective supernatural effort because Earth's future affected every Pantheon and faction connected to the planet.[6]
From Secret Politics to Planetary Administration
The First Gathering debated whether factions should interfere with the Youkai and how Divine Artifacts should be handled. By the Second, the rulers were discussing the physical reconstruction of Earth itself.[2][4][6]

Victor presented Demons under his government as accountable citizens of a functioning supernatural civilization.[20][21]

Multiple Pantheons and races backed Victor's campaign against the conspirators.[22]

Victor proposed healing the planet and consolidating its continents into a new supercontinent.[6]
Victor's Status Transformed
He left the Gathering as one of the primary rulers shaping the collective political future of Earth's supernatural civilizations.

### Third Supernatural Gathering
Gathering Record III
The Third Supernatural Gathering
Chapters 967–972
Convened after Ragnarök and the collapse of [[Odin]]'s rule, the Third Gathering developed into an unprecedented international auction in which Hela offered the former [[Norse Pantheon]] dimension to the surviving powers.
The Third Supernatural Gathering took place approximately two months after [[Hela Elderblood]] defeated Odin and brought the Asgardian Civil War to an end.[9]
The political landscape had again changed dramatically.
The Greek and Egyptian Pantheons had already ceased functioning in their previous forms, and Odin's defeat meant a third major ancient Pantheon had now collapsed.
Unlike those previous cases, the final battle for [[Asgard]] had publicly been won by Hela rather than [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] himself.[9]
Hela possessed the Pantheon but had little desire to become another conventional God-Queen responsible for administrating everything Odin had left behind.
Her solution was unprecedented.
She intended to auction the Norse Pantheon dimension.[9]

#### Background
Following [[Odin]]'s defeat, [[Asgard]] remained largely isolated for roughly two months.
Hela eventually released edited recordings demonstrating that she had defeated Odin and ended the previous government.[9]
Outside observers were especially confused by the disappearance of many mortal populations formerly associated with the [[Norse Pantheon]].
The truth was only revealed afterward.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction had secretly extracted multiple useful populations and resources during the conflict while Nyx Elderblood concealed the operations from outside observation.[23]
By the time Hela announced the auction, Dragon's Nest had already acquired many of the assets it considered strategically important.
Hela also did not intend to hand over everything historically belonging to Asgard. The primary commodity being sold was control of the Pantheon's dimension and territorial key; Gods and treasures she intended to retain were not simply included as miscellaneous property in the sale.[9]

An Entire Pantheon Put on the Market
The Third Gathering transformed from a conventional political summit into a bidding war over one of Earth's major Divine territories. The commodity was not a city or Artifact, but the dimensional territory upon which an ancient Pantheon had existed.

#### Violet's negotiations
[[Violet Elderblood Snow]] became one of Dragon's Nest's primary negotiators during the auction.
Rather than merely accompanying [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], she actively controlled much of the faction's presentation and bidding strategy.[24][25]
Among the creations revealed during negotiations was the Tower of Nightmares.
Its internal system allowed individuals to repeatedly fight, die and return within an Eternal Loop while their Souls were nourished by the process, making it an exceptionally valuable training environment.[24]
Even the Owner of Limbo was surprised by some of the Concepts incorporated into its construction.[24]
Violet also demonstrated [[Shiro-Hime]], her enormous technological Divine Artifact and personal spacecraft.[24][25]
These demonstrations served two purposes.
They gave Hela apparently valuable alternatives during the bidding process while simultaneously showing the surviving [[God-Kings]] how technologically advanced Dragon's Nest had become.

#### Dragon's Nest's actual strategy
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction did not urgently require Asgardian territory.
Dragon's Nest already possessed enormous territories and had secretly removed many of the races and resources it actually wanted from the Norse realm.[25][26][23]
Their participation instead increased competitive pressure.
By behaving like a serious buyer, Violet forced the other Pantheons to increase their offers while exposing resources they might otherwise have kept hidden.[25][26]
At the same time, every advanced Artifact Dragon's Nest revealed served as an indirect demonstration of the civilization's growing technical superiority.

#### The bidding war
The remaining rulers evaluated the Norse territory according to strategic value.
An additional Pantheon dimension could provide territory, afterlife infrastructure and long-term Divine advantages, but the price demanded by Hela continued increasing.[26][11]
The negotiations consequently became a contest in which every ruler attempted to acquire the dimension without surrendering resources more valuable than the territory itself.

#### Shiva's offer
[[Shiva]] eventually produced several extraordinary resources.
Among them was a mysterious black sphere resembling a miniature black sun surrounded by dark projections.[11]
Shiva stated that he had discovered the object during a journey through an abandoned Pantheon more than a million years earlier.
Its nature immediately attracted the attention of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and the Owner of Limbo because it did not appear to belong naturally to their local environment.[11]
Shiva's broader offer included resources such as:
- [[Indra]]'s Essence.
- Indra's accumulated records concerning Divinity.
- Shiva's Essence of Destruction.
- Shiva's knowledge concerning Destruction.
- The mysterious black sphere.[11]
Hela rejected the initial package.

#### Essences of Dream and Madness
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] possessed information obtained through [[Diablo]] concerning additional resources [[Shiva]] had acquired in the distant past.
Using that knowledge, Hela demanded two exceptionally rare Essences:
- Essence of Dream
- Essence of Madness[11]
Shiva reacted aggressively when he realized that Hela knew about resources he had never publicly revealed.
The confrontation became tense enough that both began releasing their powers.
Victor showed immediate interest in joining a potential fight.
The Owner stopped the escalation by reminding them that combat was prohibited within his territory.[11]

#### Closed negotiations and sale
The final stage of bargaining continued into Chapter 972.
While Hela and [[Shiva]] negotiated, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Violet, Haruna and [[Amaterasu]] remained remarkably relaxed, at one point eating popcorn while observing the discussion.[10]
Shiva had accumulated rare Essences and knowledge across an enormous lifespan and was reluctant to surrender them.
From the perspective of his Pantheon, however, control over an entire Divine dimension represented an immediate strategic asset that could justify sacrificing resources he had never been able to fully exploit.
Hela forced him to make a final decision.
Shiva accepted.[10]
A formal purchase-and-sale agreement was created.
Shiva transferred the agreed payment.
Hela surrendered the key to the [[Norse Pantheon]].
Ownership of the former Asgardian territory passed to Shiva and the [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]].[10]
Final Result — Norse Pantheon Sold
The realm once ruled by [[Odin]] became territory controlled by Shiva's faction, while Hela exchanged the political responsibility of maintaining [[Asgard]] for rare Essences, resources and knowledge with greater personal value to her and Victor's wider plans.[10]

#### Aftermath
Chapter 973 takes place after the Gathering and should therefore be treated as its immediate aftermath rather than part of the official Chapter 967–972 range.[23]
Violet, Haruna, [[Amaterasu]] and Hela later gathered with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] inside his territory and celebrated the outcome.
Their conversation revealed how extensively Dragon's Nest had manipulated the circumstances surrounding the auction.
Nyx Elderblood had concealed the extraction of races and resources during the Asgardian conflict, meaning Victor's faction had already obtained many of the assets it actually wanted before allowing the other Pantheons to compete for the remaining dimension.[23]
The auction then provided additional benefits:
- Hela relinquished responsibility for maintaining [[Asgard]].
- [[Shiva]] acquired the [[Norse Pantheon]] dimension.[10]
- Hela gained exceptionally valuable resources.[11][10]
- Victor gained access to the Essences of Dream and Madness through his arrangement with Hela.[23]

### Political Importance
The Supernatural Gathering represents one of the most important recurring diplomatic institutions associated with Earth.
It differs fundamentally from an internal governing organization such as a Pantheon council or the Council of [[Races (Flamel)|Races]]. Those institutions operate beneath a defined government.
The Gathering does not.
It creates temporary common ground between powers that remain sovereign before, during and after the meeting.
Neutral Venue
No conventional participating faction owns the meeting ground. [[Limbo's Island]] belongs to a mediator outside their normal political hierarchies.
Enforceable Rules
Rules recognized through the Gathering can possess supernatural enforcement rather than relying entirely upon voluntary diplomatic promises.[3]
International Recognition
Major political changes can be witnessed by the rulers of multiple civilizations simultaneously, greatly increasing their legitimacy and international consequences.
The evolution of the three known Gatherings also documents the rapid transformation of Earth's supernatural political structure.
The First Gathering dealt primarily with laws between the established factions and occurred immediately before [[Diablo]] shattered the existing order.
The Second occurred after that catastrophe and dealt with the new world emerging from it: merged afterlife structures, Dragon's Nest, Demon citizenship and planetary reconstruction.
The Third occurred after yet another ancient Pantheon collapsed and showed that the Gathering had become a place where even ownership of a major Divine dimension could be transferred through formal negotiation rather than direct conquest.

### Limitations
The Supernatural Gathering is powerful, but it is not an absolute supernatural government.
It cannot automatically prevent:
- Secret conspiracies.
- Espionage.
- Violations committed outside Limbo's territory.
- Wars between sovereign factions.
- Political betrayal after a meeting.
- Hidden organizations acting without the knowledge of recognized rulers.
- Powers refusing to cooperate after leaving the summit.
The First Gathering provides the clearest example.
The Owner successfully prevented violence inside his territory, yet [[Diablo]]'s armies were already invading Earth elsewhere.[7][16]
Similarly, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s enemies were not personally defeated by the Second Gathering. The summit established the political circumstances and alliances surrounding the subsequent hunt, but the actual military operations began after the meeting ended.[27]
The Gathering therefore functions best as a venue for recognition, negotiation and enforceable diplomatic rules, not as a government possessing direct control over every participating civilization.
