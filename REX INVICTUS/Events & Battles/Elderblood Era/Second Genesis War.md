The Second Genesis War was a global supernatural conflict initiated by Diablo through a coordinated Demonic invasion of the Human World. What began as an attempt to overwhelm Humanity, cripple the world's major supernatural factions and harvest an unprecedented number of Souls expanded into warfare across Earth, Nightingale, Arcane, Divine territories, afterlife systems and Biblical Hell. Diablo announced the conflict only after his armies were already moving across the planet, describing the coming war as one comparable in devastation to the ancient conflict of the Primeval Genesis Age.[1]
Diablo intended to use overwhelming military force, political coercion, hostages, pre-arranged alliances and mass death to reshape the supernatural balance of Earth. Instead, the war progressively dismantled his own power. Victor Elderblood, initially one of several independent rulers opposing Diablo's plans, was eventually banished into Biblical Hell, conquered the realm from within, defeated Diablo's Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse, took the Demonic throne and returned to Earth commanding Legions that had once served his enemy.[3][4][5]
The conflict ended when Victor defeated and devoured Diablo in Chapter 690. The surviving hostile forces either surrendered, fled or were destroyed.[2] The consequences extended far beyond Diablo's death: more than six billion Humans had died, nearly eighty percent of Humanity was gone, the supernatural world could no longer remain concealed from ordinary civilization and the political structure surrounding Victor had developed into a multi-racial power capable of challenging the ancient supernatural order.[6]

### Background

#### Diablo and Biblical Hell
Long before the open invasion of Earth, [[Biblical Hell]] had already undergone a prolonged struggle for power. [[Diablo]] had accumulated influence among its Demon nobles, Keys of Solomon, military Legions and other major powers while positioning himself as the dominant King of the realm.
Lilith, the Mother of Demons and one of the greatest independent powers of Hell, represented one of the greatest obstacles to Diablo's rule. By the time of the Second Genesis War, however, Lilith had already been defeated and deprived of her complete freedom. Evidence discovered during the war showed that Diablo had taken advantage of her circumstances and placed her under supernatural control, turning the former Demon Queen into one of his most dangerous weapons.[7][8][9]
Diablo's military establishment included vast Demonic Legions, numerous Keys of Solomon, Demon Dukes, the Seven Deadly Sins, the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse and other Higher Demons. The invasion was therefore not a large raid from Hell, but the deployment of a civilization-scale supernatural military.

#### The Soul agreement
One of the foundations of [[Diablo]]'s plan involved an agreement connected to [[The Judges of The Abyss]]. After the war began, servants of the Abyss reminded Diablo of a promised payment involving approximately two billion Souls.[10]
The agreement exposed the deeper logic behind the invasion. Human casualties were not merely collateral damage caused by Demonic warfare; the enormous quantity of Souls released through planetary destruction possessed direct supernatural value.
Diablo's strategy consequently combined military conquest with mass Soul harvesting.

#### Preparation against the supernatural world
[[Diablo]] understood that Hell could not simply invade Earth while every Pantheon and supernatural civilization remained capable of responding freely. His plans therefore attacked political relationships and defensive structures at the same time as ordinary territory.
Hostages were taken from powerful families. Agreements were created to restrain potential enemies. Demonic forces were inserted into regions far beyond ordinary Human defenses, while separate attacks against [[Nightingale]], Arcane and other supernatural territories prevented the world's greatest powers from concentrating immediately upon a single front.[11][12][13]
This decentralized strategy initially gave Diablo a decisive advantage. By the time the supernatural rulers realized how large the operation had become, the war had already started.

### Outbreak of the War
The beginning of the Second Genesis War was revealed during the Meeting of Supernatural Beings. The conference had brought together representatives of many of Earth's greatest powers, including Vampires, Gods, [[Werewolves]], Witches, Angels and Youkai.[14][15][16]
Near the conclusion of the meeting, the arrival of an unexpected sixth representative was announced.
That representative was [[Diablo]].
Rather than arriving to negotiate peace, Diablo demonstrated that diplomacy was already irrelevant. His Demons had entered the Human World and were attacking across several continents. Regions of China and Russia had already suffered catastrophic damage, while Demonic forces were advancing through Europe, Africa and South America.[1]
The war had already begun before most of the supernatural powers gathered at the meeting even realized that they were participating in one.
Diablo described the conflict as a war as devastating as the one fought during the Primeval Genesis Age.[1] Heaven immediately understood that the invasion could not be treated as an ordinary Demonic incursion.
The Second Genesis War had begun.

### Invasion of Nightingale
[[Diablo]]'s forces attacked [[Nightingale]] shortly after the beginning of the war. Demonic portals opened throughout the territories controlled by the [[Vampire Counts]] while several of Nightingale's greatest fighters were still reorganizing after the supernatural meeting.[17]
[[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] defended Fulger territory, while [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]], Siena, Lacus and Pepper fought separate invasions in Scarlett lands.[18][19]
The conflict demonstrated how drastically the traditional Vampire families had changed during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s rise. Clan Snow weaponized its supernatural flames, Clan Fulger devastated enemies with Lightning, Clan Scarlett froze battlefields and members of Clan Blank used Shadows for infiltration and assassination.[20][21][22]
The Royal Capital was attacked as well. [[Jeanne Elderblood]] and [[Morgana Elderblood]] fought high-ranking Demons, while Morgana's former experience as one of Lilith's commanders gave Victor's side valuable knowledge regarding Hell's elite.[23][24][25]
The attacks were not random. Diablo's commanders deliberately targeted individuals and institutions capable of interfering with the larger invasion.

#### Discovery of Lilith's condition
Information gathered during the [[Nightingale]] battles finally allowed Morgana and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to understand what had happened to Lilith. Her behavior did not resemble the Demon Queen Morgana had once known, and the evidence increasingly indicated that [[Diablo]] had taken control of her rather than gaining her genuine loyalty.[26][27][9]
Victor consequently refused to classify Lilith as an enemy who simply needed to be killed.
He intended to free her.[28]

#### Formation of Victor's independent coalition
The invasion also accelerated [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s separation from the political system centered exclusively upon [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]].
Victor no longer intended to wait for [[Nightingale]]'s Crown to determine every response when his family and allies were being attacked. Several Count families and independent factions began organizing around Victor directly, creating the first formal structure of what would later become his much larger civilization.[29][30]
Political Turning Point — Victor Becomes an Independent Center of Power
[[Diablo]]'s attempt to fracture Nightingale instead accelerated the formation of a coalition whose loyalty increasingly ran directly toward Victor Elderblood rather than through the existing Vampire Crown.[30]

### War in the Arcane Realm
[[The Arcane Realm]] was breached despite defenses designed to isolate the Witches from ordinary external invasion.
Demonic portals appearing inside Arcane immediately raised serious questions. A conventional army should not have possessed enough information to bypass some of the most advanced defensive [[Magic]] maintained by the Witches.[31][32]
The crisis placed increasing pressure upon Evie Moriarty and the Witch establishment. The Witches had traditionally relied upon political neutrality and the fact that every supernatural faction required their services, but the Second Genesis War demonstrated that neutrality offered little protection against an enemy willing to attack the structure supporting that neutrality itself.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later confronted Evie concerning arrangements connecting Arcane to [[Diablo]]. Rather than attempting to destroy the Witch Queen and create another enemy civilization, he used information and political leverage to limit Diablo's ability to exploit the Witches.[33][34][35]

### The Vatican Front
The war eventually reached the Vatican, where Demons fought against Angels, Inquisitors and other defenders.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] entered the battlefield wearing specialized black armor. His intervention did not represent ideological loyalty to Heaven. Victor regarded Heaven and Hell as competing supernatural powers whose continued balance remained useful, and allowing [[Diablo]] to eliminate the Inquisition would only strengthen the invader.[36]
[[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]] released troops and Battle Maids concealed within her Shadows, rapidly changing the momentum of the conflict.[37]
Victor also discovered that several Demons participating in the invasion had themselves been manipulated or treated as expendable pieces. Rather than approaching every opponent identically, Victor exploited the political divisions within Diablo's coalition.[38][39][40][41]

### The Hostage Strategy
One of [[Diablo]]'s most effective political weapons involved hostages.
Important heirs and relatives of powerful factions had been captured, allowing Diablo to prevent several organizations from intervening freely. Contracts and threats became strategically more valuable than destroying those factions outright.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] directly attacked this system.
He invaded enemy territory, destroyed enormous numbers of Demons and liberated prisoners whose captivity had been forcing their families into neutrality.[42][43]
Once the hostages were safe, much of Diablo's political leverage disappeared.
Strategic Turning Point — The Hostages Are Freed
By removing the hostages that restrained several supernatural families, Victor did more than win a battlefield. He converted Diablo's political weapons into additional potential enemies against him.[42]

### Expansion of Victor's Coalition
The destruction caused by the war left isolated supernatural groups increasingly vulnerable.
Natashia Elderblood Fulger, Sasha and Tatsuya travelled through England searching for [[Viviane]], whose remaining Fairies had survived centuries of exploitation and isolation. The Fulger faction offered them protection and an alternative to remaining exposed while Earth's supernatural order collapsed.[44][45]
The Youkai under [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]] also became increasingly connected to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s political network.
By Chapter 579, [[Diablo]] had recognized Victor as one of the greatest threats to his war. Victor did not behave according to the established borders between supernatural races. He freed hostages, negotiated with Witches, protected displaced populations and constructed alliances among factions with little historical reason to cooperate.[46]
The Second Genesis War therefore became one of the primary events that transformed Victor's household and Vampire allies into a genuine multi-racial political power.

### The Amazon Front
The hidden territory of the Amazons in Brazil became another battlefield during the later stages of the war.
[[Victor Elderblood]], [[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]], Leona, [[Nero]] and their allies entered the region while the surviving Amazons fought forces attempting to overwhelm them.[47]
The attackers included more than ordinary Demons. Behemoths, invisible monsters and other abnormal creatures appeared, indicating that additional powers were taking advantage of the chaos created by the global conflict.[47]
Victor was struck by an extraordinarily dangerous poison, but his regeneration prevented the attack from immediately killing him.
The poisoning was only one part of the actual operation.
A large-scale spatial technique activated and removed Victor from the battlefield entirely.[3]
When he regained awareness, Victor was no longer in Brazil.
He was inside [[Biblical Hell]].
The Greatest Strategic Mistake of the War
The operation designed to remove Victor Elderblood from Earth delivered him directly into [[Diablo]]'s kingdom. Victor did not treat Hell as a prison; he saw an enormous hostile realm filled with Demons, Souls, knowledge, armies and opportunities for growth.[3]

### Victor's Campaign in Biblical Hell

#### Beginning of the conquest
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] immediately began fighting the Demons around him.
Hell's hostile environment did little to restrain him, while every defeated enemy provided blood, memories, supernatural information and additional understanding of the realm.
One of Victor's earliest major confrontations involved [[Vine]], the Forty-Fifth Key of Solomon and ruler of thirty-eight Demonic Legions. Victor annihilated most of her forces so overwhelmingly that Vine ultimately begged for her life.[48]
Victor spared her.
The decision established the policy he would use throughout his conquest:
Submission or death.
Demons willing to surrender and serve were permitted to live. Those who insisted upon continuing resistance after understanding the difference in power were destroyed.[49][50][51][52]
Entire Legions gradually changed allegiance.
Victor had entered Hell alone.
He was beginning to build an army.

### The Four Horsemen
The conquest eventually brought [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] into direct conflict with the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse, some of [[Diablo]]'s greatest weapons.
Famine, Pestilence and Death possessed abilities designed for large-scale destruction, corruption and the permanent weakening of their victims.[53]
Victor defeated them while continuously adapting to their powers and consumed what was useful from their existences.[53][54][55]
The final Horseman was War.
Unlike many of Victor's enemies, War genuinely desired battle. He did not hide behind political authority or attempt to preserve himself once defeat appeared possible. War continued fighting, forced himself beyond his previous limits and earned Victor's respect through his mentality as a warrior.[56][4]
Victor defeated him.
He did not kill him.
War's mount removed its critically wounded master from the battlefield, and the [[Will Solace|Will]] of Hell itself protected War's birthplace from further interference.[4]
War became the only Horseman Victor deliberately spared after their confrontation.

### The Wider War
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s conquest of Hell did not pause the battles outside.
[[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] confronted Yama, bringing two ancient supernatural rulers into direct combat.[57] Vlad eventually revealed the terrifying True Form of the First Vampire Progenitor, demonstrating that Victor's rise had not made [[Nightingale]]'s King irrelevant.[58]
Victor's maids and wives continued fighting across other fronts while attempting to determine how he could be recovered from Hell.[59]
The [[Shinto Pantheon]] also suffered severe damage. Several Gods were injured badly enough that ordinary recovery could require centuries, while disruption to the Pantheon's spiritual systems threatened the management of its dead.[60]
The conflict had expanded beyond cities and armies.
The Second Genesis War was beginning to damage the supernatural systems responsible for Souls, afterlives and Divine territories themselves.

### Conquest of Biblical Hell
After defeating the Horsemen, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] continued toward the deepest regions of Hell.
Reports of the outsider called Alucard the Conqueror spread throughout the Demonic population.[61]
[[Diablo]]'s own invasion strategy had created the circumstances allowing this to happen. Much of Hell's strongest military power was deployed in the Human World. Returning those forces immediately would mean abandoning years of preparation and destabilizing the entire invasion.
Victor was therefore advancing through a kingdom whose King could not return home without sacrificing his war.
Cities, nobles and armies were offered the same choice Victor had established earlier.
Submit.
Or die.
The supposedly immutable barriers separating Hell's deeper territories failed to stop him. Victor eventually reached the central Demon King's castle and approached the throne.[61]
The structures responsible for recognizing legitimate Demonic authority detected something within him.
The throne changed.
Hell accepted Victor.[62]
Diablo sensed the event from Earth.
The kingdom from which his title originated no longer recognized him as its King.

#### The new King of Hell
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] did not consider possession of the throne sufficient.
A King who could only sit upon a throne while the kingdom beneath him remained dysfunctional was not a ruler.
He began reorganizing the Demons who had surrendered, assigning responsibility according to capability and loyalty rather than preserving every hierarchy created under [[Diablo]].[63]
Victor exploited the psychology of Demonic society rather than attempting to force every Demon to behave like a Human. Strength, ambition, fear and reward became tools of government. Competence could produce advancement. Loyalty could produce rewards. Betrayal produced consequences severe enough to make cooperation the rational choice.[64][65]
By Chapter 680, Demonic Legions openly acknowledged Victor as their ruler.[5]
The Second Vampire Progenitor had become the King of Demons.
Diablo Loses Hell
The trap meant to remove Victor from the Second Genesis War produced the exact opposite result. Victor survived, conquered Diablo's armies, gained new subordinates and took the throne upon which Diablo's identity as King depended.[5]

### The Final Phase
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] returned to the greater conflict with the authority of [[Biblical Hell]] behind him.
The Demonic War entered its final phase as Legions that had originally marched beneath [[Diablo]] now followed Victor into the Human World.[66]
The conflict between Heaven and Hell had already escalated dramatically. The Seven Deadly Sins fought Heavenly forces while powerful Angels and Divine combatants became increasingly involved.[66][67]
The death and injury of major Angels caused Heaven to deploy greater combatants. [[Azrael]] entered the battlefield and began annihilating high-ranking Demons.[68]
Victor's arrival transformed the conflict again.
His enormous quantities of Miasma altered the battlefield while Demonic Legions now loyal to him attacked the remaining forces of the former King.[69][70]
The Second Genesis War could no longer be described simply as Heaven against Hell.
Hell itself had changed rulers.

### Zaladrac
The Dragon [[Zaladrac Zeovnur Elderblood]] became involved during the final stage of the conflict.
Despite her enormous physical size and extraordinary power, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] discovered that Zaladrac was comparatively young by the standards of her race.[71]
Their confrontation ended not with Zaladrac becoming an enemy, but with the Dragon recognizing Victor as a worthy partner.
She initially described their relationship using the traditional concept of a Dragon and its knight.
Victor rejected the subordinate implication.
If Zaladrac intended to remain with him, he would be her King.[72]
The resulting supernatural bond connected Victor directly to the physiology and development of a genuine Dragon, establishing one of the foundations of his later Draconic evolution.[72]

### Liberation of Lilith
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] then confronted Lilith.
By this point he already suspected that her allegiance to [[Diablo]] had been artificially enforced. Rather than attacking Lilith herself, Victor targeted the mechanism controlling her.[72]
The restraint was destroyed.
Lilith regained her free will.
Her response was immediate.
Centuries of hatred toward the man who had deprived her of autonomy surfaced at once. Lilith summoned the anti-Divine weapon Genesis and turned against Diablo.[72]
In a single action, Victor had removed one of Diablo's greatest weapons and replaced it with another enemy standing directly beside him.

### Collapse of Diablo's Coalition
The loss of [[Biblical Hell]] destroyed more than [[Diablo]]'s title.
Several agreements depended upon Diablo being the recognized King of the realm. Yama, for example, had established his cooperation with the King of Biblical Hell. Once [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] possessed that position, Yama considered the original agreement invalid and abandoned Diablo rather than continue fighting for a ruler who no longer controlled the kingdom named in their arrangement.[73]
Internal betrayal followed.
Demons who had already accepted Victor's rule acted against the remaining loyalists. Major members of Diablo's hierarchy were removed, while Victor openly rewarded those who proved useful or loyal.[73]
Lilith then confronted Diablo personally.
In her True Form, the Mother of Demons wielded powers connected to Rottenness, Corruption, Despair and Beginning, the authority associated with her position as the origin of the Demonic Race.[74]
Diablo was rapidly running out of allies, armies and territory.

### Diablo's Last Stand
[[Diablo]] refused to accept the collapse of his war.
In a final attempt to exceed the limitations of ordinary Demons, he began forcing incompatible Angelic and Demonic characteristics into the same existence, moving toward the forbidden condition associated with a Nephalem.[74][75]
The transformation produced enormous quantities of Energy and represented a violation serious enough to attract the attention of higher beings associated with maintaining cosmic systems.[75]
Diablo believed the additional power would allow him to overcome [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
The confrontation demonstrated the difference between possessing Energy and mastering power.
Victor had accumulated [[Scathach]]'s martial teachings, countless combat experiences, Bloodline abilities, Soul manipulation, absorbed memories, Divine Blessings, predatory characteristics and centuries of subjective experience within Hell. His enormous reserves were supported by control and technique rather than existing as raw power alone.[76]
When Victor released his own Energy, Diablo experienced genuine fear.
His final transformation was not enough.
Victor defeated him.
In Chapter 690, Victor devoured Diablo, permanently ending the existence of the former Demon King and absorbing the knowledge and abilities accumulated throughout Diablo's enormous lifespan.[2]
The remaining hostile forces surrendered, fled or were destroyed.
The Second Genesis War ended.
End of the Second Genesis War — Chapter 690
Diablo, the ruler who initiated the invasion and sought to reshape Earth's supernatural order, was consumed by Victor Elderblood. His remaining coalition collapsed immediately afterward.[2]

### Casualties
Human Extinction-Level Losses
Over 6 Billion Humans Killed
Chapter 708 states that more than six billion Humans died because of the Demonic invasion, totaling almost eighty percent of Earth's population. The approximately eight billion Humans who existed before the war had been reduced to roughly two billion.[6]
The damage was global.
The East and West were both devastated. Major world powers were nearly erased in extremely short periods, while large portions of Europe, Africa, North America and South America lost cities, infrastructure and populations.[6]
Smaller countries had virtually no means of resisting large Demonic armies.
Even surviving major countries suffered enormous economic and demographic damage.
The ecological damage was similarly severe. Demonic activity, Miasma, the destruction of cities and the collapse of Human infrastructure affected environments far beyond the immediate supernatural battlefields.
The total number of Demons, Angels, Gods, Vampires, Witches, [[Werewolves]] and other supernatural beings killed during the conflict has never been provided as a complete figure.

### Aftermath

#### Humanity after the war
Human civilization emerged from the Second Genesis War catastrophically weakened.
The approximately eight-billion Human population existing before the invasion had been reduced to around two billion.[6] Governments, economies, transportation networks and infrastructure had been shattered across enormous regions of the planet.
For ordinary Humans, the war also permanently changed their understanding of Reality.
Gods, Demons, Angels, Vampires and other supernatural beings could no longer be dismissed as stories when entire countries had been destroyed by their battles.

#### The return of public Faith
The destruction of conventional institutions produced an enormous spiritual vacuum.
Human populations increasingly turned toward beings demonstrably capable of protecting, healing and rebuilding civilization. In a universe where Faith generated genuine supernatural power, that shift had consequences extending beyond ordinary religion.[77][78]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s own followers expanded dramatically during this period. What began as devotion among individuals close to him developed into the [[Blood God Religion]], complete with temples, clergy, administrators and supernatural systems connected directly to Victor.[79][80][81]

#### The transformation of Biblical Hell
[[Biblical Hell]] did not return to its pre-war condition after [[Diablo]]'s death.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had already taken the throne before the final confrontation and had begun reorganizing its political structure. Demons who demonstrated competence and loyalty could gain authority, while betrayal carried severe consequences.[82][83]
Victor did not require Demons to imitate Humans.
Instead, he created institutions capable of functioning through characteristics already fundamental to Demonic society: strength, ambition, reward, fear and loyalty.
This eventually allowed Hell to develop into one of the major foundations of Victor's later civilization.

#### Victor's rise
The Second Genesis War represented one of the greatest transitions in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s life.
At its beginning, he was the Second Vampire Progenitor and one of [[Nightingale]]'s increasingly independent Counts.
By its conclusion he possessed:
- The throne of [[Biblical Hell]].
- Vast Demonic armies.
- Multiple Demon rulers serving beneath him.
- Centuries of subjective combat and ruling experience.
- The memories and knowledge obtained from [[Diablo]].
- Greater understanding of Souls and Demonic Energy.
- A partnership with [[Zaladrac Zeovnur Elderblood]].
- A significantly expanded multi-racial coalition.
- Greater political independence from every pre-existing supernatural kingdom.
Diablo's attempt to destroy Victor therefore produced almost the exact opposite result.

### Historical Significance
The Second Genesis War effectively ended the old supernatural status quo.
Before the conflict, Earth's supernatural powers could still behave as largely separate civilizations. Vampires centered themselves around [[Nightingale]], Witches maintained Arcane, Pantheons protected their individual territories and Heaven and Hell continued an ancient opposition that rarely required every other supernatural race to participate simultaneously.
[[Diablo]]'s invasion demonstrated that such isolation was no longer sustainable.
Portals allowed armies to cross dimensions. Soul agreements turned civilian deaths into strategic resources. Hidden territories could be invaded. Gods could be dragged into conflicts originating outside their Pantheons. A war beginning inside [[Biblical Hell]] could devastate Earth, Nightingale, Arcane and multiple spiritual systems simultaneously.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s response represented the opposite political model.
His coalition increasingly connected Vampires, Demons, Gods, Witches, Fairies, Youkai and other species without requiring every group to erase its own identity.
Many of the relationships, governing principles and military alliances that later developed into Dragon's Nest and eventually the [[Elderblood Empire]] therefore had important foundations in the Second Genesis War.

### Legacy
The Second Genesis War permanently changed nearly every major civilization connected to Earth.
For Humanity, it was an extinction-level catastrophe.
For the supernatural world, it demonstrated that the ancient racial and political divisions established over millennia could collapse within a single global conflict.
For [[Diablo]], the war intended to establish his supremacy instead destroyed his kingdom, army, alliances and life.
For [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], the same conflict became one of the foundations of his future Empire.
The Vampire who entered the war as one rising supernatural monarch emerged from it as the ruler of [[Biblical Hell]], commander of Demonic Legions and the center of a political network extending across races that had historically considered one another enemies.
