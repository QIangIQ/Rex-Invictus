## Real-World Background (IRL)

Brynhildr is a valkyrie of the Völsunga saga and the Nibelungenlied, punished by [[Odin]], freed by Sigurd and later the cause of his death. Wagner's Brünnhilde comes from her.

Brynhildr was a Disir and the former Queen of the Disir empire. She was defeated and killed by Scathach by being thrown into a volcano, which led to the collapse of the Disir's empire on earth.

### Abilities
- [[Immortality]]: Brynhildr is Immortal and does not age. Though an immortal may be poisoned or slain in battle, they may live for thousands or millions of years.

### Trivia
- The [[Disir]] refer to the night of Brynhildr's death as Ragnarok (approximately meaning "Death of the Gods").

### Physical Appearance
Brunhilde's individual physical appearance has not yet been described in detail in the novel. She has only been referenced as one of [[Freyja|Freya]]'s Valkyries, and no chapter has yet provided a dedicated description of her clothing, hair, eyes or weaponry.[1]

### Personality
Very little is currently known about Brunhilde's individual personality because she has not received a focused scene or direct dialogue. What is known is that she operates within the disciplined military structure of [[Freyja|Freya]]'s Valkyries and followed Freya's order to retreat when the conflict became hopeless, indicating obedience and battlefield discipline.[1]

### Synopsis

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Brunhilde was one of the Valkyries serving under [[Freyja|Freya]] during the conflict involving [[Odin]]'s side. As part of Freya's forces, she joined the war alongside the Norse side. When Freya judged the battle to be hopeless and ordered a withdrawal, Brunhilde retreated together with the rest of the Valkyries.[1]

### Skills & Abilities
- Valkyrie Physiology: Brunhilde is one of [[Freyja|Freya]]'s Valkyries, placing her among a corps of supernatural female warriors serving the Norse side.[1]
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: As a Valkyrie who participated in a Divine conflict alongside the [[Norse Pantheon]], Brunhilde possesses physical capabilities far beyond those of ordinary mortals.[1]
- Combat Training: Brunhilde serves under Freya's command as part of a warrior order, indicating formal combat training and battlefield experience.[1]
- Military Discipline: Brunhilde obeyed Freya's retreat order during a hopeless conflict, demonstrating discipline and adherence to command.[1]
