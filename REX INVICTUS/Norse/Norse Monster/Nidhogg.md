Nidhogg was a huge, dinosaur-like creature dating back to the primordial earth. It was one of the most powerful monsters in the Myriad of [[Shadowrealms]] (even defeating [[Scathach]]) until [[Josh Newman|Josh]] slew it using the power of Clarent.

### History
Nidhogg is tens, if not hundreds, of millions of years old. It was used by the [[Disir]] to help the [[Elders]] to defeat the [[Archons]] and [[Earthlords]], though it eventually became too difficult to control. The Elders agreed to imprison Nidhogg within the roots of the [[Yggdrasil]] (which extended into the shadowrealm of [[Niflheim]]), where it remained until [[Dr. John Dee|Dee]] destroyed Yggdrasil with [[Excalibur]] (along with Niflheim, [[Asgard]], and [[Hekate's Shadowrealm]]).

#### The Magician
[[Dr. John Dee|Dee]] and [[Niccolo Machiavelli|Machiavelli]] then recruited the [[Disir]] to kill [[Scathach]] and kidnap the [[Twins of Legend]], [[Josh Newman|Josh]] and [[Sophie Newman]], who were all hiding out in Saint-Germain's house. The Disir decided that they would bring the Nidhogg to deal with them. Once they summoned the dragon, it attacked the house and engaged Josh in combat. When Scathach began to fight as well, but soon it knocked her out. But just before it could devour her, Josh used [[Clarent]], the Sword of Fire, to injure Nidhogg. This frightened the creature and it ran from the house, with Scathach still in its claws, unconscious.
Josh pursued the creature as it tore its way through the city of [[Paris]]. A Dísir also pursued the creature and soon, Dee, Machiavelli, and [[Dagon]] began pursuing it as well. Soon, Nidhogg stopped at the Seine and Josh, who arrived second at the scene, injured the creature once more with Clarent. This caused lava crust to start to form up Nidhogg and began to kill the creature. It then let go of Scathach and began to slowly deteriorate into lava crust. Eventually, it was completely transformed and was destroyed.

### Powers
Due to its huge size and weight, Nidhogg was incredibly strong and durable. It was so durable that no weapon is known to be able to penetrate its hide except for the five [[Swords of Power]] or one of its own claws. Despite its vast size, it could move at incredibly fast speeds. It also had a prehensile tail, and razor sharp claws and fangs. It was incredibly venomous as well and its senses were enhanced to a great degree.
[[Immortality]]: Nidhogg is Immortal and does not age. Though an immortal may be poisoned or slain in battle, they may live for thousands or millions of years.

Níðhöggr, commonly simplified as Nidhoggr or Nidhogg, is an Ancient True Dragon, a Dragon God of END, and one of the most dangerous apocalyptic entities associated with the [[Norse Pantheon]]. Unlike [[Fenrir]] and [[Jormungandr]], Níðhöggr is not one of [[Loki]]'s children, but an older and more independent calamity whose connection to Ragnarök comes from his imprisonment beneath [[Yggdrasil]], his nature as a wielder of END, and the bargain he later formed with Hela.[1][2]
For most of his existence, Níðhöggr remained trapped within a sealed dimension surrounding abandoned portions of Yggdrasil's roots, where he endlessly fed on the World Tree and saturated the entire realm with his presence. Because he is simultaneously a True Dragon and a God of END, his existence produces an abnormal contradiction: his Draconic nature remains deeply compatible with Creation and Nature, while his Divine Concept embodies termination and erasure. This contradiction transformed his prison into a bizarre environment where Positive Energy, Negative Energy, Draconic/Natural Energy and END coexisted in a fragile equilibrium that should normally have collapsed into total destruction.[3][4]
Níðhöggr's power was feared even by high-ranking Demons. Diablo stated that, for a Beast like him, physical presence was not even necessary to destroy something, because his intent alone could be enough.[1] That terror was justified during Ragnarök, when one use of his END-infused Dragon Breath completely erased [[Odin]]'s palace along with [[Frigg]], demonstrating that Níðhöggr was not merely a giant Dragon, but a conceptual weapon capable of deleting strategic targets from existence.[5]

### Physical Description
Níðhöggr is an enormous black Dragon approximately 400 meters tall, placing him among the largest naturally appearing creatures shown in the series. His body is massive even by supernatural standards, and his eyes possess a striking dark-blue coloration that stands out against his otherwise shadow-like appearance.[2]
His form embodies the age, pride and oppressive majesty associated with Elder [[True Dragons]]. He does not resemble a mere oversized beast, but a primordial Draconic calamity whose presence alone feels ancient and heavy. His speech carries that same quality, sounding old, authoritative and instinctively proud in the manner expected from one of Creation's highest Draconic existences.[2]
Unlike many Divine beings who frequently adopt Human avatars, Níðhöggr is consistently associated with his massive Draconic form. During Ragnarök, he remained in this natural body while answering Hela's orders, flying above [[Asgard]] and unleashing his power as a world-ending siege weapon rather than presenting himself through any reduced humanoid manifestation.[6][5]

### Personality
Níðhöggr is proud, aloof, independent and intensely conscious of status, displaying the deeply ingrained hierarchical instinct characteristic of Dragons. He does not casually submit, does not give favors freely, and treats promises and exchanges seriously, which is why Hela had to bargain with him rather than simply command him. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly recognized that this was natural Draconic behavior rather than mere stubbornness.[3][4]
Despite his immense age, Níðhöggr is socially less experienced than his years would imply. He spent almost all of his existence isolated inside his prison dimension instead of traveling the world like most ancient beings, meaning his chronological age far exceeds his actual lived worldly experience.[4] This isolation played a major role in shaping his mentality. More than conquest, politics or Pantheon loyalty, what Níðhöggr wanted most was freedom from his confinement. That desire explains why Hela was able to gain his cooperation at all.[3][4]
He is also profoundly vindictive. His hatred of [[Ratatosk]] was not a minor irritation, but a grudge built over eons of mockery while he remained trapped and unable to retaliate properly. Hela understood this and exploited it by delivering Ratatosk directly to him, thereby earning another favor from the Dragon.[3]
Although he embodies END and is associated with apocalyptic destruction, Níðhöggr is not mindless. He communicates clearly, understands bargains, evaluates those who approach him, remembers debts, and demonstrates enough restraint to honor the favors he owes Hela rather than simply indulging destructive impulse.[2][4] He later developed a surprisingly positive relationship with Victor Elderblood, which Hela considered natural because Victor's status as the Progenitor of the [[Blood Dragons]] effectively placed him above even an Elder Dragon within Draconic instinctive hierarchy.[7]

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Níðhöggr was first mentioned indirectly during the larger conflicts involving the Demons and the [[Norse Pantheon]], when [[Bael]] and [[Diablo]] discussed the destruction of the [[Bifrost]]. Diablo identified Níðhöggr as the force responsible and emphasized how terrifying the Dragon was, stating that a Beast like him did not even need to be present to destroy something because his intent alone was enough.[1] This statement established Níðhöggr's overwhelming reputation long before he physically appeared in the story.
Later revelations clarified that the destruction of the Bifrost was connected to Hela's bargain with him. By the time [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later investigated the matter, it became clear that Hela had already acquired favors from Níðhöggr, and the first of those favors had been used to destroy the Bifrost, thereby isolating [[Asgard]] and Helheim from one another in preparation for the future Ragnarök.[3]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Níðhöggr's first direct appearance occurred during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s covert observation of Hela, [[Fenrir]] and [[Jormungandr]] as they prepared for Ragnarök. By this point, Victor's group already knew they were dealing with an entity of extraordinary danger, and even the process of observing him required extreme caution. War explained that [[True Dragons]] instinctively perceive Nature and disturbances in Creation itself, making conventional concealment methods highly unreliable against an Elder Dragon who had spent his existence devouring the roots of a World Tree.[8][2]
Victor's Heralds only managed to remain hidden by using the contradictory nature of [[Chaos]] to confuse ordinary Draconic perception, allowing them to create a concealed observation zone near Níðhöggr without being detected. When he was finally shown directly, his full appearance was revealed as an enormous 400-meter black Dragon with dark-blue eyes. He addressed Hela regarding their agreement, and once she confirmed that he would be free after Ragnarök to do as he pleased, he accepted the arrangement. Níðhöggr then uttered a single Draconic word, “Return,” and vanished instantly from the area.[2]
Victor later learned the full history behind that agreement. For most of his life, Níðhöggr had been trapped inside a separate dimension surrounding abandoned portions of [[Yggdrasil]]'s roots. That realm functioned as both his prison and his lair, and it was especially difficult to escape because the dimension itself was bound to the same type of END-based power Níðhöggr possessed. Even for a God of END, brute force alone was not enough to easily break free.[3][4] The place was vast, with Victor estimating its scale to be roughly comparable to ancient Russia, though still relatively small compared to the larger dimensions he commonly dealt with.[4]
Inside that dimension, Níðhöggr fed on Yggdrasil's roots for eons. The roots leaked Positive and Negative Energy, which he consumed as sustenance, while his own Draconic Heart continuously produced [[Natural Energy]] and his Divine nature saturated the entire environment with END.[4] Because True Dragons possess extraordinary compatibility with Creation, these normally incompatible Energies did not simply annihilate each other, but formed an unstable yet resilient equilibrium. The result was a dark, abnormal realm with tremendous self-repairing properties, where Positive, Negative, END and Natural Energy coexisted in a way that should not have been possible.[4]
[[Ratatosk]] repeatedly used Yggdrasil's pathways to approach and mock the imprisoned Dragon, knowing Níðhöggr could not properly retaliate. This built an immense hatred over ages. Hela later exploited that grudge by capturing Ratatosk and delivering him directly to Níðhöggr, earning herself another favor from the Dragon. More importantly, she also sacrificed nearly all of her own vitality to open a temporary rift large enough for Níðhöggr to escape his prison. She did not drag him out herself; she merely gave him the opportunity, and Níðhöggr escaped through the breach before the dimension's self-repair closed it again.[3][4]
Níðhöggr's departure destabilized the abandoned root dimension. Victor later inspected the place using his Draconic Eyes and realized that Níðhöggr had been acting as an accidental stabilizing factor. Once he was gone, the remaining Energies no longer had the same living regulator, and Yggdrasil itself effectively abandoned the affected roots. Hela's sacrifice had also added enormous vitality and Time-related influence into the system, causing the entire realm to slip outside the normal operation of the [[Akashic Records]]. It eventually became so abnormal that a new Primordial-like Norse existence began forming inside, forcing Victor to contact the Primordial Administrators to resolve the anomaly.[4]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
When Ragnarök finally began, Níðhöggr did not initially act like a front-line brawler in the same manner as [[Fenrir]] or [[Jormungandr]]. Instead, he remained above the battlefield until Hela decided the correct moment had arrived to use the favor he owed her.[6] [[Odin]] had already prepared for the possibility of his involvement by commissioning exceptionally powerful Dragon-Slaying weapons from the Dwarves, specifically because an Ancient Dragon such as Níðhöggr represented one of the greatest threats the Norse defenders could face.[9]
Once Hela gave the order, Níðhöggr flew toward [[Asgard]] and unleashed his Dragon Breath, a darkness-laden blast infused with END. The target was not a random military position but Odin's palace itself. Rather than exploding or burning, the palace simply vanished from existence, and [[Frigg]] along with the others inside was erased together with it.[5] The act devastated Odin psychologically and created the opening Hela desired. Níðhöggr thus played a decisive strategic role in Ragnarök: his purpose was not merely to kill enemies, but to function as a conceptual siege weapon capable of deleting irreplaceable targets from Reality.
Following Ragnarök, Níðhöggr remained alive. Hela eventually made it clear that she had no desire to eternally govern the [[Norse Pantheon]] and was prepared to sell its control to another power instead of continuing to manage its political burden herself.[7] By this point, she also noted that Fenrir, Jormungandr and even Níðhöggr got along very well with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], which she considered understandable because Victor's existence as the Progenitor of the [[Blood Dragons]] naturally placed him above ordinary Elder Dragons in terms of instinctive Draconic status.[7] This transformed Níðhöggr from a distant Norse calamity into one of the few ancient Dragons who would naturally accept Victor's presence rather than oppose it.

### Skills & Abilities
Níðhöggr is an Ancient Elder True Dragon who also possesses the Divine Concept of END, making him exceptionally dangerous even among major Gods and apocalyptic beings. His powers come from both his natural Dragon physiology and his divine authority over terminal erasure.
- Dragon God Physiology: As a genuine Dragon God, Níðhöggr possesses a Divine body, Soul and Energy far beyond ordinary beings, combined with the overwhelming physical and metaphysical traits of a True Dragon.
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: He possesses immense strength, speed, durability, stamina, reflexes and vitality appropriate to an Ancient Elder Dragon.[2]
- [[Divine Energy]] Manipulation: He can generate and wield Divine Power according to his Concept.
- Regeneration: As both a God and a True Dragon, he possesses powerful natural recovery.
- [[Immortality]] / Agelessness: He has existed for eons and does not age by ordinary standards.
- Divinity — END: Níðhöggr's primary Divine Concept is END, a terminal power distinct from ordinary Destruction. END does not merely break or annihilate; it erases and terminates what it affects.[2][4]
- END Energy Manipulation: He naturally generates and controls END Energy.[4]
- Existence Erasure: His END can erase matter, structures and living beings from existence rather than simply damaging them.[4][5]
- Structural Erasure: He erased [[Odin]]'s entire palace with a single Dragon Breath.[5]
- Living-Being Erasure: [[Frigg]] and others inside the palace were erased together with the structure.[5]
- Energy Erasure: END naturally attempts to terminate other Energies, which is why the prison dimension required abnormal stabilizing conditions to prevent total collapse.[4]
- Conceptual Destruction / Erasure: His END nature allows him to affect supernatural structures whose existence goes beyond ordinary matter, such as the [[Bifrost]].[1][3]
- Existence-Erasing Dragon Breath: Níðhöggr can combine True Dragon Breath with END, producing an attack that manifests as pure darkness and deletes what it strikes from existence.[5]
- Destruction Through Intent: According to [[Diablo]], Níðhöggr's intent alone can be enough to destroy something, though the exact mechanics and scope of this application remain unexplained and should not be treated as unrestricted omnidirectional erasure.[1]
- END Resistance: As a natural God of END, he can permanently exist while continuously generating and being surrounded by the same Energy without being erased by it.[4]
- END Environmental Saturation: His prolonged presence passively saturates his surroundings with END Energy.[4]
- Ancient Elder True Dragon Physiology: Níðhöggr is a genuine Ancient True Dragon rather than a God merely resembling one, and therefore possesses the natural traits of one of Creation's highest Draconic races.[2]
- Gigantic Size: His standard Draconic form stands around 400 meters tall.[2]
- Flight: He can freely fly despite his tremendous size.[6][5]
- Draconic Durability: His scales and body provide extraordinary natural defense.
- Aerial Combat: He can function effectively in large-scale airborne combat.[5]
- Ancient Predatory Instincts: Even though he spent most of his life imprisoned, he retains the instinctive predatory and combative qualities inherent to Elder Dragons.
- [[Dragon Heart]]: Like other [[True Dragons]], Níðhöggr possesses a genuine Dragon Heart.[4]
- [[Natural Energy]] Generation: His Heart continuously produces Natural Energy despite his Divine Concept being END.[4]
- [[Draconic Energy]] Generation: His existence continuously releases Draconic Energy into the environment.[4]
- Continuous Energy Production: His body naturally emits supernatural Energy over time, which was one of the key factors shaping his prison dimension.[4]
- Creation Compatibility: True Dragons possess extraordinary compatibility with Creation and Nature.[2]
- Environmental Harmonization: Níðhöggr's passive presence helped maintain a fragile balance between Positive Energy, Negative Energy, END and Draconic/Natural Energy inside his prison dimension.[4]
- Contradictory Energy Coexistence: His existence enabled normally incompatible Energies to persist together instead of immediately annihilating one another.[4]
This should be understood primarily as a passive consequence of his Dragon nature rather than a consciously controlled technique.
- World Tree Interaction: Níðhöggr spent eons feeding upon the roots of [[Yggdrasil]].[2][4]
- World Tree Root Consumption: He can consume portions of the World Tree as nourishment.
- Positive Energy Consumption: He fed on Positive Energy leaking from the roots.[4]
- Negative Energy Consumption: He likewise incorporated Negative Energy leaking from the same root system.[4]
- World Tree Damage: The roots within his prison were covered in immense bite marks and extensive damage from his feeding.[4]
- Supernatural Perception: As a True Dragon, Níðhöggr instinctively senses Nature and disturbances in Creation.[2]
- Instinctive Nature Perception: He can detect abnormal fluctuations in his surroundings.
- Environmental Awareness: Ordinary disturbances entering his vicinity are difficult to conceal from him.
- Enhanced Elder Dragon Senses: His perception is especially acute because he is both an Elder Dragon and a Dragon that spent his existence devouring Yggdrasil's roots.[2]
- Concealment Detection: Conventional stealth is highly unreliable against him without special measures, which is why [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s group had to rely on [[Chaos]]-based interference rather than ordinary hiding methods.[2]
- Draconic Language: Níðhöggr naturally understands and wields the language of True Dragons.[2]
- Draconic Word — “Return”: He demonstrated a supernatural Draconic command translated as Return, after which his entire body vanished from the area.[2]
- Spatial Transportation: The demonstrated effect allowed him to instantly leave his current location through Draconic authority.[2]
- Dimensional Abilities: Níðhöggr can survive and act within abnormal self-contained dimensions saturated by hostile Energies.[4]
- Dimensional Survival: He remained alive and functional for eons inside a sealed END-infused prison realm.
- Dimensional Travel: After obtaining freedom, he could move between separated supernatural locations, as shown through the use of Return.[2]
- Aura and Presence: Níðhöggr naturally emits the overwhelming presence of an Elder True Dragon and God of END.[2]
- Overwhelming Presence: His existence exerts natural pressure on those around him.
- Draconic Dominance: He carries the instinctive authority and pride associated with Dragons.
- END Presence: His mere presence continuously releases traces of END into the environment.[4]

### Resistances
- END Resistance: He can permanently exist while generating and being surrounded by END Energy.[4]
- Extreme Environmental Resistance: He survived for eons inside a dimension saturated with Positive, Negative, END and Draconic/Natural Energies.[4]
- Energy Incompatibility Resistance: His Dragon physiology allowed him to remain at the center of several mutually hostile Energies without being destroyed.[4]
- Dimensional Isolation Resistance: He remained alive and functional despite spending the overwhelming majority of his life confined within an isolated sealed dimension.[3][4]

### Weaknesses
- [[Chaos]]-Based Perception Interference: True Dragon perception depends heavily upon their compatibility with Creation and Nature, so sufficiently contradictory Chaos-based concealment can interfere with that instinctive awareness. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s group used this principle to observe him without detection.[2]
- Dragon-Slaying Weapons: Specialized Dragon-Slaying weapons can threaten [[True Dragons]], which is why [[Odin]] commissioned exceptionally powerful examples in preparation for Níðhöggr's possible role in Ragnarök.[9] This should not be interpreted to mean that ordinary Dragon-Slaying tools are automatically capable of killing him.
- Limited Worldly Experience: Although ancient, Níðhöggr spent most of his existence imprisoned and therefore has less practical experience with the outside world than many beings far younger than himself.[4]

### Former Prison / Lair
Níðhöggr's former prison existed in a separate dimension surrounding abandoned portions of [[Yggdrasil]]'s roots.[3][4] It was enormous, with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] comparing its scale to ancient Russia, and consisted primarily of darkness, damaged World Tree roots and a bizarre Energy ecosystem that should not normally have been able to exist.[4] The place possessed powerful self-repairing properties, was difficult to access without appropriate compatibility with END, and contained Positive Energy, Negative Energy, END and Draconic/Natural Energy simultaneously.[4]
The dimension's abnormal state was not solely one of Níðhöggr's personal techniques. It developed through the interaction between his own END and Draconic nature, the Energy leaking from Yggdrasil's roots, and the later vitality and Time-based sacrifice Hela expended to free him.[4] After Níðhöggr escaped, the environment destabilized and began slipping away from the normal operation of the [[Akashic Records]], eventually becoming abnormal enough for a new Primordial-like Norse existence to begin forming within it.[4]

### Relationships

#### Hela Elderblood
Hela was the one who successfully bargained with Níðhöggr and secured his participation in Ragnarök. She earned his cooperation by doing what almost nobody else could: opening a path for him to escape his prison and later satisfying his grudge against [[Ratatosk]] by delivering the squirrel directly to him.[3][4] Their relationship was not one of master and subordinate in the ordinary sense, but one based on owed favors, mutual usefulness and a shared opposition to [[Odin]]'s order.

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] initially approached Níðhöggr as a dangerous unknown while investigating the preparations for Ragnarök, but their later dynamic became unexpectedly positive. Hela explicitly noted that Níðhöggr got along very well with Victor, which she considered natural because Victor's existence as the Progenitor of the [[Blood Dragons]] instinctively placed him above even an Elder Dragon in Draconic hierarchy.[7] This made Victor one of the few beings whom Níðhöggr would accept not merely as an ally of circumstance, but as someone naturally worthy of regard.

#### Ratatosk
[[Ratatosk]] was one of the beings Níðhöggr hated most. While the Dragon was trapped beneath [[Yggdrasil]], Ratatosk repeatedly approached him through the World Tree's pathways and mocked him, knowing Níðhöggr's confinement prevented proper retaliation. This centuries- or millennia-long harassment built into a tremendous grudge, and Hela deliberately weaponized that hatred by delivering Ratatosk to him in order to secure another favor.[3]

#### Fenrir and Jormungandr
Although not related by blood, Níðhöggr later became associated with [[Fenrir]] and [[Jormungandr]] through Hela's Ragnarök faction. All three were apocalyptic END-related beings deployed against the old Norse order, though Níðhöggr's role differed from theirs. Fenrir and Jormungandr were used more as direct battlefield forces, while Níðhöggr was reserved as a strategic erasure weapon capable of deleting critical targets such as [[Odin]]'s palace.[6][5]
