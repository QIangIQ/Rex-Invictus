Jörmungandr, also known as the World Serpent and Midgard Serpent, is an ancient Third Generation God, Jötunn-born Divine Serpent, God of Destruction and one of the natural Beings associated with <END>. He is the child of [[Loki]] and [[Angrboða]], sibling of [[Fenrir]] and Hela, and one of the principal apocalyptic beings connected to the Ragnarök prophecy. Within that prophecy, Jörmungandr was traditionally destined to confront [[Thor]], while Fenrir was expected to oppose [[Odin]].[2][3]
Jörmungandr's importance extends considerably beyond his mythological role as Thor's enemy. He is a natural END Being whose physical existence directly channels the terminal Concept rather than merely wielding it as an external Divinity. His venom carries END deeply enough to erase a victim from existence and prevent the ordinary reincarnation available even to Gods, making a successful exposure potentially more dangerous than conventional Divine destruction.[4][5]
During Ragnarök, Jörmungandr personally occupied Thor while Hela dismantled Odin's rule, spreading immense quantities of poison through the battlefield and preventing one of [[Asgard]]'s greatest warriors from freely intervening elsewhere.[6] Following the collapse of Asgard, he remained closely associated with Hela and eventually became one of the authentic END sources used by the [[Elderblood Empire]] in advanced strategic systems such as the Galaxy [[Destroyer]]-class warship Polaris.[7][8]

### Physical Appearance
Jörmungandr's natural form is that of an absolutely enormous serpent. His body is sufficiently gigantic that [[Fenrir]] considered its scale tactically disadvantageous against a much smaller humanoid opponent such as [[Thor]]. Before Ragnarök, Fenrir specifically advised Jörmungandr to abandon his enormous natural size and use a compact humanoid form when fighting Thor, since presenting an unnecessarily gigantic target would make combat more difficult.[9]
Despite being known as the World Serpent, Jörmungandr is not permanently restricted to a single body size or shape. He possesses enough control over his physiology to assume a humanoid form suitable for fighting smaller opponents.[9] The novel has not yet provided a detailed canonical description of this humanoid appearance, so specific hair color, eye color, clothing or facial characteristics should not be treated as confirmed.
His control over scale is even more extreme than ordinary transformation. After Ragnarök, Jörmungandr compressed his serpent form into a tiny snake small enough to coil comfortably around Hela's neck during negotiations over the remnants of the [[Norse Pantheon]].[4] This reduction in physical size did not diminish the supernatural danger of his existence. Powerful Gods observing him could still instinctively perceive their own End when Jörmungandr looked at them, demonstrating that his conceptual nature remains present regardless of whether he is a world-sized serpent or a harmless-looking snake.[4]
No verified chapter establishes a fixed canonical scale color, eye color, horn arrangement or ornamental pattern for Jörmungandr's serpent form. Artwork depicting such details should therefore be regarded as interpretation rather than official physical description.

### Personality
Jörmungandr's personality sharply contrasts with his apocalyptic reputation. Despite possessing one of the most dangerous powers among the Norse Gods, he is unusually carefree, relaxed and fond of avoiding unnecessary effort. Compared with the much more aggressive [[Fenrir]] and the calculating, revenge-driven Hela, Jörmungandr frequently behaves like the most laid-back member of [[Loki]]'s persecuted children.[9][10]
During preparations for Ragnarök, his siblings discussed combat strategy, Loki and the approaching destruction of [[Asgard]] while Jörmungandr increasingly lost interest in the conversation. He eventually instructed Fenrir to look after Hela because he intended to sleep, responding to further discussion with little more than annoyed noises before resting.[9] Chapter 972 later explicitly characterizes him as the most carefree of the siblings.[10]
His laziness should not be confused with cowardice. Jörmungandr willingly participated in Ragnarök, directly confronted [[Thor]] and possesses no hesitation about using lethal END against dangerous opponents. His indifference primarily concerns unnecessary arguments and effort rather than battle itself.
Jörmungandr also possesses an exceptionally strong sense of loyalty toward his siblings. Hela in particular is capable of immediately overriding his ordinarily relaxed temperament. After Ragnarök, when tensions emerged between Hela and [[Shiva]], Jörmungandr remained wrapped around his sister's neck in his tiny serpent form and prepared to attack Shiva if the God of Destruction harmed her.[10] The narration explains that Hela mattered deeply to Jörmungandr because she had consistently remained connected to her brothers despite their dysfunctional family and the enormous periods of separation forced upon them.[10]
His relationship with Fenrir is similarly informal and familiar. The brothers insult, correct and advise one another freely without the exchanges becoming genuine hostility. Fenrir was comfortable warning Jörmungandr about Loki's tricks and even providing tactical advice for his battle against Thor, while Jörmungandr responded with irritation rather than offense.[9]
Jörmungandr's attitude toward his father is radically different. He harbors open hatred toward Loki and stated that he intended to kill him if they encountered one another during Ragnarök. Hela had no objection and merely requested that Jörmungandr ensure Loki's destruction was complete, something he accepted with amusement.[9] The resentment came from Loki's failure to protect his children from the persecution resulting from [[Odin]]'s fear of the Ragnarök prophecy.
Jörmungandr likewise possesses no affection for Odin or the Asgardian order that imprisoned and persecuted his family. His participation in Ragnarök was therefore not mindless obedience to prophecy. He consciously supported Hela's war against the system responsible for the suffering of Loki's children.[6]

### History
Jörmungandr was born from [[Loki]] and the Jötunn [[Angrboða]] alongside his siblings [[Fenrir]] and Hela. The unusual circumstances surrounding Angrboða's children resulted in multiple offspring naturally associated with <END>. Later explanations establish that Fenrir and Jörmungandr did not merely acquire END later in life; their bodies naturally conduct the Concept as part of their fundamental physiology.[11][5]
Their existence became central to the prophecy of Ragnarök. Jörmungandr was identified with the prophesied confrontation against [[Thor]], while Fenrir was connected to [[Odin]] and Hela became one of the central figures associated with the destruction of the Norse order.[2][3]
Rather than attempting to change the circumstances that might produce Ragnarök, Odin's regime treated the children themselves as threats. Hela was confined to Helheim, Fenrir was restrained and isolated, and Jörmungandr was likewise separated from ordinary Norse society. The siblings were punished for a future that had not yet occurred, creating the resentment that would later motivate their voluntary participation in the very Ragnarök the Gods had tried to prevent.[9][11]
The siblings' connection survived despite their separation. When Hela eventually began the rebellion she had prepared for millennia, Jörmungandr and Fenrir answered her call rather than defending Odin's Pantheon.[12]

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Jörmungandr was first referenced indirectly in the wider discussion surrounding the instability of the [[Norse Pantheon]] and the forces connected to Ragnarök. By this period, the destruction of the [[Bifrost]] and the actions of Hela and Nidhoggr were already destabilizing the Norse system, foreshadowing the later conflict in which [[Loki]]'s children would openly oppose [[Odin]].[2]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
The approaching Ragnarök became impossible to ignore when Hela began another civil war against [[Odin]]. Unlike an ordinary rebellion, her campaign had been prepared across immense periods and incorporated powerful entities that Odin's faction had spent generations fearing, including [[Fenrir]] and Jörmungandr.[12]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction investigated the conflict rather than accepting the Ragnarök prophecy as immutable fact. Their surveillance eventually revealed the extraordinary concentration of END-aligned powers surrounding Hela: Fenrir, Jörmungandr and the Ancient END Dragon Nidhoggr.[13][9]
During these preparations, Jörmungandr openly stated that he intended to personally kill [[Loki]] if he encountered him. Fenrir also hated their father but ultimately surrendered the claim to Jörmungandr, warning only that Loki's deceptive nature made him dangerous even when apparently cornered.[9]
Fenrir then gave his younger sibling unusual tactical advice. Rather than entering the destined fight with [[Thor]] using his colossal natural form, Jörmungandr should transform into something significantly smaller and more maneuverable. The suggestion surprised both Jörmungandr and Hela because Fenrir himself had historically preferred fighting through overwhelming natural size and instinct. The strategy reflected lessons Fenrir had learned after encountering Victor and recognizing that a smaller combat profile could drastically change how opponents accustomed to an enormous monster fought.[9]
Jörmungandr showed little anxiety over the coming confrontation. After the discussion, he effectively entrusted Hela to Fenrir and went to sleep.[9]
When Ragnarök finally entered its decisive stage, Jörmungandr confronted Thor. The World Serpent continuously released enormous quantities of poison across the battlefield, devastating everything caught in its path. Thor wished to assist Odin and the other Asgardian defenders but could not simply abandon Jörmungandr, forcing one of Odin's strongest remaining combatants to remain occupied while Hela's wider strategy destroyed the old Norse order.[6]
This encounter fulfilled the central opposition between Thor and Jörmungandr contained within the old prophecy, but the outcome did not follow the traditional version exactly. Thor survived Ragnarök rather than dying alongside the World Serpent. Hela's forces ultimately defeated him, and the Thunder God remained under her control after Odin's regime collapsed.[6][14]
Jörmungandr himself survived Ragnarök and remained beside Hela rather than attempting to seize [[Asgard]] or establish a political position of his own. His behavior fit his personality: the destruction of Odin's order and protection of his siblings interested him far more than governing the civilization left behind.
Roughly two months after Ragnarök, Hela invited representatives of major supernatural factions to negotiate the sale of the former [[Norse Pantheon]]. During those negotiations she summoned Jörmungandr, who appeared not as the colossal World Serpent but as an extremely small snake coiled around her neck.[4]
The visual harmlessness of this form meant very little. Nearby Gods immediately recognized the danger surrounding him, and his gaze caused veteran Divine beings to instinctively perceive their own destruction. The gathering provided one of the clearest explanations of Jörmungandr's actual threat: his poison was not simply a biological toxin but a medium for <END>. A target sufficiently affected could be completely erased from existence, losing even the possibility of Divine reincarnation.[4]
When [[Shiva]] later confronted Hela during the negotiations, Jörmungandr immediately prepared to attack. Although ordinarily the most carefree of Loki's children, he was prepared to use his END venom against one of Earth's most powerful Gods if Shiva harmed his sister.[10] His reaction confirmed that his loyalty toward Hela was more important than the political consequences of attacking another major God.

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Later developments clarified the metaphysical nature of Jörmungandr's existence. Hela explained that natural END Beings such as Jörmungandr, [[Fenrir]] and [[Typhon]] differ fundamentally from someone like herself. Hela historically channeled END through the [[Staff of Death and End]], allowing her to treat it as an external Energy. Jörmungandr instead conducts END through his own body.[5]
His flesh and specialized biological structures are therefore not merely protected by [[Divine Energy]]; portions of his physiology are naturally configured to carry the terminal Concept. This distinction explains why the venom produced through his body can possess existentially destructive properties rather than functioning as an ordinary supernatural poison.[5]
The same revelation corrected an important misconception concerning Jörmungandr's sex. Natural END Beings of this type are fundamentally genderless. Their existence embodies termination and cannot naturally perform the metaphysical act of beginning new Life through reproduction. The series nevertheless overwhelmingly refers to Jörmungandr with masculine pronouns, so he continues to be addressed as male in ordinary dialogue despite being biologically/metaphysically genderless.[5]
Through Hela's eventual marriage to [[Victor Elderblood]], Jörmungandr also became part of Victor's extended family as his brother-in-law.[1] His direct personal interactions with Victor remain comparatively limited, but his relationship with Hela places him firmly within the Emperor's wider family network.

#### Those Who Rule Reality
By the later Imperial era, END ceased being treated solely as the terrifying supernatural phenomenon surrounding Ragnarök. The [[Elderblood Empire]] developed methods of incorporating authentic END into strategic technology.
One of the greatest examples was Polaris, a Galaxy [[Destroyer]]-class Divine warship developed as one of the Empire's highest-level weapons. Its systems integrate Reality, Space, Time and destructive Concepts, while its ultimate armament channels genuine <END>.[7][8]
Jörmungandr, [[Fenrir]] and [[Typhon]] became the primary natural END sources connected to this system. Their Power could be routed into specialized Imperial cannons designed to unleash terminal attacks against enormous targets. The force was so destructive that each END cannon could survive only a single discharge before the weapon module itself disappeared, forcing Polaris to carry hundreds of replaceable cannons.[7][8]
This role represents a major transformation in Jörmungandr's place within the setting. A Being once known almost exclusively as a prophesied apocalypse became a cooperative source of one of the Elderblood Empire's most dangerous strategic powers.

### Skills & Abilities
Jörmungandr is one of the most dangerous natural END Beings originating from Earth's Divine civilizations. Although classified as a Third Generation God, his actual threat cannot be evaluated purely through Divine generation because a successful END attack can kill or erase beings whose conventional power substantially exceeds his own.[4][5]

#### God Physiology
- Divine Physiology: Jörmungandr is a genuine Third Generation God and possesses the fundamental supernatural characteristics of Divine beings.
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: His Divine and Jötunn nature grants immense strength, speed, durability, reflexes, stamina and physical power. His ability to occupy [[Thor]] during Ragnarök demonstrates that his conventional physical capabilities are far beyond those of ordinary Gods.[6]
- [[Divine Energy]] Manipulation: Jörmungandr possesses Divine-grade Energy and can channel supernatural power through his body and Divinities.
- Divine Soul: His Soul exists at the Divine level rather than as an ordinary mortal spiritual structure.
- Agelessness: Jörmungandr has existed for eons and is unaffected by ordinary biological aging.
- Enhanced Senses: His Divine Beast physiology grants sensory abilities vastly superior to ordinary life-forms.
- Regeneration: As a major Divine being, Jörmungandr possesses supernatural recovery beyond mortal biology, although no chapter has yet established the precise upper limit of his regeneration.

#### END Being Physiology
Natural END Being: Jörmungandr is not simply a God who learned an END technique. END is integrated into his physical existence itself.[5]
- END Manipulation: Jörmungandr naturally channels the terminal Concept through his own body.[5]
- Body-Based END Channeling: Unlike Hela's historically external manipulation through a staff, Jörmungandr conducts END directly through biological structures.[5]
- Conceptual Physiology: Portions of his body function as physical conduits for a fundamental terminal Concept rather than ordinary flesh alone.
- Existence Erasure: END can terminate a target at a deeper level than ordinary physical destruction. Jörmungandr's venom is capable of completely erasing affected beings.[4]
- Spiritual Erasure: Complete exposure to END does not merely kill the body; the victim's continued spiritual existence can also be terminated.[4]
- Reincarnation Negation: Gods who would ordinarily retain the possibility of eventual reincarnation can lose that possibility entirely if completely erased by Jörmungandr's venom.[4]
- Record Termination: Later explanations distinguish END from conventional Destruction: Destruction may eliminate a body or structure while leaving deeper Records available to the greater cycle of Creation, whereas END is capable of terminating the Record of an existence itself.[15]
- Durability Negation: Because END functions through existential termination rather than ordinary kinetic damage, conventional physical durability alone is not sufficient protection against a successful application.
- END Presence: Jörmungandr's nature can be instinctively perceived by other powerful beings. Even while physically reduced to a tiny snake, nearby Gods sensed their own End through his gaze.[4]
- END Affinity: As a natural embodiment/conduit of END, Jörmungandr possesses compatibility with the Concept that would be lethal to ordinary beings.
- Genderless Physiology: Jörmungandr is canonically genderless despite masculine pronouns. Natural END Beings cannot ordinarily begin new Life through reproduction because doing so contradicts the terminal nature represented by their existence.[5]

#### Venom
END Venom: Jörmungandr's most prominently demonstrated personal weapon is his supernatural poison. The venom is dangerous not merely because of toxicity but because it carries the properties of END.[6][4]
- Poison Generation: Jörmungandr can naturally produce enormous quantities of supernatural venom.
- Poison Manipulation: He can deliberately weaponize his venom in combat.
- Large-Scale Poison Projection: During Ragnarök he continuously expelled poison across wide portions of the battlefield rather than relying exclusively upon a bite.[6]
- Area Contamination: His venom can spread across a battlefield and threaten numerous targets simultaneously.[6]
- Existence-Erasing Poison: Sufficient exposure can erase the victim's existence through END.[4]
- Reincarnation Negation: Victims completely erased by the venom cannot return through the normal reincarnation available to Divine beings.[4]
- Battlefield Denial: Jörmungandr's poison makes large areas dangerous to traverse and can force powerful opponents to continuously account for where the venom has spread.[6]
The novel has not established whether every microscopic quantity of Jörmungandr's venom automatically causes complete erasure. The Chapter 971 explanation specifically concerns complete exposure to its END properties, so weaker contact should not automatically be treated as instantaneous absolute deletion.

#### Serpent and Jötunn Physiology
- Colossal Serpent Physiology: Jörmungandr's natural body is an enormous supernatural serpent befitting the title World Serpent.
- Colossal Size: His ordinary serpent form is so gigantic that [[Fenrir]] considered the size itself a disadvantage against [[Thor]].[9]
- Immense Physical Strength: His natural body possesses enormous physical power, allowing him to contend with high-level Divine combatants.
- Constriction: His serpentine anatomy naturally permits constriction, although no major canonical fight has focused on this method.
- Supernatural Bite: As a natural END Being and giant serpent, his jaws provide a direct method of delivering his venom and bodily END.
- Jötunn Heritage: Jörmungandr descends from [[Loki]] and [[Angrboða]] and possesses the extraordinary supernatural traits associated with his Jötunn lineage.

#### Transformation
- Shapeshifting: Jörmungandr can assume a humanoid form rather than remaining permanently serpentine.[9]
- Size Manipulation: He can drastically increase or decrease his physical scale.
- Extreme Compression: Jörmungandr can reduce his immense natural body into a tiny serpent capable of wrapping around Hela's neck.[4]
- Humanoid Transformation: [[Fenrir]] directly recommends Jörmungandr's smaller humanoid form for combat against [[Thor]].[9]
- Power Retention: Extreme reduction in physical size does not remove Jörmungandr's END nature. Gods still sensed existential danger from him while he appeared as a tiny snake.[4]

#### Destruction
God of Destruction: Jörmungandr belongs to the Divine beings associated with Destruction. His natural END physiology is nevertheless more dangerous than ordinary Destruction because the two Concepts are not identical.
- Destruction Manipulation: Jörmungandr possesses Divine destructive authority appropriate to his classification as a God of Destruction.
- Conceptual Destruction: His Divine power is capable of operating through supernatural Concepts rather than relying solely upon physical force.
- END Superiority over Ordinary Destruction: Later cosmological explanations explicitly distinguish Destruction from END. Destruction may break structures while leaving their deeper informational place within Creation intact, whereas END can terminate that deeper continuity.[15]

#### Combat Ability
Jörmungandr is powerful enough to occupy [[Thor]] throughout a decisive portion of Ragnarök.[6] This does not establish that he is categorically stronger than Thor in every statistic, but it proves that Thor could not simply ignore, bypass or rapidly eliminate him.
- High-Level Divine Combat: Jörmungandr can engage one of [[Asgard]]'s greatest warriors directly.
- Battlefield Control: He uses immense amounts of poison to restrict enemy movement and create lethal zones.[6]
- Form Adaptation: His ability to alter body size allows him to change between overwhelming scale and a more tactically efficient compact form.
- Lethality Above Conventional Rank: Like other END Beings, his ability to erase targets means his killing potential can threaten beings whose conventional Divine tier exceeds his own.

#### Imperial END Contribution
By the later Imperial era, Jörmungandr's natural END became strategically valuable to the [[Elderblood Empire]].[7][8]
- END Energy Supply: Jörmungandr can provide authentic END for Imperial research and weapons.
- Strategic Weapon Integration: His END is integrated alongside [[Fenrir]]'s and [[Typhon]]'s into the system supporting Polaris.[7]
- END Cannon Channeling: The three natural END Beings act as sources for Polaris' disposable END cannons.[8]
- Reality Erasure Support: [[True Death|The END]] channeled through Polaris' ultimate weapons is capable of erasing Reality rather than functioning as ordinary artillery.[8]
These functions represent cooperation with Imperial technology rather than personal possession of Polaris' Reality, Space or Time manipulation systems.

### Power and Threat Level
Jörmungandr's threat should not be judged only through his classification as a Third Generation God. Natural END Beings occupy an unusual position within the Divine hierarchy because their conventional Energy level and their actual ability to kill a target are not necessarily equivalent.
A sufficiently stronger God may possess more Energy, better physical statistics or greater combat skill, yet still be unable to safely receive a direct END-infused attack. Jörmungandr's venom in particular allows him to threaten beings whose conventional Divine durability would otherwise make ordinary poison irrelevant.[4]
His ability to prevent [[Thor]] from disengaging during Ragnarök places him among the highest combat threats produced by the old [[Norse Pantheon]].[6] He should nevertheless not be reclassified as a Second Generation Primordial God or Primordial Being without direct evidence. He remains a Third Generation natural END God whose unusual Concept grants disproportionate offensive lethality.

### Relationships

#### Hela Elderblood
Hela is Jörmungandr's sister and the family member toward whom he has demonstrated the strongest explicit attachment. The persecution of [[Loki]]'s children left the siblings with few trustworthy familial relationships, and Hela became one of the only people Jörmungandr consistently prioritized above his own comfort.[9][10]
When Hela finally launched Ragnarök, Jörmungandr joined her willingly. He did not attempt to take command from her or question the revenge she had spent millennia preparing.[12]
Their bond remained intact after [[Odin]]'s defeat. Jörmungandr stayed near Hela during the negotiations for [[Asgard]] and appeared casually wrapped around her neck in a tiny serpent form.[4] When [[Shiva]]'s interaction with Hela appeared threatening, Jörmungandr immediately prepared to use his venom despite the extraordinary political consequences of attacking the Hindu God of Destruction.[10]
His loyalty continued into Hela's later association with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and the [[Elderblood Empire]], where Jörmungandr became one of the END sources connected to her strategic vessel Polaris.[7][8]

#### Fenrir
[[Fenrir]] is Jörmungandr's brother and another natural END Being. The two share a casual sibling relationship in which mockery, insults and practical advice occur without genuine hostility.
Before Ragnarök, Fenrir warned Jörmungandr not to underestimate [[Loki]] and later advised him to use a compact humanoid form against [[Thor]] rather than relying entirely upon his enormous natural serpent body.[9] The recommendation reflected techniques Fenrir himself had learned after receiving combat instruction from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
Both brothers share deep loyalty toward Hela and answered her call against [[Odin]].

#### Loki
[[Loki]] is Jörmungandr's biological father but receives little filial affection from him. Jörmungandr openly stated that he intended to kill Loki during Ragnarök, while Hela merely instructed him to make certain their father's body was properly destroyed.[9]
His resentment originates from Loki's failure to protect his children when [[Odin]]'s fear of prophecy resulted in their separation and persecution. From Jörmungandr's perspective, biological parentage did not excuse Loki's passivity.

#### Angrboða
[[Angrboða]] is Jörmungandr's mother and the mother of [[Fenrir]] and Hela. Her unusual nature is important to understanding the origin of the family's END compatibility. Carrying natural END children exposed both Angrboða and Hela to the Energy, while Jörmungandr and Fenrir themselves were born as direct natural conduits of the Concept.[11][5]
Very little direct emotional interaction between Jörmungandr and Angrboða has been shown.

#### Odin
[[Odin]] represented the political authority responsible for much of the suffering experienced by Jörmungandr and his siblings. Fear of Ragnarök led Odin's regime to isolate and persecute the children associated with the prophecy before they had committed the crimes feared of them.
Jörmungandr consequently supported Hela's rebellion and fought against Odin's faction during Ragnarök.[6] His hostility was based upon personal and familial grievance rather than mere obedience to an ancient prophecy.

#### Thor
[[Thor]] is Jörmungandr's prophesied opponent. The traditional Ragnarök narrative expected the World Serpent and Thunder God to destroy one another, but the actual conflict diverged from that prediction.
Jörmungandr nevertheless fought Thor directly during Ragnarök. His poison and overall danger prevented Thor from abandoning their battle to rescue [[Odin]]'s collapsing forces.[6]
Thor survived their confrontation and was later displayed alive but defeated during Hela's auction of the former [[Norse Pantheon]].[14] Jörmungandr likewise survived, meaning the prophecy's famous mutual death did not occur.

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] became Jörmungandr's brother-in-law following Hela's voluntary marriage into the Elderblood family.[1] The two have not received the same degree of personal interaction that Victor shares with [[Fenrir]], but Jörmungandr's loyalty to Hela and later cooperation with Imperial END technology place him within Victor's extended alliance and family network.
Victor's Empire later incorporated Jörmungandr's END into Polaris together with the END of Fenrir and [[Typhon]].[7][8]

#### Nidhoggr
Nidhoggr fought on the same side as Jörmungandr during Ragnarök. Both are beings associated with END, but they are fundamentally different creatures: Jörmungandr is one of [[Loki]] and [[Angrboða]]'s natural END children, whereas Nidhoggr is an ancient independent True Dragon whose relationship with Hela originated through an agreement.
No particularly close friendship between Jörmungandr and Nidhoggr has been established, but both cooperated in Hela's campaign against [[Odin]].

### Mythological Background
In Norse mythology, Jörmungandr is the gigantic serpent child of [[Loki]] and the giantess [[Angrboða]]. [[Odin]] casts the serpent into the ocean surrounding Midgard, where Jörmungandr grows until its body encircles the world and it can grasp its own tail. Because of this, it is commonly known as the Midgard Serpent or World Serpent.
Jörmungandr is traditionally the great enemy of [[Thor]]. During Ragnarök, the serpent emerges from the sea, poisons the world and battles Thor. Thor kills Jörmungandr but survives only long enough to walk nine steps before dying from the serpent's venom.
The novel adopts many of these broad elements—Jörmungandr's serpent identity, Loki and Angrboða as parents, the Ragnarök connection, destructive venom and opposition to Thor—but significantly changes the underlying supernatural mechanics. MTW's Jörmungandr is explicitly a natural END Being, and the actual Ragnarök does not result in the mutual death of Thor and the World Serpent.

### Trivia
- Jörmungandr is one of [[Loki]] and [[Angrboða]]'s children associated with Ragnarök.
- He is a natural END Being rather than someone who merely learned to manipulate END later in life.[5]
- His canonical sex is genderless despite the story using masculine pronouns for him.[5]
- The genderless nature of natural END Beings is metaphysical: an existence representing termination cannot naturally perform the act of beginning new Life through reproduction.[5]
- His natural body is a gigantic serpent, but he can also assume a humanoid form and an extremely small snake form.[9][4]
- The novel has not yet given a detailed canonical physical description of his humanoid form.
- His tiny snake form is small enough to coil around Hela's neck.[4]
- Reducing his body size does not suppress his END nature; powerful Gods still sensed their own destruction from him while he was in miniature form.[4]
- Jörmungandr is explicitly described as the most carefree among Hela's siblings.[10]
- He willingly went to sleep while his siblings were discussing preparations for Ragnarök.[9]
- He openly intended to kill Loki during the conflict.[9]
- [[Fenrir]] advised Jörmungandr to use a smaller form against [[Thor]] because his colossal serpent body would otherwise present an unnecessarily large target.[9]
- Jörmungandr personally fought Thor during Ragnarök and prevented him from freely assisting [[Odin]]'s forces.[6]
- Contrary to traditional Norse mythology, Thor and Jörmungandr both survived their Ragnarök confrontation.
- Jörmungandr's venom can carry END strongly enough to erase a victim beyond ordinary Divine reincarnation.[4]
- He was prepared to attack [[Shiva]] when he believed Hela might be threatened.[10]
- Jörmungandr, Fenrir and [[Typhon]] all channel END directly through their bodies, while Hela historically manipulated it externally through the [[Staff of Death and End]].[5]
- The staff contains END-infused material originating from one of Hela's brothers, demonstrating that biological material from natural END Beings can retain the Concept.[11][5]
- Jörmungandr later became one of the END sources incorporated into the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s strategic weapons research.[7]
- Together with Fenrir and Typhon, he contributes END to the systems supporting Polaris, Hela's Galaxy [[Destroyer]]-class Divine warship.[7][8]
- [[True Death|The END]] cannons supplied through this system are so destructive that each cannon survives only a single discharge before disappearing.[7][8]
- Jörmungandr should not be confused with Nidhoggr. Jörmungandr is a Jötunn-born natural END serpent and child of Loki, while Nidhoggr is an Ancient True Dragon.
- Older versions of the page described Jörmungandr as a conventional male God. Chapter 1059's explanation of natural END physiology establishes the more accurate classification as genderless.
- The titles God of Seas and God of Poisons are common mythology-derived associations, but the current novel material more directly establishes Jörmungandr through his END nature, destruction classification and supernatural venom rather than explicitly naming those two as independent Divinities.
