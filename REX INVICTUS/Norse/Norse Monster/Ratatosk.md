Ratatosk, also known as Ratatoskr, was an ancient supernatural squirrel associated with [[Yggdrasil]] and the [[Norse Pantheon]]. Despite his unassuming physical form, Ratatosk possessed an extremely rare relationship with the World Tree: he was described as the only known Being capable of climbing through all [[Nine Realms]] of the Norse Pantheon and visiting other Pantheons through Yggdrasil's hidden paths.[2]
This ability made Ratatosk considerably more important than his appearance suggested. After the destruction of the [[Bifrost]], ordinary entry into the Norse Pantheon became extremely difficult because using Yggdrasil's branches directly would normally alert the World Tree, whose own ego could expel intruders. Ratatosk was the known exception, having discovered and used hidden routes running through Yggdrasil without relying upon the conventional Bifrost network.[2]
Ratatosk had previously traveled beyond the Norse realms through those routes, but eventually stopped visiting foreign Pantheons after being exploited by both the [[Greek Pantheon]] and [[Egyptian Pantheon]]. The experience made him highly suspicious of outsiders and significantly harder to bribe or manipulate despite his known greed.[2]
His ability remained valuable enough that [[Viviane]], through her own connection to Earth's World Tree, believed Ratatosk could personally escort someone through Yggdrasil's branches and into the Norse Pantheon.[3] His knowledge of those routes later became one of the reasons his disappearance was notable.
Ratatosk was also responsible for creating an extraordinarily personal enemy in Nidhoggr. While the ancient Dragon remained imprisoned within the isolated Dimension surrounding Yggdrasil's deepest roots, Ratatosk repeatedly used his ability to travel through the World Tree to enter the area and deliberately taunt him. The provocations continued long enough for Nidhoggr to develop an intense hatred of the squirrel.[1]
Hela eventually exploited that hatred while preparing for Ragnarök. After sacrificing her vitality to temporarily open Nidhoggr's prison, she bargained with the Dragon by providing things he intensely desired. One of those offerings was Ratatosk himself. Hela explicitly told [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] that she had given the squirrel to Nidhoggr "as a snack", earning another favor from the Dragon of the End.[1] Ratatosk is therefore presumed to have been eaten and killed by Nidhoggr, although his death itself was not directly shown on-page.

### Physical Appearance
Ratatosk's full physical appearance has never been extensively described.
He is explicitly identified as a squirrel.[2][1] No canonical information has been provided regarding his exact size, fur color, eye color or clothing.
Despite his animal form, Ratatosk is fully sapient, capable of communication, negotiation, understanding supernatural politics and independently navigating the metaphysical structure of [[Yggdrasil]].
His current artwork should therefore be treated as an artistic representation rather than a definitive source for unspecified physical details.

### Personality
Ratatosk is best characterized as greedy, mischievous, reckless and increasingly distrustful of outsiders.
His greed was sufficiently well known that [[Baal]] initially suggested the Demons might simply negotiate with him after the destruction of the [[Bifrost]]. [[Diablo]] immediately considered that unlikely, not because Ratatosk lacked greed but because previous experiences with other Pantheons had taught him to distrust outsiders.[2]
Ratatosk had once freely traveled through the World Tree's routes leading to foreign Pantheons. The Greek and Egyptian Pantheons eventually exploited him, causing Ratatosk to stop walking those external paths. Diablo specifically describes him as having become extremely wary of outsiders afterward.[2]
His most extreme personality trait was his recklessness toward beings vastly more dangerous than himself. While Nidhoggr remained imprisoned in the isolated Dimension surrounding [[Yggdrasil]]'s roots, Ratatosk repeatedly used his Power to travel through the World Tree and deliberately taunt the ancient True Dragon.[1]
These provocations were not isolated incidents. Hela states that they happened constantly enough for a profound hatred of Ratatosk to grow within Nidhoggr. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] learned what Ratatosk had done, even he considered the squirrel a fool for intentionally provoking a True Dragon possessing the Power of The End.[1]
Ratatosk nevertheless demonstrated considerable practical intelligence regarding Yggdrasil itself. He knew routes through the World Tree that virtually nobody else could use, understood how its branches connected the [[Nine Realms]] and foreign Pantheons, and was trusted by beings such as [[Viviane]] to act as a guide through those routes.[2][3]
The earlier claim that Ratatosk was directly responsible for creating a feud between Nidhoggr and Veðrfölnir is based upon Norse mythology rather than the currently verified novel text. Within the novel, the directly confirmed conflict is Ratatosk's own repeated provocation of Nidhoggr.

### History
Ratatosk's origin and true age are unknown. He had already developed an extraordinarily intimate knowledge of [[Yggdrasil]] long before his first mention in the story and was capable of navigating routes unavailable to nearly every other supernatural Being.[2]
At some point in the past, Ratatosk began using Yggdrasil's hidden paths to travel beyond the [[Nine Realms]] and visit other Pantheons. This made him effectively one of the rare beings capable of bypassing the normal territorial isolation separating major Divine factions.
His travels eventually brought him into contact with the [[Greek Pantheon]] and [[Egyptian Pantheon]], both of which exploited him in unspecified ways.[2]
Ratatosk learned from the experience and stopped using the routes leading toward other Pantheons. He became extremely wary of outsiders and apparently remained primarily within the Norse World Tree network afterward.
Ratatosk also regularly traveled through the deeper branches and roots of Yggdrasil. These travels brought him near the isolated Dimension imprisoning Nidhoggr, the ancient Dragon of the End responsible for continually gnawing upon Yggdrasil's roots.
Rather than avoiding the imprisoned True Dragon, Ratatosk repeatedly taunted him.
Nidhoggr was unable to freely escape the Dimension at the time, allowing Ratatosk to use the World Tree's branches to approach, provoke him and then leave. The repeated humiliation eventually created an intense personal hatred between the Dragon and the squirrel.[1]
This seemingly pointless antagonism ultimately contributed directly to Ratatosk's death.

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Ratatosk is first mentioned after Hela secretly used Nidhoggr to destroy the [[Bifrost]], cutting off one of the principal methods of accessing the [[Norse Pantheon]].
While discussing the consequences with [[Baal]], [[Diablo]] explains that reaching [[Asgard]] without the Bifrost is exceptionally difficult. The remaining alternative involved traveling through the branches of [[Yggdrasil]], but attempting to do so conventionally would alert the World Tree. Yggdrasil possessed its own ego and could simply expel unauthorized travelers.[2]
Ratatosk represented the major exception.
Diablo described him as the only known Being capable of climbing through all [[Nine Realms]] and traveling to other Pantheons. More importantly, Ratatosk knew the hidden paths of Yggdrasil connecting those Pantheons, making him one of the only beings capable of bypassing the conventional dimensional entrances into the Norse territories.[2]
Baal suggested bribing Ratatosk because of his greed, but Diablo dismissed the idea. Ratatosk had once traveled more freely outside the Norse Pantheon, only for the Greek and Egyptian Pantheons to exploit him. Since then, he had stopped traveling those external routes and become deeply suspicious of outsiders.[2]
Ratatosk's unusual value became relevant again after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] took control of Hell. Victor wanted a discreet route into Helheim without alerting [[Odin]] through the conventional systems surrounding Earth's World Tree.
He approached [[Viviane]], whose Fairy nature gave her a connection to Yggdrasil, and requested that she open a path into the Norse Pantheon. Viviane eventually accepted and stated that she would speak with Ratatosk so that he could escort Victor through the World Tree's branches.[3]
The scene further establishes that Ratatosk's role was not merely knowing where the paths existed. He was recognized as someone capable of safely guiding others through them.
Whether Victor ever completed that specific journey with Ratatosk is not shown during this period.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Ratatosk's past relationship with Nidhoggr became critically important during Hela's preparations for Ragnarök.
Nidhoggr lived within a separate Dimension around the deepest roots of [[Yggdrasil]]. The Dimension functioned simultaneously as his home and prison and was sufficiently powerful that even a Being possessing The End could not simply leave under normal circumstances.[1]
Ratatosk nevertheless possessed enough familiarity with the World Tree to repeatedly use his Power to roam through its branches and approach the trapped Dragon. Rather than avoiding one of the most dangerous beings associated with Ragnarök, Ratatosk made a habit of taunting him.[1]
The repeated provocations produced a deep hatred within Nidhoggr.
Hela eventually realized this hatred could be used as bargaining material. In order to temporarily free the Dragon from his prison, she sacrificed an enormous portion of her vitality and opened a rift into the isolated Dimension. Nidhoggr then agreed to grant her favors in exchange for the things she provided him.[1]
One favor had already been spent on destroying the [[Bifrost]].
To obtain another, Hela gave Nidhoggr Ratatosk.
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later questioned how she had convinced the proud True Dragon to cooperate further, Hela answered directly:
"Ratatoskr... I gave him the squirrel as a snack for the Dragon."
[1]
Victor immediately understood why attempts to locate Ratatosk had failed. Hela explained that because Ratatosk had spent so long provoking the imprisoned Dragon, giving him to Nidhoggr was sufficient to earn another favor.[1]
Victor was incredulous that a squirrel had intentionally taunted an ancient True Dragon connected to The End and concluded that Ratatosk had effectively brought the situation upon himself.
Ratatosk is not shown again afterward.
Because Hela explicitly describes him as food offered to Nidhoggr and because his disappearance is subsequently explained by that transaction, Ratatosk is presumed to have been eaten and killed by the Dragon. The actual act of Nidhoggr consuming him, however, does not occur on-page.

### Skills & Abilities
Ratatosk's known abilities are almost entirely connected to [[Yggdrasil]]. His exact Race and supernatural classification have never been established, so abilities belonging generically to Spirits, High Spirits or mythological Ratatoskr should not automatically be assigned to him.

#### World Tree Traversal
Ratatosk possessed an extraordinary ability to move through the branches and paths of [[Yggdrasil]].
Chapter 667 identifies him as the only known Being capable of climbing through all [[Nine Realms]] of the [[Norse Pantheon]].[2]
Known applications include:
- World Tree Traversal: Ratatosk could move through the metaphysical branches of Yggdrasil rather than relying upon ordinary roads or portals.
- Dimensional Travel (Through Yggdrasil): Because the Nine Realms are distinct Divine territories connected through the World Tree, Ratatosk's traversal allowed him to move between separate realms.[2]
- Inter-Pantheon Travel (Through Yggdrasil): Ratatosk could follow hidden World Tree routes beyond the Norse Pantheon and enter other Pantheons.[2]
- Hidden Path Navigation: He knew secret routes through Yggdrasil that were unavailable or unknown to most supernatural beings.[2]
- Dimensional Route Bypassing: His paths offered an alternative to conventional structures such as the [[Bifrost]].
- Restricted-Realm Access: Ratatosk's familiarity with Yggdrasil allowed him to reach regions surrounding Nidhoggr's otherwise isolated prison and taunt the Dragon before escaping through the branches.[1]
This should not be interpreted as unrestricted Dimensional Travel independent of Yggdrasil. Every demonstrated or described application is specifically tied to the World Tree and its hidden routes.

#### Cross-Pantheon Travel
Ratatosk was explicitly described as the only known Being capable of visiting other Pantheons through [[Yggdrasil]]'s hidden paths.[2]
He had personally used these routes in the past before his experiences with the Greek and Egyptian Pantheons caused him to stop.
This ability was significant because the normal method of traveling into a Pantheon could be blocked or monitored. Even after the [[Bifrost]] was destroyed, Ratatosk remained a possible method of secretly infiltrating the [[Norse Pantheon]].

#### World Tree Path Knowledge
Ratatosk possessed exceptional knowledge of [[Yggdrasil]]'s internal geography.
He knew:
- the routes connecting the [[Nine Realms]];
- hidden paths through Yggdrasil;
- routes capable of reaching other Pantheons;
- and methods of moving through the branches without relying upon conventional dimensional infrastructure.[2]
This grants him:
- Navigation
- Dimensional Pathfinding
- World Tree Knowledge
- Secret Route Knowledge
His knowledge was valuable enough that [[Viviane]] intended to use him as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s personal escort through Yggdrasil's branches.[3]

#### Guiding Others
Ratatosk was not limited to traveling through the World Tree alone.
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] requested a hidden route into Helheim, [[Viviane]] proposed contacting Ratatosk specifically so that he could escort Victor through the branches.[3]
This establishes that Ratatosk could guide other beings through routes that they could not safely navigate independently.

#### World Tree Concealment / Bypass
Ordinary beings attempting to move into the [[Norse Pantheon]] directly through [[Yggdrasil]]'s branches risked alerting the World Tree itself. [[Diablo]] explained that Yggdrasil's ego could detect such intrusion and throw the travelers out.[2]
Ratatosk's existence is explicitly presented as the exception to this obstacle.
The exact mechanism is unknown. It may result from his relationship with Yggdrasil, unique knowledge of hidden routes, a personal Power or a combination of all three.
Consequently, this should be indexed conservatively as:
- World Tree Security Bypass
- Stealthy Dimensional Navigation (Through known Yggdrasil routes)
It should not be treated as proof that Ratatosk possesses unrestricted invisibility, presence erasure or conceptual stealth.

#### Supernatural Mobility
Hela states that Ratatosk used his Power to roam [[Yggdrasil]]'s branches while repeatedly visiting Nidhoggr's prison.[1]
This confirms that his traversal was supernatural rather than merely the physical climbing ability of an ordinary squirrel.
The original page claimed Ratatosk could move from the top of Yggdrasil to its roots "in seconds". No direct statement confirming that specific travel time was found in the reviewed chapters, so that claim has been removed.

#### Longevity
Ratatosk was clearly an ancient supernatural Being and had enough history to have traveled among multiple Pantheons, been exploited by the Greeks and Egyptians and repeatedly antagonized Nidhoggr while the Dragon remained imprisoned.
However, the novel does not directly state that Ratatosk was as old as [[Odin]] or older than him.
His age should therefore remain Unknown rather than being listed as "Eons" unless a direct chapter establishes that comparison.

### Relationships

#### Yggdrasil
[[Yggdrasil]] is the World Tree upon which Ratatosk lived and around which virtually all of his known abilities were based.
Ratatosk possessed a relationship with Yggdrasil unusual enough that he could travel throughout its [[Nine Realms]], discover paths leading toward foreign Pantheons and use routes that ordinary beings could not safely traverse.[2]
Exactly how Yggdrasil regarded Ratatosk has never been directly explained. The old description of the World Tree as his "landlady" was humorous wiki terminology rather than a formal relationship stated by the novel and should not be treated as a canonical title.

#### Nidhoggr
Nidhoggr became Ratatosk's greatest known enemy.
While Nidhoggr remained imprisoned within the isolated Dimension containing [[Yggdrasil]]'s deepest roots, Ratatosk repeatedly used his Power to travel through the World Tree and taunt the Dragon.[1]
The provocations continued frequently enough that Nidhoggr developed a deep hatred for him.
This hatred eventually became useful to Hela. Knowing how badly Nidhoggr wanted Ratatosk, she captured or otherwise obtained the squirrel and offered him to the Dragon as food in exchange for another favor.[1]
The act is the presumed cause of Ratatosk's death.
No verified novel statement establishes that Ratatosk created Nidhoggr's hatred toward Veðrfölnir or another eagle. That material originates from traditional Norse mythology and should not be mixed into the MTW article without a direct source.

#### Viviane
[[Viviane]] knew Ratatosk well enough to contact him and arrange his services as a guide.
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] requested a secret path into Helheim, Viviane agreed to speak with Ratatosk and ask him to escort Victor through [[Yggdrasil]]'s branches.[3]
This establishes Ratatosk as part of the small network of supernatural beings familiar with the deeper routes of Earth's World Tree.

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] never receives a substantial direct on-page interaction with Ratatosk in the reviewed material, but he attempted to make use of Ratatosk's unique World Tree knowledge.
Victor's original plan involved having [[Viviane]] open a route toward the [[Norse Pantheon]] and using Ratatosk as his escort through [[Yggdrasil]]'s branches.[3]
Much later, when Hela revealed that she had given Ratatosk to Nidhoggr, Victor immediately realized that this explained why [[Valeria Alekerth]] had been unable to locate the squirrel.[1]
Victor considered Ratatosk's habit of provoking Nidhoggr extraordinarily foolish.

#### Greek and Egyptian Pantheons
Ratatosk previously traveled into the [[Greek Pantheon]] and [[Egyptian Pantheon]] using [[Yggdrasil]]'s hidden paths.
Both Pantheons exploited him in unspecified ways.[2]
The experience caused Ratatosk to stop using the routes leading toward other Pantheons and become significantly more suspicious of outsiders.
The novel does not explain exactly what the Greeks or Egyptians did to him.

### Power
Ratatosk should not be treated as a conventional combat character.
No direct feat establishes exceptional attack power, durability, physical strength or combat skill. His importance came from a highly specialized ability that even major Gods and Demons lacked: safe navigation through the hidden dimensional paths of [[Yggdrasil]].
His value was therefore primarily strategic.
After the [[Bifrost]] was destroyed, [[Diablo]] considered the World Tree routes essentially unusable because Yggdrasil itself would detect and expel intruders. Ratatosk was the only known exception, making him one of the few possible methods of entering [[Asgard]] covertly.[2]
Hela's later use of Ratatosk as payment to Nidhoggr further demonstrates that his strategic and personal value could affect agreements with beings vastly beyond his own apparent combat level.[1]
The current novel material does not support ranking Ratatosk alongside Gods, High Spirits or combat-focused Divine entities.
