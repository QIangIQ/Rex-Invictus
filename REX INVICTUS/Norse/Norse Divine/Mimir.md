Mimir is a God belonging to the [[Norse Pantheon]] who is regarded as the wisest among the Norse Gods and described as the one who records everything.[1] Despite the importance implied by this reputation, Mimir has received extremely little direct focus in the novel. He has not yet been physically introduced in a dedicated scene, and most details concerning his Divine rank, generation, family, appearance, personal history and individual supernatural abilities remain unknown.
Mimir's reputation is significant because it is acknowledged by [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood, an ancient Goddess with extensive knowledge of multiple Pantheons. While discussing the dangers of manipulating Time, Aphrodite notes that the Norse Pantheon is more knowledgeable than outsiders sometimes assume and singles Mimir out as its wisest member and the God responsible for recording its knowledge.[1]
Although the title God of Records has previously been used for Mimir on the wiki, Chapter 810 does not explicitly identify Records as one of his formal Divinities. The text establishes him as the Pantheon's record keeper, but the exact supernatural mechanics of that role have not yet been revealed.

### Physical Appearance
Mimir's canonical physical appearance has not yet been described in the novel.
The current image used in the infobox is therefore an artistic representation rather than a confirmed depiction of his hair color, eye color, clothing, age or other physical characteristics.

### Personality
Mimir has not yet received enough direct dialogue or on-page characterization for a detailed personality profile.
What is known comes primarily from his reputation. He is considered the wisest individual within the [[Norse Pantheon]] and is responsible for recording its knowledge, suggesting that scholarship, preservation of information and historical record-keeping form major parts of his role within [[Asgard]].[1]
These qualities should not be expanded into specific personality traits such as kindness, arrogance, pacifism or loyalty without a direct appearance.

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Mimir is first mentioned while [[Victor Elderblood]], [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] and [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood discuss the dangers associated with the manipulation of Time.
Victor warns Violet that recklessly interfering with Time could lead to catastrophic consequences or even erasure. Aphrodite supports his warning and explains that Pantheons normally treat unrestricted Time manipulation extremely seriously because misuse of the Concept can disrupt Creation and the Balance.[1]
During this explanation, Aphrodite comments that the Norse Gods are more intelligent and knowledgeable than they are sometimes given credit for. She specifically identifies Mimir as:
the wisest among them and the one who records everything.
[1]
The statement establishes Mimir as one of the principal intellectual authorities of the [[Norse Pantheon]]. His role appears to involve preserving the knowledge and records accumulated by [[Asgard]], although Chapter 810 does not explain where those records are stored, how they are maintained or whether Mimir's record-keeping relies upon a supernatural Divinity.
Mimir does not personally participate in the conversation and is mentioned only through Aphrodite's explanation.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Mimir does not receive an individual on-page appearance during the chapters surrounding Ragnarök.
The [[Norse Pantheon]] later collapses during [[Hela Elderblood]]'s war against [[Odin]], after which the surviving territory and remaining assets of the Pantheon are reorganized and eventually sold.[2][3]
Mimir's individual location, participation in the war and condition afterward are not directly described.
Because no confirmed death scene or later survival scene has been identified, his current status should be treated as Unknown rather than automatically assuming that he survived or died during Ragnarök.

### Skills & Abilities
Very little of Mimir's personal supernatural ability set has been demonstrated. The novel establishes his status as a God and his extraordinary intellectual reputation, but it has not shown him fighting or directly using a named Divinity.

#### God Physiology
As a genuine God of the [[Norse Pantheon]], Mimir possesses a Divine existence rather than an ordinary Mortal body.
The novel has not demonstrated Mimir-specific physical feats, so generic God abilities should not be expanded beyond what his Race reasonably establishes.
- Divine Physiology: Mimir possesses the supernatural body and spiritual nature of a God.
- [[Divine Energy]]: As a God, Mimir possesses Divine Energy as part of his existence.
- [[Immortality]] / Agelessness: Gods are not subject to ordinary Human aging.
- Divine Soul: Mimir possesses the supernatural Soul of a Divine being.
No Mimir-specific feats currently establish his exact strength, speed, regeneration, durability or combat ranking relative to other Norse Gods.

#### Exceptional Intelligence
Mimir's clearest confirmed attribute is his extraordinary intelligence.
[[Aphrodite]] explicitly identifies him as the wisest member of the [[Norse Pantheon]].[1]
This grants him confirmed:
- Genius Intelligence
- Exceptional Wisdom
- Supernatural Scholarship
- Extensive Knowledge
His exact fields of expertise have not been catalogued, but his position as the Pantheon's principal record keeper strongly associates him with the preservation and accumulation of knowledge.

#### Record Keeping
Mimir is described as the one who records everything within the [[Norse Pantheon]].[1]
His responsibilities therefore include some form of comprehensive record preservation.
Confirmed or directly supported applications include:
- Historical Record Keeping: Mimir records information belonging to the Norse Pantheon.
- Knowledge Preservation: His role involves maintaining information accumulated by [[Asgard]].
- Archival Knowledge: Mimir possesses or manages records sufficiently important that [[Aphrodite]] specifically associates Norse intelligence with him.
- Information Management: His role requires the organization and preservation of large quantities of information.
The exact supernatural mechanism has never been explained.
It is currently unknown whether Mimir:
- records information manually;
- possesses supernatural omnidirectional perception;
- automatically receives information from Asgard;
- maintains a Divine archive;
- possesses a formal <Records> Divinity;
- or can access records in a manner comparable to the [[Akashic Records]].
None of these possibilities should be treated as confirmed until demonstrated.

#### Knowledge of Divine and Cosmological Matters
Mimir's reputation is mentioned specifically during a conversation concerning dangerous cosmological knowledge such as Time Manipulation, the Balance and the authority of [[Primordial Entities]].[1]
[[Aphrodite]]'s statement indicates that the [[Norse Pantheon]] possesses significant accumulated knowledge and presents Mimir as its foremost intellectual authority.
However, the chapter does not directly state that Mimir personally discovered the information being discussed or that he possesses complete knowledge of Time, Primordial Entities or the entire Cosmos.
He should therefore be described as an exceptionally knowledgeable Divine scholar rather than as omniscient.

### Power
Mimir's combat power is currently unknown.
No chapter directly establishes:
- his Divine generation;
- his Divine rank;
- whether he is a Minor, Major, Combat, God-King or Primordial-class God;
- his preferred method of combat;
- his weapon;
- or any named offensive or defensive Divinity.
His reputation in Chapter 810 concerns wisdom and records, not combat strength.[1]
Consequently, Mimir should not be assigned a high combat ranking solely because of his mythological reputation or intellectual importance.
