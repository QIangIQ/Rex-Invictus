Disir (sing. Dis), sometimes known as Valkyries, are a group of powerful Elder warriors who ruled the North several thousand years ago.

### Appearance
Apparently female-exclusive species, Disir are tall, with platinum blonde hair and eyes of varying shades of blue and purple. While they are capable of posing as humani, in which case they will wear humani clothes, they typically wear in white chainmails and robes, and wield various weapons.

### History
Prior to the fall of [[Danu Talis]], the Disir split from the [[Elders]] that were living in the city and chose to establish an empire in the North. With the fall of Danu Talis spurring the Ice Age, the Disir quickly seized control over much of the globe. To deal with the threat of global domination, [[Zephaniah]] gave [[Chronos]] her eyes in exchange for the ability to see through the strings of time. By looking through ten thousand years, she chose a warrior from each millennium, and tasked Chronos with assembling them into a team. Included in this team was Zephaniah's own granddaughter, [[Scathach]], who led the assault at the Disir capital, situated at the very top of the world. The battle, known as Ragnarok, lasted only a single night, and ended with Scathach slaying the Disir Queen [[Brynhildr]] and throwing her heart into an active volcano.

### Powers and Abilities
The Disir are highly potent warriors, commanding both magical and physical strength. They are most powerful in cold environments, and are at the height of their power during the hours of twilight and dawn-
- Control over [[Nidhogg]] : The Disir are able to wake Nidhogg, and can control it to some degree.
- Superhuman Strength: The Disir are much stronger than humani. They are able to run longer and faster, jump great heights, and strike with incredible strength, speed, and precision.
- [[Immortality]]: The Disir are biologically immortal, and cannot die from age, illness, warmth or food deprivation.
