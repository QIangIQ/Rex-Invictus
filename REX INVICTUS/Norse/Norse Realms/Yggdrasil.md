**[[Yggdrasil]]** is the ancient Positive World Tree of Earth, an Elder World Tree created by The Universal Tree as part of the cosmic system responsible for developing and maintaining Life-bearing planets. (Lore adapted from *The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel*.)

---

## Real-World Background (IRL)

Yggdrasil is the immense ash tree at the center of the Norse cosmos, whose branches and roots join the Nine Worlds. In [[Magnus Chase]] it is the World Tree linking the worlds.

---

## Flamel Series Canon (SOFTINF)

> The original Yggdrasil was impossibly wide, incalculably tall, and other measurements as well. It was the site of the Tree City of Wakah-Chan, as well as being the residence of the Elder [[Hecate|Hekate]]. The topmost branches and leaves were not able to be seen, towering high into the clouds, wreathed in wisps of white cloud, and the roots that tunneled deep into the core of the Earth and also burst from the ground like clawing fingers that were as tall as cars. It housed countless ecosystems from above the tree to below it. The tree itself was gnarled and twisted, its bark deeply etched with cracks and lines. Long vines, like huge pipes, wrapped around the tree and dangled down from the branches. There were hundreds of windows cut into the bark, with lights flickering in the rooms beyond.

### Appearance
The interior of the tree was hollow. From just inside the entrance, a person could look straight up to where wispy clouds gathered inside  of the tree. A gently curving staircase curled up along the inside of the trunk, and every few steps brought them to an open doorway spilling out light. Bridges of branches streched from one side to another. Branches could even grow to the size of sixty feet wide, if not more. Dozens of tiny waterfalls spouted from the walls and splashed down onto the floor far below, where the water gathered in a huge circular pool that took up most of the foyer. The interior walls were smooth and unadorned, except from the twists and knots of vines that broke through the surface. The Nathair are the winged guardians of Yggdrasil.

### The Second Yggdrasil
When the original Yggdrasil was destroyed, [[Hecate|Hekate]] and [[Odin]], both deities of magic, managed to grow a second Yggdrasil, with Hekate taking up residence within once again. The seed from which this tree grew was salvaged from the original Yggdrasil after the fall of [[Danu Talis]]. This tree was the place where [[Sophie Newman]] was Awakened by Hekate. The topmost branches of Yggdrasil extend far into the [[Asgard]] Shadowrealm, while its root went down to [[Niflheim]] and a formidable monster, [[Nidhogg]], was trapped there by its roots, which were believed to have been imperishable. The second Yggdrasil was killed by [[Dr. John Dee]], when he plunged one of the Four [[Swords of Power]], [[Excalibur]], into the tree. Excalibur froze Yggdrasil, then Hekate, ultimately shattering them both.

This article is about the sentient World Tree. For the planet formerly known as Earth and later renamed in her honor, see Yggdrasil.
Yggdrasil is the ancient Positive World Tree of Earth, an Elder World Tree created by [[The Universal Tree]] as part of the cosmic system responsible for developing and maintaining Life-bearing planets. She is the positive counterpart and elder sister of Qiliphoth, [[Qliphoth|Earth's Negative World Tree]], with the two forming the complementary World Tree system attached to the planet.[5][2]
Although Yggdrasil is most famously associated with the [[Norse Pantheon]], she is not fundamentally a subordinate of [[Asgard]] or a World Tree belonging exclusively to the Norse Gods. Her existence is tied to Earth itself and predates the civilizations and Pantheons that later arose upon the planet. Her roots became integrated with multiple supernatural dimensions, including structures associated with the Norse cosmology, but her primary instinct as a World Tree remains the preservation and continued development of her planet rather than loyalty to any particular Divine civilization.[6][1]
Yggdrasil was first mentioned when [[Scathach]] identified [[Roxanne Elderblood]] as a young World Tree and compared her existence to Yggdrasil, whom Scathach had previously encountered while visiting the Norse Pantheon.[3] She did not personally appear until much later, manifesting an Energy representative beside Qiliphoth while [[Victor Elderblood]] investigated a dangerous abandoned region connected to Earth's World Tree roots.[1]
Yggdrasil's relationship with Victor later changed dramatically after she absorbed Energy released during Victor's breakthrough into Attributeless Energy. The exposure affected numerous [[World Trees]] throughout the Galaxy, but older Trees such as Yggdrasil were affected particularly strongly. Yggdrasil and Qiliphoth developed an intense fixation upon Victor and his Energy, with their normal planetary instincts increasingly redirecting toward the desire to nurture Victor himself as though he were their own planet.[4]

### Physical Appearance

#### Energy Avatar
Yggdrasil's actual existence is that of Earth's colossal World Tree rather than an ordinary humanoid body. When she personally appeared before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], she used a representative humanoid manifestation composed entirely of pure white Energy.[1]
She appeared beside Qiliphoth, whose corresponding representative was composed of red Energy. Yggdrasil explicitly clarified that the women Victor saw were only representatives and not the [[World Trees]]' actual bodies.[1]
Her white Energy manifestation bears a visual resemblance to the light coloration associated with [[Amara Elderblood]], another Positive World Tree.

#### True Body
Yggdrasil's true body is Earth's immense Positive World Tree. Unlike an ordinary biological tree, her existence is integrated directly with the planetary environment and the supernatural structures connected to Earth.
Her roots extend beyond ordinary physical terrain and into dimensional spaces. This became particularly apparent when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] discovered an isolated realm containing abandoned portions of the World Tree's roots. Those roots had previously helped sustain the surrounding dimension and were connected to structures associated with the Norse supernatural system.[1]
As an Elder World Tree, Yggdrasil should therefore be understood as a planetary-scale supernatural existence whose visible tree is only one physical expression of a much larger spiritual and dimensional structure.

### Personality
Yggdrasil is calm, ancient and considerably more socially composed than her sister Qiliphoth. During their first direct meeting with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Yggdrasil handled most of the conversation while Qiliphoth repeatedly hid behind her because of her extreme shyness.[1]
She displayed patience when interacting with Victor despite his irreverent behavior. Rather than reacting violently to his jokes or casual attitude, Yggdrasil generally attempted to keep the conversation focused upon the dangerous dimensional anomaly that had brought them together. When Victor questioned Qiliphoth's behavior, Yggdrasil defended her sister and explained that Qiliphoth would approach him when she was comfortable enough to do so.[1]
Yggdrasil is also practical and understands the limitations of her own cosmic authority. When Victor asked why she had not simply summoned one of the [[Regulators of Existence]] to deal with the abnormal dimension, she explained that she had already requested assistance. Unlike Victor, however, she lacked sufficient System Authority to force an immediate response and had effectively been placed in an administrative queue.[1]
Both Yggdrasil and Qiliphoth were visibly shocked by Victor's behavior toward the Primordial administrators who eventually responded. Despite being ancient [[World Trees]] themselves, they were accustomed to treating such entities with the gravity appropriate to their cosmic positions, while Victor addressed them with almost no reverence.[1]
Like other Positive World Trees, Yggdrasil's deepest instinct revolves around the survival of her planet rather than any individual civilization living upon it. World Trees can form attachments to mortal races, Gods and supernatural societies, but those attachments remain secondary to planetary survival. If forced to choose between preserving their world and preserving its current sentient inhabitants, a Positive World Tree will choose the planet because new Life can eventually develop again.[6]
Yggdrasil is consequently not truly loyal to the [[Norse Pantheon]] despite centuries of cultural association. [[Asgard]] and its inhabitants represented one civilization existing within the planetary system she sustained, not owners with authority over her.
Her mentality became substantially less detached after exposure to Victor's Energy. Victor's unfiltered Energy carried the characteristics of his Eldritch existence and his extreme obsessive nature. When World Trees absorbed it, their behavior shifted toward similarly intense attachment. Yggdrasil was among the older Trees affected most heavily and was shown flushed and breathing heavily alongside Qiliphoth as their desire for Victor's Energy intensified.[4]
By Chapter 1116, Victor casually stated that the planet loved him while discussing Yggdrasil and her counterpart with his childhood friends. He explained that World Trees are normally effectively uninterested in ordinary romance or reproduction because their lives revolve around nurturing planets and because their Souls exist on planetary scales. Yggdrasil's attraction toward Victor was therefore extraordinary even by supernatural standards.[2]

### Nature

#### Positive World Tree of Earth
Yggdrasil is specifically Earth's Positive World Tree. Earlier information describing her as a single neutral Tree containing both Positive and Negative aspects was superseded once the actual structure of planetary [[World Trees]] was explained.[5]
A newly developing Life-bearing planet first possesses a Positive World Tree. Over millions of years, the Tree nurtures the planet and facilitates the emergence of Life. Once sufficiently developed sentient beings begin producing the necessary Negative Energy, a second World Tree begins forming as the Negative counterpart.[5]
On Earth:
- Yggdrasil is the Positive World Tree.
- Qiliphoth is the Negative World Tree.
The two are independent conscious beings but are simultaneously complementary components of the same planetary system.[5]

#### Relationship with Earth
Yggdrasil's primary responsibility is Earth itself. [[World Trees]] exist to sustain the planets connected to them, circulate the Energy necessary for Life and provide the foundation from which planetary ecosystems and supernatural civilization can develop.[5][6]
This bond is deeper than a conventional relationship between a guardian and territory. A mature World Tree is intrinsically integrated with its planet. The planet functions as the Tree's natural home and object of nourishment, while the Tree functions as one of the foundations allowing Life to flourish.
Yggdrasil therefore existed long before modern Humanity or Earth's present Pantheons. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later explained that World Trees are approximately as old as the planets they support and consequently witnessed the births of Gods and mortal civilizations over enormous spans of Time.[2]

#### Relationship with Qiliphoth
Qiliphoth emerged as the Negative counterpart to Yggdrasil after Earth's sentient Life reached the stage necessary to sustain a Negative World Tree.[5]
Although the two are separate individuals, Positive and Negative [[World Trees]] remain intrinsically connected as two aspects of one planetary system. Their personalities can differ considerably. Yggdrasil is composed and comparatively outgoing, while Qiliphoth is extremely shy and initially hid behind Yggdrasil during their first encounter with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[1]
Yggdrasil often acts as the more socially capable sister and spoke on Qiliphoth's behalf during their initial appearance.

#### Relationship with Earth's Pantheons
Yggdrasil is traditionally associated most strongly with the [[Norse Pantheon]] because of the mythology and dimensional structure surrounding [[Asgard]]. [[Scathach]] had personally encountered her during a previous visit to the Nordic Pantheon, demonstrating that Yggdrasil maintained a visible connection to that Divine civilization.[3]
That association does not establish ownership. Yggdrasil predates the Pantheon and remains fundamentally connected to Earth itself.
The abandoned roots investigated by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] demonstrate how closely World Tree infrastructure can be connected to Divine dimensions. Once Yggdrasil withdrew nourishment from the damaged region, the surrounding dimensional system began destabilizing.[1]

### Synopsis

#### A New Pillar
Yggdrasil was first mentioned after [[Roxanne Elderblood]] evolved beyond the state of an ordinary Dryad. [[Scathach]], who had previously encountered Yggdrasil while visiting the [[Norse Pantheon]], recognized the sensation produced by Roxanne and identified her as a young World Tree.[3]
Scathach's opinion of Yggdrasil was apparently poor, as she referred to her simply as "that bitch" before Ruby clarified that she meant Yggdrasil. The comparison was nevertheless important because it established for the first time that Roxanne belonged to the same extraordinary class of Being as Earth's ancient World Tree.[3]
The revelation also introduced [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and several members of his family to the importance of [[World Trees]]. Until that point, Victor's understanding of the concept had primarily come from mythology and fiction. Learning that Roxanne could develop into an existence comparable to Yggdrasil forced the group to reconsider how valuable and dangerous her existence truly was.[3]

#### King Among Kings
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s understanding of Yggdrasil became significantly more accurate after meeting [[Aurora Seraphina Evergreen]], the Positive World Tree of Samar, and her newly born Negative counterpart [[Aria Seraphina Evergreen]].[5]
Aurora explained the fundamental lifecycle of [[World Trees]]. The Positive Tree appears first, gradually nurtures the planet and helps produce Life. Once sentient Life develops and generates sufficient Negative Energy, a Negative counterpart begins forming. The two Trees remain separate individuals while simultaneously forming one interconnected planetary system.[5]
This explanation clarified the actual structure existing on Earth. Yggdrasil was not a single "neutral" Tree containing every polarity within herself; she was Earth's mature Positive World Tree, while Qiliphoth existed as her Negative counterpart.
Aurora later revealed that Positive World Trees maintain communication with their sisters across enormous distances. Among the Trees with whom she remained in contact was Yggdrasil on Earth. Through these inter-World Tree connections, Yggdrasil and her sisters could exchange information regarding the development of their planets and major events occurring throughout the wider supernatural world.[7]
The encounter also provided important information about the mentality Yggdrasil shares with other World Trees. During the later political crisis on Samar, Victor correctly stated that a Positive World Tree will prioritize planetary survival over the continued existence of the civilization currently living on that planet. Aurora, Aria and Roxanne did not dispute him. This principle explains why Yggdrasil's historical association with Earth's Pantheons should not be interpreted as unconditional loyalty to them.[6]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Yggdrasil finally appeared directly before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] during the events surrounding Ragnarök.
While investigating the circumstances surrounding Nidhoggr and [[Hela Elderblood]], Victor entered an abnormal isolated dimension containing portions of Earth's World Tree roots. The environment had become dangerously unstable because numerous incompatible Energies were interacting within the same space, and the dimension itself had developed into an anomaly outside the normal conditions expected by the cosmic System.[1]
The region had previously maintained a fragile balance while Nidhoggr remained there. After Nidhoggr left, the equilibrium collapsed. Portions of Yggdrasil's root system connected to the region had effectively been abandoned, depriving the surrounding dimensional structures of part of the nourishment previously supplied by the World Tree.[1]
Recognizing that the problem had escalated far beyond an ordinary Divine dispute, Victor used his System Authority to request the intervention of a higher cosmic administrator. The System registered that Entity <Yggdrasil> had observed his request and strongly supported it.[1]
Yggdrasil and Qiliphoth subsequently manifested beside Victor. Yggdrasil appeared as a woman composed of pure white Energy while Qiliphoth appeared through a red Energy representative. Yggdrasil immediately clarified that these forms were merely representatives rather than their true bodies.[1]
Qiliphoth's extreme shyness caused her to hide behind Yggdrasil almost immediately. Yggdrasil acted as the principal speaker and explained her sister's behavior to Victor, asking him not to pressure Qiliphoth and assuring him that she would approach naturally once she became comfortable.[1]
Victor questioned why Yggdrasil had not already called the Regulators herself. She revealed that she had indeed attempted to report the problem but did not possess enough System Authority to receive an immediate response. Victor's unusual status allowed his own request to receive dramatically greater priority.[1]
When the relevant Primordial administrators eventually arrived, Yggdrasil and Qiliphoth witnessed Victor address them with the same casual irreverence he displayed toward ordinary supernatural authorities. Their visible shock highlighted just how abnormal Victor's position had become even from the perspective of ancient [[World Trees]].[1]
Victor ultimately decided that he had already fulfilled his responsibility by reporting the anomaly and left the Regulators and World Trees to resolve the cosmic consequences themselves.[1]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Yggdrasil's importance to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s civilization became even more symbolic after the complete political unification of Earth. When the [[Elderblood Empire]] was formally founded, Victor abolished the planet's old political order and renamed Earth itself Yggdrasil, causing the planet and its Positive World Tree to share the same name.[8]
The relationship between Yggdrasil and Victor changed far more dramatically following Victor's breakthrough into Attributeless Energy.
When Victor released an immense quantity of his newly transformed Energy, most of it was absorbed by [[World Trees]] already connected to him, while the remaining Energy spread throughout the Galaxy and was absorbed by other World Trees. The effects were immediate: young Trees experienced abnormal growth, some reached maturity far earlier than expected, and many began developing an extreme dependence upon Victor's Energy.[4]
Roxanne described the affected Trees as becoming completely obsessed with Victor. Rather than remaining satisfied with ordinary symbiotic connections, they increasingly wanted to nurture him directly as though Victor himself were their planet.[4]
The older World Trees were affected particularly strongly. Roxanne's report specifically displayed Yggdrasil and her counterpart breathing heavily with flushed faces after absorbing Victor's Energy. Aurora on Samar displayed a similar reaction.[4]
The phenomenon was partly caused by the quality of Victor's Energy. Victor was both an Eldritch God and an existence whose fundamental nature included extreme obsession and attachment. World Trees that absorbed his unfiltered Energy consequently experienced a corresponding shift in their mentality.[4]
Yggdrasil and Qiliphoth's resulting feelings were later acknowledged directly during Victor's reunion with his childhood friends. When Victor commented that the planet loved him, Andrew questioned how Victor had managed to make a World Tree and its counterpart fall for him when such entities were normally effectively asexual.[2]
Victor explained that all World Trees were created female by [[The Universal Tree]], but they normally had virtually no interest in mortal concepts of reproduction or romantic relationships. World Trees are ancient, planet-focused entities whose principal purpose is to nurture their worlds. Their Souls themselves are essentially planetary in scale, further separating their normal existence from ordinary biological life.[2]
Yggdrasil's attachment to Victor therefore represents a major deviation from the normal psychology of her species.

### Skills & Abilities
Elder Positive World Tree Physiology: Yggdrasil is an Elder-class Positive World Tree and one of the oldest known members of her species. Her body, Soul and supernatural functions are intrinsically connected to the planet Earth and its Life-supporting systems.[5][2]
- Planetary Life Support: Positive [[World Trees]] are responsible for nurturing planets until they become capable of sustaining complex Life. Their Energy acts as one of the foundations allowing ecosystems and eventually sentient civilizations to develop.[5]
- Life Energy Generation: Yggdrasil produces enormous quantities of Life-associated Energy. The [[Natural Energy]] circulating throughout Earth's Ley Lines was identified as Energy originating from Earth's World Tree, with Roxanne noting that Yggdrasil's Energy was substantially denser than her own because of Yggdrasil's age.[9]
- [[Senjutsu]] Generation: The Life/Natural Energy generated by World Trees can be used as Senjutsu. During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s training, Roxanne explicitly identified the green Natural Energy circulating through Earth's Ley Lines as Energy belonging to Earth's World Tree.[9]
- Nature Manipulation: World Trees possess the ability to control non-sentient natural elements. Roxanne explained that this includes plant life, trees, rocks and similar natural structures, describing the power as an ability possessed by every World Tree.[9]
- Plant Manipulation: World Trees possess particularly high affinity with plants and other "green" forms of Life, allowing them to grow and manage vegetation.[9]
- Terrain Manipulation: Their control of non-sentient Nature extends beyond vegetation and includes natural materials such as rocks.[9]
- Positive Emotion Perception: Positive World Trees can perceive Positive emotions and intentions in living beings. Aurora demonstrated this ability and identified it as part of the sensory nature of Positive World Trees. Beings of comparable World Tree status can interfere with this perception because neither Tree can simply read an equal sister through her normal emotional senses.[5]
- Planetary Instinct: Yggdrasil possesses the fundamental instinct shared by Positive World Trees to prioritize the continued survival of her planet over any particular civilization living upon it. Even mass extinction among sentient species does not permanently defeat a Positive World Tree's purpose so long as the planet remains capable of producing Life again.[6]
- World Tree Communication: Yggdrasil can communicate with other World Trees over interplanetary distances. Aurora specifically identified Yggdrasil as one of the sisters with whom she maintained contact.[7]
- Enormous Energy Reserves: Mature World Trees possess enough Energy to sustain entire planets. Their total reserves were described as effectively immeasurable by ordinary supernatural standards, although there remains a practical limit to how much Energy can be released at once.[5]
- Dimensional Support: Yggdrasil's Energy and roots can support supernatural dimensions. When portions of her root system were abandoned, connected dimensional structures began losing the nourishment previously supplied through those roots.[1]
- Interdimensional Root System: Yggdrasil's physical-metaphysical structure can extend into supernatural dimensions rather than remaining confined to Earth's ordinary surface. The abandoned World Tree roots discovered by Victor existed inside a separate dimensional region connected to Norse cosmology.[1]
- Avatar Creation: Yggdrasil can create humanoid Energy representatives through which she can communicate directly without relocating her actual World Tree body. Her first shown representative was composed entirely of pure white Energy.[1]
- Remote Manifestation: Yggdrasil was able to manifest her representative directly beside Victor inside an isolated supernatural dimension while her true body remained connected to Earth.[1]
- System Interaction: Yggdrasil is registered within the greater cosmic System and can perceive and support certain requests made through it. Victor's Chapter 946 request explicitly caused the System to identify Entity <Yggdrasil> as supporting his appeal.[1]
- Regulator Communication: Yggdrasil can submit requests for assistance to the cosmic administrators, although her priority and authority are limited compared with Victor's later System Authority.[1]
- Energy Absorption: Like other World Trees, Yggdrasil can absorb compatible Energy entering her planetary environment. She absorbed part of the enormous wave of Victor's Attributeless Energy that spread throughout the Galaxy, and the Energy affected her deeply.[4]
- Immense Longevity: World Trees are approximately as old as the planets they inhabit. Yggdrasil therefore predates the Gods and mortal civilizations that arose on Earth and has existed across planetary timescales.[2]
- Planetary-Scale Soul: Victor explained that a mature World Tree's Soul is essentially comparable to a planet in scale. This extraordinary spiritual size is one of the fundamental distinctions between World Trees and ordinary supernatural species.[2]

#### Positive World Tree Energy
Yggdrasil produces the Positive/Life-associated side of Earth's World Tree Energy. Her counterpart Qiliphoth represents the Negative side of the same planetary system.[5]
The Positive Tree arises before the Negative counterpart and provides the environmental foundation required for biological Life to develop. Yggdrasil therefore existed first, with Qiliphoth developing later as Earth's sentient inhabitants generated sufficient Negative Energy.[5]

#### Senjutsu
The [[Natural Energy]] circulating through Earth's Ley Lines is directly connected to Yggdrasil. During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s [[Senjutsu]] training, the Energy within the Ley Lines rushed toward him because of his connection with [[Roxanne Elderblood]], who recognized it as the much denser Energy of Earth's ancient World Tree.[9]
Roxanne explained that her own World Tree Energy could have been used to nourish a planet, while Yggdrasil's far greater age made Earth's corresponding Energy substantially denser.[9]

#### Dimensional Infrastructure
Yggdrasil's role extends beyond sustaining Earth's ordinary material ecosystem. Her roots and Energy became integrated with supernatural dimensional structures associated with Earth's Pantheons.
The abandoned dimensional region encountered in Chapter 946 demonstrated the consequences of disrupting this infrastructure. When the roots were no longer properly connected to Yggdrasil, the local Energy system destabilized and the affected dimensional structures could no longer rely upon their previous nourishment.[1]
This does not mean Yggdrasil personally belongs to any single Pantheon. Rather, the Pantheons developed supernatural realms upon a planetary framework whose World Tree existed before them.

#### World Tree Network
Yggdrasil is part of the wider family of [[World Trees]] created by [[The Universal Tree]]. Positive World Trees are capable of communicating with their sisters even when separated by enormous interplanetary distances.[7]
Through this network, Yggdrasil remained in contact with figures such as [[Aurora Seraphina Evergreen]] on Samar, allowing major information concerning supernatural civilizations to circulate independently of ordinary mortal communication systems.[7]

#### Victor's Energy
Yggdrasil absorbed part of the Energy released by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] after his understanding of Attributeless Energy transformed his internal Power.[4]
The Energy caused a major psychological shift. Instead of simply maintaining an ordinary World Tree relationship with her planet, Yggdrasil developed an intense desire for Victor and his Energy. Roxanne described the affected Trees as wanting to nurture Victor directly in the same intimate manner that Roxanne and Amara nurtured his Soul.[4]
Yggdrasil's reaction was stronger than that of many younger Trees because older [[World Trees]] absorbed and responded to the aftermath especially intensely.[4]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
Yggdrasil's relationship with [[Victor Elderblood]] began indirectly.
Victor first learned of her existence after [[Scathach]] compared Roxanne to the ancient World Tree she had encountered within the [[Norse Pantheon]].[3] At the time, Victor understood very little about [[World Trees]] and had no personal relationship with Yggdrasil.
Their first direct meeting occurred during Chapter 946. Victor encountered abandoned portions of Earth's World Tree roots while investigating a dimensional anomaly and used the System to call for outside intervention. Yggdrasil supported his request and subsequently manifested beside him with Qiliphoth.[1]
Their initial relationship was relatively neutral. Yggdrasil wanted the anomaly resolved, while Victor was primarily interested in reporting the problem and returning to his vacation. She nevertheless cooperated with him and tolerated his unusually casual behavior.[1]
Their relationship changed after Yggdrasil absorbed Victor's Energy. The Energy caused her to become intensely attracted to him and increasingly desirous of treating him as a new planetary focus for her nurturing instincts.[4]
Victor later openly acknowledged that Yggdrasil and her counterpart loved him, treating the situation with his usual confidence despite how abnormal romantic attraction is among World Trees.[2]

#### Qiliphoth
Qiliphoth is Yggdrasil's sister, Negative counterpart and the other half of Earth's mature World Tree system.[5]
Yggdrasil is the older and more socially capable of the pair. During their first meeting with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Qiliphoth immediately hid behind Yggdrasil, forcing Yggdrasil to explain her sister's extreme shyness.[1]
The sisters cooperate where Earth is concerned and were affected together by Victor's Energy during the Imperial Era.[4]

#### The Universal Tree
[[The Universal Tree]] is Yggdrasil's creator, father and Progenitor.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explained that when The Universal Tree became responsible for maintaining Life throughout the Universe, he produced the seeds that became his daughters. Each World Tree was created to support a Life-bearing planet and participate in the larger cosmic system maintaining Life.[2]
Yggdrasil is therefore one of the ancient planetary daughters through which The Universal Tree's broader function is carried out.

#### Roxanne Elderblood
[[Roxanne Elderblood]] is one of Yggdrasil's younger World Tree sisters.
Yggdrasil was the first mature World Tree used as a comparison when [[Scathach]] recognized Roxanne's nature.[3]
During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s [[Senjutsu]] training, Roxanne identified the [[Natural Energy]] circulating through Earth's Ley Lines as Yggdrasil's Energy and noted that it was substantially denser than her own because Yggdrasil was much older.[9]
Roxanne later became intensely jealous when Yggdrasil and numerous other [[World Trees]] developed obsessive feelings toward Victor following his Attributeless Energy breakthrough.[4]

#### Aurora Seraphina Evergreen
[[Aurora Seraphina Evergreen]] is the Positive World Tree of Samar and one of Yggdrasil's younger sisters.
Aurora explicitly identified Yggdrasil as one of the [[World Trees]] with whom she regularly communicated. Their connection allowed Aurora to remain informed about important developments occurring far beyond Samar.[7]
Both Trees were later strongly affected by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s released Energy, with Roxanne's report showing Aurora displaying a condition similar to Yggdrasil's obsessive reaction.[4]

#### Norse Pantheon
Yggdrasil has historically been associated with the [[Norse Pantheon]], and [[Scathach]] personally encountered her during a visit to that faction.[3]
The association became deeply embedded within Norse supernatural history because portions of Yggdrasil's root structure supported dimensions connected to the Pantheon.[1]
Despite this, Yggdrasil is not fundamentally subordinate to [[Asgard]]. Her first responsibility is Earth, and the normal instincts of a World Tree would cause her to prioritize planetary survival over the survival of any individual Pantheon.[6]

### Trivia
- Yggdrasil was the first named mature World Tree introduced in the story, although she did not physically appear until Chapter 946.[3][1]
- [[Scathach]] had previously met Yggdrasil during a visit to the [[Norse Pantheon]] and referred to her simply as a "bitch" when explaining [[World Trees]] to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family.[3]
- The original belief that Yggdrasil was a single neutral World Tree containing both Positive and Negative Energy was incomplete. Later chapters establish Yggdrasil as Earth's Positive World Tree and Qiliphoth as her separate Negative counterpart.[5]
- Yggdrasil is older than most of Earth's Gods and mortal civilizations because World Trees are approximately as old as their planets.[2]
- Her Energy is substantially denser than Roxanne's World Tree Energy due to the enormous difference in their ages.[9]
- Yggdrasil maintains communication with Positive World Trees on other planets, including [[Aurora Seraphina Evergreen]] on Samar.[7]
- Her humanoid appearance in Chapter 946 is only a representative avatar and not her true body.[1]
- Yggdrasil lacks Victor's unusual System Authority. Although she could request intervention from the Regulators, her own request was subjected to the normal cosmic administrative process.[1]
- Despite her famous association with the Norse Pantheon, Yggdrasil's true allegiance is to the planet she sustains rather than to any specific group of Gods.[6]
- When the [[Elderblood Empire]] was founded, Victor renamed the planet Earth Yggdrasil, causing the planet and its Positive World Tree to share the same name.[8]
- Yggdrasil and Qiliphoth were among the older World Trees most strongly affected by the massive release of Victor's Energy in Chapter 1111.[4]
- Victor later casually stated that the planet "loves" him while discussing Yggdrasil and Qiliphoth with his childhood friends.[2]
- World Trees are normally effectively uninterested in reproduction or ordinary romance. Their primary function is planetary nourishment, their lives span billions of years, and their Souls exist on planetary scales.[2]
- Yggdrasil's current attraction toward Victor is therefore an exceptional deviation from the normal behavior of her species.[4][2]
