**[[Freyja]]** is the Norse goddess of love, beauty, war and seiðr magic, one of the Vanir. She is ranked 52nd on the [[UOE Unified Alltime Rankings]].

## IRL (Norse myth)

In the Eddas she is the daughter of Njörðr and twin of Freyr. She receives half of the slain in her hall Fólkvangr, while [[Odin]] takes the other half to Valhalla. She owns the necklace Brísingamen and a cloak of falcon feathers, and rides a chariot drawn by two cats.

## UOE (Far Future)

At the first [[Panpantheonic Summit]] during [[The Great Expansion]], [[Ares]] challenged [[Thor]] to a duel. [[Athena]] and Freyja prevented the war.

See [[Norse Pantheon]].

Freya, also known as Freyja and Vanadís, is a Third Generation Goddess of the Vanir, one of the major deities formerly belonging to the [[Norse Pantheon]], and the Queen of the Valkyries. Her principal confirmed Major Divinities are Love, Lust, War, and [[Magic]], making her an unusual combination of a love deity and an accomplished combat-oriented Goddess.[3]
Freya is frequently compared to [[Aphrodite]], who described her as the closest Norse counterpart to herself. The comparison is not exact: while Aphrodite's authority is more heavily concentrated around Beauty, Love and Sexuality, Freya's Divine portfolio combines similar emotional Concepts with the considerably more combat-oriented Divinities of War and Magic. Aphrodite herself acknowledged that these two Major Divinities made Freya troublesome despite considering herself the stronger Goddess overall.[3]
Freya is additionally one of the most accomplished Runic practitioners among the Norse Gods. Her knowledge of Runes was described as second only to [[Odin]] within the Pantheon.[3] Her association with Death is more uncertain. Freya possesses unusual abilities involving the perception and apparent interaction with Souls and maintains Fólkvangr, a Celestial Realm that receives selected worthy dead, but the claim that she possesses an actual Death Divinity is explicitly presented as an unconfirmed rumor rather than established fact.[3][5]
During the final Ragnarök, Freya initially fought on Odin's side alongside her Valkyries. However, she had already warned Odin that she would abandon him if he activated his final indiscriminate "Screw It" plan. When Odin ultimately attempted to sacrifice everyone on the battlefield through that measure, Freya immediately ordered every Valkyrie to retreat rather than allow her subordinates to die for a battle that had already become hopeless.[4] Her forces subsequently disappeared from the Norse sphere during the covert evacuation carried out by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction, and in the later political order Freya and her Valkyries are counted among the forces affiliated with Dragons Nest.[6]

### Physical Appearance
Freya is an exceptionally tall woman, standing approximately 189 centimeters, with long light-brown hair and striking golden eyes. Her body is curvaceous but distinctly athletic, possessing the muscular development expected from a Goddess whose authority includes War.[7]
She has broad and well-defined arms, thick muscular thighs, prominent abdominal definition, and a clearly visible six-pack. Her figure remains feminine despite her powerful musculature, with the narration specifically emphasizing the combination of an attractive body and the physique of an experienced warrior.[7]
During her physical debut, Freya wore a short shirt-like top that exposed her abdomen alongside black pants. Her overall appearance gave [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] the impression of someone who would fit naturally among the warrior women of [[Adrasteia Clan]], particularly because she possessed the same mixture of physical beauty, athletic development and unmistakable martial presence.[7]
Freya's golden eyes can also glow when she employs her Divine senses to examine supernatural phenomena.[7][8]

### Personality
Freya is proud, fiery-tempered, competitive, mischievous, pragmatic and considerably more informal than her public position as one of [[Asgard]]'s most respected Goddesses might suggest.
Her behavior inside [[Aphrodite]]'s private Gods' Chat Group reveals a highly playful and aggressive side. When Aphrodite initially gave her the username "Bitch 01", Freya immediately demanded that it be changed and threatened [[Loki]] with castration when he mocked her. At the same time, she gleefully exposed Susanoo's private interest in adopting [[Inari-Omikami]], demonstrating that she enjoys gossip, teasing her friends and publicly embarrassing them whenever an opportunity appears.[1]
Despite their frequent insults and competition, Freya and Aphrodite are genuine longtime friends. Their relationship mixes rivalry with familiarity: Aphrodite frequently compares herself against Freya and takes obvious pride in possessing superior Beauty and Charm, while Freya becomes irritated whenever Aphrodite receives too much attention.[3][2] The two nevertheless communicate casually enough to insult, prank and reveal embarrassing information about one another without destroying the friendship.
Freya's pride is substantial, but she is not recklessly incapable of recognizing reality. This is particularly evident in her conduct during Ragnarök. Although she initially supported [[Odin]] and personally brought the Valkyries into the war, she had established a clear boundary beforehand: if Odin resorted to his final indiscriminate plan, she would leave. When that moment arrived, Freya honored her warning immediately and refused to sacrifice her people for Odin's desperation.[4]
Her response to Brunhilde during the retreat is especially revealing. When Brunhilde initially hesitated, Freya did not appeal to honor, prophecy or obedience to Odin. She ordered her to listen to her Queen and asked whether she truly intended to die in a futile battle. Protecting the surviving Valkyries took priority over remaining loyal to a King who had decided to sacrifice everyone around him.[4]
Freya is therefore much more protective of her subordinates than Odin was of his own forces. Her Valkyries are not disposable troops to her, and she is willing to break from Asgard's ruler rather than allow them to be thrown away meaninglessly.
She can also be pragmatic about personal grudges. When Loki's conflict with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] created a dangerous situation, Freya considered simply apologizing to Victor a more rational solution than allowing pride to prolong the hostility. Loki's refusal frustrated her, demonstrating that Freya is capable of placing practicality above Divine ego when the situation genuinely demands it.[8]
Like many ancient Gods, Freya can nevertheless be arrogant toward those she considers beneath her. During her early interactions with Victor, both she and Loki attempted to inspect supernatural aspects of him without first asking permission. Victor immediately retaliated through the strange red connections he could perceive, causing both Gods severe pain and bleeding from their eyes.[8] The event forced Freya to confront the fact that Victor could not be treated casually simply because he was younger.
Freya also possesses a pronounced appreciation for beauty and strength. Upon first seeing an image of Victor, she reacted with disbelief that a supposedly mortal-born Vampire could be more beautiful than many Gods and became curious enough to consider personally visiting the Human World.[9] When she later met him physically, she was visibly affected by his appearance but gained respect for him because his gaze focused upon her with admiration rather than the uncontrolled lust she was accustomed to receiving.[7]

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Freya was first discussed when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] questioned [[Aphrodite]] about the Norse Goddess who occupied a similar mythological position to her.
Aphrodite immediately became annoyed by Victor's interest but eventually explained that Freya was considerably more combat-oriented than herself. While both possessed Major Divinities connected to Love and Lust, Freya additionally possessed Major Divinities of War and [[Magic]]. Aphrodite considered herself stronger because of her status as an older Second Generation Goddess and Titan, but admitted that Freya's War and Magic made her dangerous.[3]
Aphrodite also explained that Freya possessed Lesser Divinities associated with Beauty and Pleasure. A possible Death Divinity was discussed as well, but Aphrodite emphasized that this final claim was only a rumor. Freya was known to possess unusual Soul-related abilities, leading other Gods to speculate that she had some connection to Death, but Freya herself had never confirmed the Divinity.[3]
The same conversation revealed another major aspect of Freya's supernatural expertise: she was an accomplished Rune Master whose knowledge was considered second only to [[Odin]]'s.[3]
Freya properly entered the narrative through Aphrodite's private Gods' Chat Group.
The group contained only a handful of Gods but every member possessed considerable influence within their respective Pantheons. Freya's initial username was the deliberately insulting "Bitch 01", which Aphrodite had assigned to her. Freya immediately demanded a change, leading Aphrodite to rename her "Goddess of War".[1]
The resulting conversation established Freya's close familiarity with [[Loki]], Susanoo and Aphrodite. She threatened Loki when he teased her, exposed Susanoo's private affairs for amusement, and freely participated in the group's constant insults and gossip.[1]
When Victor was later added to the same group, Freya reacted strongly to his appearance. After seeing his image, she questioned how someone born mortal could possess beauty exceeding many Divine beings and demanded an explanation from Aphrodite.[9]
Susanoo explained Victor's identity as Alucard, the newly established Fifth Vampire Count and one of [[Nightingale]]'s new pillars. Freya became even more curious after learning that Victor had rejected Aphrodite's romantic advances, finding the idea of a man refusing the Goddess of Beauty amusing enough that she considered visiting the Human World to meet him personally.[9]
The chat also demonstrated the unusual nature of Victor's Soul. When Victor communicated directly with Kali at the Soul level, Freya, Loki and Susanoo were surprised because they themselves had been unable to accomplish the same thing despite their Divine status.[9]

#### Demonic War
Freya physically appeared alongside [[Loki]] during the period surrounding the Demonic War.[7]
Her first personal meeting with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] immediately reinforced the contrast between Freya and [[Aphrodite]]. Freya possessed undeniable beauty, but her athletic physique and warrior-like presence made her appear more like a battle Goddess than a conventional deity of Love.[7]
Freya was visibly affected by Victor's appearance, but she also noticed the unusual way he observed her. Rather than looking at her with the uncontrolled sexual desire that powerful love-related Goddesses often provoked, Victor examined her physical condition with open admiration for her training and musculature. This raised Freya's opinion of him.[7]
The meeting also exposed Freya and Loki's Divine arrogance.
Both Gods used their supernatural senses to inspect Victor and the strange Soul-level relationships surrounding him. Victor perceived their interference through the red threads he had learned to see and retaliated against those connections. The backlash left Freya and Loki with bleeding eyes and intense pain despite both being Gods substantially older than him.[8]
The incident demonstrated that Freya possesses highly developed Divine perception capable of examining Souls and spiritual connections, but also that Victor's anomalous existence could directly punish beings attempting to probe him carelessly.
Freya and Loki were also deeply concerned with the situation surrounding Hela and Helheim. Their knowledge of Norse cosmology made them aware that entering Hela's domain and dealing with the forces inside it required unusual preparation. Freya lamented that her Valkyries were already occupied and unavailable for the immediate situation.[8]
During her discussions with Loki, Freya displayed greater willingness to resolve problems diplomatically. She suggested apologizing to Victor rather than allowing Loki's pride and past behavior to create unnecessary hostility, although Loki initially refused.[8]

#### King among Kings
Freya remained active in [[Aphrodite]]'s Gods' Chat Group after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s transformation and rapidly increasing importance within the supernatural world.
By Chapter 725, news of Victor's actions had become so widespread that even the Gods who ordinarily ignored mortal affairs were following his movements. Freya became irritated at the amount of attention Aphrodite was receiving and was immediately accused by Kali, Susanoo and [[Loki]] of being jealous.[2]
Aphrodite continued abusing her administrator privileges for entertainment and temporarily changed Freya's username from "Goddess of War" to "Whore", later restoring it to Freya's actual name.[2]
The conversation also showed that Victor's rapid rise had changed the way the group regarded him. Freya and Loki, who had originally treated him as an interesting younger supernatural, were far more cautious after he grew powerful enough to stand well above the position he held when first entering their chat group.[2]
Later, during the hunt against hostile Gods and surviving enemies, Victor demonstrated Aphrodite's supernatural Charm against the assembled Divine warriors. Even figures such as [[Thor]], Loki, Susanoo and the Angels were affected.[10]
After recovering, Loki immediately declared that he would ask Freya to train him to resist Charm, with Thor agreeing to the same plan.[10]
This establishes Freya as sufficiently knowledgeable in Love/Lust-related Divine powers and their counters that other Major Gods regard her as an appropriate instructor for developing resistance against supernatural attraction.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
The deeper structure of Freya's authority within the [[Norse Pantheon]] became clearer during the events preceding Ragnarök.
Souls judged worthy within the Norse afterlife system could ascend either to Valhalla or to Fólkvangr, explicitly identified as Freya's personal Celestial Domain.[5]
This connection to the dead helps explain the long-standing rumors surrounding a possible Death Divinity, although possession of Fólkvangr alone does not confirm that rumor.
As Ragnarök approached, [[Odin]] gathered the remaining major resources of [[Asgard]], including [[Thor]], Freya, [[Loki]] and [[Heimdall]], while strengthening his forces in preparation for Hela's assault.[11]
Freya initially remained on Odin's side and deployed alongside her Valkyries.
However, her loyalty had limits.
Before the final battle, Freya explicitly warned Odin that if he activated his ultimate "Screw It" option, she would abandon him. Odin accepted this condition because using the plan would mean that every realistic hope of victory had already disappeared.[4]
During the decisive battle, Odin finally activated the measure.
A gigantic golden sphere appeared above the battlefield, revealing that Odin intended to annihilate the area regardless of how many allies remained inside it.[4]
Freya immediately understood what he was doing.
Furious that Odin was willing to sacrifice everyone present, she abandoned his side and ordered:
the Valkyries to retreat.[4]
Brunhilde, acting as one of the principal Valkyries in the battle, initially attempted to object. Freya overruled her immediately, reminding Brunhilde that she was their Queen and refusing to permit the Valkyries to die in a battle that had already become futile.[4]
The entire Valkyrie force withdrew.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction secretly ensured the retreat succeeded. Nyx and Victor's Heralds interfered with the battlefield so thoroughly that attacks which should have struck the fleeing Valkyries repeatedly missed them.[4]
Freya's withdrawal marked the end of her practical allegiance to Odin and the old Norse Pantheon.
After Ragnarök, Hela discovered that Freya, the Valkyries and numerous valuable non-Divine races of Asgard had completely vanished while Hela was occupied consolidating her victory. She correctly concluded that Victor's faction had extracted them during the chaos.[6]
Freya and her Valkyries subsequently became affiliated with Dragons Nest, surviving the collapse of Odin's Asgard rather than being absorbed into Hela's new regime.

### Skills & Abilities
Third Generation God Physiology: Freya is a Third Generation Major Goddess and one of the most important Divine beings formerly belonging to the [[Norse Pantheon]]. Her Divine existence grants her immense supernatural physical abilities, [[Divine Energy]], longevity, spiritual perception and resistance far beyond mortal supernatural species.

#### Divinities
Freya possesses multiple confirmed Major and Lesser Divinities.[3]

##### Major Divinities
- Love: Love is one of Freya's principal Divinities, giving her Divine Authority over the emotional Concept of Love.[3]
- Lust: Freya possesses a Major Divinity associated with Lust/Sexuality, making her one of the [[Norse Pantheon]]'s principal love and sexuality deities.[3]
- War: Freya possesses a Major War Divinity, distinguishing her sharply from [[Aphrodite]] and making her significantly more combat-oriented than most conventional Goddesses of Love.[3]
- [[Magic]]: Magic is one of Freya's principal Divinities and one of the reasons Aphrodite considers her troublesome in direct conflict.[3]

##### Lesser Divinities
- Beauty: Freya possesses a lesser Divinity connected to Beauty. [[Aphrodite]] specifically notes that Freya's authority over Beauty is inferior to her own Greater/Major focus on the Concept.[3]
- Pleasure: Freya possesses a lesser Divinity over Pleasure.[3]
- Death (Rumored): Freya has long been rumored to possess a lesser Death Divinity because of her unusual ability to perceive and possibly interact directly with Souls. However, the rumor has never been confirmed by Freya herself and should not be treated as an established Divine Domain.[3]

#### Magic
Divine [[Magic]]: As a Goddess whose Major Divinities include Magic, Freya possesses extremely high affinity with Mana, magical phenomena and supernatural spellcraft.[3]
The complete range of her personal spells has not yet been demonstrated in detail, so generic magical abilities should not be attributed to her without individual evidence.

#### Rune Mastery
Master Rune User: Freya is one of the greatest practitioners of Norse Runes in the entire Pantheon.
Her knowledge of Runes is explicitly described as second only to [[Odin]].[3]
This places her above the vast majority of Norse Gods in Runic knowledge and makes Rune [[Magic]] one of the clearest practical expressions of her Magic Divinity.

#### Charm and Attraction
As a Goddess possessing Major Divinities of Love and Lust alongside a lesser Beauty Divinity, Freya possesses Divine authority connected to supernatural attraction.
- Charm: Freya is capable of influencing attraction through her Love/Lust-related authority. Although her Charm is inferior to [[Aphrodite]]'s specialized power, her expertise is sufficient that [[Loki]] and [[Thor]] intended to receive training from her specifically to improve their resistance against Aphrodite-style supernatural Charm.[10]
- Charm Resistance Training: Freya possesses enough understanding of Love, Lust and attraction-based powers to train other Gods in resisting supernatural Charm.[10]
The exact degree of Freya's personal immunity to Aphrodite has never been explicitly established. Earlier claims that Aphrodite's Charm simply "does not work" on Freya should therefore be treated cautiously rather than as absolute immunity.

#### Soul Perception
Freya possesses highly developed perception of Souls.
The ability to merely see a Soul is not especially rare among trained Gods, but Freya's reputation goes beyond ordinary Soul sight. According to [[Aphrodite]], rumors claim that Freya can directly interact with Souls in a manner normally associated with Death Gods.[3]
The interaction aspect remains unconfirmed, but Freya has directly demonstrated the ability to use Divine senses to inspect Soul-level supernatural phenomena surrounding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[7][8]

#### Fólkvangr
Celestial Domain: Fólkvangr is Freya's personal Celestial Realm within the Norse afterlife structure.[5]
Souls deemed worthy can be sent to Fólkvangr rather than Valhalla, demonstrating Freya's direct institutional connection to the handling of honored dead within the [[Norse Pantheon]].[5]
This connection is one of several reasons other Gods speculated that Freya might possess some form of Death authority, although the novel has not confirmed that conclusion.

#### Divine Perception
Freya can enhance her golden eyes with Divine Power to inspect supernatural phenomena invisible to ordinary beings.[7]
She and [[Loki]] used these senses to examine [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s unusual Soul-related state. Victor perceived the intrusion and retaliated through the strange red connections linked to them, causing both Gods' eyes to bleed.[8]

#### Warrior Goddess
Freya's War Divinity is not merely ceremonial. Her physique, position among the Norse Gods and command of the Valkyries all identify her as an active warrior deity.
However, the novel has not yet shown enough direct combat from Freya to responsibly assign specific weapon-master feats or named martial techniques.

#### Valkyrie Authority
Freya is the Queen and commanding Goddess of the Valkyries.
During Ragnarök, a single order from Freya caused the entire surviving Valkyrie force to disengage from [[Odin]]'s war and retreat.[4]
Even when Brunhilde hesitated, Freya's authority as Queen superseded the Valkyries' obligations to Odin.[4]

### Relationships

#### Aphrodite Elderblood
[[Aphrodite]] Elderblood is one of Freya's oldest friends, rivals and closest Divine counterparts.
The two possess overlapping Divinities related to Love, Lust, Beauty and Pleasure, naturally producing constant comparison between them.[3]
Aphrodite considers herself superior because she is an older Second Generation Goddess/Titan with greater authority over Beauty and Charm, but openly acknowledges that Freya's War and [[Magic]] Divinities make her far more combat-oriented.[3]
Their friendship is characterized by relentless insults and pranks. Aphrodite assigned Freya humiliating usernames such as "Bitch 01" and later "Whore", while Freya responds with threats, insults and competitive jealousy.[1][2]
Despite their rivalry, nothing suggests genuine hostility. Their familiarity is instead characteristic of old friends who have known one another long enough to ignore ordinary Divine etiquette.

#### Loki
[[Loki]] is one of Freya's close friends within the [[Norse Pantheon]].
The two communicate extremely casually, and Freya regularly threatens or insults him when his jokes irritate her.[1]
She also acts as one of the more practical voices around Loki. During their conflict involving [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Hela, Freya suggested that Loki simply apologize rather than allow his pride to create further problems.[8]
Loki trusts Freya's expertise with love-related Divine powers enough that he immediately intended to ask her to train his resistance against supernatural Charm after Victor affected him with [[Aphrodite]]'s blessing.[10]

#### Kali Elderblood
Kali is one of Freya's friends and a member of the same private Gods' Chat Group.
Freya is fully aware of Kali's overwhelming status as one of the strongest female Gods and is considerably more cautious when joking with her than she is with [[Loki]] or Susanoo.[2]

#### Susanoo
Susanoo is another longtime member of Freya's Divine social circle.
Freya frequently mocks him, particularly regarding his interest in [[Inari-Omikami]], and takes obvious amusement in exposing personal information he would prefer to keep private.[1]

#### Odin
[[Odin]] was Freya's former King and leader within the [[Norse Pantheon]].
Although Freya was Vanir rather than Æsir by birth, she functioned as a major Goddess within [[Asgard]] and initially supported Odin during Ragnarök.
Her loyalty was never unconditional.
Freya explicitly warned Odin beforehand that if he activated his final indiscriminate plan, she would abandon him. Odin accepted the condition.[4]
When he eventually attempted to annihilate the battlefield regardless of his own surviving troops, Freya immediately fulfilled her warning and withdrew herself and every Valkyrie under her command.[4]
The event permanently ended her allegiance to Odin.

#### Victor Elderblood
Freya became interested in [[Victor Elderblood]] before personally meeting him.
After seeing his image in the Gods' Chat Group, she was astonished that someone born mortal could possess beauty comparable to or greater than Gods and became curious enough to consider traveling to the Human World specifically to meet him.[9]
Their first physical encounter was considerably less friendly. Freya and [[Loki]] attempted to inspect Victor with their Divine senses, prompting him to retaliate and leave both Gods bleeding from their eyes.[8]
Their relationship nevertheless evolved as Victor's position in the supernatural world rose dramatically. By Chapter 725, Freya no longer regarded him as merely an interesting young Vampire, and his growth had made both her and Loki far more cautious around him.[2]
During Ragnarök, Victor's faction covertly aided Freya's retreat and subsequently removed Freya, the Valkyries and numerous valuable Norse populations before Hela could subjugate them.[4][6]
In the later political order, Freya and her Valkyries are affiliated with Dragons Nest and counted among Victor's forces.

Freya possess all the basic abilities of Gods, like like Superhuman Strength, Superhuman Speed and Reflex, Enhanced Durability, Enhanced Agility, Enhanced Sense, Regeneration, [[Immortality]], Divine Power, Divinity, Blessing and Curse Bestowal,....,etc.
