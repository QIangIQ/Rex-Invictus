**[[Amaterasu]]** is the Shinto sun goddess, head of the [[Japanese Pantheon]] and ancestor of the Japanese imperial line. In the far future she rules the Amaterasu Dominion. This note also covers the other foreign pantheon figures in the story: Manannán mac Lir, the Morrígan, Thor and Huitzilopochtli.

## Greek History (IRL)

**Real-world myth.** In the *Kojiki* (712) and *Nihon Shoki* (720), Amaterasu was born from [[Izanagi]]'s left eye. After her brother [[Susanowo]] wrecked her rice fields and hall, she hid in the Heavenly Rock Cave, plunging the world into darkness, until the gods lured her out with a mirror and a dance by Ame-no-Uzume. The mirror Yata no Kagami is one of the three imperial regalia. Her main shrine is the Ise Grand Shrine.

#### AMATERASU (Shinto sun goddess) — future only
- **Old timeline ([[Ch01 - Rex Imperator I|Ch1]]):** when the Greeks left, "the Japanese had evacuated their pantheon the quickest, with Amaterasu at the bow, with her flagship The Royal Blade of Retribution". She "quickly suppressed the local Divine population" and left low Earth orbit within days, "presumably to head towards the already-colonized Proxima Centauri". Other pantheons later "follow[ed] the same path forged by The Royal Blade."
- **UOE era ([[Ch05 - Intermission I|Ch5]]):** the "Amaterasu Dominion" is now the majority power of the Shinto pantheon. Her envoys ([[Kagura Kuroyami]], called "Perseus Kuroyami" in [[Ch05 - Intermission I|Ch5]], and two others) face [[Zeus]] at the summit. Kuroyami must "report back to Amaterasu" about the weaponised moon, and "Re-colonization efforts of earth have to be put on a pause". Her "superiors" are "too attached to their underlings" to sacrifice priests to [[Athena]].
- **[[Ch18 - Rex Invictus XI|Ch18]]:** a Shinto orbital artillery belt is "a sanctum of Amaterasu", defended by a fox-masked Miko: "Blasphemers… You trespass upon a sanctum of Amaterasu." Shinto ships carry torii pylons and glowing wards.
- **[[Ch23 - Interlude I|Ch23]]:**
  - The Dominion "pays in favors" (Talviento). Kagura Kuroyami reactivated the [[Yamamoto Chokepoint Relay]] on "December's Eve of 16215" to ally with Voss during the [[War of Princes]]. It is the only public route between the UOE and the Dominion.
  - **Author's vault note (not yet in text):** Kagura Kuroyami is the Dominion's chief engineer and, during the War of Princes, Amaterasu's apostle (also "emissary of the [[Shinto Pantheon]] and formal ward of Amaterasu"). [[Lysandra Voss]], Supreme Elect Admiral of the Princes, allied with the Dominion ([[Annex 15632]]); the [[Rosenthal]] company extended the olive branch for that alliance. After the Treaty, the [[Voss Sovereignty]] is autonomous, restricted and at peace with the Empire (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C5).
  - [[Minerva (AI)|Minerva]] has safehouses among the Egyptians "courtesy of the royal family" and plans to "smuggle into Shinto waters".
  - [[Naomi]] of the future Hunt is a granddaughter of **[[Raijin]]**, the Shinto thunder god ([[Ch23 - Interlude I|Ch23]]).
- **Personality (inferred):** decisive, ruthless (she "suppressed the local Divine population"), protective of her people. She never speaks on-page.
- **Author's vault note (not yet in text):**
  - The future Japanese pantheon "mainly falls under the banner of the Amaterasu Dominion", and the UOE is "in shaky alliance" with it.
  - UOE Unified Ranks: Amaterasu #21, [[Susanowo]] #534, Raijin #691 (Freyja #52).

Fleet patterns: Amaterasu's Radiance, Hachiman's Vengeance, Kagura.

- **Appearance:** "hood drawn low, shirtless, and threadbare shorts"; "a face half-eroded by salt"; smells of "wet ash and brine"; "silver eyes glimmered with a salt-light"; "his form flickering like a reflection on restless water". - **Voice:** "all shorelines collapsing", "like waves breaking over rocks". Archaic ("Fare thee well").

  - "Guardianship is rarely visible, Perseus. You will learn that soon enough. Fare thee well." ([[Ch17 - Rex Invictus X|Ch17]])

Amaterasu Elderblood, formerly known primarily as Amaterasu-Omikami, is the God-Queen of the [[Shinto Pantheon]], leader of the Amatsukami, a Second Generation Divine Being descended from [[Izanagi]] and [[Izanami-no-Mikoto]], and one of the wives of Victor Elderblood. Her principal Divine authority is the Sun, alongside secondary authorities associated with Light, Day and the Sky. She historically ruled [[Takamagahara]] as one of Japan's highest Divine authorities before becoming increasingly integrated into Victor's family and later the [[Elderblood Empire]].
During the Demonic War, Amaterasu's faction emerged victorious from the Shinto civil conflict involving Izanami, but the victory left the Pantheon severely weakened. Rather than allow Divine pride to endanger Japan, she personally negotiated with [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]] and the unified Yokai, eventually securing their assistance in protecting the country while Shinto recovered.[1][2][3]
Her later attempt to permanently bind the Shinto Pantheon to Victor's rapidly expanding faction resulted in considerably more than a political alliance. After Amaterasu accepted Victor's conditions for marriage, his Yandere Divinity linked their Souls through a genuine [[Soul Marriage]]. She later underwent a Blood Dragon Race Change, transforming her Divine physiology into that of a Dragon Goddess, developing a true [[Dragon Heart]] and experiencing a qualitative increase in her Divinities, particularly her authority over the Sun.[4][5]

### Physical Appearance
Amaterasu is an exceptionally beautiful woman with long black hair, pale skin and golden eyes described as possessing the color of the Sun. As the God-Queen of [[Takamagahara]], she traditionally wore ornate Japanese royal clothing reminiscent of a feudal Queen and frequently carried a traditional hand fan.
Amaterasu historically concealed her face from most people. Her appearance during the second [[Supernatural Gathering]] attracted attention because openly showing herself was extraordinarily rare; even many Gods belonging to her own Pantheon had never properly seen her face.[6]
Following her Blood Dragon transformation, Amaterasu developed distinctly Draconic characteristics. Her transformed Divine body possessed black horns and, more importantly, a genuine [[Dragon Heart]], marking the fundamental change in her physiology rather than a merely cosmetic transformation.[5]

### Personality
Amaterasu is calm, calculating, pragmatic and politically intelligent. Unlike many ancient Gods who allow status and pride to dictate their decisions, she is capable of admitting when her Pantheon lacks the resources necessary to solve a problem alone. During the Demonic War, when Shinto's Gods were heavily injured and the systems surrounding their Hell were failing, she did not attempt to intimidate the Yokai into obedience; instead, she personally negotiated with Haruna and offered resources in exchange for cooperation.[2][3]
She is also exceptionally bold and willing to use unconventional solutions. During those negotiations Amaterasu repeatedly proposed marriage as a method of binding powerful factions together, first involving [[Yomi-Hime]] and eventually suggesting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] himself. When Haruna rejected the earlier possibilities, Amaterasu went so far as to offer herself as Victor's prospective wife.[3]
Amaterasu's original interest in marrying Victor was primarily political. She correctly recognized that Victor was becoming one of the most important powers in the supernatural world and that permanently connecting Shinto to him would provide extraordinary security. Her decision was nevertheless not entirely emotionless; she considered Victor an extremely desirable man and, after their marriage, developed genuine personal affection toward him rather than continuing to treat their union merely as an alliance.[3][4][7]
She possesses a pronounced commercial and opportunistic side. After Victor became the Blood Dragon Primogenitor and the supernatural world began fearing Dragons, Amaterasu immediately realized that Shinto's Divine blacksmiths could make enormous profits by manufacturing anti-Dragon weapons. Susanoo pointed out that doing so could cause Dragon's Nest to regard Shinto as an enemy. Amaterasu immediately abandoned the idea when she recognized that the long-term political consequences outweighed the potential profit.[8]
Despite her confidence and status as an absolute Divine monarch, joining Victor's family forced Amaterasu to alter several deeply ingrained habits. After becoming a Dragon Goddess, she caught herself speaking to other members of Victor's family as if they were subordinate Gods of [[Takamagahara]] and deliberately corrected her behavior, gradually accepting that Victor's wives stood beside one another as family rather than beneath her as subjects.[5]

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Amaterasu's Pantheon entered the later stages of the Demonic War in a severely damaged condition. Her faction had emerged victorious from the internal Shinto conflict involving [[Izanami-no-Mikoto]], creating another obstacle for [[Diablo]]'s plans, but victory had not restored Shinto's strength. Numerous Gods were critically injured, [[Tsukuyomi]] had suffered damage severe enough that his recovery was expected to require centuries, and the Divine systems responsible for Japanese Hell and the Souls of the dead were destabilizing because Izanami had abandoned responsibilities connected to the afterlife.[1][3]
Recognizing that the Pantheon could not simultaneously recover, administer its Divine responsibilities and properly defend Japan, Amaterasu approached [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]] and the unified Yokai. She proposed effectively hiring Haruna's forces to help protect the country in exchange for equipment, resources and support from [[Takamagahara]].[2][3]
Amaterasu brought [[Yomi-Hime]] into the negotiations and offered access to the Tengu reconnaissance network, whose information-gathering abilities made them particularly useful as scouts. She also revealed sensitive internal information, including Tsukuyomi's condition and the collapse of his marriage with Yomi. Seeking additional methods of binding the two factions together, Amaterasu repeatedly proposed marriage arrangements involving Yomi and Haruna's subordinates before eventually suggesting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] as a candidate. When Haruna rejected these ideas, Amaterasu escalated the proposal by offering herself as Victor's possible bride.[3]
The principal alliance was ultimately established. Haruna gained access to Shinto equipment and the cooperation of the Tengu, while Amaterasu obtained additional manpower capable of helping defend Japan while her Pantheon recovered. Amaterasu also gave Haruna a Sun-marked object through which the two could communicate.[3]

#### King Among Kings
Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s conquest of [[Olympus]] and transformation into the Blood Dragon Primogenitor, fear of Dragons spread rapidly through the supernatural world. Amaterasu immediately recognized the economic opportunity created by that fear. Although Shinto's Divine smiths could not be compared directly with someone such as [[Hephaestus]], they remained among the world's capable supernatural craftsmen, and Amaterasu considered producing anti-Dragon weapons for factions desperate to defend themselves.[8]
Susanoo quickly pointed out the strategic problem. Selling anti-Dragon weaponry on a large scale would risk making Shinto appear openly hostile toward Victor and Dragon's Nest, precisely the faction Amaterasu wanted to establish a permanent alliance with. Once the consequences were made clear, Amaterasu abandoned the idea rather than sacrificing a far more valuable relationship for immediate profit.[8]
Amaterasu later attended the second [[Supernatural Gathering]] together with Susanoo and [[Tsukuyomi]]. Her exposed face attracted considerable notice because the God-Queen had historically kept herself concealed even from many members of her own Pantheon.[6]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Three days after the private supernatural meeting, Amaterasu traveled directly to Dragon's Nest to negotiate with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[9] She formally offered herself in marriage as a means of creating a permanent alliance between Shinto and Victor's faction. Victor was not opposed to the idea, but immediately asked what Shinto could provide beyond Amaterasu herself. His response mildly offended her pride because, as an ancient God-Queen, Amaterasu had initially expected her own position and value to be sufficient.[4]
Amaterasu consequently offered Victor an Essence associated with the Creation powers of [[Izanagi]] and [[Izanami-no-Mikoto]]. She understood that Victor possessed extraordinary abilities but was not, at that point, fundamentally a conventional God of Creation capable of simply producing anything from nothing, making the Essence potentially valuable for his development.[4]
Victor accepted the possibility of marriage but made his conditions unmistakable. Marriage to him would not be a temporary political contract that could later be discarded once Shinto no longer required his protection. If Amaterasu became his wife, Victor would consider her his permanently and would pursue her regardless of how far she attempted to flee, even across distant galaxies or Dimensions. Amaterasu was momentarily taken aback by the intensity of his obsession but nevertheless accepted.[4]
Immediately afterward, Victor's Yandere Divinity responded to their mutual acceptance and linked their Souls through a genuine [[Soul Marriage]]. Amaterasu was shocked by how easily the connection formed despite the age and Divine nature of her Soul. Victor explained that the phenomenon was connected to his Divinity, although Amaterasu herself did not fully understand why the Yandere authority was capable of creating such a profound spiritual union. Victor then returned the Creation Essence to her for safekeeping rather than immediately taking it, and the relationship began shifting from political negotiation toward an actual marriage.[4]
Amaterasu later underwent a Blood Dragon Race Change. Rather than simply layering Draconic characteristics over her existing Divine body, the transformation fundamentally reconstructed her physiology around a true Dragon foundation and produced a genuine [[Dragon Heart]]. Her existing Divinities were strengthened in the process, with the Sun benefiting substantially more than her secondary authorities.[5]
The result was a qualitative increase in Amaterasu's existence. Her transformed state was superior to both Izanagi and Izanami-no-Mikoto, making her the strongest active Divine existence associated with [[Takamagahara]]. Her Sun Divinity in particular had advanced far enough that Victor recognized a path through which she could continue developing toward an even higher Primordial state centered upon the Sun itself.[5]
Becoming a Dragon Goddess did not cause Amaterasu to abandon Shinto. She continued functioning as its God-Queen while increasingly integrating Takamagahara into Victor's expanding political sphere. Her position inside the Elderblood family simultaneously required her to adjust to an environment where the women surrounding Victor were family and peers rather than lesser Divine subjects.[5]
Following Ragnarök, Amaterasu participated alongside Violet, Haruna, Hela and other members of Victor's family in the political events surrounding the sale and redistribution of the former Norse territories. By this period, the difference between the woman who had originally proposed marriage for strategic reasons and Victor's actual wife had become increasingly obvious. Amaterasu displayed genuine jealousy and openly desired the same affection and attention Victor gave the rest of his family.[7]
Victor later involved Amaterasu, Susanoo and [[Tsukuyomi]] in the future development of his old friend [[Frederick Winter]]. After reconstructing Frederick into a complete Noble Vampire while preserving his Onmyō abilities, Victor intended for the Shinto Gods to participate in his continued training rather than merely allowing Fred to depend permanently upon Victor's protection.[10]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Amaterasu remained one of the principal Divine rulers associated with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s civilization as his territories developed into the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Her position was no longer limited to an isolated [[Japanese Pantheon]]; as one of Victor's Divine wives and the ruler of Shinto, she formed part of the enormous collection of supernatural authorities incorporated into the Empire's religious, political and cultural structure.
Her continued existence within Victor's faction also helped preserve Shinto as a functioning Divine tradition rather than allowing it to disappear beneath the Empire's wider unification. [[Takamagahara]] and its Gods increasingly existed as part of a much larger Elderblood civilization while retaining Amaterasu as their central ruler.

#### Those Who Rule Reality
During the immense accelerated development period of the [[Elderblood Empire]], Amaterasu and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually had a daughter, Hinata-Omikami. Hinata later became one of the highest-ranking Goddesses within her mother's Pantheon, demonstrating that Shinto continued developing within the Elderblood civilization rather than becoming a relic of Earth's earlier supernatural order.[11]
Even after approximately hundreds of thousands of years of scientific, magical and Divine advancement, Amaterasu's expertise regarding the Sun remained exceptional. When Pepper secretly constructed an artificial star for her private experiments, her research notes specifically acknowledged that Amaterasu understood Solar phenomena considerably better than the lesser Sun Goddess Pepper had temporarily recruited to assist the experiment.[12]

### Skills & Abilities
Amaterasu is an ancient God-Queen whose original Divine physiology was later merged with the Blood Dragon race. General abilities inherently possessed by Gods, [[Blood Dragons]] and [[True Dragons]] are covered in their respective articles; the abilities below focus on powers, authorities and developments directly associated with Amaterasu in the novel.
- Blood Dragon God Physiology: After undergoing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood Dragon Race Change, Amaterasu's Divine physiology was fundamentally reconstructed into that of a Dragon Goddess. Her previous Godly existence was not discarded; instead, it was integrated into a new Draconic foundation, giving her a genuine [[Dragon Heart]] and qualitatively strengthening the Divine authorities she already possessed.[5]
- Dragon Heart: Amaterasu possesses a true Dragon Heart created through her Blood Dragon transformation. The Heart represents the foundation of her new Draconic existence and separates her transformed state from an ordinary God merely using Dragon-like powers.[5]
- Draconic Transformation: Her Race Change manifests visible Dragon characteristics, including horns, and transformed her body into a genuine Draconic Divine existence rather than simply altering her appearance.[5]
- Enhanced Divine Physiology: The Race Change substantially increased Amaterasu's overall physical and spiritual quality. As a Blood Dragon Goddess, her previous Godly foundation and new Draconic body function together instead of existing as separate systems.[5]
- Divinity: Amaterasu possesses multiple Divine authorities accumulated around the phenomena for which she is worshipped. Chapter 938 identifies the Sun as her dominant authority while also confirming secondary Divinities associated with Light, Day and the Sky.[5]
- Sun Divinity: Amaterasu's principal and overwhelmingly strongest Divine authority. Her connection to the Sun defines the core of her Godhood and grew far more dramatically than her other authorities after becoming a Blood Dragon Goddess.[5]
- Solar Manipulation: Through her Sun Divinity, Amaterasu can manipulate Solar power as a direct expression of her Divine authority. The transformation in Chapter 938 elevated this Divinity to a qualitatively higher stage rather than merely increasing its raw output.[5]
- Solar Flames: Amaterasu can manifest Divine flames associated with the Sun. During the Demonic War negotiations, her Solar fire was discussed in connection with the [[Shinto Pantheon]]'s Divine craftsmanship and equipment production.[3]
- Flame Transportation: Amaterasu can use her flames as a medium for transportation. She demonstrated this by bringing [[Yomi-Hime]] into the negotiations with Haruna through a manifestation of fire.[3]
- Primordial Solar Potential: Following the Blood Dragon transformation, Amaterasu's Sun Divinity advanced enough for a further path toward becoming an even higher Primordial existence centered specifically upon the Sun to become possible. This represents future potential rather than proof that every theoretical Solar ability is already available to her.[5]
- Solar Knowledge: Amaterasu possesses exceptional technical and Divine knowledge concerning Solar phenomena. Even during the far-future Imperial era, Pepper's research notes acknowledged that Amaterasu possessed much greater understanding of Solar processes than the Sun Goddess Pepper temporarily used while creating an artificial star.[12]
- Light Divinity: Amaterasu possesses a secondary Divine authority associated with Light. Unlike her Sun Divinity, the supplied chapters do not demonstrate a large independent catalogue of Light techniques, so its known existence should be distinguished from unsupported assumptions about every possible form of Photokinesis.[5]
- Day Divinity: Amaterasu possesses Divine authority associated with Day. The novel confirms the Divinity itself, but does not presently demonstrate claims such as indefinitely stopping night, removing day from the world or creating eternal daylight.[5]
- Sky Divinity: Amaterasu possesses a secondary authority associated with the Sky. Its existence is confirmed, although the supplied chapters do not demonstrate unrestricted weather or atmospheric manipulation.[5]
- Enhanced Divinities: All of Amaterasu's existing Divine authorities improved following her Blood Dragon Race Change. The increase was not uniform: her Sun Divinity advanced considerably further than the others because of its much greater importance to her identity and existence.[5]
- [[Soul Marriage]]: Amaterasu's Soul is permanently linked to Victor Elderblood through the marriage produced by his Yandere Divinity. The connection formed immediately after she genuinely accepted Victor's terms, surprising Amaterasu because she did not expect her ancient Divine Soul to be linked so easily.[4]
- Soul-Level Bond: Their relationship exists at the level of the Soul rather than merely as a legal, political or social marriage. The connection therefore transformed Amaterasu's proposed political alliance into a genuine supernatural union.[4]
- [[Divine Energy]]: As a high-ranking God and later Dragon Goddess, Amaterasu possesses the Divine Energy necessary to sustain and express her authorities. Her post-Race Change body substantially improved the quality of the foundation through which those Divinities operate.[5]
- Divine Crafting Support: Amaterasu's Solar flames can be supplied to Shinto's Divine blacksmithing operations as a supernatural resource, demonstrating that her Divine power is useful not only in direct combat but also in artifact production.[3]
- Ancient Divine Knowledge: Having ruled the Shinto Pantheon for an immense period, Amaterasu possesses extensive knowledge of Divine politics, supernatural civilizations and the administration of a Pantheon. Her ability to identify the Shinto Pantheon's weaknesses during the Demonic War and immediately construct an agreement with Haruna demonstrates practical strategic experience rather than purely ceremonial authority.[3]
- Leadership and Diplomacy: Amaterasu is an experienced monarch capable of negotiating military assistance, resource exchanges and marriage alliances while protecting the long-term interests of her Pantheon. Her agreement with Haruna secured both external defenders and continued access to Shinto resources without requiring either faction to submit entirely to the other.[3]
- Strategic Intelligence: Amaterasu is capable of rapidly recognizing both opportunities and dangers. Her consideration of the anti-Dragon weapons market showed strong commercial instincts, while her immediate abandonment of the idea after Susanoo explained its political consequences demonstrated that she can subordinate profit and pride to long-term strategy.[8]

### Power
Amaterasu was already one of the most important Divine rulers of Japan before joining [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. As God-Queen of the [[Shinto Pantheon]], she possessed enough personal and political strength to remain at the center of the Pantheon's internal conflict and to lead the victorious faction opposing [[Izanami-no-Mikoto]]. The existing sources establish the victory of Amaterasu's side but do not conclusively show that she personally defeated Izanami alone in an isolated one-on-one confrontation.[1]
Prior to her Race Change, Amaterasu belonged to the highest class of active Shinto Gods and was treated as a God-King-level existence. Older descriptions associated her with the same general class of rulers as powerful First Generation Gods such as Ra, although her greatest clearly demonstrated increase occurred only after joining Victor's Draconic lineage.[5]
Her Blood Dragon transformation represented a qualitative evolution rather than an ordinary increase in Energy. Chapter 938 establishes that Amaterasu's reconstructed existence was superior to both [[Izanagi]] and Izanami-no-Mikoto, making her the strongest active Divine existence in [[Takamagahara]] at that stage.[5]
The central reason for this increase was the interaction between her old Divine foundation and the Blood Dragon race. Instead of replacing one with the other, the two systems reinforced each other. Her body gained a genuine [[Dragon Heart]] while her Divinities simultaneously evolved, producing a Dragon Goddess whose Solar authority was drastically superior to what she possessed before the Race Change.[5]
Her Sun Divinity experienced the greatest advancement. Victor recognized that it had reached a threshold from which Amaterasu could eventually continue toward a Primordial state directly centered upon the Sun, placing her future development on a path far beyond her original role as merely one of several high-ranking Shinto Gods.[5]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
Amaterasu initially regarded marriage with [[Victor Elderblood]] primarily as the strongest political alliance available to the [[Shinto Pantheon]]. She had already considered offering herself to him during her Chapter 672 negotiations with Haruna, long before the proposal became reality, because she understood how valuable a permanent connection to Victor would become.[3]
When Amaterasu eventually approached Victor directly, he refused to treat marriage as a temporary alliance. He made clear that accepting her as his wife meant accepting her permanently and that distance, political disagreements or even travel across Dimensions would not cause him to simply release her from that relationship. Amaterasu accepted his conditions, and Victor's Yandere Divinity immediately transformed their agreement into a [[Soul Marriage]].[4]
The relationship subsequently became genuinely personal. Victor treated Amaterasu as another member of his family rather than as a foreign God-Queen who happened to be allied with him, while Amaterasu gradually abandoned the emotional distance of the original arrangement. By the period following Ragnarök, she openly displayed jealousy when Victor gave affection to other wives and desired his attention for herself.[7]
Their family continued developing throughout the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s accelerated history. Amaterasu eventually had a daughter with Victor, Hinata-Omikami, who became one of the highest-ranking Goddesses within the later Shinto Pantheon.[11]

#### Izanagi
[[Izanagi]] was Amaterasu's father and one of the original great Gods associated with Shinto. Amaterasu eventually inherited and exceeded the Divine position associated with her parents. Following her Blood Dragon transformation, her overall existence became qualitatively superior to Izanagi's.[5]
An Essence associated with the Creation powers of Izanagi and Izanami was preserved by Amaterasu and later offered to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] as part of her original marriage negotiations.[4]

#### Izanami-no-Mikoto
[[Izanami-no-Mikoto]] is Amaterasu's mother. The two stood on opposing sides during the Shinto civil conflict, with Amaterasu's faction ultimately emerging victorious.[1]
Despite Izanami's status as an ancient First Generation Divine existence, Amaterasu's later Blood Dragon transformation elevated her beyond her mother in overall quality and strength.[5]

#### Susanoo
Susanoo is Amaterasu's youngest brother. Their personalities differ considerably, with Susanoo being more impulsive while Amaterasu generally prefers calculated decisions. She nevertheless respects useful advice from him rather than dismissing it simply because she outranks him.
His warning in Chapter 826 directly prevented Amaterasu from pursuing the anti-Dragon weapons market. Once Susanoo explained that profiting from fear of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] could cause Dragon's Nest to regard Shinto as an enemy, Amaterasu immediately recognized that the idea threatened a much more important alliance and abandoned it.[8]

#### Tsukuyomi
[[Tsukuyomi]] is Amaterasu's younger brother. During the Demonic War, he suffered injuries severe enough that recovery was expected to require centuries. Amaterasu also revealed during negotiations with Haruna that his marriage to [[Yomi-Hime]] had ended.[3]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later involved Amaterasu, Tsukuyomi and Susanoo together in [[Frederick Winter]]'s future training after reconstructing Fred as a complete Noble Vampire who retained his Onmyō abilities.[10]

#### Otsuki Elderblood Haruna
Amaterasu and Haruna originally represented factions with a long and complicated history of conflict between Shinto Gods and Yokai. The devastation of the Demonic War forced both leaders to act pragmatically. Amaterasu personally approached Haruna, admitted the limitations of her Pantheon and negotiated an arrangement under which the Yokai would help defend Japan while receiving Shinto resources and equipment in return.[2][3]
Their relationship later became considerably more personal after both women married [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and became members of the same family. By the period following Ragnarök, Amaterasu and Haruna were participating together in the political and social maneuvering surrounding Victor's expanding faction.[7]
