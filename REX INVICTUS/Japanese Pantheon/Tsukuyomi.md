Tsukuyomi, formally known as Tsukuyomi-no-Mikoto, is a Second Generation Primordial God, the God of the Moon of the [[Shinto Pantheon]] and one of the Three Noble Children of [[Izanagi]] and [[Izanami-no-Mikoto]]. He is the younger brother of [[Amaterasu]] Elderblood and the older brother of Susanoo.
Tsukuyomi maintained a political marriage with [[Yomi-Hime]], Queen of the Tengus, for thousands of years. The marriage was arranged as part of the political relationship between the Tengu and Shinto Gods rather than being based on affection. Tsukuyomi's prejudice against Yokai prevented the relationship from becoming genuine, and Yomi eventually divorced him after later revelations concerning him.[1][2]

### Physical Appearance
The novel has not provided a sufficiently detailed canonical description of Tsukuyomi's appearance. During his debut he remained partially concealed while receiving reports from a Tengu messenger, preventing a complete physical description.[1]
He later appeared publicly alongside [[Amaterasu]] and Susanoo at the second [[Supernatural Gathering]].[3]

### Personality
Tsukuyomi is arrogant, emotionally distant and deeply prejudiced against Yokais. During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s retaliation in Japan, he openly dismissed the Yokai being killed and described their deaths as the removal of unwanted filth despite being married to Yomi, herself a Yokai.[1]
His marriage to Yomi was entirely political from his perspective. He reminded her that the Gods protected the Tengu in exchange for their service as messengers and observers and did not behave as though thousands of years of marriage created any deeper obligation. Yomi's own thoughts reveal that Tsukuyomi's prejudice was so severe that he never properly accepted or touched her as a wife.[1]
Tsukuyomi is nevertheless capable of accurately recognizing danger. He understood much earlier than many supernatural observers that Victor's political influence was abnormal for such a young Vampire Count and supported avoiding direct confrontation with Victor and [[Scathach]] while Lesser Gods were evacuated from Japan.[1]
His self-preservation becomes even clearer when confronted with [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]]. Tsukuyomi knew that Vlad possessed methods capable of permanently killing Gods rather than merely destroying their current bodies and reacted to the Vampire King with genuine fear.[4]

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Tsukuyomi first appeared while receiving a report from one of the Tengu during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and [[Scathach]]'s retaliation against the supernatural factions involved in the incident surrounding Ophis and [[Nero]]. The messenger used a talisman to show Tsukuyomi and Yomi events occurring in the Mortal World, where multiple supernatural groups were being systematically attacked.[1]
Tsukuyomi explained that the Shinto Gods had already attempted to delay Victor's group through the [[Reverse World]], not because they believed they could permanently imprison them, but because they needed time to evacuate vulnerable Lesser Gods. Natalia could return Victor's group to the Mortal World, while Scathach herself was powerful enough to escape without outside assistance.[1]
He also displayed a much better understanding of Victor's position than Yomi initially possessed. Tsukuyomi identified Victor as the youngest Vampire Count in history and considered his political influence extraordinary, noting the consequences of Victor's actions for Japan and the caution displayed by Genji after dealing with him.[1]
When Tsukuyomi learned that [[Kurama]], one of [[Inari-Omikami]]'s subordinates, had been closely involved in the events that provoked Victor, he ordered a Tengu messenger to warn Inari not to leave the Divine World. He made clear that if she ignored the warning, the Shinto Gods would not intervene on her behalf.[1] Inari later ignored that warning and was killed by Scathach.[5]
After the messenger left, Yomi confronted Tsukuyomi over his willingness to abandon the other Yokai. Tsukuyomi responded that he considered their deaths beneficial and reminded Yomi that the relationship between her people and the Gods had always been transactional. He ordered her to concentrate on protecting the Tengu because they remained useful to the Pantheon.[1]
Yomi was deeply hurt by his behavior. She had hoped that thousands of years together might eventually soften his prejudice, but his comments confirmed that his attitude toward Yokai had not changed.
Tsukuyomi later reacted strongly when [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] arrived in Japan. Having personally felt Vlad's attention before, he remembered the fear of true death and recalled stories of Gods who had fought the Vampire King and disappeared permanently. Rather than provoke him, Tsukuyomi obeyed [[Amaterasu]]'s orders to avoid unnecessary conflict.[4]

#### Demonic War
During the Demonic War, Tsukuyomi fought against Demonic forces and was severely injured. [[Amaterasu]] later described his condition as an extremely deep or "eternal" sleep and estimated that he could remain inactive for centuries.[2]
While Tsukuyomi was incapacitated, information concerning him came to light that further damaged his relationship with Yomi. The exact details have not been fully revealed, but Amaterasu confirmed that Yomi had divorced him by the time she negotiated with Haruna.[2]

#### King Among Kings
Despite [[Amaterasu]]'s earlier expectation that his recovery could require centuries, Tsukuyomi had returned to active Divine society by the time of the second [[Supernatural Gathering]]. He appeared alongside Amaterasu and Susanoo as part of the Shinto delegation.[3]

### Skills & Abilities
The novel has shown very little of Tsukuyomi's direct combat capability. General abilities belonging to Gods and [[Primordial Gods]] should therefore remain on the relevant race pages rather than being extrapolated into a large unsupported personal ability list.
- Moon Divinity: Tsukuyomi is explicitly the Shinto God of the Moon and possesses Divine authority associated with it.
- [[Divine Energy]]: As a true God and Second Generation Primordial God, Tsukuyomi possesses Divine Energy. No detailed personal techniques using it have yet been shown.
- White-Light Movement: Tsukuyomi disappeared from Yomi's presence in a flash of white light during Chapter 347. The novel does not explain whether this was teleportation, transformation or another form of Divine movement.[1]

### Power
Tsukuyomi's classification as a Second Generation Primordial God describes his lineage rather than automatically making him equal to the strongest [[Primordial Gods]].
His severe injuries during the Demonic War also demonstrate that Second Generation Primordial status did not make him untouchable against sufficiently dangerous enemies.[2]

### Relationships

#### Yomi-Hime
[[Yomi-Hime]] was Tsukuyomi's wife for thousands of years. Their marriage was politically arranged and never became genuinely affectionate. Tsukuyomi's prejudice against Yokai prevented him from accepting Yomi as an equal partner, and she eventually divorced him.[1][2]

#### Amaterasu Elderblood
[[Amaterasu]] Elderblood is Tsukuyomi's older sister and political superior within the [[Shinto Pantheon]]. Tsukuyomi generally obeys her decisions concerning major threats to the Pantheon.[4]

#### Susanoo
Susanoo is Tsukuyomi's younger brother and the youngest of the Three Noble Children. The brothers later appeared together beside [[Amaterasu]] at the second [[Supernatural Gathering]].[3]

#### Victor Elderblood
Tsukuyomi recognized [[Victor Elderblood]] as an abnormal political threat long before Victor became a Dragon or God. Through [[Amaterasu]]'s later marriage, Victor also became Tsukuyomi's brother-in-law.[1]

#### Vlad Tepes
[[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] is one of the beings Tsukuyomi genuinely feared because of his ability to permanently kill Gods.[4]

### Trivia
- Tsukuyomi is the middle member of the Three Noble Children.
- His marriage to Yomi lasted thousands of years but remained political rather than romantic.[1]
- According to Yomi, Tsukuyomi never truly accepted her because she was a Yokai.[1]
- He correctly warned Inari that Shinto would not save her if she entered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and [[Scathach]]'s conflict.[1]
- Tsukuyomi understood Victor's political importance unusually early.[1]
- He was severely injured during the Demonic War and initially expected to remain inactive for centuries.[2]
- He recovered enough to attend the second [[Supernatural Gathering]] by Chapter 863.[3]
