### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 389
Ebisu, also known as Kotoshiro-nushi-no-kami and Hiruko, is a Japanese God belonging to the [[Shinto Pantheon]] and one of the Seven Gods of Fortune. He is a Third Generation God classified within the Low-Level God range whose Divine functions are associated with Fortune, Luck and Fishermen.
Unlike many of the Shinto Pantheon's more combat-oriented deities, Ebisu is explicitly not a warrior God. His power is instead primarily defensive and territorial. Within his own temple, Ebisu possesses significantly greater control over his [[Divine Energy]] and was capable of continuously reinforcing both his barriers and the surrounding mountain strongly enough to survive attacks from [[Victor Elderblood]] that threatened to destroy the entire area.[1][2]
Ebisu became involved with Victor primarily because of his association with the Nine-Tailed Fox Okama. What began as Ebisu confidently believing his temple was inaccessible to a creature of the night ended with Victor forcibly breaching his Divine defenses, overwhelming him through repeated attacks and coercing him into accepting a magical contract that allowed Clan Alucard to establish an indirect intelligence connection to the Shinto Divine Realm.[3][4]

### Physical Appearance
Ebisu appears as an elderly Japanese man. During his introduction, he was calmly seated inside his temple drinking tea with Okama, and his appearance was ordinary enough that [[Nero Elderblood]] later questioned whether he was anything more than an old man when she first examined him closely.[3][2]
His mundane appearance sharply contrasts with the [[Divine Energy]] contained within his body. Despite looking physically elderly, Ebisu possesses the supernatural physique of a true God and is explicitly stronger than an ordinary Noble Vampire even without being specialized in physical combat.[2]
When forced to seriously defend himself, Ebisu releases a powerful white Divine aura. He is also capable of floating in the air while concentrating his Divine Energy into his defenses.[1]

### Personality
Ebisu initially presents himself as proud, dismissive and deeply confident in the natural superiority of Gods over lesser supernatural races. He belongs to an older generation of deities who pay relatively little attention to supernatural beings they consider beneath the Gods, and his ignorance concerning [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] reflects this mentality. While Okama had investigated the newly risen Vampire Count and understood that Victor was highly abnormal, Ebisu had barely concerned himself with such information.[3]
His prejudice is particularly evident in his view of creatures of the night. Ebisu believed that Victor's nature as a Vampire automatically placed him at a disadvantage against Divine power and confidently assumed that exposure to the Divine element of a God would weaken him. He therefore considered Victor little more than another night creature who could not possibly overcome the protections surrounding his temple.[3]
Ebisu is also extremely proud of the sanctity of his territory. He confidently declared that nobody could enter his domain without his permission, treating his temple's barrier as essentially absolute. Victor's decision to simply force his way through the protection consequently became both a physical and personal humiliation.[3][5]
Despite this arrogance, Ebisu is not genuinely suicidal. Once Victor demonstrated that he possessed enough power to destroy the temple and potentially kill him, Ebisu immediately prioritized survival over Divine dignity. He poured everything he had into defending himself, shouted at Victor to stop and eventually abandoned his pride enough to plead with him when additional attacks appeared overhead.[1]
His pride nevertheless remained easily wounded. After surviving Victor's attack, Ebisu was particularly irritated when Victor casually informed him that he had never been the reason for the visit at all and was merely an "extra" in Victor's actual business with Okama. Being treated as irrelevant by a Vampire after having barely survived his assault was considerably more insulting to Ebisu than a conventional challenge to his authority.[6]
Ebisu is also capable of pragmatism. Although he objected strongly when Victor attempted to transform Okama into an information source inside Japan and the Shinto Divine Realm, the continued presence of Victor's enormous ice weapons overhead quickly convinced him that survival was more important than resistance.[4]
Victor eventually concluded that beneath Ebisu's pride and outdated worldview, the God was not fundamentally malicious. After speaking with him more calmly, Victor judged Ebisu to be essentially a decent person primarily concerned with surviving an extremely dangerous situation.[4]

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes and War
Ebisu became directly involved in events following the destruction of the Japanese Noble Vampire faction associated with the conflict surrounding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family. While Victor and his forces were dealing with the remaining enemies outside, Ebisu remained inside his temple with Okama, a golden-haired Nine-Tailed Fox.[7][3]
The two calmly drank tea while waiting for the battle to conclude. When Okama announced that his forces had been defeated, Ebisu questioned how casually the Kitsune accepted the destruction of his army. Okama explained that the defeated force was not his primary strength and that Yōkai would continue existing as long as Humanity itself remained.[3]
Okama then turned the question toward Ebisu and asked whether he was truly unconcerned about Victor approaching the temple.
Ebisu remained completely confident. He stated that nobody could enter his territory without his permission and dismissed the significance of Victor being a Vampire Count. In Ebisu's view, Victor was still fundamentally a creature of the night and therefore naturally vulnerable to the Divine element wielded by Gods.[3]
Okama was considerably less certain. Unlike Ebisu, he had taken the time to investigate Victor and understood that the newly risen Count was anything but an ordinary Vampire. Ebisu's lack of information was attributed to the tendency of old-fashioned Gods to ignore beings they considered beneath themselves.[3]
Ebisu's confidence was immediately challenged when Victor arrived.
The temple was protected by an invisible Divine barrier which prevented Victor from simply walking inside. Rather than withdraw or negotiate for permission, Victor decided to use the situation as a training opportunity for [[Nero Elderblood]] and [[Bruna Elderblood]], demonstrating the mechanics of a properly executed physical strike.[5]
Victor transferred force from his legs through his hips, back, shoulders and arm before striking the barrier. The resulting impact created enormous air pressure and caused multiple earthquakes throughout the mountain, immediately demonstrating that Ebisu's supposed absolute defense was being challenged by physical force alone.[5]
Victor himself was dissatisfied with the result and escalated the attack.
The confrontation quickly transformed from an attempt to enter the temple into an extreme test of Ebisu's defensive capabilities. Victor began combining the powers inherited from the Snow, Scarlett and Fulger bloodlines, producing enormous elemental attacks against Ebisu's protections.[5][1]
Victor eventually created an enormous spike of ice high above the mountain. The attack was so large that the people observing from within Kaguya's shadow seriously questioned whether the entire mountain was about to disappear.[1]
Ebisu responded by reinforcing the area continuously with [[Divine Energy]]. The mountain's survival was consequently not evidence that Victor's attack lacked destructive force; Ebisu was actively using the power of a God to prevent the surrounding territory and his temple from being destroyed.[1]
Victor then increased the attack further.
He ignited the enormous ice spike with fire, coated it in lightning and caused it to rotate like a gigantic drill, dramatically increasing its penetration power. Ebisu immediately recognized that his existing barrier would not survive the impact.[1]
Rather than surrender immediately, Ebisu released more of his Divine Energy, levitated into the air and poured his power into his defenses. When Victor's attack pierced through the first barrier, Ebisu generated a smaller and more concentrated defensive field directly around the approaching spike.[1]
The resulting struggle forced Ebisu to use virtually everything he could produce. He ultimately succeeded in stopping the attack, but suffered minor internal injuries in the process and believed that he had finally defended his temple.[1]
His relief lasted only moments.
Victor revealed that the first projectile was not the only one he had created. Multiple additional gigantic spikes remained suspended above the battlefield, causing Ebisu's expression to immediately collapse from pride into terror.[1]
Realizing that Victor could simply continue the bombardment until his defenses failed, Ebisu begged him to stop. Victor deliberately continued frightening the God before eventually ending the attack.[1]
The encounter became one of Victor's earliest meaningful interactions with the power of a true God. Although Ebisu was only a Low-Level God and lacked combat specialization, Victor had forced a genuine Divine Being inside his own territory to seriously defend himself with his full Divine Energy.[1]
After successfully forcing his way into the protected temple, Victor behaved as though the entire confrontation had been little more than an inconvenience. He entered the building without asking permission and casually examined Ebisu's minimalist residence.[6]
Ebisu initially assumed Victor must have come because of him and demanded to know why the Vampire Count had nearly destroyed his temple.
Victor's answer deeply wounded his pride.
Victor openly stated that he had no particular interest in Ebisu at all. He had attacked the barrier simply because Ebisu had refused to let him enter. From Victor's perspective, the God was merely an "extra" standing between him and the person he actually wanted to meet—Okama.[6]
Ebisu was furious at being dismissed so casually, particularly after using so much of his power merely to keep his temple intact, but he no longer possessed any desire to antagonize Victor directly.
Victor subsequently took the seat normally reserved for Ebisu and treated the temple as though it belonged to him. Ebisu was visibly irritated by the disrespect but remained silent because his primary desire was for Victor to finish his business and leave.[2]
Ebisu's presence provided [[Nero]] and the others with an opportunity to examine an ordinary God in detail.
When Nero initially dismissed Ebisu as merely an elderly man, [[Roxanne Elderblood]] reminded her that he had survived Victor's assault—something very few beings could have accomplished.[2]
[[Roberta Elderblood]] then clarified Ebisu's nature.
Ebisu was not a fighting God and possessed little meaningful offensive specialization. His primary capability came from manipulating Divine Energy around himself and throughout his own temple. Because he was inside his territory, his control over that Energy and the actions he could perform with it were significantly improved.[2]
Even without combat specialization, Ebisu's Divine body was physically stronger than that of an ordinary Noble Vampire. Once his Divine Energy was included, he could become extremely dangerous to insufficiently developed creatures of the night because Divine Energy possesses a natural advantage against such beings.[2]
Ebisu therefore demonstrated an important distinction between raw Divine existence and combat specialization. He was superior to ordinary supernatural beings simply because he was a genuine God, yet he remained considerably less dangerous in battle than Gods whose Divinities, training and experience were specifically centered around combat.
Victor's actual objective was Okama.
After speaking with the Nine-Tailed Fox, Victor realized that Okama had not been one of the individuals primarily responsible for the actions that had provoked him. Rather than killing him, Victor offered a mutually beneficial arrangement.[4]
In exchange for providing information concerning the Japanese Noble Vampires and assisting Clan Alucard, Okama and his faction would become intelligence agents operating throughout Japan.
Okama's relationship with Ebisu made the arrangement considerably more valuable. Because the Kitsune maintained direct contact with a Shinto God, he possessed indirect access to the [[Shinto Pantheon]]'s Divine Realm. Victor therefore realized that through Okama he could potentially maintain an information source not only among Japan's Yōkai and Vampires, but inside the Shinto Divine sphere itself.[4]
Ebisu strongly objected to the arrangement.
Victor responded by allowing the enormous ice spikes still suspended above the temple to descend slightly.
Ebisu immediately reconsidered.[4]
Victor nevertheless refused to trust only the spoken promise of a God. He produced a rare gold-colored Witch contract and forced the agreement into a magically binding form. Such contracts could only be broken by highly experienced or Master-level Witches, and Victor considered the probability of the reclusive Ebisu maintaining contact with someone capable of breaking it extremely small.[4]
The terms additionally prevented Ebisu from revealing the agreement itself, effectively ensuring his silence regarding Victor's new intelligence arrangement with Okama.[4]
Through the incident, Ebisu went from confidently believing that no creature of the night could enter his territory to becoming an unwilling participant in the very intelligence network Victor intended to establish inside Japan.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
Ebisu and [[Victor Elderblood]] began their relationship as enemies almost entirely because Ebisu refused Victor entry into his territory.
Ebisu initially regarded Victor as an inferior night creature who would naturally be weakened by Divine power, while Victor treated Ebisu's supposedly impenetrable barrier as little more than an obstacle to be broken.[3][5]
The confrontation became deeply humiliating for Ebisu. Victor forced him to exhaust himself defending his temple, terrified him with multiple attacks, entered the building anyway, occupied Ebisu's seat and then revealed that Ebisu had never even been the reason for his visit.[1][6][2]
Despite this hostile beginning, Victor ultimately did not consider Ebisu genuinely evil. After speaking with him, Victor regarded him as essentially a decent individual attempting to survive a situation far beyond his control.[4]
Their relationship therefore ended less as a personal vendetta and more as a forced understanding in which Ebisu accepted Victor's demands because resisting him was no longer worth the risk.

#### Okama
Okama is a Nine-Tailed Fox with a long-standing connection to Ebisu.
The two were comfortable enough to sit together drinking tea inside Ebisu's private temple, and Okama possessed enough access through Ebisu to maintain contact with the [[Shinto Pantheon]]'s Divine Realm.[3][4]
Their personalities differed significantly. Okama was cautious enough to research [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] before meeting him, while Ebisu dismissed the Vampire Count because of his prejudice toward creatures beneath the Gods. Okama consequently understood the danger considerably earlier than Ebisu did.[3]
Ebisu was displeased when Okama agreed to become an intelligence agent for Clan Alucard, especially because the arrangement potentially gave Victor an indirect information channel into the Shinto Divine Realm.[4]

### Skills & Abilities
As a Third Generation Japanese God, Ebisu possesses a fully Divine body, [[Divine Energy]] and Divinities associated with his religious functions. He is classified as a Low-Level God and is explicitly described as a non-combat God. His demonstrated abilities are therefore heavily defensive and utility-oriented rather than offensive.
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics — Ebisu possesses the enhanced physical characteristics inherent to a true God. Despite being completely unspecialized in combat, his base physical ability is explicitly greater than that of an ordinary Noble Vampire.[2]
- Divine Energy — As a true God, Ebisu generates and manipulates Divine Energy. This Energy forms the foundation of his barriers, territorial reinforcement and supernatural defenses.[1][2]
- Divine Aura — Ebisu can release his Divine Energy around his body as a visible white aura. The intensity increases when he seriously exerts himself.[1]
- Divine Barrier Creation — Ebisu can construct extremely powerful invisible barriers around his territory. His temple's primary barrier resisted [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s physical and elemental attacks before eventually being penetrated.[5][1]
- Barrier Reinforcement — Ebisu can continuously supply Divine Energy to his barriers in order to repair, strengthen or maintain them against overwhelming attacks.[1]
- Multiple Barrier Creation — When Victor's empowered ice spike pierced his original protection, Ebisu immediately created a second smaller barrier concentrated around the projectile, greatly increasing the defensive pressure at a single point.[1]
- Territory Empowerment — Ebisu becomes considerably more effective inside his own temple. Within his territory, he possesses superior control over his Divine Energy and can perform actions that would be more difficult elsewhere.[2]
- Environmental Reinforcement — Ebisu can reinforce the physical environment surrounding his temple with Divine Energy. During Victor's assault, he continuously strengthened the mountain itself, preventing it from being destroyed by the collateral force of Victor's attacks.[1]
- Divine Territory Control — Ebisu's authority over his temple allows him to regulate entry through a Divine barrier and exert his Energy throughout the surrounding area.[3][2]
- Night-Creature Suppression — Ebisu's Divine Energy is particularly dangerous to creatures of the night. Roberta stated that when his Divine Energy is taken into account, Ebisu can become a terror to night beings that have not reached a sufficiently developed level.[2]
- Enhanced Strength — Even without specializing in combat, Ebisu is physically stronger than an ordinary Noble Vampire purely through his Divine physiology.[2]
- Enhanced Durability — Ebisu survived Victor's repeated assaults and remained functional even after sustaining internal injuries while defending his temple.[1]
- Levitation — Ebisu demonstrated the ability to float in the air while releasing and concentrating his Divine Energy.[1]
- Longevity — As an ancient God, Ebisu has existed for an extremely long period while remaining physically functional.
- Fortune Divinity — Ebisu is recognized as a God associated with Fortune.
- Luck Divinity — Ebisu possesses a Divine association with Luck.
- Fishermen Divinity — Ebisu is worshipped as a God associated with fishermen.

### Combat Style
Ebisu is explicitly not a combat-oriented God.
Unlike Gods such as Susanoo, [[Takemikazuchi]] or other deities whose powers are naturally suited for direct conflict, Ebisu's demonstrated approach centers on survival and defense.
His preferred method can be summarized as:
Temple / Divine Territory
↓
Superior control over [[Divine Energy]]
↓
Large-scale defensive barrier
↓
Continuous Divine reinforcement
↓
Environmental reinforcement
↓
Concentrated secondary barriers when necessary
This made Ebisu exceptionally difficult for conventional supernatural beings to attack inside his own territory, even though he lacked significant offensive techniques.[2]
His confrontation with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] should therefore not be interpreted as Ebisu possessing attack power comparable to Victor's strongest techniques. His primary accomplishment was his ability to defend against them long enough to protect his temple.

### Power
Ebisu is classified as a Low-Level God.
Even at this relatively low position within the Divine hierarchy, the natural difference between a God and ordinary supernatural races is considerable. Roberta explicitly judged Ebisu physically superior to a Noble Vampire despite his lack of combat specialization, while his [[Divine Energy]] made him extremely dangerous to insufficiently developed night creatures.[2]
His greatest demonstrated feat occurred during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s assault on his temple.
Victor's ordinary physical strike produced earthquake-like effects across the mountain.[5] Victor then escalated to a combined elemental attack powerful enough that observers questioned whether the mountain itself would disappear.[1]
The mountain survived because Ebisu continuously reinforced it with Divine Energy.
Victor later created a massive ice spike, ignited it with fire, covered it in lightning and rotated it like a drill. The attack pierced Ebisu's initial barrier, forcing him to create a second concentrated defense and pour nearly all of his power into stopping it.[1]
Ebisu ultimately succeeded, although the effort caused internal injuries.
Therefore:
Ebisu = Low-Level God
Ebisu inside his Temple > Ebisu outside his Temple in defensive control
Base physical characteristics > Ordinary Noble Vampire
Divine Energy = Highly dangerous to undeveloped Night Creatures
Prepared Divine Barrier + continuous reinforcement = Able to withstand Victor's early Count-era attacks
This does not mean Ebisu was equal to Victor as an overall fighter. Ebisu himself lacked significant offensive power and survived primarily because he fought defensively inside the environment where his Divine Energy was most effective.[2]

### Equipment

#### Temple
Ebisu resides within a minimalist temple protected by his Divine power.
The temple functions as more than a residence. It is Ebisu's principal territory and dramatically increases his ability to manipulate [[Divine Energy]]. His control over barriers and environmental reinforcement becomes considerably more effective while operating inside it.[2]

#### Gold Witch Contract
Ebisu was forced to sign a rare gold-colored magical contract supplied by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
Gold contracts are powerful Witch-created agreements that can generally only be broken by Master-level or highly experienced Witches. The contract prevented Ebisu from revealing the arrangement between Victor and Okama and ensured his compliance with the newly established intelligence network.[4]
The contract belongs to Victor rather than Ebisu and should therefore not be considered Ebisu's personal possession.
