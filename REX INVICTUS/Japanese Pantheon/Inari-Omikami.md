Inari-Omikami, also known as Inari-Okami or simply Inari, was a Third Generation Japanese Goddess and a ten-tailed Divine Kitsune belonging to the [[Shinto Pantheon]]. Originally a Yokai, she achieved Godhood and retained the characteristics of a Kitsune alongside her Divine nature. She possessed authorities associated with natural elements and Prosperity and maintained considerable influence over Japan's Kitsune population.
Inari became directly involved in Haruna's conflict with [[Hashimoto Genji]], only to be confronted by [[Victor Elderblood]] and subsequently challenged by [[Scathach]]. Despite possessing the raw power of a true Goddess, extensive elemental abilities, spatial techniques and a gigantic ten-tailed Divine form, Inari was comprehensively defeated by Scathach's superior combat experience and technique. Her Divine body was destroyed and her recovery was later estimated to require thousands of years.[1][2][3]

### Physical Appearance
Inari appeared as a beautiful woman with long golden hair, golden eyes, fox ears and ten golden tails. She wore traditional Japanese clothing and possessed the distinctly supernatural appearance expected of a Divine Kitsune.[1]
When she released more of her Divine power against [[Scathach]], markings appeared across her body while a massive pillar of golden [[Divine Energy]] erupted around her.[4]
Inari's true form was an enormous golden fox with ten colossal tails. Each tail represented or wielded a different natural element, and the scale of the transformation was large enough for her tails to be compared to or exceed nearby mountains.[2]

### Personality
Inari was proud, arrogant and highly conscious of her status as a Goddess. She expected respect from those beneath the Divine hierarchy and reacted badly when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] treated her with complete irreverence. His mockery of her appearance immediately angered her, while his lack of fear toward her Divine status prevented her from establishing the authority she expected.[1]
Her arrogance did not mean she was indifferent to everyone around her. When Inari learned that Victor had destroyed her temple and killed servants, followers and relatives connected to her, her anger became genuinely personal. She attempted to confirm what had happened herself before reacting with rage toward Victor.[1]
Inari also possessed significant determination in battle. [[Scathach]] repeatedly injured, humiliated and outmaneuvered her, yet Inari continued increasing the amount of power she used and ultimately transformed into her complete Divine Kitsune form instead of immediately surrendering.[5][4][2]
Nevertheless, the battle severely damaged her confidence. After her death, Inari initially threatened to remember what Scathach had done when she eventually returned. When Scathach responded that she would simply hunt her again after her resurrection, Inari immediately abandoned the threat and declared that she would forget the entire incident.[2]

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Inari entered the story during Haruna's confrontation with Genji. As the conflict between the Japanese Yokai factions escalated, Inari attempted to interfere directly by attacking Haruna through a spatial opening. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] immediately pulled Haruna away from the attack and retaliated against Inari through the same opening, dragging the Goddess into the battlefield.[1]
Victor had already attacked Inari's temple before her arrival. When Genji informed her that the temple and those living there had been destroyed, Inari initially refused to believe him and attempted to verify the situation through her own Divine power. Once she confirmed the deaths of her followers, servants and relatives, she became furious with Victor. [[Scathach]] then stepped forward and took Inari as her opponent.[1]
The opening stages of the battle demonstrated the difference between Inari's Divine power and Scathach's combat mastery. Inari possessed the superior natural existence of a God and could combine swordsmanship with elemental and spatial abilities, but Scathach repeatedly exploited gaps in her movements and concentration. Inari generated a large sun-like fire manifestation, fought with a katana surrounded by blue Energy that distorted the surrounding space, and engaged Scathach at speeds and levels of force capable of damaging the isolated battlefield itself.[5][4]
As the battle intensified, Scathach stabbed Inari through the chest, causing the Goddess to bleed a golden substance. Inari responded by releasing substantially more [[Divine Energy]], producing a golden pillar of power and markings over her body. Despite the increase, Scathach remained in control of the fight. Inari also realized that the other Shinto Gods were not intervening to save her, further destabilizing her confidence.[4]
Scathach eventually used the higher stages of her ice techniques to transform the battlefield. She generated massive ice formations, a living ice dragon and an enormous fortress equipped with supernatural siege weapons. Inari answered by entering her true form, becoming a gigantic golden ten-tailed fox whose tails each wielded a different natural element. The transformation greatly increased the scale of her power but also made her a much larger target.[2]
Scathach exploited that weakness. Her projectiles transformed after being fired, piercing Inari's body before further attacks restrained the enormous fox. Inari eventually attempted a final attack, but Scathach launched a gigantic spear that pierced her head. Inari's Divine body dissolved into golden particles while her spirit returned toward the Divine Realm.[2]
Inari's destruction was not treated as permanent existential erasure. The Gods observing the battle expected that she would eventually return, but the process would take an immense amount of time. Several days later, [[Mizuki Elderblood]] attempted to invoke Inari's Blessing of Prosperity and received only a minute response. [[Abe-no-Seimei]] concluded that Inari would require thousands of years to properly awaken again.[3]
Her defeat also freed [[Kurama]] from a restrictive pact he had made with her. The agreement had prevented him from leaving Japan without suffering a considerable loss of power, but with Inari effectively dead or in an extended Divine coma, the contract ceased functioning until her eventual return.[6]

### Skills & Abilities
Inari was a Third Generation God and ten-tailed Kitsune. General abilities belonging to those races are covered on the Gods and Kitsune pages; the abilities listed here are powers or techniques directly demonstrated or stated for Inari.
- [[Divine Energy]]: Inari could release large quantities of golden Divine Energy. When she increased her power against [[Scathach]], a massive golden pillar erupted around her and supernatural markings appeared across her body.[4]
- Elemental Manipulation: Inari possessed authority over multiple natural elements. Her true ten-tailed form explicitly displayed separate elemental powers through her tails.[2]
- Fire Manipulation: Inari used large-scale fire attacks against Scathach and created an enormous sun-like sphere of flame. The manifestation disappeared after Scathach interrupted her concentration.[5]
- Lightning Manipulation: Inari demonstrated lightning as part of her elemental arsenal during her fight with Scathach.[5]
- Spatial Manipulation: Inari could open spatial cracks and attack through them. Her first direct attack against Haruna was launched through a spatial opening.[1]
- Spatial Distortion: Blue Energy surrounding Inari's katana visibly distorted the nearby space during her battle with Scathach.[5]
- Swordsmanship: Inari fought Scathach with a katana and was capable of combining her sword techniques with her Divine and spatial powers.[5][4]
- True Divine Kitsune Form: Inari could transform into an enormous golden ten-tailed fox. Each tail was associated with a different element and was enormous compared with the surrounding terrain.[2]
- Flight: Inari fought and maneuvered through the air during the large-scale stages of her battle against Scathach.[2]
- Blessing of Prosperity: Inari could bestow a Divine blessing associated with Prosperity. Mizuki possessed a method of invoking this blessing, which normally improved her fortune for the following day.[3]
- Divine Reincarnation: Destruction of Inari's Divine body did not permanently erase her. Her spirit returned toward the Divine Realm and she was expected eventually to awaken again, although [[Abe-no-Seimei]] estimated that the process would require thousands of years.[2][3]

### Power
Inari possessed the natural superiority of a true God combined with the abilities of a ten-tailed Kitsune, but the novel repeatedly distinguished her raw supernatural power from actual combat mastery. She could produce large-scale elemental attacks, distort space, fight with a katana and assume a gigantic Divine form, yet [[Scathach]] consistently controlled their confrontation through greater skill and experience.[5][4][2]

### Relationships

#### Scathach Elderblood Scarlett
[[Scathach]] killed Inari after comprehensively defeating her in battle. Inari possessed greater raw Divine status, but Scathach's superior technique, experience and ability to adapt controlled the fight from its early stages to its conclusion.[5][4][2]

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] became one of Inari's principal enemies after intervening in her attack on Haruna and destroying her temple. Inari's anger toward him became personal once she learned that her followers, servants and relatives had died in the attack.[1]

#### Otsuki Elderblood Haruna
Inari intervened against Haruna during the conflict surrounding the Japanese Yokai. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s protection of Haruna directly triggered Inari's confrontation with his faction.[1]

#### Kurama
[[Kurama]] was bound to Inari through a supernatural pact that restricted his ability to leave Japan. Inari's death rendered the pact inactive until her eventual return, allowing Kurama to leave the country without its previous penalty.[6]

### Trivia
- Inari possessed ten tails, placing her above the Nine-Tailed stage displayed by several major Kitsune during the Japanese arc.
- Her blood was golden.[4]
- Her clashes with [[Scathach]] damaged the space of the isolated battlefield.[4]
- Scathach's threat to hunt her again after reincarnation caused Inari to immediately abandon her declaration of future revenge.[2]
- Only a minute trace of Inari's Blessing of Prosperity could still be invoked several days after her death.[3]
- According to [[Abe-no-Seimei]], Inari would require thousands of years to awaken again.[3]
- [[Kurama]] described her inactive condition as an extremely prolonged or effectively eternal coma.[6]
