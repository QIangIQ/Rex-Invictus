The Shinto Pantheon is one of the major Divine Pantheons originating from [[Yggdrasil]] and the traditional supernatural authority associated with Japan. Its principal Divine Realm is [[Takamagahara]], while the Pantheon's broader system historically included responsibility for Japan, its supernatural communities and the Japanese [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]]. The Pantheon is currently ruled by the God-Queen [[Amaterasu]] Elderblood, Goddess of the Sun, Light, Day and Sky.[1]
Historically, the Shinto Pantheon was an independent power comparable to Earth's other major Pantheons. Despite the relatively limited physical territory of Japan, the number of Shinto Gods was enormous. [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] stated that Japan possessed over one million Gods, while [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] separately remarked that the country's Shinto Gods were numerous enough to populate a small country by themselves.[2][3] The overwhelming majority were not major combat deities, but even a Lesser God was considered comparable in raw supernatural strength to an adult Vampire approximately five hundred years old.[3]
Following the rise of [[Victor Elderblood]], the Pantheon's political position changed considerably. Amaterasu first pursued cooperation and eventually a marriage alliance with Victor in order to protect and strengthen her people.[1][4] By the Imperial era, the Shinto Pantheon had been formally annexed into the Elderblood Empire. It was nevertheless permitted to retain its own internal government. Victor later explicitly used the Shinto Pantheon as the model for the treatment given to the annexed [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]]: the Gods could maintain self-governance, but were required to answer the Empire whenever summoned.[5]
The Pantheon therefore currently functions as an autonomous subordinate Pantheon within Victor's larger Imperial and Divine structure rather than as an independent sovereign Pantheon. Amaterasu remains its internal ruler while simultaneously belonging directly to Clan Elderblood and the [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]].[4][5]

### History

#### Ancient Foundation
The earliest known foundation of the Shinto Pantheon is associated with the Creator Gods [[Izanagi]] and [[Izanami-no-Mikoto]]. The pair stood at the beginning of the Pantheon's oldest Divine generation and became the parents of several of its most important later Gods, including [[Amaterasu]] Elderblood, [[Tsukuyomi]] and Susanoo.
Izanagi represented Creation and Life, while Izanami possessed authorities connected to Creation, Death, Earth and the [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]]. Their descendants eventually formed the core of the later Shinto Divine hierarchy.
Following Izanagi's death and the separation between the Heavenly and Underworld authorities, Amaterasu became the central ruler of the Gods residing within [[Takamagahara]], while Izanami remained associated with the Japanese Underworld.

#### Takamagahara and the Divine Court
[[Takamagahara]] is the principal Heavenly Realm of the Shinto Pantheon and serves as the seat of its Gods and central government. During [[Amaterasu]]'s negotiations with [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]], the narration described Takamagahara directly as the paradise of the Shinto Pantheon and described Amaterasu as commanding hundreds of Gods there.[1]
This number should not be confused with the much larger overall population attributed to the Shinto religious system. Earlier statements place the total number of Shinto Gods at over one million.[2] The hundreds directly associated with Takamagahara therefore represent only part of the Pantheon's enormous Divine population, with large numbers of Lesser and Minor Gods existing beyond the principal Divine court.
The Pantheon traditionally served as the primary Divine authority responsible for Japan. Its responsibilities extended beyond warfare and worship and included the protection of the country from hostile supernatural forces, maintenance of Divine institutions and interaction with the numerous supernatural species living throughout the Japanese territory.[1]

#### Relations with the Youkai
The relationship between the Shinto Gods and Japan's Youkai was historically complicated and frequently hostile. Various Youkai communities existed independently of the Pantheon, and several individuals associated with the Gods had poor reputations among Haruna's faction.
One of the clearest examples was [[Yomi-Hime]], a Tengu Queen and commander of the Ravens who had aligned herself with the Gods. Haruna viewed Yomi-Hime as someone who had abandoned and exploited her own people by siding with the Pantheon.[1]
Despite these historical tensions, the Demonic War forced both sides into greater cooperation. After the Japanese supernatural system became destabilized, [[Amaterasu]] personally approached Haruna and proposed hiring the Youkai to help defend Japan while the Pantheon's own forces recovered.[1]
The agreement gave Haruna's faction advanced Shinto weapons and supplies in exchange for acting as temporary protectors against dangerous supernatural entities. Amaterasu additionally placed Yomi-Hime and her Tengu scouts at the Youkai's disposal and provided Haruna with a Divine token allowing direct communication with the God-Queen.[1]

#### Conflict between Amaterasu and Izanami
During the Demonic War, the Pantheon became embroiled in a major internal conflict between [[Amaterasu]] and her mother [[Izanami-no-Mikoto]], who ruled the Japanese [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]].
Izanami had previously been confined within the Underworld, but the conflict eventually developed into a full Mythological War between mother and daughter. Amaterasu ultimately won the confrontation, but the resulting instability created serious consequences throughout Japan.[6]
Demons from the Japanese Underworld escaped into the Human World and began spreading throughout Japan. The conflict forced Haruna's Youkai forces and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s allies to participate in clearing the country of the escaped Demons while the Shinto Gods dealt with the aftermath of their civil war.[6]
The war left the Pantheon severely weakened. Numerous Lesser Gods were seriously injured, while others entered states of prolonged Divine sleep. [[Tsukuyomi]] was among the Gods temporarily incapacitated and was expected to require centuries to naturally recover.[1]
The Underworld system was also thrown into disorder. Souls belonging to people devoted to Shinto Gods could no longer properly pass through the afterlife because Izanami had ceased fulfilling her duties as its Ruler. The Gods consequently had to divert resources toward maintaining the system while simultaneously searching for someone capable of replacing or assisting the Underworld's Ruler.[1]
The combined losses were severe enough that Amaterasu admitted the Shinto Pantheon had temporarily become incapable of properly protecting Japan by itself.[1]

#### Post-War Recovery
[[Amaterasu]] responded to the crisis pragmatically rather than attempting to conceal the Pantheon's weakness. She personally travelled to Haruna's territory without an escort and negotiated for the Youkai to supplement the Pantheon's depleted defensive forces.[1]
The agreement was primarily transactional. The Shinto Pantheon supplied weapons, materials and other resources, while Haruna's forces agreed to continue protecting Japan from dangerous supernatural entities until the Gods had sufficiently recovered.[1]
Amaterasu estimated that, assuming recovery proceeded normally, the Pantheon could reorganize its [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] administration and resume its ordinary protective responsibilities within approximately a month.[1]

### Population and Military Strength
The Shinto Pantheon is unusual even among the major Pantheons because of the extraordinary number of Gods associated with Japanese belief.
Ruby estimated that Japan possessed more than one million Gods.[2] [[Scathach]] later independently described their population as large enough to fill a small country.[3]
This enormous Divine population should not be interpreted as one million high-ranking combat Gods. The Pantheon contains a vast number of Lesser and Minor Gods representing local, cultural, natural and specialized concepts.
Nevertheless, even the lower tiers possess significant supernatural power. Scathach stated that:
- a Lesser God possesses power comparable to an adult Vampire approximately 500 years old;[3]
- Divine beings damaged by sufficiently dangerous supernatural corruption could require thousands of years to recover;[3]
- and Minor Gods with insufficient Faith Energy could potentially die permanently from sufficiently severe supernatural damage.[3]
The Pantheon's principal military strength therefore comes from both its extraordinary numbers and its relatively high minimum supernatural baseline.
Above the Lesser Gods exist far more powerful Major and [[Primordial Gods]] such as [[Amaterasu]], Susanoo, [[Tsukuyomi]], Inari and [[Takemikazuchi]].

### Government

#### God-Queen
The supreme authority of the modern Shinto Pantheon is [[Amaterasu]] Elderblood. She rules [[Takamagahara]] as its God-Queen and acts as the principal political representative of the Pantheon.[1]
Amaterasu possesses sufficient authority to independently negotiate international agreements, mobilize Divine resources, employ outside supernatural factions and represent the Shinto Gods during major supernatural meetings.
She also commanded enough direct authority that even before the formation of the [[Elderblood Empire]] she was described as controlling hundreds of Gods residing within Takamagahara.[1]

#### Autonomous Imperial Government
The Shinto Pantheon no longer possesses complete sovereignty.
After Earth's supernatural factions were incorporated into the [[Elderblood Empire]], [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] allowed the Shinto Pantheon to preserve its internal government rather than dismantling its Divine hierarchy.[5]

The Pantheon may manage its own internal affairs but is obligated to respond when the Empire calls upon it. Victor explicitly described this arrangement when annexing the [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]], ordering it to receive the same treatment as the Shinto Pantheon: self-governance in exchange for submission to Imperial authority.[5]
This makes the modern Shinto Pantheon more accurately described as an autonomous Imperial sub-Pantheon than an independent allied Pantheon.

### Takamagahara
[[Takamagahara]] is the Shinto Pantheon's principal Heavenly Realm and the center of its Divine government.
The Realm houses [[Amaterasu]]'s court and a large concentration of Shinto Gods. During the Demonic War, Amaterasu was described as commanding hundreds of Gods from Takamagahara.[1]
The Realm serves as the political and spiritual counterpart to the Japanese [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]]. While Takamagahara represents the Heavenly and primarily Positive side of the Pantheon, the Underworld manages the Dead and supernatural functions associated with Death.

### Japanese Underworld
The Shinto Pantheon possesses its own [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] responsible for receiving Souls connected to the Japanese Divine system.
[[Izanami-no-Mikoto]] served as its Ruler and held authority over the Japanese Dead. Her refusal or inability to continue performing her responsibilities during the Demonic War disrupted the normal passage of Souls and forced the remaining Gods to maintain the afterlife system themselves.[1]
The instability of the Underworld became one of the most severe administrative problems facing [[Amaterasu]] after her civil war against Izanami.
The Pantheon's Death-aligned Gods, including Shinigami, are associated with this side of its Divine structure.

### Craftsmanship and Economy
One of the Shinto Pantheon's most important non-combat specialties is Divine craftsmanship.
Although [[Amaterasu]] admitted that the Pantheon could not surpass [[Hephaestus]] of the [[Greek Pantheon]] or the Dwarven smiths associated with the [[Norse Pantheon]], she considered the Shinto Gods immediately below those two great crafting traditions.[1]
Unlike the Greek and Norse Pantheons, whose Divine artisans historically created equipment primarily for their own factions, the Shinto Pantheon actively sold Divine equipment to outsiders. This created a major commercial industry through which the Pantheon accumulated wealth, resources and foreign materials.[1]
The Shinto forging system was particularly difficult to reproduce because several different Divine authorities were combined during production.
- [[Amatsumara]] contributed the Divine authority of the Forge.
- [[Sukunabikona]] used the authority of Culture to protect completed items from being easily analyzed or copied by rival Gods and craftsmen.
- Amaterasu Elderblood supplied her Flames of the Sun as an exceptionally powerful Divine furnace.[1]
The combined involvement of a God-Queen, a God of Culture and a God of the Forge allowed the Pantheon to produce weapons and artifacts of exceptional quality.[1]
This industry was valuable enough that advanced Shinto weapons and supplies became the payment Amaterasu offered Haruna's faction in exchange for protecting Japan after the Demonic War.[1]

### Relationship with Dragon's Nest
The relationship between the Shinto Pantheon and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction developed gradually rather than beginning with direct subordination.
[[Amaterasu]] first became interested in establishing a permanent alliance with Victor because she recognized the value of connecting the Shinto Pantheon to the rapidly expanding political and military network surrounding him.[1]
Even during her negotiations with Haruna, Amaterasu openly suggested that marrying Victor could permanently link the Shinto Pantheon, Noble Vampires and Youkai through a single political relationship.[1]
Her interest became significantly more important after Victor destroyed the [[Greek Pantheon]], evolved further as a Dragon and established [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]]. By the Second [[Supernatural Gathering]], Amaterasu personally attended alongside [[Tsukuyomi]] and Susanoo.[7]
Amaterasu later travelled directly to Dragon's Nest and formally offered Victor a marriage alliance.[4]
Victor immediately made the imbalance between their positions clear. The Shinto Pantheon no longer possessed resources, military support or political influence that Victor genuinely required. Amaterasu herself recognized that Dragon's Nest did not need her Pantheon; rather, the Shinto Pantheon needed Victor.[4]
Amaterasu nevertheless wanted a position closer to Victor than that of an ordinary subordinate. She offered herself as his wife and additionally offered the preserved Divine Essence associated with the Creation authorities of her parents, [[Izanagi]] and [[Izanami-no-Mikoto]], as an investment in Victor's future.[4]
Victor accepted the arrangement and established a [[Soul Marriage]] with Amaterasu. She subsequently became a member of Clan Elderblood and one of Victor's wives while continuing to serve as Queen of the Shinto Gods.[4]
The political consequences extended beyond the marriage itself. By the beginning of the [[Imperial Calendar]], the Shinto Pantheon was formally incorporated into the [[Elderblood Empire]] while retaining internal autonomy.[5]

### Imperial Era
Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ascension as Emperor of Humanity, all major supernatural powers on Earth were incorporated into the [[Elderblood Empire]].[5]
The Shinto Pantheon was one of the Pantheons given comparatively favorable treatment because of its existing relationship with Victor and [[Amaterasu]].
Rather than destroying its local institutions, Victor preserved its internal government and allowed Amaterasu and the Shinto Gods to continue managing their own Divine affairs.
The conditions of this autonomy were straightforward:
- the Pantheon may maintain self-governance;
- its Gods and internal hierarchy may continue functioning;
- its history, culture and Divine institutions remain intact;
- but the Pantheon belongs to the Elderblood Empire;
- and it must respond whenever the Empire summons it.[5]
When the [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]] was later annexed, Victor explicitly stated that it would receive the same treatment as the Shinto Pantheon, confirming that this arrangement had become an established Imperial model for powerful subordinate Pantheons.[5]
The Shinto Pantheon therefore survives not as an independent state but as one of the Empire's major self-governing Divine territories.

### Known Members

#### Ruling Family
- [[Amaterasu]] Elderblood — God-Queen of the Shinto Pantheon; Goddess of the Sun, Light, Day and Sky; ruler of [[Takamagahara]]; member of Clan Elderblood and wife of [[Victor Elderblood]].[1][4]
- [[Izanagi]] ✝ — Ancient Creator God associated with Creation and Life; father of Amaterasu, [[Tsukuyomi]] and Susanoo.
- [[Izanami-no-Mikoto]] — Creator Goddess and ruler associated with the Japanese [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]]; mother of Amaterasu, Tsukuyomi and Susanoo. Her war against Amaterasu destabilized the Pantheon during the Demonic War.[6][1]
- Tsukuyomi — God of the Moon and one of the principal children of Izanagi and Izanami. He suffered catastrophic injuries during the Demonic War and temporarily entered a prolonged Divine sleep.[1]
- Susanoo — God associated with the Sea, Lightning, Thunder and Storms and one of the major combat Gods of the Pantheon.

#### Major Gods
- [[Inari-Omikami]] — Major Fox Goddess associated with the Sun, Elements and Space.
- [[Takemikazuchi]] — God associated with Thunder and one of [[Takamagahara]]'s powerful warrior Gods.
- [[Sakuya-hime]] — Goddess associated with Beauty.
- [[Amatsumara]] — God of the Forge and one of the principal Divine craftsmen of the Pantheon.[1]
- [[Sukunabikona]] — God of Culture whose Divine authority is used to protect Shinto-crafted items from being easily copied or analyzed.[1]
- [[Ebisu]] ✝ — God associated with Fortune, Luck and Fishermen.

#### Underworld and Associated Members
- Shinigami — God associated with Death and the Pantheon's [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] system.
- [[Yomi-Hime]] — Queen of the Tengu, former wife of [[Tsukuyomi]] and Divine messenger associated with the Shinto Gods. She commanded Tengu scouts and was assigned by [[Amaterasu]] to cooperate with Haruna's faction.[1]

#### Lesser and Minor Gods
The named Gods represent only a tiny fraction of the complete Shinto Pantheon. The total number of Divine Beings connected to Japanese Shinto worship exceeds one million according to Ruby's estimate.[2]
Most of these Beings are Lesser or Minor Gods associated with narrower concepts, locations, professions, natural phenomena, families, traditions and cultural functions.

### Relations

#### Youkai
Historically strained. The Gods and Youkai frequently possessed conflicting interests, and several Youkai resented Divine interference in their communities. The relationship temporarily improved when [[Amaterasu]] hired Haruna's forces to defend Japan during the Pantheon's post-war weakness.[1]

#### Dragon's Nest
Initially a prospective political ally before becoming the Shinto Pantheon's superior Divine faction. [[Amaterasu]]'s marriage to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] created a direct connection between the two Pantheons, while later Imperial annexation formally placed Shinto beneath Victor's authority.[4][5]

#### Elderblood Empire
The Pantheon's current sovereign authority. Shinto retains self-government but is obligated to respond to Imperial commands.[5]

#### Hindu Pantheon
Historically one of the Shinto Pantheon's neighboring major Pantheons. Both were later annexed into the [[Elderblood Empire]], with the [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]] explicitly receiving the same autonomous-subordinate political arrangement previously established for Shinto.[5]

#### Greek and Norse Pantheons
The Shinto Pantheon historically competed indirectly with them in Divine craftsmanship. [[Amaterasu]] acknowledged that [[Hephaestus]] and the Norse Dwarves surpassed Shinto's smithing industry, although Shinto remained one of Earth's foremost producers of Divine equipment.[1]
