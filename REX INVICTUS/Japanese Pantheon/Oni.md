**[[Oni]]** are Japanese ogres or demons (yōkai), shown with horns, red or blue skin and iron clubs. They guard hell and punish the dead in Buddhist tradition, and at Setsubun people throw roasted soybeans shouting "Oni wa soto!" ("Demons out!"). [[Kagura Kuroyami]] of the [[Japanese Pantheon]] is an Oni. See also [[Monsters (Japanese)]].

Oni are a powerful race of demons native to Japan. They are one of the races [[Dr. John Dee|Dee]] trapped in Alcatraz with the intent of releasing them into [[San Francisco]]. [[Sophie Newman]] is able to identify what they are through the [[Zephaniah|Witch of Endor]]'s memories, indicating she may have had an encounter with them in the past.
