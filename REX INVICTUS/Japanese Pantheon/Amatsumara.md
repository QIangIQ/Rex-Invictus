### Appearances

#### First Mention
Chapter 672
Amatsumara is a Third Generation God belonging to the [[Shinto Pantheon]] and the Pantheon's God of the Forge. He is one of the Divine craftsmen responsible for the Shinto Pantheon's unusually sophisticated weapons and supernatural equipment. Although he has not received a direct combat appearance, his work is referenced during the Demonic War, where the Shinto Pantheon's equipment is described as being produced through the combined craftsmanship of its God of Culture and God of the Forge, with [[Amaterasu]] supplying her Flames of the Sun as the furnace used during production.[1]
Unlike combat-oriented Gods such as [[Takemikazuchi]], Susanoo or [[Tsukuyomi]], Amatsumara's known role within the Pantheon is primarily technological and artisanal. His Divinity is associated with the Forge, giving him Divine authority over forging and the supernatural creation of equipment. The products created through the Shinto Pantheon's Divine forge are sufficiently advanced that Amaterasu openly considers their craftsmanship superior to that of most Pantheons, with only specialists such as [[Hephaestus]] of the [[Greek Pantheon]] and the Dwarven craftsmen of the [[Norse Pantheon]] being acknowledged as superior competitors.[1]

### Physical Appearance
Amatsumara has not received a canonical physical description in the novel. His height, build, hair, eyes, clothing and any distinctive Divine manifestation remain unknown because his first known story reference concerns his role as the [[Shinto Pantheon]]'s God of the Forge rather than a direct on-page appearance.[1]
The image currently associated with Amatsumara on the wiki should consequently be treated as an artistic interpretation rather than a confirmed canonical design.

### Personality
Amatsumara has not received direct dialogue or sufficient individual characterization to establish a detailed personality. Nothing in the currently available material confirms specific traits such as arrogance, aggression, humility or eccentricity.
What can be established is that he performs an important functional role within the [[Shinto Pantheon]] as one of the Gods responsible for producing its supernatural equipment. The Pantheon's forging system is important enough to support both its own Divine population and trade with outside supernatural factions, indicating that Amatsumara's work contributes directly to the Pantheon's economy, military logistics and technological reputation.[1]

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Amatsumara is first referenced during the later stages of the Demonic War, when the [[Shinto Pantheon]] was suffering from severe internal instability. [[Amaterasu]] explained that her Pantheon had been forced to divide its resources between the ongoing war, protecting Japan and maintaining the Shinto afterlife system after [[Izanami-no-Mikoto]] abandoned her responsibilities as its Ruler. Numerous Lesser Gods had been badly injured, while even major figures such as [[Tsukuyomi]] had fallen into a state that could require centuries of recovery.[1]
Unable to personally devote the Pantheon's remaining forces entirely to protecting Japan, Amaterasu negotiated with Haruna and the Youkai faction. Part of the arrangement involved supplying them with high-quality weapons and resources produced by the Shinto Pantheon. The equipment impressed Haruna's faction because of the extraordinary quality of its materials and craftsmanship.[1]
During this discussion, the novel explains that although the Shinto Pantheon did not surpass the [[Greek Pantheon]] and Norse craftsmen at the absolute highest level of artifact construction, its own forge was exceptionally advanced and difficult for outsiders to reproduce. Its equipment was produced through the cooperation of a God of Culture and a God of the Forge, while Amaterasu herself contributed her Flames of the Sun as the furnace through which the materials were worked.[1]
As the Shinto Pantheon's God of the Forge, Amatsumara represents the forging component of this system. His craftsmanship therefore contributed indirectly to the Pantheon's wartime strategy by supplying supernatural equipment to the Youkai who would help defend Japan while the Gods dealt with their other crises.[1]
The God of Culture participating in the process also placed Divine protection upon the completed equipment, making the underlying craftsmanship difficult for other Forge Gods to copy or analyze. This anti-analysis protection belongs to the God of Culture's contribution and should not automatically be attributed to Amatsumara's personal Forge Divinity.[1]
Amatsumara himself is not shown entering the battlefield during this period, and no direct fight involving him has been depicted.

### Skills & Abilities
Amatsumara is a genuine Third Generation God whose principal known specialization is the Forge. His known abilities should therefore be separated between the normal characteristics of Divine existence and the craftsmanship specifically associated with his Divinity. The novel has not shown him fighting, so combat techniques that have never been demonstrated should not be assigned solely because he is a God.

#### Divine Physiology
- God Physiology: Amatsumara is a genuine Third Generation Shinto God and possesses the supernatural existence associated with Divine beings.
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: As a God, Amatsumara possesses physical capabilities beyond ordinary mortals and conventional Humans. His exact strength, speed and durability have never been individually demonstrated.
- [[Divine Energy]] Manipulation: Amatsumara possesses Divine Energy, the supernatural Energy used by Gods to support their Divinities and Divine abilities.
- Divine Soul: As a genuine God, his existence is founded upon a Divine Soul capable of containing and expressing Divinity.
- Agelessness / Divine Longevity: Amatsumara has existed for an immense period and does not age according to ordinary mortal limitations.
- Divine Regeneration: Gods possess supernatural recovery beyond ordinary mortal beings, although sufficient damage to their Soul, Divinity or Divine existence can still kill them.
- [[Divine Presence]]: Amatsumara possesses the supernatural presence inherent to Divine beings, although the intensity of his individual aura has not been shown.
- Blessing Bestowal: Gods can express aspects of their Divine authority through Blessings corresponding to their domains. No specific Blessing personally granted by Amatsumara has yet been depicted.
- Curse Bestowal: Divine authority can likewise produce negative supernatural effects related to a God's Concept, although Amatsumara has not demonstrated an individual Curse.

#### Forge Divinity
- Divinity — Forge: Amatsumara's principal confirmed Divine Concept is the Forge. As the [[Shinto Pantheon]]'s God of the Forge, his Divine authority is centered upon supernatural forging and the creation of equipment.[1]
- Divine Forging: Amatsumara can apply Divine power to the forging process, allowing the Shinto Pantheon to produce weapons and equipment far beyond ordinary mundane craftsmanship.[1]
- Supernatural Blacksmithing: His specialization allows him to work with supernatural materials and produce equipment intended for supernatural beings.
- Artifact Crafting: Amatsumara participates in the production of supernatural artifacts and weapons distributed by the Shinto Pantheon.[1]
- Divine Artifact Craftsmanship: The equipment produced through the Pantheon's Divine forge possesses quality high enough to impress experienced supernatural warriors and craftsmen.[1]
- Weapon Crafting: Amatsumara's Forge authority includes the production of supernatural weapons. The shipment presented to Haruna's faction contained high-quality weapons made through the Shinto Pantheon's Divine crafting system.[1]
- Equipment Crafting: His role is not limited to one particular weapon category; the Shinto Pantheon produces supernatural equipment and supplies through its forging infrastructure.[1]
- Supernatural Material Manipulation (Crafting): As a Divine blacksmith, Amatsumara can work supernatural materials that ordinary craftsmen would be incapable of properly shaping into Divine-quality equipment.
- [[Divine Energy]] Infusion (Crafting): His Forge Divinity allows Divine power to be incorporated into the creation process rather than producing merely mundane weapons.
- High-Level Craftsmanship: [[Amaterasu]] describes the Shinto Pantheon's crafting capabilities as exceptional. Although she acknowledges that [[Hephaestus]] and the Norse Dwarves possess greater reputations at the highest levels of forging, she explicitly rejects the idea that the Shinto Gods are mediocre craftsmen.[1]

#### Collaborative Divine Forging
The [[Shinto Pantheon]]'s artifact production is notable because its strongest equipment is not presented as the work of one God acting alone. Instead, several Divine specializations are combined during construction.[1]
- Forge Authority — Amatsumara: As the God of the Forge, Amatsumara supplies the Divine craftsmanship responsible for forging the equipment.[1]
- Cultural Divine Protection — [[Sukunabikona]]: The God associated with Culture contributes Divine protection preventing completed equipment from being easily copied or analyzed by rival Forge Gods. This protection should be treated as a collaborative property of the Pantheon's crafting system rather than one of Amatsumara's personal powers.[1]
- Solar Furnace — [[Amaterasu]]: Amaterasu contributes her Flames of the Sun as the furnace used during the forging process, adding the power of a God-Queen to the production system.[1]
- Anti-Reproduction Craftsmanship: Equipment created through this cooperation is exceptionally difficult for outsiders to reproduce perfectly because of the Divine protections applied during construction.[1]
- Advanced Artifact Quality: The combined involvement of a God of the Forge, God of Culture and God-Queen results in equipment considered far beyond ordinary supernatural craftsmanship.[1]

#### Crafting Knowledge
- Blacksmithing Mastery: Amatsumara possesses the technical knowledge expected from a God whose Divine identity is centered upon forging.
- Metallurgy: His role necessarily involves supernatural understanding of metals and forge-compatible materials.
- Weapon Design: He possesses expertise in constructing weapons intended for supernatural combatants.
- Artifact Engineering: The [[Shinto Pantheon]]'s equipment demonstrates the ability to integrate supernatural properties into physical objects.[1]
- Divine Material Processing: Amatsumara is capable of participating in the processing of materials using [[Divine Energy]] and supernatural heat sources such as [[Amaterasu]]'s Solar Flames.[1]
- Mass Equipment Production: The Shinto Pantheon is capable of supplying substantial quantities of weapons and materials to allied supernatural factions, indicating that its Divine craftsmen can contribute to more than isolated one-of-a-kind creations.[1]

#### Power
Amatsumara's direct combat strength is currently unknown. His status as a Third Generation God places him above ordinary mortal and lesser supernatural beings in terms of existential quality, but the novel has never depicted him fighting or provided an individual God-level ranking for him.
His importance instead lies in his specialization. Forge Gods can dramatically increase the practical military power of a Pantheon by producing weapons and artifacts usable by other combatants. The narrative repeatedly demonstrates that equipment can change the outcome of battles between Divine beings, meaning Amatsumara's value to the [[Shinto Pantheon]] should not be measured only through his personal fighting ability.
The story nevertheless explicitly places the Shinto Pantheon's forging tradition below the greatest known specialists of the Greek and Norse Pantheons. [[Amaterasu]] states that if not for [[Hephaestus]] and the Norse Dwarves, the Shinto Pantheon could potentially stand first in matters of supernatural craftsmanship.[1] This establishes Amatsumara and the Shinto crafting system as exceptionally capable without falsely equating him individually with Hephaestus.

### Relationships

#### Amaterasu Elderblood
[[Amaterasu]] is the God-Queen of the [[Shinto Pantheon]] and contributes directly to its forging system. Rather than leaving artifact production entirely to Amatsumara, she provides her Flames of the Sun as the furnace used when creating high-quality Shinto equipment.[1]
Their cooperation demonstrates that Divine crafting within the Pantheon is a coordinated process involving multiple Gods rather than the work of a single isolated blacksmith.

#### Sukunabikona
[[Sukunabikona]], the Shinto God associated with Culture, works within the same Divine production system as Amatsumara. While Amatsumara's Forge Divinity governs the actual craftsmanship, the God of Culture contributes Divine protection that prevents outsiders and rival Forge Gods from easily copying or analyzing the resulting equipment.[1]
The two Gods' powers therefore complement one another: Amatsumara creates the equipment while Sukunabikona helps protect the knowledge and techniques incorporated into it.

#### Shinto Pantheon
Amatsumara serves as one of the specialized Gods whose Divinity supports the wider infrastructure of the [[Shinto Pantheon]]. His role is particularly important because the Pantheon does not merely produce equipment for itself; it is capable of supplying and trading supernatural goods with outside factions.[1]
During the Demonic War, these crafting capabilities allowed [[Amaterasu]] to strengthen the Youkai faction through shipments of weapons and resources while the Shinto Gods remained occupied by the war and the crisis affecting their afterlife system.[1]
