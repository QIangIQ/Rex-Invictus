**[[Izanami-no-Mikoto]]**, commonly referred to as Izanami, is a First Generation Primordial Goddess, one of the ancient Creator Gods of the Shinto Pantheon, the sister-wife of Izanagi, mother of Amaterasu Elderblood, Tsukuyomi and Susanoo, and the former active ruler responsible for the Japanese Underworld. (Lore adapted from *My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** This material is adapted into the Rex Invictus world at the lowest priority: Rex Invictus wins any conflict, then the Flamel material, then MTWABV. Events of the MTWABV timeline (Victor's conquests, the fall of the pantheons, the [[Elderblood Empire]]) are distant background or rumor unless a chapter puts them on the page. See [[MTWABV Series#Integration rules]].

### Appearances

#### First Mention
Chapter 668
Izanami-no-Mikoto, commonly referred to as Izanami, is a First Generation Primordial Goddess, one of the ancient Creator Gods of the [[Shinto Pantheon]], the sister-wife of [[Izanagi]], mother of [[Amaterasu]] Elderblood, [[Tsukuyomi]] and Susanoo, and the former active ruler responsible for the Japanese [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]].
Together with Izanagi, Izanami is associated with the Creation authority from which much of the ancient Shinto Divine lineage originated. This connection is directly confirmed much later when Amaterasu possesses an Essence associated with the Creation powers of Izanagi and Izanami and offers it to [[Victor Elderblood]] during their marriage negotiations.[1]
By the modern era, Izanami's relationship with her daughter Amaterasu had deteriorated into open conflict. Izanami was sealed within the Japanese Underworld and eventually became the opposing center of a Shinto civil war against Amaterasu. Amaterasu's faction ultimately won, although the conflict destabilized the Japanese supernatural system and allowed Demons from Hell to escape into the Human World.[2]
Despite remaining alive after her defeat, Izanami ceased properly fulfilling her responsibilities as ruler of the Japanese Hell. The resulting failure of the Underworld's Soul-processing system became serious enough that souls devoted to the Shinto Gods could no longer properly pass on, forcing Amaterasu to search for a replacement capable of inheriting the position of Ruler.[3]
Izanami remained sealed even after the Shinto Pantheon became integrated into Victor's expanding civilization. In the later Imperial period, she unexpectedly developed a comparatively good relationship with [[Hinata Elderblood]], Amaterasu and Victor's daughter, despite the comparatively recent war between Izanami and Amaterasu.[4]

### Physical Appearance
The novel has not yet provided a sufficiently detailed canonical description of Izanami's physical appearance.
Although she is repeatedly discussed as one of the ancient Shinto Primordial Goddesses and remains alive within her seal, the chapters currently establishing her modern status do not provide enough information to reliably define her hair color, eye color, clothing, height or other physical characteristics.
Her current artwork should therefore be treated as an artistic interpretation rather than a complete canonical depiction.

### Personality
Very little of Izanami's personality has been shown directly on-page, and most information currently comes through the reactions and statements of other characters.
She is sufficiently independent and defiant to abandon the responsibilities expected of her as ruler of the Japanese [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] despite the severe consequences this caused for the [[Shinto Pantheon]]. [[Amaterasu]] stated that Izanami had simply decided that continuing to perform those duties was no longer a good idea, and her daughter's visible irritation while discussing the matter demonstrates that this decision created significant resentment between them.[3]
Izanami was also willing to oppose Amaterasu directly. Their disagreement escalated beyond a simple family dispute into a full Mythological civil war involving the Shinto supernatural structure. Amaterasu eventually emerged victorious, leaving Izanami sealed while the wider Pantheon dealt with the consequences of the conflict.[2]
However, Izanami's later behavior suggests that her hostility toward Amaterasu does not automatically extend toward the rest of her family. By the later Imperial era, [[Hinata Elderblood]], the daughter of Amaterasu and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], was one of the very few members of her mother's older Divine family with whom she willingly maintained a relationship. Hinata regularly visited the still-sealed Izanami, and the two surprisingly got along well enough that Amaterasu was unsure whether she should be pleased or concerned by their closeness.[4]
Because Izanami has not yet received a substantial direct scene from her own perspective, broader traits such as cruelty, benevolence, maternal affection or hatred should not be stated as established fact until they are directly shown.

### History
Izanami belongs to the oldest known generation of Shinto Divinity. Alongside [[Izanagi]], she is recognized as a First Generation Primordial God and one of the foundational Creator Gods of the Pantheon.
Her Creation authority is directly connected with Izanagi's. Long after Izanagi's death, an Essence derived from the Creation powers of both deities remained valuable enough for [[Amaterasu]] to offer it to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] as one of the principal benefits of a permanent alliance between him and the [[Shinto Pantheon]].[1]
Izanami also became ruler of the Japanese [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]]. This was not merely a ceremonial title. Amaterasu explicitly confirmed that Izanami was responsible for a system through which souls devoted to the Shinto Gods could properly pass on after death.[3]
At some point before or during the Demonic War era, Izanami was sealed within Hell. The exact circumstances surrounding the original sealing, who performed it and how long she had already been confined have not yet been fully explained.
Her later refusal to continue performing her responsibilities as ruler destabilized the system and contributed directly to the crisis that engulfed Japan during the Demonic War.[2][3]

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Izanami was first mentioned during the wider supernatural crisis surrounding the Demonic War. While [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was absent, news reached his family that a Mythological War had erupted in Japan between [[Amaterasu]] Elderblood and her mother Izanami, who was described as having been sealed within the Japanese Hell. Amaterasu had recently emerged victorious, but the conflict produced serious collateral consequences: Demons escaped from Hell into the Human World and Japanese Yokai forces under Haruna were forced to organize squads to exterminate them.[2]
The conflict did not simply concern which Goddess controlled the Pantheon. Izanami was also the active Ruler of the Japanese [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]], and her withdrawal from those responsibilities severely damaged the supernatural system responsible for managing deceased souls. Amaterasu later explained that souls devoted to the Shinto Gods since ancient times were unable to properly pass on because her mother had decided to stop carrying out her duties. With several Shinto Gods already injured or incapacitated by the war, maintaining Hell while simultaneously protecting Japan became practically impossible.[3]
Amaterasu consequently began searching for a successor worthy of inheriting the position of Ruler while seeking military support from Haruna's Yokai. The absence of a functioning Underworld ruler therefore became one of the principal reasons the [[Shinto Pantheon]] was forced into a more pragmatic alliance with supernatural groups it had historically treated with suspicion.[3]
Izanami remained alive after losing the civil war. The story does not establish that Amaterasu killed her, nor does it establish that Izanami underwent reincarnation. She continued to exist under confinement.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Izanami did not personally appear during [[Amaterasu]]'s marriage negotiation with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], but her Divine legacy became directly relevant.
When Victor asked what he would gain from marrying the Shinto God-Queen, Amaterasu offered him an Essence connected to the Creation powers of [[Izanagi]] and Izanami. Amaterasu considered the Essence useful because Victor, despite his enormous ability to create and manipulate supernatural phenomena, was not fundamentally a conventional God of Creation.[1]
Victor accepted the value of the resource but did not immediately consume it. After his [[Soul Marriage]] with Amaterasu formed, he returned the Essence to her for safekeeping until he decided to use it.[1]
The existence of this Essence directly confirms that Izanami's Creation authority was substantial enough to remain an important Divine resource even in the era of Dragon Gods and increasingly powerful members of Dragon's Nest.

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Following [[Amaterasu]]'s transformation into a Blood Dragon Dragon Goddess, the relative power hierarchy of the Shinto Divine family changed dramatically. Amaterasu's new existence became stronger than both of her First Generation Primordial parents, including Izanami.[5]
This is one of the clearest direct comparisons involving Izanami's strength. First Generation status still identified her as an older and foundational Primordial Goddess, but Amaterasu's Draconic Race Change allowed a Second Generation Goddess to qualitatively surpass the power of her mother and father.[5]
Izanami nevertheless remained alive and sealed rather than being executed. By Chapter 1053, Amaterasu's daughter with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had begun interacting with her grandmother. [[Hinata Elderblood]] was generally indifferent or disdainful toward most of the older Gods of [[Takamagahara]], regarding them as weak and obsequious, yet Izanami became the notable exception. The two unexpectedly got along well despite Izanami's comparatively recent war against Hinata's mother.[4]
Amaterasu remained cautious about the relationship because Izanami was still sealed, but Victor did not prevent Hinata from visiting her. Amaterasu therefore allowed the contact to continue while keeping watch over the situation.[4]

### Relationships

#### Izanagi
[[Izanagi]] was Izanami's brother and husband. Together, the two are identified as ancient Shinto Creator Gods whose combined Creation powers remained relevant long after Izanagi's death.[1]
The Essence later possessed by [[Amaterasu]] drew upon the Creation authority of both Izanagi and Izanami rather than either deity alone.[1]

#### Amaterasu Elderblood
[[Amaterasu]] Elderblood is Izanami's daughter and later political enemy.
Their modern relationship deteriorated severely enough to produce a Mythological civil war within Shinto. Amaterasu ultimately won the conflict, while Izanami remained sealed within the Japanese [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]].[2]
Amaterasu was openly angered by Izanami's refusal to continue performing her duties as ruler of Hell because the decision prevented Shinto-aligned souls from properly passing on and forced the already weakened Pantheon to manage the resulting crisis itself.[3]
After Amaterasu's Blood Dragon Race Change, she became substantially stronger than Izanami despite belonging to the younger Second Generation of [[Primordial Gods]].[5]

#### Tsukuyomi
[[Tsukuyomi]] is one of Izanami's sons and a Second Generation Primordial God associated with the Moon.
The novel has not yet provided a detailed modern interaction between Izanami and Tsukuyomi.

#### Susanoo
Susanoo is one of Izanami's sons and the Primordial God of Storms.
As with [[Tsukuyomi]], the modern novel has not yet shown enough direct interaction between Susanoo and Izanami to define their current personal relationship.

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] became Izanami's son-in-law through his [[Soul Marriage]] with [[Amaterasu]].[1]
Victor also became the intended recipient of the Essence associated with Izanami and [[Izanagi]]'s Creation powers, although he initially returned it to Amaterasu rather than immediately absorbing it.[1]
By the later Imperial era, Victor did not prevent his daughter Hinata from visiting the sealed Izanami, indicating that he did not consider contact between the two sufficiently dangerous to prohibit it.[4]

#### Hinata Elderblood
[[Hinata Elderblood]] is Izanami's granddaughter through [[Amaterasu]] and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
Despite Hinata's general disdain toward most of the old Gods of [[Takamagahara]], she surprisingly developed a good relationship with Izanami. Chapter 1053 specifically identifies Izanami as the one older Shinto relative with whom Hinata genuinely got along.[4]
Amaterasu remained uncertain about whether the relationship was desirable because of her recent war with Izanami, but allowed the visits to continue while monitoring them.[4]

### Skills & Abilities
General abilities belonging to Gods and First Generation [[Primordial Gods]] are covered on their respective species and classification pages. Izanami's personal section should remain limited to powers and authorities directly confirmed for her.
- Creation Divinity: Izanami possesses Divine authority over Creation. Chapter 891 directly identifies an Essence derived from the Creation powers of both Izanami and [[Izanagi]].[1]
- [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] Authority: Izanami was the Ruler responsible for the Japanese Hell. Her failure to continue performing those duties prevented souls devoted to the Shinto Gods from properly passing on, confirming that her rulership carried functional supernatural authority over the Underworld's Soul system.[3]
- Soul Administration: As Ruler of the Japanese Underworld, Izanami's position was directly tied to the system through which deceased souls were processed and passed onward. The story has not yet shown her personally manipulating individual Souls in combat, so broader Soul Manipulation should not be listed beyond this confirmed administrative authority.[3]
- Primordial God Physiology: Izanami is explicitly a First Generation Primordial God. Her general racial capabilities are covered under Gods and Primordial God rather than repeated here.
The titles Goddess of Death, Goddess of Earth and Goddess of Femininity are associated with Izanami's Divine identity, but the novel has not yet demonstrated enough specific applications of those authorities to construct separate ability lists for them without speculation.

### Power
Izanami is a First Generation Primordial God, placing her among the ancient foundational Gods of the [[Shinto Pantheon]].
Her actual modern combat feats are extremely limited because her civil war with [[Amaterasu]] occurs largely off-screen. Chapter 668 confirms that Amaterasu's side ultimately won the conflict, but it does not establish that Amaterasu defeated Izanami alone in a straightforward one-on-one battle.[2]
Therefore, the result should be recorded as:
Amaterasu's faction > Izanami's faction during the Shinto civil war[2]
rather than:
Amaterasu individually defeated Izanami
unless a later chapter directly confirms that specific duel.
A clearer individual comparison becomes available after Amaterasu's Race Change. Chapter 938 explicitly states that the transformed Dragon Goddess Amaterasu had become much stronger than both of her parents, who were First Generation [[Primordial Gods]].[5]
Thus:
Izanami-no-Mikoto
First Generation Primordial Goddess
↓
Ancient Shinto Creator Goddess
↓
Former Active Ruler of Japanese Hell
↓

↓

This does not diminish Izanami's status as an Original/First Generation Primordial God; it demonstrates the qualitative advantage created by Amaterasu's later Dragon transformation.

### Status and Imprisonment
Izanami is alive.
Chapter 668 describes her as having been sealed within Hell during the period of the Shinto civil war.[2] Chapter 1053 later confirms that she remained sealed even after the establishment of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later family and civilization, because Hinata visited her while the seal was still in place.[4]
The story has not yet fully explained:
- who originally sealed Izanami;
- the exact mechanism of the seal;
- whether she can leave under specific conditions;
- whether [[Amaterasu]] maintains the seal;
- or whether Izanami is currently capable of breaking it herself.
She should therefore be listed as Alive (Sealed/Imprisoned) rather than Deceased.

### Role as Ruler of Japanese Hell
Izanami's rulership over the Japanese [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] was a functional cosmic responsibility rather than merely a ceremonial throne.
[[Amaterasu]] explained that souls devoted to the Shinto Gods depended upon the Underworld system to properly pass on. Once Izanami refused to continue performing her duties, the system stopped functioning correctly and the Gods were forced to compensate while searching for a replacement Ruler.[3]
This establishes three important facts:
1. Izanami was the legitimate Ruler responsible for Japanese Hell.
2. Her authority was directly tied to the movement or processing of Souls.
3. Her absence could not be casually replaced by an ordinary God.
Amaterasu specifically stated that the Pantheon was searching for someone worthy of the Ruler title, indicating that the position required more than administrative appointment.[3]
Izanami should therefore be treated as one of the setting's genuine Underworld Rulers alongside figures who hold comparable supernatural authority over afterlife realms.

### Creation Authority
Izanami and [[Izanagi]] are two of Shinto's foundational Creator Gods.
The most direct modern evidence of this power appears in Chapter 891 when [[Amaterasu]] offers [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] an Essence associated with their combined Creation powers.[1]
Amaterasu considered the Essence especially valuable because Victor, despite his extensive ability to create supernatural phenomena, did not possess the conventional foundation of a dedicated Creation God.
The continued existence and value of this Essence long after Izanagi's death demonstrates that the Creation authority of the two [[Primordial Gods]] was fundamental enough to be preserved as a usable Divine resource.
The exact applications of Izanami's personal Creation Divinity have not yet been shown directly. Abilities such as spontaneous life creation, matter generation, conceptual creation or unrestricted creation should therefore not be individually attributed to her until demonstrated.
