## Real-World Background (IRL)

Benzaiten is the Japanese goddess of music, eloquence, water and wealth, one of the Seven Lucky Gods, derived from the Hindu Sarasvati.

Benzaiten is an extremely powerful Elder and the master of Niten. She is worshipped by both the Japanese and Indian people as a river goddess. She is thought to be one of the [[Great Elders]] who ripped the land that would become [[Danu Talis]] off the sea floor.
She is seen in a flashback by Niten in [[The Necromancer]], where she appears as a small lady in traditional Japanese dress riding on the back of a large dragon.

### Abilities
- [[Immortality]]: Benzaiten is Immortal and does not age. Though an immortal may be poisoned or slain in battle, they may live for thousands or millions of years.
- Can Grant Immortality: Benzaiten can grant Immortality to [[Humani]].
