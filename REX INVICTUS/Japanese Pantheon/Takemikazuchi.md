Takemikazuchi is a Third Generation God, the God of Thunder, and one of the most powerful combatants of the [[Shinto Pantheon]]. Although his name was invoked long before his physical introduction, he made his direct debut during the coalition hunt against the [[Elder Gods Pantheon]], when [[Amaterasu]] Elderblood selected him and Susanoo as the two Shinto warriors sent to reinforce [[Victor Elderblood]]'s forces.[1]
Takemikazuchi possesses authority over thunder and lightning strong enough for practitioners to invoke and borrow a manifestation of his power even while he is absent.[2] His combat reputation is considerably greater than that of an ordinary Third Generation God: he was already regarded as one of the major candidates capable of representing the Gods at a [[Supernatural Gathering]],[3] Amaterasu later used him alongside Susanoo as a benchmark when estimating the power of Morgana,[4] and he was explicitly described as one of the Shinto Pantheon's two most powerful warriors during the Elder God hunt.[1]

### Physical Appearance
Takemikazuchi's detailed physical appearance has not yet been individually described in the novel. During his first direct appearance, he was among the powerful Gods and Angels assembled for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s hunt and was fully equipped with his Divine Gear.[1]

### Personality
Only a limited amount of Takemikazuchi's individual personality has been shown. He appears substantially more disciplined than highly confrontational warriors such as [[Thor]]. When Thor openly suggested that the assembled thunder Gods determine which of them was strongest, Takemikazuchi did not escalate the challenge, remaining focused on the larger operation.[1]
He is nevertheless a proud warrior. Like Susanoo, Thor and [[Taranis]], Takemikazuchi was affected by the overwhelming presence of [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood and later by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] using Aphrodite's power. Rather than dismissing the incident, the Shinto Gods considered training themselves to resist similar Divine charm effects in the future, demonstrating a willingness to acknowledge and correct weaknesses exposed in battle.[1]
Takemikazuchi is also sufficiently trusted by [[Amaterasu]] to be deployed as one of the [[Shinto Pantheon]]'s principal representatives in a conflict involving threats as dangerous as the Elder Gods. His selection alongside Susanoo rather than one of the Pantheon's countless lesser deities reflects both his combat strength and his position within Amaterasu's military hierarchy.[1]

### Synopsis

#### A New Pillar
Takemikazuchi was first referenced indirectly long before he physically entered the story. During an incident in [[New York]], a supernatural practitioner called upon him by name and requested that he lend her the power of his lightning. Her body was immediately enveloped in lightning, allowing her to pursue her target at supernatural speed and demonstrating that Takemikazuchi's Divine authority could be invoked remotely by individuals capable of accessing his power.[2]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
While discussions were being held concerning the approaching gathering of supernatural powers, Takemikazuchi's reputation was mentioned again. When the possible representative of the Gods' faction was discussed, [[Scathach]] identified several of the divine world's principal powers who might obtain the position, with Takemikazuchi being named alongside figures such as [[Thor]], [[Shiva]] and [[Zeus]]. Even before his direct appearance, he was therefore already recognized outside Japan as one of the Gods sufficiently powerful and influential to potentially represent the Divine factions at an international supernatural gathering.[3]

#### Demonic War
The Demonic War devastated large portions of the [[Shinto Pantheon]]. Gods were forced to maintain spiritual systems normally governed through their Pantheon's hierarchy while several deities were injured or placed into extended recovery. During [[Amaterasu]]'s negotiations with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction, she evaluated the strength of the women accompanying him. When observing Morgana, Amaterasu judged her as being at least around the level of either her brother Susanoo or Takemikazuchi.[4]
This comparison established Takemikazuchi as one of Amaterasu's personal benchmarks for the Pantheon's strongest combat Gods. Rather than being counted among the numerous ordinary or Lesser Gods of Shinto, he stood in the upper combat hierarchy alongside Susanoo.[4]

#### King Among Kings
Following the [[Supernatural Gathering]] and the declaration of hostilities against the forces connected to [[New Dawn]], the [[Egyptian Pantheon]] and the [[Elder Gods Pantheon]], [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s coalition began what was effectively described as a hunt rather than a conventional war.[1]
The different Pantheons dispatched powerful representatives. The [[Heavenly Father]] sent [[Azrael]] and Ariel, the [[Celtic Pantheon]] sent [[Taranis]] and [[Cernuno]], while [[Amaterasu]] selected Susanoo and Takemikazuchi. The two were explicitly identified as two of the [[Shinto Pantheon]]'s most powerful warriors, making Amaterasu's intention to contribute serious military strength to Victor's operation obvious.[1]
Takemikazuchi arrived fully prepared for battle with his Divine Gear. The gathering contained an unusual concentration of thunder-related Gods: [[Thor]], Susanoo, Takemikazuchi and Taranis. Thor immediately became interested in discovering which of them was the strongest, although the others did not allow his competitive attitude to derail the mission.[1]
The coalition then encountered Victor, [[Scathach]], Jeanne, Morgana, Zaladrac, Rose and [[Aphrodite]]. The appearance of seven [[True Dragons]] immediately unsettled the assembled Gods, despite all of them being elite representatives of their respective Pantheons.[1]
Aphrodite's transformed existence proved particularly dangerous. Her passive beauty was powerful enough to heavily affect the male Gods without her intentionally using her authority. Victor awakened them using his killing intent before deliberately testing them again by reproducing Aphrodite's power himself. Takemikazuchi, Susanoo, Thor and Taranis were among the warriors unable to completely resist the effect. Following the demonstration, the Shinto side acknowledged the need to develop defenses against such forms of supernatural charm.[1]
Takemikazuchi subsequently participated as part of the coalition assembled for the extermination campaign against Victor's enemies. His deployment placed him among a comparatively small group of elite Gods and Angels trusted to operate in a conflict where Elder Gods were expected to appear.[1]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
By the later Imperial era, the political structure of Earth had changed completely. Pantheons such as the Shinto, Biblical and Hindu Pantheons continued to exist as distinct religious and Divine organizations, but they were subordinate to [[Victor Elderblood]]'s [[Elderblood Empire]]. They were not automatically considered part of the [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]] itself; Gods such as Susanoo were still identified as members of a foreign Pantheon despite serving beneath the Empire's greater authority.[5]
As a member of the [[Shinto Pantheon]] under [[Amaterasu]], Takemikazuchi therefore exists within the Empire's subordinate Divine hierarchy while remaining a Shinto God rather than a Dragon God directly belonging to Victor's personal Pantheon.[5]

### Skills & Abilities
- God Physiology: Takemikazuchi is a Third Generation God and possesses the supernatural physiology associated with Divine Beings. Gods are Higher Existences whose bodies, Souls and [[Divine Energy]] function on a fundamentally different level from ordinary mortals. As a major combat-oriented God, Takemikazuchi possesses immense physical strength, speed, durability, stamina, supernatural senses, longevity and regenerative capabilities.
- Divinity: Takemikazuchi possesses a Divine authority centered upon Thunder. His Divine nature grants him direct supernatural command over phenomena associated with his Divinity rather than requiring conventional [[Magic]] to reproduce them.[2][1]
- Thunder Manipulation: As the Shinto God of Thunder, Takemikazuchi can generate and control supernatural thunder through his Divinity.[1]
- Lightning Manipulation: Takemikazuchi's power can manifest directly as lightning. When a practitioner invoked his name and requested his power, she was immediately enveloped by lightning, confirming that electrical phenomena form a direct component of his Divine authority.[2]
- Divine Power Bestowal: Takemikazuchi is capable of allowing other supernatural practitioners to temporarily access his Divine power. A woman invoking him in Chapter 138 requested that he lend her his lightning, after which Takemikazuchi's power manifested around her body.[2] This establishes that his Divinity can be channeled through authorized or compatible users even when Takemikazuchi himself is not physically present.
- Empowerment: The manifestation of Takemikazuchi's lightning around another individual substantially enhanced her pursuit capabilities, covering her body in lightning while she moved at supernatural speed.[2]
- Divine Energy Manipulation: Like other Gods, Takemikazuchi possesses and manipulates Divine Energy, the higher form of supernatural Energy generated through Divine existence and connected to a God's Divinity.
- Superhuman Strength: Takemikazuchi is one of the [[Shinto Pantheon]]'s foremost warriors. His Divine body grants physical power vastly beyond ordinary supernatural beings, although an isolated maximum strength feat has not yet been shown.[1]
- Superhuman Speed and Reflexes: His status as one of the Shinto Pantheon's strongest Divine combatants grants him supernatural speed and reactions appropriate for battles between high-level Gods.[1]
- Enhanced Durability: As a combat-oriented God capable of being deployed against Elder Gods and their forces, Takemikazuchi possesses the durable Divine body expected of a major deity.[1]
- Regeneration: Divine Beings possess supernatural recovery capabilities and can survive injuries far beyond those tolerable to mortals.
- [[Immortality]]: As a God, Takemikazuchi is not subject to conventional Human aging and possesses an effectively indefinite natural lifespan. He can nevertheless be killed through sufficient supernatural or Soul-level destruction.
- [[Divine Presence]]: Takemikazuchi possesses the supernatural presence inherent to Gods. His standing is particularly high within Shinto: long before his physical debut, he was considered among the Divine powers capable of potentially representing the Gods' faction at a major supernatural gathering.[3]
- Elite Divine Combatant: Takemikazuchi is explicitly one of the two most powerful warriors of the Shinto Pantheon sent by [[Amaterasu]] during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s hunt, the other being Susanoo.[1] This places him far above the countless Lesser Gods belonging to the Pantheon and establishes combat as one of his primary specialties.
- Combat Scaling: Amaterasu used Takemikazuchi and Susanoo as reference points when evaluating Morgana's combat strength, judging Morgana's power as at least comparable to theirs. This establishes Takemikazuchi as one of the Pantheon's established high-level combat standards.[4]
- Divine Gear: Takemikazuchi possesses Divine Gear suitable for battle. When the coalition assembled for the Elder God hunt, Takemikazuchi and the other participating Divine warriors were fully armed with their Divine equipment.[1] The exact properties and name of Takemikazuchi's personal Divine Gear have not yet been revealed.
- Experienced Warrior: Amaterasu specifically chose Takemikazuchi for an operation involving the Elder Gods rather than deploying an ordinary Shinto deity. Combined with his description as one of the Pantheon's most powerful warriors, this indicates extensive combat experience and skill.[1]

### Power and Standing
Takemikazuchi should not be confused with a Primordial God. He is a Third Generation God, but generation alone does not determine a God's combat capability. Takemikazuchi is one of the exceptional Third Generation Gods whose specialization and experience place him among the major combatants of his Pantheon.
His established standing includes:
- Being considered one of the major Divine powers who could plausibly represent the Gods' faction during discussions surrounding the [[Supernatural Gathering]].[3]
- Being directly compared with Susanoo by [[Amaterasu]] when she evaluated another powerful supernatural combatant.[4]
- Being selected alongside Susanoo as one of the two most powerful Shinto warriors available to Amaterasu during the Elder God hunt.[1]
- Being deployed in the same multinational strike group as elite figures such as [[Thor]], [[Taranis]], [[Azrael]], Ariel and Susanoo.[1]
His exact maximum destructive capacity has not been independently demonstrated, meaning more specific claims such as planetary destruction or equivalence to a Primordial God should not be assigned to him without additional feats.

### Relationships

#### Amaterasu Elderblood
[[Amaterasu]] Elderblood is Takemikazuchi's God-Queen and the ruler of the [[Shinto Pantheon]]. Takemikazuchi serves beneath her authority and is one of the principal warriors she trusts for major external conflicts. During the Elder God campaign, Amaterasu personally selected Takemikazuchi and Susanoo as the Shinto Pantheon's representatives, deliberately sending two of her strongest combatants.[1]

#### Susanoo
Susanoo is another of the [[Shinto Pantheon]]'s greatest warriors and frequently serves as Takemikazuchi's closest comparison in combat standing. [[Amaterasu]] considered both Gods appropriate standards when evaluating Morgana's strength,[4] and the pair were later sent together to reinforce [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] during the Elder God hunt.[1]
Although Susanoo is a significantly older and more primordial Divine Being, Takemikazuchi's specialization as a combatant allows him to occupy the same upper military circle of the Shinto Pantheon.

#### Victor Elderblood
Takemikazuchi initially existed outside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction and met him directly during the hunt against the Elder Gods. He joined the multinational force organized around Victor as one of [[Amaterasu]]'s representatives.[1] In the much later Imperial era, Takemikazuchi's [[Shinto Pantheon]] became politically subordinate to Victor's Empire while remaining an independent Pantheon rather than being absorbed directly into Dragon's Nest.[5]

### Trivia
- Chapter 384 is instead an important early statement regarding his reputation, identifying Takemikazuchi among the Divine powers who could potentially become the Gods' representative at a [[Supernatural Gathering]].[3]
- Despite being a Third Generation God, Takemikazuchi is one of the [[Shinto Pantheon]]'s most powerful warriors.[1]
- He is one of multiple major Thunder Gods in the setting. During the Elder God hunt, Takemikazuchi was gathered alongside [[Thor]], Susanoo and [[Taranis]], causing Thor to immediately become interested in determining which Thunder-related deity was strongest.[1]
- Takemikazuchi's individual Divine Gear has been confirmed to exist, but its name, appearance and abilities remain unrevealed.[1]
- The novel has confirmed his Divinity over Thunder and the ability to lend his lightning to others, but has not yet demonstrated additional mythological authorities sometimes associated with the historical Takemikazuchi. Those abilities should therefore not be treated as canonical powers unless they are shown or stated in the novel.
