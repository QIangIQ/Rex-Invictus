### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 363
Susanoo, formally known as Susanoo-no-Mikoto, is a Second Generation Primordial God, one of the Three Noble Children and one of the major warrior Gods of the [[Shinto Pantheon]]. He is the younger brother of [[Amaterasu]] Elderblood and [[Tsukuyomi]] and possesses Divine authorities associated with Storms, the Sea and Lightning.
Chapter 873 explicitly identifies Susanoo as the Primordial God of Storms and places him alongside [[Takemikazuchi]] as one of the strongest warriors chosen by the Shinto Pantheon to participate in the multinational hunt organized after the second [[Supernatural Gathering]].[1]
Unlike many ancient Gods, Susanoo is highly social and maintains friendships outside his own Pantheon. He belonged to [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood's private chat group alongside [[Kali Elderblood]], [[Freyja|Freya]] and [[Loki]], giving him an unusual informal connection to several major Divine factions and later to [[Victor Elderblood]].[2]

### Physical Appearance
The novel has not yet provided a sufficiently detailed canonical description of Susanoo's physical appearance to establish specific features such as hair color, eye color or height. His current artwork should therefore be treated as an interpretation rather than a complete canonical reference.

### Personality
Susanoo is carefree, humorous and considerably less formal than many ancient Gods. His first direct appearance occurred while the Gods were observing [[Inari-Omikami]]'s battle against [[Scathach]], where Susanoo participated in increasingly unserious banter rather than maintaining the dignified behavior expected of a Primordial deity.[3]
His behavior in [[Aphrodite]]'s private chat group reinforces this side of his personality. Using the name CourtingDeath, Susanoo casually joked with Aphrodite, [[Freyja|Freya]], [[Loki]] and Kali and complained when Aphrodite changed the members' usernames for her own amusement.[2]
Susanoo is also capable of becoming jealous, shameless and highly informal when attractive women are involved. His conversations with Aphrodite's group frequently lack the ceremonial distance normally associated with Gods.[2]
However, his carefree behavior should not be mistaken for stupidity. Susanoo demonstrated strong political judgment when [[Amaterasu]] became excited about the possibility of selling anti-Dragon weapons after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s transformation into the Blood Dragon Primogenitor. He immediately pointed out that supplying weapons specifically designed to kill Dragons could make Shinto appear hostile to Victor and destroy the alliance Amaterasu actually wanted. She accepted his reasoning and abandoned the idea.[4]
Susanoo also quickly recognized suspicious political timing. When another supernatural meeting was called shortly after Victor's rise, he immediately suspected that some form of conspiracy was involved.[4]

### History
Susanoo is the youngest of the Three Noble Children born from [[Izanagi]] and [[Izanami-no-Mikoto]]. Alongside [[Amaterasu]] and [[Tsukuyomi]], he belongs to the Second Generation of Primordial Shinto Gods.
His Divine authority has been worshipped and invoked by Japanese supernatural practitioners for a long period. Even before Susanoo personally appeared, [[Mizuki Elderblood]] invoked him as the Japanese God of the Sea and Storms and used his protection to defend allied [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]].[5]
Susanoo also possesses the traditional reputation and title of Dragon Slayer. The novel has not yet shown enough of the ancient event behind that title to construct a detailed canonical history of it, so the title should not be expanded through external mythology on this page.

### Synopsis

#### The Awakening
Susanoo's power was demonstrated long before his personal debut. During an early battle involving Mizuki and allied [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]], Mizuki invoked Susanoo as the Japanese God of the Sea and Storms and asked for his protection. Water appeared around the Hunters and formed protective armor modeled after Japanese warriors, demonstrating that Susanoo's Divine authority could be invoked remotely through Shinto supernatural techniques.[5]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Susanoo personally appeared while Gods were observing the battle between [[Inari-Omikami]] and [[Scathach]]. Once it became clear that Inari was likely to lose and that conventional Divine death would not necessarily erase her forever, Susanoo joined the other male Gods in joking about what would happen after her eventual return. He openly told Inari not to struggle too hard and participated in the increasingly ridiculous conversation among the observing Gods.[3]
The scene primarily established Susanoo's personality and his familiarity with other Gods rather than functioning as a combat appearance.

#### King Among Kings
Susanoo later appeared as one of the members of [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood's private Divine chat group alongside [[Freyja|Freya]], [[Loki]] and Kali. He communicated casually with the group under the username CourtingDeath and eventually complained until Aphrodite restored his normal name.[2]
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] conquered [[Olympus]] and became the Blood Dragon Primogenitor, [[Amaterasu]] immediately recognized the commercial opportunity represented by widespread fear of Dragons and considered selling anti-Dragon weapons produced by Shinto's smiths. Susanoo stopped her by explaining that such a business could make Shinto an enemy of Dragon's Nest. He reminded Amaterasu that their real objective was an alliance with Victor, causing her to abandon the proposal.[4]
Susanoo also questioned Amaterasu's habit of discussing herself as a political marriage asset, although Amaterasu maintained that as God-Queen she was willing to use herself if doing so benefited her people. Shortly afterward, news arrived concerning another supernatural gathering, and Susanoo immediately suspected the timing concealed a conspiracy.[4]
He later attended the second [[Supernatural Gathering]] alongside Amaterasu and [[Tsukuyomi]].[6] After the political meeting turned toward open conflict against Victor's enemies, the [[Shinto Pantheon]] selected Susanoo and [[Takemikazuchi]] to join the multinational operation. The narration described them as two of Shinto's strongest warriors and explicitly identified Susanoo as the Primordial God of Storms.[1]
The assembled force included multiple Gods associated with storms, thunder or lightning, including [[Thor]], [[Taranis]] and Takemikazuchi. Thor immediately showed interest in comparing their strength, but the group remained focused on the actual operation.[1]
Susanoo also spoke with Ariel about Heaven's participation in the hunt. Ariel's complete obedience to the [[Heavenly Father]]'s command led Susanoo to privately regard Angels as nearly perfect soldiers because of their lack of hesitation.[1]
When Victor arrived accompanied by several evolved True Dragon wives, Susanoo and the other Gods were exposed to the passive supernatural effect of Aphrodite Elderblood's beauty. Susanoo was unable to fully ignore it and later decided he should seek help from [[Sakuya-hime]] to improve his resistance against such effects.[1]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
[[Amaterasu]] later completed the alliance Susanoo had supported by marrying [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and entering a [[Soul Marriage]] with him.[7] This made Victor Susanoo's brother-in-law and permanently connected Shinto to Dragon's Nest at the ruling-family level.
Susanoo later became involved in the development of [[Frederick Winter]]. After Victor transformed Frederick into a complete Noble Vampire while preserving his Japanese supernatural training, Frederick was informed that he would eventually be tested by Amaterasu, [[Tsukuyomi]] and Susanoo once he had adapted to his new body.[8]
The chapter does not state that Susanoo personally practices the entire Onmyojutsu system. His involvement instead establishes him as one of the major Shinto Gods whose knowledge and authority were considered useful for evaluating Frederick's development.

### Skills & Abilities
General God and Primordial God abilities are covered on their respective race pages. Susanoo's personal section should remain limited to powers actually stated or demonstrated.
- Storm Divinity: Susanoo is explicitly called the Primordial God of Storms in Chapter 873.[1]
- Sea Divinity: Susanoo is invoked as the Japanese God of the Sea and Storms. His Sea authority was directly demonstrated through Mizuki's invocation of his protection.[5]
- Protection of the Seas: Mizuki invoked Susanoo's power to protect allied [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]]. Water surrounded the targets and formed defensive armor resembling Japanese warriors.[5]
- Lightning/Thunder Divinity: Susanoo is identified among the major Shinto storm and lightning Gods and was grouped with other thunder-associated Gods during the hunt in Chapter 873.[1]
- [[Divine Energy]]: As a true Second Generation Primordial God, Susanoo possesses Divine Energy. No detailed maximum-output feat has yet been shown directly.
- Divine Blessing: The Protection of the Seas demonstrates that Susanoo can grant supernatural protection through Shinto invocation.[5]

### Power
Susanoo is a Second Generation Primordial God and one of Shinto's major warriors, but his generational classification should not be confused with automatic superiority over every younger God.
Chapter 873 explicitly establishes that Susanoo and [[Takemikazuchi]] were chosen as two of the [[Shinto Pantheon]]'s strongest warriors.[1]

### Relationships

#### Amaterasu Elderblood
[[Amaterasu]] Elderblood is Susanoo's older sister and God-Queen. Susanoo respects her authority but is willing to challenge her decisions when he believes she is overlooking an obvious danger. His intervention in Chapter 826 prevented her from pursuing the anti-Dragon weapons market.[4]

#### Tsukuyomi
[[Tsukuyomi]] is Susanoo's older brother and another of the Three Noble Children. The three siblings later appeared together during the second [[Supernatural Gathering]].[6]

#### Victor Elderblood
Susanoo knew [[Victor Elderblood]] through [[Aphrodite]]'s Divine social circle before Victor became his brother-in-law. His early preference for cooperation over hostility helped keep Shinto from taking an unnecessarily antagonistic position toward the Blood Dragon Primogenitor.[2][4]

#### Aphrodite Elderblood
[[Aphrodite]] Elderblood is one of Susanoo's Divine friends and administrator of the private chat group he frequently used. Their relationship is highly informal compared with ordinary diplomacy between ancient Gods.[2]

#### Kali Elderblood
[[Kali Elderblood]] belonged to the same Divine social group and was treated by Susanoo as a friend rather than as a distant foreign Goddess.[2]
