Izanagi (イザナギ/伊邪那岐/伊弉諾), also known as Izanaki and formally referred to as Izanagi-no-Mikoto, was a First Generation Primordial God, a Creator God associated with Creation and Life, and one of the ancient founders of the [[Shinto Pantheon]]. Together with his sister and wife [[Izanami-no-Mikoto]], Izanagi stood at the beginning of the ancient Japanese Divine lineage from which many of the Pantheon's later Gods descended.
He was the father of several of the Shinto Pantheon's most important deities, most notably Amaterasu Elderblood, Tsukuyomi and Susanoo. By the events of the main story Izanagi had already died, leaving the leadership of the Heavenly side of the Shinto Pantheon to later generations. Despite his death, a portion of the Divine Essence belonging to the Creation authorities of Izanagi and Izanami continued to exist. Thousands of years later, Amaterasu retained this Essence and considered it valuable enough to offer to [[Victor Elderblood]] during negotiations for an alliance and marriage between the Shinto Pantheon and Dragon's Nest.[1]

### Physical Appearance
Izanagi has never physically appeared in the present-day story and the novel has not provided a detailed canonical description of his appearance. His current depiction is therefore an artistic interpretation rather than a confirmed representation of his historical appearance.

### Personality
Very little is presently known about Izanagi's personality. He died long before the principal events of the novel and has never been shown directly through a living scene, memory or reconstructed consciousness. Consequently, the behavior traditionally attributed to the mythological Izanagi should not automatically be treated as the personality of his counterpart within the novel.
What is known of him primarily survives through his position in the Shinto Divine lineage, his relationship with Izanami and the Divine Essence left behind after his death. His status as a Creator God and progenitor of several extraordinarily powerful Gods demonstrates his foundational importance to the early [[Shinto Pantheon]], but no reliable statement has established his temperament, morality or personal relationship with his children in detail.

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Although Izanagi had died long before the Demonic War, the Divine family and Pantheon that he helped establish remained central to the conflict in Japan. His wife [[Izanami-no-Mikoto]], another First Generation Primordial God and Creator Goddess, ruled the Japanese [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] while their daughter [[Amaterasu]] represented the Heavenly side of the [[Shinto Pantheon]].
By the time [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction became involved with the Japanese Gods, the Pantheon had already been destabilized by internal conflict. Amaterasu had opposed Izanami and ultimately emerged as the ruling God-Queen of the Shinto Pantheon. The surviving Divine order was therefore primarily controlled by Izanagi's descendants rather than its original founders.[2][3]
Izanagi's descendants continued to constitute the upper levels of Shinto power. Amaterasu ruled the Pantheon, while [[Tsukuyomi]] and Susanoo remained major Second Generation Gods beneath the same primordial family lineage.[3]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Izanagi's greatest direct relevance to the later story came through the Divine Essence remaining after his death. After [[Victor Elderblood]] ascended as the Dragon God of [[Chaos]], [[Amaterasu]] sought to establish a much deeper alliance between the [[Shinto Pantheon]] and Victor's newly formalized Dragon's Nest. Victor asked what the Shinto Pantheon could offer him that he did not already possess.
Amaterasu responded with an exceptionally valuable inheritance: the preserved Essence associated with the Creation powers of Izanagi and Izanami.[1] Although Victor already possessed several methods capable of producing matter, life and supernatural constructs, Amaterasu distinguished those abilities from actually possessing the foundation of a true God of Creation. The Essence of the two ancient Creator Gods could therefore provide Victor with knowledge or power directly connected to authentic Creation Divinity.[1]
The value of the Essence demonstrates that the Divine authority accumulated by a sufficiently powerful God does not necessarily become meaningless after physical death. At least part of the power belonging to Izanagi and Izanami could be preserved in a transferable form and remained relevant even in an era containing beings vastly beyond ordinary Gods.[1]
Amaterasu offered this inheritance as part of her proposed marriage alliance with Victor. Victor accepted the larger alliance and eventually formed a [[Soul Marriage]] with Amaterasu, but he did not immediately consume the Creation Essence. Instead, he returned it to her for safekeeping, stating that he could obtain it later.[1]

### Skills & Abilities
As one of the ancient Creator Gods of the [[Shinto Pantheon]], Izanagi belonged to the First Generation of [[Primordial Gods]]. Very few personal combat feats have been shown because he died before the events of the story, but his classification and surviving Divine Essence establish the fundamental nature of his abilities.
- Primordial God Physiology: Izanagi was a First Generation Primordial God, placing his original existence far above ordinary Lesser and Third Generation Gods. His Divine body possessed the fundamental supernatural characteristics of a high-ranking God, including immense physical attributes, supernatural perception, [[Divine Energy]], agelessness and a highly developed Soul.
- First Generation Divinity: Unlike later Gods born from increasingly distant Divine generations, Izanagi belonged to the earliest generation of the Shinto Divine family. His authorities were therefore ancient, highly developed Divine powers that formed part of the foundation upon which later Shinto Gods emerged.
- Divinity: Izanagi possessed major Divine authority associated with Creation and Life. His Creation authority was sufficiently substantial that an Essence derived from his and Izanami's powers survived his death and remained a uniquely valuable supernatural inheritance.[1]
- Creation Divinity: Izanagi was a genuine God of Creation. His power represented actual Divine Creation rather than merely reproducing objects through [[Magic]], transmutation or Energy manipulation. [[Amaterasu]] specifically distinguished the Creation Essence inherited from Izanagi and Izanami from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s existing ability to create things, explaining why the inheritance remained useful even to the Dragon God of [[Chaos]].[1]
- Creation: Through his Creation Divinity, Izanagi could generate and shape things through Divine authority. The full upper limit of this ability has never been demonstrated directly in the novel, but his designation as a Creator God places Creation among the fundamental functions of his existence.[1]
- Matter Creation: As an extension of his Creation authority, Izanagi possessed the ability to bring physical creations into existence rather than being restricted to reshaping pre-existing material. The surviving Creation Essence was explicitly valuable because it represented the authentic authority of Creator Gods.[1]
- Life Divinity: Izanagi was also recognized as a God of Life. His Divine nature therefore included an affinity with living existence in addition to general Creation. The precise techniques he developed through this authority have not been individually shown.
- Life Creation: As a Creator God whose authority included Life, Izanagi possessed the Divine foundation necessary for creating or generating living beings. The novel has not displayed the exact limitations, requirements or scale of his use of this ability.
- Life Manipulation: Izanagi's association with Life granted him supernatural authority over living existence. No direct combat application has been shown, so specific effects beyond his established Divine domain should not be assumed.
- Divine Energy Manipulation: As a Primordial God, Izanagi possessed Divine Energy on a scale far beyond ordinary supernatural beings. His Divinities and Creation abilities were expressions of this higher Divine existence.
- Conceptual Authority: Gods exercise supernatural authority through the Concepts embodied by their Divinities. Izanagi's status as a Creator God consequently gave him Conceptual influence over the aspects of Creation and Life represented by his Divine existence.
- [[Immortality]]: Izanagi possessed the natural agelessness of a God and could exist indefinitely without dying from ordinary biological aging. His deceased status also confirms that Divine immortality is not absolute; Gods can still be permanently killed by sufficient supernatural means.
- Regeneration: Like other high-ranking Gods, Izanagi possessed Divine regenerative capabilities capable of recovering from injuries vastly beyond mortal limits.
- Enhanced Soul: As a First Generation Primordial God, Izanagi possessed a highly developed Divine Soul able to contain and support multiple major Divinities.
- Divine Essence: A portion of Izanagi's Divine power survived his death as an extractable Essence. Amaterasu preserved an Essence formed from the Creation powers of both Izanagi and Izanami and later offered it to Victor.[1] The existence of this Essence indicates that a sufficiently powerful God's accumulated authority can remain as a usable supernatural resource even after the original wielder has died.
- Power Bestowal through Essence: Although Izanagi himself was already dead, his preserved Essence could theoretically transfer or reinforce aspects of his Creation authority in another compatible being. Amaterasu intended Victor to receive the Essence specifically because he lacked the particular foundation possessed by authentic Creator Gods.[1]

### Power and Standing
Izanagi was not an ordinary First Generation God but a First Generation Primordial God and Creator God. These classifications place him among the ancient Divine founders from the earliest periods of Earth's supernatural civilizations.
His exact combat level while alive is unknown. The novel has never shown Izanagi fighting, nor has it provided a direct comparison between him and modern high-ranking figures such as [[Victor Elderblood]], the evolved [[Amaterasu]] Elderblood, or the most powerful Primordial entities. His placement should therefore be based on his established Divine status rather than unsupported scaling.
What can be established is that:
- He was one of the foundational Gods of the [[Shinto Pantheon]].
- He possessed genuine Creation Divinity rather than an imitation of Creation through [[Magic]] or technology.[1]
- His Divine Essence remained valuable thousands of years after his death.[1]
- He fathered Second Generation [[Primordial Gods]] including Amaterasu Elderblood, [[Tsukuyomi]] and Susanoo, who became some of the Shinto Pantheon's principal Divine powers.[3]
- His original status as a First Generation Primordial God placed him substantially above the overwhelming majority of the Shinto Pantheon's later-generation Gods.

### Legacy
Izanagi's greatest impact upon the setting survives through the Shinto Divine lineage rather than through personal participation in the present story. The Pantheon's modern upper hierarchy consists heavily of his descendants, with [[Amaterasu]] becoming its God-Queen while [[Tsukuyomi]] and Susanoo remained among its most important Gods.
His Creation authority also survived separately from his descendants through the preserved Essence held by Amaterasu. This made Izanagi relevant even after the [[Shinto Pantheon]] entered the political sphere surrounding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. The ancient power of the Pantheon's founder effectively became a strategic inheritance that could potentially contribute to the development of an entirely newer Divine civilization.[1]
Following Amaterasu's union with Victor, Izanagi also became the deceased father-in-law of the ruler who would eventually transform the supernatural order surrounding Earth and bring the Shinto Pantheon beneath the much larger Elderblood civilization.

### Relationships

#### Izanami-no-Mikoto
[[Izanami-no-Mikoto]] was Izanagi's sister, wife and fellow Creator God. Together they formed the primordial Divine pair at the foundation of the [[Shinto Pantheon]]. Both possessed Creation-related authority, and their powers were closely enough associated that [[Amaterasu]] later preserved and offered a combined Essence derived from their Creation Divinities.[1]
Unlike Izanagi, Izanami survived into the period immediately preceding the main events and became the ruler of the Japanese [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]]. Her later conflict with their daughter Amaterasu divided the Shinto Pantheon into competing factions.[2][3]

#### Amaterasu Elderblood
[[Amaterasu]] Elderblood is Izanagi's daughter and eventual successor within the Heavenly hierarchy of the [[Shinto Pantheon]]. She became the Pantheon's God-Queen and inherited responsibility for maintaining the civilization established by the older generations.[3]
Amaterasu also became the custodian of the preserved Creation Essence belonging to Izanagi and Izanami. She later offered this Essence to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] during negotiations for a permanent alliance between their factions.[1]

#### Tsukuyomi
[[Tsukuyomi]], the God of the Moon, is one of Izanagi's sons and a Second Generation God belonging to the ancient upper lineage of the [[Shinto Pantheon]].[3]

#### Susanoo
Susanoo, the God associated with storms, sea, lightning and thunder, is another of Izanagi's sons. He became one of the [[Shinto Pantheon]]'s greatest warriors and remained a major military asset under [[Amaterasu]]'s reign.[3]

#### Victor Elderblood
Izanagi died long before [[Victor Elderblood]] entered the supernatural world and the two never met. Nevertheless, Victor eventually became connected to Izanagi's lineage by marrying [[Amaterasu]]. More importantly, Amaterasu offered Victor the preserved Creation Essence originating from Izanagi and Izanami because she believed the authority of genuine Creator Gods could improve capabilities that Victor's existing powers did not perfectly reproduce.[1]
Victor declined to absorb the Essence immediately and returned it to Amaterasu for safekeeping, leaving Izanagi's surviving Divine inheritance intact for possible future use.[1]

### Trivia
- Izanagi is one of the comparatively few confirmed Creator Gods belonging to Earth's old Pantheons.
- He and [[Izanami-no-Mikoto]] are the primordial Divine pair associated with the foundation of the [[Shinto Pantheon]].
- Unlike Izanami, Izanagi was already deceased before the main events of the novel. The circumstances and exact cause of his death have not been revealed.
- His Creation Divinity remained relevant long after his death because a portion of the combined Creation Essence of Izanagi and Izanami was preserved by [[Amaterasu]] Elderblood.[1]
- Amaterasu considered the Essence valuable even to [[Victor Elderblood]] after he had already become the Dragon God of [[Chaos]], emphasizing the qualitative distinction between possessing methods of creation and possessing the authentic authority of a Creator God.[1]
- Although traditional Shinto mythology contains many additional stories, artifacts and abilities involving Izanagi, powers not stated or demonstrated within [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]] should not automatically be attributed to his novel counterpart.
- The novel has not revealed Izanagi's complete set of Divinities, his personal Divine Gear, his maximum combat strength or the circumstances surrounding his death.
