### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 389
Okama, usually called Oka by [[Victor Elderblood]], is a powerful Japanese Yokai and an exceptionally rare Nine-Tailed Kitsune. Originally an independent Yokai leader who maintained a mutually beneficial arrangement with the Shinto God [[Ebisu]], Oka became associated with Victor after their encounter in Japan. Rather than oppose Victor, he surrendered the fugitive Noble Vampires sheltered within his territory and entered an agreement under which his organization would operate as an intelligence network for Clan Alucard in Japan.[1][2]
Unlike many of the powerful supernatural beings Victor encountered during this period, Oka proved remarkably pragmatic. He researched Victor rather than dismissing him because he was a Vampire, openly recognized the changing balance of power within Japan and privately hoped that Haruna's unification of the Yokai would eventually allow their race to stop depending upon the Gods. Victor ultimately considered him a reasonable man and potential friend, even giving Oka his personal contact information.[3][2]

### Physical Appearance
Oka normally appears as a golden-haired man with prominent fox ears and nine fox tails. His Kitsune characteristics remain visible while in his humanoid appearance, immediately identifying him as an extremely advanced member of his species.[3]

### Personality
Oka is pragmatic, observant and highly conscious of the political realities surrounding Japan's supernatural society. When [[Ebisu]] confidently assumed that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was merely another Being of the Night who would be weakened by Divine power, Oka was considerably more cautious. Unlike the isolated God, Oka had independently researched Victor and was aware of how rapidly the Second Progenitor had become infamous throughout the supernatural world. Consequently, he never shared Ebisu's certainty that Victor could be treated like an ordinary Vampire.[3]
He is also highly adaptable and places survival above pointless pride. Oka has little interest in dying merely to defend another person's ego or preserve an arrangement that no longer benefits him. This became particularly clear once Victor confronted him regarding the Noble Vampires under his protection. Rather than maintain a false sense of honor and fight for them, Oka truthfully explained that he had only accepted the Vampires because he owed them a previous favor. When Victor revealed his intention to take them away, Oka was openly relieved.[1]
Despite his willingness to sacrifice expendable resources, Oka appears to care about the broader position of the Yokai race. His cooperation with Ebisu was practical rather than ideological; Oka needed Ebisu's useful blessing while Ebisu needed Oka's forces to deal with rival factions. Internally, Oka resented the fact that Yokai had to depend upon the Gods for such advantages and viewed Haruna's growing unification campaign with considerable interest. He recognized that a unified Yokai population could become a major supernatural power and potentially free itself from asking Gods for what he regarded as "crumbs."[3]
Oka is considerably less sentimental toward subordinates he considers troublesome or expendable. When Victor and [[Nero]] destroyed forces connected to him, Oka explained that those Yokai were not his primary forces and did not particularly care about their deaths. He later admitted that he had deliberately gathered badly behaved Yokai around Ebisu because he considered them compatible with the God's personality.[3][1]
He possesses a surprisingly blunt side beneath his normally reserved behavior. During his conversation with Victor, Oka released years of frustration toward Ebisu, describing him as an egotistical lesser God who constantly gave orders, before complaining equally about the Noble Vampires who had taken refuge with him. According to Oka, the Vampires arrived seeking his assistance only to begin behaving arrogantly and treating his Yokai like slaves. His candid complaints surprised even Ebisu, who had apparently never realized how dissatisfied the normally quiet fox was with their arrangement.[1]
Oka nevertheless knows when fear is appropriate. He remained composed throughout much of Victor's assault on Ebisu's temple, even privately recognizing how badly Ebisu had underestimated the Vampire, but Victor's direct attention eventually caused him to visibly tremble. Rather than allowing that fear to turn into irrational hostility, he adapted to the situation, spoke honestly with Victor and converted what could have become his execution into a highly advantageous alliance.[4][5][1]

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Oka became involved in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s affairs after the incident surrounding [[Nero]] and Ophis. Victor discovered through the memories of defeated Yokai that a suspicious fox connected to the Noble Vampires was hiding within a temple protected by a Japanese God. This led Victor and his group toward the territory controlled by [[Ebisu]].[6]
Inside the temple, Oka calmly drank tea with Ebisu while the remaining forces outside were being eliminated. When Ebisu questioned whether Oka cared that his army was being destroyed, Oka explained that those forces were not his main military strength and that Yokai would continue to exist for as long as Humans existed. His calm response demonstrated that he regarded those particular forces as expendable.[3]
Their conversation revealed much more about Oka's position in Japan. He and Ebisu had worked together for a long period, but their relationship was based on mutual utility rather than genuine loyalty. Oka benefited from Ebisu's blessing of luck, while Ebisu used Oka's army whenever he needed forces against his rivals. Oka privately disliked the unequal relationship between Gods and Yokai but continued participating because the blessing remained useful.[3]
Oka had also been closely following the expansion of Haruna. Through his informants, he learned that Haruna was absorbing smaller Yokai clans after defeating [[Hashimoto Genji]], bringing Japan progressively closer to having almost all Yokai united beneath a single ruler. Oka understood the wider implications immediately. Yokai possessed powerful Yōuki techniques and numerous unusual racial abilities but historically remained politically fragmented. He reasoned that Haruna's unification could finally transform the Yokai into a coherent major supernatural faction rather than scattered clans dependent upon Gods.[3]
Unlike Ebisu, Oka had also researched Victor. While the God regarded Victor as merely another creature of the night who should become weaker before Divine power, Oka knew that Count Alucard had become internationally infamous almost immediately after entering supernatural society. Oka therefore suspected that Victor was far from an ordinary Vampire and quietly doubted Ebisu's confidence.[3]
Those doubts were proven correct when Victor began physically attacking the Divine barrier protecting the temple. His casual punches caused the entire mountain to tremble before he escalated the attack further. Oka watched as Victor progressively overwhelmed Ebisu's defenses, while Ebisu was forced to confront the enormous difference between the Vampire he had imagined and the being actually standing outside his temple.[3][4]
Victor eventually destroyed Ebisu's confidence and forced his way into the temple. After humiliating the God, Victor shifted his attention toward the Nine-Tailed Fox and referred to him as an "old friend", immediately alarming Oka. Until that point Oka had remained comparatively calm while Ebisu panicked, but being personally singled out by Victor caused the fox to tremble.[5]
Once they spoke directly, Victor shortened Okama to Oka after becoming distracted by the meaning associated with his full name. Victor then asked why Oka had been hiding Noble Vampires from him. Oka denied deliberately hiding them and explained that the surviving Noble Vampires had simply sought refuge in his territory because he owed them a favor from the past.[1]
Oka further explained that the Japanese Noble Vampire faction had once been considered one of the area's great clans before being devastated by the last Onmyōji, Mizuki. After their decline they sought his protection, which he initially provided out of obligation. Their subsequent behavior quickly exhausted his patience. According to Oka, the Vampires behaved as though they were entitled to his resources and treated his Yokai like slaves despite having arrived as refugees themselves.[1]
When Victor revealed that he intended to take the Noble Vampires, Oka immediately expressed relief rather than resistance. During the ensuing conversation he openly complained about both his unwanted guests and Ebisu, calling the latter an arrogant lesser God and explaining that their association existed primarily because Ebisu's luck was useful to him. Oka also admitted that many of the Yokai Victor's group had destroyed were expendable secondary forces composed largely of troublesome individuals whom Oka had intentionally gathered around Ebisu.[1]
Victor's supernatural perception convinced him that Oka was telling the truth. Rather than seeing the fox as another enemy who needed to be eliminated, Victor concluded that he was effectively an innocent party attempting to survive within an unpleasant political arrangement. Victor consequently became increasingly amused by Oka and began to regard him positively.[1]
Their interaction ultimately ended in an agreement rather than bloodshed. In exchange for surrendering the Noble Vampires to Victor, Oka's group became an intelligence network for Clan Alucard within Japan. Oka's relationship with Ebisu was especially valuable because it granted him indirect access to information associated with the Divine Realm of the [[Shinto Pantheon]]. Victor therefore obtained both an intelligence agent operating among Japan's Yokai and a potential source of intelligence from the Shinto Gods.[2]
Ebisu initially objected to the arrangement, but Victor persuaded him by allowing the enormous ice spikes still suspended above the region to descend slightly. Victor nevertheless refused to rely exclusively upon verbal promises. Oka and Ebisu were bound by a gold-colored magical contract, an exceptionally high-quality agreement that ordinarily required an experienced or master Witch to break. The contract also prevented them from revealing the confidential information involved in their agreement.[2]
Victor came away from the negotiation genuinely liking Oka. He thought the fox was a decent person with whom he could potentially become friends and therefore gave Oka his private contact number, allowing the Nine-Tailed Fox to contact him directly if necessary.[2]

#### King Among Kings
Oka continued fulfilling the agreement long after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] left Japan. By the time Victor returned to negotiate directly with Haruna, Oka was actively cooperating with the Witches under Victor's command. When Victor asked Ruby whether she had contacted Oka to work alongside their Witches, Ruby confirmed that she had and specifically stated that the Nine-Tailed Fox was doing very well.[7]
The conversation also provided additional information about Oka's capabilities. While discussing another Nine-Tailed Fox, it was explained that a Kitsune of Oka's level could naturally generate Yōuki even when removed from a homeland rich in that energy. Although operating away from the environment most favorable to Yokai would greatly reduce a Nine-Tailed Fox's overall combat effectiveness, their species could still independently replenish the energy necessary to use Yokai abilities.[7]
Oka's absence had not gone unnoticed among Japan's new Yokai leadership. During Victor's meeting with Haruna, she immediately raised the subject of a member of her race working for him. Victor confirmed that Oka was in his service. Haruna explained that when she completed the process of gathering Japan's Yokai factions, she had been surprised that Oka was nowhere among them.[8]
Haruna further clarified why his absence was noteworthy. Although Oka was not comparable to herself or Genji in direct strength, he was still a genuine Nine-Tailed Fox, something exceptionally rare among Kitsune. According to Haruna, the number of Nine-Tailed Foxes could be counted extremely easily because very few Kitsune ever succeeded in developing nine tails and even fewer were naturally born possessing them. Victor consequently realized that the seemingly unassuming intelligence agent he had recruited was a considerably rarer Yokai than he had initially appreciated.[8]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
Oka initially viewed [[Victor Elderblood]] as an extremely dangerous unknown rather than an automatic enemy. He independently researched Victor and correctly concluded that treating him as an ordinary Vampire would be a mistake, placing him in direct contrast with [[Ebisu]]'s complacency.[3] After Victor breached the temple, Oka was genuinely terrified when Victor focused upon him, but he quickly recognized that honesty and negotiation offered a better option than resistance. His decision resulted in Oka becoming an intelligence agent rather than another casualty of Victor's campaign in Japan.[5][1]
Victor, in turn, came to view Oka very positively. After confirming that Oka had not maliciously hidden the fugitive Vampires, Victor considered him a reasonable man simply attempting to survive supernatural politics. He liked Oka enough to believe that they could eventually become friends and gave him his personal number, something that also allowed Oka unusually direct access to Victor when intelligence or assistance was required.[2]

#### Ebisu
[[Ebisu]] was Oka's long-term supernatural partner, although their relationship was transactional rather than based upon devotion. Ebisu supplied Oka with his useful luck blessing while Oka provided armies which the God could employ against rival forces. Oka tolerated Ebisu because the arrangement benefited him but privately resented the God's inflated ego and the broader position in which Yokai were forced to depend upon Divine beings.[3][1]
Oka's normally quiet behavior concealed considerable frustration. When given an opportunity to speak freely before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], he openly complained that Ebisu primarily knew how to give orders and failed to understand his comparatively low status among the Gods. Even Ebisu was surprised by how many complaints Oka had accumulated during their partnership.[1]

#### Otsuki Elderblood Haruna
Before personally joining [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s network, Oka observed Haruna's rise through his own intelligence sources. He understood earlier than many others that her campaign to absorb Japan's scattered Yokai clans could fundamentally change the position of their species. Oka was particularly interested in the possibility that a unified Yokai population could stop depending upon Gods for political protection and Divine favors.[3]
Haruna was likewise aware of Oka. After completing most of her unification campaign, she noticed that he was missing and later questioned Victor about him directly. She regarded his absence as unusual because, although Oka was weaker than herself and Genji, a genuine Nine-Tailed Fox was far too rare to disappear without attracting attention.[8]

### Skills & Abilities
- Yokai Physiology: Oka is a powerful Yokai and therefore possesses the supernatural physiology and spiritual characteristics of the Japanese supernatural race.[3]
- Nine-Tailed Kitsune Physiology: Oka has reached the Nine-Tailed stage of Kitsune development, placing him among an exceptionally rare group. Haruna stated that very few Kitsune ever develop nine tails and that the known Nine-Tailed Fox population could effectively be counted individually.[8]
- Yōuki Generation: As a Nine-Tailed Fox, Oka can naturally produce Yōuki through his own species even when removed from the energy-rich environment of Japan. His strength would still decrease considerably outside a suitable homeland, but he would not become completely unable to produce the energy necessary for Yokai techniques.[7]
- Yōuki Manipulation: As an advanced Kitsune, Oka possesses and utilizes Yōuki, the supernatural energy fundamental to Yokai techniques. His status as a Nine-Tailed Fox indicates a highly developed connection to this energy.[3][7]
- Enhanced Senses: Oka's fox ears possess heightened sensory capabilities. While inside [[Ebisu]]'s temple, his ear reacted before he announced that the fighting outside had ended, demonstrating his ability to perceive events beyond ordinary Human sensory range.[3]
- Luck Enhancement — Ebisu's Blessing: Oka maintained his partnership with Ebisu partly because the God's blessing of Luck was useful to him. The exact degree and mechanics of this blessing have not been demonstrated, but Oka valued it enough to tolerate years of working alongside a God he privately disliked.[3]
- Intelligence Gathering: Oka operated his own information network before joining [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. His informants followed Haruna's movements as she absorbed minor Yokai clans, allowing him to track the changing political situation across Japan.[3]
- Espionage: After his agreement with Victor, Oka and his organization became intelligence agents for Clan Alucard in Japan. His connections provided access not only to Yokai society but indirectly to the Shinto Divine Realm through Ebisu, making him particularly useful as a covert operative.[2]
- Leadership: Oka commanded his own faction and multiple groups of Yokai. The forces destroyed during Victor's arrival were specifically described by Oka as only secondary troops rather than his primary strength, confirming that he possessed a wider organization beyond the Yokai present at Ebisu's temple.[3]
- Political Acumen: Oka accurately recognized the significance of Haruna's attempt to unify Japan's Yokai and understood how their historical fragmentation had prevented them from competing with the world's major supernatural races. He also researched Victor rather than accepting Ebisu's assumptions and immediately adjusted his strategy once he recognized Victor's overwhelming advantage.[3]
- Negotiation and Survival Skills: Oka successfully transformed a confrontation with Victor—who had arrived while hunting the people Oka was sheltering—into an alliance that removed unwanted dependents, preserved Oka's life and organization, and granted him direct communication with one of the world's most dangerous supernatural leaders.[1][2]

### Power and Status
Despite being a Nine-Tailed Fox, Oka is not among the absolute strongest Kitsune shown in the series. Haruna explicitly stated that he was weaker than both herself and [[Hashimoto Genji]]. His significance comes instead from the rarity of his evolutionary state, his own resources and his unusually effective intelligence network.[8]
Oka nevertheless should not be treated as an ordinary Yokai. Reaching nine tails is extraordinarily uncommon, and even after leaving an environment rich in Yōuki, a Nine-Tailed Kitsune can continue generating the energy through their own physiology. Oka therefore remains a high-ranking supernatural being even when his primary role under [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is intelligence rather than frontline combat.[7][8]

### Trivia

- Oka has golden hair, fox ears and nine tails.[3]
- He is one of the extremely small number of known Nine-Tailed Foxes. Haruna specifically noted that Nine-Tailed Kitsune could effectively be counted on one's fingers.[8]
- Although Oka worked alongside [[Ebisu]] for a long period, he did not particularly respect the God and primarily valued the relationship because of Ebisu's Luck blessing.[3][1]
- Oka deliberately stationed many badly behaved Yokai near Ebisu because he considered their personalities compatible. He consequently felt little loss when those secondary forces were destroyed.[1]
- He was aware of Haruna's unification campaign through informants before joining Victor and privately believed a unified Yokai race might finally free itself from depending upon the Gods.[3]
- The Noble Vampires under his protection were not his subordinates. Oka sheltered them only because he owed them a favor from the past and became openly relieved when Victor offered to remove them.[1]
- Victor considered Oka sufficiently trustworthy and likable to give him his personal contact number.[2]
- By Chapter 586, Ruby described Oka's work with Victor's Witches as going very well.[7]
- Haruna noticed Oka's absence while gathering Japan's Yokai beneath her authority, demonstrating that he was prominent enough among Japanese Kitsune that his disappearance from the political landscape was conspicuous.[8]
