Shuten Douji is a powerful Yōkai of the Oni species and the leader of one of Japan's major Oni Clans. Despite his comparatively refined appearance and composed disposition, Shuten is explicitly the strongest man in his Clan, with his friend and fellow Oni [[Ibaraki Douji]] ranking immediately behind him.[1] He serves as the more level-headed counterpart to Ibaraki, frequently restraining the battle-obsessed Oni before his behavior creates unnecessary conflicts with other supernatural factions.

### Physical Appearance
Shuten stands approximately 180 cm tall and possesses an unusually refined appearance for an Oni. Unlike [[Ibaraki Douji]] and many other members of his Clan, who tend to possess large and heavily muscular physiques, Shuten is relatively slim, tall and lightly built. His appearance is considered exceptionally handsome, with Ibaraki describing his Clan Leader as an ikemen whose looks make him extremely popular among the female Oni of their village.[2]
At his debut, Shuten wore an elegant red yukata with black details and carried a smoking pipe in one hand. His restrained clothing and composed bearing visibly contrasted with Ibaraki's much more imposing and unruly appearance.[3]

### Personality
Shuten is calm, pragmatic and considerably more restrained than most of the prominent Oni introduced alongside him. He is particularly accustomed to dealing with Ibaraki's impulsive personality, openly stating that he needs to keep Ibaraki on a "leash" or the latter will bring chaos upon their faction. Rather than merely complaining about Ibaraki, Shuten actively stops him whenever his provocations threaten to escalate into a serious political incident, even physically striking him to force him to stop antagonizing other Yōkai and Divine figures.[3][4]
His restraint should not be mistaken for timidity. Shuten is the strongest man within his Clan and commands enough respect that the other male Oni do not challenge him even when their customs would permit them to do so.[2] His caution instead comes from an accurate understanding of consequences. During the [[Hyakki Yakō]], he realizes that apparently harmless spectators may actually be powerful Yōkai or lesser Gods, and regards [[Gyuki]]'s willingness to hunt indiscriminately in such an environment as dangerously foolish.[4]
Shuten also possesses a clear sense of what behavior he considers acceptable for an Oni. He openly despises Gyuki, whose faction is notorious for violence and massacres against innocents, and reacts with disgust when Gyuki kills a weaker Yōkai simply because he regarded him as prey. Shuten goes as far as calling Gyuki a disgrace to the Oni race, demonstrating that he does not share the belief that being an Oni inherently justifies uncontrolled cruelty toward weaker beings.[5]
Despite being the responsible member of his partnership with Ibaraki, Shuten is not humorless. The two casually argue, insult one another and discuss their Clan's traditions like old friends. Shuten is comfortable enough around Ibaraki to joke about his extraordinary popularity with the women of their village and entertain absurd discussions concerning [[Buddha]] and [[Sun Wukong]].[2] Their relationship nevertheless retains a clear hierarchy: when Shuten decides that they are leaving, Ibaraki ultimately follows him without hesitation.[5]
Shuten is also notably discreet regarding information belonging to the Yōkai. When [[Victor Elderblood]] asks whether Vampires can learn to manipulate Yōuki, Shuten initially refuses to answer. After Ibaraki casually reveals that Japanese Noble Vampires are capable of using it, Shuten strikes him for disclosing information he clearly considered sensitive.[6]

### Legend
In Japanese folklore, Shuten-dōji is one of the most famous and powerful Oni. He is traditionally depicted as the ruler of a band of Oni residing around Mount Ōe and as the superior or leader of Ibaraki-dōji. The Oni under his command terrorized the region surrounding Kyoto and were accused of abducting and murdering humans, particularly young women.
Shuten-dōji was eventually targeted by the warrior Minamoto no Yorimitsu, commonly known as Minamoto no Raikō, and his companions. According to the best-known versions of the tale, the warriors disguised themselves to infiltrate Shuten-dōji's residence and gave the Oni enchanted sake that incapacitated him. Raikō then decapitated Shuten-dōji while he slept. His severed head remained supernaturally active and attempted to attack Raikō, but the warrior survived with the protection of his helmet.
The novel preserves the traditional association between Shuten and Ibaraki while giving their relationship a more personal element; Ibaraki is explicitly described as Shuten's Clan member, friend and the second strongest man of their Clan.[1]

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Shuten first appears during the massive [[Hyakki Yakō]] held in Shibuya. His arrival immediately draws [[Nero]]'s attention; despite already being surrounded by famous Yōkai Clan leaders, the sight of Shuten makes her seriously consider leaving the gathering. He appears beside [[Ibaraki Douji]], whom he reprimands for recklessly provoking everyone around him. Shuten explains that if he does not personally restrain Ibaraki, the battle-obsessed Oni will inevitably bring chaos upon their faction.[3]
Shuten subsequently notices [[Gyuki]], the leader of a rival Oni faction, and immediately reacts with disgust. Gyuki commands Oni known for indiscriminate violence and massacres, making his faction a direct rival of Shuten's own. While Ibaraki eagerly begins exchanging provocations with Gyuki, Shuten is left attempting to contain the increasingly volatile situation. He nevertheless keeps close attention on Gyuki and quickly realizes when the other Oni begins considering Ophis, Nero and weaker Yōkai around the parade as possible prey.[3][4]
Unlike Gyuki, Shuten has no desire to create unnecessary trouble during a gathering attended by numerous powerful supernatural figures and Gods. He understands that appearances among Yōkai can be deceptive and that even seemingly fragile individuals may possess overwhelming power. His caution proves justified as the gathering continues to attract increasingly dangerous figures. When Haruna openly challenges [[Hashimoto Genji]] and attempts to force his entire Hyakki Yakō beneath her banner, Shuten is stunned by the scale of her ambition and bluntly considers her insane. At the same time, he repeatedly strikes Ibaraki to stop him from provoking Genji, [[Inari-Omikami]] and the surrounding Yōkai simply for entertainment.[4]
Following Haruna's departure, Gyuki resumes his predatory behavior and kills a weaker Yōkai. Shuten openly condemns him, calling his actions a disgrace to the Oni race. Gyuki and Ibaraki nearly escalate their hostility into another confrontation before Genji intervenes. Once the spectacle is effectively finished, Shuten orders Ibaraki to leave with him, and the normally uncontrollable Oni follows his leader.[5]
Approximately a week later, Shuten and Ibaraki are again present in Shibuya while waiting for the confrontation created by Haruna's challenge. Their conversation reveals considerably more about Shuten's position inside his Clan. Oni women within their village traditionally select their own partners; if another male objects, he is expected to challenge the selected partner and prove himself superior. Shuten's combination of unusual good looks and overwhelming strength makes him extraordinarily popular with the women of the Clan. Although the other men frequently complain about this, none are willing to challenge him because everyone in the village understands why Shuten became their leader. Shuten and Ibaraki jokingly discuss how dramatically the number of women choosing him has increased, with Shuten exaggerating that he would not be surprised if much of the village had already produced his sons.[2]
Their conversation is interrupted when Shuten senses several people watching him and notices [[Victor Elderblood]]. Before Shuten can remember why Victor seems familiar, Ibaraki's fighting instincts immediately react to the Vampire. Shuten realizes what his friend is about to do and tries to stop him, but Ibaraki challenges Victor before he can intervene. Victor answers with a casual punch that sends Ibaraki crashing through several buildings.[1]
The result leaves Shuten genuinely shocked. Ibaraki is not simply an ordinary Oni; he is the second strongest man in Shuten's Clan, surpassed only by Shuten himself. More importantly, Shuten possesses enough perception to clearly observe what happened despite the attack occurring too quickly for most beings present to follow: Victor had merely struck Ibaraki once without seriously exerting himself. Rather than responding recklessly, Shuten attempts to apologize for his friend's conduct when Ibaraki returns.[1]
Shuten and Ibaraki are even more surprised when the seemingly unknown Vampire identifies himself as Victor Alucard, the Fifth Vampire Count of [[Nightingale]], while the child casually sitting on him is revealed to be Ophis Tepes. Their exchange quickly demonstrates the difference between Shuten and his friend once again. When Victor jokingly tells Ibaraki that the strongest warriors traditionally carry children on their shoulders, Ibaraki begins seriously considering the statement until Shuten incredulously points out that Victor is obviously deceiving him.[1]
As the confrontation between Haruna and Genji develops, Shuten remains close enough to Victor's group to answer questions concerning Yōkai abilities. He explains that Yōuki is a form of negative energy existing within nature and naturally wielded by Yōkai. According to Shuten, the arts constructed from Yōuki can be extremely versatile. When Victor asks whether a Vampire could learn to use the same energy, Shuten deliberately remains silent, only for Ibaraki to reveal that Japanese Noble Vampires are indeed capable of using it. Shuten immediately reprimands Ibaraki for casually exposing the information, but the revelation is already enough to ignite Victor's interest in obtaining and studying Yōuki for himself.[6]
By the conclusion of the conflict surrounding Haruna, Shuten and Ibaraki witness her increasingly friendly interaction with Victor. Both Oni are left visibly stunned as Haruna accepts a means of contacting Victor and departs after their conversation. [[Alexios Alioth]], observing them, privately concludes that Shuten and Ibaraki are likely to have considerable trouble in the future now that Victor has taken an interest in them—particularly because Victor is unlikely to forget Ibaraki's desire for a proper battle.[7]

### Relationships

#### Ibaraki Douji
[[Ibaraki Douji]] is Shuten's Clan member, close friend and the second strongest man within their Oni Clan.[1] Their personalities differ dramatically: Ibaraki actively searches for battles and provocations wherever he goes, while Shuten is normally responsible for preventing those impulses from dragging their faction into unnecessary conflicts. Shuten frequently scolds or physically strikes Ibaraki when the latter refuses to stop antagonizing powerful figures, yet their constant banter and relaxed conversations demonstrate considerable familiarity rather than a purely formal leader-subordinate relationship.[3][4][2] Despite Ibaraki's difficult personality, he respects Shuten's authority enough to follow him when ordered to leave.[5]

#### Gyuki
[[Gyuki]] was the leader of a rival Oni faction and someone Shuten openly despised. Whereas Shuten's faction maintained its own customs and internal order, Gyuki controlled Oni associated with indiscriminate violence and massacres against weaker beings. Shuten regarded his behavior as shameful rather than an acceptable expression of Oni nature and directly called him a disgrace to their race after witnessing the remains of one of his victims.[3][5]

#### Victor Elderblood
Shuten initially encounters [[Victor Elderblood]] without immediately recognizing him. After witnessing Victor casually overpower Ibaraki, Shuten immediately understands that the Vampire is operating on a completely different level from what he first expected and approaches the situation considerably more cautiously than his friend.[1] Victor later questions Shuten about Yōuki, with Shuten reluctantly providing information concerning its nature while attempting to avoid revealing whether Vampires could acquire it.[6]

### Skills & Abilities
- Oni Physiology: As an Oni, Shuten possesses a supernatural body and naturally belongs to one of the physically formidable Yōkai races. The full extent of his racial abilities has not yet been directly demonstrated in combat.
- Supernatural Strength: Shuten is explicitly the strongest man of his Oni Clan, ranking above [[Ibaraki Douji]], who is considered second.[1] His strength is sufficiently well known among his own people that other male Oni refuse to challenge him even when Clan tradition would otherwise give them reason to fight him.[2]
- Yōuki Manipulation: Like other Yōkai, Shuten can make use of Yōuki, a naturally occurring form of negative energy used as the foundation for Yōkai supernatural arts. Shuten describes Yōuki-based techniques as extremely versatile and demonstrates considerable knowledge of its properties, although no personal named Yōuki technique has yet been revealed.[6]
- Enhanced Perception: Shuten can detect attention directed toward him and accurately trace the source of a distant gaze.[2] More significantly, he was among the small number of beings capable of visually following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s extremely fast counterattack against Ibaraki and clearly recognized that Victor had defeated him with a single casual punch.[1]
- Combat Proficiency: Although Shuten has not yet been given an extended on-screen battle, his position as the strongest man and leader of a powerful Oni Clan establishes him as a highly capable combatant. His status places him above Ibaraki within their Clan's internal hierarchy.[1]
- Leadership: Shuten commands his Oni faction and is recognized as its leader due to strength rather than appearance or ceremony alone. Even the notoriously unruly Ibaraki ultimately follows his decisions, while Shuten regularly takes responsibility for preventing his subordinate's actions from creating wider conflicts.[3][5]
- Supernatural Knowledge: Shuten possesses significant knowledge concerning Yōkai society, Yōuki, supernatural racial distinctions and the political dangers surrounding Japan's supernatural factions. He is notably cautious about disclosing knowledge that could provide another race with access to Yōkai techniques.[4][6]
