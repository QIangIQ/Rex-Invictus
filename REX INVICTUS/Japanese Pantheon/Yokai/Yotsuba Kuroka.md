### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 356
Yotsuba Kuroka is a Nekomata Yōkai, one of the highest-ranking members of the unified Japanese Yōkai faction, and the longtime right hand and second-in-command of [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]]. When Haruna's army first openly confronted the forces of [[Hashimoto Genji]], Kuroka was introduced as one of its principal commanders and immediately began directing the Yōkai under Haruna's banner.[1]
By the first [[Supernatural Gathering]], her military position was formally identified as the Commander of the First Battalion of Yōkai, while she continued to be publicly recognized as the woman serving directly at Haruna's side.[2] Her responsibilities later extended beyond military command. Following the expansion of [[Victor Elderblood]]'s faction, Kuroka was selected as the representative of the Yōkai within its early governmental council because Haruna's obligations in Japan prevented the Supreme Commander from constantly attending personally.[3]
Although Kuroka normally presents herself as a playful, curious and somewhat lazy cat-like woman, the narration repeatedly establishes that this behavior should not be mistaken for incompetence. She is considered responsible enough to politically represent her entire race, intelligent enough to have remained Haruna's right hand, and powerful enough by the Imperial era to fight against War Gods and withstand a confrontation with the Nine-Tailed Hashimoto Genji.[3][4]

### Physical Appearance
Kuroka is a beautiful woman with long black hair, sapphire-blue eyes, black cat ears and multiple black cat tails. During her first appearance, she wore a modern yukata and was explicitly described as possessing three cat tails.[1]
By the first [[Supernatural Gathering]], Kuroka wore a kimono designed similarly to Haruna's clothing but decorated with purple details. Unlike Haruna's more controlled presentation, Kuroka wore hers much more loosely, exposing her shoulders and emphasizing her carefree personality. This appearance also describes her with cat ears and two tails.[2]
The discrepancy between the number of tails in Chapters 356 and 510 is never explained within the novel. Her debut explicitly gives her three tails, while the later Gathering description gives her two; consequently, her page should not use the later description as proof that she permanently lost a tail.
Her feline characteristics are highly expressive. Her tails and ears react visibly to surprise, irritation and perceived danger, and her hair can stand on end when sufficiently frightened or intimidated.[5][4]

### Personality
Kuroka is cheerful, playful, curious and extremely free-spirited. Her speech frequently ends in "-nya", and she often laughs with an exaggerated "Nyahahahaha". Even when armies were preparing to clash during her debut, her immediate reaction was excitement over how entertaining the coming battle would be.[1]
Her behavior is strongly influenced by her nature as a cat Yōkai. She becomes distracted easily, dislikes excessively formal environments, enjoys teasing and amusement, and can become troublesome when bored. Haruna herself recognizes this problem: despite acknowledging Kuroka's competence, she was reluctant to leave the entirety of her army solely under Kuroka's control because an insufficiently entertained Kuroka had a tendency to begin bothering the people around her in search of something interesting to do.[2]
Kuroka is also physically affectionate toward people she trusts. Upon reuniting with Haruna at the [[Supernatural Gathering]], she immediately hugged her commander while complaining that Haruna had suddenly disappeared and made her worry, completely ignoring Haruna's instruction to first fix her disheveled clothing.[2]
Despite this carefree exterior, Kuroka is considerably more responsible than her behavior suggests. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s growing faction established an early governmental council, she was chosen to represent the Yōkai specifically because she could reliably fulfill the position even though she was playful most of the time.[3] This contrast is one of her defining characteristics: Kuroka prefers an easy and entertaining life, but when Haruna gives her an important responsibility, she performs it seriously.
She is also more intelligent than she normally bothers to appear. During the Imperial era, the narration explicitly remarks that Kuroka was not Haruna's right hand without reason and that she could be quite intelligent when she wanted to be. Her problem was not a lack of intelligence but motivation; after thinking seriously for an extended period, she immediately complained that she had used her brain too much and wanted to relax, with the narration describing her as naturally lazy and unwilling to think deeply when doing so was unnecessary.[4]
Kuroka possesses strong survival instincts and is highly aware of dangerous individuals around her. While supervising members of the Imperial Family, her instincts remained continuously alert because of the Royal Guards surrounding the princesses. She also became visibly frightened under [[Jeanne Elderblood]]'s attention and found Jeanne's ability to predict her thoughts through nothing more than body language and personality even more unsettling than outright mind-reading.[4]
Despite such reactions, Kuroka does not abandon her duties when intimidated. Even when uncomfortable around beings vastly stronger than herself, she remains professional and follows Haruna's orders.

#### Relationship with Formality
Kuroka strongly dislikes stiff social atmospheres. This became especially obvious in her early interactions with [[Victor Elderblood]]. After Victor deliberately called her "Kuroka-nya", she became irritated and insisted that it was not her name, only to accidentally finish the complaint with "-nya" herself. Victor continued teasing her specifically because he found her reactions entertaining.[5]
Kuroka outwardly called him a jerk afterward, but there was no genuine hostility behind the complaint. She understood that Victor was joking and privately admitted that she actually enjoyed the exchange because she disliked the excessively formal atmosphere of the [[Supernatural Gathering]].[5]

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes and War
Kuroka made her first appearance during the decisive conflict between Otsuki Haruna and [[Hashimoto Genji]] for control of Japan's Yōkai. As Haruna and Genji confronted one another, dimensional gates opened and their respective armies began entering the battlefield. Dragons emerged first, followed by large numbers of Yōkai belonging to the opposing factions.[1]
Kuroka appeared beside Haruna laughing excitedly at the prospect of battle. She was immediately identified as Haruna's second-in-command and right hand. Opposing her on Genji's side was his son [[Hashimoto Gin]], another three-tailed Yōkai serving as a commander. After briefly observing one another, Kuroka and Gin began issuing orders to their respective troops, establishing Kuroka as an actual battlefield commander rather than merely Haruna's personal attendant.[1]
Haruna's victory and subsequent consolidation of Japan's Yōkai dramatically increased Kuroka's responsibilities. As Haruna's closest subordinate, she became one of the principal officers beneath the new Supreme Commander and remained part of the leadership of the unified faction.

#### Demonic War
Kuroka accompanied Haruna to the first [[Supernatural Gathering]], representing the newly unified Yōkai before the other major supernatural powers. Her entrance formally introduced her to the wider supernatural political community as the Commander of the First Battalion of Yōkai and "the demonic cat", as well as Haruna's right hand.[2]
Her behavior immediately contrasted with the Gathering's formality. After becoming separated from Haruna during their arrival, Kuroka rushed toward her commander, hugged her and openly complained that Haruna had disappeared without warning. Once reassured, she immediately began looking around the unfamiliar environment with the curiosity of a cat.[2]
Kuroka later interacted directly with [[Victor Elderblood]]. Victor deliberately addressed her as "Kuroka-nya", causing her to become visibly annoyed while simultaneously proving his joke correct by responding with the same verbal tic. Although she protested and glared at him, Kuroka understood that Victor was merely teasing her and privately enjoyed the informal interaction.[5]
The Gathering subsequently became enormously important for the future of Haruna's people. When the major factions debated the protection and recognition that would be granted to the Yōkai, Victor rejected the idea of beginning an alliance by forcing Haruna into a Soul Contract. He instead offered the full sponsorship of his faction without demanding contractual control over her people.[6]
Kuroka was visibly shocked by Victor's behavior. Haruna nevertheless understood the mentality behind it and accepted his sponsorship, resulting in the Yōkai receiving five hundred years of protected non-interference alongside the support of Clan Alucard. Kuroka was subsequently among those briefly affected by Victor's supernatural beauty when he smiled after Haruna accepted the agreement.[6]
When copies of the Gathering's Rulebook were offered, Kuroka initially showed curiosity alongside the other newcomers. The moment she saw how enormous the book was, however, she immediately lost interest, another example of her aversion to unnecessary mental labor.[6]
Kuroka was present during the period surrounding Haruna's public duel with Victor. As the confrontation escalated, she and the other Yōkai maintained the isolated battlefield in which their leaders could fight without unnecessarily destroying the surrounding territory.
After Haruna exhausted herself demonstrating the final extent of her techniques, Victor decided to show her how large the difference between them truly remained. Before releasing his power, he looked directly through the isolated space toward Kuroka and called her "Kitten", warning her to reinforce the world with additional Youki or risk its destruction.[7]
Kuroka did not question the warning. She immediately ordered Genji and the Oni to assist her. Even with their combined effort, Victor's unrestricted presence overwhelmed the area, and Kuroka, Genji and the others ultimately collapsed from Youki exhaustion after maintaining the battlefield through the demonstration.[7]
Two days later, Kuroka entered Haruna's office and discovered her normally rigid commander in an exceptionally vulnerable state with Victor. The sight stunned her because Haruna was the woman who had unified the Yōkai, openly defied the Gods and built her reputation around overwhelming personal strength.[8]
Victor silently signaled Kuroka to leave rather than disturb Haruna, and Kuroka quietly closed the door. Outside, she spent several moments attempting to process what she had seen before concluding that Haruna would never have allowed herself to become so defenseless around someone she did not genuinely trust. Rather than objecting, Kuroka became excited by the possibility that Haruna and Victor might eventually have children and wondered whether she could become an "aunt" to them in the future.[8]
The incident also demonstrates how highly Kuroka values Haruna's judgment. She knew her commander well enough to understand that physical attraction alone would never have been sufficient for Haruna to expose herself so completely around another person.
As Victor's coalition continued expanding, its leaders realized that the numerous races living beneath the same political umbrella required a formal method of representing their interests. A new governmental mansion was created, and representatives of the major races were selected to participate in what became the Council of [[Races (Flamel)|Races]].[3]
Haruna would ordinarily have been the obvious Yōkai representative, but her continued responsibilities in Japan made constant attendance inefficient. Kuroka was therefore selected in her place.
The narration specifically addressed the apparent contradiction in this appointment: although Kuroka was playful most of the time, she was also quite responsible. Her selection confirms that Haruna and Victor's broader faction trusted her with considerably more than battlefield duties.[3]
As the Yōkai representative, Kuroka was expected to speak for the interests of her people alongside representatives of the Noble Vampires, Humans, Goddesses, Fairies, Amazons and Demons. This made her one of the early administrative figures in the coalition that eventually developed into the [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]] and, much later, the [[Elderblood Empire]].[3]

#### King Among Kings
Kuroka once again accompanied Haruna to the second [[Supernatural Gathering]]. By this point, the difference between the first and second Gatherings was enormous. Haruna was no longer merely an emerging Yōkai ruler seeking international recognition; she arrived under the name Otsuki Alucard Haruna, publicly revealing the political and marital union between herself and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[9]
Kuroka's growth was also immediately noticed. The supernatural powers watching the Yōkai delegation were surprised by how much stronger she had become since the previous Gathering. Although her improvement was overshadowed by Haruna's extraordinary advance toward becoming a Ten-Tailed Fox, Kuroka herself had nevertheless undergone a major increase in strength during the intervening period.[9]
Her appearance beside Haruna at both Gatherings further demonstrates the consistency of her role. Even after Genji and numerous other powerful Yōkai joined Haruna's organization, Kuroka continued occupying the position closest to the Supreme Commander.
Following the formation of [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]], Haruna's Yōkai became one of the principal racial factions operating beneath Victor's wider civilization, placing Kuroka within the same political structure as Vampires, [[Werewolves]], Demons, Gods, Witches and the numerous other races gathered beneath Dragon's Nest.

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Kuroka remained active long after the creation of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Her later appearances demonstrate that she had not simply become an irrelevant officer beneath vastly stronger Divine and Draconic beings.
During the Imperial era, Haruna assigned Kuroka the difficult responsibility of helping supervise and guide several of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters while they toured important sections of the Empire. Kuroka privately complained that she had not volunteered for the task and had effectively been thrown into it by her "Boss", but she nevertheless performed the assignment professionally.[4]
Her instincts remained continuously alert around the Imperial Princesses because they were surrounded by enormous numbers of Imperial soldiers and Royal Guards. Kuroka was also acutely aware of how abnormal Victor's daughters were, particularly because one of them was Haruna's own daughter and all of the girls possessed extraordinary bloodlines from birth.[4]
When [[Jeanne Elderblood]] thanked Kuroka for accepting the difficult responsibility of watching over her daughters, Kuroka suppressed her complaints and formally expressed gratitude for the opportunity. Jeanne recognized the anxiety beneath her behavior and casually suggested that if Kuroka desired greater strength, she could either request assistance from Haruna or exchange her accumulated achievements for a reward directly from the Emperor.[4]
The exchange provides one of the clearest measurements of Kuroka's own strength. The narration explicitly states that Kuroka was not weak: she was capable of fighting against Gods of War and could even withstand a confrontation with [[Hashimoto Genji]], despite Genji being a Nine-Tailed Fox.[4]
Kuroka nevertheless understood that the problem was relative scale. She existed in an Empire increasingly populated by Dragon Gods, Emperor-level beings and children born from some of the strongest existences in Reality. Even a fighter capable of confronting War Gods could therefore feel comparatively ordinary when surrounded by Victor's immediate family.[4]
After Jeanne departed, Kuroka quickly analyzed the circumstances surrounding the Imperial Princesses and arrived at the correct conclusion, prompting the narration to reiterate that she was Haruna's right hand for a reason and could be extremely intelligent when she chose to apply herself. Immediately afterward, however, she complained that she had already used her brain too much and returned her attention to the princesses' schedule, professionally guiding them toward the Empire's space-development sector.[4]

### Relationships

#### Otsuki Elderblood Haruna
[[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]] is Kuroka's commander, leader and the person to whom she shows the greatest loyalty. From Kuroka's first appearance she is identified as Haruna's second-in-command and right hand, a position she continues to retain even after Haruna unifies Japan's Yōkai and later becomes one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wives.[1][9]
Their relationship is not purely formal. Kuroka openly worries about Haruna, hugs her when relieved to see her and frequently refers to her simply as Boss during the Imperial era.[2][4]
Haruna trusts Kuroka with battlefield command, political representation and sensitive responsibilities. Even when Haruna could not personally participate in Victor's early government because she needed to remain in Japan, Kuroka was the person selected to represent the Yōkai in her place.[3]
Kuroka also understands Haruna's personality exceptionally well. When she discovered Haruna asleep and vulnerable around Victor, she quickly realized that such behavior meant Haruna had already judged Victor trustworthy enough to lower her defenses completely.[8]

#### Victor Elderblood
Kuroka maintains a friendly and highly informal relationship with [[Victor Elderblood]]. Victor finds her cat-like behavior entertaining and deliberately teases her, particularly by calling her "Kuroka-nya" because of her habit of ending sentences with "-nya".[5]
Although Kuroka becomes visibly irritated by his teasing, she recognizes it as harmless and secretly enjoys being able to interact without the rigid etiquette expected at supernatural political gatherings.[5]
Victor later calls her "Kitten" while warning her to reinforce the battlefield before he releases his power during his training with Haruna.[7] By the Imperial era, Kuroka is sufficiently integrated into Victor's civilization that Jeanne explicitly tells her she can use accumulated achievements to request rewards directly from the Emperor.[4]

#### Hashimoto Genji
[[Hashimoto Genji]] was originally Haruna's principal rival for supremacy over the Japanese Yōkai. Kuroka consequently began on the opposite side of the political conflict from him and his family.
After Haruna unified the Yōkai, Genji became one of the major figures operating beneath the same leadership. Kuroka and Genji consequently became fellow senior officers rather than continuing as enemies.
By the Imperial era, Genji also functions as one of Kuroka's benchmarks of personal strength. The narration explicitly states that she can withstand a confrontation with him despite Genji being a Nine-Tailed Fox.[4]

#### Jeanne Elderblood
Kuroka interacts directly with [[Jeanne Elderblood]] during her assignment supervising the Imperial Princesses. She treats Jeanne with considerable respect but is also deeply intimidated by her.
Kuroka initially fears that Jeanne has read her mind after the Empress accurately identifies her desire for greater strength. Jeanne instead explains that she merely inferred Kuroka's thoughts from her body language and personality, which Kuroka finds even more frightening.[4]

### Skills & Abilities
Kuroka is an experienced Yōkai combatant and commander whose actual level is considerably higher than her casual behavior suggests. By the Imperial era, the narration directly places her within a range capable of fighting Divine combatants.[4]
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics — As a powerful Nekomata Yōkai, Kuroka possesses physical abilities vastly beyond those of ordinary Humans.
- Youki Manipulation — Kuroka can generate and manipulate Youki, the traditional supernatural Energy used by Yōkai. During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Haruna's training, she contributed large quantities of Youki to maintain the isolated world surrounding them.[7]
- Enhanced Strength — By the Imperial era, Kuroka is strong enough to directly fight beings classified as Gods of War.[4]
- Enhanced Durability — Her ability to withstand confrontations against Divine combatants and Genji demonstrates supernatural durability far beyond ordinary Yōkai.[4]
- Enhanced Speed and Reflexes — As one of Haruna's principal combatants and a fighter capable of contending with War Gods, Kuroka possesses the speed and reactions necessary to operate against high-level supernatural opponents.[4]
- Enhanced Instincts — Kuroka possesses extremely developed animalistic instincts. While accompanying the Imperial Princesses, she remained continuously aware of the numerous Royal Guards observing them, and her body instinctively reacted to dangerous individuals around her.[4]
- Feline Characteristics — Kuroka possesses cat ears, multiple tails and strongly feline behavioral and physiological traits. Her ears, tails and even hair visibly react to her emotions.[1][5][4]
- High-Level Combat Ability — Kuroka is explicitly powerful enough to fight War Gods and withstand a confrontation with [[Hashimoto Genji]], a Nine-Tailed Fox.[4]
- Battlefield Command — Kuroka commanded Haruna's forces during the conflict against Genji's army and was later formally recognized as Commander of the First Battalion of Yōkai.[1][2]
- Military Leadership — As Haruna's second-in-command, Kuroka is trusted with commanding significant portions of the unified Yōkai military and issuing orders independently when necessary.[1]
- Political Administration — Kuroka served as the official Yōkai representative on the early Council of [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] within Victor's faction, demonstrating that her competence extends beyond combat.[3]
- Intelligence — Despite preferring not to overthink matters, Kuroka is explicitly stated to be highly intelligent when she applies herself. The narration identifies this quality as one of the reasons she is worthy of serving as Haruna's right hand.[4]
- Strong [[Will Solace|Will]] — Kuroka has operated in the presence of Gods, Progenitors, Dragons and other overwhelmingly powerful beings while continuing to fulfill her duties. Although she does not possess Haruna's extreme willpower, she maintains her composure sufficiently to represent and command the Yōkai in hostile political environments.

### Power
Kuroka's strength grows substantially over the course of the story.
During her debut, she is already one of Haruna's two most important military assets and trusted to command large numbers of Yōkai.[1] By the first [[Supernatural Gathering]], Haruna considers only Kuroka and Genji capable enough to accompany her without being significantly restricted by the circumstances surrounding the meeting, although she ultimately brings Kuroka as her sole direct escort.[5]
At the second Supernatural Gathering, observers who remembered the earlier Kuroka immediately notice that she has become significantly stronger.[9]
Her clearest later scaling comes from Chapter 1106:
Ordinary Yōkai
↓
Experienced / High-Level Yōkai
↓
Yotsuba Kuroka
├── Capable of fighting War Gods
└── Capable of withstanding [[Hashimoto Genji]]
↓
Nine-Tailed Fox Genji
This does not mean Kuroka is explicitly stated to be stronger than Genji. The narration specifically states that she can withstand a confrontation with him, establishing that she can survive and meaningfully operate against his level without establishing superiority.[4]
