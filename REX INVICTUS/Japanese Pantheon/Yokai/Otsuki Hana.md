Otsuki Hana was a Nine-Tailed Black Kitsune of the [[Otsuki Clan]], later a Noble Vampire, the sixth wife and most beloved consort of [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]], and the biological mother of [[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]]. She was also the elder sister of [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]], although Haruna grew up without knowing that Hana had ever existed. Hana's death at the hands of the Elder God Shur became one of the defining tragedies of Vlad's life and was the event that transformed his hostility toward [[Nightingale]]'s Elder Gods into an obsessive desire to permanently destroy them.[1][2][3]
Hana was remembered as a quiet and difficult woman to read, speaking only when necessary and usually maintaining a nearly expressionless face. Her reserved demeanor concealed considerable strength and charisma, enough that those around her naturally paid attention when she acted. She also possessed a gentler disposition than her younger sister Haruna and displayed an exceptionally strong attachment toward her family, particularly Ophis.[1][4]
Although Hana died long before the main events of the novel, her legacy remains important. Ophis inherited a small portion of her mother's Yōkai lineage despite Vlad's overwhelmingly dominant Progenitor Bloodline. When [[Victor Elderblood]] later created specialized Divine gloves to awaken that dormant heritage, Ophis manifested Nine-Tailed-Fox characteristics, Fire, Wind and other Yōkai abilities explicitly identified by Victor as the Power of her mother.[5]
Hana later became central to Ophis' trial for Godhood when Victor constructed an artificial Reality allowing his daughter to experience life with her deceased mother. The recreation gave Ophis an opportunity to confront grief she had never completely processed and ultimately contributed to the self-understanding necessary for the awakening of her Divinity.[6][7]

### Physical Appearance
Hana was an exceptionally beautiful woman with very long black hair and pale skin. As a Nine-Tailed Black Kitsune, her most distinctive characteristics were the nine large black fox tails extending from behind her body.[1]
She normally wore clothing inspired by traditional Japanese fashion. In [[Alexios Alioth]]'s memories, Hana appeared in dark clothing resembling a modernized black yukata, while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s reconstructed Reality later presented her in an outfit described as a mixture between a short black yukata and a dress.[1][6]
Hana resembled her younger sister [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]], making their familial relationship visually obvious once both women were compared. The primary difference was their expressions: Haruna generally possessed a colder and more severe face, whereas Hana's features were noticeably gentler.[6]
Her appearance and mannerisms were also strongly reflected in [[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]]. Both mother and daughter frequently maintained unreadable expressions, spoke relatively little and conveyed much of what they felt through small changes in behavior rather than extensive conversation.

### Personality
Hana was an extremely quiet and reserved woman. [[Alexios Alioth]] remembered her as someone who spoke very little and rarely changed her neutral expression, often using only the minimum number of words necessary to communicate.[1]
Her silence did not make her socially insignificant. Alexios specifically remembered that Hana's:
- charisma;
- presence;
- and strength
compensated for her lack of words, allowing her to naturally command attention despite rarely speaking.[1]
She possessed a gentler temperament than Haruna. Vlad remembered that although Hana normally showed very little emotion, speaking about her younger sister could cause a subtle smile to appear on her face. She genuinely loved Haruna despite the enormous age difference between them and remembered her from the period when Haruna was still a small one-tailed fox.[4]
Hana's strongest demonstrated emotional trait was her extreme maternal attachment toward Ophis.
Within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s artificial Reality, Hana immediately became intensely protective after encountering the young Ophis. She instinctively treated her daughter as something that needed to remain beside her at all times and quickly began regarding anyone who might interfere with that bond as a potential threat.[6][8]
This protectiveness could become possessive and ruthless.
Hana disliked Vlad's other wives and internally refused to regard them or their children as Ophis' true family. From her perspective, Ophis' real familial connection outside herself was primarily Hana's younger sister in Japan. She regretted bringing Ophis into an environment filled with women she distrusted and became increasingly willing to contemplate killing anyone she believed might threaten or manipulate her daughter.[8]
Despite this darker side, Hana's actions toward Ophis were rooted in genuine love rather than simple control. When Ophis eventually revealed the strength and identity she had acquired in the future, Hana's initial shock did not change her feelings. Even after realizing that the daughter she wanted to protect had become a Being vastly stronger than herself, Hana's conclusion remained simple:
Ophis was still her daughter.
[7]

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes and War
Hana died long before the beginning of the main story, and information concerning her existence was deliberately hidden from much of her surviving family.
Her earliest appearance chronologically within the narrative occurred through a memory belonging to [[Alexios Alioth]]. Upon seeing [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]], Alexios was immediately reminded of another black-haired, nine-tailed Kitsune he had known centuries earlier: Otsuki Hana, Ophis' mother.[1]
Alexios remembered Hana approaching him and asking where Vlad was using only a few words. The interaction emphasized her character immediately; Hana was a woman of few words who normally maintained a neutral expression, yet possessed enough charisma and personal strength that her silence never diminished her presence.[1]
Her death became one of the most important events in Vlad's history.
According to Alexios' recollections, Vlad made a disastrous mistake because of excessive confidence in his own strength. The result was the death of:
the most important woman in Vlad's life.
[1]
Hana was killed during a conflict involving [[Nightingale]]'s Elder Gods. Her death was so complete that there was no body available for burial afterward.[1][2]
The Elder God responsible was later identified as Shur, the God of Power.[3]
Hana's death fundamentally altered Vlad.
Before losing her, Vlad's goals had been centered largely upon:
- conquest;
- expansion;
- ruling Nightingale;
- and strengthening Vampire civilization.
After Hana's death, his priorities shifted toward revenge.
Vlad swore to destroy the Elder Gods responsible and began searching for methods capable of killing Beings that ordinary methods could not permanently eliminate.[1]
His grief and obsession also contributed to the emotional deterioration of his remaining household. Vlad increasingly withdrew from his wives and children while focusing upon revenge and the greater conflicts surrounding Nightingale. The neglect that followed eventually became one of the factors destabilizing several of his later marriages.
Hana's younger sister Otsuki Elderblood Haruna knew none of this.
Haruna had been extremely young during Hana's lifetime, and [[Otsuki Yoichi]] deliberately kept the existence of her older sister hidden. Vlad remembered Haruna from Hana's stories as a small one-tailed fox whom Hana deeply loved.[4]
The truth was finally revealed centuries later.
Haruna learned that she had possessed an older sister and that Ophis Tepes Elderblood was therefore her biological niece. The discovery shattered much of what she believed she knew about her family and caused her to confront Yoichi over having hidden Hana from her.[2]
Yoichi eventually brought Haruna to the memorial created for Hana.
The location was a protected paradise situated upon an isolated island. Vlad and Alexios Alioth had arranged its protection, with Alexios spatially separating the island so completely that no unauthorized Being could enter—even Gods.[2]
At the center stood Hana's grave.
The monument carried her name, but her actual body was not buried beneath it. Yoichi explained that there might not even have been enough of Hana's remains left to recover after her death.[2]
Haruna demanded to know how her sister had died.
Yoichi told her that an Elder God had killed Hana but admitted that only Vlad knew the exact circumstances because he had personally been present during the incident. Yoichi had blamed Vlad directly for failing to protect his granddaughter.[2]
The memorial remained extremely important to both Yoichi and Vlad. When Haruna later considered using the island for other members of the [[Otsuki Clan]], Yoichi strongly resisted allowing Hana's resting place to be treated casually or occupied by unrelated Yōkai.[9]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Centuries after Hana's death, her legacy became increasingly important through Ophis.
Because Ophis was born from the union of Hana and the Vampire Progenitor, she technically inherited characteristics from both parents. However, Vlad's Progenitor Bloodline was overwhelmingly dominant, leaving only a very small portion of Hana's Yōkai heritage visibly accessible during Ophis' childhood.[5]
[[Victor Elderblood]] eventually decided to deliberately awaken those suppressed powers.
He created a pair of specialized black gloves for Ophis using:
- his Soul-related powers;
- Draconic Runes;
- Creation;
- and other supernatural materials and techniques.[5]
The gloves allowed Ophis to access the tiny portion of her mother's Yōkai lineage that had previously remained dormant.
When activated, Ophis instinctively produced a Fireball despite having received no meaningful training with the ability. A black mantle of Energy subsequently covered her body before fox ears and nine tails composed from dark Energy appeared behind her.[5]
Victor directly explained:
This is the Power of your mother.
[5]
Ophis also demonstrated control over Wind, gathering the surrounding air around herself and using it to propel her body upward. The abilities felt natural to her despite their previous dormancy.[5]
During the awakening, Victor perceived something even more significant.
Behind Ophis appeared the transparent spiritual image of a woman possessing nine fox tails.
The woman silently bowed toward Victor in gratitude before disappearing.[5]
The manifestation was Hana.
Whether this represented a remaining fragment, spiritual echo or another form of post-mortem manifestation was not fully explained, but the scene demonstrated that some connection to Hana persisted strongly enough to respond when Ophis' maternal heritage was awakened.
The powers inherited through Hana remained part of Ophis even after Ophis later became a Dragon Goddess. Her maternal Nine-Tailed-Fox characteristics were not erased by her Draconic evolution.[10]
Ophis' Teleportation was later specifically identified as an ability inherited from her Yōkai mother, further establishing that spatial movement belonged to Hana's supernatural lineage.[10]
Hana's killer also finally faced the consequences of her death.
During the confrontation with Shur, both Vlad and Haruna fought the Elder God responsible for killing Hana. Vlad's memories of holding Hana's dead body and the rage surrounding her loss resurfaced during the encounter, reinforcing that Shur had been the target of his hatred for centuries.[3]
Shur was ultimately killed, allowing Vlad and Hana's surviving family to finally avenge her death.[3]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Hana later became central to Ophis' development toward Godhood.
After teaching Ophis and [[Nero Elderblood]] about Divinity and self-understanding, [[Victor Elderblood]] concluded that neither girl could awaken a proper personal Concept simply by being handed power. They needed experiences capable of forcing them to confront their identities and unresolved emotions.
Victor therefore created an elaborate false Reality through his authorities over Dreams and other aspects of existence and placed his daughters within specially constructed lives.[6]
Ophis' trial returned her psychologically toward a younger version of herself and placed her in circumstances where she could encounter the person whose absence had shaped much of her childhood:
Otsuki Hana.
[6]
The Hana appearing in this Reality was not the genuine resurrected Hana. She was a highly detailed reconstruction existing inside the artificial world Victor had created for Ophis' trial.
When Hana first saw Ophis, she was initially curious before quickly recognizing her as her daughter. Ophis' reaction was considerably more emotional. Despite everything she had experienced since Hana's death, seeing her mother again caused Ophis to regress almost immediately into the child who had lost her.[6]
Ophis cried and rushed directly into Hana's embrace.
Hana responded instinctively, embracing and protecting her daughter.
Her maternal instincts rapidly became intense enough that she began separating Ophis from Vlad's other wives, whom she regarded with distrust. Hana did not view those women as Ophis' true family and became convinced that remaining around them would only expose her daughter to unnecessary danger and manipulation.[8]
She took Ophis to her own quarters and kept her close, with her nine tails reacting visibly to her emotional state.
Hana's internal thoughts revealed that her gentle appearance concealed an extremely dangerous side. Anyone she perceived as a genuine threat toward Ophis could provoke openly murderous thoughts.[8]
The situation changed dramatically as Ophis' real power began leaking through the false Reality.
Her eyes briefly changed into the slit pupils of a Dragon.
Both Hana and Vlad instinctively experienced the sensation of becoming:
prey before a predator.
[7]
Hana initially struggled to understand how the child she remembered could possess such an overwhelming existence.
She eventually accepted that Ophis had become a Dragon and, more importantly, that none of those changes altered the fact that she remained Hana's daughter.[7]
Ophis subsequently revealed more information about her future.
She referred to Victor Elderblood as her other father and showed her parents an image representing him. Both Hana and Vlad reacted strongly to Victor's appearance, with Hana momentarily becoming visibly flustered despite the seriousness of the situation.[7]
Ophis also inadvertently revealed Hana's future death.
Hana quickly understood from her daughter's words and behavior that the expedition involving the Elder Gods would end disastrously. Vlad likewise realized what Hana's loss would do to him: he understood that his grief would drive him into an obsessive pursuit of revenge and cause him to neglect his surviving children and wives.[7]
Despite learning what awaited her, Hana remained focused primarily upon Ophis.
Before the artificial Reality ended, Ophis embraced her mother and finally expressed feelings she had carried for centuries. She admitted how badly she had missed Hana and thanked her for being her mother.[7]
Ophis told Hana that she had been a:
good mother.
[7]
The artificial Reality then collapsed as Ophis completed the emotional development Victor had intended the trial to provoke.
The experience did not truly resurrect Hana, but it gave Ophis an opportunity to confront grief and attachment that had remained unresolved since childhood.
[[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]] subsequently learned what Victor had done.
Unlike Ophis, Haruna reacted with considerably more fear and uncertainty toward the possibility of Hana returning for real. Having spent so long constructing an identity around a sister she had never personally known, Haruna was frightened by how an actual reunion might change her family and herself.[11]
Victor made clear that the false Reality was not intended to permanently substitute for Hana.
He promised both Ophis and Haruna that after resolving the danger surrounding [[Azathoth Elderblood]] and the larger problems preventing him from acting freely, he intended to:
bring Hana back properly.
[11]

### Relationships

#### Ophis Tepes Elderblood
[[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]] is Hana's only known daughter and the person toward whom Hana demonstrated the greatest emotional attachment.
Hana's death occurred while Ophis was still young enough that the loss shaped much of her childhood. Ophis subsequently grew up within Vlad's household without her biological mother and developed into an emotionally withdrawn child who struggled to properly express many of her feelings.
Despite Hana's death, Ophis retained a small portion of her mother's Yōkai lineage.[5]
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later awakened that inheritance, Ophis immediately demonstrated abilities associated with Hana's Nine-Tailed-Fox heritage, including Fire, Wind and the manifestation of nine dark fox tails.[5]
Ophis also inherited Teleportation from Hana's lineage.[10]
The strength of their spiritual connection was further suggested when Hana's transparent nine-tailed spirit appeared behind Ophis during the activation of Victor's gloves and silently bowed to Victor.[5]
Their reunion inside Victor's false Reality revealed the full emotional depth of the relationship.
Ophis immediately returned to behaving like the child who had lost her mother, while Hana reacted with overwhelming protective instincts.[6][8]
Hana struggled when confronted with how profoundly Ophis had changed but never stopped recognizing her as her daughter.
Ophis, in turn, finally told Hana directly that she had missed her, loved her and regarded her as a good mother.[7]

#### Vlad Dracula Tepes
[[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] was Hana's husband and the father of Ophis.
Among all of Vlad's wives, Hana was remembered as the woman he loved most deeply.[1]
Her importance to him was so great that her death permanently changed his personality and priorities.
Before Hana died, Vlad remained primarily focused upon conquest and the expansion of Vampire civilization. After losing her through a conflict in which his own arrogance had contributed to the disastrous outcome, his goals transformed into an obsessive desire to destroy the Elder Gods responsible.[1]
The grief did not disappear with time.
Centuries later, confronting Shur caused memories of Hana's death and Vlad holding her remains to immediately resurface.[3]
Vlad's reaction to Hana's death also contributed to the breakdown of his remaining family. His obsession with revenge caused him to become increasingly emotionally distant from his other wives and children.
This does not mean Hana herself was responsible for the betrayals that later occurred within Vlad's household. Rather, Vlad's inability to process her loss and his subsequent behavior created circumstances that progressively damaged those relationships.
Within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s artificial Reality, Hana and Vlad's relationship was shown from an earlier period before tragedy had completely changed them. Although Hana could be possessive over Ophis and distrusted Vlad's other wives, she still interacted with Vlad as her husband and expected him to protect their daughter.[8][7]

#### Otsuki Elderblood Haruna
[[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]] is Hana's younger sister.
Hana had known Haruna when the latter was still a very young one-tailed fox. Despite Hana's generally emotionless demeanor, Vlad remembered that mentioning Haruna could cause Hana to display a small but genuine smile.[4]
This established that Hana sincerely loved her younger sister.
Haruna herself was denied the opportunity to develop the same relationship.
Her family concealed Hana's existence after her death, causing Haruna to grow up without ever knowing that she had possessed an elder sister.[2]
When Haruna finally learned the truth, the revelation deeply destabilized her.
She discovered simultaneously that:
- Hana had existed;
- Hana had died;
- Ophis was her niece;
- and her grandfather had intentionally hidden all of it from her.[2]
Haruna's later battle against Shur therefore carried deeply personal significance even though she had barely known Hana herself.
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] recreated Hana inside Ophis' trial, Haruna was forced to confront an even more complicated possibility: Hana might genuinely be resurrected.
Rather than reacting only with happiness, Haruna admitted fear and uncertainty over meeting the sister she had built up in her mind for centuries.[11]
Victor nevertheless promised that Hana's genuine restoration remained one of his future intentions.

#### Otsuki Yoichi
[[Otsuki Yoichi]] is Hana and Haruna's grandfather.
Yoichi deeply mourned Hana and blamed Vlad for failing to protect her from the Elder God who killed her.[2]
After Hana's death, he participated in maintaining the secrecy surrounding her existence and did not tell Haruna about her elder sister.
His decision eventually damaged his relationship with Haruna once the truth came out.
Yoichi also treated Hana's memorial with extreme reverence and refused to casually allow other Yōkai to use the protected island dedicated to her memory.[9]

### Skills and Abilities
Hana's full combat ability was never shown directly before her death. However, she was an exceptionally powerful Nine-Tailed Black Kitsune, and several abilities inherited by [[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]] are explicitly identified as originating from Hana's Yōkai lineage.

#### Nine-Tailed Black Kitsune Physiology
- Yōkai Physiology: Hana was originally a Yōkai belonging to the [[Otsuki Clan]] and possessed the supernatural characteristics of a Kitsune.
- Nine-Tailed Kitsune Physiology: Hana possessed nine black fox tails, marking her as an exceptionally developed member of her species. Nine-Tailed Kitsune represent highly advanced fox Yōkai whose power greatly exceeds that of younger members of the species.
- Youki Manipulation: As a high-level Yōkai, Hana possessed the supernatural Energy traditionally associated with her Race. The dark Yōkai power inherited by Ophis remained dormant beneath Vlad's Bloodline until [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] deliberately awakened it.[5]
- Nine-Tailed Fox Manifestation: Hana naturally possessed nine physical black fox tails. Ophis' inherited maternal power reproduced fox ears and nine tails composed from dark Energy when activated.[5]
- Dark Energy Manifestation: When Ophis accessed Hana's heritage, a mantle of black Energy spread over her body before her fox characteristics manifested.[5] This provides a direct demonstration of the energetic characteristics inherited from Hana's lineage.
- Elemental Manipulation: Hana's maternal lineage possessed natural control over multiple Elements. When Ophis accessed her mother's power, Fire and Wind manifested instinctively despite Ophis having no prior meaningful training with them.[5]
- [[Pyrokinesis]]: Ophis immediately created a Fireball after accessing Hana's power, establishing Fire manipulation as part of the supernatural inheritance originating from her mother.[5]
- Aerokinesis: Ophis instinctively manipulated surrounding Wind through her inherited Yōkai abilities.[5]
- Wind Propulsion: Ophis used Wind to propel herself upward and move through the air after her maternal lineage awakened.[5]
- Teleportation: Ophis' Teleportation was later explicitly identified as an unusual power originating from her Yōkai mother.[10] This establishes spatial movement as one of the abilities associated with Hana's lineage.
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Hana possessed strength, speed, durability and supernatural physical capabilities consistent with an advanced Nine-Tailed Kitsune. Alexios specifically remembered her strength as one of the characteristics that compensated for her quiet personality.[1]
- Enhanced Senses: As a developed Kitsune, Hana possessed senses beyond those of ordinary Humans.
- Longevity: Yōkai possess supernatural lifespans far exceeding Humans, and Hana had lived long enough to reach the Nine-Tailed stage before her death.
- Supernatural Charisma: Despite rarely speaking, Hana possessed enough natural charisma that Alexios remembered her presence as immediately significant.[1]
- Supernatural Beauty: Hana possessed the extraordinary attractiveness common to high-ranking supernatural beings and retained a gentle appearance distinct from Haruna's more severe features.[6]

#### Noble Vampire Physiology
After becoming one of Vlad's wives, Hana was converted into a Noble Vampire while retaining characteristics associated with her original Kitsune lineage.
- Noble Vampire Physiology: Hana acquired the supernatural racial characteristics of a Noble Vampire.
- Enhanced Physical Characteristics: Vampirism further strengthened her already supernatural body.
- Regeneration: Noble Vampires possess regenerative abilities superior to ordinary Humans and lesser supernatural beings.
- Enhanced Senses: Her Vampiric nature further enhanced her perception.
- [[Immortality]] / Longevity: Vampires possess extreme longevity and cease aging normally after transformation.
- Vampiric Charm: As a Noble Vampire, Hana possessed the supernatural attraction and influence characteristic of the Race, although no individual feat establishes the maximum strength of her Charm.
- Hybridized Power Retention: Hana did not lose the supernatural characteristics of her Kitsune lineage after becoming associated with the Vampire Royal Family. Her nine tails and maternal Yōkai heritage remained sufficiently integral to her existence that Ophis inherited them centuries later.[5][10]

### Power
Hana's exact position among the strongest supernatural beings of her era cannot be quantified because the novel has not shown a complete battle involving her.
She was nevertheless explicitly remembered as possessing significant:
- strength;
- charisma;
- and supernatural presence.[1]
Her status as a Nine-Tailed Black Kitsune also places her far beyond an ordinary young Yōkai.
The Otsuki lineage itself is exceptionally powerful, and Ophis retained enough of Hana's diluted inheritance for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to consider awakening it worthwhile even after Ophis had already obtained enormously powerful abilities from Vlad and Victor.[5]
Hana should not, however, automatically be scaled to later Haruna.
Haruna underwent numerous additional evolutions, battles and transformations after Hana's death, eventually developing powers Hana was never shown using.
Likewise, Ophis' eventual strength does not represent Hana's personal level.
During Victor's artificial Reality, a manifestation of Ophis' later Draconic power caused both Hana and Vlad to instinctively feel like prey before a superior predator.[7]
The comparison establishes that Ophis had eventually grown beyond the power level represented by her parents within the reconstruction.
Hana was ultimately killed by Shur, an Elder God. This does not establish that she was weak; Elder Gods belonged to a category of ancient supernatural entities capable of threatening even some of [[Nightingale]]'s most powerful inhabitants.

### Death and Legacy
Hana's death had consequences extending far beyond the loss of one Queen.

#### Vlad's Revenge
Her death transformed Vlad's attitude toward the Elder Gods.
The Vampire Progenitor abandoned his previous emphasis upon conquest and began seeking methods capable of permanently killing the ancient entities responsible.[1]
This obsession lasted for centuries and culminated in the confrontation with Shur.[3]

#### Ophis' Childhood
Ophis grew up without her biological mother.
The loss contributed to her emotional isolation and left feelings that remained unresolved even after she joined [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family.
Victor's artificial Reality was specifically useful because it allowed Ophis to emotionally experience the mother she had lost rather than merely being told information about her.[6][7]

#### Haruna's Identity
Haruna's family concealed Hana's existence after her death.
Discovering Hana centuries later forced Haruna to reconsider her grandfather, Vlad, Ophis and her own understanding of the Otsuki family.[2]

#### Maternal Bloodline
Hana's supernatural lineage survived through Ophis.
Despite Vlad's Progenitor Blood suppressing nearly all visible Yōkai traits during Ophis' childhood, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later awakened the remaining maternal heritage and demonstrated that Hana's powers had never truly disappeared from her daughter.[5]

#### Possible Restoration
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] has explicitly stated his intention to restore Hana properly after resolving the greater problems surrounding [[Azathoth Elderblood]].[11]
This is stronger than simply theorizing that Victor might someday be capable of resurrection.
He directly promised Ophis and Haruna that Hana was someone he intended to bring back.
As of her latest confirmed status, however, that resurrection has not yet occurred.
