### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 368
Kurama, known as the Calamity Fox, was an ancient nine-tailed Kitsune and one of the most manipulative Yokai to operate within Japan. Rather than seeking territory or direct political rule for himself, Kurama spent centuries influencing wars from behind the scenes because conflict and bloodshed directly contributed to his own growth. Beginning as a seven-year-old fox who genuinely wished for a peaceful world, his experiences during Japan's wars radically changed his worldview until he concluded that lasting peace was neither natural nor desirable and that civilization advanced most effectively through deception, violence and conflict.[1]
Kurama became one of [[Oda Nobunaga]]'s principal followers while still extraordinarily young and eventually gained fame in both Human and supernatural society as one of Oda's Great Generals. Despite helping Nobunaga achieve his dream of unifying Japan, Kurama later joined [[Akechi Mitsuhide]] and [[Toyotomi Hideyoshi]] in betraying him. He subsequently turned that betrayal into yet another conflict by accusing Hideyoshi of treason and inciting war against him.[1]
Over the following centuries Kurama repeatedly exploited Human conflicts for his own benefit, including both World Wars, and entered a pact with [[Inari-Omikami]] that increased or maintained his power while binding him geographically to Japan. During the modern era, he attempted to engineer a war between Japan's supernatural factions and the Vampires by deliberately exposing the presence of [[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]], the daughter of [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]]. Instead of producing the balanced conflict Kurama desired, his scheme attracted [[Victor Elderblood]], whose retaliation devastated Japan's supernatural population and indirectly led to Inari's defeat. Inari's resulting coma suspended Kurama's pact and finally allowed him to leave Japan.[1]
Kurama successfully disappeared overseas and remained hidden for years, but Victor never forgot that his manipulation had placed Ophis in mortal danger. After Victor's ascension as the [[Chaos]] Dragon God, War eventually captured Kurama from his hiding place in Europe and delivered him for judgment. Unlike several of Victor's lesser enemies, Kurama was denied both conventional death and ordinary punishment because Victor considered him directly responsible for harming his daughter. He was exposed to Victor's incomprehensible Cosmic Horror existence and subjected to repeated bodily destruction and restoration before being dragged into Victor's domain.[2][3]
Although Chapter 934 initially described Kurama's punishment as effectively endless, his final fate was clarified immediately afterward. Victor ultimately devoured Kurama. Through the evolved power of Predation, Victor obtained complete access to Kurama's form, abilities, characteristics and memories and could reproduce a Kyuubi Kitsune body based upon him. Kurama therefore no longer remains alive under eternal torture; his existence was ultimately consumed by Victor.[4]

### Physical Appearance
Kurama normally manifested as a pale-skinned man with red hair, red eyes and nine large fox tails. His tails were long and red and became particularly visible when he was pleased or excited, such as when he realized Inari's coma had suspended the pact preventing him from freely leaving Japan.[1]
His appearance was not fixed because transformation was one of Kurama's greatest natural talents. When preparing to leave Japan, he completely transformed himself into an ordinary white coastal bird whose appearance would not attract attention. The transformation was convincing enough to function as a practical disguise rather than merely altering superficial characteristics.[1]
Kurama retained his nine tails in his normal humanoid state centuries later. When he was captured and brought before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s former enemies, he was immediately identifiable as a nine-tailed Youkai, and after War transferred him to Victor's Cosmic domain he appeared there as a man possessing nine tails.[2][3]

### Personality
Kurama was intelligent, deceptive, calculating and extraordinarily patient. His defining characteristic was his belief that deception and bloodshed were the true forces responsible for changing civilization. As a child he desired peace, but witnessing Japan descend into war when he was seven destroyed that idealism. Kurama came to believe that the world required little more than a convincing liar and sufficient bloodshed for history to move in a new direction.[1]
His philosophy eventually became considerably more extreme. Kurama no longer merely accepted war as unavoidable; he actively considered continuous conflict desirable. In his view, peace should only ever be temporary because warfare forced Humans and supernatural beings to adapt, evolve and become stronger. This philosophy also conveniently benefited Kurama personally because war and bloodshed strengthened him.[1]
Kurama was consequently willing to manufacture reasons for entire wars. He considered political truth almost irrelevant so long as he could construct a sufficiently believable justification for violence. After helping betray Nobunaga, he immediately accused Hideyoshi of treason and used the accusation to generate another war even though Kurama himself had participated in the exact same betrayal.[1]
Despite this ruthlessness, Kurama followed an unusual personal concept of repayment. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s actions against Inari unintentionally freed Kurama from the geographical restrictions of their pact, Kurama considered himself indebted to Victor. Before leaving Japan, he sent Victor information concerning another nine-tailed Kitsune who was sheltering Noble Vampires Victor had been searching for. Kurama framed this as repaying a debt, although he also privately hoped that giving Victor another target might encourage the dangerous Vampire to forget Kurama existed.[1]
Kurama was cautious enough to understand when manipulation had failed catastrophically. Once Victor arrived in Japan, Kurama immediately recognized that the situation was no longer the war he had attempted to engineer. The Japanese factions were simply too weak compared with Victor and his companions, making their conflict a one-sided extermination rather than the mutually competitive bloodshed Kurama valued. For the first time in his extremely long life, Kurama regarded another individual less as a political piece and more like a natural disaster.[1]
His instinct for self-preservation was extremely strong. Kurama had survived centuries precisely because he avoided openly exposing himself while manipulating conflicts from safety. After Victor's massacre he hid inside a cave constructed by a Witch and deliberately suppressed his Youki because he understood the danger presented by Victor's assassins. When his instincts later warned him that something terrible was approaching, he immediately fled rather than investigating the sensation.[1]
Despite all of his intelligence, Kurama could become visibly frustrated when deprived of control. After awakening in Victor's prison in Chapter 933, he initially attempted to leave despite the other prisoners' amusement. He repeatedly tested the sealed room, struck the door and attempted to escape through a window before accepting that the prison was beyond his ability to overcome. Once he realized Victor had finally come to collect the debt created by Ophis' suffering, much of Kurama's confidence gave way to resignation and fear.[2]

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Kurama's story began centuries before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s arrival in Japan. When he was only seven years old, Japan was engulfed in warfare. Until that point Kurama had been an unusually kind child who genuinely believed that a world without conflict might be possible. Witnessing war at such a young age fundamentally changed him. Instead of concluding that violence needed to be stopped, Kurama decided that bloodshed revealed the true nature of the world and that deception combined with violence was capable of reshaping history.[1]
During this period he encountered [[Oda Nobunaga]], who referred to himself as the Seventh Heavenly Demon and sought to unify Japan. Even at seven years old, Kurama was already capable of using the transformation abilities characteristic of fox Youkai and entered Human warfare in disguise. Nobunaga asked whether he wanted to change the world, and Kurama accepted. The young Kitsune considered Nobunaga a charismatic liar whose ambitions aligned with his newly developing worldview, while Kurama himself already recognized deception as the natural specialty of a fox.[1]
Kurama rapidly established himself as an important figure beneath Nobunaga. He became known in both the supernatural and Human worlds as the Calamity Fox and ultimately served as one of Oda's Great Generals. Nobunaga's forces also employed the [[Kuroyami Clan]], a family of Vampires capable of controlling darkness who functioned as highly effective assassins. This was the early name later associated with the lineage eventually known as [[Blank Clan]].[1]
In less than several years, Nobunaga successfully unified Japan and temporarily achieved the peace he had sought. Kurama's loyalty ended almost immediately afterward. Only three days following the unification, [[Akechi Mitsuhide]], [[Toyotomi Hideyoshi]] and Kurama betrayed Nobunaga and forced him to commit seppuku during what became known as the Honnō-ji Incident.[1]
Kurama then demonstrated how completely his beliefs had changed. After participating in Nobunaga's betrayal himself, he accused Hideyoshi of betraying and killing their former master and used the accusation to create yet another war. Kurama no longer wanted a permanently changed or peaceful Japan. Instead, he wanted a repeating cycle in which peace existed only long enough for the next conflict to begin.[1]
This obsession was partially practical. Kurama explicitly grew stronger from wars and bloodshed. The conflicts he personally engineered increased his power, and when Humanity eventually produced the First and Second World Wars without his direct intervention, Kurama used both global conflicts as opportunities to strengthen himself even further.[1]
The World Wars also dramatically expanded Kurama's understanding of the supernatural world. Foreign nations exposed him to supernatural races and powers beyond those he had previously encountered in Japan, giving him an increasing desire to travel abroad and manipulate conflicts on a global scale. Unfortunately for Kurama, a pact he had made with [[Inari-Omikami]] prevented him from freely doing so. Leaving Japan would cause his power to become significantly weaker, forcing him to remain inside the country and continue serving Inari despite increasingly regretting the agreement.[1]
Centuries later, the development of modern communication finally gave Kurama a new opportunity. One of his subordinates informed him about an unusual young girl present in Japan. Kurama initially ignored the report, but further investigation revealed that the girl was Ophis Tepes, daughter of the Vampire King [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]].[1]
Rather than avoiding someone connected to one of the oldest Vampires alive, Kurama saw an opportunity. He deliberately spread rumors and photographs exposing Ophis' location, expecting Japan's supernatural creatures to pursue the apparently vulnerable princess. Kurama's objective was to provoke a confrontation between the Vampires and Japan's supernatural factions and then benefit from the resulting war.[1]
His plan initially worked exactly as intended. Within days supernatural beings throughout Japan began hunting Ophis. What Kurama failed to account for was the identity of the person Ophis considered her father.
Count Alucard arrived in Japan.
Kurama immediately felt Victor's overwhelming presence descend upon the country and understood that something far beyond an ordinary Vampire Count had entered the conflict. He had expected Victor's arrival to ignite a major supernatural war, but the power difference was far too extreme. Victor and the people accompanying him began massacring the supernatural factions responsible for targeting Ophis, and approximately half of Japan's supernatural population was eliminated over the course of the resulting disaster.[1]
Kurama was horrified by the result. To him, proper war required two sides capable of meaningfully opposing each other. Victor's faction and Japan's existing supernatural forces were not remotely comparable, meaning the bloodshed Kurama had engineered had ceased being a war at all. Kurama compared Victor to a calamity or natural disaster rather than another political actor and recognized that he had badly miscalculated.[1]
The consequences nevertheless created an unexpected opportunity. [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] defeated Inari so decisively that the Goddess entered an effectively indefinite coma. Because Inari was no longer actively functioning, Kurama's pact with her became suspended until she recovered. For the first time in centuries, Kurama could leave Japan without suffering the severe reduction in strength normally imposed by the contract.[1]
Rather than remaining to rebuild his influence, Kurama immediately decided to exploit his freedom. He still possessed followers in Japan even after losing [[Gyuki]], including Oni who could potentially be recruited, but considered remaining in the country unnecessary. He transformed into an ordinary white bird, completely suppressed his Youki and prepared to leave Japan for the wider world.[1]
Before departing, Kurama decided to repay Victor for inadvertently freeing him. He possessed a scroll containing information about a nine-tailed fox woman sheltering Noble Vampires Victor had been actively searching for. Kurama's eyes glowed red and the scroll disappeared as he sent it elsewhere, intending the information to reach Victor. Kurama characterized the action as repayment for his accidental liberation, while privately recognizing that giving Victor another target might also keep the Vampire occupied.[1]
Immediately afterward Kurama experienced a familiar warning sensation: a shiver that historically accompanied the feeling that something bad was going to happen. Rather than ignore his instincts, he fled Japan as quickly as possible.[1]
His caution worked for a considerable period. Kurama eventually reached Europe and disappeared into an isolated hiding place, successfully remaining outside Victor's immediate reach for years. However, Victor never forgot what Kurama had done to Ophis.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
By the time [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had evolved into the [[Chaos]] Dragon God, Kurama's past finally caught up with him. He suddenly lost consciousness while hiding in Europe and awakened inside a strange sealed room without understanding how anyone had located or captured him. He found himself imprisoned alongside Lucy, Karen, [[Luan Davis]] and [[Corneliu Funar]], each of whom possessed their own history with Victor.[2]
Lucy questioned Kurama about his identity and asked what he had done to anger Victor. Kurama's immediate physical reaction upon hearing Victor's name revealed that he understood exactly why he might have been targeted. He nevertheless initially refused to cooperate and attempted to escape from the room.[2]
Every attempt failed. Kurama could not open the door even after channeling greater power through his body, and striking it only injured his hand. He attempted to escape through a window and instead smashed directly into an indestructible barrier. More concerningly, Kurama felt noticeably weaker inside the prison. He could not remember being attacked in Europe at all; one moment he had been safely hidden and the next he had simply lost consciousness.[2]
Luan eventually explained the obvious reality: Kurama had harmed Victor in the past, and Victor had finally decided to settle the debt. Kurama recognized that the person he had once manipulated from afar was no longer merely a Vampire Count but the acknowledged Chaos Dragon God. Unlike Lucy, Karen and Luan, whose crimes Victor regarded as comparatively minor or whose later actions had earned them other opportunities, Kurama and Corneliu understood that they had personally crossed lines Victor would never forget.[2]
The room darkened when War, the Bearer of the [[Will Solace|Will]] of the Chaos Dragon God, arrived to judge the prisoners. Kurama's instincts immediately reacted with terror to War's presence. The difference in power was so great that resistance became meaningless before the judgment even reached him.[2]
War first rewarded Thomas and Jimmy for their later service, then extended similar opportunities to Lucy and Karen. Corneliu was offered either complete Soul erasure or eternal torture and chose erasure. When War finally turned toward Kurama, the treatment was fundamentally different.[3]
War declared that ordinary death and even eternal torture were too merciful for Kurama because he had been one of the individuals responsible for harming Victor's daughter. Before Kurama could react, War seized him by the throat and transported him away through darkness.[3]
Kurama reappeared in an incomprehensible region where the newly created [[Heralds of Chaos]] knelt before Victor's enormous Cosmic Horror manifestation. The sight before him was not merely a gigantic creature. Victor's Eldritch presence altered the surrounding Reality, distorted celestial bodies and simultaneously devoured and generated abnormal cosmic structures.[3]
[[Amara Elderblood]] and [[Roxanne Elderblood]] appeared and erected a defensive barrier around the Heralds before Victor acted. A strange sound spread through space and Victor released his Energy directly toward Kurama. The effect immediately overwhelmed the ancient Kitsune. Blood poured from his eyes while he screamed and tore at his own hair; his body distorted into an unrecognizable mass of flesh, exploded and then returned to its normal appearance so that the punishment could continue.[3]
Kurama was ultimately dragged deeper into Victor's Cosmic existence, where Chapter 934 described him as destined to suffer incomprehensible horrors. This originally created the impression that Kurama remained alive under permanent punishment.[3]
Chapter 935 subsequently clarified his final fate. Victor had ultimately consumed Kurama. While experimenting with the evolved Predation ability obtained from [[Ikor-Kar-VI]], Victor transformed his body into a Kyuubi Kitsune possessing fox ears and ten tails and explained that, because he had made Kurama his "cosmic snack", he possessed complete access to the fox's form, powers, abilities and memories. Victor's own Draconic nature modified what he reproduced, but the source of the Kitsune characteristics was Kurama.[4]
Kurama's punishment therefore ended not with continued imprisonment but with his complete defeat and consumption by the Being he had once tried to manipulate from the shadows.

### Skills and Abilities
Kurama was an exceptionally old and experienced nine-tailed Kitsune, but relatively few of his abilities were demonstrated directly in combat. His known powers instead emphasize transformation, concealment, survival and manipulating large-scale conflicts from the shadows. Abilities not directly demonstrated or specifically attributed to Kurama are not listed here merely because other Kitsune possess them.
- Nine-Tailed Kitsune Physiology: Kurama was a Kyuubi Kitsune possessing nine fox tails. His supernatural body generated and used Youki, and his existence was powerful enough to survive for more than five centuries while continuously increasing his strength through warfare.[1][2]
- Youki: Kurama possessed the spiritual Energy characteristic of Youkai. He could consciously suppress this Energy to prevent supernatural observers from detecting him.[1]
- War and Bloodshed Empowerment: Kurama explicitly stated that war, conflict and bloodshed made him stronger. This was one of the major reasons he deliberately created wars rather than pursuing peaceful political power.[1]
- Conflict Empowerment: Wars engineered by Kurama increased his strength over time.[1]
- World War Empowerment: Kurama took advantage of both the First and Second World Wars and stated that they strengthened him significantly more than the smaller wars he had previously created himself.[1]
- Transformation: Transformation was one of Kurama's primary supernatural abilities. Even at seven years old he could use the natural transformation talent of foxes to disguise himself and participate in Human warfare.[1]
- Shapeshifting: Kurama was described as capable of transforming himself into whatever form he wanted.[1]
- Animal Transformation: When escaping Japan, Kurama transformed completely into an ordinary white bird commonly found near Japanese beaches.[1]
- Human Transformation: Kurama used transformation to enter Human society and participate directly in Nobunaga's campaigns while still a young fox.[1]
- Disguise Mastery: Kurama was explicitly described as a master of disguise, combining his shapeshifting with extensive practical experience remaining hidden while influencing conflicts.[1]
- Youki Concealment: Kurama could completely hide his Youki, allowing him to suppress the supernatural signature that would otherwise reveal him as a powerful Yokai.[1]
- Presence Concealment: By combining suppressed Youki with transformation, Kurama could convincingly pass as an ordinary creature rather than merely disguising his physical appearance.[1]
- Danger Intuition: Kurama occasionally experienced an instinctive shiver when something dangerous was about to happen. He recognized the sensation from previous experiences and immediately treated it as a warning to flee rather than an ordinary physical reaction.[1]
- This ability was not omniscient. It warned Kurama that danger was approaching after the Japanese incident but did not reveal the identity, location or exact nature of the future threat.[1]
- Remote Transmission: Before leaving Japan, Kurama caused a scroll containing intelligence to disappear from his table and sent it elsewhere after his eyes briefly glowed red. The chapter does not explain whether this was spatial manipulation, a prepared spell, a Youkai technique or another method, so no broader teleportation ability should be inferred from the feat.[1]
- Divine Pact: Kurama maintained a long-term supernatural pact with [[Inari-Omikami]]. The contract tied his power to Japan and prevented him from freely traveling abroad without becoming substantially weaker.[1]
- Geographical Restriction: Leaving Japan while the pact remained active would cause Kurama's power to decrease considerably.[1]
- Contract Suspension: When Inari entered her coma following her defeat by [[Scathach]], the pact ceased actively restricting Kurama until Inari's eventual return, allowing him to leave Japan without accepting the previous loss of strength.[1]
- The precise benefits Kurama originally received from the pact are not completely explained. The chapter establishes the restriction and his continued service to Inari but does not provide a complete list of powers granted by the Goddess.[1]
- Deception Mastery: Kurama considered deception the defining specialty of a fox and spent centuries refining his ability to manipulate both individuals and societies.[1]
- Political Manipulation: He repeatedly created believable narratives designed to turn existing factions against one another rather than directly fighting his targets himself.[1]
- False Accusations: After betraying Nobunaga alongside Hideyoshi, Kurama successfully used Hideyoshi's involvement in the betrayal as justification for creating another war against him.[1]
- Rumor Manipulation: Kurama spread information, rumors and photographs concerning Ophis throughout Japan's supernatural community, deliberately turning numerous factions against her.[1]
- Information Warfare: Kurama understood that modern communications allowed him to create conflicts far more efficiently than traditional physical manipulation. His plan involving Ophis depended upon rapidly distributing information across supernatural society rather than personally confronting anyone.[1]
- War Manipulation: Kurama possessed centuries of experience engineering political and supernatural conflicts. Rather than simply participating in wars, he deliberately attempted to manufacture the conditions necessary for them to occur.[1]
- Conflict Instigation: Kurama could identify existing tensions and create a trigger capable of turning those tensions into open violence.[1]
- Proxy Warfare: His preferred strategy was to make other groups fight while he remained hidden and grew stronger from the resulting bloodshed.[1]
- Long-Term Planning: Kurama was patient enough to manipulate events across decades or centuries and understood that periods of peace could be exploited as preparation for the next conflict.[1]
- Espionage and Intelligence Gathering: Kurama maintained subordinates capable of investigating targets and collecting information throughout Japan. Ophis only became relevant to him after one of his followers discovered her and Kurama ordered a deeper investigation.[1]
- Information Network: Even after losing [[Gyuki]] and suffering the collapse of much of Japan's supernatural society, Kurama still possessed additional followers and potential Oni recruits.[1]
- Target Investigation: Kurama was able to determine Ophis' connection to [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] before deliberately exposing her identity.[1]
- Military Leadership: Kurama served as one of [[Oda Nobunaga]]'s Great Generals and participated directly in the campaigns that culminated in Japan's unification.[1]
- Command Experience: His position under Nobunaga and later possession of supernatural subordinates demonstrate extensive experience directing others rather than functioning solely as an individual infiltrator.[1]
- Exceptional Survival Instincts: Kurama survived for centuries despite repeatedly manipulating wars and extremely dangerous supernatural factions. His survival depended upon avoiding direct exposure, concealing his true location and retreating whenever a situation exceeded what he could control.[1]
- Hidden Bases: After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s arrival in Japan, Kurama immediately retreated to a magically concealed cave constructed by a Witch and specifically designed to remain impossible to locate against his wishes.[1]

### Relationships

#### Oda Nobunaga
[[Oda Nobunaga]] was the figure most responsible for shaping Kurama's worldview. Kurama encountered him at only seven years old while Japan was engulfed in war. Nobunaga's charisma and determination to unify the country convinced the young fox to abandon passive idealism and directly enter Human conflict.[1]
Kurama rose beneath Nobunaga until he became one of his Great Generals and participated in the campaigns responsible for unifying Japan. Despite this history, Kurama eventually betrayed Nobunaga alongside [[Akechi Mitsuhide]] and [[Toyotomi Hideyoshi]] only days after unification had been achieved. Kurama's later behavior demonstrated that he valued the conflict Nobunaga created more than he valued Nobunaga himself.[1]

#### Inari-Omikami
Kurama eventually entered a pact with Inari and served the Fox Goddess for centuries. The agreement prevented him from freely leaving Japan without losing a substantial amount of his strength, something Kurama came to deeply resent once he discovered how extensive the supernatural world outside Japan actually was.[1]
Kurama's loyalty toward Inari was largely practical rather than affectionate. He used her Divine backing as another convenient justification for violence and privately insulted her while regretting the contract. When [[Scathach]] placed Inari into a coma, Kurama showed no grief. Instead, he immediately celebrated the suspension of their agreement and used the opportunity to leave Japan.[1]

#### Gyuki
[[Gyuki]] was one of the supernatural figures associated with Kurama's faction. By the end of the Japanese incident Kurama acknowledged that he had lost Gyuki, but considered the loss survivable because other followers and potential Oni recruits still remained available to him.[1]
The currently available Kurama chapter does not establish enough detail to accurately reconstruct the full nature of their relationship or justify a precise comparative power ranking between the two.

#### Ophis Tepes Elderblood
Kurama never targeted Ophis because of a personal grievance. To him, she was initially a political tool whose identity as [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]]' daughter could be exploited to create a major supernatural war.[1]
After learning who she was, Kurama spread photographs and rumors concerning her location throughout Japan's supernatural society. His actions caused numerous supernatural factions to pursue Ophis and directly placed her in danger.[1]
This decision ultimately caused Kurama's destruction. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] regarded Ophis as his daughter, and years later War specifically identified Kurama's responsibility for harming her as the reason he would not be granted the comparatively merciful punishments offered to Victor's other enemies.[3]

#### Victor Elderblood
Kurama initially viewed [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] as a useful source of conflict. After exposing Ophis, he expected the newly established Vampire Count to clash with Japan's supernatural factions and create the kind of prolonged war from which Kurama could benefit.[1]
Victor's arrival completely destroyed that calculation. The disparity between Victor's faction and the Japanese forces transformed Kurama's planned war into an overwhelming massacre. Kurama subsequently viewed Victor as something closer to a natural calamity than an ordinary supernatural ruler and immediately began taking precautions to avoid his attention.[1]
Kurama nevertheless considered Victor indirectly responsible for granting him freedom because [[Scathach]]'s defeat of Inari suspended his restrictive pact. In response, he sent Victor intelligence concerning another nine-tailed Kitsune as repayment. The gesture did not erase his original actions against Ophis.[1]
Victor remembered Kurama for years. Once his power and reach had expanded sufficiently, War captured Kurama on Victor's behalf and condemned him specifically for harming Ophis. Victor then subjected Kurama to Eldritch punishment and ultimately devoured him.[3][4]
Kurama's existence subsequently became part of Victor through Predation. Victor gained access to Kurama's memories and could reproduce his complete Kitsune form, powers and abilities while retaining Victor's fundamentally Draconic Soul, Heart and Eyes.[4]

#### War
War acted as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s judgment and executioner when the God Emperor decided to resolve his remaining enemies. Kurama immediately reacted to War with instinctive fear and was incapable of meaningfully resisting him.[2]
Unlike the options War offered other captives, Kurama received no meaningful choice. War declared that ordinary death and conventional eternal torture were too good for someone responsible for harming Victor's daughter and personally delivered Kurama into Victor's Cosmic Horror manifestation.[3]

### Trivia
- Kurama was only seven years old when he first joined [[Oda Nobunaga]].[1]
- The chapter does not provide an exact calendar year for seven-year-old Kurama; the previously used "Year 1580" date should therefore not be presented as a direct novel statement without a separate source.
- Kurama originally wanted a peaceful world before warfare radically changed his worldview.[1]
- He became known in both Human and supernatural society as the Calamity Fox and Oda Nobunaga's Great General.[1]
- Kurama personally participated in Nobunaga's betrayal before later accusing [[Toyotomi Hideyoshi]] of betraying Nobunaga and using the accusation to create another war.[1]
- He explicitly became stronger through war and bloodshed and used both World Wars to increase his strength.[1]
- Kurama's pact with Inari prevented him from freely leaving Japan without becoming substantially weaker.[1]
- His plot involving Ophis was intended to cause a war between Vampires and Japan's supernatural community. Instead, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s arrival resulted in the destruction of approximately half of Japan's supernatural population.[1]
- Kurama was one of the few characters who openly recognized the distinction between an actual war and a completely one-sided massacre; he considered Victor's Japanese campaign the latter because Japan's supernatural forces could not meaningfully oppose him.[1]
- He could transform into virtually any form he wanted and completely conceal his Youki.[1]
- Kurama successfully escaped Japan and remained hidden in Europe until War captured him years later.[2]
- Chapter 934 initially described Kurama as being dragged into Victor's Cosmic existence to suffer indefinitely, but Chapter 935 clarifies that Victor ultimately consumed him. His current status should therefore be Deceased, not "Alive (Eternal Torment)".[3][4]
- After devouring Kurama, Victor demonstrated that Predation gave him access to Kurama's complete Kitsune form, powers, abilities and memories.[4]
