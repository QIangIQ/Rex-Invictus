### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 58
Eddy is a Kitsune who works as a bartender and information broker at [[The Lost Club]], the supernatural establishment operated by Esther in Brooklyn. Although he normally presents himself as an eccentric bartender, Eddy is heavily involved in the Club's information business, possesses extensive knowledge of the supernatural underworld and frequently sells intelligence to mercenaries and other clients. He is particularly close to [[Johnny Lykos]], serving as one of Johnny's oldest friends and regular sources of information.[2][3]
Eddy is fiercely loyal to Esther and treats her authority over The Lost Club seriously. Despite his friendship with Johnny, he explicitly warned the Werewolf that friendship would not take priority over his loyalty to Madam if Johnny's actions endangered the Club or provoked the great Vampire Clans.[4] His relationship with Esther is also sexual, with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later discovering the two engaged in a BDSM relationship in which Esther occupied the dominant role.[1] However, Victor's evolved eyes revealed that their romantic feelings were one-sided in the opposite direction: Esther's red thread of affection was connected to Eddy, while Eddy's corresponding thread did not point back toward Esther. Instead, it connected to Johnny, revealing that Eddy was secretly romantically attracted to his Werewolf friend.[1]

### Physical Appearance
Eddy is a man with black hair and distinctive neon-green eyes. During his work at [[The Lost Club]], he normally dresses in a conventional bartender uniform.[2]
His eyes can become visibly luminous when his supernatural nature becomes more pronounced. During an argument with Johnny, Eddy's eyes glowed neon green as he warned him that his loyalty belonged to Esther and that he would personally turn Johnny over to the [[Vampire Counts]] if the Werewolf endangered The Lost Club.[4]
Although Eddy openly works among supernatural beings, his identity as a Kitsune is normally concealed. Esther placed several magical wards around him specifically to hide his Race from others. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] nevertheless identified Eddy immediately as a fox after examining him with his supernatural perception, greatly surprising both Eddy and Esther because the wards should have prevented ordinary observers from discovering his true nature.[5]

### Personality
Eddy is energetic, theatrical, sarcastic and highly business-minded. Even during ordinary conversations he tends to use exaggerated gestures and comedic reactions, causing Johnny to remark that Eddy's behavior remained as overdramatic as ever.[2] Beneath that eccentric exterior, however, Eddy is considerably more observant and practical than his mannerisms initially suggest.
Money is one of his most obvious motivations. Eddy treats virtually any useful piece of information as merchandise and refuses to allow friendship to interfere with business. When Johnny asked him for information concerning Leona's new romantic interest, Eddy had already prepared a complete report on [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] but still required Johnny to pay before handing it over.[3] He displays the same opportunistic mentality when violence erupts inside [[The Lost Club]]; after watching Johnny repeatedly get into fights, Eddy began considering whether the Club could profit by formally organizing a fighting venue because customers clearly enjoyed watching the conflicts.[3]
Despite his love of profit, Eddy takes the supernatural consequences of his work seriously. He warned Johnny not to become unnecessarily involved in Vampire affairs because Vampire problems tended to escalate far beyond what ordinary mercenary work justified.[2] He also understands the political importance of major supernatural families and immediately recognized how disastrous it would be if Johnny provoked [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]], daughter of [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]]. Eddy directly warned Johnny that attacking Ruby could create a conflict between Vampires and [[Werewolves]] and attempted to prevent him from confronting her.[4]
Eddy's strongest professional loyalty is toward Esther. Even though Johnny is his friend, Eddy explicitly states that he would sacrifice that friendship if Johnny caused enough trouble to threaten Madam or her organization.[4] His loyalty does not prevent him from teasing Esther or participating in her more unusual personal life, but he clearly recognizes her as his superior within The Lost Club.
Toward Johnny, Eddy behaves with a combination of friendship, exasperation and concealed affection. He knows enough about Johnny's life that Roberta's mysterious appearance particularly disturbed him; Eddy considered himself one of Johnny's closest friends and knew almost everything about him, yet was completely unable to remember when Roberta had entered Johnny's life.[6] Chapter 315 later reveals that this attachment is deeper than simple friendship. Victor's ability to see emotional connections showed Eddy's red thread extending directly toward Johnny, exposing an unrequited romantic attraction that Eddy had never openly confessed.[1]

### Synopsis

#### Vampire Society
Eddy was introduced working behind the counter of [[The Lost Club]] when [[Johnny Lykos]], the mercenary known as the White Wolf, returned despite previously being banned from the establishment. Eddy greeted him with the exaggerated familiarity expected between long-time friends, prepared Johnny's usual drink and immediately began questioning him about his latest mercenary contract. Although Johnny casually dismissed his Vampire client as a "leech", Eddy warned him that becoming involved in Vampire problems was rarely worth the consequences because their conflicts had a tendency to become considerably more complicated than an ordinary paid job.[2]
Their conversation soon shifted toward Johnny's family. Eddy informed Johnny that Leona had developed feelings for a newly transformed Vampire named [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] Walker. Johnny initially refused to believe that his younger sister was old enough to become romantically involved with anyone, prompting Eddy to remind him that eighteen years had passed since the period Johnny continued remembering her from.[3] When Johnny demanded more information, Eddy immediately turned the situation into business. He required payment despite their friendship, remarking that friendship should not interfere with commerce, and produced a dossier he had already prepared on Victor. The report contained Victor's Human identity, age, physical statistics and an assessment of his personality describing him as honest toward people he liked, vindictive, petty, loyal to family and potentially possessing sociopathic or yandere tendencies.[3]
Eddy remained behind the bar while Johnny's territorial instincts created another disturbance. After a patron sexually harassed Judy, Johnny nearly beat the man to death. Rather than being shocked, Eddy had already predicted the confrontation well enough to place the resulting damage on Johnny's account. Watching the surrounding customers become excited by the spectacle, Eddy immediately considered whether organizing a formal fight club could become another source of revenue for The Lost Club.[3] Esther arrived furious over Johnny causing yet another incident, but Eddy quickly redirected her attention by proposing the profitable idea.

#### A New Pillar
Eddy remained at [[The Lost Club]] when [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] later arrived seeking information from Esther. The entire establishment became silent when Ruby entered, but Johnny initially interpreted the reaction only as admiration for her appearance. Eddy immediately understood the actual reason and stopped him from approaching her, explaining that Ruby was one of [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett|Scathach Scarlett]]'s daughters and that antagonizing her could have consequences extending far beyond a simple bar fight.[4]
Johnny continued arguing about Vampire–Werewolf relationships and Leona's interest in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Eddy, increasingly irritated by the subject, warned him to stop creating unnecessary problems and reminded him that Vampires and [[Werewolves]] traditionally did not mix. When Johnny dismissed his warning, Eddy's neon-green eyes began glowing and his demeanor became significantly colder. He made his priorities explicit: he was loyal to Madam, and if Johnny provoked the five great Vampire Count Clans badly enough to place Esther or The Lost Club in danger, Eddy would personally hand his friend over to them.[4]
The warning quickly turned back into business. When Johnny realized Eddy had referred to five [[Vampire Counts]] rather than the traditional four, Eddy deliberately exploited his curiosity, refusing to explain the existence of the new Count without payment. Johnny immediately recognized that the "stupid fox" had intentionally phrased his warning to make him curious enough to purchase the information.[4] Eddy's behavior during the encounter demonstrates how naturally his friendship with Johnny, loyalty to Esther and work as an information broker overlapped.

#### A New Pillar
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later arrived unexpectedly inside [[The Lost Club]] after passing through one of Natalia's portals. By this point he had become the Fifth Vampire Count and was already infamous throughout supernatural society. Eddy was among the employees and patrons who reacted cautiously to his presence, but Victor immediately noticed something most people in the Club did not know: Eddy was a Kitsune.[5]
Esther had placed multiple magical wards over Eddy specifically to conceal his true Race, making Victor's casual identification of him as a "little fox" deeply surprising to both of them. Esther took Victor's ability to see through Eddy's concealment as evidence that his perception could bypass deceptions and barriers that should normally obscure supernatural information.[5][6]
Despite his surprise, Eddy remained professional and served Victor when the Count requested a drink suitable for Vampires. He prepared a special beverage consisting fundamentally of Blood enhanced with [[Magic]] to reproduce the flavor of ordinary food, allowing Victor to experience something resembling orange despite normal Vampires deriving nutrition exclusively from Blood. Victor enjoyed the novelty enough to immediately ask Eddy whether he could prepare additional flavors.[5][6]
Johnny entered soon afterward and nearly created another disaster by confronting the stranger occupying his usual seat before realizing the man was Count Alucard. Eddy immediately attempted to redirect his friend elsewhere, silently warning him not to start another conflict. Johnny surprisingly listened, but Victor's interest in him led to a direct conversation. When Victor used Charm and successfully compelled the Alpha Werewolf to reveal his full name, both Eddy and Esther were visibly shocked because Johnny's status as an Alpha should have granted substantial resistance to ordinary Vampiric mental influence.[5]
The situation became considerably stranger after [[Roberta Elderblood]] appeared. Victor immediately noticed irregularities surrounding the woman and began asking where Johnny had met her. Johnny could not answer. Esther remembered Roberta appearing beside Johnny but could not remember when, while Eddy vaguely associated her arrival with Jinsei without being capable of reconstructing the actual event.[6] Eddy was particularly disturbed because he regarded himself as one of Johnny's closest friends and knew almost everything about the Werewolf's life; having no coherent memory of how one of Johnny's supposed lovers had appeared made no sense to him.
Victor eventually exposed the artificial nature of Johnny and Roberta's relationship. After Roberta released her influence, Johnny's supposed mate mark, emotions and supporting memories began disappearing, leaving him physically and mentally overwhelmed. Johnny collapsed unconscious shortly afterward, and Eddy immediately rushed to his side in concern.[7] The incident reinforced both Eddy's genuine attachment to Johnny and the danger posed by supernatural powers capable of altering memory on a large scale.

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Eddy continued working under Esther as [[The Lost Club]] became increasingly integrated into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Ruby's information network. By Chapter 315, Victor returned to the establishment accompanied by Natalia and Mizuki while searching for several Witches connected to Esther. Esther was temporarily unavailable because she was privately engaged with Eddy, whom Victor jokingly described as the Witch's "pet fox".[1]
Victor's improved eyes allowed him to see through the numerous magical barriers Esther had erected around the room and accidentally revealed the actual emotional structure between the two. A thin red thread extended from Esther toward Eddy, indicating a romantic/emotional connection. Eddy possessed a red thread of his own, but it did not return to Esther.[1]
Victor followed Eddy's thread through the Club and discovered that it connected directly to [[Johnny Lykos]], who was downstairs drinking beside two women. Realizing whose son Johnny was and what the connection apparently represented, Victor became amused and remarked that he had never expected the fox to "like Wolves".[1]
The revelation establishes that although Eddy and Esther maintain a sexual relationship and Esther possesses romantic feelings for him, Eddy does not return those feelings in the same manner. His own concealed romantic affection is instead directed toward Johnny, making his relationship with the Werewolf considerably more complicated than the casual friendship both men outwardly display.[1]

### Skills & Abilities
Eddy is a Kitsune, a supernatural fox Yokai. The novel has revealed comparatively little about his personal combat abilities, and powers commonly associated with Kitsune should not automatically be attributed to him without direct demonstration.

#### Kitsune Physiology
- Supernatural Physiology: Eddy is not Human but a Kitsune belonging to the broader Yokai category.[5]
- Supernatural Eyes: Eddy possesses distinctive neon-green eyes which can visibly glow when he becomes serious or draws upon his supernatural nature.[4]
- Supernatural Longevity: As a Yokai rather than an ordinary Human, Eddy possesses the supernatural lifespan associated with his species. His exact age has never been provided.
- Supernatural Presence: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was capable of identifying Eddy's fox nature despite magical measures deliberately hiding his Race.[5]
Eddy has not directly demonstrated enough personal combat to safely index abilities such as Fox-Fire Manipulation, Shapeshifting, Illusion Manipulation or multiple-tail transformations solely from his species.

#### Concealed Supernatural Identity
Eddy's identity as a Kitsune is normally hidden beneath supernatural concealment.
Esther placed several magical wards upon or around him specifically to hide his true Race from supernatural observation.[5][6]
The concealment was effective enough that other patrons of [[The Lost Club]] did not immediately understand why [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] called him a fox. Victor's enhanced perception bypassed the wards without difficulty.
The [[Magic]] itself belongs primarily to Esther rather than Eddy, so the concealment should not be listed as proof that Eddy personally possesses advanced barrier or illusion Magic.

#### Information Brokerage
One of Eddy's most important demonstrated skills is collecting and selling supernatural intelligence.
- Information Gathering: Eddy possesses access to personal and supernatural information regarding individuals operating around the Human World's supernatural community.[3]
- Dossier Preparation: He had already prepared a detailed report on [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] before Johnny formally purchased the information.[3]
- Supernatural Political Knowledge: Eddy is informed about the [[Vampire Counts]], major Clans, [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett|Scathach Scarlett]] and the consequences of provoking important supernatural families.[4]
- Client Manipulation: He deliberately phrases information in ways likely to increase a customer's curiosity and encourage them to pay for the complete answer.[4]
- Professional Discretion: Working for an establishment that functions simultaneously as a neutral club and information center requires Eddy to handle sensitive information without casually revealing everything he knows.

#### Bartending
Eddy is an experienced supernatural bartender capable of preparing drinks for beings whose biology differs considerably from that of Humans.
- Vampire Mixology: He prepared a Blood-based beverage enchanted to reproduce the flavor of orange for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[5]
- Supernatural Beverage Preparation: [[The Lost Club]] serves multiple supernatural species, requiring drinks compatible with very different physiologies.
- Customer Knowledge: Eddy remembers Johnny's preferred drinks and can prepare his usual order without being told the exact ingredients.[2]

#### Business Acumen
Eddy consistently looks for ways to monetize opportunities around [[The Lost Club]]. He refuses to provide valuable information to Johnny for free simply because they are friends and immediately considered transforming repeated supernatural bar fights into a formal source of revenue when he noticed how much the customers enjoyed watching them.[3]
His business mentality closely complements Esther's own personality, which similarly prioritizes information, control and profit.

#### Supernatural Awareness
Eddy possesses extensive practical knowledge acquired through working at a gathering point for numerous supernatural races.
He correctly recognized Ruby's identity and the political consequences of antagonizing Clan Scarlett, understood the significance of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] becoming the Fifth Count and immediately appreciated how abnormal it was for Victor's Charm to overwhelm an Alpha Werewolf such as Johnny.[4][5]

### Relationships

#### Esther
Esther is Eddy's employer, superior and sexual partner. Eddy works directly beneath her at [[The Lost Club]] and consistently identifies his loyalty as belonging to "Madam".[4]
Their private relationship is strongly shaped by Esther's preference for control. Chapter 315 shows the two engaged in BDSM roleplay with Esther occupying the dominant role, leading [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to jokingly refer to Eddy as her "pet fox".[1]
Their emotional relationship is considerably more complicated. Victor's relationship-perception ability revealed a red thread extending from Esther toward Eddy, strongly indicating genuine romantic affection on Esther's side. Eddy's corresponding thread did not connect back to Esther, proving that the romantic attachment is not mutual in the same form.[1]
Despite this, there is no indication Eddy is hostile toward Esther. He remains loyal to her professionally, continues their sexual relationship and is willing to place her safety and authority above his friendship with Johnny.

#### Johnny Lykos
[[Johnny Lykos]] is Eddy's closest known friend, regular customer and secret romantic interest.
Their friendship predates Johnny's introduction. Eddy knows extensive details about Johnny's family and personal affairs, regularly serves him his preferred drinks and acts as one of his main sources of supernatural information. Their friendship does not remove their business relationship: Eddy repeatedly charges Johnny for information and openly states that friendship should never interfere with commerce.[3]
Eddy also attempts to protect Johnny from his own impulsiveness. When Ruby entered [[The Lost Club]], Eddy physically stopped Johnny from approaching her and explained exactly why antagonizing [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett|Scathach Scarlett]]'s daughter could produce catastrophic consequences.[4] His willingness to protect Johnny has limits, however. Eddy explicitly warned him that his ultimate loyalty belonged to Esther and that he would personally surrender Johnny if his actions endangered Madam by provoking the [[Vampire Counts]].[4]
Roberta's manipulation demonstrated how well Eddy normally knew Johnny. Eddy was the most disturbed by his inability to remember when Roberta had appeared because he considered himself familiar with virtually every important part of Johnny's life.[6] After the false memories surrounding Roberta collapsed and Johnny lost consciousness, Eddy immediately rushed to help him.[7]
Chapter 315 reveals that Eddy's feelings toward Johnny extend beyond friendship. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] followed the red relationship thread emerging from Eddy and discovered that it connected directly to Johnny rather than Esther. Victor's reaction—remarking that the fox apparently "liked Wolves"—makes the intended implication clear.[1]
There is currently no evidence Johnny is aware of Eddy's feelings or reciprocates them. Eddy's attraction should therefore be treated as unrequited unless later material establishes otherwise.

#### Victor Elderblood
Eddy first properly interacted with [[Victor Elderblood]] after Victor unexpectedly arrived at [[The Lost Club]] as the newly established Fifth Vampire Count.
Victor immediately exposed Eddy's Kitsune identity despite Esther's magical wards, shocking both of them and demonstrating that his perception could bypass their concealment.[5] Eddy nevertheless recovered quickly enough to serve Victor professionally, preparing Blood-based supernatural drinks for him.
He also witnessed Victor casually overpower Johnny's Alpha resistance to Vampiric Charm and became involved in the confusion surrounding Roberta's memory manipulation.[5][6]
Their later encounter in Chapter 315 was even more personally embarrassing for Eddy. Victor's evolved eyes accidentally exposed Eddy's romantic feelings toward Johnny while Eddy was privately engaged with Esther.[1]
