### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 385
Otsuki Yoichi is an ancient Japanese Yokai, a Nine-Tailed Kitsune, the former commander of the [[Otsuki Clan]]'s military forces and the grandfather of [[Otsuki Hana]] and [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]]. Among Humans he was historically known as Nasu no Yoichi, the famed Hero of the Battle of Yashima. Haruna identifies him directly with the legendary archer who participated in the Battle of Yashima, establishing that Yoichi has lived for more than eight centuries.[1]
Yoichi was the original commander and founder of the military organization later inherited by Haruna. Despite having formally relinquished command, he remains one of the Otsuki Clan's most respected senior figures and a powerful warrior in his own right. Haruna herself considers the possibility of him being weak absurd, noting that he founded the clan's military tradition and developed techniques capable of being passed through generations.[2]
He is also the maternal great-grandfather of [[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]]. For centuries, Yoichi concealed from Haruna the existence and death of her older sister Hana, believing that revealing the truth would only send his remaining granddaughter after the extraordinarily dangerous Elder Gods responsible for Hana's death.[2]

### Physical Appearance
Yoichi possesses a humanoid appearance resembling a man in his forties despite being more than eight centuries old. Like Haruna, his Kitsune heritage remains visibly manifested through nine fox tails and a pair of fox ears.[1]
His apparent age is therefore completely disconnected from his true chronological age. By the time of his introduction he had already lived long enough to have participated in the Battle of Yashima in 1185 under the Human identity Nasu no Yoichi.[1]
Although usually seen relaxing and drinking tea rather than displaying himself as a frontline warrior, his demeanor can change dramatically when provoked. When Haruna addressed him by his old Human name, his expression immediately became dangerous enough that he momentarily appeared less like an elderly relative and more like the veteran warrior he had once been.[2]

### Personality
Yoichi is normally calm, patient and deceptively relaxed. He enjoys tea, indirect conversation and the kind of evasive verbal games Haruna associates with an old fox. His habit of avoiding uncomfortable subjects can make him appear irritatingly casual, particularly to his straightforward granddaughter.[1][2]
Beneath that demeanor is an experienced and extremely stern warrior. Yoichi's personality changes immediately when something he considers sacred is threatened. When Haruna attempted to claim the island containing Hana's memorial as part of her new operational territory, Yoichi's usual gentle manner disappeared entirely. His supernatural pressure was powerful enough to send a shiver through Haruna, and he made it clear that even her authority as commander did not extend to Hana's resting place.[3]
Yoichi is intensely devoted to his family. His decision to hide Hana's existence from Haruna was not motivated by indifference but by fear. Hana had already been killed by an Elder God, and Yoichi knew Haruna well enough to understand that learning the truth would cause her to seek revenge regardless of the difference in power. At the time, Haruna was effectively his only remaining close blood relative, making the possibility of losing her as well intolerable.[2]
This protective instinct can become controlling. Yoichi concealed Hana's history, Ophis' existence and even the truth that Hana's physical body was not actually buried beneath her memorial. He believed Haruna would refuse to rest if she discovered that her sister's remains had never been recovered.[2]
Despite his secrecy, Yoichi ultimately respects Haruna as an adult and commander. After considering whether to lie about Hana's death, he consciously chose to tell her that an Elder God had killed her, reasoning that Haruna was no longer a child and deserved the truth even if it created danger.[2]
Yoichi also displays considerable pride in his family. When Haruna properly paid respects before Hana's memorial, he quietly became pleased because, despite believing she had inherited many of his worst qualities, she had also inherited her father's honor and strong devotion to family.[2]
His younger personality appears to have been considerably more impulsive. Yoichi privately acknowledges that Haruna resembles him strongly when he was young, to the point that watching her behavior occasionally leaves him uncomfortable. He even concludes that, had his younger self discovered a murdered sibling under the same circumstances, he would probably have reacted more violently than Haruna did.[2]
Yoichi retains a surprisingly childish enthusiasm toward exceptional weapons. Centuries of experience did nothing to diminish his appreciation for high-quality craftsmanship, as demonstrated when [[Amaterasu]] presented Divine equipment during negotiations with Haruna. Yoichi became fascinated with the offered katana before Haruna had even agreed to the deal and openly encouraged her to accept because he had never possessed a blade of comparable quality.[4]

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Yoichi is introduced shortly after the Japanese conflict involving [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], the Yokai and the Shinto Gods. Haruna, having noticed the extraordinary sense of familiarity she felt toward Ophis, investigated her own family records and discovered information Yoichi had deliberately hidden. She entered the private area of the Otsuki household and confronted him directly.[1]
Haruna reminded Yoichi that her first rule after becoming commander was that nobody was permitted to betray her, including her own grandfather. She deliberately addressed him not merely as Otsuki Yoichi but as Nasu no Yoichi, Hero of the Battle of Yashima. The name immediately provoked a dangerous reaction from Yoichi, who ordered her not to call him that before regaining his composure.[1][2]
The confrontation also established Yoichi's former position in the clan. He had been the first leader of the military force Haruna now commanded, effectively serving as its original commander and founder. His status was so respected that members of the household normally would not dare enter his private room, which was why he believed sensitive records concerning Hana were safely hidden there.[2]
Haruna nevertheless found the documents and demanded to know why Yoichi had concealed that she possessed an older sister and that this sister had a daughter. Yoichi initially attempted to evade the subject before accepting that his granddaughter already possessed too much evidence to be deceived further.[2]
Rather than explaining everything inside the manor, Yoichi led Haruna through a concealed passage protected by his own Yōuki. To ordinary or weak Yokai, the hidden objects associated with the passage merely appeared to be foreign swords stored among his possessions. The route eventually transported them to a secluded island isolated from normal Space.[2]
Yoichi explained that the island had been created as a memorial for [[Otsuki Hana]], Haruna's unknown older sister and the mother of Ophis. [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] had ordered [[Alexios Alioth]] to isolate the island through his Space-Time abilities, creating a small paradise into which no Being could freely enter without authorization, with Yoichi claiming that even Gods could not simply trespass upon it.[2]
At the highest point of the memorial stood a grave engraved with Hana's name. Haruna respectfully knelt before it while Yoichi watched in silence. Unknown to Haruna, Hana's physical remains were not actually buried there. The grave existed as a place where her spirit and surviving family could symbolically find peace because her real body had never been recovered.[2]
When Haruna asked how Hana died, Yoichi admitted that he wished her death had been similar to the murder of Haruna's father, because at least then he might have possessed the strength necessary to hunt the killer himself. Instead, Hana had been killed by an Elder God, one of the native Gods associated with the world containing [[Nightingale]].[2]
Yoichi did not know all the circumstances surrounding Hana's death. Vlad had been with her when the incident occurred but refused to explain the complete story even when Yoichi confronted and blamed him. Yoichi understood that attempting to force information from the Vampire King was beyond his power.[2]
Yoichi also revealed why Ophis had never been raised within the [[Otsuki Clan]] despite the clan's tradition that children remain among their people until adulthood. Vlad personally requested that his daughter grow within Nightingale because he considered it the safest possible environment for her. Yoichi accepted that reasoning because Vlad possessed the protection of the Vampire Kingdom, its Noble Vampires and allies such as Alexios.[2]
Haruna eventually decided that the secluded island could become a new operational base. She intended to preserve the entire northern region containing Hana's memorial while utilizing the untouched southern, eastern and western areas. Yoichi immediately refused.[2][3]
When Haruna argued that the decision belonged to her as commander, Yoichi's normally gentle personality disappeared. He reminded her that while command of the village and clan belonged to her, Hana's memorial did not. He declared that no random Yokai would be allowed to set foot upon the sanctuary and that he would not permit anyone to defile it while he remained alive.[3]
Haruna clarified that her intention was partly motivated by revenge and that she considered Hana family even though they had never met. Yoichi nevertheless refused to compromise regarding the sanctuary itself, showing that there remained limits to Haruna's authority when his deceased granddaughter was concerned.[3]

#### Demonic War
Centuries later in terms of his personal lifespan, Yoichi remained close to Haruna and continued appearing among the senior members of her faction. During the crisis affecting Japan, [[Amaterasu]] approached Haruna seeking assistance from the Yokai while the Shinto Gods were occupied managing their damaged Pantheon and the problems surrounding Japanese Hell.[4]
Amaterasu offered extensive weapons and supplies in exchange for the Yokai helping protect Japan from dangerous supernatural entities. Among the equipment were exceptionally high-quality katanas manufactured through the resources and craftsmanship of the [[Shinto Pantheon]].[4]
Before Haruna had finished considering the political implications of the agreement, Yoichi had already begun inspecting one of the swords. He openly expressed amazement at its construction, remarking that he had never seen material worked to such a level of quality.[4]
When Haruna insisted that she had not yet accepted the contract, even Yoichi reacted with disbelief. He encouraged her to examine the equipment properly and argued that he had never possessed a katana as good as those being offered. His enthusiasm embarrassed and irritated Haruna, who felt that her grandfather was making the Yokai appear desperate for equipment in the middle of a diplomatic negotiation.[4]
Haruna ultimately accepted Amaterasu's proposal. After the Goddess departed, Yoichi remained focused on his new katana with the enthusiasm of someone who had just received a new toy, causing Haruna, Morgana and Jeanne to stare at him in exasperation.[4]

### Skills & Abilities
- Yokai Physiology: Yoichi is an ancient Yokai and possesses the supernatural physiology, longevity, physical capabilities and Yōuki characteristic of his race.[1][2]
- Kitsune Physiology: Yoichi belongs to the Kitsune race and possesses visible fox ears and tails alongside his humanoid form.[1]
- Nine-Tailed Fox: Yoichi possesses nine tails, marking him as one of the exceptionally developed members of the Kitsune race. His granddaughter Haruna similarly possesses nine tails.[1]
- Longevity/Agelessness: Yoichi participated in the Battle of Yashima in 1185 and remained physically active more than eight centuries later while retaining the appearance of a middle-aged man.[1]
- Yōuki Manipulation: Yoichi possesses advanced control over Yōuki. He concealed objects and information in his room beneath a special Yōuki effect so that weak ordinary Yokai would perceive them merely as foreign weapons rather than recognize what was actually hidden there.[2]
- Perception Manipulation/Concealment: Through Yōuki, Yoichi could disguise or obscure hidden objects from weaker Yokai, protecting sensitive clan information from casual discovery.[2]
- Supernatural Pressure: When Yoichi became genuinely angry with Haruna, the pressure released from him was powerful enough to send a shiver through his granddaughter despite Haruna herself being an exceptionally strong Nine-Tailed Kitsune and commander of Japan's Yokai.[3]
- Supernatural Strength: Yoichi could physically seize Haruna by the face and forcibly remove her from Hana's sanctuary when he decided their argument there had ended.[3]
- Supernatural Speed: Yoichi moved in front of Haruna with enough speed to abruptly seize her before she could continue the confrontation.[3]
- [[Martial Arts]] Mastery: Yoichi was the original commander of the fighting force later inherited by Haruna. Haruna explicitly regarded him as an extremely powerful warrior and reasoned that someone weak could never have founded their clan's military tradition or created techniques capable of being inherited by subsequent generations.[2]
- Technique Creation: Yoichi developed techniques that were preserved and passed down through later generations of his family and clan.[2]
- Weapons Proficiency: Yoichi has centuries of experience with weapons and maintains a personal collection containing foreign swords received from acquaintances abroad. His ability to immediately appreciate the workmanship of Divine-grade katanas further demonstrates extensive familiarity with bladed weapons.[2][4]
- Swordsmanship: As the former Great Commander of the Otsuki forces and an ancient Japanese warrior, Yoichi is highly familiar with katanas and other swords. Although the novel has not shown a complete sword battle involving him, his collection, military history and evaluation of Shinto-forged weapons demonstrate considerable experience.[2][4]
- Archery: Yoichi is explicitly identified in-universe as Nasu no Yoichi, the Hero of the Battle of Yashima. This connects him to the identity historically famous for archery at Yashima; however, the novel has not yet depicted Yoichi using a bow directly in present-day combat.[1]
- Leadership: Yoichi was the first commander and founder of the military organization later controlled by Haruna. His position remained sufficiently respected that members of the clan avoided entering his private quarters even after he had relinquished active command.[2]
- Combat Experience: Having lived for more than eight centuries and served as the founder of the Otsuki military structure, Yoichi possesses an enormous amount of accumulated experience compared with younger Yokai.[1][2]
- Enhanced Willpower: Yoichi could suppress an intense emotional reaction almost immediately after Haruna invoked memories connected to his former identity, returning from a dangerous warrior-like state to controlled conversation within moments.[2]

### Relationships

#### Otsuki Elderblood Haruna
Haruna is Yoichi's granddaughter, successor and in many respects a reflection of his younger self. Yoichi regularly complains that she inherited many of his worst personality traits, while simultaneously acknowledging her strength, honor and devotion to family.[2]
Their relationship combines affection with frequent conflict. Yoichi respects Haruna's authority over the clan and village but does not consider himself obligated to obey her regarding every private family matter. His refusal to allow Hana's memorial to become ordinary Yokai territory is the clearest example of this boundary.[3]
Yoichi's secrecy concerning Hana was primarily motivated by fear that Haruna would recklessly pursue the Elder God who killed her sister. Despite this overprotectiveness, he eventually chose honesty because he recognized that Haruna was an adult and commander rather than the child he once protected.[2]

#### Otsuki Hana
Hana was Yoichi's other granddaughter. His memories indicate that she possessed an unusual personality but was considerably better behaved than Haruna, and he missed her deeply after her death.[2]
Hana's murder by an Elder God remained one of Yoichi's greatest sources of anger and helplessness. Unlike the enemy responsible for the death of Haruna's father, Hana's killer was beyond the level Yoichi believed he could personally hunt. He subsequently became fiercely protective of the memorial created for her by Vlad and Alexios.[2][3]

#### Ophis Tepes Elderblood
Ophis is Yoichi's great-granddaughter through Hana and [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]]. Yoichi knew of Ophis' existence long before Haruna discovered their relationship but respected Vlad's decision to raise the girl within [[Nightingale]] because he believed the Vampire King's resources made it the safest available location.[2]

#### Vlad Dracula Tepes
Yoichi's relationship with Vlad is complicated. Vlad was Hana's husband and the father of Ophis, making him part of Yoichi's family, but Yoichi continued to resent him for refusing to reveal everything that happened during Hana's death.[2]
Yoichi confronted Vlad after the incident, blamed him for what happened and attempted multiple arguments to make him explain himself. Vlad remained silent. Yoichi ultimately recognized that he lacked the power to force answers from one of the world's most powerful supernatural beings.[2]
Despite his anger, Yoichi accepted Vlad's efforts to protect Hana's memory and Ophis. Vlad commissioned the isolated island that became Hana's sanctuary and kept Ophis under the protection of [[Nightingale]] following her mother's death.[2]

### Historical Identity
Yoichi's Human identity is directly connected in the novel to Nasu no Yoichi, whom Haruna calls the Hero of the Battle of Yashima. The Battle of Yashima occurred in 1185, making this one of the clearest chronological indicators of Yoichi's extreme age.[1]
The names Munetaka, Nasu no Suketaka and the traditional account of Yoichi marrying a daughter of Yoshishige Nitta derive from historical or legendary accounts surrounding Nasu no Yoichi rather than information extensively developed inside the novel itself. They should therefore be distinguished from the supernatural Otsuki family relationships directly established within [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]].

### Trivia
- Yoichi appears physically to be approximately forty years old despite being at least eight centuries old.[1]
- He is one of the known Nine-Tailed Kitsune in the series.[1]
- Yoichi was the first commander of the military organization later led by Haruna and can effectively be considered its founder.[2]
- Haruna explicitly identifies him as Nasu no Yoichi, Hero of the Battle of Yashima.[1]
- Yoichi strongly dislikes being addressed by the name Nasu no Yoichi. Merely hearing Haruna call him that caused an involuntary and visibly dangerous reaction associated with memories he preferred not to revisit.[2]
- Yoichi kept the documents concerning Hana hidden near his personal weapons and further concealed them with his Yōuki. He underestimated Haruna's willingness to search through his room.[2]
- He owns foreign swords received as gifts from acquaintances outside Japan, indicating that his life and contacts were not restricted exclusively to Japanese Yokai society.[2]
- Yoichi privately believes Haruna is extremely similar to himself when he was younger.[2]
- He considered Haruna's restraint after learning about Hana surprisingly impressive because he believed his younger self would already have lost control under the same circumstances.[2]
- The grave marked [[Otsuki Hana]] does not actually contain Hana's body. Yoichi deliberately concealed this from Haruna because he knew she would attempt to recover her sister's remains.[2]
- Yoichi fiercely prohibited ordinary Yokai from entering Hana's memorial sanctuary even after Haruna became commander of their clan.[3]
- Despite his age and stern reputation, exceptional swords can make him behave with almost childlike excitement. [[Amaterasu]]'s Divine-quality katana nearly convinced him of her proposed agreement before Haruna had finished negotiating it.[4]
- His reaction to Amaterasu's weapons embarrassed Haruna enough that she internally wished he would stop making their faction appear unsophisticated.[4]
