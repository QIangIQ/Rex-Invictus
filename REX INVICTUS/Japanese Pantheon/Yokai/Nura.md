#### Debut

Nura was a Japanese Yokai belonging to the Nurarihyon species. He was an illusion specialist who became one of the supernatural beings involved in the pursuit of [[Nero]] and Ophis after information concerning Ophis' presence in Japan spread throughout the supernatural community.[2][3]
Nura's abilities were particularly suited to concealment and ambush. He was capable of hiding an entire mixed group of Yokai, Vampires and [[Werewolves]] behind an illusion and could create false manifestations of himself that caused physical attacks to pass harmlessly through them. His greatest disadvantage during the pursuit was Nero's unusually sharp perception, which repeatedly allowed her to recognize his illusions.[3] He ultimately became one of the people held personally responsible by [[Victor Elderblood]] for what happened to Ophis and Nero. Victor prevented his escape, critically injured him and later selected the surviving Yokai for a considerably worse fate than immediate death.[4][1]

### Physical Appearance
Nura was a small humanoid Yokai with an abnormally large head, a physical characteristic immediately distinctive enough for [[Nero]] to recognize him as a Nurarihyon upon seeing him.[3]

### Personality
Nura was cautious, calculating and generally composed. When [[Nero]] demonstrated knowledge of Yokai despite being a foreigner, he approved of her decision to learn about the supernatural beings inhabiting the country, regarding caution as an important trait for any supernatural creature.[3] He also tended to behave casually even during dangerous situations, initially addressing Nero in a bored manner and later laughing openly when he discovered that she did not fully understand Ophis' political importance.[3]
He possessed a mocking sense of humor, particularly toward his temporary allies. During Nero's battle with the Japanese Vampire Kuroki, Nura immediately mocked Kuroki for struggling against her. Their exchange showed a loose and abrasive working relationship in which both men readily insulted the other's competence.[5]
Nura was nevertheless significantly more observant than many of the supernatural beings participating in the hunt. His group understood that the various rival factions pursuing Ophis were operating under a temporary truce because their opportunity to capture her was rapidly disappearing. Nura attempted to conceal his urgency, although Nero was perceptive enough to notice it.[3]
Although he participated in an attempt to capture and divide the Vampire King's daughter among several factions, Nura was not completely indifferent to the physical consequences of Ophis' powers. When the inexperienced girl attempted to use her spatial ability despite already bleeding from her eyes and nose, Nura warned her to stop because continuing without Yōuki could kill her. His warning did not represent a change of allegiance, however, as he remained part of the coalition attempting to capture her.[3]
Nura was also pragmatic enough to recognize overwhelming danger. The instant [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] appeared before the coalition, both Nura and Kuroki immediately recognized him as Alucard and were terrified. Victor's Bloodlust was so intense that Nura's body stopped properly responding to him, leaving him incapable of taking meaningful action while Victor recovered Ophis and Nero.[5]
His survival instincts eventually overcame any remaining loyalty toward the other attackers. After Victor began massacring everyone responsible, Nura used his illusions to try escaping unnoticed rather than continuing the confrontation. The attempt failed completely. Once captured and later confronted again by Victor, Nura no longer maintained any bravado and simply asked for a quick death, already understanding that Victor was unlikely to grant him one.[4][1]

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Nura became involved in the international hunt surrounding Ophis after knowledge that the Vampire King's daughter was stranded in Japan began circulating among supernatural factions. For several days, [[Nero]] protected Ophis while Vampires, [[Werewolves]], Yokai, [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] and other groups progressively converged upon them.[2]
Nero eventually detected Nura hiding nearby despite his supernatural concealment. After appearing, Nura was surprised that the foreign girl immediately identified him as a Nurarihyon. Nero explained that she had deliberately studied the supernatural inhabitants of Japan because she considered it necessary to understand the dangerous environment in which she lived. Nura approved of the mindset before asking what she intended to do next. Nero simply answered that she would kill him and continue moving.[3]
Nura responded by dispelling part of the illusion surrounding the area. An entire group of hidden supernatural beings suddenly became visible around the girls, including Werewolves, Vampires and several types of Yokai. One of the Yokai specifically praised Nura's power for its usefulness, confirming that the assembled force had been concealed by his technique.[3]
The gathering represented an unusual temporary cooperation between otherwise hostile races. Nura explained that the factions had temporarily suspended their conflicts because time was running out. Their objective was Ophis, whose position as the daughter of the Vampire King made her extraordinarily valuable to numerous interested parties.[3]
Nero attempted to shoot Nura when he lowered his guard while laughing at her ignorance concerning Ophis' identity. The bullet passed directly through what appeared to be Nura's body without causing damage. Nura later appeared beside Ophis while she attempted to use her spatial powers; when Nero fired at him again, the projectile once more passed harmlessly through his apparent form before Nura disappeared. The encounters demonstrated the defensive application of his illusions, allowing him to misdirect attacks through false manifestations of himself.[3]
The situation developed into an extended battle. Nero killed numerous members of the coalition while continuing to protect Ophis. Nura's illusion techniques proved considerably less effective against her than expected. When Kuroki demanded that he use his powers to resolve the situation, Nura admitted that he had already attempted to deceive Nero several times, but her senses were sufficiently developed to recognize his illusions.[5]
Nura nevertheless continued coordinating with the attackers and mocked Kuroki for his difficulty dealing with Nero. Their opportunity eventually attracted additional participants, including Hunters. When the Hunter August suddenly intervened and severed Nero's arms, Nura reacted with irritation because August had acted independently rather than allowing the existing group to proceed according to plan. August replied that he was merely accelerating the process and reminded Nura and the others that they would receive their promised share of the King's daughter.[5]
August subsequently decapitated Ophis. Although her Progenitor physiology prevented the injury from becoming immediately fatal, the pain caused Ophis to release a supernatural scream powerful enough to overwhelm the heightened senses of the surrounding beings. Nura urgently ordered August to silence her, but the Hunter was himself temporarily disabled by the scream.[5]
Before the coalition could finish what it had started, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] arrived. Nura and Kuroki instantly recognized Alucard and reacted with terror. The pressure produced by Victor's presence was so extreme that neither man's body properly obeyed him, leaving them unable to move while Victor restored Ophis' body and transformed the critically wounded Nero into a proper Vampire.[5]
After ensuring both girls were safe, Victor declared that every Witch, Yokai, Demon, Werewolf, Hunter and Vampire responsible for the incident would be hunted. His Bloodlust intensified until Nura and the other survivors experienced a vision-like sensation of an enormous sea of blood and corpses surrounding them. Victor then announced that everyone who had participated in the incident or attempted to profit from his daughter would die.[5]
Nura survived the initial slaughter long enough to attempt an escape. While Victor's attention was divided by the broader conflict, Nura activated his illusion technique and tried slipping away unnoticed. Victor detected him immediately. A massive spike of ice erupted from the ground in front of the fleeing Yokai and critically injured him, causing Nura to cough blood. When Nura demanded to know how Victor had seen through his illusion, Victor merely laughed at the idea that Nura believed he could deceive him.[4]
Victor did not immediately kill him. After dealing with the remaining threats, he deliberately returned for Nura. By this point the Yokai understood his position completely and asked Victor to grant him a quick death, immediately acknowledging that such mercy was unlikely.[1]
Victor confirmed his suspicion. He told Nura that death would be a blessing and that he had other plans for him. After reminding the Yokai of what had happened to one of his Vampire associates, Victor requested that Nura become his "meat bomb". Nura's expression darkened as he realized what Victor intended and acknowledged that he had never possessed a choice in the matter. Nura did not survive Victor's retaliation.[1]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
Nura was one of the supernatural beings directly involved in the attempted capture of Ophis and consequently became one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s personal targets. He immediately understood how dangerous Victor was when the Vampire arrived, becoming physically incapable of moving under the pressure of Victor's Bloodlust.[5]
Nura later attempted to escape Victor through illusion, but Victor perceived him without difficulty and critically injured him with an ice spike. Victor deliberately kept Nura alive rather than granting him a quick death, later informing him that he intended to use the Yokai as a "meat bomb" during his retaliation against everyone involved in the incident.[4][1]

#### Kuroki
Nura and Kuroki temporarily worked together during the pursuit of Ophis and [[Nero]]. Their relationship appeared more practical than friendly, with both openly insulting one another during the confrontation. Nura mocked Kuroki when Nero repeatedly frustrated him, while Kuroki called Nura useless after learning that Nero could see through his illusions.[5]
Both nevertheless understood the danger represented by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] considerably faster than many of the weaker combatants. When Victor arrived, Nura and Kuroki immediately recognized him and were simultaneously overwhelmed by his presence.[5]

#### Nero Elderblood
[[Nero]] was the principal counter to Nura's abilities. Although his illusions were effective enough to conceal an entire multi-racial group and repeatedly divert bullets through false versions of himself, Nero's heightened perception allowed her to discover his hiding place and identify his illusions during battle.[3][5]
Nura acknowledged that he had attempted to affect Nero multiple times without success, explicitly attributing his failure to the quality of her senses.[5]

### Skills & Abilities
- Yokai Physiology: Nura was a supernatural Japanese Yokai belonging to the Nurarihyon species and possessed the enhanced senses, physical attributes and supernatural abilities associated with Yokai.[3]
- Illusion Manipulation: Nura's primary supernatural ability was the creation and manipulation of highly effective illusions. He concealed an entire collection of Vampires, [[Werewolves]] and Yokai around [[Nero]] and Ophis until voluntarily releasing the illusion surrounding them.[3]
- Presence Concealment: Nura could hide both himself and other supernatural beings from ordinary observation. His concealment was effective enough for a large mixed fighting force to remain unnoticed until he chose to reveal them.[3]
- Illusory Body: Nura could present false manifestations of himself that appeared physically present while attacks simply passed through them. Nero fired directly through his apparent head and later through another manifestation near Ophis without harming his real body.[3]
- Illusory Evasion: By shifting or concealing his actual position behind illusions, Nura could cause opponents to attack false targets rather than his physical body.[3]
- Illusory Escape: Nura attempted to use his illusions to disappear from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s battlefield and escape unnoticed. The technique was ineffective against Victor's perception.[4]
- Stealth: Nura was capable of approaching and observing opponents without immediately revealing his presence, although Nero's senses allowed her to detect him.[3]
- Enhanced Senses: As a Yokai, Nura possessed heightened supernatural senses. Ophis' scream affected him and the other supernatural beings particularly severely because of their enhanced sensory perception.[5]
- Supernatural Knowledge: Nura understood Yokai abilities, Yōuki and the limitations of other supernatural powers. He recognized that Ophis was attempting to use an ability without Yōuki and warned that forcing it further in her exhausted condition could kill her.[3]
- Strategic Coordination: Nura worked within a temporary alliance containing normally hostile supernatural races and used his illusions to support their coordinated ambush against Nero and Ophis.[3]

### Limitations
- Exceptional Perception: Nura's illusions were not flawless against beings with sufficiently developed senses. [[Nero]] repeatedly recognized his techniques despite being substantially exhausted, forcing Nura to admit that her perception rendered his illusions largely ineffective against her.[5]
- Overwhelming Perception: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] required essentially no effort to discover Nura when he attempted to flee under the protection of his illusions. Nura himself was shocked that Victor could identify his actual position so easily.[4]
- Limited Direct Combat Showings: Nura primarily operated through concealment, deception and coordination. The chapters depicting him do not establish any notable direct offensive technique comparable to his illusion abilities.

### Trivia

- Nura's illusions could conceal multiple members of several different supernatural races simultaneously.[3]
- Nero was a particularly poor matchup for his abilities because her supernatural senses repeatedly allowed her to see through his illusions.[5]
- Nura mocked Kuroki for struggling against Nero, only for Kuroki to immediately retaliate by calling Nura useless when his illusions failed against her.[5]
- Although he was participating in Ophis' capture, Nura warned her that continuing to force her spatial ability in her weakened state could kill her.[3]
- Nura and Kuroki were among the characters who immediately recognized [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] when he arrived in Japan.[5]
- An ice spike created by Victor critically wounded Nura when he attempted to escape, but Victor intentionally did not kill him at that moment.[4]
- When Nura later asked for a quick death, Victor refused and instead told him that he would become a "meat bomb".[1]
