#### Debut

Gyuki was a powerful Japanese Yokai of the Oni species and the leader of one of Japan's strongest Oni clans. His faction was a direct rival of [[Shuten Douji]]'s Oni faction and was infamous for its violent members, who enjoyed massacring innocent people. Gyuki himself embodied those tendencies, being an aggressive, predatory Oni with a particular appetite for children.[1][2]
Gyuki became indirectly involved in [[Victor Elderblood]]'s plans in Japan when Victor deliberately sent [[Watanabe Gintoki]] to provoke him, intending to weaponize Gintoki's abnormal bad luck against Gyuki's faction. The resulting sequence of increasingly impossible accidents annihilated Gyuki's forces and ultimately ended with an ice spike from [[Scathach]] and Inari's distant battle crossing dimensions and striking Gyuki directly in the head.[3][4]

### Physical Appearance
Gyuki was a large, heavily built and bearded Oni. His physique was explicitly compared to that of [[Ibaraki Douji]], who was an exceptionally muscular Oni standing around two meters tall, making Gyuki considerably more physically imposing than the comparatively slender [[Shuten Douji]].[1]

### Personality
Gyuki was cruel, violent, predatory and openly sadistic. Unlike Shuten's faction, Gyuki commanded Oni specifically noted for enjoying massacres among innocent people, and Shuten regarded both Gyuki and the behavior of his faction with undisguised disgust.[1] Gyuki himself had a particularly disturbing appetite for children. During the [[Hyakki Yakō]], merely seeing the disguised [[Nero]] and Ophis caused him to imagine how they would taste, with the narration explicitly identifying him as an Oni who enjoyed eating children's flesh. After noticing another young Yokai with bear-like paws, Gyuki immediately selected him as additional prey.[2]
Despite his brutality, Gyuki was not completely reckless. He understood the basic rule among Yokai that outward appearance could be misleading due to transformation abilities and did not immediately assume Nero and Ophis were helpless simply because they resembled children. After noticing that neither emitted Yōuki, he considered whether they were ancient Yokai concealing their power or creatures who simply did not possess Yōuki. Rather than abandon his interest, however, he decided to investigate them further, with the narration noting that once Gyuki selected prey, he did not willingly let it escape.[2]
Gyuki was also arrogant and highly confrontational. He maintained a bitter rivalry with Shuten's faction and readily traded provocations with Ibaraki, with both Oni displaying enthusiasm at the prospect of fighting one another.[1] His confidence was partially supported by his position: Gyuki led one of the strongest Oni clans in Japan and was personally considered a strong Yokai. Nevertheless, he possessed enough self-preservation to recognize overwhelming authority. When his predatory actions toward Nero and Ophis drew [[Hashimoto Genji]]'s attention, Genji's Divine power and blue flames were sufficient to make Gyuki abandon the confrontation rather than challenge him directly.[5]
Gyuki was especially easy to provoke when his pride or family was insulted. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] exploited this weakness by having Gintoki deliver an intentionally childish insult about Gyuki's mother. Rather than dismissing the provocation, Gyuki became furious, intercepted Gintoki when he attempted to flee and ordered his subordinates to kill him.[6]

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Gyuki first appeared during the great [[Hyakki Yakō]] gathering in Japan, where numerous important Yokai faction leaders had assembled to witness Haruna's parade. [[Shuten Douji]] immediately reacted with disgust upon noticing him. Gyuki was introduced as an Oni from a rival faction who controlled violent Oni known for massacring innocent people, making his organization the direct rival of Shuten's own faction. [[Ibaraki Douji]] openly challenged him, declaring that Gyuki's arrival finally gave him an opportunity to beat him, while Gyuki laughed off the threat and answered with equal confidence.[1]
As Haruna's Hyakki Yakō continued, Gyuki watched the spectacle before directing his attention toward the disguised [[Nero]] and Ophis. He regarded the girls as potential food and became particularly interested after realizing neither possessed detectable Yōuki. Although aware that Yokai could conceal or drastically alter their appearance, his confidence as the leader of one of the strongest Oni clans caused him to pursue the matter rather than remain cautious. He subsequently noticed a young bear-like Yokai and chose him as prey as well. Shuten immediately understood Gyuki's intentions and internally condemned him for attempting to cause trouble at a gathering being observed by numerous powerful supernatural beings and Gods.[2]
Gyuki soon acted on those impulses. By the time [[Hashimoto Genji]] investigated nearby gunshots, the bear-like boy Gyuki had targeted was already dead, with the remains lying close to him. Gyuki then approached Nero and Ophis, prompting Nero to fire both revolvers at him. The shots penetrated Gyuki's arms, surprising him with the effectiveness of the ammunition, but they did not stop his advance. Shuten confronted him over the corpse and called him a disgrace to the Oni race, while Gyuki dismissed his criticism. Genji eventually intervened, and the threat represented by his Divine blue flames forced Gyuki to abandon his pursuit of the girls.[5]
Gyuki later became an unwilling component of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wider plan against Japan's hostile supernatural factions. While coordinating the coming conflict, Victor specifically ordered that Gintoki be sent to Gyuki, stating that the plan to exploit Gintoki's bad luck was still active and that using him against Gyuki would create additional chaos.[3]
The plan was put into action when Gintoki approached Gyuki and repeated an insulting joke supplied by Victor concerning Gyuki's mother. Gyuki took the insult seriously and became enraged. When Gintoki attempted to escape, Gyuki rapidly appeared in front of him and prevented his retreat before ordering the surrounding Yokai to kill him.[6]
What followed demonstrated the abnormal nature of Gintoki's power. The attacking Yokai repeatedly suffered impossible accidents instead of successfully harming him. Projectiles inexplicably missed a stationary Gintoki and struck his enemies, an attack failed because he accidentally stumbled out of its path, and several Yokai killed one another when Gintoki merely fell to the ground at the correct moment. Even severed body parts and weapons continued producing lethal friendly fire. Victor had anticipated precisely this phenomenon and had ordered Eve and Roberta not to interfere unless Gintoki was genuinely about to die, something Victor already considered extremely unlikely.[6]
The chain of coincidences ultimately extended far beyond Gyuki's immediate battlefield. During [[Scathach]]'s battle with Inari, Scathach launched an enormous barrage of ice weapons against Inari's gigantic fox form. One of the ice spikes struck one of Inari's tails and, through an absurd sequence of circumstances, was redirected through a different dimension. The projectile emerged at the location where Gintoki and Gyuki's faction were fighting and struck Gyuki directly in the head, killing him instantly.[4]
Victor later learned that Gintoki had effectively destroyed Gyuki's entire Yokai clan despite being vastly weaker than the beings attacking him. He explained that the impossible ricochet which killed Gyuki was another manifestation of Gintoki's extraordinary power, even noting Scathach's surprise when she learned that one of her attacks had somehow travelled through such an improbable sequence and killed a completely unrelated Yokai elsewhere.[4]

### Relationships

#### Shuten Douji
[[Shuten Douji]] was Gyuki's principal rival among the Oni faction leaders. Their clans represented opposing groups, with Gyuki's faction being particularly notorious for violence against innocents. Shuten openly disliked Gyuki, reacting with disgust simply upon seeing him during the [[Hyakki Yakō]] and later directly calling him a disgrace to the Oni race after witnessing the consequences of his predatory behavior.[1][5]

#### Ibaraki Douji
[[Ibaraki Douji]] and Gyuki shared an openly antagonistic rivalry. Ibaraki immediately celebrated Gyuki's arrival because it gave him another opportunity to challenge him, while Gyuki confidently laughed off his threats. Unlike Shuten's disgust toward Gyuki, Ibaraki's hostility was heavily influenced by his own love of combat and desire to fight powerful opponents.[1]

#### Watanabe Gintoki
[[Watanabe Gintoki]] became Gyuki's final opponent despite being overwhelmingly inferior to him in conventional combat ability. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] intentionally placed Gintoki in Gyuki's path specifically because his strange bad luck could cause catastrophic coincidences around anyone attempting to harm him.[3] Gyuki attempted to have Gintoki killed after being insulted, only for his subordinates to repeatedly kill or injure one another through impossible accidents. The phenomenon eventually culminated in an ice spike from [[Scathach]]'s distant battle being redirected through another dimension and destroying Gyuki's head, making Gintoki the indirect cause of his death.[6][4]

### Skills & Abilities
- Yokai Physiology: As a Yokai, Gyuki possessed Yōuki and the supernatural physical characteristics common to his kind. He was specifically an Oni, a species distinguished by its great physical power and combat-oriented culture.[1]
- Oni Physiology: Gyuki was powerful enough to lead one of the strongest Oni clans in Japan, and Shuten independently regarded him as a strong supernatural being despite despising him personally.[2]
- Supernatural Strength: Gyuki's status as a powerful Oni and clan leader placed him among Japan's physically formidable Yokai. His massive physique was directly compared with Ibaraki's.[1]
- Supernatural Speed: When Gintoki attempted to run away, Gyuki was capable of rapidly intercepting him and appearing directly in his path before he could escape.[6]
- Enhanced Endurance/Pain Tolerance: [[Nero]] fired two bullets through Gyuki's arms, leaving visible holes in them. Although surprised that the ammunition penetrated him so easily, Gyuki remained conscious and continued confronting the girls rather than being incapacitated by the injuries.[5]
- Yōuki: As an Oni, Gyuki possessed the supernatural energy utilized by Yokai. He was familiar enough with Yōuki to immediately notice its complete absence from Nero and Ophis and determine that they either possessed a method of concealing it or were beings that did not naturally use Yōuki.[2]
- Energy Perception: Gyuki could assess the presence or absence of Yōuki in other beings, using this information while evaluating potential prey.[2]
- Supernatural Awareness: Gyuki understood the deceptive nature of Yokai appearances and knew that transformations could allow even extremely old and dangerous Yokai to appear harmless. He therefore considered multiple possibilities before deciding what Nero and Ophis were.[2]
- Leadership: Gyuki was the leader of one of Japan's strongest Oni clans and commanded a large group of violent Yokai. His authority was sufficient for his subordinates to immediately attack Gintoki when ordered.[2][6]
- Combat Experience: His position as the leader of a powerful Oni faction, confidence when confronting other clan leaders and recognition as a strong Oni indicate extensive experience within the highly strength-oriented society of Japanese Yokai.[1][2]

### Weaknesses
- Temper and Pride: Gyuki could be manipulated through direct insults. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] successfully provoked him into attacking Gintoki simply by giving Gintoki an insulting joke about Gyuki's mother to repeat.[6]
- Predatory Fixation: Once Gyuki identified someone as prey, he was extremely reluctant to let them escape, which could override better judgment even in dangerous circumstances.[2]
- Gintoki's Bad Luck: Gyuki had no effective countermeasure against the passive chain of coincidences surrounding Gintoki. Conventional attempts to attack him instead caused Gyuki's own subordinates to suffer friendly fire and ultimately resulted in an unrelated attack from a distant battlefield killing Gyuki himself.[6][4]

### Trivia
- Gyuki was the leader of one of the strongest Oni clans in Japan.[2]
- His faction was a direct rival of [[Shuten Douji]]'s faction and was notorious for massacring innocent people.[1]
- Gyuki was explicitly stated to enjoy eating the flesh of children.[2]
- Despite his predatory nature, he followed the general Yokai principle of not judging supernatural beings purely by their physical appearance.[2]
- The novel explicitly calls Gintoki's phenomenon "bad luck" when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] orders Gintoki to be used against Gyuki. From Gintoki's perspective, however, this bad luck repeatedly produces extraordinarily fortunate outcomes whenever his life is threatened.[3][6]
- Gyuki's death was not caused by Gintoki physically attacking him. Gintoki indirectly killed him when his abnormal luck caused an ice spike from [[Scathach]]'s distant battle with Inari to ricochet from Inari's tail, cross through another dimension and strike Gyuki in the head.[4]
- Victor later described Gintoki as having effectively destroyed Gyuki's entire Yokai clan by himself despite his otherwise unimpressive combat ability.[4]
