#### Debut

Ibaraki Douji is a powerful Oni Yokai from Japan, a close friend and subordinate of [[Shuten Douji]], and the second strongest member of Shuten's Oni Clan, ranking immediately beneath Shuten himself.[1] He is an extremely battle-oriented Yokai whose defining characteristics are his enormous physical strength, exceptional Oni regeneration, overwhelming enthusiasm for combat and tendency to provoke powerful opponents simply because fighting them appears entertaining.
Ibaraki is one of the better-known Oni participating in Japan's supernatural society. Even the Tengu responsible for policing the country's supernatural population generally avoid provoking him because of his reputation as a battle maniac and the difficulty involved in putting down an Oni with his regenerative ability.[2] Despite his reckless personality, Ibaraki is genuinely loyal to Shuten and will ultimately obey him when his leader becomes serious.[3][4]

### Physical Appearance
Ibaraki is an enormous and heavily muscular Oni standing approximately two meters or taller. He possesses two large horns protruding from his forehead, messy hair and a thick beard, giving him a considerably more rugged and traditionally monstrous appearance than the comparatively slender and handsome [[Shuten Douji]].[2]
He normally carries an enormous mace over his shoulder, a weapon suited to both his physique and direct combat style. His appearance becomes visibly more threatening when excited by battle; his eyes can glow with supernatural power, his presence becomes increasingly wild and oppressive, and his body language immediately reveals his desire to fight.[1]
Ibaraki's physique is also representative of many members of Shuten's Oni community. Most female Oni within their village are described as tall, muscular and warrior-like, resembling somewhat less muscular versions of Ibaraki.[5]

### Personality
Ibaraki is loud, reckless, crude and almost pathologically enthusiastic about combat. He actively searches for fights wherever he goes, to the point that supernatural authorities familiar with him consider confrontations with Ibaraki an ordinary problem surrounding his presence.[2]
His personality is dominated by his desire to test himself against powerful opponents. When the Tengu responsible for enforcing supernatural order appeared during the [[Hyakki Yakō]], Ibaraki openly insulted them despite knowing their political position. The Tengu were visibly irritated but chose to ignore him because fighting a battle-obsessed Oni possessing extreme regeneration would only create unnecessary problems.[2]
Ibaraki becomes especially animated whenever someone demonstrates significant strength. He eagerly watched Otsuki Haruna challenge [[Hashimoto Genji]], immediately becoming excited by the possibility of witnessing a battle between powerful Yokai.[6] He reacted similarly upon meeting [[Victor Elderblood]], immediately attempting to challenge him despite knowing virtually nothing about Victor beforehand.[1]
Although extremely reckless, Ibaraki is not completely incapable of recognizing an impossible fight. After witnessing the killing aura accumulated by [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] over approximately two thousand years of warfare, even Ibaraki abandoned any desire to challenge her and concluded that doing so would simply result in a meaningless death.[7]
He also possesses enough political awareness to understand situations in which combat would threaten his entire Clan. When Shuten prevented him from interfering in the confrontation involving Haruna, Genji and the Gods, Ibaraki reluctantly accepted the order because attacking at that moment could have turned the Shinto Gods against the Oni faction.[4]
Ibaraki has a particularly antagonistic relationship with [[Gyuki]]. The two repeatedly provoke, insult and threaten one another whenever they meet. Their exchanges are sufficiently aggressive that Shuten frequently finds himself attempting to prevent the situation from turning into another battle.[2][3]
Despite his constant complaints, Ibaraki is deeply loyal to [[Shuten Douji]]. Shuten is both his leader and close friend, and Ibaraki ultimately follows his instructions whenever Shuten becomes serious. Even when visibly disappointed at being denied a fight, he does not directly rebel against Shuten's authority.[3][4]
Ibaraki also has a surprisingly childish side beneath his intimidating appearance. He frequently complains about Shuten's popularity among the female Oni, openly curses his leader's good fortune and engages in petty arguments regarding their Clan's marriage traditions.[5]

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes and War
Ibaraki was introduced during the [[Hyakki Yakō]] taking place in Tokyo. Numerous Yokai leaders and supernatural figures had gathered to witness Otsuki Haruna publicly display her forces and challenge the existing balance of power among Japan's Yokai.[2]
During the gathering, Ibaraki immediately distinguished himself through his complete lack of restraint. When the Tengu appeared to supervise the supernatural population, he loudly insulted the woman they served despite being surrounded by beings who could potentially retaliate. Several observers immediately recognized him by name, while the Tengu themselves angrily chose to ignore him rather than start a confrontation with a notorious battle maniac.[2]
Ibaraki was accompanied by [[Shuten Douji]], who complained that if he did not personally keep Ibaraki under control, his subordinate would inevitably create problems for their faction. Ibaraki responded by telling Shuten that he had never asked to be followed, demonstrating the familiar argumentative dynamic between the two.[2]
The gathering also brought Ibaraki into contact with [[Gyuki]], the leader of a rival Oni faction known for cruelty toward weaker beings. Shuten openly despised Gyuki, while Ibaraki immediately treated his arrival as another opportunity for violence. The two Oni began loudly boasting about defeating one another and effectively competed to see who could laugh more obnoxiously.[2]
Ibaraki also noticed the disguised Ophis Elderblood watching the gathering. When Ophis commented that Ibaraki reminded her of her father, Ibaraki enthusiastically concluded that her father must therefore be someone he would get along with.[2]
His attention soon moved toward Haruna when she arrived at the head of thousands of Yokai. Ibaraki was visibly excited by her appearance and tightened his grip around his mace, wanting to see what would happen when the leader of the Black Kitsune confronted Genji.[2]
The following events further demonstrated Ibaraki's fascination with strong opponents. When Haruna challenged Genji's position and threatened the existing Yokai hierarchy, Ibaraki enthusiastically watched the confrontation rather than reacting with the caution displayed by most other observers.[6]
In Chapter 339, the situation surrounding Gyuki escalated after he targeted the disguised [[Nero Elderblood]] and Ophis. Gyuki had already killed a weaker Yokai and attempted to approach the two girls, causing [[Nero]] to fire upon him with her revolvers.[3]
Shuten condemned Gyuki as a disgrace to the Oni race. Ibaraki immediately joined in, laughing at Gyuki and reminding him that he had fled during their previous battle. Gyuki insisted that his retreat had been strategic, further intensifying their hostility.[3]
The tension between the two Oni continued until [[Hashimoto Genji]] intervened. Even after Genji warned the remaining Oni to stop causing problems, Ibaraki openly admitted that remaining peaceful was virtually impossible because he was bored.[3]
Once the confrontation ended, Shuten ordered Ibaraki to leave with him. Despite staring at Gyuki for several additional moments, Ibaraki ultimately obeyed his leader without protest.[3]
Approximately one week later, Ibaraki and Shuten encountered [[Victor Elderblood]] during Victor's own arrival in Japan. Ibaraki's instincts immediately reacted to Victor's presence. His demeanor became considerably wilder and his fighting spirit exploded as he approached Victor with his enormous mace and a broad smile.[1]
The pressure released by Ibaraki was strong enough to make the surrounding atmosphere suffocating for several moments. His eyes glowed with supernatural power and Shuten immediately realized that his friend was about to challenge the unfamiliar man.[1]
Shuten attempted to stop him.
He was too late.
Before the surrounding Yokai could properly understand what happened, Victor casually punched Ibaraki in the abdomen and launched him through multiple buildings.[1]
Only a small number of sufficiently powerful observers could even perceive the exchange.
Shuten was stunned. Within their Clan, Shuten himself was the strongest fighter, while Ibaraki ranked immediately behind him in second place. Seeing Victor eliminate Ibaraki through a single casual punch therefore immediately changed Shuten's understanding of the stranger standing before him.[1]
Victor explained that under ordinary circumstances he might have entertained Ibaraki's challenge, but the current situation was not appropriate for unnecessary fighting.
Ibaraki eventually recovered, demonstrating the resilience expected from a high-level Oni.
During the escalating conflict between Haruna and Genji, Ibaraki quickly became interested again. When Victor and Haruna's similar personalities caused their supernatural presences to clash almost unconsciously, Ibaraki visibly wanted to join the excitement.[4]
Shuten physically grabbed his arm and ordered him not to interfere.
The Oni leader explained that if their Clan joined the fighting at that moment, they could accidentally offend the Gods and create consequences their faction was incapable of handling. Ibaraki reluctantly turned away and claimed that he had never intended to interfere, although Shuten immediately pointed out that both of them knew this was untrue.[4]
Ibaraki later remained among the supernatural observers during the confrontation involving [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] and [[Inari-Omikami]]. When [[Scathach]] released an aura reflecting the countless battles and deaths accumulated throughout approximately two thousand years of constant warfare, Ibaraki's usual enthusiasm disappeared.[7]
The sheer killing intent surrounding Scathach was enough to make Ibaraki swallow nervously. Rather than becoming excited at the possibility of another strong opponent, he acknowledged that fighting her would simply result in him dying uselessly.[7]
This remains one of the clearest demonstrations that despite his obsession with combat, Ibaraki can recognize an opponent whose level of danger exceeds what even he considers entertaining.

### Skills & Abilities
Ibaraki is one of the strongest known Oni in Japan. Within [[Shuten Douji]]'s Clan, he is explicitly ranked second in strength, surpassed only by Shuten himself.[1]

#### Oni Physiology
- Oni Physiology: As a powerful Oni, Ibaraki possesses a supernatural body dramatically superior to ordinary Humans and weaker Yokai.
- Superhuman Strength: Ibaraki possesses enormous physical strength consistent with his position as the second strongest Oni in Shuten's Clan. His preferred weapon is a massive mace designed around direct physical combat.[2][1]
- Superhuman Speed: Despite his enormous size, Ibaraki can move at supernatural speeds. His attempt to attack [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] occurred quickly enough that ordinary observers could not properly follow the exchange.[1]
- Superhuman Durability: Ibaraki survived being casually punched by Victor with enough force to send him through multiple buildings.[1]
- Enhanced Stamina: His Oni physiology allows him to sustain combat and physical exertion far beyond normal biological limits.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Ibaraki is specifically regarded as difficult to fight because Oni possess extraordinarily powerful regeneration, with observers describing his regenerative ability as one of the reasons confronting him is troublesome.[2]
- Enhanced Senses: As a high-level Yokai, Ibaraki possesses supernatural perception capable of recognizing powerful presences and changes in the surrounding supernatural environment.
- Longevity: As a Yokai, Ibaraki possesses a lifespan far exceeding that of ordinary Humans.
- Horns: His two large horns are natural characteristics of his Oni physiology.

#### Yōuki
- Yōuki Manipulation: As an Oni Yokai, Ibaraki naturally possesses and emits Yōuki, the supernatural Energy used by Yokai.[2]
- Aura Generation: Ibaraki can release enough supernatural pressure for the surrounding environment to become physically oppressive.[1]
- Power Amplification: His Yōuki enhances his already extraordinary physical abilities during combat.
- Supernatural Presence: When Ibaraki's fighting spirit rises, his presence becomes visibly more feral and oppressive, with his eyes glowing from the power he releases.[1]

#### Enhanced Regeneration
Ibaraki's regenerative ability is one of the traits for which he is specifically known.
- Rapid Recovery: The Tengu consider fighting Ibaraki unnecessarily troublesome partly because of the extreme regeneration inherent to his Oni body.[2]
- Severe Injury Survival: Ibaraki survived [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] launching him through several buildings with a casual punch despite the enormous difference in strength between them.[1]
The exact upper limits of his regeneration have not been revealed.

#### Killing Intent
- Killing Intent: Ibaraki can release a powerful predatory and murderous presence when preparing for battle. His confrontations with [[Gyuki]] cause the atmosphere around them to become increasingly hostile.[3]
- Intimidation: His reputation and supernatural presence are sufficient for ordinary Yokai and even supernatural law-enforcement forces to avoid unnecessary confrontation with him.[2]

#### Combat Skills
- Expert [[Melee]] Combatant: Ibaraki is a highly experienced Oni warrior whose position as the second strongest fighter in Shuten's Clan indicates extensive close-range combat ability.[1]
- Mace Proficiency: Ibaraki's primary weapon is an enormous mace which he casually carries over one shoulder and immediately prepares whenever a worthwhile battle presents itself.[2][1]
- Battle Instinct: Ibaraki possesses strong instinctive awareness regarding dangerous opponents and becomes particularly responsive to beings displaying considerable power.
- Combat Experience: His rivalry with [[Gyuki]] includes previous battles, with their dialogue establishing that the two had already fought before their introduction.[3]
- Threat Assessment: Although normally reckless, Ibaraki can recognize an opponent who is completely beyond his ability. After perceiving [[Scathach]]'s killing aura, he correctly abandoned any desire to challenge her.[7]

### Power and Standing
Ibaraki is an exceptionally powerful Yokai by the standards of Japan's Oni community.
Within [[Shuten Douji]]'s Clan, the hierarchy in raw combat strength is explicitly stated to place Shuten first and Ibaraki second.[1] His reputation is significant enough that supernatural authorities familiar with his behavior prefer ignoring his provocations rather than risk triggering a destructive battle.[2]
His strength should nevertheless not be inflated beyond what the story demonstrates. Ibaraki was completely unable to react effectively to [[Victor Elderblood]] when they met in Chapter 354 and was launched through multiple buildings by a single casual punch.[1] Likewise, after witnessing [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] release her genuine killing aura, Ibaraki immediately understood that attempting to fight her would simply result in his death.[7]
Ibaraki is therefore extremely powerful within ordinary Yokai society but remains vastly beneath monsters operating at the highest levels of the supernatural world.

### Relationships

#### Shuten Douji
[[Shuten Douji]] is Ibaraki's Clan Leader, close friend and the only Oni within their faction explicitly confirmed to be stronger than him.[1]
Their relationship is unusually informal. Ibaraki regularly insults Shuten, complains about his popularity with the women of their Clan and tells him not to follow him around. Shuten responds with exasperation rather than punishment and openly describes keeping Ibaraki under control as equivalent to holding his leash.[2][5]
Despite this constant arguing, Ibaraki respects Shuten's authority. When Shuten seriously orders him not to fight or interfere, Ibaraki ultimately obeys even when he clearly wants to do the opposite.[3][4]
Shuten similarly treats Ibaraki as both an important Clan member and personal friend, reacting with genuine shock when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] casually sends him flying in Chapter 354.[1]

#### Gyuki
[[Gyuki]] was Ibaraki's rival and a member of an opposing Oni faction.
Their relationship was openly hostile. Whenever the two met, they immediately began provoking each other and boasting about who would win another fight.[2]
Chapter 339 establishes that they had fought previously. Ibaraki mocked Gyuki for fleeing their last battle, while Gyuki insisted that his departure had merely been a strategic retreat.[3]
Although both men were violent Oni, their values were not identical. Shuten and Ibaraki considered Gyuki's habit of preying upon weaker beings disgraceful enough for Shuten to openly call him a shame to the Oni race.[3]

#### Victor Elderblood
Ibaraki first met [[Victor Elderblood]] approximately one week after the initial [[Hyakki Yakō]] gathering.
His immediate response to Victor was exactly what Shuten feared: Ibaraki sensed an interesting opponent and attempted to challenge him without hesitation.[1]
Victor defeated him through a single casual punch before Ibaraki could begin a real fight, sending the Oni through several buildings.[1]
The event was particularly significant because Ibaraki was the second strongest member of Shuten's Clan. Shuten's shock at seeing him defeated so effortlessly immediately established Victor as a threat far beyond ordinary Japanese Yokai.[1]

#### Ophis Elderblood
Ibaraki encountered Ophis Elderblood while she was disguised during the [[Hyakki Yakō]].
When Ophis remarked that Ibaraki reminded her of her father, he immediately became interested and concluded that her father must be someone he would get along with.[2]
The remark became unintentionally accurate when Ibaraki later met [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and immediately attempted to fight him.

#### Scathach Elderblood Scarlett
Ibaraki did not directly fight [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], but witnessing her power produced one of the rare occasions where his desire for battle disappeared.
When [[Scathach]] released the killing aura accumulated through approximately two thousand years of warfare, Ibaraki recognized that challenging her would not result in an enjoyable fight but simply in his death.[7]

### Oni Clan
Ibaraki belongs to the Oni Clan led by [[Shuten Douji]].
The Clan possesses a heavily female-skewed population, with the ratio described as approximately eight women for every two men.[5] Female members are generally tall, muscular and warrior-like, with many resembling smaller and somewhat less muscular versions of Ibaraki.
Their community follows an old mating tradition in which women choose their preferred partners. If another male objects to the woman's choice, he is expected to challenge the selected man and prove himself the superior partner through combat.[5]
This tradition contributes greatly to Ibaraki's frustration with Shuten. Because Shuten is both exceptionally powerful and unusually handsome compared with most Oni, a disproportionate number of female Oni select him as their partner. Ibaraki frequently complains about this situation and jokingly curses Shuten's romantic luck.[5]
No one within the Clan seriously attempts to challenge Shuten over these selections because his position as leader is backed by overwhelming strength.

### Legend
Ibaraki-dōji is a famous Oni appearing in Japanese legends associated with the Heian period and is commonly connected to the stories of Shuten-dōji. Depending upon the tradition, Ibaraki is portrayed as Shuten-dōji's most important subordinate, close companion or one of the principal Oni of Mount Ōe.
The legends surrounding Ibaraki's origin vary considerably, with traditions connecting the Oni to locations in the former Settsu and Echigo Provinces. Even Ibaraki's gender differs between some versions of the mythology.
One of the most famous stories concerns Ibaraki-dōji's confrontation with the warrior Watanabe no Tsuna at Kyoto's Rashōmon gate. Tsuna succeeds in severing one of the Oni's arms. Ibaraki later returns in disguise and tricks Tsuna into revealing the preserved arm before reclaiming it.
The novel adapts the names and broad historical associations of Ibaraki and Shuten while presenting its own supernatural versions of the characters.
