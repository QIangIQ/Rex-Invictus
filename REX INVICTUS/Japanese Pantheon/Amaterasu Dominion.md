The **[[Amaterasu Dominion]]** is the far-future realm of the [[Japanese Pantheon]] under [[Amaterasu]]. After its war with the Greeks it is also known simply as the Dynasty.

---

## UOE (Far Future)

- **Relations with the UOE.** In shaky alliance with the [[Undying Olympian Empire]]. The Shinto-Greek relationship broke down after [[The War of Heavens]], and the Dominion's relays were taken during [[The Great Expansion]].
- **The Yamamoto Chokepoint.** The [[Yamamoto Chokepoint Relay]], reopened by the chief engineer [[Kagura Kuroyami]], is the only public route between the two powers.
- **[[War of Princes]].** [[Rosenthal]] allied the new [[Voss Sovereignty]] with the Dominion, and the supplies it sent let the Sovereignty rebuild a partial fleet. Later the Shinto front held despite staggering losses. Details of the talks are in [[Annex 15632]].
- **Allies.** The [[Heavenly Palace]] of the cultivation world is loosely allied with it.
