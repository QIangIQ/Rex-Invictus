### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 702
Shinigami is the unidentified God of Death associated with the [[Shinto Pantheon]] and the Japanese [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]]. His actual personal name has never been revealed; Shinigami is instead the title of the office he occupies. Unlike unique Divine identities such as [[Amaterasu]] or Susanoo, the position of Shinigami is explicitly replaceable: another God can assume the office when the Ruler of the Shinto Underworld appoints a successor.[1]
As the Shinto God of Death, Shinigami belongs to the supernatural administration responsible for maintaining the movement of Souls through the afterlife. Gods of Death operate directly beneath the Ruler of a Pantheon's Hell and act as lower agents of the greater Soul-management system supervised by [[The Judges of The Abyss]]. They investigate Souls that fail to follow their intended course, including spirits that remain trapped on Earth instead of proceeding into the appropriate afterlife.[1]
Despite this official responsibility, Shinigami was secretly involved with forces cooperating with [[New Dawn]]. He appeared at a concealed New Dawn installation inside a Pocket Dimension above the Bermuda Triangle alongside [[Anubis]] and [[Taranis]], where New Dawn was maintaining an isolated Human population as part of an experiment involving Faith and the artificial creation of new Gods.[2][3]

### Physical Appearance
Shinigami is a tall man with healthy skin, long black hair and distinctly East Asian features. When [[Victor Elderblood]] first observed him inside [[New Dawn]]'s hidden Pocket Dimension, Shinigami was wearing an elegant black suit decorated with golden trim. Victor reproduced his appearance through an Ice sculpture so the others could later identify him.[2]
His outward appearance is considerably more Human than many Gods associated with death or the [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]]. He does not possess an exposed skeletal body, monstrous appearance or the stereotypical hooded form associated with some Death-related entities.
The most striking characteristic Victor noticed was instead supernatural. His presence reminded Victor of an Angel of Death, immediately indicating some relationship with Death, yet the sensation of Death surrounding him appeared comparatively weak. Victor simultaneously sensed a stronger quality that he described as being more "dark" than purely Death-oriented.[2] The exact nature of this darker characteristic has never been explained.

### Personality and Traits
Shinigami receives no direct dialogue during his only confirmed physical appearance, leaving much of his individual personality unknown. His actions nevertheless demonstrate that he was willing to operate covertly outside the ordinary public responsibilities of the [[Shinto Pantheon]]. He secretly visited a [[New Dawn]] installation alongside [[Anubis]] and [[Taranis]] and disappeared before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s group infiltrated the facility, suggesting awareness that his activities were not intended to become public.[2]
His cooperation with Gods from unrelated Pantheons also demonstrates that he was not restricted by conventional Divine nationalism. The Gods assisting New Dawn came from different mythological systems yet were participating in the same broader operation involving Faith and the creation of new Divine beings.[2][3]
Beyond this, Shinigami's personal motivations remain unknown. The novel has not revealed whether he joined New Dawn because he supported its objective of changing the existing supernatural order, because of an arrangement with the negative factions associated with the various Underworlds, because of personal ambition or because of another unrevealed reason.

### History

#### God of Death
Shinigami occupies a special administrative position within the [[Shinto Pantheon]] rather than merely being a God who happens to possess a Death-related Divinity. Chapter 704 explains that Shinigami is effectively an occupational title belonging to the Shinto [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] and that the individual holding it can be replaced if the Ruler of that Hell appoints someone else.[1]
This office exists because sufficiently developed Pantheons possess an organized afterlife infrastructure connected to the greater cosmic Soul System. A Pantheon's Hell must become sufficiently large and receive a large enough flow of Souls to attract the attention of [[The Judges of The Abyss]]. Once incorporated into the greater system created by the [[Primordial Entities]], such an Underworld possesses a Ruler and subordinate Gods of Death responsible for helping maintain the proper flow of Souls.[1]

As the Shinto Pantheon's God of Death, Shinigami therefore traditionally operates beneath [[Izanami-no-Mikoto]], the Ruler associated with the Japanese Underworld. His ordinary responsibilities include assisting with Soul-related irregularities and investigating spirits that fail to pass through the afterlife system correctly.[1]

#### Connection to New Dawn
At some point, Shinigami became involved with [[New Dawn]] or the network of Gods cooperating with the organization. The extent of this relationship is unclear. He should more accurately be described as an ally or collaborator of New Dawn rather than one of its confirmed original members, as the organization's core founders and membership originated separately.[2]
His involvement appears to be part of a larger pattern in which "dark" Gods from different Pantheons cooperated with the organization. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] connected this activity with [[Diablo]]'s long-term intention of gathering Dark-aligned Deities from different Divine civilizations into a unified Pantheon. Because New Dawn had maintained close relations with Diablo's faction, Victor considered cooperation between the organization and Gods associated with Underworlds or negative Divine aspects unsurprising.[3]

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
After destroying several [[New Dawn]] facilities, [[Victor Elderblood]] and his allies discovered information leading them toward a location above the Bermuda Triangle. [[Oda Nobunaga]], working with specialized Demons serving Victor, traced the organization's activity to what appeared to be an ordinary region of ocean. The group eventually discovered that New Dawn possessed a hidden Pocket Dimension containing a heavily developed island and military installation.[2]
Victor examined the concealed Dimension with his Dragon Eyes and discovered an entire Human settlement inside it. While observing the base, he detected the presence of a powerful God. Victor created an Ice sculpture reproducing the figure he could see: a tall East Asian man with healthy skin, long black hair and a black suit decorated with golden trim. The man's supernatural signature reminded Victor of an [[Azrael|Angel of Death]], although the actual sensation of Death surrounding him appeared weaker than expected and was mixed with something Victor regarded as more fundamentally "dark".[2]
Before the group could identify him, a portal opened beside the God and two additional Divine beings arrived. Victor generated sculptures of them as well, recognizing that one appeared Egyptian while being unable to identify the other. The three Gods remained together briefly before disappearing and leaving the Human inhabitants to continue operating the facility.[2]
Further investigation revealed that the island was not merely a New Dawn military base. The Humans living there were receiving food and supplies while being deliberately isolated from the outside world. Communications had been destroyed and the inhabitants had been led to believe that Humanity had effectively been annihilated by supernatural monsters. Every household contained a shrine dedicated to a supposedly heroic God named Thorron, a figure represented as a spiky-haired warrior carrying an axe.[3]
[[Jeanne Elderblood]] realized that the conditions were deliberately reproducing the environment in which Gods had naturally emerged during ancient history. Humans living in terror and uncertainty, convinced that the outside world had been destroyed and depending upon Divine protection for survival, generated intense Faith. Combined with a manufactured myth surrounding Thorron, this Faith could eventually cause an entirely new God to be born. The facility was effectively an experiment designed to artificially recreate the myth-making process that once generated powerful Gods naturally.[3]
This placed Shinigami directly within one of New Dawn's most ambitious known projects. Although the novel never specifies his individual responsibility in constructing the experiment, he was physically present at the installation alongside the other Gods supporting its operation.[2][3]
Unable to identify the three Gods through appearance alone, Victor summoned [[Aphrodite]], [[Rhea]] and [[Alexios Alioth]]. Aphrodite examined Victor's Ice sculptures and immediately identified the Eastern God not by a personal name but by his office: the Shinigami, or Shinto God of Death. She explained that unlike many Divine titles, Shinigami was an occupational position rather than an irreplaceable identity. A different God could be appointed to the role whenever the Shinto Ruler of Hell declared a replacement.[1]
Aphrodite also identified the Egyptian figure as [[Anubis]] and the third God as [[Taranis]]. This discussion led to a broader explanation of the function of Gods of Death. In Pantheons possessing Hells integrated into the universal Soul System, the God of Death serves directly beneath the Ruler and helps administer Soul irregularities on behalf of that [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]]. They function as lower-level agents of the system overseen by the Judges of the Abyss, ensuring that Souls which become trapped, displaced or otherwise fail to reach their intended destination are investigated and returned to the proper cycle.[1]
Victor's faction subsequently infiltrated the Dimension and destroyed New Dawn's operation, preventing the isolated community from continuing to serve as an artificial Faith farm. Shinigami was no longer present when the assault occurred and was therefore not confronted or killed during the destruction of the base.[1]
After the island was destroyed, Taranis and Anubis later returned to the location and discovered that Victor had erased the operation. [[Erebus]] also appeared and confirmed from the lingering [[Draconic Energy]] that Alucard had been responsible. Shinigami was notably absent from this later gathering, and the novel has not explained where he went after his initial appearance.[4]

### Skills & Abilities
Shinigami has not participated in a direct battle, so his complete combat capabilities remain unknown. His confirmed powers and functions come primarily from his status as a genuine God and his office as the [[Shinto Pantheon]]'s God of Death.
- God Physiology: Shinigami is a genuine Third Generation Shinto God and possesses the fundamental supernatural characteristics of Divine beings, including a Divine Soul, [[Divine Energy]], supernatural physical capabilities, extreme longevity and resistance exceeding ordinary mortal species.
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: As a God, Shinigami possesses supernatural strength, speed, durability, stamina and perception. None of these attributes have been individually tested on-page.
- Divine Energy Manipulation: Shinigami possesses and manipulates Divine Energy as the fundamental supernatural Energy of his Divine existence.
- Divine Soul: His existence is supported by a Divine Soul capable of containing and expressing Divinity.
- Agelessness / Divine Longevity: Shinigami has existed for an immense period and is not subject to ordinary Human aging.
- Divine Regeneration: Gods possess supernatural recovery significantly greater than ordinary mortals, although sufficient damage to the Soul or Divinity can still kill them.
- Godly [[Immortality]]: Shinigami does not naturally die through aging. This is not absolute immortality against Soul destruction, conceptual attacks or sufficiently powerful supernatural means.

#### Death Divinity
- Divinity — Death: As the officially appointed God of Death of the Shinto [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]], Shinigami possesses Divine authority associated with Death.[1]
- Death Manipulation: His Divine office gives him supernatural authority related to Death. The exact offensive applications of this authority have never been demonstrated, so unrestricted instant-death effects should not be attributed to him without further evidence.
- Death Energy / Divine Death Authority: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] immediately recognized a Death-related quality in Shinigami's supernatural presence, comparing the feeling to that produced by an Angel of Death.[2]
- Death Detection: Shinigami's role requires interaction with phenomena surrounding deceased beings and irregular Souls. The precise sensory range of this ability has not been demonstrated.
- Underworld Compatibility: As a God whose formal office exists within the Shinto Underworld, Shinigami is naturally suited to operating within Yomi and other Soul-related Divine environments.

#### Soul Administration
Gods of Death are not merely combat Gods associated with killing. Their principal cosmological function is helping maintain the flow of Souls beneath the authority of their Pantheon's Ruler.[1]
- Soul Management: Shinigami assists in administering Souls belonging to the Shinto afterlife system.[1]
- Psychopomp Authority: Gods of Death and their subordinates investigate Souls that fail to proceed naturally to their assigned afterlife, including spirits trapped on Earth.[1]
- Soul Detection: His occupational duties require the ability to identify or locate abnormal Soul activity, although his exact detection range is unknown.
- Soul Interaction: Shinigami can interact with disembodied Souls as part of his Divine office. The novel has not shown enough to establish the maximum extent of his Soul Manipulation.
- Soul Guidance: Gods of Death are responsible for helping Souls return to the intended supernatural system when their natural transition has been interrupted.[1]
- Afterlife-System Knowledge: Shinigami possesses specialized knowledge of the structure through which Souls are processed, transported and managed within a major Pantheon's Hell.
- [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] Administration: His position places him beneath the Ruler in the operational hierarchy of the Shinto Underworld.[1]
- Agent of the Soul System: Gods of Death function as lesser agents maintaining the universal Soul System created by the [[Primordial Entities]] and supervised by the Judges of the Abyss.[1]
The above administrative powers should not be exaggerated into unrestricted authority over every Soul. Shinigami operates beneath a Ruler and ultimately beneath the greater cosmic system maintained by the Primordial Entities.

#### Dark Nature
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] sensed that although Shinigami possessed a clear relationship with Death, the feeling of Death itself was comparatively weak and another quality surrounding him appeared more strongly dark.[2]
- Dark Divine Nature (Unexplained): Shinigami possesses an unidentified supernatural characteristic that Victor perceived as darker than his ordinary Death signature.
- Dark Energy Affinity (Possible): His presence suggests compatibility with supernatural forces associated with Darkness or the negative side of Divine existence, but no separate Darkness Divinity has been confirmed.
- Negative-Aligned Divine Compatibility: Shinigami was able to operate alongside other Dark-aligned Gods working with [[New Dawn]] without any demonstrated conflict between their Divine natures.
The exact source of this characteristic remains unknown and should not be listed as a confirmed Divinity of Darkness without a direct statement.

#### Divine Office
Shinigami's position is unusual because Shinigami itself is an office rather than a permanently unique Divine identity.[1]
- God of Death Office: The Shinto Ruler can designate a God to act as Shinigami.
- Replaceable Divine Position: Another individual can inherit the title and responsibilities if the Ruler appoints a replacement.[1]
- Ruler Subordination: As a God of Death, Shinigami officially operates directly beneath the Ruler of the Shinto [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]].
- Delegation: Gods of Death can possess subordinate agents or minions who perform Soul-related duties in their name.[1]
The replaceable nature of the office means that the current Shinigami should not automatically be identified with every mythological or historical entity ever referred to by that title.

#### Power
Shinigami was described by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] as a very strong God when first detected inside [[New Dawn]]'s hidden Dimension.[2] This establishes that he is not merely one of the countless insignificant Kami belonging to the [[Shinto Pantheon]].
His exact ranking within the Shinto Pantheon is nevertheless unknown. He has never fought Victor, another God or any comparable supernatural combatant on-page, and there is insufficient evidence to classify him as a God-King or Primordial God. His Third Generation classification and specialized office as a God of Death are more appropriate than attempting to scale him from unrelated Shinto Gods.
Victor was cautious about directly consuming Shinigami, [[Anubis]] or [[Taranis]] after discovering them because all three were mature Gods possessing established Divinities. At that point Victor lacked sufficient understanding of what absorbing an already-developed foreign Divinity might do to him, contrasting them with the newly ascended War, whose immature Divinity had posed far less uncertainty.[3]

### Relationships

#### Izanami-no-Mikoto
[[Izanami-no-Mikoto]] is the traditional Ruler of the Japanese [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] and therefore occupies the authority above Shinigami's office within the Shinto afterlife hierarchy. Chapter 704 explains that the Shinto God of Death is a subordinate position controlled by the Underworld's Ruler and that the person occupying the office can be replaced through that Ruler's decision.[1]
The novel has not shown a direct conversation between Izanami and the current Shinigami, so the nature of their personal relationship remains unknown.

#### New Dawn
Shinigami secretly cooperated with [[New Dawn]] and appeared at one of its most important hidden installations alongside [[Anubis]] and [[Taranis]].[2] His exact status inside the organization is unclear. Unlike figures such as Radu Tepes, James and Fenir, he has not been identified as one of New Dawn's foundational members and is better classified as an allied God or collaborator.
The base at which he appeared was being used to isolate Humans, manufacture artificial myths and accumulate the Faith necessary to produce new Gods. Shinigami's exact responsibility within the project was not revealed before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] destroyed the installation.[3]

#### Anubis
[[Anubis]] was one of the two Gods who appeared beside Shinigami at [[New Dawn]]'s hidden island. As another God closely associated with Death and the afterlife, Anubis shared a natural thematic connection with Shinigami even though they belonged to separate Pantheons.[2][1]
No direct dialogue between them was shown during Shinigami's appearance.

#### Taranis
[[Taranis]] arrived at the [[New Dawn]] facility alongside [[Anubis]] and operated as another Divine collaborator in the Faith project.[2] [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] initially found Taranis especially strange because the Celtic God produced a presence resembling an [[Azrael|Angel of Death]] despite traditionally being identified as a God of Thunder. [[Aphrodite]] theorized that he had somehow acquired a powerful Death Divinity despite the [[Celtic Pantheon]] lacking the full Ruler/God-of-Death structure possessed by the Seven Great Pantheons.[1]
Shinigami's own Death authority, by contrast, came from an established formal office within the Shinto [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]].

#### Victor Elderblood
Shinigami has never directly confronted [[Victor Elderblood]]. Victor first observed him remotely through his Dragon Eyes while investigating [[New Dawn]]'s hidden Pocket Dimension and immediately recognized him as a powerful Divine being with some relationship to Death.[2]
Shinigami departed before Victor entered the Dimension and therefore survived the destruction of the island. Victor later eliminated the Faith experiment but did not capture or consume Shinigami.[1]

### Current Status
Shinigami remains alive based on the currently available material. He was never present during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s destruction of [[New Dawn]]'s Faith facility and is not shown among the Gods killed in subsequent conflicts.
His current location and exact political status are unknown. The [[Shinto Pantheon]] was eventually brought beneath Victor Elderblood's wider Imperial authority while retaining much of its internal administration, but no later chapter has specifically shown Shinigami submitting to Victor, abandoning New Dawn or being removed from his position as God of Death. His continued place within the Shinto Pantheon should therefore be distinguished from any assumption that his secret alliances automatically disappeared following the Pantheon's annexation.
