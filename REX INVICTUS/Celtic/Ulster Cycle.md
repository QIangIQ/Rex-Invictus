The **[[Ulster Cycle]]** is the body of medieval Irish sagas about the Ulaid (the people of Ulster) under King Conchobar mac Nessa at Emain Macha. Its centerpiece is the *Táin Bó Cúailnge* ("The Cattle Raid of Cooley"), in which Queen Medb of Connacht invades Ulster for the Brown Bull and the teenage [[Cuchulain|Cú Chulainn]] holds the ford alone.

**Key texts.** *Tochmarc Emire* ("The Wooing of Emer") sends Cú Chulainn to train with [[Scathach|Scáthach]] in Alba, where he fights [[Aoife|Aífe]]. *Aided Óenfir Aífe* tells how he kills their son Connla. The stories survive in the *Book of the Dun Cow* (c. 1100) and the *Book of Leinster*.

**In the vault.** [[Scathach]]'s [[Rex Invictus]] note grounds her mythic origin here. See [[Celtic Pantheon]].
