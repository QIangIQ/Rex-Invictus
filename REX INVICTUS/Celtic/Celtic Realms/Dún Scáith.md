**[[Dún Scáith]]** ("fortress of shadows") is [[Scathach|Scáthach]]'s stronghold in the [[Ulster Cycle]], where she trains heroes such as [[Cuchulain|Cú Chulainn]] in arms. Tradition places it on the [[Isle of Skye]]; the ruins of Dunscaith Castle at Tokavaig on Sleat bear its name. To reach it Cú Chulainn crosses the Plain of Ill-Luck and the Bridge of the Leaps, which bucks anyone who steps on it.

**In the vault.** [[Scathach]]'s domain, the "Land of Shadows", draws on it.
