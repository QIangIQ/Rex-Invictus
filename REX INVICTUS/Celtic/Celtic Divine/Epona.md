### Physical Appearance
Epona's physical appearance has not yet been described in the novel. She is only mentioned indirectly in Chapter 873 and does not personally appear during the scene.[1]

### Personality
Very little is currently known about Epona's personality because she has not been given direct dialogue or an individual scene in the novel. [[Taranis]] nevertheless regards her as someone knowledgeable enough in Beauty and charm-related powers to help him develop resistance against abilities of that nature. His immediate decision to seek Epona's assistance after being affected by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and [[Aphrodite]]'s charm suggests that she is recognized within the [[Celtic Pantheon]] for her expertise concerning supernatural attraction and Beauty-based influence.[1]

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
During the hunt organized after the Gathering of Supernatural Beings, several Pantheons sent representatives to assist [[Victor Elderblood]] against his enemies. The [[Celtic Pantheon]] was represented at the gathering by [[Taranis]] and [[Cernuno]], while Epona herself did not accompany them.[1]
When Victor arrived with several of his wives, the assembled Gods and Angels were immediately affected by [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood's extraordinary beauty despite Aphrodite not actively using her supernatural charm. Victor criticized the gathered warriors for being so easily distracted and decided to demonstrate how dangerous an actual charm-based attack could be. Using Aphrodite's blessing through himself, Victor released a burst of pink Energy that caused nearly everyone present to look at him with overwhelming desire.[1]
After Victor withdrew the effect, the embarrassed Gods immediately began considering methods of resisting similar abilities in the future. [[Loki]] decided to seek training from [[Freyja|Freya]], while Susanoo considered asking [[Sakuya-hime]] for assistance. Taranis likewise stated that he would seek Epona for the same purpose. Aphrodite responded by dismissively referring to Freya, Epona and Sakuya-hime as other Goddesses of Beauty, directly establishing Epona's Divine association with Beauty in the novel.[1]
Although Epona does not physically participate in the scene, Taranis's response establishes that her expertise is considered relevant to resisting high-level supernatural charm and attraction effects.[1]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
The [[Celtic Pantheon]] was later confronted by the forces of the [[Elderblood Empire]] during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s campaign to bring Earth's supernatural factions under Imperial authority. [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] personally entered the Celtic Divine territory with Imperial forces and demanded that [[Sucellus]] and the assembled Celtic Gods either peacefully submit to Victor's rule or be forced into submission.[2]
Epona is not individually named during the confrontation, and the novel does not provide a separate account of her actions during the Celtic Pantheon's loss of political independence. No direct scene depicting Epona's death has currently been established, so her individual status should not be inferred solely from the fate of the Pantheon itself.

### Skills & Abilities
- God Physiology: Epona is an ancient Third Generation Goddess of the [[Celtic Pantheon]]. As a Divine Being, her existence is fundamentally spiritual and conceptual rather than that of an ordinary Mortal, granting her the supernatural characteristics common to Gods.
- Beauty Manipulation: Epona is explicitly identified as a Goddess of Beauty. Her Divine authority therefore concerns the Concept of Beauty and supernatural phenomena associated with it.[1]
- Supernatural Attraction / Charm Manipulation: Beauty-related Divine authority can influence the perception and attraction of other Beings rather than functioning as ordinary physical attractiveness. Epona is specifically brought up when the Gods discuss how to resist [[Aphrodite]] and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s supernatural charm, indicating that her abilities and knowledge concern this class of influence.[1]
- Charm Resistance Expertise: After being overwhelmed by Victor using Aphrodite's blessing, [[Taranis]] immediately decides to ask Epona to train him against such effects. This establishes that Epona possesses sufficient understanding of supernatural Beauty and charm to be considered capable of teaching resistance against them.[1]
Note: Epona has not personally demonstrated her Beauty-based abilities on-page. Their precise strength, range and individual applications therefore remain unknown and should not be expanded using abilities from real-world mythology or unrelated Beauty Goddesses.
- [[Divine Energy]] Manipulation: As a Goddess, Epona can generate and wield Divine Energy associated with her Divine existence.
- Conceptual Existence: Like other Gods, Epona's Divine existence is intrinsically connected to the Concept represented by her authority rather than functioning as a purely biological organism.
- [[Immortality]] / Agelessness: Epona is an ancient Goddess whose age is measured in eons and possesses the supernatural longevity characteristic of Gods.
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: As a Divine Being, Epona possesses physical and supernatural capabilities vastly exceeding those of ordinary Mortals. Her individual combat ability has not yet been demonstrated.
- Regeneration: Gods possess supernatural spiritual bodies capable of recovering from injuries that would be fatal to ordinary biological Beings, provided their deeper Divine existence has not been permanently destroyed.
- Divine Senses: As a Higher Existence, Epona possesses supernatural perception superior to that of ordinary Mortals, although no individual sensory feat has yet been attributed specifically to her.

### Trivia
- Epona's first mention occurs in Chapter 873, but she does not physically appear in the chapter.[1]
- Her status as a Goddess of Beauty is established indirectly when [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood groups Epona together with [[Freyja|Freya]] and [[Sakuya-hime]] as other Beauty Goddesses.[1]
- [[Taranis]] considers Epona the appropriate Celtic Goddess to teach him how to resist supernatural charm after he is affected by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] using Aphrodite's blessing.[1]
- During the same conversation, [[Loki]] chooses Freya for similar training while Susanoo intends to seek Sakuya-hime, placing Epona among the Pantheon-specific specialists associated with Beauty and charm.[1]
- The novel has not revealed Epona's weapon, Divine Gear, appearance, relationships, combat style or additional Divine authorities.
- Real-world mythological attributes associated with the historical Epona are not automatically treated as abilities of the novel character unless the story itself confirms them.
