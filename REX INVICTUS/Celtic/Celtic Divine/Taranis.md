### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 702
Taranis was an ancient Celtic God, the God of Thunder of the [[Celtic Pantheon]], and one of the Divine Beings secretly associated with [[New Dawn]] and its experiments involving Humanity, Faith and the artificial creation of new Gods.[1][2]
Although Thunder was his original and publicly recognized Divinity, Taranis possessed another abnormal Divine authority related to Death. When [[Victor Elderblood]] observed Taranis through his Draconic Eyes, his existence felt more similar to an Angel of Death than to other Thunder Gods such as [[Thor]] or [[Zeus]]. [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood confirmed that Taranis was normally known as a Celtic God of Thunder and concluded that he must somehow have acquired a powerful Divinity of Death. The circumstances through which this happened were never revealed.[2]
This abnormality was particularly significant because the Celtic Pantheon was not one of the Seven Great Pantheons possessing a major Hell recognized by the cosmic system administered through the Abyss. Taranis therefore should not naturally have occupied the conventional position of a Pantheon's God of Death, making the strength of his Death Divinity especially suspicious.[2]
Taranis later abandoned his covert hostility toward Victor when the political situation changed. Under orders from [[Sucellus]], he represented the Celtic Pantheon in Victor's multinational hunt against Ra, [[Anubis]], Seth and their allies, fighting alongside Gods, Angels and newly created [[True Dragons]] from factions that had previously operated as rivals.[3]
He ultimately disappeared from the narrative during the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s conquest of the Celtic Pantheon. The independent Celtic Divine civilization was violently destroyed after [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] deliberately forced its Gods into battle rather than allowing an immediate surrender, and later scenes confirm that resisting Celtic Gods were killed, chained or consumed. Taranis is subsequently treated as deceased, although his individual death was never depicted.[4][5]

### Physical Appearance
The novel has not provided a sufficiently detailed description of Taranis' physical appearance.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] directly observed Taranis while investigating the hidden [[New Dawn]] facility above the Bermuda Triangle and reproduced his appearance through an Ice sculpture so that [[Aphrodite]] could identify him.[1][2] However, unlike [[Erebus]], whose body and clothing were explicitly described during the same investigation, the narration did not specify Taranis' hair color, eye color, height or clothing.
His current artwork therefore should not be treated as a canonical representation of details that have not been described by the novel.

### Personality and Traits
Taranis was short-tempered, outspoken and easily frustrated. After returning to the Bermuda Triangle facility and discovering that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had completely destroyed the island, Dimension and research site, his immediate reaction was an angry outburst over the loss of his work. [[Anubis]] had to repeatedly tell him to calm down.[6]
Despite this temper, Taranis was not incapable of fear or caution. The arrival of [[Erebus]] immediately changed his behavior. Taranis physically shuddered when the ancient Primordial God focused his attention on him, demonstrating that he understood the enormous difference between himself and one of the Original Primordials.[6]
He also possessed enough pragmatism to recognize Victor as an increasingly dangerous threat. After Erebus identified the remnants of Victor's Dragon Breath, Taranis did not suggest retaliation. Instead, he concluded that their remaining research operations needed to be hidden even more carefully.[6]
Taranis nevertheless remained morally ruthless toward anyone outside his interests. After losing the Bermuda Triangle experiment, he casually considered kidnapping [[Demeter]] or another agriculture-related God because such a deity would solve the food-production problems affecting [[New Dawn]]'s isolated Human populations.[6]
His later appearance during Victor's hunt revealed a more competitive side associated with his identity as a Thunder God. [[Thor]] became excited upon realizing that four Thunder or Storm Gods—himself, Taranis, Susanoo and [[Takemikazuchi]]—were gathered together and immediately suggested discovering which of them was strongest. Taranis did not directly accept the challenge because the group had a more important mission.[3]
Taranis was also capable of recognizing personal weaknesses. After both [[Aphrodite]]'s passive beauty and Victor's temporary use of her Charm affected him, Taranis decided that he would ask [[Epona]] to train him against Charm-based abilities rather than simply dismiss the humiliation.[3]

### History

#### Association with New Dawn
At an unknown point, Taranis became associated with [[New Dawn]] and the coalition of Gods secretly cooperating with the organization.
His role went considerably beyond simply knowing members of the group. The Bermuda Triangle facility investigated by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] belonged to a project in which isolated Human populations were manipulated into generating enormous quantities of Faith for artificially constructed Divine myths.[1][7]
The project's objective was effectively to recreate the environmental conditions under which powerful ancient Gods had naturally emerged.
The process used:
- isolated populations cut off from the outside world;
- staged supernatural disasters;
- false narratives convincing Humans that most of Humanity had been destroyed;
- Divine intervention presented as miraculous salvation;
- artificial myths centered around nonexistent Gods;
- shrines placed throughout the settlement;
- and the systematic accumulation of Faith over generations.[7]
One prospective deity was a nonexistent heroic God named Thorron, represented as a heroic man with spiked hair carrying an axe. The inhabitants were manipulated into worshiping such figures until sufficient Faith and mythology accumulated for a genuine God to potentially emerge.[7]
Taranis was one of the Gods involved with this operation. When he returned after Victor destroyed the facility, his furious reaction that his "work" had been eliminated and his immediate discussion of other research sites confirmed that he was actively participating in the project rather than merely being an outside observer.[6]
Earlier versions of this article incorrectly described the project as collecting Fate Energy. The experiment was actually based primarily upon Faith, mythology and the supernatural mechanism through which belief can contribute toward the birth of new Gods.[7]

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Taranis first appeared indirectly during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s investigation of a hidden dimensional facility operated above the Bermuda Triangle.
Victor, [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], [[Jeanne Elderblood]], [[Morgana Elderblood]] and [[Oda Blank]] discovered a concealed pocket Dimension containing an isolated Human settlement. While observing the facility through his Draconic Eyes, Victor detected several powerful Divine signatures entering the area.[1]
The group could not immediately identify all of the Gods. Victor therefore reproduced their appearances through Ice sculptures and later summoned [[Aphrodite]], [[Rhea]] and [[Alexios Alioth]] for assistance.[1][2]
Aphrodite immediately identified one of the unknown men as Taranis, the Celtic God of Thunder. Victor found this identification strange. When he had observed Taranis directly, the Divine sensation coming from the Celtic God reminded him much more strongly of an [[Azrael|Angel of Death]] than of Thunder Gods he had previously encountered.[2]
Aphrodite became equally suspicious. She proposed that Taranis had somehow acquired a Death Divinity, but pointed out that even this explanation did not completely resolve the mystery because the Death authority surrounding him was abnormally strong.[2]
The [[Celtic Pantheon]] possessed an [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]], but unlike the Seven Great Pantheons, its Hell was not large enough to participate in the full system governed by the Judges of the Abyss. Consequently, Taranis was not supposed to occupy the conventional position of an official Death God serving a Ruler of Hell. His possession of such a powerful Death Divinity therefore implied that something unusual had happened to him.[2]
While investigating the settlement, Victor's group discovered the true purpose of the operation. The Humans were being deliberately isolated and manipulated into creating Faith surrounding fabricated myths. [[New Dawn]] and the cooperating Dark Gods were attempting to artificially reproduce the conditions under which powerful Gods had historically been born from Human belief.[7]
Victor refused to allow the experiment to continue. After evacuating the Human inhabitants and stripping the facility of useful information and resources, he destroyed the entire island and pocket Dimension with an enormously destructive Dragon Breath attack.[8]
Taranis and [[Anubis]] arrived shortly after Victor's group departed.
Seeing that the entire project had vanished, Taranis immediately lost his temper and complained that all of his work had been destroyed. Anubis attempted to calm him before a third presence interrupted their conversation.[6]
The newcomer was [[Erebus]].
Taranis immediately became much more cautious. He shuddered beneath the Original Primordial God's attention, while the secretly observing Nyx Elderblood noticed something else unusual: Erebus was apparently supplying some form of his own Energy to the foreign Celtic God.[6]
The exact reason for this transfer was never explained. There is also no direct confirmation that Erebus was responsible for Taranis' anomalous Death Divinity, and the two facts should therefore not be treated as equivalent.
Erebus examined the remnants of the destroyed facility and identified the lingering Energy as the aftermath of a Dragon's Breath belonging to Victor. Taranis reacted with frustration, describing Victor as increasingly troublesome and deciding that their other research fields needed to be concealed more carefully.[6]
Rather than abandoning the artificial-God program, the conspirators immediately considered how to improve their surviving facilities. Taranis complained about the logistical problem of producing food for the Human populations used in their experiments and suggested acquiring an agriculture-related God. With the [[Greek Pantheon]] trapped in civil war, the group briefly discussed kidnapping [[Demeter]] until Erebus informed them that she had already joined Victor's faction.[6]
Taranis and Anubis subsequently departed to warn their other allies that Victor had discovered one of their hidden operations.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Taranis appeared again much later under radically different political circumstances.
During the third major [[Supernatural Gathering]], [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] exposed the alliance between Ra, [[Anubis]], Seth, the [[Elder Gods Pantheon]] and the conspirators responsible for targeting his family. Rather than declaring unrestricted war against the complete [[Egyptian Pantheon]], Victor announced a hunt specifically targeting the responsible Gods and their collaborators.[9]
[[Sucellus]], seeking to demonstrate the [[Celtic Pantheon]]'s cooperation with Victor's growing coalition, sent Taranis and [[Cernuno]] as his representatives.[3]
Taranis consequently found himself hunting some of the same supernatural figures with whom he had previously cooperated through [[New Dawn]].
The force assembled for the operation was unprecedented. It included representatives from multiple Pantheons and Heaven:
- [[Azrael]] and Ariel from the Biblical forces;
- Taranis and Cernuno from the Celtic Pantheon;
- Susanoo and [[Takemikazuchi]] from the [[Shinto Pantheon]];
- [[Thor]] from the [[Norse Pantheon]];
- and Victor's own Dragon faction.[3]
Thor immediately noticed the unusual concentration of Thunder-related Gods. Counting himself, the gathering contained Thor, Susanoo, Takemikazuchi and Taranis, prompting Thor to enthusiastically suggest a competition over who was the strongest.[3]
The atmosphere changed once Victor arrived accompanied by several recently evolved [[True Dragons]]. Taranis and the others became acutely aware of how drastically the balance of supernatural power had shifted.
[[Aphrodite]]'s transformed Draconic beauty then affected virtually every male Divine Being present without her consciously activating her Charm. Victor mockingly criticized them for being incapable of resisting even his wife's passive presence.[3]
To prove the point, Victor deliberately activated Aphrodite's Blessing through himself. Taranis, Thor, Susanoo and the others were immediately affected again. Embarrassed by how easily his senses had been compromised, Taranis declared that he would ask [[Epona]] to help train him against supernatural Charm.[3]
Taranis subsequently participated in the coalition's hunt under Victor's overall direction.

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
The alliance between [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and the [[Celtic Pantheon]] did not survive Victor's eventual decision to completely unify Earth.
After the foundation and expansion of the [[Elderblood Empire]], Victor abandoned the previous arrangement in which multiple independent Pantheons existed as political peers. The remaining supernatural factions were required to become part of the Empire or be eliminated.
[[Violet Elderblood Snow]] personally led the attack upon the Celtic Pantheon. She arrived with overwhelming Imperial military forces and confronted [[Sucellus]], who quickly understood that defeating her was effectively impossible.[4]
Sucellus attempted to invoke the previous treaty with Victor and subsequently prepared to surrender. Violet deliberately refused to allow an easy capitulation. She intended to break the Celtic Pantheon's remaining military power and independence before incorporating its surviving population into the Empire, preventing an intact rival faction from later becoming a source of rebellion.[4]
Sucellus therefore ordered the Celtic Gods to fight.
Although Taranis is not individually followed during this battle, he remained a member of the Celtic Pantheon and later continuity treats him as one of its deceased Gods.
By Chapter 1085, the independent Celtic Pantheon had been completely crushed. Its territory was devastated, numerous Gods had been killed, surviving Gods were being restrained and Victor's clones were consuming Divine Beings from the Celtic and smaller Pantheons that had refused annexation.[5]
Taranis' exact final battle and method of death remain unrevealed.

### Skills & Abilities
Taranis was a Major Celtic God whose original Divine authority was associated with Thunder. He later acquired an additional and unusually powerful Death Divinity, although its origin and full applications were never revealed.
- God Physiology: As an ancient natural-born God, Taranis possessed the supernatural physiology of a Divine Being, including [[Divine Energy]], a Divine Soul, immense longevity and physical capabilities far beyond ordinary Mortals.
- Divine Energy Manipulation: Taranis could wield Divine Energy through his Divinities and supernatural existence. He was considered powerful enough for [[Sucellus]] to select him as one of only two Celtic representatives sent to participate in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s international hunt.[3]
- Thunder Manipulation: Taranis' primary and publicly recognized Divinity was Thunder. [[Aphrodite]] explicitly identified him as the Celtic God of Thunder, and [[Thor]] later counted Taranis among the four Thunder- or Storm-related Gods gathered for Victor's hunt.[2][3]
- Electricity Manipulation: As the God of Thunder, Taranis possesses Divine authority over the electrical phenomena associated with his principal Concept. The novel has not shown a dedicated combat scene demonstrating the upper limits of this ability.
- Lightning Manipulation: Taranis' Thunder Divinity grants him Divine association with lightning, although no named lightning technique has presently been revealed.
- Death Manipulation: Taranis somehow possessed a powerful Divinity of Death. Victor's Draconic perception immediately noticed this aspect because Taranis felt more like an Angel of Death than a conventional Thunder God.[2]
- Death Divinity: Aphrodite concluded that the only reasonable explanation for Victor's observation was that Taranis had acquired a Death-related Divinity. She was particularly disturbed by how powerful it had become despite Taranis not occupying the normal role of a Celtic Death God.[2]
- Death Aura: Taranis' association with Death was powerful enough to be naturally perceptible to Victor even when Taranis was not actively performing a Death technique.[2]
- Divine Equipment: Taranis arrived for Victor's hunt fully equipped with Divine Gear alongside the other Pantheon representatives. The exact identity and abilities of his personal Divine Item were not revealed.[3]
- [[Immortality]]: Like other Gods, Taranis was not normally permanently destroyed through simple physical death. Permanent elimination of Gods required attacks capable of properly affecting their Divine existence, Soul or other fundamental components.
- Supernatural Senses: Taranis could perceive Divine signatures and supernatural Energy. He and [[Anubis]] investigated Victor's destroyed Bermuda facility shortly after the Dragon Breath attack.[6]
- Faith and Divine-System Knowledge: Taranis possessed practical knowledge of Faith and its role in creating Gods. He directly participated in [[New Dawn]]'s experiment intended to artificially reproduce the circumstances under which powerful Divine Beings emerge from Human mythology and belief.[7][6]

### Divinities

#### Thunder
Thunder is Taranis' original and publicly recognized Divinity.
[[Aphrodite]] immediately identified him through this role when shown [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s reconstruction of his appearance.[2] [[Thor]] likewise treated Taranis as one of the Thunder Gods gathered during Victor's hunt, alongside himself and [[Takemikazuchi]], with Susanoo occupying the related position of Primordial Storm God.[3]
Despite the importance of this authority, Taranis never received an extended individual battle in which the full scale of his Thunder Divinity was displayed.

#### Death
Taranis' second confirmed Divine authority is considerably more mysterious.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was confused when observing him because Taranis' supernatural presence did not resemble the Thunder Gods he knew. Instead, he gave Victor the impression of looking at an [[Azrael|Angel of Death]].[2]
[[Aphrodite]] explained that Taranis was not traditionally the [[Celtic Pantheon]]'s God of Death and should not naturally possess the level of Death authority Victor was sensing. She therefore concluded that he had acquired a Death Divinity through some unknown event.[2]

### The Death Divinity Mystery
Taranis' Death Divinity is one of the most unusual aspects of the character.
Under the supernatural system governing Earth, major Pantheons possessing sufficiently developed Hells are incorporated into the system overseen by the Judges of the Abyss. A Ruler emerges within such a Hell, and a corresponding God of Death assists with ensuring Souls move through the proper cycle.[2]
The [[Celtic Pantheon]] did not occupy the same position as the Seven Great Pantheons. Its Hell existed, but it was not sufficiently important to possess the same recognized Ruler-and-Death-God structure.[2]
Taranis therefore possessed an authority he was not naturally expected to hold.
Several suspicious circumstances surround the mystery:
- his original identity was that of a Thunder God;
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] nevertheless perceived a strong Death signature;
- [[Aphrodite]] explicitly considered the Death authority abnormal;
- Taranis worked closely with Dark Gods and [[New Dawn]];
- Nyx Elderblood observed [[Erebus]] transferring some form of Energy to Taranis;[6]
- and Taranis participated in experiments involving the artificial creation and development of Gods.
However, the novel has never confirmed that Erebus created or granted Taranis' Death Divinity. His Energy transfer and Taranis' Death authority may be related, but treating that connection as established fact would presently be speculation.

### New Dawn and the Artificial God Experiment
Taranis' most important contribution to [[New Dawn]] was associated with the organization's artificial-God research.
The experiment was built around the fact that powerful Gods could historically emerge when sufficient mythology, belief and Faith accumulated around an identity.[7]
Modern society made this process much harder because information could be verified too easily and widespread belief was fragmented. New Dawn therefore artificially recreated ancient social conditions.

### Relationships

#### Sucellus
[[Sucellus]] was Taranis' God-King and political superior within the [[Celtic Pantheon]].
Whatever independent relationships Taranis had developed through [[New Dawn]], he remained answerable to Sucellus. When Sucellus ordered the Celtic Pantheon to contribute forces to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s hunt, Taranis obeyed and joined [[Cernuno]] as the faction's representatives.[3]
Taranis presumably remained with the Celtic Pantheon until its destruction during the Elderblood conquest.

#### Anubis
[[Anubis]] was one of Taranis' closest known associates during the [[New Dawn]] conspiracy.
The two worked around the Bermuda Triangle artificial-God project and returned together after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] destroyed it. Anubis frequently played the calmer role in their interactions, telling the furious Taranis to control himself after discovering that their work had been annihilated.[6]
Their alliance later ended politically when Victor exposed Anubis as one of the principal conspirators involved in attacking his family. Taranis was subsequently ordered by [[Sucellus]] to participate in the hunt against the faction with which Anubis was associated.[3]

#### Erebus
[[Erebus]] was another powerful associate connected to the conspiracy.
Taranis clearly feared and respected the Original Primordial God. When Erebus suddenly spoke beside him in Chapter 706, Taranis froze and visibly shuddered beneath his attention.[6]
Nyx also observed that Erebus was supplying Energy to Taranis for reasons she did not understand.[6]
The precise nature of their arrangement remains unknown.

#### Victor Elderblood
Taranis initially regarded [[Victor Elderblood]] as a severe obstacle to [[New Dawn]]'s operations.
After discovering that Victor had destroyed the Bermuda Triangle facility, Taranis complained that the Demon King was becoming increasingly troublesome and concluded that their surviving research locations needed greater secrecy.[6]
He and his associates even considered kidnapping [[Demeter]] before learning that she had entered Victor's protection, causing Taranis to complain once again that Victor continuously interfered with their plans.[6]
Their relationship later changed from covert enemies to temporary allies. [[Sucellus]] sent Taranis to participate in Victor's hunt, forcing him to operate under the same coalition led by the man who had destroyed his research.[3]
Victor was not particularly impressed by him. After Taranis and the other Gods were affected by [[Aphrodite]]'s passive beauty, Victor openly mocked their lack of resistance and then deliberately demonstrated the problem by using Aphrodite's Charm himself.[3]

#### Cernuno
[[Cernuno]] accompanied Taranis as the [[Celtic Pantheon]]'s second representative during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s hunt.
While Taranis was selected for his position as a powerful Thunder God, Cernuno was the Celtic God of Animals and complained about being reduced from a God to a "hunter".[3]
Both remained associated with the Celtic Pantheon until its eventual conquest and are later treated as deceased.

#### Epona
Taranis knew [[Epona]] well enough to consider asking her for specialized assistance after being humiliated by [[Aphrodite]]'s supernatural beauty.
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] demonstrated how easily Charm could incapacitate the gathered Gods, Taranis resolved to seek Epona's help developing resistance to such powers.[3]
The exact nature of their personal relationship was not explained.

### Death
Taranis' individual death is not directly shown.
During the creation of the [[Elderblood Empire]], [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] ordered the remaining independent supernatural civilizations of Earth to be absorbed under one authority.
The [[Celtic Pantheon]] became one of the factions targeted for complete annexation. Violet personally led the Imperial offensive and deliberately forced the Celtic Gods into battle after [[Sucellus]] attempted to surrender.[4]
By Chapter 1085, the conquest had already ended. The Celtic Pantheon's territory was devastated, Gods had been killed, survivors were in chains and Victor's clones were consuming Divine Beings belonging to factions that had refused submission.[5]
Later continuity treats Taranis as deceased alongside Sucellus, [[Cernuno]] and other members of the former Celtic Pantheon.
Because the novel never isolates Taranis during the final battle, it is unknown whether he was:
- killed directly by Violet;
- killed by another Dragon Goddess;
- killed by Imperial soldiers;
- or consumed by one of Victor's clones.
