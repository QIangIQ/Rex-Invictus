### Synopsis

#### Those Who Rule Reality
During approximately the 170,000th year of the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s accelerated development, Dagda was mentioned as the Divine ancestor of [[Nikolai Francis Nichev]], a [[Demigod]] discovered operating within the Sagittarius Dwarf Elliptical Galaxy, approximately 80,000 light-years from [[Yggdrasil]].[1]
Nikolai was identified as one of Dagda's numerous descendants, with the Celtic God described as occupying a position comparable to a great-great-grandfather in his ancestry. Nikolai's father, [[Ivankov Nichev]], belonged to Dagda's bloodline, while his mother, [[Juliette Dubois]], was an Imperial citizen born on Yggdrasil. This established that Nikolai inherited his Divine ancestry through his paternal family.[1]
Despite his Divine heritage, Nikolai became involved with a foreign God originating from the higher regions of the Cosmos. Acting under this entity's influence, he participated in efforts to investigate [[Victor Elderblood]]'s Bloodline, reproduce its characteristics and uncover the origin of the Emperor's extraordinary existence. His activities eventually drew the attention of War, Victor's most powerful Herald, who personally intervened to apprehend him.[1]
Dagda was not shown participating in Nikolai's conspiracy. There is no confirmation that he knew about his descendant's activities, maintained contact with the foreign God or faced any consequences following the investigation.[1]

### Skills & Abilities
As a member of the Divine race, Dagda possesses a supernatural existence associated with the [[Celtic Pantheon]]. However, his specific Divinities, Divine Authorities, combat abilities and relative standing within the Pantheon have not been established in the available novel material.

#### Divine Heritage
- Divine Bloodline: Dagda possesses a lineage extending across multiple generations, with numerous descendants inheriting his Divine ancestry. [[Ivankov Nichev]] and [[Nikolai Francis Nichev]] are confirmed members of this lineage.[1]
- [[Demigod]] Descendants: Nikolai's classification as a Demigod establishes that Dagda's Divine ancestry remains present within at least one distant descendant. The precise abilities inherited through this bloodline have not been revealed.[1]

### Trivia
- Dagda originates from [[Celtic Pantheon|Irish mythology]], where he is a prominent deity associated with the Tuatha Dé Danann. His mythological attributes should not be mistaken for abilities confirmed in the novel.
- He is described as having numerous descendants rather than only a single known family line.[1]
- [[Nikolai Francis Nichev]] is not Dagda's direct son. The novel compares their generational separation to that of a great-great-grandfather and his descendant.[1]
- Dagda's connection to the [[Elderblood Empire]] is presently established through his descendants. His own political allegiance and current whereabouts remain unconfirmed.
- His first confirmed mention in the available chapter sources occurs in Chapter 1158.[1]
