#### Debut
Chapter 577
I'm Half Faerie, Half Goddess. I was born able to see The Truth of The World. I may not interact with Souls or see them as deeply as the Gods of Death or the Vampire Progenitors, but I can still see them... And that damn old man knew a lot about Souls. He told me a few things.
—Viviane to [[Victor Elderblood]], Chapter 718
Viviane, better known as the Lady of the Lake, is the Queen and guardian of Earth's surviving Fairies. She is a natural hybrid born from a Fairy and a God, making her both a Fairy and a [[Demigod]]. She is an ancient supernatural being whose history predates modern supernatural society and is directly connected to the Arthurian era, most famously as the Lady of the Lake who entrusted the Holy Sword [[Excalibur]] to Arthur Pendragon.[1]
Despite her age and power, Viviane spent a considerable period almost completely isolated from the supernatural world. The Fairy race had been driven close to extinction by the greed of Mortals, Gods and supernatural beings who sought Fairies for their supernatural gifts, blessings and association with [[Natural Energy]]. Viviane consequently concealed the survivors within a hidden territory in England and withdrew from wider society. The isolation protected her people for centuries, but it also left Viviane unaware of enormous changes in the world, including the rise of Victor Elderblood as the second Vampire Progenitor and the death of her old friend [[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]].[2]
During the Demonic War, Natashia, Sasha and [[Tatsuya Rider]] located Viviane and convinced her that secrecy alone could no longer guarantee the survival of the Fairies. Learning that Victor's household included Roxanne Elderblood, a genuine World Tree, fundamentally changed Viviane's situation. The surviving Fairies were relocated into an environment sustained by Roxanne, allowing their population and connection to Nature to begin recovering. Viviane subsequently became associated with Victor's growing faction, assisted Roxanne and [[Demeter]] in managing Nature, enchanted weapons for the [[Blood God Religion]], helped Victor obtain passage through Earth's World Tree toward the Norse territories and eventually entered Victor's inner world after his evolution into the Blood Dragon Primogenitor.[3][4][5]

### Physical Appearance
Viviane possesses the appearance of a remarkably young woman despite having lived for thousands of years. She is approximately 165 cm tall, with pale skin, long black hair and dark-blue eyes whose color was compared to the lake surrounding her when she was first introduced. Her body is slender, lean and graceful, emphasizing youthful and ethereal beauty rather than the more mature physical proportions possessed by many ancient supernatural women. Her appearance is repeatedly associated with purity and the supernatural beauty characteristic of the Fairy race.[1][2]
During her introduction, Viviane wore a simple white dress which complemented the exceptionally pure atmosphere surrounding her. Her Fairy characteristics are not always externally prominent, but when she openly manifests her nature she possesses two pairs of transparent Fairy wings. The manifestation of these wings is accompanied by an intense aura of purity perceptible to other supernatural beings. She is also capable of naturally floating above the ground rather than relying exclusively upon ordinary physical movement.[2][3]

### Personality
Viviane is peaceful, compassionate and intensely protective of the surviving Fairies. Her mentality is heavily influenced by the naturally pure disposition of her race: she loves Nature, dislikes corruption and reacts strongly toward supernatural beings whose existence feels impure to her senses. This initially causes her to respond negatively toward Vampires, whose supernatural nature she considers corrupted, although she is sufficiently intelligent and reasonable to judge individuals separately once she understands their intentions. Her distrust of Humans is similarly rooted in experience rather than simple racial hatred, as Fairies had repeatedly been hunted or exploited by Mortals seeking immortality, blessings and supernatural benefits.[1][2]
Her responsibility as Queen takes precedence over her personal comfort. Viviane remained hidden for centuries because she believed isolation represented the safest option for an endangered species, but once the Demonic War proved that even hidden territories could eventually be discovered, she accepted abandoning the sanctuary rather than gambling the existence of her people on pride or nostalgia. The same mentality appeared when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] proposed using his [[Natural Energy]] to accelerate the recovery of the Fairy population. Although Viviane initially misunderstood his wording and became deeply embarrassed, she accepted his assistance once she understood the actual proposal because restoring her race mattered more than her personal discomfort.[2][4]
Viviane's reclusiveness was not motivated entirely by political necessity. Her exceptional longevity forced her to repeatedly witness the deaths of people she cared about, and she eventually began avoiding new attachments partly because she no longer wanted to experience the pain of losing them. Her friendship with Carmila demonstrates the cost of that decision. Viviane had isolated herself so completely that she remained unaware of Carmila's death until Natashia personally informed her centuries later, deeply affecting Viviane and showing that withdrawing from the world had protected her from some dangers while creating losses of its own.[2]
Despite her generally composed demeanor, Viviane retains an unexpectedly innocent side regarding romance and vulgar subjects. Natashia's shameless behavior can easily embarrass her, and Victor's appearance and enormous concentration of Natural Energy repeatedly disrupt her usual calmness. She is nevertheless difficult to deceive in serious situations because her supernatural perception allows her to examine intent, emotional truth and impurity rather than relying only upon spoken words. Viviane particularly dislikes deliberate dishonesty when she already knows that something is being concealed from her.[2][4]

### Synopsis

#### Early Life and the Arthurian Era
Viviane is ancient enough to have personally participated in events that later became Arthurian legend. She became known as the Lady of the Lake and was the woman responsible for giving the Holy Sword [[Excalibur]] to the young Arthur Pendragon. She also personally knew [[Merlin]], from whom she learned part of her theoretical knowledge concerning Souls. Although Viviane acknowledged Merlin's extraordinary knowledge, her later description of him as a "damn old man" indicates that she remembered him with considerable irritation rather than unquestioning admiration.[1][6]
Arthur's legend ultimately created severe consequences for Viviane's people. As stories concerning the Lady of the Lake and supernatural weapons spread, other supernatural beings increasingly searched for the Fairies. Their ability to bless objects with [[Holy Energy]] made them valuable, while the Fairies themselves were coveted for their unusual racial properties. These repeated attempts to exploit her race became one of the major reasons Viviane eventually chose complete isolation.[4]
At some point during her long life, Viviane also became a close friend of [[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]]. Their friendship was unusually personal considering Carmila's position and temperament. Carmila trusted Viviane enough to privately complain about frustrations and burdens she concealed even from her own family, making Viviane one of the comparatively few people outside Clan Fulger who saw the ancient Countess without the expectations attached to her public role.[2]

#### Isolation of the Fairies
The Fairy population gradually declined until Viviane became responsible for protecting one of the last surviving communities on Earth. Fairies possess a profound dependence upon Nature and [[World Trees]], with most new Fairies being produced through concentrations of [[Natural Energy]] rather than ordinary biological reproduction. The deterioration of their circumstances therefore threatened more than their political security: without an adequate source of Natural Energy, recovering the population naturally would require an enormous amount of time.[4]
Viviane eventually withdrew with the survivors into a concealed Fairy Kingdom hidden in England. The entrance was protected by supernatural concealment powerful enough that even [[Tatsuya Rider]], an accomplished user of illusion-based abilities, could not identify the true path through the apparently ordinary forest surrounding the realm. The Fairies controlled access to the hidden territory and could guide permitted visitors through defenses that otherwise prevented outsiders from reaching Viviane's sanctuary.[1]
The isolation succeeded so completely that Viviane lost contact with much of supernatural society. Major geopolitical changes, wars and the emergence of new powers occurred without her knowledge. She did not know that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had become the second Vampire Progenitor and, more painfully, had never learned that Carmila had died. Her longevity only intensified the emotional consequences, as Viviane's combined Fairy and Divine nature gives her a lifespan described as almost limitless so long as she is not killed and the natural environment sustaining her existence remains viable.[2]

#### Demonic War
During the Demonic invasion of Earth, Natashia, Sasha and Tatsuya traveled through England in search of the remaining Fairies. Their journey brought them to Viviane's hidden kingdom, where the supernatural concealment prevented Tatsuya from independently finding the correct route. Once permitted through, the visitors were guided deeper into the realm until they discovered Viviane meditating above a large lake.[1]
Viviane immediately recognized that her visitors were Vampires and reacted negatively to the corruption she naturally perceived from their race. Her attention soon shifted toward something considerably stranger: Natashia and Sasha carried enormous quantities of unfamiliar vitality. Viviane could tell that neither woman was pregnant despite the unusual Life Energy she perceived inside them, initially leaving her confused regarding its origin. She eventually learned that the Energy came from their intimacy with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], whose abnormal nature and connection with Life had left traces even Viviane could immediately detect.[1]
The conversation exposed the extreme extent of Viviane's isolation. She had never heard that another Vampire Progenitor had appeared and required Natashia to explain much of what had happened throughout supernatural society. Among the revelations was Carmila's death. Viviane was visibly saddened when she realized that one of her oldest friends had died while Viviane remained hidden and unaware, reinforcing the personal cost of the isolation she had adopted to protect herself and her people.[2]
Natashia then explained the scale of [[Diablo]]'s Demonic War. Viviane quickly recognized that the invasion differed from an ordinary supernatural conflict: coordinated forces of Hell were attacking multiple supernatural civilizations, meaning remaining hidden could no longer be considered an absolute defense. Even if reaching the Fairy Kingdom was exceptionally difficult, Viviane refused to risk her entire species on the assumption that Diablo or another powerful faction would never discover a method of entering it.[2]
Natashia offered the Fairies a safer home beneath the protection of her allies. Viviane's ability to perceive lies and intent immediately revealed that Natashia had additional motivations beyond simple humanitarian concern, eventually forcing the Vampire to admit that she also hoped to introduce Viviane to Victor. The admission embarrassed Viviane considerably, but it did not invalidate the genuine offer of protection.[2]
The decisive factor was [[Roxanne Elderblood]]. Natashia revealed that Victor was connected to an actual World Tree and produced one of Roxanne's leaves. Despite the Energy around the leaf being restrained, Viviane recognized its nature once exposed. The reaction extended beyond Viviane herself: large numbers of Fairies gathered around the lake in response to the World Tree's Energy, experiencing something resembling the comfort of returning home. A genuine World Tree represented both sanctuary and the [[Natural Energy]] required for the race to recover, so Viviane ultimately agreed to relocate the Fairies.[2]

#### King Among Kings
By the time [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] personally visited Viviane, the Fairies had already moved into a forest created and sustained by Roxanne. Their new environment was vastly superior to the declining sanctuary in which they had previously survived, and proximity to a World Tree allowed the species to begin recovering. Viviane became directly involved in maintaining the area alongside Roxanne and [[Demeter]], whose respective powers over Nature complemented one another.[3]
Viviane also contributed materially to Victor's growing civilization. She produced enchanted weapons carrying supernatural properties associated with Nature and purity, including blessed daggers intended to be distributed as rewards through the [[Blood God Religion]]. While enchanting objects is connected to the abilities of the Fairy race, Viviane was specifically noted as being capable of producing the level of enchantment Victor's faction required.[3]
Her first impression of Victor was dominated by what she could perceive rather than his political titles. Through the Truth of the World, Viviane sensed the enormous [[Natural Energy]] contained within him. Victor's intimate connection to Roxanne made his presence resemble standing beside a World Tree, yet the amount of Energy she perceived from him was so extreme that the sensation appeared even greater than what she normally experienced around Roxanne. This immediately gave Viviane significant respect for Victor despite his Vampiric nature.[3]
Viviane later participated in a discussion concerning transformations and the Soul. She explained that being half Fairy and half Goddess caused her to be born capable of seeing the Truth of the World. This perception allowed her to observe Souls, although she explicitly acknowledged that she could not interact with or understand them as deeply as Gods of Death or Vampire Progenitors. [[Merlin]] had also taught her portions of what she knew regarding Souls, giving Viviane both innate perception and accumulated theoretical knowledge.[6]
Victor subsequently asked about the long-term condition of the Fairies. Viviane explained that ordinary reproduction was possible, but most Fairies were produced naturally from the Natural Energy generated by a World Tree. Even after relocating to Roxanne's forest, waiting for an entirely new generation through ordinary conditions could require decades. Victor therefore proposed supplying the required Natural Energy himself. Viviane initially interpreted his words in a considerably more intimate way and became mortified, but once the misunderstanding was resolved she agreed to accept his help.[4]
The discussion also revealed more of the Fairy race's history. Viviane referred to the structure that had supported her people as the Tree of Creation. Victor identified it as an original branch originating from Earth's World Tree whose power had diminished over time before being revitalized by Roxanne's influence. When Viviane discovered that Victor knew the location of Roxanne's true body, she immediately asked whether the Fairies could live beside it. Victor refused, not because he distrusted Viviane, but because every additional person aware of Roxanne's location increased the possibility that hostile Gods or other supernatural factions could discover and attempt to claim the World Tree.[4]
Viviane accepted Victor's reasoning because she had already experienced exactly that type of greed. Arthur's legend had once attracted supernatural beings seeking Fairies and Holy blessings, while a mature World Tree represented a prize important enough to provoke conflicts between entire factions. Their conversation consequently strengthened the political and personal understanding between Viviane and Victor.[4]
Victor's visit also had a strategic purpose. Viviane retained a connection to Earth's World Tree and knew [[Ratatosk]], allowing her to arrange passage through the World Tree's branches toward the territories of the [[Norse Pantheon]]. She agreed to contact Ratatosk so that Victor could be escorted toward Helheim. Viviane specifically avoided simply appealing directly to the World Tree because [[Odin]] monitored the Norse network; if he discovered the surviving Fairies, she feared that he could attempt to seize them while claiming that he was merely placing them under his protection.[4]
Viviane later participated in the climactic conflict surrounding the [[Greek Underworld]]. She arrived as part of the powerful group assembled beneath [[Jeanne Elderblood]], alongside figures such as Agnes, Natashia, Mizuki, Haruna, Meya, Eleonor and Rose. Her presence among Victor's elite allies demonstrated how completely her position had changed since the period in which she attempted to keep the Fairies separated from every major supernatural power.[5]
After Victor completed his evolution into the Blood Dragon Primogenitor, he noticed Viviane among the arriving women and immediately appeared before her. Victor kissed her before transferring her into his vastly expanded inner Soul world, openly moving their relationship beyond ordinary diplomacy. Viviane encountered Roxanne inside that internal Reality and was quickly given a task suited to her nature: the forest contained enormous quantities of Negative Energy, and Viviane would assist Roxanne in purifying and regulating the environment so ordinary green vegetation and natural Life could develop within it. Roxanne also intended for the remaining Fairies eventually to be moved into the inner world, providing them with an environment under the direct protection of Victor and a World Tree.[5]

### Skills & Abilities
Viviane is an ancient Fairy-[[Demigod]] whose greatest strengths are not presented primarily through conventional destructive combat. Her abilities instead emphasize supernatural perception, purification, Nature, [[World Trees]], spiritual sensitivity, concealment and enchantment. Her age, Divine heritage and status as Queen place her far beyond an ordinary Fairy, while her usefulness to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction derives heavily from abilities that even many physically stronger beings do not possess.
- Fairy-Demigod Physiology: Viviane is the natural child of a Fairy and a God, possessing the spiritual characteristics of both races. Divine-racial hybrids do not suffer the same ordinary biological incompatibilities found in many mortal cross-species hybrids, allowing her Fairy and Divine aspects to coexist as a stable supernatural existence.[1]
- Near-Immortal Longevity: Her combined Fairy and Divine heritage grants Viviane an almost limitless natural lifespan. She has already lived for thousands of years while retaining a youthful appearance. Her lifespan can nevertheless end if she is killed, and the natural existence supporting Fairies remains important to their continued survival.[2]
- Enhanced Physical Abilities: As an ancient supernatural hybrid, Viviane possesses physical capabilities enormously beyond ordinary Humans even though the story emphasizes her spiritual and supportive abilities rather than raw physical combat.[1]
- Flight and Levitation: Viviane can naturally float above the ground and manifest two pairs of transparent Fairy wings, allowing aerial movement.[2][3]
- Supernatural Purity: Viviane possesses an exceptionally pure supernatural nature. When her Fairy characteristics are manifested more strongly, surrounding beings can directly perceive the purity radiating from her existence.[2]
- Truth of the World: Because of her Fairy-Demigod nature, Viviane was born able to perceive what she calls the Truth of the World. Rather than functioning as ordinary eyesight, this perception allows her to observe aspects of supernatural existence normally hidden from conventional senses. Viviane explicitly acknowledges that the amount of "truth" visible depends upon the level and nature of the observer; beings with superior perception, such as high-level Divine entities, Vampire Progenitors and powerful Dragons, can perceive structures beyond her own current depth.[6][4]
- Soul Perception: Viviane can directly see Souls and examine aspects of their condition and structure. Her perception does not reach the depth of Gods specializing in Death or Vampire Progenitors, but it is sufficiently advanced for her to understand connections between a Being's physical form and Soul.[6]
- Life-Force Perception: She can perceive abnormal concentrations of vitality and Life Energy. Viviane immediately detected the immense vitality carried by Natashia and Sasha while simultaneously determining that neither woman was pregnant.[1]
- [[Natural Energy]] Perception: Viviane is extraordinarily sensitive to Natural Energy and can recognize the distinctive Energy of a World Tree. Roxanne's leaf immediately provoked a powerful response from Viviane and the surrounding Fairies once its nature was revealed.[2]
- Energy Analysis: Her senses allow her to distinguish supernatural qualities rather than merely detect raw power. Upon meeting Victor, Viviane recognized the extraordinary quantity and nature of the Natural Energy connected to him through Roxanne.[3]
- Impurity Perception: As a Fairy, Viviane possesses extreme sensitivity toward corruption, unnatural spiritual stains and impure supernatural existences. This is one reason she initially found the nature of Vampires unpleasant.[1][2]
- Intent Perception: Viviane can look beyond spoken statements and perceive malicious or dishonest intent within another person's heart, making deliberate deception exceptionally difficult around her.[2]
- Lie Detection: She recognized that Natashia's explanation for visiting contained concealed motives despite most of what Natashia said being technically true, eventually forcing Natashia to reveal the remaining reason behind her visit.[2]
- Purification: Viviane can use her Fairy powers to identify and purify negative supernatural influences affecting another Being. She tested Natashia for external interference after hearing about Victor and confirmed that Natashia's feelings were genuinely her own rather than the result of supernatural manipulation.[2]
- Mental Influence Purification: Her purification was considered applicable to unnatural influences capable of altering thought or affection, including effects comparable in principle to supernatural Charm.[2]
- Corruption Cleansing: Her Fairy nature is naturally opposed to spiritual impurity, allowing her powers to be used in environments or situations requiring the removal of harmful supernatural contamination.[2][5]
- Environmental Purification: Roxanne specifically assigned Viviane to help reduce the excessive Negative Energy saturating the red forest inside Victor's inner world so conventional vegetation and Life could begin developing properly.[5]
- Nature Manipulation: Viviane possesses an exceptional affinity with Nature characteristic of high-level Fairies. Her presence supports an abundant and healthy natural environment, making her abilities particularly compatible with Roxanne and [[Demeter]].[3]
- Natural Environment Maintenance: Viviane can assist in maintaining and restoring forests and other living environments, a role she performed after the Fairies settled within Roxanne's territory.[3]
- Natural Energy Affinity: Natural Energy is fundamental to Fairy existence. Viviane can sense, interact with and benefit from enormous concentrations of it, particularly those produced by World Trees.[2][4]
- Ecological Restoration: Her Nature and purification abilities allow her to participate in converting environments saturated by hostile Energy into regions capable of supporting conventional Life.[5]
- Fairy Enchantment: Fairies possess the ability to place supernatural blessings upon objects, and Viviane is exceptionally proficient in this field. During her work for Victor's faction she was the Fairy specifically capable of producing the quality of enchanted weaponry required by Roxanne.[3]
- [[Holy Energy]] Blessing: Viviane can imbue objects with Holy properties. The ability of Fairies to bless objects with Holy Energy was one of the principal reasons outsiders historically hunted for their hidden settlements.[4]
- Weapon Enhancement: She created enchanted and blessed weapons, including daggers intended to become rewards for higher-ranking members of the [[Blood God Religion]].[3]
- Natural Property Imbuement: Her enchantments can carry properties associated with the purity and Nature affinity of the Fairy race rather than functioning as purely conventional magical reinforcement.[3]
- Fairy Realm Concealment: The Fairy Kingdom under Viviane's protection was hidden through extremely powerful supernatural concealment. Even Tatsuya, whose abilities include highly advanced illusions, could not identify the real entrance through the false environment surrounding it.[1]
- Illusion and Perception Interference: The defensive concealment surrounding the Fairy settlement could alter what intruders perceived and prevent experienced supernatural observers from recognizing the correct path.[1]
- Restricted Access: The Fairies could determine who was permitted to proceed through the hidden territory, guiding accepted visitors while keeping unauthorized outsiders away from Viviane and the surviving population.[1]
- Hidden-Realm Security: Viviane considered the sanctuary difficult enough to locate that even extraordinarily powerful beings would normally require unusual means or information to discover it, although the Demonic War eventually convinced her that no concealment should be considered absolutely inviolable.[2]
- World Tree Connection: Viviane possesses a deep racial and spiritual connection with Earth's World Tree. Fairies regard World Trees with an almost maternal reverence because their Natural Energy sustains the race and enables the birth of new Fairies.[1][4]
- World Tree Recognition: Viviane can identify the presence and Energy of a genuine World Tree even when only a small portion of that Being, such as one of Roxanne's leaves, is presented to her.[2]
- World Tree Nourishment: Proximity to a World Tree provides the Natural Energy necessary for Fairies to thrive and reproduce efficiently. Relocating beneath Roxanne therefore solved one of the most serious long-term threats facing Viviane's people.[2][4]
- World Tree Branch Passage: Viviane's connection to Earth's World Tree and her relationship with [[Ratatosk]] allow her to arrange travel through its branches into territories linked to the [[Norse Pantheon]], including routes toward Helheim.[4]
- Tree of Creation Knowledge: Viviane understood the ancient World Tree branch used by her people as the Tree of Creation. The structure had gradually lost power before Roxanne's Energy helped replenish it.[4]
- Fairy Generation Knowledge: As Queen, Viviane possesses extensive knowledge regarding the creation and reproduction of Fairies. Although Fairies are capable of conventional reproduction, most are naturally born from the Natural Energy produced around a World Tree.[4]
- Soul Knowledge: In addition to her innate Soul perception, Viviane has accumulated theoretical knowledge regarding Souls over her long life, including information learned from [[Merlin]]. This allows her to participate meaningfully in discussions involving the relationship between a Soul, physical transformation and supernatural species.[6]
- Ancient Supernatural Knowledge: Viviane's age gives her personal knowledge of beings and events dating back to the Arthurian period. Her isolation left her uninformed about many modern political developments, but it did not erase her extensive knowledge of older supernatural systems, Fairies, World Trees and Soul-related phenomena.[1][6][4]
- Leadership: Viviane successfully preserved an endangered supernatural race for centuries while avoiding the attention of numerous factions that wanted to exploit it. She is willing to abandon territory, traditions or personal comfort when necessary for the survival of her people.[2]

### Limitations
- Relative Truth Perception: Seeing the Truth of the World does not make Viviane omniscient. She explicitly acknowledges that the depth of supernatural information visible to an observer depends upon their level and nature, with specialized Gods, Vampire Progenitors and powerful Dragons capable of perceiving more than she can.[6][4]
- World Tree Dependence of the Fairy Race: Viviane herself is extraordinarily long-lived, but the prosperity and reproduction of her species are strongly linked to environments rich in [[Natural Energy]], particularly those sustained by a World Tree. Without sufficient Natural Energy, rebuilding the Fairy population is extremely slow.[4]
- Purity Sensitivity: Viviane's extreme sensitivity to supernatural impurity can cause immediate aversion toward races or Beings she perceives as corrupted before she has personally evaluated their character.[1]
- Political Exposure Through [[Yggdrasil]]: Viviane's ability to use Earth's World Tree as a route is not without risk. The Norse network was monitored closely enough that openly relying upon the Tree could reveal the surviving Fairies to [[Odin]], something Viviane specifically wished to avoid.[4]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
Viviane first learned about [[Victor Elderblood]] through Natashia and Sasha. She was initially astonished that a second Vampire Progenitor had emerged without her knowledge, but her curiosity increased considerably when Natashia revealed that Victor was connected to a genuine World Tree. His existence also explained the extraordinary vitality Viviane perceived lingering around Natashia and Sasha.[1][2]
Their first personal meeting immediately altered Viviane's impression of him. Through her supernatural perception she sensed an enormous concentration of [[Natural Energy]] inside Victor and recognized the profound connection between him and Roxanne. His presence resembled the sensation Fairies experienced near a World Tree, except the amount of Energy Viviane detected from Victor was even more overwhelming. Victor subsequently involved himself directly in the restoration of the Fairies, offering enough Natural Energy to accelerate a recovery that would otherwise require decades.[3][4]
Viviane's normally composed personality becomes noticeably more vulnerable around Victor. She is easily embarrassed by discussions implying intimacy with him and repeatedly reacts to his appearance and supernatural presence. Victor nevertheless treats her value as extending far beyond attraction: he trusted her with information concerning Roxanne, relied upon her World Tree connection to reach Norse territory and later placed her within his personal inner world where her abilities could contribute directly to the ecosystem.[4][5]
Their relationship became explicitly romantic during the [[Greek Underworld]] conflict. Immediately after becoming the Blood Dragon Primogenitor, Victor approached Viviane and kissed her before transferring her into his inner world. Viviane has consequently moved beyond the position of a purely political ally, although she has not been explicitly established as one of Victor's wives in the material surrounding her latest direct involvement.[5]

#### Roxanne Elderblood
Viviane possesses profound reverence for [[Roxanne Elderblood]] because Roxanne is a genuine World Tree, an existence fundamentally important to the Fairy race. A single leaf carrying Roxanne's Energy caused Viviane and thousands of Fairies to react immediately, with the sensation evoking the comfort and familiarity Fairies associate with returning to their natural origin.[2]
After relocating, Viviane lived within a forest created by Roxanne and worked with her to maintain the surrounding ecosystem. Their abilities are exceptionally compatible: Roxanne supplies enormous quantities of [[Natural Energy]] and supports Life at a fundamental level, while Viviane's Fairy nature allows her to cultivate, purify and work within that environment. Roxanne later welcomed Viviane into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s inner world and personally assigned her the task of helping cleanse excessive Negative Energy from the forest. She also intended for the remaining Fairies eventually to join Viviane there.[3][5]

#### Carmila Fulger Elderblood
[[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]] was one of Viviane's oldest and closest friends. Carmila trusted Viviane sufficiently to complain openly about responsibilities and frustrations she normally concealed behind the dignified image expected from the Fulger matriarch. Natashia was initially jealous upon learning that her mother had shown Viviane a side of herself that she rarely revealed even to her daughters, but came to understand that Carmila had needed a friend outside her family's political structure.[2]
Viviane's isolation meant she never learned of Carmila's death when it occurred. Natashia personally delivered the news centuries later, deeply saddening Viviane and forcing her to confront one of the consequences of withdrawing from everyone she once knew.[2]

#### Annasthashia Elderblood Fulger
Viviane knew of Natashia through her connection to Carmila but had become sufficiently detached from supernatural society that their meeting during the Demonic War also functioned as a reunion between Viviane and the modern Fulger family. Natashia became the principal person responsible for explaining what Viviane had missed during her isolation, including Carmila's death, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s emergence and the scale of [[Diablo]]'s war.[1][2]
Their conversation also demonstrates the limits of deception around Viviane. Natashia genuinely wanted to protect the Fairies, but she additionally wanted Viviane to meet Victor. Viviane perceived that the explanation was incomplete and continued questioning her until Natashia admitted the secondary motive.[2]

#### Sasha Elderblood Fulger
[[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] accompanied Natashia and Tatsuya during the expedition that rediscovered Viviane. Like Natashia, Sasha carried enough of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s extraordinary vitality for Viviane to detect it immediately, contributing to Viviane's first awareness that an abnormal new Progenitor had appeared in the outside world.[1]

#### Yggdrasil
Viviane and the Fairies possess a profound racial connection to the World Tree associated with Earth and the Norse territories, which Viviane regards with maternal reverence as Mother [[Yggdrasil]]. The sensations generated by powerful [[Natural Energy]] remind Fairies instinctively of the safety and nourishment associated with a World Tree.[1]
Despite this reverence, Viviane avoids carelessly using her connection to Yggdrasil. [[Odin]]'s influence over the Norse World Tree network created the risk that attracting attention to herself could expose the surviving Fairies. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] requested passage toward Helheim, Viviane therefore preferred arranging the journey through [[Ratatosk]] rather than openly attracting Odin's attention.[4]

#### Ratatosk
Viviane possesses enough of a relationship with [[Ratatosk]] to contact him when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] required passage through Earth's World Tree. She agreed to ask Ratatosk to guide Victor through its branches and toward Helheim, providing Victor with a route into Norse territory that did not require him to depend directly upon [[Odin]].[4]

#### Merlin
Viviane personally knew [[Merlin]] during the distant past and learned part of her knowledge concerning Souls from him. She acknowledges that Merlin knew an extraordinary amount about the subject, although the irritated way she remembers him indicates that their relationship was considerably less reverential than the legendary reputation surrounding the ancient magician might suggest.[6]

#### Arthur Pendragon
Viviane is the Lady of the Lake who gave the Holy Sword [[Excalibur]] to Arthur Pendragon when he was young, directly connecting her to one of the most famous supernatural legends originating from Britain. The fame created by Arthur's legend later became dangerous for the Fairies because supernatural beings began searching for Viviane's hidden home, either seeking the Fairies themselves or hoping to exploit their ability to bless objects with [[Holy Energy]].[1][4]
