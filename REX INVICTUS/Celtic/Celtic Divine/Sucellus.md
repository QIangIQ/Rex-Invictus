### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 863
The Eras have changed, The Era of Men has been left behind; this is The Era of Supernatural Beings, and we should not remain silent only for the mistakes of the past to be repeated.
—Sucellus during the [[Supernatural Gathering]], Chapter 872
Sucellus was the God-King of the [[Celtic Pantheon]] and the deity recognized by the wider supernatural community as the Supreme God of the Celts. He represented the Celtic Gods during the later Supernatural Gatherings and served as their principal political authority during the rapid transformation of Earth's supernatural order following the destruction of the [[Greek Pantheon]].[1]
Although a God-King, Sucellus did not rule a faction comparable in geopolitical strength to Earth's former Seven Great Pantheons. The Celtic Pantheon was explicitly separate from the Seven Great Pantheons, and by the time of the auction for the conquered [[Norse Pantheon]], Sucellus privately regarded his own Pantheon as the weakest of the major factions present. This weakness heavily influenced his political behavior: rather than attempting to compete directly with stronger Gods, he relied on caution, diplomacy, concealed resources and advantageous alliances.[2][3]
Sucellus initially developed a mutually beneficial relationship with [[Victor Elderblood]]. He supported Victor during the conflict against the [[Egyptian Pantheon]], [[New Dawn]] and the [[Elder Gods Pantheon]], sent Celtic Gods to participate in Victor's hunt, and supported Victor's proposed restructuring of Earth into a civilization centered upon supernatural powers rather than the old Human political order.[4][5]
His cooperation with Victor was nevertheless pragmatic rather than unconditional submission. Sucellus remained protective of his Pantheon's independence and concealed at least one exceptionally valuable object left behind by a Primordial God of the Celtic Pantheon. He feared that revealing it would attract the greed of stronger Pantheons and potentially place his already vulnerable faction in danger.[3]
This cautious political strategy ultimately became irrelevant once Victor decided to completely unify Earth beneath the [[Elderblood Empire]]. [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] personally invaded the Celtic Pantheon in Chapter 1082. Sucellus immediately recognized that even his unknown trump cards could not overcome Violet, much less the full Empire, and intended to surrender. Violet deliberately denied him that opportunity, forcing the Celtic Gods into a battle intended to shatter their remaining independence and pride before annexation. Sucellus consequently ordered his Pantheon to fight for its home.[6]
The resistance ended in complete defeat. Shortly afterward, the Celtic Pantheon's territory was shown devastated, with its Gods either dead or placed in chains while Victor's forces consumed Gods who refused incorporation into the Empire. Sucellus did not receive an individually narrated death scene, but subsequent continuity treats him as deceased following the destruction of his Pantheon.[7]

### Physical Appearance
Sucellus has not received a sufficiently detailed physical description in the novel for his hair color, eye color, height or ordinary clothing to be stated canonically.
During his appearances at the [[Supernatural Gathering]], he appeared in a humanoid Divine form like the other [[God-Kings]]. During the invasion of the [[Celtic Pantheon]], the narration focused principally upon Sucellus' reactions to [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] and the overwhelming Imperial forces rather than describing his physical characteristics.[6]
The mallet, drinking vessel, dog and similar symbols commonly associated with the real-world Gaulish deity Sucellus have not been directly established as equipment or physical characteristics of the novel's Sucellus and therefore should not be treated as confirmed canonical possessions.

### Personality
Sucellus was an intelligent, pragmatic and highly calculating ruler. Unlike many Gods whose pride repeatedly drove them into catastrophically unfavorable confrontations, Sucellus was willing to evaluate the actual balance of power before acting. He understood that the [[Celtic Pantheon]] lacked the military and political weight of larger factions and consequently preferred diplomacy, secrecy and strategic cooperation over unnecessary conflict.[3]
This pragmatism was clearly demonstrated through his relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Sucellus recognized relatively early that opposing Victor would provide little benefit and instead chose to support him during the campaign against the Egyptian and Elder God factions. His decision was not portrayed as blind loyalty; it was the behavior of a ruler who understood which side offered the greatest chance of preserving and strengthening his own people.[4][5]
Sucellus was also considerably more forward-looking than conservative Gods who remained attached to the old Human order. During the [[Supernatural Gathering]], he agreed with Victor that the era in which supernatural civilizations silently remained behind Humanity had ended. He advocated active guidance to prevent the mistakes of previous civilizations from simply repeating themselves.[4]
At the same time, Sucellus was ambitious and morally opportunistic. While observing the advantages enjoyed by Gods personally connected to Victor, he briefly considered using his own married sister to establish a closer relationship with the rapidly rising Dragon. Her existing marriage did not initially concern him; from his perspective, emotions were temporary while political benefits could endure far longer.[3]
He abandoned the thought primarily because Victor genuinely terrified him. Sucellus openly admitted to himself that he had no shame in being frightened. He had existed for thousands of years and understood the normal pace at which powerful Beings developed, yet Victor had reached an absurd level of power in less than a millennium. To Sucellus, Victor's existence contradicted the established rules he had spent his entire life observing.[3]
His caution did not mean that he lacked pride. When [[Hela Elderblood]] reduced the effective price of the [[Norse Pantheon]] after Sucellus had already withdrawn from the auction, he realized that he had underestimated the long-term value of additional Pantheon territory. Despite regretting the decision, he refused to immediately reverse himself because doing so after publicly declaring disinterest would damage his reputation.[3]
Sucellus nevertheless placed the survival of his Pantheon above personal arrogance when destruction became unavoidable. Faced with Violet and the Imperial invasion fleet, he correctly concluded that victory was impossible and prepared to surrender. Only after Violet intentionally prevented that surrender and began destroying his territory did Sucellus abandon diplomacy and command the Celtic Gods to fight for their home.[6]

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Sucellus made his first direct appearance in Chapter 863 during the enormous [[Supernatural Gathering]] held after the destruction of the [[Greek Pantheon]], the transformation of Hell and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s unprecedented rise in supernatural politics. His arrival attracted attention because the Supreme God of the Celts rarely appeared personally at gatherings of this magnitude.[1]
The meeting gradually evolved from a discussion of recent catastrophes into a confrontation over the conspiracy involving the [[Egyptian Pantheon]], [[New Dawn]] and the Elder Gods. Victor initially intended to treat the entire Egyptian faction as an enemy, but after discussion with [[Shiva]] and the [[Heavenly Father]], he narrowed the coming war toward the individuals directly responsible while still mobilizing an enormous coalition against them.[8][9]
Sucellus aligned the [[Celtic Pantheon]] with this coalition.
When Victor subsequently presented his proposal for the reconstruction of Earth, Sucellus was among the principal [[God-Kings]] who immediately agreed that repairing the planet was necessary. The plan involved merging Earth's surviving territories into a new supercontinent, rebuilding civilization without the old national borders and using Divine power to repair the ecological devastation created through both Human activity and the Demonic invasion.[4]
Sucellus summarized his position by declaring that the Era of Men had ended and that supernatural Beings should no longer remain silent while the same mistakes were repeated. His statement closely aligned with Victor's belief that supernatural civilizations needed to take responsibility for Earth's future rather than continuing the previous policy of almost complete non-interference.[4]
Sucellus' support continued beyond rhetoric.
When Victor initiated the hunt against the conspirators, the Celtic Pantheon contributed powerful Gods to the coalition. Sucellus sent [[Taranis]], the Celtic God associated with Thunder, and [[Cernuno]], the God of Animals, to participate in the operation alongside forces from Heaven, the [[Shinto Pantheon]] and Victor's expanding faction.[5]
The decision placed Sucellus among the rulers who chose cooperation with Victor during one of the decisive conflicts that established the future balance of Earth's supernatural world.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Sucellus appeared again after [[Hela Elderblood]] conquered the [[Norse Pantheon]] and decided to auction the entire Divine territory to the other rulers gathered around [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
Unlike rulers willing to spend exceptional resources for more territory, Sucellus immediately withdrew from the negotiation, claiming that he possessed nothing Hela would consider sufficiently valuable.[3]
His statement was deliberately false.
Sucellus possessed an unnamed object of extraordinary value that had been left behind by a Primordial God of the [[Celtic Pantheon]]. Despite recognizing that purchasing the Norse Pantheon would increase his authority, he refused to expose this treasure. Revealing its existence before the assembled Gods could attract their greed, and Sucellus knew that the Celtic Pantheon was currently the weakest faction present. Acquiring additional territory was not worth potentially revealing an asset his Pantheon might be unable to defend.[3]
His internal thoughts during the negotiation also revealed how closely he watched Victor's political ascent. Seeing the benefits received by women personally connected to Victor, Sucellus briefly considered involving his own sister despite the fact that she was already married. He abandoned the plan not because of her marriage but because Victor's existence frightened him. Sucellus had lived for thousands of years and considered Victor's rise to his current level in less than one thousand years fundamentally abnormal.[3]
Sucellus later realized that he had made a strategic mistake. When Hela lowered the practical requirements for purchasing the Pantheon and pointed out the future importance of additional Divine territory once Earth's Sector advanced, Sucellus understood that he had focused too heavily on immediate risk and overlooked long-term expansion. He nevertheless refused to retract his earlier withdrawal because doing so would make him appear indecisive and damage his pride as a God-King.[3]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
The [[Celtic Pantheon]] remained politically active as Earth approached its complete unification. Alongside the [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]], Celtic factions attempted through multiple non-military methods to gain influence over the increasingly valuable Arcane Realm, whose connection to another world had transformed it into an exceptional source of resources and strategic opportunity.[10]
Their efforts ultimately failed when Evie Moriarty aligned the Arcane Realm with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
This period also revealed an important limitation upon Sucellus' rule: although he was the Celtic God-King, he did not possess absolute authority over every God within the Pantheon. The Celtic Pantheon contained multiple internal factions with their own objectives, meaning Sucellus could not simply impose every decision upon the entire Divine civilization.[10]
That political fragmentation later became one of the Pantheon's greatest weaknesses.
Victor eventually decided that alliances and fragmented sovereignty were no longer sufficient. His objective changed from maintaining a coalition among Earth's supernatural powers to placing the entire planet beneath a single permanent Imperial authority.
When the [[Elderblood Empire]] began moving across Earth, Sucellus and the Celtic Gods immediately sensed several immensely powerful forces approaching their territory. One of Sucellus' subordinates asked what they should do, but before he could formulate a response he recognized that the decision had effectively already been made for them.[6]
Two enormous Imperial ships appeared above the Celtic Pantheon. Between them opened a portal from which [[Violet Elderblood Snow]], now one of the Elderblood Empire's principal Empresses, emerged in full armor accompanied by an overwhelming military presence.[6]
The moment Violet looked directly at Sucellus, his existence trembled. Despite being a God-King, he immediately understood that he was completely outclassed; the narration compared the sensation to looking upon a fundamentally superior existence.[6]
Sucellus attempted to invoke the previous treaty between Victor's faction and the Celtic Pantheon. Violet dismissed it, declaring that an agreement on paper held no authority against the Emperor's present ambition. More Imperial portals then opened across the Celtic sky and began releasing ships and elite soldiers until the entire visible region had effectively become an occupation zone.[6]
Violet formally offered two possibilities: peaceful surrender to the Emperor or forced submission.
Sucellus assessed the situation rationally. Even if he deployed every hidden asset and trump card available to him, he would still have to defeat Violet and then face the complete Elderblood Empire. He could not see any possibility of defeating Violet herself. Recognizing that resistance would be futile, he prepared to surrender.[6]
Violet did not permit him to finish.
She immediately interpreted his unfinished response as choosing forced submission and ordered the Imperial military to attack. Her objective was deliberate: she wanted the Celtic Pantheon comprehensively defeated, stripped of its resources and reduced to a condition where its surviving members would become dependent upon the Empire instead of entering it with enough independent power to become a future source of rebellion.[6]
As bombardment devastated his territory, Sucellus realized that Violet had never intended to accept an easy capitulation. Enraged by the destruction of his home and no longer possessing a peaceful alternative, he ordered every Celtic force available to fight back, use everything and defend their home.[6]
[[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] and [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] subsequently joined Violet, turning an already hopeless engagement into an overwhelming Imperial conquest.
The Celtic resistance was completely crushed.
While confronting the Hindu Gods shortly afterward, Victor revealed the condition of the Celtic Pantheon as an explicit demonstration of what happened to factions that resisted annexation. The territory had been devastated, Celtic Gods lay dead across the battlefield and survivors were being placed in chains. Victor's clones were simultaneously consuming Gods from the Celtic Pantheon and other smaller factions that refused incorporation into the Empire.[7]
The exact moment of Sucellus' death is not individually narrated. However, later continuity identifies him among the deceased Celtic Gods following the conquest. His reign therefore ended with the destruction of the independent Celtic Pantheon and its absorption into Victor's new world order.

### Skills & Abilities
Sucellus was an ancient God-King and possessed the supernatural abilities inherent to a high-ranking God. However, his direct combat abilities and personal Divinities were never properly demonstrated before his death. The following list therefore separates confirmed capabilities from unsupported mythological assumptions.
- God Physiology: Sucellus was a natural Divine Being and possessed the Higher-Existence physiology of a God, including a Divine Soul, supernatural longevity and the ability to exist through [[Divine Energy]].
- Enhanced Physical Characteristics: As an ancient God-King, Sucellus possessed physical attributes vastly beyond ordinary Mortals and conventional supernatural species, although no isolated strength or speed feat was directly shown.
- Divine Energy Manipulation: Like other Gods, Sucellus could wield Divine Energy and possessed supernatural authority corresponding to his unknown personal Divinities.
- [[Immortality]]: Sucellus possessed the effective immortality associated with Gods. During the conquest of the [[Celtic Pantheon]], the narration reiterated that Gods normally return after ordinary physical death unless killed through specialized methods capable of permanently affecting their Divine existence.[6]
- Regeneration / Resurrection: Gods who were not killed through an appropriate anti-Divine or Soul-destroying method could eventually return. This was one of the reasons Violet's forces treated ordinary battlefield deaths as temporary during the Celtic invasion.[6]
- Supernatural Senses: Sucellus could perceive the approach of extraordinarily powerful supernatural forces. He and the surrounding Celtic Gods sensed the Elderblood invasion force before Violet physically entered their immediate location.[6]
- Energy Perception: Sucellus could recognize overwhelming differences in supernatural power. Merely meeting Violet's gaze was sufficient for him to understand that she existed at a level he could not realistically overcome.[6]
- Pantheon Authority: As God-King, Sucellus possessed political and Divine command over the principal forces of the Celtic Pantheon. He could mobilize Celtic Gods for international supernatural conflicts and later ordered the entire faction to resist the Elderblood invasion.[5][6]
- Strategic Intelligence: Sucellus consistently evaluated political and military risks before committing his Pantheon. He concealed his most valuable Divine resource during Hela's auction, recognized the dangers of attracting stronger Pantheons' greed, aligned himself with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] when cooperation was beneficial and correctly concluded that fighting Violet was unwinnable before hostilities began.[3][6]

#### Divinities
Sucellus' specific Divinities have not been explicitly identified in the currently available novel chapters documenting him.
Earlier versions of this page listed:
- Agriculture;
- Boundaries;
- Wine.
These attributes originate from real-world traditions surrounding the historical Gaulish deity Sucellus rather than demonstrated statements or feats from the novel. Until the story explicitly identifies those Concepts as belonging to its version of Sucellus, they should not be indexed as confirmed canonical Divinities.
Similarly, the traditional mallet and associations with protection, provision, drink and agriculture should not automatically be converted into powers without novel evidence.

#### Unknown Trump Cards
During the Elderblood invasion, Sucellus considered using his remaining trump cards against Violet. The narration nevertheless states that even with them he could see no realistic way of defeating her.[6]
The nature, number and abilities of these trump cards were never revealed.
They may or may not have included the Primordial relic Sucellus protected during Hela's auction; the novel does not explicitly confirm that the two references describe the same resource.

### Political Authority
Sucellus' title as God-King represented his position as ruler of the [[Celtic Pantheon]] rather than proof that his personal combat power was identical to rulers such as [[Zeus]], [[Shiva]], [[Odin]] or the [[Heavenly Father]].
The Celtic Pantheon was outside Earth's traditional Seven Great Pantheons and possessed considerably less geopolitical influence.[2]
Furthermore, Sucellus' authority was not absolute. The Celtic Pantheon contained multiple internal factions, some of which pursued interests independent of their ruler. Even as God-King, Sucellus could not simply command the entire Pantheon as a completely centralized state.[10]

### Possessions

#### Primordial Relic
Sucellus possessed an unnamed object of exceptional value that had been left behind by a Primordial God of the [[Celtic Pantheon]].[3]
The relic was important enough that Sucellus believed revealing it before other Pantheons could immediately attract their greed. Because the Celtic Pantheon lacked the strength to comfortably defend itself against every stronger Divine faction, he refused to offer the object even in exchange for an entire conquered Pantheon.[3]
No information has been revealed regarding:
- its name;
- physical appearance;
- creator;
- exact original owner;
- abilities;
- purpose;
- or ultimate fate after the Elderblood conquest.
Because Violet ordered Imperial assassins to seize the Celtic Pantheon's treasures during the invasion, it is possible the artifact eventually entered Imperial possession, but this has not been explicitly confirmed and should not presently be stated as fact.[6]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
Sucellus initially regarded [[Victor Elderblood]] as an extraordinarily useful political ally and chose cooperation rather than hostility.
He supported Victor's hunt against the factions responsible for threatening his family, contributed Celtic Gods to the coalition and supported Victor's proposal to reorganize Earth's civilization.[4][5]
Privately, however, Sucellus was deeply frightened by Victor.
His fear did not arise from cowardice alone but from the rate of Victor's development. Sucellus had lived for thousands of years and possessed a strong understanding of how powerful supernatural Beings normally progressed. Victor violated those expectations completely, attaining overwhelming power in less than a millennium.[3]
Sucellus consequently avoided attempting more aggressive political manipulation involving Victor even when doing so might have benefited the [[Celtic Pantheon]].
Their alliance ultimately ended when Victor abandoned the previous system of independent allied Pantheons and ordered the complete annexation of Earth.[6]

#### Violet Elderblood Snow
[[Violet Elderblood Snow]] personally led the invasion that ended Sucellus' rule.
Sucellus immediately understood that the difference between them was overwhelming. Meeting her gaze caused his existence to tremble, and despite his position as God-King he could see no realistic possibility of defeating her.[6]
He attempted to invoke the prior treaty and later intended to surrender peacefully. Violet deliberately rejected both approaches because her objective was not merely political capitulation; she wanted the [[Celtic Pantheon]] comprehensively broken before integration into the Empire.[6]
Sucellus therefore fought Violet only after she removed surrender as a viable option.

#### Celtic Pantheon
Sucellus was responsible for protecting and representing the [[Celtic Pantheon]] in the wider supernatural world.
His decisions repeatedly reflected awareness of its weakness. He concealed strategically valuable assets, avoided unnecessary confrontations with larger powers and entered beneficial coalitions whenever possible.[3]
His rule was nevertheless constrained by internal factions. The Celtic Gods did not operate as a completely unified political body, meaning even their God-King could not dictate every action taken within his Pantheon.[10]
During the final invasion, Sucellus initially attempted to preserve his people through surrender. Once Violet prevented that option, he ordered them to defend their home despite knowing the battle was effectively unwinnable.[6]

#### Hela Elderblood
Sucellus participated in [[Hela Elderblood]]'s auction of the conquered [[Norse Pantheon]] but refused to bid.
He lied that he possessed nothing capable of satisfying Hela even though he secretly owned an exceptionally valuable Primordial relic. Protecting the [[Celtic Pantheon]]'s hidden resource was more important to him than gaining additional territory.[3]
When Hela later offered substantially easier conditions, Sucellus regretted withdrawing but refused to reverse his position because of pride and the political embarrassment it would cause.[3]

#### Unnamed Sister
Sucellus has at least one unnamed sister who was already married by the time of Chapter 970.
While considering methods of obtaining a closer connection to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Sucellus briefly contemplated having his sister pursue him despite her existing marriage. He ultimately abandoned the idea because Victor frightened him too much for Sucellus to risk involving himself in such an intrusive scheme.[3]
Her identity and ultimate fate remain unknown.

#### Taranis and Cernuno
[[Taranis]] and [[Cernuno]] were among the Celtic Gods acting beneath Sucellus' authority.
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] announced his hunt against the conspirators threatening his family, Sucellus dispatched both Gods to participate alongside the allied forces.[5]

### Death
Sucellus' precise death is not shown directly on-panel.
Chapter 1082 ends his personal role with the beginning of the [[Celtic Pantheon]]'s final battle. After Violet intentionally rejected his attempted surrender, Sucellus ordered every available Celtic force to defend their home against the invading Elderblood military.[6]
By Chapter 1085, the battle was already over. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] displayed an image of the conquered Celtic Pantheon in which:
- the territory had been devastated;
- Celtic Gods lay dead across the ground;
- surviving Gods were being chained;
- and Victor's clones were consuming Gods who resisted annexation.[7]
Later continuity identifies Sucellus as deceased.
Because his individual final moments were not narrated, the specific person or attack responsible for permanently killing him remains unknown.

### Trivia
- Sucellus was introduced in Chapter 863 as the Supreme God of the Celts.[1]
- He was the recognized God-King of the [[Celtic Pantheon]] despite not possessing absolute political control over every internal Celtic faction.[10]
- The Celtic Pantheon was not counted among Earth's traditional Seven Great Pantheons.[2]
- By Chapter 970, Sucellus internally regarded the Celtic Pantheon as the weakest Pantheon among the major factions represented at the auction.[3]
- Sucellus secretly possessed a valuable artifact left behind by one of his Pantheon's [[Primordial Gods]] but refused to expose it even for the chance to acquire the entire [[Norse Pantheon]].[3]
- His fear of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was explicitly acknowledged by Sucellus himself. What disturbed him most was the speed at which Victor had broken every conventional expectation surrounding supernatural growth.[3]
- Despite being a cautious ruler, Sucellus could be highly opportunistic. He briefly considered using his already-married sister to establish a closer political relationship with Victor.[3]
- Sucellus was one of the supernatural rulers who supported Victor's plan to eliminate Earth's old national structure and reorganize the surviving population beneath a unified civilization.[4]
- He attempted to surrender during the Elderblood invasion rather than sacrifice his Pantheon in a hopeless fight, but Violet deliberately prevented him from completing the surrender.[6]
- The previous claim that Sucellus' confirmed Divinities were Agriculture, Boundaries and Wine was based upon real-world mythology rather than presently cited novel material. They are therefore omitted from the canonical ability list.
- The traditional aliases, spouse Nantosuelta, ancestry through Danu, mallet symbolism, drinking vessel and dog imagery similarly require direct novel confirmation before being treated as canonical details of the MTW character.
- Sucellus should not automatically be described as the Celtic equivalent of [[Zeus]] in raw power. God-King identifies his rulership position; the story repeatedly demonstrates enormous power differences between rulers of different Pantheons.
