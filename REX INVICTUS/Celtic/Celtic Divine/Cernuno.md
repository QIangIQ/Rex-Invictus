Cernuno, also known as [[Cernunnos]], is a God belonging to the [[Celtic Pantheon]]. He holds the position of the God of Animals and was one of the Celtic Gods dispatched to participate in the multinational hunt organized against the enemies of [[Victor Elderblood]] following the Gathering of Supernatural Beings.[1]

### Physical Appearance
Cernuno's individual physical characteristics have not been described in detail in the novel. During his debut, he was among the powerful Divine Beings gathered in Earth's sky for the hunt, with the assembled representatives described as being fully armed with their Divine Gear.[1]

### Personality
Cernuno appears comparatively straightforward and expressive. Unlike several of the more battle-oriented Gods gathered for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s hunt, he openly questioned why he had been sent to participate, grumbling that he was the God of Animals, not a God of hunters. His reaction suggests that he distinguishes his own Divine role from that of Gods whose authority is directly associated with hunting or warfare and was not particularly enthusiastic about being reduced from a deity to what he considered a mere hunter.[1]
He is also capable of openly experiencing ordinary emotions such as envy. Like many of the male Gods present, Cernuno was heavily affected by [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood's transformed beauty and the supernatural attraction produced by her presence. After witnessing the intimate bond between Aphrodite and Victor, Cernuno privately admitted that he had never felt so envious of another person before.[1]
Despite his complaints, Cernuno did not refuse the assignment or abandon the coalition. He remained with the assembled Pantheon representatives and followed Victor's operation against the Egyptian Gods, showing that he was ultimately willing to fulfill the duties assigned to him by the leadership of the [[Celtic Pantheon]].[1]

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Following the conclusion of the Gathering of Supernatural Beings, the conflict against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s enemies ceased to resemble a conventional war and instead became what the narration called a hunt. Several Pantheons sent powerful representatives to support the operation, creating an unprecedented coalition of Divine and supernatural forces. The [[Celtic Pantheon]] contributed Cernuno, the God of Animals, alongside [[Taranis]], the Celtic God of Thunder.[1]
Cernuno gathered in Earth's sky with Taranis, [[Azrael]], Ariel, Susanoo, [[Takemikazuchi]], [[Thor]], [[Loki]] and the other representatives chosen by their respective Pantheons. All of them had been ordered by their superiors to participate in the hunt and arrived prepared for battle with Divine equipment.[1]
While the other warriors discussed the unusual concentration of Thunder Gods among the coalition, Cernuno complained about the nature of the assignment. He found it strange that a God whose authority concerned Animals had been sent to participate in a hunt, remarking that being reduced from a God to a hunter left him with mixed feelings.[1]
Victor soon arrived with [[Scathach]], Jeanne, Morgana, Zaladrac, Rose and [[Aphrodite]]. Cernuno, like the other representatives, was confronted with the revelation that Victor's faction already possessed numerous [[True Dragons]], causing the assembled observers to reconsider the actual military strength of the Dragons Nest.[1]
Aphrodite's arrival had an especially strong effect upon the group. Even without consciously activating her supernatural charm, her evolved beauty drew the attention of nearly everyone present. Cernuno was among those overwhelmed by the sight and internally concluded that ordinary Goddesses would be difficult to look at in the same way afterward. Seeing Aphrodite's relationship with Victor also caused Cernuno to experience an unprecedented degree of envy toward Victor.[1]
Victor subsequently demonstrated the danger of charm-based powers by temporarily applying Aphrodite's power through himself, overwhelming the assembled representatives and proving how vulnerable even experienced Divine Beings could be to sufficiently powerful attraction-based abilities. Once the demonstration ended, the coalition returned its attention to the operation against the [[Egyptian Pantheon]].[1]
Cernuno then continued as one of the Celtic representatives participating in Victor's coalition during the hunt against the Egyptian Gods.[1]

### Skills & Abilities
- God Physiology: Cernuno is an ancient Divine Being and a Major God of the [[Celtic Pantheon]]. His existence is fundamentally Divine rather than Mortal, granting him the supernatural qualities associated with Gods and the ability to wield Divine power.[1]
- Animal Divinity / Animal Manipulation: Cernuno is explicitly identified as the God of Animals. His principal Divine authority therefore concerns Animals, giving him conceptual authority over the aspect represented by his Divine position.[1] The novel has not yet shown the detailed applications, range or limitations of this power in combat, so abilities from the mythological [[Cernunnos]] should not automatically be attributed to him.
- [[Divine Energy]] Manipulation: As a God, Cernuno possesses and can wield Divine Energy associated with his Divine existence. No individually named Divine technique belonging to Cernuno has yet been demonstrated on-page.
- Divine Gear: Cernuno arrived for the hunt as part of the group of powerful Beings described as being fully armed with their Divine Gear.[1] The novel has not identified the name, form or abilities of Cernuno's personal Divine equipment.
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: As a Major God selected to participate directly in a military operation involving powerful Gods, Angels and other high-level supernatural combatants, Cernuno possesses physical capabilities far exceeding ordinary Mortals. His exact physical statistics have not been independently demonstrated.
- [[Immortality]] / Agelessness: As an ancient God whose age is measured in eons, Cernuno possesses the supernatural longevity characteristic of Divine Beings.
- Divine Senses: Cernuno can perceive and react to supernatural presences and Divine phenomena. He was immediately affected by and capable of recognizing the extraordinary supernatural qualities surrounding [[Aphrodite]] and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s group during the coalition's assembly.[1]
Note: Cernuno has received very little individual combat focus in the novel. Outside of his confirmed status as the God of Animals, no additional personal Divinities, specialized combat techniques, transformations or named weapons have been explicitly revealed. Mythological characteristics associated with the real-world Cernunnos are therefore not treated as canon abilities unless the novel itself assigns them to Cernuno.

### Trivia
- Cernuno's name is an alternate spelling of [[Cernunnos]], the name also listed for him in the series.
- His alias, The Horned One, reflects the name traditionally associated with Cernunnos.
- Despite being sent on a Divine hunt, Cernuno himself pointed out that he was the God of Animals, not a God of hunters.[1]
- Cernuno debuted alongside several representatives of other major Pantheons during one of the largest cooperative supernatural operations shown on Earth at the time.[1]
- He and [[Taranis]] were the two explicitly named representatives sent by the [[Celtic Pantheon]] to participate in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s hunt.[1]
- Cernuno became extremely envious of Victor after seeing the relationship between Victor and [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood.[1]
