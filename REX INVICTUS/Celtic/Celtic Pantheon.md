**[[Celtic Pantheon]]** collects the Irish, Scottish, Welsh and Gaulish myth figures in the vault. Most arrive through [[Scathach]] and the [[Flamel Series]].

## Greek History (IRL)

Celtic myth survives mainly in medieval Irish and Welsh manuscripts written by Christian monks, plus Roman descriptions and Gaulish inscriptions. Irish material is grouped into four cycles: the Mythological Cycle (the Tuatha Dé Danann), the [[Ulster Cycle]] ([[Cuchulain|Cú Chulainn]]), the Fenian Cycle (Fionn mac Cumhaill) and the Cycles of the Kings. The Welsh *Mabinogion* preserves British tradition.

The Celtic Pantheon was one of the surviving minor Pantheons of Earth, ruled by the God-King [[Sucellus]]. Unlike the original Seven Great Pantheons, the Celtic Pantheon did not possess a sufficiently important afterlife system to be recognized as one of Earth's primary Divine powers. It possessed its own Heaven and a comparatively small Hell, but its [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] lacked a recognized Ruler and consequently did not possess the normal God of Death hierarchy found within the Seven Great Pantheons.[1]
Despite its classification as a minor Pantheon, the Celtic Pantheon contained numerous Major and High-Level Gods, including [[Taranis]], [[Cernuno]], [[Epona]], [[Lugh]] and the famous Ascended Goddess and Rune Master [[Dún Scáith|Dun Scaith]]. Its ruler Sucellus possessed the status of God-King and represented the Pantheon in gatherings involving Earth's most important Divine factions.[2][3]
The Pantheon's political position during [[Victor Elderblood]]'s rise was complicated. Sucellus generally acted pragmatically and repeatedly cooperated with Victor when doing so benefited his Pantheon. The Celtic Gods participated alongside Victor's coalition against his enemies, with Taranis and Cernuno being formally deployed during the hunt following the [[Supernatural Gathering]].[4] However, the Pantheon was not ideologically unified, and at least one important Celtic God, Taranis, had secretly participated in [[New Dawn]] activities before the alliance.[1][5]
The Pantheon ultimately ceased to exist when Victor Elderblood initiated the complete conquest and unification of Earth. Violet Elderblood Snow, acting as Dragon Empress and Imperial commander, personally led the invasion of the Celtic Divine territory. Sucellus was offered the choice of peaceful surrender or forced submission, but the resulting battle ended with the Pantheon's territory devastated, numerous Gods dead and the survivors restrained in chains.[6][7]
Unlike the [[Shinto Pantheon]] and [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]], which retained internal self-government after surrendering, the Celtic Pantheon was used by Victor as an example of the fate awaiting factions that failed to submit under favorable terms. Its independent Divine government was completely destroyed and any surviving Gods were absorbed into the new Imperial order.[7]

### Classification

#### Minor Pantheon
The Celtic Pantheon was explicitly not one of Earth's Seven Great Pantheons. The distinction was determined primarily by the importance of a Pantheon's afterlife system rather than merely its number of Gods or military power.[1]
For a Pantheon to qualify as one of the Seven Great Pantheons, its Hell needed to possess a sufficiently large flow of Souls to attract the attention of the Judges of the Abyss. Once recognized, the [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] would become integrated into the larger Soul-management System established by the cosmic authorities. A corresponding Ruler would then exist within the Hell alongside a God of Death responsible for helping manage Souls.[1]
The Celtic Pantheon did not meet this standard.
It possessed a Hell, but the Realm was comparatively small and lacked the importance necessary to receive a recognized Ruler. As a result, the Pantheon did not possess a conventional God of Death directly appointed to manage its afterlife.[1]
This distinction became important when [[Victor Elderblood]], [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood and their allies investigated [[Taranis]]. Although Taranis was traditionally identified as the Celtic God of Thunder, Victor sensed an abnormally strong Death-related authority from him. Aphrodite considered this highly suspicious because Taranis should not naturally have possessed such a powerful Death Divinity within the Celtic Pantheon's existing structure.[1]
A simplified comparison can be represented as:
EARTH'S DIVINE PANTHEONS
│
┌────────────────┴────────────────┐
│                                 │
SEVEN GREAT PANTHEONS              MINOR PANTHEONS
│                                 │
Major Heaven + Hell                Smaller Divine Realms
Recognized Hell System             Limited Soul Flow
God-King + Ruler                   God-King possible
God of Death                       No recognized Ruler
│                                 │
Norse / Hindu / etc.               CELTIC PANTHEON
The Celtic Pantheon's status as a minor Pantheon should therefore not be interpreted as meaning its Gods were individually weak. It merely lacked the same cosmological and afterlife authority possessed by the Seven Great Pantheons.

### Divine Realm
The Celtic Gods possessed their own independent Divine territory and Paradise. The exact canonical name of this Realm has not been stated.
[[Sucellus]] governed this territory as its God-King and was sufficiently attached to his authority over it that he protested when the cosmic restructuring of Earth's afterlife system threatened the territorial privileges of Pantheon leaders.[2]
During the [[Supernatural Gathering]], Sucellus questioned whether the withdrawal and redistribution of Divine territories by higher cosmic authorities was legitimate. He was reminded that matters directly involving Souls fell under the authority of the Primordial system and that dissatisfied [[God-Kings]] could surrender their Paradises if they refused to comply.[2]
Sucellus immediately abandoned the objection rather than lose his Pantheon's Paradise.
The Realm possessed at least a palace belonging to Sucellus and a populated Divine territory inhabited by other Celtic Gods. During the Imperial invasion, Sucellus and his subordinates left his palace and observed Imperial ships filling the sky above the Pantheon.[6]

### Government

#### God-King
The Celtic Pantheon was ruled by [[Sucellus]], who possessed the title of God-King despite the Pantheon not belonging to the Seven Great Pantheons.[2][3]
The title demonstrated that God-Kingship itself was not restricted exclusively to the seven original major Pantheons. Sucellus possessed sufficient authority over his Divine faction to represent it during international supernatural negotiations and to deploy Celtic Gods on military missions.
He was also considerably more pragmatic than many of Earth's older Gods.
Rather than immediately opposing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] as his influence expanded, Sucellus repeatedly positioned the Celtic Pantheon alongside whichever political direction he considered most beneficial for his people.
During discussions concerning Earth's future civilization, Sucellus supported Victor's argument that supernatural rulers should establish fundamental restrictions capable of preventing the mistakes committed during the Human era from being repeated.[8]
He also sent members of his Pantheon to assist Victor's military operations.[4]
This cooperation did not ultimately preserve the Pantheon's independence. When Victor decided that every sovereign faction on Earth would be brought beneath his Empire, his previous treaties and alliances became subordinate to his larger objective of complete planetary unification.[6]

### Afterlife System
The Celtic Pantheon possessed both a Heaven and a Hell, but its afterlife system was substantially less important than those belonging to the Seven Great Pantheons.[1]
Its Hell did not possess enough Soul traffic to qualify for integration into the primary afterlife administration managed through the Judges of the Abyss.
Consequently:
- the Celtic Hell existed;
- it processed or contained Souls associated with the Pantheon;
- it was smaller than the Hells of the Seven Great Pantheons;
- it did not possess a recognized Ruler;
- and it did not possess a normal Pantheon God of Death acting as the Ruler's subordinate.[1]
This made [[Taranis]]' later Death authority particularly anomalous.
[[Aphrodite]] knew Taranis primarily as a God of Thunder and specifically stated that he was not part of the Celtic Pantheon's Hell administration. Nevertheless, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] perceived him more like an Angel of Death than a conventional Thunder God.[1]
The discrepancy was ultimately connected to Taranis's secret activities outside his Pantheon.

### New Dawn Infiltration
The Celtic Pantheon was not politically or ideologically uniform.
The clearest example was [[Taranis]], one of its important Major Gods.
Taranis had secretly become involved with [[New Dawn]], the organization conducting experiments intended to manipulate Humanity, Faith and Divine evolution. His activities placed him alongside figures such as [[Anubis]] and the Shinto Shinigami.[1][5]
During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s investigation of one of New Dawn's hidden research facilities, Taranis was identified as the Celtic God present behind the operation. Victor immediately noticed that the God possessed a Death-related presence inconsistent with his known identity as a Thunder God.[1]
After Victor completely destroyed the hidden island and its Dimensional research facility using Dragon Breath, Taranis and Anubis returned to investigate the destruction.[5]
Taranis reacted angrily because the facility represented a substantial amount of his work. His conversation with Anubis and [[Erebus]] further revealed that multiple research sites existed and that the conspirators intended to hide the remaining facilities after Victor discovered the operation.[5]
They also discussed obtaining a God associated with Agriculture to solve the food-production problems affecting their Human experimental population, even considering kidnapping [[Demeter]].[5]
Erebus had also supplied Taranis with some of his own Darkness-related Energy, further demonstrating how deeply the Celtic God had become involved with Divine forces outside his own Pantheon.[5]
There is no direct evidence that [[Sucellus]] or the entire Celtic Pantheon participated in Taranis's New Dawn activities. Taranis's involvement should therefore be treated as the actions of an individual member rather than automatically attributed to the Pantheon as a whole.

### Relationship with Victor Elderblood

#### Early Neutrality
The Celtic Pantheon was initially one of the many Divine factions observing [[Victor Elderblood]]'s rapid rise.
[[Sucellus]] attended the major [[Supernatural Gathering]] alongside [[God-Kings]] such as [[Odin]], Ra, [[Amaterasu]], the [[Heavenly Father]] and representatives of the [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]].[2]
When Victor arrived at the Gathering after his evolution, his presence alone shocked Sucellus. Despite already being a God-King, Sucellus had never personally experienced pressure comparable to Victor's and openly questioned whether Victor could still meaningfully be considered a former Mortal.[9]
The encounter made the difference in their potential increasingly obvious.

#### Political Cooperation
[[Sucellus]] subsequently supported several of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s political proposals.
During the Gathering's discussions about Earth's future, Victor argued that the coming supernatural civilization required fundamental rules preventing Mortals and supernatural species from recklessly destroying Nature and repeating the failures of previous Human civilization.
Sucellus agreed, stating that the Human Age had ended and that supernatural rulers could no longer remain passive while the same mistakes were repeated.[8]
The Celtic Pantheon also participated directly in Victor's military coalition.
When Victor initiated the hunt against the remaining enemies identified after the Gathering, the Celtic Pantheon deployed two Major Gods:
- [[Taranis]], God of Thunder;
- [[Cernuno]], God of Animals.[4]
They fought alongside representatives sent by the Biblical, Shinto and other allied factions.
The decision demonstrated that Sucellus was willing to commit actual Pantheon combatants rather than limiting his support to political statements.

#### The Supernatural Hunt
[[Taranis]] and [[Cernuno]] gathered alongside Gods and supernatural warriors from numerous factions to participate in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s hunt.
Among the force were figures such as Ariel, [[Azrael]], Susanoo and [[Takemikazuchi]].[4]
The scene also indirectly confirmed [[Epona]]'s continued importance within the Celtic Pantheon. After Victor demonstrated [[Aphrodite]]'s supernatural Charm against the assembled Gods, Taranis decided that he would ask Epona to train him in resisting Beauty- and Charm-based supernatural influence.[4]
This indicates that Epona possessed sufficient authority or expertise over Beauty to serve as the Pantheon's natural specialist for such training.

#### Temporary Alliance
By the period following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s establishment of [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]], [[Sucellus]] continued to interact diplomatically with Victor and the other major [[God-Kings]].
He was present during the auction of the former [[Norse Pantheon]] following Ragnarok and observed the rapid political expansion of Victor's new faction.[3][10]
The resources Dragon's Nest could offer had already reached a level capable of astonishing the remaining Pantheons. During the auction, the value of some Imperial artifacts was considered so enormous that even [[Indra]] and the Celtic God-King would have willingly sacrificed extraordinary portions of their own Pantheons to acquire them.[10]
The economic and military gap between Dragon's Nest and the old Divine factions therefore continued expanding long before the final Imperial conquest.

### Dun Scaith
[[Dún Scáith|Dun Scaith]] was historically associated with the Celtic Pantheon and became one of its most unusual Divine figures.
Unlike ancient Gods born directly as Divine Beings, Dun Scaith was an Ascended Goddess who had previously existed as a Mortal/Witch before reaching Godhood through her own development.
She became renowned throughout the supernatural world for her unparalleled mastery of Runes, earning titles such as the Grandmaster of Runes and God Slayer.
Dun Scaith was particularly famous for having trained numerous Heroes and warriors. Her most important known disciple was [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], to whom Dun Scaith personally gave the name "[[Scathach]]".
By [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later era, Dun Scaith no longer functioned as an ordinary subordinate of the Celtic Pantheon. Her interests increasingly centered upon Runes, Scathach and Victor's rapidly developing Rune system.
During the [[Supernatural Gathering]], she remotely observed Victor using Demonic Runes, Dragon Runes and Creation Energy together and immediately recognized the extreme abnormality of his techniques.[11]
Her relationship with Victor eventually became sufficiently close that she fully left her former Celtic alignment and entered [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]], later becoming a member of Clan Elderblood.
Consequently, Dun Scaith survived the eventual destruction of the Celtic Pantheon because she was no longer politically tied to [[Sucellus]]'s faction by that period.

### Major Gods

#### Sucellus
[[Sucellus]] was the God-King and supreme political leader of the Celtic Pantheon.
He represented the Pantheon at international gatherings, governed its unnamed Paradise and possessed authority to deploy Celtic Gods into foreign military operations.[2][4]
Sucellus generally displayed a pragmatic political mentality. He understood power differences better than many Divine rulers and frequently supported arrangements he believed would improve his Pantheon's chances of survival.
Despite this pragmatism, his Pantheon was ultimately destroyed during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s planetary conquest.

#### Taranis
[[Taranis]] was a Major Celtic God traditionally associated with Thunder. He later displayed Divine authorities associated with Death strong enough to confuse even [[Aphrodite]].[1]
His confirmed abilities and Divine associations include:
- Thunder;
- Light;
- Sky;
- and an acquired or developed Death authority.
Taranis secretly participated in [[New Dawn]]'s experiments alongside [[Anubis]] and other conspirators.[5]
Despite this hidden affiliation, [[Sucellus]] later deployed him alongside [[Cernuno]] as an official representative of the Celtic Pantheon in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s supernatural hunt.[4]
This indicates that either his New Dawn activities remained unknown to Sucellus or had not yet resulted in his formal expulsion from the Pantheon.

#### Cernuno
[[Cernuno]], also known as [[Cernunnos]] or the Horned One, was a Major Celtic God associated with Animals.
[[Sucellus]] selected Cernuno alongside [[Taranis]] to represent the Celtic Pantheon during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s hunt against the surviving enemies identified after the [[Supernatural Gathering]].[4]
His deployment alongside Taranis suggests that Cernuno ranked among the Pantheon's more capable combat Gods.

#### Epona
[[Epona]] was a Celtic Goddess associated with Beauty.
Although little of her direct combat history has been shown, her Divine specialization was sufficiently respected that [[Taranis]] immediately considered asking her to train him after experiencing the overwhelming supernatural Charm generated through [[Aphrodite]]'s influence on [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[4]

#### Lugh
[[Lugh]] was a High-Level Celtic God associated primarily with the Sun. He was also connected to concepts traditionally associated with Light, Justice, War, Kingship, Craftsmen, Skills, Trade and Harvests.
By the later era, Lugh was counted among the stronger Gods belonging to the Celtic Pantheon.[12]

#### Dun Scaith
[[Dún Scáith|Dun Scaith]] was an Ascended Goddess of Runes and former member of the Celtic Pantheon. Unlike the other major Celtic Gods, she eventually separated from [[Sucellus]]'s faction and entered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Dragon's Nest before the Pantheon's destruction.

### Imperial Conquest

#### Beginning of the Unification
Once [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] decided to become the Emperor of Humanity, the previous system of independent nations, Pantheons and supernatural sovereign powers became incompatible with his plans.
His forces began moving throughout Earth simultaneously, targeting independent civilizations and bringing them beneath a single Imperial authority.[6]
The Celtic Pantheon became one of the first major examples of what happened when an old Divine faction faced the new Empire without sufficient strength to dictate terms.
[[Sucellus]] sensed the mobilization of Victor's forces and understood what was about to happen before the attack even began.
When one of his subordinate Gods asked what they should do, Sucellus remained silent before concluding that the choice had effectively already been made for them.[6]

#### Arrival of the Dragon Empress
As [[Sucellus]] and the Celtic Gods left his palace, they observed two enormous Imperial ships positioned in their sky.
A portal opened between them and [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] entered the Celtic territory personally.[6]
Violet appeared in full armor while wearing a violet Crown of Energy representing her Imperial authority. Her transformed body exceeded four metres in height and displayed her Draconic eyes and horns.[6]
The difference between her and Sucellus was immediately apparent.
Despite being a God-King, Sucellus's very existence trembled when their eyes met. He recognized Violet not merely as a stronger combatant but as a Being existing on a superior level.[6]
Sucellus nevertheless attempted to defend his Pantheon politically.
He demanded to know whether the Dragon Empress intended to violate the treaty existing between their factions.
Violet dismissed the agreement, making the Empire's new position explicit: previous treaties had become irrelevant in comparison with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s decision to conquer the planet.[6]
She declared that Earth would be conquered that day because the Emperor had decreed it.

#### Imperial Invasion
Violet raised her hand and additional portals opened throughout the Celtic sky.
Gigantic Imperial ships emerged until the visible heavens were filled with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s forces. Their hangars opened and Elite Imperial soldiers began descending directly into the Pantheon.[6]
Violet then presented the Celtic Gods with a final choice:
- peacefully submit to the Emperor;
- or be forcibly brought into submission.[6]
The Pantheon did not emerge from the confrontation intact.
By the time Victor later confronted [[Shiva]] and Kali, the Celtic Pantheon's defeat was already complete.

### Destruction
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] deliberately showed [[Shiva]] and Kali an image of the Celtic Pantheon's fate while demanding the [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]]'s surrender.
The image displayed an utterly defeated country.
The entire Celtic territory had been devastated. Gods lay dead across the ground while surviving Gods had been restrained in chains.[7]
Three Imperial women remained floating above the conquered Realm, with Violet recognizable as their commander through the violet Crown of Power above her head.[7]
Victor specifically used the Celtic Pantheon as the contrasting example when explaining why Shiva and Kali were being offered favorable surrender terms.
Because the Hindu Pantheon possessed Shiva and Kali, Victor granted it the "treatment of the strong" and allowed its internal government to survive.
The Celtic Pantheon had received no such privilege.[7]
Its defeat therefore differed fundamentally from the annexation of the Shinto and Hindu Pantheons:

The Celtic Pantheon therefore ceased to exist as an independent Divine institution.

### Aftermath
The destruction of the Celtic Pantheon occurred immediately before the formal beginning of the [[Imperial Calendar]].
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] subsequently annexed every remaining Pantheon, supernatural race and independent civilization on Earth beneath the [[Elderblood Empire]].[7]
Any surviving Celtic Gods consequently lost their former sovereignty and became subjects, prisoners or assets of the Empire rather than members of an independent Celtic Divine state.
The Pantheon's destruction also demonstrated an important difference in Victor's Imperial policy.
Submission could preserve:
- local government;
- cultural identity;
- Divine hierarchies;
- and a degree of administrative autonomy.
Resistance offered no comparable guarantee.
Victor intentionally used the Celtic Pantheon's ruined state as a psychological demonstration to [[Shiva]] and Kali, proving that the threat of complete destruction was not hypothetical.[7]

### Former Members

#### Dun Scaith
- [[Dún Scáith|Dun Scaith]] — Ascended Goddess of Runes, famous Rune Grandmaster and former mentor of [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]]. She separated from the Celtic Pantheon's political structure and eventually joined [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]] and Clan Elderblood, allowing her to survive the destruction of her former Pantheon.

### Relations

#### Dragon's Nest Pantheon
Initially neutral before developing into a pragmatic alliance under [[Sucellus]].
The Celtic Pantheon supported [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] during the hunt following the [[Supernatural Gathering]] and Sucellus participated constructively in discussions regarding the future supernatural government of Earth.[8][4]
The relationship ended once Victor determined that Earth would no longer contain independent sovereign Pantheons.

#### New Dawn
The Pantheon itself was never confirmed to be formally allied with [[New Dawn]].
However, [[Taranis]] secretly worked alongside New Dawn figures including [[Anubis]] and received assistance from [[Erebus]].[5]
His actions demonstrate that individual Celtic Gods could pursue agendas independent of [[Sucellus]] and the Pantheon's official foreign policy.

#### Elderblood Empire
The Empire became the Pantheon's final enemy and conqueror.
[[Violet Elderblood Snow]] personally led the military operation that destroyed its independent government.[6][7]
