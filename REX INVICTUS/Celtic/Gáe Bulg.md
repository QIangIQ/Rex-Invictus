The **[[Gáe Bulg]]** is the spear [[Scathach|Scáthach]] gives only to [[Cuchulain|Cú Chulainn]] in the [[Ulster Cycle]]. It is thrown with the foot, enters as one point and opens into thirty barbs, so it must be cut out of the body. With it Cú Chulainn kills his son Connla and his foster-brother Ferdiad at the ford in the *Táin*. In [[Scathach]]'s [[Rex Invictus]] note it marks her role as a maker of champions.
