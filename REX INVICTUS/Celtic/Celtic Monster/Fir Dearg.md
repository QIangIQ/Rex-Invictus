**[[Fir Dearg]]** is a figure of Rex Invictus lore. (Lore adapted from *The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** Flamel material is adapted into the Rex Invictus present (c. 2021). Rex Invictus wins any conflict; [[Robespierre]] fills [[Dr. John Dee]]'s role and hunts the Philosopher's Stone rather than [[The Codex]]. See [[Flamel Series#Integration rules]].

---

## Real-World Background (IRL)

The Fear Dearg ("red man") is a solitary Irish fairy in a red coat known for gruesome practical jokes.

---

## Flamel Series Canon (SOFTINF)

> The Fir Dearg are first mentioned in one of the 'Lost Stories' collection. Specifically, '[[Scathach]] the shadow and the Clan of the Eriu'. She referred to them as 'hairy stunted dwarves' and compared them to Leprechauns.
