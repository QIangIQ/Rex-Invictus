**[[Wild Hunt]]**: The Wild Hunt are a race of ghostly hounds controlled by the Archon [[Cernunnos]]. (Lore adapted from *The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** Flamel material is adapted into the Rex Invictus present (c. 2021). Rex Invictus wins any conflict; [[Robespierre]] fills [[Dr. John Dee]]'s role and hunts the Philosopher's Stone rather than [[The Codex]]. See [[Flamel Series#Integration rules]].

## Real-World Background (IRL)

The Wild Hunt is a European folk myth of a ghostly hunting party riding across the sky, led by figures such as [[Odin]], Herne the Hunter or Gwyn ap Nudd.

The Wild Hunt are a race of ghostly hounds controlled by the Archon Cernunnos. When slain, their bodies turn to dust. The Wild Hunt appear in numerous European pagan theologies.
