Black Annis, also known as Black Agnes, was a formidable witch in English folklore. Rather than conforming to traditional dietary habits, Black Annis chose to feast upon the flesh of humans, specifically children. According to English legend, she prowls the countryside around Leicestershire, looking for her next unwary victim.
As mentioned in [[The Alchemyst]], Black Annis is a vile, dark creature of the night and even thinking about her could cause madness. When [[Nicholas Flamel]] and [[Scathach]] are contemplating an Elder that could awaken [[Josh Newman]], they consider Black Annis, along with [[Persephone]]. Both, however, were deemed insane and too dangerous to make contact with. [[Bastet]] said that Black Annis was residing in the Catskills, and according to Scatty, if The [[Zephaniah|Witch of Endor]] (also known as Zephaniah), knew where Black Annis was, she would not let her live.
Additionally, we witness a prequel story from the perspective of [[Niccolo Machiavelli]]. He finds Black Annis in [[Paris]], brewing addictive hard-candy. This orange concoction lures children into her lair, to be used for slave-labor and food.

### Appearance
She is said to have a terrifying blue face and glowing eyes. In the extended short stories; we witness [[Niccolo Machiavelli|Machiavelli]]’s first hand encounter with the infamous elder. Her “change” seems to be that of a Strigiform. She’s seen with her iconic talons, but also a pinched, avian beak.

### Abilities
- [[Immortality]]: Black Annis is Immortal and does not age. Though an immortal may be poisoned or slain in battle, they may live for thousands or millions of years.
- Potion Brewing: Black Annis can brew formulas to aid her in acquiring prey, as seen in [[Paris]].
- Power Bestowal: Black Annis can awaken [[Humani]].
