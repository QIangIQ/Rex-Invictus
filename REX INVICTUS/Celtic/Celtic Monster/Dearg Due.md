## Real-World Background (IRL)

The Dearg Due ("red bloodsucker") is an Irish folk legend of a woman forced into marriage who rose from the grave to drink the blood of men.

Dearg Due are a type of Vampire mentioned in The Sorceress. Originating in Ireland, they are adept warriors and are very difficult to kill. The first Dearg Due was a woman, according to legend. Her tale is told all over Ireland.

### Description
It's been stated by [[Dr. John Dee]] that female Dearg Due are much deadlier and more dangerous than males. Also, its been stated that Dearg Due despise [[Cucubuths]] and would hunt down and kill them on sight. This is seen where a pack of three Cucubuths were immediately hunted down by a group of female Dearg Due after noticing them.

### Trivia
- According to legend, the Dearg Due was a woman who killed herself and returned as a vengeful creature which could only be stopped by putting rocks on top of her grave.
