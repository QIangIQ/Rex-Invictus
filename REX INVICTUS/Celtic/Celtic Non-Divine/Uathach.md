**[[Uathach]]** is the daughter of [[Scathach]] in the [[Ulster Cycle]].

---

## Celtic Myth (IRL)

Her name means "spectre". When [[Cuchulain]] arrives at [[Dún Scáith]] on the [[Isle of Skye]] to train, Uathach serves him, and he breaks her finger by accident. Her lover Cochar Crufe challenges him and is killed, and [[Cuchulain]] takes his place. Uathach then tells him how to force [[Scathach]] to teach him everything: leap onto the yew where she trains her sons and hold his sword to her breast until she grants his wishes.

## Rex Invictus

Named in [[Scathach]]'s relationships as her daughter. See [[Aoife]] and [[Ard-Greimne]].
