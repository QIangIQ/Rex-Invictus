**[[Cuchulain]]** is a figure of Rex Invictus lore. (Lore adapted from *The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** Flamel material is adapted into the Rex Invictus present (c. 2021). Rex Invictus wins any conflict; [[Robespierre]] fills [[Dr. John Dee]]'s role and hunts the Philosopher's Stone rather than [[The Codex]]. See [[Flamel Series#Integration rules]].

---

## Real-World Background (IRL)

Cú Chulainn is the great hero of the Ulster Cycle of Irish myth, trained by Scáthach at Dún Scáith on the Isle of Skye, wielder of the Gáe Bulg spear, and defender of Ulster in the *Táin Bó Cúailnge*.

### Abilities
- [[Immortality]]: Cuchulain gained Immortality through an unknown means, and therefore does not age (though he is vulnerable to injury and poison). Unless he is slain or poisoned by an enemy, Cuchulain may live for thousands or millions of years.
