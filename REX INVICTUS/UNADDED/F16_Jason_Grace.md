## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **This file:** the present-day Roman praetor.
- **Not to be confused with:** the mythical Argonaut Jason ("the first one, the one her brother had been named after") whose statue stands on the far-future Via Heroum (Ch 23). Future Thalia's thoughts there confirm that her brother is this Jason, but they say nothing about his fate in the future.
- **Sister:** Thalia Grace. Present Thalia is revived from the tree in Ch 15 (`C06_Thalia_Grace_Present.md`). Future Thalia is a Lieutenant of the Hunt (`C07_Thalia_Grace_Future_Lieutenant.md`). The siblings do not meet on page, and Jason never mentions her.
- **Canon baseline (labelled canon, not story):** In canon, Jason is Thalia's younger full brother (Zeus/Jupiter and Beryl Grace), given to Lupa as a toddler. He becomes praetor of the Twelfth Legion, loses his memory to Hera in *The Lost Hero*, and dies in *The Burning Maze*. His canon weapon is Ivlivs, an Imperial gold coin that becomes a gladius or a lance. None of the Hera amnesia plot has happened here.

**What he knows:** Percy's claims (citizenship in Legio XXI Rapax, a water-formed Seal of Neptune that Reyna pronounced genuine, the Othrys warning), Percy's skill and Eleni's jargon. He has no knowledge of the future Empire. He is not shown at the Ch 22 events.

## Personality
- **Curious and good-humoured at first.** He is amazed that the wolves like Percy: "They keep trying to bite my fingers off every time I try to pet them!" (Ch 17).
- **Sceptical and a little territorial.** He asks why Percy didn't go to "your Greek base". He flatly says Legio XXI Rapax "is gone" (Ch 17).
- **Competitive and showy.** He proposes the Trial by Steel with a "sharp, almost mischievous" grin and gives himself the final bout: "And if you survive, you get me." He jumps into the arena "with a powerful bound" and makes a grandiose declaration: "I will not hold back," … "No more games. This is how it ends." Percy answers "Riiiiiight." (Ch 17–18).
- **Teasing.** "All right, Graceus. Let's see if you can back it up." ("Graceus" seems to mean *Graecus*, "Greek".) (Ch 17).
- **Temper under pressure.** As Percy outclasses him, "Jason was visibly getting angry." He yields at knife-point with a simple "Yes." (Ch 18).
- **Honest about confusion.** He admits to Eleni: "We have no idea what that means." (Ch 18).
- **A competent officer in battle.** He reads the enemy feints the same way Percy does ("For Atlas. For sure they're waiting for him to make his move") and orders the pila volley (Ch 19).

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Casual, slangy American teen ("You've got to be kidding", "thingy"), with Roman flourishes when performing. He is confident to the point of bravado.
- "You've got to be kidding. The wolves like him?" (Ch 17)
- "Even if that's true, why tell us? You could've gone to your Greek base of yours." (Ch 17)
- "That legion is gone." (Ch 17)
- "By tradition. Trial by Steel!" (Ch 17)
- "You've got nerve, I'll give you that. All right, Graceus." (Ch 17)
- "I will not hold back," … "No more games. This is how it ends." (Ch 18)
- "Could you define this… 'metaphysical pressure' thingy?" (Ch 18)
- "Front ranks," … "Pila ready." (Ch 19)

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Lightning:** His gladius is "charged with lightning that danced along the blade and licked up his forearms". He makes "lightning-augmented" swipes (Ch 18).
- **Athletic swordsmanship:** wide horizontal cleaves, overhead cuts, jumps over Percy's leg sweep (Ch 18).
- **Command:** He co-directs the Othrys operation and orders the front ranks (Ch 18–19).
- He is still beaten by a combat knife: Percy alternates grips, pommel strikes and kicks, and ends with the blade at Jason's throat.

## Backstory
Praetor alongside Reyna. Zoë's thought, "right after the Grace twins" (Ch 4 narration), dates Percy's birth relative to Jason and Thalia's, which means Zoë at least knows about Jason's existence.

## Continuity Notes
- "The Grace twins" (Ch 4) is Zoë's internal narration, not speech. In canon Thalia is about seven years older than Jason. Profiles call them siblings and quote "twins" only as Zoë's label (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C22).
- **Seal of Neptune.** Percy shapes it from water (Ch 17) and Reyna pronounces it genuine (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C16).
- His age is never given. As praetor during the Titan's Curse arc, he holds the office years earlier than in canon.
- His weapon is a "divine gladius" here, not canon's coin-sword Ivlivs.
- "Graceus" is probably a garbled *Graecus* ("Greek").
