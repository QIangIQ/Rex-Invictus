## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
There is one Labyrinth, but its **mood changes with offerings**: favorable after Percy's sacrifices in Ch3, and hostile when he "might have forgotten to appease it" in Ch21. **Daedalus** never appears. According to Chiron he has died (see Continuity).

## Appearance
- The entrance at Yancy is "An inconspicuous brick, inscribed with a neon-blue delta symbol," in a side passage of the boiler room (Ch3). The Ekdromoi summon "a shimmering blue Delta symbol" to get in (Ch20).
- Inside: twisting passages, walls that "seemed to mold and grow colorful," and dust or mist in the air (Ch3).
- **The starry shortcut (Ch3):** "Its walls shimmered like the night sky, constellations etched in stone," and it smells of pine (Percy wonders whether it runs near Pan's domain). It comes out in the basement of an old stone church in Yosemite.
- **Ch21:** "backroom-like hallways," fractured floors over bottomless drops, intersections "that had not been there a moment before." Merek says it feels like "the bowels of a living machine."

## Personality
Alive, shifting and transactional. It "had always whispered its secrets," it "might not side with" a demigod, and it can "path a horde of monsters" toward anyone who displeases it (Ch3). It "pulse[s] with an approving beat" when given an offering. It holds grudges: "Daedalus' creation did not forgive easily" (Ch21). It also shows visions of other places and times (Ch21).

## Voice & Speech Patterns
It has only one spoken line: clipped, broken-grammar demand speech carried on the wind. Lines about it are included below.
- "Provide offering, demigod. Then, I guide to location" (the Labyrinth, Ch3)
- "I shall dedicate this death and its spoils to the Labyrinth of Daedalus" (Percy's formula, Ch3)
- "That is deeply concerning. The Labyrinth should not be accessible so easily. It has been unstable since Daedalus' passing." (Chiron, Ch7)
- "This place feels worse than you described, Milord." (Eleni, Ch21)
- "Feels like we're moving through the bowels of a living machine," / "Not a metaphor." (Merek and Neumond, Ch21)

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- Creates shortcuts, exits and "swift passage" in exchange for sacrifices (Ch3).
- Absorbs the essence of offerings: "merging them into the essence of the maze" (Ch3).
- Can turn hostile. Minerva warns it might "fold you into a decorative wall feature" (Ch21).
- After a blood offering and an invocation "in a dialect of Greek that predated Athens," it gives "only a single corridor" with no maze to solve (Ch21).
- Shows visions. In Ch21 a wall flickers to a fallout bunker where "a broad, heavy shape in a rust-streaked bull's-skull helmet" with a sledgehammer chases three armed men, bellowing "УБЕЙ УБЕЙ УБЕЙ!" (Russian for kill, kill, kill; a translation, not a quote). Minerva notes that "the man bearing the axe" is wearing "some variant of the Crye Prescision [sic] AVS plate carrier" and promises to "request someone to deal with it." The narration gave the brute a sledgehammer, and the three fleeing men also wear plate carriers, so who wears the carrier is ambiguous (see Continuity). This is probably a nod to the *Escape from Tarkov* boss Tagilla; the text does not confirm it.
- Lets non-Greeks through. The future Ekdromoi open it with a Hecate rune array and the incantation "This land is not of mine, and I shall feed the thirsty earth with my flesh" (Ch20).
- Keeps demons out (Ch3).

## Backstory
Built by Daedalus.
- **Author's vault note (not yet in text):** the vault describes the Labyrinth as a transport hub connecting to Olympus and Camp Half-Blood, with an entrance at **Zeus's Fist** (the text's Ch22 entrance). Its `Daedalus.md` (a canon-baseline note) makes Daedalus a son of Athena, hunted by Hades, with the alternate form Quintus, and lists him as "previously hunted by" Luke, Percy, Clarisse, Ethan Nakamura and Nico, the canon *Battle of the Labyrinth* hunt.

*Canon baseline (not story):* in *The Battle of the Labyrinth*, the maze is tied to Daedalus's life force and collapses when he dies at the end of that book.

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch3:** Percy finds the boiler-room entrance and spies on a monster camp: a cyclops, hellhounds and a dracaena planning to attack "a few demigods and a satyr" (see `I09_Nemean_Lion_and_Monsters.md`). The Labyrinth demands an offering. Percy kills the camp and burns the survivors alive with three dedications: for safe passage, an exit at Yosemite, and swift passage. It opens the starry tunnel. He flees back inside when the demon strikes, since demons cannot follow him there.
- **Ch7:** Percy tells Chiron about the entrance and the offering. Chiron is alarmed.
- **Ch20:** The Ekdromoi conjure a Delta entrance on the steppe and head for "Obscurus One."
- **Ch21:** Percy and Eleni walk to Alaska ("land beyond the gods") through a resentful Labyrinth. The Ekdromoi cross separately ("Route complete in 12 hours on the nose") and come out in Alaska too. After the firefight, Percy pays in blood. He gets a single corridor to Camp Half-Blood, sees the bunker vision on the way, and says "Add it to the list."
- **Ch22:** Percy returns through "the Labyrinth entrance at Zeus's Fist." The Roman cohorts march through it from New Rome "two hours later."

## Continuity Notes
- Chiron speaks of "Daedalus' passing" (Ch7). In canon Daedalus is still alive at this point. The vault still lists Daedalus as "previously hunted by" Luke, Percy, Clarisse, Ethan and Nico, so treat his "passing" as uncertain.
- **School name:** Ch7's location ("a maintenance corridor under the school… tucked behind the boiler room") matches Ch3's boiler-room entrance. Only the school's name differs: Ch3 is set at **Yancy Academy**, Percy's Ch7 account says "it started at Goode High", and by Ch15 he really is at Goode High. Ch7 is either a slip or an anticipation.
- **The vision brute's weapon:** the narration gives him "a sledgehammer the size of a mailbox" (Ch21), but Minerva then speaks of "the man bearing the axe". This is a story-level slip flagged for the author (`00a_CANON_RULINGS.md` §5). "Crye Prescision" is the text's spelling [sic].
- Percy says the Ch3 offerings "felt right" and that he didn't know how he knew to make them. That is a cover story.
