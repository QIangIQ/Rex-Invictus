## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
A far-future-only character. She is one of the "few of the little ones" who heard the Watchpoint verdict naming the Lord Imperator, which makes her knowledge sealed under Directorate authority. She stands between the Bulwarks (`H06_Bulwarks_and_Spears.md`) beside Aphrodite's throne (`E05_Aphrodite_Future_Mother_Majesty.md`).

## Appearance
She wears the Rose Gallery officers' uniform: pure white without stain or crease, the crest of the Dove's Fleet on one shoulder and the Imperial eagle on the other. No other physical description is given.

## Personality
- **Careful and correct.** She speaks "carefully" when she corrects the goddess: "Your Majesty … Approximately four million people."
- **Dutiful to a fault.** Her refrain is "Yes, Your Majesty", which she says "very quickly" when ordered to have monsters erased.
- **A logistics mind.** She delivers exact numbers on request, because Aphrodite "love[s] it when you do the numbers".
- **Respectfully frank.** She asks "May I ask why so many?" and observes, "That is more scientists than most sector fleets have soldiers."
- **Shaken by the verdict.** She "stood frozen at her station" when it was read.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Formal, precise, deferential. She always says "Your Majesty". She talks in counts and inventories.
- "Your Majesty … Approximately four million people." (Ch 23)
- "Yes, Your Majesty." (Ch 23, repeatedly)
- "The detachment is on station, Your Majesty. Thirty-eight thousand corvettes in the picket." (Ch 23)
- "That is more scientists than most sector fleets have soldiers." (Ch 23)
- "May I ask why so many, Your Majesty?" (Ch 23)

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
Strategic coordination of the Dove's Detachment. Her report lists 38,000 Vanguard-pattern corvettes; 11,000 Mastodon light frigates; 400 Hammerheads; 250 AnnChase cruisers; 40 Perseus-pattern dreadnoughts; the Rosed Knights Titan legion under Commander Thorne; the *Sovereign's Glory* and the *Flame of Atrox* on the flanks; and 12,000 survey squads (the Institute Academy's chronometric faculty, Tochter des Elysium's reality-mapping division, Hecatean augurs, Athenian archivists, Perioeci townships and 4,000 Apollonian medics). She opens the bays and dispatches the scholars.

## Backstory
Not given in the text, beyond her long service among the navy's best officers, who were "chosen for this ship from the whole of the navy".
- Author's vault note (not yet in text): in the War of Princes (16212–16227 per the vault), Valeria Sevrin served as Fleet Commander Class II (Strategic Coordination) in the UOE Vanguard Fleet Alpha, alongside the *Aphrodite's Grace*. That war ended about four years before the Ch 23 shear (vault dating).

## Story Arc So Far
**Ch 23:** She greets Aphrodite's arrival in the Rose Gallery and corrects the goddess's tactless remark. Aphrodite says "Val-Val, remind me not to say silly things" and then predicts "You won't, though. You never do." Sevrin reports the numbers, questions the scholar count, opens the bays and relays the order to erase any monster near the scholars. She is frozen at the verdict.

## Relationships

- **Commander Thorne** (`H05_Commander_Thorne_and_Rosed_Knights.md`): she relays the erase order to him.

## Secrets, Mysteries & Open Threads

## Continuity Notes
- "Val-Val" is only ever used by Aphrodite.
- **Ship name:** the text's *Flame of Atrox* (Ch 23, holding at Aphrodite's flank) may be the author's *Flame of Ambition*, Michael Black's ship in the vault's War of Princes notes. Keep the text spelling; the identity is unconfirmed. Its ship class is not stated in the text.
- **Rank:** the text calls her only "Fleet Commander Valeria Sevrin of Strategic Coordination"; "Class II" is vault-only.
- **Four million:** the people she counts are Marien Pyrrhus's Reserve Echelon, lost in the shear (a sphere eleven kilometres in radius); see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C7 and C27.
