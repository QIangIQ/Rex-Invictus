## Backstory
As the gods prepared "their newest cadre of demigods for interstellar travel", Rammael set foot on **Cyrax** in the Sirius system. Poseidon laughed triumphantly on his azure throne. Percy "had never seen such joy and genuine pride from every major Olympian before", and "seeded a small abyss of jealousy and hatred". Percy, bound to guard Earth, reasoned that if not his brother then "one of his cousins would have assumed the mantle anyways".

- Author's vault note (not yet in text): the vault's `Rammael Marccusson` note lists him as a "Demigod of Poseidon" in the Greek pantheon. The author's ASSETS list spells him "Ramael Marcusson" and notes "Norse roots", a planned idea (author intent) that would explain the Scandinavian-style "-sson" surname.

## Story Arc So Far

## Continuity Notes
- The patronymic "Marccusson" (literally Marcus's son) conflicts with his being Poseidon's son. It may refer to a mortal stepfather or be a naming convention; it is unexplained in the text. The author's ASSETS note ("Norse roots") suggests a planned Norse ancestry.
- **Spelling:** the text has "Rammael Marccusson" (Ch1); the vault ASSETS list has "Ramael Marcusson". Use the text spelling.
- **Chronology:** the Cyrax landing belongs to Percy's long old-timeline career, before the gods' departure from Earth (the Abandonment, year 3045 per the vault; see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R4).
