## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
Only one Lord Varro exists. Do not confuse him with **Archmagister Cael Varros** of Quarterdeck 19 (Ch14), a different logistics officer whose name is similar; no link is stated (see `H15_Arenbergs_and_Luna_Secundus.md`). In Ch10 he deals with the **future-era Minerva in Percy's clone body** (`B02_Minerva_Percy_Clone_Interlude.md`), not with present-day Percy.

## Appearance
- **Ch10:** a pristine uniform "bore none of the wear typical of field command"; "delicate hands" suited to paperwork.
- **Ch23:** "had very clean hands". He works in a records hall on his barge with forty clerks at forty terminals.

## Personality
- **Bureaucratic self-importance, quickly punctured (Ch10).** He asserts jurisdiction over the "unregistered entity", then caves when Minerva threatens to turn one of "the forty two nearby Zeus-Wrath hunter-killer satellites" on the fortress-planet: "Where do you want to go?"
- **Self-aware and unbothered (Ch23).** He knows soldiers despise him and "did not particularly care. Somebody had to count the dead."
- **Arithmetic reflex.** "Varro did the arithmetic in his head, because he always did."
- **Quiet dignity.** He accepts Aphrodite's request to read all the names aloud: "Very well," … "Tell Her Majesty that the Legio Administratoris will begin at once."

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Formal and procedural. He is defensive when challenged and soft-spoken in grief.

## Backstory
He is a career administrator with no combat experience ("never held a rifle or dug a trench").

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch10.** He interrogates Minerva-in-Percy's-clone in a sterile room at the Apollonian Hospitalier Complex. She hacks the facility, finds PROJECT ARTEMIS and threatens to turn one of forty-two Zeus-Wrath hunter-killer satellites on the fortress-planet. He asks "Where do you want to go?" but says her destination, the Nereid Expanse, is "beyond my authorization level". Minerva answers "That is not my problem", walks out regardless, and commandeers the facility's transit and the AnnChase-Pattern battlecruiser squadron herself.
- **Ch22 (referenced).** Minerva notes that the shear casualties' names "were documented by Lord Varro".
- **Ch23.** He processes the KIA list for the Pyrrhuiesian Reserve Echelon: 3,998,050, including Marien Pyrrhus. Michael Black, Aphrodite's Selenide Bulwark, brings her request that every name be read aloud. Varro objects ("Bulwark, with respect. There are four million names.") and calculates about ninety-two days; Black answers that Aphrodite "has placed a hundred of her own choristers at your disposal". When the recovered verdict proves the echelon alive, he stares at Marien's name. He receives the Director's order: "AMENDED TO: MISSING IN ACTION. LEAVE SPACE ON WALL."

## Continuity Notes
- **Spelling** (see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md S10): "Legio Administratoris" in prose. The text spells it "Adminstratoris" in Ch10 and Ch22 (kept inside quotes with [sic]) and "Administratoris" in Ch23.
- **Not Cael Varros** (see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md S6): his name resembles that of Archmagister Cael Varros (Ch14), a different person.
- **Story-level slip** (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md §5): in present-day Ch22, Minerva relays "Sister's records" stating that the shear dead "were documented by Lord Varro". Ch23 shows that documentation happening in the future after the shear, and the Mars Watchpoint has been cut off since the shear, so it should have no access to post-shear records.
- **Shear** (see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C7, C27): a sphere eleven kilometres in radius; it swallowed the Pyrrhuiesian Reserve Echelon (3,998,050 on his list), not Percy.
