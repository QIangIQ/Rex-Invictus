## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- A far-future-only figure. Do NOT confuse him with **Dr. Thorn**, the present-day Manticore (`I06_Dr_Thorn_Manticore.md`). The names are nearly identical but the two are unrelated.
- **Andros** the Titan pilot is distinct from **Andros Melinos** and **Andros Melinos the Younger**, names on the dead/missing list of the lost echelon (Ch 23).
- The Rosed Knights are a different force from the Titans of Task Force ORPHEUS in the present-day timeline (Keyes's 11 m Titan, Ch 22, `G14_Sciritae_Neodamodes_and_Ch22_Units.md`), though they are the same class of 11-metre war frame.

## Appearance
- **Thorne:** heard only on the net. His voice is "deep, amused and utterly unafraid", "cheerful and casual".
- **The Rosed Knights:** "forty eleven-meter frames in gold, pink, red, silver, and teal", "embedded with miniature garnets, pearls, and turquoise". They drop "in drop cradles that split open at a thousand meters" and fight with lit energy blades.

## Personality
Thorne is breezy, swaggering, artistic about violence, and completely calm, with a teacherly streak ("Remember the critical anatomy lessons"). He treats killing Porphyrion like tucking in a child: "back in his bed before his bedtime story's over." He is confident enough to tease the Primus Pilus mid-crisis.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Casual and playful even on the command net. He uses "big boys", "big guy" and "say hello".
- "Fulminata, this is Thorne. The Rosed Knights got eyes on him." (Ch 23)
- "Hold your line. The big boys are about to say hello." (Ch 23)
- "Hey … Don't rush art." (Ch 23)
- "Rosed Knights, go for the neck." (Ch 23)
- "Remember the critical anatomy lessons." (Ch 23)
- "Let's get this big guy back in his bed before his bedtime story's over." (Ch 23)
- "Left arm, copy." (Ch 23; unattributed, presumably Sophia or her file). The legion acknowledges with "Copy that, Commander."

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
Forty Titan frames with energy blades that cut through Porphyrion's scales; orbital drop cradles; coordinated assault by files (arms, neck). One Titan was crushed "like a tin can" in the Giant's fist, one hurled 300 m onto the glass, and one flung into a Perioeci station.

## Backstory
Aphrodite's personal Titan legion.
- Author's vault note (not yet in text): the vault's War of Princes notes list **Xander Thorne** as Naval Commander Class I (Titan Force) and **Sophia Vael** as Naval Commander Class III (Titan Force), both in Aphrodite's UOE fleet. The war ran 16212–16227 per the vault, ending about four years before the Ch 23 shear. The Ch 23 "Commander Thorne" is very probably Xander Thorne, but the text never gives his first name.
- On Aphrodite's orders, Sevrin relays to Thorne that any monster coming within a kilometre of the scholars must be erased "so thoroughly that Tartarus forgets how to make it."

## Story Arc So Far
**Ch 23:** The legion drops to reinforce the Fulminata. When Porphyrion rises, Tullia calls for support. Thorne answers at once, waits through the dreadnought and cruiser bombardment, then tells the impatient Primus Pilus, "Don't rush art." The Titans land directly on the Giant, drive him to his knees and cut at the neck and arms. They jump clear as the *Sovereign's Glory* presses him into the ground. Callidora: "Rosed Knights, well done."
