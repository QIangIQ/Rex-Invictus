# Reyna Avila Ramírez-Arellano — Praetor of the Legio XII Fulminata (Present-day timeline)
> A dry, iron-disciplined praetor who let a Greek stranger talk his way into New Rome, marched her legion on Othrys on his word, lost forty-four legionaries to an enemy that fired from space, and still stood nose to nose with that enemy's centurion and refused to lower her hand first.

## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **This file:** the present-day praetor of Camp Jupiter.
- **Her legion's future:** In the far future, the Legio XII Fulminata is still alive as "Heavy Tartarean front legion. The Thunderbolts. Best shield wall in the Empire" (Ashmead, Ch 22). Its Primus Pilus in the Interlude is Tullia Ardens (`H01_Tullia_Ardens.md`), with signifer Lucan Corvo (`H02_Lucan_Corvo.md`). Reyna has no far-future self in the text, and no Imperial character mentions her personally.
- **Canon baseline (labelled canon, not story):** In the *Heroes of Olympus* books, Reyna is the daughter of Bellona, a former servant on Circe's island with her sister Hylla, and co-praetor with Jason. Her metal dogs Aurum and Argentum can sense lies. In canon, Camp Jupiter's existence was kept secret from the Greeks until *The Son of Neptune*. This AU puts her as praetor years early and has Percy reveal Camp Jupiter's link to the Greeks during the Titan's Curse arc.

**What she knows:**
- Percy claims Roman citizenship in a dead legion (Legio XXI Rapax), produces a water-formed "Sigillum Imperii Neptuni" that she pronounces genuine, and is a frighteningly skilled duellist.
- Eleni speaks incomprehensible future-military jargon.
- By the end of Ch 22: the enemy came "From Mars". They are soldiers from the future Empire. Her legion survives into their era. Their computer has named Percy "Lord Imperator" ("we've been shooting at the Emperor").
- She does **not** know about Percy's reincarnation details, Minerva's nature beyond "the computer", Artemis's bond or the Apostolos title, unless she was told off-page.

## Personality
- **Disciplined, controlled and precise.** At Othrys, "Reyna's voice carried, calm and precise." She halts five cohorts with a raised fist "in perfect sequence" and gives clipped corrections ("Adjust spacing… Closer ranks.") (Ch 19).
- **Sceptical but fair.** She doesn't take Percy's word, but she accepts evidence: the seal is genuine ("No idiot dares to forge a sigil") and her wolves have accepted him. She sets terms rather than refusing outright: "Then prove you're worth listening to" (Ch 17).
- **Rule-conscious.** Her one condition for the Trial by Steel is "No divine interference." Win, "and the Senate will consider your proposal" (Ch 17). She does not promise more than her office can give.
- **Proud of her command, quietly.** "Reyna seated herself with crisp authority" at the feast (Ch 18).
- **Dry wit under pressure.** She deadpans her way through the Mars revelation ("From Mars." / "The planet." / "Named for our patron.") (Ch 22).
- **Diplomatically helpful.** She tries to translate Eleni's jargon for her own officers ("I believe she means… fire the ballistae in order"), then apologises to Percy on Eleni's behalf: "She means we need you. I think." (Ch 18).
- **Corrects outsiders.** She tells Eleni flatly: "Mars' demigods and legacies don't have divine abilities." (Ch 19).
- **Firm with her own people.** "Gwen," … "Not now." (Ch 22).
- **Grieving but steady.**
  - After the Psiloi volley kills 41 of her Fifth Cohort, she meets the enemy centurion on the beach "Her voice was flat." She will not be the first to lower her hand.
  - "Something flickered across Reyna's face" when told her legion survives into the future. Her first response is "You're lying."
  - After the battle she writes the names of all forty-four dead legionaries on a wax tablet (Ch 22).
- **Brave and loyal to allies in battle.** She digs the wounded Imperial centurion Ashmead out from under a dissolved hellhound, though Imperial fire (the Psiloi volley) killed forty-one of her soldiers hours earlier (Ch 22).
- **A blind spot or weakness:** Percy's look at the feast silences her: "Reyna started to speak, but the look on his face silenced even her" (Ch 18). She is not used to being overruled by a guest.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Clipped, formal, Roman-military. She favours short declaratives and imperatives, rarely jokes except in the dry register, and uses titles and full names in formal moments ("Praetor Reyna Avila Ramírez-Arellano. Twelfth Legion Fulminata."). She addresses Percy as "Jackson" and Eleni as "Lady Theodosia". There is no profanity on page.

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Command:** She runs five cohorts in testudo, controls their spacing under Titan-warped gravity and coordinates artillery (scorpiones and ballistae) (Ch 19). She brings the First, Second and Fifth cohorts through the Labyrinth to Long Island, leaving the rest "holding Camp Jupiter in case this is a feint" (Ch 22).
- **Aurum and Argentum:** gold and silver metal wolves/dogs. They don't leave her side without orders, but they bow to Percy in recognition and follow him "like they'd found a new master" (Ch 17–18). In canon the dogs detect lies; the story only implies it.
- **Personal combat:** Not shown in detail. She digs Ashmead out and fights on the beach (Ch 22).
- **Golden Standard:** Ashmead, dying, tells her "You gotta use the Golden Standard's ability next time" (Ch 22). This implies her legion's standard (the canon golden eagle) has a power she did not use. The ability is unexplained.
- No personal divine powers are shown on page.

## Backstory
- She holds a praetorship alongside Jason Grace and convenes with him "in closed session" at the curia praetoria (Ch 17).
- She has a working relationship with Lupa and the Roman Senate. Lupa can outvote the Senate (Ch 22).
- Chiron had an "old favor" to call in with Camp Jupiter (Ch 22), which means the camps have some contact in this AU.

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch 17:**
  - Percy, Eleni, Zoë, Bianca and Grover come to New Rome and ask for "Reyna Ramírez-Arellano. Or, Jason Grace." Her wolves find Percy first and bow to him.
  - Reyna arrives with Jason, whistling for "Aurum! Argentum!" She demands an explanation and pronounces his water-formed Seal of Neptune genuine ("No idiot dares to forge a sigil").
  - Percy warns that the Titans are regrouping at Othrys and claims citizenship in Legio XXI Rapax. She challenges him to prove himself. Jason proposes a Trial by Steel, and Reyna sets the conditions.
- **Ch 18:**
  - Reyna watches from the praetorian box as Percy beats Decimus (sabre and cloak), Marcus (claymore against halberd) and Jason (combat knife).
  - At the feast she presides with "crisp authority". Percy's look silences her when he leaves abruptly to answer Artemis's agony.
  - At the Othrys command tent she and Jason are "officially directing the operation", but Eleni takes over. Reyna tries to translate "AtRep Formation Protocol Two Theta" and apologises: "She means we need you. I think."
- **Ch 19:**
  - She commands the assault on Othrys: testudo against boulder fire, "Ad vīam", spacing corrections on the warped shelf.
  - She corrects Eleni about Mars's legacies having no powers.
  - When Atlas blows up the mountain, Artemis (drawing power through Percy) teleports the whole legion about 20 km clear, and it lands "staggering and shouting".
- **Ch 22:**
  - Chiron calls in a favor. Lupa outvotes the Senate, and Reyna brings three cohorts to Camp Half-Blood via the Labyrinth at Zeus's Fist. She learns the enemy comes from Mars and silences Gwen.
  - The legion forms a red-and-gold shield wall on the eastern slope. Sabine Ro's Sciritae mark it "priority two". The Psiloi volley from the gunship *Longsight*, 96 km up, kills forty-one of the Fifth Cohort in ninety seconds, including Gwen.
  - The surviving Romans learn "their lesson about formation". They spread out into cover and meet Ashmead's Neodamodes on the southern beach. The standoff: hands raised, a hundred rifles against fifty spears. Ashmead says her legion survives into his era. Then CODE 18 plays from every Neodamode speaker, Ashmead says "we've been shooting at the Emperor," and both sides lower their weapons.
  - Romans and Neodamodes pull survivors out of Lander Three together. Against Luke's monster wave, "The Romans were in the center, shields locked," with two Neodamodes in every gap.
  - At dawn a hellhound crushes Ashmead in the gap by the Fifth Cohort. Reyna digs him out, and he tells her about the Golden Standard.
  - At the sunset council (arm in sling) she is present when the Anabasis Accord is agreed "in the presence of the Legio XII Fulminata". Article VI names her legion "the military partner of the Command upon Earth", with joint operations "under Roman and Imperial officers together".
  - Percy sits with her while she writes her 44 dead on a wax tablet. In the final scene Artemis invokes Article VI for a joint operation to Alaska. Reyna's personal role in it is not yet shown.

## Continuity Notes
- Her name is "Reyna Ramírez-Arellano" in Ch 17 and "Reyna Avila Ramírez-Arellano" in Ch 22 (the latter matches canon).
- Aurum and Argentum are "metallic wolves" in Ch 17–18 but "two metal dogs" in Ch 22. They are dogs (greyhounds) in canon.
- Dead count: the Fifth Cohort lost "forty-one legionaries in ninety seconds". The council total for the whole legion is "44 DEAD. 38 WOUNDED"; the extra three presumably died later.
- She is praetor during the Titan's Curse arc, years earlier than in canon.
- **Seal of Neptune.** Percy shapes the "Sigillum Imperii Neptuni" from water (Ch 17:133) and Reyna pronounces it genuine (Ch 17). Profiles call it neither simply "genuine" nor simply "forged" (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C16).
- Ashmead's estimate of her age ("about seventeen") is the only age given.
