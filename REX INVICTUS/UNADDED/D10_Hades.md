## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **Present-day Hades (main subject):** first sends a skeletal courier ("I am listening. Do not fill my halls carelessly"), then attends the Ch22 sunset council "standing in the shadows by the door in a cloak that seemed to drink the light".
- **Old timeline (Ch1):** only a concept. Lord-Commander Percy longs for "the blessed land of Hades" after millennia of guard duty. See `A01_Percy_LordCommander_OldTimeline.md`.
- **Far-future Empire:** Hades and "the Director" (future Artemis) are "almost permanently helping us hold the containment lines" of the Tartarean Front (Merek, Ch22). The Empire has a location called "Watchpoint Hades, Depth 3", which houses the AtRep locus (Ch14). Castellan Oriel is "a daughter of a Hades line" (Ch22). No separate roster file covers future Hades. See `00_START_HERE_Version_Guide_and_World_Primer.md` and `99_Minor_Characters_Compendium.md`.
- **Knowledge boundary:** He knows what reached his halls and what the Ch22 council said. He has met 406 Imperial souls who speak "a Greek I have never heard". He does not know his future role on the Tartarean Front.

## Appearance
A cloak "that seemed to drink the light". "Dark eyes". When he steps forward "the room got colder" (Ch22).

## Personality
- **Aloof and watchful.** Doesn't attend the first Ch22 council. He sends one terse line.
- **Grave, quietly compassionate.** He describes the Imperial dead with real feeling: "They are frightened, Commander. They have no coin for the ferry and no temple that knows their names."
- **Decisive.** No debate. He simply says, "Then I will build them one."
- **Possessive of his halls.** "Do not fill my halls carelessly."

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Few words, slow and weighty. He speaks of souls with dignity. No profanity.
- "I am listening. Do not fill my halls carelessly." (Ch22, courier message)
- "Four hundred and six souls came into my halls last night speaking a Greek I have never heard." (Ch22)
- "They are standing on my shore in the dark, and they will not stop asking for their Wall." (Ch22)
- "Then I will build them one. On the shore of the Styx." (Ch22)

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- Rule over the dead and the Styx shore, and authority over Charon. Skeletal couriers.
- Demons regard Cyclops eyes as treasures held mostly in "the greedy clutches" of Hades (Ch4).

## Backstory
Canon: Big Three brother of Zeus and Poseidon. Father of Bianca and Nico di Angelo (Bianca is introduced in Rome as "daughter of Pluto", Ch17).

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch3–4, 6:** Oaths and references ("Oh Hades"; "you would be in Hades' realm"). A demon praises "the beneficent Lord Hades".
- **Ch22 (first council):** Absent. Sends his courier.
- **Ch22 (sunset council):** After Athena reads Article VIII, he steps out of the shadows and describes the 406 Imperial dead on the Styx shore asking Charon for "a… Wall". Marien explains the Phlegethon Wall, where the Empire keeps the names of the dead. Hades vows to build one and keep their names "until you take them home".

## Continuity Notes
- The board counts 406 ORPHEUS dead. Hades also says "Four hundred and six". This is consistent, and 406 is the final figure in the author's reconciliation (Zeus 140, the CODE 2 spoof 118, drowned Peltasts 71, ≈340 mid-battle, 406 final; 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C8). Luke's "four hundred" is rounding.
- Asmodeus calls Hades "the Sovereign of the Abyss" (Ch4). In the author's vault *Sovereign of the Abyss* is the Divine Being title of **Tartarus**, not Hades. Treat Asmodeus's phrase as a demon's flattery, not as Hades's title.
- The Divine Being title *Divine Arbiter* is vault-only (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C32).
- Bianca di Angelo is alive in this story; the vault's "deceased" status is copied from the original books (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C21).
- "Watchpoint Hades" (Ch14) is a place name, not the god.
- Present-day year: c. 2021 per the author's timeline, nominally the canon PJO era (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R2).
