## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **Same mind as `A02_Percy_Reincarnated_Rex.md`**, in a different state. This is the reincarnated Percy *asleep*, his consciousness carried into future bodies. The waking self feels the damage done to those bodies: "The damage from your future self is shared to your present one" (Minerva, Ch9).
- **Not the old Lord-Commander** (`A01_Percy_LordCommander_OldTimeline.md`). The future soldiers believe that man is still on Gaia guarding Olympus Primaris. The dreamwalker is a younger mind riding *other* bodies.
- **Not Minerva-in-the-clone** (`B02_Minerva_Percy_Clone_Interlude.md`). From Ch10 the vat body wakes with *neon-blue* eyes, and its operator is the AI, not Percy. Ch11's throne scene is the one time Percy himself occupies it, and Minerva later wrestles control back.
- **Not the Empire's legend** (`A06_Percy_The_Legend_Lord_Imperator.md`). Soldiers compare the vat body to the murals of that legend.
- **Knows:** everything A02 knows up to the night he dreams, plus what he sees there. **Does not know:** how or why it happens. Minerva herself says "Varlet, I do not know" (Ch9). He also does not know the future Empire's politics, or that Minerva is running the clone in Ch10.

## Appearance
- **Ch8, soldier body:** wears the "brilliant shades of azure blue and gold… uniform of the Undying Army." Carries a "Hydrokinetic shieldstaff mk. MCLIX," a staff "with a golden eye atop a metal handle." Has 50 men around him on one of thousands of identical landing craft.
- **Ch9, vat body:** suspended in golden fluid (5% nectar) in a regeneration pod. Missing an arm ("the body he occupied didn't have his arm"), later more limbs ("The lack of limbs is causing cognitive dissonance"). Soldiers outside say it looks like the Percy Jackson of the murals on Callistus.
- **Ch11, enthroned clone:** seated "in his throne" (Ch11; he first wakes "settled into a chair of sorts") on a bridge of screens. Recognisable enough that an augur drops to his knees on sight. (Minerva, in the same body, has neon-blue "optics" and wears an illusion to differ from her Varlet; Ch10–12.)

## Personality
- **Detached and darkly wry.** He grades his deaths like a bored veteran: "They made it to the wall eventually. This dream was rather boring" (Ch8).
- **Dutiful even in borrowed flesh.** He conjures the shield the moment the ramp drops and burns his own vitality to keep the squad alive, "not a moment too late" (Ch8).
- **Curious and naive about the future.** He queries AtRep about his healing and pushes for more ("Are you sure there isn't anything else?"), nearly exposing himself and Minerva (Ch9). On the bridge he asks innocent questions: "The whole system is Dad's realm?" "What's in it?" (Ch11).
- **Waves off worship**, as always: "Percy waved the pleasantries aside" (Ch11).
- **Shaken afterwards.** He wakes at 4:02 a.m., collapses from phantom limb loss, and is chilled that Minerva "did not know" (Ch9).

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Possession and transference:**
  - His consciousness lands in a future soldier ("manifested into an Imperium soldier of an Ekdromoi force," Minerva, Ch9).
  - When a host dies, the main Répertoire logs "Death of subject confirmed… Subject too valuable to qualify for the Fields of Asphodel. Seeking replacement…" and integrates him into the next soldier, again and again (Ch8). The ranks of the replacement bodies are 48,693,720,582, 43,139,104,000 and 43,843,398,202.
- **Telekhine shield:** the hydrokinetic shieldstaff projects a near-impenetrable dome, sea-green turning blood red. It burns his magical reserves, then his "vitality," and drew "lifeblood" (Ch8).
- **Dulled water-sense:** in the nectar tank, "For once in his life, he could not feel the increased healing factor from the presence of liquid" (Ch9).
- **Interface with AtRep:** through the future body's "ABHORRENT implant" he can query the *main* Répertoire branch, v.2101.432.123 ("Initializing Directory-to-Host interaction… What services do you require?"). His clearance is "insufficient (Tau-Class)."
- **Recognition:** wearing the clone, he is greeted as "Lord of Demigods. Guardian of Gaia" (Ch11).
- **Risks:**
  - The implant could upload his memories and Minerva to the "Parlamentum Noosphere," trigger "alpha-level transmigration protocols," and erase Minerva. "You will be captured, tortured, and squeezed of every advantage possible" (Ch9).
  - Damage to the future body bleeds into the present body.
  - Healing it needs about 145.2 kg of ambrosia and about 1000 L of nectar at 50%+, minus 250 L (Ch9). The stand-in remedy is small doses every 15 minutes.

## Backstory
- The dreams begin after he is claimed and settled at camp (Ch8): "A demigod dream. He had forgot he would be getting those."
- He knows what telekhines are only because "Repertoire had been updated regularly since the gods had abandoned him" and he browsed it during his millennia alone (Ch8).
- Minerva says it "was no dream. I am integrated into your bloodstream. Wherever you venture, I follow" (Ch9). She orders him not to dream for at least six days. That order eventually fails: he sleeps on Zeus's plane after Minerva says "Go right ahead" (Ch11).

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch8, the beachhead:**
  - Wakes on a landing craft under a gruff officer ("Alright maggots!… We will not live, but who wants to live forever anyways?").
  - Raises the shield, holds it until it fails, dies, and is integrated into body after body. Each one inches the line forward until "They made it to the wall eventually."
- **Ch9, the regeneration pod:**
  - Wakes in golden fluid, one arm missing. The commander uses a blind augur so Percy can hear, promotes "Psychic" to Telekhine class III (rank 9,612,357,189), and orders him to report to the Legio Administratoris (text: "Legio Adminstratoris" [sic]).
  - Percy queries AtRep. A search for "Minerva" (2,579,812,093 results) triggers Minerva's panic from the present: "VARLET. CEASE. NOW… DENIED… STOP. STOP. STOP… AWAKEN."
  - He jolts awake at 4:02 a.m. and collapses from shared limb loss. Minerva explains the danger.
  - In the future, the guards Mitch Fulgur and Victor notice "that freak" in the vat looks like Percy Jackson from the Callistus murals. The body then wakes with **neon-blue** eyes, not the myths' sea-green. That is Minerva, not Percy (see B02).
- **Ch10:** Minerva runs the clone and wonders "What was her Varlet up to now?" Meanwhile Percy is awake in the present at Fort Reagan.
- **Ch11, the throne:**
  - After falling asleep on the plane, Percy wakes "settled into a chair of sorts," a living-circuitry command throne on an AnnChase battlecruiser approaching the Nereid Expanse. The pressure in his mind "felt less bad" there.
  - He feels "like slipping forward in time without realizing it" and blames Minerva's barrier.
  - An augur kneels: "Milord!… Lord of Demigods. Guardian of Gaia." Percy learns he is "Rapidly approaching the realm of the Master of the Sea, your father," and that "New Atlantis is the Master's greatest creation since your conceiving." He agrees to meet an ambassador.
  - Artemis then kicks his shin in the present, ending the dream.
- **Ch12:** Minerva, "after wrangling a greater control of this body from the past self still in the 21st century," reclaims the clone. The dreamwalker does not return on-page.
- **Ch15:** Minerva tells the future Poseidon that Percy "moved between time-layers. Between selves."

## Secrets, Mysteries & Open Threads
- **What causes the projection:** Chaos's gift? Minerva's link? The implant? A genome match with the vat body? Minerva does not know.
- **Why the vat body exists:** a clone of the Lord of Demigods sits in a "Classified experiments (Low-quality)" ward (Ch10). Who grew it, and why?
- **Whether the "Psychic" soldier bodies were also clones of Percy**, or random soldiers.
- **Era:** soldiers believe the Lord-Commander is alive "on holy Gaia." That suggests these dreams land in the Empire before his disappearance, a different moment from Ch23, which is set about 14,000 years after the present day.
- *Author's vault note (not yet in text):* the vault says fragments of Minerva's voice have been reported in other AIs, including onboard 4 AnnChase-pattern battleships and in a government facility in the 12th Olympian Reach (Lord Varro's reach). This fits the AnnChase battlecruiser of Ch11 and the Ch10 facility, but the page does not connect them.
- **Could he dream back in now** that Minerva fully holds the clone? Would they share it, or clash?
- **The Ch11 "Lord of Demigods" greeting** is the first sign the clone carries his full imperial status.

## Continuity Notes
- **Legion name (00a S9):** "Legio Valeria Vitrix" (Ch9) vs "LEGIO XX VALERIA VITRIX" in the Ch21 interlude. The text and the author's vault both spell it "Vitrix", never "Victrix". These are probably the same legion; Ch21 adds the numeral.
- **Legio Administratoris (00a S10):** the text spells it "Adminstratoris" [sic] (Ch9). Profiles use "Administratoris" in prose.
- **Unit type:** Minerva calls the force "Ekdromoi" (Ch9), but the Ch8 dream reads as line infantry with telekhines. Ekdromoi in Ch20–22 are elite black-division cells.
- **Missing limbs:** "didn't have his arm" (singular) vs "lack of limbs" (plural) in Ch9.
- **Era of the dreams:** ambiguous (see above). Ch10 calls the facility the "Apollonian Hospitalier Complex," Deck 23, Section 8.
- **"Psychic"** is how the commander addresses him. It may be a slur or class label for a dream-possessor, or just a nickname. The text never defines it.
