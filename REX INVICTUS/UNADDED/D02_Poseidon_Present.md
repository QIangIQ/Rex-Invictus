## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **This file:** the 21st-century god. He loves his son loudly, teases him about Artemis, and only gradually learns that Percy is far older than he looks (Percy: "Give or take a millenia [sic] or twenty", Ch19).
- **Not** the far-future Poseidon of the Undying Olympian Empire: "the Master" of New Atlantis in the Nereid Expanse, whose augurs serve him and whose adjutants "deal with" disloyal colonels. That Poseidon grieves Percy, calls Minerva "Impostor" and "Minnie", and tells his nephew Varric "Die well" (Ch5, 12, 13, 15). See `E02_Poseidon_Future.md`.
- **Not** the old-timeline Poseidon of Ch1, who laughed with "genuine pride" when Rammael landed on Cyrax, boarded *The Spear of Zeus*, and whose palace Typhon destroyed. Also in `E02_Poseidon_Future.md`.
- **Knowledge boundary:** By Ch22 he knows Percy carries nanomachines and an AI (Minerva), that Percy is Artemis's Apostolos, that a future Empire honours Percy as "Lord Imperator", and that Marien Pyrrhus is his: "She is mine, brother. Several thousand years removed, but mine." He does not know the full old-timeline story or Chaos, nor the Aventine bond in detail. He also does not yet know that his future self "abandoned" Percy in the Great Expansion. Minerva says this only to the future Poseidon.

## Appearance
- Ch19: forms out of a column of water in the council chamber, "trident resting casually against his shoulder". His aura is deep blue.
- Ch22: "tall and bearded and wore a fisherman's shirt and khaki shorts, which should have been absurd". He stands on the crest of a 200-metre swell. "His voice was the sea." Later he walks "up out of the surf… looking tired". He changes into a clean shirt to meet Marien, "for him… a gesture of enormous formality".
- Signs of him: sea-green fire and a green trident when he claims Percy (Ch8); the Kraken's eye in the Sea of Monsters (Ch14).

## Personality
- **Boisterous, proud and embarrassing.** He bursts in on Percy and Artemis's private moment: "Oh, this is priceless" (Ch19). He brags, "My son. Pulling a goddess. I told Zeus you had game!" He slings an arm round Percy and jokes that he "skipped like twelve tiers of emotional incompetence."
- **Dangerously calm when protective.** When Apollo calls Percy's blood "strange", he stands: "That is my son you are talking about" (Ch19). When Artemis says Percy is under her protection, "The grin fades. Just a little… 'And we are going to talk about that.'" The clash of their auras cracks marble.
- **Knows his son's tells.** "That's my 'I broke something important' face! It's always before Amphitrite blows a fuse at me" (Ch19).
- **Elemental and impersonal at full strength.** He calls the Imperial pegasi to him, and the horses come with "the joy of a child running to its father". Then he drowns 71 riders of the Peltast ala (Ch22; see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C8). He sinks the Titan by turning the field into ocean.
- **Capable of remorse.** He stands on the dock "with Keyes's blood still drying on his hands", counting the Pyrrhus soldiers he drowned. When Marien confronts him he answers simply: "I did." / "I did not know." / "I'm sorry" (Ch22).
- **A paternal disciplinarian, even to other gods.** He scolds Ares: "You were getting prayed to, nephew… Go and think about why" (Ch22).
- **Gentle with the defeated.** He carries the injured Titan pilot Keyes "the way a man lifts a wet kitten" to the infirmary.
- **Attentive to Percy above all.** In the Ch22 council he spends "the whole meeting watching Percy rather than the footage".
- **Pragmatic problem-solver.** He resolves Article IV by offering Triton, not Percy, as Helmsman.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Casual, warm and teasing with family. He uses modern slang with Percy ("game", "Family field trip"). He calls Percy "my boy" and "the boy", calls Zeus "brother" and Ares "nephew", and uses "Uncle" from the younger gods. In crisis he grows brief and weighty. Very mild profanity. He laughs easily.

Quotes:

- "Salt and storm. She is mine, brother. Several thousand years removed, but mine." (Ch22)

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Trident.**
- **Sea mastery:** raises a swell 200 m across; turns a dry field into ocean to swallow an 11-metre Titan; flattens the tsunami from a 0.3 kt shaped nuclear blast so it "simply lay down"; holds the Sound and the sea around Long Island (Ch22).
- **Horses:** every pegasus answers his call, Imperial mounts included. "He had never once… thought to ask where the first horse came from" (Herrman's POV).
- **Water travel and eavesdropping:** forms out of water; spies on the council through water "seeping between marble seams" (Ch19). He can mist-travel "constantly in his realm" (Ch15).
- **Blood-sense:** feels Marien Pyrrhus as his descendant when her name is spoken (Ch22). Senses Kronos's approach: "He had felt it once before, a long time ago, when his father's scythe came up out of the dark" (Ch22).
- **Watching:** has "observational instruments" over Percy, which Percy blocked with his barrier (Ch3). Would notice a hostile presence in his cabin within about 10 minutes (Minerva, Ch8).
- **Divine aura:** strong enough that clashing with Artemis's overflowed into Percy and reactivated Minerva (Ch19).

## Backstory
- Broke the oath of the Big Three to father Percy with Sally Jackson (Ch3, Ch4). He saved a college fund for Percy in an overseas account; Minerva sold it (Ch8).
- Has a temper Amphitrite fears setting off (Ch19). Triton is "my other son, the one who was born to the sea" (Ch22).
- Author's vault note (not yet in text): Triton is "First son of Poseidon and Amphitrite" and Prince of Atlantis; Kymopoleia (*Storm-Wrath of the Deep*) is listed as Percy's sister. Neither Kymopoleia nor the Prince title appears on the page.
- Percy's "Father told me about you" to Manannán mac Lir (Ch17) shows that Poseidon told Percy about other sea gods at some point. From context this was probably in the old timeline.

Author's reconciliation (vault Plot Threads; 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C8): Zeus 140, the CODE 2 spoof 118, drowned Peltasts 71, ≈340 mid-battle, 406 final.
- The Divine Being title *King of Atlantis* comes from the author's vault (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C32); on the page Zoë's narration uses the same phrase (Ch4), and Percy calls him the "Eternal Lord of Atlantis" (Ch3).
- The Ch13 line "Don't do that. You remind me of Athena too much" is Poseidon's (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C10), but it belongs to the far-future Poseidon; see `E02_Poseidon_Future.md`.
- Ch19 narration slips between past and present tense in Poseidon's scene.
- Apollo calls him "Uncle". In canon Poseidon is Apollo's uncle, so this is consistent.
- Present-day year: c. 2021 per the author's timeline, nominally the canon PJO era (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R2).
