## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
These are the story's own versions of Michael Scott's characters, transplanted into the PJO present. Neither has a future-era version. Their enemy here is **Robespierre**, standing in for Scott's Dr. John Dee (see `I05_Robespierre_and_the_Triumvir.md`).

## Appearance
- **Nicholas:** described only through manner: "calm authority," a hand that is "both grounding and commanding." His magic smells "minty."
- **Perenelle:** an "older woman" reading in her café.
- *Scent signature:* Nicholas associates his wife with "the smell of paper tomes."

## Personality
- **Nicholas** is protective (he sends his employee Josh out of danger at once), perceptive (he reads each quester by scent), guarded ("I have nothing for your kind"), and wry about the past ("Bah, bad memories of him").
- **Perenelle** is defiant and fearless. She crosses the street *into* an exploding shop, names her enemy to his face, and refuses him outright.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Nicholas speaks softly and firmly, in measured, businesslike sentences. Perenelle snaps in short, contemptuous refusals.
- "Josh," … "you're done here for now." (Nicholas, Ch17)
- "Tell my wife I sent you, with some Greek guests." (Nicholas, Ch17)
- "I have nothing for your kind. The Romani have a chokehold on your pantheon's resources here." (Nicholas, Ch17)
- "Bah, bad memories of him." (Nicholas, thought, Ch17)
- "Where is Nicholas?" (Perenelle, Ch17)
- "I wish I could say the same for you, Maximilien Robespierre of Free France." (Perenelle, Ch17)
- "You will never get it" / "Never happening!" (Perenelle, Ch17)

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Scent-sensing of auras.** Nicholas reads each quester's smell. Percy is "an overwhelming smell of the sea." Eleni is petrichor. Grover and Zoë smell of moss and dirt. Bianca smells of "Sulfur and Brimstone." Perenelle smells the brimstone from across the street.
- **Magic.** Nicholas's magic smells of mint or peppermint. Peppermint is also noted during the attack, possibly Nicholas fighting back offstage (ambiguous).
- **The Philosopher's Stone, "the Stone of Life."** They hold it, and it keeps them young. Its location is secret.
- **Supplies.** The shop sells herbs, candles, scrolls and tomes.

## Backstory
The couple "settled down in San Francisco a few years ago." Both have a history with Robespierre. Robespierre's joke about Dumbledore as a "disciple of Nick's" nods to *Harry Potter*, where Flamel is Dumbledore's partner. *Canon baseline (not story):* Nicholas Flamel (c. 1330–1418) was a real Parisian scribe, later legend made him an alchemist, and Michael Scott's books keep him and Perenelle alive into the modern age.

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch17:** Condottiero's advice is "Find the Alchemist." Eleni detects "3 neutral auras" in the city. The questers buy supplies at the Small Book Shop. Nicholas sends Josh to the café, takes over the counter, reads the group, and tells them Rome controls Greek resources there. After they leave, Perenelle smells brimstone. The shop's windows crack and explode in green and yellow smoke. She finds Robespierre among the wreckage hunting the Stone, refuses him, and he raises his glowing hands at her. The chapter cuts away.

## Relationships
