## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **Present-day Triton:** the god Poseidon volunteers in Ch22.
- **Future "Lord Regent Triton":** the Imperial title Marien knows. That implies Triton holds a regency in the far future; the text doesn't say over what. The vault gives Amphitrite command of Atlantis "as a Regent/Proxy", so "Lord Regent" most likely means regent of Atlantis (inference).
- Neither version appears on-page.

## Appearance
Not described. In canon he has a merman form with twin fish tails. Not confirmed here.

## Personality
Only Poseidon's characterisation: dutiful but grumbling. "Triton will do what I tell him," … "and he will complain about it for a thousand years." (Ch22)

## Voice & Speech Patterns
No lines. Extrapolated: proud, formal, put-upon, protective of his status as Poseidon's sea-born heir.

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Can serve as Helmsman:** the Imperial role of binding to a ship's nervous system to pilot it. Imperial Helmsmen have died doing it ("It's how two of them died… Over sixty years of service", Marien, Ch22).
- **Teacher:** to teach the Pyrrhus line, who carry Poseidon's blood, to wake their ships themselves.
- Accord Article IV limits him: "The Helmsman shall bind only to the vessels needed for the Command's supply and defense and shall not bind to any vessel of war without the leave of the Council."

## Backstory
Canon: herald of the sea, son of Poseidon and Amphitrite. In the story's future he is "Lord Regent" (Ch22).
- **Author's vault note (not yet in text):** "First son of Poseidon and Amphitrite", **Prince of Atlantis**, brother of Percy Jackson, and comrade of **Delphin** (General of Atlantis). His UOE Unified Rank is #630. The vault's Imperial Helmsmen are lobotomised direct descendants of Poseidon with limbs amputated, interred in their ships; that is the fate Artemis refuses for Percy.

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch22:** the Imperials need a Helmsman. The only son of Poseidon on Earth is Percy, and Artemis vetoes him ("Binding to a ship's nervous system… is how their Helmsmen died"). Poseidon offers Triton instead. Marien: "Lord Regent Triton would do that?" The arrangement goes into Article IV.
