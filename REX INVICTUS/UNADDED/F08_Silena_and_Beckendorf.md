## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- They are the only versions of these two. They are present-day campers and know nothing of the future.
- They are a couple: Travis says to Beckendorf, "You're saying that because Silena's your girlfriend" (Ch8).
- Don't confuse Silena with **Drew** (Tanaka), the other Aphrodite girl on the raid (Ch8–9), or with the future Aphrodite's Bulwarks (`H06_Bulwarks_and_Spears.md`).

## Appearance
- No physical descriptions are given for either. Silena "smiled graciously" and can "deliberately [look] a little baffled" on cue (Ch8).

## Personality
**Silena**
- A confident teacher and hustler. She claims her cabin's "poker faces" won "550,000 worth crisp bills across 180 days" in "our casino". She "expertly" deals blackjack and explains card counting and casino countermeasures (Ch8).
- A social engineer: "Everyone, act like you're stinking rich." She plays the baffled heiress to win free limos and suites (Ch8).
- Frank and boy-wise. On the raid she comments on butts ("The one thing I don't like about Beck is his bony ass") and warns Drew that "lots of men are pigs" (Ch9).
- Rules-curious: "Are we planning to cheat if we get shot?" (Ch9).
- Possibly suspect. Percy is relieved that "Luke and Silena" are on the other bus during the bolt discussion (Ch11), which hints at her canon role as Kronos's spy.

**Beckendorf**
- A calm, patient rationalist: "I'm saying it because it's based upon probability" (Ch8).
- Laughs at Percy's crude jokes and earns a thump from Annabeth (Ch8).
- Wry: he adds "world-class bullshitter" to Luke's résumé (Ch10).
- Steady under divine fire, though shaken: he freezes with a jar of Greek fire in each hand while Zeus wrecks the landers. "Is that your uncle?… Is he winning?" (Ch22).

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Silena:** card skills, charm and con artistry, recon spotting with binoculars and earpiece (Ch9), and the Aphrodite cabin's evacuation, which Chiron orders her to lead (Ch22).
- **Beckendorf:** probability and engineering. He builds Greek-fire firing positions and catapults along the ridge (Ch22). Six jars of Greek fire set the Imperial Titan ablaze, and the second volley hits its head (Ch22).

## Backstory
- As a canon baseline: Silena is the Aphrodite counselor who secretly spied for Kronos out of love for Luke's cause, and later redeemed herself. Beckendorf is the Hephaestus counselor. In canon both die in the Titan war.
- In this story, Silena had a strange past attempt at the cabin casino game: "Couldn't hack my siblings… Luckily I was so hopeless they kicked me out before I lost" (Ch8). This sits oddly beside her claimed 550k winnings.
