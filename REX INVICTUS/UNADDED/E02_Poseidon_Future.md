## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **This file:** the future Poseidon. He knows his son as a legend who went missing "between time-layers" (Ch15). He has spent millennia apart from him since "The Great Expansion" (Ch12). He knows the hidden Tartarus war, the War of Princes and the cult around "that creature in Luna".
- **Present-day Poseidon** is the boisterous father who claims Percy, teases him about Artemis, drowns the Imperial Peltast cavalry, sinks Keyes's Titan, apologises to Marien Pyrrhus and offers Triton as Helmsman (Ch8, Ch19, Ch22). See `D02_Poseidon_Present.md`.
- **The future Poseidon does NOT know** what happens in the present-day Rex timeline. He senses "Dreams. Too many of them. Too much bleeding through" (Ch15), which may be faint bleed-through.
- **Who he is talking to:** the "Percy" he sees in Ch12–15 is **Minerva** wearing a vat-grown clone of Percy (`B02_Minerva_Percy_Clone_Interlude.md`). For the real Percy see `A01_Percy_LordCommander_OldTimeline.md` / `A02_Percy_Reincarnated_Rex.md`, and for the lunar shard `A05_Him_Within_the_Moon.md`.

## Appearance
- He holds "his signature trident", glowing faintly. He stands in a throne hall with Amphitrite and the Nereids (Ch12).
- He emerges "in a curtain of fog. Barefoot and frowning" (Ch15). He leans on a forcefield wall "as if it were just another dock in Key West of old Earth".
- At Tartarus he wears war plate, and "the salt tang of the sea god's presence" mixes with the ozone (Ch15).
- On Orbital Platform One he stands with arms folded while "the currents thrashed around them" (Ch13).
- Old timeline: an "azure throne" (Ch1).

## Personality
- **Grief-stricken father.** His first reaction to the clone's face is hope: "Percy?" … "By the tide… you've returned." It turns to cold rage when he realises it is not his son: "You—you're wearing him." (Ch12)
- **Perceptive and hard to fool.** "That's not how he would've said it." "Your voice doesn't match the face." (Ch12) "You're not who you say you are." He sees through Minerva's simulated nervousness, but chooses to work with her: "Just don't lie to me again, Minerva." (Ch15)
- **Weary and haunted.** "Just tired… Dreams. Too many of them. Too much bleeding through. So many Mortals." His "sigh was a tidal thing" (Ch15).
- **Secretive.** He keeps the Tartarus war "compartmentalized" and refuses Minerva's offer of Hunters (Ch15). "We learned to watch from a distance. Power draws attention." (Ch15)
- **Glorifies war.** He rhapsodises about the War of Princes: "Olympus, it had been beautiful. No better war had ever been fought." He relishes the memory of a "beautiful death", and finds the Tartarus grind "filthy" by comparison (Ch15).
- **Mischievous and competitive.** He gloats at Zeus with "regular mischievous smiles" after his descendant killed Zeus's daughter (Ch5).
- **Sheepish rule-breaker.** He slipped an entire battleship past the orbital works; when Varric asks how they got in, "Poseidon coughed. Suddenly the walls looked like a very interesting view." (Ch15)
- **Formally respectful of the doomed.** He acknowledges Varric's fate and sends him off: "Die well, nephew." (Ch15)
- **Ruthless sovereign.** His adjutants have a disloyal colonel "dealt with" and arrange an "accidental failure" of another's Repertoire infusers (Ch12). The text implies but does not state that this was on his orders.
- **Inactive at the crucial moment.** When the Olympian Court crumbled, "Poseidon would not act" (Ch13). Minerva accuses him of "your abandonment many millennia ago" (Ch12).

## Voice & Speech Patterns
His voice rumbles. He speaks in short, gruff lines, and grief turns them dangerous. He uses sea oaths ("By the tide"). He calls Minerva "Impostor", "son" (by slip) and "Minnie", and calls Varric "nephew". When reminiscing he becomes expansive, even purple. Mild profanity is not shown on-page in the future.
- "Percy?" … "By the tide… you've returned." (Ch12)
- "Percy. Son. Don't toy with me." (Ch12)
- "You are not my son." (Ch12)
- "You're early, Impostor." (Ch15)
- "They are the reinforcements." (Ch15)
- "He did it for her, didn't he?" (Ch15)
- "He shouldn't be able to. Not yet. Not while fragmented. It isn't even the real him." (Ch13)
- "Die well, nephew." (Ch15)

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Trident** (glowing). Command over currents ("the currents thrashed around them", Ch13). He can arrive and depart in fog.
- **Realm-level protection:** inside his realm "augurs could not peer into reality" (Ch12).
- **New Atlantis / the Nereid Expanse:** a star system whose waters form "flat, circular disc" currents "like Saturn's rings around a star". It has towers of coral and metal exhaling steam and bioluminescent mist, and Oceanic Terraforming Stations in orbit around the ice moon Enceladus (Ch10–11). An augur calls it "the Master's greatest creation since your conceiving" (Ch11).
- **Orbital assets:** orbital water towers (possibly undersupplied, Ch15). Orbital Platform One is "larger than most gas giants" (Ch12).
- **Forces:**
  - The New-Atlantis Navy.
  - The 32nd Scylla's Sisters (about 300,000 troops) and the 121st (Ch12).
  - The mechanical Titans of Stephanos Pyrrhus's Honour Guard.
  - Adjutants such as Cyria (also "Cyrus" twice in Ch12; see Continuity Notes).
  - Augurs.
- **Bloodlines:** only 127 first-born descendants remain in his System-Dominion (Ch15), plus legacies. Poseidon legacies are amputated and "integrated" as neural relays in AnnChase ships (Ch11). The Pyrrhus dynasty ("Waves of Poseidon") is taught to pray to him (Ch22).
- **His blood in Regen-ex:** Percy's healing fluid contained "fragments of the Lord of Atlantis' blood" (Ch1).
- **Genetic tie to Minerva:** his data matches "sixty-seven percent of my embedded Aegis matrix" (Ch12).

## Backstory
- **Old timeline:** he laughed "triumphant" with "genuine pride" when Percy's half-brother Rammael set foot on Cyrax. That moment seeded Percy's jealousy (Ch1). At the Great Abandonment the gods "ascended to the bridge of The Spear of Zeus", and Percy bade "his father, and the rest of his Divine family, a farewell" (Ch1). Four centuries after the Great Abandonment, Typhon destroyed his palace on Olympus Primaris (Ch1).
- **The Great Expansion:** Minerva says he abandoned his son "many millennia ago" with the beginning of the Great Expansion (Ch12).
- **War of Princes (16212/13–16227 per the vault, a few years before the future-era scenes; Poseidon says "millennia ago", a slip; see Continuity Notes):** his sons tried to fight through to the last legions of Apollo, but the Princes' ships split the armies. He watched the last Sun-Born, who "had screamed the Orchestras of Hate into space", die, and a blonde, imperial-gold-clad Legacy of Apollo keep the Roman standard upright while he was run through (Ch15).
- **Caligo Vale:** when the Olympian Court crumbled he "would not act", which led to Percy's political marriage to Artemis (Ch13).
- **The near-monthly Zeus skirmishes:** Zeus blames his "damned direct descendant" for killing Eleni Theodosia (Ch5). She died at Fulguris to the Dionysus–Poseidon–Aphrodite coalition (Melina Callisto's ramming, Ch2; Stephanos Pyrrhus was in the coalition, `G03_Stephanos_Pyrrhus.md`); her last memory curses Dionysus (Ch15). The individual killer is unresolved.
- **Author's vault note (not yet in text):** at the Great Expansion he claimed the **Thalassus Belt** and created **Karcharonexus** and **New Atlantis** (dated 3045.06.3.5). New Atlantis is his chief residence with Amphitrite, one of the UOE's main centres of authority, and one of only three entrances to the Forge of Hephaestus. Its residents include Benthesikyme, Ceto (fleeing Pontus), Delphin, Eidothea, Galene, Glaucus, the Hippocampi, Kymopoleia, Leucothea, Nereus, the Nereids, Phorcys, Proteus, Rhode and Scylla. Helmsmen are lobotomised direct descendants of Poseidon interred in ships. His UOE Unified Rank is #27.

## Continuity Notes
- Minerva's eyes are described as "golden" on Platform One (Ch13). That is an outlier: her signature is neon blue when she is in control, and the grown body's natural eyes are sea-green (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C1).
- In Ch13, after "Minerva tilted her head", the next line ("Don't do that. You remind me of Athena too much…") has no explicit speaker tag. It is **Poseidon's** line, objecting to her Athena-like head tilt (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C10).
- Ch15 says that at Tartarus "nobody seemed to recognize the two Olympian gods", which counts Minerva as a god.
- "Posiedon" [sic] spelling (Ch5, Ch15). His messenger is **Cyria** (five uses); Ch12 twice slips to "Cyrus" (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md S5). Ch12 also spells "Stephanos Pyrrus" [sic]; the canonical spelling is Pyrrhus (S1).
- **War of Princes dating:** Ch15's "millennia ago" is a slip. Per the vault (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R5) the war ran 16212/13–16227 and ended only a few years before the future-era scenes.
- **Version caution (C33):** everything in this file comes from far-future scenes (Ch1, Ch2, Ch5, Ch10–13, Ch15, Ch23) or from what future Imperials say about him. Present-day Poseidon material (Ch8, Ch13 present segments, Ch19, Ch22 battle and Accord) belongs to `D02`.
