## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **She is not the mythic Atalanta.** Ch6 says she was "Named for a swift-footed warrior". In Ch23 a statue of the mythological "Atalanta, looking smug" stands on the Via Heroum as a separate hero.
- **Present vs future:** the present Atlanta knows nothing of the future. The Ch23 Atlanta is a 14,000-year veteran of the Director's Hunt. The text never states outright that they are one person, but the author's vault has a single Atlanta entry ("Daughter of Apollo / Apothecary") covering both eras, so they are the same immortal. When roleplaying either version, keep their knowledge separate.

## Appearance
No description is given beyond silver Hunter garb. She "raised a singular eyebrow" (Ch4). Her hands emit golden healing light (Ch6).

## Personality
- **Dry and snarky.** "Maybe both, sister." (to Phoebe, Ch4)
- **Precise and professional.** She gives a crisp scouting report (Ch4), and in Ch6 she "had a clipboard in her hand, and read off the injuries at a rapid pace".
- **"A mind disciplined, honed for battle and survival"** (Minerva's assessment, Ch6).
- **Not superstitious** (Ch23), which makes it all the more telling when she says the old Hound "He's doing it too."
- **Perceptive.** In the future she is the first to notice that the Bakuto feels wrong, asking "This place remind you of anything?" Thalia names it: "The Lotus." (Ch23)

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Apollonian healing.** A golden-light "diagnosis scan" (Ch6) and medical triage.
- **Scouting and tracking.** She reads the man-made cave-in and the gold-decomposing werewolf corpses (Ch4).
- **Mobile phone** (present), "a minor luxury to a Hunter of Artemis". Minerva piggybacked into it through the noosphere and used its cellular connection to escape into the wider world (Ch6).
- **Future:** apothecary kit, handler of the Hound Scaramucius.
- **Ch4 medipack:** Zoë asks "Celyn, Atlanta" for a medipack, and "the daughter of Apollo" hands over her sling pouch. That Hunter is **Atlanta** (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C30; the author's vault makes Celyn a daughter of Demeter).

## Backstory
Nothing is revealed beyond her parentage and her Hunter service.
- **Author's vault note (not yet in text):** the vault's Hunt roster lists Atlanta, "Daughter of Apollo", with the role **Apothecary**, among the Hunt's active members. This matches the Ch23 description of her as "the Hunt's apothecary". The vault also has a separate "Atalanta, a renowned huntress" among Chiron's first students, who is the mythic heroine, not this Hunter.

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch4:** Sent to scout the collapsed den. She reports that the cave-in was man-made, the pack wiped out, a few "Individuals of interest" missing, and the corpses "decomposing into gold". She identifies the crimson rug under Percy as Lycaon's pelt.
- **Ch6:**
  - She performs a golden-light diagnosis scan on Percy at camp.
  - Unaware, she becomes Minerva's gateway when the AI nests in her phone.
  - In Artemis's tent she reads out Percy's injuries from her clipboard.
- **Ch16:** Zoë names her for the rescue team. She is left behind when Zoë takes only Bianca.
- **Ch23 (future):**
  - With Thalia's contingent on Voss Secundus, she brings Scaramucius to the plaza and notes "He's doing it too" when the old Hound lies down where the cab turned.
  - In the Bakuto lobby she holds her teacup at arm's length and asks Thalia, "This place remind you of anything?" Thalia names it: "The Lotus."
