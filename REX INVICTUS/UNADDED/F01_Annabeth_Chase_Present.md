## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **This file** covers the *present-day* Annabeth, a pre-teen camper who has only just met this Percy (Ch7). She knows nothing about the future. All she has are clues: Percy's uncanny knowledge, the voice in his head, his blueprints, and his slip in Ch13 ("Not you Annabeth, the future you").
- **Future/old-timeline Annabeth** is a separate person. That Annabeth is Percy's ex, Second-in-command of the Council of Olympic Sentinels, the "High-Lord-Engineer", and "the Architect". She designed the hardware of Parlamentum Mk.I (the AI that became Minerva), secretly re-plumbed his suit's cooling with Lethe water, and championed his Aegis Vestments surgery. She did not design the suit itself, which is "of his own design" (Ch1); see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C14. See `F02_Annabeth_Chase_Future_Architect.md`. The present Annabeth must never speak as if she remembers any of that.
- **The fake Annabeth (Ch8)** is Athena wearing her face through the Mist to question Percy. That was not Annabeth. See `D03_Athena_Present.md`.
- **Verrill Chase** is a far-future legacy who looks exactly like Annabeth. She is a different person, about 14,000 years later. See `F03_Verrill_Chase.md`.
- **The Monument of the Two** (Ch23) is a far-future statue of the adult Annabeth and Percy. It is legend, not this girl.

## Appearance
- Blonde curls and storm-gray eyes; she often stands with her arms crossed (Ch7, Ch11).
- She taps her fingers on her knee when she's thinking, and steeples them when she's "interrogating" (Ch11).
- She carries a knife or dagger that Percy didn't know she had (Ch13–15). She also has an invisibility cap, which she "capped" in Ch14.
- Off duty in Vegas she wears a hotel robe and slippers (Ch8). On the Sea of Monsters she carries a notebook and pencil and sketches symbols (Ch14). She paces with a notebook in the arena (Ch15).
- After touching the Golden Fleece, her palms are branded with welts shaped like tiny sunbursts, tridents and crescent moons. They fade quickly (Ch14).

## Personality
- **Suspicious and analytical.** From her first scene she sizes Percy up "from head to toe, assessing" (Ch7). She notices he is "up to something" when he asks for adrenaline and morphine (Ch7). She thinks he "knew too much at all times" (Ch11). She even shoots down his ADHD excuse: "You've zoned out on us too many times for it to be your ADHD" (Ch13).
- **Bossy and protective.** She escorts the injured Percy to the infirmary, "Worried you'll do something reckless". She also thumps Beckendorf for encouraging Percy's crude jokes (Ch8).
- **A brilliant tactician and engineer.**
  - She designs the three-man cell structure of the Fulminus insurgency, with the Athena cabin as the "sole connection between these cells" (Ch9).
  - She runs the drone-hangar raid and flies a captured drone by joystick (Ch9).
  - The narration calls her "an aeronautical engineer" (Ch9). Connor calls her "a maths whiz" (Ch8).
- **Ruthless in a fight.** She points a paint-loaded machine gun at a technician's face ("You wanna go blind?"). When the technician mouths off, she empties twenty rounds into her (Ch9). A fellow raider remarks: "Remind me to never ever make her mad."
- **Theatrical and dry when she wants answers.** Her bus "efficiency assessment" in Ch11 is a deadpan parody of a military debrief, to Clarisse's delight.
- **Loyal underneath the suspicion.** When Athena calls Percy "the vessel", Annabeth snaps back: "He's not a vessel. He's Percy" (Ch13). She leaves camp without a quest to save Grover (Ch13).
- **Proud, and wounded by her heritage.** When the Fleece rejects her, she says bitterly, "Because I'm my mother's daughter" and "I wasn't prepared for my bloodline to be the enemy" (Ch14).
- **Possibly vulnerable.** She insists on hearing the Sirens: "I have to see… I have to know" (Ch14). The scene cuts before we learn what she heard.
- **Squeamish about worship.** She is openly disgusted by Eleni's religious hymns to Percy (Ch15).
- **Growth.** She starts out treating Percy as a reckless new kid. She ends up treating him as a mystery that may save the camp, and as a friend she defends to her own mother. By Ch20 she watches him in silence, unsure whether she is seeing "shenanigans, insanity, or… both."

## Voice & Speech Patterns
- Clipped, direct and confident. She uses "Seaweed Brain" and "Jackson" for Percy and "Thals" for Thalia. She swears moderately ("No shit, idiot", "we can't do shit", "kick your ass").
- When she is mocking or interrogating, she switches to stiff bureaucratic diction ("efficiency assessment", "gross negligence or a calculated objective").
- She uses short commands under pressure: "Talk. Now.", "Extraction! Double time!"
- With her mother she is respectful but clipped.

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Strategy and leadership.** She planned the cell network and the "special strategy" of laxative-dosing the base's water, run with Malcolm and Mr. D (Ch9–10). She coordinates by radio and earpiece (Ch9–10).
- **Engineering.** She understands drone construction, including their carbon-fiber skin under 2 mm thick, and can fly them (Ch9). She is the obvious recipient of Percy's future "design ideas" (Ch8).
- **Combat.** Knife fighting; she cuts Grover free (Ch14). She handles firearms in the war game (Ch9). She uses the invisibility cap to rush the Fleece (Ch14).
- **Divine sense.** She senses that Eleni is a child of Zeus by aura: "Same aura as Thalia… but stronger. Wilder" (Ch15).
- **Iris messages** with Athena (Ch13).
- **Weakness.** In this timeline the Golden Fleece is cursed to treat any child of Athena as "a threat. An intruder" (Ch14).

## Continuity Notes
- **Age.** She calls Percy 16 ("ripe old age of 16", Ch13), and the narration also calls him sixteen (Ch7, Ch22). Only the premise blurb ("12-year-old self") and the Yancy Academy setting imply twelve. Profiles describe him as a young teen whom the text calls sixteen; see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R1 (flagged for the author). Her own age is never given.
- **"Aeronautical engineer"** (Ch9) and the high-level tactics in Ch8–9 are far beyond canon age. The Vegas/war-game chapters also use British phrasing ("maths whiz").
- **Ch8 blackjack.** She says "Connor, the dealer's showing a six…" but it is Travis who is playing. The narration mixes up the brothers' names.
- **Ch13–14 knife.** Percy "had no idea she even had" a knife, yet she draws one again in Ch15. In canon she always carries one.
- **The Fleece.** Percy notes "She could last time" (Ch14). In his original timeline the Fleece did not reject her; this timeline has "changed slightly."
- **Pronoun slip in Ch13.** Annabeth's line "She picked Clarisse… Tantalus picked Clarisse" uses "she" for Tantalus, who is male. It is an author slip.
- **Ch13 map line.** "Okay seaweed brain, lets see if 'Minerva' can help us out" is spoken by "someone". The narration attributes the posturing to Annabeth, so it is most likely hers.
