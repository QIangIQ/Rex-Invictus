## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **This file:** the future/old-timeline Hermes who leaves Earth with the gods and refuses Percy's plea.
- **Present-day Hermes** is a different character. He is cloaked in Mist and thrown out of a Vegas casino (Ch8). He shares water with Percy on the bus and lies about Mr. D's lady friend (Ch8). He takes a 5% cut for spiriting godly food out of Area Fulminus (Ch11). At the Ch19 and Ch22 councils he is the god who wants to talk to the Imperials. See `D05_Hermes_Present.md`.
- **What this version knows:** Percy as "Lord-Commander Perseus", the Sentinels, and the departure. He does NOT know Zeus's return plans ("I am not privy of Father's plans"). He knows nothing of the present-day Rex timeline.

## Appearance
- "Salt-and-pepper hair". An aura that "dim[s]" when he notices Percy.
- An amicable smile; he "flashed a smile".
- His caduceus serves as a clipboard ("caduceus-clipboard"), then transforms into a walking cane (spelled "Caudecus").
- He departs "With a flash and the scattering of a few small feathers" (Ch1).

## Personality
- **Friendly and solicitous at first.** "Easy hero, slow down and tell me slowly." He grabs Percy's arm and jokes about running "continents worth of road". He seems "genuinely confused" by Percy's presence.
- **Turns cold and paternalistic when accused.** "his eyes hardened". He gives a lecture about divine ownership: the gods created demigods, fought for them and gave them power, so demigods "have no right to demand anything".
- **Restless and expansionist.** "We have gotten tired of walking the same roads, seeing the same sights, and fighting the same problems over and over again." The gods "were never meant to be shackled by the place we reside in".
- **Pragmatic about power.** "We cannot stay, for we risk losing control over countless mortals, and subsequently, our domains."
- **A sliver of mercy.** Seeing Percy broken on the concrete, he swears to return and nods, because "depriving their greatest hero of the legends this request would simply be too cruel."
- **Untrustworthy oath.** He swears by his own name, yet aeons later the gods have not come back (Ch1). Whether he broke the oath or Zeus did is unstated.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
He switches from warm and folksy ("my boy", "hero", "my child") to formal, scolding and rhetorical, with capitalised emphasis ("YOU") and parallel sentences ("Do not… Do not… Do not…"). He calls Zeus "Father" and Gaia "our lovely great grandmother".
- "Ah Lord-Commander Perseus, what has befallen you, for you to stray from your post?" (Ch1)
- "Easy hero, slow down and tell me slowly" (Ch1)
- "YOU have no right to demand anything from us boy, for we have suffered longer than you shall ever live." (Ch1)
- "We must leave for new frontiers, new lands, and new people to influence and guide." (Ch1)
- "We, as gods, were never meant to be shackled by the place we reside in" (Ch1)
- "we shall return, I swear it upon my name, Hermes Clepsiphron" (Ch1)

## Backstory
- On the eve of the Abandonment there was "only a brief party on Olympus Primaris". Percy learned of the departure only the night before and caught Hermes alone at the *Spear of Zeus* doing last checks with the fuelling crew (Ch1).
- **Author's vault note (not yet in text):** in the UOE era he builds the **Tachyon Bridge Network** with Athena (3046), which fits the text's "Hermes relays" (Ch23). His father is Zeus. His UOE Unified Rank is #324.

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch1 (memory):**
  - Percy runs across "the ominously empty field" shouting. Hermes greets him and steadies him.
  - Percy accuses the gods ("You can't do this to us! We have bled, cried, and lost for this land!"). Hermes lectures him coldly and justifies the departure.
  - Percy collapses and begs for a promise of the Council's eventual return. Hermes swears on "Hermes Clepsiphron", nods and vanishes.
  - The *Spear of Zeus* launches. It ruptures Percy's eardrums and blinds one of his eyes.
- **Later chapters:** no on-page future appearances. He lives on through Imperial institutions and descendants.

## Relationships
- **Percy (old-timeline Lord Commander):** fond condescension and a broken promise. See `A01_Percy_LordCommander_OldTimeline.md`.
- **Zeus:** "Father", whose plans he follows blindly. See `E01_Zeus_Future.md`.

## Secrets, Mysteries & Open Threads
- Did Hermes ever try to honour his oath? Percy is still waiting millennia later (Ch1).
- Was the oath on his name binding? The text implies the gods simply did not return.
- What are "Father's plans for our return to the loving embrace of our lovely great grandmother"?

## Continuity Notes
- "Caudecus" is a typo for caduceus (Ch1).
- His Divine-Being title in the vault, *The One Upon Swift Wings*, is used on-page only for the *present-day* Hermes (Annabeth, Ch10); it is listed here as a vault title, not as evidence for this version (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C32, C33).
- Calling Gaia "our lovely great grandmother" fits Greek genealogy (Gaia → Kronos → Zeus → Hermes), which makes her his great-grandmother.
