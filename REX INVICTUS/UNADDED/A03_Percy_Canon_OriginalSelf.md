## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **This file** covers two related things:
  - **(a) Story-implied original-body Percy:** the boy who lived in the body until the morning of Ch3.
  - **(b) Canon baseline:** the Percy of Rick Riordan's books, which the story assumes as the shared past of everyone involved. Canon material is labelled **[CANON, not story]** below.
- **Not** `A02_Percy_Reincarnated_Rex.md`, the millennia-old mind now living in this body. A02 *imitates* this boy and does it badly.
- **Not** `A01_Percy_LordCommander_OldTimeline.md`, the man this boy grew into in the original timeline. A01 is canon Percy after the canon books and after thousands of years more.
- **Not** `A04_Percy_Dreamwalker_Psychic.md` or `A06_Percy_The_Legend_Lord_Imperator.md`.
- **Knowledge:** at the takeover he knew none of the divine world. In canon he would learn it at the museum trip and the Minotaur attack. He knows nothing of the future, Minerva, demons or the Empire.

## Appearance
- **Story:** the same body A02 now wears: messy black hair, a soft jawline, sea-green eyes, soft skin with no scars, and a right pinkie still intact (Ch3–4). His room has a turquoise-blue ceiling and a cartoon-seal blanket. His desk is "still filled with incomplete homework from yesterday" (Ch3).
- **[CANON, not story]:** messy black hair, green eyes, thin and wiry. He looks like a troublemaker. He wears whatever is clean.

## Personality
- **Story-implied:**
  - Sleepy and unmotivated in class. Teachers "seemed to be already accustomed to his behavior and only sigh when they saw him" (Ch3).
  - Behind on schoolwork (the unfinished homework).
  - Affectionate with his mother. Sally expects his usual muffled "Coming mom, just gimme a second" (Ch3).
  - The contrast is drawn sharply. Sally is met instead with "the strangely terrifying piercing green eyes of her son," a rigid posture and a formal "Mother," which tells us the original boy was warm, casual and a little lazy.
- **[CANON, not story]:**
  - Sarcastic, impulsive and loyal to a fault.
  - Has ADHD and dyslexia.
  - Protective of his mother and of underdogs.
  - Quick to anger at injustice, self-deprecating, brave, and bad with authority.
  - Has a strong sense of fairness, and in canon a "fatal flaw" of personal loyalty.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
**Story evidence is minimal.** The only line attributed to him is the one Sally expects to hear. Below are 1 story quote and canon-style reference lines.

## Backstory
- **Story:** a son of Poseidon raised by Sally in a moldy apartment under his stepfather, named in the text only as "Gabriel" (canon/vault: Gabe Ugliano; 00a S14), "out cold, reeking of alcohol," among "veritable mountains of empty beer cans" and cheap tobacco smoke (Ch3). He attends Yancy Academy. Mrs. Dodds (Alecto in canon) is already watching him "far too much" (Ch3). Chiron, as Mr. Brunner, already "personally shadowed" him (Ch7).
- **[CANON, not story]:**
  - Expelled from multiple schools.
  - On the Yancy museum trip, Mrs. Dodds reveals herself as a Fury; Grover is his protector.
  - The Minotaur attacks him at Half-Blood Hill and his mother seemingly dies.
  - He becomes the hero of *The Lightning Thief*: retrieves the Master Bolt, confronts Ares, returns the bolt on Olympus.
  - *Sea of Monsters*: the Fleece, Tyson, Polyphemus.
  - *Titan's Curse*: Westover Hall, Artemis and Atlas, Zoë's death.
  - Later: *Battle of the Labyrinth*, *The Last Olympian* (Battle of Manhattan), and the Heroes of Olympus.

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch3:** his last implied moments are the night before. On the morning of Ch3 (the Répertoire's evening readout that day is stamped "2005, August 19th", the nominal canon date; the author's timeline puts the present at c. 2021, see 00a R2), a "thin wispy form" falls through the clouds toward his apartment, and the future Percy wakes in his bed. The narration says this was "his room, though he only faintly remembered what it was like." The original's daily life (dozing in class, unfinished homework) is visible only as habits A02 copies. His fate is undescribed: no struggle, no voice, no merging scene. He is simply replaced.

  - The junkyard Talos with Thalia (Ch13).

  - Zoë is meant to survive the Titan's Curse (Ch19).

## Secrets, Mysteries & Open Threads
- **Is the original boy dead, dormant, or merged?** The text says the old mind's memories had "faded" and he "only faintly remembered" this life, which suggests merger rather than coexistence.
- **The mindscape fragments of Ch11** include a child-like voice: "I want to go home!" / "What did the scary lady just say?" It is possible but unconfirmed that a remnant of the original 12-year-old survives among A02's splintered selves (see A02).
- **Does anyone mourn him?** Sally's reaction after Ch3 is never shown.

## Continuity Notes
- **School:** Ch3 is set at Yancy. Percy's Ch7 account says "Goode High". By Ch15 he is at Goode High. Ch7 is either a slip or an anticipation. In canon Goode High comes later (*Battle of the Labyrinth* onward).
- **Eyes:** Sally's eyes are blue (Ch3, Percy's POV: "piercing blue eyes boring into him"). Percy's are green (Ch3, Sally's POV: "piercing green eyes of her son"). This is not a slip.
- **Ages (00a R1, flagged for the author):** the body is 12 per the premise blurb, but no chapter line says "twelve", and later chapters call A02 "sixteen" (Ch7, Ch13, Ch22). Profiles describe A02 as a young teen. This file keeps twelve because it covers the pre-takeover boy as the premise and canon define him.
- **Gabriel (00a S14):** the text says only "Gabriel"; "Gabe Ugliano" is the canon/vault name.
- **Present-day year (00a R2):** the Ch3 readout says 2005; the author's timeline puts the present at c. 2021 (Perseverance rover, Ch19, Ch22). Do not state 2005 as fact.
- **The pinkie:** the mechanical pinkie (old life) is gone; the body's original finger is intact (Ch3).

He goes to Yancy Academy, a boarding school for troubled kids in upstate New York, where he sleeps through algebra, forgets his homework, and gets glared at by his creepy English teacher, Mrs. Dodds.
