## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- All are far-future soldiers living in the present after the shear.
- They are distinct from **Kappa-Seven** (the five captives; see `G09_Kappa7_Ekdromoi_Squad_Overview.md`) and from the far-future Fulminata of Ch23 (`H01_Tullia_Ardens.md`).

## Appearance
- **Sciritae:**
  - Schattenfeld adaptive-camouflage harnesses that bend light.
  - Silent folding glider wings and folding grav-bikes.
  - Suppressed carbines and Geistjäger divine-presence counters.
  - Their shuttle is the matte-black *Nightjar*.
- **Neodamodes:** squads of 13 with "graft scars showing pale at every exposed seam".
  - They ride Aquila-pattern assault landers with Hyperium drives and divine-composite carapaces.
- **Peltasts:** pegasi about 3 m at the shoulder with Hadrian-pattern bronze wing-armor and grav-stamp shoes.
  - Riders carry crescent pelte shields and plasma-thruster jump rigs.
- **Keyes's Titan:** 11 m and slab-shouldered, "chipped and rechipped". It has an orange monocular, a car-sized rotary cannon and an energy blade.
  - It drops in a cradle from the heavy lifter *Anchises*.
- **Psiloi:** rifle cases taller than the women carrying them, fired from the Durham gunship *Longsight* at 96 km altitude.
  - Cerelli's eyes have gone "pale gold from decades of looking down scopes".
- **Castellan Oriel:** tall, with a cracked visor showing "one pale grey eye", close-cropped grey hair and a scar across her jaw.
- **Hrafn:** broad and bearded, with runes burned into the backs of his hands and a pale-rune axe.
- **Handler Corvin:** "gaunt, grey-haired", and looks like he hasn't slept in a week.

## Personality
- **Handler Corvin:** calm and even, "a man who had lost count of how many bodies he had sent through doors". He gives strict orders against "side business".
  - He forbids firing on the Director.
  - The first time he raises his voice is "CODE 2 is not legit!"
  - He calls Cerelli's refusal mutiny, authorises exactly one tactical nuke, and his voice goes rough when he confirms casualty recovery.
- **Sabine Ro:** a cocky, grinning banterer who was moved by Earth's beauty ("Pretty planet. I didn't expect that.").
  - She is a former Firstborn cadet from the Asphodel Colony queue, raised on a hive-station over Nysa, and calls the camp "a war camp".
  - She recognises Zoë as she dies.
- **Tarrow:** an unimpressible Augur. "I'd suggest we avoid it." / "I'm usually right."
- **Ashmead:** "gravel and brass", loud, funny and paternal toward his "doomed" convicts.
  - He has a soldier's respect for Reyna ("Best shield wall in the Empire") and turns quiet when he learns "we've been shooting at the Emperor".
  - He rallies against monsters, puts himself in the gap and nearly dies.
- **Herrman:** a horseman who sings to his mounts. He lets go of his pegasus when it chooses Poseidon, and grieves the seventy who did not.
- **Keyes:** an easy-going "Hey there, buddy" pilot with his boot on the console.
  - He recites the Mars catechism when he faces Ares himself and keeps reciting it in the infirmary.
- **Cerelli:** ritualistic: she touches each marksman's helmet with two fingers and whispers the same thing to each. Asked the rule of engagement on the gods, she says only "Don't miss."
  - She fires on the Romans under orders but refuses to fire on the Director and her Hunt, which is mutiny on the record.
  - She kneels to Artemis.
- **Brannoc:** a gruff ordnance man. "Nobody swore any oaths to monsters." "Quidquid latet, apparebit."
- **Oriel:** disciplined ("It was a discipline, not looking down"), icy toward Eleni ("Step aside, traitor"), and honest with Apollo ("I don't know"). She kneels first to the Lord Imperator.
- **Hrafn:** archaic, warm and mystical ("One rarely goeth anywhere to meet family. And yet family is always there."). He fights all night beside the Ares cabin, and two ravens watch him.

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Sciritae (stealth scouts).**
  - Three lances, 18 operators.
  - Motes (rice-sized sensor seeds).
  - Geistjäger counters, rating presences MINOR, MODERATE, MAJOR or UNRESOLVABLE.
  - Sabine's Hermes "Relay Voice" works for four seconds after her death.
- **Neodamodes:** twelve Aquila landers carrying infantry who earn citizenship through service. The lead Aquila, Ashmead's, is the *Hammer of Dawn*.
- **Peltast ala:** "a hundred and forty pegasi" in the cavalry shuttle.
  - Götterbrechende Raumzeitverankerungs-Langstreckenlanze javelins, which pierce the *Princess Andromeda*'s time-shield.
- **Titan:** rotary cannon and energy blade (Ares tore the blade arm off).
- **Psiloi volley:** 12 marksmen at the edge of space firing at 11 times the speed of sound.
  - Their rounds include QVARTUS and DMAPFSDS (Stygian Iron and Celestial Bronze cores).
- ***Longsight* and *Anchises* ordnance:**
  - DMHE airbursts (divine-material HE laced with Celestial Bronze and Imperial Gold).
  - CLSTR cluster munitions.
  - A **THMNCLR**, a 0.3-kiloton shaped thermonuclear charge, contained by the *Nightjar*'s lattice.
- ***Nightjar*:** ECM that blinds radar, comms and mortal satellites, plus an EMP shaping lattice.
- **Ekdromoi Lambda-Two and Sigma-Four:** cloaked arrowhead dropships, MORROW-IX rifles, thermal grenades and divinely hardened helmets. These held for about five seconds against Dionysus's madness.
- **Trophimoi (counter-pantheon operators with an "echo"):**
  - an Egyptian-blooded operator who unmakes Greek wards with blue-gold hieroglyphs;
  - Hrafn's seidr, which puts the dragon Peleus to sleep;
  - an Aztec-blooded woman with a whip of blue fire;
  - an unnamed Shinto operator;
  - and their handler.

## Backstory
- All of them came through the shear with Marien's echelon (a sector near the Phlegethon; staging on the Lethe side, per Straban in Ch22).
- **Ashmead:** 31 combat descents, 26 of them into Tartarus.
- **Herrman:** fought on three worlds and lost two mounts in the Salient.
- **Keyes's Titan:** its armor is "chipped and rechipped from four rotations in the Phlegethon Salient" (Ch22).
- **Oriel:** 18 years in the Ekdromoi and 28 retrieval operations, none failed (per Eleni: "Eighteen years in the Ekdromoi. Twenty-eight confirmed retrieval operations. You have never failed one.").
- Author's vault note (not yet in text): the vault's `Titan Pilots.md` name list includes Cerelli, Keyes and Ashmead (with Rosseau, Focault, Herrman, Frued, Friedman and others), so these names were first conceived as Titan-pilot names; the text uses them for a Psiloi Volley-Lead, a Titan pilot and a Neodamode centurion. The vault's formation notes define the Sciritae as reconnaissance/pathfinders, the Neodamodes as "Newly Enfranchised" convict and block-born heavy assault infantry, the Peltasts as jump and anti-armour skirmishers, the Psiloi as long-range fire support and orbital snipers, and the Trophimoi as counter-pantheon foreign-blood "foster-sons". The vault's Ekdromoi are mostly recruited from the Peltast corps.
- **Cerelli and the Psiloi:** as children they swore before a statue of the Director: "My eye to your aim. My hand to your will. My life to your hunt."

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch22, Mars.** The Watchpoint authorises retrieval and names it ORPHEUS. The hangar vignettes:
  - Corvin's orders.
  - Sabine's banter with Ashmead.
  - Herrman with Brontes.
  - Keyes eating in the cradle.
  - Cerelli's ritual.
  - Oriel's briefing: "We do not engage it unless it engages us."
  - Hrafn feeling the old gods awake.
- **II. The Woods.**
  - The Sciritae drop and map the camp. Tarrow's counter shows 211 presences, one of them UNRESOLVABLE.
  - Zoë's Hunters ambush them; Pell dies first.
  - Corvin forbids firing on the Director.
  - Sabine shoots a Hunter through her pauldron and is killed by Zoë. Her relay sends the map, the Roman wall (flagged priority two) and a frame showing Artemis, Zoë and Percy.
  - Eleven Sciritae dead.
- **III. Landfall.**
  - Zeus destroys landers 4, 9, 7 and 2.
  - Minerva's spoofed CODE 2 causes more crashes (11, 6, 3). Corvin countermands it.
  - Ashmead holds the beach.
  - Poseidon calls the pegasi; Herrman jumps clear, and about 70 Peltasts drown.
  - Keyes's Titan drops and kicks Clarisse. Ares fights it barehanded and Keyes recites the catechism. Poseidon sinks the Titan and rescues Keyes.
- **IV. The Big House.**
  - The Psiloi volley destroys the Roman Fifth Cohort's shield wall: 41 dead, later 44, including Gwen.
  - The Trophimoi break the border and put Peleus to sleep.
  - Lambda-Two and Sigma-Four drop on the Big House (two dropships, ten operators) and their cone of fire cuts down the Apollo cabin on the porch, including Michael Yew. Merek: "It could have been any of them. Probably all of them." Dionysus's madness breaks Sigma-Four.
  - Oriel faces Percy on the landing. Dace fires at Percy; Artemis catches the round, and "The bullet he had fired himself went through his own visor", killing him.
  - Cerelli refuses to fire on the Hunt. Of the other eleven marksmen, eight take their fingers off their triggers; three keep them there ("Not all of them. Three of the volley kept their eyes to their scopes"), and at dawn those three fire the DMAPFSDS rounds at the *Princess Andromeda*.
  - CODE 18 arrives. Ashmead and Reyna stand down. Oriel kneels first and relays the Lord Imperator's order: casualty recovery, all parties.
- **Dawn battle.**
  - Luke's *Princess Andromeda* arrives. Quillon asks for "standard protocol" and Ashmead answers that they kill monsters.
  - Brannoc's DMHE, cluster munitions and one THMNCLR destroy the monster wave. Kronos's time-field stops the rail rounds.
  - Herrman's 38 surviving Peltasts ride the pegasi, with Percy on Blackjack, and javelin the ship.
  - Artemis appears in the *Longsight*'s bay and gives Cerelli authority. "Twelve rounds from the edge of space" destroy the bow.
  - Ashmead kills the last hellhound and is dug out by Reyna.
- **Aftermath.**
  - At dawn Huitzilopochtli appears and the Aztec operator kneels.
  - Corvin, Oriel and Cerelli attend the sunset council.
  - At the joint pyre, Oriel sits with Apollo: "I don't know."
  - Corvin relays the Jade Court and Brooks Range alert.

## Continuity Notes
- **Casualties** (see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C8). Use the author's reconciliation: Zeus killed **140**, the CODE 2 spoof **118**, Poseidon's wave drowned **71** Peltasts, and ground fighting made up the rest: **≈340** mid-battle (Poseidon's "three hundred and forty") and **406** final ORPHEUS dead. Earlier figures are in-scene estimates: Minerva's "ninety to one hundred and twenty" after four landers and "sixty to seventy" Peltasts, Herrman's "Seventy of my men". The Fifth Cohort lost 41 in the volley ("forty-one legionaries in ninety seconds") and 44 in all ("forty-four legionaries").
- **Ch22 (the Hunt's return to camp):** the speech about the Psiloi being "my lines… My blessings" is attributed to Zoë in the text, but the content and Zoë's reply ("Yes, my lady") show it is Artemis speaking.
- **Cerelli** "touched each helmet with two fingers and whispered the same thing each time"; her oath reappears in Ch22 with Artemis's Latin addition "coget omnes ante thronum".
- **Dace's death** (story-level slip, 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md §5): Dace is killed by his own QVARTUS round thrown back by Artemis, but the text later says "Dace's body hung on the wall with the arrow through it". Treat the bullet as canonical.
- **Hammer of Dawn** is the name of one lander, the lead Aquila (the text's header AQUILA LEAD — "HAMMER OF DAWN"), not a class.
- **Psiloi altitude:** the gunship is at "96,000 meters" while "the Kármán line" is invoked in the briefing (roughly the same height).
