# Minor Characters Compendium — Rex Invictus

> Every named or significant figure in Ch 1–23 who does not have a profile of their own (or a named slot in a group profile), in 2–6 lines each. Some figures who also appear in a profile or group profile are kept here for lorebook completeness; each of those entries ends with an explicit **See also** pointer to the profile that covers it.

**How to use:** each entry gives the name, the version/era, the role, chapters, key facts and (where they speak) one voice note. The entries are sized to paste straight into a lorebook or World Info entry; use the name as the keyword. Era tags: **(O)** old/original timeline · **(P)** present day (c. 2021 per author's timeline; nominally the canon PJO era; see `00a_CANON_RULINGS.md` R2) · **(F)** UOE / far-future era · **(A/N)** author's note only. For the eras themselves, the glossary and the main cast, see `00_START_HERE_Version_Guide_and_World_Primer.md`. **See also** points to the profile or roster file that covers the same figure or scene; each code is the prefix of exactly one file in this folder (for example `F18` = `F18_Mortals_NASA_and_Government.md`). Canon rulings cited by ID (R1, S2, C20 …) are in `00a_CANON_RULINGS.md`. Vault-only facts are labelled *Author's vault note (not yet in text)* and never override the text.

Entries are numbered so they can be counted. Groups of interchangeable unnamed extras count as one entry.

## Part 1 — Old / original timeline and cosmic background

**1. Aeolus** — (O) god of winds · Ch1 (memory).
As the *Spear of Zeus* left Earth he withdrew "the very clouds from the atmosphere". This is Percy's last memory of the gods' departure. He has no lines. See also `A01`.

**2. Typhon ("Typhoon")** — (O) storm giant · Ch1 (memory).
Destroyed Poseidon's palace on Olympus Primaris "in his eternal rage four centuries after The Great Abandonment". The text spells his name "Typhoon". See also `A01`, `D02`.

**3. The Huntress who shot off Percy's pinkie** — (O) unnamed Hunter of Artemis · Ch3 (memory).
While Percy was scouting a possible monster camp, she took him for a trespasser and "furiously" shot off his right pinkie by accident. He replaced it with a mechanical finger and never told anyone. The new body in Ch3 has the pinkie back. See also `A01`, `C12`.

**4. The Greek-captured reincarnator ("the poor kid")** — (O) unnamed time traveller · Ch3 (memory).
A reincarnator from the future whom the Greeks tortured for knowledge. That knowledge let them dominate the western hemisphere "by the end of the 5th millennia" [sic]. This is why Percy fears exposure to Olympus. See also `A01`, `A02`.

**5. The Chinese time-traveller ("Autumn Cicada")** — (O) unnamed · Ch3 (memory).
"Some dude in China" who travelled in time "through artificial means. Something about an Autumn Cicada?" This is a nod to Fang Yuan's Spring Autumn Cicada in *Reverend Insanity*, the author's model for Percy's intended arc. See also `A01`, `A02`.

**6. The other Sentinels (First Council of Olympic Sentinels)** — (O) Percy's order · Ch1, 5.
The surviving remnants guard Olympus Secundus and Tertius, where human survivors live, while Percy holds Primaris alone. Chaos says the Council "shattered merely centuries after" the Abandonment, in the Great Cleansing. Annabeth was its second-in-command. See also `F02`, `A01`.

**7. Thanatos** — (F) god of death · Ch2 (mention).
His "black armored titans" collect fallen demigods and carry them to the Elysian Necropolis. Melina expects "the harvest of thanatos". See also `E08`, `G12`.

**8. Gaia (the Earth Mother)** — (O/F) primordial; also the name of Earth · Ch1–2, 5, passim.
Hermes calls her "our lovely great grandmother". In the future "Gaia" simply means Old Earth ("holy Gaia", "Lands of our Birth"), the polluted world the gods fled. Time units are Gaian Years, minutes and seconds (Ch2, Ch15). Percy jokes about Ouranos as "the fatass sky who wants to hug his wife" (Ch18).

**9. The dying form** — (F) unidentified · Ch2.
After the *Radiant Crusade* explodes, "a dying form crumbles to dust" in vacuum and whispers, "And so it ends… But from the ashes of the destruction, we shall be reborn." It could be Melina, Eleni (who later turns up alive) or something divine. See also `G12`, `G01`.

## Part 2 — Present-day: school, home and the road (Ch3–4)

**10. The math teacher (Yancy Academy)** — (P) unnamed · Ch3.
"droned on about algebraic equations, his monotone voice blending into the background" while Percy struggles to keep his eyes open. The teachers are "already accustomed to his behavior". See also `F18`.

**11. The church ghost (Yosemite)** — (P) "a remnant ghost, perhaps" · Ch3.
Haunts the old stone church whose basement hides the Labyrinth exit near Half Dome. Percy senses it but does not engage. See also `I08`.

**12. The Labyrinth monster camp: the Cyclops** — (P) monster · Ch3.
Twirls a club and snaps at the hellhounds ("Shut up! Stop barking at me! I need quiet dogs!"). Percy severs his neck, and he is the likely source of the Cyclops eye that Percy trades to Asmodeus. See also `I08`, `I09`, `I01`.

**13. The Labyrinth monster camp: the Dracaena** — (P) serpent-woman leader · Ch3.
Plans an attack on "a few demigods and a satyr" and speaks Lingua Achaicus. Her blood is fiery ("plethegonic"). Percy stabs her and burns her alive as an offering. Voice: sing-song and hissing, "What do we have here~? a lone demigod? A brilliant feast just offered itself to us." See also `I08`, `I09`.

**14. The Labyrinth monster camp: the Hellhounds** — (P) several desk-sized hounds · Ch3.
They breathe fire and bark first. Percy beheads them, and the last one is crippled and burned alive as an offering for "swift passage". See also `I08`, `I09`.

**15. Paimon** — (P) demon, Asmodeus's superior · Ch4 (mention).
She is female, and Asmodeus fears her ("How do you know her, boy?"). Percy suggests that giving her the Cyclops eye could get Asmodeus promoted above his rival. See also `I01`, `A02`. *Author's vault note (not yet in text):* the vault ranks Paimon 2nd of the eight Demon Lords (kings) of Hell under Lucifer; Asmodeus is 5th.

**16. Dantalion** — (P) demon lord, Asmodeus's rival · Ch4 (mention).
The mention of his name makes Asmodeus slam his fist. Percy plays on the rivalry during the bargain. See also `I01`.

**17. The Evergreen Family** — (P/F) shadow faction · Ch4, 8, 17, 18, 20 (mentions).
A power "that sought to control both Heaven and Hell". Their spies infiltrate Asmodeus's camp, and Percy knows they will collapse the demon lords "a few millennia later". The Eye of the Cyclops exposes the spies (Ch8, 17). Asmodeus: "The bastards of Evergreen still crawl in shadows." They survive into the far future, with "Evergreen pilots" (Ch18) and Asimov's friend, "One of the Evergreen Family branch managers" (Ch20). Possibly elvish (compare the A/N OC Elowen Greenwood, entry 179). *Author's vault note (not yet in text):* the Evergreen Family is a mining corporation (asteroid belts, moons, blank planets), recently caught in a scandal with the Astralnet Conglomerate. See also `I01`, `H10`, `B03`.

**18. Dracula and "the blood leeches in nightingale"** — (P) vampires · Ch4, 17 (mention).
Vampires who pose as nobility. Condottiero hates them: "Dracula! That Lucifer dammed lamprey! Curses!" In Ch17 Asmodeus reports that the vampires are fighting the Shinto. *Author's vault note (not yet in text):* the vault has notes on Vlad Dracula Tepes, vampire progenitor and Vampire King, and on Scathach; neither is named in the text beyond Ch4's "Dracula" (unused / planned). See also `I02`, `I01`.

**19. Lucifer** — (P) mention · Ch4.
Appears only in Condottiero's curse "Lucifer dammed". In Ch5, "Luciferian demons" are rumoured to be plotting to reform Old Earth. *Author's vault note (not yet in text):* Lucifer heads the eight Demon Lords of Hell and is ranked #100 on the Unified Alltime Rankings. For the "Luciferian demons" see entry 89. See also `I02`, `H11`.

**20. Churchill** — (P) historical son of Poseidon · Ch4 (mention).
One of "the last few sons of Poseidon". Zoë: "Churchill could barely summon a pool sized water himself", which she uses as a contrast to Percy's unprecedented nectar control. This implies Winston Churchill. See also `C04`.

**21. Pan** — (P) wild god · Ch3 (mention).
Percy wonders whether the starry, pine-scented Labyrinth shortcut passes near Pan's domain. See also `F04`, `I08`.

**22. The newer Hunter** — (P) unnamed Hunter · Ch4.
Took Naomi's maintenance and repair duty in exchange for a promise of extra rations, an example of the Hunt's duty-for-rations economy. See also `C12`.

**43. The Colchis bull** — (P) bronze bull · Ch13 (mention).

## Part 4 — Present-day: Ch6 journey, Area Fulminus / Las Vegas, Zeus's plane

Wears a flowing green cloak and vines in her hair, and her eyes glow faintly. She guards "sacred ground" by making plants grow fast. Minerva rates her stronger than a dryad. She smells the Hunt and "something… mangy, like a dog" on Percy, but accepts that he is just passing through. Voice: "Son of Poseidon, you tread on sacred ground." See also `A02`, `I03`.

**45. The air-base security officer** — (P) "a second-born of Ares. A legacy" · Ch8.
Stamps the campers' papers at the concealed air base. Voice: "Haven't seen you come through here in a while." See also `F18`.

**46. The air-spirit stewards of Zeus's jet** — (P) · Ch8, 11, 12.
White-gloved spirits. In Ch8 the chief steward hauls Connor off the royal bed, and a stewardess says the plane isn't officially commissioned "until the Sky Lord on a tour Down Under". In Ch11 they avert their eyes and flee Artemis. In Ch12 a stewardess offers "Snack?" … "Peanuts or cookies?" and Percy asks for cookies. See also `D01`.

**47. Julio Sweet's casino staff (Taipei hostess, security guard, gift-shop clerk)** — (P) Las Vegas mortals · Ch8.
A Casino Taipei hostess offers dining, spa and "special services". A guard in dark glasses with an earpiece escorts the ejected Hermes. A gift-shop clerk reads *People* and glares at an Elvis button. For Julio see `F18`; see also `D05`.

**48. Mr. D's "lady friend"** — (P) "forty-something looking spirit" · Ch8.
Dionysus brings her along in Vegas. According to the hung-over Hermes she later propositioned him for $600, and Hermes claims he said he'd "rather have a nice cup of tea". Minerva and Percy both know Hermes is lying. Hermes also says she is in cahoots with Julio Sweet. See also `D05`, `D07`.

**49. Dionysus's lost brothers** — (P) mention · Ch8.
Mr. D's bare room holds paintings of himself with an identically dressed boy. Mr. D: "Many of my brothers were slow," … "It got them killed." This hints at grief behind his mockery of Percy's speed. See also `D07`.

**50. President John Leader** — (P) fictional · Ch9.
The "democratically elected government" in the Area Fulminus scenario that General Mikhail's task force defends. He is part of the exercise, not a real president. (The real President appears in Ch22.) See also `F18`.

**51. The SAS "boys"** — (P) 16 British special-forces men under Sgt Clayton · Ch9.
They run insurgent cells 3–10 with 800 paid civilian sympathizers. Clayton: "We'll have sixteen of my boys helping out you lot." See also `F18`.

**52. "Mechanic"** — (P) name-drop · Ch9.
Sarge hands out "Ak-47s, 74s, 101s, and .300s… Mechanic would be proud." Who Mechanic is goes unexplained; possibly an in-joke. See also `F18`.

**53. The Auxilia-One soldier** — (P) unnamed female soldier · Ch9.
Runs the induction plaza. Voice: "Welcome to Auxilia-One… Papers please." See also `F18`.

**54. The patrol officer and the marshmallow soldier** — (P) US soldiers · Ch9.
The officer barks, "What are you doing out in the dark?" A friendly soldier offers Percy "Peanut brittle, or marshmallow?" and advises goggles. Percy eats marshmallows partly to annoy Minerva. See also `F18`.
