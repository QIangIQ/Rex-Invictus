## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- Verrill is a far-future descendant who looks *exactly* like Annabeth: "She didn't look like a descendant… She looked like Annabeth" (Ch23). She has none of Annabeth's memories. She knows Annabeth only as a historical figure, "The Architect".
- For the present-day camper, see `F01_Annabeth_Chase_Present.md`. For the original Annabeth and the legend, see `F02_Annabeth_Chase_Future_Architect.md`.
- Her partner "Minerva" here is the far-future Minerva piloting Percy's vat-grown body (`B02_Minerva_Percy_Clone_Interlude.md`). It is not the Minerva inside young Percy.

## Appearance
- Curly blonde hair tied in a messy knot with a pencil stuck through it.
- A narrow, sharp face and a stubborn chin.
- Storm-grey eyes ("the grey of a sky before rain").
- A smudge of reagent on her cheek and a burn scar on the back of one hand.
- She carries a loupe in her jacket pocket.
- Her voice is "crisp and dry and utterly unafraid."

## Personality
- **Calm and calculating under duress.** Her eyes had been "calculating the distance to the door, the weight of the chain" since the lock turned.
- **Pragmatic.** She waited three months to be rescued rather than sabotage the lab, because "They'd have killed me."
- **Petty and vindictive.** On release she smashes the brain-ark, wipes the gene-printer and snaps her pencil into the vats: "Now they'll be dead in a week."
- **Perceptive.** She spots at once that Minerva is "not a legacy… You move like a machine."
- **Honest, even when it costs her.** She exposes Augustus's jade book as a fake, then apologizes to Minerva for ruining the deal.
- **A giddy fangirl.** She had a Hunt of the Director poster at nine and squeals at seeing Thalia Grace in person.
- **Laughs in danger.** She laughs hysterically while being chased and makes puns ("I, a Chase, chased by the hunting Hunters…"). She cheekily waves at Thalia mid-pursuit.
- **Hates heights.** "I hate this so much!" on the Themis Needle. She sits clutching the railing with her eyes shut.
- **Bold.** She volunteers for the time-road project because Minerva broke her chain "And you look like him. It seems unlucky to say no."
- Her banter with Minerva has the rhythm of Percy and Annabeth arguing, which is exactly what makes Thalia hesitate.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
- Crisp, dry and brisk, "as though delivering a report". She is sarcastic and gives technical detail on demand. She swears mildly ("Oh my gawd", "Oh my gods"). She talks fast when excited. She calls Minerva by name.

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Genius chemist.** She kept the Rustabouts' double-bloodline addicts alive by cooking stable brain-ark. She knows it gives seizures if handled early.
- **Engineer.** The Chases "designed almost every" tachyon relay. "Nobody in the galaxy knows more about bridging two places that shouldn't be connected."
- **Forensic eye.** With a loupe she identifies the fake Jade Court *Book of Life and Death*:
  - synthetic binding thread with extrusion lines (the real Court now uses phoenix tendons);
  - fluorescence under the lamp;
  - jade from the Pelagic Reach;
  - characters copied from the public facsimile in the Tian-Qiang museum, which she saw in the Forge-system of Hephaestus.
- She has no stated divine powers.

## Backstory
- The Chase line of Athena "had not produced a face like hers in nine thousand years." Her mother used to put Annabeth's old Gaia photo (on a porch, in a "stupid Yankees cap") beside Verrill's school portrait for comparison.
- Her grandmother had a Percy tea towel.
- Three months before Ch23 she was working a Deyang contract in the docks. A slaver crew grabbed her, drugged her, shipped her across the basin and sold her to the Rattlebone Rustabouts. They kept her chained in a cold-storage sub-basement lab in the Kilns.
- She knows about "the Salient. The shear… The four million" (Ch23). "Everyone heard about that."

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch23.**
  - Minerva asks Talviento to find "Verrill Chase. Blonde hair, blue eyes. A person of import". In exchange, Minerva will kill nine Rustabout leaders.
  - The chemist Flask reveals that the Rustabouts hold "the Owl."
  - Minerva enters the lab ("Hallo, ist jemand zu Hause?"). Seeing Verrill's face, the Superintelligence is silent for three full seconds.
  - Freed, Verrill destroys the lab's product and rides a cab with Minerva and a mutant dog past the Monument of the Two. The cab AI is stunned by the resemblance and offers a free photo. Both refuse in unison.
  - She volunteers to build the past-side of a time road.
  - At the Bakuto, on Augustus's 7th floor, she unmasks the jade book as a forgery and apologizes in the elevator.
  - She recognizes the Hunt and Thalia in the lobby. Minerva grabs her wrist and flees.
  - Carried piggyback through the kitchen, across New Rome at supersonic speed, over the Colosseum Gigantus and up the Themis Needle, she shrieks, laughs and waves at Thalia.
  - At the summit: "Minerva, what did you do?" "Called us a cab." Swept onto the hijacked shuttle, she escapes with Minerva.

## Relationships
- **Minerva** (`B02_Minerva_Percy_Clone_Interlude.md`). Rescuer, then partner. They watch each other sidelong and banter like Percy and Annabeth. Minerva smiles "her Varlet's crooked smile" at her despite herself, and keeps the real Annabeth's memories "behind a very old door."
- **Annabeth "The Architect"** (`F02_Annabeth_Chase_Future_Architect.md`). Famous ancestor and her own mirror. Verrill has lived with the comparison her whole life and jokes about the "AnnChase pattern."
- **Thalia Grace** (`C07_Thalia_Grace_Future_Lieutenant.md`). Her childhood hero. Thalia freezes on seeing her, sees "a boy and a girl she had last seen alive… fourteen thousand years ago," and pursues her.
- **Talviento and Augustus** (`H07_Augusti_Talviento_Augustus.md`). The Augusti who helped free her (by contract). She embarrassed them over the fake book.
- **The Rattlebone Rustabouts** (`H16_Callistus_Narrator_and_Tartarus_Front_Soldiers.md` covers the Rustabouts). Her captors. She sabotaged them on the way out.
- **The grey mutant dog.** Rode in the cab with her. It was left behind at the Bakuto.

## Secrets, Mysteries & Open Threads

- Will she really build the "other end" of a road into the past? That would make her a key to bringing the four million home, and possibly to reaching Percy.

## Continuity Notes
- **Eye colour.** Minerva describes her as having "blue eyes" (Ch23, to Talviento), but every on-page description says grey/storm-grey. Grey is canonical for the scene. The "blue" is probably a slip, or a description from secondhand data. Plot Threads lists it as a known slip ("blue in XVI, storm-grey in Int I"). Use storm-grey.
- She is "perhaps twenty", though the Monument's Annabeth is "perhaps seventeen" (Ch23).
- **Time distance.** About 14,000 years separate her from the present-day story (Ch23's "fourteen thousand years ago"; 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R3).
- **The Shear.** The "four million" she mentions are Marien Pyrrhus's Reserve Echelon, swallowed by the Shear and listed as MIA in her era (Ch22 shows the shear threw them back across time to present-day Mars). The Shear did not swallow Percy (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C7).
- The name "ESPERANZA" is shared by the tram and the cab registration (VS-11-ESPERANZA).
