## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
This is **the protagonist**: the "Rex" Percy of the present-day timeline. He has the original Lord-Commander's memories, soul and personality in a young, underpowered body. He is not:
- **The Lord-Commander of the old timeline** (`A01_Percy_LordCommander_OldTimeline.md`), the rusting sentinel of Ch1 who stood alone at the gate of Olympus Primaris. That man's consciousness *became* this one when Chaos sent it back. Everything A01 remembers, A02 remembers. A02 lives in a world where none of it has happened yet.
- **Canon / original-body Percy** (`A03_Percy_Canon_OriginalSelf.md`), the ordinary troubled Yancy kid whose life this mind overwrote. A02 only "faintly" remembers being him and imitates him badly.
- **Percy-the-Dreamwalker / "Psychic"** (`A04_Percy_Dreamwalker_Psychic.md`). This is the same mind, but only while asleep and slipping into future Imperial soldier bodies and the vat clone. A02 is the waking, embodied version.
- **Him Within the Moon / the Riptide fragment** (`A05_Him_Within_the_Moon.md`). This is a shard of future Percy, formed from Artemis's energy and Anaklusmos, that calls A02 "My Original" (Ch13). After Ch14 it seems to live inside Riptide, where it speaks as "a sliver cast from the Jackson" (Ch16).
- **The Empire's legend** (`A06_Percy_The_Legend_Lord_Imperator.md`): murals, the "Myths of Perseus Jackson", cult hymns, the Imperial key and CODE 15. Imperial soldiers have been "told since childhood that you are a god" (Ch22). A02 is the real, flawed boy behind that title. He insists "I'm not him yet."
- **The clone body Minerva pilots in the future** (`B02_Minerva_Percy_Clone_Interlude.md`). It wears his face, but the operator is an AI.

**What A02 knows:** the whole canon PJO arc as he originally lived it (to the extent memory survives), plus millennia of future history: the Great Abandonment, the Great Expansion, foreign pantheons, demon hierarchies, Imperial technology, and Roman law. **What he does not know:** many specifics of the Empire's later era (he did not recognise Eleni's cult lore or the Caligo Vale marriage, Ch15), what Artemis erased from his memory in Ch11, what Riptide has become, and the ways "this reality" already differs from his own (Ch14: "Something about this reality had changed slightly").

## Appearance
- **Body:** a dark-haired boy who looks young for what he does. Zoë sees "a mop of messy black hair framed a soft jawline" (Ch4). Mr. D says the black hair and green eyes "really do remind me of Poseidon" (Ch8). He is out of shape: "I don't work out" (Ch15). His legs burn on hikes, he lacks endurance, and his reflexes are slow; Dionysus says he has "the reflexes of an old lady" (Ch8).
- **Eyes:** sea-green. Sally calls them "strangely terrifying piercing green eyes" (Ch3). They glow faintly blue when Minerva is active (Ch6) and flicker neon-blue when she speaks or acts through him (Ch22).
- **Skin and scars:** on waking in Ch3 his old pale, cracked skin, battle scars and mechanical right pinkie are gone, and the soft skin and real finger are back. He bears new wounds: a self-surgery scab over the ribs (Ch4); a palm shot clean through by a QVARTUS round and healed "rosy-pink" (Ch21–22); repeated cuts on his palm from blood offerings (Ch18, Ch21).
- **Blood:** "silver-red" (Ch7), "crimson-gold" when Artemis stabs him (Ch19), and in Ch22 "cyan-yellow and too bright, gold-shot ichor marbled with thin filaments of yellow circuity [sic]."
- **Hidden hardware:** under the skin of his chest, sternum and ribs are fine angular metallic lattices, visible to Apollo's scan. Nanomachines glitter inside his blood vessels. When the systems wake, "Hidden plates of divine armor shimmer beneath his skin, phasing in and out of reality" and his veins "light up like circuitry" (Ch19). Silver nanobots once leaked from his fingertips onto a laptop (Ch8). In Ch11 silver-blue particles poured out of cuts on his arms to form Minerva's bust.
- **Clothes and kit:** the orange Camp Half-Blood shirt ("Hey. It's a good shirt", Ch22; Ashmead orders his men to salute "a man in an orange shirt"). A jacket in Rome. A rucksack. Riptide as a pen. Cargo shorts and a polo bought in Vegas (Ch8). In Ch14–15 he wears the Golden Fleece on his back.
- **Under stress:** water floods out of him; the plane cabin ends up "submerged in a small lake of seawater" after the memory wipe (Ch11). Steam rises off him after he unmakes Thorn (Ch16).

## Personality
**Core: a veteran soldier in a boy's body.** He thinks like a commander and a survivor. He scouts the monster camp from above before striking (Ch3), wears the Minotaur down methodically (Ch7), and instantly reads the Othrys monsters' feints as stalling for Atlas (Ch19). His body keeps failing his instincts. In Ch7 his hydrokinesis does not answer: "body and soul were still misaligned."

**Socially rusty, and bad at acting normal.** "Millenia of standing guard and zero social interaction had deteriorated his social skills into nothingness" (Ch3). He tries to mimic other kids, fakes headaches and ignorance ("I thought they were just Greek myths", Ch6), and is seen through by Zoë, Artemis, Dionysus, Athena, Apollo and Annabeth. The Athena cabin knows "you shouldn't be this smart" (Ch13).

**Cold and formal when it counts, flippant otherwise.** With Sally in Ch3 he is stiff and archaic, and finds her questions "merely a waste of time in his eyes", though he privately thinks "Hoo boy, this is going to be really hard". With gods and officials he can be precise and legalistic, as in the Roman speech of Ch17. Most of the time he is a sarcastic, deadpan teenager: "Yeah, that's kind of my thing" (Ch6), "I'm so cooked" (Ch15), "Nanomachines, Son" (Ch19).

**Cunning and willing to deceive, but with a code.**
- He lies to Chiron about a lost pencil case to get Riptide (Ch3).
- He lies to Asmodeus about the Evergreen family wanting the Eye (Ch4).
- He bluffs and blackmails Ares with his foreign aspects (Ch10).
- He gives cover stories on the bus (Ch11).
- He tells Artemis the biovirus is safe when Minerva said "It might" (Ch22).
- He also says "I usually don't lie, you know" (Ch17).
- His one hard line is refusing to deceive enemies *in their own voice*: "We don't lie to them in their own voice" (Ch22).

**Pragmatic to the point of cruelty, early on.** In Ch3 he burns a living dracaena and a whimpering, crippled hellhound alive as offerings to the Labyrinth, with a formal dedication for each. In Ch4 he has a demon slaughter Lycaon's whole pack, including "the young", so he can harvest the king's heart. Then he cuts out his own heart with an ice mouthguard in his teeth.

**Reckless self-sacrifice.** He climbs Othrys alone and takes the sky from Artemis against her orders (Ch19). He pours his blood, divine core and Achilles curse into a shrine fire for her: "He did not regret it… Good." (Ch18). He spends power his body cannot hold; Artemis tells him "You spend power your body is unsuitable for" (Ch16).

**Increasingly merciful, by Ch19–22, which complicates the "degradation" arc.**
- He keeps all five Ekdromoi alive after they nearly killed him (Ch21).
- He argues for them to Artemis: "They're lost… doing their job" (Ch22).
- He stops Apollo from killing the bound prisoners (Ch22).
- When landers sink he "command[s] the waters to crush their heads to grant them a quick death" (Ch22), which is a mercy killing.
- He confesses Minerva's false CODE 2 to Castellan Oriel and apologises (Ch22).
- His first order as Lord Imperator is "casualty recovery, all parties, no distinction" (Ch22).

**Embarrassed by worship.** "No more titles!" (Ch15). "Don't call me a despot." "Please," … "Get up. I'm not... I'm not him yet." (Ch22).

**Playful provocateur.** After the tent scene he deliberately torments Artemis with a florid, mock-courtly voice in front of the Hunt and the camp (Ch19–20). He knows exactly when to stop: he bows his head at her warning "Perseus." He banters crudely with Apollo ("my greatsword's thrusts will be far superior", Ch16) and jokes about fishnets and hookers in Vegas (Ch8).

**Haunted, and grieving under the jokes.** He carries the Abandonment and millennia alone. In Ch12 he describes being "effectively dead" after the wipe, "a hole now, cleanly carved". He cried for the old world in his past life. In Ch22 his hands shake, his voice cracks, and he sits with Apollo beside Michael Yew's shroud.

**Contradictions.** He acts on the timeline aggressively (saving Zoë, rousing Rome, bulldozing "Ancient Laws", Ch17) while also deferring to the "pattern" (letting Artemis be captured, Ch16). He is a hardened killer who cannot bear being knelt to. He hates being used as a weapon, yet happily lets the gods treat him as a "power bank" (Ch19).

### The moral-degradation arc (author's intent vs. what the text shows so far)
- **Stated intent (Ch1 A/N):** Percy will be "very OOC" with huge knowledge and strength, and will slowly develop "a personality similar to Fang Yuan from Reverend Insanity… a slow degradation of ethics and morals, but still honorable (which is contradictory, I know)." The fic's summary adds: "tearing down the looms of fate… and damn the consequences… Alea Iacta Est."
- **Early evidence of erosion (Ch3–10):**
  - Torturous live burnt offerings.
  - Commissioning a massacre through a demon lord.
  - Manipulating demons by naming their rivals and their masters.
  - Blackmailing a god.
  - Poisoning an army's water supply with laxatives as a war-game tactic.
  - Pocketing Dionysus's money card (Ch16).
  - Bribing Iris for silence (Ch18).
- **Ambiguous seeding of conflict:** he invites the angel Sariel to "trail us… You might encounter something interesting" (Ch17), and Sariel then attacks Asmodeus. The text does not say whether he set this up on purpose.
- **His inner voices (Ch11 mindscape)** show the fork in him. A purgist fragment says "We should have killed them upon their thrones." A guide fragment says "We must guide them, shape them." A moral fragment asks "If we become convicted and executioner both, then what are we?"
- **Counter-movement (Ch18–22):** the most ruthless calls are increasingly made by **Minerva**, not Percy. She makes him get shot through the hand as a test (Ch21). She issues CODE 2 against his direct order: 118 dead by her own count (Landers Eleven and Six, and part of Three), traded against her projection of "between forty and seventy of your campers" (Ch22; see 00a C8). Percy's own choices trend toward mercy, accountability and restraint. Minerva even "commend[s] the restraint" when he declines to intervene in the Labyrinth vision of a Russian brute: "Add it to the list" (Ch21). That is also a colder, triage-minded detachment.
- **Reading for roleplay:** as of Ch23 he is *honourable but hardening*. He is a pragmatist who will burn monsters, cut deals with demons and let distant strangers die if they are, as Minerva puts it, "not your problem" (Ch21). He keeps his word, owns his mistakes, and protects the people in front of him. The Fang Yuan endpoint has not arrived; treat it as a slope, not a destination.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
- **Default:** casual, modern American teen. Short answers ("Yeah." "Yep." "Bad."), sarcasm, deadpan understatement, meme references ("Nanomachines, Son"), the odd Southern-ish "Ya'll" (Ch22).
- **Greek-flavoured oaths:** "No way in Hades," "What the Hades," "gods—". Moderate to strong profanity: "Fuck," "Shit," "Fuuuu—". He uses the Greek insult "Valkas!" (vlakas, "idiot") when he realises the Hunters are coming (Ch4).
- **Ceremonial / archaic register:** used with demons, angels and old powers ("I hope that this evening finds thee well", Ch4; "I have one for thee, Holy Watcher", Ch17). He invokes gods by full epithets ("Phoibos Apollon. 'Blessed Symphony', 'Apostle of Fate', 'Undying Light'", Ch21). He speaks a pre-Athenian Greek dialect to the Labyrinth (Ch21).
- **Legal / Latin register:** a torrent of Roman law ("ius gentium and lex sacrata… Concordia Compact… mos maiorum… fides publica", Ch17).
- **Mock-courtly performance:** a florid, ornate voice he uses to annoy Artemis ("Dear lady," "fair lady," "my dear and warlike ladies," Ch20).
- **Inner voice:** profane, wry, self-deprecating ("Hoo boy," "Oooh shit," "I'm going crazy").
- **How he addresses people:**
  - Artemis: "Milady" (early), "Artemis", and once, softly, "only for her", "Arty" (Ch22). Apollo (Ch19) and the future Poseidon (Ch15) also use the nickname (00a C11).
  - His mother: "Mother", formally (Ch3).
  - His father: "Dad" (Ch19).
  - Gods: "Lord Ares," "Lord Zeus," "Milady Iris."
  - Minerva: "Minerva" said as a reprimand; once "Répertoire."
  - Annabeth: "Annabeth," sometimes "the future you."
  - Condottiero: "clown-dude."
  - Atlas: "the fatass sky who wants to hug his wife."

## Backstory
- **Old life (A01):**
  - He lived the canon arc: he returned the Master Bolt in the original throne room, and was at "their subjugation of Lord Atlas in the very beginning of his journeys", learning ancient dialects from "The Eternal Hunter Princess".
  - He became Lord-Commander of the Council of Olympic Sentinels.
  - He saw every Olympus built, from Secundus to Olympus Mons on Mars.
  - His armour was "of his own design" (Ch1). Annabeth, his "ex", built the hardware of its AI core, Parlamentum Mk.I (Ch5); she did not design the suit (00a C14). She secretly laced its nectar coolant with Lethe water to limit the AI (Ch5). He unknowingly loosened that limiter by unhooking his cooling unit for the winter (Ch5).
  - The gods left on the *Spear of Zeus*. He begged Hermes and got only a sworn promise of return. The launch ruptured his eardrums and blinded one eye.
  - He then defended Olympus Primaris alone for millennia against Titans, Giants and foreign gods. Minerva taught him Philoxenia during that "indentured servitude to some empty thrones" (Ch6).
  - A Hunter accidentally shot off his right pinkie, which he replaced with a mechanical one.
- **Future-history fragments revealed in the present:**
  - A political marriage to Artemis at Caligo Vale (Ch12–13; Eleni's cult lore, Ch15). It belongs to the Empire era, early in the Great Expansion, never before the Abandonment (00a R8).
  - *Author's vault note (not yet in text):* the oathbinding was held on Olympus Tertiae, invoked by Hera, and the future Percy was mind-wiped within hours and sent back to Gaia, which may be why he does not recognise the marriage lore.
  - Commanding the defence of Alaska Hill: "commanded by a son of Poseidon" (Merek). Minerva: "I was there. You were there. Or we will be" (Ch22).
  - *Author's vault note (not yet in text):* the vault dates Alaska Hill to 2032, with Annabeth Chase and Malcolm Pace as co-commanders and Percy in an experimental exoskeleton integrated with Parlamentum Mk.I.
  - Holding a temporary command under Legio XXI Rapax (Ch17). He was in the Lotus Casino at twelve; Thalia remembers it in Ch23.
  - The Romans besieged Othrys during his original Battle of New York (Ch16).
  - Nico's eyes "that he had lost in the many later years of service for the pantheon" (Ch15).
  - Apollo apparently met him once while drunk (Ch19).
- **The crossing (Ch1/Ch3):**
  - Chaos found him in Hestia's courtyard and told him of the primordial war and the sealed vaults.
  - She offered him a choice between an exit too far away to walk (his rangefinder read "Undefined") and a leap into the abyss, after which she "might even grant a gift or two for your confidence" (Ch3). He jumped: "Must have been part of her plan."
  - **Transit form:** he fell through clouds as "a thin wispy form," phasing through birds at supersonic speed, the water vapour energising him "for the first time in millennia." He braced for impact remembering Annabeth's joke, "It's not the fall that kills you…", and woke under a turquoise ceiling with a cartoon-seal blanket.
  - Chaos's terms are still unstated. She did ask him for "a few more offerings."
- **Time travel lore he brings:** he witnessed three time-travel incidents in his watch, all "absolute chaos and destruction" as pantheons fought over the traveller. He also knew of "some dude in China" with an "Autumn Cicada," and of a Greek-captured reincarnator who was tortured for knowledge. This is why he fears exposure to Olympus.

## Continuity Notes
- **Age (see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R1; flagged for the author):** profiles describe him as a young teen; the text calls him sixteen (Ch7, Ch13, Ch22), though the premise blurb says he returned as a twelve-year-old. No line of chapter text ever says "twelve" for his body. Detail: the premise and fic summary say 12. Zoë says "early teens," Chiron "barely into his teens." But the Ch7 narration says "sixteen-year-old," Annabeth jokes "ripe old age of 16" (Ch13), and Ch22 says "sixteen" three times (Minerva, Marien, narration). In-story time from Ch3 to Ch22 is only about a year (January in Ch8 to mid-December in Ch15, plus "several months" in Ch19), so twelve and sixteen cannot both hold. The text never explains it. In-character dialogue may say sixteen.
- **Time figures (00a R3):** the far-future era is about 14,000 years after the present (vault: about 14,200). The figures below measure different things and are not rival answers. Old-life length: "approximately twenty thousand standard years" (Ch3), "many millennia" (Ch1), "a millenia or twenty" (Ch19), "the individual in your archive is six thousand years old" (Minerva to the Watchpoint, Ch22; the Imperials are also "six thousand years in the future", a known slip), and the Ch23 packet's "APPROXIMATELY FOURTEEN THOUSAND [CORRUPTED] YEARS BEFORE PRESENT" (the epoch of the key's presentation). Ch23 also puts his boyhood "A thousand years before the Abandonment" (00a R4).
- **School:** Yancy Academy in Ch3, but he tells Chiron the story began at "Goode High" (Ch7). In Ch15 he is at Goode High.
- **Dates and seasons:** the Répertoire readout is stamped "2005, August 19th" (Ch3), the nominal canon date; the author's timeline puts the present at c. 2021 (Perseverance rover, Ch19 and Ch22; 00a R2). Yet Vegas is "January… wintertime" (Ch8), Minerva counts "377 hours to the winter solstice" (Ch9), and Goode High is "three days before Christmas" (Ch15). Per 00a R6 it is January in Vegas; the "377 hours" line is a slip.
- **Bleeding:** Grover says Ch18 is "the first time I've actually seen you bleed." Percy had bled before (Ch4, Ch7, Ch11), but never in front of Grover.
