# Voices from the Bottom of the Empire — the Segment Twelve narrator, the Callistus narrator (Ezra, Petra), Commander Thaleia, Tertius Oban & the Rattlebone Rustabouts (UOE era; Ch 14, 19, 21, 23)
> The Empire as seen by the people it spends: a child soldier who lost count of his age in a Tartarus trench, a civilian who ran from the Tithe Day riot, a cold Lethe commander, a twenty-year-old's half-boot, and a gang of double-blooded junkies.

All entries are **far-future Undying Olympian Empire era** (~14,000 years after the present-day story, which is c. 2021 per the author's timeline; see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R2/R3). None has a present-day counterpart.

## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **Commander Thaleia is NOT Thalia Grace.** Her name is a Greek spelling of Thalia, but nothing in the text links them. Treat her as separate from future Thalia (`C07_Thalia_Grace_Future_Lieutenant.md`; 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md S7).
- The **Segment Twelve narrator** is one of Marien Pyrrhus's echelon's neighbours. The scene ends as Marien falls into the shear and the ships follow to Mars (`G02_Marien_Pyrrhus.md`, `G10_The_Major.md`). Whether the narrator went through to Mars is not stated.
- **Tertius Oban** belonged to the same Pyrrhuiesian echelon. His boot was left on the near side of the curve, in the far future, while the rest of the echelon landed on Mars in the present day.
- **Callistus** is the world whose murals of Percy Mitch Fulgur and Victor remember (`H13_Mitch_Fulgur_and_Victor.md`). The same planet holds hero murals and the Tithe Day riots.
- The **Rattlebone Rustabouts** fight Minerva-in-Percy's-body (`B02_Minerva_Percy_Clone_Interlude.md`) on Voss Secundus.

## Personality
- **Segment Twelve narrator:** numb, observant, quietly bitter about "a system that rewarded obedience and punished improvisation." Memories of the surface make the trenches "feel narrower." He or she trusts the scarred squad leader's judgement over personal fear.
- **Callistus narrator:** hopeful at first ("They'll stop when they see how many of us there are… I believed it"), then traumatised, ashamed of running, and grieving. Tender toward Petra's orphaned boy.
- **Petra:** worldly-wise and cynical ("They've got Tins for a reason, love").
- **Thaleia:** flat, cold and pragmatic, cynical about Athena: she "would not render another true judgment today. She never did, not until loss was already certain." Profane: "this godforsaken river… fucking ludicrous". She treats soldiers as assets: "still usable."
- **Rustabouts:** loud, cruel, drug-crazed and very hard to kill. Coil: "Coil doesn't die."

## Backstory
- **Segment Twelve narrator:** arrived at the front at twelve; his or her father "had been a Legacy too, and he had died in a trench that looked cleaner than this one."
- **Callistus:** the Office of the Tithe levies households for **Legio XX Valeria Vitrix**. Author's vault note (not yet in text): Callistus is a "fortress-planet under UOE control; recruiting grounds for Legio XX Valeria Vitrix"; the vault's Neodamodes note quotes a Legio XX Tithe Office enrollment address. The '09 grain riots are in family memory, and "Precintator Arqbal's block" rarely came home from the last levy. Petra's cousin's boy was taken off Meridian.
- **Thaleia:** Day 4,122 of Lethe Hold. Ten thousand years ago the Lethe "claimed an entire battalion by whispering their names in their mother's voices."
- **Rustabouts:** moved into the Kilns six months earlier and took two chemical plants, a Chem-Dyna warehouse, a hospital and the Underlane Regulars' trams.

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch 14:** Thaleia reviews codes, denies escalation, logs hallucinated "faces" in the fog as a potential Code 18, and signs off "Ave Imperator."
- **Ch 19:** The Segment Twelve narrator fights through a subreality breach. A comrade is speared beside him or her, and a gladius soldier vaults the parapet screaming the Roman oath. The centurion calls orbital fire. Marien Pyrrhus (Dulaberre-Six) arrives: "Why is she here?" The narrator keeps firing. The narrative then shifts to the Major and the shear.
- **Ch 21:** Ezra is levied. Ten thousand gather at Founder's Plaza. Six Tins sentence the crowd and wade in with mauls. The narrator is paralysed on one side, runs, and hides in a stairwell. Two days later the narrator learns Petra's boy is safe and Petra is not.
- **Ch 23:** Tertius Oban's half-boot lies at the curve; his name is in the columns being read aloud. Minerva kills the nine Rustabout leaders (Gristle sniped into a rain barrel; the Kettle Twins thrown 40 m by a burst water main; Tinplate; Flask kneecapped and questioned; Coil killed in 14 seconds with a greel-deflector; the three lieutenants trying to blow the chemical tanks). Verrill sabotages the brain-ark: "Now they'll be dead in a week." (Tinplate's death is implied rather than narrated.)

## Continuity Notes
- "Legio XX Valeria Vitrix" (Ch 21) is the same legion as "Legio Valeria Vitrix" in Ch 9's dream (where Percy's dream-body served). The text and the author's vault both use "Vitrix" (the vault file is `Legio XX Valeria Vitrix.md`). The Latin-correct "Victrix" is not used anywhere in these profiles (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md S9).
- **Petra:** in the text it is Petra's *cousin's* boy who was taken off Meridian (Ch 21); Petra's own boy is safe. The author's Plot Threads note saying "Petra's boy: sent to a front" is a vault slip (flagged in 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md §5).
- **Tertius Oban and the shear:** the shear is a sphere eleven kilometres in radius (C27); it swallowed Marien Pyrrhus's Reserve Echelon, about four million people, now MIA (C7).
- **Ten thousand years:** the Ch 14 narration's statement that the Lethe claimed a battalion "Ten thousand years ago" is consistent with an old Tartarus front; it is not a dating of the era.
- The narrators' genders are never stated.
- Ch 19 spells "Apollolonians" (sic) and "Demeter-Dionyosian" (sic).
