- **The remembered Thorn:** future Artemis (`C02_Artemis_Future_The_Director.md`) remembers Percy as he was in the original timeline, "lost outside of Westover Hall, facing the long-gone Dr. Thorn" (Ch13). That is the canon-style first meeting. In this timeline the encounter plays out differently.

## Appearance
He wears a faculty disguise and is "cunning enough to mask his scent" (Artemis). He has an "arrogant smirk" and "the sharp glint in his eyes." In monster form he has fur and bone, and a tail whose "spines... flaring wide."

## Personality
Arrogant, theatrical and cruel. He monologues ("telling of The Great Stirring and dark plans"), sneers at Grover, and goes after the helpless di Angelo siblings (the narration's "twins") first. At the end, faced with Percy's power, he shows "recognition," the first time Percy had seen that "from a monster before."

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Mocking, villain-speech cadence. He calls Percy "the Sea Lord's spawn" and "boy," and Grover "the goat-boy."
- "So, the Sea Lord's spawn has come to play hero. How quaint." (Ch15)
- "End? No, boy. This is only the beginning." (Ch15)
- "The Great Stirring will tear Olympus apart." (Ch15)
- "You're just a pawn, flailing in the tides you cannot control." (Ch15)
- "Ah, the goat-boy speaks. How touching. But futile." (Ch15)
- "But they die first today!" (Ch15)

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
A spined tail he can flare and launch. He can disguise himself and mask his scent. He also had mortal support: a "mortal patrol chopper" of mercenaries (Percy thinks of it as "The Helicopter of Mercs"), which the Hunters shot down (Ch16).

## Backstory
*Canon baseline (not story):* in *The Titan's Curse*, Dr. Thorn is a manticore posing as Westover Hall's vice principal. He tries to capture the di Angelo siblings, survives a fall from the cliff at Westover, and menaces the heroes again later in the book.

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch15:** The Hunt watches Westover. Artemis wants "confirmation that the Manticore is inside," and a scout reports: "He has taken the guise of a faculty member. There are two demigods. Young." Grover Iris-messages Percy. Percy and Eleni arrive by bolt-shard and mist travel. "The trio" (Percy, Eleni and Grover) follow the familiar script: they mist the teacher, go to the gym dance, and chase Thorn and the di Angelo siblings ("the twins" in the narration) outside. Eleni breaks off to deal with "multiple potentially hostile signatures, 800 meters northeast." In the clearing Thorn gives his speech and turns on Bianca and Nico.
- **Ch16:** He lunges. Percy's instinct sets off a hurricane-roar surge: frost, a maelstrom, and "A torrent of water, stained with white-gold and crimson." Thorn freezes in recognition, is pinned to a tree by "bands of color that writhed like living serpents," and disintegrates. Riptide is left dim and drained. The helicopter is shot down by Hunters' arrows. Percy later justifies himself to Artemis: "Thorn wasn't going to stop. Not unless someone stopped him."
