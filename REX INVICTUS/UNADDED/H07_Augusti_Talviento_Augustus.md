## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
Both are far-future, Voss-only characters with no present-day versions. The Augusti are "a Roman legacy clan", unrelated to the present-day Camp Jupiter Romans (`F17_Roman_Legion_Present.md`). They deal with **Minerva in Percy's vat-grown body** (`B02_Minerva_Percy_Clone_Interlude.md`), whom they call "Parlamentum", and with Verrill Chase (`F03_Verrill_Chase.md`). They know Minerva is looking for a road into the past to bring back four million soldiers.

## Appearance
- **Talviento:** sleek, blue, nearly skintight armor that clinks; a helmet shaped like a black demon mask showing his black eyes and mouth. Unmasked he is "younger than Minerva had guessed": dark curly hair and a scar through one eyebrow.
- **Augustus:** "a big man, heavy through the shoulders and going soft around the middle, with a gentle face and a grandfather's smile." When he moves his hand, "the air around his fingers bent slightly, like heat over a road"; when he smiles, "the lights in the room brightened by a fraction."
- **Bakuto bouncers** (Augusti staff): jade-green slab armor resembling feudal Chinese armor, stained-glass visors, halberds, each a head taller than Minerva.

## Personality
- **Talviento:**
  - Earnest and a bit naive. "I'm Romani!" he protests, "clearly missing the point" of Minerva's Spanish-name joke. He has a Chinese girlfriend.
  - Quick to swear when teased ("Fuck you.").
  - Idealistic about the family ("men and women of honor. Not villains") and a true believer that Augustus's rule would bring peace ("It will be like before the Wars").
  - Morally offended by harming children: "We don't even sell brain-ark to minors… Not like the CarniVoles."
  - Fair-dealing. He offers "Favor for a favor. Open contract," and he apologises sincerely for the fake book.
  - Perceptive. His face changes when Minerva admits she lied.
- **Augustus:**
  - Courteous, soft-spoken, menacing by implication.
  - Notices everything: he detected every one of Minerva's intrusions and praised the till hack.
  - Keeps strict rules and is a gracious loser: "I'm afraid, my dear, that my family appears to have been swindled. How embarrassing."
  - "He sounded like a disappointed grandfather."

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Talviento:** an armored enforcer; screens gamblers for precognition (Lambda-class and above are banned); has authority to issue contracts.
- **Augustus:** two divine bloodlines, visible as heat-shimmer and brightening light; he detects electronic intrusion; he commands the Bakuto, the "biggest commodity-cartel on this planet". His seventh-floor table brokers between pantheons. Guests include a raven-tattooed man (implied Norse) and the Dominion's attaché.
- **Family assets:** the Bakuto (a pagoda casino with a pachinko corridor, sumo ring, sushi bar and a lobby with a Lotus-like haze), Slag-knights (fighters), and the bouncers, who now hold Minerva's thermonuclear "Bellicose Folly".

## Backstory
The Augusti are one of the four families who take tribute from New Rome's gambling dens; the other three are "ordinary criminals". Augustus served the Princes in the War of Princes (per the author's vault, 16212–16227, ending about four years before the Ch 23 era). The family sells guns and brain-ark; "It's not illegal" because there is no government Watchpoint in Voss. A Slag-knight claimed to have extracted a jade book (supposedly the Jade Court's Book of Life and Death) from Martial Monastery Vortern, paying "180 degrees of a drach". It was a forgery.

## Story Arc So Far
**Ch 23:**
1. Talviento chaperones Minerva at roulette, checks her for precognition, banters, and offers the contract: kill nine Rattlebone Rustabout leaders, with no civilians and no dogs. In exchange Minerva gets Verrill and a seat at "the table".

## Continuity Notes
- Talviento is "Romani" (he means Roman). The narration uses he/him for Minerva in parts of this section.
- The currency is written variously as "drachamae", "degrees of a drach" and "drachs". Author's vault: 1 drachma is roughly US$1,000, subdivided into degrees (°).
- **"Second-Born" tension (story-level, flagged for the author in 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md §5):** the text calls Augustus "a Second-Born demigod in the Princes' service" (Ch 23). The author's vault defines Second-Born Demigods as the legacies the gods elevated in 16212, who sided with the Empire *against* the Pure-Demigod Princes. Either Augustus is a defector, or this is a text/vault tension. Keep the text's wording.
- **His table:** the text shows only "The raven-tattooed man" (implied Norse) and "The Dominion's attaché" at the seventh-floor table. The Jade Court is named only in Talviento's payment line, so do not place Jade Court dealers at the table as fact.
