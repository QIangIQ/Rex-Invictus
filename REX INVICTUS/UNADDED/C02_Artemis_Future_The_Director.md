## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **This file:** the Artemis of the far-future Empire. She rules surveillance and counter-espionage across the Empire, keeps her Hunt on fortress-moons, and is bound in a political marriage to the legendary Lord of Demigods / Lord Imperator, Perseus Jackson.
- **Present-day Artemis** (`C01_Artemis_Present.md`) is the goddess of the present day (c. 2021 per the author's timeline; nominally the canon PJO era) who meets the reincarnated Percy, a young teen (the text calls him sixteen; the premise blurb says twelve; see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R1). The text makes them a single continuous person across a time loop. In Ch23 the Director remembers "a night on Long Island when soldiers fell out of the sky" (Ch22) and remembers being refused Zoë's death date "because some future version of me had sealed it". Then she realises "It's tonight", and becomes that sealer. Present-day Artemis hates this woman for it (Ch22).
- **Him Within the Moon** (`A05_Him_Within_the_Moon.md`) is the shard of Percy she guards in Luna's core. He is not her husband himself: "He is not Percy Jackson… Not anymore. Not ever."
- **The crimson-haired Artemis (Ch14 vision).** Percy sees in the sea "Not the one he knew in the myths, or the goddess who glared at him across council chambers. But another version… Hair like crimson starlight and frost," with a look of "Grief? Regret? Love?" The text never names her. The best fit is this future Artemis: Ch5 calls the future Artemis "red-haired", and the vision's grief and love match Ch13. It could also be a resurfacing stolen memory. **Treat it as ambiguous.**
- **Ch5 versus Ch13/23 tone.** The Ch5 Artemis is lazy and dismissive, with no visible reaction when Athena calls Percy "that man" and Zeus grumbles "I knew Poseidon's children were unreliable" (Ch5; see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C9). The Ch13/23 Artemis is grieving and devoted. Two readings fit the text: (a) the public "persona" Thalia describes in Ch23, which "the Empire needed her to be", or (b) the timeline shifting ("Something about this reality had changed slightly", Ch14). Neither is confirmed.
- **Not "Commander Thaleia"**, and not Thalia (`C07_Thalia_Grace_Future_Lieutenant.md`), her Lieutenant.

## Appearance
- **Ch5:** "the red-haired huntress sitting lazily in her marbled throne, idly picking lazily at something under her fingernails with her silver dagger". She wears a blindfold "helpfully provided by Apollo", and "a sliver [sic] glow seeped past the gold-weave of the fabric" when she scans.
- **Ch13:** "almost exactly like how she had been described in the old epics. The silver gossamer tunic, covered by numerous pouches and gear. Her silver eyes…" Her bow, "which entire generations of Hunters have not even seen", is slung on her back. In battle it unfolds into "a thing made of raw moonlight, twice as tall as a mortal". Her face carries "the weight of centuries" and "tiredness".
- **Ch23:** "a woman in grey and silver with a bow on her back and a face like a winter morning". Her boots make no sound on marble. She arrives as "a silver flicker" at the edge of the augurs. Afterwards her hands shake "so badly that she had to fold them behind her back".
- **Insignia:** a crescent-moon seal (the Director's seal is stamped on Hunter pauldrons, Ch23).
- **Iconography in the Empire:** temple statues of "a silver woman with a bow" before which Apollonian and Artemisian children swear oaths. Posters on children's walls (Cerelli, Ch22; Verrill Chase, Ch23). Prayer icons on rifle stocks (Dace, Ch22). Cerelli: "It was nothing like the real thing."

## Personality
- **Weary duty.** "I had to do it… For peace. For stability. For the Empire" (Ch13). "Now I watch over moons I do not sleep on. I command girls who no longer run beneath the stars but on fortress-moons." She has not hunted "in millennia" and yearns for forests: "Sometimes, I wonder if any part of me still remembers how to hunt."
- **Buried love.** She calls it "a quiet love. The kind that hides beneath duty. That goes unspoken. That will never, ever be returned." Asked why it will never be returned: "Because I am me." She loved Percy "because he was real with me. Even when I hated him for it… And because he never wanted me to change."
- **A wound she cannot close.** "He is… my wound. A divine wound." She tells the shard: "I hated you… For what you did. For being forced into it… I don't anymore."
- **Cryptic, terse authority.** To two junior Hunters: "Watch carefully… Even the smallest stone may bruise the skin of a god" (Ch13). To Aphrodite: "Sister's Work… Told me something interesting" (Ch23).
- **Calm lethality.** She walks alone into a cult bombardment to "tell them" they have "misunderstood everything" (Ch13).
- **Secrecy as care.** She seals Zoë's death record "INCLUDING THE DIRECTOR, IN ANY EPOCH". That keeps the information from her own past self, and she knows it.
- **"Nights."** Every few centuries "something would reach in under the persona, and she would stop sleeping". Then "the Hunt went out" (Ch23). She keeps a scrap of blue cloth that smells of "salt and sea wind" in a sealed case, and every Hound of the Luna kennel-crypt is trained on it as a pup.
- **Ch5 persona:** lazy, mildly teasing ("If only to humour you, Theenie"), dismissive of anomalies ("The spike was surely a glitch in the system, I am sure"), but instantly sharp when the data surprises her ("Wait.").
- **Severe with her people, protective of them.** Imperial doctrine: soldiers who fire on the Director "will be held accountable under Imperial law" (Ch22). Her "standing orders" about the Lord Imperator are "Personal ones. Nobody gets to lay a hand on it before she does" (Ch22).

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Celestial surveillance.** In Ch5 she checks Gaia through "the old Iris cameras on Luna's orbital platforms", "orbital saturators" and "Hephaestus' dyson sphere automatons", and finds the energy spike in Athena's former palace on Old Earth. That spike is old Percy leaving for the past. In Ch13 she runs "a surveillance network that ensures the safety of thousands of trillions." Doctrine: "Any contact above god-tier threshold is flagged straight to the Director" (Ch22).
- **Archery.** One starlight shaft compacts a cult warship into "a neat sphere of divine steel, barely an arm's length across" (Ch13).
- **Defence.** She raises a silver wall against a plasma barrage (Ch13).
- **Movement and command.** She flashes with Thalia and commands through a "comm-thread" (Ch13). She moves across the Empire at will (11 minutes to reach the Rose Gallery, Ch23).
- **Bond-magic.** She is the only one besides Thalia who can see Him Within the Moon, who "formed from my energy alone with Anaklusmos". She weaves a "thread of moonlight" that sends him into the past, "a world of cabins, of campfires" (Ch13). Minerva: "Artemis was the anchor… She always was."
- **Authority.** She issues Répertoire-wide seals "under Directorate authority", binds personnel under the 14th Undying Law, and reclassifies casualties (Ch23).
- **Forces:** the Hunters of Artemis as a military order on Luna and Luna-Secundus (Ch12–13), with ritual-spliced gunlines, the 6th Moonwall, shieldmaidens, flame hunters, sister-augurs and the Hounds of Artemis. The Psiloi orbital marksmen are her oath-sworn "lines" (Ch22). Hunters' orbital bombardment glassed "Traitor moon S3-R12-P1-M2" (Ch18).
- **Containment:** "Lord Hades and the Director are almost permanently helping us hold the containment lines" in Tartarus (Ch22).
- **Author's vault note (not yet in text):** every major UOE moon keeps a kennel-crypt of at least 40 active Hounds of Artemis (domesticated wolves and werewolves) with an embedded Hunter war-keeper and a Commander, reinforced from Lupa and the Wolf House. The vault names eighteen Hound pack-leaders, among them Columbina and Scaramucius (both on-page in Ch23). In the Great Expansion (3047) she took control of every moon under UOE control, finished the orbital platforms above Gaia and forbade men from her territory; Ares's scouts who strayed in vanished (Annex 1023). Ch5's "orbital saturators" and Iris cameras on Luna fit this.
- **Namesake weapon:** an Imperial file, "PROJECT ARTEMIS: GRAVITIC ORDINANCE & ORBITAL DISPLACEMENT CANNON UPON PLANETARY SATELLITES" (Ch10). The text does not say she controls it.
- **Equipment:** silver dagger (Ch5), moonlight bow, Apollo's gold-weave scanning blindfold (Ch5), and the sealed case holding the blue cloth (Ch23).

## Backstory
- **Meeting Percy:** she remembers him as "the boy… lost outside of Westover Hall, facing the long-gone Dr. Thorn. The one who had changed her, even if she never said it" (Ch13).
- **Caligo Vale (Ch13):** "Caligo Vale did not matter. Its location was chosen by Hera on a whim, but the bond was a necessity. The Olympian Court was crumbling. Poseidon would not act. Zeus would not bend. The Empire needed a stabilizer, someone to represent both the human demigod legacy and divine continuity." Percy, as "Guardian of Gaia", "would not intrude as much as any other entity eligible under Poseidon's dominion." "Deep down, I had already given him a part of myself long before then."
- **Cult and legend (Eleni, Ch15; reliability uncertain):** "the Moonborn's forbidden devotion to the Tidebearer"; "the Silent Pact" (she would not speak his name "lest the sky itself weep"); "his blade never struck true unless she had watched its arc"; marble statues of them on Caligo Vale; Hunters wearing silver-green remembrance bands; she "carved his name into the surface of Luna Secundus with her arrows" when he vanished from history; she "wept beneath a black eclipse when he was re-exiled into the Lands of our Birth, Gaia"; "The Lord of Demigods did indeed marry the Huntress of the Moon", and a wedding mural exists.
- **Future Poseidon (Ch15):** "The Avatar, Arty's obsession… He did it for her, didn't he?" This implies Percy went back in time for her.
- **Future Minerva (Ch12):** "Artemis always preferred it warmer" (the pitch of Percy's voice). In Ch23, Minerva calls her "this body's wife".
- **Future Aphrodite (Ch23):** "She's always been like that. Even when she was young. Even when she was just a girl on a dock with a boy who could never stay out of trouble."
- **Zoë:** her "First of the Director's Lieutenants", whose name is on the Wall and taught to schoolchildren. Thalia has been her Lieutenant for ~14,000 years.

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch5 (Intermission, far future):** at the bi-annual meeting of Divine Beings, Zeus asks "Artemis dear" to check Gaia. Blindfolded, she reports all clear except "an insane spike of energy from a few days ago for a few milliseconds", dismisses it as "a glitch", then pinpoints it to "your former palace Athena". This is the moment old Percy left.
- **Ch10:** Minerva, newly awake in Percy's clone body, finds the classified "PROJECT ARTEMIS" file. She thinks "Her Sister's name? No… Her creator's sister."
- **Ch12:** Luna-Secundus, held by her Hunters for 17 months against the Hollow Crescent, falls to Virre and Orran Arenberg. A Hunter sergeant prays, "Lady Artemis save our souls"; a sister-augur reports "Artemis has been warned."
- **Ch13:** she arrives at the Luna bastion with Thalia and senior Hunters "from Eos to Hecatia", speaks briefly to Sylvi Morn and Kaia Telan, and rides to the Moon's hollow core. She shows Thalia Him Within the Moon and confesses Caligo Vale and her quiet love. The Hollow Crescent attacks, chanting to raise "Perseus". She seals the sanctum, compacts a warship with one arrow, raises a silver wall and walks alone toward the bombardment. Him rises as a colossus and shields her, catching a dark-energy lance, shattering beams "into salt", raising a constellation-shimmering wall of water and crushing a cruiser. "Perseus," she says quietly; "You shouldn't have answered me. Not now. Not like this." Their private exchange: "Then you still—" / "Always." She tells him "I hated you… I don't anymore", touches two fingers to his chest, and admits "I don't want this part of you to go." He says his "Original" is calling from the past. She threads moonlight to send him: "Then go to him, Anaklusmos…"
- **Ch14:** a crimson-haired Artemis appears in Percy's reflection in the Sea of Monsters. This is probably her (ambiguous).
- **Ch16:** the Riptide fragment shows present-day Artemis visions of this era: warships, Hunters on the lunar surface, "herself raising her bow against fire that split the stars… a reflection of him, colossal and weary, shielding her". That is the Ch13 battle seen from the past.
- **Ch22 (referenced by Imperials in the past):** she is the object of Imperial doctrine, oaths and fear. She has "personal" standing orders about the Lord Imperator. She has sealed Zoë's death date, which Castellan Oriel refuses to reveal even under her younger self's command.
- **Ch23 (Interlude):** eleven minutes after the Répertoire confirms that the Lord Imperator's key was accepted 14,000 years in the past, she arrives in the Rose Gallery of the *Aphrodite's Grace*. Aphrodite greets her as "Sister-in-law" and needles her: "You were already there." She remembers Long Island and Oriel's refusal, says "It's tonight", and issues the seals: all wound data under Directorate authority, all knowing personnel under the 14th Undying Law, Zoë's record sealed "IN ANY EPOCH", and "THE PYRRHUIESIAN RESERVE ECHELON, SECOND ROTATION" (Marien Pyrrhus's lost echelon, about four million people) amended from "KILLED IN ACTION, PRESUMED" to "MISSING IN ACTION" with "LEAVE SPACE ON WALL". She comes back up "four hours later with her face like a winter morning" and her hands shaking. Twelve hours later every Hunter has orders: "Anything that carries his scent, his signature or his key… Do not approach. Do not engage. Find it." Her Hunt, under Thalia, chases a Percy look-alike (future Minerva) across New Rome in the Voss system. At chapter's end Thalia says: "She's already on her way."

**Do / Don't:**
- DO keep her quiet, economical and heavy with unspoken grief. Silences are part of her voice.
- DO show the persona cracking only slightly: a caught breath, shaking hands hidden behind her back, a "ghost of a smile".
- DO let her be absolute and cold in command, and protective of her Hunt and of anything touching Percy.
- DON'T make her flustered, petty or playful like her younger self (C01). That warmth is buried here.
- DON'T reveal Zoë's death details. Don't confirm the Ch19 tent events or other present-day private matters beyond what Ch23 shows she remembers.
- DON'T call Him Within the Moon "Percy Jackson" without qualification. She insists he is "not Percy Jackson… Not anymore."
- DON'T call her "Director of Camp Half-Blood" (that Accord witness is the camp's director; C4), and don't say the Shear swallowed Percy (it swallowed Marien's echelon; C7).
- DON'T explain why the Hunt never found Percy's scent. It is an intentional mystery (C6).
