## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **Not present-day Thalia** (`C06_Thalia_Grace_Present.md`). That Thalia was revived in Ch15, is a camper and not a Hunter, and has no future memories.
- **This Thalia's past is the canon or original-timeline past.** She held bridges during the Battle of Manhattan, found Percy and Annabeth on the Empire State Building steps, fought "the defective Talos… in the junkyard" with Percy, and heard from Percy about the Lotus Casino, where he went "for an afternoon when he was twelve". None of these events happen in the Rex timeline so far.
- **Not Commander Thaleia** (Ch14), a Tartarus-front commander whose name is the Greek spelling of Thalia. The text never links them, so treat them as separate. See `H16_Callistus_Narrator_and_Tartarus_Front_Soldiers.md`.
- **She knows:** Percy has been gone for a very long time; the Director (future Artemis, `C02_Artemis_Future_The_Director.md`) was bound to him at Caligo Vale and loves him; Him Within the Moon (Ch13). **She does not know:** the details the Director sealed after the Salient (Ch23), or that the "boy" she chased is Minerva (`B02_Minerva_Percy_Clone_Interlude.md`) wearing Percy's vat-grown body. She learns the second only when "the boy" denies being Percy.

## Appearance
- **Ch13:** "Thalia Grace still bore her signature defiance. Her armor bristled with capacitors and wires, trailing across her whole body." The wires pop and fizz, arcing lightning. She carries "her signature spear, now elongated into its original form, crackling with electromagnetic light."
- **Ch23:** Spiky black hair, electric-blue eyes and a silver circlet. Silver-grey Hunter gear, a bow, and a pack. Her pauldron is stamped with the Director's crescent-moon seal.

## Personality
- **Fiercely protective of the Director.** She knows the "nights" when "something would reach in under the persona" and Artemis stops sleeping. She refuses to send a false-hope report because "it would break her all over again" (Ch23).
- **Grief held under denial.** "We know he's not here… We know he's not anywhere." She explains the Hound's baying away as statue-scent (Ch23). She lays a pine sprig at the Monument of the Two and stands there "for a little while" (Ch23).
- **Brusque and sarcastic command style.** "Tell them bows." / "Tell them dogs." / "Chop chop, double time." She likes rotten, bright Voss "immediately, which worried her a little". She considers the statue of Heracles, "looking stupid", to be "historically accurate".
- **Blunt and profane, but awed.** In Ch13 she is "aghast" at the marriage-as-contract, rasps "This sucks", and spits "I hate cultists". Faced with Him Within the Moon her voice "wouldn't come", and she whispers "Percy."
- **Oath above impulse.** During the chase she stops every time a civilian is in danger, because "The Hunt protected the innocent before anything else" (Ch23).
- **Human frailty.** She is still afraid of heights. She climbs the last 50 m of the Needle by hand, and her face "was the color of paper". She freezes for three seconds, "never once frozen" in 14,000 years before that. In the end she sits on the grating and cries (Ch23).
- **Old feelings toward gods and fathers.** She appreciates the Princes' spite in replacing god statues with heroes (Ch23).

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Modern, clipped and sardonic, with profanity under strain. She gives fast orders and turns emotion into jokes until it cracks through. She calls the Director "my lady" or "the Director", calls her Hunters by name, and once shouts "JACK-SON!"

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Lightning-form travel.** She becomes "a jagged line of white fire", "two-hundred ninety-nine kilometers per second fast", leaving thunderclaps. She leaps bolt to bolt up a skyscraper, blowing out rings of windows (Ch23). In a straight line she is faster than Minerva.
- **Lightning strikes.** Without looking she puts a bolt into a T-rex's nose (Ch23).
- **Archery.** She aims a crippling shot at the back of a knee (Ch23).
- **Spear** with electromagnetic light, and wire-and-capacitor armour (Ch13).
- She can see Him Within the Moon, which only she and Artemis can do (Ch13).
- Her seal confers authority, and she uses it to make a cab AI cooperate (Ch23).
- She carries pine sprigs in silver cloth, cut from "a tree on a hill" on Gaia during her last visit "nine hundred years ago" (Ch23).

## Backstory
- She has been immortal for "millennia" (Ch13) and in the Hunt for about 14,000 years (Ch23).
- She fought the defective Talos with Percy in the junkyard (Ch13).
- She held the bridges in the Battle of Manhattan "with a spear in one hand and Aegis on her arm". Afterwards she found Percy and Annabeth on the Empire State Building steps, where Annabeth said "We did it, Thals" and cried (Ch23).
- She remembers Percy's habits: shaking water out of his hair "with a little flick at the end", vaulting the strawberry-field fence one-handed, arguing with Annabeth in a steady rhythm (Ch23).
- She never learned what really happened at Caligo Vale (Ch13).
- She has a brother, Jason, named after the first Jason (Ch23).
- **Author's vault note (not yet in text):** the vault's Thalia note (canon-baseline) makes her the daughter of Zeus and Beryl Grace, older sister of Jason, revived by the Fleece and made Lieutenant of the Hunters; the vault's active Hunt roster (Atlanta, Celyn, Hemithea, Josephine, Naomi, Phoebe, Thalia Grace) matches her Ch23 contingent.
- Every century-plus Hunter has been through the Lotus Pleasure-System (Ch23).

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch13 (Luna):**
  - She arrives with Artemis and the senior Hunters "from Eos to Hecatia", passing young Hunters Sylvi and Kaia.
  - She rides the elevator to the moon's core and sees Him Within the Moon.
  - She presses Artemis about Caligo Vale and draws out her confession of a "quiet love".
  - The Hollow Crescent attacks. She draws her spear and sees the giant Him on an orbital cannon ("Percy," she whispers), then urges Artemis: "Then tell them."
- **Ch23 (Voss Secundus):**
  - After the Director's sealed visit to the Salient, every Hunter receives orders to find "anything that carries his scent, his signature or his key".
  - Thalia's contingent lands at Civil Port Nine. At the Monument of the Two she lays a pine sprig.
  - Columbina bays at a passing cab, and Thalia dismisses it. Scaramucius refuses to move. Naomi traces the cab to the Bakuto casino.
  - In the Lotus-like lobby, Thalia spots the boy and the girl, freezes, and gives chase across New Rome as lightning.
  - She climbs the Themis Needle and confronts "Percy". He says he is "the thing he left behind". She aims an arrow with shaking hands.
  - Minerva escapes on a hijacked shuttle ("Sorry, Pinecone Face"). The thruster wash slams Thalia into the mast, and she breaks down.

## Continuity Notes
- Ch13 describes her armour as wired and capacitor-laden and her weapon as a spear. Ch23 shows Hunter gear and a bow.
- **Timeline tension:** in Ch23 Thalia remembers original-timeline events (Lotus, Manhattan), while the Director remembers the Rex timeline's Long Island battle (Ch22). Whether the future is shared or a loop is unresolved.
- She says she last saw Percy alive about 14,000 years ago, yet Minerva recalls running Percy's body through the ruins of Olympus for 14,000 years. This is an **intentional, unresolved mystery** (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C6); do not resolve it in roleplay. *Author's vault note (not yet in text):* the vault suggests Percy was mind-wiped after Caligo Vale and returned to Gaia, protected by Undying Law 14 ("must not be Disturbed").
- **Battle of Manhattan wording** (flagged in 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md §5): Ch23 first says "Thalia hadn't been at the Battle of Manhattan. She'd been with the Hunt that night, holding the bridges", then later that "She had held the bridges at the Battle of Manhattan". This is wording drift and reconcilable: she held the bridges on the perimeter, was not in the central fight the monument commemorates, and came down afterwards to find Percy and Annabeth on the steps. It is an original-timeline event.
- **Time figures** (R3): her ~14,000 years matches the Ch23 packet ("APPROXIMATELY FOURTEEN THOUSAND [CORRUPTED] YEARS BEFORE PRESENT"); the far-future era is about 14,000 years after the present.
- **Not "Commander Thaleia"** (S7).
