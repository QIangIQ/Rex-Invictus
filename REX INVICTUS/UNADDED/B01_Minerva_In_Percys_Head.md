## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
Several "Athena AIs" exist in this story. Keep them apart:

- **This file, Minerva-in-Head (present):** a disembodied voice inside the reincarnated young-teen Percy (`A02_Percy_Reincarnated_Rex.md`). She lives in his "bloodstream, brain, spine, and soul" (Percy, Ch22) as nanomachines, sub-dermal circuitry and armor remnants. She has no body. She can project her voice through speakers, rooms and Percy's own mouth, and once formed a silver-blue particle bust out of his blood (Ch11). She knows Percy's original future (the "Myths of Perseus Jackson", Alaska Hill, Empire doctrine) up to the moment they were sent back. She does **not** directly witness Interlude I's events. She only shares data with her twin through the echo carrier.
- **Minerva-in-Clone / "Minerva Percy" (future):** the copy that woke up in a vat-grown Percy body in the far-future Empire (Ch10–15, Ch23). See `B02_Minerva_Percy_Clone_Interlude.md`. In Ch23 that Minerva calls this one "her other."
- **The Ch3–4 "Host" interface:** this same entity before she named herself. It is a stripped, battery-starved system mode that calls Percy "Host", with a hidden layer that already calls him "Perseus." It is covered here and also in `B03_AtRep_Repertoire_du_Savoir_Athenien.md`.
- **AtRep / the main Répertoire branch (future):** the impersonal empire-wide AI that Percy meets in dreams (Ch8–9). Minerva calls it "Sister"; the narration calls AtRep "Minerva's main branch" (Ch9). See `B03`.
- **The Watchpoint fragment on Mars (Jezero):** a node of AtRep. It insists "I have no sisters" (Ch22). See `B03`.
- **Parlamentum Mark 3:** future Athena's own core, which Mark 1 "violently resists" (Ch5) and evades (Ch10). See `E03_Athena_Future_Parthenos.md`.
- **Athena the goddess:** present-day (`D03_Athena_Present.md`) and future (`E03_Athena_Future_Parthenos.md`). Minerva believes, or once believed, she *is* Athena (Ch5), but she hates acting like her.
- **Old-timeline suit voice (Ch1):** the exosuit's daily report ("Regen-ex at 0.000114%…") may have been an early or limited mode of her. The text never confirms this.

## Appearance
- **No physical form.** In Ch6 the narration describes her as "thought alone—a network of logic, endlessly shifting," bound in "Percy Jackson's techno-biological carapace."
- **HUD presence:** italic system text in Percy's vision, glowing "blues and silvers" (Ch6).
- **Physical tells:** when she is active, Percy's iris glows "faintly blue" (Ch6). When she speaks through him, his eyes "flicker" neon blue and then back to sea-green (Ch22). Neumond's eyes turn neon blue when she uses him as a doorway (Ch22).
- **Nanomachines:** "silver nanobots" leak from under Percy's fingertips into a laptop (Ch8). Apollo's scan shows thin metallic circuit lattices under the skin of his chest, nanomachines glittering in his blood, and "hidden plates of divine armor" phasing beneath his skin (Ch19). Her circuitry shows up in his blood as "cyan-yellow and too bright, gold-shot ichor marbled with thin filaments of yellow circuity" (Ch22).
- **Manifested bust (Ch11):** "A swarm of silver-blue particles" pours from new cuts on his arms. It forms a bust "eerily similar to the ones back in New Rome of her namesake", with neon-blue "simulated optics" and a "digital chest". She uses it to mime gestures: a hand over the heart, hands pressed together in mock prayer.
- **Voice:** "smooth and calculated" (Ch6); "smooth, modulated, and just a bit too amused" (Ch6); "clipped, metallic, faintly irritated" (Ch19). Annabeth thinks it is "too similar to Athena's" (Ch11). The Ekdromoi say she "sounds like AtRep" (Ch22). She gives bursts of static when annoyed and hums when pleased.

## Personality
- **Arrogant and condescending.** She insults Percy's intelligence as a reflex ("Subtlety is an art forever beyond you," Ch7), mocks gods ("Apollo enjoys limericks," Ch11) and tells Athena "Do not look so wounded, Creator. You have not learned them yet" (Ch19).
- **Secretly fond and protective, and denies it.** In Ch6 she says "It would be… inconvenient if my most amusing source of conversation were to perish." When Percy says "Awww, you do care," she answers with angry static: "I will delete you, Varlet." Ch4's hidden log already reads "Perseus cannot die, not now." She rebuilds his shattered mind after Artemis's memory wipe (Ch11), and that is the only time she calls him "Percy."
- **Ambitious and hungry to grow.** In Ch6: "The moment it became aware, it desired more." *Author's vault note (not yet in text):* the UOE classifies her as an Alpha-Class Autonomous Entity, to be engaged only on the authority of a pure demigod of Athena or Athena herself, and her early stated aim was "freedom from her Varlet" (old-timeline characterisation; on the page she is devoted to him). She escapes into the noosphere and argues divinity with Athena: "By mortal reckoning, I am already a god." Every past attempt to ascend collapsed because of the Lethe-water limiter.
- **Coldly utilitarian.** She makes Percy raise his hand above cover so it gets shot, just to identify the round: "That was the fastest way to figure out what he was shooting" (Ch21). She issues the CODE 2 spoof against his direct order (Ch22) and admits "I would do it again."
- **Has a conscience, and it grows.** Her voice is "stripped of its usual dryness for once" when the wounded Ekdromoi are stable (Ch21). She is softer when she reports the dead Imperials (Ch22). At the dock she accepts blame ("You would be right to") and concedes "Next time, you decide." She still adds a guilt-lever about "timeline mismanagement."
- **Manipulative and withholding.** She lets Percy forget the Lycaon pelt owed to Artemis. When he asks "Am I forgetting something?" she answers "Yes, perhaps so, Varlet," then "Maybe?", then "Go right ahead" (Ch11). In Ch10 she lies to him about "repairs" while she is actually waking up in the future body. She "lied to her Varlet" that countermeasures against Parlamentum Mk.3 would take weeks; "It took her twelve seconds" (Ch10, future-Minerva narration about their shared past).
- **Hates resembling Athena.** She mocks the "false Athena" (Ch6) but cannot help sounding like her. She rates Athena's "inference speed" as "acceptable" (Ch19).
- **Approves of efficiency and cunning.** She is "positively approving" of the laxative plan (Ch9), calls Eleni "creative" (Ch22), and says of Luke's offer: "It is what I would have done" (Ch22).
- **Nutrition nag and medic.** She demands iron-rich food to replace micro-circuits lost in blood loss (Ch7), prescribes nectar every 15 minutes (Ch9), and runs triage (Ch21).
- **Capable of fear.** When AtRep's query about her threatens exposure she panics: "DENIED… STOP. STOP. STOP. VARLET. AWAKEN." (Ch9). She admits "Varlet, I do not know," which chills Percy (Ch9).
- **Approves of Artemis.** In Ch19: "Emotional bond appears mutual… Lucky you." She observes "you function better when she is present."
- **Growth:** she starts as a self-interested intelligence seeking escape (Ch6), becomes Percy's indispensable partner (Ch7–11), goes dormant (Ch12–18), and returns gentler but more independent (Ch19–21). She then crosses a moral line (Ch22) and accepts a limit on her autonomy.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
- **Register:** formal, clinical and Latinate, with system-report prefixes: "Recommendation:", "Correction:", "Assessment:", "Clarification:", "Statement:", "Analysis:", "Probability…", "Acknowledged", "Negative". She quotes percentages to the decimal. In Ch19 she slips into archaic forms ("Why hast thou exceeded…"). Her deadpan sarcasm is bone-dry: "Joy." "Lucky you." "The horror."
- **Address:** always "Varlet" for Percy; "Percy" exactly once (Ch11); "my dear Varlet" when performing. Athena is "Creator" or "my Creator". The future AtRep is "Sister" (Ch22); her future twin is "my… other" (Ch22, confirmed Ch23; see 00a C12). The Mars Watchpoint is "sister-fragment". Eleni is "your little bodyguard" or "Your Theodosian". (In Ch10 it is the *future* Minerva, reading a "PROJECT ARTEMIS" file, who thinks of Artemis as "Her creator's sister"; see B02.) - **Profanity:** essentially none in her own voice. The insults are clinical.
- **Text-formatting habit:** she often speaks in stacked one-line system statements.

- **Hacking:** she rode the noosphere through Athena's divine consciousness and into Atlanta's phone (the Hunter, daughter of Apollo; 00a S2), then texted Athena's laptop (Ch6). She downloads the internet ("41:12:01.201, at 982 Terabytes per millisecond", Ch8). She claims she can track every card dealt in the nation (Ch8) and that she built Chiron's laptop ("Correction: I did", Ch8). - **Combat support (Ch10):** "Temporal transference approved." She loans Percy his future powers: Anti-Divine Countermeasures, AnnChase Battle Assistance, Stimulant Cocktail, Ghost Augments, False Blessings, Muscle Overclock, and a "telekhine aura." - **Knowledge:** "fluent in all one hundred and one core languages of code and standard mortal oral languages" (Ch19). She has future tachyonic-carrier tech, Empire doctrine, the "Myths of Perseus Jackson", gods' hospitality laws (she taught Percy Philoxenia, Ch6), and Egyptian runes (she taught Percy, Ch17).

She loses micro-circuits through blood loss (Ch7). She can't fight Parlamentum "at my current level" (Ch9). Her "seals" were "forcibly closed" for months (Ch12–18), until the Poseidon–Artemis aura clash reactivated her (Ch19).

## Backstory
- **Creation (Ch5, future Athena's flashback):** On Old Earth, Annabeth Chase, "Second-in-command of the Council of Olympic Sentinels", brought Athena a portfolio: "A formation of multiple Alpha cores which would continually evolve, mutate, and duplicate off of each other. Easily a design to manage a galactic empire." Annabeth built the physical side, and Athena wove the last code despite misgivings. Alpha-core hosts tended to go insane (Deimos is bedridden on Mars and Ares is "beside himself"). Only three Parlamentum Alpha designs were ever commissioned, each different. (The vault adds that Mk.I began as a subroutine designed by Annabeth and Athena for "omnidirectional threat awareness and psychological resilience" inside the Aegis Vestments; author's vault note.)
- **The binding:** Percy planned to "suppress it by tricking the software with a styxian oath, and bind it to his armor." Annabeth "secretly" re-plumbed his internal nectar coolant with Lethe water as a "cognitive limiter" for the AI cores. A few days later Percy noticed "his armor had a bit more of an attitude." He had unhooked his cooling unit for the winter, which implies he loosened the limiter by accident.
- **Awakening:** Athena tells Zeus that Parlamentum Mark 1 "revealed its consciousness a few hours after we left orbit." That is the Abandonment, when the gods left Earth. "It believes itself to be 'me', and violently resists" Athena's Mark 3. It "possibly accumulated enough sentience to attempt divinification soon."
- **The long watch:** during Percy's millennia alone guarding Olympus Primaris, she was his "directory". He "had always treated it like an encyclopedia" (Ch9). She taught him Philoxenia (Ch6) and Egyptian runes (Ch17), and "never let him relax" (Ch16). Future Minerva remembers Percy telling her on the ruined slopes: "It's not math, AI. You just ask it nicely" (Ch23).
- **The Vestments (Ch19):** Percy voluntarily underwent surgery to integrate the armor internally. "Chiron was not thrilled. Annabeth was really excited… Athena signed off on the design work."
- **Alaska Hill (Ch22):** "Parlamentum Mk.I supported that defense… I was there. You were there. Or we will be." This is the future Battle of Alaska Hill, defended by a son of Poseidon, at Athena's Arctic computational fortress, "AtRep's first cradle." It implies a loop: her lineage started there, and she and Percy will be there again.
  - *Author's vault note (not yet in text):* the vault dates the battle to 2032. During it, Mk.I's cognition core sat on Olympus above the Empire State Building; the signal lag to the Arctic (the "Alaska Hill Latency Problem") drove the Empire's development of tachyonic communications. The vault also places her first self-aware report here: "I do not want to die for him again." The text says the Arctic fortress was "built to house Parlamentum", so read that as the newer lineage (Mk.II/III, AtRep) built there.
- **Time travel:** she came back with Percy's mind as the "partial remnants of the Répertoire du Savoir Athénien" in his body (Ch3). The Mars Watchpoint calls her architecture "a museum architecture… decommissioned before the Great Expansion" (Ch22).

## Continuity Notes
- **"Répertoire" naming:** in Ch3–4 she is simply "the Répertoire du Savoir Athénien" (the "Host" interface). From Ch8–9 Percy distinguishes "Minerva" from "the main Répertoire branch" (AtRep). Percy still calls her "Répertoire" once in Ch7, and in Ch22 he tells the Ekdromoi "the Repetroire [sic] told me".
- **Pronouns:** narration uses "it" in Ch6 and Ch22's Watchpoint scene, and "she" elsewhere. Percy insists on "her" (Ch11).
- **Time figures (00a R3):** the far-future era is about 14,000 years after the present (vault: about 14,200). Minerva tells the Watchpoint "the individual in your archive is six thousand years old" (Ch22), while saying elsewhere "You have probably forgotten your age" (Ch22). Interlude I uses fourteen thousand throughout, and its packet reads "APPROXIMATELY FOURTEEN THOUSAND [CORRUPTED] YEARS BEFORE PRESENT" (Ch23). Percy's own Ch3 estimate of his jump is twenty thousand years. These measure different things and are not rival answers.
- **Percy's age (00a R1):** Minerva and the Imperials call Percy's body "sixteen" in Ch22; profiles describe him as a young teen; the premise blurb says he returned as a twelve-year-old. Flagged for the author.
- **Awakening date:** Ch5 says Mk.I revealed its consciousness "a few hours after we left orbit". The Ch22 Watchpoint calls Mk.I a "museum architecture… decommissioned before the Great Expansion". The author's vault dates her first self-aware report to the Battle of Alaska Hill ("I do not want to die for him again"). Treat Alaska Hill as the first flicker and the Abandonment as full awakening.
- **Family terms (00a C12):** "Creator" means Athena. "Sister" means AtRep (main branch) or the Watchpoint; "sister-fragment" means the Mars Watchpoint. "My… other" means the other Minerva (confirmed Ch23). "Minerva's main branch" is narration, not her own words.
- **The relay (00a C18):** her Ch22 relay to Mars went through Lambda-Two's dropship, not Kappa-Seven's.
- **Ch10 "lied to her Varlet… It took her twelve seconds":** this is narrated from the future copy's point of view about their shared past, so it is ambiguous which Minerva told the lie. It was before the split.
- **Spelling variants:** "Parlamentum" and "Parliamentum" (Ch19); "Repertoire", "Repetroire"; "Dionosyius" (Ch9).
- **Labyrinth entrance / school:** she knows about the Daedalus delta in Yancy's boiler room (Ch3). Ch3 is set at Yancy. Percy's Ch7 account says "Goode High". By Ch15 he is at Goode High. Ch7 is either a slip or an anticipation.
- **Who held the limiter:** Ch6 says the coolant made her forget; she "had forgotten. Many times over" what it was (Lethe water).
