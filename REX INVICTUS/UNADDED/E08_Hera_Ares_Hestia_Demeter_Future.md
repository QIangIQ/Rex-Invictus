# Hera, Ares, Hestia & Demeter — Future Era (combined profile)
> Four Olympians whose far-future selves survive in the text mostly as ruins, offhand remarks and the prayers of descendants: a queen who picked a wedding venue "on a whim", a war god whose son an AI broke, a hearth-goddess whose fire was a primordial in disguise, and a harvest goddess who left only salted fields.

*Combined file: the source material for each of these four in the far future is thin. Each gets its own sub-profile below. An appendix collects the scattered future references to Hephaestus and Hades for completeness. Their own files are `D09_Hephaestus.md` and `D10_Hades.md`.*

## HERA (future)

### Appearance
No description. Her old-timeline palace on Olympus Primaris: "her peacocks gone, a crater still smoking millenia later" (Ch1).

### Personality
- **Image-conscious and steady:** she calms Zeus at the conference by lightly tapping "the Sky Lord's other shoulder". "Mind your manners Dear, our reputation is on the line." (Ch5) Zeus then "grabbed his wife's hand" and left (Ch5).
- **Capricious matchmaker:** Caligo Vale, where Percy and Artemis were politically bound, was "chosen by Hera on a whim" (Ch13). The binding was an Empire-era political oathbinding early in the Great Expansion, never before the Abandonment (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R8).

### Voice & quotes
Wifely, cool, reputation-first.
- "Mind your manners Dear, our reputation is on the line." (Ch5)

### Story arc
- **Ch1:** palace crater.
- **Ch5:** soothes Zeus and leaves with him.
- **Ch13:** backstory; she chose the venue of the Percy–Artemis binding.

### Author's vault note (not yet in text)
- She invoked the Caligo Vale rite. Per the vault it was held on **Olympus Tertiae**, at a site she chose, and was followed by Percy's mind-wipe and return to Gaia (vault only; R8).
- In 3047, when Zeus pushed for preemptive strikes, she "slapped him down" (vault wording). Her UOE Unified Rank is #844.

### Relationships
Zeus (`E01_Zeus_Future.md`); Artemis and Percy through Caligo Vale (`C02_Artemis_Future_The_Director.md`).

## ARES (future)

### Appearance
None in the future. In the old timeline a few surviving demigods still leave offerings on the gods' altars, and Percy liberates "a few crumbling MREs from Ares' altar" (Ch1).

### Personality / situation
- **A grieving father:** "Poor Ares is still beside himself over the bedridden Deimos on Mars" after one of Athena's alpha cores "overloaded its users" (Athena, Ch5, pre-Abandonment flashback).
- **Worshipped by soldiers:** Imperial troops call Mars "The planet named after our lord of war" (Neumond, Ch22). Titan pilots learn "a Mars catechism… during initiation". Keyes keeps a prayer icon of Ares on his dashboard and calls him "Our God of War" (Ch22).
- **A brand:** ARES Technica manufactures suit CPUs ("ARES Technica 3800 2.4 THz", Ch18) and the "Megafighter Mark III" automaton (Ch23).
- His prow statue, "Ares with his shield", rides AnnChase cruisers (Ch23).

### Voice & quotes
He has no future dialogue. The closest is soldiers speaking of him:
- "I think I'm about to fight the God of War, Handler. Our God of War." (Keyes, Ch22)

### Story arc
- **Ch1:** altar offerings.
- **Ch5:** grief over Deimos.
- **Ch18 and Ch23:** his brand name on hardware.
- **Ch22:** Imperial devotion carried into the present (future soldiers' worship only; the present god's side belongs to `D06`).

### Relationships
- Deimos (son, bedridden on Mars).
- Athena (her cores harmed his son, Ch5). See `E03_Athena_Future_Parthenos.md`. (The *present-day* Ares's fear of "Athena Parthenos" in Ch10 is not evidence for this version; see `D06` and 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C33.)
- Imperial soldiers who pray to him (the Mars catechism). (Patronage of the Imperial garrison on Mars under the Anabasis Accord is the *present-day* Ares's role; see `D06`, C33.)

### Author's vault note (not yet in text)
- He challenged Thor at the first Panpantheonic Summit (3047). His scouts vanished in Artemis's space. An Annex (13287) quotes him: "Neutral ground is only real if you can hold it." His tech helped Hades form Charon's Heralds, the first Titan legions (3046). He is the father of Clarisse. His UOE Unified Rank is #166.

## HESTIA (future)

### Appearance
- Her courtyard on Olympus Primaris has "hand-wrought celestial gates".
- On Percy's last day, smoke rises from it. The figure crouched beside "a strange, pitch black fire" is not Hestia but Chaos (Ch1).

### Personality / situation
- **Absence and hope:** Percy asks, "Milady Hestia? Is that you? Have you truly returned?" (Ch1). His hope implies that Hestia, like the others, left with the Abandonment and had not come back.
- **The eternal flame:** Chaos tells Percy the exit path would return him "back at the eternal flame of your first Olympus" (Ch3), meaning Hestia's hearth on Primaris.

### Voice & quotes
None for Hestia herself. Percy's line is the key one:
- "Milady Hestia? Is that you? Have you truly returned?" (Ch1)

### Story arc
- **Ch1:** the courtyard, the smoke, and the false hope.
- **Ch3:** her "eternal flame" as the way home.

### Relationships
Percy, who hoped for her (`A01_Percy_LordCommander_OldTimeline.md`); Chaos, who used her hearth (`E13_Chaos_The_Last_Creator.md`).

### Author's vault note (not yet in text)
- Her UOE Unified Rank is #171. Compare her alternate title *Lux Aeterna* (Latin for eternal light) with the unconfirmed "Undying Light" Percy uses for Apollo (Ch21; see `E04`).

### Appearance / situation
- Her old-timeline palace: "empty, salted fields, as far as the eye could see" (Ch1).
- Descendants persist in the UOE era:
  - A "Demeter-Dionyosian lineage diluted through four generations" mortar key-er on the Tartarus front (Ch19).

  - Celyn, "a daughter of Demeter, sunburned and freckled and utterly unflappable", a Hunter of the future Director (Ch23). (This is the future Celyn; whether she is the same immortal as Ch4's Celyn is unconfirmed. See `C12`. The vault also makes Celyn a daughter of Demeter, 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C30.)

### Voice & quotes
None in the future.

### Relationships
Her bloodlines (see `C12_Hunters_of_Artemis_Roster.md` for Celyn, `99_Minor_Characters_Compendium.md` for the Kettle Twins).

### Author's vault note (not yet in text)
- In 3046 she founded the **Verdant Expanse** and its Farm-Planets, a neutral ground. She has a secret mutual defence pact with **Isis**. Her UOE Unified Rank is #217.

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Hera:** queenly influence over Zeus and over events (her "whim" decided Caligo Vale).
- **Ares:** war-god patronage; Mars as his domain; his sons Phobos and Deimos. (Present-day Apollo's complaint that "Phobos and Deimons [sic], his sons, keep my vision obscured" (Ch22) concerns the present-day gods and belongs to `D06`; keep the two versions separate.)

- **Demeter:** fertility. Her salted fields are the absence of it.

## Backstory
All four left Earth in the Great Abandonment aboard the *Spear of Zeus* (Ch1, implied by "the Gods" leaving; Hestia's departure is implied by Percy's hope that she had "returned").
