## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- Tullia exists only in the far-future UOE era. She has no present-day counterpart.
- Her **legion**, however, does. In the present-day timeline (Ch 22) the Legio XII Fulminata is Reyna's legion at Camp Jupiter (see `F15_Reyna.md`, `F17_Roman_Legion_Present.md`). Centurion Ashmead, who comes from Tullia's era, says the Fulminata is "still around, in my time. Heavy Tartarean front legion. The Thunderbolts. Best shield wall in the Empire" (Ch 22). Tullia is effectively the far-future heir of Reyna's legion. Ashmead also mentions a "Golden Standard's ability" that Reyna should use, which hints that the legion's standard has a power the future knows about.
- Do not confuse her with **Commander Thaleia** of the Lethe Sector (Ch 14), another Tartarus-front officer (see `H16_Callistus_Narrator_and_Tartarus_Front_Soldiers.md`), or with **the Major** of Segment Twelve (`G10_The_Major.md`).
- She knows nothing of present-day Camp Half-Blood. She knows the shear happened and that the echelon vanished. She may have heard the Watchpoint verdict, because the Director's seal binds everyone with knowledge of the packet; whether Tullia was told is not stated.

## Appearance
- Seen first standing "on the lip of the forward trench with her helmet off and her face bare to the stinking air" (Ch 23). Otherwise she wears legionary armor and a helmet, which she puts back on at the end as a gesture of resolve.
- Like everyone in the Salient after the shear, her face is grey from exhaustion. She has not slept in 31 hours.
- The text gives no hair, eye or build details.

## Personality
- **Steady, laconic and unflappable.** Her first word in the story is "Speak." When told not to go near the wound, she answers "We weren't planning to."
- **A front-line professional.** She keeps her signifer focused ("Keep your eyes on the ridges, signifer") while thousands of scholars land. She is checking ammunition counts when she feels a Giant reforming through the duckboards.
- **Unimpressed by spectacle.** Corvo calls the fleet bombardment "overkill". She reframes it calmly as "the Grace's escort doing its job." When Corvo says he has never seen anything like a dreadnought burying Porphyrion, she replies, "You'll see it again … if you live long enough."
- **Quiet, deep care for her soldiers.** She knows the exact number of her dead, 1,104, "the way she knew her own name", and she knew every one of their names. Aphrodite sees this as the reason they understand each other.
- **Perceptive.** She reads the monsters' fear before the scientists do, and she guesses why Porphyrion is coming: "They can smell it too. Whatever's calling from the other side of that hole, they want it."
- **Disciplined around divinity.** Posted to guard Aphrodite, she "was trying very hard not to stare." She answers the goddess briefly and honestly.
- **Growth.** She begins the chapter keeping the line because it is her job. She ends it accepting an open-ended vigil for people she never knew: "Until they come home."

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Clipped military imperatives. She rarely uses more than a sentence. She addresses Corvo as "signifer" and Aphrodite as "Your Majesty". She does not swear on-page. When she does say more, it comes out dry and fatalistic.

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **No supernatural powers are shown.** Her strengths are command, experience and nerve.
- Commands the Fulminata's shield line. Her "Stand to!" brings the legion onto the firing steps with shields locked.
- Calls fire support over the net: "Rosed Knights… Fulminata Primus Pilus. We have a Class Primus Giant on the ridge… Requesting support."
- Has the veteran's sense for reforming monsters: the hum in the duckboards that "every legionary in the Salient learned to recognize within their first week".
- Her gear is legionary: helmet, shield, net comms. Her signifer carries the data slate.

## Backstory
- She has held ground in the Salient (the Phlegethon sector of the Tartarean Front) for nineteen years, and in that time "had never seen the enemy stop coming."
- In her fourth year she saw Porphyrion from forty kilometres away. Putting him back in the mud took two Titan legions and a cruiser squadron.
- She has lost exactly 1,104 legionaries in nineteen years.
- During the shear her legion was four kilometres back, holding the second line. The Fulminata lost no one, but watched two Pyrrhuiesian reserve cohorts, a carrier, part of its escort wing and a Waves of Poseidon Senior Commander (Marien Pyrrhus, see `G02_Marien_Pyrrhus.md`) vanish into "a sphere of nothing in the time it took to draw a breath." (Varro's casualty list later gives the full Reserve Echelon as 3,998,050 personnel, including a "CARRIER GROUP (TWO VESSELS)"; see Continuity Notes.)

## Story Arc So Far
**Ch 23, Interlude I (UOE era):**
1. **The wound.** She stands at the forward trench looking at the wound where the staging ground was: "a perfect sphere, eleven kilometers in radius". Rain falls upward at its edge, and the Lethe fog folds away from it. For 31 hours the monsters on the Phlegethon ridges have refused to approach, the first time she has ever seen Tartarus's creatures show fear.
2. **Orders.** Signifer Lucan Corvo relays Sector Command's orders: hold, no patrols past 3 km, nobody near the edge, and expect a huge detachment. It is the *Aphrodite's Grace*.
3. **The fleet.** She watches the Dove's Detachment come through the vault (corvettes, Mastodons, AnnChase cruisers, Perseus-pattern dreadnoughts) and hears Aphrodite's "Mother is here" on every channel. She tells Corvo to keep his eyes on the ridges while the scholars "came down like snow", which is how she later describes it.
4. **Porphyrion.** At the ninth hour of the survey she feels a Giant reforming and orders "Stand to!" She identifies the threat with Corvo, calls the Rosed Knights and gets Commander Thorne. The dreadnoughts glass the ridge and the cruisers saturate no-man's-land. When Corvo cries "overkill", she corrects him. She presses Thorne ("any time now—") and gets back "Don't rush art." The Titans and the *Sovereign's Glory* bury Porphyrion, and Callidora signs off with "Fulminata, nice shielding." Tullia tells the laughing, shaken Corvo he will see it again if he lives long enough.
5. **Guarding the goddess.** At the sixtieth hour Michael Black hastily posts her ten metres from Aphrodite at the wound's edge. Aphrodite asks how long she has held the line, how many she has lost, and whether she knew their names. Weeping, Aphrodite says, "Then you know … why I came." Then Aphrodite gives her a personal order: hold until the door opens again and meet whoever comes back. When Tullia asks how long, the answer is "Fourteen thousand years, perhaps. Perhaps a week. Perhaps next Tuesday!"
6. **The vow.** Tullia puts her helmet back on and orders permanent, deep positions. Corvo asks how long, and she answers "Until they come home" while the reading of the four million names goes on overhead.

## Relationships

- **Aphrodite / "Mother Majesty"** (`E05_Aphrodite_Future_Mother_Majesty.md`): first awed formality, then a moment of real recognition between two people who count the dead. Aphrodite makes her the keeper of the vigil.
- **Commander Thorne and the Rosed Knights** (`H05_Commander_Thorne_and_Rosed_Knights.md`): fire-support partners. She does not know Thorne's voice before this battle. He is breezy; she is urgent.

- **Michael Black** (`H06_Bulwarks_and_Spears.md`): posts her to guard Aphrodite.
- **Doctor Ilse Wendriner** (`H04_Ilse_Wendriner_and_Aurelian.md`): Tullia notices her standing her ground against the Giant "with an expression of pure scientific outrage". They never speak.

## Continuity Notes
- In Ch 23 the monsters' watch at the wound is given as 31 hours in Tullia's opening and as "forty hours" just before Porphyrion. Both are stated; time has simply passed between the scenes.
- The legion name "Fulminata" is shared with the present-day Roman legion. This is the same legion across 14,000 years, not a coincidence (Ashmead, Ch 22). Author's vault note (not yet in text): the vault's Legio XII Fulminata entry calls it Jupiter's personal legion, with New Rome as its old residence.
- **One carrier or two.** Tullia's opening narration has the Fulminata watch "a carrier" (singular) vanish (Ch 23). Varro's casualty list in the same chapter has "CARRIER GROUP (TWO VESSELS)", and Ch 19/22 also show two ships (per the author's notes, the *Axis Dawn* and *Warbringer Fury*). Treat the lost echelon as having two vessels.
- **Shear geometry:** the wound is a sphere eleven kilometres in **radius** (Ch 23), not "an 11-km sphere" (see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C27). It swallowed Marien Pyrrhus's Reserve Echelon, not Percy (C7).
- **Time figures:** Aphrodite's "Fourteen thousand years, perhaps" is her guess at how long the door may take to reopen, not a dating of the era. The era's distance from the present is ~14,000 years (Ch 23 CODE 18 readout; see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R3). Ch 22's "six thousand years" is a known slip and does not apply here.
- Spelling: **Pyrrhus / Pyrrhuiesian** (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md S1).
- Her name is Latin (Tullia), unlike the Greek naming common elsewhere in the Empire.
