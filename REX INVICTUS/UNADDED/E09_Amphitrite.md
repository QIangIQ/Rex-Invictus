## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **Future Amphitrite (Ch12):** present in the throne hall during Minerva's video call with the future Poseidon (`E02_Poseidon_Future.md`).
- **Present-day Amphitrite (Ch19):** only mentioned, as the wife whose temper Poseidon fears.
- Neither version knows about Percy's time travel.

## Appearance
Not described. She is part of "the gathered sea-court" alongside "the Nereids" (Ch12).

## Personality
- **Unsettled, sceptical:** when Poseidon greets the Percy-faced clone, "Amphitrite's expression faltered. Even the Nereids stilled." When Minerva says her armor "refuses to render silk", "Amphitrite raised a brow" (Ch12).
- **Formidable temper (present day):** Poseidon knows his "'I broke something important' face! It's always before Amphitrite blows a fuse at me." (Ch19)

## Voice & Speech Patterns
She speaks no lines. In canon tradition she is regal and reserved. Any dialogue is extrapolation.

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
None shown. Canon: sea goddess with authority over sea creatures.

## Backstory
Canon only. Wife of Poseidon, mother of Triton (see `E10_Triton.md`). Canon also gives her a strained relationship with Poseidon's demigod children; the text neither confirms nor contradicts this.
- **Author's vault note (not yet in text):** she can command Delphin and Atlantis as a Regent/Proxy, and she "ensures the cycles of the ocean remain unbroken". New Atlantis is Poseidon's chief residence *with Amphitrite*, one of the UOE's main centres of authority. Her UOE Unified Rank is #194.

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch12:** silent witness to Poseidon's call with Minerva.
- **Ch19:** mentioned by present-day Poseidon.

## Relationships
- **Poseidon** (husband): he fears her anger (present), and she stands at his court (future). See `E02_Poseidon_Future.md`, `D02_Poseidon_Present.md`.
- **Minerva-in-the-clone:** wary scrutiny. See `B02_Minerva_Percy_Clone_Interlude.md`.

- **Percy:** stepson (canon); no direct interaction in the text.

## Secrets, Mysteries & Open Threads

- Her vault role as Regent/Proxy over Atlantis may explain Triton's future title "Lord Regent" (Ch22); see `E10_Triton.md` (inference).
