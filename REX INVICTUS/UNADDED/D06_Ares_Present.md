## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **This file:** the 21st-century war god who ran the Fort Reagan exercise and held the Master Bolt.
- **Not** the far-future Ares, "beside himself" after an alpha-core overload left his son Deimos bedridden on Mars (Ch5), whose altar on ruined Olympus Primaris still receives MRE offerings in the old timeline (Ch1). See `E08_Hera_Ares_Hestia_Demeter_Future.md`.
- **Aspects:** Mars Ultor is his Roman aspect (and, per the author's vault, his main title). When Percy names it, "His form changed momentarily" (Ch10). Percy also taunts him as "Huitzilopotchli", the Aztec war god, and his form flickers "between his alternate forms" when Minerva speaks. In Ch16 Eleni senses Huitzilopochtli as smelling "similar to Lord Mars, his counterpart", which treats them as parallel war gods of different houses. In Ch22 **Huitzilopochtli appears in person as a separate deity** who threatens Olympus (`E18_Amaterasu_and_Foreign_Pantheons.md`). The text never states that Ares and Huitzilopochtli are one being. Percy's taunt implies a link between aspects or domains, but treat them as distinct characters.
- **Knowledge boundary:** He knows Percy has a "mechanical creep" in his head, knows the Imperials exist and pray to him, and knows the Accord. He does not know Percy's old-timeline past.

## Appearance
- "Clad in the motorcycle leather jacket outfit that Percy remembered seeing the god in the first time". He fills the doorway, and his aviators reflect gold light. They shatter when Minerva intervenes (Ch10).
- Ch22: "leather jacket and wraparound aviator sunglasses and combat boots… grinning like it was the best day of his life". His aura is red, "flickering at the edges like a burning city". He bleeds gold when rounds hit him.
- Lounges sideways in his throne with boots on the armrest (Ch19, Ch22).

## Personality
- **Bloodthirsty joy in combat.** "Oh, hell yes," he says, facing an 11-metre Titan. He walks through rotary-cannon fire laughing (Ch22).
- **Bully with a tactician's mind.** Thuggish, but "his mind was working too fast, too tactically" (Ch10). He weighs the risk of exposure and yields the bolt.
- **Vain, sulky and resentful.** Mocked by the council for his army's failure, he slumps on his couch sharpening a javelin (Ch10). He sulks that the Imperials squat on "my planet… and nobody so much as sends me a card" (Ch22).
- **Sore loser who holds grudges.** "You better pray to whatever that mechanical creep's whispering in your head, Cheat… She won't be there to save you" (Ch10).
- **Afraid of Athena.** "Ares Theritas was always afraid of the wrath Athena Parthenos would beat into him" (Ch10). Minerva sounding "like Sister" unsettles him.
- **Hard-line about war realism.** He refuses General Mikhail's request to halt the exercise over food poisoning: "What would the soldiers do if they fell with food poisoning in a real war?" (Ch10). He wanted minimal civilian casualties in the exercise and berated his commanders when they failed.
- **Unexpectedly moved by worship.** When the Titan pilot Keyes recites the Mars catechism to his face, "The god's grin faltered… 'Huh'" (Ch22). He then fiercely claims the garrison: "if any of you touch them without asking me first, you'll answer to me."
- **Grudging respect for discipline.** On Eleni: "She is disciplined" (Ch15).

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Gruff, macho, slangy and taunting. He calls people "runt", "kid", "Jackson" and "Cheat", and drops his g's ("sniffin'"). He swears casually. He uses short threats and boasts and laughs out loud.

Quotes:

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Domain suppression:** his divine pressure makes fighting him feel "slimy… as if he were fighting underwater" (Ch10).
- **Weapons:** a halberd and a blade whose heat blisters skin (Ch10). He catches an Imperial energy blade barehanded, burned to the bone, and tears the Titan's arm off (Ch22).
- **Durability:** shrugs off rotary-cannon rounds. Some don't bounce, and "Blood ran gold down his jacket" (Ch22).
- **Teleporting duels:** transports Percy to the empty Colosseum (Flavian Amphitheater), an "Olympian-sanctioned battleground" where "no mortal eyes would witness a duel with Ares" (Ch10).
- **The Fulminus artefact:** grows stronger nearer to it, obscures the senses of meddling gods, buffs his children and soldiers (a "mist-cloaked red tinge"), and suppresses non-Ares demigod powers. Whoever captures it can demand a minor favour (Ch9–10).
- **Mars:** his planet. His sons Phobos and Deimos ("Phobos and Deimons" [sic], Apollo, Ch22) keep Apollo's sight off it. Ares "covers" the Morrígan's domain in the Americas (Percy, Ch17).
- **Resources:** built Area Fulminus, "A quarter million acres of Nevada desert", for $6.3 billion, "the second biggest military training facility in the world" (Ch8). He raises the CSS *Birmingham* for Clarisse (Percy's foreknowledge in Ch13, confirmed in Ch14).

## Backstory
- Father of Clarisse La Rue and Rebecca and the Ares cabin. Old rival of Athena.
- In this AU he was "played into holding" Zeus's stolen Master Bolt (Ch10). Who played him is implied but unconfirmed: Luke, and possibly Silena.

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch8:** Dionysus describes Area Fulminus: "Ares was very proud… still moaning about how it cost him six-point-three billion dollars."
- **Ch9:** The artefact and its favour reward. Minerva tells Percy the "Myths of Perseus Jackson" say he should duel Ares for the bolt.
- **Ch10 — Fort Reagan exercise:** the demigod insurgents poison the base's water with laxative, bug the HQ and win. Ares is mocked for an hour by Mr. D on Olympus. He catches Percy in the HQ storeroom, beside the artefact and tons of ambrosia. Percy names his aspects and blackmails him. Minerva takes over ("Designation: Ares. Olympian Combat Protocol Alpha-02…") and threatens to broadcast to her "Creator". Ares hands over the Master Bolt, then teleports Percy to the Colosseum. Under Minerva's future-tech countermeasures (telekhine aura, False Blessings, AnnChase Battle Assistance and others), Percy breaks Ares's left leg ("Snap"), stabs his shoulder and slashes his ribs. Ares throws Percy the bolt and vanishes with a threat.
- **Ch11:** Clarisse asks why "dad" had the bolt. Percy: "Because he wasn't the only one."
- **Ch13–14:** Raises the CSS *Birmingham*; Clarisse is "Sent ahead with her undead crew and a war god's smile" (Ch13).
- **Ch15:** At Eleni's council: "She is disciplined."
- **Ch19:** At the council, "permanently unimpressed".
- **Ch22:** Rants about the Imperials on Mars. Hermes: "You don't even go there." Says he'll "do whatever he felt like". Charges Keyes's Titan barehanded, catches its blade and rips the arm off, until Keyes's prayer gives him pause. Poseidon ends the fight: "You were getting prayed to, nephew… Go and think about why." At sunset he laughs at Article I and claims the garrison as patron.

## Continuity Notes
- Ch8 calls the compound "Area Fulminus". Ch10 calls the base area "Fort Reagan". Both refer to the same exercise.
- Ch8 says "We are sending some of you to train at Fulminus with Ares' boy". Who "Ares' boy" is (General Mikhail? a son?) is unclear.
- Percy's spelling is "Huitzilopotchli" (Ch10). Eleni's is "Huitzilopotchtli" (Ch16). The Ch22 narration uses "Huitzilopochtli".
- "Ares Theritas" is a real cult epithet, used here as narration.
- Version scoping (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C33): the Ch10 Athena Parthenos scene and the Mars-garrison patronage (Ch22, Accord Art. I) are **present-day** Ares material and belong here, not in the future-Ares file. The future Deimos who lies bedridden on Mars (Ch5) is a different, far-future figure from the present Deimos who hides Mars from Apollo (Ch22).
- The Divine Being title *Eternal Warmonger* is vault-only (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C32).
- Present-day year: c. 2021 per the author's timeline, nominally the canon PJO era (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R2). The Imperial garrison on Mars is about four million strong (Ares, Ch22); Task Force ORPHEUS, the Earth landing force, was about 1,200.
