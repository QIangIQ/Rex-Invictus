## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
Only one Straban exists. The text's descriptions of "the storm-eyed demigod" (lean, dark hair tied back, light in his irises, who propels the dropship with air vortices) **probably** refer to Straban (see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C24): the author's vault gives the dropship-propelling elemental role to Straban. Ch21 once lists both side by side; see Continuity Notes. See `G09_Kappa7_Ekdromoi_Squad_Overview.md`.

## Appearance
- He wears a visor (Ch21).
- If he is the storm-eyed demigod: lean, dark hair tied back, "light flickering faintly across his irises". The metal warps under his palm when he channels power (Ch20).
- When attacked he coughs crimson blood laced with drugs that glows neon-orange (Ch21).
- In Ch22 he "looked worse" than the others, from his extensive lung damage.

## Personality
- **Cool and faintly amused.** "There are more efficient ways to achieve this" (Ch20), said while air-walking down from orbit.
- **Doctrinal and formal under questioning.** He answers in doctrine and runs Percy through a methodical faction checklist ("You are not PCA… not one of Citadel's children… not one of the Cohors Praetoria… not Extra-pantheonic… not Theodosian… Then what faction do you serve?") (Ch22).
- **Steady and logistical.** "Mars cannot feed a legion forever." He reports that the Major ordered the rover's uplink cut: "The Major's orders were to sever its uplink, and we did" (Ch22).
- **If he is the storm-eyed one:** confident ("There is a reason AtRep chose us"), dismissive of nitpicking ("So shut it. That's the part that matters"), and a quiet leader on the march.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Measured and clipped. He uses formal military register ("Pyrrhuiesian Command." "Then what faction do you serve?") and only rarely slips into panic.

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Aerokinesis / pressure fields.** He descends from orbit by compressing air under his feet. If he is the storm-eyed one, he also propels the dropship with spiralling air vortices (Ch20).
- **Grid projection and vibration sensing.** He projects faint grids along walls to check structural integrity (Ch21). If storm-eyed, he also senses minute vibrations and the Labyrinth exit.
- **Snow and ice.** He swirls snow and smoke into cover and hardens snow into "a rotating mass of particulate ice, a grinding shield" that blocks Eleni's powers (Ch21).
- **Water-sensitive.** As a Poseidon legacy he can feel Percy seizing his blood and partly resist it.
- **Gear.** Divine Stimulants, a MORROW-IX rifle, perfluorocarbon breathing fluid.

## Backstory
A Poseidon-Zeus legacy chosen by AtRep for Kappa-Seven. As a Pyrrhuiesian-Command soldier, his bloodline mixes both of the rival houses' gods (a background detail; not commented on in the text).

- Author's vault note (not yet in text): the vault's Ekdromoi note makes Straban the cell's **Elemental Operator**, a Poseidon–Zeus mixed legacy "able to propel a dropship through vacuum". Poseidon (11%) and Zeus (12%) lines are among the commonest Ekdromoi lineages in the vault's table.

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch20.** Named as "the Poseidon-Zeus legacy, Straban", he descends from the dropship by air-walking, remarking "There are more efficient ways to achieve this", and Iane tells him to spend his spare time clearing their drop spot; Merek calls him "airboy". If he is storm-eyed, he also propels the dropship, senses that the steppe "isn't it", and keeps the lead on the march.
- **Ch21.** He checks the Labyrinth's integrity. In Alaska, Percy's hydrokinesis attacks his blood and he coughs neon-orange blood, crying "They're hydros!" until the Pariah Field goes up. He shields against Eleni with grinding ice, which lets the cell's rifle fire hit her. He is left dying, partially self-stabilised, and Percy saves him.
- **Ch22.** He names Pyrrhuiesian Command, runs through the faction list and describes the shear ("Our reserve echelon had been pulled back to rotate out…"). He explains Operation Anabasis-105's fourth objective, whether Earth can support the force. He reports that the rover's uplink was severed on the Major's orders and estimates retrieval size ("around a hundred to a thousand-ish"). He kneels to the Lord Imperator.

## Continuity Notes
- **Storm-eyed demigod** (see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C24). Probably Straban, but not certain. Ch20 lists five people in the dropship bay: Ione, Iane, the storm-eyed one, the Roman (Merek), and a cloaked woman (not Iane; unresolved author loose end). Neumond is not among them; the storm-eyed one says "Neumond can feel the debris and machinery around Gaia right now" and is "interfacing with navigation", which fits Neumond's double role as operator and ship/drone name. In Ch21 "The storm-eyed demigod floated…" and "Straban hovered ahead" appear in consecutive sentences as if they were different people.
- **Vault evidence.** Author's vault (`Ekdromoi.md`) assigns the dropship-propelling elemental role to Straban, which supports the identification, though the same note credits the storm-eyed line "There *is* a reason AtRep chose us" to an "unnamed Ekdromoi operator". The author's Plot Threads also flags "the roster doesn't match, and Straban flies Lambda-Two" (an unpublished draft put Straban on Oriel's Alaska team); in the published text he is a Kappa-Seven captive throughout Ch22.
- **Demigod or legacy.** He is called a "Poseidon-Zeus legacy", while the storm-eyed figure is called a "demigod".
- **Injuries.** The Watchpoint lists "pulmonary compromise, stabilized by unknown intervention".
- **Carrier count.** In Ch22 Straban describes the echelon as "Two understrength cohorts, the support elements, a carrier, and part of its escort wing" and speaks of "The carrier's fabricators", while narration and Iane speak of two carriers. Treat the force as two vessels (Ch19 "two ships"; Ch23 "CARRIER GROUP (TWO VESSELS)").
