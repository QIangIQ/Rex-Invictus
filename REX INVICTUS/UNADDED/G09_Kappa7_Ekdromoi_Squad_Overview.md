> Five CODE 24E stormtroopers from a future Empire, sent from Mars to scout Earth for the birthplace of their god-machine. They ran into Percy Jackson and Eleni Theodosia in Alaska and became the hinge of an interplanetary treaty.

## Appearance
- Five figures in armored kit with helmets and visors.
- Merek's gold-and-bronze power armor; Neumond's bronze wings; the twins' rune-branding boots.
- Ch20 adds two figures whose identity is unclear: a "storm-eyed demigod" (lean, dark hair tied back; probably Straban) and a quiet woman with "a cloak that didn't quite behave like fabric", which "lagged half a second behind movement" (not Iane; unresolved).

## Personality
They behave as a tight, bickering family. Merek eats everything. The twins argue in unison ("No," they said at the same time) and Merek calls them "Freaks". Neumond talks too much and Iane kicks him for it. Straban is cool and efficient.

In combat they move "like one animal": disciplined cones of fire, a veteran's precision (Ch21–22). In captivity they first stay silent under suppression locks, then answer candidly after CODE 4. They are reverent toward "the Director" (Artemis), whom they answer in unison: "Director." They know what Percy "is supposed to be."

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Banter laced with profanity in the field ("buttfuck nowhere", "Fuck you", "hella nice"). Clipped doctrine in interrogation. For individual quotes see the member files.

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **VA-RF-762 "MORROW-IX"** battle rifles in 7.62×54R. They fire **VAS-762R APDS "QVARTUS"** rounds: 8.2 g at 2.2 km/s, an anti-divine penetrator with a depleted-uranium core and a Stygian iridium compound. The rounds slow regeneration and pierce Achillean skin; one went through Percy's palm.
- A **Pariah Field**: an anti-divine black sphere from Merek's armor.
- UOE IFF, anti-Scry, anti-Augur, ECM and ECOTI.
- **HH-IFG-73 "LUNA-ASH"** Quick-ECM smoke.
- Thermal anti-armor grenades.
- **Divine Stimulants** enough "to keep us running hot for the next week or so". An unattributed speaker adds: "you won't need to sweat, shit, eat, drink, or blink for the next few months" (Ch20). The drug-laced blood shows "neon-orange" (Ch21).
- **Lungs filled with oxygenated perfluorocarbon suspension** (perfluoro-tert-butyl-perfluorodecalin + perfluorohexyl-perfluoroisopropyl ether + fluorinated silica), plus organ-stimulating life support.
- **Suppression locks** at the base of the skull that physically prevent disclosure. A valid **CODE 4** from high authority releases them.
- A cloaked orbital dropship carrying an AtRep relay, a weapons locker, a tachyonic transmitter and future schematics.
- Hecate rune gates. In Ch20 the twins' gate shows the blue Delta, the mark of the Labyrinth.

## Backstory
- Near the Phlegethon, the Pyrrhuiesian reserve echelon (Straban: "Two understrength cohorts, the support elements, a carrier, and part of its escort wing"; elsewhere two carriers) was staging at a transit anchor on the Lethe side. A "shear" took "everything within eleven kilometers of the anchor" (Iane; Ch23 gives the shear as a sphere eleven kilometres in radius) and threw it back to Mars. Iane says "We are thousands of years before our departure point"; canonically the gap is about 14,000 years (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R3). They woke to an empty sky with no Watchpoint handshake (Ch22).
- AtRep fixed the date by stellar drift.
- Author's vault note (not yet in text): the vault's Ekdromoi note describes the Ekdromoi as AtRep's black-division special-operations "divine stormtroopers", rated CODE 24E (Suppression Force). Cells are five operators drawn from Legacies, Neo-demigods and Second-Born demigods (no augmented mortals), chosen by AtRep without application and mostly recruited from the Peltast corps. The vault's lineage mix is Ares 22%, Hermes 16%, Zeus 12%, Poseidon 11%, Hecate 9%, Hades 7%, Athena 7%, Hephaestus 6%, others 10%. Their doctrine traces to the Alaska Hill strike teams (2032). Kappa-Seven's roles: Merek, Heavy Vanguard; Ione and Iane, the Arcanist Pair; Straban, Elemental Operator; Neumond, Systems and Air Operator. Vault kit adds the HE "PHOEBUS-3", RIP "NAIIL" and micro-thermonuclear "PROMETHEUS-IX" rounds, and the Hexenlaufgerät boots are a Tochter des Elysium product.
- **Operation Anabasis-105** has four objectives:
  1. establish the era and divine presence;
  2. catalogue the Pantheon's strength;
  3. find a fixed point to return through, most likely Tartarus;
  4. determine whether Earth can support the force.
- Kappa-Seven were sent to Alaska ("Obscurus One") and "the 2 other spots". Alaska was the future site of **Alaska Hill** (mid-2030s), Athena's Arctic "computational fortress" and "AtRep's first cradle". It was "the first extra-pantheonic war in nearly two thousand years", and its defense was "commanded by a son of Poseidon" (Ch22). Author's vault note (not yet in text): per the vault, Alaska Hill is also where Minerva first became self-aware (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C15).

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch20.** They fly from Mars to Earth and drop onto a remote steppe (in Ch22 the Space Force general reports an object that "was decelerating" … into "Mongolia, sir"). The twins open a rune-gate toward the Arctic.
- **Ch21.** They cross the Labyrinth for 12 hours and exit in Alaska right where Percy and Eleni are testing Percy's powers.
  - They fight. Percy is shot through the hand, side, shoulder, thigh and chest. Iane's shot clips Eleni "through the side of the head".
  - All five are crippled: Iane's ribs and throat, Ione's spine, Merek's crushed armor and chest, Neumond's wings and bones, Straban's lungs.
  - Percy stabilises everyone with nectar, ambrosia, injector pens and their own perfluorocarbon fluid. He drags them through the Labyrinth to Camp Half-Blood, where Apollo takes over.
  - Percy later says "I dropped a glacier on them" (Ch22). USGS logged a glacier moving 400 m in 11 minutes, and Eielson recorded a thermal bloom in the Brooks Range (Ch22).
- **Ch22.**
  - They are interrogated in the Big House, bolted to reinforced chairs. Eleni invokes CODE 4 and they reveal everything.
  - Neumond accidentally prays Artemis into the room.
  - They missed their 18- and 36-hour contact windows, triggering Task Force ORPHEUS (thirty-one craft; "one thousand, two hundred and six" personnel).
  - Merek's harness beacon tells Mars "Theodosian" and "unresolvable". Minerva mines the harness for net codes and uses them for the CODE 2 spoof.
  - During the battle Merek breaks free to stop Lambda-Two. Neumond becomes Minerva's doorway: she rides through his wings into **Lambda-Two's** cloaked dropship overhead, up its relay chain and into the Watchpoint, which issues CODE 18 (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C18). All five kneel to the "Lord Imperator".
  - Merek attends the council in armor and explains the citizenship tokens at the pyre.
  - Their cloaked dropship in the Brooks Range becomes the next crisis.

## Continuity Notes
- **Roster** (see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C24). Ch20's bay scene gives five figures (Ione, Iane, the storm-eyed demigod, the Roman, the cloaked woman). Elsewhere the five are Ione, Iane, Straban, Merek and Neumond. Ch21 also places "the storm-eyed demigod" and "Straban" in consecutive sentences, as if they were separate. The storm-eyed one is **probably Straban** (the author's vault gives the dropship-propelling Elemental Operator role to Straban). The cloaked woman is **not Iane** (Iane is described separately in the same scene) and is **unresolved**; the author's Plot Threads lists her as a loose end. Neumond is a winged Hephaestus legacy and also the name of the drone/ship, which is why the storm-eyed one speaks of "Neumond" interfacing with navigation. The author's Plot Threads also flags "Straban flies Lambda-Two" as a roster problem.
- **Injuries.** Ch21: Ione's spine is broken (armor held, "barely, but his spine did not"). Ch22 narration: Ione's "several ribs". Watchpoint: Ione "fractures, repaired". Iane's crushed ribs (Ch21) are absent from the Watchpoint list, which gives her only "tracheal trauma, repaired".
- **Dropship location.** Ch20 lands the dropship at the steppe insertion point; Ch22 says it is "in the Brooks Range, cloaked, where they left it". Author's vault note (not yet in text): it flew to the Brooks Range on autopilot from the Ch20 landing site.
- **Michael Yew** (see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C17): killed in the Lambda-Two / Sigma-Four drop on the Big House porch (ten operators), not by Kappa-Seven, who were prisoners inside.
- **"Let's make some money!"** (Ch21) is unattributed.
