## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **Present-day Hecate:** made the Hunt's tent array (Ch4). The Council lends "the aid of Hecate and the Mist" to hide Mars (Accord Article II, Ch22).
- **Future Hecate:** "gone talking with some other gods" during the bi-annual conference (Ch5). Her descendants serve the Empire as casters and augurs.
- Neither version speaks on-page.

## Appearance
Not described.

## Personality
Undetermined. She works on commission (for Artemis) and is socially independent of the Greek table at the future summit (Ch5). Anything further is extrapolation.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
No lines. The closest is the incantation spoken by her legacies of Kappa-Seven to raise a teleport array. The text gives it to "The Hecate legacies" together (Ione and Iane), not to one of them:
- "This land is not of mine, and I shall feed the thirsty earth with my flesh." (Ch20)

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Teleportation arrays:** "Lady Artemis had commissioned Lady Hecate into creating a teleportation array in the tent". It moves people from wherever the tent is pitched "into the central atrium of Artemis' palace on Olympus". It draws power from a battery Athena hooked to the Moon Chariot (Ch4).
- **Mist concealment:** Accord Article II (Ch22).
- **Her bloodlines' magic (future):**
  - **Ione & Iane** (Hecate-legacy twins, Ekdromoi Kappa-Seven): rune-walking boots ("Hexenlaufgerät…"), magma-red and violet magic circles, teleport arrays, magesight. Output is measured in "Parzivals per second" (Ch20–21).
  - **Tarrow** (Sciritae Augur, Hecate legacy): reads the Geistjäger divine counter (Ch22).
  - **Hecate demigod in the Tartarus trench:** hardens the wall at the cost of a nosebleed (Ch19).
  - **Hecatean augurs** in violet: kneel with palms to the mud and feel the "echo" from the Phlegethon Wound (Ch23).
  - **Aurelian:** "a nervous young Hecatean" who identifies the Répertoire carrier (Ch23).
  - **Coil:** a "Rustabout" bounty target in the Voss underworld; "Bloodlines: Hephaestus, Hecate. Unstable."; rides a scrap platform floating on "crackling violet lightning" (Ch23).
