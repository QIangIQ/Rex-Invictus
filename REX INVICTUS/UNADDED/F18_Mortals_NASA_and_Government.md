## Personality
**NASA (Ch 19, Ch 22)**
- **Karen (Flight Director):** decisive and procedural. She asks for the previous pass, calls security, then NASA leadership and the White House. She stares "longer than protocol allowed". By Ch 22 she has gone 40 hours without sleep and is quietly frightened: "Are they looking at us?"
- **Marco (left row, HiRISE):** the first to spot it. Informal ("Take a lookie here"), references Reddit conspiracy theorists, and voices the dread: "If they see it, they see us." By Ch 22 he is hoarse from measuring the tower.
- **Jenna (thermal):** sharp and analytical ("Those look like earthworks to me"). She notices the rover turning its mast. She swears casually ("The interns fucked with it a lil'").
- **Tyler:** the sceptic ("Could it be SpaceX."). By Ch 22 he is no longer joking: "Still think it's SpaceX?"
- **The comms lead:** "This is real." Later: Perseverance is healthy but "Something is sitting on its high-gain antenna and holding it closed."
- **Jack from IT:** off-screen; "clocking in later" to fix the crashed heat-signature counter.
- **The NASA Administrator:** clamps down. No Mars data leaves the agency without his signature, and every Jezero image is pulled. He reminds Karen that ESA and China have orbiters too.

**Government (Ch 22)**
- **The President:** tired, direct, restrained. "Give it to me straight," … "Is it hostile?" He refuses to panic about rivals: "They have their own pictures. Let them come to us." Later, quietly: "Where are they going?"
- **The Secretary of Defense:** "It turned off our rover," … "I'd call that hostile."
- **The Director of National Intelligence:** "I'd call it cautious."
- **The Space Force four-star general:** reports a Space Fence track of a decelerating object without a transponder, coming down into Mongolia.
- **The Chairman of the Joint Chiefs:** relays the NORTHCOM file: Eielson saw a thermal bloom in the Brooks Range, and USGS logged a glacier that moved 400 m in 11 minutes. That was Percy's glacier drop on Kappa-Seven.
- **The National Security Advisor:** asks about Beijing and Moscow.
- **The 11th Airborne:** a company converging on the Ekdromoi dropship in the Brooks Range, three days out (Ch 22 ending).

**Fort Reagan / Area Fulminus (Ch 9–10)**

- "Tell me this is software." / "This is real." (Karen / comms lead, Ch 19)

  - Perseverance landed in 2021.
  - The Burj Khalifa opened in 2010.
  - Space Force was founded in 2019.
  - The MRO's HiRISE was operational from late 2006.
  
  Profiles describe the present as c. 2021 per the author's timeline, nominally the canon PJO era. These details are not anachronisms under that ruling.
