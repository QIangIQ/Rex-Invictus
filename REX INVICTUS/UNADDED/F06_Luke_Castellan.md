## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- This is the only Luke in the story. He is the present-day son of Hermes, who is apparently already secretly serving Kronos.
- Percy knows Luke's canon future from his old life: "Luke would probably go missing" (Ch12). He suspects Luke helped steal the Master Bolt: "he wasn't the only one" (Ch11).
- Do not confuse him with **Castellan Oriel**. "Castellan" is her Imperial rank, and she has no relation to Luke. See `G14_Sciritae_Neodamodes_and_Ch22_Units.md`.

## Appearance
- Lean, with two days' stubble at Fulminus (Ch10).
- In Ch22 he stands on the bridge wing of the *Princess Andromeda*, sword in hand, "face set."
- No other physical description is given; canon details such as his scar are not mentioned.

## Personality
- **Charismatic and manipulative.** He whips a crowd of drunk college kids into storming the base with patriotism and money ("ya'll receive over two thousand for two days' work!", Ch10). His Ch22 speech to the Imperials targets exactly what they want, a way home. "He had always known exactly what people wanted to hear" (Ch22).
- **Playful and showy.** On Zeus's jet: "I demand Beluga caviar and the finest wines available to man!… Chop chop" (Ch8).
- **A dangerous fighter.** In the hangar raid he whispers "skotóno" ("I kill") and moves "impossibly fast." He floors two technicians with a kick, a punch and a roundhouse (Ch9). He duelled Percy offscreen (Ch8).
- **Patient opportunist.** He watched the whole Ch22 battle from the fog and struck only when "everyone here was half dead." Minerva: "It is what I would have done."
- **Bitter at the gods.** "The gods don't win. In six thousand years, they're still up there… sending kids to die in a hole, and they put you in charge of it" (Ch22).
- **A trace of the old Luke.** "Something moved behind his eyes, something that might once have been the counselor from Cabin Eleven" (Ch22).

## Voice & Speech Patterns
- Persuasive, rhetorical, casual American ("ya'll"). He is commanding when shouting orders and soft and bitter one-on-one. He uses Greek for battle intent ("skotóno").

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- Superior speed and hand-to-hand skill (Ch9). Swordsmanship (the duel with Percy, Ch8; sword in hand, Ch22).
- Scouting: he scouts the airfield ahead of the raid (Ch9).
- Oratory and crowd manipulation (Ch10, Ch22).
- Commands the *Princess Andromeda*, a cruise ship crewed by "several thousand" monsters. A golden time-field (Kronos's power) around the ship stopped Imperial rail rounds (Ch22). His monsters include giants, dracaenae and hellhounds the size of pickup trucks.

## Backstory
- Author's vault note (not yet in text; canon future): host of Kronos, wielder of Kronos's scythe, bearer of the Curse of Achilles, and formerly in a relationship with Annabeth. The vault also lists him as **Alpha-Class Traitor #1** in UOE records.
- Author's vault note (PLANNED, author intent): the Order of the Hollow Crescent reaches Luke in the present through the pit, which is where his Ch22 "road home through Tartarus" offer comes from.
- As a canon baseline: son of Hermes and Cabin Eleven counselor. He secretly turned to Kronos after an embittering quest, and orchestrated the theft of the Master Bolt.
- In this AU Ares physically held the bolt (Ch10), and Percy hints at a co-conspirator. Luke is the obvious implication.
- Percy wondered as early as Ch3 whether the Labyrinth monster camp was "part of Luke's army."

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch3.** Mentioned only, as a possible commander of monsters.
- **Ch7.** Chiron tells Percy to "talk to Luke by the end of dinner" if unclaimed.
- **Ch8.**
  - At dinner, a goblet of juice Percy dodges arcs onto Luke's lap. Luke curses, but is cut off by Percy's claiming.
  - He duels Percy over the following days, offscreen.
  - On Zeus's jet he jokes with the stewards. He dozes on the bus in front of his father, Hermes, and opens one eye at Percy's laughter.
- **Ch9.** Scouts the airstrip, takes down two technicians "impossibly fast" for their uniforms ("No shooting, Luke", Annabeth), and helps strip them.
- **Ch10.** Delivers the megaphone speech that launches the final assault on Fort Reagan. Percy, privately: "If only he knew."
- **Ch11.** Rides the second bus home with Silena. Percy is relieved they are absent during the bolt discussion, hinting he suspects both.
- **Ch12.** Percy expects that "Luke would probably go missing."
- **Ch13.**
  - The Athena cabin calls him a "Traitor, apparently, if Percy is right."
  - Percy scribbles where the *Princess Andromeda* waits for Luke's arrival.
  - Athena plans for "Luke Castellan" to face Percy on the return voyage. That interception never appears on-page.
- **Ch19.** The *Princess Andromeda* docks behind Othrys and disgorges monsters. Luke is not seen.
- **Ch22.**
  - After the night-long Imperial battle, the *Princess Andromeda* sails out of the fog with cheerful music and Luke on the PA. He offers the Imperials Kronos's road home through Tartarus.
  - Ashmead's Neodamodes refuse (Quillon asks "Standard protocol?"; they kill monsters), and the Imperials fight his monsters instead. Peltasts with Percy on Blackjack javelin the ship.
  - Luke and Percy exchange words across the burning deck. Luke: "They called you Emperor." Percy: "You heard a title." … "You don't know what I do with it" (Ch22).
  - The Psiloi, firing on Artemis's command, destroy the bow with twelve rounds from the edge of space (Ch22:1563–1568). Luke escapes in a small boat and "didn't look back."

## Continuity Notes
- Athena planned a *Princess Andromeda* confrontation on the Fleece return (Ch13), but Ch15 shows the trio sailing home without incident.
- Luke's "six thousand years" (Ch22:1533) matches the Imperial figure in the same chapter (Minerva's "six thousand years old", Ch22:1303). It is a known slip: the canonical distance to the Empire era is about 14,000 years (Ch23). Quote his line accurately, but do not treat it as the real figure (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R3).
- **Monster count.** The *Princess Andromeda* carries "several thousand" monsters (Minerva: "Divine signatures aboard: several thousand", Ch22:1400), but Percy later says the Imperials held the beach "against a thousand monsters" (Ch22:1645).
- Ch8's "son of Hemes" is a typo.
