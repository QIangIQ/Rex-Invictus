## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **Name ruling.** The canonical name is **Kagura Kuroyami** (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md S4). Ch 5 calls the Oni diplomat "Perseus Kuroyami" once ("Perseus Kuroyami huffed"); everywhere else Ch 5 says "Kuroyami", "the Oni" or "Diplomat Kuroyami". Ch 23 names "Kagura Kuroyami", "the Shinto emissary". The surname, the Shinto emissary/diplomat role and the era all match, and the author's vault has a note for Kagura Kuroyami but none for "Perseus Kuroyami". The Ch 5 first name is very probably a slip (likely cross-contamination from "Perseus Jackson"). Profiles treat the two as the same person and mention "Perseus Kuroyami" only as the Ch 5 name.
- **She has nothing to do with Percy Jackson.** Do not merge her with any Percy file.
- **Kagura in Ch 23** is the emissary who reactivated the Yamamoto Chokepoint Relay "on December's Eve of 16215, when the Shinto emissary had reached across the dark to a besieged Matriarch Voss and offered her an alliance". Minerva also mentions "the coordinates Kagura Kuroyami gave their fleets during the war that could skip past your sensor net" (Ch 23).
- **Era.** Ch 5 and Ch 23 are both in the far-future era (~UOE year 16231 per the author's dating), a few years after the War of Princes. Ch 5's "a few millenia past the War of Princes" is a slip (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R5; see Continuity Notes).
- The meeting she attends includes future Zeus, Hera, Athena, Artemis and Apollo (see `E01_Zeus_Future.md`, `E03_Athena_Future_Parthenos.md`, `C02_Artemis_Future_The_Director.md`), plus Justicar Themis (`H12_Justicar_Themis.md`).

## Appearance
Her horns "glowed an angry red" when Zeus overrules her. She carries papers, then "a positively ginormous binder, overflowing with paper." No other description is given.

## Personality
- **Nervous but brave.** She "steeled her nerves" to speak first, then defies Zeus by citing the Treaty of Mount Rümker: "That is not your decision to make, Roar of Clouds."
- **Bureaucratic and earnest to a fault.** She can cite page numbers of her budget from memory.
- **Pragmatic and cold where her pantheon's interest lies.** She agrees at once to sacrifice 20 souls to Athena ("Steep, but terms are acceptable"), offering prisoners, but refuses to give up priests or firstborn, because her superiors "are far too attached to their underlings as of late."
- **Shrewd.** She fishes for information about Old Earth ("Perhaps you would know something about that?") and privately panics at what she learns: "Crap, orbital saturators? When did they weaponize the moon?"
- **Eager to escape awkwardness.** She "brightened up" when Themis asks about the budget.
- **(Author's vault, not yet in text)** an engineer by trade: the vault calls her the Dominion's chief engineer, which fits her command of figures and page numbers.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Formal, procedural diplomatic register ("humbly requests", "Exactly on the point, Justicar"), with a panicked casual inner voice ("Crap"). She addresses Zeus as "Roar of Clouds" and "milord", and Athena as "milady".
- "This message is towards the Voss Sovereignty and their allies, the Undying Olympian Empire." (Ch 5)
- "That is not your decision to make, Roar of Clouds." (Ch 5)
- "Steep, but terms are acceptable, it shall be done." (Ch 5)
- "My lips are sealed milord." (Ch 5)
- "Of course, here I look. The overview starts at page 15…" (Ch 5)

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
Diplomatic authority; a budget of "a few million drachmae worth of material and barter" for the embassy expansion plus "an additional 200 thousand for partial Répertoire integration"; prisoners held "in our penal holdings in the embassy". No combat powers are shown.
- Ch 23 credits her with reactivating the Yamamoto Chokepoint Relay in 16215 and with giving Dominion fleets coordinates "that could skip past your sensor net".
- Author's vault note (not yet in text): the Yamamoto relay was "fixed by Kagura Kuroyami"; she was lead engineer in 16214 and "personally activated" the relay on 16215.12.1. In 16225 she is listed as "Shinto Emissary and Fleet Commander Class II", fighting on the Princes' side of the War of Princes. One of the Shinto fleet patterns in the vault is named "Kagura".

## Backstory
- **On the page (Ch 23):** during the War of Princes, as "the Shinto emissary", she reactivated the Yamamoto Chokepoint Relay "on December's Eve of 16215" and offered a besieged Matriarch Voss an alliance. The Amaterasu Dominion's fleets, parked on the far side of the relay, made the Empire leave it open, and it is now "the only public route between the Undying Olympian Empire and the Amaterasu Dominion".
- **Author's vault note (not yet in text):** chief engineer of the Amaterasu Dominion and, during the War of Princes, apostle of Amaterasu; "formal ward of Amaterasu" (Annex 15632, where she addresses "Matriarch Voss" as "Emissary Kuroyami"); lead engineer in 16214; relay reactivation 16215.12.1; "Shinto Emissary and Fleet Commander Class II" for the Princes in 16225; UOE Unified Alltime rank #9973. Other vault-only Shinto War of Princes figures: commanders Yamato Makoto and Mikage Seiryu, Warden Yurei Tensei (killed 16216) and Commander Nakatomi.

The Dominion's Ch 5 requests:
1. A counter-piracy patrol waystation near Voss Primaris and the Yamamoto Chokepoint Relay, because piracy threatens trade across "a minimum of one-hundred-and-fifty systems".
2. An expanded embassy.
3. Partial Répertoire integration.

The Shinto believe "the luciferian demons are in a plot to reform" Old Earth. The Dominion had been running "Re-colonization efforts of earth", which she now thinks "have to be put on a pause for now".

## Story Arc So Far
**War of Princes (referenced in Ch 23):** reactivates the Yamamoto relay on December's Eve of 16215 and brokers the Shinto alliance with Matriarch Voss.

**Ch 5:**
- She presents the waystation request. Zeus says "Denied". She invokes Justicar Themis's jurisdiction under the Treaty of Mount Rümker; Hera calms Zeus.
- She repeats the request to Themis, who approves and suggests expanding the embassy.
- Athena demands "20 Lambda-Class divine souls" of her pantheon. Kuroyami offers prisoners, is refused (they are Phi-class "at most"), and declines to give priests or firstborn.
- She asks about Old Earth. Artemis scans via the "orbital saturators" and Iris cameras and finds a spike at Athena's former palace. Athena then reveals the rogue Parlamentum Mark 1 AI (Minerva).
- Zeus asks whether she will keep this quiet ("monster?"). She promises silence while privately resolving to report to Amaterasu.
- Afterward, Themis asks what the money is for, and she launches into her binder while the gods sigh.

## Continuity Notes
- **Name:** "Perseus Kuroyami" (Ch 5, once) vs "Kagura Kuroyami" (Ch 23, twice; author's vault). Use Kagura; mention the Ch 5 name as a variant only (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md S4).
- **Treaty spelling:** Ch 5 spells it "Mount Rümker"; Ch 23 and the vault spell it "Mount Rumker". Profiles use "Treaty of Mount Rümker" (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md S11).
- **War of Princes dating:** Ch 5 (Themis) says the embassy systems have not been updated "since a few millenia past the War of Princes", and Ch 15 has Poseidon say "millennia ago". Ch 23 says the war ended "Quite a few years earlier", the Voss graffiti refers to gods not allowed in the system "for four years", and Kagura's relay work is dated "16215". Author's dating: the War of Princes ran 16212–16227 and the far-future scenes (including Ch 5 and the Ch 23 shear) are ~16231. Ch 5's and Ch 15's "millennia" lines are slips (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R5). Ch 5 cannot sit millennia after Ch 23, because it is tied to Percy's Ch 1 departure spike, which is in the same ~14,000-years-later era.
- **Voss and the UOE:** Ch 5 calls the Voss Sovereignty and the UOE "allies"; Ch 23 shows Voss as a former rebel state whose treaty bars divine governance. Not a contradiction: after the Treaty (16227) Voss is an autonomous, treaty-restricted state at peace with the Empire, and its wartime ally was the Amaterasu Dominion. Ch 5's "allies" is diplomatic courtesy (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C5).
- **Treaty site:** Ch 23 says the war ended in a draw at "the neutral ruins on Gaia's moon". The vault puts the ratification at Tears of Lysandra (a Voss moon) as a UOE tactical victory. The text wins; flagged for the author in 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md §5.
- **Energy spike location:** Ch 5 puts the spike at Athena's former palace, while Ch 1 takes Percy from Hestia's courtyard. Story-level slip, flagged in 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md §5.
- **Binder size:** the text says "a positively ginormous binder" and cites payroll on "page 2438". Earlier versions of this profile called it a two-thousand-page binder; at least 2,438 pages is the accurate floor.
- **Zeus's lines:** "That man" in Ch 5 is Athena's phrase; Zeus's line is "I knew Poseidon's children were unreliable" (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C9).
