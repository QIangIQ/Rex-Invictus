## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **Present-day Iris** (Ch3–18) is the character. She has observation channels over Percy (Ch3), relays the Iris messages of Annabeth–Athena (Ch13) and Grover–Percy (Ch15), and gossips in Ch18.
- **Future/old-timeline Iris** never appears in person. Her legacy is technology:
  - "the old Iris cameras on Luna's orbital platforms", which future Artemis uses to watch Gaia (Ch5).
  - Before the Abandonment, "Iris helped me develop the faceshield", a live-feed visor that lets the future Annabeth look past Athena's Aegis (Ch5).
- **Fleecy** appears in one line (Ch18).

## Appearance
- Not physically described. She manifests as a voice in the shimmer of the rainbow ("Her voice whispered from the light").
- The rainbow "thinned into a narrow stream", "cracked and dissolved" and finally "died in silence" (Ch18).

## Personality
- **Exasperated but pleased by over-payment:** "You really do not understand subtlety. When you use that many coins, it feels like you are throwing gold bars at my head." She then "materialized faster than usual" (Ch18).
- **Empathic busybody:** she feels Percy's emotions "through the rainbow" and tells him to work on his mental barriers (Ch18).
- **Gossip-loving:** "When can I tell Hephaestus TV about you two?" and "I'm going to combust from the emotional pressure in here." (Ch18)
- **Perceptive and amused:** "Young one… you should not doubt what you heard. That went both ways." She has handled Artemis's calls "for centuries" and knows how the goddess sounds "when she is trying not to show something" (Ch18).
- **Mercenary discretion:** she accepts bribes for silence ("Good"), but teases anyway: "Whatever you say, child." (Ch18)

## Voice & Speech Patterns

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Iris messages:** paid with drachmae scraped or offered into the light. Bandwidth is limited (Ch18).
- **Emotional sensing** through the rainbow link (Ch18).
- **Observational instruments:** she has surveillance channels on Percy. His Ch3 barrier pulse neutralises them "As long as we do not speak their standard names". Percy avoids saying "Iris", which is why she is "The one of Many Colors".
- **Future tech (Ch5):** the Luna orbital "Iris cams", and the faceshield co-developed with the future Annabeth.

## Backstory
Canon goddess of the rainbow. She has handled Artemis's calls "for centuries" (Ch18).

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch3:** Percy blocks her and Poseidon's observation of his room.
- **Ch13:** relays Annabeth's call to Athena. "Iris conveniently panning the view" at Athena's papers is a joke about her camera work.
- **Ch15:** relays Grover's message from Westover Hall into Percy's geometry class.
- **Ch18:**
  - Percy bribes her with 4 and then 10 coins to reach Artemis under the sky. She scolds his subtlety and connects the call.
  - Percy and Artemis share "I feel what I feel." / "So do I." Iris interrupts over her bandwidth and asks when she can tell Hephaestus TV.
  - In the after-image she tells Percy it "went both ways". He pays 3 coins, and Fleecy's "temporarily" prompts 5 more for "Permanently". Iris says Artemis sounded "like a person" and teases "Whatever you say, child."
