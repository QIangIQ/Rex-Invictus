## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **Present-day Hestia (main subject):** a silent observer at two councils. In Ch15 she is "seated off to the side, watched with eyes soft but unblinking". In Ch22 the sunset council has "Hestia at the hearth".
- **Old-timeline Hestia (Ch1):** never seen. Her courtyard on ruined Olympus Primaris ("the dwelling of the guardian of eternal flame") has "hand-wrought celestial gates". When Lord-Commander Percy sees smoke there after millennia alone, he runs to it hoping: "Milady Hestia? Is that you? Have you truly returned?" It was Chaos, crouched by a pitch-black fire. See `A01_Percy_LordCommander_OldTimeline.md` and `E13_Chaos_The_Last_Creator.md`.
- **Future Hestia:** for any far-future references see `E08_Hera_Ares_Hestia_Demeter_Future.md`.
- **Knowledge boundary:** As present-day Hestia she has heard the Ch15 and Ch22 councils. She has no knowledge of the old timeline.

## Appearance
Not physically described. Ch15 gives only her eyes, "soft but unblinking". In Ch22 she is at the hearth. Hestia's hearth is literally the centre of the Mars debate.
