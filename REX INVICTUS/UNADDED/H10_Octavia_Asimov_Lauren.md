## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- These three exist in the **latest era shown in the story**: roughly 30,000 years after the UOE's rise. That is far later than Interlude I (Ch 23, ~14,000 years after the present-day story) and the Minerva-clone segments. In their time Sol/Gaia is a quarantined radiation graveyard behind a vast automated orbital defense lattice. Do not place them in the Interlude's world, where Gaia is still reachable and Thalia was last there "nine hundred years ago" (Ch 23; verified). The Ch 20 Octavia era is its own, later era (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R7).
- **Octavia is not the Octavian of Legio XXI Rapax** (Percy's Roman claim, Ch 17) and not canon PJO's Octavian.
- Her Hunter past ties her to the Hunters of Artemis. Her service era is unknown: she may have known future-era Hunters, the Director, or Thalia as Lieutenant (see `C12_Hunters_of_Artemis_Roster.md`, `C02_Artemis_Future_The_Director.md`, `C07_Thalia_Grace_Future_Lieutenant.md`), but the text says nothing.
- The woman they find is the far-future "Bianca Di'Angelo" (`C09_Bianca_DiAngelo_FarFuture.md`). Do not confuse her with present-day Bianca (`C08_Bianca_di_Angelo_Present.md`).
- None of them know Percy personally. The Empire's "greatest weapon" (Octavia also calls it "the universe's greatest weapon") is a rumour; whether it means Percy, Bianca or something else is unresolved.

## Appearance
- **Octavia:** "silver hair tied back in a rough knot"; a sword at her side; radiation shield strapped to her waist; usually twisted with fury "that only someone living permanently on the edge of bankruptcy could produce."
- **Asimov:** no physical description. He ducks a lot.
- **Lauren:** "looked barely fifteen"; "a polite young girl"; a smooth, calm voice.

## Personality
- **Octavia:**
  - Explosive, extremely profane and physically violent with her partner: a datapad she throws flies past Asimov's head and shatters, and she smacks him into the console.
  - Bangs her own forehead on the control panel in despair.
  - Chronically broke and blames others; Lauren points out the money is short "because of the property damage you caused."
  - Greedy but shrewd. She weighs "Low risk. Low return. High risk. High return." and grins.
  - Brave and decisive: she jumps through radiation storms rather than pay for ship shields.
  - Has a soft spot. She won't send Lauren down, because Lauren is a "teenage girl who doesn't even have divine blood."
  - Her Hunter training shows in her instincts. Bianca's aura makes even her "knees weaken", which tells you how strong Bianca is.
- **Asimov:**
  - Naive, eager and loyal, a self-described "third generation legacy".
  - Trusts his "childhood friend", an Evergreen Family branch manager who supposedly hacked AtRep.
  - Stubbornly hopeful ("wouldn't there be a possibility?"); he runs the human-life scan on his own initiative, and it pays off.
  - Engaged, and saving for a wedding.
  - Fears Lauren far more than Octavia.
  - Gleeful when right: "See! I told you so, didn't I?!"
- **Lauren:**
  - Unfailingly polite and calm, the crew's voice of reason and accountant.
  - Quietly ruthless. She volunteers Asimov's savings (his wedding fund) to cover the trip's expenses, and Asimov has seen her dismantle "entire corporate shell companies just because someone refused to pay her commission". Every bounty hunter "in three sectors" knows crossing her leaves you "homeless in a matter of minutes."
  - Methodical with the scans; gently softens her report that the lifeforce is "extremely weak."

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Ship:** a small, battered shuttle hidden "inside a field of distortion noise", threading the blind spots of Sol's orbital defense lattice. It survives "only by being too insignificant to notice."
- **Gear:** radiation shields for jumping through storms; top-grade scanning equipment rented from "the Hadrian Harmonic bigheads" (Hadrian Harmonics, "Energy and Harmonic Shielding and Shieldbreaking" in the author's Ch 2 corporate list); human-signature and spatial scans (Octavia also asks for a thermal scan).
- **Octavia:** a sword and Hunter-trained instincts (they "screamed" when Bianca's power flared). Whether she still has any Hunter blessing is unknown.
- **Asimov:** minor legacy powers ("you can use some power, even if you're second-rate").
- **Lauren:** an android with database access (the ancient Earth map) and ruthless financial and corporate skills.

- Author's vault note (not yet in text; PLANNED / draft material): Octavia took the Hunt's oath about 15 years before Ch 20 and kept her silver band. One brainstorm option has her drop into the Hunt's salute when she meets Bianca. Treat this as author intent, not canon.

(The Evergreen Family also appear in the present-day timeline, at war with Asmodeus's faction and manipulating Hell, Ch 4/8/17, and in the UOE era as "Evergreen pilots", Ch 18. ## Story Arc So Far
**Ch 20 (third section):**
1. **The argument.** Hidden near Earth, Octavia screams at Asimov for dragging them across the galaxy on a treasure hunt in a graveyard everyone has looted for 30 millennia. Lauren calms her and offers up Asimov's wedding savings. Octavia regrets passing up the other bounty.
2. **The scan.** Asimov gives coordinates 27.99, 86.92 and runs a human-signature scan anyway. Octavia reaches for her sword, and then Lauren says, "We've got something." A human-sized life sign is underground, inside a man-made structure with a surface entrance, in an area of lower radiation, with "extremely weak" lifeforce.
3. **The decision.** Octavia picks the high-risk, high-reward option: capture it themselves instead of reporting it to the Empire. She and Asimov strap on rad-shields and jump. Lauren names the place: Mount Everest.
4. **The ruins.** Hours later Octavia lands and finds dozens of defeated soldiers in UOE insignia, beaten by one woman who mocks their "bizarre contraptions". A wounded Roman legacy threatens her in the name of the Undying Olympian Empire. She repeats "…Olympian Empire, you say." Power explodes from her: hair lifting, pale blue fire in her eyes. Octavia's knees weaken. Asked who she is, the woman answers: "I am Bianca Di'Angelo."

## Relationships
- **Octavia & Asimov:** abusive-affectionate, sibling-like ("Sis"). She hits him and calls him useless, but keeps him around because "idiots had a strange tendency to stumble onto useful information," and she takes him along to assist.
- **Octavia & Lauren:** Lauren calls her "Big sis" and manages her. Octavia is protective of her and listens to her.
- **Asimov & Lauren:** he is terrified of her.
- **Asimov's fiancée:** unnamed; Octavia pities her.
- **Bianca Di'Angelo** (`C09_Bianca_DiAngelo_FarFuture.md`): first encounter, overwhelming. Bianca reacts specifically to the name "Olympian Empire".
- **The Empire:** they work around it, smuggling past its quarantine and considering whether to report to it for a reward.
