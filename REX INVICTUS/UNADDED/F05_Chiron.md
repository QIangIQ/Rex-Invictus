## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **Present-day Chiron (this file).** He knows Percy only as a promising son of Poseidon with strange knowledge. He has suspected an unusual "origin" since Ch7, and he learns of Minerva and of future soldiers. He does not know the full truth of Percy's past life.
- **Future/old-timeline Chiron (note only).** In Percy's original life, Chiron was "not thrilled" about Percy's surgery to integrate the Aegis Vestments (Ch19). In the far future Percy had been at Camp Half-Blood "for many millennia" (Ch8 narration), which implies a camp that lasted that long and possibly a Chiron who did too. No UOE-era Chiron appears on-page.
- Do not confuse this file with the "Centaur" codename, which is a war-game role Chiron played (Ch9).
- Related: `A02_Percy_Reincarnated_Rex.md`, `D07_Dionysus_MrD_Present.md`, `F01_Annabeth_Chase_Present.md`.

## Appearance
- **As Mr. Brunner:** wheelchair-bound and old, with Riptide clipped to his jacket (Ch3).
- **At camp:** sits at the card table with "his wheelchair tucked beneath him" (Ch7). In centaur form: hooves "shifting on the porch" (Ch12), a tail that "swished nervously" (Ch11), "hooves planted on the grass" (Ch22).
- He steeples his fingers when thinking (Ch7).
- He drinks coffee "like a soldier behind siege walls" (Ch20).
- On the war-game screen, his "blurry twenty-year-old picture" stands in for the terrorist "Centaur" (Ch9).

## Personality
- **Patient strategist.** He plays cards with Dionysus and needles him: "You haven't played your move yet" (Ch7).
- **Perceptive, and keeps his own counsel.** "If this boy's origin was who Chiron suspected…" (Ch7). He gives Percy "a long look that suggested he was reserving his judgment" (Ch20).
- **Warm, with gentle teasing.** "Right on time as always" (Ch3). "Try not to lose it now Percy, that thing is practically a family heirloom" (Ch3). He jokingly threatens Travis with Ares Cabin's "four-week intensive fitness programmes" (Ch8).
- **A good sport about cons.** In Vegas he poses as a posh Scot, "a distant cousin" of the Queen, while struggling to keep a straight face. He loses "eight bucks playing the five-cent slot machines" (Ch8).
- **Indulgent toward Mr. D.** "Dionysius is a big boy… I'll go down and fish him out of the casino if it looks like Julio's getting the better of him" (Ch8).
- **Nervous before powerful goddesses.** Facing Artemis on the plane his tail swishes nervously, and he sits "solemnly" through Percy's screams (Ch11).
- **Calm under fire.** He organises the whole camp's defence "in a voice that didn't carry any panic at all" (Ch22). He calls in an old favor; Lupa agrees and outvotes the Senate, and the Romans come (Ch22).
- **Slightly technophobic.** He wiped his self-destructing laptop twice. A twelve-year-old called him "a security risk because of my age" (Ch8).

## Voice & Speech Patterns
- Calm, measured, courteous and lecturing; he calls Percy "boy" kindly. He is formal with gods ("Milady Artemis", "Milord"), dry with Mr. D, and grim when the news is bad. He doesn't swear except a mild "the damned thing" (Ch8).

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Teaching and lore.** He gives the Kronos/Chronos lecture (Ch3). He explains the theory that far-future demigods may have power "rivaling the gods of today… Enough to punch through Chronos' Hourglass" (Ch15).
- **Logistics.** He handles travel documents and "export licenses for weapons, explosives and drugs" for the Fulminus trip (Ch8). He organises the camp's defence (Ch22).
- **Disguise.** Mist-backed wheelchair form as Mr. Brunner.
- **Keeper of Riptide.** He held it until Percy tricked it out of him (Ch3).
- **Connections.** Lupa and the Romans (Ch22). He is on good terms with Hermes ("Hello, hello, Milord", Ch8).

## Backstory
- As a canon baseline: the immortal centaur trainer of heroes, son of Kronos, who disguised himself as Mr. Brunner to watch Percy at Yancy.
- In this story he "personally shadowed" Percy (Ch7). He has friends in Vegas from "her short stint as a casino host" [sic: the narration uses "her"] (Ch8).
- He knows Percy's mother's secret about Poseidon; he suspects it in Ch7 and announces it at the claiming in Ch8.
- **Future note.** In Percy's old life, Chiron opposed or disliked the Aegis Vestments surgery: "Chiron was not thrilled" (Ch19).
- Author's vault note (not yet in text): canon baseline as son of Kronos, leader of the Party Ponies, and teacher of Achilles, Jason, Percy, Actaeon, Ajax, Asclepius, Hercules and Patroclus.

## Continuity Notes
- **"She/her" in Ch8.** The narration uses "she" twice for Chiron ("friends she'd made", "Chiron checked over her shoulder"). These are slips; Chiron is male.
- **"Goode High."** Percy tells him the story began at Goode High (Ch7), but Ch3 places it at Yancy Academy. By Ch15 (l.289) Percy really is at Goode High. Ch7 is either a slip or an anticipation.
- **Status.** Confirmed alive after Ch22 (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C20).
- **Wheelchair vs hooves.** He is in the wheelchair in Ch7 and in full centaur form elsewhere. Both are consistent with canon.
- **Ch11.** Annabeth says "We need to tell Chiron. Now." The next line, "Tell me what? And who's this Lady?", appears to be Chiron arriving, but it is unattributed.
