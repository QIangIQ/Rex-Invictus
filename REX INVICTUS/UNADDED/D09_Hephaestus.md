## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **Present-day Hephaestus (this file's main subject):** appears only at the Ch22 Mars council. He "said nothing at all", turning "one of Neumond's wing feathers over in his scarred hands", holding it to the light and making "a small sound". He knows only what was said at that council.
- **Old-timeline / future Hephaestus (referenced only, not a separate roster file):**
  - In the old timeline his blessing is in Lord-Commander Percy's exosuit: "hephaestus-blessed servos" (Ch1). Minerva says the armor "was divine, yes, forged by Hephaestus" (Ch6).
  - In the far-future Empire he builds "dyson sphere automatons" (Ch5). AnnChase-class ships are built on "the system-wide Forge of Hephaestus", and Minerva steals *The Eye of Light* from inspection "in Hephaestus' domain" (Ch15). Eleni's armor has "Hephestean-forged capacitors" [sic] (Ch15). "Forge Cohorts" built the Aquila landers (Ch22). Percy's clone body was grown in "a Hephaestean vat" (Ch23).
  - For the future Empire's broader context see `E08_Hera_Ares_Hestia_Demeter_Future.md` and `00_START_HERE_Version_Guide_and_World_Primer.md`.
- Athena in Ch19 first suspects Percy's implants might be "some experimental graft from Hephaestus' workshops". They are not the present Hephaestus's work. The future Athena signed off on the Aegis Vestments design and wrote Minerva's final code, and the future Hephaestus forged the Vestments into armour (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C14).

Scarred hands (Ch22). Nothing else is described.

## Personality
- **Silent, absorbed craftsman.** At a council full of shouting he speaks not a word. He studies Imperial technology with fascination.
- **Curious about future tech.** The "small sound" he makes over Neumond's feather suggests admiration or puzzlement.

## Continuity Notes
- Who designed what (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C14): Ch1 calls the suit "of his own design" (Percy's), with "hephaestus-blessed servos", and Ch6 says the armour "was divine, yes, forged by Hephaestus". The internal Aegis Vestments are a different object: the future Athena signed off on their design (Ch19), future Annabeth championed the surgery, and Hephaestus forged them into armour. Never write that Annabeth designed the suit or the Vestments.
- Spelling: the text writes both "Hephaestean" (Ch22) and "Hephestean" [sic] (Ch15, Ch21). Use "Hephaestean" in prose.
- Neumond is a winged Hephaestean **legacy** operator, not a drone (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C24).
- The Divine Being title *Forgemaester* is vault-only (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C32).
- Present-day year: c. 2021 per the author's timeline, nominally the canon PJO era (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R2).
