## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **Not the Titaness Themis** of Greek myth (goddess of divine law). This Themis is explicitly "the aforementioned demigod" and is referred to as "him".
- **Solon Themis (Ch 23)** is a Voss Sovereignty figure who "shipped in by the million" the colonists of Voss Secundus and who built the Themis Needle, a 2.5-km black-glass spire in New Rome, "in the second year of the war, when the Princes still believed they could win." He is also the subject of "very detailed and anatomically improbable" tunnel graffiti. The text never says whether Solon Themis is the Ch 5 Justicar. (A "Solon Themistokles" also appears in the author's Ch 2 OC list.)
- **Author's vault note (not yet in text):** Solon Themis was the **first Justicar of the Voss System** and second-in-command ("SIC") to Lysandra Voss, the Princes' Supreme Elect Admiral, in the War of Princes; he shipped colonists to Voss Primaris and Voss Secundus. The author's notes identify Ch 5's "Justicar Themis" with Solon Themis. PLANNED (vault): "his tomb lies at the heart of Voss I, waiting to be revived by Apollo eventaully" [sic]. If that death has already happened by Ch 5, the Ch 5 Justicar would instead be a successor holding the office and the name; the text does not say which, so present the identification as author intent, not page fact.
- Ch 5 and Ch 23 describe Voss differently but compatibly. In Ch 5 Kuroyami calls the Sovereignty and the Empire "allies", and the Sovereignty is under a Justicar. In Ch 23 it is the product of the Princes' rebellion, with a treaty barring any divine being from governing it. After the Treaty (16227) Voss is an autonomous, treaty-restricted state at peace with the Empire, governed by a demigod Justicar, so Ch 5's "allies" is diplomatic courtesy (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C5). Both chapters are in the same era (~16231).

## Appearance
He sits with "the few attendants" beside him and "scooted a bit closer" to Kuroyami. No physical description is given.

## Personality
- **Diplomatic and constructive.** He confers briefly with his attendants, then approves counter-piracy measures and proactively suggests expanding the embassy, because its systems haven't been updated since "a few millenia past the War of Princes" (a dating slip; see Continuity Notes).
- **Kind and observant.** He notices the gods have lost interest in "the present mortals" and draws Kuroyami out of the uneasy silence with a polite question.
- **Formal and courteous.** "The Voss Sovereignty would be honoured…"

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Measured, gracious and formal. British spelling ("honoured"). He addresses her as "Diplomat Kuroyami".
- "The Voss Sovereignty would be honoured to allow the establishment of counter-piracy measures." (Ch 5)
- "Might I suggest expanding the current embassy alongside this?" (Ch 5)
- "Diplomat Kuroyami, may I inquire what the millions of drachamae will be used for?" (Ch 5)

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
Treaty authority: Kuroyami cites the Treaty of Mount Rümker to override Zeus's veto, since "the Voss Sovereignty is under the jurisdiction of Justicar Themis." No divine powers are shown.

## Backstory
His jurisdiction comes from the Treaty of Mount Rümker. Ch 23 says the War of Princes "had ended in a draw" "at the neutral ruins on Gaia's moon", "Quite a few years earlier"; the treaty barred Voss from expanding for three centuries and barred any divine being from military or executive governance in the Voss system.
- Author's vault note (not yet in text): the War of Princes ran 16212–16227 (war declared 16213.1.1.1; Treaty ratified 16227.2.4.1). The vault places the ratification at the neutral ruins of Tears of Lysandra (a moon in the Voss system) and records a "Tactical victory declared for the UOE", with Voss keeping regional autonomy. The text's Gaia's-moon site and "draw" take precedence. If the Ch 5 Justicar is Solon Themis, he was the Princes' second-in-command in that war.

## Story Arc So Far
**Ch 5:** Kuroyami invokes his jurisdiction against Zeus. He approves the waystation and proposes the embassy expansion. Athena's soul-tax and the Old Earth discussion happen around him. After Zeus storms out, he asks Kuroyami about the budget and the gods resign themselves to "another few hours of nothing."

## Continuity Notes
- "Rümker" (Ch 5) vs "Rumker" (Ch 23 and the vault). Profiles use "Treaty of Mount Rümker" (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md S11).
- **War of Princes dating:** Themis's "a few millenia past the War of Princes" (Ch 5) and Poseidon's "millennia ago" (Ch 15) conflict with Ch 23 ("Quite a few years earlier"; gods barred from the system "for four years"; Kagura's relay work in "16215") and with the author's dating (16212–16227, ending about four years before the ~16231 far-future scenes). The "millennia" lines are slips (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R5). Ch 5 cannot be millennia after Ch 23, because it is tied to Percy's Ch 1 departure spike in the same era.
- **Treaty site and outcome:** text = neutral ruins on Gaia's moon, a draw (Ch 23); vault = Tears of Lysandra, a UOE tactical victory. Use the text (flagged for the author in 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md §5).
- **Voss "allies":** diplomatic courtesy, not a military alliance (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C5).
- **Solon Themis:** the author's vault makes him the first Justicar of Voss; the text never links him to the Ch 5 Justicar by name.
