## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- This is the only Grover in the story. He is the present-day satyr of the timeline Percy reincarnated into.
- Percy remembers an *old-timeline* Grover only once. In his original life he first came to camp "barely conscious, slumped over Grover's shoulder" (Ch7). That earlier Grover lived the canon events. This Grover is meeting a very different Percy.
- He knows nothing of the far future. He has seen Minerva's manifestation only secondhand, through camp gossip; he wasn't on the Ch11 bus.

## Appearance
- Hooves and goat ears that "flatten" when worried and "flick" toward every shadow (Ch12, Ch16, Ch17).
- Carries reed pipes (Ch16, Ch19).
- At Polyphemus's island he is found tied between two rocks "like some horrible parody of a scarecrow," in a sagging "fake bridal veil" (Ch14).
- Sleeps "curled up with his jacket as a pillow" (Ch18).

## Personality
- **Loyal, anxious and brave when it counts.** He bleats nervously (Ch12, Ch15), fusses, and grabs pillars during Eleni's power demonstration (Ch15). Yet he shields friends, joins every quest, and stands beside Percy facing the Manticore.
- **Earnest and dramatic.** His battle cry at Thorn, "You won't win. Not while we stand," makes Percy suppress a cringe at the cliché (Ch15).
- **Caring.** He presses Percy to eat ("You'll pass out before tomorrow", Ch18) and notices when Percy looks bad ("Like you fought a cement mixer", Ch18).
- **Perceptive about nature and the divine.**
  - He senses the camp's "wrongness" ("You feel it too?", Ch12).
  - He whispers to Thalia's dying pine in Ancient Greek, and it twitches "Like they were listening" (Ch12).
  - He senses vibrations through floorboards (Ch17) and smells Othrys's "bad stench" (Ch19).
- **Thoughtful under the nerves.** After the Fleece fight he works out that it cut off Polyphemus once it rejected Annabeth, and that Percy is "the intended recipient" (Ch14).
- **Easily embarrassed.** Zoë jabs that the bad stench "is you," and he looks away (Ch19).

## Voice & Speech Patterns
- Casual, breathless, nervous, often trailing off. He uses "Like…" emphases and bleats. He swears mildly or not at all. He calls Percy by name and uses the camp's usual slang.

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Empathy link with Percy.** Minerva warns that "the Satyr will realize our connection too quickly if he comes too close" (Ch7). Percy deliberately keeps his distance from "the Satyr's emotional link" (Ch12). In canon this link forms during *Sea of Monsters*; here it already exists, or is assumed, by Ch12.
- **Nature magic.** His reed pipes force roots through bare stone at Othrys to tangle monster legs, "bought seconds that meant lives" (Ch19).
- **A goat-call** that times the Fleece ambush (Ch14).
- **Iris messaging.** He IMs Percy from Westover Hall, under his desk during a geometry quiz (Ch15).
- **Satyr instincts.** Annabeth: "He'll wake up if anything dangerous comes close. Satyr instincts." (Ch15). He has a keen nose and sense of vibration.
- He can speak Ancient Greek to trees (Ch12).

## Backstory
- As a canon baseline, he is the satyr who protected Percy at Yancy and brought him to camp in the old timeline. In this timeline Percy skipped that: he walked to camp alone with the Minotaur's horn (Ch7).
- Chiron calls him in from the forest when Percy arrives (Ch7).
- In Ch3, the Labyrinth monster camp was planning "an attack on a few demigods and a satyr". This was possibly aimed at Grover's escort mission, but the text never confirms it.

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch7.** Chiron orders "Call Grover, a satyr in the forest." Percy recalls arriving on Grover's shoulder in his old life. Minerva worries Grover's empathy link will expose her.
- **Ch8–11.** Not shown on the Vegas/Fort Reagan trip.
- **Ch12.**
  - Grover greets the returning group with "bleating excitement," which implies he met them at the airport; that isn't explained.
  - At the dying camp his ears flatten: "You feel it too?" He whispers to Thalia's tree, and the branches twitch.
- **Ch13.**
  - Grover is "currently detained in Polyphemus' territory." Athena sends a dream-vision of his pleading voice to Percy to manipulate him into leaving.
  - The barrier breaks when "an new unnamed demigod escorted by an exhausted satyr" arrives. Whether that satyr is Grover is unclear; probably not, since Grover was already captive.
- **Ch14.** Found tied up in a bridal veil. Annabeth cuts him free, and he warns about the Fleece cloak. His goat-call distraction helps topple Polyphemus. He explains the Fleece's choice of Percy.
- **Ch15.**
  - Asleep under the sail on the way home. Annabeth: "Satyr instincts."
  - Assigned to the Eleni watch squad, he pretends to eat celery and grabs a pillar during her "two percent" display.
  - Three days before Christmas he IMs Percy: freezing at Westover Hall in Maine, sure "the vice principal suspects."
  - At the tree line: "About time… You look like you just ran a marathon." He leads the chase after Thorn and the di Angelos and faces the Manticore ("goat-boy").
- **Ch16.**
  - Gasps "Percy… your sword…" as Riptide flies to Artemis and speaks.
  - Rides Apollo's bus to camp ("Grover fussed").
  - Joins the quest west with Percy, Zoë, Bianca and Eleni, "fingers twitching around his reed pipe."
- **Ch17.**
  - At Ammit-Sekhem's shrine: "That's… not Greek. Like, at all." He bears witness to Sariel, Manannán, Asmodeus's fortress and the Hellway.
  - Senses vibrations in Flamel's shop.
  - Introduced in New Rome as "a companion familiar with the wilds." He crosses the Little Tiber (the text's "Mini Tiber", Ch17) with Zoë and Bianca.
- **Ch18.** Watches Percy's three duels from the praetorian box, pale. At dinner: "Eat." In the morning he notices Percy bleeding for the first time; Percy had given fragments of his Achilles curse to Artemis and is left at about half strength (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C13).
- **Ch19.** At Othrys he and Zoë trade barbs, and his pipes tangle monsters. He holds the line below with Bianca and is pulled clear in the mass teleport.
- **Ch20–23.** Not present.

- **Annabeth Chase** (`F01_Annabeth_Chase_Present.md`). Close friend who crosses the sea to rescue him.

## Continuity Notes
- **Ch12 vs Ch13.** Grover is with the group returning to camp (Ch12), then "currently detained" on Polyphemus's island (Ch13). The gap is unexplained.
- **"First time I've actually seen you bleed" (Ch18).** Percy had bled before (Ch4, Ch7 "spitting silver-red blood", Ch11), but never in front of Grover.
- **"The twins."** The narration and Percy's dialogue call Bianca and Nico "the twins" (Ch15–16). In descriptive prose they are siblings; quote "twins" only as the text's label (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C22).
- **Status.** Grover is confirmed alive (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C20); he is simply absent from Ch20–23.
