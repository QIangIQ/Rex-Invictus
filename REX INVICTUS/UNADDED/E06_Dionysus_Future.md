## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Madness:** his domain in canon. The future scale is implied to be orders of magnitude greater than the present-day god's (the Ch22 Watchpoint's records). Present-day demonstrations of his madness belong to `D07`.
- **Dionysian Division:**
  - Mercenary platoons of conscripted Legacies, such as Captain Kallix's 32-operator strike platoon.
  - Operators wear exo-suits running deprecated RDSA OS 9.341.Α.
  - Kill-cost billing, raised moonside to "20 Drachamae per entity" [sic] (Ch18), live-streamed on a Dionysian holonet channel.
- **Dionysian aerospace:** Quarterdeck 19, "Nineteen", a logistics station above Nysa IX, one of 6,000. It sits on a Relay Gate between Olympian Airspace and a Tartarus distribution nexus, is colour-coded by failure probability, and has "no green". "Dionysian aerospace was rarely given too much maintenance" (Ch14).
- **Fleet (Ch2):** commanders of his line, such as Damocles of the *UOE Celestial Martyr* (Rank 14,923).
- **Bloodlines:** Damocles Euphemios (Ch2); Demeter-Dionysian legacies on the Tartarus front (Ch19).

## Backstory
- He is allied with Poseidon's and Aphrodite's children in the Ch2 coalition against Zeus's line ("Our fathers would be greatly displeased").
- He fights recurring border wars with Zeus's Imperiality. These put Eleni's battlegroup into repair dock, which is why she was patrolling Fulguris (Ch15: "while the Battlegroup underwent repairs due to Dionsyian-Zeus [sic] border conflicts"). She died at Fulguris to the Dionysus–Poseidon–Aphrodite coalition (Melina Callisto's ramming, Ch2); her last memory curses "Dionysus" (Ch15).
- **Author's vault note (not yet in text):** an Annex (3331) records his "D-05" order: "let 'em party". In 3048 isolated Dionysus and Hecate legacies form fringe cults. PLANNED (author intent): a reduced divine fragment of him will co-oversee **The Citadel**. His UOE Unified Rank is #168.
