## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- There is only one Chaos. She meets the **old-timeline Lord Commander Percy** (`A01_Percy_LordCommander_OldTimeline.md`) and sends him back into his younger body (`A02_Percy_Reincarnated_Rex.md`): a young teen; the text calls him sixteen (Ch7, Ch13, Ch22), though the premise blurb says he returned as a twelve-year-old (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R1).
- **Not** to be confused with Chronos/Kronos (Chiron distinguishes "the Primordial of time Chronos" from the Titan, Ch3; see `E14_Kronos.md`), nor with the "Heart of Tartarus" that opened the Ch23 wound (a different primordial organ).
- **Knowledge:** she knows Percy's whole life ("thy strain upon my existence even before your conception"), the future "Great Cleansing", and the sealed vaults. She cannot see where the vaults are.

## Appearance
- First seen as "an eerily familiar form crouched beside a strange, pitch black fire" in Hestia's courtyard, hooded. Percy mistakes her for Hestia (Ch1).
- "A pair of eyes, infinitely vast, filled with stars". In them Percy "saw despair". She has "a hand, infinitely cold" (Ch1).
- She speaks "with multiple voices, many female, and an equal amount male", then chooses "a more feminine figure, her tone shifted to match her new form" (Ch1).
- She materialises "upon the throne" above "an endless abyss" with "a quiet roar", her "signature dull roar".
- For storytelling she becomes "flowing dark robes, a pair of comically out-of-place reading glasses, marble slab in hand" (Ch1).
- She crosses "event horizons of infinity" "with barely a noise" (Ch1). At parting she "flashed a smile" and faded (Ch3).

## Personality
- **Bored and seeking entertainment:** "The plane of chaos has become too lethargic, as of late. Aeons of repeating movements can cause wear on even a soul of my caliber." She asks him to "make what you do interesting, okay?" (Ch1, Ch3)
- **Nostalgic and theatrical:** "I've missed this." She plays storyteller, complete with props.
- **Pitying:** she "looked on with a pitying glance".
- **Admiring:** she calls Percy "one of the greatest descendants from the humani" and his life "a tapestry surpassing the legends of most gods".
- **Manipulative:** she offers "the path to the exit" or a leap into the abyss. Athena's rangefinder reads the exit's distance as "Undefined", and Percy realises "Must have been part of her plan." (Ch3)
- **Petty and wry:** "could you please sacrifice a few more offerings to me in the future? I only receive a handful every millennia you know." (Ch3)
- **Critical of the Olympians:** she says her "great-grandchildren would have been wise to incorporate your presence as an arbiter and justiciar", since "the council shattered merely centuries after…" (Ch1). "The council" is unspecified; the context of "my great-grandchildren… needed it" favours the Olympian council.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
She mixes archaic forms ("dost thou", "thee", "thy") with Yoda-like inversions in her tale ("a war there was", "Hidden lies the Vaults", "fashion I the keys") and breezy modern asides ("hmm?", "okay?", "Best hurry along now", "you know").
- "Shall we conduct this meeting somewhere else, perhaps?" (Ch1)
- "I've missed this" (Ch1)
- "For what reason dost thou not accompany my own great-grandsons in their journeys?" (Ch1)
- "Allow me to regale you in a story, Rex Graecus, one told to only my closest of Creations." (Ch1)
- "Fire we all yoked, never believing it would burn us." (Ch1)
- "Hidden lies the Vaults, from even my infinite sight," (Ch1)
- "Perhaps we can make a deal hmm? I know of your talents, after all." (Ch1)
- "I only receive a handful every millennia you know." (Ch3)

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Absolute presence:** her touch freezes Percy in place, and black fire transports him to her abyss (Ch1).
- **Shape and voice:** shifts between many voices and forms.
- **Omniscience over a life:** she knew Percy "even before your conception".
- **Key-forging:** "fashion I the keys, as limited as the opening" to the sealed vaults (Ch1).
- **Time and reincarnation:** the "gift" of her gamble sends Percy's soul back into his younger body. Percy himself estimates that he "transmigrated approximately twenty thousand standard years into the past" (Ch3). That is his own estimate of the jump; the canonical distance between the present and the far-future scenes is about 14,000 years (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R3).
- **Limits:** the vaults are "Hidden… from even my infinite sight."

## Backstory
- **The primordial war:** "Long before… your race… a war there was between entities you would think gods." On one side were "gods of energy and star", on the other "the ceaseless and unending encroachment of the sentient void". Their powers were beyond their own understanding. "One after one we toppled". Her "our" places Chaos among the combatants (Ch1).
- **The vaults:** their "lesser creations" sealed the devices "in the most secure vault-tombs and ossuaries in the nexus of their most defended fortresses" (Ch1).
- **The future:** she tells Percy that "the council shattered merely centuries after", in what he would call "The Great Cleansing" (Ch1). Which council is unspecified; the context of "my great-grandchildren… needed it" favours the Olympian council (compare the Olympian Court that crumbles in Ch13).
- **Author's vault note (not yet in text):** Chaos is the Creator and holds UOE Unified Rank #1. She is androgynous and switches easily between male and female pronouns (compare her Ch1 chorus of "many female, and an equal amount male" voices). She manifests as female, human-shaped constellations, and no one can meet her eyes without risking insanity. The power she grants transports a portion of the power of a random god, a gamble that the recipient must then force into practical use.

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch1:**
  - At smoke from Hestia's courtyard, Percy finds her by a black fire and calls her Hestia. She turns her starry eyes on him, freezes him with a cold hand and takes him to her abyss throne.
  - She names him, mentions the Great Cleansing, and tells the tale of the primordial war and the vaults. Percy refuses to be a pawn of the "Divinities", but privately wants the power.
  - She lifts his chin and proposes a deal.
- **Ch3:**
  - She notes "your disappearance has already been noticed by far too many of my children", asks for more offerings, and offers two choices: the exit back to "the eternal flame of your first Olympus", or a jump into the abyss for "a gift or two for your confidence".
  - The exit is "Undefined" far, so Percy jumps and wakes in the present day (Ch3's nominal date is August 2005; the author's timeline puts the present at about 2021; see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R2).
- **Ch23 (possible echo):** Aphrodite smells "Chaos… On the other side. A great deal of it." at the Phlegethon Wound, where Percy's verdict comes through. It is ambiguous whether this refers to the primordial.

## Relationships

## Continuity Notes
- "Baselius" is a garbled *basileus*.
- **Which council shattered (Ch1):** "the council" is unspecified. The context of "my great-grandchildren would have been wise to incorporate your presence… They certainly needed it" favours the **Olympian** council. Earlier drafts of this file read it as the Sentinels' council; that reading is dropped. (The Ch13 Olympian Court that "crumbled" may be the same event.)
- Era figures (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R3): Percy's "twenty thousand standard years" (Ch3) is his own estimate of his jump; the far-future scenes are about 14,000 years after the present.
- Percy's body age after the jump: see R1. Present-day year: see R2.
- Vault eye-contact rule: the vault says no one can meet her eyes without risking insanity, yet in Ch1 Percy looks into her starry eyes and sees despair. Treat the vault rule as author intent for the later mythology (vault note only).
