## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
Eleni is a single character, but she must be played in the correct **state**:

- **Ch2 Eleni (pre-displacement, UOE era).** A newly appointed, insecure commander on the bridge of the *Divine Crusader* above the shipyard planet Fulguris. She bites her nails, drinks coffee, swears ("fucking sitrep", "haul ass"), and whispers "Why me?" under a ceiling mural of Perseus Jackson. She knows Percy only as a legend ("the legendary Autokrætor"). She spots the overextended *Vengeful Wrath* and "a faltering in the ship on its starboard" (the *Radiant Crusade*; Stephanos's POV reveals that sleeper cells have sabotaged its engines). The chapter ends with Melina Callisto's *Radiant Crusade* ramming her ship and detonating; her fate is left open, and a "dying form" whispers of rebirth. In Ch5, future Zeus has "recently" lost a daughter "Eleni… something?" to "Poseidon's damned direct descendant", so in her own era she is counted as killed.
- **Displaced Eleni (Ch15 onward).** She emerges from Thalia's pine with Thalia, horribly wounded, sure she is dead and that Percy is the "Tidefather" come to guide her to Elysium. From then on she is formal, liturgical and machine-precise, calls Percy "High Lord" / "milord", speaks UOE jargon nobody understands, and serves as his bodyguard. The nervous swearing commander of Ch2 is largely gone (see Continuity Notes).

She is **not** connected to:
- **Thalia Grace** beyond being a half-sister. Thalia dreamed of her and believes Eleni "clung to me" in the tree (Ch15). See `C06_Thalia_Grace_Present.md` and `C07_Thalia_Grace_Future_Lieutenant.md`.
- **Marien Pyrrhus** or the Kappa-Seven Ekdromoi, who came back through a separate event, the Tartarus "shear" to Mars (Ch19–22). Eleni came through Thalia's tree. See `G02_Marien_Pyrrhus.md`, `G09_Kappa7_Ekdromoi_Squad_Overview.md`.
- The **future Zeus** who forgot her name (`E01_Zeus_Future.md`) versus the **present Zeus** who claims her (`D01_Zeus_Present.md`).

Related files: `G12_Rex_Imperator_II_Cast.md` (her Ch2 crew and enemies), `G03_Stephanos_Pyrrhus.md` (the Poseidon commander whose fleet beat her), `A02_Percy_Reincarnated_Rex.md`, `A06_Percy_The_Legend_Lord_Imperator.md` (the cult she grew up in), `B01_Minerva_In_Percys_Head.md`.

## Appearance
- **Ch2:** An overcoat "each celestial-bronze thread imbued with the power of thunder and lightning". She carries an electrically crackling spear and shield and a ceremonial sword whose sheath scrapes her thigh. She floats from her seat; the floor sparks and fizzes where she walks; her voice carries "a usual buzzing static from her uncontrolled powers". Her bridge crew wear ultramarine blue and gold.
- **On arrival (Ch15):** Taller than Thalia, "older, well-built", **pale bronze skin**, **white hair** matted with blood, a scar beneath one eye. Static buzzes in the grass around her.
- **Armor (Ch15):** Blue, gold and red; it shimmers unnaturally and is "not celestial bronze, not Imperial gold". Jagged runes, partly Olympian. A pauldron of Zeus: an eagle with outspread wings clutching a thunderbolt. Sleek and geometric, "almost like the way Daedalus had etched his designs, but even more advanced". The imperial crest of the Navy, smeared with soot. Hephaestean-forged capacitors along the spine; life support and self-repair; divine-nanofluid IV threads; an ichor-based hemostatic gel infused with hydra blood; auto-nectar-adrenal injectors at the spinal base; empty magnetic holsters; steel boots.
- **Wounds on arrival (Ch15):** radiation burns on temples and neck; one arm bent with the gauntlet fused to the skin; breastplate caved in "as if struck by a direct-impact warhead"; ribs through the suit lining; a spike of lunar alloy below the collarbone ("probably from her own command bridge"); one boot gone and the foot seared to the bone; shards of glowing reactor glass in the other leg.
- **After healing:** stiffness in the left leg and tremors in the right hand (Ch15). At Goode High she wears a long coat over her armor and moves "like a ship through still water".
- **Eyes** (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C2): **silver at rest**, blazing **white / white-gold** when her power flares. When she screams awake they are "bright white and glowing"; then they flash "white-gold", with "twin slits of raw divine current". At rest she has "silver eyes" (Ch15). Outliers: Nicholas Flamel's impression of "the blue-eyed girl in futuristic armor" who "emanated petrichor" (Ch17) and Ch21's "dark eyes". See Continuity Notes.
- **Aura:** unstable and arcing (Ch16); she leaves stinging sparks on leaves (Ch15); birds stop chirping near her; satyrs avoid her. In Ch21–22 her augments throw out about **50 tesla** of magnetic field; she has a forearm augment with a capacitor and projector (Minerva hijacks it in Ch22). In battle, loose metal (nails, hinges, a doorknob, coat buttons) rises and spins around her.

## Personality
- **Insecure but determined (Ch2).** Alone in her sanctum she says "Why me?" and "I am not ready", and fears "my inadequacies will lead us to ruin". She still resolves to "fashion herself a story to equal the legendary Autokrætor" and rises to the moment.
- **Calm command over private panic (Ch2).** She bites her nails while reorganising a scrambled repair fleet into Strike Teams Alpha–Gamma and Defense Forces Delta–Zeta, but her orders come out "steady despite the turmoil churning within her".
- **Tactically sharp.** She spots the gap between the *Vengeful Wrath* and the *Radiant Crusade* and quotes Athena: "A confident enemy will always make a mistake." (Ch2). In the present she reads attrition rates, Roman formations and artillery timings at a glance (Ch18–19).
- **Devout, almost fanatical, about Percy (Ch15).** She believes him a deity. She recites cult titles and hymns, describes shrines and murals, and reports the Artemis–Percy marriage lore with radiant conviction ("There is."). She cannot hear the others mocking her.
- **Rigid protocol.** She stands "one fist over her heart, head bowed. Not in weakness. In structure. In protocol." She looks to Percy for permission before showing her power (Ch15).
- **Terse and precise.** She "barely spoke unless questioned" (Ch15). Her scan results come out "flat, almost routine" (Ch16).
- **Honest but withholding.** Before the Olympian Council she answers truthfully but incompletely ("That was not a lie. But it left too much untouched") and keeps silent about where she trained (Ch15). She never says what she and Zeus discussed. In Ch22 she lets slip more than she meant ("Eleni seemed to realize that she had said more than she intended") about the Pyrrhus dynasty.
- **Impatient with incompetence, dry pedant.** "Do not force me to repeat myself." "That interpretation is technically incorrect… But it will suffice." (Ch18).
- **Small human cracks.** She kicks a rock and kicks a pebble at Percy to warn him (Ch17), smiles with relief when Condottiero leaves (Ch17), gives Minerva a flat, exasperated look (Ch21), and makes a dry joke about "the Empire's naming committee" (Ch22). She tactfully turns away when Artemis and Percy embrace (Ch19).
- **Wary of the unknown.** At Ammit-Sekhem's shrine she is suspicious of Percy; after Sariel she watches him "with a trace of unease" (Ch17). In San Francisco she is unusually nervous: "Too many aggressive signatures" (Ch17).
- **Disgust for demons.** "Demons. Kin of the 32nd. Lord Asmodai the Wrathful." (Ch16).
- **Relentless in combat.** In Alaska she keeps advancing with a shattered capacitor and an opened neck, until a head shot drops her (Ch21).
- **Loyal, and afraid of being a traitor (Ch22).** In the elevator her hands shake: "I am about to fight my own people." She swore as a girl to protect Imperials and knows the Empire's "very old laws" for traitors. Faced with Oriel she says simply "I am not a traitor."
- **Creative with regulation.** To unlock the prisoners' suppression locks she requests a temporary promotion to "Selenide Bulwark for Lord Phoebus Apollo" and invokes CODE 4 with Percy and Apollo signing. Minerva calls her "creative" (Ch22).
- **Proud, not cowed by gods.** She contradicts Hera in open council without lowering her eyes (Ch22). She meets Zeus's gaze "for the first time" with "neither challenge nor submission" (Ch15).

**Growth/contradiction:** The arc runs from a commander who doubts herself, to a worshipper who thinks she is dead, to a capable, slightly sardonic subordinate who treats Percy as a superior officer rather than a god, and finally to a soldier facing a loyalty crisis against her own Empire.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
- **Ch2:** Modern military with profanity ("fucking sitrep", "haul ass"), clipped radio orders ("Over and out"), and pious set-phrases ("For we are the defenders of Zeus").

- Mostly no contractions in formal moments. In Ch21 she loosens a little ("That's concerning", "I'm sure she's invaluable").

Minerva is "Milady".
- Swearing in the present is rare. Ch15 has "Gods Be Damned! Dionysus."

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Raw power of a far-future demigod.** Chiron calls it "the mark of my father's namesake. Chronos". He believes future demigods will be born "with power rivaling the gods of today… Enough to punch through Chronos' Hourglass" (Ch15). Annabeth calls her a "demi-primordial". Present Poseidon: "She's stronger than us." (Ch15).
- **The 2% demonstration (Ch15).** "Without armor. Or capacitors.", she produced a ring of golden flame, a soundless thunderclap, a spiral split in the clouds, celestial script burning into the ground, multicoloured lightning, trees bending inward and the Big House windows cracking. The air tasted of copper, and a psychic pressure made even Percy bend. Thalia was flattened with her hair singed. She warns: "Do not step within thirty feet of my Hammer." What the "Hammer" is is never explained.
- **Electrokinesis.** She casts man-sized bolts into the horde at Othrys (Ch19). Point-blank she compresses an electrokinetic beam that caves in Merek's power armor twice (Ch21).
- **Air-pressure and magnetic fields.** A "giant fist" of compacted air rips Neumond out of the sky (Ch21). Short detonating pressure pulses hit "like invisible hammers"; the field is strong enough to vitrify snow into glass. Augments emit about 50 tesla. A magnetic pulse throws two Ekdromoi into a wall, and she makes Oriel's rifle tremble (Ch22).
- **Divine sensing.** She can sweep 500 km and name pantheons and auras: the Nemean Lion, the General (Atlas) "of Titanic origin", Huitzilopochtli, Christian, Leech, Shinto, Sino, and Asmodeus's demons (Ch16). She detects "hostile signatures" and "neutral auras" (Ch15, Ch17). She identifies Trophimoi, Seidr and cloaked Ekdromoi dropships (Ch22).
- **Master-bolt shard travel.** She carries a "shard of the master bolt", a finger-sized crackling replica. She hurls it as a beacon and combines it with Percy's mist-travel to "piggyback" across hundreds of miles (Ch15).
- **Suit systems.** Self-repair, life support, forced healing, and an IFF carrying an "Imperial encoding, Theodosian house key" (Ch22). In Ch21 Minerva flips her IFF. She credits "High-Lord-Engineer Annabeth Chase's foresight and genius" for the contingency systems that saved her life (Ch15).
- **Training.** Shock-trooper reflexes, Imperial staff doctrine, and knowledge of codes (CODE 4, 24E, Pariah Field, Selenide Bulwark ranks, suppression locks). She has read Empire archive texts about the Labyrinth (Ch21).
- **Limitations.**
  - She is at a fraction of her strength ("restored capacity").
  - She is weakened "away from the light of the pantheon", as in Alaska, "the land beyond the gods" (Ch21).
  - She cannot affect targets under a **Pariah Field** (Ch21).
  - At New Rome, Percy orders "Zoe, Bianca, Grover, cross. Eleni and I will take the alternate path." He lifts a section of the river (the Little Tiber; "the Mini Tiber" in the text), and the two duck under it, "avoiding the truth-revealing waters" (Ch17). It is Percy's choice; the text does not say why she must avoid it.
- **Lost gear.** Her Ch2 spear, shield and ceremonial sword do not come with her ("No blade", Ch15).

## Backstory
- **Future life.**
  - One of Zeus's "Firstborn Million" children, though "not raised as a Sacred Hundred in your Dominion" (Ch15).
  - Of the Theodosian house, which is an old rival of the Poseidon-descended Pyrrhus dynasty; the two "frequently butt heads for border conflicts" (Ch22).
  - As a girl she "swore an oath to protect" Imperials (Ch22).
  - She grew up in the cult of Perseus Jackson. Children chanted "He who fought Titans, who turned back Night!". She saw murals and a 100-metre lunar-steel shrine fed with "dew from comet petals and blood from the descendants of the Sea Lord", and watched "recordings of pantheons falling by your hand" (Ch15).
  - She says she heard his stories "before The Great Expansion"; the wording is ambiguous.
  - She had a mural of Percy on her ship. Ch2 shows it on her sanctum ceiling, carved by "the skilled artisans of Phoebus Apollo": a figure ringed by hands inscribed "Perseus Jackson, the greatest of us all".
- **Command (Ch2/Ch15).** "Only a few Gaian Years" before Ch2 she was appointed commander and pilot of the *UOE Divine Crusader*, a dreadnought guarding the frontier and against "the insidious assaults of their coalition members".
  - She was patrolling the shipyard planet Fulguris while the battlegroup underwent repairs "due to Dionsyian-Zeus [sic] border conflicts" (Ch15).
  - She had personal Augurs in "starched royal blue uniforms", and one officer always burned incense of "sickly sweet flowers and olive" (Ch15). Her tactical officer was Pericles Sophocles (Ch2; see `G12_Rex_Imperator_II_Cast.md`).
  - **Author's vault note (not yet in text):** the vault gives her UOE unified rank as **#301** and lists her honour guard under the Zeus nomenclature RT-ET-{n} 'callsign': RT-ET-Primus 'Eagle Eye' (#568), Duox 'Earl' (#516), Trex 'Prism' (#665), Quaxtro 'Thundercloud' (#669), Quintus 'Therapist' (#680) and Hexas 'Banshee' (#683); Septimus to Dex are unnamed. The Ch2 author's note calls Pericles Sophocles the "Tactical commander above UOE Divine Crusader".
- **Her last battle (Ch2).** A Dionysus / Poseidon / Aphrodite coalition attacked. Damocles Euphemios's *Celestial Martyr* led, Niko's *Vengeful Wrath* spearheaded the vanguard, and Grand Commander Stephanos Pyrrhus's 13-ship *Waves of Poseidon* arrived on her flank (he promised them "within the next 6 Gaian minutes"; her augurs gave "Expected time until contact, 240 Gaian seconds").
  - Her Bolt-class volley (980 beams of lightning) crippled the *Radiant Crusade*. Its commander, Melina Callisto, rammed the *Divine Crusader* and detonated in a "Terra Concrematum".
  - Her own memory: "I was holding the line. My ship was breaking. Gods Be Damned! Dionysus." (Ch15). In Ch22 she blames the Pyrrhus dynasty: "They caused my defeat and the events to send me next to you, my lord."
  - Future Zeus registers the loss of "one of his daughters… Eleni… something?" to "Poseidon's damned direct descendant", and Poseidon gloats (Ch5).
- **How she got to the present.** Unexplained. Chiron: "whatever battle she fought… it was not in this time", "Or something worse" (Ch15). Annabeth: "The Fates must've intervened." Thalia, inside the pine, dreamed of Eleni commanding a battle and believes Eleni "saw me, and clung to me". Ch2's closing "dying form" whispers "But from the ashes of the destruction, we shall be reborn"; this could be Eleni, but the text does not say.

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch2 (Rex Imperator II, far future).** In her sanctum under the Perseus mural she doubts herself, then steels herself. Alarms sound and she drops her coffee. On the bridge she demands a sitrep, sends a distress call through the augurs and organises the scrambled Fulguris repair fleet. Stephanos's *Waves of Poseidon* (13 dreadnoughts, commander ranked 51st) is detected 240 Gaian seconds out. She launches fighter squadrons 1–1500, then sees the overextended *Vengeful Wrath* and the faltering ship beside it (the *Radiant Crusade*, which Stephanos's POV reveals was sabotaged by sleeper cells) and orders a counterattack. 980 Bolt-class beams cripple the *Radiant Crusade*, which rams and detonates on the *Divine Crusader*. Her fate is left unknown.
- **Ch5 (far future, referenced).** At the divine conference Zeus is in a rage, having lost his daughter "Eleni… something" to Poseidon's descendant.
- **Ch15.**
  - The Golden Fleece is hung on Thalia's pine. Two figures emerge: Thalia, and Eleni, dying. She screams with white glowing eyes and faints.
  - In the infirmary tent her suit forcibly heals her. She wakes, realises "This is not the Crusader's bridge", sees Percy and believes he is the Tidefather come to take her to Elysium. She recites his titles, cult and murals and the Artemis marriage lore while Annabeth and Thalia mock her.
  - Three days later Thalia goads her into a demonstration: "two percent".
  - On Olympus she stands before the council in silence. Zeus claims her: "She is mine." Told to swear herself to Olympus, she answers "I already have." She never tells anyone what was discussed and retreats to her cabin.
  - In December she waits for Percy at Goode High ("High Lord… The satyr calls for you"), then bolt-and-mist-travels him to Westover Hall. There she peels off to deal with "multiple potentially hostile signatures, 800 meters northeast", which turns out to be the mercenary helicopter.
- **Ch16.** She "stumbled forward, her expression helpless" behind Artemis as the Hunt arrives. She feels a connection to Riptide when it flies to Artemis. She flickers at the edge of the Hunt camp, avoided by the Hunters. Percy saves a quest slot for her ("She could definitely be useful for the Romans"). She joins the quest (Percy, Zoë, Bianca, Grover, Eleni) and scans 500 km southwest: the Nemean Lion, the General, foreign pantheons, the "Kin of the 32nd".
- **Ch17.**
  - She is suspicious when Percy speaks with Ammit-Sekhem and uneasy after Sariel. She kicks a pebble at Percy: "The 32nd's guards are watching us."
  - At Asmodeus's fortress she stares at Percy's dealings. She asks Condottiero "And you're leaving?" with relief.
  - In San Francisco she is wary of the "aggressive signatures" and guides the group to Flamel's bookshop ("3 neutral auras"), where she guards the door.
  - Percy introduces her at New Rome as "Eleni of Jupiter's". She and Percy pass beneath the lifted Little Tiber.
- **Ch18.** She holds Percy's duelling sabre and cloak during the Trial by Steel and stands like a "silent bodyguard" at the feast. At Othrys the Roman command tent "ceded all practical control to Eleni", and Reyna and Jason cannot follow her jargon. She summons Percy back from his Iris call with Artemis.
- **Ch19 (Battle of Othrys).** She stands at the command cluster casting bolts and complaining that "Mars-Ares elements" do not use their abilities. "Where are their berserkers or commisars?" She reaches the summit after the explosion "silent and precise", watches the Percy–Artemis embrace and deliberately looks away.
- **Ch21 (Alaska).**
  - She escorts Percy through the hostile Labyrinth to Alaska to test his powers without Olympus's coverage. She bickers with Minerva via Percy. Minerva likes her "pattern recognition".
  - Five Ekdromoi emerge. She explains CODE 24E ("I barely qualify for High tier 24E").
  - In the fight:
    - She tears Neumond from the sky and collapses Iane's ribs.
    - She is hit in the abdomen, the forearm capacitor and the neck.
    - She crushes Ione into the ground, breaking his spine, and beams Merek twice.
    - Iane shoots her through the side of the head and she falls.
  - Percy stabilises her by controlling her blood to stop brain swelling. Minerva: "Coma… Acceptable outcome." Percy carries her through the Labyrinth to camp.
- **Ch22.**
  - Up and active again (healed off-page by Apollo), she runs much of the interrogation of the five prisoners. She reveals she is Theodosian and that the Pyrrhus dynasty "are your half-brothers". She invokes CODE 4 and explains suppression locks. She drily explains the Xenophon joke behind "Operation Anabasis-105". She kneels when Artemis appears.
  - Minerva commandeers her forearm projector to play the NASA and White House recordings ("Milady could have asked").
  - On Olympus she corrects Hera ("Forgive me, Lady Hera. A retrieval element is not an invasion force.") and answers Zeus's "And if we do not give them back?" with "Then they will take them anyway, my lord."
  - In the elevator she admits her fear of fighting her own people. Artemis offers her protection; Eleni replies drily "Or they will shoot at both of us."
  - During the battle she identifies the Trophimoi, the Seidr and the dropships. She comes up the back stairs "like a thunderstorm" to save Percy from Oriel and Dace, and is called a traitor by Oriel.
  - She helps Iane refit Neumond's wings so Minerva can reach the Watchpoint.
  - She attends the sunset council, standing apart "with her hands behind her back".

She calls him "High Lord" and "milord", scolds him for hiding, and wants to measure "how far you are above the rank-and-file like us". Before CODE 18 she already treats him as the figure her Empire reveres.

Eleni realises Minerva is "a very unique version" of the Empire's intelligence and complains that "Athena, and by proxy, Minerva" insult demigods in the Empire. Minerva: "I like her… She demonstrates pattern recognition"; "Your Theodosian is creative."

- **Annabeth Chase (present)** (`F01_Annabeth_Chase_Present.md`). Grossed out by the worship ("She's fanficcing you in real time"). Eleni separately venerates the *future* Annabeth as "High-Lord-Engineer" (`F02_Annabeth_Chase_Future_Architect.md`).

- **Apollo** (`D04_Apollo_Present.md`). Provisional "Selenide Bulwark" under him for the CODE 4. He wonders aloud if she's "part automaton" (Ch15).
- **Athena (present)** (`D03_Athena_Present.md`). Suspicious of her liturgical dialect ("I have only recently experienced something similar").
- **Dionysus** (`D07_Dionysus_MrD_Present.md`). Holds a grudge because her demo woke him from his nap and calls her "dangerous".
