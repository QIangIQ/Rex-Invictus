## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
Both boys exist only in the present-day timeline. Neither has a far-future version in the text. Do not confuse them with:
- **Michael Black**, the Selenide Bulwark of Aphrodite in the far-future Interlude (Ch 23; see `H06_Bulwarks_and_Spears.md`). Aphrodite's "Oh, Michael" in Ch 23 refers to him, not to Michael Yew.
- The far-future "Sun-Born" sons of Apollo who died in the War of Princes (Ch 15), and the Apollonian medics/aedificators of the Empire. Those are different people.

**Canon baseline (labelled canon, not story):** In the books, Will became Apollo counselor only after Lee Fletcher and Michael Yew died in *The Last Olympian*, and he was not a senior figure in the canon era. In this AU, Will is already "the counselor" in Ch 7 while Michael is alive, and Michael is the one leading the archers on the porch in Ch 22. The story does not explain the order of seniority. Both details may be author compression. See Continuity Notes.

Neither boy knows anything about Percy's future life, Minerva's nature (beyond the bus scene) or the Empire, except what the whole camp learns in Ch 22.

## Appearance
- **Will:** No physical description is given. In Ch 7 he is "kneeling beside a cot, tending to an injured camper with his usual practiced ease". He arches an eyebrow, crosses his arms, narrows his eyes. Earlier the same day he is seen leaning against a post, chatting with a "taller girl" from the Athena cabin (Ch 7).
- **Michael:** No physical description. His shroud is described: "gold, with a sun on it and a lyre that his half-siblings had stitched by hand, crying" (Ch 22).

## Personality
**Will Solace**
- **Wry, theatrical humour.** He greets Percy with a mock-archaic line ("The hero hath come to my door") and enjoys the bus interrogation as "Theatrics… what a spectacle" (Ch 7, Ch 11).
- **Professional and responsible.** He hands over nectar and ambrosia freely but refuses to give out adrenaline "like candy". He asks what Percy is planning and threatens to tell Chiron if the supplies are misused (Ch 7).
- **Practical and fair.** He corrects Percy's "efficient" to "reckless", then shrugs: "who am I to argue with results?" He gives Percy hemostats and energy boosters in the end (Ch 7).
- **A capable fighter.** At Fort Reagan he and "the Apollo kids" snipe the US Army checkpoints so effectively that General Mikhail stands them down (Ch 10).

**Michael Yew**
- **Brave and decisive.** When two dropships drop ten armored operators in front of the porch, "Michael was the first to shoot." His arrow bounces off a visor. He immediately orders a full volley (Ch 22).
- He is the only named camper among the camp's 19 dead (Ch 22). We see him for only a few seconds, so nothing else about his character is shown.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Will is casual, teasing and slightly formal for comic effect. He uses medical common sense and calls girls "dear" playfully ("dear Rebecca"). Michael speaks only in a battle order.
- "Well, well. The hero hath come to my door." (Will, Ch 7)
- "Most people don't walk into my cabin after fighting a Minotaur. They usually get carried." (Will, Ch 7)
- "It's called being reckless." (Will, Ch 7)
- "I'm not handing over adrenaline shots like candy. What are you planning?" (Will, Ch 7)
- "But if you end up using it for something stupid, I'm gonna personally make sure Chiron hears about it." (Will, Ch 7, abridged)
- "Theatrics, dear Rebecca. He's being judged. And what a spectacle it is." (Will, Ch 11)
- "Apollo cabin!" … "Loose!" (Michael, Ch 22)

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Will:** Field medicine. He controls the Apollo cabin's stock of nectar, ambrosia, hemostats and "energy boosters". He has long-range marksmanship (the "JFK treatment" on Fort Reagan checkpoints, Ch 10). His Ares-blessed paint-round rifle was presumably used then (Ch 9 established that Ares-blessed guns work for demigods).
- **Michael:** Archery. He leads volleys of twelve arrows at a time. The arrows "broke, glancing off, splintering" against Ekdromoi armor. One arrow went through the calf of a woman on a runic platform (Ch 22).
- **The Apollo cabin generally:** In Ch 22 it "split in two": half strung bows on the archery range and half set up infirmary cots "with the grim efficiency of people who had done it before". Apollo's children later cross the lines to meet Imperial medics, and a daughter of Apollo asks "How did you do that?" about a Regen-EX injector (Ch 22). In Ch 21 Apollo orders his "Healers" to save the wounded that Percy brought from Alaska (Percy asks "Those six gonna live?").

## Backstory
Not given for either boy. They are long-time campers by the time Percy arrives (Ch 7).

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch 7:** Will is among the campers who greet the battered Percy after the Minotaur. Chiron sends Percy to "Will Solace, the counselor" for nectar and ambrosia. In the Apollo cabin, Percy (coached by Minerva to collect Regen-Ex ingredients) asks for blood-loss meds and morphine. Will gets suspicious, refuses adrenaline, hands over hemostats and boosters, and warns he'll tell Chiron. Annabeth watches him with suspicion.
- **Ch 9:** Annabeth plans to use "the Apollo campers" to shoot down the army drones after dark. An unnamed Apollo camper walks with Percy and Drew ("At least the local samosas are good").
- **Ch 10:** Annabeth reports that "Will and the Apollo kids" are sniping checkpoints, forcing General Mikhail to stand them down.
- **Ch 11:** On the bus home, Will narrates Annabeth's mock interrogation of Percy to Rebecca of the Ares cabin.
- **Ch 22:** The Apollo cabin is on the Big House porch when two Ekdromoi dropships put ten operators (Cells Lambda-Two and Sigma-Four) in front of it. Michael fires first and calls the volley. The Ekdromoi answer with a four-second cone of fire that "raked across the porch from left to right… and the Apollo cabin went down." Upstairs, Apollo feels his son die. The lamps blow out, and Apollo, as a plague god, almost kills the five bound Kappa-Seven prisoners until Percy talks him down. Afterwards Percy sits "with Apollo beside Michael Yew's shroud until the sun went down." Michael burns on the shared pyre. Castellan Oriel tells Apollo she didn't know "the boy on the porch" was his. Asked if it would have mattered, she says she doesn't know, and Apollo answers "That's the right answer," … "I hate it."
- Will is not named anywhere in Ch 22. Whether he was on the porch, in the infirmary, or survived is **not stated**: his fate is unstated (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C20).

## Continuity Notes
- Will is called "the counselor" in Ch 7, while Michael is shown leading the cabin's archers in Ch 22. This reverses canon order. Treat Will as the healer-counselor and Michael as the senior archer unless later chapters say otherwise.
- "Will" in Ch 10 and Ch 11 is unsurnamed. Since Ch 7 names only one Will at camp, the profile assumes it is Will Solace.
- Neither boy's appearance is described.
- **Wounded count.** Ch 21 says "Those six" and Ch 22 says "the 5 people he brought in". The five are probably the Kappa-Seven Ekdromoi and Eleni is the sixth. Flagged as a story-level slip in 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md §5.
- **Michael's killers.** Earlier drafts named Lambda-Two alone. Two cells dropped (Lambda-Two and Sigma-Four); see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C17.
- **Ch 22 casualties.** Michael is one of the camp's 19 dead. For the battle's overall figures use the author's reconciliation (Zeus 140, the CODE 2 spoof 118, drowned Peltasts 71, about 340 mid-battle, 406 final ORPHEUS dead; 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C8).
