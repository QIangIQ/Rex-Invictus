## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
Only one Neumond exists. Note that the name is used for **him**, for a **small drone** that "detached… and hovered", and in connection with **his ship** ("Mind the way you talk to my ship, prick"). Treat the person as the Hephaestean operator with bronze wings, and the drone and ship as his linked equipment (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C24: Neumond is a winged Hephaestus legacy, and also the name of the drone/ship). See `G09_Kappa7_Ekdromoi_Squad_Overview.md`.

## Appearance
- **Bronze wings.** They unfold in layered segments from his back, with micro-thrusters along the edges and plates that lock audibly into place (Ch20). They retract into housing. In Ch21 they emit "LUNA-ASH" ECM smoke.
- In Ch22 his wings rest on a separate table, removed from damaged mounting points. His organic spine is reinforced, and his "mechanical wings and bodily augments" are partly stabilised by Minerva.
- During the Minerva handshake his eyes turn **neon blue** and every bronze feather is edged with blue fire.
- A HUD and gauntlet with scanning filaments (Ch20).

## Personality
- **Most relaxed of the five; chatty.** In Ch22 he "had gotten comfortable" and talks on autopilot without reading the room. That is how he accidentally prays to Artemis by naming her with her titles twice.
- **Clinical in the air.** "Control surfaces responding within expected parameters." "It remains relevant." "That is sufficient." (Ch20).
- **Teasing.** He jokes "Lucky, eh?" He needles Merek about stolen rations and the mousetrap cheese, and defends his ship ("Mind the way you talk to my ship, prick").
- **Easily unsettled but game.** "That is unsettling." (on Minerva watching him). He goes "Oh shiet." when he realises he prayed. He agrees to become Minerva's doorway with a mix of fear and awe.
- **Gallows humour.** "I was starting to think I'd die tied to a chair, by a guy in a T-shirt." "This is going to hurt," … "isn't it." "Owwwwwwwwwww."

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Wings.** He glides from high altitude with continuous pitch and roll corrections. The wings emit enough ECM "to annul most attempts" to contact a Watchpoint. Minerva explains they are a **systems relay**, "designed to let you interface with your cell's dropship and manage its electronic warfare picture" (Ch22). In the handshake she uses them to reach not Kappa-Seven's dropship (left in Alaska) but "The Castellan's dropship", Lambda-Two's, cloaked overhead.
- **Interfacing.** He can feel "the debris and machinery around Gaia" and interfaces with navigation (Ch20, as reported by the storm-eyed demigod).
- **Scanning.** Gauntlet filaments detect human signatures. His augurs count "140-ish" demigods at camp (Ch22).
- **Drone "Neumond".** It projects routes ("Route established… Proceeding toward Arctic region Obscurus One").
- **Weapons.** A thimble-sized thermal anti-armor grenade (it dug a van-sized ditch); a MORROW-IX with switchable modes; HH-IFG-73 "LUNA-ASH" Quick-ECM smoke (Ch21).
- **Knowledge.** He explains the cooling logic behind Alaska Hill and the Arctic Resource War (Ch20). He recognises a hacking biovirus as "restricted technology" requiring a Tribune's seal (Ch22).

## Backstory
A Hephaestean in Kappa-Seven, chosen by AtRep. He knows standing doctrine: any contact above the god-tier threshold is flagged to the Director, and the Director has "Personal" standing orders about one particular contact (Ch22).

- Author's vault note (not yet in text): the vault's Ekdromoi note calls him a **Hephaestean legacy**, the cell's **Systems and Air Operator** who "flies overwatch on a wing rig"; Hephaestus lines are about 6% of Ekdromoi in the vault's lineage table. The author's Plot Threads flags "Neumond: a winged legacy in one place, a drone or ship in another" as an open roster issue.

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch20.** He glides down and confirms the landing. He bickers with Merek and launches the drone, which charts the route to Obscurus One. He explains the Arctic history.
- **Ch21.** He manoeuvres through the Labyrinth and reports the route complete "in 12 hours on the nose". In Alaska he throws the ditch grenade, lays the ECM smoke and shoots down at Percy, which lifts Straban's suffocation. Eleni's air fist rips him out of the sky. He strafes Percy's chest with tight bursts. Percy's slush column then snaps his wings and bones.
- **Ch22.**
  - Interrogation: he is astonished that Percy can reach the Repertoire and says Minerva "sounds like AtRep". He laughs that he thought he'd die "tied to a chair, by a guy in a T-shirt".
  - He blurts out the Director's standing orders and accidentally summons Artemis. "Standing orders confirmed, Director", and Iane kicks him again.
  - He counts the campers. When Apollo's grief-light explodes, he cries out.
  - He agrees to be Minerva's doorway. Eleni and Iane refit his wings and Athena lays a hand on his shoulder. His eyes go neon blue as Minerva rides through his wings into **Lambda-Two's** cloaked dropship overhead ("The Castellan's dropship"), up its tachyonic relay chain and into the Watchpoint on Mars (the Watchpoint logs "ORIGIN: EKDROMOI DROPSHIP LAMBDA-TWO. CARRIER: KAPPA-SEVEN SYSTEMS OPERATOR NEUMOND"); the result is CODE 18.
  - He collapses ("Owwwwwwwwwww"), is saved by Apollo, and kneels to the Lord Imperator propped up by Apollo.

## Continuity Notes
- "Legacy" (Ch20) versus "demigod" (Ch21–22). The author's vault (`Ekdromoi.md`) says "Hephaestean legacy", the cell's Systems and Air Operator who "flies overwatch on a wing rig".
- **Minerva's relay** (see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C18) ran through **Lambda-Two's** dropship, not Kappa-Seven's; Kappa-Seven's own dropship is in the Brooks Range.
- The name "Neumond" is shared by the operator and the drone ("Neumond detached first… The small drone").
- **Ch20 bay roster.** The five people listed in the bay do not obviously include him. A "cloaked" woman appears instead, and the storm-eyed demigod speaks of "Neumond" in the third person. This is a textual inconsistency.
- **Spellings:** "Hephestean", "Hephaestian", "Hephaestean".
