## AMATERASU (Shinto sun goddess) — future only
- **Old timeline (Ch1):** when the Greeks left, "the Japanese had evacuated their pantheon the quickest, with Amaterasu at the bow, with her flagship The Royal Blade of Retribution". She "quickly suppressed the local Divine population" and left low Earth orbit within days, "presumably to head towards the already-colonized Proxima Centauri". Other pantheons later "follow[ed] the same path forged by The Royal Blade."
- **UOE era (Ch5):** the "Amaterasu Dominion" is now the majority power of the Shinto pantheon. Her envoys (Kagura Kuroyami, called "Perseus Kuroyami" in Ch5, and two others) face Zeus at the summit. Kuroyami must "report back to Amaterasu" about the weaponised moon, and "Re-colonization efforts of earth have to be put on a pause". Her "superiors" are "too attached to their underlings" to sacrifice priests to Athena.
- **Ch18:** a Shinto orbital artillery belt is "a sanctum of Amaterasu", defended by a fox-masked Miko: "Blasphemers… You trespass upon a sanctum of Amaterasu." Shinto ships carry torii pylons and glowing wards.
- **Ch23:**
  - The Dominion "pays in favors" (Talviento). Kagura Kuroyami reactivated the Yamamoto Chokepoint Relay on "December's Eve of 16215" to ally with Voss during the War of Princes. It is the only public route between the UOE and the Dominion.
  - **Author's vault note (not yet in text):** Kagura Kuroyami is the Dominion's chief engineer and, during the War of Princes, Amaterasu's apostle (also "emissary of the Shinto Pantheon and formal ward of Amaterasu"). Lysandra Voss, Supreme Elect Admiral of the Princes, allied with the Dominion (Annex 15632); the Rosenthal company extended the olive branch for that alliance. After the Treaty, the Voss Sovereignty is autonomous, restricted and at peace with the Empire (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C5).
  - Minerva has safehouses among the Egyptians "courtesy of the royal family" and plans to "smuggle into Shinto waters".
  - Naomi of the future Hunt is a granddaughter of **Raijin**, the Shinto thunder god (Ch23).
- **Personality (inferred):** decisive, ruthless (she "suppressed the local Divine population"), protective of her people. She never speaks on-page.
- **Author's vault note (not yet in text):**
  - The future Japanese pantheon "mainly falls under the banner of the Amaterasu Dominion", and the UOE is "in shaky alliance" with it.
  - UOE Unified Ranks: Amaterasu #21, Susanowo #534, Raijin #691 (Freyja #52).
  - The Sea of Monsters (Bermuda) is the border between Poseidon and Susanowo.
  - Shinto War of Princes figures: commanders Yamato Makoto and Mikage Seiryu; Warden Yurei Tensei (killed in 16216 by an Apollonian colonial fleet's solar-flare cannonade); Commander Nakatomi (13 Hoshikage-class battlecruisers erased by a Soteria Node, 16221). Fleet patterns: Amaterasu's Radiance, Hachiman's Vengeance, Kagura.
  - The Heavenly Demonic Sect is loosely allied with the Dominion and neutral toward the Greek pantheon.

## MANANNÁN MAC LIR (Irish sea god) — present day, Ch17
- **Appearance:** "hood drawn low, shirtless, and threadbare shorts"; "a face half-eroded by salt"; smells of "wet ash and brine"; "silver eyes glimmered with a salt-light"; "his form flickering like a reflection on restless water". He waits at a crossroads of three black steles carved with shifting script.
- **Voice:** "all shorelines collapsing", "like waves breaking over rocks". Archaic ("Fare thee well").
- **Personality:** a cryptic guardian who serves "they" who wish things preserved. "I am both present and absent."
- **Role:** in the Americas instead of Ireland, watching the Morrígan, who is "contracted elsewhere, bound to serve some man's schemes".
- **Percy:** greets him "as though seeing an old creditor": "Father told me about you."
- **Quotes:**
  - "Traveler… You know of me." (Ch17)
  - "Ireland… is not where I am needed now." (Ch17)
  - "Guardianship is rarely visible, Perseus. You will learn that soon enough. Fare thee well." (Ch17)

## THE MORRÍGAN (Irish war goddess)
- **Old timeline (Ch1):** the straggler pantheons' "parting gift to humanity was to glass half the hemisphere with their Morrigan-pattern dreadnoughts".
- **Present day (Ch17):** in the Americas, contracted to "some man's schemes", possibly the Roman Triumvirate Holdings. That link is not confirmed. Percy: "She knows not to defy Fate or order… Ares covers her domain here." Manannán: "Morrígan is a force I cannot allow to roam unchecked."
- She never appears on-page.

## THOR (Norse thunder god) — present day, mention only
- "Thor's absence leaves Norse lines weak in Canada." (Asmodeus, Ch17)
- **Wider Norse presence:**
  - Hrafn, a Norse-blooded Trophimoi operator, says: "Mine father's father, perhaps, is walking the north of that planet this very hour." Two ravens (implied Odin's) watch him from Thalia's pine (Ch22).
  - Huitzilopochtli warns "The ravens of the north are watching" (Ch22).
  - In the future, the Norse "pay in blood", have the Norns, and the Norn Urðr "is the past itself". Einherjar veterans serve as mercenaries (Ch23).
  - **Author's vault note (not yet in text):** future Ares challenged Thor at the first Panpantheonic Summit (3047).

## HUITZILOPOCHTLI (Aztec war and sun god)
- **Ch10:** Percy taunts Ares with "Huitzilopotchli" and "Mars Ultor". Ares "stilled" and flickers between alternate forms.
- **Ch16:** Eleni senses a pocket "heavy, heat-soaked—likely Aztec. It smells similar to Lord Mars, his counterpart. Huitzilopotchtli."
- **Ch22 (on-page):**
  - At dawn after the Long Island battle, the sun rises "very red". Hrafn says "That," … "is not thine Apollo's sun." Apollo answers "No," … "It isn't." (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C10: the line is Hrafn's.)
  - A giant golden eagle with smoking wingtips lands and becomes "a man in feathered armor, dark-skinned and painted, with a headdress of hummingbird plumes and a face like a knife", carrying an obsidian-edged war club, with heat rolling off him. An Aztec-blooded Trophimoi operator kneels.
  - He delivers the other houses' ultimatum.
- **Voice:** accented English; terse and imperious; calls Apollo "Little sun".
- **Quotes:**
  - "Little sun… Your family has made a great deal of noise on my continent tonight." (Ch22)
  - "The oldest agreements still stand." (Ch22)
  - "Tell your father… that the other houses are watching." (Ch22)
  - "Then we will come and control it for you," … "All of us. Together." (Ch22)
- **Ultimatum:** Olympus has "one day to show us that it can control" the Imperial army, or the houses (Aztec, Norse, the Jade Court) will act together.

## SARIEL (angel, "Holy Watcher") — present day, Ch17
- **Appearance:** "a figure of pale light, six wings folded close, its head faceless save for a single line of flame", perched on a burned farmhouse. It speaks by thought and vanishes as "a charred beam".
- **Personality:** a judge of balance, startled to be recognised: "You are allowed to witness our kind?"
- **Percy** invites it to trail them: "Come, I have one for thee, Holy Watcher."
- **Later:** it manifests in Asmodeus's throne room "wings unfurling… six blades of radiant light" and attacks him for the Eye-of-the-Cyclops affair.
- **Quotes:**
  - "You dared manipulate the Way of Fate… You upset the balance!" (Ch17)
  - "Eternal or not, no shadow may claim unchecked dominion while the cosmos watches upon thee." (Ch17)
- **Outcome:** the fight with Asmodeus is unresolved. See `I01_Asmodeus.md`.

## AMMIT-SEKHEM (Egyptian guardian demon) — present day, Ch17
- **Shrine:** "A crude stone arch, leaning half-collapsed", carved with "A triple coil, a burning disc, and a form that resembled both man and vulture."
- **Appearance:** "vast and hungry, scaled hide coiling into the earth. Crocodile teeth like obsidian saws". She is female ("She added"). "Its eyes softened when they landed on Percy."
- **Situation:** "still sealed". Percy has foreknowledge of her death: "I watched you fall."
- **Quotes:**
  - "You are no priest… Yet you call my name with the voice of one who watched me die." (Ch17)
  - "Arrogant lies. You have grit, Iawanu… Greek." (Ch17)
- Percy reads her runes because "Minerva was right in teaching me some Egyptian Runes".

## OTHER HOUSES (brief)
- **Jade Court** (Sino-pantheonic): its "cultivators are already walking in the far north" (Ch22) and are converging on Kappa-Seven's dropship in Alaska. In the future it keeps "a Book of Life and Death" with crane-winged judges and "pays in years". "At least 7 in the 36 Cave Heavens and 72 Paradises" own time-gates (Ch23). Deyang Foundries is Jade Court-linked.
- **Egyptians:** "sail a boat through the twelve hours of the night every night and come out at the dawn of the same day" (Ch23). Egyptian-blooded Trophimoi unmake camp wards with hieroglyphs (Ch22). Eleni: "The Egyptians wrote rules for magic before the Greeks learned to write at all" (Ch22).
- **Asian and African mythologies** jointly scraped resources to flee Earth; South American and "fringe european" stragglers glassed half a hemisphere (Ch1).
