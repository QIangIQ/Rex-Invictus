## Appearance
How the legend depicts him:
- **The *Divine Crusader* ceiling mural (Ch2):** among "Murals of ancient Greek Demigods", a figure "surrounded by seemingly hundreds of hands". It "had its Xiphos raised triumphantly, and the hurricane above him seemed to stretch a hand down to the sword". Each hand is inscribed "Perseus Jackson, the greatest of us all", carved "over thousands of hours by the skilled artisans of Phoebus Apollo".
- **Murals in Eleni's hymns (Ch15):** "bare-footed, tridentless, crowned in salt". "They never quite captured thine chin". On Caligo Vale there is marble of him with Artemis: "He—eyes like drowned starlight. She—her bow pointed toward the future they could never share". There is also a wedding mural.
- **The Callistus murals (Ch9):** soldiers recognise the vat clone's face from "them murals back home on Callistus".
- **The Monument of the Two, Via Heroum, New Rome, Voss Secundus (Ch23):** a 30-metre bronze, "The largest bronze in the Sovereignty". A boy and girl of about 17 stand back to back with a leaf-bladed sword and a bronze knife. The boy's hair is in his eyes, and he holds his sword "a little too loosely". The plaque reads "Perseus the Blessed King and Annabeth the Architect, defenders of Gaia, at the Battle of Man-hat-tan". "A face every Hound in the Empire had been trained on since it was a pup."
- **Everyday objects (Ch23):** "He's on everything. Coins. Stamps. Recruitment posters. My grandmother had a tea towel" (Verrill Chase).
- **His seal (Ch22):** "a trident crossed with a lightning bolt, within a ring of laurel". It is kept on a ceremonial tablet in the High Temple on Olympus Secundus, "behind three layers of shielding and a guard of Praetorians".
- **Scroll doctrine (Ch15):** "the eye would bleed if it looked too long", so mortals, "even godborn", need purification to look on his "unveiled presence".

## Personality
How the legend characterises him:
- **Savior and conqueror.** "Half the reason weez got the majority of the galaxy and the Empire is as big as it is is cuz of him" (Mitch, Ch9). "His contributions and services to the Undying Empire had been mythical, and equally unbelievable for his origins" (Ch2).
- **Lonely guardian.** "He's apparently alone on holy Gaia, clearing out the monsters solo while making sure Oylmpus Primaris don't fall" (Ch9). This is the one point where the legend matches A01's reality.
- **Psychopomp and judge.** "In the moment between death and rest, the Greatest Son would walk beside the fallen and lead them home" to Elysium. "Your silence would judge the monsters and demons that plague us. That Olympus would rise again by your hand" (Eleni, Ch15).
- **Forbidden lover.** "The Moonborn's forbidden devotion to the Tidebearer… the Silent Pact. She would not speak his name, lest the sky itself weep… his blade never struck true unless she had watched its arc." The Lady of Luna "wept beneath a black eclipse when he was re-exiled into the Lands of our Birth, Gaia". "The Lord of Demigods did indeed marry the Huntress of the Moon" (Ch15).
- **Contrast with the man:** Artemis warns Rex Percy that the soldiers "will be very, very disappointed" when they find out he is "also a man" (Ch22). The private memories in Ch23 tell a gentler story. Aphrodite: "just a boy with a sword and a sea god for a father and a terrible habit of getting himself hurt". Minerva: "It's not math, AI. You just ask it nicely."

## Backstory
How the myth formed, according to the text:
- **Before the Expansion:** he lived on Gaia. Eleni: "I had heard your stories before The Great Expansion" (Ch15). The vault dates the Great Expansion / Abandonment to year 3045 (00a R4); Ch23 puts his boyhood "A thousand years before the Abandonment".
- **His fame among the gods vs. among mortals:**
  - The gods themselves are dismissive. Athena: "Have we not left that man to guard the grounds of Olympus Primaris?" Zeus: "I knew Poseidon's children were unreliable" (Ch5), while mocking Athena for having "proudly" named him host of a Parlamentum Alpha core (00a C9).
  - Imperial institutions sanctified him: Apollo's artisans painted him, Athena forged his key, and the Council granted it.
- **The Caligo Vale bond (Empire era, early Great Expansion, never before the Abandonment; 00a R8; vault: rite held on Olympus Tertiae):** Artemis was bound to him "For peace. For stability. For the Empire", in a marriage "chosen by Hera on a whim" (Ch13). In legend it became the romance of the Silent Pact.
- **The "re-exile" to Gaia:** the cult believes he was "re-exiled into the Lands of our Birth" and later "vanished from history". Artemis "carved his name into the surface of Luna Secundus with her arrows". The Hunters wore "silver-green bands in remembrance" (Ch15).
  - *Author's vault note (not yet in text):* within hours of the Caligo Vale oathbinding the future Percy was mind-wiped and sent back to Gaia. This is the vault's version of the "re-exile", and may explain why Rex Percy remembers nothing of the marriage.
- **Shrines:** a "humble" 100-metre shrine "Cast in lunar steel". Its daily offerings were "dew from comet petals and blood from the descendants of the Sea Lord" (Ch15).
- **Heresy:** the Order of the Hollow Crescent turned the myth into a liberation cult against "the uncaring gods" (Ch13). See `A05_Him_Within_the_Moon.md`.

## Continuity Notes
- **Time figures (00a R3):** the Empire era is about 14,000 years after the present (vault: about 14,200). The three figures measure different things and are not rival answers: (a) Ch3, Percy's own estimate of his jump, "approximately twenty thousand standard years"; (b) Ch22, "six thousand years old" (Minerva, of the archived key-holder) and "six thousand years in the future" (narration; Luke), a known slip; (c) Ch23, the packet's "APPROXIMATELY FOURTEEN THOUSAND [CORRUPTED] YEARS BEFORE PRESENT".
- **What the gods call him:** "that man" to Athena and one of "Poseidon's children" to Zeus (Ch5; 00a C9), but "our savior" and Lord Imperator to the Empire.
- **His age in art:** the Monument depicts both figures at about 17. The Imperials call Rex Percy "sixteen" (Ch22). Profiles describe Rex Percy as a young teen; the text calls him sixteen (Ch7, Ch13, Ch22), though the premise blurb says he returned as a twelve-year-old (00a R1).
- **Spelling:** Autokrætor / AUTOKRÆTOR is spelled with "æ" throughout. Ch9 spells Olympus as "Oylmpus".
- **Code numbering:** the same number "CODE 15" carries two meanings in different code lists (Ch14 Tartarus list vs. Ch15 Perseus definition).
