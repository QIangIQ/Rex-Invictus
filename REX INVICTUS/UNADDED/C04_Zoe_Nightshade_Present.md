## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **This file** covers the living present-day Zoë, Ch4–Ch22. She knows nothing about the future Empire beyond what she witnesses. In Ch22 a dying Imperial scout tells her she is "on the Wall" and "supposed to be dead", and her only answer is "Not yet."
- **Future Zoë** (`C05_Zoe_Nightshade_Future_Legend.md`) is "Lieutenant Zoë Nightshade, First of the Director's Lieutenants". Imperial optics tag her "POSSIBLE MATCH: LIEUTENANT ZOË NIGHTSHADE (ARCHIVED, DECEASED)" (Ch22). In Ch23 the future Artemis seals "THE ARCHIVED RECORD OF LIEUTENANT ZOË NIGHTSHADE": no one may disclose "THE DATE OR MANNER OF HER DEATH" to anyone, "INCLUDING THE DIRECTOR, IN ANY EPOCH" (see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C7). Imperial children are taught her name in school. Present Zoë must not know any of this as fact.
- **"The Eternal Hunter Princess"** (Ch1) taught old-timeline Percy ancient dialects during "their subjugation of Lord Atlas". This is most likely the old-timeline Zoë, but the text never names her. That version belongs with the legend file.
- **"The ghost of Zoë"** (Ch14), the golden apparition over Riptide, is not this character acting; it is covered in `C05_Zoe_Nightshade_Future_Legend.md` (see also `A05_Him_Within_the_Moon.md`).

## Appearance
- She has dark hair, worn braided, and a circlet on her brow. Her face is "so pale and so still it could have been carved" (Ch22).
- She wears a silver parka in winter (Ch22) and silver Hunter garb otherwise.
- She carries a hunter's bow, plus knives that include a throwing knife that was "small, balanced, meant for throwing rather than combat" (Ch18).
- Flamel notes that she smells "like moss and dirt" and looks like a Persian princess (Ch17).
- She moves lightly, "floated quietly above the ground" in the bookshop (Ch17), and watches "from the shadows" (Ch18).

## Personality
- **Commanding and practical.** At the war table she adjusts figurines on a map of America (Ch4). She sends scouts, orders the pelt brought along, and keeps discipline with chores ("laundry duty for the next week", Ch4). One look silences Phoebe.
- **Contemptuous of males, at least on the surface.** She assumes a fire means "drunken males getting rowdy" and calls Percy "that male" and "boy" (Ch4) and "Freak" (Ch16). Phoebe coined the idea in Ch4 ("So, a freak?"); Zoë's own first "Freak" is in Ch16.
- **Sharply analytical.** From Percy's sea-green eyes she deduces that Poseidon broke the oath, guesses his age, and works out that he is the prime candidate for the Great Prophecy. She knows the history of earlier sons of Poseidon ("Churchill"). She flags his nectar control as unprecedented (Ch4). She also sees straight through his amnesia act (Ch6).
- **Fiercely loyal to Artemis.** She prays to summon her Lady (Ch6) and grows "agitated" and urgent once Artemis is taken (Ch16). One gentle "Zoë." from Artemis is enough to check her fury (Ch19).
- **Grudging fairness.** She lets Percy join the quest because "our Lady seems to trust thee" and she sees "no falsehood in thine eyes" (Ch16).
- **Rule-bound.** She looks "scandalized" as Percy talks to Sariel and bulldozes "Ancient Laws" (Ch17), and again when he and Artemis embrace (Ch19).
- **Dry humour.** She tests Percy with a thrown knife (Ch18) and turns Grover's "bad stench" back on him (Ch19).
- **A lethal, quiet hunter.** In the Ch22 woods fight she kills Imperial scouts she cannot see, counting "Five" under her breath, and adapts the moment they adapt.
- **Growth.** She moves from open hostility (Ch16) to a working tolerance of Percy and teasing tests (Ch18). After Othrys her anger at him is real (Ch19). By Ch22 she is simply Artemis's blade again. When the dying Sabine speaks, she kneels and "looked like she might say something", a rare crack of softness.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Her English is archaic and often ungrammatical: thee/thou/thine, "-eth" and "-est" verb endings, "shouldst", "doth", "hast". She sometimes slips into plain modern speech ("5:30 Tomorrow. Don't be late"). She addresses Artemis as "Milady", "my Lady" or "Lady", Percy as "Freak", "boy" or "son of Poseidon", and her Hunters by name with a title ("Huntress Naomi"). She almost never swears. Her style is terse and imperative, and she gives distances in archaic units ("Two-half stadia thousands", roughly 500 km).
- "Huntress Naomi. Speaketh thy mind. What troubles you so?" (Ch4)
- "Aye, Phoebe, we must be careful around this one. Vigilance shallst ever be my watchword, as it should be thine" (Ch4)
- "Cease thy act, son of Poseidon. Thoust eyes possess an undeniable clarity and knowledge." (Ch6)
- "Thy presence is unwanted, Freak. Thy scent is of the deep, of corruption, of oathbreakers." (Ch16)
- "In the three times we have met, our Lady seems to trust thee." (Ch16)
- "5:30 Tomorrow. Don't be late, Freak." (Ch16)
- "Unless my senses deceive me, it is you." (Ch19, to Grover)
- "Thou knowest my name." / "Not yet," (Ch22)

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- Immortal Hunter. She is a master archer and knife-fighter, with throwing knives and hunting knives.
- **"The Lady's blessing"** lets her feel where prey will be before it moves (Ch22). The Hunters are so "saturated" in Artemis's blessings that Imperial Geistjäger counters, thermal and ELINT cannot detect them (Ch22).
- She can summon Artemis by prayer (Ch6).
- She tracks by scent and senses at long range ("Mine senses detect something afoul brewing", Ch16).
- She carries field medicine (nectar, ambrosia, medipacks).
- She has a long history with Anaklusmos. Artemis recalls that "Anaklusmos had always been more than bronze, Zoe had told her that long ago" (Ch16), and calls the sword "Zoë's sword".

## Backstory
- She is a former Hesperide and Artemis's long-serving Lieutenant. The story leans on canon for this and adds few new details.
- In canon, Riptide began as her gift. The story nods to this with "Zoë's sword" (Ch16).
- Percy's future memory: in the original timeline she died on the Titan's Curse quest. He resolves "He couldn't let Zoe die again" (Ch16).
- **Author's vault note (not yet in text):** the vault files her as "Former Lieutenant" of Artemis, a Hesperide and daughter of Atlas, cast out of the Garden of the Hesperides for betraying them by giving Riptide to Hercules; its Calypso note makes her Calypso's sister. This is canon-baseline lore the story leans on.

- **Ch16:**
  - Artemis is taken. Zoë first names a team (Atlanta, Phoebe, Flavia, Daphnis).
  - Percy knocks on the Hunters' cabin and is dragged inside. Zoë threatens him, then admits him on Artemis's trust.
  - In the end she brings only herself and Bianca, sets out at dawn and pushes the party to leave sooner.
- **Ch17:**
  - She is scandalised as Percy talks with Sariel.
  - She walks the Hellway, stays silent in Flamel's shop, and is introduced in New Rome as an "agent of Diana".
  - Decimus eyes her bow. She crosses the Little Tiber (called the "Mini Tiber" in the text, Ch17) with Bianca and Grover, while Percy and Eleni take the alternate path.
- **Ch18:**
  - She watches the duels from the praetorian box and the feast from the shadows.
  - Next morning her thrown knife cuts Percy's palm. This reveals that his Achilles curse is weakened (he gave fragments of it to Artemis; Apollo later puts it at about half strength, Ch19; see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C13).
- **Ch19:**
  - She quips at Grover on the march.
  - After Othrys detonates she arrives first, "blades drawn", and rounds on Percy ("You absolute fool…") until Artemis's "Zoë." stops her. "That does not absolve him, at all, Milady."
  - The prophecy's "parent's hand" claims a Hesperide instead of her: Atlas's own flailing bolt kills her after Artemis spears him (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C19). The Hesperides and their grove are annihilated. The text does not show how she feels about her sisters.
- **Ch22:**
  - She leads the Hunt back at dusk and answers Artemis's warning about the Psiloi with "Yes, my lady".
  - In the woods she shoots Sciritae from a 30-metre oak, kills Sabine Ro, and hears that she is on the Wall and "supposed to be dead". She answers "Not yet."
  - The Hunt loses Ismene and two others. Artemis learns that history says her Lieutenant dies.

## Relationships
- **Artemis** (`C01_Artemis_Present.md`): total devotion. Artemis is gentle with her (Ch19) and grieves the thought of her death (Ch22). Zoë is visibly shaken by her Lady's open feeling for Percy.
- **Percy** (`A02_Percy_Reincarnated_Rex.md`): she calls him "Freak". She distrusts his scent ("of oathbreakers") and is appalled by his recklessness, but she accepts his place on the quest and later tests him like a sparring partner. Percy is secretly set on keeping her alive.
- **Phoebe, Naomi, Celyn, Atlanta** (`C10_Phoebe_Hunter.md`, `C11_Atlanta_Hunter.md`, `C12_Hunters_of_Artemis_Roster.md`): their commander. She uses chores as leverage and her gaze as discipline.
- **Bianca** (`C08_Bianca_di_Angelo_Present.md`): the only Hunter she trusts to come on the quest.
- **Grover** (`F04_Grover_Underwood.md`): she makes him the butt of a dry joke.
- **Eleni** (`G01_Eleni_Theodosia.md`): they travel together. Percy converts Zoë's "stadia" into klicks and has Eleni scan that range (Ch16).
- **Sabine Ro** (`G14_Sciritae_Neodamodes_and_Ch22_Units.md`): her kill. Sabine recognises her.
- **Atlas / the Hesperides** (`E16_Atlas.md`): her father and sisters. On-page this is only implied; the author's vault names Atlas as her father (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C28).
