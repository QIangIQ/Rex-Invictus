## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **The present-day Kronos** is the only on-stage relevance. He is never seen directly, only through Luke and through his time-field.
- **Not Chronos:** Chiron insists Kronos is "the Titan of harvest, not to be mistaken with the Primordial of time Chronos" (Ch3). Yet in Ch22, Minerva attributes the momentum-stealing field to the "only one being in this world [with] authority over time", implying Kronos. Separately, Chiron says Eleni bears "the mark of my father's namesake. Chronos", and future demigods may "punch through Chronos' Hourglass" (Ch15).
- **Future:** in the old timeline, Percy's war against Kronos is history. The future Thalia "had faced Kronos" (Ch23, remembering the distant past; this is not a UOE-era appearance).

## Appearance
- Unseen. Chiron's class shows a statue of him "eating his own children" and notes he is "always" depicted "with a scythe" (Ch3).
- His presence in Ch22: "a shimmer… faint and golden and old" around the ship. Poseidon feels the cold: "He had felt it once before, a long time ago, when his father's scythe came up out of the dark." (Ch22)

## Personality
Seen only through his agents.
- **Patient and opportunistic (via Luke):** Luke "waited until everyone here was half dead" before striking (Ch22). Minerva: "It is what I would have done."
- **Seductive offers:** through Luke he offers the Imperials passage home via Tartarus, "his father's body", if they "stand aside" (Ch22).

## Voice & Speech Patterns
He has no lines. He speaks through Luke:
- "My name is Luke Castellan. I speak for the Lord Kronos, rightful king of the gods." (Ch22)
- "Tartarus is his father's body. He knows every road in it." (Ch22)
- "The gods don't win." (Luke, Ch22)

Chiron's description: "Kronos was a Titan, to be more specific, the Titan of harvest, not to be mistaken with the Primordial of time Chronos." (Ch3)

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Time manipulation:** a golden field around the *Princess Andromeda* "takes speed away". DMAPFSDS rail rounds fired at eleven times the speed of sound hang "like insects caught in amber" and drop harmlessly. Minerva: "time runs differently around that ship… The faster it is going, the more it loses." It also catches lightning ("your uncle's lightning") (Ch22). Peltast javelins, designed with space-time anchoring, pierce it and create "a wobbly unstable rift".
- **Armies:** thousands of monsters on the *Princess Andromeda*: hellhounds, dracaenae, giants, sea serpents, harpies, Stymphalian birds, telkhines (Ch19, Ch22).
- **The Great Stirring:** the monster awakening that Dr. Thorn invokes (Ch15). Its link to Kronos is implied.
- **Knowledge of Tartarus:** Minerva concedes that he "may know a road through Tartarus" (Ch22).

## Backstory
Canon: overthrown by Zeus. In the story's present he is "still out there" (Ch12). Chiron's camp suspects "the forces of Kronos" are targeting the camp (Ch13).
- **Author's vault note (not yet in text):** his Scythe was forged from Gaia with Chronos's Eternal Sands and Kronos's own blood. Luke Castellan is his host and wields the Scythe (canon/vault). Dr. Thorn is an agent of Atlas and Kronos. His UOE Unified Rank is #9.
