## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **This file:** the 21st-century Zeus who presides over the Olympian Council during the Lightning Thief → Titan's Curse → Anabasis period. He has never heard of the Undying Olympian Empire until Ch22. He does not remember Eleni (because, from his point of view, he has not sired her yet) and says "There are no demigods beyond the Americas" (Ch15).
- **Not** the far-future Zeus of the Undying Olympian Empire, "Roar of Clouds", who has "lost count after a hundred thousand" children, holds near-monthly skirmishes with Poseidon, mourns a daughter named Eleni and threatens the Shinto diplomat Kuroyami (Ch5). That Zeus is in `E01_Zeus_Future.md`.
- **Not** the old-timeline Zeus of Ch1, whose ship *The Spear of Zeus* led the Great Abandonment and whose lightning bolt Lord-Commander Percy once returned to its "disgruntled owner". That Zeus is also covered in `E01_Zeus_Future.md`.
- **Knowledge boundary:** As of Ch22 he knows that a future Empire exists, that four million of its soldiers are on Mars, that their Watchpoint calls his nephew Percy "Lord Imperator", and that the verdict named Percy as Poseidon's son but said "nothing about you" (Hera, Ch22). He does **not** know Percy's full origin (the old timeline, Chaos), the Artemis bond made at the Aventine shrine (Ch18), or Minerva's full capabilities.

## Appearance
- Ch15: a "crisp business suit with a tie that cracked faintly with lightning". He leans forward to study Eleni, and "even he seemed to hesitate".
- Ch19: seated on his throne; slams his fist once to bring silence; steeples his fingers; rubs his temples in exasperation.
- Ch22: sits forward "with his elbows on his knees and the Master Bolt resting across them". The air around him tastes of copper, and threads of static crawl up the columns. During the battle he "stood on nothing, two miles above the eastern end of Long Island", inside a storm he had built.
- Ch8: owns a private airline in purple and gold, with a Pegasus-and-lightning-bolt livery.

## Personality
- **Authoritarian and transactional about heroes.** He sees demigods as assets. On Percy (Ch19) he invokes Hercules: "A mortal elevated through service. Bound to Olympus. Directed where needed." He then says Percy "would be useful. Properly assigned, of course."
- **Possessive and territorial.** He claims Eleni at once: "She is mine" (Ch15). During the Imperial landing he wants to strike "In the sky. Where I am king" (Ch22), and he waits three hours inside his storm to do it.
- **Escalation-prone.** His first answer to the retrieval force is to burn it from the sky. When Artemis warns that the next force will not be a retrieval force, he answers, "Then we burn that one too" (Ch22). He pauses only at "There are four million on Mars."
- **A tired, baffled father.** His daughter's open defiance confuses him. "You always say that when we talk nowadays," he says somberly (Ch19). He also tells her, "You are still my daughter, even when you make choices I do not understand." He gives in loudly: "Fine! But if this ends poorly—" (Ch19).
- **Prideful and jealous.** After the CODE 18 verdict he stands at the window gripping the Bolt. When Hera points out that the verdict "said nothing about you", his "knuckles went white" (Ch22).
- **Keeps his word in his own way.** His only promise is a threat: "If they strike my children, I strike them." He does not promise to hold back (Ch22). He does eventually turn his lightning on the *Princess Andromeda*, "at last, to aim his fury at the right target" (Ch22).
- **Teaches stoicism to his children.** Artemis recalls that gods are not supposed to tremble, because "Father emphasized it so" (Ch18).
- **Dismissive of soft options.** He calls Hermes naive for wanting to talk to the Imperials (Ch22).

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Formal and royal. He uses short declaratives and commands, and almost never contractions in council. He calls Artemis "my daughter" or "Daughter" and Poseidon "brother". He addresses Percy by full name ("Perseus Jackson") and calls him "boy" only through others. He uses no profanity on-page. He tends to restate a number or a word to make others explain it ("Thirty-one").

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **The Master Bolt:** "a spear of white-hot energy… humming with power" (Ch10). It was stolen and held by Ares, recovered by Percy (Ch10), and returned off-page. Zeus has it again in Ch22.
- **Storm and sky control:** In Ch22 he conjures a storm two miles above Long Island. One bolt goes through an Aquila lander's divine-composite carapace "as though it wasn't there". The Imperial engineers "had never imagined" they would have to shield against "the original". He destroys Landers Four, Nine, Seven and Two, and his lightning also takes Lander Eleven when it climbs back into the storm after Minerva's fake order. Minerva notes that his accuracy drops near the ground.
- **Divine territory:** Demons avoid Greek divine territory, "lest they invite the wrath of Zeus" (Ch3).
- **Assets:** a private airline (a brand-new luxury Airbus that has not yet been officially commissioned before "the Sky Lord on a tour Down Under", Ch8); white-gloved air-spirit stewards.

## Backstory
- Canon backstory applies: he overthrew Kronos, and he is bound by the oath of the Big Three. Zoë deduces that Poseidon broke it (Ch4).
- His Master Bolt went missing before the story. In this AU, Ares was "played into holding it" (Ch10), and Percy hints that Ares "wasn't the only one" involved (Ch11). Luke and Silena are implied.
- He fathered Hercules, his model of an obedient hero (Ch19).
- His conflict with Poseidon over the bolt is ongoing in Ch6 ("the current conflict between your father and mine").

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch3–4:** Exists as the background threat that keeps demons out of Greek territory.
- **Ch6:** Artemis threatens to tell "Father" about Percy unless the Lycaon pelt is offered. She also calls Percy "thief" over "Father's weapon".
- **Ch8:** "Requested (read commanded)" Athena to punish Dionysus for drinking, adding another decade to his sentence. "Zeus threw up our name" for red-team commander at Area Fulminus. Lends his new private jet ("Zeus rarely approves flights of this scale," Chiron).
- **Ch9–11:** The bolt arc. Minerva gives a deadline of "377 hours to the winter solstice" (a slip: the Vegas chapters are set in January; see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R6). Percy takes the bolt from Ares. Dionysus warns Percy that Zeus "would have my head" if Percy were turned into wine, and that the Smart Sister will deal with him "once you talk to Father about his treasure".
- **Ch11:** Artemis refuses to involve Zeus over the pelt, because he would smite Percy and her offering would be lost.
- **Ch13:** The Council (under him) replaces Chiron with Tantalus.
- **Ch15:** Presides over Eleni's judgment. He studies her, hesitates, denies siring her, then claims her and orders her to swear to Olympus. She answers, "I already have."
- **Ch18:** Artemis, after Percy's offering, becomes "stronger than Zeus now". Zeus does not know.
- **Ch19:** Council after Othrys. He holds up Hercules as the model for Percy ("A mortal elevated through service. Bound to Olympus. Directed where needed."). Artemis refuses ("He will not be made into another Hercules") and names Percy her Apostolos. Zeus fails to stop it and retreats with "Fine!"
- **Ch22:** Council over the Mars force. He argues to destroy it, then holds the sky and destroys landers. After the CODE 18 verdict he broods with Hera at the window. He finally strikes the *Princess Andromeda*. At the sunset council he relays the "Others'" ultimatum ("One day"), asks Marien whether Olympus controls her forces ("No"), and is again contradicted by Artemis ("Father… She's right"). The Anabasis Accord passes.

- Ch8 says Vegas is in January while the bolt deadline is the winter solstice (Ch9). Ruling: it is January; Minerva's "377 hours to the winter solstice" is a slip (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R6).
