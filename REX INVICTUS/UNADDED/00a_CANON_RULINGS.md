# Canon Rulings — how every profile resolves the story's inconsistencies

These rulings apply to every file in this folder.

**Precedence:**
1. The published text (Ch1–23) wins over everything else.
2. The author's vault notes come next. They settle anything the text leaves open, and they set name spellings and titles.
3. A vault-only fact (one not yet on the page) may be added to a profile only under the label **"Author's vault note (not yet in text)"**. It never overrides the text.
4. Where the text contradicts itself, the profiles use the ruling below, and the Continuity Notes section names the variant.

The Manuscript copies in the vault are word-for-word identical to the published chapters, so none of these contradictions has been revised yet.

## 4. Quote discipline

- Text in quotation marks must be **verbatim**.
- When joining two fragments across narration, join them with " … ".
- Keep author typos inside quotes, marked [sic].
- Paraphrases go **without** quotation marks.

## 5. Story-level slips with no profile-level fix (flagged for the author)

- **Dace** is killed by his own bullet, thrown back by Artemis, but later "hangs on the wall with the arrow through it" (Ch22).
- **Riptide:** Artemis keeps it in Ch16, yet Percy has it in Ch22.
- **The Labyrinth vision brute** carries a sledgehammer, but Minerva calls him "the man bearing the axe" (Ch21).
- **Wounded count:** Percy brings back "six" wounded in Ch21 but "5 people" in Ch22.
- **Melina's parentage:** Aphrodite in the text, Demeter in the vault ship list.
- **Treaty of Mount Rümker:** signed on Gaia's moon as a draw (text) vs signed at Tears of Lysandra as a UOE victory (vault).
- **Augustus** is called a "Second-Born" in the Princes' service, but the vault defines Second-Borns as Empire loyalists.
- **Varro in Ch22:** present-day Minerva already knows that Varro records the shear dead, which only happens later in the future.
- **Energy spike location:** Ch5 puts it in Athena's former palace, while Ch1 takes Percy from Hestia's courtyard.
- **Thalia at the Battle of Manhattan** is described differently at Ch23 l.678 and l.858.
- **Plot Threads §15.7** says Petra's boy was sent to the front; in the text it is her cousin's boy.
- **The age question (R1)** is the largest open item: decide whether Percy is twelve or sixteen.
