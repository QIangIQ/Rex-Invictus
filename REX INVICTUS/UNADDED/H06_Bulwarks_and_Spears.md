## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **The titles.** "Helion" means of the sun (gold) and "Selenide" means of the moon (silver). Each pair contains a Bulwark (guardian) and a Spear (attacker). In the present-day timeline (Ch 22), Eleni Theodosia invokes a temporary promotion to "**Selenide Bulwark** for Lord Phoebus Apollo" to authorise a CODE 4, which shows these are standard Imperial divine-guard ranks held under several gods, not unique to Aphrodite (see `G01_Eleni_Theodosia.md`; 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C25). The author's vault agrees: Helion/Selenide Bulwarks and Spears are the "primary defensive servants" of any UOE god, in paired ranks.
- **Stephanie Callidora and the Ch 2 "Callidora of the Eternal Glory".** Ch 2's space battle mentions a "Callidora of the Eternal Glory" once, a commander on Damocles's attacking side (`G12_Rex_Imperator_II_Cast.md`). The text states no link. The author's vault makes the Callidoras a family: **Elektra Callidora** (demigod of Aphrodite) is Stephanie's mother, and the vault assigns the *Sovereign's Glory* to Stephanie as early as the War of Princes. The Ch 2 "Callidora of the Eternal Glory" is unassigned in the vault (possibly Elektra or Stephanie). A UOE ship named *Sovereign's Glory* also appears in Ch 2's Fulguris list, its shields failing; given the vault assignment it is most likely Stephanie's ship, though the text does not say so. Ch 2 is set in the same far-future era as Ch 23 (the original timeline), not millennia earlier.
- **Michael Black** is not Michael Yew of the present-day Apollo cabin (`F10_Will_Solace_and_Michael_Yew.md`).
- All four were present when the verdict naming the Lord Imperator was read, so they are bound by the Director's seal.

## Appearance
- **Callidora:** "tall and golden-haired, and her eyes were as hard as a drill instructor's", golden eyes, gold armor. Her "golden face had gone white" at the verdict.
- **Black:** "dark and lean and quiet", in silver, with "a face so still it might have been carved".
- **Chrysos:** gold armor "scratched and scorched and dented", worn "open at the throat"; "broad and scarred across both cheeks, grinning at nothing, a bare sword resting against his shoulder." He crouches beside the throne.
- **Nox:** blackened silver, "the dull tarnished color of a knife left out in the rain"; "small and pale and very pretty". She sits cross-legged on the edge of the dais, spinning "a narrow curved dagger on the tip of one finger", humming.

## Personality
- **Callidora:** crisp, cold, efficient and soldierly. She calls Aphrodite "Mother", reports what Command believes, and is curious about what her goddess smells. In battle she is decisive and brutal: she crushes a Giant with a whole warship rather than firing a broadside. She is generous with praise for others' work.
- **Black:** reserved; "never spoke unless he had to". His first words are a quiet, sharp question: "Your Majesty … from whom?" He is a gentle, grave envoy, carrying Aphrodite's request that four million names be read aloud. He closes his eyes at the verdict. Practical: he posts Tullia to guard the goddess while he covers the front.
- **Chrysos:** a cheerful brute. He grins at nothing, and his "grin grew wider" at the order to erase monsters from existence.
- **Nox:** playful and predatory. She glances at passing servitors with "the look of a cat noticing a bird", so they find somewhere else to be. She "stopped spinning her dagger" at the erase order, a sign of eager attention. Unsettling.
- **Shared:** "The Spears looked at nobody but the throne." It is absolute devotion.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Callidora:** commands the *Sovereign's Glory*, "a Jackson-pattern dreadnought twenty kilometers long, built in gleaming gold with a sunburst on its prow", named for Percy Jackson. She flies it down on its keel engines and lowers it onto Porphyrion until he crumbles into golden dust: estimated reformation, eleven years.
- **Black:** Aphrodite's envoy, carrying her request to the Legio Administratoris. He tells Varro that Aphrodite "has placed a hundred of her own choristers at your disposal" to read in shifts.
- **Chrysos:** bare sword; a heavily scarred melee champion.
- **Nox:** curved dagger. Her menace suggests an assassin's role.

## Backstory
Black was a son of Aphrodite "given to the moon's service as a child", possibly meaning to Artemis or a lunar order; this is unexplained. Nothing is given for the others in the text.
- Author's vault note (not yet in text): **Stephanie Callidora** is the daughter of **Elektra Callidora**, a demigod of Aphrodite, and commanded the *UOE Sovereign's Glory* as Helion Bulwark of Aphrodite during the War of Princes. **Michael Black** is listed as Selenide Bulwark commanding the *UOE Flame of Ambition* in the same war. The vault's War of Princes ran 16212–16227, ending about four years before the Ch 23 shear.

## Story Arc So Far
**Ch 23:**
- **Rose Gallery:** all four wait by the empty throne. Callidora reports the monsters' fear, Black asks "from whom?" about the Chaos Aphrodite smells, and the Spears react with relish to the erase order.
- **Battle:** Callidora's *Sovereign's Glory* buries Porphyrion.
- **Records barge:** Black visits Lord Varro and requests the four million names be read aloud (about 92 days), which begins an hour later.
- **Verdict:** Callidora goes white; Black closes his eyes.
- **Sixtieth hour:** Black posts Tullia Ardens near Aphrodite at the wound and guards the front himself.
