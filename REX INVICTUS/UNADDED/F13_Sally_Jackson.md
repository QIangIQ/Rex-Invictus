## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
There is only one Sally in the story: the present-day one. The old-timeline Lord Commander's mother is never mentioned in the future chapters. Percy's future life (millennia on Olympus Primaris) implies his original Sally died long ago, but the text never says so.

She knows now that her son claims to be "Autokrætor Rex Perseus Jackson", a time-traveller from "twenty thousand standard years" ahead, carrying armor remnants and part of "the Répertoire du Savoir Athénien". She knows he plans to hunt Lycaon at Yosemite. She does not know about Minerva by that name, Artemis, the Empire or anything after that morning.

**Canon baseline (labelled canon, not story):** In the books Sally is a warm, witty writer who protects Percy by marrying the smelly Gabe, whose stench hides him from monsters. She later frees herself from Gabe (with Medusa's head) and marries Paul Blofis, an English teacher at Goode High.

## Appearance
"Piercing blue eyes" peek around the hallway corner (Percy's point of view, Ch 3). She wears an apron while making lunch.

## Personality
- **Loving and attentive.** She hums while making Percy's boxed lunch, "making extra sure that she was giving him the proper vegetables" (Ch 3). She calls him "Percy baby".
- **Harried.** She panics about the clock: under fifteen minutes to leave, and her shift in an hour.
- **Dignified about Gabe.** She walks past him "pointedly ignoring" him, passed out drunk on the floor.
- **Brave and quick.** When her son's eyes turn "strangely terrifying" and he throws a blue pulse through the walls, her first question is whether a divine being is present or her son is possessed.
- **Uses humour as a defence.** She laughs it off as a play rehearsal, until he mentions her "past relationship with the Eternal Lord of Atlantis".
- **Overwhelmed into numbness.** She "could only nod". She drives him to school and comes back to herself only after pulling away from the drop-off. Her first coherent thought is about Poseidon's reaction, not her own. "Even though she knew she should be panicking, she was still too shocked to feel anything."

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Warm and maternal, with mild, Greek-flavoured oaths she keeps in her head.
- "Percy baby, where are you? It's time for you to go to school." (Ch 3)
- "School starts soon and my shift starts in an hour." (Ch 3)
- "Am I in the presence of a Divine Being right now? Is my son currently possessed by someone?" (Ch 3)
- "How do you know all of this? Did someone teach you? Percy, please answer me!" (Ch 3)
- "Did you read all of this in a book? Are you practicing for a play I wasn't told about?" (Ch 3)
- Thought: "Oh Hades, Poseidon is going to be so mad." (Ch 3)

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
No powers. She is mortal but knows the gods are real, recognises Poseidon's epithet, and knows how a demigod's scent works ("something that I am sure you know about," Percy tells her). She has a car.

## Backstory
- She had a past relationship with Poseidon, "the Eternal Lord of Atlantis", and Percy is their son.
- She is married to Gabriel (surname from canon/vault: Ugliano; never printed in the story) and lives in a mouldy apartment full of beer cans and tobacco smell (Ch 3).
- She knows Percy's ordinary morning routine ("Coming mom, just gimme a second"), so the change is obvious to her at once.

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch 3:** Sally calls Percy for school and finds him staring at her with those eyes. He tells her "Come, we need to talk" and "Close the door please". He fires a barrier pulse that blinds Poseidon's and Iris's "observational instruments" and leaves the room smelling of rancid seaweed. Sitting on his homework-cluttered desk, she hears him reveal his titles, his time travel and his plan: leave after school, chase Lycaon's pack to Yosemite, "You cannot come for me, and you should not worry." She drives him to Yancy in shock. Percy then disappears from her life on page.
- **Ch 4:** As he passes out after transplanting Lycaon's heart into his own chest, Percy's last thought is "Mom is gonna kill me".
- **Ch 11:** During Artemis's memory wipe, Minerva rebuilds Percy's core identity. "His mother's face" is the second thing saved, right after his name.
- **Later:** Percy attends Goode High in Ch 15 and has an apparently normal school life in December. This suggests he went home at some point, but the text never shows a reunion or Sally's reaction.

## Relationships
- **Percy (the reincarnated son):** He loves her but treats her with cold, archaic formality ("Calm yourself, mother"). He leaves partly "for the protection of both you and the future". She still sees her son and is frightened for him. See `A02_Percy_Reincarnated_Rex.md`; for the boy he replaced, `A03_Percy_Canon_OriginalSelf.md`.
- **Poseidon:** her past love. She fears his anger at the boy's exposure. See `D02_Poseidon_Present.md`.
- **Gabriel (canon: Gabe Ugliano):** her husband, whom she tolerates in contemptuous silence. See `F14_Gabe_Ugliano.md`.

## Continuity Notes
- Eye colour: Sally's eyes are blue ("piercing blue eyes", Ch 3, from Percy's point of view). Percy's are green ("piercing green eyes of her son", Ch 3, from Sally's point of view). This is not a slip: the blue eyes are Sally's and the green are Percy's.
- Ch 7: Percy says "I guess it started at Goode High", although the Ch 3 events happen at Yancy Academy. By Ch 15 he really is at Goode.
- No time skip with Sally is shown, so her knowledge state stays as of Ch 3.
- **Percy's age.** The card avoids a number. The text calls Percy sixteen (Ch7, Ch13, Ch22), though the premise blurb says he returned as a twelve-year-old (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R1, flagged for the author).
- **Year.** The Ch 3 readout's "2005" is the only printed year; the author's timeline and in-text technology put the present at about 2021 (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R2).
- Author's vault note (canon baseline, not yet in text): Sally later marries Paul Blofis and has a daughter, Estelle. Neither appears in the story.
