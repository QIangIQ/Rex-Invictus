## Personality
The Ch 9 dialogue is mostly unattributed, so it is often unclear who says which line. The text supports the following.
- **Mitch:**
  - Knows and believes the Percy myths. He tells his friend about them ("Didn't yeez mum told you about the myths of Percy Jackson?").
  - Later he is spooked by the vat: "it's getting scary watching the freak, Victor."
  - The line about not looking "through the eyes of a witch" and the reluctance to be "stared at by them higher-ups" probably belong to him, but the attribution is ambiguous.
  - Afraid when the body wakes: "You—You're not supposed to be awake."
- **Victor:**
  - Was "born into the Legio… never had a mum", so he never heard the myths. Yet it is apparently Victor who first notices the vat body looks like "dat one dude on them murals back home on Callistus".
  - Skeptical of Mitch's nerves ("the shitty Legio ale is getting to ya").
  - Practical: he calls an aedificator for his "friend" (payment through Mitch) and escalates at once to high command.
  - Reaches for his sidearm when the clone speaks.

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch 9:** On vat duty, one guard (by alternation, Victor) notices the body's resemblance to the Callistus murals. Mitch explains the legend: "a living legend", "alone on holy Gaia… making sure Oylmpus Primaris don't fall". They decide not to tell the higher-ups. Later Mitch is spooked, and Victor calls an Apollonian aedificator to "Deck 23, Section 8. Wire payments through one Mitch Fulgur." The body wakes with neon-blue eyes, not the myths' sea-green, and Victor calls for high command.
- **Ch 10:** Minerva boots up: "Explain your designation, function, and purpose within this facility." Mitch stammers; the fear neurotransmitters of one guard spike 31.2%. Victor reaches for his gun; "You are irrelevant." He hesitates. Minerva: "Get me a bed. I'm tired." Lord Varro takes over later (`G11_Lord_Varro.md`).

## Continuity Notes
- In Ch 9 the soldiers believe Percy is still alive guarding Olympus Primaris on Gaia. That matches the old-timeline Percy (`A01_Percy_LordCommander_OldTimeline.md`), whereas other future-era chapters treat Percy as long absent.
- Dialect typos are preserved: "Oylmpus", "woudlsn't".
- Ch 10 says Minerva's frightened target is "the one who had called for an aedificator" and contrasts him with "Victor, the other soldier", implying Mitch made the call. Ch 9 reads as Victor making the call with payment through Mitch. This is a minor inconsistency.
- By strict alternation, the guard who does not know the hero's name ("Wuzzat guy called again?") is the same one who refers to the murals "back home on Callistus", i.e. Victor. So both may be from Callistus, even though Victor says he was born into the Legio.
- **Eyes:** the woken body's eyes are "neon-blue" (Minerva in control), not "the sea-green eyes that the myths had talked about" (Percy's natural colour). See 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C1.
- **Callistus:** author's vault note (not yet in text): a fortress-planet under UOE control and recruiting grounds for Legio XX Valeria Vitrix (spelling per 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md S9).
