## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **This file:** the 21st-century Apollo. He drives a flaming Lamborghini, plays matchmaker for his twin, and only discovers Percy's internal cybernetics in Ch19.
- **Not** the far-future Apollo of the Undying Olympian Empire. That Apollo's artisans painted the Perseus mural on the *Divine Crusader* (Ch2), he supplies Artemis's blindfold (Ch5), his "Sun-Born" legions died in the War of Princes and he once owned *The Eye of Light* (Ch15), and the Imperial suppression-lock's medical tolerances are designed by him ("Your future self designed its medical tolerances", Ch22). See `E04_Apollo_Future.md`.
- **Knowledge boundary:** Knows Percy's Curse of the Styx (at about half strength), sub-dermal cybernetics, nanomachines, Minerva (by report), the Mars force, CODE 18 and the Accord. He seems to sense more than he says ("He knows?", Ch16). He does not know Percy's old timeline or Chaos.

## Appearance
- "Tall, bright-eyed, radiant, and as annoyingly shiny as Percy last witnessed" (Ch16). Teeth that flash sunlight.
- Wears "enough gold to bankrupt several city-states" and arrives "in a blaze of light" (Ch20). Sunglasses that slide down his nose (Ch21). Uses a stylus and tablet at "impossible speed" (Ch19).
- His chariot is "a Lamborghini of white-hot flame" that turns into a bus (Ch16).
- In grief-wrath: every lamp in the Big House blows out and comes back so bright that the bulbs explode. "His face was the face of a god" (Ch22).

## Personality
- **Theatrical, flirtatious, crude.** He kidnaps Percy for "ancient Greek traditions of manly bonding": hot baths, maids, oils and philosophy (Ch16). He trades sword innuendo with Percy in front of the Hunters.
- **Sibling tease.** "Arty… you can just stand there," and, asked why, "Because it's you" (Ch19). He calls Percy "your little sea-prince" (Ch20) and Artemis "Sis", "Arty" and "Art".
- **Perceptive under the noise.** He immediately sees that Percy looks "through Tartarus twice" (Ch16) and notices Artemis softening (Ch20). He jokes, "a miracle I didn't notice last time we talked" about the cybernetics (Ch19).
- **Serious, competent physician.** In the exam his tone loses its edge. He rates the Achilles curse with a stabbing-based pain scale and offers "the good answer or the honest one". He complains about the Imperials' "completely new form of medicae" (Ch22).
- **Protective of family.** He calls Percy family ("Cuz"), fears what Artemis and Poseidon will do if Percy is hurt, and grounds him "until I say otherwise" (Ch21).
- **Plague god underneath.** When Michael Yew dies: "Who." Percy sees him "thinking about how easy it would be" to kill the bound prisoners. He restrains himself after Percy's plea, but then simply looks away while Merek tears his chair free (Ch22).
- **Capable of grace in grief.** At the pyre he accepts Oriel's honest "I don't know" with "That's the right answer. I hate it" (Ch22).
- **Pop-culture goofball.** Loves isekai anime ("hit by a truck"). Does a Trump-parody speech about his "tremendous" healer kids (Ch21).

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Fast, bright and sarcastic. He mixes royal decrees ("this is my godly decree") with modern slang ("Brotha", "Buddy", "Cuz", "Perce"). He uses mild profanity ("Fuuuuuck", "regenerate my balls"). He switches abruptly to clipped clinical sentences when healing, and to single heavy words when enraged. He mutters in Ancient Greek.

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Healing:** golden diagnostic scan-light that reveals sub-epidermal circuitry and nanomachines (Ch19). He repaired Straban's lungs and Neumond's spine and healed the QVARTUS wound through Percy's hand "nearly invisible" (Ch22). He commands his Healer children (Ch21).
- **Light and sun:** blinding arrivals ("BANG"); golden sparks that zap; can warm a room and melt frost (Ch22). His sight cannot reach Mars, because Ares, Phobos and Deimos keep it hidden (Ch22).
- **Plague-wrath:** his divine presence can terrify bound soldiers (Ch22).
- **Transport:** a sun chariot that becomes a bus or a Lamborghini.
- **Imperial authority (borrowed):** he co-signs Eleni's CODE 4 hologram and becomes her provisional "Selenide Bulwark" lord (Ch22).
- **Bow:** "a bow he hadn't touched in months" (Ch22).

## Backstory
- Twin of Artemis. Father of the Apollo cabin (Will Solace, Michael Yew) and of Atlanta among the Hunters (the Hunt's apothecary; she is the "daughter of Apollo" with the medipack in Ch4, per 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C30).
- His court is full of beautiful companions, which makes Artemis jealous (Ch16).
- "Last time we talked… you were drunk" (Ch19). This implies he met Percy before the exam, probably at his palace in Ch16, though that is not stated.

- **Ch15:** At Eleni's judgment: "No lies. She doesn't blink… You're sure she's not part automaton?"
- **Ch16:** Collects the Hunters and the di Angelos in his chariot. Kidnaps Percy for a bath and sparring at his palace. His "promiscuous" maids anger Artemis.

- **Ch20:** Steals fruit at breakfast and teases about the "exquisitely awkward arrangement". Notices Artemis easing.

- **Ch22:** Guards and heals the five Ekdromoi. Co-signs the CODE 4. Senses that Neumond has accidentally prayed to Artemis. Fails to see Mars. Is assigned "the prisoners and the infirmary" at council. Michael Yew dies, and his wrath follows. Lets Merek go. Saves Neumond after the handshake. On the beach Huitzilopochtli calls him "Little sun". When Hrafn says, "That … is not thine Apollo's sun", Apollo concedes that it "isn't" (the line is Hrafn's, not Apollo's; 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C10). Sits with Michael's gold shroud, then with Oriel at the pyre.

## Continuity Notes
- Ch22: Apollo says the lost legion is "sitting on my planet". Mars is Ares's planet, so this may be a slip or a sarcastic remark.
- Michael Yew is called "the boy with the bow" and leads the Apollo cabin on the porch. Will Solace was the Apollo counselor in Ch7. Both are anachronistic placements relative to canon.
- His healer children are "Healers" and medics. Imperial "Apollonian medics" are their future counterparts.
- Wounded count: Percy brings back "six" wounded in Ch21 but "5 people" in Ch22. This is an open story-level slip (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md §5).
- Spelling: the Hunter is **Atlanta** (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md S2); "Atalanta" is only the ancient heroine and her Ch23 statue.
- "Arty" is used by Percy, by Apollo (Ch19) and by the future Poseidon (Ch15); it is not Percy's alone (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C11).
- Present-day year: c. 2021 per the author's timeline, nominally the canon PJO era (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R2).
