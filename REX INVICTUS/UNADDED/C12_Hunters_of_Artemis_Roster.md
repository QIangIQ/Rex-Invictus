# The Hunters of Artemis — Collective & Roster (All Eras)
> A silver-clad sisterhood of immortal archers. In the present day they track werewolves through Yosemite. Fourteen thousand years later, as the Hunt of the Director, they hold fortress-moons and bombard traitor worlds, and still hunt one scent of salt and sea wind that no Hound has ever found.

## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
Keep the eras apart:
- **Present Hunt** (Zoë's command): a forest war-band with tents, wolves and chores.
- **Future "Hunt of the Director"**: an imperial military and intelligence arm under the Director of Anti-Espionage and Celestial Surveillance, with fortress-moons, orbital platforms, units, ranks, Hounds bred on Luna and Thalia as Lieutenant.
- **Far future**: only Octavia, an ex-Hunter.
- **Old timeline**: memories only (the huntress who shot off Percy's pinkie, and the "Eternal Hunter Princess").

Individual profiles: `C04_Zoe_Nightshade_Present.md`, `C05_Zoe_Nightshade_Future_Legend.md`, `C07_Thalia_Grace_Future_Lieutenant.md`, `C08_Bianca_di_Angelo_Present.md`, `C10_Phoebe_Hunter.md`, `C11_Atlanta_Hunter.md`.

## Appearance
- **Present:** "figures clad in silver… their bows at the ready"; silver cloaks; "silent as wolves" (Ch6). In winter, silver parkas (Ch22).
- **Future:**
  - Silver-grey gear with the Director's crescent-moon seal on the pauldrons (Ch23).
  - A "flame hunter" with a silver rebreather (Ch12).
  - Silver-green remembrance bands for the Percy–Artemis "Silent Pact" (Eleni's cult lore, Ch15).

## Personality
- **Present:**
  - Disciplined and wary of males ("They do not make a habit of rescuing males", Ch6).
  - They trade chores for rations, and laundry duty is a punishment (Ch4).
  - They groan at Apollo's and Percy's jokes and fall into disarray when Artemis vanishes (Ch16).
  - Curious about Percy as Apostolos. Artemis compares them to "a flock of curious ravens" (Ch19).
  - Faintly distrustful of the "boys and men making fools of themselves" at camp (Ch20).
- **Future:**
  - Professional and dutiful, but still a sisterhood.
  - They tease and indulge each other (Celyn: "I told them good kind").
  - Fiercely protective of the Director.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Varied. Zoë is archaic. Most Hunters speak plain modern English. The future Hunters use military clipped speech ("Confirmed. Direct transit signature."). A sample of lines:
- "We've got a fire, a pretty significant one I think." (Naomi, Ch4)
- "I've got a lighter if you need light" (Celyn, Ch4)
- "Does he always talk like that?" (an unnamed young Hunter, Ch20)
- "Confirmed. Direct transit signature. No decoys. No proxies. It's actually... her." (Kaia Telan, Ch13)
- "Lieutenant. What do we tell the Director?" (Celyn, Ch23)
- "Someone is hunting us. Ave Imperator, Lady Artemis save our souls." (a sergeant at Luna-Secundus, Ch12)

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Present:**
  - Immortality and Artemis's blessing.
  - Lethal archery, which brings down the Westover mercenary helicopter (Ch16).
  - Nectar and ambrosia in medipacks.
  - The Hunt's central white tent holds an atrium with a war table and a map of America. Its teleportation array was built by Hecate and links to Artemis's palace on Olympus, running on a battery Athena hooked to the Moon Chariot (Ch4).
  - The Hunt is so "saturated" in Artemis's blessing that Imperial Geistjäger counters, thermal and ELINT cannot see its members (Ch22).
  - Wolves accompany the camp (Ch6), and Artemis threatens to feed Percy "to the Hounds" (Ch22).
- **Future:**
  - Ritual-spliced gunlines, divinely reinforced bunkers and anti-Titan javelins (Ch12).
  - Orbital bombardment: the Hunters glassed the Traitor moon S3-R12-P1-M2 (Ch18).
  - Surveillance of "thousands of trillions" (Ch13).
  - **Hounds of Artemis**, bred in the Luna kennel-crypt and trained as pups on a scrap of blue cloth that smells of "salt and sea wind" (Ch23).
- **Psiloi link (Ch22):** Artemis says the future Imperial orbital marksmen are "my lines… My blessings". The Psiloi are Apollonian or Artemisian legacies. Their Volley-Lead Cerelli swears to the Director: "My eye to your aim. My hand to your will. My life to your hunt."

## Backstory
- In the old timeline, a "furious huntress of Artemis" accidentally shot off Percy's right pinkie while he scouted a monster camp, and he never told anyone (Ch3). "The Eternal Hunter Princess" taught him ancient dialects (Ch1).
- In the future, the Hunt evolved into the Director's service, and Artemis says: "I have not indulged in the simple pleasure of chasing prey in millennia" (Ch13).
- **Author's vault note (not yet in text):** the vault's Hunt roster lists the active Hunters as Atlanta (daughter of Apollo, Apothecary), Celyn (daughter of Demeter), Hemithea (daughter of Hecate), Josephine (daughter of Hephaestus), Naomi (legacy of the Japanese pantheon, granddaughter of Raijin; the *future* Naomi), Phoebe (daughter of Ares) and Thalia Grace. This matches the future Hunt of Ch23.
- Every Hunter with more than a century of service has dragged people out of the Lotus Pleasure-System at least once (Ch23).

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch4:** A patrol under Zoë (Naomi, Phoebe, Celyn, Atlanta and others, "about a dozen silver archers") finds Percy and Lycaon's pelt.
- **Ch6:** The Hunt camp. Artemis interrogates Percy.
- **Ch11:** The Hunt exterminates a huge monster horde in the Appalachians. Artemis leans on her Hunters while her offerings are missing.
- **Ch15–16:**
  - Westover Hall: the Hunt shoots down the helicopter.
  - Bianca is recruited. Artemis disappears.
  - Zoë picks a team, the Hunters seize Percy at their cabin, and only Zoë and Bianca go on the quest.
- **Ch19:** "Nearly forty" Hunters meet Percy as Artemis's Apostolos. Some recognise him, the young ones are curious, and one hands him breakfast.
- **Ch20:** The Hunt breakfasts at Camp Half-Blood and leaves by midday.
- **Ch22:**
  - The Hunt returns at dusk. Artemis warns them that the Psiloi wear her blessings.
  - In the woods they ambush the Sciritae scouts and kill 11.
  - Ismene dies in the infirmary, two others die in the woods, and Phoebe loses two fingers. The casualty tally lists the Hunters at 3 dead and 4 wounded.
- **Ch12–13 (future):**
  - The Hunters hold Luna-Secundus for 17 months until the Arenbergs break it.
  - Artemis assembles every senior Huntress on Luna, and the Hunters witness Him Within the Moon.
- **Ch23 (future):** Every Hunter is ordered to find his scent, signature or key. Thalia's contingent chases Minerva across New Rome.

Columbina and Scaramucius are the two on the page.
