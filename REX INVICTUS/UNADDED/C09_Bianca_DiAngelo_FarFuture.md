## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **NOT** present-day Bianca (`C08_Bianca_di_Angelo_Present.md`), the novice Huntress of Ch15–19 who struggles with her bow.
- **Not obviously** the original-timeline Bianca, who in canon died on the Titan's Curse quest. How a Bianca exists tens of millennia later is unexplained. She could be an immortal survivor, a resurrection, a reincarnation, someone who took the name, or an entity using the identity.
- **Era note:** her scene is set "thirty thousand years after the rise" of the UOE, which is later than the Interlude (Ch23), dated about 14,000 years after the present-day story. Treat her as the furthest-future figure in the story so far.
- She reacts to the phrase "…Olympian Empire, you say." with recognition or interest, and then her power flares. The text does not say why.

## Appearance
- She "looked like a woman". No other physical detail is given before her power manifests.
- When provoked: "The woman's hair lifted as if caught in an invisible storm. Pale blue fire ignited in her eyes."
- Her aura is overwhelming: "The air itself trembled with the pressure of something that felt less like a person and more like a natural disaster given human form." Even a former Hunter of Artemis feels her knees weaken.

## Personality
- **Haughty and disdainful.** She calls UOE soldiers "third-rate fools" and "lowlifes" who rely on "bizarre contraptions" and "haven't even mastered the basics of your own power".
- **Proud and archaic.** She refers to herself as "my esteemed self".
- **Calm and amused.** She speaks "calmly", with "a faint trace of amusement", and "smiled slightly".
- **Cryptic.** "What use is there in telling you?" She gives her name anyway, almost as a flourish.
- **Perhaps provoked by the Empire's name.** Her calm turns to an eruption of power exactly when "Olympian Empire" is spoken.
- Her register, contempt for "contraptions" and jibe at those who "haven't even mastered the basics of your own power" echo the arrogant-master archetype of cultivation (xianxia) fiction. The Jade Court cultivators elsewhere in the story suggest this is deliberate, but the text does not confirm it.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
She speaks in elevated, formal, slightly archaic and condescending language, in long contemptuous sentences followed by short cool ones. She uses no contractions and no profanity.
- "This is truly lamentable," (Ch20)
- "To think that third-rate fools with no understanding of the divine dare to discuss power while relying on bizarre contraptions." (Ch20)
- "How dare you lowlifes," (Ch20)
- "…attempt to lay hands on my esteemed self?" (Ch20)
- "…Olympian Empire, you say." (Ch20)
- "What use is there in telling you?" (Ch20)
- "I am Bianca Di'Angelo." (Ch20)

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Single-handedly defeated "dozens"** of UOE soldiers, including Roman legacies, apparently without weapons. "They had been defeated. By one person."
- **Overwhelming aura and pressure.** A "shockwave of power exploded outward". The pressure is felt physically by onlookers, including a former immortal Hunter.
- **Pale blue fire in her eyes** and a storm-like lifting of her hair.
- She survives in a radiation wasteland, possibly underground. Asimov's human-life scan found a "human-sized response" underground with "extremely weak" lifeforce, in a man-made structure with a surface entrance and lower radiation. Whether that signal was her is not confirmed.
- No equipment is mentioned.

## Continuity Notes
- Her surname is spelled "Di'Angelo" here and "di Angelo" for present-day Bianca.
- Chapter 20 dates the scene "thirty thousand years after the rise of the Undying Olympian Empire" and "over thirty millennia since we ventured into space". Ch23 sets the UOE-era scenes about 14,000 years after the present day. Per 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R7 these are **different eras**: Ch20 is about year 33,000, a separate and later era than the UOE scenes (Ch1, 5, 10–15, 23). Not a contradiction.
- **Vault "deceased" status** (C21) applies to present-day Bianca's canon fate and is not carried over; her link to this figure remains unrevealed on the page.
- The life-scan found weak lifeforce underground, yet she is encountered above ground among the soldiers, fully powerful.
- The Roman legacy calls her "A mortal" and "young hero", either sarcasm or misjudgement.
