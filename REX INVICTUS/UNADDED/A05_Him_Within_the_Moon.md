> A fragment of Percy Jackson grown in the hollow heart of the Moon from Artemis's power and his lost sword. It was worshipped by heretics, it refused them to shield the goddess it loves, and it then crossed fourteen thousand years to answer the call of the boy it calls "My Original".

## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
This file covers **the lunar shard and the sentient fragment inside Riptide/Anaklusmos**, treated as one continuous entity because the text strongly links them. The link is never formally confirmed (see Secrets).

- **vs. `A01_Percy_LordCommander_OldTimeline.md`:** A01 is the real, original Perseus, a whole person who chose Chaos's gamble. The shard is *not* him. Future Artemis: "He is not Percy Jackson," … "Not anymore. Not ever." Poseidon: "It isn't even the real him." It is "what Perseus becomes": an echo built from A01's lost sword and Artemis's love and power.
- **vs. `A02_Percy_Reincarnated_Rex.md`:** Rex Percy is the "Original" the shard is called toward. When the shard enters Riptide, Rex Percy feels "an old part of him returning" (Ch14), but he does not become the shard. The sword fights his grip.
- **vs. `A06_Percy_The_Legend_Lord_Imperator.md`:** the cult titles (Rex Deus, Moonbound, Everfather) belong to the Hollow Crescent's heresy about *this* entity. The mainstream Imperial myth of the Lord Imperator, Tidefather and "The Two" is a separate thing. The shard openly rejects the cult's version of him.
- **vs. `B01`/`B02` Minerva:** Minerva also claims "I am him. Just not… exclusively." (Ch12). She is Percy's armor AI wearing his clone body, and she calls the shard a separate entity to be tracked. The female voice in Riptide in Ch14 is "Like hers, but not her".
- **vs. Riptide the ordinary weapon:** Riptide/Anaklusmos is Zoë's sword from myth (author's vault: original owner Zoë Nightshade, then Hercules, then Chiron, then Percy). It was Rex Percy's pen-sword in Ch3–Ch14, and it is in his hand again in Ch22. Its sentience appears only from Ch14 to Ch18.

## Appearance
- **Dormant, in Luna's core (Ch13):** a "perfect sphere" chamber hollowed from the Moon, "barely a few ships wide". In the center he sits "outlined faintly in refracted silver, like a shape reflected in the far corners of her eyes". He has "no clear features", is "still", and is "only waiting". When he looks up, Thalia's "thoughts falter, her rage falter".
- **Visibility:** "Nobody else could see him except for me" (Artemis); Thalia can see him too. During the cult summoning the manifested giant is visible to everyone. Zealots stop mid-charge, and Hunters drop to one knee.
- **Watching the battle:** Thalia sees him "sitting cross-legged on the barrel of an orbital cannon, Almost as big as the defective Talos". He is "The blurred reflection… older. Tired. But aware. Listening."
- **Manifested colossus:** "Vast". "His face was familiar, but flickered like a glitched memory file. His form seemed to be holding on, if just barely. Divine energy… drifted from his shoulders like steam." His hand is "massive, translucent, and radiating broken blue-gold light".
- **After the battle:** he shrinks "down to something that cast only a slightly longer shadow than her own". For a moment "he looked just like the boy she remembered", the one "lost outside of Westover Hall, facing the long-gone Dr. Thorn". Two of Artemis's fingers glow faintly against "the shimmer" of his chest. As he is pulled away, "The blue-glow of his limbs began to fray at the edges". He flickers "Like static running through a memory".
- **As the sword (Ch14):**
  - Percy sees "the blade of Riptide in its full, uncapped form. A coiled mass of metal and scripture", the "old riptide, which he lost a few millennia ago".
  - In the hold, "The ghost of Zoë bloomed over it like a golden corona. Like a halo around a biblical archangel." The blade "merge[s] with itself", then collapses into a pen that is "Way heavier… Sharper too".
  - It "no longer hummed like a demigod's relic. It sang like a god's."
- **As the sword (Ch15–16):** in Ch15 Percy's "trusty blade" feels "very.. off". In Ch16 the bronze is "dim, its glow nearly extinguished" after Percy's surge. It snaps upright and flies to Artemis, and "The snow hissed where the bronze blade touched it."

## Personality
- **Protective above all else.** Summoned as a cult's god, he turns "toward Artemis… Slowly. Protectively" and shields her from every barrage (Ch13). "I know that I would shield you again. Even if I disappear for it."
- **Devoted to Artemis.** To her "Then you still—" he answers one word: "Always." He came because "You were so… alone."
- **Uncertain of his own nature, and humble about it.** "I don't know what I am." He gave her the same answer she gave him: "I didn't either" think he would come.
- **Quiet and sparing with words.** In battle "Percy's form did not answer" the cult. He "only stood". His face is "unreadable".
- **Rejects worship.** He crushes a cult cruiser, raises a wall against their fleet, and leaves the zealots screaming "He denies us! WHY!?"
- **Sad, gentle and dutiful.** Leaving Artemis, he smiles "soft and sad". "I'm not sure I do either." … "But I think he needs me." He goes to the Original out of duty, against his own wish.
- **As the sword, colder, plural and oracular.** It speaks as "We", "a thousand whispers braided into one", in verdicts: "This divergence is necessary… soon all will bow, or break." It manages the timeline: it tells Artemis she must be captured by Atlas, and (as Artemis relays it to Percy) that Percy has "not done too much to change the timeline yourself" (Ch16). It is willful. It tries "to wrestle itself from his grasp", resists going back in his pocket "seething like a coiled snake", and obeys only "For now" (Ch14). It chose Artemis's hand over Percy's (Ch16).
- **Contradiction:** on Luna he is tender and personal. In the sword he is impersonal and fate-bound. The sword admits it is "not whole". This may be the difference between the shard *with* Artemis and the shard *cut off* from her, or the influence of the second voice (see Secrets).

## Voice & Speech Patterns
- **As the Moon shard:** short, plain, quiet sentences. His voice is "lighter now. Worn, but more closely familiar". He uses no titles and no archaic forms, the way a tired young man talks. He trails off with ellipses. He calls the reincarnated boy "My Original".
- **As the sword:** heard "across every thread of perception. In her mind, in Percy's bones, in the cracks of reality itself". Plural "We". Liturgical, prophetic, fragmentary phrasing ("vessel, memory, splinter"). He addresses Artemis as "Moon-Goddess".
- **Profanity:** none.

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
**Luna (Ch13):**
- Waking makes the Moon shudder ("not from fire, but from the divine waking").
- Catches a concentrated lance of dark energy in one hand "like a child's toy". The shockwave rips up stone and wards, but "not a scratch touched Artemis".
- Shatters a heavy beam, the "Light scattering into salt".
- Raises "a wall of celestial water, massive, shimmering with constellations" behind the Moon, between Luna and the fleet. Volleys "simply... stopped. Like arrows against the tide."
- Crushes a cult cruiser "under divine gravity… as if held in a child's hand".
- Changes size from colossus to roughly human height.
- Invisible to almost everyone. Senses being called. Travels through time on Artemis's moonlight thread.
- Can apparently sense or "hear" across time ("As if hearing something through the void").

**As Riptide/Anaklusmos (Ch14–18):**
- Transforms itself and the blade "merge[s] with itself" (text: "merge with itself"). It burns white-hot.
- Its pull is strong enough to make the Golden Fleece "recognize" it and accept Percy ("I think it recognizes the sword," … "Or what's inside it."). "The light seemed to try to flow into the weapon", and Percy "felt something crack inside the sword" (Ch14).
- Shares or drains power: it was "drained by the outburst" of Percy's primal sea surge (Ch16).
- Moves on its own: it snaps upright, extends its blade, and "landed in Artemis's outstretched palm" (Ch16).
- Telepathic speech to Artemis and Percy at once.
- Shows Artemis visions of the future: warships, Hunters fighting on the lunar surface, Artemis raising her bow "against fire that split the stars", and "a reflection of him, colossal and weary, shielding her against the end". That last vision is the Ch13 battle.
- It "regaled her in the tales of what it had experienced in the millennia of its life" (Ch16), and it "showed her what must come" (Ch18).
- Eleni "evidently felt some sort of connection to the sword" (Ch16).

## Backstory
What the text actually establishes, with the gaps marked.
- **The old Riptide was lost.** In his original life, Percy lost "his old riptide… a few millennia ago" (Ch14). How, where, and whether Artemis came to hold it are never stated. Artemis has a long history with the sword: "Anaklusmos had always been more than bronze, Zoe had told her that long ago" (Ch16).
- **Formation.** Future Artemis: "He seemed to have formed from my energy alone with Anaklusmos." So he formed from her divine energy together with the lost sword, not from a soul. Poseidon calls him "fragmented" (Ch13). Whether Artemis did this on purpose is left open. The text implies she did not.
  - *Author's vault note (not yet in text):* the shard formed inadvertently at the Caligo Vale oathbinding (a rite invoked by Hera on Olympus Tertiae, early in the Great Expansion). After Percy was mind-wiped and sent back to Gaia, Artemis's repressed affection and soul-fragments Percy projected "into dreams and silence" coalesced into a shard at a time when Artemis's domain fully overlapped Earth's moon "for the first time in three millennia". The vault calls it "not Percy Jackson, nor is it wholly Artemis… an unintended union of unspoken words and unrealized emotion". Scholars of the Delphic House of Apollo call it "a relic of unsanctioned love, an accidental demi-deity", and the Olympians ban its worship as the "heresy of fragmentation". The vault also says it "has unknowingly taken over the facility's power matrix and communications relay". None of this is on the page yet.
- **Luna.** The text says "Luna" and "the Moon" (Ch13); the author's notes call the shard's moon "Luna Primaris" (vault), as distinct from Luna-Secundus, the fortress the Hollow Crescent besieged (Ch12). He rests in a sanctum at the Moon's gravitational core, beneath a Hunter fortress-moon. Above it Artemis, as Director, runs "a surveillance network that ensures the safety of thousands of trillions" (Ch13).
- **The cult.** The Order of the Hollow Crescent, led by "the Twins" (very probably Orran and Virre Arenberg per the author's vault; see 00a C23), has hunted for "something in Luna" for years. They besieged Luna-Secundus for 17 months, until the Arenbergs broke it (Ch12). They believe "We are all of us but echoes of his shadow" and that "He was Our Lord before you even cared" (Ch13). They captured the battleship *The Eye of Light* 32 years before Minerva reappeared (Ch15).
- **Known to the powerful.** Minerva has "indexed three hundred thousand references, cross-referenced with suppressed Oracle logs" and is preparing "a data declassification on a certain lunar entity". Poseidon knows of "that creature in Luna" and denies it is his son (Ch12).
- **Love.** Future Artemis's marriage to Perseus at Caligo Vale was political, but "deep down, I had already given him a part of myself long before then". She calls the shard "my wound… If you could see him when we were bound, you would have felt it" (Ch13).

## Secrets, Mysteries & Open Threads
**Known (stated in the text):**
- He formed from future Artemis's energy together with Anaklusmos (Ch13).
- He is not the real or whole Percy (Artemis, Poseidon, and the sword itself).
- He calls reincarnated Percy "My Original" and left Luna for "the past" on a moonlight thread aimed at "a world of cabins, of campfires" (Ch13).
- In the present, Riptide merged with a future version of itself (Ch14) and later spoke as "a fragment… a sliver cast from the Jackson" that is "what Perseus becomes" (Ch16).

**Strongly implied but never confirmed:**
- **That the Luna shard and the sword's voice are the same entity.** Supporting evidence:
  - Artemis's farewell names him "Anaklusmos" and tells him to "let the Riptide remind him".
  - The Ch14 merge happens in the same story-window as his Ch13 departure.
  - In Ch16 the sword shows Artemis "a reflection of him, colossal and weary, shielding her", which is the Ch13 battle.
  - The sword uses "the Him that was and will be", echoing the cult's "Him".
- **That the shard is a splinter of the *future/old* Percy (A01) rather than of Rex Percy.** He says he is "cast from the Jackson", carries the old Riptide "lost a few millennia ago", and remembers being bound to Artemis.

**Ambiguous or open:**
- **Is he a soul-shard of Percy, or a construct?** (The author's vault leans to a composite: neither wholly Percy nor wholly Artemis; see Backstory.) "Formed from my energy" points to a divine construct. The sword is "vessel, memory, splinter", which suggests an imprint or memory, not a soul. "A thousand whispers braided into one" and its plural "We" suggest a composite: perhaps sword plus Artemis plus Percy-memory, or more.
- **The two voices in Ch14.** The male echo "Your hands are mine…" is presumably the shard. The female voice, "Calmer… Regretful… Like hers, but not her", is unidentified. Candidates include Zoë's ghost (who appears at that moment), a facet of Artemis's energy within the shard, or another Minerva-like construct.
- **What "cracked" inside the sword** when the Fleece's power flowed into it (Ch14).
- **Whether he still exists in Riptide by Ch22,** and how Riptide got back from Artemis to Percy.
- **Minerva's "when the shard wakes"** and her wish to be "on the inside of the breach". What breach?
- **Who Poseidon means** by "He's disappeared… The Avatar, Arty's obsession" (Ch15).
- **"This divergence is necessary… soon all will bow, or break."** What the sword is steering toward is unknown. It may be the bond between Rex Percy and present-day Artemis that forms in Ch18 and Ch19.
- **Why the cult wanted him, and what "Rex Deus" would have done** for them.
- **The connection between "Core Commandment 14"** ("The Guardian of the Past, Perseus Jackson… must not be Disturbed") and the sealed lunar sanctum is unclear.

## Continuity Notes
- **The sword's history:** Ch14 calls the old weapon "His old riptide, which he lost a few millennia ago", but the Ch1 old-timeline Percy still wields "his legendary xiphos". That xiphos is presumably a different blade, or the text is inconsistent.
- **Where Riptide is:** Artemis takes the sword in Ch16, and Percy explicitly has "no Riptide". From Ch18 to Ch21 he uses Roman weapons, water and ice. Then in Ch22 he holds Riptide again with no return scene. This is flagged for the author as a story-level slip (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md §5). The vault's Plot Threads (written through Ch21) still says Percy is without his signature weapon.
- **"Anaklusmos" vs "Riptide":** the text alternates deliberately in Ch14 ("forced Anaklusmos—no, Riptide, back into his pocket"). Treat "Anaklusmos" as the name for the awakened, shard-bearing form.
- **His visibility:** "Nobody else could see him except for me" (Artemis), yet the manifested colossus is seen by the cult and the Hunters, some of whom kneel.
- **How he looks:** "older. Tired" on the cannon barrel, but "just like the boy she remembered" at the end of the same scene.
- **Who "Him" refers to:** the cult says "Him" and "Perseus Jackson" interchangeably. Artemis insists the entity "is not Percy Jackson".
