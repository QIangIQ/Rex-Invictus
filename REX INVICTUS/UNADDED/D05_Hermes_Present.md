## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **This file:** the 21st-century Hermes. He is a gambler and a hard bargainer, and the father of Luke and the Stoll brothers.
- **Not** the old-timeline Hermes of Ch1: salt-and-pepper hair, caduceus used as a clipboard, pre-flight checks on *The Spear of Zeus*, "YOU have no right to demand anything from us boy", swearing on "Hermes Clepsiphron" that the gods will return. That Hermes, and the far-future Empire's Hermesean institutions (Hermes relays, the Hermesean logistical caste, Hermes Holds on the Tartarus front), are in `E07_Hermes_Future_Clepsiphron.md`.
- **Key contrast:** the old-timeline Hermes abandoned Lord-Commander Percy on Earth. This Hermes meets the reincarnated Percy as a friendly stranger on a bus. Percy remembers the betrayal. Hermes does not, because it hasn't happened.
- **Knowledge boundary:** Knows the bolt was moved (he took a cut of Percy's divine food haul from Fulminus), knows about the Mars force and the Accord. No sign he knows Percy's future origin.

## Appearance
- Ch8, Mist-cloaked: a "sandy haired… large man who probably could have taken out the security guard and half the room". His face is "berry red", and his shirt has "a horrible grey stain where he'd toppled an overstuffed ashtray".
- Next morning: "a rather sorry-looking Hermes", coughing, hungover on the army bus.
- His Mist is "too weak to cloak from more powerful beings" (Minerva), who identifies him at once: "Divine Energy, Cloaked. Hermes."

## Personality
- **Gambler and sore loser.** "I bet on black six times and six times it comes up red. I swear the game was rigged!" Then: "Six bastard times" (Ch8).
- **Unabashed liar with charm.** He claims he turned down the $600 proposition from Mr. D's casino "lady friend" with "I'd rather have a nice cup of tea". Minerva: "He is lying." Percy: "I know" (Ch8).
- **Friendly to strangers.** He chats with Percy on the bus and thanks him for water: "Thank you, young one."
- **Watchful father.** Hungover and "sorry-looking" rather than sorrowful, he still keeps his voice low because his son Luke is dozing in the row ahead. This is quietly ominous given Luke's betrayal.
- **Transactional.** For a prayer and offering he spirits Percy's stockpile of ambrosia and nectar out of Area Fulminus, "with a simple gaze, before only asking for a 5 percent cut" (Ch11).
- **Diplomatic and dry.** After Percy's council report: "Well. That could have gone worse" (Ch19). In the Mars debate he wants to talk to the Imperials, which Zeus calls naive. When Ares sulks about "my planet", Hermes deflates him: "You don't even go there" (Ch22).

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Casual, wry and conversational. He calls Dionysus "Brother" and Percy "young one". He swears mildly ("bastard"), tells bar stories, and has a commerce-minded brevity ("Get me a steak").

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Mist disguise:** passable to mortals and demigods, transparent to Minerva.
- **Transport and smuggling:** moved "tons" of godly food out of Ares's sealed artefact storeroom at Fulminus for Percy (Ch11).
- **Messages and speed** (canon), implied by Percy's line "he's the messenger of the gods… Stuff like this usually gets broadcasted" (Ch10).
- **Commerce:** takes a percentage cut, the god of trade.
- **Future echoes:** the Imperial Sciritae "Relay Voice" is "a Hermes gift" carried by Hermes legacies (Ch22). See `G14_Sciritae_Neodamodes_and_Ch22_Units.md`.

## Backstory
- Father of Luke Castellan (Hermes cabin counselor), Travis and Connor Stoll, and the unclaimed-hosting Hermes cabin.
- Roman sons of Mercury: the twin gate-guards Castor and Pollux of New Rome (Ch17, an AU change from canon).
- In this AU the theft of Zeus's Master Bolt was a conspiracy. Ares was "played into holding it", and Percy implies others were involved (Ch10–11). Percy's cover story claims "Hermes dropped off a hint" (Ch11). Whether Hermes actually gave any hint is unconfirmed; it is presented as Percy's fib.

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch8:** In Las Vegas, Hermes is thrown off the Reef Casino floor after a roulette meltdown. Casino host Julio Sweet escorts him to the camp's rooftop dinner. Chiron greets him ("Hello, hello, Milord"). Mr. D hugs him and gives him five hundred-dollar chips. He orders a steak. Julio "successfully persuaded Hermes and Mr. D to lose ten thousand dollars". Next morning on the army bus he shares Percy's water and tells the $600 story, and he lies about it.
- **Ch10:** Percy fishes for news of Hermes from the Athena cabin and is caught fibbing.
- **Ch11:** Revealed: Percy prayed and sacrificed to Hermes to get the ambrosia and nectar out. Hermes took 5%. Percy uses "Hermes dropped off a hint" as his cover story for confronting Ares.
- **Ch19:** At the post-Othrys council he breaks the silence after Percy's report.
- **Ch21:** Apollo brags that his cabin is "Better than what Hermes does."
- **Ch22:** At the Mars council he deflates Ares and argues for talking to the Imperials, and is overruled. The Watchpoint guesses that Kappa-Seven were moved to Long Island by "Hermes?". In fact Percy hauled them through the Labyrinth (Ch21).
