## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **This file:** the present-day Romans.
- **Far-future Legio XII Fulminata:** It still exists as the Empire's "Heavy Tartarean front legion. The Thunderbolts. Best shield wall in the Empire", with lightning bolts on centurions' helmets (Ashmead, Ch 22). Its Interlude members are Tullia Ardens and Lucan Corvo (`H01_Tullia_Ardens.md`, `H02_Lucan_Corvo.md`). The future legion's soldiers are not these people.
- **Legio XXI Rapax** is not a present-day legion. Percy claims he "commanded under its standard, albeit temporarily" ("sworn to the Octavian of old, formed in the thirty-first year before the current era"). Jason says "That legion is gone" (Ch 17). The claim refers to Percy's original-timeline past and is never explained. Author's vault note (not yet in text; future era): in the UOE era the Rapax defected to Poseidon (linked to the 32nd Scylla's Sisters Anti-Siege Corps), which fits Percy holding a command in it in his old future.
- Author's vault note (not yet in text): the vault calls the Legio XII Fulminata "Jupiter's personal legion", with its old residence at New Rome, and gives it a full future order of battle (1st–19th Line Infantry, 1st–4th Assault Armies).
- **The Empire's Roman-flavoured troops** (Neodamodes, Legio XX Valeria Vitrix and others) are future forces, not these Romans. See `G14_Sciritae_Neodamodes_and_Ch22_Units.md`.
- **Canon baseline (labelled canon, not story):** In canon, Castor and Pollux are sons of Dionysus at Camp Half-Blood (Castor dies in *The Battle of the Labyrinth*), and Camp Jupiter's gate is watched by Terminus. Aurum and Argentum are Reyna's gold and silver greyhounds that detect lies. Gwen and Dakota are canon Fifth Cohort members. Here Castor and Pollux are recast as Roman "sons of Mercury", the dogs are mostly "wolves", and Gwen dies.

## Appearance
- **Castor:** "tall, broad-shouldered, with a bronze-toned breastplate", spear vertical. **Pollux:** "shorter but wiry, carrying a long spear tipped with polished steel." Both wear New Rome insignia (Ch 17).
- **Marcus:** a scar bisecting his lip, a crimson plume, gladius and pilum. In his duel he uses a commander's halberd with "gold and purple frills" (Ch 17–18).
- **Decimus:** a crimson plume; a heavy bronze shield painted crimson, and a gladius (Ch 17–18).
- **Gwen:** "a stocky girl with a grin that seemed wrong for the occasion, freckles and a helmet with a dent in it" (Ch 22).
- **Aurum and Argentum:** gold and silver metallic beasts (Ch 17).
- **The legion:** galeae (helmets) with fresh chalk marks and old dents, scuffed shields, scorpiones and ballistae, pila racks (Ch 19). In Ch 22 their shields are "painted with the eagle and the letters SPQR", red and gold. To the Sciritae they look like "a museum's idea of Rome".

## Personality
- **Castor & Pollux:** cautious, practical, by-the-book. "Ave, but why should we trust you?" / "Bene, passage granted to the perimeter. Stay under watch." (Ch 17)
- **Marcus:** gruff and sceptical. He calls the newcomers "Barbari" and Percy's legalese "hocus-pocus" ("Negotiatio what now?"). He mocks the idea of attacking Othrys ("send a polite letter to Cronus?") but is shaken by the seal and the wolves, and yields honourably (Ch 17–18).
- **Decimus:** mocking and confident. "Praetor Reyna doesn't parley with strays." / "You even Roman?" / "All talk, Greek?" He fights cleverly (shield bash, pommel strike) and gives "grudging respect" after yielding (Ch 17–18).
- **Gwen:** irrepressibly friendly and curious ("Is this the Greek we heard about?" / "Tell her hi back"). Reyna has to rein her in (Ch 22).
- **Dakota:** seen only as the witness to Gwen's death; "later, he would say that she looked surprised" (Ch 22).
- **The rank and file:** wry gallows humour ("the only difference between a fortress and a prison is which direction the spears point"), and disciplined courage under boulder fire ("His place was filled without pause"). They are frightened by the divine forces around them ("By Jupiter… what is happening?") and confused by Eleni ("Who's this Incoop guy?") (Ch 18–19). They adapt after the volley: "They had learned their lesson about formation" (Ch 22).

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Soldierly, terse, with sprinklings of Latin ("Ave", "Bene", "Barbari", "Ad vīam"). Young legionaries use American slang.
- "State your business, Barbari." (Marcus, Ch 17)
- "You mean the Titan place? Yeah… So what, you want us to send a polite letter to Cronus?" (Marcus, Ch 17)
- "Praetor Reyna doesn't parley with strays." (Decimus, Ch 17)
- "By decree of the Senate and People of Rome, the praetors convene in closed session. State your petition." (gate guard, Ch 17)
- "I just want the pokey bits facing away from us." (veteran, Ch 19)
- "They bleed!" / "Everything bleeds." (legionaries, Ch 19)
- "Hold the line. Do not chase! Kill what is in front of you." (centurion, Ch 19)
- "Is this the Greek we heard about?" (Gwen, Ch 22)

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- Roman discipline: testudo, contubernium hand-signals, shield walls, rotating ranks, pila volleys, scorpiones and ballistae. "The multicolored powers… from individual demigods and legacies within the ranks… invisibly sped the legion along" (Ch 19). Young engineers pray to Vulcan as they calibrate the siege engines (Ch 18).
- Reyna insists that Mars's demigods and legacies "don't have divine abilities" (Ch 19).
- Their divine "output's higher than the Greeks'. Organized." (Imperial Sciritae scan, Ch 22).
- The Golden Standard has an unexplained "ability" (Ashmead, Ch 22).
- **Aurum and Argentum** recognise Percy and bow to him (Ch 17).
- **The Little Tiber** (the text's "Mini Tiber", Ch 17) has truth-revealing waters that strip the Mist. Percy lifts a section so he and Eleni can pass beneath it (Ch 17).

## Backstory
New Rome sits in the Berkeley Hills: walls, the "hive-acropolis", the Little Tiber, the curia praetoria, the Senate, the Colosseum and the Field of Mars, plus an Aventine shrine (Ch 17–18). Lares watch the duels from the arches (Ch 18). In Percy's original timeline, "During the battle for New York, the Romans had besieged Mount Othrys". He counts on rousing them "far earlier this time" (Ch 16), and that plan is why he goes west. Lupa can outvote the Senate (Ch 22).

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch 17:**
  - Castor & Pollux pass the questers to the perimeter. Percy looks for Terminus, who doesn't appear.
  - Marcus and Decimus halt them. Percy recites Roman law and shapes a "Sigillum Imperii Neptuni" from water (Reyna later pronounces it genuine), and the wolves bow to him.
  - Decimus strikes his pilum thrice, and Cohort II escorts them to the curia.
  - The praetors arrive, and Jason proposes the Trial by Steel.
- **Ch 18:**
  - The crowd votes Percy's weapons. He beats Decimus (sabre and cloak), Marcus (claymore against halberd) and Jason (knife).
  - The Romans "dragged him into celebration."
  - Five cohorts fortify the base of Othrys. Eleni overruns the command tent.
- **Ch 19:**
  - The legion assaults Othrys: boulders, testudo, ballistae, the Princess Andromeda's monster wave, the Titan-warped gravity shelf.
  - Artemis teleports the whole army about 20 km away just before the mountain detonates. They land "staggering and shouting".
- **Ch 22:**
  - Lupa outvotes the Senate, and the First, Second and Fifth cohorts march out of the Labyrinth at Zeus's Fist. Gwen greets Percy.
  - The shield wall on the eastern slope is flagged "priority two" by the dying Sciritae and hit by the Psiloi from 96 km up. Forty-one of the Fifth Cohort die in ninety seconds, including Gwen, as she turns to Dakota.
  - The survivors face Ashmead's Neodamodes on the beach until CODE 18 ("we've been shooting at the Emperor").
  - Romans and Neodamodes pull survivors out of Lander Three together. They form a mixed line against Kronos's monsters, with two Neodamodes in every gap of the Roman shields.
  - At the sunset council the legion's toll is "44 DEAD. 38 WOUNDED". Reyna writes every name on a wax tablet. Accord Article VI makes the Legio XII "the military partner of the Command upon Earth", and Artemis immediately invokes it for Alaska.

## Continuity Notes
- Aurum and Argentum are "wolves" in Ch 17 but "metal dogs" in Ch 22.
- Fifth Cohort dead: 41 in ninety seconds. Total legion dead: 44.
- Castor & Pollux are sons of Mercury and gate guards here, not canon's sons of Dionysus.
- The New Rome river is called the "Mini Tiber" in the text (Ch 17). Profiles use the Little Tiber (canon and vault), noting the text form at first mention (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md S15).
- **Seal of Neptune.** Water-formed by Percy and pronounced genuine by Reyna; do not call it simply "forged" or simply "genuine" (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C16).
- **Legion name.** The future legion is "Legio XX Valeria Vitrix" in both text and vault (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md S9).
- At Othrys, Percy locks onto "Annabeth. Grover. Thalia." during the teleport (Ch 19). Annabeth and Thalia were not listed as questers, which is an apparent slip.
