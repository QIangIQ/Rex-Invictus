## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- A far-future figure, discovered by **Minerva in Percy's clone body** (`B02_Minerva_Percy_Clone_Interlude.md`).
- He is one example of a system. In Ch 19 Marien Pyrrhus laments that "our 2 Helmsmen have died, and we don't have the tech or the sons of Poseidon to reweld into the ship's nervous system". In Ch 22 the Anabasis Accord has **Triton** serve as Helmsman (`E10_Triton.md`), and "Helmsman" is the Imperial role binding a being to a ship's nervous system. Ikaris is the clearest on-page case.
- **Author's vault note (not yet in text):** the vault's Helmsman entry confirms the system: Helmsmen are "direct descendants of [[Poseidon]]" (children or legacies), "pre-emptively lobotomized", given a "Full-body amputation" and sealed in a "Sealed Helmsman Sarcophagus". This matches Ch 11 exactly. Ikaris is a Helmsman in all but name; the text never uses the word "Helmsman" for him.
- He is unrelated to "Demetrius Lykourgos" in the author's Ch 2 OC list.

## Appearance
"A body… amputated from all four of its limbs, twitched weakly as… seemingly endless amounts of unknown liquids [were] pumped in and out of his body." He lies sealed beneath the command throne's floor slab, made of a material even Minerva's optics cannot see through.

## Personality
Almost nothing remains. "Cognitive Capacity Remaining: 3.7%"; "Pain Response: Suppressed." His involuntary "jolt[s]" turn the ship "a few degrees to port or starboard." After Minerva's override, a sense of clarity returns to him and about a hundred others like him.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
He does not speak. He is known only through Minerva's status readout:
- "Subject: Legacy Designate 02476 – Formerly Demetrius Ikaris." (Ch 11)
- "Status: Permanent Integration." (Ch 11)
- "Cognitive Capacity Remaining: 3.7%" (Ch 11)
- "Remaining lifespan: 3,000+ years" (Ch 11)

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
He gives the warship "faster-than-light cognition, instantaneous response times beyond even [Minerva's] abilities." The ships are steered by mental command through integrated Poseidon legacies, because "no mere cable or wire could transmit the data fast enough" when railgun slugs are kilometres long. "A god's blood… A demigod's mind, still tethered to the fabric of fate itself… That was the ultimate control system."

## Backstory
"Across kilometer-long warships, across entire fleets, certain descendants of Poseidon… were forcibly integrated into the nexus of naval command, their minds hollowed out, shackled to the ship itself." "The majority of Poseidon's children did not command the fleet. They were the fleet." This is "a secret guarded jealously by the divine denizens of Olympus Tertiae."

## Story Arc So Far
**Ch 11:** Minerva's commandeered AnnChase squadron exits a tachyon bridge at a transit hub on the Zeus/Poseidon border. Sitting on the command throne, she senses a Poseidon legacy beneath her feet, rips up the floor slab with Percy's fingers and finds Ikaris. Her old self would have called it efficiency. Minerva, "who had learned the weight of a soul", calls it "A crime." She sets a directive to overhaul UOE Naval Intelligence: "Override neural integration systems. Preserve remaining cognitive faculties of all interred Legacies. They will be of use." A hundred dim minds spark with partial clarity.

## Relationships
- **Minerva** (`B02_Minerva_Percy_Clone_Interlude.md`): his liberator, partly out of conscience and partly out of utility ("They will be of use").
- **Poseidon** (`E02_Poseidon_Future.md`): his divine ancestor, whose descendants are the Empire's "most necessary casualties". Poseidon's own knowledge or complicity is not stated.
- **Percy**: a fellow Poseidon-blooded relative, very distantly.

## Continuity Notes
- The ship is called an "AnnChase-Class Battleship" in his readout, while the squadron is called "AnnChase-Pattern Battlecruisers". The terms are used interchangeably.
- "Helmsman" is the Ch 19/22 term; Ch 11 never applies it to Ikaris. The identification rests on the matching description and the author's vault (Helmsmen are lobotomised, limb-amputated Poseidon descendants sealed in a sarcophagus).
- Ikaris is a *legacy* (descendant), not a child, of Poseidon. Marien's Ch 19 line speaks of "sons of Poseidon"; the vault's "direct descendants" covers both.
