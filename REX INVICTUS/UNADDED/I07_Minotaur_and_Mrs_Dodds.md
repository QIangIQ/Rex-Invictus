## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **This timeline's Minotaur** (Ch6–7) is fought by reincarnated Percy alone, with Minerva commentating.
- **The original-timeline Minotaur** exists only in memories. In Ch23 future Minerva recalls Annabeth "on the first day, when a boy with a Minotaur's horn in his hand had looked up and seen her," which is canon Percy's arrival. Future Thalia "had faced Kronos and the Minotaur" (Ch23), in her own past.
- **The Ch21 Labyrinth vision** of "a broad, heavy shape in a rust-streaked bull's-skull helmet" with a sledgehammer is **not** the Minotaur. See `I08_Labyrinth_of_Daedalus.md`.

## Appearance
- **Minotaur (Ch7):** "easily ten feet tall, broad as a wrecking ball, its bull-like face twisted in an expression of confusion." It snorts "plumes of steam," and its horns "sparked with some lingering divine energy."
- **Mrs. Dodds (Ch3):** her appearance is not described. She simply spends "far too much time" with "her eyes on him."

## Personality
- **Minotaur:** brute power with surprising tactics. It waits at the camp's border "Like it knew I was coming," and leaps "faster than anything that big had a right to be."
- **Mrs. Dodds:** watchful and suspicious. That is all the text shows.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Neither speaks. The Minotaur roars, snorts and bellows. Lines about them:
- "WARNING: Major divine-class hostility detected. Threat level: Majoris." (Minerva, Ch6)
- "IT CAN DO THAT!?" (Percy, Ch7)
- "That's the Minotaur. As in the Minotaur. And you took it down alone?" (Ares camper, Ch7)
- "The Minotaur? You're certain?" (Chiron, Ch7)
- "Passable." (Minerva, Ch7)
- "That thing was waiting for me at the border of camp. Like it knew I was coming." (Percy, Ch7)

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
The Minotaur has enormous weight and reach, charges that send "a tremor through the ground," leaps that land with "a concussive shockwave," and divinely charged horns. Mrs. Dodds is not shown using any ability.

## Backstory
*Canon baseline (not story):* in *The Lightning Thief*, Mrs. Dodds is Percy's pre-algebra teacher at Yancy Academy and secretly Alecto, one of the Furies. She attacks him at the Metropolitan Museum. The Minotaur chases Percy, Sally and Grover to Half-Blood Hill, appears to kill Sally, and is killed when Percy stabs it with its own horn.

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch3:** In English class, Mrs. Dodds sets an essay on Shakespearean sonnets and watches Percy closely. Percy leaves Yancy through the Labyrinth that same day, so the museum attack never happens.
- **Ch6:** Near camp, Minerva flags a Majoris threat, and the Minotaur charges out of the tree line.
- **Ch7:** The fight on Half-Blood Hill. Percy cuts the outer calf, and the beast leaps. His hydrokinesis fails ("body and soul were still misaligned"), and he coughs "silver-red blood." He slides under its arm, hamstrings it, climbs onto its back and **tears off a horn**. It collapses and its carcass dissolves. Lycaon's heart reknits Percy, and an Ares camper runs to report. Chiron "had gone very still" (Ch7) and suspects who the boy is.
- **Ch8:** The kill is still the talk of camp. When Athena visits Percy disguised as Annabeth, someone says "I guess fighting the Minotaur solo and walking away does that to a guy." The text muddles who says it: it follows "She shrugged," but reads like Percy's voice. The fake Annabeth replies, "Yes, remarkable. Almost too remarkable."

## Relationships
- **Percy** (`A02_Percy_Reincarnated_Rex.md`): the Minotaur's killer. He keeps the horn "partially shoved into his pocket." For Mrs. Dodds, he is a target who escaped her.
- **Minerva** (`B01_Minerva_In_Percys_Head.md`): mocking ringside commentator.
- **Chiron** (`F05_Chiron.md`): alarmed by the kill.

## Continuity Notes
- In canon Mrs. Dodds teaches pre-algebra; here she is the **English** teacher.
- **Percy's age:** a young teen; the text calls him sixteen (Ch7 "a sixteen-year-old with a sword", Ch13, Ch22), though the premise blurb says he returned as a twelve-year-old (`00a_CANON_RULINGS.md` R1, flagged for the author).
- **Mrs. Dodds's fate:** write *fate unstated*, not *alive* or *dead* (C20). Her identity as Alecto, a Fury serving Hades, comes from canon and the author's vault (a canon-baseline note); the text never states it.
- The author's vault has no note on the Minotaur.
- Unlike canon, Sally and Grover are not with Percy at the Minotaur fight.
