## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **Present-day Atlas** (Ch16–19) is the on-page antagonist.
- **Old-timeline Atlas** existed only as the canon Titan's Curse foe whom Percy, Zoë (probably "the Eternal Hunter Princess"; Ch1 does not name her) and others subjugated (Ch1). In the old future, Titans swarmed Percy at Olympus Primaris, but Atlas is not named there.
- **Divergence:** in canon Zoë dies. Here "one shall perish by a parent's hand" is fulfilled when Artemis spears Atlas and his own flailing bolt kills one of his Hesperide daughters (Ch19; 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C19). Zoë is alive in this story (C21).

## Appearance
- Not physically described.
- "His aura is distinctly of Titanic origin" (Eleni's scan, Ch16).
- "Atlas laughed. A deep, grinding sound that echoed through the cavern." (Ch18) He bleeds "blood like molten metal" (Ch19).
- His domain is Othrys: "a broad shelf of black stone veined with glowing fractures", with warped gravity ("Oraunos gravitational interference"), and the "Titan's thrones looming nearby" (Ch19).

## Personality
- **Cruel and mocking:** he taunts Artemis under the sky: "little moon goddess", she "would break sooner than anyone". "Struggling already?" "Poor Artemis," he crooned. "The sky suits you poorly, perhaps you are used to your bow?" (Ch18)
- **Rattled when outmatched:** "You should see Atlas, he's panicking even now." (Artemis, Ch18, as the Roman legion approaches)
- **Spiteful to the point of self-destruction:** pinned and humiliated, he "laughs again, broken and furious", slams Percy away from the sky, "And Atlas just stands there, making no move to hold up the sky as it came crashing down towards Gaia." (Ch19)
- **A strategist who uses feints:** his monsters feint to buy time while he waits (Percy: "Feints… They are buying time"; Jason: "For Atlas. For sure they're waiting for him to make his move", Ch19).

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Deep, grinding and taunting. Contemptuous diminutives.
- "Struggling already?" (Ch18)
- "Poor Artemis… The sky suits you poorly, perhaps you are used to your bow?" (Ch18)

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Bearing the sky:** he normally carries it. Here he tricks Artemis into holding it (canon). His fortress interferes with gravity.
- **Divine bolts:** "Bolts of divine energy rip free from him in blind fury", and one "obliterates a Hesperide mid-flight" (Ch19).
- **Titanic strength:** he wrenches Artemis's spear from his chest and "slamming into Percy with titanic force" (Ch19).
- **The Nemean Lion** ("Auric Felinid") accompanies him near the Smithsonian (Ch16). See `I09_Nemean_Lion_and_Monsters.md`.

## Backstory
Canon: condemned to hold the sky after the Titanomachy. Father of the Hesperides and of Zoë Nightshade (canon and author's vault; 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C28).
- **Author's vault note (not yet in text):** son of Iapetus and Clymene; father of the Hesperides, Zoë Nightshade, Calypso and the Pleiades. Dr. Thorn the manticore is an agent of Atlas and Kronos. Zoë was cast out of the Garden of the Hesperides for betraying them and giving Riptide to Hercules.

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch1:** remembered as subjugated early in Percy's original journeys.
- **Ch16:**
  - Eleni senses "The General" with the Nemean Lion near the Smithsonian.
  - Artemis tells Percy she *must* be captured by Atlas to keep the timeline ("You think I wish to bow my neck to Atlas?").
- **Ch18:** Artemis holds the sky while he taunts her. Percy's offering makes her stronger than Zeus, and Atlas panics at the legion.
- **Ch19:**
  - The Roman assault on Othrys. Percy climbs alone and takes the sky from Artemis.
  - Artemis summons an ancient hunting spear and "pins Atlas through the chest". His wild bolts kill a Hesperide, fulfilling the prophecy.
  - He wrenches free, body-slams Percy away from the sky, and refuses to take it back. The sky's release detonates Othrys and vaporises the Hesperides and their grove.

- **The Hesperides** (daughters): one is killed by his bolt; the rest die in the blast. See `99_Minor_Characters_Compendium.md`.

## Secrets, Mysteries & Open Threads
- Did Atlas survive the Othrys detonation? Is he back under the sky, or loose?
- Who holds the sky now? The text implies the sky "crashed" and the release went "down" into Othrys. The consequences are unaddressed.

## Continuity Notes
- "Oraunos" [sic] is a misspelling of Ouranos (Ch19).
- **The Hesperide's death (C19):** Artemis spears Atlas; Atlas's own bolt then kills the Hesperide. Do not write that the spear killed her.
- **Zoë in Ch1:** "The Eternal Hunter Princess" is unnamed in Ch1; identifying her as Zoë is a strong inference, not a statement of the text.
- Present-day year: see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R2.
- Percy's "fatass sky who wants to hug his wife" (Ch18) refers to Ouranos and Gaia, not Atlas.
