## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
Only one Merek exists. He is a future-native in the present. In Ch20 he is free and on mission; in Ch21 he is crippled; in Ch22 he is a prisoner and later an ally. See the squad overview `G09_Kappa7_Ekdromoi_Squad_Overview.md`.

## Appearance
- Huge. Even unarmored he has "enough reinforced musculature to make the chair look undersized beneath him" (Ch22).
- He implies he is 8 feet tall: "Not all of us get the liberty of being not 8 feet tall" (Ch20).
- Roman features, broad shoulders.
- **Power armor**, the heaviest of the five: "imperial gold alloy plating" and layered bronze circuitry visible when the gauntlet segments open. Helmet oculars throw red lidar light (Ch21). "A hulking, giant humanoid hunk of divine steel." Servos grind. The armor "fucks with my stiff joints".
- His lungs are filled with a synthetic oxygenated perfluorocarbon suspension, and life support stimulates his organs (Ch21).
- Bandaged in Ch22. He walks out with a torn-off chair frame hanging from his wrists.

## Personality
- **Crude, hungry, irreverent (Ch20).**
  - He stole three days of protein packs.
  - He poisoned himself on mousetrap bait cheese and got a black eye.
  - He calls power armor "boring".
  - He likes to fall rather than glide: "I reach the ground before the ground reaches me in a way I do not like."
  - "That is hella nice."
- **Relentless in battle (Ch21).** With a spike broken off in his leg joint he keeps advancing, shoulder-charges an ice pillar taller than himself, and fires "with machine precision" until Eleni's second beam drops him.
- **Observant and steady in captivity (Ch22).**
  - He watches faces for "the smallest change in expression".
  - He counts the evidence on his fingers: "You are from the UOE."
  - He states truths calmly: "The beginning is Tartarus." "The defense of Alaska Hill was commanded by a son of Poseidon."
- **Respectful of the Director.** Restrained, he bends forward "as close to a bow as he could manage" and says "Director".
- **Brave and pragmatic.** He volunteers to go out "Unarmored. Alone" to talk Oriel down. He tears his chair out of the floor and walks out to stop the shooting: "Oriel," … "It's me."
- **Honest with Apollo.** Asked who killed Michael Yew, he answers, "It was a cone… Probably all of them."
- **Quietly humane.** He kneels last to the Lord Imperator. At the pyre he explains the gold citizenship tokens on the Imperial dead.

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Heavy Vanguard harness / power armor.** Inertial dampers allow an unpowered orbital drop; his landing "vaporize[d] the stone beneath and around him". The armor carries a VA-RF-762 MORROW-IX rifle firing QVARTUS rounds and life support. Its automated beacon sends the 2.3-second burst that reaches Mars (Ch22).
- **Deployable black sphere.** A baseball-sized ball projects a **Pariah Field**, an anti-divine dome that blocks Percy's water and Eleni's powers (Ch21).
- **Great physical strength.** Even unarmored he shears floor bolts and tears a reinforced chair out (Ch22).
- He carries Divine Stimulants like the rest of the cell.
- His harness was a security leak for his own side. When Percy stabilised him, Minerva pulled the Task Force formation-net codes out of it. She later used them to spoof the CODE 2 retreat (Ch22).

## Backstory
- He came through the shear with the Pyrrhuiesian reserve echelon and woke on Mars. Straban says the echelon had come down "on a sector near the Phlegethon" and was staging at a transit anchor on the Lethe side; Iane says the event "took everything within eleven kilometers of the anchor" (Ch22).
- He was selected with his cell by AtRep for Operation Anabasis-105 reconnaissance (the operation is named by Straban, Ch22): Alaska ("Obscurus One") and two other sites (Ch20, Ch22).
- Author's vault note (not yet in text): the vault's Ekdromoi note assigns him the **Heavy Vanguard** role as a **Roman legacy** (both consistent with the text) and says Ekdromoi are chosen by AtRep without application, mostly from the Peltast corps. A non-canon vault vignette (Annex 15370) shows him fighting cultists beside Ione, cocky and archaic in turn ("Thoughts are like fish", "Mine senses detect something afoul"); use it as a voice reference only.
- Oriel calls Kappa-Seven "the best cell on this planet" (Ch22).
- He knows Oriel's voice and her cell, Lambda-Two.

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch20.** He rides the dropship from Mars and jokes about his armor. He drops without gliding and lands on a steppe. He banters with Neumond and the twins, then walks through the Hecate rune-gate toward Alaska.
- **Ch21.**
  - He crosses the Labyrinth with the cell ("the bowels of a living machine") and they exit into Alaska.
  - He deploys the Pariah Field.
  - He advances through Percy's ice storm until Eleni's electrokinetic beam caves his armor twice.
  - Percy tears him out of the locked suit and stabilises him. He is carried to camp in two trips by rope.
- **Ch22.**
  - Interrogation: he tells Percy "You're going first.", deduces Percy is UOE, and narrates the Tartarean Front and the shear. He estimates "A little over a legion's worth… Approximately 4 million".
  - He bows to Artemis.
  - He identifies Lambda-Two. He tells Apollo the truth about Michael's death, then tears free and walks into the standoff.
  - He kneels to the Lord Imperator, attends the council in armor, and explains the citizenship tokens at the pyre.
