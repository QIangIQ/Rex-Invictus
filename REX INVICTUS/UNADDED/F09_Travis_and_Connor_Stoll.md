## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- They are the only Stolls in the story: present-day, with no future knowledge.
- **Twins or not?** Ch7 calls them "near-identical demigods… the Stoll brothers," and Ch10 calls them "the Hermes twins." In descriptive prose they are brothers; quote "twins" only as the text's label (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C22). In canon they are not twins; Travis is the elder.
- The narration sometimes swaps their names; see Continuity Notes. When roleplaying, Travis is the bolder risk-taker at cards and Connor is the self-proclaimed card-counting teacher.

## Appearance
- "Near-identical" (Ch7). There is no other description, apart from Connor's trainers flexing on a plastic roof (Ch10).

## Personality
- **Cheeky and irreverent.** Travis tells Chiron, "Well, you are getting on a bit" (Ch8). Connor charges onto the off-limits royal bed on Zeus's plane (Ch8). They tongue-poke, sneer and laugh at authority.
- **Gamblers and schemers.** Travis hits on seventeen and lands blackjack ("Fortuna smiles upon me"). Connor brags, "Because I taught you how to count cards" (Ch8). Camp rule three is "never bet against the Stoll brothers" (Ch8).
- **Rowdy.** In Vegas they hold an M&M battle, race "around like lunatics," flick each other with towels and blast the TV, driving Percy out of the suite (Ch8). They get "indoctrinated" by the hotel's promo channel into visiting the arcade and aquarium (Ch8).
- **Gossipy teasers.** They ambush Percy and Annabeth with honeymoon and wedding-band jokes, then scatter "in a flurry of laughter and mock-terror" when Annabeth draws her knife (Ch15).
- **Brave in a pinch.** Connor, laughing, rides piggyback into gunfire and drops the grenade alone after losing his brother in the smoke (Ch10).
- **Appreciate good pranks.** They whistle when Percy's dodged juice soaks Luke ("Nice one", Travis, Ch8).

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- Card counting and gambling luck (Ch8).
- Scouting with binoculars (Ch10).
- Light-fingered Hermes stealth. Connor slips an M67 "baseball" paint grenade through a ventilation slat, timing four seconds, and paints General Mikhail neon orange (Ch10). His "blood was pulsing quite strongly" to Percy's water-sense (Ch10).
- Rifles and heavy equipment packs in the war game (Ch10).

## Backstory
- As a canon baseline: Hermes Cabin pranksters under counselor Luke. Their cabin is known for theft ("don't steal from the Hermes cabin unless you enjoy waking up glued to the ceiling", rule two, Ch8).
