## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- There is only one version on the page. Do not confuse her with **"Phoebe Artemis"**, the narration's name for the goddess Artemis in Ch6, or with the Titaness Phoebe (who has her own vault note). She does not appear in the far-future chapters, although the author's vault roster lists her among the Hunt's *active* Hunters, so she presumably still serves in the future era (vault only).
- In canon *Titan's Curse*, Phoebe was knocked out of the quest by the Stolls' poisoned shirt. Percy notes that "Last time, a few of the quest candidates were taken out by a poisoned shirt", and this time no poisoning happens (Ch16).

## Appearance
- Silver-clad and physically strong enough to carry Percy over her shoulder. No other description is given.
- After Ch22 she is missing two fingers of her left hand.

## Personality
- **Grumpy and boy-averse.** She "huffed slightly and muttered something about weak boys", complains about having to carry Percy ("Must I?"), and calls him "you prick".
- **Pushes back, then folds.** She argues with Zoë ("Why can't you do it then?") until Zoë "leveled her gaze" at her, and then she drops it.
- **Self-deprecating humour.** When the Répertoire drains her, she blames herself: "Might need to cut down on the desserts this month."
- **Observant.** She notices the nectar being used up "so completely" (Ch4).
- **Reliable in combat.** "A second arrow from Phoebe's tree" finishes a Sciritae in Ch22.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Blunt, modern and colloquial ("outta practice"), with some whining and mild oaths ("Gods!"). She calls Atlanta and Celyn by name or nickname ("'Lanta").
- "So, a freak?" (Ch4)
- "Must I?" (Ch4)
- "Ughhh, why can't Celyn or 'Lanta do it?" (Ch4)
- "Either he's heavy, or I'm outta practice!" (Ch4)
- "Might need to cut down on the desserts this month." (Ch4)
- "Wake up, you prick! If you're really a son of Poseidon, you'd wake up right now" (Ch4)

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- Strength inherited from Ares. Zoë cites it: "As a daughter of Ares, surely it is not a problem?"
- An archer. She shoots from a tree position in Ch22.
- Immortal Hunter energy, Lambda-class, which the Répertoire drained to recharge until it detected Artemis's protection.

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch4:**
  - She is on the patrol to the fire. She flips Percy's body over, comments on the nectar, and calls him "a freak".
  - Zoë orders her to carry him under threat of laundry duty.
  - En route the Répertoire force-drains her energy. She gasps, finds Percy suddenly heavier, and yells at him to wake up.
- **Ch16:** Zoë names her for the five-Hunter rescue team ("Atlanta, Phoebe, Flavia, and Daphnis"), but in the end Zoë takes only Bianca.
- **Ch20 (possible):** At the camp breakfast, a young Hunter asks whether Percy always talks like that, and "Phoebe shook her head." The next line, "Pass the honey, Percy.", is ambiguously attributed. Percy then passes the honey to Artemis, so the line is probably Artemis's, not Phoebe's.
- **Ch22:** She fights in the woods ambush on the Sciritae, shooting one in the chest. She loses two fingers of her left hand in the battle.

## Relationships
