## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
There is only one Marien. She is a far-future officer who, since Ch19, lives in the present era on Mars. In Ch23 her own future knows her only as a name on Lord Varro's casualty list and as the countersignature on a recovered verdict. Do not confuse her with:
- **Stephanos Pyrrhus**, her father, a Grand Commander ranked 51st (`G03_Stephanos_Pyrrhus.md`);
- **Eleni Theodosia**, a rival-house Zeus demigod displaced by a *different* event (`G01_Eleni_Theodosia.md`);
- **the Major**, her subordinate node commander (`G10_The_Major.md`).

## Appearance
- **Ch19:** armor "brighter than most field kit and less scarred", a command crest, and visor optics. On Mars she is "still covered in sand and scratches".
- **Ch22:**
  - She is younger than Percy expected ("Maybe thirty"), with sea-grey eyes and short dark hair.
  - Her face "had been beautiful before it had become a commander's face".
  - Her command crest is "dented where the void had tried to take her and failed", and her armor is still scratched from the void.
  - She travels light: a small, fast shuttle, alone, without her bodyguard.

## Personality
- **Clinical and assessing.** "You held," she tells the Major, and it sounds "like assessment rather than praise". She reads the trench "the way a surgeon read an incision" (Ch19).
- **Physically brave and protective.** When the ground dematerializes she runs toward the edge, throwing soldiers back by their harnesses, before she falls herself (Ch19).
- **Pragmatic and decisive.** "We will build here as if we intend to stay." "The mortals… will survive seeing some ships." "Send a patrol over." (Ch19, Ch22).
- **Grim.** She reads the Watchpoint's verdict on the weak Dionysian signature "the way a person reads out a sentence of execution": "A young god" … "in a young world" (Ch22).
- **Dryly ironic.** She notes that the rover's name "Perseverance" "is sentiment, no?" (Ch19).
- **Devout but unanswered.** She has prayed to Poseidon every night of her life and never been answered. When he apologises, "something in her face broke, just slightly". She calls it "the first answer" (Ch22).
- **Proud and principled before present-day gods.** She refuses to kneel to Zeus: "I have loyalty to what you will become… I don't yet know if I have any loyalty to what you are." (Ch22).
- **Honest about her chain of command.** She is owed only to the Watchpoint, her chain, the (not-yet-existing) Council and "the Lord Imperator", and she apologises to Percy for noting he is sixteen.

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Combat abilities.** Her personal powers are not shown. She is of Poseidon's line and ranked 9051st on the Unified Leaderboard.
- **Command assets.**
  - Two carriers. Iane explains that a UOE carrier is "a little over four kilometers from bow to stern" and that its foundries "can print a forward base within a week" (Ch22). The text calls them only "two ships" (Ch19), "two carriers" and the "port carrier" (Ch22). Per the author's vault (Plot Threads) they are the *UOE Axis Dawn* and *UOE Warbringer Fury*; the Ch19 Segment Twelve readout lists "UOE Axis Dawn: 23.00% operational" and "UOE Warbringer Fury: 41.28% operational" as the orbital assets (see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C26).
  - Perioeci townships and the Grid power lattice.
  - The 2.1 km Spire housing the Watchpoint fragment (Ch22).
  - A small, fast shuttle.
  - A bodyguard element. One bodyguard followed her into the void in Ch19 and has not been seen since. At Camp Half-Blood she walks off her shuttle "alone, without her bodyguard" (Ch22).
- **The Watchpoint fragment.** A piece of AtRep's architecture that she "held… in my holds for this exact scenario". She carried it through Tartarus in a sealed casket, and it brings the Répertoire back online (Ch19, Ch22).
- **Authority.** She can countersign Watchpoint verdicts, and her countersignature authenticates CODE 18 (Ch22–23).
- **Limitations.**
  - Her two Helmsmen died in transit, and she has "no… sons of Poseidon to reweld into the ship's nervous system" (Ch19).
  - Water and food on Mars are finite. Straban warns that "Mars cannot feed a legion forever" and that the water reclamation is overburdened (Ch22).

## Backstory
- She is the child of Grand Commander Stephanos Pyrrhus. The Pyrrhus dynasty is "one of the strongest in the rankings", a Poseidon line and rival of Eleni's Theodosians (Ch22).
- Every child of her house "is taught to pray" to Poseidon (Ch22).
- She once saw Percy's key-seal (a trident crossed with lightning in a laurel ring) on a ceremonial tablet in the High Temple on Olympus Secundus (Ch22).
- She arrived at Segment Twelve of the Tartarean front with supplies; it was "inspection, or intervention, or both" (Ch19).
- Author's vault note (not yet in text): the vault file "Marien Phyrrus" [sic] confirms her as the "daughter of Stephanos Pyrrhus" and gives her callsign as Dulaberre-Six (both also on the page). The vault names her two carriers the *Axis Dawn* and *Warbringer Fury* (see Abilities).

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch19.**
  - Her callsign scrolls across the trench augurs. A field centurion mutters "Why is she here?"
  - She lands and inspects; the Major reports attrition.
  - The landing shelf dematerializes. She throws soldiers clear, then falls into the void. Her bodyguard follows her. The Major takes the ships in after her rather than face Stephanos's wrath.
  - They emerge on **Mars**. She orders a permanent base, notes the dead Helmsmen, brings the Repertoire online from the Watchpoint fragment, and examines NASA's Perseverance rover.
- **Ch22.**
  - At Forward Base "Anabasis" she hears that Kappa-Seven missed contact windows. She plays Merek's beacon burst, which reports a Theodosian and something "wearing our key that the Watchpoint cannot name". Retrieval is authorized and she orders "Send a patrol over" (Task Force ORPHEUS).
  - Present-day Poseidon senses her blood when Minerva speaks her name.
  - When the Watchpoint detects Minerva's intrusion (through Neumond's wings and Lambda-Two's dropship), Marien orders "Hold". Minerva then presents Percy's key and the Watchpoint accepts it; Marien's countersignature authenticates CODE 18, naming Percy Lord Imperator. She asks to speak to Percy and his father.
  - At noon she lands alone and confronts Poseidon on the beach. He drowned 71 of her cavalry, "Pyrrhus cousins" among them, including her nephew. He apologises: her first answered prayer.
  - At the sunset council she refuses to kneel and says nobody controls her "but the Watchpoint and my chain of command".
  - She accepts the Anabasis Accord: Ares as patron, Triton as Helmsman, Hades to build a Wall for her 406 dead. She names the "Phlegethon Wall".
  - Afterward Minerva relays a message from Handler Corvin on the Imperial net: the Watchpoint has detected Jade Court cultivators (two days out) and a company of the mortal 11th Airborne converging on Kappa-Seven's cloaked dropship in the Brooks Range.
- **Ch23 (far future, referenced).** Lord Varro's list shows "COMMAND ELEMENT: DULABERRE-SIX. MARIEN PYRRHUS… KIA PRESUMED". Researchers realise she carried a Watchpoint fragment. Her countersignature authenticates the recovered CODE 18 packet. The Director amends the echelon to MIA.

## Continuity Notes
- **Casualty figures** (see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C8). Use the author's reconciliation: Zeus killed **140**, the CODE 2 spoof killed **118**, Poseidon's wave drowned **71** Peltasts, and ground fighting made up the rest, giving **≈340** mid-battle (Poseidon's "three hundred and forty", Ch22) and **406** final ORPHEUS dead (council board: "406 DEAD. 212 WOUNDED."). Minerva's "ninety to one hundred and twenty" (after four landers) and "sixty to seventy" (Peltasts) were in-battle estimates; Herrman's "Seventy of my men" is a round number.
- **Unit types:** Ch19 says "two ships"; Ch22 narration says "The two carriers", though Straban describes the echelon as including "a carrier, and part of its escort wing"; Ch23 lists "CARRIER GROUP (TWO VESSELS)", while the chapter's opening narration has "a carrier". Treat the force as two vessels.
- **Time gap** (see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R3). Canonically the future era is about 14,000 years after the present (the shear falls in UOE year ~16231 per the vault). Label each figure for what it measures: Ch22 uses "six thousand years" three times (Minerva tells the Watchpoint that "the individual in your archive is six thousand years old"; Luke; narration), a known slip quoted as written; Ch23's archive gives "FOURTEEN THOUSAND [CORRUPTED] YEARS". Poseidon's "several thousand years removed" is vague, not a rival figure.
- **Percy's age** (see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R1). Marien says "The Lord Imperator is sixteen" (Ch22). Narration fields in this profile describe Percy as a young teen; the text calls him sixteen (Ch7, Ch13, Ch22), though the premise blurb says he returned as a twelve-year-old.
- **Spelling** (see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md S1). "Pyrrhus" throughout; adjective "Pyrrhuiesian". The vault note is filed as "Marien Phyrrus" and Ch12 spells the family "Pyrrus"; neither is used in this profile.
- **Ships** (see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C26). The text never names Marien's two carriers; the *Axis Dawn* and *Warbringer Fury* identification is the author's (Plot Threads), consistent with the Ch19 Segment Twelve readout. A "UOE Warbringer's Fury" also appears in Eleni's Ch2 Fulguris list.
- **Ch23 shear** (see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C7, C27). The shear, a sphere eleven kilometres in radius, swallowed Marien's Pyrrhuiesian Reserve Echelon (about four million people); the Director reclassifies it from KIA-presumed to "MISSING IN ACTION. LEAVE SPACE ON WALL." It did not swallow Percy.
- **Varro's records** (story-level slip, flagged in 00a §5): present-day Minerva in Ch22 already relays "Sister's records" stating that Lord Varro documented the shear dead, which happens only later in the future (Ch23).
