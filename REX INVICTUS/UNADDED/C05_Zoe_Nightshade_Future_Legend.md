## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **Present-day Zoë** (`C04_Zoe_Nightshade_Present.md`) is the living Lieutenant of the present day (c. 2021 per the author's timeline; nominally the canon PJO era) who rescues Percy, calls him "Freak", and survives Othrys in this timeline (a Hesperide dies in her place, Ch19). When the dying Sabine Ro tells her "You're supposed to be dead," she answers "Not yet" (Ch22).
- **This file** covers Zoë as a *legend and archival record*:
  - In the **old timeline** (Ch1), "The Eternal Hunter Princess" taught Percy "ancient dialects when they were on their subjugation of Lord Atlas in the very beginning of his journeys", the canon *Titan's Curse* quest. The text never names her, but "Princess" (a Titan's daughter), the Atlas quest and her archaic speech point to Zoë. Artemis is the only other candidate. **Flag as a strong inference.**
  - In the **far-future Empire**, she is "on the Wall", taught to children, "Oldest Hunter of the Director", her record "ARCHIVED, DECEASED".
- The **ghost of Zoë** that "bloomed over" Percy's old future Riptide "like a golden corona" (Ch14) belongs to the sword's far-future history ("His old riptide, which he lost a few millennia ago"). This file is the owner of that entry; C04 only cross-references it. Present Zoë is alive elsewhere at that moment, so it is an echo tied to the sword, not her acting.
- Thalia Grace has been Lieutenant for ~14,000 years in the future (`C07_Thalia_Grace_Future_Lieutenant.md`), which implies Zoë's death came early in Imperial history. The text does not say when.

## Appearance
No future-era description exists. Imperial optics identify her from her present-day look (Ch22): dark braided hair, a circlet, a silver parka, a pale, still face "so still it could have been carved". The Ch14 ghost appears only as "a golden corona… Like a halo around a biblical archangel."

## Personality
Known only by reputation and implication:
- **Teacher.** She taught Percy archaic dialects on the Atlas quest, a skill that later lets him understand Chaos (Ch1).
- **Exemplary, revered.** She is taught "at school" across the Empire (Ch22).
- **Bound to the sword.** Riptide was originally hers ("Zoë's sword", Ch16). She had told Artemis "long ago" that it "had always been more than bronze" (Ch16).
- The Ch14 regretful female voice in the sword ("Don't resist it. You will lose yourself.") is **unattributed**. It could be Zoë or Minerva ("Like hers, but not her"). Do not assert it.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
There are no future-era lines. Her known voice (present-day) is archaic English: thee, thou, thine, hast, "Thou knowest my name" (Ch22), and "Not yet" (Ch22). Roleplay of the legend should use this register.

## Backstory
- **Old timeline:** joined Percy on the subjugation of Atlas (canon *Titan's Curse*), teaching him dialects. Her fate there is unstated; in canon she dies on that quest.
- **Author's vault note (not yet in text):** the vault makes her a Hesperide and daughter of Atlas, cast out of the Garden of the Hesperides for betraying her sisters by giving Riptide to Hercules, and (in its Calypso note) Calypso's sister. Canon-baseline lore; the story only alludes to it ("former hesperide", "Zoë's sword").
- **Imperial history:** became the Director's first Lieutenant, died at an unknown time, and was memorialised on the Wall. On Long Island (Ch22) present-day Artemis learns, through Zoë's report of what the dying Sciritae Owl-Lead Sabine Ro told her, that Zoë "is supposed to be dead". In Ch23, ~14,000 years later, the Director seals the record: "NO IMPERIAL PERSONNEL MAY DISCLOSE THE DATE OR MANNER OF HER DEATH TO ANY PARTY, INCLUDING THE DIRECTOR, IN ANY EPOCH."

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch1:** old-timeline Percy silently thanks "The Eternal Hunter Princess" as he deciphers Chaos's archaic speech.
- **Ch14:** her ghost haloes the old Riptide as it merges into the present-day sword.
- **Ch16:** Artemis reclaims Riptide as "Zoë's sword".
- **Ch22:** Sciritae Owl-Lead Sabine Ro, dying under Zoë's arrow, recognises her from the Wall. The Imperial optics tag reads "ARCHIVED, DECEASED". Castellan Oriel refuses to tell Artemis when Zoë dies; the record is "sealed. By the Director."
- **Ch23:** the Director realises "It's tonight" and issues the seal, closing the loop.

## Continuity Notes
- In canon *Titan's Curse* Zoë dies after Othrys. In this timeline a Hesperide fulfils the "parent's hand" line instead (Ch19), yet the Empire still records her as deceased, so she dies later.
- The "first" and "oldest" titles conflict slightly with Thalia's ~14,000-year tenure, unless Zoë died early. The text gives no date.
- **Sealed, not "MISSING"** (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C7): the author's Plot Threads once said the Director marks Zoë "MISSING. NOT DEAD. LEAVE SPACE ON WALL." That wording is a superseded draft. In the published Ch23, "MISSING IN ACTION… LEAVE SPACE ON WALL" applies only to the Pyrrhuiesian Reserve Echelon; Zoë's record is sealed.
- **Vault status** (C21): the vault files Zoë under "Deceased", copied from the original books. Do not carry this over to the present-day Zoë, who is alive in this story.
- **"The Eternal Hunter Princess"** (Ch1) is a strong inference for Zoë, not a confirmed identification; other profiles (e.g. E16) should say "probably".
