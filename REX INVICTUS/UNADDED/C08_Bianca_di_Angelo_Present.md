## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **This file:** the ordinary present-day demigod girl, newly a Hunter, still "untrained" with the bow.
- **NOT** "Bianca Di'Angelo" of far-future Gaia (`C09_Bianca_DiAngelo_FarFuture.md`). That figure is a godlike woman who appears more than 30,000 years after the Empire's rise, flattens dozens of Imperial soldiers on the ruins of Mount Everest, and speaks with lofty disdain. Present Bianca knows nothing of her and shows no such power. How the two are connected is an open mystery.
- In Percy's original timeline Bianca died on this quest (canon). The present Bianca does not know that.

## Appearance
- "Wide eyes" full of awe and fear on meeting Artemis (Ch16).
- On the road west her "once innocent eyes" adapt "sharply to every movement along the ridges" (Ch17).
- Silver Hunter gear by implication. She carries a bow and keeps "arrows still nocked" (Ch17).
- She smells of "Sulfur and Brimstone" to the alchemist Nicholas Flamel (Ch17). His wife Perenelle (spelled "Pernelle" in this passage) smells the same stench and thinks: "Perhaps a daughter of Pluto or another death god. Or… Robespierre." (Ch17)

## Personality
- **Panicked at first, then brave.** She looks "panicked" when Thorn has her and Nico cornered. She insists "Percy saved us", but Artemis tells her "You stood your ground" (Ch16).
- **Burdened and lonely.** Percy sees in her eyes "the weariness of years she never got to live out. The loneliness she carried, even with Nico beside her" (Ch16).
- **Decisive under her own terms.** She asks for time ("I need to think"), then stands "taller, brushing her brother's words aside with trembling dignity" (Ch16).
- **Quickly hardening.** She keeps arrows nocked in San Francisco, scans the shop shelves, and keeps watch (Ch17).
- **Wry, plain-spoken observer.** "Morning. You look… rough?" On Eleni's jargon: "This is like watching someone speak English but also not English at all." (Ch18)
- **Practical and kind.** She points the injured Percy toward the Roman healers (Ch18).
- **Adaptive in battle.** Her untrained arrows "did not arc properly, so she compensated without thinking, releasing low, fast shots" (Ch19).

## Voice & Speech Patterns
She speaks quiet, ordinary modern English and makes short, slightly wry remarks. She is not archaic like Zoë and does not swear on-page. She calls Percy by name.

## Backstory
- The story follows canon: she and Nico were students at Westover Hall, hunted by Dr. Thorn, the Manticore (Ch15).
- Canon background, not stated in the story: born to Hades and Maria di Angelo before World War II and kept for decades in the Lotus Casino.
- **Author's vault note (not yet in text):** the vault's Lotus Hotel note says Hades used the Lotus to slow time for Nico and Bianca. Its Council of Olympic Sentinels note lists a Bianca on **Strike team Typhan**, which implies that in Percy's original timeline she survived and served under him. The vault's Bianca card ("Deceased Hunters of Artemis… Shortest tenure under Artemis") is canon-baseline status and does not apply to this story (C21).

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch15:** Grover calls Percy to Westover. In the clearing Thorn holds the di Angelo "twins" and Bianca looks panicked. Thorn lunges at them: "But they die first today!"
- **Ch16:**
  - Percy's surge destroys Thorn, and the Hunters arrive.
  - By the bonfire, Artemis praises Bianca and offers her the Hunt: "You'd never grow older, you'd never be powerless again." Bianca asks for time while Nico protests. She accepts, implied by the later chapters.
  - Bianca and Nico ride Apollo's sun-bus to camp.
  - After Artemis is taken, Zoë brings only herself and Bianca on the quest, with Percy, Grover and Eleni.
- **Ch17:**
  - She marches west, alert. She witnesses the encounters with Ammit-Sekhem, Sariel and Manannán, Asmodeus's fortress and the Hellway.
  - In Flamel's shop she scans the shelves. She is introduced in New Rome as a daughter of Pluto and crosses the Little Tiber (the "Mini Tiber" in the text) with Zoë and Grover.
- **Ch18:**
  - She watches Percy's three duels from the praetorian box.
  - The next morning she sees Zoë's knife cut him and suggests the healers.
  - In the Roman command tent she whispers her verdict on Eleni's jargon.
- **Ch19:** She shoots from behind the Roman line at Othrys ("Bianca stood back from the line, bow singing"); later she and Grover "remain below… as they hold the line" while the others reach the summit. Artemis teleports the questers clear of the blast. She survives the quest.
