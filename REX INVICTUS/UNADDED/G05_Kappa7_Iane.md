## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
Only one Iane exists. Do not confuse her with her twin brother **Ione** (`G06_Kappa7_Ione.md`), who is male. The names differ by one letter and the text sometimes blurs them. Iane is the sister: seated cross-legged, eyes closed, bossy. See `G09_Kappa7_Ekdromoi_Squad_Overview.md`.

## Appearance
- Ch20: sits cross-legged and perfectly still, eyes closed, with thin dim violet lines tracing across the deck beneath her boots. She wears the Hexenlaufgerät rune boots.
- Ch21: after injecting stimulants her fingers tremble and her muscles coil "as if preloaded".
- Ch22: unarmored in a bolted chair, her throat bearing a faint, perfectly healed line. Her expression is "completely unreadable" and she stays almost perfectly motionless.

## Personality
- **Precise and pedantic.** "You are not 'cheating.' You are forcing a localized spatial collapse—" (Ch20). She corrects her brother's output in real time.
- **Bossy and short-tempered with her twin.** "It will not remain within tolerance if you continue feeding at that rate, idiot!" She claims credit for his corrections ("Because I told you to").
- **Crude when provoked.** She gives Merek the finger and calls him "retarded" (Ch20).
- **Studious.** She recites the history of the Arctic Resource War and Alaska Hill "like someone reciting a text she had been made to memorize" (Ch20, Ch22).
- **Ruthless.** With one arm useless and her ribs shattered, she drags herself up and shoots Eleni in the head (Ch21).
- **Cool and judgmental under interrogation.** She calls Percy "déspota" and tells him to stop behaving like a despot. She dismisses his garbled sentence: "That sentence was atrocious." She twice kicks Neumond under the table for oversharing (Ch22).
- **Practical once allied.** She helps Eleni refit Neumond's wings (Ch22).

## Voice & Speech Patterns
- Formal and clipped, rarely using contractions. When annoyed she slips into bluntness.
- She uses the Greek "déspota" ("master"/"lord", which Percy hears as "despot") for Percy.

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Hexenlaufgerät boots**, in full "Hexenlaufgerät Erweiterte Thaumaturgische Bewegungsplattform für Runische Vorfeldkonstruktion und Kontinuierliche Glyphenvernetzungseinheit", "Special issue from the upper command" (Ch20). They:
  - etch runes wherever she shifts weight;
  - build stepping constructs, and with Ione a vast shared anti-gravity runic array for freefall;
  - draw magma-red teleport circles.
- With Ione she performs a teleport invocation. The text gives the chant to "The Hecate legacies" without a single speaker: "This land is not of mine, and I shall feed the thirsty earth with my flesh." … "And everything that walks upon this land shall be my ally." It produces a blue Delta symbol, the Labyrinth's mark (Ch20).
- **Monitoring.** She tracks her brother's output in "Parzivals per second". Above 6 they become visible to Gaian-age ELINT (Ch20).
- **Constructs.** In the Labyrinth her constructs solidify the path and spread weight across the floor (Ch21).
- **Equipment.** Her armor emits smoke and chaff when hit (Ch21). She carries a MORROW-IX rifle with QVARTUS rounds, Divine Stimulants and a perfluorocarbon breathing fluid kit.

## Backstory
She is a Hecate legacy and a member of the cell AtRep chose for Operation Anabasis-105. Like every Imperial cadet she learned the Battle of Alaska Hill in her first year (Ch22).

- Author's vault note (not yet in text): the vault's Ekdromoi note makes Ione and Iane the cell's **Arcanist Pair**, "Hecate twins" who handle runic platforms, smoke, chaff and wards (consistent with her smoke-and-chaff armor in Ch21). Hecate lines make up about 9% of Ekdromoi in the vault's lineage table. The vault also says Ekdromoi are selected by AtRep without application.

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch20.** She silences Ione's questions. She navigates the drop, polices his rune output and builds the descent array. She explains why AtRep was born in Alaska, and with Ione opens the rune-gate.
- **Ch21.** She observes the Labyrinth's layered Roman stonework and solidifies the final path. In the fight, Eleni's pressure pulse collapses her ribs and throws her into a crater. She shoots Eleni through the side of the head. Percy's ice needle pierces her throat; he holds her "severed neck" together with an ice cast and later reconstructs it.
- **Ch22.**
  - She probes how Percy knows classified ammunition. She calls him "déspota", deduces "you have enough augments to host a Watchpoint", and recites the Alaska Hill history.
  - She warns that Command dispatches retrieval at the 36-hour mark and describes the size of a UOE carrier.
  - She kicks Neumond twice and flinches at Apollo's grief-light.
  - She helps Eleni refit Neumond's wings and kneels to the Lord Imperator.

## Continuity Notes
- **Ch20 cloaked fifth woman** (see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C24). Ch20 mentions a quiet "fifth" woman whose cloak "lagged half a second behind movement" ("It's all overdesigned"). She is **not** Iane: Iane ("the Hecate legacy … seated cross-legged") is described separately in the same scene. The author's Plot Threads lists the cloaked woman as an unresolved loose end. Lagging semi-autonomous cloaks are standard Ekdromoi kit (author's vault). See `G09`.
- **Injuries.** Ch21: Eleni's pressure pulse collapses Iane's ribs ("ribs shattered", one arm useless) and Percy's ice needle goes through her throat. Ch22 narration gives Ione the "several ribs". The Watchpoint lists Iane only as "tracheal trauma, repaired"; her crushed ribs (Ch21) are absent from it.
