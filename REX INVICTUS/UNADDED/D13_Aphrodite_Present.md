## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **This file:** the present-day goddess. On-page she has exactly one described beat: at the Ch19 council, "Aphrodite was smiling at nothing in particular, which somehow felt worse."
- **Not** "Mother Majesty", the far-future, cyber-augmented, veiled Empress of the Dove's Fleet in Interlude I (Ch23). That Aphrodite has a neural wire that changes colour with her mood, calls everyone "my darlings", calls Artemis "Sister-in-law", insists that four million names be read aloud by a hundred of her choristers, and crushes Porphyrion with a dreadnought. See `E05_Aphrodite_Future_Mother_Majesty.md`.
- **Not** "The Heartmaker" of the Ch2 Anadyomene Belt, whose daughter Melina Callisto ("daughter of Aphrodite" in the text; 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md S3) rams Eleni's ship in a Terra Concrematum. Also covered by `E05_Aphrodite_Future_Mother_Majesty.md` and `G12_Rex_Imperator_II_Cast.md`.
- **Not** the old-timeline Aphrodite of ruined Olympus Primaris: "Aphrodite, with colors so faded it could pass as greyscale" (Ch1).
- **Knowledge boundary:** She has heard the Ch19 and Ch22 councils. Her smile at Ch19 may imply she perceives the Percy–Artemis bond before others do, but the text does not say so.
