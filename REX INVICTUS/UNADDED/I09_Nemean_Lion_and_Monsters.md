## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
All of these are **present-timeline** creatures. Percy remembers each one from his original life, and he keeps noticing differences ("He didn't remember that part," Ch14; "Something about this reality had changed slightly").

## Appearance
- **Nemean Lion:** not seen directly. Eleni only senses it.
- **Polyphemus:** wears the Golden Fleece "like a cloak"; carries a stone club; has a voice "jagged, rusted with age and madness."
- **The Kraken:** "a great eye blinked up at them from the water."
- **CSS *Birmingham*:** "reanimated and bristling with Confederate undead," whose crew watch "with empty sockets."
- **Hesperides:** seen only as the one obliterated "mid-flight," and then their grove.

## Personality
- **Polyphemus:** gluttonous and gleeful ("Oh, happy day").
- **The Sirens:** cruelly intimate. "They sang with memories. With regrets."
- **Monster-camp dracaena:** sing-song and greedy, then terrified.
- **The Kraken:** watchful and silent.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

- "What do we have here~?, a lone demigod? A brilliant feast just offered itself to us." (dracaena, Ch3)
- "Young one, it seems you possess some skill. It will be no match for-" (dracaena, Ch3)

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Polyphemus:** the Fleece heals him: "Most injuries get healed instantly."
- **Sirens:** they peel open "your heart." Percy resists because "Lycaon's heart throbbed, its divine origins helping him."
- **Hellhounds:** fiery breath, each "the size of a desk" (Ch3); later ones are "the size of trucks" (Ch22).
- **Dracaena:** "plethegonic, fiery blood."

## Backstory (canon baseline, not story)
In canon *The Sea of Monsters*, Polyphemus survives and the Fleece hangs on an oak tree. In *The Titan's Curse*, the Nemean Lion attacks at the Smithsonian's Air and Space Museum, Ladon guards the Hesperides' garden, and Zoë dies fulfilling "one shall perish by a parent's hand."

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch3: Labyrinth monster camp.** A cyclops, several hellhounds and a dracaena plan an attack on "a few demigods and a satyr." Percy kills them all and burns the survivors as offerings (see `I08_Labyrinth_of_Daedalus.md`). The cyclops is presumably where the Eye that went to Asmodeus came from, though this is not stated.
- **Ch13: the Colchis bull.** Clarisse's armor "still smoked charcoal black" from fighting one outside camp. Percy predicts Clarisse's route: the CSS *Birmingham*, which "The God of War will raise," then Circe's Island, Scylla and Charybdis, and Polyphemus's island.
- **Ch14: the Sea of Monsters.**
  - Percy sees "his father's Kraken."
  - Annabeth insists on hearing the Sirens: "I have to see… I have to know." She dives into the sand. Then the scene cuts.
  - The *Birmingham* lies anchored off Polyphemus's island. Riptide transforms in its hold.
  - The fight: Riptide shaves the club and pierces the Fleece. Grover lets out a goat-call, and Percy drives the blade into Polyphemus's shoulder. The Fleece rejects Annabeth, reverses its healing, and Polyphemus "collapsed, his body swallowed by the island's own enchantments," leaving a "golden and powdery patch of earth." He is **dead or dissolved**.
  - The Fleece accepts Percy.
- **Ch16: the Nemean Lion.** Eleni senses it in the Smithsonian with "The General," probably Atlas (`E16_Atlas.md`). The quest never engages it on the page.
- **Ch19: the Hesperides.** After Artemis pins him with her hunting spear, Atlas wrenches free and his own wild bolt "obliterates a Hesperide mid-flight," fulfilling "one shall perish by a parent's hand," so Zoë survives. The Othrys blast then vaporizes the rest and their grove: "The Hesperides never had time to scream." **Ladon is never named in the text.**
- **Ch19: the Othrys horde.** The Princess Andromeda unloads hellhounds, dracaenae, empousai and cyclopes against the legion.
- **Ch22: the Long Island horde.** Snake-women, dog-headed creatures, giants, truck-sized hellhounds, sea serpents, harpies, stymphalian birds and telkhines come off Luke's ship. Most are destroyed by Imperial ordnance, by Poseidon, and by Huitzilopochtli's red sun.

## Relationships

## Continuity Notes
- Polyphemus wearing the Fleece is AU (Percy: "He didn't remember that part").
- No Tyson appears, and Annabeth and Percy reach the island without Clarisse fighting beside them.
- The Hesperide death, rather than Zoë's, changes the prophecy's outcome. The Hesperide is killed by **Atlas's own bolt**, after Artemis spears him (`00a_CANON_RULINGS.md` C19).
- **Zoë's parentage:** Zoë is Atlas's daughter (vault, consistent with canon; `00a_CANON_RULINGS.md` C28). She is alive in this story; the vault's "deceased" status is canon baseline only (C21).
- **Hellhound sizes:** "the size of a desk" (Ch3), "the size of trucks" (Ch22) and "the size of a pickup truck" (Ch22) are all in the text.
