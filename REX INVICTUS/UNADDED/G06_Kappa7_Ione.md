## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
Only one Ione exists. **He is male**, not the female twin **Iane** (`G05_Kappa7_Iane.md`). He is the one who says "sis". See `G09_Kappa7_Ekdromoi_Squad_Overview.md`.

## Appearance
- Helmeted in the field. His eyes glow with magesight when he tracks targets (Ch21).
- He wears the Hexenlaufgerät boots, which brand "sizzling runes into the dusty marble" (Ch21).
- In Ch22 his arms are secured behind the chair and his torso is "wrapped in layers of soaked medical gauze". His eyes keep drifting to the confiscated equipment.

## Personality

  - "Now, tell me why we have this gear again."
  - "Feels like cheating."
  - "So we just landed in the middle of buttfuck nowhere, I take it?"
  - "Good. I was worried we'd actually hit something important."
- **Likes style.** "Finally, someone with taste." (agreeing that the gear is "overdesigned").

- **Distracted by gear.** He keeps glancing at the containment fields (Ch22).
- **Naively literal about the past.** "Is that what they're called?" (about NASA).

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Hexenlaufgerät rune boots.** They build arrays with Iane, including the freefall anti-gravity circle and the teleport gate. Iane warns him that he is "approaching threshold output on your third chain" (her augur reads 5 Parzivals per second; above 6 they become visible to Gaian-age ELINT) (Ch20).
- **Magesight.** His glowing eyes track enemies through Neumond's ECM smoke (Ch21).
- **Gear.** He injects Divine Stimulants, carries a MORROW-IX rifle, and has perfluorocarbon breathing fluid.

## Backstory
He is a Hecate legacy, Iane's twin, selected with Kappa-Seven by AtRep for Operation Anabasis-105 (Ch20, Ch22).

- Author's vault note (not yet in text): the vault's Ekdromoi note pairs Ione and Iane as the cell's **Arcanist Pair** (runic platforms, smoke, chaff, wards). A non-canon vault vignette (Annex 15370) shows Ione beside Merek against cultists with a runic lattice; there Ione is clipped ("Then stop blinking"). Use it only as a voice reference.

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch20.** He questions their gear and teases his sister about "cheating". He co-builds the descent array and nearly overloads his chain. He co-casts the rune-gate, and introduces the Alaska Hill topic.
- **Ch21.** He reinforces the Labyrinth floor with runes. In Alaska he stims and tracks Percy and Eleni with magesight. Eleni's collapsing pressure crushes him into the permafrost, his armor holds "barely, but his spine did not". Percy stabilises him.
- **Ch22.** In the interrogation he tests Percy's knowledge and describes waking on Mars with no Watchpoint handshake. He asks about NASA, sits through the battle and kneels to the Lord Imperator.

## Continuity Notes
- **Injuries disagree.** Ch21: Ione's armor held, "barely, but his spine did not". Ch22 narration: his torso gauze covers the place "where Eleni's pressure had broken several ribs". Watchpoint: "Operators IONE, MEREK: fractures, repaired." In Ch21 it was Iane whose ribs were crushed, and her ribs are absent from the Watchpoint list.
- **Who said "It's all overdesigned"?** The cloaked "fifth" woman in Ch20 (see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C24): unresolved (author loose end), and **not** Iane, who is described separately in the same scene. Ione is the one who agrees with her ("Finally, someone with taste.").
- **The rune-gate chant** ("This land is not of mine…") is given to "The Hecate legacies" jointly in Ch20; no single speaker is tagged.
