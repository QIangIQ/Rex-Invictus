## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **This file:** the present-day child at Westover Hall.
- **Percy's memory of an older Nico (original timeline):** Percy recalls the boy's "naive, wide eyes that he had lost in the many later years of service for the pantheon" (Ch 15). This means that in Percy's original life Nico grew up and served the gods for a long time. That older Nico never appears. Only Percy's knowledge of him exists.
- **Not to be confused with:** Bianca di Angelo (present, `C08_Bianca_di_Angelo_Present.md`) or the godlike far-future "Bianca Di'Angelo" on Everest (`C09_Bianca_DiAngelo_FarFuture.md`). Nico has no far-future appearance.
- **Canon baseline (labelled canon, not story):** In *The Titan's Curse*, Nico is about ten, a Mythomagic-obsessed chatterbox, son of Hades, who blames Percy for Bianca's death and later becomes the Ghost King. In this AU, Bianca survives the quest (as of Ch 22), so Nico's canon grief arc may never happen.

## Appearance
"Nico's small face crumpled with confusion" (Ch 16). Otherwise undescribed. He is shaken and cold, warming by the Hunters' bonfire (Ch 16).

## Personality
Shown in very few lines:
- **Frightened but clinging to family.** He stands beside Bianca while she holds his shoulder (Ch 16).
- **Protests losing his sister.** "Nico's protests rang thin" as Bianca considers Artemis's offer (Ch 16).
- **Naive.** Percy's whole impression of him is the wide-eyed innocence he knows Nico will lose (Ch 15).
- He has no spoken lines in the text.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
No dialogue is quoted. His protests are only summarised ("Nico's protests rang thin", Ch 16). For roleplay, a scared, earnest, talkative kid voice fits canon, but label it as an extrapolation.

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
None shown. Bianca is described by Flamel as smelling of "Sulfur and Brimstone", which marks Hades/Pluto blood (Ch 17). Nico's own underworld powers do not appear.

## Backstory
Not given in the story. Artemis tells Bianca that she is "not bound to the world that has forgotten you", and "not chained to a brother who must always be your responsibility" (Ch 16). This hints at the canon backstory: the siblings were pulled out of time (the Lotus Hotel), and Bianca has been Nico's sole caretaker.

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch 15:** Grover Iris-messages Percy from Westover Hall in Maine. A Hunter scout reports "two demigods. Young. Likely unaware." Percy, Grover and Eleni follow Dr. Thorn, who has taken "the twins" into the snowy clearing. "Bianca looked panicked; Nico's naive, wide eyes." Thorn turns on the siblings: "But they die first today!"
- **Ch 16:** Percy's primal sea surge unmakes Thorn. Nico and Bianca are brought to the Hunters' bonfire. Artemis praises Bianca for standing her ground "with their brother at their side" and offers her a place in the Hunt. Nico's face crumples and his protests fail. Bianca asks for time but later joins. Apollo arrives, and "Nico and Bianca lingered uncertainly" before the Hunters board his transformed sun-bus. Percy is taken separately to Apollo's palace. Nico presumably goes to Camp Half-Blood, but the text never says.
- **Ch 17–23:** Not mentioned. Bianca leaves on the quest and survives the battle of Othrys (Ch 19).

## Continuity Notes
- The text calls Nico and Bianca "the twins" four times (Ch 15 ×3, including Percy's spoken "Let the twins go"; Ch 16 ×1). They are not twins in canon, and the story calls them "the rescued siblings" elsewhere (Ch 16). Quote "twins" only as the text's label; describe them as siblings (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C22).
- **Status.** Earlier drafts said he was presumed alive. Profiles write *fate unstated* (C20).
- His parentage is never stated directly for him.
