## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
Lucan Corvo exists only in the far-future UOE era. Nobody else shares his name. He is not related to the present-day Roman legion members of Ch 17–22 (`F17_Roman_Legion_Present.md`), though he serves in the far-future descendant of Reyna's Legio XII Fulminata (see `H01_Tullia_Ardens.md`). He knows the shear happened and saw the Grace's fleet. Nothing indicates he has heard the sealed verdict about the Lord Imperator.

## Appearance
"A lanky legacy of Zeus". He comes up the duckboards "with a data slate". His face is grey, like everyone's after 31 hours of watching the wound. During the bombardment he is "pressed against the trench wall with his hands over his ears."

## Personality
- **Nervous but competent.** He relays orders precisely and hesitates before the strange ones ("There's more").
- **Calm in the moment of crisis.** His voice is "very calm" as he identifies Porphyrion: "Giant … Class Primus. Augur identifies it as... Olympus. It's Porphyrion."
- **Easily awed.** At the landing scholars he says "Olympus … Look at them all." At the barrage he says "Overkill … Olympus, this is overkill—"
- **Cracks afterwards.** His laugh is "a thin, hysterical sound" and he can't stop: "Eleven years! … She put him in the ground for eleven years!"
- **Honest about his limits.** "I've been in the Salient for six years and I've never seen anything like that."
- He swears by "Olympus".

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Formal with his superior ("Primus Pilus", "Yes, Primus Pilus"). He reads orders aloud in report style and lapses into breathless exclamation under stress.
- "Relay from Sector Command. We're to hold this line." (Ch 23)
- "The Grace, Primus Pilus … They're sending the Aphrodite's Grace." (Ch 23)
- "Olympus … Look at them all." (Ch 23)
- "Giant … Class Primus. Augur identifies it as... Olympus. It's Porphyrion." (Ch 23)
- "Overkill … Olympus, this is overkill—" (Ch 23)
- "Eleven years! … She put him in the ground for eleven years!" (Ch 23)
- "How long for, Primus Pilus?" (Ch 23)

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
Data slate and net comms; he reads the augur identification. As a legacy of Zeus he may have minor divine blood, but no ability is shown.

## Backstory
Six years in the Salient. Nothing else is given.

## Story Arc So Far
**Ch 23:** He brings Sector Command's orders (hold, no patrols past 3 km, prepare for a detachment) and then the news that the *Aphrodite's Grace* is coming. He watches the scholars land and is told to keep his eyes on the ridges. When the monsters attack he identifies Porphyrion, cowers through the overkill barrage, then laughs hysterically after the *Sovereign's Glory* buries the Giant. Tullia: "You'll see it again … if you live long enough." At the end he receives the order to dig permanent, deep positions and asks how long. He gets the answer "Until they come home."

## Relationships
- **Tullia Ardens** (`H01_Tullia_Ardens.md`): his commander and anchor. He brings her the news; she keeps him steady.
- **Stephanie Callidora** (`H06_Bulwarks_and_Spears.md`): the object of his awe ("She put him in the ground").
- The Grace's fleet and scholars: he is a wide-eyed spectator.

Whether he learns of the sealed verdict. His Zeus-legacy line is unexplored.

## Continuity Notes
No contradictions within Ch 23. His surname "Corvo" (Italian for crow) echoes the Roman-flavoured naming of the Fulminata.
- The wound he watches is a sphere eleven kilometres in radius (Ch 23; 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C27). It swallowed Marien Pyrrhus's Reserve Echelon, not Percy (C7).
- "Fourteen thousand years" is the era distance per Ch 23 and the author's dating (shear ~UOE 16231); see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R3.
