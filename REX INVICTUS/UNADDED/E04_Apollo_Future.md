## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **This file:** future Apollo, known mostly through institutions and lore.
- **Present-day Apollo** is the on-page comic-relief "big bro". He takes Percy to a bath, scans Percy's nanomachines in BIOMECHANICS 110, heals the Ekdromoi, grieves Michael Yew, and concedes the red dawn "isn't" his sun when Hrafn points it out (Ch15–22; "not thine Apollo's sun" is Hrafn's line, 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C10). See `D04_Apollo_Present.md`.
- **Link between the two:** in Ch22 present Apollo asks "And my future self signs off on that?", and is told "Your future self designed its medical tolerances, milord", referring to the Imperial suppression lock. This is the one fact about *future* Apollo given in a present-day scene; it is a statement by a future-era speaker about this version, so it may be used here. Nothing else from the present-day scenes applies to this file (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C33).
- **What future Apollo knows:** the War of Princes and the loss of his "Sun-Born" legions; the Empire's medical systems. There is no evidence he knows of Percy's time travel.

## Appearance
- On-page he only provides Artemis's gold-weave blindfold and "repaired the hems of his robe" after Zeus storms out (Ch5).
- His imagery is a kilometre-high stone statue with a bow on the prows of AnnChase cruisers (Ch23).

## Personality
- **Light, unbothered comic presence (Ch5):** "Aaaaand he's gone." The line sums up a council that tolerates Zeus's tantrums.
- **Helpful to his twin:** he lends Artemis the blindfold for her surveillance scan (Ch5).
- **Seen by peers as wanting to prove himself:** Poseidon: "Apollo sent them there to show us that he actually had some power to prove other than songs and sex" (Ch15). This is how Poseidon sees him, not Apollo's own words.
- **A patron who loses many:** the "last handful of Sun-Born… screamed the Orchestras of Hate" at the end of the War of Princes, and "Many of Apollo's sons fell" (Ch15).

## Voice & Speech Patterns
There is only one on-page future line. It is casual and drawled, with exaggerated vowels, consistent with the present-day Apollo's breezy tone.
- "Aaaaand he's gone." (Ch5)

For roleplay, extrapolate from the present-day voice (`D04_Apollo_Present.md`) but make him older and more Imperial. Flag any extrapolation as such.

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Light and sight:** his blindfold lets Artemis scan through Luna's Iris cameras (Ch5).
- **Imperial assets:**
  - **Solar Auxilia:** the security fleet orbiting Gaia and the solar system (Ch12, Ch13).
  - **Apollonian Hospitalier Complex:** a fortress-planet facility built of "Hephestian Ferrocrete reinforced with polymerized stygian mesh". It holds regeneration pods with 5% dilute nectar, and Percy's clone was stored there ("Classified experiments (Low-quality)", Ch10).
  - **Apollonian aedificators:** paid medic-technicians ("If they charge me, those drachamae… are coming out of YOUR pocket", Ch9).
  - **Regional Apollonian Command:** receives logistics projections (Ch14).
  - **Four thousand Apollonian medics** come with Aphrodite's fleet (Ch23). Apollonian medics on the Tartarus front (Ch19).
- **Bloodlines:**
  - Sun-Born (his sons).
  - Legacies such as Volley-Lead Cerelli of the Psiloi orbital marksmen ("Apollonian or Artemisian, or both… the Empire's archers", Ch22).
  - The Kettle Twins (Apollo + Demeter, Ch23).
  - Atlanta of the future Hunt, "a daughter of Apollo and the Hunt's apothecary" (Ch23). (Spelling: **Atlanta**, not "Atalanta", which refers only to the ancient heroine; 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md S2.)
- **Formerly his:** the AnnChase-Class battleship *The Eye of Light* (AC-09-1642), later captured by the Hollow Crescent (Ch15).
- **Artisans:** "the skilled artisans of Phoebus Apollo" carved the Perseus mural on the *Divine Crusader* over thousands of hours (Ch2).
- **Medical design:** designed the medical tolerances of the Ekdromoi suppression lock (Ch22).

## Backstory
- **War of Princes:** his last legions were "annihilated by the Princes". A lone blonde, imperial-gold-clad Legacy kept his Roman standard upright as he was killed: "The War-Banner would never be allowed to fall while one of the Suns yet lived" (Poseidon, Ch15).
- **Pre-Empire:** no future-era backstory is given beyond canon.
- **Author's vault note (not yet in text):** his lost colonists became the **Sunborne Cult** (dated 3045.10.3.4), led by the "Lord of Coming Dawn", with a sister organisation, the **Blackstar Crucible**. (The vault's Sunborne Cult is not the same as the text's "Sun-Born", his sons.) In the War of Princes an Apollonian colonial fleet killed the Shinto Warden Yurei Tensei with a solar-flare cannonade (16216). He is "currently seeking a relationship with Hathor" (future era, vault only). PLANNED (author intent): Solon Themis's tomb on Voss I lies "waiting to be revived by Apollo eventually". His UOE Unified Rank is #131.

## Continuity Notes
- Apollo's crude jokes and his "big bro" or "divine brother-in-law" lines (Ch16–22) all come from the present-day god. Don't attribute them to the future Apollo, whose on-page dialogue is one line.
- Ch2 has Melina invoke "any star in the Apollo", which seems to be a truncated phrase (perhaps a star system or formation named for Apollo). Its meaning is unclear.
- Ch19: "Apollolonians" [sic] (typo for Apollonians).
- Spelling of the Hunter: **Atlanta** (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md S2).
- "Not thine Apollo's sun" (Ch22) is **Hrafn's** line to the present Apollo (C10).
