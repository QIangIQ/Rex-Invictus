- The **Arenberg** name also appears in the author's Ch 2 OC list, which names "Lysis Arenberg" with the notes "Horse of Arenberg" [sic; House] and "Legacy of Athena, Ares, Zeus, Aphrodite", so a family is implied. Author's vault: the **House of Arenberg** includes Virre, Lysis, Kleon, Orran and Astrid.
- **The Ch 13 Twins.** The text never names them ("Twin voices snarled", "The Twins hesitated"). The author's vault calls Orran and Virre the **Arenberg Twins** ("They speak in tandem"), places them with the Hollow Crescent around Him Within the Moon, and the author's Plot Threads describes "A cult on Luna with the Arenberg twins". So the Ch 13 Twins are very probably Orran and Virre (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C23). This is author intent, not page fact.
- **Stephanos Pyrrhus** (patron of the Titans; Ch 12 spells it "Pyrrus") has his own file: `G03_Stephanos_Pyrrhus.md`. Future **Thalia** and **Artemis** at Luna appear in `C07_Thalia_Grace_Future_Lieutenant.md` and `C02_Artemis_Future_The_Director.md`. **Him Within the Moon** is in `A05_Him_Within_the_Moon.md`. The young Hunters **Sylvi Morn** and **Kaia Telan** (Ch 13) are in `C12_Hunters_of_Artemis_Roster.md`. **Commander Thaleia** (Ilon's superior) is in `H16_Callistus_Narrator_and_Tartarus_Front_Soldiers.md`.

## Appearance
- **Virre:** fast enough to be "like ghosts in sprint". She wields a knife named **Backstab**, "flashing like a silver comet". A Hunter survivor's description: "Like Artemis herself, if she was pissed off."
- **Orran:** walks unhurried through artillery barrages and a frontal bunker, dragging a sword named **Severance**, "big as a damn anti-tank rifle… like a lead weight", with "eyes glowing dull green."
- **Author's vault note (not yet in text):** Orran is nearly seven feet tall, slender, scarred, with sharp cheekbones, close-cropped dark hair and glowing green eyes; the vault calls Severance "an oversized fencing rapier" (the text's anti-tank-rifle-sized blade takes precedence). Virre is shorter and physically stronger, paper-pale, with long midnight-black hair and green eyes; her bayonet knife Backstab glows faintly with Artemis' Blessing, and she can move at up to 500 m/s.
- **Hollow Crescent forces:** "angular, bristling" warships "chained together by strange materials"; warriors who are "a fusion of mortals, twisted demigods, and screaming zealots".
- **Tony:** a standard-issue UOE helmet and greatcoat.
- **Cyria:** an azure-blue uniform; "easy on the eyes".
- **Gamma Peacekeeper (TW-SP-HMMR):** a skyscraper-high titan with a pyramid-like head, a jet-black gloss frame, and azure banners on its thin shoulders bearing the icon of a Poseidon demigod's guard. **Lambda Baron (TW-SP-CLVR):** carries "ominous weapons and huge melee weapons".
- **Cael Varros:** sits in a Chair of Command (model Aureus XII); a data strip prints from his right forearm.

## Personality
- **Virre:** silent, joyful violence. "Her laugh was barely audible." Even cultists fear her: "I don't like the way she looks at us. Like she's hungry."
- **Orran:** silent and inexorable. "He said nothing. He didn't need to."
- **The Twins:** fanatical, possessive and archaic ("How dare thee!"). They take Him's rejection as corruption: "Poisoned by the Moon."
- **Tony:** relaxed, irreverent, blunt and profane. She openly dislikes Cyria and cheers Xilo's death.
- **Cyria:** pompous, insecure and a liar ("I have long been entrusted with certain aspects of the Master's plans … that men, and women, such as yourself are only now being made aware of"; the narration adds that "Cyria lied"). He considers reporting troops for disrespect.
- **Cael Varros:** numb and weary, a bureaucrat of atrocity. "He would have felt bad, if only this was not the first time." He delegates everything worth doing.
- **Logos Petronius-11:** efficient, wordless obedience. "No motion wasted. No argument."
- **Ilon:** cynical realist. "Because we don't have the reserves, more like."

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Arenbergs:** superhuman speed and lethality (Virre); imperviousness to artillery (Orran); signature named weapons (Backstab, Severance). They have no Olympus clearance marks or designations ("Not human"), which suggests something outside the Imperial system.
- Author's vault note (not yet in text): the twins' power is a **Power Leech** (they absorb a dead being's experience and strength), and their aim is to harvest the divine power of Him Within the Moon. "They speak in tandem. Or not at all."
- **Hollow Crescent:** a fleet with dark-energy lances and wide-beam plasma, teleportation breaches, and psychic/divine broadcast chants. They hold the captured *Eye of Light* (Ch 15) for 32 years.
- **Tony:** commands 300,000 troops of the 32nd; test-fires orbital guns.
- **Cael Varros:** signs Bloodline Draft requisitions (firstborn, myth-quotient ≥0.9) and artificial-womb births (900 units with a ~13.5-year lifespan, a 3.4-year tactical utility span, no creative module, "command Latinic" only), and can authorise "cognitive excision". His seal reads "Continuity Maintained".

## Backstory
- **Author's vault note (not yet in text), the Arenberg twins:** rogue descendants of the House of Arenberg (with Lysis, Kleon and Astrid; Kleon and Astrid were Princes' Naval Commanders Class I in the War of Princes). Orran is the elder, Virre the younger. The vault calls them "half-siblings to Percy Jackson" (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C23; odd vault wording, not on the page). They are **Alpha-Class Traitors** (#2/#3, after Luke) for murdering "a full regiment of Hunters of Artemis and Hounds of Artemis", carry a joint **280,000-drachmae** bounty (1 drachma ≈ US$1,000), and rank **Virre #74, Orran #75** in the UOE Unified **Alltime** Rankings, the only criminals in the double digits. That list is distinct from the text's "Rank 1 and 2 on the current active bounties". The vault heavily implies cannibalism ("good eating in Virre Arenberg's barracks"). PLANNED (author brainstorm): a Hollow Crescent slaughter-march to Everest and the "Low Road" through Tartarus; the twins are pure fanatics.
- **Luna-Secundus** is a fortress-satellite in Luna's outer orbit, held by the Hunters of Artemis for 17 months against the Hollow Crescent, who "were searching for something in Luna", meaning Him Within the Moon.
- **The Hollow Crescent** venerate Perseus as "Rex Deus", "Moonbound", "The One Son", "The Tidebreaker", "The Gentle Storm", "The Not-God-Who-Walked", "the Everfather of Demigods". They want him "risen" to free them "from the uncaring gods".
- **Xilo** was removed by "an completely accidental failure of his Repertoire infusers" (an implied assassination). Oberon was "dealt with". Poseidon is purging disloyal commanders as he returns "back in action".
- **Quarterdeck 19** is a Dionysian logistics station above Nysa IX, one of 6,000, with "no green" in its failure colour-coding.

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch 12, Orbital Platform One:** Cyria brings Tony orders from "the Master" (Poseidon): redeploy the 32nd to Orbital Platform One within eleven hours, replace Xilo, and make first contact with the approaching squadron (Minerva's ships). Tony meets the Titans of "Stephanos Pyrrus' [sic] Honour Guard" (themselves "recently came back from a border skirmish") and finds them "detached and distracted. Underwhelming."
- **Ch 12, Luna-Secundus:** the Arenbergs break the 17-month siege. Virre slaughters Trench-6, the 6th Moonwall and the shieldmaidens; a junior sister-augur reports "They don't have designations… Not human"; a scout recalls "Aren-something". Orran walks alone into the command tent, and the fortress cancels its SOS.
- **Ch 13, Luna:** the Hollow Crescent fleet bombards Luna and chants a summoning ("Rise, Perseus Jackson"). Him Within the Moon rises and **shields Artemis instead**, catches a dark-energy lance, raises a wall of celestial water and crumples a cruiser. The Twins rage: "He was Our Lord before you even cared."
- **Ch 14, Quarterdeck 19:** Cael Varros triages shortages, denies inhibitors, signs the womb-births and the Bloodline Draft, and tells Logos Petronius-11 to "Do nothing".
- **Ch 14, Lethe Sector:** Ilon reports Fulmen Watch failing and soldiers seeing faces in the fog.
