## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
There is a single version of him. Ch4 tells us the Ch3 Yosemite ambusher and the Ch4 escort are the same demon: "The Demon that first attacked Percy emerged from the shadow behind the throne." He has no future-era counterpart.

## Appearance
- **Ch4:** He takes shape from red mist as "a tall, imposing man with bright red skin, a singular horn protruding from above his left eye, and pitch black pupils." He is bare-chested, has clawed fingers, and his Daemonblood is "crystalline black." He grows larger when he wants to impress, and he can "summ[on] his battlegear" (Ch4, which reads "summed" [sic]). He hovers by sitting on "an invisible rock in the air." When he leaves, he turns to ash scattering on the wind. Signs that he is near: the temperature drops, the air smells of sulfur, and shadows deepen.
- **Ch17:** He appears as "a ripple of black smoke" that becomes "a familiar shape," hovering with "an impish tilt to his horned head." He leaves trails of black flame and his shadow moves "like smoke."

## Personality
- **Contemptuous at first, then appreciative.** He calls Percy "puny child" and "runt." Once Percy coldly orders the pack destroyed, he gives Percy "the first appreciative look all night" (Ch4).
- **Playful and sardonic, even while killing.** After tossing werewolf corpses into their own campfire, he turns and waves at Percy (Ch4).
- **Curious.** He asks about the heart exchange and the "Repository," and is told "That's classified, demon" (Ch4).
- **Holds grudges.** He rants about vampires: "Dracula! That Lucifer dammed lamprey! Curses!" (Ch4).
- **By Ch17, impish and theatrical.** He riddles, spins in the air, perches on a lamppost "that hadn't existed moments ago," and scatters pigeons for fun.
- **Quietly respectful.** "You've grown… Not much, but enough to remember" (Ch17). He gives free advice as a "parting gift."

## Voice & Speech Patterns
His speech is archaic and formal on duty ("Open thy mouth," "It shall be done my liege") and teasing and riddling off it. Percy is "Greek," "runt," "demigod" or "small humani." He doesn't swear, though he does curse by Lucifer.
- "You have called, puny child, and I have answered" (Ch4)
- "Barbaric bunch aren't they? Almost as bad as you, Greek." (Ch4)
- "What could a Greek demigod of the sea possibly need a werewolf heart for?" (Ch4)
- "I'll be watching for a bit" (Ch4)
- "Well, well, if it isn't the Greek demigod of Poseidon. Still playing hero, I see." (Ch17)
- "Thoughts are like fish!" … "they slip away if you blink!" (Ch17)
- "Find the Alchemist, before anything… unpleasant begins." (Ch17)
- "Remember, demigods: some shadows are friendly, some… merely polite." (Ch17)

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Speed.** He moves faster than Percy's young senses can follow (the "*thwip*" in Ch3).
- **Corrosive demonic energy.** A strike from him infects a body with demonic energy that erodes it. Holy water, Regen-Ex, or the Répertoire's absorption cures it (Ch3).
- **Teleportation by blood.** Whoever swallows his Daemonblood turns to ash and arrives before Asmodeus (Ch4).
- **Senses immortality.** He can smell the Hunters coming: "They stink with immortality" (Ch4).
- **Raw destruction.** He kills the werewolves outside the den and collapses the den in a way that looked "as if someone had detonated a high-yield explosive inside" (Ch4).
- **Fast butchery.** He removes the heart and pelt "in mere moments" and could skin a corpse "in a few moments" (Ch4).
- **Movement.** He flies, travels through shadow and smoke, and guides others along the Hellway (Ch17).

## Backstory
Nothing is revealed beyond his service to Asmodeus. *Name note (not story):* a *condottiero* is a Renaissance Italian mercenary captain.
- **Author's vault note (not yet in text):** the author's ASSETS/IDEAS note lists "Condottiero" beside "Disciples of the lesser keys" and "Major demons underneath lesser demon kings". This matches the text's "one of the Disciples of the Lesser Keys, under great lord Asmodeus" (Ch4) and places Disciples as major demons serving the demon kings. Nothing else about him is in the vault.

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch3:** He ambushes Percy near the Yosemite church. Percy coughs blood from demonic erosion and escapes back into the Labyrinth, which counts as Greek divine territory.
- **Ch4:** He answers Percy's bow, accepts the offer of the Eye of the Cyclops, and teleports him to Asmodeus with Daemonblood. Assigned as escort ("It appears that we shall be seeing each other, runt"), he kills the werewolves outside the den, collapses it, and brings Percy Lycaon's body. Percy asks for the heart. Condottiero warns that "about a dozen silver archers" are coming and Percy cries "Valkas!" He hands over the heart and pelt, turns to ash, and leaves the line "I'll be watching for a bit."
- **Ch17:** He reappears on the road west and leads the five questers to Asmodeus's fortress ("the 32nd is… in a good mood as of late, thanks to you"). He guides them through the Hellway to San Francisco, points them to "the Alchemist" (`I04_Nicholas_and_Perenelle_Flamel.md`), and leaves. Percy says: "We shall meet again."

## Relationships
- **Asmodeus:** loyal Disciple and his master's right hand.
- **Percy** (`A02_Percy_Reincarnated_Rex.md`): from would-be killer to wary ally. Percy trusts that he "will not break his master's word."
- **Eleni** (`G01_Eleni_Theodosia.md`): she distrusts him and seems relieved when he leaves.
- **Vampires (Dracula, "the blood leeches in nightingale"):** he despises them.
- **Lycaon's pack** (`I03_Lycaon.md`): his victims.

## Continuity Notes

- His name is spelled "Condotierro" once (Ch4); profiles use **Condottiero** (`00a_CANON_RULINGS.md` S13).
- Era: *present day* is c. 2021 per the author's timeline (R2). Percy is a young teen; the text calls him sixteen (Ch7, Ch13, Ch22), though the premise blurb says he returned as a twelve-year-old (R1).
