## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
These are far-future-only figures, the "Dionysian Division" of mercenaries under Hadrian Corporation (HadriCorp) contract. Dionysus is their sponsor ("his name was stamped on the payroll"). This is the future Dionysus (`E06_Dionysus_Future.md`), not present-day Mr. D. They share a suit operating system with AtRep (`B03_AtRep_Repertoire_du_Savoir_Athenien.md`): "Version: 9.341.Α (Deprecated)". They have no contact with Percy or Minerva.

## Appearance
- **Platoon:** 32 operators in power armor of "deep violet plates striped with gold filigree, decals of ivy curling over pauldrons". Each helmet carries a live uplink to a Dionysian holonet channel.
- **Serika:** mag-boots and an exo-frame that locks needles into her spine, with a nectar-gold pulse in her veins. Her suit's boot sequence flags "PARTIAL INTERFERENCE / SOURCE: EXTERNAL NEURAL STRESSOR" (Ch 18). Her suit is degraded: auxiliary coils at 14.7%, a damaged shoulder hardpoint, a gauntlet with haptic delay, a failing CPU (ARES Technica 3800).
- **Kallix:** an "immense" silhouette, "cruciform antennae folded along his back, reactor vents glowing faintly purple"; a "rasping", "mechanical and slow" voice filtered by his mask.

## Personality
- **Serika:**
  - Sardonic and stressed: heart rate 161 BPM, stress index "EXCEEDS SAFE THRESHOLD".
  - Contemptuous of the bureaucracy. "Patch it," she mutters at failing systems. "Ain't no way," at a 67,612-hour repair wait.
  - A resourceful improviser. She rewires her suit by hand with dripping tubes.
  - Defies the odds: she overrides AtRep's 22% penetration estimate and fires anyway.
  - Brave enough to tackle a Lambda-class priestess.
- **Kallix:**
  - Cold, pragmatic and unshakeable. "One casualty. Maintain focus."
  - Walks through the Miko's onslaught. Crude and blunt in briefing ("Gather up ladies").
  - Conscious of the audience: "Remember who's watching."
- **The platoon:** callous. They watch a comrade suffocate ("More for us."), and Juno's epitaph is "Pity, kid was pretty tolerable."

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
Power armor with neural mesh sync, adaptive plating, thrusters, mag-clamps and shoulder cannons; AtRep threat indexing and probability readouts that the operator can override; demolition charges; APCs on lunar-lander chassis (13 per squad, launched by magnetic rails); guided launchers. Fuel and ammunition allocation was raised from 25% to 75% because executives were watching. Kallix carries a rifle and a blade, and crushed the Miko's throat with his gauntlet.

## Backstory
"Most were legacies, descendants of a few minor gods, conscripted into the desire of profit," with "every breath… accounted for… every movement an statistic in some investor's spreadsheet."

## Story Arc So Far
**Ch 18 (future section):**
1. Briefing in a battlecruiser hangar overlooking the Traitor moon S3-R12-P1-M2, glassed earlier by a Hunters of Artemis orbital bombardment. Mission: destroy the flak emplacements of Orbital Artillery Belt 5 for the cameras.
2. APC launch. A trooper's seal fails and he is left to die, then thrown out the hatch. Juno destroys a turret and is killed. The lead APC is hit and spins out; only two of the three return to the hangar.
3. Tunnel breach to the core chamber. A **Miko** (Shinto shrine priestess, porcelain fox mask, LAMBDA-CLASS) appears: "You trespass upon a sanctum of Amaterasu." She kills several. Kallix walks through her attacks; Serika's overridden plasma shot breaks her barrier; Serika tackles her; Kallix crushes her throat. The charges blow and the platoon escapes.
4. On the *Dionysian Sovereign*, HadriCorp patrons treat it as a show ("Jolly good show. Small bite, though."). The patrons re-select the target to chase a fleeing Shinto vessel and refuse Admiral Hyette's request for a course change. Hyette then insists on engaging the moon directly, because the orbital Tian-Qiang batteries are straining the shields; the HadriCorp liaison refuses ("we're being paid to run down that heretical mothership") and threatens to make him "the public face of this fiasco". After Medusa squadron's report, the holo announces: "Increased divine resistance moonside. Raising kill-cost to 20 Drachamae [sic] per entity." Only then does the liaison relent: "New plan. Tell the Admiral to engage the moon."

## Continuity Notes
- The RDSA is misnamed once as "Repertoire du Sauveur Athénien".
- The text spells both "Dionysius" and "HardriCorp".
- The platoon is "thirty-two operators", but three APCs at 13 each would be 39.
- The kill-cost figure is verified in Ch 18: "Raising kill-cost to 20 Drachamae per entity" (spelled "Drachamae" in the text; the vault spells it the same way). It is announced by the tactical holo, not set by the patrons themselves.
- The CODE/rank figures are as printed; Serika's "UNIFIED RANK: 71,325,989,221" is from her suit's boot sequence.
- Admiral Hyette is the one who wants to engage the moon; the patrons and liaison hold out until the kill-cost rises.
