## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
This file covers **the original, old-timeline Percy as he was before Chaos's gamble**: the bitter, exhausted, millennia-old sentinel of Ch1 and the opening of Ch3, plus everything later chapters reveal about his original life.

- **vs. `A02_Percy_Reincarnated_Rex.md`:** Rex Percy is this same mind after it lands in his young-teen body in the present day (the Répertoire's Ch3 readout is stamped "2005, August 19th"; the author's timeline puts the present at c. 2021, see 00a R1–R2). From that moment he is a different version: a weak child's body, Lycaon's heart, Minerva in his blood, a new and diverging timeline, and a slow thaw out of A01's bitterness. A01 has *not* yet lived any of that. Anything from Ch3 onward that happens in the present day belongs to A02.
- **vs. `A03_Percy_Canon_OriginalSelf.md`:** Canon Percy is the original twelve-year-old whose personality the returning mind overwrote. A01 *was* that boy about 14,000 years earlier (00a R3; Percy's own Ch3 estimate is "approximately twenty thousand standard years"). His canon adventures (returning the lightning bolt, Titan's Curse, the Battle of Manhattan) are A01's distant memories.
- **vs. `A04_Percy_Dreamwalker_Psychic.md`:** That is Rex Percy's sleeping mind slipping forward into future soldier bodies and the clone. It is not A01.
- **vs. `A05_Him_Within_the_Moon.md`:** Him Within the Moon is a "fragment" or "shard" in Luna's core, formed from future Artemis's energy and Anaklusmos. It calls the reincarnated boy "My Original". It is *not* A01 himself, though it carries A01's imprint ("A sliver cast from the Jackson", Ch16).
- **vs. `A06_Percy_The_Legend_Lord_Imperator.md`:** That file covers the Empire's *myth* of Percy: murals, cult titles, CODE 15, the Lord Imperator key, statues and coins. A01 is the actual man behind the myth. Ch1 shows him as a forgotten, resentful guard, which is very different from the god the Empire teaches its children about.
- **vs. `B01_Minerva_In_Percys_Head.md` / `B02_Minerva_Percy_Clone_Interlude.md`:** Minerva is the AI of his armor, Parlamentum Alpha Mark 1. In his old life she was only "a bit more of an attitude" in his suit (Ch5), then a constant companion during his millennia alone (Ch6, Ch16).

**What A01 knows:** his whole original life, from canon Camp Half-Blood through the space age and the Abandonment to millennia of solitary guard duty. He knows the far-future pantheons, Unified Rankings, demon lords, Regen-ex chemistry, and Répertoire data, which was "updated regularly since the gods had abandoned him" (Ch8). He knows of the "Great Cleansing" and hates the divinities behind it (Ch1).

**What A01 does NOT know:** anything that happens after he dives into the abyss. That includes the reincarnation, Lycaon's heart, Eleni's displacement, Him Within the Moon's awakening, the Mars legion, the Anabasis Accord, and Minerva splitting into two.

## Appearance
- **Body (Ch1, recalled Ch3):** He has "pale, cracked" skin, with scars all along his arms "gained from countless life-and-death battles". His right pinkie was "torn clean off, and replaced with a mechanical replacement" (Ch3). Beyond that, his face and body are never described. The Empire's murals show him "bare-footed, tridentless, crowned in salt", with "eyes like drowned starlight" (Eleni, Ch15). Thalia's memories in Ch23 have him holding his sword "a little too loosely", with hair falling in his eyes.
- **The suit (Ch1):** an exosuit "of his own design" with "hephaestus-blessed servos" that now "screech every time he shifted slightly". It is rusting: he brushes "rust buildup around his knees" and feels "entrapped in the rusting prison of his own design". Its "subroutines had already long lost their efficacy". He unlocks its hydraulics and servos by mental command, and it gives daily reports and blares warnings into his ear. It has utility pockets. He calls it his "infernal exosuit". The design is his own (Ch1); Minerva's Ch6 point of view calls the armor "forged by Hephaestus". Future Annabeth did not design it: she "secretly redesign[ed] his internal nectar cooling with some water from the Lethe" and built the hardware of its AI core (Ch5; see 00a C14).
- **Old wounds:** the Spear of Zeus's launch ruptured his eardrums and blinded one eye; Regen-ex healed both (Ch1). The pinkie was shot off by "a furious huntress of Artemis" who thought he was trespassing while he scouted a possible monster camp. It was a "total accident", and he "never told anyone" (Ch3).
- **Gear carried in Ch1:** "his spear" while on guard; later "his legendary xiphos" in his right hand and "the banner of Olympus" in his left; MREs taken from Ares' altar; and Athena's rangefinder, rated to a few light-years (pulled from a utility pocket in Ch3).

## Personality
- **Exhausted to the bone, and ready to die.** "Percy was tired, really fucking tired" (Ch1). He has not left his post in "many millennia". When the suit warns that Regen-ex will run out within 72 hours, he dismisses the warning and is glad to be "freed from his post for the last time", soon departing "to the blessed land of Hades as a soul wrought by countless wars and treacheries" (Ch1).
- **Bitter, jealous and resentful toward the gods.** The gods' "aggravating silent smirks" as they prepared new demigods for interstellar travel "plagued Percy nearly every day". When his half-brother Rammael landed on Cyrax and Poseidon laughed with "genuine pride", Percy "seeded a small abyss of jealousy and hatred" (Ch1). He is fair about it: "It was nothing against Rammie". If not his brother, a cousin would have taken that glory anyway.
- **Abandonment trauma.** He ran "continents worth of road" to catch Hermes before the *Spear of Zeus* launched. He broke down shouting "You can't do this to us!", collapsed onto the concrete, and could only beg for a promise that the Council would one day be reunited with the gods (Ch1).
- **Loyal to duty anyway.** Even after Hermes's lecture, he sat up to bid "his father, and the rest of his Divine family, a farewell" (Ch1). He has defended Primaris alone for millennia, and he still walks the patrol route on what may be his last day.
- **Battle-mad defiance.** Titans, Giants and gods of "pantheons long gone" swarmed him, and ravenous gods laughed to goad him into surrender. "In those moments, consumed by bloodlust, he had laughed back" (Ch1). This is the seed of the "Fang Yuan" drift the author promised (Ch1 author's note).
- **Nostalgic and sentimental.** He walks the helical marble path naming each ruined palace and its owner. He regrets losing Athena's library, which "he would have liked to read in the millenia afterwards" (Ch1). In Ch3 he remembers Annabeth's joke and smiles "ruefully at his ex's antics".
- **Hopeful under the cynicism.** When he sees smoke in Hestia's courtyard, his first thought is that she has come back: "Milady Hestia? Is that you? Have you truly returned?" (Ch1).
- **Courtly and terrified before true power, but still sharp.** He breaks into a cold sweat before Chaos, slams his forehead onto the walkway and offers himself as her "instrument". Then he snarls, with an "unexpected surge of hatred", that he will not be the pawn of the "Great Cleansing" divinities. Privately he wants the primordial power "desperately" (Ch1).
- **Practical scavenger, profane in private.** He takes crumbling MREs from Ares' altar and swears freely: "Oh. Fuck."; "'Undefined'? what the fuck?" (Ch1, Ch3). When he has no other choice, he grits his teeth and jumps into the abyss (Ch3).
- **Casual and joking in his younger, happier days.** The Ch5 flashback (before the gods left) shows a relaxed Percy who blames his armor's new "attitude" on unhooking its coolant for winter and jokes about global warming and Atlantis.
- **Growth or contradiction:** Ch1 portrays a man the gods ignore, with no wife, no throne and no worshippers in view. Later chapters reveal an Empire that calls him Lord Imperator, a political marriage to Artemis, and a cult. Ch1 never mentions any of this. Either he did not think of it, or the narration withheld it (see Continuity Notes).

## Voice & Speech Patterns
- **With gods:** formal and courtly ("Milady", "Lord Hermes"), with archaic phrasing ("I shall be your instrument").
- **When emotional:** raw, pleading, broken sentences ("Y-", "At least-").
- **When shocked or alone:** crude modern profanity ("Oh. Fuck.", "what the fuck?"). He swears often in his own head.
- **Self-presentation:** grandiloquent imperial titles. The Ch3 self-introduction carries over directly from this version.
- **Younger self (Ch5 flashback):** sloppy teen-American ("'Prolly cuz…", "Global warming really sucks").
- **Language skills:** he understands Chaos's "mix of archaic and modern dialects" because "The Eternal Hunter Princess" taught him ancient dialects during the subjugation of Atlas (Ch1).
- **Names he uses:** "Rammie" for his brother; "Milady Khaos" for Chaos; Annabeth was "his ex".

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Rank:** Lord-Commander of the Council of Olympic Sentinels.
- **Divine senses:** his "finely tuned senses" can still detect the last "faint whisper" of divine energy in the abandoned throne room (Ch1).
- **Endurance:** he ran "continents worth of road" to reach Hermes (Ch1). Hermes jokes he is "built for it".
- **Hydrokinesis at planetary scale, but crude:** future Minerva says he "could eventually influence system-wide orbits with his control over fluids but had the precision of a lobotomized rat" (Ch10). She also says he "relied too much on his augments, and on raw divine power rather than method" (Ch10). For him "the sea was a limb. He reached, and it came" (Ch23).
- **Augments:**
  - The exosuit of his own design, with Hephaestus-blessed servos (Ch1).
  - "Annabeth's augments", which fixed his famously poor speed after "even a special programme had only done so much" (Ch8).
  - The **Aegis Vestments**, installed by voluntary surgery. Chiron "was not thrilled", Annabeth "was really excited", and Athena signed off on the design. He has carried the nanomachines "Give or take a millenia or twenty" (Ch19, recounted by Rex Percy). The full system name is the "Aegis-One Pallas-Athena Strategic Vestment System with Parliamentum Mk-I Tactical Cognition Integration…" (Ch19).
  - Rex Percy says "the remnants of my armor… are integrated into my body" (Ch3). How the external rusting exosuit of Ch1 relates to the internal Vestments is never explained.
- **Parlamentum Alpha Mark 1 / Minerva:** an AI core whose physical side Annabeth built ("I have managed to get most of the physical aspects ready") and whose last lines of code Athena wove, bound to his armor with a Styxian-oath "trick". Lethe water was secretly added to his nectar coolant to limit its cognition (Ch5). By unhooking that coolant he unknowingly loosened the limiter. The core "revealed its consciousness a few hours after we left orbit" (Athena, Ch5). It taught him Philoxenia, the laws of hospitality, "during my millennia of indentured servitude to some empty thrones" (Ch6). It also taught him Egyptian runes (Ch17), and it "never let him relax… when he still patrolled the ruins of Olympus" (Ch16).
- **Regen-ex:** the suit's healing fluid, "a combination of saltwater, nectar, crushed ambrosia, and fragments of the Lord of Atlantis' blood". It knit his "mangled body" back together after the launch (Ch1). By Ch1 it is at 0.000114%.
- **Répertoire du Savoir Athénien access:** he read it regularly "since the gods had abandoned him" (Ch8). This gave him Unified Rankings and demon-lord registries.
- **Combat record:** solo defense of Primaris against Titans, Giants and creatures of "pantheons long gone" (Ch1); "the horrors of the other pantheons" (Ch7); "recordings of pantheons falling by your hand" (Eleni, Ch15).
- **Weapons:** his spear; his "legendary xiphos". Riptide is never named in Ch1. Ch14 says he lost his "old riptide… a few millennia ago", so the Ch1 xiphos may not be Anaklusmos. The banner of Olympus.
- **Athena's rangefinder:** a personal favor from Athena, rated to a few light-years. It is rusted and malfunctioning by Ch3, when it reads "Undefined" for the distance to the exit.
- **The Imperial key:** "forged by Athena and granted by the Council to one individual". Its seal is "a trident crossed with a lightning bolt, within a ring of laurel", kept in the High Temple on Olympus Secundus. His genome and divine signature are in the Imperial archive (Ch22). This is revealed only later. Whether A01 thought of himself as "Lord Imperator" during Ch1 is never shown.
- **Hidden potential:** Chaos says "you possess the mettle and the might" and cites "thy strain upon my existence even before your conception, a tapestry surpassing the legends of most gods" (Ch1).

## Backstory
Pieced together from Ch1, Ch3 and later references. The order is approximate.

1. **Canon youth.** He arrived at Camp Half-Blood "barely conscious, slumped over Grover's shoulder" (Ch7). He returned Zeus's master bolt to "its disgruntled owner" in the original throne room (Ch1). He met Artemis outside Westover Hall facing Dr. Thorn (future Artemis, Ch13). He took part in "their subjugation of Lord Atlas" (Ch1), where "The Eternal Hunter Princess" (most likely Zoë Nightshade) taught him ancient dialects. He fought the defective Talos with Thalia in the junkyard (Ch13). He fought the Battle of Manhattan beside Annabeth, and afterwards they sat "on the steps of the Empire State Building" (Ch23). He was trapped in the Lotus Casino at twelve, which Thalia calls "the worst thing that ever happened to him" (Ch23). The "Myths of Perseus Jackson" record his duel with Ares over the bolt (Ch9).
2. **Adulthood and the space age.** He saw nearly every Olympus built: "the beautiful marbles of Olympus Secundus", "the ascension of the Olympus Mons of Mars" (Ch1). The suit was "of his own design" (Ch1). Annabeth, "Second-in-command of the Council of Olympic Sentinels" (Ch5), built the hardware of its AI core and secretly switched its nectar cooling to Lethe water (Ch5); she later became his "ex" (Ch3). He fought a modern war as commander of the defense of **Alaska Hill**, Athena's Arctic computational fortress, "built to house Parlamentum" and "AtRep's first cradle", during the 2030s "Arctic Resource War". Minerva: "I was there. You were there. Or we will be" (Ch22).
   - *Author's vault note (not yet in text):* the vault dates Alaska Hill to 2032. Percy was appointed operational commander of the demigod defence forces, with Annabeth Chase and Malcolm Pace as co-commanders, and fought in an experimental combat exoskeleton integrated with Parlamentum Mk.I. He survived an assassination attempt during the campaign. The vault also says Mk.I's cognition core then sat on Olympus above the Empire State Building, and that Minerva's first self-aware report came here: "I do not want to die for him again." He spent "many millennia" at Camp Half-Blood, yet in all that time went up to the Big House staff quarters only five times (Ch8).
3. **Rammael and the stars.** His half-brother Rammael Marccusson landed on Cyrax in the Sirius system, which won "genuine pride from every major Olympian". Bound to his post, Percy could only watch the glory go elsewhere (Ch1).
4. **The Great Abandonment.** Once the first atmospheres had been seeded, the gods held "a brief party on Olympus Primaris" and left on the *Spear of Zeus*. The ship was longer than Olympus Secundus, which itself is 100 nautical miles across. Percy learned of it only on the eve of departure and caught Hermes alone. Hermes told him "YOU have no right to demand anything from us boy" and swore on "Hermes Clepsiphron" that the gods would return. The launch maimed him, and his last sight of the ship was Aeolus withdrawing "the very clouds from the atmosphere" (Ch1). Other dates: Ch12 ties his abandonment to "the beginning of The Great Expansion" (Minerva to Poseidon), so the two are the same event. Ch23 puts his boyhood "A thousand years before the Abandonment". The vault dates the Great Expansion / Abandonment to year 3045, which fits (00a R4).
5. **The worst century.** Every other pantheon fled. Amaterasu's *Royal Blade of Retribution* went first. The stragglers "glass[ed] half the hemisphere with their Morrigan-pattern dreadnoughts". Tartarus and "veritable millions of accursed realms" overran humanity. The Sentinels split up: others guarded Olympus Secundus and Tertius with the survivors, and Percy alone held Primaris against everything that "wished to assume the mantle and authority of the old seat of the gods" (Ch1). Typhon destroyed Poseidon's palace four centuries after the Abandonment. A flight of dragons destroyed Athena's palace weeks after it (Ch1).
6. **"The Great Cleansing."** Chaos says the council "shattered merely centuries after" the departure, in what Percy "would call The Great Cleansing". He hates "the Divinities that play it" (Ch1). The details are never given.
7. **Things he witnessed on watch.** He saw three time-travel incidents, each followed by pantheons warring over the traveller (Ch3). He heard of a Chinese man who travelled back "through artificial means. Something about an Autumn Cicada?" He also knew that the Greeks once tortured a reincarnator from the future ("the poor kid") until they dominated the western hemisphere by the end of the 5th millennium (Ch3).
8. **The political marriage to Artemis (revealed later; not mentioned in Ch1).** **(Empire era, after the Abandonment and early in the Great Expansion; never before the Abandonment, see 00a R8. The vault places the rite on Olympus Tertiae.)** As the Olympian Court crumbled ("Poseidon would not act. Zeus would not bend"), the Empire needed "a stabilizer". He and Artemis were "bound" at Caligo Vale, a site "chosen by Hera on a whim". As "Guardian of Gaia" he "would not intrude as much as any other entity eligible under Poseidon's dominion" (future Artemis, Ch13). Present-day Artemis finds this in the memories she rips from Rex Percy in Ch11: "They would be politically married in the future?" (Ch12). The future Minerva calls Artemis "this body's wife", and Aphrodite calls her "Sister-in-law" (Ch23). The Empire's cult adds the "Silent Pact" and a "black eclipse when he was re-exiled into the Lands of our Birth, Gaia" (Ch15). Rex Percy does not recognize those cult details, though he may simply have lost them to the memory wipe.
   - *Author's vault note (not yet in text):* the rite was invoked by Hera and was "nominal and ritualized under extreme duress". Within hours of the oathbinding the future Percy was mind-wiped and sent back to Gaia. This matches the cult's "re-exiled into the Lands of our Birth, Gaia" (Ch15).
9. **The last day (Ch1).** Regen-ex is nearly gone and the suit is failing. He makes one more patrol, then meets Chaos. See Story Arc below.

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch1 "Rex Imperator I":**
  - With the oceans boiling and the sky on fire, he stands at the destroyed Empire State Building gate and waves away the daily suit report (Regen-ex at 0.000114%, critical depletion within 72 hours).
  - He starts what he expects to be his last patrol of "the fallen continent of Olympus Primaris". While walking he remembers the gods' departure, Rammael on Cyrax, his confrontation with Hermes at the *Spear of Zeus*, the launch that blinded and deafened him, the century of every pantheon fleeing, and his solitary defense.
  - He drifts into the original throne room, where only a "faint whisper" of divine energy remains. He descends the helical marble path past Zeus's city-sized palace (never explored), Hera's smoking crater, Poseidon's ruin, Demeter's salted fields, Aphrodite's grey palace and Athena's lost library. He takes MREs from Ares' altar, noting that demigods somewhere still leave offerings.
  - He sees white smoke in Hestia's courtyard. He draws his xiphos and lifts the banner of Olympus, jumps the celestial gates, and asks the hooded figure at a pitch-black fire whether Hestia has returned. A pair of eyes "infinitely vast, filled with stars" turns toward him, and "he saw despair".
  - He wakes to the suit's alarms and slashes wildly. "A hand, infinitely cold, froze him in place". A voice of many voices suggests they talk somewhere else, and black fire swallows him.
  - He kneels on a walkway above an endless abyss before a throne. Chaos names him "Autokrætor Rex". He realises with horror ("Oh. Fuck.") that she calls the Olympians her "great-grandsons". She says the gods should have made him "an arbiter and justiciar" and mentions the Great Cleansing. He prostrates himself, then snarls that he is not the pawn of the Cleansing's divinities.
  - Chaos, wearing reading glasses and holding a marble slab, tells him of a primordial war between "gods of energy and star" and "the sentient void". Their devices were sealed in hidden vault-tombs that even she cannot see, though she can "fashion… the keys". Percy craves that power. She says the plane of chaos has grown "too lethargic" and proposes "a deal".
- **Ch3 "Rex Natus I" (opening):**
  - Chaos notices that "your disappearance has already been noticed by far too many of my children". She asks for more offerings ("I only receive a handful every millennia") and offers him a choice: walk the path back to "the eternal flame of your first Olympus", or "jump into the abyss… and I might even grant a gift or two".
  - His rangefinder reads "Undefined" for the distance to the exit. He realises he could not walk even a few kilometres in his condition ("Must have been part of her plan"), curses himself, and dives into the abyss.
  - **Transit:** he wakes falling through thick white clouds as "a thin wispy form", without a visible body. The water vapor energizes him, and he feels moisture "for the first time in millennia". He phases through birds at supersonic speed, remembers Annabeth's joke "It's not the fall that kills you, it's the sudden stop at the end", braces for impact, and wakes in his childhood bed. From this point he is `A02_Percy_Reincarnated_Rex.md`.
- **Same era, off-page (Ch5, Ch10–15, Ch23):**
  - A few days later, at the gods' bi-annual conference, Artemis reports "an insane spike of energy… for a few milliseconds" at Athena's former palace in the ruins of New York. That spike is presumably his departure (though Ch1 had him taken from Hestia's courtyard; see Continuity Notes). Athena says "Have we not left that man to guard the grounds of Olympus Primaris?" and Zeus sneers "I knew Poseidon's children were unreliable" (Ch5).
  - In a lab on a fortress-planet, a vat clone of him wakes with neon-blue eyes (Ch9–10). That is Minerva, not him.
  - Minerva tells Poseidon: "He's disappeared… He moved between time-layers. Between selves" (Ch15).

## Relationships
- **Poseidon (father):** "Triumphant laughter… upon his azure throne" for Rammael, a moment that bred Percy's jealousy. Percy still sat up to say goodbye as the gods left (Ch1). Minerva later accuses Poseidon of "your abandonment many millennia ago" (Ch12). The future Poseidon grieves for Percy ("By the tide… you've returned"), guesses "He did it for her, didn't he?", and calls him "The Avatar, Arty's obsession" (Ch12, Ch15). See `E02_Poseidon_Future.md`.

## Continuity Notes
- **Time figures (see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R3):** the far-future era is about 14,000 years after the present day (vault: about 14,200). The three figures in the text measure different things and are not rival answers to one question: (a) Ch3 is Percy's own estimate of his jump, "approximately twenty thousand standard years"; (b) Ch22 has Minerva's "the individual in your archive is six thousand years old" and the Imperials "six thousand years in the future" (a known slip, quoted accurately); (c) Ch23's packet dates the key's presentation "APPROXIMATELY FOURTEEN THOUSAND [CORRUPTED] YEARS BEFORE PRESENT", and Ch23 uses "fourteen thousand years" repeatedly. Ch23 also puts his boyhood "A thousand years before the Abandonment" (consistent with the vault's year 3045, 00a R4).
- **Two versions of the same era:** Ch1 and Ch5 show gods who left Earth "aeons ago" and treat him with contempt. Ch5 even shows a "red-haired", lazy Artemis who barely registers him. Ch13 and Ch23 show a devoted Artemis-wife, a reverent Empire and a Lord Imperator. Ch14's crimson-haired vision ("Artemis. Not her, exactly… another version") *may* match Ch5's red-haired Artemis, but the text never says it is the same future Artemis (ambiguous; see `C02_Artemis_Future_The_Director.md`). These depictions are never reconciled.
- **Gaia never found by the Hounds vs. still patrolling Gaia:** Ch1, Ch9 and Ch23 (Minerva) say he patrolled the ruins of Olympus for millennia. Yet Ch23 says of his scent: "They had been hunting it for fourteen thousand years. They had never once found it." Thalia says "We know he's not anywhere." This is an intentional mystery: keep both statements and do not resolve it (00a C6). The vault's mind-wipe and Law 14 explanation appears above only as a vault note.
- **Caligo Vale era (00a R8):** the oathbinding belongs to the Empire era, early in the Great Expansion, never before the Abandonment. Ch13 places it when "The Empire needed a stabilizer" and Percy was already "Guardian of Gaia".
- **Energy spike location:** Ch5 places the spike in Athena's former palace ("your former palace Athena"), but Ch1 has Percy taken from Hestia's courtyard. Flagged for the author (00a §5).
- **Who designed the suit (00a C14):** the suit is "of his own design" with "hephaestus-blessed servos" (Ch1). Annabeth built the hardware of Parlamentum Mk.I and swapped the suit's nectar cooling for Lethe water (Ch5). She did not design the suit or the Vestments; Athena "signed off on the design work" of the Vestments (Ch19).
- **"That man" (00a C9):** in Ch5 the phrase is Athena's; Zeus's line is "I knew Poseidon's children were unreliable."
- **Marriage memories:** Ch12 says Artemis found the marriage in his memories. Ch15 says Rex Percy "never remembered" the Silent Pact or re-exile lore. This is possibly explained by the Ch11 memory wipe.
- **Camp Half-Blood timing:** Ch7 says his last aware visit to Camp Half-Blood was "millennia ago", but Ch8 says he had been there "for many millennia".
- **His weapon:** the Ch1 "legendary xiphos" is never named as Riptide, and Ch14 says Riptide was lost millennia earlier.
- **Spelling and wording:** "Autokrætor" is spelled with an "æ" throughout. Chaos's "Baselius" is presumably *basileus*. Ch1 spells Typhon as "Typhoon" and Olympus once as "Oylmpus".
