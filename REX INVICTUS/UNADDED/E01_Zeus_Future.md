## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **This file:** the *future* Zeus. In the old timeline he abandons Earth. In the UOE era he rules an Imperiality, has "a hundred thousand"-plus children, wages near-monthly border skirmishes with Poseidon and fights border conflicts with Dionysus.
- **Present-day Zeus** (present day, c. 2021 per author's timeline; nominally the canon Lightning Thief / Titan's Curse era): his bolt is stolen and recovered, he lends his plane, he claims Eleni at the Ch15 council, wants to make Percy "another Hercules" in Ch19, and smites Imperial landers in Ch22. See `D01_Zeus_Present.md`.
- **What this version knows:** the Abandonment, the Great Expansion, the War of Princes, and that Percy was left "to guard the grounds of Olympus Primaris" (Ch5). He knows that Percy hosts one of Athena's "Parlamentum Alpha" cores. He does *not* know that Percy has been sent back in time; to him the energy spike is a mystery.
- **Timeline caution:** Ch1 and Ch5 belong to the *original* future, in which Percy guards ruined Olympus alone. The UOE-era references in Ch10–15 (Percy as revered "Guardian of Gaia" and Artemis's husband) appear to be the same far future seen from another angle. Ch23, however, is causally linked to the present-day timeline: the future Artemis *remembers* Ch22's Long Island battle. The text never says whether the far future is one fixed history or a loop, so treat dates loosely (see Continuity Notes).

## Appearance
- No physical description is given in the future scenes beyond his effects. He slams his hand on his throne and "showers of sparks" fly from its base. "The electricity coiled around Zeus' arms." He leaves with "a rumble of thunder" (Ch5).
- His palace on Olympus Primaris was "larger than some cities", and Percy never bothered to explore it (Ch1).
- His emblem in the Empire is "Zeus' Golden Eagle", which overwrites the war-room screens at the Tartarus front (Ch15). Eleni's pauldron shows an eagle clutching a thunderbolt (Ch15).

## Personality
- **Wrathful and quick-tempered.** He answers the Shinto request with a single word, "Denied.", slams his throne, then threatens the envoy (Ch5).
- **Petty and grudge-holding.** He is "under a usual fit of rage recently" because Poseidon's direct descendant killed one of his daughters in their "near monthly" skirmishes, and Poseidon keeps "rubbing it into his face" (Ch5).
- **Callous toward his children.** "He didn't bother remembering names, not anymore. He lost count after a hundred thousand." The dead daughter is just "Eleni… something?" (Ch5). This is Eleni Theodosia, see `G01_Eleni_Theodosia.md`.
- **Image-conscious.** He backs down only when Hera reminds him their reputation is on the line and other tables are watching (Ch5).
- **Dismissive of Percy.** When Athena asks whether "that man" left guarding Olympus Primaris should have handled the spike, he snaps "How should I know? I knew Poseidon's children were unreliable" (Ch5). ("That man" is Athena's phrase, not his; see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C9.) This fits the old-timeline gods' indifference that Percy resents in Ch1.
- **Secretive and paranoid.** He orders silence about the rogue AI and threatens to "personally come hunt you down" (Ch5).
- **Restless expansionist (through Hermes's account).** He leads the gods to "new frontiers"; Hermes says he isn't privy to "Father's plans" for any return (Ch1).
- **Rigid ("would not bend").** In the UOE backstory, as the Olympian Court crumbled, "Zeus would not bend" (Ch13). That stubbornness forced the political marriage of Percy and Artemis.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Imperious, curt, royal. He uses one-word verdicts ("Denied.", "Fine. Continue."). He calls foreigners "Outsider" or "monster" and his children "Daughter". He speaks in threats with capitalised emphasis ("ANY"). He does not swear on-page. His sarcasm comes as rhetorical questions.
- "Denied." (Ch5)
- "You dare to command me? Outsider, I suggest that you consider your next words carefully" (Ch5)
- "Fine. Continue." (Ch5)
- "How should I know? I knew Poseidon's children were unreliable." (Ch5)
- "Enough, Daughter. Take care in your words, for our enemies may attempt to take advantage of the unguarded mind." (Ch5)
- "If I hear word of ANY rumors of rogue AI on Gaia, I will personally come hunt you down. Understand?" (Ch5)

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Lightning and storm:** sparks, arm-coiling electricity, departure by thunder (Ch5).
- **The *Spear of Zeus*:** the colossal ship in which the gods left Earth. It is longer than Olympus Secundus (100 nautical miles across), and its engines are larger than Central Park (Ch1).
- **Imperial military assets bearing his name:**
  - The Steel Legions and the Third Lance battlegroup (Ch15).
  - Bolt-class cannons, the "Strikes of Zeus" (Ch2).
  - Forty-two "Zeus-Wrath hunter-killer satellites" near the Apollonian Hospitalier Complex, able to disintegrate a fortress-planet (Ch10).
  - The Golden Eagle overlay on command systems (Ch15).
- **Dominion and bloodlines:** the "Firstborn Million" (his demigod children) and the "Sacred Hundred" raised in his Dominion (Eleni, Ch15). The Theodosian house (Ch22) and many legacies (Lucan Corvo, Ch23; Poseidon-Zeus legacies such as Straban, Ch20–22).

## Backstory
- **Before the Abandonment:** Percy returned the lightning bolt to its "disgruntled owner" in the original throne room (canon Lightning Thief, remembered Ch1).
- **The Great Abandonment:** after a brief party on Olympus Primaris, the gods "ascended to the bridge of The Spear of Zeus and their future dominions." Hermes refers to "Father's plans for our return" but doesn't know them (Ch1).
- **UOE era:** he sits on the Olympian Council of an interstellar empire allied with the Voss Sovereignty. He feuds with Poseidon and fights "Dionsyian-Zeus [sic] border conflicts" (Ch15). As the Olympian Court crumbled he "would not bend", and Artemis was bound to Percy to stabilise the Empire (Ch13).
- **Author's vault note (not yet in text):** the Zeus-Wrath hunter-killer satellites are planet-glassing weapons, first used in year 3048. In 3047 Hera "slapped him down" over preemptive strikes. His Titan legion **Rolling Thunder** is dated 16221 (War of Princes). Justicar Primaris is his main market system. He is the father of Hermes. His UOE Unified Rank is #8.

## Continuity Notes
- **Who killed Eleni:** she was killed at Fulguris by the Dionysus–Poseidon–Aphrodite coalition (Melina Callisto's ramming, Ch2; Stephanos Pyrrhus was part of that coalition). Zeus blames "Poseidon's damned direct descendant" (Ch5). Her last memory curses "Dionysus" (Ch15). Ch22 Eleni blames the Pyrrhus dynasty generally. Treat the individual killer as unresolved.
- Spellings: "Posiedon" [sic] (Ch5); "Dionsyian" [sic] (Ch15). The treaty is "Mount Rümker" in Ch5 and "Mount Rumker" in Ch23 (see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md S11).
- **Era figures (see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R3):** the canonical distance from the present to the far-future scenes is about 14,000 years. The text's figures measure different things and are not rival answers: (a) Percy's own estimate of his jump, that he "transmigrated approximately twenty thousand standard years into the past" (Ch3); (b) Ch22's "six thousand years" (Minerva: "the individual in your archive is six thousand years old"; Luke: "In six thousand years, they're still up there on their thrones"), a known slip; (c) Ch23's packet, "FOURTEEN THOUSAND [CORRUPTED] YEARS". Ch22 also has an Imperial saying the planet "should not have Theodosians on it for another four thousand years" (Ch22). The Abandonment is year 3045 (R4).
- **Version caution:** this file uses only far-future scenes (Ch1, Ch2, Ch5, Ch10's clone-Minerva segment, Ch13 and Ch15 UOE flashbacks, Ch23). Present-day Zeus material (Ch15 council, Ch19, Ch22) belongs to `D01` (C33).
