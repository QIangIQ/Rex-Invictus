## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **This file:** the 21st-century camp director, sober "for two hundred years, give or take" on Diet Coke, serving a punishment that keeps getting extended.
- **Not** the far-future Dionysus: the "Hawaiian-print lump collapsed at the bar" at the divine conference (Ch5), patron of the "Dionysian Division" mercenaries and HadriCorp's live-streamed war (Ch18), whose border wars with Zeus ("Dionsyian-Zeus border conflicts" [sic], Ch15) put Eleni's battlegroup into repair dock and whose name is Eleni's last curse ("Gods Be Damned! Dionysus.", Ch15), and father of Damocles Euphemios (Ch2). Eleni was killed at Fulguris by the Dionysus–Poseidon–Aphrodite coalition (Melina Callisto's ramming, Ch2); Zeus blames Poseidon's descendant (Ch5). See `E06_Dionysus_Future.md`.
- The Imperial Watchpoint reads his present-day signature as "weaker than any Dionysus on record by several orders of magnitude" (Ch22). That measures how much he will grow, not a different person.
- **Knowledge boundary:** He knows "mini-Sister" (Minerva) lives in Percy's head, knows the Mars force, and has seen Imperial commandos. He hints he has seen "aberrations" like this before ("You really think you're the first?"). He does not know Percy's old timeline.

## Appearance
- Rumpled grape-coloured camp shirt or Hawaiian shirt, "slightly unbuttoned", curly hair in disarray, deep purple eyes, cargo pants, a Diet Coke can or goblet (Ch7–8).
- Ch22: "a pudgy man in a leopard-print shirt… with eyes the color of burning wine".
- His vine staff has a gnarled handle and a head "worn away after thousands of years of regular polishing" (Ch8).
- His room: bare white walls, Swan Lake at full blast on floor-standing speakers, conducting with a Berol marker. Paintings show him in a chiton beside "an identically dressed and similar-looking boy" (Ch8).

## Personality
- **Lazy, sardonic surface.** "Not my job, sweetheart" (Ch8). He calls Percy "Parcey" and "Perry". "Insurance on this camp is a nightmare" (Ch7).
- **Sharply perceptive.** One madness-laden glance tilts Percy's world, and he spots "another consciousness riding shotgun" before anyone else (Ch7). Minerva: "He sees more than he lets on."
- **Mocking.** Imitates Minerva's electronic voice: "Varlet, be cautious!" (Ch7).
- **Dangerous when he drops the act.** On the bus "he was speaking like a god". He calls Minerva "a parasite. A tumor" and launches "vines of pure madness" at Percy's mind, which Minerva repels (Ch11).
- **Rule-maker about aberrations.** "Rules about what belongs in mortal minds… Some of which I've personally made" (Ch11).
- **Principled about games.** "We want to win, but if you cheat in a war game the whole thing becomes pointless" (Ch9). He threatens slackers with "a nice twenty-kilometer speed hike with a sack of rocks".
- **Hates Ares.** He laughs at him on Olympus for an hour (Ch10) and "wishes that the weapons were real" (Annabeth).
- **Grieving and fatalistic.** "Many of my brothers were slow. It got them killed" (Ch8).
- **Hedonist and gambler.** Loses "seven Gs at the baccarat" and returns "sloshed" with a "forty-something looking spirit" (Ch8). Has a "secret alcohol stash". Has been punished an eighteenth time for drinking (Ch8).
- **Quietly kind.** Gives Percy a silent nod and "a glance toward the cove" as Percy leaves for the Sea of Monsters (Ch13). In Ch22 he "for once" expands his haze over the Big House to protect it.
- **Protective rage.** When the Ekdromoi "stepped over the body of one of his campers, the headache went away completely" (Ch22).

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Drawling, sarcastic and theatrical, with florid wine metaphors when he wants to sound divine. He mangles names, calls girls "sweetheart", Clarisse "Niece", Athena "The Smart Sister" and Zeus "Father". He ends conversations with "Toodles!" Mild profanity.

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Madness:** a glance that bends perception (Ch7). A mind-assault of "vines of pure madness" (Ch11). The "old madness" of Pentheus and the Tyrrhenian pirates: grapevines erupt from floor, walls, pinochle table and the leopard's head, with flutes and purple light. Two Imperial operators break, one beating his head against a wall and one leaping through a window (Ch22).
- **Protective haze:** a purple haze over the Big House and camp that swallows signals and slows the Imperials' Watchpoint relay (Ch22).
- **Combat:** flips and pins Percy with his vine staff in an instant (Ch8).
- **Manifestation:** wine and spirits for the insurgents' party (Ch10).
- **Espionage:** with Sarge, planted a video transmitter in General Mikhail's HQ (Ch9).
- **Limits:** Oriel's thermal grenade breaks his madness "just for a moment" (Ch22).

## Backstory
- Serving as camp director as a punishment. His eighteenth drinking offence adds "another additional decade at his post" (Ch8).
- Lost "brothers" (the painted boy in his room; possibly an AU detail).
- Old rival and enemy of Ares.

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch3:** Percy asks Chiron to tell "our resident Courier of Insanity" that Percy is responsible if Artemis gets angry.
- **Ch7:** Cards with Chiron. Senses Minerva and mocks her. Leaves for his alcohol stash.
- **Ch8:** Warns Percy at the forest edge, then deflects Athena's punishment visit by telling her about Percy. Pins Percy in his Swan Lake room and recruits him for the Area Fulminus red team ("Zeus threw up our name"). In Vegas he gambles, drinks and bails out Hermes.
- **Ch9–10:** Orders thirty kegs, plants the bug, enforces no-cheating, and is "on cloud nine" as Ares's army collapses.
- **Ch11:** On the bus he outs "mini-Sister", calls her a tumor, attacks Percy's mind and fails ("It fought back"). Minerva answers through the speakers: "Statement: I do not require your approval." He warns that "The whole council is simply dying to interrogate you."
- **Ch13:** Smugly announces Chiron's replacement by Tantalus ("Someone… stricter"), then silently lets Percy go.
- **Ch15:** Grudge against Eleni for waking him from his nap. At council: "She is dangerous."
- **Ch16:** Percy swipes a card of Dionysus's "filled with unending amounts of money".
- **Ch22:** Holds his haze over camp all day. Is summoned to the Mars council, furious, and wants everyone to stop shouting so he can get back to the haze. During the raid his madness erupts and floors Sigma-Four. Oriel's grenade stuns him briefly.

## Continuity Notes
- Name spellings vary: Dionysius, Dionysus, Dionosyius.
- Ch22 says he has "been sober for two hundred years, give or take", while Ch8 records his eighteenth drinking offence and Vegas drunkenness. Either the "sobriety" is nominal or this is an inconsistency.
- Ch3 calls him "Courier of Insanity", an unusual epithet that the narration reuses in Ch8. The author's vault confirms it as his Divine Being title (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C32).
- Eleni's death (future): use one sentence everywhere: killed at Fulguris by the Dionysus–Poseidon–Aphrodite coalition (Melina's ramming, Ch2); Zeus blames Poseidon's descendant (Ch5); her last memory curses Dionysus (Ch15). Ch15 does not say Dionysus's forces broke her ship.
- "Director of Camp Half-Blood" in the Accord preamble refers to the camp's director (him or Chiron), not to Artemis (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C4).
- Present-day year: c. 2021 per the author's timeline, nominally the canon PJO era (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R2).
