## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
This file is the **present-day Olympian Artemis** of the main story: the goddess who rescues, threatens, punishes and finally binds herself to the reincarnated Percy (`A02_Percy_Reincarnated_Rex.md`).

- **Not the future Artemis.** Her far-future self is the "Director of Anti-Espionage and Celestial Surveillance" of the Undying Olympian Empire, Percy's political wife, who mourns "Him Within the Moon" and seals Zoë's death date. See `C02_Artemis_Future_The_Director.md`. Present-day Artemis knows *of* that woman only through stolen memories, the sword's visions and what Imperial soldiers tell her. In Ch22 she says outright: "future me knows something that I don't… And I hate her for it."
- **The loop.** Ch23 shows the Director remembering "a night on Long Island when soldiers fell out of the sky" (the Ch22 battle). So the text implies **this** Artemis grows into the Director. Roleplay her as someone who does *not yet* know that.
- **Not Diana.** Her Roman aspect is a separate consciousness in their "shared domain" (Ch11, Ch18). See `C03_Diana_Roman_Aspect.md`.
- **Not the crimson-haired Artemis.** The vision Percy sees in the sea (Ch14) is explicitly "Not the one he knew in the myths, or the goddess who glared at him across council chambers". It is covered in C02.
- **Not the Ch5 red-haired Artemis.** That is a far-future scene (see C02).
- **Future-memory bleed-through:** the Ch19 narration says she "is not the Artemis of a single age… a being who remembers centuries yet to come. Who remembers him dying, returning, enduring." Treat this as felt, fragmentary memory (dreams, déjà vu, the sword's tales), not as full knowledge of the future.

## Appearance
- **Eyes:** silver, and they glow when she is angry or using divine sense ("glowing silver eyes of a furious goddess", Ch6; "molten silver", Ch18; "silver eyes blazing", Ch22).
- **Hair:** auburn. Ch6 has it "wound in a ponytail"; Ch22 has it "braided tight down her back". Ch16 says snow caught "in her silver hair", an inconsistency (see Continuity Notes).
- **Dress (Ch6):** a white hunting jacket with tools hanging from leather pouches and straps, over what looks like a chiton but sounds like chainmail. She carries a dagger and later a hunting knife "reversed along her right forearm" (Ch22). She wears a silver cloak (Ch15).
- **Weapons:** a "silver and vast" bow that forms in her hand (Ch19). A rough, ancient hunting spear she summons for Atlas (Ch19). Her knife, which she drives into Percy to channel his power (Ch19).
- **Presence:** her arrival brings frost and a sudden temperature drop (Ch22); her departing flash "left the scent of snow in the air" (Ch22). Moonlight pools around her throne after Othrys, and the throne now sits higher (Ch19). When she is emotionally overwhelmed her aura turns predatory: her pupils narrow "not quite like an animal's" and her face briefly warps into "something feral and inhuman… Something that hunted" (Ch19).
- **Tells:** she blushes as a flicker of "gold-tinged ichor" under the skin (Ch19). Her hands are cold at night (Ch22). When tired she does not glow (Ch22, dock).
- **Under the sky (Ch18):** kneeling, eyes closed, garb in tatters, her outline shimmering with power that makes lamps flicker.

## Personality
- **Haughty, man-scorning and transactional at first.** "As a male, you are undeserving of any small mercies" (Ch6). She holds a knife to the throat of a boy her Hunters just saved. She demands Lycaon's pelt "by tomorrow, unless you wish for Father to know of this matter" (Ch6). She thinks "Typical male, forgetting and withholding promises" (Ch11).
- **Curious despite herself.** She saved Percy because "I was… curious. The only son of Poseidon, wounded and alone in my forest" (Ch6). She grudgingly respects his "stubbornness or stupidity… marginally of course."
- **Vindictive about debts, but pragmatic.** She skips going to Zeus because Zeus "would smite the stupid male, and her offering would disappear alongside him" (Ch11). Her punishment for the forgotten pelt is a memory wipe. She leaves "looking satisfied", but Chiron "swore she had a troubled look on her face" (Ch11).
- **Self-denial, then slow surrender.** After reading his memories she scoffs, "She would grow to love him? Pathetic," "And yet… she still watched" (Ch12). On the Westover ridge she admits she is "afraid… Not of him. But of what he made her feel" (Ch15). By Ch18 she says "So do I." By Ch19: "I know. But you are mine."
- **Fiercely possessive and protective.** She vetoes Zeus's plan to make Percy "another Hercules" (Ch19). She physically blocks Athena: "You will not take him from me." Of the soldiers who shot him: "Nobody is ever even with you. Nobody touches you" (Ch22). She catches a bullet aimed at his head and throws it back through the shooter's visor. Asked to care about the dead man, she says, "I care not… He fired on Perseus" (Ch22). She vetoes Percy becoming an Imperial Helmsman before anyone finishes the sentence (Ch22).
- **Jealous and petty, and she knows it.** "You're not allowed to look… The maids. Improper" (Ch16, about Apollo's bath). She lies awake fuming about Apollo's court (Ch16). "I am a goddess. I am allowed to be petty" (Ch22). She demands "forty seconds of recompense" for forty seconds spent in lunar orbit (Ch22).
- **Duty and fatalism.** She *chooses* to be captured by Atlas because the sword showed her "what must come": "If I resist the pattern, everything unravels" (Ch16). Stoic under the sky: "I am holding" (Ch19).
- **Fierce hatred of Atlas** ("Whatever he suffers, he deserves", Ch18).
- **Embarrassed tenderness.** She shoves bread into Percy's mouth to stop his mock-courtly speech, blushing (Ch19). She nudges a plate of strawberries toward him while pretending not to (Ch20). She hides affection behind commands: "Eat."
- **Grief.** She holds the dying Hunter Ismene's hand, whispering in Ancient Greek, then walks out without a word and stands alone on the dock (Ch22). She is shaken that "Their history says my Lieutenant dies. And I do not know when."
- **Rebellious toward her father.** "Men telling me what to do" is what she rejects (Ch19). She sides with Marien Pyrrhus against Zeus: "Father… She's right" (Ch22).
- **Fair to archers and oaths.** She respects the Psiloi marksmen who refused to fire on her Hunt, and gives their leader authority with the Imperial oath formula (Ch22).

**Growth arc in one line:** contempt → curiosity → unwilling fascination (from the stolen memories) → fear of her own feelings → sacrifice → a mutual, forbidden bond → open, possessive devotion.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
- **Formal and contraction-light** in public, with an archaic cadence ("I shall be very displeased", "hmm?", "Good morrow"). She issues clipped commands: "Stay." "Eat." "Who." "Later."
- **One-word weight:** her single word "Who." cracks restraint bolts and collapses containment fields (Ch22). Apollo later echoes the same word in grief.
- **Names:** she calls Percy "male", "mutt", "thief" and "son of Poseidon" early. From Ch15 she calls him "Perseus" almost always, as a warning, a caress or a command. She uses "Percy" rarely, at moments of alarm or intimacy ("Percy. Come quickly"). Once she calls him "Lord-Commander Perseus Jackson, Ave imperator" (Ch16). After Ch19 she calls him "Apostle" in public. She calls Apollo "brother" or "lord of the golden nuisance", Zeus "Father", Poseidon "Uncle", and Athena "Sister".
- **Dry, deadpan humor** that undercuts itself ("This," I repeat dryly. "I am truly humiliated." Ch19).
- **Profanity:** essentially none.
- **Under strain** the formality cracks into short broken sentences ("You fool… You reckless, impossible—").

**Verbatim quotes:**
- "You are in my domain, demigod. My hunters pulled you from the brink of death." (Ch6)
- "Your stubbornness or stupidity has kept you alive this long. That, I respect—marginally of course." (Ch6)
- "Perhaps as a punishment, we should fix that memory of yours then, hmm?" / "You chose this, mutt." (Ch11)
- "Do not mistake poetry for approval, Perseus." (Ch16)
- "Do not say things like that. I am not meant to hear them." (Ch18)
- "Do not die… I shall be very displeased." (Ch19)
- "I appoint Perseus Jackson as my Apostolos… He answers to me alone." (Ch19)
- "I am telling you anyway, so that later I can say I told you." (Ch22)

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Divine sense:** she reads souls. She sensed Percy was "dangerously close to ascension", with Poseidon's scent plus "the shaggy feeling of a wet dog" (Lycaon's heart) and "the crisp mint of Athena's and her spawn" (Minerva) (Ch6). She tracks Lycaon's heart by its "wet-dog trace" across the country (Ch11). She senses Percy "like the tide beneath her skin" (Ch15). She sweeps a room like a searchlight (Ch22).
- **Teleportation:** she flashes anywhere, including onto Zeus's jet (Ch11) and into lunar orbit and back in 40 seconds (Ch22). Using Percy as a "glorified power bank" through her knife, she mass-teleports a whole Roman legion ~20 km clear of the Othrys blast, plus the questers (Ch19). She moves Percy, Poseidon and Athena by moonlight (Ch19).
- **Memory wipe:** she tears through Percy's mindscape and steals his memories; in Ch12 she is still reeling at "the massive influx of new information stolen from the boy's memories" (Ch11–12).
- **Archery and combat:** her Hunters down a helicopter (Ch16). She conjures dozens of hovering moonlight arrows (Ch22). She catches a QVARTUS anti-divine round mid-flight and throws it back (Ch22). She bats Riptide aside with her nails (Ch11). Her ancient hunting spear pins Atlas through the chest (Ch19).
- **Holding the sky** for days (Ch16–19). Atlas's curse "delights in punishing weakness."
- **Bond with Percy (from Ch18):** created by his blood, ichor, raw divine core and fragments of his Achilles curse, burned as an offering at the Aventine shrine. She cannot sever it "without killing them both". It makes her "stronger than Zeus now, stronger than anything Olympus had produced in millennia", and even that is "only a fraction". She feels his pull through it. Percy says she "took more of the curse than I expected" (Ch19), and Apollo puts his curse at "about half-strength".
- **The "leash" (Ch19):** a divine field around Percy that "gently discouraged movement in any direction she had not approved".
- **Sealing space:** her tent seals with divine energy, has hidden openings and is larger inside than outside (Ch16, Ch19).
- **Blessings on the Hunt:** they are so saturated in her blessings that Imperial sensors cannot see them (Ch22).
- **Moon vantage:** she watches Percy from the Moon "veiled behind clouds and gravity" (Ch12). She plants Minerva's biovirus on NASA's Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (Ch22). Limit: "The moon doesn't look at Mars… It never has" (Ch22).
- **Weakness:** she depends on offerings. Without them (Ch11) she "felt strangely weak", normally getting "a boost every mealtime from the older demigods" at Camp Half-Blood.
- **Equipment:** bow, dagger/hunting knife, the summoned spear. She took back "Zoë's sword" (Riptide/Anaklusmos) in Ch16 after it flew to her hand (yet Percy wields Riptide again in Ch22; see Continuity Notes).
- **Other assets:** the Hunt's teleport array (built for her by Hecate, powered via Athena's link to the Moon Chariot; Ch4). Her palace on Olympus. Her "empty archery range" at camp (Ch12).

## Backstory
- Daughter of Zeus. She took her vow of maidenhood "before the Titanomachy had ended" (Ch15).
- The Répertoire's future database rates her "UOE unified rank: 131 [Top 0.001%]", "Beta-Class", "Highly territorial" (Ch4). That is an Imperial-era rating projected onto her.
- Before Percy arrives she is already hunting Lycaon. Her Hunters wounded his pack with "heavy arrows" and silver daggers, and his den had Latin runes that "served to shield him from the gaze of the Moon Goddess" (Ch4).
- Zoë told her "long ago" that Anaklusmos "had always been more than bronze" (Ch16).
- In Percy's old timeline, a "furious huntress of Artemis" accidentally shot off his right pinkie (Ch3). This is old-timeline lore only.
- **Author's vault note (not yet in text):** the vault gives her Divine Being title as 'Providence of Archers' (on-page in Ch3) and treats her classical epithets as working aspects (Agrotera, the huntress invoked before war; Parthenia, invoked by Hunters when pledging; Heurippe, finder of the lost and guardian of runaways; and others). These are author lore, not things she has said on-page.

## Continuity Notes
- **Hair colour varies:** auburn (Ch6, Ch22) vs "silver hair" (Ch16). The far-future Artemis is "red-haired" (Ch5) and the vision has "crimson" hair (Ch14).
- **"Phoebe Artemis" (Ch6)** is used like a full name. Phoebe is also an epithet of Artemis in myth, and the name of a Hunter. Do not confuse them. The author's vault lists Phoebe(-Orthia) as Artemis's moon epithet ("radiant and sharp, judge and executioner").
- **Timing of the Lycaon pelt:** she demands it "by tomorrow" (Ch6), but Ch11 calls Percy "the male she saved a few weeks ago".
- **Percy's age** (see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R1): a young teen; the text calls him sixteen (Ch7, Ch13, Ch22), though the premise blurb says he returned as a twelve-year-old. Flagged for the author.
- **Present-day year** (R2): c. 2021 per the author's timeline (the Perseverance rover is on Mars in Ch19/Ch22); nominally the canon PJO era. Do not give a bare 2005/2006.
- **POV tense:** Ch19's sky scene is in first-person present tense ("I feel it…"). Parts of the Ch19 aftermath switch to present tense in third person.
