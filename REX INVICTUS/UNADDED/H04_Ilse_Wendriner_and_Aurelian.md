## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
Both exist only in the far-future era. **Tochter des Elysium** (German for Daughter of Elysium) is one of the corporations in the author's Ch 2 note ("TDE-Tochter des Elysium R&D: Faith-oriented tech (Specialized mythical material procurement like: Holy Water, Celestial Bronze, Blood of Divine Beings)"), here appearing as a reality-mapping firm. The vault also uses the variant "Tochter Aus Elysium"; profiles use the text's spelling (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md S12). Both heard the decrypted verdict, so both are bound to silence under the 14th Undying Law. Not to be confused with the Athenian Répertoire itself (`B03_AtRep_Repertoire_du_Savoir_Athenien.md`), which performed the decryption.

## Appearance
- **Wendriner:** "a short, stout woman with iron-grey hair pinned up in a bun and a Tochter des Elysium insignia on her shoulder." Like the other reality-mappers she wears black. She carries a surveyor's rod with a crystal tip, and the crystal turns black at the wound. By the decryption she has gone 52 hours without sleep, and her hands shake.
- **Aurelian:** young and nervous. He sleeps on a cot in the lab and stumbles over "bleary-eyed".

## Personality
- **Wendriner:**
  - Dry, understated wit. Faced with the impossible she says "Well … That's new."
  - Pure scientific passion: "The rain … is the most beautiful thing I have seen in forty years. Measure it."
  - Fearless to the point of absurdity. She stands at the edge of the wound with her rod in both hands, "not running at all", glaring at a 300-m Giant "with an expression of pure scientific outrage".
  - Quietly humane. She keeps Tertius Oban's severed boot in a sealed evidence box.
  - Proud of her career, and wry about her pay ("extremely underpaid").
  - Overwhelmed at the verdict: she finds she "had sat down on the floor."
- **Aurelian:** anxious, quick, deductive. He recognises the waveform as "ours" and draws the conclusion before she says it. He sits down "very suddenly on an equipment crate."

## Voice & Speech Patterns
**Wendriner** speaks briskly and precisely, in short declaratives and dry asides, with a lecturer's habit of correcting definitions. **Aurelian** speaks in halting, rising realisation.

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Wendriner:** reality cartography. She has measured god-death scars, the craters of bound Primordials, the Glass Worlds of the Voss System (two planets fused into silica), the place in the Pelagic Reach where "the ocean remembered being a desert", and the place on Asphodel where time runs backward for eleven seconds every century. Her equipment is the crystal rod, the augur array and a chronometric filter. She can correct for the 11:1 time gradient and request a CODE 44 decryption from the Répertoire.
- **Aurelian:** Hecatean augur-reading. He knows Imperial standard procedure (senior commanders carry Watchpoint fragments).

## Backstory
Wendriner has spent forty years measuring holes in the world, "more impossible things than any other living person in the Empire, she thought at least." She "had never measured nothing" until now. Nothing is given for Aurelian.
- Author's vault note (not yet in text): the vault's Tochter des Elysium R&D entry describes it as a faith-oriented tech firm; the vault credits it with the Hexenlaufgerät boots. No vault note exists for Wendriner or Aurelian themselves.

## Story Arc So Far
**Ch 23:**
1. **First reading.** Her team lands, she holds the rod over the curve, and the crystal goes black. She explains that the shear removed "eleven kilometers of *here*", coordinates included. She orders the time gradient measured every 10 cm and the rain measured too.
2. **Findings.** The survey shows an 11:1 time gradient at the edge, objects cut with zero-thickness blades, Tertius Oban's boot (see `H16_Callistus_Narrator_and_Tartarus_Front_Soldiers.md`) and the Hecateans' "echo".
3. **Porphyrion.** She stands her ground at the curve while the Giant approaches. A Titan flung by the Giant demolishes a Perioeci station; her own lab at Survey Station Nine is later said to have been "rebuilt twice since the Giant".
4. **Discovery.** 52 hours in, she filters the echo through the chronometric filter and finds a carrier wave. Aurelian identifies it as a tachyonic Répertoire carrier, and they infer Marien Pyrrhus's Watchpoint fragment. She can't decode the packet, so she submits it under CODE 44. The Répertoire is silent for eleven seconds, then returns the verdict: CODE 18, the Lord Imperator, Perseus, roughly fourteen thousand years before present. She sits down on the floor.
