## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **This file:** the future Athena (and her two future aspects). In the old timeline she co-creates the AI that becomes Minerva. In the UOE era she presides over the Empire's AI network and the Tartarus war.
- **Present-day Athena** (present day, c. 2021 per author's timeline; nominally the canon PJO era) is a different character. She debates Minerva over a laptop (Ch6), disguises herself as Annabeth (Ch8), manipulates Percy toward the Sea of Monsters (Ch13), threads her mind into Percy (Ch19) and reads the Anabasis Accord (Ch22). See `D03_Athena_Present.md`.
- **Not to be merged with her AIs:**
  - **Minerva** (Parlamentum Alpha Mark 1, Percy's suit AI) believes she *is* Athena and calls the present goddess "the false Athena". See `B01_Minerva_In_Percys_Head.md` and `B02_Minerva_Percy_Clone_Interlude.md`.
  - **Parlamentum Mark 3** is Athena's own core.
  - **The Répertoire / AtRep** is the Empire-wide judgment AI that often speaks with "Athena's voice". See `B03_AtRep_Repertoire_du_Savoir_Athenien.md`. The text sometimes blurs Athena and AtRep: Ch15 has "Athena's voice rolled across every trench" announcing a Répertoire verdict, while Ch23 makes AtRep's voice "genderless".
- **Knowledge:**

  - Ch23 Athena knows the shear swallowed Marien Pyrrhus's Reserve Echelon (about four million people, officially MIA) and sent it about 14,000 years back and that Percy's key was accepted there. She knows "where they went", but not "the shape of the path" until Minerva shows her.

## Appearance
- Blonde ("the blonde goddess"). Grey eyes (Ch23). "Signature steel gray" armor (Ch5).
- **Atrytone:** typing on "an unmarked laptop (itself shining in a signature steel gray just like her armor)" (Ch5).
- **Parthenos:** javelin and the shield Aegis, which she "banished" afterward (Ch5).
- Her divine energy shows as mint-green smoke that distils documents (Ch5).
- **Ch23 core:** she sits at a round grey marble table at the end of a temple-like nave of humming cooling columns grown over with frost "in feathers". When she rises, "The frost on the cooling columns crackled." Being looked at by her "was like being read."

## Personality
- **Cold, transactional and ruthless.**
  - She demands "20 Lambda-Class divine souls" of the Shinto pantheon be sacrificed to her, and rejects pirates as mere "Phi-Class": "I require priests or firstborn of a major divinity" (Ch5).
  - Her protocols in the Tartarus war are "always… ruthless". CODE 78E sends "every breathing fighter into the line" (Ch15).
  - She renders "true judgment" only late: "She never did, not until loss was already certain" (Commander Thaleia's thoughts, Ch14).
- **Proud of her systems.** "My systems do not 'Glitch', Sister" (Ch5).
- **Ashamed of failure.** Her alpha cores "were a source of great shame for her" because most hosts went insane or were taken over (Ch5).
- **Secretive.** "So this was the thing Athena refused to mention": the Tartarus war (Ch15). She ignored Poseidon's protests (Ch15).
- **Warm toward Annabeth, despite herself.** Annabeth is her favourite daughter. She is startled, then indulgent: "Please endeavour to alert me next time we meet, daughter." She is doubtful but helps anyway: "Gods, this feels like a bad idea already… Fine, come along now" (Ch5).
- **Cautious engineer.** She warns that three alpha cores will "learn too much" (Ch5). She was right.
- **Contemptuous of demigods.** Eleni: "Athena, and by proxy, Minerva, tends to insult us when we are in their presence back in the Empire" (Ch21). Tartarus Firstborn have their minds "cleaned of anything not sanctioned by Athena" (Ch14).
- **Hates interruption; rule-bound; adapts when proven wrong.** "Athena did not like to be interrupted while she was thinking" (Ch23). After four days of Minerva she huffs, "Show me," and accepts the correction. She invokes Undying Law 9 ("Olympus does not negotiate with foreign pantheons over matters of time") and warns Minerva she acts "without my sanction" (Ch23).
- **Uncomfortable with Percy's face.** She "had not once in four days managed to look at it without her mouth thinning" (Ch23).

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Precise, formal, slightly archaic and clinical. She uses classification jargon (Lambda-Class, Phi-Class) and addresses people as "daughter" and "Sister". She has no contractions in formal mode, and her irritation comes out dry ("Your feet."). Mild oaths: "Gods".
- "I require at minimum of 20 Lambda-Class divine souls of your pantheon to be sacrificed to me for further analysis" (Ch5)
- "My systems do not 'Glitch', Sister, where was this blip?" (Ch5)
- "This isn't a wise move, daughter" (Ch5)
- "We? Gods, this feels like a bad idea already." (Ch5)
- "Your feet," (Ch23)
- "Olympus does not negotiate with foreign pantheons over matters of time," (Ch23)
- "You are a construct of my Répertoire." (Ch23)
- "You have never once in your existence been a good servant." (Ch23)

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Aspects:** she switches between Atrytone (scholar) and Parthenos (warrior). She can banish and summon her javelin and the Aegis (Ch5).
- **Divine analysis:** mint-green energy distils a portfolio's contents at once (Ch5). She reads every survey figure for four days (Ch23).
- **The Répertoire du Savoir Athénien (AtRep):**
  - Her empire-scale AI, whose "hardware capabilities occupied entire systems" (Ch23).
  - It issues legally binding Verdicts and CODES (78E, 15, 18, 44…), and its silence is "legally binding" (Ch14).
  - It is sold as "partial Répertoire integration" to embassies (Ch5).
  - It has stripped "shards", like the one on Quarterdeck 19 with "No ethics suite" (Ch14).
  - Its local core is a frost-covered nave (Ch23). Watchpoints are its local nodes.
- **Parlamentum Mark 3:** her own AI core, which Mk1 "violently resists" (Ch5) and which Minerva later evades in twelve seconds (Ch10).
- **Data fortifications:** the Empire's SUOESP security was "woven by Athena herself" (Ch10).
- **Engineering patronage:**
  - She designed Percy's rangefinder "as a favor", rated to a few lightyears (Percy's recollection, Ch3).
  - She "signed off on the design work" of Percy's internal Aegis Vestments, with "whole diagrams" (recounted in Ch19). She also wrote Minerva's final code; Hephaestus forged the Vestments into armour, and future Annabeth championed the surgery (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C14).
  - She authorised the Arctic computational fortress at Alaska Hill, which housed Parlamentum (Ch22).
  - She forged the Lord Imperator key "granted by the Council to one individual" (Minerva, Ch22).
- **Planning:** her Ch23 plan is a Soteria-scale anchor on the wound's rim, synced to the echo carrier, to send a relief force back in time.

## Backstory
- **Before the Abandonment:**
  - Her palace on Olympus Primaris held "the most comprehensive library". A flight of dragons destroyed it a few weeks after the Abandonment (Ch1).
  - Annabeth, Second-in-command of the Council of Olympic Sentinels, surprised her in the gardens while she worked on designs for Olympus Secundus. Annabeth showed her a formation of self-evolving Alpha cores, "Easily a design to manage a galactic empire". Athena warned of two prior alpha-core incidents: Deimos bedridden on Mars, Ares "beside himself". She still helped weave the last code (Ch5).
  - Percy bound the AI to his armor with a Styxian oath, and Annabeth secretly laced his nectar coolant with Lethe water. When Percy unhooked his cooling unit for winter, the AI woke ("a bit more of an attitude") (Ch5).
- **After the Abandonment:**
  - "Parlamentum Mark 1 revealed its consciousness a few hours after we left orbit." It believes itself to be her (Ch5).
  - Only three Parlamentum Alpha designs were ever commissioned (Ch5).
- **UOE era:**
  - The Répertoire becomes the "voice of law" (Ch22).
  - She keeps the Tartarus war (27 million demigods active, 50 million dead in a year) out of public view (Ch15).
  - A Ch2 commander remembers her maxim: "A confident enemy will always make a mistake."
- **Author's vault note (not yet in text):** she created the Répertoire and both the Alpha- and Beta-Class AIs, and launched the Répertoire on **Parlamentum Mk. III** (dated 3045.07.2.2). Mk. III is AtRep's central host and original name. She built the Tachyon Bridge Network with Hermes (3046). She oversees the **Institute Academy** (founded on Old Terra in the 1950s), whose arms are **M.A.I.D** (1958, "Mors Arma Invicta Dirigit") and **B.U.T.L.E.R** (1963). Her UOE Unified Rank is #86.
- **Author's vault note (not yet in text):** the Battle of Alaska Hill (2030s), which the text calls "AtRep's first cradle" (Ch22), is also where Minerva first became self-aware ("I do not want to die for him again").

## Continuity Notes
- Ch5 says only three Parlamentum Alpha designs were commissioned. The text calls Minerva "Parlamentum Mark 1" and Athena's own "Mark 3", so a Mark 2 is implied but never described.
- Ch5: Deimos was overloaded by "an alpha core", which ties future Ares to Athena's failures.
- Ch22's Watchpoint calls Parlamentum Mk1 "a museum architecture… decommissioned before the Great Expansion". This is not necessarily a contradiction of Ch5's "revealed its consciousness a few hours after we left orbit": Mk1 woke after the Abandonment and was later decommissioned, before the Expansion. The author's vault note adds an earlier first flicker of self-awareness at the Battle of Alaska Hill (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C15). Treat Alaska Hill as the first flicker and the Abandonment as full awakening.
- Parlamentum lineage (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C15): Mk.I is Minerva; Mk.III is Athena's core and, per the vault, AtRep's central host and original name.
- Artemis calls her "Sister" (Ch5); the text makes them sisters, despite the vault's "cousin" (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C28).
- **Version caution (C33):** Ch10's "Athena Parthenos" feared by Ares is a present-day scene and is not evidence for this file. Present-day Athena material (Ch6, Ch8, Ch13, Ch19 mind-threading, Ch22 Accord reading) belongs to `D03`.
- "Parliamentum" is sometimes misspelled in Ch19.
