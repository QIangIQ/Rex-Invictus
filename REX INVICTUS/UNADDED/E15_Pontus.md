## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
Only one version exists in the story. He is distinct from Poseidon ("the King") and from Manannán mac Lir (`E18_Amaterasu_and_Foreign_Pantheons.md`). He seems to perceive Percy's displaced future and Minerva.

## Appearance
- Only his voice, and Percy's reflection "blinked" in the water.
- **The shrine:** "a crumbling half-circle of marble sunk into the earth, choked by moss and ivy. Barnacles clung to the base of its old columns. At its center, a stone bowl filled with brackish water" (Ch13). It is hidden along the Long Island coast; Percy knows of it from the future.

## Personality
- **Perceptive and cryptic:** he names what Percy carries and what Percy is.
- **Proud of his old dominion:** "Atlantis watches through thee. But the currents still remember my name."
- **Transactional:** he grants transport against a future offering.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Old and rasping, prophetic, archaic ("thee"), in short declaratives.
- "You carry the scent of the King" (Ch13)
- "You walk with a dead future, mortal. A goddess with no pantheon. A war with no victor." (Ch13)
- "Atlantis watches through thee. But the currents still remember my name. What do you wish for?" (Ch13)

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Sea summoning:** a "gust of sea-wind blasted out from the bowl". He produces an **obsidian-black coin engraved with a spiral trident**, and raises a ship: "No sails. No crew. Just a long, narrow vessel carved from something too dark to be wood." It "emerged like a wound in the sea" and moves of its own will (Ch13–14).
- **Prophetic perception:** "a dead future" (Percy's lost future), "A goddess with no pantheon" (possibly Minerva, possibly future Artemis; ambiguous), "A war with no victor" (possibly the Tartarus front).

## Backstory
Canon: the primordial sea, older than the Olympians. The story calls the shrine his "last known marker".
- **Author's vault note (not yet in text):** his UOE Unified Rank is #10. In the far future the sea-goddess **Ceto** lives in Poseidon's New Atlantis, "fleeing Pontus", which suggests Pontus is still active and hostile in the Empire era (vault only; no far-future Pontus appears in the text).
