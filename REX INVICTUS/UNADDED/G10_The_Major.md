## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
He is one person. Do not confuse him with the nameless **Legacy-soldier narrator** of Segment Twelve (see `H16_Callistus_Narrator_and_Tartarus_Front_Soldiers.md`), whose first-person account precedes his. Note that the Major's own section also slips into first person ("I said", "my command node"). He is also distinct from Marien's unnamed bodyguard, who followed her into the void and vanished.

## Appearance
He wears an armored kit with augur optics. No physical description is given beyond that.

## Personality
- **Anti-deception discipline.** "no order is valid unless it carries my signature and the signature of your immediate commander… treated as a Code 1" (Ch19).
- **Numbers-driven.** He reports attrition "in the only currency that commanders respected" (Ch19).
- **Fear-driven pragmatism.** He takes the ships into the void because "angering Stephanos Pyrrhus offered certainty" of annihilation (Ch19).
- **Efficient builder.** He has the Mars outpost looking weeks old within an hour.
- **Dry fatalism.** On the task-force name ORPHEUS: "Surely it knows how that story ends?" On concealment: "Partial," … "Partial?" (Ch22).
- **Endurance.** He stood eleven hours beside the Watchpoint watching casualty reports (Ch22).

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Clipped, formal, professional. He uses "Ma'am", quietly swears, and asks short questions.

**Quotes:**
- "We held." (Ch19)
- "Establish perimeter. Establish trenches. Establish sensor grid. Assume hostile contact within one Gaian hour." (Ch19, unattributed; the surrounding paragraphs are the Major's, so context implies him)
- "Legacy human." (Ch19)
- "Repaired by whom?" (Ch22)
- "Surely it knows how that story ends?" (Ch22)

## Backstory
He commanded the Segment Twelve node on the Tartarean Front. He was there when Marien's inspection arrived.

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch19.**
  - He issues the signature rule and reads Marien's callsign.
  - He meets her on the landing shelf and reports that the demigods are burning out.
  - The shelf dematerializes and Marien falls. He reports "Marien Pyrrhus has fallen through a subreality dematerialization event", then orders the ships into the void.
  - On Mars he sets up trenches and sensors, reports to Marien, and analyses the "Legacy human" rover Perseverance.
- **Ch22.**
  - He stands at attention by the Watchpoint as Kappa-Seven is listed MIA. He asks "Repaired by whom?" and suggests Hermes moved the cell.
  - He swears at the 61% probability of CODE 4, worries about mortal orbiters, and questions "Partial" concealment.
  - He is present for the Minerva intrusion and CODE 18.

## Continuity Notes
- His Ch19 section shifts between "I" and "he" narration.
- "The Major steeped his hands" [sic] means *steepled*.
- **Ships.** The text says only "the ships" / "two ships" (Ch19) and "two carriers" (Ch22). The Ch19 Segment Twelve readout lists "UOE Axis Dawn" and "UOE Warbringer Fury" as orbital assets, and the author's Plot Threads identifies them as the two ships that fell to Mars (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C26).
- **Time gap** (see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R3): the echelon landed about 14,000 years before its own era; Ch22's "six thousand years" is a known slip.
