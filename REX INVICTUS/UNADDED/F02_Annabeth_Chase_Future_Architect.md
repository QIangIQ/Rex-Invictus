## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **This file**: the adult Annabeth of Percy's ORIGINAL life. She was his partner and later his "ex". She was Second-in-command of the Sentinels, re-engineered his power suit's cooling (the suit itself is "of his own design", Ch1), and built Parlamentum Mk I, the AI that became Minerva. ## Appearance
- A "mop of honey-blonde hair eerily similar" to Athena's (Ch5).
- In the flashback she wears "ivory-white armor that only one of her daughters could wear" and a face shield developed with Iris. The shield gives her "a live feed for my eyes to counter stuff like" the Aegis. It turns transparent, and "fluid armor" pools away from her throat (Ch5).
- The legendary statue shows her at about 17, back to back with Percy, holding a bronze knife, her hair "pulled back in a knot." It is famous for getting her chin right ("Nobody ever got Annabeth's chin right", Ch23).
- An old Gaia photograph kept by the Chase Dynasty shows her "standing on a porch with a stupid Yankees cap" (Ch23).
- Minerva's archived memory of her canon first day (Ch23): "the girl on the hill, in the orange shirt, with the knife."
- Her face recurs in Verrill Chase: curly blonde hair, a narrow sharp face, a stubborn chin and storm-grey eyes (Ch23).

## Personality
- **Bold, playful and fearless.** She grabs Athena by the shoulder from behind, nearly getting a javelin in the throat. She then cheerfully raises her hands with a portfolio: "Mum, I've come with a project that might interest you" (Ch5).
- **Inventive and ambitious.** She designs a formation of Alpha cores that "would continually evolve, mutate, and duplicate", which Athena judges "Easily a design to manage a galactic empire" (Ch5).
- **Sneaky and paternalistic toward Percy.** She has "taken the liberty to secretly redesign his internal nectar cooling" with Lethe water as a cognitive limiter on the AI (Ch5). She keeps safety measures from him.
- **Confident in her team.** "We are confident in its success" is about herself and "Perce" (Ch5).
- **A joker.** During his suit test flights she quips, "It's not the fall that kills you, it's the sudden stop at the end" (Ch3). Percy remembers it "ruefully."
- **Enthusiastic about augmentation.** When Percy's internal Vestments were proposed, "Annabeth was really excited" and "technically Annabeth helped convince me" to have the surgery (Ch19).
- **Emotional and bonded to Percy.** After the Battle of Manhattan she sat filthy and exhausted on the Empire State Building steps, leaning on Percy. She told Thalia "We did it, Thals," and burst into tears (Ch23, Thalia's memory). The two "argued with each other the way other people breathed" (Ch23).

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Master engineer and AI architect.**
  - She designed the physical side of **Parlamentum Alpha Mark 1** and asked Athena to "weave the last lines of code together" (Ch5).
  - Her limiter design: an internal nectar coolant laced with Lethe water, run through the AI processors (Ch5).
  - She re-engineered Percy's exosuit: she integrated the AI into it and redesigned its "internal nectar cooling" (Ch5). Ch1 calls the suit "of his own design". The text never says she designed or co-designed the suit itself, so profiles must not say so (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C14). She worked on its cooling, AI and augments, and her test-flight joke (Ch3) suggests she was involved in its trials.
- **Augments.** "Annabeth's augments" finally fixed Percy's notoriously slow speed after "a special programme had only done so much" (Ch8).
- **"AnnChase Battle Assistance."** A combat-prediction subsystem in Percy's armor, "refined through millennia of raw data. Courtesy of Annabeth's assistant" (Ch10). Minerva can still loan it to present-day Percy.
- **Aegis Vestments** (Ch19). Annabeth championed the surgical integration of Percy's internal armor; she did not design it. Athena signed off on the design work ("Athena signed off on the design work", Ch19), wrote Minerva's final code, and Hephaestus forged it (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C14).
- **Her own armor.** Ivory-white fluid armor and an Iris-made anti-Aegis face shield (Ch5).
- **Legacy hardware in the UOE era.**
  - **AnnChase-pattern/AnnChase-class** cruisers and battleships: 12-km bronze-green slabs, built "back when… the Empire was young" (Ch23).
  - The *Blood Martyr* (Ch11) and *The Eye of Light*, AC-09-1642 (Ch15).
  - Her contingency life-support designs in Eleni's armor (Ch15).

## Backstory
- **Canon past (assumed, mostly unstated).** Old-timeline Percy lived the canon events: the Master Bolt (Ch1), Atlas (Ch1), the Battle of Manhattan (Ch23). So this Annabeth presumably shared the canon PJO arc with him. Minerva's memory of "the first day… a boy with a Minotaur's horn" confirms their canon first meeting (Ch23).
- **Battle of Manhattan.** She fought beside Percy, and the two became "The Two" of legend (Ch23).
- **Sentinel era (old timeline).**
  - She was Second-in-command of the Council of Olympic Sentinels, presumably under Lord-Commander Percy.
  - She and Percy were together at some point; by his far-future life she was his "ex" (Ch3). The breakup is never described.
  - She designed Parlamentum Mk I while Olympus Secundus was being designed (Ch5).
  - Author's vault note (not yet in text): she was **Vice-President** of the Council of Olympic Sentinels, **co-commander at the Battle of Alaska Hill** (2030s; 2032 per the vault), and ranked **#153** in the UOE Unified Alltime Rankings. She also served as **ambassador to the Egyptian, Norse and Indian pantheons**.
  - Author's vault note (not yet in text): the vault calls her a co-designer of Parlamentum Mk.I, and Plot Threads describes her as the High-Lord-Engineer who designs the armor and the AnnChase-class ships. Treat the armour credit as author intent only; the published text credits the suit to Percy (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C14).
- **Athena's view.** Mk I "revealed its consciousness a few hours after we left orbit." Only three Parlamentum Alpha designs were ever commissioned (Ch5). So Annabeth's AI outlived the gods' presence on Earth and became Minerva.
- **Later legend.**
  - The Empire built "AnnChase-pattern" warships and a 30-m bronze Monument of the Two on the Via Heroum in New Rome, Voss Secundus (Ch23).
  - The Chase Dynasty keeps her photographs. Her face "had not produced" itself again in that bloodline "in nine thousand years" until Verrill (Ch23).

## Continuity Notes
- **"Ex" placement.** The word "ex" appears in Ch3, not Ch1.
- **Spelling.** "Mum" (Ch5) is British usage.
- **Time figures (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R3).** The canonical distance from the present to the UOE era is about 14,000 years (UOE year ~16231; Ch23's "FOURTEEN THOUSAND [CORRUPTED] YEARS"). The other figures measure different things: Percy estimates he jumped about 20,000 years back (Ch3), and Ch22 calls the key's owner "six thousand years old" (Ch22:1303; Luke repeats "six thousand years", Ch22:1533), a known slip. Monument dates should be reckoned from the ~14,000-year figure.
- **Thalia at Manhattan.** Ch23 contradicts itself: l.678 says she "hadn't been at the Battle of Manhattan. She'd been with the Hunt that night, holding the bridges", and l.858 says she "held the bridges at the Battle of Manhattan". Harmonised reading: she held the bridges on the battle's perimeter and was not at the Empire State Building fight. Flagged as a story-level slip in 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md §5.
- **Design credit.** Earlier drafts of this file said she built his armor or co-designed his power suit. The text credits the suit to Percy ("of his own design", Ch1); see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C14.
- **Percy's chin.** Eleni's murals "never quite captured" Percy's chin (Ch15), while Annabeth's chin is the famous hard part on the statue (Ch23). This is a deliberate echo, not a contradiction.
