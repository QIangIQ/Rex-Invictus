## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
Stephanos exists only in the far future. He is not Percy's son. He is a son of Poseidon from Percy's distant future, and Eleni says the Pyrrhus dynasty are Percy's "half-brothers" (Ch22). He is distinct from:
- his daughter **Marien Pyrrhus** (`G02_Marien_Pyrrhus.md`), who is displaced to the present;
- **Rammael Marccusson**, an old-timeline son of Poseidon (`G13_Rammael_Marccusson.md`).

**Nikos Pyrrhus** appears only in the Ch2 author's note, as a planned OC: "WW2 Veteran, now in C.O.S" (the vault's ASSETS note says "now in COS", i.e. the Council of Olympic Sentinels). He shares the surname, but no relation is stated and he never appears in the story; the vault treats him as an unused idea for a present-day or old-timeline Pyrrhus ancestor. Do not confuse him with **Niko**, pilot of the *Vengeful Wrath* in Ch2, whom the vault names **Nikos Phobos**, demigod of Hades (`G12_Rex_Imperator_II_Cast.md`).

## Appearance
- Sea-green eyes that "hardened" (Ch2).
- His pauldrons are "far more adorned with marks of authority and rank" than Damocles's.
- His voice "rumbled like the sea, the signature accent of Poseidon's' spawn".
- His Honour Guard Titans carry azure-blue banners bearing "the azure blue icon of one of the guards of a Demigod of Poseidon" (Ch12).

## Personality
- **Measured and commanding.** He gives Damocles orders "measured and commanding". He insists on keeping captured commanders as hostages because "Our fathers would be greatly displeased at your losses" (Ch2).
- **Strategist.** He brings 13 dreadnoughts from behind the enemy to arrive "at the opportune moment". He spots the gap caused by sleeper-cell sabotage at once (Ch2).
- **Irritable with incompetent allies.** "Why do I always have to pick after you little shits?" He develops "a rapidly developing migraine" and complains about Aphrodite's "lax security" (Ch2).
- **Pious.** "With a silent prayer to his divine lineage… He could only leave it up to his Father now." (Ch2).
- **Feared.** His own officers dread him. The Major follows Marien into the void because "angering Stephanos Pyrrhus offered certainty" of annihilation (Ch19).

## Voice & Speech Patterns

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch2.** He calls Damocles to remind him of their "arrangement" and pledges 13 dreadnoughts arriving "within the next 6 Gaian minutes" (Eleni's augur then reads "Expected time until contact, 240 Gaian seconds"). On approach he sees the gap in Damocles's attack: overextension by Niko's ship and a sleeper-cell-sabotaged Aphrodite ship beside it (the *Radiant Crusade*). He berates a stalling Damocles, orders interceptors, boarding parties and a tractor beam, and prays to his father. The battle ends with Melina Callisto's *Radiant Crusade* ramming Eleni's *Divine Crusader* in a Terra Concrematum.
- **Ch5 (probable).** Future Zeus has lost his daughter "Eleni" to "Poseidon's damned direct descendant", and Poseidon gloats. Stephanos is the obvious candidate, but he is not named.
- **Ch12.** In Poseidon's realm a few Titans of "Stephanos Pyrrus' Honour Guard" [sic], who themselves "recently came back from a border skirmish", escort Colonel Tony's party. Their "liege has retreated to the Master's palace".
- **Ch19.** Marien is identified as his child. The Major dreads his wrath.
- **Ch22.** Eleni blames the Pyrrhus dynasty for her defeat and tells Percy that it "is one of the strongest in the rankings and are your half-brothers" [sic].

## Continuity Notes
- **Spelling** (see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md S1): "Pyrrhus" (Ch2, Ch19, Ch22, Ch23) is the canonical form. Ch12 spells it "Pyrrus" and the vault files Marien as "Phyrrus"; both are variants only.
- **Rank** (see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C29): "Ranked 51st" (text, Ch2) over the vault's 251st.
- Damocles says "The Eternal Devotion will not disappoint" (Ch2), but that is Stephanos's ship (text and vault). This is probably an author slip.
- **Ch12 messenger** (see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md S5): she is **Cyria** (five uses); the text's two "Cyrus" forms are slips.
- **Arrival time**: Stephanos promises "6 Gaian minutes"; Eleni's augur shows "240 Gaian seconds" (Ch2).
- **"Half-brothers"** (Ch22) is Eleni's word for the Pyrrhus dynasty; it presumably means fellow descendants of Poseidon, not literal half-brothers of Percy.
- **Who killed Eleni**: Ch2 shows Melina's ramming within the coalition; Ch5 has Zeus blame "Poseidon's damned direct descendant"; Ch22 has Eleni blame the Pyrrhus dynasty. The text never names Stephanos as her killer.
