## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **This file:** future Aphrodite. Her charm has been magnified by 18,000 years of worship until mortals cannot safely look at her, and she is cybernetically linked to her ship through a neural wire.
- **Present-day Aphrodite** is a minor figure, "smiling at nothing in particular" at the Ch19 council. See `D13_Aphrodite_Present.md`. The future Aphrodite's memory of "a boy with a sword" and of Artemis "a girl on a dock" refers to the present-day era from her own past.
- **Knowledge:** she knows the Lord Imperator verdict (Ch23). She calls Artemis her sister-in-law, implying the future Percy–Artemis marriage. She senses "Chaos… on the other side" of the wound.
- **Timeline:** Ch23's future explicitly remembers Ch22's present-day events (Artemis recalls "a night on Long Island when soldiers fell out of the sky"). This Aphrodite therefore belongs to a future linked to the present-day Rex timeline. Whether it is the same future as Ch1's old timeline is unresolved.

## Appearance
- **Veiled:** her divine charm "had been drawn out and strengthened over eighteen thousand years of worship until it was no longer something a mortal could survive looking at directly". Even veiled it presses "like heat from an open oven". Young officers who glance at her lose minutes staring, flushed (Ch23).
- **The neural wire:**
  - A single wire "snaked out" from the nape of her neck, "slow and sinuous", tipped with a polished bronze socket that plugs into her throne.
  - Its colours follow her mood: pink when pleased; soft grey-blue when chastened; gold when delighted or excited; deep glowing rose when sensing chaos; white when thoughtful or shocked; steady quiet blue when listening and grieving; "the soft deep blue of the sea at dusk" through tears.
  - Unplugged, she wears it "coiled loosely over one shoulder like a pet snake".
- **The mechanical arm:** she descends from the ceiling iris on "a long, jointed thing of bronze and ivory, segmented like the spine of some enormous animal". She rides it "the way a queen rides a palanquin, reclining, one hand trailing lazily".
- **The throne:** rose-gold and mother-of-pearl, shaped like an open scallop shell. It "flowed around her body" to fit her.
- **Gown:** pale. When she walks through Tartarus mud, "it did not get muddy".
- **Voice:** "low and warm and faintly amused", and "sounded like it was standing right beside her, speaking into her ear". She giggles, "a girlish, bubbling sound".

## Personality
- **Whimsical, childlike and theatrical.**
  - Her opening line is "Good evening, my darlings… Mother is here."
  - She loves "the numbers" and tea, and scolds the cherubs for "zooming about, it's rude."
  - She claps with delight at twelve thousand survey squads, and admits Athena was furious and that she'll give the scholars back: "Most of them."
- **Tactless, then contrite.** On arrival, telling the officers to sit, she quips "It's like some died in here!" When Sevrin reminds her, "Approximately four million people", she says "That was a silly thing for me to say, wasn't it? Val-Val, remind me not to say silly things."
- **Profoundly compassionate.** She insists every one of the 3,998,050 names be read aloud before they go to the Wall: about 92 days, read by a hundred of her own choristers. "Her Majesty feels that four million people should not go into the stone without anyone having said their names." She walks alone to the wound, asks Tullia how many legionaries she has lost (1,104) and whether she knew their names, and weeps: "Then you know," … "why I came."
- **Terrifyingly ruthless in protection.** "I want it erased from the pit so thoroughly that Tartarus forgets how to make it. I want it gone. I want its name gone. I want its mother to forget she ever had it." Her Spears grin.
- **Perceptive, a reader of hearts.** She knows the monsters fear "what went out" of the wound, not the wound itself. She smells "Chaos… On the other side. A great deal of it." She senses "a great deal of" love there too.
- **Teasing intimacy with Artemis.** "Sister-in-law… Hello, hello. You came quickly." She teases Artemis about having been "there" 14,000 years ago and slips into "Art– ahem… The Director".
- **Hopeful.** When the verdict confirms the Lord Imperator, she "began, very quietly, to laugh", and her wire pulses "like a heart beating". She orders the line held "until the door opens again".

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Sweet and endearing, with pet names everywhere ("my darlings", "sweetheart", "dear", "Val-Val"). She asks rhetorical questions and switches suddenly between whimsy and steel. She uses exclamations ("Isn't it wonderful?", "Perhaps next Tuesday!"). She does not swear. Her threats are delivered in the same soft voice.
- "Good evening, my darlings," … "Mother is here." (Ch23)
- "Val-Val, remind me not to say silly things." (Ch23)
- "They're not afraid of the wound, sweetheart. They're afraid of what went out of it." (Ch23)
- "Chaos," … "On the other side. A great deal of it." (Ch23)
- "I want it gone. I want its name gone. I want its mother to forget she ever had it." (Ch23)
- "Now. Somebody bring me a cup of tea." (Ch23)
- "Fourteen thousand years, perhaps. Perhaps a week. Perhaps next Tuesday!" (Ch23)
- "But I have always found that love is stranger." (Ch23)

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Unsurvivable charm:** direct sight of her is dangerous to mortals. Scholars who see her pass "could not remember afterward what they had been doing". Legionaries feel her "like the warmth of a fire on a cold night".
- **The best augurs in the Empire:** "They can taste the shape of a thing that isn't there anymore. Athena's can't. Hermes's can't. Mine can." (Ch23)
- **Mud repels her:** her gown and her path stay clean.
- **Neural interface:** the wire links her to the throne, and every screen on the bridge flickers in answer.
- **The *Aphrodite's Grace*:**
  - A 35-km white-marble-and-gold hospital and sanctuary ship. It has no guns and has "never carried guns".
  - Its prow is a statue of a woman rising from a scallop shell with her eyes closed. It carries gardens of cypress, myrtle and roses under glass domes, fountains, and thousands of hospital wards.
  - Its bridge is the **Rose Gallery**: polished bronze, ivory levers, white marble from Gaia, tapestries of the gods, a bronze-petal iris, masked women at the stations, porcelain-masked servitors, and baby-sized cherubs with bows and censers of rose-and-honey incense.
- **The Dove's Detachment** (white-and-gold chevrons):
  - 38,000 Vanguard-pattern corvettes.
  - 11,000 Mastodon light frigates.
  - 400 Hammerheads.
  - 250 AnnChase cruisers.
  - 40 Perseus-pattern dreadnoughts.
  - The Jackson-pattern dreadnought *Sovereign's Glory* and the *Flame of Atrox* on her flanks (ship class of the *Flame of Atrox* unstated; see Continuity Notes).
  - The **Rosed Knights** Titan legion: forty 11-m jewelled frames.
- **Her champions:**
  - Helion Bulwark Stephanie Callidora (gold).
  - Selenide Bulwark Michael Black (silver).
  - Helion Spear Lysander Chrysos.
  - Selenide Spear Thessaly Nox.
  - Fleet Commander Valeria Sevrin.
  - Note: Bulwark and Spear (Helion/Selenide) are guardian ranks that exist under any god; they are not unique to Aphrodite (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C25).
- **Requisition power:** she pulled 12,000 survey squads, the Institute Academy's whole chronometric faculty, Tochter des Elysium's reality-mapping division, Hecatean augurs, Athenian archivists, Perioeci townships and 4,000 Apollonian medics.

## Backstory
- **Old timeline:** her palace on Olympus Primaris is "with colors so faded it could pass as greyscale" (Ch1).
- **Ch2 era:**
  - Her home system is the Anadyomene Belt, which relies on volunteers instead of conscription. That made it vulnerable to the sleeper cells that crippled the *Radiant Crusade*.
  - Her daughter Melina Callisto rams Eleni Theodosia's ship at Fulguris and dies in a Terra Concrematum, invoking "our Divine Being, The Heartmaker" and "the Aura of Love".
  - Stephanos Pyrrhus disdains Aphrodite's lax security.
- **Past (from her Ch23 memory):**
  - She knew Percy "when he was just a boy with a sword and a sea god for a father and a terrible habit of getting himself hurt".
  - She knew Artemis "when she was just a girl on a dock with a boy who could never stay out of trouble". This echoes the dock scenes of the present-day story (Ch22).
  - Percy is her brother-in-law through Artemis: she greets Artemis as "Sister-in-law" (Ch23).
- **Author's vault note (not yet in text):** in the War of Princes she commanded **Vanguard Fleet Alpha** from the 35-km flagship *UOE Aphrodite's Grace*. She cloaked the fleet with a Dematerialization Field and used **Fortuna's** favour (16214). Her servants are Stephanie Callidora, Helion Bulwark (*UOE Sovereign's Glory*), and Michael Black, Selenide Bulwark (*UOE Flame of Ambition*). Her fleet staff in 16225 included Valeria Sevrin, Fleet Commander Class II (Strategic Coordination). Her UOE Unified Rank is #146.

## Continuity Notes
- "It's like some died in here!" [sic] is a typo-ish line (probably meaning someone died).
- Ch23's "Fourteen thousand years ago. A thousand years before the Abandonment, even" is consistent with the canon timeline: the Abandonment is year 3045, about a thousand years after Percy's youth (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R4). The far-future scenes are about 14,000 years after the present (R3). Percy's "approximately twenty thousand standard years" (Ch3) is his own estimate of his jump and measures something else; it is not a rival answer.
- **Ship names:** the text's *Flame of Atrox* (Ch23) may be the author's *Flame of Ambition* (Michael Black's ship in the vault). Keep the text spelling and flag it.
- **Melina Callisto** is a daughter of Aphrodite in the text (three times). The vault ship list calls her a daughter of Demeter; the text wins (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md S3).
- "Sister-in-law" is capitalised only as a spoken address (Ch23).
- **Version caution (C33):** this file uses only far-future scenes (Ch1, Ch2, Ch23). Present-day Aphrodite material (Ch19 council, Ch22) belongs to `D13`.
- The narration says the fleet was "assembled to guard each of the Twelve", which suggests each Olympian has such a fleet.
