## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- He has a single on-page version, the present-day king who dies in Ch4.
- Do not confuse **Lycaon the person** with **"Lycaon's heart"**, the organ now inside Percy (see `A02_Percy_Reincarnated_Rex.md`). The heart keeps "base divine functionality" (Ch6), but it is never shown to hold Lycaon's mind.
- Percy's old timeline: Percy expected the Hunters "to chase the remnants of Lycaon's…" pack at this point (Ch4), which suggests that in his original life Lycaon was hunted by Artemis's Hunters instead. *Canon baseline (not story):* in Riordan's *The Lost Hero*, the Hunters are tracking Lycaon and his wolves.

## Appearance
- He is reduced to "the signature hunched, twisted hybrid... a form of sinewed muscle and fur." His "usual human form" is lost for the moment (Ch4).
- He is wounded by "Heavy arrows" and "silver daggers." He limps, picks at scabs, and has left claw marks on the den walls in "fits of rage" (Ch4).
- His **pelt is crimson**. The Hunters first take it for "a rug of some kind" (Ch4).

## Personality
- **Fallen pride.** "He'd once been a king, proud and arrogant. But now, he was degraded into a monster, bound by the moon's cycles" (Ch4).
- **Selfish instinct, overruled by duty.** He meant to take the safe inner den for himself, "but had to accept that it was for the good of the Pack."
- **Grateful for loyalty.** He nods his thanks to Nyctimus.
- **A careful survivor.** He keeps dens "littered across America," warded with Latin runes, orders a night watch, and tells the pack to avoid moonlight.
- **Bitter and wild.** The fits of rage show in the claw-scarred walls.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
He says very little on the page. His voice is weary and commanding, a king giving orders through pain. The pack speaks to him with feudal respect. Quotes (pack lines are labelled):
- "Fine, tell them to prioritize the protection of the young, and the rest are to sleep outside." (Lycaon, Ch4)
- "Avoid direct beams of moonlight, and set up a night watch schedule for a few days as we recover." (Lycaon, Ch4)
- "Rest, my King," … "We'll guard the entrance." (Nyctimus, Ch4)
- "Pack leader, the young are exhausted from the burdens of traveling this far." (Lycurgus Nikias, Ch4)
- "Use the heart wisely, do not fall under its vices." (Artemis, about his heart, Ch6)
- "You carry the stench of the Hunt and something... mangy, like a dog." (nature goddess, Ch6)

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Werewolf lore (Ch4):** only Lycaon's direct descendants, "the young," can pass on the lupine curse. Other werewolves who die become "golden dust" that sinks back to Tartarus to reform. Silver wounds them.
- **Wards:** glowing Latin runes in the den hide him "from the gaze of the Moon Goddess" and keep other monsters away. Zoë speaks of his "mortal-repelling wards" (Ch6).
- **His heart in Percy:**
  - It is held together by the Répertoire's life support ("Foreign species(?)", Ch4) and diagnosed as "Improper heart assimilation" (Ch6).
  - It gives off a "wet dog" scent. Artemis senses "the shaggy feeling of a wet dog" (Ch6) and later tracks Percy by its "wet-dog trace" (Ch11).
  - It has "maintained base divine functionality" (Minerva, Ch6).
  - It helps rebuild Percy after the Minotaur fight (Ch7).
  - Its "divine origins" help him resist the Sirens (Ch14).
  - It works "triple overtime" against Polyphemus (Ch14).
  - It reminds him "that his heart was not his" after he overuses mist-travel (Ch15).
  - It is called "Lycaon's divine heart" (Ch21).
  - Percy's stated aims were to "better my current physique" and possibly "mask my demigod scent," and he called the exchange temporary ("temporarily," Ch4).

## Backstory
*Myth baseline (not story):* Lycaon was a king of Arcadia who served Zeus human flesh and was turned into a wolf. In myth Nyctimus is his son. In the story, Lycaon was once "proud and arrogant" and is now the pack's battered king, recently driven off in a fight with the Hunters of Artemis.
- **Author's vault note (not yet in text):** the vault's `Lycaon.md` calls him "Pack leader of [[Werewolves]]" and a rival of **Lupa** and the **Hounds of Artemis**; `Werewolves.md` says werewolves are "Generally all under [[Lycaon]] or [[Lupa]]"; `Hounds of Artemis.md` gives the Hounds "Enhanced detection towards [[Lycaon]] and his pack". The vault ranks him #1317 on the Unified Alltime Rankings. It names no den location; the text's Yosemite stands (C3).

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch3:** Percy tells Sally he will "chase one of the werewolf packs to Yosemite, where Lycaon is currently residing."
- **Ch4 (Lycaon POV):** Lycaon limps into a vine-covered cave den with his wounded pack. The narration places it in "Yellowstone National Park", a one-off slip: the den is at **Yosemite** (`00a_CANON_RULINGS.md` C3; see Continuity). Nyctimus offers to guard the entrance. Nikias asks that the young be given the inner den, and Lycaon agrees. Outside, the pack struggles to light a fire and cheers when it catches.
- **Ch4 (his death, from Percy's side):** Percy's order to Condottiero is "Kill the ones outside. Collapse the den, hand me the corpse of the king." The demon burns the outer wolves in their own fire and collapses the hill. The young in the inner den are buried with it. Condottiero brings out Lycaon's limp body. Learning the Hunters are near, Percy has the demon remove the heart and pelt, then cuts out his own heart and pushes Lycaon's into his chest.
- **Ch4 (aftermath):** Atlanta (the Hunt's daughter of Apollo; `C11_Atlanta_Hunter.md`) reports that almost the entire pack was wiped out, with "a few Individuals of interest" missing, and that the bodies are "decomposing into gold" rather than fading. She identifies the crimson pelt under the boy.
- **Ch6:** Artemis complains that Percy "deprived my dear hunters of the reward of a month-long hunt," and demands the pelt be sacrificed to her "by tomorrow."
- **Ch11:** Percy admits the pelt is still in his cabin: "I forgot." Artemis wipes his memories as punishment.
- **Ch16:** Artemis asks, "Tell me of your journeys after Lycaon."

## Relationships (Lycaon and Pack)
- **Nyctimus:** called "a friend." Female in this story ("she whispered"). Loyal guard of the entrance. Probably killed in the massacre.
- **Lycurgus Nikias:** "A strong subordinate" (male). Speaks for the needs of the young, carries out orders, and sighs at having to sleep outside. Probably killed. He comes from the author's Ch2 list of original characters.
- **The pack and "the young":** "bred to be fierce and loyal." The young are his direct descendants. They were sheltered in the inner den and lost when it collapsed.
- **Artemis and the Hunt** (`C01_Artemis_Present.md`, `C12_Hunters_of_Artemis_Roster.md`): his hunters. They wounded him and were cheated of the kill.
- **Percy** (`A02_Percy_Reincarnated_Rex.md`): his killer, through a demon proxy, and the bearer of his heart.
- **Condottiero and Asmodeus** (`I02_Condottiero.md`, `I01_Asmodeus.md`): his executioners. Asmodeus was enraged by "A den of mutts" in his domain.
- **Lupa** (vault; see `F17_Roman_Legion_Present.md`): per the author's vault, his rival for leadership of the wolves. Not on the page in connection with him.
- **Hounds of Artemis** (vault; see `C12_Hunters_of_Artemis_Roster.md`): per the author's vault, bred with enhanced detection to track him and his pack.

## Secrets, Mysteries & Open Threads
- What are the heart's "vices" (Artemis's warning)? Will Percy turn lupine, or will the heart affect his mind?
- Did any of "the young" or the missing "Individuals of interest" survive? If so, the curse can still spread.
- Why did the bodies turn to gold instead of fading away (Ch4)?
- What happened to the pelt? It is never confirmed as sacrificed. After the memory wipe, the Poseidon cabin feels as if "someone had come in and rearranged things just slightly" (Ch12). It is ambiguous whether someone took the pelt.

- Does Lycaon re-form in Tartarus?

## Continuity Notes
- **Location:** **Yosemite** (`00a_CANON_RULINGS.md` C3). The text says Yosemite four times in Ch3 (Percy's plan, two Labyrinth offerings, and his arrival with Half Dome in view) and again in Ch7 ("It led me out to Yosemite"). Lycaon's POV in Ch4 says "Yellowstone National Park" once; treat it as a slip.
- **Nyctimus's gender:** female here; male, and Lycaon's son, in myth.
- **The pelt's movements:** the Hunters "Bring it along" (Ch4), yet Percy later has it in his cabin (Ch11). How it came back to him is not shown.
- **Pelt deadline:** Artemis wants the pelt "by tomorrow" (Ch6), yet in Ch11 Percy is "the male she saved a few weeks ago" and still has the pelt. See `C01_Artemis_Present.md`.
- **Who reported the pack's fate:** the Ch4 Hunter who reports to Zoë is **Atlanta** (daughter of Apollo; `00a_CANON_RULINGS.md` S2, C30). "Atalanta" is reserved for the ancient heroine.
