## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- She is the only Clarisse. She is the present-day daughter of Ares and knows nothing of Percy's future past.
- Don't confuse her with the **unnamed Ares camper** who first finds Percy outside camp in Ch7 ("It wasn't Clarisse—too tall, broader shoulders"), with **Rebecca**, her Ares sister (Ch10–11), or with **Phoebe**, a Hunter and daughter of Ares.

## Appearance
- Arms folded, with an "unreadable but not overtly hostile" expression (Ch7).
- Her armor is still "smok[ing] charcoal black" from fighting a Colchis bull (Ch13).
- She wields an electric spear (Ch22).
- She "smiles" and "grins" a lot in the war-game chapters.

## Personality
- **Blunt and aggressive.** "We go after them, right? Smash whoever's behind it" (Ch13). She asks "Why can't we run in directly? Surely nobody could stop us with our powers" (Ch9).
- **Gleefully mean when someone else is in the hot seat.** She cheers on Annabeth's interrogation of Percy and laughs so hard she nearly falls out of her seat (Ch11).
- **Grudgingly respectful.** She never treats the Minotaur-killer as a joke: "People are gonna ask questions, new guy" (Ch7). Her jab, "Ol' Goldfish Genius… throws himself into the abyss and hopes it turns into a swimming pool", is half insult, half admiration (Ch11).
- **Protective of her father and side.** "Right. Next you're gonna tell me he charmed Dad into handing it over" (Ch11). But she also demands to know "why was dad holding onto the Bolt in the first place?" (Ch11).
- **Fearless to a fault.** First to reach the Imperial Titan, "screaming at the top of her lungs" (Ch22).
- **Practical about terrain.** She knows the airfield route: two kilometres direct, three indirect (Ch9).

## Voice & Speech Patterns
- Gruff, loud and slangy, with mild profanity ("Where in the Styx", "what the hell"). She calls Percy "Jackson" and "new guy", and Annabeth "Chase".

**Quotes:**

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- Ares-born combat strength. Ares's artefact buffs her at Fort Reagan, so she can carry Travis Stoll piggyback through gunfire (Ch10).
- Electric spear: it discharges "a burst of blue light that should have fried a car engine" into the Titan's knee (Ch22).
- She commands the Ares Cabin's defence of the southern treeline (Ch22).
- She ran a Fleece quest crewed by Confederate undead on the CSS *Birmingham*, which "the god of war" raised for her (Ch13–14).

## Backstory
- As a canon baseline: Ares Cabin bully turned hero, a rival of Percy who leads the Fleece quest in *Sea of Monsters*.
- Author's vault note (not yet in text; canon baseline): future girlfriend of Chris Rodriguez, and an explorer of the Labyrinth. Chris is never named in the story. (The vault's `Chris Rodriguez.md` calls him a son of Hephaestus, while canon makes him a son of Hermes; possibly a vault slip, flagged for the author.)
- In this AU she fought a Colchis bull outside camp before the quest (Ch13). Tantalus chose her "because he knew no one would object" (Junior, Ch13).

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch7.** Present when Percy arrives with the Minotaur horn. Unreadable, but warns him that people will talk.
- **Ch8.** On Zeus's jet she wins at blackjack; Silena collects pennies from everyone "except Travis and Clarisse." In Vegas she has a headache and goes to bed early. Annabeth's verdict on her: "Loud."
- **Ch9.** "Ares don't do things by halves." A sibling scolds her: "Sister, words have power, do not refer to father so directly." She questions the cell strategy and guides the route to the airfield.
- **Ch10.** Buffed by the artefact, she carries Travis toward the HQ roof and vanishes in the smoke ("no sign either of Travis or Clarisse").
- **Ch11.** Grills Percy on the bus, enjoys his discomfort, half-stands to confront Dionysus, and gets "Niece. Sit." She asks the key question: why was Ares holding the bolt?
- **Ch13.** Smoking from the bull fight, she wants to smash the attackers. Tantalus gives her the quest. She leaves "with her undead crew and a war god's smile."
- **Ch14.** Her CSS *Birmingham* lies anchored off Polyphemus's island. Percy uses its hold for Riptide's transformation. She is not seen.
- **Ch15.** "Clarisse's return, the quest a success": camp credits her with the Fleece. She glares at the newly emerged Eleni and asks "Kronos?"
- **Ch20.** At breakfast she challenges Percy's Apostolos act. He replies "Restraint at breakfast has never been among" Ares's virtues, and she rolls her eyes.
- **Ch22.** Chiron assigns the Ares Cabin to the southern treeline. When Keyes's Titan lands, she charges first and is kicked into the strawberry fields. She "didn't get up". Her half-brothers drag her out, and later fight beside the Neodamodes without her (Ch22:1504). Her fate is unstated.

## Relationships
- **Percy Jackson** (`A02_Percy_Reincarnated_Rex.md`). Teasing rival with growing respect. He needles her about restraint; she calls him "Ol' Goldfish Genius."
- **Annabeth Chase** (`F01_Annabeth_Chase_Present.md`). Rival strategist; Clarisse enjoys watching Annabeth roast Percy. Annabeth expects her to "fumble the quest" (Ch13).
- **Ares** (`D06_Ares_Present.md`). Father; she calls him "Dad." She is loyal but questions his role in the bolt affair. Ares and Percy are now enemies.
- **Dionysus** (`D07_Dionysus_MrD_Present.md`). Calls her "Niece"; he silences her.
- **Rebecca and the Ares cabin.** Siblings and battle-mates (Ch10, Ch22).
- **Travis Stoll** (`F09_Travis_and_Connor_Stoll.md`). Carried him into battle ("You're a lump", which is most likely her line).
- **Tantalus.** He picked her for the quest.
- **Keyes** (`G14_Sciritae_Neodamodes_and_Ch22_Units.md`). The Titan pilot who kicked her ("Hey there, buddy… You might want to get out of the way").

## Continuity Notes
- In Ch10 the line "You're a lump" belongs to "Travis' ride", who is Clarisse by implication.
- **Ch22 fate.** Earlier drafts of this file called her alive but injured. The text only says she "didn't get up" and was dragged out, so profiles write *fate unstated* (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C20).
- In Ch15 camp celebrates "Clarisse's return, the quest a success," even though Ch14 shows Percy recovering the Fleece. Whether she claimed the credit is unexplained.
