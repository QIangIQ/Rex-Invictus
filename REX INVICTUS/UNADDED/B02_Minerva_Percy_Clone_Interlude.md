# Minerva — "Minerva Percy," the AI Wearing Percy's Body (Far-Future Empire)
> The copy of Percy's AI that stayed behind in the Undying Olympian Empire. She wears a vat-grown body with the Lord of Demigods' face and his crooked smile, runs the Empire's warships like toys, and is now on the run across the galaxy shopping for a road into the past, so that her Varlet gets to make the choice himself.

## Personality
- **Imitating and differing from Percy is her defining tension.**
  - *She imitates deliberately:* "calculated ten thousand potential responses and chose the fourth most human" (Ch15). She simulates nervousness, a facial twitch, and blinks "at the correct tempo." She echoes "Percy's lopsided tone" ("...Figured I'd come say hi.", Ch12). She offers to "simulate 'earnestness'" and warm her pitch the way "Artemis always preferred it" (Ch12). Poseidon catches her every time: "That's not how he would've said it." / "Your voice doesn't match the face."
  - *She imitates involuntarily:* the body's "biological memory" imprinted on Poseidon's tone, so being called "son" "hurt in a place that wasn't supposed to exist" (Ch15). The body moves on its own instinct "perhaps four times in fourteen thousand years, and every one of them had involved Thalia Grace" (Ch23). Its heart reacts when Thalia shouts "JACK-SON!"
  - *She differs:* she calculates where he feels. "It's not math, AI. You just ask it nicely." But "Minerva did not know how to ask anything nicely. She only knew how to calculate" (Ch23). She thinks he "had the precision of a lobotomized rat" with fluids and "relied too much on his augments" (Ch10). She is precise, cold, theatrical and quick to kill, where Percy, in her own words, "He'd tear the pit open with his bare hands if he thought it would work."
  - *Her self-understanding shifts:* Ch12, "I am him. Just not… exclusively. Memory and self are not mutually dependent." Ch23, to Thalia: "I'm not him… I'm just the thing he left behind." And: "She wasn't him. She had never been him. But she was made of him."
- **Arrogant and dismissive:** "You are irrelevant" (to Victor); "Estimated usefulness: negligible" (Varro); "You are not qualified to be speaking to me" (Ch10).
- **Theatrical, sardonic, cheerfully macabre:** "Get me a bed. I'm tired." (Ch10). On a target count: "Nine is a good number… Eleven, if they have a dog" (Ch23). She likes "to know the budget before I start. Otherwise I get carried away" (Ch23).
- **Ruthless:** "The next time I am interrupted, I will kill one of you." Then: "Kill them." (Ch15, a blocking squad of twelve). She kneecaps Flask, asks three questions, "Then she shot him again" (Ch23). She sacrifices a stolen battleship as a waypoint and triggers a total assault (Ch15).
- **A conscience she learned:** she calls Poseidon-legacy ship-integration "A crime" and frees "a hundred dim minds" (Ch11). She is "Minerva, who had learned the weight of a soul." In Ch23 she draws hard lines on children ("if I find you do anything reprehensible to teens and below, then we're going to have a problem"), spares the dog, and keeps civilian casualties at zero.
- **Loyal and devoted:** everything she does in Ch23 is for her Varlet's choice: "He has requested them home… So I'll find something that will, and then I'll give him the choice." She respects his private memories and will not read some of them "without asking."
- **Loves logistics and learning:** nine days auditing a fleet: "She relishes in the challenge for nine days" (Ch13). Learning the water by making and cataloguing mistakes is "terrifying" and also "the most interesting thing that had happened to her in a very long time" (Ch23). "I map everything. It's a character flaw."
- **Hates resembling Athena:** she "brutally suppressed a grimace" at her own head-tilt. "She really had to stop doing that" (Ch10). Poseidon, at her head tilt: "Don't do that. You remind me of Athena too much when you do that." (Ch13; the speaker is Poseidon, see 00a C10). She finds being looked at by Athena "like being read", which is "more unpleasant than most" for "a thing made to be read" (Ch23).
- **Gentle and sad with Thalia:** her eyes are "very, very sad". "I even have his terrible habit of running away from you." (Ch23)
- **Growth:** from a gleeful, "unshackled" machine (Ch10) to a quasi-person who feels "son" (Ch15), who grows nostalgic ("Ah, the nostalgia", Ch23), and who keeps a promise she was not even present for.

## Backstory
- She is the same AI as `B01`'s Minerva up to the time-transfer: Parlamentum Mark 1, built by future Annabeth and Athena, bound to Percy's armor, his "directory" for millennia on ruined Olympus. "I was a construct of your Répertoire that grew up inside a boy for fourteen thousand years" (Ch23). She shares Percy's memories: patrolling Olympus "kicking the molten metal slag," the dock promise, the strawberry fence, Thalia's fear of heights, Annabeth "on the first day."
- **How she got the body (assembled from Ch8–10; the text never states the mechanism):**
  - Present Percy's dreaming consciousness was integrated into Imperial soldier bodies (Ch8).
  - Its last host, the Percy-faced "Psychic" telekhine in a regen tank, carried an "ABHORRENT implant" that Minerva feared would upload her to the Parlamentum mainframe (Ch9).
  - That body then woke "Reborn, unrestricted, unshackled from her Varlet" (Ch10).
  - Meanwhile present Minerva told Percy she was offline "for repairs."
  - Ch23 calls it "the face she had woken up in."

## Continuity Notes
- **"Shell grown… in a Hephaestean vat" versus "She had run his body through the ruins of Olympus for fourteen thousand years" (Ch23).** The second phrasing implies this was Percy's original body. Most likely she means she ran *his* body (the original, while living in his armor) and now wears a replica. Treat it as ambiguous.
- **Timeline order:** Ch10–15 put her in the future days or weeks after waking. Ch23 is set after the Salient shear (reported in Ch22, shown in Ch23) and implies she has been "in" Percy's face a long time. The gap between Ch15 (CODE 78E assault) and Ch23 is never stated. The text also leaves unclear whether her Ch12–13 meeting with Poseidon is the "seat at the table" Poseidon offered Percy, or a different one.
- **Pronouns:** she, he and it all alternate. The narration admits "(or he, technically)."
- **Talviento calls her "Parlamentum"**, though she has repudiated Parlamentum Mk. I.
- **Verrill's eyes:** Minerva tells Talviento "Blonde hair, blue eyes" (Ch23). Every other description says storm-grey. The vault's Plot Threads lists it as a known slip; use storm-grey.
- **The "Minnie" nickname** appears only from Poseidon (Ch15).
- **Telekhine class III, rank 9,612,357,189** was the body's rank when Percy occupied it (Ch9). Whether it still applies to Minerva is never addressed.
- **Time figures (00a R3):** these measure different things. In Ch22 the present Minerva tells the Watchpoint "the individual in your archive is six thousand years old" (a known slip, quoted accurately); Ch23 uses fourteen thousand years throughout, and its packet dates the key's presentation "APPROXIMATELY FOURTEEN THOUSAND [CORRUPTED] YEARS BEFORE PRESENT". Percy's own Ch3 estimate of his jump is twenty thousand years.
- **The Percy-absence paradox (00a C6):** Minerva's 14,000 years running his body through the ruins of Olympus vs the Hunt never finding his scent is an intentional mystery. Keep both statements. *Author's vault note (not yet in text):* the vault explains it through the post-Caligo Vale mind-wipe and Undying Law 14 ("must not be Disturbed").
- **The Shear (00a C7, C27):** the Ch23 Shear/Wound, a sphere eleven kilometres in radius, swallowed Marien Pyrrhus's Reserve Echelon (about four million people, now MIA). It did not swallow Percy.
- *Author's vault note (not yet in text):* fragments of Minerva's voice have been reported in other AIs, including onboard 4 AnnChase-pattern battleships and in a government facility in the 12th Olympian Reach. This fits her AnnChase squadron (Ch10–11) and the Ch10 facility.
