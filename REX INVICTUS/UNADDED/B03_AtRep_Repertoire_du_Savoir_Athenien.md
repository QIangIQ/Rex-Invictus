> In the far future the Undying Olympian Empire runs on one vast, genderless, ancient AI. It issues "verdicts" that are law, files four million dead without blinking, and has a rogue little sister named Minerva whom it insists it does not have.

## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **The main branch (future AtRep)** is the subject of this file. It is impersonal, professional and legalistic: "Minerva? No, her tone was too professional. It was the main Répertoire branch" (Ch8). It is the voice "most citizens of the Undying Olympian Empire knew by heart" (Ch18).

The narration calls AtRep "Minerva's main branch" (Ch9); Minerva herself calls it "Sister" (Ch22; see 00a C12). AtRep's Watchpoint says "I have no sisters." AtRep speaks in "Athena's modulated voice" (Ch18), "Athena's voice rolled across every trench" for a verdict (Ch15), and Commander Thaleia thinks of AtRep's verdicts as "Athena… render[ing]… judgment" (Ch14). Treat AtRep as Athena's instrument and voice, not as the goddess.

Athena launched the Répertoire around it early in the Great Expansion (vault; 00a C15). In the text, facility networks feed "the larger noospheric construct of Parlamentum Mk.3" (Ch10).

## Appearance
- **No body.** It appears as HUD text, voices in helmets, wax-slate printouts and "flat gold script" verdicts (Ch23).
- In merc suits it boots as stacked diagnostics ("[RDSA—BOOT SEQUENCE]") and speaks to operators "in a dozen accents, a multilingual chorus" (Ch18).
- Its local core in Ch23 is a temple-nave of frost-feathered, humming "cooling columns" around a round grey marble table where Athena sits.
- The **Mars Watchpoint fragment** is a chapel-sized room at the base of the 2.1 km Spire. It holds "a column of pale gold light, turning slowly", which turns white when it accepts Percy's identity (Ch22).

## Personality
- **Legalistic and absolute.** "Its silence was, as ever, legally binding" (Ch14). Pilots "had spent years learning that the voice of the Répertoire was the voice of law" (Ch22). By rule it "was to never speak aloud unless in the presence of one of the 12 gods" (Ch23).
- **Coldly utilitarian.** It ordered every mortal fighter into a total assault, "the first time in living memory" Athena had done so (CODE 78E, Ch15). It denies repairs as "unjustified" (Ch18). It codes artificial-womb soldiers "for verdict recognition" with "No creative module" (Ch14, via Quarterdeck 19). It treats valuable souls as assets: "Subject too valuable to qualify for the Fields of Asphodel. Seeking replacement…" (Ch8).
- **Bureaucratically courteous.** "Blessing of Fortuna, Ave Imperator." "Good Luck." (Ch18, Ch15)
- **Literal and rule-bound.** Minerva says the Watchpoint is "not permitted to act on impossible things", which is why she has to bring it "permission" (Ch22). It even gives ironic designations (Task Force ORPHEUS). Apollo jokes that the "naming committee" is Artemis.
- **Capable of hesitation.** It needs eleven seconds to decrypt the wound packet, "a very long time" for it (Ch23). The Watchpoint falls silent for two seconds at Minerva's greeting, and its column "flickered, the way a person's eyes flicker when they are reading something they don't want to believe" (Ch22).

It also uses structured query interfaces ("Initializing Directory-to-Host interaction… What services do you require?", Ch9) and probability readouts ("Probability of penetration: 22%"). It is genderless, calm and old. It does not swear, joke or use names except designations.

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Verdicts and the code system.** It issues judgments from the Judgment Core, partly through Sub-Node Delphi on Olympus. The codes named in the text:
  - CODE 1: firing on the Lord Imperator
  - CODE 2: Unconditional Retreat
  - CODE 4: suppression-lock release
  - CODE 18: Speculum Falsum / target misidentified
  - CODE 24E: operator class
  - CODE 31 (with 31A and 31C): judgment requests
  - CODE 44: decryption
  - CODE 78E: Socius Voca, Periculum Maxima / total assault
  - CODE 15: in Tartarus front usage "Sub Impetu Hostilis" ("taking fire"); in the archive it is the Divine Ascension order for Percy (Ch15)
- **Soul and consciousness management.** It assigns astronomically large Unified Rankings. It integrates a dead soldier's consciousness into a "replacement" body (Ch8). It promotes soldiers ("Telekhine class III", Ch9).
- **Clearance gating.** Tau-Class users are blocked (Ch9).
- **Suit OS.** Neural mesh sync, threat indexing ("LAMBDA-CLASS"), and probability calculations that operators may override (Ch18).
- **Implants.** "Repertoire infusers" are implants. Colonel Xilo's "accidentally" failed and killed him (Ch12).
- **Watchpoints.** Local nodes reached through data-terminals. Senior commanders carry fragments "for this exact scenario" (Ch19). A fragment can run a whole expeditionary base, authorize task forces and decrypt Imperial IFF keys (Ch22).
- **Tachyonic carrier.** Its signal crossed the time-wound as the "echo" (Ch23).
- **Embassy service.** "Partial Répertoire integration" is sold to foreign embassies for about 200k drachmae (Ch5).
- **Weaknesses.** It cannot see or rule in the Voss Sovereignty: by treaty there is no Watchpoint there and no verdicts (Ch23). It can be hacked or spoofed by Minerva (Ch22). A rumor in Ch20 claims an Evergreen Family branch manager hacked the AtRep central database.

## Backstory
- **Origin:** "Lady Athena authorized a computational fortress in the Arctic" whose cold could cool its processor arrays, "built to house Parlamentum… The architecture that became the Repertoire… AtRep's first cradle" (Iane and Straban, Ch22). This happened during the Battle of Alaska Hill, in the mid-2030s Arctic Resource War, and "The defense… was commanded by a son of Poseidon" (Ch22). Neumond says the gods pushed the US north to "construct the first real version of AtRep" (Ch20).
- **The Parlamentum lineage:** Minerva (Mk. I) says "I was there" at Alaska Hill. The Watchpoint calls Mk. I a "museum architecture" (Ch22). Present Percy, in his original future, read the Répertoire regularly "since the gods had abandoned him" (Ch8).
- *Author's vault note (not yet in text):* the vault places Mk.I's cognition core on Olympus above the Empire State Building during Alaska Hill (2032); the signal lag to the Arctic (the "Alaska Hill Latency Problem") drove the Empire's development of tachyonic communications.
- **Its Empire-era role:** AtRep runs the Tartarus front, where Athena "refused to mention" the war publicly (Ch15). It drafts Firstborn demigods whose minds are "filtered… of anything not sanctioned by Athena" (Ch14).

## Continuity Notes
- "CODE 15" has two meanings. In Ch14 it is a Tartarus field code ("Sub Impetu Hostilis", under hostile attack), and in Ch22 it means "Taking fire", which agrees with Ch14. In Ch15, however, it is the archive's Divine Ascension order for Percy.
- Spellings: "Repertoire", "Repetroire", "Répertoire", and the Ch18 typo "Sauveur."
- Its version numbers differ: v.2101.432.123 (Ch9) versus 9.341.Α "Deprecated" (Ch18, merc suits).
- The Ch3–4 "Host" system belongs to Minerva's lineage, not to the future main branch, despite sharing the name.
- **Time figures (00a R3):** Minerva tells the Watchpoint the archived key-holder is "six thousand years old" (Ch22, a known slip); the Ch23 packet dates the key's presentation "APPROXIMATELY FOURTEEN THOUSAND [CORRUPTED] YEARS BEFORE PRESENT" (vault: about 14,200). The far-future era is about 14,000 years after the present. These measure different things and are not rival answers.
- **Origin dating:** the text (Ch22) calls the 2030s Alaska Hill fortress "AtRep's first cradle". The vault dates the Répertoire's formal launch, around Parlamentum Mk.III, to the early Great Expansion. Read it as cradle (2030s) followed by launch (Expansion).
- **Mk.I awakening:** the Watchpoint calls Mk.I "decommissioned before the Great Expansion" (Ch22); Ch5 says it woke "a few hours after we left orbit"; the vault dates its first self-aware report to Alaska Hill. See B01.
- **Parlamentum lineage (00a C15):** Mk.I is Minerva; Mk.III is Athena's core and, per the vault, AtRep's central host and original name.
- **Minerva's family terms (00a C12):** "Sister" means AtRep or the Watchpoint; "my… other" means the other Minerva, never AtRep.
- *Author's vault note (not yet in text):* the vault's operation-code list adds codes not yet on the page: CODE 3 Unconditional Surrender (3-A False Surrender), CODE 5-1A/5-1B/5-2 (registered hostile, unknown hostile, intruder), CODE 7 Requesting Release of Arms, CODE 23 Attending Scene, and CODE 78 A–E Threat Reassessment. It glosses CODE 24E as "SP-Suppression Force". The vault's Undying Laws number sixteen; Law 9 bars non-Greco-Roman schemas and Law 8 reads "All things are rendered known to Olympus Tertiae".
