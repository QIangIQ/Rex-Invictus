## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- Only the far-future Tartarus Porphyrion appears.
- No present-day Porphyrion appears. In canon he rises in *The Son of Neptune* / *Blood of Olympus*, which are outside the story's chapters so far.
- Within the future, Giants and Titans reform on average "every 24 hours" somewhere along the Front (Ch22, Straban), but Porphyrion reforms only "perhaps once a decade" (Ch23).

## Appearance
- "First there was a hand as big as a house, clawing at the earth. Then an arm. Then a head, crowned with a tangle of serpents, and a pair of eyes like furnaces… a chest, broad and scaled and plated in bronze."
- He rises "three hundred meters tall", and "its roar shook the vault of Tartarus itself" (Ch23).
- After the dreadnought barrage: "His scales were glowing red-hot and half his serpent crown had burned away." Golden ichor fountains from his wounds (Ch23).

## Personality
- **Hungry and fixated:** "His furnace eyes were fixed on it, hungry, and he was walking toward it through his own army, stepping on monsters as he came." He wants whatever is calling from the other side of the Phlegethon Wound (Tullia's reading).
- **Unrelenting rage:** he keeps walking while burning: "the roar was pure rage".

## Voice & Speech Patterns
He has no words. He only roars.

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Colossal size and durability:** he survives a 40-dreadnought macro-cannon barrage that turns an 80-km ridge to glass.
- **Lethality:**
  - He crushes a Rosed Knights Titan "like a tin can", throws another 300 m, and hurls one into a laboratory.
  - In Tullia's fourth year it took "two Titan legions and a cruiser squadron" to put him down.
  - He "had killed more of the Empire's soldiers than any other single entity in Tartarus."
- **Reformation:** about once a decade.

## Backstory
Canon: King of the Giants. In the story's future he is a recurring horror of the Salient. Tullia Ardens saw him once before, from 40 km away.

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch23:**
  - At the ninth hour of the survey he rises out of the ridge mud as the "Echo Resonance" from the wound spikes.
  - The Perseus-pattern dreadnoughts fire "Designation: glass", and he walks on, burning. The AnnChase cruisers' broadsides storm the no-man's-land.
  - Forty Rosed Knights Titans drop *on* him and cut at his neck and arms.
  - The *Sovereign's Glory* lowers itself onto his head and presses him "down, down, down" until his fingers crumble to golden dust.
  - "Target neutralized," … "Estimated reformation: eleven years." (Stephanie Callidora)
