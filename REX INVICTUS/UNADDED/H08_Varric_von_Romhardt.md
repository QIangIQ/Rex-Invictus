## Appearance
He occupies the command deck's "raised dais" ("its raised dais occupied by the Lord Commander") and wears a campaign cap, which he pulls low and fastens as he leaves. He is described as "an imposing figure". No other detail is given.

## Personality
- **Formal and reverent toward divinity.** "Minerva, your arrival honors this command."
- **Dutiful but principled.** He objects to the theft of a battleship: "Such actions court disaster, Milady. You know the consequences of breaching imperial security, even for gods such as thee."
- **Stoic.** When CODE 78E orders every soldier into the line he leaves "without ceremony", does not look back at the gods, and accepts a death that "will not be recorded in the annals of history."

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Courtly and archaic toward gods ("thee", "Milady", "Milord"); formal military register otherwise.
- "Minerva, your arrival honors this command." (Ch 15)
- "Tartarus can not fall while the gods themselves stand here." (Ch 15)
- "A whole battleship? That vessel is now unaccounted for." (Ch 15)
- "Such actions court disaster, Milady." (Ch 15)
- "I shall make a great toll in your name, Milord." (Ch 15)

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
Command of a bunker-city base with trenches, Howitzers, railguns and autocannons; access to the strategium, the hololiths and the Répertoire's verdicts. As a demigod of Athena he presumably has divine abilities, but none are shown.

## Story Arc So Far
**Ch 15:**
- A captain and his "squad of twelve demigods" block Minerva and Poseidon at the entrance to the central command post (Athenian High Command requires authorisation). The captain mocks her; Minerva says "Kill them."
- Minerva summons Varric through the command speakers: "Lord Commander Varric. Attend to us." He greets her and Poseidon. Learning that Poseidon "slipped a battleship past the heavy orbital works", he protests the security breach.
- *The Eye of Light* (AC-09-1642, an AnnChase-class ship once Apollo's, captured by the Hollow Crescent) crashes into Tartarus as a waypoint. Poseidon recalls the War of Princes, where "Many of Apollo's sons fell at the Final Battle in the War of Princes millennia ago" (see Continuity Notes).
- Minerva tells Varric the sacrifice will neutralise 0.09% of active Tartarus macroforms. Athena's Répertoire then issues "Verdict. Accepted… CODE 78E", a total assault through Grid Delta, Sector Nineteen-F.
- Every officer leaves. Varric goes last. Poseidon warns him what waits. Varric pledges, "I shall make a great toll in your name, Milord," and Poseidon answers, "Die well, nephew."

## Continuity Notes
- Poseidon's line reads "Lord-Command [sic] Varric von Romhardt" (missing the "-er"), a typo. The command deck's screens had been "overwritten by the Zeus' Golden Eagle".
- **War of Princes dating:** Poseidon places the War of Princes "millennia ago" (Ch 15). Ch 23 says it ended "Quite a few years earlier", and the author's vault dates it 16212–16227, about four years before the far-future scenes (~16231). Treat Ch 15's "millennia ago" as a slip (or, at most, divine hyperbole); see 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R5.
- The CODE 78E verdict is used in the text as a total-assault order; the author's vault defines CODE 78E differently ("Major Threat"). Profiles quote codes as the text uses them.
- No vault note exists for Varric von Romhardt. He is unrelated to **Lord Varro** (Legio Administratoris) and **Archmagister Cael Varros** (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md S6), despite the similar names.
