> Annabeth's siblings: a cabin of planners, recorders and kidnappers-with-pillowcases. They decided early that Percy Jackson "shouldn't be this smart", and they set out to find out why.

## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
All are present-day campers. They are not the far-future "Owls" or the Chase Dynasty engineers (see `F03_Verrill_Chase.md`), and not Athenian archivists or Athenian demigods of the Empire (for example Varric von Romhardt, `H08_Varric_von_Romhardt.md`). The goddess they serve is present-day Athena (`D03_Athena_Present.md`), not future Athena or Minerva.

**Name warning:** "Callista" here is a camper in Ch 13. The A/N in Ch 2 lists a planned OC named "Callista Euphemia". They are not linked in the text. "Thersites" is a camper. He shares his name with the mocking soldier from the *Iliad*, but nothing else connects them.

**What they know:** Percy knows too much. He has a voice called "Minerva" in his head (Mr. D exposed it on the bus, Ch 11). He mentioned "the future you" to Annabeth (Ch 13). They know nothing concrete about the Empire, reincarnation or Aegis Vestments unless the roleplay is set after Ch 22, when the Imperials land on camp.

## Personality
- **Analytical and suspicious as a group.** "It always felt like the son of Poseidon knew too much at all times" (Ch 11). They nod along with every one of Minerva's mock-accusations on the bus (Ch 11).
- **Malcolm: gleeful, sneaky, cautious, practical.**
  - He grins while recounting how Mr. D laughed at Ares for an hour (Ch 10).
  - He secretly records Annabeth's interrogation of Percy with a microphone (Ch 11).
  - He set Chiron's laptop to wipe itself after three wrong passwords (Ch 8).
  - He states bluntly that Percy is "possessed," … "Mr. D said so" (Ch 13).
  - When Annabeth storms off, he takes over and pragmatically decides to use Percy's map to save Clarisse (Ch 13).
- **Julia:** grumbles about Tantalus's abuse of power. She is the first to suggest that "Lady Artemis might have done a number on him" (Ch 13).
- **Junior:** cynical about politics ("No one can object. Not when the Council backs him"). He is a stickler for rules: Percy is "untested… Technically" (Ch 13).
- **Callista:** observant. "I caught him finishing my sentence before I started it" (Ch 13).
- **Thersites:** blunt; he pins down Annabeth's real worry about Clarisse (Ch 13).
- **The kidnapping:** a pillowcase, duct tape and towels. They're clever ("Too clever. Not enough dumb giggling," Percy thinks) but physically harmless to him. They resort to "more unsavory methods" only as a threat (Ch 13).

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Brisk, academic, a little superior. They use military-planner phrasing ("cells", "counter-strike", "sapping"). Malcolm is calm and matter-of-fact. Annabeth's cabin calls Percy "Seaweed Brain" and "Jackson".
- "The Americans are expecting us to be ducking and weaving. So our plan is to remove most of them early." (Malcolm, Ch 9)
- "We know how to count." (Malcolm, Ch 9)
- "The Oracle didn't even speak." (Malcolm, Ch 13)
- "He's possessed," … "Mr. D said so." (Malcolm, Ch 13)
- "Cared? Is she... a god?" (Malcolm, Ch 13)
- "We'll use it. Your map. It might save Clarisse's life." (Malcolm, Ch 13)
- "Lady Artemis might have done a number on him." (Julia, Ch 13)
- "We need to make sure the daughter of Ares does not fail the quest." (unnamed sibling, Ch 13)

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- Strategy and logistics. At Fort Reagan the cabin is "the sole connection" between the three-man insurgent cells (Ch 9). The special strategy of removing troops early (the laxative plan) belongs to them and Annabeth. Seven armed demigods "stood around waiting for orders from Athena Cabin" (Ch 10).
- Tech: Malcolm's self-wiping laptop security (Ch 8). Athena Cabin's "haul of special equipment" is loaded onto Zeus's plane (Ch 8).
- Intelligence: they listen to the bug in General Mikhail's HQ (Ch 10).
- They map and study the Sea of Monsters using Percy's drawing. The map he "etched in the Athena cabin" later "came to life" near Polyphemus' island (Ch 14).

## Backstory
Not given in the text. Annabeth says the cabin and most campers are "more like normal people with useful abilities" next to Luke, Clarisse and Percy (Ch 13).
- Author's vault note (not yet in text): Malcolm's surname is **Pace**, and he is vice-leader of the Athena cabin.
- Author's vault note (old timeline, not yet in text): in Percy's original life Malcolm served on the Council of Olympic Sentinels' **Strike team Titus** and was a co-commander at the **Battle of Alaska Hill**. The present-day Malcolm knows none of this.

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch 7:** A newer Athena girl stands with Will at Percy's arrival.
- **Ch 8:** At Percy's claiming an older Athena camper explains: "It's his godly parent… Poseidon." A daughter of Athena calls Percy to the Big House ("Mr. D needs to see you… in 5!"). Malcolm's laptop booby-trap comes up. Chiron grumbles that "some twelve year-old girl" called him a security risk; she may be an Athena camper, but the text doesn't say.
- **Ch 9:** Annabeth and Malcolm lead the kids into Auxilia-One. At the strategy meeting Malcolm pitches the plan to "remove most of them early". A girl objects that there are fourteen of them against fifteen hundred troops. Malcolm warns that "The patrols are everywhere."
- **Ch 10:** Malcolm and Annabeth brief Percy. They "shared a look" when he fishes about Hermes. "The Athena cabin blinked."
- **Ch 11:** On the bus, Malcolm records the interrogation. The cabin nods along with Minerva's theatrics.
- **Ch 13:** Annabeth calls a cabin meeting about Tantalus picking Clarisse. The siblings debate Percy (untested, different since Artemis, possessed). That night they kidnap him with a pillowcase. He draws the Sea of Monsters map, mentions "the future you", and the cabin freezes. Annabeth leaves to Iris-message Athena. Malcolm takes over, keeps the map, and Percy scribbles the Princess Andromeda's location.
- **Ch 15:** Annabeth returns to the cabin after Eleni's revival.
- **Ch 20:** Annabeth sits "very still" at the Athena table, watching Percy's courtly act.
- **Ch 22:** A tiny Athena child reports that the five prisoners are locked upstairs in the Big House. During the battle, Athena cabin members are not singled out.

## Relationships
- **Annabeth Chase:** their leader. They follow her plans and back her investigation. See `F01_Annabeth_Chase_Present.md`.
- **Percy Jackson:** the subject of their curiosity and suspicion. They respect his results and are wary of his "possession". See `A02_Percy_Reincarnated_Rex.md`.
- **Minerva:** an unknown Roman-named "malignant spirit", according to Annabeth's guess. See `B01_Minerva_In_Percys_Head.md`.
- **Athena (mother):** Annabeth reports to her. She calls Percy "the vessel" and manipulates events around the cabin's map. See `D03_Athena_Present.md`.
- **Chiron:** Malcolm built (or secured) his laptop. See `F05_Chiron.md`.
- **Tantalus / Clarisse:** they resent the Council-backed choice of Clarisse but want her to succeed. See `F07_Clarisse_La_Rue.md`.
