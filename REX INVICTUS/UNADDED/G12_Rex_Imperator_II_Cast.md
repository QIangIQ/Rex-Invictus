## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- All are far-future Imperials from one prologue battle.
- **Callidora of the *Eternal Glory*** is a coalition commander. The text does not say who she is or link her to **Stephanie Callidora, Helion Bulwark of Aphrodite** in Ch23 (`H06_Bulwarks_and_Spears.md`). Author's vault note (not yet in text): the vault makes the Callidoras a family: **Elektra Callidora**, a demigod of Aphrodite and pilot of the *UOE Radiant Dawn*, is Stephanie's mother. The vault's ship list leaves the *Eternal Glory*'s pilot blank, so the Ch2 "Callidora of the Eternal Glory" is unassigned (possibly Elektra or Stephanie).
- The *UOE Sovereign's Glory* appears in Ch2 on the Augur's list of ships whose shields "will fall" (the defenders' side). In Ch23 the *Sovereign's Glory* is "Stephanie Callidora's ship, the Helion Bulwark's flagship, a Jackson-pattern dreadnought". The text does not say whether the Ch2 ship of that name is the same vessel. Author's vault note (not yet in text): the vault assigns the *Sovereign's Glory* to Stephanie Callidora in the Ch2-era ship list and again in the War of Princes (16213–14). That would make the Ch2 *Sovereign's Glory* most likely Stephanie's ship, though its place on the Zeus side of the Ch2 list is unexplained.
- **Stephanos Pyrrhus** has his own file: `G03_Stephanos_Pyrrhus.md`.
- **The Ch2 author's note** lists more planned OCs (Lysis Arenberg, Nikos Pyrrhus, Lycurgus Nikias, and others). These are not story characters unless they appear later. Lysis and the Arenbergs are in `H15_Arenbergs_and_Luna_Secundus.md`.

## Appearance
- **Pericles Sophocles:** "a seasoned veteran with a steely gaze"; grim voice.
- **Damocles Euphemios:** an "insane grin"; leans back in his throne; spreads his arms when revelling. His pauldrons carry fewer rank marks than Stephanos's.
- **Melina Callisto:** "brilliant yellow eyes" reflecting the fires; a voice "ethereally beautiful" with "the signature ring of charmspeak". She stands on a flaming bridge in tears.
- **The Battle-Augur:** "the worried man" on Eleni's bridge.
- **The *Divine Crusader* crew:** ultramarine blue and gold.

## Personality
- **Pericles:** grim realism. He states the odds bluntly and executes orders.
- **Damocles:**
  - Manic, theatrical, bloodthirsty, bored by peace ("the eternal party and joy of war"). He believes in "the law of the strong".
  - Arrogant toward "Zeus's dogs". Wine is his "favorite antidepressant", and he wonders where it went.
  - He resents being Stephanos's subordinate but defers, and goes into shocked "gibber[ing]" when his attack falters.
  - His crew says he is "a bit too theatrical sometimes".
- **Melina:**
  - Notorious for her relaxed pace and disregard for rules. "She was never meant to be a commander."
  - Facing death she turns from sorrow to righteous fury and gives a charmspeak eulogy-war-cry. She expects Thanatos's black-armored titans to carry her to the Elysian Necropolis.
- **Callidora and Niko:** named only. Niko's *Vengeful Wrath* overextends in Damocles's vanguard ("A simultaneous overextension from Vengeful Wrath's advance").
- **The sleeper cells:** traitors in Melina's volunteer crew who sabotage engines 5 through 17.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
- **Pericles:** terse reports.
- **Damocles:** shouting, laughing, profane, grandiose.
- **Melina:** charmspoken, oratorical, with archaic flourishes ("brothers and sisters of our Divine Being").

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- ***UOE Divine Crusader*** (Eleni; Pericles aboard): dreadnought, Bolt-class main batteries, Hermesian interceptors, fighter squadrons 1–1500.
- ***UOE Celestial Martyr*** (Damocles): flagship of the attack.
- ***UOE Eternal Glory*** (Callidora): ordered to flank the defenders' left.
- ***UOE Vengeful Wrath*** (Niko; the text places Niko's ship beside the *Radiant Crusade*, and Eleni sees the overextension from "Vengeful Wrath's advance" there): vanguard spearhead. Author's vault: pilot Nikos Phobos, demigod of Hades.
- ***UOE Radiant Crusade*** (Melina): engines 5–17 sabotaged; crippled by 980 Bolt-class lightning beams; uses a battle-ram and **Terra Concrematum**, the "near extinct" ritual of a fatally crippled ship ramming and self-detonating.
- **Melina's charmspeak** carries over the ship's comms to "millions".
- **The Battle-Augur** relays fleet status, opens distress channels and accepts transmissions.

## Backstory
- **The Zeus–coalition conflict.** Divine-parent factions fight within the "Undying Empire". Damocles calls the defenders "Zeus's dogs" and Melina calls them "Lightningspawn". Stephanos tells Damocles "Our fathers would be greatly displeased", implying Dionysus and Poseidon sponsor the attack.
- **Aphrodite's sector.** Her home system, the Anadyomene Belt, is "one of the only known sectors that didn't employ conscription", relying on volunteers. That made it vulnerable to sleeper cells.
- **Pericles** is listed in the A/N as an intended OC, "Tactical commander above UOE Divine Crusader".
- Author's vault note (not yet in text): the vault's dreadnought list confirms **Damocles Euphemios** as demigod of Dionysus, pilot of the *UOE Celestial Martyr*, rank 14,923 (all as in the text), and names **Nikos Phobos** (Hades) as pilot of the *Vengeful Wrath*. It also lists many unused Ch2-era pilots (for example Nikos Agathon of Ares, *Fanatical Zealot*; Kallisto Evadne of Athena, *Radiant Avenger*) and a **UOE Warbringer Fury**, later one of Marien Pyrrhus's two ships (`G02_Marien_Pyrrhus.md`). The text's Ch2 list includes a "UOE Warbringer's Fury" among Eleni's reinforcements.

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch2.**
  - Pericles reports the breach to Eleni.
  - Damocles opens the assault, sending Callidora to flank and the *Vengeful Wrath* to spearhead. Stephanos calls him, and Damocles agrees to keep hostages.
  - The Battle-Augur reports reinforcements, the 35.33% victory estimate, and the arrival of the Waves of Poseidon.
  - Eleni spots the gap between Niko's ship and the sabotaged *Radiant Crusade*. Stephanos sees it too and berates Damocles, who can only gibber.
  - The Bolt-class volley cripples the *Radiant Crusade*. Melina gives a charmspoken speech, orders "Burn!", rams the *Divine Crusader* and detonates. Narration: "An ending fitting for a daughter of Aphrodite."
  - In the vacuum, "a dying form crumbles to dust" and whispers of ending and rebirth. Its identity is unknown; it could be Melina or Eleni.
