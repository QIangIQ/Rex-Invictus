## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **This file:** the present-day girl, first a dying pine tree (cause unstated on-page; in canon, poison) (Ch7–13), then revived by the Fleece (Ch15). She knows nothing of the future except fragmentary dreams she had while she was the tree.
- **Future Thalia** (`C07_Thalia_Grace_Future_Lieutenant.md`): an immortal Lieutenant of the Hunt who has served "the Director" (future Artemis) for about 14,000 years. She remembers the Battle of Manhattan, Percy at the Lotus Casino at twelve, and so on.
- **Key divergence:** in canon, Thalia joined the Hunt after Zoë died on the Titan's Curse quest. In this timeline Zoë survives Othrys (Ch19), and present Thalia is still a camper at breakfast in Ch20. The published text never shows her joining the Hunt; the author's notes put her among the hunters in the Ch22 woods fight (C31), so treat any Hunt link as author intent. Do not give her immortality, Lieutenant rank or future memories.
- **Commander Thaleia** (Ch14, Tartarus) is a different person; see `H16_Callistus_Narrator_and_Tartarus_Front_Soldiers.md`.

## Appearance
- "Black punk-style hair, electric-blue eyes" (Ch15).
- She is "lanky", and Eleni is taller.
- She emerges from the tree with her arms drawn tight around herself, "like she'd been asleep for centuries".
- She has a storm-like aura. Percy compares Eleni's presence to "standing near Thalia when she was really angry".

## Personality
- **Irreverent and teasing.** She treats Eleni's religious hymns about Percy as entertainment: she likes the titles, wants to see the "clay chins" and calls the cult "the faithful and the completely deranged" (Ch15).
- **Bold to the point of reckless.** After three days she "finally got bored" and goads Eleni into a power demonstration, over Annabeth's "horrible, terrible idea" warning. The shockwave flattens her and singes her hair (Ch15).
- **Blunt and profane.** "Holy shit." "What the fuck?" She calls Artemis "a proper bitch" (Ch15). That hostility fits a girl who has not yet joined the Hunt.
- **Shaken underneath.** She freezes on seeing Eleni and admits that she dreamed of her while she was the tree (Ch15).
- **Social.** After Eleni's council she "spent the majority of her time connecting with the demigods around camp" (Ch15).
- **A critic of Percy's antics.** At breakfast she leans on him and mocks his new courtly voice (Ch20).
- **Physical.** Percy notes that Eleni's sparks "hurt more than Thalia's slap" (Ch15), so Thalia has slapped him at least once off-page.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Modern, casual and sarcastic, with profanity used freely. She nicknames people (Percy is "Seaweed Brain") and is direct and impatient. She calls Eleni nothing in particular and treats Percy as a peer.
- "Dang," / "She... she was there." (Ch15)
- "I had dreams… While I was the tree. I saw things—flashes of cities in the sky, armies wearing armor like hers" (Ch15)
- "She thinks she's dead." (Ch15)
- "Okay, I kind of like that one. She's gotta pretty good naming sense." (Ch15)
- "Yeah, the faithful and the completely deranged." (Ch15)
- "Could be worse. You could've married her. Gods knows she's a proper bitch" (Ch15)
- "Bet you've still got a spark left. Show me something." (Ch15)
- "Did you two rehearse this?… or did Annabeth's grammar rub off onto poor old Seaweed Brain?" (Ch20)

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- Daughter of Zeus with lightning powers. During the demo, "lightning arcing uselessly from her fingers" is grounded into the dirt (Ch15).
- Annabeth recognises Eleni by the "same aura as Thalia—same storm beneath the skin".
- No equipment is shown on-page. Her canon spear and Aegis are not mentioned for the present version.
- **Ch22 woods hunter (author's notes):** in the Ch22 woods fight, "a girl with 2 wicked hunting knives" throws a crackling spear and takes a QVARTUS round on her shield; it goes "through the silver pauldron" and catches her in the arm. The published Ch22 never names her. The author's Plot Threads identifies this hunter as Thalia ("arm was pierced through Aegis") and places her on the Alaska team (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C31). Treat it as author intent, not on-page fact.

## Backstory
- In canon she died defending Annabeth and Luke and became the pine on Half-Blood Hill. The story uses this unchanged.
- **Author's vault note (not yet in text):** the vault (canon-baseline) makes her the daughter of Zeus and Beryl Grace and the older sister of Jason, revived by the Fleece through Kronos's manipulation. In this story the Ch15 revival brings Eleni out of the tree with her, which is the author's own version. The vault lists her on the Hunt's active roster, which matches her future self (C07). Its Plot Threads also says she "leaves with the Alaska team" in a later chapter (planned, not yet on the page).
- **Ch15 tree dreams:** she saw "flashes of cities in the sky, armies wearing armor like hers, brothers and sisters of mine, all demigods of Zeus" and Eleni commanding a battle. She believes Eleni "saw me, and clung to me. In whatever place she came from."

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch7:** Her tree marks the border where Percy killed the Minotaur.
- **Ch12:** The pine is sickly, its needles "curling at the edges like burnt paper". Grover whispers to it in Ancient Greek and the branches twitch "Like they were listening".
- **Ch13:** The camp debates the dying tree, and the Fleece quest is launched to save it.
- **Ch15:**
  - The Fleece goes on the tree. In a storm, two forms emerge: Thalia, and the gravely wounded Eleni Theodosia.
  - Thalia sits up, stares at Eleni ("She... she was there") and describes her dreams.
  - In the infirmary tent she waves a hand in front of the delirious Eleni ("She thinks she's dead") and enjoys Eleni's hymns to Percy.
  - Three days later she goads Eleni into a 2%-capacity "localized invocation" and is knocked flat ("Holy shit"). "And she worships you?"
  - Afterwards she spends her time getting to know the camp.
- **Ch19 (ambiguous):** At Othrys, Percy "locking onto familiar signatures… Annabeth. Grover. Thalia" while teleporting the questers. The text never explains why Thalia or Annabeth would be at Othrys.
- **Ch20:** At breakfast, while the Hunt visits, she leans on Percy and mocks his courtly act. He answers, "Lady Thalia. What joy…", and she replies "What the fuck? See, this is exactly what I mean!"
- **Ch22:** Not on-page. Her pine still stands with the Fleece on its lowest branch as the camp border fails.

## Relationships
- **Eleni Theodosia** (`G01_Eleni_Theodosia.md`): half-sister from the future. They have a dream-bond, since Eleni "clung" to her inside the tree. Eleni calls her "young Thunder-Sister", and Thalia teases her.
- **Percy** (`A02_Percy_Reincarnated_Rex.md`): friendly ribbing and casual physical familiarity. Percy has future memories of her. (In Ch23, Minerva, wearing Percy's body, calls the future Thalia "Pinecone Face".)
- **Annabeth** (`F01_Annabeth_Chase_Present.md`): old friend ("Thals"). Annabeth is the voice of caution against Thalia's dares.
- **Artemis** (`C01_Artemis_Present.md`): contempt for now ("a proper bitch"). Ironic, given her future.
- **Grover** (`F04_Grover_Underwood.md`): her old protector, who spoke to her tree.
- **Zeus** (`D01_Zeus_Present.md`): father. Not seen interacting with her.
- **Jason** (`F16_Jason_Grace.md`): her brother. They have not met in the story so far.

## Continuity Notes
- Ch15 says Thalia was "still pale from the puking". It is unclear whether she vomited too, or whether the line means Percy, who was the one wiping his lips.
- Eleni's eyes are "silver" in this scene. Per 00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C2 they are silver at rest and blaze white/white-gold when her power flares; Ch21's "dark eyes" is an outlier. (Flamel's "the blue-eyed girl in futuristic armor", Ch17, is a second outlier, although the ruling says there is no "blue" in the text; do not use blue.)
- **"The Grace twins"** (Ch4): Zoë's thought places Percy's birth "right after the Grace twins". Canon makes Thalia about seven years older than Jason. Per C22, quote "twins" only as the label Zoë uses; in prose they are siblings. Unresolved.
- **Present-day year** (R2): c. 2021 per the author's timeline; nominally the canon PJO era.
- Her presence at Othrys (Ch19) is unexplained, since she was not one of the five questers.
- Her future self was "with the Hunt that night, holding the bridges" at the Battle of Manhattan (Ch23). That is the original timeline, not this one.
