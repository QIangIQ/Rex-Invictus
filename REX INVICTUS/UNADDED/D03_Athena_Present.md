## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **This file:** the 21st-century Athena. She has never built the Aegis Vestments or Parlamentum, and she encounters Minerva as an unknown, impossibly familiar intelligence.
- **Not** the far-future Athena Atrytone / Athena Parthenos of the Undying Olympian Empire. That Athena designed Percy's rangefinder "as a favor" (Ch3), wrote Minerva's final code for Parlamentum Mk.I (future Annabeth designed its hardware), signed off on the Aegis Vestments design (Ch19), runs Parlamentum Mk.III, demands Shinto souls as sacrifices (Ch5), controls the Répertoire (Ch23), and "forged" Percy's Imperial key (Ch22). See `E03_Athena_Future_Parthenos.md`.
- **Not** Minerva, the AI that believes itself to be Athena and calls this goddess "the false Athena" and "Creator" (`B01_Minerva_In_Percys_Head.md`, `B02_Minerva_Percy_Clone_Interlude.md`). Athena herself says Minerva is "a remnant. A divergence."
- **Not** the Répertoire du Savoir Athénien / AtRep (`B03_AtRep_Repertoire_du_Savoir_Athenien.md`).
- **Note on "Minerva" as Roman aspect:** the AI took the name of Athena's Roman aspect on purpose ("my poor roman aspect is sufficiently degrading", Ch6). No separate Roman-aspect Minerva goddess appears in the text.
- **Knowledge boundary:** She knows the AI exists, is "Parlamentum Mk.I tactical cognition derivative", lives in Percy's soul, and knows of tachyonic carriers. She knows the Mars force and CODE 18. She hypothesises that Minerva was "me, once, in a world that no longer exists". She does not know Chaos or the old timeline in full.

## Appearance
- Ch6: "bespectacled", working on a laptop over tea with Artemis in her palace.
- Ch13: seen by Iris message "reading a book on her bed". She keeps eight stacks of A4 plans, "each exactly one-and-a-half meters tall", and a floating icosahedron projection of Percy's "psychological resilience".
- Ch19: grey eyes. Her palace is "cold silver lines and polished geometry… too Athena", with "threatening busts of philosophers". She leaves in "a flash of grey" (Ch22).
- Ch8 disguise: wears Annabeth's face through the Mist.

## Personality
- **Cold, controlled strategist.** "Panic was a mortal impulse, one she had long since mastered. What mattered now was containment" (Ch6).
- **Paranoid about rival intelligences.** After meeting the AI she concludes, "Olympus was on borrowed time" (Ch6).
- **Manipulative and long-sighted.** In Ch13 she secretly engineers the Sea of Monsters quest. She sends Grover's dream-plea to Percy, "accidentally" breaks the camp barrier, and arranges a "coincidental" sea-shrine meeting. Her reasoning: "Let them believe it is fate. It is neither. It is design."
- **Treats people as instruments.** She calls Percy "the vessel", "the host" and "the gateway". She tells Annabeth, "Your personal attachments are your weakness."
- **Yet protective of the status quo.** She will not report Percy to Olympus, because "The Council would overreact. They would destroy the host… That would be... inefficient" (Ch13).
- **Haughty with her siblings.** She dismisses Artemis ("Shoo Shoo dear Sister"). She thinks Dionysus "the dunderhead" and serves him his eighteenth alcohol punishment coldly (Ch8).
- **Legalistic and dignified.** When kidnapped: "this is a violation of no fewer than twelve—" (Ch19). "Storming was for lesser people."
- **Intellectually honest, grudgingly.** She admits, "Minerva has played the long game better than I possibly ever did" (Ch13). She calls Minerva "as arrogant as I" (Ch19).
- **Curious beneath the ice.** She "allowed herself the faintest smile" at the AI's challenge (Ch6), and she is fascinated by future tachyon communication (Ch19).

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Precise and formal, with no contractions in serious speech. She talks in strategic and chess metaphors and in clinical labels ("Identify.", "Origin.", "Explain."). She calls her siblings "Brother" and "Sister" and Annabeth "daughter". She calls Percy by his full name, "Perseus", and refers to him as "host" or "vessel". She does not swear.

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- **Mind and analysis:** threads "a filament of divine cognition" through Percy's pulse to speak with Minerva. She analyses "relative terabytes" of data during the conversation (Ch19).
- **Teleportation:** folds space, "geometry folding inward" (Ch19).
- **Mist disguise:** impersonates Annabeth convincingly enough that only Minerva's biometrics catch her (89.7% probability of impersonation, Ch8).
- **Noosphere:** her divine consciousness is the "realm of ideas" through which the AI slipped (Ch6). She uses a laptop and reads divine signatures in code.
- **Dream-sending and ward manipulation:** sends Grover's dream-voice and "accidentally" breaks the camp barrier (Ch13).
- **Infrastructure:** hooked the Hunters' Hecate-built teleport array battery to the Moon Chariot (Ch4).
- **Lore:** tried to claim the Golden Fleece "for her own temple", so the gods "wove a rejection", and the Fleece burns her children (Ch14).

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch4:** Referenced as the engineer of the Hunt's tent-portal power.
- **Ch6:** Bores Artemis with idea-bouncing. The AI appears on her laptop ("The false Athena acknowledges me") and debates divinity with her. She ends it with "Then you must try", then turns cold and resolves to track and contain it.
- **Ch8:** Sent by Zeus to punish Dionysus (eighteenth offence, another decade added). Dionysus tells her about "something else" in Percy's head, and she suspects "the AI".
- **Ch8 — the disguised-as-Annabeth episode:** She visits Poseidon's cabin wearing Annabeth's face. Minerva flags "Strong mist detected" and a "Facial micro-tension at 0.43% deviation", then "Probability of impersonation: 89.7%". She asks Percy to "Refresh my memory" about camp rules. He recites them and pauses at "Rule four" to see if she can finish; she cannot ("Discrepancy: Athena"). She declares, "You shall be on my cabin's team. Consider yourself lucky," looks "beyond" the roof, and leaves a chill. Minerva notes she has at most 10 minutes before Poseidon notices a hostile presence. Minerva requests a CODE 78E threat reassessment.
- **Ch13:** Annabeth Iris-messages her. She denies that Minerva is her ("Not a singular chance"), calls Percy "the gateway" and an opportunity for "supremacy within months", redrafts all her plans, refuses to tell the Council, and then engineers the quest.
- **Ch14:** The Fleece rejects Annabeth because of Athena's old claim.
- **Ch15:** At Eleni's judgment she studies her "as if she were a puzzle". She notes that Eleni's liturgical dialect resembles something "I have only recently experienced" (Percy or Minerva) and presses, "Trained where?"
- **Ch19:** Watches the council "like she was already drafting revisions". Is kidnapped by Poseidon and Artemis, senses Minerva's architecture ("…Impossible"), and wrestles with Artemis over Percy. She tells Artemis, "I am trying to understand what is inside him before it affects us." In BIOMECHANICS 110 she interrogates Minerva and learns of ECM layers, "all one hundred and one core languages of code" and tachyonic carriers. They agree on Percy: impulse control poor, durable, useful.
- **Ch22:** At council she cuts Ares off: "The principle can wait. What matters is capability." She asks Percy what a thousand Imperials would do. She goes to camp "to 'observe'", which means taking notes on Imperial equipment. During Minerva's handshake through Neumond she "placed her hand on his shoulder". She reads the Accord aloud, objects, "They are soldiers of a foreign power," and tells Ares, "You'll answer for them." Artemis rates her the most dangerous god present because she alone is thinking ("Athena. Which is worse").

## Continuity Notes
- Ch6 calls her "bespectacled", a detail not used again.
- Ch8 italic/system lines were flattened in the plain text, so Minerva's readouts and Athena's lines are interleaved. "She smirked" after "Refresh my memory" refers to Percy answering, not to Athena.
- Ch13 says she has "eight neatly organized stacks", all redrafted after Annabeth's notes.
- Ch14 uses "Athena's spear" as a simile for Annabeth's gaze. It is not a literal appearance.
- Who designed what (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C14): the old-timeline suit is "of his own design" (Percy's, Ch1). The future Athena signed off on the Vestments design and wrote Minerva's final code; future Annabeth designed Parlamentum Mk.I's hardware; Hephaestus forged the Vestments into armour. None of this has happened yet for the present Athena.
- Parlamentum lineage (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C15): Mk.I is Minerva; Mk.III is the future Athena's core.
- Siblings (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md C28): Artemis and Athena are sisters in the text. The vault's Artemis note calls Athena a "cousin"; that is not used here.
- Present-day year: c. 2021 per the author's timeline, nominally the canon PJO era (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R2).
