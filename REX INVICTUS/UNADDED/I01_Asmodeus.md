## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
There is only one Asmodeus in the story: the present-day demon lord. Two points to keep in mind:
- **Future data.** The Répertoire (see `B03_AtRep_Repertoire_du_Savoir_Athenien.md`) lists him with a "UOE unified" ranking (2563). Since it ranks him in the Undying Olympian Empire's system, he presumably exists, or is at least recorded, in that future era. The text never shows a future Asmodeus.
- **Old-timeline knowledge.** Percy knows from his old timeline that Evergreen spies "would collapse the structure from the inside a few millennia later" (Ch4). By giving Asmodeus the Eye, Percy may have changed that future. Evergreen still exists in the far-future era (Ch18 "Evergreen pilots"; Ch20, where Asimov's friend is "One of the Evergreen Family branch managers").
- **Not to be confused with** his servant Condottiero (`I02_Condottiero.md`), who did the ambush and the killing.

## Appearance
- **Ch4:** He looks like an ordinary rich man: "Shoulder length blonde hair, sharp nose, and high cheekbones." What gives him away are his eyes, "glowing eyes burning with balefire" (the race's signature), and "a mouthful of spiked teeth." When angered, his face "briefly twisted into an unrecognizable form." His arm makes "strange cracking noises" as it reaches out, and he turns his head "a full 180 degrees" with crunches. His voice has a "grinding timbre."
- **Ch8 and Ch17:** His eyes are "crimson" and "glowing with intensity," with sharp fangs. He has shrunk the Eye of the Cyclops and set it in his **left eye socket**, where it sits "pulsing faintly." Smoke curls from him, and in Ch17 he has a "toothy grin."

## Personality
- **Greedy and openly treacherous, at least in what he says.** Offered a bargain, he first threatens to take the Eye "from your cold, dead hands" and sneers that demons "backstab, kill, and plunder" (Ch4).
- **Ruled by fear of his superiors.** One mention of Paimon makes him freeze and ask how Percy knows her (Ch4).
- **Jealous of rivals.** The name Dantalion makes him slam his fist on the armrest. He closes the deal at once to get ahead of that rival (Ch4).
- **Territorial.** Werewolves in his domain outrage him: "You should have started with that first, demigod. I would have wiped them out without the offering" (Ch4).
- **Paranoid.** His first question about Percy is "A spy? Or an assassin?" (Ch4).
- **Cold, calculating revenge.** Through the Eye he finds the Evergreen spies. Instead of flying into a fury he feels "a cold, calculating satisfaction," burns the spies to ash in front of terrified staff, and swears the Evergreen Family "would be left with nothing beneath the soil they stood on" (Ch8).
- **Honors contracts in practice.** For all his talk, he keeps the deal: he sends Condottiero and orders "Spare no expense." Later he rewards Percy with intelligence and safe passage. Percy trusts that his servant "will not break his master's word" (Ch17).
- **Capable of gratitude.** In Ch17 his satisfaction is "a rare warmth in a being of shadow." He even says it "would be horrible if you perished midway in your quest."
- **Arrogant toward heaven.** He mocks Sariel ("Balance? Child...") and calls the angel "but a flare" (Ch17).

## Voice & Speech Patterns
He is aristocratic and theatrical, slipping from silky invitation into snarling contempt. He calls Percy "small child", "child", "runt", "slug", "boy", "demigod" and "young man", and the werewolves "mutts" and "mangy dogs." When he gives orders his phrasing turns formal ("with extreme prejudice"). He doesn't swear in the modern sense, though he does say "bastards" about Evergreen.
- "Now, small child," … "speak your desires, and let us see what bargain we can strike." (Ch4)
- "And what is preventing me from taking it from your cold, dead hands?" (Ch4)
- "Haven't you heard? Demons don't have honor, runt." (Ch4)
- "How do you know her, boy?" (Ch4)
- "A den of mutts? In my domain!?" (Ch4)
- "You have flushed out every traitor hiding in my camp. Truly, you have earned my favor." (Ch17)
- "Balance? Child, I merely gained a reward from a finished contract." (Ch17)
- "I have endured eons. I have seen pantheons rise and crumble. You… are but a flare." (Ch17)

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
- Commands Disciples of the Lesser Keys. Drinking a drop of a Disciple's Daemonblood teleports the drinker to him (Ch4).
- Catches objects "with a swirl of red energy"; his body can stretch and twist (Ch4).
- **Eye of the Cyclops.** Worn in his left socket, it shows hidden truths. Through it he sees spies as "faceless shadows" along with their "Encrypted messages, dead drops" (Ch8). He can hide it magically ("magicked away") in the middle of a fight (Ch17).
- Turns traitors to ash from a distance (Ch8).
- In combat against an angel: shadows soak up holy light, he reforms his shape in mid-air, strikes with "blackened tendrils," and moves "faster than any mortal eye could track" (Ch17).
- Controls the **Hellway**, a demonic shortcut road that brought the questers from his fortress to the outskirts of San Francisco (Ch17).
- Has a personal castle 0.8 km northwest of the Yosemite church (Ch3), and a fortress "in Hell" with an obsidian throne (Ch8 and Ch17).
- Gathers intelligence across pantheons (Ch17; see Story Arc).

## Backstory
Very little is given. He is the 32nd demon lord, serves the female demon Paimon, and competes with Dantalion. His faction is at war with the Evergreen Family, "a power that sought to control both Heaven and Hell" (Ch8), whose spies had worked their way into the demon lords' ranks. Percy's old-timeline knowledge says that infiltration would eventually bring the demon hierarchy down. *Canon/myth baseline (not story):* in the Ars Goetia, Asmodeus (Asmoday) is the 32nd spirit, a king. Paimon is traditionally male, while this story makes her female. In the Book of Tobit he is the demon of lust.
- **Author's vault note (not yet in text):** the vault's *Demon Lords of Hell* note lists eight Demon Lords (kings) of Hell under **Lucifer**: Baal, Paimon, Beleth, Purson, **Asmodeus (5th)**, Vine, Balam and Zagan. Ranking him just below Paimon (2nd) fits his service to Paimon; "32nd" is his Goetic spirit number. The vault's own ranking for him matches the text (#2563). The vault's `Asmodeus.md` and `Paimon.md` are otherwise empty.

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch3:** Percy reaches Yosemite through the Labyrinth and is ambushed by a fast red demon (Condottiero) whose strike fills him with demonic energy. The Répertoire wakes, absorbs that energy, and identifies the regional lord: "32nd Demon Lord Asmodeus, Rank 2563... last known location in Personal Castle located 0.8km NW."
- **Ch4:** Percy bows to the unseen Disciple and offers a "genuine Eye of a Cyclops." He swallows Daemonblood and wakes in Asmodeus's bare hall (a crimson carpet, a simple wooden chair, no exits, bone-dry). Asmodeus threatens him. Percy appeals to his "honor," then plays his trump: "Paimon would not approve." He follows up with Evergreen and Dantalion as bait, though his claim that the Evergreens would want the Eye is a lie. Asmodeus agrees, outraged that there are werewolves on his land, and orders Condottiero to escort Percy and "Wipe out those mangy dogs."
- **Ch6–7:** Minerva detects "Asmodean interference" near Yosemite, "no more than two weeks old." Percy assumes Asmodeus is "keeping a curious watch" over him. Percy deliberately keeps Asmodeus's name from Chiron and Dionysus (Ch7).
- **Ch8:** Asmodeus POV. He sets the Eye in his socket, sees the Evergreen spies, burns them to ash and vows to ruin Evergreen.
- **Ch16:** Eleni senses "Demons. Kin of the 32nd" between the quest and the Smithsonian, and judges them "not strong enough to matter."
- **Ch17:** "The 32nd's guards are watching us." Condottiero brings the questers (Percy, Zoë, Bianca, Grover and Eleni) to the fortress. Asmodeus thanks Percy and gives intelligence: Evergreen "still crawl in shadows"; "Thor's absence leaves Norse lines weak in Canada"; the Shinto watch is faltering; "the leeches" are fighting the Shinto. He also warns that "The Roman Triumvirate Holdings" has put a bounty of "ten-tens of gold Talents" (about $430 million) on each of them (see `I05_Robespierre_and_the_Triumvir.md`). He sends them along the Hellway. Afterwards the angel **Sariel**, whom Percy had invited earlier that day to "trail us," appears and accuses him: "You dared manipulate the Way of Fate." They fight among cracking obsidian pillars. The scene ends without a result.

## Continuity Notes
- **Where his seat is.** It is a castle 0.8 km northwest of the Yosemite church (Ch3), a "fortress in Hell" with crumbling marble walls (Ch8), and a black-walled fortress in a misty forest clearing off a highway on the road west (Ch17).
- **Eyes and seat.** Balefire eyes and a wooden chair (Ch4) versus crimson eyes and an obsidian throne (Ch8 and Ch17).
- **Spelling.** "Asmodai" is Eleni's form of the name.
- **Who gave the Eye.** Some notes assume Lycaon gave it. The text says it was "gifted to Asmodeus in exchange for a service" (Ch8), and the giver was Percy. Where Percy got it is never stated; most likely it came from the cyclops he killed in the Labyrinth (Ch3).
- **Fate.** The Sariel fight has no shown outcome; profiles write *fate unstated* (`00a_CANON_RULINGS.md` C20), not *alive* or *dead*.
- **Era.** *Present day* means c. 2021 per the author's timeline (nominally the canon PJO era); see `00a_CANON_RULINGS.md` R2. Percy's apparent age: a young teen; the text calls him sixteen (Ch7, Ch13, Ch22), though the premise blurb says he returned as a twelve-year-old (R1).
