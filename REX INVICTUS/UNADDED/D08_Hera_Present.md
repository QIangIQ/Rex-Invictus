## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
- **This file:** the present-day Queen of Olympus, a cold observer at council.
- **Not** the far-future Hera who taps Zeus's shoulder at the divine conference ("Mind your manners Dear, our reputation is on the line", Ch5) and whose choice of Caligo Vale ("chosen by Hera on a whim", Ch13) set the site of the future Percy–Artemis political oathbinding, an Empire-era rite early in the Great Expansion, never before the Abandonment (00a_CANON_RULINGS.md R8). Nor the old-timeline Hera whose palace on Olympus Primaris is "a crater still smoking millenia [sic] later" with its peacocks gone (Ch1). See `E08_Hera_Ares_Hestia_Demeter_Future.md`.
- **Knowledge boundary:** She knows what was said in the Ch15 and Ch22 councils (Eleni, the Mars force, CODE 18). She does **not** know she will one day choose the venue of Percy and Artemis's oathbinding.

## Appearance
No physical description is given. In Ch15 her "expression was a perfect mask". In Ch22 her "lip curled" and she speaks "coldly". She stands beside Zeus at the throne-room window during the battle.

## Personality
- **Inscrutable.** Watches Eleni's judgment without reaction (Ch15).
- **Contemptuous of outsiders.** Mocks the captured Imperial for speaking "of his own army arriving as if it were a delivery" (Ch22).
- **Containment-minded and conservative.** Wants "the Mist reinforced over all of Long Island and the prisoners handed back at the border like parcels" (Ch22).
- **Pointed and needling with Zeus.** She uses his pride against him (or for her own ends). After CODE 18: "The verdict called your nephew their Emperor… It called him the son of Poseidon. It said nothing about you" (Ch22). Zeus's knuckles go white.
- **Challenging.** When Artemis and Marien defy Zeus, she demands, "Then what do you propose?" (Ch22).

## Voice & Speech Patterns
Cold, clipped and aristocratic, with barbed observations rather than open argument. No profanity.
- "The prisoner speaks of his own army arriving as if it were a delivery." (Ch22)
- "The verdict called your nephew their Emperor." (Ch22)
- "It said nothing about you." (Ch22)
- "Then what do you propose?" (Ch22)

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
No powers are shown on-page. Canonically she has queenly authority over marriage and family and the power of a senior Olympian.

## Backstory
Canon backstory applies: Zeus's wife and sister, mother of Ares (and Hephaestus). No AU backstory is given for the present version.

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch15:** Present at Eleni Theodosia's judgment, masked and silent.
- **Ch22 (Mars council):** Scorns Merek's "delivery" phrasing. Eleni answers her directly and "did not lower her eyes", saying "Forgive me, Lady Hera. A retrieval element is not an invasion force." Hera argues for Mist reinforcement and handing the prisoners back.
- **Ch22 (during the battle):** Stands with Zeus at the window and needles him about the CODE 18 verdict.
- **Ch22 (sunset council):** Coldly asks, "Then what do you propose?", which hands the floor to Percy and leads to the Anabasis Accord.

- Foreshadowing: her future self chooses Caligo Vale "on a whim" (Ch13). What is her present attitude toward Percy and Artemis?
