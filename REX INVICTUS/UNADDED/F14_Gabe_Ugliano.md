## Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
There is only one Gabriel, in the present-day timeline. He never learns anything about Percy's transformation: he is unconscious throughout the revelation. Percy's barrier pulse and the rancid-seaweed smell in Percy's room do not wake him.

**Canon baseline (labelled canon, not story):** In *The Lightning Thief*, Gabe is a lazy, abusive, poker-playing slob whose stench masks Percy's demigod scent from monsters. He mistreats Sally and gambles away money. Sally later turns him to stone with Medusa's head. None of this is shown in *Rex Invictus*, though the apartment details match.

## Appearance
"Out cold, reeking of alcohol from last night", on the living-room floor (Ch 3).

## Personality
Not shown directly. His presence is felt through the apartment: "moldy walls, veritable mountains of empty beer cans, and a disgusting smell of cheap tobacco coming from down the hallway" (Ch 3). Sally walks past him "pointedly ignoring" him, which suggests a cold, resentful marriage.

## Voice & Speech Patterns
He has no lines. For roleplay, canon Gabe is crude, demanding and self-pitying, and the story's details (beer, cheap tobacco, passing out) fit that. Label any dialogue as extrapolation. Example in canon spirit, invented for roleplay: "Hey, kid, where's the bean dip?"

## Abilities, Powers & Equipment
None. In canon his stench helped hide Percy, but the reincarnated Percy says the Mist and his scent are no longer protecting him once he is aware of his origins (Ch 3). The story does not credit Gabe's smell with anything.

## Backstory
Married to Sally. Lives with her and Percy in the apartment Percy falls toward from the sky in Ch 3, which Percy finds "strangely familiar".

## Story Arc So Far
- **Ch 3:** Gabriel lies drunk on the floor while Sally makes lunch, and stays unconscious while Percy reveals everything to Sally in the next room. Percy smells the tobacco on waking.
- **After Ch 3:** No further mention. The notes on Ch 1–4 flag "Gabe" as an unresolved thread.
