The **[[Hephaestus Forge Cohorts]]** are the far-future engineering corps of [[Hephaestus]], the [[Divine Being 'Forgemaester']]. They share control authority over [[Hyperium]] with the [[Athena]] Strategic Resource Command and co-designed the [[Aquila-Pattern Assault Lander]] with the [[Athena Strategic Design Bureau]]. They work out of the [[Forge of Hephaestus]] and forge-worlds such as [[Vulcanis-Prime]]. [[Neumond]] of [[Ekdromoi Cell Kappa-Seven]] is a Hephaestean systems operator.
