The ground forces of the [[Undying Olympian Empire]] are built around the Legions, but the Legions do not fight alone. Each one is supported by specialist formations named after the troop types of ancient Greece. Every formation has its own role, recruitment pool, divine lineage profile and equipment suite, and each is supplied by a different mix of the Empire's [[CORPORATION LIST|corporations]].

### Formations in a Planetary Assault

A standard Imperial invasion uses every formation in sequence.

1. **Sciritae** lances land weeks early, map the defenses and choose the landing zones.
2. **Ekdromoi** cells remove high-value targets such as enemy commanders, gods' champions and key installations.
3. **Psiloi** volleys and orbital fires, guided by the Sciritae, strike air defense in the final minutes before the landing.
4. **Neodamodes** assault squads land in the first wave and seize the beachhead.
5. **Peltast** wings pour through the breach and hunt enemy armor and Titans.
6. **Perioeci** townships land behind them and turn the beachhead into a fortified base.
7. **Trophimoi** circles are committed wherever the defenders call on a foreign pantheon.

Formation ratings follow the [[OPERATION CODES]] system. A unit rated under a given [[CODE 24 VARIANTS|CODE 24]] variant is cleared by AtRep to engage the matching [[CODE 78 VARIANTS|CODE 78]] threat levels.

| [[CODE 78C]] Concerning | Perioeci, Sciritae, Psiloi, Peltasts, Neodamodes, Trophimoi |
| [[CODE 78D]] Significant | Sciritae, Psiloi, Peltasts, Neodamodes, Trophimoi, Ekdromoi |
| [[CODE 78E]] Major | Neodamodes (in numbers), Trophimoi, Ekdromoi, [[Titan Pilots]] |
