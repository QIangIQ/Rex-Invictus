**Neodamodes** (singular *Neodamode*) are the heavy assault infantry of the [[Undying Olympian Empire]]. They are recruited from the Empire's unenfranchised populations, including block-born laborers, colonial subjects and convicts serving under [[Attended Edicts]], and are offered full Imperial citizenship in exchange for a fixed term of frontline service. In return the Empire encases them in some of the heaviest powered armor ever issued to infantry and sends them first into places where nothing else survives.

Neodamodes form the breaching wave of Imperial assaults. They are the troops who go down the ramp of the [[Aquila-Pattern Assault Lander]], walk into prepared fire, and hold ground under bombardment until the rest of the Legion arrives.

> *"You came in as block-fodder. You will leave as a citizen, or you will leave as a name on the Wall. Either way, you leave better than you came."*
> Enrollment address, [[Legio XX Valeria Vitrix]] Tithe Office

### Name

In Sparta the *neodamodeis* were helots freed in return for military service. They fought as hoplites and received a share of civic status, though never the full rights of a Spartiate. The Imperial Neodamodes follow the same bargain. Their citizenship is real and hereditary, which makes it one of the few routes out of the undercities that the Empire honors in full.

---

### Role and Responsibilities

- **Breaching** fortified positions, orbital artillery belts, bunker complexes and city walls
- **First-wave landing** during planetary assaults, immediately behind the [[Sciritae]]
- **Holding ground** under sustained bombardment, including anti-divine fire
- **Shield-wall operations**, forming mobile barriers behind which lighter troops advance
- **Close assault** through tunnels, hive-city blocks and ship interiors
- **Sustained attrition** in the [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] theater, where the Empire loses tens of millions of soldiers a year

### Divine Lineage

Neodamodes are overwhelmingly mortal. Divine blood is welcome but never required, and the Empire compensates for its absence with machinery. Where lineage exists, it is usually thin and skews toward endurance.

| Lineage | Share | Contribution |
|---|---|---|
| Augmented mortals | 58% | Unlimited supply, total dependence on equipment |
| [[Telekhines]] (minor talent) | 14% | Powering shieldstaffs and squad barriers |
| [[Ares]] (trace legacy) | 9% | Pain tolerance, battle frenzy, refusal to break |
| [[Hephaestus]] (trace legacy) | 7% | Armor field repair, heat resistance, affinity with machines |
| [[Nike]] (trace legacy) | 4% | Morale, momentum in the assault |
| [[Poseidon]] and [[Zeus]] (trace legacy) | 4% | Hydrokinetic and electrokinetic shield boosting |
| Other | 4% | Varied |

Anyone who displays power above the trace level during service is pulled for assessment and usually reassigned to the [[Peltast]] corps or the officer track.

### Organization

The basic unit is the **assault squad** of thirteen, sized to fill one lander bay. Three squads make a **breach platoon**, and a breach platoon is led by a centurion who is almost always a legacy.

A squad typically includes:

- **Squad Leader**, a veteran with at least one prior tour
- **Shieldbearer**, a Telekhine carrying the squad's shieldstaff
- **Two Heavy Gunners** with support weapons
- **Breacher** with demolition charges and a HESH launcher
- **Field Smith**, usually a Hephaestus legacy, who repairs armor in combat
- **Seven Assaulters**

### Abilities

Neodamodes have few abilities of their own. Their strength comes from what is built into them and bolted onto them.

- **Graft Frame.** Every Neodamode receives an [[Everaude]] spinal and skeletal graft that ties them permanently to their armor. The graft cannot be removed without killing the wearer. At the end of service it is deactivated and left in place.
- **Pain Gating.** Neural governors turn pain into a data feed. A Neodamode knows exactly how badly they are hurt without feeling it.
- **Sustained Stimulant Load.** [[Chem-Dyna]] combat compounds keep operators awake and aggressive for up to a week of continuous fighting.
- **Squad Barrier.** Shieldbearers project a hardened barrier using a [[Telekhines|Telekhine]] shieldstaff. The barrier is almost impenetrable for a few seconds, then draws on the bearer's own vitality once their divine reserves run dry.

### Doctrine and Tactics

#### Walk Into It

Neodamode doctrine assumes the enemy will fire first and fire accurately. The squad advances in a tight wedge behind its shieldbearer, absorbing fire on the barrier and on the frontal armor of the leading assaulters. Gunners fire through ports in the barrier. The squad does not stop until it reaches the enemy line.

#### Dig In

When the barrier fails, Neodamodes go to ground. Every suit carries an entrenching tool and a shaped charge for blowing an instant foxhole, and every squad can raise a hardened firing position within a minute of landing.

#### Relay Wall

Breach platoons advance in overlapping echelons. When one squad's shieldbearer collapses, the next squad steps forward and raises its barrier in front of the first. Assaults can be kept moving under continuous fire for hours this way.

#### Hold Until Relieved

Once a position is taken, Neodamodes are expected to keep it until relieved or killed. [[CODE 2]] retreat orders are rarely issued to them.

### Equipment

#### Armor

- **[[Tian-Qiang Heavy Foundries]] TQ-HX "Ironwarden" assault frame.** A 2.6-meter powered exoskeleton built from scaled-down Titan limb assemblies. It carries 400 kg of armor and lets its wearer carry a Titan-crew weapon one-handed.
- **[[Arisawa Heavy Industries]] ARS-RA-12i "THUNDERPLATE" infantry reactive armor**, a man-portable version of Arisawa's Titan [[Reactive Armor]] that counter-detonates against incoming penetrators.
- **Inner life support** that floods the lungs with the same [[Perfluoro-tert-butyl-perfluorodecalin - Perfluorohexyl-perfluoroisopropyl Ether - Fluorinated Silica Shear-Thickening Oxygenated Suspension Medium|oxygenated suspension medium]] used by the [[Ekdromoi]] when the suit is breached or the atmosphere is toxic.
- **[[Divine Hardening]]** throughout, so that the suit's systems keep working under divine fire and [[Divine Single Event Effect (DSEE)|DSEE]] strikes.

#### Arms

- [[VA-RF-762 “MORROW-IX”]] with 45-round drums, issued as a sidearm in the power frame
- Heavy rotary guns firing [[VAS-762R HE “PHOEBUS-3”]]
- [[ARS-HS-11 “IRON BLOOM”]] shoulder-fired HESH launchers for breaching
- [[Götterspalter Plasmawerfer]] plasma projectors from [[Tochter des Elysium R&D]], issued to breach platoons facing entrenched divine defenders
- Powered mauls and breaching spears from [[Vanguard Armament Solutions]]

#### Shieldstaffs

- **Hydrokinetic Shieldstaff Mk.MCLIX**, the standard [[Telekhines|Telekhine]] barrier focus, capped with a golden eye. Variants exist for electrokinetic and geokinetic bearers.
- **Staff Reservoir Packs**, [[Hyperium]] cells that extend barrier life by up to forty seconds before the staff begins drawing blood.

#### Sustainment

- [[Chem-Dyna]] "Standing Order" stimulant series, cheap and produced in bulk
- Regeneration pods at the Legion rear, where badly wounded Neodamodes are rebuilt in dilute nectar
- [[Vestal Fringe Corporation]] hearth-barracks, which handle rehabilitation and reintegration after service ends

### Countermeasures

| Threat | Neodamode Countermeasure |
|---|---|
| Kinetic penetrators | Reactive armor, layered plate, Telekhine barriers |
| Anti-divine weapons | Divine hardening, redundant mechanical systems that work with no divine input |
| Chemical, biological and radiological agents | Sealed suits, liquid breathing, full decontamination cycles |
| Psychic and fear effects | Neural governors, pain gating, chemically suppressed panic response |
| Elemental attacks | Squad barriers, heat-sink plating, grounding spikes against lightning |

#### Known Vulnerabilities

- Neodamodes are slow. Mobile enemies can bypass a breach platoon entirely.
- Shieldbearers are the key to the squad and are targeted first. Losing one leaves the squad exposed.
- Their dependence on equipment makes them vulnerable to [[Single Event Effects (SEE)]] and electronic attack against the graft frame.
- The Empire treats them as expendable, and casualties are correspondingly enormous.

---

### Social Standing

Neodamodes occupy an awkward place in Imperial society. Veterans hold full citizenship and are legally equal to any other citizen, but they carry visible graft scars and are often treated as lesser by the old citizen families. Many veterans stay in the military and move into the [[Perioeci]], where their experience with heavy equipment is valued.

In the undercities, the Neodamode enrollment lists are both a hope and a threat. Tithe Offices fill quotas from them, and the line between volunteering and being volunteered is often thin.
