# Behemoth

Walking fortress of the [[UOE]] anti-Tartarean line. The [[47th Siege Army]] carries nine in the holds of the *Warbringer Fury* (First Behemoth Wing 1–4, Second Wing 5–9).

**Role (user ruling 2026-11-16):** the heavy bomber / mobile fortress of the UOE. Slow, deliberate and nearly impossible to hurt. It works alongside the [[Titan (Mech)|Honour Titans]], which play the bleeding-edge fighter role.
