**Sciritae** (singular *Sciritas*) are the forward reconnaissance, pathfinder and vanguard troops of the [[Undying Olympian Empire]]. They move days or weeks ahead of the Legions, map what the enemy has, decide where the Empire will land, and hold the first square kilometers of a beachhead until heavier formations arrive. Every major Imperial offensive begins with Sciritae already on the ground.

They are the most sensor-dense infantry the Empire fields. A single Sciritae lance carries enough [[Electronic Intelligence (ELINT)]], [[Augur]]-grade divination and passive detection equipment to build a complete order of battle for a planetary defense network, and to hide itself from that same network while doing so.

> *"The Legion sees what the Sciritae saw yesterday."*
> Imperial staff college proverb

### Name

The name comes from the *Skiritai*, the hill-folk of Skiritis who served Sparta as its permanent advance guard and night patrol. They marched at the head of the army, camped on its exposed flank and went out first when the ground ahead was unknown. The Imperial Sciritae keep that position in the order of march, now measured in light-hours.

---

### Role and Responsibilities

Sciritae exist to remove uncertainty. Their responsibilities are:

- **Strategic reconnaissance** of hostile systems before an invasion is committed, including orbital defense grids, planetary garrisons and pantheonic presence
- **Emitter mapping**, compiling an Electronic Order of Battle and threat library for every radar, jammer, ward and divination array on a target world
- **Pathfinding**, selecting and marking drop zones, landing corridors and [[Aquila-Pattern Assault Lander]] approach lanes
- **Target designation** for orbital fires, naval bombardment and [[Titan (Mech)|Titan]] strikes
- **Beachhead seizure**, holding landing zones for the first minutes of an assault until [[Neodamodes]] and line infantry arrive
- **Screening** the flanks and rear of a Legion in motion
- **Divine census**, counting and classifying every god, demigod and monster in the area of operations for AtRep's threat assessment

Sciritae are expected to avoid combat. A lance that has to fight has usually failed at its primary task.

### Organization

The basic unit is the **lance** of six operators. Three to five lances form a **screen**, which is attached to a Legion or an independent task force.

A lance typically includes:

- **Pathfinder Lead.** Commands the lance, chooses routes and makes the decision to engage or break contact. Usually a Hermes legacy rated [[CODE 24D]].
- **Signals Operator.** Runs the lance's ELINT, SIGINT and communications jamming suite.
- **Augur.** A divination specialist who reads the local [[Noosphere]] and scries for hostile divine presence.
- **Two Scouts.** Move ahead of the lance on foot or drone, placing passive sensor nodes.
- **Designator.** Lays laser, tachyon and divine markers for orbital and Titan fires, and serves as the lance's marksman.

---

### Abilities

Sciritae operators are trained and augmented around the senses.

- **Deep Sight.** Neural overlays fuse radar, infrared, flux data and the operator's own divine perception into a single picture. A Sciritas in full kit can see through three meters of rock and count heartbeats in a bunker.
- **Silent Step.** Hermes and Nyx legacies can suppress sound, scent and footprints within a few meters of themselves.
- **Crossroads Reading.** Augurs and Hecate legacies can identify wards, traps and scrying arrays by the way they bend probability around them.
- **Relay Voice.** Hermes legacies act as living communication nodes, able to pass compressed data to AtRep through heavy jamming.
- **Extended Endurance.** Sciritae are routinely deployed for 60 to 90 days without resupply.

### Doctrine and Tactics

#### Look Without Being Seen

Sciritae doctrine is built on passive collection. A lance emits nothing it does not have to. Its sensors listen, its Augur reads, and its drones drift on air currents with their motors off. Active emissions are limited to short, encrypted burst transmissions timed against the enemy's own scan cycles.

#### Seeding

Scouts plant thousands of dust-sized passive sensor motes along roads, rivers and power lines. The motes build a moving map of enemy traffic that the Signals Operator reads from kilometers away. When the invasion comes, the same motes become targeting beacons.

#### The Quiet Beachhead

When the Legion arrives, the Sciritae shift from watchers to guides. Designators light up air defenses for orbital strikes in the minutes before the landers descend. Scouts mark safe corridors through minefields. The lance then fights for the landing zone just long enough for the first heavy wave to take over.

#### Break Contact

When discovered, a lance scatters, blinds its pursuers with multi-spectral smoke and ECM, and regroups at a pre-arranged point. Sciritae carry no heavy weapons because they are not meant to win firefights.

### Countermeasures

Sciritae are hunted by every competent defender, so their kit is designed to defeat the hunters.

| Threat | Sciritae Countermeasure |
|---|---|
| Radar and lidar | Photonic skin, radar-absorbent coatings, low emission discipline |
| Thermal imaging | Phase-change heat sinks, LUNA-ASH smoke |
| [[Flux Attenuation Scanning]] | Ambient flux mimicry in the Schattenfeld harness |
| Divination and scrying | Anti-scry and anti-augur shrouds, Hecatean wards |
| [[Divine Sense]] and spiritual perception | Nyx-lineage suppression of presence, soul-signature dampeners |
| Direction finding | Burst transmission timed to enemy scan windows, relay through Hermes legacies |

#### Known Vulnerabilities

- Lances carry very little armor and cannot survive a sustained firefight.
- A [[Divine Pariah]] or strong [[Pariah Field]] strips away the divine half of their concealment.
- Cultivator sects with mature [[Divine Sense]] have been recorded detecting Sciritae through intent alone, bypassing technical stealth.

---

### Relationship with Other Formations

Sciritae work most closely with the [[Psiloi]], who shoot at what the Sciritae find, and with the [[Perioeci]], who build on the ground the Sciritae choose. [[Ekdromoi]] cells frequently use Sciritae emitter libraries to plan their own insertions, and a Sciritae career is one of the more common routes into Ekdromoi selection for Hermes legacies.
