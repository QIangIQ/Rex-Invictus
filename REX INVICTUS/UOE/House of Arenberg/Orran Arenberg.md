**[[Orran Arenberg]]** is the elder of the [[Arenberg Twins]], a rogue descendant of the [[House of Arenberg]] and half-sibling to [[Percy Jackson]]. He is ranked 75th on the [[UOE Unified Alltime Rankings]], one place behind his twin [[Virre Arenberg]]. They are the only criminals known to hold double-digit ranks.

## UOE (Far Future)

- Nearly seven feet tall and slender, with scarred skin, high cheekbones and cold eyes that glow a faint green.
- With Virre he takes the Hunter fortress-moon [[Luna Secundus]] ([[Ch12 - Rex Invictus V|Ch12]] to [[Ch14 - Rex Invictus VII|Ch14]]).
- The twins aim to harvest the power of [[Him within the moon]], which they believe is a god-fragment. Intercepted [[Order of the Hollow Crescent]] traffic says they think [[Percy Jackson|Percy]]'s soul was never meant to stay bound to [[Luna Primaris]] or [[Gaia (Planet)]].
