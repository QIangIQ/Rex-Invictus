Mentioned sparsely in the gigantic data-libraries of [[Répertoire du Savoir Athénien]], [[Him within the moon]] is believed to be the metaphysical shard of [[Percy Jackson]]. [[Pure Demigod]] of ancient Gaian origin, the Lord of Demigods. Tethered to the divine consciousness of the goddess [[Artemis]] following their controversial political betrothal during the early phases of the [[The Great Expansion]].
Currently, it has unknowingly taken over the facility's power matrix, and communications relay.
##  Origin and Ascension

The entity was formed inadvertently during the [[Oathbinding at Caligo Vale]], an ancient rite invoked by the [[Hera]] upon [[Olympus Tertiae]], With [[Artemis]], goddess of the hunt and chastity, to Percy Jackson. Though the marriage was nominal and ritualized under extreme duress, both deities carried hidden emotional scars from the event.

Percy Jackson himself had been mindwiped hours later, and sent back to [[Gaia (Planet)]]

Artemis, through divine repression and denial, theorized to be kickstarted though [[Domain Resonance]], began to develop dormant affection for Percy, while Percy, resisting the will imposed upon him, projected fragments of his soul into dreams and silence instead. This convergence of divine affection and mortal defiance coalesced into a shard during a time where Artemis’s domain fully overlapped with Earth’s satellite for the first time in three millennia.
## Nature and Consciousness

Him Within the Moon is not Percy Jackson, nor is it wholly Artemis. Rather, it is an unintended union of unspoken words and unrealized emotion. While it exhibits no overt intelligence, the guards upon [[Luna Primaris]], a few [[Hunters of Artemis]] have reported interactions suggesting awareness, such as dream-echoes or silverlight apparitions speaking in the creatures presence.

Some scholars from [[Delphic House of Apollo]] consider it a relic of unsanctioned love, an accidental demi-deity.
## Suppression and Heresy

The [[Olympians]] has since forbidden any direct worship of Him Within the Moon, branding such reverence as “heresy of fragmentation” due to its disruption of Olympian unity. This has only heightened underground interest, with rogue sects like the [[Order of the Hollow Crescent]] claiming the shard is the _true_ Percy, awaiting reawakening.

The [[Arenberg Twins]] are currently on the path towards [[Luna Primaris]], determined to leech the remnant power off their demigod brother.
