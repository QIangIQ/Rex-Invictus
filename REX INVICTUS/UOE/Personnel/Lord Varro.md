**[[Lord Varro]]** is Overseer of the Legio Administratoris for the Twelfth Olympian Reach, the Empire's records and pensions legion that keeps the Wall of the dead. [[Minerva (AI)|Minerva]] bullied him in [[Percy Jackson|Percy]]'s face, and he later reads aloud the 3,998,050 names of [[Marien Phyrrus]]'s lost echelon.

---

## Greek History (IRL)

**Name.** Varro recalls Marcus Terentius Varro (116 to 27 BC), Rome's great scholar of antiquities and the Latin language.

---

#### Which Version Is This? (Disambiguation)
Only one Lord Varro exists. Do not confuse him with **Archmagister Cael Varros** of Quarterdeck 19 ([[Ch14 - Rex Invictus VII|Ch14]]), a different logistics officer whose name is similar; no link is stated (see `H15_Arenbergs_and_Luna_Secundus.md`). In [[Ch10 - Rex Invictus III|Ch10]] he deals with the **future-era [[Minerva (AI)|Minerva]] in [[Percy Jackson|Percy]]'s clone body** (`B02_Minerva_Percy_Clone_Interlude.md`), not with present-day Percy.

#### Appearance
- **[[Ch10 - Rex Invictus III|Ch10]]:** a pristine uniform "bore none of the wear typical of field command"; "delicate hands" suited to paperwork.
- **[[Ch23 - Interlude I|Ch23]]:** "had very clean hands". He works in a records hall on his barge with forty clerks at forty terminals.

#### Personality
- **Bureaucratic self-importance, quickly punctured ([[Ch10 - Rex Invictus III|Ch10]]).** He asserts jurisdiction over the "unregistered entity", then caves when [[Minerva (AI)|Minerva]] threatens to turn one of "the forty two nearby [[Zeus]]-Wrath hunter-killer satellites" on the fortress-planet: "Where do you want to go?"
- **Self-aware and unbothered ([[Ch23 - Interlude I|Ch23]]).** He knows soldiers despise him and "did not particularly care. Somebody had to count the dead."
- **Arithmetic reflex.** "Varro did the arithmetic in his head, because he always did."
- **Quiet dignity.** He accepts [[Aphrodite]]'s request to read all the names aloud: "Very well," … "Tell Her Majesty that the Legio Administratoris will begin at once."

#### Voice & Speech Patterns
Formal and procedural. He is defensive when challenged and soft-spoken in grief.

#### Story Arc So Far
- **[[Ch10 - Rex Invictus III|Ch10]].** He interrogates [[Minerva (AI)|Minerva]]-in-[[Percy Jackson|Percy]]'s-clone in a sterile room at the Apollonian Hospitalier Complex. She hacks the facility, finds PROJECT ARTEMIS and threatens to turn one of forty-two [[Zeus]]-Wrath hunter-killer satellites on the fortress-planet. He asks "Where do you want to go?" but says her destination, the Nereid Expanse, is "beyond my authorization level". Minerva answers "That is not my problem", walks out regardless, and commandeers the facility's transit and the AnnChase-Pattern battlecruiser squadron herself.
- **[[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]] (referenced).** Minerva notes that the shear casualties' names "were documented by Lord Varro".
- **[[Ch23 - Interlude I|Ch23]].** He processes the KIA list for the Pyrrhuiesian Reserve Echelon: 3,998,050, including [[Marien Phyrrus|Marien Pyrrhus]]. [[Michael Black]], [[Aphrodite]]'s [[Selenide Bulwark]], brings her request that every name be read aloud. Varro objects ("Bulwark, with respect. There are four million names.") and calculates about ninety-two days; Black answers that Aphrodite "has placed a hundred of her own choristers at your disposal". When the recovered verdict proves the echelon alive, he stares at Marien's name. He receives the Director's order: "AMENDED TO: MISSING IN ACTION. LEAVE SPACE ON WALL."
