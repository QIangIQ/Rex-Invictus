The **[[Dove's Fleet]]** is the far-future fleet of [[Aphrodite]], "[[Aphrodite|Mother Majesty]]", stationed above the [[Phlegethon Wound]] in [[Ch23 - Interlude I|Ch23]]. Its flagship is the [[UOE Aphrodite's Grace]].

## UOE (Far Future)

- [[Aphrodite]] orders [[Tullia Ardens]] and the [[Legio XII Fulminata]] to dig permanent positions at the Wound and insists the lost Pyrrhuiesian echelon is alive.
- The [[Commander Thorne|Rosed Knights]] pinned the Giant king [[Porphyrion]] when he rose against the scholars studying the Wound, until a twenty-kilometre dreadnought pressed him into the ground for eleven years.
- Valeria reminds the goddess that "some died in here" means about four million people.

## IRL background

The dove was [[Aphrodite]]'s sacred bird in Greek religion, and doves drew her chariot in poetry such as Sappho's Hymn to Aphrodite.
