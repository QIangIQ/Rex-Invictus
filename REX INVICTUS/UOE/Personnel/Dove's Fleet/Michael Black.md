**[[Michael Black]]** is the [[Selenide Bulwark]] of [[Aphrodite]] in the far future, one of her two guardian champions alongside [[Bulwarks and Spears of Aphrodite|Stephanie Callidora]] of the [[Bulwarks and Spears of Aphrodite]]. He flies the *UOE Flame of Ambition* ([[UOE Dreadnoughts]]).

## UOE (Far Future)

- In the [[War of Princes]] he fought in [[Aphrodite]]'s force with [[Bulwarks and Spears of Aphrodite|Stephanie Callidora]], 87 [[Second-Born Demigod]]s and 124 [[Neo-demigod]]s.
- In [[Ch23 - Interlude I|Ch23]] he brings [[Lord Varro]] Aphrodite's request that every name on the Pyrrhuiesian Reserve Echelon's KIA list (3,998,050) be read aloud. Varro objects that there are four million names.
- Aphrodite's "Oh, Michael" in [[Ch23 - Interlude I|Ch23]] refers to him, not to [[Michael Yew]].
