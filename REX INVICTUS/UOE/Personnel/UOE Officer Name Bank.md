**UOE Officer Name Bank** lists the names the author set aside on 2026-11-18 for the captains, lieutenants and squad leaders of the [[Undying Olympian Empire]]. They serve in the [[UOE Forces]], from the trench regiments of the 47th Siege Army to [[Task Force ORPHEUS]]. See also [[Names (2026-11-16)]] and [[Tartarus Front Soldiers]].

## Officer Families (dual bloodlines)

**Author ruling (2026-11-18):** twins and siblings in these families carry dual or synced bloodlines, with paired or polar affinities (for example an Artemisian and an Apollonian legacy in the same pair). The affinity pairs below are a **PROPOSAL** until the author confirms them.

| Family | Members | Proposed paired affinity |
|---|---|---|
| Brack | Captain Anders Brack, Captain Joakim Brack | [[Apollo]] / [[Artemis]] (sun and moon) |
| Mackarill | Lt Helge Mackarill (Rodin slot 06), Lt Saga Mackarill (Rinda slot 01), Captain Malte Mackerill | [[Poseidon]] / [[Hephaestus]] (sea and forge). The game text spells Malte's surname **Mackerill**, with an e. |
| Okril | Lt Astrid Okril, Lt Torbjörn Okril | [[Hypnos]] / [[Thanatos]] (sleep and death) |
| Pardack | Captain Daniel Pardack, Captain Sigurd Pardack | [[Nyx]] / [[Hemera]] (night and day) |
| Porrock | Captain Henrik Porrock, Lt Lisa Porrock, Lt Olaf Porrock | Boreas / Notus (north and south wind), synced across all three |
| Sköld | Lt Freya Sköld, Lt Sten Sköld | [[Ares]] / [[Athena]] (fury and strategy) |

## Used in the Manuscript

- **Lt Freya Sköld**: Twelfth Trench Regiment forward line, Overpeck Creek, XXI 0536 (was "Lt Dara Quell"). She calls the Apollo and Nemesis rounds.
- **Sgt Lars Kradd**: her sergeant at Overpeck (was "Pell").
- **Lt Olaf Porrock**: handler of Sgt Ragna Holt's [[Trophimoi]] circle, killed permanently by Hel's crossing, XXI 0612 (was "Lt Petros Saye").
- **Lt Torbjörn Okril**: Third Company's lieutenant, lost before 0541, XXI 0546.
- **Sgt Malte Brast**: Third Company's sergeant, XX and XXI (was "Brask").
- Women officers already in the text (Captain Elin Varga, Aedificator-Captain Junia Calloway, Sgt Orsolya Hent, Sgt Ragna Holt) keep their names.
