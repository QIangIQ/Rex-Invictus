## UOE (Far Future)

- **[[Battle of Fulguris]].** Stephanos brought thirteen dreadnoughts to the [[Battle of Fulguris]], detected 240 Gaian seconds out.
- **The Reserve Echelon.** Marien's echelon, four million soldiers, fell through the [[Phlegethon Wound]] onto Mars fourteen thousand years in the past. It now holds the Mars Watchpoint and signed the [[Anabasis Accord]] with [[Olympus]].
- **Helmsmen.** Every [[UOE Navy]] hull is flown by a lobotomized [[Helmsman]].
