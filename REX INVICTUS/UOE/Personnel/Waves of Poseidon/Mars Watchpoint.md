The **[[Mars Watchpoint]]** is the AI-run command node of [[Marien Phyrrus]]'s displaced Pyrrhuiesian Reserve Echelon on present-day Mars, fourteen thousand years before the Empire. It is a fragment of the [[Répertoire du Savoir Athénien|Répertoire]], which [[Minerva (AI)|Minerva]] calls her "sister-fragment".

## Rex Invictus (Present Day)

- **[[Ekdromoi|Kappa-Seven]].** It marks [[Ekdromoi Cell Kappa-Seven]] missing, plays [[Merek]]'s harness burst about a Theodosian and something "wearing our key that the Watchpoint cannot name", computes a 61% probability that CODE 4 has been invoked, and authorizes retrieval as [[Task Force ORPHEUS]].
- **[[Alaska]].** It detects Jade Court cultivators and a company of the mortal [[Mortals of the Present Day|11th Airborne]] converging on Kappa-Seven's dropship in the Brooks Range ([[Alaska]]).
- **CODE 18.** When [[Minerva (AI)|Minerva]] rides through [[Neumond]]'s wings into Lambda-Two's dropship and up its relay chain, the Watchpoint issues CODE 18 ([[00a_CANON_RULINGS]] C18), and Kappa-Seven kneels to the "[[Percy Jackson|Lord Imperator]]".
- **[[The Major]].** [[The Major]] stands at attention there as Kappa-Seven is listed MIA.
- **Link.** The two Minervas talk through its "echo carrier" over the [[Tachyon Bridge Network]].

## UOE (Far Future)

In [[Ch23 - Interlude I|Ch23]] the [[Répertoire du Savoir Athénien|AtRep]] decrypts a Watchpoint packet whose verdict confirms "The [[Percy Jackson|Lord Imperator]]'s key has been presented and accepted". See [[Phlegethon Wound]], [[Anabasis Accord]] and [[OPERATION CODES]].

## IRL background

Mars has had orbiting and landed watchers since Mariner 4 (1965). NASA's Perseverance rover has worked in Jezero Crater since 2021.
