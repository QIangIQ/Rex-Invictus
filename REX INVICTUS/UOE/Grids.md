The [[Grids]] are circumplanetary orbital megastructures classified numerically between **000–999**, each designation representing a complete planetary encirclement architecture spanning the entire [[Kármán Line]]. A Grid is a distributed armored lattice forming a continuous orbital superstructure integrating defense, transit, energy capture, and planetary governance.

Where a [[Dyson Sphere]] encloses a star, a Grid encloses a world.

---

## Design & Construction Authority

Primary Architect: [[Proxima Point-Defence]]  
Strategic Industrial Partner: [[Astralnet Conglomerate]]

[[Proxima Point-Defence]] is responsible for weapons doctrine, orbital structural geometry, plasma projection arrays, and defensive segmentation systems.

[[Astralnet Conglomerate]], a large-scale merchant-transport-trader coalition, supplies manpower, logistics chains, raw material acquisition, and strategic route integration across the [[Tachyon Bridge Network]]. Astralnet also maintains the largest known repository of mapped trade and safe transit corridors within that network.

---

## Strategic Construction Policy

The construction of a Grid is reserved exclusively for the most critical planetary assets within the [[UOE]] sphere of influence.

A Grid requires:

- Planetary-scale mass input
    
- Multi-decade industrial mobilization
    
- Inter-system logistics synchronization
    

It is common for entire minor planets, asteroid belts, or even inhabited but strategically expendable worlds to be stripped and harvested to supply sufficient structural mass. In extreme cases, planetary crusts are dismantled for alloy feedstock.

The decision to authorize a Grid is therefore equivalent to declaring a world indispensable.

---

## Structural Architecture

Each Grid consists of:

- A circumplanetary armored ring-lattice at the [[Kármán Line Skeleton]] boundary
    
- Distributed weapons pylons across equatorial and polar arcs
    
- Elevator spines linking surface anchor-citadels to orbital infrastructure
    
- Integrated energy capture and redistribution systems
    
- Transit corridors aligned to the [[Tachyon Bridge Network]]
    

The structure forms a semi-continuous armored shell, segmented for redundancy but unified through synchronized command architecture.

---

## Core Functions

### Orbital Defense Lattice

- Local point-defense arrays
    
- Long-range plasma artillery batteries
    
- Planetary-scale kinetic mass-driver systems
    
- Intercept and anti-fleet suppression grids
    

A Grid can independently deny orbital access without fleet reinforcement.

---

### Space Elevation & Transit

- Multi-node elevator spines
    
- Armored cargo and military lift corridors
    
- Launch assist and controlled re-entry shielding
    

Traditional spaceports become obsolete under Grid governance.

---

### Slingshot & Strategic Acceleration

- Orbital mass accelerators
    
- High-energy outbound launch corridors
    
- Direct synchronization with the [[Tachyon Bridge Network]]
    

This enables rapid deployment of Astralnet fleets and military strike elements.

---

### Planetary Dyson Analog

Functionally, a Grid mirrors the concept of a [[Dyson Sphere]] at planetary scale:

- Energy capture
    
- Defensive containment
    
- Trade chokepoint control
    
- Orbital sovereignty enforcement
    

A world encircled by a Grid is sealed, defended, and economically dominant.

---

## Integration with Higher Systems

### [[Noosphere]]

Early Grid command-lattice architecture provided the foundation for the [[Noosphere]] concept — a distributed planetary cognition network integrating sensor arrays, defense logic, and atmospheric regulation systems.

Grid-class planets typically operate as Noospheric anchor nodes.

---

### [[Gaia Paradigm]] under the [[Eden Project]]

Current Grid deployments align with the [[Gaia Paradigm]], a planetary optimization framework within the broader [[Eden Project]].

Under this doctrine, Grids provide:

- Atmospheric stabilization
    
- Climate control
    
- Biosphere monitoring
    
- Orbital hazard suppression
    

The Grid becomes not only a weapon, but a governor.

---

## Classification Range (000–999)

Grid designation numbers correspond to:

- Generation lineage
    
- Weapon density tier
    
- Planetary strategic value
    
- Depth of [[Noosphere]] integration
    

Higher-series Grids exhibit heavier armament, deeper automation, and greater energy throughput capacity.

---

## Scale Comparison

Relative to conventional orbital platforms:

- Surface anchor-citadels rival continental megacities
    
- Elevator spines extend thousands of kilometers
    
- Plasma batteries equal capital-ship main armaments
    
- Kinetic rails exceed planetary artillery in scale
    

A single Grid can dominate inner-system orbital space without external support.
