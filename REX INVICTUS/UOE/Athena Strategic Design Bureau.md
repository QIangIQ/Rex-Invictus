The **[[Athena Strategic Design Bureau]]** is [[Athena]]'s far-future weapons design office. It designed the [[Zeus-Wrath Hunter Killer Satellites]] and, with the [[Hephaestus Forge Cohorts]], the [[Aquila-Pattern Assault Lander]]. It sits beside the [[Répertoire du Savoir Athénien]] and [[Parlamentum mk.III]] in [[Athena]]'s intelligence apparatus.
