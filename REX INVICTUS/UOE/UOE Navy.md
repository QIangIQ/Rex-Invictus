The **[[UOE Navy]]** is the void fleet of the [[Undying Olympian Empire]]. Each major Olympian holds a fleet, its hulls run from 500-metre line ships to capital hulls over 20 kilometres, and every ship is flown by a [[Helmsman]].

---

## Fleets and commands

- [[Waves of Poseidon]]: [[Poseidon]]'s fleet under [[Stephanos Pyrrhus]].
- [[Dove's Fleet]]: [[Aphrodite]]'s fleet under [[Valeria Sevrin]].
- [[Zeus UOE|Imperiality of Zeus]]: includes the [[UOE Divine Crusader]] commanded by [[Eleni Theodosia]].
- Capital ships: [[UOE Dreadnoughts]], [[UOE Flagships]], [[UOE Aphrodite's Grace]].

## Suppliers

- [[Institute Academy]] provides capital-ship power cores, gravitational containment fields and the exclusive outfitting of black-hole launchers.
- [[Arisawa Heavy Industries]], [[Vanguard Armament Solutions]] and the [[Hephaestus Forge Cohorts]] supply hulls and weapons.

## History

- In the [[War of Princes]] a huge share of the Navy was diverted to anti-pantheonic operations, leaving only 30% of the galaxy's ships to defend home sectors while the rest besieged the [[Voss System]].
- Naval vulnerabilities include [[Electronic Warfare (EW)]], divine interference and relativistic delay, which is why the [[Helmsman]] program exists.
