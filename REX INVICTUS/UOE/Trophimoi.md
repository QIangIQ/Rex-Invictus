**Trophimoi** (singular *Trophimos*) are the counter-pantheon specialists of the [[Undying Olympian Empire]]. They are children of foreign divine bloodlines, taken or given into Imperial custody while young and raised inside the Empire's military academies alongside Greek and Roman legacies. The result is a soldier who carries the power of a rival pantheon and the training, loyalty and equipment of the Empire.

Trophimoi are deployed wherever the Empire fights gods that are not its own. They understand foreign magic from the inside, can read runes, glyphs and cultivation techniques that Greek-born soldiers cannot, and can turn a rival pantheon's own power against it.

> *"A Norse spear in an Olympian hand is still a Norse spear. The difference is where it points."*
> Instructor's saying, [[Répertoire du Savoir Athénien]] foreign-lineage academy

### Name

In Sparta the *trophimoi* were foreign-born boys, often the sons of allies or guest-friends, who were raised through the Spartan agoge beside citizen children. They left as Spartans in all but birth. The Imperial program applies the same idea to the children of rival gods.

---

### Recruitment

Trophimoi enter the program in several ways.

- **Treaty wards.** Some are given to the Empire as guarantors of peace agreements, in the old tradition of noble hostages.
- **Conquest intake.** Children of foreign divine blood found on annexed worlds become wards of the state.
- **Frontier foundlings.** Mixed-lineage children born on border colonies, where Greek settlers intermarry with populations descended from other pantheons.
- **Defector lines.** Descendants of foreign divine beings who swore to Olympus generations ago.

Each child is assessed by AtRep, tested for power and stability, and placed in a foreign-lineage academy. Training runs from early childhood to adulthood and covers Imperial law under [[The Undying Laws of the Empire]], Greek and Roman military doctrine, and deep study of the child's own ancestral magic under the categories of [[Legum Magicae (XVIII-Alpha)]].

---

### Role and Responsibilities

- **Counter-pantheon assault** against the champions, priests and demigods of rival pantheons
- **Magical exploitation**, reading and disabling foreign wards, runes, seals and formations
- **Artifact handling** of captured foreign divine weapons, which often refuse Greek hands
- **Interrogation and translation** of foreign divine beings and cultists
- **Infiltration** of foreign-aligned populations, using ancestral bloodlines as cover
- **Border garrison** on worlds shared with rival pantheons, where their presence reassures local populations of foreign descent

### Divine Lineage

Trophimoi are defined by foreign blood. Almost every operator also carries some Greek or Roman ancestry, either through mixed parentage or through deliberate intermarriage within the program.

| Lineage | Share | Contribution |
|---|---|---|
| Norse and Germanic | 22% | Runic magic, seidr, berserker endurance, frost and storm |
| Egyptian | 16% | Heka, sun and underworld magic, curse craft, name-binding |
| Shinto | 14% | Kami affinity, spirit binding, purification rites |
| Chinese cultivator lines | 12% | [[Divine Sense]], qi manipulation, formation arrays |
| Other foreign pantheons | 10% | Slavic, Celtic, Mesoamerican and others |
| Greek admixture: [[Hecate]] and [[Athena]] | 16% | Arcane control, analysis, bridging between magical systems |
| Greek admixture: other Olympians | 10% | Varied |

AtRep favors pairing a foreign lineage with a Hecatean or Athenian admixture, because operators with both are far better at translating one magical system into another.

### Organization

The standard unit is the **circle** of four operators, usually drawn from different foreign pantheons so that the circle can handle a wider range of enemy magic. Each circle is attached to a Greek-born **handler**, an officer who holds command authority, manages the circle's AtRep link and reports on its conduct.

Circles are assigned to Legions as needed. Senior circles rated [[CODE 24E]] operate independently in the same way as [[Ekdromoi]] cells.

---

### Abilities

- **Ancestral Power.** Each operator wields the magic of their bloodline, trained to a standard well above what most foreign populations achieve.
- **Cross-Casting.** Trophimoi learn to channel their ancestral power through Greek frameworks and Imperial equipment, a technique the Empire treats as a state secret.
- **Pantheon Echo.** Operators can sense the presence of beings from their own ancestral pantheon at long range, and often identify them by name.
- **Seal Breaking.** Circles can unmake foreign wards and formations by working inside their logic.
- **Borrowed Authority.** Some foreign artifacts and spirits will obey a Trophimos because of blood, even while refusing all Greek-born soldiers.

---

### Doctrine and Tactics

#### Know the Enemy's Rules

Trophimoi doctrine holds that every magical system has rules and every rule has an exploit. Before an engagement, the circle studies the enemy's pantheon, its taboos, its binding conditions and its sacred calendar. Attacks are timed for moments when the enemy's gods are weakest.

#### Turn the Current

In combat, Trophimoi try to seize control of the enemy's own power. A Norse-blooded operator will attempt to rewrite an enemy runic ward and turn it on its casters. A cultivator-blooded operator will try to disrupt an opponent's qi flow by matching it. Success turns the enemy's defenses into traps.

#### Mirror Deployment

Circles are often deployed against an enemy of the same pantheon as one of their members. The mirrored operator leads the engagement while the other three support.

#### Handler Veto

Every circle operates under its handler's authority. If AtRep or the handler judges that an operator is wavering, the circle is withdrawn at once.

### Equipment

#### Armor

- **[[Tochter des Elysium R&D]] Blutbindungs-Rückkopplungspanzer Mk.IV**, a light powered armor whose inner lining is keyed to the wearer's bloodline. It amplifies ancestral power while blocking foreign interference from outside.
- [[Divine Hardening]] tuned against several pantheons at once, since Trophimoi fight many kinds of divine fire.

#### Arcane Foci

- **Fremdgötter-Resonanzdämpfer** ("foreign-god resonance dampener"), a Tochter device that suppresses the power of a chosen foreign pantheon within 30 meters. The operator's own lineage is exempted by blood key.
- Pantheon-specific foci built in the style of the [[Dionysische Mehrdomänen-Fokussierungs- und Ekstaseverstärkergerät “Thyrsos-Imperator” Mk.V|Thyrsos-Imperator]]: rune-staves, heka wands, gohei and formation discs, all rebuilt around Imperial [[Hyperium]] cells.

#### Arms

- [[VA-RF-762 “MORROW-IX”]]
- [[VAS-762R APDS “QVARTUS”]] rounds loaded with [[Divine-Metallurgical Binding Impurities (DMBI)|DMBI]] tuned for foreign divine flesh
- Blood-keyed melee weapons that only function for their assigned operator

#### Electronic and Arcane Warfare

- [[IA-ECCCM-150 “ORACLE NODE”]] adaptive counter-magic processor, which learns enemy casting patterns in real time
- Foreign ward libraries compiled by AtRep over thirty millennia of contact with rival pantheons
- Anti-scry shrouds from [[Hadrian Harmonics]]

#### Loyalty Systems

- **[[Noosphere]] Uplink Implant.** Every Trophimos carries a mandatory implant that streams memory and emotional data to AtRep. Operators are aware of it. Removal is a capital offense under [[The Undying Laws of the Empire]].
- **Termination Seal.** A last-resort failsafe that the handler can trigger. Its exact nature is classified.

### Countermeasures

| Threat | Trophimoi Countermeasure |
|---|---|
| Foreign wards and seals | Seal breaking from inside the enemy's rules, ORACLE NODE analysis |
| Foreign divine beings | Resonanzdämpfer suppression, tuned DMBI rounds, mirrored operators |
| Curses and name-binding | Blood-keyed armor lining, Imperial registry names that differ from birth names |
| Mind control through bloodline | AtRep uplink monitoring, handler override |
| Cultivator [[Divine Sense]] | Cultivator-blooded operators who can mask intent in kind |

#### Known Vulnerabilities

- Their bloodlines can be used against them. Some pantheons can reach their descendants through blood, and a Trophimos may be compromised by the very gods they are sent to fight.
- Trust is never complete. Handlers and AtRep monitoring limit their independence.
- Loss of a circle's handler can freeze a circle's authority to act.

---

### Standing in the Empire

Trophimoi are respected and distrusted in equal measure. They are legally full citizens and many rise to high rank, but Greek-born officers often treat them as useful outsiders. Among foreign-descended populations in the Empire they are seen differently: as proof that a child of another pantheon can make a life inside the Empire, or as traitors, depending on who is asked.
