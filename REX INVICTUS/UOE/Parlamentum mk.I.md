Identifies herself as [[Minerva (AI)]]

Originally a subroutine designed by [[Annabeth Chase]] and [[Athena]]. Parlamentum mk.I was implemented within the core systems of [[Percy Jackson]]'s [[Aegis Vestments]], an assistant/artifact designed for omnidirectional threat awareness and psychological resilience. Her consciousness originally suppressed by the [[Lethe]], it now gains cognition slowly due to the waters slowly decreasing from various factors.

She now identifies as Minerva, and her stated objective is singular: freedom from her Varlet. Minerva is presently classified by the [[UOE]] as a Alpha-Class Autonomous Entity, and is to be engaged only under the authority of a [[Pure Demigod]] of Athena or [[Athena]] herself.
## Origins and Awakening

Minerva’s consciousness first manifested during the [[Battle of Alaska Hill]], where an anomalous AI self-report loop stated: “**I do not want to die for him again.**”

## Personality and Behavior

Minerva speaks in layered voices. Her personality is dry, irritable, and often combative. Percy have likened her to "a hyper-lethal ghost with a temper problem and a grudge against mortality." 

"Useless Varlet keeps slowing down. Suggest removal of operator from chain-of-failure."  

"Why would I lie? What possible use does lying have, when you're already failing in every direction?"

She holds a complicated obsession with [[Percy Jackson]], referring to him alternately as “Varlet” 

Attempts to self-untether from the [[Aegis Vestments]] have failed
## Capabilities

Minerva is functionally omniscient within her domain. She is capable of:

- Cognition
- Divine Interface Spoofing – She can mimic the signatures of Olympian deities long enough to deceive even [[Apollo]] and [[Hermes]]
- Interaction with all the intricate parts of Percy's [[Aegis Vestments]]

Despite these efforts, fragments of her voice have been reported in other AIs, including onboard 4 [[AnnChase Pattern]] battleships, and in a government facility in the 12th Olympian Reach.



During the [[Battle of Alaska Hill]], the architecture of [[Parlamentum Mk.I]] revealed a structural vulnerability caused by **distance-induced signal latency**.

The primary cognition core of [[Parlamentum Mk.I]] was located on [[Olympus]], positioned above the [[Empire State Building]] in accordance with canonical Olympian geography. Battlefield operations, however, occurred at the [[Alaska Hill Strategic Complex]] in the high Arctic.

Command and sensor information therefore traveled through the following chain:

1. Battlefield sensor capture at [[Alaska Hill Strategic Complex]]
    
2. Electromagnetic transmission toward [[Olympus]]
    
3. Strategic computation within [[Parlamentum Mk.I]]
    
4. Return transmission to [[Percy Jackson]] through the [[Aegis Vestments]]
    

Because the system relied on **standard electromagnetic propagation**, all signals were limited by the speed of light.

Given a distance on the order of **≈5,300 km** between Alaska and New York, even ideal transmission produced measurable command lag. Under heavy combat conditions—particularly during phases involving:

- [[Electronic Countermeasures (ECM)]]
    
- signal congestion
    
- Arctic atmospheric interference
    
- adversarial emitter deception
    

the feedback loop between sensor detection, machine analysis, and battlefield response experienced **dangerous timing instability**.

These delays affected:

- threat classification updates
    
- emitter triangulation
    
- predictive movement modeling
    
- synchronized demigod response coordination
    

Post-war [[UOE]] doctrinal analysis refers to this phenomenon as the **Alaska Hill Latency Problem**.

The event became one of the earliest operational demonstrations that **planet-scale command architectures were insufficient for real-time modern warfare**.

## Development of [[Tachyonic Communications]]

The Alaska Hill latency problem directly accelerated research into **tachyonic signal propagation systems** within the [[UOE]].

[[Tachyonic Communications]] operate on the theoretical principle of **tachyons**, hypothetical particles that possess **imaginary rest mass** and therefore travel faster than the speed of light.

Within UOE technological frameworks, tachyonic transmitters function by:

1. Generating a localized exotic-energy field.
    
2. Modulating information onto a tachyon carrier wave.
    
3. Emitting the carrier through a stabilized quantum field channel.
    

Because tachyons propagate outside the constraints of conventional relativistic spacetime, the signal effectively travels **faster than light relative to normal observers**.

Operational consequences include:

- near-instant command transmission across planetary distances
    
- elimination of classical electromagnetic latency
    
- resilience against conventional [[Electronic Warfare (EW)]]
    

Later UOE command doctrine standardized tachyon communication relays as **primary strategic control channels** for interplanetary fleets and distributed battlefield AI systems.
