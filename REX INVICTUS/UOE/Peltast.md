**Peltasts** (*Peltastai*) are the mobile assault and anti-armor skirmishers of the [[Undying Olympian Empire]]. They are jump infantry who fight in the open spaces between the Legion's heavy formations, hunting enemy armor, [[Titan (Mech)|Titans]], monsters and demigods with high-velocity javelin weapons, then vanishing before the enemy can respond. Where [[Neodamodes]] hold a line, Peltasts break the enemy's line and run down whatever flees.

Peltasts are the largest body of divinely powered line troops in the Empire and the backbone of its mobile warfare. A Legion without Peltasts can take ground but cannot exploit it.

> *"Throw, move, throw again. If the Titan is still standing, you did not move enough."*
> Peltast drill instructor

### Name

Peltasts were the javelin skirmishers of the ancient Greek world, originally Thracian, named after the *pelte*, a light crescent shield of wicker and hide. They were fast enough to escape hoplites and dangerous enough to wear them down with thrown javelins. At Lechaion in 391 BC, Athenian peltasts destroyed a whole Spartan regiment by striking and retreating until it broke. The Imperial Peltasts keep the shield, the javelin and the tactic.

---

### Role and Responsibilities

- **Anti-armor and anti-Titan hunting**, using javelin and lance weapons at close to medium range
- **Monster hunting**, killing large divine and mythic creatures in the open field
- **Exploitation**, pouring through breaches made by the [[Neodamodes]] and attacking enemy rear areas
- **Flank security** for Legion formations
- **Pursuit** of routed enemies
- **Raiding** supply lines, artillery positions and command posts
- **Counter-demigod engagements** against enemy champions who break through the Legion line

### Organization

The basic unit is the **file** of eight. Files operate in pairs, one moving while the other throws. Four files make a **wing**, and a Legion cohort usually fields two wings.

A file typically includes:

- **File Leader**
- **Five Javeliners**, the main anti-armor element
- **Lancer**, carrying the file's heaviest weapon for finishing Titans
- **Shield Runner**, who projects extended barriers to cover the file as it moves

---

### Abilities

- **Burst Movement.** Hermes and Zeus legacies can cross fifty meters in a blink, turning jump rigs into short-range teleportation.
- **Charged Throw.** Peltasts channel their divine power into thrown weapons. A lightning-charged javelin can fry a Titan's systems on impact.
- **Weak-Point Sense.** Athena and Ares legacies instinctively see joints, seams and reactor vents on armored targets.
- **Unstoppable Momentum.** Nike legacies grow stronger with each hit their file lands, and a file on a streak is extremely hard to stop.
- **Ground Strike.** Poseidon legacies can buckle the ground under a Titan's feet, forcing it off balance for the throw.

---

### Doctrine and Tactics

#### Strike and Withdraw

Peltast doctrine is the Lechaion method. A file closes to throwing range, delivers a coordinated volley against a single target, and withdraws before the target or its escorts can respond. The second file then strikes from another angle. The target is attacked from every side and never gets to fight back.

#### Hunting Titans

Against a Titan, a wing breaks into files that circle at different ranges. Javeliners target sensors and joints first to blind and slow it. Once it is crippled, the Lancer closes and drives a lance round into the reactor.

#### Monster Protocol

Against large monsters, Peltasts fight like hunters. Poseidon and Zeus legacies pin the creature, javeliners bleed it with DMBI weapons that stop regeneration, and the file keeps moving until it falls.

#### Never Stand Still

Peltasts do not dig in. A file that has stopped moving has stopped being a Peltast file. When the enemy brings up heavy force, Peltasts fall back behind the Neodamode line and wait for the next opening.

### Equipment

#### Armor

- **Medium powered armor** balancing protection with speed, lighter than the Neodamode frame and heavier than Sciritae kit
- **[[Hadrian Ionics]] HI-PT-7 "Crescent" pelte**, an arm-mounted crescent energy shield. It deploys in a tenth of a second, stops heavy rifle fire and anti-tank rounds for a few seconds, then collapses to recharge.
- Shield Runners carry the larger HI-PT-9 "Horned Moon" variant, which can cover a whole file

#### Mobility

- **Jump rigs** built on [[Institute Academy]] vectorized plasma thrusters and gravitic compensation drives. Peltasts can make bounds of 200 meters and hover briefly for a throw.
- **[[Hadrian Tachyonics]] HT-BL-3 "Stride"**, a short-range displacement pulse issued to veteran files, allowing a jump of up to 30 meters without crossing the space between

#### Javelin Weapons

- **[[Vanguard Armament Solutions]] VAS-JV-11 "Aichme" smart javelin**, a reusable guided javelin with a [[Hyperium]]-core tip, recalled to the thrower's hand by magnetic tether
- Shoulder launchers firing [[ARS-DK-12 “EXCOMMUNION”]] divine-material penetrators
- Man-portable versions of [[ARS-KN-18 “SUNPIERCE”]] HEAT rounds for enemy armor
- **Titan lances**, crew-portable recoilless spears firing [[ARS-VN-44 “CATACLYSM PIKE”]] rounds, carried by the Lancer

#### Small Arms

- Compact carbine versions of the [[VA-RF-762 “MORROW-IX”]]
- Xiphos and gladius sidearms from [[Vanguard Armament Solutions]] for close quarters

#### Electronic Warfare

- Personal chaff and flare dispensers on the jump rig
- [[IA-ECM-12 “SILVER TEMPEST”]] chaff cartridges to break Titan target locks
- Short-range jammers to blind enemy point defense during the throw

### Countermeasures

| Threat | Peltast Countermeasure |
|---|---|
| Titan weapons | Constant movement, jump rigs, displacement pulses |
| Point defense and active protection | Saturation volleys from multiple angles, jamming during the throw |
| Enemy heavy infantry | Refusing close combat, withdrawing behind the Neodamode line |
| Monster regeneration | DMBI javelin tips, lightning-charged throws |
| Area-effect divine attacks | Dispersed file spacing, pelte shields, burst movement out of the blast |

#### Known Vulnerabilities

- Peltasts are exposed in the open. Well-sited artillery and air defense can shred a wing mid-leap.
- Jump rigs and pelte shields depend on power cells. A long engagement can leave a file grounded.
- Peltasts lose much of their effectiveness in tight terrain such as tunnels and ship corridors, where Neodamodes are preferred.

---

### Relationship with Other Formations

Peltasts are the most common source of [[Ekdromoi]] candidates. The Peltast corps teaches aggression, independent movement and anti-armor skill, and the Ekdromoi selection process takes those qualities further. Veteran Peltasts rated [[CODE 24D]] are frequently watched by AtRep for possible selection.
