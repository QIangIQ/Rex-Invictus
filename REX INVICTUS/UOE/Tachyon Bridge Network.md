The **[[Tachyon Bridge Network]]** (TBN) is the faster-than-light highway of the far future. [[Hermes]] and [[Athena]] built it during [[The Great Expansion]] as the travel network for Greek territory. Ships enter and leave it at [[TBN Relay]] stations, and information moves along it "near instantly".

---

## UOE (Far Future)

- **Trade.** [[Astralnet Conglomerate]] holds the largest repository of mapped trade and safe routes through the network and sells passages "that aren't on any chart". [[Aspina Aggregates]] moves [[Hyperium]] and [[Eurobium]] along it.
- **Communications.** The [[Noosphere]] carries information from a planet's populace up to its [[Grids]], then along the TBN.
- **Defense.** The [[Kármán Line Skeleton]] guards worlds integrated into the network.
- **Accidents.** A power burst from an unknown [[Primordial]] on the network threw Apollonian colony ships off the highway into the void. Their descendants forgot their god, began worshipping the sun and became the [[Sunborne Cult]].
- **War.** In the [[War of Princes]] the [[Voss System]] relays decided the campaign. [[Kagura Kuroyami]] reopened the [[Yamamoto Chokepoint Relay]], the only public link between the [[Undying Olympian Empire]] and the [[Amaterasu Dominion]], and Shinto ships later bypassed UOE sensor nets on routes she calculated. [[Tochter des Elysium R&D]] researches TBN interference technology.
- **In the manuscript.** [[Minerva (AI)|Minerva]]'s commandeered squadron exits a tachyon bridge at a [[Zeus]]/[[Poseidon]] border hub in [[Ch11 - Rex Invictus IV|Ch11]]. The displaced present-day [[Minerva (AI)|Minerva]] links to the Mars Watchpoint over the network.

## IRL background

A tachyon is a hypothetical particle that always travels faster than light. No experiment has found one, and in relativity a faster-than-light signal would break causality. Science fiction often uses the name for FTL communication.
