**Perioeci** (singular *Perioecus*) are the combat engineers, siege troops and logistics corps of the [[Undying Olympian Empire]]. They build the fortresses, dig the tunnels, raise the field [[Grids]], run the supply lines and knock down the enemy's walls. Where other formations fight for ground, the Perioeci make ground worth holding.

A Perioeci "township" can turn a bare landing zone into a fortified base with power, shields, point defense, fabrication and a hospital in under seventy-two hours. The Legions do not move without them.

> *"The Sciritae chose the hill. The Neodamodes took the hill. We made it a fortress, and we'll still be here when they've forgotten its name."*
> Township foreman, [[Legio X Fretensis]]

### Name

The *perioikoi* ("dwellers around") of Laconia lived in the towns surrounding Sparta. They were free, but not citizens in the full sense. They ran the trades, the smithies and the commerce that the Spartan state depended on, and they marched to war as hoplites alongside the Spartiates. The Imperial Perioeci keep the same role: the craftsmen of the army, who fight when they must and build when they can.

---

### Role and Responsibilities

- **Fortification**, from trench lines and bunkers up to orbital-grade citadels
- **Field Grid construction**, raising local shield, sensor and point-defense networks around bases and beachheads
- **Siegecraft**, including breaching fortress walls, collapsing tunnels and reducing hardened [[Grids|Grid]] anchor nodes
- **Mine warfare**, both laying and clearing
- **Logistics**, running supply depots, fuel lines, [[Hyperium]] reactors and fabrication plants
- **Recovery and repair** of damaged armor, vehicles and [[Titan (Mech)|Titans]]
- **Civil works** on occupied worlds, working with [[Vestal Fringe Corporation]] to make captured territory livable

### Organization

The basic unit is the **township**, a self-sufficient engineer company of about 400 personnel. A township contains:

- **Sapper Platoons**, frontline engineers who breach, mine and demine under fire
- **Grid Section**, which raises field shield, sensor and point-defense networks
- **Forge Section**, a mobile fabrication plant able to build ammunition, spare parts and prefabricated fortifications from raw material
- **Siege Battery**, with bunker-buster and earthshaker equipment
- **Supply Section**, running depots and convoys
- **Hearth Section**, providing medical, food and morale services, often staffed jointly with [[Vestal Fringe Corporation]]

Several townships make up a **Polis**, the engineer brigade attached to a Legion.

---

### Abilities

- **Field Forging.** Hephaestus legacies can shape metal by hand at forge temperatures, weld armor plates in combat and repair Titan joints without tools.
- **Earthshaking.** Poseidon earthshaker lines can liquefy soil under enemy positions, collapse tunnels or open trenches for their own troops in seconds.
- **Rooted Ground.** Demeter legacies can bind loose soil with root networks, stabilizing crater fields into ground that can carry armor.
- **Hearth Ward.** Hestia legacies can raise a sanctuary field around a base that calms troops, slows panic and resists fear effects.
- **Structural Sight.** Athena legacies can see load paths and weak points in any structure at a glance.

### Doctrine and Tactics

#### Build Under Fire

Perioeci doctrine assumes that construction will be contested. The first things a township builds are its own protection: a field Grid, point defense and a shield dome. Everything else is built inside that umbrella.

#### Siege Arithmetic

Against fortifications, the Perioeci work methodically. The Siege Battery identifies the structure's load-bearing points, the Sapper Platoons approach by trench and tunnel, and the earthshaker operators collapse the foundations before the walls are touched. A fortress falls when its ground gives way.

#### Denial

When the Legion withdraws, the Perioeci leave behind minefields, collapsed passes and booby-trapped infrastructure. Retreating from a Perioeci-held position is expensive for the enemy to follow.

#### The Long Supply Line

Perioeci convoys run on AtRep-optimized schedules. Depots are dispersed, redundant and buried, so no single strike can starve a Legion.

### Equipment

#### Armor

- **Arisawa ARS-EX-30 "Foundry Plate"** heavy engineering exoskeleton with integrated welding, cutting and lifting tools
- Heat-proof liners for forge and reactor work
- [[Explosive Reactive Armor (ERA)]] panels for sappers operating in the breach

#### Field Grid Systems

- **[[Proxima Point-Defence]] PPD-FG-9 "Little Skeleton"**, a man-portable field Grid based on the orbital [[Kármán Line Skeleton]]. A township can deploy a shield dome and point-defense lattice covering four square kilometers.
- [[Energy Shielding]] projectors fed by portable [[Hyperium]] reactors
- [[IA-ECCM-52 “ATHENA GRID”]] frequency-agile sensor network to keep the field Grid running under jamming

#### Siege and Demolition

- [[ARS-BR-60 “UNDERKING”]] and [[ARS-GX-48 “BLACK ANVIL”]] bunker busters in crew-served launchers
- [[ARS-TN-72 “CITADEL REND”]] for reducing hardened Grid anchor nodes
- Shaped and thermobaric demolition charges
- Tunnel boring machines able to dig at walking pace through bedrock

#### Mine Warfare

- [[Serpentine Baylock]] smart mines, including pop-up anti-Titan mines and divine-triggered mines that detonate on the presence of [[Ichor]]
- [[Flak]] emplacements and [[Swarm Missile]] launchers for base air defense
- Mine-clearing line charges and robotic demining swarms

#### Logistics and Fabrication

- Mobile forges powered by [[Institute Academy]] micro-reactors
- Matter printers able to produce [[VA-RF-762 “MORROW-IX”]] ammunition from raw ore
- [[Saatverteilungs- und Zweitbefruchtungsautomatikgerät Demeter-Erweiterungssystem Mk.III|Demeter-Erweiterungssystem]] rigs for growing garrison food on hostile soil
- Vestal hearth-nodes: field hospitals, clean water plants and shelter domes

### Countermeasures

| Threat | Perioeci Countermeasure |
|---|---|
| Artillery and orbital strikes | Field Grid shield domes, point defense, deep-buried construction |
| Sappers and tunnelers | Seismic sensors, earthshaker counter-mining |
| Air and drone attacks | Flak, swarm missiles, point-defense lasers |
| Electronic attack on shields | Frequency-agile Grid control, redundant mechanical backups |
| Divine siege attacks | Hearth wards, [[Divine Hardening]] of structural cores |

#### Known Vulnerabilities

- Townships are large, slow to relocate and easy to find.
- Their field Grids depend on reactor power. Striking a township's reactors can bring down every shield at once.
- Perioeci are trained to fight, but they are not frontline troops. A township caught without infantry cover is in serious danger.
