The **[[Olympian High Council]]** is the ruling council of the [[Olympians]] in the far-future [[Undying Olympian Empire]]. It holds directive authority over strategic weapons: [[Athena]] built the [[Zeus-Wrath Hunter Killer Satellites]] under its orders, and production of [[Pathogen Alpha-7]] is classified at its highest tier.

**Myth background.** In Greek myth the Twelve Olympians met in [[Zeus]]'s hall on [[Olympus]]. In PJO canon the Olympian Council sits on [[Olympus|Mount Olympus]] above the Empire State Building, and its winter solstice meeting closes *The Titan's Curse*.
