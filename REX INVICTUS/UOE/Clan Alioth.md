Clan Alioth is an ancient Human supernatural family possessing an exceptionally rare hereditary affinity for the manipulation of Space and Time. The family is among the oldest organizations associated with [[Nightingale]], having served [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] since the earliest history of the Vampire Kingdom. Although its members possess characteristics related to Witches, the Alioth Bloodline differs fundamentally from ordinary Witch [[Magic]] because its supernatural development is restricted to abilities associated with Space and Time. The family's extraordinary powers originate from the mysterious Alioth Eyes, an inherited ocular ability whose complete capabilities and original source remain unknown.[1][2][3]
Unlike Nightingale's Vampire Count Clans, Clan Alioth does not derive its political importance from a Count title, territory administered as one of the Kingdom's Pillars, or overwhelming physical combat capabilities. Its position originated from the indispensable services it provided directly to the Vampire King. The family was responsible for extraordinary spatial and temporal operations, including interdimensional transportation, the isolation of entire environments, restrictions upon travel between worlds, and the alteration of Time itself. Such abilities gave Clan Alioth an unusual degree of strategic importance within Nightingale's political hierarchy, allowing its members to exercise influence disproportionate to their comparatively limited physical power as Humans.[4][5]
The current known leader is Alexios Alioth, a powerful Space-Time practitioner, longtime confidant of Vlad, and one of the Vampire King's most trusted advisers. His daughter, Nataliana "Natalia" Elderblood Alioth, originally served as the personal maid of [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] before becoming one of the wives of [[Victor Elderblood]]. Natalia inherited the family's supernatural Eyes and eventually developed their powers beyond the known limitations of her father, becoming a genuine Dragon Goddess of Space and Time after her transformation into a True Blood Dragon.[6][3]
Clan Alioth is particularly unusual because the family's hereditary abilities possess characteristics increasingly difficult to explain through conventional Magic. Although the Bloodline was historically believed to have originated from a Human who developed an extraordinary mutation after encountering Magic, later investigations revealed that this explanation was inadequate. Victor suspected that the Eyes might originate from an ancient Primordial God or a powerful Being associated with the Upper Sectors, but he could not confirm either possibility. Even beings possessing extensive knowledge of supernatural history, including Albedo Moriarty, Alexios, Natalia, and [[Jeanne Elderblood]], lacked a definitive explanation for the family's abilities.[3]
The family's legacy extends into the [[Elderblood Empire]] through Natalia and her daughter, Norabell Elderblood. Natalia's Draconic evolution revealed the extraordinary potential concealed within the Alioth Bloodline, while Norabell inherited an even more unusual combination of Space-Time affinity and the supernatural perception associated with Victor's lineage. Their development demonstrated that Clan Alioth's hereditary powers could continue evolving far beyond the limitations of the Human family through which they originally emerged.[7]

### Overview

#### Nature of the Clan
Clan Alioth is a hereditary supernatural family rather than an independent species. Its original members were Humans possessing the extraordinary ability to utilize Space- and Time-related powers through their Bloodline. The family is commonly associated with Witches because Witch [[Magic]] forms part of its supernatural ancestry, but the Alioth lineage possesses several characteristics that distinguish it from ordinary practitioners of Magic.[2]
The family is unusual even within the broader classification of Witches. Whereas ordinary Witches can develop different magical specialties according to their abilities, knowledge, and personal circumstances, Alioth practitioners are restricted to a particularly narrow supernatural domain. Their Magic is inherently associated with Space and Time, making it impossible for members of the Bloodline to learn unrelated forms of conventional Witch Magic through the same hereditary system.[2]
This restriction should not be interpreted as evidence that Clan Alioth possesses weak or incomplete Magic. The limitation is accompanied by an extraordinary concentration of potential within two of the most important Concepts governing physical existence. As the user's understanding develops, the hereditary ability can evolve beyond conventional spellcasting into a form of supernatural authority approaching direct manipulation of Space-Time itself.[2][3]
The distinction was explained by [[Otsuki Yoichi]] to Haruna when discussing the unusual powers of Alexios. Yoichi recognized that although the family possessed Witch-related characteristics, describing its developed abilities simply as Magic was increasingly inaccurate. Alexios' power had progressed until it resembled an innate supernatural ability rather than a conventional collection of learned spells.[2]

#### Position in Nightingale
Clan Alioth occupies a unique position in [[Nightingale]]'s history. The family served Vlad for thousands of years and was already present during the Kingdom's establishment. Its members were so closely connected to the founding of Nightingale that their arrival was associated with the earliest families to settle within the Vampire world.[1]
Although the family was Human rather than Vampiric, its historical position was comparable in importance to that of the principal Noble Vampire families. The Vampire Count Clans were responsible for major political, military, and administrative functions, while Clan Alioth provided specialized services that even powerful Vampires could not easily reproduce.[5]
Its influence was based upon direct service to the King. Clan Alioth was not an ordinary subordinate organization that could be commanded freely by the [[Vampire Counts]] or the royal children. Its principal allegiance belonged to Vlad himself, and the family's privileged relationship with the throne provided unusual political protection and access.[5]
The distinction became especially important when rival Noble Vampires discussed the extraordinary isolation of Nightingale. They recognized that the family possessed enough power to separate an entire world and manipulate Time without the broader population immediately understanding what had happened. Although the political importance of the Five Vampire Counts was considerable, the King's relationship with Clan Alioth was even more direct.[5]

#### Leadership and Succession
Clan Alioth possesses a hereditary leadership system associated with the transmission of its extraordinary Eyes. Alexios is the current known Clan Leader, while Natalia is his biological daughter and the most prominent member of the next known generation.[6][8]
The importance of succession extends beyond the preservation of a surname. Alexios explained that when the successor of Clan Alioth inherits the family's Eyes, a special ritual can be performed to alter the passage of Time affecting the successor's body. The ritual provides a substantial extension of lifespan, allowing the bearer to continue developing the family's supernatural powers beyond the ordinary limits of Human physiology.[8]
The exact requirements determining which descendant inherits the Eyes, whether multiple individuals can simultaneously possess their complete capabilities, and the procedures used to select the next Clan Leader have not been fully established. Natalia possesses the hereditary Eyes and eventually surpassed Alexios in certain applications, but the novel has not explicitly confirmed that she formally replaced him as the administrative head of Clan Alioth.

### History

#### Origins
The original founder of Clan Alioth has not been identified by name. The earliest known historical information concerns an individual referred to as the first Alioth, whose extraordinary supernatural characteristics attracted the interest of powerful beings during the ancient development of the Human supernatural world.[3]
The appearance of the Alioth Eyes was initially regarded as an unusual mutation that occurred after the first bearer came into contact with [[Magic]]. This explanation eventually became part of the family's accepted history, providing a seemingly straightforward reason why Humans belonging to the lineage possessed abilities so different from those of ordinary supernatural practitioners.[3]
However, the apparent explanation concealed a much greater mystery. During his investigation of Natalia's evolving powers, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] concluded that the original mutation theory did not adequately explain the hereditary Eyes. Their potential was too extraordinary, and their ability to interact with fundamental aspects of Reality suggested a more unusual origin than an ordinary Human acquiring Witch Magic.[3]
The true source of the Eyes therefore remains unknown. Neither the first bearer's original circumstances nor the supernatural process that introduced such power into a Human lineage has been conclusively established.

#### The First Alioth and Albedo Moriarty
The first known member of the family attracted the attention of Albedo Moriarty, one of the most knowledgeable and powerful Witches associated with the supernatural world. Albedo recognized the unusual nature of the Alioth ability and attempted to acquire its bearer for study.[3]
The intervention of [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] prevented the attempt from succeeding. Vlad protected the first Alioth from Albedo, establishing the earliest specifically described relationship between the Vampire King and the unusual Human Bloodline.[3]
The event is particularly significant because it demonstrates that the family's protection by Vlad predates the political circumstances involving Natalia and Alexios. The relationship between Clan Alioth and the Vampire King was not simply a contractual arrangement developed after the establishment of modern [[Nightingale]] society; its origins were connected to the survival and protection of the family's earliest known bearer.[3]
Albedo's interest also establishes that the Alioth Eyes were considered exceptional by supernatural scholars long before Natalia developed them into genuine Divine authority. The most powerful Witches possessed extensive knowledge of [[Magic]], yet the family's abilities remained sufficiently unusual that Albedo wished to examine their origin directly.
The first Alioth's name, biological sex, complete abilities, and subsequent life remain unknown. The circumstances establish Vlad's historical intervention without explaining precisely how the original bearer obtained the Eyes.

#### Establishment in Nightingale
Clan Alioth became associated with [[Nightingale]] from the Kingdom's earliest period. The family was present from its founding and maintained a direct relationship with Vlad over thousands of years, establishing itself among the oldest organizations within the Vampire King's sphere of influence.[1]
The family provided services that were essential to the developing kingdom's security and stability. Its ability to manipulate Space allowed the royal government to establish transportation routes, control access to important locations, and isolate territories that required additional protection. Its temporal abilities offered possibilities unavailable to ordinary Vampires, including changes to the passage of Time and the creation of environments operating under different spatial or temporal conditions.[4][2]
The exact sequence through which the early members developed these techniques has not been recorded. However, the continued survival and advancement of the family over thousands of years demonstrates that its supernatural inheritance became an established component of Nightingale's political and magical infrastructure.

#### The Royal Family's Trusted Specialists
The family's service to Vlad developed into a particularly privileged relationship. Alexios eventually became the King's principal adviser and one of his most trusted subordinates, retaining access to information and responsibilities that were not ordinarily shared with the Noble Clans.[6][5]
The political influence of the family was especially apparent when other Vampires discussed its position within [[Nightingale]]. The Five Counts possessed enormous authority because they served as the Kingdom's principal Pillars, yet Clan Alioth enjoyed a level of personal access to Vlad that exceeded even that of some members of the royal family.[5]
The family consequently functioned as a specialized royal institution rather than an ordinary Noble Vampire Clan. Its members were entrusted with matters concerning the security and dimensional structure of the Kingdom, while Alexios' continued presence beside Vlad reflected the King's reliance upon his extraordinary abilities.

### Hereditary Bloodline

#### The Alioth Eyes
The Alioth Eyes are the supernatural foundation of Clan Alioth. These inherited Eyes allow their bearers to perceive and interact with Space and Time through a mechanism that differs from ordinary visual perception or conventional [[Magic]].[2][3]
When activated, the Eyes possess an extraordinary appearance. Alexios' normally closed eyes reveal a structure resembling a galaxy filled with stars, rather than conventional sclera and pupils. Natalia's developed Eyes possess a similarly cosmic appearance, with the visual characteristics becoming increasingly pronounced as her authority over Space-Time develops.[8][9]
The ocular ability is more than a sensory enhancement. It allows the user to perceive and manipulate the supernatural structure of Space, establish connections between separated locations, and eventually interact with Time through a level of precision that ordinary practitioners cannot easily reproduce.[3]
The extent of the Eyes' power depends upon their bearer. Alexios developed extraordinary Spatial and temporal techniques through centuries of practice, while Natalia eventually surpassed the Human limits of the Bloodline after acquiring the superior physical and spiritual foundation of a True Dragon.[4][3]
The complete potential of the Eyes remains unknown. Even Natalia had not fully mastered their abilities after approximately two thousand subjective years of Draconic development, demonstrating that their supernatural foundation extended beyond the techniques already established within the family's recorded history.[3]

#### Restriction to Space and Time
Clan Alioth possesses an unusual restriction upon the type of [[Magic]] its members can learn. Although their Bloodline contains Witch-related supernatural characteristics, its development is dedicated exclusively to abilities associated with Space and Time.[2]
According to Yoichi's explanation, members of the family cannot learn unrelated forms of Witch Magic through their hereditary system. This makes Clan Alioth an irregularity even among Witches, who can ordinarily develop different magical specialties according to their aptitudes and training.[2]
The limitation is accompanied by extraordinary specialization. Alioth practitioners possess an unusually direct relationship with the two Concepts, allowing them to develop techniques that would be extremely difficult for ordinary supernatural beings to reproduce without comparable hereditary advantages.
The restriction applies to the original Alioth magical foundation. It should not be interpreted as proof that a descendant who later acquires independent racial powers, Divine Blessings, or another supernatural system is incapable of using abilities provided through those separate sources. Natalia's development as a True Blood Dragon demonstrates that the Bloodline can coexist with additional supernatural characteristics without losing its original specialization.[10][3]

#### Evolution Beyond Conventional Magic
Clan Alioth's powers become increasingly unusual as the bearer develops. The family's earliest abilities were commonly treated as a specialized branch of Witch [[Magic]], but the supernatural properties of the Eyes eventually evolved toward something more closely resembling an innate authority.[2]
Yoichi recognized this distinction while explaining Alexios' abilities. Although the family possessed Witch-related characteristics, its developed powers no longer operated like ordinary spells. The Eyes provided a specialized supernatural foundation that could manipulate Space and Time directly.[2]
Natalia later demonstrated the greater potential of this development. After becoming a Blood Dragon Goddess, her powers evolved into genuine Divinities of Space and Time, allowing her to perform operations whose complexity extended far beyond the conventional techniques historically employed by Human Alioth practitioners.[3]

### Powers and Abilities
Clan Alioth's hereditary powers are primarily associated with Space Manipulation, Time Manipulation, dimensional engineering, spatial Runecraft, and the extension of Human lifespan through temporal techniques. The family's individual members possess different levels of mastery, and the feats performed by Alexios or Natalia should not automatically be attributed to every descendant.[2][8][3]

#### Space Manipulation
Space Manipulation is the most consistently demonstrated specialty of Clan Alioth. Its practitioners can connect locations, manipulate spatial distances, create isolated environments, expand enclosed spaces, and establish restrictions upon dimensional access.[4][2][8]
- Space Manipulation: Alioth practitioners can manipulate Space through their hereditary supernatural Eyes and associated techniques.[2]
- Portal Creation: Members of the family can establish gateways connecting separate points in Space.[4]
- Long-Distance Transportation: Alioth portals can transport individuals across enormous geographical distances without requiring conventional travel.[4]
- Interworld Transportation: The family's abilities permit movement between separate supernatural territories and worlds when the destination is accessible and the necessary spatial information is available.[11]
- Dimensional Isolation: Alexios can isolate an environment from ordinary spatial access, preventing entry by unauthorized individuals.[2]
- Spatial Sealing: The family possesses techniques capable of restricting or closing routes between supernatural realms.[4]
- Space Expansion: Alexios can expand the interior of a confined environment far beyond the dimensions of its physical exterior.[8]
- Spatial Runecraft: Alexios can incorporate Space-related effects into Runes, allowing artificially expanded environments to be maintained through an external Energy source.[8]
- Spatial Surveillance: Natalia developed the ability to use extremely small portals for remote observation and information gathering.[12]

#### Time Manipulation
Time Manipulation is the second principal specialty of Clan Alioth. Its techniques can alter the passage of Time within an individual or environment, allowing the user to accelerate or decelerate development and establish differences between internal and external temporal conditions.[4][8]
The ability is exceptionally dangerous to Human practitioners. Alexios explained that utilizing advanced Time techniques consumes the user's own lifespan, making temporal manipulation fundamentally more costly than ordinary Spatial [[Magic]].[8]
- Time Manipulation: Alioth practitioners possess the hereditary potential to alter Time through their specialized Eyes.[2][8]
- Time Dilation: The family's abilities can alter the relationship between the passage of Time inside an affected environment and the surrounding world.[4]
- Time Acceleration: Advanced users can accelerate the passage of Time for a selected target or environment.[13]
- Time Deceleration: The family's hereditary longevity ritual can slow the personal Time of the successor's body.[8]
- Localized Temporal Isolation: Advanced users can establish environments where Time progresses differently from the outside world.[4]
- Temporal Longevity: Members who inherit the Eyes may undergo a ritual designed to extend their lives through the manipulation of personal Time.[8]
- Temporal-State Manipulation: Natalia eventually developed the ability to retrieve a historical condition from an individual's personal timeline and establish it within the present.[9]

#### Spatial Coordinates and Restrictions
The family's portals are not unrestricted gateways to any arbitrarily imagined location. Alioth practitioners ordinarily require reliable spatial information, including sufficient knowledge of the intended destination or its coordinates.[11]
This limitation means that an unknown environment cannot necessarily be reached simply because the user possesses enough Energy to open a portal. The practitioner must also understand where the passage should lead.
The family's Space-related abilities can also be opposed by sufficiently powerful dimensional restrictions. During the Demonic invasion, the unusual security surrounding the Arcane Realm was considered beyond the ordinary ability of Clan Alioth to penetrate through its established portal techniques.[14]
These restrictions distinguish the family's extraordinary specialization from absolute authority over all Space. Even Alexios' most advanced Human techniques remained subject to the user's knowledge, available Energy, and the supernatural conditions surrounding the target environment.

### Lifespan and Succession Ritual

#### Natural Longevity
Members of Clan Alioth possess unusually high vitality compared with ordinary Humans. Alexios attributed this characteristic to the family's hereditary powers, explaining that the Alioth Bloodline allows its members to survive considerably longer than conventional Human physiology would ordinarily permit.[8]
However, even this enhanced vitality possesses limits. Alexios explained that members of the family ordinarily cannot exceed a lifespan of approximately 500 years through their natural vitality alone.[8]
The statement establishes a hereditary advantage rather than unconditional immortality. Alioth practitioners remain subject to physical deterioration, supernatural injury, and the consequences of excessive use of their powers unless they acquire additional methods of preservation.

#### The Longevity Ritual
When the successor of Clan Alioth inherits the hereditary Eyes, the family can perform a special ritual intended to extend the successor's lifespan. The procedure works by slowing the passage of Time affecting the recipient's physical body.[8]
The resulting temporal deceleration can provide approximately 500 additional years of life, potentially allowing the successor to survive for around a millennium under favorable circumstances. The extension is dependent upon the user's condition and the subsequent use of Time-related techniques.[8]
The ritual does not provide an unlimited reserve of life. Advanced manipulation of Time consumes the practitioner's own lifespan, meaning that extensive temporal operations can exhaust the additional years gained through the ritual.

#### Lifespan Expenditure
Alexios explained that the fundamental cost of his temporal powers was his own life. While Spatial [[Magic]] could be sustained through conventional supernatural Energy, manipulating Time at an equivalent level imposed an additional burden upon the finite duration of his Human existence.[8]
The separation of [[Nightingale]] from the outside world was one of the examples associated with this cost. Alexios acknowledged that his use of temporal manipulation during that incident had consumed a portion of the time that otherwise would have remained available to him.[8]
This limitation also explains why extraordinary temporal techniques were not routinely used for every problem involving Nightingale's development. The family possessed powers capable of accelerating training and manipulating entire environments, but the personal cost to the practitioner could become unacceptable even when the necessary technical knowledge was available.

#### Alexios' Temporal Deterioration
Alexios eventually exceeded the limitations imposed upon ordinary Human Alioth practitioners. Through the repeated use of Time manipulation, he extended his existence far beyond the period during which his body should naturally have survived.[3]
The method eventually produced an abnormal condition in which his physical existence became increasingly associated with Time Energy rather than ordinary Human biological functions. Although he continued living, the deterioration threatened his ability to maintain an independent Human identity.[3][9]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Natalia later intervened to preserve Alexios. Natalia used her advanced temporal authority to retrieve a healthier state from her father's personal history, while Victor supplied the additional supernatural support necessary to stabilize the result.[9]
Alexios subsequently received a more advanced Human foundation through Victor's intervention, becoming a High Human rather than continuing toward the complete loss of his ordinary Human existence.[15]
His development represents an exceptional individual case rather than a normal hereditary stage reached automatically by all members of Clan Alioth.

### Spatial Engineering

#### Expanded Spaces
Clan Alioth's powers can be used for construction and infrastructure rather than only transportation. Alexios demonstrated the ability to manipulate the interior dimensions of enclosed environments, allowing a comparatively small physical container to hold an area far larger than its exterior would ordinarily permit.[8]
During discussions concerning the development of a new city, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] recognized that this capability possessed enormous economic and logistical potential. Rather than treating the ability merely as a convenient supernatural trick, he proposed using expanded spaces in residential buildings, luggage, and other structures to create new forms of storage and habitation.[8]
Alexios explained that, given sufficient Energy, he could create an expanded space approximately one kilometer in scale without extraordinary difficulty. With enough high-level Mana crystals, he considered it possible to maintain a country-sized interior within a house.[8]
The larger example was presented as a conditional technical possibility rather than a completed construction. Its importance lies in demonstrating the extraordinary relationship between the Clan's Spatial [[Magic]] and the development of supernatural infrastructure.

#### Runic Maintenance
Alexios could encode spatial expansion into Runes, allowing the altered interior to remain functional without requiring his continuous personal presence. However, the resulting structure needed a reliable source of Energy to maintain its supernatural properties.[8]
Mana crystals provided the practical solution. These crystals contained condensed Mana and could serve as batteries sustaining the spatial effects created by Alexios' Runes. The technique was consequently suitable for commercial applications, although the extraordinary cost of Mana crystals imposed significant economic limitations.[8]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] recognized that such systems could be rented or sold as services rather than provided indiscriminately. He proposed developing an economic model in which the users of expanded spaces would pay for the cost of the crystals and continued maintenance, creating a potential source of revenue for the kingdom or the organizations controlling the technology.[8]
Alexios also recognized the possibility of teaching Natalia how to construct the relevant spatial Runes, increasing the family's capacity to produce dimensional storage devices and other specialized equipment. The discussion demonstrated that Clan Alioth's abilities were not inherently restricted to one individual's presence, provided the appropriate knowledge and Energy infrastructure could be established.[8]

#### Dimensional Storage
The expanded-space techniques provided the foundation for containers whose interiors greatly exceeded their ordinary physical dimensions. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] proposed producing large suitcases and smaller bags with internal areas measured in hundreds or thousands of square meters, allowing substantial quantities of equipment, supplies, and personnel to be transported through otherwise manageable physical objects.[8]
Such devices possessed obvious military value. An organization could transport extensive supplies or conceal equipment within containers requiring comparatively little ordinary storage space. The same principles could be applied to civilian transportation, housing, and commercial development.
Natalia later participated in a more advanced application of dimensional storage when she and Victor provided the Imperial Princesses with individual pocket-dimension inventories. This development reflects the continuity between the practical Spatial engineering originally proposed through Alexios' expertise and the far more advanced dimensional systems developed within the [[Elderblood Empire]].[16]

### Known Applications

#### The Isolation of Nightingale
One of the most extraordinary historical demonstrations of Clan Alioth's power occurred when Vlad ordered [[Nightingale]] isolated from the outside world. Alexios employed his Spatial and temporal abilities to restrict entry and exit, separating the realm from the locations ordinarily connected to it.[4]
The operation also altered the passage of Time between Nightingale and the outside world. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Ruby experienced approximately one year and six months while most of their family remained separated from them, creating an enormous difference between the subjective experiences of individuals on opposite sides of the restriction.[4]
The scale of the technique shocked those who eventually learned of it. Other powerful supernatural beings recognized that separating an entire world and manipulating its temporal relationship to the outside environment represented an achievement approaching the work ordinarily associated with powerful Gods.[5]
The isolation was nevertheless extraordinarily expensive. Alexios later acknowledged that the temporal portion of the operation had consumed his own lifespan, demonstrating that the technique's consequences extended beyond the inconvenience and political problems experienced by those trapped outside Nightingale.[8]

#### Scathach's Twenty-Year Exile
Approximately one thousand years before the main events of the story, Vlad employed Clan Alioth's powers to isolate [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] within another environment for approximately twenty years.[17]
The decision was motivated by Vlad's desire to keep [[Scathach]] from interfering with plans that her unpredictable and confrontational personality might have disrupted. Rather than attempt to control the ancient Countess through conventional political orders, Vlad relied upon the family's ability to manipulate dimensional access and isolate individuals from events occurring elsewhere.[17]
The incident had lasting consequences for Scathach's relationship with the Vampire King. She remained angry over the forced separation long after the period of confinement had ended, demonstrating the personal consequences of using Clan Alioth's abilities for political control.

#### The Sanctuary of Otsuki Hana
Alexios also participated in creating a protected sanctuary associated with [[Otsuki Hana]], one of Vlad's most important former wives.[2]
The sanctuary existed within an isolated region of Earth and contained a peaceful natural environment dedicated to Hana's memory. Vlad and Alexios collaborated to establish its supernatural structure, while Alexios' powers separated the location from ordinary access.[2]
The protected region was sufficiently isolated that individuals lacking Alexios' authorization could not freely enter, even if they possessed the power of Gods. [[Otsuki Yoichi]] later explained these characteristics while showing the sanctuary to Haruna, who had been unaware of the complete history surrounding her family.[2]
The sanctuary illustrates the family's ability to perform a function distinct from transportation or imprisonment. Alexios could establish a protected environment intended to remain separate from ordinary interference, preserving a location whose emotional and political significance made unrestricted access unacceptable.
The site should not be confused with a completely independent Universe possessing its own universal Laws. Its demonstrated nature was that of an extraordinarily well-isolated spatial environment whose protection depended upon the techniques of Clan Alioth.[2]

#### The Hell Communication Portal
During the Demonic War, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s separation from his family created another problem requiring Clan Alioth's specialized abilities. After Victor became the new King of Hell, the supernatural authority of the realm prevented ordinary portals from extracting him or maintaining a stable connection.[18]
Natalia brought Alexios to the gathering of Victor's wives, and the family attempted to establish a connection to the deepest regions of Hell. The operation required substantial supernatural Energy because the target was not an ordinary traveler but the ruling King of a realm whose own structure protected him.[18]
Jeanne provided additional Energy to sustain the passage, allowing the group to communicate with Victor. Although the portal did not provide a simple means of removing him from Hell, it demonstrated that Clan Alioth's powers could serve as the foundation for communication across exceptionally difficult dimensional boundaries.[18]
The operation also illustrates an important distinction between opening a passage and successfully transporting its intended target. Alexios could establish a connection under extraordinary conditions, but extracting the King of Hell required overcoming additional supernatural resistance that the available Energy could not safely support.

### Relationship with Other Organizations

#### Tepes Clan and the Vampire King
Clan Alioth's oldest and most important institutional relationship is with [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] and the [[Tepes Clan]]. Vlad's protection of the first Alioth established the earliest known connection between the family and the Vampire King, while centuries of subsequent service transformed that relationship into one of the foundations of [[Nightingale]]'s royal administration.[3][1]
Alexios eventually became one of Vlad's most trusted advisers, assisting with matters requiring advanced Spatial and temporal intervention. His abilities provided solutions to problems that conventional military strength or political authority could not easily resolve, including the isolation of dangerous individuals and the control of dimensional access.[17][4]
The relationship is notable because Vlad did not simply employ the family as replaceable servants. His personal connection to Alexios remained important even after the rise of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and the transformation of the political structure surrounding Nightingale. Victor and Natalia's restoration of Alexios consequently became an emotionally significant event for the Vampire King, who recognized the importance of preserving the life of his old companion.[9][15]

#### The Vampire Count Clans
Clan Alioth's position differs from that of [[Nightingale]]'s principal Vampire Count families, including the Snow, Fulger, Scarlett, Adrasteia, and later Elderblood Clans. Those organizations obtained their political positions through the Count system, while Clan Alioth acquired influence through exclusive royal service and the extraordinary usefulness of its hereditary powers.[5]
The family nevertheless interacted with the Count Clans through its official duties. Natalia served in the Snow household, Alexios participated in royal proceedings involving the Counts, and the family's transportation abilities became important to the movement of individuals whose political responsibilities extended across Nightingale and the Human world.
The family's power also represented a limitation upon the independence of individual Counts. Vlad could use Alioth Spatial [[Magic]] to prevent powerful Vampires from reaching particular destinations or interfering with plans whose success required their absence. [[Scathach]]'s exile and the later isolation of Nightingale both demonstrate the political implications of this relationship.[17][4]

#### The Witch Community
Clan Alioth possesses an unusual relationship with the broader Witch community because its hereditary powers originated from or were historically associated with Witch [[Magic]], yet remained restricted to Space and Time.[2]
Its abilities were recognized as highly unusual by knowledgeable supernatural practitioners. Ordinary Witches possessed considerably broader magical possibilities, but they could not necessarily reproduce the techniques of Clan Alioth simply by examining their effects.[8]
Alexios explicitly considered the family's spatial expansion techniques impossible for unrelated Witches to replicate through ordinary study. Their operation depended upon the Alioth Bloodline rather than a universally accessible magical formula.[8]
The relationship with Albedo further demonstrates the family's exceptional position. One of the most powerful Witches had attempted to study the first Alioth, but Vlad's intervention prevented her from acquiring the original bearer. The incident established the Bloodline's importance to supernatural researchers while also contributing to its protection under the Vampire King.[3]

#### Victor's Faction
Clan Alioth became strategically important to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] when he began organizing an independent faction capable of operating beyond [[Nightingale]]'s traditional political structure. Victor recognized that the family's Spatial abilities would provide immense advantages in transportation, logistics, and the development of a new settlement.[19]
However, obtaining the Clan's formal allegiance was considerably more complicated than gaining Natalia's personal assistance. Clan Alioth had served Vlad for thousands of years, and Alexios' position as the King's most trusted adviser made a direct political defection unlikely.[19]
Natalia recognized the conflict between her loyalty to her father and her developing relationship with Victor. Rather than demand that Alexios abandon Vlad, she chose to provide Victor with her own assistance, distinguishing her personal decisions from the official political allegiance of Clan Alioth.[19]
This distinction remained important throughout the family's later development. Natalia's membership in Victor's household and eventual transformation into an Elderblood did not automatically establish that every member of Clan Alioth had transferred allegiance from Vlad to Victor.

#### Elderblood Empire
Clan Alioth's connection to the [[Elderblood Empire]] is primarily represented through Natalia and the continuation of her hereditary Bloodline among [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s descendants.
Natalia's powers eventually developed beyond the limitations of ordinary Human Alioth practitioners. After acquiring a True Dragon body, she obtained sufficient Energy capacity and spiritual compatibility to perform temporal operations on a scale impossible for Alexios during his original Human life.[3]
Her achievements became essential to several of the Empire's most important projects, including the creation of advanced Space-Time Runes, the acceleration of planetary geological development, and the regulation of the Galactic Time-acceleration ritual.[20][21]
The family also contributed indirectly to the Empire's infrastructure through the continuation of its dimensional engineering tradition. The advanced pocket-dimensional inventories created by Natalia and Victor represent an evolution of the practical spatial storage applications Alexios had previously discussed during the development of Victor's earlier settlements.[8][16]

### Known Members

#### Alexios Alioth
[[Alexios Alioth]] is the current known leader of Clan Alioth, Natalia's father, and the longtime adviser of [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]]. He is one of the oldest and most accomplished Human Space-Time practitioners associated with [[Nightingale]], having served the Vampire King across an extraordinary period of supernatural history.[6][1]
Alexios possesses the distinctive hereditary Eyes and can manipulate Space and Time through techniques whose scale approaches or exceeds what many ordinary supernatural practitioners consider possible for a Human. His accomplishments include the isolation of Nightingale, the creation of protected dimensional environments, large-scale spatial restrictions, and the development of spatial expansion Runes capable of maintaining enormous interiors within comparatively small containers.[4][2][8]
His temporal abilities were accompanied by extraordinary costs. The prolonged expenditure of his lifespan eventually caused severe deterioration of his physical existence, leaving him increasingly dependent upon Time Energy. Natalia and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later restored him through advanced temporal and supernatural intervention, while Victor provided the superior physical foundation of a High Human.[9][15]
Alexios' personal history represents both the extraordinary potential and the original limitations of the Alioth Bloodline. He possessed abilities capable of influencing entire worlds, yet remained vulnerable to the biological costs of utilizing those powers through a Human body.

#### Nataliana Alioth
Nataliana "Natalia" Elderblood Alioth is Alexios' daughter, a former Human Witch, and the most powerful known descendant of Clan Alioth. She originally served as Violet Snow's personal maid and subsequently became one of [[Victor Elderblood]]'s wives.[4][3]
Natalia inherited the family's extraordinary Spatial talent and developed applications beyond ordinary transportation, including microscopic portals, concealed surveillance, and the circumvention of spatial restrictions established by other Alioth practitioners.[4][12]
After receiving Divine Blessings and becoming a True Blood Dragon, Natalia's supernatural development accelerated dramatically. Her Eyes evolved to support genuine Divine authority over Space and Time, allowing her to perform advanced temporal techniques without suffering the same Human lifespan restrictions that had endangered Alexios.[10][3]
Natalia later created the technique Time Cocoon, which restored Alexios through the recovery of a healthy state from his personal timeline. She also developed an advanced fused Space-Time Rune and became a principal specialist responsible for the temporal component of the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s enormous development ritual.[9][20][22]
Her transformation did not erase her original Alioth heritage. Instead, it allowed the Bloodline to develop beyond its former Human limitations, revealing extraordinary capabilities that its earlier bearers had been unable to fully exploit.

#### The First Alioth
The first Alioth is the earliest known bearer of the family's hereditary powers. Their real name, appearance, lifespan, and complete history remain unknown.[3]
The original bearer developed or acquired the unusual Eyes under circumstances that were later attributed to a mutation associated with [[Magic]]. The extraordinary ability attracted Albedo Moriarty's interest, prompting an attempt to obtain the individual for supernatural research.[3]
Vlad intervened and protected the first Alioth, establishing the earliest recorded connection between the Bloodline and the Vampire King. The founder's survival subsequently allowed the family to continue developing, although the precise relationship between the first bearer and later generations has not been fully documented.[3]

#### Norabell Elderblood
[[Norabell Elderblood]] is Natalia's biological daughter with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and a direct descendant of the Alioth Bloodline through her mother.[7]
Norabell inherited an extraordinarily powerful relationship with Space-Time, supplemented by the unusual Draconic and Eldritch-influenced characteristics associated with Victor. Her development demonstrates that the hereditary Eyes and related supernatural potential can continue through descendants whose racial foundation differs from the original Human lineage.[7]
Although Norabell possesses Alioth ancestry, she belongs to the [[Elderblood Imperial Family]] and has not been explicitly identified as a formal member of Clan Alioth's historical administrative organization. She is therefore best classified as a known descendant rather than automatically listed as its successor or Clan Leader.

### Significance
Clan Alioth is one of the most influential Human supernatural families in the history of [[Nightingale]]. Its political importance originated from its extraordinary relationship with Vlad and its ability to perform tasks that conventional Noble Vampire strength could not accomplish. Through Space and Time manipulation, the family contributed to royal security, dimensional transportation, the confinement of powerful individuals, and the isolation of entire environments.[5][1]
Its importance also extends beyond military or political operations. Alexios demonstrated that the Bloodline could provide the foundation for entirely new forms of infrastructure, including buildings with expanded interiors, portable dimensional storage, and Rune-maintained environments. These techniques possess substantial economic and logistical value, allowing the supernatural world to overcome ordinary physical restrictions upon transportation and construction.[8]
The Bloodline's greatest significance, however, lies in its unexplained potential. Its original supernatural foundation remains unknown, and even advanced beings have failed to determine how a Human family acquired Eyes capable of developing toward genuine Divine authority over Space-Time.[3]
Natalia's transformation into a Dragon Goddess revealed that the family's limitations had been closely connected to the fragility of its original Human physiology. Once those restrictions were removed, the inherited Eyes could support far greater abilities, including sophisticated temporal restoration, high-level Conceptual Runecraft, and astronomical-scale temporal operations.[3][9][20]
Clan Alioth consequently represents an unusual intersection between Human heredity, Witch-related supernatural characteristics, the Divine Concepts of Space and Time, and the extraordinary racial evolution made possible through the Elderblood lineage.
