**Psiloi** (singular *Psilos*) are the long-range fire support troops of the [[Undying Olympian Empire]]. They are the Empire's snipers, missile crews, drone operators and forward artillery, and their job is to kill the enemy before the enemy ever sees an Imperial soldier. Psiloi fight at distances measured in tens or hundreds of kilometers, guided by targeting data from the [[Sciritae]] and the orbital [[Grids]].

Psiloi are lightly armored and rarely see the enemy's face. They are among the most lethal soldiers in the Empire.

> *"The Legion wins the battle. The Psiloi decide how many enemies are left to fight it."*
> Fire control doctrine, [[Legio XX Valeria Vitrix]]

### Name

The *psiloi* ("bare ones") were the unarmored light troops of the Greek world: archers, slingers and javelin men who fought outside the phalanx and harassed the enemy at range. They wore little or no armor so they could move and shoot freely. The Imperial Psiloi keep the light armor and the missile weapons, now carried across kilometers.

---

### Role and Responsibilities

- **Precision sniping** of officers, casters, demigods and key equipment
- **Missile fires** against armor, air defense, fortifications and [[Titan (Mech)|Titans]]
- **Drone swarm operations** for strike, reconnaissance and saturation attacks
- **Counter-battery fire**, locating and destroying enemy artillery and missile launchers
- **Air defense** for Legion formations on the move
- **Anti-divine precision strikes**, killing divine targets with rounds engineered to defeat regeneration before they can close to melee range

### Divine Lineage

Psiloi are recruited for eyesight, patience and aim. The corps is dominated by Apollonian and hunter lineages.

Children of Artemis herself are rare in the Empire's forces because of her vows. The Psiloi recruit heavily from legacies of her Hunters' mortal relations and from lines blessed by her in earlier eras.

### Organization

The standard unit is the **volley** of twelve, organized into:

- **Two Bow Teams** of two operators each, a marksman and a spotter
- **Sling Battery** of four, crewing a portable missile or mortar system
- **Swarm Wing** of two drone operators
- **Fire Controller**, who ties the volley into Legion fire control and AtRep targeting
- **Volley Leader**

Several volleys form a **quiver**, the fire support element of a Legion cohort.

---

### Abilities

- **Sun-Sight.** Apollo legacies can see targets through haze, smoke and darkness by the light they emit, including heat and divine radiance.
- **True Flight.** Marksmen can nudge a projectile's path in flight. Veterans can bend a round around cover.
- **Foresight Shot.** Some Apollonian marksmen fire at where the target will be, trusting prophetic instinct over the ballistic computer.
- **Lightning Guidance.** Zeus legacies can steer munitions along ionized channels and drive them through jamming.
- **Grievance Lock.** Nemesis legacies can mark a target that has harmed the volley. Munitions fired at a marked target are drawn toward it.

### Doctrine and Tactics

#### Shoot and Scoot

Every Psiloi fire mission ends with displacement. Bow teams fire once or twice, then relocate. Sling Batteries fire their salvo and move before counter-battery fire can arrive. Staying in one place is the most common cause of Psiloi casualties.

#### Kill Chain Integration

Psiloi rarely find their own targets. [[Sciritae]] lances and orbital sensors feed targets into AtRep, which assigns them to volleys by priority. A demigod spotted by a Sciritae screen can be under Psiloi fire within twenty seconds.

#### Divine Target Priority

Against divine opponents, Psiloi follow a strict order: first the enemy's casters and priests, then its demigods, then its heavy armor, then everything else. Divine targets are engaged with anti-regeneration munitions from maximum range and never allowed to close.

#### Saturation

When precision fails, the Psiloi switch to volume. Swarm Wings launch hundreds of drones, Sling Batteries ripple-fire their whole load, and the target area is buried.

### Equipment

#### Armor

- Light composite plating over the torso only
- **Hawkes Precision** HPI-OC-12 ghillie cloak with multi-spectral camouflage
- Stabilizing exo-harness for marksmen, locking the body still for long shots

#### Sniper Systems

- **Hawkes Precision HPI-LR-50 "Longsight"** anti-materiel rifle, firing divine-material penetrators accurate to 6 km
- **[[Sonnenbruch Tachyonkanone]]**, a [[Tochter des Elysium R&D]] man-portable tachyon rifle for engaging armored and shielded divine targets
- Marksman variants of the [[VA-RF-762 “MORROW-IX”]] with [[VAS-762R APDS “QVARTUS”]]
- Hawkes Precision fire-control scopes with ballistic AI, wind mapping and target prediction

#### Missiles

- [[Serpentine Baylock]] [[HS Missiles]] (hypersonic) for time-critical targets
- Serpentine Baylock [[DF Missiles]] for dual-function air and ground attack
- Serpentine Baylock [[Swarm Missile]] pods, launching dozens of micro-missiles per salvo
- Man-portable variants of Arisawa's [[ARS-MX-41 “TIDEHUNTER”]] ATGM

#### Heavy Fires

- **[[Durham Aerospace]] DA-PL-4 "Hearthspear"**, a crew-served protonic lance scaled down from naval [[Broadside Weapons]]
- Guided mortar systems with divine-material warheads

#### Drones

- Loitering munitions for strike and overwatch
- Micro-drone swarms for reconnaissance and saturation
- Decoy drones that mimic Psiloi firing signatures

#### Electronic Warfare

- [[IA-ECCM-83 “NEMESIS VECTOR”]] home-on-jam guidance, which turns enemy jammers into targets
- [[IA-ECCM-97 “PREDATOR LENS”]] passive targeting for firing without emitting
- [[IA-ECCM-49 “TYPHON SCOPE”]] doppler filtering to see through chaff

### Countermeasures

| Threat | Psiloi Countermeasure |
|---|---|
| Counter-battery fire | Shoot-and-scoot doctrine, decoy firing signatures |
| Enemy jamming | Home-on-jam guidance, lightning-guided munitions, passive targeting |
| Regenerating divine targets | [[Divine-Metallurgical Binding Impurities (DMBI)|DMBI]] anti-reconstitution munitions |
| Enemy snipers | Thermal-masking cloaks, acoustic shot locators |
| Shields and barriers | Tachyon rifles that bypass conventional [[Energy Shielding]], protonic lances |

#### Known Vulnerabilities

- Psiloi are lightly armored and die quickly when found.
- They depend on Sciritae and orbital targeting. Cutting the kill chain blinds them.
- Enemies with strong teleportation or short-range speed can close the distance before the Psiloi can displace.
