[[Orran Arenberg]] and [[Virre Arenberg]], are rogue descendants of the [[House of Arenberg]] lineage, half-siblings to [[Percy Jackson]].

They are the only known criminals to occupy double-digit rankings in the [[UOE Unified Alltime Rankings]] currently, Virre and Orran holding ranks 74 and 75 respectively.

They hold a twin bounty of 280,000 drachmae, surpassing the cost of some planetary systems.

They have the dreaded power of a Power leech, which allows the twins to absorb the experience and strength of a dead being. They speak in tandem. Or not at all.
## Motivations and Methods

Officially branded [[Alpha-Class Traitor]]s, due to their murder of a full regiment of [[Hunters of Artemis]] and [[Hounds of Artemis]], it puts them at the same level as [[Luke Castellan]], the betrayer. The twins operate outside the jurisdiction of the [[Olympians]] and the [[UOE]]. Their current objective is the direct harvesting of divine power emitted by [[Him within the moon]], which they believe to be a god-fragment.

According to intercepted communiqués from [[Order of the Hollow Crescent]], the twins believe Percy’s soul was never meant to remain bound to [[Luna Primaris]] or [[Gaia (Planet)]]. 
## Appearance and Combat Doctrine

Orran, the elder, his appearance strikes distinctive. Standing nearly seven feet tall, he is a slender presence, a lithe form of damaged and scarred skin. His face is sharp, with high cheekbones. His eyes, hard, cold, and calculating, glow with a faint, eerie green hue that hints at the unnatural obsession he shares with the shard of [[Him within the moon]]. His hair is dark and cropped close to his head.

He wields "Severance," an oversized fencing rapier, a weapon capable of poking through even the toughest of armors. 

Virre, the younger, in contrast to her twin, is shorter and physically stronger, yet her presence is no less imposing. Her skin is paper-pale. Her eyes, glow with the similar green as her brother. Her hair flows long, black as midnight, the splayed hair often distracting the enemy she fights. In her hand, she always holds “Backstab” a bayonet knife that glows faintly with Artemis' Blessing. Her choice in weaponry has allowed her to move around the battlefield and speeds of up to 500 m/s, rivalling the maneuverability of a [[Titan (Mech)]]
