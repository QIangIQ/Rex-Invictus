In the dwindling hours of the solar cycle known as December's Eve, **[[Kagura Kuroyami]]**, emissary of the [[Shinto Pantheon]] and formal ward of [[Amaterasu]], initiated the controlled ignition sequence of a dormant [[TBN Relay]]—one of the last functioning tachyon salt bridges still capable of piercing the veil between empires.

The construction and activation sequence relied on FTL-grade drone swarms provided by [[Deyang Foundries]], whose programmable field matrices allowed the creation of a temporary bridgehead, threading through tachyonic subspace without collapsing local spacetime integrity.

"Matriarch Voss, I extend the greetings of the Shinto Pantheon. The reactivation of this relay signifies our intent to establish dialogue during these tumultuous times."

"Agreed. Our envoys will coordinate the specifics. May this be the dawn of a collaborative path forward."
