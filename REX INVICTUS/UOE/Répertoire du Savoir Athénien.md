Affiliation: [[Greek Pantheon]]

Sovereign Alignment: [[UOE]]

Established on Old Terra in the 1950s, [[Institute Academy]] predates the formation of the modern [[UOE]] and survived planetary collapse, pantheonic realignment, and interstellar expansion. It evolved from terrestrial research institutions into the single most technologically advanced mortal-aligned entity in known space.

Only the [[Répertoire du Savoir Athénien]] — the grandest divine archive of knowledge — exceeds it in total informational scope.

## Strategic Identity

[[Institute Academy]] is universally regarded as the bleeding edge of:

- Military technology
    
- Hyper-intelligent AI systems
    
- Energy generation systems
    
- Titan frame architecture
    
- High-density propulsion systems
    

It is frequently said across both corporate and military sectors that Institute technology is indistinguishable from magic.
