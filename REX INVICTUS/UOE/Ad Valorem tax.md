An **[[Ad Valorem tax]]** ("according to value") is a tax set as a percentage of an item's assessed value rather than per unit. Property taxes, sales taxes, VAT and most import tariffs are ad valorem. The contrast is a specific tax, such as a fixed charge per litre of fuel.

In the [[UOE]], commodity markets in drachmae (see [[Annex 13287]] and [[Eurobium]]) suggest value-based duties on trade along the [[Tachyon Bridge Network]].
