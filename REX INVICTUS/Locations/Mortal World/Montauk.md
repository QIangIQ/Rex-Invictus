**[[Montauk]]** is a hamlet at the eastern tip of [[Long Island]], known for its lighthouse (commissioned by [[George Washington]] in 1792) and its beaches.

---

## PJO Canon

[[Sally Jackson]] rents a cabin on the beach at Montauk every year, because it is where she met [[Poseidon]]. In [[The Lightning Thief]] [[Percy Jackson]], [[Sally Jackson|Sally]] and [[Grover Underwood]] are attacked by the [[Minotaur]] after leaving the cabin, which leads [[Percy Jackson|Percy]] to [[Camp Half-Blood]] nearby.
