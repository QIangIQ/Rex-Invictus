**[[Empire State Building]]** is a 102-storey Art Deco skyscraper in Midtown [[New York|Manhattan]], [[New York]], finished in 1931.

---

## History (IRL)

Built in about 13 months, it was the world's tallest building until 1970. It stands 443 metres to the tip of its antenna.

## PJO Canon

Its special elevator, opened with a keycard from the guard, goes to the 600th floor, where [[Olympus]] floats above the city. In [[The Last Olympian]] it is the last line of defense during the [[Battle of Manhattan]].

## Rex Invictus

[[Parlamentum mk.I]] was placed on [[Olympus]] above the building.
