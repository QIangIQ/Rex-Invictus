**[[San Francisco]]** is a figure of Rex Invictus lore. (Lore adapted from *The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** Flamel material is adapted into the Rex Invictus present (c. 2021). Rex Invictus wins any conflict; [[Robespierre]] fills [[Dr. John Dee]]'s role and hunts the Philosopher's Stone rather than [[The Codex]]. See [[Flamel Series#Integration rules]].

---

## Rex Invictus Integration

In [[Rex Invictus]] the Flamels run the Small Book Shop in San Francisco, with [[Nicholas Flamel|Perenelle]]'s café across the street. [[Condottiero]] leaves the questers outside the city, and [[Robespierre]] follows them to the shop ([[Ch17 - Rex Invictus X|Ch17]]).

---

## Real-World Background (IRL)

San Francisco is a city on the peninsula at the mouth of San Francisco Bay, California, founded by the Spanish in 1776 and transformed by the 1849 Gold Rush and the 1906 earthquake.

> San Francisco, officially the City and County of San Francisco. The only City-County in California, it has a total land area of about 47.9 square miles on the northern end of the San Francisco Peninsula. It is one of many international cities favored by immortals and [[Elders]] in the 21st century.
> The City is a primary setting for the series. The twins of prophecy live there as do [[Nicholas Flamel]] and his wife Perenelle, alongside several hidden immortals and Elders. It is the centre of the [[Dark Elders]]' plans on [[Alcatraz]]. It was featured in [[The Alchemyst]],[[The Necromancer]], [[The Warlock]] and [[The Enchantress]].
