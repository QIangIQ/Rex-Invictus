**[[Stonehenge]]** is a figure of Rex Invictus lore. (Lore adapted from *The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** Flamel material is adapted into the Rex Invictus present (c. 2021). Rex Invictus wins any conflict; [[Robespierre]] fills [[Dr. John Dee]]'s role and hunts the Philosopher's Stone rather than [[The Codex]]. See [[Flamel Series#Integration rules]].

---

## Real-World Background (IRL)

Stonehenge is a prehistoric stone circle on Salisbury Plain, England, built in stages from about 3000 BC to 1500 BC and aligned with the solstice sunrise.

> Stonehenge is a large stone monument on Salisbury Plain in Wiltshire county, England. It was built by neolithic Celtic Humani in several stages over the span of several thousand years, the earliest of which taking place slightly over 5,000 years ago. Stonehenge is notable for its access to numerous ley lines. In [[The Sorceress]], Stonehenge was used by [[Sophie Newman]], [[Josh Newman]], and [[Nicholas Flamel]] to escape [[Cernunnos]] and the [[Wild Hunt]].
