**[[Alcatraz]]** is a figure of Rex Invictus lore. (Lore adapted from *The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** Flamel material is adapted into the Rex Invictus present (c. 2021). Rex Invictus wins any conflict; [[Robespierre]] fills [[Dr. John Dee]]'s role and hunts the Philosopher's Stone rather than [[The Codex]]. See [[Flamel Series#Integration rules]].

---

## Rex Invictus Integration

Not yet a [[Rex Invictus]] location. In the [[Flamel Series|Flamel series]] [[Dr. John Dee|Dee]] and [[Virginia Dare]] free the monsters imprisoned there; in an adaptation the role is [[Robespierre]]'s.

---

## Real-World Background (IRL)

Alcatraz is an island in [[San Francisco]] Bay, named by [[Juan Manuel de Ayala]] in 1775. It held a federal prison from 1934 to 1963 (Al Capone was an inmate) and was occupied by Native American activists from 1969 to 1971.
