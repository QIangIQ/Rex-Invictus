**[[Berkeley, California]]** is a city on the east shore of [[San Francisco]] Bay, home of the University of California's flagship campus (founded 1868).

---

## PJO Canon

[[Camp Jupiter]] lies hidden in the [[Oakland, California|Oakland]] and Berkeley Hills, between Berkeley and [[Oakland, California|Oakland]], reached through the Caldecott Tunnel. [[New Rome]] sits in the same valley, and [[Mt. Othrys]] rises across the bay. See [[San Francisco]].
