**[[New York]]** is the largest city in the United States and the seat of [[Olympus]] in the modern age.

---

## History (IRL)

Founded as the Dutch post of New Amsterdam on Manhattan in 1624 and taken by the English in 1664, it grew into the country's main port and financial center. Its five boroughs hold about 8.3 million people. The state of the same name stretches from [[Long Island]] to the Great Lakes.

## PJO Canon

Because the gods follow the center of Western power, [[Olympus]] floats above the [[Empire State Building]] on the 600th floor. [[Percy Jackson]] grew up on the Upper East Side, and Manhattan was the battlefield of the [[Battle of Manhattan]] in [[The Last Olympian]]. [[Camp Half-Blood]] lies on [[Long Island]].
