**[[Long Island]]** is a 190-kilometre island stretching east from [[New York]] City into the Atlantic.

---

## Geography (IRL)

Its western end holds the boroughs of Brooklyn and Queens; the rest is Nassau and Suffolk counties. The east splits into the North and South Forks, and [[Montauk]] Point marks the tip of the South Fork. The North Shore along Long Island Sound is hilly and wooded, the setting of *The Great Gatsby*.

## PJO Canon

[[Camp Half-Blood]] sits on the North Shore, near the end of the island, at Farm Road 3.141. See [[Camp Half-Blood#Grounds|Camp Half-Blood grounds]].
