**[[Oakland, California]]** is a port city on the east side of [[San Francisco]] Bay, linked to [[San Francisco]] by the Bay Bridge.

---

## PJO Canon

The Caldecott Tunnel through the Oakland Hills is the guarded entrance to [[Camp Jupiter]], which lies between Oakland and [[Berkeley, California|Berkeley]].
