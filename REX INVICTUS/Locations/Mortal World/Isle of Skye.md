The **[[Isle of Skye]]** is the largest island of the Inner Hebrides off the west coast of Scotland, known for the Cuillin mountains. Its Gaelic name links it to [[Scathach|Scáthach]] (the Cuillin are said to be named for [[Cuchulain|Cú Chulainn]]), and Dunscaith Castle on Sleat is the traditional site of [[Dún Scáith]].
