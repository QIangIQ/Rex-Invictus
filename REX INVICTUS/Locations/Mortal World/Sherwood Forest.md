> Sherwood Forest is the forest shadowrealm inhabited by Tammuz. It is home to many dryads, hamadryads, nymphs, and other nature spirits.

In [[The Warlock]], the [[Comte de Saint-Germain]], accompanied by [[Palamedes]] and [[William Shakespeare]], meet Tammuz in Sherwood Forest. They ask the Elder to send the trio back in time to rescue [[Scathach]] and [[Joan of Arc]] after they are sent to the [[Pleistocene Era Shadowrealm]].

### Trivia
- The hamadryad [[Ptelea]] notes that [[Tammuz]] has spent five centuries replicating the long-lost Grove of Eridhu shadowrealm. She states that Tammuz has been summoning various dryads, hamadryads, nymphs, and other nature spirits from across the globe to settle the shadowrealm once it is completed.
- Sherwood Forest is a real forest in Nottinghamshire, England. In British folklore, Sherwood Forest is the home of Robin Hood.
