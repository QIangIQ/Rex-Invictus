**[[Mount Everest]]** (Sagarmatha, Chomolungma) is Earth's highest mountain at 8,849 m, on the Nepal and Tibet border. Edmund Hillary and Tenzing Norgay made the first confirmed ascent in 1953. In [[Rex Invictus]] its ruins on dead, quarantined [[Gaia (Planet)|Gaia]] are where the [[Bounty Hunters of Dead Gaia]] meet the far-future [[Bianca di Angelo|Bianca Di'Angelo]] ([[Ch20 - Rex Invictus XIII]]).
