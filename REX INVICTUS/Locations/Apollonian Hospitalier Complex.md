The **[[Apollonian Hospitalier Complex]]** is an [[Apollo]]-run fortress-planet hospital in the far future, built of Hephestian ferrocrete reinforced with polymerized [[Stygian Iron]] mesh. Its regeneration pods hold 5% dilute nectar.

## UOE (Far Future)

- [[Percy Jackson|Percy]]'s vat clone was stored on Deck 23, Section 8, labelled "Classified experiments (Low-quality)", watched by [[Mitch Fulgur]] and Victor ([[Ch09 - Rex Invictus II|Ch9]] and [[Ch10 - Rex Invictus III|Ch10]]). [[Minerva (AI)|Minerva]] woke in it.
- Forty-two [[Zeus-Wrath Hunter Killer Satellites]] guard it, enough to disintegrate a fortress-planet.

## IRL background

The name recalls the Knights Hospitaller, a military order founded in 11th-century Jerusalem to care for pilgrims, later based on Rhodes and Malta.
