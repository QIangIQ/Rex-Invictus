**[[Luna Secundus]]** is a fortress-moon of the [[Hunters of Artemis]] in the far future.

## UOE (Far Future)

- In [[Ch12 - Rex Invictus V|Ch12]] to [[Ch14 - Rex Invictus VII|Ch14]], [[Virre Arenberg]] and [[Orran Arenberg]] of the [[House of Arenberg]] take it while the [[Order of the Hollow Crescent]] tries to wake [[Him within the moon]].
- [[Brigellus]]'s Legio XIII Lupae Fridulus holds the Southern Trench Network, and [[Doctus]]'s Legio IX Scriptum Obscurus is stationed there.
