The Greek Underworld, also known as Greek Hell or simply The Underworld, was the principal infernal dimension and Soul Zone of the [[Greek Pantheon]]. Unlike [[Biblical Hell]], which developed primarily as an infernal civilization governed by Demons and a King/Ruler, the Greek Underworld simultaneously functioned as an afterlife realm, Soul-processing system and divine territory populated by numerous Chthonic Gods. The most important Gods possessed their own Territories within the greater dimension, giving the Underworld an internal political structure comparable in some respects to [[Olympus|Mount Olympus]].[1]
The Underworld historically processed Souls associated with the Greek supernatural system and contained the metaphysical infrastructure necessary for judgment, punishment and Soul recycling. Its legitimate Ruler possessed authority connected to [[The Judges of The Abyss]] and the greater Soul System, while the Core of the Underworld functioned as the central mechanism through which Souls flowed and the Underworld's afterlife processes were maintained.[2][3]
Hades originally served as the King and Ruler of the Underworld, but his abandonment of his duties during the Demonic War caused the Soul-processing system to fall into disrepair. Persephone Elderblood later passed the Ruler Trial administered by The Judges of The Abyss and became the Underworld's legitimate True Ruler.[2][4] She retained this position until the Greek Underworld was merged with Biblical Hell and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s own planetary Hell, at which point the old Greek Hell ceased existing as an independent dimension and its territories became part of the newly created Hell within Victor Elderblood's Soul.[5][6]

### Nature and Function
The Greek Underworld was one of Earth's major Hells and formed part of the greater universal system responsible for processing dead Souls. Like other legitimate Soul Zones, its fundamental responsibility was not simply torturing sinners but participating in the cycle through which Souls were judged, processed and eventually returned toward [[The Universal Tree]] and Reincarnation.[2] Different Hells possessed different local environments and methods of interacting with the dead, but their fundamental administrative purpose remained similar.
The Underworld therefore operated through two overlapping systems. On the cosmological level, it was a Soul Zone connected to the universal mechanisms administered by [[The Judges of The Abyss]]. On the political level, it was the homeland of numerous Greek Chthonic Gods, each of whom could maintain a personal Domain within the greater dimension.[1] This distinction explains why the legitimate Ruler possessed immense authority over the Underworld itself without automatically possessing unrestricted control over every divine territory contained within it.
Everything possessing a Soul ultimately participates in the greater Soul cycle, including non-sentient life and even the faint Souls associated with natural objects. Much of this low-level processing occurs automatically, meaning the Ruler does not individually judge every insignificant Soul. The Ruler's primary responsibility is maintaining the functioning of the local system and dealing with Souls or circumstances requiring actual judgment.[2]

### Appearance
When directly explored in Chapter 775, the Greek Underworld was described as considerably darker and more depressing than the [[Biblical Hell]] familiar to Morgana. Its general landscape consisted of death, decay, oppressive darkness and an unusual absence of ordinary signs of life.[1] The atmosphere was sufficiently gloomy that Morgana compared it to the Biblical Hell of Suicide.
The appearance was not uniform throughout the entire realm. Nyx Elderblood explained that the Underworld only began resembling Biblical Hell more closely as one travelled toward [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]].[1] Other Territories could differ radically from the surrounding wasteland; [[Hecate]]'s Domain, for example, contained a comparatively vibrant environment of plants, herbs and carefully controlled magical life despite being surrounded by the otherwise desolate Underworld.[7]
During the Chapter 775 expedition, the region surrounding the River [[Styx]] was unnaturally silent. Nyx noted that the Souls normally expected around the river had completely disappeared and that even ordinary Underworld creatures were absent.[1] This disappearance reflected the abnormal political circumstances occurring within the realm at the time and should not be treated as the normal permanent condition of the River Styx.

### Structure and Territories
The Greek Underworld was decentralized compared with [[Biblical Hell]]. Chapter 775 explicitly states that important Gods maintained their own Territories throughout the greater dimension, including [[Hades]], [[Persephone Elderblood]], [[Hecate]], Nyx Elderblood, [[Erebus]] and [[Hypnos]].[1] These domains could possess their own environment, defenses and supernatural laws, with the authority of the domain's owner becoming particularly strong inside their territory.
[[Persephone]] inherited Hades' principal Domain after becoming the effective sovereign of the Underworld, but she deliberately did not seize the Territories belonging to Gods who had assisted her, such as Hecate and Hypnos. She was also incapable of casually imposing her will upon the personal domains of [[Primordial Gods]] such as Nyx and Erebus without risking a direct confrontation with beings whose individual authority could rival or exceed her own outside the administrative structure of the Underworld.[1]

### Persephone's Domain
Following [[Hades]]' departure, [[Persephone Elderblood]] became the principal political authority within the Underworld and occupied his former position at its center. Her castle contained a sumptuous throne room and magical infrastructure supplied by [[Hecate]], including enchanted mirrors capable of long-range communication.[1] [[Persephone]]'s awareness and Ruler authority were particularly powerful within her central territory, allowing her to immediately sense significant intrusions and dispatch servants such as [[Thanatos]], [[Hypnos]] and [[Nemesis]] to investigate.
Her authority nevertheless possessed territorial limits. Nyx's nature as the Mother of Night allowed her to conceal herself even from extremely powerful Gods, while Nyx's private territory existed outside Persephone's ordinary control because it was not itself one of the Soul-processing regions governed through the Ruler system.[1] [[Erebus]] possessed similar advantages through Darkness. Persephone therefore ruled the Underworld as its legitimate Soul administrator without becoming an unrestricted absolute sovereign over every independent Primordial domain embedded within the greater dimension.

### Hecate's Territory
Main article: [[Magic]] goddess [[Hecate]]
Hecate maintained one of the most distinctive independent domains in the Greek Underworld. Her principal structure was Archaeon, The Castle of the Eclipse, a castle constructed over her original temple.[7] Whereas the surrounding Underworld consisted largely of death and decay, Hecate's interior territory appeared significantly more alive and contained a carefully maintained garden filled with unusual herbs and supernatural plants, including vegetation capable of affecting the emotional state of Gods.
Hecate had spent extensive periods researching methods of protecting herself against the [[Primordial Gods]] living nearby, particularly Nyx and [[Erebus]], who could ordinarily enter most places without being detected. Deep beneath Archaeon existed an enormous creation or structure powerful enough to interfere with Nyx's perception and authority, demonstrating that Hecate's Domain possessed specialized anti-Primordial defenses.[7]
The Territory therefore demonstrates why the Underworld should not be described as one uniform bleak landscape. Individual Gods could dramatically alter the environment inside their personal Domains.

### Primordial Domains
Several of the most dangerous regions within the Greek Underworld belonged to the older [[Primordial Gods]] of the [[Greek Pantheon]]. Nyx Elderblood, [[Erebus]] and [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] possessed authority that predated many younger Olympian and Chthonic Gods, and their domains could not be treated as ordinary territories subordinate to [[Persephone]]'s political rule.
Nyx existed within a plane associated with Night that could only be properly perceived by beings of comparable or greater level when she deliberately concealed herself.[1] Persephone explicitly recognized that her Ruler authority could not simply seize or control Nyx's private domain. Erebus similarly possessed a territory and an existence fundamentally associated with Darkness; during the confrontation in Chapter 794, his influence covered the surrounding area in Darkness through which ordinary light could not pass.[8]
Tartarus occupied a particularly deep portion of the Underworld. Nyx described regions closer to Tartarus as increasingly resembling [[Biblical Hell]], distinguishing it from the more desolate but comparatively restrained landscapes nearer Persephone's central territory.[1] Tartarus should be treated carefully because the name refers both to a region/domain and to the Primordial God associated with that region.

### Rulership and the Helm
Main article: [[The Helm]]
See also: [[Three Judges Realm]]
The legitimate Ruler of the Greek Underworld was not merely a monarch with political control. The position represented an administrative authority granted through the universal Soul System and recognized by [[The Judges of The Abyss]]. The Helm associated with [[Hades]] carried the authority necessary to initiate the Ruler Trial; when a prospective successor wore it, they were transported before the Judges and tested to determine whether they possessed the impartiality necessary to judge Souls.[2][9]
The test required far more than personal strength. A Ruler had to demonstrate the ability to judge without allowing personal relationships, emotional attachment or divine arrogance to override the responsibilities of the Soul System. [[Persephone]]'s trial included numerous moral judgments and increasingly cruel choices involving Souls created specifically by the Judges, culminating in situations involving people emotionally important to her.[9][4]
After successfully completing the trial, Persephone returned as a True Ruler of Hell whose authority and overall power had increased enormously.[4] Her status as Queen of the Underworld therefore changed from primarily political inheritance through Hades to legitimate cosmological recognition by the Judges. This authority granted her extensive control over the Underworld's Soul-processing regions, allowed her to manipulate the realm itself and enabled her to enforce the administrative rules associated with the dead.

### Soul System
The Greek Underworld functioned as one component of the larger Soul System administered by [[The Judges of The Abyss]]. Souls assigned to the Greek Hell were processed according to its local afterlife structure, with sinners receiving punishment before their Soul material eventually returned toward the universal cycle of Life and Reincarnation maintained by The Universal Tree.[2]
The Ruler was not personally required to handle every Soul. Ordinary processes, especially those involving minor or non-sentient Souls, were largely automated. The existence of a legitimate Ruler was nevertheless necessary to keep the system operating correctly and handle matters requiring actual judgment or authority. [[Hades]]' abandonment of his responsibilities therefore created a serious administrative problem until [[Persephone]] successfully inherited the position.[2]
Powerful supernatural Souls could receive treatment different from ordinary mortal Souls because the greater authorities responsible for Soul judgment could intervene directly. The exact universal mechanics belong primarily on The Judges of The Abyss, [[The Core of the Afterlife]] and the general Soul-system pages rather than being repeated in full here.

### Core of the Underworld
Main article: [[The Core of the Afterlife]]
At the heart of the Greek Underworld existed the Core of the Underworld, a massive metaphysical sphere through which countless streams of Souls continually flowed.[3] The Core was not merely symbolic; [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s examination revealed that it was a genuine mechanism created by the Primordial authorities so the local Soul System could function.
Access was highly restricted. [[Persephone]] explained that the central area was normally accessible only to the legitimate King and Queen/Ruler of the Underworld, and even [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood had never previously entered it despite her age and status.[3] Victor recognized that his own Hell possessed a structurally similar Core whose main differences resulted from local customization rather than a fundamentally separate mechanism.
The Core also functioned as an interface to deeper information contained within the greater Soul and Record systems. Victor later used the Greek Underworld's infrastructure, Persephone's Ruler authority and [[Thanatos]]' connection to Death while investigating the possibility of temporarily reconstructing an Akashic Echo of [[Adonis Snow]].[3]

### History

#### Rule of Hades
[[Hades]] historically served as the King and legitimate Ruler of the Greek Underworld. His position granted authority over its Soul-processing functions as well as political leadership over much of the Chthonic civilization. The Underworld nevertheless remained decentralized because major Gods and Primordials retained territories whose internal autonomy could exceed Hades' ordinary political reach.
During the Demonic War, Hades allied himself with [[Diablo]] and attacked [[Olympus]] while [[Zeus]] was absent.[10] He subsequently abandoned his responsibilities as Ruler and left the Greek Underworld without the legitimate administrator required to properly maintain its Soul-processing system.

#### Persephone Becomes Ruler
With [[Hades]] absent and foreign powers threatening the Underworld, Nyx Elderblood approached [[Persephone]] and urged her to assume the vacant position.[2] Persephone initially resisted but ultimately accepted that the Underworld was her home and that leaving it without a legitimate Ruler would allow outsiders to exploit it.
Using Hades' Helm, Persephone entered the [[Three Judges Realm]] and underwent the trial required by [[The Judges of The Abyss]].[2][9] After surviving the Judges' tests of impartiality and personal attachment, she returned in Chapter 641 as a fully recognized Ruler whose authority vastly exceeded the largely ceremonial title she had previously held.[4]

#### Underworld Intrusion
During the events preceding the destruction of the [[Greek Pantheon]], [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett|Scathach Scarlett]], Morgana, [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood and Nyx Elderblood secretly entered the Underworld while travelling toward [[Hecate]]'s Territory.[1] Their presence illustrated the realm's fragmented geography: [[Persephone]] could sense intrusion into her own territory and dispatch [[Thanatos]], [[Hypnos]] and [[Nemesis]], but the group could potentially reach domains where her direct political authority was weaker.
The expedition eventually led through Hecate's Domain and later into a confrontation involving [[Erebus]]. In Chapter 794, Erebus revealed himself within the Underworld and used his primordial Darkness while advancing the conspiracy involving [[Zeus]], Kronus and other forces against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction.[8]

#### War in the Greek Underworld
The final war surrounding the [[Greek Pantheon]] spilled directly into the Underworld. [[Zeus]] eventually destroyed his own [[Master Bolt]] to fuel an enormous Word of Power that imposed the Territory of [[Olympus]] over the battlefield.[11] Because the affected region was still fundamentally part of the Underworld, [[Persephone]] regarded the act as a direct violation of her sovereign territory and ordered [[Thanatos]] to mobilize her army.
Persephone's forces consisted primarily of the children of Nyx and [[Erebus]] and the Gods descended from them, including Second-Generation [[Primordial Gods]] associated with Death, Dreams, Revenge and related Chthonic Concepts.[12] The military therefore differed substantially from Olympus' primarily Olympian/Titan structure and reflected the Underworld's own divine population.
Persephone used her Ruler authority to dismantle Zeus' imposed Territory. Chapter 800 explicitly notes that Zeus' declaration was ultimately a Word of Power sustained by [[Poseidon|the Master]] Bolt, whereas the battlefield remained technically inside the Underworld, where Persephone possessed overwhelming administrative control.[13] Removing the foreign influence required considerable Energy but was within her capabilities as the legitimate Ruler.

#### Core and Adonis
Following the destruction of the principal Greek enemy factions, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later entered the Core of the Underworld while dealing with unresolved issues involving [[Persephone]], Agnes and Adonis.[3] His examination of the Core revealed the deeper mechanical structure underlying the Soul Zone and demonstrated that Greek Hell's Soul-processing infrastructure was fundamentally related to the same universal system used by other legitimate Hells.

#### Merger into the New Hell
The Greek Underworld ceased existing as an independent realm during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s reconstruction of the supernatural cosmology surrounding his new world. With the involvement of [[The Universal Tree]] and [[The Judges of The Abyss]], the Greek Underworld was merged with [[Biblical Hell]] and Victor's native planetary Hell, after which the resulting infernal dimension became part of Victor's Soul.[14][5]
The merger did not merely place the old dimensions beside one another. Chapter 827 explicitly states that the old Greek Hell and old Biblical Hell no longer existed because they had fused into a new Hell.[6] Their former territories, environmental characteristics, Soul infrastructure and inhabitants were incorporated into the larger merged realm.
[[Persephone]] therefore ceased being the Ruler of an independently existing Greek Underworld. Victor subsequently strengthened the Ruler authority of both Persephone and [[Aline Elderblood Valefar]] and formally recognized them as Rulers of the newly created Hell.[6] Later developments expanded the ruling structure further, while Victor remained the True King and ultimate sovereign of the realm.

### Current Status
The Greek Underworld is no longer an independent dimension. Its former structure survives as part of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s New Hell, which exists within Victor Elderblood's Soul and belongs to the [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]].[5][6]

### Relationship with Biblical Hell
Main article: [[Biblical Hell]]
The Greek Underworld and Biblical Hell fulfilled similar cosmic responsibilities but developed very different societies. Biblical Hell was principally an infernal civilization dominated by Demons and structured around a King, Ruler and Demonic hierarchy, while Greek Hell contained numerous Gods whose private territories made the realm resemble both an afterlife and a second divine country comparable to [[Olympus]].[1]
Their different political structures disappeared after the merger. Both realms became components of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s New Hell, producing a considerably larger infernal dimension containing territories and biomes inherited from each predecessor.[5][6]
