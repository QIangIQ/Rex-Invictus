**[[Voss III]]** is the smallest and most distant of the three stars of the [[Voss System]]. Its relative isolation from [[Voss Primaris|Voss I]] and [[Voss II]] made it the site of the [[Voss Sovereignty]]'s first [[Dyson Sphere]]. When the [[War of Princes]] began, the sphere had barely finished its primary satellite phase and had only begun to generate energy.

**IRL background.** A Dyson sphere is a megastructure proposed by Freeman Dyson in 1960: a swarm of collectors around a star that captures most of its output.
