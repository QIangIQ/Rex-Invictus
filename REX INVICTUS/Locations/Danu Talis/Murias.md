## Real-World Background (IRL)

In Irish myth Murias is one of the four cities of the Tuatha Dé Danann, from which they brought the Cauldron of the Dagda.

## Flamel Series Canon (SOFTINF)

> Murias was a city located on the wild northeast shores of [[Danu Talis]]. The Elder and Great Elder inhabitants were all masters of [[Elemental magic]].

### Description
Murias was described as an ancient city with an impossibly tall, incredibly slender twisting glass spire that rose directly out of the sea off Murias. Much of the ancient city was fused with enormous globes of melted glass shot through with threads of solid gold, evidence of the terrible battles the [[Earthlords]] had fought with the [[Archons]] and [[Great Elders]] in the Time Before Time. The crystal spire was pristine and gleaming, untouched or unaffected by the incredible heat that had melted the surrounding buildings. The spire occupied a rocky spur of land that became an island at every high tide. It changed color with the weather and the tides, from chill gray to icy blue, to alabaster white, to an arctic green.
When the high tides lashed against the smooth white walls, the salt water would hiss and boil, so that the tower was perpetually wreathed in steam even though the stones themselves were cool. At night, the spire glowed with a pale phosphorence the color of sour milk, throbbing to a slow regular rhythm like a great heart, sending pulsing streaks of color - reds and purples - up the length of the needle. During the winter months, when bitter hailstorms sleeted in from the Great Ice at the Top of the World and sheathed the city of Murias in thick snow and solid ice, tower remained untouched. The island the spire was located was named Tor Ri, which means "The King's Tower" in the ancient language of the [[Danu Talis]]. The crystal spire was the home of [[Abraham the Mage]].
