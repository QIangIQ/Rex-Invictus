**[[Luna Primaris]]** is the far-future name of Earth's Moon, the fortress-moon of [[Artemis]] and the prison of [[Him within the moon]].

## UOE (Far Future)

- **[[Him within the moon]].** Guards on Luna Primaris and a few [[Hunters of Artemis]] report dream-echoes and silverlight apparitions around the entity. The [[Order of the Hollow Crescent]] wants to wake it, and the [[Arenberg Twins]] want to harvest its power. The twins believe [[Percy Jackson|Percy]]'s soul was never meant to stay bound to Luna Primaris or [[Gaia (Planet)]].
- **Garrisons.** [[Arlechinus]]'s Legio VI Cuspide Dolosa holds the outer crater sanctums. The armories belong to [[Pantaleon]]'s Legio IV Argentum Avarus.
- **[[Artemis]]' moons.** Artemis gained control of every moon under [[UOE]] control during [[The Great Expansion]].

See also [[Luna Secundus]].

## IRL background

*Luna* is the Latin name of the Moon and of its Roman goddess, the counterpart of the Greek [[Selene]]. [[Artemis]] and her Roman form [[Diana]] were also tied to the Moon in later antiquity.
