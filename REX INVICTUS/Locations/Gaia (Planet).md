**[[Gaia (Planet)]]** is the far-future name for Earth in the [[Undying Olympian Empire]], also called Old Terra. Gaian hours measure time ([[Perdition]] kills within 24 Gaian hours). By the far-far future of [[Ch20 - Rex Invictus XIII]] it is dead and quarantined. Not to be confused with the primordial [[Gaia]] or [[Earth]].
