**[[Voss Secundus]]** is a world of the tri-star [[Voss System]], seat of the [[Voss Sovereignty]] and home of the far-future [[New Rome]].

## UOE (Far Future)

- **Colonization.** Before the [[War of Princes]], [[Solon Themis]] moved the Icarus corvettes and Mastodon light frigates to help colonize [[Voss Primaris]] and Voss Secundus, which left the defenders short of [[Point-Defence]] ships when the war came.
- **[[New Rome]].** [[The Augusti]] run the casinos of New Rome. The [[Themis Needle]] rises above the city.
- **[[Hunters of Artemis|The Hunt]].** [[Thalia Grace]] leads a contingent of the [[Hunters of Artemis]] there, with [[Atlanta]]. [[Thalia Grace|Thalia]] is left in the rain atop the [[Themis]] Needle after [[Minerva (AI)|Minerva]] escapes.
- **Minerva's escape.** In [[Ch23 - Interlude I|Ch23]] [[Minerva (AI)|Minerva]] escapes New Rome by hijacking [[Astralnet Conglomerate|Astralnet]] Scheduled Service 118 with [[Verrill Chase]] on her back.
