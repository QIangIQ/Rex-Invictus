**[[Ancient Greece]]** is the Greek world from the end of the Mycenaean palaces (about 1100 BC) to the Roman conquest (146 BC).

---

## Greek History (IRL)

**Periods.** The Dark Age; the Archaic period (about 800 to 480 BC), which brought the polis, alphabetic writing, colonies around the Mediterranean and the first Olympic Games (776 BC); the Classical period (480 to 323 BC), from the Persian Wars through the Athenian empire and the Peloponnesian War to the death of Alexander; and the Hellenistic period, when Alexander's successors ruled from Egypt to Bactria.

**Religion.** The [[Olympians]] were worshiped at city sanctuaries and at the panhellenic sites of Olympia, Delphi ([[Oracle of Delphi]]), Delos and Dodona, alongside hero cults and the [[Mystery Religions]]. Myths were fixed in writing by Homer and [[Hesiod]].
