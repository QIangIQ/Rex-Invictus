**[[Sicily]]** is the largest island in the Mediterranean, off the toe of Italy.

---

## History and Myth (IRL)

Greek colonies such as Syracuse, Akragas and Gela shared the island with Carthaginian and native towns until Rome took it in the First Punic War (264 to 241 BC), making it Rome's first province and a grain store. [[Sextus Pompeius]] held it against [[Octavian (Roman Emperor)|Octavian]] until 36 BC. In myth Mount Etna is the forge of [[Hephaestus]] and the prison of [[Typhon]] or the giant Enceladus, the [[Cyclopes]] of the *Odyssey* were placed on its coast, and [[Minos]] died there hunting [[Daedalus]]. [[Persephone]] was carried off from a meadow near Enna.
