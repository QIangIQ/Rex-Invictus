**[[Mount Pelion]]** is a wooded mountain in Thessaly, Greece, on the peninsula above the modern city of Volos.

---

## Greek History (IRL)

It was the home of the [[Centaur|centaurs]], and [[Chiron]] lived in a cave near its summit, where he raised [[Achilles]], [[Jason (Argonaut)|Jason]] and [[Asclepius]]. The wedding of [[Peleus]] and [[Thetis]], where Eris threw the golden apple that led to the [[Trojan War]], took place there. When the giants Otus and [[Ephialtes]] made war on the gods they tried to pile Pelion on Ossa to reach heaven. The timber of the [[Argo]] was cut from its slopes.

## PJO Canon

The first [[Camp Half-Blood]] stood at the foot of Pelion while [[Chiron]] trained heroes in [[Ancient Greece]].
