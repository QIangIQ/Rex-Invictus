**[[Troy]]** was a Bronze Age city in northwest Anatolia, the setting of the [[Trojan War]].

---

## Greek History (IRL)

The site at Hisarlik in modern Turkey was identified by Frank Calvert and excavated by Heinrich Schliemann from 1871. It holds nine main settlement layers. Troy VI and VIIa (roughly 1700 to 1180 BC) are the usual candidates for Homer's city, and Hittite texts mention a western kingdom called Wilusa that most scholars equate with Ilion. In myth its walls were built by [[Apollo]] and [[Poseidon]] for King Laomedon, and it fell to the Greeks after a ten-year siege through the wooden horse. [[Aeneas]] escaped its fall.
