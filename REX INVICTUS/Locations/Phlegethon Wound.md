The **[[Phlegethon Wound]]** is an eleven-kilometre-radius sphere of nothing in [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]], where the Pyrrhuiesian staging ground used to be. At its edge rain falls upward and clocks run eleven times too fast. The thread it opened goes "Not down. Back." ([[Ch23 - Interlude I|Ch23]]). All data recovered from it is sealed under Directorate authority.

---

## UOE (Far Future)

- **The fall.** [[Marien Phyrrus]]'s Reserve Echelon of the [[Waves of Poseidon]], four million soldiers, fell through it onto Mars fourteen thousand years before the Empire exists.
- **The siege.** [[Aphrodite]]'s [[Dove's Fleet]] holds station above it. [[Tullia Ardens]] and the [[Legio XII Fulminata]] dig in at the rim "until the door opens again".
- **The survey.** [[Ilse Wendriner]] of [[Tochter des Elysium R&D]] measures it and finds a [[Répertoire du Savoir Athénien|Répertoire]] carrier wave inside. Her junior [[Ilse Wendriner|Aurelian]] realizes what it means: "Then they're alive."
- **[[Porphyrion]].** When something calls through the Wound, the Giant king [[Porphyrion]] wades through his own army toward it. [[Commander Thorne]]'s [[Commander Thorne|Rosed Knights]] pin him down.

## Myth background

The [[Phlegethon]] (Pyriphlegethon, "fire-blazing") is the river of fire of the Greek [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]], which in Plato's *Phaedo* flows down into [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]]. See [[Phlegethon]].
