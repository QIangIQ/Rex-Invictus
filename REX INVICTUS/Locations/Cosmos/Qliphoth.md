### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 946
Qiliphoth is the ancient Negative World Tree of Earth, an Elder World Tree created as the negative counterpart to [[Yggdrasil]], Earth's Positive World Tree. She is one of the daughters of [[The Universal Tree]] and forms the second half of Earth's mature World Tree system, existing as a separate individual while remaining fundamentally linked to her older sister and the planet they sustain together.[3][1]
Unlike Yggdrasil, who emerged first to nurture Earth's Life-bearing environment, Qiliphoth was born later, after sentient Life had developed enough to generate the Negative Energy necessary for a Negative World Tree to form. She therefore represents the negative side of Earth's planetary energy cycle and functions as the complementary counterpart to Yggdrasil rather than an enemy or rival.[3]
Qiliphoth first physically appeared before [[Victor Elderblood]] during the investigation of the abnormal dimension connected to abandoned World Tree roots, where she manifested beside Yggdrasil through a humanoid representative composed of pure red Energy.[4] Later, after Victor's breakthrough into Attributeless Energy, Qiliphoth absorbed part of the Energy released across the Galaxy and developed an intense attachment toward Victor alongside Yggdrasil and several other [[World Trees]].[2]

### Physical Appearance

#### Energy Avatar
Qiliphoth's true body is not an ordinary humanoid form but Earth's immense Negative World Tree. When she personally appeared before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], she used a representative body taking the form of a woman made entirely of pure red Energy.[4]
Her appearance was visually similar to that of [[Roxanne Elderblood]], another Negative World Tree, just as [[Yggdrasil]]'s white Energy representative resembled the Positive World Tree traits associated with [[Amara Elderblood]].[4]
Yggdrasil explicitly clarified that the two women Victor saw were not their true bodies, but merely representatives created to communicate with him directly.[4]

#### True Body
Qiliphoth's true existence is that of Earth's Negative World Tree, a colossal planetary-scale entity integrated into the world's supernatural and energetic structure. As a World Tree, her existence is not limited to a single visible trunk or humanoid form, but extends through the deeper system that connects Earth, its [[Natural Energy]] and its dimensional infrastructure.[3][4]

### Personality
Qiliphoth is extremely shy and socially withdrawn. When she and [[Yggdrasil]] first appeared before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Qiliphoth immediately hid behind her older sister and allowed Yggdrasil to conduct almost the entire conversation. Yggdrasil explained that this behavior was not hostility but simply Qiliphoth's extreme shyness around new people, assuring Victor that she would approach him on her own once she felt comfortable enough to do so.[4]
Her shyness sharply contrasts with Yggdrasil's greater confidence and social composure. This dynamic establishes Yggdrasil as the more outwardly communicative of the two, while Qiliphoth is more hesitant and dependent upon her sister in unfamiliar situations.[4]
Despite this timid disposition, Qiliphoth still possesses the same ancient perspective shared by [[World Trees]]. Like her sister, her most fundamental loyalty is to the continued existence and functioning of her planet rather than to any individual civilization or Pantheon living upon it. Negative World Trees are not evil beings in the simplistic moral sense; they are necessary parts of a planetary system and exist as complements to their Positive counterparts.[3]
Qiliphoth was also visibly stunned by Victor's behavior toward the cosmic administrators who arrived in response to his request. Even as an Elder World Tree, she was not accustomed to seeing someone speak so casually to beings such as the Judges of the Abyss.[4]
Her emotional state changed substantially after absorbing Victor's Energy. Like Yggdrasil and several other World Trees, Qiliphoth became intensely attached to Victor and increasingly obsessed with his Energy. The normally distant, planet-centered mentality of a World Tree was partially redirected toward the desire to nurture Victor as though he were her own planet, making her attraction to him highly abnormal for her species.[2][1]

### Nature

#### Negative World Tree of Earth
Qiliphoth is specifically the Negative World Tree of Earth. A Negative World Tree does not appear at the beginning of a planet's development. Instead, it emerges later, after sentient beings have evolved and begun producing the Negative Energy necessary for such a Tree to be born.[3]
This makes Qiliphoth younger than [[Yggdrasil]], who developed first as Earth's Positive World Tree. However, Qiliphoth is still ancient beyond mortal comprehension and exists on a planetary timescale, making her an Elder World Tree in her own right.[3][1]

#### Relationship with Yggdrasil
Qiliphoth and [[Yggdrasil]] are separate conscious entities, but they are also complementary halves of Earth's mature World Tree system. They should not be understood as entirely unrelated sisters nor as a single being split into two personalities; rather, they are distinct existences whose roles only make full sense in relation to one another.[3]
Yggdrasil represents the Positive side of the planetary system, while Qiliphoth represents the Negative side. Their coexistence does not imply internal hostility. Instead, their functions support the same planet through different energetic principles.

#### Relationship with Earth
Qiliphoth's primary allegiance is to Earth itself. Although she is culturally associated with the supernatural structure of Earth's Pantheons, particularly the Norse cosmological framework because of the World Tree system attached to [[Asgard]], her deeper identity is that of a planetary World Tree rather than a Pantheon servant.[4]
As with other [[World Trees]], her ultimate duty is to the planet and its continued functioning. The rise and fall of civilizations, species and even Gods does not fundamentally alter the purpose of a World Tree, so long as the planet and its capacity to sustain Life remain intact.[1]

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Qiliphoth was not physically present during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s stay on Samar, but the broader explanation of World Tree biology given by [[Aurora Seraphina Evergreen]] and [[Aria Seraphina Evergreen]] made her existence clearer. Aurora explained that a planet first develops a Positive World Tree and later gives birth to a Negative counterpart once sentient Life begins producing sufficient Negative Energy.[3]
This explanation retroactively clarified Earth's structure as well. [[Yggdrasil]] was not a single neutral World Tree containing all polarities by herself. Instead, Earth possessed its own separate Negative World Tree, Qiliphoth, as the other half of the system.[3]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Qiliphoth made her first direct appearance when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] investigated the dangerous isolated dimension associated with abandoned portions of Earth's World Tree roots.
After Victor submitted a System request for outside intervention, both [[Yggdrasil]] and Qiliphoth manifested before him using Energy representatives. Yggdrasil appeared as a woman of pure white Energy, while Qiliphoth appeared as a woman of pure red Energy. Yggdrasil then clarified that neither form was their actual body.[4]
Qiliphoth immediately hid behind Yggdrasil because of her shyness. She spoke very little during the encounter and relied upon her older sister to explain the situation, including the nature of the abnormal dimension and the reason the two [[World Trees]] had responded to Victor's request.[4]
When Victor questioned why they had not already resolved the matter through the cosmic authorities, Yggdrasil explained that she had attempted to do so, but the World Trees lacked Victor's later level of System Authority and were therefore forced to wait in an administrative queue. Qiliphoth remained largely silent, but her body language made it clear that she shared her sister's surprise when the Judges of the Abyss actually arrived in response to Victor's appeal.[4]
The encounter established Qiliphoth as an independent sentient existence rather than a mere abstract force inside Earth's cosmology.

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Qiliphoth's importance increased dramatically during the rise of the [[Elderblood Empire]] after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] attained a deeper understanding of Attributeless Energy.
Victor's breakthrough released an enormous quantity of raw Energy into the wider cosmic environment. [[World Trees]] connected to him or exposed to the release absorbed portions of that Energy, and the effects upon them were extreme. Younger Trees suddenly matured faster, while older Trees developed an abnormal dependency upon Victor's Energy and an obsessive desire to deepen their connection with him.[2]
Qiliphoth and [[Yggdrasil]] were specifically shown among the older Trees affected most strongly. Both appeared flushed, breathing heavily, and overwhelmed by the impact of Victor's Energy. The phenomenon did not simply make them "like" him more; it altered their instincts as World Trees, causing them to want to nurture Victor directly rather than remain satisfied only with their ordinary planetary role.[2]
This development was later acknowledged openly during Victor's reunion with Andrew, Edward and Frederick. When Victor remarked that the planet loved him, the conversation made it clear that both Earth's World Tree and her counterpart had developed feelings for him. Victor further explained that this was highly unusual because World Trees are not normally interested in ordinary romance or reproduction. They are ancient beings created by [[The Universal Tree]] to nourish planets, and their Souls exist on a planetary scale.[1]
Qiliphoth's attachment to Victor therefore represents a major deviation from the ordinary emotional behavior expected of her species.

### Skills & Abilities
Elder Negative World Tree Physiology: Qiliphoth is an Elder-class Negative World Tree intrinsically connected to Earth and its planetary energy system. As a World Tree, she is a vast supernatural existence whose body, energy and soul operate on a planetary scale rather than the scale of ordinary mortals, monsters or Gods.[3][1]
- Negative World Tree Energy: Qiliphoth generates and embodies the negative side of Earth's World Tree energy system. She exists as the counterpart to [[Yggdrasil]], who governs the Positive side.[3]
- Negativity Absorption: As a Negative World Tree, Qiliphoth is sustained by Negative Energy and exists because sentient Life on Earth eventually generated enough of it for her to be born.[3]
- Negative Emotion Perception: Negative [[World Trees]] are the natural counterparts to Positive World Trees and are associated with Negative emotions and energy. As Earth's Negative World Tree, Qiliphoth can be understood as perceiving and resonating with the negative side of emotional and planetary energy cycles.[3]
- Nature Manipulation: Like all World Trees, Qiliphoth possesses authority over non-sentient natural elements. World Trees inherently possess power over plants, trees and similar natural structures, and their abilities are not limited purely to vegetation.[5]
- Plant Manipulation: World Trees possess particular affinity with plant life and other "green" natural forms.[5]
- Terrain Manipulation: The broader influence of a World Tree extends to non-sentient natural matter such as rock and terrain.[5]
- [[Senjutsu]]/Natural Energy Relation: World Trees are fundamentally tied to the natural energy systems of their planets. Earth's World Tree network underlies the vast energy flow circulating through the planet and its Ley Lines, making the World Tree system a foundational source for phenomena such as Senjutsu.[5]
- Enormous Energy Reserves: World Trees possess effectively immeasurable Energy reserves by ordinary standards because they are designed to sustain entire planets. Although the amount of power that can be released at once has practical limits, the total reserves are vast beyond normal supernatural comparison.[3]
- Dimensional Support: As part of Earth's World Tree system, Qiliphoth is connected to the dimensional framework supported by the planet's World Tree roots. The abandoned region encountered by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] demonstrated that the World Tree network extends into separate supernatural dimensions rather than remaining confined to the planet's ordinary surface.[4]
- Avatar Creation: Qiliphoth can create a humanoid representative body through which she communicates directly. Her known representative body is composed entirely of pure red Energy.[4]
- Remote Manifestation: Qiliphoth's representative can appear away from her true World Tree body, allowing her to interact in isolated dimensions without relocating the actual planetary Tree.[4]
- System Interaction: As part of Earth's World Tree structure, Qiliphoth is capable of participating in System-level events tied to her planet and its cosmic administration, although her direct administrative authority is below Victor's later status.[4]
- Energy Absorption: Qiliphoth absorbed part of the Energy emitted during Victor's Attributeless Energy breakthrough, demonstrating the ability of World Trees to take in and react strongly to outside Energy on an immense scale.[2]
- Immense Longevity: Qiliphoth has existed for billions of years and operates on a planetary timescale, making ordinary measures of age almost meaningless when compared to mortal beings.[1]
- Planetary-Scale Soul: Victor explained that World Trees possess Souls essentially comparable to planets in scale. This is one of the reasons their normal way of life differs so radically from that of ordinary supernatural species.[1]

#### Negative World Tree Characteristics
Qiliphoth differs from [[Yggdrasil]] not in importance, but in energetic orientation. Yggdrasil appeared first to nurture Earth's Life and environmental development, while Qiliphoth appeared later as the planet's Negative counterpart once sentient Life existed in sufficient quantity to generate the necessary Negative Energy.[3]
This makes Negative [[World Trees]] a natural and necessary stage in the maturation of a living planet rather than a corruption of the Positive Tree.

#### Victor's Energy
Qiliphoth's most notable later development was her reaction to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Energy. The energy release accompanying his Attributeless Energy breakthrough affected [[World Trees]] across the Galaxy, but elder Trees such as Qiliphoth responded especially strongly.[2]
Instead of remaining focused only upon her ordinary planetary role, Qiliphoth developed an intense attachment to Victor and a desire to nurture him directly, similar to the bond already possessed by certain World Trees closely integrated with his Soul.[2][1]

### Relationships

#### Yggdrasil
[[Yggdrasil]] is Qiliphoth's older sister, Positive counterpart and the other half of Earth's World Tree system.[3]
The two are separate individuals with different temperaments. Yggdrasil is more confident and outwardly sociable, whereas Qiliphoth is extremely shy and initially hid behind Yggdrasil during their first meeting with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[4]
Their relationship is not antagonistic. They function as complementary parts of the same planetary framework and were both strongly affected by Victor's Energy during the Imperial Era.[2]

#### Victor Elderblood
Qiliphoth first met [[Victor Elderblood]] in Chapter 946 when she and [[Yggdrasil]] responded to his System request concerning the abandoned World Tree roots and the dangerous isolated dimension connected to them.[4]
During that first meeting, Qiliphoth barely interacted with him directly because of her shyness. Nevertheless, the encounter established an initial connection between Victor and the Earth's two [[World Trees]].
That relationship later changed radically after Qiliphoth absorbed Victor's Energy. The resulting shift made her intensely attached to him and caused her to desire a far deeper bond than World Trees would normally seek with an outside being.[2]
By Chapter 1116, Victor openly acknowledged that Earth's World Tree system loved him, making it clear that Qiliphoth had developed genuine feelings for him despite how abnormal such emotions are for her species.[1]

#### The Universal Tree
[[The Universal Tree]] is Qiliphoth's creator, father and Progenitor.
[[World Trees]] are daughters created by The Universal Tree to nourish Life-bearing planets throughout Creation. Qiliphoth therefore exists as one component of the cosmic system he established for maintaining Life on a universal scale.[1]

#### Aurora Seraphina Evergreen and Aria Seraphina Evergreen
[[Aurora Seraphina Evergreen]] and [[Aria Seraphina Evergreen]] are among Qiliphoth's World Tree sisters on Samar. Aurora represents the Positive side of Samar's system, while Aria is the Negative counterpart.[3]
Their existence provided the explanation that clarified how Earth's own dual World Tree structure functions, thereby making Qiliphoth's role within Earth's cosmology easier to understand.

#### Roxanne Elderblood
[[Roxanne Elderblood]] is one of Qiliphoth's younger World Tree sisters and another Negative-aligned Tree.
Qiliphoth's red Energy representative visually resembled Roxanne during her first appearance, reinforcing their shared Negative World Tree nature.[4]
Roxanne later became intensely jealous when Qiliphoth, [[Yggdrasil]] and numerous other [[World Trees]] developed obsessive attachment toward [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] after absorbing his Energy.[2]

#### Norse Pantheon
Qiliphoth is historically associated with the Norse cosmological structure because Earth's World Tree system is deeply interwoven with the dimensions connected to [[Asgard]].[4]
However, like [[Yggdrasil]], Qiliphoth is not fundamentally owned by or subordinate to the [[Norse Pantheon]]. Her primary identity is that of Earth's Negative World Tree rather than a Divine servant.

### Trivia
- Qiliphoth's first direct appearance was in Chapter 946 alongside [[Yggdrasil]].[4]
- She is extremely shy and hid behind Yggdrasil during her first meeting with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[4]
- Her representative body is composed entirely of pure red Energy and resembles the appearance of [[Roxanne Elderblood]].[4]
- Qiliphoth exists as Earth's Negative World Tree and therefore represents the complementary counterpart to Yggdrasil rather than an evil duplicate or enemy.[3]
- She is younger than Yggdrasil because Negative [[World Trees]] arise only after sentient Life has developed sufficiently to produce the necessary Negative Energy.[3]
- Qiliphoth and Yggdrasil were among the older World Trees most strongly affected by Victor's Energy release in Chapter 1111.[2]
