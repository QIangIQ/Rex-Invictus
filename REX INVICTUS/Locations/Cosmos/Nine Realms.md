## MTWABV Canon

### The Nine Realms
The Nine Realms are the greater supernatural dimensional structure belonging to the [[Norse Pantheon]]. Rather than representing one continuous ordinary landmass, the Realms form a network of different territories associated with the Gods and supernatural races of the Pantheon and connected through [[Yggdrasil]] and the [[Bifrost]].
[[Asgard]] served as the principal celestial realm and political center of the Pantheon, while Helheim functioned as its [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] under Hela. Other known Realms were inhabited or associated with Gods, Giants, Dwarves and other supernatural races.
The dimensional network was severely destabilized after the Bifrost was destroyed and ultimately collapsed as a unified political system during Ragnarök.[1][2][3]

### Structure
The Nine Realms formed the spatial and supernatural foundation of the [[Norse Pantheon]].
The Pantheon was home not only to the Æsir and Vanir Gods but to numerous supernatural species, including Giants, Dwarves, Elves, Dark Elves and Fairies. Different populations were distributed across separate territories within the greater dimensional network.
The Realms were interconnected strongly enough that specialized Divine Artifacts could travel between them while still remaining part of the same Pantheon system.

### Yggdrasil
[[Yggdrasil]] was fundamental to the structure connecting the Nine Realms.
The realms were not merely maintained through artificial portals. Paths, roots and branches of the World Tree created deeper dimensional connections throughout the Pantheon.
After the [[Bifrost]] was destroyed, the branches of Yggdrasil represented one of the only alternative means of entering or moving through the Pantheon. Such travel was dangerous because Yggdrasil could detect the intrusion and expel the traveler.[6]
[[Ratatosk]] possessed unusually extensive knowledge of these hidden routes and could navigate paths through the World Tree unavailable to most other Beings.

### Bifrost
The [[Bifrost]] was the principal transportation network of the Nine Realms.
It was not merely advanced magical technology. The Artifact possessed the Concept of Travel and a Divinity directly linked to the [[Norse Pantheon]].[7]
Because of this connection, destroying it should normally have been extraordinarily difficult. Nevertheless, its destruction caused [[Asgard]] and the other Realms to become inaccessible through their standard route and severely fragmented the Pantheon's transportation system.[8]

### Draupnir
Draupnir was a Divine ring belonging to [[Odin]].
Among its abilities was direct transportation throughout the Nine Realms, allowing Odin to move between locations within the Norse dimensional system without relying entirely upon the [[Bifrost]].[9]
This demonstrates that the Nine Realms were treated as a defined supernatural territory through which specialized spatial artifacts could navigate.

### Dimensional Stability
The Realms ultimately depended upon deeper supernatural structures rather than surviving as isolated independent universes.
The roots and dimensional connections associated with [[Yggdrasil]] helped sustain portions of the Pantheon. When damaged or abandoned World Tree connections failed, the dimensions dependent upon them could destabilize and collapse.[10]
This became particularly important during Ragnarök, when attacks against the structural foundations of the Pantheon became as dangerous as battles against its Gods.

### Ragnarök
The Nine Realms ceased functioning as a stable unified civilization during the final conflict between Hela and [[Odin]].
Hela had spent thousands of years preparing forces capable of dismantling the advantages accumulated by Odin. [[Asgard]] became one of the central battlefields while other Norse territories were affected by the collapse of transportation, political authority and World Tree connections.[11][12][13]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction simultaneously extracted useful populations and resources from the collapsing Pantheon.[14]
Following Odin's defeat, the [[Norse Pantheon]] was no longer capable of functioning as the integrated Divine civilization that had once united the Nine Realms.

### Known Inhabitants
The greater dimensional system historically contained:
- Æsir
- Vanir
- Fire Giants
- Ice Giants
- Dwarves
- Elves
- Dark Elves
- Fairies
- Other supernatural populations belonging to the [[Norse Pantheon]]
Their disappearance from the surviving territory after Ragnarök was partly the result of war and partly the deliberate evacuation conducted by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction.[15][16]

### Notes
- The Nine Realms refer to the greater Norse dimensional system; [[Asgard]] is only one of those Realms.
- Helheim is the Pantheon's [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] and should remain a separate location article due to the amount of information surrounding Hela, Nidhoggr and its dimensional structure.
- The novel does not provide equal information about all nine Realms. Missing details should not be filled solely from Norse mythology.
- The [[Bifrost]] connects the Realms but is not itself the fundamental source of their existence. Deeper connections through [[Yggdrasil]] continued after its destruction.[17][18]
