The World Core is a planetary-scale Divine Artifact developed by [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]] for the [[Elderblood Empire]]. It is the evolved form of Velnorah's earlier [[City Core]] technology, expanding a system originally capable of monitoring and protecting a single city until it could encompass the terrain and infrastructure of an entire planet.[1]
World Cores form one of the principal layers of the Elderblood Empire's Energy infrastructure. Rather than generating infinite Energy themselves, they receive enormous quantities of power from the [[Dragon Heart]] and redistribute that Energy throughout their assigned planets using channels formed through the Roots of [[World Trees]]. According to [[Akasha Elderblood]], each World Core is intended to guarantee the protection, surveillance and nourishment of the planet placed under its jurisdiction.[1]
The intended Imperial hierarchy places the Dragon Heart above the World Cores:

### History

#### City Core
The World Core originated from Velnorah's [[City Core]], a Divine Artifact created to manage and protect designated territories.
An early demonstration of the technology occurred when [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] provided two City Cores for an Imperial base. The Artifacts appeared as cubes and existed in at least two configurations: a complete violet model and a reduced red model.[3]
The complete violet City Core could record events throughout its territory and prevent unauthorized or hostile Beings from entering. Violet explained that it was directly connected to another Artifact within Velnorah's territory and required considerably more Energy than the reduced model.[3]
The red model was intentionally restricted and primarily functioned as a recording device. Unlike the complete version, it required relatively little Energy and drew its power through an accompanying bracelet worn by its operator.[3]
The known City Core functions included:
- observing a designated territory;
- recording events occurring within that territory;
- storing information or Energy;
- repelling unwanted targets;
- preventing individuals not included on an authorized list from entering;
- identifying or responding to hostile Beings;
- and operating automatically according to established instructions.[3][1]
The City Core was therefore already a highly sophisticated combination of surveillance system, territorial barrier, information repository and autonomous defensive Artifact before its planetary upgrade.

#### Development of the World Core
The appearance of [[Akasha Elderblood]] fundamentally changed what was possible with Velnorah's Core technology.
Previously, City Cores relied more heavily upon predetermined schedules and automated routines to maintain their designated territories. With Akasha capable of efficiently supervising enormous numbers of systems simultaneously, she could assume operational responsibility for the Empire's City Cores without wasting excessive quantities of Energy.[1]
This improvement in computational and administrative capability allowed Velnorah to upgrade the concept itself.
Rather than continuing to restrict each Core to a single city, she developed the World Core—an Artifact functioning according to the same fundamental principles as a [[City Core]] but capable of encompassing an entire planet.[1]
Akasha explained:
"This Artifact functions similarly to a City Core, but where it differs is that it's not limited to just one City. It can encompass the terrain of an entire planet!"[1]
The expansion in operating scale resulted in a correspondingly enormous increase in Energy requirements. A single planetary Core was already demanding; [[Victor Elderblood]] intended for World Cores eventually to be installed upon every planet conquered and incorporated into the [[Elderblood Empire]].[1]

#### Connection to the Dragon Heart
The World Core project became practical because of the [[Dragon Heart]].
The Dragon Heart was created by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] as the central Energy Artifact of the Empire. It generates enormous quantities of Energy on its own while also receiving Energy from [[World Trees]] that have become connected to Victor.[2][1]
By Chapter 1100, two critical stages of the World Core network had been completed:
1. Velnorah had upgraded the existing Core technology into World Cores.
2. Victor had established a connection between the World Cores and the Dragon Heart.[1]
This allowed the Dragon Heart to transmit Energy into a World Core, after which the Core could send that Energy throughout its planet using channels created through World Tree Root systems.[1]
The result was the foundation of a unified Imperial planetary Energy network.

### Functions

#### Planetary Jurisdiction
The fundamental improvement of the World Core over its predecessor is scale.
A [[City Core]] governs a defined city or territory. A World Core can encompass the terrain of an entire planet.[1]
This makes it suitable for the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s transition from a civilization centered upon individual cities and worlds into an expanding interplanetary and interstellar Empire.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s long-term intention was to install World Cores upon every planet conquered by the Empire.[1]

#### Planetary Protection
Akasha explicitly identified protection as one of the three principal responsibilities of a World Core.[1]
Because the World Core evolved from the [[City Core]], its protective function extends the underlying territorial-security principles of the earlier technology to planetary scale.
City Cores were capable of:
- repelling targets;
- preventing unauthorized Beings from entering;
- detecting hostile individuals;
- and enforcing permission-based access restrictions.[3][1]
The precise limits of a World Core's planetary defenses have not been fully demonstrated. Therefore, the Core should not presently be described as an absolutely impenetrable planetary shield or as capable of automatically defeating every invader.
Its confirmed role is to provide planetary protection as part of the Empire's larger defensive infrastructure.[1]

#### Planetary Surveillance
Surveillance is another explicitly confirmed World Core function.[1]
Its predecessor could observe an entire designated city, record events occurring inside that region and even gather extraordinarily sensitive information concerning people within its jurisdiction.[3]
The World Core extends this basic concept from urban territory to planetary scale.
Akasha's existence is especially important to this function. Instead of requiring isolated Cores to independently process everything occurring within their jurisdictions, Akasha can coordinate the Empire's Core network with extreme computational efficiency.[1]
This makes the World Core both a local planetary system and part of a broader Imperial information architecture.

#### Recording and Storage
The original [[City Core]] possessed functions described as observing and storing, while the reduced red City Core was specifically designed to record everything occurring inside its designated territory.[3][1]
Because Akasha states that World Cores function similarly to City Cores with the primary difference being their vastly expanded range, these recording and storage capabilities form part of the technological basis inherited by the planetary model.[1]
However, the novel has not established that every piece of Imperial intelligence is physically stored inside each World Core.

#### Access Control
City Cores could prevent people not included on an authorized list from entering protected territory and could repel hostile or unauthorized Beings.[3][1]
As the World Core is explicitly described as operating similarly to a [[City Core]], access regulation remains part of the system's underlying functionality.
The exact degree to which a World Core can block planetary entry from space, teleportation, dimensional travel or overwhelmingly superior Beings has not yet been established.

#### Autonomous Operation
City Cores possess an automatic mode allowing the Artifact itself to make decisions based upon its configured instructions.[1]
The World Core inherits the same technological foundation but gains the further advantage of Akasha's direct coordination.
This arrangement reduces the need for individual Imperial administrators to manually supervise every minor Energy fluctuation, unauthorized entry or planetary system.
The Core should therefore be understood as semi-autonomous infrastructure rather than a completely passive battery.

#### Planetary Nourishment
One of the most important differences between an ordinary defensive or administrative system and a World Core is its role in planetary nourishment.
Akasha explicitly stated that World Cores would guarantee the protection, surveillance and nutrition of all planets belonging to the Empire.[1]
This nourishment is closely tied to the Energy relationship among the [[Dragon Heart]], World Core and World Tree network.
The novel has not yet individually detailed every biological or environmental process controlled through this function, so the term should not automatically be expanded into unsupported abilities such as complete climate manipulation, instant terraforming or unlimited ecological reconstruction.
What is confirmed is that the World Core participates in supplying the Energy necessary to sustain and develop its assigned planet.[1]

#### Energy Distribution
The World Core's most important infrastructural role is acting as the planetary intermediary between the [[Dragon Heart]] and the planet itself.
The basic sequence is:
Dragon Heart
↓
World Core
↓

[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] connected the World Cores directly to the Dragon Heart. Each World Core can subsequently send Energy into its planet through channels formed by the Roots of local [[World Trees]].[1]
The World Core is therefore an Energy distributor and regulator, not the ultimate origin of the Empire's Energy.

### Energy Network

#### Dragon Heart
At the top of the Imperial Energy hierarchy is the [[Dragon Heart]].
Akasha described it as the Artifact that sustains the Empire. Like the biological heart of a Dragon, it functions as an enormous Energy Core; in the context of the [[Elderblood Empire]], Akasha metaphorically described it as effectively being her Father's heart.[1]
This description should not be interpreted as the Artifact literally replacing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s biological heart. The Dragon Heart is an artificial Imperial Artifact linked to Victor and functions as the central Energy heart of his civilization.[2]
The Dragon Heart performs two relevant functions within the World Core network:
- it passively produces immense quantities of Energy;
- and it channels Energy supplied by [[World Trees]] connected to Victor.[1]
This combined supply is then transmitted downward into World Cores.

#### World Trees
[[World Trees]] are an essential component of the system rather than merely another Energy source connected in parallel.
A World Tree naturally provides Energy to its planet. Once connected to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and the [[Dragon Heart]] network, part of the Tree's Energy can also be channeled toward the Dragon Heart.[1]
The Dragon Heart then supplies Energy back into the World Core, which redistributes it through Root-based channels into the planet.
The relationship was explicitly described by Victor's daughters as an infinite Energy loop.[1]

#### Infinite Energy Loop
The phrase infinite Energy loop refers to the interconnected relationship among the [[Dragon Heart]], [[World Trees]] and planets rather than to an individual World Core independently producing infinite Energy.[1]
This distinction is important.
The World Core's old infobox description as an "Infinite Energy Source" is inaccurate. The Dragon Heart is the primary Energy source at the top of the system, while World Trees contribute additional Energy. The World Core receives and redistributes this supply.[2][1]

### Relationship with Akasha
[[Akasha Elderblood]] was one of the principal reasons World Core technology became viable.
The earlier [[City Core]] system could perform automated tasks, but planetary expansion created an enormous problem of calculation and coordination. An Artifact capable of governing an entire world would require immense processing capacity in addition to enormous quantities of Energy.[1]
Akasha solved the computational aspect.
She explained that she could take responsibility for all of the Empire's City Cores efficiently without wasting excessive Energy. This capability encouraged Velnorah to upgrade the existing system into World Cores capable of planetary operation.[1]
Akasha therefore functions as the principal coordinating intelligence behind the Core network rather than being the physical source of the World Core's Energy.
Her responsibilities include performing the calculations necessary for planetary Core operation and coordinating large numbers of systems throughout the Empire.[1]

### Relationship with Velnorah
[[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]] is the principal architect of both the [[City Core]] and its World Core successor.
The original City Core was directly identified as one of Velnorah's creations.[3][1]
After Akasha's existence made vastly more efficient system-wide coordination possible, Velnorah upgraded the existing Artifacts into World Cores.[1]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] subsequently completed another essential part of the network by connecting the upgraded World Cores to the [[Dragon Heart]].[1]
The World Core system is consequently the result of multiple members of the Elderblood family contributing different capabilities:
- Velnorah — developed the Core technology and performed the World Core upgrade.
- Akasha — provides the calculation and coordination required to operate enormous numbers of Cores efficiently.
- Victor — established the direct Energy connection between the World Cores and Dragon Heart.
- [[World Trees]] — provide the Root architecture through which planetary Energy can be circulated.[1]

### Deployment
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] intended World Cores to become standard infrastructure on every planet conquered by the [[Elderblood Empire]].[1]
However, the network was still incomplete when Akasha explained it.
By Chapter 1100:
- Velnorah had already upgraded the World Cores;
- Victor had connected them to the [[Dragon Heart]];
- planetary Energy could be sent through channels formed from World Tree Roots;
- but most Imperial territories were not yet physically prepared to safely receive and use the resulting quantity of Energy.[1]
At that point, only the city in which the demonstration occurred possessed infrastructure capable of efficiently handling the full Energy supply.[1]
The three Cities of Hell and Elvenorah still required their Energy systems to be completely reworked, while recently conquered planets would require entirely new infrastructure.[1]
Consequently, the World Core network was conceived from the beginning as a civilization-scale project rather than an Artifact that could simply be placed on a planet and instantly bring every system online.

### Security and Classification
Detailed information concerning Imperial Core technology was treated as highly restricted information.
During the Chapter 1100 demonstration, [[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]] became excited and accidentally explained classified [[City Core]] information in front of an Imperial subordinate. Akasha immediately identified what Velina had revealed as Level 6 Imperial Secret information that only [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family and particularly trusted subordinates were permitted to know.[1]
The subordinate accepted having the relevant memory erased and was compensated for her loyalty afterward.[1]
This incident demonstrates the secrecy surrounding the Core system during its early development.
However, it does not establish that the World Core itself is primarily a classified-data storage device. The classified material was the knowledge concerning the Artifact and Imperial infrastructure.

### Strategic Importance
The World Core represents the point at which Elderblood infrastructure stopped being designed around isolated cities and began being constructed for an interplanetary Empire.
Before its creation, Velnorah's City Cores already gave the Empire advanced systems for territorial observation, recording, protection and access control.[3]
The World Core applied the same principle to an entire planet.
Its importance lies in several areas:
- Scalability: A single Core can govern planetary-scale territory rather than one city.[1]
- Energy Management: It connects a planet to the [[Dragon Heart]]'s enormous Energy network.[1]
- World Tree Integration: It uses World Tree Roots as planetary transmission channels.[1]
- Defense: Protection becomes an integrated planetary function rather than being limited to separate local fortifications.[1]
- Surveillance: The Empire can apply the observational principles of the [[City Core]] at planetary scale.[1]
- Planetary Development: Energy can be directed toward nourishing worlds and supporting Imperial infrastructure.[1]
- Automation: Akasha can coordinate the network without requiring every Core to be manually operated.[1]
The World Core thus acts less like a conventional generator and more like the administrative and energetic heart of an Imperial planet.

### Current Status
The World Core is active technology belonging to the [[Elderblood Empire]].
The Core architecture and [[Dragon Heart]] connection were successfully completed by Chapter 1100, although the civilization-wide distribution infrastructure remained unfinished at that point.[1]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] intended World Cores to eventually become standard infrastructure across the Empire's conquered planets. The exact number of World Cores presently operating throughout later Imperial territory has not been directly stated.
The Core should therefore be considered an established and functional Imperial technology whose network deployment continued alongside the Empire's expansion.

### Notes
- The World Core is an artificial Divine Artifact, not a naturally occurring planetary core.[1]
- It was developed from Velnorah's earlier [[City Core]] technology.[3][1]
- [[Akasha Elderblood]] was the principal reason operating Core technology at planetary and Empire-wide scales became computationally practical.[1]
- A World Core can encompass the terrain of an entire planet.[1]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] planned to place World Cores on every planet conquered by the [[Elderblood Empire]].[1]
- The World Core does not produce infinite Energy by itself. It receives Energy from the [[Dragon Heart]].[1]
- [[World Trees]] also contribute Energy to the Dragon Heart, creating the "infinite Energy loop" described in Chapter 1100.[1]
- World Tree Roots form the channels used by World Cores to transmit Energy throughout their planets.[1]
- The three explicitly stated main functions of the World Core are protection, surveillance and nourishment.[1]
- Energy generation was no longer the Empire's primary problem by the time the World Core network was developed; Energy transmission and infrastructure had become the greater limitation.[1]
- The details of the Core system were treated as Level 6 Imperial Secrets during its early development.[1]
- The upgraded World Core should not be confused with the Dragon Heart, [[The Core of the Afterlife]] or naturally occurring metaphysical planetary structures.
