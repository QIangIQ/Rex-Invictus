The Pillar of Memory is a Divine Artifact personally created by [[Victor Elderblood]] to preserve the important memories he shared with his family. Victor constructed the artifact inside an Ice Castle being used as a repository for meaningful objects, photographs and locations connected to his wives. Rather than allowing those memories to disappear with the passage of Time, Victor created a permanent supernatural archive connected directly to his own Soul.[1]
The Pillar is made from Divine Materials and contains a small fragment of Victor's Soul. Through Draconic Runes governed primarily by Victor's intent, the artifact determines who is allowed to access its contents and which memories a particular person is permitted to view. Victor specifically designed it for his wives and daughters, while preventing one member of the family from freely accessing intimate or personal memories belonging exclusively to another.[1]
Because the Pillar remains connected to Victor, its archive is not static. It automatically continues updating as Victor creates new memories, making it a continually expanding record of the Elderblood family's personal history.[1]

### History

#### Inspiration
The Pillar of Memory was created after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explored an Ice Castle containing important objects and memories connected to his wives.
The castle had effectively become a private memorial and family archive. Victor observed photographs, significant locations and personal objects preserved there, including the first Odachi created by [[Mizuki Elderblood]].[1]
Victor realized that the structure was being used to preserve the history of his relationships.
He particularly appreciated the idea that important moments involving his wives could remain available rather than gradually disappearing beneath centuries or millennia of accumulated memories.
After considering the concept, Victor decided to create a more permanent and supernatural method of preservation.[1]

#### Creation
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] moved to the center of the Ice Castle and generated an enormous sphere of blue Energy.[1]
A dark pillar composed of Divine Material then emerged from the ground.
At the center of the pillar was an imprint shaped specifically for Victor's palm.[1]
Victor subsequently created a simple wooden plaque and inscribed the artifact's purpose upon it:
Pillar of Memory. Created by Victor Alucard/Walker for the most important people in his life.
[1]
The physical structure alone was not enough.
Victor placed a claw against his forehead and extracted a minute fragment of his own Soul. The Soul fragment appeared as thin strands of darkness and white before Victor inserted it into the Energy surrounding the Pillar.[1]
The Energy immediately reacted.
It:
- stopped rotating;
- turned completely black;
- shifted into red;
- and finally stabilized as violet Energy containing dark-red and black tones.[1]
This process permanently connected the artifact to Victor.

#### Draconic Restriction
After integrating the Soul fragment, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] approached the Pillar and spoke in Draconic Language:
These memories should only be seen by those who are worthy.
[1]
The Pillar responded and stabilized.
Victor did not need to construct an enormous sequence of complicated instructions. Draconic Runes operate heavily through intent, and Victor's intent during creation defined the artifact's fundamental access rules.[1]
His intent was simple:
Only his wives and daughters should possess access to the preserved memories.
[1]
The result was a Divine Artifact capable not merely of storing information, but of understanding the intended relationship between each viewer and the memories contained within it.

### Appearance
The Pillar of Memory is a dark pillar constructed from Divine Material.[1]
Its central feature is a palm-shaped imprint corresponding to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
During its creation, the structure was surrounded by Energy that changed through several colors before finally stabilizing as violet Energy mixed with dark-red and black shades.[1]
Victor also placed a simple wooden plaque near the artifact identifying it as the Pillar of Memory and explaining that he had created it for the most important people in his life.[1]
The exact:
- height;
- width;
- weight;
- type of Divine Material;
- and permanent visual appearance of its Energy
have not been stated.

### Composition
The Pillar is composed of several supernatural components.

#### Divine Material
The physical body of the Pillar was explicitly created from Divine Material.[1]
The exact material used has not been identified.
Its use establishes the Pillar as a genuine Divine Artifact rather than an ordinary magical monument.

#### Victor's Soul Fragment
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] placed a small fragment of his own Soul into the Pillar during its creation.[1]
This is one of the artifact's most important components.
The Soul fragment creates a continuing connection between Victor and the Pillar, allowing the artifact to remain synchronized with him even after its initial construction.
Unlike a simple storage device that contains only the information manually placed inside it, the Pillar can continue receiving new memories through this connection.[1]
The fragment should not be interpreted as meaning Victor's life depends upon the Pillar. No chapter establishes the Pillar as a Soul anchor, resurrection point or weakness capable of killing Victor if destroyed.

#### Draconic Runes
The Pillar uses Draconic Runic principles to regulate access.[1]
Rather than requiring [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to write thousands of detailed conditions, the Runes interpreted his intent.
This allowed the Artifact to understand broad concepts such as:
- "wife";
- "daughter";
- appropriate memories;
- personal memories;
- and whether a particular viewer should be allowed to see a particular event.
The mechanism demonstrates the extraordinary flexibility of Draconic Runecraft when used by someone capable of clearly defining their intent.

### Functions

#### Memory Preservation
The primary function of the Pillar is to preserve [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s important memories involving his family.[1]
It acts as a supernatural family archive rather than a conventional historical database.
The memories selected for preservation primarily consist of:
- affectionate moments;
- family experiences;
- important personal events;
- and memories Victor considered meaningful enough for future generations to witness.
The Pillar was explicitly designed to preserve the emotional history of Victor's family rather than every event he had ever experienced.

#### Selective Memory Storage
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] deliberately excluded memories he considered too private or intimate.[1]
The Pillar therefore does not contain an unrestricted duplicate of Victor's entire memory.
He specifically allowed the artifact to preserve the "cute" and meaningful moments connected to his wives while excluding:
- highly intimate experiences;
- sensitive private moments;
- and other memories inappropriate for broader family access.[1]
This distinction makes the Pillar a curated family archive rather than a complete copy of Victor's mind.

#### Selective Access
The artifact does not simply allow every authorized user to access every stored memory.
It determines what each person is permitted to see.[1]
For example, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly considered that:
- [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] would not be able to freely view personal moments belonging specifically to Victor and [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]];
- Ruby's children would likewise not be able to access memories unrelated to themselves or their own mother.[1]
The Pillar therefore possesses a sophisticated relationship-based filtering system.

#### Relationship-Based Recognition
The artifact distinguishes users according to their relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and the people represented within a particular memory.

#### Automatic Memory Filtering
The Pillar itself determines which memories should be displayed to each user.[1]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] does not need to manually supervise every viewing session.
This suggests that the Soul fragment and Draconic Runes allow the artifact to interpret Victor's original intention and apply it dynamically.
The process is significantly more sophisticated than password-based security because it considers:
- identity;
- family relationship;
- memory ownership;
- and Victor's intended privacy restrictions.

#### Continuous Updating
One of the Pillar's most important properties is its ability to automatically update itself.[1]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] specifically noted that the artifact would continue receiving new memories because it remained connected to him.
The Pillar is therefore not limited to memories from the moment of its creation.

The exact delay between Victor experiencing a memory and the Pillar storing it has not been stated.

#### Long-Term Family Archive
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] created the Pillar partly with future generations in mind.
After completing it, he imagined that his future children might eventually visit the artifact and see how their mothers behaved when they were younger.[1]
This establishes the Pillar's intended lifespan and function beyond Victor's immediate generation.
It was designed as a permanent Elderblood family heirloom.

### Access
The Pillar was specifically designed for:
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wives;
- Victor's daughters.[1]
Victor's intent during creation restricted access to these groups.
However, his later thoughts regarding future children indicate that descendants connected to those family relationships may also gain appropriately restricted access.[1]
The exact boundaries of this inheritance system have not been fully explained.
For example, the chapter does not establish whether:
- grandchildren automatically receive access;
- sons possess identical permissions to daughters;
- stepchildren are treated differently;
- later wives are automatically recognized;
- or Victor must consciously update the authorization system.
Because the Pillar remains connected to Victor and interprets his intentions, some of these relationships may be dynamically recognized, but this has not been directly demonstrated.

### Memory Privacy
Privacy is one of the Artifact's defining design principles.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] deliberately prevented unrestricted access even among members of his own family.[1]
The system recognizes that being part of the Elderblood family does not mean every individual should automatically see every personal experience involving every other member.
This results in multiple layers of restriction:

This makes the Pillar fundamentally different from an unrestricted Akashic-style record.

### Relationship with Victor
The Pillar is directly connected to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] through the fragment of Soul incorporated during its creation.[1]
This connection enables:
- continuous updates;
- interpretation of Victor's intent;
- recognition of appropriate memories;
- and long-term synchronization.
The artifact is consequently less like an external hard drive and more like a carefully restricted extension of Victor's family memories.
However, the Pillar does not contain Victor's entire consciousness.
Nothing in Chapter 890 establishes that it can:
- recreate Victor after death;
- resurrect him;
- house his full Soul;
- independently think as Victor;
- control Victor;
- or reproduce every memory Victor possesses.
Its confirmed function is limited to preserving selected family memories.

### Relationship with Draconic Runes
The Pillar demonstrates one of the defining characteristics of Draconic Runes:
Intent can replace excessive procedural complexity.
[1]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] did not construct countless individual commands such as:
- Ruby can see this memory;
- Sasha cannot see that memory;
- this child can access these records;
- this wife cannot access those records.
Instead, he expressed the fundamental intention that only appropriate people should see appropriate memories.
The Draconic Runic system interpreted that intent and created a dynamic set of restrictions.
The Pillar therefore serves as an important example of how Draconic Runes differ from conventional programming or rigid magical arrays.

### Relationship with the Ice Castle
The Pillar was created inside an Ice Castle already functioning as a physical memorial to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family.[1]
The Castle contained:
- photographs;
- significant destinations;
- personal objects;
- and meaningful artifacts connected to Victor's wives.
Victor understood that Ruby had transformed the Castle into a place where their shared memories could be preserved.[1]
The Pillar expanded this concept from physical memorabilia into direct supernatural memory preservation.

### Significance
The Pillar of Memory reflects several important aspects of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s character and the Elderblood family.

#### Family
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] created the Artifact specifically for the people he considered most important in his life.[1]
Unlike many of his other Divine creations, it was not designed primarily for:
- warfare;
- defense;
- political power;
- Energy generation;
- or conquest.
Its purpose is almost entirely personal.

#### Dragon Mentality
The creation also reflects [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s increasingly Draconic mentality.
The chapter occurs during the period in which Victor had fully embraced his existence as a Dragon, and the Artifact demonstrates the Dragon-like tendency to preserve valuable possessions—in this case, memories and family history rather than material treasure.

#### Legacy
The Pillar was designed with future generations in mind.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly imagined children in the future accessing the Artifact to see the younger versions of their mothers.[1]
This makes the Pillar one of the earliest artifacts Victor created specifically as a long-term inheritance for the Elderblood family.

### Limitations

#### Restricted Subject Matter
The Pillar does not contain every memory [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] possesses.
Its archive is deliberately limited to selected family memories.[1]

#### Private Memories Excluded
Highly intimate or unsafe memories are deliberately omitted.[1]

#### Access Restrictions
Even authorized family members cannot freely access every memory.
The Artifact filters content according to the viewer's relationship with the memory and its subjects.[1]

#### Dependent on Victor's Connection
Its continuous updating function depends upon its connection to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[1]
The story has not explained what would happen if that connection were somehow severed.

#### No Demonstrated Offensive Capability
The Pillar has never demonstrated a combat function.
It should not be assumed to possess:
- offensive Soul attacks;
- defensive barriers;
- mind manipulation;
- memory erasure;
- or weaponized information abilities
merely because it contains a fragment of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul.

#### Not the Akashic Records
The Pillar is a private and selective memory archive.
It should not be compared directly to the [[Akashic Records]], which record information on a vastly broader universal scale.
