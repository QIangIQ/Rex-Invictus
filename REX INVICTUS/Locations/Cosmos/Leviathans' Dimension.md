The World of Water, also called the Leviathans' Dimension, is an artificial aquatic dimension created by Victor Elderblood through the Divine Concept of <Creation> with additional support from <BEGIN>. It is the homeland of the Leviathans, a Race of colossal aquatic Beings created from Victor's Essence to function as one of the most destructive military forces of the [[Elderblood Empire]].[1] Although the dimension exists separately from Victor's principal planet, it remains contained within his immense Soul and therefore forms part of Victor's Personal Universe.
The World of Water represented an important milestone in Victor's development because it was the first major work explicitly recognized by the Heavenly Father as Victor's own Creation.[1] Its creation required the establishment of an entirely new living Race, the production of original Souls and a specialized existential system through which those Souls could remain tied directly to Victor rather than participating in conventional Reincarnation.

### Description
The World of Water is a separate dimension composed almost entirely of water.[1] No conventional continents or permanent landmasses have been described, and the environment was deliberately created to accommodate the enormous bodies of the Leviathans. Its total dimensions have never been quantified, although the presence of hundreds of thousands of Leviathans—including individuals reaching planetary scale—demonstrates that the available volume is enormous.[2] Claims that the realm must therefore be Solar-System-sized, star-sized, galactic or literally infinite are speculative and should not be presented as established measurements.
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] revisited the realm in Chapter 1080 it was completely dark until he illuminated the surrounding dimension with a snap of his fingers, revealing hundreds of thousands of Leviathans observing him from the surrounding water.[2] The chapters do not clarify whether this darkness is an intentional environmental property or simply the result of the dimension lacking a conventional natural light source.

### Creation
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] created the World of Water after his mastery of <Creation> underwent a dramatic increase. Unlike several earlier feats in which <BEGIN> or Progenitor-related abilities played the dominant role, Creation served as the principal authority while <BEGIN> functioned primarily as support.[1] [[Amaterasu]] Elderblood was shocked by the advancement because Victor's understanding had developed far beyond the fragment of Creation-related knowledge previously associated with her father, while [[Gaia]] Elderblood compared the process to watching a single Being perform work that would ordinarily require several authorities within a Pantheon.
The feat was powerful enough for the Heavenly Father, the greatest known conventional authority over Creation, to sense Victor's actions from [[Biblical Heaven]]. Victor's authority over the Concept increased so rapidly during the process that the Heavenly Father concluded it had effectively been upgraded, ultimately reaching a position immediately beneath his own authority over Creation.[1] Victor formed a small blue sphere and declared, "From the Water, they were born, and with water, they brought both life and destruction," after which the newly created dimension manifested within his Personal Universe.
Victor then created the Leviathans as proud aquatic Beings born from the Essence of the Blood Dragon Progenitor and carrying his [[Will Solace|Will]]. Their pride was compared to that of Dragons, but the story explicitly distinguishes them from actual Dragons.[1] Producing the dimension and its population consumed enormous quantities of Energy, requiring extensive support from [[Roxanne Elderblood]] and [[Amara Elderblood]].

### Leviathans and Soul System
The creation of the Leviathans required more than producing physical organisms because [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was attempting to establish a genuine Race possessing new Souls. The event therefore attracted the attention of <Life> and <Soul>.[1] Both ultimately accepted the Race, and the System formally recognized the Leviathans as Beings created by <[[Chaos]]> within an aquatic dimension.
The System then asked whether their Souls should enter the conventional Cycle of Reincarnation. Life rejected that arrangement and Soul agreed, leaving the Leviathans directly bound to Victor instead.[1] When the System attempted to formalize this structure, no suitable existing Concept could represent the function Victor had assumed. It therefore established the new Divine Concept <He Who Creates Monsters> as a Sub-Concept of <BEGIN>, allowing Victor to sustain the Race and its resurrection cycle directly.
The System established unusually extreme prerequisites for another Being to acquire the same Concept: possession of <BEGIN>, possession of <Creation>, status as the Progenitor of the Sector's Dragon Race and sufficient Energy to maintain both the created species and its Soul cycle.[1] The Universal Tree immediately recognized how unrealistic these requirements would be for an ordinary God.
All Leviathan Souls consequently belong directly to Victor.[2] They do not undergo ordinary reincarnation after death; Victor states that a killed Leviathan can simply return through the system connected to him. This grants the Race enormous military value but also makes its existence unusually dependent upon its creator.

### Population and Growth
By Chapter 1080 the World of Water contained hundreds of thousands of Leviathans.[2] Their sizes vary considerably, and the chapter distinguishes their demonstrated scale from their eventual biological potential. By Chapter 1166, the Leviathan population had expanded enormously, with billions already living on their designated planet. The species commonly hunts in groups, allowing groups of Leviathans to bring down prey far larger than themselves, including a young Star Animal whose body exceeded the size of [[Jupiter]]. The largest Leviathans had developed to the point that they could easily devour an entire planet the size of Earth.[3][4]

### Military Purpose
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] created the Leviathans primarily as strategic weapons rather than precision soldiers. They are unsuitable for delicate missions or individual assassination when compared with Victor's other specialized forces, but exceptionally well suited to mass invasion, planetary destruction and annihilation of enormous enemy armies.[2] Victor internally describes them as his "Nuclear option", meaning they are intended for circumstances in which preserving the target's infrastructure is no longer important.
Victor specifically considers that if he abandoned the goal of conquering a Galaxy intact and instead released the Leviathan force to destroy it, everything within the target could be consumed within several hours.[2] This should be understood as his assessment of the collective Race's strategic capability rather than a demonstrated feat in which one individual Leviathan consumed an entire Galaxy.
Their Soul structure makes them particularly valuable in such warfare. Losses are less permanent than among conventional troops because a dead Leviathan remains connected to Victor and can return. The World of Water therefore functions as a protected reserve containing hundreds of thousands of renewable large-scale invasion organisms.

### Deployment Against the Hindu Pantheon
The Leviathans received one of their most important known deployments during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ultimatum to the [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]].[5] Victor demanded that [[Shiva]] and Kali either submit to the [[Elderblood Empire]] or be killed and made useful afterward. To demonstrate the difference between their respective forces, Victor revealed several components of his military, causing enormous Leviathans to appear across the sky alongside the Emperor's Heralds, Demonic armies, Dragon Goddesses and other Imperial forces.
Shiva observed that even the smallest visible specimens were approximately mountain-sized.[5] Their presence functioned primarily as deterrence rather than requiring actual combat. After Shiva surrendered, Victor immediately dismissed the Leviathans and returned them to their realm.[6]

### Trivia
- The World of Water is [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s first major Creation explicitly recognized as such by the Heavenly Father.[1]
- Creation served as the principal authority used to construct it, with <BEGIN> providing additional support.[1]
- The Leviathans are born from Victor's Essence and possess Dragon-like pride but are explicitly not Dragons.[1]
- Life and Soul both participated in formally accepting the Race into Creation.[1]
- The Leviathans were excluded from conventional Reincarnation.[1]
- The System created <He Who Creates Monsters> because no suitable Concept already existed for the structure Victor created.[1]
- The largest observed Leviathans in Chapter 1080 were approximately Earth-sized; [[Jupiter]]- and Sun-scale sizes are stated growth potential rather than the average demonstrated size.[2]
- Victor describes the Leviathans as his military "Nuclear option".[2]
- The realm's total dimensions remain unquantified.
