## MTWABV Canon

The Void Between Cosmos is the outer expanse separating completely independent cosmoses. Unlike the [[Dimensional Void]], which separates dimensions belonging to the same greater Creation, the Void Between Cosmos exists between cosmological systems that can possess entirely different Universes, Rules, Primordials and originating forms of [[Chaos]].[1]
It was directly explained by [[Azathoth Elderblood]] when [[Victor Elderblood]] questioned the possibility of abandoning their native cosmos if its [[Primordial Entities]] ever became an insurmountable threat. Azathoth revealed that escaping the cosmos itself was theoretically possible, but survival within the Void Between Cosmos required a degree of existence and power far beyond conventional Gods or even ordinary Primordial beings.[1]

### Nature
Azathoth explained the structure by creating representations of two separate spheres.
The first represented [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s entire cosmos—its Universe, Rules, Existence and everything belonging to that system. She then produced another sphere representing a different cosmos possessing its own Rules, different Primordials and a different [[Chaos]] responsible for directing its existence.[1]
The Void Between Cosmos was identified as the region or distance separating these independent cosmological structures.[1]
This means that the Void does not merely separate alternate timelines, parallel realities, Pantheon dimensions or different regions of the same Universe. The structures on either side can be fundamentally independent creations governed by entirely different cosmic systems.

### Separate Cosmoses
Different cosmoses are not required to possess the same fundamental arrangement as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s native Creation.
According to Azathoth, another cosmos can possess:
- Its own Universe.
- Its own Rules of Existence.
- Different Primordial beings.
- A different [[Chaos]] responsible for the development or governance of that cosmos.[1]
The existence of such foreign cosmoses confirms that the cosmology surrounding Victor is not the only complete system of Creation in existence.
[[Azathoth Elderblood]] herself originated from another cosmos before entering Victor's, demonstrating that interaction between separate cosmoses is possible.[1]

### Survival
The Void Between Cosmos is an extremely hostile environment.
Azathoth stated that only the highest levels of [[Primordial Chaos]] are naturally capable of surviving within it.[1] Even she considered further growth necessary before using passage through the Void as a reliable method for evacuating [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s entire family from their native cosmos.
As an Eldritch [[Chaos]], Azathoth possesses characteristics that make surviving such an environment easier than it would be for more conventional beings, but this does not make the Void harmless.[1]
This places inter-cosmological travel on a completely different scale from dimensional travel. Gods capable of moving through the [[Dimensional Void]] are not thereby established as capable of leaving their entire cosmos.

### Dangers
The environment itself is not the only danger existing between cosmoses.
Azathoth explained that other beings of [[Chaos]] may be encountered while traversing the Void. These entities may attack another Chaos in an attempt to take its cosmos and consume it in order to increase their own power.[1]
Cosmological conflict can therefore occur on a scale in which the target is not merely a civilization, planet, dimension or Universe, but an entire cosmos and the system of Creation associated with it.
This is consistent with Azathoth's own history. She originally came from outside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s cosmos and intended to use that cosmos as nourishment for her own growth.[1]

### Conceptual Void Between Multiverses
A later description in Chapter 1135 introduces the term Conceptual Void between Multiverses.[2]
While discussing advanced civilizations and the existence of his future "Outer Self", [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] theorized that this future version of himself existed outside the Universe and protected those within it against invaders that could originate from beyond their Multiverse, coming through this Conceptual Void.[2]
The same discussion uses [[Azathoth Elderblood]] as direct proof that beings originating from completely different Universes can enter Victor's reality.[2]
Because Chapter 1075 had already established a greater Void separating complete cosmoses, the Conceptual Void between Multiverses appears to describe the same general outer inter-cosmological environment, or at minimum another description of the region beyond individual Multiversal structures. However, the novel has not yet explicitly stated that the two names are perfectly interchangeable.

### Foreign Invasion
The Void Between Cosmos makes invasion from entirely foreign Creations possible.
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] directly asked whether beings from other cosmoses could invade his own, Azathoth confirmed that they could and pointed to herself as an example.[1]
This revelation significantly expanded Victor's understanding of external threats. Protecting his family could no longer be treated solely as defending against enemies originating within their Universe or against the [[Regulators of Existence]]. There existed entities belonging to completely independent cosmological systems capable of crossing the boundary between Creations.
In the future, Victor's protection of the [[Akashic Records]] against foreign intruders reflects this larger concern with beings approaching his Creation from beyond its normal boundaries.[3]

### Relationship with Nothingness
The Void Between Cosmos and Nothingness should be kept separate.
Nothingness is described relative to the [[Akashic Records]]. The Records exist within an infinite surrounding state where concepts and chronology become scrambled or cease to function once outside their coherent influence.[3]
The Void Between Cosmos is instead explained by comparing two complete and independent cosmoses and identifying the expanse that must be crossed to move from one cosmological system to another.[1]
Therefore, the two occupy different roles in the cosmological hierarchy:
- Nothingness — Surrounds the Akashic Records and exists outside their coherent conceptual influence.
- Void Between Cosmos — Separates complete cosmoses from other complete cosmoses.

### Relationship with the Dimensional Void
The [[Dimensional Void]] is a much more internal cosmological structure.
The Dimensional Void connects the various dimensional planes belonging to Creation. Pantheons and other dimensions can be reached by traveling through it, and powerful Gods have demonstrated the ability to navigate or enter dimensions from this Void.[4][5]
The Void Between Cosmos, by comparison, is beyond the boundaries of an entire cosmos and requires entities approaching the highest levels of [[Chaos]] merely to survive within it.[1]
Consequently, dimensional travel and inter-cosmological travel should not be treated as equivalent feats.

### Known Beings Associated with the Void
- [[Azathoth Elderblood]] — Personally explains the existence and dangers of the Void and originates from a foreign cosmos.[1]
- [[Primordial Chaos]] — Only the highest-level beings of this class are stated to be capable of surviving the Void.[1]
- [[Victor Elderblood]] — Learns about the structure from Azathoth and later believes his future Outer Self protects his Creation from entities originating beyond its Multiverse.[1][2]

### Trivia
- The Void Between Cosmos was first directly explained to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] by Azathoth after he considered methods of protecting his family from the [[Primordial Entities]].[1]
- Separate cosmoses may possess completely different Primordials, Rules and originating [[Chaos]].[1]
- Merely being capable of entering the [[Dimensional Void]] does not establish the ability to survive the Void Between Cosmos.[1]
- Azathoth's existence confirms that beings originating from foreign cosmoses can enter Victor's cosmos.[1]
- Chapter 1135 later uses the related expression "Conceptual Void between Multiverses", although the story has not yet explicitly confirmed whether this is formally another name for the exact same structure.[2]
