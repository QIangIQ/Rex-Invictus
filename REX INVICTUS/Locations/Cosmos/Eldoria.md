ELDORIA
The ancient homeworld of the [[Xyrra]] and heart of the Thaloria Dynasty — a civilization whose mastery of technology carried it from planetary unification to galactic supremacy before the Convergence exposed it to the predators of the Upper Sectors.
Eldoria is the home planet of [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]], [[Thaloria Xyphora]] and the technologically gifted Xyrra race. It was once the political and technological center of the [[Thaloria Empire]], first ruled by Thaloria as the First Empress and later by Velnorah as the [[Anna Elderblood|Second Empress]] Thaloria. Under their dynasty, Eldoria developed from an extraordinarily advanced planetary civilization into the center of a galactic power.[1]
Eldoria originally existed within a Mid-Level Sector. After Velnorah inherited the Imperial legacy and rose to the status of an Overlord, a cosmic Convergence occurred and the surrounding Sector developed into a higher region. Eldoria consequently became located within what Velnorah later identified as Upper Sector 8975. The change exposed the former Thaloria domains to Galactic Emperors and civilizations operating on a scale enormously beyond the opponents the dynasty had previously conquered.[2][1]
Important distinction
Eldoria is a planet, not the entire Sector. It began as the homeworld and capital of Velnorah's civilization inside a Mid-Level Sector. Following the Convergence, the surrounding cosmic region became an Upper/High-Level Sector, while Eldoria itself remained the dynasty's home planet within Upper Sector 8975.[2][1]

### Description
Eldoria was shown through Velnorah's historical projection as a remarkably futuristic planet that combined an extremely advanced society with sustainable technological development. Unlike industrial civilizations that destroyed their ecosystems while advancing, Eldoria's technological growth existed alongside preserved natural environments. The planet was presented as a world where technological civilization and nature had developed together rather than as mutually exclusive systems.[1]
This coexistence is one of the few aspects of Eldoria's planetary infrastructure described directly in the story. Specific claims that the planet universally employed fusion reactors, floating cities, anti-gravity rail networks, a single planet-wide artificial intelligence, automated police drones or an impenetrable planetary shield have not been established by the currently revealed chapters and should not be treated as confirmed features of Eldoria.
What is established is that its native [[Xyrra]] population possessed an extraordinary natural affinity for technology, allowing even ordinary members of the species to interact with and create advanced machinery with an ease that appeared almost supernatural to outsiders. Under the Thaloria Dynasty, that racial advantage was industrialized on a planetary and later interstellar scale.[1]

### The Xyrra
The [[Xyrra]] were the dominant native species of Eldoria. They were giant humanoids whose normal adults generally exceeded approximately three meters in height. In addition to powerful bodies, the Xyrra possessed an inherent talent for technology. Velnorah demonstrated the degree of this affinity through a historical recording in which a member of her race could produce an advanced futuristic weapon with little apparent difficulty.[1]
Technology was consequently more than an academic discipline in Eldorian society. For the Xyrra it was a racial specialty comparable in principle to the natural supernatural abilities possessed by other species. [[Thaloria Xyphora]] represented the most extreme known example. Despite being unusually small and physically frail for her race, her technological talent was so exceptional that Velnorah compared her to a goddess within a technological world.[1]
The combination of Xyrra talent and Thaloria's leadership accelerated Eldoria's development dramatically. Within less than approximately 2,000 years, the Thaloria Dynasty had brought the entire planet beneath its control and was already exploring space.[1]

### The Thaloria Dynasty
The Thaloria Dynasty
From one planet to galactic dominion

#### Thaloria Xyphora
[[Thaloria Xyphora]] became known as the First Empress of the Planet Eldoria. Although physically inferior to most [[Xyrra]], she compensated through technology and became one of the greatest scientific minds produced by the species. Her rule transformed Eldoria from a planetary civilization into an expanding space power.[1]
Thaloria's technological superiority allowed the dynasty to absorb or overcome numerous civilizations encountered during expansion. Exceptional individuals and races were incorporated into the growing Empire, extending Eldoria's influence increasingly far beyond its original planetary borders.[1]

#### Velnorah Xyphora Thaloria II
Thaloria was eventually succeeded by her daughter, [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]]. Velnorah inherited her mother's accumulated research, technology and Imperial legacy before becoming the [[Anna Elderblood|Second Empress]] Thaloria.[1]
Velnorah ultimately rose beyond the position of planetary or galactic monarch and achieved recognition as an Overlord. Her rise represented the point at which Eldoria's history stopped being confined to ordinary galactic politics and became directly connected to the larger hierarchy of the Cosmos.[1]

### Galactic Expansion
As Eldoria expanded beyond its home system, the Thaloria Dynasty came into conflict with other major galactic powers. Among the most important was the [[Kolo Empire]], ruled by [[Galactic Emperor Kolo]]. Kolo's greatest military asset was the Yuurakyo, a race whose physiology and battle talent allowed individual warriors to threaten forces that normally dominated through technological superiority.[1]
A Yuurakyo infiltrator proved difficult enough to capture that several of Thaloria's generals died, technological restraints were destroyed and Thaloria's own equipment was damaged. Rather than merely kill the prisoner, Thaloria began researching his race in an attempt to reproduce their physical advantages.[1]
Her experiments eventually revealed that the extraordinary strength of mature Yuurakyo was partly produced by a non-hereditary enhancement serum administered during a coming-of-age ceremony. Using an artificially raised Yuurakyo as a biological baseline, Thaloria developed a virus that attacked the species' brains and exterminated the Yuurakyo population.[1]
The destruction of the Yuurakyo removed the greatest known military power serving Kolo. With the primary obstacle to Eldorian technological supremacy eliminated, the Thaloria Dynasty continued its expansion until it dominated its galaxy.[1]

### The Sector Convergence
CONVERGENCE
Eldoria's greatest crisis began only after its civilization had effectively won the struggle for its original region. Velnorah's ascension as an Overlord caused the surrounding Mid-Level Sector to undergo a Convergence and enter a higher cosmic environment where the standards of power changed completely.[1]
The old sector surrounding Eldoria had originally been a Mid-Level Sector. After Velnorah became its Overlord, a Convergence occurred and the region developed into a High-Level/Upper Sector. The event did not merely increase the size or Energy of the region. It exposed Eldoria to civilizations, Galactic Emperors and supernatural beings originating from a substantially more dangerous level of the Cosmos.[1]
This explains the terminology used by Velnorah during her introduction. By the time she met [[Victor Elderblood]], she identified herself as the ancient Overlord of Eldoria and described the planet as being located in Upper Sector 8975.[2]
The Convergence effectively destroyed the security that galactic supremacy had once provided. Powers began appearing whose greatest individuals could rival or surpass Eldoria's highest elites. Some Galactic Emperors possessed mastery over technology comparable to the fields pioneered by Thaloria, while others relied upon overwhelming supernatural power. Velnorah described enemies capable of destroying planets through individual attacks rather than conventional fleets or weapons.[1]

### War of the Galactic Emperors
The Thaloria Dynasty had reached the pinnacle of its original environment, but the new High-Level Sector contained entities against whom that previous supremacy was no longer sufficient. Velnorah and Thaloria themselves remained powerful enough to directly confront some of the Galactic Emperors, but the same was not true for the overwhelming majority of their civilization.[1]
This exposed the greatest weakness produced by Thaloria's earlier victory over the Yuurakyo. The warrior race had once been dangerous enough to threaten Eldoria even before the Convergence. Had Thaloria preserved, integrated or reproduced them instead of exterminating them, the Yuurakyo could have served as a large population of soldiers physically capable of participating in the wars that followed.[1]
Velnorah consequently regarded the Yuurakyo genocide as one of the decisive mistakes that doomed the old Empire. Eldoria possessed technology capable of confronting extraordinary enemies, but too few individuals capable of personally surviving battles fought on the level of the new Galactic Emperors.[1]

### Fall of Eldoria
The wars following the Convergence eventually overwhelmed the Thaloria Dynasty. Velnorah and her mother could fight the invading Galactic Emperors, but they could not single-handedly compensate for the enormous gap separating their civilization's ordinary forces from the highest powers entering the new Sector.[1]
Velnorah was eventually driven beyond the borders of her former territory and fled from the civilization she had ruled. By the time she encountered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction, she no longer described herself as Eldoria's active ruler but as its ancient Overlord. Her homeworld remained behind inside Upper Sector 8975 while the Thaloria Dynasty had lost practical control over it.[2][1]
The precise identity of Eldoria's present ruler has not yet been revealed. Likewise, the story has not shown the modern condition of the planet in detail, so claims that Eldoria has been physically destroyed, reduced to ruins, completely depopulated or converted into a machine world remain unconfirmed.
Current Status
Eldoria itself still exists and is identified as a planet within Upper Sector 8975. What fell was the political supremacy of the Thaloria Dynasty. Until the story returns to the planet directly, its exact population, ruler, infrastructure and territorial condition remain unknown.[2]

### World Trees
Like other naturally developed inhabited worlds, Eldoria belongs to the universal planetary cycle associated with [[World Trees]]. A [[Amara Elderblood|World Tree of Positivity]] appears first and nourishes the developing planet and its life. Once sentient existence develops sufficiently, a corresponding World Tree of Negativity emerges to govern and process the world's more spiritual and negative aspects.[3]
This distinction is important because World Trees are not merely ornamental plants or political institutions. They are fundamental planetary anchors connected to the processes that allow supernatural worlds to sustain life, spiritual ecosystems and dimensional development.[3]
Accordingly, the World Trees associated with Eldoria should be understood as part of the planet's cosmic ecology rather than as founders of the Thaloria Dynasty. The [[Xyrra]] civilization and its Empire developed around a living world that already participated in this universal system.

### Legacy
Eldoria's greatest surviving legacy is [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]]. Although she lost her original Empire, Velnorah preserved its accumulated knowledge, research methods, Imperial traditions and technological expertise. After becoming part of [[Victor Elderblood]]'s faction, those skills were incorporated into the rapidly developing civilization surrounding Dragons Nest.[1]
Velnorah later governed the civilization of Elvenorah, where she once again applied knowledge acquired from Eldoria while integrating it with [[Magic]], Divine systems and technologies unavailable to her original people. The new society became an opportunity for Velnorah to build something beyond the failures of the civilization she had lost rather than simply reproduce Eldoria exactly as it had existed.[4][5]
The old world also remained central to Velnorah's motivations. Her initial search for powerful allies was driven heavily by the destruction of her former position and the enemies responsible for taking her civilization from her. Her encounter with Victor consequently connected Eldoria's unresolved history with the future expansion of the Elderblood civilization.[1]
Historical Significance
Eldoria demonstrates one of the central realities of Sector progression: becoming supreme within one cosmic environment does not mean remaining supreme after that environment ascends. The Thaloria Dynasty reached galactic dominance inside its Mid-Level Sector, only for the Convergence to expose it to powers for whom that achievement represented merely the beginning of interstellar competition.[1]

### Trivia
- Eldoria is a planet, while Upper Sector 8975 is the cosmic region in which it is currently located.[2]
- Velnorah introduced herself as the ancient Overlord of Eldoria, a planet located in Upper Sector 8975.[2]
- The planet originally belonged to a Mid-Level Sector before the Convergence associated with Velnorah's rise as an Overlord.[1]
- Eldoria's native [[Xyrra]] commonly exceeded three meters in height and possessed an innate affinity for technology.[1]
- [[Thaloria Xyphora]] was considered unusually small for a Xyrra at approximately 2.5 meters despite becoming the First Empress of their civilization.[1]
- In less than approximately two thousand years, the Thaloria Dynasty had unified the planet and begun exploring space.[1]
- Eldoria's introduction specifically emphasizes that its technological progress did not require the destruction of nature.[1]
- The [[Yuurakyos]] were once the greatest military obstacle faced by the expanding Thaloria Dynasty. Thaloria exterminated them through a biological weapon, but Velnorah later concluded that preserving them could have dramatically improved Eldoria's chances against the Galactic Emperors.[1]
