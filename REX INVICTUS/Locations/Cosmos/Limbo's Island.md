Limbo's Island, sometimes referred to as the [[Supernatural Gathering]] Island, is an anomalous floating island and created domain belonging to The Owner of Limbo. Its precise physical location is unknown, and the space does not obey the ordinary behavior of time, space or natural ecosystems. The island is primarily known as the location used by the Owner of Limbo to host the Supernatural Gathering, where the rulers and representatives of Earth's major supernatural powers convene under his protection and mediation.[1]
Despite its association with the Owner of Limbo, Limbo's Island should not be confused with the Limbo Realm, the vast prison created to contain beings and entities that violate the laws governing Creation. The Island functions primarily as the Owner's personal domain and neutral meeting ground, whereas the Limbo Realm serves as his prison and containment territory.

### Description
Limbo's Island was first shown in an unknown location high above an enormous sea of clouds. From a conventional perspective, it resembles a gigantic medieval island floating unsupported in the sky, but its internal nature immediately distinguishes it from a normal geographical landmass. The island contains numerous exotic animals and other forms of life that should not naturally coexist or, in some cases, should not exist at all. The normal concept of an ecosystem consequently does not function there in the conventional sense.[1]
The abnormality extends beyond its biology. Ordinary time and space do not properly interfere with the Island, and events within it were described as occurring both simultaneously and not occurring at all. Its existence therefore contradicts the ordinary logic through which physical locations are understood.[1]
The Island was described as existing outside the ordinary jurisdiction of existence itself, fitting the nature of its ruler, [[The Limbo Guy]], a Primordial Entity associated with Balance and one of the beings responsible for maintaining the laws governing existence.[1]
Despite these abnormalities, invited supernatural beings are capable of moving, speaking and interacting normally while attending the Gathering. This indicates that the Owner maintains the environment in a usable state for his guests even though its underlying nature does not behave according to conventional space-time.

### Geography
The full size and geography of Limbo's Island have never been mapped in detail. The portion observed during the first [[Supernatural Gathering]] was nevertheless described as extraordinarily large and contained both natural-looking landscapes and a monumental structure belonging to the Owner.[1]
Known features include:
- An immense floating landmass positioned above clouds.
- Forests and other natural terrain.
- Exotic and anomalous animals.
- An ecosystem that does not obey normal ecological rules.
- A massive white palace located prominently upon the Island.
- Interior spaces used to receive, accommodate and assemble representatives attending the Supernatural Gathering.[1][2][3]
Because very little of the Island outside the Gathering complex has been explored directly in the story, its total landscape, boundaries and internal geography remain unknown.

### Anomalous nature

#### Space and time
The Island's most important characteristic is its separation from ordinary spatial and temporal behavior. Chapter 509 explicitly establishes that time and space themselves do not interfere normally within the Island. Events can effectively be considered to occur simultaneously and not occur at all, making the location fundamentally incompatible with the ordinary rules through which the Universe is experienced.[1]
This should not be interpreted as meaning that every individual visiting the Island automatically gains control over time or exists omnipresently throughout it. The phenomenon is a property of the domain itself.

#### Existence outside conventional reality
The Island is portrayed as a place where existence defies common sense. Its unusual nature is appropriate for a domain belonging to the Owner of Limbo, whose own existence and responsibilities operate at a level far beyond ordinary supernatural societies.[1]
The fact that Kings, Progenitors and Gods enter the Island without attempting to contest the Owner's control also demonstrates that it is treated as his territory rather than belonging to any Pantheon or conventional supernatural government.

#### Anomalous ecosystem
The animals and biological environment of the Island do not conform to an ordinary ecosystem. Creatures that should not exist or coexist naturally are present within the same environment, yet the Island remains stable.[1]
The novel does not provide enough information to determine whether these beings were created directly by the Owner, transported there from elsewhere, or are simply natural products of the Island's abnormal nature.

### The White Palace
The most important known structure on Limbo's Island is an enormous white palace. It serves as the primary venue for the [[Supernatural Gathering]] and contains the rooms used for introductions, preparation and the formal negotiations between representatives.[1]
The palace should not be interpreted as an ordinary medieval castle despite its external presentation. Its interior is subject to the Owner's supernatural control, and different rooms can be created, adjusted or presented according to the needs of the Gathering.

#### Reception area
During the first Gathering, the arrival of the different factions was formally presented before the meeting itself. The environment surrounding these entrances changed dramatically according to the faction being introduced. For example, the presentation associated with [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]] and the Youkai adopted the appearance of Japanese temples, while other arrivals received environments reflecting their own backgrounds.[2]
This demonstrates that the Gathering environment is spatially malleable and can be altered by the Owner.
However, the available chapters do not explicitly establish that the entire geographical Island constantly reshapes itself whenever a visitor arrives. The effect is most clearly demonstrated within the spaces used for the Gathering and its introductions.[2]

#### Waiting areas
Before the formal negotiations begin, participants and their companions are provided areas in which they can wait, interact and conduct informal political discussions. These spaces are important because several major negotiations occurred before the official meeting began.[4][5][6]
The Island therefore functions not only as a council chamber but also as diplomatic neutral ground where factions can approach one another without immediately entering a formal vote.

#### Meeting chamber
The formal [[Supernatural Gathering]] takes place within a vast chamber containing a large round table. The recognized Representatives of the participating factions occupy the primary seats, while companions and other important members accompanying them remain behind their respective representatives.[3]
The round-table arrangement reinforces the role of the Gathering as a negotiation between recognized powers rather than a court belonging to one of the participating factions.
The Owner of Limbo nevertheless remains the mediator and possesses authority over the proceedings.

### Control of the Owner of Limbo
[[The Limbo Guy]] possesses overwhelming control over Limbo's Island. He can manipulate its space, create structures and objects required for the Gathering, and transport individuals into or out of the venue.[1][7][8]
His control is sufficiently complete that even beings such as [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]], [[Shiva]], [[Zeus]], [[Volk Fenrir]] and other high-level supernatural rulers respect the restrictions imposed during the Gathering.
The Island's neutrality therefore does not come from every faction possessing equal military strength. It is maintained by the Owner's personal authority.

### Neutrality
Limbo's Island serves as neutral territory during the [[Supernatural Gathering]].
Direct acts of aggression between participants are prohibited. During the first Gathering, the Owner explicitly warned those present that acts of aggression would not be tolerated and reminded them that anyone violating his rules could become a resident of his prison.[9]
The threat is particularly significant because the Owner controls the Limbo Realm, a prison capable of containing extraordinarily dangerous entities.
Consequently, even factions that are normally hostile to each other can occupy the same chamber without immediately beginning a battle.
This neutrality does not prevent:
- Political threats.
- Hostile arguments.
- Refusal of proposals.
- Diplomatic pressure.
- Declarations of war.
- Actions performed by armies outside the Island.
It only prevents the participants from turning the Gathering itself into an uncontrolled battlefield.

### Access
Limbo's Island is not presented as a location that ordinary supernatural beings can freely visit.
Attendance at the [[Supernatural Gathering]] is controlled by the Owner of Limbo, and the participants consist primarily of recognized rulers, representatives and companions belonging to major supernatural powers.[1][7]
The Owner can directly control transportation connected to the Gathering, allowing beings from distant territories and separate supernatural dimensions to attend the same meeting.
The location of the Island itself remains unknown.

### Role in the Supernatural Gathering
The Island's primary known function is hosting the [[Supernatural Gathering]].
During these meetings, representatives of major supernatural factions can:
- Propose new rules.
- Challenge or abolish existing rules.
- Vote upon matters affecting multiple factions.
- Establish agreements recognized by the supernatural community.
- Resolve disputes.
- Present threats or security problems.
- Negotiate alliances.
- Declare political or military intentions.[3][8][10]
The Island is therefore one of the few known locations where rulers who normally possess independent jurisdictions can negotiate under a neutral authority capable of enforcing order.

#### First Supernatural Gathering
The first Gathering shown directly in the novel took place across Chapters 509–521.
During this event, representatives of Vampires, [[Werewolves]], Gods, Angels, Witches, Youkai and Demons gathered on the Island.[1][10]
Several important political developments occurred during the event, including:
- Negotiations between [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]].
- The creation of a long-term non-interference agreement concerning the Youkai.[3][8]
- Discussion regarding rules affecting the supernatural world.
- [[Shiva]] raising the problem of duplicated Greek Divine Artifacts.[10]
- The arrival of [[Lilith Elderblood]] as the representative of the Demons.[10]
- The beginning of the large-scale Demonic conflict occurring outside the Island.[9][11]

#### Second Supernatural Gathering
The Island was later used again for the second major Gathering shown in Chapters 864–872.
By this period, the balance of power on Earth had changed dramatically. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had become the Blood Dragon Primogenitor, created Dragons Nest and conquered Hell, while several Pantheons were undergoing major political changes.[12]
The second Gathering consequently involved significantly more Pantheon-level representatives and addressed issues involving the future structure of Earth, Demons, Heaven, Hell and Victor's conflicts with several organizations.[13][14][15]

Limbo's Island and the Limbo Realm are separate locations.
This distinction is important because both belong to the same ruler and are frequently discussed together.
