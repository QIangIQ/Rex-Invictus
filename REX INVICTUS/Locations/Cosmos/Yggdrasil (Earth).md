Earth, officially renamed Yggdrasil following the establishment of the Elderblood Empire, is a life-bearing planet located in the Northern Region of Victor Elderblood's native Mid-Level Sector.[1] It is the birthplace of Victor, the original homeworld of Humanity and the world around which most of the early supernatural civilization of the novel developed.
For most of the story, Earth was a divided world in which ordinary Humans inhabited the Mortal Realm while Gods, Vampires, [[Werewolves]], Witches, Yokais, Angels, Demons and numerous other supernatural races operated either secretly among Humanity or from dimensions metaphysically connected to the planet. This system was supported by Earth's paired [[World Trees]], principally Yggdrasil and her Negative counterpart Qiliphoth, whose existence was intertwined with the planet's Life, spiritual systems and supernatural dimensions.[2]
Earth's old civilization was shattered by the Demonic War, after which Victor gradually became the dominant authority over the surviving Human and supernatural powers. He eventually eliminated the planet's remaining independent sovereignty, established the Elderblood Empire, physically united the continents, created a common Imperial language and renamed the planet Yggdrasil.[3]
For almost two hundred thousand years of accelerated Imperial development, Yggdrasil remained one of the Empire's most important economic, political and population centers. This status finally changed in Chapter 1178. With the planet becoming increasingly crowded and Victor unwilling to endlessly expand the world in which he had spent most of his life, he designated Mars as the Empire's new central planet and ordered Yggdrasil preserved as a protected garden world. The long-term plan is for its sentient population to migrate elsewhere until the planet becomes an empty natural monument to the civilization's origins.[4][5]

### Terminology

#### Earth
Earth is the planet's original and historical name. It is the name used throughout most of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Human life and the majority of the supernatural world's earlier history.
In supernatural discussions, "Earth" can also refer specifically to the planet's ordinary physical Mortal Realm, as distinct from the many Divine dimensions, Heavens, Hells and Underworlds metaphysically connected to it.[6]

#### Mortal Realm
The Mortal Realm is Earth's material world: the physical planet inhabited by Humanity and terrestrial supernatural populations.
This distinction is important because the Pantheons associated with Earth's religions are not ordinary countries physically hidden somewhere on the planet. Their principal Divine territories exist in separate dimensions connected to Earth's supernatural system.[6]

#### Yggdrasil
After Earth was completely unified beneath [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], the planet was formally renamed [[Yggdrasil]] in honor of its principal Positive World Tree.[3]
The renaming coincided with the beginning of the [[Imperial Calendar]] and represented the end of Earth's fragmented Human and supernatural political order.

### Cosmological Position

#### Northern Region
Earth is located within the Northern Region of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s native Sector.[1]
When Victor first performed a large-scale survey of the Sector, he broadly divided the Reality into four major regions:
- Northern Region — Earth's region.
- Southern Region.
- Eastern Region.
- Western Region.[1]
Each region contained at least one developed World Tree civilization, with additional worlds existing at far earlier evolutionary stages.

#### Mid-Level Sector
Main article: [[Victor Elderblood]]/Overlord's Domain
Earth belongs to a Mid-Level Sector. The boundaries of that Reality separate it from less-developed and more-developed cosmic regions.[1]
Although the barrier toward the higher regions progressively weakened as the Sector developed, this should not be interpreted as Earth already belonging to a High-Level Sector. Even during the mature Elderblood era, Victor's Reality continued to be described as a Mid-Level or mid-tier Sector.[7]

#### Milky Way Galaxy
Earth is located in the Milky Way Galaxy, which later became the original Galactic core of the [[Elderblood Empire]].
By the Imperial period, the significance of Earth could no longer be understood only on a planetary scale. The civilization that began there expanded throughout the Milky Way, incorporated neighboring Galactic territories and eventually spread across numerous galaxies within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Sector.[7]

### Planetary Structure
Earth originally possessed geography broadly corresponding to the ordinary Human world: several continents, oceans, diverse climates and a large natural biosphere.
What separates the setting's Earth from an entirely mundane planet is its hidden supernatural infrastructure. The world contains planetary Energy pathways, World Tree systems and metaphysical connections to numerous supernatural dimensions.

#### Ley Lines
Earth possesses vast planetary Energy channels referred to as Ley Lines and compared to the blood vessels of Mother Earth.[8]
During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s [[Senjutsu]] training, [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]] brought him into one of these regions and explained that natural Energy circulated through the planet through these channels.[8]
The Ley Lines demonstrate that Earth's biosphere is not sustained solely through conventional physical processes. Life, Nature and supernatural Energy are integrated into the planet at a fundamental level.

#### Yggdrasil
[[Yggdrasil]] is Earth's principal Positive World Tree and one of the oldest [[World Trees]] directly involved in the story.
Although traditionally associated with the [[Norse Pantheon]], her responsibility extends far beyond a single Divine faction. When Earth required planetary restoration, the supernatural rulers planned to place the gathered restorative power into Yggdrasil because she was capable of properly distributing that power throughout the planet.[6]
Her role is therefore fundamentally planetary, not merely Norse.

#### Qiliphoth
Qiliphoth is Earth's Negative Elder World Tree and [[Yggdrasil]]'s counterpart.[2]
The two represent complementary components of Earth's supernatural structure rather than political rulers or literal founders of the physical planet.

#### Connection to Victor
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually formed extremely deep connections with Earth's [[World Trees]].
By Chapter 986, his relationship with the World Trees of Earth, [[Nightingale]] and Samar had progressed far enough for his authority over the three worlds to be described in terms approaching ownership.[9]
The connection continued deepening as Victor's own existence evolved. In the late Imperial era, the World Trees associated with him spent most of their time connected directly to Victor, receiving Energy and protection from his existence.[5]
When the new Mars World Tree Network was created, the connected World Trees projected themselves from Victor and planted roots into the reconstructed planet. [[Yggdrasil]] explicitly participated in this system despite remaining the World Tree historically associated with Earth.[5]

### History

#### Ancient Earth
Earth developed around an exceptionally old supernatural ecosystem supported by its [[World Trees]].
Over immense periods of time, Humans, supernatural species and Divine civilizations developed around the planet. Numerous Pantheons became associated with Human cultures, and myths remembered by Humanity often reflected real supernatural Beings or events.
The complete history of Earth's earliest eras remains only partially documented.

#### Age of Pantheons
As Human civilizations grew, their faiths became connected to numerous real Divine factions.
The major Pantheons developed their own rulers, Gods, dimensions, Heavens and Underworlds, while lesser Pantheons maintained smaller supernatural civilizations.
Despite their shared connection to Earth, these factions frequently operated as independent powers rather than as members of one planetary civilization.

#### Hidden Supernatural Era
At the beginning of the novel, ordinary Human civilization was largely unaware that the supernatural world existed.
Humanity possessed nation-states, governments, religions, armies and modern technology, while supernatural races maintained parallel societies outside ordinary public knowledge.
[[Victor Elderblood]] himself began his life as an ordinary Human unaware of this larger structure.
His transformation into a Vampire ultimately connected him to the supernatural civilization that had remained hidden around him since birth.

#### Demonic War
The old status quo effectively ended when [[Diablo]] began the large-scale Demonic invasion of the Human World.
The conflict devastated Human civilization and killed more than six billion people.[11]
Governments, cities, economies and military organizations collapsed across enormous portions of the planet. The destruction was sufficiently extensive that Humanity could no longer realistically return to the geopolitical structure that had existed before the supernatural world became public.

#### Post-War Reconstruction
The surviving supernatural rulers eventually began discussing the reconstruction of Earth.
During the Meeting of Supernatural Beings, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] proposed rebuilding the Mortal Realm as a unified civilization rather than restoring the same divided political system that had existed before the war.[6]
His proposal included the elimination of old national divisions, ecological reconstruction, large-scale urban development and greater cooperation between formerly separate supernatural groups.
Powerful Nature and Mother Goddesses could supply the required Energy, while [[Yggdrasil]] would distribute it throughout the planetary system in a manner suitable for Earth's long-term recovery.[6]
By Chapter 986, the environmental recovery was already visible. Pollution and accumulated waste were being removed from damaged environments as the planet's supernatural systems recovered.[9]

#### Rise of Victor
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s influence expanded dramatically following the Demonic War.
He progressively became connected to Earth's [[World Trees]], remaining Human civilization, surviving Pantheons, Vampire society, Werewolf civilization, Yokai factions, Witches, Demons, Angels and numerous Gods and supernatural species.
By Chapter 986, his authority over Earth, [[Nightingale]] and Samar had already developed far beyond the position of an ordinary political ally.[9]

#### Conquest of Earth
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually concluded that indirect leadership and treaty-based cooperation were no longer sufficient.
He began placing Earth beneath direct Imperial control, destroying or subordinating the remaining forces capable of disputing his sovereignty.[13]
The [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]] became the most significant remaining independent Divine power. Victor offered its leadership submission or destruction, and [[Shiva]] ultimately accepted incorporation rather than forcing a hopeless war.[14][3]
Other factions met different outcomes according to their relationship with Victor. Some submitted peacefully, some were already allied with him and others were destroyed.

#### Formation of the Elderblood Empire
With the remaining opposition eliminated, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] formally established the [[Elderblood Empire]].[3]
This became Year 1 of the [[Imperial Calendar]].
Victor annexed or subordinated the remaining Human political territories, supernatural races, Pantheons, hidden organizations and surviving independent supernatural communities.[3]
The distinction between Human Earth and the hidden supernatural world effectively disappeared.

#### Unification of the Continents
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] physically united Earth's continents into one enormous continental landmass.[3]
The old system of independent countries ceased to exist. Former territories such as Japan, the United States, China, India, Brazil and the various European nations remained historically and culturally identifiable, but no longer functioned as sovereign states.
For the first time in recorded history, the physical planet existed under one political authority.

#### The Imperial Language
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] also created a unified Imperial Language and transferred knowledge of it directly to the population.[3]
The language eliminated one of the greatest practical barriers between Earth's formerly separate cultures and became the common linguistic system of the new Empire.

#### Renaming to Yggdrasil
Earth was subsequently renamed [[Yggdrasil]].[3]
The name honored the World Tree that had protected the planet across its history while symbolically separating the fragmented pre-Imperial world from the civilization [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] intended to build.

#### Imperial Capital
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] created a massive Imperial Capital at the center of the unified continent.[3]
The city became the principal seat of the newly formed Empire during its planetary and early interstellar period.
It should now be distinguished from the Empire's current capital on Mars, established in Chapter 1178.

#### Tower of Challenges: Babylon
Main article: [[The Tower of Nightmares]]/Tower of Challenges
The Tower of Challenges, Babylon was created at the center of the Imperial Capital.[3]
Babylon possesses 999 floors and serves as a large-scale training and development system for both warriors and scholars. Its purpose was to give ordinary Imperial citizens a method of developing beyond the limitations of their birth rather than reserving supernatural advancement for ancient bloodlines and Divine races.
As the Empire expanded to other worlds, copies of Babylon were constructed on additional planets and linked into a much larger Imperial network.[12]

#### Aphrodisias
In the 1000th Year of the [[Imperial Calendar]], the city of Aphrodisias was recreated within [[Yggdrasil]] and became closely associated with the First Imperial Legion.[12]
Its establishment demonstrated that Yggdrasil continued to develop beyond the original Imperial Capital rather than becoming a static ceremonial homeworld.

#### Two Hundred Thousand Years of Development
The Empire eventually undertook its most ambitious developmental project.
Through a civilization-scale temporal ritual supported by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Natalia, [[Jeanne Elderblood]], the [[World Trees]] and the wider Imperial Family, the civilization was granted approximately 200,000 years of internal development while only a comparatively short period passed outside.[15][16]
[[Yggdrasil]] continued functioning throughout this era as one of the major historical, economic and population centers of the Empire.
As the civilization expanded beyond the Solar System, however, the planet gradually ceased being the sole center of Imperial life. Entire worlds, Solar Systems and galaxies were colonized, specialized planets were constructed and transportation networks reduced the importance of astronomical distance.

#### Population Pressure
By the final stages of the development project, [[Yggdrasil]], [[Nightingale]] and Samar had become enormous economic centers.
The prestige associated with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] caused a particular problem: citizens naturally preferred living in locations where the Emperor and Imperial Family were most likely to appear. Billions of beings concentrated around the Empire's major worlds and cities, producing increasingly severe demand for space.[4]
The Empire refused to solve the problem simply by making desirable housing inaccessible through extreme prices.
Instead, Victor approached it as a planetary engineering problem: if there was insufficient space, he would simply create more.

#### Mars and the End of Yggdrasil's Capital Era
Main article: Mars
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] originally considered enlarging an existing populated world. While examining the still-barren Mars, however, he decided on a fundamentally different solution.[4]
I will make Mars the main planet and preserve [[Yggdrasil]] as a garden world, the cradle of the empire.
—Victor Elderblood, Chapter 1178
Victor did not want Yggdrasil's population to continue expanding indefinitely. The planet held enormous personal value to him because it was his birthplace and the world in which he had spent most of his life.[5]
With the 200,000th anniversary of the [[Imperial Calendar]] approaching, he considered the transfer of the capital symbolic of a new stage in Imperial history.

### Imperial Society
Following unification, Earth ceased being a civilization divided primarily by Human nationality.
The [[Elderblood Empire]] increasingly defined citizenship above species or former national identity.
Imperial society incorporated Humans, Vampires, [[Werewolves]], Yokais, Witches, Angels, Demons, Gods, Dragons and numerous hybrid races beneath the same overall political structure.[17]
The new civilization emphasized:
- Education.
- Merit.
- Individual growth.
- Scientific advancement.
- Supernatural development.
- Military preparedness.
- Racial integration.
- Loyalty to the Empire.
The creation of Babylon was particularly important because it provided ordinary citizens with a formal path toward personal development rather than leaving power entirely in the hands of naturally gifted species.

### Government

#### Pre-Imperial Earth
Earth historically possessed no unified government.
The physical world was divided among Human nation-states while supernatural civilization was split between countless independent organizations and rulers.
Authority included Human governments, [[God-Kings]], Vampire rulers, Werewolf authorities, Witch leadership, Yokai rulers, religious organizations and independent supernatural families.
This fragmentation became one of the systemic problems [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later eliminated.

### Religion
Earth's history is deeply connected to religion because many of the Gods worshiped by Humanity objectively exist.
For most of Human history, different religions provided Faith to separate Pantheons whose Gods maintained their own Divine civilizations.
The Demonic War and subsequent supernatural revelation fundamentally changed this system.
Under [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Empire, the distinction between supernatural political authority and Divine authority became increasingly blurred because Victor himself simultaneously possesses positions as Emperor, God, Dragon Progenitor, Demon King and supreme authority of the civilization.
The [[Blood God Religion]] and the broader Imperial faith surrounding Victor became major institutions, while the older Pantheons continued primarily as subordinate Divine and cultural traditions rather than independent planetary governments.

### Environment

#### Pre-War Environmental Damage
Human industrial civilization had significantly damaged portions of Earth's environment before the supernatural reconstruction.
Pollution, waste, destruction of habitats and contamination of the oceans were specifically discussed by the supernatural rulers while debating Earth's future.[6]

#### Planetary Restoration
The post-war reconstruction plan included large-scale ecological restoration.
[[Gaia]] Elderblood and other powerful Nature-oriented Beings could provide the Energy necessary for regeneration, while [[Yggdrasil]] would distribute that power throughout the planet.[6]
By Chapter 986, this process had already begun producing visible results, including the removal of waste and pollution from planetary environments.[9]

#### Imperial Preservation
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Chapter 1178 decision represents the final extension of this philosophy.
Rather than continuously urbanizing his birthplace as the Empire grows, he intends to remove the population and preserve [[Yggdrasil]] as a beautiful garden world devoid of sentient inhabitants.[5]
The planet is intended to become the Empire's historical and ecological jewel rather than its most densely populated metropolis.

### Military and Defense
Before the Demonic War, Earth lacked any unified planetary defense capable of coordinating Humanity and the supernatural world. The consequences of that fragmentation were catastrophic.
Under [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], [[Yggdrasil]] became part of an Empire possessing:
- Imperial Legions.
- Dragons and Dragon Goddesses.
- Divine armies.
- Advanced spacecraft.
- Runic technology.
- Teleportation infrastructure.
- Planetary and interstellar monitoring.
- World Tree connections.
- Victor's personal protection.
Its importance as Victor's birthplace and historical home gives the planet strategic value even after the transfer of the capital.
The decision to convert Yggdrasil into a garden world should therefore not be interpreted as abandonment. Victor explicitly intends for it to remain protected even after the population is relocated.[5]

### Relationship with the World Tree Network
The late [[Elderblood Empire]] transformed [[World Trees]] from isolated planetary guardians into part of a civilization-scale infrastructure.
During the reconstruction of Mars, the World Trees connected to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] projected roots into the new planet. [[Yggdrasil]] was explicitly among them.[5]
The network enables effectively instantaneous transportation and communication throughout vast portions of the Empire.
Yggdrasil therefore remains integrated into the Empire's strategic infrastructure even after Mars replaces the planet as political capital.
By the late Imperial era, Yggdrasil herself is connected directly to Victor and participates in a World Tree network spanning planets across numerous galaxies.[5]

Birthplace of Victor and cradle of the Elderblood Empire.
Population
Still inhabited during the transition, but the permanent sentient population is intended to be relocated.
Environmental
Designated to become a protected garden world.
World Tree
Associated with Yggdrasil and Qiliphoth; Yggdrasil also participates in Victor's larger World Tree Network.
Strategic
Protected Imperial heritage world rather than abandoned territory.

### Notes
- Earth and [[Yggdrasil]] refer to the same physical planet at different historical stages.[3]
- The planet should not be confused with Yggdrasil the World Tree.
- Earth is located in the Northern Region of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Sector.[1]
- The Sector was still treated as Mid-Level in Chapter 1136. Earlier statements concerning approaching promotion should not be rewritten as if the Sector had already become High-Level.[7]
- [[The Universal Tree]] is the progenitor of [[World Trees]] but has not been directly established as the physical creator of planet Earth. It should therefore not be listed as Earth's confirmed creator.
- Yggdrasil and Qiliphoth are Earth's World Trees rather than the political "founders" of the physical planet.
- Earth's Pantheons exist primarily in separate dimensions and should not be represented as ordinary geographic regions of the Mortal Realm.[6]
- The Seven Great Pantheons historically associated with Earth are the Greek, Norse, Shinto, Egyptian, Hindu, Biblical and Chinese Pantheons.[10]
- More than six billion Humans died during [[Diablo]]'s invasion.[11]
- Earth's Human population was approximately one billion around Chapter 986.[9]
- Victor was already described in terms of ownership over Earth, Samar and [[Nightingale]] by Chapter 986.[9]
- The original Human nation-state system ended when Victor united Earth's continents and created the [[Elderblood Empire]].[3]
- The [[Imperial Calendar]] begins with the formal unification of the planet.[3]
- Babylon contains 999 floors.[3]
- Yggdrasil remained one of the Empire's principal economic centers throughout the accelerated Imperial era.[4]
- As of Chapter 1178, Mars is the capital of the Elderblood Empire, not Yggdrasil.[5]
- Victor intends Yggdrasil to eventually possess no permanent sentient population, becoming a preserved garden world marking where the Elderblood civilization began.[5]
- Yggdrasil the World Tree participated in the World Tree Network established through Mars.[5]
