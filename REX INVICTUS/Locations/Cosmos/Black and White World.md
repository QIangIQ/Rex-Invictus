The Black and White World is an unusual alternate plane of existence accessed by Agares, the Second Key of Solomon and one of the most powerful Demonic Dukes of Hell. Unlike an ordinary location within the material Universe, the plane exists on a separate dimensional layer that allows Agares to remove his existence from conventional Reality while continuing to perceive and move relative to individuals remaining within it. From his perspective, the surrounding world appears entirely white, while individual existences are represented by black forms. This unusual perception, together with the absence of conventional directional orientation and the different behavior of movement and speed, distinguishes the plane from ordinary spatial environments.[1]
The plane is most closely associated with Agares' supernatural ability known as Dimensional Walking, which enables him to shift his existence between dimensional layers rather than simply teleporting between two points in ordinary Space. By moving into the Black and White World, Agares becomes inaccessible to conventional physical attacks originating from the material plane and can approach an opponent without producing the ordinary indications of movement or teleportation. He can subsequently return part of his body to the target's dimensional layer, allowing him to launch attacks from unexpected positions while retaining the defensive advantages provided by dimensional separation.[1]
The unique nature of this power was later explained by Victor Elderblood during his instruction of his daughters. Victor described Agares as a Demon capable of concealing himself within the shadows of Reality, an expression referring to dimensional layers ordinarily inaccessible to the perception of most supernatural beings. The technique was exceptionally dangerous against opponents unable to perceive or interact with those layers, but its effectiveness diminished considerably against beings possessing sufficiently advanced dimensional perception and authority over Reality.[2]
Although the plane is commonly identified as the Black and White World because of Agares' perception of it, the novel does not establish that this is its formal name. It is a descriptive designation derived from the unusual characteristics directly observed during his confrontation with Victor.

### Nature

#### Alternate Plane of Existence
The Black and White World is an alternate dimensional plane occupying a different layer of existence from the ordinary material world. Its separation is sufficiently substantial that an individual who has completely entered it no longer interacts with conventional physical attacks originating from outside the plane. The distinction makes it fundamentally different from invisibility, camouflage or the concealment of supernatural Energy, since [[Agares]] does not merely hide his presence within ordinary Reality but shifts his actual existence into another dimensional environment.[1]
The plane remains connected to ordinary Reality through the relationship between Agares' Dimensional Walking and the existences he can perceive from within it. Although he occupies a separate layer, individuals remaining in the material world are still represented in his perception, allowing him to identify their relative positions and approach them without first returning to their dimensional environment.
This relationship makes the plane exceptionally useful for concealment, infiltration and combat. An ordinary opponent cannot reliably intercept Agares simply by following his last visible position, since his movement continues through a spatial layer unavailable to conventional perception. The same separation also prevents Agares from freely interacting with an opponent through ordinary physical contact until he returns the relevant portion of his body to the target's plane.[1]
The dimensional separation does not establish that the Black and White World is a completely independent Universe. Its relationship with ordinary Reality is more consistent with the subordinate dimensional layers later described by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], which can exist separately from material Space while remaining governed by the larger cosmological structure.[2]

#### Shadows of Reality
The expression shadows of Reality provides a broader explanation of the dimensional concealment employed by [[Agares]]. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] used the description while teaching [[Norabell Elderblood]] about the relationship between ordinary dimensional structures and the deeper conceptual foundations of existence. He explained that Agares could enter an alternate dimension, conceal himself within a layer most beings could not perceive and subsequently attack opponents from that position.[2]
The description does not refer to conventional Darkness Manipulation or an ordinary shadow-based ability. Agares' concealment depends upon dimensional separation, meaning his location is hidden because he occupies a different layer of Reality rather than because he has suppressed the Light surrounding his body.
Victor also explained that the effectiveness of this concealment depended heavily upon the opponent's ability to perceive and interact with the relevant dimensional layer. Against an ordinary supernatural being, Agares could exploit the separation to approach without being detected. Against an individual possessing more developed perception of Reality, the supposedly concealed dimension could become visible and accessible.
Norabell's inherited powers offered an example of a much more advanced form of dimensional perception. Her [[Eyes of Space-Time]] and the truth-perceiving characteristics inherited from Victor enabled her to recognize deeper structures within Reality. Such perception could potentially expose dimensional environments that would remain inaccessible to ordinary senses.[2]

#### Dimensional Subordination
The distinction between subordinate dimensions and independent Realities is important when considering the Black and White World. An ordinary dimension can exist outside material Space while borrowing the fundamental Rules necessary for its operation from the greater Universe. Such a structure may possess unusual spatial properties without independently reproducing every Concept required to sustain an autonomous Cosmos.[2]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] demonstrated this principle by creating an ordinary pocket dimension and comparing it with a more complex miniature galaxy supported by advanced Divine Concepts. Norabell could interfere with the simpler structure because its foundations were comparatively accessible to her perception and authority. The conceptual galaxy proved considerably more difficult because its existence possessed additional layers of supernatural information and greater existential weight.
[[Agares]]' dimensional power represents a more limited application of the same underlying relationship. His ability exploits a spatial layer that ordinary beings cannot access, but the concealment itself does not establish the superior existential foundation possessed by a fully independent Reality. Its usefulness depends primarily upon the dimensional separation between Agares and his opponent.[2]

### Appearance

#### Monochromatic Environment
The defining visual characteristic of the Black and White World is its monochromatic appearance. From [[Agares]]' perspective, the surrounding environment consists of white existence, while individual beings appear as black representations. This allows him to identify an opponent without perceiving that individual through the ordinary visual characteristics associated with material Reality.[1]
During his confrontation with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Agares perceived the Vampire as a dark individual existence against the white surroundings. He could therefore distinguish Victor's position despite occupying a different dimensional plane, allowing him to approach the target through the alternate environment before returning his attacking claw to ordinary Reality.
The contrast between black individual existences and the white surrounding environment is an established characteristic of Agares' perception. However, the description does not necessarily mean that every object or supernatural phenomenon within the plane possesses literal black or white material composition. The observed colors may represent the manner in which existence becomes perceptible through Agares' dimensional ability rather than the physical composition of the realm itself.

#### Representation of Individual Existences
Individual existences remain perceptible within the Black and White World despite their separation from [[Agares]]' dimensional layer. Their black representations provide points of reference that allow him to approach a target without directly occupying the same ordinary Space.[1]
This characteristic is particularly significant because the plane lacks conventional directional orientation. Without the ability to perceive individual existences, Agares would have substantially greater difficulty determining how to move toward a target through the otherwise featureless environment.
The black representations therefore function as identifiable manifestations of beings existing outside the plane. The novel does not establish whether every form of Matter, Energy or conceptual existence receives an identical representation, and the available demonstration concerns Agares' perception of an individual opponent.

### Spatial Properties

#### Absence of Conventional Direction
The Black and White World does not possess the ordinary directional orientation familiar to beings inhabiting material Reality. [[Agares]] perceived no conventional up, down, left or right while occupying the plane, making it fundamentally different from an environment in which movement can be understood through ordinary spatial reference points.[1]
This absence of intuitive orientation makes the plane particularly difficult to navigate for an unfamiliar traveler. Agares acknowledged that someone lacking experience with the environment could easily become lost, indicating that successful movement depends upon familiarity with the plane's unusual spatial characteristics.
The difficulty does not prevent deliberate movement. Agares possesses enough understanding of the environment to identify individual existences, establish a useful direction relative to his intended target and move toward that position despite the absence of conventional spatial orientation.
His proficiency consequently derives partly from experience rather than exclusively from the supernatural ability to enter the plane.

#### Movement
Movement remains possible within the Black and White World despite the absence of conventional directional reference points. [[Agares]] demonstrated that he could push against what appeared to be nothing and use the resulting momentum to propel himself toward [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s black representation.[1]
The demonstration establishes that the environment supports movement through a mechanism different from ordinary locomotion. Agares did not require a visible physical surface upon which to stand, nor did he need to follow a conventional path through the material environment surrounding Victor.
His momentum nevertheless allowed him to approach the target deliberately, indicating that relative movement and separation remain meaningful within the plane even though their operation differs from familiar physical Space.
The precise underlying mechanism was not fully explained. The demonstrated movement should therefore be understood as an unusual property of the plane rather than evidence that ordinary spatial relationships have been eliminated in every possible sense.

#### Relationship with Speed
Speed behaves differently within the Black and White World than within ordinary Reality. During his approach toward [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], [[Agares]] perceived the movement of the surrounding world as extraordinarily slow and recognized that speed did not interact with the alternate plane in the same manner as the material environment.[1]
This difference contributes substantially to the effectiveness of his Dimensional Walking. Because Agares can move relative to an opponent while occupying a separate layer, the ordinary relationship between the opponent's movement and his own attack preparations becomes difficult to evaluate through conventional senses.
The unusual behavior of speed does not establish that Time is completely stopped within the plane or that Agares possesses unlimited movement speed. He still demonstrates motion through momentum, approaches a recognizable target and must perform an additional dimensional transition before making physical contact.
Its principal significance is therefore the altered relationship between movement inside the alternate plane and events occurring within ordinary Reality.

#### Relative Position
The Black and White World preserves a functional relationship with the positions of beings outside it. [[Agares]] could approach [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s representation and subsequently manifest his claw near the corresponding position in ordinary Space, demonstrating that his movement through the alternate plane influenced where he could re-enter material Reality.[1]
This property allows Dimensional Walking to function as a combat technique rather than merely a means of hiding from an opponent. Agares can use the alternate environment to change his effective position before returning part of himself to the target's plane.
The exact correspondence between distance traveled inside the Black and White World and distance within ordinary Space is not numerically established. Nevertheless, the demonstrated relationship is sufficient to permit deliberate positioning and accurately directed attacks.

### Relationship with Ordinary Reality

#### Dimensional Separation
[[Agares]]' transition into the Black and White World removes his existence from the ordinary plane in which most supernatural combat occurs. An opponent relying exclusively upon physical attacks cannot reliably strike the Demon while the relevant portion of his body occupies another dimensional layer.[1]
This protection is different from conventional durability. Agares does not necessarily survive an incoming attack because his body possesses enough physical strength to endure its destructive force. Instead, he avoids the interaction by shifting his existence away from the dimensional layer containing the attack.
Consequently, a powerful attack can fail to connect even when the attacker possesses superior strength or speed. The defensive advantage persists only while the target and attack remain separated by the relevant dimensional boundary.
The principle also operates in reverse. Agares cannot deliver an ordinary physical strike to a target remaining in normal Reality while his attacking limb remains completely separated from that target's plane.

#### Partial Dimensional Manifestation
[[Agares]] can manipulate which portions of his existence occupy ordinary Reality. His Dimensional Walking does not require him to move his entire body between planes every time he wishes to attack or defend himself.
During his confrontation with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Agares approached the Vampire while concealed within the Black and White World. After reaching the appropriate relative position, he activated his ability again and caused his claw to emerge into ordinary Space near Victor.[1]
This technique allows Agares to retain much of the protection provided by dimensional separation while exposing only the portion of his body necessary to perform an attack. It also provides a means of avoiding incoming strikes by removing the threatened portion of his body from the opponent's plane.
Partial Dimensional Manifestation is therefore one of the most important combat applications associated with Agares' power, combining defensive avoidance with the ability to attack from unexpected positions.

#### Interaction Between Planes
Physical interaction becomes possible when [[Agares]] returns the relevant part of his existence to the dimensional layer occupied by his opponent.
The transition creates a critical moment in which the previously inaccessible Demon can once again make contact with his target. His technique minimizes the warning associated with conventional movement because he does not approach through ordinary material Space before launching the attack.[1]
However, the separation does not make his strikes completely unavoidable. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s combat instincts warned him when Agares manifested an attacking claw near his body, allowing him to react despite the absence of the usual visual indications.
This establishes that an opponent possessing sufficiently developed instincts, perception or the ability to interact with dimensional structures may counter the attack when Agares begins crossing between planes.

### Dimensional Walking
Dimensional Walking is [[Agares]]' principal supernatural ability and the method through which he accesses the Black and White World. It allows him to shift his existence between dimensional layers, move while separated from ordinary Reality and return to the material plane when necessary.[1]
The technique differs fundamentally from conventional teleportation. Teleportation ordinarily transports a target between two positions, while Agares changes the dimensional layer occupied by his existence. He can subsequently move within the alternate environment before returning to ordinary Space.
The absence of a conventional teleportation transition makes this method exceptionally difficult to anticipate. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] recognized that the ability was more troublesome than ordinary teleportation because Agares could remain concealed in another plane and manifest an attack without providing the usual signs associated with a rapid spatial relocation.[1]

#### Dimensional Camouflage
Dimensional Camouflage is the concealment provided by [[Agares]]' separation from ordinary Reality. Rather than hiding himself through the suppression of Light, smell or supernatural Energy, he occupies a dimensional layer inaccessible to conventional senses.
This allows him to avoid detection by opponents who cannot perceive or interact with the alternate plane. The protection is especially effective against ordinary supernatural combatants whose senses remain restricted to the material layer.
The effectiveness of the concealment nevertheless depends upon the opponent's capabilities. Advanced dimensional perception, specialized detection methods and sufficiently developed spatial authority can overcome advantages that would otherwise prevent an ordinary being from locating Agares.[3][2]

#### Dimensional Evasion
[[Agares]] can avoid attacks by shifting his existence into another plane before the incoming strike makes contact. The technique is particularly effective against opponents relying upon direct physical attacks, as their superior strength becomes irrelevant if their attacks cannot reach his dimensional position.[1]
The power does not require Agares to possess greater conventional speed than every opponent. Instead, it allows him to avoid remaining in the same layer as the attack, creating a defensive advantage based upon dimensional position.
This makes Agares exceptionally difficult to engage in conventional close combat, particularly when he continually changes which portions of his body remain available for physical interaction.

#### Dimensional Ambush
Dimensional Ambush combines concealment, movement through the alternate plane and partial re-entry into ordinary Reality. [[Agares]] can approach an opponent through the Black and White World, position himself near the target's representation and manifest an attacking limb at the intended point of contact.[1]
The principal advantage lies in the reduced opportunity for the target to observe the attack's preparation. Since Agares does not move toward the opponent through ordinary material Space, conventional visual tracking provides little warning before the dimensional transition occurs.
His attempted attack on [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] demonstrates both the strength and limitation of this technique. Agares reached a favorable position and manifested his claw near the Vampire, but Victor's heightened instincts still enabled him to respond.

#### Dimensional Escape
[[Agares]] can also employ Dimensional Walking as a means of retreat. By shifting himself out of the ordinary plane and continuing to move between dimensional environments, he can make conventional pursuit extremely difficult.
His ability remained valuable long after the Demonic War. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually renewed the pursuit of Agares, the Demon relied heavily upon his dimensional mobility to avoid capture, forcing his enemies to employ specialized methods capable of detecting and restricting his movements.[3]
The later confrontation demonstrated that access to multiple dimensional layers does not guarantee escape against beings whose supernatural abilities can affect the spatial structures supporting those transitions.

### Combat Applications

#### Stealth and Infiltration
The dimensional separation provided by the Black and White World offers an effective means of approaching guarded locations or hostile individuals. [[Agares]] can conceal his existence within an alternate plane and move relative to ordinary Reality without maintaining a continuously detectable physical presence.[1]
This form of stealth is particularly dangerous because ordinary measures designed to identify invisible or concealed opponents may not be sufficient to reveal a being occupying a different dimensional layer.
However, sufficiently advanced perception can detect or counter such movement. The effectiveness of the technique therefore depends upon the relationship between Agares' dimensional abilities and the sensory or conceptual powers possessed by the opposing individual.

#### Defense
Dimensional Walking allows [[Agares]] to avoid many attacks by removing himself from their plane of interaction. The technique is especially advantageous when the opponent possesses greater physical strength but lacks powers capable of affecting dimensional layers beyond ordinary Reality.
Agares can also shift only the portions of his body threatened by an attack, reducing the need to expose his entire existence to the opponent.[1]
This defensive method remains vulnerable to sufficiently advanced dimensional interference, spatial sealing and attacks capable of reaching the concealed layer itself.

#### Offense
[[Agares]] can launch attacks by returning part of his body to material Reality after positioning himself through the alternate plane. His claw attacks demonstrate the ability to combine Dimensional Walking with ordinary Demonic physical weapons and other supernatural characteristics.[1]
The dimensional technique does not independently determine the destructive power of the resulting strike. Its advantage comes from the unexpected direction and limited warning afforded by the dimensional transition, while the damage remains dependent upon the attacking limb and any supernatural effects applied to it.

#### Pursuit and Retreat
Dimensional Walking allows [[Agares]] to move between planes during pursuit, making it difficult for ordinary enemies to determine his position or maintain direct contact with him.
The technique also allows him to withdraw from an unfavorable confrontation without necessarily traveling through the intervening material environment. Against enemies unable to enter or perceive the dimensional layer, this provides an extremely effective method of avoiding capture.
The advantage diminishes when an opponent possesses a means of identifying his concealed location or sealing the Space through which he must travel.[3]

### Countermeasures

#### Advanced Dimensional Perception
The most fundamental countermeasure to [[Agares]]' dimensional concealment is the ability to perceive the alternate layer in which he resides.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later explained that Agares' technique was exceptionally dangerous against ordinary beings because they could not perceive the concealed plane. Against sufficiently developed beings whose senses and authority encompassed that dimensional structure, the concealment could become ineffective.[2]
This distinction establishes that dimensional invisibility is not absolute. Agares' advantage depends upon occupying a layer unavailable to his opponent, rather than removing himself from every possible form of perception or interaction.
The extraordinary development of [[True Dragons]] and beings possessing advanced authority over Reality can therefore provide means of overcoming a concealment technique that would otherwise be extremely difficult to counter.

#### Victor's Tracking Method
After acquiring [[Diablo]]'s memories, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] possessed considerably more information concerning [[Agares]] and the weaknesses of his powers.
He subsequently developed a method capable of tracking the Demon despite his ability to move between dimensions. The method was sufficiently effective that Agares could no longer rely upon dimensional transitions alone to escape the Imperial forces pursuing him.[3]
Victor's preparation demonstrates that the movement of a being between dimensional layers can be tracked through sufficiently specialized supernatural methods. However, the precise construction of his tracking technique is not independently explained in the cited passage.
The existence of the method consequently establishes successful dimensional tracking rather than unlimited perception of every possible plane throughout Creation.

#### Zack's Detection
Zack demonstrated an unusual ability to detect [[Agares]] despite the Demon's attempts to conceal his presence.
Agares believed he had successfully hidden his scent, aura and other detectable characteristics before attempting to attack [[Anna Elderblood]]. Nevertheless, Zack reacted to his presence and repeatedly identified his location, frustrating the Demon's expectation that his concealment would prevent discovery.[3]
The exact mechanism responsible for Zack's detection was not explained. His success establishes that Agares' ordinary concealment could be overcome, but it does not independently prove that Zack possessed direct visual access to the Black and White World.
The distinction is important because detecting an enemy through a specialized supernatural sense is not necessarily equivalent to entering or directly observing the dimensional environment occupied by that enemy.

#### Zaladrac's Dimensional Interference
[[Zaladrac Zeovnur Elderblood]] demonstrated one of the most decisive countermeasures against [[Agares]]' dimensional powers.
Agares had previously exploited Dimensional Walking to frustrate Zaladrac, repeatedly moving between planes to avoid her and provoke her through his evasiveness. This earlier humiliation contributed to her eagerness to confront him when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] ordered his capture.[3]
During the confrontation, Agares attempted to remain concealed within another dimension, but Zaladrac appeared directly before him and struck him with an attack that penetrated the dimensional separation protecting him.
The blow sent Agares crashing into the ground and demonstrated that sufficiently developed Draconic powers could affect him despite his attempt to remain outside ordinary Reality.
The event establishes a direct counter to Agares' dimensional defense, as Zaladrac could interact with a target who believed himself safely concealed beyond the surrounding material plane.[3]

#### Spatial Sealing
After intercepting [[Agares]], Zaladrac employed her Draconic authority to seal the surrounding Space.
The technique prevented the Demon from escaping through further dimensional transitions, effectively removing the principal advantage provided by his Dimensional Walking.[3]
Spatial Sealing differs from ordinary detection because it does not merely reveal Agares' position. It restricts the spatial structures necessary for him to shift between planes, preventing the use of his ability even after he becomes aware that he has been located.
This establishes that sufficiently powerful spatial control can counter Dimensional Walking at the level of the ability's operation rather than requiring the opponent to match Agares' movements through every alternate dimension.
The demonstrated restriction applies to Agares' ability to travel between dimensions. It does not independently establish that Zaladrac permanently sealed or destroyed the Black and White World itself.

### Known Users

#### Agares
[[Agares]] is the only directly demonstrated user of the Black and White World. His Dimensional Walking allows him to enter the alternate plane, perceive individual existences through its unusual monochromatic environment, move relative to beings occupying ordinary Reality and return selected portions of his body to attack.[1]
His proficiency is the result of both his innate supernatural ability and his familiarity with the alternate plane. The environment's unusual spatial properties would be difficult to navigate for an inexperienced being, making his practical mastery an important component of the technique.
Although Agares can move between multiple dimensions, the Black and White World remains the particular plane directly depicted from his perspective during his original confrontation with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Later references to dimensional jumping do not establish that every transition necessarily occurs through the same environment.

#### Other Potential Users
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] possesses extraordinary dimensional abilities and expressed considerable interest in acquiring [[Agares]]' power. His decision to capture the Demon alive was influenced by the usefulness of Dimensional Walking and the potential advantages such an ability could provide.[3]
However, the established demonstrations do not confirm that Victor personally entered the exact Black and White World depicted in Agares' original confrontation. His ability to perceive deeper dimensional structures and his potential to obtain or reproduce Agares' powers should therefore remain separate from confirmed use of this particular plane.
Zaladrac's ability to intercept Agares across dimensional separation likewise establishes advanced dimensional interference, not necessarily direct entry into the Black and White World.

### Relationship with Other Dimensions

#### Dimensional Void
The [[Dimensional Void]], also known as the Void Between Dimensions, is the larger interdimensional expanse associated with the separation and connection of dimensions throughout the native Universe.
The Black and White World is an alternate plane accessed through [[Agares]]' individual supernatural ability. Although both involve separation from ordinary material Reality, they should not automatically be treated as the same location.
Agares' broader Dimensional Walking permits movement between multiple planes, but the directly observed Black and White World possesses specific perceptual and spatial characteristics not universally established for every region of the Dimensional Void.

#### Ordinary Pocket Dimensions
Ordinary pocket dimensions are enclosed spatial environments that can be created through sufficiently developed supernatural powers. Such dimensions may exist outside conventional material Space while remaining dependent upon the greater Universe's underlying Rules.[2]
The Black and White World possesses a comparable relationship with ordinary Reality insofar as it exists on a distinct dimensional layer. Its unusual perception and movement properties nevertheless make it a specialized environment rather than an ordinary empty pocket space.
The novel does not establish the plane's original creator, construction method or complete conceptual foundation.

#### Independent Realities
A fully independent Reality requires more than separation from ordinary material Space. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explained that a genuine autonomous creation requires appropriate conceptual foundations rather than merely an enclosed region possessing Space and Time.[2]
The Black and White World has not been established as a complete independent Universe. Its existence on a different plane and its resistance to conventional physical interaction do not independently demonstrate the ability to sustain a separate Cosmos with all necessary foundational Concepts.

#### The Abyss
The Abyss is a cosmological region associated with the consequences of the true End of astronomical structures and the absence of ordinary foundational Concepts.
The Black and White World differs fundamentally because individual existences remain perceptible within it, relative movement is possible and [[Agares]] can deliberately interact with the ordinary world through dimensional transitions.
The unusual absence of conventional direction within Agares' plane should therefore not be confused with the far more fundamental conceptual conditions associated with the Abyss.

#### Void Between Cosmoses
The Void Between Cosmos concerns the separation of distinct cosmological structures, whereas the Black and White World is a dimensional plane associated with the native Reality in which [[Agares]] operates.
Its existence does not establish that Agares can freely traverse the boundaries between independent Universes or that the plane inherently provides access to foreign cosmologies.
