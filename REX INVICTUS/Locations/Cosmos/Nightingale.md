Nightingale is an enormous supernatural planet located within a separate Lower Sector and is the ancestral homeland and historical center of civilization for the Vampires.[1][2] The name has several overlapping meanings: it originally referred to Nightingale, the City of Moonlight, the settlement founded under [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]]; the city later expanded until its territory became comparable to a large country and the same name came to describe Vlad's wider Vampire realm; later chapters also use Nightingale for the planet itself.[3]
Nightingale is considerably larger than Earth, exists beneath perpetual night and possesses two visible moons.[4][5] Its enormous surface contains Vampire cities, Clan territories, mountain ranges, Monster-infested frontiers, the Wildlands and extensive regions that remained unexplored even after millennia of settlement. The planet originally possessed the conventional pair of Positive and Negative [[World Trees]] represented by [[Amara Elderblood]] and [[Roxanne Elderblood]], although both later became deeply bound to [[Victor Elderblood]] rather than remaining ordinary planet-bound World Trees.[5][6][7]
Following Victor's victory over the [[Elder Gods Pantheon]], he claimed Nightingale itself and acquired a form of authority above its previous political powers. This did not remove Vlad from government; Victor explicitly allowed him to continue ruling the Vampire population and using the territory required for his civilization.[7] By Chapter 986, Victor was described as effectively owning Nightingale alongside Samar and Earth through his planetary and World Tree connections.[8]
By Chapter 1179, Nightingale had undergone another enormous transformation. The planet had become even larger, the old Royal Capital remained recognizably Gothic but had developed into a vibrant multi-species metropolis, and Vampires lived openly alongside Humans, Shadow Demons, Dark Elves, [[Ghosts]], Specters and other Imperial citizens. Elderblood technology had also overcome the old Vampire dependence upon blood as their only practical diet, allowing modern Vampires to eat ordinary food normally. Nightingale's entire Lower Sector subsequently became one of the external cosmic territories selected for merger with Victor's Sector.[9]

#### The City of Moonlight
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was introduced to Nightingale as the City of Moonlight when he first entered Vampire society.[1] The settlement was constructed around Vampire life, possessing permanent darkness, enormous buildings and a strongly medieval-Gothic aesthetic.
Over millennia the city continuously expanded until Vampire scholars considered it comparable in size to a large country. Vlad never renamed the settlement despite its expansion, causing Nightingale to become the common name for both the original city and the entire territory administered by the Vampire monarchy.[3]

#### Nightingale Planet
Later chapters distinguish the city from the much larger planet. Chapter 820 explicitly establishes that the physical world is larger than Earth and contains enormous unused and unexplored regions even after centuries of Vampire expansion.[5]
Accordingly, a statement concerning Nightingale must be interpreted according to context. The Royal Capital, Vlad's country-sized historical territory and the planet itself are not interchangeable in physical scale.

### Cosmological Position

#### Lower Sector
Chapter 835 explicitly classifies both Nightingale and Samar among the Lower Sectors.[2] Lower Sectors are protected developmental regions containing intelligent Life but civilizations that have not yet naturally reached the level of more advanced Sectors.
Albedo explains that Lower Sectors receive protection particularly from the Universal Tree because premature invasion by much stronger civilizations can destroy the developmental potential of an entire young Sector.[2]
Nightingale itself demonstrates this principle. Vlad entered the world as a hostile outside power and established Vampire civilization there, accelerating the development and conflict of its native inhabitants. This intervention attracted Primordial balancing measures rather than being treated as an ordinary planetary conquest.[2]

#### Development and Imperial Influence
Nightingale's original Lower-Sector classification did not prevent it from developing enormously once [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s civilization became involved. Vampire evolution, Imperial technology, World Tree integration and direct interaction with stronger supernatural races pushed the planet far beyond the society first shown at the beginning of the novel.
By Chapter 1179, Ruby states that the external Sectors participating in the merger had already reached their current development limits through their connection with Victor's civilization.[9] Nightingale was consequently no longer being left as an isolated developing territory; it was prepared for direct cosmological integration with Victor's Sector.

### Planetary Characteristics

#### Size
Nightingale was already explicitly larger than Earth before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later Imperial development.[5] Its exact original diameter and surface area have never been numerically provided.
The size difference was significant enough that Victor could manipulate enormous quantities of land during the construction of his private territory without exhausting the planet's available undeveloped regions. [[Gaia]] extracted land from the unexplored areas while the established Vampire civilization remained intact.[5]
Chapter 1179 establishes that the modern planet had become much larger than Ophis remembered.[9] Unlike Samar, whose modern expansion is quantified as roughly three times the size of old Earth, no exact current figure is supplied for Nightingale. Its modern dimensions should therefore remain unspecified.

#### Perpetual Night
Nightingale exists beneath almost permanent darkness. The Sun does not rise in the conventional manner experienced on Earth, making the passage of time difficult to judge naturally through daylight.[1][10]
This environment historically made the planet exceptionally suitable for Vampires, whose older racial weaknesses included sunlight.
The lack of conventional daylight also shaped Nightingale's architecture, culture and ecosystem. Species naturally comfortable in darkness became especially suited to the world, something increasingly visible after the Empire opened the planet to additional races.[9]

#### Moons
Nightingale possesses two moons, both visible within its permanent night sky.[4]
The moons later became relevant during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s large-scale planetary and dimensional projects, including the construction of territories associated with his developing Pantheon.[5]

#### Environment
Nightingale is not an entirely urbanized Vampire world. Its planetary environment includes forests, mountains, Wildlands, Monster territories, unexplored regions and vast natural zones extending beyond the established Vampire population centers.[11][5]
The western regions are particularly dangerous and historically required the specialized defensive culture of the [[Adrasteia Clan]].

### World Trees
Nightingale possesses an abnormal World Tree history involving [[Amara Elderblood]] and [[Roxanne Elderblood]].

#### Amara Elderblood
[[Amara Elderblood]] is Nightingale's original mature Positive World Tree. Before joining [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], she existed within the Wildlands and was deeply integrated with Nightingale's planetary Life.[5]
Her connection was originally so fundamental that Amara believed removing her from the planet would cause Nightingale to deteriorate or die.[6]
Victor's later evolution made that limitation obsolete. Roxanne explained that Victor had become capable of sustaining several planets independently, allowing Amara to leave her former planetary position safely.[6] Victor subsequently named her Amara, incorporated her into his family and bound her existence to himself.[7]

#### Roxanne Elderblood
[[Roxanne Elderblood]] originated as Nightingale's Negative World Tree. Unlike a conventional Negative World Tree, however, Roxanne eventually merged her existence with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] through their Soul relationship.[5]
Amara could no longer properly detect the younger World Tree's connection to Nightingale because Roxanne's primary existential anchor had become Victor rather than the planet.
This made Nightingale highly unusual: both its Positive and Negative [[World Trees]] eventually became wives of the same Being who later claimed the planet.

#### Modern World Tree Support
Chapter 1179 provides an important update to Nightingale's modern condition. While observing the enlarged planet, Ophis specifically thought of Roxanne and Amara and noted that Nightingale was extremely well nourished because the two Trees associated with the planet were now also her father's wives.[9]
This does not mean Roxanne reverted to her original conventional planet-bound state. Both Trees remain connected fundamentally to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], but his World Tree network allows their power to sustain Nightingale without the older restrictions that required Amara to remain permanently attached to the planet.

### Geography

#### Royal Capital
The historical City of Moonlight eventually became Nightingale's Royal Capital and the center of Vlad's Vampire monarchy. Its Gothic character survived the transformation of the civilization across enormous periods of time.
Chapter 1179 shows that the modern Royal Capital still retains its old Gothic architectural style while becoming dramatically more populated and vibrant.[9] Ophis remembered earlier periods when streets were comparatively quiet and primarily occupied by Vampires, nobles and servants; the modern capital is instead filled with multiple races and constant civilian activity.

#### Royal Territory
[[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] historically controlled the central royal territory surrounding the monarchy and Vlad's Castle.
Even after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s rise above the old Nightingale political system, Vlad retained local administrative authority over the population.[7] Chapter 1179 confirms that he continues functioning as the practical Vampire ruler: when informed that Nightingale's merger was beginning, Vlad immediately ordered Alexios to prepare the population and keep everyone calm.[9]

#### Snow Clan Territory
The [[Snow Clan]] historically governed much of Nightingale's economy, commercial authorization and internal and external political administration.[3]
Businesses traditionally required Snow Clan approval, making the family one of the most economically influential organizations in old Vampire society.
By Chapter 1179, the Snow territory is described as exceptionally wealthy, with the enormous status of the Emperor's wives contributing directly to the prosperity of the Clan territories.[9]

#### Fulger Clan Territory
The [[Fulger Clan]] historically administered domestic production and important Vampire supplies, particularly Blood Crystals, blood-based food and other necessities.[3]
Its economic functions often overlapped with the [[Snow Clan]], creating a close institutional relationship between the two.
Chapter 1179 continues to identify the Fulger territory as one of the prosperous major Clan regions of modern Nightingale.[9]

#### Scarlett Clan Territory
The [[Scarlett Clan]] traditionally represented the military and warfare branch of Nightingale's political structure.[3]
Under [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], the Clan became one of Nightingale's greatest historical combat deterrents and later an important component of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wider family and Empire.
Its territory remains identifiable during Ophis' modern survey of Nightingale and is described among the wealthy Clan regions.[9]

#### Adrasteia Territory
The [[Adrasteia Clan]] historically controlled the western defensive frontier. Its role differed from the Scarletts: Scarlett specialized broadly in warfare, while Adrasteia existed primarily to prevent threats from Nightingale's dangerous outer regions from entering established Vampire civilization.[3]
The territory includes Warfall, enormous mountain ranges and the fortified [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] Gate.[11][12]
The modern territory remains economically prosperous and politically recognizable by Chapter 1179.[9]

#### Wildlands
The Wildlands were enormous native regions historically associated with the Elder Gods, Monsters and Nightingale's original population.[5]
Amara originally resided there, and the region remained largely separate from conventional Vampire civilization for much of the story.
Following the defeat of [[Ikor-Kar-VI]] and collapse of the [[Elder Gods Pantheon]], the Wildlands ceased functioning as the seat of an independent rival Divine government.

#### Unexplored Regions
Large portions of Nightingale remained unexplored even after thousands of years of Vampire settlement.[5]
These regions became strategically useful to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] because enormous quantities of planetary material could be manipulated without destroying established population centers.
The later expansion of the planet shown in Chapter 1179 indicates that Nightingale's available territory increased even further.[9]

### Settlements

#### Nightingale, City of Moonlight
The original City of Moonlight is the historical center from which the entire Vampire civilization expanded. It began as a Vampire city and eventually became country-sized while preserving the same name.[1][3]
In the Imperial era it remains the Royal Capital but is no longer an overwhelmingly Vampire-exclusive city. Humans and multiple supernatural races now move openly throughout the streets alongside Vampires.[9]

#### Warfall
Warfall is the principal known settlement of the Adrasteia frontier and functions as a heavily militarized fortress-city.[12]
Weapons, armor, explosives and combat equipment form ordinary parts of local commerce because the population historically faced recurring Monster attacks.

#### Additional Cities
Vlad ordered the construction of additional urban centers during the instability surrounding the Demonic War.[13]
Chapter 1179 explicitly describes Ophis observing the Royal Capital and its surrounding cities, confirming that modern Nightingale consists of multiple major urban centers rather than one continuous city.[9]

### Government

#### Vampire Monarchy
[[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] remains the Vampire King and practical administrator of Nightingale's Vampire civilization.
Historically, Vlad governed through a centralized monarchy supported by the major Vampire Count Clans, which possessed broad autonomy within their own domains but remained beneath the King.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s eventual planetary claim did not immediately replace this administrative system.[7]

### Modern Society

#### From Vampire Realm to Imperial Metropolis
Old Nightingale society was overwhelmingly centered around Vampires, their servants, Clan politics and the blood economy. Chapter 1179 demonstrates that this is no longer an accurate description of the modern planet.
Ophis observed the streets populated by:
- Vampires;
- Humans;
- Shadow Demons;
- Dark Elves;
- [[Ghosts]];
- Specters;
- and other creatures capable of living comfortably beneath Nightingale's near-permanent darkness.[9]
[[Races (Flamel)|Races]] that had historically fought one another had gradually become ordinary citizens of the same Empire. Ophis considered the resulting civilization almost utopian, although that description represents her personal evaluation rather than an objective classification.[9]
The modern Nightingale identity is therefore increasingly Imperial rather than exclusively racial.

#### Vampire Dietary Revolution
Traditional Vampires could consume ordinary food, but it tasted extremely unpleasant, making blood the center of their diet and economy.[3]
This limitation was effectively solved during the Imperial era. By Chapter 1179, Elderblood technology allowed Vampires to eat anything normally instead of being functionally dependent upon blood as their exclusive practical food source.[9]
This represents a major change to Vampire civilization. Blood remains biologically and culturally important, but the population no longer requires an economy structured entirely around making ordinary food tolerable or obtaining blood for every meal.
The transition was initially difficult for traditional Vampires to accept, but over the enormous Imperial development period the new system became normal.[9]

#### Humans
Humans historically existed within Nightingale primarily through blood-supply arrangements, servitude and relationships with Vampire Clans.[3]
By Chapter 1179 ordinary Humans walk openly through the Royal Capital alongside supernatural citizens, indicating a far more integrated social status than the predatory hierarchy of old Vampire Nightingale.[9]

#### Formerly Hostile Races
The presence of Demons, Dark Elves, [[Ghosts]] and Specters illustrates the extent to which old racial hostilities have weakened beneath Imperial government.
Chapter 1179 states that many races which once fought one another had long since overcome those differences and now regarded themselves simply as citizens of the Empire.[9]
Individual racial characteristics and cultures remain intact; political identity no longer requires permanent inter-species conflict.

### Law and Order
Historical Nightingale law was created from a strongly Vampire-centered perspective. Crimes against Vampires were generally treated more seriously than crimes against ordinary Humans, while dangerous offenders could be imprisoned inside [[The Limbo Realm]].[14]
Modern Nightingale operates under the broader Elderblood Imperial order. Chapter 1179 indicates that the new social rules were enforced harshly during the transition, with Hell serving as a serious punishment for those who violated Imperial law.[9]
The exact modern Nightingale legal code has not been shown in sufficient detail to determine how much of Vlad's old legal structure remains independently active, but the racial hierarchy that once treated Humans primarily as blood resources is clearly no longer the dominant social model.

### Economy

#### Historical Economy
The historical Nightingale economy was dominated by the Snow and Fulger Clans. The [[Snow Clan]] regulated businesses and major financial affairs, while the [[Fulger Clan]] produced Blood Crystals, blood-derived foods and other necessities essential to Vampire life.[3]
Human blood suppliers also participated in the economy by voluntarily trading blood for wealth, resources or supernatural protection.

#### Imperial Economy
By Chapter 1179, Nightingale had become one of the Empire's major wealthy urban worlds. Ophis observed extensive prosperity throughout the territories of Clans Snow, Fulger, Scarlett and Adrasteia and directly associated that wealth with the fact that their leaders were wives of the Emperor.[9]
The old blood-centered economy has also become less structurally necessary because Vampires can now consume ordinary food normally.
Nightingale consequently evolved from a specialized Vampire economy into part of a vast multi-species Imperial economic system.

### Military and Defense

#### Scarlett Clan
The [[Scarlett Clan]] traditionally handled Nightingale's broader warfare and military affairs.[3] Its members became famous for overwhelming personal combat power, with [[Scathach]] standing among the most dangerous warriors produced by Vampire civilization.

#### Adrasteia Clan
The [[Adrasteia Clan]] specializes in direct defense of Nightingale's western frontier.[3] Its territory contains Warfall, the [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] Gate, automated weapon systems, anti-air defenses and a population accustomed to Monster attacks.[11][12]

#### Modern Imperial Protection
Nightingale's present defensive position is incomparable to its historical state. The planet is owned by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], its former Count families belong to his household, both associated [[World Trees]] are his wives and the entire world exists within the military sphere of the [[Elderblood Empire]].
The [[Elder Gods Pantheon]], formerly Nightingale's most powerful native rival government, has ceased to exist as an independent hostile civilization.

### Native Inhabitants

#### Original Population
Nightingale possessed sentient Life before Vlad and the Vampires settled the planet.
Its native peoples inhabited regions associated with the Wildlands and the Elder Gods. Their civilization was heavily damaged by [[Ikor-Kar-VI]], whose experiments used his own population and other supernatural beings as material.[6]
After the final Elder God conflict, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] discovered surviving groups beneath the battlefield. Approximately 1,700 individuals remained among the groups he located, including families, women and children.[6]
The current size and political status of this surviving native population have not been separately detailed.

#### Elder Gods
The [[Elder Gods Pantheon]] represented the principal Divine civilization native to the older Nightingale order.
Their God-King, [[Ikor-Kar-VI]], ruled the faction and maintained a longstanding hostile relationship with Vampire civilization.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s victory destroyed the Pantheon's leadership and ended it as a competing ruling authority on Nightingale.

#### Monsters
Nightingale contains numerous creatures generally categorized as Monsters. Many of the Monsters encountered around the western frontiers originated from [[Ikor-Kar-VI]]'s biological and supernatural experiments rather than ordinary natural evolution.[6]
The [[Adrasteia Clan]] historically existed in large part to prevent these creatures from reaching the principal Vampire territories.

### History

#### Natural Nightingale
Long before Vampire settlement, Nightingale possessed its own Life, World Tree system, native inhabitants, supernatural creatures and Divine civilization.
Amara developed as its Positive World Tree, while the Elder Gods and native peoples occupied large regions of the planet.

#### Vlad's Conquest
Vlad eventually entered the Lower Sector and established Vampire civilization on Nightingale. Chapter 835 clarifies that his intervention accelerated the development and conflict of the native civilization enough to attract the attention of the Primordial authorities responsible for maintaining developmental balance.[2]
The Vampire settlement expanded across millennia until the City of Moonlight reached country-scale proportions.

#### Rise of the Count Clans
As Vampire civilization expanded, the Snow, Fulger, Scarlett and Adrasteia Clans became the four principal Count families responsible for major functions of the realm.[3]
This political arrangement became the foundation of Nightingale society encountered by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] early in the story.

#### Victor's Arrival
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] first entered Nightingale soon after becoming a Vampire and was introduced to the settlement as the City of Moonlight.[1]
His relationships with Violet, Ruby, Sasha, their mothers, [[Scathach]] and the other major families progressively tied him to every important institution within Nightingale.
He eventually became a Vampire Count himself, receiving responsibility for exploration and territorial expansion beyond the traditional Clan regions.[14]

#### Expansion of Vampire Civilization
Population growth and changing supernatural politics eventually required the creation of additional urban centers. During the Demonic War Vlad ordered the Counts to assist in establishing another city.[13]
By the late Imperial era, multiple surrounding cities are explicitly visible around the old Royal Capital.[9]

#### Victor's Planetary Restructuring
During the construction of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s new Divine territory, he directly manipulated Nightingale on a planetary scale.[5]
The territories associated with Snow, Fulger and Adrasteia were raised from the planet as part of his project, while [[Gaia]] manipulated portions of the Wildlands and unexplored terrain. The operation demonstrated both Nightingale's enormous scale and the degree to which Victor had outgrown conventional planetary limitations.
It also brought him into direct conflict with the Elder Gods inhabiting the Wildlands.

#### War Against the Elder Gods
The longstanding conflict between Vampires and Nightingale's Elder Gods eventually escalated into a decisive war involving [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], his family, [[Ikor-Kar-VI]] and the Wildlands.
Victor defeated the God-King and uncovered the experiments conducted on the native population. The destruction of Ikor's faction removed the [[Elder Gods Pantheon]] as a rival government.

#### Claiming Nightingale
Following the battle, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] resolved the abnormal situation involving Amara and the planet.
Amara believed she could not leave without Nightingale collapsing, but Victor had grown powerful enough to replace the planetary Energy she provided.[6] He subsequently incorporated Amara into his family and claimed Nightingale as his territory.[7]
Vlad accepted the new arrangement after Victor confirmed that the Vampire population would remain under his local administration.

#### Imperial Transformation
Across the later Imperial era, Nightingale changed from a predominantly Vampire country into an enormous cosmopolitan world integrated with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s civilization.
The planet physically expanded, multiple species settled openly within its cities, the Clan territories became extremely prosperous and technology solved major limitations of traditional Vampire biology.[9]
The result was sufficiently different from the old Nightingale that Ophis, despite being born into the royal family, was struck by how much the planet had changed when she finally took time to observe it again.[9]

### Merger of Nightingale's Sector
Chapter 1179 marks the beginning of the largest cosmological change in Nightingale's history.
Ophis Elderblood, formerly Ophis Tepes, was assigned to Nightingale as one of the individuals responsible for preparing the external Sectors for merger.[9] She informed Vlad directly:
"Nightingale will soon join us, prepare yourself, Father."[9]
Vlad asked whether any special action was required. Ophis instructed him only to keep the population calm and wait until the ritual concluded. Vlad immediately ordered Alexios to begin preparations.[9]
The simplicity of the local task was possible because Ophis carried one of the dark-purple spheres prepared for the merger. Ruby later explained that equivalent spheres had been entrusted to Albedo, Ophis and Tasha and that the objects would draw the associated Sectors toward [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Sector.[9]

- The City of Moonlight expanded until it became comparable in size to a large country, but Vlad never renamed it.[3]
- The planet itself is much larger than the historical Vampire territory and was already larger than Earth before its later Imperial expansion.[5]
- Nightingale possesses two moons.[4]
- The world remains beneath perpetual or near-perpetual night.[1][10]
- Chapter 835 explicitly classifies Nightingale among the Lower Sectors.[2]
- [[The Universal Tree]] should not be listed as Nightingale's direct confirmed physical creator merely because it is the progenitor of [[World Trees]].
- [[Amara Elderblood]] should not be listed as the political founder of Nightingale. She is the planet's original Positive World Tree and predates Vampire civilization.
- [[Roxanne Elderblood]] originated as Nightingale's Negative World Tree but became primarily bound to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] through their Soul connection.
- By Chapter 1179, both Amara and Roxanne contribute to Nightingale's extremely well-nourished planetary condition despite their primary connection to Victor.[9]
- [[Ikor-Kar-VI]] is a former ruler and should not remain listed as one of Nightingale's current leaders.
- Victor owns Nightingale at a superior planetary level while Vlad continues serving as Vampire King and local administrator.[7][8]
- [[The Limbo Realm]] is a separate dimension; Nightingale only contains an entrance to it.[14]
- The territory beyond the [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] Gate is inhabited and contains Warfall and members of Clan Adrasteia.[11][12]
- Modern Nightingale is no longer populated primarily by Vampires. Humans, Shadow Demons, Dark Elves, [[Ghosts]], Specters and other Imperial citizens openly inhabit the capital.[9]
- Elderblood technology removed the practical requirement for Vampires to rely exclusively upon blood as food, allowing them to eat ordinary food normally.[9]
- The old Human blood-supplier economy should therefore be treated as historically important rather than necessarily defining the current economy.
- Nightingale has expanded further since Chapter 820, but Chapter 1179 provides no exact modern planetary dimensions.[9]
- The Snow, Fulger, Scarlett and Adrasteia territorial identities still exist in the modern era and are described as extremely wealthy.[9]
- Ophis Elderblood was entrusted with Nightingale's Sector-merger sphere and personally informed Vlad that the merger was beginning.[9]
- Nightingale's merger concerns the Sector, not simply moving the planet through ordinary Space.
- The Sector merger does not by itself evolve Victor's Sector into a higher classification; it creates or incorporates additional Galactic structures.[9]
- Victor is responsible for preventing collisions between Galactic structures as the external Sectors are integrated.[9]
