#### First Appearance
Chapter 803
Dimensional Void, also known as the Void Between Dimensions, is the immense interdimensional expanse that separates and connects the dimensional realms existing alongside the ordinary material Universe. It represents a distinct cosmological layer through which independently enclosed dimensions can be approached, located and entered without requiring those realms to occupy conventional physical positions within the material world. Divine Pantheons, Heavenly and Infernal realms, artificial dimensions and numerous other supernatural territories exist through dimensional structures whose relationship with ordinary Space differs fundamentally from that of planets, stars and galaxies.[1][2]
The Dimensional Void is not an individual Pantheon, a conventional pocket dimension or an alternative name for ordinary outer Space. Rather, it constitutes the surrounding interdimensional medium associated with the dimensional structures belonging to the native Cosmos. These structures may possess their own environments, inhabitants, spatial dimensions and governing Rules while remaining connected to the greater Reality through which they were established. A dimension can consequently exist beyond the ordinary material environment of its associated world without becoming a completely separate Universe possessing an independent cosmological foundation.[2][3][4]
The interdimensional expanse possesses considerable importance because it allows supernatural territories to exist within the same larger cosmological structure while remaining physically inaccessible through ordinary movement. The [[Norse Pantheon]], for example, can be reached by searching for its dimensional entrance rather than traveling toward an ordinary astronomical destination. Nyx Elderblood demonstrated this distinction while attempting to locate the Norse realm through the Void Between Dimensions, a process requiring an actual search for the appropriate dimensional route.[5]
The Dimensional Void also possesses a structural relationship with the stability of Creation. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] banished [[Typhon]] into it, the [[Regulators of Existence]] intervened because the unrestricted influence of the Beast's Concept of END threatened to erase the Void Between Dimensions itself. The potential consequences were considered sufficiently severe that [[True Death]] personally sealed the destructive Concept and preserved Typhon's existence within the interdimensional environment.[1]

### Overview
The native Universe consists of more than the material Space occupied by stars, galaxies and inhabited planets. Numerous separate realms exist through supernatural dimensional structures, allowing Pantheons, afterlife systems, prisons and other specialized environments to function outside the ordinary spatial conditions associated with their corresponding physical worlds.
The Dimensional Void occupies an important position within this organization because it provides the interdimensional expanse separating those structures. Its existence allows two realms belonging to the same larger Cosmos to remain distinct without requiring them to possess conventional astronomical distances or corresponding physical coordinates within material Space.

### Nature and Structure

#### Interdimensional Expanse
The Dimensional Void is described as an endless Void Space associated with the dimensions belonging to the greater cosmological structure. It occupies a different layer from the ordinary material Universe and provides the surrounding environment through which dimensional destinations can be approached.[2]
Its emptiness is one of its defining characteristics. Unlike a physical world containing recognizable astronomical structures, geographical landmarks or inhabited territories, the Void does not ordinarily provide travelers with conventional reference points. Its principal structural significance derives from its relationship with the dimensional realms existing through it rather than from any known native civilization or ecosystem occupying the expanse itself.
The enormous scale of the Dimensional Void also means that entering it does not automatically reveal the location of a destination. A traveler can possess enough power to survive the transition yet lack the knowledge, senses or specialized abilities necessary to identify a particular realm.
This difference between reaching the Void and successfully navigating it explains why exceptionally powerful beings may still require considerable effort to locate a specific dimensional entrance.

#### Separation from Material Reality
Material Space and the Dimensional Void operate as distinguishable layers of the native Cosmos. Ordinary movement through the material Universe does not necessarily provide access to a dimensional territory, even when the realm is associated with a planet located somewhere within that material Universe.
A Divine Pantheon can therefore be connected to a civilization without occupying the same conventional Space as the civilization's physical world. The relationship is maintained through dimensional structures, supernatural connections and the governing systems associated with the relevant realm.
The [[Norse Pantheon]] provides an important example. Nyx did not simply travel toward a physical location in the ordinary Universe when seeking the realm. Instead, she searched through the Void Between Dimensions for an appropriate entrance, demonstrating that the route depended upon the dimensional organization of the Pantheon rather than ordinary astronomical navigation.[5]
The distinction applies more broadly to other independently maintained planes, including certain Heavenly and Infernal dimensions associated with the supernatural civilizations of Earth.

#### Dimensional Boundaries
Dimensional realms possess boundaries separating their internal environments from the surrounding interdimensional expanse. These boundaries allow a realm to maintain an enclosed supernatural environment whose spatial relationships differ from those of the material Universe.
Entering a dimension consequently involves more than reaching an ordinary physical coordinate. The traveler must interact with a dimensional entrance, cross the relevant boundary or employ a power capable of bypassing the separation.
The difficulty of this process varies according to the supernatural abilities of the traveler and the defenses protecting the destination. A properly established Pantheon can possess barriers and other security systems preventing unwanted entry, while extraordinarily powerful individuals may be capable of directly interfering with the Space surrounding those defenses.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] demonstrated the latter capability when he approached the [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]] through the Void Between Dimensions and physically breached its spatial boundary, entering the Territory of the Gods despite the defensive preparations established by [[Shiva]].[6]

#### Dimensional Independence
An ordinary dimension can possess an enclosed environment, internal Time and Space, supernatural structures and inhabitants without becoming a genuinely independent Universe.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] clarified this distinction during his instruction of [[Norabell Elderblood]], who had inherited exceptional authority over Space and Time. Norabell initially struggled to create what she believed was a simple dimension because she was unconsciously attempting to establish an independent Reality possessing its own conceptual foundation.[3]
Victor explained that ordinary dimensions borrow many of the fundamental Rules governing their existence from the greater Reality. This allows a supernatural being possessing sufficient authority to create an enclosed dimension without independently reproducing the entire structure required to sustain a complete Universe.
By contrast, establishing a genuinely independent Reality requires a more extensive collection of foundational Concepts. Space and Time alone are insufficient when the intended creation must independently sustain all the processes necessary for genuine existence.[4]
This principle is important to the Dimensional Void because the dimensions associated with it should not automatically be interpreted as completely separate Universes. Their separation from material Space is a dimensional characteristic, whereas their degree of existential independence depends upon the foundation of the individual realm.

#### Existential Weight
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] introduced the concept of existential weight while comparing an ordinary pocket dimension with a miniature galaxy reinforced by high-order Divine Concepts.
Although both structures could be created through his supernatural abilities, they possessed substantially different relationships with Reality. The ordinary dimension relied upon the greater Cosmos for most of its foundational Rules, while the conceptual galaxy incorporated additional properties directly into its existence.[3]
Norabell demonstrated that she could perceive and manipulate the comparatively simple dimensional construct. However, she could not reproduce the same result against the more complex galaxy because its deeper conceptual foundations made it considerably more difficult to interfere with.
Victor also used [[Agares]] as an example of how dimensional advantages depend upon the perception and authority of an opponent. Agares had once been exceptionally dangerous because he could occupy a dimensional layer inaccessible to ordinary supernatural perception. Against a being capable of directly observing and interacting with that layer, however, the advantage became considerably less absolute.[3]
This establishes that dimensional separation and existential superiority are not necessarily identical. A realm may be inaccessible to ordinary movement without possessing the same conceptual complexity or stability as a fully independent Reality.

### Dimensional Realms

#### Divine Pantheons
Divine Pantheons frequently occupy separate dimensional territories rather than existing exclusively within the material worlds associated with their civilizations. These realms can contain extensive supernatural environments, the residences of Gods, important religious institutions and the structures through which Pantheon authority is exercised.
Their existence within a dimensional layer allows Pantheon territories to remain separate from ordinary Mortal civilization while retaining supernatural connections to their worshippers, associated worlds and other Divine systems.
The [[Norse Pantheon]] is one of the clearest examples because its dimensional entrance had to be located through the Void Between Dimensions. The [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]] provides another demonstration through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s direct arrival from the interdimensional expanse.[5][6]
The spatial scale and defenses of an individual Pantheon depend upon its particular history, creators and governing powers. The Dimensional Void connects such territories at the level of the greater cosmological structure without making their internal characteristics identical.

#### Heavenly Dimensions
Heavenly realms constitute supernatural environments associated with Divine civilizations, Angelic beings and the systems governing the souls of the deceased.
Their existence does not require them to occupy the ordinary material Space of the civilizations with which they are associated. They can instead function through dimensional structures connected to the relevant planetary and religious systems.
The Heavenly realms associated with Earth are particularly important because their existence involves the interaction between the planet's [[World Trees]] and the Divine authorities responsible for particular supernatural territories.
Such realms should be understood as destinations associated with the larger dimensional structure rather than permanent regions of ordinary material Space.

#### Infernal Dimensions
Infernal realms likewise occupy separate supernatural territories associated with the negative aspects of planetary and spiritual existence.
The various Hells possess their own governing systems, internal environments and supernatural inhabitants. Their connections with the material worlds of the living are maintained through the structures responsible for the operation of the afterlife.
Examples include [[Biblical Hell]], the [[Greek Underworld]] and [[Nordic Hell]], each of which possesses characteristics distinct from those of an ordinary physical planet.
The Dimensional Void is separate from these Infernal realms. Entering the Void does not automatically mean entering Hell, and the existence of Hell does not establish that the surrounding interdimensional expanse possesses the same corrosive environment or Demonic Energy.

#### World Trees and the Afterlife
[[World Trees]] possess a fundamental relationship with the formation and maintenance of planetary supernatural systems. Their powers support the development of Life and the existence of the corresponding spiritual environments, including the Heavenly and Infernal dimensions connected to inhabited worlds.
As civilizations develop, the World Tree system supports the dimensions associated with their spiritual and afterlife structures. These realms can exist on separate planes while retaining a connection to the material planet and its inhabitants.[7]
The relationship is particularly important because a realm's continued existence may depend upon its connection with the appropriate supernatural foundation. Removing the support sustaining an afterlife dimension can compromise its stability and threaten the continued operation of its associated spiritual system.
Individual Gods can also possess important roles in constructing or governing particular realms. The relationship between World Trees and dimensional systems therefore does not mean that every Divine territory is created exclusively through the same method.

#### Artificial and Personal Dimensions
Separate dimensions can also be constructed through sufficiently developed supernatural abilities.
Witches, Gods, Dragons and other beings possessing appropriate spatial powers can establish enclosed supernatural environments whose internal characteristics differ from those of the surrounding material Universe.
An ordinary pocket dimension can depend upon the Rules of the greater Cosmos, while more elaborate creations may incorporate specialized Concepts to increase their independence and resistance to interference.[3][4]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Personal Universe and the dimensions associated with his Soul demonstrate the extraordinary development possible through the combination of Space Manipulation, Divine Concepts, Draconic powers and advanced knowledge of Reality.
The Dimensional Void provides the broader context for ordinary interdimensional separation, but the novel does not establish that every personal Soul dimension or every exceptionally independent Reality must occupy an identical position within that expanse.

### Dimensional Travel

#### Entering the Void
Travel into the Dimensional Void requires a means of interacting with the dimensional structure separating the ordinary material world from the interdimensional expanse.
Such methods may involve the manipulation of Space, the use of specialized portals, dimensional teleportation or Divine authorities capable of identifying and crossing the boundaries of a realm.
The exact method depends upon the traveler's abilities and the structure of the destination. A practitioner utilizing a prepared portal may not need to navigate the Void consciously, while a being entering the interdimensional expanse directly may have to identify and approach the relevant dimensional entrance.
The existence of these different methods demonstrates that access to a dimension and the ability to travel freely through the surrounding Void are related but distinguishable capabilities.

#### Dimensional Navigation
Locating a destination within the Dimensional Void can be difficult even for exceptionally powerful Gods.
Nyx's search for the [[Norse Pantheon]] demonstrates that knowing a realm exists does not necessarily grant immediate knowledge of its position. She was required to seek out the appropriate dimensional entrance rather than reaching it through an ordinary movement across material Space.[5]
Navigation therefore depends upon the traveler's ability to recognize dimensional structures, identify routes and interact with the boundaries separating the desired realm from the Void.
The required effort can also depend upon the traveler's familiarity with the destination. A known portal or established supernatural connection may provide an effective route, while an unfamiliar dimension can require exploration through an otherwise featureless expanse.

#### Spatial Perception
A being capable of perceiving dimensional structures possesses a considerable advantage over one whose senses are limited to ordinary material Space.
Such perception can make it possible to recognize concealed dimensional layers, locate entrances and identify the boundaries of supernatural territories.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s development as a Dragon substantially expanded his understanding of Reality, allowing him to perceive extraordinary quantities of supernatural Energy and the relationships between different components of Creation. His awakening as the Primogenitor of the Draconic Vampires also enabled him to recognize the presence of the Primordial Beings observing him from the Dimensional Void.[1]
However, detecting an extraordinarily powerful being within the Void should not automatically be equated with possessing unrestricted knowledge of every dimension or entrance throughout Creation.

#### Dimensional Teleportation
Teleportation techniques can transport a target into the Dimensional Void without requiring the target to willingly enter or navigate it.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] demonstrated this during the conflict surrounding [[Olympus]] when he combined the teleportation methods associated with Witches and [[Clan Alioth]]. By integrating these methods, he established a sufficiently powerful dimensional transfer to remove [[Typhon]] from the battlefield and deposit him directly into the Dimensional Void.[1]
The technique demonstrates that interdimensional displacement can be used offensively, particularly against opponents who lack the knowledge or abilities necessary to escape from the destination.
Its effectiveness nevertheless depends upon the target's characteristics. A being capable of deliberately searching for and entering another dimension may eventually escape the Void despite having been forcibly transported there.

#### Breaching a Dimensional Boundary
Advanced Space Manipulation can allow a sufficiently powerful being to bypass conventional dimensional entrances.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s arrival at the [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]] provides one of the most explicit demonstrations. Rather than relying upon an ordinary portal, he approached from the darkness of the Void Between Dimensions and forcibly opened the Space separating him from the Pantheon's territory.[6]
The entrance overwhelmed the preparations of [[Shiva]], whose defenses were insufficient to prevent Victor from breaching the realm. His arrival also demonstrated how the overwhelming supernatural presence of a sufficiently powerful being could affect a dimension even before the individual began employing ordinary offensive techniques.
This incident establishes that dimensional barriers can be overcome by superior supernatural authority, but it does not imply that every barrier or independent Reality can be breached through the same method regardless of its conceptual foundations.

### Hazards and Limitations

#### Interdimensional Isolation
One of the principal dangers of the Dimensional Void is the possibility of becoming stranded within it.
The Void possesses no ordinary environment that would allow a traveler to determine the position of another dimension through conventional navigation. A being lacking appropriate powers or knowledge may remain isolated for an indefinite period without discovering an entrance.[8]
The danger is particularly relevant to unintelligent supernatural creatures that possess enormous physical power but lack the ability to search deliberately for another dimensional realm.
The Dimensional Void can therefore function as a method of confinement without necessarily possessing constructed prison walls or a governing authority.

#### Dependence on Dimensional Abilities
The ability to survive within the Void does not necessarily grant the ability to leave it.
An individual may possess extraordinary durability or supernatural reserves while lacking the spatial perception or dimensional manipulation necessary to locate a destination.
Conversely, a knowledgeable practitioner possessing appropriate teleportation or dimensional techniques may be able to escape without demonstrating comparable raw physical strength.
This distinction becomes especially important when evaluating beings such as the Beasts of END, whose abilities differ considerably despite their common connection to the Concept of finality.

#### Conceptual Threats
The Dimensional Void is vulnerable to sufficiently powerful supernatural effects capable of targeting its underlying existence.
The clearest demonstration occurred when the Judges of the Abyss recognized that [[Typhon]]'s END Concept could erase the Void Between Dimensions. Unlike ordinary physical attacks, END directly affects the continuation of a target's existence, making the unrestricted presence of a Beast carrying that Concept exceptionally dangerous.[1]
The danger was not merely that Typhon might damage one dimensional realm. His continued presence threatened the interdimensional structure itself and could produce severe consequences for the dimensions associated with it.
[[True Death]]'s intervention prevented the immediate danger by suppressing the destructive Concept rather than attempting to defeat Typhon through conventional combat.

#### Restrictions Imposed by Dimensional Structures
Dimensional realms can possess barriers, environmental restrictions and governing Rules that limit the effectiveness of outside interference.
The strength of these restrictions depends upon the realm's construction and the authority supporting it. An ordinary pocket dimension may be vulnerable to a sufficiently knowledgeable spatial manipulator, while a more complex creation incorporating high-order Concepts can possess considerably greater resistance.[3][4]
Such limitations originate from the dimensional structure or its controlling powers rather than constituting one universal law preventing all travel through the Dimensional Void.

### Typhon's Banishment
The most important early demonstration of the Dimensional Void occurred during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s transformation into the Primogenitor of the Draconic Vampires.
At the conclusion of his evolution, Victor acquired extraordinary new senses and a much broader relationship with the supernatural Energy governing Creation. The presence of [[Typhon]], a Beast associated with the Concept of END, continued to threaten the battlefield even after Victor's transformation.[1]
Rather than attempt to defeat Typhon directly, Victor utilized dimensional teleportation to remove him from the battlefield. He combined a Witch teleportation circle with the spatial methods of [[Clan Alioth]], creating a transfer capable of relocating the enormous Beast into the Dimensional Void.
The technique immediately attracted the attention of the Primordial Beings observing the conflict. Although Victor had removed the danger from [[Olympus]], the Beast's unrestricted presence within the interdimensional expanse created a much larger cosmological problem.

#### Threat to the Dimensional Void
[[Typhon]] possessed a body covered by the Concept of END. Unlike an ordinary destructive aura, the Concept threatened the continued existence of the environment surrounding him.
The Judges recognized that leaving Typhon in the Void would eventually result in the erasure of the interdimensional expanse itself. The resulting disruption was regarded as an unprecedented danger to the greater cosmological structure.[1]
This establishes that the Dimensional Void possesses genuine existential significance. It is not merely a descriptive absence of physical Matter; it is a distinct part of the native structure whose destruction would affect the relationship between dimensional realms.

#### True Death's Intervention
The Regulators ultimately assigned responsibility for [[Typhon]] to [[True Death]], whose relationship with END allowed him to address the danger directly.
Death entered the Dimensional Void, approached Typhon and temporarily sealed the Concept covering the Beast. He subsequently surrounded Typhon with his own power to preserve the creature while it remained isolated within the interdimensional environment.[1]
The intervention prevented END from continuing to erase the surrounding structure while also ensuring that Typhon would not simply perish through exposure to the Void.
The event demonstrates both the danger of conceptual destruction within the interdimensional structure and the extraordinary authority of the Primordial Being responsible for the fundamental operation of END.

### The Beasts of END and Dimensional Exile
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later explained that the effectiveness of dimensional exile depends heavily upon the characteristics and intelligence of the being transported.
[[Typhon]] was considered vulnerable to this form of confinement because of his limited rationality. A creature possessing extraordinary physical strength but lacking the ability to search deliberately for a dimensional entrance could remain stranded until it happened to encounter another realm.[8]
By comparison, [[Fenrir]] and [[Jormungandr]] possessed enough intelligence to employ their Divine powers in a purposeful search. If banished into the Dimensional Void, they could attempt to locate another dimensional entrance and return to an inhabited realm.
The comparison demonstrates that raw supernatural strength alone does not determine the effectiveness of interdimensional confinement. Spatial abilities, perception and the capacity for deliberate exploration can be equally important.
The principle also distinguishes dimensional exile from the permanent sealing of a target. Transporting a being into the Void separates it from its original location, but does not necessarily impose an absolute restriction upon its ability to interact with dimensional boundaries.

### Relationship with Other Cosmological Structures

#### Material Universe
The material Universe is the physical environment containing stars, planets, galaxies and other astronomical structures.
The Dimensional Void constitutes a separate interdimensional layer associated with the dimensional realms belonging to the same greater Cosmos. Although the two are distinguishable, they remain connected through supernatural structures and methods of dimensional travel.[2]
A dimension associated with a physical planet may therefore exist separately from the material Space occupied by that planet without belonging to an entirely unrelated Universe.

#### Sectors
Main article: Sectors
Sectors are large cosmological regions associated with the organization of the native Universe. Each can contain numerous galaxies, inhabited worlds and supernatural civilizations together with dimensional realms connected to those civilizations.
The Dimensional Void is described as extending through the dimensional structure associated with a Sector, providing the surrounding interdimensional medium for its various realms.[2]
This relationship should not be interpreted as proof that each Sector necessarily possesses a completely independent Dimensional Void with no connection to any other Sector. The chapters establish the Void's relationship with the dimensional structure of the Cosmos without providing a complete map of every possible interdimensional connection.

#### The Abyss
Main article: The Abyss
The Abyss is fundamentally distinct from the Dimensional Void.
The Abyss is associated with the remnants of galaxies and cosmic regions that have reached their true End. Its defining characteristics involve the breakdown or absence of ordinary foundational Concepts, including Life, Time, Space and even the conventional Concept of Void.[9]
The Dimensional Void, by contrast, is an existing interdimensional expanse within Creation. Its relationship with Space and dimensional boundaries allows beings to enter it, move through it, locate other dimensions and manipulate the separation between realms.
The absence of conventional physical landmarks within the Dimensional Void should therefore not be confused with the more fundamental conceptual absence associated with the Abyss.

#### Nothingness
Main article: Nothingness
Nothingness is associated with the region beyond the coherent influence of the [[Akashic Records]], where the concepts defining Reality no longer operate according to the ordinary structure of the native Universe.
The Dimensional Void remains part of the native cosmological system and possesses recognizable relationships with Space, dimensions and supernatural travel. Nothingness represents a fundamentally different condition in which the organization of existence becomes far less coherent.
A being capable of entering the Dimensional Void does not automatically possess the ability to survive or navigate Nothingness.

#### Void Between Cosmoses
Main article: [[Void Between Cosmoses]]
The Void Between Cosmoses is associated with the separation between entirely different cosmological structures, including foreign Universes and Realities.
The Dimensional Void connects or separates dimensional realms belonging to the native cosmological structure, whereas the Void Between Cosmoses concerns travel between cosmologies whose fundamental Rules and sources of existence may differ.
These two structures should not be treated as interchangeable merely because both are described through the term Void.

#### Void of Existence
The Void of Existence is a conceptual condition associated with the absence of conventional physical or supernatural characteristics. [[Nocturnus]] possessed an unusual relationship with the Void and was described as having characteristics neither conventionally Positive nor Negative.[10]
This conceptual Void should not be confused with the Dimensional Void, which functions as an interdimensional environment through which separate realms can be accessed.
The distinction is important because the word Void may describe several fundamentally different phenomena throughout the novel.

#### Limbo
Main article: [[The Limbo Realm]]
The Limbo Realm is a specialized supernatural prison established through the authority of The Owner of Limbo. Its structure possesses protections and restrictions designed to confine exceptionally powerful beings.
Limbo is a distinct realm rather than a synonym for the Dimensional Void. Its construction, purpose and governing authority differ from the ordinary interdimensional expanse.
The existence of such a prison demonstrates the complexity of dimensional construction, but its exceptional characteristics should not automatically be attributed to every dimension or to the Void surrounding them.

### Trivia
- The Dimensional Void is also called the Void Between Dimensions and is explicitly distinguished from ordinary material Space.[1][2]
- Its principal cosmological role is the separation and connection of dimensional realms belonging to the native Universe.
- Its expanse is described as endless, but the novel does not establish a universally applicable numerical measurement of its complete size.[2]
- The Void has no confirmed permanent civilization or governing ruler of its own.
- Reaching the Void does not guarantee that the traveler can locate another dimensional realm or safely escape it.[8]
- Nyx's search for the [[Norse Pantheon]] demonstrates that even powerful Gods may require time and effort to navigate dimensional destinations.[5]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] demonstrated that dimensional exile can be achieved by combining teleportation systems originating from different supernatural disciplines.[1]
- The Concept of END possesses sufficient destructive significance to threaten the interdimensional expanse itself, requiring the intervention of [[True Death]] when [[Typhon]] was banished into it.[1]
- Intelligent Beasts of END may be capable of navigating out of the Void, while less rational beings can remain stranded.[8]
- Victor can forcibly breach a Pantheon's spatial boundary from the Void Between Dimensions through his advanced supernatural powers.[6]
- Dimensional separation and existential independence are distinct properties. An ordinary dimension can exist outside material Space while borrowing its foundational Rules from the larger Universe.[3][4]
- The Dimensional Void is not identical to the Abyss, Nothingness, the Void of Existence or the [[Void Between Cosmoses]].
- The term dimension in this context primarily refers to a separate supernatural realm or plane of existence, not automatically to a numbered mathematical spatial axis.[4]
