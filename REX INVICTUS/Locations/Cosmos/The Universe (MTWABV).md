The Universe is the principal cosmic system in which My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires takes place. At its largest established physical scale, it is an infinite and constantly expanding Universe containing innumerable astronomical structures, Sectors, galaxies, stars, worlds, civilizations and supernatural ecosystems.[1] The physical Universe forms only one part of the setting's complete cosmology, which additionally includes Divine Concepts, Souls, [[The Laws of Existence]], World Trees, dimensional Realms, the Regulators of Existence, the System and the Akashic Records.[2][3]
The word Universe is used at more than one scale throughout the story. Chapter 956 establishes that Sectors are divisions of the entire Universe, confirming the existence of a greater physical Universe containing the Sector hierarchy.[4] Individual Sector Realities may nevertheless also be called Universes in narration or dialogue because they function as enormous self-contained cosmic environments. The [[Magic]] World, [[Nightingale]] and other isolated Realities provide repeated examples of this usage.[5][6][7]
At the highest presently revealed scale, native Creation is only one complete Cosmos among others. [[Azathoth Elderblood]] demonstrates this by representing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s entire Universe—including its Rules and fundamental existence—as one sphere and placing beside it another Universe possessing different Rules, different Primordials and a different [[Primordial Chaos]].[3] The space separating such complete Creations is known as the [[Void Between Cosmoses]].

### Terminology

#### Universe
The greater physical Universe encompasses the Sector system and is explicitly described as infinite and constantly expanding.[1]
Infinity is the Primordial most closely connected to this cosmic expansion. During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Divine Ascension, Infinity's function is associated with the endless expansion of Creation.[12]

#### Sector
A Sector is one of the major divisions established by the Primordial order throughout the greater Universe.[4] Each Sector is separated by barriers and may contain countless galaxies, civilizations, worlds, Pantheons, [[World Trees]] and dimensional systems.

#### Reality
Reality is a broad term used for coherent systems of existence. Depending upon context, it can describe the surrounding Universe, an individual Sector, a supernatural Dimension or an artificially created environment.

#### Dimension
Dimensions are spatial or metaphysical Realms existing alongside ordinary astronomical Space. They range from comparatively simple pocket Dimensions to vast Pantheon Realms and highly complex artificial spaces.

#### Cosmos
A Cosmos is the broadest useful designation for a complete Creation with its own fundamental Rules and cosmic authorities. Chapter 1075 establishes that other Cosmoses can possess different Rules, Primordials and [[Primordial Chaos]].[3]

### Origin of Creation

#### Primordial Chaos
[[Primordial Chaos]] was the original supreme existence ruling native Reality.
Chapter 1137 identifies Primordial Chaos as the Being that created the entire native Universe and its Rules.[8]
The Primordial Chaos of one Cosmos is distinct from the [[Chaos]] belonging to another. Chapter 1075 establishes that independent Creations may possess their own Primordial Chaos, allowing separate Cosmoses to develop beneath fundamentally different cosmic orders.[3]

#### Positivity and Negativity
The unified primordial state was divided into Positivity and Negativity, two complementary universal principles whose influence permeates native Creation.[3][13]

The complete [[Primordial Chaos]] position is currently vacant.

### Akashic Records
Main article: [[Akashic Records]]
The Akashic Records preserve the information and history of native Creation.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s early explanation describes the Records as containing information concerning events occurring throughout the Universe.[16]
Their importance extends far beyond ordinary historical storage. Akashic information can remain relevant after:
- death;
- reincarnation;
- changes to identity;
- temporal alteration;
- existential erasure;
- and timeline convergence.[2][11][17]

#### Recorded Information
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] demonstrates a limited imitation of Akashic retrieval when reproducing information belonging to [[Adonis Snow]]. The process reconstructs preserved information instead of physically traveling into the past.[16]
The same underlying principle later becomes important when Victor restores [[Vanessa Alekerth]] from information preserved through her Soul and historical Record.[17]

### The System
Main article: System
The System is the principal administrative mechanism supporting the cosmological order.
Its functions encompass areas such as:
- identification of supernatural entities;
- Racial development;
- Divine status;
- Authority;
- Soul-related processes;
- reincarnation systems;
- cosmic abnormalities;
- and administrative interaction with the Primordials.
The System is primarily an administrative existence rather than a conventional combat entity.[14]

#### Post-Convergence Evolution
The timeline convergence substantially altered the System.
Historically, separate versions of the System operated throughout the infinite active timelines. Akashic later merged those counterparts into the System belonging to the Main Timeline.[11]
This increased its processing capability and contributed to the stronger manifestation and greater [[Will Solace|Will]] demonstrated in later chapters.
By Chapter 1176, even the surviving Primordials recognize that the System's behavior has changed in ways they do not completely understand.[14]

#### Destiny
Destiny was the eighth member of the original Primordial structure and governed the predetermined future of Creation.[11]
The Primordial was erased so completely that the other Primordials no longer retained ordinary awareness that Destiny had existed.
With Destiny gone, Creation no longer possesses the same predetermined future once enforced through that Primordial authority.[11]
Possible futures, prediction, prophecy and temporal observation can still exist without every observed future becoming an already-active independent timeline.

At the end of the process, Akashic merges the remaining System counterparts into the Main-Timeline System.[11]
The active cosmology is consequently reduced from its former infinite timeline structure to one principal Reality.

### Sectors
Main article: Sectors
A Sector is one of the fundamental divisions of the physical Universe.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] describes the boundaries between them as enormous invisible barriers established through the Primordial order.[4]
Sectors can contain:
- numerous galaxies;
- countless stellar systems;
- inhabited and uninhabited planets;
- supernatural civilizations;
- Gods and Pantheons;
- [[World Trees]];
- and attached dimensional Realms.

#### Sector Barriers
The barriers do more than organize Space.
They protect weaker Realities from stronger civilizations.
A civilization belonging to an Upper Sector cannot simply descend into a protected Lower Sector and conquer everything without interacting with the Primordial restrictions separating those regions.[9]
The opposite boundary becomes increasingly accessible as a Sector develops.
By Chapter 1136, the barrier separating [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Middle Sector from the Upper Sectors had weakened enough that Victor could pierce it using Non-Attributed Energy.[18]

#### Size
Sectors do not possess one fixed numerical diameter.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Middle Sector contains numerous galaxies, and Chapter 1136 shows his Cosmic Consciousness expanding through the Sector until it reaches its surrounding barrier.[18]
Using the World Tree network, Victor receives millions of connections distributed throughout the Reality and uses them to map the galaxies belonging to his Sector.[18]

By Chapter 1179, three Lower Sectors closely connected with the [[Elderblood Empire]] reach the limit of their existing development:

### Dimensions and Realms
The cosmology contains numerous supernatural spaces existing beside ordinary astronomical Space.

#### Dimensional Void
Main article: [[Dimensional Void]]
The Dimensional Void is the environment separating or connecting many Dimensions belonging to native Creation.[23]
It is distinct from the [[Void Between Cosmoses]].

#### Pantheon Dimensions
Many Pantheons possess supernatural territorial Realms such as:
- [[Greek Pantheon]];
- [[Greek Underworld]];
- [[Norse Pantheon]];
- [[Nordic Hell]];
- [[Biblical Pantheon]];
- [[Biblical Hell]];
- [[Shinto Pantheon]];
- and other Divine territories.
These Realms can contain vast landscapes and internal spatial systems while remaining parts of the native Cosmos.

#### Artificial Dimensions
Powerful beings can construct their own Dimensions through Space-Time abilities, Runes, [[Magic]] or Divine authority.
Chapter 1167 demonstrates the difference between a normal artificial Dimension and a Reality reinforced with deeper Concepts.[24]
An ordinary Dimension can borrow its foundational Rules from the Reality surrounding it.
A more heavily constructed Reality can incorporate Divine Concepts into its structure and develop far greater existential weight.

#### Existential Weight
[[Norabell Elderblood]] perceives this difference when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] creates:
1. an ordinary cube-shaped Dimension;
2. a miniature Galactic Reality reinforced with higher Concepts.[24]
The simple Dimension can be altered comparatively easily because its deeper Laws come from the surrounding Reality.
The Galactic construct contains more conceptual layers and therefore resists manipulation.
Chapter 1168 further develops this experiment by showing that Victor's artificial Eldritch organisms can imitate Life extremely convincingly while still lacking all of the deeper foundations possessed by genuine independent existence.[25]
Thus, greater existential weight allows an artificial Reality to become progressively more independent, while a fully complete autonomous Cosmos requires far more than simply producing matter, stars or living-looking organisms.

### The Abyss
Main article: The Abyss Realm
The Abyss exists in the deep regions of the physical Universe outside the normal predetermined Sector structure.[37]
It represents regions that have reached the terminal stage of cosmic development.

#### Dead Cosmic Regions
Within the Abyss, stellar and Galactic structures have already reached their End.
Stars disappear, black holes dominate enormous areas and the familiar conditions supporting ordinary cosmic Life cease to function normally.[37]
The Abyss therefore represents the opposite side of cosmic development from the [[Edge of the Cosmos]].

#### Creatures of the Abyss
[[Creatures of the Abyss]] inhabit these terminal regions.
Their power varies dramatically because the Abyss receives remnants and organisms associated with cosmic regions possessing very different levels of development.[37]

#### Relation to the System
The Abyss remains part of native Creation's known cosmological structure.
The System possesses information concerning its inhabitants and does not treat them as beings originating from an independent foreign Cosmos.[37]

### Edge of the Cosmos
Main article: [[Edge of the Cosmos]]
The Edge of the Cosmos is the frontier generated by the continued expansion of the infinite Universe.
During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s battle with [[Ikor-Kar-VI]], the combat reaches this region and exposes the unusual nature of newly forming Creation.[38][39][40]
At the Edge:
- stable Reality has not completely settled;
- Space behaves abnormally;
- Time becomes difficult to interpret;
- and the ordinary Rules governing mature cosmic regions become unstable.[40]
The phenomenon is directly connected with the Universe continuing to expand despite already being infinite.[40]
The Edge is consequently not a wall enclosing Creation.
It is the changing frontier at which new cosmic structure continues to stabilize.

### Void Between Cosmoses
Main article: [[Void Between Cosmoses]]
The Void Between Cosmoses is the exterior state separating complete Creations.[3]
Travelling through it is fundamentally different from:
- travelling between planets;
- crossing galaxies;
- moving between Sectors;
- or travelling through the [[Dimensional Void]].
Only extremely developed [[Primordial Chaos]] existences are naturally suited to surviving the journey.
Eldritch [[Chaos]] possesses greater compatibility with this environment, allowing beings such as Azathoth to survive conditions that would destroy ordinary native entities.[3]
The Void is also dangerous because other Primordial Chaos beings can attack travelling Chaos and consume their Cosmoses.[3]

### Outer Gods and Outsiders
Main article: [[Outer Gods]]
Outer Gods occupy an unusual place in the cosmology because their existence is not completely dependent upon the Rules of one native Cosmos.
Azathoth represents a genuine entity originating beyond [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Creation.[3]
Victor later becomes an unusual hybrid case:
- he was born inside native Creation;
- possesses native Divinities and authorities;
- was selected by Negativity;
- and simultaneously develops the Essence of an Eldritch [[Chaos]].[3]
Because Victor originated inside this Cosmos, Azathoth believes he can potentially take its empty [[Primordial Chaos]] throne despite possessing the nature of an Outsider.[3]

### Later Cosmological Developments

#### Moira Eutychia Elderblood
The birth of [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]] creates an effect perceived throughout the entire Universe.[36]
The System identifies Moira as an unprecedented anomaly whose existence naturally causes Reality to accommodate her desires.
According to the System, no Being since [[Primordial Chaos]] had naturally been born with equivalent compatibility with universal Authority.[36]
Because Destiny had been erased, the System attempts to recruit Moira as the new Primordial Destiny.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] refuses.
Moira consequently remains outside the Primordial office as of Chapter 1179.

#### Victor and Negativity
The confrontation surrounding Moira reveals the extent of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s connection to Negativity.
The System describes his position as carrying half of the authority related to the missing Rank-0 whole.[14]
Negativity exists throughout approximately half of native Reality's fundamental structure.
Victor demonstrates that forcing the Concept into catastrophic collapse would therefore damage enormous portions of the Universe and destabilize Positivity as well.[14]

#### Azathoth's Prison
Main article: [[Azathoth's Prison]]
Azathoth reveals in Chapter 1176 that her true body has been moved into a dimension outside the Universe.[14]
The ordinary Rules belonging to the native Creator do not apply there normally, and conventional Time becomes difficult to measure.
The location represents another exterior cosmological environment distinct from ordinary Sector or dimensional travel.

#### Imperial World Tree Network
Chapter 1178 establishes Mars as a central junction linking [[World Trees]] throughout the Galactic [[Elderblood Empire]].[22]

#### Sector Consolidation
Chapter 1179 begins the merger of the [[Magic]] World, [[Nightingale]] and Samar Lower Sectors into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wider Middle-Sector structure.[19]
These developments mark the transition from the earlier isolated Sector cosmology toward a progressively consolidated Imperial cosmic territory.

### Notes
- Universe, Reality, World, Dimension and Cosmos are used contextually throughout the novel. The surrounding scene determines the intended cosmological scale.
- Chapter 956 establishes that Sectors are divisions of the entire greater Universe.[4]
- The physical Universe is explicitly infinite and constantly expanding.[1]
- Historical Creation contained infinite active timelines; the present cosmology contains one Main Timeline.[10][11]
- Sector barriers separate cosmic regions and restrict travel between civilizations possessing radically different levels of development.
- Sectors can contain numerous galaxies and do not possess one established universal numerical diameter.
- The Abyss and [[Edge of the Cosmos]] are both regions of the native physical Universe but represent opposite cosmic processes: terminal decay and continuing expansion.
- The [[Dimensional Void]] and [[Void Between Cosmoses]] are separate environments.
- Foreign Cosmoses possess their own Rules, Primordials and [[Primordial Chaos]].[3]
- The complete Primordial Chaos throne of the native Cosmos remains vacant as of Chapter 1179.
- The merger of the Lower Sectors in Chapter 1179 does not automatically advance [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Middle Sector to Upper status.[19]
