Elvenorah, formally named Elvenorah, the Eternal Utopia and also referred to as Elvenorah City or the City of Velnorah, is one of the principal territories of the [[Elderblood Empire]] and [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]]. It developed from the conquered territory of the former [[Egyptian Pantheon]] after Victor Elderblood defeated its ruling Gods and incorporated the Divine realm, its resources and surviving population into his expanding faction.[3][4]
Rather than preserve the former Egyptian Divine order, Victor repurposed the territory into a new multiracial civilization intended to serve people belonging to his faction who either lacked sufficiently secure homelands or required a common territory capable of supporting vastly different supernatural species. [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]] eventually assumed responsibility for the city's development and transformed it into an enormous Technocracy integrating advanced extraterrestrial technology, [[Magic]], Divine infrastructure and the abilities of Victor's numerous subordinate races.[5]
By the later Imperial Era, Elvenorah had become considerably more than an ordinary city. Its dimensional territory was counted among the immense internal realms contained within Victor's Soul, it housed populations originating from multiple civilizations, incorporated the entire Arcane Realm, contained major Imperial infrastructure and functioned alongside [[Biblical Hell]] as one of the principal Divine territories of Dragon's Nest.[6][1][2]
Chapter 1100 describes Elvenorah and Hell collectively as the [[Rex Invictus|Home]] of the Gods or Heaven of Victor's Pantheon, while Victor's much more isolated Personal World serves as the private Paradise belonging principally to himself and his family.[2]

### History

#### Conquest of the Egyptian Pantheon
Elvenorah did not begin as an independently created city. Its foundation was the Divine territory previously belonging to the [[Egyptian Pantheon]].
The Egyptian Gods became enemies of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his allies during the conflict surrounding [[New Dawn]], the Elder Gods and the wider supernatural conspiracy. Once diplomacy failed, Victor's coalition attacked their forces and Victor personally confronted Ra. Rather than merely defeating Ra physically, Victor stripped away the Divine foundation supporting the Egyptian God-King and eventually acquired authority over the conquered Pantheon.[7][8][9]
The old Egyptian political order effectively ended afterward. Rather than allow another power vacuum to form, Victor immediately began claiming the abandoned territories, populations and resources left by the defeated factions and integrating them into the civilization developing around Dragon's Nest.[10]
The Egyptian Divine realm consequently ceased functioning primarily as the homeland of its previous Gods and became one of Victor's major territorial acquisitions.

#### The Great Relocation
The first major transformation of the territory began in Chapter 888.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] ordered allies belonging to his faction who lacked an appropriate homeland comparable to Samar to relocate into the conquered Egyptian territory.[3]
Among the populations transferred there were Noble Vampires allied with the great Vampire Clans surrounding Victor, including people associated with the Snow, Scarlett, Fulger, Adrasteia and other allied lineages. Their previous territories could consequently be returned to the remaining political structure of [[Nightingale]] rather than forcing Victor's growing population to remain scattered throughout lands controlled by other Vampire rulers.[3]
The Egyptian territory was particularly suitable because its lands were significantly more prosperous than ordinary Human regions. It had previously been a realm occupied by Gods, meaning its environment and resources included materials and conditions influenced by Divine power.[3]
Victor additionally protected relocated Vampires against the ordinary difficulties caused by sunlight, making the formerly Divine realm viable even for inhabitants whose species would traditionally have preferred Nightingale's permanent darkness.[3]
The migration extended far beyond Vampires.
Haruna's Youkai were assigned territory there, Humans connected to Victor's supernatural factions were relocated, and Goddesses without another strongly established homeland were likewise incorporated into the developing population.[3]
[[Werewolves]] represented an exception because they already possessed major territories of their own. Rather than transfer their entire civilization, they established an embassy inside the new territory.[3]
Victor's Demonic wives also arrived with multiple Demon Legions and established a permanent Demonic base, adding another species and military culture to what was rapidly becoming a deliberately multiracial settlement.[3]
Several hundred important members of the [[Blood God Religion]] additionally migrated while transporting their possessions and religious infrastructure, including an enormous statue of Victor.[3]
What had formerly been a predominantly Egyptian Divine territory was therefore transformed almost immediately into a meeting point for Vampires, Youkai, Humans, Gods, Demons, religious organizations and representatives of other species allied with Victor.

#### A Multiracial Civilization
The territory continued developing after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ascension as the Dragon God of [[Chaos]].
By Chapters 937–938, the former Egyptian realm had already become a visibly multiracial civilization where species that had historically remained politically and culturally separated could coexist in the same territory.[11][12]
Gods, Vampires, [[Werewolves]], Demons and other supernatural beings could openly move through the same society. The constant racial conflict traditionally expected between many of these groups was largely suppressed by the authority surrounding Victor. Citizens understood that internal conflicts capable of threatening the stability of his territory would ultimately attract the attention of the Emperor and the powerful rulers serving beneath him.[11]
Victor had not formally demanded that his population address him as the God Emperor, but the title nevertheless began spreading organically among the inhabitants. To people watching him conquer Pantheons, unite species and distribute new territories, "Emperor" alone increasingly seemed insufficient to describe his position.[11]
The city's architecture developed according to the same multicultural principle.
Rather than force every species into one identical architectural style, the rulers created separate cultural environments while ensuring that all districts remained compatible with the larger city infrastructure.[12]
Helena Gremory Elderblood and [[Aline Elderblood Valefar]] constructed residential areas for Haruna's Youkai and offered multiple possible designs, including a more technologically advanced aesthetic. Haruna deliberately selected traditional Japanese architecture because she wanted her people to preserve their cultural identity rather than live inside a completely futuristic environment.[12]
The resulting city therefore developed into a controlled mixture of:
- Egyptian cultural remnants;
- traditional Youkai architecture;
- Demonic construction;
- Vampire territories;
- Werewolf diplomatic areas;
- Divine residences;
- modern urban design;
- and highly advanced futuristic technology.[12]
Despite the enormous variety, the city was not intended to become an uncontrolled patchwork. Construction and territorial distribution were centrally supervised so different cultures could preserve their aesthetics without creating infrastructure incompatible with neighboring districts.[12]
[[Aphrodite]] Elderblood also played an important role during this period by helping manage the enormous number of people, Gods and supernatural groups receiving new territory.

#### The Eternal Utopia
After the destruction of the previous Norse political order and the events surrounding Hela's auction of [[Asgard]], [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] formally gave the former Egyptian territory its new identity.
In Chapter 973, the narration identifies Victor's personal castle as being located within the former [[Egyptian Pantheon]] and states that the territory had been:
renamed by Victor to Elvenorah, the Eternal Utopia.[4]
The name honored [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]], who was placed in charge of building the territory into something dramatically more advanced than the civilization it replaced.
The word Utopia was not merely decorative. Victor explicitly tasked Velnorah with attempting to create a civilization where radically different existences could coexist while receiving enough security and material support to pursue actual personal and civilizational development.[5]

#### Reconstruction
Approximately two months after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] announced Elvenorah's new identity and Velnorah's position, the entire city underwent an extraordinary reconstruction.
Despite its immense scale, the primary rebuilding process required less than two weeks.[5]
The rebuilt Elvenorah was deliberately enlarged. Velnorah understood that a civilization containing Humans, Vampires, Gods, Demons, Dragons and other supernatural species could not safely rely upon urban dimensions designed exclusively for ordinary Human bodies.
Buildings were enlarged, streets widened and general infrastructure redesigned so that beings approximately ten metres tall could comfortably use major sections of the city.[5]
This did not mean every resident or building was ten metres tall. The purpose was compatibility: Elvenorah was being constructed as a civilization capable of serving multiple species without forcing larger supernatural beings to constantly alter themselves merely to enter ordinary public spaces.
Transportation received similar attention.
[[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] and [[Hestia]] Elderblood assisted in redesigning the city's road and traffic infrastructure. Major highways were connected directly toward important establishments to minimize congestion even when the population included supernatural beings whose movement, vehicles and physical dimensions varied enormously.[5]
The result was deliberately futuristic without completely replacing the cultural districts established earlier.

### Government

#### Supreme Authority
Elvenorah ultimately exists beneath the sovereignty of:
- [[Victor Elderblood]] — Emperor of the [[Elderblood Empire]], God-King of [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]] and ultimate owner of the Soul-realm containing Elvenorah.
Victor possesses supreme authority over the territory but deliberately avoids personally micromanaging its ordinary administration.
His approach toward Elvenorah reflects one of the governing principles he developed as his civilization expanded: he surrounds himself with people who possess far greater expertise than him within specific fields and allows them genuine authority rather than pretending that being the strongest individual automatically makes him the best administrator, scientist or engineer.[5][13]

#### Velnorah
Main article: [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]]
The direct ruler and principal architect of modern Elvenorah is Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II.
Velnorah's previous experience with civilizations dramatically more technologically advanced than Earth made her especially suited to the role. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] therefore gave her broad freedom to rebuild the former Egyptian territory according to her own knowledge while providing the resources, supernatural specialists and political power necessary to implement her plans.[5]
For Velnorah, the project also carried substantial personal significance. Her history was heavily associated with the destruction and failures of her previous civilization. Elvenorah gave her an opportunity to use everything she had learned to construct an entirely new society rather than remain defined solely by the civilization she had lost.[13][14]
Victor trusted her enough that he primarily observed and questioned her plans rather than attempting to override them.

#### Technocracy
Elvenorah's governmental model differs noticeably from the monarchies traditionally associated with supernatural Pantheons.
Velnorah was developing the city into a Technocracy.[5]
The term does not mean [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] ceased being the absolute sovereign of the territory. Rather, it describes the city's internal administrative and social model: scientific knowledge, technological development, research, education and proven competence are deliberately given enormous importance in determining how society operates.
Velnorah effectively regarded the entire city as an enormous experiment.[5]
Instead of simply reproducing the economic and political systems of existing Human countries, Elvenorah was intended to test whether an advanced supernatural civilization could eliminate many basic material pressures and redirect more of its population toward productive development.
Residents receive access to fundamental necessities including:
- food;
- water;
- housing;
- and financial support necessary for ordinary participation in society.[5]
In exchange, the civilization continuously studies how its population responds to different social structures, technologies and policies. Velnorah limited the city's authorized experimentation to projects that were not intended to directly harm ordinary citizens.[5]
Thousands of observation systems and cameras were distributed throughout the territory, allowing the administration to collect information about social interactions, conflicts and the effectiveness of the developing system.[5]
The city was therefore both a home for millions of supernatural citizens and an ongoing large-scale study in civilization design.

#### Administration
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Velnorah did not want Elvenorah to depend completely upon one irreplaceable administrator.
Important positions were distributed among trusted individuals, while deputies were selected who could continue operating institutions when their primary leaders were absent.[5]
This was particularly important because many of Elvenorah's highest authorities were members of Victor's family and could frequently be required elsewhere for military, Divine or Imperial responsibilities.
The system was also deliberately structured to reduce conventional political corruption. Many of the most sensitive positions were entrusted to individuals whose direct connections to Victor made betrayal against the wider Empire extremely unlikely.

#### Security and Military
[[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] became heavily involved with Elvenorah's security and military administration during its reconstruction.
Her responsibilities included command over major portions of the city's armed/security forces, while another trusted woman connected to [[Blank Clan]] served as a deputy capable of acting in her absence.[5]
The city's security is further reinforced by the fact that Elvenorah exists inside [[Victor Elderblood]]'s Soul.
Although Victor is not constantly manually observing every resident, important Imperial territories located within him fall inside an environment where his awareness and Authority are overwhelmingly greater than they would be across ordinary external territory.[15]

### Society

#### The Eternal Utopia Project
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s objective for Elvenorah was not merely to construct another rich capital.
He ordered Velnorah to pursue an Eternal Utopia capable of allowing different forms of intelligent life to coexist and develop together.[5]
The project was partly ideological and partly practical.
Victor's faction contained an increasingly absurd number of incompatible civilizations:
- Vampires historically lived within aristocratic Clan structures;
- Youkai maintained strongly traditional societies;
- Demons had emerged from Hell's violent feudal order;
- Humans were accustomed to modern national governments;
- Gods came from Pantheons structured around worship and Divine authority;
- [[Werewolves]] organized themselves through packs and strength-based hierarchies;
- Witches maintained their own isolated magical civilization;
- and later extraterrestrial knowledge introduced social systems developed far beyond Earth.
Elvenorah attempted to give these groups one shared civic structure without requiring every culture to erase its own identity.

#### Multiracial Population
Elvenorah is among the most diverse known settlements associated with Dragon's Nest.
Confirmed populations or permanent representatives include:
- Vampires — particularly Noble Vampires associated with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s allied Clans.[3]
- Youkai — relocated beneath [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]].[3][12]
- Humans — including populations previously associated with Victor's Vampire territories and later Imperial citizens.[3]
- Gods and Goddesses — particularly Divine Beings integrated into Victor's faction without their own preferred territory.[3]
- Demons — including permanent military forces established by Victor's Demonic wives.[3]
- [[Werewolves]] — primarily represented through an embassy rather than a full racial relocation because the Werewolves already possessed major territories elsewhere.[3]
- Witches — following the relocation of the entire Arcane Realm into Elvenorah.[6]
- Members of the [[Blood God Religion]] — including several hundred important early members relocated during the initial settlement.[3]
Later Imperial development made the city's population even more varied as Dragon's Nest and the [[Elderblood Empire]] incorporated additional races and civilizations.

#### Social Stability
The early population contained groups with thousands of years of mutual hostility, yet open racial warfare failed to become the defining characteristic of the city.
A major reason was [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] himself.
By the time the settlement had matured, virtually every major racial leader understood that Victor would not tolerate attempts to destabilize a civilization belonging to his family. The same population that once might have viewed one another primarily as rival supernatural races consequently had an overwhelming common authority standing above them.[11]
That fear was accompanied by genuine respect and increasingly shared Imperial identity.
Elvenorah therefore became one of the earliest practical demonstrations of Victor's larger political philosophy: race determined biology and supernatural potential, but did not automatically determine whether someone was an ally, citizen, criminal or enemy.

### Architecture and Urban Design
Elvenorah does not possess one universal architectural style.
Its development deliberately incorporates multiple cultural aesthetics inside a common technological and infrastructural framework.[12]

#### Cultural Districts
Different racial and cultural populations were permitted to maintain recognizable environments rather than being forced into completely uniform futuristic construction.
Examples include:
- traditional Japanese-style territory associated with Haruna's Youkai;
- remnants and adaptations of Egyptian Divine architecture;
- Demonic construction;
- Vampiric districts;
- modern Human-style buildings;
- and extremely advanced futuristic structures produced through Velnorah's technology.[12]
This mixture was carefully planned rather than allowed to develop randomly.

#### Yokai Residential District
One of the clearest examples is the residential territory created for the Youkai.
Helena Gremory Elderblood and [[Aline Elderblood Valefar]] prepared multiple possible designs for Haruna's population, including advanced futuristic structures.
Haruna rejected the more futuristic option and deliberately selected traditional Japanese architecture because she wanted the Youkai to preserve recognizable aspects of their culture.[12]
The district demonstrates the basic principle behind Elvenorah's cultural design: technological compatibility does not require every species to abandon its history or aesthetics.

#### Large-Species Accommodation
Modern Elvenorah was redesigned to accommodate intelligent beings reaching approximately ten metres in height.[5]
Public infrastructure, roads and buildings were enlarged accordingly.
This accommodation became increasingly important as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction incorporated Dragons, giants, Divine Beings and extraterrestrial species whose natural sizes could vastly exceed those of ordinary Humans.

#### Transportation Network
The rebuilt city possesses an extensively reorganized road system.
Major highways were connected directly toward important locations, reducing unnecessary intersections and minimizing the possibility of severe congestion among supernatural populations whose transportation needs differ significantly from ordinary Humans.[5]

### Major Locations and Infrastructure

#### Victor's Castle
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] maintained a personal castle in the territory even before its formal renaming.
Chapter 973 begins inside a room of Victor's castle in the former [[Egyptian Pantheon]] immediately before the narration formally identifies the realm as Elvenorah, the Eternal Utopia.[4]
The castle should not be confused with Victor's primary private residence upon [[Etherea]].

#### Imperial Guest Castle
During the early construction period, a large white castle was being prepared specifically for important visitors.[12]
The structure was designed on an enormous scale capable of accommodating beings who might appear in much larger supernatural forms, reflecting Elvenorah's expected role as a diplomatic center for races and Gods whose physical sizes could vary dramatically.

#### Werewolf Embassy
Rather than relocating the Werewolf civilization completely, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] ordered the faction to establish an embassy inside the former Egyptian territory.[3]
The embassy allowed [[Werewolves]] to maintain permanent political representation within Elvenorah while retaining their primary homeland elsewhere.

#### Demonic Base
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Demonic wives arrived during the original migration accompanied by multiple Demon Legions and established permanent military infrastructure within the territory.[3]
This made Elvenorah one of the places where the military integration between Hell and Victor's broader faction became physically visible.

#### Blood God Religion Territory
Several hundred important members of the [[Blood God Religion]] migrated during the initial settlement and transported significant religious property, including an enormous statue of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[3]
The migration helped establish the Religion's presence inside one of Dragon's Nest's principal Divine territories rather than leaving it centered exclusively on Earth.

#### Tower of Nightmare
Main article: [[The Tower of Nightmares]]
An older/prototype version of the Tower of Nightmare existed within the developing territory by Chapter 938.[12]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] intended the Tower system to become a method through which ordinary citizens and supernatural inhabitants could safely develop their abilities.
At this stage, the structure was not necessarily openly visible or available to every ordinary resident. Its presence represented Victor's much larger plan to transform military and supernatural advancement into institutional infrastructure rather than something available only to elite families.

#### The Arcane Realm
Main article: [[The Arcane Realm]]
One of the greatest additions to Elvenorah occurred in Chapter 990.
After curing [[Emily Moriarty]] and reaching an agreement with Evie Moriarty, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] enveloped the entire Arcane Kingdom in his Energy and physically relocated it.[6]
The complete realm disappeared from its previous location and reappeared:
specifically, in the city ruled by Velnorah.[6]
Victor then described Elvenorah to Evie as:
a technocracy ruled by my wife Velnorah, the place where you and the witches will live from now on.[6]
The entire Witch Queendom therefore became part of Elvenorah's larger dimensional territory rather than merely establishing an embassy or residential district there.
[[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]] was already familiar with the city and admitted that she could not remain away while such an interesting civilization was being created.[6]
This integration added Earth's principal Witch civilization, its accumulated magical knowledge and Arcane's unique dimensional structure to Elvenorah.

#### Dragon Heart and Imperial Core Project
Velnorah and her daughter [[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]] later became central to the creation of one of the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s most important technological artifacts.
Working with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], they created the Dragon Hearts, constructs combining Technomancy, Divine Materials, Runes, Divine craftsmanship and Victor's own power.[16]
The completed prototype manifested as an approximately ten-metre-tall dark-violet icosahedron covered in Draconic Runes and pulsating in a manner resembling a living heart.[16]
Victor further modified the artifact using properties associated with [[Junketsu]], a fragment of his Soul and his own Reality-altering abilities.
Its Energy output rose from its original measurements toward more than 10,000% while continuing to increase, and the Energy generated was sufficiently pure to be converted into different forms required by the Empire.[16]
Victor consequently described the project as:
the core of the Empire from now on.[16]
The Dragon Hearts became foundational to later projects involving:
- strategic weapons;
- large-scale Energy production;
- artificial soldiers;
- advanced Imperial infrastructure;
- and the future technological development of the civilization.[16]
However, Chapter 1004 does not clearly establish the exact physical location of the prototype. Because the project was principally developed through Velnorah and her technological administration, it is strongly associated with Elvenorah's Technocracy, but the [[Dragon Heart]] should not be listed as a confirmed physical sub-location of the city unless a later chapter explicitly places it there.

### Size
Elvenorah's name is somewhat misleading because the word city suggests an ordinary urban settlement.
Its actual dimensional territory is vastly larger.
During Jeanne's explanation of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul in Chapter 1006, the City of Velnorah is treated alongside Victor's Personal World and Hell as one of the three enormous Dimensions contained within his Soul, with each described as being larger than Earth.[1]
Accordingly, references to buildings, roads or a "city" should not be interpreted as establishing conventional metropolitan dimensions.
Elvenorah is effectively a planetary-scale or greater dimensional civilization organized around an urban identity.
Its size later becomes even more difficult to represent conventionally after entire independent regions such as [[The Arcane Realm]] are inserted into its territory.

### Relationship with Victor's Soul
Main article: [[Victor Elderblood]]/Personal Universe
Elvenorah exists inside Victor Elderblood's Soul.
This is not merely a metaphor for political ownership.
Chapter 1100 directly states that Elvenorah is located within Victor's Soul and identifies it as one of his principal Pantheon territories.[2]
Chapter 1124 further clarifies its relative spiritual location.
Victor's private personal planet exists within a deeper section of his Soul, whereas Hell and the City of Velnorah occupy comparatively more superficial regions.[15]
Deeper still lie Victor's [[World Trees]], followed by his Soul Core and finally the concealed Eldritch Core associated with his True/Eldritch existence.[15]
The term "superficial" therefore describes metaphysical depth rather than importance or physical size.
Elvenorah remains one of the Empire's most significant territories despite existing farther from Victor's deepest Soul layers.

### Role in Dragon's Nest

#### Home of the Gods
By Chapter 1100, Elvenorah's Divine function had become explicitly defined.
The narration explains that:
Hell and Elvenorah could be considered the [[Rex Invictus|Home]] of the Gods or the Heaven of the Pantheon.[2]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Personal World, by contrast, functioned as his private Paradise and possessed substantially greater isolation.
Elvenorah should therefore be considered one of the principal Divine homelands of [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]], not merely an Imperial civilian city.

#### Afterlife System
Elvenorah is directly involved in the Pantheon's spiritual infrastructure.
Elvenorah, Hell and the Cities of Hell are interconnected and collectively participate in the system handling Souls belonging to people who worship the Gods of Dragon's Nest.[2]
This means Elvenorah possesses both a living civilian population and a function related to the Pantheon's dead.
The precise destination of an individual Soul can vary according to the Pantheon's systems, beliefs and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s evolving afterlife administration.

#### Connection to the Universe
Unlike [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s private Paradise, Elvenorah is not completely cut away from the surrounding cosmology.
Chapter 1100 explicitly states that Elvenorah and Hell retain connections to the wider Universe.[2]
Those connections possess strategic disadvantages.
Primordial administrators could exploit the remaining cosmological pathways to enter the realms, leading [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] to identify their continued external connection as a security weakness that eventually needed to be eliminated.[2]
Victor's Personal Paradise did not suffer from the same problem because its only fundamental connection was described as Victor himself.

#### Imperial Resource Center
By Chapter 1100, much of the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s accumulated resources remained concentrated within its internal Domains, with Elvenorah specifically identified as one of the most important locations holding those assets.[2]
The city consequently serves several simultaneous roles:
- Divine homeland;
- administrative center;
- technological center;
- multiracial population center;
- resource repository;
- religious territory;
- and part of the afterlife infrastructure.
This combination makes Elvenorah one of the most strategically important locations under [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s control.

### Role in the Elderblood Empire
Main article: [[Elderblood Empire]]
Elvenorah predates the formal establishment of the Elderblood Empire.
The city was already developing beneath Dragon's Nest during the period when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction remained a collection of conquered territories, allied supernatural civilizations and Pantheon holdings.[4][5]
Once Victor formally reorganized his holdings into an Empire, Elvenorah naturally became one of its major central territories rather than being replaced by the new Imperial political structure.
Its importance increased because many of the institutions, researchers and resources responsible for the Empire's technological development were directly associated with Velnorah.
By Chapter 1100, [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]], [[Aline Elderblood Valefar]] and Velnorah were described as the three women possessing the greatest influence over the Empire's technological development, regardless of the narrower departmental titles officially assigned to them.[2]
Elvenorah therefore represents an important bridge between two stages of Victor's civilization:
Dragon's Nest multiracial territory

Elvenorah, the Eternal Utopia
↓
Major internal territory of the Elderblood Empire
It should not be confused with Victorius Aeternus on [[Yggdrasil]], Victor's private world [[Etherea]], or the cities of [[Biblical Hell]].
Each serves a separate function within the Empire.

### Later Imperial Era
The city's importance continued after the establishment of the [[Elderblood Empire]] and the enormous temporal development that followed.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s accelerated civilization eventually operated for tens of thousands and later more than one hundred thousand subjective years, during which the technologies, species and institutions originally concentrated around Elvenorah continued developing alongside the rest of the Empire.
The city's role gradually became less exclusive as Victor constructed entire planetary networks, scientific worlds, Legions and specialized Imperial Departments elsewhere. Nevertheless, Elvenorah remained one of the civilization's oldest and most important internal Domains.
Chapter 1137 further demonstrates that Victor continued using Velnorah's internal territory to house people personally important to him who did not belong upon the intensely private planet reserved principally for his wives and daughters.[17]
The distinction reflects the mature geography of Victor's Personal Universe:
- [[Etherea]] / Personal Paradise — private territory of Victor and his closest family;
- Elvenorah — major Divine, civilian, administrative and Imperial domain;
- Hell — Infernal civilization and major afterlife territory;
- other internal Dimensions — specialized populations and functions.

### Clarifications
- Elvenorah is not located on [[Etherea]]. It is a separate dimensional territory within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Personal Universe.[1][15]
- Elvenorah and the City of Velnorah refer to the same territory. Later narration occasionally uses Velnorah's name descriptively because she rules the city.[15]
- Elvenorah is not the original [[Egyptian Pantheon]] politically. It uses the conquered Egyptian Divine territory as its foundation, but the old Egyptian governmental structure was destroyed and replaced by Victor and Velnorah's civilization.[3][4]
- Elvenorah predates the formal [[Elderblood Empire]]. It was originally developed as a major territory of Dragon's Nest and only later became part of the Empire when Victor reorganized his civilization.[4][20]
- Calling Elvenorah a "city" should not be interpreted literally as ordinary metropolitan scale. The associated Dimension is stated to be larger than Earth.[1]
- [[The Arcane Realm]] was physically relocated into Elvenorah. The Witches did not merely establish an embassy or political alliance there.[6]
- Elvenorah is more externally connected than Victor's Personal Paradise. Primordial administrators can potentially enter through its remaining connections to the Universe and larger cosmological systems.[2]
- Its position in a "superficial" layer of Victor's Soul does not imply that Elvenorah is small or unimportant. The term describes metaphysical depth relative to Victor's deeper Personal World, [[World Trees]] and Soul Core.[15]
- The [[Dragon Heart]] is strongly associated with Velnorah's technological program, but Chapter 1004 does not explicitly state where the prototype is physically installed. It should therefore not presently be listed as a confirmed geographical sub-location of Elvenorah.[16]
- Elvenorah is not simply the capital of one supernatural race. Its defining purpose is the coexistence and development of multiple species beneath the same civilization.
- Elvenorah and Hell together can function as the Heaven/Home of the Gods of Dragon's Nest, while Victor's Personal World is his private Paradise. These are separate functions within Victor's larger Soul domain.[2]

### Trivia
- The name Elvenorah was given by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] in honor of [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]], who became the principal ruler and architect of the modern territory.[4]
- Its complete title is Elvenorah, the Eternal Utopia.[4]
- The modern city's primary reconstruction was completed in less than two weeks despite its enormous scale.[5]
- The rebuilt infrastructure was intentionally made large enough for beings approximately ten metres tall.[5]
- Velnorah considers the city itself an enormous social and technological experiment.[5]
- Albedo was already spending significant Time in Elvenorah before the Arcane Realm was relocated there because she found the developing Technocracy too interesting to ignore.[6]
- Elvenorah's multicultural architectural system permits a district to use traditional construction externally while remaining connected to highly advanced common infrastructure.[12]
- The [[Werewolves]] were among the few major allied populations not ordered to fully relocate because they already possessed suitable territory; they established an embassy instead.[3]
- The city became one of the earliest examples of Victor deliberately attempting to create a civilization where historically hostile supernatural species lived beneath one shared political order.
- Despite being one of Victor's internal territories, Elvenorah is not as inaccessible as his Personal Paradise and therefore remained a potential route for Primordial interference as of Chapter 1100.[2]
