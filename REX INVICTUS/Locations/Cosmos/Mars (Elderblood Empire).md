Mars is a planet of the Solar System and the current capital world of the Elderblood Empire. Originally the barren neighboring planet of [[Yggdrasil]], Mars was completely reconstructed by Victor Elderblood, [[Anna Elderblood]], Natalia and Zaladrac near the conclusion of the Empire's 200,000-year development project. Victor transformed the comparatively small barren world into an enormous megaplanet capable of supporting trillions of inhabitants, filled it with radically different supernatural environments and lifeforms, and made it the central node of the Empire's new World Tree Network.[1]
Mars was chosen to replace Yggdrasil as the Empire's primary population and administrative center. Victor wanted his homeworld preserved rather than continuously expanded to accommodate an ever-growing population, intending for Yggdrasil to eventually become an empty protected garden world commemorating the birthplace of his civilization. Mars consequently represents the Empire's transition from a civilization originating on one world into a mature intergalactic society spread across countless worlds and galaxies.[1]

### History

#### Barren Mars
For most of its existence, Mars remained the barren planet familiar to Humanity. Even after the creation of the [[Elderblood Empire]] and the transformation of [[Yggdrasil]]'s civilization into an interstellar power, Mars itself remained largely unchanged.[2]
Near the end of the 200,000-year temporal development project, population concentration became increasingly problematic. Samar, [[Nightingale]] and Yggdrasil had developed into immense economic and population centers, and billions of citizens naturally wanted to live in places where the Imperial Family and especially [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] could be encountered more frequently. Rather than artificially increase housing prices or restrict settlement through ordinary economic measures, Victor decided to solve the problem by creating more habitable space.[2]
Victor initially considered simply enlarging an existing populated planet. While looking upon the still-barren Mars, however, he changed his plan and decided to transform it into something far more important.[2]

#### The New Heart of the Empire
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] declared that Mars would become the Empire's main planet, while [[Yggdrasil]] would be preserved as a garden world and the historical cradle of Imperial civilization. With the 200,000th anniversary of the [[Imperial Calendar]] approaching, he considered the transfer symbolic of the beginning of another stage in the Empire's history.[1]
Victor's long-term intention was to remove the sentient population from Yggdrasil entirely. His homeworld would remain protected as the "jewel of the Empire", while Mars would become the primary gathering point for the Empire's increasingly immense population.[1]
There was also a practical reason for choosing Mars. Victor intended to perform an experiment involving the Empire's [[World Trees]], but attempting it on a planet already possessing an established World Tree risked overwhelming that Being with an impossible volume of information. Mars possessed no native World Tree, making it an ideal foundation for the experiment.[1]

### Reconstruction
The reconstruction of Mars was performed primarily by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] with direct assistance from Anna, Natalia and Zaladrac. Because Victor had already expended more than 80% of his available strength sustaining the Empire's enormous temporal ritual, he relied upon his wives to reduce the burden of the operation.[1]
Natalia controlled the surrounding Space and Time, isolating the process and ensuring that the enormous alterations could be performed without disrupting the outside universe. Anna reinforced the planet's physical structures, allowing Mars to survive transformations that would otherwise have destroyed an ordinary celestial body. Victor handled the more complicated reconstruction and manipulation of the planet itself, while Zaladrac voluntarily joined the operation and assisted the others.[1]
The process lasted approximately seven months within the isolated Space-Time field while essentially no time passed outside. Victor stated that, had he possessed his full strength rather than operating while heavily drained by the greater ritual, he could have completed the same work in approximately seven minutes.[1]

### Scale
The original Mars was expanded into a colossal megaplanet. The novel does not provide a numerical diameter for its reconstructed form, but explicitly states that the resulting planet could easily support trillions of inhabitants.[1]
During the reconstruction, Anna and Natalia assumed Dragon forms already far larger than the original Mars despite continuing to restrain their true sizes. Their unrestricted Dragon forms were stated to be capable of reaching Solar-System scale, while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] remained considerably larger still. Victor temporarily adjusted his own Dragon form to approximately the same scale as his wives while working on Mars.[1]
The resulting world should therefore not be treated as ordinary astronomical Mars after Chapter 1178. Its physical structure, habitable area, internal stability and ecosystems were deliberately reconstructed through supernatural means.

### Environment
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] deliberately rejected the idea of transforming Mars into a simple paradise. He wanted the new Imperial capital to be a world capable of challenging its inhabitants rather than a perfectly safe environment.[1]
The reconstructed planet contains numerous artificial environmental regions, including:
- Magma Regions — enormous zones dominated by pure magma and extreme heat.[1]
- Eternal Ice Regions — permanently frozen areas designed around extreme cold.[1]
- High-Gravity Zones — territories where gravity is intentionally increased far beyond ordinary planetary conditions.[1]
- Weightless Zones — areas where conventional gravitational conditions are greatly reduced or absent.[1]
- Miasma Regions — dangerous locations saturated with pure Miasma.[1]
- Floating Islands — enormous islands suspended in the sky through Victor's reconstruction of the planetary environment.[1]
- Oceans — newly developed seas inhabited by smaller Leviathans and other dangerous aquatic organisms.[1]
- Monster Territories — regions populated by primordial and mutated organisms deliberately designed to resemble Monsters rather than conventional animals.[1]
- [[Nightingale]] Fauna — several species of Nightingale Monsters were transplanted onto Mars as part of its new ecosystem.[1]
The enormous differences between these environments were intentional. Mars was designed not merely as a place to live but as a world where Imperial citizens would encounter environments capable of forcing adaptation, training and continued development.

### Life and Ecosystem
Mars originally lacked the planetary supernatural foundation normally provided by a World Tree. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] initially considered performing that function himself, as his powers over Life and planetary development were sufficient to support the planet directly.[1]
He ultimately rejected the idea because his Energy had become far too powerful and abnormal. Directly permeating Mars with Victor's Energy would expose future generations to the Eldritch characteristics mixed into his existence, creating the possibility that inhabitants could eventually discover methods of exploiting or misusing that power.[1]
Instead, Victor populated Mars through deliberately introduced ecosystems and relied upon the collective World Tree network to maintain its deeper planetary functions. Smaller Leviathans were introduced into the oceans, numerous prehistoric or mutated species were created, and Monsters originating from [[Nightingale]] were transplanted into suitable environments.[1]

### World Tree Network
Mars became the physical hub of one of the most important infrastructure systems in the later [[Elderblood Empire]].
Rather than assigning a single World Tree to the planet, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] called upon the numerous [[World Trees]] connected to him. The Trees emerged from his body as light and extended their roots into Mars, creating a collective planetary structure rather than allowing one individual Tree to bear the entire burden.[1]
This arrangement solved the information-overload problem that prevented Victor from using an ordinary World Tree-bearing planet for the experiment. A single established Tree receiving information from the Empire's enormous network could potentially be overwhelmed to the point of entering a coma. Mars instead became the common junction through which the wider network could operate collectively.[1]
Victor subsequently placed on Mars a central core containing the seedlings of the connected World Trees. Through this system, Mars became the Empire's logistical and informational "heart".[1]

#### Communication
Before the Mars network was completed, even the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s extremely advanced communication technology still required minutes for information originating in sufficiently distant galaxies to reach the Imperial center. Considering the enormous distances involved, this was already considered an extraordinary technological achievement.[1]
The World Tree Network effectively removed that limitation. Once the system came online, Akasha confirmed that information from even the most distant connected galaxies reached the Imperial center immediately. She described the Empire's galactic communication capabilities as having improved by approximately one thousand percent.[1]
The network therefore provides:
- Virtually instantaneous communication between connected Imperial worlds.[1]
- Near-instantaneous transmission across Galactic and intergalactic distances.[1]
- Integration with the [[Dragon Heart]], which became directly connected to the network.[1]
- Connection between planets distributed throughout countless galaxies.[1]
- The infrastructure necessary for virtually instantaneous transport through World Tree structures.[1]
This transformed the [[World Trees]] from isolated planetary life-support systems into part of a civilization-scale information and transportation network.

#### Security
The [[World Trees]] physically associated with the Mars network are not truly dependent upon the planet for survival. Their protected existences remain connected to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and reside within Victor's inner world. Consequently, simply destroying Mars would not destroy the actual Trees responsible for sustaining its systems.[1]
Victor considered this one of the planet's greatest safeguards. An enemy attempting to cripple the Empire by destroying its capital could eliminate Mars' physical body, yet the World Trees would remain unharmed within Victor. Provided those Trees survived, the planet could be regenerated.[1]
The network also raised concerns regarding the relationship between ordinary World Trees and [[The Universal Tree]]. Akasha confirmed that World Trees remained connected to the Universal Tree at a fundamental level, but Victor's presence interfered with that communication. Violet additionally proposed installing Eldritch Runes that would sever Imperial communication automatically if one of the Trees left the security of Victor's domain, although Roxanne considered such precautions largely unnecessary because the Trees had little interest in leaving.[1]

### Planetary Defenses
Mars was intentionally constructed as both the Empire's capital and a gigantic defensive trap.
After completing the primary reconstruction, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] installed hundreds of traps throughout the planet. These systems were designed so that ordinary Imperial citizens could not accidentally activate them. Their intended targets were hostile outsiders, effectively making Mars itself function as a planet-wide automated defensive system.[1]
The exact nature of most traps has not yet been revealed. Their existence nevertheless means that the visible dangerous environments of Mars represent only part of its defenses. A hostile force successfully reaching the planet would still have to contend with hidden mechanisms deliberately created by Victor to punish invaders.[1]
The World Tree system provides an additional layer of strategic resilience. Because the actual Trees remain protected through Victor's Soul and the planet can theoretically be regenerated while they survive, destroying Mars physically is insufficient to permanently eliminate its central infrastructure.[1]

### Imperial Capital
After the reconstruction was completed, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] created a gigantic castle upon one of Mars' floating islands with a single thought. The structure became one of the first confirmed major constructions on the newly remade world.[1]
[[Violet Elderblood Snow]], acting as Empress, subsequently issued an Empire-wide proclamation. [[Akasha Elderblood]] coordinated the transmission, projecting Violet simultaneously through enormous holograms across Imperial metropolises and distant Galactic colonies.[1]
During the proclamation, Violet presented the transformed planet to the population and formally declared:
Today I, Empress Violet Elderblood, Dragon Goddess of Order, proclaim: From this day forward, Mars is the new capital of the Empire.
—Violet Elderblood Snow, Chapter 1178
The declaration marked the formal transfer of the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s principal center away from [[Yggdrasil]]. Mars was presented not merely as another colony but as a symbol of what the Empire had become: a civilization that had moved beyond its original world, founded cities across distant Solar Systems and conquered entire Galaxies.[1]
Violet also confirmed that a major city would be constructed on Mars to represent the modern Empire. The city's name and complete structure have not yet been revealed.[1]

### Relationship with Yggdrasil
Main article: Earth
Mars and [[Yggdrasil]] occupy fundamentally different roles in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s mature Empire.
Yggdrasil remains Victor's homeworld and the historical cradle of the civilization that eventually became the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Rather than continue increasing its population until the planet became little more than an overcrowded administrative center, Victor intends to preserve it as a protected garden world without a permanent sentient population.[1]
Mars, by contrast, represents the Empire's future. It was reconstructed specifically to support enormous populations, centralize infrastructure and act as the physical heart of a civilization that now spans countless worlds.

### Known Locations

#### Imperial Castle
A gigantic castle created directly by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] after the planet's reconstruction. It stands upon one of Mars' floating islands and was produced almost instantly after the seven-month reconstruction process concluded.[1]

#### Floating Islands
Numerous floating landmasses exist throughout Mars' sky. At least one is sufficiently large to support the Imperial castle.[1]

#### World Tree Core
The central core into which the connected [[World Trees]] planted their seedlings was placed on Mars by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. It functions as a major physical component of the World Tree Network and helps establish Mars as the heart of the Empire's communications and transportation infrastructure.[1]

#### Future Imperial City
Violet announced that the Empire would construct a city on Mars intended to reflect the civilization's development and new identity. No official name has yet been revealed.[1]

### Strategic Importance
Mars combines several functions that were previously distributed across numerous Imperial territories:
- Political Center: It is the officially proclaimed capital of the [[Elderblood Empire]].[1]
- Population Center: The planet was engineered to comfortably support trillions of inhabitants.[1]
- Communications Center: It houses the hub of the World Tree Network, enabling effectively instantaneous communication across the connected Empire.[1]
- Transportation Center: World Tree structures provide the basis for virtually instantaneous transport between connected territories.[1]
- Defensive Fortress: Hundreds of automated traps and hostile environmental regions turn the planet itself into part of the Empire's defensive system.[1]
- Regenerative World: The [[World Trees]] supporting Mars remain protected within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], allowing the planet to theoretically be rebuilt even after catastrophic destruction.[1]
- Symbolic Center: Mars embodies the Empire's transition from a civilization centered upon one homeworld into a multi-Galactic power.[1]
