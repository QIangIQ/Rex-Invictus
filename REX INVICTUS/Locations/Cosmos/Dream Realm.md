#### First Mention

#### First Appearance
Chapter 974
The Dream Realm is the collective name for the artificial worlds and alternate realities created by [[Victor Elderblood]] through his Divine authority over Dreams. Rather than being a single conventional planet or permanently fixed Dimension, the Dream Realm is a malleable Reality whose geography, inhabitants, history, passage of Time and even the conditions under which its occupants exist can be determined by Victor.
Victor first demonstrated practical Dream manipulation through a Dream Field capable of altering subjective Time, allowing approximately seven days to be experienced within the affected area while only one day passed outside.[2] His mastery later advanced considerably beyond accelerated dreaming. By combining the Divinity of Dream with Akashic information, Creation and his wider supernatural abilities, Victor became capable of creating entire false alternate realities that were experienced as genuine Reality by those placed within them.[3]
The most important early example was the artificial Reality created for [[Nero Elderblood]] and [[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]]. Victor used the Dream Realm to provide his daughters with experiences that their privileged existence as members of the Elderblood family could not naturally provide, stimulating their minds and Souls until they became capable of beginning the path toward genuine Divinity.[4][5][6][7][3]
Victor later developed the principle much further during the War Games of his daughters. Instead of creating a short scenario, he established a shared Dream world in which participants lived for years, developed careers, commanded armies, created political organizations and interacted with inhabitants who considered themselves genuine living beings. Some participants experienced approximately a decade within the Dream while comparatively little Time passed in normal Reality.[8][9]
The Dream Realm is consequently one of Victor's most versatile creations: a Reality-sized training system capable of providing Time, experience, adversity and entire artificial lives without requiring those lessons to occur in the physical world.

### Origin
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] obtained the Divinity of Dream during the events surrounding the exchange of Primordial Essences involving [[Hela Elderblood]], [[Shiva]] and Victor.[10]
His first major practical demonstration came shortly afterward. Victor established a Dream Field capable of causing those within it to experience approximately seven days while only one day passed outside.[2]
At this stage, Dream was already considerably more useful than conventional illusion. Victor was not simply creating visual hallucinations. He could alter subjective experience and Time sufficiently for the period experienced within the Dream to possess practical value.
Later developments transformed this basic Dream authority into complete artificial realities.

### Nature

#### Artificial Reality
The Dream Realm is not merely a collection of images placed into someone's mind. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later Dreams can possess:
- geography;
- inhabitants;
- societies;
- political factions;
- ecosystems;
- histories;
- personal identities;
- supernatural systems;
- relationships;
- conflict;
- and an internal passage of Time.
The people affected experience these circumstances as their current Reality. Victor explicitly developed this method by placing a target within a Dream while drawing upon information available through the [[Akashic Records]], creating a false alternate reality capable of stimulating both the target's mental state and Soul.[3]
The Reality is false in the sense that it does not replace the actual historical timeline. However, the experiences undergone by the participant are meaningful enough to produce genuine psychological, spiritual and even conceptual development.

#### Not a Fixed Dimension
Unlike [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Personal Universe, World of Water or a Pantheon Dimension, the Dream Realm has not been shown to possess one permanent geography.
Victor instead creates the environment required for a particular purpose.
One Dream may consist only of a room affected by altered subjective Time.[2] Another may become an alternate world containing entire civilizations and years of history.[3][8]
For this reason, "Dream Realm" should be understood as the encyclopedia term for the class of Reality Victor creates through his Dream authority rather than one permanently static planet.

### Creation

#### Dream Divinity
The primary foundation of the Dream Realm is [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s authority over Dreams.[10][2]
This allows him to:
- place others into Dreams;
- manipulate Dream environments;
- connect different individuals to a shared Dream;
- create artificial experiences;
- alter subjective Time;
- and construct Dream Realities.

#### Akashic Records
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s most sophisticated artificial Reality uses information from the [[Akashic Records]].
During [[Nero]]'s trial, Victor explained his method directly. By placing the target in a Dream while accessing the Akashic Records, he could construct a false alternate reality specifically designed to stimulate the mind and Soul.[3]
This is particularly important because it explains why the Dream can reproduce individuals and situations with considerably greater depth than a normal illusion.
Victor is not necessarily inventing every personality and historical detail manually. He can use Records as informational foundations and then construct a Reality from them.

#### Creation and Other Authorities
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Dream constructions have also been associated with his powers over Creation and Madness, giving him additional methods of shaping the environment and making its circumstances convincing.[6][7][3]
The Dream Realm should therefore not be treated as a feat produced by Dream alone in every case. Victor frequently combines multiple authorities when constructing complex supernatural systems.

Victor's later shared Dream demonstrates substantially greater subjective duration. During the Elderblood War Games, members of the family had experienced approximately ten years inside the Dream.[8][9]
This makes the Dream Realm particularly useful for training because its occupants can accumulate years of experience without requiring the same amount of external Time.

#### Shared Dreaming
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] can place numerous people into the same Dream World.
This was explicitly demonstrated during the later War Games involving his family, in which his daughters, wives and other participants occupied the same developing world and interacted with one another normally.[8][9]
Shared dreaming means Victor's Dream authority is not restricted to individualized hallucinations.
A single Dream can support:
- competing factions;
- alliances;
- families;
- armies;
- political systems;
- commerce;
- warfare;
- and long-term social development.

#### Artificial Inhabitants
The later Dream World contained beings who behaved as living individuals and believed themselves to be alive.[8]
These beings demonstrated:
- emotions;
- independent reactions;
- social behavior;
- self-preservation;
- political organization;
- and responses to the actions of Dream participants.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters could not simply rely on being the Emperor's children because the inhabitants of the Dream did not automatically recognize their status. They had to obtain influence through their own actions.[8]
Whether every Dream inhabitant possesses a genuine independent Soul comparable to a natural living being has not been directly established. They should therefore be described as Dream inhabitants rather than automatically classified as independently created Souls.

#### Independent Development
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] does not have to manually dictate every event inside the Dream.
During the War Games, factions and political structures developed naturally from the actions of the participants. Victor himself observed that certain developments had occurred without him intentionally directing the narrative.[8]
Later events moved sufficiently outside the expected plan that Victor and his wives were forced to adapt to what the participants had done.[11][12]
The Dream Realm can therefore function as a partially autonomous simulation once its initial circumstances and rules have been established.

### Scale

#### Confirmed Scale
The exact size of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Dream Realm has never been numerically measured.
Confirmed examples demonstrate at minimum that Victor can create Dream environments supporting:
- multiple continents;
- large populations;
- organized nations and factions;
- armies exceeding tens of thousands of combatants;
- years of history;
- and large-scale supernatural ecosystems.[8][9]
The later War Game Dream is therefore unquestionably far larger and more sophisticated than a localized illusion.

#### Earth and Nightingale Distance
A much greater theoretical scale can be derived from the false alternate Reality used for [[Nero]] and Ophis.
[[Nightingale]] is not located near Earth. During the Demonic War, while speaking from Nightingale, [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] explicitly described the planet as being billions of light-years away from Earth.[1]
This statement predates the later explanation that Nightingale belonged to a separate Low-Level Sector.[13]
The two statements establish that Nightingale is both:
- cosmologically distinct from Earth's normal environment; and
- described through ordinary distance terminology as billions of light-years from Earth.[1][13]

#### Theoretical Cosmological Extent
The false Reality used for [[Nero]] and Ophis reproduced an alternate historical framework based upon their lives, family and Records.[6][7][3]
If [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] reproduced the complete spatial environment required for both Earth's and [[Nightingale]]'s locations to coexist with their canonical separation, then the artificial Reality would necessarily possess a represented spatial extent reaching at least billions of light-years.

### Nero and Ophis' Alternate Reality

#### Purpose
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] created the false alternate Reality because [[Nero Elderblood]] and [[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]] possessed extraordinary raw power but lacked the natural life experiences normally required for Divine maturation.
[[Jeanne Elderblood]] explained that achieving genuine Divinity involved more than simply possessing large quantities of Energy. The individual needed a sufficiently mature Soul and an understanding of themselves and the Concept they would embody.[4][5]
Victor therefore created a shortcut—not one that simply handed them Divinity, but one that supplied experiences capable of accelerating the psychological and spiritual preparation normally accumulated across life.[3]

#### False Alternate Reality

#### Nero's Trial
[[Nero]]'s circumstances were constructed to remove the security created by her position as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughter.
The trial forced her to experience situations where inherited status and overwhelming racial ability could not automatically provide an answer.[6][7][3]
The pressure produced genuine Soul development and eventually contributed toward the awakening of the Eldritch Concept of Apocalypse.[14][15]
The Dream did not simply manufacture Apocalypse and insert it into Nero.
Instead, it accelerated the circumstances necessary for Nero's own developing nature to reveal itself.

#### Ophis' Trial
Ophis' trial was more emotionally focused.
The false Reality reproduced [[Otsuki Hana]], Ophis' deceased biological mother, allowing Ophis to interact with the woman whose death had shaped much of her childhood.[3][16]
The reconstructed Hana possessed recognizable aspects of the original woman's personality, including her powerful maternal instincts and extreme protectiveness toward Ophis.[3][16]
The experience forced Ophis to confront emotions that could not be reproduced through simple combat training.
Her developing Concept eventually manifested as Infinity.[14]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] then interfered with the normal external influence connected to the Concept, replacing it with his own Eldritch Essence so that Ophis' developing Divinity would draw from him rather than another cosmic source.[14]

#### Otsuki Hana Reconstruction
The Hana existing inside the Dream Realm should not be confused with a complete physical resurrection.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] used information and Dream construction to reproduce an alternate version of Hana sufficiently convincing for Ophis to interact with her as a person.[3][16]
Victor nevertheless continued speaking afterward about restoring Hana properly, demonstrating that he himself distinguished the Dream reconstruction from a genuine permanent return of the original woman.[17][18][19]

### War Games of the Elderblood Family
The Dream Realm later developed from a specialized Divinity-training method into one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s primary tools for educating his daughters.

#### Purpose
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] realized that many of his daughters possessed an inherent disadvantage despite their overwhelming potential: they had been born into the protection of the most powerful family around them.
They had:
- exceptional bodies;
- extraordinary bloodlines;
- access to the greatest teachers;
- enormous resources;
- powerful parents;
- and a father capable of solving nearly any threat directed toward them.
Victor therefore created training circumstances in which those advantages could be restricted.

#### Changed Roles
During the War Games, several daughters were assigned supernatural conditions different from their real existences.
Examples included:
- [[Stella Elderblood]] — Demi-Goddess;[20]
- [[Rosemary Lavina Elderblood]] — Fairy Queen;[21]
- [[Gina Elderblood]] — Dryad;[21]
- [[Yuno Elderblood]] — Necromancer.[22]
Their ordinary overwhelming Blood Dragon advantages were restricted, forcing them to learn how to operate under unfamiliar limitations.

#### A World of Consequences
Inside the shared Dream, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters could not simply declare themselves daughters of the Emperor and expect obedience.
The inhabitants considered themselves alive and did not automatically recognize the Elderblood family's status.[8]
The girls therefore needed to:
- obtain followers;
- develop reputations;
- create alliances;
- command troops;
- manage resources;
- learn politics;
- and survive hostile forces.
This gave them practical experience impossible to obtain through ordinary classroom instruction.

#### Ten Years in the Dream
By the time [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] observed the mature War Game environment, some members of his family had experienced approximately ten years within the Dream.[8][9]
During that period, Victor's wives also used the Dream construct for their own improvement.
For example, [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] used the period in which her normal overwhelming abilities were restricted to revisit fundamentals and improve her swordsmanship.[8][9]
The Dream Realm therefore benefits even already-experienced Gods and Dragons, not merely inexperienced children.

#### Emergent Factions
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters eventually organized themselves into three large factions carrying the names:
- Scarlett;
- Fulger;
- Snow.
These factions emerged through the participants' own decisions rather than because Victor scripted the entire political situation.[8]
Victor specifically observed that the narrative had naturally developed into this arrangement without his intervention.[8]
This demonstrates that Dream societies possess enough functional complexity to develop unexpected outcomes.

### Capabilities
The Dream Realm demonstrates the following properties:
- Dream Reality Creation: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] can construct self-contained worlds through Dream authority.[2][3]
- False Alternate Reality: He can create alternate versions of Reality using Dream and Akashic information.[3]
- Dreamwalking: Victor can enter and manipulate the Dreams of other beings.[10][2]
- Shared Dreaming: Multiple participants can inhabit the same Dream World simultaneously.[8][9]
- Subjective Time Dilation: Victor can alter how much Time is experienced inside relative to outside.[2]
- Long-Term Dream Duration: Dream participants can accumulate years of experience within a constructed Reality.[8][9]
- Environment Creation: Landscapes, cities, countries, continents and other environments can exist inside the Dream.
- Artificial History: A Dream World can possess a pre-existing historical framework used to support its scenario.[3]
- Reconstruction of Individuals: Victor can produce Dream versions of people based upon Records and information, as demonstrated with [[Otsuki Hana]].[3][16]
- Artificial Populations: Large numbers of Dream inhabitants can exist and interact with participants.[8]
- Social Simulation: Political groups, economies, military organizations and interpersonal relationships can emerge inside the Dream.[8]
- Power Restriction: Victor can seal or limit the normal powers of participants for training purposes.[8]
- Role Assignment: Participants can experience different supernatural conditions and identities.[20][21][22]
- Psychological Training: Victor can create circumstances designed to force emotional development.[3]
- Soul Development: Experiences within the Dream can contribute toward the maturation required for Divinity.[3]
- Conceptual [[Awakening]] Assistance: Dream trials helped [[Nero]] and Ophis reach the conditions necessary for Apocalypse and Infinity to awaken.[14]
- Training Simulation: Victor can use entire Dream civilizations as training grounds for strategy, leadership and personal development.[8][9]
- Adaptive Narrative: Events can develop independently through the decisions of participants rather than remaining completely predetermined.[8][11][12]

Dream provides the environment, while Akashic information supplies records capable of making the constructed Reality substantially more convincing and historically coherent.[3]

### Significance
The Dream Realm represents one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s most important developments as a teacher and father.
Victor himself developed through illness, danger, combat, loss and continuous confrontation with stronger beings. His daughters were born into radically different circumstances. Many possessed overwhelming bloodlines and lived beneath the protection of a father capable of eliminating almost any ordinary threat.
This created a problem that raw training could not solve.
They possessed power without necessarily possessing the equivalent amount of experience.
The Dream Realm allowed Victor to manufacture circumstances without manufacturing the final lesson itself. He could remove status, restrict power, create adversity, reproduce emotional conflicts and provide years of subjective experience, but the participants still needed to make their own decisions.
This distinction became especially important with [[Nero]] and Ophis. Victor did not choose their final Concepts for them. Instead, he created an environment harsh and meaningful enough for their Souls to reveal what they were becoming.[3][14]
The later War Games expanded that principle from individual development to civilizational training. Victor's children could lead armies, fail politically, build alliances, experience responsibility and live with the consequences of decisions for years without requiring the [[Elderblood Empire]] itself to become their experimental battlefield.[8][9]
The Dream Realm is therefore not merely another supernatural location.
It is an experience engine—a Reality Victor can shape so that those precious to him are allowed to grow through lives they would otherwise never have the opportunity to live.

- Chapter 1009 provides the clearest explanation of the mechanism behind Victor's false alternate Reality.[3]
- [[Otsuki Hana]]'s appearance within the Dream should not be confused with a complete resurrection of the original Hana.[19]
- The War Game Dream had existed subjectively for at least approximately ten years by Chapters 1055–1056.[8][9]
- The artificial inhabitants of the later Dream considered themselves alive and responded to the participants as genuine people would.[8]
- The Dream Realm and Victor's Personal Universe should remain separate wiki articles because one is an artificial experiential Reality while the other is an actual persistent internal cosmology.
