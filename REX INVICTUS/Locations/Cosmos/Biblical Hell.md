Biblical Hell was one of the principal Hell dimensions associated with Earth and the [[Biblical Pantheon]]. It was an enormous infernal realm inhabited primarily by Demons and functioned as both a place of punishment for sinners and a component of the universal system responsible for processing and recycling Souls.[1]
Biblical Hell was exceptionally large even among Earth's underworlds. During the Demonic War, it was explicitly stated that the demonic realm was 100 times the size of Earth.[2] [[Vine]] Elderblood later described it as one of the largest Hells alongside the Buddhist Hell, attributing its enormous population and scale partly to its association with two of the Mortal World's largest religions. At the time, the number of Demons within the realm was stated to surpass 50 billion.[3]
The dimension did not possess a uniform spatial structure. Hell was organized into increasingly expansive levels, with the lower levels forming its central or "true" portion. Unlike a conventional descending structure where deeper floors become smaller, Biblical Hell functioned like a pyramid: its upper levels possessed less land and weaker concentrations of Miasma, while its lower levels contained considerably more usable territory, much denser Miasma, the greatest sinners, the strongest Demons, and vast unexplored regions.[4]
Biblical Hell was historically ruled by figures such as [[Lucifer]] Morningstar, [[Diablo]], and [[Lilith Elderblood]], before eventually being conquered by [[Victor Elderblood]]. Victor was accepted by the Throne and the ancient Keepers of Hell as its True King, after which he reorganized its government, military, economy, and Soul-processing system.[1]
Biblical Hell later ceased to exist as a separate dimension. [[The Universal Tree]] and [[The Judges of The Abyss]] reorganized the Hells of the Sector, merging Biblical Hell with the [[Greek Underworld]] and incorporating the resulting infernal structure into the Hell connected to Victor's newly created world.[5] Victor later explicitly explained that the former Biblical and Greek Hells no longer existed as independent Hells, having instead produced an entirely new Hell through their merger.[6]

### Overview
Biblical Hell was considerably more complex than an ordinary dimension populated by Demons. Although it contained nations, cities, armies, Demon nobles, and an established political hierarchy, its fundamental purpose was cosmological rather than political.
The ancient Keepers [[Zahal]] and Albu described Hell as a dimension whose purpose was to punish sinners and recycle Souls. Their loyalty was therefore directed toward the continued well-being and proper operation of Hell rather than unconditionally toward whoever called themselves its King.[1]
This distinction was important because being politically recognized as the Demon King was not necessarily equivalent to being the true Ruler of Hell. The Ruler possessed a position integrated into the greater system governing Souls, Hells, Paradises, and reincarnation. When this system ceased functioning correctly, the consequences extended beyond Demonic society itself.[7][1]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] learned after claiming the Throne that a Hell which completely ceased performing its assigned function could ultimately be destroyed by [[The Judges of The Abyss]] and [[The Universal Tree]]. The proper management of Biblical Hell was therefore part of the wider maintenance of reality's Soul cycle.[1]

### Universe Role

#### Soul Processing
Biblical Hell acted as one of the supernatural mechanisms through which deceased Souls were processed.
Hell's responsibilities included the punishment of sinners and the recycling of Souls after their infernal treatment. Because this was part of the greater cosmic system, dysfunction within Hell could interfere with the movement and processing of Souls.[1]
The responsibilities of the Ruler consequently went beyond commanding Demons. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] sat upon the Throne of Hell, knowledge concerning both the duties of the King and the cosmological position known as a Ruler entered his mind.[1]
[[Zahal]] and Albu considered [[Diablo]]'s administration particularly dangerous because he refused to properly assume the position of Ruler, leaving the system in disorder. Diablo and Lilith had previously exercised the necessary influence between them, but their absence from Hell prevented the role from functioning correctly.[1]

#### Relationship with the Judges and Universal Tree
[[The Judges of The Abyss]] possess authority over Hells, Paradises, and Souls, while [[The Universal Tree]] is involved with Life and reincarnation. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] described the two systems as working together to ensure the continuation of reality and Life.[1]
The Rulers of Hells therefore exist beneath a much larger cosmic administration. Although a Ruler can possess enormous authority inside an underworld, the Hell itself remains part of a system monitored by the Judges and Universal Tree.[1]
This is why a failed Hell cannot simply remain permanently dysfunctional. If the dimension stops carrying out its cosmic role, the higher administrators possess the authority to intervene or destroy it.[1]

#### Negative World Tree
Biblical Hell was intimately connected to the negative aspect of Earth's World Tree.
The dimension's extraordinary durability was attributed to its existence being sustained by the negative counterpart of Earth's World Tree together with the higher supernatural system surrounding Hell.[7]
The negative World Tree itself also resided within Biblical Hell. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] specifically described the realm as the home of Earth's negative World Tree and suspected that its presence was responsible for some of Biblical Hell's unusual characteristics.[8]
This connection should not be confused with a confirmed statement that the negative World Tree directly created Biblical Hell. The known chapters explicitly establish that the realm is sustained by and houses the negative World Tree, while the exact original formation of Biblical Hell is not explained in the same detail.[7][8]

### Special Position Among Earth's Hells
Biblical Hell was considered unusual even compared to other infernal dimensions.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explained that the Kings of several other Hells, including those belonging to the Hindu, Norse, and Greek Pantheons, were capable of leaving their respective underworlds and entering the Mortal World. Biblical Hell was considerably more restrictive, as the dimension itself prevented its Demon King from freely leaving.[8]
[[Diablo]] consequently required sacrifices and external methods to be summoned into the Mortal World. Recovering the [[Key of Hell]] was therefore particularly important for Victor because the Key was connected to the authority necessary to bypass Hell's restrictions.[8]
When attempting to explain Biblical Hell's unusual relationship with the other underworlds, Victor compared it to a planet around which other Hells orbited. Souls belonging to other Hells could occasionally enter Biblical Hell as though they were being attracted toward it.[8]
This explanation was presented as an analogy. It does not mean that the other Hells were literally astronomical bodies physically orbiting Biblical Hell. Rather, the comparison was used to explain Biblical Hell's unusual centrality and its tendency to draw Souls from neighboring underworld systems.[8]
[[Vine]] additionally identified Biblical Hell as one of the largest Hells, comparable to the Buddhist Hell. She attributed this in part to its connection with two of the Mortal World's most widespread religions and their enormous numbers of believers.[3]

### Size and Spatial Structure

#### Overall Size
Biblical Hell was explicitly stated to be 100 times the size of Earth.[2]
Its scale was also reflected in its enormous population. [[Vine]] estimated that the number of Demons within the realm exceeded 50 billion, with the majority at that period belonging to [[Diablo]]'s side.[3]
Even these numbers did not mean the dimension had been thoroughly explored. Large portions of Biblical Hell remained uncharted even after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] unified most of its population and gained billions of Demons capable of exploring the realm.[9]

#### Pyramid Structure
Biblical Hell's levels were not equal in size.
Its structure was explicitly compared to a pyramid. The upper levels possessed smaller amounts of land and weaker concentrations of Miasma. As one descended, both the concentration of Miasma and the amount of available territory increased substantially.[4]
The lower levels consequently occupied the most important and expansive portion of Biblical Hell. They were described as the place where the "true Hell" existed, where the greatest sinners were sent and where the highest-ranking Demons lived.[4]
The lower levels were additionally identified as the central part of Hell. Even gigantic cities occupying these regions represented only a portion of the available land, with enormous stretches of unused and unexplored territory extending beyond them.[4]
The structure can therefore be understood as:
Upper Levels
├── Lowest concentration of Miasma
├── Comparatively less land
├── Lesser Demons
└── Less hostile environment
↓
Middle Levels
├── Greater territorial scale
├── Numerous Demon Pillar territories
├── Increasing Miasma
└── Cities separated by enormous distances
↓
Lower Levels
├── Central portion of Biblical Hell
├── "True Hell"
├── Greatest concentration of land
├── Greatest concentration of Miasma
├── Highest-ranking Demons
├── Greatest sinners
├── Vast unexplored territory
└── Demon King's domain
This pyramid comparison refers to the distribution of land and Miasma throughout the levels rather than implying that Hell was literally shaped like a physical pyramid.[4]

#### Upper Levels
The upper levels contained comparatively little Miasma and less available land.
They were consequently much more suitable for weaker Demons. Lesser Demons that could not tolerate the concentration of Miasma present farther below were effectively prevented from safely entering the deeper sections.[4]

#### Middle Levels
The middle levels contained considerably larger territories and numerous settlements ruled by the Demonic Pillars.
During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s conquest, the cities belonging to individual Pillars were separated from each other by hundreds of kilometers. Even though the middle levels were explicitly smaller than the lower levels, traversing and conquering them required a considerable amount of time.[4]
Victor encountered multiple Pillar-controlled cities while advancing through this region. [[Shax]], the 44th-ranked Pillar, possessed his own capital and forces before Victor arrived and demanded their submission.[10]

#### Lower Levels
The lower levels contained the highest concentration of Miasma and the greatest amount of usable territory in Biblical Hell.[4]
Their environment was so hostile that Demons who had been capable of surviving the middle regions could begin physically trembling simply from exposure to the Miasma surrounding the entrance.[4]
A gigantic gate separated the lower region from the levels above. The gate had been created by [[Lucifer]] and was sustained using Hell's own Energy. It was regarded as effectively impossible to breach until [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] physically split it apart.[4]
Beyond the gate were the territories of the highest-ranking Demons and the region containing the Demon King's seat of power.[4]

### Unexplored Scale and Possible Expansion
Even after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] unified Hell and established a centralized government, the realm remained incompletely mapped.
By Chapter 756, Victor's administration possessed billions of Demonic Hordes, yet enormous portions of Hell still remained outside complete exploration. Helena estimated that, at the current pace, fully exploring and mapping the realm could still require almost another four centuries.[9]
This surprised her because Hell appeared significantly larger than she expected from historical knowledge. The farther Victor's forces explored, the more territory they continued discovering.[9]
Helena consequently questioned whether Hell itself might be growing.[9]
This observation should not be treated as direct confirmation that Biblical Hell was infinite or that continuous expansion was an established law of the dimension. The chapter presents the idea through Helena's uncertainty. What is definitively established is that Biblical Hell was extraordinarily vast, incompletely explored even by billions of inhabitants, and apparently larger than expected from older knowledge.[9]

### Miasma
Miasma was the dominant infernal Energy of Biblical Hell and an essential part of its natural environment.
Its concentration increased dramatically with depth. The lowest levels possessed Miasma so dense and toxic that weaker Demons could not safely enter them.[4]
The deepest portions contained such enormous concentrations that Miasma could exist in forms resembling rivers.[11]
Miasma was not simply harmful pollution that could be removed without consequence. It was part of Hell's natural functioning, and removing excessive quantities could damage the dimension or interfere with the infernal environments responsible for producing Demons.[12]

#### Temporal Distortion
The concentration of Miasma also affected the flow of time.
The deeper portions of Hell experienced increasingly irregular temporal conditions because of their extraordinarily dense Miasma.[11]
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] took control, he worked alongside [[Aline Elderblood Valefar]] and [[Vepar Elderblood]] to reduce excessive Miasma without damaging Hell itself. Their objective was to establish a balance where the Energy remained sufficient for Hell's natural functions while no longer causing extreme temporal problems.[12]
Following these reforms, the temporal relationship was stabilized to approximately:
One year on Earth = Five years in Hell.[12]
Victor intentionally did not continue reducing the Miasma beyond the point considered safe for the dimension.[12]

### Will of Hell
Biblical Hell possessed something comparable to a [[Will Solace|Will]].
This did not mean the dimension was sentient in exactly the same manner as a person. [[Diablo]] explained that every Hell and Heavenly Plane possessed a Will comparable in certain respects to the authority a God-King exercised over their own dimension.[7]
Hell could use this Will to interfere with events inside itself.
After War was defeated by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and returned toward the Hell of Flames, the entire region became inaccessible. Even [[Baal]] was incapable of entering or contacting the Demons inside. Diablo concluded that Hell itself was protecting War's body.[7]
Diablo further noted that a similar event had occurred in the past when Hell concealed Lilith after she had been severely injured during a battle with an Archangel.[7]
The existence of this Will also helps distinguish the dimension itself from its Ruler. The King could possess enormous authority over Hell, but Hell remained a structure with its own underlying system and interests.[7]

### Durability
Biblical Hell possessed extraordinary natural durability.
Before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s battle against War, Demons considered it effectively impossible for battles inside Hell to cause permanent damage to the dimension. Extremely powerful beings could destroy regions, but the damage itself was not expected to remain irreversible.[7]
This changed when Victor fought War after the latter ascended into a [[Demon God]].
Their clash left an enormous crater, persistent clouds of black Miasma, and red lightning across the battlefield. The damage remained instead of naturally disappearing or being repaired.[7]
[[Diablo]] regarded this as unprecedented. Neither he, Lilith, nor even [[Lucifer]] had previously produced comparable irreversible damage to Biblical Hell.[7]
The event therefore demonstrated both the extraordinary resilience of the dimension and the abnormal destructive power involved in Victor and War's battle.

### The Seven Hells of Sinners
Within Biblical Hell existed Seven Hells of Sinners, specialized infernal regions involved with the punishment of Souls and the production of particular Demons.[12]
These regions depended heavily on their local concentrations of Miasma. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s reforms reduced the overall Miasma throughout Hell, the production of Demons within the Seven Hells also decreased.[12]
[[Aline Elderblood Valefar]] addressed the problem through seven specialized Orbs that concentrated Miasma within the appropriate regions. This allowed the rest of Hell to remain more stable while maintaining the environmental conditions required by the Seven Hells.[12]

#### Hell of Flames
The Hell of Flames was one of the specialized infernal regions and was associated with Wrath.
Its sinners were subjected to Hellfire, while Demons born from the region possessed particularly strong affinities for infernal flames.[7][12]
War originated from the Hell of Flames. Following his defeat by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], his steed carried his body back to this region, after which Hell sealed the area from outside interference.[7]

#### Hell of Despair
The Hell of Despair was another of the specialized Hells and was connected to the origin of the [[Horseman of Death]].[12]
The region possessed its own distinctive infernal characteristics and Miasma, further demonstrating that Biblical Hell contained multiple specialized internal environments rather than a single uniform landscape.[12]

### The Fields of Hell
Biblical Hell also contained distant hostile territories generally referred to as Fields.
These regions lay far from the central authority of the Demon King and were known for producing particularly powerful Infernal Demons.[9]
Even after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had conquered the major population centers, several of these areas remained outside his immediate influence because of the sheer scale of the dimension.[9]
[[Lily Elderblood]] eventually led campaigns against these regions.
Known Fields included:
- Field of Hatred
- Field of Famine
- Field of Destruction
- Field of Fear[9]
The Fields attacked by Lily were described as Lesser Hells where Souls could arrive and where Demons were born.[9]

#### Field of Hatred
The Field of Hatred received Mortals who died while carrying exceptionally intense hatred.
The environment produced powerful Demons, with the former Wrath being specifically identified as a Demon originating from this region.[9]
The other Fields are confirmed to exist and were conquered during Lily's campaigns, but their exact individual Soul-assignment rules are not explained in the same level of detail in Chapter 756.[9]

### Demonic Society
Biblical Hell's society traditionally operated according to strength.
Powerful Demons accumulated armies, territories, resources, and political authority, while weaker Demons were frequently consumed, killed, or subordinated by stronger ones.[3]
Demons themselves regarded conflict as an inherent aspect of their existence. A Demon King who displayed weakness risked losing the respect and obedience of his subjects.[3]
This instability was one of the reasons no previous ruler had completely unified every part of Biblical Hell. [[Vine]] stated that not even [[Lucifer]] had fully controlled every Demon, as influential Demons continued plotting independently despite recognizing his strength.[3]

#### Demon Pillars
The 72 Demon Pillars controlled numerous territories throughout Biblical Hell.
Their cities could be separated by hundreds of kilometers, particularly throughout the middle levels.[4]
The ranking of a Pillar correlated broadly with its power and position within Demonic society. The highest-ranked Pillars occupied or operated closer to the deeper portions of Hell, where environmental conditions and Miasma were substantially more extreme.[4]

#### Population
During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s initial conquest, [[Vine]] stated that Biblical Hell contained more than 50 billion Demons.[3]
This population helps explain why complete political unification had historically been considered nearly impossible. Even exceptionally powerful Kings could dominate the major factions without exerting direct control over every distant or ancient Demonic group.[3][9]

### Ruler of Hell
The Ruler of Hell was a cosmological position distinct from merely possessing the political title of Demon King.
[[Diablo]] described the Ruler as the True King of Hell and the one responsible for judging sinners.[7]
The position was connected to the underlying administration of Souls and to the proper operation of the infernal realm. A political King who refused or failed to properly exercise this role could leave Hell's Soul system dysfunctional.[1]

#### Key of Hell
The [[Key of Hell]] was a major connection point for the authority of the Ruler.
[[Diablo]] compared its function to [[Hades]]' Helm, which similarly served as a connection point for the Ruler of the [[Greek Underworld]].[7]
Possession of the Key allowed Diablo and Lilith to passively maintain parts of the system even without a true Ruler. After defeating Lilith and recombining the divided Key, Diablo still did not fully become the legitimate Ruler because he intentionally avoided accepting the position's restrictions.[7]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] also initially possessed incomplete authority after claiming the Throne because the Key remained outside Hell.[1]

#### Throne of Hell
The Throne represented another major aspect of Hell's rulership.
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] approached it, the Throne automatically altered its size and appearance to accommodate him. Upon taking his seat, he received knowledge concerning the responsibilities of the King of Hell and the cosmic role of a Ruler.[1]
The ancient Keepers recognized this event, together with Victor's connection to the Energy of the negative World Tree, as confirmation that he was the True King of Hell.[1]

### Keepers of Hell
[[Zahal]] and Albu were ancient guardians whose existence predated [[Lucifer]]'s fall.
Their role was to protect Hell itself, including protecting the dimension from a King whose incompetence could threaten its continued existence.[1]
Their allegiance therefore differed from ordinary Demonic loyalty. They obeyed a King only so long as that ruler fulfilled the responsibilities necessary for Hell's continued operation.[1]
Both were connected to Earth's negative World Tree. [[Roxanne Elderblood]] explained that the Keepers had been created by Earth's World Tree, specifically its negative aspect.[1]
Because [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] carried an Energy extremely similar to that of their creator through his connection with Roxanne, Zahal and Albu immediately regarded his arrival as exceptional.[1]
Victor later elevated the two from Keepers into the [[Elders]] of Hell, assigning them the additional responsibility of preserving Hell's history and teaching him the knowledge that could not be obtained simply by consuming Demonic memories.[1]

### History

#### Lucifer's Rule
[[Lucifer]] Morningstar was the previous recognized Ruler of Biblical Hell before his departure.
The fact that [[Diablo]] later stated that no proper Ruler had existed since Lucifer left establishes Lucifer as the previous true holder of that cosmological position.[7]
Lucifer was an overwhelmingly respected figure among Demons, but even he never achieved complete political control over every inhabitant of the enormous dimension. Ancient and influential Demons continued plotting independently despite acknowledging his strength.[3]
[[Zahal]] and Albu had already existed before Lucifer's fall and continued serving Hell after his era ended.[1]

#### Diablo and Lilith
Following [[Lucifer]]'s departure, [[Diablo]] and Lilith became the dominant figures responsible for Biblical Hell.
The two possessed the divided authority of the Key and jointly maintained aspects of the Ruler's function.[7]
Diablo eventually defeated Lilith and reunited the Key, but intentionally refused to fully become the Ruler. He did not want his ambitions restricted to Biblical Hell.[7]
To prevent the Soul system from completely collapsing and provoking direct intervention by the Judges, Diablo temporarily placed Lilith into the Ruler position. However, she had not undergone the proper Trial and functioned primarily as a temporary component maintaining the system.[7]
By the time [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] reached the Throne, [[Zahal]] and Albu openly criticized Diablo for allowing Hell's administration to deteriorate.[1]

#### Victor's Arrival
During the Demonic War, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was trapped inside Biblical Hell by [[Diablo]]'s allies.
Instead of dying from the hostile environment, Victor was unusually compatible with the realm because of [[Roxanne Elderblood]] and his access to Negative Energy.
He began defeating Demonic armies and progressively transformed what initially appeared to be an attempt to survive into a campaign to conquer the entire dimension.
After defeating [[Vine]] Elderblood, Victor offered her greater power in exchange for permanent loyalty. Vine accepted and became one of his first major Demonic Generals.[3]
Victor subsequently declared his intention to take Biblical Hell for himself and become its King.[3]

#### Conquest of the Pillars
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] advanced through the territories belonging to the Demon Pillars, destroying or subjugating their forces.
His growing reputation gradually changed the nature of the campaign. Demons that initially intended to fight increasingly chose submission as news of his victories spread throughout Hell.[4]
His forces expanded with each conquered city, turning the campaign into an enormous moving Demonic horde.[4]

#### Battle with the Four Horsemen
[[Diablo]]'s Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse eventually confronted [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
Pestilence, Death, and Famine were defeated and absorbed by Victor.[7]
War survived longer and ascended during the battle, becoming a [[Demon God]].[13]
Despite this transformation, Victor defeated him.
Their final confrontation permanently altered the landscape of Biblical Hell, leaving an enormous crater, persistent red lightning, and clouds of Miasma behind.[7]

#### Conquest of the Lower Levels
After defeating the Horsemen, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] continued into the deeper portions of Hell.
By this point his Generals and forces had become powerful enough that numerous Demons no longer seriously questioned his advance.[4]
The gate leading into the lower levels had been closed in an attempt to prevent his approach. Because the gate had been constructed by [[Lucifer]] and sustained by Hell's own Energy, Demons regarded it as impossible to break.[4]
Victor simply cut it apart.
The destruction of the gate allowed his forces to enter the central and most expansive portion of Biblical Hell.[4]

#### The True King
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually entered the Demon King's Castle and encountered [[Zahal]] and Albu.
The Keepers recognized his connection to the negative World Tree and hailed him as the True King of Hell.[1]
Victor approached the Throne, which transformed itself to accommodate him. Knowledge concerning the universal responsibilities of a Ruler was subsequently transferred to him.[1]
Although his authority remained incomplete while the Key was absent, his recognition by the Throne and Keepers established him as the legitimate King of the dimension.[1]

#### Reformation
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] radically reorganized Biblical Hell following his conquest.
Instead of allowing lineage and inherited status alone to determine authority, he increasingly promoted Demons according to competence, strength, loyalty, and usefulness.
His principal Generals and administrators included [[Vine]] Elderblood, [[Vepar Elderblood]], [[Helena Elderblood Gremory]], [[Lily Elderblood]], and later [[Aline Elderblood Valefar]].
Vine oversaw major military matters, Vepar handled logistics and administration, Helena served as an important strategist, while Lily was sent to conquer hostile Lesser Hells and distant Fields.[9]
By Chapter 756, the government could mobilize billions of Demonic Hordes with a command from Victor.[9]
Despite this enormous manpower, complete exploration and unification of every distant region remained an undertaking measured in centuries because of Biblical Hell's scale.[9]

#### Miasma Reforms
Excessive Miasma created environmental and temporal problems throughout the dimension.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Aline, and Vepar developed a method of reducing and redistributing it without damaging Hell's natural ecosystem.[12]
The reforms stabilized the passage of time to approximately five Hell years for every Earth year.[12]
Because the Seven Hells of Sinners required high concentrations of Miasma to function and produce Demons, Aline developed specialized Orbs that concentrated Energy within those regions rather than simply removing it.[12]

#### Unification of the Fields
Lily subsequently expanded [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s government into the distant Fields of Hatred, Famine, Destruction, and Fear.[9]
These campaigns brought regions that even previous Kings had struggled to fully dominate into Victor's sphere of influence.
Ancient groups continued to resist in isolated territories, but Victor possessed something previous Kings lacked: billions of organized Demons and powerful Generals capable of continuing conquest without his direct participation.[9]

### End of Biblical Hell
Biblical Hell's independent existence ended following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ascension and the creation of his new world.
[[The Universal Tree]] summoned [[The Judges of The Abyss]] and proposed reorganizing the infernal system of the Sector. The existing number of Hells was considered unnecessarily fragmented, and the administrators ultimately decided to consolidate them into three major infernal systems.[5]
Under the final arrangement:
- Biblical Hell was merged with the [[Greek Underworld]].
- Hindu Hell was merged with the emptied former Chinese Hell.
- Norse Hell was merged with the remaining Hells.[5]
The Biblical-Greek merger was incorporated into the infernal structure attached to Victor's newly created world. When the Judges and Universal Tree performed the restructuring, Victor directly felt his Hell expand by several hundred times.[5]
Because of the resulting scale, the Universal Tree stated that three Rulers would be required to manage the Souls passing through Victor's Hell.[5]
The merger did not simply relocate Biblical Hell intact.
When Victor later strengthened [[Aline Elderblood Valefar]] and [[Persephone Elderblood]] as Rulers, [[Persephone]] questioned why she needed to become a Ruler again. Victor explained that they had previously been the Rulers of Biblical Hell and Greek Hell respectively, but that those two Hells no longer existed.[6]
Their former realms had merged into a new Hell, and Victor was therefore appointing them as Rulers of the newly born successor dimension rather than restoring their authority over the old ones.[6]

### Legacy
Although Biblical Hell ceased to exist as a separate dimension, its inhabitants, territories, culture, Demonic civilization, and administrative system became major components of the successor Hell.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s government continued operating after the merger, while Aline and [[Persephone]] were formally established as Rulers of the new infernal system.[6]
The old Biblical territories consequently remained historically identifiable within the combined structure, but cosmologically they were no longer an independent Hell.
The New Hell should therefore be treated as a successor structure produced from the merger rather than as a simple enlargement or renaming of Biblical Hell.

### Known Locations

#### Abbadon
Abbadon was located within the lowest layer of Hell and became the central Royal City of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s infernal government.[9]
The Demon King's Castle was situated within this region.

#### Demon King's Castle
The Demon King's Castle was the traditional seat of the King of Hell.
The Castle contained the Throne of Hell and was guarded by [[Zahal]] and Albu when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] first arrived.[1]
Following Victor's reforms, the Castle increasingly became the personal residence of the King and his highest-ranking subordinates rather than the sole administrative center of Demonic society.[12]

#### Lower Gate
The Lower Gate separated the deeper levels from the upper and middle portions of Hell.
[[Lucifer]] created the gate, and Hell's own Energy sustained it. Before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s arrival, it was regarded as impossible to destroy or breach.[4]
Victor split it in half while advancing toward the lowest levels.[4]

#### Pillar Cities
Numerous individual Demon Pillars possessed their own cities and territories.
These settlements were often separated from one another by hundreds of kilometers, illustrating the enormous scale of even the middle portion of Biblical Hell.[4]

### Cosmological Notes
- Biblical Hell was a dimension in its own right, not simply an underground physical location on Earth.[2][1]
- Its directly stated size before the merger was 100 times Earth.[2]
- It was described as one of the largest Hells alongside Buddhist Hell.[3]
- Its Demon population exceeded 50 billion during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s conquest.[3]
- Biblical Hell should not be considered infinite based on the currently established statements. Chapter 756 only establishes that it was extraordinarily difficult to completely explore and that Helena wondered whether it was growing.[9]
- Hell's pyramid structure concerns the distribution of space and Miasma. The deeper levels actually contain more territory than the upper levels.[4]
- The lower levels are specifically identified as Hell's central portion and the location of the "true Hell".[4]
- The comparison of Biblical Hell to a planet with other Hells orbiting it is an explanatory analogy used by Victor and should not be interpreted as a literal astronomical structure.[8]
- Biblical Hell housed Earth's negative World Tree and was unusually restrictive toward its Demon King.[8]
- Hell possessed its own [[Will Solace|Will]] capable of interfering with events inside the dimension.[7]
- The King of Hell and the cosmological Ruler are not automatically the same position. [[Diablo]] was politically the Demon King while deliberately refusing to fully become the Ruler.[7][1]
- After Chapter 822's restructuring, Biblical Hell became one component of a larger merged infernal realm.[5]
- Chapter 827 explicitly establishes that Biblical Hell and Greek Hell no longer exist independently after the merger.[6]

### Trivia
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and War were the first known combatants to permanently damage Biblical Hell, leaving behind a crater, red lightning, and clouds of Miasma that did not disappear naturally.[7]
- Neither [[Lucifer]], Lilith, nor [[Diablo]] had previously produced comparable permanent damage to the dimension.[7]
- [[Zahal]] and Albu existed before Lucifer's fall.[1]
- The Throne of Hell changed its physical form to accommodate Victor when it accepted him.[1]
- Even after the Throne recognized Victor, his authority was initially incomplete because the [[Key of Hell]] remained outside the dimension.[1]
- The lower levels contained so much Miasma that Demons capable of surviving higher portions could become incapable of proceeding farther downward.[4]
- Despite Victor possessing billions of Demons under his government, completely mapping the dimension was still estimated to require centuries.[9]
- Biblical Hell's old 100-times-Earth measurement should not be confused with the later size of the New Hell, which expanded several hundred times during the restructuring performed by the Universal Tree and Judges.[2][5]
- The New Hell is a successor to Biblical Hell, not merely another alias for it.[6]
