The Gate of Tartar, referred to in the novel as the [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] Gate, is a massive fortified gateway located in the western territory of [[Nightingale]], within the lands controlled by the Adrasteia Clan. It stands between two enormous mountain ranges and separates the western territory from the other regions of Nightingale.[1]
The Gate serves as the controlled entrance into the isolated territory inhabited by Clan Adrasteia. The region beyond it is home to Warfall, the Clan's heavily militarized city, and is protected by a broader defensive network consisting of guards, anti-aircraft defenses, automatic weapons, and a special identification system for outsiders.[1][2]

### Location
The [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] Gate is situated at the boundary of the western territory of [[Nightingale]], within the enormous territory controlled by the [[Adrasteia Clan]].[1]
Unlike the territories governed by the [[Snow Clan]], [[Fulger Clan]], and [[Scarlett Clan]], much of the land surrounding Adrasteia territory was described as largely deserted and overgrown with vegetation. Although Clan Adrasteia possessed the largest territory among the Vampire Count Clans at the time, most of its land remained unused.[1]
The approach to the western territory is dominated by two gigantic mountain ranges extending for several kilometers. The two ranges form what resembles an enormous corridor, with the Tartarus Gate positioned in the opening between them.[1]
The Gate therefore acts as a clear geographical boundary separating the western territory from the remainder of Nightingale.[1]

### Appearance
The [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] Gate is an enormous structure constructed between the two colossal mountain ranges surrounding the western territory.[1]
Standing beside the Gate are two gigantic hooded skeletal statues. Each statue carries an enormous scythe, giving the entrance a threatening appearance appropriate to the dangerous region beyond it.[1]
When Siena Scarlett saw the structure during her journey to Adrasteia territory, it was only the second time she had seen it in her life. Despite having encountered it previously, the sight of the Gate and its surrounding mountains was imposing enough to make her instinctively hold her breath.[1]

### Surrounding Mountains
The two mountain ranges surrounding the [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] Gate extend for several kilometers and form a massive corridor leading toward the western territory.[1]
Their exact origin is unknown.
Siena observed that the mountains appeared unusually artificial and suspected that they might have been created by someone rather than formed naturally. Knowing the capabilities associated with the Adrasteia bloodline, she considered it possible that one of Clan Adrasteia's ancestors had created them.[1]
However, this was only Siena's speculation. The novel does not confirm that the mountains were artificially created, nor does it identify the creator of either the mountains or the Tartarus Gate itself.[1]

### Reputation
The [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] Gate possesses an ominous reputation within [[Nightingale]]. According to the myths surrounding it, anyone who passes beyond the Gate will encounter nothing but death.[1]
Siena initially considered the reputation exaggerated. Upon returning to the region, however, she reflected that myths were often preserved as a means of transmitting warnings to later generations. To her, the message represented by the Tartarus Gate was straightforward:
Danger.[1]
The warning reflects the nature of the western territory itself. Shortly before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s party passed through the Gate, an Adrasteia messenger reported that a monster horde larger than usual was approaching the city walls.[1]

### Function
The [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] Gate functions as both the boundary and the controlled point of entry into Clan Adrasteia's western territory.[1]
Access to the region is not limited to the physical Gate alone. The mountains and surrounding territory are incorporated into an extensive security system intended to prevent unauthorized movement through Adrasteia lands.[1]
This makes bypassing the Gate directly extremely dangerous. When [[Victor Elderblood]] attempted to travel ahead by air during his first visit, Eleanor Adrasteia immediately warned him not to fly over the mountains, explaining that the territory's anti-aircraft defenses would bombard him. She instructed him to enter through the Gate instead.[1]

### Security and Defenses
The western territory possesses multiple layers of security beyond the physical Gate itself.[1]

#### Gate Guards
Travelers arriving at the [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] Gate are inspected by Clan Adrasteia's personnel before being permitted to proceed.
When Siena, Lacus, and Pepper arrived as Eleanor's guests, a guard verified their identities with the soldiers accompanying them before proceeding with the Clan's standard entry procedure. After confirming their status, he informed them that he would notify the doorman and issued the sisters temporary identification tags.[1]
The fact that even well-known members of Clan Scarlett traveling under Eleanor's protection underwent this procedure demonstrates that visitor identification was treated as a normal part of entering the territory.[1]

#### Visitor Identification
Outsiders entering Adrasteia territory are provided with special identification tags.[1]
The tags given to Siena and her sisters appeared as black symbols. To activate one, the visitor was required to place a drop of their blood upon it. Once activated, the identification allowed the visitor to move through Clan Adrasteia's territory without being targeted by its defensive systems.[1]
Visitors were explicitly warned not to lose their identification. Anyone moving within the territory without being properly recognized could be attacked by the Clan's automatic weapons.[1]
These tags were specifically designed for outsiders because visitors to the western territory were comparatively rare.[1]

#### Clan Recognition System
Members of Clan Adrasteia do not require the same visitor tags.
According to the Gate guard, the Clan's weapons are capable of recognizing members of Clan Adrasteia. Consequently, their own residents can move through the protected territory without being mistaken for intruders.[1]
This system is particularly effective because virtually everyone permanently residing beyond the mountains belonged to Clan Adrasteia at the time. The Clan also received very few outside visitors, allowing unknown individuals to be treated as potential intruders unless properly identified.[1]

#### Anti-Aircraft Defenses
The mountains surrounding the Gate are protected by anti-aircraft defenses. Attempting to simply fly over the mountain ranges instead of using the designated entrance can result in the intruder being bombarded by the defensive system.[1]
Eleanor specifically warned [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] of this when he attempted to rush toward the monster attack by air, telling him to travel through the Gate rather than cross directly over the mountains.[1]
Together with the automatic weapons operating throughout Adrasteia territory, the anti-aircraft network prevents the Gate from being easily circumvented by supernatural beings capable of flight.[1]

### Territory Beyond the Gate
Contrary to the implication that the region beyond the [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] Gate is uninhabited, the western territory contains a permanent population.
The Gate guard explicitly stated that all inhabitants living behind the mountains were members of Clan Adrasteia at the time of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s visit.[1] The isolation of the territory instead comes from the scarcity of outsiders and the dangerous conditions associated with the western region.
The principal known settlement beyond the Gate is Warfall, the city associated with Clan Adrasteia.[2]

#### Warfall
Warfall reflects the military nature of the territory protected by the [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] Gate.
Unlike the Royal Capital and the territories of the other [[Vampire Counts]], which resemble comparatively conventional Vampire societies, Warfall was described as possessing the atmosphere of a militarized society. Armed Vampires routinely walked its streets wearing full black armor, and virtually every individual carried a weapon with which they were proficient.[2]
Even merchants operating within Warfall wore armor. Their businesses focused heavily on military necessities, selling weapons, bombs, provisions, and other supplies desired by Clan Adrasteia's warriors.[2]
Outside merchants nevertheless maintained branches within the dangerous territory because of their confidence in Clan Adrasteia's ability to defend the settlement.[2]
The Tartarus Gate should therefore not be confused with the walls or internal defenses of Warfall itself. The Gate marks and controls entry into the broader western territory, while Warfall lies within that territory and possesses its own defenses.[1][2]

### Danger of the Western Territory
The ominous reputation surrounding the [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] Gate is closely associated with the conditions of the territory behind it.
During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s first journey through the region, an Adrasteia messenger arrived before the group had even completed passage through the Gate and informed Eleanor that a horde of monsters was approaching the city walls. The horde was described as slightly larger than what the Clan normally encountered, indicating that monster attacks were sufficiently routine for the soldiers to possess an established expectation for their usual size.[1]
Victor himself immediately sensed the difference between the western territory and the safer areas of [[Nightingale]]. To him, the region was saturated with blood and constant danger, making the Gate's deadly reputation considerably less exaggerated than it initially appeared to outsiders.[1]
The events immediately following his arrival further demonstrate why Clan Adrasteia maintained such an intensely militarized territory and extensive defensive infrastructure.[2]

### History

#### Victor's First Visit
[[Victor Elderblood]] first passed through the [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] Gate while traveling to Clan Adrasteia's territory alongside Eleanor Adrasteia, Siena Scarlett, Lacus Scarlett, Pepper Scarlett, and Mizuki.[1]
As they approached the Gate, Siena observed its immense structure and reflected upon both its reputation and the unnatural appearance of its surrounding mountains. It was during this journey that she speculated that an ancient member of Clan Adrasteia might have possessed enough power to create the mountain ranges.[1]
Their arrival coincided with a monster horde advancing toward Warfall. After receiving news of the attack, Victor attempted to rush toward the fighting, forcing Eleanor to warn him about the anti-aircraft defenses surrounding the mountains.[1]
Meanwhile, Siena and her sisters proceeded through the normal checkpoint procedure and were issued blood-activated visitor IDs so that Adrasteia's automatic defenses would recognize them as authorized outsiders.[1]
The group subsequently entered the western territory, where the conflict with the approaching monster horde occurred around Warfall.[2]

### Notes
- The novel directly refers to the structure as the [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] Gate, while Chapter 324 itself is titled The Gates of Tartarus.[1]
- The creator of the Tartarus Gate has never been confirmed.
- Siena only speculated that the surrounding mountains might have been artificially created by an ancestor of Clan Adrasteia; this should not be presented as an established historical fact.[1]
- The land immediately surrounding Adrasteia territory is largely deserted and much of the Clan's territory was unused, but the land behind the mountains is not uninhabited. Clan Adrasteia's members reside there, with Warfall serving as the primary known city in the region.[1][2]
- Outside visitors are uncommon enough that Clan Adrasteia created a specialized temporary identification system for them.[1]
