The Universal Tree, also known as the Universal World Tree, Life and simply The Tree, is one of the [[Regulators of Existence]] and a completed Primordial Entity responsible for the universal functions associated with Life and Reincarnation. The author directly describes him as responsible for sustaining the Universe, the existence of Life, reincarnation and the maintenance of the Universe, while all conventional [[World Trees]] originate from him.[9]
Unlike an ordinary God who possesses a Life-related Divinity, the Universal Tree is one of the foundational entities through which the corresponding function of the Cosmos operates. Completed Primordials are fundamental pieces of the Universe rather than merely extraordinarily powerful biological Beings; eradicating one completely would require eradicating the Aspect of Creation represented by that Primordial.[10] Chapter 1176 further clarifies the Universal Tree's scale, describing him as existing throughout the Cosmos through his connection with every World Tree, possessing a cosmic consciousness operating on a universal level and functioning as a Pillar whose destruction would effectively bring an end to Life throughout the Universe.[1] Chapter 1181 expands what Life means at this level: the Universal Tree explicitly states that he embodies not merely conscious or biological life, but the life of the entire Universe, from the dust of planets to the largest stars and galaxies, all of which participate in the same cycle of beginning, decay, Death and renewal.[3]
The Universal Tree is also deeply connected to the universal cycle of Souls. His duties overlap with those of [[The Judges of The Abyss]] without replacing their authority: the Judges possess the primary Primordial jurisdiction over Soul, whereas the Universal Tree governs the Life and form into which Souls incarnate and the process through which completed Souls return to living existence.[11][12] He eventually created the [[Reincarnation System]] to automate this process on a universal scale.[7] By Chapter 1181, he explains the larger mechanism directly: the Judges register and process existences through the [[Akashic Records]], [[True Death]] governs their end, and the Universal Tree recycles the resulting Energy through reincarnation so that new Life can arise.[3]
He is additionally the adoptive brother of [[Jeanne Elderblood]], who was assigned by [[Primordial Chaos]] to protect the seed from which the Universal Tree developed. Their bond is one of the strongest personal relationships demonstrated by any Regulator and eventually caused the Universal Tree to extend his protection to [[Victor Elderblood]] and the Elderblood family.[5][8]

### Physical Appearance
The Universal Tree's true existence cannot be accurately represented through conventional humanoid anatomy. His fundamental body is the Primordial World Tree and the universal Life-system connected to the [[World Trees]] distributed throughout Creation. By Chapter 1176, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] describes him as existing throughout the entire Cosmos through this connection rather than being restricted to one ordinary physical location.[1]
The Universal Tree can nevertheless create comprehensible manifestations when personally interacting with lesser Beings. One manifestation appears as an immense white-and-golden humanoid figure whose existence radiates supernatural power.[13][2]

#### Humanoid Form
When reuniting directly with Jeanne, the Universal Tree decided that his luminous Primordial manifestation was inappropriate for a familial conversation and transformed into a middle-aged man with blond hair, green eyes, fair skin and an elegant suit.[2]
His chosen apparent age is intentional. Despite Jeanne being chronologically older than him, the Universal Tree considers himself her older brother and deliberately chose a mature male appearance that reflected the role he had assigned himself.[2]

### Personality
The Universal Tree is calm, patient, affectionate and unusually expressive for a completed Primordial Entity. He is capable of discussing universal-scale catastrophes with considerable composure and frequently displays humor, sarcasm and familial affection even in situations involving other Regulators.
His individuality is particularly notable when compared with Primordials whose behavior remains heavily defined by their cosmic functions. During the events of Chapter 1176, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] specifically contrasts the Universal Tree with the Judges of the Abyss, observing that some Primordial Beings possess significantly more developed personalities while the Universal Tree is particularly full of Life and personality.[1]
The Universal Tree places high value upon genuine emotional relationships. When Jeanne requested a family after spending thousands of years protecting him, he understood that simply forcing someone to love her would not provide the authentic bond she wanted. Rather than using his enormous Power to manipulate another Being, he personally chose to become her family and declared himself her brother.[5]
Jeanne consequently became the greatest exception to the neutrality normally expected of him. The Universal Tree is extremely protective of his sister and has openly indicated that he will choose her over maintaining good relations with another Primordial if forced to make such a decision.[14][8]
His attitude toward Victor developed from gratitude into genuine familial approval. Victor restored Jeanne's lost memories, later became her husband and continuously demonstrated enough strength to protect her. The Universal Tree consequently became pleased by Victor's growth rather than instinctively viewing it as a threat, famously remarking that his sister was in good hands after observing Victor's progress in Chapter 802.[13]
His protectiveness eventually expanded to Victor's entire family. In Chapter 1074 he formally placed Victor's family beneath his Primordial protection.[8] By Chapter 1176 he was prepared to stand against other Primordials if they attempted to take [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]], openly declaring that anyone approaching his niece would also have to deal with him.[1]
Despite his normally relaxed demeanor, the Universal Tree can become extremely serious when his sister, family, [[World Trees]] or cosmic Domain are threatened. His willingness to oppose fellow Regulators demonstrates that his friendliness should not be mistaken for passivity.
His treatment of the World Trees reveals a much stricter philosophy of Life than Victor's. The Universal Tree believes his daughters should mature naturally by being placed upon worlds, confronting responsibility and hardship over thousands of years and developing their own personalities rather than remaining permanently sheltered. He views difficulty, mortality and eventual Death as meaningful parts of Life because scarcity and impermanence are what cause living beings to value their existence.[4][3] Victor fundamentally disagrees with this approach and instead shelters the World Trees connected to his Empire, creating one of the clearest philosophical differences between the two.

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
The Universal Tree originated from a small seed entrusted to [[Jeanne Elderblood]] by [[Primordial Chaos]]. Jeanne guarded the seed for thousands of years as it developed, eventually witnessing the emergence of the entity that would become the Universal Tree.[5][15]
After attaining consciousness, the Universal Tree asked Jeanne what she desired in exchange for the immense period she had spent protecting him. Jeanne answered that she wanted a family. Despite possessing enormous authority, the Universal Tree understood that a genuine relationship could not simply be manufactured through supernatural coercion. Instead, he personally offered to become her family, establishing their relationship as siblings.[5]
Jeanne later became separated from this history and lost her memories. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually entered Jeanne's Soul and restored what had been lost, severely straining his own Soul in the process. The Universal Tree directly intervened, repaired Victor's accumulated spiritual damage and granted him a Blessing in gratitude for returning his sister to him.[5]

#### Demonic War
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s connection with [[Roxanne Elderblood]] eventually attracted the attention of the Universal Tree, revealing that the [[World Trees]] scattered across Creation originated from a vastly greater existence rather than being isolated supernatural organisms.[16]
The relationship among Life, Soul and reincarnation became progressively clearer as Victor encountered the greater cosmic administrators. The Judges of the Abyss explained that their own authority and that of the Universal Tree form different parts of the same process: Life produces the form of an existence while Soul supplies the Soul itself.[12]
The Universal Tree demonstrated the depth of his reincarnation authority when Jeanne requested that he deal with [[Paimon]]. Rather than merely killing the Demon and allowing him to return through the ordinary infernal process, the Universal Tree dismantled the existing Being, processed his spiritual foundation and recycled it through reincarnation so that it could later become a different existence associated with Jeanne.[17]
This established that his control of reincarnation extends beyond transferring an intact personality between bodies. The Universal Tree can end the continuity of an old existence and use its spiritual foundation in the creation of another.
As the Demonic War approached its climax, the Universal Tree also directly strengthened [[Yggdrasil]], reinforcing the World Tree in preparation for the enormous stresses the coming conflict would place upon Earth and its supernatural infrastructure.[18]

#### King Among Kings
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s conflict in the [[Greek Pantheon]] produced a disturbance significant enough to attract multiple Primordial observers. The Universal Tree projected himself alongside the Judges of the Abyss and The Owner of Limbo.[13]
Unlike the other Primordials, who were primarily concerned with Victor's effect upon the Balance, the Universal Tree reacted positively to Victor's growth because it meant Jeanne possessed a stronger protector. When the Owner of Limbo questioned whether Victor would destabilize the Balance, the Universal Tree argued that Victor's Essence was naturally aligned with Negativity and therefore fundamentally different from [[Diablo]]'s attempt to forcibly acquire incompatible opposing Aspects.[13]
During the later discussion among the Primordials, the Universal Tree detected the beginning of the Convergence.[14] The conversation demonstrated that the Regulators cooperate because their cosmic functions are interconnected, but they are not a single political mind and can disagree considerably over how individual situations should be handled.
The Universal Tree later directly reunited with Jeanne while Victor and his family were establishing their newly constructed planet. The project demonstrated that physically assembling rock, oceans, atmosphere and celestial bodies was not enough to create a genuine supernatural living world. Such a world also required integration with the deeper systems governing Life, Souls, Energy and planetary development.[2]
During the reunion, the Universal Tree transformed from his radiant white-and-golden appearance into his humanoid form, embraced Jeanne and inspected her Soul. He confirmed that the Being previously assigned to protect her remained present.[2]
Another cosmic problem emerged after the Witches of [[The Arcane Realm]] created a portal to a foreign world operating under dramatically altered temporal conditions. The Universal Tree detected that the effects of accelerated Time had begun passing through the portal and immediately alerted the Judges of the Abyss.[19] The incident illustrates the Regulators' role in correcting abnormalities that threaten the larger systems under their jurisdiction.
When Victor later ascended into true Godhood, the Universal Tree was among the [[Primordial Entities]] observing the event. Victor's existence increasingly violated the expectations of conventional supernatural theory without automatically collapsing the cosmic Balance, drawing continued attention from the administrators of Creation.[20]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Further information concerning Jeanne revealed that her role as the Universal Tree's Guardian had been established directly by [[Primordial Chaos]].[15] Their later sibling relationship was therefore not simply a continuation of a predetermined cosmic function; it was a genuine relationship they personally chose after the Universal Tree became conscious.
The ancient history of [[Azathoth Elderblood]] later demonstrated that even the native Primordials are not unrestricted outside the system they administer. When Azathoth entered the native Cosmos as an Eldritch existence originating from foreign [[Chaos]], Beings associated with the Universal Tree, Death, Infinity, Limbo and the other universal functions attempted to deal with her. Her existence did not properly belong to the Rules their authorities were designed to administer, and the native Primordials ultimately resorted to imprisonment rather than simply erasing her as they could a conventional native existence.[21]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
The universal reincarnation infrastructure was eventually explained in considerably greater detail. The [[Reincarnation System]] had been created by the Universal Tree so that the innumerable Souls passing through the cycle of death and rebirth could be processed without requiring his direct personal attention in every case.[7]
The Reincarnation System is separate from The System. The former is specifically associated with returning processed Souls to new Lives, while the latter functions as a much broader administrative mechanism connected to the Rules and authorities of the Universe.
The Universal Tree's relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became substantially more important when the Judges of the Abyss began treating Victor's increasingly abnormal existence as a possible cosmic threat. The Universal Tree immediately opposed reckless action against him, particularly because any confrontation would inevitably involve Jeanne.[8]
He explicitly declared that if Jeanne chose Victor over the other Primordials, he would stand beside his sister regardless of his relationship with his fellow Regulators. When Victor's Negative Power escalated, the Universal Tree was capable of dispersing the surrounding Power with a wave of his hand and preventing the confrontation from immediately becoming a battle between Primordial forces.[8]
After understanding that Victor's violent response had primarily been triggered by threats toward his wives, the Universal Tree took an unprecedented step and declared Victor's family to be his own children.[8] Through this declaration he extended his Primordial protection over the Elderblood household, making interference with them an issue that could directly involve the Regulator of Life himself.
Victor subsequently investigated whether a genuine Primordial could be permanently killed. [[Azathoth Elderblood]] explained that completed Primordials are fundamental pieces of the Universe representing Aspects required for Creation to function. Permanently eliminating one would consequently require eliminating the Aspect itself rather than merely destroying a physical manifestation.[10]
The Universal Tree's control of Souls and reincarnation was later demonstrated at an even greater level through the Soul formerly associated with [[Merlin]]. The previous Soul had been heavily affected by Demonic corruption and could not simply be treated as a normal reincarnation. The Universal Tree fundamentally reconstructed the Soul's internal structure, resulting in an existence sufficiently different that Victor no longer considered the child to simply be Merlin restored to life. He named the new existence [[Melida Charlotte Elderblood]].[22]
The origins of the [[World Trees]] were later clarified. The Universal Tree created the seeds from which his daughters developed and distributed them throughout Creation so they could maintain Life-bearing worlds.[6] Conventional World Trees are therefore not merely similar supernatural species; they form a greater lineage and cosmic system descending directly from the Universal Tree.
Victor's expanding Empire eventually forced him to study this infrastructure from the perspective of planetary colonization. A barren or newly constructed planet could not become a complete supernatural living world through ordinary terraforming alone. It required either an actual World Tree or another existence capable of performing the same metaphysical function. Victor eventually developed the ability to connect worlds directly to himself and act as their substitute World Tree.[23]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s lessons concerning Divine Concepts, dimensions and Primordial Beings, he provided the most detailed description yet of the Universal Tree's actual scale.
Victor explained that the Universal Tree exists throughout the entire Cosmos, is connected to all [[World Trees]] and possesses a cosmic consciousness on a universal level. Through this consciousness he can acquire enormous quantities of information with a thought and manifest himself across the Universe. Victor used him as an example of a genuine completed Primordial whose existence transcends simple descriptions based upon ordinary dimensional theories.[1]
Victor further described the Universal Tree as a fundamental component of the cosmic mechanism. His existence is so deeply integrated into Life that completely destroying him would effectively result in the end of Life throughout the Universe.[1]
The Universal Tree became directly involved again during the crisis surrounding [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]]. The System approached Victor because Moira had been born with an unprecedented relationship with Reality and attempted to claim her as a suitable candidate for the vacant Primordial function associated with Destiny.[24][1]
Victor categorically rejected the proposal and threatened to damage the Universe itself if anyone attempted to take his daughter. The System subsequently informed the active Primordials, causing the Universal Tree, the Judges of the Abyss, the Owner of Limbo, [[True Death]] and Infinity to appear.[1]
As the confrontation escalated, the Universal Tree and the Owner of Limbo positioned themselves between Victor and the other Primordials. Jeanne demanded that her brother assist her husband, prompting the Universal Tree to formally declare that no one would approach Moira because anyone attempting to do so would also have to deal with him.[1]
When the Judges questioned whether Life was neglecting his responsibilities by taking a side, the Universal Tree rejected the accusation. Moira was his sister's niece, and protecting Jeanne's family remained compatible with what he considered his own responsibilities. The other Regulators eventually accepted that the matter would not be pursued.[1]
After the others departed, the Universal Tree remained with Victor and acknowledged that the System's behavior had been abnormal. According to him, the rules governing the Primordial Beings normally prevent unnecessary interference with ordinary existence, and the System's direct effort to acquire Moira went far beyond its usual authority. He promised to investigate what had changed while continuing to protect his sister's home and family.[1]
The Universal Tree met Victor again at the edge of the Elderblood Sector while Victor prepared to merge the smaller Sectors containing [[Nightingale]], Samar and [[The Arcane Realm]] into his own. Their conversation confirmed that the Universal Tree had still failed to discover why the System had targeted Moira; even the active Primordial administrators were being denied information because the System continued to recognize the surviving higher authority of [[Primordial Chaos]].[4] The Universal Tree nevertheless remained concerned for Moira as his niece and continued treating the Elderblood household as extended family.
Their discussion then turned toward the World Trees under Victor's control. The Universal Tree criticized Victor for sheltering his daughters too thoroughly, arguing that World Trees traditionally mature by living upon worlds for thousands of years and developing personality through the responsibility and hardship associated with preserving Life. Victor rejected the idea that newborn World Trees should be abandoned to survive alone. During the argument, the Universal Tree clarified that the World Trees are not merely independent planetary organisms: each functions as a node in a vast network that extends his influence throughout Creation, and if one of these nodes disappears another World Tree eventually emerges to replace it.[4] Victor's collection of protected World Trees represented less than one percent of the total network, emphasizing the enormous scale of the system.
When the sector merger began, the Universal Tree explained his cosmic office in the greatest detail yet. He stated that he embodies the Life of the entire Universe, not simply conscious organisms; planetary dust, stars and even galaxies possess Life in the sense governed by his Authority. All such structures eventually reach Death, and the Energy released through their decay becomes material for new creation. He gave the example that the death of a supergalaxy in the higher Sectors can release enough Energy to contribute to the creation of hundreds of smaller galaxies in the lower Sectors, helping renew and stabilize the Universe.[3]
He further explained the cooperation among the fundamental Primordial functions. The Judges of the Abyss ensure that beings are registered within the [[Akashic Records]], True Death governs their end, and the Universal Tree recycles their accumulated Energy through the reincarnation cycle to create new Life. Ordinary beings may require roughly one hundred to one thousand years for this recycling depending upon the Energy they accumulated, while an anomaly on Victor's scale would theoretically require trillions of years. The Universal Tree summarized the process as a cycle in which Life begins with him, is processed through Soul, reaches Death and ultimately returns to Life for renewal, while the Akashic Records preserve the additional information created by existence.[3]
The same conversation exposed another purpose of the World Tree system. The Universal Tree internally acknowledged the network as a means of detecting suspicious existences, while Victor independently deduced that the World Trees, Death, Infinity, Soul and the System collectively form layers of protection against potential intruders originating beyond their native Universe.[4][3] The Universal Tree was irritated by how quickly Victor reconstructed the larger design but did not meaningfully contradict the conclusion.
Finally, the Universal Tree directly participated in the unprecedented merger of the three lower Sectors with Victor's middle Sector. While Victor pulled Nightingale, Samar and Arcane toward his territory, the Universal Tree stabilized the surrounding cosmic structure, prevented unrelated lower Sectors from being dragged into the process and ensured that neighboring regions moved aside rather than colliding with the incoming galaxies. After the merger succeeded, he judged Victor's first attempt positively.[3]

### Cosmic Function

#### Life
The Universal Tree occupies the Primordial office associated with Life. This differs fundamentally from a conventional God possessing a Life Divinity. A completed Primordial is a component of the universal structure itself rather than merely a user drawing Power from a corresponding Concept.[10]
The Universal Tree therefore governs the continuation and maintenance of Life throughout Creation. The author directly identifies him as responsible for sustaining the Universe, the existence of Life, reincarnation and maintenance of the Universe.[9]
Chapter 1181 establishes that Life at the Primordial level is substantially broader than ordinary biological life. The Universal Tree explicitly states that he embodies the Life of the entire Universe, ranging from the dust of a planet to the largest stars in the Cosmos. Celestial structures possess a form of Life and eventually reach their end, after which their remaining Energy returns to the greater cycle and contributes to the birth of new structures and existences.[3] In this framework, galaxies themselves participate in a cosmic life-cycle: the death of a supergalaxy in the higher Sectors can release enough Energy to contribute to the formation of hundreds of smaller galaxies in lower Sectors, continuously renewing and stabilizing Creation.[3]
His authority consequently extends beyond healing or biological manipulation. Life in this context includes the physical and metaphysical form into which a Soul incarnates, the infrastructure required by living planets, the development and eventual decay of celestial structures and the processes necessary to continually return accumulated Energy to new existence.

#### Matter
Chapter 1176 additionally refers to the Universal Tree as the Primordial progenitor associated with Life, Matter and Reincarnation.[1]
Chapter 1181 clarifies why Matter can overlap with his Primordial function. The Universal Tree's concept of Life includes planetary dust, stars and galaxies rather than separating "living" biology from supposedly inert cosmic matter.[3] This still should not be interpreted as proof that every possible application of Matter Manipulation belongs exclusively to him; the demonstrated context instead shows that Matter participates in the universal Life-cycle administered through his Authority.

#### Reincarnation
The Universal Tree governs the return of processed Souls and Energy into Life. Chapter 1181 finally describes this process as part of a closed universal cycle shared among several independent Primordial authorities.[3]

#### Maintenance of the Universe
The title Maintainer of the Universe does not mean that every function of Reality belongs to the Universal Tree. The Universe operates through multiple interconnected Primordial functions.
The Universal Tree maintains Life and reincarnation; the Judges administer Soul and Akashic registration; [[True Death]] represents Death and the End; Infinity continually expands the Universe through its existence; and the Owner of Limbo maintains Balance among sentient systems.[3] Each represents a different component required for the total cosmic structure.
Chapter 1181 shows that this maintenance is not limited to living civilizations. The birth, aging and death of stars and galaxies feed directly into the same renewal process. Energy released from dead cosmic structures becomes material for later creation, preventing the Universe from becoming permanently clogged by structures that never end and allowing new galaxies and Life to arise.[3]
The author explicitly states that the seven Primordial Beings do not possess a conventional hierarchy and instead lists them according to importance. In that auxiliary scale, the Universal Tree is listed after [[Primordial Chaos]], Positivity/Negativity, Infinity and Death and before the Judges of the Abyss and the Owner of Limbo.[9] This importance scale should not be interpreted as a simple combat ranking.

### World Tree System
The Universal Tree is the progenitor and creator of all conventional [[World Trees]].[6] Each World Tree functions as a localized extension of the greater Life-system, maintaining a planet or comparable supernatural ecosystem rather than the entire Universe.
A sufficiently mature World Tree can establish or sustain the conditions required for Life on a hostile planet. This is why World Trees are valued even by Gods and [[God-Kings]] independently of their direct combat ability.[25]
The Universal Tree's daughters are connected to him through a greater metaphysical network. This relationship allows the World Trees to function as individual personalities while simultaneously remaining parts of the cosmic Life infrastructure originating from their father.

The World Tree network is far more than a family lineage. Chapter 1176 establishes that the Universal Tree is connected to all World Trees and exists throughout the Cosmos through this connection.[1] Chapter 1180 clarifies the structure further: every conventional World Tree functions as a localized node extending the Universal Tree's influence through Creation, collectively forming a gigantic network tied to the Life of the Universe.[4]
The network consequently provides him with a distributed cosmic presence and awareness extending across the worlds supported by his daughters. Chapter 1178 confirms that this connection remains fundamental even when World Trees are incorporated into Victor's separate Imperial network: information could normally propagate toward the Universal Tree through that connection, although Victor's presence inside his own inner world interferes with the transmission strongly enough to prevent information from leaking while the World Trees remain there.[26] The network also possesses redundancy. The Universal Tree explains that if a World Tree node disappears, another World Tree eventually appears to replace it, preventing the permanent loss of that portion of the Life-network.[4]
A second function of the network is cosmic surveillance. The Universal Tree internally acknowledges that the distributed World Trees help detect suspicious existences, while Victor later deduces that the network forms part of the Universe's layered protection against potential intruders from beyond its native cosmological system.[4][3] The World Trees therefore function simultaneously as planetary Life-maintainers, extensions of their father's presence and an enormous distributed early-warning network.
World Trees were originally propagated through seeds created by the Universal Tree rather than ordinary biological reproduction.[6] Traditionally, young World Trees are placed upon worlds and develop over immense periods while maintaining those worlds and interacting with the Life that emerges around them. The Universal Tree believes this process—and the hardship involved—is important for the development of individual personality.[4] Victor's later ability to produce children with World Trees and shelter large numbers of them inside his own protected system therefore represents an abnormal development outside both the species' original reproductive structure and their traditional upbringing.
By Chapter 1180, the World Trees under Victor's direct control still represent less than one percent of the Universal Tree's total daughters, illustrating the astronomical scale of the complete network.[4]

### Reincarnation System
Main article: [[Reincarnation System]]
The Reincarnation System is the universal mechanism created by the Universal Tree to automate the process of rebirth.[7]
Without such a mechanism, the Primordial of Life would need to personally administer an astronomical number of individual reincarnations throughout Creation. The Reincarnation System allows processed Souls to remain within the larger cycle until conditions exist for them to enter another living existence. Chapter 1181 further establishes that what is recycled is not only a Soul's identity but also the enormous quantity of Energy accumulated by existence itself; once the relevant data has entered the [[Akashic Records]], that Energy is gradually returned to the Universal Tree's cycle and used to produce new Life.[3]
Reincarnation does not mean that every Soul simply returns unchanged. Memories, identity and existential structure can be altered depending upon the process involved. The Universal Tree has demonstrated enough authority to reconstruct a Soul so fundamentally that the resulting Being is no longer meaningfully the same individual, as demonstrated by the transformation of the Soul associated with [[Merlin]] into [[Melida Charlotte Elderblood]].[22]
The Reincarnation System should not be confused with The System. They are separate structures with different purposes. The Reincarnation System is an infrastructure of Life and rebirth created by the Universal Tree; The System is a broader cosmic administrative mechanism associated with the governance and registration of Reality's authorities.

### Primordial Dominion
The Universal Tree possesses a Primordial Domain corresponding to his office as Life.
A Primordial Domain is not merely political territory. It represents the systems, existences and portions of cosmic function that fall beneath a Regulator's Authority.
The [[World Trees]] naturally exist beneath the Universal Tree's Dominion as his daughters and creations. Interfering with them can therefore become interference with the Primordial system that protects them.
In Chapter 1074, the Universal Tree deliberately extended comparable protection to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family by declaring them his children.[8] This was not simply affectionate language. The declaration placed the Elderblood family beneath his Primordial protection and made hostile interference with them a matter capable of involving Life directly.
The consequences of conflicts between Primordial Domains are severe because a fight between Primordials is simultaneously a conflict between fundamental cosmic functions. This is why the Regulators generally avoid direct war even when they strongly disagree.

### Authority
Within the in-story System hierarchy, the Universal Tree's registered Primordial Authority is <Life> and has been indexed as Rank 4 within the later Authority structure.
This should not be confused with the author's earlier auxiliary importance scale, which lists the Universal Tree as the fifth entry after [[Primordial Chaos]], Positivity/Negativity, Infinity and Death.[9] The auxiliary explanation explicitly states that the Primordials do not possess an ordinary hierarchy; the ordering measures importance to the functioning of Creation rather than guaranteeing the result of direct combat.

### Limitations
Despite his status as a completed Primordial, the Universal Tree is not unrestrictedly omnipotent.
His highest authority is specialized around the universal functions associated with Life, [[World Trees]] and reincarnation. Other Primordials possess their own superior jurisdiction within corresponding Aspects of Creation.
For example:
- [[The Judges of The Abyss]] possess primary authority over Soul.
- [[True Death]] possesses primary authority over Death and the End.
- Infinity possesses the Primordial function associated with Infinity.
- The Owner of Limbo possesses primary authority over Balance.
The Universal Tree can interact with Souls because Life and reincarnation require such interaction, but this does not make him the Primordial of Soul. Likewise, detecting and helping correct a temporal anomaly does not make Time one of his primary Concepts.
His universal-scale consciousness also has limits. Although [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] states that the Universal Tree can acquire enormous amounts of information through his cosmic awareness, Chapter 1176 demonstrates that the Tree can still be unaware of hidden changes affecting the cosmic administration. He did not know why the System had suddenly begun exceeding its former limitations and needed to investigate the anomaly.[1] Chapter 1178 further demonstrates that the World Tree connection itself can be obstructed: while the World Trees remain inside Victor's protected inner world, his Eldritch presence interferes with their fundamental connection strongly enough that information does not normally leak back to the Universal Tree.[26] By Chapter 1180 the investigation into Moira's case still had not produced an answer because the System deliberately withheld information from the Primordial administrators while continuing to recognize a surviving superior authority belonging to [[Primordial Chaos]].[4] The Universal Tree therefore possesses extraordinarily broad knowledge without unrestricted access to information deliberately concealed or blocked by higher/foreign authority.
The Primordials are also constrained by the responsibilities and Rules of their offices. They normally avoid directly interfering with ordinary existence unless an event threatens the larger cosmic system.[1] The Universal Tree repeatedly bends this neutrality because of Jeanne and her family, but doing so carries the risk of conflicts between fundamental cosmic functions.
His current state should also not be projected backward onto his infancy. The Universal Tree explicitly compares the ancient need for Jeanne to protect him with the vulnerability suffered by Primordial Chaos during an unknown primordial crisis, confirming that the entity who would become Life once required an external Guardian while still developing.[3]

### Relationships

#### Jeanne Elderblood
[[Jeanne Elderblood]] is simultaneously the Universal Tree's original Guardian and adopted sister.
She was assigned by [[Primordial Chaos]] to protect the seed from which he would develop and remained beside him for thousands of years.[5][15]
After gaining consciousness, the Universal Tree asked what Jeanne wanted in return. Her answer—family—became the foundation of their relationship. He understood that forced affection would defeat the purpose of Jeanne's request and therefore chose to become her brother himself.[5]
Their relationship became one of the strongest personal bonds held by any known Primordial. The Universal Tree consistently prioritizes Jeanne's safety and openly admits that his normal neutrality does not fully apply to her.[14][8]
Although Jeanne is chronologically older, he insists upon acting as her older brother.[2]

#### Victor Elderblood
The Universal Tree initially became favorably disposed toward [[Victor Elderblood]] because Victor restored Jeanne's memories at considerable personal cost. The Tree repaired Victor's Soul and blessed him in gratitude.[5]
His opinion of Victor continued improving as Victor became stronger. Rather than perceiving every increase in Victor's power as inherently dangerous, the Universal Tree evaluated whether Victor's existence actually violated the cosmic Balance. By Chapter 802 he was openly pleased because Victor's increased strength made Jeanne safer.[13]
Their relationship later developed into an unusual familial alliance. The Universal Tree opposed the Judges when they considered Victor a possible target, extended Primordial protection over Victor's household and eventually openly supported Victor's refusal to surrender Moira to the System.[8][1]
Although he does not automatically agree with Victor's actions, he possesses a considerably better understanding of Victor's priorities than several other Regulators. He recognizes that Victor's most extreme reactions occur when his family is threatened and generally prefers de-escalation over attempting to dominate him through cosmic authority.
By Chapters 1180–1181 their relationship has developed into an openly cooperative friendship as well as a familial alliance. They casually argue, exchange jokes and disagree over how the [[World Trees]] should be raised, yet the Universal Tree still travels personally to Victor's sector boundary to assist with the unprecedented merger of [[Nightingale]], Samar and Arcane.[4][3] He handles the surrounding cosmic structure while Victor performs the merger itself and afterward openly acknowledges that Victor handled his first attempt well.

#### World Trees
All conventional [[World Trees]] originate from the Universal Tree and are treated as his daughters.[6]
Their relationship is both familial and metaphysical. Each World Tree possesses her own identity and personality, but all are linked to the greater system originating from their father.
The Universal Tree can reinforce his daughters, perceive them through the greater network and remains connected to their existence across the Cosmos.[18][1] Chapter 1180 reveals that every World Tree also functions as a node spreading his influence, with replacement trees eventually appearing if an individual node disappears.[4]
His affection for the World Trees is considerably less protective than [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s parental approach. The Universal Tree expects young World Trees to develop through long periods of responsibility and hardship on their assigned worlds because he believes such experience produces personality and causes Life to appreciate its own value.[4] He is therefore simultaneously their father, creator and cosmic administrator rather than a conventional indulgent parent.

#### The Judges of the Abyss
The Universal Tree and [[The Judges of The Abyss]] possess closely interconnected responsibilities.
The Judges administer Souls, whereas the Universal Tree administers the Life and reincarnation into which those Souls return.[12] Their functions therefore cooperate continuously despite being separate Primordial Domains. Chapter 1181 clarifies the operational relationship: the Judges ensure that existences are entered into the [[Akashic Records]] and their accumulated spiritual information is preserved, after which the Universal Tree can recycle the corresponding Energy through the reincarnation cycle to produce new Life.[3]
This does not prevent disagreement. The most significant known conflict arose when the Judges considered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] too dangerous and the Universal Tree refused to allow their concern to endanger Jeanne or her family.[8]
A direct fight between Life and Soul would be extraordinarily destructive because the conflict would not simply involve two individuals; it would involve two foundational systems required by the Universe.

#### True Death
[[True Death]] and the Universal Tree administer opposite but inseparable stages of the same universal process. Life begins and develops beneath the Universal Tree, while Death governs the end of existences and celestial structures. The Energy and material released through that ending eventually returns to the Universal Tree's reincarnation cycle, making Death a necessary condition for future Life rather than an absolute contradiction of it.[3]
The Universal Tree explicitly rejects endless persistence as inherently desirable, arguing that structures which never end would prevent the Universe from renewing itself. In the cosmic cycle described in Chapter 1181, Death therefore clears what has completed its existence so that Life can make use of what remains.[3]

#### Infinity
Infinity administers the endless expansion of the Universe. According to the Universal Tree, Infinity performs much of this function simply by existing and consequently appears considerably less active than the administrators responsible for Life, Soul, Death and Balance.[3]
The Universal Tree nevertheless understands Infinity as another component of the same defensive and maintenance structure surrounding Creation. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later inferred that Infinity's apparent inactivity is partially explained by its position at the outermost frontier of the native Universe, where it would naturally be among the first systems exposed to threats approaching from beyond.[3]

#### The Owner of Limbo
The Owner of Limbo is one of the Regulators with whom the Universal Tree most frequently cooperates.
Both appeared during the observation of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Greek conflict and participated in discussions concerning the state of the cosmic Balance.[13][14]
During the Moira incident, the Universal Tree and the Owner of Limbo were the primary Primordials attempting to de-escalate the confrontation between Victor and the other Regulators.[1] The Universal Tree later described Balance as the cosmic authority that monitors sentient Beings and civilizations so that no individual existence destabilizes the larger Universe, complementing Life's broader maintenance role.[3]

#### Primordial Chaos
[[Primordial Chaos]] is directly responsible for the circumstances surrounding the Universal Tree's origin.
Jeanne was created and assigned by Primordial Chaos to guard the seed from which the Universal Tree developed.[15]
The current Primordials subsequently received functions within the structure of the Universe created from Primordial Chaos. Chapter 1176 indicates that these cosmic offices were granted to the Primordial Beings rather than every administrator naturally being born with complete governing authority.[1]
Chapter 1181 further reveals that the complete Primordial Chaos was once a single conscious Being before becoming divided into the halves now represented by Positivity and Negativity. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] questioned how an effectively supreme Being could have reached such a damaged condition, the Universal Tree compared the event to the reason he himself required Jeanne as a protector during infancy.[3] The exact ancient enemy or event remains unrevealed, and the Universal Tree refused to provide Victor with a direct explanation.
This helps explain why the Universal Tree is simultaneously an individual person and a cosmic mechanism: he possesses a genuine ego and personality while remaining fundamentally connected to the function he was created to administer.
