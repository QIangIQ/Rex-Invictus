Azathoth's Prison is the collective designation for the succession of cosmological containment spaces used by the Regulators of Existence to imprison the true body of Azathoth Elderblood, an Outer God whose existence originated outside Victor's native Creation. Unlike conventional prisons, the confinement was designed for a Being whose Essence did not originate from the Rules of the Universe and therefore could not be dealt with through the same mechanisms normally governing native Gods, Souls and supernatural entities.[1]
Azathoth was originally sealed inside an enormous closed space maintained through the powers of Infinity and End. Although enclosed, the space behaved as though it possessed effectively endless extension, preventing Azathoth from reaching a conventional spatial boundary even when she expanded her true body and distorted the Reality surrounding herself.[1] Unable to escape immediately, Azathoth began consuming the prison while simultaneously scattering concealed fragments of her Essence throughout the Cosmos, allowing her to observe, feed upon and influence Creation despite her primary body remaining imprisoned.[1]
The original prison was eventually abandoned by Azathoth's jailers. By Chapter 1176, her true body had secretly been transferred into a still more isolated confinement: a Dimension outside the Universe where the Rules of the Creator do not apply. Azathoth describes the place as something that "doesn't really exist", while the narration characterizes the environment as a dimensionless space in which ordinary Time cannot be reliably measured.[2] Her consciousness nevertheless remains capable of leaving the confinement and interacting with Victor and their family, meaning the prison currently restrains her true body without completely isolating her mind or influence.

### Background
Azathoth did not originate within the native Universe.
She emerged from foreign [[Chaos]] and developed independently of the Rules governing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Creation. Her earliest existence was spent travelling through the Cosmos in search of understanding, consuming Energy, civilizations and powerful Beings while gradually developing self-awareness.[1]
Her growth rapidly became astronomical.
Azathoth consumed an extraordinarily advanced world populated by powerful Beings and absorbed enormous quantities of information and memories. As her understanding increased, so did the scale of her existence. Her body grew beyond conventional celestial proportions and naturally distorted Reality around itself.[1]
This eventually brought her into conflict with the native cosmic authorities.

### Capture of Azathoth
Azathoth's foreign nature created an unusual problem for the [[Regulators of Existence]].
The Primordials are deeply connected to the Rules and functions of their own Creation. Azathoth, however, had not been produced by those Rules.
Her Essence belonged to a fundamentally different cosmological origin.[1][3]
When the Regulators attempted to deal with her, ordinary methods proved insufficient.
Death could not simply erase Azathoth as it could an ordinary native existence. Infinity created effectively limitless separation around her, while the other Primordial authorities attempted to suppress or eliminate the foreign entity.[1]
Azathoth's natural Reality distortion also failed to solve the problem.
Her powers were operating inside an environment dominated by cosmic authorities vastly more established within their native Creation than she was at that stage of her development.
The solution was imprisonment.
Rather than destroying Azathoth, the Primordials isolated her true body inside a specially created containment Reality.[1]

#### Attempted Escape
Azathoth reacted violently after realizing she had been imprisoned.
Her true body expanded.
Reality around her distorted.
She attempted to overwhelm the confinement through sheer scale and the natural warping produced by her existence.[1]
It failed.
The prison was specifically capable of containing a Being whose body had already reached astronomical proportions.
Azathoth eventually realized that direct escape was inefficient.
She could instead consume the prison.

#### Consuming the Prison
Azathoth concluded that the confinement was not absolutely permanent.
Given enough maturation and accumulated Energy, she believed she would eventually be capable of devouring the prison itself.[1]
Her real objection was the amount of time required.
Waiting passively for sufficient growth would dramatically slow her development.
Azathoth therefore created another method of growing while confined.

### Essence Beyond the Prison
Azathoth scattered fragments of her Essence throughout the Cosmos.[1]
These fragments were concealed beneath the characteristics and powers of beings she had previously consumed, making their true origin difficult to recognize.
They acted as extensions of Azathoth.

### Discovery of Victor
One of Azathoth's fragments eventually reached a Lower Sector.[1]
At first, she considered the region unimpressive compared with the scales she had previously encountered.
Its existential environment was so limited relative to her true form that Azathoth noted that even a portion of her body could not comfortably manifest there.[1]
Her opinion changed as she observed the unusual supernatural species developing within the region:
- Vampires possessing Concepts normally associated with much higher Gods;
- [[Werewolves]] drawing upon Positive World Tree Energy;
- Witches manipulating unfamiliar forms of Energy;
- Youkai interacting directly with natural World Tree power;
- and increasingly abnormal Divine Beings.
Eventually, Azathoth detected [[Victor Elderblood]].
Victor possessed the characteristics of native Creation—Vampire, Dragon, God and other supernatural attributes—but beneath them existed an Eldritch nature resembling Azathoth's own foreign Essence.[4]
For the first time in her existence, Azathoth had discovered what she perceived as a genuine companion.
Her hatred of the prison intensified.
She could see Victor.
She could study him.
She could interact indirectly with him.
But her true body remained unable to reach him.

### Yog-Sothoth
Azathoth eventually obtained a minute portion of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Essence through their distant connection.
She combined it with her own.
The result was [[Yog-Sothoth]] Elderblood.[4]
I will call you... Yog-Sothoth, my first daughter, a girl who is my hope to escape from this accursed prison and find my companion. Now go, my daughter, go find your father.
—[[Azathoth Elderblood]], Chapter 980
Yog represented several things simultaneously:
- Azathoth and Victor's first daughter;
- a living connection between their Essences;
- an existence combining native and foreign cosmological characteristics;
- and one of Azathoth's potential paths out of confinement.[4][5]
Azathoth sent Yog toward Victor rather than keeping her within the prison.
Yog's creation demonstrated that the prison did not completely sever Azathoth's ability to act beyond its boundaries.

### Victor's Investigation
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually learned enough about the Primordial structure to begin considering where Azathoth's true body had actually been confined.
During his conversation with The Owner of Limbo in Chapter 1130, Victor initially considered whether Azathoth might be located somewhere inside [[The Limbo Realm]].[6]
He quickly questioned that assumption.
The Owner of Limbo explained that the deepest portions of his prison contained Beings whose Wills were too powerful to be erased and recycled normally through reincarnation.[6]
Victor reasoned that Azathoth was significantly more complicated than even those prisoners.
He consequently suspected that she had been imprisoned somewhere else where the Primordials could collectively monitor her.[6]

### Relocation
By Chapter 1176, Azathoth's prison had changed.
After the confrontation surrounding [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]], [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] directly contacted Azathoth and asked a simple question:
Where is your prison?[2]
Azathoth revealed that her true body had been moved.
She had felt the transfer occur some time earlier, although "earlier" had little conventional meaning from her perspective because the new environment no longer provided a reliable temporal reference.[2]
The relocation indicates that those responsible for imprisoning Azathoth no longer considered the original containment sufficient for the long term.
Azathoth herself interpreted the transfer as evidence that her jailers had become more cautious.[2]

### Nature of the Current Realm

#### Dimensionless Space
Although Azathoth calls the prison a "Dimension", the narration describes its environment as dimensionless.[2]
The terminology therefore describes a containment Reality rather than a conventional spatial Dimension possessing ordinary length, width, depth and temporal coordinates.

#### Time
Time does not provide Azathoth with a reliable chronological reference inside the prison.
Something she experiences as recent may have occurred:
- moments earlier;
- centuries earlier;
- or thousands of years earlier
from the perspective of ordinary Creation.[2]
Her sense of elapsed Time is therefore effectively nonfunctional while judging events through the location of her main body.

#### Existence
Azathoth states that the place "doesn't really exist."[2]
The phrase accompanies the description of:
- being outside the Universe;
- lacking the Creator's normal Rules;
- existing in a dimensionless environment;
- and possessing abnormal Time.
The prison therefore occupies an unusual state of existence beyond ordinary native Reality.

#### Unknown Boundaries
No conventional size has been established for the current prison.
Terms such as:
- kilometres;
- light-years;
- Galactic diameter;
- universal diameter
have not been applied to it.
Its defining properties concern the absence of conventional cosmological structure rather than measurable physical extension.

### Relationship with Limbo
Azathoth's prison and [[The Limbo Realm]] are closely related historically but should not be treated as automatically identical.
The Owner of Limbo specializes in imprisoning entities that cannot safely be returned to the ordinary universal cycle.[6]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] initially suspected Azathoth might be somewhere within his prison.
He then reasoned that Azathoth was too exceptional to be treated like an ordinary Limbo prisoner and suspected a separate location watched by multiple Primordials.[6]
The current outside-Universe prison confirms that Azathoth's later containment is not simply an ordinary cell in the accessible regions of Limbo.[2]

### Escape Plan
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] intends to free Azathoth.
He does not intend to attack the prison blindly.
Azathoth herself acknowledges that Victor and his family are not yet ready for the confrontation that recovering her body would likely produce.[2]
Two daughters are central to Victor's plan:
- [[Yog-Sothoth]] Elderblood;
- [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]].[2]

#### Yog-Sothoth
Yog possesses a deep metaphysical connection with Azathoth.
Azathoth states that Yog can guide [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to her, despite the true body having been transferred outside the Universe.[2]
Yog therefore solves the first problem:
finding a location that does not possess ordinary coordinates.

#### Moira Eutychia Elderblood
Moira possesses an abnormal natural relationship with Reality.
Rather than merely commanding Reality through a conventional activated ability, Reality naturally changes itself around her existence and desires.[8]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] immediately recognizes the relevance of this property when considering Azathoth's new prison.
A confinement that exists beyond ordinary Creator Rules and in a state that "doesn't really exist" presents a problem that conventional Space-Time manipulation may not solve normally.
Moira's compatibility with Reality provides another possible route around those restrictions.
