The Arcane Realm, commonly called Arcane, the Witch's Realm or the Realm of Witches, is the principal homeland of the Witches. Created by [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]] and historically governed by [[Evie Moriarty Elderblood]], Arcane developed as an isolated nation where Witches could live, research [[Magic]] and establish their own civilization without depending upon other supernatural Factions.[1][2]
Despite commonly being called a realm or dimension, Chapter 705 clarifies that Arcane was not originally an independently created dimension. It consisted of territory isolated from the ordinary world through extraordinarily advanced Magic, functioning effectively as a separate realm while remaining fundamentally a magically protected land.[3] For most of its history it was geographically associated with Australia, but [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later acquired and physically relocated the entire Witch nation into the technocratic territory governed by Velnorah.[4]

### Description
Arcane developed into an advanced magical-technological civilization whose architecture deliberately integrates Nature with artificial construction. When directly shown, its cities contain enormous trees, sophisticated buildings, spatially expanded interiors and magical infrastructure integrated into ordinary residences.[5] Its environment was compared more strongly to an Elven Realm than the stereotypical image of a Witch settlement, and its climate and seasons could be regulated through [[Magic]].
Witch society is overwhelmingly scholarly, with research and magical experimentation occupying central cultural roles. Transportation includes flying brooms, carpets, enchanted vehicles, teleportation and other personal artifacts, while disputes are frequently resolved through either Magic Duels or competitions of magical research.[5] Conventional crime was comparatively uncommon, although disagreements over research, discoveries and resources remained normal.

### History

#### Foundation and Isolation
Albedo created Arcane during an era in which the lives of Witches were considerably harsher. Many eventual inhabitants had suffered persecution, exploitation and severe trauma before gaining the security necessary to rebuild their lives within the Realm.[1] Arcane consequently served simultaneously as a political homeland, protected refuge and research center. Evie eventually became the Witch Queen and exercised extensive authority over access, national security and the Realm's magical systems, although Albedo retained unique access to structures she had personally created.
During the Demonic War, Arcane appeared to suffer a Demonic invasion, prompting Witches stationed elsewhere to return home. The apparent assault was actually a controlled exercise arranged through Evie's cooperation with [[Diablo]], allowing her to evaluate the Witches' response and recall personnel from outside territories.[5] Evie subsequently closed Arcane to ordinary travel, allowing only herself and selected individuals unrestricted movement.[6]

#### Inter-World Portal
Evie's long-term desire for genuine Witch independence eventually led her to pursue access to another world rather than remain permanently surrounded by Earth's competing supernatural Factions. Extensive research produced an inter-world portal, but the project connected Arcane to an organized foreign civilization possessing its own Gods and military rather than to an empty world suitable for settlement.[7][2]
The portal allowed Alfey to enter Arcane and exposed limitations in defenses designed primarily against known Earth-based supernatural systems. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually investigated the opposite side and concluded that the foreign civilization's greatest strength was its political and military unity.[8] The connection subsequently became a major geopolitical problem because Earth's Pantheons recognized the strategic value of access to another world.
The portal also transmitted effects far more fundamental than ordinary travel. When the Regulators accelerated Time on the connected world to allow its civilization to develop, traces of the altered temporal flow leaked through the direct dimensional connection and affected the region around the Arcane portal.[9] The incident demonstrated that the connection linked the two environments strongly enough for large-scale cosmological effects to propagate between them.

#### Submission to Victor
External pressure surrounding the portal eventually combined with a personal crisis when [[Emily Moriarty]] developed a Divine illness Evie could not cure.[4] Evie sought [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s help and accepted his demand for everything in exchange for curing Emily, surrendering herself, Arcane and everything beneath her authority. Victor immediately cured Emily and became the ultimate sovereign of the Witch nation.
Victor then surrounded the entire Realm with his Power and physically relocated the whole country into the technocratic city governed by Velnorah.[4] Evie remained the Witch Queen and local ruler of Witch society, while ultimate sovereignty transferred to Victor. He ordered the inter-world portal sealed rather than destroyed, preserving it as a future strategic asset.

### Government and Society
Arcane was historically ruled by the Witch Queen, with Evie holding authority over national security, access to the Realm, foreign diplomacy and major research projects. She also maintained seven Witches bearing the title of her daughters, most of whom were not biological children but prestigious political appointees granted exceptional resources and influence.[5] These daughters could act as arbiters in disputes between Witches and were expected to remain neutral; deliberate favoritism risked removal from the position.
Albedo's position differs from conventional political rulership. She created Arcane and therefore possesses unique authority over its original magical framework, but generally preferred research to government. Since Chapter 990, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] holds ultimate sovereignty while Evie continues administering Witch society beneath him.

### Defenses
Arcane's principal historical defense was extreme magical isolation. Forced entry was considered extraordinarily difficult even for supernatural Beings possessing advanced spatial abilities, while Evie could restrict exit, recall Witches and seal external access.[5][6] Individual structures could possess additional authentication and defensive systems layered beneath the Realm's greater protections.
These defenses were never absolute. Albedo could interact with systems she had personally created, the foreign portal bypassed assumptions underlying Arcane's conventional security, and beings on [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s level could overwhelm the Realm's protections entirely. Victor ultimately relocated the whole nation without difficulty after acquiring it.[4]

### Current Status
Arcane remains the principal homeland of Witch civilization but is no longer an independent neutral Faction. It exists within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Empire and the technocratic territory associated with Velnorah, while Evie remains Witch Queen and later became a Goddess associated with <Research>.[10] The same [[Magic]] World whose discovery endangered Arcane was later assigned to Evie as a future Imperial conquest target.
