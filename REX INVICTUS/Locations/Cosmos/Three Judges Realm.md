### Description
When [[Persephone]] wore the [[The Helm]], she was teleported to 'elsewhere' and found herself on a stone platform, and the entire landscape around her was made up of countless galaxies.[1]

### Rulers Test
This Realm is used to test the impartiality of the rulers, while none of the rulers except [[Persephone]] have been shown to had taken the test in the Novel itself, its assumed that all of these authority granting tests related to <Souls> are done here.
One of the Test that shown was when Persephone took [[The Helm]] and became the Ruler of [[Greek Underworld]].

#### Questions of Choices
This is the second part of the overall test where [[Persephone]] had to make a 'choice' as to which soul dies in the given options. Usually the souls in the question are related to their loved and closed ones just to ensure they can truly be impartial when it comes to their loved ones being judged.

##### Key Points
1. All souls are real and created by Judges for the test itself.
2. If no one is chosen by the Ruler, Both dies.
