## MTWABV Canon

### Takamagahara
Takamagahara is the principal celestial realm and traditional seat of the [[Shinto Pantheon]]. Also described as the High Plane of Heaven, it serves as the Divine territory from which [[Amaterasu]] governs the Shinto Gods and represents the positive or celestial side of the Pantheon, in contrast to Yomi, the [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] governed by [[Izanami-no-Mikoto]].
The Shinto Pantheon possesses one of the largest known populations of Gods on Earth, with more than one million Divine Beings attributed to the Pantheon.[1] Takamagahara consequently represents the political center of a Divine civilization vastly larger than the relatively small number of named Shinto Gods who directly participate in the story.
Following [[Victor Elderblood]]'s rise, the Shinto Pantheon was annexed into his greater Imperial order while retaining internal administration under Amaterasu.[2]

### Description
Takamagahara is the heavenly residence and administrative center of the [[Shinto Pantheon]].
Like the Divine territories belonging to other major Pantheons, it exists on a supernatural plane rather than as an ordinary geographical region of Japan. The Shinto Gods use it as the principal seat from which they manage their Divine society and respond to supernatural developments affecting Japan and the wider world.
Detailed information regarding Takamagahara's physical geography, dimensions or internal districts has not been established to the same degree as realms such as [[Olympus|Mount Olympus]], [[Asgard]] or [[Biblical Heaven]]. Such details should therefore not be imported from real-world Shinto mythology without confirmation from the novel.

### Government
Takamagahara is ruled by [[Amaterasu]], the Shinto Sun Goddess and Queen of the High Plane of Heaven.
Amaterasu inherited the principal celestial authority of the Pantheon following the earlier generation represented by [[Izanagi]] and [[Izanami-no-Mikoto]]. While Amaterasu governs the heavenly side, Izanami is associated with Yomi and the negative side of the Shinto supernatural system.
Important Gods operating beneath or alongside Amaterasu include Susanoo, [[Tsukuyomi]], [[Takemikazuchi]], [[Inari-Omikami]], [[Ebisu]], [[Sakuya-hime]], [[Amatsumara]], [[Sukunabikona]] and others.
The Pantheon possesses an enormous Divine population. More than one million Gods are stated to belong to Shintoism,[3] while another description emphasizes that the Pantheon's Gods alone could populate a small country.[4]
The small number of named Gods appearing directly in the story therefore represents only a tiny portion of Takamagahara's wider Divine society.

### Divine Society
The [[Shinto Pantheon]] developed its own supernatural crafts, resources and specialized Divine professions.
Among its important figures are Gods associated with forging, culture, warfare and other functions necessary to maintaining a large Divine civilization. The Pantheon became particularly notable for producing supernatural equipment and selling crafted items outside its own internal society, distinguishing its economic behavior from several other Pantheons that primarily produced Divine equipment for themselves.[5]
[[Amaterasu]]'s own Divine Fire could be incorporated into these forging processes, demonstrating that the authority of high-ranking Gods could contribute directly to the Pantheon's supernatural production.[6]

### Relationship with Yomi
Yomi serves as the principal [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] associated with the [[Shinto Pantheon]].
Takamagahara and Yomi therefore represent opposing sides of the same broader supernatural civilization:

### Military
The Pantheon possesses numerous warrior Gods capable of representing Takamagahara in supernatural conflicts.
Among the most prominent are Susanoo and [[Takemikazuchi]]. When the major Pantheons organized the hunt against surviving hostile forces following the Gathering of Supernatural Beings, [[Amaterasu]] personally sent both Gods as representatives of Shinto.[7]
The choice demonstrated both their status among the Pantheon's strongest combatants and Amaterasu's authority to deploy Takamagahara's Gods in international Divine conflicts.

### History

#### Founding
The earliest Shinto Divine order is associated with [[Izanagi]] and [[Izanami-no-Mikoto]], who represent the foundational generation behind the Pantheon.
[[Amaterasu]] eventually became the principal ruler of the heavenly side and Queen of Takamagahara.

#### Supernatural Era
The [[Shinto Pantheon]] remained one of Earth's major Divine civilizations and continued governing Japanese supernatural affairs alongside Yomi, Yokai factions, Onmyō practitioners and other supernatural organizations.
Its extraordinarily large number of Gods gave the Pantheon considerable numerical depth, although only a small number of high-ranking Gods regularly became involved in international supernatural politics.

#### Demonic War
The Demonic War significantly weakened Earth's traditional Pantheon system.
Although Takamagahara survived the conflict, the Shinto faction's ability to maintain the supernatural order surrounding Japan was strained, particularly as problems involving Yomi, the dead and independent Yokai factions accumulated.
[[Amaterasu]] increasingly relied on diplomacy rather than assuming that the Gods alone could maintain absolute authority over every supernatural faction.

#### Alliance with Victor
[[Amaterasu]] later developed a close political and personal relationship with [[Victor Elderblood]].
The [[Shinto Pantheon]] repeatedly cooperated with Victor's faction, including contributing Susanoo and [[Takemikazuchi]] to the hunt against hostile Gods and supernatural enemies.[8]

#### Annexation
Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s rise to supreme authority over Earth, the [[Shinto Pantheon]] was formally incorporated into the [[Elderblood Empire]].
Victor did not completely abolish its local administration. The Pantheon was permitted to retain substantial internal self-government under [[Amaterasu]] so long as it acknowledged the greater Imperial authority.[9]
Takamagahara therefore remained an active Shinto Divine center even after the political independence of Earth's Pantheons ended.

### Current Status
Takamagahara remains part of the [[Shinto Pantheon]]'s supernatural infrastructure, but the Pantheon no longer functions as a completely independent world power.
Its Gods ultimately exist beneath the greater authority of [[Victor Elderblood]] and the [[Elderblood Empire]], while [[Amaterasu]] continues to exercise direct authority over Shinto internal affairs.[10]

### Notes
- Takamagahara is the [[Shinto Pantheon]]'s heavenly realm; Yomi is its [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]].
- It should not be populated with locations or structures taken solely from real-world mythology unless they are confirmed in the novel.
- The statement that Shinto possesses more than one million Gods refers to the Pantheon as a whole and should not automatically be interpreted as one million named major Gods residing simultaneously in a single city.[11]
- Despite annexation, the Shinto Pantheon retained internal administration beneath the [[Elderblood Empire]] rather than being culturally abolished.[12]
