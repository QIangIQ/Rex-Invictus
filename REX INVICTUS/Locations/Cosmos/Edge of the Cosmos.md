The Edge of the Cosmos, also known as the Edge of Creation, Edge of the Universe and the Threshold of the Cosmos, is the continuously expanding outer frontier of The Universe, existing at the point where established Creation transitions into newly forming reality.[1][2] Rather than being an ordinary planet, Sector or artificially created dimension, the Edge is the region in which the infinite expansion of Creation is actively occurring under the influence of the Primordial Infinity. Reality within this frontier has not yet completely established the Rules normally governing conventional existence, causing Space, Time and abilities dependent upon stable Creation to behave abnormally. The region is consequently described as a place where everything simultaneously exists and does not exist.[1][3]
The Edge became one of the most important locations in [[Victor Elderblood]]'s development when [[Ikor-Kar-VI]] transported Victor, [[Var-Kar-VI]], [[Lucifer]] Morningstar and a Type 6 [[Destroyer]] there through Akashic Creation. Ikor deliberately selected the location because its incomplete spatial and temporal Laws suppressed Victor's abnormal speed and restricted his Creation-related abilities. The resulting battle continued for an indeterminable subjective period and forced Victor to rely extensively upon his [[Martial Arts]], eventually providing the conditions necessary for him to overcome the barrier preventing his ascension to Godhood.[1][3][4]

### Nature and Structure
The Edge is best understood as a moving cosmological frontier rather than a fixed location. Infinity is responsible for the continuous expansion of Creation, and the Edge naturally exists wherever that expansion is presently occurring.[2][4] Chapter 882 describes it as the region where the infinite Universe is constantly expanding and where reality has not yet established its Rules. Infinity therefore should not be described as having deliberately constructed the Edge in the manner of a personal dimension; instead, the Edge is a natural consequence of his universal function.
The region possesses an enormous and explicitly infinite extent. During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ascension, his laughter echoed throughout infinite space while generating spatial distortions across the surrounding frontier.[4] This infinite extent exists simultaneously with the continuous expansion of Creation, which the cosmology does not treat as contradictory. Newly forming galaxies can be observed near the frontier, and Chapter 879 describes hundreds of billions of galaxies across the horizon while additional cosmic explosions marked the formation of new galactic structures.[1]
Because the Edge is still undergoing the transition into established Creation, it should not be treated as another conventional Sector. It represents the boundary between reality whose Laws have already stabilized and reality that is still being formed. The exact process through which these unfinished regions mature into ordinary cosmic structures has not been completely explained, although Victor's ascension later demonstrates that sufficiently powerful stabilizing forces can accelerate that process.

### Space, Time and Reality
Space and Time function abnormally at the Edge because the Rules defining them have not yet fully stabilized. Chapter 883 states that concepts such as Space and Time became effectively irrelevant during the prolonged battle, making ordinary measurements of distance and duration unreliable.[3] [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually became incapable of determining whether he had been fighting for one hundred years, one thousand years, one hundred thousand years or another period entirely. These possibilities are explicitly his attempts to conceptualize an experience in which normal temporal perception had ceased to be useful, and none should therefore be treated as the battle's confirmed duration.
Space nevertheless continues to exist in some form. It can be distorted, shattered and manipulated, but conventional spatial relationships are unreliable. During Victor's ascension his existence generated spatial distortions throughout the infinite expanse, while Ikor's final technique later shattered Space and sent Victor through hundreds of universes before returning him to [[Nightingale]].[4] The Edge is therefore more accurately described as possessing unstable or incompletely established Space rather than no Space whatsoever.
The repeated statement that everything exists and does not exist simultaneously likewise refers to the paradoxical condition of unfinished Creation rather than literal absolute Nonexistence. Physical Beings can enter the region, fight, use Energy, awaken Divinities and interact with their surroundings. The Edge should consequently not be equated with Nothingness, which is a separate cosmological structure outside ordinary Creation and the normal influence of the [[Akashic Records]].

### Environmental Effects
The abnormal properties of the Edge can severely restrict abilities that depend upon established Creation. Ikor specifically chose the location because [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s extraordinary speed and his ability to manipulate Creation were among the greatest obstacles to defeating him.[1] Within the Edge, Victor could no longer exploit his movement capabilities in their normal manner, while several Creation-related abilities were heavily restricted by the incomplete state of reality.
This suppression was not universal Power Nullification. Victor, Ikor, Var, [[Lucifer]] and the [[Destroyer]] continued using physical power, Energy attacks, Divinities and numerous supernatural abilities throughout the fight. Victor's access to Negativity was also not completely sealed: he maintained his connection to [[Roxanne Elderblood]] and continued receiving Negative Energy while Ikor's stored Positive Energy gradually diminished because he had been separated from [[Amara Elderblood]] and [[Nightingale]].[3] The weakening of Ikor's reserves also reduced the Energy sustaining the [[Immortality]] he had granted to other Elder Gods fighting elsewhere.[2]

### Access
Reaching the Edge normally appears to require extraordinarily advanced dimensional capability because it is not a fixed conventional coordinate. Information associated with Velnorah indicates that sufficiently high authority over Space or powers connected to Infinity can provide methods of reaching the expanding frontier.[5] Ikor circumvented ordinary travel through Akashic Creation, demonstrating that specialized techniques can transport individuals there even if the passengers themselves do not possess the necessary authority.

### Akashic Creation
Akashic Creation was the technique used by [[Ikor-Kar-VI]] to turn the Edge into a battlefield advantageous against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[1] Ikor summoned a white staff, gathered concentrated Positive Energy and generated a white domain around himself, Victor, Var, [[Lucifer]] and a Type 6 [[Destroyer]] before transporting the group away from [[Nightingale]] and into the cosmic frontier. The white environment immediately surrounding the combatants should not be treated as synonymous with the entire natural Edge; it was associated with Ikor's technique and the controlled battlefield he established within the much larger threshold.
Ikor's plan successfully neutralized several of Victor's largest advantages, but it also separated Ikor from Nightingale's renewable Positive Energy and unintentionally gave Victor an environment in which he could continuously refine his [[Martial Arts]]. The longer the battle continued, the more the strategic advantage shifted away from Ikor.

### Battle at the Edge
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] initially fought Ikor, Var, [[Lucifer]] and the Type 6 [[Destroyer]] simultaneously. With his speed and Creation manipulation heavily restricted, he entered an extreme combat state centered upon physical ability, Energy control and [[Martial Arts]].[3] Because conventional Time was effectively meaningless, the battle continued for an unknowable subjective period. Instead of exhausting Victor, this prolonged confrontation allowed him to repeatedly refine his techniques until Ikor and Var realized that their attempt to suppress him had effectively become training.
The battle simultaneously became a conflict of resources. Victor continued receiving Negative Energy through Roxanne, whereas Ikor possessed only the Positive Energy he had carried away from [[Nightingale]]. As his reserves diminished, Ikor began using Predation upon his own allies. He consumed the Type 6 Destroyer and later Lucifer, restoring Energy and incorporating their abilities into himself.[3] Despite these improvements, Victor continued approaching the metaphysical barrier that had prevented his development from progressing further.

### Victor's Ascension
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] ultimately broke through that barrier at the Edge and began awakening numerous Divine Concepts. His major awakenings included <War>, <Destruction>, <Negativity>, <BEGIN> and <Blood>, alongside multiple lesser Divinities such as Murder, Strength, Beauty, Revenge, Martial Honor, [[Rex Invictus|Home]], Family, Nature and Yandere.[4] His awakening of Destruction caused his laughter to echo throughout the infinite space of the frontier, while the manifestation of Blood transformed the surrounding expanse and exposed Ikor and Var to an aspect of Victor's deeper existence that their minds could not comprehend.
The resulting madness experienced by Ikor and Var should not be attributed to the Edge itself. Their Souls were overwhelmed because they perceived Victor's incomprehensible cosmic-horror nature.[4] There is no evidence that merely observing or attempting to understand the Edge naturally inflicts madness.
Victor's ascension attracted the attention of the [[Regulators of Existence]], including [[The Universal Tree]], [[The Judges of The Abyss]], The Owner of Limbo, [[True Death]] and Infinity. Infinity initially referred to Victor as a God of [[Chaos]] before the Universal Tree identified the more accurate description, Chaos Dragon God. Victor's Divine Dragon body expanded from hundreds of meters to beyond planetary scale before he deliberately compressed it to approximately 500 meters, preferring greater density over unnecessary physical size.[4]

### Stabilization of the Edge
One of the most important consequences of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ascension was his passive effect upon the unfinished reality surrounding him. The Primordials initially suspected that his unusual combination of opposing forces was destabilizing Creation, but closer observation demonstrated the opposite: Victor simultaneously produced [[Chaos]], Order and Balance, causing the unstable frontier around him to become increasingly coherent.[4]
[[True Death]] observed that if Victor simply remained sleeping within the region for several years, the surrounding unfinished Creation would eventually stabilize into a new galaxy.[4] This does not mean Victor deliberately created a galaxy during the battle. His existence was instead accelerating the natural transition from unstable expanding reality into conventional cosmic structure. The System also began automatically correcting thousands of minor errors while Victor remained there, further demonstrating that his newly established existence was functioning as an unusual stabilizing influence upon Creation.
The Owner of Limbo consequently identified Victor as a fundamental Pillar of Balance. This phenomenon was caused by Victor rather than being a normal property of the Edge.

### Ikor's Escape
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] completed his ascension, Ikor understood that remaining at the Edge would result in his death. He consumed [[Var-Kar-VI]], acquiring Adaptation and combining it with his existing [[Immortality]] and Predation.[4] Ikor then released a concentrated attack and used a technique that caused existence around it to become unbalanced. Rather than travelling normally through Space, the attack appeared directly before Victor as if the distance between them had been bypassed.
The impact shattered Space and propelled Victor through hundreds of universes.[4] This should not be rewritten as hundreds of Sectors. Victor's post-ascension defenses prevented the attack itself from inflicting significant lasting damage; the visible damage occurred primarily because he was travelling between universes without the protection supplied by Ikor's transportation technique. A portal subsequently returned Victor to [[Nightingale]], with Ikor following immediately afterward.
Upon returning to established reality, Victor felt the abilities suppressed at the Edge beginning to operate normally again.[4] This directly confirms that their restriction resulted from the environment rather than a permanent loss of Power.

### Relationship with Other Cosmological Structures
The Edge occupies a distinct position from The Abyss and Nothingness. The Edge represents the frontier of newly forming Creation and is closely associated with Infinity, whereas the Abyss contains cosmic regions that have reached their End and collapsed into dead or decaying states. Nothingness is further removed still, existing outside the ordinary structure and influence of Creation.
