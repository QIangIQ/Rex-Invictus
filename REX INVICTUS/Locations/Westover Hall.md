**[[Westover Hall]]** is the castle-like military boarding school in Bar Harbor, Maine, where [[Bianca di Angelo]] and [[Nico di Angelo]] are found in [[The Titan's Curse]]. [[Dr. Thorn]] is its vice principal. In [[Rex Invictus]] the extraction happens in [[Ch15 - Rex Invictus VIII]], with [[Hunters of Artemis|the Hunt]] arriving in [[Ch16 - Rex Invictus IX]].
