**[[Camp Jupiter]]** is the Roman demigod camp in the Oakland Hills of California, the Roman counterpart of [[Camp Half-Blood]]. It is home to the [[Legio XII Fulminata]] and borders [[New Rome]], where veterans live. The [[Tiber|Little Tiber]] runs through it and [[Terminus]] guards the city line. [[Lupa]] trains new arrivals at the [[Wolf House]]. Leaders include [[Reyna Avila Ramírez-Arellano]], [[Jason Grace]], [[Frank Zhang]] and [[Hazel Levesque]]; the augur [[Octavian]] reads entrails. It appears in [[The Son of Neptune]] and [[The Tyrant's Tomb]]. In [[Rex Invictus]] [[Jason Grace|Jason]]'s cohorts hold it during the [[Ch22 - Rex Invictus XV|Ch22]] battle.
