> The Pleistocene Era Shadowrealm  is a Shadowrealm created by Marethyu and his all powerful [[Aether]] Hook. The Shadowrealm was modelled on the time Marethyu was born, before he was taken away [[Isis]] and [[Osiris]] and transported into another Shadowrealm.

### Description
The appearance of this Shadowrealm is modelled after the real time era, so closely that Joan and [[Scathach]] believe they have been transported into the past.
This Shadowrealm has all the creatures that would have resided in that time, including sabretooth tigers and dire wolves. Large lions, cave bears, bison herds and condor flocks inhabited the land. This world, made to [[Marethyu]]'s desires, has no snakes or serpentine creatures.
This Shadowrealm's main noticeable difference is the presence of four moons in the nighttime sky. One "huge and yellow, another smaller and tinged sepia; the third was a bright green emerald, while the fourth was colorless" ([[The Necromancer]]) [1]. Other differences became apparent; only a southward breeze that smelled of lemons, an absence of insects and a sun that maintained its peak position for too long. A night sky that closely but not perfectly resembled the Milky Way Galaxy and a missing polestar became visible at night. A large lake sits in the middle of the Shadowrealm that animals will drink from.
All the features and inhabitants are directly controlled by Marethyu. [2] He demonstrated by turning the watering hole into ice, then lava and finally reverting it to water. [[Palamedes]] also lost his (favorite) sword when he threatened Marethyu, who transformed it into a sprouting branch, forcing Palamedes into dropping it to be swallowed by the earth below. How much of this power is derived from being the shadowrealm creator and how much is control over the [[Aether]] hook is unclear.

#### Sequenced Leygates
A trilithon (two standing stones topped by a slab) modeled on [[Stonehenge]] lies within the shadowrealm as a secondary leygate. It connects directly to [[Xibalba]]. Inside is the first group of 13 temporally manipulated leygates, sequenced as a favor by [[Chronos]]. [3]
Some of the myriad shadowrealms briefly mentioned through these specific numbered gates include:
- The zero gate located directly at the Crossroads intersection of shadowrealms in Xibalba.
- An entirely crystalline world. (1st gate made of spun glass).
- Shifting green sand on the ground, a giant red sun close enough to see the solar activity. Both patterns matched exactly. (1st gate made of silica pillars).
- Ice that smelled of sour milk. (2nd frozen gate).
- A metal landscape of steel and lead. (3rd gate of rusted iron slabs).
- Gaseous yellow fog echoing baby cries. (5th gate of indistinguishable fog).
- Fully carbon-based, dominated by fields of oil and tar eaten by metallic insects. (8th gate of carved coal).
- Apocalypse wrung city. Abandoned. (13th gate of an empty hotel doorway).

### History
[[Marethyu]] brings [[Joan of Arc]], [[Scathach]], [[Comte de Saint-Germain]], [[Palamedes]], and [[William Shakespeare]] to the Shadowrealm by force to explain to them that he needs them to bring about the destruction of [[Danu Talis]]. Joan and Scatach get transported there first, after the ley gate at Notre Dame was tampered with, sending the ladies back in time. They fend off local wildlife for a night, and are greeted lakeside by Marethyu. He grants Palamedes' Elder master [[Tammuz]] passage for the remaining immortals to his realm, settling an old debt.
Marethyu soon after leads the group through a series of complicated ley gates from [[Xibalba]] to the Isle of Danu Tails before it sank. Marethyu then dismisses the Shadowrealm, no longer needing it, so it crumbles to dust as soon as he leaves. Palamedes is the last to depart and watch the artificial world fade away into nothingness.
Mentioned briefly in a letter to [[Sophie Newman]] by description of the fauna alone.[4]

### Trivia
- [[Marethyu]] probably modelled the night sky off the Milky Way Galaxy because his former 'parents' [[Osiris|Richard Newman]] and [[Isis|Sara Newman]] had painted the Milky Way night sky on the ceiling of his sister, [[Sophie Newman]]'s room at their old house. This probably brought back good old memories of the time when things were simpler.
