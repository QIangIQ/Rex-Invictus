**[[Alaska]]** is the northernmost U.S. state. In Riordan canon it is the "land beyond the gods", where the Olympians' power fails. In [[Rex Invictus]] it is where the first [[Répertoire du Savoir Athénien|AtRep]] was built and where the [[Battle of Alaska Hill]] was fought.

---

## IRL

- Purchased by the United States from the Russian Empire in 1867 and admitted as the 49th state in 1959.
- The Brooks Range crosses the north of the state above the Arctic Circle. The North Slope oil fields lie beyond it.
- The U.S. Army's [[Mortals of the Present Day|11th Airborne]] Division was reactivated in Alaska in 2022 as an Arctic-focused division.

---

## PJO Canon

- In *The Son of Neptune*, [[Percy Jackson|Percy]], Hazel and [[Frank Zhang|Frank]] go to Alaska, "beyond the reach of the gods", to free [[Thanatos]] and recover the lost eagle of the Fifth Cohort from the giant Alcyoneus. They recover Legion arms of [[Imperial Gold]] from the Hubbard Glacier.

---

## Rex Invictus (Present Day)

- [[Ekdromoi Cell Kappa-Seven]] crosses the [[Labyrinth]] and emerges onto the Alaskan tundra to reconnoiter it ([[Ch20 - Rex Invictus XIII|Ch20]] and [[Ch21 - Rex Invictus XIV|Ch21]]). Imperial records place the construction of the first [[Répertoire du Savoir Athénien|AtRep]] there.
- The Mars Watchpoint reports Jade Court cultivators and a company of the mortal [[Mortals of the Present Day|11th Airborne]] ([[Mortals of the Present Day]]) converging on [[Ekdromoi|Kappa-Seven]]'s cloaked dropship in the Brooks Range.

---

## UOE (Far Future)

- The [[Battle of Alaska Hill]] (mid-2030s, at the Alaska Hill Strategic Complex) is where [[Percy Jackson]] and [[Annabeth Chase]] defended the [[Parlamentum mk.I]] construction node, the early construct later known as [[Minerva (AI)|Minerva]].
