### Olympus
Mount Olympus, commonly shortened to Olympus, was the principal celestial dimension, capital and seat of government of the [[Greek Pantheon]]. It served as the residence of [[Zeus]] and the Olympian Gods and represented the positive or heavenly side of the Greek Divine system, existing separately from the [[Greek Underworld]].
For most of its history, Olympus was governed by Zeus as God-King and supported one of Earth's most powerful Divine civilizations. The dimension contained the Gods, Divine resources and institutional power accumulated throughout successive generations of the Greek Pantheon.
Olympus was eventually devastated during the final war between Zeus' Pantheon and [[Victor Elderblood]]. Victor defeated its ruling Gods, forcibly sealed the dimension and later used his authority as its new King to declare that Olympus would cease to exist. Its surviving dimensional foundation was repaired, expanded and transformed into the basis of Victor's new [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]].[1][2][3]

### Description
Mount Olympus was a true supernatural Divine realm rather than the physical mountain known to Mortals.
It possessed an environment described as a heavenly world with normally sunny conditions. During [[Typhon]]'s awakening, his roar echoed throughout Mount Olympus, the earth shook and the previously bright heavenly environment grew dark as the realm reacted to the appearance of a Being associated with The End.[4]
Olympus was sufficiently vast to support the large population and institutions of the [[Greek Pantheon]] and possessed its own dimensional defenses separating it from both the [[Greek Underworld]] and external space.[5]

### Dimensional Structure
Olympus and the [[Greek Underworld]] were separate supernatural territories.
This distinction became explicit during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s final battle against the Greek Gods. One of Victor's attacks tore through the spatial separation between the Underworld and Mount Olympus before continuing through the boundary separating Olympus from the outer dimension.[6]
The resulting damage allowed the surface of Olympus and the space outside the dimension to be seen through the rupture. The attack was described as having effectively pierced the Pantheon itself in half.[7]

### Government
Olympus was ruled principally by [[Zeus]], God-King of the [[Greek Pantheon]].
The Olympian Gods operated beneath his leadership, although Greek Divine politics were notoriously unstable. Earlier generations—including [[Ouranos]], [[Gaia]], Kronus and the [[Titans]]—formed the ancient foundations from which the later Olympian order developed.
The Twelve Olympians and numerous other Gods lived or maintained authority within Mount Olympus, including [[Hera]], [[Athena]], [[Apollo]], [[Artemis]], [[Ares]], [[Hermes]], [[Hephaestus]], [[Dionysus]], [[Demeter]], [[Hestia]] and others.
Olympus therefore functioned simultaneously as:
- A Divine residence.
- The capital of the Greek Pantheon.
- Zeus' political territory.
- A source of Pantheon power.
- The center of Greek celestial society.

### Pantheon Authority
Gods fighting within their own Pantheon could draw upon the surrounding Divine structure as an additional source of authority and power.
During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s invasion, the Greek Gods attempted to exploit their home-field advantage while [[Zeus]] used the accumulated authority and artifacts of Olympus against him.[8]
The influence of Mount Olympus could also be imposed upon other Greek dimensions under extraordinary circumstances. During the confrontation involving the [[Greek Underworld]], Zeus temporarily forced his authority into the Underworld through the power of [[Poseidon|the Master]] Bolt, before [[Persephone]] broke the intrusion.[9]

### Resources

#### Ambrosia
Ambrosia was one of the important Divine resources historically cultivated on Mount Olympus.
The fruit could greatly increase Mortal longevity and possessed even greater effects when empowered through [[Divine Energy]]. Vampires could also consume Ambrosia to suppress their dependence upon Blood for extended periods.[10]
The existence of Ambrosia made Olympus more than a political center; it was also a source of valuable Divine resources coveted by supernatural factions outside the Pantheon.

### History

#### Greek Divine Civilization
Mount Olympus served as the center of Greek Divine society for an enormous period of history.
Successive generations of Gods occupied the Pantheon, beginning with ancient [[Primordial Gods]] and [[Titans]] before the Olympian generation rose to power under [[Zeus]].
The realm eventually became one of Earth's dominant heavenly territories.

#### Typhon's Awakening
During the Demonic War, [[Typhon]] awakened and transformed an already dangerous internal conflict into an existential crisis.
His roar reverberated throughout Mount Olympus strongly enough to shake the realm and darken its normally sunny heavenly environment.[11]
[[Aphrodite]] concluded that the situation had ceased to be another Divine civil war and had instead become an extinction event for Olympus. She attempted to convince [[Demeter]], [[Hestia]], Nike, [[Tyche]] and other Goddesses to abandon the Pantheon before they were destroyed.[12]

#### Victor's Invasion
Olympus later became the principal battlefield of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s war against [[Zeus]].
Victor confronted the combined forces of the [[Greek Pantheon]], including Olympians, [[Titans]], [[Primordial Gods]], Hecatonchires and other Divine entities.[13][14][15][16]
The fighting caused catastrophic damage to the realm. Zeus' resistance and Victor's increasingly destructive attacks destabilized the Pantheon's structure, while the destruction of powerful Divine Artifacts deprived Olympus of several of its greatest advantages.
In Chapter 801, Victor released an attack powerful enough to tear through the dimensional boundaries separating the [[Greek Underworld]], Mount Olympus and the exterior of the dimension.[17]

#### Fall of Olympus
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] completed his transformation into the Draconic Vampire Primogenitor, he took control of the surviving battlefield.
He surrounded Olympus with an enormous magical formation composed of vast numbers of [[Magic]] circles and forcibly closed the dimension, preventing the remaining Gods from freely escaping or drawing upon external advantages.[18]
Victor then declared that the old Greek order would end.

#### Transformation into a New Pantheon
After becoming the King of the conquered dimension, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] formally addressed the [[Akashic Records]] and declared:
"Olympus shall cease to exist, and a new pantheon shall rise from the ashes, a pantheon led by me."[19]
The damaged dimension was analyzed by the cosmic system. Victor and his allies restored the territory and greatly expanded its structure so that it could accommodate the planet being incorporated into his new Divine civilization.[20]
For a short period the System designated the replacement dimension Pantheon 7475909777 before it developed into the [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]].[21]
Mount Olympus therefore ceased to exist as the political and dimensional center of the old [[Greek Pantheon]], though much of its former population, resources and Divine legacy survived through Victor's faction.

### Legacy
Although the old Mount Olympus was abolished, its legacy remained central to [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]].
Numerous former Greek Goddesses entered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction, while surviving Gods were enslaved, recruited or otherwise incorporated into the new order.
Much later, Victor gave the Greek members of his family an enormous planet where they could preserve Greek Divine architecture, temples, culture and traditions without reconstructing the political system that had existed under [[Zeus]].[22]
Thus the destruction of Olympus ended the independent [[Greek Pantheon]] but did not eliminate Greek Divine culture from the [[Elderblood Empire]].

### Notes
- Mount Olympus is a supernatural Divine dimension and should not be confused with the ordinary geographical mountain in Greece.
- Olympus and the [[Greek Underworld]] are separate dimensions.[23]
- "Mount Olympus", "Olympus" and the "[[Greek Pantheon]]" are sometimes used interchangeably in dialogue, but technically the Pantheon includes the Gods, political faction and related Divine territories rather than only Olympus.
- The old Olympus ceased to exist as an independent Pantheon after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s declaration in Chapter 821.[24]
- Greek culture itself survived within Victor's faction and was later granted dedicated territory inside the [[Elderblood Empire]].[25]
