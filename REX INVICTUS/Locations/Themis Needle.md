The **[[Themis Needle]]** is a spire above [[New Rome]] on [[Voss Secundus]], probably named for [[Solon Themis]], the first [[Justicar Themis|Justicar]] of the [[Voss System]]. In [[Ch23 - Interlude I|Ch23]] [[Thalia Grace]] is left sitting in the rain at its top after [[Minerva (AI)|Minerva]] escapes, telling her contact that the Director ([[Artemis]]) is "already on her way".
