Var-Kar-VI, commonly called Var, was an Elder God of [[Nightingale]], the son of [[Ikor-Kar-VI]] and one of the strongest combatants Ikor selected for his confrontation with Victor Elderblood. His defining Divinity was Extreme Adaptation, an ability that allowed him to permanently adapt to methods that successfully killed him. Combined with the supernatural immortality continuously maintained by his father, Var could repeatedly die, resurrect and progressively eliminate the effectiveness of the methods used against him.[1][2]
Var fought beside Ikor, [[Lucifer]] Morningstar and a Type 6 [[Destroyer]] at the [[Edge of the Cosmos]], where their abilities were deliberately combined to counter Victor. The prolonged battle repeatedly triggered Var's Adaptation and forced his body to evolve into an increasingly abnormal form.[2] When Victor eventually ascended to Godhood and manifested an existence neither Var nor Ikor could comprehend, fear caused father and son to turn against each other. Ikor overpowered Var and consumed him through Predation, acquiring Extreme Adaptation for himself.[3]

### Physical Appearance
Var initially possessed the enormous body characteristic of the surviving Elder Gods. His body was colossal and humanoid, with dark gray-black skin that appeared unusually sharp and hardened, resembling naturally grown armor. His bright blue eyes were the primary visible feature he shared with his father, [[Ikor-Kar-VI]].[1]
Unlike Ikor, Var considered his enormous body inefficient when confronting an opponent as fast and skilled as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. After Victor killed him through hundreds of thousands of rapid cuts, Var deliberately rejected Ikor's warning and condensed his gigantic body at tremendous speed until he stood approximately two metres tall.[1]
His smaller form retained the same dark armored skin but was substantially more suitable for close combat. His body also became progressively harder as his adaptation advanced. After Victor initially cut through him with little difficulty, repeating the same attack shortly afterward failed to penetrate his body at all.[1]
During the extended battle at the [[Edge of the Cosmos]], Var's appearance became increasingly abnormal. After Ikor consumed both the Type 6 [[Destroyer]] and [[Lucifer]], cracks began spreading across Var's black skin. As his Extreme Adaptation continued intensifying, his body developed into what the narration described as an increasingly bizarre appearance, physically reflecting the continuous evolution caused by his Divinity.[2][3]

### Personality
Var was arrogant, aggressive and highly confident in the effectiveness of his abilities. Upon directly confronting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], he immediately criticized the Dragon Progenitor for attempting to fight both him and Ikor simultaneously and dismissed the decision as foolish.[1]
Despite respecting his father's authority, Var was not blindly obedient. When Ikor ordered him not to discard his gigantic body, Var refused, correctly determining that the enormous form would only hinder him against Victor. He consequently condensed himself into a smaller combat form despite Ikor's objections.[1]
Var nevertheless remained an effective partner to his father throughout their battle. He repeatedly intercepted Victor whenever he attempted to reach Ikor, warned his father about dangerous attacks and continued fighting even after being killed numerous times. His Adaptation encouraged an extremely aggressive fighting style because successful deaths could directly eliminate weaknesses that an opponent had already exploited.[1][2]
He was also aware of how dangerous Victor's continued improvement had become. During the prolonged confrontation at the [[Edge of the Cosmos]], Var was among the first to openly recognize that Victor was not merely enduring their attacks but continuously refining his techniques, urgently warning Ikor that they needed to eliminate him before his development progressed further.[2]
Despite his confidence, Var was not immune to fear. When Victor's newly awakened Blood Divinity exposed him and Ikor to an incomprehensible aspect of Victor's deeper existence, Var's Soul was overwhelmed by madness and terror. After Victor completed his transformation into a [[Chaos]] Dragon God, both Elder Gods instinctively recognized him as something they could no longer properly comprehend.[3]
That terror ultimately destroyed whatever loyalty existed between Var and his father. Var and Ikor instinctively attacked one another, but Ikor possessed greater experience and strength. Var was restrained almost immediately and devoured before he could properly resist.[3]

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Var first appeared beside [[Ikor-Kar-VI]] during Ikor's direct confrontation with [[Victor Elderblood]]. When Victor attacked the two Elder Gods simultaneously, Var blocked Victor's first strike with a shield of pure Darkness before revealing that Ikor was his father. He mocked Victor for attempting to fight them both at once, believing that Victor's confidence had crossed into arrogance.[1]
Victor immediately tested Var's capabilities by attacking him with [[Junketsu]] at extreme speed. Hundreds of thousands of cuts appeared across Var's enormous face, apparently killing him, but the wounds rapidly disappeared and Var returned. Recognizing that his gigantic body was unsuitable against Victor's speed and [[Martial Arts]], Var ignored Ikor's objections and began compressing himself. Victor continued attacking throughout the transformation and discovered that Var's flesh was already becoming progressively more difficult to damage.[1]
Within approximately three seconds, Var had compressed his colossal body to roughly two metres. He revealed the nature of his power shortly afterward. Victor attempted to cut him with the same method that had previously killed him, only for Junketsu to fail to penetrate his body. Var declared that the same attack could no longer kill him. Victor then destroyed his head with a physical blow, but Var resurrected again through the immortality supplied by Ikor.[1]
Victor correctly identified their complementary abilities: Var possessed Adaptation while Ikor possessed [[Immortality]]. Var could therefore experience a lethal attack, resurrect through Ikor's protection and return having adapted against the method responsible for his previous death.[1]
The confrontation escalated when Ikor activated Akashic Creation and transported Victor, Var, [[Lucifer]] and the Type 6 [[Destroyer]] to the [[Edge of the Cosmos]]. This location existed at the frontier of expanding Creation, where ordinary Space and Time had not yet completely established themselves. Victor's greatest speed and Creation-related advantages were consequently restricted, allowing Ikor's four-man formation to fight him under deliberately unfavorable conditions.[1]
The resulting battle continued for an unknowable amount of subjective Time. Victor could not determine whether they had fought for a century, a thousand years or a hundred thousand years because conventional temporal perception had become meaningless. Throughout that period Var repeatedly confronted Victor and continued developing through Extreme Adaptation.[2]
Victor eventually understood exactly how dangerous the ability was. Anything that successfully killed Var once could no longer kill him in the same manner. Victor therefore became reluctant to repeatedly kill him until he understood the source of the Elder God's resurrection. That source was eventually identified as Ikor, who was continuously using his own reserves of Positive Energy to maintain the immortality of Var and the other combatants.[2]
This discovery changed Victor's strategy. Rather than wasting attacks against enemies who would continually return and adapt, he repeatedly attempted to bypass them and kill Ikor. Var consequently became one of his father's principal defensive pieces, intercepting Victor whenever he attempted to reach the source maintaining their immortality.[2]
The battle steadily turned against the Elder Gods as Victor's Martial Arts became more efficient. Although Ikor had deliberately assembled abilities intended to complement one another—Lucifer's Darkness, the Type 6 Destroyer's probability-related power, Var's Adaptation and Ikor's support—Victor eventually began pressuring the entire formation through Martial Arts alone.[2]
Ikor responded by activating Predation. He first devoured the Type 6 Destroyer, restoring some of his Positive Energy and assimilating its strength, before consuming Lucifer and manifesting the Fallen Angel's wings. The original four-against-one formation consequently collapsed into a two-against-one battle between Ikor and Var against Victor.[2]
Ikor warned Var not to disappoint him. Var reacted with visible disgust while cracks spread across his black skin, and his Extreme Adaptation intensified further. His body became increasingly abnormal as the accumulated evolutionary changes produced by his Divinity continued developing.[2]
Var and Ikor continued attacking Victor together as Victor finally reached the metaphysical barrier preventing his ascension. Var struck Victor directly while Ikor attacked from range, and both attempted to kill him before the transformation could finish. Their efforts failed as Victor began successively awakening his Divine Concepts.[3]
The awakening of Victor's Blood Divinity fundamentally changed the confrontation. Ikor and Var were engulfed by Victor's manifestation and exposed to an aspect of his existence that neither Elder God could properly process. Even as Gods, they could not understand what they were witnessing; merely perceiving it caused their Souls to shake under overwhelming madness.[3]
Victor subsequently completed his establishment as a [[Chaos]] Dragon God. Faced with an existence that their instincts identified as incomprehensibly dangerous, both Elder Gods became visibly terrified. Whether due to fear or instinct, Var and Ikor immediately turned against one another.[3]
Ikor was the stronger and more experienced of the two. He restrained Var before his son could properly defend himself and immediately activated Predation. Var was completely consumed, while Ikor displayed no hesitation or remorse. His only concern was survival.[3]
Through consuming Var, Ikor acquired Extreme Adaptation. The three abilities of Adaptation, Predation and Immortality were subsequently described together as an exceptionally dangerous combination, although even this collection of powers was insufficient to overcome Victor after his ascension.[3]

### Skills & Abilities
Var was one of the Elder Gods personally selected by Ikor to fight [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] at the [[Edge of the Cosmos]]. His combat style revolved around combining the immortality supplied by his father with his own Extreme Adaptation, allowing him to transform lethal failure into permanent defensive improvement.[1][2]

#### Elder God Physiology
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics: As an Elder God, Var possessed immense physical strength, speed, durability and stamina. After compressing his body, he could engage [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] directly at close range, intercept his movements and physically strike him during the battle at the [[Edge of the Cosmos]].[1][3]
- Divine Power: Var possessed the supernatural Power and Soul of a God and could channel this Energy through his body and abilities.[1]
- Enhanced Durability: Even independently of resurrection, Var's body became extremely resistant to physical damage as Extreme Adaptation modified it. A blade that had previously cut through his body eventually became incapable of penetrating his skin.[1]
- Regeneration: Var could rapidly recover from catastrophic physical damage. Hundreds of thousands of cuts covering his body disappeared shortly after being inflicted.[1]
- [[Immortality]] (Granted by Ikor): Var possessed the supernatural immortality distributed by [[Ikor-Kar-VI]]. The ability allowed him to return after otherwise fatal damage so long as Ikor continued maintaining the necessary Energy supply.[1][2]
- Resurrection: Var resurrected after being killed repeatedly by Victor, including after having his head completely destroyed.[1]
- Soul Immortality (Granted): The immortality of the Elder Gods fundamentally operated through their Souls. Earlier information concerning the Elder Gods established their immortal Soul as the defining basis of their repeated return from death, while the later battle confirmed Ikor as the source maintaining this effect.[4][2]
- Immortality Dependence: This immortality was not an independent ability belonging to Var. Ikor was required to continuously expend Positive Energy to maintain it; if Ikor's reserves or support disappeared, the protection would eventually fail.[2]

#### Divinity
- Extreme Adaptation: Var's principal Divinity and defining ability. Whenever an attack or method successfully killed him, his existence adapted against the cause of death after his resurrection. A method capable of killing Var once therefore became progressively less effective or completely ineffective when repeated.[1][2]
- Reactive Adaptation: Var's adaptations occurred as responses to actual lethal damage. After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] killed him through enormous numbers of sword cuts, Var's body immediately began becoming harder to cut.[1]
- Resistance Development: Damage types and attack methods that previously worked against Var could lose effectiveness after he adapted to them. Victor's blade initially cut through him, but shortly afterward could no longer penetrate the adapted body.[1]
- Immunity Development: Chapter 883 describes the extreme form of Var's adaptation directly: once a specific method successfully killed him, the same method could no longer kill him again. This forced Victor to vary how he fought rather than repeatedly exploit one successful method.[2]
- Adaptive Body Modification: Extreme Adaptation physically altered Var's body. His skin hardened after repeated deaths, while prolonged use eventually produced cracks across his black exterior and transformed him into an increasingly bizarre form as the ability intensified.[1][2][3]
- Adaptive Evolution: Var's adaptation was not limited to superficial healing. The repeated changes modified his actual defensive properties, progressively reconstructing his body around previously lethal experiences.[1]

#### Other Abilities
- Size Manipulation: Var could radically alter the size of his body. He compressed his original colossal Elder God form into a roughly two-metre humanoid body within approximately three seconds because the smaller form was more efficient for fighting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[1]
- Darkness Manipulation: Var generated and controlled pure Darkness. Upon his introduction, he created a shield composed entirely of Darkness to stop Victor's attack against himself and Ikor.[1]
- Barrier Creation: Var could shape his Darkness into defensive barriers capable of intercepting incoming attacks.[1]
- Energy Manipulation: Dark supernatural Energy erupted from Var's body during the activation of his transformation and adaptation, demonstrating direct control over his [[Divine Energy]].[1]
- Power Combination: Var's abilities became considerably more dangerous when combined with Ikor's [[Immortality]]. Resurrection allowed Var to repeatedly trigger lethal adaptations without permanently dying, effectively converting Ikor's Energy reserves into additional opportunities for Var to evolve.[1][2]
- Combat Coordination: Var fought in coordination with Ikor, [[Lucifer]] and the Type 6 [[Destroyer]] for an immeasurably long period at the [[Edge of the Cosmos]]. He repeatedly intercepted Victor when the latter attempted to reach Ikor and warned his father when Victor prepared dangerous attacks.[2]

### Weaknesses
- Adaptation Requires Exposure: Var did not automatically begin immune to unfamiliar attacks. A method generally had to successfully affect or kill him before the corresponding adaptation developed.[1][2]
- Adaptation Depends on Survival Through [[Immortality]]: Var's most dangerous combat cycle depended upon Ikor resurrecting him after lethal attacks. Without Ikor's immortality support, deliberately experiencing fatal attacks would no longer provide a safe method of triggering further adaptation.[2]
- Finite Energy Source: Ikor's ability to sustain Var's immortality depended upon his finite reserves of Positive Energy while separated from [[Nightingale]]. A sufficiently prolonged battle could therefore indirectly exhaust the mechanism allowing Var to continuously resurrect and adapt.[2]
- Overwhelming Existential Difference: Adaptation did not make Var immediately capable of opposing any stronger existence. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ascension produced such a drastic difference in nature and power that both Var and Ikor instinctively recognized the resulting Being as incomprehensible and terrifying before Var could meaningfully adapt to it.[3]
- Mental and Soul-Based Overload: Var was unable to process Victor's deeper cosmic manifestation. Attempting to perceive it caused overwhelming madness and made his Soul shake violently.[3]
