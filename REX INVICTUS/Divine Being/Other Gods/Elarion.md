### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 1196
This man will tear the balance of power off its hinges.
—Elarion, watching the dragon leave, Chapter 1197
Elarion is the God of Goodness and the envoy of Light. Light's faction is the [[Veillon Empire]], the Empire of Light. He comes for War after [[Vaelthar]] has already restored [[Kaelith]], [[Nerath]], and [[Vhalor]], and after Justice is already on the field. Golden armor is the body he arrives in. He is not one of the Chief Gods, and he is not one of the [[Seven Black Thrones]]. His patron wants War. War refuses.[1][2]
He does not join the attack that follows. He is still standing when Vaelthar is under a foot. [[Victor Elderblood]] does not take him with the prisoners. He tells him to carry a message to his leader: Victor will visit soon, because that leader has something that belongs to him. Elarion frowns, nods, and obeys. His own judgment, once the dragon lets the galaxy go, is that this man will tear the balance of power off its hinges.[3]

### Physical Appearance
Golden armor. Justice, who arrives in front of him, is faceless, suited, and sharp-toothed. Elarion wears gold, and nothing in the entrance gives him that face, or [[Vaelthar]]'s ivory plates, four arms, or four eyes.[2]
At the end of the attack he is still in that armor, standing with Justice. Both of them are in a cold sweat. He frowns when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] addresses him, then nods like a soldier. He does not move while the projection leaves with War and the fallen Gods.[3]

### Personality
He speaks for Light first. The demand is his patron's: Light wants War. He does not greet [[Vaelthar]], he does not rebuke the Chief Gods, and he does not answer War's refusal with a second claim.[2]
The fight does not pull him in. He stays where he is while Black Eclipse spends itself on a projection. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] speaks to him, the frown comes first. The nod comes after. He knows he has no say in it. In the Upper Sectors only strength counts, and Victor has already proved he has more than enough of it. The answer he gives is "Understood." Victor calls him a dog. He takes the message. He does not run. Justice wants to be gone. Elarion watches the departure without moving, and the thought he keeps is that Victor will tear the balance of power off its hinges.[3]

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Light already rules one of the great factions of the Upper Sectors. The civilization is the Empire of Light. A clone of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] meets it in its own territory. The empire presents itself as righteous and classes whatever its Light rejects as evil. Soldiers, Light-built defenses, the frontline commander [[Vik-Ir-loah]], and [[Solus 969]] fail to beat the clone by ordinary means. The empire then erases the Solar System the clone is standing in. The God of Goodness is not in that army. He comes later, for the same patron.[4][1]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
The same Light is named, with Darkness, Emperor, and Law, among the rulers of the higher cosmos. The beings who took [[Niklaus Horseman]]|Niklaus are Darkness. The soldiers the clone fought are Light. Elarion is the envoy who later stands on a battlefield for that patron.[1]
By the time he arrives, the duel inside [[Gol-goroth]]'s domain is already over. [[Azrakar]] is erased. War, Famine, Death, and Pestilence have driven [[Kaelith]], [[Nerath]], and [[Vhalor]] to the point of execution. [[Vaelthar]], Throne of Origin, has come through with the other Chief Gods. War has shut the domain down. Vaelthar has put the three back with a beam of pure darkness and told them they disappointed the Great Darkness. They have bowed.[5][2]
Justice arrives first. Faceless, suited, sharp-toothed, a presence at the level of a Throne, from the Emperor's faction. Elarion follows. God of Goodness, golden armor, from Light. Each says that his own patron wants War. He is not one of the ten who closed around the wounded three. Those ten were nine galactic-level threats and Vaelthar. His entrance is Light's errand.[2]
The claim on the Herald has three sides. Light, through Elarion. The Emperor's faction, through Justice. Darkness, already on the field under Vaelthar. A divinity of <Beginning> flares while the two envoys make their claims. It is Vaelthar's. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] sees it, kin to his own Beginning, and corrupted. It is not Elarion's power. War refuses to be portioned out. He names himself the incarnation of War and the Dragon Emperor's herald, and he says he will kill anyone in his way. Victor answers, "Well said, my herald," and appears as a giant dragon holding the whole galaxy. Light, Darkness, and the Emperor-aligned patron turn toward him.[2]
Victor praises War. War sees that his father has fully recovered. Victor calls the Gods a disappointment and kills the restored Kaelith by the Soul. The body is erased. He holds the Soul. Only the patrons perceive the method. Light is one of those who turn. Elarion is not the hand that strikes her. Victor then names Vharzath and declares extinction. A God reports that their planets are under attack from every side. Vaelthar orders Black Eclipse in anyway. Elarion is not given that order.[2]
The attack spends itself on a projection. The true body is the dragon, still holding the galaxy. Vaelthar's restraints, his blow, and his domain prison fail. He is left under a foot, the sword at his head. The sword does not come down. Justice and Elarion are still standing, the two enforcers of the other factions, both in a cold sweat. Victor asks Justice whether these famous factions are children with too much power or simply weak. Justice says this faction is among the weakest and the most isolated. Victor says that soon it will no longer exist. Vharzath does not come out.[3]
The prisoners are Black Eclipse. Elarion is not put with them. Victor tells him to inform his leader that he will pay a visit soon. That leader has something that belongs to him. Elarion frowns, then nods like an obedient soldier. He knows he has no say. "...Understood." Victor smiles, calls him a dog, and the projection vanishes with War and the fallen Gods. The dragon lets the galaxy go and drifts between the galaxies. Elarion watches without moving. This man, he judges, will tear the balance of power off its hinges.[3]

### Skills & Abilities
He does not strike, heal, or open a domain. What he does is speak for Light, stand through a battle that is not his, and carry a message back.

#### God of Goodness
- The Title: He is the God of Goodness. That is the office he stands in. He does not spend it as a blessing, a judgment, or an attack.[2]
- Golden Armor: The armor is the body he arrives in. He does not turn it into a weapon or a barrier.[2]

#### Light's Envoy
- The Claim: He says Light wants War. Justice makes the same kind of demand for the Emperor-aligned patron. The two do not share a master. War refuses both, and refuses Black Eclipse with them.[2]
- Not the Reinforcement: He is outside the ten who closed on the wounded three, and outside the order [[Vaelthar]] gives to attack. He is still on his feet when that attack has failed.[2][3]
- The Message: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] charges him to tell his leader that a visit is coming, because that leader has something of Victor's. Elarion understands the gap in strength and accepts it. The projection's last word to him is "dog."[3]
- The Judgment: Once they are gone, his conclusion is that Victor will tear the balance of power off its hinges. He does not say it aloud.[3]

### Trivia
- He is the God of Goodness. That is the only title he carries.[2]
- He is from Light's faction. That faction is the [[Veillon Empire]], the Empire of Light. The clone war against that empire is already over when he comes for War.[4][1][2]
- He arrives after Justice. Justice is the Emperor's faction and has the Throne-level presence. Elarion is Light and has the golden armor.[2]
- He is not part of [[Vaelthar]]'s reinforcement. The ten who stand with the wounded three are counted before he arrives.[2]
- The <Beginning> that flares while he and Justice claim War is Vaelthar's. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] sees it, and he sees that it is corrupted.[2]
- When the dragon appears, Light, Darkness, and the Emperor-aligned patron all turn. Elarion is Light's envoy on that field.[2]
- War refuses to be given to him. Victor's answer to that refusal is "Well said, my herald."[2]
- He does not join the attack. He and Justice are still standing at the end, in a cold sweat. Justice is the one Victor asks about the faction. Elarion is the one sent home with a message.[3]
- His answer is "Understood." Victor calls him a dog. The thought he leaves with is that Victor will tear the balance of power off its hinges.[3]
- He is alive. [[Kaelith]] is the one Victor kills. The capture order is for Black Eclipse. Elarion is not in it.[2][3]
