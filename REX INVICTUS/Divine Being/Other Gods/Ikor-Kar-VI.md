Welcome to the threshold of the cosmos, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] Alucard. In this realm where everything exists, and nothing exists at the same time, your greatest capabilities related to manipulating creation, your irritating speed are sealed.
—Ikor to Victor, Chapter 879
Ikor-Kar-VI, commonly called Ikor, was the God-King and leader of [[Nightingale]]'s [[Elder Gods Pantheon]]. He was the source of the supernatural immortality possessed by the Elder Gods and wielded the Divinities of [[Immortality]] and Predation. His control over Immortality extended beyond himself, allowing him to maintain the immortal condition of other Elder Gods and chosen subordinates so long as he continued supplying the necessary power.[2][3]
Ikor was one of the principal architects of the civilization existing within Nightingale's Wildlands. While presenting himself as a defender of the planet's natives against the Noble Vampires, he spent thousands of years exploiting the conflict for the development of the Elder Gods. Native inhabitants were subjected to experimentation, unusual abilities that emerged among them were harvested through Predation, weaker Elder Gods were consumed to strengthen the survivors, and the colossal bodies used by the Elder Gods were constructed from the remains of Gods and Nightingale's native population.[4]
His experiments were also fundamental to the creation of Nightingale's Monsters. Victor later learned through Ikor's memories that the Monster Race had been created through a combination of Ikor's Divinity, Nightingale's natives and Noble Vampires.[4]
Ikor directly entered the war against Victor Elderblood during the final conflict for Nightingale. After determining that Victor's speed, control over Creation and connection to [[Roxanne Elderblood]] gave him too many advantages in normal Reality, Ikor used Akashic Creation to transport the combatants to the [[Edge of the Cosmos]], where conventional Space and Time had not completely established themselves.[5] Although this temporarily restricted several of Victor's powers, the prolonged battle instead allowed Victor to perfect his [[Martial Arts]] and overcome the barrier preventing his ascension to Godhood.[3][6]
After Victor became the [[Chaos]] Dragon God, Ikor sacrificed even his own son [[Var-Kar-VI]], devouring him to acquire his Adaptation. The combination of Immortality, Predation and Adaptation still proved insufficient against Victor's new existence. Ikor attempted to retreat to Nightingale but was ultimately trapped and completely devoured by Victor.[6]

### Physical Appearance
Ikor's most recognizable form was an enormous humanoid body exceeding 1,000 metres in height. The construct possessed white skin covered by thick black markings, luminous blue irises surrounded by pitch-black sclera, crown-shaped horns and a smooth lower face without a visible mouth.[7]
His gigantic appearance was not his natural body. The exterior was a biological construct composed from the accumulated corpses of deceased Gods and [[Nightingale]]'s native population. This explained why [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s enhanced perception initially failed to recognize the enormous body as an ordinary living organism. Rather than sensing a conventional Divine Being, Victor perceived what resembled a gigantic mass of flesh connected through a supernatural thread to something beyond the immediate body.[7][4]
When Victor cut the construct completely open, the exterior body was revealed to be effectively a walking corpse containing a smaller version of Ikor within its abdomen. Destroying the external shell therefore did not necessarily kill the God inhabiting it.[5]
Ikor's appearance could change through Predation. After consuming the Type 6 [[Destroyer]] accompanying [[Lucifer]], his arms became considerably larger and more muscular as the creature's physical strength was assimilated. After devouring Lucifer Morningstar, Ikor manifested a pair of large angelic wings. His body continued acquiring increasingly abnormal characteristics as he absorbed additional beings during the battle.[3]

### Personality
Ikor was intelligent, calculating, pragmatic and extraordinarily ruthless. He considered the Noble Vampires foreign invaders who had infected [[Nightingale]] and openly described the coming war as an opportunity to cleanse the planet of the "cancer" that had occupied it for millennia.[7] His hostility toward the Vampires was genuine, but his claimed concern for Nightingale's native population contradicted his own actions. The conflict had provided him with enemies to study, biological material for experimentation and a permanent external threat that helped maintain unity among the natives and Elder Gods.[8]
Ikor approached living beings primarily according to their usefulness. Native inhabitants who developed rare powers could become experimental material or targets for Predation, while weaker Elder Gods were consumed so their capabilities could strengthen more valuable members of the Pantheon. This process was eventually responsible for the extremely small number of Elder Gods remaining by the final war.[4]
His treatment of allies followed the same principle. Ikor recruited [[Lucifer]] Morningstar and supplied him with several of the strongest Monsters available, yet had no hesitation in later consuming Lucifer when the Fallen Angel became more valuable as a source of power than as an independent combatant.[8][3] He ultimately treated his own son identically, devouring [[Var-Kar-VI]] immediately after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ascension when Adaptation appeared to offer him a greater chance of survival.[6]
Despite his ruthlessness, Ikor was not reckless. He analyzed Victor's known advantages, prepared a battlefield specifically designed to restrict them and assembled abilities that complemented one another. At the [[Edge of the Cosmos]], Lucifer's Darkness, the Type 6 [[Destroyer]]'s probability-related ability, Var's Extreme Adaptation and Ikor's support were deliberately combined to create a coordinated countermeasure against Victor.[5][3]
Ikor was also capable of recognizing danger and changing strategy accordingly. Once he realized that Victor was continuously becoming more efficient during their battle, he abandoned his support of distant Elder Gods and concentrated his remaining Positive Energy upon the combatants fighting beside him. When even this failed, he began consuming his own forces to replenish himself and acquire their attributes.[3]
Survival ultimately outweighed every other principle Ikor possessed. The appearance of Victor's fully established Divine existence terrified both Ikor and Var to the point that they instinctively turned against one another. Ikor immediately overwhelmed his son and consumed him without hesitation or remorse, and after returning to Nightingale he attempted to escape as soon as he understood that fighting the newly ascended Victor was impossible.[6]

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Ikor's influence was first revealed indirectly through [[Lucifer]] Morningstar. After disappearing from the wider supernatural world, Lucifer returned in a completely different body and revealed that he had become an Elder God. [[Diablo]] correctly deduced that Lucifer's primary reason for changing species was the Elder Gods' immortality, specifically their immortal Souls. Physical death could no longer permanently eliminate Lucifer, and Diablo concluded that only the Being responsible for that immortality—the Leader of the Elder Gods—could truly revoke it.[2]
The encounter established the nature of Ikor's authority before he personally appeared. Lucifer's transformation had not merely given him a durable body; his Soul had been altered into something capable of returning from death repeatedly, giving him considerably greater freedom to cultivate dangerous [[Divine Energy]] and develop Divinity. Diablo nevertheless understood that the supposed freedom Lucifer had acquired came with a hidden dependency: his immortality ultimately existed because the Elder Gods' leader permitted it.[2]
Shortly afterward, a messenger of the Elder Gods approached Diablo. The messenger confirmed that their leader had been observing events outside [[Nightingale]] and was interested in Diablo's rise. The Elder Gods offered assistance while asking that Diablo's future faction remain outside their territory. The messenger reinforced the threat behind the agreement by referring to the possibility of an immortal army, demonstrating the scale upon which Ikor could potentially distribute his Divine protection.[2]

#### King Among Kings
The Elder Gods remained largely isolated within [[Nightingale]]'s Wildlands. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] began dramatically restructuring Nightingale in Chapter 820, the [[Amara Elderblood|World Tree of Positivity]] residing there observed the transformation while several Elder Gods watched Victor from a distance. Their refusal to immediately intervene continued the Pantheon's long-standing preference for isolation and indirect action even while an increasingly dangerous foreign power reshaped their planet.[1]
By the final war, the surviving Elder Gods openly emerged. Only five gigantic Elder Gods were visibly present when they confronted Victor's coalition. Among them, Ikor was immediately distinguishable by his white-and-black body, blue eyes, black sclera, crown-shaped horns and smooth mouthless face. Victor's perception found the Elder God unusually difficult to interpret and detected an enormous supernatural connection extending away from his body.[7]
Ikor introduced himself as Ikor-Kar-VI and confronted Victor over the invasion of Nightingale. He described the Noble Vampires as a cancer that had occupied the planet for millennia and declared that the time had finally come to remove them. With a gesture he summoned hundreds of thousands of Monsters from beneath the land, from the oceans and from within the gigantic Elder Gods themselves, beginning the Pantheon's full participation in the war.[7]
Ikor had already prepared additional forces specifically for Victor. He contacted [[Lucifer]] Morningstar and ordered him to eliminate either the planetary "host" involved in the conflict or the Being loved by [[Chaos]]—Victor. Lucifer was promised what he wanted if either target was eliminated, while killing Victor would grant him Ikor's complete support. Ikor assigned him a Type 6 [[Destroyer]], two Type 5 [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] and a Priest capable of maintaining the immortality blessing granted by the Elder Gods' leader.[8]
Victor attacked Ikor directly and quickly discovered the unusual nature of his powers. Ikor could teleport his gigantic body, deny cause and effect, interfere with Concepts, strengthen surrounding allies and maintain their immortal Souls. His authority was sufficiently unusual that Victor became increasingly interested in discovering what Divinity could produce such a combination.[8]
During the confrontation, Ikor's son [[Var-Kar-VI]] joined him. Var possessed Extreme Adaptation, allowing him to adapt to attacks after being killed by them. Victor consequently faced a combination in which Var could progressively become resistant to methods of death while Ikor continuously restored him through [[Immortality]].[5][3]
Victor attempted increasingly extreme attacks against Ikor, including creating a genuine black hole through his control over Creation. Ikor simply grabbed the singularity and swallowed it, further demonstrating the abnormal nature of his Divinity and absorption abilities. Victor eventually cut Ikor's enormous body completely in half and discovered the smaller true Being residing inside the corpse-construct.[5]
Recognizing that normal combat on Nightingale overwhelmingly favored Victor, Ikor produced a white staff and activated Akashic Creation. The technique transported Ikor, Victor, Var, Lucifer and the Type 6 Destroyer to the [[Edge of the Cosmos]], a region situated at the threshold of expanding Creation where conventional concepts such as Space and Time had not completely established themselves.[5]
Ikor had deliberately selected the location as a countermeasure. Victor's ability to manipulate Creation was restricted, his normally overwhelming speed was drastically reduced and his ordinary access to Negative Energy appeared to be severed. Ikor had not anticipated the depth of Victor's bond with [[Roxanne Elderblood]], however. Because Roxanne was fused directly with Victor's Soul, the two could not be separated merely by transporting him away from Nightingale, allowing Victor to retain a limited connection to her power.[5]
The battle at the Edge developed into an immeasurably prolonged war of attrition. With conventional Time essentially meaningless, Victor eventually became incapable of determining whether they had fought for centuries, millennia or hundreds of thousands of years. Ikor, Var, Lucifer and the Type 6 Destroyer coordinated their abilities continuously, yet Victor became more dangerous rather than more exhausted. His [[Martial Arts]] were refined with every exchange and his body became increasingly efficient at converting movement into combat power.[3]
Ikor eventually realized that his stored Positive Energy represented his greatest limitation. Unlike Victor, who still possessed Roxanne within his Soul, Ikor had carried only a finite reserve of Positive Energy from Nightingale. Every time he restored his subordinates or maintained their immortality, those reserves decreased. He therefore abandoned support for the Elder Gods remaining elsewhere and concentrated everything upon the combatants surrounding Victor.[3]
When this remained insufficient, Ikor finally used Predation. A dark monstrous manifestation emerged from his body and completely consumed the Type 6 Destroyer. Its strength was added to Ikor, his arms became noticeably more powerful and a portion of his Positive Energy was restored. After Victor continued overwhelming them, Ikor repeated the process upon Lucifer. The Fallen Angel was swallowed instantly and two angelic wings appeared behind Ikor as his powers and characteristics were assimilated.[3]
Victor nevertheless continued progressing. As the golden signs of Divinity began appearing around him, Ikor became increasingly desperate and repeatedly ordered Var to kill him before the transformation could complete. Ikor restrained Victor's body and vaporized his head with a concentrated Divine attack, but Victor simply regenerated while his awakening continued.[6]
Victor successively established several major Divine Concepts, while the manifestation connected to his Blood exposed Ikor and Var to an incomprehensible aspect of Victor's existence. Their Souls shook under the madness produced by attempting to perceive it. Victor ultimately completed his metamorphosis into a Chaos Dragon God, an existence whose arrival was recognized by the highest cosmic Beings observing the event.[6]
The moment Victor's new existence was established, Ikor and Var understood instinctively that their previous battle had become meaningless. Fear drove father and son against one another. Ikor, being considerably stronger and more experienced, immediately restrained Var and devoured him through Predation. He displayed no hesitation or remorse; survival was his only concern. Through Var he acquired Extreme Adaptation, creating the combination of Adaptation, Predation and Immortality.[6]
Ikor used his newly acquired strength to launch one final attack. By destabilizing the surrounding existence, his blast appeared directly in front of Victor rather than crossing the intervening distance. The resulting impact shattered Space and sent Victor through hundreds of universes before Ikor created a route returning both of them to Nightingale.[6]
Returning to the planet restored Ikor's connection to Positive Energy, but it also restored the abilities that the Edge of the Cosmos had suppressed in Victor. Victor revealed that he had deliberately allowed Ikor's attack to return him because the Elder God had already accomplished everything Victor needed from him: the impossible battle had allowed Victor to overcome his progression barrier, refine his Martial Arts and reach Godhood.[6]
Ikor immediately recognized that even Immortality combined with Adaptation no longer gave him a realistic chance of victory and attempted to escape. Victor sealed the surrounding Space, preventing him from leaving Nightingale. He thanked Ikor for the role he had played in his ascension and declared that the Elder God's reward would be becoming his food. When Ikor attempted one final resistance, Victor appeared before him, manifested the abyssal aspect of his existence filled with countless eyes, mouths and Eldritch forms, and swallowed Ikor's entire existence.[6]
Ikor's death exposed the full truth of the Elder Gods. Because Victor had consumed him completely, he inherited access to information contained within Ikor and discovered the extent of his experiments. Only approximately 1,700 Nightingale natives remained hidden from the war. Ikor had subjected their population to systematic experimentation, consumed individuals who developed rare powers and performed the same process upon other Elder Gods, explaining the Pantheon's greatly reduced numbers.[4]
Victor also revealed that the gigantic Elder God bodies were constructed from the corpses of Gods and natives. More significantly, Nightingale's Monster Race itself was the product of Ikor's work: the creatures had been created through experimentation combining Ikor's Divinity, Nightingale's native population and Noble Vampires.[4] His supposed defense of the native civilization had therefore existed alongside the systematic destruction and exploitation of that same population for his own pursuit of power.

### Skills and Abilities
As the God-King of the Elder Gods, Ikor possessed an enormous collection of Divine and supernatural abilities accumulated through his natural Divinities, his position as leader of the Pantheon and repeated use of Predation. Many of his abilities were not individually named by the story; the following uses generic power classifications according to what Ikor directly demonstrated.

#### Natural and Divine Abilities
- God Physiology: Ikor was a high-ranking Divine Being whose Soul, Energy and existence greatly exceeded conventional supernatural beings. His enormous corpse-body could withstand attacks capable of devastating its surroundings, while his smaller true body remained active even after the external shell was bisected.[7][5]
- [[Immortality]]: Immortality was one of Ikor's principal Divinities. Severe destruction of his body did not permanently kill him, and his protection could automatically activate when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] inflicted otherwise lethal damage.[2][3]
- Resurrection: The immortality governed by Ikor allowed Elder Gods and selected beneficiaries to return even after physically fatal injuries. [[Lucifer]] was described as being capable of repeatedly returning to life after becoming an Elder God.[2]
- Soul Immortality: Ikor's authority primarily protected the Soul rather than merely preventing physical injury. [[Diablo]] identified the Elder Gods' defining advantage as an immortal Soul, making conventional physical death insufficient to permanently eliminate those protected by Ikor.[2]
- Immortality Bestowal: Ikor could extend his Immortality to other Elder Gods and selected followers. The Priest assigned to Lucifer specifically maintained the blessing of the Elder Gods' leader upon chosen individuals, while Ikor himself directly maintained the immortality of the combatants accompanying him at the [[Edge of the Cosmos]].[2][8][3]
- Immortality Negation: Because Ikor was the source of the immortality granted to his followers, that condition ultimately depended upon him. Victor realized during the battle that killing Ikor would remove the mechanism continuously preventing the others from dying permanently.[3]
- Regeneration: Ikor could recover from extreme physical injuries as part of his immortal condition. His body continued functioning after being cut apart and after receiving attacks powerful enough to destroy enormous portions of his form.[5][3]
- Predation: Ikor's second primary Divinity allowed him to consume other beings and assimilate their useful properties. Predation could absorb physical strength, Energy and supernatural abilities while altering Ikor's body according to what had been consumed.[3][4]
- Power Absorption: Ikor harvested rare supernatural abilities from [[Nightingale]]'s natives and other Elder Gods through Predation.[4]
- Energy Absorption: Consuming the Type 6 [[Destroyer]] partially restored Ikor's depleted Positive Energy reserves.[3]
- Physical Attribute Absorption: The Type 6 Destroyer's physical strength was added to Ikor's own after its consumption, visibly enlarging and strengthening his arms.[3]
- Biological Assimilation: Characteristics belonging to consumed beings could manifest upon Ikor's own body, most visibly demonstrated when Lucifer's wings appeared after Ikor consumed him.[3]
- Positive Energy Manipulation: Through his connection with Nightingale's [[Amara Elderblood|World Tree of Positivity]], Ikor could wield enormous quantities of Positive Energy. He used it offensively, defensively and to sustain his other supernatural abilities.[5][3]
- Energy Storage: Ikor possessed the ability to store a chosen type of Energy within himself. He stored Positive Energy supplied by Nightingale's World Tree, allowing him to continue using it even after travelling to the Edge of the Cosmos and becoming separated from the planet.[3]
- Energy Projection: Ikor could release concentrated beams, blasts and other attacks composed of Divine Power and Positive Energy. During Victor's ascension he restrained Victor and completely vaporized his head with a point-blank beam.[6]
- Energy Constructs: Ikor could create supernatural weapons and structures from his Power, including barriers, projectiles and a spear which he launched at Victor.[3][6]
- Barrier Creation: Ikor repeatedly created barriers to intercept or resist Victor's attacks. He attempted to defend himself from Victor's Dragon Breath with a barrier before the destructive properties of the flames broke through it.[5][3]
- Statistics Amplification: Ikor could strengthen other beings through his Divine Power. His presence and blessings enhanced the Elder Gods, Monsters and natives under his authority, while at the Edge he continually increased the power of the allies fighting Victor.[8][3]
- Power Bestowal: Ikor's blessings could provide supernatural effects to others rather than merely increasing physical strength, with his Immortality being distributed throughout his Pantheon and maintained through his own Energy.[2][3]
- Causality Manipulation: Victor directly identified one of Ikor's demonstrated abilities as the capacity to deny cause and effect. This allowed Ikor's supernatural defenses to interfere with the normal relationship between an action and its consequence.[8]
- Conceptual Manipulation: Victor likewise identified Ikor as possessing the ability to deny concepts, demonstrating that his defensive authority extended beyond purely physical or Energy-based phenomena.[8]
- Teleportation: Ikor could instantaneously relocate his enormous body. While fighting Victor he surrounded himself with a white miasma before disappearing and reappearing elsewhere despite his colossal size.[8]
- Spatial Manipulation: Ikor could manipulate spatial conditions through his techniques. During his final attempt to escape Victor, he used his newly accumulated power to destabilize the surrounding existence and caused an attack to appear directly before Victor instead of travelling through the intervening distance.[6]
- Dimensional Travel: Through Akashic Creation, Ikor transported himself, Victor, Var, Lucifer and the Type 6 Destroyer away from Nightingale and directly to the Edge of the Cosmos.[5]
- Power Nullification (Environmental): Ikor deliberately exploited the unusual metaphysical environment of the Edge of the Cosmos to restrict Victor. Creation Manipulation, Victor's extreme movement speed and his normal access to Negative Energy were severely limited because the ordinary Laws of Space and Time had not fully established themselves there. This was not conventional unrestricted nullification; Ikor specifically selected an environment whose nature countered Victor's abilities.[5]
- Gravity Manipulation: Ikor could massively increase the gravitational force surrounding himself, at one point increasing local gravity by hundreds of thousands of times in an attempt to hinder Victor.[5]
- Sound Manipulation: Ikor could produce an extremely powerful high-frequency sound from the opening in his face. The attack caused nearby observers to bleed from their ears and was powerful enough to disperse Victor's incoming Dragon Fire.[8]
- Summoning: Ikor called enormous numbers of Monsters into the final war. Hundreds of thousands emerged from the ground, the oceans and even the bodies of other Elder Gods after his command.[7]
- Monster Manipulation: As leader of the Elder Gods, Ikor possessed authority over Nightingale's Monsters and could organize them as military forces through the Alpha hierarchy. His forces included Behemoths, [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]], terrestrial and aerial Monsters, Alphas and Dragons.[7][8]
- Biological Manipulation: Ikor's Divinity was one of the components used to engineer Nightingale's Monster Race. The Monsters were produced by combining Ikor's Divinity with Nightingale's natives and captured Noble Vampires.[4]
- Body Modification: Ikor and the Elder Gods developed gigantic artificial bodies constructed from the corpses of Gods and native inhabitants, allowing their true existences to operate from inside colossal flesh constructs.[4]
- Enhanced Intelligence: Ikor was an experienced planner capable of identifying an opponent's strongest advantages and constructing coordinated counters to them. His plan against Victor combined environmental suppression, specialized allies, Immortality, Probability Manipulation, Darkness and Adaptation rather than relying only upon raw power.[5][3]

#### Powers Gained Through Predation
Ikor's arsenal was not static. Predation allowed him to continually expand his capabilities by consuming suitable targets. Chapter 886 revealed that he had already used this method for an unknown period of time before his direct confrontation with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], harvesting rare abilities from [[Nightingale]]'s natives and weaker Elder Gods.[4] Consequently, several powers demonstrated by Ikor may themselves have originated from previous victims even when the exact source was never identified.
- Unknown Harvested Abilities: Ikor repeatedly used Predation upon native inhabitants who naturally developed rare powers, adding useful abilities to himself. The exact number and identities of these abilities were never revealed.[4]
- Abilities of Consumed Elder Gods: Weaker Elder Gods were subjected to the same practice, with Predation being directly responsible for the Pantheon having so few remaining members by the final war. Which individual Divine abilities Ikor personally retained from them was not specified.[4]
- Enhanced Strength (Type 6 [[Destroyer]]): Ikor consumed the four-armed Type 6 Destroyer after determining that it was no longer sufficiently useful as an independent fighter. Its physical strength was directly added to his own, causing his arms to become larger and more muscular.[3]
- Energy Replenishment (Type 6 Destroyer): The same act of Predation partially restored Ikor's depleted Positive Energy reserves.[3]
- Probability Manipulation (Type 6 Destroyer): The Destroyer possessed a probability-related supernatural ability that had previously been used together with [[Lucifer]]'s Darkness and Ikor's support to prevent Victor from reaching Ikor. Predation was capable of acquiring supernatural abilities from consumed beings, making the Destroyer's probability power part of the collection available through its consumption.[3]
- Darkness Manipulation (Lucifer Morningstar): Lucifer's Darkness had been one of the principal abilities used to restrain Victor during the battle at the Edge. Ikor subsequently consumed Lucifer through Predation when the Fallen Angel ceased being useful as an independent combatant.[3]
- Wing Manifestation (Lucifer Morningstar): Immediately after consuming Lucifer, two angelic wings manifested behind Ikor, visibly demonstrating Lucifer's traits being incorporated into his body.[3]
- Flight (Lucifer Morningstar): Ikor's manifested angelic wings provided the physical characteristics associated with Lucifer's aerial form after assimilation.[3]
- Reactive Adaptation ([[Var-Kar-VI]]): Ikor's final and most important acquisition was Var's Extreme Adaptation. Var could adapt to methods that had previously killed him, eventually preventing the same method from working again. Ikor devoured Var after Victor's ascension and acquired his son's Adaptation, producing the combined set of Adaptation, Predation and [[Immortality]].[3][6]
- Resistance Development (Var-Kar-VI): Through Adaptation, Ikor could potentially survive an effect through Immortality and subsequently develop resistance against it. The combination was described as extraordinarily dangerous, although Victor's newly established Divine existence was already too powerful for Ikor to meaningfully exploit it.[6]

#### Skills & Abilities
As the God-King of the [[Elder Gods Pantheon]], Ikor possessed the standard supernatural characteristics of an exceptionally powerful God alongside two highly unusual personal Divinities: [[Immortality]] and Predation.[1][2] These abilities complemented one another extremely well; Immortality made Ikor exceptionally difficult to permanently destroy, while Predation allowed him to consume other beings, restore depleted resources and incorporate their powers and physiological traits into himself.
During his final battle against [[Victor Elderblood]], Ikor progressively assimilated abilities from a Type 6 [[Destroyer]], [[Lucifer]] Morningstar and [[Var-Kar-VI]], eventually combining Immortality, Predation and Adaptation into a single composite state.[3][4]

##### God Physiology
As an ancient God-King, Ikor possessed extreme physical strength, durability, speed, stamina and supernatural senses. Despite the enormous size of his external flesh construct, he could participate in high-level close combat against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and physically restrain him during his ascension.[4] His body endured tremendous punishment across an extremely prolonged battle and continuously recovered through the effects of [[Immortality]].
Ikor also possessed enormous reserves of Divine and Positive Energy, could fly, manipulate his Energy offensively and defensively, and operate normally in environments that would be inaccessible to ordinary Gods.

##### Immortality
[[Immortality]] was one of Ikor's principal Divinities and the foundation of the abnormal survivability possessed by the Elder Gods.[1] Its effects extended beyond ordinary longevity and supported regeneration, resurrection and resistance to conventional God-killing methods. The ability was not limitless, however, and depended upon sufficient Energy to continue reconstructing the affected existence.
Ikor's Immortality could affect both body and Soul. Elder Gods carrying his power could recover after damage that would normally permanently kill divine beings, including catastrophic physical destruction and severe Soul damage.[2][5] The power also allowed repeated reconstruction after lethal attacks as long as the Energy sustaining it remained available.
One of Ikor's most important applications was Immortality Bestowal. He could extend his Immortality to other Elder Gods and maintain the effect by continuously supplying them with Energy.[5] This created a distributed system in which Ikor functioned as the source supporting their continued resurrection. When he became isolated from [[Nightingale]] and his Positive Energy reserves began declining, Shur noticed that the Immortality Energy within him was no longer being replenished and that his condition was progressively becoming mortal again.
The system therefore possessed a clear weakness: repeated resurrection consumed resources, and separation from Ikor or exhaustion of his Energy could weaken the Immortality supplied to others. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] ultimately bypassed Ikor's own Immortality by consuming his entire existence rather than attempting to kill him through conventional physical or Soul destruction.[4]

##### Predation
Predation was Ikor's second principal Divinity and allowed him to consume living or divine beings and assimilate useful properties from their existence.[3][4] The ability was not limited to Energy absorption; it could acquire foreign powers, physical traits and supernatural characteristics and integrate them into Ikor's own body.
During the battle at the [[Edge of the Cosmos]], Ikor consumed the Type 6 [[Destroyer]], [[Lucifer]] Morningstar and eventually [[Var-Kar-VI]].[3][4] Each consumption strengthened him and added new abilities. The process also restored depleted Energy, making Predation particularly useful once his stored Positive Energy began running low.
Predation caused visible physiological assimilation. After consuming Lucifer, Ikor developed angelic wings and became increasingly chimera-like as additional traits were incorporated.[3] Chapter 886 further reveals that he had long used Predation outside combat, consuming [[Nightingale]] natives who developed rare abilities so that those powers could be added to his own arsenal.[6]
Ikor was also capable of using Predation against dangerous supernatural phenomena. During the battle against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] he consumed a black-hole attack rather than being destroyed by it.[2] This demonstrates extreme gravitational resistance and the ability to consume such an attack, but it should not automatically be expanded into blanket immunity to all forms of Existence Erasure.

##### Positive Energy Manipulation
Ikor possessed extensive access to Positive Energy supplied by [[Amara Elderblood]], [[Nightingale]]'s World Tree of Positivity.[2][3] While connected to Nightingale, he could replenish his reserves through the planet and World Tree, while separation forced him to rely upon stored Energy.
He used Positive Energy to reinforce his body, generate barriers, create offensive constructs and power Akashic Creation. During the prolonged battle at the Edge, this stored supply gradually declined because Ikor had been disconnected from Amara.[3] Returning to Nightingale immediately restored his reserves, demonstrating that the planet functioned as his primary renewable source.
Ikor also produced thorn-like constructs using Positive Energy and could create defensive barriers capable of resisting high-level attacks.[3]

##### Gravity Manipulation
Ikor demonstrated the ability to manipulate gravity around [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] during their initial confrontation at the Edge.[2] He used the increased gravitational pressure as another method of restricting Victor's movement and reducing the effectiveness of his extreme speed. The exact numerical multiplier should not be stated unless directly supported by the chapter text.

##### Energy Projection
Ikor could generate extremely powerful ranged attacks using concentrated supernatural Energy. During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ascension he restrained Victor's body and fired a concentrated beam directly into his head, vaporizing it before Victor regenerated.[4] He could also shape Energy into projectile weapons such as spears and launch them at high speed.

##### Corpse-Flesh Construct
The enormous body associated with Ikor was a constructed shell created from the flesh and corpses of Gods and [[Nightingale]]'s natives.[6] It functioned as biological armor surrounding his smaller true existence and provided enormous size, durability and physical force. Destruction of the outer construct did not automatically kill Ikor, and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was able to tear open the body and expose the smaller existence contained within.[2]
The unusual composition also interfered with Victor's perception. When attempting to examine Ikor, Victor initially saw something closer to an incomprehensible mass of flesh than the conventional structure expected from a God.[7]

##### Monster Creation and Biological Experimentation
Ikor was one of the principal figures responsible for creating [[Nightingale]]'s Monsters. After consuming him, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] learned that the Race had been produced through experiments combining Ikor's Divinity, Nightingale's native population and Vampire Nobles.[6]
Ikor's experiments extended beyond Monster creation. He systematically used natives as test subjects, searched for useful mutations and abilities, harvested rare powers through Predation and used bodies as material for the gigantic Elder-God constructs. His knowledge therefore included extensive supernatural biological experimentation in addition to conventional divine abilities.

##### Monster Dominion
As God-King of the Elder Gods, Ikor possessed supreme command over [[Nightingale]]'s Monster armies. In Chapter 877 he called forth hundreds of thousands of Monsters from land, sea and the bodies of the Elder Gods to begin the final war.[7] These forces included Behemoths, [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]], winged Monsters, terrestrial Monsters, Alphas and Dragons carrying Alphas.
This is better classified as large-scale command and deployment rather than unrestricted magical Summoning unless a chapter explicitly confirms the latter.

##### Remote Communication
Ikor could communicate over long distances with subordinates and allies. He directly contacted [[Lucifer]] Morningstar from elsewhere during the [[Nightingale]] conflict, issuing orders and negotiating his reward for killing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[8]

##### Akashic Creation
Akashic Creation was one of Ikor's most important specialized techniques and was prepared specifically as a countermeasure against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[2] Ikor summoned a white staff, gathered Positive Energy and generated a white domain that transported himself, Victor, Var, [[Lucifer]] and the Type 6 [[Destroyer]] to the [[Edge of the Cosmos]].
The technique effectively performed large-scale dimensional or cosmological transportation and battlefield removal. Ikor deliberately selected the Edge because Reality had not yet completely established its Rules there, causing Space and Time to behave abnormally and suppressing Victor's movement speed and several Creation-related abilities.[2][3] Akashic Creation therefore did not simply nullify Victor's powers directly; it moved him into an environment whose properties naturally interfered with them.

##### Spatial / Existential Distortion
After consuming Var and accumulating several additional powers, Ikor performed a technique that caused the surrounding existence to become unbalanced.[4] He released a concentrated attack and made a gesture that caused the attack to appear directly before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] rather than travelling through the intervening Space in a conventional manner. The impact shattered Space and propelled Victor through hundreds of universes before a portal returned him to [[Nightingale]].
Because the chapter describes existence becoming unbalanced and the attack appearing as though teleported, Spatial / Existential Distortion is more accurate than automatically classifying the feat as Causality Manipulation.

##### Final Composite State
Immediately before his death, Ikor possessed a composite arsenal combining his original Divinities with abilities obtained through Predation.

#### Weaknesses
Ikor's greatest limitation was the dependence of several abilities upon Energy. His Positive Energy reserves were renewable while connected to [[Nightingale]] but finite while separated from the planet, and the [[Immortality]] granted to other Elder Gods continuously consumed those reserves.[5][3] Predation required successful consumption before foreign abilities could be acquired, while Adaptation required exposure to or death from a phenomenon before resistance could develop.
His Immortality also did not provide absolute protection against complete existential consumption. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] bypassed the entire regenerative and resurrection mechanism by devouring Ikor's existence directly.[4]
