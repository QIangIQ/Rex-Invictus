Shur was an Elder God of [[Nightingale]] and the holder of the Divinity of Power. During the final war for Nightingale, he was one of the surviving members of the [[Elder Gods Pantheon]] who confronted the forces allied with [[Victor Elderblood]]. Shur described himself as the incarnation of Nightingale's power, possessing an unusual connection to the power existing throughout the planet that allowed him to wield enormous quantities of raw [[Divine Energy]].[1]
Like the other Elder Gods under [[Ikor-Kar-VI]], Shur was protected by supernatural immortality originating from Ikor. Destruction of his body and damage to his Soul were repeatedly reversed while Ikor continued supplying the necessary Energy. This immortality was not Shur's own Divinity, however. When Ikor became isolated at the [[Edge of the Cosmos]] and stopped replenishing the supply, Shur's remaining immortality began weakening until he became mortal again.[2][1]
Shur fought Vlad Tepes and Otsuki Elderblood Haruna simultaneously. Even while losing the direct exchange, his immense Power, reconstruction abilities and immortality allowed him to continuously pressure both opponents. After realizing that Ikor's protection was disappearing, Shur abandoned his previous strategy and released a forbidden crimson miasma powerful enough to corrode Vlad's severed arm until it completely disappeared. Haruna subsequently forced her evolution into a ten-tailed form and bombarded him with concentrated starlight while Vlad exploited the loss of Shur's immortality to destroy both his body and Soul.[1]

### Physical Appearance
Shur originally possessed the colossal proportions characteristic of the surviving Elder Gods, whose bodies during the final war exceeded 1,000 metres in height.[3] Unlike several of the other Elder Gods, Shur's exterior was described as completely white.[1]
After [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]] shattered his gigantic body into thousands of pieces with Mugetsu and its normal regeneration temporarily failed, Shur demonstrated another method of reconstruction. His massive body melted away into white Power that gathered in the sky before reforming into the same general appearance at approximately two metres tall.[1]
His appearance changed radically after he activated the dangerous ability Ikor had instructed him to reserve for an appropriate moment. Shur's body initially darkened completely while releasing an extremely toxic crimson miasma. After reconstructing himself again, his skin had become crimson red and the atmosphere immediately surrounding him was saturated with the same destructive substance.[1]
The miasma eventually surrounded him as a large red dome while scarlet Power extended from his body in tentacle-like structures. In this state, Shur appeared less like the completely white Elder God who had begun the battle and more like the center of an expanding field of corrosive red Energy.[1]

### Personality
Shur was aggressive, prideful and openly hostile toward the Vampires occupying [[Nightingale]]. He regarded them not merely as enemies but as foreign parasites, referring to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s allies as invaders, pests and a plague upon the planet.[1]
His hatred was sufficiently intense that he disagreed with Ikor's decision not to simply exterminate Nightingale's Vampire population. Shur admitted that Ikor had repeatedly explained his reasoning, but he had never accepted it. Once the battle deteriorated and his immortality began failing, Shur openly abandoned the plan established by his King and decided that he would personally eliminate the invaders instead.[1]
Shur was extremely confident while protected by Ikor's immortality. Even after Vlad damaged both his flesh and Soul and Haruna reduced his enormous body to thousands of fragments, he continued insisting that his opponents could not kill him.[1]
That confidence quickly deteriorated once his immortality supply stopped being replenished. Shur became confused when his regeneration failed, immediately wondered whether something had happened to Ikor and panicked once he realized the remaining Energy sustaining his immortality was continuously decreasing. His sudden fear demonstrated that he clearly understood the difference between his own Power and the borrowed immortality keeping him alive.[1]
Despite this fear, Shur remained dangerous and continued fighting rather than attempting to flee. He activated an ability Ikor had specifically instructed him to reserve, expanded his miasma across the battlefield and fought Vlad and Haruna until the final collapse of his immortality.[1]

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
An unnamed Elder God messenger appeared before [[Diablo]] shortly after Diablo discovered that [[Lucifer]] Morningstar had abandoned his original race and become an Elder God. The messenger emerged through a tear in Space and was described as completely pale white, without visible eyes and possessing a mouth filled with sharp teeth.[2]
Acting on behalf of the still-unnamed leader of the Elder Gods, the messenger offered Diablo a vial containing a black substance capable of amplifying the darkness already existing within an individual. The purpose was to make one of the First Angels fall naturally rather than through direct artificial corruption, assisting Diablo's plans while simultaneously establishing a political agreement between his future faction and the Elder Gods.[2]
In return for their assistance, the Elder Gods demanded that Diablo completely ignore their homeland after becoming a Ruler. The messenger warned that violating the agreement could result in Diablo confronting an immortal army and subsequently carried Lucifer back through the spatial opening.[2]
The story never explicitly identifies this messenger as Shur. His entirely pale-white appearance is compatible with Shur's later description, and the existing association has led to the possibility that this was Shur's first chronological appearance, but this remains unconfirmed and should not be treated as established canon.

#### Volume 6: King Among Kings
During the final battle against the [[Elder Gods Pantheon]], Shur confronted [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] and [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]] while [[Victor Elderblood]] was fighting Ikor, Var, [[Lucifer]] and the Type 6 [[Destroyer]] elsewhere.[1]
Vlad attacked Shur with overwhelming hatred, repeatedly piercing the Elder God's enormous body while using attacks capable of damaging both flesh and Soul. Shur nevertheless regenerated from the injuries. Even when Vlad completely tore his body apart, Ikor's immortality continued restoring him.[1]
Haruna subsequently used Mugetsu, shattering Shur's gigantic body into thousands of pieces. Shur confidently told her that killing him was useless, only to realize moments later that his body had stopped regenerating normally.[1]
Rather than remain in his shattered form, Shur allowed the gigantic body to melt into white Power. The Energy gathered above the battlefield and reconstructed Shur as a much smaller humanoid approximately two metres tall. Immediately after reforming, he fired a beam of pure Power at Haruna fast enough that she only barely avoided the attack, losing part of one of her tails to its destructive force.[1]
Shur declared himself the incarnation of the planet's Power and claimed that so long as Power continued existing, he could not be destroyed. He fired another beam at Vlad, who blocked it with his weapon but still suffered physical damage from the impact.[1]
Shur then formed two more concentrated beams from his hands. Unlike ordinary projectiles, these constructs possessed enough solidity to physically clash against Vlad and Haruna's weapons. The three began fighting at extremely high speed. Although Shur was gradually losing the direct exchange, his unusual constitution and continued restoration allowed him to maintain pressure against both opponents.[1]
Becoming irritated by their attacks, Shur released an explosion of pure Power and generated an enormous Energy sphere above himself. Rather than launching the sphere itself, he caused numerous beams to continuously fire from it toward Vlad and Haruna, forcing both combatants to evade the bombardment.[1]
Vlad noticed that Shur was vastly stronger than he remembered. They had fought previously, and although that earlier battle had been difficult, Vlad had still defeated him with relative ease. Now, even with Haruna fighting beside him, Shur's increased strength and immortality made permanently defeating him extraordinarily difficult.[1]
The situation abruptly changed when Shur coughed in pain and his enormous Energy sphere disappeared. He immediately sensed that the Energy responsible for his immortality was no longer being replenished. Ikor had been transported to the [[Edge of the Cosmos]] and was expending his limited Positive Energy reserves in his battle against Victor, preventing him from continuing to maintain Shur normally.[1]
Shur became distracted by the realization. Vlad immediately cut him in half and Haruna followed with another strike, while Shur noticed that the remaining Energy inside his body continued decreasing. He correctly concluded that if the process continued he would become mortal.[1]
Panicking, Shur activated an ability that Ikor had ordered him to use only when necessary. His body darkened completely and began releasing an extremely toxic red miasma. Both Vlad and Haruna's instincts immediately warned them of the danger, but neither escaped before the substance reached their arms.[1]
The defensive Energy Victor had previously placed within Haruna immediately activated to protect her. Vlad instead amputated his infected arm before the miasma could spread. The severed limb was then consumed by the red substance until it completely vanished.[1]
Shur reconstructed himself again inside a dome of miasma. His previously white skin had turned crimson and the surrounding atmosphere was filled with the poisonous red substance. Rather than continue following Ikor's strategy, Shur apologized to his King and openly declared that he would disregard the plan.[1]
He released the miasma outward, causing the surrounding environment to rot, and condemned Victor's allies as invaders and parasites. Scarlet tentacles emerged from his body and attacked the surrounding battlefield while Vlad used enormous quantities of Blood to create defensive barriers around Haruna.[1]
Haruna responded by forcibly advancing her existence and temporarily becoming a Ten-Tailed Moonlight Fox. Her transformation produced an enormous increase in Power, after which she invoked an attack that brought countless streams of starlight toward Shur from Space.[1]
Shur attempted to stop her by releasing an enormous crimson beam upward, but one of Haruna's tails physically deflected the attack. The incoming starlight then struck Shur repeatedly and drove him toward Space.[1]
By this point, the Energy responsible for Shur's immortality had been completely exhausted. For the first time during the confrontation, the incoming attacks inflicted pain without his restoration immediately negating the consequences. Shur screamed while repeated beams of starlight destroyed his body.[1]
Hearing Shur's genuine cries of pain caused Vlad to abandon his earlier plan to merely seal him. Realizing the Elder God could finally be killed, Vlad attacked with everything he possessed and impaled Shur while Haruna's starlight continued crushing and burning his body.[1]
Vlad refused to leave even the possibility of another resurrection. Using his Soul Power, he seized Shur's head and tore the Elder God apart, deliberately destroying both his physical body and Soul. Shur's death released a final pillar of white Power into the atmosphere.[1]
Unlike the other Elder Gods eliminated during the battle, Shur was ultimately killed through comparatively conventional destruction once the supernatural immortality granted by Ikor had disappeared. Vlad and Haruna never learned why the immortality failed, but the loss of Ikor's continuous support removed the protection that had previously made Shur effectively impossible for them to permanently kill.[1]

### Skills & Abilities
Shur was one of the surviving Elder Gods of [[Nightingale]] and possessed the Divinity of Power. His combat abilities primarily revolved around overwhelming quantities of raw supernatural Power, Energy projection, physical reconstruction and a highly destructive red miasma. His extraordinary immortality should be distinguished from these natural abilities because the immortality was supplied and continuously maintained by [[Ikor-Kar-VI]].[1]

#### Elder God Physiology
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics: As an Elder God, Shur possessed tremendous physical strength, speed, durability and endurance. He could engage [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] and [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]] simultaneously at such speeds that the three repeatedly disappeared from ordinary observation while their clashes resounded throughout the battlefield.[1]
- Divine Power: Shur possessed the supernatural Energy and authority of a God. His Divine Power could be released directly from his body, concentrated into projectiles or shaped into solid constructs.[1]
- Enhanced Durability: Shur endured repeated attacks from Vlad and Haruna, including strikes capable of damaging both his body and Soul. Although much of his effective survivability depended upon Ikor's immortality, his own Divine constitution remained powerful enough to participate directly in close combat against both opponents.[1]
- Flight: Shur could freely fight and maneuver through the air during his high-speed confrontation with Vlad and Haruna.[1]
- Large Size: Shur's original Elder God body possessed the colossal proportions associated with the surviving members of the Pantheon, exceeding one thousand metres in scale.[3]

#### Divinity
- Divinity — Power: Shur possessed the Divinity of Power. He described himself as the incarnation of [[Nightingale]]'s Power and claimed that so long as Power continued to exist he would not be destroyed.[1]
- Conceptual Embodiment (Power): Shur's statements directly associated his Divine existence with the concept of Power present within Nightingale. The exact metaphysical mechanics of this relationship were never fully explained, so it should not be treated as unrestricted embodiment of all forms of Power throughout existence.[1]
- Planetary Power Connection: Shur specifically identified himself as the incarnation of the planet's Power, establishing a connection between his Divinity and Nightingale. The precise amount of Energy he could draw from this relationship was not quantified.[1]
- Energy Manipulation: Shur could freely manipulate enormous quantities of raw Divine Power and use it offensively in numerous forms.[1]
- Energy Projection: He repeatedly fired concentrated beams of white Power directly from his hands. The attacks could burn Haruna's tails and injure Vlad even when blocked with his weapon.[1]
- Energy Constructs: Shur could condense his Power until it possessed physical solidity. Two Energy beams created from his hands directly clashed against Vlad and Haruna's weapons like solid objects.[1]
- Energy Sphere Creation: He created an enormous sphere composed entirely of Power above himself.[1]
- Multi-Directional Energy Projection: The sphere could release numerous independent beams rather than functioning as a single projectile, allowing Shur to bombard multiple moving opponents simultaneously.[1]
- Energy Aura: Shur could explosively release Power from his entire body, generating a large-scale aura around himself.[1]
- Energy Tentacle Manifestation: After activating his crimson state, Shur produced numerous tentacle-like extensions of red Power from his body and directed them independently against Vlad and Haruna.[1]

#### Body Manipulation
- Size Manipulation: Shur could abandon his gigantic Elder God body and reform at a considerably smaller scale. After his enormous body was shattered, he reconstructed himself at approximately two metres tall.[1]
- Body Reconstruction: Even after his normal regeneration temporarily stopped, Shur was able to melt his destroyed gigantic body into white Power and reconstruct an entirely new physical form from that Energy.[1]
- Transformation: Activating the dangerous power reserved by Ikor substantially changed Shur's appearance and properties. His body initially darkened before ultimately reforming with crimson skin while continuously producing red miasma.[1]

#### Miasma
- Miasma Manipulation: Shur possessed an exceptionally dangerous crimson miasma that he released only after his normal immortality began failing. Ikor had previously instructed him to reserve this ability until an appropriate moment.[1]
- Corrosion: The miasma rapidly consumed physical matter. Contact damaged both Vlad and Haruna immediately and caused the surrounding environment to rot when Shur expanded it across the battlefield.[1]
- Disintegration: Vlad was forced to amputate his contaminated arm, after which the severed limb was completely consumed by the miasma until nothing remained.[1]
- Existence Destruction (Limited; via Miasma): The narration describes Vlad's severed arm as vanishing completely after being consumed by the substance. The exact metaphysical depth of this effect was not explained, so it should not automatically be interpreted as unrestricted conceptual erasure.[1]
- Area-of-Effect Attack: Shur could spread the miasma outward over a large portion of the battlefield, rotting the environment around him.[1]
- Miasma Aura: After transforming, Shur continuously surrounded himself with the substance, making direct approach inherently dangerous.[1]
- Miasma Barrier: The released substance formed a large dome around Shur while his body reconstructed itself.[1]

#### Borrowed Immortality
Shur's extraordinary immortality was not generated by his Divinity of Power. It originated from [[Ikor-Kar-VI]], whose Divine authority continuously supplied Shur and the other Elder Gods with the Energy necessary to return from otherwise fatal destruction.[2][1]
- [[Immortality]] (Granted by Ikor): Shur repeatedly survived injuries that should have permanently killed a God. His continued existence depended upon the immortality Energy supplied by Ikor.[1]
- Resurrection: Destruction of Shur's physical body was normally insufficient to kill him while Ikor's protection remained active. He repeatedly returned after Vlad tore his body apart and after Haruna shattered him into thousands of pieces.[1]
- Regeneration: Shur rapidly reconstructed severe injuries, including damage inflicted directly upon both his body and Soul.[1]
- Soul Restoration (Through Ikor's Immortality): Vlad's attacks repeatedly damaged Shur's Soul alongside his flesh without permanently killing him while the immortality supply remained active. Once that protection disappeared, destruction of his Soul became fatal.[1]
- Immortality Dependence: Ikor was the source maintaining Shur's supernatural immortality. When Shur suddenly stopped receiving additional Energy, he immediately noticed the loss and correctly realized that his remaining protection would eventually disappear.[1]
- Finite Immortality Reserve: Once separated from Ikor's active supply, the Energy already contained within Shur gradually weakened with continued damage until his immortality was completely exhausted.[1]

### Weaknesses
- Dependence on Ikor: Shur's greatest defensive advantage was externally maintained. If [[Ikor-Kar-VI]] could no longer supply the necessary Energy, Shur's immortality progressively weakened until he could be permanently killed.[1]
- Finite [[Immortality]] Energy: Even before the protection disappeared completely, Shur could sense his remaining Energy being depleted. Continued damage therefore accelerated his return to mortality once Ikor stopped replenishing the supply.[1]
- Soul Destruction: Shur could survive Soul damage while Ikor's immortality remained active, but once that protection disappeared his Soul was vulnerable to permanent destruction. Vlad exploited this directly when finishing him.[1]
- Emotional Instability: Shur lost concentration when he realized his immortality was failing, giving Vlad and Haruna an immediate opening to attack him.[1]
- Overreliance on Immortality: Shur initially fought with extreme confidence because physical destruction had repeatedly failed to kill him. The sudden loss of that protection caused him to panic and abandon the strategy established by Ikor.[1]
