The Seven Black Thrones are the seven Gods who stand immediately under <Darkness>, the Great Darkness of the [[Black Eclipse Pantheon]], and above the Ten Chief Gods. Victor Elderblood addresses that ruler as Vharzath. They are his supreme Divine command, and the only fighters of Black Eclipse who threaten a galaxy in their own right. The Chief Gods reach that tier because his blessing forces them up to it. He replaces those. He does not replace a Throne.[1][2][3][4]
All seven offices are named. [[Vaelthar]] is the Throne of Origin, the Great Origin, and the holder of a Darkness-stained <BEGIN>. He was made to be a Chosen One of Negativity and was not chosen. A projection of Victor broke him. In Lily's Hell he was emptied, and his body was ordered burned. [[Xytherion]] is the Throne of the End, Vharzath's right hand, his deputy, and the supreme commander of the enemy forces. [[Aetherys]] holds Time, [[Oryndal]] Space, [[Elyndra]] Reality, [[Nharzuel]] Existence, and [[Zhaeroth]] [[Chaos]]. Read as one design, the seven are the organs of a universe. Life, death, and infinity are the functions still missing from it.[5][6][3][4]
Two of the six who remained have been sent. The imperial offensive was taking his conquests apart faster than a cautious war could bear, and Vharzath loosed his Thrones with their own safety set above the objective. Two answered. They are not named. He then told <Emperor> to keep their bargain. The skull will not bring his own elites into Victor's galaxy.[7]

### Overview
The first report of the seven comes from a subordinate God's memory and puts their strength near Darkness himself. That comparison was never a measurement taken on a battlefield. What the field later showed was one seat, and it lost. [[Vaelthar]] arrives above the Chief Gods, strains an Eldritch domain by presence alone, and is broken by a projection whose true body never joins the fight. The count that matters after that defeat is simpler. Black Eclipse has seven beings who can genuinely threaten a galaxy, and those seven are the Thrones. The Empire has more than forty.[1][6][8][2][3]
The seat and the God are not the same word. Great Origin is what [[Kaelith]] calls him. Vaelthar is the name. Throne of Origin is the office. <BEGIN> is what the office carries. [[Xytherion]]'s office is the End, and nothing in Vaelthar's making is said to have been repeated for him. He is not given that stained Beginning, and he is not shown using <END> himself. The weapon he is ordered to spend is the [[Nihilvore]].[6][3]
A Throne-level presence is not, by itself, one of these seats. Justice arrives with that presence and belongs to <Emperor>'s faction. [[Elarion]] serves the Empire of Light. <Emperor> has Thrones of his own, and Vharzath measures [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s elites against those, not against this seven. The seats on this page answer to Vharzath. [[Nerath]] is held in the highest esteem by the Thrones. Esteem does not make her one of them.[9][6][3]
The Chief Gods beneath them are another order. Their places are not won by strength. Vharzath bestows the title, the realm, and the blessing, and he trains the next holder so that a fallen God can be replaced. The ten titles are [[Azrakar]], Kaelith, Nerath, [[Vhalor]], [[Khariel]], [[Morvane]], [[Azelith]], [[Nihrael]], [[Draveth]], and [[Vaelzara]]. In that order their charges are emptiness, night, silence, shadow, soul, fear, darkness, oblivion, inevitability, and Vaelthar himself. The void between the galaxies is Azrakar's charge, and the predecessor who held it had the same one. A newly blessed God can threaten a galaxy and still not have mastered the power. Years in the office are what bring him to its full use. Until then he is an untrained hand on a weapon meant to end a world, swinging a force that belongs to Darkness and not to the realm he was given.[3][4]
The Thrones are the part of that host which is not interchangeable. Lily, looking at the captured Origin beside the ruined Chief Gods, separates them in one line. The failure on her table was an experiment. The others are much more stable. They are the true elites of Darkness.[4]

#### Vaelthar
Nearly three meters, and humanoid, where the Chief Gods on that field are not. Ivory armor veined with violet. Four golden eyes. Four arms. Reverse-jointed legs. Black energy pours off his back like a cloak. In the attack it wraps the four arms and builds structures across the field. Separate structures hover behind the shoulders until the blow to his chest breaks them. Torn armor shows violet lines under the skin.[6][8]
He was not born into the office. [[Vharzath]] made him, artificially, as a possible Chosen One of Negativity. The making succeeded. The choice did not. A portion of Vharzath's own power is in him anyway, and the <BEGIN> he carries is stained by that Darkness. The stain is what makes the existence strange. It is also why losing him costs more than a seat. Territory can go. A Chief God who has served for millennia can die, and another can be raised into the role, even if the new one is not the equal of the old. [[Vaelthar]] cannot be replaced that way. Vharzath regrets the order that sent him. He ought to have weighed it.[3]
Lily, with the body in front of her, sees the same making from the other side. He looks violently created in the light, then disfigured by the darkness until the two will not sit in one being. He is a failed version of what [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] could have become. The Dragon Emperor was chosen by Negativity and still carries the other side. Creation and Beginning are already in him, and the two poles came with his growth. Vaelthar was forced into both. Positivity and Negativity are not enemies. Together they are the balance. The experiment, Lily judges, was very likely the attempt to manufacture a Chosen One of Negativity.[4]
On the field he speaks for the ruler before he speaks for himself. The restored three have disappointed the Great Darkness. They bow. He has already put Victor at the level of the four faction leaders, and their chance of living through him under one percent. He orders the attack anyway. When [[Junketsu]] has cut him and the Gods with him apart, the order is "Stand firm!" He floods them with his own power. The wounds close in seconds, including the arm he lost. Armor torn, he still calls the survivors for a last attack. The whole of it is spent on a projection. The true body is the eye of the dragon, still holding the galaxy. The sword stops at his head. He is alive when the living are ordered taken.[6][8]
What remains of him after that is not a prisoner waiting on a second fight. In the deepest part of Hell, a place made so Lily can practice her art and warded so that Darkness has no power there and can perceive nothing, he is one of her works. Nothing is left of the grandeur. Memories, blood, existence, and soul are taken. The deep record, the strength and disposition of Darkness's troops among it, erases itself out of his mind. The defense on him is the same kind Victor built for his own elite, treaties against soul attacks and mental assaults. Used in the open universe, that defense would have destroyed the servant's soul at the first deep intrusion. Used where Darkness cannot see, it leaves a catatonic shell. Violet takes the memories. Once Ruby has her samples, the bodies are ordered destroyed. Dragonfire that obliterates them is called an honor.[4]

#### Xytherion
Black armor, red accents, and a human shape. He looks like a medieval warrior out of a fantasy world. His allies do not. He greets [[Vharzath]] as the Great Darkness, sees that the rite which raised a new [[Azrakar]] has worked, and is told the truth of it. The new Chief God is not yet the equal of the one who died, and nine more still have to be made. He does not argue the point.[3]
The station under that silence is the highest of the six who remain. He is Darkness's right hand, comparable to [[Scathach]] and Violet. He is supreme commander of the enemy forces, deputy when the ruler is absent, and the most trusted man Vharzath has. The order he is given, while the Thrones are still being withheld, is not a seat on the invasion. Deploy the [[Nihilvore]]. He takes it as a command and goes. When the weapon is later reported lost, he is still there, and silent. He has never seen that expression on the Great Darkness. END bound to a cannon has just erased the thing he sent, and the only answer to END is BEGIN. The Throne who carried BEGIN is already gone.[3][10][4]

#### Aetherys and Oryndal
Time and Space are a pair. [[Aetherys]] is responsible for the flow of time through the galaxies Darkness has conquered, and he regulates the shift of that time on command. [[Oryndal]] often works with him. Distance and dimension are his, and through them the enemy's galaxies are joined. He is the bridge.[4]

#### Elyndra
[[Elyndra]] holds the reality of the hostile galaxies. The charge is maintenance. No separate campaign, appearance, or act of hers is recorded.[4]

#### Nharzuel
[[Nharzuel]] is Existence. He embodies being, and the domain covers the existence of beings, objects, worlds, and concepts. No separate appearance is recorded.[4]

#### Zhaeroth
[[Zhaeroth]] is [[Chaos]], and the charge under that name is everything beneath Darkness. For that, he was considered the leader's right hand. It is not the same office as [[Xytherion]]'s. The deputy and the most trusted man is the Throne of the End. Zhaeroth is the seat the prisoner could say the least about.[4]
The name offends the women who read it. [[Scathach]] calls it arrogant, and the last of the list in particular. Jeanne cannot reconcile it with the altitude of the Upper Sectors. An ignorant creator god may call himself Chaos because he thinks he knows everything. These beings know how the cosmos works. Calling one of their own pillars by that word is, to her, madness.[4]

### What the Offices Are For
Velnorah is the one who says the shape aloud. The titles are not a war council. They are an attempt at a self-contained universe, and the BEGIN Throne is the part that gives the attempt a starting point. Jeanne adds the condition underneath it. The universal laws are too strong to be rewritten by a local arrangement. A fundamental change takes Negativity and Positivity.[4]
Ruby refuses the word Throne for the design. Taken as potential Primordial Beings of a new universe, the seven cover almost every essential function of a stable cosmos. Jeanne counts what is absent. Life, death, and infinity are not among them. Ruby does not treat the absence as a hole he has failed to notice. He may not intend a universe that runs on the same rules as this one. A universe can be made to set its own.[4]
Kaguya's answer to the theory is that it changes nothing. The mission remains the destruction of the enemy. Violet agrees, and then tells [[Scathach]] to be ready. The man Violet has read, out of [[Vaelthar]]'s memories and out of every comparable record in an empire of trillions, is cautious, a born leader, and arrogant. That man does not sit still and absorb losses without end. The memories are the Throne's view of him, not a portrait taken from his own mind. The deep facts that would have completed it erased themselves.[4]

### War
Four of the ten Chief Gods reach the war with War: [[Azrakar]], [[Kaelith]], [[Nerath]], and [[Vhalor]]. Azrakar is the one who counts four Chief Gods on the field and, in the same breath, the esteem in which the Thrones hold Nerath. The Great Silence wants War alive. Azrakar accepts. The four Horsemen then erase him. Darkness is displeased. The order becomes alive or dead, all of them. A Throne answers, "Yes, Great Darkness," and calls the rest of the Chief Gods. That voice is not [[Vaelthar]], who has not yet entered, and it is not identified as [[Xytherion]].[9][11][5]
War does not give the three survivors the pause. Their execution is set when pressure hits [[Gol-goroth]]'s domain. The second wave starts to break it. War drops the stroke. The gap opens, and Vaelthar comes through at the head of a reinforcement counted as ten. Nine are galactic-level threats. He is the one true God at Throne level in that count. The nine are not the other six seats. Kaelith greets him, broken, as Great Origin. His presence strains the domain. War judges the new leader stronger than himself, sees no way out, and shuts the domain down. A beam of pure darkness puts Kaelith, Nerath, and Vhalor back on their feet. The rebuke comes with the healing. They have disappointed the Great Darkness. They bow.[5][6]
Justice and [[Elarion]] arrive for patrons of their own. Each wants War. The <BEGIN> that flares between those claims is Vaelthar's, kin to the Emperor's Beginning, and corrupted. War refuses to be decided over. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] answers, "Well said, my herald," and appears holding the galaxy. He kills the restored Kaelith by the Soul. She does not return for the attack. He names Vharzath and declares extinction. The planets are already under attack from every side. Vharzath can sense more than twenty elite women, each at Throne level or beyond, and cannot recall his forces. Vaelthar orders Black Eclipse in anyway.[6]
The Gods open with beams that could destroy stars, and with dimensional spears. Victor does not raise the sword. One God, unnamed, comes out of a spatial distortion with a sword that cuts barriers spanning dimensions. Victor catches it in two fingers and throws him through planetary wreckage. Vaelthar spends the restraints and a focused blow on the chest. The projection does not take one step back. A knee breaks his concentration. Victor throws him into his own Gods.[8]
[[Junketsu]]'s plain cut halves what is in front of it. Nerath sees the allies cut in two and the Throne missing an arm. She judges that they cannot win, and that only the Great Darkness can keep up with him. They are alive because they are being spared. "Stand firm!" closes the wounds. Victor lets the healing happen. He is waiting for the ruler, who does not come. Beams that could wreck solar systems are gathered into a sphere and crushed. His aura strips their control.[8]
Vaelthar raises a domain and closes it as a prison of black constructs, held by burning immense energy. The other Gods fire into the center. The projection walks out. An energy wave, a dimensional weapon, and a chain meant for the Soul all dissolve against him. The God who runs is crushed into particles. That God is not named, and he is not one of the seven. Vaelthar blocks Junketsu with all four arms, is hit in the chest, and is thrown hard enough to open a rift in space. A foot comes down on his chest. The sword stops at his head. The survivors do not step in. The only damage the whole assault leaves on the projection is a crack in the gauntlet.[8]
Victor tells them what they have been fighting. The true body is the eye of the dragon, still holding the galaxy. The living are ordered taken prisoner. The remains of the fallen are to be collected. The projection leaves with War and the fallen Gods. The dragon lets the galaxy go. Vharzath has still not taken the field. Victor's judgment of that refusal is that the leaders would not have lasted eons if they were fools.[8]
The invasion that follows does not pull the seven onto the border. Fleets and low-ranked soldiers take the losses. Darkness still has territories, fleets, and resources, and the elite forces and the Thrones have not appeared. A new Azrakar is raised in the palace. He can threaten a galaxy. He is not yet as strong as the predecessor, who had lived far longer. Nine more still have to be made. Xytherion is the one who sees that the rite worked, and the loss Vharzath will not set beside those replacements is Vaelthar.[2][3]
He wants the Dragon Emperor understood before he spends what is left. The bargain he offers the flaming skull is a diversion, paid in spoils. Twenty percent is refused. The skull will not become the enemy of the Chosen One of Negativity for that, and he will not send his main fleet while he can spend smaller factions on the test. The price that closes it is half the spoils, the skull choosing first, against the Abominations. Neither means to keep the bargain if a better chance appears. What Vharzath actually spends, through Xytherion, is the [[Nihilvore]].[3]
Hela erases it with <END> bound to a cannon. The faction, the skull says, must disappear. END is answered only by BEGIN. Vharzath has lost the Throne who carried it, and <Emperor> has no subordinate who can supply another. The skull's demand is total war. The Thrones against his Enforcers. Vharzath refuses. A blind offensive is not advisable while the enemy may still have other secrets. The skull leaves him to fight his own way. Xytherion, who has never seen the Great Darkness look like that, is not given the field.[10]
The refusal does not hold. Within days the imperial legions are loose across his territories, his supply lines break, and the choice in front of him is one galaxy or the release of an elite unit. He will not accept the loss of everything.
"Go, my thrones, and put your own safety first."
Two answer. "Yes, Great Darkness." They leave. Which two is not said. Xytherion's command of the Nihilvore does not identify him as one of them.[7]
After they are gone he tells the skull to keep the agreement. The answer is that he will, but not in Victor's galaxy. Elites sent there are dead or taken. That galaxy is the domain of the Chosen of Negativity, and only the skull himself could enter it. What he offers instead is a joint hunt of the elites and the troops outside it, and the galaxy afterward, once enough of them are gone. Vharzath will handle his own war in his own way. The skull says he will wait.[7]
