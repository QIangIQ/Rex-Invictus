Aurethys Vaelthion is the System entry <Light>, the Emperor of Light, and the Chosen of Positivity. He is a primordial god of Light. He rules the [[Veillon Empire]], the Realm of Light, and he is one of the four faction leaders of the Upper Sectors, with <Darkness>, <Emperor> and <Law>.[1][2][3]
He is not Positivity. The System lists <Light> apart from the primordial Authorities of Positivity and Negativity. Positivity chose him, taught him every method of attributeless energy, and still speaks to him as an old friend.[4][3]
His body is pure light. He sits the throne of a sacred palace in the imperial capital, and from that seat he watched [[Victor Elderblood]] reshape a scattered territory into a supergalaxy. His judgment is that Victor learned attributeless energy alone, and that <Darkness> and <Emperor> will move before he has to.[3]

### Physical Appearance
In the imperial capital, in a palace of sacred architecture, he sits the throne as a humanoid figure. His long golden hair is made entirely of light and flows about his shoulders. His body is pure light, with none of the features of flesh and blood. His golden eyes hold a constant gleam, and his gaze stays serene.[3]
The soldiers of the realm are not copies of him. Many common troops have no conventional race and are made mostly of Light-based Energy around abnormal Souls. Commander Vik-Ir-Loah still had an organic body under the armor. The Solus have more individuality and more depth.[5][6]

### Personality
He speaks to Positivity as an old friend, and the gaze on the throne is serene. When Positivity calls [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] arrogant, extravagant, provocative, and petty, and the perfect Chosen of Negativity, Aurethys answers that Victor at least wants them to believe that. He refuses to call Negativity's Chosen incapable. Victor had not been in the upper sectors thirty minutes, and he had already understood how the whole thing works and had started putting it in order. Aurethys's own thought is that the control is flawless. A soft smile is what reaches his face while he watches the territory being reshaped.[3]
He does not spend himself. Positivity asks what he will do if her Chosen will not give up authority without a fight. The answer is that the question is pointless. He will take back what rightfully belongs to him, and he does not even have to do anything. <Darkness> and <Emperor> will not stand idle while a faction rises that threatens their position. Then they watch. He is patient. He will not act in a rush only because a place on the Throne of Reality is there to be taken.[3]
The realm under him is not that patience. Commander Vik-Ir-Loah's definition of Evil is that anything their Light cannot accept is Evil, and Evil is eradicated. Prolonged integration into the Pure Positive Energy network takes independent thought and action away until a person depends on the Light. Victor, an absolute Emperor himself, found that repulsive. [[Solus 969]] was shocked that anyone but the Emperor could turn a Representative of Light into a Representative of Darkness.[5][6]

### History
His homeland, his family, and his age before the throne are not given. What is stated of him is the present rank. A primordial god of Light, chosen by Positivity.[3]
The selection is older than [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s arrival in the Upper Sectors. In the records Victor saw, the Judges of the Abyss reacted to Emperor <Light> after Positivity chose him, and eliminating him was discussed.[1]
Positivity and Negativity do not pass their Authorities out as ordinary Divinities. The primordial aspect selects the Representative. By the time Victor's clone entered the realm, his Light was already in the religion, the military doctrine, the artificial intelligence, the Divine technology, and the Pure Positive Energy infrastructure.[1][5][7][6]

### Cosmic Nature
[[Primordial Chaos]] divided, and Positivity and Negativity are the two halves. They are not moral Good and Evil. Negativity holds the greater affinity with the spiritual, the abstract, and the negative, Souls and emotions among them. Positivity holds the greater affinity with the material, the positive, and the concrete. Other Primordials keep their own domains. Death, End, Life, Space, and Time are not swallowed by either half.[1][8][9]
The split was of one being. The opposites came out of that division, and the division is what keeps the balance of the universe. Knowledge of the former self went to Positivity. Instincts went to Negativity. That is why Positivity has always been the more awake of the two, even in semi-consciousness, and why he could teach his Chosen. Negativity usually wakes a little more when her Chosen appears in the upper sectors. Positivity was already awake.[3]
<Light> is the man. Positivity is the primordial. The System lists them apart. <Light> sat at Authority Rank 7, with Positivity and Negativity still above that list as the highest conceptual pair.[4]
Attributeless energy sits outside ordinary energy. Positivity knows every method of its use, and he is the one who taught those methods to Aurethys. Even the primordial beings, by Positivity's own word, do not know how to use it correctly. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] uses it anyway, and he was not taught.[3]
The shape of his realm is not [[Vharzath]]'s. Vharzath turned his own body into the territory. <Light>, <Emperor>, and <Law> immersed a whole galaxy in their power and made that galaxy their domain. Below their level, that region is a death zone.[3]

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] met the name in the records tied to Negativity. One of the scenes was the Judges treating Emperor <Light>, newly chosen by Positivity, as dangerous enough that his death was discussed. The same revelation set the rule. These Authorities are not cultivated. The semi-conscious aspects choose.[1]
The System then showed <Light> as its own entry, Authority Rank 7, separate from Positivity.[4]
Victor later had the rough division. Negativity on the spiritual and abstract side, Positivity on the concrete and material side, and neither side free to overwrite every specialized Primordial.[8]
The Owner of Limbo told him that Positivity already had a Representative in the Upper Sectors, and that the two Representatives mattered to the future Balance. Victor did not yet have the name, and he would not send his real body. The reconnaissance clone was almost entirely Energy, cut off from the links that could be followed home.[10][11]
The clone met the [[Veillon Empire]]. Vik-Ir-Loah's law was absolute. What their Light could not accept was Evil, and Evil was destroyed. The Eleventh Frontline put tens of thousands in the field, with interstellar ships, an intelligence that classified powers during the fight, and devices built to interfere with extreme speed, Space, and Reality manipulation. Many of the troops were Light-Energy around abnormal Souls, powerful and shallow. Victor took them for incomplete Gods.[5][7]
The Solus run from 1000 to 1. A lower number is a higher place. The single digits stand closest to the Emperor. A normal candidate must hold the highest Divinity of their Concept, what the Empire calls Primordial God-level Divinity, and must prove it by conquest. A true Grandmaster was valuable enough that the Empire would accelerate him if he accepted purification and submission. Victor connected that Emperor to the System entry. The ruler was Emperor <Light>.[7]
He opened the Negative Divinities in the clone. The soldiers treated it as contamination. [[Solus 969]] came in with more depth than the line troops and fought the restricted clone directly. At the same time Victor found the heart of the system, a sphere of Pure Positive Energy. It fed the technology, the Light in the soldiers, and the reach of the infrastructure. Anna's reading was that a seeded system let the Emperor perceive the territory through his Divinity. Violet's reading was that bodies made of Light could function as extensions of his [[Will Solace|Will]]. Victor's own reading was harsher. Integration took free will. Thought and ordinary action could be made to depend on permission from the Light.[6]
He stopped the deletion of Vik-Ir-Loah, inverted him from a Representative of Light into a Representative of Darkness, and the man knelt. He copied the installation, sent it to his real body, and destroyed the core. The suppression field failed, the star was no longer interfered with, and the Light on the ordinary soldiers thinned. He absorbed Solus 969. The memories showed a fortress larger than the Solar System, already aimed. The fortress fired and erased the system, clone included. The real Victor was untouched.[6]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
<Light>, <Darkness>, <Emperor>, and <Law> sit together as the rulers above his Sector. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] would not submit, and the realm he had just measured already sorted incompatible powers by its own absolute rule. He consolidated the Sector, put the map in [[Akasha Elderblood]]'s hands, and started the temporal project. About 200,000 years inside, about one year outside, with Natalia on the Time and the Empresses on the Energy.[2][12][13][14]
The Sector was fortified across those internal millennia. An interrogation beyond the border widened the board again. Powers outside his territory meant to kill him and take his Authorities. The prisoner named <Darkness>, <Emperor>, and <Law>, and a contract killed the captive whenever a forbidden thing was said. Victor rebuilt the captive until the information was gone. The operation belonged to the political order around <Darkness> and the [[Black Eclipse Pantheon]], not to a Veillon attack.[9][15]
Teaching his daughters, Victor described an easier path than killing Death. Become the administrator of the machine and redesign it. He already held half the key. Powers other than <Light>, hearing that, would have been sick with envy. He also set down why no one else had developed <Negativity>. The concepts of <Negativity> and <Positivity> still carry a consciousness from the earlier [[Primordial Chaos]], and they choose their bearer.[16]
A fragment of a light deity later wrapped the soul of [[Ryujin Ikaros]]. Victor would not charge the <Light> faction with it. Light gods are not uncommon, and a descendant can inherit that divinity. It was a fragment of invaders, not a complete possession.[17]
After the first contact with the upper factions, Victor counted the mistakes of a scattered realm. The four great factions each keep a principal galaxy as body or realm. Vharzath's territory is his body. <Light>, <Emperor>, and <Law> did the other thing. They immersed a galaxy in their power. Below their level, that galaxy is a death zone. [[Scathach]] turned back from Vharzath for that reason.[3]
He agreed with Ruby that the advance had to stop. Expansion was over. Elites decide wars between gods who can endanger a galaxy. What he wanted under the royal line were super-elites who were not the family. A smaller area is easier to defend, and he can make the whole of it his realm. He would gather every scattered territory into one supergalaxy, six regions off an Imperial Core, each a supermetropolis of trillions, and he would do it by reshaping what already existed. Creating a galaxy from nothing is the heavy work. Shaping is lighter. Destroying is absurdly easy. He was fit again, and he called it a trifle. He began at once.[3]
Velion was already watching.
Aurethys Vaelthion sat the throne, and Positivity stood before him as a being of pure energy. Aurethys called Victor's rebellion the nature of Positivity's other half. Positivity's answer was that she had always been rebellious. They are one being split in two. Knowledge stayed with him. Instincts went to her. He has always been the more awake.[3]
The dragon did not hide. Aurethys compared him to a blinding lighthouse. Everyone knew the moment he appeared. Only the four faction leaders could finish that god. He was not prey. Positivity named him arrogant, extravagant, provocative, and petty, and the perfect Chosen of Negativity. Aurethys would not call him incapable. In less than thirty minutes Victor had understood the order of the upper sectors and started imposing it. The control was flawless.[3]
The supergalaxy is how those factions keep security. Inside the domain they are omniscient. Nothing escapes them unless a being of the same level intervenes. The important ground is the core. The rest feeds it. A middle sector that crosses into the upper sector has two outcomes, subjugation or death. A subjugated faction loses the territory, the population is enslaved, and the resources are taken. The four great factions are the world powers. The lesser powers are the countries they use.[3]
Positivity saw the method and called it impossible. Victor was using attributeless energy. Aurethys looked harder and agreed. He asked if Negativity had taught it, the way Positivity had taught him. The answer was no. Only Positivity inherited the knowledge of the former self. Victor had learned it alone. Positivity refused the conclusion. This is not ordinary energy. Even the primordials do not know the correct use. Aurethys had been taught every method, and the facts were in front of them.[3]
He will not be the one to spend the first blow. What rightfully belongs to him, he will take back, and he does not have to act for that. <Darkness> and <Emperor> will not watch a rival faction rise. He waits, and lets the others test the enemy. He will not move early only for a seat on the Throne of Reality.[3]

### Skills and Abilities
The headers follow Superpower Wiki terminology. What the realm's machines do is kept apart from what he is.

#### Light Mimicry
- Body of Light: The body on the throne is pure light. The hair is light. The eyes are golden light, constantly bright. There is no flesh and no blood in that form.[3]

#### Positivity
- Chosen: Positivity selected him. The Authority is not taken by training.[1][10][3]
- The Material Half: Positivity is the material, positive, and concrete side of the split of [[Primordial Chaos]]. Negativity is the spiritual, negative, and abstract side.[8][9]
- Not the Primordial: The System lists <Light> apart from Positivity. He represents the aspect. He is not the aspect.[4]
- The Teaching: Positivity kept the knowledge of the former self and taught him. Negativity kept the instincts.[3]

#### Attributeless Energy
- Every Method: Positivity taught him every method of applying attributeless energy. Positivity's word is that even the primordial beings do not know the correct use, and that this energy is not learned in the ordinary way.[3]
- Recognition: He sees [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] using it, confirms his friend's reading, and concludes that the Dragon Emperor learned it alone.[3]

#### Territorial Domain
- The Immersed Galaxy: His principal galaxy is a galaxy immersed in his power and made into his domain. It is not his body. [[Vharzath]]'s territory is the body. Light's method is the one he shares with <Emperor> and <Law>.[3]
- Death Zone: Below his level, that region kills.[3]
- Omniscience: An upper faction holds a supergalaxy so that, inside the domain, nothing escapes the senses. Another being of the same level is the exception.[3]
- Core and Provinces: The important ground is the core of the realm. The rest exists to feed it.[3]

#### Light Manipulation
- The Distributed Light: Soldiers, commanders, and Solus use the civilization's Light to fight, move, reinforce, and regenerate. A lower number among the Solus holds a greater concentration. Destroying the local Pure Positive Energy core thins the Light on the ordinary troops.[5][6]

#### Mind Control
- The Network: Prolonged incorporation into the Positive Energy system suppresses independent thought and action. Deep enough, a being depends on the Light even for basic actions. Violet read the Light-built soldiers as extensions of the Emperor's [[Will Solace|Will]].[6]

#### Clairvoyance
- Seeded Territory: A system seeded with his Positive Energy lets him perceive what happens there through his Divinity.[6]

#### Authority
- Rank 7: <Light> holds Authority Rank 7.[4]
- One of Four: Only the leaders of the four factions are in a position to finish [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. He is one of those four. Victor's name sitting on the highest levels of the System is, by Aurethys's measure, justified.[3]

### Imperial Authority and Resources
- Veillon Military: One frontline fielded tens of thousands, and the number rose as reinforcements arrived.[5][7]
- Primordial-Level Troops: Many ordinary soldiers held raw Power at the broad Primordial God level, with less depth, individuality, and mastery than a naturally developed God of that rank.[5][7]
- Light-Built Life: Numerous soldiers had no recognizable race. Divine Light Energy, and abnormal Souls.[5]
- Divine Technology: The intelligence analyzes an ability during the battle and classifies it. Studied powers are answered with specialized devices. Extreme speed, Space, and Reality manipulation each have a countermeasure. A destroyed suppression pillar was replaced at once.[5]
- Solus: Ranked from 1000 to 1. Lower is stronger. The single digits stand closest to him. A candidate needs the highest Divinity of their Concept and a conquest in the Empire's name. A Grandmaster can be advanced faster. [[Solus 969]] fought the restricted clone with more individuality and more of the Light than the line.[7][6]
- Positive Energy Cores: A sphere of Pure Positive Energy around a star is the heart of a system's network. It feeds the military systems and deepens the population's connection to the Light.[6]
- Fleets and the Star: The ships fight in open space. During the clone's battle the forces tried to destabilize the local star.[5][7][6]
- System Erasure: A structure larger than the Solar System, set far outside it, erased that system with a coordinated volley. The shot belongs to the fortress, not to a blow from the throne.[6]
- The Upper Rule: A middle sector that enters the upper sectors is subjugated or killed. Subjugation strips the territory, enslaves the population, and takes the resources. The original galaxy becomes one of many. The four great factions use the lesser powers as world powers use countries.[3]

### Relationships

#### Positivity
Positivity chose him and remains a separate being. They speak as old friends. Positivity appears before the throne as a being of pure energy.[1][4][3]
The primordial was one being, split in two so the universe would stay in balance. He inherited the knowledge and the thinking of that former self. Negativity inherited the instincts. He taught Aurethys every method of attributeless energy. He has always been more awake than his other half. Her Chosen's arrival in the upper sectors is what wakes her further. His Chosen's arrival is not what woke him.[3]

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is the Chosen of Negativity. Light is the Chosen of Positivity. The opposition is the two halves, not Good against Evil.[1][10]
Their first conflict was the clone. Thousands of soldiers died, Vik-Ir-Loah was inverted into Darkness, the Positive Energy core was destroyed, [[Solus 969]] was absorbed, and the fortress erased the Solar System.[5][7][6]
Aurethys watches the real body from the throne. He credits Victor with understanding the upper sectors in under thirty minutes, with flawless control, and with learning attributeless energy alone. He expects Victor to refuse to surrender authority, because Negativity's temperament is rebellion. He also expects <Darkness> and <Emperor> to answer that rise, and he is willing to learn from their test before he spends himself. A place on the Throne of Reality is not enough to make him rush.[3]

#### Veillon Empire
The realm is the Realm of Light. Its doctrine is the Light. What the Light cannot accept is Evil. The Positive Energy network integrates populations, and many soldiers are built as Light-aligned existences.[5][6][3]

#### Solus
The warrior caste under him, numbered from 1000 to 1. The single digits are the closest. Even 969, far from those digits, was another order of fighter from the ordinary troops.[7][6]

#### The Owner of Limbo
Balance is the one who told [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] that Positivity's Representative was already alive in the Upper Sectors, and that the pair of Representatives mattered to the Balance. He did not hand Victor the outcome.[10]

#### The Judges of The Abyss
They knew him after the selection. The record [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] saw had them discussing his elimination.[1]

#### <Darkness>
<Darkness> is the other method of realm. Vharzath's galaxy is his body. Light's galaxy is a galaxy immersed in his power. Aurethys names <Darkness>, with <Emperor>, as a power that will not sit still while a new faction threatens their place. No alliance between the two thrones is stated.[15][3]
