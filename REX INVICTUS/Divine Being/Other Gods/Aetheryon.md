Aetheryon is the personal name of the ruler the System registers as <Emperor>. He stands ninth in the Authority Ranking, beneath <Darkness> and above <Law>, and two places beneath <Light>. The rank is the System's measure of an understanding of Creation. It is not a list of who would win a fight, and it does not make him an Administrator. He is one of the four rulers of the Upper Sectors. He is not [[Victor Elderblood]], and he is not the Emperor of Light.[1][2]
His faction shows ten chief gods to the world and keeps the rest of its strength behind them. Each of the ten stands for one aspect of justice. [[Auralion]] is the one the [[Elderblood Empire]] has named. Behind that public face he holds a main fleet, smaller factions used to test an enemy, Thrones of his own, and Enforcers. His domain is a galaxy soaked in his power, with a throne at the center of it. The Empire knew him only by the System's codename until his own elite answered him as Emperor Aetheryon.[3][4]
The [[World Trees]] no longer serve him. His planets are dying, and his people have been ordered off them and into space. What remains of his energy is his own divinity. He has still not traded a blow with Victor. When Darkness came for the captured Thrones, he came with him, larger than galaxies, and left without striking.[4][5][6]

### Appearance
His ordinary face has never been shown.
When he speaks with [[Vharzath]], he appears as a gigantic skull of pure fire. The skull talks, smiles when a bargain is closed, and returns without the smile when the bargain's weapon has been erased.[7][8]
In his own galaxy he is a man seated on a throne. He rises from it, gives orders, and pulls a sword free with a flick of his wrist. That man's face is not given.[4]
The shape he uses when he stands with Darkness against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is neither the man nor the skull. He is pure golden energy, larger than galaxies, the embodiment of justice. He does not strike. He turns and leaves.[6]
The image on this page is generated art. It is not a depiction from the novel.

### Personality
He does not spend his seat on a stranger. [[Vharzath]] already knows the habit. The main fleet stays back, and smaller factions go out first, until the enemy is understood. Twenty percent of another man's spoils is not enough to make him the enemy of Negativity's Chosen. Half the spoils, and his fleet moving only when the Abominations move, is a price he will take, and he lets Vharzath choose first what is received. He does not intend to keep the bargain if a better chance appears, and he smiles when it is closed anyway.[7]
The smile ends when <END> is bound to a weapon. He wants the faction that can do that removed, and he wants both sides to spend their real strength. Refused, he does not argue. He goes to act on his own.[8]
He is used to having both his homeland and his best fighters. Forced to choose, he keeps the homeland and spends hundreds of [[World Trees]] to save the fighters. He calls Darkness's retreat from that spell cowardice, and then cannot make his own judges move. He tells the Universal Tree not to interfere. He had expected anger. He had not expected the Tree to side with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. The loss of that support leaves him with his own divinity and with planets that are dying. The wound to his dignity is earlier, and it is the sword, not the Tree. The explosion does not touch him. It tears into his elite.[9][4]
When he stands beside Darkness, the same caution is what stops him. Victor's bearing, the Negativity, and the stench of death do not let him be at ease. He asks himself why a man that far above them does not attack at once. Darkness leaves. His inward word for that leaving is cowardice, and then he leaves as well.[6]

### Synopsis

#### Volume 8: God Emperor Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] asked Azathoth whether a Primordial could be killed. They are pieces of the Universe. Killing one would be an attempt to cut an aspect out of Creation. The supreme throne under that order was empty, and Authority was the road toward the beings who still administered what remained. The more one understands Creation, the more Authority the System recognizes.[1]
The ranking he opened placed an unidentified Rank 0 above the Administrators: <Negativity> and <Positivity>, then <Infinity>, <Death>, <Life>, <Soul>, and <Balance>. Beneath them stood four more names. Light was seventh. Darkness was eighth. Emperor was ninth. Law was tenth. Those four are not the Administrators, and the rank does not give their privileges over the Sectors.[1]
Victor's own designation, <[[Chaos]]>, later rose until it stood at Law's tier. Emperor remained the place above that pair.[2]

#### Volume 9: Those Who Rule Reality
The Empire of Light erased the Solar System [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s clone had entered. That was the proof of what the Upper Sectors could do, and Light was not the only name of that kind. Darkness, Emperor, and Law stood in the same tier. A ruler at that level of understanding would try to control Victor, use him, or remove him. He would not subordinate himself to any of them.[10]
An operative who tried to speak on Darkness, Emperor, and Law was killed, body and Soul, by an existential Soul Contract. Victor restored him and the contract killed him again, 623,596,777,174 times. What remained was an elite servant's incomplete memory, not the ruler's voice. Akasha locked the report to the adult family.[11]
[[The Universal Tree]] told Victor that the Primordial of Balance has a Herald at the head of one of the strongest factions in the Higher Sectors, a faction the other leaders generally do not fight. Victor later identified that faction as <Law>. Law moves only as Balance allows. Aetheryon's faction is one of the three that compete.[12][13]
Justice came for War after [[Vaelthar]] had already restored Darkness's wounded gods. He was faceless, dressed in a suit, sharp-toothed, and the presence he brought sat at the level of a Throne. [[Elarion]] came with him from the Empire of Light. Each said his own patron wanted the Herald. The <BEGIN> that rose with them was Vaelthar's, stained by Darkness, kin to the Emperor's divinity and not a clean copy of it. Justice did not join the attack. When it was over he was still there, in a cold sweat, and he called Black Eclipse one of the weakest and most isolated of the famous factions. He wanted to leave. The warning Victor sent to a leader went to Elarion.[14][15]
The four rulers each keep a principal galaxy. Vharzath made his into his own body. Light, Emperor, and Law soaked a galaxy in their power and made that the domain. Inside it, the ruler knows the territory unless someone of the same level interferes. Aurethys Vaelthion watched Victor fold his worlds into a supergalaxy and stayed where he was. Darkness and Emperor would test the newcomer. Light would watch.[16]
Vharzath reached him after the loss of [[Azrakar]] and the worse loss of Vaelthar. The skull of fire answered. A new player had appeared, and the Upper Sector was supposed to hold four factions. Vharzath offered twenty percent of the spoils if Emperor would strike the Dragon Emperor and draw his attention. The skull refused. Twenty percent was far too little to become the enemy of the Chosen One of Negativity. Vharzath told him to drop the act. He would never open with his main fleet. He was already testing the enemy with smaller factions. The man was already strong enough to contend with the Light, and his realm held more than forty warriors as strong as Emperor's Thrones.[7]
The price Aetheryon accepted was his own. Half the spoils, and his main fleet, when Vharzath sent the Abominations. Vharzath would choose first what he received. The skull smiled and vanished. Neither of them meant to keep it. Vharzath's next order was the creature that price had bought. [[Xytherion]] was sent to deploy the [[Nihilvore]].[7]
Hela erased that creature with <END> fired from a ship. A force of his that had been arming in the confusion asked whether to attack, was told to contact him first, and withdrew. The skull came back without the smile. The faction had to disappear. END is answered only by BEGIN. Vaelthar had held that throne and lost it, and Emperor had no subordinate who could supply another. He told Vharzath to send the Thrones and took his Enforcers as the matching piece. Vharzath refused a blind offensive. The skull left him to that choice.[8]
The tests then had a name. [[Auralion]], one of the ten chief gods, had financed the [[Varkhess League]] when it took three Imperial worlds. The League knew those ten names, each an aspect of justice, and nothing else about the man above them. Kaguya reported the shape of the pressure. Emperor and Darkness were acting against the Empire. Light was making it worse.[3]
Victor let part of that probing happen, then unleashed the Legions on Darkness. Vharzath told Emperor to keep the agreement. He would, but not inside Victor's galaxy. Elites sent into the domain of Negativity's Chosen would be lost. Only he himself could enter it. They would hit the Imperial forces, stay out of that galaxy, and enter it together only after enough of those elites were gone. He would handle his own side in his own way.[13]
Three of his galaxy-level fighters came for the Legions while Kali and Haruna were already fighting two of Darkness's Thrones. [[Scathach]] was there first. [[Laelzorith]] named himself the Law of his Emperor, and a spear was in his hand. [[Seraphel]] named himself Truth. [[Dravakar]] named himself Retribution. She took them for Grandmasters. They fought as a set, each one covering the ground the others left open. She ranked them, with great difficulty on their side, as able to challenge Rose and Eleanor, and as nothing beside Victor. They were too methodical. They lacked his aggression and his flexibility. Dravakar was wounded, and the spear in the wound stopped the regeneration that should have closed it. Seraphel lost an arm. Laelzorith told Dravakar to use their Emperor's power. Gold rose in him, and the healing that should have been instant took crucial seconds. Their Emperor ordered the retreat. They could not move. The spell she cast was Heptagrammakos.[17][9]
He was the most desperate of the rulers watching that spell. Losing the three would cripple his influence, and the darkness of the attack would destroy souls. Darkness pulled its nearby Thrones back. He called that retreat cowardice, rose from his throne, and meant to go to his own. Victor's gaze, from the other galaxy, met him before he left. He read it as a man waiting for an empty homeland. He stayed, and he spent the trump card he knew would anger the primal being of life. Beams of light opened in front of him. Hundreds of [[World Trees]] appeared, empty-eyed, reason gone, and answered, "Yes, my Emperor." He sent them to protect his elite.[4]
The Big Bang Scathach held in that patch of space reduced Laelzorith, Dravakar, and Seraphel to elementary particles. [[The Limbo Guy|The Limbo]] type and Infinity kept the blast from running into other galaxies. Where the three had been, new figures took shape under the Trees. Seven of his elite emerged, each an aspect of justice, and the women facing them were outnumbered five to one. [[Orynthas]], a god of order, held a zone Nyx could have broken if the Emperor had not been holding it up. Eleanor's greatsword broke the zone and impaled him.[4]
Victor's order was to kill them. Aetheryon pulled Scathach, Eleanor, Rose, Kaguya, and Nyx into his galaxy before the blow landed and demanded the retreat he had already given. They answered, "Yes, Emperor Aetheryon." He drew Eleanor's sword out of Orynthas with a flick of his wrist. Orynthas hissed and did not scream. He had time to call the material impressive. The sword detonated. Shrapnel tore through his elite. He was not harmed. His dignity was. He cursed the Dragon Emperor, ordered the wounds treated and a counterattack prepared, and turned on the Universal Tree.[4]
The Tree asked which faction he had attacked. The Chosen Ones of Negativity. Negativity is a Primordial, and its Chosen are treated as Primordial beings. The Tree forbade every World Tree in his territory from helping him and drove them out of his sphere, because he had taken their will and used them as tools. He had expected anger. He had not expected the Tree to side with Victor. The loss cut his strength down to his own divinity. The life went out of his planets. He ordered the evacuation off those worlds and into space.[4]
The cost was counted again after the spell. Hundreds of World Trees in his territory had spent the energy that held their planets, and a planet dies with the tree that fed it. He was then forbidden any contact with a World Tree. Anyone marked by him who approached one lost it. The tree was moved, and the leaders of the highest factions knew where. The trees stood under the Dragon Emperor's protection. His faction had to live in space, on worlds that were dying and could explode. Light did not stay silent at a Primordial taking a side. The Tree's public word was that a rule had been broken and that he was not supporting anyone. The plans he and Darkness had been spending on the conquered ground came to nothing. Victor did not want that ground. He was using it to watch them.[5]
The Limbo type, in that same accounting, had not yet seen the leaders intervene in person, and had not seen Darkness or the Emperor show their full potential. Light had only watched.[5]
When [[Aetherys]] and [[Oryndal]] were taken, Darkness answered in a body of pure darkness, larger than a galaxy. Aetheryon came with him. The form was pure golden energy, larger than galaxies, the embodiment of justice, and what he said was that Victor could not manage it alone, but with him. Victor's answer was that even that was not enough. Hundreds of thousands of eyes opened on the dragon, and the stench of death was there. Both of them growled. Victor said he was always prepared, and asked if they were. Aetheryon asked himself the question Darkness was already asking from the other side. If Victor were that far above them, why did he not attack at once. Darkness said it was not over and vanished. Victor hoped it would stay that way. Aetheryon's inward word was "Coward," and he turned and left as well. Violet said they had not walked into the trap. The trap needed the attack. Neither of them gave it.[6]

### Powers and Abilities
What he uses is the Authority registered as Emperor, the galaxy soaked in his power, a golden strength he can put into a subordinate, the pull that takes a fight into that galaxy, and a colossal body of golden energy. He has not traded blows with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. His full potential is not shown.[1][16][4][6]

#### The Rank
- Ninth: Light is seventh. Darkness is eighth. Law is tenth. <[[Chaos]]> later shares that tenth place and does not move him off the ninth. The number tracks an understanding of Creation. It is not a promise that he beats everyone beneath it, and it does not make him an Administrator.[1][2]

#### The Domain
- A Soaked Galaxy: He immerses a galaxy in his power and makes that the domain. He does not wear the galaxy as a body. That is [[Vharzath]]'s method. A throne sits in his. Inside a domain of that kind, an upper-faction ruler is in essence omniscient, unless a being of the same level intervenes. The region is a death zone for anyone who does not stand at that level. The sense is the domain. It is not the whole universe.[16]
- The Throne: He sits, rises, gives orders, and pulls a sword free with a flick of the wrist. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s gaze, from the other galaxy, meets him when he means to leave. He reads the empty homeland as the invitation and stays. The gaze is not shown binding his limbs.[4]

#### The Skull
- Fire, and a Voice: When he answers [[Vharzath]] he is a gigantic skull of pure fire. It speaks, refuses twenty percent, sets the price at half and the main fleet, smiles when the bargain closes, and comes back without the smile when <END> has erased the [[Nihilvore]]. He is not shown striking in that shape.[7][8]

#### Patron Strength
- Golden Healing: [[Dravakar]] is told to use their Emperor's power. Gold rises in the body. The healing that should have been instant takes crucial seconds. Dravakar's own reading is that even that strength is not enough to close the wound quickly while [[Scathach]]'s spear is in it. The spear is read, on their side, as a mix of divine concepts blocking the regeneration.[9]
- The Zone of Order: [[Orynthas]] holds it. Nyx could break a zone of that kind. She cannot while the Emperor is holding it up. Eleanor's greatsword breaks the territory and impales him. The support does not stop the sword.[4]

#### The Pull
- Into His Galaxy: After the order to kill, he pulls [[Scathach]], Eleanor, Rose, Kaguya, and Nyx out of the attack and into his galaxy before the blow lands. There he requires that a retreat be obeyed at once. His elite answer him by the name Emperor Aetheryon.[4]

#### World Trees
- The Trump Card: Beams of light, then hundreds of [[World Trees]], empty-eyed and without reason, answering yes. He sends them to protect the three. The price is their energy. A World Tree that spends all of it cannot hold its planet, and the planet dies with the tree. [[Scathach]] counts the gift as hundreds of worlds. [[The Universal Tree]] then forbids every World Tree in his territory from helping him and drives them out of his sphere, because their will was taken and they were used as tools. He is forbidden contact. Anyone marked by him who approaches a World Tree loses it. The tree is moved, and those trees stand under the Dragon Emperor. What remains of his energy is his own divinity.[4][5]

#### Colossal Manifestation
- Golden, and Larger than Galaxies: Pure golden energy, the embodiment of justice. He appears with Darkness and says [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] cannot manage it alone, but with him. Victor says even that is not enough. The eyes, the Negativity, and the stench of death do not let him be at ease. He asks himself why a man that far above them does not attack immediately. He does not strike. When Darkness leaves, the inward word is "Coward," and he leaves as well. The form is not called a projection. It is not described as the full potential. The trap fires only if he attacks, and he does not attack.[5][6]

#### The Faction
- What He Spends Instead of Himself: A main fleet he will not open with. Smaller factions, sent to test an enemy. Thrones, which [[Vharzath]] uses as the measure for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s forty-odd galaxy-level fighters. Enforcers, offered against those Thrones and not sent, because the matching offer was refused. Ten chief gods of justice, named in public, with [[Auralion]] the one name taken from the [[Varkhess League]]. Judges who carry Law, Truth, and Retribution in his name. He has no subordinate who can answer <END> with <BEGIN>.[7][8][3][9]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is the Chosen of Negativity and the System's <[[Chaos]]>. Aetheryon stands above the place Chaos later shares with Law, and he has never traded blows with the man. He agreed to test him, then to hit his forces, then to stay out of his galaxy until enough of those elites were gone. Victor's gaze met him as he rose to leave, and he stayed. He later stood in front of that dragon, larger than galaxies, and left without striking. The evacuation was the price of having used the [[World Trees]] against that household.[7][13][4][6]

#### Vharzath
[[Vharzath]] is the eighth name, one place above him, and the ruler he bargains with. The contact was Vharzath's. The price that closed was half the spoils and the main fleet, against the Abominations, with Vharzath choosing first. After END erased the [[Nihilvore]], Aetheryon wanted the Thrones and offered the Enforcers. He was refused, and he left. The later order was narrower. Hit the Imperial armies. Do not send elites into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s galaxy. He called Darkness's retreat from Heptagrammakos cowardice. He still appeared beside him when the Thrones were taken, and the same word, spoken inwardly, was what he gave the second leaving.[7][8][13][4][6]

#### Aurethys Vaelthion
[[Aurethys Vaelthion]] is <Light>, seventh, and the Emperor of Light. That title is not this designation. He expected Darkness and Emperor to test [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] while he watched. No meeting between the two emperors is shown. [[Elarion]] is his envoy. When the [[World Trees]] changed sides, Light did not stay silent.[14][16][5]

#### Law
<Law> is tenth, directly under him on the first list. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] identifies that faction, not Aetheryon's, as the Herald of Balance. Law watched Heptagrammakos and did not step in. The Law [[Laelzorith]] carries is his Emperor's aspect, not that faction.[12][13][17][4]

#### His faction
[[Auralion]] is a chief god, and the financier of the [[Varkhess League]]. Justice is the envoy who came for War, and he is not Auralion. [[Laelzorith]], [[Seraphel]], and [[Dravakar]] are the judges he tried to pull out of [[Scathach]]'s spell, carrying Law, Truth, and Retribution. [[Orynthas]] is a god of order among the elite who emerged under the [[World Trees]]. The ten public chief gods and the fighters named in the war are not given as one roster.[14][3][17][4]

#### The Universal Tree
[[The Universal Tree]] is the primal being of life whose daughters he spent. They bowed to him with nothing left in their eyes. The Tree cast every World Tree in his territory out of his service, forbade him any contact with them, and drained the life from his planets. Its public word was that a rule had been broken. It did not call the act an alliance.[4][5]

#### Vaelthar
[[Vaelthar]] is Darkness's Throne of Origin, not his servant. The BEGIN Vaelthar carried was stained, and it was kin to the Emperor's divinity. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] took him. That loss left both rulers without a counter to END. Aetheryon had no one of his own who could replace it.[14][8]

### Current Status
Alive, and weakened. He still rules his faction, and he still has the galaxy he soaked in his power. The [[World Trees]] will not serve him, and he is forbidden to approach one. His planets are dying, one of them close to exploding, and he has ordered his people off them and into space. His energy has been cut down to his own divinity. His elite were battered in his own galaxy. He has manifested beside Darkness, larger than galaxies, and he left without striking. He has not fought [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] in person. The full potential has not been shown.[4][5][6]
