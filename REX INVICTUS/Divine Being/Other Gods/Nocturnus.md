Nocturnus was a Second-Generation Primordial God of the [[Greek Pantheon]], the secret son of [[Erebus]] and an unnamed Primordial Goddess from another Pantheon, and the first known Divine Being introduced with the Divinity of the Void. Unlike ordinary supernatural beings aligned with one of the two sides of Balance, Nocturnus was born with neither a Positive nor Negative Aspect. This unusual state resulted from Erebus deliberately conceiving him with a Primordial Goddess whose nature belonged to the opposite side of Balance from his own.[2]
Erebus intended Nocturnus' birth as a natural method of interfering with Balance. The attempt failed to destabilize Balance itself, but produced something potentially even more extraordinary: according to Victor Elderblood, Nocturnus possessed the potential to eventually transcend Pantheon-based Godhood and become a true Primordial Entity responsible for the Void of Existence. He never reached that state, remaining a Second-Generation Primordial God until Victor devoured him during the fall of [[Olympus]].[2][1]
Despite never reaching the ultimate state his father envisioned, Nocturnus' Void was among the most dangerous abilities encountered by Victor during the Greek conflict. Space did not function normally within the Void, his attacks could behave as though conventional Speed or Time did not apply to them, and his Void [[Spears]] repeatedly destroyed enormous portions of Victor's Draconic body. Victor consequently identified Nocturnus as the greatest immediate offensive threat among the trio of Nocturnus, Erebus and [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]].[3][4]

### Physical Appearance
Nocturnus was a young-looking man who strongly resembled his father [[Erebus]]. Beneath the mask he initially wore, he possessed pale skin, dark black hair and completely black eyes compared to Darkness itself. The resemblance was so pronounced that he was described as the spitting image of Erebus.[2]
Before formally revealing himself, Nocturnus concealed his face behind a mask and operated as an unidentified man among the ancient forces involved in the Greek conflict. When Erebus finally called his name, Nocturnus crushed the mask with one hand and openly released his Divinity, allowing the gathered Gods to recognize the unfamiliar Concept surrounding him as the Void.[2]

### Personality
Nocturnus was arrogant, dismissive and extremely confident in the rarity of his existence. He expected the revelation of an unknown Second-Generation Primordial God possessing the Void to shock the other Greeks and appeared to anticipate being treated with fear or awe. That confidence collapsed almost immediately when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] analyzed the circumstances of his birth in front of everyone and revealed that Nocturnus had originated as [[Erebus]]' attempt to produce an existence capable of naturally disturbing Balance.[2]
Learning the truth visibly destabilized him. Nocturnus had not understood that his birth was effectively an experiment, and Victor deliberately exploited this ignorance to attack him psychologically before their physical confrontation had properly begun. The revelation made Nocturnus visibly irritated with Erebus, although he ultimately continued obeying his father and joined him against Victor.[2]
He was considerably less composed than the ancient Erebus. Nocturnus became increasingly frustrated whenever Victor survived techniques that should have killed ordinary Gods, eventually shouting in disbelief after repeatedly destroying Victor's body only for the [[Dragon Heart]] to survive and reconstruct him. He was nevertheless capable of adapting tactically rather than attacking completely without thought. After realizing that wasting enormous quantities of Void against Victor's Negative Energy was inefficient, he deliberately compressed his Power into denser attacks and focused upon Victor's Dragon Heart as the most important target.[4]
Nocturnus was also deeply loyal to Erebus during battle. He addressed him as his father, protected him with a Void Barrier when Victor nearly landed a fatal punch, communicated with him telepathically and repeatedly coordinated his attacks around Erebus' Gravity and Darkness. Erebus reciprocated that attachment, immediately saving Nocturnus when [[Scathach]] nearly killed him and later screaming in horror when Victor devoured his son.[4][1]
Despite his arrogance, Nocturnus was capable of genuine fear. [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]]'s killing intent and Grandmaster-level attack terrified him after he realized his Void Barrier could not guarantee his safety, while the evolved Victor reduced him to complete panic once [[Olympus]] stopped supporting his Divine existence. During his final moments, the arrogance that characterized his reveal disappeared entirely as he desperately attempted to escape Victor.[4][1]

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
An unidentified man appeared among the ancient forces observing the escalating destruction surrounding [[Olympus]] while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] fought through the Greek conflict. He remained concealed and was not formally identified at the time, making the purpose of his presence unclear.[5]
Nocturnus' identity was finally exposed after [[Zeus]] and [[Kronos]] had fallen and the surviving powers surrounding Olympus reorganized themselves. [[Erebus]] ordered the masked man to stop playing around and reveal himself. Nocturnus crushed his mask, exposing a face almost identical to Erebus, and openly manifested an unfamiliar Divinity. The Gods immediately recognized the sensation surrounding him as Void.[2]
The revelation horrified Nyx. The armies fighting around Olympus included many children born from Nyx and Erebus, but Nyx immediately knew Nocturnus was not hers. She had already known Erebus betrayed her with another woman, an event that contributed to the collapse of their marriage, but she had never known that the affair produced a child. [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]]' lack of surprise demonstrated that he had already been aware of Nocturnus' existence.[2]
Victor then exposed the deeper reason behind Nocturnus' birth. While [[Diablo]] had attempted to disrupt Balance through artificial methods, Erebus had attempted to produce the same result naturally by conceiving a child with a Primordial Goddess belonging to the opposite side of Balance. Instead of inheriting either parent's polarity, Nocturnus was born without a Positive or Negative Aspect and developed into a neutral Being associated with the Void. Balance itself was not destroyed by his existence, but Victor concluded that the experiment had unintentionally produced a God with the potential to someday become the Primordial Entity responsible for the Void of Existence.[2]
Nocturnus was visibly disturbed by this explanation. He had apparently expected the revelation of his identity to establish him as an exceptional hidden Primordial God, only for Victor to publicly explain that his existence originated from his father's failed attempt to break Balance. Erebus recognized how effectively Victor's words had destabilized his son, while Nocturnus looked toward his father with visible annoyance before returning to the battle.[2]
Victor soon created an enormous sphere of destructive Energy containing enough accumulated power to threaten the Pantheon itself. Nocturnus immediately recognized the danger, moved into position and spoke the command "[[Black Hole]]". A singularity manifested before him and absorbed the entire attack. Victor became fascinated that Nocturnus could casually create what he described as one of the most powerful singularities in existence and began analyzing the mechanics of the Void even while fighting him.[2]
Nocturnus and Erebus subsequently combined their respective authorities. Because conventional Space did not exist within the Void, their attacks did not need to cross normal distance before reaching Victor. Strikes could effectively emerge beside their target rather than traveling conventionally across the battlefield. Their Void-based interaction also allowed them to respond to Victor's attempts to manipulate the local flow of Time, demonstrating that the Void did not obey ordinary spatial-temporal behavior in the same manner as the surrounding Reality.[2]
The battle intensified when Nocturnus, Erebus and Tartarus confronted Victor simultaneously. Erebus flooded the battlefield with Darkness, while Nocturnus' Void became stronger within the environment created by his father. Victor attempted to eliminate Nocturnus with a concentrated violet Dragon Breath, but Nocturnus immediately created another singularity and caused the attack to disappear into it. Victor used the exchange as a distraction to appear behind him, only for Erebus to protect his son with Darkness.[3]
Nocturnus then demonstrated why Victor considered him the most dangerous member of the trio. A Void [[Spears|Spear]] struck Victor's abdomen before he could properly evade it, behaving as though ordinary Speed or Time did not apply to its movement. The attack left an enormous hole through Victor's Draconic body before his regeneration restored the damage. Victor immediately concluded that Nocturnus needed to be eliminated first because he was the only one of the three consistently capable of penetrating his defenses while simultaneously suppressing his movement.[3]
Nocturnus became increasingly aggressive after both he and Erebus concluded that Void was their best weapon against Victor. Rather than continuing to waste enormous quantities of Power, he began concentrating the Void so it could overcome the natural armor created by Victor's Negative Energy. He simultaneously used his Void to seal Victor's Speed, limiting the supernatural acceleration Victor normally relied upon even though the Dragon's base physical speed remained enormous.[4]
Father and son communicated telepathically throughout the confrontation. Nocturnus recognized that forcing Victor to regenerate repeatedly might eventually exhaust him but warned Erebus that they had no way of knowing how long such an attrition strategy would require. Erebus consequently waited for an opening before combining his Darkness with a Lesser spatial Divinity and creating a Gravitational Field exceeding one thousand times Earth's gravity.[4]
The combination became extraordinarily dangerous. Erebus' Gravity restrained Victor while Nocturnus repeatedly manifested Void Spears directly against him. Entire sections of Victor's body disappeared under the attacks, only to regenerate moments later. The assault eventually destroyed virtually everything except Victor's violet [[Dragon Heart]]—the Core of his physical existence. Nocturnus recognized the opportunity and immediately attacked the Heart itself, but the Negativity naturally produced by the organ erased his spear before it could reach the Core.[4]
Rather than abandoning the strategy, Nocturnus attempted to concentrate even more Void into a single attack. Victor regenerated before the technique was complete, forcing Nocturnus to continue the cycle. Another concentrated spear once again eliminated almost the entirety of Victor's body while leaving his Heart behind, driving Nocturnus into visible frustration when the Demon King continued reconstructing himself.[4]
[[Scathach]] finally intervened directly. Nocturnus had deliberately kept track of her because his instincts warned him that she represented an exceptional threat. When he sensed danger from his side, he immediately created a Void Barrier and stopped her spear before it pierced his head. Scathach calmly withdrew her weapon from the invisible defense, shocking Nocturnus because he did not understand how she could interact with the barrier so naturally.[4]
Victor later broke a Void Barrier Nocturnus created to protect Erebus, demonstrating that overwhelming physical power could destroy his defenses. The situation became even worse when Scathach unleashed a Grandmaster attack powerful enough to erase everything along its path. Erebus was forced to abandon his offense and pull Nocturnus away from the attack's trajectory, saving his son's life. Nyx then dragged Erebus away from Victor, disrupting the Gravity-Void combination that had been suppressing him.[4]
The battle ultimately contributed to Victor's transformation into the Primogenitor of the Draconic Vampires. After Victor consumed Zeus and Kronos and gained the authority necessary to impose himself upon Olympus, the [[Primordial Gods]] began losing their connection to the Divine territory that had been empowering them.[1]
Nocturnus suffered severely from that loss. When Victor finally turned his attention toward him, Nocturnus realized that Olympus no longer supplied the Power he had possessed during their previous battle. The dimension was sealed, escape appeared impossible and the newly evolved Victor had become overwhelmingly faster and stronger. Nocturnus panicked and desperately threw Void Spears at him, but without Olympus' support the attacks could not even penetrate the passive layer of Negative Energy surrounding Victor's body.[1]
Victor seized Nocturnus by the head and thanked him. Nocturnus, Erebus and Tartarus had brought Victor closer to genuine death than almost any opponents in his life, and the confrontation had helped force another stage of his evolution. Victor therefore declared that Nocturnus would become his reward and food. Part of Victor's body transformed into a crimson-violet Dragon head that swallowed Nocturnus' entire existence, killing the God of the Void permanently.[1]
Nocturnus' death immediately affected Erebus. His father screamed when Victor consumed him and briefly lost focus on Nyx, allowing her to drive her scythe through Erebus' abdomen and throw him toward Victor. Erebus himself was killed and absorbed only moments later.[1]
Nocturnus' influence did not disappear completely with his death. Victor's ability to consume supernatural Beings allowed the powers and information acquired from defeated enemies to become resources within his own existence. During the development of the Tower of Nightmares, Victor later demonstrated access to Void by creating creatures containing a tiny portion of its properties, specifically using the effect to produce enemies whose presence and attacks were more difficult to perceive through conventional methods.[6]

### Skills and Abilities
Nocturnus was one of the most unusual Divine Beings produced by the [[Greek Pantheon]]. Although classified as a Second-Generation Primordial God, his fundamental nature differed from the other children of the original Primordials because he had been deliberately conceived from parents belonging to opposite sides of Balance. The resulting child inherited neither polarity and instead awakened an unprecedented Divinity connected to the Void.[2]
His existence must be distinguished from a genuine Primordial Entity. Nocturnus possessed the potential to become the Primordial responsible for the Void of Existence, but potential was not accomplishment. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly noted that even extraordinarily talented Gods required immense understanding and development to cross the barrier between merely possessing a Divine Concept and actually becoming that Concept. Nocturnus died before achieving such an evolution.[2]

#### Primordial God Physiology
- Second-Generation Primordial God Physiology: Nocturnus was a naturally born Second-Generation Primordial God and operated on the level necessary to participate in the highest stages of the war for [[Olympus]].[2][3]
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics: He could perceive, react and continue fighting during a battle involving [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], [[Erebus]], [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] and [[Scathach]], where ordinary Gods could scarcely follow the movement of the combatants.[3][4]
- Divine Endurance: Nocturnus continued fighting after Victor's Negative-Energy-coated weapon cut through Erebus' Darkness, one of his singularities and reached Nocturnus himself.[3]
- Primordial [[Divine Energy]]: He possessed sufficient reserves to repeatedly create singularities, Void [[Spears]], barriers and Speed-suppressing effects throughout a prolonged Primordial-level battle.[3][4]
- Flight/Levitation: Nocturnus could freely remain suspended and maneuver in the air during the conflict around Olympus.[2]
- Olympus Empowerment: Nocturnus drew substantial support from Mount Olympus while the [[Greek Pantheon]] remained intact. The disappearance of that connection caused an enormous decline in his combat output.[1]

#### Neutral Existence
- Neutral Aspect: Nocturnus was born without either the Positive or Negative Aspect normally associated with supernatural existence under Balance.[2]
- Absence of Positive Alignment: His fundamental nature did not belong to the Positive side inherited from one parent.[2]
- Absence of Negative Alignment: He likewise did not naturally inherit [[Erebus]]' Negative alignment.[2]
- Void Affinity: This neutral foundation resulted in Nocturnus awakening the rare Concept of Void.[2]
- Balance Experiment: Erebus deliberately created the circumstances of Nocturnus' birth because he hoped the combination of opposite Primordial alignments would naturally disrupt Balance.[2]
- Failed Balance Disruption: Nocturnus' existence did not break Balance in the manner Erebus intended.[2]
- Primordial Potential: The result nevertheless possessed the potential to eventually become the Primordial Entity responsible for the Void of Existence.[2]
- Unrealized Potential: Nocturnus never became the Void itself and should not be indexed as an actual Universal Primordial Entity or Regulator of Existence.[2][1]

#### Void Manipulation
- Void Manipulation: Nocturnus' defining power was his Divinity over the Void, described as the emptiness where conventional Space does not exist and where ordinary spatial-temporal rules cease functioning normally.[2]
- Void Energy Manipulation: He could generate, shape, concentrate and project Void Power according to his [[Will Solace|Will]].[2][3][4]
- Void Generation: Nocturnus could create localized manifestations of the Void inside normal Reality.[2]
- Void Shaping: His Power could be formed into singularities, spears, barriers and other concentrated constructs.[2][4]
- Void Projection: Nocturnus could attack targets at range without requiring conventional physical contact.[3][4]
- Power Concentration: When uncontrolled quantities of Void proved inefficient against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Negative Energy armor, Nocturnus deliberately compressed the same Power into smaller and denser attacks to improve penetration.[4]
- Void Amplification (via [[Erebus]]' Darkness): Nocturnus' Divinity became stronger while operating inside the supernatural Darkness generated by Erebus.[3]

#### Black Hole Creation
- [[Black Hole]] Creation: Nocturnus could create singularities through his Void Divinity with extraordinary ease.[2]
- Singularity Generation: By speaking the command "Black Hole", Nocturnus immediately manifested a singularity before himself.[2]
- Attack Absorption: The Black Hole absorbed [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s enormous Energy sphere instead of conventionally enduring or opposing the attack.[2]
- Extreme Absorption: Victor assessed the Energy contained in the absorbed attack as sufficient to potentially destroy the Pantheon approximately twice over, yet Nocturnus' singularity removed the attack from the battlefield.[2]
- Defensive Singularity: He could create singularities directly between himself and incoming attacks as defensive counters.[2][3]
- Dragon Breath Nullification: Nocturnus created another singularity against Victor's concentrated violet Dragon Breath and caused the attack to disappear.[3]
- Rapid Activation: His singularities could be manifested quickly enough to counter large attacks after they had already been launched.[2]
- Destructible Singularity: His Black Holes were not invulnerable. Victor's Greatsword coated in concentrated Negative Energy sliced through [[Erebus]]' Darkness and Nocturnus' singularity during their battle.[3]

#### Spatial Bypass
- Spatial Bypass: Ordinary Space did not function inside Nocturnus' Void, allowing attacks transmitted through it to bypass conventional distance.[2]
- Distance Negation: Attacks did not need to physically travel across the intervening area between Nocturnus and a target when routed through the Void.[2]
- Attack Manifestation: Void-routed attacks could effectively appear beside their target rather than visibly approaching from their original position.[2][4]
- Pseudo-Teleportation: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] initially compared the phenomenon to teleportation but identified an important difference: Nocturnus was not simply moving something rapidly through Space; the attack traveled through a state in which ordinary Space did not exist.[2]
- Reaction Bypass: The lack of conventional travel made these attacks exceptionally difficult to anticipate or evade through ordinary movement and reaction speed.[2]
- Physical Attack Routing: Nocturnus and [[Erebus]] could combine their abilities so attacks emerged through the Void without needing to cross normal spatial distance.[2]

#### Speed Nullification
- Speed Nullification: Nocturnus could use his Void Power to seal or suppress [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s supernatural Speed.[4]
- Supernatural Speed Suppression: Victor temporarily lost access to the full supernatural acceleration normally available to him while Nocturnus maintained the effect.[4]
- Conceptual Interference: The suppression originated from the unusual properties of Void rather than from conventional slowing of Victor's body.[2][4]
- Partial Suppression: The ability did not render Victor physically motionless. His natural Draconic body remained extraordinarily fast even while his enhanced Speed was sealed.[4]
- Gravity-Void Restraint: The technique became significantly more dangerous when combined with [[Erebus]]' Gravitational Field, suppressing Victor's supernatural Speed while extreme Gravity restricted the movement of his physical body.[4]

#### Temporal Bypass
- Temporal Bypass: The Void could operate in ways that ignored conventional temporal behavior. Nocturnus' Void-based attacks were perceived by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] as though the Concepts of Speed or Time did not apply to their travel.[3]
- Time-Slow Counterplay: Nocturnus remained capable of participating in attacks through the Void even while Victor manipulated the surrounding flow of Time.[2]
- Void-Time Independence: This effect originated from the nature of the Void rather than a separate Divinity of Time.[2]
- No Independent Time Divinity: Nocturnus is never demonstrated possessing Time as an independent Divine Concept; his temporal interactions are applications or properties of Void.[2][3]

#### Void Spears
- Void [[Spears|Spear]] Creation: Nocturnus could condense Void Power into spear-shaped attacks capable of inflicting catastrophic damage against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Draconic body.[3][4]
- Energy Weapon Creation: The spears were directly formed from concentrated Void Power.[3]
- Ranged Projection: Nocturnus could repeatedly launch Void Spears from a distance.[4]
- Spatial Bypass: A thrown Void Spear could appear directly at its target without visibly crossing the intervening Space.[4]
- Speed Bypass: Victor described the attack that pierced him as behaving as though it ignored the Concept of Speed.[3]
- Temporal Bypass: The same attack appeared to disregard ordinary Time during its travel.[3]
- Draconic Durability Penetration: A Void Spear pierced through Victor's abdomen despite the durability of his Dragon body.[3]
- Massive Body Destruction: Successive Void Spears erased or destroyed enormous sections of Victor's body, including his arms, legs and abdomen.[4]
- Near-Total Body Destruction: Nocturnus' barrage eventually reduced Victor until essentially only his [[Dragon Heart]] remained.[4]
- Concentrated Void Spear: Nocturnus could concentrate additional Void into one attack when ordinary spears failed to penetrate a specific defense.[4]
- Core Targeting: After realizing Victor could regenerate from catastrophic bodily destruction, Nocturnus deliberately began targeting the exposed Dragon Heart in an attempt to destroy Victor's Core.[4]
- Negative Energy Counter: The Negativity naturally produced by Victor's Dragon Heart erased a Void Spear before it could make contact, forcing Nocturnus to attempt greater concentration.[4]

#### Void Barrier
- Void Barrier: Nocturnus could condense Void into powerful defensive barriers.[4]
- Instantaneous Defense: He rapidly placed a Void Barrier between [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and [[Erebus]] when Victor was moments away from punching his father's head.[4]
- Protection of Others: His defensive constructs could be projected around another person rather than being restricted to himself.[4]
- Weapon Blocking: Nocturnus generated another barrier quickly enough to intercept [[Scathach]]'s spear before it pierced his head.[4]
- Interaction Resistance: Nocturnus was visibly shocked when Scathach calmly interacted with and withdrew her weapon from his Void defense, indicating that most opponents could not casually treat the barrier as ordinary physical matter.[4]
- Breakable Defense: Victor's physical strike completely shattered a barrier protecting Erebus, proving that overwhelming force could destroy the construct.[4]

#### Void Destruction
- Void Destruction: Nocturnus' Void could cause physical matter struck by his attacks to disappear rather than merely inflicting conventional blunt or cutting trauma.[3][4]
- Body Removal: His first successful Void [[Spears|Spear]] left a large hole through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s abdomen.[3]
- Partial Body Destruction: Later spears removed limbs and significant portions of Victor's torso.[4]
- Near-Total Physical Destruction: His repeated barrage eventually removed virtually Victor's entire physical body except for his surviving [[Dragon Heart]].[4]
- Regeneration Counter Strategy: Nocturnus understood that destroying Victor's external body accomplished little while the Heart remained intact and consequently attempted to destroy the Core directly.[4]
- Limit: These feats should not automatically be indexed as unrestricted Conceptual Erasure. Nocturnus demonstrated extreme destructive effects through Void, but Victor's Core and sufficiently dense Negativity directly resisted those attacks.[4]

#### Void-Darkness Synergy
- Void-Darkness Synergy: Nocturnus' Void possessed strong supernatural compatibility with [[Erebus]]' Darkness.[2][3]
- Void Empowerment: Erebus' large-scale Darkness increased the effectiveness of Nocturnus' Void Divinity.[3]
- Concealment Combination: Erebus could obscure the battlefield while Nocturnus generated singularities and Void attacks from within that environment.[3]
- Surprise Attack Combination: Darkness obscured perception while the Void eliminated conventional spatial travel, making attacks extremely difficult to predict.[2][3]
- Gravity-Void Combination: Erebus' Gravity physically restrained [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] while Nocturnus' Void [[Spears]] repeatedly targeted his vital regions.[4]
- Father-Son Coordination: Their compatible powers allowed them to operate as a significantly greater threat together than either ability would have represented independently.[3][4]

#### Telepathy
- Telepathy: Nocturnus could communicate mentally with [[Erebus]] during combat.[4]
- Mental Communication: Father and son exchanged complete tactical instructions without speaking aloud.[4]
- Silent Battle Coordination: They used this communication while simultaneously maintaining Void, Darkness and Gravity techniques against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[4]

#### Extrasensory Perception
- Supernatural Perception: Nocturnus possessed senses capable of following combat between high-level Divine and Primordial-class entities.[2][4]
- Energy Perception: He immediately recognized the enormous destructive potential contained within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Energy sphere and moved to intercept it before detonation.[2]
- Threat Detection: Nocturnus sensed danger approaching from his side before [[Scathach]]'s spear struck and generated a barrier quickly enough to save himself.[4]
- High-Speed Perception: He could perceive and respond while fighting Victor despite the latter's exceptional speed.[3][4]
- Void-Assisted Temporal Perception: Nocturnus remained capable of acting through the Void during altered temporal conditions that interfered with conventional movement.[2]

#### Combat Analysis
- Combat Analysis: Nocturnus demonstrated a strong ability to evaluate his own Power during battle despite his arrogance and relative youth compared with [[Erebus]].[4]
- Efficiency Analysis: He recognized that simply releasing more Void was an inefficient method of overcoming [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Negative Energy defenses.[4]
- Power Compression: He changed strategies and concentrated his Power into denser attacks specifically intended to penetrate Victor's armor.[4]
- Weak-Point Identification: After repeatedly destroying Victor's body without killing him, Nocturnus recognized the [[Dragon Heart]] as the critical target.[4]
- Regeneration Analysis: He understood that external bodily destruction was strategically meaningless if Victor could continually regenerate around an intact Core.[4]
- Attrition Analysis: Nocturnus reasoned that repeatedly forcing Victor to regenerate could eventually exhaust him, although he admitted that neither he nor Erebus knew how much endurance Victor possessed.[4]
- Tactical Communication: He communicated these conclusions directly to Erebus and coordinated their finishing attempts accordingly.[4]

#### Potential Primordial Evolution
- Potential Primordial Entity Evolution: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] stated that Nocturnus possessed the potential to eventually transcend his state as a Pantheon God and become the genuine Primordial associated with the Void of Existence.[2]
- Potential Concept Embodiment: Reaching that stage would require more than merely possessing a Void Divinity; Nocturnus would have needed to cross the barrier separating a God who wields a Concept from a Being that has become that Concept.[2]
- Potential Void Primordial: Victor specifically identified his possible future state as the Primordial responsible for the Void of Existence.[2]
- Extraordinary Potential: Victor considered Nocturnus' birth a failure at [[Erebus]]' original objective but an extraordinary success in another respect because it created a Being capable of theoretically reaching a previously vacant Primordial position.[2]
- Unachieved: Nocturnus never completed this evolution and died as a Second-Generation Primordial God.[1]

#### Olympus Empowerment
- Pantheon Empowerment: Nocturnus received a major increase in effective power from his connection to [[Olympus|Mount Olympus]] while the Pantheon continued recognizing and supporting him.[1]
- Enhanced Void Output: With Olympus' support, his Void [[Spears]] repeatedly penetrated [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Draconic body and could destroy almost all of it.[3][4]
- Enhanced Suppression: His Void could seal Victor's supernatural Speed and function effectively alongside [[Erebus]]' Primordial abilities.[4]
- Loss of Support: After Victor began taking control of Olympus, Nocturnus completely lost the Pantheon's supernatural backing.[1]
- Extreme Power Reduction: Nocturnus explicitly could no longer wield the level of Power he possessed during the earlier battle.[1]
- Loss of Penetration: Void Spears that had previously torn through Victor could no longer penetrate even the passive Negative Energy Barrier surrounding his evolved body.[1]

#### Resistances
- Spatial Manipulation Resistance/Counterplay: Nocturnus' defining Divinity operated through a state in which conventional Space did not exist, allowing him to bypass ordinary spatial restrictions and providing natural counterplay against effects dependent entirely upon normal Space.[2]
- Temporal Manipulation Resistance/Counterplay (via Void): Void-based techniques continued functioning through conditions in which ordinary Time had been manipulated, allowing Nocturnus to participate in attacks that did not obey normal temporal travel.[2]
- Speed Manipulation Counterplay (via Void): Nocturnus' attacks could disregard ordinary Speed and he could suppress [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s own supernatural Speed through his Divinity.[3][4]
- Void Tolerance: Nocturnus could create and manipulate singularities, barriers and concentrated Void directly around himself without suffering from his own manifestations.[2][4]

#### Limitations and Weaknesses
- Negative Energy: Negative Energy was the most consistently demonstrated counter to Nocturnus' Void. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Negative-Energy-coated weapon could cut through a singularity, while sufficiently dense Negativity around his [[Dragon Heart]] erased incoming Void [[Spears]].[3][4]
- Power Concentration Requirement: Extremely powerful Negative Energy defenses could not be overcome efficiently by simply increasing the quantity of Void. Nocturnus needed to compress his Power into denser attacks.[4]
- Void Barriers Are Breakable: Victor shattered a Void Barrier through overwhelming physical force.[4]
- Singularities Are Not Invulnerable: Victor's Negative-Energy-covered Greatsword sliced through one of Nocturnus' singularities.[3]
- Pantheon Dependency: A substantial portion of Nocturnus' demonstrated combat output depended upon [[Olympus]]. Losing its support reduced him from a Being capable of repeatedly destroying Victor's body to someone whose Void Spears could not penetrate Victor's passive defense.[1]
- Inexperience: Nocturnus possessed an extraordinary Divinity but did not possess the composure or experience demonstrated by ancient combatants such as [[Erebus]] or [[Scathach]]. Victor repeatedly exploited his emotions and forced him into increasingly desperate decisions.[2][4]
- Emotional Instability: Learning that his existence originated as Erebus' experiment visibly disturbed him, while Victor's regeneration progressively drove him into frustration and panic.[2][4]
- Not the Void Itself: Nocturnus possessed the Divinity and potential associated with Void but never became its universal embodiment. Claims or abilities belonging to a hypothetical completed Primordial Entity of Void should therefore not automatically be assigned to Nocturnus.[2]

#### Legacy
- Consumed by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]: Victor devoured Nocturnus' entire existence after conquering [[Olympus]].[1]
- Void Retention: Victor subsequently demonstrated the ability to employ the properties of Void, including incorporating a small amount of it into creatures generated by the Tower of Nightmares.[6]
- Tower of Nightmares Application: A creature inside the Tower was deliberately given a tiny amount of Void so its characteristics could bypass the type of perception upon which Sasha had been relying, demonstrating that Nocturnus' former power had become part of the resources Victor could reproduce after consuming him.[6]
- Distinct from Later Void Development: Techniques Victor later developed using Void should not automatically be retroactively credited to Nocturnus. Only applications Nocturnus personally demonstrated or that the story explicitly attributes to his Void belong in Nocturnus' ability index.

### Trivia
- Nocturnus is the first named God in the series explicitly demonstrated possessing a natural Divinity of Void.[2]
- His true appearance strongly resembles [[Erebus]], with the narration describing him as the spitting image of his father.[2]
- Nocturnus' existence was hidden so effectively that even Nyx, Erebus' former wife and one of the original Greek [[Primordial Gods]], did not know Erebus had fathered him.[2]
- [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]] was not surprised by Nocturnus' reveal, indicating that he already knew about the secret child before the Battle of [[Olympus]].[2]
- Nocturnus himself did not appear to understand the true purpose behind his birth. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] revealing that he originated as an experiment to disrupt Balance visibly shook him.[2]
- Erebus' original experiment technically failed because Nocturnus did not break Balance, but Victor considered the result extraordinary because Nocturnus possessed the potential to become a genuine Primordial Entity of Void.[2]
- Potential is not equivalent to achieved status. Nocturnus remained a Second-Generation Primordial God throughout his life and was never the universal embodiment or source of Void.[2][1]
- Victor immediately identified Nocturnus as the highest-priority target among Erebus, Tartarus and Nocturnus once the God of the Void demonstrated that his attacks could consistently damage and suppress him.[3]
- Nocturnus came extremely close to destroying Victor's physical body completely several times during Chapter 801, but the [[Dragon Heart]] survived every attempt and continuously regenerated him.[4]
- [[Scathach]] nearly killed Nocturnus with a Grandmaster-level spear attack. Erebus was forced to save his son from the attack's path.[4]
- Nocturnus' death caused Erebus to lose concentration in his own battle, creating the opening Nyx used immediately before Erebus was killed by Victor.[1]
- Unlike Erebus, Victor did not extract information from Nocturnus before killing him. Nocturnus was swallowed directly by the crimson-violet Dragon manifestation created from Victor's body.[1]
- Later use of Void by Victor confirms that Nocturnus' power remained relevant after his death, but Nocturnus himself remained permanently deceased.[6]
