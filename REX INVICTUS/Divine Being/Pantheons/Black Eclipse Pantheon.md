The Black Eclipse Pantheon, also called Black Eclipse, is a Divine pantheon of the Upper Sectors, ruled by <Darkness>, the Primordial God of Darkness. Victor Elderblood addresses that ruler as Vharzath. He sits at the eighth recorded place on the System's Authority Ranking. The pantheon is one of the four largest factions of the Upper Sectors. Its territories cover hundreds of galaxies. Beneath the ruler stand the [[Seven Black Thrones]], then the Ten Chief Gods, Greater Gods, Lesser Gods, and the servants.[1][2][3]
The shape under him is now named. [[Vaelthar]] was the Throne of Origin, made as a possible Chosen One of Negativity and not chosen, and he is the loss Vharzath cannot replace. [[Xytherion]] is the Throne of the End, the deputy, and the most trusted. [[Aetherys]] holds Time, [[Oryndal]] Space, [[Elyndra]] Reality, [[Nharzuel]] Existence, and [[Zhaeroth]] [[Chaos]]. The ten Chief Gods are bestowed, not won. Their titles are [[Azrakar]], [[Kaelith]], [[Nerath]], [[Vhalor]], [[Khariel]], [[Morvane]], [[Azelith]], [[Nihrael]], [[Draveth]], and [[Vaelzara]]. A successor already wears the void. Nine of the old generation sit with Vaelthar in Lily's collection, emptied, and the bodies are ordered burned.[4][5]
The war has moved past the first battle. That battle was lost to a projection. The invasion that followed took the transit routes, the supply stations, and then, within days, the depth of the conquest itself. Vharzath's own body is the principal galaxy, and the fleets were turned back from it. What he would not spend at the start, he spends when the alternative is a single galaxy left. Two Thrones are sent, and told to put their own safety first. He still does not take the field.[6][7][8]

### Overview
Black Eclipse is an intergalactic civilization, not a circle of Gods over one world. One sovereign rules hundreds of galaxies. The civilization has its own hierarchy, armies, and living vessels. Beneath the Gods, the population is classed as servants. The lower and middle ranks include soldiers built like organic machines, many of them with little thought of their own. [[Azrakar]]'s transport was a living organism, not a mechanical ship. The technology the later campaign runs into is the same marriage. Biology and engineering, joined into one macabre mix.[2][3][9][8]
The faction also works by fragments. Gods send pieces of Concepts into a Sector that armies cannot yet cross. The fragments look for hosts, fasten to Souls, and open a foothold. That is how Black Eclipse enters [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s protected Sector. [[Niklaus Horseman]] serves the [[Vharzath|Great Darkness]] directly and warns them that an open invasion can destroy the pantheon. The ruler listens. Several of the Gods beneath him do not.[10][11]
Its age is one of the reasons those Gods are sure of themselves. The civilization is estimated at more than two million years. The ruler's followers say he already held power before the Middle Sectors were set in their present shape. Victor's later judgment of the leaders who refuse his bait is separate from that count. They would not have lasted eons, he thinks, if they were fools.[3][11][6]
Most of its Gods hold Concepts on the negative side of Existence. The early Imperial report ties Darkness, Night, Void, Silence, Shadows, Death, Fear, Eclipse, and Oblivion to the pantheon. The ten offices extracted later are narrower, and they are not the same list. Emptiness, night, silence, shadow, soul, fear, darkness, oblivion, inevitability, and [[Vaelthar]] himself. Death and Eclipse are not among those ten titles.[3][5]

#### Territory
The domain is hundreds of galaxies in the Upper Sectors. Some galaxies there reach about 10,000 times the Milky Way, and Ruby's scanners are already finding larger. When War's battle starts to wreck the surrounding systems, the [[Vharzath|Great Darkness]] lays his Authority over those Solar Systems and galaxies and stops their motion. That reach is the territory he governs. It is not a claim on every body in the Cosmos.[2][12][7]
The galaxies are not equal. The ones around the core are conquests. The principal galaxy is the base, sunk in the deepest darkness, without stars to a mortal eye. [[Scathach]] recognizes it for what it is. The galaxy is Vharzath's body. A leader of this class stands on a level of his own, as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] does. Any force but the Emperor's, sent into that place, is extinguished. Entering it is the same act as an enemy trying to step onto the planet that lies inside Victor's Soul. She turns the fleets back. Azathoth might deal with him. Jeanne might, if she grows into what she was. No one on that campaign is sent in to try.[7]
The capital and the borders stay unnamed. The ruler presides from a throne, with a council beneath him. During the first personal attack, Victor isolates the region. Vharzath can feel his planets being hit from every side and cannot send relief or call the army back. The later offensive does not need that isolation. It takes the roads.[11][13][7][8]

#### Great Darkness
<Darkness> is the Primordial God of Darkness and the Overlord of the pantheon. Rank 8 on the System list. His order outranks a Chief God. [[Azrakar]] accepts [[Kaelith]] on his battlefield once he is told the order is the ruler's. Niklaus's warning is the reason the first answer to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Sector is caution, not an immediate invasion. The leader had always decided alone. That he listens to this servant is, to Azrakar, a thing that had not happened before.[1][3][11][9]
When War's fight begins to tear the neighboring galaxies, Darkness stops the stars himself. Pure Darkness spreads from his body and locks the affected systems. He gives the dead Souls of that battle to Soul, so Equilibrium and a Judge of the Abyss do not step in. The Souls would otherwise have returned to him. He spends them to keep the [[Primordial Entities]] out of a war between Gods. Individual deaths do not move him later, for the same reason. The souls come back. Losing the conquests themselves is the thing he will not accept.[12][8]
After Azrakar is erased, the order changes. The Horsemen are to be brought in, all of them, alive or dead. One of the Thrones answers and calls the remaining Chief Gods. Victor names Vharzath and declares extinction. The planets are already under attack. Vharzath senses more than twenty elite women, each at Throne level or beyond, and the isolation stops both rescue and recall. He does not come out. Victor reads the refusal as sense.[14][13][6]
The man read afterward, out of [[Vaelthar]]'s memories, is cautious, a born leader, and arrogant. He does not sit through losses without end. The campaign proves the reading. He withholds the Thrones while he tries to understand the Dragon Emperor. When understanding fails and the map is coming apart, he spends two of them, and still tells them to live.[4][8][5]

#### Seven Black Thrones
Main article: [[Seven Black Thrones]]
Seven Gods, directly under the [[Vharzath|Great Darkness]], above the Ten Chief Gods. They are the only fighters of the pantheon who threaten a galaxy in their own right. The Empire's count, set against them, is more than forty, and higher still if [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters are included. The first report of their strength came from a subordinate God's memory and put them near Darkness himself. That was not a measurement taken on the field. The field later showed one of them, and the projection broke him.[3][7]
[[Vaelthar]], Throne of Origin, was made to be a Chosen One of Negativity. The making succeeded. The choice did not. A portion of Vharzath's power is in him, and the <BEGIN> he carries is stained by that Darkness. [[Kaelith]] greets him as Great Origin. He is the greatest loss of the war. Territory can go. A Chief God who served for millennia can die, and another can be raised. This seat cannot. He is emptied in Lily's Hell and ordered burned.[15][4][5]
[[Xytherion]], Throne of the End, is the deputy, the supreme commander, and the most trusted, set beside [[Scathach]] and Violet. He wears black armor with red accents and looks like a medieval warrior. He deploys the [[Nihilvore]]. [[Aetherys]] regulates the time of the conquered galaxies. [[Oryndal]] is the bridge of distance and dimension, and often works with him. [[Elyndra]] maintains the reality of the hostile galaxies. [[Nharzuel]] embodies being: beings, objects, worlds, and concepts. [[Zhaeroth]] is charged with everything beneath Darkness, and for that was considered a right hand. He is not the deputy. The prisoner knew less of him than of any other seat. Read together, the seven are the organs of a universe. Life, death, and infinity are the functions still absent.[4][5]
[[Nerath]] is held in the highest esteem by the seven. Esteem is not a seat. Justice has a Throne-level presence and belongs to <Emperor>. [[Elarion]] serves the Empire of Light. Neither is one of these seven.[12][13]

#### Greater Gods, Lesser Gods, and Servants
Greater Gods sit under the commanders. Lesser Gods sit under them and above the servants. Neither class is named person by person. The servants are the population and the armies, including the organic-machine soldiers. Niklaus is a servant of the ruler by station, not a Chief God, and [[Azrakar]] cannot order him. His access outranks the void.[3][9]

### History

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Black Eclipse is already one of the four great factions when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Sector comes to its attention. The civilization is put at more than two million years. The first Imperial report is held at security level G-7. Fragments of foreign Concepts are sent into the Sector to find hosts. Niklaus, already wrapped in Darkness, warns the throne room that Victor can end the pantheon. The Great Darkness takes the warning. The hunt does not stop. What they want includes the Sector tied to <[[Chaos]]>, and the growth that would come from devouring it. Niklaus tells that court the man will destroy them and eat the faction. They laugh. The traps go on.[2][3][10][11]
Victor sends War, with [[Gol-goroth]], into the Upper Sectors. War hits [[Azrakar]]'s living vessel and kills the assistant who was reporting the fragments. The duel wrecks star systems and reaches neighboring galaxies. [[Kaelith]] joins on the ruler's order. [[Nerath]] and [[Vhalor]] follow. Azrakar counts four of the ten on the field and says the Thrones hold Nerath in the highest esteem. The Great Silence wants War alive. Azrakar accepts. The four Horsemen then erase him. Darkness, displeased, orders the Horsemen taken alive or dead. A Throne answers and calls the rest of the Chief Gods. War does not give the three survivors the pause. Their execution is set when the second wave breaks Gol-goroth's domain. War drops the stroke. [[Vaelthar]] comes through.[9][12][14][15]
What stands with the wounded three is counted as ten. Nine are galactic-level threats. Vaelthar is the Throne in that count. The nine are not the other six seats, and they are not a list of which Chief God offices are empty. He restores Kaelith, Nerath, and Vhalor with a beam of pure darkness and tells them they have disappointed the Great Darkness. They bow. Justice and [[Elarion]] arrive for patrons of their own. The <BEGIN> that flares is Vaelthar's, kin to the Emperor's, and corrupted. Victor appears holding the galaxy, kills the restored Kaelith by the Soul, names Vharzath, and declares extinction. Vaelthar puts their chance of living through him under one percent and orders the attack anyway.[13]
The attack is spent on a projection. Star-breaking beams, dimensional spears, a sword caught in two fingers, restraints, a domain used as a prison, a wave, a dimensional weapon, and a chain meant for the Soul all fail. [[Junketsu]] halves what stands in front of it. Nerath judges that they cannot win, and that only the Great Darkness can keep up with him. "Stand firm!" closes the wounds, including Vaelthar's lost arm. A foot comes down on his chest. The sword stops at his head. The only mark on the projection is a crack in the gauntlet. Victor tells them the true body is the eye of the dragon, still holding the galaxy. The living are ordered taken. The remains are to be collected. Vharzath has not come. The dragon lets the galaxy go.[6]

#### The Roads
The invasion opens on the sectors nearest the territory, with priority on the systems that control transit and supply. John finds the first concentration: ships around a route, armed platforms, surveillance stations. Black Eclipse sorties to kill the transports and is ordered back before the squadrons are encircled. The Empire keeps the approaches. Kaleb turns the result into the next station, a maintenance and supply base in a neighboring system. Defended worlds are fired on until the planet explodes. Worlds without a defense are marked for conquest, and for the experiment of bringing a people off this rule and onto the Emperor's. It is a battlefield without super-elites. John's First Legion takes its piece of it. [[Scathach]], above five meters and in armor, commands the whole, with only the First and the Second committed. Those two alone are more than a trillion soldiers. The other legions stay home.[7]
The principal objective is the dark galaxy. She looks at it and turns the fleets around. That place is [[Vharzath]]. She will not spend an army on his body. The objective is postponed. The secondary one is the conquests around it. She means to take galaxies on the way, and to hunt a Throne. The other factions have not moved yet. She expects them to. Black Eclipse, by Violet's count, outnumbers the Empire three to one. The Empire's lack is the low and middle ranks. The pantheon's lack is the top. Seven genuine galaxy-level beings, against more than forty.[7]

#### The Bargain and the Abomination
[[Vharzath]] regrets having sent [[Vaelthar]]. The offensive is eating his forces, and the enemy has not shown his true strength. Passivity displeases him even when the dead do not. He contacts <Emperor>. Twenty percent of the spoils, for a diversion. The skull refuses. Twenty percent is too little to become the enemy of the Chosen One of Negativity, and the skull will not send his main fleet while smaller factions can do the testing. The price that closes it is half, the skull choosing first, against the Abominations. Neither means to keep the bargain if a better chance appears. What is actually spent, through [[Xytherion]], is the [[Nihilvore]].[4]
The weapon is a shapeless black body, organic plates over warped metal, dark tissue moving between them. Hela erases it with <END> bound to a cannon. The skull says the faction must disappear. END is answered only by BEGIN. The Throne who carried BEGIN is already gone, and <Emperor> has no subordinate who can supply another. He demands the Thrones against his Enforcers. Vharzath refuses a blind offensive while the enemy may still have secrets. Xytherion, who has never seen that expression on the Great Darkness, is not given the field. A new [[Azrakar]] has already been raised. He is not the God who died. Nine more still have to be made.[4][16]

#### One Galaxy
The refusal does not hold the map. Within days the legions are loose across the territories. The Second and the Third are each given a galaxy, one on the defenses and one on the communications, so the fleets cannot be gathered. The Fourth stays on the research, under Ruby and Velnorah, and the equipment coming back is the organic-machine mix. Outposts fall or are abandoned. Supply lines break. If he masses in one place, another legion walks into the place he left. Everything conquered over the years is coming apart. He can bear the dead. He cannot bear being left with one galaxy.[8]
He releases one elite unit.
"Go, my thrones, and put your own safety first."
Two answer. They leave. They are not named. He then tells the skull to keep the agreement. The answer is that he will, but not in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s galaxy. Elites sent there are dead or taken. Only the skull himself could enter the domain of the Chosen of Negativity. What he offers instead is the hunt outside it, and the galaxy afterward, once enough of the elites are gone. [[Vharzath]] will handle his own war in his own way. The skull says he will wait.[8]
What Lily takes from the prisoners arrives in the same war. [[Vaelthar]] and nine Chief Gods are works in her collection. Memories, blood, existence, and soul are gone. The bodies, once Ruby has her samples, are ordered destroyed by dragonfire. The upper command learns the seven names and the ten titles in the time it takes Akasha to pass a report. Violet, reading the ruler through the Throne's memories, tells [[Scathach]] to be ready. The elites are coming. Two of them have already been told to go, and to live.[8][5]

### Relationship with the Elderblood Empire
Black Eclipse is an enemy of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. The fragments in Imperial territory are the open act. The prize includes the Sector tied to <[[Chaos]]>, and the power around [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], to use against the other Upper-Sector factions. The ages do not match. The pantheon has the millions of years, the hundreds of galaxies, and three times the Empire's numbers in the ranks that fill a fleet. The Empire has the individuals. A projection erases a Chief God, kills another, and breaks a Throne. Justice, who is not of this pantheon, calls it one of the weakest and most isolated of the famous factions. Victor accepts that and says it will soon be gone.[2][3][11][6][7]
The bargain with <Emperor> is the pantheon's attempt to borrow a second faction against that imbalance. Half the spoils, against the Abominations. The [[Nihilvore]] is what was paid. The main fleet does not come. After END erases the weapon, the skull wants the Thrones and is refused, then agrees to hunt outside Victor's galaxy and wait. He will not send his elites into the domain of the Chosen of Negativity. Only he himself could enter it. [[Vharzath]] keeps the war in his own hands.[4][16][8]

### Current Status
The pantheon is at war, and it is losing the conquests. The first battle on [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s projection is already lost. The roads, the stations, and then the depth of the territory follow. Supply lines are broken. The defender who masses in one galaxy gives away another. The line [[Vharzath]] will not cross is the last galaxy, and that galaxy is his body. The fleets have already been turned back from it.
[[Vaelthar]] is an empty shell, and the body is ordered destroyed. Nine Chief Gods are in the same collection, under the same order. A new [[Azrakar]] can threaten a galaxy and is not the God the Horsemen erased. Nine offices were still waiting on a holder when that successor left the palace. [[Xytherion]] has spent the [[Nihilvore]] and watched it die. Two Thrones have been sent, unnamed, and told to keep themselves alive. The other elites, and the ruler, have not taken the field. The souls of the ordinary dead still return to him. The map does not.[6][4][16][8][5]
Justice and [[Elarion]] are not members. The Great Silence's order to take War alive died with Azrakar's acceptance of it. The order that replaced it has not been carried out. The Horsemen left with the projection.[12][14][6]
