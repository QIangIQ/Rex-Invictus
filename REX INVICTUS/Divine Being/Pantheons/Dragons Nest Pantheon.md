### Objectives

#### Goal(s)
- Maintain [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s personal Pantheon and Divine family
- Protect the Elderblood Clan
- Maintain the native Divine and Faith structure surrounding Victor
- Provide a Divine source for worship, blessings and Pantheon-based techniques
- Historically unite multiple supernatural races beneath Victor's authority
The Dragons Nest Pantheon, commonly styled Dragon's Nest, is the personal Pantheon founded and ruled by Victor Elderblood. It was born from the Divine Dimension left behind by the ruined [[Greek Pantheon]], but Victor deliberately rejected the continuation of [[Olympus]] and established an entirely new Divine order under his ownership. The System initially recorded the reconstructed territory as Pantheon 7475909777 before Victor formally named it Dragon's Nest.[1][2]
Dragon's Nest has changed considerably since its foundation. During its early period, the name overlapped with the enormous multiracial civilization developing from Victor's Faction, and Vampires, Demons, [[Werewolves]], Youkai, Humans, Gods, Fairies, Angels and other species could all be described politically as belonging to Victor's growing Pantheon. After the creation of the [[Elderblood Empire]], those civilian, territorial and governmental functions passed increasingly to the Empire, while Dragon's Nest became more precisely identifiable as Victor's own Divine Pantheon: the God-King, the Dragon Goddesses directly incorporated beneath him, his Divine descendants and the religious network through which their powers are worshipped and accessed.[3][2][4][5]
The late Imperial era establishes a particularly clear boundary between political subordination and Pantheon membership. The Shinto, Hindu and Biblical Pantheons can exist beneath Victor's greater Imperial authority without their Gods automatically becoming members of Dragon's Nest. [[Amaterasu]] Elderblood and [[Kali Elderblood]], for example, are Dragon Goddesses belonging directly to Dragon's Nest, whereas Susanoo and [[Shiva]] remain Gods of foreign Pantheons even while existing within Victor's larger order. The same distinction separates the Pantheon from the Empire's male Gods, soldiers and administrators: Dragon's Nest itself is described as having no male Gods apart from Victor, with its modern Divine body consisting overwhelmingly of women related or directly bound to him.[5][6]

#### Pantheon, State and Dynasty
The modern Elderblood order contains three structures that frequently overlap through the same people. The Elderblood Clan is [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s dynasty and Bloodline; Dragon's Nest is the Divine Pantheon to which Victor's Dragon Gods belong; and the [[Elderblood Empire]] is the civilization ruled by that dynasty. A woman can simultaneously be Victor's wife, an Elderblood, a Dragon Goddess of Dragon's Nest and an Empress of the Empire, but those titles describe different relationships.
This distinction is especially important when cataloguing Gods. Political obedience to Victor does not make every God a Dragon's Nest deity, and kinship with Victor does not automatically make a non-Divine relative a Pantheon God. The strongest direct confirmation comes from Imperial religious techniques, which recognize Dragon's Nest as a distinct Divine network even while other Pantheons are politically subject to the same Emperor.[5]

### History
PRE-821
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Faction
Dragon's Nest inherited its social and governmental foundation from Victor's Faction, the multiracial coalition built around Victor, his wives, the Noble Vampire Clans, Demonic subjects, Youkai, Fairies, Humans and increasingly numerous supernatural allies. By Chapter 730 the faction already required a central governmental mansion and a Council of [[Races (Flamel)|Races]], establishing the administrative principles later inherited by Dragon's Nest and the Empire.[3]
819–820
A World of Their Own
Following the collapse of [[Olympus]]' old order, Victor and his family began constructing a permanent supernatural world of their own. [[Gaia]], Nyx, Natalia, Jeanne and Roxanne contributed the planetary matter, spatial control, natural systems, primordial power and World Tree Energy necessary to transform astronomical material into a viable living world.[10][11]
821
Pantheon 7475909777
Victor formally abolished Olympus and declared the creation of a new Pantheon ruled by himself. The System restored and expanded the damaged Greek Divine Dimension through equivalent exchange, consuming approximately 95% of Victor's available Energy, and registered the reconstructed structure as Pantheon 7475909777 under Victor's ownership.[1]
822–824
[[Etherea]] and the Inner World
The newly created world required more than physical matter to become a properly integrated supernatural ecosystem. [[The Universal Tree]] intervened in the abnormal relationship among Victor, Roxanne and the planetary system, after which Etherea became tied to Victor's internal existence. Victor, [[Scathach]] and Zaladrac subsequently covered the world with Draconic Runic defenses intended to make invasion extraordinarily difficult.[8][12][13]
868
Dragon's Nest
At the [[Supernatural Gathering]], Victor formally gave his Pantheon the name Dragon's Nest. Its political identity reflected the faction he had already assembled: species traditionally hostile to one another could exist beneath the same sovereign so long as they obeyed the law and did not reduce intelligent individuals to racial stereotypes.[2][14]
876–888
Divine Territorial Expansion
Victor stripped Ra of his Divine position and acquired authority over the Egyptian Divine territory, then claimed further territories after defeating the hostile Elder Gods. Populations without secure homelands were relocated into the conquered Divine lands, beginning the transformation of those territories into a permanent public multiracial civilization.[15][16][17]
937
A Multiracial Divine Civilization
The former Egyptian territory had developed into a society where Gods, Demonic creatures, Vampires and [[Werewolves]] openly lived together despite generations of racial hostility. Victor's population had also begun calling him God Emperor after his authority expanded across multiple Pantheons, with the title spreading socially before becoming part of his mature Imperial identity.[18]
1085
The Public Dragon-Goddess Pantheon
Temples of the [[Blood God Religion]] publicly represented officially known Dragon Goddesses of Dragon's Nest. The registry was deliberately incomplete: several Divine women rejected public worship or avoided public appearances, and even the Religion could not determine the complete number of Dragon Gods surrounding Victor.[4]
1086–1087
The [[Elderblood Empire]]
Victor replaced Earth's fragmented supernatural sovereignty with the Elderblood Empire. Older Pantheons could survive as subordinate Divine systems rather than being absorbed into Dragon's Nest itself, while the Empire became responsible for the greater political state. Victor was subsequently recognized as the Overlord of his Sector.[7][19]
1118
Faith as an Imperial Power
Victor's relationship with [[Frederick Winter]] and [[Andrew Walter]] demonstrated the practical reach of Dragon's Nest's Faith. Religious and Onmyō techniques could draw real supernatural power from the Gods being worshipped, while Victor's personal Blessing allowed his closest followers to deepen those connections rather than treating Faith as an abstract belief.[20]
1163
Pantheon Jurisdiction Clarified
The mature Imperial Inquisition demonstrated that Dragon's Nest had become a distinct Divine power network. Andrew could draw upon Victor, Violet and the other Gods of Dragon's Nest, including [[Amaterasu]] and Kali, but could not access [[Shiva]] or Susanoo through the same method because they remained Gods of foreign Pantheons despite existing beneath the Emperor's political authority.[5]
1166+
The Modern Divine Family
Dragon's Nest had become overwhelmingly synonymous with Victor's Divine household. The Pantheon was described as having no male Gods apart from Victor, while its other Divine members consisted almost entirely of women related or personally integrated into his family. The many male Gods, soldiers and officials of the Elderblood Empire remained politically subordinate without thereby becoming Dragon's Nest deities.[6]

### Foundation of the Pantheon

#### Destruction of Olympus
Dragon's Nest inherited the Divine territory of the [[Greek Pantheon]], not the political identity of [[Olympus]]. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had defeated the forces attempting to exploit the Greek Divine structure against his family and acquired ownership of the damaged Dimension. Rather than become another King of Olympus, he formally declared Olympus finished and ordered the formation of a new Pantheon led by himself.[1]

Victor's internal reality continued expanding enormously after Dragon's Nest's creation. By the late Imperial era, his Soul could theoretically shelter tens of trillions of Beings and could be expanded with additional planets and dimensions if necessary, although Victor deliberately preserved its deepest regions primarily as a sanctuary for his family.[21] This later scale should not be confused with the original size of Etherea itself.

#### Runic Fortress
[[Etherea]] was conceived as a home but never left undefended. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], [[Scathach]] and Zaladrac traveled throughout the world establishing extensive Draconic Runic protections, with Victor treating the initial network as only the first layer of a system that could continue growing alongside the family.[12][13]
Its location inside Victor's private existence, connection to the World Tree system and accumulated Runic defenses made Etherea fundamentally different from ordinary Imperial planets. It was a family sanctuary before it was ever a public capital.

### Formal Naming
Pantheon 7475909777 remained only a System-generated designation until the [[Supernatural Gathering]]. By then, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction had expanded far beyond its original Noble Vampire core and visibly contained forces and citizens from species that conventional supernatural politics usually separated into hostile blocs.[22][23]
Victor formally chose Dragon's Nest in Chapter 868. The name represented a Pantheon built around his emerging Draconic dynasty rather than around an inherited ethnic mythology, and the System accepted the new designation.[2]
The name also suited the political philosophy Victor defended at the Gathering. He rejected the claim that an intelligent Demon should automatically be categorized as a monster while Humans, Gods or Vampires were judged as individuals. Under his rule, behavior, loyalty and willingness to live within society mattered more than species.[2][14]

### Expansion

#### Egyptian Divine Territory
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s defeat of Ra brought another major Divine territory beneath his authority. He removed Ra's Sun Divinity, reduced the former Primordial God to mortality and claimed the Egyptian Divine Dimension rather than allowing the territory and its supernatural infrastructure to become another uncontrolled vacuum.[15]
After the conflict with the Elder Gods, Victor possessed even more conquered territory than his original population could immediately use. Rather than leave those lands empty, he ordered allied groups without secure homelands to settle the former Egyptian territory.[16][17]
The relocated population included Noble Vampires associated with his allied Clans, Youkai under Haruna, Human followers, Goddesses without independent realms, members of the [[Blood God Religion]] and Demonic forces. The [[Werewolves]] already possessed Samar and therefore established an embassy rather than abandoning their native world.[17]

#### Elvenorah
Main article: [[Elvenorah, the Eternal Utopia]]
Elvenorah became the clearest public expression of Dragon's Nest's early multiracial civilization. By Chapter 937, Demonic creatures, Gods, Vampires and [[Werewolves]] openly lived within the same society even though their species had spent much of history in conflict. The stability arose from shared allegiance, social integration and the certainty that racial violence threatening [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s order would not be tolerated.[18]
The city's development also marked a change in scale. Dragon's Nest was no longer merely Victor's private Pantheon territory and immediate family; the civilization associated with it was capable of administering large unrelated populations and Divine territories. This political side would later be inherited and expanded dramatically by the [[Elderblood Empire]].

#### God Emperor
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] did not initially proclaim himself God Emperor. The title spread among the population after he came to control multiple Pantheons and Divine territories. By Chapter 937 it had become sufficiently common that inhabitants used it naturally despite its initially unofficial status.[18]
The title eventually became appropriate in a more literal political sense. Victor ceased ruling only his own Pantheon and became the sovereign above multiple surviving Pantheons, making God-King of Dragon's Nest and God Emperor of the [[Elderblood Empire]] complementary rather than contradictory offices.

### Organization

#### Supreme Ruler
♛
GOD-KING OF DRAGON'S NEST
[[Victor Elderblood]]
Founder · Owner · Supreme Divine Authority · God Emperor
Victor's position differs fundamentally from that of a God-King who inherits a throne belonging to an older mythology. He personally abolished the predecessor political order, founded the replacement Pantheon and was recorded by the System as owner of its Divine territory. No Divine council or subordinate God possesses authority above him inside Dragon's Nest.[1]
His authority is also personal rather than ceremonial. The Gods of Dragon's Nest are overwhelmingly his wives, daughters or women directly integrated into his family, while religious techniques can identify and draw upon the Pantheon's Gods specifically because they belong beneath the Emperor's direct Divine command.[4][5]

#### Elderblood Clan
The Elderblood Clan is the ruling dynasty around which modern Dragon's Nest is organized. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wives and daughters occupy most of its Divine positions and simultaneously provide many of the Empire's greatest military, political, religious, scientific and administrative authorities.
Clan membership is nevertheless broader than Pantheon membership. A non-Divine Elderblood remains part of the family without automatically becoming a God, while a Dragon Goddess incorporated into Victor's household can belong to Dragon's Nest even if she originally came from another Divine civilization.

#### Historical Council of Races
The Council of [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] predates Dragon's Nest and belonged primarily to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Faction. It was created because the number of races and organizations surrounding Victor had made informal household administration inadequate.[3]
The Council remains important to the Pantheon's history because Dragon's Nest inherited its principle of allowing different peoples representation beneath a common ruler. It should not, however, be presented as the current supreme administration of the [[Elderblood Empire]].

### Divine Structure

#### God-King
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is the singular God-King and ultimate Divine authority of Dragon's Nest. The [[Blood God Religion]] places him above every individual Goddess in the public hierarchy, and worshippers regard the Dragon Goddesses as belonging to a Divine order whose center is the Emperor.[4]
This religious description sometimes portrays Victor as the "creator" of the Gods of his Pantheon. The statement should be understood through the modern structure rather than as a claim that Victor literally brought every Goddess into existence from nothing: many members were ancient Goddesses before meeting him, but their present Dragon-Goddess forms, Bloodline, place within Dragon's Nest or later Divine evolution are directly connected to Victor.

#### Dragon Goddesses
The defining Divine population of Dragon's Nest is its Dragon Goddesses. Their origins are highly varied. Some were ancient native Goddesses transformed into [[True Dragons]], some were members of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family who awakened Divinity after their evolution, some were born as Divine daughters and others joined the family after ruling completely different Pantheons.
This makes Dragon's Nest fundamentally different from a traditional mythological genealogy. Membership continues changing as the Elderblood family evolves, converts older Gods, produces Divine descendants and incorporates new wives.

#### Divine Daughters
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters form the rapidly growing second generation of Dragon's Nest. Many were born with Divine potential or awakened Concepts naturally because of the extraordinary nature of their parents. [[Nero Elderblood]], [[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]], Kassandra Elderblood, [[Yog-Sothoth]] Elderblood, [[Akasha Elderblood]], [[Nigredo Elderblood]] and numerous others consequently belong to a dynasty in which becoming a Goddess is increasingly normal rather than exceptional.
Not every daughter possesses the same supernatural system. Some have native Divinities, some are part of the separate [[Eldritch Pantheon]], some possess Primordial characteristics and many combine several classifications simultaneously. Their family membership therefore overlaps multiple metaphysical hierarchies without making those hierarchies identical.

#### Public and Private Gods
The [[Blood God Religion]] distinguishes between publicly recognized Dragon Goddesses and Divine members who have no interest in being objects of mass worship. Its temples display statues and information concerning official public members, but women including [[Bruna Elderblood]], [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]], [[Eve Elderblood]], [[Roberta Elderblood]], [[Maria Elderblood]] and Maya were specifically absent from the public temple because they avoided worship or public appearances.[4]
Their absence from a temple does not exclude them from the Divine family. Public registration describes what worshippers know, not the absolute boundary of Dragon's Nest.

### Membership and Jurisdiction
The modern story provides a direct functional test for the difference between Dragon's Nest and Gods who are merely subjects or allies of the [[Elderblood Empire]].
PANTHEON MEMBERSHIP ≠ IMPERIAL SUBORDINATION
A God can obey [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], serve the Empire or rule a subordinate Pantheon without becoming a God of Dragon's Nest. Dragon's Nest consists of Gods directly incorporated into Victor's own Pantheon.
During Imperial operations in the Andromeda Galaxy, [[Andrew Walter]] used a Faith-based Inquisition technique that borrowed Energy directly from the Emperor and the Gods of Dragon's Nest. He could draw upon the powers of Victor, Violet and other Dragon Gods belonging to the Pantheon. [[Amaterasu]] Elderblood and [[Kali Elderblood]] were valid sources specifically because both were Dragon Goddesses belonging to Dragon's Nest, whereas Susanoo and [[Shiva]] were rejected by the same system as members of foreign Pantheons.[5]
This remains true even though the Shinto, Hindu and Biblical Pantheons exist beneath Victor's greater Imperial authority in the late era. The Empire can rule multiple Pantheons without collapsing them into one Divine identity.[7][5]

#### The Sole Male God
The modern composition of Dragon's Nest is overwhelmingly female. During the Elderblood family gathering, the narration attributes much of the Pantheon's unusual internal harmony to the fact that there are no male Gods within Dragon's Nest apart from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and that virtually all its other Divine members are women connected to him.[6]
This does not imply that Victor's civilization lacks male Gods. The [[Elderblood Empire]] contains numerous male Divine citizens, allies, officials and subordinate rulers. What they lack is direct membership in Dragon's Nest.
Consequently, surviving male Gods such as [[Thanatos]], [[Hypnos]], [[Hephaestus]], [[Ares]], [[Dionysus]], [[Shiva]], Susanoo and others should not be placed automatically among Dragon's Nest's Gods merely because they exist beneath Victor's political authority.

Dragon's Nest was created through the native System using a reconstructed Divine Dimension and became Victor's conventional Pantheon of Dragon Gods, Faith and public worship. The Eldritch Pantheon developed from Victor and Azathoth as Eldritch [[Chaos]], producing a separate conceptual lineage whose Divinities can originate from a foreign cosmological source.[24][9]

### Religion
Main article: [[Blood God Religion]]
The Blood God Religion is the principal religious institution connecting ordinary believers with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Dragon's Nest. It existed before the mature Pantheon but expanded alongside Victor's rise from a hidden supernatural ruler into a publicly acknowledged God Emperor.

#### Public Divine Registry
By Chapter 1085, Blood God temples contained representations of officially recognized Dragon Goddesses. When another public Dragon Goddess became known, Bishops could register her association with Dragon's Nest and distribute basic information about her identity and Divine Domain.[4]

#### Victor as Supreme Divine Figure
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] receives the overwhelming majority of the Religion's devotion. To ordinary worshippers, Dragon's Nest's Goddesses are immensely important, but the Emperor is the foundational God-King around whom the entire religious order is organized.[4]
This relationship is reinforced by direct intervention. Victor has appeared personally before worshippers, rewarded exceptional loyalty, created a Paradise for deserving faithful and granted Blessings capable of materially improving the supernatural abilities of those who genuinely place their Faith in him.[27][28][29][20]
Unlike religions whose Gods are distant or uncertain, Victor's faithful can point to an Emperor who physically exists, answers followers, punishes enemies and allows Faith to generate observable supernatural effects. This direct reciprocity is repeatedly identified as one of the reasons the [[Blood God Religion]] became so attractive in an era when Humanity had learned that many traditional Gods existed but did not necessarily care about individual believers.[20]

#### High Priestess
[[Valeria Alekerth]] is the supreme mortal/religious authority beneath [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and one of the people closest to the public administration of his Faith. Victor selected her because her devotion did not eliminate judgment or competence, then personally strengthened her and established a supernatural relationship allowing her to represent his authority.[27][30]
By the Imperial era she commands a religious structure containing Bishops, Inquisitors, Onmyō practitioners and combat orders rather than merely temple clergy.[4][5]

#### Battle Sisters
The temples of the Blood God are defended by Battle Sisters, combat-trained religious women drawn from multiple races. Their equipment is Runic rather than ceremonial, and even ordinary members are expected to fight mature Vampires; superior Sisters can confront Elder Vampires, while coordinated groups can threaten a Low-Level God.[4]
Their power reflects Dragon's Nest's multi-Goddess religious structure. Battle Sisters receive Blessings associated not only with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] but also with powerful Dragon Goddesses including [[Aphrodite]], [[Scathach]] and Nyx.[4]
The order also preserves the multiracial philosophy inherited from Victor's original faction. Membership includes Humans and supernatural races alike, including species that might otherwise have struggled to find institutional protection within the old political order.[4]

#### Faith and Divine Power
Faith is a usable supernatural connection rather than solely a social belief. Both Imperial Inquisitors and Onmyō practitioners can use relationships with Gods as power sources, although their methods are different.[20][5]
[[Frederick Winter]] represents the Onmyō path. His techniques can call upon different Gods with the appropriate relationship or permission, including Gods outside Dragon's Nest. [[Andrew Walter]] represents the Imperial Inquisition's more direct relationship with the Emperor: he can borrow pure power from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and from Dragon's Nest's Gods through Faith.[5]

#### Private and Public Space
[[Etherea]] retained a special position because it was constructed specifically for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his family and became embedded within his private existence. Elvenorah, by contrast, became an early public society populated by unrelated races. The distinction foreshadowed the eventual division between Dragon's Nest as a Divine family core and the [[Elderblood Empire]] as a civilization intended to govern populations far beyond Victor's household.

#### Conquered Pantheons
Conquering a Pantheon does not necessarily make its entire territory part of Dragon's Nest. The Greek Divine Dimension became the actual foundation of Dragon's Nest because [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] deliberately replaced [[Olympus]] with his own Pantheon. Later conquests were handled differently: Egyptian territory became a settlement center, surviving Divine systems could remain subordinate governments and other territories were incorporated directly into the Empire.[1][17][7]

### Military
Dragon's Nest has always existed beside enormous military forces, but modern Imperial armies should not be mislabeled as the Pantheon's personal army.

#### Early Faction Forces
The forces inherited from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Faction included Noble Vampires, the [[Maid Squad]], Youkai under Haruna, Amazons, Fairies, [[Gorgons]], Humans, [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]], Goddesses and the armies of Hell. Their diversity gave Victor access to radically different supernatural traditions before the Empire possessed a standardized military.

#### Dragon Goddesses
The Pantheon's Gods themselves form strategic military assets. During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s confrontation with the [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]], his Dragon Goddess wives formed part of the vanguard supporting the Demonic host commanded by [[Scathach]]. Their collective presence demonstrated that Victor's Pantheon could deploy multiple Divine combatants without requiring every conflict to be solved personally by its God-King.[4]

#### Religious Forces
The [[Blood God Religion]] maintains Battle Sisters, Inquisitors and other militant orders whose supernatural abilities can draw directly from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Dragon's Nest. These organizations are religious/Imperial institutions rather than Pantheon Gods themselves.[4][5]

#### Imperial Forces
The later Spartans, Imperial Legions, enhanced soldiers, Dragonoids, naval fleets and Commanders such as John, [[Clare Bloodhunter]], [[Kiana Bloodhunter]], [[Laura Bloodhunter]], Conan, [[Kaleb Hunter]] and [[Caelus Hunter]] belong to the military of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Their loyalty to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] does not give them Divine membership in Dragon's Nest.
The same distinction applies to the [[Heralds of Chaos]]. War, Thomas, Jimmy, Lucy and Karen serve Victor personally and possess a supernatural connection to his [[Chaos]], but their existence does not make them Dragon's Nest Gods.

### Political Philosophy

#### Family as the Divine Core
Modern Dragon's Nest is intensely family-centered. The overwhelming majority of its Gods are [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wives, daughters or women who have been integrated deeply enough into the Elderblood household to share its Draconic and Divine structure.
This family basis creates a different internal dynamic from the [[Greek Pantheon]] that preceded it. Rivalry, jealousy and competition remain common, but the household imposes strict limits on hostility capable of damaging the family itself. The late-era observation that the Pantheon lacks competing male Gods and is composed almost entirely of women related to Victor is presented as one reason the internal Divine structure avoids the endless dynastic conflicts that plagued [[Olympus]].[6]

#### Species Is Secondary
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s earliest civilization rejected the idea that moral or political status should be determined automatically by species. Demons, Vampires, Humans, Gods and [[Werewolves]] could all produce monsters or responsible citizens. This philosophy shaped both Dragon's Nest's early public society and the later Empire.[2][14]

#### Loyalty
Loyalty is one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s most important political standards. A Demon or alien species obeying Imperial law can receive more protection than a Human or God who knowingly betrays the Emperor, his family or the state.
The same principle operates inside Dragon's Nest on a more personal scale. Membership does not simply mean existing beneath Victor politically; the Pantheon consists of Gods whose Divine and familial relationship places them directly within his order.

#### Merit and Competence
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s civilization consistently distinguishes loyalty from incompetence. The [[Blood God Religion]] does not grant senior offices solely to the most emotional worshippers, and Victor delegates political, military, scientific and religious responsibility according to who can actually perform the task.[31]
The principle extends even to his daughters. Exceptional Bloodline and Divine potential are treated as foundations to be trained rather than excuses for remaining inexperienced.

### Former Enemies and Conquered Powers

#### Greek Pantheon
The [[Greek Pantheon]] ceased to exist as an independent political Divine order. Its damaged Dimension became the territorial foundation repaired and registered as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s new Pantheon, but [[Olympus]] itself was not continued under another dynasty.[1]

#### Egyptian Pantheon
The [[Egyptian Pantheon]] was conquered after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s retaliation against Ra and his allies. Its Divine territory later became an important settlement region for Victor's faction and eventually developed into Elvenorah.[15][17]

#### Elder Gods Pantheon
The hostile [[Elder Gods Pantheon]] was destroyed during the conflict culminating in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ascension as the Dragon God of [[Chaos]]. Their defeat contributed additional territories and resources to the civilization developing around Dragon's Nest.[16]

#### Norse Pantheon
The old [[Norse Pantheon]] collapsed during Ragnarök. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction removed useful people and resources before the final destruction of [[Odin]]'s order, while Hela later sold the surviving Divine territory rather than rebuilding [[Asgard]] as part of Dragon's Nest.[32]

#### Hindu Pantheon
The [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]] was offered the choice of submission or destruction after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] began completing the political unification of Earth. [[Shiva]] chose survival and lost independent sovereignty, but the Pantheon retained a separate Divine identity rather than becoming Dragon's Nest.[4][5]

#### Later Imperial Conquests
Campaigns involving the Celts, extraterrestrial civilizations and other Sectors belong principally to the history of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. They demonstrate [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s greater sovereignty but should not be retroactively presented as expansions of Dragon's Nest's founding territory.
