The Elder Gods Pantheon was the native Pantheon of Gods inhabiting Nightingale, ruled by Ikor-Kar-VI and principally based within the vast territory known as the Wildlands. The Elder Gods predated the Noble Vampire civilization established on Nightingale and regarded the Vampires as foreign invaders who had occupied their world for thousands of years. When [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] first explored Nightingale, the Elder Gods were already recognized as the mysterious leaders of the planet's native creatures, although even Vlad knew almost nothing about their numbers, intelligence, organization or the mechanism behind their apparent immortality.[1]
The Pantheon was closely associated with Nightingale's [[Amara Elderblood|World Tree of Positivity]]. Pantheons are established through the influence of a World Tree, and Nightingale's World Tree actively supported the Elder Gods because they belonged to the native side of the planet's conflict.[2][3] However, the story never explicitly states that Amara personally created Ikor, founded the Elder Gods Pantheon or selected Ikor as its leader. The Pantheon's exact founding period and original founder therefore remain unknown.
The Elder Gods were most infamous for their extraordinary resistance to permanent death. Early encounters led Vlad to believe an external mechanism or unknown Being was resurrecting them because even completely destroying an Elder God's Soul failed to permanently kill it.[1] This mystery was later resolved: the source was Ikor himself. His Divine authority allowed him to grant and maintain immortality in other Elder Gods and selected servants, protecting their Souls and repeatedly restoring them after otherwise fatal destruction. The effect required Ikor's continued support and expenditure of Energy rather than functioning as an independent racial immortality possessed equally by every Elder God.[4][5]
By the final war only five native Elder Gods remained active on Nightingale.[6] Their small number was not natural. After consuming Ikor, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] learned that Ikor had repeatedly used Predation against other Elder Gods, absorbing useful powers and reducing the Pantheon's population over Time. The same experiments were performed upon Nightingale's native inhabitants, while the gigantic bodies worn by the surviving Elder Gods were constructed from the accumulated corpses of Gods and natives.[7]
The Elder Gods were also intimately connected to the origin and continued production of Nightingale's Monsters. Victor later discovered that the Monster Race resulted from experimentation combining Ikor's Divinity, Nightingale's native inhabitants and Noble Vampires.[7] During the final war an unnamed Elder God functioned as a mass-production source capable of continuously generating additional Monsters until [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]] compressed the Elder God's complete physical and spiritual existence and killed it, immediately stopping the production process.[8]

### Nature of the Elder Gods

#### Native Gods of Nightingale
The Elder Gods were already established on [[Nightingale]] when Vlad discovered the planet several millennia before the final war. Nightingale at the time was inhabited by numerous monstrous native creatures, and Vlad identified the Elder Gods as the leaders standing above them. Ordinary creatures were relatively manageable, but the Elder Gods represented the true obstacle to unrestricted Vampire expansion.[1]
Vlad had personally fought at least one Elder God during this early period. Although he considered the battle difficult, he ultimately defeated the creature and completely destroyed its Soul. The supposed victory proved temporary when the Elder God regenerated and returned to life. Vlad consequently rejected the idea that the creature possessed ordinary biological immortality and correctly theorized that either an external device or another Being was responsible for restoring it.[1]
The Elder Gods were also distinct from ordinary Gods in appearance and spiritual scale. When the five survivors finally entered the war directly, every one of them exceeded 1,000 metres in height, while their Souls were so enormous that they appeared almost like conglomerations or clusters of multiple Gods. Their appearances varied dramatically: some possessed eyes and mouths, others only mouths, others lacked conventional faces entirely, and their physical composition ranged from smooth or spiked white bodies to bodies apparently made of earth.[6]

#### Pantheon Formation and the World Tree
The Elder Gods' existence was intertwined with [[Nightingale]]'s native Divine ecosystem. The general rules governing Pantheons establish that a Pantheon requires the influence of a World Tree in order to form, making Nightingale's [[Amara Elderblood|World Tree of Positivity]] foundational to the existence of Nightingale's native Pantheon in the same broad sense that other [[World Trees]] support their respective Divine systems.[2]
Amara herself lived within the Wildlands, the same enormous region occupied by the Elder Gods.[9] She supported the Elder Gods during their conflict with the Noble Vampires because the Elder Gods and their people were natives of Nightingale, while the Vampires had originated elsewhere.[3]
This relationship was also a practical source of power. Ikor possessed an ability that allowed him to store a chosen type of Energy and used it to accumulate Positive Energy supplied by Nightingale's World Tree. Even after travelling to the [[Edge of the Cosmos]], he could continue using this stored Positive Energy to empower his followers and maintain their immortality.[5]
Amara's support should not be interpreted as proof that she personally approved of everything Ikor did. The full scale of Ikor's experimentation upon Nightingale's natives and other Elder Gods was only exposed after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] consumed him.[7]

### Wildlands
The Wildlands were the principal homeland of the Elder Gods and the location in which [[Nightingale]]'s [[Amara Elderblood|World Tree of Positivity]] resided. Unlike the territories controlled by the Noble Vampire Clans, the Wildlands remained largely outside the political and territorial system established by Vampire civilization.[9]
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] began radically modifying Nightingale and creating a new world upon the planet, several exceptionally powerful observers watched his actions from the Wildlands. Victor could sense their gazes even before the Elder Gods formally entered the later war, demonstrating that the Pantheon remained aware of major changes occurring elsewhere on Nightingale despite normally remaining within its own territory.[9]
The Elder Gods' isolation was not unique to their behavior on Nightingale. When an Elder God messenger later contacted [[Diablo]], Diablo immediately found the appearance unusual because the Elder Gods normally did not voluntarily leave their territory. Their leader's principal condition for assisting Diablo was similarly territorial: once Diablo completed his plans and established his own power, he was to leave the Elder Gods and their lands completely alone.[4]

### Immortality System

#### Ikor as the Source
The most important collective ability of the Pantheon was the system of immortality maintained by [[Ikor-Kar-VI]]. It was initially misunderstood as an inherent property of the Elder God race because members repeatedly returned after physical and spiritual destruction.[1]
Chapter 615 clarified the mechanism when [[Lucifer]] Morningstar revealed that he had abandoned his previous race and become an Elder God. [[Diablo]] immediately recognized the reason for the transformation: the Elder Gods possessed immortality of the Soul. Lucifer could repeatedly return from death and considered himself effectively immortal, but Diablo correctly pointed out that this immortality ultimately remained dependent upon the Being responsible for granting it—the Leader of the Elder Gods.[4]
The system later became explicit during Ikor's battle with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. [[Var-Kar-VI]] could repeatedly return after Victor killed him because Ikor was restoring him. Victor eventually realized that the key to the immortality of Ikor's members was Ikor himself.[5]
Ikor's Blessing was not restricted to natural Elder Gods. A specialized Priest assigned to Lucifer was capable of supporting the immortality granted by the leader of the Elder Gods, demonstrating that Ikor's protection could be distributed through subordinate agents and applied to selected combatants.[3]

#### Limitations
The immortality system consumed Energy. While fighting at the [[Edge of the Cosmos]], Ikor continuously spent his stored Positive Energy to prevent [[Lucifer]] and the Type 6 [[Destroyer]] from dying permanently and to maintain the advantages of his immediate combat group.[5]
Unlike [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Ikor had no continuous source capable of replenishing his reserves while separated from [[Nightingale]]. His Positive Energy therefore progressively decreased until maintaining the immortality of his entire faction became impossible. Ikor ultimately abandoned his support for the Elder Gods and other Beings fighting elsewhere so that he could concentrate his remaining power upon Var, Lucifer and the Destroyer assisting him against Victor.[5]
This decision had immediate consequences elsewhere. Shur, who was fighting [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] and [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]], noticed that the Energy sustaining his immortality was no longer being replenished. His remaining reserve progressively disappeared until he became capable of permanently dying.[10]
The Pantheon's immortality was therefore exceptionally powerful but not absolute. It depended upon:
- Ikor remaining capable of supporting the target;
- sufficient Energy being available;
- the connection or Blessing remaining active;
- and the target not being destroyed through an authority capable of overwhelming the protection before restoration could occur.[8][10][5]

### The Native Population
Although Ikor publicly presented the Elder Gods as defenders of [[Nightingale]]'s native inhabitants, the relationship between the Pantheon and those inhabitants was far more exploitative than it initially appeared.
Native settlements treated Ikor as a God and received direct supernatural benefits from him. When Ikor distributed a Blessing, the inhabitants could immediately feel their bodies becoming stronger. The strongest adult males and family leaders could also become Alphas, individuals capable of commanding Nightingale's Monsters in war.[3][7]
The native population consequently formed a major part of the Elder Gods' military civilization. Alphas commanded Monsters against [[WarFall]] and Clan Adrasteia, while generations of native forces fought beside the creatures controlled by the Pantheon.[3]
The truth discovered after Ikor's death was considerably darker. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] located the surviving civilian population, only approximately 1,700 natives remained, divided among roughly one hundred family groups. Victor explained that the catastrophic reduction had resulted from experimentation and Predation. Ikor had used the native population as experimental material and personally consumed individuals who developed rare abilities that interested him.[7]
Ikor applied the same attitude to members of his own Pantheon. Elder Gods who possessed useful traits could become targets for Predation, explaining why an apparently ancient native Pantheon had been reduced to only five active native Elder Gods by the time of the final war.[5][7]
The gigantic exterior bodies used by the Elder Gods were another result of this system. Victor revealed that these enormous forms were constructed from the corpses of Gods and Nightingale's natives. Ikor's own body during the final war demonstrated this structure directly: after Victor split the gigantic shell apart, he discovered a much smaller Elder God existing inside what otherwise resembled a walking corpse.[11][7]

### Monsters
The Monsters of [[Nightingale]] were not simply an unrelated native species commanded by the Elder Gods. Their later form was directly connected to Ikor's experiments.
After consuming Ikor and gaining access to his information, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] revealed that the Monsters were created through experiments combining:
- Ikor's Divinity;
- Nightingale's native inhabitants;
- and Noble Vampires.[7]
The resulting creatures became the principal military forces of the Elder Gods. They existed in enormous numbers and possessed numerous specialized forms, including Behemoths, [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]], Destroyers and other terrestrial and aerial creatures. More advanced units possessed specialized abilities, while Alphas served as commanders capable of directing groups of Monsters.[6][3]
Some Monster abilities were sufficiently useful for the Elder Gods to deliberately deploy specialized combinations against particular enemies. When Ikor ordered [[Lucifer]] to target Victor, he supplied him with a Type 6 [[Destroyer]], two Type 5 Hunters and a Priest. The Type 6 Destroyer possessed probability-related abilities later used in combination with Lucifer's Darkness and Ikor's support against Victor.[3][5]
During the final war, the Monster army numbered at least in the hundreds of thousands. Enormous quantities emerged from beneath Nightingale's surface, from its seas and even from openings within the colossal bodies of Elder Gods when Ikor declared war.[6]
The Pantheon also possessed an unnamed Elder God specifically capable of continuously producing additional Monsters. Even after known production facilities were destroyed, new Monsters continued entering the battlefield. [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]] eventually identified the responsible Elder God, contained it from the beginning of their confrontation and compressed both its body and Soul into a small mass. Its death immediately stopped the ongoing Monster production.[12][8]

### Relationship with the Noble Vampires
The defining political conflict of the Elder Gods Pantheon was its prolonged war against [[Nightingale]]'s Noble Vampires.
The Vampires were not native to Nightingale. Vlad and his people had arrived from elsewhere and eventually established the Vampire civilization that came to dominate large portions of the planet. From the Elder Gods' perspective, they were foreign colonizers occupying territory that belonged to Nightingale's original inhabitants.[1][6]
Ikor openly expressed this position during the final war, describing the Vampires as a cancer that had invaded Nightingale thousands of years earlier and declaring that the time had finally come to cleanse them from the planet.[6]
However, the Elder Gods had possessed opportunities to intervene directly long before the final war. [[Shiva]] confronted Ikor over this contradiction, pointing out that Ikor and the other Elder Gods could likely have acted together against Vlad during the early stages of the conflict instead of allowing the war to continue for roughly three thousand years.[3]
Shiva argued that the prolonged conflict had benefited Ikor and the native side in several ways. Continuous war forced the native population and Monsters to evolve, accelerated military development, unified the population around a common enemy and allowed Ikor's faction to study captured enemies and foreign powers. [[Lucifer]]'s transformation into an Elder God represented one example of Ikor incorporating a powerful outsider into this system.[3]
The Noble Vampires nevertheless suffered enormous casualties from the Elder Gods' forces. Numerous Vampires died to Monsters and Alphas over the centuries, and the conflict was responsible for devastating losses among Clan Adrasteia.[3]
Later revelations concerning Ikor's experiments gave additional weight to Shiva's criticism. Although Ikor genuinely opposed the Vampire occupation, he simultaneously used the native population he claimed to defend as experimental material, consumed those who developed useful abilities and incorporated both natives and captured Noble Vampires into the development of the Monster Race.[7]

### Isolationism and External Relations
The Elder Gods normally avoided direct involvement with civilizations beyond their territory. This isolationism was sufficiently well known that [[Diablo]] was surprised when one of their messengers personally appeared before him.[4]
Despite this policy, Ikor was willing to intervene indirectly when doing so protected the Pantheon's long-term interests. During Diablo's preparations to establish himself as a greater demonic ruler, the Elder Gods offered assistance and provided a strange black substance capable of drawing out the darkness already existing within another Being. Their intention was to assist Diablo's plans while ensuring that his future faction would have no reason to interfere with [[Nightingale]].[4]
The price demanded by Ikor was simple: Diablo and his future faction were to forget the Elder Gods' lands existed and remain outside their territory. The Elder God messenger reinforced the demand by invoking the threat of an immortal army.[4]
[[Lucifer]] Morningstar became the Pantheon's most notable converted outsider. After changing his race into an Elder God, Lucifer acquired the Soul-based immortality maintained by Ikor and later fought directly under his orders. He was not one of the five native Elder Gods who appeared together during the final war; he operated as an additional converted combatant subsequently deployed by Ikor.[4][6][3]

### Organization

#### Leader
- [[Ikor-Kar-VI]] ✝ — Leader of the Elder Gods and father of [[Var-Kar-VI]]. Ikor possessed the Divinities of [[Immortality]] and Predation. He maintained the Pantheon's collective immortality, stored Positive Energy received from [[Nightingale]]'s World Tree and used Predation to absorb the strength and abilities of other Beings. His Predation against other Elder Gods was explicitly identified as the reason so few remained by the final war.[5][7]

#### Native Elder Gods
- [[Var-Kar-VI]] ✝ — Ikor's son and holder of Extreme Adaptation. Any method that successfully killed Var once became ineffective as a method of killing him again after he resurrected and adapted. His repeated resurrection depended upon Ikor's immortality support. Ikor ultimately devoured Var to acquire Extreme Adaptation for himself.[11][5][13]
- Nur ✝ — [[Nightingale]]'s God of the Elements. Nur possessed control over the known Elements and their combinations, explicitly including Water, Fire, Air, Earth, Steam, Lava, Ice, Light and Darkness. He was completely destroyed by [[Shiva]] using the Divinity of Destruction.[8]
- Shur ✝ — Holder of the Divinity of Power, who described himself as the incarnation of Nightingale's Power. He fought [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] and [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]]. Once Ikor's immortality stopped being replenished, Haruna destroyed his body with starlight while Vlad destroyed his Soul to prevent another return.[10]
- Unnamed Monster-Producing Elder God ✝ — One of the five native Elder Gods present during the final war. This Elder God continuously produced new Monsters even after ordinary production facilities had been destroyed. [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]] trapped it from the beginning of their confrontation and eventually compressed its entire physical and spiritual existence into a small mass, permanently stopping its Monster production.[12][8]

#### Converted Member
- [[Lucifer]] Morningstar ✝ — An outsider who deliberately changed his race and became an Elder God because of the race's Soul-based immortality. Lucifer served under Ikor during the final war and was supplied with elite Monster units for the mission against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Ikor eventually consumed him with Predation after deciding Lucifer was more useful as absorbed power than as an independent fighter.[4][3][5]

#### Former and Unnamed Elder Gods
The complete historical membership of the Pantheon is unknown. Chapter 124 explicitly states that Vlad did not know how many Elder God leaders existed, and Chapter 886 later explains that the population had been greatly reduced because Ikor applied Predation to other Elder Gods in the same way he preyed upon [[Nightingale]]'s natives.[1][7]
The five native Elder Gods appearing in Chapter 877 should therefore be understood as the surviving members at the time of the final war, not the Pantheon's original total population.[6]

### Final War
The Elder Gods remained largely outside the open conflict until the final struggle for [[Nightingale]]. When they finally entered the battlefield in Chapter 877, five native Elder Gods appeared together. Their enormous bodies towered over the battlefield while their gigantic Souls immediately drew the attention of the assembled Gods.[6]
Ikor confronted [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and formally declared war, summoning hundreds of thousands of Monsters throughout the surrounding territory. His strategy centered upon eliminating Victor, whom the Elder Gods regarded as the most dangerous abnormality on the enemy side.[6][8]
The surviving Elder Gods were consequently divided among Victor's most powerful allies:
- Ikor and Var initially fought Victor Elderblood, later accompanied by [[Lucifer]] and a Type 6 [[Destroyer]].[11]
- Nur confronted [[Shiva]].[8]
- Shur confronted [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] and [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]].[10]
- the unnamed Monster-producing Elder God was contained by [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]].[8]
Ikor transported Victor, Var, Lucifer and the Destroyer to the [[Edge of the Cosmos]], separating himself from Nightingale and forcing him to rely upon the Positive Energy he had previously stored from the World Tree.[11][5]
The confrontation became a prolonged battle of attrition. As Ikor's Energy reserves declined, he stopped supporting members elsewhere and concentrated his remaining Blessing upon those fighting alongside him. He then activated Predation, first consuming the Type 6 Destroyer and later Lucifer to replenish some Energy and absorb their attributes.[5]
The loss of Ikor's distant support simultaneously made Shur mortal. Haruna and Vlad killed him shortly afterward.[10] Nur was erased by Shiva's Destruction, while Velnorah eliminated the Monster-producing Elder God and ended its continuing production of reinforcements.[8]
At the Edge of the Cosmos, Victor's prolonged battle allowed him to break through the final barrier preventing his ascension to Godhood. Once Victor completed his transformation, Ikor and Var became terrified of his new existence and instinctively turned against each other. Ikor overpowered and devoured his own son, gaining Extreme Adaptation in an attempt to improve his chances of survival.[13]
The effort failed. Ikor eventually returned to Nightingale, where his access to Positive Energy was restored, but Victor had simultaneously regained the abilities suppressed at the Edge. Victor prevented Ikor from escaping and ultimately devoured Ikor's entire existence.[13]
With Ikor dead, the Pantheon lost both its leader and the source responsible for its immortality system. Combined with the deaths of Nur, Shur, Var, Lucifer and the Monster-producing Elder God, the Elder Gods ceased to exist as an organized Pantheon after the war.

### Posthumous Revelations
The destruction of the Pantheon exposed information that had remained hidden throughout [[Nightingale]]'s history. Because [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had completely consumed Ikor, he obtained access to information concerning the Elder God's activities.[7]
The surviving native population numbered only approximately 1,700 individuals. Ikor had used their ancestors and communities for extensive experimentation, occasionally consuming individuals through Predation when they developed powers he considered useful.[7]
Other Elder Gods had been subjected to the same treatment, explaining why only a handful survived despite the Pantheon's ancient history.[7]
The gigantic Elder God forms were revealed to be corpse-based constructs assembled from dead Gods and natives rather than the natural bodies of the Elder Gods themselves.[7]
The Monster Race was likewise revealed to be the result of experimentation involving Ikor's Divinity, Nightingale's natives and Noble Vampires.[7]
These revelations drastically changed the apparent nature of the conflict. The Elder Gods had genuinely opposed a foreign Vampire civilization that had invaded their planet, but Ikor had simultaneously exploited the native people he claimed to defend and transformed both allies and enemies into resources for strengthening his Pantheon.

### Trivia
- The number of Elder Gods that existed throughout the Pantheon's entire history is unknown.[1]
- By Chapter 877 only five native Elder Gods remained on [[Nightingale]].[6]
- [[Lucifer]] Morningstar should not be counted as one of those five; he was a converted Elder God deployed separately after the five native Elder Gods entered the battlefield.[4][6][3]
- Ikor's Predation was explicitly stated to be the reason the Pantheon had so few Elder Gods remaining.[5][7]
- The Elder Gods' famous immortality was ultimately dependent upon Ikor rather than being an unlimited independent racial trait.[4][5]
- Ikor could extend this immortality beyond natural Elder Gods, with Lucifer and other selected combatants benefiting from the same system.[4][5]
- At least one specialized Priest could maintain Ikor's immortality Blessing on selected individuals.[3]
- The Elder Gods normally remained within their own territory and rarely interacted directly with outside civilizations.[4]
- Their homeland was the Wildlands, which also contained Nightingale's [[Amara Elderblood|World Tree of Positivity]].[9]
- The giant bodies displayed by the Elder Gods during the final war were constructed from the corpses of Gods and Nightingale natives.[7]
- Nightingale's Monsters were created through experiments combining Ikor's Divinity, natives and Noble Vampires.[7]
- Approximately 1,700 native inhabitants remained after the final conflict, with the dramatic decline attributed to experimentation and Predation under Ikor.[7]
- One unnamed Elder God was responsible for producing new Monsters during the final war until Velnorah eliminated it.[12][8]
- Although the Elder Gods fought as defenders of Nightingale against foreign Vampires, later revelations established that Ikor also exploited both the native population and members of his own Pantheon for power.[7]
- The exact founder of the Elder Gods Pantheon has never been identified. Amara Elderblood's role is that of Nightingale's World Tree and the native Pantheon's supporting World Tree rather than a directly stated founder.
- The Elder Gods should not be confused with [[Primordial Entities]] or [[Regulators of Existence]]. Elder God is the designation of Nightingale's native Divine race/faction rather than a synonym for those cosmic classifications.
