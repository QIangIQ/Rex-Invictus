**[[The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel (series)]]** is part of *The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel* series by [[Michael Scott]].

> **Rex Invictus canon.** Flamel material is adapted into the Rex Invictus present (c. 2021). Rex Invictus wins any conflict; [[Robespierre]] fills [[Dr. John Dee]]'s role and hunts the Philosopher's Stone rather than [[The Codex]]. See [[Flamel Series#Integration rules]].

## Flamel Series Canon (SOFTINF)

> [[Flamel Series|The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel]] is a Hexalogy written by [[Michael Scott]]. The books include: [[The Alchemyst]], [[The Magician]], [[The Sorceress]], [[The Necromancer]], [[The Warlock]] and [[The Enchantress]].

### Release Dates
- [[The Alchemyst]] - Was released on May 24 2007 (UK), May 22 2007 (USA)
- [[The Magician]] - Was released on June 5 2008 (UK), June 24 2008 (USA)
- [[The Sorceress]] - Was released on June 25 2009 (UK), May 26 2009 (USA)
- [[The Necromancer]] - Was released on 5 August 2010 (UK), May 25 2010 (USA)
- [[The Warlock]] - Was released on 2 June 2011 (UK), 24 May 2011 (USA)
- [[The Enchantress]] - Was released on 24 May 2012 (UK), 22 May 2012 (USA)

### Awards
Each of the four titles released to date has featured in the top ten of the New York Times Best Seller Children's Books list, [[The Alchemyst]] reached #2 on August 26, 2007 and as of March 24, 2008 it had sold in excess of 150,000 copies. [[The Magician]] reached #4 on 13 July, 2008 . [[The Sorceress]] reached #5.[[The Necromancer]] reached #3

### Title Significants
Each book, it would seem, in the series has a title character and there has been much speculation as to their identities. On 30 June, 2010 the author issued this statement naming the titular characters so far.
"So, to lay this to rest, here's who the first three titles relate to.
- [[The Alchemyst]] is [[Nicholas Flamel]].
- [[The Magician]] is [[Dr. John Dee]].
- [[The Sorceress]] is Perenelle Flamel.
- [[The Necromancer]] is [[Josh Newman]].
- [[The Warlock]] is Niccolò [[Niccolo Machiavelli|Machiavelli]].
- [[The Enchantress]] is [[Sophie Newman]].
— [[Michael Scott]] As posted on his Flamel's Secret Fan Forum.
