**[[Film Adaptation]]** is part of *The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel* series by [[Michael Scott]].

> **Rex Invictus canon.** Flamel material is adapted into the Rex Invictus present (c. 2021). Rex Invictus wins any conflict; [[Robespierre]] fills [[Dr. John Dee]]'s role and hunts the Philosopher's Stone rather than [[The Codex]]. See [[Flamel Series#Integration rules]].

## Flamel Series Canon (SOFTINF)

> In 2012 it was confirmed that AMPCO Studios would be making a film adaptation of [[The Alchemyst]]. Lorenzo di Bonaventura (GI Joe, Salt, Matrix, Transformers) is set to produce the series of all 6 movies, which will be scripted be [[Michael Scott]]. The first film is set to enter preliminary pre-production sometime in 2016-2017.[References 1]
