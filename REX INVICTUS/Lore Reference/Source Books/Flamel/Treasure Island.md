**[[Treasure Island]]** is part of *The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel* series by [[Michael Scott]].

> **Rex Invictus canon.** Flamel material is adapted into the Rex Invictus present (c. 2021). Rex Invictus wins any conflict; [[Robespierre]] fills [[Dr. John Dee]]'s role and hunts the Philosopher's Stone rather than [[The Codex]]. See [[Flamel Series#Integration rules]].

## Flamel Series Canon (SOFTINF)

> Treasure Island
> Treasure Island is an artificial island built in 1936-7 between [[San Francisco]] and Oakland. It is accessible via the Oakland Bay Bridge or boat. The island is at high risk of liquefaction during an earthquake as few if any of the building are built to withstand one. [[Virginia Dare]] mentions it is name after the novel by Robert Louis Stevenson as he spent sometime there while writing, she also mentions that it is sinking. She pulls on the irony of the name as it is built on landfill. Virginia, [[Josh Newman]] and [[Dr. John Dee]] use the island to escape the sack men to [[Alcatraz]].
