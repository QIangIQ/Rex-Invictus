**[[Michael Scott]]** is part of *The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel* series by [[Michael Scott]].

> **Rex Invictus canon.** Flamel material is adapted into the Rex Invictus present (c. 2021). Rex Invictus wins any conflict; [[Robespierre]] fills [[Dr. John Dee]]'s role and hunts the Philosopher's Stone rather than [[The Codex]]. See [[Flamel Series#Integration rules]].

> Michael Scott, the author of [[The Alchemyst]] series, was born in Dublin, Ireland, in 1959. When reviewing The De 'Denann Tales, the Irish Times  described him as "the King of Fantasy in these Isles". Scott has written under the pen name Anna Dillon.
