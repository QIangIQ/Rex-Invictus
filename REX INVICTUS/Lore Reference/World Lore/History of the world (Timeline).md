**[[History of the world (Timeline)]]** is a figure of Rex Invictus lore. (Lore adapted from *The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** Flamel material is adapted into the Rex Invictus present (c. 2021). Rex Invictus wins any conflict; [[Robespierre]] fills [[Dr. John Dee]]'s role and hunts the Philosopher's Stone rather than [[The Codex]]. See [[Flamel Series#Integration rules]].

## Flamel Series Canon (SOFTINF)

> In the series, the world is revealed to be older than people think it is. Earth has been ruled by five races: the [[Humani]], [[Elders]] and [[Great Elders]], [[Archons]], [[Ancients]], and [[Earthlords]]. [[Marethyu]] is older than all six races due to his time travel. [[Chronos]] may also be older than the six races due to him being a Master of Time, and each Shadowrealm having different concepts of time.

### Beginnings of Intelligent Life
In [[The Alchemyst]], there is mention of a fossil of humanoid prints that is 155 million years old. This is used as the start date. The [[Archons]] definitely existed during the Cretacous period, evidenced by [[Cernunnos]]' club. This means that the [[Ancients]] and [[Earthlords]] are older than that. The Four [[Great Races]] are certainly geologically old.

### Timeline

#### Pre-Elder
This period might be referred to in the books as the "Time Before Time." If it is revealed that it is not, please create the subsection "Time Before Time." If it is, rename this section as "Time Before Time."
- Unknown time in the past-[[Marethyu]] wanders through the timelines before any of the [[Great Races]] exist.
- After that-[[Earthlords]] exist. They create large gold-and-glass cities like the [[Nameless City]].
- After that-[[Ancients]] exist. They are famed for their libraries.
- After that-Archonw exist. They have extremely advanced technology and a more elegant way of building.

#### Elder Times
- [[Ancients]] die out or disappear from the rest of history.
- War over the [[Nameless City]] with [[Earthlords]], [[Archons]] and both types of [[Elders]], [[Great Elders]] and Elders win, Nameless City is beyond repair.
- Great Elders raise [[Danu Talis]] from the Sea Bed with the help of [[Bastet]] and some other incredibly powerful Elders.
