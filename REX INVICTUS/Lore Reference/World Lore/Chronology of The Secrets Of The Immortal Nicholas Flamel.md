### The Nature of Time & Timelines
This section is a stub. You can help by expanding it.
In the series, it is demonstrably possible to travel through time, such as when [[Marethyu]] uses [[Chronos]]' system of leygates to bring himself and the Twins roughly 10,000 years back in time to [[Danu Talis]]. What is less clear is whether time travel occurs within a single timeline, or whether it involves crossing between two or more timelines.
In [[The Alchemyst]], [[Zephaniah]] alludes that the present always branches into multiple possible timelines. These timelines are independent of each other, and there is no known method for determining which of these timelines will be followed. As such, while Zephaniah can see timelines (thanks to her limited knowledge of [[Time magic]] she learned from Chronos), the knowledge is of finite value, as she cannot anticipate which of her predictions will eventually come to pass.
Though laws governing time travel are never explicitly defined in the books, it is likely that time travel must involve multiple timelines, as this minimizes bootstrap paradoxes. For example, Marethyu is responsible for sending [[Josh Newman]] back in time to Danu Talis, but Marethyu could not exist unless Josh had already been sent back in time. The existence of multiple timelines (even ones which are completely identical but are independent of each other) could explain paradoxes such as this, though it is never confirmed. Further information about causal loops may be found here.
Revealed in [[The Lost Stories Collection]] "[[Aoife|Aoife of the Shadows]]" Preface, the Codex is Marethyu's personal journal from the future and later translated by [[Nicholas Flamel]] on the Shadowrealm Isle of Tir na nOg.
