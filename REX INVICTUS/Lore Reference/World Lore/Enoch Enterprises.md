**[[Enoch Enterprises]]** is a figure of Rex Invictus lore. (Lore adapted from *The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** Flamel material is adapted into the Rex Invictus present (c. 2021). Rex Invictus wins any conflict; [[Robespierre]] fills [[Dr. John Dee]]'s role and hunts the Philosopher's Stone rather than [[The Codex]]. See [[Flamel Series#Integration rules]].

---

## Flamel Series Canon (SOFTINF)

> Enoch Enterprises is a company owned by [[Dr. John Dee]].  Dee purchased [[Alcatraz]] using the company name so he could turn it into a Menagerie to house some of the most dangerous and oldest monsters until his Elder masters order him to release them upon [[San Francisco]].  When his masters declare Dr. Dee [[Utlaga]], he plans to awaken the monsters and use them to help him destroy the [[Elders]]. They have offices in [[London]] and San Francisco.
> In [[Ojai]], John Dee used Enoch Enterprises as an excuse for all of the destruction that he caused.
