**[[Flamel Series]]** is the hub for material from [[Michael Scott]]'s *The Secrets of the Immortal Nicholas Flamel* (six novels, 2007–2012) integrated into the vault. [[Rex Invictus]] borrows the Flamels, their bookshop in [[San Francisco]], [[Scathach]] and the [[Dr. John Dee|Dee]] role (filled by [[Robespierre]]) as a present-day crossover layer. Home: [[Rex Invictus]].

---

## Integration rules

1. **[[Rex Invictus]] first.** Where the Flamel series contradicts Rex Invictus, PJO canon or the [[00a_CANON_RULINGS]], the Rex Invictus version stands.
2. **[[Dr. John Dee|Dee]] becomes [[Robespierre]].** Everything Dr. John Dee does in the books is the pattern for [[Robespierre]], who serves the Triumvir (very probably the Roman Triumvirate Holdings) and hunts the Philosopher's Stone ([[Ch17 - Rex Invictus X|Ch17]]). [[Dr. John Dee]] himself has not appeared.
3. **Gods outrank [[Elders]].** Where a [[Nicholas Flamel|Flamel]] Elder shares a name with a god already in the vault ([[Hecate]], [[Mars Ultor]], [[Persephone]], [[Prometheus]], [[Odin]], [[Anubis]], [[Isis]] and others), the Rex Invictus or PJO god is canon. Elder history ([[Danu Talis]], the Fall, [[Shadowrealms]]) is background lore.
4. **Timeline.** The books run from 2007 to 2012; the Rex Invictus present is c. 2021. Book events used in the story are moved to the Rex Invictus present, and Flamel-series deaths of shared gods (such as [[Hecate|Hekate]] in 2012) do not apply.
5. **Not yet on the page.** [[Josh Newman]], [[Sophie Newman]], [[Niccolo Machiavelli]], [[Virginia Dare]] and the other Flamel characters have not appeared in Rex Invictus. Their notes record Flamel canon for planning.
6. **Labels.** Integrated sections are headed **Flamel Series Canon (SOFTINF)** and tagged `source/flamel`, alongside the vault's Greek History (IRL), PJO Canon, Rex Invictus and UOE labels.

## Rex Invictus crossover so far

- [[Nicholas Flamel]] and [[Nicholas Flamel|Perenelle]] run the Small Book Shop in [[San Francisco]] ([[Ch17 - Rex Invictus X|Ch17]]).
- [[Robespierre]] attacks Perenelle there after the questers lead him to the shop; [[Asmodeus]] names the Roman Triumvirate Holdings bounty.
- [[Condottiero]] leaves the questers outside the city.
- [[Scathach]] is a [[Next Generation]] vampire from the series, with her family [[Aoife]] and [[Ard-Greimne]].
