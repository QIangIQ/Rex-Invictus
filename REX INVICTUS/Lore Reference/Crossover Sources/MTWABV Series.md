**[[MTWABV Series]]** is the hub and rulebook for material adapted from the web novel *My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires* (MTWABV), integrated into the [[Rex Invictus]] vault on 2026-11-02 from a wiki import of 896 pages. It sits beside the [[Flamel Series]] as a source of lore. Rex Invictus already borrows from it: [[Victor Elderblood]], [[Clan Alioth]], [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]], [[Evergreen Family]] and Volk Fenrir come from MTWABV.

---

## Integration rules

1. **Rex Invictus first.** Anything already established in Rex Invictus, PJO canon as used by the story, or the [[00a_CANON_RULINGS|canon rulings]] wins over MTWABV.
2. **Flamel second.** Where the [[Flamel Series]] (NF) and MTWABV disagree, the Flamel version wins. MTWABV material is used to fill gaps.
3. **Different continuity.** MTWABV events (the deaths of [[Zeus]] and other Olympians at Victor's hands, the [[Elderblood Empire]] ruling Earth and Mars, the annexed pantheons) are background lore, not Rex Invictus history, unless a chapter puts them on the page.
4. **Same names, separate notes.** Mythic figures that MTWABV renames as Elderbloods (Aphrodite, Gaia, Hestia, Medusa, Metis, Nyx, Tyche, Amaterasu) were merged into the existing god notes under an "MTWABV Canon" heading. Elderblood daughters who share a name with a goddess (Persephone, Euryale, Stheno) have their own notes.
5. **Labels and folders.** There is no separate MTWABV folder; notes sit in the normal Rex Invictus folders (Immortals & Vampires/Houses, Definitions, Locations, Unaligned and so on). New notes carry the tag `source/mtwabv`. Merged sections start with an `<!-- mtwabv -->` marker and the heading "MTWABV Canon".
