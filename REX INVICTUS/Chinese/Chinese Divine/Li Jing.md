**[[Li Jing]]**, the Pagoda-Bearing Heavenly King, is commander-in-chief of the armies of the [[Heavenly Palace]] in Chinese myth and father of [[Nezha]]. His [[Rex Invictus]] profile is under [[Divine Sense#Li Jing|Divine Sense]].

---

## Chinese Myth (IRL)

The figure blends the Tang dynasty general Li Jing (571 to 649) with Vaisravana, the Buddhist guardian king of the north. In *Investiture of the Gods* he is the commander of Chentang Pass whose quarrel with his son ends when Randeng Daoren gives him a golden pagoda that can trap [[Nezha]]. In *Journey to the West* he leads the Heavenly host that fails to subdue [[Sun Wukong]].
