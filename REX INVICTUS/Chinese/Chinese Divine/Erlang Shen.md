**[[Erlang Shen]]** is a Chinese god of engineering and hunting, best known for the third eye in his forehead that sees through disguise. In [[Rex Invictus]] his [[Divine Sense]] profile is kept under [[Divine Sense#Erlang Shen|Divine Sense]].

---

## Chinese Myth (IRL)

Folk cults trace him to Li Bing's son, credited with the Dujiangyan irrigation works in Sichuan. In *Journey to the West* and *Investiture of the Gods* he is Yang Jian, nephew of the [[Jade Emperor]], who fights with a three-pointed double-edged spear and the Howling Celestial Dog. He is the one warrior of the [[Heavenly Palace]] able to match [[Sun Wukong]] change for change in their duel of transformations, and with Laozi's help he captures the [[Sun Wukong|Monkey King]].

## Rex Invictus

Listed among the Chinese cultivators in [[Divine Sense#Erlang Shen|Divine Sense]], specializing in sight that pierces illusion. Related: [[Nezha]], [[Li Jing]], [[Jiang Ziya]].
