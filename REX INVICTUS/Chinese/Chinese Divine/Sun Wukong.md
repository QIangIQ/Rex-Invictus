**[[Sun Wukong]]**, the Monkey King, is the hero of the 16th-century novel *Journey to the West* attributed to Wu Cheng'en. In [[Rex Invictus]] he is one of the named cultivators of the Chinese faction whose techniques are catalogued under [[Divine Sense#Sun Wukong|Divine Sense]].

---

## Chinese Myth (IRL)

Born from a stone egg on the Mountain of Flowers and Fruit, he learned the 72 transformations, cloud-somersaulting and immortality from the Taoist master Subhuti. He took the As-You-[[Will Solace|Will]] Gold-Banded Staff (Ruyi Jingu Bang) from the Dragon King of the Eastern Sea, erased his name from the registers of death, and styled himself Great Sage Equal to Heaven. After he wrecked the Peach Banquet and fought off the armies of the [[Heavenly Palace]], including [[Erlang Shen]] and [[Li Jing]], the [[Buddha]] pinned him under Five Elements Mountain for five hundred years. He was released to guard the monk [[Tang Sanzang]] on the pilgrimage to India and was finally made the Victorious Fighting Buddha.
