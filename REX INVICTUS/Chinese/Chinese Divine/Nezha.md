**[[Nezha]]** is a child protection deity of Chinese folk religion, the third son of the general [[Li Jing]]. In [[Rex Invictus]] his profile sits in [[Divine Sense#Nezha|Divine Sense]].

---

## Chinese Myth (IRL)

In *Investiture of the Gods* he is born after three and a half years in the womb as a ball of flesh, a reincarnation of the Pearl Spirit and a disciple of Taiyi Zhenren. As a boy he kills the third son of the Dragon King of the Eastern Sea. To spare his parents he gives back his flesh and bones, and Taiyi Zhenren rebuilds him from lotus stems. His weapons are the Fire-Tipped [[Spears|Spear]], the Universe Ring, the Red Armillary Sash and the Wind Fire Wheels he rides. He later serves the Zhou cause under [[Jiang Ziya]] and appears in *Journey to the West* fighting [[Sun Wukong]].
