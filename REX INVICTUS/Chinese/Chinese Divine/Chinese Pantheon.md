Chinese Pantheon, also known as the Ancient Chinese Pantheon, was one of the Seven Great Pantheons of Earth, formerly governing the supernatural and Divine aspects of Chinese civilization. Its supreme ruler was the [[Jade Emperor]], a God-King who presided over the Pantheon's Celestial Realm. Like the other Great Pantheons, the Chinese Pantheon possessed both a Heavenly Realm and an associated [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] connected to the supernatural system governing the passage and judgment of Souls.[1][2]
The Pantheon was completely destroyed during the Demonic War initiated by Diablo, who sacrificed its Gods and other Souls to facilitate the manifestation of himself and his elite Demonic forces in the Human World. Its annihilation was one of the most devastating events of the invasion, removing an entire Great Pantheon from the supernatural balance of Earth. By Chapter 532, its destruction had already been confirmed, and the territory associated with the former Chinese Pantheon was being described as Hell on Earth.[3][4]
Its destruction extended beyond the deaths of its Gods. The Pantheon lost its independent position within the greater system of Heavens and Hells, its Divine possessions were plundered, and its abandoned Underworld was subsequently incorporated into another major Hell during the restructuring of the supernatural afterlife system.[5][2]

### Overview
The Chinese Pantheon was one of the oldest established Divine factions associated with humanity on Earth. Before the Demonic War, it belonged to the group of Great Pantheons whose standing was supported not merely by the strength of their Gods, but also by their recognized positions within the cosmic system overseeing Life, Death, Divine authority and the circulation of Souls.[1]
Unlike smaller Pantheons, a Great Pantheon possessed an established Celestial Realm and an [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] integrated into the supernatural order. Its Gods exercised authority over their respective Concepts, while its Heaven and Hell served functions associated with the spiritual existence of the mortals connected to that Pantheon. These realms were subject to the greater regulations imposed by [[The Judges of The Abyss]] and the mechanisms responsible for maintaining the balance between the living and the dead.[2]
The Pantheon's principal territory on Earth was associated with China, while its supernatural civilization was centered upon its own Divine realms. Although its Celestial ruler is identified as the [[Jade Emperor]], the precise structure of its Divine government, the number of its Gods and the identity of its former Underworld ruler have not been established in detail by the available novel material.

### History

#### Before the Demonic War
For an extensive period preceding the events of the novel, the Chinese Pantheon functioned as one of Earth's established supernatural powers. Under the [[Jade Emperor]], its Gods formed part of the system of Divine civilizations that governed their respective territories while maintaining the broader balance between the different Pantheons.
The Pantheon's existence was connected to the religious and supernatural traditions of China, with its Divine authority extending beyond ordinary political boundaries through its Heavenly and [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] realms.
Although the precise history of its foundation has not been revealed, its recognition as one of the Great Pantheons establishes that it possessed the supernatural institutions necessary to participate in the greater Divine system.[1]
The Pantheon also existed within a political environment where conflicts between Gods, Demons, Angels and other supernatural civilizations were constrained by powerful agreements and the mechanisms of the [[Regulators of Existence]]. Such restrictions prevented the strongest factions from acting without consequences, but they did not make their territories invulnerable to a sufficiently prepared invasion.

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
The Chinese Pantheon's destruction occurred during [[Diablo]]'s invasion of Earth, which began while the leaders of the supernatural world were attending the [[Supernatural Gathering]]. The Demon King had prepared a massive offensive intended to bring his armies from [[Biblical Hell]] into the Human World, overwhelm the established supernatural factions and advance his own plans while the other rulers were unable to coordinate an immediate response.[3]
Diablo's military operations struck China and Russia before the invasion expanded into additional regions of Earth. The attack rapidly devastated the affected territories, while Demonic armies began spreading across continents. By the time the other supernatural leaders understood the scale of the offensive, the destruction of the Chinese Pantheon had already become one of its defining consequences.[3][4]
The loss of the Pantheon's Gods also created an opportunity for other supernatural factions to acquire the Divine resources it had accumulated. During the events surrounding the Arcane Realm, June met [[Poseidon|the Master]] Witch Yue, who revealed information concerning Evie Moriarty's cooperation with Diablo. Through a recording, Yue showed June an enormous collection of Divine possessions, including Artifacts, equipment and other supernatural resources believed to originate from the destroyed Chinese Pantheon.[5]
Some of these objects were contaminated by Demonic Miasma, reflecting the circumstances under which they had been obtained. Yue explained that these Divine possessions were part of the payment the Witch Queen had received for her assistance to Diablo. Their value was extraordinary because Divine Artifacts could possess abilities and qualities beyond the safe operating limits of ordinary mortals; even Witches required considerable care when handling equipment originally intended for Gods.[5]
The acquisition represented a tremendous increase in the material resources available to the Witches. Yue believed that the accumulated Divine equipment could significantly strengthen the Arcane Realm and potentially allow the Witches to compete more effectively with established Pantheons. She also suspected that only a portion of the Chinese Pantheon's remaining possessions had been collected by Evie, with other resources likely distributed among Diablo's collaborators.[5]
The catastrophe was explicitly confirmed when Ruby Scarlett, during her confrontation with [[Zagan]], contacted Esther to learn what was happening on Earth. Esther informed her that Demons had invaded China and Russia, that the invasion was advancing through other continents and that the Chinese Pantheon had been completely wiped out. Ruby was visibly shocked by the possibility that an entire Pantheon could disappear so rapidly, particularly because the supernatural world had appeared comparatively stable before the invasion began.[4]
Diablo's reasons for destroying the Pantheon were later clarified during the final stages of the Demonic War. His original plans had involved eliminating the Chinese Gods and obtaining the cooperation or use of the Demonic forces associated with their Hell. However, he had underestimated the number of Souls necessary to manifest himself and his elite forces within the Human World. The requirements eventually forced him to sacrifice the entire Pantheon instead of limiting the destruction to its Divine rulers.[6]
The ritual demanded billions of Souls, contributing to the immense scale of the destruction. Diablo was also attempting to violate the existing balance between the supernatural realms, and his arrangements were intended to buy him sufficient time before the consequences of his actions could be enforced against him.[6]
The destruction of the Chinese Pantheon therefore served more than one purpose within Diablo's campaign: it removed a Great Pantheon from the political and military balance, supplied Souls for his manifestation and deprived the former Chinese Divine order of its accumulated resources and independent institutions.

#### King Among Kings
Following the Demonic War, the disappearance of the Chinese Pantheon became one of the central examples of how drastically Earth's supernatural order had changed.
During his discussions concerning the future of the Pantheons, [[Victor Elderblood]] considered the consequences of Earth's greatly reduced population and the massive disruption to its system of Heavens and Hells. The previous arrangement had supported multiple major Divine realms, each receiving Souls through the supernatural mechanisms governing mortal existence. With enormous numbers of Humans dead and entire Pantheons destroyed, maintaining every former Realm in its original condition was no longer necessarily practical.[1]
Victor predicted that the number of officially recognized Pantheons would eventually decrease, identifying the Chinese Pantheon's destruction and the likely loss of the [[Egyptian Pantheon]]'s independent position as important developments. He expected the remaining Pantheons to acquire greater responsibilities and influence over the circulation of Souls, although he also recognized that concentrating too much authority among too few factions could create problems for the overall balance.[1]
The consequences of the Chinese Pantheon's destruction became more concrete when the Universal Tree and [[The Judges of The Abyss]] reorganized the existing Hells. Rather than maintaining the previous collection of separate [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] realms, the system was consolidated into three principal Hell regions.[2]
During this restructuring, the abandoned and purged Hell of the Chinese Pantheon was incorporated into the Hindu Underworld. The Greek Underworld was combined with [[Biblical Hell]], while the remaining Hells were gathered under the Nordic region. The resulting arrangement redistributed the responsibilities previously divided among a greater number of independent Underworlds.[2]
The incorporation of the Chinese Hell did not restore the destroyed Chinese Pantheon. Its independent government, former Divine hierarchy and original political position remained lost, while its surviving realm infrastructure was repurposed as part of the greater system governing the dead.

### Organization

#### Leadership
The Chinese Pantheon was formerly ruled by the [[Jade Emperor]], its supreme Celestial sovereign and representative among the [[God-Kings]] of Earth.
The Jade Emperor's position identified him as the ruler of the Chinese Heavenly Realm, although the precise details of his administrative authority and relationships with other Chinese Gods have not been extensively explored in the novel.
His death during the destruction of the Pantheon brought an end to its known Divine leadership. No successor has been confirmed.[4]

### Territories and Realms

#### Chinese Heaven
Chinese Heaven was the Celestial Realm formerly governed by the [[Jade Emperor]] and associated with the Divine authority of the Chinese Pantheon.
As one of the Great Pantheons, the faction possessed a Heavenly Realm participating in the supernatural structure of Earth. Such realms formed an essential part of the broader system of Divine territories and Soul-related administration.[1]
Its precise appearance, dimensions, internal divisions, geography and architectural characteristics have not been established in the available chapter material. The fate of every individual structure after the Pantheon's destruction is likewise unknown.

#### Chinese Hell
Chinese Hell was the [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] associated with the Chinese Pantheon and the negative side of its supernatural administration.
Before the destruction of the Pantheon, the realm formed part of the greater system of Hells responsible for the management and circulation of Souls. Its Demonic inhabitants were also important to [[Diablo]]'s original plans, since he intended to exploit the forces connected to the Chinese Underworld while preparing his invasion.[6]
Following the catastrophe, the realm no longer possessed its former functioning Divine administration. It was subsequently described as an abandoned, purged Underworld whose original inhabitants and governing structure had effectively been eliminated.[2]

#### Incorporation into Hindu Hell
The Chinese [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] was ultimately incorporated into the [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]]'s Hell through the restructuring arranged by the Universal Tree and the Judges of the Abyss. This merger was part of a larger effort to reduce the number of independent major Hells and reorganize the systems necessary for Soul judgment and spiritual administration.[2]
The change concerned the territory and function of the former Chinese Underworld, not the restoration of its original Pantheon. The merged realm became part of the new structure rather than remaining an autonomous Chinese Divine territory.

### Power and Abilities
The Chinese Pantheon was a Divine civilization whose principal members were Gods. As such, its original power structure was founded upon the supernatural characteristics of Divine Beings, their individual Divinities, their influence over Concepts and the institutional advantages provided by possessing recognized Heavenly and [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] realms.
However, the destruction of the faction occurred largely outside the direct perspective of the main characters. Consequently, the novel has not provided a complete record of the Chinese Gods' individual abilities, combat techniques or Divine Authorities.

#### Divine Abilities
- Divine Physiology: The Pantheon's members belonged to the Divine race and possessed the supernatural existence characteristic of Gods. Individual attributes and limitations depended upon each God's nature and development.[1]
- Conceptual Authority: Gods wield supernatural authority associated with their respective Concepts. The exact distribution of Divinities within the Chinese Pantheon remains unknown.
- [[Divine Energy]] Manipulation: As a Divine civilization, the Pantheon possessed Energy-based supernatural systems and equipment supported by Divine power. Its surviving Artifacts retained enough significance to become exceptionally valuable resources for the Arcane Realm.[5]
- Divine Artifacts: The Chinese Pantheon accumulated supernatural weapons, equipment and other resources containing Divinity. Numerous examples were acquired by other factions after its destruction, although their individual names and properties have not been identified.[5]
- Heavenly and [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] Administration: Its status as a Great Pantheon established that it possessed both Celestial and Underworld institutions participating in the greater spiritual system of Earth. Their individual administrative powers have not been described in detail.[1][2]

#### Limitations
The Chinese Pantheon's complete destruction demonstrates that its former position as a Great Pantheon did not make it invulnerable to attacks capable of overcoming its Gods and supernatural institutions. [[Diablo]]'s preparations ultimately exceeded the Pantheon's ability to survive the invasion.[4][6]
Nevertheless, the circumstances of the destruction do not establish that every individual Chinese God was personally weaker than Diablo, nor do they provide a reliable basis for ranking every member of the Pantheon against the Gods of other factions. The precise battles, opponents and methods involved in eliminating its Divine leadership were not individually documented.

### Relations

#### Diablo and the Demonic Legions
[[Diablo]] was directly responsible for the destruction of the Chinese Pantheon during his invasion of Earth. His original objectives involved eliminating its Gods and exploiting its [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] forces, but the enormous Soul requirements of his summoning ritual caused the operation to escalate into the sacrifice of the entire Pantheon.[6]

#### Arcane Realm
[[The Arcane Realm]] benefited materially from the Pantheon's destruction after Evie Moriarty obtained a substantial collection of Divine Artifacts through her dealings with [[Diablo]]. These resources were expected to considerably strengthen the Witches and improve their position among the remaining supernatural factions.[5]

#### Hindu Pantheon
The [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]] became connected to the remains of the Chinese Pantheon's supernatural territory when the abandoned Chinese Hell was incorporated into the Hindu [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] during the reorganization of the afterlife system.[2]

#### The Universal Tree and the Judges of the Abyss
[[The Universal Tree]] and [[The Judges of The Abyss]] were responsible for the broader restructuring of the Hells following the disruptions to the supernatural balance. Their arrangements prevented the former Chinese [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] from continuing as a separate, abandoned component of the original system.[2]

### Legacy
The destruction of the Chinese Pantheon was one of the most consequential events of the Demonic War because it demonstrated that even an established Great Pantheon could be eliminated in a comparatively short period. The loss of its Celestial authorities, the devastation of its territory and the distribution of its Divine possessions permanently altered the political balance among the surviving supernatural factions.[5][4]
Its disappearance also accelerated the decline of the old system in which Earth's major Pantheons independently controlled numerous distinct Heavenly and [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] realms. With the Chinese Pantheon destroyed and other factions suffering similar collapses or political transformations, the Regulators eventually reorganized the afterlife system into a smaller number of major administrative regions.[1][2]
Although the Chinese Pantheon no longer exists as an independent Divine faction, its former importance remains evident through the surviving Artifacts taken from its territories and the continued incorporation of its Underworld into the revised cosmic structure.

### Trivia
- The Chinese Pantheon was one of the original Great Pantheons of Earth, with the [[Jade Emperor]] serving as its supreme God-King.[1]
- It was among the first Great Pantheons confirmed to have been completely destroyed during the Demonic War.[4]
- [[Diablo]] originally intended to eliminate the Chinese Gods while exploiting the Demonic forces of their [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]]. His plan escalated into the destruction of the entire Pantheon because of the immense number of Souls required for his manifestation.[6]
- The destruction allowed other supernatural factions to obtain Divine resources previously belonging to the Pantheon. Evie Moriarty received a particularly valuable collection through her cooperation with Diablo.[5]
- The Chinese Hell was later incorporated into Hindu Hell rather than restored as an independent Underworld.[2]
- Although Chinese mythology includes figures such as Pangu, Nüwa, Fuxi, [[Sun Wukong]], [[Nezha]] and the Dragon Kings, their membership and abilities in the novel's Chinese Pantheon have not been independently established by the sources used for this article.
- The destruction of the Pantheon should not be confused with the complete disappearance of Chinese culture or the extinction of every Human living in China. The novel identifies the destruction of the supernatural Pantheon and the devastating Demonic invasion of its associated territory, not the erasure of Chinese civilization from all existence.
