**[[Tang Sanzang]]** is the pilgrim monk of *Journey to the West*, based on the real Buddhist monk Xuanzang (602 to 664). His [[Rex Invictus]] profile is under [[Divine Sense#Tang Sanzang|Divine Sense]].

---

## History and Myth (IRL)

The historical Xuanzang left Chang'an in 629 without imperial permission, crossed Central Asia to study at Nalanda in India, and came back in 645 with hundreds of Sanskrit texts. His account, *Great Tang Records on the Western Regions*, is a key source for medieval Central and South Asia. In the novel he is the reincarnation of the Golden Cicada, a disciple of the [[Buddha]], and demons hunt him because eating his flesh grants immortality. [[Sun Wukong]], Zhu Bajie, Sha Wujing and the White Dragon Horse guard him through eighty-one tribulations.
