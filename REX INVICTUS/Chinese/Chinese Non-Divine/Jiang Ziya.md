**[[Jiang Ziya]]** was the strategist who helped King Wen and King Wu of Zhou overthrow the Shang dynasty around 1046 BC. His [[Rex Invictus]] profile is under [[Divine Sense#Jiang Ziya|Divine Sense]].

---

## History and Myth (IRL)

Tradition says King Wen found him fishing at the Wei River with a straight hook and no bait, waiting for a ruler who wanted him. He commanded at the Battle of Muye and was enfeoffed as the first ruler of the state of Qi. The military treatise *Six Secret Teachings* is attributed to him. In *Investiture of the Gods* he is a disciple of the Primeval Lord of Heaven who carries the Apotheosis Roster and assigns the dead of the war their places as gods, with [[Nezha]], [[Li Jing]] and [[Erlang Shen]] fighting under him.
