### Appearances

#### First Mention
Chapter 81

#### Debut
Chapter 82
Tatsuya Rider, later also known as Tatsuya Fulger, is a Noble Vampire, swordsman and member of the [[Fulger Clan]]. He is the son of [[Victoria Fulger Elderblood]] and an unnamed Japanese Noble Vampire, inheriting the Fulger family's Lightning through his mother and an unusual perception-altering Illusion ability through his father. His father also trained him in Japanese swordsmanship, allowing Tatsuya to combine Lightning, Illusions and katana techniques into a highly specialized combat style.[1][2][3]
Tatsuya was initially introduced as Clan Rider's representative during the wager against [[Horseman Clan]], with his true identity as Victoria's son deliberately hidden from most of Vampire society. His first major battle eventually placed him and [[Einer Horseman]] against [[Victor Elderblood]], whose interest in Tatsuya's talent developed into one of Victor's few close friendships with another adult man.[4][5][6]
Unlike many members of Victor's extended family, Tatsuya never became a Dragon. Instead, he continued developing as a Noble Vampire along his own path. By Chapter 1171, this decision had produced extraordinary results: Tatsuya was explicitly described as the second strongest Noble Vampire, surpassed only by [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]], and both men were members of the Empire's elite capable of destroying entire Solar Systems.[7]
Victor's respect for Tatsuya also survived the enormous changes that transformed the rest of his family. At one point Victor even wanted Tatsuya to become one of his Heralds, but Tatsuya refused. Rather than forcing the matter, Victor accepted his choice and continued secretly supporting Tatsuya's independent development on the condition that he remain faithful to his own desire to become stronger.[7]

### Physical Description
At his debut, Tatsuya was a tall young man standing approximately 185 centimeters, with pale skin, long dark-blond hair and dark, almost black eyes. He wore a black Japanese yukata during the Clan Rider-Horseman Game and carried a katana, immediately reflecting the Japanese swordsmanship inherited from his paternal side.[1]
Despite being only ninety years old, Tatsuya possessed the trained physique and bearing of a dedicated swordsman. His movements were controlled and economical, and even during high-speed combat he generally maintained a cold or neutral expression.
When heavily using the Fulger Lightning inherited from Victoria, Tatsuya's body becomes surrounded by electricity. During Rairyū no Zangeki, his hair rose beneath the electrical discharge, his eyes glowed gold and one of his hands became darkened while sparks of concentrated Lightning surrounded it.[8]
The novel has not provided an equally detailed description of Tatsuya's appearance after his immense development during the Imperial Era. He nevertheless remained a Noble Vampire rather than transforming into a Dragon.[7]

### Personality
Tatsuya is stoic, reserved, independent and overwhelmingly focused upon becoming stronger. Swordsmanship has historically occupied a far greater portion of his attention than politics or romance, and even after entering [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s enormous extended family he continued following the path of a wandering martial artist rather than allowing himself to be completely absorbed into Imperial domestic life.
His obsession with improvement is one of the primary reasons Victor respects him. Their friendship began through combat rather than ordinary social interaction: Victor considered Tatsuya sufficiently talented to be useful as a sparring partner, while Tatsuya recognized that fighting Victor exposed weaknesses that conventional training did not.[9][6]
Tatsuya can become visibly irritated by Victor's behavior. Shortly after their first battle, Victor entered Victoria's residence, appeared in Tatsuya's room and immediately demanded another fight. Tatsuya's response was frustration rather than enthusiasm, particularly because he had only recently recovered and wanted to eat before being dragged into another battle.[9]
Despite complaining, Tatsuya possesses enough of the same warrior mentality that their friendship developed naturally. During their private spar, Victor repeatedly criticized the rigid manner in which Tatsuya used his supernatural abilities, comparing him to a horse that only knew how to run directly forward. Tatsuya became annoyed but continued listening and adapting because the criticism genuinely improved his fighting style.[6]
Tatsuya is capable of accepting harsh evaluations when they expose genuine weaknesses. Kaguya observed that after only a few corrections from Victor, Tatsuya's technique was already improving. The realization that he was not strong enough to protect Victoria from enemies he could not even perceive became motivation rather than an excuse to abandon his path.[6]
His attachment to Victoria is extremely strong. When Natashia appeared before Victoria after her original personality resurfaced, Tatsuya immediately covered himself in Lightning and prepared to defend his mother despite correctly recognizing that he had virtually no possibility of defeating his aunt.[10]
Victoria reciprocates that attachment intensely. She later described Tatsuya as her blood, son and hope. Victoria's lifelong insecurity over failing to inherit Fulger Lightning made Tatsuya particularly important to her because he successfully inherited both the Fulger family's Lightning and the supernatural ability of his father.[3]
Tatsuya is also unusually independent in a family increasingly centered upon Victor. This becomes most obvious in the Imperial Era. Victor once offered to make him a Herald—an extraordinary position directly beneath the Emperor—but Tatsuya refused. Victor respected the decision rather than attempting to override it, and Tatsuya continued developing through his own efforts while receiving discreet support from his friend.[7]
His independence should not be mistaken for ingratitude or hostility toward Victor. Tatsuya simply possesses a strong sense of the path he wants to follow. Chapter 1171 specifically places him among the very small number of men Victor genuinely respects, alongside War.[7]
Tatsuya is considerably less competent when dealing with romance. His interest in combat is so overwhelming that Victor recognized early that he posed virtually no romantic threat toward the women around him. Roxanne later recalled that Victor immediately trusted Tatsuya because the swordsman was effectively "married to his sword" and completely focused on becoming stronger; [[Hecate Moriarty]] had to take the initiative herself to develop their relationship.[11]
This romantic density did not prevent Tatsuya from eventually building a large household. By Chapter 1143 he had approximately five wives, including [[Hecate]]. Even that comparatively small harem overwhelmed him enough that he complained to Victoria about managing "problematic women" and questioned how Victor handled a family many times larger.[12]
Tatsuya nevertheless remained fundamentally adventure-oriented after marriage. The Fulger women described him as the type of man more interested in traveling, discovering new places and challenging powerful opponents than becoming a conventional domestic patriarch.[12]

### Synopsis

#### Vampire Society
Tatsuya was first mentioned during preparations for the Game between Clan Rider and [[Horseman Clan]]. [[Victoria Fulger Elderblood|Victoria Rider]] had challenged [[Niklaus Horseman]] for the title of Vampire Count. Rather than fighting personally, each side selected a younger representative: Niklaus selected [[Einer Horseman]], while Victoria publicly presented Tatsuya as a mercenary working for Clan Rider.[4]
The arrangement concealed Tatsuya's true identity as Victoria's son. He was approximately ninety years old, making him even younger than Einer, who was approximately 105. Despite their age, both possessed combat ability far beyond what most Vampires expected from members of their generation.[1]
Tatsuya entered the arena wearing a black yukata and wielding a katana. His fighting style immediately combined sophisticated swordsmanship with the Fulger Lightning inherited from his mother. Electricity accelerated his movements while his swordsmanship allowed him to use that speed with considerably greater precision than someone relying solely upon physical enhancement.[1][13]
Tatsuya then revealed the supernatural ability inherited from his father. Although commonly described as an Illusion, the ability operates on a deeper perceptual principle than simple false images. Tatsuya deceives an observer's ability to properly understand his existence, producing situations in which attacks, injuries and even Tatsuya's position become difficult to distinguish from illusion.[2]
The ability was powerful enough to affect multiple young Vampires observing the battle without them realizing that they had been caught in it. [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] explained that Tatsuya was making his own existence difficult for the brain to correctly comprehend; an opponent that could not properly understand what it was perceiving could not reliably fight him.[2]
Einer Horseman eventually overcame the surrounding Illusion through overwhelming power after entering his transformed state. Tatsuya reacted not with fear but with satisfaction that he had discovered another area requiring improvement.[14]
Forced into a more serious exchange, Tatsuya demonstrated Ryujin: Hakai no Sora. After assuming an Iaijutsu stance and concentrating his power, he drew his katana and released an enormous Eastern Dragon with blue eyes and golden scales surrounded by Lightning. The attack destroyed the black thorns surrounding him and tore through Einer's defenses.[14]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had become increasingly interested while watching the battle. After spending approximately six months fighting opponents vastly above himself under [[Scathach]], Victor wanted opponents closer to his own current level through whom he could measure his improvement. Tatsuya and Einer provided exactly that opportunity.[15]
Scathach therefore interrupted the original contest with a separate wager involving Victor. If Tatsuya or Einer defeated Victor, their faction would receive Scathach's support for one year. If Victor defeated both, he would receive permission to visit their Clans and use the two young Vampires as future training partners.[15]
Tatsuya and Einer initially fought Victor separately before realizing that neither could reliably defeat him alone. They therefore formed a temporary alliance, with Einer using his power to support Tatsuya while the swordsman became their principal offensive weapon.[8]
During the resulting battle, Tatsuya demonstrated Rairyū no Zangeki. After surrounding himself with enormous quantities of Lightning, he accelerated toward Victor's massive elemental attack and performed an extraordinarily fast katana draw, releasing a gigantic Lightning slash that cut Victor's falling ice structure apart.[8]
Their teamwork still failed to defeat Victor. He eventually incapacitated both young Vampires and won Scathach's separate wager, although the original political dispute between Rider and Horseman remained unresolved.[5]
Victor became more interested in Tatsuya after the battle rather than less. He entered Victoria's mansion soon afterward and directly sought another fight. Tatsuya initially struggled to understand Victor's aggressive approach to friendship, but their relationship rapidly developed around mutual training.[9]
When infiltrators were discovered within Victoria's residence, Tatsuya cooperated with Victor and eventually referred to him as a friend while asking for assistance. The moment represented a major change from the young Vampire he had only recently fought in the arena.[9]
Their later spar became as much instruction as competition. Victor repeatedly criticized the direct and inflexible way Tatsuya applied his power and pushed him to combine his abilities more creatively. Tatsuya's improvement was rapid enough for Kaguya to recognize that he, like Victor, possessed an unusual capacity to grow while fighting.[6]
The two eventually fought through the air using Lightning. When Victor fired a concentrated Energy attack toward him, Tatsuya focused Lightning into his katana and cut the projectile apart with an Iaijutsu strike, further demonstrating how quickly he was incorporating Victor's advice.[6]
Tatsuya's family situation became considerably more complicated after Natashia's original personality resurfaced. Having been raised hearing that Annasthashia was Victoria's greatest enemy, Tatsuya immediately prepared to defend his mother when Natashia approached her.[10]
Natashia evaluated Tatsuya's Lightning and noted that its quality was significantly below Victor's, comparing Victor's Lightning to first-rate Lightning and Tatsuya's to roughly third-rate. She also sensed another component mixed into his supernatural power, reflecting his inheritance from two separate parental bloodlines.[10]
Tatsuya then learned the truth regarding Natashia's divided personalities and Victoria's original relationship with her sister. His response was primarily disbelief at how complicated his family had become. The situation became even more absurd when Natashia openly referred to Victor as her husband before dragging Victoria and Tatsuya away to assist with her plans for restoring the Fulger family.[10]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Victoria later explained the depth of her attachment to Tatsuya during a private discussion with Natashia. She described him as her "blood", her "son" and her "hope".[3]
Victoria's feelings were heavily connected to her own history. Unlike the other major members of the Fulger family, Victoria had failed to inherit their signature Lightning and spent much of her life believing herself defective because of it. Tatsuya represented the opposite outcome: despite Victoria's own failure to awaken the ability, her son successfully inherited Fulger Lightning while simultaneously inheriting the supernatural power of his unnamed father.[3]
Victoria consequently regarded Tatsuya as proof that her branch of the family could continue and that the [[Fulger Bloodline]] had not ended with her inability to wield its Lightning herself.[3]

#### Demonic War
By the Demonic War, Tatsuya had become fully integrated into his maternal family and was no longer merely the hidden heir of Clan Rider. When the Demon Duke Phoenix, Thirty-Seventh Key of Solomon, attacked Fulger territory, Tatsuya fought beside [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] and [[Hecate Moriarty]] against the Demonic forces.[16]
Tatsuya's Illusions had developed substantially since his fight against Einer. Phoenix himself resisted direct manipulation, but Tatsuya could now impose his Illusions upon the surrounding environment so effectively that Phoenix remained affected indirectly even while personally recognizing the existence of the ability. Phoenix explicitly noted that this represented an evolution from Tatsuya's earlier limitations.[16]
Tatsuya combined these evolved Illusions with his Lightning and swordsmanship while Sasha moved throughout the battlefield at extreme speed. Their effectiveness forced Phoenix to stop treating them as ordinary young Vampires and recognize that allowing them to continue maturing could create serious future threats.[16]
Tatsuya again used Ryujin: Hakai no Sora, causing an enormous Eastern Lightning Dragon to rise into the sky and illuminate the entirety of Fulger territory. Despite the scale of the attack, he showed little exhaustion afterward, demonstrating how significantly his efficiency had improved since his first appearance.[16][17]
Phoenix then entered a much more dangerous state and demonstrated the difference between raw speed and experienced combat application. Although Sasha and Tatsuya were both faster in certain respects, Phoenix possessed centuries of experience fighting beings capable of moving faster than himself and used short bursts of speed far more efficiently.[17]
Tatsuya was struck before he could properly react, receiving multiple holes through his abdomen while Phoenix's Demonic Miasma burned the wound and interfered with his Vampire regeneration. His direct Illusions also failed against the Demon Duke in this state.[17]
Sasha criticized Tatsuya for neglecting the development of his Lightning. Tatsuya responded that, unlike abnormalities such as Sasha and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], he could not train every discipline simultaneously and had chosen to concentrate primarily upon his sword.[17]
Despite his injuries and slowed regeneration, Tatsuya returned to his feet and continued fighting. Sasha later ordered him to prepare his strongest attack while she directly occupied Phoenix.[17]
Tatsuya accumulated Lightning in an Iaijutsu stance until Sasha gave the signal, then released Rairyū no Zangeki. The enormous golden slash engulfed Phoenix immediately after Sasha escaped its path and destroyed the Demon's body, although Phoenix's supernatural immortality allowed him to begin recovering afterward.[17]
The battle demonstrated how drastically Tatsuya had improved since meeting Victor. His swordsmanship, Illusions and Lightning had evolved from the techniques of an unusually talented young Vampire into abilities capable of meaningfully participating in combat against a Duke of Hell.[16][17]
Tatsuya later accompanied Natashia and Sasha to England while the Fulger family searched for [[Viviane]], the Lady of the Lake. Their objective was to locate the surviving Fairies and offer them protection as the Demonic War destabilized the Human World.[18]
Viviane's territory was hidden behind a supernatural Illusion powerful enough that even Tatsuya, despite specializing in Illusions himself, could not penetrate it. The encounter provided a direct example of the limits of his ability against beings possessing far greater mastery over similar supernatural principles.[18]
Tatsuya subsequently participated in training alongside [[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]], [[Nero Elderblood]], [[Yuki Snow]], [[Adam Tepes]] and other members of the younger generation.[19]
During a conversation about children, Tatsuya confidently declared that he would raise any future child to become a warrior. Adam immediately warned him against forcing a child to follow a path they had not chosen, drawing upon his own experience beneath Vlad's expectations. Tatsuya did not dismiss the warning and said that he would consider it when the situation actually arose.[19]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s trust in Tatsuya became especially notable when his possessiveness surrounding his wives was later discussed. Victor normally monitored men approaching his family extremely carefully, but Roxanne recalled that Tatsuya was one of the only men whom Victor trusted almost immediately.[11]
The reason was simple: Tatsuya's priorities were obvious. Victor recognized that the swordsman was effectively "married to his sword" and overwhelmingly focused upon becoming stronger rather than pursuing the women around Victor. [[Hecate]] herself had been required to take the initiative in their relationship because Tatsuya was unlikely to do so.[11]
This trust distinguished Tatsuya from almost every other adult man entering Victor's increasingly possessive family environment and reflected how thoroughly their friendship had survived the centuries following their first fight.[11]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
By the Imperial Era, Tatsuya had developed a surprisingly large family despite remaining almost comically uninterested in actively pursuing romance. During a conversation among the Fulger women, Victoria explained that Tatsuya had approximately five wives, including [[Hecate Moriarty]].[12]
Victoria considered this development largely the result of the women around Tatsuya taking the initiative. Without a sufficiently determined partner forcing the issue, she doubted her son would have pursued a conventional relationship at all.[12]
Even five wives were enough to overwhelm him. Tatsuya complained to Victoria about dealing with so many problematic women and questioned how [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] could manage a household vastly larger than his own.[12]
The Fulger family also learned that one of Tatsuya's wives was pregnant. [[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]] and the other Fulger women were particularly pleased because Tatsuya's descendants ensured the continuation of their Bloodline into another generation.[12]
Marriage and approaching fatherhood did not fundamentally change Tatsuya's interests. Victoria continued describing him as an adventure-oriented man whose natural response to the enormous scale of the new Empire would be to explore, seek powerful opponents and find new challenges rather than remain permanently occupied by domestic administration.[12]
His development as a warrior eventually became considerably more important than his family expected. Unlike many relatives connected to Victor, Tatsuya never became a Dragon. He continued developing entirely as a Noble Vampire.[7]
By Chapter 1171, the results of that decision were extraordinary. Tatsuya had become the second strongest Noble Vampire in existence, with only [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] remaining above him.[7]
Both Tatsuya and Vlad were counted among the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s elite and were powerful enough to destroy entire Solar Systems. The comparison was especially notable because Tatsuya reached this level without accepting a Draconic evolution, demonstrating just how far he had pushed the original Noble Vampire path.[7]
Tatsuya's relationship with Victor had also reached an unusual level of mutual respect. Victor once wanted to appoint Tatsuya as one of his Heralds, a position that would have placed him among the Emperor's chosen representatives. Tatsuya refused.[7]
Victor did not react with anger or attempt to force him into the role. Instead, he accepted Tatsuya's decision and continued respecting the independent [[Will Solace|Will]] that had caused him to refuse such an extraordinary offer in the first place.[7]
Even after the refusal, Victor continued secretly assisting Tatsuya's development. His only condition was that Tatsuya remain faithful to his own desire to become stronger rather than abandoning the path that had defined him throughout his life.[7]
Tatsuya consequently stands as one of the clearest examples within the Imperial Era that extraordinary power does not require following Victor's exact racial evolution. While many members of the Elderblood family pursued Dragonhood, Tatsuya remained a Noble Vampire and nevertheless developed enough to stand immediately beneath the Vampire Progenitor himself.

#### Skills & Abilities
Tatsuya is an exceptionally powerful Noble Vampire whose combat style combines three independent disciplines: the [[Fulger Clan]]'s Lightning inherited through [[Victoria Fulger Elderblood]], perception-altering Illusions inherited from his unnamed Japanese father and highly developed Japanese swordsmanship. Although he was already considered extraordinarily talented during his youth, his greatest achievements occurred much later. By Chapter 1171, Tatsuya had become the second strongest Noble Vampire, below only [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]], and was counted among the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s elite beings capable of destroying entire Solar Systems.[1][2][3]
Unlike many members of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s extended family, Tatsuya never became a Dragon. His current strength is the result of continuing to develop the Noble Vampire path rather than replacing it with a Draconic evolution.[3]

##### Noble Vampire Physiology
Noble Vampire Physiology: Tatsuya is a naturally born Noble Vampire and inherited the powerful supernatural body associated with his race. Even at only ninety years old during his debut, his physical ability was already exceptional for his age.[1]
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Tatsuya possesses supernatural strength, speed, reflexes, agility, durability and stamina far beyond ordinary Humans. At ninety years old he could already exchange attacks with other exceptional young Noble Vampires and later survive direct combat against a Demon Duke.[1][4]
- Enhanced Speed and Reflexes: Speed is one of Tatsuya's principal combat strengths. His natural Vampire speed is further amplified through Fulger Lightning, allowing him to move at velocities capable of overwhelming ordinary supernatural opponents.[5][4]
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Tatsuya possesses the regenerative ability of a Noble Vampire. He remained capable of continuing to fight after Phoenix pierced and burned his abdomen, although Demonic Miasma substantially slowed his healing.[4]
- Enhanced Durability: Tatsuya can survive injuries that would immediately kill an ordinary Human and continue functioning while regeneration repairs the damage.[4]
- Enhanced Senses: As a trained Noble Vampire swordsman, Tatsuya possesses supernatural senses and battlefield perception appropriate to his species and combat specialization.[1][6]
- Longevity/Immortality: Tatsuya is extraordinarily long-lived as a Noble Vampire. He was already ninety years old during his debut while still being considered extremely young by Vampire standards.[1]
- [[Vampiric Energy]] Manipulation: Tatsuya can manipulate the supernatural Energy inherent to his Vampire physiology and combine it with Lightning and swordsmanship for combat.[7][5]
The commonly available racial abilities of Vampires that Tatsuya has not personally demonstrated are not separately indexed here as developed techniques.

##### Fulger Bloodline
Lightning Manipulation: Tatsuya inherited the [[Fulger Clan]]'s characteristic Lightning through his mother, [[Victoria Fulger Elderblood]]. This inheritance is particularly notable because Victoria herself failed to awaken the traditional Fulger Lightning despite carrying the Bloodline. Tatsuya nevertheless successfully inherited it alongside his father's supernatural power.[8][9]
- Electricity Generation: Tatsuya can generate supernatural Lightning directly from his body and release it in combat.[1]
- Electricity Manipulation: He can control, shape and concentrate his Lightning rather than merely releasing uncontrolled electrical discharges.[1][7]
- Electricity Aura: Tatsuya can envelop his body in Lightning, covering himself in continuous electrical discharge during combat.[1][5]
- Speed Enhancement: Tatsuya circulates Lightning through his body to dramatically increase his movement and attack speed. During Rairyū no Zangeki, his sword draw became fast enough that only a small number of spectators could properly perceive it.[5]
- Lightning-Enhanced Reflexes: By reinforcing his body with Lightning, Tatsuya can react and reposition at speeds substantially above his normal Vampire state.[5][4]
- Lightning Projection: Tatsuya can project concentrated Lightning beyond his body instead of relying exclusively upon close-range electrical reinforcement.[7][6]
- Lightning Weapon Infusion: He regularly channels Lightning through his katana, increasing the speed, Energy concentration and destructive power of his sword techniques.[7][5]
- Lightning Constructs: Tatsuya can shape his Lightning into large supernatural constructs. His most prominent application is the enormous Eastern Dragon created through Ryujin: Hakai no Sora.[7][6]
- Energy Accumulation: Tatsuya can remain in an Iaijutsu stance while continuously gathering Lightning and supernatural Energy before releasing the accumulated power through a single attack.[7][4]
- Aerial Mobility: During his spar with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Tatsuya was capable of fighting through the air while using Lightning to support his movement.[10]
- Large-Scale Lightning Manipulation: By the Demonic War, Tatsuya's Lightning techniques had grown sufficiently powerful for Ryujin: Hakai no Sora to illuminate the entire Fulger territory when unleashed against Phoenix.[6]
- High Energy Efficiency: Tatsuya's efficiency increased drastically over time. After using the evolved version of Ryujin: Hakai no Sora against Phoenix, Sasha observed that he did not appear significantly exhausted.[4]
During his earlier life, Tatsuya had not developed his Lightning as thoroughly as his swordsmanship. Sasha directly criticized this during the Phoenix battle, while Tatsuya explained that he could not develop every discipline simultaneously like exceptional abnormalities such as Sasha and Victor and had therefore chosen to prioritize the sword.[4]
Natashia also evaluated Tatsuya's Lightning during their first meeting. At that stage, she considered its quality substantially below Victor's, comparing Victor's Lightning to first-quality Lightning and Tatsuya's to roughly third-quality Lightning.[8]

##### Illusion Manipulation
Illusion Manipulation: Tatsuya inherited a highly unusual illusion-related Bloodline from his unnamed Japanese father. The ability is significantly more complex than creating false visual images. Its defining characteristic is its ability to interfere with how an observer comprehends Tatsuya and the surrounding Reality.[2][9]
- Perception Manipulation: Tatsuya can manipulate what targets perceive as real, making them experience attacks, injuries and situations that did not actually occur.[2]
- Sensory Manipulation: His Illusions affect an opponent's sensory interpretation strongly enough that affected Vampires may not initially recognize that they have entered an Illusion.[2]
- Existence Obfuscation: Tatsuya can make his own existence difficult for an observer's brain to correctly comprehend. [[Scathach]] explained that by becoming difficult to properly observe, Tatsuya prevents an opponent from reliably understanding what they are fighting.[2]
- Presence Concealment: By disrupting an opponent's ability to correctly comprehend him, Tatsuya can obscure his real position and movement during battle.[2]
- Illusory Substitution: Tatsuya can allow an opponent to attack a false image of himself while his real body occupies another location.[2][6]
- False Injury Inducement: He can make opponents perceive injuries that never occurred. [[Einer Horseman]] temporarily believed one of his arms had been severed before recognizing that the event itself had been an Illusion.[2]
- Reality Confusion: Tatsuya can interweave real and false attacks so effectively that an opponent may be unable to immediately determine whether a wound, movement or attack actually occurred.[2]
- Multi-Target Illusion Manipulation: Tatsuya's ability can simultaneously affect numerous individuals. During his battle with Einer, several young Vampires observing the fight were caught within the effect without realizing it.[2]
- Environmental Illusion Manipulation: Tatsuya later evolved his power from primarily affecting individuals into manipulating perception of the surrounding environment itself.[6]
- Wide-Area Illusion Manipulation: By the battle against Phoenix, Tatsuya's Illusions could influence an entire battlefield and numerous Demons simultaneously.[6]
- Indirect Perception Manipulation: Phoenix resisted Tatsuya's direct Illusions, but still remained affected by the false environmental information produced around him. Phoenix explicitly recognized that Tatsuya had evolved beyond the earlier limitation where awareness of the Illusion was sufficient to weaken the environmental effect.[6]
- Illusionary Evasion: Tatsuya can permit an enemy to apparently strike his body only for the image to disappear, revealing that the opponent attacked an Illusion instead of his real position.[2][6]
- Battlefield Misdirection: Tatsuya's ability is particularly effective in group combat because enemies cannot reliably determine which movements, targets and environmental conditions are genuine.[6]
If you can't understand me, you can't fight me since you can't fight what you don't understand.
—Tatsuya explaining the principle of his ability to Einer Horseman, Chapter 84
Tatsuya's Illusions are not absolute. Einer was capable of destroying the surrounding Illusion through overwhelming raw power after transforming, while Phoenix's greater strength and experience rendered Tatsuya's direct Illusions ineffective against him personally.[7][4]
His inability to perceive [[Viviane]]'s hidden Fairy territory also demonstrates that significantly superior illusion or concealment users can exceed his own perception and manipulation capabilities.[11]

##### Swordsmanship
Master Swordsman: Swordsmanship is Tatsuya's primary martial discipline and historically received more of his attention than either Lightning or Illusions. He was trained extensively by his biological father and specializes in Japanese katana techniques.[2][7]
- Kenjutsu: Tatsuya possesses exceptional mastery of Japanese swordsmanship and uses a katana as his principal weapon.[1][7]
- Iaijutsu: Tatsuya specializes in high-speed sword-drawing techniques. Both of his major named attacks rely upon entering an Iaijutsu stance before releasing accumulated power through a decisive draw.[7][5]
- Advanced Footwork: Tatsuya uses sophisticated martial footwork that allows him to navigate barrages of attacks with precise and efficient movement. His technique was sufficient to attract the attention of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and [[Scathach]] during his fight with Einer.[7]
- Minimal-Movement Evasion: Tatsuya avoids attacks through small, controlled adjustments rather than wasting movement, allowing him to preserve balance for immediate counterattacks.[7]
- Lightning Swordsmanship: Tatsuya combines his Fulger Lightning directly with his katana techniques, using the element to increase acceleration, cutting power and Energy output.[7][5]
- Illusion Swordsmanship: He combines sword strikes with his Illusions so that opponents cannot easily determine whether the Tatsuya they are observing, the direction of an attack or an inflicted wound is genuine.[2]
- Energy Cutting: During his spar with Victor, Tatsuya concentrated Lightning into his katana and used an Iaijutsu strike to cut Victor's incoming Energy attack in half.[10]
- Power Concentration: Tatsuya can condense large quantities of Energy into an individual sword strike rather than continuously releasing it, allowing his strongest techniques to greatly exceed his normal attacks in destructive output.[7][5][4]
- Combat Adaptation: Tatsuya is capable of rapidly modifying his technique when flaws are identified. Victor's criticism during their spar immediately produced noticeable improvement in the flexibility of his fighting style.[10]
- High-Speed Combat: Tatsuya can integrate swordsmanship with Lightning-enhanced movement without sacrificing control over his katana.[5][10]
- Combat Specialization: Tatsuya consciously chose swordsmanship as his primary field of development, accepting slower progression in other abilities in exchange for deeper mastery of his weapon.[4]

##### Techniques
Ryujin: Hakai no Sora: One of Tatsuya's signature Iaijutsu techniques. Tatsuya assumes a sword-drawing stance and concentrates a tremendous quantity of supernatural power into his katana. The pressure released during preparation can suppress surrounding attacks before he even draws the weapon. Upon executing the strike, an enormous Eastern Dragon with blue eyes and golden scales surrounded by Lightning emerges from the sword and charges toward the target.[7]
- During its first known use, the pressure generated by Tatsuya's preparation destroyed the black thorns closing around him before the Dragon itself was released.[7]
- The Lightning Dragon tore through [[Einer Horseman]]'s surrounding defenses and continued upward into [[Nightingale]]'s sky.[7]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later countered the technique by freezing the Lightning Dragon itself before destroying the frozen construct.[12]
- By Chapter 525, the technique had developed dramatically. When Tatsuya used it against Phoenix, the enormous Lightning Dragon illuminated the entire Fulger territory.[6]
- Tatsuya's later efficiency had improved enough that he was not visibly exhausted after releasing the technique against Phoenix.[4]
Rairyū no Zangeki: A high-output Iaijutsu technique in which Tatsuya surrounds himself with concentrated Lightning and channels the accumulated power into his katana. His hair rises beneath the electrical discharge, his eyes glow golden and his hand darkens beneath the concentration of Lightning before he releases an extraordinarily fast sword draw and a gigantic golden cutting wave.[5]
- Tatsuya associates the attack with the principle of "one hit, one kill", emphasizing the technique's focus upon ending an exchange through one decisive strike.[5]
- Its first demonstrated use sliced through Victor's enormous falling ice attack and divided the structure into two sections.[5]
- By Chapter 526, Tatsuya could accumulate the technique while Sasha directly fought Phoenix, maintaining enough concentration to wait for her command before releasing it.[4]
- The resulting slash engulfed Phoenix immediately after Sasha escaped and destroyed the Demon Duke's body. Phoenix survived only because of his own exceptional Demonic immortality and regenerative abilities.[4]

##### Combat Skills
Exceptional Combat Talent: Tatsuya was already one of the greatest Vampire talents of his generation during his youth and continued developing for an enormous period afterward. By Chapter 1171 he had surpassed virtually every other Noble Vampire in existence.[6][3]
- Combat Instinct: Tatsuya possesses strong instincts for danger and immediately recognized when opponents such as Natashia or Phoenix were operating at a level far beyond ordinary combatants.[8][6]
- Battlefield Awareness: He can maintain awareness of large engagements while controlling Illusions, Lightning and sword techniques simultaneously.[6][4]
- Teamwork: Tatsuya can effectively cooperate with other fighters despite normally preferring individual sword combat. He coordinated with Einer against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and later worked closely with Sasha and [[Hecate]] against Phoenix.[5][4]
- Tactical Coordination: Tatsuya trusted Sasha sufficiently to charge Rairyū no Zangeki while she remained directly in its path, releasing the attack at the exact moment required for her to escape and Phoenix to be struck.[4]
- Combat Adaptation: Tatsuya can incorporate criticism and experience into his fighting style rapidly. Victor's corrections during Chapter 93 produced immediate improvements to the flexibility of his technique.[10]
- Provocation: Tatsuya learned enough from Victor's behavior to deliberately provoke Phoenix during their confrontation, attempting to manipulate the Demon Duke's attention and emotional state.[6]
- Pain Tolerance: He continued fighting after Phoenix pierced his abdomen, burned the wound and contaminated it with Miasma that substantially slowed his regeneration.[4]
- Concentration: Tatsuya can maintain complex Illusions, Lightning reinforcement and high-output sword techniques under battlefield pressure.[6][4]
- Independent [[Will Solace|Will]]: Tatsuya possesses an unusually strong commitment to his personally chosen path. Even when Victor offered him the position of Herald, Tatsuya refused and continued pursuing strength independently. Victor respected this choice rather than forcing him to accept.[3]

##### Current Power
Second Strongest Noble Vampire: By Chapter 1171, Tatsuya is explicitly described as the second strongest Noble Vampire, surpassed only by the Vampire Progenitor [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]].[3]
- Solar System-Level Destructive Capacity: Tatsuya and Vlad are both stated to possess enough power to destroy entire Solar Systems.[3]
- Imperial Elite: Tatsuya is counted among the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s elite combatants.[3]
- Superior Noble Vampire Development: Tatsuya reached this position without becoming a Dragon, demonstrating that he developed the Noble Vampire path to a degree far beyond almost every other member of his race.[3]
- Below Vlad: Despite his growth, Vlad remains explicitly stronger than Tatsuya.[3]
- Above Other Noble Vampires: The Chapter 1171 comparison places Tatsuya above every known Noble Vampire other than Vlad.[3]
Tatsuya's current level represents an enormous progression from his early appearances. During the Demonic War he was still unable to fight a Demon Duke like Phoenix evenly and suffered from limited Lightning development and insufficient experience.[4] By Chapter 1171, he had advanced into a Solar System-destroying Imperial elite without abandoning his Vampire race.[3]

#### Weaknesses & Limitations
- Specialized Development: During his earlier life, Tatsuya could not train every supernatural discipline simultaneously and deliberately concentrated upon swordsmanship at the expense of developing his Lightning to the same degree.[4]
- Early Lightning Quality: Natashia judged Tatsuya's Lightning to be considerably lower in quality than [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s during Chapter 128.[8]
- Illusions Can Be Overpowered: [[Einer Horseman]] was capable of destroying Tatsuya's surrounding Illusion through overwhelming raw power after transforming.[7]
- Direct Illusion Resistance: Phoenix was sufficiently powerful and experienced that Tatsuya's direct Illusions failed against him, although Tatsuya could still manipulate the surrounding environment.[6][4]
- Superior Illusion Users: Tatsuya could not penetrate [[Viviane]]'s concealment protecting the Fairies' territory.[11]
- Demonic Miasma: High-level Demonic Miasma is harmful to Tatsuya. Phoenix's Miasma contaminated his wounds, weakened him and substantially slowed his Vampire regeneration.[4]
- Experience Gap (Earlier): During the Demonic War, Phoenix demonstrated that Tatsuya's superior movement speed did not compensate for the Demon Duke's vastly greater experience in using short bursts of speed during actual combat.[4]
- Below Vlad: Even after becoming the second strongest Noble Vampire, Tatsuya remains explicitly weaker than [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]].[3]
- Not a Dragon: Tatsuya has never undergone the Draconic evolution available to members of Victor's extended lineage. This is not presented as preventing his growth, as he still reached the Imperial elite through his Vampire path.[3]
