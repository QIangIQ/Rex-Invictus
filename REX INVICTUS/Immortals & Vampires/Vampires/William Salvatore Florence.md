### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 99
William Salvatore Florence was an ancient Noble Vampire, the first son and heir of the Salvatore family, the first husband of Natashia Fulger, and the biological father of [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]]. Despite descending from a Vampire family renowned for its abnormal physical resistance and possessing more than 1,800 years of natural growth, William never seriously trained or fulfilled his responsibilities as heir. Instead, after being kidnapped by Natashia and becoming her husband, he spent centuries living comfortably through his association with the powerful [[Fulger Clan]].[1]
William's marriage to Natashia also connected the unusual physical constitution of the Salvatore bloodline with Clan Fulger. The Salvatore family's principal hereditary characteristic was resistance considerably above that of ordinary Vampires, complementing the Fulger Clan's destructive lightning and helping produce Sasha's exceptionally resilient body.[1]
Despite being Sasha's father, William possessed almost no genuine paternal attachment toward her. He regarded his daughter primarily as a means through which he could maintain his connection to Natashia and the benefits offered by Clan Fulger. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] directly asked what he thought about Sasha's abusive upbringing, William openly admitted that he did not care, causing Victor to decide that both of Sasha's parents needed to be punished.[2]
William subsequently fought Victor alongside Natasha. Although his superior age, physiology and hereditary resistance initially made him overwhelmingly stronger on paper, his lack of training, extreme complacency and tendency to underestimate the newborn Vampire allowed Victor and Kaguya to repeatedly exploit openings in his defense. Victor ultimately decapitated William before completely crushing and burning his head, permanently killing him.[3][4][5]

### Physical Description
William was a mature and conventionally handsome Noble Vampire with short blond hair, green eyes and the pale complexion typical of his race. Despite being more than 1,800 years old, his Vampiric physiology preserved the appearance of a considerably younger adult.[6]
He commonly wore aristocratic clothing resembling the formal attire of a medieval nobleman, reflecting both his ancient age and his privileged lifestyle within [[Nightingale]].[6]
William initially carried himself with the composure and appearance expected of an old Noble Vampire. This dignified image rapidly disappeared once [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] began humiliating and injuring him in battle, with William becoming increasingly furious and openly aggressive.[4]

### Personality and Traits
William was lazy, complacent, selfish and opportunistic. Although he was born as the first son and heir of the Salvatore family, he never seriously worked for his Clan and apparently showed little interest in developing the responsibilities or abilities expected from his position.[1]
His personality became even more passive after meeting Natashia. At some point in his life, the Vampire Countess kidnapped him and made him her first husband. Rather than seriously resisting his circumstances or attempting to establish an independent life afterward, William grew comfortable with the enormous privileges that came from being the husband of one of [[Nightingale]]'s [[Vampire Counts]].[1]
Luna later described William as having lived "like a parasite", benefiting from the wealth and authority of Clan Fulger while contributing very little himself. Despite being more than eighteen centuries old, William had apparently never undertaken serious combat training, meaning that much of his actual fighting strength came naturally from his age, Noble Vampire physiology and the unusually resistant Salvatore bloodline.[1]
William was heavily submissive toward Natashia. Even when he disagreed with circumstances surrounding their daughter, he showed little willingness to oppose his wife and instead accepted whatever benefits resulted from remaining attached to her.[2]
His relationship with Sasha was particularly poor. William showed almost none of the affection expected from a father. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] confronted Sasha's parents over the psychological and physical damage caused by her upbringing, William openly stated that he did not care about the situation.[2]
Internally, William viewed Sasha largely as a tool. His primary interest was Natashia herself and the power and security that his connection to Clan Fulger provided his family. Sasha's existence helped preserve that relationship, making her useful to him without creating a genuine paternal bond.[2]
This attitude contrasted sharply with Victor's obsessive protectiveness toward his wives. William's casual dismissal of Sasha's suffering ultimately became the principal reason Victor decided to kill him.[2]
William was also exceptionally arrogant toward younger Vampires. Despite observing Victor's unusual behavior and knowing that he had become [[Scathach]]'s disciple, William continued viewing him primarily as a newborn who could not possibly overcome the enormous age gap between them.[3]
His arrogance was not entirely unreasonable. Even Victor understood that an older Vampire normally possessed overwhelming advantages over someone only months old. However, William allowed that knowledge to become complacency, repeatedly lowering his guard against an opponent whose fighting style centered around exploiting exactly those moments.[3][4]
Only when Victor was about to permanently kill him did William's confidence collapse completely. He attempted to negotiate for his life by invoking Sasha, arguing that Victor would hurt his wife by killing her biological father. Victor regarded the argument as hypocritical given William's earlier admission that he did not care about her.[5]

### Synopsis

#### Life Before Victor
William was born into the Salvatore family, an ancient Noble Vampire lineage whose principal distinguishing trait was physical resistance considerably above the Vampire norm. He was the family's first son and heir.[1]
Despite the expectations attached to his birth, William never seriously dedicated himself to his family or personal development. He did not train consistently and apparently made little meaningful contribution to the Salvatore family despite being its heir.[1]
At some point during his long life, William attracted the attention of Natashia Fulger, then one of [[Nightingale]]'s Vampire Countesses. Natashia kidnapped William and made him her first husband.[1]
William subsequently became deeply integrated into Clan Fulger's household. Rather than resenting his situation, he became accustomed to the luxurious lifestyle available to the husband of a Countess and spent much of his remaining life benefiting from Natashia's status.[1]
Their union eventually produced [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]]. Through William, Sasha inherited traits associated with a lineage possessing greater-than-normal physical resistance, while through Natashia she inherited the [[Fulger Clan]]'s powerful lightning.[1]
William nevertheless failed to develop a meaningful relationship with his daughter.
Natashia's alternate personality subjected Sasha to an extremely harsh upbringing intended to repeatedly test the limits of her body and lightning abilities. William did little to protect his daughter from these methods and became complicit through indifference and continued support of his wife's behavior.[2]
While Sasha came to regard the maid Julia as her genuine mother figure, her relationship with both biological parents deteriorated almost completely.

#### Vampire Society
William was first introduced around the political events involving the four Vampire Count families. Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s increasingly famous performance during [[Nightingale]]'s Clan game, William and Natasha became interested in personally meeting the newborn Vampire who had become Sasha's husband and [[Scathach]]'s disciple.[7]
He later arrived at Scathach's mansion alongside Natasha, while [[Agnes Elderblood Snow]] and [[Adonis Snow]] also visited to meet Victor.[8]
During the first gathering, William largely observed Victor with curiosity. He noticed Victor's unusual height, appearance and confidence and wondered what exactly had caused Scathach to become interested enough to personally train him.[6]
The atmosphere eventually deteriorated when Victor directly confronted Natasha over Sasha's childhood.
Victor accused Natasha of traumatizing their daughter through abusive "training". Natasha defended herself by claiming that Sasha belonged to her because she had given birth to and raised her, asserting that as the head of Clan Fulger she possessed the right to determine what happened to her daughter.[2]
Victor then deliberately turned toward William and asked for his opinion.
William's answer permanently destroyed whatever possibility existed for a peaceful relationship between them.
William casually responded that he did not care about Sasha's suffering. He cared primarily about Natasha and the advantages Clan Fulger could provide, while viewing Sasha as a useful means of preserving those benefits.[2]
Sasha was visibly devastated despite already knowing that her parents had never treated her with genuine affection.
Victor's killing intent briefly filled the room before suddenly disappearing. Instead of immediately attacking in anger, Victor calmly apologized internally to Sasha before informing her parents that, if that was truly how they regarded their daughter, then neither of them needed to continue existing.[2]
Victor grabbed William and Natasha by their faces, smashed through the wall of Scathach's mansion and carried both Vampires away from the Royal Capital using Fulger lightning.[2]

#### Battle Against Victor
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] understood that both opponents were far older and naturally stronger than himself. He therefore did not attempt the type of straightforward battle he normally preferred.
Kaguya, hidden within Victor's shadow, continuously advised him to exploit their arrogance, avoid prolonged direct exchanges and take every opportunity to inflict lethal damage.[3]
Both William and Natasha possessed the natural advantages accumulated by old Vampires. Their age had increased their physical abilities and even granted considerable resistance to conventional Vampire weaknesses that would be significantly more dangerous to younger members of their species.[3]
Victor nevertheless used William's complacency against him.
During one of their first exchanges, Victor charged him directly. William prepared to meet the attack with a clawed hand, only for Victor to suddenly disappear through Kaguya's shadow and reappear beside him. Victor immediately used an Ice Greatsword covered in Fire to sever William's entire arm.[3]
The injury infuriated William, but his regeneration prevented the loss of the limb from immediately removing him from combat.
William and Natasha subsequently pursued Victor as he repeatedly manipulated the battlefield, switched between Fire, Ice, Lightning and shadow movement, and created environmental hazards intended to distract his much older opponents.[4]
At one point Victor caused an enormous construct of ice to collapse around them. William barely reacted to the falling ice boulders; his natural resistance was sufficiently great that the impacts essentially did not hurt him.[4]
This confidence became another opening.
While William dismissed the debris as harmless, Kaguya concealed Victor's attack among the surrounding shadows. A Fire-covered Ice Sword suddenly pierced directly through William's brain.[4]
Natasha intervened before Victor could finish him. She severed William's neck herself, removed his head from the freezing body and prevented the Fire from completely destroying his brain.[9]
Despite being decapitated and having his brain pierced, William remained conscious.
He explained that Victor had failed to completely destroy his head and that, because Natasha had rescued him before the Fire spread further, he could regenerate. William stated that reconstructing his body would require only several seconds to begin, although fully restoring himself would take longer.[9]
This demonstrated both the extraordinary regenerative ability of old Noble Vampires and William's personal resistance.
Natasha subsequently protected William's detached head while continuing the fight against Victor. His body gradually regenerated until he was once again capable of participating.[9]

#### Death
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually entered his incomplete [[Vampire Count Form]], dramatically increasing his physical abilities and turning the battle more decisively in his favor.[5]
William nevertheless continued underestimating him.
During the chaos of the battle, William briefly allowed his attention to lapse. [[Scathach]], who had arrived to observe rather than interfere, immediately recognized the mistake: regardless of whether an enemy was weaker, lowering one's guard on a battlefield was foolish.[5]
Victor exploited that exact instant.
Before William could respond, Victor decapitated him again.
This time, Victor did not allow Natasha enough time to save him. William's body fell away and began burning while Victor retained his severed head.[5]
Initially furious, William demanded that Victor release him.
His anger rapidly transformed into panic once he realized Victor intended to destroy his head completely—the one form of damage his Vampiric regeneration would not allow him to survive.[5]
William attempted to bargain.
He reminded Victor that he was Sasha's biological father and argued that killing him would hurt her. He promised that he and Natasha would leave Victor alone if he was released.[5]
Victor showed no sympathy.
From his perspective, William had already admitted that he regarded Sasha as a tool rather than a daughter. Invoking her feelings only when his own life was threatened therefore carried no weight.
Victor crushed William's skull completely before setting the remains on fire, destroying his head and preventing his Vampiric regeneration from restoring him.[5]
William Salvatore Florence died permanently.

#### Aftermath and Legacy
Natasha was deeply affected by witnessing William's death despite the dysfunctional nature of their marriage. His destruction briefly destabilized her emotionally and gave [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] another opportunity to attack her.[5]
The confrontation subsequently triggered much larger consequences for Clan Fulger. Victor's continued attacks and accusations forced the personality controlling Sasha's mother to confront the damage she had inflicted upon her daughter, eventually allowing the original Natashia personality to re-emerge.[10][11]
A few days later, Victor personally told Sasha that he had killed her father rather than allowing anyone else to hide the truth from her.[1]
Sasha was affected by William's death but did not condemn Victor. Her feelings toward her father were complicated: William remained her biological parent, but his lifelong indifference meant she did not possess the same emotional bond with him that she had possessed with Julia.[12]
The aftermath also revealed more information about William himself.
While discussing Victor's victory, Luna explained that William had been more than 1,800 years old and was the first son and heir of the Salvatore family. The family possessed resistance above the normal Vampire standard, explaining part of William's unusual toughness.[1]
However, Luna also emphasized that William had largely wasted his potential. Despite his age and inheritance, he had never seriously trained. After Natashia kidnapped him, he became accustomed to a luxurious existence and relied on her status rather than cultivating his own strength.[1]
William was therefore dangerous primarily because centuries of Vampiric growth and an exceptional hereditary constitution made even an untrained old Vampire powerful.
Ruby summarized the true reason Victor could kill him: power alone did not decide battles. Physical conditioning, mentality, battle experience and the ability to exploit opportunities mattered just as much. William possessed age and raw resistance, but Victor possessed vastly superior combat mentality and was willing to exploit every mistake his opponent made.[1]

### Skills and Abilities
Despite being an ancient Noble Vampire and heir to a naturally powerful lineage, William never developed his abilities to their full potential. His combat power came principally from more than 1,800 years of natural Vampiric growth, the exceptional resistance inherited from the Salvatore family and the basic powers possessed by Noble Vampires.[1]

#### Noble Vampire Physiology
- Noble Vampire Physiology: William was a naturally born Noble Vampire and possessed the superior physical and supernatural characteristics of his species.
- Superhuman Strength: William possessed tremendous physical strength accumulated over more than eighteen centuries of Vampiric existence. Even [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] understood that directly fighting him through raw physical power would normally be extremely dangerous.[3]
- Superhuman Speed and Reflexes: William possessed the movement and reaction speed expected of an ancient Noble Vampire and could perceive Victor's high-speed attacks under ordinary circumstances.[3][4]
- Superhuman Durability: His body could withstand impacts that would annihilate Humans and significantly injure younger Vampires.[4]
- Enhanced Stamina: As an old Noble Vampire, William could continue operating after receiving extreme physical trauma, including dismemberment and severe damage to the brain.[4][9]
- Enhanced Senses: William possessed the heightened sensory abilities characteristic of Vampires, although Victor and Kaguya repeatedly bypassed them through deception and shadow movement.[3]
- Longevity: William had lived for more than 1,800 years while retaining the body and appearance of a much younger man.[1]

#### Salvatore Bloodline
- Enhanced Resistance: The primary supernatural characteristic of William's birth family was resistance considerably above the Vampire norm.[1]
- Exceptional Physical Resistance: William could ignore enormous falling blocks of ice during his battle with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], with the impacts effectively failing to meaningfully hurt him.[4]
- Pain Resistance: His physiology allowed him to continue thinking and acting despite injuries such as losing an arm, having his brain pierced and being decapitated.[3][4][9]
- Resistance to Vampire Weaknesses (Age-enhanced): Like other sufficiently old Vampires, William had developed partial resistance toward weaknesses that are significantly more dangerous to young Vampires. Kaguya explicitly warned Victor that old Vampires retain their racial weaknesses but become increasingly resistant to them with age.[3]
This resistance was not immunity. Victor's Fire remained dangerous enough to permanently kill William once his head was sufficiently damaged.[5]

#### Regeneration
- High-Speed Regeneration: William possessed the extraordinary regenerative capabilities of an ancient Noble Vampire.
- Limb Regeneration: Losing an entire arm did not permanently incapacitate him.[3]
- Brain Regeneration: William survived having an Ice Sword pierce through his brain because [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] failed to completely destroy the head before Natasha intervened.[4][9]
- Decapitation Survival: William remained fully conscious after being decapitated and could continue speaking as an isolated head.[9]
- Body Reconstruction: After Natasha separated his head from his damaged and freezing body, William stated that he could begin recreating his body within seconds, although complete regeneration would require additional time.[9]
- Fatal Limitation — Complete Head Destruction: William's regeneration could not save him after Victor completely crushed his skull and burned the remains. Destruction of the head therefore represented a sufficiently complete form of damage to permanently kill him.[5]

#### Natural Weapons
- Vampire Claws: William could harden and transform his hands into sharp claws for close-range combat. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] initially charged him, William immediately manifested a claw and prepared to tear out his heart.[3]
- Vampire Fangs: As a Noble Vampire, William possessed the sharpened fangs characteristic of his species.

#### Combat Ability
- Centuries of Experience: William's enormous age naturally gave him familiarity with the supernatural world and basic combat situations.
However, this should not be confused with genuine martial expertise. William is explicitly stated to have never seriously trained despite living for more than 1,800 years.[1]
- Basic Close-Quarters Combat: William could use his enhanced body and claws in direct combat, but demonstrated no named martial art or refined fighting system.
- Poor Combat Discipline: William repeatedly underestimated [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], lowered his guard and depended too heavily upon the assumption that an older Vampire could not lose to a newborn. This became one of the principal reasons for his death.[4][5]

### Weaknesses
- Lack of Training: Despite being over 1,800 years old, William never seriously trained. This left his technical skill and combat mentality significantly below what his age and bloodline could otherwise have supported.[1]
- Arrogance and Complacency: William consistently underestimated [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] because of the enormous age difference between them. Victor repeatedly exploited this behavior through surprise attacks.[3][4][5]
- Dependence on Raw Physiology: William relied heavily on his natural durability, regeneration and age rather than refined techniques. Once Victor used attacks capable of bypassing those advantages, William had comparatively few answers.
- Vampire Weaknesses: William retained the standard weaknesses of Vampires despite possessing increased resistance to them because of his age.[3]
- Complete Head Destruction: William could survive decapitation and even substantial brain damage as long as sufficient portions of his head remained intact, but complete destruction of the head permanently killed him.[9][5]

### Trivia

- He was the first son and heir of the Salvatore family.[1]
- The Salvatore family's defining characteristic was supernatural resistance above the Vampire norm.[1]
- Despite being his family's heir, William never meaningfully worked for the Salvatore family.[1]
- Natashia kidnapped William at some point during his life and made him her first husband.[1]
- William referred to Annasthashia as "Natasha" because he considered her full name unnecessarily long.[6]
