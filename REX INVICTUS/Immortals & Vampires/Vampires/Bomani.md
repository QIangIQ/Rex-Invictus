### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 669
Bomani is a Noble Vampire and one of the leaders of the Asanbosam, an extremely isolated community of Vampires living in South Africa. Unlike the overwhelming majority of modern Vampires, Bomani and his people do not descend from [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]]' sireline. The Asanbosam are remnants of an ancient Vampire bloodline descended from a Vampire Progenitor who existed before Vlad, making their lineage one of the few surviving examples of pre-Tepes Vampire ancestry.[1]
Bomani serves as one of the two recognized leaders of the Asanbosam alongside the unnamed priestess who would later become Vlad's new wife. He was introduced during the Demonic War after Yama's forces discovered the Asanbosam's concealed settlement, captured members of the tribe and used them as hostages to force Vlad into battle.[2]

### Physical Appearance
Bomani is a tall and heavily muscular man. When first shown, he wore ceremonial clothing resembling that of a shaman, similar in style to the clothing worn by the priestess who shared leadership of the Asanbosam with him, although his attire was described as more masculine.[2]
The Asanbosam as a whole display considerably more physical variation than the Vampires descended from Vlad. Different branches of their ancient bloodline possess unusual characteristics including differently colored skin, golden or blood-red eyes, metallic fangs and claws, beast-like legs, naturally armored arms and monstrous claws. Bomani's individual body was not examined in enough detail to establish which of these inherited mutations he personally possesses beyond his clearly Vampiric physiology.[2]

### Personality
Bomani is portrayed as serious, restrained and practical. Even while being held captive by Yama's forces, he did not panic or recklessly provoke his captors. Instead, his immediate concern was how the Demons had bypassed the defenses protecting the Asanbosam village. From the fact that the attackers had entered through a passage known only to the inhabitants, Bomani quickly concluded that a spy or traitor was likely operating among his people.[2]
Despite belonging to a sireline completely independent from Vlad's, Bomani addressed him respectfully as "Lord Vlad" when the Vampire King arrived. The Asanbosam nevertheless remained extremely protective of their independence and secrecy; their village maintained additional vigilance even around entrances that Vlad already knew about, showing that their relationship with the Vampire King did not erase the tribe's deeply isolationist nature.[2]

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Before Bomani personally appeared, the existence of his people was revealed during an emergency meeting concerning the expanding Demonic invasion of Earth. Vlad announced that he intended to personally assist a people known as the Asanbosam, a small community of Noble Vampires residing in South Africa. Their existence surprised several of [[Nightingale]]'s Vampires because the Asanbosam had remained so isolated that even prominent members of Vampire society knew almost nothing about them.[1]
[[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett|Scathach Scarlett]] explained that the Asanbosam were not descendants of Vlad. During her travels she had encountered their people and learned that their lineage came from an Ancient Vampire Bloodline that predated Vlad himself. Vlad confirmed that the title of "First Progenitor" did not literally mean that no Vampire Progenitors had ever existed before him. Other Progenitors had appeared in earlier ages, but most failed to preserve themselves and their descendants, whereas Vlad survived and established the civilization that came to dominate the modern Vampire race. The Asanbosam were therefore one of the rare surviving bloodlines descended from those earlier Progenitors.[1]
During the Demonic War, the Asanbosam became targets of Yama, the ruler of the Hindu [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]], and his forces. By the time Vlad reached Yama, numerous captured Vampires were summoned before him. Their appearances showed the extensive diversification that had occurred within the Asanbosam bloodline over generations: some possessed metallic teeth, unusual skin colors and golden eyes, others had beast-like legs, while another group possessed blood-red eyes and naturally formed metallic claws or arm-like armor.[2]
Among the captives were Bomani and the unnamed priestess who served beside him as the two leaders of the Vampires. Upon seeing Vlad, Bomani addressed him as "Lord Vlad". Vlad immediately recognized that Bomani and his people had been captured.[2]
Bomani explained that he did not understand how the Demons had discovered a way through the protections surrounding their village. Only the inhabitants knew every entrance, while Vlad knew only a limited number of them, and the Asanbosam had deliberately increased security around the entrances known to outsiders. Because Yama's forces had attacked through an entrance supposedly known only to the residents themselves, Bomani concluded that there was most likely a spy within their ranks.[2]
Yama revealed that the captured Vampires were leverage. He challenged Vlad to defeat him in exchange for the Vampires, effectively using Bomani and the Asanbosam as hostages to ensure that the Vampire King participated in the confrontation. Vlad recognized the tactic immediately and nevertheless engaged Yama, beginning a battle between the Vampire Progenitor and the Demon King.[2]
The confrontation escalated dramatically as Vlad abandoned his Human disguise and demonstrated the monstrous True Form of the Vampire Progenitor. Yama eventually revealed far more power than initially expected, while Merlin secretly supported him and intervened when the battle became too dangerous. The conflict ultimately ended without Yama obtaining the decisive result he wanted, with Merlin helping withdraw the Demonic side from the confrontation.[3] Bomani ceased to be a central participant once the battle between Vlad and Yama began, and his later individual activities following the rescue of the Asanbosam have not been extensively shown.

### Skills & Abilities
As the chief and one of the two leaders of an isolated community of Noble Vampires descended from a surviving pre-Vlad Progenitor bloodline, Bomani possesses the fundamental characteristics of a Noble Vampire. However, the story has not shown him in direct combat or revealed a personal inherited ability unique to him.
- Noble Vampire Physiology: Bomani is a naturally supernatural Vampire and possesses the enhanced physiology associated with Noble Vampires, including superhuman physical capabilities, greatly enhanced senses, longevity, regeneration and the ability to sustain himself through Blood. Unlike Plebeian Vampires, Noble Vampires possess a living reproductive lineage capable of preserving and developing inherited traits over successive generations.[1][2]
- Ancient Progenitor Bloodline: Bomani does not descend from [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]]. His ancestry traces back to one of the Vampire Progenitors who existed before Vlad's era, making his bloodline fundamentally separate from the Romanian Vampires who form the majority of the modern Vampire race.[1] The survival of the Asanbosam demonstrates that traces of several ancient Vampire lineages endured even after the Progenitors who originated them disappeared.[2]
- Asanbosam Physiology: Thousands of years of independent development caused different sections of the Asanbosam sireline to manifest unusual biological characteristics. Recorded traits among the tribe include metallic fangs, metallic claws, naturally armored forearms, monstrous arms, powerful beast-like legs, unusual skin coloration and permanent golden or blood-red eyes.[2] These characteristics are not uniformly possessed by every member of the sireline, and the exact combination inherited by Bomani has not been fully revealed.
- Enhanced Strength: Bomani is visibly tall and extremely muscular even compared with the other Vampires of his tribe. As a Noble Vampire, his body possesses strength vastly exceeding that of an ordinary Human, although no individual strength feat has yet been shown for him.[2]
- Regeneration: Like Noble Vampires in general, Bomani possesses supernatural regenerative capabilities allowing his body to recover from injuries that would be fatal to ordinary Humans.
- [[Immortality]] / Longevity: As a Noble Vampire, Bomani does not age according to normal Human limitations and can potentially survive for extremely long periods unless killed through sufficient supernatural damage.
- Enhanced Senses: Bomani possesses the heightened sensory capabilities associated with Vampires, allowing him to perceive supernatural phenomena and physical stimuli beyond normal Human limitations.
- Blood Sustenance: As a Vampire, Bomani can consume Blood as nourishment and as a means of supporting his supernatural physiology.
- Leadership: Bomani is not merely an ordinary member of the Asanbosam but one of the two individuals explicitly identified as leading the tribe. His position as Tribe Chief gives him authority over a community that had successfully remained hidden from most of the supernatural world for generations.[2]
- Security and Counter-Infiltration Awareness: After his village was compromised, Bomani immediately analyzed the manner in which the enemy had bypassed its defenses and recognized that the attack could only have occurred through information supplied from within the community. Although this is not a supernatural ability, it demonstrates his experience managing an exceptionally secretive settlement.[2]

### Relationships

#### Vampire Queen
Vlad's future wife was introduced alongside Bomani as the other leader of the captured Asanbosam. She served in a priestess-like role and wore ceremonial shaman clothing similar to Bomani's. The two jointly represented the leadership of their surviving ancient Vampire bloodline.[2]

#### Vlad Tepes
Although Bomani's sireline predates Vlad and does not descend from him, Bomani recognizes Vlad's authority and addresses him respectfully as "Lord Vlad". Vlad was already familiar with the Asanbosam and knew entrances to their hidden settlement, although their people maintained increased security around those routes. When Yama's forces captured Bomani and the tribe, Vlad personally confronted the Demon King to aid them.[1][2]

#### Asanbosam
Bomani is the chief and one of the leaders of the Asanbosam Vampires. His people are highly isolationist and maintained a hidden village in South Africa protected well enough that its entrances were normally known only to its inhabitants. Their culture and secrecy allowed a Vampire bloodline older than Vlad's to survive into the modern era.[1][2]

### Trivia
- Bomani is the only Asanbosam leader introduced by name during the group's first direct appearance; the female priestess beside him remained unnamed and later became known through her position as Vlad's new Queen.[2]
- The Asanbosam are one of the few confirmed surviving Vampire sirelines that do not originate from [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]].[1]
- Their existence demonstrates that Vlad's title of First Progenitor is not literally chronological; Vampire Progenitors existed before him, but Vlad became the dominant surviving Progenitor whose descendants formed most of modern Vampire civilization.[1]
- Bomani and the Asanbosam lived in South Africa and remained sufficiently secretive that even members of [[Nightingale]]'s upper Vampire society were unaware of their existence.[1]
- The Asanbosam's enormous physical diversity is attributed to different branches of the same ancient bloodline developing separately over the course of many generations.[2]
