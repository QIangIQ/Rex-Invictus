### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 281
Stella, also known as Stella Tepes, was the first wife of [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]], the original Queen of [[Nightingale]], mother of [[Theo Tepes]], and one of the oldest Noble Vampires belonging to the [[Tepes Clan]]. As Vlad's first wife, she predated the later Queens who joined the Vampire King's household and lived beside him for thousands of years before their marriage deteriorated through prolonged emotional neglect.[1][2]
Although Stella had once been loved by Vlad, the Vampire Progenitor gradually became detached from his wives over the millennia. His continued absence eventually created a particularly severe problem because his wives had become accustomed to drinking his Progenitor blood and their bodies began rejecting ordinary blood. Unlike several of Vlad's later wives, Stella avoided falling into a coma because her son Theo, as Vlad's firstborn, possessed enough of his father's blood for her body to accept it.[2]
Years of Stella relying upon Theo's blood and Vlad's continued abandonment eventually developed into an intimate relationship between mother and son. Vlad knew about the affair but initially ignored it until evidence of Stella's previous encounter with [[Adonis Snow]] was used to expose her infidelity to the entirety of Nightingale. The scandal made Theo and Stella's relationship public as well and damaged Vlad's reputation severely enough that his pride compelled him to personally execute them.[3][2][4]

### Physical Appearance
Stella was an exceptionally beautiful woman with long golden-blonde hair and red eyes. Her appearance retained the supernatural beauty characteristic of high-ranking Noble Vampires despite her immense age.[3]
[[Victor Elderblood]], while observing [[Adonis Snow]]'s memories, saw Stella during one of their encounters and described her as a beautiful woman with golden hair and red eyes. At the time she held the position of Vlad's Queen and was immediately recognizable as a member of the highest level of [[Nightingale]]'s Vampire society.[3]
Like other ancient Noble Vampires, Stella retained a youthful adult appearance despite having lived for several millennia.

### Personality
Stella was proud, sensual, emotionally dissatisfied and increasingly resentful toward Vlad after thousands of years of neglect. Although she had once loved the Vampire King, their relationship deteriorated as Vlad progressively lost interest in his wives while continuing to regard them possessively as things that belonged to him.[2][4]
Unlike Vlad's fourth and fifth wives, who remained loyal despite suffering from the same blood dependency, Stella sought companionship elsewhere. Her known affairs included [[Adonis Snow]] and eventually her own son, [[Theo Tepes]].[3][2]
Her encounter with Adonis demonstrates an assertive and dominant personality. According to Adonis' memories, Stella was the person who initiated the relationship and effectively took him away for several nights. Adonis was considerably weaker than her and did not appear to have much ability to refuse the Queen's advances, although Stella treated him far more affectionately than several other women who had pursued him.[3]
Her relationship with Theo was more emotionally complicated. Stella initially relied upon him because his blood was the only substitute sufficiently close to Vlad's for her body to accept. Over many years, that dependency and their mutual alienation from Vlad developed into a romantic and sexual relationship.[1][2]
Stella did not appear ashamed of her relationship with Theo once their secret was exposed. When Vlad arrived to execute them, she neither apologized nor attempted to restore their marriage. Instead, her final statement expressed complete regret over ever meeting Vlad, declaring that if she had another opportunity at life she would choose never to encounter him at all.[4]
This final reaction demonstrates how completely her feelings toward Vlad had deteriorated by the end of her life.

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Stella was first directly shown in a secluded location far from [[Nightingale]]'s Royal Capital with her son, [[Theo Tepes]]. Theo suddenly sensed danger while Stella was drinking his blood, correctly feeling the threat represented by Vlad's increasing awareness of the conspiracies occurring around the Royal Family.[1]
Stella had already been drinking Theo's blood for an extremely long period. Her dependency originated from her previous exposure to Vlad's blood. After drinking the blood of a Vampire Progenitor for millennia, her body became so accustomed to its extraordinary quality that ordinary blood became effectively intolerable. Theo represented the only sustainable substitute because, as Vlad's firstborn, he possessed slightly more of his father's Bloodline than Vlad's younger children.[2]
The relationship eventually extended beyond blood consumption. Vlad's prolonged absence and emotional abandonment caused Stella and Theo to become intimate, creating a relationship that continued secretly for years.[1][2]
Stella had already been unfaithful before her relationship with Theo became public. During the period when [[Adonis Snow]] was still alive and married to Agnes Snow, Stella encountered him and took him away for several nights. Adonis' memories later revealed that Stella herself had initiated the encounter and instructed him to stop addressing her formally as Queen.[3]
Adonis was weaker than Stella and had little control over the situation, although his memories indicated that Stella treated him much more affectionately than several other women who had pursued him.[3]
Centuries later, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] inherited Adonis' memories after consuming his Soul and discovered the affair. Realizing the political damage the information could cause, Victor contacted [[Selena Moriarty]] and arranged for the relevant memory to be weaponized against Vlad.[3]
The memory of Stella and Adonis ultimately became the spark for a much greater scandal.
Selena spread evidence of Stella's infidelity throughout Nightingale and deliberately ensured that the information reached people hostile to Vlad. Rumors rapidly expanded beyond the original affair and eventually exposed the fact that the Vampire King's [[Violet Elderblood Snow|First Wife]] had also been sleeping with her own son.[2]
The scandal spread throughout the Royal Capital with extraordinary speed.
Nightingale's Vampires already feared Vlad, but many secretly hated, envied or resented him. These enemies eagerly spread the humiliation throughout the supernatural world, turning what could have remained a Royal-family secret into a direct attack upon the Vampire King's reputation.[2]
Vlad had actually known about Stella and Theo for some time.
He had simply stopped caring enough to interfere.
After living for millennia and emotionally distancing himself from most of his wives, Vlad viewed Stella and the others less as romantic partners and more as possessions belonging to him. He no longer loved Stella as he once had, but neither was he willing to release her from their marriage.[2]
The situation changed when the affair became public.
Vlad's reputation as the King of Vampires depended heavily upon fear, authority and the image of absolute control. Allowing his wife and son to publicly humiliate him without consequence would undermine that image, and his pride made inaction impossible.[2]
Theo learned from [[Niklaus Horseman]] that the evidence had spread across Nightingale and immediately understood that both he and Stella were going to die.[4]
Rather than flee, Theo remained with his mother and used the situation as the final stage of his conspiracy against Vlad.
When Vlad arrived, he gave Stella and Theo the opportunity to speak their final words.
Stella's final response was directed entirely toward her husband. She told Vlad that meeting him had been the greatest mistake of her life and that, if she were given another chance, she wished she had never encountered him.[4]
Theo then cursed his father and attacked.
The resistance was meaningless against the Vampire Progenitor.
Vlad killed and dismembered both Stella and Theo almost immediately.[2][4]
Vlad subsequently killed his second and third wives as well after learning that they had also betrayed him. He then absorbed the bodies of Theo and the deceased women rather than destroying them outright, demonstrating the same possessiveness that had helped create the situation in the first place.[2]
Stella's death therefore marked the end of Vlad's original marriage and became one of the greatest public humiliations suffered by the Vampire King during the early history of the novel.

### Blood Dependency
Stella's dependence upon Vlad's blood was one of the central circumstances that shaped the final centuries of her life.
Although Vlad had never performed the normal Vampire marriage ritual with his wives, the extraordinary quality of his blood created a different form of dependency.
As a Vampire Progenitor, Vlad possessed blood of such extreme quality that his wives eventually became incapable of accepting ordinary substitutes after consuming it for millennia.[2]
Their bodies reacted to ordinary blood as though it were disgusting and unsuitable for consumption.

### Relationships

#### Vlad Dracula Tepes
[[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] was Stella's husband and the father of her only known child.
As Vlad's first wife, Stella was one of the earliest women to share his life after the creation of his Vampire society and eventually became the first Queen of [[Nightingale]].
Their relationship deteriorated severely over thousands of years.
Vlad gradually became emotionally numb toward his wives. By the later period of their marriage, he no longer meaningfully interacted with Stella while simultaneously refusing to let her leave because he continued thinking of his family as possessions belonging to him.[2][4]
His neglect extended even to her biological survival. Although Vlad could have easily provided the blood necessary to prevent the dependency crisis affecting his wives, he simply chose not to address the problem.[2]
Theo later described Stella as having never truly been happy with Vlad, even before the King began completely neglecting his wives.[4]
Stella's final words confirm that any affection she once possessed had disappeared entirely. Before her death, she stated that she regretted ever meeting Vlad and would choose differently if given another life.[4]

#### Theo Tepes
[[Theo Tepes]] was Stella's biological son and later her lover.
Their unusual relationship began primarily through blood.
Stella's body had become dependent upon Vlad's Progenitor blood and rejected normal substitutes. Theo, as Vlad's firstborn, possessed a sufficiently high concentration of his father's Bloodline for Stella to survive by drinking from him.[2]
She fed from Theo for years.
As Vlad continued to abandon both of them emotionally, the relationship eventually became intimate.[1][2]
Theo's feelings toward Stella extended considerably beyond physical desire. His final explanation to Niklaus revealed that one of the main reasons he wanted Vlad's throne was to free his mother from the existence Vlad had imposed upon her.[4]
Theo believed Stella had spent millennia watching Vlad acquire new wives, gradually lose interest in them and move on while refusing to release any of his previous partners.
He wanted both:
- Vlad's throne;
- and Stella's freedom.[4]
When their relationship became public, Theo refused to abandon her even though he knew remaining at her side guaranteed his death.
They ultimately faced Vlad together.[4]

#### Adonis Snow
[[Adonis Snow]] was one of Stella's known extramarital partners.
While Adonis was married to [[Agnes Elderblood Snow]], Stella encountered him and initiated a relationship that lasted several nights.[3]
Adonis was substantially weaker than the Queen and did not appear capable of meaningfully opposing her decision to take him with her. His memories nevertheless portrayed Stella as comparatively affectionate toward him.[3]
The affair ultimately became extraordinarily consequential long after it had ended.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later obtained Adonis' memories and discovered Stella's involvement. He gave the information to [[Selena Moriarty]], who used it as the basis for the scandal that eventually exposed Stella's relationship with Theo and led directly to Stella's death.[3][2]

### Skills and Abilities
Stella received very few direct combat scenes before her death, meaning the full extent of her personal power is unknown. Her confirmed supernatural abilities primarily derive from her existence as an ancient Noble Vampire who lived for several millennia.
- Noble Vampire Physiology: Stella was an extremely old Noble Vampire and member of the Tepes Royal Family. She possessed the racial characteristics common to high-ranking Vampires, amplified by thousands of years of development.
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: As an ancient Noble Vampire, Stella possessed strength, speed, durability, reflexes and stamina vastly beyond ordinary Humans and younger Vampires. No individual upper-limit feat was shown before her death.
- Regeneration: Stella possessed the regenerative capabilities inherent to Noble Vampires. The exact speed and limits of her personal regeneration were never demonstrated.
- Enhanced Senses: Noble Vampire physiology grants greatly enhanced sight, hearing, smell and other supernatural sensory capabilities.
- Longevity: Stella lived for several thousand years while maintaining the appearance of a young adult.
- Supernatural Beauty: Stella possessed the unnatural attractiveness associated with Noble Vampires and was described as a beautiful golden-haired woman.[3]
- Vampiric Charm: As an ancient Noble Vampire, Stella possessed the supernatural allure characteristic of her Race. No individual feat establishes the maximum strength of her Charm.
- Blood Consumption: Stella could sustain herself through the blood of sufficiently compatible Vampires. After becoming unable to tolerate ordinary blood, she survived by drinking Theo's blood for years.[1][2]
- High-Quality Blood Adaptation: Stella's body became adapted to the extraordinary quality of Vlad's Progenitor blood after consuming it for millennia. This allowed her to benefit from extremely high-grade blood but eventually caused severe dependency when Vlad stopped feeding her.[2] This was as much a weakness as an advantage.
- Bloodline Recognition / Compatibility: Stella's body could distinguish the quality of blood sufficiently strongly that ordinary blood was rejected while Theo's blood remained consumable because he carried a particularly concentrated portion of Vlad's Bloodline.[2]
- Ancient Vampire Experience: Having lived for thousands of years as Vlad's First Queen, Stella possessed extensive experience with [[Nightingale]], Vampire society and its Royal structure. The novel does not demonstrate specialized combat or political techniques unique to her.

### Power
The novel does not establish Stella's exact position among [[Nightingale]]'s strongest Vampires.
She was:
- thousands of years old;
- a Noble Vampire;
- the First Queen of Nightingale;
- and physically superior enough to [[Adonis Snow]] that he had little meaningful ability to resist her.[3]
However, she is never directly shown fighting an opponent of comparable strength.
Her status should therefore not be used to assign her Count-level or Progenitor-level power without evidence.
Most importantly, Stella was completely helpless against [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]]. When the Vampire Progenitor finally decided to execute her, he dismembered Stella and Theo without needing a serious battle.[2][4]
Her age consequently demonstrates that longevity alone does not place every ancient Noble Vampire near the level of a Progenitor or exceptional Vampire Count.

### Trivia
- Stella was [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]]'s first wife and the original Queen of [[Nightingale]].[2]
- She was the biological mother of [[Theo Tepes]], Vlad's firstborn son.
- Stella's first direct appearance was in Chapter 281, where she was shown drinking Theo's blood in a secluded location outside the Royal Capital.[1]
- She survived Vlad's prolonged neglect because Theo's blood contained enough of Vlad's characteristics for her body to tolerate it.[2]
- Vlad's fourth and fifth wives were less fortunate and eventually entered coma-like states after their bodies rejected ordinary blood.[2]
- The relationship between Stella and Theo developed after years of blood dependency and Vlad's abandonment.[2]
- Vlad knew about Stella and Theo before the affair became public but initially chose to ignore it.[2]
- Stella also had an affair with [[Adonis Snow]] while he was married to [[Agnes Elderblood Snow]].[3]
- Adonis' memories indicate that Stella initiated their encounter and took him away for several nights.[3]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] discovered the Adonis affair after obtaining Adonis' memories and later used that information to create political chaos for Vlad.[3]
- The scandal that ultimately destroyed Stella began with the distribution of evidence connected to her affair with Adonis before expanding into the exposure of her relationship with Theo.[3][2]
- Stella's affair with Theo became dangerous to Vlad primarily because it publicly humiliated the Vampire King and damaged the image of absolute authority he had cultivated.[2]
- Incest itself was not presented as the principal scandal within Vampire society; Stella was still publicly Vlad's wife, making Theo's relationship with her an attack upon the King's marriage, status and authority.[2]
- Before dying, Stella told Vlad that meeting him had been the greatest mistake of her life and wished that she had never encountered him.[4]
- Vlad killed Stella together with Theo and later absorbed their remains instead of simply destroying them.[2]
- Theo deliberately anticipated being absorbed and used his own death to deliver a [[New Dawn]] poison into Vlad's body.[4]
- Stella should not be confused with [[Stella Elderblood]], the later daughter of Victor Elderblood and [[Anna Elderblood]], who was named after a different Stella in Anna's family line.
