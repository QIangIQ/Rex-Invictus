### Appearances

#### Debut
[[Ch13 - Rex Invictus VI|Chapter 13]]
Luna is a Russian-born former Noble Vampire who serves as Ruby Scarlett's personal maid and is one of the oldest servants associated with Clan Scarlett. Although she spent much of the story presenting herself as an ordinary Noble Vampire maid, Luna is considerably more powerful and skilled than her position initially suggests. Her exceptional control over her own power allowed her true strength to remain unnoticed by most observers for years, with [[Victor Elderblood]] eventually realizing that the amount of power contained within her body was abnormal for a supposedly common Noble Vampire.[1]
Luna's loyalty to the Scarlett household is deeply connected to her personal history. Before joining [[Scathach]], she belonged to a Vampire family in Russia whose destruction became one of the defining events of her life. Although the experience originally drove her toward strength and eventually led her to [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], the security she later found among the Scarletts caused Luna to become increasingly content with remaining merely Ruby's maid rather than continuing to pursue greater power. Victor eventually confronted this complacency directly and forced Luna to decide whether she genuinely intended to continue walking beside people whose strength was constantly increasing. After Luna reaffirmed her determination, Victor transformed her into a Blood Dragonoid, greatly increasing her potential while ordering her to become stronger.[2]

### Physical Description
Luna is an exceptionally tall and voluptuous woman of Russian origin, standing approximately 180 centimeters. She possesses shoulder-length white hair, pale skin characteristic of Vampires and striking pink eyes. Her most immediately noticeable physical characteristic is her extremely large bust, which Kaguya internally considered comparable even to Ruby, herself an exceptionally well-endowed woman.[3]
As Ruby's personal maid, Luna traditionally wears a maid uniform and usually presents herself with the polished appearance expected of a servant belonging to one of [[Nightingale]]'s major Count families. Her relatively harmless appearance and tendency to suppress virtually every unnecessary fluctuation of supernatural power make it extremely easy to underestimate her.[1]
After being transformed by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] into a Blood Dragonoid, Luna retained her humanoid body but developed clearly Draconic characteristics. Horns appeared upon her head and her eyes transformed into those of a Dragon. Unlike a True Dragon, however, Luna did not develop wings during the transformation and possesses no complete Dragon Form because the change did not grant her the Soul of a True Dragon.[2]

### Personality and Traits
Luna is playful, teasing and considerably less formal than her position as a Count family's maid might initially suggest. Her earliest appearance establishes this side of her through her interactions with Ruby. Luna deliberately attempted to provoke her mistress with false claims about destroying Ruby's possessions, only for Ruby to immediately recognize the prank because Luna had attempted similar tricks before. Rather than becoming discouraged when her attempt failed, Luna simply began considering another method of provoking Ruby, demonstrating the comfortable and unusually informal relationship between mistress and maid.[3]
Beneath this playful behavior is a disciplined and experienced servant. Luna can immediately become serious when [[Scathach]] speaks to her in an official capacity, responding more like a trained subordinate than an ordinary domestic maid. She has been trusted with sensitive information, reconnaissance, prisoner management, confidential household matters and missions associated with Clan Scarlett, making her position considerably broader than merely cleaning or serving meals.[4][5][6]
Luna also possesses a subtle sadistic streak and enjoys provoking people she knows well. This personality fits comfortably within the unusually violent and eccentric Scarlett household, although much of Luna's sadism is considerably less extreme than the battle obsession displayed by Scathach or several of her daughters. Her playful exterior frequently causes observers to underestimate how dangerous she can actually be.
Despite her confidence in ordinary circumstances, Luna possesses deep insecurities concerning her place within the Scarlett family. Although she had lived and served beside Scathach and her daughters for an extremely long period, she was not one of Scathach's biological or adopted daughters and had never undergone the formal Clan Initiation Ritual that would have incorporated her into the Scarlett bloodline and granted her its inherited abilities. Luna consequently sometimes viewed herself as fundamentally different from the women she served—as merely a maid standing beside a family of monsters whose strength and potential she could never truly equal.[7]
These insecurities surfaced when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family received a supernatural blessing associated with familial bonds. Luna questioned why she had been included alongside members of Victor and Scathach's family, revealing that despite decades of loyalty she had never completely internalized the idea that she herself belonged among them. Victor rejected that distinction and treated her continued presence beside Ruby and Scathach as sufficient reason for Luna to be considered part of the family surrounding them.[7]
Luna's insecurity regarding her own potential is particularly ironic because she possesses far greater power than she normally displays. When Victor later examined her through his Draconic perception, he was astonished by the quantity of Energy contained inside her. Luna was not deliberately suppressing herself through some specialized concealment technique; her control was simply so refined that practically no Energy escaped her body. Unless someone deliberately focused their senses upon her, she could appear far weaker than she actually was.[1]
This extraordinary control reflects one of Luna's most important traits: she is highly talented but historically lacked the ambition necessary to exploit that talent to its full potential. After finding a stable life beside Scathach and Ruby, Luna gradually became satisfied with her position and stopped actively seeking major advancement. Victor eventually recognized that complacency as her greatest weakness.[2]
When Victor threatened to remove Luna from Ruby's side if she remained content with weakness, he deliberately forced her to confront the reason she had originally pursued power. Luna remembered her former home in Russia and the destruction of the Vampire Clan to which she once belonged. Their weakness had made them incapable of protecting themselves, and the tragedy had ultimately placed Luna upon the path that led her to Scathach. Remembering that past immediately changed her attitude. Rather than continuing to complain about the additional effort required to evolve, Luna seriously asked Victor what she needed to do.[2]
Victor's confrontation therefore reignited an ambition Luna had allowed to fade during her years of security. Following her evolution into a Blood Dragonoid, she accepted his order to become stronger rather than continuing to remain satisfied with merely being Ruby's maid.[2]

### Synopsis

#### The Awakening
Luna was introduced alongside Ruby Scarlett shortly after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] Walker's transformation into a Vampire. When Kaguya visited Ruby to discuss the increasingly strange consequences of Victor's transformation ritual, Luna was already serving as Ruby's personal maid and living beside her.[3]
Her first appearance immediately established the casual relationship she possessed with her mistress. Luna attempted to provoke Ruby by claiming that she had damaged something Ruby valued, but Ruby recognized the prank immediately because Luna had attempted similar tricks before. Luna responded by pouting and considering how she could successfully provoke Ruby the next time, displaying a familiarity that would have been unusual between most Noble Vampires and their servants.[3]
The atmosphere quickly became more serious as Ruby and Kaguya discussed Victor, [[Corneliu Funar]] and the abnormal transformation ritual that had connected Ruby, Violet and Sasha to the same man. Ruby's worsening thirst for Victor's blood and the possibility of conflict with Violet concerned those around her, and Luna considered contacting [[Scathach]] if Ruby's condition continued deteriorating.[3]
Luna subsequently accompanied Ruby during the growing conflict involving the Inquisition. When Ruby and Luna were surrounded and injured by the hunters, Victor sensed Ruby's blood and pain through their supernatural connection. His discovery of Ruby and the wounded Luna caused him to abandon his confrontation with Corneliu and rush directly across the city toward them.[8]
By the time Victor arrived, Luna had already been seriously injured while remaining beside Ruby. The situation escalated into Victor's first major battle against experienced supernatural hunters, particularly Mizuki, whose techniques were capable of harming even transformed Vampires. The confrontation eventually forced Ruby and the others to retreat after Victor himself became critically injured.[9][10]
Luna's actions during this period also resulted in Scathach becoming directly aware of the danger surrounding Ruby. When Scathach later spoke with Luna, she explicitly thanked the maid for contacting her and acknowledged that she had done well before giving her further instructions. The exchange demonstrated that Luna was trusted to act independently when Ruby's safety required intervention from the Countess.[11]

#### Vampire Society
Luna remained primarily at Ruby's side throughout [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s early months in [[Nightingale]]. During Victor's training beneath [[Scathach]], Ruby eventually returned to the Human World accompanied by Luna to investigate the remaining Inquisition prisoners and unresolved threats surrounding Victor's family. Luna therefore became involved in the quieter intelligence and support operations occurring while Victor's attention remained focused upon combat training.[12]
Ruby and Luna helped secure Victor's Human World residence and coordinated with Natalia Elderblood Alioth, [[Maria Elderblood]] and the other servants responsible for protecting his family. These tasks placed Luna inside an increasingly interconnected network of maids who originally belonged to different Count Clans but were becoming personally connected through Victor and his wives.[13]
Following Victor's six months of training, Luna remained attached to the Scarlett household and assisted with preparations surrounding the approaching political game. She personally informed Scathach when only a few days remained before the event and observed how dramatically Victor had changed since beginning his training.[14]
Luna frequently handled mundane but necessary tasks that allowed Ruby, Scathach and Victor's family to concentrate upon larger political or military issues. She procured supplies, managed documents and could immediately provide contracts or other materials when requested. This background efficiency became one of the reasons she remained a trusted member of the Scarlett household even when she was rarely placed at the center of the narrative.[15][16]
She also increasingly interacted with the wider group of maids surrounding Victor, including Kaguya, Natalia, Maria and [[Yuki Snow]]. Although these women belonged to different Clans and served different mistresses, Victor's marriages gradually drew them into a shared domestic and operational structure.[17]

#### A New Pillar
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became the Fifth Vampire Count and returned to the Human World, Luna continued acting as one of Clan Scarlett's principal attendants. When [[Scathach]] and her daughters traveled to Victor's underground residence believing Ruby might be in danger, Luna accompanied the broader Scarlett entourage, although she was temporarily away obtaining items required by the family when portions of the initial confrontation occurred.[18]
She subsequently became familiar with the enormous subterranean complex constructed beneath Victor's family residence and remained present during the Scarlett family's increasingly frequent interactions with Victor's wives, parents and maids. What had originally been several separate Noble Vampire households was gradually transforming into an extended family in which Luna could move naturally between Ruby, Scathach and Victor's people.[19]
Luna's responsibilities were not limited to domestic service. Ruby's growing scientific interests resulted in Luna assisting her and members of the Scarlett family with research and logistical work involving technology captured or acquired through Victor's campaigns. Her reliability allowed Ruby to involve her in work that could contain sensitive supernatural information rather than treating her merely as a household servant.[20]
During Victor's absences, Luna also remained among the maids helping maintain the household. She interacted naturally with Kaguya, Natalia, Roberta and the other increasingly eccentric women gathered around Victor, demonstrating that by this period she was no longer simply an isolated Scarlett servant appearing only when Ruby required something.[21][22][23]

#### Discoveries, Changes and War
As [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction continued expanding, Luna remained attached to Ruby and Clan Scarlett rather than developing an independent faction of her own. She accompanied the Scarlett women during family movements, supported Ruby's projects and remained available when Victor's wives required reliable servants to coordinate household or operational matters.[24]
Her comparatively low-profile role concealed the fact that Luna was already a highly experienced Vampire. Years of service beneath [[Scathach]] had exposed her to one of the most dangerous environments available to a Noble Vampire, and Scathach had trusted her enough to use Luna during various missions. Nevertheless, Luna herself continued thinking of her position primarily through the lens of being a maid rather than a warrior worthy of standing beside the Scarlett family.

#### Demonic War
Luna's internal conflict regarding the Scarlett family eventually became considerably clearer. Although she had spent years beside [[Scathach]], Ruby, Siena, Lacus and Pepper, she had never undergone the Clan Initiation Ritual through which a Noble Vampire formally entered a Clan and inherited aspects of its supernatural lineage. Consequently, Luna remained biologically separate from the Scarlett bloodline despite functioning socially as part of the household.[25][7]
When members of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s extended family were affected by a supernatural phenomenon emphasizing their familial connections, Luna was included among them. Rather than accepting this naturally, she questioned why someone like herself was being treated as part of the family. Her reaction exposed insecurities she had largely hidden beneath her playful personality: Luna still saw herself as an outsider standing beside Scathach and her daughters rather than as someone who genuinely belonged among them.[7]
Victor rejected the idea that Luna's lack of formal blood relation or Clan initiation erased the years she had spent with Ruby and Scathach. From his perspective, the bonds created through loyalty, shared experience and genuine affection were sufficient for Luna to be counted among the people surrounding his family.[7]
The incident did not immediately solve every insecurity Luna possessed, but it established that her relationship with the Scarlett household had long since grown beyond a conventional employer-servant contract.

#### King Among Kings
Luna's true combat potential was finally exposed when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] examined the people surrounding him through his increasingly advanced Draconic senses. Upon focusing upon Luna, Victor discovered something that left him genuinely surprised: the amount of power contained within her body should not have been possible for what appeared to be an ordinary Noble Vampire.[1]
The greater surprise was the complete absence of uncontrolled Energy fluctuations. Luna was not consciously hiding herself through illusion, stealth or a specialized suppression technique. Her control was simply so advanced that essentially none of her power leaked unnecessarily from her body. Unless an observer deliberately focused sophisticated senses upon her, Luna could easily pass as considerably weaker than she actually was.[1]
Victor consequently began regarding Luna as a kind of hidden boss among the people surrounding the Scarlett family. Even before any later racial evolution, she possessed an amount of power comparable to a properly trained Adult Vampire while maintaining near-perfect control over it. Victor also remembered that [[Scathach]] had previously trusted Luna on missions, giving further context to why an apparently ordinary maid had survived for so long beside one of the strongest Vampire families in existence.[1]
The revelation reframed many of Luna's earlier appearances. Her lack of spectacular battles was not evidence that she was physically incapable of fighting; she had simply spent most of the story occupying a support role and possessed sufficient control that her strength remained almost invisible when she was not actively using it.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Despite possessing considerable talent, Luna was one of the last important members of the Scarlett household to undergo a major racial evolution. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually summoned her and examined her development, he discovered that Luna herself was initially reluctant to change. She had grown comfortable with her existence as a Noble Vampire and regarded evolution primarily as something that would create additional work and responsibility.[2]
Victor reacted harshly to this attitude. He pointed out that Luna lived beside some of the strongest beings in existence, had access to training from [[Scathach]] and the resources of his faction, yet had allowed herself to become satisfied with remaining comparatively weak. For the personal servant of Ruby Scarlett, such complacency was unacceptable. Victor went so far as to threaten to remove her from Ruby's side if she refused to improve—not because Luna had betrayed anyone, but because he refused to permit someone in such a privileged position to deliberately stagnate.[2]
His words forced Luna to remember the event she had spent years placing behind her. Before becoming part of the Scarlett household, Luna had possessed a home and Clan in Russia. Their insufficient strength left them unable to defend themselves when disaster arrived, leading to the destruction of the people around her. That tragedy ultimately placed Luna on the path that brought her to Scathach in the first place.[2]
The memory immediately removed Luna's casual resistance. Realizing that she had become complacent precisely because Scathach and Ruby had given her the safety her former family lacked, Luna seriously asked Victor what she needed to do. Victor accepted the change in mentality and personally altered her race.[2]
Luna's Vampire body evolved into that of a Blood Dragonoid. Horns emerged from her head and her eyes acquired a Draconic appearance, but no wings appeared. Victor explained that Luna had become a simple humanoid Dragon rather than a True Dragon. She therefore lacked the Soul of a True Dragon, possessed no natural full Dragon Form and did not automatically receive the instinctive understanding of the Draconic Language and Draconic Runes possessed by [[True Dragons]].[2]
The transformation was nevertheless an enormous improvement. Luna's new existence combined her former Vampire characteristics with those of a Dragon, dramatically increasing her racial potential. Victor further clarified that although Dragonoids did not instinctively understand Draconic Runes or language, they were still Dragons and could learn such abilities through genuine study and training.[2]
Victor described the species as effectively a weakened or "nerfed" version of a True Dragon, but emphasized that even such a being remained extraordinarily powerful because of the advantages created by combining two supernatural races. More importantly, Victor had deliberately expanded Luna's potential rather than simply handing her immediate strength. What she became afterward would depend upon whether she actually trained.[2]
Before dismissing her, Victor warned that he expected visible improvement and could take back what he had granted if she disappointed him. Luna accepted the challenge, marking the end of the complacency that had defined much of her life after finding safety among the Scarletts.[2]

### Trivia
- Luna is originally from Russia.[3]
- She served Ruby as her personal maid long before Ruby's marriage to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and is one of the oldest recurring servants associated with Clan Scarlett.[3]
- Luna enjoys attempting to provoke Ruby with small pranks, although Ruby has become familiar enough with her behavior that these attempts rarely work.[3]
- Luna was the person who contacted [[Scathach]] during the early crisis surrounding Ruby and the Inquisition. Scathach later personally praised her for doing so.[26]
- Despite serving Clan Scarlett for years, Luna never underwent the Clan Initiation Ritual and therefore was not originally a biological member of the Scarlett bloodline or a natural user of its inherited Ice.[7]
- Luna's lack of formal initiation contributed to a long-standing insecurity that she was ultimately nothing more than a maid standing beside Scathach and her daughters.[7]
- Victor nevertheless considers Luna part of the broader family surrounding Clan Scarlett because of her loyalty and the years she has spent beside them.[7]
- Luna possesses exceptionally refined control over her supernatural Energy. Unless an observer deliberately focuses upon her, virtually no Energy fluctuation reveals how much power she actually contains.[1]
- Before becoming a Dragonoid, Victor judged Luna's power to be abnormal for a common Noble Vampire and comparable to that of a properly trained Adult Vampire.[1]
- Victor mentally regarded Luna as a "Hidden Boss" after discovering her concealed level of strength.[1]
- Luna's original Vampire Clan in Russia was destroyed because they lacked sufficient strength to protect themselves. The event eventually led Luna to Scathach and became the memory Victor used to break her later complacency.[2]
- Luna initially resisted evolving because she had become comfortable as a Noble Vampire and believed becoming stronger would only create additional work.[2]
- Victor threatened to remove Luna from Ruby's service if she deliberately continued neglecting her growth, considering weakness by choice unacceptable for someone with access to Scathach and his family's resources.[2]
- Following her transformation, Luna became a Blood Dragonoid possessing Dragon horns and Draconic eyes.[2]
- Luna is not a True Dragon. She possesses no True Dragon Soul, no natural Dragon Form and no instinctive mastery of Draconic Language or Draconic Runes.[2]
- Because Dragonoids are still a Dragon species, Luna can learn Draconic Language and Draconic Runes through training despite not inheriting that knowledge instinctively.[2]
