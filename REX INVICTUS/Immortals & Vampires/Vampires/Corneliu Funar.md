### Appearances

#### Debut
[[Ch08 - Rex Invictus I|Chapter 8]]
Corneliu Funar was a Noble Vampire, the heir of House Funar, and one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] Walker's earliest supernatural enemies. He lived in the United States and maintained a position at Victor's university, nominally serving as the basketball team's coach while using his Vampiric influence to remain close to Ruby Scarlett, whom he was attempting to court.[2]
Corneliu was also the master of [[Luan Davis]], a Plebian Vampire whom he had taken under his control after Luan's transformation. Their relationship was extremely abusive: although Luan remained grateful because Corneliu had rescued him from his previous sickly condition, Corneliu regularly tortured him, treated him as little more than property and expected absolute obedience.[2]
After discovering that Violet Snow had married a seemingly insignificant newborn Vampire, Corneliu redirected his attention toward her and decided to murder Victor so that he could pursue Violet himself.[3][2] He later ambushed Victor and initially overwhelmed him through the enormous difference between an experienced Noble Vampire and a three-day-old newborn. The confrontation instead triggered one of Victor's earliest abnormal transformations, and Corneliu was left helpless after Victor awakened powers normally associated with [[Vampire Counts]].[4][5]
Corneliu survived the encounter and disappeared from Victor's immediate life for centuries. After Victor became the [[Chaos]] Dragon God, however, War systematically gathered several individuals who still carried unresolved debts toward his creator. Corneliu was captured and eventually offered a choice between eternal supernatural torture and complete destruction of his Soul. Accepting that he would have acted similarly in Victor's position, Corneliu chose erasure and permanently ceased to exist.[6][1]

### Physical Appearance
Corneliu was a tall, attractive blond man possessing the refined appearance commonly associated with Noble Vampires. Luan remembered him as resembling a prince from a fairy tale when Corneliu first appeared beside his hospital bed, while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later described him as looking like an ordinary aristocratic prince.[2][4]
His eye color is inconsistent between appearances. [[Ch09 - Rex Invictus II|Chapter 9]] depicts Corneliu with green eyes, while Victor's description during their confrontation in Chapter 26 calls them blue.[3][4]
Despite his relatively youthful and aristocratic appearance, Corneliu was more than fifty years old by the beginning of the series.

### Personality
Corneliu was initially an exceptionally spoiled, arrogant and entitled Noble Vampire. [[Luan Davis]], whose understanding of Noble Vampires was almost entirely based upon his master, described Corneliu as someone who believed himself to be the center of the world and possessed an ego that was simultaneously enormous and extremely fragile.[2]
He displayed a pronounced superiority complex toward Plebian Vampires. When he discovered that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]—a Vampire he believed to be an ordinary newborn Plebian—was married to Violet, Corneliu considered the relationship inherently unacceptable. Rather than recognizing Violet's own choice, he treated her as something that should naturally belong to a Noble Vampire such as himself and decided that killing Victor would leave him free to claim her.[3][2]
His attitude toward women was similarly possessive. Before turning his attention to Violet, Corneliu had embedded himself at the university and abused his Vampiric Charm in an attempt to pursue Ruby Scarlett, one of the most desirable young Noble Vampires of her generation.[2]
Corneliu was also cruel toward those beneath him. Luan had endured his punishments and torture for approximately a year by [[Ch10 - Rex Invictus III|Chapter 10]] and had become so accustomed to being abused that he regarded further torture as relatively insignificant as long as Corneliu left his family alone.[2]
His treatment of Luan demonstrates that Corneliu did not simply consider him a subordinate. He treated the younger Vampire as a slave whose personal opinion had no importance. When Luan questioned why they needed to kill Victor, Corneliu immediately reminded him of his position and ordered obedience.[2]
Corneliu was nevertheless capable of recognizing overwhelming danger once his pride could no longer protect him. Victor's first major power eruption immediately shattered his confidence. Upon realizing that the newborn before him was displaying power that Corneliu believed should belong only to [[Vampire Counts]], he collapsed in terror and became incapable of meaningfully resisting Victor.[5]
Centuries later, Corneliu appeared considerably more resigned and self-aware. When brought before War for judgment, he did not behave like the arrogant young Noble Vampire who had attacked Victor. He understood that resistance was meaningless, questioned only why Victor had decided to settle the debt after so much time had passed, and ultimately admitted that he would probably have done the same thing if their positions had been reversed.[6][1]
Faced with eternal torture or permanent Soul destruction, Corneliu calmly selected the latter rather than beg for mercy.[1]

### Synopsis

#### The Awakening
Corneliu first appeared at [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s university while observing a basketball match between Victor and [[Luan Davis]]. Luan was Corneliu's subordinate and had attracted unwanted attention by displaying suspicious physical ability in public.[7]
Corneliu sat beside Ruby Scarlett, whom he already knew and had been unsuccessfully attempting to pursue. When Ruby noticed that Victor and Luan had damaged part of the basketball court, she warned Corneliu that supernatural beings were not supposed to attract unnecessary Human attention and expected him to discipline his subordinate.[7]
While watching Victor, Corneliu asked Ruby who the unfamiliar newborn belonged to. Ruby informed him that Victor was the husband of Violet Snow, Princess of the [[Snow Clan]]. Corneliu initially laughed at the idea that an apparently ordinary Plebian Vampire could be married to Violet, but his expression rapidly changed into open hatred once he accepted that Ruby was serious.[7]
Ruby immediately recognized that Corneliu intended to attack Victor.[3]
During the match itself, Corneliu attempted to command Luan from the stands. Victor noticed Luan looking toward the blond Noble Vampire and mocked the relationship by referring to Luan as a dog looking toward its owner. Luan eventually surrendered rather than continue disobeying Corneliu.[3]
Corneliu's frustration worsened afterward.
He reprimanded Luan for attracting attention and struck his desk hard enough to leave a handprint in the wood despite deliberately restraining himself to avoid destroying it completely. The scene revealed that Corneliu was the heir of House Funar and that his apparent work as the university's basketball coach was largely a pretext. Rather than seriously managing the team, he abused Vampiric Charm to maintain his position at the university while attempting to win Ruby's affection.[2]
Luan's memories also revealed the nature of their relationship. Corneliu had encountered Luan while the latter was hospitalized and suffering from a debilitating physical condition. Luan subsequently became a Vampire under Corneliu and remained grateful for being freed from his previous weakness, but his supposed benefactor quickly revealed himself as an abusive master who regularly tortured him.[2]
After learning that Violet was present in the city and married to Victor, Corneliu decided that Victor needed to die. He ordered Luan to assist him, reasoning that once the newborn was removed he would be free to pursue Violet himself.[2]
Ruby later warned Kaguya Blank about the developing threat. Although Corneliu had lived considerably longer than Victor and possessed the advantages of an established Noble Vampire, Kaguya showed little concern for Victor's chances and treated Corneliu as little more than another arrogant young Noble.[8]
Corneliu eventually carried out his plan.
At night, after Victor finished swimming at the university, Corneliu approached him through the empty corridors. Victor initially failed to recognize the Noble Vampire despite their previous encounter.[4]
Corneliu attacked without warning. Moving faster than Victor could initially follow, he appeared directly in front of him, seized his face and hurled him into a concrete wall. He then mocked Victor for being a weak Plebian and demanded that he abandon Violet, threatening to kill Victor and his family if he refused.[4]
Victor's lack of fear irritated him further.
Corneliu repeatedly disappeared from Victor's vision through superior speed, struck him in the abdomen, broke both of his arms and finally threw him through a window from the third floor of the building.[4]
At this stage Corneliu was physically superior to the three-day-old Victor under ordinary conditions.
Instead of finishing Victor immediately, however, Corneliu continued mocking him and declared that he intended to kill him and take Violet afterward.[4]
The threat against Victor's family and Victor's growing acceptance of his own Vampiric mentality triggered a violent change.
A pillar of Fire erupted from Victor's body.[4]
Corneliu immediately realized that something was fundamentally wrong. Victor's body became covered in flames while his eyes, teeth and overall appearance transformed into something increasingly monstrous. Corneliu recognized the flames as a power that, according to his understanding, should only have been available to [[Vampire Counts]] and demanded to know who Victor truly was.[5]
His confidence collapsed completely.
Corneliu fell to the ground in terror, eventually becoming so frightened that he lost control of his bladder. Victor crossed the distance between them almost instantly, seized Corneliu by the throat and allowed the surrounding flames to burn directly into his flesh.[5]
When Corneliu begged Victor to release him, Victor deliberately interpreted the request literally. He ripped apart Corneliu's throat before leaving him kneeling and unable to speak.[5]
Victor intended to continue tormenting him, but suddenly sensed Ruby Scarlett in mortal danger nearby. His priorities immediately changed, and he abandoned Corneliu to rush toward Ruby's battle with the Inquisition.[5]
Contrary to the older version of Corneliu's article, Corneliu did not successfully frighten Victor away and was not shown escaping from the confrontation. Victor was the one who left because Ruby was in danger after having already completely overwhelmed Corneliu.[5]
Corneliu survived his wounds, although the novel did not follow his recovery or activities immediately afterward.
For most of the following story he remained absent from Victor's life.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Centuries later, Corneliu was eventually located when [[Victor Elderblood]] decided to resolve the surviving enemies and unfinished grudges from his past.
War, acting as the Bearer of the [[Will Solace|Will]] of the [[Chaos]] Dragon God, gathered numerous individuals whose histories were connected to Victor. Corneliu appeared among the captives together with [[Kurama]], [[Luan Davis]], Lucy, Karen and others.[6]
Unlike Kurama, Corneliu already understood enough about the situation to know that escape was effectively impossible.
By then Victor had become an existence so far above the Vampire society Corneliu once knew that Corneliu no longer attempted to compare their power. He understood that the man he had once regarded as an insignificant newborn now possessed enough influence to locate him regardless of how much time had passed.[6]
When confronted by War, Corneliu questioned why Victor was pursuing him after such an enormous period had passed, noting that he and those connected to him had long since stopped interfering with Victor.[1]
The answer was simple: Victor had decided to clear away every enemy and unresolved debt that remained within his reach.
Corneliu did not attempt to claim innocence. He accepted that he had deliberately attempted to murder Victor and acknowledged that, placed in Victor's position, he probably would have made the same decision.[1]
His punishment was nevertheless significantly harsher than Luan's.
Luan had largely functioned as Corneliu's subordinate and was allowed to choose between death with the possibility of eventual reincarnation or continued existence as a slave. Corneliu had acted willingly and had personally attempted to murder Victor, resulting in War presenting him with far worse alternatives.[1]
Corneliu was allowed to choose between:
- indefinite supernatural torment under a torturer capable of producing horrors beyond conventional understanding; or
- the complete erasure of his Soul.[1]
Corneliu immediately chose Soul Erasure.
War fulfilled the sentence, completely destroying Corneliu's spiritual existence and removing any possibility of ordinary reincarnation or return.[1]
His final response contrasted sharply with the arrogance of his youth. Corneliu accepted his destruction without resistance and regarded immediate annihilation as preferable to the alternative Victor's forces could have inflicted upon him.

### Relationships

#### Luan Davis
[[Luan Davis]] was Corneliu's subordinate, servant and effectively his slave.
Corneliu encountered Luan while the latter was hospitalized because of the debilitating condition that had previously left him physically weak. Luan later became a Plebian Vampire under Corneliu and credited his master with freeing him from that condition.[2]
Luan initially imagined himself as the knight of a fairy-tale prince.
Reality was considerably worse.
Corneliu treated Luan with contempt, regularly punished and tortured him and demanded complete obedience. By [[Ch10 - Rex Invictus III|Chapter 10]], Luan had already endured this treatment for approximately a year and regarded torture as routine.[2]
Despite his abuse, Luan continued serving him partly from gratitude and partly because he understood that he lacked the power to oppose a Noble Vampire.
Corneliu also supplied Luan with blood; Luan had not personally killed anyone during his first year as a Vampire because he drank his master's blood instead.[2]
When both men were later captured by War, their punishments reflected the difference between them. Corneliu was deemed personally responsible for his actions and erased, while Luan was treated as a subordinate whose culpability was considerably lower and was allowed to continue living as a slave.[1]

#### Ruby Elderblood Scarlett
Corneliu was romantically interested in [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] before his confrontation with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
He remained around the university largely because Ruby attended there and abused his Vampiric Charm while attempting to gain access to her.[2]
Ruby did not reciprocate his interest.
She regarded Corneliu as an arrogant, immature Noble Vampire and internally mocked both his ego and his inability to admit when Victor's abilities impressed him.[3]
When she realized that learning about Victor and Violet's marriage had driven Corneliu into obvious hatred, Ruby immediately concluded that he would eventually attack Victor and considered warning either Victor or Violet.[3]

#### Violet Elderblood Snow
[[Violet Elderblood Snow]] became the primary object of Corneliu's obsession after he discovered that she was in the Human world.
Violet was regarded as one of the most desirable young Noble Vampires because she combined exceptional beauty with the rare Bloodline of [[Snow Clan]]. Corneliu considered it humiliating that someone he believed to be an ordinary Plebian newborn had become her husband.[2]
Rather than seeing Violet as a person who had chosen [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Corneliu repeatedly spoke as though she were something Victor had stolen and which rightfully should have belonged to someone of Corneliu's status.[2][4]
This possessiveness ultimately became the direct reason he attempted to murder Victor.

#### Victor Elderblood
Corneliu was one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s earliest Vampiric enemies.
Their hostility began almost entirely from Corneliu's side. Victor had little interest in him and initially struggled even to remember his name.[4]
Corneliu saw Victor as an unworthy Plebian who had somehow acquired the attention of women he believed should belong to Noble Vampires. He therefore planned and eventually attempted Victor's murder.[2][4]
The confrontation became important to Victor for an entirely different reason.
Corneliu's threats and superiority helped provoke one of Victor's earliest moments of releasing the personality and power he had unconsciously restrained after becoming a Vampire. Once Victor's abnormal Fire erupted, the difference between them reversed almost instantly.[4][5]
Victor ultimately spared Corneliu unintentionally, abandoning him only because Ruby was in immediate danger.[5]
Centuries later, Victor had still not forgotten the attempted murder.
Corneliu himself recognized the logic of Victor's decision and admitted that he would likely have acted similarly if their situations had been reversed.[1]

### Skills and Abilities
Corneliu was a relatively ordinary Noble Vampire by the standards of the wider supernatural world. He did not possess a demonstrated Count Bloodline ability, Divinity or unique named power. His danger during the beginning of the story instead came from his mature Noble Vampire physiology and the enormous difference in experience and physical development between himself and a newly transformed [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
- Noble Vampire Physiology: Corneliu was a naturally born Noble Vampire and heir to House Funar. He possessed the superior physical and supernatural characteristics associated with Noble Vampires, placing him considerably above ordinary Humans and newly created Plebian Vampires.[2]
- Superhuman Strength: Corneliu possessed enough physical strength to leave the shape of his hand embedded in a wooden desk while deliberately restraining himself to prevent the desk from being completely destroyed.[2] During his confrontation with Victor, he repeatedly overpowered the newborn physically, smashed him into a concrete wall, broke both of his arms and threw him from the third floor of the university building.[4]
- Superhuman Speed: Corneliu could initially move faster than newborn Victor could visually react. Victor repeatedly perceived him disappearing before Corneliu reappeared beside or directly in front of him to attack.[4]
- Superhuman Durability: As a Noble Vampire, Corneliu could withstand physical forces vastly exceeding Human limitations. His durability was nevertheless completely insufficient once Victor's abnormal Count-level Fire manifested.[5]
- Regeneration: Corneliu survived Victor burning and tearing apart his throat during their first battle and was alive centuries later when War captured him.[5][6] Although the actual recovery process occurred off-panel, his later survival confirms that the injury was not permanently fatal.
- Enhanced Senses: Corneliu possessed the heightened sensory characteristics of Noble Vampire physiology. No exceptional individual sensory feat has been attributed to him.
- Longevity: Corneliu was already more than fifty years old during the opening events and survived for centuries afterward without visibly aging in the Human sense.
- Supernatural Beauty: Corneliu possessed the unnatural attractiveness characteristic of Noble Vampires. Luan remembered him as resembling a fairy-tale prince when they first met.[2]
- Vampiric Charm: Corneliu could use the supernatural Charm characteristic of Vampires to mentally influence Humans. He openly abused this ability while remaining around Victor's university, using it to maintain his position there as part of his attempts to approach Ruby.[2]
- Mind Manipulation: Vampiric Charm can influence the behavior and perception of ordinary Humans. Corneliu's use of the ability was sufficiently routine that the narration directly described him as abusing it.[2]
- Plebian Vampire Creation: Noble Vampires possess the ability to transform Humans into Plebian Vampires. Corneliu was Luan's master and the Vampire responsible for giving him the supernatural "gifts" he possessed after their encounter while Luan was hospitalized.[2] Luan's later status as Corneliu's Plebian servant strongly identifies Corneliu as the source of his Vampirism.
- Blood Sustenance: Corneliu could provide his own blood as nourishment for his subordinate. Luan survived for his first year as a Vampire without killing Humans because he regularly drank Corneliu's blood instead.[2]
- Vampiric Domination (Social): Corneliu maintained overwhelming authority over Luan through the natural hierarchy between a Noble Vampire and the Plebian he controlled. Luan was terrified of disobeying him and described his own position as essentially that of a slave.[2] This should not be confused with a separately named supernatural domination ability beyond Corneliu's demonstrated Charm and master-servant relationship.
- Combat Ability: Although Corneliu was not presented as an elite warrior, decades of Vampiric development made him overwhelmingly stronger and faster than Victor under normal newborn conditions. He dominated the opening portion of their confrontation before Victor's abnormal powers awakened.[4]
- Pain Tolerance: Corneliu remained conscious after Victor's flames severely burned his neck and after Victor physically tore apart his throat, although the pain left him helpless and terrified.[5]

### Power and Limitations
Corneliu provides an early example of the enormous difference between a normal Noble Vampire and a newly transformed Vampire.
Before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] released his abnormal inherited powers, Corneliu was:
- too fast for Victor to properly follow;
- physically strong enough to casually injure him;
- capable of repeatedly breaking his body;
- and confident that Victor posed essentially no danger.[4]
However, Corneliu himself understood that his power remained vastly below the [[Vampire Counts]].
When Victor produced flames associated with the [[Snow Clan]], Corneliu immediately panicked because, according to his understanding at the time:
only Vampire Counts should possess power of that level.
[5]
The gap was so extreme that Corneliu went from completely dominating Victor to being unable to meaningfully resist him.

### Weaknesses
- Standard Vampire Weaknesses: Corneliu possessed the weaknesses associated with ordinary Noble Vampire physiology. Unlike members of certain special Bloodlines, no unique resistance to sunlight, blessed weapons or other standard Vampire weaknesses was demonstrated.
- No Demonstrated Bloodline Ability: Corneliu was never shown possessing a special inherited ability comparable to the elemental powers of the Count Clans.
- Inferior to [[Vampire Counts]]: Corneliu himself recognized Count-level supernatural power as something far beyond what a normal Noble Vampire like himself should possess.[5]
- Arrogance: His greatest practical weakness was his inability to correctly evaluate people he considered socially inferior. He assumed [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Plebian appearance meant that he was fundamentally insignificant and never seriously considered the possibility that the newborn was abnormal until it was too late.[2][4]
- Emotional Instability: Corneliu's pride could easily override rational judgment. Simply learning that Violet had married a Plebian caused him to decide upon murder despite having no real understanding of Victor's nature.[3][2]
- Fear: Once Victor displayed power Corneliu associated with Vampire Counts, Corneliu's composure collapsed completely and he became too terrified to mount an effective defense.[5]
