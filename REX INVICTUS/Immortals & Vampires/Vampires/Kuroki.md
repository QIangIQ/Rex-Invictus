### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 341
Kuroki was a Japanese Vampire who lived within Japan's supernatural society. He was one of the opportunistic supernatural beings who became involved in the pursuit of Ophis Tepes and [[Nero]] after rumors spread that the Vampire King's young daughter was wandering through Japan without her usual protection.[1]
Kuroki operated alongside the Nurarihyon [[Nura]] and other Japanese supernatural beings during the hunt. Despite initially treating the confrontation with confidence and showing irritation when Nura's illusions repeatedly failed against Nero, his composure completely disappeared when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] Alucard arrived. Victor's killing intent and supernatural pressure rendered both Kuroki and Nura incapable of moving, and Kuroki was subsequently killed during the massacre that became associated with the Crimson Nightmare.[2][3]

### Physical Appearance
Kuroki's physical appearance was not described in sufficient detail by the novel.
He is identified as a male Japanese Vampire, but his hair color, eye color, height, clothing and other distinguishing physical characteristics were never established before his death. Consequently, the current artwork used for Kuroki should be regarded as an artistic interpretation rather than a confirmed representation of his canonical appearance.

### Personality
Kuroki was arrogant, impatient and dismissive toward people he considered ineffective. During the confrontation with [[Nero]], he became visibly irritated that a comparatively small girl had continued resisting the assembled supernatural attackers and mocked [[Nura]] when the Yōkai's illusion techniques failed to deceive her.[2]
He possessed a degree of curiosity alongside his hostility. While observing Nero fighting, Kuroki noticed that she never appeared to reload her firearms and privately wondered whether her weapons possessed effectively unlimited ammunition. This demonstrates that even during combat he paid attention to unusual details regarding an opponent's equipment.[2]
Kuroki was nevertheless considerably more confident when he believed himself to be part of the stronger group. He participated in a pursuit in which numerous supernatural beings surrounded two injured girls, but that confidence vanished immediately when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] arrived. The difference between ordinary Japanese supernatural combatants and the newly established Vampire Count was so overwhelming that Kuroki's body ceased obeying him under Victor's pressure.[2]
His reaction was not merely caution. Kuroki and Nura recognized Victor immediately and were terrified because they understood his reputation and the consequences of having attacked someone Victor regarded as his daughter. Their inability to move demonstrated that Kuroki was fully aware of how badly the situation had turned against them.[2][3]

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Kuroki became involved in the events surrounding Ophis after the young Royal Vampire accidentally transported herself from [[Nightingale]] to Tokyo while attempting to use her undeveloped spatial ability.[4]
Ophis eventually encountered [[Nero]], who decided to protect her until [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] or another member of her family could arrive. Their presence coincided with the preparations surrounding the [[Hyakki Yakō]], meaning Japan was already filled with unusually large numbers of Yōkai, Witches, Vampires and other supernatural beings.[5][6]
Rumors eventually spread that an important child connected to the Nightingale Royal Family was moving through Japan without her normal guards. Numerous supernatural beings interpreted the situation as an opportunity and began pursuing Ophis and Nero.[1]
Kuroki was one of the Japanese Vampires involved in the pursuit.
For approximately seven days, Nero fought continuously to keep Ophis away from the various groups hunting them. The increasing number of corpses left behind only attracted stronger opponents, eventually creating a situation in which Nero and Ophis were surrounded by multiple factions simultaneously.[1][7]
By the time Kuroki directly confronted them, Nero was already severely wounded but refused to stop protecting Ophis. Kuroki watched with growing frustration as she continued fighting despite her condition.[2]
Nero's firearms particularly interested him. Kuroki noticed that she did not seem to reload them despite continuously firing and privately questioned whether the weapons possessed an unlimited supply of ammunition.[2]
[[Nura]] mocked Kuroki for struggling against Nero.
Kuroki responded irritably and demanded that Nura use his own supernatural techniques to resolve the problem. Nura admitted that he had already attempted to trap Nero with illusions but discovered that her senses were sufficiently sharp to see through them.[2]
Kuroki dismissed him as useless.
Despite the numerical advantage possessed by their group, Nero continued intimidating the surrounding supernatural beings enough that many instinctively backed away whenever she advanced. Eventually another attacker managed to sever both of Nero's arms, leaving her unable to continue protecting Ophis normally.[2]
The battle appeared to have ended.
Instead, the situation became catastrophically worse for Kuroki and everyone else involved.
Victor Alucard arrived.
Kuroki and Nura immediately recognized him. The two gulped in terror upon realizing that the infamous Vampire responsible for multiple supernatural incidents was standing directly in front of them.[2]
Victor's bloodlust and pressure were so intense that neither Kuroki nor Nura could move their bodies. Rather than simply feeling intimidated, they experienced the sensation that the entire world was crushing down upon them while their bodies refused to obey their commands.[2]
Victor ignored them initially.
He walked directly to the injured Ophis, picked her up and fed her his Blood to stabilize her condition. The scent immediately affected the nearby Vampires, including Kuroki, but none of them were capable of acting because of Victor's overwhelming pressure.[2]
After confirming the condition of Ophis and Nero, Victor turned his attention toward the people responsible.
He declared that everyone involved would die.[3]
Victor's statement caused the gathered supernatural beings to abandon rational thought entirely. Yōkai, Vampires, [[Werewolves]] and other combatants transformed and attacked him simultaneously, driven largely by instinctive terror rather than any expectation that they could actually defeat him.[3]
The attack accomplished nothing.
Victor proceeded to massacre the hostile supernatural forces gathered around Ophis and Nero, beginning the event that became remembered as the Crimson Nightmare.[3]
Kuroki was among the Japanese Vampires who were killed as a consequence of the incident. His involvement in the attempt to capture Ophis placed him among the people Victor had already condemned to death, and he did not survive Victor's retaliation.[3]
The destruction continued beyond the immediate battlefield. Victor subsequently hunted additional supernatural beings connected to the attack rather than allowing those who escaped the initial confrontation to disappear, turning the attempted capture of a vulnerable Vampire princess into one of the most infamous supernatural massacres in modern Japan.[8][9][10]
Kuroki's death consequently became part of a much larger collapse among Japan's hostile supernatural factions. What had initially appeared to be an opportunity to exploit an unprotected child instead brought the attention of a Vampire Count whose response permanently changed how those factions viewed Victor and his family.

### Skills & Abilities
Kuroki received very little individual combat focus before his death. He was nevertheless a supernatural Vampire belonging to the Japanese population of Oriental Vampires and therefore possessed the fundamental racial characteristics associated with Vampires. Abilities not personally demonstrated by Kuroki should not be interpreted as evidence that he had mastered their advanced forms.
- Vampire Physiology: Kuroki was a Japanese Vampire and possessed the basic supernatural body of his race.[1]
- Superhuman Strength: Vampires naturally possess physical strength vastly exceeding ordinary Humans. Kuroki was considered capable enough to participate among the supernatural combatants pursuing [[Nero]] and Ophis.
- Superhuman Speed: As a Vampire, Kuroki possessed enhanced movement and reaction speed beyond ordinary Humans.
- Enhanced Durability: His supernatural body possessed greater resistance to physical damage than that of a Human.
- Enhanced Senses: Vampires possess heightened supernatural senses. Kuroki was capable of following Nero's movements and observing unusual details regarding her firearms while fighting.[2]
- Regeneration: Vampires naturally possess strong regenerative abilities. Kuroki did not receive an individual regeneration feat before his death.
- [[Immortality]]: Vampires cease conventional aging after reaching physical maturity and cannot normally die from age or ordinary Human disease. This did not protect Kuroki from supernatural execution.
- Night Vision: As a creature of the night, Kuroki possessed the natural ability to see effectively in darkness.
- Vampiric Claws: Vampires can transform their fingers into natural claws for close combat.
- Fog Walking: One of the basic racial techniques available to Vampires, allowing them to transform their bodies into mist.
- Bat Transformation: Vampires can divide or transform their bodies into bats as one of their basic racial abilities.
- Vampiric Charm: Vampires can influence weaker beings through supernatural charm. Kuroki never demonstrated this ability directly.
- Blood Empowerment: Consuming Blood can restore and temporarily strengthen a Vampire's physical condition.

#### Limitations
Kuroki belonged to the Japanese/Oriental Vampire population rather than one of [[Nightingale]]'s major Noble houses.
The Oriental Vampires were generally considered considerably weaker than the established Romanian Vampire population connected to [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]]. Kuroki's encounter with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] demonstrated this disparity particularly clearly: Victor's pressure alone prevented him from moving before any direct fight could meaningfully occur.[2]
No unique bloodline ability, advanced transformation or specialized supernatural technique was ever demonstrated by Kuroki.

### Relationships

#### Nura
[[Nura]] was a Yōkai who cooperated with Kuroki during the attempt to capture Ophis and [[Nero]].
Their relationship was characterized more by temporary cooperation and mutual irritation than genuine friendship. Nura mocked Kuroki when Nero continued resisting him, while Kuroki immediately retaliated by criticizing Nura's inability to affect Nero with illusions.[2]
Despite their arguments, the two were effectively part of the same group during the pursuit.
Their similarities became especially apparent when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] arrived. Both immediately recognized him, became terrified and found themselves physically incapable of moving under his pressure.[2]

#### Nero Elderblood
Kuroki was one of the supernatural beings hunting [[Nero]] while she protected Ophis.
He underestimated her ability to continue fighting despite accumulating wounds and became increasingly frustrated when the numerical advantage of the surrounding attackers failed to immediately overwhelm her.[2]
Kuroki also became interested in Nero's unusual guns after noticing that she appeared capable of continuously firing without reloading.[2]

#### Ophis Tepes Elderblood
Ophis was the principal reason Kuroki became involved in the conflict.
As the daughter of [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] and a member of [[Nightingale]]'s Royal Family, the apparently unprotected Ophis represented an extraordinary target for opportunistic supernatural factions operating within Japan.[1]
Kuroki's decision to participate in the attempt to capture her ultimately resulted in his death once [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] arrived.

#### Victor Elderblood
Kuroki immediately recognized [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] Alucard and understood the danger associated with him.
Victor did not need to attack Kuroki directly to establish the difference between them. His supernatural pressure alone rendered the Japanese Vampire unable to move despite Kuroki desperately attempting to regain control of his body.[2]
Kuroki had participated in the attack upon two people Victor considered family. Once Victor saw Ophis and [[Nero]]'s condition, he declared that everyone responsible would die.[3]
Kuroki consequently died during Victor's retaliation and the wider Crimson Nightmare.

### Death
Kuroki died during the Crimson Nightmare after [[Victor Elderblood]] arrived in Tokyo.
Unlike [[Nura]], whose attempted escape and subsequent defeat received additional individual focus, Kuroki did not receive a prolonged one-on-one confrontation with Victor. He was instead one of the hostile Japanese supernatural beings condemned when Victor decided to eliminate everyone involved in harming Ophis and [[Nero]].[3]
His exact final injury is not individually described in sufficient detail to assign a specific cause of death beyond being killed during Victor's massacre.
