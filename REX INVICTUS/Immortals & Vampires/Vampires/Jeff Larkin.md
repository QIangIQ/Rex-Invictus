### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 174
Jeff Larkin is a Noble Vampire, the surviving head or senior member of Clan Larkin, and the longtime butler serving Clan Fulger. Clan Larkin is a subordinate Vampire Clan that has served the Fulger family since at least the generation of [[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]], functioning in a position comparable to the relationship once held between the [[Blank Clan]] and its superior Clan.[1]
Clan Larkin was once considerably larger, but an unspecified incident killed most of its members. By Jeff's introduction, the Clan had declined to the point that only Jeff and his unnamed son were stated to have survived.[1]
Despite the near-destruction of his family, Jeff continued fulfilling Clan Larkin's hereditary service to Clan Fulger. He became Natashia's primary butler and one of the people trusted to deliver sensitive information directly to her, monitor activity inside the Fulger estate and handle administrative matters when the Countess or her family were unavailable.[1][2]
Although Jeff repeatedly downplays his own suitability for leadership, Natashia considered him dependable enough to temporarily leave the administration of Clan Fulger in his hands. When Natashia, Sasha and Victoria abruptly departed, Jeff was forced to assume responsibility for the Clan's documents and internal affairs despite his protests.[2]

### Physical Appearance
Jeff is described as a tall man who appears somewhat older than many of the youthful-looking Vampires surrounding the Count families. He is normally seen dressed in a formal butler's suit appropriate to his position within Clan Fulger.[1]
The novel has not provided a detailed description of his hair color, eye color or exact age.
Jeff's posture is formal and restrained. When reporting to Natashia, he entered her office, closed the door behind himself and placed a hand over his chest in a respectful gesture before delivering his report.[1]

### Personality
Jeff is loyal, respectful, responsible and highly conscious of his position as a subordinate of Clan Fulger.
Even after Clan Larkin was almost completely destroyed, Jeff continued fulfilling the responsibility his family had maintained since Carmila's generation rather than abandoning the Fulger household.[1]
He maintains considerable formality when addressing Natashia.
Even after Natashia encouraged him to address her less formally, Jeff continued referring to her by her title, demonstrating both ingrained etiquette and his awareness of the hierarchy separating a subordinate Clan from the former Count family it serves.[2]
Jeff is also cautious and somewhat anxious when burdened with responsibilities outside his normal role.
When Natashia suddenly informed him that he would be responsible for the Fulger estate while she left with Sasha and Victoria, Jeff immediately protested and suggested that Victoria should remain behind instead.[2]
His reluctance did not come from laziness.
Jeff's concern was specifically that any mistake occurring while the Clan was under his administration would become his responsibility.
This demonstrates that he takes his duties seriously enough to understand the consequences of accepting authority over one of [[Nightingale]]'s major Vampire families.[2]
Despite his protests, Jeff does not abandon his responsibilities.
Once Natashia and Sasha disappeared and left him with the work, Jeff quickly accepted the situation, examined the documents being delivered to him and instructed the Fulger maids to place everything inside the office so he could personally review it.[2]
Jeff therefore tends to complain when Natashia unexpectedly increases his workload, but ultimately performs the work entrusted to him.
He is also observant of disturbances inside the Fulger residence.
When Natashia and Victoria created a commotion during their conversation, Jeff noticed that something abnormal was happening and contacted Sasha rather than simply ignoring it.[3]

### Synopsis

#### A New Pillar
Jeff first appeared at the Clan Fulger mansion while Natashia was working through the large amount of administrative paperwork required of her position.
Jeff knocked on the Countess' office door and informed her that Clan Fulger had received important information originating from one of [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]]'s daughters.[1]
Natashia immediately understood that the information was important enough to justify interrupting her work.
Jeff entered wearing his usual butler uniform and formally presented the report.
His introduction also revealed the history of his family.
Clan Larkin was described as a subordinate Vampire Clan that had served Clan Fulger since the generation of Natashia's mother, [[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]].[1]
The arrangement was compared to other subordinate Clan relationships in [[Nightingale]].
Clan Larkin had once possessed numerous members, but a past incident had killed most of them.
By the time Jeff appeared:
only Jeff and his son remained.
[1]
Despite this collapse, Jeff had maintained Clan Larkin's service to the Fulger family and occupied the position of Natashia's butler.
Jeff reported that the information had come from Siena through a Witch named June, acting as the intermediary responsible for transmitting the message to Clan Fulger.[1]
His role in this scene established that Jeff was trusted with communications important enough to be carried directly to the Clan leader rather than being handled exclusively by ordinary servants.

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Jeff continued serving Clan Fulger throughout the instability affecting the family.
During a conversation between Natashia and [[Victoria Fulger Elderblood]], the sisters created enough commotion inside the Fulger residence to attract Jeff's attention.
Realizing that something unusual was happening, Jeff contacted Sasha, who was elsewhere in the mansion at the time.[3]
The scene demonstrates that Jeff remained actively responsible for monitoring the residence rather than functioning merely as a ceremonial servant.

#### Demonic War
Jeff later appeared while Natashia, Sasha and Victoria were preparing for the political changes surrounding [[Nightingale]] and the upcoming gathering of major supernatural powers.
At this point, the novel explicitly reaffirmed Clan Larkin's relationship with Clan Fulger:
Clan Larkin exclusively served Clan Fulger.
[2]
Natashia unexpectedly summoned Jeff and announced that she intended to leave with Sasha and Victoria.
She then ordered Jeff to:
take care of everything while they were gone.
[2]
Jeff immediately panicked.
He attempted to convince Natashia not to leave and suggested allowing Victoria to remain in charge instead.
Natashia dismissed his concerns by explaining that the largest problems had already been handled and that Jeff only needed to monitor the remaining affairs.[2]
Jeff then appealed to Sasha for help.
Sasha initially agreed that Natashia's decision was irresponsible.
However, after Natashia reminded her that leaving meant visiting [[Victor Elderblood]], Sasha instantly changed sides and declared:
"Jeff, Clan Fulger is yours."
before disappearing with Lightning.[2]
Natashia immediately followed her.
Jeff was consequently left standing alone in the office, shocked that the three women had effectively placed the administration of one of Nightingale's major Clans in his hands without further discussion.
The situation became worse when Fulger maids began arriving with stacks of administrative documents requiring approval.
The paperwork had become especially important because Clan Fulger had recently suffered an espionage incident.
Until a permanent security solution could be created, many matters were being handled directly by the Clan's leadership rather than being freely delegated throughout the organization.[2]
Jeff examined the documents and determined that they were not major emergencies, but they still required the attention of an authorized representative.
Rather than continuing to complain, he instructed the maids to place the documents in Natashia's office and personally began reviewing them.[2]
This incident provides the clearest demonstration of Jeff's actual importance within the Clan.
Although officially a butler, Natashia trusted him enough to leave him as temporary administrator of the Fulger household and its internal affairs.

### Clan Larkin
Clan Larkin is a minor Noble Vampire Clan historically subordinated to Clan Fulger.
Its known history is extremely limited.
The Clan had already served the Fulger family since at least the generation of [[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]].[1]
Unlike alliances based primarily upon temporary politics, the relationship between Larkin and Fulger appears to be hereditary and institutional.
Chapter 503 explicitly states that Clan Larkin:
exclusively serves Clan Fulger.
[2]
This means its traditional purpose is tied directly to supporting the Fulger household rather than independently participating in [[Nightingale]]'s Count politics.
Historically, Clan Larkin possessed several members.
An unidentified past incident caused catastrophic casualties throughout the family.
By Jeff's introduction, only:
- Jeff;
- and his unnamed son
were confirmed survivors.[1]
The nature of the disaster has not yet been revealed.
It is therefore unknown:
- who attacked Clan Larkin;
- when the incident occurred;
- why Jeff and his son survived;
- how many members died;
- or whether additional distant descendants still exist elsewhere.
Despite the Clan's near-extinction, Jeff continued carrying out its original obligation toward Clan Fulger.

### Relationship with Clan Fulger
Jeff's identity is almost completely tied to his service toward Clan Fulger.
The Larkin family had served the Fulgers since Carmila's generation, meaning the relationship existed long before Natashia became the Clan's principal leader.[1]
Jeff later became directly associated with Natashia's household.
His duties include:
- receiving and delivering important communications;
- monitoring the Fulger residence;
- coordinating with household servants;
- assisting the Clan leader;
- responding to disturbances;
- handling administrative documents;
- and temporarily supervising the Clan when senior members are absent.[1][3][2]
His relationship with Clan Fulger is therefore considerably more substantial than that of an ordinary domestic servant.
Jeff functions as part:
- butler;
- steward;
- administrator;
- and trusted retainer.

### Relationships

#### Annasthashia Elderblood Fulger
[[Annasthashia Elderblood Fulger]] is Jeff's principal mistress and the person he serves most directly on-screen.
Jeff treats Natashia with considerable respect and consistently addresses her formally even when she encourages him to simply call her Natashia.[2]
Natashia, in turn, trusts Jeff substantially more than his formal position initially suggests.
She allows him to:
- handle sensitive information;
- interrupt her work with important reports;
- monitor the Fulger household;
- and temporarily assume responsibility for the Clan's administration.[1][2]
Jeff sometimes becomes visibly stressed by Natashia's impulsive behavior.
The best example occurred when she suddenly abandoned the estate and left him in charge of Clan Fulger despite his repeated protests.[2]
Nevertheless, he continues obeying her.

#### Carmila Fulger Elderblood
[[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]] was the previous generation of Clan Fulger leadership and Natashia's mother.
Clan Larkin had already been serving Clan Fulger during Carmila's generation.[1]
The novel does not provide enough direct information to establish the exact personal relationship between Jeff and Carmila.
It is therefore safer to state that Clan Larkin served Clan Fulger during Carmila's era rather than claiming detailed personal service that has not been shown.

#### Sasha Elderblood Fulger
[[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] is another high-ranking member of the Fulger household whom Jeff serves.
Jeff is comfortable enough with Sasha to appeal directly to her when Natashia makes unreasonable demands.
When Natashia attempted to leave the entire Clan under Jeff's temporary administration, Jeff immediately asked Sasha to convince her mother otherwise.[2]
Unfortunately for Jeff, Sasha abandoned him almost immediately after learning that Natashia intended to visit [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].

#### Victoria Fulger Elderblood
[[Victoria Fulger Elderblood]] is Natashia's younger sister and another senior member of Clan Fulger.
When Jeff objected to being placed in charge of the Clan, his first proposed alternative was for Victoria to remain behind and manage the household instead.[2]
This suggests Jeff recognizes Victoria as possessing greater formal authority within Clan Fulger than himself.
Jeff also noticed the disturbance caused by Natashia and Victoria during one of their private conversations and contacted Sasha after becoming concerned.[3]

#### Unnamed Son
Jeff has one confirmed son.
The son has not yet been named or directly characterized.
Chapter 174 establishes that Jeff and his son are the only confirmed survivors of the incident that devastated Clan Larkin.[1]
Because the Larkin Clan has been reduced to these two members, Jeff's son is particularly important to the continuation of their family line.

### Skills & Abilities
Jeff's combat capabilities have never been meaningfully demonstrated. His page should therefore not attribute advanced Vampire techniques or Count-level combat abilities to him without evidence.
- Noble Vampire Physiology: Jeff is a Noble Vampire belonging to [[Nightingale]]'s aristocratic Vampire population.[1]
- Superhuman Physiology: As a Noble Vampire, Jeff possesses the supernatural physical constitution characteristic of his Race. However, the novel has not shown a direct combat feat establishing his personal physical limits.
- Regeneration: Jeff possesses the regenerative physiology inherent to Noble Vampires, although no personal regeneration feat has been shown.
- Enhanced Senses: Jeff possesses the superior senses associated with Vampires. His awareness of disturbances within the Fulger residence also supports his attentiveness, although this is not enough to quantify his supernatural sensory range.[3]
- Longevity: Jeff has served a family whose history spans multiple generations of Vampire leadership, but his exact age has never been stated.
- Administrative Expertise: Jeff is experienced in the administration of a major Noble Vampire Clan. Natashia considered him reliable enough to temporarily supervise Clan Fulger while she, Sasha and Victoria were absent.[2]
- Stewardship: Jeff coordinates household affairs, servants, communications and documents within the Fulger estate.
- Information Handling: Jeff receives and transmits sensitive communications directly to Clan leadership. He personally delivered Siena's message to Natashia after it was relayed through June.[1]
- Observation: Jeff is attentive to unusual activity within the residence and reacted when the disturbance involving Natashia and Victoria suggested that something abnormal was occurring.[3]
- Professional Etiquette: Jeff possesses the formal manners expected of a high-level butler serving one of Nightingale's historic Count families. He consistently observes hierarchy and proper forms of address.[1][2]
- Leadership: Although reluctant to assume large-scale responsibility, Jeff can coordinate Fulger servants and administrative work when required.[2]
- Responsibility and Judgment: Jeff can distinguish between urgent and routine Clan documents and understands which matters require direct authorization from the Clan's leadership.[2]

### Power
Jeff has not participated in a major on-screen battle.
Therefore, his exact combat level is unknown.
His classification as a Noble Vampire establishes that he is superhuman compared with ordinary Humans, but there is currently insufficient evidence to accurately scale him against:
- [[Vampire Counts]];
- combat-specialized Noble Vampires;
- Alpha [[Werewolves]];
- Demigods;
- or other high-ranking supernatural fighters.
His primary demonstrated importance lies in:
loyalty, administration and Clan service rather than combat.

### Current Status
Jeff remains alive.
No later chapter has confirmed the destruction of Clan Larkin or Jeff's death.
He therefore remains the principal known surviving adult member of Clan Larkin and a retainer associated with Clan Fulger.
The later transformation of the Fulger family into part of the much larger Clan Elderblood does not automatically establish that Jeff personally became an Elderblood retainer.
Unless a later chapter directly confirms a change of allegiance, his demonstrated institutional allegiance remains:
Clan Larkin → Clan Fulger
