### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 65
M-Master, please... let go of me...
—Yuki to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] Walker, Chapter 67
Yuki Snow is a main-line member of the [[Snow Clan]], the daughter of Agnes Snow's late brother and a female Noble Vampire descended from an ancient Japanese Miko bloodline. Although her birth gave her a legitimate position within the Snow family and she could have lived as one of its Ladies, Yuki voluntarily relinquished her inheritance and chose the life of a maid instead, avoiding the political and social responsibilities attached to her lineage.[1]
Originally introduced as a young Noble Vampire maid temporarily assigned by Agnes and [[Adonis Snow]] to serve Victor Walker, Yuki gradually ceased being merely a Snow Clan servant attached to Violet's husband and became personally loyal to Victor. Over the course of the story she integrated into his wider household, developed her inherited Precognition, and eventually declared that she wanted to continue following Victor's path regardless of where it led. Victor subsequently transformed her into a Blood Dragonoid and appointed her Leader of the Maids stationed at his headquarters, placing Yuki directly beneath himself and Kaguya in the household hierarchy.[2]
Yuki also developed deep romantic feelings for Victor that she did not openly confess. By Chapter 1096 those feelings had become sufficiently obvious that Violet identified Yuki as a devoted woman carrying an unspoken love for him and argued that allowing such feelings to remain unresolved indefinitely could create emotional problems within the family. Violet therefore included Yuki among the women she believed would eventually have to be properly integrated into Victor's family. This conversation establishes Yuki as a recognized love interest, but does not itself establish that she had already become Victor's wife.[3]

### Physical Description
Yuki is a beautiful young-looking woman standing approximately 165 centimeters tall. She has long white hair, pale skin and light-blue eyes characteristic of her [[Snow Clan]] ancestry. She possesses a curvaceous figure, with her official profile listing her bust as G-cup.[4][5] When first introduced, she wore a modern maid dress and was described as possessing a beautiful voice comparable to that of an angel.[4]
As a professional maid, Yuki is most commonly seen wearing variations of the Snow Clan or Elderblood household maid uniform. Her clean and carefully maintained appearance reflects the traditional etiquette in which she was trained.
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] transformed her into a Blood Dragonoid, Yuki retained her predominantly humanoid appearance but developed unmistakably Draconic characteristics. A pair of white horns appeared on her head and her eyes changed into a Dragon-like form. Unlike Victor's True Dragon wives, Yuki did not receive a True Dragon Soul or a full Dragon Form; her current species is specifically a humanoid Dragonoid rather than a True Dragon.[2]

### Personality
Yuki is polite, dutiful and highly conscious of proper maid etiquette. Even when first introduced amid Kaguya's obvious irritation at her presence, Yuki attempted to maintain a professional expression and respond calmly rather than openly escalate the argument. Her composure is not absolute, however; when sufficiently annoyed or embarrassed, her carefully maintained poker face breaks into much more expressive reactions, which are repeatedly compared to those of an irritated or saddened squirrel.[4][6]
She was initially much more easily intimidated than [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s veteran maids. After Victor completed his six months of training under [[Scathach]], Yuki was visibly shaken by how dramatically his presence had changed, and she remained noticeably nervous around his overwhelming aura and unpredictable behavior. This nervousness was especially visible during Victor's early interactions with Ophis, when Yuki repeatedly worried that his casual treatment of the Vampire King's daughter could be misunderstood as kidnapping or create an enormous political incident.[7][8][9][10]
Yuki is nevertheless neither cowardly nor incompetent. Her anxiety comes primarily from understanding the potential consequences of Victor's actions rather than from being incapable of functioning under pressure. She continued accompanying him, performing her duties and voicing concerns when necessary even when Victor repeatedly involved himself with people capable of causing international supernatural incidents.
Her choice to become a maid is also genuinely personal rather than merely the consequence of illegitimacy or low social status. Yuki is the daughter of Agnes' brother and therefore belongs to the [[Snow Clan]]'s main family. Although born from her father's relationship with a foreign Noble Vampire and regarded as a bastard daughter, Agnes still considered her the daughter of her late brother and was prepared to protect her. Yuki could have claimed the position of a Snow Clan Lady if she wished, but voluntarily surrendered her inheritance because she did not desire the restrictive responsibilities attached to aristocratic life.[1]
Yuki possesses a desire to improve herself but, unlike Victor and several of his wives, was originally unwilling to pursue strength through extreme suffering merely for its own sake. When Victor discovered her hidden Precognition and offered to personally subject her to the brutal training necessary to develop it rapidly, Yuki hesitated after remembering the severity of his training methods. Victor understood immediately and reassured her that her reluctance did not disappoint him, pointing out that she lacked the kind of desperate need for power that would justify voluntarily enduring such torture. Yuki instead continued developing at a more gradual rate under Hilda and was particularly affected when Victor told her that he was proud of her progress.[1]
This makes her later behavior toward Victor especially significant. By Chapter 960, when Victor directly examined her Soul and questioned whether she truly wished to follow his path, Yuki answered without hesitation that she wanted to follow him no matter what. After being made responsible for judging the talent of other maids and knowing that any failure would reflect personally upon her, Yuki showed no fear and simply promised Victor that she would not disappoint him.[2] Her progression therefore moves from a nervous borrowed maid uncomfortable around Victor's presence into a trusted subordinate willing to accept personal responsibility within his growing civilization.
Yuki's devotion also gradually became romantic. She occasionally displays embarrassment and jealousy around the intimacy shared between Victor and his women, although she usually keeps those feelings hidden beneath her professional demeanor. By Chapter 1096, Violet explicitly recognized that Yuki had developed deep love for Victor despite refusing to openly reveal it.[1][3]

### Synopsis

#### Vampire Society
Yuki first appeared after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] completed approximately six months of brutal training under [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]]. She arrived near the heavily damaged coliseum wearing a modern maid uniform and examining the destruction left behind by Victor's training. Kaguya, who already knew why Yuki had come, repeatedly displayed obvious annoyance toward her. Despite becoming irritated herself, Yuki attempted to remain professional and eventually formally introduced herself to Ruby, Sasha and the others as Yuki Snow, explaining that [[Agnes Elderblood Snow|Countess Agnes]] Snow and Count [[Adonis Snow]] had ordered her to serve Victor Walker as her new master.[4]
Her transfer immediately complicated the existing maid hierarchy because Kaguya had also recently been transferred from Violet into Victor's personal service. Yuki quickly learned that Victor's household operated very differently from the rigid environment of a traditional Count Clan. She watched Victor casually tease Kaguya, discussed his marriages with Kaguya, Maria and Luna, and expressed disbelief that the young Vampire had already married three of the four women popularly regarded as the "flowers" of Vampire society. When Maria jokingly predicted that Victor would eventually become involved with the remaining flower—the Vampire King's daughter—Yuki shared the others' disbelief.[11]
Yuki's interactions with Victor also established that her nervousness did not prevent her from becoming comfortable around him. During the period surrounding Eleonor Adrasteia's arrival, Victor casually held Yuki despite her embarrassed request that he release her, with Yuki becoming increasingly flustered while admitting internally that she did not entirely dislike his attention.[12]
When Victor later entered the Royal Capital, Yuki accompanied him as one of his visible attendants while Kaguya frequently operated from his shadow. This placed Yuki directly beside him when he encountered Ophis. Victor treated the lonely child with immediate affection without initially knowing she was a royal princess, and even after learning the truth his behavior changed very little. Yuki served as the much more cautious counterweight to his impulsiveness, repeatedly worrying about how Victor's actions—buying Ophis clothes, carrying her around the Capital and casually assuming responsibility for her—would appear to the Royal Family.[13][14][15]
By the time they returned to [[Scathach]]'s mansion, Yuki was visibly exhausted by the entire experience. When Pepper asked if she was all right after Victor recounted meeting both royal princesses, Yuki answered bluntly that she clearly was not. Despite this, she remained beside Victor and continued performing her responsibilities, establishing the recurring dynamic in which Yuki often understood the danger of Victor's actions far better than Victor cared about those dangers himself.[16]
Yuki continued appearing among the maids surrounding Victor's extended household as his status rapidly changed from Scathach's disciple into the Fifth Vampire Count. She remained connected to the [[Snow Clan]] while increasingly functioning as part of Victor's personal domestic staff, appearing alongside maids such as Kaguya, Natalia, Maria and Luna during family meetings, household preparations and Victor's interactions with his rapidly expanding supernatural family.[17][18][19]

#### A New Pillar
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] returned to the Human World as a Vampire Count, Yuki continued appearing among the household staff associated with his expanding underground residence. Although Kaguya and the newly created Alucard maids increasingly occupied the central combat and personal roles around Victor, Yuki remained part of the broader domestic group and retained her connection to both the [[Snow Clan]] and Victor's household.[20][21][22]
Unlike [[Bruna Elderblood]], [[Eve Elderblood]], [[Maria Elderblood]], [[Roberta Elderblood]] and the other women gradually transformed into Victor's dedicated combat maids, Yuki remained comparatively removed from the violent core of the [[Maid Squad]] during this period. Her role was still primarily that of a traditionally trained maid from Clan Snow rather than an assassin or battlefield specialist.

#### Discoveries, Changes and War
Yuki remained present throughout the continuing expansion of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction, usually serving in the background of household scenes while the original maids and Victor's wives handled the era's major conflicts. Her [[Snow Clan]] background meant that she remained connected to the internal affairs of Agnes and Violet even while her personal loyalty toward Victor continued to deepen.[23][24]
Her apparent lack of major combat activity during these arcs did not mean she lacked unusual potential. Yuki possessed a second bloodline inherited not from her Snow father but from her mother, a foreign Noble Vampire descended from an ancient Japanese lineage of Miko. This maternal inheritance eventually became the foundation of one of Yuki's most valuable abilities.[1]

#### Demonic War
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually confronted Yuki directly about her hidden ability after returning to the mansion. Having temporarily governed the [[Snow Clan]] and acquired extensive knowledge concerning its main members, Victor already knew that Yuki was not merely an ordinary Snow maid. He revealed that he knew both her family circumstances and the existence of her maternal bloodline power.[1]
Yuki was the daughter of Agnes' late brother and a foreign Noble Vampire, making her Violet's first cousin and a member of the Snow Clan's main family despite her illegitimate birth. She had personally surrendered her inheritance and status because she preferred being a maid to accepting the restrictive duties of a Clan Lady. Agnes respected that decision while continuing to protect Yuki as the daughter of her deceased brother.[1]
Victor then questioned Yuki about her training. Her maternal bloodline granted her Precognition, allowing her to see several seconds into her own future while also instinctively sensing danger directed toward her. The bloodline originated from an ancient Japanese Noble Vampire clan associated with Miko or priestesses, making Yuki's heritage significantly more unusual than her appearance initially suggested.[1]
Yuki admitted that the ability was extremely difficult to train. Victor offered to personally train her, but warned through implication that meaningful rapid improvement under him would involve the kind of brutal suffering experienced by the people who normally followed his training. Yuki hesitated. Rather than condemn her, Victor acknowledged that she had no desperate reason to willingly subject herself to torture merely to gain power and reassured her that he was proud of the progress she had already made. He trusted Hilda to continue developing Yuki more gradually.[1]
The conversation had a noticeable emotional effect on her. Yuki focused particularly strongly upon Victor's praise that he was proud of her, demonstrating how important his opinion had already become to her. Shortly afterward, she rejoined Kaguya and Victor's other maids, displaying mild embarrassment and jealousy at the intimacy surrounding Victor's household while still remaining outside its openly romantic core.[1]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Yuki's relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] reached a decisive turning point after his transformation of several members of his household into Draconic races. Victor secretly brought Yuki together with the others and directly examined her Soul before asking whether she understood the path she was choosing. Yuki answered without qualification that she wanted to follow his path no matter what.[2]
Satisfied with her answer, Victor transformed Yuki into a Blood Dragonoid. White horns emerged from her head and her eyes became Dragon-like, while her already powerful Vampire physiology was elevated into a hybrid humanoid Dragon existence. The transformation did not turn Yuki into a True Dragon: she did not receive a True Dragon Soul, could not naturally assume a full Dragon Form and did not acquire the instinctive understanding of Draconic Language and Runes possessed by [[True Dragons]]. Nevertheless, Victor explicitly stated that Dragonoids remained genuine Dragons and therefore possessed the potential to learn those abilities through study and training.[2]
Victor simultaneously elevated Yuki's position within his household. He appointed her Leader of the Maids in the headquarters mansion, ordering her to report directly to himself and Kaguya. Her first instruction was simply to become stronger. Her second was considerably more important: Yuki was to examine the maids and other personnel under her authority, separate those with genuine talent from those without it and present the exceptional candidates to Victor. Those she selected would themselves be transformed into Dragonoids and placed within an elite group trained personally by Victor and Kaguya.[2]
Victor made the responsibility personal. If Yuki recommended unworthy candidates, their failure would damage his assessment of her judgment. Unlike the nervous young maid who had once hesitated before Victor's brutal training, Yuki showed no fear toward the responsibility and simply promised that she would not disappoint him. Her appointment demonstrated that Victor now trusted Yuki not merely as a household servant but as someone capable of evaluating and cultivating future elite personnel.[2]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
By the Imperial Era, Yuki's attachment to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had long surpassed the original master-servant relationship created by Agnes and Adonis' order. Although she continued hiding those emotions rather than openly seeking a place beside Victor, the women already integrated into his family noticed them.[3]
During a conversation concerning the future structure of Victor's family, Violet explained to Hilda that unresolved emotional attachments could eventually become dangerous weaknesses within an immortal family. She identified Yuki specifically as a devoted woman who deeply loved Victor but had not revealed those feelings. Violet concluded that allowing the situation to continue indefinitely could create unnecessary emotional rifts and stated that Yuki Snow would eventually need to be added to the family.[3]
Yuki was discussed alongside several other extraordinarily capable women whom Violet believed should eventually become permanently tied to Victor and the Empire. However, the conversation was a projection of future family expansion rather than an already completed marriage. At this point Yuki is therefore properly treated as one of Victor's recognized romantic interests rather than one of his confirmed Soul Wives.[3]

### Trivia
- Yuki is not simply a branch-family Snow maid; she is the daughter of Agnes' late brother and therefore a direct member of the [[Snow Clan]]'s main family and Violet's first cousin.[1]
- Yuki was considered an illegitimate daughter because her mother was a foreign Noble Vampire, but Agnes still regarded her as her late brother's daughter and intended to protect her.[1]
- She voluntarily relinquished her inheritance and the possibility of becoming a Snow Clan Lady because she did not want the restrictive responsibilities attached to the position.[1]
- Yuki inherited Snow Clan [[Pyrokinesis]] through her paternal bloodline and Precognition through her mother's ancient Japanese Miko lineage.[1]
- Her Precognition allows her to see several seconds into her own future and instinctively sense danger threatening her.[1]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] offered to personally train Yuki's Precognition, but she initially hesitated because she was unwilling to endure the extreme suffering involved in his training. Victor explicitly stated that he was not disappointed and was proud of her existing progress.[1]
- Yuki is noticeably affected by Victor's praise and selectively focused on his statement that he was proud of her.[1]
- She was one of Victor's earliest personal maids, entering his service long before the later formation of his larger Imperial household.[4][25]
- After becoming a Blood Dragonoid, Yuki was promoted to Leader of the Maids at Victor's headquarters and reports directly to Victor and Kaguya.[2]
- Victor entrusted Yuki with selecting talented personnel worthy of being transformed into Dragonoids and personally trained as an elite force.[2]
- Despite becoming a Dragon, Yuki is not a True Dragon. She lacks a True Dragon Soul, full Dragon Form and instinctive mastery of Draconic Language and Runes.[2]
- Because she remains a genuine Dragon species, Yuki can potentially learn Draconic Language and Draconic Runes through training even though the knowledge is not instinctively inherited.[2]
- By Chapter 1096, Violet openly recognizes Yuki's deep but unspoken love for Victor and believes she should eventually be formally incorporated into the family.[3]
- Chapter 1096 does not itself establish a completed marriage between Yuki and Victor; she remains a love interest at that point.[3]

#### Skills and Abilities
Blood Dragonoid Physiology: After willingly choosing to continue following [[Victor Elderblood]]'s path, Yuki was personally transformed by Victor from a Noble Vampire into a Blood Dragonoid. Her new body combines the supernatural characteristics of her previous Noble Vampire physiology with those of a humanoid Dragon, dramatically raising her physical abilities and future potential.[1]
- Enhanced Physical Characteristics: Yuki's transformation greatly increased the physical potential of an already superhuman Noble Vampire body. As a Blood Dragonoid she possesses greatly enhanced strength, speed, reflexes, agility, stamina, durability and senses compared with her former state.[1]
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Yuki retains the powerful regeneration inherent to her Vampiric physiology, now reinforced by her Draconic transformation.[1]
- [[Immortality]]/Longevity: As a former Noble Vampire subsequently transformed into a Dragon species, Yuki possesses the extreme lifespan and resistance to ordinary aging associated with both supernatural races.
- Metamorphosis: The Vampiric portion of Yuki's hybrid existence retains the supernatural bodily adaptability associated with Noble Vampires.
- Draconic Characteristics: Following her transformation, a pair of white Dragon horns appeared on Yuki's head and her eyes became visibly Dragon-like.[1]
- Enhanced Potential: Victor explicitly transformed Yuki in order to raise her racial potential and ordered her to become stronger. Although significantly weaker in innate potential than a True Dragon, a Blood Dragonoid possesses a much higher developmental ceiling than Yuki's former Noble Vampire state.[1]
- Draconic Language and Rune Learning: Yuki does not instinctively understand Draconic Runes or the Draconic Language because she lacks the Soul of a True Dragon. However, Victor explicitly states that Dragonoids are still Dragons and therefore possess the potential to learn both through study and training.[1]
- True Dragon Limitations: Unlike Victor and his True Dragon wives, Yuki does not possess a True Dragon Soul, cannot naturally transform into a full Dragon Form and does not instinctively possess the reality-affecting racial abilities or knowledge characteristic of [[True Dragons]].[1]
[[Snow Clan]] Bloodline: Yuki is the daughter of Agnes Snow's brother and therefore a direct member of the Snow Clan's main lineage rather than merely a servant adopted into the Clan. She inherited the supernatural characteristics associated with Clan Snow through her paternal bloodline.[2]
- [[Pyrokinesis]]: As a direct member of Clan Snow, Yuki possesses the Clan's inherited ability to create, control and manipulate supernatural flames.
- Fire Immunity/Resistance: The Snow Clan's natural affinity with fire grants its members exceptional resistance to their own flames and other forms of extreme heat.
- Sunlight Immunity: Snow Clan Vampires are naturally capable of operating beneath sunlight, a trait inherited through their bloodline rather than acquired through training.
- Snow Clan Noble Vampire Heritage: Before her transformation, Yuki inherited the enhanced physical abilities, blood dependence, supernatural senses, immortality and regeneration characteristic of Noble Vampires belonging to one of [[Nightingale]]'s major Count lineages.
Precognition: Yuki possesses an unusual secondary bloodline inherited through her mother, a foreign Noble Vampire descended from an ancient Japanese clan of Miko or priestesses. Unlike the fire inherited from her Snow ancestry, this power allows Yuki to perceive portions of her immediate personal future.[2]
- Short-Term Future Sight: Yuki can see several seconds into her own future, allowing her to anticipate events before they occur.[2]
- Danger Sense: Her Precognition also manifests instinctively as an awareness of threats and danger directed toward her, allowing Yuki to sense when something harmful is about to occur even before consciously interpreting the future vision.[2]
- Combat Application: Victor immediately recognized the enormous combat usefulness of the ability. Properly developed Precognition can allow Yuki to anticipate attacks, movement and immediate danger before an opponent completes an action.[2]
- Training Difficulty: Yuki herself admitted that Precognition is difficult to develop. Victor offered to personally train the ability through his extreme methods, although Yuki initially chose the slower route of continued training rather than immediately subjecting herself to his brutal regimen.[2]
Professional Maid Training: Yuki was trained as a Snow Clan maid long before entering Victor's personal service. She demonstrates traditional maid etiquette, household management, formal conduct and the ability to remain functional during politically dangerous situations.[3]
- Household Administration: Yuki's competence eventually earned Victor enough confidence to place her in charge of the maids serving at his headquarters.[1]
- Personnel Evaluation: Victor personally ordered Yuki to evaluate the people beneath her, separate genuinely talented individuals from mediocre personnel and recommend only those worthy of being transformed into Dragonoids and admitted into his elite training program.[1]
- Leadership: Yuki serves as Leader of the Maids at Victor's headquarters and reports directly to Victor and Kaguya, placing her above the ordinary household staff operating there.[1]
- Judgment and Responsibility: Victor explicitly warned Yuki that recommending unworthy candidates would negatively affect his evaluation of her own performance, meaning the position requires her to independently judge talent rather than merely relay names supplied by others.[1]

#### Former Physiology
Noble Vampire Physiology: Before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] transformed her into a Blood Dragonoid, Yuki was a main-line Noble Vampire of the [[Snow Clan]]. Her Vampire characteristics were not simply discarded after the transformation but became part of the hybrid foundation of her new species.[2][1]
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: As a Noble Vampire, Yuki naturally possessed strength, speed, reflexes, agility, durability and stamina vastly exceeding ordinary Humans.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Yuki could rapidly recover from injuries through Noble Vampire regeneration.
- Enhanced Senses: Her hearing, smell, vision and other senses were naturally superhuman.
- [[Immortality]]/Longevity: Like other Noble Vampires, Yuki possessed extreme longevity and did not age according to ordinary Human limitations.
- Blood Consumption: As a Vampire, Yuki could consume blood to replenish herself and support her supernatural physiology.
- Metamorphosis: Noble Vampire physiology includes supernatural bodily transformation and adaptability, characteristics subsequently incorporated into her Dragonoid state.
- Emotional Intensification: Like other Vampires, Yuki's emotions could be experienced with supernatural intensity, something reflected in her embarrassment, jealousy and increasingly deep attachment toward Victor.
