#### Skills & Abilities
Noble Vampire Physiology: As a Noble Vampire from Vlad's sireline and an very old vampire, Alienova possesses all Vampires basic abilities to a greater degree, like Superhuman Strength, Superhuman Speed, [[Immortality]], Regeneration, Fog Walking, Bat Transformation, Shapeshifting, Enhanced Senses, Supernatural Beauty,....,etc.
- [[Pyrokinesis]]: All direct member of the [[Snow Clan]] has the innate ability to control and generate flames, their mastery over it allows them to inflict flames of severe heat towards their fellow vampires. Their power also grants them fire immunity.
- Sunlight Immunity: All vampires have an inherent weakness toward sunlight. However, the Snow Clan's ability to govern fire grants them immunity to this weakness. A peculiarity that is unique to their clan, as such they are the only vampires capable of roaming in broad daylight.
- Emotion Empowerment: Members of the Snow Clan's strength is proportionally related with the intensity of their emotions. The stronger their emotions (Specially rage) is the stronger they become.
