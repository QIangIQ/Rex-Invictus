#### Debut

Vlad III Tepes, commonly referred to simply as Vlad Tepes and historically known as The Impaler, was an exceptional Human ruler of Wallachia, the older brother of Radu Tepes and the man whose identity was later adopted by the Vampire Progenitor now known as [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]].[2] The modern Vampire King is therefore not the original historical Vlad Tepes. Instead, the Progenitor eventually consumed Vlad's Soul and assigned enough value to his existence that many of Vlad's characteristics became integrated into the Vampire King's own identity.[2]
Before that absorption occurred, Hela secretly approached Vlad after using her extraordinary Affinity with Death to predict when he would cease to exist. She offered him a contract: in exchange for a portion of his Core Existence that could later be used to summon him into a future war, Hela would protect and bless his direct bloodline so that his descendants could live prosperous lives.[3] Vlad accepted because, although he trusted his Vampire friend, he understood the Progenitor's shortcomings and knew the Vampire could become neglectful when focused upon multiple objectives.[3]
Hela's intervention divided Vlad's existence. One portion of his Soul remained with Hela, while the remainder was later absorbed by the Vampire Progenitor. Hela also removed the memories surrounding their agreement from the portion that would be absorbed, preventing the Vampire King from discovering that part of Vlad's Soul had survived outside him.[3]
Thousands of years later, Hela used the preserved fragment to resurrect Vlad during Ragnarök. He returned commanding more than 20,000 soldiers of the Wallachian Royal Army and willingly fulfilled his ancient agreement, openly expressing excitement at the prospect of impaling a Divine Army.[1] He later personally defeated [[Vidar]], one of [[Odin]]'s sons, and impaled the God in a manner deliberately designed to keep him alive for Hela's subsequent punishment of Odin's family.[4]

### Physical Appearance
Vlad is described as a man with black hair and a beard.[1] When Hela resurrected him during Ragnarök, he appeared at the head of more than twenty thousand fully armored Wallachian soldiers mounted upon skeletal horses.[1]
The novel has not established his exact height, eye color, skin tone or a sufficiently detailed description of his personal armor. His current artwork should therefore be treated as an artistic interpretation rather than a definitive canonical design.

### Personality
Vlad is proud, fearless, pragmatic and intensely martial. His very first reaction to resurrection was not fear or confusion at finding himself on a battlefield filled with Gods, Divine Beasts and supernatural armies. After realizing that Ragnarök had begun, he immediately remembered his agreement with Hela and expressed genuine excitement at the idea of impaling a Divine Army.[1]
His enthusiasm for battle is accompanied by a particularly cruel and theatrical fighting style strongly associated with his identity as the Impaler. During the post-battle purge of [[Asgard]], Vlad appeared while dragging the defeated [[Vidar]] by the hair. He then created an impaling pillar and threw the God onto it with enough precision to avoid every vital organ, ensuring that Vidar remained alive and conscious rather than receiving a quick death.[4]
Vlad is nevertheless not portrayed as reckless. His ancient agreement with Hela demonstrates considerable foresight. He understood that his own death or disappearance would leave his bloodline without his protection, and although he trusted the Vampire Progenitor he called a friend, Vlad recognized that the Vampire's priorities and flaws made him an unreliable sole guardian of Vlad's descendants.[3] He therefore made an additional arrangement with a Goddess capable of protecting his lineage after his death.
His treatment of Radu Tepes similarly reveals genuine concern for his family. Radu was extremely sickly while Human, and Vlad personally asked his Vampire friend to transform his younger brother into a Noble Vampire so that he would survive.[2]
Vlad also possesses a strong sense of obligation. Thousands of years passed between his agreement with Hela and Ragnarök, yet after being summoned he immediately acknowledged the ancient promise and fulfilled his side of the contract without hesitation.[1]

### History
Vlad III Tepes was the original Human ruler historically known as The Impaler.
At some point during his Mortal life, Vlad developed a close relationship with the ancient Vampire Progenitor who would later adopt his identity. The Progenitor had existed under numerous names and appearances throughout history before eventually becoming known as [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]].[2]
Vlad also had a younger brother named Radu Tepes. Radu was physically weak and sickly as a Human, prompting Vlad to ask his Vampire friend to transform him into a Noble Vampire.[2] The Progenitor agreed, giving Radu supernatural life but unintentionally creating the foundation for a conflict that would continue for centuries.
Vlad's own fate became even more unusual.
As a Vampire Progenitor, the future Vampire King possessed the ability to consume Souls and incorporate aspects of particularly valued individuals into his own existence. He eventually consumed the original Vlad Tepes. Because Vlad was given substantial value during the absorption, the Progenitor incorporated enough of Vlad's identity and characteristics that he later adopted Vlad's name and historical identity for himself.[2]
Radu apparently witnessed or otherwise learned of his brother's consumption and eventually developed intense hatred toward the Vampire King, believing him responsible for Vlad's destruction. This grudge ultimately became one of the principal motivations behind Radu's later identity as [[Niklaus Horseman]].[2]
Unknown to both Radu and the Vampire King, however, Vlad had prepared for his eventual disappearance.
Hela had predicted the point at which Vlad would cease to exist and approached him beforehand. She proposed taking a portion of his Core Existence so that she could summon him during a future war. In return, Hela promised to place her protection over his direct descendants and help ensure that they lived good lives.[3]
Vlad accepted.
His existence was consequently divided. One portion remained with Hela, while the remainder was eventually absorbed into the Vampire King. Hela also took the memories of their meeting along with the portion she retained, meaning the Vampire King acquired no knowledge of the agreement after consuming Vlad.[3]
This arrangement allowed the original Vlad Tepes to effectively persist independently despite the Progenitor's absorption.

### Synopsis

#### Dragon God of Chaos
After thousands of years of planning, Hela finally initiated Ragnarök and revealed that the forces she had accumulated extended far beyond the ordinary dead of the Norse [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]]. Using agreements established with exceptional Humans throughout history, she resurrected legendary rulers, commanders and armies to serve in her war against [[Odin]].[1]
The first great Human force revealed was Leonidas and an army of thirty thousand Spartans. Hela then raised another army from the ground: more than 20,000 fully armored soldiers accompanied by skeletal horses. Standing before them was a black-haired, bearded man who immediately recognized that the day promised in his agreement with Hela had finally arrived.[1]
Vlad looked toward the Divine battlefield and expressed obvious excitement, wondering what it would be like to impale a Divine Army before formally acknowledging that he would fulfill his agreement with the Goddess of Death.[1]
[[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] immediately recognized both the man and his troops. The soldiers were the Royal Army of Wallachia, personally commanded by the historical Vlad Tepes. His appearance stunned her because the original Vlad's Soul should theoretically have disappeared after being absorbed into the Vampire King.[1]
The revelation forced the observers to distinguish the two men. [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]], the modern Vampire King and Progenitor, had merely adopted the historical Vlad's identity after consuming his Soul. The resurrected commander standing beside Hela was the actual Human Vlad Tepes upon whom that later identity had been based.[2][1]
Hela subsequently summoned [[Julius Caesar]] and more than seven hundred thousand Roman soldiers, revealing just how many exceptional Human forces she had secretly accumulated over the centuries. She empowered the resurrected armies with her Divine Power, transforming the historical Human soldiers into supernatural warriors capable of participating in Ragnarök.[1]
Vlad and his Wallachians subsequently joined Hela's assault on [[Asgard]] alongside Leonidas, Julius, [[Fenrir]], [[Jormungandr]], Nidhoggr, the undead and creatures from the Abyss.
After the primary battle was decided, Hela ordered her commanders to begin capturing or eliminating the remaining Norse Gods according to the lists she had prepared.[4]
Vlad soon returned while dragging a badly injured God by the hair. The prisoner was [[Vidar]], the God of Revenge and one of Odin's sons. Vidar's body had already been completely broken during his fight with Vlad.[4]
With a movement of his hand, Vlad created a pillar designed for impalement and threw Vidar onto it.
The placement was deliberate.
Every vital organ was avoided so that the Divine resilience of a God would keep Vidar alive while he remained impaled. Vlad had no intention of granting him an immediate death because Hela wanted Odin's children preserved for prolonged punishment.[4]
Vlad then commented that Vidar became unexpectedly lively when facing an entertaining opponent even though the God did not like admitting that side of himself. The remark suggests Vlad had personally fought Vidar long enough to observe his behavior before defeating him.[4]
Following Ragnarök, [[Victor Elderblood]] questioned Hela about how she could possess the Soul of the original Vlad Tepes when the Vampire King had supposedly consumed him centuries earlier.[3]
Hela explained that her extraordinary Affinity with Death allowed her to predict when individuals would die or otherwise cease to exist. Knowing Vlad's future, she had approached him before his disappearance and negotiated a contract.[3]
Vlad's price was not power or another opportunity to rule. His concern was the future of his descendants.
He trusted his Vampire friend but understood the Progenitor's flaws, especially his tendency to neglect matters when concentrating upon too many objectives at once. Vlad therefore accepted Hela's offer to bless and safeguard his direct lineage after his death.[3]
In exchange, Hela took a small portion of his Core Existence for use during the future war.
The action split Vlad's Soul between Hela and the Vampire King. Hela additionally removed the information concerning their agreement along with the portion she claimed, preventing the Vampire King from learning about their arrangement when he eventually absorbed the remainder of Vlad's Soul.[3]
Vlad's resurrection during Ragnarök was therefore not ordinary [[Necromancy]] or the recreation of a historical person from records. The warrior summoned by Hela represented a preserved portion of the original Vlad Tepes' genuine existence.
His final fate following Hela's victory has not been separately detailed.

### Skills & Abilities
Vlad was an exceptional Human warrior and ruler during his original life. After being summoned during Ragnarök, his preserved Soul was additionally supported by Hela's Death authority and Divine empowerment.
The novel has not established Vlad possessing a Divinity, conventional [[Magic]] system or Divine race of his own.

#### Resurrected Soul
Before Vlad's independent existence ended, Hela removed a portion of his Core Existence and retained it for thousands of years.[3]
- Soul Fragment Survival: A genuine part of Vlad continued existing separately even after the rest of his Soul was absorbed by the Vampire Progenitor.
- Post-Mortem Existence: Vlad's consciousness, memories and personality could be restored from the portion retained by Hela.
- Resurrection (External; through Hela): Hela used the preserved fragment to manifest Vlad physically during Ragnarök.[1][3]
- Spiritual Preservation (External): Hela prevented the preserved portion of Vlad from disappearing into the normal reincarnation process.
These abilities depend upon Hela and should not be interpreted as Vlad possessing independent resurrection.

#### Divine Empowerment
Hela empowered the legendary Human armies she resurrected so they could meaningfully participate on the Divine battlefield of Ragnarök.[1]
As one of her principal commanders, Vlad benefited from this enhancement.
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics (Empowered)
- Enhanced Strength (Empowered)
- Enhanced Speed (Empowered)
- Enhanced Durability (Empowered)
- Supernatural Combat Capability (Empowered)
The exact numerical degree of Vlad's enhancement is not stated.

#### Impaling Pillar Creation
Vlad demonstrated the ability to create a large supernatural structure specifically designed for impalement.
After defeating [[Vidar]], he created a pillar with a simple movement of his hand and threw the God onto it.[4]
- Construct Creation
- Impaling Weapon Creation
- Supernatural Structure Creation
The exact Energy or supernatural system used to produce the pillar is not explained.
It should therefore not be arbitrarily classified as Earth Manipulation, [[Magic]] or Divine Power.

#### Precision Impalement
Vlad possesses extraordinary precision when using his preferred method of execution.
When he impaled [[Vidar]], the stake pierced the God while avoiding every vital organ.[4]
Vlad did this intentionally because he needed the God to remain alive.
This demonstrates:
- Anatomical Knowledge
- Precision
- Expert Impalement
- Non-Lethal Torture Expertise

#### Combat Skill
Vlad was capable of personally defeating [[Vidar]], a Major God and son of [[Odin]], after being resurrected and empowered by Hela.[4]
The battle itself is not shown in full, but its aftermath establishes that Vlad had completely broken Vidar's body before dragging him back to the other commanders.
Known combat attributes include:
- Master Combatant
- Close-Quarters Combat
- Battlefield Experience
- God-Combat Capability (While empowered by Hela)
- Torture Combat
- Military Warfare Experience
His exact historical Martial Art or weapon specialty has not been named.

#### Military Leadership
Vlad commanded more than 20,000 members of the Wallachian Royal Army.[1]
- Military Command
- Army Leadership
- Battlefield Coordination
- Strategic Experience
- Large-Scale Warfare
The fact that Hela specifically chose Vlad and his army for Ragnarök indicates that she considered both the commander and his soldiers exceptional among Human forces throughout history.

#### Exceptional Willpower
Vlad retained his sense of identity, personality and ancient agreement after his original death, Soul division and resurrection thousands of years later.
He immediately recognized the purpose of his return and remembered his promise to Hela.[1]
This demonstrates exceptional stability of self after prolonged spiritual preservation.

### Power
Vlad was originally Human, so his Mortal-era physical power should not be confused with his condition during Ragnarök.
Hela's Divine empowerment transformed her resurrected historical forces into supernatural warriors capable of fighting inside [[Asgard]].[1]
Vlad's strongest direct feat during this state was his defeat of [[Vidar]]. When he returned in Chapter 961, Vidar's body had already been completely broken and Vlad casually handled the defeated God as a prisoner.[4]
This establishes that the resurrected and empowered Vlad could defeat at least one Norse Major God under Ragnarök conditions.
However, the exact source distribution of that strength is unclear.
His Ragnarök power represented a combination of:
- his original combat talent and experience;
- the supernatural body created through Hela's resurrection;
- Hela's Divine empowerment;
- and whatever additional properties Hela granted to her armies.
Vlad should therefore not automatically be ranked at Major God level during his original Human life.
No evidence establishes him at God-King or Primordial level.

### Relationships

#### Vlad Dracula Tepes
[[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] was an ancient Vampire Progenitor who knew the original Vlad during his Human life.
Chapter 962 explicitly describes the Vampire as Vlad's friend. Vlad trusted him enough to ask him to transform his sickly younger brother Radu Tepes into a Noble Vampire.[2][3]
Their relationship eventually became much stranger.
The Vampire Progenitor possessed an ability allowing him to consume Souls and incorporate the existence of sufficiently valued individuals into himself. At some point, he consumed Vlad's Soul and later adopted Vlad's identity as his own.[2]
Because the original Vlad was given considerable value during the process, the later Vampire King became strongly influenced by the Human ruler's characteristics.
Despite trusting his Vampire friend, Vlad did not believe the Progenitor was reliable enough to guarantee his descendants' future alone. This concern contributed directly to Vlad accepting Hela's agreement.[3]
The Vampire King never discovered the agreement because Hela removed the relevant memories together with the portion of Vlad's Soul she claimed before the absorption.[3]

#### Radu Tepes
Radu Tepes was Vlad's younger brother.
Radu was sickly and physically weak while Human. Concerned for his survival, Vlad personally asked his Vampire friend to transform Radu into a Vampire.[2]
The request succeeded, but later produced tragic consequences.
Radu apparently witnessed or became aware of the Progenitor consuming his brother's Soul and developed an intense desire for revenge. Centuries later, he operated under the identity [[Niklaus Horseman]] while continuing to pursue the Vampire King.[2]
Because the Vampire King was not actually the original Vlad but had absorbed him and adopted his identity, Radu's relationship with the modern Vlad became extremely complicated.

#### Hela
Hela approached Vlad before his independent existence disappeared because her extraordinary Death Affinity allowed her to predict when he would cease to exist.[3]
She offered him a contract.
Vlad would surrender a portion of his Core Existence so that Hela could summon him during a future war.
In exchange, Hela would place supernatural protection upon his bloodline and help guarantee good lives for his direct descendants.[3]
Vlad accepted the agreement as a precaution for his family's future.
Thousands of years later, Hela fulfilled her side sufficiently for Vlad to willingly answer her call during Ragnarök.[1]
Their relationship is contractual rather than one of ordinary allegiance.

#### Vidar
[[Vidar]] became one of Vlad's opponents during the final stages of Ragnarök.
The actual fight occurs off-page, but Vlad returned carrying the defeated God by his hair with Vidar's body already completely broken.[4]
Vlad then impaled him through non-vital organs to keep him alive for Hela.
He also commented that Vidar became surprisingly lively when facing an entertaining opponent, suggesting Vlad had observed the God's behavior directly during their battle.[4]

#### Leonidas
Leonidas was another legendary Human commander resurrected by Hela for Ragnarök.
Both men had entered agreements with the Goddess of Death before their respective deaths, although their motivations differed.
Vlad's primary concern was the protection of his descendants, while Leonidas desired the opportunity to participate in a higher-level war worthy of his warrior spirit.[3]
The two fought together under Hela during the fall of [[Asgard]].

#### Julius Caesar
[[Julius Caesar]] was another ruler resurrected by Hela.
Julius returned commanding more than seven hundred thousand Roman soldiers, while Vlad commanded more than twenty thousand Wallachians.[1]
Like Vlad, Julius had entered an agreement with Hela before his death, although Julius' desire was to observe the future development of his Empire.[3]

### Identity and the Vampire King
The existence of two characters using the name Vlad Tepes creates significant confusion within the story.
Character
Identity
Vlad III Tepes
The original Human ruler of Wallachia and historical Impaler. Brother of Radu Tepes. His Soul was later consumed by the Vampire Progenitor, although Hela secretly preserved part of his Core Existence beforehand.[2][3]
[[Vlad Dracula Tepes]]
An ancient Vampire Progenitor who existed long before meeting the Human Vlad. He possessed numerous names and forms throughout history before consuming Vlad III's Soul and later adopting his identity.[2]
The modern Vampire King is therefore neither a simple reincarnation nor literally the same individual as the original Human Vlad.
The process resembles, in principle, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s absorption of Adonis, where the Progenitor consumes another existence and integrates its characteristics into himself.[2]
