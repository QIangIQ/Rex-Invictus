### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 119
Adam Tepes is the youngest known son of [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] and the only child born from Vlad's relationship with Jeanne d'Arc. Originally born as a Noble Vampire, Adam is the Fourth Prince of [[Nightingale]]. He was approximately five hundred years old when first introduced and had only recently reached adulthood according to Vampire standards; he later explicitly identified himself as a 518-year-old adult Vampire.[1][2]
Adam inherited considerably more of his original physical constitution from Vlad than Jeanne. Consequently, he was born as a genuine Noble Vampire rather than reproducing the unusual spiritual existence Jeanne possessed before becoming a Dragon. Nevertheless, he inherited Jeanne's appearance, an affinity toward Wind and a portion of her abnormal spiritual nature. Even this comparatively small spiritual inheritance caused Adam's Soul to become significantly larger and stronger than the Soul of an ordinary Vampire, to the point that Jeanne agreed that Adam could be compared with a [[Demigod]] on a spiritual level.[3]
Despite possessing an exceptional lineage, Adam historically failed to properly realize his potential. Vlad considered him the son with the greatest potential to eventually become King during Adam's youth, while [[Victor Elderblood]] later concluded that Adam possessed a good Bloodline but lacked the discipline, motivation and desire required to properly develop it.[4][5][6]
Adam's racial status later changed considerably. By Chapter 1171, he had successfully passed the trials required for entry into Victor's Draconic lineage, first evolving into a Lesser Dragon and later advancing into a full Dragon. He did not reach the vastly superior state of a True Dragon, but Jeanne nevertheless regarded him as theoretically a Dragon God originating from Victor's lineage. Despite this evolution and his enormous Soul, Adam's realized strength remained comparatively low by Imperial standards, with his current threat level being described as only Planetary Level.[7]
Following Jeanne's marriage to Victor, Adam also became Victor's stepson and the maternal half-brother of [[Isabelle Elderblood]].

### Physical Appearance
Adam originally appeared as a youthful Noble Vampire with pale skin, blonde hair and red eyes. Despite already being several centuries old, he had only recently reached adulthood by the standards of his species and therefore appeared considerably younger than his millennia-old elder brothers.[1]
His appearance predominantly reflects Jeanne. Although his original body inherited overwhelmingly more from Vlad's Vampire lineage, Jeanne later explained that Adam inherited aspects of her physical appearance alongside a smaller portion of her spiritual nature.[3]
Adam later evolved from Noble Vampire to Lesser Dragon and eventually into a full Dragon through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s lineage.[7] The novel has not yet provided a sufficiently detailed physical description of Adam's current Draconic form to establish specific horns, wings, scales, coloration or other visible Dragon characteristics.

### Personality
Adam is considerably more restrained than several members of the older Tepes generation. Vlad himself regarded Adam more favorably than his elder sons, telling him that he was doing well while warning him never to stop training or allow the privileges of royalty to make him soft.[4]
Despite his royal upbringing, Adam possesses a casual sense of humor. While observing the confrontation between Natashia Fulger and [[Niklaus Horseman]], Adam casually discussed the enormous ages of his relatives with [[Lucas Tepes]] and eventually described the Tepes household as a "strange family".[8]
Adam's strongest emotional attachment during his youth was toward Jeanne. After Jeanne left Vlad, Adam wanted to follow his mother and was distressed when he was prevented from doing so. Although he continued seeking Vlad's attention, approval and respect, Jeanne was the parent who had consistently remained beside him before her illness and coma. The narration explicitly established that if Adam were asked which of his parents he loved most, his answer would always have been his mother.[9]
His relationship with Vlad also heavily influenced Adam's attitude toward parental expectations. When [[Tatsuya Rider]] stated that he intended to make a future child become a warrior, Adam immediately warned him that forcing a child to follow a path they had not personally chosen was a poor decision, speaking from his own experience as Vlad's son.[10]
Adam does not reject responsibility or leadership itself. He voluntarily joined lessons concerning Clan administration because he believed the knowledge might become useful and admitted to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] that he was considering establishing a Clan of his own in the future.[10]
Adam's feelings concerning Victor and Jeanne's increasingly intimate relationship were initially complicated. Nevertheless, he understood that Vlad had neglected Jeanne and recognized that he had rarely seen his mother as happy as she became around Victor. Rather than allowing his personal discomfort to become hostility, Adam consciously chose not to interfere with her happiness.[10]
His most persistent personal weakness is his tendency to undervalue both himself and the opportunities created by his lineage. Adam once dismissed himself as ordinary compared with Victor and had largely abandoned serious development of his inherited Wind ability after concluding that his inability to perfectly reproduce Jeanne's powers meant his inheritance was defective.[10][5][6]
Victor considered this mentality far more damaging than Adam's actual limitations. Adam was the son of a Vampire Progenitor and Jeanne d'Arc, possessed access to exceptional teachers and had inherited a powerful elemental Bloodline. Victor believed Adam simply lacked the obsessive motivation necessary to transform those advantages into strength.[5][6]
Adam also developed a strong fear of [[Scathach]]'s training after previously undergoing one of her evaluations. Victor later revealed that Adam had misunderstood the experience: what terrified him had merely been Scathach determining whether he was worthy of instruction rather than the genuine training received by disciples she actually accepted.[2][5]
Adam eventually developed enough determination to pass the Elderblood family's trials and evolve from Lesser Dragon into a full Dragon. Even after accomplishing this, however, Jeanne and Scathach continued to regard motivation as the primary obstacle preventing him from realizing his potential. By Chapter 1171, Jeanne remained dissatisfied that someone possessing Adam's Bloodline, Dragon body and enormous Soul was only classified at Planetary Level and resolved to push him toward further training so that he could eventually become capable of protecting [[Isabelle Elderblood]].[7]

### Synopsis

#### Vampire Society
Adam first appeared when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] Alucard was summoned before [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]]. Vlad's four sons were introduced during the meeting, with Adam occupying the position of Fourth Prince. While Theo was approximately three thousand years old, Lucas around two thousand and Saul roughly one thousand, Adam was only approximately five hundred years old and had recently reached adulthood by Vampire standards.[1]
Adam already displayed greater restraint than some of his elder brothers during the gathering, questioning whether they seriously intended to begin fighting in their father's presence.[1]
Victor's rapid development later caused Vlad to reconsider the failures of his own children. After harshly criticizing several of his elder sons, Vlad turned toward Adam and simply stated that he was doing well. He warned Adam never to stop training, particularly because his status allowed him access to some of the finest instructors available.[4]
Vlad privately regarded Adam as the son with the greatest potential to eventually become King at that stage of his life. He did not regard the result as guaranteed and understood that Adam's future choices and development could still radically alter his character.[4]

#### A New Pillar
Adam later attended the confrontation between Natashia Fulger and [[Niklaus Horseman]] alongside other members of the Royal Family.[8]
During the gathering Adam displayed a considerably less formal personality than expected from a Vampire Prince. While speaking with Lucas, he joked about the absurd ages of their relatives and eventually described the Tepes household as a "strange family", with Lucas agreeing.[8]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
The collapse of Vlad's household affected Adam far more personally through Jeanne than through politics. After Jeanne left [[Nightingale]], Adam wanted to follow his mother but was prevented from leaving when Vlad ordered his children to remain within the Royal residence.[9]
Although Adam still wanted Vlad's approval, his strongest emotional attachment remained Jeanne. She had been consistently present throughout his childhood before her coma, and Adam had suffered considerably during the period in which her deteriorating condition left her unconscious.[9]
Adam later reunited with Jeanne while she was with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. He formally introduced himself as Vlad's fourth male child and a 518-year-old adult Vampire. Victor had only encountered Adam a small number of times previously and had never properly interacted with him.[2]
Adam immediately recognized the enormous difference between himself and Victor. Victor's presence alone placed him under pressure unlike anything Adam normally experienced around his elder brothers. Jeanne openly acknowledged that Adam was nowhere near Victor's level, but Victor nevertheless offered to train him.[2]
The offer immediately reminded Adam of his previous experience with [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]]. Adam had already undergone one of [[Scathach]]'s evaluations alongside Royal personnel and remembered the experience with considerable fear. Jeanne nevertheless accepted Victor's offer on Adam's behalf. She explained that Adam had recently undergone the major Vampire growth increase associated with reaching adulthood and still needed to properly adapt to and develop his strengthened body.[2]
Because Jeanne had always treated him kindly and rarely demanded anything directly from him, Adam found it particularly difficult to reject one of the few occasions when she explicitly ordered him to do something.[2]

#### Demonic War
Adam later joined lessons being conducted for the younger generation surrounding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. The original students included [[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]], [[Nero Elderblood]], [[Tatsuya Rider]] and [[Yuki Snow]], while Adam, [[Lilith Tepes]] and [[Elizabeth Tepes]] also participated.[10]
During a discussion concerning future children, Tatsuya stated that he intended to make his future child become a warrior. Adam immediately advised against forcing a child to follow a path they had not personally chosen, explicitly drawing upon his experience growing beneath Vlad's expectations.[10]
Adam's own participation in the lessons was voluntary. He wanted to learn how to administer and control a Clan because he believed he might need those skills in the future. When Victor asked whether Adam intended to establish a Clan of his own, Adam confirmed that he was considering doing so.[10]
The conversation also exposed Adam's feelings regarding Jeanne and Victor. Although their relationship created understandable discomfort for him, Adam recognized how poorly Vlad had treated Jeanne and admitted that he had never seen his mother so happy. He therefore chose not to interfere merely because the situation was personally awkward.[10]
Victor regarded Adam as one of Vlad's children who still possessed considerable room for growth because he had only recently entered Vampire adulthood. Adam nevertheless surprised Victor by dismissing himself as ordinary and treating Victor's development as something possible only for an abnormal genius.[10]
Victor strongly rejected that attitude. Adam was the direct biological son of the First Vampire Progenitor and Jeanne d'Arc, giving him advantages that ordinary Noble Vampires could never possess. In Victor's assessment, Adam's true problem was not insufficient talent but his lack of commitment, discipline and determination.[5]
Victor also explained that Adam had misunderstood his previous experience under [[Scathach]]. The brutal treatment he remembered was merely an evaluation designed to determine whether Scathach considered him worthy of actual training. Adam had never experienced the genuine instruction Victor and Scathach's accepted disciples endured.[5]
When Victor questioned Adam regarding his personal ability, Adam demonstrated control over Wind by gathering air into a sphere above his hand. Adam explained the fundamental difference between himself and Jeanne: Adam could control Wind, while Jeanne could both create and control it.[5]
Adam had interpreted this limitation as evidence that his inherited ability was an inferior version of Jeanne's power. He consequently concentrated most of his effort upon ordinary Vampire abilities while using Wind primarily as support.[6]
Victor rejected that interpretation. Vlad's Progenitor Blood naturally strengthened the Bloodlines inherited by his descendants, meaning Adam's inability to reproduce every aspect of Jeanne's ability did not make the Bloodline inherently weak. Victor argued that Adam had simply failed to explore its possibilities.[6]
At that stage Adam admitted that he could neither create Wind from nothing nor control sufficiently large quantities to produce enormous phenomena. Victor nevertheless explained that Adam's power possessed destructive potential comparable to the hereditary elements of [[Nightingale]]'s Count Clans and stated that hurricane-scale manipulation was achievable if Adam acquired sufficient power, control and determination.[6]

#### King Among Kings
Much later, [[Morgana Elderblood]] asked Jeanne how Adam was doing. Jeanne answered that he was progressing normally and stated that Vlad's reduced presence had finally allowed Adam to become more like a "normal" young man.[11]
Despite her own problems with Vlad, Jeanne did not attempt to erase him from Adam's life. She acknowledged that Vlad remained Adam's biological father and would inevitably continue to occupy a place in his son's future.[11]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Vlad later brought Adam personally to another [[Supernatural Gathering]]. Jeanne immediately became furious because the gathering contained Gods, Progenitors and other beings operating at levels vastly beyond Adam's own.[12]
Jeanne's anger was not caused by embarrassment over Adam. She understood his current limitations and considered placing him among beings of that scale needlessly dangerous.[12]
Vlad's decision was nevertheless deliberate. He wanted Adam to personally witness powers existing far beyond ordinary Vampire society rather than allowing his understanding of strength to remain confined to [[Nightingale]]. Vlad also remained interested in discovering whether Adam had inherited greater potential from Jeanne than had yet manifested.[12]
Jeanne understood Vlad's reasoning but remained less optimistic regarding how much of her unusual existence Adam could realistically inherit. Jeanne had never truly been equivalent to a conventional Noble Vampire even before becoming a Dragon, making Adam's inheritance fundamentally more complicated than simply combining two ordinary Vampire Bloodlines.[12]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
The exact nature of Adam's inheritance from Jeanne was clarified much later. Jeanne explained that before receiving her Dragon body, her original existence had fundamentally been an exceptionally powerful Soul imitating the body of a Noble Vampire. Her nature was consequently closer to a Divine Spirit than to an ordinary biological Vampire.[3]
When Adam was conceived, he inherited overwhelmingly more from Vlad. His physical existence consequently developed as a genuine Noble Vampire rather than reproducing Jeanne's unusual spiritual state. From Jeanne he primarily inherited her appearance and a relatively small portion of her spiritual characteristics.[3]
That "small" spiritual inheritance nevertheless produced a major abnormality. Adam's Soul became considerably larger and stronger than that of a normal Vampire. When Ruby compared his spiritual state to that of a [[Demigod]], Jeanne agreed that the comparison was appropriate.[3]
Adam should therefore not be classified as a conventional biological Demigod. The comparison specifically concerns the size and power of his Soul rather than possession of every racial characteristic normally belonging to Demigods.[3]
Adam's racial status later changed considerably. By Chapter 1171, the Elderblood family's rules regarding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Dragon lineage had been firmly established. Entry into that lineage was not automatically granted merely because someone was related to Victor. Candidates were required to pass trials imposed by the family.[7]
Adam successfully passed those trials. He first evolved into a Lesser Dragon and subsequently advanced into a full Dragon.[7]
He did not become a True Dragon. Chapter 1171 explicitly distinguishes ordinary members of Victor's extended Draconic lineage from the vastly superior [[True Dragons]] of his immediate family. Nevertheless, Jeanne regarded Adam as theoretically a Dragon God belonging to Victor's lineage.[7]
His racial evolution did not automatically unlock his enormous spiritual potential. Jeanne observed that Adam's powerful Soul remained largely unrealized and that his overall development continued to fall far below what his heritage should theoretically permit.[7]
Adam's threat level at this stage was explicitly described as Planetary Level. Although enormously stronger than an ordinary Noble Vampire, this level was still insufficient to qualify among the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s elite forces.[7]
The comparison became especially unfavorable when Adam was contrasted with [[Tatsuya Rider]]. Despite never becoming a Dragon, Tatsuya had developed into one of the most powerful Noble Vampires and reached a level where both he and Vlad could destroy entire Solar Systems. Adam, despite possessing a Dragon body and enormous Soul, remained far below them.[7]
Jeanne attributed much of the disparity to Adam's continued lack of motivation. She nevertheless refused to accept his stagnation and decided that he would undergo further training, including returning him to [[Scathach]] if necessary. Jeanne's immediate objective was for Adam to become strong enough to protect his younger sister [[Isabelle Elderblood]].[7]
Scathach agreed that Isabelle herself was an unfair standard because of the extraordinary circumstances of her birth, but likewise considered Adam's mentality his central weakness. In her assessment, Adam lacked even a fraction of the relentless motivation that allowed Victor to repeatedly surpass his original limitations.[7]

#### Powers & Abilities
Adam's powers originate from three principal sources: his original Noble Vampire physiology and direct descent from [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]], the Wind-related Bloodline inherited from [[Jeanne Elderblood]], and his later evolution through [[Victor Elderblood]]'s Draconic lineage. Although his ancestry gives him exceptional theoretical potential, the story repeatedly emphasizes the considerable gap between that potential and Adam's realized strength.[1][2][3][4]

##### Dragon God Physiology
Dragon God Physiology: Adam is currently a Dragon God belonging to [[Victor Elderblood]]'s Imperial Draconic lineage. After successfully passing the trials established by Victor's family, Adam first evolved into a Lesser Dragon before eventually advancing into a full Dragon. Jeanne explicitly regarded him as a Dragon God from Victor's lineage.[4]
Adam should not be confused with a True Dragon God. [[True Dragons]] are primarily members of Victor's immediate family and represent a vastly superior stage of Draconic existence. Whereas True Dragons are described as embodiments of nature itself and naturally compatible with virtually all aspects of existence, ordinary Dragons are weaker members of the same broader lineage.[4]
- Dragon Physiology: Adam possesses the supernatural physiology and racial characteristics of a full Dragon.[4]
- Imperial Dragon Bloodline: Adam belongs specifically to Victor's Imperial Dragon lineage, which is stated to be significantly superior to ordinary Dragons outside the lineage.[4]
- Noble Vampire-Dragon Hybridization: Members of this lesser Imperial Dragon lineage retain powers originating from their previous races. Adam therefore possesses characteristics of both a Noble Vampire and a Dragon.[4]
- Draconic Evolution: Adam progressed from Noble Vampire to Lesser Dragon and ultimately to full Dragon after successfully completing the required trials.[4]
- Enhanced Potential: As the son of [[Jeanne Elderblood]], bearer of a tremendous Soul and member of Victor's Dragon lineage, Adam possesses far greater theoretical potential than his current strength suggests.[5][4]
- Planetary-Level Power: Despite being a Dragon God, Adam's current realized threat level is only Planetary Level. Jeanne regards this as severe underdevelopment rather than his natural limit.[4]
True Dragon Distinction: Adam is not a True Dragon. Chapter 1171 distinguishes Lesser Dragons, Dragons and the vastly superior True Dragons associated primarily with Victor's immediate family. Adam advanced from Lesser Dragon into Dragon but has not been stated to possess the True Dragon state.[4]

##### Noble Vampire Physiology
Noble Vampire Physiology: Adam was originally born as a naturally occurring Noble Vampire and direct biological son of the First Vampire Progenitor, [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]]. He consequently possesses the supernatural physical foundation and racial characteristics inherited from high-level Noble Vampire ancestry.[6]
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Adam naturally possessed physical strength, speed, reflexes, agility, durability and stamina far beyond ordinary Humans even before obtaining Dragon physiology.[6]
- Regenerative Healing Factor: As a Noble Vampire, Adam possesses supernatural regenerative capabilities.
- Enhanced Senses: Adam's original Vampire physiology possesses senses significantly superior to those of ordinary Humans.
- Longevity/Immortality: Adam had already lived for more than five centuries while only recently reaching adulthood according to Vampire standards. He explicitly identified himself as a 518-year-old adult Vampire in Chapter 439.[7]
- [[Vampiric Energy]] Manipulation: Adam originally possessed and utilized Vampiric Energy as the supernatural Energy associated with his race.
- Vampire Growth Boosts: Noble Vampires undergo significant increases in physical and supernatural strength as they age. Adam had only recently experienced the growth increase associated with reaching Vampire adulthood when Jeanne arranged additional training for him.[7][8]
Adam's later Dragon evolution builds upon rather than completely invalidates his original Vampire heritage. Chapter 1171 establishes that members of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s lesser Draconic lineage can retain characteristics originating from their previous races.[4]

##### Progenitor Bloodline
Direct Progenitor Descent: Adam is the biological son of the Vampire Progenitor [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]]. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly rejected Adam's tendency to treat himself as ordinary, explaining that the direct child of a Progenitor naturally possesses an exceptional Bloodline and developmental foundation.[2]
- Enhanced Potential: Vlad considered Adam the son with the greatest potential to eventually become King during Adam's youth.[1]
- Bloodline Empowerment: Vlad's Progenitor Blood strengthens Bloodlines inherited by his descendants. Consequently, Adam's inability to reproduce every aspect of Jeanne's Wind ability did not mean that his inherited Bloodline was intrinsically weaker.[3]
- Hybridized Inheritance: Adam inherited the majority of his original physical constitution from Vlad but also received appearance, Wind-related characteristics and spiritual traits from Jeanne.[5]
- Exceptional Developmental Ceiling: Victor considered Adam's Bloodline good and believed his poor development resulted primarily from insufficient training and mentality rather than weak ancestry.[2][3]

##### Wind Manipulation
Wind Manipulation: Adam inherited an elemental affinity toward Wind from [[Jeanne Elderblood]]. During his training with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], he demonstrated this ability by gathering and controlling air above his hand.[2]
- Aerokinesis: Adam can manipulate existing Wind and air currents.[2]
- Air Manipulation: Adam can gather and shape controlled air into concentrated formations.[2]
- Wind Projection: His Wind can be externally directed rather than remaining limited to passive environmental manipulation.[2]
- Support Usage: Adam originally used Wind primarily as a secondary ability while concentrating his development on ordinary Vampire powers.[3]
- Destructive Wind Manipulation: Victor stated that Adam's Wind possesses the potential to become extremely destructive and could eventually rival the offensive value of the elemental Bloodlines belonging to [[Nightingale]]'s Count Clans.[3]
- Large-Scale Wind Manipulation (Potential): Victor rejected Adam's assumption that his ability was permanently restricted to small amounts of Wind and argued that the amount he could control would increase with sufficient development.[3]
- Hurricane Generation (Potential): Victor explicitly used the creation of a hurricane as an example of what Adam could eventually accomplish through sufficient strength and control. Adam had not demonstrated hurricane-scale manipulation at the time.[3]
- Bloodline Development: Adam's elemental power is capable of continued refinement and evolution rather than remaining permanently limited to its original manifestation.[2][3]
Wind Generation Limitation: During the period in which Adam explained his ability, an important distinction existed between himself and Jeanne. Adam could control Wind, whereas Jeanne could both create and control Wind. Adam specifically stated that he could not create Wind from nothing.[2][3]
Scale Limitation (Earlier): Adam also admitted that he could not control sufficiently large quantities of Wind to reproduce hurricane-scale effects. Victor considered this a developmental limitation rather than proof of an absolute ceiling.[3]

##### Spiritual Abilities
Enhanced Soul: Adam inherited a portion of Jeanne's abnormal spiritual nature. Before obtaining her Dragon body, Jeanne's original existence was essentially an extraordinarily powerful Soul imitating a Noble Vampire body, placing her closer to a Divine Spirit than an ordinary biological Vampire.[5]
- Abnormally Large Soul: Adam's Soul is explicitly stated to be significantly larger than that of a normal Vampire.[5]
- Abnormally Powerful Soul: Jeanne described Adam's Soul as considerably stronger than normal rather than merely larger.[5]
- [[Demigod]]-Like Soul: [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] compared Adam's spiritual condition to that of a Demigod, and Jeanne agreed with the comparison.[5]
- Enhanced Spiritual Potential: Adam's enormous Soul represents one of his greatest unrealized developmental advantages.[5][4]
- Untapped Soul Potential: Even after Adam evolved into a Dragon, Jeanne stated that he had barely exploited the potential contained within his Soul.[4]
Note: Adam's Demigod comparison refers specifically to the size and strength of his Soul. He is not established as a conventional biological Demigod and should not automatically be assigned every racial characteristic or power possessed by ordinary Demigods.[5]

- Vampire Combat Training: Adam has centuries of experience using his Vampire body and racial abilities.
- [[Scathach]]'s Evaluation: Adam previously underwent one of [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]]'s brutal evaluations alongside members of Nightingale's Royal forces. The experience left him extremely reluctant to undergo similar instruction again.[7][2]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Instruction: Jeanne accepted Victor's offer to assist Adam's development after the young Prince entered Vampire adulthood.[7]
- Power Control Training: Victor specifically instructed Adam to stop relying exclusively upon the abilities automatically provided by his race and properly develop his Wind Bloodline.[2][3]
- Bloodline Development: Adam learned from Victor that hereditary abilities can continue evolving through training, creativity, output and greater control rather than remaining permanently limited to their original state.[3]
- Draconic Transformation Trials: Adam eventually passed the Elderblood family's tests for entry into Victor's Dragon lineage, progressing from Lesser Dragon to Dragon.[4]
Leadership and Clan Administration: Adam voluntarily participated in lessons concerning Clan administration because he intended to potentially establish an independent Clan in the future.[8]
- Clan Management: Adam studied the knowledge required to organize and control a supernatural Clan.[8]
- Leadership Training: His education included preparation for eventually commanding subordinates and assuming political responsibility.
- Royal Education: As the Fourth Prince of Nightingale, Adam grew up within the Vampire Royal Family and possesses education concerning Vampire politics and Royal society.

#### Power and Threat Level
Adam's development is characterized by an unusually large disparity between potential and realized strength.
During his youth, Vlad regarded Adam as the son with the greatest potential to become King, while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] independently recognized that Adam possessed an exceptional foundation through Vlad's Progenitor Blood and Jeanne's lineage.[1][2]
Nevertheless, Victor considered much of that potential wasted. Adam had spent centuries relying heavily upon the automatic advantages provided by Noble Vampire physiology while barely developing his individual Wind Bloodline.[2][3]
Adam's hidden spiritual potential later proved even greater than previously understood. Jeanne revealed that his Soul was vastly larger and stronger than that of a normal Vampire and could reasonably be compared with a [[Demigod]]'s Soul.[5]
His racial evolution eventually granted him a major increase in power. After passing the Elderblood family's trials, Adam evolved first into a Lesser Dragon and then into a full Dragon.[4]
Despite these advantages, Adam's confirmed threat classification by Chapter 1171 remained only Planetary Level. The Imperial family explicitly considered this insufficient for one of their elite combatants.[4]
The gap becomes more obvious when compared with [[Tatsuya Rider]]. Tatsuya never became a Dragon yet developed into one of the strongest Noble Vampires, reaching a level where he and Vlad were capable of destroying entire Solar Systems. Adam possessed both Dragon physiology and an enormous Soul but remained considerably below them.[4]
Jeanne therefore viewed Adam's Planetary level as evidence of stagnation rather than his natural maximum. She intended to subject him to further training until he became powerful enough to properly exploit his Bloodline, Dragon body and Soul.[4]

#### Potential
Adam possesses several independent sources of exceptional developmental potential:
- Vampire Progenitor Blood: Adam is the direct biological son of [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]].[6][2]
- Jeanne's Wind Bloodline: Adam inherited control over Wind from [[Jeanne Elderblood]].[2]
- Enhanced Bloodline Quality: Vlad's Progenitor Blood strengthens hereditary characteristics inherited by his descendants.[3]
- Enormous Soul: Adam possesses a Soul far larger and stronger than that of an ordinary Noble Vampire and spiritually comparable to a [[Demigod]].[5]
- Dragon Evolution: Adam successfully entered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Draconic lineage and progressed from Lesser Dragon to full Dragon.[4]
- Exceptional Tutors: Adam has access to individuals such as Victor, Jeanne and [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], giving him training opportunities unavailable to ordinary supernatural beings.[7][2][4]
Vlad originally believed Adam possessed the greatest potential among his sons to become King.[1] Victor later independently recognized that Adam had an excellent Bloodline but criticized him for failing to exploit it properly.[2][3]
By Chapter 1171, Jeanne remained convinced that Adam could become vastly stronger than his present level. She intended for him to develop enough strength to protect his younger sister [[Isabelle Elderblood]].[4]

#### Weaknesses & Limitations
- Lack of Motivation: Adam's greatest recurring weakness is insufficient motivation to improve. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] identified this problem during his training, and Jeanne continued making the same criticism much later.[2][3][4]
- Underdeveloped Bloodline: Adam spent much of his early life prioritizing ordinary Vampire abilities while using Wind only as support because he believed his inherited power was defective.[3]
- Wind Generation Limitation (Earlier): Adam originally could manipulate existing Wind but could not create Wind from nothing as Jeanne could.[2][3]
- Limited Wind Scale (Earlier): Adam could not initially control sufficiently large amounts of Wind to reproduce hurricane-scale phenomena.[3]
- Fear of Extreme Training: Adam's experience with [[Scathach]]'s evaluation left him strongly reluctant to undergo further training under her.[7][2]
- Untapped Soul: Despite possessing an enormous Soul, Adam had still failed to properly exploit its potential even after becoming a Dragon.[4]
- Not a True Dragon: Adam is a Dragon but has not reached the vastly superior True Dragon state associated with Victor's immediate family.[4]
- Below Imperial Elite Standard: Adam's Planetary-Level threat classification remains below the minimum expected from the Empire's highest-level warriors.[4]
