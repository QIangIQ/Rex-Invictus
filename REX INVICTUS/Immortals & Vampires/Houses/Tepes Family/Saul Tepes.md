### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 119
Saul Tepes is the third son of [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]], the Third Prince of [[Nightingale]], and a Noble Vampire of the [[Tepes Clan]]. He was approximately one thousand years old when [[Victor Elderblood]] first entered the Vampire Royal Court, placing him between [[Lucas Tepes]] and [[Adam Tepes]] in age among Vlad's four sons.[1]
Compared with his elder brothers, Saul received considerably less attention within the political intrigues of the Royal Family. Vlad nevertheless described him as occasionally perceptive, while simultaneously criticizing his laziness, poor memory and tendency to recognize danger without following through on what he had noticed. One such failure had previously allowed an assassination attempt against Saul to indirectly result in his younger sister [[Elizabeth Tepes]] being injured.[2]
Saul remained loyal to the Tepes Royal Family through the collapse of Vlad's original household. His mother, Vlad's unnamed third wife, was executed after her affair with the Greek God [[Eros]] was exposed. Saul privately believed his mother did not deserve death because Vlad's prolonged neglect had contributed to her betrayal, but he suppressed his resentment out of fear that openly opposing his father could result in his own execution.[3][4]
During the Demonic War, Saul fought alongside his surviving siblings and Vlad's Royal Guards when [[Bael]] invaded the Royal Castle. The defenders collectively inflicted severe injuries upon the First Duke of Hell before Saul, Lucas Tepes, Adam Tepes and Elizabeth were defeated.[5]

### Physical Appearance
Very little has been revealed about Saul's physical appearance.
He is a male Noble Vampire with black hair and the youthful appearance typical of ancient members of his Race despite having lived for more than a millennium.[1]
As a direct child of the Vampire Progenitor, Saul possesses the supernatural attractiveness and physical refinement generally associated with Vlad's Royal lineage, although the novel has not provided a detailed individual description of his height, eye color, clothing or other distinctive characteristics.

### Personality
Saul is comparatively quiet and less politically aggressive than his two elder brothers.
During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s first appearance before the Royal Family, Saul did not immediately participate in Theo and Lucas' argument. Instead, he silently observed Vlad and correctly suspected that his father had summoned the entire family because he intended to show them something rather than merely hold an ordinary meeting.[1][2]
This demonstrated the trait Vlad later acknowledged directly:
Saul could be perceptive.
[2]
His problem was that this perception was unreliable.
Vlad described Saul as someone who could notice something important and then fail to think deeply enough about it, forget it or simply neglect to act. He compared his son's mentality to a horse that continually walked forward and jokingly questioned whether Saul possessed the brain of a chicken.[2]
Saul's greatest explicitly demonstrated flaw was laziness.
Several years before Victor's arrival, Saul realized that a maid intended to assassinate him. Rather than immediately dealing with the danger, he ignored the situation because he was too lazy to act. The consequences extended beyond himself and resulted in Elizabeth being injured.[2]
Vlad used the incident to force Saul to recognize that negligence from someone possessing Royal responsibilities could harm other people.
Unlike Theo, Saul did not openly challenge Vlad or become consumed by ambition for the throne. He was nevertheless capable of privately disagreeing with his father.
After Vlad executed Saul's mother, Saul believed her death had been unjust. Although she had betrayed Vlad, Saul understood that Vlad's own treatment of his wives had played a major role in creating the circumstances that led to the affair.[4]
Saul kept these feelings entirely to himself.
Seeing Vlad kill Theo and multiple wives without visible remorse convinced Saul that expressing his resentment could result in his own death. His reaction therefore differed from Theo's rebellion and Lucas' conspiratorial fear: Saul mourned quietly and chose self-preservation.[4]

### Synopsis

#### Vampire Society
Saul first appeared when [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] summoned all seven of his children to the Royal Castle before meeting [[Victor Elderblood]]. At the time, Saul was identified as the Third Prince and approximately one thousand years old.[1]
While [[Lucas Tepes]] openly questioned why their father had called them together and [[Theo Tepes]] attempted to silence him, Saul remained quiet and watched Vlad from a distance.
He already suspected that the gathering was unusual.
Vlad rarely summoned every one of his children simultaneously unless he wanted them to witness something important.[1]
Saul's suspicion proved correct.
After Victor attacked Vlad despite the almost unimaginable difference in strength between them, Vlad later gathered his children and explained that the confrontation had been a demonstration.
He wanted them to see someone capable of challenging an overwhelmingly superior enemy rather than instinctively submitting through fear.[2]
Saul had already realized that something about the meeting had been intended as a demonstration, but he had failed to follow the thought far enough to understand what his father wanted them to learn.[2]
Vlad subsequently evaluated each of his adult children.
When he reached Saul, he described him as:
perceptive at times, but too dumb.
[2]
Vlad's criticism was not that Saul completely lacked intelligence.
His problem was inconsistency.
Vlad reminded him of an incident several years earlier when one of the household maids had attempted to murder Saul. Saul had actually noticed her hostile intentions but did nothing because he considered dealing with the problem troublesome.[2]
That negligence eventually resulted in [[Elizabeth Tepes]] being injured.
When Vlad pointed toward Elizabeth, she instinctively touched her arms, demonstrating that the incident had left enough of an impression for her to remember the injury.[2]
Saul did not attempt to justify himself beyond admitting:
"I was too lazy."
[2]
Unlike several of his siblings, he ultimately understood the criticism.

#### A New Pillar
Saul remained largely outside the conflict surrounding [[Theo Tepes]], [[Lucas Tepes]] and their sons.
When the two elder princes' sons were eventually killed as a consequence of their conflict with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Theo and Lucas demanded Vlad's intervention while Saul did not become directly involved in their attempt to seek revenge.
This relative distance from Theo's political ambitions later became important because Saul was not identified as one of the sons conspiring with Theo to overthrow Vlad.

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
The collapse of Vlad's original family affected Saul far more directly.
Vlad's first three wives had all betrayed him after centuries of increasing emotional abandonment.
Saul's mother—the unnamed Third Queen—had entered into an affair with [[Eros]], the Greek God associated with Love and Desire.[3]
Once Vlad's household scandals became impossible to conceal, the Vampire King killed the women who had betrayed him.
Saul's mother was one of those executed.[3]
Shortly afterward, [[Alexios Alioth]] was ordered to explain the entire situation to Vlad's surviving sons.
Both Lucas and Saul were devastated by the deaths of their mothers and their elder brother Theo.[4]
Their reactions differed considerably.
Lucas was particularly affected by Theo's death because the two had spent thousands of years together despite their love-hate relationship. He was also terrified because he had participated in Theo's conspiracy to overthrow Vlad.
Saul's grief centered more strongly upon his mother.[4]
Although Saul knew that she had betrayed Vlad, he believed:
she did not deserve to die.
[4]
In Saul's view, her betrayal had not occurred in isolation. Vlad's emotional abandonment and neglect of his wives had contributed to the collapse of their relationships.
Saul therefore held genuine grievances toward his father.
He did not express them.
After seeing Vlad execute his eldest son and several wives without hesitation, Saul feared that openly criticizing the King could simply result in Vlad killing him as well.[4]
He buried his anger and remained within the Royal Family.
The reaction provides one of the clearest insights into Saul's relationship with Vlad: he could recognize his father's mistakes but lacked either the willingness or courage to openly oppose someone so overwhelmingly above him.

#### Demonic War
Saul remained inside [[Nightingale]] during the large-scale invasion organized by [[Diablo]]'s forces.
When the attack reached the Vampire Royal Capital, high-ranking Demons attempted to attack Vlad's family directly and use his daughters as political hostages.
[[Bael]], the First Duke of Hell, penetrated the Royal Castle while Vlad and several of Nightingale's most powerful combatants were occupied elsewhere.[5]
The defense was considerably stronger than Bael initially expected.
Six Royal Guards entered transformations comparable to [[Vampire Counts]], while Vlad's older children also fought against him.[5]
By the time [[Jeanne Elderblood]] and [[Morgana Elderblood]] reached the battlefield, Bael had suffered severe damage:
- his right hand was missing;
- his right leg was missing;
- his left eye had been destroyed.[5]
The defenders had nevertheless lost.
The six transformed Royal Guards were dead, while:
- [[Adam Tepes]];
- Saul Tepes;
- [[Lucas Tepes]];
- and [[Elizabeth Tepes]]
were lying defeated on the ground.[5]
[[Lilith Tepes]] had been captured directly by Bael.
Bael acknowledged that Vlad's elder children were strong, but their strength remained insufficient to defeat him.[5]
Saul's precise contribution to Bael's injuries is not identified.
Because Bael fought the Royal Guards and several Royal children together, the loss of his hand, leg and eye should be treated as a collective feat of the defenders rather than individually attributed to Saul.
Saul survived the battle and remained a living member of the [[Tepes Clan]] afterward.

### Relationships

#### Vlad Dracula Tepes
[[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] is Saul's father, King and the central authority governing nearly every aspect of his early life.
Vlad regarded Saul as neither his greatest disappointment nor his most promising son.
His opinion was more specific: Saul occasionally noticed important details but lacked the discipline and intelligence to properly act upon them.[2]
The assassination incident involving Elizabeth became Vlad's clearest example.
Saul had already discovered the danger, meaning perception was not the issue. His unwillingness to act transformed a manageable problem into one that harmed his younger sister.[2]
Saul's feelings toward Vlad became considerably more complicated after the execution of his mother.
He privately blamed Vlad's neglect for helping create the circumstances of her betrayal and believed killing her was excessive.[4]
However, Saul also feared Vlad enough that he never voiced those beliefs.

#### Unnamed Third Queen
Saul's mother was Vlad's unnamed third wife and one of the original Queens of [[Nightingale]].
After years of Vlad's emotional neglect, she entered into an affair with [[Eros]], a God belonging to the [[Greek Pantheon]].[3]
When Vlad learned of the betrayal during the collapse of his household, he killed her.
Saul was devastated.
Unlike Lucas, whose grief was split strongly between his mother and Theo, Saul's strongest emotional reaction centered upon the loss of his mother.[4]
He did not excuse her betrayal, but he considered execution an unjust punishment and believed Vlad bore responsibility for the circumstances that had pushed his wives away.[4]

#### Lucas Tepes
[[Lucas Tepes]] is Saul's older half-brother and the Second Prince of [[Nightingale]].
Both survived the purge that killed Theo and their respective mothers.
Alexios informed them of their losses together, and both became deeply affected by the event.[4]
They later fought beside one another during [[Bael]]'s invasion of the Royal Castle and were defeated together while defending their family.[5]

#### Theo Tepes
[[Theo Tepes]] was Saul's eldest half-brother and the former First Prince.
Saul was saddened by Theo's death, although Chapter 335 explicitly indicates that the death of his own mother affected him more strongly.[4]
Unlike Lucas, Saul is not directly stated to have participated in Theo's conspiracy against Vlad.

#### Adam Tepes
[[Adam Tepes]] is Saul's younger half-brother.
The two were present together during Vlad's lesson following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s first audience.
Vlad's assessment of Adam differed sharply from Saul's: while Saul was reprimanded for laziness and poor judgment, Adam received comparatively straightforward encouragement to continue training.[2]
Saul and Adam later fought together against [[Bael]]'s invasion and were found defeated near one another inside the Royal Castle.[5]

#### Elizabeth Tepes
[[Elizabeth Tepes]] is Saul's younger half-sister.
An incident from several years before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s arrival directly connected them.
Saul realized that a maid intended to assassinate him but ignored the danger out of laziness. His failure to act ultimately resulted in Elizabeth being injured.[2]
Vlad specifically invoked Elizabeth's injury when reprimanding Saul, forcing him to confront the consequences of his negligence.
The siblings later fought on the same side during [[Bael]]'s attack and were defeated together.[5]

### Skills and Abilities
Saul is a thousand-year-old Noble Vampire and a direct biological son of the Vampire Progenitor. His complete individual combat style has not been demonstrated, but his Royal lineage, age and participation in the fight against [[Bael]] establish him as considerably more powerful than ordinary Humans and young Vampires.
- Noble Vampire Physiology: Saul was born directly from [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] and possesses the supernatural constitution of a Royal Noble Vampire.[1]
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Saul possesses supernatural strength, speed, durability, reflexes and stamina far beyond Humans and lower supernatural beings. He was capable of participating alongside other Royal Vampires and Count-form Royal Guards against Bael.[5]
- Superhuman Durability: Saul survived being defeated by Bael, one of the strongest Dukes of Hell, and remained alive after the Demonic invasion.[5]
- Regeneration: As a Noble Vampire, Saul possesses the Race's regenerative physiology. His survival after Bael's assault demonstrates that the injuries inflicted upon him were not permanently fatal.[5]
- Enhanced Senses: Saul possesses the supernatural senses inherent to Noble Vampires.
- Longevity: Saul had already lived approximately one thousand years by Chapter 119 while retaining a young adult appearance.[1]
- Supernatural Beauty: As a Noble Vampire and direct child of Vlad, Saul possesses the enhanced appearance characteristic of the Race.
- Vampiric Maturation: Vampires naturally experience increases in power and potential approximately every five hundred years. Saul had already lived through multiple such maturation periods by his debut.[1]
- Combat Ability: Saul possessed enough combat capability to take part in the Royal Castle's defense against Bael rather than being immediately evacuated as a noncombatant.[5]
- Royal Vampire Experience: Having lived for approximately a millennium inside the center of [[Nightingale]]'s supernatural society, Saul possesses extensive experience with Vampire politics, Royal protocol and supernatural threats.
- Perception: Vlad acknowledged that Saul could occasionally be perceptive. Saul correctly realized that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s first audience had been intended as some form of demonstration before Vlad formally explained it.[2]
- Collective High-Level Combat Feat: Saul, Lucas, Adam, Elizabeth and six transformed Royal Guards collectively fought Bael strongly enough that the Duke lost his right hand, right leg and left eye before defeating them.[5] The exact portion of this damage caused by Saul is unknown and should not be assigned to him individually.

### Power
Saul's exact combat ranking among Vlad's sons is never directly stated.
The novel explicitly establishes:
- [[Lucas Tepes]] is substantially stronger than [[Theo Tepes]] because Lucas continually trained;[2]
- [[Adam Tepes]] was considered to possess the best potential to become King at the time of Vlad's evaluation;[2]
- Saul's primary criticism concerned his mentality and laziness rather than an explicit statement that he was physically weak.[2]
Saul is therefore clearly an experienced ancient Noble Vampire, but there is insufficient evidence to place him precisely above or below Theo, Adam or other named Royal Vampires solely through inference.
His strongest confirmed combat showing is his participation against [[Bael]].[5]
Bael explicitly acknowledged that Vlad's older children were strong, but they remained unable to defeat a top-ranking Duke of Hell even with support from the Royal Guard.[5]
