### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 119
Lucas Tepes is the second son of [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]], the Second Prince of [[Nightingale]], and an ancient Noble Vampire of the [[Tepes Clan]]. At more than two thousand years old, Lucas is one of Vlad's eldest surviving children and was explicitly considered considerably stronger than his older half-brother, [[Theo Tepes]], due to maintaining his training throughout his long life.[1][2]
Unlike Theo, whose strengths lay primarily in intelligence, manipulation and conspiracy, Lucas developed himself as a fighter. Vlad directly praised him for never neglecting his training and described him as much stronger than his elder brother. However, Vlad also considered Lucas too dependent upon Theo and insufficiently independent-minded to be a worthy successor to the throne.[2]
Lucas nevertheless possessed greater practical judgment than Theo in several situations. When Theo wanted to use their sons to provoke the newly appointed Count Alucard, Lucas immediately recognized the suicidal nature of the idea, correctly predicting that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was exactly the type of person who would kill their sons regardless of their Royal heritage.[3]
Despite attempting to restrain Theo's more reckless decisions, Lucas ultimately became involved in his brother's conspiracy to overthrow Vlad. After Vlad killed Theo and Lucas' mother, Lucas became terrified that his own involvement would be discovered and completely abandoned activities connected to the rebellion.[4]
Lucas survived the destruction of much of Vlad's original household and later remained one of the senior surviving Princes of the Tepes Clan. During [[Bael]]'s attack on the Royal Castle, he acted as the eldest surviving prince present, ordered his siblings toward the shelters and subsequently participated in the defense of the castle before being defeated alongside Adam, Saul and Elizabeth.[5][6]

### Physical Appearance
The novel has provided relatively little detailed description of Lucas' individual appearance. He possesses the naturally pale complexion associated with Noble Vampires and retains a youthful adult body despite being more than two thousand years old.[1]
Like his siblings, Lucas' eyes can glow blood-red when his emotions or Vampiric instincts intensify. During an argument with Theo at the Royal Castle, Lucas' eyes visibly shone red when his elder brother attempted to order him around.[1]
His posture and behavior are repeatedly associated with that of a warrior. Vlad himself described Lucas as possessing the bearing of a strong fighter when he was composed, although that confidence could quickly disappear whenever he was placed under severe emotional pressure by his father.[7]

### Personality
Lucas is proud, disciplined, cautious and considerably more combat-oriented than [[Theo Tepes]]. Unlike his elder brother, he consistently trained throughout his life and consequently became the stronger of the two.[2]
He is also more pragmatic.
When Theo proposed sending their sons against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Lucas immediately rejected the idea and questioned whether Theo had stopped thinking rationally. He had personally witnessed Victor attack the Vampire King inside Vlad's own castle and correctly concluded that someone willing to challenge Vlad directly would have no hesitation about killing two spoiled Royal grandchildren.[3]
Lucas also possessed a more realistic understanding of their father than Theo. He respected Vlad immensely and understood that the King rarely spoke without purpose. After Vlad criticized his children, Lucas attempted to convince Theo that the reprimand should be taken seriously rather than treated only as humiliation.[3]
His respect for Vlad was mixed with considerable fear.
Lucas admitted that despite living for thousands of years, he had never become accustomed to his father's overwhelming presence. He found it disturbing that Vlad appeared emotionally indifferent while simultaneously knowing far more about his children's actions than they expected.[3]
This combination of fear and admiration contributed to Lucas' central weakness: dependency.
Vlad considered Lucas powerful but mentally reliant upon Theo. Lucas frequently followed his elder brother's plans despite recognizing their flaws and struggled to establish an independent path of his own.[2]
The problem became especially visible after Lucas' son was killed.
Rather than personally confronting the situation, Lucas accompanied Theo to ask Vlad to intervene. Vlad was disgusted that his sons—three thousand and two thousand years old respectively—still immediately sought their father's help when confronted with a problem outside their comfort zone.[7]
Lucas could therefore behave like a composed warrior one moment and regress into the behavior of an insecure child the next when Vlad became involved.
Despite this weakness, Lucas genuinely cared about Theo.
The two frequently argued and had what was described as a love-hate relationship, but Theo had been part of Lucas' life for thousands of years. Theo's sudden death devastated Lucas far more severely than it did several of their other siblings.[4]
Lucas' fear after Theo's execution was also practical. He knew he had participated in Theo's plan to overthrow Vlad and believed his father might eventually decide to kill him as well. Consequently, Lucas isolated himself and stopped participating in conspiratorial activities connected to Vlad.[4]
Later appearances show a more responsible side of him. During the Demonic attack upon the Royal Capital, Lucas reacted to the escalating danger by ordering his younger siblings to evacuate toward the shelters, naturally assuming responsibility as the eldest surviving prince present.[5]

### Synopsis

#### Vampire Society
Lucas was formally introduced when [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] summoned all seven of his children to the Royal Castle for his meeting with [[Victor Elderblood]]. He was identified as the Second Prince, approximately two thousand years old, placing him between Theo and Saul in age.[1]
Lucas immediately demonstrated his contentious relationship with Theo.
When Theo ordered him to remain quiet, Lucas clicked his tongue and told his elder brother not to command him, his eyes glowing blood-red in irritation. [[Adam Tepes]] intervened before the argument could escalate, reminding them that they were standing directly before their father.[1]
After Victor's audience with Vlad, the King gathered his children and explained the actual purpose behind allowing them to witness the confrontation.
Victor, despite being enormously weaker than Vlad, had willingly attacked the Vampire King rather than allowing fear to completely control him. Vlad wanted his children to understand the mentality necessary to confront superior enemies.[2]
His evaluation of Lucas was notably more positive than Theo's.
Vlad directly stated:
Lucas was much stronger than Theo.
[2]
Unlike his elder brother, Lucas had never stopped training and was not considered a complete coward. However, Vlad immediately followed the praise with criticism.
Lucas needed to:
- think more independently;
- stop depending upon Theo;
- and finally behave according to his actual age.[2]
Lucas accepted the criticism with comparatively little protest.
Afterward he found Theo furiously destroying his office because of Vlad's words. Rather than encouraging Theo's anger, Lucas attempted to make him understand their father's perspective.[2][3]
Lucas reminded Theo that Vlad had reprimanded them seriously only twice throughout their lives.
The first occasion had occurred centuries earlier when the brothers had gone hunting [[Werewolves]] out of arrogance and nearly provoked an entire war, almost dying in the process.[3]
The second was Victor's appointment as Count.
Lucas concluded that Vlad's rare criticism should therefore be taken seriously.
He also acknowledged something that frightened him: even when Vlad appeared uninterested in his children, he seemed to know almost everything they were doing.[3]
Theo eventually revealed that he wanted to recall both of their sons and use them to create problems for Victor.
Lucas rejected the idea almost immediately.
He reminded Theo that Victor had:
- attacked Vlad inside the King's own castle;
- openly challenged Royal authority;
- created a Clan name derived from Vlad's own name written backward;
- and behaved without regard for normal political restraint.[3]
Lucas correctly predicted that Victor would have no hesitation about killing their sons and personally presenting their heads to Vlad if they provoked him sufficiently.[3]
Theo attempted to argue that Vlad would never allow anyone to harm his grandchildren.
Lucas knew otherwise.
He bluntly explained that Vlad's protectiveness extended primarily toward his direct children and that their father effectively pretended his grandchildren did not exist.[3]
Lucas therefore advised Theo to stop acting emotionally and think clearly.
Ironically, his prediction eventually became reality.

#### A New Pillar
Lucas and Theo each had a son who became involved in the increasingly dangerous conflict surrounding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
The two younger Vampires were ultimately killed, and their frozen severed heads were personally brought to Vlad's throne room by [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]].[7]
Lucas and Theo arrived shortly afterward.
Upon seeing their sons' heads lying before their father, both men released visible bloody auras in anger and demanded that Vlad retaliate against the Noble responsible.[7]
Vlad instead laughed at them.
From his perspective, the situation demonstrated exactly why his sons remained unfit to inherit anything from him. They were two-thousand- and three-thousand-year-old Vampires whose first response to a personal tragedy was to run to their father and demand that he solve it.[7]
Vlad asked them how old they were.
After hearing their answers, he ordered them to:
Grow up.
[7]
Both immediately lowered their heads under the pressure of his presence.
Vlad internally recognized the contradiction in Lucas' character. At times Lucas possessed the posture of a strong warrior, but under sufficient pressure from his father that composure disappeared and he reverted to behaving like a frightened adolescent.[7]
The narration also placed part of the blame upon Vlad himself.
Despite demanding independence from his sons, Vlad had spoiled them for millennia and had rarely allowed them to genuinely confront consequences without his shadow hanging over them. The result was that even otherwise capable individuals such as Lucas had never completely developed emotional independence.[7]

### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Lucas' life changed dramatically after the collapse of Vlad's original household.
The scandal involving Stella and [[Theo Tepes]] eventually led Vlad to kill Theo together with several of his wives.
Lucas' mother—the King's unnamed second wife—was also executed after Vlad confirmed that she had been having an affair with a Werewolf.[8]
Lucas therefore lost both:
- his mother;
- and the older brother who had been present throughout nearly his entire life.
Alexios was tasked with explaining what had happened to the surviving princes.
Lucas was devastated.[4]
His relationship with Theo had frequently been argumentative, but it was explicitly described as a love-hate relationship. After thousands of years together, the sudden absence of his older brother affected Lucas profoundly.[4]
The emotional shock was made substantially worse by fear.
Lucas had himself been:
plotting together with Theo to overthrow Vlad.
[4]
Seeing that Vlad had killed his firstborn son without displaying obvious remorse forced Lucas to seriously consider that he might be next.
As a result, Lucas withdrew almost completely.
He isolated himself and ceased participating in activities connected with conspiracies against his father because he did not want to become another addition to what he viewed as the ancient Vampire King's collection of consumed victims.[4]
This also clarifies his relationship with [[New Dawn]].
Lucas was unquestionably connected to Theo's anti-Vlad conspiracy, and Theo himself worked with New Dawn. However, the available chapters do not directly establish Lucas as a formal member of New Dawn in the same manner as Theo, Radu, James or Fenir.
His previous wiki listing as:
New Dawn — Presumed
should therefore be treated as an inferred association rather than confirmed membership.

#### Demonic War
When [[Diablo]]'s faction launched its invasion of [[Nightingale]], Lucas remained within the Royal Castle alongside his surviving siblings.
The attack rapidly escalated after several high-ranking Demons entered the Royal Capital.
When [[Morgana Elderblood]] released a massive burst of Demonic power during her confrontation with the invaders, Lucas immediately recognized that the situation was becoming too dangerous for the Royal siblings to remain exposed.[5]
With Theo dead, Lucas was now the eldest surviving prince.
He therefore naturally took charge and said:
Let's evacuate. Things are starting to get dangerous.
[5]
When Adam asked whether they should head toward the shelters, Lucas confirmed the decision and led the evacuation effort.[5]
The attempt did not keep the Royal Family completely out of the conflict.
[[Bael]], the First Duke of Hell and one of Diablo's most powerful subordinates, eventually penetrated the Royal Castle while attempting to capture Vlad's daughters and force the Vampire King into neutrality.[6]
Bael encountered heavy resistance.
Six members of Vlad's Royal Guard fought him using forms comparable to [[Vampire Counts]], while Vlad's children also participated in resisting the invasion. By the time Jeanne and Morgana reached the battlefield, Bael had lost:
- his right hand;
- his right leg;
- and his left eye.
However, the defenders had also been defeated.[6]
Lucas was found lying on the ground alongside:
- [[Adam Tepes]];
- [[Saul Tepes]];
- and [[Elizabeth Tepes]].
Lilith had been captured directly by Bael.[6]
The chapter confirms that Lucas participated in the defense but does not individually identify which injuries he personally inflicted upon Bael. The Demon had fought the Royal Guards and several Royal children simultaneously, meaning those injuries must be treated as a collective defensive feat rather than attributed solely to Lucas.
Bael himself nevertheless acknowledged that Vlad's older children possessed significant strength, although they remained insufficient against someone of his level.[6]
Lucas survived the invasion.
He remained a member of the surviving Tepes Royal Family afterward.

### Relationships

#### Vlad Dracula Tepes
[[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] is Lucas' father, King and the person whose approval Lucas sought throughout most of his life.
Lucas greatly respects Vlad.
Unlike Theo, who frequently interpreted Vlad's criticism primarily as humiliation, Lucas attempted to understand why his father had spoken and believed that an ancient Being such as Vlad did not waste words without purpose.[3]
At the same time, Lucas is deeply afraid of him.
Even after thousands of years, Vlad's presence could cause Lucas to immediately retreat or abandon his confidence. Lucas openly admitted that he found it terrifying how much Vlad appeared to know about his children despite acting emotionally detached.[3]
Vlad's evaluation of Lucas was mixed.
He considered Lucas:
- strong;
- disciplined;
- more courageous than Theo;
- and consistent in his training.
But he also saw Lucas as overly dependent upon Theo and insufficiently capable of independent judgment.[2]
Their relationship deteriorated severely after Vlad killed Lucas' mother and Theo.
Lucas knew he had been conspiring with his older brother to remove Vlad from power and became convinced that the King might eventually execute him as well.[4]
This fear caused him to isolate himself and abandon his conspiratorial activities.

#### Theo Tepes
[[Theo Tepes]] was Lucas' older half-brother and the sibling with whom he shared his most developed relationship.
The two constantly argued.
Lucas refused to blindly obey Theo simply because he was the First Prince and frequently criticized his brother when Theo allowed jealousy or anger to override rational thought.[1][3]
At the same time, Lucas repeatedly remained at Theo's side.
Vlad specifically criticized Lucas for depending too heavily upon his elder brother, indicating that Theo exerted substantial influence over his decisions.[2]
Lucas also eventually joined Theo's plan to overthrow Vlad.[4]
Their relationship was therefore appropriately described as:
love-hate.
[4]
Theo could frustrate Lucas immensely, but he had also been part of Lucas' life for thousands of years.
His death devastated Lucas.

#### Unnamed Mother
Lucas' mother was Vlad's second wife and one of the original Queens of [[Nightingale]].
She ultimately became unfaithful to Vlad and entered a relationship with a Werewolf after years of neglect from the Vampire King.[8]
Once the betrayal became public during the collapse of Vlad's household, Vlad killed her.
Lucas was devastated by her death and became even more fearful of his father afterward.[4]
Unlike Theo, Lucas did not openly retaliate after his mother's execution.
His response was withdrawal and self-preservation.

#### Unnamed Son
Lucas had at least one son.
Vlad regarded both Lucas' son and Theo's son as spoiled disappointments who behaved as though being grandchildren of the Vampire King made them untouchable.[2]
When Theo considered sending their children to provoke [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Lucas explicitly objected and predicted that Victor would kill them.[3]
His prediction was eventually fulfilled.
Lucas' son was killed, and his frozen severed head was delivered to Vlad's castle together with the head of Theo's son.[7]
Despite having warned Theo about exactly this possibility, Lucas still reacted emotionally to his son's death and accompanied Theo in demanding that Vlad intervene.
Vlad refused.

#### Saul Tepes
[[Saul Tepes]] is Lucas' younger half-brother.
After Theo's death, Lucas became the eldest surviving Prince, placing him naturally above Saul in the remaining Royal sibling structure.
Both men lost their mothers during Vlad's purge and were informed of the deaths together by Alexios.[4]
Their responses differed.
Lucas' grief focused heavily upon both Theo and his mother, while Saul was primarily devastated by his mother's execution.
The brothers later fought alongside one another during [[Bael]]'s attack upon the Royal Castle.[6]

#### Adam Tepes
[[Adam Tepes]] is Lucas' younger half-brother.
Lucas initially had considerably greater age and combat experience than Adam.
During the Demonic invasion, Lucas took responsibility as the eldest surviving prince and ordered Adam and the others toward the shelters when the battle outside became dangerous.[5]
Both were later defeated together while resisting [[Bael]]'s attack on the Royal Castle.[6]

#### Victor Elderblood
Lucas initially regarded [[Victor Elderblood]] primarily as a dangerous political anomaly.
Unlike Theo, he quickly understood how dangerous provoking Victor would be.
After observing Victor's willingness to physically attack Vlad inside the Royal Castle, Lucas concluded that Victor was completely irrational by normal political standards and would have no hesitation about killing Royal descendants who interfered with him.[3]
Lucas' unnamed son was eventually killed as part of the conflict involving Victor, making the relationship personally hostile.[7]
Despite that hostility, Lucas did not later pursue open revenge against Victor.
The enormous change in supernatural power throughout the following arcs and Lucas' withdrawal after Theo's death effectively ended his earlier involvement in anti-Victor schemes.

### Skills and Abilities
Lucas is an ancient Noble Vampire and one of [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]]' oldest biological children. Unlike Theo, whose development focused upon schemes, Lucas consistently trained his combat abilities for more than two thousand years.
- Noble Vampire Physiology: Lucas is a naturally born Noble Vampire and direct son of the Vampire Progenitor. He possesses the superior racial characteristics of Noble Vampires together with the benefits of millennia of maturation.[1][2]
- Superhuman Strength: Lucas possesses physical strength vastly superior to Humans and younger Vampires. Vlad specifically considered him significantly stronger than Theo due to his consistent training.[2]
- Superhuman Speed: As an ancient Noble Vampire, Lucas possesses greatly enhanced movement, reaction and combat speed.
- Superhuman Durability: Lucas can withstand supernatural combat far beyond Human limits. He survived participating in the defense against [[Bael]], one of the strongest Dukes of Hell, although he was ultimately defeated.[6]
- Regeneration: Lucas possesses the regenerative abilities inherent to Noble Vampires. He survived the severe injuries inflicted during Bael's assault and remained alive afterward.[6]
- Enhanced Senses: Lucas possesses the heightened senses common to Noble Vampires.
- Longevity: Lucas had already lived for approximately two thousand years at his debut while retaining a youthful adult appearance.[1]
- Supernatural Beauty: As a high-ranking Noble Vampire and direct descendant of Vlad, Lucas possesses the enhanced appearance common to his Race.
- Vampiric Charm: Lucas possesses the racial supernatural Charm available to Noble Vampires, although no individual feat has established the maximum strength of his Charm.
- Fog Walking: As a Noble Vampire, Lucas possesses access to the basic Vampiric ability to transform or move through mist. No Lucas-specific high-level application has been demonstrated.
- Bat Transformation: Lucas possesses the basic racial transformation capabilities of Vampires, although he has not demonstrated a specialized individual use.
- Claw Manifestation: Noble Vampires can develop supernatural claws as part of their racial combat abilities.
- Night Vision: Lucas possesses the natural nocturnal perception characteristic of Vampires.
- Elder Vampire Development: At more than two thousand years old, Lucas has experienced several of the natural five-hundred-year maturation cycles through which Vampires increase in physical power and potential.[1] His age alone does not make him comparable to exceptional [[Vampire Counts]], but it places his baseline far above that of young ordinary Nobles.
- Martial Training: Lucas consistently trained throughout his life. Vlad directly contrasted this discipline with Theo's neglect of combat and identified it as the reason Lucas was substantially stronger.[2]
- Combat Experience: Having lived for millennia and participated in violent conflicts, Lucas possesses extensive battle experience.
- Werewolf Combat Experience: Lucas and Theo once hunted [[Werewolves]] together and provoked a conflict severe enough to nearly cause a war. Both brothers almost died during the incident.[3]
- Demonic Combat Experience: Lucas participated in the Royal Castle's defense against Bael during the Demonic invasion.[6]
- Blood Aura: Lucas can manifest a visible bloody aura when his emotions and Vampiric power intensify. He and Theo both produced such an aura after discovering the severed heads of their sons.[7]
- Killing Intent: As an ancient combat-trained Vampire, Lucas can release hostile supernatural pressure through his Vampiric aura.
- Leadership: Following Theo's death, Lucas became the eldest surviving Prince and demonstrated the ability to assume responsibility over his younger siblings during emergencies. He ordered the Royal Family toward the shelters during the Demonic invasion.[5]
- Tactical Judgment: Lucas possesses better immediate threat assessment than Theo. He accurately recognized the danger of provoking [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and understood that Royal status would not protect their sons from someone with Victor's mentality.[3]
- Political Awareness: Lucas is knowledgeable about Vlad's Court, Vampire politics and the conflicts involving the Count Clans. His weakness is not complete ignorance but his historical tendency to allow Theo to make many of the larger political decisions.

### Weaknesses
- Dependence on Theo: Vlad explicitly identified Lucas' reliance upon his elder brother as one of his greatest flaws.[2]
- Limited Independent Ambition: Although stronger than Theo, Lucas rarely acted as the principal architect of their plans.
- Fear of Vlad: Lucas' composure can collapse in the presence of his father. After Theo's execution, fear of sharing the same fate caused him to abandon his conspiratorial activities entirely.[4]
- Emotional Immaturity: Despite his age, Lucas still sought Vlad's intervention when his son was killed rather than independently confronting the consequences.[7]
- Royal Upbringing: Vlad's excessive protection prevented Lucas and Theo from fully developing the independence expected from Vampires thousands of years old.[7]
- Inferior to Higher-Tier Combatants: Lucas was defeated by [[Bael]] despite fighting alongside other members of the Royal Family and Royal Guard.[6]
- No Confirmed Unique Bloodline Ability: Unlike the principal Vampire Count Clans, Lucas has not demonstrated an individualized elemental or hereditary ability beyond his direct Tepes physiology.
- Standard Vampire Weaknesses: Lucas remains subject to the normal limitations of Noble Vampire physiology unless a specific resistance is demonstrated.

### New Dawn Association
Lucas has historically been listed as having a presumed allegiance to [[New Dawn]].
The novel directly confirms that Lucas:
- conspired with Theo to overthrow Vlad;[4]
- was closely connected to Theo's political activities;
- and later feared execution because of his participation in that conspiracy.[4]
However, it does not directly establish that Lucas was formally inducted into New Dawn or possessed the same organizational status as:
- [[Theo Tepes]];
- Radu Tepes;
- James;
- or Fenir.
For this reason, Lucas should not currently be classified as a confirmed New Dawn member without a direct chapter statement.
His association is more accurately described as:
an ally and co-conspirator of Theo who became indirectly connected with the broader anti-Vlad conspiracy.
