### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 119
Lilith Tepes is the eldest daughter of [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] and [[Morgana Elderblood]], the blood-sister of [[Elizabeth Tepes]], and the First Princess of [[Nightingale]]. She is a full-blooded Noble Vampire descended directly from the Vampire Progenitor, but unlike most of Vlad's children she also inherited a surviving characteristic from her mother's original Demonic nature: the ability to generate and manipulate Miasma, the characteristic Energy of Demons.[1]
Although she inherited relatively little of Morgana's original Demonic Race compared with her overwhelmingly dominant Tepes bloodline, Lilith's Miasma affinity makes her one of Vlad's more unusual children. During the events of the Demonic War, she was captured by [[Diablo]]'s faction and held alongside [[Emily Moriarty]] and [[Thomas Fenrir]] before being personally rescued by [[Victor Elderblood]].[2][3]

### Physical Appearance
Lilith is an exceptionally beautiful Noble Vampire with very long black hair described as resembling pure darkness and flowing all the way toward the ground. She possesses violet-purple eyes, pale skin and a highly feminine figure whose appearance visibly reflects the supernatural heritage of her mother, despite Lilith herself having been born as a full Vampire.[4]
She strongly resembles her younger blood-sister [[Elizabeth Tepes]], with the two being described as nearly identical enough for their shared parentage to be immediately obvious. Lilith's appearance, however, carries somewhat stronger traces of Morgana's former Succubus heritage, giving her a particularly alluring and mature presence even among Noble Vampires.[4]
During her imprisonment by [[Diablo]]'s forces, prolonged confinement and isolation left Lilith disheveled and visibly furious. When she was returned to the common prison cell after being kept separately for two days, her eyes had turned blood-red from anger and bloodlust, and her expression carried open killing intent toward her captors.[2]

### Personality
Lilith is generally calmer and more composed than [[Elizabeth Tepes]], often serving as the more rational sister when Elizabeth becomes emotional. When [[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]] disappeared for an entire week, Elizabeth became increasingly frantic while Lilith attempted to calm her and encouraged her to think before acting.[1]
Despite this calm exterior, Lilith is deeply attached to her family. She was genuinely worried about Ophis' disappearance, although she possessed greater confidence than Elizabeth that Vlad would eventually find their younger sister. Her confidence came from understanding that Ophis was the child toward whom Vlad displayed the most obvious affection, something that occasionally caused Lilith herself to feel petty jealousy.[1]
Lilith is also emotionally attached to her mother. Following Morgana's awakening and separation from Vlad, Lilith openly admitted to herself that she had missed her badly and wanted to follow her. She remembered Morgana as an affectionate mother who, despite her violent Demonic personality, had always been present when Lilith and Elizabeth needed her.[1]
One of Lilith's strongest motivations during this period was learning how to properly control the Miasma she had inherited from Morgana. Because Demonic Energy is dangerous to non-Demons, Lilith feared accidentally harming herself or members of her family. Morgana had repeatedly refused to teach her, insisting that Lilith was not yet ready.[1]
Lilith is also sociable when placed outside the rigid atmosphere of the Tepes Royal Family. [[Emily Moriarty]], after spending time imprisoned beside her, reported that Lilith was surprisingly easy to get along with.[2]
She can also be sarcastic and possesses a dry sense of humor. During Elizabeth's conversation with [[Violet Elderblood Snow]], Lilith was stunned when Violet behaved responsibly enough to help locate Ophis and openly wondered whether the world's end was approaching.[1]
Lilith developed a visible attraction toward [[Victor Elderblood]] before he eventually became her stepfather through Morgana. This attraction became particularly obvious when Victor personally rescued her from [[Diablo]]'s prison; despite the danger surrounding them, Lilith momentarily became distracted by how much more attractive Victor had become since they had last met.[3]

### Synopsis

#### Vampire Society
Lilith was formally introduced as one of the seven children of [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] when [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] visited the Vampire King's castle. She was identified as Elizabeth's older sister and the eldest Royal Princess, possessing the same mother and a visibly similar appearance.[4]
Unlike Elizabeth, who was visibly nervous over Victor's arrival due to their earlier encounter, Lilith initially appeared bored with the proceedings and considered returning to sleep before Victor was announced. She remained present with the Royal Family as Victor entered Vlad's throne room and the confrontation between the two powerful Vampires began.[4]
Lilith later appeared alongside Elizabeth and the other members of the Tepes Royal Family during the public game between Natashia Fulger and [[Niklaus Horseman]]. Their exceptional appearances drew considerable attention from younger Vampires in the audience, with observers understanding why Vlad rarely allowed his daughters to freely move throughout [[Nightingale]] without protection.[5]
Lilith and Elizabeth also became increasingly irritated by Ophis' attachment to Victor. Ophis openly regarded Victor as a paternal figure, repeatedly calling him "Father" and seeking his blood, creating an unusual situation in which the youngest daughter of the Vampire King behaved as though another Vampire were her parent.[6][7]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
The collapse of Vlad's household changed Lilith's family considerably. [[Theo Tepes]] was killed, several of Vlad's wives died, and both [[Jeanne Elderblood]] and Morgana permanently separated themselves from the Vampire King. Vlad subsequently became even more emotionally distant and ordered his remaining children not to leave the Royal Capital.[1]
Lilith was particularly affected by Morgana's awakening. Unlike Elizabeth, whose immediate concern remained Ophis' disappearance, Lilith was excited that her mother had finally recovered and desperately wanted to reunite with her. She privately reflected that despite Morgana's Demonic mentality and often extreme behavior, she had always been kind toward both of her daughters.[1]
This period also revealed the unusual nature of Lilith's inheritance.
Morgana had originally been a high-level Succubus and Demon before Vlad converted her into a Vampire. Under ordinary circumstances, the child of such a powerful Demonic lineage might have inherited considerable Demonic characteristics. Vlad's Progenitor Bloodline was so dominant, however, that it effectively overwhelmed Morgana's Race and caused Lilith to be born as a complete Vampire.[1]
One characteristic survived:
Miasma.
Lilith possessed the natural ability to generate and manipulate the Energy used by Demons.[1]
Because Miasma is harmful to most non-Demonic beings, Lilith had avoided freely experimenting with the power. She feared losing control and harming her relatives or herself, repeatedly asking Morgana to teach her proper control. Her mother refused because she believed Lilith was not yet ready.[1]
At the same time, Ophis disappeared from [[Nightingale]]. Lilith attempted to calm the increasingly desperate Elizabeth, reminding her that Vlad cared deeply for Ophis and had already mobilized his forces. Although Lilith was worried herself, she possessed enough confidence in her father's concern for their youngest sister to remain relatively composed.[1]

#### Demonic War
During [[Diablo]]'s campaign against the major supernatural factions, Lilith was captured and used as one of the hostages connected to the Vampire Kingdom. Her abduction became particularly personal for [[Morgana Elderblood]], whose distress over her daughter directly influenced [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s decision to intervene.[8]
Victor explicitly told [[Alexios Alioth]] that Diablo's faction had taken:
Morgana's daughter
and that from the moment Morgana had cried helplessly against him, Victor had already decided what he would do: he would retrieve Lilith and return her to where he believed she belonged—with her mother rather than Vlad.[8]
Lilith was held by high-ranking Demons. At one point she was isolated for approximately two days before being returned to a cell containing [[Emily Moriarty]] and [[Thomas Fenrir]]. Her captors had apparently been forbidden from physically torturing her by Diablo himself, although the Demons guarding her openly desired to do so.[2]
Lilith remained openly hostile toward her captors and returned to the cell with intense killing intent. Despite the circumstances, she quickly interacted normally with Emily and Thomas. Emily later reported to her mother that Lilith was unexpectedly easy to befriend, in contrast to the much more cautious Thomas.[2]
Victor eventually located the prison and violently breached the chamber holding the three captives. Lilith was shocked to see him and briefly became distracted by his dramatically increased appearance and presence before remembering the danger surrounding them.[3]
Victor killed the high-ranking Demon guards before they could react and ordered the [[Werewolves]] accompanying him to evacuate Thomas. When [[Agares]], the Second Key of Solomon, attempted to attack Lilith, Victor used Fulger Lightning to remove her from the Demon Duke's reach before entrusting her to [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]] and the Maids within his shadow.[3]
Lilith remained protected inside Victor's shadow while he confronted Agares. Victor eventually escaped with the remaining captives after using [[Magic]] to blind the Demon Duke and prevent him from stopping the rescue.[3]
The event substantially strengthened Lilith's attraction toward Victor. She had already found him interesting, but being personally rescued from Diablo's forces while witnessing the overwhelming difference between Victor and her captors caused her feelings to intensify.
Victor later became her stepfather after Morgana entered a relationship with him.

### Relationships

#### Morgana Elderblood
[[Morgana Elderblood]] is Lilith's biological mother and the parent with whom she possesses the strongest explicitly demonstrated emotional attachment.
Despite Morgana's history as a ruthless Demon General, Lilith remembers her as a genuinely caring mother who consistently supported both her and Elizabeth.[1]
Lilith inherited her Miasma affinity directly from Morgana's original Demonic nature and repeatedly wanted her mother to teach her how to safely control it. Morgana refused during Lilith's earlier life because she considered her daughter unprepared for handling Demonic Energy.[1]
After Morgana awakened from her coma and separated from Vlad, Lilith desperately wanted to follow her but was prevented from leaving by Vlad's order.[1]
Her later capture became one of the factors that pushed [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to personally attack [[Diablo]]'s forces, as he refused to ignore Morgana's distress over her missing daughter.[8]

#### Elizabeth Tepes
[[Elizabeth Tepes]] is Lilith's younger blood-sister and the only other daughter born from the union of Vlad and Morgana.
The sisters possess a close relationship and are frequently shown together. Lilith tends to be the calmer of the two, advising Elizabeth to slow down and think when her emotions become overwhelming.[1]
Elizabeth likewise speaks openly around Lilith about the Royal Family's problems, indicating considerable trust between them.

#### Ophis Tepes Elderblood
[[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]] is Lilith's younger half-sister.
Lilith cares deeply for Ophis and became worried when she disappeared from [[Nightingale]]. She nevertheless displayed considerably greater confidence than Elizabeth that Vlad would successfully recover her.[1]
Lilith is also aware that Vlad treats Ophis differently from his other children. The obvious affection he displays toward his youngest daughter occasionally provokes jealousy in Lilith, although she recognizes the emotion as petty and attempts to suppress it.[1]
Ophis' habit of calling [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] "Father" and seeking his blood also caused irritation among her older sisters.[6][7]

#### Vlad Dracula Tepes
[[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] is Lilith's biological father and the source of the overwhelmingly dominant Vampiric Bloodline that determined her Race.[1]
Lilith understands that Vlad cares for his children in his own emotionally distant manner. Unlike Elizabeth, who became uncertain whether to think of Vlad primarily as her father or King, Lilith retained enough trust in him to believe that his concern for Ophis would guarantee a serious search effort.[1]
She nevertheless notices the difference in how Vlad treats Ophis and occasionally feels jealous of the open affection their father displays toward his youngest daughter.[1]

#### Victor Elderblood
Lilith first encountered [[Victor Elderblood]] as one of the Vampires visiting Vlad's castle and later became increasingly interested in him.
Her attraction became unmistakable during the Demonic War when Victor personally rescued her from [[Diablo]]'s prison. Even while surrounded by enemies, Lilith briefly lost focus because of Victor's appearance and overwhelmingly dominant presence.[3]
Victor's motivation for saving her was heavily connected to Morgana. After seeing Lilith's mother emotionally devastated by her capture, Victor decided that he would personally bring Lilith back.[8]
Morgana's later relationship with Victor made him Lilith's stepfather.

### Skills and Abilities
Lilith has not received enough direct combat scenes for the full limits of her personal strength to be determined. Her confirmed abilities primarily derive from her position as a direct daughter of the Vampire Progenitor and the surviving Demonic characteristics inherited from Morgana.
- Noble Vampire Physiology: Lilith is a full-blooded Noble Vampire and direct daughter of [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]], the Vampire Progenitor. Despite Morgana's powerful Demonic heritage, Vlad's Vampiric Bloodline overwhelmingly dominated Lilith's racial development and caused her to be born completely as a Vampire.[1]
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: As a Noble Vampire of the Royal Bloodline, Lilith possesses supernatural strength, speed, reflexes, stamina and durability far above ordinary Humans and lower-ranked Vampires. Her precise personal combat ceiling has not been demonstrated.
- Regeneration: Lilith possesses the regenerative characteristics inherent to Noble Vampire physiology.
- Enhanced Senses: Noble Vampires possess senses greatly surpassing Humans.
- Longevity: Lilith has lived for more than a millennium while retaining the physical appearance of a young adult.
- Supernatural Beauty: Lilith possesses the extreme supernatural attractiveness associated with Noble Vampires. Her appearance is additionally influenced by the small amount of Succubus heritage she inherited from Morgana.[4][3]
- Vampiric Charm: As a Noble Vampire, Lilith possesses the racial supernatural charm inherent to high-ranking Vampires. She has not received an individual feat establishing the maximum effectiveness of her Charm.
- Progenitor Bloodline: Lilith is a direct biological descendant of the Vampire Progenitor rather than a Vampire belonging to one of the later Noble Bloodlines. Vlad's Bloodline was sufficiently dominant to almost completely suppress Morgana's original Demonic lineage during Lilith's birth.[1]
- Demonic Heritage: Although Lilith was born as a complete Vampire, she retained a small but genuine portion of the supernatural characteristics originating from Morgana's existence as a high-level Succubus and Demon.[1]
- Miasma Manipulation: Lilith's principal confirmed Demonic inheritance is the ability to use Miasma, the characteristic Energy of Demons.[1]
- Miasma Generation: Lilith can naturally produce or access Miasma despite not belonging to the Demon Race.[1]
- Demonic Energy Affinity: Unlike ordinary Vampires, Lilith possesses natural compatibility with Demonic Energy inherited through Morgana. During her earlier life she had not yet properly learned to control this Energy and actively wanted Morgana to train her.[1]
- Limited Miasma Control (Early): Lilith deliberately avoided freely using Miasma because she understood how dangerous it was to non-Demonic beings and feared accidentally injuring herself or members of her family. Morgana repeatedly told her that she was not yet ready for serious training.[1]
- Bloodlust / Killing Intent: During her captivity, Lilith's anger became intense enough for her eyes to turn blood-red and for her captors to clearly perceive her murderous intent.[2]
- Mental Fortitude: Lilith remained psychologically functional despite being abducted, isolated for two days and held by hostile high-level Demons. After being returned to the shared cell she quickly regained her composure and was able to interact normally with Emily and Thomas.[2]

### Potential
Lilith's supernatural composition is unusual because she combines the direct Bloodline of the Vampire Progenitor with a surviving fragment of one of the most powerful Demonic lineages associated with Morgana.
However, the novel has only explicitly confirmed:
- complete Noble Vampire physiology;
- direct Progenitor descent;
- partial Succubus/Demonic inheritance;
- and Miasma manipulation.[1]
She should not automatically be assigned:
- Morgana's Dream Manipulation;
- Succubus-specific mind manipulation;
- Demonic transformation;
- Dream-to-Reality creation;
- Radiation powers;
- or Morgana's later Draconic abilities
without direct evidence.
Her ability to use Miasma establishes that Vlad's Bloodline did not erase every characteristic inherited from Morgana, but it does not prove that Lilith inherited her mother's complete Demonic power set.

### Weaknesses
- Lilith possesses the ordinary weaknesses associated with Vampires, although her status as a direct daughter of Vlad gives her a significantly stronger constitution than ordinary members of the Race.
- Her early Miasma control was undeveloped. She actively feared injuring herself or her family if she used Demonic Energy incorrectly.[1]
- Miasma is naturally dangerous to non-Demonic life, making uncontrolled use hazardous around allies.[1]
- Lilith has not demonstrated Morgana's full natural compatibility with Demonic Energy and originally required instruction to safely develop the power.[1]
- Despite her Royal Bloodline, Lilith was successfully captured by [[Diablo]]'s forces and required rescue during the Demonic War.[2][3]

### Trivia
- Lilith is the First Princess of [[Nightingale]], while [[Elizabeth Tepes]] is the Second Princess.
- Lilith and Elizabeth are the only known children of Vlad who share [[Morgana Elderblood]] as their biological mother.
- Despite Morgana having originally been a powerful Succubus and Demon, Lilith was born as a complete Vampire because Vlad's Progenitor Bloodline overwhelmed the majority of her mother's Demonic inheritance.[1]
- Miasma is the primary confirmed characteristic Lilith inherited from Morgana's Demonic existence.[1]
- Lilith wanted Morgana to personally teach her Miasma control, but Morgana repeatedly told her that she was not ready.[1]
- She deeply missed Morgana while her mother was in a coma and wanted to follow her after she awakened and left Vlad.[1]
- Lilith occasionally felt jealous because Vlad displayed considerably more visible affection toward Ophis than toward his other children.[1]
- [[Emily Moriarty]] considered Lilith surprisingly easy to get along with despite her status as a Vampire Princess.[2]
- Lilith was isolated from the other hostages for approximately two days during her captivity.[2]
- [[Diablo]]'s forces were ordered not to physically abuse Lilith during her imprisonment, although her Demonic guards openly wished to do so.[2]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] personally breached the prison containing Lilith, Thomas and Emily and killed two high-level Demon guards before they could react.[3]
- Victor removed Lilith from [[Agares]]' attack using Fulger Lightning before sending her into Kaguya's shadow for protection.[3]
- Lilith already possessed an attraction toward Victor, and her reaction during the rescue makes clear that his later appearance and actions only intensified it.[3]
- She is known to enjoy collecting expensive cars and other luxury items.
- Her name should not be confused with [[Lilith Elderblood]], the Demon Progenitor and former master/creator of Morgana.
