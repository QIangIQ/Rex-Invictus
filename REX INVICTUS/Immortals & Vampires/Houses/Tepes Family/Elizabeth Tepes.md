### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 73
Elizabeth Tepes, commonly referred to as Eliza, is the second daughter of [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] and the younger biological daughter of [[Morgana Elderblood]]. She is a Noble Vampire of the [[Tepes Clan]], the Second Princess of [[Nightingale]], and the younger blood-sister of [[Lilith Tepes]]. Among Vlad's children, Elizabeth has historically been particularly close to her younger half-sister Ophis Tepes Elderblood, frequently taking responsibility for finding and bringing Ophis home whenever the young princess wandered away.[1][2]
Elizabeth's birth is highly unusual even among the children of the Vampire Progenitor. She was not conceived entirely through ordinary reproduction. Morgana used her ability to temporarily turn Dreams into Reality together with her own nature as a Dream Demon and an Essence taken from Vlad to artificially create life. The resulting Elizabeth was described as something resembling a spiritual clone or younger version of Morgana while nevertheless possessing her own independent thoughts, emotions and identity.[3]

### Physical Appearance
Elizabeth is an exceptionally beautiful young woman standing approximately 172 cm tall. She possesses long black hair carrying noticeable violet shades, violet eyes and the pale complexion common among Noble Vampires. Her body is slender and feminine, with a relatively petite frame despite having reached physical adulthood.[1]
Elizabeth normally dresses in dark, comparatively simple clothing that nevertheless retains the elegance expected of a Royal Princess. She has been shown wearing short dark dresses accompanied by a choker decorated with a small black jewel. Her movements, mannerisms and general presentation are consistently described as elegant and aristocratic, to the point that simply walking through the Royal Capital beside her immediately drew the attention of surrounding Vampires.[1][2]
Her physical resemblance to [[Morgana Elderblood]] has a supernatural explanation rather than being the product of inheritance alone. Morgana specifically constructed Elizabeth through her own supernatural power and Essence alongside Vlad's, resulting in Elizabeth being described as resembling a younger version of her mother.[3]

### Personality
Elizabeth is generally elegant, observant and curious, carrying herself with the natural confidence expected from a daughter of the Vampire King. Unlike several members of the Royal Family who openly emphasize hierarchy and status, she can interact relatively casually when sufficiently interested in another person. During her first meeting with [[Victor Elderblood]], she quickly became curious about him after seeing that he neither reacted dramatically to her royal identity nor appeared affected by the strange supernatural condition surrounding Ophis.[1][2]
She is particularly protective of Ophis. Elizabeth frequently acts as the older sister responsible for retrieving her whenever she escapes the Royal Castle and becomes visibly stressed when Ophis disappears. This protectiveness goes significantly beyond obligation. When Ophis vanished from [[Nightingale]] for an entire week, Elizabeth became increasingly frantic, bit her nails while thinking, repeatedly attempted to contact people who could help and stated that she would already have personally left the Capital to search if Vlad had not explicitly forbidden his children from leaving.[4]
Her affection is reciprocated. When Victor directly asked Ophis whether she liked Elizabeth, Ophis immediately answered that her sister was a good person despite initially clinging to Victor rather than approaching her.[1]
Elizabeth can also be impatient and easily irritated. Ophis repeatedly escaping frustrates her, while Victor's influence on the young princess—particularly Ophis learning inappropriate words—quickly provoked Elizabeth's annoyance. Her interactions with [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] likewise demonstrate that she is willing to argue openly with people outside the Royal Family instead of maintaining a permanently restrained princess-like persona.[2][4]
Despite her frustrations, Elizabeth is capable of setting pride aside when her family is threatened. Her concern for Ophis was great enough that she willingly contacted Violet, someone with whom she had a hostile and competitive relationship, simply because she believed Victor and Natalia might be able to locate her missing sister.[4]
Elizabeth's feelings toward Vlad are considerably more complicated. Like many of his children, she desires her father's attention and approval, yet Vlad's emotional distance left her uncertain whether she should primarily think of him as her father or merely as her King. This confusion became particularly obvious after Morgana awakened from her coma and separated from Vlad.[4]
In contrast, Elizabeth remembers her mother considerably more warmly. Despite Morgana's violent history and unstable personality, she was described as kind to both Elizabeth and Lilith and as someone who was actually present when her daughters needed their mother.[4]

### Synopsis

#### Vampire Society
Elizabeth first appeared in the Royal Capital while searching for Ophis Tepes, who had once again wandered away from the Royal Family. She eventually discovered her younger sister accompanying the newly arrived [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Yuki Snow]]. Rather than immediately forcing Ophis to return, Elizabeth observed Victor's interaction with her and became intrigued when Ophis—normally withdrawn and uncomfortable around most people—allowed Victor to freely touch, carry and spoil her.[1]
Victor introduced himself simply as Victor Walker, while Elizabeth formally introduced herself as the King's daughter. Victor's almost complete lack of reaction to her status surprised her; she was accustomed to other Vampires immediately reacting to the authority represented by the Tepes name. Ophis then confirmed that Elizabeth was her sister and, when Victor asked whether she liked her, called Elizabeth a good person.[1]
Elizabeth subsequently accompanied Victor, Ophis and Yuki through the Royal Capital. Although initially motivated by keeping watch over her younger sister, she gradually participated in their casual exploration of the city, explaining aspects of [[Nightingale]]'s ecology and culture to Victor while observing his unusual behavior. The group appeared almost like ordinary friends touring the Capital despite containing two Royal Princesses and one of the fastest-rising Vampires in Nightingale.[2]
When Elizabeth finally attempted to take Ophis home, the younger princess refused, insulted her with a word she had recently learned from Victor and then drank some of Victor's blood before disappearing through her mysterious concealment ability. Elizabeth immediately became frustrated that her sister had escaped yet again.[2]
Concerned that Victor had witnessed one of Ophis' secrets, Elizabeth attempted to use her Vampire Charm and ordered him to keep what he had seen confidential. Victor was completely unaffected, though he voluntarily agreed to preserve the secret. Elizabeth was surprised by both his immunity to her Charm and his apparent immunity to the strange influence surrounding Ophis. She also realized that Victor had deliberately avoided giving her much personal information despite allowing her to accompany him.[2]
The encounter left Elizabeth increasingly curious about Victor and marked one of the earliest interactions between the Tepes Royal Family and the man who would eventually become deeply intertwined with several of its members.

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Much later, the circumstances surrounding Elizabeth's birth were revealed after Morgana, then still known as Anna, awakened from her long coma and left Vlad.[3]
Before becoming one of Vlad's wives, Morgana had served Lilith in Hell and had entered into an agreement requiring her to eventually surrender one of her children to replace the position she had abandoned. When Morgana's first daughter, [[Lilith Tepes]], was born, she discovered that despite her Demonic mentality she could not bring herself to send her firstborn into Hell's endless conflicts.[3]
Morgana therefore attempted to solve the problem through her supernatural control over Dreams. At her full strength, Morgana could temporarily bring Dreams into Reality. Combining this ability with her own Essence and an Essence obtained from Vlad, a Vampire Progenitor, Morgana accomplished something bordering upon the authority of Gods: she created a new living Being.[3]
That Being was Elizabeth.
Elizabeth was not an emotionless artificial construct. Although her existence resembled a spiritual clone of Morgana and physically developed as a younger version of her mother, she possessed her own thoughts and personality. Morgana had initially convinced herself that creating a daughter artificially would prevent her from developing the same attachment she felt toward Lilith and would make surrendering the child to Hell easier. The plan failed because Morgana came to love Elizabeth as her daughter regardless of the circumstances of her creation.[3]
The revelation establishes Elizabeth as one of the earliest successful examples in the series of artificially created sentient life, produced through the combined supernatural characteristics of a powerful Dream Demon and a Vampire Progenitor rather than through normal conception alone.[3]
Shortly afterward, the Tepes household entered another crisis when Ophis disappeared from [[Nightingale]]. Elizabeth had initially believed Ophis had simply gone to visit [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], something Vlad had allowed in the past, but contacting the Scarlett residence revealed that Ophis was not there.[4]
Elizabeth became increasingly distressed as an entire week passed without news. Vlad had forbidden his children from leaving the Capital while his forces searched, preventing Elizabeth from personally looking for her sister. Her anxiety became severe enough that she eventually contacted Violet Snow and demanded Victor's contact information, believing that Natalia's abilities could assist in locating Ophis.[4]
Violet refused to give Elizabeth Victor's personal number but agreed to inform him herself. Elizabeth accepted the arrangement because she understood how strongly Victor cared for Ophis and trusted that he would act immediately once he learned the young princess was missing.[4]
The same period exposed the increasingly fractured relationship between Elizabeth and Vlad. Following Morgana's awakening and separation from the King, Elizabeth found herself unable to determine whether she truly regarded Vlad as her father or merely the ruler she was expected to obey. Although she still desired his recognition like his other children, his inability to properly express affection had left her emotionally confused.[4]
By contrast, Elizabeth and Lilith retained strong affection for Morgana. Lilith remembered that despite their mother's dangerous personality, Morgana had consistently treated both daughters kindly and had been present whenever they needed her.[4]

### Origin
Elizabeth possesses one of the most unusual origins among the children of [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]].
Morgana, originally a high-ranking Succubus and Dream Demon, possessed the ability to temporarily transform Dreams into Reality. To avoid surrendering her firstborn daughter Lilith to Lilith, Morgana attempted to artificially create another child who could fulfill the terms of her old agreement.[3]
The process utilized:
- Morgana's supernatural Dream ability;
- Morgana's own Essence;
- Vlad's Essence as a Vampire Progenitor;
- and Morgana's ability to transform an imagined existence into something physically real.[3]
The result was not a conventional illusion or temporary dream.
Elizabeth became a genuine living individual possessing:
- an independent mind;
- personal emotions;
- a stable physical body;
- a Vampire Race;
- and a distinct identity separate from Morgana.[3]
She was nevertheless described as being something similar to a:
spiritual clone of Morgana
because her mother's Essence and Dream-based creation formed an unusually direct part of her existence.[3]
Her creation should not be confused with ordinary cloning. Elizabeth was produced through supernatural life creation involving two immensely powerful supernatural lineages rather than through biological copying alone.

### Relationships

#### Ophis Tepes Elderblood
[[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]] is Elizabeth's younger half-sister and the Royal sibling with whom Elizabeth has received the greatest amount of direct interaction.
Elizabeth frequently acts as the person responsible for finding Ophis after the young princess escapes from home. She understands Ophis' unusually quiet personality, isolated upbringing and special supernatural condition better than outsiders and is immediately suspicious when another person can interact normally with her.[1][2]
Despite frequently becoming frustrated by Ophis' behavior, Elizabeth's concern is genuine. Ophis herself describes Elizabeth as a good person, while Elizabeth's reaction to Ophis' disappearance demonstrates that she is willing to disregard pride and seek help from people she dislikes if doing so might protect her sister.[1][4]

#### Lilith Tepes
[[Lilith Tepes]] is Elizabeth's only full biological sister and the other daughter born from the union of Vlad and Morgana.
The sisters possess a relatively comfortable relationship. Lilith can calm and advise Elizabeth during moments of panic, while Elizabeth openly discusses family problems around her.[4]
Both sisters also share a stronger emotional relationship with Morgana than many of Vlad's children possess with their respective parents.

#### Morgana Elderblood
[[Morgana Elderblood]] is Elizabeth's mother and, in a supernatural sense, forms an unusually direct component of Elizabeth's creation.
Elizabeth was produced through Morgana's Dream power and Essence, causing her to resemble a younger version or spiritual clone of her mother.[3]
Morgana initially intended Elizabeth to become a substitute child that she could surrender to Hell in place of Lilith, believing she would be less emotionally attached to a daughter she had artificially created. She was unable to follow through because Elizabeth became her daughter in every meaningful sense and Morgana developed genuine maternal love for her.[3]
Later recollections indicate that Morgana was consistently present for both Elizabeth and Lilith when they needed her.[4]

#### Vlad Dracula Tepes
[[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] is Elizabeth's biological father and supplied the Progenitor Essence used in her creation.[3]
Elizabeth nevertheless has a complicated relationship with him. Like Vlad's other children, she desires his attention and approval, but his emotional detachment made it difficult for her to separate the man who was supposed to be her father from the King whose commands she was expected to follow.[4]
Despite this uncertainty, Elizabeth obeyed Vlad's prohibition against leaving the Royal Capital during Ophis' disappearance even though she desperately wanted to personally search for her sister.[4]

#### Victor Elderblood
Elizabeth initially met [[Victor Elderblood]] before his rise to the position of Vampire Count and long before Morgana later joined Victor's family.
Their first encounter was defined primarily by Elizabeth's curiosity. Victor showed no significant reaction to her title, freely carried Ophis despite the girl's unusual condition and was completely unaffected when Elizabeth later attempted to use Vampire Charm on him.[1][2]
Elizabeth subsequently came to recognize Victor's deep affection for Ophis. During Ophis' disappearance, she considered contacting him one of the most reliable available methods of finding her younger sister.[4]
Morgana's later relationship with Victor technically made him Elizabeth's stepfather, although the novel has not extensively explored a direct stepfather–stepdaughter relationship between the two.

### Skills and Abilities
- Noble Vampire Physiology: Elizabeth is a full Noble Vampire and direct daughter of the Vampire Progenitor, [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]]. She possesses the fundamental supernatural characteristics of Noble Vampires, including enhanced physical ability, supernatural senses, regeneration, longevity and Vampiric powers. Her creation through Morgana's Dream ability did not prevent Vlad's overwhelmingly dominant Vampire nature from producing a complete Vampire existence.[3]
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: As a member of the Vampire Royal Family, Elizabeth possesses physical abilities far beyond Humans and ordinary Plebian Vampires. The exact upper limit of her combat capability has not been directly demonstrated.
- Enhanced Senses: Elizabeth possesses the heightened sensory abilities characteristic of Noble Vampires. During her walk through the Capital with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], the Vampires present could hear quiet remarks that would normally escape Human hearing.[2]
- Regeneration: As a Noble Vampire, Elizabeth possesses the Race's natural regenerative capabilities. No exceptional Elizabeth-specific regeneration feat has currently been demonstrated.
- Longevity: Elizabeth has lived for centuries while retaining the appearance of a young adult, possessing the extremely extended lifespan characteristic of Noble Vampires.
- Vampire Charm: Elizabeth can influence other beings through the supernatural Charm inherent to Vampires. She attempted to use this power on Victor by making her eyes glow blood-red and commanding him to keep Ophis' ability secret.[2]
- Compulsion: Elizabeth's Charm can be expressed through direct verbal orders. Victor completely resisted the attempt, meaning the scene establishes that Elizabeth possesses the technique but does not establish that her Charm can overcome beings comparable to him.[2]
- Supernatural Beauty: Elizabeth possesses the extraordinary physical attractiveness characteristic of high-ranking Vampires and was repeatedly described as dazzling and exceptionally elegant.[1][2]
- Dream-Created Existence: Elizabeth was artificially created through Morgana's Dream Manipulation, Morgana's Essence and Vlad's Progenitor Essence. Her body and Soul stabilized into a genuine independent living Being rather than remaining a temporary dream construct.[3] This describes the nature of her origin; the novel has not demonstrated that Elizabeth herself automatically inherited Morgana's ability to turn Dreams into Reality.
- Progenitor-Derived Constitution: Vlad's Essence was directly involved in Elizabeth's creation, and Elizabeth emerged as a complete Noble Vampire despite Morgana's Demonic origin.[3] The novel has not directly established every specific advantage this grants Elizabeth beyond her confirmed Vampiric nature.
- Mental Independence: Although Elizabeth was described as resembling a spiritual clone of Morgana, she possesses her own thoughts, emotions and identity rather than functioning as an extension or puppet of her mother.[3]

### Weaknesses
- Elizabeth retains the standard racial weaknesses associated with Vampires, although her direct descent from Vlad gives her a much stronger supernatural constitution than lower-ranked Vampires.
- Her Vampire Charm is not absolute. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] completely resisted her attempt to compel him in Chapter 74.[2]
- Despite her unusual creation, no evidence currently demonstrates that Elizabeth possesses Morgana's full Demonic powers, Dream Manipulation or Succubus abilities. Such abilities should not be assigned to her solely because Morgana supplied part of the Essence used to create her.

### Trivia
- Elizabeth and [[Lilith Tepes]] are the only two known daughters of Vlad who share [[Morgana Elderblood]] as their mother.
- Elizabeth's birth was supernatural rather than entirely biological. Morgana combined her Dream ability and Essence with Vlad's Progenitor Essence to produce a new living Being.[3]
- Elizabeth was described as something similar to a spiritual clone and younger version of Morgana, although she possesses her own independent mind.[3]
- Morgana originally created Elizabeth because she could not bring herself to surrender her firstborn Lilith to [[Lilith Elderblood]] as required by an old agreement. She believed an artificially created child would be easier to give away, but ultimately came to love Elizabeth as well.[3]
- Despite Morgana being a powerful Succubus/Dream Demon, Elizabeth was born as a complete Vampire because Vlad's Vampiric nature dominated the resulting lineage.[3]
- Elizabeth is the Royal sibling most frequently shown personally chasing after Ophis whenever the younger princess escapes.
- Ophis directly described Elizabeth as a good person when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] asked whether she liked her.[1]
- Elizabeth attempted to Charm Victor into concealing Ophis' secret in Chapter 74, but the power had no effect on him.[2]
- Elizabeth was immediately intrigued by Victor's apparent immunity both to her Charm and to Ophis' unusual supernatural condition.[2]
- During Ophis' disappearance, Elizabeth became worried enough to call [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] despite their hostile relationship because she wanted Victor and Natalia's help locating Ophis.[4]
- Elizabeth was so surprised by Violet behaving responsibly during their Chapter 335 conversation that she jokingly wondered whether Armageddon would occur the following day.[4]
- Unlike Lilith, Elizabeth has not been directly confirmed to possess Miasma Manipulation. Lilith's Demonic Energy affinity should therefore not automatically be assigned to Elizabeth simply because the sisters share the same mother.[4]
