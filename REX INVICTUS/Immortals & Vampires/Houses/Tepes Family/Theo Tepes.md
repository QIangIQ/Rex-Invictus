### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 119
Theo Tepes was the eldest son of [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] and Stella, the First Prince of [[Nightingale]], and one of the oldest members of the Vampire Royal Family. At approximately three thousand years old, Theo was considerably older than most living Noble Vampires and, as Vlad's firstborn, possessed a particularly concentrated inheritance of his father's Bloodline.[1][2]
Despite his age and political status, Theo was not considered an exceptional warrior. Vlad regarded intelligence as his eldest son's only outstanding quality and repeatedly criticized Theo for neglecting his personal strength in favor of schemes, conspiracies and indirect methods. Even though [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] was younger than him, Vlad stated that she could defeat Theo easily without even needing her full transformation.[3]
Theo's defining ambition was to remove his father from the throne and eventually rule Nightingale himself. He considered the throne his birthright as Vlad's firstborn and believed Vlad had become an ineffective ruler after millennia of emotional detachment. His rebellion also had a deeply personal motivation: Theo wanted to free his mother, Stella, from a marriage in which Vlad had effectively abandoned her while refusing to let her leave.[4]
Over time, Theo became associated with the conspiratorial network later known as [[New Dawn]], particularly through his friendship with [[Niklaus Horseman]]. His final act as a conspirator was to ingest a specially prepared poison so that when Vlad killed and absorbed him, the poison would enter his father's body and weaken the Vampire Progenitor.[4]
Theo was ultimately killed alongside Stella after their longstanding affair became public throughout Nightingale. Although Vlad had already known about the relationship, the scandal became so widespread that his reputation as King forced him to publicly act. Theo knowingly accepted his death, cursed his father with his final words, attempted one last attack and was immediately dismembered by Vlad.[2][4]

### Physical Appearance
Theo's physical appearance has received relatively little detailed description in the novel. He was a male Noble Vampire with black hair and the youthful appearance characteristic of his Race despite being approximately three thousand years old.[1]
As the First Prince and direct son of the Vampire Progenitor, Theo carried himself with the bearing expected from a member of [[Nightingale]]'s Royal Family. During his appearances beside his siblings he was treated visually and socially as the eldest prince, although the novel focused significantly more upon his temperament and political behavior than his clothing or specific facial features.
Like other Noble Vampires, Theo's eyes could display the characteristic blood-red glow of his Race when emotionally stimulated or exerting supernatural power.

### Personality
Theo was intelligent, cunning, ambitious and deeply prideful. Of Vlad's sons, he was the one most inclined toward schemes, manipulation and long-term plots rather than direct confrontation. Vlad explicitly acknowledged Theo's intelligence while simultaneously condemning this mentality, comparing his eldest son to a poisonous snake who preferred plotting over becoming strong enough to directly confront his enemies.[3]
Theo strongly believed that his position as Vlad's firstborn gave him a legitimate claim to the throne of [[Nightingale]]. He resented [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s extraordinarily rapid rise, particularly when the twenty-one-year-old newborn was granted the title of Vampire Count while Theo had lived for millennia without receiving comparable recognition from his father.[5][3]
This sense of superiority was mixed with severe insecurity.
Vlad's criticism affected Theo considerably more than he initially admitted. After his father publicly declared him weak and disappointing, Theo returned to his office and destroyed furniture in rage because he felt he had been treated like a child.[3]
His jealousy toward Victor similarly came from feeling that his own importance as the Vampire King's eldest son was being undermined by a newly created Vampire who had accomplished more in an extremely short period.
Although Theo viewed himself as independent and politically capable, he remained heavily dependent upon Vlad's authority. When Victor killed Theo's son, Theo and [[Lucas Tepes]] immediately went to their father and demanded that Vlad retaliate for them. Vlad harshly criticized both men for being thousands of years old yet still running to their father whenever something went wrong.[6]
Theo's behavior could therefore alternate dramatically between:
- a patient conspirator capable of hiding plots from his father;
- an ambitious prince attempting to seize the throne;
- and an emotionally immature son desperate for Vlad's recognition.
Despite his flaws, Theo's motivations were not purely selfish.
His final conversation with Niklaus reveals that much of his hatred toward Vlad developed from witnessing Stella's unhappiness. Theo believed his father repeatedly collected women, eventually lost interest in them and then refused to release them because he viewed his family as possessions. He wanted the throne for himself, but he also genuinely wanted to remove his mother from what he considered an intolerable existence.[4]
Theo was fully aware of his own greed and did not attempt to disguise it. When Niklaus accused him of wanting both the throne and Stella, Theo simply accepted the criticism and characterized greed, selfishness and depravity as fundamental traits of Vampires.[4]
Near his death, Theo demonstrated considerably more courage than Vlad had previously credited him with.
Knowing that Vlad would arrive to execute him, Theo did not attempt to flee. He deliberately drank the poison prepared by [[New Dawn]], accepted that his death could still damage his father and finally stood before Vlad without the fear he had displayed throughout most of his life.[4]

### Synopsis

#### Vampire Society
Theo first appeared when [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] summoned all seven of his children to the Royal Castle for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s arrival.
As the eldest of the four princes, Theo attempted to maintain authority over his younger brothers and ordered Lucas to remain silent when the latter questioned why their father had gathered the entire family.[1]
Theo was already approximately three thousand years old, making him not only Vlad's eldest son but one of the oldest Noble Vampires in [[Nightingale]].[1]
During Victor's audience with Vlad, Theo became increasingly disturbed by the special treatment given to the young Vampire. When Vlad offered Victor the position of Vampire Count, Theo immediately attempted to object, only for his father to silence him before he could properly speak.[5]
Vlad's behavior toward Victor deeply affected Theo.
A Vampire barely into adulthood had attacked the Vampire King inside his own throne room, survived, obtained Vlad's interest and then been elevated to Count. Theo, meanwhile, remained merely the First Prince after thousands of years.
After Victor departed, Vlad gathered his children and explained that the entire encounter had been intended partly as a lesson.
He wanted them to observe Victor's willingness to challenge overwhelmingly superior opponents rather than automatically submitting to them.[3]
Vlad then criticized each of his children individually.
Theo received the harshest criticism.
Vlad acknowledged that Theo was intelligent but said intelligence was his only redeeming trait. Despite living for three thousand years, Theo had failed to properly develop his strength and was significantly weaker than [[Scathach]] despite being older than her. Vlad accused him of spending his time scheming like a poisonous snake instead of training.[3]
Theo was humiliated.
After returning to his office, he lost his normally composed demeanor and destroyed furniture while raging over being treated like a child. Lucas confronted him and pointed out that reacting so immaturely only reinforced their father's opinion.[3]
Theo's conversation with Lucas also revealed his growing resentment toward Victor.
He disliked that someone only twenty-one years old had become a Count and admitted that Victor's existence made him feel inferior. Theo considered using his and Lucas' sons to create problems for the newly appointed Count, although Lucas immediately warned that provoking Victor was an extremely dangerous idea.[3]
Theo believed Vlad would protect his grandchildren if Victor retaliated.
Lucas understood their father better and warned him that Vlad barely acknowledged the two young men at all.[3]
The warning ultimately proved correct.
Some time later, Victor killed both Theo and Lucas' sons in retaliation for actions connected to the Royal Family's schemes. [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett|Scathach Scarlett]] personally brought their frozen severed heads to Vlad's throne room.[6]
Theo and Lucas arrived shortly afterward and demanded that Vlad punish Victor.
Instead, Vlad mocked them for immediately running to their father for help despite being thousands of years old. He ordered them to grow up and solve their own problems.[6]
The incident further damaged Theo's relationship with Vlad.
Vlad himself internally recognized that Theo was capable of considerable cunning and was actively attempting to take the throne, yet Theo could abruptly regress into behaving like a spoiled adolescent whenever events escaped his control.[6]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Theo's deeper conspiracy developed alongside his relationship with his mother, Stella.
Vlad's first wife had spent an enormous period emotionally neglected by her husband. Like several of Vlad's wives, she had become dependent upon the extraordinary quality of the Vampire Progenitor's blood and eventually found herself unable to consume ordinary substitutes.[7][2]
Unlike Vlad's other wives, Stella managed to remain active by drinking Theo's blood.
Because Theo was Vlad's firstborn son, his blood retained a greater proportion of Vlad's characteristics than the blood of his younger siblings and was close enough to temporarily sustain her.[2]
What began as Theo providing blood for his mother eventually developed into a long-running intimate relationship as a consequence of Vlad's prolonged abandonment.[7][2]
Theo and Stella carried on the affair in secret for years.
Vlad was actually aware of the relationship but initially did nothing. By that stage of his life, he had become emotionally detached from several of his wives and largely treated them as possessions he refused to release rather than partners with whom he maintained meaningful relationships.[2]
Theo's hatred toward his father intensified because of Stella's situation.
At the same time, he became increasingly connected to [[Niklaus Horseman]] and the network surrounding [[New Dawn]]. Niklaus became one of Theo's closest allies and was aware of both his political goals and his relationship with Stella.[4]
Theo wanted Vlad's throne.
He considered it his birthright as the First Prince, but his reasoning extended beyond simple inheritance. He believed Vlad had ruled for long enough, had become inefficient and was no longer properly governing [[Nightingale]]. Theo saw himself as someone capable of actually making decisions rather than remaining seated upon what he considered an increasingly hollow throne.[4]
He also wanted to remove Stella from Vlad's control.
In Theo's view, Vlad repeatedly found women interesting, bound them to himself and then abandoned them once the novelty disappeared. Even after losing interest, Vlad's possessiveness prevented those women from freely leaving him.[4]
Theo intended to obtain both objectives:
the throne and his mother.
[4]
His long-term conspiracy eventually became intertwined with New Dawn's plans against Vlad.
Niklaus and James helped develop an extremely dangerous poison intended specifically to affect even a Vampire Progenitor. The mixture incorporated several supernatural components, including Hybrid-derived materials, Elder God blood, Hunter Faith and high-level Soul-cleansing [[Magic]].[4]
The poison was not intended to let Theo defeat Vlad directly.
Theo knew he could not.
Instead, it exploited Vlad's habit of consuming the bodies and Souls of his own Vampiric descendants.
Theo deliberately drank the poison before his execution so that Vlad would absorb the substance after killing him.[4]
Niklaus further ensured that the mixture would destroy or cleanse sensitive information within Theo's Soul, reducing the chance that Vlad could recover New Dawn's secrets from him after death.[4]
Before this plan could unfold, the affair between Theo and Stella became public.
[[Selena Moriarty]], acting upon information connected to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s earlier actions, obtained a recording of their relationship and distributed it throughout Nightingale. Within approximately two days, nearly the entire Vampire society knew that the King's eldest son had been sleeping with the King's first wife.[2]
The scandal was especially damaging because Stella was not merely Theo's mother but still publicly Vlad's wife.
Although incest itself was not particularly shocking among Vampires, the violation of Vlad's marriage and Royal authority humiliated the King publicly.[2]
The rumor spread beyond Nightingale as Vlad's hidden enemies deliberately amplified it.
Vlad's reputation began to suffer, and his pride made ignoring the situation impossible.[2]
Theo learned about the leak from Niklaus.
By the time Niklaus contacted him, Theo understood that his father would kill both him and Stella within hours.[4]
He did not attempt to escape.
Theo drank New Dawn's poison, endured its immediate painful effects and prepared himself for death.[4]
During his final conversation with Niklaus, Theo openly explained why he wanted Vlad removed from power.
He considered the throne his birthright, believed Vlad had become an ineffective King and wanted to free Stella from the life Vlad had forced upon her.[4]
When Vlad eventually arrived, Theo and Stella were fully aware of what was about to happen.
For perhaps the first time in his life, Theo stood directly before his father without being overwhelmed by fear.[4]
Vlad granted them final words.
Stella expressed regret that she had ever met him.
Theo went further and cursed his father, wishing that every blood bond Vlad had created would eventually break and that Vlad would be left eternally alone as a lonely King ruling an empty kingdom.[4]
Theo then attacked.
Using all the physical power he could muster, he struck Vlad hard enough that the impact destroyed part of the castle.[4]
The attack had essentially no effect upon the Vampire Progenitor.
Before Theo could finish speaking afterward, Vlad dismembered him with his own hands. Stella was killed immediately afterward.[4]
Vlad subsequently absorbed Theo and the bodies of his wives rather than simply destroying them, inadvertently completing the final stage of Theo and New Dawn's poisoning plan.[2][4]
Theo's death therefore achieved the one thing he knew he could never accomplish through direct combat:
he successfully delivered a weapon into Vlad's body.

### Relationships

#### Vlad Dracula Tepes
[[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] was Theo's father, King and greatest source of resentment.
As Vlad's firstborn son, Theo believed himself naturally entitled to inherit [[Nightingale]]. Vlad instead regarded him as deeply disappointing.[3]
Vlad acknowledged Theo's intelligence but criticized virtually every other aspect of him. He believed Theo had wasted three thousand years by prioritizing schemes over personal strength and considered him mentally unfit to inherit the throne.[3]
Theo spent much of his life intimidated by his father's overwhelming power.
Even when actively conspiring against Vlad, a single shift in his father's mood could reduce Theo to the behavior of a frightened child.[3][6]
His hatred was nevertheless genuine.
Theo considered Vlad:
- neglectful;
- possessive;
- increasingly inefficient as King;
- emotionally absent toward his wives;
- and unwilling to relinquish anything he regarded as belonging to him.[4]
By the time of his death, Theo had stopped fearing him.
His final words were not an apology but a curse, wishing for Vlad to lose every bond he possessed and ultimately live alone forever.[4]

#### Stella
Stella was simultaneously Theo's mother and longtime lover.
Their relationship originated partly from Vlad's neglect.
After Stella became dependent upon Vlad's Progenitor blood but stopped receiving it regularly, Theo began supplying her with his own blood. As Vlad's firstborn, Theo possessed slightly more of his father's blood than his younger siblings, allowing Stella to survive on it when other substitutes were rejected.[2]
Years of dependence and emotional abandonment gradually turned their relationship intimate.[2]
Theo was genuinely protective of Stella.
His desire to remove Vlad from the throne was motivated not only by ambition but by his belief that his mother had spent thousands of years trapped in an unhappy marriage from which Vlad refused to release her.[4]
When their affair was publicly exposed, neither Theo nor Stella attempted to deny what they had done.
They accepted that Vlad would kill them and faced him together.[4]

#### Radu Tepes
[[Niklaus Horseman]] was Theo's closest confirmed friend and one of his principal allies within [[New Dawn]].
Niklaus knew about:
- Theo's ambitions;
- his relationship with Stella;
- his plans against Vlad;
- and his likely death once the affair became public.[4]
When the scandal began spreading through [[Nightingale]], Niklaus personally warned Theo that Vlad would kill him within hours.[4]
He also provided Theo with the poison designed to harm Vlad and prevent New Dawn's information from being recovered through Theo's Soul.[4]
Their final conversation shows that Niklaus understood Theo more deeply than most characters and questioned him directly about his true reasons for wanting his father's throne.

#### Lucas Tepes
[[Lucas Tepes]] was Theo's younger half-brother.
Despite frequent arguments, the two often acted together politically and were sufficiently close that Lucas could openly criticize Theo's behavior.[1][3]
Lucas was physically stronger than Theo and considerably more dedicated to training, a difference Vlad explicitly emphasized.[3]
Theo nevertheless possessed greater political cunning and frequently attempted to influence his brother.
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became a Count, Theo considered sending their sons against him. Lucas immediately rejected the plan as suicidal and correctly predicted that Victor would kill them if sufficiently provoked.[3]
Their sons were eventually killed by Victor, after which both Theo and Lucas went to Vlad seeking retaliation.[6]

#### Unnamed Son
Theo had at least one unnamed son.
Vlad openly considered both Theo's and Lucas' sons spoiled disappointments who behaved as though their position as the King's grandchildren made them untouchable.[3]
The two eventually became involved in actions against [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and were killed by him. Their severed heads were later delivered to Vlad by [[Scathach]].[6]
Theo was furious over his son's death and demanded that Vlad retaliate against Victor, only for his father to mock him for expecting parental intervention.[6]

### Skills and Abilities
Theo was an extremely old Noble Vampire and direct firstborn son of the Vampire Progenitor. Although much stronger than ordinary Humans and younger Vampires, his development was considered disappointing relative to his age and heritage.
- Noble Vampire Physiology: Theo was a naturally born Noble Vampire directly descended from [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]]. At three thousand years old, he possessed an extremely mature Vampiric body compared with ordinary Noble Vampires.[1]
- Superhuman Strength: Theo possessed physical strength vastly beyond Human limits. During his final confrontation with Vlad, he attacked his father with all of his strength and the impact destroyed part of the Royal Castle despite failing to injure Vlad himself.[4]
- Superhuman Speed: During his final moments, Theo crossed the distance between himself and Vlad with a single step before attacking.[4]
- Superhuman Durability: Theo possessed the enhanced physical durability expected from an Elder Noble Vampire, although it was completely insufficient against Vlad.
- Longevity: Theo lived for approximately three thousand years while retaining the physical appearance of a comparatively young man.[1]
- Vampiric Blood Aura: When Theo and Lucas became enraged after discovering their sons had been killed, a bloody aura visibly emanated from both princes.[6]
- Enhanced Progenitor Bloodline: As Vlad's firstborn, Theo possessed a particularly strong concentration of his father's blood. His blood was sufficiently similar in quality to Vlad's that Stella could survive by drinking it after becoming unable to tolerate ordinary blood.[2]
- High-Quality Blood: Theo's blood could sustain Stella for thousands of years despite her severe dependence upon the blood of the Vampire Progenitor.[7][2]
- Intelligence: Vlad explicitly acknowledged Theo as intelligent and considered this his greatest positive trait.[3]
- Cunning and Scheming: Theo specialized in indirect conflict, political manipulation and long-term schemes rather than direct battle. Vlad compared him to a poisonous snake and knew Theo was actively plotting to obtain the throne.[3][6]
- Political Manipulation: As First Prince, Theo understood [[Nightingale]]'s Royal structure and spent a considerable portion of his life maneuvering around Vlad's rule. His ambitions ultimately aligned him with [[New Dawn]].
- Deception: Theo hid his conspiratorial activities from much of the Royal Family and attempted to conceal the full extent of his plans even from Vlad.[3]
- Poison Delivery Strategy: Knowing he could not defeat Vlad directly, Theo deliberately ingested a specialized New Dawn poison shortly before his death. The plan depended upon Vlad consuming Theo afterward, allowing Theo to deliver the substance into the Vampire Progenitor's body from within.[4] This was a prepared tactic rather than an innate ability.
- Pain Tolerance: The specialized poison immediately caused Theo severe internal pain and vomiting of blood, yet he suppressed the visible symptoms and behaved normally enough to proceed with the plan.[4]
- Mental Resolve (Final Moments): Despite fearing Vlad throughout much of his life, Theo ultimately accepted his impending death and stood before the Vampire Progenitor without attempting to flee or beg for mercy.[4]

### Power
Theo's age placed him among the oldest Noble Vampires of his generation, but chronological age did not translate into exceptional combat strength because he neglected training.
Vlad summarized Theo's problem directly:
He was intelligent, but weak.
[3]
Despite Theo being approximately three thousand years old, Vlad stated that the younger [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett|Scathach Scarlett]] could defeat him easily without even using her transformation.[3]
Theo's younger brother [[Lucas Tepes]] was likewise explicitly stronger because Lucas consistently trained while Theo devoted himself to schemes.[3]

### Weaknesses
- Lack of Martial Development: Despite being three thousand years old, Theo neglected physical training and failed to develop strength appropriate to his age and Bloodline.[3]
- Inferior to Lucas: Vlad explicitly considered Lucas stronger because Lucas consistently trained while Theo did not.[3]
- Far Inferior to [[Scathach]]: Scathach could defeat Theo without needing her full transformation despite being younger than him.[3]
- Dependence on Schemes: Theo habitually relied upon manipulation and indirect plans. Against overwhelmingly powerful opponents, Vlad considered this approach fundamentally inadequate.[3]
- Pride and Jealousy: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s rapid advancement deeply destabilized Theo because it made him feel inferior despite his position as Vlad's firstborn.[5][3]
- Emotional Immaturity: Although thousands of years old, Theo repeatedly reverted to behaving like a spoiled young man when humiliated or placed under pressure.[3][6]
- Dependence on Vlad: Despite wanting to overthrow his father, Theo initially relied upon Vlad whenever serious problems exceeded his own ability to resolve them.[6]
- No Demonstrated Unique Bloodline Ability: Theo has not been shown possessing an individualized elemental or Bloodline power comparable to the abilities of the major Vampire Count Clans.
- Overwhelming Power Difference: Theo was completely incapable of physically harming Vlad even with a full-force final attack.[4]
