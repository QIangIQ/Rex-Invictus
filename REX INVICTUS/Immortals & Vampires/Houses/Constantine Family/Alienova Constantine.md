**[[Alienova Constantine]]**, referred to by Radu Tepes as Alienova Constantine of Snow, was an ancient Noble Vampire, the illegitimate daughter of Alienova Snow, wife of Radu Tepes—better known in Nightingale as Niklaus Horseman—and mother of Jessica Horseman Snow. (Lore adapted from *My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires*.)

### Appearances

#### First Mention
Chapter 870
Alienova Constantine, referred to by Radu Tepes as Alienova Constantine of Snow, was an ancient Noble Vampire, the illegitimate daughter of [[Alienova Snow]], wife of Radu Tepes—better known in [[Nightingale]] as [[Niklaus Horseman]]—and mother of [[Jessica Horseman Snow]].[1] Through her mother, Alienova belonged by blood to one of the oldest branches of the [[Snow Clan]]'s main family despite apparently never occupying a public position within the Clan itself.
Alienova's existence was only revealed centuries after her death when Radu explained the truth of Jessica's ancestry during the destruction of [[New Dawn]]. Her mother, Alienova Snow, was the sister of [[Agnes Elderblood Snow]]'s mother, making Alienova Constantine and Agnes first cousins. Consequently, Alienova was not Agnes' sister or Violet's direct aunt as older versions of the family records sometimes implied; [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] was Alienova's first cousin once removed, while Jessica and Violet descended from neighboring branches of the same main Snow bloodline.[1]
Although Alienova never physically appears in the present storyline, her lineage became extremely important. Radu revealed that Jessica had been naturally born from two exceptionally powerful Noble Vampire bloodlines and had inherited Alienova's connection to the Snow Clan. Alienova had also possessed an important Snow-family relic made from the heart of the Dragon [[Fafnir]], which Radu preserved after her death and eventually entrusted to Jessica.[1]

### Physical Appearance
Alienova never appears directly on-page, so the novel provides no detailed narrative description of her body, height, clothing or facial features. Existing family records describe her as a pale-skinned Noble Vampire with white hair and red Vampiric eyes, visually consistent with her direct Snow ancestry.
Her white hair is particularly appropriate to the Snow lineage, whose strongest members frequently display extremely pale or white hair when their Bloodline is fully expressed. Her daughter [[Jessica Horseman Snow]] originally possessed dark hair but developed platinum-white hair after her dormant [[Snow Bloodline]] awakened, further demonstrating the strength of the hereditary traits transmitted through Alienova.[2]
Because Alienova herself never physically appears in the narrative, more specific details concerning her build, height, preferred clothing or combat appearance remain unknown.

### Personality
Alienova receives no direct dialogue and died before her first chronological mention, leaving her individual personality largely unknown. The available information instead survives through her husband, daughter and possessions.
The most meaningful indication of her connection to her maternal family is the fact that she retained a powerful relic of the [[Snow Clan]] despite being the illegitimate daughter of [[Alienova Snow]]. The relic was specifically described as something that could only properly be used by a member of the Snow main family, indicating that Alienova's blood was recognized by the Artifact even if her social position within the Clan was considerably more complicated.[1]
Nothing in the novel establishes how Alienova felt toward Radu Tepes, the Snow Clan, her mother, or the political circumstances surrounding her illegitimate birth. Likewise, the circumstances of her marriage, her relationship with Jessica and the events leading to her death have not yet been revealed.

### History
Alienova was born as the illegitimate daughter of [[Alienova Snow]], an older member of the Snow family's main line and the sister of [[Agnes Elderblood Snow]]'s mother.[1] The identity of Alienova Constantine's father has never been revealed, and the novel does not explain why she carried the surname Constantine rather than Snow.
Despite the circumstances of her birth, Alienova inherited the blood of the principal Snow family. This later became important because her daughter Jessica was capable of awakening the same ancient Snow traits and using a main-family relic that had previously belonged to Alienova.[1]
At some point Alienova became the wife of Radu Tepes, who operated publicly in [[Nightingale]] under the identity [[Niklaus Horseman]]. Their relationship connected an unacknowledged branch of the Snow main line to the family that would later become known as the [[Horseman Clan]].
The couple had [[Jessica Horseman Snow]], Radu's explicitly natural biological daughter. Jessica was unusual even among Noble Vampires because she inherited two exceptionally powerful bloodlines at birth. Radu contrasted her natural birth with the more artificial circumstances surrounding his other children and experiments, emphasizing that Jessica's heritage had emerged naturally through himself and Alienova rather than being manufactured entirely through experimentation.[1]
Alienova was already dead long before Radu revealed her identity to Jessica. The novel has not explained when she died, who or what caused her death, whether she died in battle, or whether her death had any connection to Radu's later obsession with experimentation and revenge.

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Alienova was first mentioned during the destruction of [[New Dawn]]. After [[Victor Elderblood]] openly began moving against New Dawn and its allies, [[Niklaus Horseman]] realized that remaining at the organization's headquarters would eventually result in his death. Determined to survive so that he could continue pursuing his revenge against [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]], Radu prepared to flee with his remaining family.[1]
During his escape, he encountered [[Jessica Horseman Snow]], who refused to accompany him to the place where he intended to hide. Rather than forcing her to follow, Radu unexpectedly decided to send Jessica back to [[Nightingale]]. He told her to seek protection from the [[Snow Clan]], confusing Jessica because she had never understood herself to possess any meaningful relationship with that family.[1]
Radu then revealed the secret he had kept from his daughter: Jessica's mother was Alienova Constantine of Snow, the illegitimate daughter of [[Alienova Snow]]. Alienova Snow herself had been the sister of Agnes Snow's mother, meaning Jessica was a direct descendant of the central Snow family rather than merely an unrelated member of Clan Horseman.[1]
The revelation fundamentally changed Jessica's understanding of her ancestry. Radu explained that Jessica had been naturally born carrying two extraordinarily powerful Noble Vampire Bloodlines. Unlike the unnatural experiments surrounding portions of the Horseman family, Jessica's Snow inheritance came directly from Alienova and had simply remained dormant for most of her life.[1]
Radu then produced a collar that had belonged to Alienova Constantine. The object was a genuine Snow Clan heirloom created using the heart of [[Fafnir]]. According to Radu, the relic could only be properly used by someone belonging to the Snow family's main lineage, making its acceptance of Jessica direct supernatural confirmation of the ancestry he had just revealed.[1]
The collar was related to the far more famous [[Blade of Fafnir]], another Snow Clan treasure. Whereas Fafnir's sword contained the Dragon's Soul, Alienova's collar contained its Heart. Radu explained that although the sword was superior as an individual weapon, the collar remained an extraordinarily valuable relic; according to Snow tradition, combining both relics would allow their user to wield flames comparable to Fafnir's own.[1]
Radu admitted that he had originally intended to exploit Jessica's Snow ancestry for political purposes. His long-term plan had been to use her Bloodline to interfere with or eventually seize influence over the Snow Clan after he had secured control of Clan Fulger. The rise of Victor destroyed those ambitions, leaving the relic and Jessica's lineage unused until New Dawn itself came under attack.[1]
Ironically, Radu ultimately relied upon the family he once intended to manipulate. Despite his hatred of Victor, Radu acknowledged that Victor protected the people he considered family with extraordinary determination. Since Snow was part of Victor's family through Violet and Agnes, Radu concluded that sending Jessica to Clan Snow was the safest option available to him.[1]
Radu placed Alienova's collar around Jessica's neck before knocking his daughter unconscious to prevent her from resisting. The Artifact immediately reacted to protect its wearer. Flames surrounded Jessica, her dormant [[Snow Bloodline]] awakened, her hair began changing white, and the relic transported her away from New Dawn toward Nightingale.[1]
Alienova therefore never appears personally, but her blood and the heirloom she left behind directly saved her daughter's life.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Jessica's integration into her maternal family continued after the revelation of Alienova's identity. Having spent most of her life as a Horseman, Jessica suddenly found herself surrounded by members of the Snow lineage and was forced to reconsider everything she understood about her family.
Her dormant Snow inheritance continued expressing itself after the awakening triggered by Alienova's relic. Jessica's hair became platinum-white, and characteristics associated with the [[Snow Bloodline]] became increasingly visible in her physiology and behavior.[2]
Alienova's posthumous importance therefore became greater than her lack of direct appearances might suggest. Through her, the supposedly separate Horseman family contained a surviving branch of the main Snow Bloodline, and Jessica eventually returned that branch to the family from which Alienova herself had descended.

### Skills & Abilities
Alienova never participates in an on-page battle, so her personal combat ability cannot be accurately ranked. However, her race, direct Snow ancestry and the inheritance demonstrated by Jessica establish several characteristics with reasonable certainty.
- Noble Vampire Physiology: Alienova was a Noble Vampire and therefore possessed the enhanced physical condition, longevity, Blood-based supernatural nature and regenerative capabilities associated with her race.
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: As a Noble Vampire, Alienova possessed physical strength, speed, reflexes, durability and senses greatly exceeding ordinary Humans.
- [[Immortality]] / Agelessness: Noble Vampires cease aging in the ordinary Human sense after reaching maturity and can survive for extremely long periods unless killed.
- Regeneration: Noble Vampires possess supernatural regenerative abilities considerably beyond those of Humans.
- Enhanced Senses: Alienova possessed the heightened sensory capabilities inherent to Vampires, including extremely developed smell, hearing and supernatural perception.
- Bloodline Inheritance: Alienova carried a direct main-family [[Snow Clan]] Bloodline through her mother. This is explicitly demonstrated by Jessica naturally inheriting and later awakening that same lineage.[1][2]
- [[Snow Bloodline]] (Hereditary): The Snow family possesses a powerful hereditary affinity associated principally with supernatural Fire. Alienova necessarily carried the Bloodline strongly enough to transmit it to Jessica, although no chapter directly depicts Alienova personally using Snow Fire.[1]
- Main-Line Relic Compatibility: Alienova possessed the Snow Clan collar forged from the heart of [[Fafnir]], an Artifact stated to be usable only by members of the main Snow family. Her ownership of the relic further confirms her legitimate supernatural connection to the primary Snow Bloodline despite her illegitimate social status.[1]
The novel has not demonstrated a personal [[Vampire Count Form]], named technique, martial art or individually unique power belonging to Alienova. Such abilities should not be attributed to her solely because later Snow members possess them.

### Snow Bloodline
Alienova's greatest confirmed supernatural importance comes from her ancestry.
Her mother, [[Alienova Snow]], belonged directly to the main Snow family and was the sister of Agnes' mother. Alienova Constantine was born outside the publicly recognized family structure but nevertheless inherited the [[Snow Bloodline]] genetically and spiritually.[1]
This distinction between social legitimacy and Bloodline legitimacy is particularly important. Alienova could be treated as an illegitimate daughter politically while still possessing the actual Blood required to activate Snow-family supernatural inheritance. Her daughter Jessica later proved this decisively when Alienova's [[Fafnir]] relic accepted her, awakened her dormant lineage and initiated her return to Clan Snow.[1]

### Relationships

#### Alienova Snow
[[Alienova Snow]] was Alienova Constantine's mother and a member of an older generation of the Snow main family. She was the sister of Agnes' mother, making her Agnes' maternal aunt.[1]
Alienova Constantine was explicitly described as her illegitimate daughter. The identity of Alienova Constantine's father and the reasons behind her illegitimate status have never been explained.
Despite that social status, Alienova inherited enough of her mother's Bloodline to possess a relic restricted to the main Snow family and later transmitted the Bloodline to Jessica.[1]

#### Radu Tepes
Radu Tepes, better known in [[Nightingale]] as [[Niklaus Horseman]], was Alienova's husband. The novel has not shown how the two met, why Alienova joined Radu, how long their marriage lasted or what their personal relationship was like.
Radu nevertheless preserved Alienova's Snow relic long after her death, eventually giving it to Jessica when his own plans collapsed. He had also secretly built political ambitions around Alienova's ancestry, intending to use Jessica's inherited [[Snow Bloodline]] as a means of eventually gaining influence over Clan Snow.[1]

#### Jessica Horseman Snow
[[Jessica Horseman Snow]] is Alienova's biological daughter with Radu and the most important surviving continuation of her Bloodline.[1]
Jessica spent most of her life unaware of her mother's true ancestry. Radu revealed the secret only when [[New Dawn]] came under direct attack and he needed a safe place to send his daughter. Jessica subsequently inherited Alienova's [[Fafnir]] collar, awakened the [[Snow Bloodline]] and returned to [[Nightingale]] under the protection of Clan Snow.[1]
Jessica later developed the white hair and psychological traits strongly associated with the Snow family, demonstrating how deeply Alienova's Bloodline had remained preserved despite generations of separation from the Clan.[2]

#### Einer and Zwei Horseman
[[Einer Horseman]] and [[Zwei Horseman]] were members of Alienova and Radu's immediate family and are conventionally listed among Alienova's children. Their origins, however, are significantly more complicated than Jessica's.
Radu explicitly contrasted Jessica's natural birth with the artificial circumstances surrounding his other children, who were closely connected to his experiments and the use of his brother's Blood.[1] Jessica is therefore the child whose biological inheritance from both Alienova and Radu is most directly established by Chapter 870.

#### Agnes Elderblood Snow
[[Agnes Elderblood Snow]] is Alienova's first cousin. [[Alienova Snow]] and Agnes' mother were sisters, placing both women within neighboring branches of the Snow main family.[1]
There is no known direct interaction between Agnes and Alienova in the novel, and it is unknown whether Agnes knew about her cousin before Radu exposed the secret lineage.

#### Violet Elderblood Snow
[[Violet Elderblood Snow]] is Agnes' daughter and therefore Alienova's first cousin once removed. Older family records sometimes described Alienova as Violet's aunt, but the genealogy established in Chapter 870 makes the more precise relationship first cousin once removed.[1]
Alienova's daughter Jessica and Violet are consequently second cousins.

### Legacy
Alienova never appears alive within the story, but the secret surrounding her ancestry had consequences extending across multiple generations.
Through her marriage to Radu, the blood of the Snow main family entered the Horseman household without the wider [[Snow Clan]] apparently realizing it. Radu originally intended to exploit this fact politically. His plan involved strengthening his own position, taking control of Clan Fulger and eventually using Jessica's Snow ancestry to gain influence over the Snow Clan itself.[1]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s rise destroyed those ambitions before they could be completed. Ironically, the same hidden Bloodline Radu had planned to weaponize became the reason he trusted Clan Snow to protect Jessica. Alienova's daughter survived because she possessed exactly the family connection Radu had spent years concealing.[1]
The revelation also expanded the known Snow family. Alienova Constantine established a previously hidden branch connecting the Snow and Horseman Clans, while Jessica's awakening demonstrated that the ancient Bloodline could remain dormant yet fully viable across a branch excluded from the recognized succession.

#### Skills & Abilities
Noble Vampire Physiology: As a Noble Vampire from Vlad's sireline and an very old vampire, Alienova possesses all Vampires basic abilities to a greater degree, like Superhuman Strength, Superhuman Speed, [[Immortality]], Regeneration, Fog Walking, Bat Transformation, Shapeshifting, Enhanced Senses, Supernatural Beauty,....,etc.
- [[Pyrokinesis]]: All direct member of the [[Snow Clan]] has the innate ability to control and generate flames, their mastery over it allows them to inflict flames of severe heat towards their fellow vampires. Their power also grants them fire immunity.
- Sunlight Immunity: All vampires have an inherent weakness toward sunlight. However, the Snow Clan's ability to govern fire grants them immunity to this weakness. A peculiarity that is unique to their clan, as such they are the only vampires capable of roaming in broad daylight.
- Emotion Empowerment: Members of the Snow Clan's strength is proportionally related with the intensity of their emotions. The stronger their emotions (Specially rage) is the stronger they become.
