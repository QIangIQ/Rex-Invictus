### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 388
Alice Moriarty is an elite Witch, master practitioner of Black [[Magic]], and the current Second Daughter of the Witch Queen. She occupies one of the highest positions beneath Evie in Arcane and belongs to the small group of Witches incorporated into the Witch Queen's familial and political hierarchy. Alice's principal specialty is one of the most feared branches of Witchcraft: Black Magic, a field associated with curses and direct interference with the minds, thoughts and behavior of living beings.[1]
Although Alice is sometimes described as a "daughter" of Evie, the designation is not biological. The Witch Queen's ranked Daughters are elite Witches elevated into a relationship that is simultaneously familial, political and hierarchical, representing some of Evie's most important subordinates and assets. Alice currently occupies the Second Daughter position beneath [[Selena Moriarty]], while [[Hecate Moriarty]] occupies the Third Daughter position.[1]
Alice is unusual even among elite Witches because prolonged use of Black Magic has visibly altered her body. Her skin has become unnaturally ashen and corpse-like, while her black eyes, hair and fingernails reinforce an appearance compared to that of a ghost. Alice herself explicitly identifies the unnatural complexion as a side effect of her Magic rather than her natural skin color.[2]

### Physical Appearance
Alice possesses an extremely distinctive gothic appearance. She has long black hair, completely black eyes, black fingernails and unnaturally pale, ashen skin that makes her resemble a ghost or corpse.[3][1] Her complexion is not simply a natural characteristic; when [[Victor Elderblood]] questioned why she looked even paler than a Vampire, Alice explained that the condition was produced by the side effects of her [[Magic]].[2]
During the [[Supernatural Gathering]], Alice wore an entirely black dress with a low neckline. Combined with her black eyes, hair, fingernails and ghost-like complexion, the outfit gave her an appearance Victor associated naturally with someone deeply immersed in Black Magic.[1][2]
Alice carries a long black magical staff topped by a neon-green gemstone.[1] The exact properties of the staff have never been revealed, although its design and her constant possession of it indicate that it functions as magical equipment or a casting focus.

### Personality
Alice is quiet, observant, intelligent, curious and socially inexperienced despite her elevated position within Arcane. She is considerably less outwardly manipulative than Evie and appears uncomfortable when interactions move outside areas she understands well. This becomes particularly obvious during her first extended conversation with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], where relatively ordinary kindness and personal attention are enough to make her wonder whether he has developed romantic interest in her.[2]
Her reaction is explicitly connected to a lack of ordinary social experience. Alice had spent comparatively little time navigating conventional interpersonal relationships and consequently fell into a simplistic misunderstanding: an extremely attractive man was speaking kindly to her, therefore perhaps he was attracted to her. Victor's natural charisma only amplified the confusion.[2]
Alice is nevertheless far from naïve in matters involving [[Magic]] or danger. She understands perfectly why Black Magic is feared and responds to the subject with a cold smile rather than shame. Her chosen field attacks some of the most intimate aspects of a living Being—the mind, thoughts, [[Will Solace|Will]] and personality—and Alice is accustomed to other supernatural races reacting to such powers with immediate suspicion.[1][2]
Victor's refusal to condemn her solely because of the type of Magic she practices therefore surprises her. Victor explains that dangerous powers do not automatically make their owner evil and that he prefers judging a person through their actions. Alice is visibly affected by the statement because she is accustomed to being evaluated through the reputation of Black Magic before people evaluate her personally.[2]
Her curiosity can overpower her fear. During Victor's confrontation with Evie, Alice watches his behavior transform abruptly from charming and conversational to threatening enough that his Bloodlust changes the atmosphere around him. Alice visibly shivers and immediately realizes she had been viewing him too favorably, but instead of withdrawing completely she remains interested enough to ask him to explain the meaning behind the economic metaphor he had used against the Witch Queen.[2]
Alice is also perceptive. Upon arriving at the [[Supernatural Gathering]] she immediately remarks that she expects trouble, a prediction that proves correct once the assembled rulers begin maneuvering against one another.[1] The scene should not, however, be interpreted as evidence of supernatural precognition; nothing confirms that her statement was anything more than experience and intuition.
Alice appears loyal to Evie and accepts her authority without the visible resentment shown by Selena. She accompanies the Witch Queen personally into dangerous political gatherings, follows her orders when matters involving Victor arise and addresses her as Mother. Evie, in turn, shows a degree of protectiveness toward Alice, immediately noticing when Victor's charisma begins affecting her and intervening before Alice becomes more emotionally involved in their conversation.[3][1][2]

### Position within Arcane

#### Second Daughter of the Witch Queen
Alice's title of Second Daughter is both a family designation and one of the highest political ranks among the Witches beneath Evie.[1] The Witch Queen's Daughters are not normally biological children. Rather, they are exceptional Witches accepted into Evie's personal family structure while simultaneously becoming highly ranked subordinates within Arcane.
The arrangement prevents Evie's closest followers from being treated purely as disposable servants. The title creates an acknowledged familial connection while still reflecting authority and standing inside Witch civilization.[1]
Known members of this structure include:
- [[Selena Moriarty]] — First Daughter; specialist in [[Necromancy]].
- Alice Moriarty — Second Daughter; specialist in Black [[Magic]].
- [[Hecate Moriarty]] — Third Daughter.
- Other unnamed ranked Daughters beneath them.
- [[Emily Moriarty]] — Evie's biological daughter, whose position as her actual child exists separately from the older ranked-Daughter system.
The numbering should not automatically be interpreted as biological birth order or exact age. Chapter 388 originally identifies Alice as the Third Daughter, while Chapter 510 explicitly introduces her as the Second Daughter and identifies [[Hecate]] as the Third.[3][1] Hecate's internal reaction also implies that these positions can be reassigned or replaced rather than functioning as immutable birth-order titles.

#### Black Magician
Alice's political status is supported by exceptional magical specialization rather than being ceremonial. At the [[Supernatural Gathering]], The Owner of Limbo specifically introduces her by emphasizing how deeply she has advanced into Black [[Magic]], describing her progress through the field as noteworthy enough to reveal publicly despite Evie's irritation at having one of her daughter's capabilities announced to other supernatural powers.[1]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] independently recognizes Alice as a master Black [[Magician (Flamel)|Magician]] after speaking with her.[2] Her Second Daughter status therefore combines political importance with expertise in an exceptionally dangerous form of Witchcraft.

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Alice first appears during the aftermath of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s destructive intervention in Japan. Multiple Witches had become involved in the events surrounding Ophis, and their deaths eventually reached the attention of the Witch Queen. Alice appeared before Evie dressed almost completely in black and asked how they should respond.[3]
Evie declared that regardless of the circumstances surrounding the deaths, Count Alucard would need to answer for what had happened to members of her faction and instructed Alice to summon him for an audience. Alice accepted the order without argument, establishing her as one of the elite Witches trusted to act directly on the Witch Queen's behalf in politically sensitive matters involving one of [[Nightingale]]'s [[Vampire Counts]].[3]
The scene originally calls Alice the Third Daughter of the Witch Queen.[3] This classification was subsequently superseded by Chapter 510, where Alice is explicitly introduced as the Second Daughter while [[Hecate]] is identified as Third.[1] The later ranking should therefore be treated as her canonical current position.

#### Demonic War
Alice received her full introduction when she accompanied Evie to the [[Supernatural Gathering]] attended by Earth's major supernatural rulers. The Owner of Limbo announced her as the Witch Queen's Second Daughter and publicly revealed that Alice had explored extremely deeply into Black [[Magic]].[1] Evie immediately complained that the host was revealing information about her daughter's abilities, demonstrating that Alice's level of expertise was something the Witch Queen would ordinarily prefer not to advertise.
Alice entered carrying a black staff topped by a neon-green gemstone and quietly predicted that the Gathering would become troublesome. The chapter then explained why Alice's magical specialization generated so much hostility: Black Magic focuses heavily upon interfering with the minds of living beings and includes applications involving suggestion, curses, mental control, subjugation and changes to a victim's mentality.[1]
The discipline was regarded as even more disturbing than [[Selena Moriarty]]'s [[Necromancy]]. Necromancers dealt heavily with Death and the dead, while a powerful Black [[Magician (Flamel)|Magician]] could potentially interfere directly with a living person's mind, [[Will Solace|Will]] and sense of self.[1]
Alice's appearance simultaneously clarified the nature of Evie's Daughters. [[Hecate Moriarty]], observing the Gathering from elsewhere, recognized Alice as the woman occupying the Second Daughter position. [[Hecate]]'s thoughts explain that being one of the Witch Queen's daughters represents far more than an ordinary subordinate relationship: these Witches are among Evie's closest and most important assets, with the familial terminology deliberately giving the relationship greater meaning than merely calling them servants.[1]
Alice remained beside Evie as the rulers interacted until [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] approached them. Evie began arguing with Victor over his habit of ignoring her attempts to contact him, only for Victor's attention to shift unexpectedly toward Alice.[2]
Victor asked whether Alice was physically healthy because her complexion appeared even more lifeless than that of a Vampire. Alice explained that she was fine and that the appearance was a side effect of her Magic. Victor quickly identified the nature of her specialization and understood why Evie considered Alice valuable.[2]
Alice acknowledged that her powers naturally caused people to hate or fear her. Instead of agreeing with that prejudice, Victor argued that Black Magic was hated principally because it was invasive and difficult to defend against, not because every person capable of using it was automatically malicious. He told Alice that he would judge her according to what she actually did rather than what her powers theoretically allowed her to do.[2]
The response caught Alice completely off guard. Combined with Victor's appearance and charisma, his friendly treatment caused the socially inexperienced Witch to become flustered enough to wonder privately whether he had become interested in her. The narration directly mocks the misunderstanding as the consequence of Alice having comparatively little normal social interaction.[2]
Evie noticed the direction in which Alice's thoughts were moving and quickly interrupted. She was already familiar enough with Victor's relationships to recognize the danger of allowing another inexperienced woman to misinterpret his natural behavior.[2]
Victor then returned his attention to Evie and transformed the conversation into a political warning. Through an analogy about a merchant possessing a monopoly over a product everyone required, he explained that an organization could dominate a market only while competitors lacked alternatives. Excessive greed and dependence upon monopoly would eventually motivate others either to reproduce the product themselves or remove the monopolist through force.[2]
Alice listened closely. When Victor released enough Bloodlust to expose the predatory temperament hidden beneath his earlier charm, Alice immediately shivered and realized how incomplete her first impression had been. The attractive man who had spoken kindly about her Magic was also an extraordinarily dangerous Vampire capable of changing the atmosphere around him through hostility alone.[2]
Despite the fear, Alice's curiosity remained. She asked Victor to explain the lesson behind his example and listened while he effectively warned Evie that Witch civilization's control over supernatural services and information would become increasingly dangerous if the Witch Queen continued exploiting her monopoly without considering how other factions would respond.[2]
The interaction provided Alice with an unusually direct lesson in Victor's political behavior. He did not need to threaten Evie explicitly or attack anyone; he redirected the attention, greed and dissatisfaction of an entire room of supernatural rulers simply by presenting an idea and allowing them to reach the dangerous conclusion themselves.[2]

### Current Status
Alice receives substantially less individual focus after the [[Supernatural Gathering]] than Evie, Selena, [[Hecate]] and Emily. She remains alive and no later available material establishes her death, expulsion from Arcane or replacement as a member of the Witch Queen's family.
Arcane eventually became subordinate to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s civilization after Evie surrendered control of the Witch Realm in exchange for saving [[Emily Moriarty]] from a Divine curse and poison. This necessarily changed the wider political environment in which Alice lived, but no chapter currently establishes that Alice personally became a Goddess, Dragon, Blood Dragon, member of Clan Elderblood or wife of Victor. Until such a transformation is directly shown, Alice should remain indexed according to her demonstrated state as a Human Witch and Black [[Magician (Flamel)|Magician]].

### Skills & Abilities
Alice is one of Arcane's elite Witches and a master practitioner of Black [[Magic]]. Her threat comes primarily from Mana, spellcraft and specialized supernatural effects rather than from the naturally enhanced physiology of Vampires, [[Werewolves]] or Dragons.

#### Witchcraft
- Mana Manipulation: As an elite Witch, Alice possesses advanced control over Mana and uses it as the basis of her Witchcraft.[1]
- Advanced Spellcasting: Alice has developed far enough within Witchcraft to occupy the Second Daughter position and to be publicly recognized for reaching exceptional depths within a difficult magical field.[1]
- Black [[Magic]] Mastery: Alice's principal specialization is Black Magic. The Owner of Limbo identifies her as having ventured deeply into the field, while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] independently recognizes her as a master Black [[Magician (Flamel)|Magician]].[1][2]

#### Black Magic
Black [[Magic]] is one of the most feared branches of Witchcraft because its most infamous applications directly interfere with living minds rather than merely producing external elemental attacks.[1]
Known capabilities of the magical field Alice has mastered include:
- Suggestion: Black Magic can implant or encourage thoughts and impulses inside another individual's mind.[1]
- Mind Manipulation: Advanced practitioners can directly interfere with a target's mental state.[1]
- Mental Subjugation: Black Magic can be used to suppress or dominate another person's mind and behavior.[1]
- Personality/Mentality Alteration: The field is capable of changing aspects of a victim's mentality rather than merely creating temporary illusions.[1]
- Curse Magic: Curses are included among the known applications associated with Black Magic.[1]
Alice is explicitly a master of this field, but the novel has not personally shown her casting every one of these effects against a target. They should therefore be understood as the established capabilities of the branch of Magic she has mastered rather than a list of individually demonstrated named spells.

#### Mental Interference
Alice's chosen specialization potentially allows her to bypass the normal advantages of physically stronger opponents by attacking the mind instead of the body. This is one of the principal reasons Black [[Magic]] produces such severe fear among other supernatural races.[1]
The danger is conceptual rather than based purely upon destructive output: a target physically capable of surviving enormous attacks may still consider powers capable of controlling thoughts, altering mentality or imposing subjugation exceptionally threatening.

#### Curse Magic
Curse [[Magic]] exists as an established component of advanced Black Magic.[1] The exact curses Alice personally knows, their activation conditions and their effects have not yet been demonstrated, so specific unsupported curses should not be assigned to her.

#### Magic Staff
Alice carries a black staff topped with a neon-green gemstone.[1] It is presumably used as a magical focus or tool, but no chapter has revealed its name, origin, enchantments or unique abilities.

#### Magical Side Effects
Alice's Black [[Magic]] has affected her own body. Her extremely pale, ashen complexion is explicitly stated to be a side effect of her Magic.[2]
The precise mechanism remains unexplained. It is unknown whether the change results from long-term exposure to Black Magic, a particular technique, accumulated corruption, a magical adaptation or another process.

#### Power Classification
Alice remains biologically Human and should not be classified as a God simply because of her combat level. An Author-provided auxiliary power hierarchy from the Chapter 630 period placed elite faction members such as [[Selena Moriarty]], Alice Moriarty and [[Emily Moriarty]] broadly within the same combat category as Mid-Level Gods.[4]
This classification represents overall combat threat rather than species. The same hierarchy emphasizes that power categories do not guarantee victory because technique, experience, compatibility, preparation, psychological warfare and unusual abilities can allow a lower-ranked Being to defeat someone with greater raw power.[4]
The classification is also historical. It represents Alice around the period in which the hierarchy was published and should not automatically be treated as a precise measurement of her current maximum after the enormous later changes to Arcane and the supernatural world.

### Strengths
- Highly Invasive [[Magic]]: Alice specializes in a magical discipline capable of attacking the mind rather than depending entirely upon damaging an opponent's body.[1]
- Difficult-to-Defend Effects: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly recognizes that much of the fear directed toward Black Magic comes from how dangerous and difficult its effects can be to counter.[2]
- Political Rank: As Second Daughter, Alice occupies one of the highest positions beneath the Witch Queen and possesses access to Arcane's ruling structure.[1]
- Specialization: Alice did not merely learn Black Magic casually; her progress through the field is exceptional enough to receive public recognition from the Owner of Limbo.[1]
- Composure: Although Victor's behavior can fluster or frighten her, Alice is capable of recovering quickly enough to continue observing and questioning an extremely dangerous situation.[2]
- Curiosity and Analysis: Alice remains focused upon understanding unfamiliar political and supernatural behavior even when personally uncomfortable.[2]

### Limitations
Alice remains a Human Witch. Unlike Vampires, [[Werewolves]] or other naturally superhuman species, her greatest advantages come from [[Magic]] rather than extraordinary baseline physical physiology. This makes spellcasting, preparation and maintaining control of an encounter particularly important.
Her specialization also creates a social disadvantage. Black Magic is widely hated precisely because of its ability to interfere with the minds and identities of living beings, meaning people who understand Alice's powers may treat her as dangerous before she has personally acted against them.[1][2]
Alice's limited ordinary social experience is another demonstrated weakness. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s relatively simple kindness and charisma caused her to misinterpret his attention as possible romantic interest despite her intelligence and magical experience.[2] This is a social weakness rather than a combat limitation.
No supernatural Foresight has been demonstrated. Alice saying that she expected trouble at the [[Supernatural Gathering]] reflects a reasonable prediction in a room filled with hostile rulers and should not be classified as precognition without further evidence.[1]

### Relationships

#### Evie Moriarty
Evie Moriarty is Alice's adoptive or title-based mother, sovereign and direct superior. Alice addresses her as Mother and is trusted enough to stand beside her during meetings involving the most powerful supernatural rulers on Earth.[1]
The familial terminology is meaningful even though Alice is not Evie's biological child. Evie's ranked daughters represent elite Witches she brought into a relationship deeper than ordinary service, while simultaneously using their numbered positions as part of Arcane's hierarchy.[1]
Alice appears considerably more openly loyal to Evie than Selena. She immediately accepts Evie's order to summon [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] following the Japan incident and later accompanies her personally to the [[Supernatural Gathering]].[3][1]
Evie is also protective of Alice. During Victor's conversation with her, Evie quickly realizes that Alice is becoming flustered by Victor's charm and intervenes before the interaction develops further.[2]

#### Selena Moriarty
[[Selena Moriarty]] is Alice's elder adoptive sister and occupies the First Daughter position above her. Both specialize in branches of [[Magic]] associated with fear and taboo, although their Fields differ substantially.
Selena specializes principally in [[Necromancy]], while Alice specializes in Black Magic.[1] Black Magic is described as being even more hated than Necromancy because Alice's field can directly interfere with the minds and behavior of living beings rather than focusing primarily upon Death and the dead.[1]
Their ranks place Selena and Alice among the highest political figures beneath Evie inside the traditional Witch hierarchy.

#### Hecate Moriarty
[[Hecate Moriarty]] is Alice's younger adoptive sister and the current Third Daughter of the Witch Queen. [[Hecate]]'s thoughts during Alice's introduction provide much of the explanation surrounding the ranked Daughter system and establish Alice as the Witch occupying the Second Daughter position.[1]
The wording is particularly important because Chapter 388 had originally referred to Alice as the Third Daughter.[3] Chapter 510 instead positions Alice as Second and Hecate as Third, making the later arrangement the current canonical hierarchy.
Hecate's reaction also suggests that Daughter positions function as offices capable of changing rather than immutable labels determined strictly by birth order.

#### Emily Moriarty
[[Emily Moriarty]] is Alice's younger sister through Evie's family but differs fundamentally from the ranked Daughters because Emily is Evie's actual biological child.
Alice, Selena and [[Hecate]] belong to the political, adoptive and familial hierarchy constructed around the Witch Queen, whereas Emily's relationship with Evie exists by birth. This distinction becomes increasingly important as Emily grows into Arcane's heir and one of its greatest magical talents.

#### Albedo Moriarty Elderblood
[[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]] is Evie's biological mother and therefore occupies the position of Alice's adoptive or title-based grandmother through the Moriarty family structure. There is currently no substantial direct interaction between Alice and Albedo shown in the available chapters.
Alice should therefore not be assigned any powers, training or personal relationship with Albedo beyond the family connection created through Evie unless later material establishes one.

#### Victor Elderblood
Alice first interacted directly with [[Victor Elderblood]] during the [[Supernatural Gathering]]. Victor initially treated her with unexpected concern, asking about her ghost-like complexion and then discussing her Black [[Magic]] without the immediate disgust Alice was accustomed to receiving.[2]
Victor's refusal to judge her solely through the danger of her Magic affected Alice strongly. Combined with his appearance and charisma, the conversation led her to wonder whether his attention represented romantic interest. The misunderstanding was temporary and no romantic relationship between Alice and Victor has been established.[2]
Her perception of Victor changed rapidly once he confronted Evie. Victor deliberately exposed enough hostility to remind everyone that the charming man speaking casually with Alice was also a violent and extremely dangerous Vampire. Alice shivered under the shift but continued following the argument closely and asked him to explain his reasoning.[2]
There is no evidence that Alice later joined Victor's harem, married him or became an Elderblood. Her current connection to him is indirect through Evie, Arcane and the broader political structure that eventually came under Victor's rule.
