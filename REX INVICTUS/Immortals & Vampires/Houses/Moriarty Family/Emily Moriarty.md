### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 538
Admitting defeat isn't bad, you know? You always tell me that failure is just a way of learning.
—— Emily Moriarty to Evie Moriarty.[11]
Emily Moriarty is the biological daughter, disciple and intended successor of Evie Moriarty, the Witch Queen, and the maternal granddaughter of [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]]. She occupies a unique position within the Moriarty family: unlike [[Selena Moriarty]], [[Alice Moriarty]], [[Hecate Moriarty]] and the other women recognized through Evie's political, adoptive or hierarchical system of "Daughters", Emily is explicitly the Witch Queen's biological child and was raised within the highest levels of Witch society as the future heir to her mother's civilization.[12][11]
Emily is one of the greatest natural magical prodigies introduced among the Witches. At only twelve years old, her Mana reserves were already compared with those of Selena, Evie's First Daughter, despite Selena possessing more than two thousand years of accumulated magical development.[1] Her talent was not limited to raw Mana. By the cross-Reality experiments of Chapter 831, Evie regarded Emily as the only woman in Arcane whom she considered an equal when discussing [[Magic]], an extraordinary assessment given Evie's own age, knowledge and position as Witch Queen.[8] This comparison concerns Emily's natural comprehension, magical analysis and ability to participate in advanced research rather than automatically making their overall experience identical.
Emily first entered the wider supernatural conflict during the Demonic War, when she participated in Evie's secret arrangement with [[Diablo]] and posed as one of his prisoners alongside [[Lilith Tepes]] and [[Thomas Fenrir]].[1] Her encounters with [[Victor Elderblood]] during the resulting rescue operation introduced her to supernatural powers far outside the sheltered environment in which she had been raised, while Victor took enough interest in her spellcraft to observe and memorize her Teleportation Array and later jokingly designate her his "eldest disciple".[13][7][14]
Emily later became directly involved in the dimensional research that connected the original Arcane Realm to the separate Magic World, the Reality eventually conquered by the Witches and renamed or referred to as Arcane during the late Imperial era.[15][2] As Arcane's political situation deteriorated, Emily became one of the strongest voices urging Evie to cooperate with Victor rather than continue accepting increasingly unfavorable relationships with stronger supernatural factions.[16]
The issue became personal when Emily was afflicted by a combination of Divine Poison and Curse powers that Evie could not cure. Evie ultimately surrendered herself, her civilization and its political independence to Victor in exchange for her daughter's survival. Victor removed the affliction and Arcane subsequently entered the growing [[Elderblood Empire]].[6]
By Chapter 1179, Emily has physically matured into an adult woman and remains part of Arcane's ruling household beneath Evie.[2] She later witnesses Arcane's entire Lower Sector being drawn into Victor's expanded Mid-Level Sector alongside [[Nightingale]] and Samar, while her long-standing relationship with Thomas develops into confirmed mutual romantic interest.[3] Chapter 1182 further establishes that Emily considers Thomas sufficiently important to reject any suggestion of pursuing Victor romantically, referring to herself as already having a "lover", although the immediately preceding chapter clarifies that she and Thomas had not yet formalized their relationship.[3][4]

### Physical Appearance
At her debut, Emily was a twelve-year-old girl with pale skin, long black hair generally arranged into twin tails, a fringe covering part of her forehead and vivid blue eyes. Her most unusual visual characteristic is the manifestation of intricate magical structures resembling [[Magic]] Circles around her eyes. These formations become visible or extend outward when Emily concentrates intensely on Magic and can also appear when her emotions become particularly strong.[7][3]
During the Demonic War, Emily wore a red dress decorated with golden details. Despite its appearance, the dress was an extremely sophisticated magical artifact personally prepared by Evie to compensate for Emily's comparatively vulnerable Human body. Numerous protective enchantments had been layered into the clothing and were powered by a rare magical stone from the Witch Queen's collection containing an enormous independent reserve of Energy.[1]
Chapter 1179 explicitly describes Emily as a grown woman.[2] Her complete adult appearance has not yet received the same detailed description as her childhood form, so her current hairstyle, clothing and exact height should not be extrapolated solely from older artwork. Chapter 1182 provides one useful clarification: Emily considers herself to be of approximately average height and objects when Albedo continues calling her "little".[4] Her characteristic magical ocular circles remain present in adulthood, visibly appearing again when she becomes emotionally determined while speaking about Thomas.[3]

### Personality
Emily is intelligent, cheerful, proud, stubborn, outspoken, competitive and intensely curious. Her childhood personality reflected both her immense magical talent and the sheltered confidence of someone raised as the biological daughter and future heir of the Witch Queen. When first introduced, she proudly announced her full identity and parentage despite operating in hostile territory, prompting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to criticize her for carelessly revealing information that could be used against her. Emily initially became angry at the reprimand but ultimately understood his reasoning, demonstrating that although she dislikes being corrected, she can recognize a valid argument once her pride subsides.[12][17]
Her confidence in her [[Magic]] was equally pronounced. Evie specifically warned Emily about Victor's abnormal charisma before the two met and equipped her daughter with powerful mental defenses, yet Emily assumed that sufficient discipline and enchantment would allow her to ignore him. Discovering that these preparations were inadequate frustrated her considerably and provided an early lesson in the difference between possessing exceptional theoretical knowledge and encountering supernatural anomalies in practice.[1][13]
Emily's stubbornness is balanced by a strong capacity for rational analysis. She is willing to contradict Evie when she believes her mother's pride or personal biases are interfering with sound judgment. During the cross-Reality portal experiment, Emily sensed that the Ritual was behaving incorrectly and advised Evie to terminate the connection while doing so was still possible.[8] Later, when Arcane was pressured by stronger factions, Emily openly questioned Evie's refusal to seek Victor's assistance and argued that owing Victor a favor was preferable to allowing the Witches to become politically dependent upon powers whose intentions were considerably less favorable.[16] When Evie personally entered negotiations with Yama and [[Merlin]], Emily went further and declared that the Witches would monitor the meeting and intervene if her mother was endangered whether Evie approved or not.[18]
Emily also possesses a mischievous side, particularly when Evie's dignity is involved. After Victor politically outmaneuvered the Witch Queen, Emily openly teased her mother and reminded her that Evie herself had taught that failure should be treated as a method of learning.[11] She eagerly accepted Victor jokingly calling her his "eldest disciple" largely because the title annoyed Evie.[14]
Her behavior around [[Thomas Fenrir]] has always been more emotional. The pair began by constantly arguing as children, with Thomas repeatedly provoking Emily through the nickname "Flat Earth". Emily responded with predictable anger but demonstrated genuine concern when Thomas was poisoned by a modified form of Wolfsbane and immediately attempted to help him.[12][17] This competitive attachment survived into adulthood. When Thomas's feelings eventually became romantic, Emily reciprocated rather than dismissing him as merely a childhood companion.[10][3]
Adult Emily is more socially composed than the child Victor first encountered but retains the same intensity. She recovers quickly from surprise, speaks directly to powerful members of her family and is considerably more willing to state what she wants. When Evie questions her relationship with Thomas, Emily admits that they are not formally together but declares that she intends to "get him" once he stops being stubborn; her eyes begin shining and her magical circles manifest while she confidently insists he will not break her heart.[3]
Chapter 1182 further demonstrates Emily's independence. When Albedo attempts to involve both Evie and Emily in the possibility of joining Victor's harem, Emily bluntly states that she already has someone and has no romantic interest in the Emperor. Even when confronted by Violet's intense devotion to Victor, Emily clarifies that she is not denying Victor's attractiveness but simply does not desire him romantically.[4] After returning to Arcane, she also establishes boundaries with Evie concerning her relationship with Thomas, explicitly banning her mother from conducting experiments and reserving the right to refuse overly sensitive questions. Evie accepts both conditions.[4]

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Emily first appears during the Demonic War among the prisoners connected to [[Diablo]]'s plans, where she is held alongside [[Lilith Tepes]] and [[Thomas Fenrir]]. She introduces herself proudly as Emily Moriarty, the biological daughter of the Witch Queen and immediately begins arguing with Thomas, who is approximately her age and repeatedly mocks her lack of physical development.[12] Her captivity is later revealed to be staged rather than genuine. Evie had entered a covert arrangement with Diablo, and Emily was participating in the deception rather than being an ordinary hostage.[1]
While maintaining the appearance of captivity, Emily wears an enchanted dress created by Evie and communicates secretly with her mother. Evie warns her that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is approaching and specifically cautions Emily about the abnormal supernatural effect created by his appearance and presence. Emily believes her mental discipline and protective enchantments will be sufficient, but Victor affects her almost immediately after arriving, leaving the normally confident girl frustrated by her inability to completely ignore him.[1][13]
Emily observes Victor's confrontation with [[Agares]] and sees him transform even comparatively basic [[Magic]] such as Lumos into something far beyond its conventional use. Victor's later high-speed transportation also leaves Emily severely motion-sick, an embarrassingly mundane weakness that her enormous Mana reserves do nothing to prevent.[13][17]
When Thomas is discovered suffering from a modified Wolfsbane poison, Emily immediately assists in examining his condition. Her concern demonstrates that their constant arguing does not represent genuine hostility.[17] During the same period, Victor reprimands Emily for casually revealing her identity as the Witch Queen's biological daughter to people she barely knows. Emily initially dislikes being lectured but eventually accepts that Victor is correct about the danger of unnecessarily exposing politically valuable information.[17]
Her magical talent becomes much more obvious during the group's stay together. After Victor previously uses Silence to protect a private conversation, Emily enters the room and immediately notices residual traces of the spell. Magical circles form around her eyes as she examines the area, after which she casts [[Poseidon|the Master]]-Level spell Appraisal on Lilith to investigate her condition.[7] Emily subsequently constructs a Teleportation Array capable of establishing a fixed connection with Evie's location. She explains the difference between Witch teleportation and the natural Space-Time abilities of [[Clan Alioth]] while Victor observes the Array closely enough to memorize its magical construction after seeing it once.[7]
Evie later arrives and enters a prolonged political confrontation with Victor. Emily takes obvious amusement in watching her mother repeatedly lose control of the negotiation, and Victor eventually begins jokingly referring to Emily as his "eldest disciple". Emily embraces the title largely because of how much it irritates Evie.[14]
After returning to Arcane, Emily continues teasing her mother over the outcome. When Evie remains frustrated by her defeat, Emily repeats one of Evie's own lessons back to her: failure is another method of learning.[11] Their conversation also reveals the larger objective that shaped much of Witch civilization. Evie intended to locate another suitable world or Reality where the Witches could build a civilization less vulnerable to persecution and political pressure from stronger supernatural powers. As Evie's biological heir, Emily knew of this ambition and was expected to continue it if her mother could not.

#### King Among Kings
The Witches eventually approach this centuries-old objective through a dimensional project conducted by [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]], Evie, Emily and Arcane's researchers. Rather than traveling between familiar dimensions, their Ritual attempts to form a connection with an entirely different inhabited cosmic Reality.[19][15] Emily works directly beside her mother, supplying [[Magic]], observing the active structure and helping monitor the stability of the portal.
Before the Ritual fully stabilizes, Emily develops a strong sense that something is wrong. She determines that the connection remains reversible and advises Evie to shut the project down before proceeding further.[8] Evie takes the warning seriously because Emily's magical ability is not treated as childish speculation; the narration explicitly identifies her as a magical genius and states that Evie considers Emily the only woman in Arcane equal to herself when discussing Magic.[8] Nevertheless, the experiment continues.
Emily's concern proves justified when the inhabitants of the other Reality begin interacting with the portal from their side. What the Witches intended as a controlled exploration route becomes a potential invasion pathway, and Emily participates in stabilizing and analyzing the rapidly changing magical structures surrounding the connection.[20] [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually passes through the portal and discovers that the destination contains its own civilization, Gods and supernatural systems.
The situation becomes still more dangerous because the two Realities operate under different temporal conditions. Their Space-Time structures begin affecting one another through the artificial connection, escalating the experiment beyond ordinary Witchcraft until [[The Universal Tree]] and [[The Judges of The Abyss]] are forced to intervene.[21] Emily's warning before the portal stabilized becomes one of the clearest demonstrations that her magical judgment could identify a catastrophic problem even while older and more experienced researchers remained focused on the discovery itself.
The separate Reality discovered through this experiment is the Magic World. It should not be confused with the original Arcane Realm where the portal was created. The Witches later conquer and develop the Magic World, and by the late Imperial era its principal Witch-controlled planet is referred to simply as Arcane or the Planet of the Witches.[2]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
The discovery of the [[Magic]] World does not initially provide the independent sanctuary the Witches hoped for. Instead, Arcane's unusual dimensional knowledge and strategic value attract increasingly powerful factions. Yama and [[Merlin]] become directly involved while stronger Pantheons offer arrangements that could gradually place the Witches beneath their influence.[16][18]
Emily recognizes the political danger and directly questions Evie's refusal to ask [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] for assistance. From her perspective, Evie had previously cooperated with [[Diablo]] when doing so was advantageous, while Victor had repeatedly demonstrated greater strength and a substantially more favorable attitude toward the Witches. Selena reaches a similar conclusion.[16] Emily's argument reflects the pragmatic judgment she developed beyond her childhood: independence has little value if preserving the appearance of independence forces Arcane into agreements that leave it weaker and more dependent.
When Evie chooses to personally negotiate with Yama and Merlin because normal precautions are unreliable against them, Emily refuses to leave her mother completely unprotected. She declares that the other Witches will monitor the discussion and intervene if Evie is endangered regardless of the Witch Queen's objections.[18]
During this broader era, Thomas's feelings toward Emily become explicitly romantic. After Tasha encourages him to consider his own future rather than living solely around the political responsibilities of the Werewolf Royal Family, Thomas describes a powerful young Witch with unusual eyes and eventually admits that the woman he is interested in is Emily Moriarty.[10] At this stage the narration establishes Thomas's attraction, while Emily's reciprocal feelings are only confirmed much later.

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Emily eventually becomes the catalyst for the end of Arcane's independent political era. By Chapter 990 she is afflicted by a supernatural condition produced through Divine authorities connected to Poison and Curses. Evie's [[Magic]], accumulated knowledge and resources prove incapable of curing the condition, leaving the Witch Queen unable to save her biological daughter.[6]
Evie ultimately turns to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. When Victor demands effectively everything in exchange for solving the problem, Evie accepts without hesitation because Emily's life matters more to her than Arcane, her throne or nearly two thousand years of accomplishments. Victor examines Emily and removes the affliction with ease compared with the difficulty it presented to the Witches.[6] The event permanently changes the relationship between Arcane and Victor: the Witches enter his sphere rather than remaining an entirely independent supernatural power, while the dangerous portal and the knowledge surrounding the other Reality are placed beneath Imperial control.
The original Witch ambition of reaching another world is subsequently realized in a manner Evie could not have predicted. Victor eventually places the development and expansion of the Magic World beneath Evie's responsibility, allowing the Witches to build the civilization they had spent centuries seeking while backed by the protection and resources of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Emily remains part of the ruling household as the Witch Queen's heir.
Albedo's later acceptance into Victor's family also makes Victor Emily's step-grandfather through her maternal grandmother.[9] The passage of Time and the massive developmental eras that follow eventually carry Emily from the twelve-year-old child introduced during the Demonic War into adulthood, although no chapter provides a precise numerical value for her current personal age.

#### Those Who Rule Reality
By Chapter 1179, the world ruled by the Witches has transformed into an advanced magical civilization under Imperial influence. The location is introduced as a Lower Sector: Planet of the Witches. Magical devices are embedded throughout the cities, Witches move through the sky using magical circles, talking animals and enormous supernatural creatures live openly among the population, magical blood has spread throughout the civilization and even Demigods can be found among its inhabitants.[2] The world is now referred to as Arcane, reflecting the Witch civilization's complete domination of the former [[Magic]] World.
[[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]] returns shortly before the planned merger of the Empire's subordinate Lower Sectors. During Albedo's conversation with Evie, Emily enters the throne room and is explicitly described as now a grown woman. Although initially surprised by her grandmother's unexpected appearance, Emily rapidly recovers and politely welcomes her back to Arcane.[2] Albedo asks her to stop calling her "Grandmother" because she considers herself too young for the title, while Evie privately reflects that Albedo is actually older than many Human generations.
The merger reaches its decisive stage in Chapter 1181. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] assumes a colossal Dragon form while drawing the Lower Sectors of Samar, [[Nightingale]] and Arcane toward his Mid-Level Sector. Emily watches the enormous Dragon visible across Arcane's sky and remarks that the sight still amazes her every time.[3] Victor subsequently draws all three Realities into his sphere of influence while entire galaxies from the Lower Sectors enter his expanding boundary.
The same chapter finally confirms Emily's reciprocal interest in Thomas. When Albedo attempts to involve Emily in her plans concerning Victor, Emily immediately claims that she is already "in a relationship". Albedo remembers the young Werewolf connected to Tasha, while Evie is surprised because Emily had never told her she was seeing anyone. Emily then admits that she and Thomas are not actually together yet, but states that she will get him once he stops being so stubborn.[3] Evie tells her not to come crying if Thomas breaks her heart, prompting Emily to insist that he will not and that she will personally make certain of it. Her magical circles visibly manifest around her eyes while she speaks.[3]
Albedo regards Thomas as an acceptable choice once she remembers that he is Tasha's son and therefore already connected to the extended Elderblood family, although she warns Emily that hurting him would create problems with Tasha.[3] The scene establishes mutual attraction because Thomas had independently admitted his feelings for Emily centuries earlier, while Emily now explicitly declares her intention to pursue him.[10][3]
Albedo nevertheless drags Evie and Emily into the Dragon's Nest to speak with [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] about her belief that they should consider Victor. Chapter 1182 resolves the matter more clearly. When Violet asks about Emily, she states that she already has a lover and is not romantically interested in the Emperor.[4] The terminology is more definitive than her statement only moments earlier in Chapter 1181, but should be read alongside her own clarification there that she and Thomas were not formally together yet. The safest description of their current status is therefore mutual romantic interest with Emily actively treating Thomas as her chosen partner rather than a formally confirmed marriage or established couple.
Violet accepts loyalty to an existing romantic partner as understandable but objects to Emily claiming no interest whatsoever in Victor. Emily quickly clarifies that she means only that she is not romantically interested in him. Violet then gives her and Evie an album from her private collection of Victor photographs; both women become visibly embarrassed while viewing it, but nothing in the encounter establishes that Emily's romantic preference changes.[4]
Once the meeting ends, a blonde maid transports Evie and Emily directly back to Arcane with such effortless spatial movement that both assume she must have been one of Victor's wives. Evie's attention immediately returns to Thomas and she insists upon learning everything about Emily's relationship. Emily attempts to minimize the situation as something temporary and, recognizing exactly what kind of researcher her mother is, immediately establishes two rules: no experiments and the right to refuse sensitive questions. Evie considers the conditions and accepts them.[4]
At approximately the same time, Victor completes the post-merger restructuring of the imported territories. Nightingale and Samar must remain within their original Solar Systems, while Arcane's planet can be relocated independently. Victor places the three territories inside a heavily protected central region of the Empire and reorganizes the surrounding younger galaxies so they can continue developing safely.[4] Emily therefore currently resides within a Witch-ruled world that is no longer an external Lower Sector but a protected planetary domain physically incorporated into Victor's enlarged Mid-Level Sector.

### Skills & Abilities
Emily is an exceptionally gifted Witch whose greatest confirmed strengths are her enormous Mana reserves, magical comprehension, spell analysis and ability to perform advanced Witchcraft at an extraordinarily young age. Her current adult maximum remains unknown because her latest appearances focus upon Arcane's merger and her family rather than direct combat.
- [[Magic]] / Prodigious Witchcraft: Emily possesses an extraordinary natural affinity for Magic. At twelve years old she could already perform techniques associated with far more experienced Witches, and Evie later regarded her as the only woman in Arcane equal to herself when discussing Magic.[1][8]
- Mana Manipulation: Emily possesses unusually refined control over Mana despite her youth, allowing her to construct advanced Magic Circles, participate in large Rituals and operate Master-Level spells.[1][7][8]
- Enormous Mana Reserves: At only twelve years old, Emily's Mana Pool was compared with that of [[Selena Moriarty]], Evie's First Daughter, despite Selena possessing more than two thousand years of magical development.[1]
- Magical Genius: Emily can rapidly understand active magical systems, identify inconsistencies and provide meaningful technical judgments even while working beside ancient Witches. Evie's statement in Chapter 831 identifies her comprehension rather than merely her raw Energy as exceptional.[8]
- Magic Detection: Emily can perceive extremely faint magical residue. After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Silence spell had already ended, Emily entered the affected room and immediately detected traces left behind by the technique.[7]
- Magical Analysis: Emily can inspect active spells, residual magical structures and complex Rituals while they are operating. She analyzed Victor's residual Magic and later recognized dangerous irregularities within Arcane's cross-Reality portal before its activation was complete.[7][8]
- Ocular Magic Circles: Intricate Magic Circles can manifest around Emily's eyes while she concentrates on Magic or becomes emotionally intense. The phenomenon remains present in adulthood, appearing again in Chapter 1181 when she speaks about Thomas.[7][3] No canonical name has been given to this trait, so it should not be treated as an independently named ocular power without further evidence.
- Appraisal: Emily can cast [[Poseidon|the Master]]-Level spell Appraisal to inspect the condition of another being. She used it on [[Lilith Tepes]] after the Demonic War rescue operation.[7]
- Teleportation Magic: Emily can construct Witch-style Teleportation Arrays linking predetermined locations. She created one side of an Array while Evie established the corresponding endpoint, allowing the Witch Queen to travel directly to Emily's position.[7] Emily explains that this fixed-point system differs from the much freer Space-Time manipulation naturally available to [[Clan Alioth]].
- Ritual Magic: Emily possesses advanced knowledge of large-scale collaborative Rituals and directly participated in the project connecting Arcane to another cosmic Reality. She supplied Magic, monitored the active formation and determined that the connection remained reversible before final stabilization.[8][20]
- Dimensional Magic Knowledge: Through the Witches' portal research, Emily developed practical knowledge of Magic capable of establishing links between separate cosmic Realities.[15][8][21] This is not evidence that Emily possesses unrestricted personal interdimensional travel; the demonstrated connection required a massive collaborative Ritual and specialized Arcane infrastructure.
- Space-Time Phenomena Analysis: Emily participated in studying a portal whose connected Realities possessed incompatible temporal conditions, giving her direct experience with magical structures capable of interfering with both Space and Time.[8][21] The phenomenon ultimately surpassed the Witches' safe level of control.
- Medical and Diagnostic Knowledge: Emily possesses sufficient supernatural and magical knowledge to assist in examining unusual physical conditions. She immediately offered assistance when Thomas was discovered suffering from modified Wolfsbane, while Appraisal gives her a direct magical diagnostic method.[17][7]
- Strategic Intelligence: Emily demonstrates political judgment beyond laboratory Magic. She recognized that Arcane's increasingly unfavorable agreements with powerful Pantheons threatened genuine independence, advocated seeking Victor's assistance and refused to allow Evie to negotiate with dangerous entities completely unobserved.[16][18]
- Research Aptitude: Emily has participated in some of the most ambitious projects of Witch civilization from a young age and can work productively beside Evie and Albedo. Her ability to question assumptions rather than simply follow senior researchers was essential during the portal incident.[19][8]

#### Enchanted Dress
During her childhood, Emily's red dress functioned as a sophisticated protective magical artifact created by Evie. Its multiple enchantments were supplied by an extraordinarily rare magical stone containing an immense independent reserve of Energy, allowing the equipment to maintain defenses without forcing Emily to sustain every effect personally.[1]
- Mind Manipulation Resistance: The dress contains enchantments intended to protect Emily's mind from supernatural interference and control.[1]
- Soul Protection: The artifact possesses defensive [[Magic]] directed toward influences or attacks targeting Emily's Soul.[1]
- Behavioral Manipulation Resistance: Its protections include safeguards against supernatural powers intended to alter or control Emily's behavior.[1]
- Corruption Resistance: The dress was specifically designed to protect Emily from harmful Demonic Miasma and similar corruptive supernatural influences.[1]
- Elemental Resistance: Multiple enchantments protect Emily against Elemental attacks.[1]
- Physical Protection: Defensive wards compensate for the physical vulnerability of Emily's Human body and protect her from direct harm.[1]
- Independent Energy Supply: A rare magical stone embedded within the equipment provides the enchantments with their own enormous Energy source.[1]
These protections are powerful but not absolute. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s abnormal Charm affected Emily despite Evie's mental defenses, establishing that sufficiently powerful or unconventional effects can bypass or overwhelm at least some of the equipment's enchantments.[13] It has not been confirmed that adult Emily continues using the same dress.

#### Power Classification and Limitations
An Author-provided hierarchy dating from around the Chapter 630 period placed Emily alongside elite Witches such as Selena and Alice within the broad Mid-Level God combat category.[5] This classification does not mean that Emily was literally a Goddess; she remained a Human Witch. It represented a combat bracket attributed to her during that period and was particularly remarkable because Emily possessed only a fraction of the age and practical experience of the ancient Witches listed beside her.
The ranking is extremely outdated relative to current events and should not be treated as Emily's present maximum. Chapters 1179–1182 establish that she has become an adult after a substantial passage of Time, but none of those chapters supplies a modern combat feat or comparative statement sufficient to determine her current destructive capacity.[2][3][4]
Emily's greatest childhood limitation was the gap between extraordinary magical talent and practical experience. She could possess Mana comparable to ancient Witches and perform Master-Level [[Magic]] while still lacking the centuries of combat, diplomacy and survival experience accumulated by people such as Evie and Selena. Her confidence that preparation alone would allow her to resist [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is one direct example of this weakness.[1][13] That limitation should not simply be applied unchanged to her adult self; the novel has not yet demonstrated enough of current Emily's abilities to determine how much experience she accumulated during the later eras.[2][3][4]

### Relationships

#### Evie Moriarty
Evie Moriarty is Emily's biological mother, magical master and the ruler who raised her as the future heir of Witch civilization. Their relationship is fundamentally different from Evie's connections with the women carrying the ceremonial or adopted title of Daughter. Emily knows aspects of Evie's plans and personality unavailable to most Witches and is comfortable teasing, criticizing or directly opposing the Witch Queen when she believes her mother is wrong.[11][8]
Evie also possesses extraordinary respect for Emily's magical talent. By Chapter 831 she considers Emily the only woman in Arcane equal to herself in discussions of [[Magic]], despite Emily's youth.[8] This respect does not prevent them from arguing; if anything, Emily's intelligence makes her one of the few people capable of challenging Evie's conclusions on technical rather than emotional grounds.
Emily's importance to her mother is demonstrated most decisively in Chapter 990. When Divine Poison and Curse powers place Emily beyond Evie's ability to cure, Evie willingly gives [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] herself, Arcane and the independence she defended for almost two thousand years in exchange for saving her daughter.[6] Emily's survival therefore ranks above Evie's throne, pride and political ambitions.
Their adult relationship remains affectionate but argumentative. Evie is genuinely surprised in Chapter 1181 to discover that Emily has been pursuing Thomas without telling her.[3] After returning to Arcane in Chapter 1182, Evie immediately demands every detail about the romance. Emily knows her mother well enough to prohibit experiments before the questioning begins and insists upon the right to refuse sensitive questions. Evie accepts both conditions and describes her daughter's love as something worth celebrating.[4]

#### Albedo Moriarty Elderblood
[[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]] is Emily's maternal grandmother and one of the foundational figures in the history of the Witches. Emily descends directly from Albedo's magical lineage through Evie, while Albedo later becomes a member of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family.[9]
Albedo was also involved in the research that eventually connected Arcane to the [[Magic]] World. Emily's participation placed three generations of the Moriarty line within the same project: Albedo supplied ancient magical knowledge, Evie led the Witch civilization and Emily contributed as the young prodigy whose technical judgment eventually warned that the portal was becoming dangerous.[19][8]
Their later interactions are considerably more comedic. In Chapter 1179 Emily politely greets Albedo as her grandmother, prompting Albedo to insist that she is too young to be called "Grandmother".[2] During Chapter 1181, Albedo interferes directly in Emily's romantic life, initially criticizing Thomas before deciding he is an acceptable choice because he is Tasha's son and therefore already belongs to the extended Imperial family. She nevertheless warns Emily not to hurt him because dealing with Tasha afterward would be troublesome.[3]
Albedo's interference ultimately drags Emily and Evie into an audience with Violet in Chapter 1182. Emily emerges from the experience with an even greater understanding of why Evie considers Albedo dangerous to her peace of mind.[4]

#### Victor Elderblood
Emily first encounters [[Victor Elderblood]] as the dangerous Vampire Evie specifically warned her about during the Demonic War. She expects her magical protections to let her resist his supernatural presence, fails almost immediately and becomes increasingly irritated by how strongly he affects her despite her preparations.[1][13] Victor responds by treating her less like a princess and more like a talented but inexperienced child, calling her "Little Witch" and reprimanding her when she carelessly reveals strategically important information.[17]
Victor also takes genuine interest in Emily's [[Magic]]. He observes her construction of a Teleportation Array, questions its mechanics and memorizes the structure after seeing it once.[7] He later jokingly calls Emily his "eldest disciple", a designation Emily embraces largely because it irritates Evie.[14]
As Emily becomes increasingly involved in Arcane's political problems, her opinion of Victor becomes considerably more pragmatic. She repeatedly argues that seeking his assistance is preferable to becoming dependent upon more hostile Pantheons.[16] Victor ultimately validates that judgment by saving Emily's life when Evie's accumulated Witchcraft cannot remove the Divine poison and curses afflicting her.[6]
Through Albedo's later relationship with Victor, he also becomes Emily's step-grandfather.[9] Emily subsequently witnesses the enormous scale of what Victor becomes, including his Galaxy-sized Dragon form during the merger of Arcane, Samar and [[Nightingale]]. Even after living within his Empire for a long period, she admits that the sight still amazes her.[3]
Albedo attempts to push Emily toward Victor romantically during Chapters 1181–1182, but Emily rejects the idea. She states that she already has Thomas and does not consider herself romantically interested in the Emperor.[4] Violet's subsequent attempt to demonstrate Victor's attractiveness embarrasses Emily, but the chapter does not establish any change in her romantic intentions.

#### Thomas Fenrir
[[Thomas Fenrir]] is one of Emily's oldest relationships outside the Moriarty family. They met as children during the Demonic War and almost immediately developed a dynamic based upon constant teasing and arguments. Thomas coined the nickname "Flat Earth" to mock Emily's childish lack of physical development, reliably provoking her temper.[12] Beneath the insults, Emily already cared about his safety and immediately became serious when he was discovered suffering from modified Wolfsbane poisoning.[17]
Thomas's feelings eventually develop beyond friendship. In Chapter 855, after Tasha encourages him to consider his personal future rather than living exclusively for political duties, Thomas describes a powerful young Witch with unusual eyes whom he is interested in. When pushed to identify her, he admits that he means Emily.[10]
Chapter 1181 reveals that Emily reciprocates those feelings. When Albedo asks about her romantic situation, Emily first insists that she is already in a relationship. Evie then questions whether she is actually dating Thomas, forcing Emily to admit that they are not formally together yet. Rather than denying her feelings, she declares that she intends to get him once he finally stops being so stubborn.[3] When Evie warns her not to come crying if he breaks her heart, Emily confidently states that he will not and that she will make certain of it, with her magical eye circles manifesting during the declaration.[3]
Chapter 1182 strengthens the identification further. When Violet asks whether Emily is interested in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Emily replies that she already has a "lover" and has no romantic interest in the Emperor.[4] Because Chapter 1181 immediately establishes that she and Thomas were not formally together, the terminology should not be used to invent a marriage or fully formal relationship. It does, however, make clear that Emily considers Thomas her chosen romantic partner in practice.
After returning to Arcane, Emily calls the situation temporary when Evie becomes overly enthusiastic about questioning her, but she still refuses to deny the attachment and instead establishes boundaries regarding how much her mother is allowed to investigate.[4] Their present canonical status is therefore best described as mutual romantic interest with active pursuit: Thomas has independently admitted wanting Emily, Emily has independently declared her intention to obtain Thomas, and she later refers to herself as already having a lover, but no formal marriage or explicit official partnership has yet been shown.

#### Lilith Tepes
[[Lilith Tepes]] was held alongside Emily and Thomas during the Demonic War. Emily's own captivity was staged, but she nevertheless spent enough time around Lilith to become concerned with her condition after their rescue. Emily later uses [[Poseidon|the Master]]-Level spell Appraisal to examine Lilith and determine whether she remains physically or magically compromised.[7]

#### Selena Moriarty
[[Selena Moriarty]] is Evie's First Daughter and one of Emily's elder adoptive sisters. Selena provides one of the clearest measures of Emily's natural potential: while Emily was only twelve, her Mana reserves were already compared with Selena's despite the latter possessing more than two thousand years of development.[1] The comparison concerns Emily's extraordinary innate capacity rather than making their accumulated experience identical.
Selena later agrees with Emily that Evie's refusal to seek [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s assistance is becoming increasingly impractical, placing the two sisters on the same side of one of Arcane's most consequential political disagreements.[16]

#### Alice Moriarty and Hecate Moriarty
[[Alice Moriarty]] and [[Hecate Moriarty]] are among Emily's elder adoptive sisters through the Witch Queen's Daughter hierarchy. Unlike Emily, they do not occupy the unique position of Evie's confirmed biological child, but all belong to the broader Moriarty household and the elite structure surrounding the Witch Queen.

- Chapter 1182 describes Emily as approximately average height through her own internal reaction to Albedo continuing to call her "little".[4]

- Following the Sector merger, Arcane is no longer an external Lower Sector. Its Reality was physically incorporated into Victor's Mid-Level Sector alongside Nightingale and Samar, after which Victor repositioned Arcane inside a protected central Imperial region.[3][4]
