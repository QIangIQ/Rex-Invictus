### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 82
Hecate Moriarty is an elite Human Witch, the Third Daughter of the Witch Queen, a longtime contracted Witch of [[Victoria Fulger Elderblood]], and one of the most versatile magical support specialists associated with the former Clan Rider and later Clan Fulger.[2][3]
Although [[Hecate]] was originally introduced as Victoria's highly paid contractor, she holds considerably greater status within Witch society than her early role suggests. She is one of the elite Witches incorporated into Evie's ranked Daughter hierarchy, currently occupying the Third Daughter position beneath [[Selena Moriarty]] and [[Alice Moriarty]]. The designation is simultaneously familial and political rather than proof of biological descent.[3][4]
Hecate specializes less in direct destructive combat than in the extreme versatility associated with experienced Witchcraft. Her demonstrated abilities include Memory [[Magic]], information storage, territory-wide observation, long-distance magical communication, large-scale battlefield support, Miasma suppression and preparation of sealing Magic capable of being used against a Duke of Hell.[3][5][6][7] She also maintains an independent supernatural information network and possesses extensive knowledge concerning Demons, Divine beings, Soul attacks, Witchcraft and the political structures of the supernatural world.
For a large portion of the story, Hecate remains closely associated with Victoria and [[Tatsuya Rider]]. What began as a lucrative contract gradually developed into genuine personal attachment; by the Imperial era, Hecate and Tatsuya had become lovers, with Roxanne Elderblood joking that Tatsuya was so obsessed with swordsmanship that Hecate practically had to kidnap him before their relationship progressed.[1]

### Physical Appearance
[[Hecate]] is a beautiful woman approximately 170 centimeters tall with long black hair and a slender figure.[2] During her earliest appearance beside Victoria, she wore a modern purple dress with white accents, contrasting with Victoria's darker and more aristocratic style.[2]
Unlike Witches such as [[Selena Moriarty]] or [[Alice Moriarty]], whose specializations visibly influence their appearances, Hecate can appear relatively ordinary when she is not actively using [[Magic]]. Nothing about her casual appearance immediately reveals that she occupies one of the highest positions beneath the Witch Queen or possesses enough magical experience to perform high-level support spells on a territorial scale.

### Personality
[[Hecate]] is pragmatic, intelligent, observant, sarcastic, professionally minded and strongly motivated by money. Her earliest scenes make this immediately apparent: Victoria paid enormous sums to conceal and protect Tatsuya, and when Clan Horseman unexpectedly discovered him, Hecate's greatest visible concern after failing to explain the leak was Victoria threatening to reduce her payment.[2] Working for a woman with Victoria's wealth was effectively Hecate's ideal contract, and she had no intention of losing such an extraordinarily profitable client.
Her greed does not make her incompetent. Hecate generally treats accepted work seriously and maintained Tatsuya's secrecy for years while simultaneously assisting Victoria with intelligence, prisoners and other supernatural matters.[2][8] By the Demonic War, she possessed an information network independent enough that ancient beings such as [[Jeanne Elderblood]] and [[Morgana Elderblood]] approached her specifically to purchase intelligence.[3]
Hecate is willing to admit when something exceeds her reach. When Jeanne and Morgana questioned her about [[Diablo]]'s incoming invasion, Hecate did not invent information to preserve her reputation. She openly admitted that even her own network could not penetrate the secrecy surrounding the Demon forces and correctly concluded that Evie herself was deliberately restricting what other Witches could learn.[3]
Her relationship with the Witch Queen's Daughter hierarchy is notably cynical. Hecate refers to herself as "only the Third Daughter" and understands that these positions are determined largely by how useful the individual Witch is to Evie rather than representing immutable biological birth order. She acknowledges that Selena and Evie possess intelligence networks superior to her own without allowing that fact to diminish her confidence in the resources she has built independently.[3]
Hecate also possesses strong survival instincts and practical judgment. She understands that knowledge, contracts and support [[Magic]] can be more valuable than attempting to personally overpower every enemy. During the defense of Clan Fulger, she remained in a protected command position and used observation, communication and large-scale support spells to influence the entire battlefield rather than recklessly entering direct combat against Phoenix.[5][6]
Her feelings for Tatsuya eventually become one of her strongest personal attachments. During his battle against Phoenix, Hecate attempts to maintain a professional demeanor while monitoring him but becomes increasingly worried when he is wounded and contaminated by Demonic Miasma.[7] The relationship later becomes explicitly romantic, with Hecate being far more proactive because Tatsuya's near-exclusive obsession with swordsmanship makes him exceptionally difficult to approach through ordinary romance.[1]

### Position within Arcane

#### Third Daughter of the Witch Queen
[[Hecate]] is the current Third Daughter of the Witch Queen, placing her beneath [[Selena Moriarty]], the First Daughter, and [[Alice Moriarty]], the Second Daughter.[3][4]
The Daughter title does not normally indicate biological descent. Evie's ranked Daughters are exceptional Witches elevated into a relationship combining adoption, familial recognition, political status and service beneath the Witch Queen. The numbering therefore represents hierarchy and usefulness within Arcane rather than simple birth order.
The known upper structure is:
- Selena Moriarty — First Daughter.
- Alice Moriarty — Second Daughter.
- Hecate Moriarty — Third Daughter.[3][4]
[[Emily Moriarty]] occupies a fundamentally different position because she is Evie's confirmed biological daughter rather than merely another numbered member of the Daughter hierarchy.
Hecate's own commentary indicates that the ranked positions are not necessarily permanent. She regards the system pragmatically and recognizes that a Daughter's standing depends heavily upon her usefulness to Evie.[3]

#### Contract Witch
[[Hecate]] has worked extensively as a professional Contract Witch independently of her position beneath Evie. Her most important long-term client was Victoria, who employed her for extraordinary sums to conceal Tatsuya's existence from the supernatural world and provide magical protection.[2]
This distinction is important to Hecate's character. Her rank as Third Daughter does not prevent her from maintaining private clients, independent contracts, personal wealth and an information network outside Evie's immediate administration.

### Synopsis

#### Vampire Society
[[Hecate]] first appeared while accompanying [[Victoria Fulger Elderblood|Victoria Rider]] during the confrontation between [[Tatsuya Rider]] and [[Einer Horseman]]. One of her principal contractual responsibilities was helping Victoria hide Tatsuya's existence from supernatural society. Victoria had concealed her son even from close relatives such as Natashia, and Hecate was being paid billions for her assistance in maintaining the secret and protecting him.[2]
Clan Horseman's unexpected knowledge of Tatsuya consequently placed Hecate in an extremely uncomfortable position. Victoria demanded an explanation for how information had leaked despite the fortune she was paying, but Hecate genuinely could not identify the source. She considered the possibility of supernatural clairvoyance or another information-gathering ability but possessed no definitive answer. When Victoria threatened to reduce her payment, Hecate immediately defended the technical terms of their contract and agreed to investigate the breach further.[2]
Hecate remained involved with Victoria's household after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s confrontation with Tatsuya. When enemies were captured inside Victoria's estate, Victoria left with Hecate and the prisoners while Victor remained behind to train Tatsuya, showing that Hecate's role extended beyond a single secrecy contract into general magical and logistical support for the Rider household.[8]

#### Demonic War
By the beginning of the Demonic War, [[Hecate]] had developed an independent supernatural intelligence network. Jeanne and Morgana approached her because they required information concerning the changing supernatural world, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s recent actions and the approaching Demonic invasion. Hecate immediately warned that useful intelligence would be expensive, but also admitted that the most important information concerning [[Diablo]]'s actual invasion forces remained inaccessible even to her.[3]
The information blackout was suspicious because Hecate normally possessed two potential sources: her own network and, indirectly, the vastly greater resources available through the Witch Queen. She concluded that Evie herself was deliberately restricting the flow of information concerning Diablo's operation.[3]
Hecate nevertheless possessed extensive intelligence concerning the broader situation and transferred it to Jeanne and Morgana using Memory [[Magic]]. With a simple gesture, she created two crystal-like spheres containing the relevant information and gave one to each woman. The technique was notable because Hecate performed alone a complex information-storage spell that would have required multiple ordinary Witches working together, demonstrating the difference between an experienced high-level Witch and common practitioners.[3]
The same conversation revealed more of Hecate's political status. She identified herself as the Witch Queen's Third Daughter but treated the title with considerable disdain, understanding that the numbered Daughter positions were based largely upon usefulness. Hecate also acknowledged that her own intelligence network, while extensive, remained inferior to Selena's and Evie's.[3]
Hecate later observed the [[Supernatural Gathering]] remotely. She immediately recognized the subtle supernatural influence carried through Evie's voice and identified it as a form of Seduction or Suggestion Magic. She explained that Evie's passive influence encouraged listeners to view the Witch Queen favorably, while sufficiently strong [[Will Solace|Will]] could resist its weaker manifestation.[4] The scene demonstrates Hecate's ability to identify sophisticated magical effects even through an indirect broadcast.
Her most important direct contribution during the Demonic War occurred when the 37th Key of Solomon, Phoenix, attacked Clan Fulger with a Demonic Legion. Hecate established an enormous observation spell capable of monitoring activity across Fulger territory and operated from the command center beside Victoria. Through the spell she identified Phoenix, followed the movement of the invading forces and monitored the battles occurring throughout the territory.[5]
At Victoria's request, Hecate established direct magical communication with [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] and Tatsuya while they fought elsewhere, effectively turning herself into the magical command-and-control center of the Fulger defense. She could observe distant combat, relay Victoria's instructions, follow Phoenix's position and communicate tactical information without physically entering the battlefield.[5]
Phoenix's presence created a particularly dangerous problem because his Demonic Miasma interfered with the Vampires' regeneration and contaminated the surrounding battlefield. Hecate responded by personally casting a large-scale suppression spell. Two Magic Circles formed around her hands as Mana poured from her body, followed by a gigantic magical formation appearing over the battlefield. The spell suppressed Phoenix's Miasma and temporarily removed one of the greatest environmental disadvantages affecting Sasha and Tatsuya.[6]
Hecate's knowledge became even more valuable after Phoenix was physically killed and immediately began resurrecting. She warned Sasha and Tatsuya not to relax and explained that ordinary bodily destruction would not permanently eliminate the Duke. According to Hecate, reliably killing him required an appropriate Light-aligned Being, an attack capable of damaging the Soul directly, or the intervention of a Progenitor such as Victor Elderblood or [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]], whose abilities could inflict damage beyond Phoenix's normal resurrection.[7]
Because none of those solutions was immediately available, Hecate began constructing a sealing spell intended to contain Phoenix instead. She estimated that she required approximately five additional minutes and ordered Sasha and Tatsuya to survive until the technique could be completed.[7] The battle changed before the seal needed to be fully demonstrated, so its ultimate effectiveness against Phoenix remains unknown.
The defense of Clan Fulger establishes Hecate as an exceptionally valuable support Witch. She did not need to personally overpower a Duke of Hell to determine the battle's course; her surveillance, communications, anti-Miasma Magic, knowledge and sealing preparations repeatedly gave stronger frontline combatants the conditions necessary to continue fighting effectively.

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
[[Hecate]] remained associated with Victoria and Tatsuya long after the Demonic War, and her relationship with Tatsuya eventually became romantic.[1]
The progression was unconventional because Tatsuya remained overwhelmingly focused upon swordsmanship, training and personal strength. During a later conversation, Roxanne used Tatsuya as an example of a man [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] never regarded as a realistic romantic threat around the women close to him because Tatsuya's interest in the sword eclipsed almost everything else. Roxanne joked that Hecate had effectively needed to kidnap him before anything romantic or sexual happened between them.[1]
The remark confirms both their relationship and Hecate's role as the more proactive partner. By this stage, her connection to the Rider/Fulger household had long since developed beyond the purely transactional arrangement through which she had originally entered Victoria's service.
Arcane itself was later absorbed into Victor's growing civilization, placing Hecate within the wider [[Elderblood Empire]] through the Witch Realm's political integration. No available chapter, however, establishes Hecate personally becoming a Goddess, Dragon, Blood Dragon or member of Clan Elderblood. She should therefore remain classified as a Human Witch unless a later transformation is directly shown.

### Skills & Abilities
[[Hecate]] is a highly experienced Witch whose demonstrated strength lies primarily in versatility, information control and support [[Magic]]. Her spells repeatedly affect areas and situations far larger than her immediate physical surroundings, allowing her to influence battles without functioning as the principal frontline combatant.

#### Witchcraft
- Mana Manipulation: As a high-level Witch, [[Hecate]] can manipulate large quantities of Mana and simultaneously sustain complex magical formations.[3][6]
- [[Magic]] Circle Creation: Hecate routinely produces Magic Circles as part of observation, communication and large-scale support spells.[5][6]
- Advanced Spellcasting: Hecate can personally perform complex Magic that would require several ordinary Witches cooperating to reproduce.[3]
- Large-Scale Magic: Her anti-Miasma spell formed a gigantic Magic Circle over the battlefield surrounding Phoenix, demonstrating that Hecate can extend spell effects across considerable areas.[6]

#### Memory Magic
- Memory/Information Storage: [[Hecate]] can encode large quantities of knowledge or memories into magical objects.[3]
- Memory Crystals: She created crystal spheres containing her accumulated intelligence and distributed them to Jeanne and Morgana.[3]
- Efficient Information Transfer: The recipient can obtain extensive information through the prepared magical medium rather than requiring Hecate to explain every detail verbally.[3]
Hecate accomplished the Memory Crystal technique alone, whereas reproducing equivalent [[Magic]] would require several less experienced Witches.[3]

#### Observation Magic
- Large-Scale Surveillance: [[Hecate]] can construct magical observation systems covering enormous territories.[5]
- Remote Battlefield Observation: During the assault on Clan Fulger, she followed Sasha, Tatsuya, Phoenix and the movements of Demonic forces from a protected command room.[5]
- Target Identification: Her observation spell allowed her to identify the attacking commander as Phoenix, 37th Key of Solomon.[5]
- Real-Time Tactical Monitoring: Hecate could continuously observe developments and report them to Victoria as the battle changed.[5]

#### Communication Magic
- Long-Distance Communication: [[Hecate]] can magically establish communication with targets fighting far from her location.[5]
- Battlefield Communication Network: She connected Victoria's command position directly to Sasha and Tatsuya during the Fulger invasion.[5]
- Command Relay: Hecate could transmit strategic instructions and information between the leadership and frontline combatants without requiring conventional devices.[5]

#### Miasma Suppression
[[Hecate]] demonstrated the ability to counteract the environmental Miasma generated by Phoenix. After channeling large quantities of Mana through multiple [[Magic]] Circles, she created a gigantic formation above the battlefield that suppressed the Duke's Miasma.[6]
The feat was tactically important because Phoenix's Miasma hindered the Vampires' normal regeneration and made direct combat substantially more dangerous. By suppressing it, Hecate restored conditions under which Sasha and Tatsuya could use their own abilities more effectively.[6]
This should be classified as Miasma Suppression/Neutralization rather than complete immunity to Demonic Miasma; Hecate demonstrated the ability to suppress the external phenomenon through Magic, not a personal racial immunity to it.

#### Sealing Magic
[[Hecate]] possesses high-level sealing knowledge and began preparing a spell intended to restrain Phoenix after ordinary destruction proved incapable of permanently killing him.[7]
She estimated that the spell required approximately five minutes of preparation. Its final effectiveness was never demonstrated because the battle changed before completion, so the seal should not be described as definitively capable of permanently imprisoning Phoenix.

#### Seduction and Suggestion Magic Recognition
[[Hecate]] identified the subtle magical influence carried through Evie's voice during the [[Supernatural Gathering]] as a form of Seduction/Suggestion [[Magic]].[4]
She understood that the effect encouraged listeners to view Evie more favorably and that sufficient Willpower could resist its less forceful manifestation. This is evidence of Hecate's magical perception and theoretical knowledge, not proof that Hecate herself possesses Evie's exact Seduction Magic.

#### Supernatural Knowledge
[[Hecate]] possesses substantial knowledge regarding Demons, Vampires, Soul phenomena and supernatural combat.
During the battle against Phoenix she correctly identified:
- Demonic Miasma: Its ability to interfere with Vampire regeneration and contaminate the battlefield.[6]
- Phoenix Resurrection: Destroying Phoenix's physical body was insufficient because he could restore himself from his ashes.[7]
- Soul Damage: Attacks affecting the Soul could bypass ordinary resurrection more effectively than conventional physical destruction.[7]
- Light/Holy Compatibility: Light-aligned powers were among the viable means of permanently threatening Phoenix.[7]
- Progenitor Intervention: Progenitors such as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Vlad possessed abilities capable of bypassing ordinary Demonic regeneration through deeper forms of damage.[7]
- Sealing: When permanent destruction was not practical, magical containment provided an alternative strategy.[7]

#### Information Network
[[Hecate]] maintains an independent supernatural intelligence network rather than relying entirely upon Evie's resources.[3]
Her network is inferior to those maintained by Selena and the Witch Queen, something Hecate freely admits, but remains extensive enough that powerful ancient beings actively purchase information from her. She can also identify information suppression by comparing what her own sources should normally be capable of acquiring against what has become inaccessible.[3]

#### Information Brokerage
[[Hecate]] treats supernatural information as a commercial resource. When Jeanne and Morgana sought intelligence concerning the approaching Demonic War, her immediate response was that the information would be expensive.[3]
Unlike an unreliable seller, however, she distinguishes between information she actually possesses and information unavailable even through her contacts.

#### Contract Expertise
[[Hecate]] is highly familiar with professional Witch contracts. She immediately defended the technical wording of her agreement with Victoria when accused of failing to conceal Tatsuya and attempted to prove that the unexpected information leak did not necessarily constitute a direct violation of her contractual obligations.[2]

#### Magical Support
[[Hecate]] is particularly effective when operating behind stronger frontline combatants. During the Fulger battle, she simultaneously provided surveillance, communications, enemy analysis, environmental suppression and preparations for containment.[5][6][7]
Her role demonstrates that Witch strength cannot be evaluated exclusively through direct destructive output. A support Witch with sufficient preparation can substantially alter the conditions under which physically stronger allies fight.

### Limitations
[[Hecate]] remains biologically Human. Unlike Vampires, [[Werewolves]] or Dragons, her primary strength comes from [[Magic]] rather than naturally overwhelming physical durability, speed or regeneration. Maintaining distance, preparation and access to Mana is consequently important to her combat effectiveness.
Some of Hecate's most powerful demonstrated techniques also require preparation. Her proposed seal for Phoenix required approximately five minutes to construct, making frontline allies necessary to hold the target in place long enough for the technique to become useful.[7]
Her information network, while impressive, is not absolute. Hecate openly admits that Selena and Evie possess superior networks and that sufficiently high-level secrecy can prevent even her from obtaining meaningful intelligence.[3]

### Relationships

#### Victoria Fulger Elderblood
[[Victoria Fulger Elderblood]] is [[Hecate]]'s longtime employer, contract holder and one of the people with whom she has spent the greatest portion of her on-page life. Victoria originally employed Hecate for enormous sums to conceal Tatsuya's existence and protect him from the supernatural world.[2]
Their early dynamic is openly transactional. Victoria expects results proportional to the extraordinary amount of money she pays, while Hecate views a client with Victoria's wealth as an opportunity too valuable to lose. When Tatsuya's existence leaked to Clan Horseman, Victoria immediately threatened Hecate's payment and Hecate became far more alarmed by the financial consequence than by wounded professional pride.[2]
The relationship eventually developed into something more stable than a temporary contract. Hecate continued working beside Victoria after Tatsuya became publicly known, assisted her with prisoners and intelligence, and later served as Victoria's principal magical support specialist during the defense of Clan Fulger.[8][5]

#### Tatsuya Rider
[[Tatsuya Rider]] is [[Hecate]]'s longtime companion and eventual lover. Hecate had already known and protected him before his public introduction, with concealing his existence forming one of the principal duties of her contract with Victoria.[2]
Her attachment becomes increasingly personal over time. During the battle against Phoenix, Hecate's concern is obvious when Tatsuya is injured and exposed to Miasma, even while she continues performing her command-and-support responsibilities.[7]
Their relationship eventually became romantic. Tatsuya's overwhelming obsession with swordsmanship made him extremely passive in romance, and Roxanne later joked that Hecate practically had to kidnap him before the two progressed beyond companionship.[1] The statement makes Hecate the clearly more proactive member of the relationship.

#### Evie Moriarty
Evie Moriarty is [[Hecate]]'s adoptive or title-based mother, sovereign and the woman whose hierarchy grants Hecate the status of Third Daughter.
Hecate does not display unquestioning devotion toward her. She understands that the Daughter rankings are based heavily upon usefulness, refers to her own Third Daughter status somewhat cynically and is perfectly capable of recognizing when Evie is deliberately withholding information from the wider Witch population.[3]
Nevertheless, Hecate continues to identify herself through the Witch Queen's family and political hierarchy. Their relationship is therefore better understood as a mixture of family recognition, political allegiance, professional hierarchy and individual independence rather than conventional biological motherhood.

#### Selena Moriarty
[[Selena Moriarty]] is [[Hecate]]'s elder adoptive sister and the First Daughter of the Witch Queen. Hecate openly acknowledges that Selena possesses a superior information network, placing the First Daughter among the few Witches whose intelligence resources exceed her own.[3]

#### Alice Moriarty
[[Alice Moriarty]] is [[Hecate]]'s elder adoptive sister and current Second Daughter of the Witch Queen.[4] Hecate's thoughts during Alice's introduction help establish the nature of the ranked Daughter system and confirm that the positions are hierarchical offices rather than simple biological birth order.

#### Emily Moriarty
[[Emily Moriarty]] is [[Hecate]]'s younger sister within the Moriarty family but differs from the numbered Daughters because she is Evie's biological child. Hecate therefore occupies an adoptive or title-based relationship with Emily rather than sharing direct biological descent through Evie.

#### Albedo Moriarty Elderblood
[[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]] is Evie's biological mother and therefore [[Hecate]]'s adoptive or title-based grandmother through the Moriarty structure. No major direct relationship between Hecate and Albedo has been explored in the available material.

#### Jeanne Elderblood and Morgana Elderblood
[[Jeanne Elderblood]] and [[Morgana Elderblood]] approached [[Hecate]] specifically for supernatural intelligence before the Demonic War.[3] Hecate provided them with Memory Crystals containing the information she possessed while openly explaining the limits of her knowledge concerning [[Diablo]]'s secret preparations.
Their willingness to seek her out demonstrates that Hecate possesses an independent reputation as an information broker rather than functioning solely as Victoria's hired Witch or Evie's subordinate.
