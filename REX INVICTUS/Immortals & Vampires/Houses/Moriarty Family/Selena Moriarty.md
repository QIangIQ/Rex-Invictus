### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 221
Selena Moriarty is an ancient Witch, powerful Necromancer and the First Daughter of the Witch Queen. She is one of the highest-ranking members of Arcane's Witch hierarchy and one of Evie's oldest adopted daughters, having already possessed a significant reputation while [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] was still young. Selena has lived for more than two thousand years, giving her millennia to accumulate [[Magic]], knowledge, resources, countermeasures and an extensive information network.[1]
Unlike many combat-oriented supernatural beings, Selena's danger does not come primarily from an overwhelmingly powerful body. She remains biologically Human and relies on Witchcraft, preparation, defensive Magic, [[Necromancy]], curses, concealment and accumulated experience. Her reputation is considerable enough that [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] specifically warned his agents to be cautious of her curses, while [[Scathach]] immediately recognized that Selena was concealing dangerous resources she had not openly revealed.[2][1]
Selena initially became involved with [[Victor Elderblood]] because his appointment as [[Nightingale]]'s Fifth Vampire Count created an unusual political opportunity for the Witches. Victor possessed authority to explore and acquire territory beyond the established domains of Nightingale's old nobility, making a long-term partnership with him potentially useful for establishing new Witch settlements without directly challenging Vlad's sovereignty.[3][1]

### Physical Appearance
Selena is an exceptionally beautiful mature-looking woman with long red hair ending in distinctive golden streaks, pale skin and normally black eyes. She wears a floor-length black Witch's dress with exposed shoulders and a high slit along the right leg, long black stockings and a traditional pointed Witch hat decorated with a small skull ornament.[2][3]
She almost always carries a long white staff resembling polished bone. Her clothing, staff and the noticeable presence of Death surrounding her make her magical specialization relatively obvious to supernatural observers familiar with Witchcraft; [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]] recognized Selena as a Necromancer shortly after meeting her.[3]
When Selena commands the unidentified supernatural presence connected to her, her eyes briefly glow neon blue.[1]

### Personality
Selena is cautious, intelligent, calculating, opportunistic, prideful and intensely pragmatic. Millennia of dealing with Vampires, Witches and other dangerous supernatural factions taught her that preparation and restraint are more useful than pointless displays of pride. She can openly despise someone while maintaining perfect courtesy if provoking them would endanger herself or Arcane.[2][1]
Her caution was shaped heavily by a past encounter with Vlad. Approximately a thousand years before her reappearance in [[Nightingale]], Selena unintentionally offended the Vampire King, and one of her sisters was killed as a consequence. Selena never forgot the lesson and became extremely careful when speaking around Vlad despite privately hating him.[2]
The incident also damaged Selena's relationship with Evie. Selena believed that someone genuinely acting as a mother should have retaliated when one of her daughters was killed, and Evie's failure to avenge the deceased Witch caused Selena to develop substantial resentment toward the Witch Queen. Her distrust became even more justified when Vlad and [[Alexios Alioth]] revealed that parts of Selena's memories had apparently been erased through Evie's interference.[2]
Selena's pride is tempered by responsibility. During her confrontation with [[Scathach]], Selena privately believed that thousands of years of Witch preparation gave her possible methods of killing even extraordinarily powerful Vampires under sufficiently favorable circumstances. She nevertheless rejected an actual fight because any conflict between herself, Scathach and their respective factions could result in the deaths of large numbers of Witches. Her sisters' survival mattered more than proving herself.[1]
She is strongly attracted to valuable knowledge and information. Like many high-ranking Witches, Selena can become distracted when encountering something new or potentially profitable, and her extensive intelligence network is one of her greatest non-combat assets. Her initial interest in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] combined political opportunity with curiosity about the unprecedented young Count.[4]
Selena also uses seduction and appearance deliberately as negotiation tools. When she finally met Victor privately, she attempted to exploit what she believed to be his weakness toward attractive women. The strategy backfired because Victor immediately recognized what she was doing, while his own supernatural attractiveness repeatedly disrupted Selena's composure instead.[5]
Despite her manipulative tendencies, Selena is capable of making comparatively fair assessments of people she has dealt with. Centuries later, when Arcane was being pressured by multiple Pantheons, Selena argued internally that Evie should already have contacted Victor. She remembered him as dangerous and deeply abnormal but also as someone who had been fair in business toward people who had not wronged him.[6]

### Synopsis

#### A New Pillar
Selena first appeared in [[Nightingale]] shortly after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became the Fifth Vampire Count. She entered Vlad's castle for the first time in roughly a thousand years and treated the Vampire King with carefully controlled politeness despite carrying a deep personal grudge against him. Vlad initially suspected that Evie had renewed her interest in obtaining territory inside Nightingale, but Selena clarified that her visit concerned Victor and that investigating a potential partnership with the new Count was her own initiative.[2]
Victor's political position interested Selena because the Witches had historically faced severe restrictions whenever they attempted to establish permanent territory inside the domains of stronger supernatural rulers. As a newly established Count with rights to explore and acquire undeveloped lands, Victor potentially offered an alternative route through which Witches could gain homes, resources and commercial access without directly contesting Vlad's territory.[2]
The meeting also exposed the history behind Selena's hostility toward Vlad. She remembered accidentally offending him in the distant past and losing one of her sisters as a result. Selena had never forgiven the Vampire King and was equally bitter toward Evie for refusing to avenge the dead Witch. After Selena departed, Alexios informed Vlad that portions of her memories had been erased, and Vlad attributed the interference to the Witch Queen. He subsequently ordered Selena to be monitored while warning his agents to remain cautious of her curses.[2]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Selena later approached [[Scathach]]'s mansion while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was absent. She used advanced concealment [[Magic]] that caused ordinary Vampires nearby to ignore her completely, suppressing her appearance, presence, aura and smell sufficiently well that she could stand openly near the estate without attracting ordinary attention. Kaguya nevertheless detected her and immediately recognized the smell of Death surrounding Selena and her bone-like staff as signs of [[Necromancy]].[3]
After identifying Kaguya as a member of [[Blank Clan]], Selena surrounded herself with a magical barrier. She understood that even an extremely experienced Witch remained physically Human and that a specialist assassin capable of bypassing spellcasting represented a serious threat. Selena's caution intensified after entering Scathach's residence, where Scathach ordered her to stop using Magic. When Selena refused, Scathach destroyed the barrier through overwhelming pressure; Selena simply recreated it and explained that she had no intention of remaining defenseless inside the territory of someone as dangerous as the Countess.[1]
The confrontation almost escalated when a dark unidentified voice addressed Selena as "Master". Selena's eyes glowed neon blue and she ordered the presence to remain inactive until called. Scathach immediately sensed something dangerous connected to the Witch, but the identity and nature of the entity were never revealed.[1]
Selena ultimately refused to allow pride to create a war. Although she believed an ancient Witch with sufficient preparation could possess methods capable of killing even powerful Vampires, she understood that fighting Scathach would cause consequences far beyond a duel and potentially sacrifice thousands of Witches. She therefore returned to the political purpose of her visit: creating a long-term relationship with Victor that could give Witches access to new lands and opportunities.[1]
Negotiations continued principally through Kaguya, who acted as Victor's representative while he was unavailable. Selena became visibly excited when discussing new information and occasionally lost herself in thought, but maintained a professional demeanor during negotiations. Kaguya refused to make decisions requiring Victor's personal authority, forcing Selena to wait until the Count returned.[7][4]
Selena eventually obtained a private meeting with Victor and entered it believing she possessed the advantage. She had researched him extensively and constructed a psychological profile portraying him as an extraordinarily powerful but impulsive warrior with comparatively little political experience. Selena deliberately used provocative body language and seduction to manipulate him during the negotiation, only to discover that her information about Victor's mentality had already become obsolete.[5]
Victor had inherited centuries of political and negotiation experience from [[Adonis Snow]] and immediately recognized Selena's strategy. He treated the negotiation as another form of combat, using information, psychological pressure and Selena's own hidden desires against her. When he deliberately mentioned Vlad, Selena briefly reacted despite immediately restoring her expression. Victor noticed that her heartbeat remained unnaturally stable and correctly concluded that she was using Magic to regulate her physical reactions so supernatural observers could not easily read her emotional state.[5]
Victor then offered Selena an extremely valuable magical memory containing politically destructive information concerning Vlad. Because the memory was genuine but could be selectively edited and distributed, Selena recognized that it could be transformed into both a profitable commodity and a weapon against the King she hated. Victor demanded compensation and a portion of the future profits rather than simply giving the information away.[5]
Selena accepted the opportunity and acted quickly. She spent the following period preparing the material and then began selling the information through her network at extremely high prices. Victor's "seed of chaos" therefore spread directly through Selena's commercial information network and contributed to the political instability surrounding Vlad and the Royal Family.[8]
The arrangement changed Selena's opinion of Victor substantially. She had approached him expecting to manipulate a young warrior and instead discovered a Progenitor capable of using politics, information and greed as effectively as physical force. Selena left the encounter significantly happier and freer than before, having simultaneously gained a lucrative information source and a means of attacking Vlad without confronting him directly.[5][8]
Selena appeared again after Victor's catastrophic retaliation in Japan. One of her disciples delivered a magical report detailing the deaths of Witches and other supernatural beings involved in the pursuit of Ophis. Although Selena acknowledged that Witches had died, she analyzed the situation politically rather than emotionally. Because Ophis was a daughter of the Vampire King and had been endangered, Victor possessed a sufficiently strong justification under supernatural law for his retaliation; Selena even concluded that Victor personally handling the situation was preferable to Vlad intervening, since Vlad's response could have been far more destructive.[9]
Her disciple expected Selena to respond formally to the deaths of their "sisters", but Selena refused to burden herself with the political aftermath and left the official response to Evie and the appropriate younger daughter. The scene also clarified that Witch solidarity was often institutional rather than emotional: Witches publicly called one another sisters under the Queen's laws while remaining highly competitive individuals who might exploit one another's discoveries whenever possible.[9]

#### Demonic War
Selena remained one of Arcane's principal benchmarks for elite Witch power during the Demonic War. When the extraordinary Mana reserves of the twelve-year-old [[Emily Moriarty]] were evaluated, Emily's reserves were directly compared to Selena's despite Selena having accumulated magical development for more than two thousand years. The comparison demonstrated both Emily's abnormal talent and the enormous quantity of Mana associated with Selena after millennia of growth.[10]
During the political realignment surrounding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Evie, Selena remained part of the Witch Queen's upper hierarchy while Arcane increasingly reconsidered its relationship with other factions. Victor's growing influence, his recognition as another Vampire Progenitor and his willingness to deal directly with Witches created opportunities Selena had already anticipated when she first approached him.[11]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Selena was present during the crisis caused by Arcane's unstable inter-reality portal. The portal attracted the attention of multiple Pantheons, whose rulers began offering "assistance" through agreements that would ultimately make Arcane dependent upon them. [[Odin]] and other Gods attempted to exploit the Witches' increasingly vulnerable position, while Arcane lacked enough military strength to resist an entire Pantheon directly.[6]
When Evie admitted that she did not know how to resolve the situation, Emily argued that they should contact [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Selena privately agreed. Having previously conducted business with him, she believed Arcane should already have attempted to negotiate with the [[Chaos]] Dragon God; for all of Victor's dangerous qualities, Selena considered him extremely fair toward people who had not acted against him.[6]
Before Selena could openly press the argument, a [[Magic]] Circle appeared beside her ear and one of her disciples transmitted an intelligence report. Selena informed Evie that unexpected guests had arrived, allowing Arcane to identify Yama and [[Merlin]] approaching the Realm. The incident demonstrates that even centuries after her earliest appearance Selena continued maintaining an active disciple and intelligence network capable of rapidly relaying strategically important information directly to her.[6]

### Skills & Abilities
Selena is an ancient elite Witch whose strength is built around Witchcraft, preparation, [[Necromancy]] and more than two millennia of accumulated experience. She remains biologically Human, meaning she cannot rely upon the naturally overwhelming physical statistics possessed by races such as Vampires or [[Werewolves]]; her [[Magic]] is both her greatest weapon and the principal means through which she compensates for that vulnerability.[1]

#### Magic
- Mana Manipulation: As an elite Witch, Selena possesses extensive control over Mana and uses it to perform multiple branches of [[Magic]].[3][1]
- Enormous Mana Reserves: Selena's accumulated Mana is used as the benchmark for evaluating Emily's extraordinary potential. At twelve years old, Emily's Mana reserves were already compared with Selena's despite Selena having more than two thousand years of magical development.[10]
- [[Necromancy]]: Selena's primary magical specialization is Necromancy. Her connection to Death is pronounced enough that Kaguya could identify her field from the Death-like smell and aura surrounding her.[3]
- Concealment: Selena can hide herself from ordinary supernatural perception. Her spell caused nearby Vampires to ignore her while suppressing her visible presence, aura and smell.[3]
- Presence Concealment: Her concealment extends beyond conventional invisibility by interfering with multiple supernatural indicators of her presence.[3]
- Barrier Generation: Selena can surround herself with defensive magical barriers. She instinctively erected one after recognizing Kaguya as a [[Blank Clan]] assassin and immediately reconstructed another after [[Scathach]] destroyed the first.[3][1]
- Curse Magic: Vlad regarded Selena's curses as dangerous enough to specifically warn the agents monitoring her to be cautious. The individual effects of those curses have not been directly demonstrated.[2]
- Movement Magic: Selena possesses magical methods for rapid movement or escape and intended to use Movement Magic after leaving Scathach's territory. Its exact range and mechanism remain unknown.[1]
- Physiological Manipulation: Selena can magically regulate her own biological reactions. During negotiations with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] she maintained an unnaturally stable heartbeat even when surprised, preventing enhanced supernatural senses from easily reading her emotional state.[5]
- Emotion Regulation: The same technique allows Selena to suppress or conceal involuntary physical manifestations of fear, excitement or surprise during high-level negotiations.[5]
- Magical Communication: Selena can receive information from distant subordinates through Magic Circles that manifest near her ear, allowing her intelligence network to communicate urgent developments directly to her.[6]
- Summoning/Familiar Control (Unconfirmed Nature): An unidentified dark presence addressed Selena as "Master" and obeyed her command to remain inactive. Its exact species and relationship to Selena have never been revealed.[1]

#### Necromancy
Selena is one of the setting's confirmed specialist Necromancers, but the novel has deliberately revealed very little of her complete Necromantic arsenal. Her reputation, Death-associated aura, bone-like staff and unidentified servant all reinforce the specialization, but specific powers such as Soul extraction, corpse resurrection or Undead creation should not be attributed to Selena unless directly demonstrated.[3][1]
Her longevity is particularly important to evaluating her potential. Ancient Witches do not simply become powerful because time automatically increases their physical strength; they become dangerous because centuries or millennia provide opportunities to discover spells, acquire Artifacts, research weaknesses and prepare specialized countermeasures. Selena had already been famous while [[Scathach]] was young and had more than two thousand years to build such resources.[1]

#### Unidentified Entity
During Selena's confrontation with [[Scathach]], an unidentified dark voice addressed her as "Master". Selena's eyes became neon blue and she commanded the presence to remain quiet until summoned. Scathach immediately sensed danger from whatever was connected to Selena.[1]
The novel has never confirmed whether the entity is an Undead, Spirit, familiar, summoned creature, contracted Being or something residing within Selena. Its powers and identity therefore remain unknown.

#### Magical Staff
Selena carries a long white staff resembling bone, visually reflecting her connection to [[Necromancy]] and Death [[Magic]].[2][3] The staff functions as magical equipment or a catalyst, but its specific enchantments and limits have not been revealed.

#### Preparation and Countermeasures
- Extensive Preparation: Selena has accumulated more than two thousand years of magical study, resources and contingency plans.[1]
- Anti-Vampire Knowledge: She understands the vulnerabilities inherent to Vampires and believes sufficiently prepared Witches can create specialized methods capable of killing beings whose raw statistics greatly exceed their own.[1]
- Assassination Awareness: Selena immediately recognized why Clan Blank represented a severe threat to a Human-bodied Witch and activated defenses before allowing Kaguya close to her.[3]
- Threat Assessment: She distinguishes between possessing a theoretical method of defeating an opponent and the strategic consequences of actually initiating that battle. Her refusal to fight [[Scathach]] despite confidence in Witch countermeasures is one of her clearest demonstrations of this skill.[1]

#### Intelligence and Non-Combat Skills
- Expert Negotiation: Selena possesses centuries of experience negotiating with powerful supernatural beings and treats negotiation as a form of combat involving information, leverage and psychological pressure.[5]
- Psychological Profiling: Before negotiating with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Selena researched his personality and attempted to design her strategy around his expected behavior.[5]
- Seduction: Selena deliberately uses her appearance, posture and sexual appeal as tools for influencing negotiating partners.[5]
- Information Brokerage: Selena can convert sensitive supernatural intelligence into a highly profitable commodity. After obtaining Victor's memory concerning Vlad, she rapidly prepared and sold the information through her network.[8]
- Information Network: Selena possesses numerous contacts, subordinates and disciples capable of gathering and transmitting intelligence across supernatural society.[8][9][6]
- Information Analysis: She immediately understood the political, commercial and propaganda value of the magical memory Victor offered her, including how selective editing could alter public perception without eliminating the authenticity of the original memory.[5]
- Political Knowledge: Selena understands [[Nightingale]]'s laws, Arcane's position, Pantheon politics and the informal principle that a sufficiently powerful supernatural faction can justify extreme retaliation if it possesses a socially acceptable reason.[2][9]
- Strategic Pragmatism: Selena consistently prioritizes useful outcomes over pride, including refusing a pointless confrontation with [[Scathach]] and later supporting the idea of negotiating with Victor when Arcane faced exploitation by Pantheons.[1][6]

#### Power Classification
Selena is not a God; she remains a Human Witch. However, the Author's auxiliary power hierarchy around the Chapter 630 period placed elite faction members such as Selena, [[Alice Moriarty]] and [[Emily Moriarty]] broadly within the Mid-Level God combat classification.[12]
This represents overall combat threat rather than species or Divinity. Selena's placement is produced through [[Magic]], Mana, preparation, experience and specialized techniques, and the same hierarchy explicitly warns that numerical or categorical superiority does not automatically determine the outcome of a fight.[12]

### Relationships

#### Evie Moriarty
Evie Moriarty is Selena's adoptive or title-based mother, sovereign and the Witch who recognizes her as the First Daughter. Selena continues serving within Evie's hierarchy and participates in the highest-level discussions affecting Arcane, but their personal relationship has long contained severe distrust.[2][6]
The death of Selena's unnamed sister became the central fracture between them. Selena believed Evie should have retaliated after Vlad killed one of her daughters and interpreted her refusal as evidence that the Witch Queen did not view her adopted daughters as a genuine mother should. The discovery that Evie had apparently tampered with Selena's memories further deepened that resentment.[2]
Despite their damaged personal relationship, Selena remains practical enough to work beneath Evie when Arcane's interests require it. During the portal crisis she continued addressing Evie as Mother, participating in strategic discussions and providing intelligence through her own network.[6]

#### Victor Elderblood
Selena initially approached [[Victor Elderblood]] as a potential political and commercial opportunity. His Count authority could provide the Witches access to territory beyond Vlad's direct control, and his rapid accumulation of wealth and influence made a long-term contract potentially transformative for Arcane.[2][3]
Her early assessment of Victor was badly mistaken. Selena believed he was principally a battle-oriented young man whose power had advanced much faster than his political experience. Their private negotiation proved that Victor could manipulate information and psychology with the same ruthlessness he used in combat. He recognized her attempt at seduction, identified the [[Magic]] controlling her heartbeat and exploited her hatred of Vlad to sell her politically devastating information.[5]
Selena acted upon the deal immediately and used her network to monetize and distribute the information, becoming one of the principal agents through which Victor's attack on Vlad's reputation spread across supernatural society.[8]
Her opinion of Victor later became noticeably more favorable. During the Arcane crisis, Selena considered contacting him the obvious solution because her own experience had shown that Victor, despite being dangerous and extreme, was fair in business toward people who had not wronged him.[6]

#### Vlad Tepes
[[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] is one of the individuals Selena hates most. A careless offense approximately a millennium before the main story resulted in Vlad killing one of Selena's sisters, teaching Selena never to underestimate the consequences of angering an ancient Progenitor.[2]
Selena's hatred never overcomes her caution. She treats Vlad politely in person and avoids directly challenging him, preferring indirect retaliation when an opportunity appears. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s magical memory therefore represented an ideal weapon: Selena could damage Vlad politically, satisfy part of her grudge and profit simultaneously without personally attacking the Vampire King.[2][5][8]

#### Scathach Elderblood Scarlett
Selena and [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] possess an openly tense relationship. Selena was already a known Witch while [[Scathach]] was still young, giving both women substantial knowledge of the other's reputation by the time they met directly.[1]
Scathach dislikes the indirect, concealed methods favored by Witches, while Selena considers Scathach's overwhelming physical power precisely the reason she should never voluntarily lower her magical defenses. Their first meeting nearly became violent when Scathach destroyed Selena's barrier and Selena immediately restored it despite being ordered not to cast [[Magic]].[1]
Selena believed preparation could theoretically provide methods for defeating powerful Vampires, but refused to test that belief against Scathach because the resulting factional war could kill thousands of Witches. Their encounter demonstrates the contrast between Scathach's preference for direct overwhelming force and Selena's reliance upon preparation, contingencies and political consequence.[1]

#### Kaguya Elderblood Blank
[[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]] was the principal representative who handled Selena's initial negotiations with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction. Selena immediately recognized Kaguya as a member of [[Blank Clan]] and regarded her assassination abilities as dangerous enough to justify activating a defensive barrier before continuing their conversation.[3]
Kaguya detected Selena despite her concealment spell and remained highly suspicious of her motives. Selena nevertheless respected Kaguya's authority enough to conduct prolonged negotiations through her while Victor was absent. Kaguya refused to make decisions exceeding the authority Victor had delegated to her, forcing Selena to wait for Victor personally before finalizing the proposed relationship.[7][4]

#### Emily Moriarty
[[Emily Moriarty]] is Selena's younger sister through the Moriarty hierarchy and Evie's biological daughter. Selena serves as one of the most important benchmarks for understanding Emily's extraordinary natural talent: at only twelve years old, Emily's Mana reserves were already compared to Selena's despite Selena possessing more than two thousand years of magical development.[10]
The two later shared a similar political judgment regarding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. During Arcane's portal crisis, Emily openly argued that Evie should seek Victor's assistance, while Selena privately considered the same course the most practical solution.[6]

#### Alice Moriarty
[[Alice Moriarty]] is one of Selena's younger adoptive sisters and another elite daughter of the Witch Queen. Both specialize in darker branches of Witchcraft, though Selena is principally associated with [[Necromancy]] while Alice is more strongly associated with Black [[Magic]] and mental interference.
During later Arcane crises, Selena and Alice both participated in Evie's strategic discussions as members of the Witch Queen's upper hierarchy.[6]

#### Hecate Moriarty
[[Hecate Moriarty]] is another of Selena's younger adoptive sisters. Selena's long-established intelligence network is considered particularly extensive among the Witch Queen's daughters, reflecting the resources she accumulated through thousands of years of activity.

#### Unnamed Deceased Sister
One of Selena's adoptive sisters was killed by Vlad after Selena unintentionally offended him.[2] The death permanently affected Selena's personality, creating her hatred of Vlad, damaging her relationship with Evie and teaching her to treat extremely powerful supernatural rulers with calculated restraint rather than emotional impulsiveness.

#### Unnamed Disciple
Selena possesses at least one personal disciple who also functions as a subordinate and information operative. The disciple delivered reports concerning [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s actions in Japan and discussed the political consequences of Witch deaths with Selena.[9]
By Chapter 902 Selena continued receiving reports from her disciples through magical communication, showing that she retained an organized intelligence network long after her earliest appearances.[6]
