### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 25
Adam William Lykos is a powerful Alpha Werewolf, former university professor, former leader of the [[Lykos Clan]], and one of the veteran military figures of Samar's Werewolf civilization. He is one of the many children of [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] and rose high enough within Werewolf society to serve as one of its Generals before temporarily withdrawing from active Samar politics and living in the Human World.[2][3][4]
Adam is the father of [[Johnny Lykos]], [[Edward Jonathan Elizabeth Lykos]] and [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]]. Through Leona's marriage to [[Victor Elderblood]], Adam eventually became one of Victor's fathers-in-law, although their relationship predates Victor's supernatural awakening by many years. Adam had already known Victor as Edward and Leona's sickly Human friend and later became one of his university professors, making him one of the comparatively few people still alive who personally remembers Victor before he became a Vampire.[2][5]
Although Adam has never been confirmed as a direct carrier of the rare Elizabeth Trait, he is the son of Maya, one of its greatest natural inheritors. Descendants of an Elizabeth carrier can inherit unusually high talent and developmental potential even without manifesting the mutation itself, helping explain Adam's considerable ability compared with much of Maya's enormous family.[6][7]

### Physical Appearance
Adam is an exceptionally tall and heavily built man standing approximately 200 cm tall. He has white hair, sapphire-blue eyes and a prominent white mustache that became one of his most recognizable features. Despite already being several centuries old during the early story, his Werewolf physiology preserves his body in the appearance of a powerful mature man rather than allowing him to age like an ordinary Human.[2]
His physique is heavily muscular and reflects centuries of combat training and military service. Even while Adam publicly presented himself as an ordinary university professor, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s developing supernatural instincts immediately recognized that there was something dangerous about him.[2]
While living among Humans Adam generally dressed in neat formal clothing appropriate for his position as a professor. During serious combat on Samar, he instead wore battle armor and wielded a naginata designed to complement his fighting style and Nature Energy techniques.[8][4]
When Adam draws more heavily upon his Werewolf nature, his appearance becomes increasingly bestial. His claws lengthen, his physical strength increases and green Nature Energy mixed with lightning begins surrounding his body and weapon.[4][9]

### Personality
Adam is disciplined, proud, stubborn and intensely protective of his family. His mentality reflects both his long military career and the hierarchical instincts of an Alpha Werewolf. Although he successfully lived among Humans for years and worked as an ordinary professor, his calm exterior conceals the instincts of an experienced supernatural predator and commander.[2]
His protectiveness is especially pronounced where his children are concerned. After discovering that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had become a Vampire, Adam immediately ordered Leona not to involve herself in Vampire affairs. His reaction was not based entirely upon hatred of Victor as an individual; he understood the centuries of conflict between Vampires and [[Werewolves]] and believed that allowing his daughter to become involved with one of them would expose her to unnecessary danger.[2]
Adam is nevertheless capable of separating racial hostility from personal relationships. When Victor later returned after becoming Count Alucard, Adam could still see traces of the sickly young man who had spent years around his family despite Victor's radically altered appearance, confidence and supernatural presence.[5] This resulted in a strange but genuine bond between them. Adam complains about Victor, threatens him and becomes furious whenever Leona is involved, yet he also appreciates that Victor continues addressing him as "Professor" even after becoming powerful enough that most people from his Human life could no longer meaningfully challenge him.[5][10]
Adam is also exceptionally prideful as a warrior. During his later duel against Victor, he understood relatively quickly that Victor's martial skill had become superior to his own, but he repeatedly refused to surrender. He abandoned ineffective weapons, changed fighting styles, reinforced his claws and continued attacking until Victor finally incapacitated him.[4][9]
His pride does not prevent him from fighting pragmatically. Adam is willing to exploit the environment or use tactics that a more rigid warrior might consider dishonorable. When Victor's technique prevented him from creating a conventional opening, Adam simply threw sand into Victor's eyes and immediately attacked.[9] His personal code therefore revolves less around formal honor and more around refusing to accept defeat without exhausting every practical option available to him.
Adam also has a petty and comedic side that becomes particularly visible around Victor. Insults directed toward his age or mustache can provoke disproportionate irritation, and Victor frequently takes advantage of this simply because he finds Adam's reactions amusing.[10]

### Synopsis

#### The Awakening
Adam first appeared as one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s university professors. He had already known Victor for years through Victor's friendship with Edward and Leona and was therefore familiar with the chronically ill and physically limited young man Victor had once been.[2]
After Victor's transformation into a Vampire, Adam immediately noticed the change. Victor's physique, behavior, confidence and presence were completely different from what he remembered, prompting Adam to subtly examine him while attempting to determine whether Victor had awakened as a Werewolf. Victor simultaneously sensed that his apparently ordinary professor was far more dangerous than he appeared.[2]
During their conversation, Adam criticized Victor for maintaining the artificial social personality he had used throughout much of his Human life. He questioned why Victor continued hiding behind a "mask" instead of simply behaving according to his true personality. Adam's words contributed to Victor becoming increasingly willing to abandon his former facade during subsequent confrontations.[2][11]
After Victor left, Adam casually demonstrated that he was not Human by leaving the university through the rooftops rather than conventional transportation. He contacted Leona and learned that Victor had become a Vampire rather than awakening as a Werewolf. Concerned by the implications, Adam ordered his daughter not to become involved in Vampire affairs and attempted to limit her relationship with Victor to ordinary friendship.[2]
The attempt ultimately failed as Victor became increasingly entangled with the supernatural world and Leona's relationship with him continued developing.

#### A New Pillar
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually visited Adam again after becoming Count Alucard. Their reunion was considerably different from their first supernatural encounter: Victor was no longer simply a recently transformed Vampire but one of [[Nightingale]]'s Counts and a rapidly developing supernatural combatant.[3][5]
Despite the enormous difference in status, their old dynamic remained intact. Victor continued calling Adam "Professor", while Adam allowed him onto the family property and spoke with him in the backyard where Victor had spent time during his Human years. The familiar surroundings reminded Victor of barbecues, swimming and other ordinary moments shared with Edward, Leona and the Lykos household before he knew anything about the supernatural world.[5]
Adam remained suspicious of Vampires as a Race but increasingly recognized that Victor had not fundamentally ceased being the young man he remembered. Victor's aggression and confidence had intensified dramatically, yet his attachment to his Human-era relationships remained genuine.[5][10]
The visit also placed Victor in closer contact with figures such as Anderson and revealed more of the complicated hierarchy surrounding Adam, Johnny and the wider Werewolf population. Adam's home effectively became temporary neutral ground where several supernatural relationships connected to Victor's Human past could develop without immediately descending into violence.[3][5]
By the end of the interaction, Adam privately acknowledged that despite his ingrained hostility toward Vampires, he could not bring himself to personally hate Victor.[10]

#### King Among Kings
Adam's most substantial role began when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] traveled to Samar. By this point Victor had become the Demon King, a Progenitor-level existence and one of the supernatural world's fastest-growing powers, while his relationship with Leona had advanced far beyond the ordinary friendship Adam had once attempted to permit.[12]
Adam eventually detected Victor's scent on Leona and immediately understood that their relationship had become physically intimate. His paternal instincts overwhelmed his caution, and he went looking for Victor despite already understanding how monstrously powerful his former student had become.[12]
He found Victor shortly after the latter had brutally dealt with [[Zaion Uruky]], and the aftermath was disturbing enough to make even the veteran Werewolf physically ill. Adam nevertheless recovered and still demanded a duel.[13][14][8]
Victor accepted but deliberately restricted himself. Instead of overwhelming Adam through Blood, Divinity or his other supernatural powers, Victor treated the confrontation as a martial contest and relied primarily on physical ability, technical skill and observation. Adam entered wearing battle armor and wielding his naginata, determined to personally test the man involved with his daughter.[8]
The confrontation was temporarily interrupted by the arrival of Adam's mother, [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]]. Maya's immense presence immediately demonstrated how different she was from ordinary [[Werewolves]], while Adam was forced to endure Victor becoming increasingly interested in yet another member of his family.[15][16]
When the duel properly resumed, Adam demonstrated why he had earned the position of General. He combined immense Werewolf physical strength with martial training, his naginata, Nature Energy and a personal affinity toward green lightning.[4] His skill was considerable, but Victor's centuries of accelerated training in Hell had transformed him into a martial artist capable of reading and adapting to Adam's techniques during the fight itself.
One of Adam's most sophisticated abilities involved redirecting supernatural lightning through his naginata, repeatedly circulating the Energy to accumulate greater power before release. He also developed a technique that allowed electrical attacks to originate from different points within a surrounding area instead of always being launched directly from himself.[4]
Victor became sufficiently interested in the principle that he began analyzing and reproducing aspects of Adam's technique during the duel. Maya was particularly frustrated by how easily Victor began understanding something Adam had spent considerable time developing.[4][9]
Once the naginata became ineffective at close range, Adam discarded it and switched to claw combat. Victor continued overwhelming him technically, but Adam briefly created an opportunity by throwing sand into Victor's eyes before attacking. The strategy succeeded in creating an opening but failed to produce meaningful damage; Adam's natural claws instead broke against Victor's vastly tougher body.[9]
Adam immediately reinforced his claws with Nature Energy and continued fighting. Victor eventually caught him in a chokehold and offered him an opportunity to surrender, but Adam refused outright.[9]
Respecting his determination, Victor released him and decided to properly end the battle. During their final exchange, Victor combined several precisely controlled internal strikes into the technique later known as Alucard Original Style: Inner Destruction. Adam's armor shattered when he struck the ground and the resulting internal damage finally ended the duel.[9]
Although Adam lost decisively, the battle validated his reputation as one of the more capable older Werewolf warriors. It also permanently changed his relationship with Victor. The former sickly student was now a warrior Adam had personally tested and the man who would eventually become Leona's husband.
Victor's later investigation into Clan Lykos revealed even more about Adam's heritage. Adam was one of more than fifty children of Maya, whose Bloodline descended from [[Elizabeth Lykos]], the strongest wife of the original Werewolf Progenitor Licael.[17][6]
The Elizabeth mutation periodically resurfaced among their descendants, greatly accelerating instinctive development while increasing overall potential. Although Adam himself was never identified as a direct carrier, children of an Elizabeth bearer could still inherit abnormally high talent. Adam's military success and unusually high development among Maya's children are therefore consistent with the advantages present within his lineage.[6][7]
Adam later remained involved with Clan Lykos during the political struggle between [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]] and [[Volk Fenrir]]. Clan Lykos maintained neutrality while both remained legitimate candidates for leadership, and Adam continued functioning as one of the senior figures involved in the Clan's political decisions.[18]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Adam survived into the far later era of the [[Elderblood Empire]] and was still active during the celebration of the 100,000th Imperial Year. He appeared together with several members of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s oldest Human-era circle, including [[Andrew Walter]], [[Frederick Winter]] and Edward.[1]
Despite the almost incomprehensible amount of Time that had passed and the enormous changes to supernatural civilization, the relationships between Victor's oldest acquaintances remained recognizable. Adam could still interact with Edward and the others in essentially the same familial and informal manner established before the rise of the Empire.[1]
By this point, [[Edward Jonathan Elizabeth Lykos]] had become the active Alpha and effective leader of Clan Lykos. Adam no longer functioned as its day-to-day leader, instead occupying the position of a senior elder and ancestral authority within the family. His son [[Johnny Lykos]] openly respected Edward's new position, while Adam himself remained much less formal about the hierarchy.[1]
Adam was also present alongside later generations of the Lykos and Elderblood families, including his granddaughter Leone Luna Elena Elderblood. The scene emphasized Adam's unusual place within Victor's history: he had begun as the professor of an ill Human student and survived long enough to witness that student become the Emperor of a civilization spanning worlds while Adam's own descendants became part of the Imperial Family.[1]

### Skills & Abilities
Adam is one of the stronger conventional Alpha [[Werewolves]] shown among the older generations of Clan Lykos. His abilities come primarily from his Werewolf physiology, centuries of combat experience, Nature Energy, Lightning affinity and extensive military training.

#### Alpha Werewolf Physiology
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Adam possesses supernatural strength, speed, durability, stamina, reflexes and agility far beyond ordinary Humans and lesser [[Werewolves]].[4][9]
- Superhuman Strength: Adam is physically powerful enough to engage high-level supernatural opponents in direct combat and can further amplify his strength through Nature Energy.[4]
- Superhuman Speed: His combat movements and reactions operate at levels completely beyond Human capability.[4]
- Superhuman Durability: Adam endured repeated heavy attacks from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and continued fighting despite accumulating significant damage.[9]
- Enhanced Stamina: Adam possesses tremendous endurance and continued fighting even after his weapon became ineffective, his claws broke and Victor repeatedly overpowered him.[9]
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Like other Werewolves, Adam possesses accelerated supernatural recovery.
- Longevity: Adam had already lived for several centuries during the early story and remained alive into the 100,000th Imperial Year.[1]
- Enhanced Agility: Even while pretending to be an ordinary Human professor, Adam could casually move across rooftops through supernatural physical ability.[2]
- Werewolf Transformation: Adam can release greater portions of his Werewolf nature, increasing his physical performance and making his claws and bestial characteristics more pronounced.[4]
- Fangs and Claws: His physiology naturally provides supernatural predatory weapons suitable for close combat.
- Predatory Instincts: Centuries as an Alpha grant Adam instinctive awareness of danger, hierarchy, hostile intent and other supernatural predators.

#### Enhanced Senses
- Supernatural Senses: Adam possesses the highly developed smell, hearing and perception common to powerful [[Werewolves]].
- Scent Detection: Adam can identify and track supernatural scents with extraordinary precision. He recognized [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s scent on Leona and immediately understood the nature of their recent interaction.[12]
- Species Recognition: He can determine whether someone has undergone supernatural changes by studying scent and behavior. Adam initially examined Victor because he suspected that Victor might have awakened as a Werewolf.[2]
- Enhanced Hearing: His hearing operates significantly beyond Human limits.

#### Alpha Authority
- Alpha Presence: Adam possesses the instinctive pressure associated with a dominant Alpha Werewolf.
- Pack Authority: His status and experience allow him to command lower-ranking [[Werewolves]] and maintain authority over members of his own pack.
- Military Presence: Centuries spent serving as a General further reinforce the commanding demeanor expected from Adam.

#### Nature Energy
- Nature Energy Manipulation: Adam can manipulate the [[Natural Energy]] used by advanced [[Werewolves]] to enhance both his body and techniques.[4]
- Physical Reinforcement: Adam can use Nature Energy to increase his physical strength and durability.[4][9]
- Weapon Enhancement: He channels Nature Energy through his naginata, allowing the weapon to function as a conduit for his supernatural attacks.[4]
- Claw Reinforcement: After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] broke his natural claws, Adam coated them in Nature Energy and continued using them as effective weapons.[9]

#### Lightning Manipulation
- Lightning Affinity: Adam possesses a natural affinity toward Lightning, expressed through distinctive green electrical Energy.[4]
- [[Electrokinesis]]: He can generate and manipulate supernatural lightning.[4]
- Lightning Coating: Adam can cover his body and weapon with lightning to enhance attacks.[4]
- Lightning Redirection: His naginata can redirect electrical Energy through its structure rather than allowing the Energy to disperse normally.[4]
- Lightning Accumulation: Adam can repeatedly redirect his own lightning through the weapon, progressively accumulating a stronger charge before releasing it.[4]
- Remote Lightning Generation: Adam developed a technique that allows electrical attacks to emerge from multiple chosen positions within a limited surrounding radius instead of requiring every attack to originate directly from him.[4][9]
- The technique was sophisticated enough that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] actively studied its mechanics during their duel and began reproducing its underlying principle.[9]

#### Natural Weapons
- Werewolf Claws: Adam possesses sharp and durable natural claws capable of functioning as close-range weapons.[9]
- Energy-Enhanced Claws: He can reinforce his claws with Nature Energy after their ordinary durability becomes insufficient.[9]
- Fangs: Adam possesses the predatory fangs of an Alpha Werewolf.

#### Combat Skills
- Master Martial Artist: Adam possesses centuries of combat experience and highly developed martial ability. Maya explicitly recognized the amount of time and effort he had invested into creating and refining his techniques.[4][9]
- Naginata Mastery: Adam is highly skilled with a naginata and integrates weapon movements with Nature Energy and Lightning manipulation.[8][4]
- Claw Fighting: He is experienced in the natural close-combat style of [[Werewolves]] and can immediately transition from armed combat to his claws.[9]
- Combat Adaptation: Adam can rapidly abandon ineffective strategies and change weapons, range and fighting style according to the opponent.[4][9]
- Improvised Combat: Adam is willing to exploit terrain and unconventional tactics. He threw sand into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s eyes when normal attacks failed to create an opening.[9]
- Pain Tolerance: Adam continued fighting after severe injuries and refused to surrender while restrained in Victor's chokehold.[9]
- Strong Willpower: He continued challenging Victor even after recognizing the enormous technical difference between them.[9]

#### Military and Intellectual Skills
- Military Leadership: Adam served as one of Samar's Werewolf Generals and possesses extensive experience commanding supernatural soldiers and participating in military operations.[3][4]
- Clan Leadership: Adam formerly served as leader of Clan Lykos and possesses extensive experience managing one of Samar's oldest Werewolf families.
- Tactical Intelligence: His combat and military background allows him to analyze opponents and alter strategy when direct methods fail.[4][9]
- Teaching: Adam successfully worked as a university professor for years and taught [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] before the latter entered the supernatural world.[2]
- Supernatural Concealment: He maintained a convincing ordinary Human identity despite being a centuries-old Alpha Werewolf.[2]

#### Lykos Heritage
Adam descends from one of the oldest Werewolf bloodlines on Samar through [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]], [[Elizabeth Lykos]] and the first Werewolf Progenitor Licael.[6]
- Exceptional Bloodline Potential: As the son of an Elizabeth carrier, Adam inherited unusually high natural talent even though he has never been confirmed to possess the mutation himself.[6]
- Enhanced Developmental Potential: Descendants of a natural Elizabeth carrier can possess superior talent and potential compared with ordinary members of Clan Lykos.[6][7]
- No Confirmed Elizabeth Trait: Adam should not be classified as an Elizabeth Trait carrier unless a later chapter directly confirms the mutation in him.

### Power and Standing
Adam is considerably stronger than an ordinary Werewolf and was talented enough to become a General of Samar, but he does not belong to the extreme upper tiers later reached by characters such as Maya, Tasha, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] or the Elderblood Dragon Goddesses.
His battle with Victor demonstrates both his strengths and his limits. Adam possessed enough combat ability, Nature Energy control and technical creativity to interest Victor and force him to actively study several techniques, but the overall martial gap was enormous. Victor deliberately restricted himself during the fight and still remained technically superior in almost every direct exchange.[4][9]
Adam's strength therefore comes primarily from experience, technique, Alpha physiology and efficient use of Nature Energy rather than from possessing an advanced Divine, Primordial or Draconic state.
Adam has not been confirmed to possess:
- The Elizabeth Trait.
- True Werewolf physiology.
- Ancient Werewolf physiology.
- True Alpha status.
- Complete Werewolf Transformation.
- Godhood.
- A Divinity or Divine Concept.
- Blood Dragon physiology.
- True Dragon physiology.
He should remain classified as an Alpha Werewolf unless a later chapter explicitly establishes an evolution.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] was originally one of Adam's students and a close friend of Edward and Leona. Adam knew Victor long before the supernatural world became part of his life and remembers him as the sickly Human who frequently visited the Lykos household.[2][5]
Their relationship became increasingly complicated after Victor became a Vampire and began a relationship with Leona. Adam initially attempted to keep the two apart, later challenged Victor directly over Leona and eventually became his father-in-law.
Despite the repeated conflicts, genuine respect exists between them. Victor continues to call him Professor and does not discard their old relationship despite the enormous difference in power between them.[5][10]

#### Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos
[[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]] is Adam's daughter and the child toward whom his protectiveness is most consistently shown. Adam initially attempted to keep her away from Vampire affairs and later became furious upon realizing how far her relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had progressed.[2][12]
Despite his resistance, Leona ultimately married Victor and became one of the principal women of the Elderblood family.

#### Edward Jonathan Elizabeth Lykos
[[Edward Jonathan Elizabeth Lykos]] is Adam's son and one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s oldest Human friends. Unlike Johnny, Edward remained much closer to Adam's household and later became the active Alpha and leader of Clan Lykos.[1]
By the Imperial Era, Adam no longer carries the burden of Clan leadership while Edward manages the family's political structure.

#### Johnny Lykos
[[Johnny Lykos]] is Adam's eldest known son from an earlier relationship. Johnny developed far more independently than Adam's younger children and spent much of his life as a supernatural mercenary rather than remaining under Adam's direct pack authority.[3]
Their relationship was strained by Johnny's desire for independence, but Adam never completely rejected him. Anderson interpreted Adam's refusal to classify Johnny as a rebel as evidence that Adam still considered Johnny part of his family and pack.[3]

#### Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood
[[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] is Adam's mother and the ancient Matriarch responsible for much of Clan Lykos' modern family tree. Adam is one of more than fifty children Maya produced across her long life.[6]
Maya acknowledges Adam's martial talent and the enormous amount of time he invested into developing his techniques, even when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s learning ability allows him to begin reproducing their principles almost immediately.[4][9]
