Jonas Lykos is a minimally documented Werewolf associated with the [[Lykos Clan]] and one of the former partners of Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood. Existing Lykos family records place him among the numerous [[Werewolves]] who were formerly married to Maya, alongside [[Mark Lykos]] and more than sixty other men.
Very little canonical information has been revealed about Jonas individually. Unlike several of Maya's children and descendants, he has not received a substantial on-page role, confirmed rank, combat scene or detailed personal history.

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Jonas is primarily known through the history and family structure of the [[Lykos Clan]] rather than through a direct storyline.
During [[Victor Elderblood]]'s visit to Samar, considerably more information was revealed about [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]]'s personal history. Maya had accumulated a very large number of husbands and partners throughout her long life, but she rarely maintained equal interest in all of them. She primarily continued visiting men who remained competent, continued developing themselves or otherwise succeeded in regaining her attention.[1]
Maya explained that her powerful Werewolf instincts caused her to desire partners capable of satisfying her need for a sufficiently strong Alpha. As she grew stronger, fewer and fewer men could meet that standard, resulting in much of her enormous collection of husbands becoming increasingly irrelevant to her personally.[1]
Jonas belonged to this older generation of men associated with Maya and Clan Lykos. However, no currently indexed chapter provides a dedicated scene for him or explains when his relationship with Maya began, how long it lasted, whether he fathered any of Maya's known children, or why their relationship eventually ended.
By the time Maya entered a [[Soul Marriage]] with Victor Elderblood and joined the Elderblood Clan, Jonas was no longer one of her active spouses.

### Skills & Abilities
Jonas has not participated in a confirmed battle and has no individually demonstrated supernatural abilities.

#### Werewolf Physiology
As a member of the Werewolf race, Jonas possesses the fundamental supernatural characteristics associated with [[Werewolves]].
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Werewolves possess strength, speed, durability, stamina, reflexes and agility beyond ordinary Humans.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Werewolves possess accelerated recovery from physical injuries.
- Enhanced Senses: Werewolves possess heightened smell, hearing and predatory perception.
- Longevity: Werewolves possess substantially greater natural lifespans than ordinary Humans.
- Werewolf Transformation: Members of the Race can manifest increasingly pronounced lupine characteristics.
- Natural Weapons: Werewolf transformation provides enhanced claws and fangs.
- Predatory Instincts: Werewolves possess powerful instinctive responses toward hierarchy, dominance and danger.
Jonas has not been confirmed to possess:
- Alpha Werewolf status.
- True Werewolf physiology.
- Complete Werewolf Transformation.
- The Elizabeth Trait.
- Nature Energy techniques.
- An individual Element.
- Godhood or Divinity.
- A named combat technique.

### Relationships

#### Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood
[[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] is Jonas' former partner.
Maya historically possessed more than sixty husbands and lovers, choosing partners largely according to their strength, talent and ability to continue developing themselves.[1] Jonas is recorded among these former partners, although the novel has not provided an individual account of their relationship.
No chapter currently establishes Jonas as the father of any specific named child of Maya. He therefore should not be assigned as the father of [[Adam William Lykos]], [[Bellatrix Lykos]], [[Connor Lykos]], [[Daphne Lykos]], [[Ivan Lykos]], [[Marcelo Lykos]], [[Ariana Lykos]], [[Rao Lykos]] or any other named Lykos descendant without a direct statement.
