### Appearances

#### First Mention
Chapter 772
Elizabeth Lykos was an ancient Werewolf, the wife of the first Werewolf Progenitor Licael, and the ancestral figure from whom the [[Lykos Clan]] and the hereditary Elizabeth Trait derive their name. She is described in the Clan's historical records as the Strongest Wife of the Progenitor of the [[Werewolves]] and as the Strongest Werewolf of her time.[1]
Elizabeth possessed an extremely unusual genetic mutation that even Licael himself did not possess. This mutation greatly accelerated the development of the instinctive side of a Werewolf and significantly increased the bearer’s overall potential. The trait survived within Elizabeth's descendants and periodically reappeared throughout the Lykos bloodline, eventually manifesting in individuals such as [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]], [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]] and [[Liliana Elizabeth Lykos]].[1][2]
The mutation eventually became known throughout the Clan as the Elizabeth Gene or Elizabeth Trait, turning Elizabeth's name into one of the most important hereditary designations in Lykos history.

### Physical Appearance
Elizabeth's physical appearance has never been directly shown or described in detail.
Later members of the [[Lykos Clan]] commonly possess snow-white hair and sky-blue eyes, with emerald-green eyes also appearing in some descendants. Chapter 787 attributes the unusually dominant physical characteristics of the modern Lykos bloodline to the hereditary lineage originating from Elizabeth.[3]
These characteristics should not automatically be treated as Elizabeth's confirmed appearance, however, because the novel has not directly described her hair color, eye color, skin tone, height or clothing.

### Personality
Nothing definitive is currently known about Elizabeth's personality.
Her status as the strongest wife of the first Werewolf Progenitor and the strongest Werewolf of her era demonstrates that she possessed extraordinary power and potential, but the novel has not yet shown her directly or provided enough historical dialogue to establish her temperament, ambitions or relationship with Licael beyond their marriage.[1]
The later behavioral characteristics associated with the Elizabeth Trait—such as intensified instincts and greater difficulty suppressing the Wolf side of one's personality—originated from her mutation, but they should not automatically be used as a complete description of Elizabeth herself.

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Elizabeth was first revealed through the history of the [[Lykos Clan]] during [[Victor Elderblood]]'s visit to Samar. While accompanying [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]] and [[Natalia Elderblood née Alioth]] through the Clan's territory, [[Bellatrix Lykos]] explained that Clan Lykos was one of the oldest Werewolf families on the planet. Its ancestors had already been present when the first Werewolf Progenitor arrived on Samar and members of the Clan had participated in many of the world's major historical events.[1]
Inside the Clan's library, Bellatrix showed Leona records concerning one of their most important ancestors: Elizabeth Lykos. Elizabeth was identified as the Strongest Wife of the Progenitor of the [[Werewolves]] and the Ancestor of the Lykos Clan. More importantly, she possessed a genetic mutation that not even the Werewolf Progenitor himself had possessed.[1]
The mutation dramatically accelerated development of the instinctive side of a Werewolf. As those instincts developed, the bearer's overall supernatural potential increased accordingly. Elizabeth herself became so powerful that the historical record described her as the Strongest Werewolf of her time.[1]
The same mutation survived within her descendants and eventually became known as the Elizabeth Gene. Unlike an ordinary hereditary characteristic, it does not manifest consistently in every generation. The mutation can remain dormant throughout several branches of the family before resurfacing in a later descendant.[2]
Bellatrix explained that it was considered an extremely important event whenever the mutation returned. [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] had inherited it several generations earlier, and the next major natural manifestation appeared within Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos. Maya personally recognized the mutation in Leona and gave her the name Elizabeth, marking her as a direct successor to that ancestral legacy.[2]
The importance of Elizabeth's lineage was further demonstrated in Chapter 787. [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] observed that the Blood of the Lykos Clan was extraordinarily dominant. Even when Clan members reproduced with individuals belonging to other [[Races (Flamel)|Races]], characteristic Lykos traits frequently remained dominant.[3]
Vlad described the Clan's genes as predatory. Even the Blood of a Vampire Progenitor would normally enhance the maternal side rather than overwrite the Lykos lineage, while reproduction with a God would affect the Soul of the resulting child without necessarily replacing the Clan's Blood characteristics.[3]
Among mortal species, Vlad speculated that only Dragons possessed Blood sufficiently powerful to truly compete with the hereditary dominance of Clan Lykos.[3]
Elizabeth's mutation therefore became one of the defining biological foundations of the Clan long after her own era had ended.

### Skills & Abilities
Very little of Elizabeth's individual fighting style has been revealed. Her historical reputation and the properties of her mutation nevertheless establish several important characteristics.

#### Werewolf Physiology
- Werewolf Physiology: Elizabeth was a Werewolf who lived during the era of the first Werewolf Progenitor.
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics: As a Werewolf, Elizabeth possessed supernatural strength, speed, durability, stamina and reflexes beyond ordinary mortal beings.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: [[Werewolves]] possess accelerated supernatural healing.
- Enhanced Senses: Her Race possessed greatly enhanced smell, hearing and predatory perception.
- Werewolf Transformation: Elizabeth possessed the racial ability to develop and express the instinctive Wolf side of her existence.
- Longevity: Werewolves possess substantially longer lifespans than ordinary Humans.
- Natural Weapons: Werewolf transformations provide enhanced claws and fangs.
Elizabeth's exact Werewolf classification has not been directly stated. Although the previous version of the article classified her as both a True Werewolf and True Alpha Werewolf, Chapters 772–773 only explicitly identify her as the strongest Werewolf of her era. Those more specific racial stages should therefore not be assigned without a direct statement.

#### Elizabeth Trait
Elizabeth was the original known bearer of the hereditary mutation later named after her.[1]
- Genetic Mutation: Elizabeth possessed a natural Werewolf mutation that even Licael, the first Werewolf Progenitor, did not possess.[1]
- Accelerated Instinctive Development: The mutation causes the instinctive side of a Werewolf to develop considerably faster than normal.[1]
- Enhanced Overall Abilities: As the instinctive side develops, the individual's overall supernatural abilities increase accordingly.[1]
- Exceptional Potential: Anyone who possesses the mutation is considered to have extremely high future potential.[1]
- Enhanced Predatory Instincts: The mutation intensifies the natural instincts of a Werewolf and gradually causes the Wolf side of the individual's nature to become increasingly prominent.[1]
- Hereditary Transmission: Elizabeth's mutation survived within her descendants and became an inherited characteristic of the Lykos bloodline.[1]
- Generational Reappearance: The mutation is extremely rare and generally resurfaces only after approximately three or four generations.[2]
- Enhanced Descendant Potential: Even descendants who do not directly manifest the complete mutation can inherit increased talent and potential from an Elizabeth carrier.[2]
- Dominant Bloodline: The hereditary characteristics associated with Elizabeth became extraordinarily dominant within Clan Lykos. Members reproducing outside the Clan frequently continue passing characteristic Lykos traits to their descendants.[3]
- Resistance to Bloodline Overwriting: Vlad observed that even the Blood of a Progenitor or the influence of a God's lineage would not simply overwrite the Lykos hereditary characteristics.[3]
- This should not be interpreted as absolute immunity to every possible racial transformation. Later superior transformations, such as [[Victor Elderblood]]'s Blood Dragon conversion, were capable of integrating the existing Lykos Essence rather than being rejected outright.

##### Side Effects
The Elizabeth Trait possesses significant psychological risks alongside its advantages.
- Instinctive Instability: Accelerated development of the Werewolf side can make the individual's instincts increasingly difficult to suppress.[1]
- Emotional Instability: Stronger instincts can produce greater emotional volatility.[1]
- Increasingly Predatory Mentality: Without proper discipline, the individual can gradually become more Wolf-like and less governed by rational thought.[1]
- Need for Early Training: Because the mutation can manifest while the bearer is still young, training and discipline from childhood are considered especially important.[1]

### Legacy
Elizabeth's greatest lasting influence is the Bloodline that carries her name.
Within Clan Lykos, receiving the name Elizabeth is not merely ceremonial. The name is associated with descendants who inherit the ancestral mutation and are regarded as possessing the potential to become future leaders or exceptional members of the Clan.[2]
Known natural or awakened inheritors associated with the Trait include:
- Elizabeth Lykos: Original known bearer and namesake of the mutation.[1]
- [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]]: Natural inheritor who became one of the strongest [[Werewolves]] in Samar's modern era.[2]
- [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]]: Natural inheritor identified by Maya and named Elizabeth as recognition of her status as a direct successor.[2]
- [[Liliana Elizabeth Lykos]]: Natural carrier whose dormant potential was later artificially awakened by [[Victor Elderblood]].[4][5]
The mutation's presence became sufficiently important that Clan Lykos historically preferred leaders who inherited Elizabeth's characteristic, since such individuals were considered virtually guaranteed to become extremely powerful with sufficient development.[2]

### Trivia
- Elizabeth was first mentioned in Chapter 772.[1]
- She was the wife of the first Werewolf Progenitor, Licael.[1]
- The old article incorrectly listed Licael as both Elizabeth's father and spouse. The novel only establishes him as her husband.
- Elizabeth is described as the Strongest Wife of the Progenitor of the [[Werewolves]].[1]
- Her historical record calls her the Strongest Werewolf of her time, not the strongest female Werewolf of all time.[1]
- The old titles "Apex Predator" and "First Leader of Clan Lykos" are not directly stated in the reviewed chapters and have therefore been removed.
- Elizabeth possessed a genetic mutation that even Licael did not possess.[1]
- The mutation eventually became known as the Elizabeth Gene or Elizabeth Trait.
- The Elizabeth mutation is stated to appear only once every approximately three or four generations.[2]
- Maya and Leona are specifically identified as later inheritors of the mutation.[2]
- Elizabeth's exact age has never been revealed.
- Her exact cause of death has never been revealed.
- The story has not explicitly confirmed whether she died naturally, was killed or survived for some other period after Licael's era; her current status is therefore better left Unknown rather than listing "Old Age" as a confirmed cause of death.
- Elizabeth has never been directly confirmed to possess the specific classifications True Werewolf or True Alpha.
- Her physical appearance has not been directly revealed.
- Chapter 787 describes the modern Lykos Bloodline as so genetically dominant that even Progenitor Blood normally strengthens rather than replaces its maternal hereditary traits.[3]
- Vlad speculated that among mortal species, Dragons were the only beings whose Blood might be strong enough to properly compete with the predatory hereditary characteristics of Clan Lykos.[3]
