### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 846
Ariana Lykos is an Alpha Werewolf of the [[Lykos Clan]], one of the numerous daughters of former Matriarch [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]], the sister and wife of [[Rao Lykos]], and the mother of [[Liliana Elizabeth Lykos]]. She first appeared when [[Victor Elderblood]] noticed Liliana among Maya's enormous number of descendants and discovered that the young girl naturally carried the dormant Elizabeth Trait.[1]
Ariana herself has received very little individual focus. Maya describes both Ariana and Rao as comparatively ordinary among her children—neither among her most talented descendants nor weak enough to be considered useless. Their daughter nevertheless inherited significantly greater potential through the reappearance of Elizabeth's rare hereditary mutation.[1]

### Physical Appearance
Ariana's physical appearance has not been described in detail.
During Chapter 846, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] looks toward Rao and Ariana after Liliana identifies them as her parents. Rao is specifically described as a man with long white hair and a serious expression, while Ariana is only identified as the woman standing beside him.[1] Her hair color, eye color, skin tone, height, clothing and Werewolf transformation have therefore not been canonically established.

### Personality
Very little has been directly revealed about Ariana's personality, although her introduction establishes that she is highly protective of her daughter.
When the four-year-old Liliana became visibly frightened while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] questioned her, Ariana and Rao immediately prepared to intervene. Maya stopped both parents with a single look before either could approach.[1] Ariana remained visibly concerned but obeyed Maya's judgment, trusting that the Clan Matriarch understood Victor well enough to know he would not deliberately harm a child who had done nothing to threaten him.
Her behavior therefore demonstrates strong maternal protectiveness alongside considerable respect for Maya's authority. Beyond her concern for Liliana, Ariana has not received enough individual dialogue to establish a more detailed personality.

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Ariana first appeared during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later involvement with the [[Lykos Clan]]. After dealing with several members of Maya's family, Victor examined the enormous gathering of her descendants and concluded that most were competent in one way or another but did not meet his increasingly extreme standards for exceptional talent.[1]
Only a few immediately attracted his attention, including [[Connor Lykos]], [[Bellatrix Lykos]] and a four-year-old girl with short snow-white hair who strongly resembled a younger [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]]. The child was Liliana Lykos.[1]
Victor approached Liliana while surrounding members of Clan Lykos instinctively moved aside. The girl was terrified by his presence but attempted to remain standing because she was proud of being Maya's granddaughter. When her fear became increasingly obvious, her parents prepared to intervene. Maya stopped them with a single glance, and both Ariana and Rao reluctantly remained where they were.[1]
Liliana eventually introduced herself as:
"Liliana Lykos, daughter of [[Rao Lykos]] and Ariana Lykos."[1]
Victor looked toward the couple and called Maya's name. Maya immediately understood what he wanted to know and explained that Rao and Ariana were both her children. She described them as neither particularly talented nor especially useless among her enormous family.[1]
Victor found their daughter much more unusual. Despite coming from two comparatively ordinary members of Maya's lineage, Liliana possessed an exceptionally promising future. Maya had independently suspected that her granddaughter might carry dormant traces of the rare Elizabeth mutation, although Liliana's manifestation was much less obvious than Leona's had been during childhood.[1]
Victor confirmed Maya's suspicion. Using his ability to perceive the information contained within a Soul, he recognized that the mutation already existed naturally within Liliana and brought the dormant characteristic to the surface.[1][2]
He then formally gave the girl the name [[Liliana Elizabeth Lykos]]. The declaration shocked the surrounding Clan because the name Elizabeth carried enormous historical importance, but Maya immediately sensed the ancestral trait awakening within her granddaughter.[1]
Victor subsequently bestowed separate blessings associated with his Blood and Dragon aspects upon Liliana and expressed high expectations for her future. He later entrusted the girl's development to Maya, telling the former Matriarch to personally train the promising child.[1]
Chapter 847 further clarified that Victor had not created an Elizabeth Trait where none previously existed. His Soul manipulation could bring an already existing characteristic to the surface, but he could not arbitrarily create a hereditary quality absent from an individual's Essence. Liliana's mutation was therefore inherited naturally through Ariana and Rao's branch of the Lykos family, even though neither parent has been confirmed as a direct Elizabeth carrier.[2]
Ariana has not received another substantial individual role in the currently documented material.

### Skills & Abilities
Ariana has not participated in a dedicated battle and has no confirmed unique supernatural technique. Her known abilities derive primarily from her Werewolf physiology and Lykos ancestry.

#### Alpha Werewolf Physiology
- Alpha Werewolf Physiology: Ariana is classified as an Alpha Werewolf and possesses the enhanced physical and supernatural characteristics associated with high-ranking members of her Race.
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: [[Werewolves]] possess supernatural strength, speed, durability, reflexes, agility and stamina far exceeding ordinary Humans.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Ariana possesses the accelerated biological recovery characteristic of Werewolves.
- Enhanced Senses: Her Race possesses heightened smell, hearing and predatory perception.
- Longevity: Werewolves possess substantially greater natural lifespans than ordinary Humans.
- Werewolf Transformation: Ariana possesses the racial ability to manifest more pronounced Werewolf characteristics, although her individual transformed state has never been shown.
- Natural Weapons: Werewolf transformations provide enhanced claws and fangs.
- Predatory Instincts: Like other Werewolves, Ariana possesses instinctive sensitivity toward danger, dominance and supernatural hierarchy.
- Alpha Presence: Her Alpha classification grants a stronger supernatural presence than that of lower-ranking Werewolves, though Ariana has not demonstrated an individual feat of Alpha authority.

#### Lykos Heritage
- Lykos Bloodline: Ariana is one of the biological daughters of [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] and therefore belongs directly to the ancient lineage descending from [[Elizabeth Lykos]] and the first Werewolf Progenitor Licael.
- Inherited Potential: Descendants of an Elizabeth carrier can inherit increased talent and potential even without directly manifesting the full mutation themselves.[3][4]
- Carrier Lineage: Ariana's daughter naturally inherited the dormant Elizabeth mutation, proving that the hereditary characteristic remained present within Ariana and Rao's branch of the family.[1][2]
- No Confirmed Elizabeth Trait: Ariana herself has never been identified as an active carrier of the Elizabeth Trait.
Ariana has not been confirmed to possess:
- The Elizabeth Trait.
- True Werewolf physiology.
- Complete Werewolf Transformation.
- Ancient Werewolf physiology.
- True Alpha status.
- Godhood or Divinity.
- An individualized Element.
- A named supernatural technique.

### Relationships

#### Rao Lykos
[[Rao Lykos]] is both Ariana's biological brother and husband. Both are children of [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] and together they are the parents of [[Liliana Elizabeth Lykos]].[1]
Maya evaluates Rao and Ariana similarly, describing neither as particularly talented but also making clear that neither is useless. The couple nevertheless produced a daughter whose dormant ancestral potential significantly exceeded what [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] initially expected from the parents.[1]
Both display the same immediate concern for Liliana when Victor approaches her, preparing to intervene together before Maya stops them.[1]

#### Liliana Elizabeth Lykos
[[Liliana Elizabeth Lykos]] is Ariana's daughter and the most important known member of her immediate family branch.
Liliana naturally inherited the extremely rare Elizabeth mutation despite neither Ariana nor Rao being identified as active carriers. Maya had already begun monitoring the child because her instincts suggested the ancestral characteristic might eventually emerge.[1]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] confirmed the dormant mutation, awakened it through Soul manipulation and personally gave Liliana the name Elizabeth.[1][2]
Ariana's strongest directly shown characterization occurs through her concern for Liliana during this event.

#### Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood
[[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] is Ariana's mother and former Matriarch of Clan Lykos.
Ariana obeys Maya's authority even when her maternal instincts push her toward intervention. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] frightened Liliana, Maya needed only a single look to stop Ariana and Rao from approaching.[1]
Maya later became responsible for Liliana's personal training after Victor awakened the girl's Elizabeth potential.
