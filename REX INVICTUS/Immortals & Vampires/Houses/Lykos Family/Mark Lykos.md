### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 765
Mark Lykos is an Alpha Werewolf affiliated with the [[Lykos Clan]] and one of the former husbands of [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]]. He was introduced alongside [[Jonas Lykos]] during [[Victor Elderblood]] and Maya's walk through Samar, when the two men confronted the increasingly obvious attraction developing between Maya and Victor.[1]
Mark genuinely loved Maya, but his relationship with her suffered from the enormous difference between their respective strength and ambition. Upon witnessing Victor beside his wife, Mark immediately recognized that he could not compete with Victor's power, confidence or presence. Although jealous and deeply insecure about Maya's growing attraction toward Victor, he did not attempt to attack him and instead resolved alongside Jonas to improve himself and try to prove that he remained worthy of Maya's affection.[1]
By the time Maya fully joined Victor's family, her previous marital relationships had ended, making Mark one of her former husbands.[2]

### Physical Appearance
Mark is a chocolate-skinned man with silver hair. He possesses the powerful physique expected of an Alpha Werewolf of the [[Lykos Clan]].
The novel has not provided a detailed description of his height, eye color, clothing or other distinguishing physical characteristics.

### Personality
Mark is shown to genuinely care for Maya despite the unusual nature of her enormous family and the fact that he was only one among many men she had married throughout her long life. His brief appearance reveals that his attachment to her was considerably more emotional than Maya's own interest in most of her husbands.[1]
When Mark witnessed Maya walking through Samar with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], he became deeply jealous and insecure. Rather than simply resenting Victor's Race, Mark focused upon the qualities separating the two men. He recognized that Victor possessed strength, charisma and an overwhelming presence that he himself could not match, causing him to question whether he could ever satisfy Maya in the same manner.[1]
Mark was nevertheless realistic enough not to challenge Victor physically. Both he and Jonas understood from merely observing him that attempting to fight Victor over Maya would accomplish nothing. Their reaction contrasts with the impulsive behavior sometimes associated with Werewolf dominance disputes; neither man allowed jealousy to push him into an obviously suicidal confrontation.[1]
Despite his insecurity, Mark did not immediately give up on his relationship. After Victor and Maya left, Jonas proposed that the two men continue doing their best to regain Maya's affection. Mark agreed, showing that he was willing to confront his own inadequacy and attempt to improve rather than simply blame Maya or Victor for the situation.[1]
Ultimately, however, Mark could not provide the kind of partner Maya's Elizabeth-enhanced instincts desired. Maya had spent centuries searching for an Alpha capable of genuinely standing beside or above her, while most of her husbands were considerably weaker than she was.[3][4] Her encounter with Victor exposed that incompatibility more clearly than before.

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Mark first appeared during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Maya's exploration of Eclipse Ventus. What had begun as Maya showing Victor around Samar gradually developed into an informal date as the two visited various shops, joked with one another and became increasingly comfortable together.[1]
Their outing was interrupted when Maya encountered two of her husbands, Jonas and Mark. Seeing them immediately affected Maya's mood. Until that point she had been allowing herself to enjoy Victor's company without seriously confronting the implications of their mutual attraction, but the presence of her husbands reminded her that she already possessed numerous established relationships and obligations.[1]
Mark and Jonas were understandably displeased by the sight of Maya walking intimately beside another man, particularly a Vampire. The situation became more intimidating once they properly examined Victor. His presence alone made the enormous difference between them obvious, and neither husband seriously considered attempting to fight him.[1]
Mark was especially frustrated by the situation. He questioned why Maya had become interested in someone so fundamentally different from her existing husbands and why that person had to be a Vampire despite her previous hostility toward the Race. More importantly, Mark recognized that he lacked the strength, confidence and presence Victor naturally displayed. This realization caused him to wonder whether he had ever truly been capable of satisfying Maya.[1]
Maya dismissed her husbands and continued walking with Victor. Left behind, Mark and Jonas discussed the threat Victor represented to their relationships with her. Both were jealous and insecure, but their feelings were mixed with sincere affection for Maya. Jonas suggested that they continue trying to improve themselves and prove that they were worthy of her, and Mark agreed.[1]
Their determination did not change the deeper incompatibility within Maya's relationships. Maya's instincts had always desired a partner powerful enough to challenge her, while none of her contemporary husbands possessed the strength necessary to satisfy that aspect of her nature. Victor's appearance made the disparity impossible to ignore.[3][4]
Later, as Maya abandoned the restraints she believed were preventing her own advancement and became increasingly integrated into Victor's family, her previous marriages ended. Mark consequently became one of Maya's former husbands.[2]

### Skills & Abilities
Mark is an Alpha Werewolf, although he has received no direct combat scene and very little information exists regarding his individual techniques.
- Alpha Werewolf Physiology: As an Alpha Werewolf, Mark possesses the superior supernatural physiology associated with high-ranking members of his Race.
- Superhuman Strength: Mark possesses physical strength beyond ordinary [[Werewolves]] and Humans, although his individual upper limit has never been demonstrated.
- Superhuman Speed: His Werewolf physiology grants supernatural movement speed and reflexes.
- Superhuman Durability: Mark possesses the naturally enhanced resilience of an Alpha Werewolf.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Like other Werewolves, he possesses accelerated supernatural healing.
- Enhanced Senses: Mark possesses the heightened smell, hearing and predatory perception characteristic of Werewolves.
- Longevity: Werewolves possess lifespans considerably greater than Humans.
- Werewolf Transformation: As an Alpha, Mark possesses the racial ability to transform and release more of his Werewolf characteristics. His individual transformed appearance has not been shown.
- Enhanced Fangs and Claws: Mark possesses the natural predatory weapons associated with Werewolf physiology.
- Predatory Instincts: Mark possesses the supernatural instincts common among Werewolves, including sensitivity toward strength, dominance and other powerful predators.
- Alpha Presence: His Alpha status grants him greater instinctive authority than ordinary Werewolves.
Mark has not demonstrated an individual Element, Divinity, Elizabeth Trait, True Werewolf state, Complete Werewolf Transformation or other higher racial evolution.

### Relationships

#### Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood
[[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] was Mark's wife and the central known relationship of his character. Mark genuinely loved Maya and was distressed when he realized how strongly she was becoming attracted to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[1]
The fundamental problem in their relationship was the enormous disparity between them. Maya was substantially stronger than her husbands and possessed instincts that continuously pushed her toward increasingly powerful partners. Mark understood that he could not presently provide the challenge or overwhelming presence she experienced around Victor.[3][1]
Rather than immediately abandoning her, Mark initially resolved to become better and attempt to regain her attention. The relationship nevertheless eventually ended as Maya severed her former marital commitments and joined Victor's family.[2]

#### Jonas Lykos
[[Jonas Lykos]] was another of Maya's husbands and appears alongside Mark in Chapter 765. Both men experienced similar jealousy and insecurity upon seeing Maya with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and understood that attempting to physically challenge him was unrealistic.[1]
Jonas was the one who encouraged them to continue trying to prove themselves worthy of Maya, and Mark agreed with him. Their shared reaction suggests a degree of solidarity between Maya's husbands despite technically competing for the same woman's affection.[1]

#### Victor Elderblood
Mark encountered [[Victor Elderblood]] while Victor was spending time with Maya in Samar. He immediately recognized Victor as someone he could not realistically challenge.[1]
Mark's feelings toward Victor were dominated by jealousy, frustration and insecurity rather than direct hatred. He questioned why Maya would become attracted to a Vampire but was even more troubled by the fact that Victor possessed precisely the qualities he himself lacked: overwhelming strength, confidence and presence.[1]
No actual battle occurred between them.
