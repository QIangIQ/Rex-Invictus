### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 58
Jonathan "Johnny" Lykos, commonly known as Johnny and by his mercenary title The White Wolf, is an Alpha Werewolf, former supernatural mercenary and senior member of the [[Lykos Clan]]. He is the eldest son of former Werewolf General [[Adam William Lykos]], born from one of Adam's previous relationships, making him the older paternal half-brother of [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]] and [[Edward Jonathan Elizabeth Lykos]].[2][3]
Unlike Adam, who abandoned his position as a Werewolf General and spent decades living quietly among Humans, Johnny initially rejected much of the structure surrounding his family and built an independent life in the supernatural underworld. He became a mercenary associated with [[The Lost Club]], accepting contracts involving [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]], Vampires and supernatural Artifacts while maintaining his own pack and household independently of Adam.[2][4][5]
Johnny possesses strong natural Alpha blood but failed to develop as efficiently as several other members of his family. Anderson openly described him as disappointing compared with Adam, not because Johnny lacked potential but because pride, lack of discipline and an excessively independent lifestyle had prevented him from properly exploiting it.[5] Despite this, Johnny remained considerably stronger than ordinary [[Werewolves]] and eventually matured from an impulsive mercenary into a devoted father and senior member of Clan Lykos who openly respects Edward's later authority as the Clan's Alpha.[1]

### Physical Appearance
Johnny is an extremely tall, heavily muscular man standing approximately 195 cm tall. He has dark skin, short white hair, sapphire-blue eyes and a naturally stern expression. His body is covered by distinctive black-striped tattoos, while several scars are visible across his arms.[2]
At his debut he normally wore dark trousers with a simple white shirt and carried a necklace from which a large, visibly sharp fang hung.[2] His appearance becomes increasingly predatory as he releases more of his Werewolf nature. His sapphire eyes begin glowing, his fangs become more prominent and his features become progressively more lupine.[6]
During a deeper partial transformation, Johnny's body increases in size and fur begins spreading across him as his Alpha physiology becomes more pronounced.[7] He has never been shown achieving the advanced Complete Werewolf state possessed by figures such as [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]], nor has any later chapter confirmed him becoming an Ancient Werewolf, True Werewolf or True Alpha.

### Personality
Johnny is proud, territorial, stubborn, confrontational and frequently vulgar, displaying many of the instinctive characteristics associated with an Alpha Werewolf. He dislikes being insulted or treated as inferior and can allow challenges to his pride to override better judgment. His confrontation with [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] is the most obvious example: although Johnny knew Ruby belonged to Clan Scarlett and was the daughter of [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], repeated insults toward his status as an Alpha eventually provoked him into attacking her directly.[7]
His arrogance is one of the primary reasons his development lagged behind his potential. Anderson considered Johnny's bloodline excellent but his mentality undisciplined, believing that Adam's eldest son could have become substantially stronger had he taken his training and responsibilities more seriously.[5] Johnny himself spent much of his earlier life avoiding formal hierarchy, operating as a mercenary and maintaining his own household rather than fully returning to Adam's pack.
Despite his pride, Johnny is not recklessly suicidal when someone under his protection is involved. When he encountered Anderson and several powerful [[Werewolves]] while accompanied by Judy, Johnny immediately placed Judy behind himself, recognized that fighting the entire group would be impossible and refused to allow Anderson's provocations to bait him into a battle that might place her in danger.[5] His willingness to suppress his ego for another person's safety distinguishes his protective instincts from simple territorial aggression.
Family and pack are Johnny's strongest emotional priorities. He nearly beat a man to death after the individual sexually harassed Judy, immediately became serious when Jessica revealed that Clan Horseman intended to hunt Adam for Alpha Werewolf blood and initially reacted furiously when he learned that Leona had become interested in a Vampire.[6][4] His objection to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was ultimately less about Victor being a Vampire than about Johnny's difficulty accepting that the little sister he remembered had grown into an adult capable of choosing her own partner.
Johnny is therefore comparatively tolerant of interracial supernatural relationships. He openly questioned the traditional claim that Vampires and Werewolves could never be together and was willing to judge Victor based upon Victor himself rather than automatically rejecting him because of Race.[6] His own relationship with [[Jessica Horseman Snow]], however, revealed that Johnny was not entirely free from the instincts and prejudices created by centuries of conflict between the two species. Jessica noticed that he unconsciously treated her differently from his other women because she was a Noble Vampire.[4]
Johnny's largest emotional weakness is his tendency to avoid difficult conversations. When the contradiction between Jessica's hatred of Vampire–Werewolf Hybrids and their own relationship became impossible to ignore, Johnny admitted that he disliked dealing with complicated emotional matters and postponed the problem instead of resolving it.[4] The relationship eventually failed, and by the Imperial Era Johnny admits through his behavior that Jessica's departure caused him to appreciate the companions who remained with him much more deeply.[1]
Fatherhood substantially matured Johnny without removing his harsher Werewolf values. By Chapter 1146 he has several young children and is deeply concerned that they become strong enough to survive the future surrounding the [[Elderblood Empire]]. He is so committed to this that he asks Leona to arrange training under Scathach Elderblood Scarlett, remaining enthusiastic even after being repeatedly warned that [[Scathach]]'s methods are regarded as excessively brutal by members of Victor's own family.[1]

### Synopsis

#### Vampire Society
Johnny was introduced at [[The Lost Club]], a supernatural nightclub, information exchange and mercenary center where he had already established a reputation as The White Wolf. He regularly accepted paid work from different supernatural factions and had no issue taking Vampire money despite frequently insulting Vampires personally. Around this period he was working for Lucy, including retrieving a supernatural relic from [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] and disposing of it in the ocean.[2][4]
While drinking with Eddy, Johnny learned that his younger half-sister Leona had become interested in a recently transformed Vampire named [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] Walker. Johnny initially rejected the idea almost instinctively because he continued imagining Leona as the young girl who once followed him around calling him her big brother. Eddy reminded him that nearly eighteen years had passed and that Leona was now an adult capable of making her own decisions.[6]
Rather than immediately attempt to kill Victor, Johnny purchased information about him. The report described Victor as a twenty-one-year-old Human who had recently become a Vampire and characterized him as intensely loyal to people he cared about, vindictive toward enemies, unusually honest, petty and psychologically abnormal by conventional Human standards. Several of those characteristics actually sounded familiar to Johnny because they resembled extreme Werewolf territoriality. He consequently decided that he would judge Victor personally before determining whether he was worthy of Leona.[6]
Johnny's relationship with his own household was also established during this period. When a man at The Lost Club grabbed Judy and demanded sexual services from her, Johnny immediately attacked him and nearly beat him to death before Jinsei calmed him.[6] Judy's history revealed that Johnny had previously helped her family escape difficult circumstances, and he regarded the women surrounding him as part of his own pack rather than disposable lovers.
The greatest threat to Johnny's biological family emerged through [[Jessica Horseman Snow]]. Jessica revealed that members of Clan Horseman were cooperating with Lucy's operation only as cover for another objective: hunting [[Adam William Lykos]] in order to acquire Alpha Werewolf blood for experiments attempting to stabilize an artificial Vampire–Werewolf Hybrid.[4] Johnny immediately abandoned his casual attitude when he learned his father was being targeted.
Jessica's disgust toward the Hybrid also exposed a contradiction in their own relationship. Any biological child between a Noble Vampire and an Alpha Werewolf could itself become a Hybrid, meaning Jessica's inherited beliefs effectively condemned the possibility of her own future family. Johnny understood the problem but, characteristically, postponed addressing it because he was uncomfortable dealing with emotionally complicated subjects.[4]

#### A New Pillar
Johnny later encountered [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] and [[Natalia Elderblood née Alioth]] when the two women visited [[The Lost Club]]. His instinctive hostility toward Vampires rapidly collided with Ruby's complete lack of respect for an unknown Alpha Werewolf. Ruby repeatedly called Johnny a "mongrel", and after attempting to restrain himself because of her Clan affiliation, Johnny eventually allowed his pride to take control and punched her directly in the face.[7]
The strike generated a powerful shockwave but failed to injure Ruby because she had already covered her face with a thin protective layer of Ice. Ruby immediately retaliated by freezing Johnny's entire body and nearly piercing his heart before Madam intervened.[7] Johnny released more of his Werewolf transformation, increasing his body size and developing fur before breaking free of the Ice, but the overall difference between them remained enormous. Ruby continued regarding him as insignificant, while Natalia was prepared to remove him from the confrontation entirely by teleporting him into outer space.[7]
The defeat highlighted the gap between Johnny's pride and his actual position among the supernatural world's strongest figures. He was dangerous compared with ordinary [[Werewolves]], Humans or lower-level supernatural fighters, but the daughters of the [[Vampire Counts]] existed on an entirely different scale.
Johnny later returned to Adam's neighborhood accompanied by Judy and immediately noticed that a normally populated street had become unnaturally silent. He ordered Judy to remain behind him, using enough Alpha authority that her own Werewolf instincts automatically complied despite her attempt to argue.[5] The individual responsible for the disturbance was Anderson, accompanied by several powerful Werewolves.
Anderson openly criticized Johnny's development. He acknowledged Johnny's strong Alpha blood and position as Adam's eldest son but considered him a disappointment because he had failed to discipline himself sufficiently.[5] The conversation also revealed that Johnny had distanced himself from Adam's pack without entirely severing their connection. Anderson warned that if Johnny became a completely independent Alpha, the Werewolf hierarchy could eventually classify him as a rival power and order him hunted.
Johnny nevertheless refused to completely return to Adam's pack because he would not abandon the women who had built their lives around him. His loyalty to his chosen household therefore outweighed the personal safety offered by rejoining his father's hierarchy.[5]
Johnny's first direct interaction with [[Victor Elderblood]] occurred when Victor visited The Lost Club. Victor's supernatural gaze successfully overcame the natural resistance Alpha Werewolves possess toward ordinary Vampiric mental influence, surprising Johnny and the surrounding observers.[8]
Johnny subsequently revealed that his full name was Jonathan Lykos and that he was Adam's eldest son.[3] Victor's attitude changed immediately because Adam was one of the people from Victor's Human life whom he genuinely respected.
The meeting also exposed the truth behind Johnny's supposed relationship with [[Roberta Elderblood]]. Johnny genuinely believed Roberta was his wife, believed that he loved her and believed that he had placed a legitimate Werewolf mate mark upon her. Victor noticed inconsistencies in his emotions and began questioning him until Johnny became incapable of explaining exactly what Roberta meant to him.[9]
Roberta then released her supernatural influence. Johnny's supposed mate mark began disappearing, his affection faded and the memories supporting their relationship dissolved.[9] The apparent marriage had never been genuine; Roberta had altered Johnny's memories, emotions and perception until he accepted a relationship that did not actually exist.

#### King Among Kings
Johnny later returned to the center of Werewolf politics when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] travelled to Samar. The newborn Vampire Johnny had once intended to evaluate was now the Demon King, a Progenitor-level supernatural ruler and one of the most dangerous individuals connected to Earth. Victor's relationship with Leona had likewise become impossible for Clan Lykos to treat as a minor personal matter.[10]
The historical hostility between Vampires and [[Werewolves]] created particular concern because offspring between the two species had traditionally produced unstable Hybrids. Victor refused to accept this history as justification for controlling Leona's personal life and revealed that he had already succeeded in stabilizing a Vampire–Werewolf Hybrid, proving that the biological incompatibility could be overcome.[10]
[[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]], Adam and Johnny confronted Victor during the dispute. Victor made it clear that any attempt to persecute or control Leona because she had chosen him would be treated as hostility toward his family. His patience eventually ended, and he expelled the Lykos representatives from the room through overwhelming power.[10]
Outside, Johnny finally asked Adam whether the terrifying man they had just encountered was genuinely Leona's husband. Adam confirmed it and revealed an even more unbelievable detail: only a few years earlier Victor had been physically weaker than Johnny himself.[10] Johnny found the statement almost impossible to reconcile with the current Victor, whom he now considered so powerful that even Maya no longer appeared to be a realistic opponent.
The encounter permanently reversed Johnny's original relationship with Victor. The man Johnny once intended to judge as a prospective partner for his little sister had advanced so far beyond him that Johnny could no longer meaningfully evaluate Victor through his own strength.
Johnny remained involved with Clan Lykos during the later conflict between [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]] and [[Volk Fenrir]]. When both emerged as viable candidates to lead Samar, Maya invoked an ancient Lykos principle of neutrality: while two competent rulers remained in contention, Clan Lykos would not determine the result by arbitrarily supporting one side.[11]
Johnny stood beside Adam while Maya explained the Clan's position to Victor. When Victor asked Adam whether he personally agreed with the decision, Johnny attempted to answer on his father's behalf but was immediately silenced by a single look from Victor.[11] Unlike his reckless attack upon Ruby years earlier, Johnny now understood the scale of the Being in front of him well enough to stop without argument.

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Johnny survived into the far later era of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. During the celebration of the 100,000th Imperial Year, he appeared alongside Adam, Edward, Andrew and Frederick while watching the enormous Imperial military parade.[1]
By this point, [[Edward Jonathan Elizabeth Lykos]] had become the new Alpha and active leader of Clan Lykos. Johnny openly treated his younger half-brother according to the respect demanded by Werewolf hierarchy, addressing Edward with considerably more formality than Edward himself considered necessary. Adam mocked Johnny for acting like the ideal subordinate despite casually refusing to show Edward the same respect himself.[1]
The gathering later expanded to include Maya, Leona, [[Bellatrix Lykos]] and members of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s younger generation. Johnny's protectiveness toward the family remained intact; when Andrew made a remark capable of being interpreted as insulting Maya, Johnny joined Adam and Edward in immediately directing hostile attention toward him.[1]
The conversation also revealed how drastically Johnny's private life had changed. When Maya asked where his women were, Johnny explained that they were caring for the children. Jessica had eventually left him and revealed her connection to the Snow Vampire lineage, and the experience had caused Johnny to value the companions who remained by his side more strongly. He had since become the father of several children, although their names, exact number, genders and biological mothers have not been disclosed.[1]
Johnny's attitude toward raising them reflects both his maturity and the harsher values he retained from Werewolf culture. He asked Leona to arrange future training under [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], whose role as Supreme Commander of the Empire's military had also earned her the widespread title Divine Commander.[1] Nearly everyone present considered the request insane because [[Scathach]]'s methods were brutally demanding even by Elderblood standards.
Johnny refused to reconsider. He argued that death was no longer necessarily permanent within their civilization and that he cared more about ensuring his children became strong and disciplined than protecting them from difficult training. When Leona told him to send them to the Imperial Capital after they reached the Empire's military age of twenty-one, Johnny's first reaction was to ask whether twenty-one was already too old to begin.[1]
Leona clarified that she could arrange training as a family favor but could not force Scathach to accept Johnny's children as true disciples. Scathach chose her disciples according to talent, potential and [[Will Solace|Will]]. Johnny was disappointed but accepted that their future would ultimately depend upon the children themselves.[1]

### Relationships

#### Adam William Lykos
[[Adam William Lykos]] is Johnny's father and the source of much of his powerful Alpha heritage. Johnny was born from one of Adam's relationships before the family Adam later created with Leona and Edward's mother, making Johnny the eldest of Adam's known children.[3]
Johnny spent much of his adult life operating independently of Adam's pack, but this distance never meant he stopped caring about his father. When Jessica revealed that Clan Horseman intended to hunt Adam for his blood, Johnny immediately treated the matter as a threat against his own family.[4]
Their relationship with pack hierarchy was more complicated. Johnny claimed independence while Anderson maintained that Adam had never fully classified him as a rebel, implying that the connection between father and son remained active despite Johnny's refusal to formally return.[5]
By the Imperial Era the relationship is considerably more relaxed. Adam freely mocks Johnny's excessive formality toward Edward, while Johnny reacts with the frustration of an adult son accustomed to his father's personality.[1]

#### Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos
[[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]] is Johnny's younger paternal half-sister. Johnny remembers her most strongly as the small child who followed him around calling him her big brother, making it difficult for him to initially accept that she had grown into an adult woman capable of pursuing her own relationships.[6]
His early suspicion of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] therefore originated largely from overprotective brotherly instinct. Johnny never fundamentally accepted the idea that interracial relationships should be prohibited solely because one person was a Vampire and the other a Werewolf.[6]
Johnny eventually accepted Victor as Leona's husband and became one of Victor's brothers-in-law. By the Imperial Era he is comfortable enough with Leona's position inside the Imperial Family to ask her directly for access to [[Scathach]]'s training for his own children.[1]

#### Edward Jonathan Elizabeth Lykos
[[Edward Jonathan Elizabeth Lykos]] is Johnny's younger paternal half-brother. Their lives initially followed drastically different paths: Johnny became an independent mercenary while Edward remained much closer to Adam, Leona and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Human-era social circle.
Edward eventually progressed far beyond Johnny in racial development and became the active Alpha of Clan Lykos. Johnny does not resent this outcome. By Chapter 1146 he openly respects Edward's rank and deliberately adopts the formal attitude expected of a subordinate speaking to the Clan's Alpha.[1]

#### Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood
[[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] is Johnny's grandmother and the former Matriarch of Clan Lykos. Johnny displays considerably more restraint around Maya than he did around most authority figures during his earlier life and repeatedly accompanied her during major political disputes involving [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Samar.[10][11]
By the Imperial Era Maya remains personally interested in Johnny's household and asks directly about his women and children. She strongly supports his intention to have the children trained harshly, agreeing that strength and discipline are preferable to allowing them to remain weak.[1]

#### Judy
Judy is one of Johnny's longest-established partners and a member of his personal pack. Johnny previously helped her escape difficult circumstances and refers to her as his wife during his early appearances.[6]
His protectiveness toward her is extreme. When a man at [[The Lost Club]] attempted to force Judy into providing sexual services, Johnny nearly beat him to death.[6] During his encounter with Anderson, he likewise placed Judy behind himself before considering anything else.
Judy's response to Johnny's Alpha command in Chapter 187 demonstrates the instinctive hierarchy between them. Despite attempting to object, her Werewolf instincts automatically responded when Johnny's eyes glowed and he ordered her to remain behind him.[5]

#### Jinsei
Jinsei is another long-term member of Johnny's household and one of the people capable of calming him during his most aggressive outbursts. She successfully stopped Johnny from killing the man he attacked on Judy's behalf at [[The Lost Club]].[6]
Jinsei was formerly subjected to supernatural exploitation before Johnny took her into his household. Their relationship became strong enough that she was recognized as part of his personal pack.

#### Jessica Horseman Snow
[[Jessica Horseman Snow]] was one of Johnny's former partners and a Noble Vampire associated with Clan Horseman. Despite the danger of acting against her own family, Jessica warned Johnny that her brothers intended to target Adam for Alpha Werewolf blood.[4]
Their relationship suffered from the historical prejudices surrounding their species. Jessica had been raised to consider Vampire–Werewolf Hybrids abominations, only to realize that any child she and Johnny produced could logically become such a Hybrid. Johnny rejected the prejudice consciously, but Jessica also noticed that he unconsciously treated her somewhat differently because she was a Vampire.[4]
Johnny avoided confronting the problem properly, and the relationship eventually ended. Chapter 1146 states that Jessica's departure caused him to appreciate the companions who remained with him more deeply.[1]

#### Roberta Elderblood
[[Roberta Elderblood]] was never genuinely Johnny's wife or lover despite Johnny originally believing otherwise. Roberta manipulated Johnny's memories and emotions strongly enough that he believed they had an established relationship and that he had placed a legitimate Werewolf mate mark upon her.[9]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] exposed the inconsistencies by repeatedly forcing Johnny to think about what Roberta actually meant to him. When Roberta released her influence, the supposed mate mark, affection and supporting memories began disappearing.[9]
Their connection should therefore be treated as a false relationship produced through supernatural manipulation, not an actual former marriage.

#### Anderson
Anderson regarded Johnny as someone who had wasted significant potential. He acknowledged Johnny's Alpha blood and ancestry but believed that pride, poor discipline and independence had prevented him from becoming the warrior expected from Adam's eldest son.[5]
Anderson also warned Johnny that complete independence from Adam's pack could eventually cause Samar's hierarchy to classify him as a rival Alpha. Johnny accepted the danger rather than abandon the women he considered his family.[5]

#### Ruby Elderblood Scarlett
Johnny and [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] developed immediate hostility after meeting at [[The Lost Club]]. Ruby repeatedly referred to him as a mongrel, eventually provoking Johnny into attacking her.[7]
The resulting confrontation was overwhelmingly in Ruby's favor. Johnny's punch failed to penetrate her Ice defense, Ruby froze his entire body almost instantly and she would have driven an Ice-covered hand through his heart if Madam had not intervened.[7]
Johnny managed to break free only after releasing more of his Werewolf transformation.

#### Victor Elderblood
Johnny originally learned of [[Victor Elderblood]] solely because Victor had become romantically involved with Leona. At the time Johnny regarded Victor as an unknown newborn Vampire whose worth as a potential partner needed to be evaluated by Leona's older brother.[6]
Their eventual direct meeting immediately demonstrated how quickly that assumption had become obsolete. Victor overpowered Johnny's natural resistance to Vampiric mental influence, discovered his connection to Adam and later exposed Roberta's manipulation.[8][3][9]
By the time Johnny encountered Victor again on Samar, the gap between them had become incomprehensible. Adam revealed that Victor had once been physically weaker than Johnny, yet Johnny now regarded Victor as a Being even Maya could not realistically defeat.[10]
Victor's marriage to Leona eventually made the two men brothers-in-law.

#### Unnamed Children
Johnny is confirmed to have several children by Chapter 1146. Their exact number, names, genders and biological mothers remain unknown.[1]
Johnny is extremely demanding regarding their future development. He wants them trained by [[Scathach]] and considers the dangers of such training acceptable because he believes weakness would expose them to greater danger in the future.[1]

### Skills & Abilities
Johnny is a natural Alpha Werewolf. Unlike several later members of the Lykos lineage, he has not demonstrated an individualized Divinity, unique Element, Elizabeth Trait or advanced racial evolution. His known abilities are primarily expressions of Alpha Werewolf physiology and the practical skills developed through his mercenary career.

#### Alpha Werewolf Physiology
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Johnny possesses supernatural strength, speed, durability, stamina, agility and reflexes far beyond ordinary Humans and lower-ranking [[Werewolves]].[7][5]
- Superhuman Strength: A direct punch from Johnny against Ruby generated a powerful shockwave capable of damaging the surrounding environment. After increasing his transformation, he also possessed enough strength to break the Ice imprisoning his entire body.[7]
- Superhuman Speed: Johnny possesses supernatural combat speed and reactions, although stronger Werewolves such as Anderson could move beyond his ability to properly follow them.[5]
- Superhuman Durability: Johnny remained alive and combat-capable after Ruby completely froze his body.[7]
- Regenerative Healing Factor: As an Alpha Werewolf, Johnny possesses the accelerated healing inherent to his Race.
- Enhanced Senses: Johnny can detect environmental and supernatural irregularities beyond Human perception. He recognized that the populated street surrounding Adam's home had become unnaturally silent before Judy understood anything was wrong.[5]
- Enhanced Instincts: Johnny possesses strong territorial, predatory and hierarchical instincts characteristic of an Alpha Werewolf.
- Longevity: Johnny survives and remains physically active into the far later Imperial Era, appearing during the celebration of the 100,000th Imperial Year.[1]
- Enhanced Fangs: His fangs become increasingly pronounced when he releases more of his Werewolf nature.[6]
- Enhanced Claws: His partial transformations allow increasingly animalistic natural weaponry.

#### Werewolf Transformation
- Partial Werewolf Transformation: Johnny can release more of his Werewolf nature without fully abandoning his Humanoid body.[6][7]
- His sapphire-blue eyes begin glowing.
- His fangs become more pronounced.
- His body increases in size.
- Fur begins spreading across his body.
- His general appearance becomes increasingly lupine.
- Physical Augmentation: Deeper transformation substantially increases Johnny's physical capabilities. The additional strength allowed him to break free from Ruby's supernatural Ice after being completely immobilized.[7]
- Predatory Presence: Johnny's demeanor and supernatural pressure become increasingly animalistic as his transformation progresses.
- Limitation: Johnny has never demonstrated Maya's Complete Werewolf Transformation or any confirmed Ancient/True Werewolf state.

#### Alpha Authority
- Alpha Command: Johnny possesses instinctive authority over lower-ranking [[Werewolves]] connected to his pack. When Judy attempted to argue during a dangerous confrontation, Johnny asserted his Alpha status and her Werewolf instincts automatically compelled her to obey his order.[5]
- Pack Dominance: Johnny can establish himself as the dominant Alpha inside his personal household and pack structure.
- Hierarchical Influence: This ability operates through Werewolf racial hierarchy rather than conventional magical Mind Manipulation.
- Limitation: Comparable or superior Alphas are not automatically subordinated to Johnny.

#### Werewolf Marking
- Mate/Pack Marking: Johnny possesses access to the Werewolf practice of marking individuals accepted into his personal pack. Jinsei is associated with one of Johnny's marks, while Roberta manipulated Johnny into falsely believing that he had placed a legitimate mate mark upon her.[6][9]
- Pack Connection: The mark represents a supernatural and instinctive relationship between the Alpha and the marked individual.
- Limitation: Roberta's apparent mark was fraudulent and disappeared when she released her manipulation.[9]

#### Resistance to Vampiric Charm
- Mental Manipulation Resistance: Alpha [[Werewolves]] possess natural resistance to ordinary Vampiric Charm. Johnny and the surrounding observers were surprised when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] successfully overwhelmed that resistance.[8]
- Limitation: The resistance is not immunity. A sufficiently powerful Vampire can overpower it, as Victor directly demonstrated.

#### Danger Perception
- Danger Sense: Johnny possesses strong predatory instincts for recognizing threatening situations. He immediately detected that Adam's neighborhood had been unnaturally cleared before Judy noticed anything unusual.[5]
- Threat Assessment: Johnny correctly recognized that fighting Anderson and the [[Werewolves]] accompanying him while protecting Judy would be suicidal and refused to allow pride to dictate his response.[5]

#### Pack Leadership
- Pack Creation and Management: Johnny established a personal household and pack independently of Adam and was willing to accept political danger rather than abandon its members.[5]
- Protective Leadership: He places the safety of individuals he accepts as his own above challenges to his pride.
- Hierarchical Awareness: Despite his independent personality, Johnny understands and respects legitimate Werewolf hierarchy. In the Imperial Era he formally recognizes Edward as Clan Lykos' new Alpha.[1]

#### Mercenary Skills
- Supernatural Combat Experience: Johnny spent years operating as a professional supernatural mercenary and repeatedly dealt with [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]], Vampires and other hostile supernatural actors.[2]
- Artifact Retrieval: His contracts included recovering supernatural objects and disposing of dangerous relics.[4]
- Information Gathering: Johnny regularly purchased information through contacts such as Eddy and used supernatural networks to investigate targets.[6]
- Supernatural [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] Knowledge: His profession gave him working familiarity with Vampires, [[Werewolves]], Hunters, Witches, mercenaries, criminal organizations and establishments such as [[The Lost Club]].
- Professional Pragmatism: Johnny is willing to accept work from supernatural species he personally dislikes when the payment and risk are favorable.[2]
- Combat Judgment: Although his pride can lead him into poor decisions, his mercenary experience allows him to recognize situations where direct combat would accomplish nothing.[5]

### Power and Standing
Johnny is substantially stronger than an ordinary Werewolf and possesses the natural advantages of an Alpha, but he has never reached the highest levels available to members of the Lykos bloodline.
During his confrontation with Ruby, Johnny demonstrated enough physical strength to create a major shockwave through a direct punch and later shattered the Ice imprisoning him after increasing his transformation. Ruby nevertheless remained vastly superior, blocking his strike without injury and incapacitating him almost instantly.[7]
Anderson's later assessment establishes that Johnny's actual power does not represent the limit of his natural potential. As Adam's eldest son, Johnny possesses excellent Alpha blood, but insufficient discipline and excessive pride prevented him from developing as efficiently as he could have.[5]
Johnny also serves as a useful reference point for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s extraordinary growth. Adam states that Victor had once been physically weaker than Johnny only a few years before their meeting on Samar. By Chapter 748, Victor had become so overwhelmingly superior that Johnny considered even Maya incapable of realistically opposing him.[10]
The standards surrounding Johnny became even more extreme during the [[Elderblood Empire]]. By the 100,000th Imperial Year, figures capable of Divine, planetary and greater levels of destruction had become increasingly common among Victor's elite forces and descendants. Johnny remains alive and respected as a Lykos Alpha but has never been shown developing into one of these higher racial or Divine categories.[1]
Johnny has not been confirmed to possess:
- The Elizabeth Trait.
- Ancient Werewolf physiology.
- True Werewolf physiology.
- True Alpha status.
- Complete Werewolf Transformation.
- Godhood or Divinity.
- An individualized Divine Concept.
- Blood Dragon lineage.
- True Dragon physiology.
Until a later chapter explicitly establishes a racial evolution, he should remain classified simply as an Alpha Werewolf.
