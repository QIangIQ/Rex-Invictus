### Appearances

#### Debut
[[Ch21 - Rex Invictus XIV|Chapter 21]]
Edward Jonathan Elizabeth Lykos, originally known as Edward Jonathan Lykos, is an Ancient Werewolf, Alpha, and current head of the [[Lykos Clan]]. He is the son of [[Adam William Lykos]], younger half-brother of [[Johnny Lykos]], older brother of [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]], husband of [[Bellatrix Lykos]], and one of [[Victor Elderblood]]'s oldest childhood friends.[2][1]
Before the supernatural world became a central part of Victor's life, Edward worked as a fitness trainer and personally helped train the physically frail Victor in basic self-defense. Despite being born into one of the most prominent Werewolf families, Edward continued treating Victor as his childhood friend even after learning that he had become a Vampire, refusing to allow the historical hostility between their races to dictate their relationship.[3]
During the Imperial Era, Edward's existence underwent a major transformation. Even before receiving Victor's direct enhancement, he had already developed enough power to contend with Mid-Level Gods. Victor subsequently activated Edward's dormant Elizabeth Genes and restored his racial state into that of an Ancient Werewolf, the ancestral form of modern [[Werewolves]] that existed before the race's decline.[4] Edward later received Victor's personal Divine Blessing alongside [[Andrew Walter]] and [[Frederick Winter]].[5]
After Adam retired from leadership, Edward eventually succeeded him as the Alpha and head of Clan Lykos. Although fully capable of carrying the position, Edward openly admitted that he had never wanted the responsibility and regarded leading the Clan as an exhausting and thankless role.[6]

### Physical Appearance

#### Human Form
Edward is an exceptionally tall man standing approximately 195 centimeters tall. He possesses a muscular and well-trained physique, platinum-blond hair, and blue eyes similar to those of his younger sister Leona. His athletic appearance reflects his background as a professional fitness trainer.[2]
Edward's pale hair and blue eyes are characteristic of the dominant physical traits commonly displayed throughout the Lykos bloodline. Even after enormous periods of accelerated Imperial development, Edward retained a youthful physical appearance due to his supernatural physiology and longevity.[6]

#### Ancient Werewolf Form
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s intervention during the Imperial Era, Edward was transformed from a modern Werewolf into an Ancient Werewolf. This restored him to the ancestral racial state of the Werewolf species before later generations became limited by environmental and biological degradation.[4]
Ancient [[Werewolves]] differ significantly from ordinary modern Werewolves because their development is not fundamentally dependent upon a Pack. They are capable of independently increasing their own strength while still receiving additional benefits when gathered beneath a sufficiently powerful Alpha.[4]
The novel has not yet provided a highly detailed Edward-specific description of his Ancient Werewolf transformation.

### Personality
Edward is generally relaxed, practical, sarcastic, and considerably more grounded than many members of his supernatural family. Around his childhood friends, particularly [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Andrew, and Frederick, he speaks casually and frequently participates in jokes, arguments, and insults regardless of how powerful or politically important they eventually become.[2][4][6]
This familiarity never disappeared even after Victor became the God Emperor of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Edward does not primarily view Victor as an Emperor, Dragon God, or overwhelmingly powerful supernatural Being; to him, Victor remains the same friend he knew while growing up.
Edward is extremely protective of the people he considers family. This was particularly apparent with Leona. When she was bullied because of her appearance during their youth, Edward violently attacked one of the people responsible after the bullying became physical. The incident became serious enough that Edward nearly faced arrest, and Anna Walker and Adam had to intervene to prevent the situation from destroying his future.[2]
His protectiveness does not mean that he disregards the independence of his friends. When Victor was being bullied by [[Luan Davis]], Edward disliked the fact that Victor prevented his friends from interfering, but ultimately respected Victor's decision to settle the matter personally. Edward understood that Victor's pride required him to confront some of his own problems rather than constantly depending upon stronger friends.[2]
Edward is also highly protective of Leona's romantic life. Although he regularly teases her himself, he reacts threateningly when he believes another man is becoming too physically familiar with her. His behavior toward Victor when Victor casually touched Leona illustrates the stereotypically overprotective older-brother side of his personality.[2]
Despite eventually becoming the head of one of the most important Werewolf Clans in existence, Edward possesses little ambition for political authority. After succeeding Adam as Alpha, Edward openly complained about the responsibilities attached to the position and envied his father's retirement. His leadership is consequently based more upon obligation and competence than personal desire for power.[6]
Edward is also remarkably adaptable. Throughout his life he witnessed Victor become a Vampire, Vampire Count, Progenitor, Dragon, God, Emperor, and later an existence far beyond ordinary supernatural standards. Edward himself eventually married his aunt, acquired the Elizabeth Trait, became an Ancient Werewolf, survived vast periods of accelerated time, and assumed control of Clan Lykos. Despite these extraordinary changes, his personality remained recognizably similar to that of the man introduced near the beginning of the story.[1][4][6]
Edward also possesses some lingering caution concerning romantic relationships. Before the beginning of the story, he nearly married an extremely jealous and obsessive woman. The experience left enough of an impression on him that Victor's descriptions of his possessive wives immediately caused Edward to become concerned.[7]

### Synopsis

#### The Awakening
Edward had already been one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s closest friends for many years before the latter entered the supernatural world. Because Victor's RH-null blood left him chronically weak and physically restricted, Edward helped him train within the limitations of his body. Rather than attempting to make Victor physically overpower ordinary people, Edward taught him practical self-defense techniques intended to help a weaker person escape danger or incapacitate an aggressor long enough to flee.[2]
Edward's protective nature was also well established during this period. Leona had frequently been bullied because of her unusual physical characteristics, and Edward eventually assaulted one of the students responsible after the bullying escalated. His retaliation nearly led to his arrest, but the resulting fear surrounding him caused the harassment around Leona to stop.[2]
By the beginning of the story, Edward had stopped working primarily as a fitness trainer and had become the owner of a bar. He reunited with Victor at Leona's veterinary clinic alongside [[Andrew Walter]]. Edward immediately noticed Victor's dramatic increase in height, musculature, and physical health, but did not aggressively interrogate him about the change.[2]
The conversation quickly turned toward Victor's past conflict with Luan. Edward admitted that he disliked Victor refusing his and Andrew's help at the time, but also understood why Victor wanted to settle the matter personally. This demonstrated that Edward's friendship with Victor was not based upon constantly protecting him from every problem, but upon respecting his decisions even when Edward disagreed with them.[2]
When Victor revealed that he was married, Edward and Andrew were stunned. Their surprise became greater after learning that Victor's wives displayed intensely jealous and possessive personalities. Because Edward had previously suffered through an unhealthy relationship with a similarly obsessive woman, he warned Victor about the dangers of becoming involved with "yanderes", only to discover that Victor actively liked precisely those traits.[7]
Edward's supernatural identity remained unknown to Victor during these early interactions. Shortly afterward, Adam determined that Victor had become a Vampire rather than awakening as a Werewolf and instructed Leona to avoid involving herself in Vampire affairs. Edward overheard the discussion and disagreed with the idea that Victor's Race should change their relationship. He had known Victor since childhood and considered that history far more important than the instinctive rivalry between Vampires and [[Werewolves]].[3]
Edward also witnessed the rapid development of Leona's newly awakened Werewolf strength. After repeatedly teasing his sister regarding Victor, Leona punched Edward hard enough to send him through a wall. Edward recovered from the impact and immediately recognized how quickly Leona's physical power had increased despite having awakened only recently.[3]

#### A New Pillar
Several months later, Edward reunited with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] after the latter returned from [[Nightingale]] as its newly appointed Fifth Vampire Count. Despite Victor's new strength, political status, and reputation, Edward and the rest of his original Human friend group continued interacting with him naturally rather than treating him as a distant supernatural aristocrat.[8]
Victor tested Edward's Werewolf strength during their reunion while discussing the hierarchy and development of the Werewolf race. Their interaction reinforced how unusual Victor's friendship with the Lykos family was: Edward belonged to a Race traditionally hostile toward Vampires, yet the two men continued regarding one another as friends instead of enemies.[8]
Edward later demonstrated the mobility of a powerful Werewolf when he scaled a building more than thirty stories tall within seconds, reaching its upper levels with ease despite the extreme vertical distance.[9]
Victor's later dealings with Adam, Johnny, and other members of the Lykos family gradually stabilized the relationship between Clan Lykos and Victor's growing Vampire faction. Edward therefore remained one of the clearest examples that the ancient hostility between Vampires and [[Werewolves]] did not prevent genuine personal relationships from developing between individuals.[10]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
By the era of the [[Elderblood Empire]], Edward remained part of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s original circle of friends alongside Andrew and Frederick. Despite the immense difference in status between themselves and Victor, their reunions continued resembling ordinary meetings between longtime friends rather than audiences with an Emperor.[11]
By this period, Edward had also married [[Bellatrix Lykos]], one of his aunts. The marriage had become another subject of jokes among the group, with Andrew openly asking Edward what it was like to be married to his own aunt.[1]
Victor later evaluated Edward's current strength and discovered that his old friend had already reached a level where he could fight Mid-Level Gods. This assessment occurred before Victor performed any new genetic or racial modifications upon him.[4]
Victor then decided to improve Edward even further. He first activated Edward's dormant Elizabeth Genes. Edward was initially confused because the Elizabeth mutation had historically manifested primarily in women of Clan Lykos, but Victor simply ignored the limitation and awakened the potential within him. The activation immediately caused Edward's strength and instincts to increase dramatically.[4]
Victor then altered Edward on an even deeper racial level and transformed him into an Ancient Werewolf. Edward immediately understood the significance of what had happened: rather than simply strengthening his existing body, Victor had restored the ancestral state possessed by the Werewolf Race before generations of regression weakened their descendants.[4]
Ancient [[Werewolves]] could develop individually without being fundamentally restricted by Pack dependence. Packs and powerful Alphas could still amplify their growth, but their existence was no longer built around the same developmental limitations affecting modern Werewolves.[4]
Edward regarded the restoration of his ancestral Race as one of the most important aspects of Victor's gift, understanding that his potential had been fundamentally changed rather than temporarily increased.[4]
Victor later revealed a fraction of his overwhelming existence to Edward, Andrew, and Frederick before bestowing a personal Divine Blessing upon them. He declared that even if their individual journeys eventually took them to distant worlds or separate Realities, the Emperor would continue to remain with his oldest friends.[5]
Shortly afterward, Edward observed Frederick use Victor's Divinity of Fear through an Onmyō spell. Edward judged that the attack was powerful enough to kill some of his strongest Werewolf subordinates, but remained confident that he personally could evade and destroy it without great difficulty. This demonstrated the enormous difference between Edward's post-transformation strength and that of ordinary elite Werewolves.[5]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Edward continued living within the [[Elderblood Empire]] throughout its accelerated development. Even after approximately one hundred thousand internal years had passed, Edward, Andrew, and Frederick could still gather in a bar, watch Imperial events, and exchange essentially the same jokes and insults they had used during their youth.[6]
By this era, Clan Lykos had undergone a leadership transition. Adam had retired, and Edward had succeeded him as the Clan's new Alpha and head. His older half-brother [[Johnny Lykos]] formally acknowledged Edward's authority within Clan affairs despite being older and powerful in his own right.[6]
Edward himself had little enthusiasm for the promotion. He complained that he had never wanted to become Alpha and openly described leadership as a thankless position, while Adam enjoyed the freedom of retirement and mocked his son's frustration.[6]
Despite his complaints, Edward continued fulfilling the responsibilities of the position and remained recognized as the legitimate leader of Clan Lykos. His rise transformed him from the fitness trainer and bar owner introduced near the beginning of the story into an Ancient Werewolf Alpha, bearer of the Elizabeth Trait, and head of one of the most important Werewolf families within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Empire.

### Skills & Abilities
Ancient Werewolf Physiology: Edward possesses the restored ancestral physiology of the Werewolf Race after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] fundamentally reconstructed him during the Imperial Era. Ancient [[Werewolves]] possess significantly greater developmental freedom than ordinary modern Werewolves and can increase their individual strength without being fundamentally dependent upon a Pack, although gathering beneath a powerful Alpha can still provide further benefits.[4]
- Superhuman Strength: Edward possesses enormous physical strength even by supernatural standards. Before Victor activated his Elizabeth Genes or restored his Ancient Werewolf physiology, Edward had already become strong enough to fight Mid-Level Gods.[4]
- Superhuman Speed: Edward can move far beyond Human physical limitations. He has demonstrated the ability to scale a building more than thirty stories tall within seconds.[9]
- Enhanced Agility: His Werewolf physiology grants exceptional jumping, climbing, balance, and movement capabilities, allowing him to traverse vertical urban environments with little difficulty.[9]
- Superhuman Durability: Edward possesses physical durability far beyond ordinary Humans. Even much earlier in the story, he recovered rapidly after Leona punched him through a wall shortly after awakening her own Werewolf abilities.[3]
- Enhanced Endurance: Edward can sustain extreme physical exertion and supernatural combat beyond the limits of ordinary Humans. His Ancient Werewolf evolution further improved the developmental potential of his body.[4]
- Regeneration: As a Werewolf, Edward possesses the accelerated regenerative abilities characteristic of his Race.
- Enhanced Senses: Edward possesses supernatural senses capable of detecting and evaluating changes in the physiology of other Beings. After Victor transformed Andrew, Edward could recognize that Andrew remained fundamentally Human while simultaneously having evolved into a higher form of the species.[4]
- Longevity: Edward remained physically active and youthful after living through approximately one hundred thousand years of accelerated Imperial development.[6]
- Werewolf Transformation: Edward can transform into the combat form of his Race. After Victor's intervention, this transformation reflects the physiology of an Ancient Werewolf rather than that of an ordinary modern Werewolf.[4]
- Predatory Instincts: Edward possesses highly developed animalistic instincts associated with Werewolf physiology. These instincts were further enhanced when Victor activated his Elizabeth Genes.[4]
- Alpha Authority: Edward eventually became the recognized Alpha and head of Clan Lykos after Adam's retirement. Even other powerful members of the Clan, including Johnny, formally recognize his position within Clan hierarchy.[6]

#### Elizabeth Trait
The Elizabeth Trait is a rare mutation originating from [[Elizabeth Lykos]], one of the most important ancestors of the Lykos bloodline. The mutation strengthens the instinctive side of its bearer and significantly increases their potential for physical and supernatural advancement.[12]
Natural bearers of the mutation can experience considerable difficulty controlling their heightened instincts and emotions. The mutation is extremely rare and historically appears only once every several generations, with children possessing it traditionally receiving the name Elizabeth.[13]
Edward represents an unusual case. Unlike Leona, he did not naturally manifest the Elizabeth Trait during childhood. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later discovered dormant Elizabeth potential within Edward and deliberately activated it during the Imperial Era.[4]
Edward initially believed that the mutation was restricted to women of the Clan, but Victor ignored that traditional limitation and forcibly awakened the corresponding ancestral genetics. Edward immediately experienced an enormous increase in strength and instinctive potential.[4]
The Lykos bloodline itself is exceptionally dominant. Its inherited characteristics are described as sufficiently predatory that even the bloodlines of Gods or Progenitors do not automatically overwrite the Lykos traits in descendants.[14]

#### Ancient Werewolf Evolution
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s transformation of Edward into an Ancient Werewolf represented a deeper change than merely activating the Elizabeth Trait. He restored Edward to the ancestral form of the Werewolf Race, fundamentally changing the developmental limits of his species.[4]
Ancient [[Werewolves]] possess several advantages over their modern descendants:
- They can grow through their own individual power rather than being fundamentally dependent upon a Pack.[4]
- Their racial potential is significantly greater than that of ordinary modern Werewolves.[4]
- They can still receive additional benefits when united beneath an Alpha.[4]
- Their physiology represents the more complete ancestral state of the Werewolf species before later generations regressed.[4]
Because Edward simultaneously possesses Ancient Werewolf physiology and an activated Elizabeth Trait, his current racial state combines two exceptional sources of Werewolf potential.

#### Combat Ability
Edward possesses extensive practical combat knowledge. Even during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Human life, he understood enough self-defense and physical training to teach Victor techniques adapted specifically around his severe medical limitations.[2]
His combat ability continued developing alongside his supernatural strength. By Chapter 1117, Victor judged Edward capable of fighting Mid-Level Gods before giving him any additional racial enhancement.[4]
After becoming an Ancient Werewolf, Edward demonstrated enough combat awareness to evaluate Frederick's magically amplified Fear attack and determine that it could kill some of Clan Lykos' strongest ordinary fighters. Despite recognizing its danger, Edward believed he could evade and destroy it himself.[5]

#### Divine Blessing
Edward received a personal Blessing directly from [[Victor Elderblood]] alongside [[Andrew Walter]] and [[Frederick Winter]]. Victor bestowed the Blessing after showing his friends a fragment of his true existence and promising that the Emperor would remain with them even if their journeys eventually carried them into distant worlds or separate Realities.[5]
The exact individual effects of Edward's Blessing have not yet been fully explained.

#### Leadership
Edward became the head and Alpha of Clan Lykos following Adam's retirement.[6]
Although Edward personally dislikes administration and never sought the position, he possesses the strength and authority necessary to command one of the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s major Werewolf Clans. His older brother Johnny formally acknowledges Edward's authority when Clan hierarchy is relevant.[6]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
Edward and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] have been friends since childhood. Edward knew Victor when he was still an ordinary Human suffering from severe physical limitations and became one of the people who helped him compensate for those weaknesses through basic physical training and self-defense.[2]
Their friendship survived Victor's transformation into a Vampire despite the traditional hostility between Vampires and [[Werewolves]]. Edward explicitly rejected the idea that Victor's new Race erased the person he had known since childhood.[3]
Edward later became Victor's brother-in-law through Leona. Even after Victor rose to become God Emperor, Edward continued speaking to him as an old friend rather than treating him with the reverence shown by ordinary Imperial citizens.
Victor likewise continued valuing Edward as one of his original friends. He personally activated Edward's Elizabeth Genes, restored him into an Ancient Werewolf, and later gave him his Divine Blessing.[4][5]

#### Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos
Leona is Edward's younger sister and one of the people he has protected most fiercely throughout his life.
When Leona suffered bullying during their youth, Edward responded violently enough to nearly be arrested after one of the incidents became physical. His reputation afterward effectively ended the local harassment directed toward her.[2]
Their relationship also contains constant sibling teasing. Edward openly mocked Leona's feelings toward [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] until she eventually punched him through a wall shortly after awakening her Werewolf abilities.[3]
Despite teasing her himself, Edward remains highly protective when other men approach Leona romantically or physically.[2]

#### Adam William Lykos
Adam is Edward's father and predecessor as head of Clan Lykos.
After Adam retired from leadership, Edward inherited the position of Alpha and Clan Head. Their relationship remained informal even after the transition, with Adam openly enjoying his retirement while mocking Edward for complaining about the responsibilities he inherited.[6]

#### Johnny Lykos
Johnny is Edward's older half-brother.
Despite being older and an Alpha himself, Johnny recognizes Edward as the current head of Clan Lykos and addresses him according to his position when Clan hierarchy is involved.[6]
Edward finds Johnny's excessive formality irritating outside official situations and prefers that his older brother simply behave normally around him.[6]

#### Bellatrix Lykos
Bellatrix is both Edward's aunt and eventual wife.
Their marriage had already occurred by the time Edward reunited with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] during the Imperial Era. The unusual relationship quickly became a recurring source of jokes among Edward's friends, particularly Andrew.[1]

#### Andrew Walter
Andrew is one of Edward's oldest friends and belonged to the same childhood social circle as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Frederick.
Their relationship remained remarkably stable throughout the enormous passage of time within the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Even after thousands upon thousands of years, Andrew and Edward continued exchanging jokes, insults, and complaints as though very little had changed.[11][6]

#### Frederick Winter
Frederick is another member of Edward and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s original friend group.
Edward remained close enough to Frederick throughout the Imperial Era to evaluate his supernatural development directly and casually compare Fred's spells to the combat capabilities of Clan Lykos fighters.[5]
Even after approximately one hundred thousand years of accelerated civilization development, Edward, Frederick, and Andrew still regularly spent time together.[6]
