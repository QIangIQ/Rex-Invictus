### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 773
Iona Lykos is an Alpha Werewolf of the [[Lykos Clan]], a granddaughter of former Matriarch [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]], and both the niece and wife of [[Connor Lykos]]. She is one of Connor's two known wives alongside her aunt [[Daphne Lykos]] and regularly accompanies him during matters concerning the internal politics and succession of Clan Lykos.[1]
Compared with Daphne, Iona is considerably more outspoken, sarcastic and provocative. Although she supports Connor's efforts to ensure that Clan Lykos receives a competent future leader, she frequently complains about the complicated political game he created around [[Bellatrix Lykos]].[2]

### Physical Appearance
Iona has white hair, a characteristic commonly seen among members of the Lykos bloodline.
No detailed canonical description of her height, eye color, skin tone, clothing or transformed Werewolf appearance has currently been provided.

### Personality
Iona is outspoken, sarcastic, playful and considerably less restrained than [[Daphne Lykos]]. She has little hesitation about saying whatever comes to mind, even when her comments are inappropriate enough for Daphne to immediately reprimand her.[2]
She possesses the strong hierarchical instincts associated with the women descended from [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]]. Connor's biological father explains that many women of Maya's lineage possess an instinctive desire to seek a sufficiently powerful male Alpha and, when unable to find one, may instead attempt to dominate the men around them. Iona and Daphne are specifically identified as having displayed this behavior, while Connor proved powerful enough to establish himself as the Alpha within their relationships.[2]
Iona therefore places considerable importance on strength and dominance. When Connor's father complained about seeing Maya spending time with [[Victor Elderblood]], Iona showed very little sympathy and instead mocked him for being unable to compete with Victor.[2]
Despite frequently complaining about Connor, Iona genuinely admires his dedication to Clan Lykos. When Connor states that Lykos is his home and that he refuses to allow an incompetent individual to govern his family, both Iona and Daphne smile because his devotion to the Clan is one of the qualities they admire about him.[2]
She is also possessive toward Connor. When he admits that his apparent attraction toward [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]] was not entirely an act, both Iona and Daphne react with visible possessiveness.[2]

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Iona first appeared during [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]]' visit to the sacred library of the [[Lykos Clan]]. Leona and her companions were discussing the history of [[Elizabeth Lykos]] and the rare Elizabeth Trait with [[Bellatrix Lykos]] when [[Connor Lykos]] arrived at the entrance.[1]
Connor was accompanied by his two wives. One was his biological sister [[Daphne Lykos]], while the other was his niece, Iona Lykos.[1]
Connor immediately questioned why Vampires had been allowed into the Clan's sacred library. His attention eventually shifted toward Leona, and when he appeared interested in her, the members of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household assigned to protect her immediately reacted.[2]
[[Bruna Elderblood]] appeared in front of Connor and struck him in the abdomen before he could properly respond. Iona and Daphne attempted to intervene but were immediately restrained by blood-red threads controlled by Victor's maids.[2]
[[Maria Elderblood]] restrained the women closely enough that neither attempted to continue resisting. The maids casually discussed several ways they could incapacitate the [[Werewolves]] before [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]] ordered them not to escalate the confrontation unnecessarily.[2]
Connor, Iona and Daphne were subsequently expelled from the library.
Once outside, Connor revealed that the confrontation had largely been deliberate. He wanted to determine what kind of people Victor had assigned to protect Leona and what their reaction would reveal about Leona's importance within Victor's faction.[2]
Iona and Daphne were irritated by his method. They pointed out that deliberately provoking members of Victor's household could have caused a diplomatic disaster between Clan Lykos and the Demon King.[2]
Connor nevertheless considered the information worthwhile. The encounter demonstrated that the women accompanying Leona were not ordinary servants but extremely dangerous members of Victor's inner circle.
The conversation then shifted toward Connor's apparent rivalry with Bellatrix over leadership of Clan Lykos. Iona directly questioned whether Leona's arrival meant Connor could finally stop pretending to want the position.[2]
Both Iona and Daphne already knew that Connor had no genuine desire to govern the Clan. He maintained his position as Bellatrix's rival because he believed she required genuine competition to develop into a competent leader.[2]
Iona openly complained about the situation and blamed Connor for creating an unnecessarily complicated political game. Connor explained that even if he never became Clan Leader, he would retain enough influence to remove Bellatrix if she eventually proved incapable of protecting their family.[2]
When Iona complained that Connor cared too much about the Clan, he answered that Lykos was his home and that his family lived there. He therefore refused to allow someone mediocre to control it. Despite their complaints, both Iona and Daphne smiled at his answer.[2]
The women then reminded Connor that Maya had apparently already offered him the position of Clan Leader directly. Connor admitted that he rejected the offer because actually governing the Clan would be troublesome, leaving both wives irritated by the fact that his alternative plan was significantly more complicated.[2]
Later, Connor's biological father encountered the group after witnessing Maya spending time with Victor. He explained that Victor possessed precisely the overwhelmingly dominant Alpha qualities that the women of Maya's lineage instinctively desired.[2]
He used Iona and Daphne themselves as examples, explaining that both women had inherited similar instincts and that Connor had been powerful enough to establish himself as their Alpha rather than being dominated by them.[2]
Iona responded to the man's distress by jokingly suggesting that they should buy him a green hat. Daphne immediately reprimanded her, demonstrating once again the contrast between Iona's provocative personality and Daphne's more restrained behavior.[2]
Iona has not received another substantial individual storyline in the currently documented material.

### Skills & Abilities
Iona has not participated in a dedicated battle and has not demonstrated a unique Element or named supernatural technique. Her confirmed capabilities primarily derive from her Alpha Werewolf physiology and Lykos ancestry.

#### Alpha Werewolf Physiology
- Alpha Werewolf Physiology: Iona is an Alpha Werewolf descended from one of Samar's oldest Werewolf bloodlines.
- Superhuman Strength: Alpha [[Werewolves]] possess physical strength considerably greater than ordinary Humans and lower-ranking Werewolves.
- Superhuman Speed: Iona possesses supernatural movement speed and reflexes.
- Superhuman Durability: Her Werewolf physiology grants supernatural physical resilience.
- Enhanced Stamina: Alpha Werewolves possess substantially greater endurance than ordinary Humans.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Iona possesses the accelerated biological recovery inherent to Werewolf physiology.
- Enhanced Senses: Her Race grants heightened smell, hearing and predatory perception.
- Longevity: Werewolves possess significantly greater natural lifespans than ordinary Humans.
- Werewolf Transformation: Iona possesses the racial ability to manifest more pronounced Werewolf characteristics, although her individual transformed form has not been shown.
- Natural Weapons: Werewolf transformations provide supernatural claws and fangs.
- Alpha Instincts: Iona possesses powerful instincts relating to dominance and hierarchy. Connor's father specifically identifies her as one of the women who inherited Maya's tendency to seek a sufficiently powerful Alpha partner.[2]
- Predatory Awareness: Like other Alpha Werewolves, Iona possesses instinctive sensitivity toward powerful supernatural beings and threats.

#### Lykos Heritage
- Lykos Bloodline: Iona is a granddaughter of [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] and consequently belongs to the ancient lineage descending from [[Elizabeth Lykos]] and the first Werewolf Progenitor Licael.
- Enhanced Ancestral Potential: Descendants of an Elizabeth carrier can inherit increased potential even when the complete mutation does not manifest within them.[3][1]
- Strong Alpha-Oriented Instincts: Iona inherited the instinctive tendency found among several female descendants of Maya to seek a sufficiently powerful Alpha.[2]
- No Confirmed Elizabeth Trait: Iona has never been identified as a carrier of the Elizabeth Trait.

#### Combat and Political Skills
- Combat Readiness: Iona immediately attempted to respond when Bruna attacked Connor, demonstrating that she is willing to enter combat in defense of her husband.[2]
- Threat Recognition: After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s maids restrained her, Iona correctly recognized the overwhelming difference in power and did not continue resisting recklessly.[2]
- Political Awareness: Iona understands the internal succession struggle of Clan Lykos, Connor's actual objectives and Bellatrix's position well enough to actively participate in Connor's plans.[2]

### Relationships

#### Connor Lykos
[[Connor Lykos]] is simultaneously Iona's uncle and husband.[1]
Iona regularly accompanies Connor alongside Daphne and is fully aware of the truth behind his supposed campaign to become leader of Clan Lykos. Although she frequently complains about how troublesome Connor's political schemes are, she continues supporting him because she understands that his ultimate objective is protecting their family.[2]
Connor's father explains that Iona possessed the same powerful Alpha-seeking instincts displayed by many women descended from Maya. Connor was sufficiently powerful to establish himself as Iona's Alpha rather than being dominated by her.[2]
Iona is also openly possessive toward Connor and reacts negatively when he admits that he genuinely found Leona attractive.[2]

#### Daphne Lykos
[[Daphne Lykos]] is Iona's aunt and fellow wife of Connor.[1]
The two women frequently accompany one another and cooperate in Connor's plans. Their personalities differ considerably: Daphne is cautious and restrained, while Iona is far more sarcastic and provocative.
Daphne consequently spends a noticeable amount of time reprimanding Iona for inappropriate comments.[2]

#### Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood
[[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] is Iona's grandmother.
Iona inherited several strong Werewolf instincts associated with Maya's bloodline. Connor's political rivalry with Bellatrix also appears to have developed after discussions involving Maya, something Iona complains about because it dragged both her and Daphne into the Clan's succession struggle.[2]

#### Bellatrix Lykos
[[Bellatrix Lykos]] is Iona's aunt and Connor's nominal rival for leadership of Clan Lykos.
Iona knows that Connor does not genuinely desire Bellatrix's position. His rivalry is intended to force Bellatrix to improve until she becomes competent enough to lead the Clan without endangering it.[2]
Iona does not display genuine hostility toward Bellatrix.
