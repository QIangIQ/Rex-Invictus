### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 762
Marcelo Lykos is an Alpha Werewolf of the [[Lykos Clan]], one of the many sons of former Matriarch [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]], and one of the husbands of his sister [[Bellatrix Lykos]]. He was introduced alongside Bellatrix, [[Ivan Lykos]] and [[Rodrigo Lykos]] when they encountered [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]] and [[Natalia Elderblood née Alioth]] during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s visit to Samar.[1]
Marcelo has received very little individual focus in the story. His primary canonical appearance establishes his place within Maya's enormous family and demonstrates the unconventional family structure practiced by several members of Clan Lykos.[1]

### Physical Appearance
Marcelo is a chocolate-skinned man with white hair. Bellatrix describes him as having a notably baby-faced appearance, distinguishing him from his older and more intimidating brother [[Ivan Lykos]].[1]
No detailed description of Marcelo's height, eye color, clothing or transformed Werewolf appearance has currently been given.

### Personality
Very little has been established about Marcelo's personality. During his introduction he behaves calmly and casually when Bellatrix presents him to Leona, simply greeting his niece rather than making a dramatic first impression.[1]
He also displays a teasing and sarcastic side. After Bellatrix introduces the considerably shorter [[Rodrigo Lykos]] by pointing out that he stands only 165 cm tall, Rodrigo protests that he would be considered normal-sized in parts of Asia. Marcelo immediately dismisses the argument and sarcastically calls him a "dwarf", suggesting that playful insults are common among the members of Bellatrix's household.[1]
Beyond this brief interaction, the novel has not provided enough material to establish a more detailed personality.

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Marcelo first appeared in Chapter 762 during [[Victor Elderblood]]'s visit to Samar. [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]] and [[Natalia Elderblood née Alioth]] encountered [[Bellatrix Lykos]], one of Maya's many daughters, accompanied by three male members of the Lykos family.[1]
After introducing herself, Bellatrix presented each of the men to Leona. The oldest and most intimidating-looking member of the group was [[Ivan Lykos]], whom Bellatrix identified as both her brother and husband. She then introduced Marcelo:
"The baby-faced one is Marcelo; he's my brother, my husband, and your uncle as well."[1]
Marcelo simply greeted Leona, who was increasingly confused by the complicated marital structure of her maternal family.
Bellatrix next introduced [[Rodrigo Lykos]], her nephew, husband and Leona's cousin. Bellatrix immediately mocked Rodrigo for standing only 165 cm tall. When Rodrigo protested that the rest of the family were simply abnormally tall and that his height would be ordinary in Asia, Marcelo sarcastically dismissed his complaints and called him a dwarf.[1]
The scene primarily serves to introduce several members of Maya's enormous family and demonstrate how different the marital customs within older Werewolf society could be from the Human family structure Leona was accustomed to.
Marcelo has not received another substantial individual storyline in the currently documented chapters.

### Skills & Abilities
Marcelo has not participated in a dedicated battle or demonstrated any named supernatural technique. His confirmed abilities are primarily those associated with being an Alpha Werewolf.

#### Alpha Werewolf Physiology
- Alpha Werewolf Physiology: Marcelo is an Alpha Werewolf and therefore possesses physical and supernatural capabilities beyond ordinary Humans and lower-ranking [[Werewolves]].
- Superhuman Strength: His Alpha physiology grants physical strength far exceeding conventional Humans.
- Superhuman Speed: Marcelo possesses supernatural movement speed and reflexes.
- Superhuman Durability: His body possesses the resilience characteristic of powerful Werewolves.
- Enhanced Stamina: Werewolf physiology provides substantially greater endurance than ordinary Humans.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Marcelo possesses the accelerated healing characteristic of his Race.
- Enhanced Senses: He possesses the heightened smell, hearing and predatory awareness natural to Werewolves.
- Longevity: As a supernatural Werewolf, Marcelo possesses a lifespan significantly exceeding that of ordinary Humans.
- Werewolf Transformation: Marcelo possesses the racial capacity to transform and manifest more pronounced Werewolf characteristics, although his individual transformed state has never been shown.
- Enhanced Fangs and Claws: His Werewolf physiology provides natural predatory weapons when sufficiently transformed.
- Predatory Instincts: As an Alpha, Marcelo possesses heightened instincts concerning danger, hierarchy and dominance.
- Alpha Presence: Marcelo possesses the supernatural presence associated with Alpha Werewolves, though no individual feat involving his authority over other Wolves has been shown.

#### Lykos Heritage
- Lykos Bloodline: Marcelo is one of the biological children of [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] and therefore descends from the ancient lineage of [[Elizabeth Lykos]] and Licael.
- High Racial Potential: Maya's descendants possess strong Werewolf ancestry and greater potential than ordinary members of the Race, although the exact extent of Marcelo's individual talent has never been specified.[2][3]
- No Confirmed Elizabeth Trait: Marcelo has never been identified as a bearer of the rare Elizabeth Trait.

### Relationships

#### Bellatrix Lykos
[[Bellatrix Lykos]] is both Marcelo's sister and wife. Their relationship reflects the unconventional family structure practiced within portions of the enormous [[Lykos Clan]].[1]
Bellatrix introduces Marcelo openly as her brother and husband when meeting Leona and displays no discomfort regarding the relationship, indicating that such arrangements are accepted within their branch of Werewolf society.[1]
Marcelo appears alongside Bellatrix, Ivan and Rodrigo as part of her immediate household during his introduction.

#### Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood
[[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] is Marcelo's mother and the former Matriarch of the [[Lykos Clan]]. Marcelo is one of more than fifty children Maya produced across her extremely long life.
No substantial one-on-one interaction between Maya and Marcelo has currently been shown.

#### Ivan Lykos
[[Ivan Lykos]] is Marcelo's brother and a fellow husband of Bellatrix. Both men appear together during Chapter 762 when Bellatrix introduces her family to Leona.[1]
Ivan is described as the oldest-looking member of the three men and possessing a much more intimidating face, while Marcelo is distinguished by his comparatively youthful, baby-faced appearance.

#### Rodrigo Lykos
[[Rodrigo Lykos]] is Marcelo's nephew and another member of Bellatrix's household. During their introduction, Marcelo openly mocks Rodrigo's comparatively short height after Bellatrix points out that he stands only 165 cm tall.[1]
When Rodrigo protests that everyone else in the family is simply abnormally tall, Marcelo sarcastically calls him a dwarf.[1]

#### Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos
[[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]] is Marcelo's niece through [[Adam William Lykos]]. Marcelo first meets her on-page during Chapter 762, when Bellatrix introduces him as both her brother, husband and Leona's uncle.[1]
Leona's confusion toward the family structure provides much of the humor surrounding Marcelo's introduction.
