### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 773
Connor Lykos is an Alpha Werewolf of the [[Lykos Clan]], one of the many children of the former Clan Matriarch [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]]. He was formerly regarded as one of the principal candidates to succeed Maya as leader of the Clan and is stated to be among the strongest of Maya's children, ranking below [[Adam William Lykos]] among the siblings directly compared during [[Victor Elderblood]]'s visit to Samar.[1]
Although Connor publicly participated in the Lykos succession struggle against his sister [[Bellatrix Lykos]], he did not actually desire the position for himself. His rivalry with Bellatrix was deliberately maintained to force her to mature into a ruler capable of protecting the Clan. Connor was prepared to support her if she proved competent, but equally willing to remove her if she endangered the Lykos family after taking power.[1]

### Physical Appearance
Connor is a tall man with a strong, well-developed physique and an imposing posture befitting an Alpha Werewolf. His eyes are sky blue and can visibly brighten when his predatory instincts or interest are stimulated.[1]
The novel has not provided a detailed canonical description of Connor's hair color, exact height or other individual physical characteristics. His current page image is therefore an artistic representation rather than an established canonical design.

### Personality
Connor is intelligent, calculating and unusually pragmatic for someone raised within the instinct-driven culture of the [[Lykos Clan]]. [[Victor Elderblood]] directly assessed him as a man with a sharp mind but also a noticeably lazy disposition. Despite the enormous size of Maya's family, Connor was one of only a small number of her descendants who immediately attracted Victor's attention because of his competence.[2]
His apparent ambition for leadership is largely deceptive. Connor considers ruling the Clan excessively troublesome and has no genuine desire to become its permanent leader. Nevertheless, his lack of personal ambition does not translate into indifference toward the Clan. Connor is intensely protective of the Lykos family and refuses to allow someone he considers mediocre or incompetent to gain uncontested control over it. He deliberately positioned himself as Bellatrix's political rival because he believed she required genuine competition to grow into an acceptable leader. If she ultimately failed in that responsibility, Connor was prepared to use his own influence to remove her.[1]
Connor also possesses a methodical and somewhat manipulative approach to gathering information. Rather than openly questioning Bellatrix or Leona, he deliberately created an uncomfortable encounter with Leona's escorts in order to observe how they reacted and estimate the political importance Victor placed upon his wife. Even after being effortlessly subdued, Connor focused on analyzing what the confrontation revealed rather than allowing wounded pride to determine his next action.[1]
This pragmatism extends to Victor himself. After observing the quality of the women Victor had assigned to protect Leona, Connor concluded that maintaining a favorable relationship with the Demon King would be much more beneficial to Clan Lykos than antagonizing him. He was equally quick to recognize how dangerous Victor's growing relationship with Maya could become for the Clan's existing political structure and immediately began gathering information instead of reacting emotionally.[1]
Connor nevertheless retains the instinctive characteristics of a powerful Lykos Alpha. He is confident, territorial and capable of genuine predatory attraction, while his relationship with [[Daphne Lykos]] and [[Iona Lykos]] is built partly around the fact that he was strong enough to establish himself as the dominant Alpha between them.[1]

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Connor was introduced during [[Victor Elderblood]]'s extended visit to Samar, when Leona began learning more about the history of her maternal family and the [[Elizabeth Bloodline]]. While [[Bellatrix Lykos]] explained the significance of the Elizabeth mutation and Leona's position within the Clan, Connor entered the Clan library accompanied by his wives, [[Daphne Lykos]] and [[Iona Lykos]]. Bellatrix identified him as her brother and one of the leading candidates to become the next head of Clan Lykos.[3]
Connor immediately questioned why Vampires had been permitted inside the sacred territory of a Werewolf Clan. His attention shifted between Leona and the women accompanying her, but the confrontation ended almost instantly when Victor's maids responded to what they interpreted as a possible threat. [[Bruna Elderblood]] struck Connor in the abdomen with enough force to cripple his movement, while the remaining maids subdued Daphne and Iona before any of them could mount an effective response.[1]
The encounter demonstrated that Connor was far from weak. Bellatrix internally regarded him as one of the strongest members among their siblings, surpassed only by Adam, yet Victor's maids overwhelmed him before he could properly react.[1]
After being released, Connor revealed that his behavior had served a larger purpose. He wanted to evaluate the individuals Victor had sent to guard Leona and determine how seriously Victor regarded her safety. From the maids' strength, behavior and familiarity with one another, Connor concluded that they were not disposable subordinates and were likely personally connected to Victor. He consequently judged that opposing Victor unnecessarily would be disastrous, while maintaining a favorable relationship with him could considerably improve the Clan's future.[1]
His conversation with Daphne and Iona also revealed the truth behind the Clan's succession dispute. Connor had no particular desire to replace Maya as leader. He had deliberately become Bellatrix's rival because he considered genuine competition necessary for her development. In his view, merely handing Bellatrix leadership without forcing her to prove herself would risk leaving Clan Lykos under an inadequate ruler. Connor intended to continue the contest until he understood both Bellatrix's capabilities and Leona's intentions regarding the succession.[1]
Connor stated that even if he did not become Clan leader, he would retain enough influence to act if the eventual ruler became incompetent. His loyalty was directed primarily toward the survival and prosperity of Clan Lykos rather than toward possessing the title himself.[1]
Later, Connor encountered his biological father, one of Maya's husbands, who had been shaken after witnessing Maya interacting with Victor. From the man's description, Connor rapidly understood the real danger: Victor possessed the qualities of a True Alpha powerful enough to provoke Maya's instincts to a degree none of her previous partners had achieved.[1]
Connor recognized that such a development could completely alter Clan Lykos' internal structure. Rather than simply oppose it, he decided to observe Victor, contact Maya when she returned and prepare for whatever political consequences followed.[1]
During Victor's later involvement with the Clan, Victor personally evaluated Maya's descendants. Despite considering most of them merely competent by his increasingly extreme standards, Connor was one of the few who genuinely drew his attention. Victor characterized him as sharp-minded but lazy, placing Connor alongside Bellatrix and the young [[Liliana Elizabeth Lykos]] as the Lykos descendants he considered especially noteworthy.[2]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
By the later Imperial period, Connor's former succession campaign had not resulted in him becoming permanent head of the [[Lykos Clan]]. [[Edward Jonathan Elizabeth Lykos]] had assumed the position of Clan leader, with Bellatrix closely associated with the new leadership structure.[4]
Connor nevertheless remained a living member of Clan Lykos and part of the extended family that continued operating within the Werewolf civilization under [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]], who had replaced the previous Werewolf monarchy as the current Werewolf Progenitor.

### Skills & Abilities
- Alpha Werewolf Physiology: Connor is an Alpha Werewolf born into one of the oldest and strongest Werewolf families of Samar. His physiology provides him with physical capabilities vastly exceeding ordinary [[Werewolves]], including enhanced strength, speed, durability, senses, regenerative ability, longevity and the ability to assume a Werewolf form.[3][1]
- Superhuman Strength: Connor is considered one of the strongest of Maya's numerous children. During the succession period, Bellatrix regarded him as one of the strongest siblings, with [[Adam William Lykos]] being the primary family member explicitly placed above him.[1]
- Superhuman Durability: Connor endured a direct strike from [[Bruna Elderblood]] powerful enough to immediately weaken his legs without being launched away or rendered unconscious. He remained conscious throughout the subsequent restraint placed upon him by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s maids.[1]
- Enhanced Instincts: As an experienced Alpha Werewolf, Connor possesses highly developed predatory instincts. He quickly recognized when further resistance against Victor's maids would only place himself and his wives in greater danger and immediately adjusted his behavior.[1]
- Alpha Dominance: Connor possesses sufficient strength and presence to establish himself as the dominant Alpha within his household. His relationship with Daphne and Iona developed despite the highly competitive nature inherited by women of Maya's lineage because Connor was powerful enough to occupy the Alpha position himself.[1]
- Leadership: Connor was formally considered a viable candidate to lead one of Samar's oldest and most politically important Werewolf Clans. Although he did not personally want the position, neither Bellatrix nor the rest of the Clan could dismiss his candidacy as meaningless.[3][1]
- Strategic Intelligence: Connor deliberately provoked a confrontation with Leona's entourage in order to evaluate their power, relationship with Victor and likely response to threats. From a brief encounter he correctly inferred that Victor had sent elite individuals with close personal connections to protect Leona and adjusted his political calculations accordingly.[1]
- Political Acumen: Connor understands that leadership is maintained through competence rather than title alone. He deliberately acted as Bellatrix's rival to force her development while planning to support her if she proved worthy and remove her if she later threatened the Clan through incompetence.[1]
- Observation and Deduction: Connor rapidly connected his father's description of Victor and Maya to the instinctive behavior of female Lykos Werewolves, correctly understanding that Victor's status as an overwhelmingly superior Alpha could fundamentally change Maya and the Clan's existing relationships.[1]
- Deception and Acting: Connor can intentionally manipulate how others perceive his motivations. Much of his apparent desire to seize leadership from Bellatrix was maintained as part of a long-term strategy to pressure her into becoming a stronger successor rather than because Connor genuinely wanted to rule.[1]
- Strong [[Will Solace|Will]]: Connor is capable of suppressing pride and instinct when continued resistance would be strategically pointless. Even after being humiliated by Victor's maids, he focused upon the information gained from the encounter instead of immediately seeking revenge.[1]

### Relationships

#### Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood
Maya is Connor's mother and the former Matriarch of Clan Lykos. Connor inherited much of her strong-willed and predatory disposition, although his approach to Clan politics is considerably more calculated. He considers Maya's decisions important enough that he intended to wait for her position before making any final choice concerning Leona, Bellatrix and the future of the Clan.[1]

#### Bellatrix Lykos
Bellatrix is Connor's sister and former rival in the succession struggle. Connor deliberately presented himself as a serious challenger for leadership because he believed Bella needed an opponent capable of forcing her to grow. Despite their rivalry, his objective was not to destroy her ambitions; he wanted her to become competent enough that he would never need to replace her.[1]

#### Daphne Lykos
Daphne is both Connor's sister and one of his wives. She accompanied him during his confrontation with Leona's entourage and understood the real purpose behind much of his behavior. Although she frequently complained about Connor's overly complicated schemes, she continued supporting his succession strategy.[3][1]

#### Iona Lykos
Iona is Connor's niece and second known wife. Like Daphne, she participated in Connor's political maneuvering within the Clan and openly questioned why he insisted on using elaborate schemes instead of simply supporting Bellatrix directly. Despite her complaints, she understood Connor's obsession with ensuring the [[Lykos Clan]] had competent leadership.[1]

#### Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos
Connor initially treated Leona as an unknown variable in the Clan succession because her possession of the Elizabeth mutation gave her an exceptional claim to the Clan's leadership. Rather than immediately treating her as an enemy, he wanted to learn whether Leona actually intended to participate in Lykos politics before deciding his own position.[3][1]

### Trivia
- Connor made his first direct appearance at the end of Chapter 773 and received most of his characterization in Chapter 774.[3][1]
- He was one of the principal candidates for leadership of Clan Lykos during Leona's return to Samar, but he privately admitted that he considered actually ruling the Clan too troublesome.[1]
- His rivalry with Bellatrix was deliberately maintained to force her to improve rather than because Connor genuinely desired the Clan leadership.[1]
- Connor stated that if Bellatrix eventually became an incompetent ruler, he would use his influence to remove her from power.[1]
- He is one of Maya's strongest children and was described as being below Adam among the siblings compared during that period.[1]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later identified Connor as sharp-minded but lazy and one of the very few members of Maya's enormous family who immediately interested him.[2]
- Connor's known wives are [[Daphne Lykos]], his sister, and [[Iona Lykos]], his niece. The [[Lykos Clan]]'s internal relationships reflect its unusual ancient Werewolf family structure.[3][1]
- Connor was able to establish himself as the Alpha in his relationships with Daphne and Iona despite both women possessing the strong predatory tendencies inherited through Maya's lineage.[1]
- Although Connor initially behaved aggressively toward the Vampires protecting Leona, the confrontation was partly an intelligence-gathering maneuver intended to determine the strength and importance of Victor's people.[1]
