### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 846
Rao Lykos is a Werewolf of the [[Lykos Clan]], one of the numerous children of former Matriarch [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]], the husband of his sister [[Ariana Lykos]], and the father of [[Liliana Elizabeth Lykos]]. He made his first confirmed appearance when [[Victor Elderblood]] inspected Maya's descendants and discovered the dormant Elizabeth Trait within Rao and Ariana's four-year-old daughter.[1]
Rao has received little individual focus within the story. Maya considers both him and Ariana comparatively ordinary among her enormous number of children, describing them as neither particularly talented nor useless. Their daughter, however, possesses considerably greater potential due to naturally inheriting the rare Elizabeth mutation.[1]

### Physical Appearance
Rao is described as a man with long white hair and a serious expression. He possesses the characteristic white hair frequently seen among members of Maya's branch of the Lykos family.[1]
No detailed description of Rao's height, eye color, complexion, clothing or transformed Werewolf appearance has currently been provided.

### Personality
Very little has been directly established about Rao's personality. His clearest characterization comes from his behavior toward his daughter, [[Liliana Elizabeth Lykos]].
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] approached the frightened four-year-old Liliana, Rao and Ariana immediately prepared to intervene. Both stopped only after Maya gave them a warning look, demonstrating that their parental concern was strong enough to make them consider stepping forward despite Victor's overwhelming presence, while their respect for Maya's authority was sufficient for them to trust her judgment and remain where they were.[1]
Rao was visibly concerned while Victor questioned and touched Liliana, but neither he nor Ariana openly challenged him. Maya understood that Victor would not harm a child who had done nothing to threaten him and prevented the parents from interfering unnecessarily.[1]
According to Maya, Rao is not one of the outstanding talents among her children. However, she also explicitly rejected the idea that he was useless, placing him somewhere within the ordinary range of competence expected from the enormous Lykos family.[1]

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Rao was introduced during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later involvement with the [[Lykos Clan]]. After observing Maya's enormous family, Victor noticed that only a small number of her descendants immediately attracted his attention. Among those individuals was a young girl with short snow-white hair who strongly resembled a younger [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]].[1]
Victor approached the girl while the surrounding Clan members instinctively moved aside. When Liliana became frightened, her parents prepared to intervene, but Maya stopped them with a single look. Both Rao and Ariana reluctantly remained where they were while Victor questioned their daughter.[1]
Liliana eventually introduced herself as:
"Liliana Lykos, daughter of Rao Lykos and [[Ariana Lykos]]."[1]
Victor looked toward Rao and Ariana, prompting him to ask Maya about them without needing to state the entire question. Maya explained that both were her children and described them as neither especially talented nor particularly useless.[1]
Rao is specifically described during the scene as a man with long white hair and a serious expression, standing beside Ariana while Victor examined their daughter.[1]
Victor found the contrast between the parents and child interesting. Despite describing Rao and Ariana as relatively ordinary members of Maya's family, Liliana possessed a remarkably promising future. Maya had already been monitoring the girl because her instincts suggested Liliana carried dormant traces of the rare Elizabeth mutation.[1]
Victor confirmed Maya's suspicions. Liliana naturally possessed the potential for the Elizabeth Trait, although its manifestation was considerably less obvious than Leona's had been at a similar stage of development. Victor used his ability to perceive and manipulate the Soul to awaken that dormant characteristic much earlier than it would normally have appeared.[1][2]
After awakening the mutation, Victor renamed the girl [[Liliana Elizabeth Lykos]] and bestowed blessings associated with his Blood and Dragon nature upon her.[1] Maya was then instructed to personally oversee Liliana's development, turning Rao and Ariana's daughter into one of the most promising young members of the Lykos Clan.
Rao does not receive a significant individual role after this scene in the currently available material.

### Skills & Abilities
Rao has not participated in a dedicated battle or demonstrated any unique supernatural technique. His known capabilities primarily derive from his Werewolf heritage.

#### Werewolf Physiology
- Werewolf Physiology: Rao is a natural Werewolf born into the ancient [[Lykos Clan]] and possesses the physical and supernatural characteristics associated with his Race.[1]
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: [[Werewolves]] naturally possess strength, speed, durability, stamina and reflexes beyond ordinary Humans.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Werewolf physiology provides accelerated recovery from physical injury.
- Enhanced Senses: Rao possesses the heightened smell, hearing and general sensory capabilities natural to his Race.
- Longevity: Werewolves possess substantially longer lifespans than ordinary Humans.
- Werewolf Transformation: Rao possesses the racial ability to manifest Werewolf characteristics, although his individual transformed state has never been shown.
- Natural Weapons: As a Werewolf, he possesses access to enhanced claws and fangs while transformed.

#### Lykos Bloodline
- Lykos Heritage: Rao is one of Maya's biological children and therefore belongs directly to the ancient lineage descending from [[Elizabeth Lykos]] and the first Werewolf Progenitor Licael.
- Enhanced Ancestral Potential: Children descended from Maya's Elizabeth-enhanced lineage can possess greater natural potential than ordinary [[Werewolves]], even when they do not personally manifest the complete Elizabeth mutation.[3][4]
- No Confirmed Elizabeth Trait: Rao himself has not been identified as a carrier of the Elizabeth Trait. The mutation instead manifested naturally within his daughter Liliana.[1]

#### Alpha Werewolf
Rao is associated with the Alpha branch of the Lykos family, although the story has not provided enough individual combat material to determine his standing among the Clan's stronger Alphas.
- Alpha-Level Physicality: Rao possesses the enhanced physical foundation expected from an Alpha Werewolf.
- Predatory Instincts: Like other [[Werewolves]], he possesses instincts sensitive to supernatural hierarchy and powerful presences.
- Limitations: No individual Element, specialized Nature Energy technique, complete transformation or higher Werewolf evolution has been demonstrated.
Rao has not been confirmed to possess:
- The Elizabeth Trait.
- True Werewolf physiology.
- Ancient Werewolf physiology.
- True Alpha status.
- Complete Werewolf Transformation.
- Godhood or Divinity.
- An individualized Element.
- Blood Dragon physiology.

### Relationships

#### Ariana Lykos
[[Ariana Lykos]] is Rao's wife and one of his many siblings through [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]]. Together they are the biological parents of [[Liliana Elizabeth Lykos]].[1]
Both are described by Maya as relatively ordinary among her children, neither belonging to the most talented members of her family nor being considered useless.[1]
Their strongest shared characterization is their concern for Liliana. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] approached their daughter, both instinctively prepared to intervene despite the enormous danger represented by him, stopping only because Maya directly ordered them through her gaze to remain where they were.[1]

#### Liliana Elizabeth Lykos
[[Liliana Elizabeth Lykos]] is Rao and Ariana's daughter and the most important known member of their immediate family branch.
Although neither parent is presented as one of Maya's exceptional children, Liliana naturally inherited the rare Elizabeth mutation. Maya had already begun observing the girl because of an instinctive suspicion that the trait existed within her, while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later confirmed and forcibly awakened the dormant potential.[1]
Victor considered Liliana promising enough to personally grant her the name Elizabeth, bless her and entrust her future training to Maya.[1][2]

#### Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood
[[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] is Rao's mother and former Matriarch of the [[Lykos Clan]]. Rao is one of more than fifty children produced throughout Maya's exceptionally long life.
Maya does not regard Rao as one of her greatest talents, but she explicitly describes both Rao and Ariana as competent enough not to be considered useless.[1]
She also exercises overwhelming authority over the couple. A single look from Maya was sufficient to prevent them from interfering when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] approached Liliana, despite their obvious parental concern.[1]
