Rodrigo Lykos is an Alpha Werewolf of the [[Lykos Clan]], a grandson of former Matriarch [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]], and one of the husbands of his aunt [[Bellatrix Lykos]]. He was introduced during [[Victor Elderblood]]'s visit to Samar, when Bellatrix presented the members of her household to [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]].[1]
Rodrigo has received very little individual focus in the story. His known appearance primarily establishes his relationship to Bellatrix and the wider Lykos family, as well as his unusually short stature compared with the generally very tall members of his Clan.[1]

### Physical Appearance
Rodrigo is a male Werewolf standing 165 cm tall.[1] This makes him notably short compared with the other members of Bellatrix's household and the generally tall members of the Lykos family.
Bellatrix specifically introduces Rodrigo by calling him the smallest of the three men accompanying her. Rodrigo immediately objects to being considered short, arguing that the rest of his family are simply unusually tall and that 165 cm would be a normal height in parts of Asia.[1]
No detailed canonical description of Rodrigo's hair color, eye color, skin tone, clothing or transformed Werewolf appearance has currently been given.

### Personality
Only a small amount of Rodrigo's personality has been shown. His introduction portrays him as outspoken and somewhat sensitive regarding his height.
When Bellatrix introduces him by immediately emphasizing that he is only 165 cm tall, Rodrigo protests that he is not actually small and instead accuses the rest of the family of being "tall freaks". He further argues that his height would be considered normal in Asia, suggesting that this is a recurring argument within the family rather than the first time he has been teased about it.[1]
His reaction also demonstrates that Rodrigo is comfortable arguing casually with his relatives. When [[Marcelo Lykos]] sarcastically dismisses his explanation and calls him a dwarf, Rodrigo does not display any serious hostility, making the exchange appear to be ordinary familial teasing rather than an actual conflict.[1]
Beyond this scene, the novel has not provided enough material to establish Rodrigo's broader temperament, ambitions or views.

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Rodrigo first appeared in Chapter 762 while [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]] and [[Natalia Elderblood née Alioth]] were interacting with members of Leona's enormous maternal family on Samar. They encountered [[Bellatrix Lykos]], one of [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]]'s many daughters, accompanied by three men belonging to the Lykos family.[1]
Bellatrix introduced the oldest-looking man as [[Ivan Lykos]], describing him as both her brother and husband. She then introduced the younger-looking [[Marcelo Lykos]] in the same manner, identifying him as another brother and husband.[1]
Finally, Bellatrix introduced Rodrigo:
"The smallest one is Rodrigo, who is only 165 cm tall; he's my nephew, my husband, and your cousin."[1]
The introduction establishes Rodrigo as Bellatrix's biological nephew and Leona's cousin, placing him within the generation below Maya's direct children.
Rodrigo immediately objected to Bellatrix describing him as small. He argued that his relatives were abnormally tall and insisted that his own height would be completely normal in Asia. Marcelo sarcastically replied that they had heard the same argument many times before and called Rodrigo a dwarf.[1]
The increasingly complicated introductions left Leona experiencing considerable culture shock. Bellatrix was simultaneously sister and wife to Ivan and Marcelo, while Rodrigo was both her nephew and husband. None of the Samar-born members of the group appeared to consider these relationships unusual, further demonstrating the differences between the old Lykos family structure and the more conventional Human family environment in which Leona had originally been raised.[1]
Rodrigo has not received another substantial individual storyline in the currently documented material.

### Relationships

#### Bellatrix Lykos
[[Bellatrix Lykos]] is simultaneously Rodrigo's aunt and wife.[1] She openly introduces him in both roles when meeting Leona and treats the arrangement as completely ordinary.
Rodrigo appears as part of Bellatrix's immediate household alongside Ivan and Marcelo. Unlike Ivan and Marcelo, who are Bellatrix's brothers, Rodrigo belongs to the following generation of the family as her nephew.

#### Ivan Lykos
[[Ivan Lykos]] is one of Rodrigo's uncles and a fellow husband of Bellatrix. The two appear together during Chapter 762 but have no substantial direct interaction.[1]

#### Marcelo Lykos
[[Marcelo Lykos]] is one of Rodrigo's uncles and another husband of Bellatrix.
Marcelo openly teases Rodrigo about his height. When Rodrigo insists that 165 cm is normal and that the rest of the family are simply too tall, Marcelo sarcastically responds that they have heard the argument many times and calls him a dwarf.[1]
The casual nature of the exchange suggests that teasing Rodrigo about his height is a recurring joke within their household.

#### Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos
[[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]] is Rodrigo's cousin through the wider Lykos family. Bellatrix explicitly identifies Rodrigo as Leona's cousin when introducing him.[1]
Their first meeting primarily contributes to Leona's realization of how enormous and unconventional her maternal family actually is.

#### Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood
[[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] is Rodrigo's grandmother. Because Rodrigo is Bellatrix's biological nephew and Bellatrix is one of Maya's daughters, Rodrigo descends from one of Maya's other children.
The specific identity of Rodrigo's biological parent has not been revealed.
