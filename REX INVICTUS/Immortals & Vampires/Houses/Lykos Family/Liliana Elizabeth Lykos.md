### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 846
Liliana Elizabeth Lykos, born Liliana Lykos, is a young Werewolf of the [[Lykos Clan]] and the daughter of [[Rao Lykos]] and [[Ariana Lykos]], both children of former Clan Matriarch [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]]. She is one of the extremely rare natural inheritors of the Elizabeth Trait, the hereditary mutation descending from [[Elizabeth Lykos]].[2][3]
Liliana's latent Elizabeth potential was discovered by [[Victor Elderblood]] during his involvement with the Lykos Clan. Rather than artificially giving her a foreign Bloodline, Victor used his ability to manipulate the Soul to bring an existing characteristic already present within Liliana to the surface. After awakening the dormant Elizabeth Trait, he personally gave her the name Liliana Elizabeth Lykos and bestowed two supernatural blessings associated with his Blood and Dragon aspects.[2][1]
Victor subsequently entrusted Liliana's development to Maya, describing the child as a "gem" whose growth could also teach Maya something about herself as a mentor. Liliana consequently became one of the first known Lykos descendants whose dormant Elizabeth potential was artificially awakened through direct Soul manipulation rather than waiting for the mutation to manifest through its ordinary developmental process.[2][1]

### Physical Appearance
Liliana is a small girl with short snow-white hair and light-brown skin. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] immediately noted that she strongly resembled a younger [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]], with the primary visual difference being her complexion. Whereas Leona possesses exceptionally pale skin, Liliana's coloration is closer to the darker complexion characteristic of [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]], although significantly lighter than Maya's own chocolate-brown skin.[2]
Her appearance strongly reflects the dominant physical traits of Clan Lykos, particularly its characteristic white hair. Her current profile identifies her eyes as blue, another common feature among prominent members of the Lykos bloodline.

### Personality
Despite being only four years old at her debut, Liliana is unusually perceptive and strong-willed for her age. When [[Victor Elderblood]] approached her after effortlessly imposing his presence upon the surrounding Lykos family, Liliana was visibly terrified. Her heart raced, tears gathered in her eyes and she even stumbled over her own name while trying to answer him, yet she refused to run away or openly cry because she took pride in being Maya's granddaughter.[2]
Her bravery should not be mistaken for emotional maturity equal to an adult. Chapter 846 explicitly emphasizes that Liliana remains a four-year-old child who is frightened by Victor's overwhelming presence despite attempting to stand her ground. The previously listed "mental age of 12" is not supported by the chapter material and should not be treated as a canonical measurement.
Liliana is nevertheless described as exceptionally intelligent for a child of her age. She quickly understands the seriousness of Victor's attention and responds directly when questioned about herself and her parents. Her fear also disappears rapidly after Victor gently touches her head. Instead of sensing hostility, Liliana experiences an unusual feeling of warmth, comfort and familiarity around him, instinctively perceiving him as someone connected to her family. She subsequently relaxes enough to smile and laugh while Victor continues patting her head.[2]
Her future personality after the full development of the Elizabeth Trait remains largely unknown. The mutation is known to intensify Werewolf instincts and emotional responses, meaning proper guidance from Maya is particularly important during Liliana's development.[3]

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Liliana first appeared when [[Victor Elderblood]] returned his attention to the members of [[Lykos Clan]] while dealing with Samar's changing political structure. Victor examined Maya's enormous family and found that, although many of her descendants were reasonably competent, only a few immediately caught his attention. Among them were [[Connor Lykos]], [[Bellatrix Lykos]] and a small girl with short snow-white hair who looked remarkably similar to a younger Leona.[2]
Victor approached the girl while the surrounding [[Werewolves]] instinctively moved out of his way. Several relatives wanted to remain close enough to protect her, but Maya prevented them from interfering because she understood that Victor had no intention of harming a child who posed no threat to him. Victor asked Liliana whether she was afraid. She attempted to deny it, but her trembling body and gathering tears made the truth obvious. Even so, she refused to break down because she considered herself the granddaughter of Maya and wanted to maintain her pride in front of the Clan.[2]
When Victor asked her name, Liliana initially became so nervous that she bit her tongue while trying to speak. After recovering, she formally introduced herself as Liliana Lykos, daughter of [[Rao Lykos]] and [[Ariana Lykos]]. Both parents were present nearby. Maya confirmed that Rao and Ariana were two of her own children and described them as neither exceptionally gifted nor particularly useless by the standards of her enormous family.[2]
Victor nevertheless saw something unusual in their daughter. He described Liliana as a child born from comparatively ordinary parents but possessing an exceptionally promising future. The observation immediately caught Maya and [[Adam William Lykos]]' attention. Maya had already been watching Liliana because her instincts suggested that the girl carried the potential for the Elizabeth Trait. Unlike Leona, whose mutation had been unusually obvious from childhood, Liliana's dormant characteristic was weaker and had not yet naturally developed enough to fully manifest.[2]
Victor placed his hand on Liliana's head. The fear she had felt around him disappeared almost immediately and was replaced by warmth and an instinctive sense that Victor was somehow part of her family. Liliana relaxed, smiled and laughed as Victor gently stroked her hair.[2]
Victor then publicly renamed her:
Liliana Elizabeth Lykos.
The declaration shocked the assembled Clan. The name "Elizabeth" carried enormous meaning because it marked a connection to one of the most exceptional hereditary characteristics in Lykos history. Some Clan members internally questioned Victor's authority to grant such a name, but Maya's own reaction immediately demonstrated that the situation was more than symbolic: she could physically sense the dormant Elizabeth Blood beginning to grow within Liliana.[2]
Victor had not created the mutation from nothing. Liliana already possessed the latent Elizabeth characteristic naturally. His intervention simply forced the dormant potential to manifest much earlier than it otherwise would have.[2][1]
After awakening the trait, Victor gave Liliana two blessings. The first was associated with his Blood, with Victor wishing that the Blessing of Blood would allow her enemies to be swallowed within a "Sea of Blood." The second was associated with his Dragon nature and was intended to protect her against natural lifeforms.[2]
[[Metis]] was particularly shocked because she sensed a small amount of Divine Power leave Victor and enter Liliana even though Victor had not yet undergone an ordinary Divine Ascension. Victor himself did not completely understand why his evolved existence could already perform such an act, further demonstrating how abnormal his state had become by this period.[2]
Victor then privately spoke with Maya. He described Liliana as a gem that Maya should personally shape and instructed her to become the child's teacher. His reasoning was not limited to improving Liliana: Victor reminded Maya that a master could learn through teaching a disciple just as a disciple learned from a master. Liliana's development was therefore intended to become part of Maya's own continued growth as well.[2]
In Chapter 847, Victor further explained the mechanism behind what he had done. As a Progenitor with extremely advanced control over Souls, he could perceive the information contained within a person's Soul and bring an existing characteristic to the surface. He could not simply create an arbitrary hereditary trait that did not belong to the target's Essence, but if the potential already existed—as it did within Liliana—he could awaken and strengthen it directly.[1]
Victor used Liliana as proof when offering Maya a larger possibility for Clan Lykos. If Maya properly reorganized the Clan and personally selected suitable descendants who already carried dormant Elizabeth potential, Victor was capable of awakening that potential in them as well.[1]
Liliana therefore became the first directly shown example of Victor artificially accelerating the awakening of the Elizabeth Trait through Soul manipulation.

### Skills & Abilities
Liliana possesses very little demonstrated combat experience because she was only four years old when introduced. Her known abilities should therefore be separated between characteristics directly shown in Chapter 846 and the general properties of the Elizabeth mutation she carries.

#### Werewolf Physiology
- Werewolf Physiology: Liliana was naturally born as a member of Clan Lykos and possesses the supernatural racial foundation of a Werewolf.[2]
- Superhuman Potential: [[Werewolves]] possess physical potential greatly exceeding ordinary Humans, including enhanced strength, speed, durability, senses and healing as they develop.
- Werewolf Transformation: As a Werewolf, Liliana possesses the racial capacity to develop Werewolf transformations. No completed adult transformation has yet been individually demonstrated by her.
- Enhanced Senses: Her Race naturally possesses heightened predatory senses, although no exceptional Liliana-specific sensory feat has yet been shown.
- Regenerative Potential: Werewolf physiology possesses supernatural healing capabilities; Liliana's individual upper limit remains unknown.
- Longevity: Werewolves possess substantially greater lifespans than ordinary Humans.
The older version of this article classified Liliana as an Alpha Werewolf. The reviewed Chapter 846–847 material confirms that she is a Werewolf with exceptional potential but does not explicitly identify the four-year-old Liliana as an established Alpha. Until a chapter directly confirms that classification, her demonstrated status should remain simply Werewolf / Elizabeth Trait carrier.

#### Elizabeth Trait
Liliana is a natural carrier of the Elizabeth Trait, an extremely rare hereditary mutation descending from [[Elizabeth Lykos]], the legendary wife of the first Werewolf Progenitor Licael.[3][4]
- Natural Elizabeth Carrier: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] did not artificially implant the Elizabeth Trait into Liliana. Maya had already suspected that her granddaughter naturally carried it before Victor intervened.[2]
- Accelerated [[Awakening]]: Victor used Soul manipulation to bring Liliana's dormant Elizabeth potential to the surface years earlier than it might have naturally emerged.[2][1]
- Accelerated Instinctive Development: The Elizabeth mutation accelerates development of the instinctive Werewolf side of its carrier, allowing supernatural growth that would ordinarily require considerably more time.[3]
- Enhanced Physical Potential: The mutation increases the long-term physical and supernatural potential of its bearer beyond that of ordinary Lykos descendants.[3][4]
- Enhanced Predatory Instincts: Carriers possess significantly stronger Werewolf instincts, affecting their reactions to threats, hierarchy, dominance and combat.[3]
- Enhanced Growth Potential: Maya expected Liliana's mutation to be weaker than Leona's unusually strong manifestation but still considerably more significant than that of ordinary descendants.[2]
- Hereditary Potential: The Elizabeth Trait can reappear after several generations rather than following a simple parent-to-child inheritance pattern.[3][4]
- Bloodline Dominance: The Elizabeth mutation possesses an unusually predatory hereditary nature and is difficult for ordinary external Bloodline influences to overwrite.[5]

##### Side Effects
The Elizabeth Trait is not purely beneficial. Its accelerated instinctive development can create serious psychological problems if its bearer lacks sufficient guidance.
- Instinctive Instability: The stronger Werewolf instincts produced by the mutation can become difficult to control.[3]
- Emotional Instability: Carriers may experience intensified emotional responses as instinctive development accelerates.[3]
- Predatory Mentality: Without sufficient discipline, the instinctive Wolf side can begin overpowering rational judgment.[3]
- Early [[Awakening]] Risk: Natural Elizabeth carriers generally begin displaying the mutation while still very young, making proper guidance especially important because the individual may possess powerful instincts before developing the maturity necessary to control them.[3]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] specifically placed Liliana under Maya's instruction after awakening her Trait, making one of the most experienced Elizabeth carriers in existence responsible for teaching the child how to control what had been brought to the surface.[2]

#### Blessings of Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] personally bestowed two supernatural blessings upon Liliana immediately after awakening her Elizabeth Trait.[2]
- Blood Blessing: Victor invoked his authority over Blood and wished that the Blessing of Blood would allow Liliana's enemies to be consumed within a Sea of Blood.[2]
- The precise mechanics and limits of this blessing have not yet been demonstrated in combat.
- It should therefore not be treated as proof that Liliana independently possesses all of Victor's Blood abilities.
- Dragon Blessing: Victor invoked his Draconic nature to place a second blessing upon Liliana, wishing that the Blessing of the Dragon would protect her from all forms of natural life.[2]
- The story has not yet demonstrated the exact defensive mechanism, activation conditions or maximum limit of this protection.
- The wording should not be interpreted as absolute invulnerability to every living Being without a direct feat establishing such a scope.
- Divine Blessing Recipient: [[Metis]] directly sensed Divine Power leaving Victor and entering Liliana when the blessings were given.[2] This was particularly abnormal because Victor had not yet completed a conventional Divine Ascension.

#### Awakened Bloodline Potential
- Soul-Activated Potential: Liliana's dormant Elizabeth characteristic was forcibly brought to the surface through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s manipulation of her Soul.[1]
- Natural Essence Preservation: Victor explicitly could not simply add arbitrary characteristics that did not belong to Liliana. His intervention amplified what already existed inside her Essence.[1]
- Enhanced Future Growth: Victor considered Liliana sufficiently promising to personally identify her among Maya's enormous number of descendants and later called her a "gem" for Maya to shape.[2]

### Skills
- Exceptional Courage for Her Age: Liliana remained standing in front of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] despite being visibly terrified by his presence and wanting to cry.[2]
- Above-Average Intelligence: Chapter 846 explicitly presents Liliana as unusually intelligent for a four-year-old child, although she remains emotionally and developmentally a child.[2]
- Strong Pride: Liliana's refusal to break down was partly motivated by her identity as Maya's granddaughter and her desire to live up to the reputation of the Lykos family.[2]
- Developing Instincts: Following Victor's awakening of the Elizabeth Trait, Liliana possesses the foundation for significantly accelerated Werewolf instinctive development.[2][1]

### Relationships

#### Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood
[[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] is Liliana's grandmother through both of her parents, since [[Rao Lykos]] and [[Ariana Lykos]] are both Maya's children.[2] Maya had already been observing Liliana before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s intervention because her instincts suggested that the child carried dormant Elizabeth potential.
After Victor awakened the Trait, he explicitly entrusted Liliana to Maya and instructed the ancient Matriarch to become her teacher. Victor viewed the relationship as beneficial to both of them: Maya would shape a promising successor while also learning through the process of teaching her.[2]

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is the individual responsible for Liliana's transformation from an ordinary young Lykos descendant with dormant potential into an awakened Elizabeth carrier. Victor noticed her among Maya's many descendants, recognized the future hidden within her Soul, awakened the latent mutation and personally bestowed the name Elizabeth upon her.[2]
Liliana was initially terrified of Victor but rapidly became comfortable when he touched her head. She experienced him as strangely familiar and instinctively associated his presence with family rather than danger.[2]
Victor additionally granted her Blood and Dragon blessings and expressed unusually high expectations for her future.[2]

#### Rao Lykos and Ariana Lykos
[[Rao Lykos]] and [[Ariana Lykos]] are Liliana's biological parents. Both are children of Maya and were described by Maya as comparatively ordinary among her descendants—neither exceptionally talented nor especially weak.[2]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] found the contrast between the parents and daughter particularly interesting because Liliana possessed far greater latent potential than either parent's reputation initially suggested.

#### Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos
[[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]] is Liliana's cousin, not her older sister. Leona is the daughter of [[Adam William Lykos]], while Liliana is the daughter of Adam's siblings Rao and Ariana.[2]
The two girls nevertheless possess an important hereditary connection because both are natural carriers of the Elizabeth Trait. Maya considered Leona's manifestation particularly powerful and obvious from childhood, whereas Liliana's was weaker and more dormant until [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] awakened it.[2]

- Victor did not create the Elizabeth Trait from nothing. Chapter 847 explains that he could only bring forward a characteristic already contained within Liliana's Essence.[1]
- Liliana is the first directly shown Lykos descendant whose dormant Elizabeth Trait was deliberately awakened through Victor's Soul manipulation.[2][1]
- Victor personally gave her the name Liliana Elizabeth Lykos after awakening the mutation.[2]
