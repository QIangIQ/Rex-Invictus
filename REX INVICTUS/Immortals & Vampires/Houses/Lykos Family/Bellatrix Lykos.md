### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 762
Bellatrix Lykos, usually called Bella, is an Alpha Werewolf, one of the numerous children of [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]], and a senior member of the ancient [[Lykos Clan]]. She is the sister of [[Adam William Lykos]], [[Connor Lykos]], [[Daphne Lykos]], [[Ivan Lykos]], [[Marcelo Lykos]], and many other members of Maya's enormous family, making her the aunt of [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]], [[Edward Jonathan Elizabeth Lykos]], and [[Johnny Lykos]].[1][3]
Bellatrix belongs to the direct lineage descending from [[Elizabeth Lykos]], the strongest wife of the original Werewolf Progenitor Licael and the ancestor from whom Clan Lykos derives much of its exceptional hereditary potential. Although Bellatrix herself is not an Elizabeth Gene bearer, she belongs to the generation born directly from Maya, whose mutation significantly increased the potential and talent inherited by her children.[4][3]
During the succession dispute within Clan Lykos, Bellatrix openly pursued the position of Clan Leader and attempted to gain Leona's political support. She regarded herself as a serious candidate for the role and competed indirectly against her brother Connor Lykos, another leading candidate.[3][5] Bellatrix did not, however, remain the eventual current leader of the Clan. By the Imperial Era, Edward Jonathan Elizabeth Lykos had assumed leadership, and much later he was explicitly recognized as the Clan's new Alpha following Adam's retirement.[6][7]
Bellatrix is also notable for the complex marital traditions practiced within her family. At her debut, she introduced Ivan Lykos and Marcelo Lykos as both her brothers and husbands and [[Rodrigo Lykos]] as both her nephew and husband.[1] She later married Edward, another of her nephews. Edward explained that although his position, his father, and Maya influenced the circumstances surrounding the relationship, he ultimately moved toward Bellatrix of his own free will after discovering that the two were highly compatible and shared similar tastes.[2]

### Physical Appearance
Bellatrix is a tall and exceptionally attractive woman with short snow-white hair, emerald-green eyes, lightly bronzed skin, and a powerfully athletic physique.[1]
At her introduction, she wore a short black jacket that barely covered her breasts and exposed considerable cleavage, leaving her arms uncovered. She paired the jacket with tight black pants and high heels. Her fashion immediately reminded Leona of Maya, making the influence of her mother's style obvious.[1]
One of Bellatrix's most prominent physical characteristics is her extremely defined abdomen. She possesses a visible six-pack with sharply delineated abdominal muscles, and the narration specifically emphasizes that her physique reflects considerable discipline and effort rather than merely the natural advantages of Werewolf biology. Her body combines pronounced musculature with an otherwise feminine figure, giving her the balance of strength and beauty frequently associated with the women of Clan Lykos.[1]

### Personality
Bellatrix is confident, outspoken, socially uninhibited, ambitious, and remarkably straightforward regarding what she wants. She does not attempt to disguise either her unusual family relationships or her political ambitions. When first meeting Leona, she casually introduced Ivan and Marcelo as both her brothers and husbands and Rodrigo as both her nephew and husband, treating the arrangement as entirely ordinary according to Lykos standards.[1]
She can also be playful and teasing. Bellatrix introduced herself to Leona with a seductive yet gentle smile, evaluated her niece's unusual sense of humor, and casually mocked Rodrigo for his comparatively short height. Her disposition is generally relaxed enough that even Leona's increasingly bizarre comments usually cause Bella to become confused or exasperated rather than openly hostile.[1][4]
Bellatrix is highly knowledgeable about Clan Lykos' history and bloodline. While guiding Leona and Natalia through the Clan grounds, she explained that Clan Lykos had existed since the earliest periods of Werewolf civilization on Samar, had been present when the Werewolf Progenitor arrived on the planet, and had representatives involved in essentially every major event in Samar's history. She was also knowledgeable enough to explain the origin, inheritance, benefits, dangers, and political significance of the Elizabeth Gene in considerable detail.[4][3]
Despite her attachment to Clan tradition, Bellatrix is surprisingly pragmatic regarding leadership. She explicitly rejects the idea that a person should be forced to become Clan Leader merely because they possess the greatest theoretical potential. In her view, forcing an unwilling person into such a critical position would damage the Clan; a competent individual who genuinely wants responsibility is preferable to a more talented person who has no desire to rule.[3]
This philosophy is tied to Bellatrix's broader view of the Clan. When Leona feared that her relatives intended to reduce her to a breeding tool because of her Elizabeth mutation, Bellatrix rejected the idea and explained that members of Clan Lykos were permitted to live as they wished provided they did not harm the Clan and fulfilled their obligations. To Bellatrix, the Clan was fundamentally a family rather than a prison.[3]
Bellatrix is nevertheless politically ambitious. Once Leona made it clear that she had no immediate interest in becoming Clan Leader, Bella immediately grasped both of her niece's hands and openly requested her support, declaring that Leona's backing could help her obtain leadership herself. Leona considered the request brazen but actually developed a favorable impression of Bella precisely because she was so direct about her intentions.[3]
She is also politically observant and opportunistic. When [[Connor Lykos]], her principal rival for Clan leadership, behaved aggressively toward Leona's Vampire companions, Bellatrix recognized that he had made a poor impression on someone whose support could heavily influence the succession dispute. Rather than overlooking the political consequence, Bella understood that Connor's mistake benefited her own position.[5]
Despite her ambition, Bellatrix is not blindly obsessed with strength at any cost. Far into the Imperial Era, when [[Johnny Lykos]] wanted his children subjected to [[Scathach]]'s infamous training, Maya enthusiastically approved. Bellatrix internally sided with Adam, Edward, and Leona in considering Maya almost as insane as Scathach for supporting such an extreme decision so casually.[7]

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Bellatrix first appeared during [[Victor Elderblood]]'s visit to Samar, when Leona was finally brought into direct contact with the enormous extended family descended from [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]]. Bella encountered Leona and Natalia within the Lykos territory and introduced herself as Bellatrix Lykos, allowing her niece to simply call her "Bella". Leona immediately made a joke connecting her name to the fictional Bellatrix Lestrange and the Cruciatus Curse, leaving Bella confused by her niece's unusual sense of humor.[1]
Bellatrix then introduced the three men accompanying her. [[Ivan Lykos]] was simultaneously her brother and husband, [[Marcelo Lykos]] was likewise her brother and husband, and [[Rodrigo Lykos]] was her nephew and husband. The completely casual manner in which Bella presented these relationships left Leona momentarily unable to process the family structure, while Bella and the others treated the arrangement as normal within Clan Lykos.[1]
Later, Bella personally guided Leona, Natalia, and [[Diablo|Big Guy]] through the enormous Clan headquarters. She explained that Clan Lykos was one of the oldest Werewolf Clans on Samar, with ancestors who had been present when the original Werewolf Progenitor arrived and when the current Werewolf monarchy was established. Its age had earned the Clan privileges unavailable even to ordinary Alpha Clans, including a headquarters and territory comparable in scale to the Royal Capital.[4]
The tour was interrupted when [[Fenrir]]'s roar caused the instincts of everyone nearby to react, followed by the even more oppressive presence generated by Victor. Bellatrix collapsed to the ground clutching herself, experiencing cold, fear, weakness, and an overwhelming sense of helplessness. Leona and Victor's attendants, by contrast, recognized the second presence immediately. Bella was stunned to learn that the terrifying pressure belonged to Leona's husband and that Victor was personally confronting Fenrir.[4]
The incident also allowed Bella to appreciate just how heavily protected Leona had become. Kaguya, Eve, Bruna, Roberta, Anna, and Maria emerged and immediately established protection around her. Several of them made Bella instinctively uncomfortable despite the fact that she herself was an Alpha Werewolf.[4]
Once the disturbance passed, Bellatrix resumed explaining the Clan's history. Inside a library restricted to high-ranking members, she showed Leona records concerning [[Elizabeth Lykos]], the strongest wife of the Werewolf Progenitor and ancestor of their Clan. Bella explained that Elizabeth possessed an abnormal Werewolf mutation that even the Progenitor himself had not possessed. The mutation, later called the Elizabeth Gene, accelerated the instinctive development of its bearer, greatly increased overall potential, and caused individuals to awaken their Werewolf side much younger than normal.[4]
Bella also explained the mutation's drawbacks. Greater instinctive development could produce emotional instability, difficulty maintaining rational control, and an increasingly wolf-like mentality. Because natural bearers could awaken between approximately three and six years old rather than the ordinary eighteen to twenty-one range, proper training from childhood was considered essential.[4]
When Leona doubted that she possessed such a mutation because she had remained sickly throughout childhood, Bella led her toward the truth by pointing out that only two contemporary Clan members carried the name "Elizabeth": Maya Elizabeth Lykos and Leona Elizabeth Lykos. Bellatrix's silence when Leona directly questioned whether she possessed the mutation effectively confirmed the answer.[4]
Bella continued the explanation by revealing that the mutation naturally appeared only once every three or four generations. Maya had been the previous bearer, and the next manifestation occurred with Leona. Maya herself had identified the mutation in her granddaughter and personally granted Leona the name "Elizabeth", making her a direct candidate to eventually succeed the Matriarch.[3]
According to the information Bellatrix possessed at the time, the mutation naturally manifested in women. More importantly, a female bearer transmitted substantially enhanced potential to the following generation. Bella specifically used herself and her siblings as examples: because they were Maya's children, they developed noticeably greater talent than ordinary Wolves of their generation, while Adam was considered an anomaly even among those already exceptional siblings.[3]
Bellatrix refused to reveal every secret connected to the mutation, explaining that certain information was restricted to the Matriarch. Natalia consequently inferred that the Elizabeth Gene must possess additional importance beyond simple physical improvement, especially because Clan Lykos had historically produced Generals for the Werewolf monarchy.[3]
The prestige of the bloodline caused members of other Alpha Clans to seek relationships with Lykos men and women in hopes of acquiring its potential. Bellatrix regarded many of these efforts with contempt, knowing Maya would never allow an actual Elizabeth bearer to be separated from Clan Lykos. Even descendants produced through relationships with other Alpha Clans were generally raised within the Lykos family.[3]
Leona interpreted this information as evidence that the Clan wanted to use her as a breeding tool. Bella immediately denied it, explaining that Maya's enormous reverse harem was a consequence of Maya's own eccentric personality rather than an obligation imposed upon Elizabeth bearers. Bellatrix emphasized that members could live however they wished as long as they fulfilled their responsibilities and did not damage the Clan.[3]
Bellatrix then explained the political situation more directly. Leona's potential had made her a temporary preferred successor, but the Clan would not force her into leadership if she did not want it. Bella considered forced leadership fundamentally self-defeating and argued that a capable person who actually desired the position would serve the family better. Immediately afterward, she openly asked Leona to support her candidacy for Clan Leader.[3]
Bella also discussed why Leona's mutation had remained dormant for so long. She carefully identified her explanation as an unconfirmed rumor, suggesting that Adam may have deliberately moved his daughter away from Samar and into the comparatively peaceful Human World to prevent the environment from stimulating her instinctive side. Bellatrix admitted she possessed no proof and found the behavior difficult to reconcile with the brother she personally knew.[3]
Their discussion was interrupted by the arrival of [[Connor Lykos]], whom Bella identified as her brother and another leading candidate for Clan leadership. Connor's confrontational behavior toward Leona's Vampire attendants immediately created tension. Bellatrix watched in astonishment when one of the strongest among her siblings was neutralized before he could meaningfully react, demonstrating the enormous difference between Leona's attendants and ordinary powerful [[Werewolves]].[5]
Bella also shared Leona's general dislike of Connor's attitude and quickly noticed that his actions had harmed his standing with his niece. Since Leona's support could greatly alter the Clan's internal political balance, Bella understood that Connor had inadvertently provided her with an advantage in their succession rivalry.[5]
Unknown to Bellatrix at the time, Connor's hostility was not simply the behavior of a rival desperate for power. Connor privately admitted that he did not particularly desire leadership himself; he maintained his rivalry with Bella because he believed genuine competition was necessary for her growth and intended to oppose her if she proved incapable of becoming a worthy leader.[5]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Much later, Bellatrix became romantically involved with [[Edward Jonathan Elizabeth Lykos]]. By Chapter 1115, Edward had already taken over the practical leadership of Clan Lykos, and [[Andrew Walter]] openly teased him after hearing that he intended to marry his aunt Bellatrix.[6]
By the following chapter, Edward explicitly described Bellatrix as his wife. When Andrew questioned how he had ended up married to his own aunt, Edward explained that his father's former position and Maya's influence had pushed circumstances in that direction, but he had ultimately continued along the path by his own choice. After meeting Bellatrix more personally, the two discovered that they shared similar tastes and were highly compatible, resulting in a genuine relationship rather than a purely political arrangement.[2]
Edward also explained that Bellatrix was far from the only female relative interested in him after he became Clan Leader. Several aunts, cousins, and nieces had approached him, but Edward believed most were more interested in the strength and authority attached to his position than in Edward himself. This made him considerably less enthusiastic about expanding his romantic household indiscriminately.[2]
Edward's account further demonstrated the difficulty of Lykos leadership. Once he assumed control, numerous male relatives repeatedly challenged his authority because the Clan expected its Alpha to prove himself the strongest, most intelligent, and most resilient candidate. Edward therefore had to establish his superiority rather than relying entirely upon inherited authority.[2]
Bellatrix's earlier ambition for leadership consequently did not translate into her being the later sole Head of Clan Lykos. The available chronology instead identifies Edward as the Clan Leader during this period.[6][2]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Bellatrix continued living as part of the Lykos family deep into the later Imperial Era. During a major Imperial celebration, she entered a bar alongside Maya, Leona, and Liliana, joining Edward, Adam, Johnny, Andrew, and Frederick while they watched the parade together.[7]
By this point, Clan leadership had undergone another explicit generational confirmation. Johnny Lykos addressed Edward as the new Alpha, while Adam declared himself retired. Edward complained that he had never actually wanted the position and considered leadership an exhausting and thankless responsibility.[7]
Bellatrix remained closely integrated into the senior Lykos family, sitting beside Maya while the group watched the festivities. When Adam embarrassed himself in front of the younger generation, Bellatrix joined Edward, Leona, and Johnny in reacting with familiar exasperation, demonstrating that the enormous passage of Imperial time had not fundamentally erased the family's ordinary interpersonal dynamics.[7]
Later in the gathering, Johnny asked Leona to arrange training for his children under [[Scathach]]. Adam considered the proposal insane, while Maya immediately approved because the children would become stronger and more disciplined. Bellatrix silently agreed with Adam, Edward, and Leona that Maya's enthusiastic approval made her nearly as crazy as Scathach herself.[7]

### Skills & Abilities
Alpha Werewolf Physiology: Bellatrix is an Alpha Werewolf, placing her among the naturally superior members of her species. She possesses the fundamental supernatural characteristics of [[Werewolves]] at a considerably higher level than ordinary members of the race. However, Bellatrix has relatively few direct combat feats shown in the novel, so the upper limits of her individual physical abilities remain unknown.
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: As an Alpha Werewolf, Bellatrix possesses physical strength, speed, durability, stamina, and agility vastly beyond ordinary Humans and lower-ranking Werewolves.
- Enhanced Senses: Bellatrix possesses the heightened sensory perception characteristic of Werewolves, including their powerful instinctive awareness of danger and supernatural presences.
- Enhanced Instincts: Her Werewolf instincts respond directly to sufficiently powerful predators and Alpha-like existences. When [[Fenrir]] roared and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] subsequently released an even more overwhelming pressure, Bellatrix's instincts immediately recognized the enormous difference in power.[4]
- Regenerative Healing Factor: As a Werewolf, Bellatrix possesses the accelerated healing characteristic of her species.
- Longevity: Bellatrix possesses the greatly extended lifespan of supernatural Werewolves and remained active into the later Imperial Era.[7]
- Werewolf Transformation: As a full Werewolf and Alpha, Bellatrix possesses the racial ability to assume the transformed combat state associated with her species.

#### Exceptional Physical Conditioning
Bellatrix maintains an extremely trained body even independently of her natural Werewolf advantages. Her debut specifically emphasizes her sharply developed abdominal musculature and the disciplined effort necessary to obtain her physique.[1]

#### Lykos Bloodline
Bellatrix belongs to the direct bloodline descending from [[Elizabeth Lykos]], the strongest wife of the Werewolf Progenitor. Clan Lykos is one of Samar's oldest Werewolf families and possesses unusually strong hereditary traits and political privileges because of its ancestry.[4]

#### Enhanced Inherited Potential
Bellatrix does not possess the Elizabeth mutation herself. During Chapter 772, she explicitly states that only Maya and Leona among the contemporary Clan carried the "Elizabeth" name associated with the known mutation.[4]
She nevertheless benefited genetically from being one of Maya's children. Bellatrix explained that the offspring of a woman carrying the Elizabeth Gene receive substantially increased talent and potential. Her own generation was noticeably superior to ordinary Wolves of the same age, with Adam standing out as an exceptional anomaly even among Maya's already enhanced children.[3]

#### Clan Knowledge
Bellatrix possesses extensive knowledge of Clan Lykos' genealogy, political history, ancient privileges, succession customs, and restricted bloodline information. She personally educated Leona and Natalia about the Clan's presence throughout Samar's history and explained most of the publicly accessible mechanics surrounding the Elizabeth Gene.[4][3]

#### Political Acumen
Bellatrix is politically perceptive and capable of recognizing how relationships, reputation, and family support affect Clan leadership. She immediately sought Leona's backing once it became apparent that Leona did not want the leadership position herself, and later recognized Connor's poor interaction with Leona as an advantage to her own candidacy.[3][5]

#### Leadership Capability
Bellatrix was one of the principal candidates considered capable of assuming leadership of Clan Lykos. Her candidacy placed her in competition with Connor, another powerful and respected member of Maya's generation.[3][5]
Her philosophy toward leadership is notably pragmatic: she believes willingness and competence are more important than simply possessing the greatest theoretical potential and openly opposes forcing an unwilling individual into command.[3]

### Relationships

#### Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood
[[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] is Bellatrix's mother, Matriarch, and the Elizabeth bearer whose genetics significantly increased the potential of Bellatrix's generation.[3]
Bellatrix respects Maya's overwhelming strength but openly recognizes her eccentricities. She described Maya's enormous reverse harem as a consequence of Maya being an unusually excessive woman rather than a normal expectation placed upon female members of Clan Lykos.[3]
She also explained that Maya's husbands had been unable to meaningfully challenge her authority because none of them possessed comparable Alpha strength. According to Bella, the only contemporary male Alpha who could properly challenge Maya was the [[Lycaon|Werewolf King]] himself.[3]
Despite generations of change within the Clan, Bellatrix remained close to her mother in the Imperial Era and was shown sitting beside Maya during a later family gathering.[7]

#### Edward Jonathan Elizabeth Lykos
[[Edward Jonathan Elizabeth Lykos]] is Bellatrix's nephew and later husband.
Their relationship developed sometime after Bellatrix's initial succession campaign. Edward acknowledged that family circumstances and Maya's influence contributed to bringing them together, but also stressed that he ultimately entered the relationship voluntarily. After spending time with Bellatrix, the two discovered that their personalities and preferences were compatible.[2]
By Chapter 1116, Edward explicitly described himself as married to Bellatrix.[2]
Bellatrix remained beside the wider family after Edward became the recognized Alpha and leader of Clan Lykos.[7]

#### Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos
[[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]] is Bellatrix's niece and, after Bellatrix's marriage to Edward, also her sister-in-law.
Bellatrix was one of the primary relatives responsible for introducing Leona to the deeper history and customs of Clan Lykos. She explained Leona's connection to [[Elizabeth Lykos]], the significance of her mutation, why Maya had personally named her, and why her return threatened to alter the political balance surrounding Clan succession.[4][3]
Although Bellatrix initially wanted Leona's political support for her own leadership ambitions, her straightforward attitude earned Leona's respect rather than immediate hostility.[3]

#### Connor Lykos
[[Connor Lykos]] is Bellatrix's brother and former principal rival in the Clan's succession dispute.[5]
Bellatrix openly sought leadership while Connor was identified as another leading candidate. She disliked aspects of his behavior and was pleased when his confrontational attitude toward Leona's companions harmed his standing with their niece.[5]
Unknown to Bella during the initial dispute, Connor privately treated their rivalry as a method of forcing her to improve. He did not particularly desire leadership for himself and intended to challenge Bella so that she could not become complacent; if she proved incapable of becoming a suitable leader, he was prepared to prevent her succession.[5]

#### Ivan Lykos
[[Ivan Lykos]] is both Bellatrix's brother and husband. Bella introduced him in precisely those terms when meeting Leona.[1]

#### Marcelo Lykos
[[Marcelo Lykos]] is another of Bellatrix's brothers and husbands. Like Ivan, his relationship with Bella reflects the internal marriage customs practiced within Clan Lykos.[1]

#### Rodrigo Lykos
[[Rodrigo Lykos]] is Bellatrix's nephew and husband. Bella introduced him to Leona while simultaneously teasing him over his height of approximately 165 centimeters.[1]

### Trivia
- Bellatrix prefers to be called Bella.[1]
- When Bella introduced herself, Leona immediately made a joke about Bellatrix Lestrange and the Cruciatus Curse from Harry Potter. Bella did not understand the reference.[1]
- Her fashion is heavily influenced by her mother Maya.[1]
- Bellatrix's extremely defined six-pack is specifically emphasized during her debut.[1]
- She is simultaneously the sister and wife of [[Ivan Lykos]] and [[Marcelo Lykos]].[1]
- She is simultaneously the aunt and wife of [[Edward Jonathan Elizabeth Lykos]].[2]
- She is simultaneously the aunt and sister-in-law of [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]].
- She is simultaneously the aunt and wife of [[Rodrigo Lykos]].[1]

- Bellatrix's generation nevertheless benefited from Maya's Elizabeth mutation. She explicitly stated that she and her siblings were noticeably more talented than ordinary Wolves of their generation.[3]
- Bellatrix genuinely wanted to become Head of Clan Lykos and directly asked Leona to support her candidacy.[3]

- Edward stated that several of his aunts, cousins, and nieces became interested in him after he rose in status, but he distrusted many of them because he believed they were interested primarily in his power rather than Edward himself.[2]
- During the later Imperial family gathering, Bellatrix internally agreed with Adam, Edward, and Leona that Maya was nearly as insane as [[Scathach]] for enthusiastically approving of Johnny sending his children through Scathach's training.[7]
