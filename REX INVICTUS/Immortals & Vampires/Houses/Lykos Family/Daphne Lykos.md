### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 773
Daphne Lykos is an Alpha Werewolf of the [[Lykos Clan]], one of the many daughters of former Matriarch [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]], and both the sister and wife of [[Connor Lykos]]. She is one of Connor's two known wives alongside their niece [[Iona Lykos]] and regularly accompanies him during matters concerning the internal politics of Clan Lykos.[1][2]
Daphne was introduced during [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]]' visit to the Clan's library, where she and Iona accompanied Connor while he investigated Leona's arrival and the unusually powerful retainers sent by [[Victor Elderblood]] to protect her.[1][2] Although she participates in Connor's political maneuvering, Daphne is considerably more cautious about provoking Victor's faction and repeatedly questions whether Connor's methods are unnecessarily dangerous.[2]

### Physical Appearance
Daphne's physical appearance has not yet been described in sufficient detail by the novel.
She is one of Maya's daughters and belongs to the old Lykos bloodline, but no chapter currently establishes her exact height, eye color, hair color, skin tone, clothing or transformed Werewolf appearance.

### Personality
Daphne is cautious, practical and comparatively level-headed. Although willing to support Connor in his political maneuvering within Clan Lykos, she is considerably more concerned than he is about the consequences of unnecessarily antagonizing powerful outsiders.[2]
This becomes especially apparent after Connor deliberately tests Leona's escorts. Daphne is genuinely shaken by how easily [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s maids restrain her, Iona and Connor, admitting afterward that she felt as though a single wrong movement could have resulted in her being reduced to pieces.[2]
She openly complains that Connor convinced her to participate in an unnecessarily dangerous test and points out that one mistake could have damaged relations between Clan Lykos and the Demon King. Connor considers the information gained worth the risk, but Daphne clearly would have preferred a less provocative method.[2]
Despite these complaints, Daphne is loyal to Connor and supports his larger objective. She agrees to continue participating in his false rivalry with [[Bellatrix Lykos]] because she understands his actual motivation: Connor wants Bellatrix to develop into a competent leader rather than inherit the Clan without meaningful opposition.[2]
Daphne also appears genuinely attracted to Connor's determination to protect the Clan. When he states that Lykos is his home and that he will not allow a mediocre individual to rule it, both Daphne and Iona smile despite frequently complaining about his obsession.[2]
She can nevertheless be exasperated by her husband's contradictions. Daphne reminds Connor that Maya had already offered him the Clan's leadership directly and points out that accepting the position would have been easier than maintaining an elaborate fake succession conflict simply to train Bellatrix.[2]
Daphne also displays some possessiveness toward Connor. When Connor admits that he genuinely found Leona attractive rather than merely pretending to show interest in her, both Daphne and Iona react with visible possessiveness.[2]
Compared with Iona's more provocative humor, Daphne often assumes the role of the more restrained member of the pair. She repeatedly reprimands Iona when her comments become inappropriate, particularly during their later conversation concerning Maya and Victor.[2]

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Daphne first appeared in Chapter 773 during [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]]' visit to the sacred library of the [[Lykos Clan]]. Leona, [[Natalia Elderblood née Alioth]] and several members of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household had been speaking with [[Bellatrix Lykos]] about the history of [[Elizabeth Lykos]] and the rare Elizabeth Trait when Connor arrived at the entrance.[1]
Connor was accompanied, as usual, by his two wives. One was his biological sister, Daphne Lykos, while the other was his niece, [[Iona Lykos]].[1]
Connor immediately questioned why Vampires were present within the Clan's sacred library. Bellatrix identified him to Leona as one of the main candidates for the future leadership of Clan Lykos. His confrontational behavior quickly attracted the attention of Leona's escorts.[1][2]
When Connor displayed interest in Leona, [[Bruna Elderblood]] immediately appeared in front of him and struck him in the abdomen. Daphne and Iona attempted to react but were subdued almost instantly by blood-red threads under the control of Victor's maids.[2]
[[Maria Elderblood]] restrained the women closely enough that neither was willing to continue resisting, while the maids casually discussed the various ways in which they could incapacitate or kill the intruders. The experience terrified both Daphne and Iona and demonstrated to them that the women accompanying Leona were dramatically stronger than ordinary servants.[2]
The three were eventually expelled from the library rather than seriously harmed.
Once outside, Daphne confronted Connor about the incident. She admitted that the maids' killing intent had convinced her that a single wrong action could have resulted in her death and complained that Connor had persuaded her into participating in an extremely dangerous plan.[2]
Connor revealed that the confrontation had largely been an information-gathering exercise. He wanted to determine what kind of individuals Victor would send to protect Leona and what that implied about the Demon King's attitude toward her. The encounter convinced him that Leona was exceptionally important to Victor and that the women accompanying her were elite members of Victor's inner circle rather than ordinary servants.[2]
Daphne argued that Connor could have investigated the situation through a less provocative approach. Connor responded that Bellatrix would have remained guarded around him and that he wanted a genuine reaction from Leona's protectors rather than information filtered through conversation.[2]
The conversation then turned toward Connor and Bellatrix's supposed competition for leadership of Clan Lykos. Daphne and Iona revealed that they were aware Connor did not actually want to become Clan Leader. Instead, he deliberately maintained the appearance of being Bellatrix's rival because he believed she needed genuine competition in order to mature.[2]
Connor explained that if Bellatrix eventually became a competent leader, he would willingly allow her to rule. If she proved inadequate, however, he intended to use his influence within the Clan to remove her before her incompetence could damage their family.[2]
Daphne supported this plan despite finding it needlessly complicated. She reminded Connor that Maya had previously offered him leadership directly and that accepting would have eliminated the need for the entire succession game. Connor refused because actually governing the Clan would be too troublesome.[2]
Daphne and Iona could only stare at him dryly, recognizing that the elaborate political game he had created was considerably more troublesome than simply becoming Clan Leader.
Later, the group encountered Connor's biological father, who had just witnessed Maya spending time with Victor. The man warned them that Victor possessed exactly the dominant Alpha qualities that women descended from Maya instinctively desired.[2]
His explanation also revealed additional context regarding Daphne and Iona's relationship with Connor. Both women had displayed the same Lykos tendency toward seeking a sufficiently powerful male Alpha, and Connor had been strong enough to establish himself as their Alpha rather than being dominated by them.[2]
Daphne's reaction to the developing situation was considerably more restrained than Iona's. When Iona began mocking Maya's existing husbands and making increasingly inappropriate comments, Daphne repeatedly reprimanded her.[2]
Daphne has not received another substantial individual storyline in the currently documented material.

### Skills & Abilities
Daphne has no dedicated combat scene and has demonstrated very little of her individual supernatural abilities. Her current abilities are primarily based upon her Werewolf classification and membership within the Lykos family.

#### Alpha Werewolf Physiology
- Alpha Werewolf Physiology: Daphne is an Alpha Werewolf of Clan Lykos and possesses the supernatural physical foundation associated with high-ranking members of her Race.
- Superhuman Strength: Alpha [[Werewolves]] possess physical strength considerably exceeding ordinary Humans and lower-ranking Werewolves.
- Superhuman Speed: Daphne possesses supernatural movement speed and reflexes.
- Superhuman Durability: Her Werewolf physiology provides greater physical resilience than ordinary beings.
- Enhanced Stamina: Werewolves possess substantially greater endurance than Humans.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Daphne possesses the accelerated healing characteristic of her Race.
- Enhanced Senses: Werewolf physiology grants heightened smell, hearing and predatory perception.
- Longevity: Daphne possesses the extended natural lifespan of a Werewolf.
- Werewolf Transformation: She possesses the racial ability to manifest greater Werewolf characteristics, although her transformed state has not been individually shown.
- Natural Weapons: Werewolf transformations provide enhanced claws and fangs.
- Predatory Instincts: Daphne possesses the strong instinctive responses toward hierarchy, dominance and powerful Alphas characteristic of Samar's Werewolves.
- Alpha Instincts: Chapter 774 establishes that Daphne was one of the Lykos women whose instincts drove her toward seeking a sufficiently powerful Alpha partner, with Connor being strong enough to assume that position in their relationship.[2]

#### Lykos Heritage
- Lykos Bloodline: Daphne is one of the biological daughters of [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] and consequently descends directly from the ancient lineage of [[Elizabeth Lykos]] and the first Werewolf Progenitor Licael.
- High Natural Potential: Maya's descendants inherit unusually strong Werewolf ancestry, with descendants of an Elizabeth carrier possessing greater potential even when they do not inherit the mutation itself.[3][1]
- Strong Instinctive Nature: Connor's father states that women of the Lykos lineage inherit Maya's tendency toward strong Alpha-oriented instincts to varying degrees, explicitly identifying Daphne and Iona as examples.[2]
- No Confirmed Elizabeth Trait: Daphne has never been identified as a carrier of the Elizabeth Trait.

#### Will and Composure
- Threat Recognition: Daphne quickly recognized that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s maids were capable of killing her if she resisted recklessly and chose not to escalate the confrontation further.[2]
- Political Awareness: She understood that harming relations between Clan Lykos and Victor over Connor's experiment could have catastrophic consequences for the Clan.[2]
Daphne has not been confirmed to possess:

- Ancient Werewolf physiology.
- True Alpha status.
- Complete Werewolf Transformation.
- Godhood or Divinity.
- An individual Element.

### Relationships

#### Connor Lykos
[[Connor Lykos]] is both Daphne's biological brother and husband.[1] Daphne frequently accompanies him alongside Iona and is one of the few people fully aware that Connor's supposed desire to lead Clan Lykos is largely an act.
Daphne complains frequently about Connor's complicated methods but nevertheless supports him. She understands that his rivalry with Bellatrix exists primarily to force Bellatrix to mature rather than because he genuinely desires the position of Clan Leader.[2]
She is also attracted to Connor's devotion to the Clan. Both Daphne and Iona visibly approve when Connor declares that Lykos is his home and that he will not allow an incompetent individual to control their family.[2]
Connor's father later states that both Daphne and Iona had historically displayed the Alpha-seeking instincts associated with the women of Maya's lineage. Connor was sufficiently powerful to establish himself as their Alpha, allowing their relationships to develop around him.[2]

#### Iona Lykos
[[Iona Lykos]] is Daphne's niece and fellow wife of Connor. The two women are regularly shown together and share knowledge of Connor's plans regarding Clan Lykos.[1][2]
Their personalities differ noticeably. Iona is much more provocative and willing to make inappropriate jokes, while Daphne repeatedly attempts to restrain her.
Despite this, the two appear close and generally act together when accompanying Connor.

#### Bellatrix Lykos
[[Bellatrix Lykos]] is Daphne's sister and Connor's nominal rival for leadership of Clan Lykos.
Daphne knows that Connor's rivalry with Bellatrix is intentionally exaggerated. Connor believes Bellatrix requires a genuine rival if she is going to develop into a leader capable of protecting their family, and Daphne agrees to continue participating in this arrangement.[2]
Daphne does not display personal hostility toward Bellatrix.

#### Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood
[[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] is Daphne's mother and the former Matriarch of Clan Lykos.
Maya apparently considered Connor sufficiently capable that she had previously suggested he simply assume leadership of the Clan, something Daphne later cites while questioning why Connor continues participating in the succession game.[2]
Daphne also inherited some of the strong Alpha-oriented instincts characteristic of Maya's female descendants.[2]

#### Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos
[[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]] is Daphne's niece through [[Adam William Lykos]].
Daphne first encounters Leona on-page during Chapter 773 while accompanying Connor into the Lykos library. Connor's attempt to evaluate Leona and her escorts becomes the catalyst for Daphne's first significant scene.[1][2]
