### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 762
Ivan Lykos is an Alpha Werewolf of the [[Lykos Clan]], one of the many sons of former Matriarch [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]], and one of the husbands of his sister [[Bellatrix Lykos]]. He was introduced alongside [[Marcelo Lykos]] and [[Rodrigo Lykos]] when Bellatrix encountered [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]] and [[Natalia Elderblood née Alioth]] during [[Victor Elderblood]]'s visit to Samar.[1]
Ivan has received very little individual focus within the story. His introduction primarily establishes his place within Maya's enormous family and Bellatrix's household. Unlike several more prominent members of Clan Lykos, Ivan has not yet received a dedicated battle, unique supernatural ability or individual storyline.

### Physical Appearance
Ivan is described as the oldest-looking of the three men accompanying Bellatrix during their introduction. Bellatrix also describes him as possessing a scary face, making his intimidating appearance the primary physical characteristic specifically established by the novel.[1]
No canonical description has yet established Ivan's exact height, eye color, hair color, skin tone, clothing or transformed Werewolf appearance.

### Personality
Very little has been revealed about Ivan's personality.
During his introduction, Ivan behaves quietly and calmly despite his intimidating appearance. When Bellatrix introduces him to Leona as her brother, husband and Leona's uncle, Ivan simply greets Leona with a brief "Hello" rather than attempting to dominate the conversation or intimidate her.[1]
His restrained behavior contrasts somewhat with Bellatrix's teasing personality and the banter between Marcelo and Rodrigo. Beyond this brief interaction, Ivan has not received enough individual dialogue or narrative focus to establish his ambitions, temperament or personal views.

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Ivan first appeared during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s visit to Samar when [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]] and [[Natalia Elderblood née Alioth]] encountered members of Leona's enormous maternal family.
While walking through Lykos territory, Leona complained about the enormous size of the area before being approached by a tall, athletic woman with snow-white hair and emerald-green eyes. The woman introduced herself as [[Bellatrix Lykos]], one of [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]]'s daughters and therefore Leona's aunt.[1]
Three male members of Clan Lykos stood behind Bellatrix. She proceeded to introduce them individually, beginning with Ivan:
"The oldest with the scary face is Ivan Lykos; he is my brother, my husband, and your uncle."[1]
Ivan simply greeted Leona, who returned the greeting while struggling to process the unusual family structure being explained to her.
Bellatrix then introduced [[Marcelo Lykos]], another of her brothers and husbands, followed by [[Rodrigo Lykos]], her nephew and another husband. The increasingly complicated relationships caused considerable confusion for Leona, who had been raised primarily within a Human family structure and was unfamiliar with the customs practiced by some branches of the ancient Lykos family.[1]
Ivan remained largely silent throughout the exchange while Marcelo and Rodrigo began arguing over Rodrigo's height.
The scene remains Ivan's only substantial individual appearance in the currently documented material.

### Skills & Abilities
Ivan has not participated in a dedicated combat scene and has not demonstrated any unique Element, technique or supernatural power. His confirmed capabilities are primarily those associated with his status as an Alpha Werewolf.

#### Alpha Werewolf Physiology
- Alpha Werewolf Physiology: Ivan is classified among the Alpha [[Werewolves]] of the [[Lykos Clan]] and possesses the enhanced supernatural physiology associated with that rank.
- Superhuman Strength: Alpha Werewolves possess physical strength significantly greater than ordinary Humans and lower-ranking Werewolves.
- Superhuman Speed: Ivan possesses supernatural movement speed and reflexes.
- Superhuman Durability: His Werewolf physiology provides enhanced physical resilience.
- Enhanced Stamina: Alpha Werewolves possess endurance considerably beyond ordinary Humans.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Ivan possesses the accelerated supernatural healing associated with Werewolf physiology.
- Enhanced Senses: His Race grants heightened smell, hearing and predatory awareness.
- Longevity: Werewolves possess substantially greater lifespans than ordinary Humans.
- Werewolf Transformation: Ivan possesses the racial capacity to manifest more pronounced Werewolf characteristics, although his individual transformation has never been shown.
- Natural Weapons: His transformed physiology provides enhanced claws and fangs.
- Predatory Instincts: Ivan possesses the instinctive sensitivity toward danger, hierarchy and dominance characteristic of Werewolves.
- Alpha Presence: As an Alpha Werewolf, Ivan possesses greater instinctive authority than ordinary Wolves, although no individual feat involving this ability has been shown.

#### Lykos Heritage
- Lykos Bloodline: Ivan is one of the biological children of [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] and therefore descends from the ancient Werewolf lineage originating with [[Elizabeth Lykos]] and Licael.
- Ancestral Potential: Members of Maya's direct lineage possess exceptionally strong Werewolf ancestry, though the novel has not specified Ivan's individual upper potential.[2][3]
- No Confirmed Elizabeth Trait: Ivan has never been identified as a carrier of the rare Elizabeth Trait.
Ivan has not been confirmed to possess:

### Relationships

#### Bellatrix Lykos
[[Bellatrix Lykos]] is simultaneously Ivan's sister and wife.[1] Bellatrix openly introduces Ivan in both roles when meeting Leona and treats the arrangement as an ordinary part of their family structure.
Ivan appears to be one of Bellatrix's established husbands alongside Marcelo and Rodrigo. No conflict or jealousy between the three men is shown during their introduction.

#### Marcelo Lykos
[[Marcelo Lykos]] is Ivan's brother and a fellow husband of Bellatrix. Both are direct children of Maya and appear together during Bellatrix's Chapter 762 introduction.[1]
Bellatrix distinguishes the two primarily through appearance, identifying Ivan as the older-looking man with the frightening face while Marcelo is described as baby-faced.

#### Rodrigo Lykos
[[Rodrigo Lykos]] is Ivan's nephew and another husband of Bellatrix.[1]
The three men appear to form Bellatrix's established household. Unlike Marcelo, Ivan does not participate in teasing Rodrigo about his comparatively short height.

#### Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood
[[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] is Ivan's mother and the former Matriarch of Clan Lykos. Ivan is one of more than fifty children Maya produced throughout her extremely long life.
No substantial direct interaction between Maya and Ivan has currently been shown.

#### Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos
[[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]] is Ivan's niece through [[Adam William Lykos]]. Ivan first meets her on-page during Chapter 762 after Bellatrix introduces him as Leona's uncle.[1]
Ivan's unusual simultaneous position as Bellatrix's brother and husband contributes to Leona's confusion regarding the structure of her maternal family.
