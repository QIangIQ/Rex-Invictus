#### Debut
Chapter 1131
Surrender? What a joke! You seem to be misunderstanding something, Vik-Ir-Loah.The one at a disadvantage here is not me. It's you. I'm not trapped here with you. You're trapped here with me.
—Victor Walker (Clone) to [[Vik-Ir-loah]], Chapter 1132
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Independent Clone was an autonomous incarnation created by Victor Elderblood to perform reconnaissance beyond the barrier separating his native Sector from the Upper Sectors. Victor originally considered sending an ordinary weaker version of himself, but rejected the idea because even a disposable body could contain traces of his true existence that an advanced enemy might exploit. He instead created a much weaker younger version of himself made from pure Energy and deliberately excluded several of his most dangerous connections, including his Eldritch Factor, his connection to the [[World Trees]], and [[Junketsu]].[1]
The Clone possessed an independent consciousness rather than being directly controlled like a puppet. The original Victor could nevertheless observe through him, and information perceived by the Clone was transmitted back to the original. His dominant personality was modeled after the reckless Victor from the beginning of the story, before the responsibilities of ruling the [[Elderblood Empire]] forced him to restrain himself, while he retained Emperor Victor's accumulated knowledge and personal combat experiences. Most personal memories were intentionally removed; he did not remember Victor's wives or family, ensuring that even successful information extraction would reveal little concerning the people Victor valued most.[1]
Despite being deliberately nerfed, the Clone was still an extremely dangerous existence. He remained a Grandmaster, possessed access to Victor's Negative Divinities and problematic Soul abilities, retained Victor's ability to evolve through combat, and demonstrated enough versatility and experience to defeat forces whose ordinary soldiers individually possessed power comparable to [[Primordial Gods]]. Rose specifically noted that the Clone's raw Power Level was only somewhat above the ordinary soldiers of the Empire of Light; his overwhelming battlefield performance came from how effectively he combined martial mastery, supernatural abilities, experience, psychological pressure and tactics.[2][3][4]

### Physical Appearance
The Clone was initially described as looking exactly like [[Victor Elderblood]] with several differences, most notably appearing as a younger version of the Emperor. He possessed Victor's black hair and general features but lacked the overwhelming presence and complete equipment of the original. After receiving a greatsword created through Victor's abilities, armor resembling a lesser version of the Emperor's manifested over his body, together with a cape formed from pure Miasma.[1]
Shortly before entering the Upper Sector, the Clone ran a hand through his hair and slicked it backward, creating a hairstyle strongly reminiscent of [[Adonis Snow]] while still retaining Victor's overall appearance. Victor immediately recognized the behavior as Adonis' inherited narcissism. The narration described the resulting appearance as a visual fusion of the two, but the Clone was not literally created by combining Victor and Adonis; Adonis' influence came from traits already inherited by Victor.[1]
During his battle against [[Solus 969]], the Clone transformed into Victor's Demon King of Tyranny form. His body grew to approximately the scale Victor possessed in his Emperor Form, his greatsword increased proportionally, and the powers of the Four Horsemen of The Apocalypse began operating passively around him.[4]

### Personality
The Clone embodied [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s younger and much less restrained personality. He was reckless, intensely competitive, arrogant, theatrical, battle-hungry and openly amused by situations that would terrify ordinary combatants. He deliberately taunted enemies, laughed while surrounded by overwhelming numbers and treated the battle against the Empire of Light as an opportunity to enjoy himself after the original Victor had spent years carrying the responsibilities of an Emperor.[1][2]
His behavior also displayed the narcissistic traits Victor inherited from Adonis. Shortly after being created, the Clone slicked his hair backward and casually praised how impressive he was, immediately causing the original Victor to recognize Adonis' influence. Victor's family later joked that he had effectively thrown an improved combination of his younger self and Adonis into another Sector, although this was a description of his personality and mannerisms rather than his actual composition.[1]
However, his recklessness never overrode his objective. The experience of Emperor Victor remained present beneath the younger personality, allowing the Clone to manipulate his opponents while appearing to behave impulsively. He fabricated a false history to conceal the [[Elderblood Empire]], deliberately searched for emotional weaknesses among the soldiers, allowed confrontations to continue in order to obtain additional information, concealed abilities after detecting unknown observers and never forgot that his primary purpose was reconnaissance. His chaotic behavior was therefore backed by Victor's accumulated tactical knowledge and experience.[2][3][4]

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Following his conversation with the Primordial of Balance, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] refused to blindly trust either the Primordial's intentions or the unknown civilizations waiting beyond his Sector. Rather than cross the barrier himself, he created a disposable independent Clone with as few exploitable connections as possible. The Clone lacked Victor's Eldritch Factor, World Tree connections and [[Junketsu]], while most memories concerning Victor's wives and family were stripped away. Victor gave him a greatsword, instructed him to "put on a show", and sent him through the opening while he and his family observed from safety.[1]
The Clone emerged before a military force belonging to the Empire of Light. After listening to its soldiers proclaim that anything rejected by their Light was inherently Evil, Victor deliberately hid his real origins by claiming that he had slaughtered his own civilization and consumed his planet. When one soldier attempted to execute him, Victor killed him and manifested a dog-like creature from his shoulder that devoured the man's head and Soul. The absorbed information identified the enemy civilization, the Commander [[Vik-Ir-loah]] and several details concerning the soldiers, although Victor discovered that their Souls contained deliberate safeguards against complete information extraction.[2]
More than twenty thousand soldiers subsequently attacked him. The Clone discovered that their individual Power Levels were approximately comparable to [[Primordial Gods]], although they lacked the depth of genuine naturally developed Gods. Victor reflected their ranged attacks, used an Energy slash as a smokescreen, fought from every direction available in outer space and alternated between raw speed, Spatial movement and bending Reality. His control was sufficiently refined that his body released almost no unnecessary Energy during combat.[2]
The Empire eventually analyzed his abilities and deployed enormous Divine-technological pillars designed to counter speedsters, Space users and Reality Benders. The suppression field successfully affected Victor: his movements became abnormally slow and even using Lightning to enhance himself caused greater pressure. Rather than attempting to overpower the unfamiliar technology directly, Victor changed methods. He assumed a proper martial stance and relied upon his Grandmaster skill, producing a single sword attack that cut everything in its path, instantly killed more than five thousand soldiers, destroyed one of the suppression devices and heavily injured Vik-Ir-loah. The Empire's systems consequently identified that it had entered combat with a genuine Grandmaster.[2]
Reinforcements increased the Empire's forces to approximately one hundred thousand warriors supported by fifty ships. Victor immediately destroyed the five largest vessels with a single violet Energy attack, disintegrating the ships and several asteroids in the attack's path. Rather than simply finish the remaining troops, the Clone continued observing them because his true mission was gathering information. His senses expanded throughout the Solar System, transmitting everything he learned back to the original Victor, while his special eyes allowed him to notice additional observers whom Vik-Ir-loah appeared completely unable to perceive.[3]
Vik-Ir-loah attempted to recruit Victor and revealed information concerning the Solus, the numbered elites of the Empire. Victor feigned interest long enough to learn more before revealing a greater portion of his nature. Darkness began leaking from him alongside Death, Blood, Fear, Hatred, Despair and other manifestations of Negativity. The Empire's soldiers were horrified by the number of Negative Divinities concentrated within a single Being, causing Vik-Ir-loah to authorize the Stellar Protocol and accept the destruction of the entire Solar System if necessary to eliminate Victor.[3]
As the battle continued, the Clone became increasingly dangerous because he retained Victor's capacity for evolution. The more he fought, the more he learned the Empire's patterns and the stronger he became. The Empire eventually deployed [[Solus 969]], an elite powerful enough to force Victor into his Demon King of Tyranny state. Victor enlarged together with his greatsword, passively activated the powers associated with the Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse and adapted the Fulger-style principle of striking an enormous number of times within one motion into the greatsword technique One Cut, One Million Cuts. He later coated the sword with the Miasma of the Abyss and incorporated it directly into his martial arts.[4]
While Solus 969 confronted one Victor, another manifestation appeared beside Vik-Ir-loah. The Empire's artificial intelligence explicitly identified Cloning and Umbrakinesis and attempted to deploy countermeasures. When the Empire tried to delete the compromised Commander to prevent an information leak, Victor used his Soul abilities to stop the process. Combining the Miasma of the Abyss, the Darkness of [[Erebus]] and Soul Manipulation, he inverted Vik-Ir-loah's Light-aligned nature and transformed the former Representative of Light into a Representative of Darkness who immediately acknowledged Victor as his Master.[4]
Victor then used strands of his own hair to create ten additional copies. These secondary clones were considerably less sophisticated than the Independent Clone created by the original Emperor: they consisted almost entirely of unstable pure Energy, lacked substantial physical bodies and could disappear from a sufficiently strong blow. Their offensive output remained dangerous, however, and destroying one caused its stored Energy to explode.[4]
One of the secondary clones infiltrated the Empire's installation located around the system's star. After killing everyone inside almost instantly, he discovered a massive sphere composed from pure Positive Energy serving as the local power source. Victor also uncovered the more disturbing function of the Empire's Light: prolonged integration gradually corrupted a Being until even their ability to think and act freely became subordinate to the Light. Before destroying the core, the Clone copied the technological information accumulated within the Solar System, compressed it into a sphere and distorted Space to transmit it to the original Victor, who handed the data to Velnorah for examination within an isolated Dimension.[4]
The secondary clone then destroyed the Positive-Energy core, causing the local Empire infrastructure, suppression systems and stellar-destabilization process to collapse. With the major restrictions removed, Victor stopped prolonging the battle. A gigantic crimson mass erupted from his body and surrounded the battlefield; countless Blood spikes emerged throughout it and pierced the remaining soldiers, with each defeated enemy becoming part of Victor. Solus 969 was left reduced to little more than his upper body before Victor killed him and incorporated him into his Legion.[4]
Only then did Victor learn from 969's memories why the Solus had remained calm. Far beyond the Solar System existed an artificial fortress described as larger than the entire system itself. Its immense cannons turned toward Victor and opened fire. The resulting attack erased the entire Solar System together with the Clone. The Clone openly laughed at the weapon's destructive output as he disappeared, and the narration confirmed that defending against the attack was impossible for the deliberately restricted body, although the original Victor possessed capabilities the Clone lacked. The mission had nevertheless succeeded: the real Victor remained unharmed while technological, military and ideological intelligence concerning the Upper Sectors had already been transmitted home.[4]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Although the Clone ceased to exist at the end of Volume 8, the consequences of his reconnaissance immediately influenced the beginning of Volume 9. His confrontation provided [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family with direct evidence that the civilizations beyond their Mid-Level Sector operated with enormous armies, specialized Divine technology, anti-ability countermeasures, elite Grandmaster-level combatants and strategic weapons capable of treating the destruction of an entire Solar System as an acceptable response to a single dangerous enemy.[5]
The information acquired during the mission reinforced Victor's decision to stop treating expansion as ordinary Imperial growth and instead prepare his entire civilization for eventual conflict with the powers governing the Upper Sectors. By Chapter 1136, Victor tested the weakened barrier toward those Sectors, released his Cosmic Consciousness across his entire native Reality, summoned millions of World Tree connections, mapped every galaxy within the Sector and began restructuring the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s transportation, planetary development and defensive infrastructure. The Clone therefore died before the events of Those Who Rule Reality, but his successful reconnaissance served as one of the direct triggers for the scale of preparation that followed.[6]

### Skills & Abilities
Despite lacking many of the original [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s strongest traits and weapons, the Clone remained extraordinarily dangerous. [[Scathach]] specifically explained that even without Roxanne, Amara and Victor's Eldritch side, the Clone remained the representative of all Negativity, possessed access to its Negative Divinities, retained Grandmaster-level martial mastery and possessed highly problematic Soul Manipulation. Rose further noted that his effectiveness came from using his entire arsenal correctly rather than merely possessing overwhelmingly greater raw Power than every opponent.[4]
- Pure Energy Physiology: The Clone was created entirely from pure Energy rather than a conventional biological Race. Despite this, he possessed a stable physical form capable of fighting, transforming, regenerating and interacting normally with his environment.[1]
- Independent Consciousness: The Clone possessed his own consciousness and was not directly controlled by the original Victor. Victor could observe through him and receive the information he perceived, but the Clone independently chose how to act.[1]
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Even while heavily restricted compared with the original Victor, the Clone possessed immense strength, speed, reflexes, durability and stamina, physically overwhelming large numbers of soldiers whose individual strength was compared to [[Primordial Gods]].[2]
- Flight and Space Survival: He could freely move, maneuver and fight in the vacuum of outer space without requiring oxygen or conventional life-support systems.[1][2]
- Enhanced Senses: His senses were powerful enough to expand across the surrounding Solar System, detect changes occurring to its star and monitor distant events while fighting.[3][4]
- Truth Perception: Even as a Clone, he retained the Dragon Emperor's special eyes capable of observing the Truth of Everything. These allowed him to perceive hidden observers whom [[Vik-Ir-loah]] could not detect.[3]
- Energy Manipulation: Victor possessed extremely refined Energy control and could concentrate, shape and release his Energy through numerous forms. During close combat, almost no unnecessary Energy escaped his body, which was explicitly treated as evidence of his exceptional control.[2]
- Energy Projection: He fired concentrated beams directly from gathered Energy and released enormous ranged attacks through his greatsword. One violet beam completely disintegrated five of the Empire's largest warships together with several asteroids in its path.[2][3]
- Energy Reflection: Through Return to Sender, Victor coated his greatsword in violet Energy and reflected the Empire's incoming Energy attacks back toward its own forces.[2]
- Spatial Manipulation: Victor repeatedly crossed short distances by directly jumping through Space and later distorted Space to transmit captured technological data back to the original Victor.[2][4]
- Reality Warping: During combat, the narration explicitly states that Victor sometimes bent Reality as one of his methods of movement. The Empire subsequently deployed technology designed to suppress Reality Benders alongside Space users and speedsters.[2]
- Ice Manipulation: Victor released cold and generated numerous enormous Ice boulders throughout the surrounding space, including structures large enough to function as artificial Ice asteroids.[2]
- Lightning Manipulation: He could activate Lightning throughout his body to enhance his movement. The Empire's specialized suppression field was capable of restricting this enhancement.[2]
- War Divinity: The Empire's analysis explicitly identified the Clone's Divinity as [[Ares|God of War]]. Victor's own thoughts also emphasized that his knowledge as a God of War allowed him to understand that combat depended upon tactics, emotions, terrain, weapons and preparation rather than Power Level alone.[2]
- Negativity / Negative Divinity Access: Scathach explicitly stated that as the representative of all Negativity, the Clone could access its Negative Divinities. When Victor revealed this side of himself, Darkness, Death, Blood, Fear, Hatred, Despair and other Negative phenomena manifested from him simultaneously.[3][4]
- Darkness Manipulation: Darkness visibly leaked from Victor when he exposed his Negative nature, while he later specifically employed the Darkness of [[Erebus]] to corrupt Vik-Ir-loah.[3][4]
- Blood Manipulation: Blood manifested as one of his Negative aspects, and Victor later generated a massive crimson sphere filled with Blood spikes that annihilated the remaining Empire forces.[3][4]
- Death, Fear, Hatred and Despair: These aspects were explicitly manifested as part of the numerous Negative Divinities radiating from the Clone. Their appearance was sufficiently disturbing that previously composed soldiers began retreating in genuine fear.[3]
- Miasma Manipulation: The Clone manifested a cape composed from pure Miasma immediately after receiving his armor and later coated his greatsword in the Miasma of the Abyss, combining it directly with his Grandmaster martial arts.[1][4]
- Soul Manipulation: Victor retained highly developed Soul abilities. He directly interfered with Vik-Ir-loah's Soul to prevent the Empire from deleting him and used Soul Manipulation as one of the three primary components responsible for transforming the Commander.[4]
- Soul Devouring and Information Absorption: A dog-like monster manifested from Victor's shoulder and devoured an enemy soldier's head and Soul, allowing him to immediately acquire memories and information concerning the Empire. The soldiers possessed specific countermeasures designed to prevent Beings with such abilities from extracting all of their information.[2]
- Corruption and Attribute Inversion: By combining Abyssal Miasma, the Darkness of Erebus and Soul Manipulation, Victor "painted" Vik-Ir-loah's Light with its opposite attribute, completely corrupting him and changing him from a Representative of Light into a Representative of Darkness.[4]
- Umbrakinesis: The Empire's artificial intelligence explicitly detected Umbrakinesis when another Victor manifestation appeared behind Vik-Ir-loah.[4]
- Clone Creation: Victor created ten secondary clones from strands of his own hair. Unlike the primary Independent Clone, these manifestations were simpler concentrations of pure Energy with almost no physical substance.[4]
- Explosive Energy Clones: The secondary clones could be destroyed by a sufficiently powerful attack, but their destruction caused their accumulated Energy to violently explode.[4]
- Reactive Evolution: The Clone explicitly retained Victor's ability to evolve and become stronger. The more he fought, the more thoroughly he learned his enemies' patterns and the stronger he became.[4]
- Adaptation: Closely connected to his evolution and combat experience, Victor continuously adapted to unfamiliar enemy formations, technologies, anti-ability measures and fighting methods throughout the confrontation.[2][4]
- Regeneration: Wounds inflicted by [[Solus 969]] regenerated rapidly during combat. Even damage inflicted upon the Clone's manifested armor was repaired together with his body.[4]
- Demon King of Tyranny Transformation: Victor retained access to his Demon King state. Transforming greatly increased his size and enlarged his greatsword to match his new body.[4]
- Four Horsemen of the Apocalypse Powers: Upon assuming the Demon King of Tyranny form, the powers associated with the Four Horsemen of The Apocalypse were explicitly stated to operate passively around him.[4]
- Absorption: During his final Blood attack, every enemy killed within the crimson sphere became part of Victor. After defeating Solus 969, the warrior's body likewise dissolved and joined Victor's Legion.[4]
- Divine Comprehension and Information Extraction: While infiltrating the Empire's stellar facility, Victor was able to process alien technological information despite not understanding its symbols or language normally. He extracted all available data from the Solar System, compressed it into a sphere and transmitted it back to his original body.[4]
- Grandmaster [[Martial Arts]]: Victor retained his original body's Grandmaster-level martial skill. Even after the Empire successfully restricted his speed and supernatural movement, a single properly executed sword attack killed more than five thousand soldiers, destroyed one of the suppression devices and nearly killed Vik-Ir-loah.[2]
- Grandmaster Swordsmanship: Victor displayed extraordinary mastery of the greatsword and could apply his martial principles through both physical and Energy-based attacks. Against Solus 969, he adapted a Fulger-style principle into One Cut, One Million Cuts, delivering an enormous number of cuts within what appeared to lesser observers as a single exchange.[4]
- Combat Genius: Victor successfully fought an army of opponents within a similar broad Power range by exploiting positioning, timing, three-dimensional movement, friendly fire, psychology, enemy hierarchy and the weaknesses of their equipment rather than relying exclusively upon superior raw strength.[2][4]
- Strategic and Tactical Analysis: The Clone never lost sight of his reconnaissance objective. He continuously analyzed the Empire's weapons, combat doctrine, command structure, technology and responses while deliberately keeping the battle active long enough to obtain additional information.[2][3][4]
- Psychological Warfare: Victor repeatedly manipulated enemy emotions despite the Empire's soldiers initially displaying unusually little individuality. He discovered their pride in the Empire, deliberately insulted it, humiliated Vik-Ir-loah before his troops and maintained an appearance of invincibility that Rose identified as one of the major reasons enemy morale deteriorated.[2][3][4]
- Strategic Deception: Victor falsely claimed that he had murdered his entire civilization and consumed his own planet, preventing the Empire from immediately realizing that he was an expendable scout representing another organized civilization.[2]

### Trivia
- The Clone was not literally a fusion between [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and [[Adonis Snow]]. He was another Victor whose dominant personality resembled his younger self. The Adonis comparison originated from narcissistic traits Victor had inherited from Adonis and the Clone's slicked-back hairstyle.[1]
- Although Victor's family jokingly described the Clone as a "perfect and improved fusion" of Victor and Adonis, Victor himself explicitly described him as "me who doesn't have most of my memories, but still me."[1]
- Victor deliberately removed the Clone's memories of his wives and family so that an enemy could not obtain meaningful information about them by capturing or consuming him.[1]
- The Clone was created without Victor's Eldritch Factor, World Tree connections or [[Junketsu]]. His greatsword was an independently created weapon rather than Junketsu.[1]
- Despite being intentionally weakened, [[Scathach]] still considered him exceptionally dangerous because he retained access to the Negative Divinities, Grandmaster martial mastery and Victor's Soul abilities.[4]
- Rose clarified that the Clone's raw Power Level was only somewhat greater than an ordinary Empire of Light soldier. His massive advantage in practice came from superior mastery, experience, ability variety and battlefield tactics.[4]
- The Empire's suppression technology genuinely worked against Victor. It restricted his movement, Lightning enhancement and problematic mobility abilities; he overcame the situation through martial mastery rather than immunity to Power suppression.[2]
- Grandmasters were considered extremely rare even within the Empire of Light, with [[Vik-Ir-loah]] believing that the number serving the Empire could be counted on one hand.[3]
- The ten secondary clones created in Chapter 1134 should not be confused with Victor himself. They were much simpler pure-Energy constructs with little physical substance.[4]
- The Clone did not die from self-destruction. His final Blood attack defeated the local forces before a distant fortress fired upon the Solar System and erased the entire system together with him.[4]
- The narration explicitly stated that the restricted Clone could not defend against the final Solar-System-destroying attack, while distinguishing him from the original Victor, who possessed capabilities the Clone lacked.[4]
- Chapter 1134 concluded Volume 8: God Emperor Victor Elderblood, while Chapter 1135 began Volume 9: Those Who Rule Reality.[4][5]
- Although already deceased during Volume 9, the Clone's reconnaissance directly influenced Victor's decision to accelerate the expansion, mapping and fortification of his native Sector in preparation for the Upper Sectors.[5][6]
