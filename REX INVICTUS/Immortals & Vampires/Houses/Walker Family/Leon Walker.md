### Appearances

#### Debut
[[Ch06 - Rex Natus III|Chapter 6]]
Leon Walker, later known as Leon Alucard, is the father of [[Victor Elderblood]] and the former husband of [[Anna Elderblood]]. Originally an ordinary Human and retired construction worker, Leon was one of the people Victor personally elevated into the supernatural world, transforming him directly into a Noble Vampire through his Progenitor Blood.[1] His rebirth awakened a unique defensive power capable of generating transparent barriers, while his unusually strong newborn instincts and suppressed desires made his early adjustment to Vampirism considerably more difficult than Anna's.[4][5]
Following the incident caused by his loss of control, Leon was placed under the direct tutelage of [[Oda Blank]], who trained him in the methods of the [[Blank Clan]]. He subsequently became involved in infiltration, intelligence and covert missions alongside the Clan and forces serving Victor.[6][7] Although his relationship with Anna ultimately ended after their transformations and prolonged separation, Leon remains part of Victor's family and continues to be acknowledged by Victor as his mortal father.[8][9]

### Physical Appearance
As a Human, Leon was a fifty-year-old man with brown hair, black eyes and a height of 183 cm. Despite carrying a noticeable beer belly, his years working in construction had left him with muscular arms and a physically solid build.[2]
Leon changed dramatically after being transformed by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. His body rejuvenated to approximately the appearance he possessed in his twenties, excess body fat disappeared and his eyes became the primal blood-red associated with his new Vampiric state.[1] Although Leon and Victor initially considered themselves physically dissimilar immediately after the transformation, their resemblance became much more evident later. By the time Victor returned from Hell, Leon was described as looking similar enough to Victor to resemble his brother, though with shorter hair and without several facial features Victor inherited from Anna.[10] This resemblance was later strong enough for [[Yuno Elderblood]] to identify Leon as visually similar to her father even while immediately recognizing that his aura and existence were incomparably weaker.[11]

### Personality
Leon is a straightforward and relatively uncomplicated man who generally prefers practical solutions over excessive discussion. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] describes him as a man of few words who traditionally solved many of his problems with his fists. Father and son also share a strong love of films and regularly watched movies together, something Leon continued wanting to do even after entering the supernatural world.[12]
Beneath this simplicity, Leon has a pronounced sense of personal responsibility. Having grown up as an orphan, his priorities during youth revolved around survival rather than education. He eventually became involved with dangerous or undesirable parts of society before his relationship with Anna helped redirect his life. Leon had dreamed since childhood of becoming a film director, but by the time his financial circumstances improved, Anna was pregnant and Victor was born with severe health problems. Leon therefore abandoned that ambition and concentrated on supporting his family. He does not consider this choice heroic or a sacrifice, instead believing that accepting responsibility for decisions he personally made was simply what an adult should do.[12]
This sense of responsibility heavily influenced Victor. Even when furious at Leon following his loss of control as a newborn Vampire, Victor remembered that his father had taught him to become a respectable man of character and to value his family.[6]
Leon nevertheless spent much of his Human life suppressing his personal desires. He habitually prioritized his family and circumstances over himself, which became a serious weakness after his transformation. Vampirism magnified impulses that had previously remained buried, and the sudden release of those desires overwhelmed his self-control shortly after rebirth.[12][1][4] Leon openly admitted that he envied Victor's enormous romantic life and entertained the idea of eventually pursuing additional women himself. After becoming a Vampire, this suppressed desire temporarily escalated into unacceptable behavior toward Victor's wives, an incident that deeply shamed him and resulted in Victor placing him under Oda's discipline.[6]
Oda's training substantially improved Leon's control and turned him into a functional operative, but it did not erase his pride or personal flaws. His eventual separation from Anna affected him more deeply than he initially admitted. Although Leon intellectually accepted that both had followed different paths, he later acknowledged that he had taken their relationship for granted after their rebirth and regretted failing to devote more attention to preserving it.[13]

### Synopsis

#### The Awakening
Leon first appears when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] returns home following his transformation and attempts to explain his dramatically changed appearance to his parents. After Anna verifies that the young man in front of her is genuinely their son, Victor introduces Violet as his wife and Kaguya as Violet's maid. Leon and Anna are shocked by the sudden marriage but ultimately accept Victor's decision, reasoning that he is already an adult capable of taking responsibility for his own life.[2]
The situation quickly turns comedic when Leon and Anna become considerably more interested in whether Victor and Violet have already consummated their relationship. After Violet confirms the implication without fully understanding why they are asking, Victor's parents leave to buy groceries and fireworks so that they can celebrate their son's unexpected success.[14]

#### Vampire Society
While [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is taken to [[Nightingale]] by [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], Leon and Anna become more directly involved with the increasingly bizarre structure of their son's family. They properly meet [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] and [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] and learn that Violet is not Victor's only wife. Leon initially struggles to understand how his son managed to acquire three spouses within such a short period, but Ruby and Sasha explain that the three women were childhood friends before Victor entered their lives. Both girls nevertheless accept Leon and Anna as family, with Leon unexpectedly finding himself treated as their father-in-law.[15]
Leon later questions how Victor's wives intend to share him without their jealousy producing constant conflict. Ruby and Sasha acknowledge that jealousy exists but explain that their relationship with one another predates Victor, making the household less comparable to several unrelated women competing for the same man than Leon initially assumed.[16]

#### A New Pillar
After approximately six months in [[Nightingale]], [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] decides that the changes to his body have become impossible to continue hiding. Although he realizes that his Vampiric Charm could potentially force his parents to accept any explanation, he refuses to manipulate the two people who cared for him throughout his sickly childhood.[17]
When Victor returns home, Leon initially struggles to recognize the much taller and more mature man claiming to be his son. Victor proves his identity with private family memories before finally revealing that he has become a Vampire.[18] Leon and Anna react far more calmly than Victor feared. Leon approaches portions of the revelation with humor, while both ultimately accept that the supernatural explanation does not change the identity of the son they raised.[18]
Victor subsequently explains the existence of Nightingale and the broader supernatural world. When Leon hears his son's chosen surname, Alucard, he immediately recognizes that it is Dracula written backwards. Although amused by Victor's audacity, Leon warns him that deliberately provoking people more powerful than himself could eventually create consequences that confidence alone would not solve.[19]
During the conversation, Victor accidentally allows part of his increasingly oppressive presence to affect his parents. Realizing that he frightened them, Victor immediately apologizes and bows to Leon and Anna, demonstrating the unusual respect he continues to give his parents despite his rapidly increasing status in Vampire society.[20]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
As [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] becomes increasingly aware that his Human parents are vulnerable targets in a world filled with supernatural enemies, he begins seriously considering their future. He eventually presents Leon and Anna with the opportunity to stop being ordinary Humans entirely and become Noble Vampires directly through his Progenitor Blood. From Victor's perspective, this would grant them immortality, a supernatural body and the protection of his own bloodline.[21][22]
Rather than treating the transformation as an order, Victor allows his parents to make the choice for themselves. Leon and Anna evaluate the offer according to their own lives instead of simply accepting because becoming a Noble Vampire directly under a Progenitor would be considered an extraordinary privilege by supernatural society.[22][23]
As the threat of a large-scale Demonic invasion becomes increasingly credible, Victor also begins constructing contingencies for the Humans connected to him. Leon and Anna are among the first people he considers protecting or relocating should the barriers separating Hell from Earth collapse.[24] Leon himself begins exercising more seriously after learning of the possible invasion, attempting to improve his Human body and lose the beer belly he had carried for years.[25]

#### Demonic War
Shortly before his transformation, Leon speaks at length with Mizuki about his Human life. He reveals that he was an orphan whose circumstances forced him to prioritize survival instead of education. From childhood, however, Leon had loved films and dreamed of becoming a movie director. By the time he became financially stable enough to contemplate pursuing that ambition, he was already in a relationship with Anna, she had become pregnant with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and their son's severe medical condition required enormous attention and responsibility.[12]
Leon also admits that Victor's enormous collection of wives and romantic interests makes him envious. Mizuki warns him that becoming a Vampire will amplify rather than erase such desires. Unlike Anna, who is naturally direct about what she wants, Leon has spent most of his life burying his personal wishes. Mizuki therefore predicts that his adjustment to Vampirism may be considerably more dangerous than Anna's.[12]
Victor eventually begins Leon's transformation personally. Unwilling to bite his father's neck because of the intimacy Vampires associate with the act, Victor instead allows Leon to drink Blood containing his Vampiric venom. Victor then uses the Blood already inside Leon to target his Soul, demonstrating that a Progenitor does not require a conventional bite to transform someone. Once the transformation begins, Victor snaps Leon's neck so that his Human death occurs immediately rather than forcing him to endure a slower and more painful death before rebirth.[1]
Leon awakens as a Noble Vampire directly descended from Victor. His body returns to the appearance of his twenties, unnecessary body fat disappears and his eyes become red. The transformation also produces an immediate racial hierarchy: Leon instinctively recognizes Victor as the Progenitor and feels compelled to acknowledge that Victor stands above him within the race he created.[1]
The transition is nevertheless unstable. The desires Leon spent decades suppressing surge to the surface so violently that he loses conscious control shortly after awakening. Victor's initial command is insufficient to calm the frenzy, forcing Kaguya, Roxanne, Maria and Roberta to restrain and subdue him until his mind stabilizes.[1] His status as a direct descendant of a Progenitor makes the problem more severe; his Vampiric instincts are described as unusually strong and proud, naturally recognizing Victor above everyone else.[4]
Leon also awakens an individual power derived from his own inner nature. He becomes capable of projecting an unidentified Energy into transparent defensive barriers. At first, the only construct he can make is a transparent wall that appears extraordinarily difficult to break. Ruby compares the principle to the Energy constructs created through Divine Power, although the exact nature of Leon's Energy remains unknown. Violet theorizes that the ability may reflect Leon's unconscious desire to act as the protective "wall" of his family, but this interpretation is never confirmed as an objective explanation.[5]
His greatest immediate problem remains self-control. Leon eventually allows his amplified desires to affect the way he looks at Victor's wives, causing Anna to nearly beat him to death and leaving Victor deeply disappointed. Victor emphasizes that his parents had been educated about Vampirism in advance and therefore could not rely upon complete ignorance as an excuse for refusing to discipline their instincts.[6]
Victor consequently assigns [[Oda Blank]] as Leon's personal trainer. Oda is an expert in infiltration, assassination, Kenjutsu, Ninjutsu and interrogation, and Victor orders him to force Leon into becoming a useful and disciplined Vampire. Leon is forbidden from freely interacting with other people—including Anna—until he gains sufficient control over his new strength, talents and desires. Anna is simultaneously placed under Hilda Snow, beginning a temporary separation between the couple that would ultimately have much larger consequences for their relationship.[6]

#### King Among Kings
By the time [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] returns from Hell, Leon has spent a significant period under Oda's supervision and is regularly participating in missions with the [[Blank Clan]]. His training has progressed far enough that Oda is willing to involve him in operations connected to Victor's major enemies.[10][7]
Leon reunites with Victor while the family is discussing Zack. He remarks that the cat had always behaved unusually and that the Walker family simply ignored the signs for years. His new youthful appearance makes his resemblance to Victor considerably more obvious, while Leon immediately notices the changes to Victor's eyes following his bond with [[Zaladrac Zeovnur Elderblood]]. When Leon turns his attention toward Zaladrac herself, his instincts identify the Ancient Dragon as overwhelmingly dangerous despite her doing nothing openly threatening.[10]
Leon then informs Victor that Oda and the Demons supplied by [[Vine]] have successfully located a base belonging to [[New Dawn]]. The fact that Oda entrusted Leon with information and operations connected to Victor's enemies is explicitly treated as evidence that the assassin master now considers Leon competent enough for such work.[7]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
The enormous expansion of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s territories gradually creates physical and emotional distance between Leon and Anna. Leon's work requires him to spend much of his time in [[Nightingale]] or the former territories of the [[Egyptian Pantheon]], while Anna increasingly works within Victor's household and administrative structure. Victor's developing personal world also initially offers little reason for Leon to remain there compared with the responsibilities assigned to him elsewhere.[26]
The prolonged separation severely damages Leon and Anna's marriage. Anna spends progressively more time around Victor while Leon remains occupied with missions and eventually begins seeing another Vampire. What was initially intended as temporary separation following their rebirth slowly becomes two increasingly independent lives.[3]
The unresolved Walker family relationship remains significant even after Victor's ascension. When Anna's own developing powers and amplified emotions create issues that cannot be solved through additional supernatural evolution, Victor recognizes that Leon must eventually be involved and that the emotional problems created by their old family life cannot simply be erased by greater power.[27]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Leon returns to prominence after being assigned to locate [[Stella Elderblood]], [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Anna's daughter, after she leaves the family's Inner World. He participates in the search alongside the Heralds and covert operatives of the [[Blank Clan]]. Leon encounters Stella while operating from concealment and is immediately struck by her resemblance to Anna. Although he is no longer surprised by the idea that Anna has become romantically involved with Victor, learning that they already have children reveals how much of Victor's family development occurred while Leon remained occupied elsewhere.[8]
Leon asks Kaguya to arrange a meeting with Victor and Anna. When he finally stands before them, the enormous difference in existence between Leon and the two Dragon Gods causes his instincts to react with intense fear. Victor deliberately suppresses the effect enough for his father to converse normally. Leon recognizes affection toward Anna, but it is now accompanied by emotional distance rather than the relationship they once shared.[8]
Victor explains that approximately two thousand subjective years have passed inside the Inner World while comparatively little time elapsed outside. Leon is stunned but adapts quickly, having long since accepted that his son routinely accomplishes things considered impossible even by Gods. He learns about the development of the [[Blood Dragons]], the accelerated growth of Victor's family and Anna's transformation into a Dragon.[9]
The three also confront Anna and Leon's failed marriage directly. Leon acknowledges that their relationship fundamentally changed after they became Vampires and that both had since pursued other relationships. Anna's increasing attachment to Victor and Leon's relationship with an unnamed Vampire are treated not as a single act that suddenly destroyed the marriage but as consequences of years in which the two gradually followed separate paths.[9][3]
Leon nevertheless remains Victor's father, and his next request is simply to meet his granddaughters. Victor considers the request carefully before agreeing, recognizing that despite technically becoming Leon's Vampiric sire, the Human relationship between them remains meaningful.[28]
The meeting immediately demonstrates how abnormal Victor's children are. Leon realizes that many of the girls have little natural interest in anyone outside their parents and sisters, while their auras alone make the gap between them and ordinary supernatural beings obvious.[29] [[Yuno Elderblood]] casually throws Leon into a wall while attempting to obtain more space beside Victor, and Leon later becomes captivated by [[Irene Elderblood]]'s supernatural charm strongly enough that Anna reinforces the protection around him.[30]
When Victor formally introduces Leon as his mortal-life father and therefore technically their grandfather, Yuno inspects him and immediately rejects the resemblance between Leon and Victor as superficial. Although Leon looks similar to her father, his aura and existence are so weak by comparison that she initially regards him as a false imitation and moves to erase him before Victor intervenes. Leon consequently understands that the girls operate according to instincts and standards radically removed from an ordinary Human family.[11]
Despite the disastrous first meeting, Leon admits that he had once imagined becoming the type of grandfather who spoiled his grandchildren. Anna explains that most of Victor's daughters remain extraordinarily young according to their species and may require thousands of years before forming more conventional relationships with extended family. Leon doubts whether even an immortal Vampire like himself will remain mentally unchanged across such spans, while Anna believes Victor will eventually create methods to help his people adapt to such longevity.[13]
After returning to his office in [[Nightingale]], Leon finally allows the frustration he had concealed during the conversation to surface. He uses his senses and barrier power to isolate the room before violently venting his anger on the furniture. Although he accepts that Anna was free to move on, Leon privately regrets assuming their relationship would simply continue because they had already spent so many years together. By the time he brought his desires under control, he had failed to take meaningful action to rebuild their marriage. He ultimately recognizes that neither he nor Anna can be reduced to the single culprit of their separation; both simply followed different paths after becoming fundamentally different people.[13]

### Powers and Abilities
For a complete breakdown, see Leon Walker's Abilities.
As a Noble Vampire personally created by the Second Vampire Progenitor, Leon possesses the standard enhanced physiology and basic racial techniques of a Vampire in addition to the stronger instincts associated with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s direct lineage.[1][4] His unique awakened ability is the generation of transparent defensive barriers through an unidentified form of Energy. Initially limited to wall-shaped constructs, the power later developed sufficiently for Leon to isolate the space surrounding his office.[5][13] Leon has also undergone extensive training beneath [[Oda Blank]] and operates alongside the [[Blank Clan]] in covert missions, although he has never been explicitly shown possessing the Clan's hereditary Shadow powers.[6][7]

### Trivia
- Leon has dreamed of directing a movie since childhood. His difficult upbringing, financial circumstances, Anna's pregnancy and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s illness prevented him from pursuing the career while Human.[12]
- Even after entering the supernatural world, Leon specifically states that he still wants to spend evenings watching movies with Victor.[12]
- Victor deliberately transformed Leon without biting him. Leon drank Victor's Blood containing Vampiric venom, after which Victor used their Blood connection to transform his body and Soul before snapping his neck to accelerate his death and rebirth.[1]
- Leon and Anna were initially forbidden from seeing one another or freely interacting with other people after their transformations. Victor treated the separation as temporary and intended it to end once both could control their new instincts, but their later responsibilities caused the emotional distance to continue far longer than originally intended.[6][26]
- Leon's unique barrier power is not confirmed to be literally indestructible. When it first manifested, Ruby only described the transparent wall as appearing or seeming unbreakable, while admitting that the Energy forming it had not yet been properly studied.[5]
- Although Leon works closely with Oda and the [[Blank Clan]], the novel has not explicitly shown him possessing the Clan's hereditary Shadow Manipulation. His confirmed connection to the Clan is through training, infiltration and covert operations.[6][7]
- Leon later entered a relationship with an unnamed Vampire while working away from Anna in [[Nightingale]].[3]

### Abilities

Leon Alucard is a Noble Vampire created directly by [[Victor Elderblood]], the Second Vampire Progenitor. His powers are divided between the basic racial abilities granted by his Vampiric physiology, the stronger instincts produced by belonging directly to a Progenitor's lineage, and the personal ability awakened from his Soul following his rebirth.[1][2]

#### Powers and Abilities

##### Noble Vampire Physiology
As a Noble Vampire, Leon possesses the standard passive and active abilities associated with the Vampire race. His body was completely reconstructed during his rebirth, restoring him from a fifty-year-old Human body to a youthful physical prime and replacing his Human limitations with supernatural physiology.[1]
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Leon possesses supernatural strength, speed, reflexes, agility, durability and stamina vastly beyond those of an ordinary Human. Even immediately after rebirth, his new existence generated enough force and unstable Energy that several of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s experienced servants prepared to restrain him when he lost control.[1]
- [[Immortality]] / Agelessness: Leon ceased aging as an ordinary Human after becoming a Noble Vampire. His transformation returned his physical appearance to approximately his twenties despite being fifty at the time of his debut.[1]
- Regeneration: As a Vampire, Leon possesses the species' supernatural regenerative abilities, allowing physical injuries to heal at a rate impossible for Humans.
- Enhanced Senses: Leon's sight, hearing, smell and supernatural perception were greatly enhanced by his transformation. His instincts can detect dangerous beings even without an overt attack; upon seeing [[Zaladrac Zeovnur Elderblood]], Leon immediately perceived her as an overwhelming threat despite her merely sitting in the room.[3]
- Night Vision: As with other Vampires, Leon can perceive his surroundings naturally in darkness.
- Claws: Leon possesses the standard Vampiric ability to transform his fingernails into supernatural claws.
- Fog Walking: As a Vampire, Leon possesses the racial ability to disperse his body into supernatural mist.
- Bat Transformation: Leon possesses the standard Vampiric ability to transform into a swarm of bats.
- Vampiric Charm: Noble Vampires inherently possess the ability to influence weaker beings through supernatural Charm. Leon has not demonstrated the exceptional mutated authority possessed by Victor, and the upper limits of Leon's own Charm have not been established.
- [[Vampiric Energy]]: Leon possesses the supernatural Energy inherent to Vampires. This should not automatically be treated as identical to the unidentified Energy responsible for his unique barriers, as Ruby specifically noted that the properties of his barrier-producing Energy still required study.[4]

##### Direct Progenitor Lineage
Leon was not converted by an ordinary Noble Vampire. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] personally transformed Leon using his own Progenitor Blood, altering both Leon's body and Soul into the same race created from Victor's lineage.[1]
- Enhanced Racial Potential: Direct descendants of a Vampire Progenitor possess exceptional potential compared with ordinary transformed Vampires. Leon's transformation produced a genuine Noble Vampire rather than a conventional Plebeian Vampire.[1]
- Powerful Vampiric Instincts: Leon's instincts are explicitly described as stronger and prouder than those of normal Vampires because he belongs directly to a Progenitor's lineage.[2]
- Progenitor Hierarchy Recognition: Immediately following his rebirth, Leon instinctively recognized Victor as the origin and superior of his new race. Despite biologically being Victor's Human father, his Vampiric instincts perceived Victor as his supernatural "father" and compelled him to acknowledge the Progenitor's superior position.[1]
- Instinctive Submission to Victor: Leon's unusually proud newborn instincts naturally recognized Victor above other beings. [[Aphrodite]] specifically notes that Leon responded to Victor and only Victor while discussing how to discipline him without damaging the Walker family relationship.[2]

##### Barrier Generation
Leon awakened a personal supernatural ability after his Human Soul was rebuilt as that of a Noble Vampire. Unlike hereditary Noble Vampire abilities passed through an established Clan, his power developed from the characteristics and unconscious desires already present within him.[1][4]
- Force-Field Generation: Leon can generate transparent defensive barriers using an unidentified form of Energy. During the earliest examination of the ability, the only shape he could produce was a transparent wall.[4]
- Exceptional Defensive Strength: Ruby described Leon's newly awakened wall as appearing to be effectively unbreakable during their initial observations. This does not establish literal or absolute invulnerability; no attack powerful enough to determine the barrier's true maximum durability was demonstrated in the chapter.[4]
- Defensive Energy Projection: Ruby compared Leon's ability to the Energy constructs produced by [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood through Divine Power. Leon's version was far less versatile at the time because he could only produce the defensive wall rather than freely construct different shapes.[4]
- Area Isolation: Leon's control developed beyond the primitive wall shown immediately after rebirth. After returning to his office in [[Nightingale]], he used his senses together with his barrier power to block off the surrounding space before venting his frustration, demonstrating that the barriers can be used to isolate an enclosed area rather than merely intercept an attack from one direction.[5]
- Barrier Shaping (Developing): The first examination of Leon's ability explicitly notes that a wall was the only shape he could create at that time, leaving the ability open to further development. His later area-isolation feat indicates that his control did in fact improve, although the full variety of shapes he can currently construct has not been demonstrated.[4][5]
- Unknown Energy Properties: The exact Energy used to produce Leon's barriers was not identified when the power awakened. Ruby specifically expressed interest in studying its properties, meaning it should not currently be indexed as [[Divine Energy]], Spatial Energy or another established Energy type without further evidence.[4]
The origin of this ability is strongly implied to reflect Leon's psychology but remains interpretive rather than completely confirmed. Violet theorized that the defensive nature of his power may have emerged from an unconscious desire to be the "wall" protecting his family, fitting Leon's Human life of repeatedly prioritizing his responsibilities toward Anna and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. The novel presents this as Violet's hypothesis rather than an objective explanation of the power's Concept.[4]

#### Skills

##### Blank Clan Training
Following Leon's inability to control his newborn instincts, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] placed him directly under [[Oda Blank]], First Master of the [[Blank Clan]]. Oda is identified as a master specializing in infiltration, assassination, Kenjutsu, Ninjutsu and interrogation, and Victor explicitly ordered him to turn Leon into a disciplined and useful operative.[6]
Leon should therefore be treated as trained in the methods of the Blank Clan, but not automatically as a master of every discipline Oda himself possesses. The novel has never stated that Leon reached Oda's level in Kenjutsu, Ninjutsu, assassination or interrogation.
- Infiltration: Leon regularly participates in covert operations under Oda's supervision. By Chapter 701, Oda trusted him enough to include him in operations concerning [[New Dawn]], an organization whose members had repeatedly evaded Victor's intelligence network.[7]
- Stealth / Presence Concealment: Leon is capable of participating in operations while remaining concealed. During the search for [[Stella Elderblood]], he operated alongside the Heralds and Blank Clan Dragonoid assassins and initially observed Stella while hidden.[8]
- Intelligence Gathering: Leon's work with Oda gives him access to intelligence operations directed against Victor's enemies. He was the person who informed Victor that Oda and the Demons supplied by [[Vine]] had located one of New Dawn's bases.[3][7]
- Tracking: Leon was assigned to the operation intended to locate Stella after she left the Inner World, demonstrating that he can participate in searches involving targets attempting to move outside the direct supervision of Victor's household.[8]
- Combat Training: Oda's program was intended not merely to control Leon's desires but to force him to master his new strength and talents. Leon consequently received combat and operational training appropriate to a Blank Clan operative following his transformation.[6]

##### Practical and Mundane Skills
- Construction Experience: Leon spent many years working as a bricklayer and construction worker. Even after retiring, he continued visiting his former workplace to advise younger workers.[9]
- Street Experience: Growing up as an orphan forced Leon to prioritize survival and practical experience instead of formal education. He later admitted that he became involved with problematic parts of society before settling into family life with Anna.[10]
- Film Knowledge: Leon is an enthusiastic film fan who has dreamed of directing movies since childhood. He regularly analyzes fictional stories in terms of whether they could be adapted into worthwhile films and shares [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s interest in movie marathons.[10]

#### Weaknesses and Limitations
- Amplified Desires: Leon's greatest weakness immediately after becoming a Vampire was the extreme amplification of desires he had suppressed throughout his Human life. His initial rebirth caused him to lose conscious control, and his later behavior toward [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wives resulted in severe disciplinary training.[1][2][6]
- Progenitor Hierarchy: As a direct male descendant of Victor, Leon's Vampiric instincts inherently recognize the Progenitor as the superior existence within his lineage.[1][2]
- Barrier Limits Remain Unknown: Leon's barrier was described as seeming unbreakable during its initial examination, but no absolute durability was established. The ability's Energy type, maximum size, maximum resistance, number of simultaneous constructs and full range of possible shapes remain unknown.[4]
- No Confirmed Shadow Manipulation: Leon's association with the [[Blank Clan]] and training beneath Oda should not be confused with possessing the hereditary Shadow abilities of Clan Blank. He has demonstrated covert training and stealth-related work, but the novel has not shown Leon personally manipulating Shadows.
- Relative Power Gap: Leon is considerably stronger than he was as a Human, but his status as Victor's direct descendant does not place him near the highest members of the Elderblood family. Victor's daughters could overwhelm him physically or existentially with extreme ease, with [[Yuno Elderblood]] casually throwing him aside and later preparing to erase him before Victor intervened.[11][12]
