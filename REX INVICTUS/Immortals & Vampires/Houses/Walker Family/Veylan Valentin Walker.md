Veylan Valentin Walker was a distant Human ancestor of [[Victor Elderblood]] through Victor's paternal Walker bloodline. The original Veylan lived during the eighteenth century, around the period beginning in approximately 1760, and spent his life working to improve the circumstances of his family. At seventy-six years old he emigrated from England to the United States, where he continued living until eventually dying after what Victor described as a long and fulfilling mortal life.[1]
Centuries after his death, information corresponding to an earlier version of Veylan was extracted from the [[Akashic Records]] through a transaction made by [[Shiva]] with a Primordial Being, strongly implied to have been the Judge of the Abyss. Shiva used that information to reconstruct Veylan in Reality and subsequently performed a ritual accepting the artificial Veylan as his own blood, effectively adopting him as his son and transforming the reconstruction into a [[Demigod]].[1]
Shiva's purpose was to exploit the ancestry of Victor Elderblood. After witnessing the extraordinary talent of Victor and his descendants, Shiva theorized that some portion of that potential must have originated from the Walker bloodline. He therefore selected one of Victor's ancestors and attempted to reproduce that hereditary advantage. The experiment ultimately failed because the person created through the Akashic Records was not the original Veylan resurrected, but an extraordinarily accurate record of Veylan given physical reality through Divinity. The reconstruction possessed neither the complete history of the original man nor the accumulated hereditary development that eventually produced Victor.[1]
Victor eventually released the reconstructed Veylan rather than allowing an artificial imitation of his ancestor to continue existing for Shiva's purposes. As the Divine power sustaining him disappeared, Veylan's confused demeanor cleared and he briefly displayed the maturity expected from the man he had once been patterned after. He thanked Victor before dissolving into white light, while Victor instead thanked Veylan, acknowledging that without the generations preceding him, Victor himself would never have existed.[1]

### Physical Appearance
The recreated Veylan initially appeared as a young boy or young man and possessed an extremely strong resemblance to [[Victor Elderblood]]. The similarity was immediately noticed by [[Kassandra Kashvi Elderblood]], who reacted with anger because the unknown youth looked enough like her father for his appearance to feel insulting.[2]
The resemblance was not coincidental. [[Shiva]] had deliberately selected Veylan from Victor's paternal ancestry because he wanted an individual belonging to the same Human bloodline that eventually produced Victor. Although separated from Victor by many generations, Veylan's reconstructed features retained enough similarity for the family connection to be visually apparent.[1]
When Victor removed the Divine force maintaining Veylan's artificial condition, his appearance and expression gradually shifted. His previously confused and youthful demeanor became clearer and more mature, giving the impression of a younger version of a man who nevertheless carried the experience of a much longer life. This change accompanied the final moments before the reconstruction dissolved completely.[1]
No confirmed description establishes Veylan's exact hair color, eye color, height, clothing or other detailed physical characteristics. Any artwork depicting those features should therefore be treated as an artistic interpretation rather than canonical design.

### Personality
The original Veylan is described principally through the life he lived rather than direct dialogue. He was a hardworking and family-oriented man who spent much of his life attempting to provide better circumstances for those who came after him. Even at an advanced Human age, he was willing to completely change his circumstances by emigrating to the United States at seventy-six, indicating considerable determination and resilience.[1]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] considered his ancestor's life successful precisely because Veylan had not required supernatural power, immortality or cosmic importance to give it value. He worked, supported his family, migrated in pursuit of a better future and eventually died naturally after a long mortal existence. Victor therefore regarded [[Shiva]]'s attempt to recreate him less as a genuine resurrection and more as an unnecessary disturbance of someone who had already completed his life.[1]
The reconstructed Veylan initially appeared confused because he did not possess the complete lifetime of memories belonging to the original. The Akashic information used to recreate him had apparently been taken from an earlier period of Veylan's existence, creating a mismatch between the young reconstruction and the full historical individual Victor observed through his own perception.[1]
During his final moments, however, Veylan's expression became noticeably calmer and more mature as Shiva's Divinity stopped sustaining him. He thanked Victor before disappearing, suggesting that enough of the original man's identity remained within the reconstruction for him to understand what Victor had done for him.[1]

### Synopsis

#### Those Who Rule Reality
During [[Kali Elderblood]] and [[Kassandra Kashvi Elderblood]]'s visit to the [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]], Kassandra detected an unknown boy whom [[Shiva]] had been keeping hidden. The youth resembled [[Victor Elderblood]] strongly enough that Kassandra immediately became hostile, interpreting the imitation of her father's appearance as an insult. Shiva eventually explained that the resemblance existed because the boy was not designed to look like Victor at all: he was one of Victor's actual Human ancestors from the paternal Walker bloodline.[2][1]
Shiva had become increasingly fascinated—and envious—of the extraordinary natural potential surrounding Victor and the Elderblood family. Victor himself had risen from a sickly Human into one of the greatest existences in the Sector, while his daughters routinely inherited absurd levels of talent and potential. Shiva concluded that at least part of this abnormality might be hereditary and began investigating Victor's Human ancestry in the hope of finding another member of the same bloodline who possessed a fraction of Victor's potential.[1]
The problem was that Victor's relevant ancestors had been dead for centuries. To bypass this limitation, Shiva made a transaction with a Primordial Being capable of providing access to information contained within the [[Akashic Records]]. Kali immediately suspected the Judge of the Abyss, reasoning that Souls were among the few meaningful resources Shiva could offer a Primordial in exchange for such assistance. Shiva sacrificed numerous Souls through the arrangement and obtained enough information to reconstruct a deceased Walker ancestor.[1]
The selected individual was Veylan Valentin Walker. When Victor personally arrived and examined him, he identified Veylan as one of his distant paternal ancestors who had lived around the eighteenth century. The original Veylan had been an ordinary but hardworking Human who dedicated himself to improving life for his family. At the age of seventy-six, he emigrated to the United States and eventually died there after completing what Victor regarded as a long and fulfilling Human life.[1]
Shiva had not simply reconstructed Veylan and left him Human. Believing that the Walker bloodline contained extraordinary hereditary potential, he performed a ritual that accepted Veylan as his own blood and adopted him as his son. Through this connection, Shiva infused the reconstruction with Divine power and transformed him into a [[Demigod]], intending to cultivate him into a powerful warrior.[1]
Victor immediately recognized that Shiva's central assumption was flawed. By examining Veylan's Past, Present and Future, Victor determined that the youth standing before them was not the deceased Veylan returned from death. The actual man's continuous existence had ended centuries earlier. What Shiva possessed was information copied from the Akashic Records and then given tangible existence through Divine power.[1]
Victor described the recreated Veylan as essentially a data record brought into Reality. The Akashic Records contained the information describing the historical Veylan, and Shiva had taken that information and produced a physical being from it. The result could think, speak and resemble the original closely enough to appear genuine, but there was no continuity between the original dead Human and the newly created entity.[1]
The incomplete memories reinforced Victor's conclusion. The recreation appeared to have been based upon a version of Veylan recorded before the end of his natural life, which explained why he lacked knowledge of many events Victor could perceive within his ancestor's complete history. Shiva had effectively reconstructed a snapshot of the man rather than retrieving the person who had actually experienced his entire lifetime.[1]
This distinction also destroyed Shiva's attempt to reproduce Victor's hereditary potential. The original Walker lineage had developed across many generations. Victor concluded that qualities within his Human ancestry accumulated slowly over time, eventually culminating in his exceptionally rare RH-null blood, later known as the Golden Blood. Predatory characteristics that became dramatically amplified after his transformations into a Vampire and later a Dragon also appeared to have existed in latent forms throughout the Walker lineage.[1]
Veylan alone therefore did not possess the complete accumulation that eventually produced Victor. More importantly, the Akashic reconstruction lacked even the original Veylan's genuine biological continuity. Shiva had recreated information about an ancestor, not restored the bloodline itself. The artificial Demigod could consequently never provide what Shiva had actually wanted from the experiment.[1]
Victor was angered by the entire project. Beyond Shiva's unauthorized dealings with a Primordial and the sacrifice of Souls involved in obtaining the information, he considered the reconstruction of his ancestor an unacceptable intrusion into his family's history. Shiva's actions contributed to Victor removing him from the highest authority of the Hindu Pantheon, elevating Kali Elderblood above him and sentencing Shiva to fight directly on the most dangerous front lines during the future Upper-Sector conflict.[1]
Victor nevertheless directed none of that anger toward Veylan himself. He understood that the reconstruction had not chosen to exist and refused to preserve him merely as the product of Shiva's failed experiment. Victor gently placed his hand upon Veylan's head and told his ancestor to rest, explaining that the original man had already experienced a long and fulfilling life and deserved to remain at peace.[1]
Victor then removed the Divinity maintaining Veylan's artificial existence. The reconstruction began dissolving into white light while his previously bewildered expression gradually became clearer and more mature. For a brief moment he appeared less like a confused recreated youth and more like the man represented by the complete life Victor had witnessed.[1]
Veylan thanked Victor. Victor rejected the implication that gratitude belonged only to him and instead thanked his ancestor, acknowledging the simple truth underlying the entire Walker lineage: without Veylan and the generations that followed him, Victor would never have existed. Veylan then disappeared completely, bringing Shiva's attempt to reproduce the Walker bloodline to an end.[1]
The incident became unexpectedly valuable for Victor despite his anger. Investigating Veylan allowed him to understand his Human ancestry more clearly and reinforced the theory that Victor's exceptional traits had not appeared spontaneously when he became a Vampire. The Walker bloodline had been accumulating unusual potential over generations, with Victor representing the eventual convergence of those hereditary traits, RH-null blood, his own mentality and the supernatural transformations that followed.[1]

### Skills & Abilities
The distinction between the original Veylan and [[Shiva]]'s reconstructed Veylan is essential when indexing his abilities. The historical Veylan was an ordinary Human ancestor of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], while the being encountered in the [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]] was an artificial reconstruction empowered by Shiva and transformed into a [[Demigod]].[1]

#### Original Veylan
- Human Physiology: The original Veylan was an ordinary Human. No evidence establishes him as possessing supernatural abilities during his historical lifetime.[1]
- [[Walker Bloodline]]: Veylan belonged to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s paternal Human lineage. The Walker bloodline gradually accumulated unusual hereditary potential across generations, eventually contributing to Victor's RH-null or Golden Blood.[1]
- Latent Hereditary Potential: Veylan existed within the ancestral chain that eventually produced Victor, but the novel does not state that Veylan personally possessed Victor-level potential.
- Latent Predatory Traits: Victor concluded that several tendencies later magnified by his Vampirism and Dragon nature had antecedents within his Human ancestry. This was a property developing through the Walker lineage rather than a demonstrated supernatural ability personally used by Veylan.[1]
- Human Longevity: Veylan remained capable of emigrating at seventy-six years old and lived beyond that age before eventually dying naturally.[1]
- Strong Work Ethic: He spent his life working toward better conditions for his family and continued pursuing a new future even at an advanced age.[1]

#### Akashic Reconstruction
- Artificial Lifeform: [[Shiva]]'s Veylan was information retrieved from the [[Akashic Records]] and given tangible existence through Divine power rather than the continuous resurrection of the deceased Human.[1]
- Akashic Record Reconstruction: His personality, appearance and memories were constructed from historical information preserved within the Akashic Records.[1]
- Physical Manifestation: Shiva's Divinity transformed information that would ordinarily exist only as a cosmic record into a physically present living reconstruction.[1]
- Partial Memory Replication: The recreated Veylan possessed only the information belonging to the period from which his record had apparently been reconstructed. He therefore lacked portions of the original Veylan's later life.[1]
- Artificial Consciousness: Despite lacking continuity with the deceased Veylan, the reconstruction possessed enough independent awareness to interact with others and ultimately thank [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] before disappearing.[1]
- [[Demigod]] Physiology: After reconstructing Veylan, Shiva performed a ritual that accepted him into his own blood and transformed him into a Demigod.[1]
- Divine Empowerment: Shiva's Divine power elevated the recreated Human beyond the ordinary mortal condition.
- Enhanced Physical Attributes: As a Demigod, the reconstruction possessed a supernatural body superior to that of an ordinary Human. No specific strength, speed or durability feat is demonstrated before his dissolution.
- [[Divine Energy]]: His Demigod transformation provided him with Divine-derived supernatural power, although no specialized application was shown.
- Divinity-Sustained Existence: The reconstructed Veylan's continued physical existence depended upon the Divine power maintaining the Akashic information in Reality.[1]
- Dissolution: Once Victor released the power sustaining the reconstruction, Veylan's manifested body gradually dissolved into white light and ceased to exist.[1]

#### Limitations
- Not True Resurrection: Veylan's greatest limitation was existential rather than combative. The reconstructed entity was not the original Veylan returned from death and did not possess continuity with the deceased Human.[1]
- Incomplete Historical Record: The information used for the reconstruction corresponded to an earlier state of Veylan, leaving him without many memories from the original man's complete lifetime.[1]
- No Genuine Walker Lineage Accumulation: Recreating Veylan's information did not recreate the generations of biological development that culminated in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Golden Blood and exceptional potential.[1]
- Dependence on [[Shiva]]'s Divinity: The artificial body required Divine power to remain manifested. Removing that support caused the reconstruction to disappear.[1]
- Unproven Combat Ability: Although Shiva transformed the reconstruction into a [[Demigod]], Veylan never fought and displayed no named techniques, Divine Concepts or combat feats. Any extensive combat abilities beyond basic Demigod traits remain unknown.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] was Veylan's distant descendant through the paternal Walker bloodline. The original Veylan died centuries before Victor's birth and therefore never personally knew him, but his life formed one link in the generations that eventually produced Victor.[1]
Victor's reaction to meeting the reconstruction was notably respectful. He did not regard Veylan as a useful experiment, a potential soldier or a resource from which to extract hereditary information. Instead, he viewed the original man as an ancestor who had already lived and completed his life.
After determining that the recreated Veylan was merely Akashic information given form, Victor chose to allow him to disappear rather than preserve an imitation indefinitely. Veylan thanked him for releasing him, but Victor instead expressed gratitude toward his ancestor because Veylan's descendants ultimately led to Victor's own birth.[1]

#### Shiva
[[Shiva]] was the creator and adoptive father of the reconstructed Veylan. He selected Veylan specifically because of his relationship to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and hoped that an ancestor belonging to the same bloodline would inherit enough of Victor's extraordinary potential to become an exceptional warrior.[1]
After obtaining Veylan's information from the [[Akashic Records]], Shiva brought the record into Reality and performed a ritual accepting Veylan as his own blood, turning him into a [[Demigod]] son. The relationship was therefore artificial from its beginning: Shiva was not raising Veylan because of familial affection but because he regarded the Walker ancestor as a potential source of power.[1]
Victor strongly disapproved of the experiment and terminated the reconstruction after discovering its nature.

#### Kassandra Kashvi Elderblood
[[Kassandra Kashvi Elderblood]] was one of the first people to detect the recreated Veylan. His resemblance to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] immediately angered her because she initially interpreted the unknown youth's appearance as an imitation of her father.[2]
Once [[Shiva]] explained that Veylan was actually Victor's ancestor, the resemblance became understandable. Her reaction nevertheless contributed to exposing the experiment that Shiva had attempted to keep concealed.

#### Kali Elderblood
[[Kali Elderblood]] quickly recognized that [[Shiva]] could not have accessed the information required to recreate a long-deceased Human ancestor through ordinary Divine methods. She deduced that he had made a transaction with a Primordial and identified the Judge of the Abyss as the most likely participant because Shiva possessed Souls valuable enough to exchange for such assistance.[1]
The Veylan incident became one of the decisive demonstrations of Shiva's poor judgment and contributed to Kali being placed above him as the primary ruler of the [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]].[1]
