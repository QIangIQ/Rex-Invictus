Laura Bloodhunter is one of the seven Original Emperor's Soldiers personally created by [[Victor Elderblood]] as the first generation of elite Imperial Commanders of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Like her six siblings, she has a Dragonoid foundation and traits taken from several races. She is the exception in the Angelic part. Victor used the Essence of [[Lucifer]] Morningstar, an Archangel, for her, and ordinary Angel Essence for the other six.[1]
The result is the small white wings on her head, where the others have horns, and the kindest disposition of the seven. Victor saw both as soon as she came out.[1] Her way of helping people and explaining a thing properly is why her blood became the base of the Operators.[2] By the time the Empire was moving on several worlds at once, she was the one passing the Emperor's orders to the commanders, and she and Kaleb were the minds of the seven.[3][4]

### Physical Appearance
Laura is a tall, slender young woman with long golden-blonde hair and piercing green eyes. Although only ten years old when she emerged from her creation chamber, like the other Original Emperor's Soldiers she already possessed a completely mature supernatural body considerably larger than that of an ordinary Human child.[1]
Her most distinctive feature is the pair of small white wings growing from the sides of her head. Unlike several of her siblings, Laura does not possess conventional Draconic horns in her normal appearance. These unusual Angelic characteristics resulted from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s decision to use [[Lucifer]] Morningstar's Archangel Essence during her creation rather than the ordinary Angelic Essence used for the other Originals.[1]
Laura possesses a comparatively slender build similar to [[Clare Bloodhunter]], contrasting strongly with the more curvaceous physique of [[Kiana Bloodhunter]]. Despite her primarily humanoid appearance, Laura remains a Dragonoid-based hybrid and possesses the supernatural physical structure of an Emperor's Soldier beneath her outward form.[1]
Like her siblings, Laura wears the standard black Imperial uniform bearing dark-violet and red details and the Dragon insignia of Victor's Empire. During combat she uses the advanced adaptive armor issued to the Emperor's Soldiers.[5]

### Personality
Laura is the kindest of the seven. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s own reading, when he looked at her, was that her disposition was the kindest among them, and that using the Essence of an Archangel in her creation must have caused it. The other six had ordinary Angel Essence. She had [[Lucifer]]'s.[1]
The kindness does not take her out of the war. In the first test she did not charge the middle of it, and she did not go looking for the man who called himself Emperor. She tied her hair and went to the Technological Empire.[5] Later, when a virus was the proposed answer to a hive, she was the one who said the enemy would make a generation that resisted it, that a simple virus was not enough, and that a mutating virus was too risky.[4]
The same restraint is how she gives an order. A World Tree had to be secured, so the planet could not be destroyed too badly, and orbital cannons were out of the question. A technological civilization was not to be opened on until the Emperor said so.[3]
Her loyalty is not softer for any of that. She knew Victor on sight and knelt with the others.[1]

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Laura was created as part of [[Victor Elderblood]]'s effort to build an Imperial military belonging directly to the Emperor rather than relying exclusively upon armies controlled by his wives and other factions. Victor wanted soldiers capable of growing alongside his civilization rather than disposable clones whose only advantage was numerical superiority.[6]
The Emperor's Soldier project used a Dragonoid as its primary biological foundation, for strength, durability, and compatibility with him. Human characteristics contributed adaptability and ingenuity, Angels contributed obedience and benevolent qualities, Demons supplied aggression and cruelty, Faerie Essence contributed a connection with Nature, and Vampiric characteristics supplied regeneration and metamorphosis. He Blessed the race with powers associated with combat and put an extremely small fragment of his Soul into the Original templates. His Essence is what holds the contradictory pieces as one species that still reads, from the outside, as Dragonoid.[6]
Laura was created alongside [[Clare Bloodhunter]], [[Kiana Bloodhunter]], John, Conan, [[Kaleb Hunter]] and [[Caelus Hunter]]. The seven were the templates later soldiers would come from, and Victor put safeguards on that genetic material so it could not be copied without leave.[6][7]
She is the one place he broke the balance on purpose. For the other six he used Essences he treated as equivalent, with the Dragonoid as the base. At the end he wanted something unique that was itself equivalent to a Dragonoid. That is why Laura was made with [[Lucifer]]'s Essence. It was a momentary curiosity, and an Archangel was on that scale.[1]
She came out after about ten subjective years with long golden hair, green eyes, and small white wings on her head instead of horns. From what he could see, her disposition was the kindest among the seven, and he put that on the Archangel Essence.[1]
Like the others, she had been given the memories of warriors and heroes, as lessons, not as a finished life. Victor's warning when they woke was that those memories did not make them heroes. The four teachers were Hassan-i Sabbah for the taking of a life, [[Julius Caesar]] for politics, [[Abe-no-Seimei]] for philosophy, and [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] for the fight.[1][7]
[[Scathach]]'s first look was unfair, because she had been comparing people to Victor and his daughters. Set against the heroes and Gods she had trained, the seven were in that company. They were not those daughters.[7]
Victor then named the seven, Laura among them, as the first Imperial Commanders, under Scathach, and as the foundations of seven future forces.[7]
The first test was the family's Nightmare Tower. Two Empires were already at war, one technological and one built on physical power, and interdimensional monsters had come in on both. The order was to pacify both sides and eliminate the invaders. The equipment was the armor, a plasma firearm, and a sword from the deepest metal of Hell. Scathach wanted the instinct, not a lesson they had already been given in memory. There are no rules in war. Only those who win are considered just.[5]
Laura's answer was not Conan's charge and not Kaleb's walk toward the strongest ruler. She tied her hair and went to the Technological Empire.[5]
While Scathach trained them, the time difference let Velnorah read years of their memories as they were made. She understood why he had chosen Laura. The approach in those memories was helping others and explaining a thing correctly. Her blood would give rise to a new batch of clone Operators. Kindness was what an Operator needed, and too much of it would get in the way, because most of the clones would die, even if that death was not forever. Velnorah's conclusion was that he had been thinking about the mental health of the clones: Operators who care about the soldiers and are not destroyed by the deaths. She thought of asking for the blood of her own people as well, a technological people. Victor's answer to adding more was that Elves and Faeries were a good idea, and then the limit: there was no need, Laura's blood would be enough, and they did not want war-addicted Operators.[2]
When the forces were actually moving, the Operators were connected to all of them, and they were her direct subordinates. The orders came through her. She passed them to the commanders: the forces were to join Commander Velnorah on [[Jupiter]]. She told John not to open the conflict with the technological civilization yet, and to wait for the Emperor. She asked Clare for the time to complete subjugation once Operation Delta had begun. Where a World Tree was the priority, she took orbital cannons off the table, because the planet could not be destroyed too much. Where the Emperor had already given the order, she told the commander to go and conquer the planet. She calls the Operators sisters.[3]
On Clare's planet the order was to take it in two days. Clare's plan was to hit the reproductive capacity, leave the hive mind unconscious, and deal with the other hostiles. Kaleb said he could make a virus for that if he had samples from the hive, and he started the simulation. Laura asked if there was a plan, and then she warned them. Do not underestimate the enemy's adaptability. As soon as any pathogen affects them, they will create a new generation that is resistant. A simple virus is not enough. A mutating virus is too risky. She said she would monitor the mission, said "May the Emperor protect you," and cut the line.[4]
The narration of that campaign is the one that places the seven. Clare and John had become the leaders. Conan, Kiana, and Caelus were the destructive line. Kaleb and Laura were the minds. The same line says the two of them could even be future gods of tactical warfare, a position that was [[Athena]]'s and is vacant.[4]

### Skills & Abilities
Emperor's Soldier Physiology: Laura is one of the seven Original Emperor's Soldiers, an artificial hybrid race personally created by [[Victor Elderblood]]. Her species uses a Dragonoid body as its primary foundation while incorporating beneficial characteristics from Humans, Angels, Demons, Faeries and Vampires. Victor's Essence, Blessings and a fragment of his Soul stabilize these normally incompatible components into a single supernatural existence.[6]
- Dragonoid Physiology: A powerful, durable and highly adaptable supernatural body, naturally compatible with Victor's Draconic Essence.[6]
- Hybrid Racial Characteristics: Human adaptability and ingenuity, Demonic aggression for war, Faerie Essence for spiritual quality and affinity with Nature, Vampiric regeneration and metamorphosis, and an Angelic component taken from an Archangel rather than an ordinary Angel.[6][1]
- Victor's Soul Fragment: A minute fragment of Victor's Soul in the Original design. It stabilizes the hybrid species and develops alongside the soldier.[6]
- Combat Blessings: Blessings associated with combat, laid on the Souls during creation.[6]
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Strength, speed, durability, stamina, agility and reflexes of an elite Dragonoid soldier built by Victor. At ten, a mistake from one of the seven was already something he treated as capable of erasing a city.[6][1][5]
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Vampire regeneration, kept on top of the Dragonoid durability.[6]
- Metamorphosis: She can alter the size and proportions of her body. The natural frame is the enormous Dragonoid design, about four meters, and the change exists so that frame is not a problem for infiltration or for dealing with ordinary people.[6]
- Enhanced Senses: The sensory reach of the composite races used to build the Emperor's Soldiers.[6]
Archangel Essence: Victor used the Essence of [[Lucifer]] Morningstar when he made her. An Archangel's Essence was on the scale of the Dragonoid foundation, which ordinary Angel Essence was not, and the result is a mutation that belongs to her alone.[1]
- Angelic Characteristics: Small white wings on the sides of her head, in place of Draconic horns.[1]
- Kindest Disposition: Victor's own judgment. Of the seven, her disposition is the kindest, and he attributes it to the Archangel Essence.[1]
- Operator Template: Her blood is the base of the Operator line. The trait taken from her is the habit of helping and of explaining a thing correctly, which produces Operators who care about the soldiers without being broken when clones die. Victor set the mix there: Elves and Faeries could be added, and her blood was already enough. He did not want war-addicted Operators.[2]
Tactical Intelligence: She and [[Kaleb Hunter]] are the minds of the seven. The narration puts that tactical ability at the level of a future God of Tactical Warfare, the place that belonged to [[Athena]] and is vacant.[4]
- Reading a Side: In the first war she tied her hair and went to the Technological Empire, taking the civilization itself as the thing to approach.[5]
- Adaptability: Against the hive she warned them not to underestimate how fast the enemy changes. The moment a pathogen takes, they make a generation that resists it.[4]
- Pathogen Judgment: A simple virus is not enough. A mutating virus is too risky. She is the one who puts that limit on the weapon.[4]
- Oversight: She stays on the mission as the one monitoring its progress, gives the blessing of the Emperor, and cuts the line when the order is in their hands.[4]
Operator Command: The Operators are connected to all the forces of the Empire, and they are her direct subordinates. She is the point the orders pass through, from [[Akasha Elderblood]] and the Emperor out to the commanders.[2][3]
- Distribution: She passes the order to the commanders. One of them sent the forces to join Commander Velnorah on [[Jupiter]].[3]
- Holding an Opening: She told John not to start the fight with the technological civilization, and to wait for the Emperor.[3]
- Timing: She asked Clare for the time to finish subjugation once Operation Delta had begun.[3]
- Restraint of Force: Where the priority is a World Tree, the planet is not to be ruined, and orbital cannons are refused.[3]
- Conquest Order: Where the Emperor has personally ordered a system taken, she sends the commander to conquer the planet.[3]
- The Operators as Sisters: They began as her blood. She addresses them as sisters, not as spare copies of herself.[3]
Inherited Knowledge and Training: The memories of heroes and warriors, covering war, tactics and strategy, were in her before she had a battlefield of her own. The teachers then made that into a commander's education.[1][7]
- Assassination: Hassan-i Sabbah, on the ways of taking a life.[7]
- Politics: [[Julius Caesar]], on rule.[7]
- Philosophy: [[Abe-no-Seimei]], on judgment.[7]
- Combat: [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], built on how Laura actually entered the first war.[7][5]
Imperial Armor: The adaptive suit issued to the Originals.[5]
- Akasha Subsystem: Combat support, body analysis, vital monitoring and equipment management, derived from Akasha Elderblood.[5]
- H.U.D.: The helmet shows the field, her condition, and the state of the equipment.[5]
- Adaptive Gear: The armor and its systems change with the role.[5]
Weapons:
- Plasma Firearm: Issued for the first test. The batteries recharge through sunlight or other Energy.[5]
- Miasma Sword: Forged from metals taken from the deepest regions of Hell, where the Miasma was dense and toxic. The wound poisons.[5]
