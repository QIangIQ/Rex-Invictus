Clare Bloodhunter is one of the seven Original Emperor's Soldiers personally created by [[Victor Elderblood]], and one of the two [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] named as natural leaders. The other is John. They are two different types of leader.[1][2]
In the first war her armor went to Conqueror Mode, her own Dragon wings came out, and a breath of violet fire left her mouth.[2] On the hive world she is the commander. She shortens the conquest to two days, has Kaleb build the virus, holds the hostiles with Conan, kills four of the five with a beam of Fire, injects the core herself, and then orders the orbital cannons to bathe the planet.[3][4]

### Physical Appearance
Clare has long black hair, Draconic blue eyes, and black horns. She came out of the chamber at ten with a mature warrior's body.[1]
The Emperor's Soldier design stands about four meters, and Vampiric metamorphosis was kept so that size is not permanent. A permanent giant cannot infiltrate or move among smaller people.[5]
Her wings are hers. In the first fight they appear, and the armor flows over them like a liquid. Conqueror Mode makes the suit flashier, bulkier, and grandiose, built to draw attention and to inspire fear and respect. Unlike John's weapons, her sword and her pistol do not change. When she is close enough for the breath, the helmet distorts and sharp teeth show, and the violet fire comes out of her mouth.[2]

### Personality
She knew [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] on sight. The word she gave him, with the others, was "Emperor."[1]
[[Scathach]]'s judgment after the first war is that she and John are both natural leaders, and that they are two different types. John looked at the tools and then broke his own plan to save a child. Clare jumped.[2]
The later command is the same temperament with a tighter hand. She tells Conan to stop playing, that he is a soldier on a mission, and that failing her is failing the Empire. A valid tactic is still the wrong one if he lets the enemy grow stronger for his own amusement. The mission takes priority. He is not to disappoint the Emperor.[4]
The memories of heroes are lessons. They are not a replacement for her.[1]

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Clare was created when [[Victor Elderblood]] decided the Empire could not depend only on the private armies of his wives. The race is a Dragonoid foundation with Human adaptability, Angelic obedience, Demonic aggression, a Faerie connection to Nature, and Vampire regeneration and metamorphosis, held together by his Essence, his combat Blessings, and a minute fragment of his Soul. She is one of the seven Originals, with John, [[Laura Bloodhunter]], [[Kiana Bloodhunter]], Conan, [[Kaleb Hunter]] and [[Caelus Hunter]]. They are the first Imperial Commanders, and the templates later soldiers come from.[5][6]
She spent about ten subjective years in the chamber with the memories of warriors and heroes, as lessons. When she came out she knelt. Victor's warning was that those memories did not make them heroes. The teachers were Hassan-i Sabbah for the taking of a life, [[Julius Caesar]] for politics, [[Abe-no-Seimei]] for philosophy, and [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] for the fight. Set against the heroes and Gods of the past, rather than against his daughters, the seven sat in that company. He called them his creation, his children, his spear and his shield.[1][6]
The first test was the family's Nightmare Tower. Two Empires were already at war, and interdimensional monsters had come in on both. The order was to pacify both sides and eliminate the invaders, with the armor, a plasma firearm, and a sword from the deepest metal of Hell. There are no rules in war. Only those who win are considered just.[2]
Another flash of light, and the armor became something flashier, bulkier, and grandiose, made to draw attention and to inspire fear and respect. Her sword and her pistol stayed as they were. Akasha-001 said, "Conqueror Mode Activated… Conquer Well, Operator. May The Emperor's grace accompany you in future battles." Clare jumped, sword in the right hand, pistol in the left. Her Draconic wings appeared, and the armor covered them like a liquid. She flew at a gigantic monster, and the pistol opened holes in its flesh. Close in, the helmet distorted, sharp teeth showed, and a gigantic breath of violet fire erupted out of her mouth.[2]
[[Scathach]] laughed. She had more or less known what Clare and John were. Both natural leaders. Two different types.[2]
By the hive campaign that type is the one who gives the order. Laura, passing the Emperor's orders, asked Commander Clare for the time to complete subjugation once Operation Delta had begun. On the planet itself Clare closed her eyes, and the determination was there when she opened them. The Emperor had issued the command, and his orders were absolute. She shortened the conquest to two days. The plan was to work smartly, hit the reproductive capacity, leave the hive mind unconscious, and deal with the other hostiles. Kaleb said he could make a virus if he had samples, and he started the simulation. Laura's warning was that a simple virus was not enough and a mutating one was too risky. Clare and John are the natural leaders. Conan, Caelus, and Kiana are the berserkers. Kaleb and Laura are the minds. Beside John, Conan, and Clare, the other two can look ordinary. Outside that group they are not.[7][3]
She told Kiana and Caelus they were responsible for every hostile on the planet once the first objective was done, and to do what they do best. She told them not to forget their subordinates. They would not be fighting alone. She told Conan and Kaleb to get ready for battle. She would collect the samples. Kaleb would prepare his equipment. Conan would see that no one approached him.[3]
Akasha cut the line once the samples were in. Clare put the sample in Kaleb's hands. "Kaleb, prepare the virus." She drew the sword, pointed it up, and Dragon Fire lit the whole area. She spun the blade and killed hundreds. A Dragon's Roar went out, and a beam of concentrated Fire covered four of the five hostiles and killed them at once. She punched Conan in the stomach hard enough that the pain twisted in him, and she told him to stop playing. He was a soldier on a mission. Failing her was failing the Empire. She would turn him over to his superiors to be fixed. A tactic that let the enemy grow stronger for his amusement was not acceptable. The mission took priority. He was not to disappoint the Emperor.[4]
The scanners in her helmet and in Conan's went off. They ignored them and looked at the hive. The alert named massive life signs, an abundance of [[Natural Energy]], a High-Level Combat God, and five hostiles. Kaleb finished the vial. She asked what she should do with it. He told her to inject it into the core. She, Conan, and Kaleb flew in with their bodies covered in Dragon Fire. He and Conan were there to keep her alive. She injected it. The hive screamed, and what remained of its effort turned to defending against the virus. She asked how long it would last. Twenty-four terrestrial hours on the first dose, less with each dose after, and his ceiling was seven terrestrial days before the hive was immune to that virus and its variants. Her answer was that the time was enough. Then she gave the last order. "Orbital cannons... Bathe this planet in Imperial fire."[4]

### Skills & Abilities
The headers follow Superpower Wiki terminology. Each entry is what she is built as, or what she does with it.

#### Hybrid Physiology
- Dragonoid Foundation: The durable frame, and the compatibility with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. The black horns, the Draconic blue eyes, and the wings are that frame.[5][1][2]
- Racial Composite: Human adaptability and ingenuity, Angelic obedience, Demonic aggression, a Faerie connection to Nature, and Vampire regeneration and metamorphosis, fused by his Essence.[5]
- Soul Fragment: A minute portion of Victor's Soul, meant to grow with her and to keep the racial pieces together.[5]
- Combat Blessing: Blessings from his combat-related authorities.[5]
- The Standard: Inside the seven, she sits with John and Conan. Laura and Kaleb can look ordinary beside those three, and they match them in battle intelligence.[3]

#### Supernatural Condition
- Supernatural Strength: At ten she is already a finished soldier. A mistake from one of the seven is something [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] treats as able to erase a city. On the hive she punches Conan hard enough that the pain twists in his stomach.[1][4]
- Supernatural Agility: The design stands about four meters and is still as agile as an Elf. She jumps from the ground into a fight in the air.[5][2]
- Supernatural Durability: The Dragonoid frame is the one built to carry the modifications and then the war.[5]

#### Regenerative Healing Factor
- Vampiric Regeneration: Kept in the design on top of the Dragonoid durability.[5]

#### Shapeshifting
- Size Shifting: She can leave the four-meter frame. The change is there so that size does not make infiltration impossible.[5]

#### Wing Manifestation
- Draconic Wings: They are hers. They appear when she takes the air, and Conqueror Mode flows over them like a liquid and covers them.[2]

#### Flight
- Aerial Combat: She leaves the ground, flies at a gigantic monster, and opens holes in it with the pistol on the way in. On the hive she flies into the core with Conan and Kaleb, her body covered in Dragon Fire.[2][4]

#### Fire Breath
- Violet Breath: Close to the monster, the helmet distorts and sharp teeth show. A gigantic breath of violet fire erupts out of her mouth.[2]
- Dragon Fire: She points the sword up and the area is lit by Dragon Fire. She spins the blade through hundreds. A Dragon's Roar goes out, and a beam of concentrated Fire covers four of the five hostiles and kills them.[4]

#### Berserker Mode
She is not one of the three berserkers. Conan, Caelus, and Kiana are. She is the one who points them and then fights in front of them anyway.[3][4]

#### Leadership
- Natural Leader: [[Scathach]]'s word is that she and John are both natural leaders, of two different types.[2]
- The Two Days: The Emperor's order is absolute. She shortens the conquest of the hive world to two days, hits the reproductive capacity, and leaves the hive mind unconscious while the other hostiles are dealt with.[3]
- The Split: Kiana and Caelus clear the planet once the first objective is done. Conan keeps anyone off Kaleb. She collects the samples, carries the virus in, and injects the core.[3][4]
- The Hand on Conan: She stops him treating the fight as play. Failing her is failing the Empire. The mission takes priority over his amusement, and he is not to disappoint the Emperor.[4]
- Orbital Fire: When the virus has bought the time, she orders the cannons to bathe the planet in Imperial fire.[4]

#### Enhanced Intelligence
- Inherited Memory: Ten years of other warriors' wars, tactics, and command, kept as lessons.[1]
- God-Class Talent: Judged against the heroes and Gods [[Scathach]] had trained, the seven sit in that company.[6]
- Assassination: Hassan-i Sabbah.[6]
- Politics: [[Julius Caesar]].[6]
- Philosophy: [[Abe-no-Seimei]].[6]
- Martial Training: [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], built on the way she entered the first war.[6][2]

#### Weapon Proficiency
- Plasma Firearm: In Conqueror Mode it does not change. She fires it on the way in and opens holes in the monster. The batteries recharge.[2]
- Miasma Sword: It also stays a sword in that mode. Forged from the deepest metals of Hell, where the Miasma is dense and toxic. On the hive she lights Dragon Fire from it and kills hundreds in the spin.[2][4]

#### Power Suit
- Conqueror Mode: Akasha-001 shifts the armor into a flashier, bulkier, grandiose suit, made to draw attention and to inspire fear and respect. The line is "Conquer Well, Operator." The weapons do not transform. The suit covers her wings like a liquid, and the helmet distorts into a toothed maw for the breath.[2]
- Akasha-001: The assistance system based on [[Akasha Elderblood]], for the body, the vitals, and the field. On the hive the helmet scanner sounds with Conan's. She looks at the hive instead of the alarm.[2][4]
