#### Debut

Kiana Bloodhunter is one of the seven Original Emperor's Soldiers personally created by [[Victor Elderblood]] to form the first generation of elite Imperial Commanders of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Like her six siblings, Kiana was created from a Dragonoid foundation enhanced through the beneficial characteristics of several supernatural races, Victor's Blessings and a minute fragment of his Soul. Among the Originals, Kiana immediately distinguished herself through an unusually intense and unsettling presence, with [[Scathach]] later identifying her as one of the group's more unpredictable and potentially dangerous personalities.[1][2][3]

### Physical Appearance
Kiana is a pale-skinned woman with long white hair, a curvaceous and mature physique, Draconic red eyes and exceptionally sharp teeth. Her appearance was immediately noted as contrasting sharply with her two sisters, [[Clare Bloodhunter]] and [[Laura Bloodhunter]]. Whereas Clare and Laura possess comparatively slender physiques, Kiana's body is much more voluptuous, and the overall impression she gives is considerably more intense.[2]
Unlike several of the other Original Emperor's Soldiers, Kiana possesses no horns or other immediately noticeable external Draconic characteristics in her normal humanoid form. Instead, her most distinctive natural trait is her unusually sharp set of teeth, which are described as noticeably sharper than those of the other Originals. Although all Emperor's Soldiers possess shapeshifting capabilities and could theoretically reproduce similar traits, Kiana appears to have naturally developed hers as part of her individual physiology.[2]
Like the other Emperor's Soldiers, Kiana possesses a naturally enormous Dragonoid body but can alter her physical proportions through the Vampiric metamorphic characteristics incorporated into her species. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] deliberately retained this adaptability because permanently maintaining a roughly four-meter natural body would make infiltration, interaction with smaller species and other specialized missions unnecessarily difficult.[1]
During military operations, Kiana uses the advanced black Imperial armor developed for the Emperor's Soldiers. The armor possesses violet details and adaptive technological systems capable of modifying themselves according to the soldier's requirements, while the Dragon symbolism associated with Victor's Empire identifies her as one of his personal forces.[3]

### Personality
Kiana possesses one of the most enigmatic and disturbing personalities among the seven Original Emperor's Soldiers. Even immediately after awakening, the impression she gave was described as considerably more intense than that of her sisters. Although she displayed the same immediate loyalty toward [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] as the other Originals, her demeanor suggested a darker and considerably less predictable temperament beneath that obedience.[2]
Her first battlefield evaluation demonstrated this quality clearly. While Conan immediately charged into combat, Laura approached the Technological Empire and Caelus stopped to observe the entire battlefield, Kiana simply remained where [[Scathach]] had left her. Rather than appearing confused or afraid, she wore a macabre smile and lightly touched her chin as though contemplating something she found deeply amusing. After approximately two minutes of thought, she suddenly disappeared at tremendous speed without revealing what she intended to do or where she was going.[3]
Scathach was not surprised by this behavior. After observing the seven Originals, she considered their reactions broadly consistent with their natural personalities and specifically grouped Kiana together with [[Kaleb Hunter]] as one of the more maniacal members of the group. This suggests that Kiana's seemingly strange behavior was neither hesitation nor fear; she had already decided upon an approach to the war and simply chose not to make her intentions obvious to anyone observing her.[3]
Kiana is therefore best characterized as calculating but unpredictable. She does not share Conan's instinct to immediately throw himself into direct violence, nor does she display John's openly analytical methodology. Instead, she appears to enjoy developing plans privately and acting suddenly once she reaches whatever conclusion amuses or satisfies her. This makes her difficult to read even compared with the other Originals.[3]
Despite her darker temperament, Kiana possesses absolute loyalty toward Victor Elderblood. The moment she emerged from her creation chamber, she immediately recognized Victor as her Emperor and Creator and knelt alongside Clare and Laura before the male Originals had even done so. Her unusual personality therefore exists within the central purpose Victor gave all seven Originals: protecting his Empire and carrying out his [[Will Solace|Will]].[2]
Like her siblings, however, Kiana was deliberately created with her own Soul and individuality rather than functioning as a mindless biological weapon. The memories implanted within her were intended as lessons and accumulated experience, not as replacements for her identity. Her personality, including its more unsettling characteristics, consequently developed as something uniquely her own.[1][2]

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Kiana's creation originated from [[Victor Elderblood]]'s desire to establish a professional military force belonging directly to the Emperor. Victor's existing civilization already contained enormous armies, supernatural followers and forces associated with his wives, but many of those soldiers ultimately belonged to particular factions or members of his family. Victor wanted a force whose first and highest allegiance was to the Imperial structure itself.[1]
Early cloning research demonstrated that simply creating ordinary copies and attempting to fill them with Victor's power was impractical. Victor's Soul and nature were too abnormal for conventional bodies to safely accommodate in meaningful quantities. Rather than creating inferior copies of himself, Victor and the scientists working beside him chose to engineer specialized beings specifically designed to serve as soldiers.[1]
The Dragonoid race became the biological foundation for the project because its physical robustness and Draconic nature gave it high compatibility with Victor. The template was then supplemented with desirable characteristics from several additional races. Human traits supplied adaptability and ingenuity; Angelic characteristics contributed obedience and benevolent qualities; Demonic traits provided aggression and cruelty useful in warfare; Faerie Essence supplied spiritual qualities and affinity with Nature; while Vampiric characteristics contributed regeneration and metamorphosis.[1]
Victor further strengthened the resulting species through Blessings associated with combat and embedded a minuscule fragment of his own Soul within the Originals. His Eldritch nature helped stabilize a racial combination that ordinarily should have suffered severe incompatibilities, creating what [[Scathach]] would later recognize as a form of stable supernatural chimera possessing primarily the beneficial characteristics of each contributing race.[1][3]
The first successful generation consisted of [[Clare Bloodhunter]], [[Laura Bloodhunter]], Kiana, Conan, [[Caelus Hunter]], [[Kaleb Hunter]] and John. These seven were created as more than elite combatants. They would become the original Imperial Commanders, serving as individual specialists and eventually providing the genetic and experiential foundations from which future Emperor's Soldiers could be developed.[1][4]
Kiana spent approximately ten subjective years developing before leaving her creation chamber. During this period, Victor exposed the Originals to the memories and experiences of warriors and heroes from the past. These implanted memories taught them about tactics, warfare, strategy, death and the behavior of powerful fighters without overwhelming their individual identities. By the time they emerged, they already possessed a theoretical education far beyond that of ordinary newly born beings.[2]
When the Originals finally awakened, Clare was the first to kneel before Victor. Laura immediately joined her, followed closely by Kiana. Kiana's appearance stood out even among her unusual siblings: she possessed white hair, pale skin, a much more curvaceous body than her sisters, Draconic red eyes and abnormally sharp teeth but lacked the horns or other obvious Draconic traits visible on several of the others. More than her appearance, however, those present noticed the unusually intense impression surrounding her.[2]
Victor subsequently explained that merely inheriting knowledge from heroes did not make the seven experienced warriors. Practical development remained necessary if they were going to become the commanders he envisioned. He therefore entrusted them to four legendary instructors representing different aspects of leadership and warfare.[4]
Hassan-i Sabbah would educate them in assassination and the many methods through which a life could be taken. [[Julius Caesar]] was responsible for politics and rulership, ensuring the future Commanders understood civilizations rather than only how to destroy them. [[Abe-no-Seimei]] would teach philosophy and wisdom, while [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], the Teacher of Heroes and Victor's former master, would take responsibility for their direct combat development.[4]
Although Scathach initially judged the children harshly because years around Victor and his extraordinarily talented family had distorted her standards, Victor told her to compare them with exceptional Heroes and Gods rather than with his daughters. Once she reassessed them properly, Scathach recognized that all seven possessed enormous potential even if they did not approach the absurd talent found within Victor's immediate family.[4]
Their first practical lesson took place within the Elderblood family's personal Nightmare Tower. Each Original was placed within an independent version of the same war simulation. Two Empires—one dependent primarily upon advanced technology and another dominated by immense physical strength—were already fighting when interdimensional monsters invaded their world. Scathach ordered the soldiers to pacify both Empires and destroy the invading threat.[3]
The exercise was intentionally brutal. The Originals received no conventional introductory training beforehand because they already possessed the memories and theoretical experience of past heroes. Scathach wanted them to experience actual danger, injury, fear and the possibility of death so she could determine what kind of warriors they naturally were and design their subsequent training accordingly.[3]
Each Original responded differently after Scathach disappeared. Conan immediately charged into battle, Laura moved toward the Technological Empire, Caelus began observing the overall situation and Kaleb marched directly toward the ruler of the Strength Empire. Kiana's response was considerably stranger. She remained completely still, wearing a macabre smile and touching her chin while apparently contemplating an idea that greatly amused her.[3]
For approximately two minutes she made no move at all. Then, without explaining herself, Kiana simply disappeared from her position at absurd speed. Neither her destination nor the plan she had devised was revealed. Scathach, observing the seven from afar, recognized the behavior as characteristic of Kiana and mentally categorized both her and Kaleb among the more maniacal personalities of the Original group.[3]
The incident established one of Kiana's defining characteristics as a soldier: unpredictability. She did not react impulsively merely because a battle existed in front of her, but whatever conclusions she reached during those two minutes were apparently unconventional enough that she departed alone without attempting to explain herself. Where other Originals immediately revealed the type of soldier they would become, Kiana deliberately kept even her instructor guessing about the exact nature of her plan.[3]
As Victor's civilization developed into the [[Elderblood Empire]], Kiana remained recognized alongside her six siblings as one of the original Imperial Commanders. The Emperor's Soldier program eventually expanded from only seven Originals into a large professional military force, with the Originals serving as the first generation and foundational templates of the species.[5]
The Empire's military was later reorganized into seven major Legions specializing in different forms of warfare. However, the currently documented chapters do not explicitly assign Kiana permanent command of one specifically named Legion. Her confirmed status remains that of one of Victor's seven Original Imperial Commanders rather than the established commander of a particular later Legion.[6][7]

### Skills & Abilities
Emperor's Soldier Physiology: Kiana is one of the seven Original Emperor's Soldiers, a hybrid species personally engineered by [[Victor Elderblood]]. Although she outwardly resembles a Dragonoid, the Emperor's Soldiers are more accurately supernatural chimeras whose bodies combine advantageous characteristics taken from several races and stabilize them through Victor's Eldritch Essence, Blessings and Soul.[1][3]
- Dragonoid Physiology: The primary physical foundation of Kiana's species is a Dragonoid body, selected for its strength, durability and natural compatibility with Victor's own Draconic existence.[1]
- Hybrid Racial Characteristics: Kiana's body contains beneficial characteristics originating from Humans, Angels, Demons, Faeries and Vampires in addition to its Draconic foundation. This gives her capabilities and developmental potential that an ordinary Dragonoid would not naturally possess.[1][3]
- Eldritch Stabilization: The racial combination used to create the Emperor's Soldiers should ordinarily have produced severe incompatibilities. The fragment of Victor's Eldritch Soul and his Blessings instead stabilize their existence, allowing the various racial components to function together without destroying the host.[3]
- Victor's Soul Fragment: A minute portion of Victor's Soul was placed within the Original Emperor's Soldier template. [[Scathach]] later recognized that the fragment was not merely passive but actively influenced the development and mutation of their supernatural abilities.[1][3]
- Combat Blessings: Victor personally Blessed the Emperor's Soldiers using powers associated with combat, further enhancing the military potential already supplied by their engineered bodies.[1]
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Kiana possesses tremendous strength, speed, durability, agility, stamina and reflexes as an elite hybrid soldier designed for warfare. During her first battlefield test, she was able to disappear from Scathach's observation point with what was explicitly described as absurd speed.[3]
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Vampiric characteristics were incorporated into the Emperor's Soldier template in part to provide powerful regeneration in addition to the natural durability of their Dragonoid bodies.[1]
- Metamorphosis: Kiana possesses shapeshifting capabilities derived from the Vampiric components of her physiology. Victor deliberately incorporated this trait so his naturally gigantic soldiers could change their proportions when infiltration or interaction with smaller species required it.[1]
- Enhanced Senses: As a hybrid of several powerful supernatural races, Kiana possesses senses greatly exceeding those of ordinary mortal beings.[1]
- Abnormal Dentition: Kiana naturally possesses exceptionally sharp teeth, noticeably sharper than those of her siblings. Although the other Emperor's Soldiers could theoretically reproduce similar features using metamorphosis, Kiana appears to possess them naturally.[2]
Inherited Combat Knowledge: Kiana received implanted memories and experiences belonging to historical heroes and warriors during her development. These memories gave her knowledge of warfare, combat, strategy and the behavior of powerful fighters before she had ever personally entered battle. Victor nevertheless intended these memories to function as lessons rather than substitutes for genuine personal experience.[2][4]
Unpredictable Tactical Thinking: Kiana's first Nightmare Tower test demonstrates an unusual tendency to independently formulate plans while revealing very little about her intentions. Rather than immediately choosing an obvious military objective, she remained in place for approximately two minutes while smiling to herself before suddenly disappearing at tremendous speed. Even Scathach could only observe that whatever Kiana intended was characteristic of her more maniacal personality.[3]
Extreme Speed: Kiana can move at extraordinary speed. During her first practical combat evaluation, she abruptly disappeared from her original location after deciding on her course of action, with the narration directly describing her movement as occurring at "absurd speed".[3]
Psychological Unpredictability: Kiana's demeanor makes her difficult to read before she acts. Her macabre amusement in the middle of a catastrophic war simulation and her refusal to visibly telegraph her intended objective distinguish her from the more easily interpreted battlefield styles of her siblings. While not itself a supernatural power, this characteristic can make predicting her actions considerably more difficult.[3]
Advanced Imperial Combat Armor: Kiana possesses the advanced adaptive armor issued to the Original Emperor's Soldiers. The armor was personally developed under Victor's Imperial technological system and contains capabilities far exceeding ordinary protective equipment.[3]
- Adaptive Construction: The armor can alter its own configuration according to the requirements and combat preferences of its operator rather than remaining a fixed suit of protection.[3]
- Integrated Artificial Intelligence: The Original soldiers' armor contains an Akasha-based artificial-intelligence subsystem designed to assist the Emperor's Soldiers in battle. The system can perform combat assistance, body analysis, vital monitoring and numerous other support functions.[3]
- Combat H.U.D.: The armor is capable of displaying the user's physical condition, equipment information and battlefield data through its helmet systems.[3]
Plasma Weapon Proficiency: Kiana was issued an Imperial plasma firearm for her first Nightmare Tower deployment. The weapons supplied to the Originals use rechargeable Energy batteries and were designed to remain operational during prolonged military engagements.[3]
Miasma Weaponry: Kiana was also issued a sword forged using metals extracted from the deepest portions of Hell, where Miasma is exceptionally dense. The weapons inflict conventional physical wounds while simultaneously exposing targets to poisonous Miasma.[3]
Martial Training: Kiana was placed under the personal instruction of [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], who intended to construct individualized training programs after observing the natural combat behavior of each Original within the Nightmare Tower.[4][3]
Assassination Training: Hassan-i Sabbah was selected to teach Kiana and her siblings assassination and the many methods through which an enemy could be eliminated.[4]
Political Education: [[Julius Caesar]] was assigned to educate the Originals in politics and leadership. Victor intended them to become Imperial Commanders capable of understanding and controlling civilizations rather than merely functioning as living weapons.[4]
Philosophy and Wisdom: [[Abe-no-Seimei]] was appointed to teach Kiana and the other Originals philosophy and wisdom, providing intellectual and ethical development to accompany their inherited knowledge and tremendous physical power.[4]
