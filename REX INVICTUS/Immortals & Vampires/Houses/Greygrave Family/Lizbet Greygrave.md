Lizbet Greygrave is a former Dark Elf, current High Dark Elf and Spriggan Queen who serves as one of the Archbishops of the [[Blood God Religion]] under [[Valeria Alekerth]]. Originally associated with the Dark Elf population of the [[Norse Pantheon]], Lizbet was expelled from her homeland by her own people and left to wander Earth until Roxanne Elderblood discovered her while searching for talented representatives from different supernatural races to become the religion's first seven Archbishops.[1]
Her natural affinity with Nature made the territory created by [[Victor Elderblood]] almost overwhelming to her senses. After Victor personally created Victorius Aeternus and saturated its vegetation with his and Roxanne's [[Natural Energy]], Lizbet reacted to the city with near-intoxicated ecstasy, openly considering her new life superior to anything the people who exiled her could have imagined.[2]
Lizbet later became one of Valeria's most trusted Archbishops alongside Rena. When Victor temporarily assumed direct supervision of the Blood God Religion, Lizbet helped formulate the religion's long-term strategy for expanding his influence through community integration, incentives, social protection and gradual political control. Impressed by her work, Victor personally evolved Lizbet from a Dark Elf into a High Dark Elf and awakened a dormant aspect of her lineage that transformed her into a Spriggan, described as the dark counterpart to [[Viviane]], the Lady of the Lake. The transformation made Lizbet a Queen-class Fairy-like Being and gave her an entirely new perception of herself and the world.[3][4]

### Physical Appearance

#### Dark Elf
Lizbet is a dark-skinned female Elf possessing long pointed ears and striking golden eyes. Her Elven appearance immediately identifies her as belonging to a race distinct from the Humans and other supernatural species making up the [[Blood God Religion]].[3]
The exact color of Lizbet's hair before her evolution has not been explicitly stated in the verified novel text.

#### High Dark Elf / Spriggan
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s evolution dramatically altered Lizbet's appearance. Like others personally improved through his Power, Lizbet became more beautiful, taller and more physically developed.[4]
Her skin changed into a deeper dark-gray coloration, her hair became snow white and her already-pointed Elven ears grew considerably more pronounced. Victor identified this state as the High Dark Elf, the next natural evolution of her Elven lineage.[4]
The greater change occurred when Victor awakened something dormant within Lizbet's existing bloodline. A pair of transparent wings suddenly manifested from her back, visually resembling the Fairy wings possessed by [[Viviane]]. This second transformation changed Lizbet into a Spriggan Queen, a dark Fairy-like counterpart to the Lady of the Lake.[4]

### Personality
Lizbet is energetic, expressive, intelligent and intensely emotional. Unlike the comparatively restrained Rena, Lizbet has little difficulty openly demonstrating what she feels when something strongly affects her.
Her strongest early characteristic is her overwhelming connection to Nature. Upon arriving in Victorius Aeternus, Lizbet became almost intoxicated by the enormous amount of [[Natural Energy]] saturating the city. She hugged one of the wooden pillars of a house, rubbed her face against it and reacted so intensely that other Acolytes compared her behavior to that of a drug addict. This reaction was not merely personal eccentricity; the religion's experienced members explained that Elves are creatures deeply connected to Nature and that Lizbet's senses were being overwhelmed by the concentrated Natural Power surrounding her.[2]
Lizbet also possesses a lingering resentment toward the Dark Elves who expelled her. While enjoying [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s city, she privately mocked the people who had thrown her out and left her for dead, taking great satisfaction in the fact that she was now living what she considered the dream of her entire race.[2]
She is extremely devoted to Victor. By the later period of the [[Blood God Religion]], this loyalty has developed into genuine religious fanaticism. Lizbet believes Victor represents the answer to the world's problems and increasingly views his authority as something that should extend over the entire planet. When discussing the future of the religion, she openly declared that Victor should become the sole supreme Divine authority and that every living Being should immediately think of the God Emperor whenever the idea of a God was mentioned.[4]
Unlike a mindless fanatic, however, Lizbet is capable of practical strategic thinking. She understands that religious expansion cannot rely entirely upon preaching or intimidation and argues that different people require different incentives. Some potential converts might respond to money, others to healing, Divine protection or help solving personal problems. She therefore favors adapting the religion's approach to individual circumstances.[3][5]
Lizbet's previous experiences with Gods initially made her unusually nervous around Victor. She was accustomed to Divine Beings possessing enormous but extremely fragile egos and consequently feared that saying something inappropriate in front of the God Emperor could result in punishment. Victor noticed her hesitation and explicitly reassured her that he would not punish one of his faithful merely for expressing an opinion he had personally requested.[5]
The realization deeply affected Lizbet. She recognized that she had unfairly judged Victor using the standards of the Pagan Gods she had known and apologized for her behavior. After receiving his forgiveness, she became considerably more comfortable speaking honestly in his presence.[5]
Lizbet also responds strongly to recognition from Victor. When he praised her and Rena for quickly understanding his goals, both women visibly flushed with happiness. After Victor evolved her into a High Dark Elf and Spriggan, Lizbet expressed profound gratitude and promised never to forget his instruction to continue advancing rather than becoming satisfied with her current state.[4]

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Lizbet was first mentioned during [[Roxanne Elderblood]] and [[Aline Elderblood Valefar]]'s review of the rapidly expanding [[Blood God Religion]]. Roxanne had finally completed the selection of seven women belonging to different races who would become the religion's first Archbishops. When Aline noticed the rare Dark Elf among them, Roxanne explained that Lizbet had been expelled from her homeland by her own people and had been wandering Earth when Roxanne discovered her.[1]
Roxanne regarded Lizbet as one of the talented individuals necessary to establish a genuinely multi-racial religion. The position of Archbishop was deliberately not restricted to Humans, and the presence of representatives such as Lizbet reinforced the principle that the Blood God Religion accepted intelligent Beings regardless of race.[1]
Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s direct intervention in the religion, the faithful relocated toward the newly created Holy Capital of Victorius Aeternus. The city had been formed through Victor's Nature-related Powers and further empowered through Roxanne, producing an environment filled with vegetation and unusually intense [[Natural Energy]].[2]
Lizbet reacted more strongly to this environment than almost anyone else. Her Elven senses became overwhelmed by the Natural Power saturating the city, causing her to behave with almost intoxicated delight. She became fascinated even with ordinary wooden structures and was known to react strongly merely to the scent of trees Victor had personally touched.[2]
For Lizbet, the situation possessed an additional personal significance. The people of her homeland had banished her and effectively left her to die, yet after being discovered by Roxanne she had become an Archbishop serving a growing Divine power and was living inside a city saturated with an amount of Nature Energy she regarded as an Elven paradise. Lizbet consequently took considerable satisfaction in how completely her circumstances had reversed.[2]
As an Archbishop, Lizbet entered the formal hierarchy beneath the newly appointed [[Valeria Alekerth]] and became responsible for helping administer, expand and represent the Blood God Religion.

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Much later, Lizbet remained one of Valeria's most trusted Archbishops. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] temporarily assumed a greater role in managing the religion while Valeria spent time with her resurrected daughter, only Rena and Lizbet were immediately present to help brief him regarding the organization's administrative state.[3]
Victor was already familiar with Lizbet and identified her as a dark-skinned Elf with golden eyes and pointed ears directly connected to the Dark Elf race associated with the [[Norse Pantheon]]. By this period those Dark Elves themselves had fallen under the broader authority of [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]].[3]
Victor asked Lizbet and Rena how they intended to expand his religion and influence across territories dominated by other Gods. Lizbet initially answered cautiously. Her experiences with Divine Beings had taught her that Gods often possessed enormous egos and could punish Mortals over insignificant perceived offenses, so she feared that criticizing or proposing changes to Victor's religious policy could provoke him.[5]
Victor noticed her fear and reassured her that he had explicitly asked for her opinion and would not punish her simply for answering honestly. Lizbet realized she had subconsciously judged him according to the behavior of the Gods she previously knew and apologized. Victor accepted the apology and encouraged her to continue.[5]
Lizbet subsequently proposed that the religion offer tangible incentives to encourage new followers. These incentives could include Divine protection, economic assistance, supernatural blessings or other benefits suited to the individual. She understood that even when the long-term rewards of devotion were extraordinary, ordinary people were often too impatient or desperate to wait years before seeing practical improvements to their lives.[5]
Victor then challenged the two Archbishops to explain how they would expand his influence while still respecting the principle of Free [[Will Solace|Will]]. After thinking carefully, Rena and Lizbet concluded that they should continue spreading the religion but avoid forcing people into formal membership. Instead, Acolytes and emissaries would become involved in local communities, publicly demonstrate their values and gradually bring entire populations beneath the religion's influence through protection and social involvement.[5][4]
Lizbet specifically proposed sending emissaries to teach the religion's doctrines without immediately intruding upon people's lives. Over time, the religion would become integrated into the surrounding community until most citizens naturally fell beneath its cultural influence.[4]
Her strategy quickly became far more ambitious. Lizbet supported Rena's proposal to protect innocent people from supernatural predators and improve conditions for populations devastated by previous apocalyptic events. However, she argued that the ultimate objective should go further: Victor should become the unquestioned supreme Divine authority of the world.[4]
Lizbet declared that when living Beings thought about Gods, they should immediately think of the God Emperor. Having personally witnessed Victor create Life, she considered the implication obvious—if creating Life was traditionally a feat attributed to creator Gods and Victor could accomplish that and much more, then by her reasoning he stood above ordinary creator deities.[4]
Her enthusiasm escalated further as she and Rena discussed removing political leaders who might secretly resist Victor's influence, controlling information and turning the religion's expansion into a gradual takeover rather than a conventional invasion. Victor was delighted by how quickly both women understood his underlying objectives and praised them as the Archbishops his disciple trusted most.[4]
Victor then decided that representatives expected to carry his Will before foreign Governments, Gods and supernatural civilizations could not remain weak. As a reward for the work Lizbet and Rena had performed for Valeria, he personally evolved both Archbishops.[4]
Lizbet was transformed first.
Purple Power surrounded her, and within seconds her body was reconstructed. Her skin became a deeper shade of gray, her hair turned snow white and her pointed ears became considerably more prominent. Victor's intervention had evolved her from a Dark Elf into a High Dark Elf, identified as the next stage of her natural Elven lineage.[4]
Victor did not stop there. Rather than simply implant an unrelated racial characteristic, he awakened something that had already existed within Lizbet's lineage but would never have been reached through ordinary development. Transparent wings suddenly emerged from her back, resembling those of [[Viviane]].[4]
Lizbet had become a Spriggan.
The narration describes her new state as the opposite counterpart to the Lady of the Lake. Where Viviane represented the Light, Lizbet represented the Darkness. Both occupied equivalent Queen positions within their respective Fairy-like racial branches, creating a form of balance between them.[4]
Victor formally welcomed her as the Spriggan who would punish those who opposed the Emperor. Lizbet thanked him with deep reverence and described the transformation as though she had previously spent her entire existence living inside a fog that had suddenly disappeared, allowing her to finally perceive the world as it truly was.[4]
Victor warned her not to become complacent simply because she had reached a higher racial state. Her current position could remain temporary if she continued advancing, learning about herself and discovering more of the world. Lizbet promised to remember those words permanently.[4]
After Victor completed Rena's evolution as well, he dismissed the two women with a single command: mold the world according to his Will. Lizbet immediately departed by flying with her newly awakened wings while Rena left on foot, marking the beginning of their expanded duties as empowered representatives of the God Emperor.[4]

### Skills & Abilities
Lizbet was already a supernatural Dark Elf before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s intervention. After Chapter 1029, her original Elven lineage was advanced into a High Dark Elf and a dormant Fairy-like aspect was awakened, transforming her into a Spriggan Queen. The full combat capabilities of this new race have not yet been demonstrated, so abilities not directly established by the novel are omitted.

#### High Dark Elf Physiology
- High Dark Elf Evolution: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] evolved Lizbet from an ordinary Dark Elf into a High Dark Elf, explicitly described as the next evolution of her Elven lineage.[4]
- Enhanced Physiology: The evolution visibly reconstructed Lizbet into a taller, more developed and superior version of her previous body. The exact numerical increase to her physical abilities has not been stated.[4]
- Enhanced Elven Traits: Her pointed ears became considerably more pronounced following the evolution, while her overall racial characteristics were strengthened.[4]
- Longevity: As an Elf and later High Dark Elf, Lizbet belongs to a naturally long-lived supernatural race. The novel has not given her exact age or maximum lifespan.
- Enhanced Senses: Lizbet possesses extremely sensitive Elven senses, particularly toward Nature and [[Natural Energy]].[2]

#### Nature Affinity
- [[Natural Energy]] Sensitivity: Elves are explicitly described as creatures of Nature, and Lizbet's senses react extremely strongly to concentrated Natural Energy. Victorius Aeternus contained enough Nature Power to overwhelm her senses with intense pleasure.[2]
- Nature Detection: Lizbet could perceive the supernatural quality of vegetation empowered by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] strongly enough that even smelling a tree he had personally touched produced an extreme reaction.[2]
- Environmental Affinity: Natural environments saturated with supernatural vitality are exceptionally compatible with Lizbet's Elven nature.[2]

#### Spriggan Physiology
- Spriggan [[Awakening]]: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] awakened a dormant aspect already present within Lizbet's lineage rather than simply grafting an unrelated race onto her. This transformation made her a Spriggan.[4]
- Fairy-Like Physiology: Spriggans are presented as a Fairy-like race positioned opposite the branch represented by [[Viviane]]. Lizbet's transformation produced transparent wings almost identical in appearance to those of the Lady of the Lake.[4]
- Queen Physiology: Lizbet did not become an ordinary Spriggan. She immediately became the Queen of the race, occupying a position narratively compared to Viviane's status among Fairies.[4]
- Flight: Lizbet can fly using the transparent wings awakened during her transformation. She immediately departed Victor's presence by flying after receiving her new mission.[4]
- Dark Counterpart: The novel explicitly describes Viviane as the Light and Lizbet as the Darkness, establishing the Spriggan Queen as Viviane's opposite racial counterpart.[4]
- Expanded Perception: Following her transformation, Lizbet described her previous existence as though she had been living inside a fog before suddenly being able to perceive the world properly. This indicates that the evolution substantially expanded her awareness or sensory perception, although the precise mechanics have not yet been explained.[4]

#### Latent Lineage Awakening
- Bloodline [[Awakening]]: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] awakened something that already existed within Lizbet's bloodline but which ordinary evolution would never have allowed her to reach.[4]
- Evolutionary Potential: Victor explicitly told Lizbet that her current state did not need to represent her final limit and encouraged her to continue advancing until she discovered more about herself and the world.[4]

#### Religious Authority
- Archbishop Authority: Lizbet is one of the seven original Archbishops of the [[Blood God Religion]], placing her directly beneath the High Priestess in its formal hierarchy.[1]
- Acolyte Command: As an Archbishop, Lizbet possesses authority over Acolytes and other lower-ranking official members of the religion.
- Missionary Authority: She can organize emissaries responsible for spreading [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s teachings and values into foreign communities.[4]
- Rite of Passage Authority: Formal entry into the Blood God Religion's inner hierarchy requires a Rite of Passage performed through Archbishop-level authority.
- Religious Representation: Lizbet is trusted to represent Victor's [[Will Solace|Will]] in territories ruled by other supernatural factions and Gods.[4]

#### Leadership & Intelligence
- Strategic Planning: Lizbet helped design the [[Blood God Religion]]'s long-term expansion strategy alongside Rena, incorporating social influence, community support, political restructuring and information control.[5][4]
- Religious Propagation: She understands that different potential followers require different motivations and proposed adapting religious rewards to the needs of individual converts.[5]
- Political Awareness: Lizbet recognizes the dangers of openly antagonizing existing Pantheons and understands the extreme sensitivity of Divine pride from her previous experience with Gods.[5]
- Community Integration: She proposed using missionaries and community involvement to spread [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s teachings gradually rather than relying exclusively upon forced conversion.[4]
- Ideological Influence: Lizbet intends for Victor's religion to become sufficiently culturally dominant that the idea of "God" itself becomes synonymous with the God Emperor among future populations.[4]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Lizbet's God Emperor and the central object of her religious devotion. Although Lizbet did not originally know Victor personally, her position within the [[Blood God Religion]] made her one of his highest-ranking mortal/supernatural representatives.[1]
Victorius Aeternus dramatically strengthened her worship. The city's enormous concentration of [[Natural Energy]] made it appear to Lizbet like an Elven paradise personally created by the God she served, reinforcing both her gratitude and her resentment toward the people who had previously expelled her.[2]
Her direct interactions with Victor initially exposed a major insecurity. Lizbet's previous dealings with Gods had taught her to expect punishment for even minor offenses, so she became visibly frightened while expressing opinions that might be interpreted as criticism. Victor reassured her that he valued honest answers and had no intention of punishing a faithful subordinate for doing exactly what he asked.[5]
After understanding the difference between Victor and the Gods she had previously known, Lizbet apologized and became significantly more comfortable speaking honestly before him.[5]
Victor later rewarded her loyalty and work by personally evolving her into a High Dark Elf and Spriggan Queen. Lizbet reacted with deep reverence and promised to carry his advice regarding continual self-improvement forever.[4]

#### Valeria Alekerth
[[Valeria Alekerth]] is Lizbet's High Priestess and direct superior within the [[Blood God Religion]]. Lizbet is one of the Archbishops Valeria trusts most heavily, alongside Rena.[4]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly framed Lizbet's evolution as a reward partly for the efforts she had made supporting his disciple. Her position therefore reflects both her devotion to Victor and her success serving Valeria's administration.[4]

#### Rena
Rena is Lizbet's closest known fellow Archbishop. The two served together during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s temporary oversight of the [[Blood God Religion]] and jointly developed the strategy intended to extend his influence over Earth.[3][4]
Their personalities differ considerably. Rena normally presents herself with greater calm and restraint, while Lizbet is much more expressive and excitable. Their religious views, however, are highly compatible; both believe that Victor should ultimately possess supreme authority over the world.[4]
Victor rewarded both women during the same meeting. Lizbet became a High Dark Elf and Spriggan Queen, while Rena became a True Werewolf enhanced with Alpha Monster characteristics.[4]

#### Roxanne Elderblood
[[Roxanne Elderblood]] was responsible for discovering Lizbet after her exile. Lizbet had been wandering Earth after being driven from her homeland when Roxanne encountered her and selected her as one of the seven racial representatives intended to become Archbishops of the growing [[Blood God Religion]].[1]
Roxanne therefore directly transformed the course of Lizbet's life, bringing a discarded Dark Elf into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction and placing her in a position that eventually resulted in her becoming the Queen of an entirely new supernatural branch.

#### Viviane
[[Viviane]] and Lizbet are presented as opposite counterparts after Lizbet's evolution. Both possess transparent Fairy wings and Queen-level positions, but where Viviane is described as representing Light, Lizbet represents Darkness.[4]
The comparison establishes a deliberate balance between the Fairy Queen associated with the Lady of the Lake and the newly awakened Spriggan Queen.[4]
