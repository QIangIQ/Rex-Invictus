### Physical Appearance
Gin is a male three-tailed Kitsune with white hair and a strong resemblance to his father, [[Hashimoto Genji]]. During his debut, he wore a noticeably flashy white yukata. His most prominent racial characteristic is the set of three fox tails behind him, distinguishing him from his father, who possesses nine.[1]
The novel has not revealed Gin's eye color, exact height, age, or any additional transformations.

### Personality
Gin has received only limited characterization, but his conduct during battle presents him as disciplined and orderly. While the forces of Genji and Haruna poured onto the battlefield, Gin immediately focused on maintaining the formation of his Yōkai rather than allowing them to scatter throughout the conflict. His first demonstrated concern was therefore the organization of the soldiers under his command.[1]
Unlike the more openly excited [[Yotsuba Kuroka]], who appeared enthusiastic about the approaching battle, Gin displayed a considerably more restrained and professional attitude. His position as a commander despite possessing only three tails also indicates that Genji trusted him with direct responsibility over a section of his forces. Beyond this brief battlefield appearance, the novel has not explored Gin's personal ambitions, beliefs, or relationship with his father in significant detail.[1]

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Gin made his first known appearance during the battle between [[Hashimoto Genji]] and [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]]. As the two Kitsune leaders began their confrontation, both sides opened portals and deployed the supernatural forces under their authority. Oriental Dragons appeared above the battlefield, including three Dragons belonging to Haruna's side and a considerably larger white Oriental Dragon associated with Genji's forces. Numerous other Yōkai followed them through the portals.[1]
The troops were accompanied by their respective commanders. [[Yotsuba Kuroka]], Haruna's right hand and second-in-command, emerged alongside Haruna's army, while Gin appeared at the head of Genji's forces. The narrative explicitly identified him as Genji's son, Hashimoto Gin, a three-tailed fox who closely resembled his father.[1]
Rather than immediately rushing into the fighting, Gin instructed his Yōkai not to spread themselves too widely and ordered them to fight in an organized manner. He and Kuroka briefly observed one another before both began directing their respective forces. Their armies subsequently clashed throughout the surrounding area while the main confrontation between Haruna and Genji remained largely isolated from the lesser combatants.[1]
Gin's role in the conflict was therefore primarily that of a field commander. Although he was present during a major Yōkai battle, the story did not individually depict him using a named technique, weapon, transformation, or direct combat ability before shifting its attention back toward the primary fighters.[1]

### Skills & Abilities
- Kitsune Physiology: Gin is explicitly identified as a three-tailed fox Yōkai. His three tails represent his current Kitsune classification and distinguish him from the much more developed nine-tailed [[Hashimoto Genji]]. The novel has not yet individually demonstrated enough of Gin's racial abilities to attribute every ability possessed by Genji or other high-ranking Kitsune to him.[1]
- Supernatural Physiology: As a Yōkai, Gin is a supernatural being rather than an ordinary Human. However, his brief appearance has not provided individual physical feats sufficient to accurately quantify his strength, speed, durability, regeneration, or other racial statistics.[1]
- Leadership: Gin held an actual command position within Genji's deployed Yōkai army. He was introduced alongside Kuroka specifically as one of the commanders leading the forces produced by the opposing portals.[1]
- Battlefield Coordination: Gin immediately ordered his forces to avoid scattering and to maintain an organized battle formation while fighting across a chaotic battlefield containing numerous Yōkai and Dragons. This demonstrates his ability to coordinate multiple supernatural combatants during large-scale warfare.[1]
- Military Command: Gin was trusted to issue orders independently while his father was occupied fighting Haruna directly. This allowed Genji to concentrate on the opposing Yōkai leader while Gin managed the broader engagement involving their subordinate troops.[1]

### Relationships
[[Hashimoto Genji]]
Gin is Genji's son and physically resembles him strongly enough for the resemblance to be immediately noted upon Gin's introduction. During the battle with Haruna, Gin served as one of the commanders of Genji's Yōkai forces while Genji personally fought Haruna.[1]
[[Yotsuba Kuroka]]
Kuroka served as Gin's counterpart during the battle. While Kuroka commanded Haruna's troops as her second-in-command and right hand, Gin directed the Yōkai deployed by Genji. The two briefly faced one another before turning their attention toward commanding their respective armies.[1]

### Trivia
- Gin's full name is Hashimoto Gin.[1]
- He is the only confirmed child of [[Hashimoto Genji]] currently identified by name.
- Gin possesses three tails, compared with his father's nine.[1]
- His debut places him in a role directly parallel to [[Yotsuba Kuroka]]: both acted as commanders while the leaders of their respective forces, Genji and Haruna, fought each other.[1]
- Despite being introduced during a major battle, Gin has not yet been given an individually named technique, weapon, transformation, or direct combat feat in his confirmed appearances.
