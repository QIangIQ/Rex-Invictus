### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 292
Hashimoto Genji, generally known as Genji, is a powerful nine-tailed white Kitsune and one of the most influential Yōkai introduced in Japan. He formerly served [[Inari-Omikami]], acted as the leader of the Kitsunes and represented the Japanese Yōkai in dealings with other supernatural factions. Following the collapse of Inari's authority and [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]]'s rise over the Japanese Yōkai, Genji became one of Haruna's generals.[1]
Genji is an old and experienced Yōkai with considerable political influence, military authority and supernatural power. Before meeting [[Victor Elderblood]], his negotiating ability was considered troublesome enough that even [[Adonis Snow]] had experienced difficulties dealing with him. In combat, Genji was powerful enough to serve as the principal rival to Haruna during her attempt to seize leadership of the Kitsunes, although Haruna eventually demonstrated that her potential and Yōuki reserves far exceeded his own.[2][3]

### Physical Appearance
Genji is a relatively tall man standing around 180 cm. He possesses white hair, prominent white fox ears and nine large white tails that move behind him, immediately identifying him as a fully developed Nine-Tailed Kitsune. At his debut he wore an elegant white kimono, matching the predominantly white coloration associated with his Kitsune lineage.[4]

### Personality
Genji is cunning, politically experienced and highly conscious of the balance of power within the supernatural world. Before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s rise, his eloquence and talent for negotiation were formidable enough to cause problems even for [[Adonis Snow]], an ancient Vampire with centuries of political experience. Genji initially intended to use those skills when negotiating with Victor, but quickly abandoned the idea after realizing that the young Count understood exactly what he was attempting to do and possessed enough military and political leverage that ordinary manipulation would be ineffective.[2]
Despite his cunning nature, Genji is considerably more pragmatic than arrogant. He is capable of recognizing when another individual possesses overwhelming leverage and adapts rather than stubbornly escalating a situation he cannot win. This is demonstrated when Victor's trade restrictions forced the Japanese Yōkai into action; Genji ultimately brought the offending [[Arachne Clan]] leader to Clan Snow rather than allow the economic pressure to continue damaging Yōkai interests.[2]
He also takes his responsibilities seriously. During the aftermath of Haruna's [[Hyakki Yakō]], Genji personally remained behind to remove supernatural contamination, restore the affected region and maintain the secrecy of the supernatural world. He was visibly exhausted by the number of problems simultaneously falling under his responsibility, yet continued dealing with them rather than abandoning his position.[5]
Genji can be severe when necessary. When [[Gyuki]] refused to stop pursuing Ophis and [[Nero]], Genji physically confronted him and threatened him with Divine flames capable of destroying his Soul so completely that reincarnation would become impossible. He was even prepared to consider killing Gyuki outright if that proved necessary to prevent a larger disaster.[5]
His experiences with Haruna and Inari also changed his outlook. Although Genji had been one of Inari's chosen servants, her interference in his battle against Haruna later became a source of irritation. When Genji witnessed the true extent of Haruna's power during her later battle with Victor, he realized she had restrained herself substantially during their own confrontation and privately wondered whether he could have forced her to reveal more had Inari not interfered.[3]
After Haruna unified the Japanese Yōkai, Genji accepted her leadership and became one of her generals. He remains cautious around overwhelmingly powerful beings such as [[Scathach]], unlike Haruna, whose refusal to lower her head even before stronger opponents repeatedly surprised him. His caution, however, should not be confused with cowardice; Genji is simply experienced enough to understand the difference between courage and provoking an entity vastly beyond him without reason.[1]

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Genji's first appearance occurred when he arrived at Clan Snow leading a group of Yōkai. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had recently used economic pressure against Yōkai trade after discovering that a member of the [[Arachne Clan]] had previously offended [[Agnes Elderblood Snow]]. When [[Nightingale]]'s commercial restrictions began affecting everyone associated with the Yōkai, the Witches and other supernatural traders pressured the Japanese faction to resolve the matter. Genji, then acting as a representative of the Yōkai, was therefore sent to Clan Snow.[4][2]
Victor already knew Genji through the memories inherited from Adonis. Genji had traded with the [[Snow Clan]] multiple times and was remembered as an exceptionally cunning man whose eloquence had occasionally caused trouble for Adonis himself. Genji initially believed that Victor's reputation as an impulsive twenty-one-year-old might allow him to negotiate his way through the crisis, but after meeting him directly he quickly realized that Victor was behaving far more like an experienced political predator than the young Count Genji had expected.[2]
Genji delivered the leader of the Arachne Clan as Victor had demanded. Victor proceeded to demonstrate that his economic maneuver had never truly been a negotiation: the pressure had been deliberately designed to force the supernatural community itself to locate and surrender the offending Yōkai. Confronted with Victor's overwhelming leverage and increasingly terrifying presence, Genji abandoned his initial attempt to manipulate the conversation. Victor then killed the prisoner and dismissed Genji after declaring that it had been a pleasure doing business with him.[2]
Genji later became involved in the upheaval caused by Otsuki Haruna. Haruna, leader of the Black Kitsune and commander of her own [[Hyakki Yakō]], challenged Genji for supremacy among the Kitsunes. Her appearance and the deployment of enormous numbers of supernatural beings created widespread Yōuki contamination and temporarily altered the affected area. After she departed, Genji was left responsible for restoring order.[6][5]
With a snap of his fingers, Genji dispelled the supernatural condition affecting the region and restored the normal moon. He then purified the remaining Yōuki covering the area, although traces of Haruna's energy resisted even his purification and remained behind. This incident also revealed another position Genji held under the Gods: he served as a gatekeeper who evaluated beings with the potential to become Gods.[5]
When [[Gyuki]] became interested in pursuing Ophis and [[Nero]], Genji immediately intervened. He warned Gyuki to abandon the girls and manifested a blue Divine flame granted to him by the Gods. Gyuki recognized the flame and became genuinely afraid because it could destroy a Soul and prevent its reincarnation. Genji's behavior demonstrated that he understood the catastrophic consequences that could result if individuals connected to the Vampire Progenitor were harmed inside Japan.[5]
The confrontation between Genji and Haruna eventually began in earnest. Genji brought his own Yōkai forces into battle, with his son [[Hashimoto Gin]] serving as one of their commanders. As Genji released his power, a large current of white energy rose toward the sky. Haruna answered by preparing her katana and surrounding herself with controlled Yōuki as their duel began.[7]
Their approaches to combat differed considerably. Genji relied upon the experience and established methods of an older Nine-Tailed Kitsune, whereas Haruna fought with an increasingly unconventional combination of swordsmanship, Yōuki control and tactics. Haruna also linked herself to the Yōkai serving beneath her, allowing her to coordinate their movements and deliberately reshape the course of the larger battle while she confronted Genji.[8]
Haruna gradually seized the advantage and was close to ending the confrontation when [[Inari-Omikami]] directly intervened. Her arrival prevented the duel between the two Kitsunes from reaching its natural conclusion. Inari's appearance then shifted the conflict away from Genji and toward the far larger confrontation between the Goddess and [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]].[9]
When the escalating battle between [[Scathach]] and Inari became too dangerous for those nearby, Genji used his white power to levitate his injured Yōkai subordinates from the ground and evacuate them from the battlefield.[10]
Inari's defeat fundamentally changed the political structure surrounding Japan's Kitsunes. Haruna subsequently became the central leader of the Japanese Yōkai, while Genji—formerly Inari's subordinate and the leader of the Kitsunes—eventually entered Haruna's hierarchy as one of her generals.[1]

#### Demonic War
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction began establishing formal relations with Haruna's united Yōkai, Genji took part in the negotiations alongside Haruna and her grandfather [[Otsuki Yoichi]]. By this point he was explicitly identified as a former subordinate of Inari and one of Haruna's generals. Genji and Yoichi remained extremely wary of [[Scathach]], having firsthand knowledge of what she was capable of, while Haruna remained comparatively relaxed despite standing before the strongest female Vampire.[1]
The meeting also concerned the Yōkai's attempts to overcome one of the greatest limitations of Yōuki: its dependence upon environments where the appropriate supernatural energy was available. Haruna's faction had begun developing and studying alternative systems such as [[Senjutsu]] and Ki. Genji was present as these discoveries became part of the strategic negotiations between the Yōkai and Victor's growing faction.[1]
Negotiations continued until the Yōkai formally moved toward an alliance with Victor's side. During a sensitive discussion concerning Victor and his family, Haruna ordered everyone outside and instructed Genji to isolate the mansion. Genji released white power that expanded around the entire building and formed a dome, preventing the conversation inside from being observed externally.[11]
Genji later attended Haruna's direct meeting with Victor. Both he and Yoichi were shocked by how much Victor had changed since his earlier appearance in Japan. During discussions about Kitsunes, Haruna explained that Nine-Tailed Kitsunes were extremely rare, with relatively few individuals ever developing nine tails and even fewer being born with them naturally like herself and Genji.[12]
The negotiations ultimately concluded with Victor and Haruna establishing a formal alliance between the Yōkai and the faction centered around Clan Alucard. The agreement was specifically an alliance with Victor's growing coalition rather than an alliance with every Vampire Clan, further separating Haruna's Yōkai from their former dependence upon the traditional supernatural political order.[13]
Genji later watched Haruna fight Victor. During the confrontation Victor encouraged Haruna to stop restraining herself, causing Genji to realize that she had never used her complete strength against him despite the importance of their earlier duel. When Haruna fully released her Yōuki, Genji determined that her reserves were approximately ten times greater than his own. Despite Genji being significantly older, a former Divine servant and himself an exceptionally powerful Nine-Tailed Kitsune, Haruna's raw reserves completely exceeded his.[3]
The realization annoyed Genji, particularly because Inari's intervention had prematurely ended his earlier fight with Haruna. Nevertheless, it also confirmed the enormous difference in potential between the new ruler of the Yōkai and the leader she had displaced.[3]

### Skills & Abilities
- White Nine-Tailed Kitsune Physiology: Genji is a fully developed Nine-Tailed White Kitsune. Nine tails place him among the highest natural stages of Kitsune development, and individuals at this level are exceptionally rare even among their own species.[4][12]
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: As a high-ranking Yōkai and Nine-Tailed Kitsune, Genji possesses physical capabilities vastly superior to ordinary Humans. He was able to directly participate in battle against Haruna and remained one of the strongest Yōkai associated with Japan.[7]
- Enhanced Senses: Genji possesses extremely acute senses. His hearing is powerful enough that the voices of thousands of Humans in Shibuya became irritating once the supernatural separation surrounding the area was removed.[5]
- Longevity: Genji is considerably older than Haruna and had already accumulated substantial experience as a Nine-Tailed Kitsune, political representative and Divine servant before her rise.[3]
- Fox Transformation: Nine-Tailed Kitsunes possess a true fox form that increases their power considerably. Genji is explicitly a Nine-Tailed White Kitsune, although his complete fox transformation has not been directly displayed in his major battles.
- Yōuki Manipulation: Genji can generate, release and control enormous quantities of Yōuki, the primary supernatural energy used by Yōkai. When preparing to fight Haruna, his power erupted upward like a white current reaching toward the sky.[7]
- Immense Yōuki Reserves: Although Haruna possesses approximately ten times Genji's amount of Yōuki, Genji is used as the benchmark for demonstrating how extraordinary her reserves are. His own amount remains exceptional by normal Yōkai standards.[3]
- White Energy Manifestation: Genji's supernatural power repeatedly manifests as white energy. He can release it around himself, direct it toward other beings and form larger supernatural constructs from it.[7][11]
- Energy Purification: Genji can purge large concentrations of Yōuki from an area. Following Haruna's [[Hyakki Yakō]], he purified the supernatural contamination covering a major section of Tokyo with simple gestures, although Haruna's residual Yōuki proved unusually persistent.[5]
- Supernatural Phenomena Dispelling: Genji was able to undo the altered supernatural state created during the Hyakki Yakō and return the red moon and surrounding region to normal with a snap of his fingers.[5]
- Barrier Creation: Genji can spread his white supernatural power into a large dome. At Haruna's command, he enveloped an entire mansion in such a barrier to isolate a confidential conversation from outside observation.[11]
- Levitation: Genji can surround other beings with his white power and levitate them. He used this ability to lift multiple injured Yōkai subordinates simultaneously so they could be evacuated from the battlefield during [[Scathach]] and Inari's confrontation.[10]
- Divine Flame Manipulation: During his service to the Gods, Genji was granted a special blue Divine flame for his work as a gatekeeper. The flame was feared even by other powerful Yōkai such as [[Gyuki]].[5]
- Soul Destruction: Genji's Divine flame can destroy the Soul of its target rather than merely burning the physical body.[5]
- Reincarnation Negation: Destruction by the Divine flame is severe enough that Genji threatened Gyuki with being unable to reincarnate afterward.[5]
- Hyakki Yakō Command: As the former leader of the Kitsunes, Genji commanded a large supernatural army. During his confrontation with Haruna, his followers entered the battlefield through supernatural gateways while commanders such as his son [[Hashimoto Gin]] coordinated their movements.[7]
- Leadership: Genji has extensive experience directing other Yōkai and formerly occupied one of the highest positions within Japanese Kitsune society. Even after Haruna displaced the previous order, she retained him as one of her generals rather than discarding his experience.[1]
- Political Expertise: Genji is an experienced representative, trader and negotiator. His eloquence was dangerous enough to occasionally cause problems for [[Adonis Snow]], and he represented the Yōkai during dealings involving Clan Snow and later [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction.[2]
- Tactical Awareness: Genji is capable of rapidly recognizing unfavorable circumstances. He abandoned his original attempt to manipulate Victor once it became clear that the Count possessed overwhelming leverage, and he repeatedly prioritized protecting the wider Yōkai population when larger supernatural conflicts threatened Japan.[2][5]

### Relationships
[[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]]
Genji and Haruna were originally political and military rivals. Haruna openly challenged his position as leader of the Kitsunes and brought her [[Hyakki Yakō]] against his own forces. Their battle demonstrated that Genji remained one of Japan's strongest Yōkai, but Haruna's combat talent, tactics and reserves placed her on a different trajectory. Their duel was interrupted by Inari before reaching its natural conclusion.[7][9]
After Inari's fall and Haruna's rise, Genji accepted Haruna as his leader and became one of her generals. Haruna trusts him with sensitive responsibilities, including participation in high-level diplomacy and the protection of confidential meetings. Genji later discovered that Haruna possessed roughly ten times his Yōuki and had restrained herself substantially during their duel.[1][11][3]
[[Inari-Omikami]]
Genji formerly served Inari and was one of the Yōkai directly connected to her authority. The Gods granted him Divine blue fire for his duties as a gatekeeper who judged candidates capable of ascending toward Godhood.[5]
His loyalty to Inari did not prevent him from later becoming frustrated with her intervention in his battle against Haruna. By the time he watched Haruna fight [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Genji regarded Inari's interference as one of the reasons he never discovered how much strength Haruna had actually been withholding against him.[3]
Victor Elderblood
Genji initially knew Victor primarily as the newly elevated Vampire Count whose economic pressure had forced the Yōkai to surrender the [[Arachne Clan]] leader. He entered their first negotiation believing that his experience and eloquence might allow him to manipulate the young Vampire, but quickly realized that Victor was far more calculating than expected.[2]
Their later interactions occurred under radically different circumstances. Victor had become the center of a powerful independent faction allied with Haruna, and Genji participated in negotiations that formally connected the Japanese Yōkai to Victor's coalition.[12][13]
[[Hashimoto Gin]]
Gin is Genji's son and a three-tailed Kitsune. Gin served as a commander in Genji's army during the battle against Haruna, directing Yōkai forces while his father personally fought the Black Kitsune leader.[7]
[[Otsuki Yoichi]]
Genji and Yoichi both serve within Haruna's leadership structure and regularly appear beside her during important political meetings. Both men were considerably more cautious around [[Scathach]] than Haruna herself and participated in the negotiations that eventually connected the Japanese Yōkai with Victor's faction.[1][11]

- Genji's white supernatural power can be used for combat, levitation, purification and barrier creation.[5][10][11]
