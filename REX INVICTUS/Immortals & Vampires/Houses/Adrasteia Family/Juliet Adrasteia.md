Juliet Adrasteia is a Vampire-Monster Balanced Hybrid of Clan Adrasteia and one of the elite Valkyries serving [[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]] and [[Rose Adrasteia Elderblood]]. She is the squad's principal assassin and one of its ranged-support combatants, using twin daggers for rapid close-range eliminations and a large black bow for long-distance attacks.[1][2] Alongside [[Alexa Adrasteia]], Juliet is explicitly identified as one of the two fastest members of the Valkyries.[3]

### Physical Description
Juliet is a tall woman with long blonde hair, pale skin and white eyes. Like the other Valkyries, she normally fights while wearing specialized Clan Adrasteia combat armor made to accommodate the altered physiology of their Vampire-Monster bloodline. Her equipment is comparatively lighter than that of heavy frontline members such as Eleanor and Anrietha, allowing Juliet to exploit her superior speed and mobility.[3]

### Personality
Juliet is confident, blunt, provocative and extremely fond of teasing the people around her. Among the Valkyries, she is one of the most shameless when discussing relationships or making deliberately inappropriate jokes, frequently pushing conversations further than the others are willing to take them.[4][5]
Despite her playful behavior around people she trusts, Juliet is naturally highly suspicious of outsiders. The other Valkyries specifically note that Juliet normally distrusts people outside Clan Adrasteia, making her willingness to behave casually around [[Victor Elderblood]] an important indication of how much trust he earned during their expedition.[4]
Juliet is also extremely proud of Clan Adrasteia. When the possibility of traditional [[Nightingale]] Vampires opposing their monstrous bloodline was discussed, Juliet openly invited them to try, dismissing such Vampires as weak and displaying complete confidence in Warfall's military defenses.[4]
Like the rest of the Valkyries, Juliet is a battle enthusiast. She becomes comfortable in dangerous environments and does not hesitate to engage enormous Nightingale monsters at close range. Unlike the more reckless members of the group, however, her role as an assassin encourages a comparatively analytical fighting style. Juliet prioritizes dangerous or strategically important enemies rather than merely attacking the closest target.[1]
She is also capable of criticizing her companions during battle. When Dorothy became overly excited while attacking monsters whose durability had increased beyond the levels they normally encountered, Juliet immediately ordered her to calm down and return to the formation before personally assuming responsibility for assassination targets.[1]
Juliet's provocative personality is particularly apparent in her interactions involving Victor. She was the Valkyrie who most aggressively promoted the group's so-called Solomon Plan, openly arguing that the Valkyries should accompany their Valkyrie Queen if she married Victor and jokingly presenting the arrangement as a package deal.[5]

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Juliet was introduced as one of the six Valkyries serving beneath Rose when Eleanor returned to Warfall alongside [[Victor Elderblood]] and her companions. As one of Eleanor's personal elite soldiers, Juliet belonged to the small group of warriors trusted with missions beyond the defensive boundaries of Warfall.[6]
Her combat specialty became clear during Victor's later expedition with Eleanor, Rose and the Valkyries. When the group encountered approximately twenty Behemoth-class monsters, Dorothy initially attempted to perform her usual high-speed assassination maneuver. The increased durability of the monsters made her attack less effective than expected, prompting Juliet to appear beside her and tell her to calm down because such failures would become increasingly common as they travelled farther from Warfall.[1]
Juliet then directly declared that the assassination work belonged to her and disappeared into the surrounding mist while examining the monsters for priority targets. Rather than attacking randomly, she immediately identified one of the most dangerous creatures: a monster capable of calling additional hordes to the battlefield.[1]
Before the creature could alert the others, Juliet appeared near it and cut its throat with her daggers. She followed the initial attack by driving the blades into its brain. When the monster attempted to drive her away using its claws and thorns, Juliet withdrew her daggers, disappeared into mist and reappeared in the air before the counterattack could reach her.[1]
She then switched from assassination to ranged combat. Juliet stored her daggers, drew a large black bow from her back and pulled the string despite carrying no conventional arrow. An arrow composed entirely of supernatural power formed on the weapon. After Juliet released it, the projectile divided into four additional arrows that simultaneously pierced the monster's head and heart, completing the kill.[1]
The battle established Juliet as an exceptionally versatile fighter capable of transitioning between close-range assassination and long-distance support almost instantly. Eleanor later explicitly stated that Juliet and Alexa were the fastest warriors in the Valkyrie squad.[3]
Juliet continued exploiting that speed during the engagement. When Judith accidentally used excessive force and completely vaporized a monster, Juliet appeared on top of another approximately ten-meter-tall creature and decapitated it before instructing Judith to use less power so that the monsters could still be harvested for food.[3]
As the group pushed beyond the 30,000-kilometer boundary previously considered safe by Clan Adrasteia, the Valkyries discussed Victor's increasingly abnormal abilities. Juliet specifically noted that Victor possessed the elemental powers inherited from the three famous Vampire Count bloodlines—water, ice, lightning and fire—while the others added his Blood powers and extraordinary close-combat skill. Rose subsequently revealed her conclusion that Victor was the Second Vampire Progenitor.[2]
When Victor jokingly promised that he would invade either Hell or Heaven to retrieve the Valkyries if they died during the expedition, Juliet was left unsure how to react to his absolute confidence. Like the others, however, she was secretly pleased by the implication that Victor would start a supernatural war rather than leave one of them dead.[2]
Juliet's combat role became even more explicit when Victor temporarily assumed command of the expedition's formation against a large monster tribe. He ordered Judith and Juliet to handle airborne enemies and provide long-distance support. The pair immediately moved onto the roofs of the monster settlement and began eliminating Wyvern-class creatures from range while the heavier members of the Valkyries fought below.[2]
Victor's tactical assessment confirmed that Juliet belonged to the lighter and faster section of the Valkyrie formation. While Eleanor and Anrietha used considerably heavier armor and occupied the primary frontline, Dorothy, Judith and Juliet wore lighter equipment and used their greater mobility to support the heavy fighters and exploit openings created by them.[2]
By the end of the expedition, Victor had earned Juliet's unusual trust. When Rose attempted to test Victor's intentions by suggesting he could marry Eleanor to obtain access to Clan Adrasteia's secret bloodline ritual, Victor reacted with genuine disgust at the idea of exploiting Eleanor's feelings merely for additional power. After Victor walked away, Juliet joined the other Valkyries in criticizing Rose's decision.[4]
Juliet reminded Rose that Victor had treated the Valkyries as genuine companions from the beginning of the expedition, repeatedly protecting them with his own body while asking for nothing in return. She argued that his intentions had clearly been sincere.[4]
When Rose explained that she had only wanted certainty because she feared losing Eleanor, the Valkyries advised her to apologize. Juliet bluntly added that Rose had lost some of Victor's respect through the unnecessary test. The others specifically pointed out the significance of Juliet saying this: she had always been unusually suspicious of outsiders, yet Victor had earned enough trust that she could relax and behave normally around him.[4]
Juliet then immediately demonstrated her usual mischievous personality by privately suggesting an indecent method through which Rose could regain Victor's favor. Rose rejected the suggestion and called her behavior inappropriate, while Juliet dismissed Rose as overly puritanical.[4]
On the return journey to Warfall, Juliet became the central instigator behind the Valkyries' Solomon Plan. When the single Valkyries complained about Eleanor and Victor openly flirting in front of them, Juliet asked whether they had truly abandoned the earlier plan. When Rose initially assumed she had merely been joking, Juliet revealed that she had been serious.[5]
Juliet argued that the Valkyries were expected to remain beside their Valkaria Queen; therefore, if Eleanor eventually married Victor, the Valkyries should accompany her rather than separate from their leader. Juliet summarized the proposed arrangement as a "1 and 7" package, causing Alexa to immediately question her sanity.[5]
Juliet then included Rose in the proposal as well, arguing that their commander deserved affection after spending so much of her life training rather than establishing a family. When Judith stated that she would participate if Rose did, Alexa and Martha immediately followed, and eventually the remaining Valkyries voiced the same position.[5]
Juliet took the joke even further by telling Rose that the future of the Valkyries depended upon her entering Victor's bed and having a child. Rose responded by striking Juliet on the head and ordering her to stop. Juliet continued insisting that the underlying proposal was not actually a joke until Rose's glowing red eyes convinced her that she had pushed the teasing far enough.[5]
Juliet's teasing continued shortly afterward when Victor prepared an enormous destructive technique to erase the abandoned City of Babylon. After Eleanor suggested moving farther away from the attack, Juliet mockingly asked whether she trusted her "husband". When Eleanor immediately protested that Victor was not her husband yet, Juliet and Dorothy both seized upon the accidental wording and teased her further.[5]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Juliet remained with the Valkyries through Clan Adrasteia's transition into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s expanding faction. When Victor reunited with them after his ascension, he immediately noted that all six Valkyries had become considerably stronger following the wars they had experienced.[7]
Unlike several of her companions, Juliet was still openly troubled by Eleanor and Rose's decision concerning the surviving native enemies of Clan Adrasteia. She admitted that she understood why her leaders had chosen enslavement rather than extermination but found the decision emotionally difficult to accept because generations of Adrasteia warriors had died fighting those enemies and the clan's main lineage had been nearly destroyed by the conflict.[7]
Victor advised Juliet and the others to trust Eleanor and Rose, reminding them that their leaders had never failed them. Juliet ultimately accepted the argument alongside the other Valkyries.[7]
Victor then revealed that he had personally created new high-level weapons for the Valkyries with the assistance of [[Hephaestus]]. Each artifact was specifically designed for its intended user rather than being a generic weapon taken from his stores. Juliet was visibly delighted with her gift and openly declared that she loved it.[7]
The gesture held considerable meaning for the Valkyries because Victor had remembered their individual combat specialties and personally invested his time and Divine craftsmanship into strengthening them despite only having fought alongside them during a comparatively small number of battles.[7]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Juliet and the other Valkyries remained part of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s longstanding inner circle even after the Elderblood family and Empire expanded enormously. When [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] later evaluated women who might still realistically become part of Victor's family, she specifically excluded the Valkyries from the category of unknown outsiders because Victor had already known them for a long time.[8]
Despite their attraction toward Victor and their repeated jokes about joining his harem, the Valkyries did not make pursuing him the center of their lives. Juliet and the others remained primarily focused upon becoming stronger and accompanying Eleanor and Rose wherever their leaders went, with Violet observing that the Valkyries behaved more like inseparable sisters than ordinary subordinates.[8]

### Skills and Abilities
Juliet is an elite Valkyrie whose combat style emphasizes assassination, speed, precision and rapid transitions between melee and long-distance combat. Rather than confronting the strongest enemy directly like the heavy fighters of the squad, Juliet identifies priority targets and eliminates them before they can meaningfully affect the battlefield.
- Noble Vampire Physiology: As a Noble Vampire, Juliet possesses supernatural physical abilities, enhanced senses, regeneration and longevity far beyond those of humans. Her natural Vampiric physiology is further modified by Clan Adrasteia's Monster bloodline.
- Monster Physiology: Juliet belongs to the elite line of Clan Adrasteia created through the Clan's Ancient Rebirth Ritual, which merged Noble Vampire blood with the highly adaptive bloodlines of [[Nightingale]]'s monsters.[4]
- Enhanced Physical Attributes: Clan Adrasteia's altered lineage produces physical strength, durability, agility and senses superior to those of ordinary Noble Vampires.[9]
- True Monster Form: Eleanor confirmed that her Valkyries belong to the select members of Clan Adrasteia capable of accessing genuine Monster Forms rather than merely displaying diluted traits.[4]
- Partial Transformation: Juliet can access portions of her monster-derived physiology without requiring a complete transformation.
- Monster Eyes: Juliet can consciously make her eyes increasingly monstrous when invoking her altered bloodline.[4]
- Enhanced Instincts: The Monster Forms of Clan Adrasteia intensify the natural instincts inherited from their non-Vampiric bloodlines.
- Adaptive Monster Physiology: The ancestral ritual created a stable hybridization between Vampire and Monster genetics because Nightingale's monsters possess extraordinary biological adaptability.[4]
- Monster Flesh and Blood Resistance: Unlike conventional Vampires, Clan Adrasteia members can safely consume the flesh and blood of Nightingale's monsters because their own Monster lineage prevents the substances from acting as lethal toxins.[2]
- Regeneration Negation: Nightingale's monsters possess extreme regenerative abilities and can restore themselves even after apparently fatal destruction. The Monster-derived power carried by Clan Adrasteia can permanently kill these creatures by preventing their regeneration from continuing.[10]
- Unique Energy: Juliet possesses the unusual power generated by Clan Adrasteia's Vampire-Monster bloodline. This energy can be channeled through the Clan's specialized equipment and allows its warriors to permanently damage Nightingale's otherwise immortal monsters.[10]
- Assassination: Juliet explicitly identifies assassination as her designated battlefield responsibility within the Valkyries.[1]
- Priority Target Elimination: Rather than attacking enemies randomly, Juliet examines the battlefield and identifies monsters whose abilities make them strategically dangerous. Her first demonstrated assassination targeted a creature capable of calling an additional monster horde.[1]
- Weak-Point Targeting: Juliet kills large monsters by attacking vulnerable anatomical locations rather than attempting to overpower their external defenses. She severed a monster's throat before immediately driving her daggers into its brain.[1]
- Silent Approach: Juliet can use her superior speed and Vampiric movement techniques to approach targets without giving them sufficient opportunity to react.
- Rapid Disengagement: When her assassination target attempted to retaliate using its claws and thorns, Juliet immediately removed her daggers and vanished into mist before the counterattack could connect.[1]
- Dagger Mastery: Twin daggers are Juliet's principal close-range assassination weapons. She possesses sufficient control to sever the throat of a gigantic monster and transition immediately into a precise brain strike before it can respond.[1]
- Archery: Juliet is an accomplished ranged fighter and carries a large black bow in addition to her daggers.[1]
- Energy Arrow Generation: Juliet does not require conventional ammunition for her bow. By drawing the string, she can generate an arrow made entirely from supernatural energy.[1]
- Projectile Duplication: A single energy arrow fired by Juliet can divide into four additional projectiles while travelling toward its target.[1]
- Multi-Point Precision: Her divided arrows simultaneously penetrated the head and heart of a large Nightingale monster, demonstrating enough accuracy to strike multiple vital locations during the same attack.[1]
- Long-Range Support: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly assigned Juliet and Judith to eliminate airborne enemies and provide long-distance battlefield support during the attack on the monster settlement beyond the 30,000-kilometer boundary.[2]
- Anti-Air Combat: Juliet operated from rooftops while eliminating Wyvern-class monsters in the sky, confirming that her bow is used as one of the Valkyries' primary anti-air weapons.[2]
- Enhanced Speed: Juliet and Alexa are explicitly identified by Eleanor as the fastest members of the Valkyries.[3]
- High-Speed Repositioning: Juliet can move from ground-level assassination combat into the air before enemies are capable of retaliating.
- Rapid Battlefield Traversal: Her speed allows her to appear directly beside other Valkyries during combat, eliminate targets and return to support positions with little interruption to the formation.
- Fog Walking / [[The Mist|Mist]] Movement: Juliet demonstrated the ability to disappear into mist and reappear elsewhere during combat. She used this immediately after a monster attempted to retaliate against her assassination strike, reappearing in the air before drawing her bow.[1]
- Decapitation: Juliet possesses enough offensive power to instantly remove the head of a ten-meter-class monster while moving at high speed.[3]
- Combat Analysis: Juliet rapidly evaluates enemy roles and abilities and prioritizes creatures whose presence could destabilize the battle. This distinguishes her role from the more direct attackers of the Valkyrie formation.[1]
- Battlefield Awareness: Her assassination role requires Juliet to continuously scan enemy positions, vulnerabilities and priority targets while maintaining awareness of the rest of the Valkyrie formation.
- Team Combat: Juliet is extensively trained to operate alongside the other Valkyries rather than fighting exclusively as an independent assassin.
- Frontline Support: During formation combat, Juliet, Dorothy and Judith use their lighter armor and superior mobility to support Eleanor and Anrietha, who attract and withstand the majority of enemy pressure.[2]
- Ranged Coordination: Juliet and Judith can operate together as the Valkyries' long-distance and anti-air support team.[2]
- Tactical Discipline: Juliet is willing to correct other Valkyries when their excitement causes inefficient behavior, as demonstrated when she ordered Dorothy to stop forcing unsuccessful assassination attacks against increasingly durable monsters.[1]

### Equipment
- Twin Daggers: Juliet's principal assassination weapons. She uses them to strike exposed vital locations such as the throat and brain of large monsters.[1]
- Black Bow: Juliet carries a large black bow capable of producing supernatural energy arrows rather than relying upon physical ammunition.[1]
- Valkyrie Armor: Juliet wears the specialized black armor used by Clan Adrasteia's Valkyries. Her equipment is lighter than that of the formation's principal tanks, preserving the speed required for assassination and ranged support.[2] The armor used by Clan Adrasteia's elite warriors is also designed to accommodate the altered physiology produced by their Monster transformations.
- Personalized Divine Weapon: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and [[Hephaestus]] later created personalized high-level artifacts for each of the six Valkyries. Juliet received equipment specifically designed for her individual fighting style and immediately expressed that she loved the gift.[7] The exact form and properties of Juliet's personalized artifact were not individually described in the chapter.

### Trivia
- Juliet is explicitly the assassin of the Valkyries; in Chapter 452 she tells Dorothy that assassination is her job before personally hunting down a priority monster.[1]
- She fights using both twin daggers and a large black bow, making her one of the most versatile weapon users among the Valkyries.[1]
- Juliet and Alexa are explicitly stated to be the fastest members of the Valkyries.[3]
- Juliet is one of the Valkyries assigned to long-distance and anti-air support, usually operating alongside Judith when the formation requires ranged combat.[2]
- She is naturally much more suspicious of outsiders than the other Valkyries. The fact that Juliet could behave normally around [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was specifically used by the others as evidence that he had earned the group's trust.[4]
- Juliet was the primary instigator behind the Valkyries' Solomon Plan and the person who openly described Eleanor marrying Victor as effectively becoming a "1 and 7" package involving the Valkyries.[5]
- She was also the Valkyrie who attempted to include Rose in the arrangement and encouraged her commander to have a child with Victor, which resulted in Rose striking her on the head.[5]
- Juliet regularly pushes her teasing far beyond the other Valkyries but is perceptive enough to recognize when she should stop. When Anrietha entered a serious protective state in Chapter 479, Juliet immediately abandoned her attempt to tease her.[5]
- By Chapter 900, Juliet remained deeply resentful toward the monsters responsible for nearly destroying Clan Adrasteia's main lineage and had difficulty accepting Eleanor's decision to enslave rather than exterminate their surviving enemies.[7]
- Although Juliet and the other Valkyries openly showed interest in Victor, Chapter 1096 establishes that their primary goals remained becoming stronger and accompanying Eleanor and Rose rather than relentlessly pursuing a romantic relationship with him.[8]
