#### Debut

Judith Adrasteia, commonly called Judy, is a Vampire-Monster Balanced Hybrid of Clan Adrasteia and one of the elite Valkyries serving [[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]] under the command of [[Rose Adrasteia Elderblood]]. Judith is the Valkyries' principal firearms specialist and heavy ranged-support combatant, utilizing an oversized anti-monster rifle together with a pair of handguns capable of transforming through her Monster bloodline into extremely destructive Energy weapons.[1][2]
Her offensive output is among the greatest of the six Valkyries. When fully empowering her twin handguns, Judith can fire concentrated beams powerful enough to completely vaporize large monsters or pierce entire formations of Behemoths, although the tremendous Energy output places severe strain upon her weapons.[3][4]

### Physical Description
Judith is a tall, pale-skinned woman with long blue hair and brown eyes.[1][5] This description is repeated much later when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] reunites with the Valkyries, where Judith is again explicitly described as a blue-haired, brown-eyed Valkyrie.[6]
During combat Judith wears the specialized black armor of Clan Adrasteia's Valkyries. Her armor is lighter than that used by the group's principal tanks, allowing her to rapidly reposition herself while maintaining long-range fire support.[7]

### Personality
Judith normally possesses a comparatively cool and composed demeanor. During the Valkyries' first major battle alongside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], she calmly calculated their distance from Warfall while preparing her rifle and was specifically described as speaking in a cold tone.[1] Despite this outward composure, Judith is still an enthusiastic warrior and can become visibly excited when given the opportunity to unleash the full power of her weapons.
Her excitement occasionally causes her to use considerably more force than necessary. During the expedition, Judith completely vaporized one of the monsters she attacked, causing Dorothy to complain that she was destroying their food. Judith immediately laughed, apologized and agreed to lower her power output.[2]
Judith is pragmatic and observant. When the Valkyries discovered Victor's increasingly abnormal abilities, she was one of the women who directly questioned what he truly was. After Rose revealed her conclusion that Victor was a second Vampire Progenitor, Judith immediately understood one of the implications and asked whether this meant Victor represented the beginning of an entirely separate Vampire lineage from Vlad's.[7]
She is also considerably more direct about her attraction toward Victor than several of the other Valkyries. When Victor declared that he would invade either Hell or Heaven rather than allow the Valkyries to remain dead, Judith openly told Rose that if Eleanor refused to make a move on him, she was seriously considering taking the initiative herself.[7]
Judith nevertheless maintains a strong sense of loyalty toward Eleanor, Rose and her fellow Valkyries. When Rose's attempt to test Victor's motives damaged his respect for her, Judith sided with Alexa in affirming that Victor's intentions toward the group had been sincere from the beginning.[8]
Her loyalty to Rose also influenced the Valkyries' later romantic discussions. When Juliet revived the group's so-called Solomon Plan and attempted to include Rose in the arrangement, Judith was the first Valkyrie to openly state that she would participate if Rose did. Alexa, Martha and eventually the remaining Valkyries followed her declaration.[9]

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Judith was introduced as one of the six Valkyries serving Eleanor and Rose following Eleanor's return to Warfall. As a member of the Countess' personal elite unit, Judith belonged to the small group of warriors regularly trusted to fight the powerful creatures inhabiting the wilderness surrounding Clan Adrasteia's territory.[10]
Her combat specialization was properly revealed when [[Victor Elderblood]] later joined Eleanor, Rose and the Valkyries on an extended expedition through [[Nightingale]]'s monster territory. After Dorothy's attempted decapitation of a powerful Behemoth failed because of its increasingly durable shell, Judith immediately drew an enormous rifle-like weapon from her back and aimed toward the monster.[1]
Judith fired a single shot through the Behemoth's head, creating a large opening in its armor. Dorothy immediately exploited the damage by driving her twin swords through the creature's heart and permanently killing it. The exchange demonstrated the Valkyries' coordinated combat style, with Judith using heavy ranged fire to breach defenses that her faster companions could then exploit.[1]
The weapon itself was specifically designed to penetrate the extraordinarily thick carapaces of Nightingale's monsters and therefore used extremely large physical projectiles. Judith was capable of reloading those oversized rounds with remarkable dexterity, preparing another shot within only a few seconds.[2]
After exhausting the rifle's available ammunition, Judith switched to the two handguns carried at her waist. When she began channeling the power of her Monster bloodline, both her armor and weapons transformed. The armor partially merged with the handguns and radically altered their structure, demonstrating the same adaptive relationship between the Adrasteia bloodline and their equipment displayed by the other Valkyries.[2]
Judith aimed both transformed guns toward a monster and fired. Rather than ordinary bullets, two green beams emerged from the weapons and combined in flight into a single enormous Energy blast. The resulting explosion completely erased the target. Judith was visibly pleased by the result until Dorothy complained that completely vaporizing their prey meant destroying the meat they intended to collect for food.[2]
Judith apologized and subsequently reduced her output, switching to smaller green Energy shots while entering the battlefield directly. The exchange demonstrated that Judith could regulate the destructive force of her transformed firearms rather than being limited to their maximum output.[2]
Rose later explained the principle behind the Valkyries' unusual ranged weapons. While Judith's heavy rifle relied upon physical ammunition, weapons such as Judith's transformed handguns and Juliet's bow could use the wielder's own supernatural Energy as a catalyst. This allowed Judith to continue firing her twin weapons without conventional bullets so long as she possessed enough Energy to sustain them.[2]
As the expedition progressed beyond the regions normally explored by Clan Adrasteia, Judith helped analyze Victor's abnormal capabilities. When Victor proved capable of consuming monster flesh despite the substance normally being poisonous to Noble Vampires, Judith explained that the Valkyries were immune because Monster mutations had been incorporated into their own bloodline. She consequently found Victor's immunity particularly strange.[7]
Rose used Victor's numerous anomalies—the powers of several [[Vampire Counts]], his extraordinarily rapid growth, unusual transformations and Blood abilities—to explain her belief that he was the Second Vampire Progenitor. Judith immediately asked whether this made Victor the originator of a completely separate Vampire lineage, which Rose confirmed.[7]
After the group reached approximately 30,000 kilometers from Warfall, Victor began temporarily coordinating the Valkyries' formation himself. When flying Wyvern-class monsters approached the group, he assigned Judith and Juliet responsibility for hostile aerial targets and long-range battlefield support.[7]
Judith and Juliet immediately began attacking the Wyverns from range. Victor later ordered both women onto the roofs of the local monster settlement, where they continued clearing the skies while Eleanor, Anrietha and Dorothy formed the main defensive group below. Judith's lighter armor and ranged weaponry made her well suited to this mobile support position.[7]
The expedition eventually escalated into a major conflict against coordinated monster hordes. When the group returned to combat following a near-fatal trap, Victor specifically instructed Judith and Juliet to continuously observe the skies and immediately report any Dragon-class monsters.[3]
During the ensuing battle, Eleanor ordered Judith and Juliet to provide covering fire while the close-range Valkyries entered the monster formation. Judith transformed her twin Deagles more completely than before. Her hands themselves began changing while the weapons merged with them, developing spikes and radiating green power.[3]
Judith then activated an attack known as Predatory Extinction. Her Monster eyes began glowing while a dark aura surrounded her body, after which a gigantic red beam erupted from her weapons toward an approaching Behemoth formation. The leading twenty-meter Behemoth initially attempted to resist the attack using the armored shell covering its head, prompting Judith to increase the weapon's output.[3]
The intensified beam pierced the Behemoth and continued through every monster positioned behind it, creating a straight line of destruction through the approaching horde. Judith casually celebrated the attack afterward, demonstrating how accustomed the Valkyries had become to extremely destructive battlefield techniques.[3]
The technique possessed a significant drawback. After using it, Judith's transformation receded and her Deagles returned to their ordinary shape, revealing visible flaws in the weapons. The guns had been unable to comfortably withstand Judith's full power output. Judith estimated that she could use the attack only twice more before the weapons completely broke, leading Rose to order her to preserve the remaining uses for another Behemoth horde or an even stronger monster.[4]
Judith nevertheless confidently stated that her own destructive capability did not fall far behind the large-scale attacks being demonstrated around her. Rose clarified that the primary difference between Judith and Vampire Counts was not necessarily whether she could cause enormous destruction but that Counts could repeatedly produce such attacks without suffering comparable exhaustion or equipment limitations.[4]
Near the expedition's conclusion, Judith participated in the discussion surrounding Clan Adrasteia's true nature. When Juliet aggressively challenged the idea of older Vampires attempting to destroy them for their "impure" blood, Judith responded more calmly, stating that Warfall's defenses were effectively impenetrable while still displaying obvious disdain toward most of Nightingale's traditional Vampires.[8]
After Rose's attempt to test Victor's motives backfired, Judith and Alexa directly affirmed that Victor's intentions had been pure from the beginning. Judith also agreed that Juliet's unusual willingness to relax around Victor demonstrated just how much trust he had earned from the normally suspicious Valkyrie.[8]
During the return journey, Juliet revived the Valkyries' Solomon Plan. When Juliet attempted to include Rose in the proposed arrangement, Judith unexpectedly became the first to openly commit, stating that if Rose participated, she would as well. Alexa, Martha and the other Valkyries subsequently followed her declaration.[9]

#### Demonic War
Judith later fought alongside Eleanor, Rose and the other Valkyries during the wider Demonic conflict. When an unexpected enemy force ambushed the group through portals, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] rapidly removed each Valkyrie from the path of otherwise lethal attacks before Rose restored their formation.[5]
During the battle, Judith continued operating as one of the formation's ranged combatants. After Victor used white flames against a group of Predators, Judith immediately recognized that several of the creatures were beginning to recover. She drew the firearm carried on her back and repeatedly shot the regenerating monsters, helping finish them while Victor dealt with the greater threat.[5]
This battle again describes Judith as a woman with blue hair and brown eyes, further establishing these as her canonical physical characteristics.[5]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Judith remained one of the Valkyries following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s rise as the Dragon God of [[Chaos]]. When he later visited them during Clan Adrasteia's relocation preparations, Victor immediately recognized that all six women had become considerably stronger following their previous wars.[6]
After Victor introduced [[Metis]] Elderblood, Judith confirmed that Eleanor and Rose had previously told the Valkyries about the former Titan Goddess. The group nevertheless remained visibly uncomfortable around Metis because, despite her non-combat specialization, she had become a genuine True Dragon.[6]
Victor then revealed that he and [[Hephaestus]] had personally created new weapons for each Valkyrie. Judith received a new pair of black Deagles specifically designed around her fighting style.[6]
Judith immediately began testing them by rapidly spinning both weapons before firing at a training target constructed from monster materials. The shots completely annihilated not only the target but also the wall behind it, surprising Judith herself with the weapons' penetration and destructive power.[6]
The firearms were similar in principle to the weapon used by [[Nero Elderblood]], although Victor explained that Judith's versions were specifically personalized for her. Their overwhelming penetration was great enough that Victor immediately warned Judith to be careful where she fired them.[6]
Judith was extremely pleased with the weapons, describing them as the best gift while the Valkyries collectively thanked Victor. Unlike the equipment she had used during the expedition—which visibly began breaking when forced to contain her full output—the new weapons were deliberately created by Victor and Hephaestus around Judith's individual power and fighting style.[6]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Judith and the other Valkyries remained members of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s established inner circle even after the Elderblood family expanded enormously. When [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] later considered the women who might realistically still become involved with Victor, the Valkyries were distinguished from unknown outsiders because they already possessed a long personal history with him.[11]
Despite their attraction toward Victor, Judith and the others did not make pursuing him their primary objective. Their main priorities remained increasing their strength and continuing to accompany Eleanor and Rose, with the six women functioning more like inseparable sisters than ordinary military subordinates.[11]

### Skills and Abilities
Judith is the Valkyries' primary firearms specialist and one of their main long-range support combatants. Her combat style combines exceptional marksmanship with specialized weapons designed to channel the enormous Energy output generated by her Vampire-Monster bloodline.
- Noble Vampire Physiology: As a Noble Vampire, Judith possesses supernatural physical attributes, enhanced senses, longevity and regenerative capabilities far beyond those of ordinary humans. Her natural Vampiric physiology is further altered by Clan Adrasteia's Monster-derived bloodline.
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics: Judith possesses enhanced strength, speed, reflexes, durability and stamina sufficient to operate alongside the other Valkyries against [[Nightingale]]'s massive monster populations.
- Enhanced Reflexes: Judith can rapidly acquire new targets, reload an oversized anti-monster rifle and transition between completely different weapons in the middle of high-speed battles.
- Enhanced Senses: Her altered Adrasteia physiology grants senses superior to those of conventional Noble Vampires.[12]
- Regeneration: As a Noble Vampire, Judith can recover from physical injuries far beyond the survivability of ordinary humans.
- Monster Physiology: Judith belongs to the elite line of Clan Adrasteia produced through the Ancient Rebirth Ritual, which fused Noble Vampire blood with the highly adaptive biology of Nightingale's monsters.[8]
- True Monster Form: Eleanor confirmed that her Valkyries belong to the select members of Clan Adrasteia capable of manifesting genuine Monster Forms rather than merely possessing diluted hybrid characteristics.[8]
- Partial Monster Transformation: Judith can transform individual portions of her body while fighting. Her hands visibly change when she channels greater levels of her Monster power into her firearms.[3]
- Monster Eyes: Judith's eyes become more monstrous and can glow when she increases her power output.[3]
- Physical Amplification: Activating the Monster side of her bloodline increases the supernatural power she can channel through her equipment.
- Monster Flesh and Blood Resistance: Judith explicitly explains that because Monster mutations exist in the Adrasteia bloodline, the venom contained within Nightingale's monsters is not poisonous to them in the manner it is to normal Noble Vampires.[7]
- Regeneration Negation: The unique properties inherited from Nightingale's monsters allow Clan Adrasteia's warriors to permanently kill the otherwise extraordinarily regenerative creatures surrounding Warfall.[13]
- Unique Energy: Judith possesses the specialized supernatural Energy created by the Adrasteia Vampire-Monster bloodline. Unlike her heavy rifle, which uses physical ammunition, her transformed handguns can use this Energy directly as a catalyst for their attacks.[2]
- Expert Marksmanship: Judith is an exceptionally skilled firearms user and the principal gunner of the Valkyrie squad.
- Precision Shooting: Judith can accurately target vulnerable sections of gigantic monsters. During her first demonstrated battle, a single rifle shot pierced the head armor of a Behemoth that Dorothy had failed to decapitate.[1]
- Rapid Target Acquisition: She can rapidly move between aerial, ground and long-range targets while maintaining effective fire.
- High-Speed Reloading: Despite her anti-monster rifle using exceptionally large physical projectiles, Judith can replace the ammunition and prepare another shot within seconds.[2]
- Anti-Air Marksmanship: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] specifically assigned Judith and Juliet responsibility for destroying Wyvern-class monsters and maintaining long-range control of the skies.[7]
- Long-Range Support: Judith frequently operates from elevated or rear positions and provides covering fire for the Valkyries fighting at close range.[7][3]
- Anti-Armor Shooting: Judith's giant rifle is specifically designed to pierce the thick carapaces covering Nightingale's advanced monsters. A shot from the weapon penetrated a Behemoth's head defenses and created an opening large enough for Dorothy to finish the creature.[1][2]
- Energy Projection: Judith's transformed handguns can replace conventional bullets with concentrated blasts of supernatural Energy.
- Green Energy Beams: During the expedition, each transformed Deagle emitted a green Energy beam rather than a physical projectile.[2]
- Beam Combination: The separate blasts fired from Judith's two weapons can combine in mid-flight into a single much larger beam.[2]
- Monster Vaporization: One combined blast completely erased a large monster rather than merely physically damaging it.[2]
- Variable Power Output: Judith can intentionally reduce the destructive output of her Energy weapons. After accidentally vaporizing prey intended to become food, she switched to smaller beams to avoid unnecessary destruction.[2]
- Weapon Transformation: Judith's Monster power interacts directly with her specialized firearms and armor.
- Armor-Weapon Fusion: When Judith activates her altered bloodline, portions of her armor merge directly into her handguns and radically change their appearance.[2]
- Hand-Weapon Fusion: At higher output, Judith's hands themselves begin transforming while merging with her Deagles, producing spiked weapons surrounded by green Energy.[3]
- Energy Catalysis: Her transformed weapons use Judith's personal supernatural power as the direct source of their projectiles, eliminating the need for conventional bullets while in this state.[2]
- Predatory Extinction: Judith's most destructive explicitly named technique. After merging her transformed hands with her Deagles and releasing a dark aura, Judith channels enormous power into both weapons and fires a gigantic concentrated beam.[3]
- Extreme Penetration: The beam overpowered the armored head of a twenty-meter Behemoth after Judith increased its output.[3]
- Line-of-Sight Destruction: After penetrating the first Behemoth, the beam continued through every monster positioned behind it, producing a straight line of destruction through the horde.[3]
- Power Amplification: Judith can increase the output of the attack after firing if its initial strength is insufficient to penetrate the target.[3]
- High Energy Consumption: Predatory Extinction requires an enormous output compared to Judith's normal attacks.
- Weapon Strain: Her original Deagles were incapable of comfortably supporting the technique. Following one use, the weapons displayed structural damage and had only approximately two additional maximum-output attacks remaining before they were expected to break completely.[4]
- High Destructive Capacity: Judith possesses enough raw offensive power to compare her destructive output to large-scale supernatural techniques, although Rose clarified that [[Vampire Counts]] retain the advantage of repeatedly using similarly destructive abilities without the same limitations.[4]
- Aerial Suppression: Judith can work together with Juliet to establish ranged control over the sky, destroying flying monsters before they can interfere with the Valkyries' frontline formation.[7]
- Regeneration Suppression Through Sustained Fire: During the Demonic War, Judith immediately fired repeatedly upon Predators that had begun regenerating after Victor's attack, preventing them from freely recovering while the others dealt with greater threats.[5]
- Team Combat: Judith is extensively trained to function as the ranged component of the Valkyries' coordinated formations.
- Fire Support: Her shots create openings which close-range fighters such as Dorothy can immediately exploit.[1]
- Ranged Coordination: Judith and Juliet frequently work together as the group's long-range and anti-air team.[7]
- Defensive Formation Support: While Eleanor and Anrietha attract the majority of enemy pressure, Judith can remain behind them and eliminate approaching Behemoths before they reach the frontline.[3]
- Navigation and Reconnaissance Equipment Proficiency: Judith was responsible for checking the group's distance from Warfall during the expedition using a wrist-mounted device programmed with the city's position. She accurately determined when the expedition had reached the previously unexplored 30,000-kilometer boundary.[7]

### Equipment
- Anti-Monster Rifle: A gigantic rifle-like weapon carried on Judith's back. Unlike her transformed handguns, the rifle uses real physical ammunition. It was specifically engineered to penetrate the extremely durable carapaces of [[Nightingale]]'s monsters and can punch holes through Behemoth-class defenses.[1][2]
- Twin Deagles: Judith's original secondary and primary Energy weapons. Under ordinary conditions they resemble large handguns carried at her waist, but her Adrasteia power can cause the weapons to merge with her armor and body, transforming them into much more powerful Energy cannons.[2]
- Their normal transformed attacks produce green Energy beams.
- The two separate beams can combine into one larger blast.
- At maximum output the weapons can perform Predatory Extinction.[3]
- The original weapons possessed insufficient durability to withstand repeated use of Judith's complete Energy output and began physically deteriorating after her strongest attacks.[4]
- Valkyrie Armor: Judith wears Clan Adrasteia's specialized black combat armor. Her armor is less robust than the equipment used by Eleanor and Anrietha, preserving the mobility necessary for a ranged fighter while still being capable of physically integrating with her transformed weapons.[2][7]
- Personalized [[Black Deagles]]: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and [[Hephaestus]] later created a new pair of high-level black Deagles specifically for Judith. Their power vastly surpassed her previous equipment; during her first test, the shots annihilated a monster-material training target together with the wall positioned behind it. Victor compared their basic principle to [[Nero]]'s firearm while clarifying that Judith's version had been individually designed for her.[6]

### Trivia
- The novel overwhelmingly refers to Judith by the nickname Judy during her actual dialogue and battle scenes.
- Judith's canonical appearance is long blue hair and brown eyes. She is explicitly described this way in Chapters 452, 646 and 900.[1][5][6]
- The previous wiki description listing Judith with black hair and blue eyes contradicts the repeated physical descriptions given in the novel.
- Judith is the main firearms specialist among the Valkyries and uses both an enormous anti-monster rifle and twin Deagles.[1][2]
- Her rifle and handguns function through different systems: the rifle uses real ammunition, while her transformed Deagles use her supernatural Energy as a catalyst.[2]
- Judith's transformed firearms initially fire green Energy beams, while her named Predatory Extinction technique produces an enormous red beam at maximum output.[2][3]
- Her original Deagles could not withstand her full strength; after using Predatory Extinction, Judith estimated she could fire the technique only twice more before the weapons broke.[4]
- Judith was the first Valkyrie to openly state that she would participate in Juliet's Solomon Plan if Rose participated, prompting Alexa, Martha and the rest of the group to follow.[9]
- After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] promised to invade Hell or Heaven to retrieve them if they died, Judith openly told Rose that she would take the initiative with Victor if Eleanor continued refusing to do so.[7]
- Victor and [[Hephaestus]] later solved the principal weakness of Judith's equipment by creating personalized Deagles specifically designed around her individual combat style and power.[6]
- When testing those weapons, Judith immediately destroyed both the training dummy and the wall behind it, causing Victor to warn her about their penetration.[6]
