Alexa Adrasteia is a Vampire-Monster Balanced Hybrid of Clan Adrasteia and one of the six elite Valkyries serving [[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]] and [[Rose Adrasteia Elderblood]]. She is explicitly identified as the spear user of the Valkyries and, together with [[Juliet Adrasteia]], is one of the fastest members of the group.[1][2] Alexa specializes in extremely fast spear combat, combining the physical attributes of Clan Adrasteia's Vampire-Monster bloodline with a spear capable of changing alongside her monstrous power.

### Physical Description
Alexa is a tall, pale-skinned woman with dark-green hair, which she wore in a ponytail when first introduced.[3] Her canonical hair color is explicitly dark green. In Chapter 468, the author directly clarified this and apologized for any earlier instance in which Alexa's hair may have been described as black.[2]
Like the other Valkyries, Alexa normally fights in the specialized full-body armor used by Clan Adrasteia's elite warriors. When utilizing the monstrous aspect of her bloodline, portions of her equipment and weapon can change together with her body, allowing her spear to take on a larger and more dangerous form.[4][1]

### Personality
Alexa is energetic, competitive, straightforward and highly battle-oriented. During her first appearance, she immediately volunteered to fight an approaching Alpha and began arguing with [[Dorothy Adrasteia]] over which of them should receive the prey. Dorothy complained that Alexa had already stolen her target during the previous month, only for Alexa to dismiss the complaint by joking that anyone who lived in the past belonged in a museum and that only the present mattered.[3]
She also has a mischievous and teasing personality. When Dorothy became so shocked by Eleanor's unusual behavior around [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] that she asked Alexa to slap her to check whether she was dreaming, Alexa immediately slapped her and then innocently reminded Dorothy that she had asked for it before walking away while whistling.[3]
Despite this casual behavior, Alexa is observant when assessing warriors. During the expedition, she noted that a person possessing Victor's strength would normally become extremely arrogant, but distinguished Victor's attitude as confidence rather than arrogance and admitted that she found this attractive. When Eleanor explained Victor's preference for powerful, battle-loving and possessive women, Alexa jokingly concluded that this gave their leader the greatest chance with him.[5]
Alexa is similarly willing to accept genuine instruction despite her pride as a Valkyrie. After Victor saved her from a subterranean monster and criticized the weakness in her battlefield awareness, she recognized that he was sincerely giving her advice and accepted the correction rather than becoming defensive.[6]
Her tendency to speak impulsively can occasionally cause problems. When Victor independently deduced that Clan Adrasteia's unusual physiology originated from an artificial ritual, Alexa blurted out her surprise that he had discovered the clan's deepest secret so easily. She immediately realized that her reaction had effectively confirmed Victor's theory, causing Dorothy to shout her name while Alexa cursed herself for speaking without thinking.[7]
Alexa is deeply loyal to Eleanor, Rose and the other Valkyries. Although she is willing to criticize Rose when necessary, she consistently approaches the group's future from the perspective of supporting their leaders. This became particularly apparent after Victor's rise, when Alexa revived the Valkyries' old "Solomon Plan" and argued that they needed to support Rose and Eleanor as the Snow, Fulger and Scarlett Clans became increasingly unified around Victor's family.[8]

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Alexa first appeared in Warfall when the Valkyries informed [[Rose Adrasteia Elderblood]] that a larger-than-normal monster horde was approaching the territory. Upon hearing that an Alpha accompanied the horde, both Alexa and Dorothy immediately wanted permission to hunt it. Dorothy complained that Alexa had stolen her prey the month before, revealing that competition over monster kills was already a regular occurrence between the two Valkyries.[3]
Alexa subsequently met [[Victor Elderblood]] when Eleanor returned to Warfall alongside him and the Scarlett sisters. Like the other Valkyries, she was surprised by Eleanor's behavior around Victor and witnessed the tremendous pressure he unconsciously displayed when irritated. She later watched Victor confront the incoming monster horde by himself.[3]
When additional monsters increased the danger of the battle, Alexa was already preparing to suggest that the Valkyries assist Victor. His subsequent display of power stopped her from doing so. After Victor froze an enormous portion of the battlefield, Alexa questioned Rose on whether such power was really normal for a Vampire Count. When the monsters began regenerating, Alexa also demonstrated familiarity with Behemoth tactics, explaining that the Valkyries normally prioritized eliminating Behemoths because their presence strengthened the surrounding monster forces.[9]
Much later, Alexa joined Victor, Eleanor, Rose and the other Valkyries on an extended expedition through [[Nightingale]]'s monster territory. Before the expedition's major battles began, Alexa observed Victor's behavior and distinguished his confidence from the arrogance normally displayed by powerful individuals, privately finding that aspect of his personality attractive.[5]
During the first major battle of the expedition, Alexa's combat specialization was fully demonstrated. When Eleanor called for Alexa and Martha to enter the battle, Alexa activated the monstrous properties of her equipment. Her spear changed shape, extending to approximately three meters in length while becoming significantly sharper. Alexa then hurled the weapon through a monster approximately twenty meters tall, piercing it before immediately appearing beside the spear and recovering it before it could fall to the ground.[4]
Alexa repeated the maneuver across the battlefield, throwing her spear through additional monsters and using her extreme speed to appear beside the weapon almost immediately after each attack. Victor was impressed enough to compare Alexa's movement speed to his own speed when he was not augmenting himself with Lightning. After finishing the sequence, Alexa casually spun the blood from her spear before disappearing toward another target.[4]
The following battle further established Alexa's position within the Valkyries. Eleanor explicitly identified Alexa and Juliet as the fastest members of the group. Victor also observed that the Valkyries were primarily relying upon the mutant abilities of their Adrasteia bloodline rather than conventional Noble Vampire powers. The manner in which their armor and weapons changed together was tied to that bloodline, with the Adrasteia power serving as a catalyst for their specialized equipment.[1]
Alexa's reliance on speed did not make her immune to being surprised. During a later engagement, Victor sensed centipede-like monsters travelling beneath the ground and noticed one approaching Alexa without her realizing it. He immediately grabbed Alexa and removed her from the location moments before the creature erupted from beneath her feet.[6]
After killing the monster, Victor criticized Alexa's awareness and explained that a spear user should treat the space surrounding herself as a complete sphere instead of focusing primarily upon the direction she was facing. Alexa immediately understood that he was giving her genuine combat advice and accepted it. Victor then decided that he would eventually ask [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], an expert spear user, to personally train Alexa.[6]
When Victor subsequently transformed the battlefield into his frozen Ice World, Alexa was the first Valkyrie to move. She grabbed one of the spears Victor had created from ice and hurled it with enough force to pierce through every monster positioned in front of her. After observing the weapon's surprising durability, she used her speed to continue fighting and easily killed a Behemoth immobilized within Victor's frozen battlefield.[6]
The expedition gradually exposed more of Clan Adrasteia's hidden physiology. Alexa and the other Valkyries possessed dangerous monster bloodlines derived from some of the strongest monsters historically defeated by the clan. Throughout most of the expedition, they deliberately avoided completely exposing their true monster forms and instead limited themselves to partial transformations involving their limbs and weapons.[10]
When Eleanor eventually revealed her complete monstrous transformation, Victor reacted with fascination rather than revulsion. During the following discussion, Alexa claimed that entering their monster forms caused the Valkyries' instincts to become stronger and therefore made them more honest about their desires. Dorothy immediately accused her of using this as an excuse, and Alexa admitted that she was partly exaggerating.[7]
Victor then began examining the logic behind the Adrasteia bloodline. He realized that a naturally reproducing Vampire lineage should not have developed such radically different monster characteristics and deduced that the clan's ancestors must have used some form of artificial procedure. Alexa, shocked by how quickly he reached the conclusion, inadvertently confirmed that he had discovered the clan's greatest secret. Eleanor was consequently forced to explain the ritual that had fused Vampire and monster blood to create Clan Adrasteia's Balanced Hybrid lineage.[7]
The same explanation revealed that the complete monster transformations belonged primarily to the clan's main family and its closest elite members, including Eleanor's Valkyries and butler, while more distant members possessed increasingly diluted versions of the bloodline.[7]
After Rose tested Victor by implying that his interest in Eleanor might be motivated by gaining access to Clan Adrasteia's ritual, Victor was disappointed by her suspicion and temporarily withdrew from the discussion. Alexa was among the Valkyries who believed Rose had unnecessarily complicated the situation. She advised Rose to simply apologize and continue training Victor, describing him as comparatively straightforward to understand once his priorities were known.[7]
Alexa's attraction toward Victor became considerably more explicit by the expedition's conclusion. When the Valkyries discussed their long-running "Solomon Plan", Juliet joked that if their leader married Victor it would effectively become a "buy one, get seven" arrangement because the Valkyries intended to remain with their Valkyrie Queen. Alexa initially insulted Juliet for the way she phrased the idea, but when Judith stated that she would participate if Rose did, Alexa immediately answered that she would as well. The remaining Valkyries expressed the same position.[11]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Alexa remained one of the Valkyries as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction expanded and the members of Clan Adrasteia continued becoming stronger. When Victor later reunited with the group, he immediately noticed that all six Valkyries had developed considerably since their earlier expedition.[8]
Alexa retained her blunt sense of humor. Together with Dorothy, she teased Victor over the reason Eleanor and Rose were temporarily absent, causing Anrietha to accuse the pair of being jealous. Their relationship with Victor had become sufficiently familiar that the Valkyries could openly joke with him without the formal distance that had existed during their first encounters.[8]
Victor and [[Hephaestus]] had also created personalized equipment for each Valkyrie. Alexa received a new spear specifically crafted for her fighting style. She was deeply affected by the gift because it had not simply been purchased or distributed from existing equipment; Victor had personally participated in creating weapons designed to make each of them stronger. Alexa internally recognized that, from a warrior's perspective, receiving such a personalized weapon could almost be interpreted like a marriage proposal, although she acknowledged that she was exaggerating the comparison.[8]
While examining her new spear, Alexa seriously approached Anrietha and asked whether their old Solomon Plan was still in effect. After watching Victor personally assist Martha with her new weapon, Anrietha agreed that the idea might deserve to be put into practice. Alexa then argued that the Valkyries should support Rose and Eleanor just as the Snow, Fulger and Scarlett families were increasingly forming a united front around Victor.[8]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Even after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family expanded enormously, Alexa and the other Valkyries remained members of his established inner circle rather than becoming distant allies. When [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] considered which women might realistically still become involved with Victor, she specifically regarded the Valkyries differently from unknown outsiders because of their long-standing relationship with him.[12]
The Valkyries were described as getting along extremely well with Victor. Although they had openly displayed interest in him and repeatedly joked about joining his harem, they had not made pursuing him their main priority. Instead, Alexa and the others remained focused on becoming stronger and accompanying Rose and Eleanor wherever they went, reflecting the sister-like bond that kept the Valkyries effectively inseparable.[12]

### Skills and Abilities
Alexa is an elite Valkyrie specializing in spear combat and high-speed movement. Her fighting style combines Clan Adrasteia's Vampire-Monster physiology with a mutated spear and the extensive monster-hunting experience expected of Warfall's elite warriors.
- Noble Vampire Physiology: Alexa is a Noble Vampire and possesses the supernatural physical abilities, senses, longevity and regenerative characteristics of her species. During the Adrasteia expedition, however, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] specifically observed that Alexa and the other Valkyries relied primarily upon their mutated monster bloodline rather than their conventional Noble Vampire abilities.[1]
- Monster Physiology: Alexa belongs to Clan Adrasteia's main combat lineage and possesses the Balanced Hybrid physiology created when the clan's ancestors fused Vampire blood with the highly adaptive blood of [[Nightingale]]'s monsters.[7]
- Enhanced Physical Attributes: Clan Adrasteia's hybrids possess bodies naturally stronger than those of ordinary Nightingale Noble Vampires, with their physical capabilities being compared to those of [[Werewolves]].[13]
- Enhanced Senses: Members of the Adrasteia bloodline possess unusually refined senses compared to ordinary Noble Vampires.[13]
- Partial Monster Transformation: Alexa can utilize the monstrous characteristics of her bloodline without remaining in a complete monster state. During the expedition, the Valkyries deliberately restricted themselves primarily to partial transformations involving their arms, bodies and weapons.[10]
- Monster Form: Alexa is one of Eleanor's Valkyries and therefore belongs to the elite main-family group identified as possessing genuine monster forms rather than merely the diluted characteristics seen in more distant Adrasteia members.[7]
- Enhanced Instincts: Alexa explained that their instincts become more pronounced while using their monster forms, although she jokingly exaggerated how much this could be used to excuse their behavior.[7]
- Monster Flesh and Blood Adaptation: Clan Adrasteia's altered physiology allows its members to consume the blood and flesh of Nightingale's monsters and Demonic Beasts as normal nourishment. Human blood provides comparatively little benefit to them, while monster blood contains far greater nutritional value for their bodies.[13][14]
- Regeneration Negation: Nightingale's monsters possess extreme regenerative abilities that allow them to recover even after apparently fatal destruction. Clan Adrasteia's monster-derived power allows its members to permanently kill these creatures, preventing the regeneration that occurs when they are attacked by conventional means.[9]
- Unique Energy: Alexa possesses the unusual energy generated by Clan Adrasteia's mutated bloodline. Rather than functioning only as conventional Vampiric power, the clan uses this energy as a catalyst for its specialized equipment and monster-derived abilities.[9][1]
- [[Spears|Spear]] Mastery: Alexa is explicitly identified as the spear user of the Valkyries and is highly proficient with the weapon.[2]
- High-Speed Spear Throwing: Alexa can use her spear as an extremely powerful projectile. During the expedition, she threw it completely through a monster approximately twenty meters tall and immediately appeared beside the weapon to retrieve it before it reached the ground.[4]
- Rapid Weapon Retrieval: Alexa repeatedly alternated between throwing and recovering her spear across the battlefield, using her extreme movement speed to reach its destination almost as soon as the attack itself.[4]
- Spear Penetration: Her throws possess sufficient force to pierce gigantic Nightingale monsters outright.[4]
- Ice Spear Usage: Alexa can immediately adapt to unfamiliar spears. Inside Victor's Ice World, she seized one of his magically created ice spears and threw it through an entire line of monsters before using it to kill a trapped Behemoth.[6]
- Spherical Battlefield Awareness: Victor personally instructed Alexa to perceive the space surrounding herself as a complete sphere rather than concentrating only on the direction of her current opponent. The advice was specifically given to correct a weakness in Alexa's awareness as a spear fighter after a subterranean monster almost ambushed her.[6]
- Enhanced Speed: Speed is one of Alexa's defining combat attributes. Victor compared her movement speed during the expedition to his own unenhanced speed without the use of Lightning, while Eleanor explicitly identified Alexa and Juliet as the fastest Valkyries.[4][1]
- Weapon Transformation: Alexa's spear can change under the influence of her Adrasteia bloodline. During battle it grew to approximately three meters in length while becoming noticeably sharper before she began using it against the monster horde.[4]
- Armor-Weapon Integration: The Valkyries' specialized equipment can change together with their monster-derived powers. A later transformation of Judith's equipment is explicitly compared to Alexa's earlier transformation, with the armor merging or changing alongside the weapon under the effect of the Adrasteia bloodline.[1]
- Experienced Monster Hunter: Alexa has extensive experience fighting Nightingale's monsters around Warfall. Even during her debut she casually competed with Dorothy for the right to kill an Alpha and was already familiar with the tactical importance of eliminating Behemoths before their strengthening effects could make a horde more dangerous.[3][9]
- Combat Analysis: Alexa can rapidly assess the battlefield and the capabilities of both allies and monsters. She recognized the tactical problem caused by Behemoths, evaluated Victor's power relative to the level expected of a Count and understood the effect of his frozen battlefield quickly enough to immediately exploit the newly created ice weapons.[9][6]
- Team Combat: As one of the Valkyries, Alexa possesses extensive experience fighting within a coordinated elite formation. Victor noted that the Valkyries' teamwork was highly refined, with each member naturally covering the weaknesses and openings of the others during battle.[1]

### Equipment
- [[Spears|Spear]]: Alexa's primary weapon and the basis of her fighting style. Her original spear responds to her monster-derived bloodline, extending to approximately three meters and becoming sharper when activated.[4]
- Valkyrie Armor: Alexa uses the specialized armor of Clan Adrasteia's Valkyries. The equipment is designed to interact with the user's monster bloodline rather than acting as ordinary protective armor.[4][1]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Personalized Spear: Victor and [[Hephaestus]] later created a new high-level spear specifically for Alexa. Like the personalized artifacts distributed to the other Valkyries, it was designed around its intended wielder rather than being a generic weapon. Alexa was visibly moved by the amount of consideration Victor had placed into creating it for her.[8]
