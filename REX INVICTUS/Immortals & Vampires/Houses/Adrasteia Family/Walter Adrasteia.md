### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 70
Walter Adrasteia is an ancient Elder Vampire, Vampire-Monster Balanced Hybrid, and one of the most senior members of Clan Adrasteia. He serves [[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]] as her butler, adviser and teacher and is traditionally known as the Shield of Clan Adrasteia. Together with [[Rose Adrasteia Elderblood]], the Clan's "Sword", Walter forms the pair of ancient Masters responsible for supporting the current leader of the Clan.[1]
Walter's title does not simply refer to his combat strength. Whereas Rose specializes primarily in military affairs and swordsmanship, Walter's principal responsibilities concern the internal functioning of Clan Adrasteia. He has mastered fields such as etiquette, leadership, politics and numerous other subjects necessary for advising and educating the Clan's ruler. The narration consequently describes him as the embodiment of the "perfect butler", someone who is constantly available to advise, teach and assist Clan Adrasteia's leader.[1]
Despite this administrative role, Walter is also an exceptionally dangerous warrior. He is one of the two ancient Adrasteia Vampires recognized as Master-level martial artists, with Walter specifically specializing in close combat. His fists are considered deadly, his physique greatly exceeds that of an ordinary Noble Vampire, and even Alpha [[Werewolves]] are stated to have difficulty dealing with him physically. [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] herself acknowledged that Walter would be difficult for her to fight if he became serious.[1]

### Physical Appearance
Walter appears as an elderly man with white hair, maintaining the dignified appearance expected of a senior butler and adviser. When he was first properly shown during Clan Adrasteia's journey to the Royal Capital, Walter was initially wearing the armor and helmet of one of Eleanor's soldiers before removing his helmet and revealing the face of an old man.[2]
His visibly aged appearance is unusual among ancient Vampires, but the novel has never explained whether Walter naturally developed this appearance, deliberately maintains an elderly form, or possesses some other reason for looking considerably older than most Noble Vampires. The previous assumption that he simply changed his appearance because Vampires do not normally age has therefore not been treated as confirmed information.

### Personality
Walter is calm, restrained and highly professional. Thousands of years of experience have made him considerably more cautious than many powerful Vampires, and he generally prefers avoiding pointless confrontations when there is nothing meaningful to gain from them. This is particularly apparent in his dealings with [[Scathach]]. Walter had previously fallen for one of her provocations and subsequently spent an entire month dealing with the battle-hungry Countess, an experience he had absolutely no desire to repeat. When Scathach attempted to provoke him again, Walter deliberately responded with humility rather than allowing his pride to draw him into another fight.[1]
His caution should not be mistaken for cowardice. Walter is an ancient Master fully aware of his own capabilities; he simply possesses enough experience to recognize unnecessary danger. He is observant, analytical and discreet, taking note of information without immediately revealing everything he has noticed and preferring to investigate suspicious matters privately rather than creating an avoidable confrontation.[1]
Walter is deeply loyal to Clan Adrasteia and particularly to Eleanor, but his loyalty is not expressed through controlling her decisions. When Scathach questioned why he had allowed Eleanor to participate in an extremely dangerous expedition beyond [[WarFall]]'s established defensive territory, Walter explained that Eleanor and the others were adults capable of making their own choices and that both he and Rose would continue supporting their master's decisions. At the same time, he had taken precautions by ensuring Rose carried one of the Clan's most important defensive assets, demonstrating the balance between his respect for Eleanor's independence and his responsibility as her Shield.[1]
His identity as a servant is also closely connected to his role as an educator. Walter does not merely obey Eleanor's instructions; he advises her, corrects her when necessary and spent years helping prepare her for the responsibilities of leadership. Alongside Rose, he continued Eleanor's development after Scathach had provided her martial foundation, ultimately helping transform the young heiress into both a capable warrior and the leader of Clan Adrasteia.[1]

### Synopsis

#### Vampire Society
Walter first appeared when [[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]] and her black-armored forces accompanied the group traveling toward the Royal Capital. When Eleanor required her soldiers to be reorganized, she called for Walter. One of the armored Vampires riding beside her carriage removed his helmet and revealed himself to be an elderly man, addressing Eleanor formally and immediately accepting her order.[2]
Eleanor instructed Walter to take the army to her personal mansion outside the Royal Capital rather than bringing the entire military force farther into the city. Walter immediately began issuing instructions to the knights, demonstrating that despite being best known for his domestic and administrative responsibilities, he possessed sufficient authority and military experience to organize Clan Adrasteia's soldiers directly when required.[2]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Walter's true importance within Clan Adrasteia was revealed much later during the Warfall expedition. While Eleanor, Rose, [[Victor Elderblood]] and the Valkyries were operating far beyond the city's ordinary defensive perimeter, [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] arrived at Eleanor's residence. Walter received her personally, and their conversation revealed that the two had known one another for a considerable period of time. [[Scathach]] immediately noticed that Walter had become stronger since their previous meeting, while Walter carefully avoided accepting her attempts to elevate his strength above hers.[1]
The narration subsequently identified Walter as an Elder Vampire more than three thousand years old and one of Clan Adrasteia's two martial Masters. Rose represented the Clan's Sword and had dedicated herself primarily to swordsmanship and military affairs, while Walter represented its Shield. Walter had reached [[Poseidon|the Master]] level through close combat, possessing fists dangerous enough that Scathach herself would have difficulty fighting him seriously. His abnormal physique was also emphasized, being far superior to an ordinary Noble Vampire and formidable enough to trouble even Alpha [[Werewolves]].[1]
Walter's non-combat abilities were described as being equally important. He had mastered etiquette, politics, leadership and thousands of other subjects connected to managing a powerful Noble household. Rose and Walter were deliberately complementary: although each understood enough of the other's specialty to function independently, Rose principally handled the military side of Clan Adrasteia while Walter specialized in its domestic and political affairs. Their ultimate responsibility was the same—to support whoever led the Clan.[1]
This arrangement had played a major role in Eleanor's development. Scathach had originally trained Eleanor but ended her direct instruction earlier than usual because Eleanor already possessed both a strong foundation and two exceptionally competent teachers. Walter and Rose subsequently refined that foundation, developing Eleanor into a warrior while, more importantly, teaching her how to function as the leader of Clan Adrasteia.[1]
Walter also demonstrated his role as [[WarFall]]'s information and command center while Eleanor was absent. He knew where Pepper, Lacus, Victor, Eleanor and the other expedition members were operating and informed Scathach that Victor and Eleanor's group intended to travel beyond the thirty-thousand-kilometer boundary. When Scathach questioned the danger, Walter calmly explained that Rose and he supported Eleanor's decision rather than preventing their leader from taking risks simply because they were worried about her.[1]
Nevertheless, Walter had prepared for the possibility of disaster. He arranged for Rose to carry one of Clan Adrasteia's treasures during the expedition and judged that ordinary Alpha-class Monsters were not a serious enough threat to require intervention. He acknowledged that native Gods represented a more legitimate danger, although he remained confident that Rose could protect the expedition unless something on the level of an Elder God appeared. The discussion demonstrated that Walter's caution did not mean automatically choosing the safest possible action; instead, he assessed threats, prepared contingencies and allowed Eleanor to make her own decisions as Clan leader.[1]

### Skills and Abilities
Walter is one of the most accomplished non-Progenitor Vampires produced by old [[Nightingale]]. His combat ability is based primarily around his ancient Vampire body, the unusual Monster characteristics of Clan Adrasteia and thousands of years devoted to close combat. More importantly, Chapter 464 explicitly identifies Walter as a Master rather than merely calling him an experienced fighter. Much later, Walter continued to be used as a reference point for Master-level close combat when [[Victor Elderblood]]'s own martial progress reached that stage.[3]
- Noble Vampire Physiology: Walter possesses the supernatural characteristics associated with an ancient Noble Vampire, including greatly enhanced physical ability, longevity, regeneration, heightened senses and the ability to sustain himself through blood. His age alone places him among Nightingale's exceptionally old Vampires, having already lived for more than three thousand years by the time of the [[WarFall]] expedition.[1]
- Elder Vampire Physiology: Walter is explicitly described as an Elder Vampire. His enormous age, accumulated experience and highly developed body distinguish him considerably from younger Noble Vampires. Walter's physical condition is particularly extraordinary even by the standards of his age group, with the narration describing his physique as being leagues beyond that of a normal Noble Vampire.[1]
- Vampire-Monster Hybrid Physiology: Like the other members of Clan Adrasteia, Walter belongs to a lineage whose Vampiric nature has been combined with the characteristics of Nightingale's Monsters. The original Adrasteia method uses a dangerous ritual in which a Vampire hunts a Monster and uses it as the basis for altering their bloodline. The resulting physiology combines Vampiric traits with the adaptive physical characteristics of Nightingale's Monsters.[4][5]
- Enhanced Physical Attributes: Members of Clan Adrasteia possess bodies considerably more physically oriented than ordinary Noble Vampires. Walter represents an extreme example even among his own Clan. His physical strength and constitution are explicitly stated to be powerful enough that Alpha [[Werewolves]]—creatures naturally renowned for overwhelming physiques—would have difficulty dealing with him.[1]
- Enhanced Senses: The altered Adrasteia bloodline grants sensory capabilities superior to ordinary Noble Vampires, complementing the Clan's role fighting Monsters throughout WarFall.[6]
- Unique Energy / [[Immortality]] Negation: Clan Adrasteia possesses a special form of Energy that allows its members to permanently kill Nightingale's Monsters despite the abnormal regenerative or immortal characteristics that make conventional attacks ineffective against them.[4] Walter belongs to this bloodline, although the story has not individually demonstrated the precise way he applies this Energy in battle.
- Monster Transformation: The complete Adrasteia ritual can allow its successful participants to manifest partial or complete Monster characteristics, dramatically increasing their combat ability. The effectiveness of the ritual varies according to the Vampire undergoing it, with weaker or less compatible participants receiving a diluted result.[4][5] Walter's own source Monster, complete Monster appearance and any abilities unique to his individual transformation have not been revealed, and should therefore not be assigned speculatively.
- Monster Flesh and Blood Adaptation: Clan Adrasteia's altered bodies are adapted to the Monsters of Nightingale to such a degree that Monster and Demonic Beast blood can function as their ordinary nourishment rather than the Human blood preferred by most Vampires. Their physiology also allows them to consume Monster flesh that would be harmful or poisonous to outsiders.[6][7]
- Master-Level Close Combat: Walter is explicitly a Master of Close Combat and one of Clan Adrasteia's two Master-level martial artists, the other being Rose. Rather than specializing in a conventional weapon, Walter developed his body and fists into his principal weapons. [[Scathach]] believed that fighting Walter seriously would be difficult even for her, directly establishing him as one of the most technically accomplished hand-to-hand fighters among Nightingale's old Vampires.[1]
- Hand-to-Hand Combat: Walter's principal martial specialty. His experience encompasses thousands of years, and his physical superiority allows him to combine refined technique with overwhelming strength rather than relying upon either quality independently.[1]
- Deadly Striking Power: Walter's fists are specifically described as deadly. Combined with his Adrasteia physique, his strikes are dangerous even to opponents whose physical bodies would normally exceed those of Noble Vampires.[1]
- Combat Experience: Walter has lived for more than three millennia and has accumulated enough practical experience to be recognized as a Master by Scathach herself. His past interactions with Scathach also demonstrate that he has survived extended confrontations with some of the strongest Vampires of Nightingale.[1]
- Leadership: Walter has formally mastered leadership and has spent centuries helping manage one of Nightingale's four traditional Count Clans. His responsibilities include supporting the Clan leader, maintaining internal order and acting with authority when Eleanor is unavailable or delegates responsibility to him.[1]
- Military Command: Although Rose is Clan Adrasteia's primary military specialist, Walter possesses considerable military knowledge himself. During the journey to the Royal Capital, Eleanor entrusted him with relocating and organizing her army, and Walter immediately began issuing orders to the knights under her command.[2] This should not be confused with Rose's position as the Clan's principal military-oriented Master; Walter's primary specialization remains the Clan's internal affairs.[1]
- Politics: Walter has explicitly mastered politics. As the Shield of a Vampire Count Clan, he is expected to understand Nightingale's political environment well enough to advise Eleanor and protect Clan Adrasteia's interests outside direct combat.[1]
- Etiquette: Walter is a master of formal etiquette and the standards expected within Nightingale's Noble society. This knowledge forms part of his role as Eleanor's butler and instructor.[1]
- Administration: Walter's description of being responsible for "domestic matters" encompasses the internal operation of Clan Adrasteia rather than merely ordinary household work. His expertise includes the political, organizational and administrative knowledge required to keep the Clan functional while its leader and military forces are occupied elsewhere.[1]
- Teaching: Walter is one of Eleanor's principal teachers. After Scathach established Eleanor's martial foundation, Walter and Rose continued her education and were directly credited with polishing her into both a capable warrior and, more importantly, a proper Clan leader.[1]
- Strategic Judgment: Walter is capable of evaluating threats without allowing emotion to dominate his decisions. During Eleanor's expedition he distinguished between Alpha Monsters, which he did not consider sufficient reason to interfere, and the considerably greater danger represented by native Gods. He also arranged for Rose to carry a Clan treasure as an emergency measure rather than attempting to prevent Eleanor from leaving altogether.[1]

### Relationships

#### Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia
Eleanor is Walter's master and the individual around whom most of his duties revolve. Walter has served as far more than a conventional butler: he is one of the people responsible for educating, advising and preparing Eleanor to govern Clan Adrasteia. [[Scathach]] provided Eleanor with an exceptional foundation during her youth, but Walter and Rose subsequently became the teachers responsible for refining that foundation and turning her into both a warrior and a Clan leader.[1]
Walter's devotion does not translate into blindly controlling Eleanor for her own protection. He recognizes her authority and independence, supporting her decisions even when they involve considerable danger. During the [[WarFall]] expedition he refused to treat Eleanor like a child who needed to be forcibly prevented from taking risks, explaining that she was an adult capable of making her own decisions. His role instead was to prepare for the consequences, advise when necessary and ensure that appropriate safeguards were available.[1]

#### Rose Adrasteia Elderblood
Rose is Walter's counterpart within Clan Adrasteia. The two are the Clan's ancient Master-level Vampires and together formed the principal support structure surrounding Eleanor. Rose is the Sword of Clan Adrasteia, concentrating primarily on military affairs and reaching extraordinary mastery of swordsmanship, while Walter is the Shield, specializing in the Clan's domestic, political and administrative affairs.[1]
The distinction is not absolute. Both are Elder Vampires who possess sufficient knowledge to function within the other's area of responsibility when required. Their different specializations instead ensure that Eleanor has access to two advisers whose skills collectively cover both the external military responsibilities and internal administration of the Clan.[1]

#### Scathach Elderblood Scarlett
Walter and [[Scathach]] have known one another for a considerable period of time. Their relationship combines mutual martial recognition with Walter's very understandable desire to avoid encouraging Scathach's obsession with battle. At some point in the past Walter fell for one of Scathach's tricks and was forced to deal with the battle-hungry Countess for an entire month, leaving him determined not to repeat the experience.[1]
Scathach nevertheless holds Walter's abilities in extremely high regard. She identifies him as one of Clan Adrasteia's two Masters and believes a serious fight against him would be difficult. Walter responds to her praise cautiously and insists that Scathach remains stronger, preferring diplomacy over giving her another excuse to challenge him.[1]

### Notes
- Walter is explicitly more than 3,000 years old; no exact age has been revealed.[1]
- The statement that Walter and [[Scathach]] are at the same "level" refers specifically to their classification as Master-level martial artists. It should not automatically be interpreted as proof that their total combat power, supernatural abilities or statistics are identical.[1]
- Walter's title as the Shield of Clan Adrasteia contrasts with Rose's position as its Sword. The titles represent their functions within the Clan as much as their fighting styles: Rose is principally associated with military affairs, while Walter handles domestic, political and administrative matters.[1]
- Walter's specific Monster bloodline has never been identified. Although the Adrasteia ritual and its possible transformations are explained in detail, the Monster Walter personally used, his individual Monster transformation and any exclusive abilities derived from it remain unknown.[4][5]
- Walter's elderly appearance is canonical, but the reason an immortal Vampire of his age outwardly appears old has not been explained. The claim that he simply chooses to look elderly should therefore be treated as speculation rather than established fact.[2]
