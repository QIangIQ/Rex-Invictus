Anrietha Adrasteia is a Vampire-Monster Balanced Hybrid of Clan Adrasteia and one of the six Valkyries serving beneath [[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]] and [[Rose Adrasteia Elderblood]]. Within the Valkyries' combat formation, Anrietha specializes in support magic, healing and defensive combat, functioning as both a tank and support while being capable of switching between the two roles according to the needs of the battle.[1] Despite usually acting as the group's gentle support specialist, she is also a highly capable frontline warrior whose combat style incorporates a greatsword, an enormous shield and a priestess staff.

### Physical Description
Anrietha is a woman with long light-brown hair and black eyes. By the time [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] encountered the Valkyries again following his ascension, she was described as having a notably plump and developed figure.[2] In combat she normally wears exceptionally heavy armor and carries a large shield, greatsword and priestess staff simultaneously, equipment whose combined weight would be impractical for an ordinary person but which her Vampire-Monster physiology allows her to use freely.[3]

### Personality
Anrietha is normally one of the gentler and calmer Valkyries. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] himself regarded her as a kind woman, while her interactions with the more excitable members of the group often carry an almost motherly quality.[1] This remains apparent centuries later, when she dismisses Dorothy and Alexa's teasing of Victor as jealousy in what the narration explicitly describes as a motherly tone.[2]
She can also be rather shy when personally receiving affection. During the expedition, after seeing Victor pat another Valkyrie's head, Anrietha quietly asked if he could stroke her hair as well and became openly pleased when he agreed.[1] Her embarrassment, however, should not be mistaken for meekness. Anrietha is capable of speaking very bluntly when she considers it necessary, particularly when addressing mistakes made by her companions. When Rose's paranoia caused her to test Victor's intentions, Anrietha acknowledged the reason behind Rose's actions but nevertheless told her directly that she had been excessively paranoid and should already have understood Victor's character.[4]
The greatest contrast in Anrietha's personality appears in battle. Although usually gentle, becoming genuinely angry causes her demeanor to change dramatically. After repeatedly being protected during the expedition, her wounded warrior pride eventually caused her to abandon her normal position at the rear of the formation and personally tear through the monster horde. She brutally dismembered Behemoths, Ogres and Minions with her shields while angrily challenging the monsters for believing her weak, causing Dorothy and the others to conclude that angering the normally gentle Anrietha was a terrible idea.[5]
Anrietha also possesses a considerably more shameless side than her initial demeanor suggests. She participates in the Valkyries' ongoing jokes and discussions concerning their attraction toward Victor and, by Chapter 900, was willing to make extremely blunt comments about that attraction even while maintaining a completely serious expression.[2]

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Anrietha was introduced as one of the six Valkyries serving under Rose when [[Victor Elderblood]], Eleanor and their companions arrived in Warfall. When Rose ordered the Valkyries to accompany her after Eleanor's return, Anrietha joined the group as they witnessed Victor confront the monster horde threatening the territory.[6]
Her particular role within the Valkyries became much clearer during Victor's later expedition with Clan Adrasteia. When the group encountered approximately twenty Behemoth-class monsters, Eleanor immediately began coordinating the Valkyries according to their specialties. After Dorothy was launched toward one of the Behemoths, Eleanor called upon Anrietha. Anrietha spun her priestess staff, struck it against the ground and recited an incantation in an unfamiliar language, causing brown energy to surround the entire group. Victor immediately felt his body become stronger under the effect.[3]
Anrietha then transitioned directly from support into the frontline. Holding an enormous black shield in one hand and a greatsword in the other, she positioned herself in front of the formation. Her armor was heavier and more robust than that worn by the other Valkyries, yet her enhanced physiology allowed her to carry all of it without difficulty. She performed another incantation, covering her shield in red power before releasing an effect that forcibly attracted the monsters' attention toward herself. Even the Behemoth previously focused on Dorothy redirected its attention toward Anrietha, establishing her as the group's primary aggro-drawing tank during that engagement.[3]
As the battle progressed, Anrietha placed her greatsword into a compartment above her shield, moved the shield onto her back and retrieved her priestess staff. After another ritualized sequence with the staff, her power surrounded the fighting Valkyries and noticeably increased their speed. Victor consequently recognized the structure of the formation: Anrietha served as both tank and support, exchanging positions with Eleanor whenever necessary. Because Victor had joined the expedition and could cover much of the frontline burden himself, Anrietha was able to concentrate far more heavily on support magic.[1]
The expedition later became considerably more dangerous when the group entered a trap constructed by the intelligent monsters inhabiting the region. Victor shielded the Valkyries from an enormous explosion but was contaminated by powerful monster venom that interfered with his normally extraordinary regeneration. Eleanor immediately called Anrietha forward. Anrietha began casting healing magic over Victor and correctly deduced from his slowed regeneration that the explosive attack had incorporated monster venom. Although Victor possessed some resistance from his previous exposure to lower-level monster poisons, the stronger toxin was still dangerous, forcing Anrietha to continuously expend her own energy preventing it from progressing through his body.[5]
Anrietha continued healing Victor for several hours. Her treatment prevented the poison from spreading while Victor's own blood gradually eliminated it, substantially accelerating his recovery. The extended healing exhausted Anrietha because her technique consumed considerably more energy than her ordinary abilities. In return for her treatment, Victor allowed Anrietha to drink a controlled amount of his blood, restoring the energy she had spent keeping him alive.[5]
When the group resumed its advance, Anrietha displayed the full range of her support magic. At Rose's command she spun her priestess staff and sequentially covered the Valkyries in white, red and green energies. Anrietha explicitly identified the resulting enhancements as increases to defense, attack and speed, stating that she would require additional time to cast her next spell.[5]
Rather than remaining behind the others, Anrietha then chose to fight directly. She drew her greatsword, deployed blades from across the surface and edges of her shield, and hurled the shield into the monster horde. The rotating weapon tore through the surrounding creatures. Using another spell, Anrietha produced a magical connection to the shield and then duplicated it, creating two bladed shields that she manipulated simultaneously as though they were puppets. The weapons repeatedly bisected Behemoths, Ogres and lesser monsters while Anrietha herself entered close combat, at one point kicking an Ogre away after evading its acid attack.[5]
Her aggression surprised the other Valkyries because of the contrast with her normal personality. Anrietha had become enraged by repeatedly needing to be rescued during the expedition and refused to remain on the sidelines. Her two shields subsequently carved through the monsters surrounding her, including Behemoths approximately fifty feet tall, leading Dorothy to openly state that she never wanted to anger Anrietha.[5]
Anrietha returned to her normal support role when circumstances required it. When Eleanor later detected Predators approaching from another direction, Anrietha immediately enhanced all of Eleanor's physical capabilities before the two prepared to intercept them.[5]
Following Eleanor's creation of the City of Babylon, the enormous expenditure of power left the Countess pale and almost completely exhausted. While the remaining Valkyries spread throughout the newly created defensive city, Anrietha deliberately stayed behind. She told Rose that she would handle Eleanor and subsequently crouched beside her leader and began healing her fatigue, confirming that Anrietha's restorative abilities were not restricted to physical wounds or poison.[7]
Near the end of the expedition, Anrietha participated in the Valkyries' discussion of their clan's unusual physiology. She explained that their monster genes made the members of Clan Adrasteia literally monstrous in the eyes of outsiders and that many traditional Vampires would be unable to accept their true nature. She believed that older [[Nightingale]] Vampires would consider their existence a corruption of Noble Vampire blood, although the Valkyries themselves displayed little concern regarding such opinions.[4]
Anrietha also observed how thoroughly Victor had earned the group's trust. She openly stated that their powerful "neighbor" would almost certainly intervene if Clan Adrasteia was genuinely threatened, an assessment with which the other Valkyries immediately agreed. Victor subsequently deduced the artificial nature of their hybrid bloodline and forced Eleanor to reveal the history of the clan's ritualistic fusion between Vampire and monster blood.[4]
When Rose attempted to test Victor by suggesting that his interest in the clan's bloodline could be satisfied by marrying Eleanor and gaining access to their ritual, Victor became disappointed by the implication that he had been helping them for selfish reasons. After he left, Anrietha confronted Rose. She understood Rose's fear of losing Eleanor but argued that Rose had already spent enough time observing Victor to know that he was unlike the Noble Vampires who had approached Eleanor in the past. Anrietha ultimately advised Rose to apologize, correctly believing that Victor would understand the reason behind her actions even if the test had damaged some of his respect for her.[4]
Once the misunderstanding was resolved, the Valkyries' developing attraction toward Victor became considerably more open. Anrietha jokingly complained that Eleanor and Rose's closeness with him forced the single Valkyries to watch from the sidelines. This led back to the group's so-called Solomon Plan: if their leader eventually married Victor, the Valkyries intended to remain with her rather than separate from the woman they regarded as their Valkaria Queen. Juliet summarized the situation as Victor effectively receiving "one and seven" together, an idea the others did not reject.[8]
When Victor subsequently detected another monster horde occupying Eleanor's city, he specifically called upon Anrietha and instructed her to protect the other Valkyries if necessary. Anrietha immediately retrieved her priestess staff, cast several spells over herself and then switched back to her shield, taking Victor's order seriously enough to immediately shut down Juliet's teasing when the latter attempted to joke about their interaction.[8]

#### Demonic War
Anrietha later accompanied Eleanor, Rose and the Valkyries during the wider Demonic conflict. When the group was ambushed by unfamiliar enemies capable of creating portals, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] repeatedly moved Anrietha and the other Valkyries away from incoming attacks before Rose reorganized their formation.[9]
As additional enemies began appearing, Rose ordered Anrietha to activate Battle Tactic A. Anrietha retrieved the ceremonial staff from her back, spun it while striking the ground and recited another incantation. Blue energy descended upon the allied group, followed by green and then red energy, applying several enhancement effects in immediate succession before Anrietha declared the preparation complete.[9]
When Victor was later struck by an abnormal dark projectile while protecting Eleanor, Anrietha immediately assumed her medical role. The narration explicitly identifies her as the group's healer as she approached Victor and began diagnosing the injury. She observed that the attack had penetrated his armor with abnormal ease shortly before the others noticed black veins spreading across his face.[9]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
By the time [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] reunited with the Valkyries following his ascension as the Dragon God of [[Chaos]], Anrietha and the others had undergone substantial growth. Victor immediately noticed the change in her and remarked that she had "evolved a lot". Anrietha acknowledged that he was capable of sensing the improvement, while Victor concluded that the Valkyries' development demonstrated how significant the previous war had been for them.[2]
Anrietha retained her familiar position as one of the more composed members of the group. When Dorothy and Alexa openly teased Victor over the reason Eleanor and Rose had been absent from the clan's relocation preparations, Anrietha told Victor to ignore them and described the two women as jealous, doing so in what the narration characterized as a motherly tone.[2]
Victor also introduced [[Metis]] Elderblood to the Valkyries. Anrietha was the first among them to personally greet Metis, while the group explained that Eleanor and Rose had previously told them about the former Titan Goddess.[2]
Victor then distributed weapons he had personally created for each Valkyrie alongside [[Hephaestus]]. Anrietha received upgraded equipment designed specifically around her established fighting style: a new shield, greatsword and staff. She was initially uncomfortable accepting artifacts of such extraordinary value, but Victor made clear that each weapon had been created specifically for its intended wielder.[2]
While testing her new equipment, Alexa privately asked Anrietha whether their old Solomon Plan was still active. Anrietha initially appeared ready to dismiss the subject as another joke, but after observing Victor personally assist Martha with her new weapon, she reconsidered and agreed that they should put the plan into practice. The two reasoned that, with the Snow, Fulger and Scarlett Clans increasingly forming a united front, the Valkyries should similarly support Eleanor and Rose.[2]

### Skills and Abilities
Anrietha is an elite Valkyrie whose combat style differs significantly from the more offensively specialized members of the group. She combines the enhanced physiology of Clan Adrasteia with support magic, healing, defensive techniques, greatsword combat and unusual shield-based abilities.
- Noble Vampire Physiology: As a Noble Vampire, Anrietha possesses physical strength, speed, durability, stamina, senses and regenerative capabilities vastly superior to ordinary humans.
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics: Anrietha can simultaneously wear exceptionally heavy armor and carry a huge shield, greatsword and staff without being noticeably impeded by their weight.[3]
- Enhanced Speed: Anrietha is capable of keeping pace with the Valkyries during their high-speed expeditions and rapidly changing between frontline and rear-support positions.
- Regeneration: As a Noble Vampire, she possesses the regenerative abilities characteristic of her species.
- Enhanced Senses: Her Vampire and monster-derived senses allow her to operate effectively within the extremely dangerous wilderness surrounding Warfall.
- Monster Physiology: Anrietha inherited the modified Vampire-Monster bloodline produced by Clan Adrasteia's ancestral ritual. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] identified the clan as genuine hybrids rather than Vampires merely displaying superficial traits of another species.[4]
- Monster Eyes: During combat, Anrietha and the other Valkyries can manifest predatory eyes associated with their monster bloodline.[3]
- Enhanced Hybrid Physiology: The Adrasteia bloodline grants its members physical qualities beyond those of conventional Noble Vampires, allowing them to hunt and permanently kill [[Nightingale]]'s powerful monsters.
- Monster Transformation: Members of the clan's main lineage can manifest increasingly pronounced monster characteristics rather than being restricted to a purely Vampiric form.[4]
- [[Magic]]: Anrietha is a dedicated spellcaster whose techniques are generally performed through her priestess staff, physical ritual movements and verbal incantations in an unidentified language.
- Support Magic: Support is Anrietha's primary battlefield specialty. Victor explicitly identifies her as the Valkyries' tank and support.[1]
- Physical Augmentation: Anrietha's first demonstrated enhancement surrounded the entire group in brown energy and immediately caused Victor to feel physically stronger.[3]
- Speed Augmentation: Another support spell noticeably increased the movement speed of every Valkyrie affected by it.[1]
- Defense Amplification: During a later engagement, Anrietha applied a white layer of energy over her allies that formed something resembling a second skin. She subsequently identified defense as one of the three attributes she had amplified.[5]
- Attack Amplification: The same sequence enhanced the offensive capabilities of the Valkyries.[5]
- Multi-Attribute Augmentation: Anrietha can simultaneously maintain enhancement effects affecting defense, attack and speed. During the expedition, these were represented by white, red and green energies applied in sequence.[5]
- Battle Tactic A: A prepared Valkyrie support sequence used at Rose's command. Anrietha performs an incantation while rotating and striking the ground with her ceremonial staff, causing blue, green and red energies to descend sequentially upon the allied formation.[9]
- Single-Target Augmentation: Anrietha can focus her support magic upon a single combatant; she enhanced Eleanor's complete physical performance immediately before the latter confronted approaching Predators.[5]
- Healing: Anrietha is explicitly identified as the Valkyries' healer.[9]
- Accelerated Healing: She can directly accelerate another person's recovery. Her intervention kept Victor alive while his body fought a powerful monster venom and dramatically accelerated the rate at which his regeneration overcame it.[5]
- Poison Suppression: Anrietha's healing magic can slow or prevent the progression of supernatural toxins. She spent several hours preventing high-level monster venom from spreading through Victor's body until his own blood could eliminate it.[5]
- Fatigue Recovery: Her restorative magic is capable of treating supernatural exhaustion. She healed Eleanor after the City of Babylon left the Countess almost completely drained.[7]
- Medical Diagnosis: Anrietha can analyze unusual supernatural injuries. She recognized that Victor's impaired regeneration during the expedition was being caused by monster venom and later immediately began diagnosing the abnormal injury inflicted upon him during the Demonic War.[5][9]
- Tank Combat: Anrietha can act as the Valkyries' primary defensive combatant and alternate this role with Eleanor when necessary.[1]
- Shield Mastery: Her principal defensive weapon is an enormous black shield capable of protecting her against the attacks of Nightingale's monsters.
- Aggro Manipulation: By surrounding her shield with red energy and releasing it outward, Anrietha can forcibly draw nearby monsters' attention toward herself. The technique was powerful enough to cause a monster already attacking Dorothy to abandon its target and focus entirely on Anrietha.[3]
- Heavy Armor Proficiency: Anrietha's combat armor is heavier and more robust than the armor normally worn by the other Valkyries.[3]
- Shield Weaponization: Anrietha's shield functions as considerably more than defensive equipment.
- Blade Deployment: Mechanical or supernatural blades can emerge from the shield's surface and edges, transforming it into an enormous rotating cutting weapon.[5]
- Shield Throwing: Anrietha can launch the bladed shield into groups of enemies with sufficient force to tear monsters apart.[5]
- Weapon Duplication: Through an incantation and her staff, Anrietha demonstrated the ability to duplicate her deployed shield, creating two identical weapons operating simultaneously.[5]
- Weapon Manipulation: Anrietha formed a magical connection between herself and the deployed shields and controlled both weapons remotely "as if they were puppets".[5]
- Large-Monster Dismemberment: The remotely controlled shields were powerful enough to repeatedly bisect approximately fifty-foot Behemoths alongside Ogres and lesser monsters.[5]
- Greatsword Proficiency: Anrietha carries a large greatsword as part of her standard equipment and can transition between greatsword combat, shield combat and spellcasting during the same engagement.[3][5]
- Close-Quarters Combat: Although primarily a support specialist, Anrietha is physically capable in direct melee combat. She effortlessly evaded an Ogre's acid attack before appearing directly in front of it and kicking the creature hard enough to send it flying.[5]
- Role Switching: One of Anrietha's most important skills is her ability to rapidly alter her battlefield function. Her equipment is specifically arranged to allow her to store the greatsword within the shield, move the shield onto her back and retrieve her staff whenever she needs to change from tanking to support casting.[1]
- Team Combat: Anrietha possesses extensive experience fighting as part of the Valkyries' coordinated formation. Her support abilities allow the more offensively oriented members to concentrate entirely upon attacking while she strengthens, protects and heals them.

### Equipment
- Priestess Staff: Anrietha's principal magical focus. She performs most of her support and healing techniques through the staff by spinning it, striking it against the ground and reciting incantations.[3][1]
- Greatsword: A large melee weapon carried alongside her other equipment. The greatsword can be secured against or within the shield when Anrietha switches to spellcasting.[1]
- Shield: An enormous black defensive weapon central to Anrietha's role as the Valkyries' tank. It can be infused with power to attract monsters, fitted with blades, thrown as a projectile, magically duplicated and controlled remotely.[3][5]
- Heavy Armor: Anrietha's armor is explicitly described as more robust and heavier than the armor used by the other members of her group.[3]
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Custom Equipment: Victor and [[Hephaestus]] later created new high-level weapons specifically for each Valkyrie. Anrietha received a new shield, greatsword and staff tailored to her established combat style.[2]
