Dorothy Adrasteia is a Vampire-Monster Balanced Hybrid of Clan Adrasteia and one of the elite Valkyries serving [[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]] and [[Rose Adrasteia Elderblood]]. A highly aggressive frontline combatant, Dorothy specializes in fighting with a pair of swords and uses the enhanced physical abilities and monstrous bloodline of Clan Adrasteia to fight the enormous creatures inhabiting the wilderness surrounding Warfall.[1]

### Physical Description
Dorothy is an exceptionally tall woman standing 189 cm tall, with short blonde hair and golden eyes. When she first appeared, she was immediately described as a tall woman whose height reached 189 centimeters.[2]
Like the other Valkyries, Dorothy normally operates in Clan Adrasteia's specialized black combat armor when entering the monster-infested regions beyond Warfall. Compared to the heavily equipped defensive members of the formation such as Eleanor and Anrietha, Dorothy uses comparatively lighter equipment intended to preserve her mobility while fighting at close range.[1][3]

### Personality
Dorothy is energetic, loud, highly competitive and noticeably more expressive than many of the other Valkyries. Her first appearance consisted of her bursting into Rose's presence while loudly announcing Eleanor's return, prompting Rose to immediately tell her to stop shouting.[2]
She possesses the intensely battle-loving mentality common among Warfall's elite warriors. When Rose asked which Valkyrie wanted to handle an approaching Alpha, Dorothy immediately volunteered with visible excitement. Her enthusiasm quickly became a competition when [[Alexa Adrasteia]] also volunteered, with Dorothy complaining that Alexa had already stolen her prey during the previous month.[2]
Dorothy is also playful, blunt and easily drawn into the Valkyries' teasing. After witnessing Eleanor smile unusually warmly toward [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Dorothy became so convinced that she might be imagining the situation that she asked Alexa to slap her. Alexa immediately complied, leaving Dorothy loudly protesting despite having personally requested it.[2]
Her openness became even more apparent after spending extended time around Victor. During preparations for their expedition, Dorothy casually addressed him as "Vic" and directly questioned what he intended to do about Eleanor. She enthusiastically supported Victor's proposal that they train using only physical ability and combat technique, immediately treating the prospect of an exhausting training session as something enjoyable.[4]
Despite her impulsive demeanor, Dorothy is an experienced soldier capable of recognizing when her behavior is affecting the formation. When one of her attacks failed against the increasingly durable monsters encountered during the expedition, [[Juliet Adrasteia]] criticized her for becoming too excited. Dorothy did not argue and returned to the formation, demonstrating that her enthusiasm does not override battlefield discipline.[1]
Dorothy is deeply attached to Eleanor, Rose and the other Valkyries. The women had watched Eleanor grow from childhood and consequently regarded her less as a distant Clan leader and more like a younger sister they were obligated to support and protect.[4] This loyalty forms the basis of the Valkyries' later "Solomon Plan": if their leaders eventually joined Victor romantically, the Valkyries had little intention of separating themselves from the women they had followed for so long.[5]

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Dorothy first appeared when she rushed into Rose's office to announce that Eleanor was returning to Warfall after a lengthy absence. Her excitement immediately established her energetic personality, with Rose having to tell the 189-centimeter-tall blonde Valkyrie to stop shouting.[2]
Shortly afterward, the Valkyries detected an approaching monster horde. When Rose revealed that an Alpha accompanied it and asked who wanted to confront the creature, Dorothy immediately volunteered. Alexa attempted to claim the same prey, causing Dorothy to complain that Alexa had stolen her target during the previous month. The argument demonstrated how routine monster hunting was among the Valkyries and how competitive the warriors could become over particularly desirable opponents.[2]
Dorothy subsequently accompanied Rose and the other Valkyries when Eleanor returned with [[Victor Elderblood]], [[Siena Scarlett Elderblood]], [[Pepper Scarlett Elderblood]], [[Lacus Scarlett Elderblood]] and [[Mizuki Elderblood]]. She was particularly shocked by Eleanor's unusually warm behavior around Victor. When Eleanor smiled toward him, Dorothy briefly believed that she was hallucinating and asked Alexa to slap her to confirm that she was awake.[2]
When Victor confronted the monster horde outside Warfall alone, Dorothy observed the battle alongside the Valkyries. She questioned whether he genuinely intended to fight the entire horde without assistance and later reacted in frustration when Victor began moving at speeds too great for her to follow normally. As the monster threat increased, Dorothy was among those who recognized that the engagement had become considerably more dangerous than an ordinary horde extermination.[6]
Dorothy later became considerably more familiar with Victor when he joined Eleanor, Rose and the Valkyries on an extended expedition into [[Nightingale]]'s wilderness. Before departing, Dorothy openly questioned Victor about his relationship with Eleanor and attempted to encourage him, while also joking about Eleanor and Rose being extraordinarily dense when romance was involved. When Victor proposed that the group train using nothing but physical techniques rather than relying upon their supernatural powers, Dorothy enthusiastically accepted the idea.[4]
The expedition revealed Dorothy's primary combat style. When the group encountered approximately twenty Behemoth-class monsters, Eleanor immediately began directing the Valkyries according to their specialties. At Eleanor's call, Dorothy drew two black swords from her waist and leapt into the air. Eleanor positioned her enormous greatsword beneath Dorothy's feet and physically launched her toward a Behemoth more than twenty meters tall.[1]
Dorothy immediately attempted to decapitate the monster, but the increasing durability of the creatures in the region prevented the strike from cleanly passing through its armored body. After [[Judith Adrasteia]] opened a vulnerability in the creature, Dorothy exploited the opportunity and drove both swords through the Behemoth's heart, killing it.[1]
Rather than returning directly to the group, Dorothy used the surrounding monsters as platforms, jumping from one creature to another as though she were dancing across the battlefield. She deliberately attacked vulnerable areas such as their exposed eyes while maintaining her momentum. Her excitement eventually caused Juliet to reprimand her because the monsters were becoming stronger the deeper the expedition travelled. Dorothy accepted the criticism and returned to the formation.[1]
The group's fighting style subsequently became clearer. Eleanor and Anrietha occupied the heavier defensive positions while Dorothy, Judith and Juliet wore lighter equipment and exploited the openings created by them. Victor noted that the Valkyries operated with highly developed teamwork, constantly compensating for one another's weaknesses rather than functioning as independent fighters.[3]
Dorothy continued participating in the increasingly dangerous battles encountered during the expedition. When Victor temporarily required a pair of weapons, he asked Dorothy for her two swords and briefly handed her [[Junketsu]] in exchange. Dorothy later complained that she had been left without weapons, only to discover that Victor had already returned her swords so quickly that she had not noticed the exchange.[7]
After [[Anrietha Adrasteia]] applied enhancements to the Valkyries' defense, attack and speed, Eleanor ordered Dorothy, Martha, Alexa and Anrietha to advance. Dorothy immediately entered the monster formation with the others and resumed killing the creatures directly.[7]
Anrietha's subsequent rampage particularly impressed Dorothy. After watching the normally gentle support specialist tear through the surrounding monsters using her bladed shields, Dorothy openly asked the others to remind her never to make Anrietha angry.[7]
Dorothy's comfort around the group continued increasing during the quieter portions of the expedition. When Victor disappeared from the group's temporary camp to patrol their surroundings, Dorothy was among those who immediately noticed his absence. During a later hot-spring break she participated openly in the Valkyries' teasing and complained about not having brought a phone when an opportunity arose to preserve an amusing image of Victor, further demonstrating how much the expedition had transformed him from an outsider into a genuine squadmate.[8]
The expedition eventually forced Clan Adrasteia's hidden nature into the open. Eleanor explained that the strongest members of their bloodline possessed sealed True Monster Forms whose instincts needed to be controlled from childhood. The Valkyries shared this characteristic, and their specialized armor had been designed to adapt alongside their transformations rather than becoming useless when their bodies changed.[9]
When Eleanor finally revealed her monstrous transformation to Victor, Dorothy was among the Valkyries who watched his response closely. Rather than reacting with disgust, Victor found the form fascinating. During the ensuing discussion Alexa jokingly claimed that entering their monster forms simply made them more honest about their desires. Dorothy immediately called the excuse nonsense, forcing Alexa to admit that she was exaggerating.[10]
The discussion soon turned toward the origin of Clan Adrasteia itself. Victor recognized that their radically different monster traits could not reasonably have developed through ordinary Vampire reproduction and deduced that some artificial process must have been responsible. Alexa accidentally confirmed his suspicion by reacting aloud, causing Dorothy to shout at her for revealing the secret.[10]
Eleanor subsequently explained the clan's Ancient Rebirth Ritual, which fused Vampire blood with the highly adaptable blood of Nightingale's monsters. The original process was extraordinarily lethal, with fewer than five percent of participants surviving despite Vampire regeneration. The ritual was later diluted for ordinary members of the Clan, while the main family, the Valkyries and a small number of elite retainers retained genuine True Monster Forms.[10]
Dorothy was visibly astonished when Rose estimated that Victor himself would have an approximately ninety-percent probability of surviving the original undiluted ritual, further emphasizing how abnormal his constitution appeared even to members of a bloodline whose existence depended upon surviving an otherwise lethal transformation.[10]
By the end of the expedition, the Valkyries' attraction toward Victor had become increasingly open. Juliet revived the group's joking Solomon Plan, explaining that if their Valkyrie Queen married Victor, the Valkyries had no intention of abandoning their leader and could effectively accompany her into the relationship. Juliet humorously summarized the arrangement as Victor receiving "one and seven" together. When the others began stating that they would participate if Rose did, the Valkyries collectively agreed with the idea.[5]
Dorothy continued contributing to the teasing immediately afterward, including joking about Victor's destructive attacks and provoking Eleanor over her increasingly obvious relationship with him.[5]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Dorothy remained with the Valkyries as Clan Adrasteia entered the vastly expanded political structure surrounding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. When Victor later visited the group during preparations for the Clan's relocation, Dorothy immediately resumed the informal relationship developed during their earlier expedition. She teasingly blamed him for temporarily depriving them of their two principal leaders because Eleanor and Rose had been occupied celebrating with him.[11]
After Alexa added an even more explicit explanation of what that "celebration" involved, Anrietha accused Dorothy and Alexa of being jealous. Both women immediately protested the accusation.[11]
Victor had not visited merely to socialize. With the assistance of [[Hephaestus]], he had personally created weapons suited to each Valkyrie's fighting style. Dorothy received a new pair of black swords with violet details. Upon drawing them, she immediately began testing the weapons through an extremely fast sequence of attacks.[11]
Dorothy quickly discovered that the swords were far superior to every weapon she had previously used. Their cutting ability was sufficiently great that, when enough power was applied, her swings could release cutting attacks through the air rather than requiring the blades themselves to physically touch the target. Victor explained that the weapons were among the highest-level artifacts he and Hephaestus could currently create and that Victor had personally participated in their forging using his own Fire.[11]
The gift carried particular meaning for the Valkyries because the weapons were not random artifacts taken from Victor's enormous resources. Each had been individually created for its intended user based upon their existing fighting style, demonstrating that Victor had remembered the warriors and considered how best to improve their strength.[11]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Dorothy and the other Valkyries continued occupying a position within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wider inner circle even after his family and Empire expanded enormously. When [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] considered the women who might still realistically become involved with Victor, she distinguished the Valkyries from unknown outsiders because they had already developed a longstanding personal relationship with him.[12]
The Valkyries were described as getting along extremely well with Victor. Although Dorothy and the others had repeatedly displayed attraction toward him and joked about eventually entering his harem, they did not organize their lives around pursuing him. Their primary focus remained becoming stronger and accompanying Eleanor and Rose, with the group functioning more like inseparable sisters than ordinary soldiers serving the same commanders.[12]

### Skills and Abilities
Dorothy is an elite member of Clan Adrasteia's Valkyries and an experienced dual-sword combatant. Her fighting style emphasizes aggressive close-range offense, mobility and exploiting openings created by the heavier members of the formation.
- Noble Vampire Physiology: As a Noble Vampire, Dorothy possesses the enhanced physical attributes, longevity, senses and regenerative capabilities characteristic of her species. Her Adrasteia bloodline further modifies these traits through its integration with [[Nightingale]]'s monster genetics.
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics: Dorothy possesses strength, speed, durability, reflexes and stamina vastly superior to ordinary humans. Clan Adrasteia's monster-derived lineage dramatically enhanced the physical constitution of its members, contributing to the unusually large and physically powerful warriors produced by Warfall.[4]
- Enhanced Agility: Dorothy's combat style makes extensive use of aerial movement, rapid changes of direction and jumping between gigantic monsters. During the Behemoth battle she moved from creature to creature while targeting vulnerable locations without returning to the ground.[1]
- Regeneration: As a Noble Vampire, Dorothy possesses supernatural regenerative capabilities.
- Monster Physiology: Dorothy underwent the transformation responsible for Clan Adrasteia's Balanced Hybrid bloodline and therefore possesses genuine monster characteristics in addition to her Vampiric nature.[10]
- True Monster Form: Eleanor explicitly confirmed that her Valkyries belong to the elite members of Clan Adrasteia capable of assuming genuine Monster Forms rather than merely manifesting diluted traits.[10]
- Partial Transformation: Like the other members of the main lineage, Dorothy can access individual aspects of her monster-derived physiology without needing to remain permanently transformed.
- Enhanced Monster Instincts: Clan Adrasteia's True Forms intensify the natural instincts inherited from their monster bloodlines. Because of the danger posed by these impulses, the strongest members of the clan have their full transformations sealed while young until they learn sufficient self-control.[9]
- Adaptive Equipment Integration: The specialized armor of the Valkyries is designed to change alongside their monster transformations, allowing their equipment to remain functional when the shape of their bodies changes.[9]
- Monster Consumption: Clan Adrasteia's altered physiology allows its members to consume Nightingale's monsters without suffering the lethal effects those creatures can cause ordinary Vampires. The clan consequently uses monster flesh and blood as a normal part of its food supply.[13]
- Monster Toxin Resistance: The mutations inherited through the Adrasteia bloodline provide resistance to the poisonous properties naturally present in Nightingale's monsters, allowing the clan to consume substances that would be dangerous to conventional Vampires.[13]
- Unique Energy: Dorothy possesses the special energy associated with the Adrasteia bloodline. This monster-derived power interacts with Clan Adrasteia's weapons and equipment and contributes to their ability to fight the highly regenerative creatures inhabiting Warfall.
- Dual Swordsmanship: Dorothy's primary combat discipline is fighting simultaneously with two swords. She has enough experience to employ both weapons in extremely fast, aggressive combinations against opponents vastly larger than herself.[1]
- Dual Piercing Attack: After Judith created an opening in a Behemoth's defenses, Dorothy drove both swords directly into the monster's heart and killed it.[1]
- Precision Targeting: Dorothy deliberately attacks vulnerable areas when a monster's external defenses are too durable to penetrate efficiently. During the expedition she moved between monsters while targeting exposed eyes and other weak points.[1]
- Aerial Swordsmanship: Dorothy can fight effectively while airborne and use gigantic enemies themselves as footholds, allowing her to maintain continuous momentum through dense groups of monsters.[1]
- Ranged Slashing Attacks: Dorothy's later custom swords are sufficiently powerful that her swings can project cutting attacks through the air when she channels enough power into them.[11]
- Enhanced Speed: Dorothy belongs to the lighter offensive portion of the Valkyrie formation. While Eleanor and Anrietha operate with heavier defensive equipment, Dorothy, Judith and Juliet emphasize greater mobility and use the openings generated by their companions to attack vulnerable enemies.[3]
- Monster Hunting: Dorothy has spent much of her life fighting the creatures surrounding Warfall. Her eagerness to personally hunt an Alpha during her introduction demonstrates how routine encounters with powerful monsters are for her.[2]
- Battlefield Mobility: Dorothy is capable of rapidly transitioning between ground combat, aerial attacks and movement across the bodies of enormous monsters. This mobility makes her particularly effective when fighting opponents far larger than herself.[1]
- Team Combat: Dorothy is extensively trained to fight as part of the Valkyries rather than simply as an individual warrior. Eleanor can launch her directly into combat, Anrietha's enhancements improve her offensive capabilities, Judith can create openings for her finishing attacks and the remaining Valkyries continuously cover one another's weaknesses.[1][3]
- Coordinated Launch Attack: Eleanor can use her greatsword as a platform to physically launch Dorothy toward distant or elevated opponents. Dorothy immediately adapted to this maneuver during the Behemoth encounter and entered combat with both swords already drawn.[1]
- Opening Exploitation: Dorothy demonstrated effective coordination with Judith when the latter damaged a Behemoth and Dorothy immediately followed by piercing its heart with both swords.[1]
- Combat Adaptability: Although naturally aggressive, Dorothy can adjust her tactics when her preferred attacks become ineffective. After discovering that the deeper-region monsters possessed stronger defenses, she accepted Juliet's warning and returned to the group rather than continuing an inefficient assault.[1]

### Equipment
- Twin Black Swords: Dorothy's original weapons were a pair of black swords worn at her waist. She used them throughout the Adrasteia expedition and demonstrated enough proficiency to target the hearts and other vulnerable areas of enormous monsters.[1]
- Valkyrie Armor: Dorothy wears Clan Adrasteia's specialized combat armor. Her equipment is lighter than the armor used by the group's principal defensive fighters, prioritizing mobility during close-range combat.[3] The armor is also designed to interact with the Adrasteia bloodline and adapt when its user accesses her monstrous characteristics.[9]
- Personalized Twin Swords: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and [[Hephaestus]] later forged a new pair of weapons specifically for Dorothy. The swords are black with violet details and represent some of the highest-level artifacts the two could create at the time. Their extraordinary sharpness allows Dorothy to project cutting attacks through the air when sufficient power is applied.[11]
