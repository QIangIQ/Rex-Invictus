### Physical Description
Martha is a tall, pale-skinned woman with long brown hair and brown eyes.[1] She possesses the muscular and highly conditioned physique characteristic of the Valkyries, having spent much of her life training and fighting the monsters surrounding Warfall.
Like the other Valkyries, Martha normally enters dangerous regions wearing Clan Adrasteia's specialized black combat armor. Her principal weapon is a large Knight's Halberd which is capable of responding to the monstrous power coursing through her body.[1]

### Personality
Martha is normally gentle, composed and comparatively soft-spoken. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] returned to Eleanor's territory with [[Morgana Elderblood]] and [[Jeanne Elderblood]], Martha was explicitly described as a gentle-looking woman and was one of the first Valkyries to correctly suspect that Victor intended to use the surrounding monster forest to train the two former Vampire Queens rather than recklessly entering the wilderness without a purpose.[3]
Although normally polite and mild-mannered, Martha is still a Valkyrie and shares the group's enthusiasm for powerful warriors, combat and unusual situations. When Victor later proposed a large-scale expedition beyond Warfall and demonstrated his confidence before departing, even Martha—who was considered kind and rarely used crude language—became visibly impressed by his behavior.[4]
She is perceptive when judging people. During the Valkyries' early discussions about Victor, Martha became interested in the fact that despite possessing tremendous strength and influence, he did not behave with the arrogance they expected from powerful Noble Vampires. She also wondered aloud who had taught him to treat women with such consideration after Rose pointed out that Victor noticed their appearances without openly objectifying or disrespecting them.[4]
Martha possesses a playful and occasionally shameless side around the other Valkyries. During the return journey from their expedition, she complained about Eleanor and Victor flirting openly in front of a group of single women before immediately supporting Juliet's revived "Solomon Plan". After Juliet was punished by Rose for pushing the joke too far, Martha laughed at her misfortune and then innocently pretended that she herself had never participated in the plan, briefly managing to make even Juliet doubt what had just happened.[5]
She is nevertheless humble enough to accept useful instruction. By Chapter 900 Martha already considered herself a master of the halberd and was initially surprised when Victor told her she was using her new weapon incorrectly. Rather than dismissing him, she reconsidered immediately, listened carefully to his explanation and successfully altered her technique after only a single demonstration.[6]

### Synopsis

#### Volume 4: Discoveries, Changes, And War
Martha first appeared as one of Eleanor's Valkyries following the Countess' return to Warfall alongside [[Victor Elderblood]] and the members of the Scarlett family. She belonged to the elite group directly serving Eleanor and Rose and regularly participated in the training and monster-hunting activities required to defend Clan Adrasteia's territory.[7]
Martha became more directly involved with Victor when he returned to Eleanor's territory carrying [[Morgana Elderblood]] and [[Jeanne Elderblood]]. When the Valkyries realized that Victor had entered the monster forest without the equipment normally required to permanently kill [[Nightingale]]'s creatures, several assumed he was behaving recklessly. Martha instead reasoned that Victor likely had another objective because he had brought Vlad's two former wives with him. Her deduction proved correct when Victor threw Morgana and Jeanne into the monster-infested region to force them back into genuine life-or-death combat.[3]
Martha later joined Victor, Eleanor, Rose and the other Valkyries during an extended expedition beyond Warfall. Before their departure, the group discussed Victor's unusual personality and strength. Martha was openly impressed by his confidence and the fact that someone possessing so much power did not display the arrogance they normally associated with powerful Noble Vampires.[4]
Her fighting style was demonstrated once the group began encountering increasingly powerful Behemoth-class monsters. When Eleanor called Martha and Alexa into battle, Martha was the first to advance. Carrying her Knight's Halberd, she leapt directly toward one of the giant monsters while her arm began becoming increasingly monstrous.[1]
The power produced by Martha's transformation flowed from her arm into the weapon. Her previously black halberd developed glowing green vein-like patterns across its surface as it responded to the Adrasteia bloodline. Upon reaching the monster, Martha delivered a single enormous horizontal swing and bisected it outright. The residual force of the attack continued into the terrain below, cutting so deeply into the ground that the bottom of the resulting fissure could not be seen.[1]
The battle demonstrated that the Valkyries' specialized weapons were not merely conventional equipment. Their mutated powers could interact directly with their weapons, allowing the characteristics of their monster bloodlines to be carried into their attacks. In Martha's case, her transformed arm directly empowered her halberd before she struck.[1]
Martha's tactical position became clearer when the expedition crossed approximately 30,000 kilometers beyond Warfall and encountered a coordinated monster settlement. After assessing the different Valkyries, Victor divided them according to their specialties. Eleanor, Anrietha and Dorothy remained together as the primary frontline formation, while Judith and Juliet occupied elevated positions to provide long-range and anti-air support. Victor specifically ordered Martha and Alexa to accompany him.[2]
Victor's reasoning was that Martha and Alexa were particularly well suited to attacking while operating independently rather than requiring the tightly structured support formation used by the others. The pair consequently functioned as mobile assault fighters alongside Victor while Rose was given freedom to move between all formations as necessary.[2]
Martha continued fighting directly during the later portions of the expedition. When Anrietha strengthened the Valkyries' defense, attack and speed, Eleanor ordered Martha, Alexa, Dorothy and Anrietha to advance alongside her and eliminate the remaining monsters while Judith and Juliet supplied ranged support.[8]
As the expedition approached its conclusion, Martha participated in the discussion concerning Clan Adrasteia's hidden origins. When Anrietha explained that older Vampires might regard their hybrid bloodline as an abomination, Martha reacted with disdain toward the possibility that Nightingale's traditionalists would attempt to eliminate them for supposedly contaminating Noble Vampire blood.[9]
Victor eventually deduced that the enormous differences between individual Adrasteia monster characteristics could not have emerged through ordinary reproduction. Eleanor consequently revealed the Ancient Rebirth Ritual, through which the clan's ancestors fused Noble Vampire blood with the extraordinarily adaptable bloodlines of Nightingale's monsters. Martha and the other Valkyries belonged to the elite line capable of manifesting genuine Monster Forms rather than only the diluted traits possessed by ordinary members of the Clan.[9]
During the return journey, Martha became increasingly annoyed by Eleanor and Victor flirting while the Valkyries were forced to watch. When Juliet revived their earlier Solomon Plan and argued that the Valkyries should accompany their Valkaria Queen if she married Victor, Martha openly agreed that she would participate if Rose did.[5]
After Rose struck Juliet for taking the discussion too far, Martha laughed at Juliet before immediately pretending she had never participated in the conversation. Her completely innocent expression momentarily confused Juliet despite Martha having openly joined the proposal moments earlier.[5]

#### Volume 7: Dragon God of Chaos
Martha remained with the Valkyries as Clan Adrasteia became increasingly integrated into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s expanding faction. By the time Victor met the group again after his ascension, he observed that all six Valkyries had developed substantially since their earlier expedition.[6]
Victor arrived with personalized weapons that he and [[Hephaestus]] had forged specifically for the Valkyries. Each artifact was created to suit the combat style of its intended wielder rather than functioning as generic high-level equipment. Martha received a new halberd designed specifically around her existing martial discipline.[6]
While watching the Valkyries test their weapons, Victor noticed that Martha was applying excessive physical force to the new halberd. Martha was initially surprised when he told her she was using it incorrectly because she already considered herself a master of the weapon. She nevertheless recognized Victor's extraordinary martial talent and allowed him to demonstrate what he meant.[6]
Victor clarified that Martha's fundamental technique was not wrong. The problem was that the new artifact was designed to grow with and respond to its wielder, meaning she no longer needed to force every movement through overwhelming physical strength. He instructed her to treat the halberd as an extension of her own body and move naturally instead.[6]
To demonstrate, Victor casually swung the weapon toward the sky. The motion appeared unremarkable until the clouds across a straight line were divided in half. Martha was stunned by the result and compared the effortless precision of the movement to watching Rose use her [[Martial Arts]].[6]
Martha then attempted the technique herself. After Victor helped her relax, she swung the halberd with an effortless natural motion rather than forcing strength through it. Her casual attack subsequently cut everything in a straight line before her, shocking the other Valkyries because the destructive result had been produced by Martha rather than by Victor himself. Victor praised her improvement and advised her to choose a more durable target during future practice.[6]

#### Volume 8: God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Martha and the other Valkyries continued to occupy a longstanding place within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s extended inner circle as his Empire and family expanded. When [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] later considered the women who might realistically still become involved with Victor, she treated the Valkyries differently from unknown outsiders because of their established history and friendship with him.[10]
Although Martha and the others had displayed attraction toward Victor and repeatedly joked about the possibility of eventually joining his family, they did not make pursuing him their primary objective. The Valkyries remained chiefly concerned with becoming stronger and accompanying Eleanor and Rose, with their relationship functioning more like that of inseparable sisters than ordinary military subordinates.[10]

### Skills and Abilities
Martha is an elite Valkyrie and master halberd user whose combat style emphasizes overwhelming cutting power, reach and independent frontline assault. Her Monster-Vampire physiology allows her to reinforce both her body and her halberd, making even straightforward swings extraordinarily destructive.
- Noble Vampire Physiology: As a Noble Vampire, Martha possesses the supernatural physical abilities and biological characteristics associated with her species, including enhanced strength, speed, durability, stamina, senses, longevity and regeneration.
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics: Martha possesses enough physical strength to wield a large Knight's Halberd against Behemoth-class monsters and bisect gigantic creatures with single swings.[1]
- Enhanced Strength: Raw strength is one of the foundations of Martha's combat style. Her original tendency was to apply enormous force through her halberd, although [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later taught her that her improved artifact no longer required such excessive exertion.[1][6]
- Enhanced Agility: Despite using a large polearm, Martha can rapidly leap toward gigantic enemies and operate as a mobile assault fighter rather than a stationary heavy combatant.[1]
- Regeneration: As a Noble Vampire, Martha possesses supernatural regenerative abilities.
- Monster Physiology: Martha belongs to the elite Adrasteia lineage created through the Ancient Rebirth Ritual, which fused Noble Vampire blood with the highly adaptive biology of [[Nightingale]]'s monsters.[9]
- True Monster Form: Eleanor confirmed that her Valkyries are among the selected members of Clan Adrasteia capable of assuming genuine Monster Forms rather than merely displaying diluted features.[9]
- Partial Monster Transformation: Martha can transform individual portions of her body without entering her complete Monster Form. During combat in Chapter 452, her arm visibly became increasingly monstrous while she prepared to attack.[1]
- Physical Amplification: Accessing her monster traits increases the physical force available to Martha and can be directly incorporated into her weapon techniques.[1]
- Enhanced Senses: The Adrasteia bloodline possesses stronger senses than conventional Noble Vampires due to its fusion with Nightingale's monsters.[11]
- Monster Flesh and Blood Resistance: Unlike ordinary Vampires, members of Clan Adrasteia can safely consume the flesh and blood of Nightingale's monsters, which form a normal part of Warfall's food supply.[2]
- Regeneration Negation: Clan Adrasteia's monster-derived power allows its members to permanently kill Nightingale's highly regenerative monsters rather than merely inflicting temporary physical destruction upon them.[12]
- Unique Energy: Martha possesses the specialized energy produced by the Adrasteia Vampire-Monster bloodline. Her power can be transmitted directly through her weapon, allowing her halberd to change and become infused with her monstrous characteristics.[1]
- Halberd Mastery: Martha is explicitly an experienced halberd specialist and describes herself as a master of the weapon by Chapter 900.[6]
- Knight's Halberd Proficiency: Martha's original primary weapon was a large black Knight's Halberd which she wielded with enough control to fight gigantic monsters at close range.[1]
- Wide-Area Sweeping Attacks: The halberd's reach and Martha's physical power make her particularly effective at large horizontal cutting attacks capable of striking gigantic opponents across broad areas.[1]
- Monster Bisection: A single enhanced horizontal swing from Martha completely split a giant monster in half.[1]
- Terrain Cleaving: The residual force of the same attack cut deeply through the ground beneath the monster, producing a fissure whose bottom could not be seen.[1]
- Natural Weapon Integration: After Victor's instruction, Martha learned to treat her later halberd as an extension of her own body instead of forcing movements through excessive muscular power.[6]
- Effortless Cutting: By relaxing her body and allowing the weapon to respond naturally, Martha became capable of generating enormous cutting attacks through apparently casual movements.[6]
- Ranged Cutting Attack: Her personalized halberd is sufficiently sharp and responsive that a normal-looking swing can transmit its cutting force far beyond the weapon's physical blade, allowing Martha to divide everything positioned along a straight line in front of her.[6]
- Weapon Empowerment: Martha can channel the power produced by her Monster transformation directly into her halberd. In Chapter 452, the energy travelled from her transformed arm into the weapon and caused green vein-like patterns to spread across its previously black surface before she attacked.[1]
- Weapon Transformation: Martha's original halberd visibly changes when exposed to her monster-derived power, demonstrating the adaptive relationship between the Valkyries' bloodlines and their specialized equipment.[1]
- Independent Assault Combat: Martha is particularly suited to operating outside the Valkyries' main defensive formation. During the monster settlement battle, Victor specifically selected Martha and Alexa to accompany him because their specialties allowed them to attack and function independently while Eleanor's group maintained the primary formation.[2]
- Monster Hunting: As one of the Valkyries, Martha possesses extensive experience fighting Nightingale's monster population across the wilderness surrounding Warfall.
- Behemoth-Class Combat: Martha can directly engage monsters belonging to the enormous Behemoth classifications rather than being limited to fighting lesser Minions or Ogres.
- Large-Target Combat: Her halberd and wide sweeping techniques are particularly suited to fighting opponents whose bodies vastly exceed human size.
- Team Combat: Martha has trained extensively alongside the other Valkyries and can rapidly adapt between independent assault and coordinated formation combat.
- Assault Formation: Eleanor can place Martha alongside Alexa, Dorothy and Anrietha as part of a direct attack group while Judith and Juliet provide ranged support.[8]
- Independent Pairing: Victor considered Martha and Alexa especially effective when detached from the primary formation and allowed to operate beside him as independent attackers.[2]
- Combat Adaptability: Martha is capable of altering established habits when shown a superior method. Despite already being a halberd master, she immediately incorporated Victor's advice regarding relaxed movement and weapon synchronization and reproduced an advanced long-range cutting effect on her first serious attempt.[6]

### Equipment
- Knight's Halberd: Martha's original primary weapon. The weapon is normally completely black but develops glowing green vein-like patterns when Martha channels her Monster-derived power through it. Empowered in this manner, it can bisect gigantic monsters and cut deeply into the surrounding terrain.[1]
- Valkyrie Armor: Martha wears Clan Adrasteia's specialized combat armor during expeditions beyond Warfall. The equipment is designed for the extreme physical conditions of [[Nightingale]]'s monster territories and to remain compatible with the altered bodies of the Valkyries.
- Personalized Divine Halberd: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and [[Hephaestus]] later forged a new high-level halberd specifically for Martha. Unlike an ordinary weapon, the artifact grows with and responds to its wielder and was designed to function as an extension of Martha herself. Its blade is extraordinarily sharp; once Martha stopped forcing her swings and synchronized with the weapon, even a casual motion produced a massive straight-line cutting attack.[6]
