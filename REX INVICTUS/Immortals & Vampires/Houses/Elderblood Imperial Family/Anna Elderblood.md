Anna Elderblood, formerly Anna Walker and Anna Alucard, is a True Blood Dragon, Dragon Goddess of Truth, Second Empress of the [[Elderblood Empire]], and the biological mother of [[Victor Elderblood]]. Originally an ordinary Human woman living in [[New York]] with her husband [[Leon Walker]] and their chronically ill son, Anna became one of the most influential members of the Elderblood family following Victor's transformation into a Vampire. She was subsequently transformed into a Noble Vampire through his Progenitor Blood, developed a supernatural Authority associated with Truth, evolved into a True Dragon, and eventually became one of Victor's wives and the mother of their daughter, [[Stella Elderblood]].[1][2][3][4]
Within the mature Imperial hierarchy, Anna occupies the position of Empress for Internal Affairs and serves as [[Violet Elderblood Snow]]'s deputy. Her responsibilities concern the internal administration of the Empire, the management and discipline of the Imperial family, and matters requiring the authority of a senior Empress rather than direct military intervention. Although she possesses extraordinary supernatural power, Anna is particularly influential because of her intelligence, professional legal experience, familiarity with Victor's personality, and the unusually strong maternal authority she exercises over the younger generations of the Elderblood family.[5][6]
Anna's supernatural development is unusual even among Victor's closest family members. Upon becoming a Noble Vampire, her subconscious desire for honesty transformed the ordinary Vampiric Charm into a unique supernatural Authority that compelled truthful expression and could directly influence the subconscious [[Will Solace|Will]] of affected individuals. Unlike conventional Charm, her ability did not require prolonged eye contact and could affect multiple people within a surrounding domain. The power later developed into the foundation of her Divine identity as the Goddess of Truth, under which she receives recognition and Faith within the [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]].[7][8][9]
Her transformation into a True Blood Dragon awakened an entirely different power centered upon Reality Distortion. Rather than possessing a conventional elemental affinity such as Fire, Ice or Lightning, Anna developed an exceptional aptitude for changing Reality through her understanding of words, abstract ideas and supernatural language. Her manifestations are governed by her conscious intentions, subconscious memories, personal beliefs and emotional associations, making her powers exceptionally versatile but potentially dangerous when insufficiently controlled. Victor recognized that her natural talent for altering Reality was extraordinary even among the members of his Dragon lineage and arranged extensive training to ensure that she could develop the necessary control.[10][11]
Over the Empire's accelerated development, Anna became recognized as the family's broad Master of Dragon Runes, acquiring exceptional knowledge of how Draconic Language, Divine Concepts, Will and Reality interact. Her later feats include astronomical-scale spatial expansion, Reality-based preservation, conceptual manifestation and the reinforcement of planetary structures. During Victor's reconstruction of Mars, Anna served as one of his principal assistants, strengthening and amplifying the structural changes while Natalia stabilized Space-Time. Her fully manifested Dragon body was also stated to possess dimensions approaching the scale of an entire Solar System.[12][13][14]
Despite her extraordinary supernatural status, Anna remains one of the strongest connections between the Imperial dynasty and Victor's original Human life. She remembers the years during which her son suffered from his rare RH-null blood, the ordinary Walker household, and the social and professional world that existed before he became a Vampire. Her familiarity with that history gives her a perspective that many of Victor's Divine wives and subordinates cannot reproduce. She continues treating him as the son she raised, regardless of his status as Progenitor, Emperor, Dragon God or an existence exceeding the ordinary limits of Creation.[1][15]

### Physical Appearance

#### Human
During her original Human life, Anna was an attractive, well-preserved woman in her early forties, standing approximately 170 centimeters tall. She possessed fair skin, long black hair, sapphire-blue eyes and a mature, curvaceous physique. Her appearance was noticeably youthful for her age, reflecting the care she devoted to maintaining herself despite the demands of her legal career and family responsibilities.[1]
Anna dressed appropriately for her profession, generally favoring clothing that emphasized her appearance without compromising the formal expectations of a successful lawyer. She openly acknowledged that physical attractiveness could provide practical advantages when dealing with wealthy clients and influential people. Her appearance consequently reflected both personal vanity and a pragmatic understanding of the professional environment in which she worked.[1]
Even before becoming supernatural, Anna possessed a strong and confident presence. Her expressive facial reactions, direct manner of speaking and willingness to embarrass [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] distinguished her from the reserved or traditionally maternal figures often associated with the role of a middle-aged parent. These characteristics remained recognizable throughout her later transformations, despite the increasingly dramatic changes to her body and supernatural existence.[1]

#### Noble Vampire
Anna's transformation into a Noble Vampire reconstructed her body through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Progenitor Blood. Her physical appearance became more youthful, while her black hair grew longer and her eyes acquired the Blood-red coloration associated with her new supernatural lineage. Her body became stronger and more developed, losing the ordinary Human limitations that had previously governed her physical existence.[2][16]
Her transformation also made the physical similarities between Anna and Victor more pronounced. Although she remained his biological mother, her new supernatural body had been created through his Bloodline, causing her appearance to acquire characteristics normally associated with individuals directly descended from a Vampire Progenitor. The relationship was consequently reflected in both their physical resemblance and the supernatural connection established between them.[2]
Anna adapted to her new appearance and intensified senses considerably faster than Leon. Although Vampirism amplified her emotions and instincts, she retained enough composure to examine those changes rather than immediately surrendering to them. Her physical transformation therefore represented a substantial increase in supernatural attractiveness and strength without eliminating the recognizable personality she had possessed as a Human.[16][17]

#### True Blood Dragon
Anna's evolution into a True Blood Dragon fundamentally reconstructed her supernatural existence. Her body acquired the characteristics of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Dragon lineage, including black Draconic horns with violet coloration, matching wings and the enhanced physical constitution of a True Dragon. She retained her long black hair and Blood-red eyes, while her Draconic features made her resemblance to Victor even more apparent.[3]
Unlike many of the women who underwent Dragonization, Anna did not develop a conventional elemental manifestation. Her nature instead became associated with the supernatural principles of Truth, Reality and Draconic Runes. This distinction made her development particularly unusual because her powers concerned the structure and interpretation of Reality rather than a specific natural Element.[10][11]
Her appearance also became increasingly affected by the extraordinary scale of her Draconic existence. Like other [[True Dragons]], Anna can control the amount of her complete physical form that she manifests, allowing her to retain a Humanoid appearance for ordinary family activities rather than continuously occupying astronomical quantities of space.[3][14]
By the later Imperial Era, Anna's full Dragon form had reached a truly enormous scale. During the reconstruction of Mars, she and Natalia manifested Dragon bodies already larger than the planet while deliberately restraining their true dimensions. The narration stated that their unrestricted Dragon forms could reach approximately Solar-System scale. This extraordinary growth reflects the development of her physical existence throughout the Empire's accelerated history, rather than the dimensions she possessed immediately after Dragonization.[14]

### Personality
Anna is an outspoken, intelligent, emotionally expressive and fiercely protective woman whose personality has remained remarkably consistent despite her transformations from Human to Vampire and eventually Dragon Goddess. She values honesty, dislikes unnecessary deception, frequently disregards social formality and rarely hesitates to confront someone when she believes their behavior is unreasonable. Her directness can make her abrasive, particularly when dealing with people accustomed to automatic obedience, but it also allows her to address problems that others avoid out of embarrassment, fear or excessive respect for authority.[1][8]
Her insistence upon honesty is among the most important characteristics of her personality. Anna strongly dislikes people concealing their true intentions through misleading statements, particularly when such dishonesty involves her family. This tendency existed long before her supernatural transformation and eventually became the foundation of her unique Vampire ability. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] investigated the power, he determined that her subconscious desire for others to behave honestly had mutated ordinary Vampiric Charm into an Authority capable of affecting the [[Will Solace|Will]] of individuals within her presence.[7][8]
Although Anna values honesty, she is not incapable of deception, professional calculation or moral compromise. Her original legal career involved defending wealthy individuals, corporations and politicians, including people whose conduct she did not necessarily admire. She understood that certain clients could be exceptionally profitable because of the serious legal problems they faced, and she was willing to operate pragmatically within that professional environment. However, when her involvement with dangerous political interests began creating unacceptable risks for her family, she reduced her participation in those activities.[1]
Anna possesses a considerable capacity for humor, frequently approaching embarrassing subjects with a degree of shamelessness that frustrates Victor. Even when he was a chronically ill Human, she openly encouraged him to pursue romantic relationships and refused to allow his circumstances to become an excuse for completely withdrawing from ordinary social experiences. On his twenty-first birthday, her increasingly outrageous suggestions concerning his lack of a girlfriend became another example of the unusual openness characterizing the Walker household.[1]
Her professional intelligence contrasts with her limited patience for tedious study. Anna became a successful lawyer partly because her parents strongly encouraged her to follow their profession, not because she possessed an unlimited passion for legal books or academic work. During the period preceding her transformation, she complained bitterly about the enormous supernatural books she had been studying. Leon explained that she had never particularly enjoyed studying and that her career had originally been shaped by the expectations of her parents.[18]
Despite this impatience, Anna is capable of extensive intellectual work when she considers the results important. Her legal background provided experience in negotiation, assessing testimony, identifying inconsistencies and examining the motives of other people. These skills became increasingly valuable after she entered the supernatural world, especially as Victor's growing family and Empire produced administrative problems that could not be solved simply by possessing overwhelming combat power.[1][5]
Anna's maternal instincts are arguably the most consistent aspect of her identity. During Victor's Human childhood, she and Leon supported him through the severe limitations caused by his RH-null blood, allowing him to grow up within a relatively stable and loving household despite the danger posed by ordinary physical exertion. His transformation into a Vampire did not diminish Anna's attachment to him, nor did his eventual emergence as an extraordinarily powerful supernatural ruler convince her to stop treating him as her son.[1][15]
She is one of the few individuals willing to criticize Victor without approaching him as a political subordinate. Ancient Vampires, Gods and military commanders may regard him as an overwhelming authority, but Anna remembers the child she raised and recognizes behavioral changes that would escape individuals who knew him only after his rise to power. She can therefore challenge his decisions, criticize his recklessness and question whether his actions remain consistent with the values that originally shaped him.[19][20]
Her influence upon Victor extends beyond ordinary parental affection. His strong attachment to family, preference for direct communication and willingness to disregard social expectations contain recognizable elements of Anna's personality. Even [[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] noticed similarities between the way Victor approached powerful rulers and Anna's own casual treatment of individuals whom most supernatural beings would regard with considerable reverence.[21]
Anna nevertheless possesses a moral perspective that does not always agree with Victor's. As his power increased, she became concerned by the increasingly violent and ruthless manner in which he confronted his enemies. Rather than assume that everything her son did was justified simply because he was stronger, she questioned him directly about the difference between necessary violence and indiscriminate cruelty. Victor responded by explaining that he continued distinguishing between individuals he considered responsible for their actions and unrelated innocents who had not earned his hostility.[19]
Her emotional nature became more complicated after she entered Victor's supernatural Bloodline. Vampirism greatly intensified desires and feelings that had previously remained manageable through ordinary Human restraint. Her affectionate attachment to Victor became increasingly possessive, and her later Dragon physiology amplified those emotions further. During an incident involving [[Persephone]], Anna's Draconic aura became sufficiently hostile that Victor intervened and reminded her that possessing extraordinary instincts did not justify threatening another member of the family.[17][22]
Anna gradually learned to distinguish between genuine personal desires and the amplification produced by her supernatural nature. Victor repeatedly explained that denying every instinct was unhealthy, but surrendering control simply because a Vampire or Dragon possessed stronger emotions was equally irresponsible. This principle became especially important when Anna's Reality Distortion awakened, because her subconscious thoughts and emotions could directly affect the supernatural world around her.[23][24][11]
Unlike individuals who regard extraordinary power as justification for doing whatever they please, Anna eventually accepted that dangerous abilities require greater discipline. When she recognized that her Reality Distortion could activate through poorly controlled thoughts or emotional reactions, she requested that Victor seal the relevant powers until she developed sufficient mastery. The decision demonstrated that her concern for family safety outweighed any desire to retain unrestricted access to a power she could not yet use responsibly.[25]
Her maternal instincts expanded alongside the Elderblood family. Anna came to regard Victor's adopted daughters and the growing younger generation as children deserving affection, guidance and protection, regardless of complicated biological or supernatural relationships. She treated [[Nero Elderblood]] with immediate warmth after learning about the girl's difficult past and eventually developed an authority over the Imperial daughters that extended far beyond her biological descendants.[26][27][6]
By Chapter 1157, Anna's authority within the family had become so firmly established that Victor deliberately prevented her from intervening in the dispute between Violet and the younger generation. He understood that nearly all of his daughters would obey Anna's instructions, making her involvement sufficient to end the disagreement before the participants resolved their underlying grievances. Her influence was therefore based upon the emotional and disciplinary role she occupied within the family rather than merely upon her political title or supernatural powers.[6]
Anna is particularly strict toward individuals who neglect family responsibilities. She considers intellectual talent, Divine power and exceptional accomplishments insufficient excuses for abandoning one's children or withdrawing completely from family life. This attitude became important during the later confrontation involving [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]], whose constant isolation within laboratories had severely weakened her relationship with her daughter, [[Nigredo Elderblood]]. Anna criticized Albedo's behavior and supported forcing her to undergo further training under [[Scathach]], believing that a Dragon Goddess needed emotional maturity and practical discipline in addition to supernatural knowledge.[28][12]
Her approach to discipline does not prevent Anna from being affectionate, playful or deeply sentimental. She possesses an extensive collection of photographs showing Victor during his Human childhood and considers those memories irreplaceable. When her daughters began secretly obtaining copies, Anna protected the originals through Reality manipulation, making ordinary supernatural duplication impossible. However, when Yog approached her directly and honestly, Anna willingly provided photographs, demonstrating that her strictness was not simply a desire to deny her children access to family memories.[29]
Anna also possesses a practical awareness of the responsibilities created by Victor's extraordinary position. During the final period of the accelerated Imperial development project, she recognized that the continual expenditure of his Energy had become dangerous even for him. She was among the individuals most strongly demanding that he abandon the ritual rather than continue weakening himself for the possibility of obtaining even greater preparation against future enemies. Her reasoning was straightforward: Victor served as the foundation of the entire Empire, and allowing him to exhaust himself beyond reasonable limits could endanger everything dependent upon his existence.[30]
Her combination of intelligence, honesty, emotional directness and maternal authority has consequently made Anna one of the family's most important internal stabilizing figures. She is capable of confronting Victor when he becomes unreasonable, disciplining daughters who disregard their responsibilities and participating in decisions concerning Imperial administration without allowing ceremonial rank to erase the personal relationships upon which the family was originally founded.[5][6][30]

### History
Anna was born into an ordinary Human family in the United States. Her parents placed considerable importance upon professional achievement and pressured her toward a legal career, expecting her to follow a path similar to their own. Although Anna never developed an unlimited enthusiasm for academic study, she proved intelligent and capable enough to become a successful lawyer.[18]
Her professional career eventually included cases involving wealthy private individuals, businesses and politicians. Anna recognized that clients possessing substantial financial resources could provide exceptionally lucrative opportunities, particularly when their legal problems were severe enough that they were willing to pay large amounts for experienced representation. Her professional decisions were often pragmatic rather than idealistic, but she remained conscious of the consequences her work could have upon her family.[1]
At some point during her Human life, Anna married [[Leon Walker]], and the couple eventually became the parents of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] Walker. Their son was born with an exceptionally rare RH-null blood type, a condition that caused serious physical weakness and prevented him from living like an ordinary healthy child. Victor's childhood was consequently defined by medical limitations, the danger associated with excessive physical exertion and the knowledge that his condition could place his life at risk.[1]
Despite the difficulties of raising a chronically ill child, Anna and Leon maintained a relatively stable and affectionate household. They supported Victor and attempted to ensure that his physical limitations did not destroy his opportunity to develop friendships, personal interests and a meaningful understanding of family. Their relationship with him became one of the strongest foundations of his personality, particularly his willingness to protect the people he loved even when doing so required extraordinary sacrifice.[1]
Anna was especially close to Victor. She encouraged him to pursue romantic relationships and enjoy the experiences available to him rather than spend his entire life withdrawing from other people because of his health. Her attempts at encouragement were frequently embarrassing, reflecting her direct and shameless personality, but they originated from a genuine desire to see her son experience happiness before his illness could take away that opportunity.[1]
During her Human life, Anna also developed a friendship with a woman known as Renata, whom she encountered through her professional activities. The relationship lasted more than a decade, and Anna eventually regarded Renata as one of her closest friends despite frequently complaining about her behavior. Unknown to Anna, Renata was the Human identity of [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood, the Greek Goddess of Love and Beauty. The friendship became particularly important because Aphrodite valued Anna's unusual honesty and regarded her as one of the few Humans capable of maintaining a genuine relationship with her without becoming overwhelmed by Divine Beauty or supernatural influence.[31]
Anna remained unaware of the full supernatural world until Victor's transformation into a Vampire began exposing the hidden civilizations and powers operating around Earth. Her previously ordinary life subsequently became inseparable from a conflict involving [[Vampire Counts]], Gods, Demons and eventually beings whose existence far exceeded the supernatural hierarchies she had first encountered.[1][31]

### Synopsis

#### The Awakening
Anna's direct involvement in the story began when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] returned home following his transformation into a Vampire. The changes to his body were immediately apparent: the formerly sickly young man had become significantly healthier, stronger and more physically developed, making it impossible for his parents to accept that nothing unusual had happened during his absence. Anna questioned him aggressively, demanding an explanation for the extraordinary transformation while continuing to approach the situation through the perspective of a concerned mother.[1]
Victor attempted to explain the changes without immediately revealing the existence of Vampires and the supernatural world. The situation became considerably more complicated when [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] appeared and introduced herself as his wife. Anna and Leon were forced to confront the fact that their twenty-one-year-old son had apparently married an extraordinarily beautiful woman whom they had never met, without informing either of his parents beforehand.[1][32]
Although the revelation initially produced shock and confusion, Anna's response was not to reject Victor or accuse Violet of manipulating him without evidence. Instead, she began questioning the circumstances of the marriage and attempting to understand the relationship. Once the immediate surprise faded, Anna and Leon resumed their familiar behavior of embarrassing Victor with increasingly personal questions, demonstrating that the supernatural changes to his body had not altered their willingness to treat him as their son.[1][32]
The encounter established a defining characteristic of Anna's involvement in the story. While Victor's transformation would eventually make him one of the most powerful supernatural beings in existence, his mother remained largely uninterested in treating him as a fundamentally different person merely because he had acquired extraordinary abilities. Anna continued prioritizing his happiness, health and personal relationships over the political or supernatural significance of his new condition.[1]

#### Vampire Society
As [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became increasingly involved with the Vampire civilization of [[Nightingale]], Anna and Leon remained among his strongest connections to the ordinary Human World. His disappearance for training, the involvement of ancient Vampire families and the increasingly complicated circumstances surrounding his marriage created additional difficulties for his parents, who initially possessed only an incomplete understanding of the supernatural world.[33]
The situation became more complicated when Anna and Leon learned that Violet was not their son's only wife. [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] and [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] were also connected to Victor through the abnormal transformation ritual responsible for his new existence. From the perspective of ordinary Human parents, discovering that their son had suddenly acquired three wives belonging to powerful supernatural families was considerably more difficult to understand than his unexplained physical transformation alone.[33]
Although Anna could become irritated or embarrassed by the increasing absurdity of Victor's relationships, she did not abandon him or attempt to force his life back into the circumstances that had existed before his transformation. She continued treating the women surrounding him as members of the family and gradually accepted that their relationships were governed by supernatural conditions substantially different from those familiar to ordinary Humans.[33]
Anna's continued presence in Victor's life also reinforced the personal principles he had inherited from his parents. He remained strongly attached to the Walker household and repeatedly demonstrated that the people who had raised him were not expendable simply because he had acquired a much larger supernatural family.[1][33]

#### A New Pillar
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became the Fifth Vampire Count, his connection to the supernatural world could no longer be concealed from his parents through ordinary explanations. He returned home and began revealing the circumstances surrounding his transformation, including the existence of Vampires and the political responsibilities he had acquired within [[Nightingale]].[34]
Anna and Leon initially reacted with understandable disbelief, but their familiarity with Victor prevented the revelation from becoming a complete rejection of his new identity. They questioned him about his transformation, the dangers surrounding his life and the unusual relationships he had developed. Although the supernatural information challenged much of their understanding of the world, both eventually accepted that their son had entered circumstances beyond ordinary Human experience.[34][35]
Victor also began introducing his wives and other important members of his supernatural household more formally. Anna's experience as a mother became particularly relevant because the women surrounding Victor often possessed personalities and emotional difficulties requiring considerably more patience than their extraordinary strength might suggest.[35]
The arrival of [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] further demonstrated the absurdity of the Walker family's new circumstances. A legendary Vampire Countess who inspired fear and respect throughout supernatural society was now appearing within the household of an ordinary Human family. Anna nevertheless continued approaching the situation through familiar domestic expectations, treating even intimidating supernatural visitors as people connected to her son rather than automatically surrendering to their political status.[36]
Victor's increasing power also created new concerns for Anna and Leon. Although their son had become considerably more capable of protecting them, they remained ordinary Humans whose bodies could be endangered by supernatural phenomena that the Vampires surrounding Victor would barely notice. This difference became increasingly important as his enemies and political responsibilities expanded.[34][36]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Anna's life became even more entangled with the supernatural world following the revelation that her longtime friend Renata was actually [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood. The discovery occurred during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s confrontation with the Goddess, whose earlier actions had created serious tensions with several members of his family. Victor initially regarded Aphrodite as dangerous, but the possibility that her life was connected to his mother forced him to reconsider how he handled the situation.[37][31]
When Aphrodite revealed her Human identity, Anna recognized the woman with whom she had maintained a friendship for more than ten years. The revelation was deeply unsettling because Aphrodite had concealed her Divine nature throughout their relationship and had used supernatural abilities to influence certain circumstances surrounding their professional lives. Anna nevertheless did not dismiss their friendship as entirely false. She acknowledged that Aphrodite had assisted her in the past, but asked for time to process the discovery and decide how their relationship should continue.[31]
For Aphrodite, Anna's reaction was particularly important. The Goddess had spent an enormous existence surrounded by people who desired her appearance or power without necessarily forming genuine personal connections. Anna's honesty and refusal to treat her simply as an untouchable Goddess had made their friendship unusually meaningful. The possibility of losing that relationship caused Aphrodite genuine distress, revealing an emotionally vulnerable side of the Goddess that Victor had not initially understood.[31]
Anna's involvement with the supernatural household expanded further following the arrival of [[Nero Elderblood]], one of Victor's adopted daughters. When she discovered that the young girl had entered the family, Anna initially misunderstood the circumstances and believed Victor might have become a biological father without informing his parents. After learning that [[Nero]] had been adopted, she approached the girl with warmth and welcomed her into the Walker family as her granddaughter.[26]
The reaction had a profound emotional effect upon Nero, who had experienced severe suffering and had little familiarity with ordinary familial affection. Anna did not treat the girl as a dangerous supernatural weapon or as an inconvenient responsibility created by Victor's decisions. She simply recognized another child connected to her son who needed a stable family and accepted her accordingly.[26][27]
Anna subsequently questioned Victor concerning Nero's history and emotional condition, recognizing that the girl's violent reactions and unusual behavior could be connected to trauma rather than ordinary childish disobedience. Her approach demonstrated the value of her maternal experience within a household increasingly populated by individuals whose supernatural abilities had developed under circumstances far removed from ordinary family life.[27][38]
The events surrounding Aphrodite and Nero also exposed the growing danger associated with Anna remaining Human. Victor recognized that his parents could eventually become targets for enemies seeking to influence or injure him, and that their ordinary physical bodies provided little protection against supernatural attackers. His relationship with Aphrodite created an additional concern because the Goddess's Human identity had become connected to Anna's life, making secrecy increasingly difficult to maintain.[39][40]
Victor considered different ways of resolving the problem. Aphrodite could abandon her Human identity and create another life, removing some of the risks surrounding Anna at the cost of severing a friendship that both women valued. Alternatively, Anna and Leon could become supernatural beings capable of protecting themselves and participating more safely in the world their son now inhabited.[41]
Victor eventually proposed transforming his parents into Noble Vampires. From his perspective, the offer represented an opportunity to grant them immortality, extraordinary physical abilities and the direct protection of his Progenitor Bloodline. From the perspective of traditional Vampire society, receiving such an opportunity from a Progenitor was an exceptional privilege that many supernatural beings would willingly sacrifice enormous resources to obtain.[41][42]
Anna and Leon nevertheless approached the decision according to the consequences it would have upon their own lives rather than simply accepting it because Victor considered immortality desirable. They had spent decades as ordinary Humans and understood that becoming Vampires would fundamentally alter their identities, relationships and expectations for the future. Victor ultimately respected their need to decide rather than treating his parents as ordinary subordinates whose choices could be determined through his authority.[42][43]

#### Demonic War
The events surrounding the Demonic War eventually convinced [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] that the continued vulnerability of his Human parents represented a danger he could no longer ignore. As his enemies became more powerful and his responsibilities expanded, he decided to transform Anna and Leon into Noble Vampires belonging directly to his Progenitor lineage.[44]
Before the transformation, Anna and Leon received information and preparation intended to reduce the danger of adapting to their new existence. Vampirism did not simply grant physical strength and longevity. It also amplified the emotional and predatory instincts of the individual, potentially making newly transformed Vampires dangerous to themselves or others if they surrendered completely to their new desires.[18][44]
Victor transformed Leon first before turning his attention toward Anna. During her transformation, Victor used his Blood to initiate the supernatural reconstruction of her body and temporarily killed her by breaking her neck, allowing the necessary transition into Vampirism to occur. Her body was subsequently reconstructed through his Progenitor Blood, granting her the characteristics of a Noble Vampire directly descended from him.[2]
The procedure created an unusual relationship between biological ancestry and supernatural lineage. Anna remained Victor's mother because she had given birth to and raised him, but Victor simultaneously became the source of her new Vampiric existence. This made him her racial Progenitor, placing Anna within the supernatural hierarchy normally occupied by individuals created through his Blood.[2]
Anna's adaptation differed considerably from Leon's. Both experienced intensified instincts, but Anna possessed a greater capacity to maintain her composure while examining the changes to her emotions and physical condition. Leon's transformation created more serious difficulties concerning his developing desires, forcing him to rely heavily upon training and supervision to avoid becoming overwhelmed by his new nature.[16][17]
Anna also awakened a unique supernatural ability unlike the conventional elemental powers expected from an ordinary Noble Vampire Bloodline. Individuals around her began speaking more honestly than they intended, sometimes revealing information that they would ordinarily conceal. The ability operated passively within her surroundings and did not require the deliberate activation associated with most conventional Vampiric Charm techniques.[7]
Further examination revealed that Anna's power had originated from the subconscious importance she placed upon honesty. Instead of developing ordinary mental attraction or hypnotic Charm, her Vampire ability had mutated into a supernatural field that compelled truthful expression. The result was particularly appropriate to Anna's personality, as her lifelong dislike of deception had become an essential characteristic of her supernatural existence.[7]
The transformation also forced Anna to confront emotions that ordinary Human life had allowed her to suppress or regulate more easily. She recognized that becoming a Vampire had intensified her feelings without necessarily creating entirely new desires. The distinction became important because she needed to understand which emotions represented genuine personal attachments and which aspects were merely amplified by her new racial instincts.[17]
Despite the extraordinary nature of her transformation, Anna retained her fundamental personality and continued treating Victor as her son. She did not regard becoming a Noble Vampire as a reason to abandon the emotional connections established during her Human life, although those relationships would gradually change as she and Leon experienced the consequences of supernatural immortality.[17]

#### King Among Kings
Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s return from approximately seven hundred years of subjective experience in Hell, Anna had become considerably more familiar with her Vampire existence. She had begun adapting to the physical and emotional consequences of immortality, although the intensified instincts of her new species continued creating difficulties.[23]
Victor discussed the problem of supernatural desires with her directly, explaining that neither complete repression nor unrestricted indulgence represented a healthy response. He believed that an immortal supernatural being needed to understand personal instincts and decide how to express them responsibly rather than merely surrendering to the strongest emotion available at a particular moment.[23][24]
Anna's supernatural ability also became the subject of further investigation. During Victor's journey toward Samar, he explained that her power was fundamentally different from ordinary Vampiric Charm. Rather than requiring eye contact, Anna's presence created an area of influence capable of affecting the subconscious minds of other beings. When supplied with sufficient Energy, the ability formed a domain of Authority in which susceptible individuals could be compelled to follow instructions they were conscious enough to understand.[8]
Victor determined that the ability communicated directly with the subconscious and altered the [[Will Solace|Will]] of affected targets without necessarily producing the visible symptoms of conventional Charm. This made the power considerably more dangerous than simple honesty compulsion because a target might obey Anna without realizing that their intentions had been supernaturally modified.[8]
The discovery also established important limitations. Victor himself was unaffected because of his superior position as Anna's Progenitor, his extraordinary supernatural power and the protections associated with his existing abilities. Consequently, Anna's domain could not simply be assumed to possess unrestricted control over every being regardless of comparative supernatural strength.[8]
During the journey to Samar, Anna witnessed further demonstrations of Victor's extraordinary protectiveness. When [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] accidentally directed enough supernatural pressure toward Anna to frighten her, Victor immediately reacted and warned Maya against threatening his mother. The incident reinforced that his mother's importance to him had not diminished despite the emergence of increasingly powerful members of his family.[45]
Anna also became concerned about the increasingly ruthless manner in which Victor confronted his enemies. She questioned him directly about the possibility that his actions were becoming monstrous, refusing to accept that overwhelming strength automatically justified every decision. Victor responded by explaining his moral distinctions and his continued willingness to protect individuals he regarded as innocent despite the extreme punishments he imposed upon certain enemies.[19]
Her independent judgment appeared again during discussions concerning future threats to Victor's faction. When he behaved with unusual restraint toward a potentially hostile civilization, Anna challenged his strategy, pointing out that the son she had raised normally preferred taking the initiative rather than allowing a danger to approach his family uncontested. Her ability to identify deviations from Victor's ordinary behavior demonstrated the value of knowing him before he became a supernatural ruler.[20]
The period also exposed increasing tensions surrounding Anna's personal relationships. Vampirism had altered the intensity of her feelings, while Victor's continued development as a Progenitor and Blood Dragon changed the circumstances under which she understood their bond. Several of his wives recognized that Anna was struggling with emotions she had not previously been willing to acknowledge. Their discussions emphasized that immortality and supernatural transformation could fundamentally change relationships that had originally developed under ordinary Human expectations.[46]
Anna did not immediately abandon her previous life or marriage because of these feelings. Her emotional development continued over a much longer period, eventually becoming inseparable from the extraordinary changes introduced by Victor's transformation into the Dragon God of [[Chaos]] and the family's subsequent accelerated existence.[46][4]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s transformation into a genuine Dragon God eventually gave him the ability to reconstruct members of his family into [[True Dragons]]. Anna participated in the enormous Dragonization process alongside many of the women connected to his Bloodline, abandoning the biological limitations of her Noble Vampire body and acquiring the fundamental characteristics of the Dragon Race.[47][3]
The transformation produced a particularly unusual result. Most Dragons possess strong supernatural relationships with particular Elements or natural phenomena, but Anna's new existence did not develop around a conventional Element. Instead, her strongest aptitude concerned the interaction between Reality, Truth, supernatural language and Draconic Runes. Her physical body acquired Draconic horns, wings and a powerful [[Dragon Heart]], while her existing Truth-oriented Authority continued evolving through the much greater supernatural foundation of a True Dragon.[3][10]
Victor recognized that Anna's transformation had produced a power requiring immediate attention. He arranged a controlled examination of her abilities, accompanied by [[Persephone]] and the [[World Trees]] connected to his existence. Before beginning the experiments, he sealed the surrounding room through Draconic Language to prevent any accidental manifestations from escaping into the rest of the household.[11]
The initial test involved asking Anna to express the word Weapon. Rather than producing a single predetermined object, her power manifested kitchen knives and representations of Victor because those were the things she associated with protection and dangerous weapons. When Victor asked her to describe a weapon of mass destruction, Reality responded by producing military equipment such as missiles and tanks, alongside another manifestation resembling her son.[11]
The experiment demonstrated that Anna's ability interpreted words through her personal understanding rather than through a fixed universal definition. Her subconscious memories and associations contributed directly to the result, creating objects and supernatural phenomena that she had not always consciously intended to produce.[11]
Victor subsequently tested the word Terror. Anna's greatest subconscious fear was the death of her son, and her power immediately attempted to impose that condition upon Victor. The manifestation could not overcome his vastly superior supernatural existence, causing the effect to generate an extraordinarily realistic representation of his corpse instead. The sight horrified Anna, who realized that her own subconscious could produce dangerous supernatural consequences even when she had no conscious desire to harm anyone.[11]
The experiment convinced Victor that Anna needed extensive training before being allowed to employ her abilities without supervision. Her natural potential was extraordinary, but the relationship between thought and manifestation made ordinary emotional mistakes considerably more dangerous than they would be for a conventional supernatural warrior.[11]
Victor assigned several of the most experienced women within his family to train her. [[Dun Scaith Elderblood]] was responsible for developing Anna's formal understanding of Runes, [[Zaladrac Zeovnur Elderblood]] assisted with the fundamental mechanics of Dragonhood, and [[Scathach]] concentrated upon the discipline and control necessary to prevent overwhelming supernatural power from becoming a substitute for personal competence.[11]
Anna's concern increased after Victor experienced a transformation affecting his Soul and briefly displayed physical symptoms that alarmed the women observing him. Her emotional reaction disturbed the surrounding Reality, demonstrating that even anger or fear could influence her abilities when she was insufficiently restrained.[25]
Recognizing the danger, Anna personally requested that Victor seal the relevant portions of her power while she learned to control them. Victor agreed and imposed the restriction, allowing her abilities to be used under conditions in which he could prevent dangerous consequences from spreading beyond his protection. The decision represented an important development in Anna's relationship with supernatural strength: she was willing to accept temporary limitations rather than endanger the people she loved.[25]
Her new Draconic instincts also intensified her possessiveness toward Victor. During a confrontation involving Persephone, Anna's hostility caused her supernatural aura to become threatening enough that Victor intervened. He reminded her that being a Dragon did not excuse abandoning emotional control or threatening another member of the family simply because of personal jealousy.[22]
Anna nevertheless continued developing closer relationships with the women surrounding Victor. Her involvement with [[Yog-Sothoth]] Elderblood illustrated her increasingly expansive maternal instincts. Rather than approach the child principally as a dangerous Outer God, Anna regarded her as another young member of Victor's family who needed affection, protection and the opportunity to develop normally.[48][49]
She subsequently joined Scathach, Jeanne and [[Hestia]] in rejecting the idea that the extraordinary abilities possessed by Victor's daughters should automatically make them tools for solving the problems of adults. Their supernatural potential was immense, but Anna considered their right to develop as children more important than immediately exploiting their powers for military or political benefit.[50]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
The first great period of accelerated development transformed Anna's life more completely than any previous change. Approximately two external years encompassed around two thousand years of accelerated Time within the territories governed by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], allowing the supernatural civilization and members of his family to develop across a period vastly exceeding the duration visible to the outside world.[4]
Anna experienced extensive personal development during this period, although the relationship between elapsed Time and subjective experience was not necessarily identical for every participant. Victor later explained that she had not experienced every year in the same manner as an ordinary Human living through continuous chronological Time, meaning that a simple numerical addition could not fully describe the psychological duration of her accelerated life.[51]
The period also changed Anna's personal relationship with Victor. Their previously complicated emotional attachment developed into an acknowledged marriage, and Anna became one of his wives within the expanding Imperial family. The transformation of their relationship did not erase their biological connection; Victor continued regarding her as his mother while also accepting the new family structure that had emerged through their supernatural existence.[4][15]
Anna and Victor became the biological parents of [[Stella Elderblood]], a daughter who inherited an extraordinary combination of her parents' supernatural characteristics. Stella possessed Reality-oriented powers similar to those of Anna and a particularly dangerous relationship with Truth and falsehood, demonstrating how their daughter's abilities could develop through the inherited potential of both Bloodlines.[4][15]
The changes also forced Anna and Leon to confront the condition of their original marriage. After an enormous period of separation and personal development, their reunion no longer resembled that of a conventional Human couple who had remained emotionally attached through ordinary married life. Leon recognized that Anna's feelings had changed, while Anna understood that the life they had shared as Humans could not simply be restored by pretending nothing had happened.[52][51]
Their relationship eventually ended through an acknowledgment of those changes rather than an attempt to preserve the marriage solely because it had existed for decades. Anna did not erase Leon from her life or deny the importance of their shared history. He remained Victor's father and an important member of the family, but their relationship no longer functioned as a marriage.[52][51][53]
Anna's Reality manipulation also advanced dramatically. By Chapter 998, she could enlarge the internal dimensions of a room without proportionally altering its exterior, creating enough space to accommodate hundreds of individuals whose physical forms possessed vastly different sizes. Her abilities could also be used for ordinary materialization and domestic convenience rather than remaining restricted to destructive combat applications.[54]
Her relationship with Leon also demonstrated another practical application of her powers. When the supernatural attraction associated with Victor began overwhelming Leon's senses, Anna employed a Reality-based filter to reduce the intensity of the effect. The technique demonstrated that her Reality manipulation could be used to regulate supernatural influences acting upon other individuals rather than merely create objects or alter the environment.[53]
Anna's participation in the family's ordinary activities increasingly reflected her new position as a senior maternal figure. Although her supernatural power was enormous, she continued focusing upon the education and discipline of the younger generation. She recognized that children born with Reality-altering abilities required supervision and emotional guidance far beyond that normally expected of Human parents.[15][55]
Her Reality specialization also acquired a particularly unusual administrative application. During the family's war games, Anna created additional versions of herself corresponding to earlier stages of her life, including her former Human and Noble Vampire states. These manifestations possessed access to her memories and could perform separate activities, allowing Anna to delegate responsibilities to supernatural representations of her previous selves.[55]
Anna's Divine identity became increasingly established as the Empire's religion developed. By Chapter 1061, she was recognized among the most prominent Goddesses receiving Faith within Victor's civilization, specifically under the title Goddess of Truth. Her supernatural authority consequently acquired an importance extending beyond her personal relationship with Victor or her role within the Imperial household.[9]
As the Empire's population and territories expanded, Anna became increasingly involved in internal administration. Victor relied upon her judgment and the authority of the senior Empresses while concentrating upon warfare, technological advancement and preparation for threats originating beyond the familiar supernatural territories of Earth.[56][57]
During Victor's reconnaissance operation beyond his native territory, Anna demonstrated that her Reality manipulation could operate within supernatural environments that were not ordinary physical spaces. While the Empresses communicated through a shared telepathic environment, Anna altered the mental space and produced a visual representation of the distant Solar System in which Victor was operating. The feat impressed the other women because it required manipulating the structure of a supernatural communication environment rather than merely altering conventional matter.[58]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
As the [[Elderblood Empire]] entered its later accelerated period, Anna's political position became increasingly formalized. During the discussions concerning [[Tyche]] Elderblood, she was identified as the Empress for Internal Affairs and the deputy of [[Violet Elderblood Snow]]. This confirmed her place among the highest internal authorities of the Imperial government, with responsibilities extending beyond ordinary household management.[5]
Anna's unique position within the family also became particularly important during the dispute concerning Violet's attempts to introduce additional women into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household. The younger generation strongly objected to the Empress making decisions affecting the family without involving the daughters, leading to a confrontation involving dozens of children and several of Victor's wives.[6]
Victor deliberately prohibited Anna from intervening because he understood that the daughters would almost certainly obey her. Rather than allow Anna's authority to end the dispute immediately, he wanted Violet and the children to address their disagreement themselves. The event demonstrated that Anna's status as one of the family's principal mothers could be more effective in domestic situations than even the formal political authority of the First Empress.[6]
During Chapter 1160, Anna's private collection of photographs showing Victor's Human childhood became the subject of a dispute between several Imperial daughters. [[Hinata Elderblood]] and [[Irene Elderblood]] had obtained pictures belonging to Stella, whose attachment to the collection was strengthened by the rarity of authentic records from her father's Human life. The originals were protected through Anna's Reality manipulation, preventing ordinary supernatural duplication or damage.[29]
Stella's inherited relationship with Truth made attempts to deceive her particularly ineffective, allowing her to recognize when her sisters were hiding information. After the dispute escalated, [[Yog-Sothoth]] Elderblood suggested that the girls simply ask Anna for copies rather than continue stealing or concealing the pictures. Anna willingly provided them, demonstrating that the principal difficulty had arisen from the daughters' assumptions and dishonesty rather than an absolute refusal to share the family memories.[29]
The photographs themselves possessed considerable emotional importance. Most of Victor's daughters had been born after his ascension into a supernatural ruler and had known him primarily as an Emperor, Dragon God or nearly incomprehensible Being. Images of him as an ordinary Human infant and young man offered a rare connection to the person he had been before the supernatural world transformed his life. Anna's collection was therefore valuable not simply because of its rarity, but because she personally remembered the circumstances surrounding those images.[29]
Anna's disciplinary authority appeared again during an incident involving daughters who had entered [[Pepper Scarlett Elderblood]]'s protected rooms without permission. When Anna discovered the intrusion, the girls feared that she would impose an exceptionally severe punishment. Pepper intervened and exercised her own authority to reduce the penalty, while Anna ultimately accepted the decision despite her irritation at the leniency shown toward the offenders.[59]
The incident illustrated the distinction between the different maternal authorities within the family. Anna favored discipline and respect for established household rules, while Pepper was considerably more forgiving toward the daughters. Both approaches existed within the same family structure, with Victor generally allowing his wives to exercise judgment over matters involving their own responsibilities.[59]
Anna's authority became particularly visible during the enormous family gathering in Chapter 1165. When [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]] discovered [[Nigredo Elderblood]] sleeping on the ceiling, she ordered her daughter to come down. Nigredo refused without hesitation, demonstrating the weakness of the relationship Albedo had developed with her daughter while spending much of her time isolated within laboratories.[60]
Anna then repeated the instruction. Nigredo immediately obeyed, despite possessing an extraordinary Eldritch and Divine existence that made her far more dangerous than an ordinary child. Anna subsequently reminded the daughters that they were expected to respect their mothers, making clear that their supernatural strength did not excuse disregarding ordinary family discipline.[60]
The contrast embarrassed Albedo and exposed the consequences of neglecting family relationships. Anna did not need to employ supernatural compulsion or threaten Nigredo with greater physical power. Her authority originated from her established maternal role and the respect she had earned throughout the younger generation.[60]
As the gathering continued, Victor arrived to spend time with his enormous family, immediately attracting the attention of daughters who competed enthusiastically for his affection. Anna attempted to maintain order, instructing the children to prepare for the upcoming meal and reminding them of the household's expectations concerning appropriate conduct. Her efforts were frequently disrupted by Victor himself, whose affectionate behavior encouraged the daughters to abandon discipline whenever he was present.[60]
The preparations for the traditional Imperial family meal produced another extraordinary demonstration of Anna's power. [[Scathach]] had obtained a gigantic Star Animal whose corpse exceeded [[Jupiter]] in size, making it impossible to prepare the creature within an ordinary dining environment. Anna immediately responded by expanding the surrounding Space.[61]
Within approximately one second, Anna enlarged the available area until three planets the size of Jupiter could fit side by side. The feat demonstrated how extensively her Reality manipulation had developed since the early experiments in which she struggled to control the manifestation of ordinary words. What had once been an exceptionally dangerous and unpredictable ability could now be employed almost casually to accommodate astronomical objects during a family celebration.[61]
Anna also contributed to the practical organization of the meal, suggesting that the enormous quantity of ingredients be prepared through different culinary traditions rather than using a single method. Her participation reinforced the unusual combination of supernatural scale and domestic familiarity characterizing the Elderblood household, where events involving creatures larger than planets could become ordinary family gatherings.[61]
During the arrival of Tyche as a new member of the family, Anna again played an important role in helping the Goddess adapt. She reassured Tyche that the household had already accepted her and that her position within the Empire made her inclusion unsurprising. Anna also wondered whether Tyche's extraordinary Luck had influenced the unusual circumstances through which her relationship with Victor developed.[62]
The later family discussions involved the possibility of Tyche becoming the mother of another exceptionally powerful Dragon daughter. Anna participated alongside Violet, Ruby, Sasha, Scathach and the other senior wives in considering the characteristics such a child might inherit, recognizing that children born from Victor's increasingly extraordinary existence frequently developed abilities exceeding the expectations of their parents.[28]
Anna also joined several of the principal wives in refusing to allow entry into Victor's Bloodline to become an automatic privilege of distant family relationships. They insisted that individuals seeking the advantages of Dragonhood needed to demonstrate merit and pass appropriate tests rather than receive the transformation merely because they possessed some biological connection to the Emperor.[28]
This position reflected Anna's belief that extraordinary power should be accompanied by responsibility. Victor possessed the ability to transform many individuals into lesser forms of Dragons, but the senior women of his family understood that indiscriminate distribution of his Bloodline could weaken the standards and discipline expected of those enjoying its privileges.[28]
Anna also became one of the principal figures criticizing Albedo's behavior toward Nigredo. When the family discussed the responsibility of monitoring their daughters' developing Divinities, Anna observed that Albedo had become excessively detached from ordinary family life. Her neglect had prevented her from understanding her daughter and developing the authority necessary to guide a child possessing an Eldritch Primordial existence.[28]
The family ultimately decided that Albedo required direct intervention. Scathach was selected to provide the necessary discipline and training, while Anna contributed her understanding of Draconic Runes, Divine development and the relationship between supernatural power and imagination.[12][13]
During Albedo's confrontation with Scathach, Anna explained why certain Reality-altering techniques failed against opponents possessing sufficiently superior power or Divine conception. She emphasized that a Being whose personal Reality had been strengthened through enormous combat experience and high-level Runes could resist attempts to impose external supernatural changes upon that existence.[12]
The narration explicitly identified Anna as the family's Master of Dragon Runes, while distinguishing her broader knowledge from Scathach's exceptional specialization in combat Runecraft. Anna herself did not claim that her technical mastery guaranteed victory against Scathach in a direct Rune confrontation, recognizing that the legendary warrior's practical experience and extreme training provided advantages within her particular field.[12]
Anna subsequently challenged Albedo's excessive dependence upon external spell structures. She explained that a Dragon Goddess should not behave like an ordinary Human magician who requires elaborate preparations to reproduce every supernatural phenomenon. Instead, Dragons possess a direct relationship with Reality that allows their own existence, imagination and understanding to serve as the medium through which their powers manifest.[13]
To demonstrate the principle, Anna spoke the word Life and manifested the qualities associated with the Tree of Life directly through her Reality manipulation. The demonstration did not mean that she had acquired the Universal Tree's actual primordial authority over Life. Rather, it showed that a sufficiently developed Dragon Goddess could reproduce conceptual qualities without needing to reconstruct every external magical mechanism normally used to access them.[13]
Anna's criticism of Albedo therefore concerned more than the woman's lack of social participation. She believed that the Dragon Goddess's isolation had limited both her imagination and the practical development of her powers. By refusing to participate in family life and relying excessively upon existing magical systems, Albedo had neglected opportunities to develop the creativity and supernatural understanding necessary for further advancement.[13]
The birth of [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]], Victor and Tyche's daughter, introduced another extraordinary child into the family. Moira immediately demonstrated a relationship with Luck and the Universe that exceeded ordinary Divine fortune, including the ability to obtain favorable outcomes without relying upon conventional supernatural Energy expenditure.[63]
Anna was astonished by the child's ability to obtain apparently impossible results through favorable circumstances. When Victor explained that Moira could partially disregard restrictions imposed upon her, Anna questioned how such a thing was possible. Her reaction reflected both her knowledge of advanced Reality manipulation and her recognition that the child's abilities originated through a mechanism different from conventional Draconic Runes.[63]
She also recognized that Moira's extraordinary power created serious parenting difficulties. A child capable of obtaining nearly anything she desired through the favor of the Universe could not be raised using ordinary methods of discipline, especially when the child possessed a comparatively immature understanding of the consequences of her actions.[63]
During the later confrontation involving Primordial threats, Victor placed Anna in charge of responsibilities associated with protecting and coordinating members of the family while the principal combatants prepared to respond. She also supported Tyche, whose recent transformation into a True Dragon had not yet provided enough practical experience for confronting threats at the level of Primordial Beings.[64]
The increasing population of the Empire eventually created another problem. The enormous number of individuals inhabiting its principal territories required the construction of new worlds capable of supporting further development. Victor decided to reconstruct Mars into a gigantic megaplanet rather than simply continue expanding the existing population centers.[65][14]
Anna participated directly in the project alongside Natalia and Zaladrac. Victor selected her because of her specialized ability to reinforce Reality, while Natalia could stabilize the surrounding Space-Time. The reconstruction required the simultaneous coordination of several advanced supernatural powers, allowing the planet to undergo enormous structural changes without collapsing under the pressure of the process.[14]
During the operation, Anna and Natalia manifested immense Dragon bodies already larger than Mars despite deliberately restraining their full dimensions. The narration explained that their unrestricted forms could approach the scale of entire Solar Systems, demonstrating the extraordinary physical development attained by the family's mature [[True Dragons]].[14]
Victor performed the principal reconstruction of Mars, enlarging the planet and establishing new environments capable of housing enormous populations. Anna reinforced the developing structures and amplified the effects of his Reality manipulation, while Natalia maintained the stability of the surrounding Space-Time. Zaladrac subsequently contributed to the project, producing a collaborative transformation far beyond the capabilities of conventional planetary engineering.[14]
The resulting world became one of the Empire's major population centers. During the public announcement concerning its completion, Anna was identified as the Goddess of Truth and credited alongside Victor, Natalia and Zaladrac for her participation in the transformation.[14]
Anna also participated in discussions concerning the merger of several Sectors with the territory governed by Victor. She questioned how the process would affect the classification of their existing Sector and confirmed that acquiring additional Galactic territory would not automatically cause the original Sector to advance into a higher cosmological category.[66]
The successful integration of additional Sectors nevertheless placed even greater demands upon Victor. As the accelerated development program continued, his enormous Energy reserves became increasingly depleted. The maintenance of the project and the need to prevent the surrounding Sector from advancing prematurely required constant expenditure, eventually consuming more Energy than his body and supporting supernatural systems could replenish.[30]
Anna became deeply concerned by his condition. Although Victor had maintained the ritual for nearly two hundred thousand subjective years, she recognized that the Empire had already achieved much of its intended development. She therefore demanded that the process be stopped rather than continue imposing an increasingly dangerous burden upon the individual supporting the entire civilization.[30]
Victor resisted because he believed that additional preparation remained necessary. His concerns were intensified by the possibility of future conflicts against Primordial Beings and the development of experimental weapons intended to confront threats exceeding the ordinary limits of the Empire's military forces. Anna nevertheless argued that uncertainty concerning the future did not justify weakening him indefinitely.[30]
She reminded Victor that his existence served as the foundation of the entire Empire. The planetary systems, [[Dragon Heart]], [[World Trees]] and other structures connected to him were affected by the growing imbalance in his Energy reserves. Although the immediate consequences remained manageable because of his extraordinary resilience, Anna recognized that allowing the situation to deteriorate further represented an unacceptable risk.[30]
Victor ultimately placed his physical body into an Energy-saving state while allowing his consciousness to remain active. Anna suggested that he use the opportunity to teach his daughters, allowing him to remain involved in family life without continuously placing the same demands upon his physical existence.[30]
The suggestion led to Victor personally assuming responsibility for lessons normally taught by the senior mothers. Anna and Jeanne were among the women who regularly instructed the younger generation, but Victor's appearance as a teacher immediately transformed the daughters' enthusiasm. The girls gathered eagerly, treating the opportunity to receive instruction directly from their father as an extraordinary event.[67]
Anna observed the lesson alongside Tyche, explaining that Victor was operating through his consciousness while his physical body recovered. She also clarified that the extraordinary strain upon his Energy reserves originated partly from the need to prevent the increasingly developed Sector from advancing before the Emperor considered the necessary preparations complete.[67]
The lesson demonstrated the difference between Victor's understanding of supernatural systems and the knowledge possessed by even his most talented daughters. He explained the importance of Draconic Language, the relationship between Runes and Creation, and the manner in which supernatural commands could alter the structure of Reality.[68]
Victor specifically identified Anna as someone who had mastered the use of Draconic Runes to alter Reality, providing his daughters with a practical example of an advanced application developed by another member of the family. Anna's status as a specialist consequently became part of the educational framework used to explain how different Dragons could refine particular supernatural principles according to their own talents.[68]
During the lesson, Moira also demonstrated the extraordinary relationship she possessed with the Universe. When she became concerned about Victor's exhausted condition and wished for him to recover, an enormous quantity of Energy entered his body despite the defensive restrictions he had established. The manifestation temporarily restored a significant portion of his depleted reserves, astonishing Anna and Tyche, who recognized that the child's power could bypass mechanisms that even Victor had expected to remain effective.[67]
Anna's reaction reflected both maternal concern and the experience she had accumulated studying Reality-altering abilities. She understood that Moira's relationship with Luck and the Universe operated through a supernatural mechanism that differed from the controlled Draconic Reality Distortion she had spent thousands of years developing.[67]
Her responsibilities did not diminish after Victor began personally teaching the daughters. Anna continued maintaining order among the younger generation and participating in the broader supervision of their development, recognizing that the educational process needed to address discipline and emotional maturity as well as the acquisition of supernatural techniques.[67][68]
By the latest events, Anna remains one of the principal stabilizing figures within the Elderblood Empire. She continues balancing her responsibilities as Second Empress, internal administrator, Dragon Goddess, Runemaster, wife and mother while confronting the increasingly difficult consequences of raising a family whose supernatural potential can affect planets, galaxies and even the fundamental structures of Reality.[30][68]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Anna's biological son, supernatural Progenitor, husband and the father of their daughter [[Stella Elderblood]]. Their relationship began as one of the strongest emotional foundations of Victor's Human life, with Anna and Leon supporting him throughout his childhood despite the physical limitations imposed by his rare RH-null blood.[1][4]
Anna played an important role in shaping Victor's personality. His preference for honesty, strong attachment to family and willingness to disregard unnecessary social formalities resemble her own behavior. Although Victor acquired extraordinary supernatural power after becoming a Vampire, he continued regarding Anna as his mother and one of the people whose safety mattered most to him.[1][20]
Their relationship became supernatural as well as biological when Victor transformed Anna into a Noble Vampire. He became her Progenitor through the reconstruction of her body, creating an unusual situation in which the son she had raised was simultaneously the source of her new supernatural lineage.[2]
The relationship changed further following Anna's Dragonization and the enormous accelerated development period. They eventually became husband and wife, while Anna continued retaining her maternal identity and Victor continued acknowledging her as his mother. Their marriage also produced Stella, whose supernatural abilities reflected the extraordinary heritage of both parents.[4][15]
Victor remains extremely protective of Anna despite her enormous power. During her earlier Vampire existence, a perceived threat against her was sufficient to provoke immediate hostility from him. Later, even after Anna became capable of manipulating Reality on astronomical scales, he continued responding strongly when her safety was endangered.[45][25]
Anna is equally protective of Victor. She regularly criticizes his tendency to expose himself to unnecessary danger or work beyond reasonable limits, and she is capable of questioning decisions that other members of the Empire might accept without hesitation. Her intervention during the accelerated development ritual demonstrates that even the strongest Being within the Empire cannot expect Anna to ignore his well-being simply because he insists that he remains capable of enduring the burden.[30]
Their relationship therefore combines the emotional familiarity of a mother and son, the supernatural connection between Progenitor and descendant, and the personal intimacy of marriage. Its development reflects the increasingly unconventional structure of the Elderblood family while preserving the fundamental affection that existed between them long before Victor entered the supernatural world.[1][4]

#### Leon Walker
[[Leon Walker]] is Anna's former husband and the biological father of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Their marriage originally provided the stable domestic environment in which Victor was raised, with both parents sharing responsibility for supporting their son through his unusual medical condition.[1]
Anna and Leon remained married throughout the early supernatural events and eventually underwent transformation into Noble Vampires through Victor's Blood. However, the changes introduced by their supernatural instincts, the enormous expansion of Victor's family and the passage of accelerated Time gradually altered the emotional foundation of their relationship.[2][17]
Leon initially struggled considerably more than Anna to control the emotional and predatory instincts awakened through Vampirism. Their subsequent separation during training and later responsibilities contributed to the weakening of the relationship, while Anna's increasing attachment to Victor introduced additional complications.[17][52]
After the first enormous period of accelerated development, Anna and Leon recognized that their relationship no longer resembled the marriage they had maintained as Humans. Rather than continue pretending that their previous feelings remained unchanged, they accepted the end of their marriage.[52][51]
Anna nevertheless retained a familial connection with Leon because of their shared history and his position as Victor's father. Their separation did not invalidate the importance of the life they had built together or the role that both had played in raising their son.[51][53]

#### Stella Elderblood
[[Stella Elderblood]] is the biological daughter of Anna and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], born during the development of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. She inherited extraordinary supernatural potential from both parents, including Reality-oriented abilities and a powerful relationship with Truth and falsehood.[4][15]
Stella possesses a particularly dangerous passive ability connected to deception, allowing her to recognize when other individuals are lying. During Chapter 1160, this characteristic made it exceptionally difficult for her sisters to conceal their involvement in taking photographs from Anna's private collection.[29]
Anna's relationship with Stella reflects the unusual circumstances surrounding the younger Elderblood generation. Her first child, Victor, had been a chronically ill Human whose physical condition restricted nearly every aspect of his childhood. Stella, by contrast, was born into one of the most powerful supernatural lineages in existence and possessed abilities capable of altering Reality itself.[1][4]
The difference reinforces Anna's commitment to discipline and education. She recognizes that children with extraordinary power require emotional maturity and strict boundaries rather than the assumption that supernatural strength alone makes them capable of making responsible decisions.[29][28]

#### Aphrodite Elderblood
[[Aphrodite]] Elderblood is Anna's best friend and one of the individuals who knew her long before she fully entered the supernatural world. Anna was one of the few Humans whom Aphrodite considered a real friend, making the relationship emotionally important despite the enormous difference between their original statuses.[31]
The revelation of Aphrodite's identity temporarily strained their friendship. Anna was disturbed by the extent of the deception and the possibility that Aphrodite had used supernatural abilities to influence their professional circumstances, but she did not immediately reject the relationship. Instead, she acknowledged the help Aphrodite had provided and asked for time to understand the truth.[31]
Their friendship eventually survived the revelation and became part of the much larger relationship network surrounding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. The two women later became members of the same Imperial family, allowing their longstanding connection to develop under circumstances neither had originally expected.[31][4]

#### Violet Elderblood Snow

Anna occupies one of the most important maternal positions within the entire younger generation of the Elderblood family. ### Role in the Elderblood Empire
Anna's mature political position is that of Second Empress and Empress for Internal Affairs, serving as a senior authority beneath Violet within the Imperial administration. Her responsibilities concern the stability of the Empire's internal institutions and the management of problems that cannot be resolved through conventional military command alone.[5]
Her previous career as a lawyer provides a foundation for understanding institutional responsibility, agreements, conflicts of interest, testimony and the practical consequences of administrative decisions. These skills remain valuable even within an Empire possessing supernatural technology and military forces capable of operating across enormous portions of the Cosmos.[1][5]
Anna also possesses a particularly important role within the Imperial family. Her authority over the younger generation allows her to enforce household expectations, intervene in disputes and provide guidance to daughters whose powers can affect Reality on a scale far beyond ordinary supernatural abilities.[6][60]
Her supernatural Authority over Truth complements these responsibilities, making deception difficult for susceptible individuals within her presence. Nevertheless, Anna's administrative influence cannot be reduced exclusively to supernatural compulsion. Much of her authority originates from the trust, respect and familiarity she has developed through personal relationships with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his descendants.[8][6]
Anna is additionally recognized as one of the family's foremost authorities on Draconic Runes. Her knowledge allows her to contribute to the education of the younger generation, evaluate advanced supernatural techniques and participate directly in projects involving Reality manipulation on planetary and greater scales.[12][14][68]
Her participation in the reconstruction of Mars demonstrates that she is not merely an administrator who happens to possess Divine power. Anna can also serve as an essential supernatural specialist during operations whose success depends upon maintaining structural Reality while Victor and other senior Goddesses perform extraordinary transformations.[14]
Perhaps her most important responsibility, however, is preserving the stability of the family itself. Anna understands Victor's behavior more intimately than most of the women who entered his life after his transformation, allowing her to identify when his pursuit of greater strength or preparation is beginning to endanger the very people and institutions he intends to protect.[30]

### Abilities

#### Powers & Abilities
Anna Elderblood is a True Blood Dragon, a Dragon Goddess, the Goddess of Truth, and one of the most naturally gifted users of Draconic Runes within the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Her supernatural abilities are primarily divided between her Divine Authority over Truth, her exceptionally advanced Reality Distortion, and the broader supernatural characteristics of her True Dragon physiology. Unlike most Dragons, whose abilities develop around a particular Element or natural phenomenon, Anna possesses an extraordinary aptitude for imposing her personal understanding of Reality upon existence through supernatural language, [[Will Solace|Will]], and Energy.[1][2]
Anna's original supernatural ability emerged when [[Victor Elderblood]] transformed her into a Noble Vampire. Her subconscious desire for honesty caused the ordinary Vampiric Charm inherited through his Bloodline to mutate into a fundamentally different power. Rather than merely influencing the emotions of a target or compelling obedience through eye contact, Anna generated an invisible domain of Authority that communicated directly with the subconscious of affected beings and altered their Will. This ability initially manifested as a passive compulsion forcing people to speak honestly, but Victor subsequently discovered that its underlying mechanism was considerably more powerful than ordinary Vampiric Charm.[3][4]
Her transformation into a True Dragon awakened an entirely different supernatural specialization. Anna discovered that words spoken with sufficient Draconic Will could alter Reality according to her own perception of their meaning. Her subconscious memories, emotional associations, personal beliefs, and understanding of the world became directly involved in determining the result. Consequently, even an apparently simple word could produce several different manifestations depending upon what Anna associated with it.[2]
During her initial training, Anna unintentionally manifested kitchen knives and representations of Victor when instructed to create weapons, because both were associated with protection and danger within her subconscious. When asked to express the Concept of Terror, her power attempted to impose her greatest fear—the death of Victor—before producing an extremely convincing manifestation of his corpse after failing to affect him directly. These demonstrations established both the extraordinary versatility of Anna's abilities and the danger of allowing them to operate without sufficient control.[2]
Anna's development continued throughout the Empire's accelerated history. She eventually became recognized as the family's broad Master of Dragon Runes, developed advanced applications of Reality Distortion, and demonstrated the ability to alter astronomical quantities of Space almost instantaneously. Her later techniques include Reality-based object creation, dimensional expansion, self-replication, supernatural effect filtering, Reality protection, Conceptual manifestation, and the reinforcement of planetary structures during extremely large-scale transformations.[5][6][7]
The nature of Anna's powers nevertheless requires an important distinction. Her confirmed Divine identity is Goddess of Truth, while Reality Distortion is her principal Draconic supernatural specialization. Although the two abilities can appear related because both concern the relationship between understanding and what is considered real, the story identifies different origins and mechanisms for them. Her Truth Authority developed from mutated Vampiric Charm, whereas her Reality Distortion awakened through the supernatural potential of her True Dragon existence.[4][2][8]
Anna's advanced Draconic existence is characterized by her Divine Authority over Truth and her extraordinary Reality Distortion.

Anna's Truth power is considerably more complex than a passive ability to identify dishonesty. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] discovered its actual mechanism after examining her Vampire-derived supernatural Energy. When the ability is active, Anna creates a domain of Authority affecting beings within its range. The influence communicates directly with their subconscious and changes their [[Will Solace|Will]], causing obedience without necessarily revealing the presence of an external mental influence.[4]
The effect differs from [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood's Divine Charm. Aphrodite's power influences desire, attraction, and motivation, causing targets to act according to the impressions generated by her Beauty. Anna's power directly influences the target's subconscious decisions rather than relying upon attraction or emotional persuasion.[4]
Anna's Authority therefore possesses characteristics associated with both Truth and mental domination, but those effects should not be treated as identical to unrestricted Mind Control over all existence. The ability must affect the target successfully, and Victor explicitly demonstrated that beings possessing sufficient protection or superior supernatural status can resist it.[4]

##### Truth Detection and Falsehood
The ability to force truthful expression should be distinguished from independently detecting every objective falsehood. Anna's original power compels susceptible people to speak honestly, but this does not necessarily mean that every statement they make is factually accurate. Anna's daughter [[Stella Elderblood]] possesses a passive ability that can immediately recognize when others are lying. - Lie Detection (Related Family Ability): Stella can directly identify lies even while controlling her passive power. This establishes a related manifestation within Anna's family but does not independently prove that Anna possesses every aspect of Stella's specific detection ability.[12]
Classification distinction: Anna's demonstrated Authority is powerful enough to compel truthful testimony and influence subconscious [[Will Solace|Will]]. - Potential Positive and Negative Energy Development: Complete access to the fundamental Positive and Negative Energies was identified as a hypothetical means of making Anna's Reality Distortion even stronger.[2]
- Potential Rune-Related Divine Development: The possession of an appropriate Rune-related Divinity was also discussed as a hypothetical development that could greatly increase her powers.[2]
- Specialized Growth Potential: Victor recognized that Anna's concentration upon Reality Distortion might theoretically allow her to surpass his corresponding specialization under highly favorable future conditions.[2]
Important distinction: Anna's natural relationship with Energy does not establish that she currently possesses complete authority over the fundamental Concepts of Positivity and Negativity. The comparison in Chapter 926 concerns the potential of her specialization, not a completed set of powers.[2]

#### Self-Restriction and Power Regulation
Anna's early Reality Distortion was dangerous partly because her subconscious could activate or influence effects that she had not deliberately intended. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] recognized that uncontrolled words, thoughts, or emotional reactions could produce severe consequences for the people around her.[2]
The danger became especially apparent following Victor's encounter with a supernatural transformation affecting his Soul. Anna reacted angrily to the possibility that her son was disregarding his own safety, causing the Reality around her to respond to her emotional state.[13]
Recognizing the danger of allowing her abilities to remain unrestricted, Anna personally requested that Victor seal her Rune-related powers until she acquired better control. Victor complied by imposing a supernatural restriction that permitted the relevant abilities to be released when she was near him.[13]
- Power Regulation: Anna can deliberately restrain aspects of her supernatural activity when she recognizes that uncontrolled use could endanger others.[13]
- Voluntary Power Restriction: Anna requested that Victor limit her dangerous abilities while she developed sufficient control.[13]
- External Power Sealing: Victor imposed the actual supernatural seal upon Anna at her request.[13]
- Conditional Power Release: The restriction permitted the relevant abilities to be used in Victor's presence, where he could prevent the consequences from escaping his control.[13]
- Emotional Control Training: Anna recognized the importance of controlling strong emotions because of their influence upon her Reality manipulation.[13]
- Subconscious Power Regulation: Her training required understanding the meanings and associations responsible for unintended manifestations.[2]
- Intent Discipline: Anna needed to develop clearer intentions so her power would manifest the result she actually wanted rather than an uncontrolled interpretation.[2]
- Concept Definition Training: Her abilities become safer and more predictable when she possesses a sufficiently precise understanding of the Concept she is attempting to express.[2]
- Protective Rune Containment: Victor initially sealed the testing environment to prevent Anna's uncontrolled manifestations from spreading beyond the room.[2]
Sealing distinction: Anna did not demonstrate the ability to impose the original restriction upon herself through independent mastery of sealing techniques. The confirmed event establishes that she requested the restriction and Victor performed the seal.[13]
The presence of mature, unrestricted Reality Distortion feats in much later chapters demonstrates that Anna continued developing her powers after this early training period. However, the precise chapter in which the original seal was permanently lifted or replaced has not been independently established.[13][16]

- Telepathy: Anna can participate in supernatural mental communication with Victor and members of the Elderblood family.[18]
- Group Telepathy: She can communicate with multiple Empresses through a shared mental connection.[18]

- Mental Communication Coordination: Anna can participate in strategic discussions and exchange information while the relevant supernatural connection remains active.[18]

Anna possesses genuine Divine status within the Pantheon associated with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Empire. Her recognized title is Goddess of Truth, reflecting the continued development of the supernatural Authority originally awakened through her Vampire transformation.[8]
- Divine Status: Anna is a Dragon Goddess belonging to the Divine hierarchy of the [[Elderblood Empire]].[8]
- Divine Concept — Truth: Her publicly recognized Divine identity concerns Truth and honesty.[8]
- Faith Reception: Anna receives Faith through the prayers and recognition of Imperial citizens.[8]
- Religious Recognition: Her title as Goddess of Truth is recognized within the Imperial religion.[8]
- Prominent Divine Worship: Anna was identified among the most prominent Goddesses receiving prayers within Victor's Pantheon.[8]
- Divine Authority Development: Her original honesty-oriented supernatural ability evolved alongside her Dragon Goddess status.[3][8]
- Divine Reality Specialization: Anna's Draconic development concentrates upon Reality Distortion, allowing her to combine exceptional racial aptitude with genuine Divine power.[2][5]
The title Goddess of Reality is sometimes used descriptively in reference to Anna's abilities, but the directly verified public worship and ceremonial identification concern the Goddess of Truth. Her Reality Distortion is independently established as her principal Draconic specialization.[2][8][7]

#### Former Powers

##### Human Physiology
Before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] transformed her into a Noble Vampire, Anna possessed ordinary Human physiology. Her early life and professional experiences nevertheless established much of the personality and judgment she retained through later supernatural transformations.[22]
- Human Physiology (Former): Anna originally possessed an ordinary Human body without the supernatural biological characteristics later acquired through Vampirism.[22]
- Human Intelligence: She possessed the ordinary cognitive abilities and learned skills associated with her Human life.[22]
- Legal Training: Anna practiced professionally as a lawyer before joining the supernatural world.[22]
- Human Aging (Former): Her original body was subject to ordinary Human aging.[22]
- Human Physical Limitations (Former): Her original constitution lacked the extraordinary physical capabilities acquired through her later transformations.[22]

##### Noble Vampire Physiology
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] personally transformed Anna into a Noble Vampire through his Progenitor Blood. The transformation reconstructed her body and awakened the supernatural foundation responsible for the development of her Truth Authority.[9][3]
- Noble Vampire Physiology (Former): Anna possessed the supernatural biological characteristics of a Noble Vampire directly descended from Victor.[9]
- Progenitor Blood Rebirth: Victor's Blood reconstructed Anna's body during her transformation into a Vampire.[9]
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Her Vampire body acquired enhanced strength, speed, reflexes, durability, and senses compared with her Human existence.[9]
- Vampiric Regeneration: Anna gained the regenerative abilities associated with Noble Vampires.[9]
- Vampiric Longevity: The transformation replaced ordinary Human aging with the extended existence associated with Vampires.[9]
- Vampiric Bloodlust: Anna acquired the increased Vampiric instincts associated with blood consumption and supernatural physical development.[29]
- Emotion Amplification: Her existing emotions and desires became more intense following transformation.[30]
- Vampiric Instinct Adaptation: Anna displayed better initial control over her transformed instincts than Leon, whose adjustment was considerably more difficult.[29][30]
- Mutated Vampiric Charm: Anna's original Charm mutated into an honesty-oriented Authority rather than developing as ordinary Vampiric mental attraction.[3]
- Passive Honesty Field: Her main awakened ability initially forced susceptible individuals in her surroundings to speak honestly.[3]
- Subconscious Authority: Victor later identified the deeper mechanism as an influence upon the subconscious [[Will Solace|Will]] of targets inside the domain.[4]
Anna's former Human and Noble Vampire biological conditions were superseded by her complete True Dragon transformation. Her legal expertise and Truth-oriented Authority survived because they were not dependent upon retaining an ordinary Human or Noble Vampire body.[1]

#### Resistances and Defensive Applications

##### Racial Resistances
- Physical Damage Resistance: Anna possesses the enhanced durability associated with True Dragon physiology.[1]
- Regenerative Resistance to Injury: Her supernatural constitution provides recovery from injuries beyond ordinary biological limits.[1]
- Enhanced Environmental Tolerance: Anna's True Dragon physiology supports activity in extreme supernatural environments, including operations conducted in the vicinity of celestial bodies.[7]
- Biological Longevity: Her Draconic existence is not subject to ordinary Human aging.[1]
- Vampiric Weakness Reduction: Biological limitations originating specifically from her former Human and Noble Vampire conditions no longer define her completed True Dragon body.[1]
- Supernatural Aura: Anna possesses an exceptionally powerful Draconic presence capable of intimidating other supernatural beings.[10]

##### Reality-Based Defensive Applications
- Reality Filter: Anna can reduce certain supernatural influences acting upon an individual by imposing a filtering condition upon Reality.[17]
- Charm Reduction: Her filter successfully reduced [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s restrained supernatural attraction affecting Leon.[17]
- Reality Locking: Anna can impose protective conditions upon specific objects, making supernatural alteration more difficult.[12]
- Object Protection: She can preserve valuable objects against damage through Reality-based effects.[12]
- Duplication Prevention: Her protection prevented powerful family members from casually reproducing the photographs she had preserved.[12]
- Structural Reinforcement: Anna can stabilize planetary structures while they are subjected to enormous supernatural transformation.[7]
- Reality Defense Understanding: She understands how greater existential power and Divine conception can protect a Being against Reality-altering attacks.[5]
- Defensive Power Regulation: Anna voluntarily requested restrictions upon her dangerous Reality abilities while developing sufficient control.[13]

##### Unconfirmed Resistances
Anna's Dragon Goddess physiology implies extraordinary supernatural resilience, but the following should not be treated as established unconditional immunities without direct demonstrations:
- Absolute immunity to all forms of Reality Manipulation.
- Unrestricted resistance to every Divine Concept.
- Complete immunity to Soul Destruction or Existence Erasure.
- Absolute immunity to all forms of Mental Manipulation.
- Automatic resistance to every Eldritch influence.
- Unconditional protection against Time Manipulation.
- Invulnerability to attacks from beings possessing greater existential authority.
- Perfect protection against every effect created through Draconic Runes.
Her demonstrated defensive abilities are significant, but their effectiveness remains subject to comparative power, Energy, Divine conception, and the specific mechanisms involved.[2][5]
