### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 1165
Alisa Sasha Elderblood is an Imperial Princess of the [[Elderblood Empire]] and the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]]. She belongs to the enormous second generation of Victor's family and is part of the Fulger branch through her mother. Alisa is first explicitly identified in Chapter 1165, where the novel directly names her as "Alisa Sasha Elderblood, daughter of Carmila and Victor."[1]
Alisa is a completely separate character from both [[Julia Elderblood]] and [[Aline Elderblood Valefar]]. An earlier publication of Chapter 997 mistakenly called Naty and Victor's daughter Aline Elderblood, despite that same character being described as having been named after the deceased maid Julia. The current official Chapter 997 corrects her name to Julia Elderblood.[2] There is no canon character established as Aline Elderblood Fulger; that identity resulted from incorrectly separating Chapter 997's erroneous name from the character who became Julia and then attaching it to Carmila's family line.

### Physical Appearance
Alisa has blonde hair and violet eyes. She is introduced alongside several other blonde descendants of the Fulger family, including [[Sylvia Elderblood]], Ekaterina Natasha Elderblood, [[Julia Elderblood]] and [[Svetlana Veronika Elderblood]]. All of the women in this group are described as having blonde hair and violet eyes.[1]
Despite being Carmila's daughter, Alisa is specifically stated to have inherited more of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s facial features. Chapter 1165 groups her with Svetlana Veronika in this respect, while Julia and Sylvia are described as resembling their respective mothers much more closely.[1]
Her exact height, hairstyle, clothing, body proportions and individual Dragon Form have not yet been described in sufficient detail.

### Personality
Alisa has not yet received enough individually identifiable dialogue or focused characterization to establish a detailed personality. Her only confirmed appearance places her naturally among [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s other daughters during a large gathering of the Imperial Princesses, but Chapter 1165 does not isolate any actions or dialogue that would justify assigning her specific behavioral traits.[1]

### Synopsis

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Alisa is first explicitly introduced during the enormous gathering of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters caused by the commotion surrounding [[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]]. [[Ariella Victoria Elderblood]] and [[Valentina Victoria Elderblood]] are discussing the disturbance when a group of blonde members of the family arrives. [[Sylvia Elderblood]] leads the group, accompanied by Ekaterina Natasha Elderblood, [[Julia Elderblood]], [[Svetlana Veronika Elderblood]] and Alisa Sasha Elderblood.[1]
The narration directly identifies Alisa as the daughter of Carmila and Victor. The women are described collectively as possessing blonde hair and violet eyes, while Alisa and Veronika are specifically distinguished by inheriting more of Victor's facial appearance. Julia and Sylvia, in contrast, more strongly resemble their mothers.[1]
The gathering rapidly grows as additional members of Victor's second generation arrive. More than thirty-five daughters eventually assemble, followed by figures such as [[Nero Elderblood]], [[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]], [[Metis]] Elderblood, [[Akasha Elderblood]] and [[Melida Charlotte Elderblood]]. Alisa remains among the assembled Imperial Princesses, although the chapter does not give her an individual conversation or combat scene.[1]

### Heritage

#### Elderblood Lineage
Alisa is one of [[Victor Elderblood]]'s biological daughters and consequently belongs directly to the Imperial Elderblood lineage. Victor's daughters inherit far more than ordinary physical characteristics from him. Chapter 1052 establishes that his daughters possess inherited Draconic traits, accelerated adaptation and extensive protections placed upon their bodies, minds and Souls.[3]
During the later Imperial era, [[True Dragons]] remain an exceptionally rare Race. Even after the tremendous expansion of Victor's civilization, only approximately forty to fifty True Dragons exist throughout the Sector, with the Race deliberately concentrated within the Imperial Family.[4] Later discussion further explains that True Dragons are restricted to the Emperor's immediate family, although several mothers deliberately chose not to make their own children True Dragons.[5]

#### Fulger Lineage
Alisa is the biological daughter of [[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]], placing her unusually close to the origin of the [[Fulger Clan]]'s supernatural lineage.
Carmila was the matriarch and ancestor from whom the Fulger family's famous association with Lightning originated. Long before Carmila's restoration, Sasha explained that Lightning existed within her family's Bloodline because of Carmila and identified her grandmother as the ancestor who founded their family line.[6] Later revelations confirmed that Carmila had originally been a powerful Lightning Spirit before becoming a Vampire, explaining why her descendants developed their characteristic affinity with Lightning.[7]
Carmila's physical body had died long before the beginning of the story, but part of her Soul remained attached to Natashia. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually discovered Carmila within Natashia's Soul and separated the abnormal spiritual existences involved, allowing Carmila to once again exist independently.[7][8] Carmila later became part of Victor's family, and Alisa was eventually born as their daughter.
Because Carmila is also the mother of Natashia and [[Victoria Fulger Elderblood]], Alisa occupies an unusual position in the extended Fulger genealogy. Natashia and Victoria are her maternal half-sisters, while [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] is her maternal niece. Several of Victor's other daughters consequently possess overlapping relationships with Alisa through both Victor and the Fulger family.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Alisa's biological father. Through him, Alisa belongs to the [[Elderblood Imperial Family]] and inherits the supernatural traits and protections shared among his biological daughters.[3]
Victor's safeguards extend beyond ordinary parental protection. A portion of each daughter's Soul is maintained within Victor, while a fragment of Victor's own existence resides within them as a final defense against attacks capable of bypassing their normal protections.[3]

#### Carmila Fulger Elderblood
[[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]] is Alisa's biological mother.[1] Through Carmila, Alisa descends directly from the original Fulger matriarch instead of reaching Carmila through the several generations separating most modern members of the Clan from its founder.
No dedicated mother-daughter interaction between Carmila and Alisa has yet been shown, so the details of their personal relationship remain unknown.

#### Annasthashia Elderblood Fulger and Victoria Fulger Elderblood
[[Annasthashia Elderblood Fulger]] and [[Victoria Fulger Elderblood]] are Alisa's maternal half-sisters because all three are daughters of Carmila.[8][1]
The enormous difference between their generations is caused by the extraordinary circumstances surrounding Carmila's death, survival within Natashia's Soul, later restoration and eventual relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].

#### Sasha Elderblood Fulger
[[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] is Alisa's maternal niece: Carmila is Natashia's mother, Natashia is Sasha's mother, and Carmila is Alisa's mother.
Because Sasha is also one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wives, the Elderblood side of the family creates an overlapping relationship in which Alisa is simultaneously the daughter of Sasha's husband and Sasha's maternal aunt.

#### Julia Elderblood
[[Julia Elderblood]] and Alisa are separate characters. Julia is the daughter of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Naty, while Alisa is the daughter of Victor and Carmila.[2][1]
The distinction is important because an older version of Chapter 997 called Julia Aline Elderblood. That publication error is the source of the former wiki character Aline Elderblood Fulger; it does not establish an additional daughter of Carmila.

### Skills & Abilities
Alisa has not yet received an individual battle or power demonstration. The abilities below are limited to characteristics explicitly stated to be shared among [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters or the Imperial True Dragon lineage rather than powers inferred solely from Carmila.
- Dragon Physiology: As one of Victor's biological daughters, Alisa belongs to the Draconic generation of the Elderblood family. Victor's daughters are repeatedly treated as Dragons, while later chapters establish that the True Dragon population is overwhelmingly concentrated within the Emperor's immediate family.[3][4][5]
- Enhanced Physical Characteristics: Dragons possess physical and supernatural characteristics far beyond ordinary races, with even the younger members of Victor's family being treated as exceptionally powerful beings by the standards of the wider Sector.[4]
- Environmental Resistance: Victor's daughters can remain unaffected by environmental conditions dangerous to most beings. During the War Games, temperatures approaching −60°C produced no meaningful discomfort among the daughters present.[3]
- Divine Physiology: Later discussion concerning Victor's children states that his daughters possess inherited Eldritch and Primordial Divine characteristics, although only some develop particularly abnormal or fully individualized Divine manifestations.[5]
- Divinity (Unknown): Alisa's personal Divine Concept has not yet been revealed.
- Adaptive Evolution / Reactive Adaptation: Victor's daughters inherited part of his passive adaptive capabilities. Their adaptation is less extreme than Victor's own resurrection-based evolution, instead functioning continuously until previously uncomfortable environmental conditions cease to affect them normally.[3]
- Soul Invasion Immunity: Victor specifically states that his daughters possess complete protection against Soul invasion.[3]
- Soul Protection: A fragment of Victor's own existence remains with his daughters as an additional defensive layer if their ordinary protections are bypassed.[3]
- Mind Invasion Immunity: Victor's protections prevent conventional hostile invasion or alteration of his daughters' minds.[3]
- Mind Manipulation Resistance: Victor's protective fragment exists specifically to defend them against mental interference and possible Mind Alteration.[3]
- Biological Threat Resistance: Victor designed his daughters' bodies to reject parasites, viruses and other invasive substances that could bypass ordinary physical defenses.[3]
- Adaptive Biological Security: These protections also account for artificial threats designed to deceive the body by presenting themselves as beneficial substances.[3]
- Soul Anchoring: Victor carries part of each daughter's Soul within himself.[3]
- Resurrection (Via Victor): If one of his daughters is physically killed by an enemy while the preserved Soul connection remains intact, she can revive through Victor in a manner compared to his Soul-Married wives.[3]
- Threat Detection (Via Victor): The protections surrounding Victor's family can warn him when someone seriously attacks or interferes with his daughters.[3]

### Continuity Notes
The former Aline Elderblood Fulger identity was created from a misinterpretation of Chapter 997 rather than from a separate canon character.
An earlier publication of Chapter 997 introduced one of Naty and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters as Aline Elderblood. However, the surrounding text simultaneously stated that the girl had been named after the Human maid who raised Sasha as a mother figure. That maid was Julia, not Aline. The current official version of Chapter 997 corrects the daughter's name to [[Julia Elderblood]].[2]
Nothing in Chapter 997 identifies that girl as Carmila's daughter. Her mother is explicitly Naty, and the physical description, long hair, Cultivation-related Divinity and other characteristics described in that chapter therefore belong to Julia Elderblood.
Chapter 1165 later introduces a different daughter for Carmila and Victor: Alisa Sasha Elderblood.[1] Alisa is not a corrected spelling of Aline, an alias of Aline, or the same character as the Chapter 997 girl.
The canon characters should therefore be treated as:
- Julia Elderblood — daughter of Victor and Naty; erroneously called "Aline Elderblood" in an older version of Chapter 997.
- Alisa Sasha Elderblood — daughter of Victor and Carmila; first explicitly identified in Chapter 1165.
- Aline Elderblood Valefar — an entirely separate Demon-derived member of Victor's family and one of the Empire's leading researchers.
Aline Elderblood Fulger is not a separate canon character.
