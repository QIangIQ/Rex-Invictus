Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II, formerly Velnorah Xyphora Thaloria II, is an ancient extraterrestrial Goddess, a former Overlord, the [[Anna Elderblood|Second Empress]] of the [[Thaloria Empire]], a Blood Dragon, and one of Victor Elderblood's wives. She originally belonged to the [[Xyrra]], a technologically gifted species native to [[Eldoria]], and inherited the throne of her mother, [[Thaloria Xyphora]], after defeating her. Velnorah ruled as an Overlord while her home belonged to a Mid-Level Sector, but lost that position after a Convergence elevated the Sector into the Higher Sectors and exposed her civilization to powerful Galactic Emperors. The resulting military catastrophe forced her into exile, eventually bringing her into Victor's Sector.[3]
Velnorah is distinguished by her extraordinary intelligence, immense scientific knowledge, extensive military experience and mastery of technological warfare. Her original race possessed an instinctive affinity for technology, allowing its members to construct and manipulate machinery with supernatural ease. Velnorah developed these characteristics to a degree far beyond ordinary Xyrra, inheriting the scientific achievements of her mother and refining them through centuries of research, conquest and Divine development. Her abilities were sufficiently advanced that she could suppress the Divine Concepts of an entire enemy Pantheon through specialized equipment and defeat extraordinarily powerful Gods without relying upon conventional prolonged combat.[4][5][3]
Her history is inseparable from the rise and fall of the Thaloria dynasty. Thaloria Xyphora established the foundations of Eldoria's technological supremacy and became its First Empress after conquering the planet and eventually overcoming the Galactic forces that opposed her expansion. However, the First Empress' decision to exterminate the Yuurakyo, an exceptionally powerful warrior race, ultimately deprived her civilization of a potential defense against threats far greater than those it had previously faced. After Velnorah succeeded her mother, the Sector's advancement introduced civilizations and Galactic Emperors whose personal power exceeded the military capabilities of the Thaloria Empire's conventional forces. Velnorah and Thaloria remained powerful enough to personally resist those enemies, but their Empire could not withstand the resulting conflict.[3]
Velnorah initially approached Victor for practical reasons. She needed an ally possessing sufficient military strength, supernatural authority and ambition to help her recover Eldoria and confront the Galactic Emperors responsible for the collapse of her homeland. Although she had previously considered an alliance with [[Odin]], her encounter with Victor revealed a far more promising candidate. Their cooperation began when she offered to assist his campaign against the [[Egyptian Pantheon]] and the Elder Gods in exchange for an opportunity to explain her circumstances.[6][4]
The alliance eventually became personal. Velnorah discovered that Victor possessed not only the extraordinary power she required but also a willingness to respect her knowledge, independence and ambitions. After joining his family, she underwent a transformation into a genuine Blood Dragon, acquiring a superior racial foundation that removed the limitations she had reached as a Xyrra. Victor subsequently entrusted her with the reconstruction of the conquered Egyptian territory, allowing her to establish Elvenorah, a technologically advanced civilization organized around research, education and scientific development.[7][8][9][1]
Within the [[Elderblood Empire]], Velnorah became one of its foremost scientific and technological authorities alongside [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] and [[Aline Elderblood Valefar]]. Their research transformed the Empire from a coalition of supernatural factions into an increasingly sophisticated interstellar civilization. Velnorah's work contributed to the development of advanced cloning, biological engineering, planetary infrastructure, military technologies, the Imperial System, and the [[Dragon Heart]], whose original prototype was developed with her daughter [[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]] before Victor transformed it into a living source of extraordinary Energy.[2][10][11]
Her responsibilities eventually extended to the Empire's Fourth Legion, [[Stellar Arsenal]], the organization responsible for developing some of its most advanced scientific, magical and military technologies. She became directly involved in projects designed to confront the extraordinary threats of the Higher Sectors, including the galaxy-destroyer-class Divine Artifact Polaris, advanced military clones, artificial intelligences and the creation of new technologically gifted populations capable of conducting dangerous research on a cosmic scale.[12][13][14]
Despite her importance as a researcher and administrator, Velnorah remains deeply attached to the family she established with Victor. Her daughter Velina preserves the naming tradition of the destroyed Thaloria dynasty, while her relationship with Victor developed from an alliance motivated by revenge into a genuine [[Soul Marriage]]. The woman who had once expected marriage to be another political arrangement eventually found a new civilization, a family she trusted and opportunities to pursue ambitions beyond merely reclaiming her former throne.[9][1][2]

### Physical Appearance

#### Xyrra
Before becoming a Blood Dragon, Velnorah possessed the towering physique characteristic of the [[Xyrra]], standing approximately three meters tall. She has pale white skin, vivid blue eyes and extremely long light-blue hair, giving her an imposing appearance even among Gods accustomed to encountering supernatural beings with extraordinary physical characteristics. Her height was not considered particularly abnormal within her original civilization, since adult members of the Xyrra routinely exceeded three meters.[6][3]
Her facial features are exceptionally beautiful, but her appearance is also defined by an almost permanently controlled expression. Years of military leadership and political rule taught her to maintain a composed demeanor regardless of the situation, making it difficult for unfamiliar individuals to interpret her emotions. Her bearing resembles that of an experienced soldier as much as an Empress: she commonly stands straight, keeps her movements economical and observes potential opponents without displaying unnecessary hostility.[15][16]
Velnorah's original clothing reflected the extraordinary technological development of [[Eldoria]]. Her principal outfit was a sophisticated futuristic suit closely fitted to her enormous body, incorporating advanced materials, technological systems and supernatural enhancements. The design originated from the engineering traditions developed by her mother, Thaloria, whose own achievements included equipment capable of compensating for physical weaknesses and maintaining the wearer's condition through sophisticated technology.[17][3]
Although the suit resembles clothing, it functions as a comprehensive technological system rather than merely protective fabric. Its capabilities include the manifestation of machinery and mechanical wings, the manipulation of specialized Energy, and the deployment of equipment powerful enough to interfere with Divine abilities. During her first major appearance alongside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], her equipment formed mechanical structures around her body before releasing a Pantheon-wide pulse that suppressed the Divine Concepts of numerous Gods.[4]
Outside military operations, Velnorah favors refined futuristic clothing that preserves the visual identity of her former civilization. White garments, blue technological accents and elegant fitted outfits appear frequently in her designs, contrasting with the more traditional clothing preferred by many of the Goddesses of Earth. Her appearance therefore remains closely associated with the scientific heritage she inherited from Eldoria.[17]

#### Blood Dragon
Velnorah's transformation into a genuine Blood Dragon fundamentally reconstructed the physical and spiritual foundation she had possessed as a [[Xyrra]]. The change was especially profound because she had already reached the limits of her former existence and developed her Divinities to an extraordinary degree. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was required to alter the internal structure of her Soul, integrating her accumulated characteristics into the superior foundation of a True Dragon.[18][7]
Her already enormous humanoid body subsequently increased in size. By the time Victor visited her in Elvenorah, Velnorah stood approximately four meters tall, while her husband chose to enlarge his own appearance so they could look directly into each other's eyes while walking together. Her transformation preserved her pale skin, blue eyes and long light-blue hair, ensuring that she remained immediately recognizable despite the alteration of her race.[8]
The transformation also introduced genuine Draconic characteristics, integrating her former Divine and technological affinities into a body whose natural relationship with Creation allowed her abilities to develop beyond the limitations of her previous species. Velnorah recognized that becoming a Dragon had fundamentally changed the relationship between her physical body, Soul and supernatural powers rather than simply giving her an increase in strength.[7]
Although Velnorah's current physiology is Draconic, she continues to favor the technological aesthetic of [[Eldoria]]. Her clothing and equipment remain closely associated with advanced engineering, while her enlarged physical appearance conveys the same imposing dignity that characterized her former position as Empress.[8]

#### Imperial Era
By the mature Imperial era, Velnorah's appearance is associated with a combination of regal elegance, technological sophistication and Draconic presence. She retains her enormous humanoid stature and distinctive blue coloration while occupying a position among the most accomplished scientific authorities of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s civilization.[2]
Her technological suit continues serving as both clothing and an extension of the scientific systems she uses, while her Divine and Draconic existence allows her to function in environments that would be dangerous even to powerful ordinary supernatural beings. Unlike the battle armor of a conventional warrior, Velnorah's equipment reflects her preference for solving problems through preparation, engineering and the precise application of specialized technology.[4][16]
Her daughter Velina strongly resembles her. During their first extended appearance together, the two appeared so similar that an observer could mistake them for sisters rather than mother and daughter, although Velina was considerably shorter, standing approximately two meters tall at that point. Differences in their eyes, hair and height distinguished them upon closer examination.[2]

### Personality
Velnorah is an extraordinarily disciplined, intelligent, pragmatic and calculating woman whose personality was shaped by centuries of political rule, warfare and scientific research. She possesses the temperament of an experienced military commander combined with the curiosity and analytical habits of a researcher. When confronted with unfamiliar supernatural phenomena, her first instinct is usually to study the available information, determine how the phenomenon operates and identify the most efficient response. Even in situations involving powerful Gods or beings whose strength she cannot fully evaluate, Velnorah generally maintains an upright posture and controlled expression rather than allowing fear or confusion to dictate her behavior.[15][16]
Her ability to conceal emotions is unusually developed. During her initial interactions with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], she carefully controlled her reactions and prevented him from easily determining her intentions. Although Victor could ordinarily evaluate the danger presented by supernatural beings through his perception, Velnorah's unfamiliar origin, sophisticated technology and unusually disciplined Soul made her difficult to interpret. She was capable of presenting technically accurate statements in ways that concealed important context, an ability she used when describing her former status as an Overlord of a Higher Sector. Only later did she explain that she had possessed the title before her Sector advanced and had subsequently lost the position.[15][3]
Velnorah places enormous importance upon efficiency. She dislikes wasting resources, time or effort upon objectives that can be accomplished through a simpler method. During Victor's assault upon the [[Egyptian Pantheon]], she did not attempt to fight seventy-six Gods individually when her equipment could suppress their Divine Concepts, identify them and capture them through a coordinated technological operation. She completed the assignment within an extraordinarily short period and behaved as though she had merely performed an ordinary duty. The accomplishment demonstrated a mentality in which success is measured by the quality of the result rather than the dramatic spectacle of the process.[4]
This pragmatism extends to warfare and civilization-building. Velnorah considers military strength meaningful only when it contributes to strategic objectives, and she recognizes that an army relying upon one overwhelming advantage may collapse when confronted by an enemy capable of countering it. The destruction of [[Eldoria]] taught her that technological superiority alone was insufficient against civilizations possessing extraordinary individual power, particularly when her own Empire had eliminated the warrior race that might have provided the military depth necessary to resist those enemies. She therefore approaches Imperial research through the development of multiple complementary systems rather than assuming that one invention or one form of power will solve every problem.[3][13]
Velnorah is also intensely proud. Her pride originally emerged from the achievements of the Thaloria dynasty and the extraordinary technological dominance established by her mother. After defeating Thaloria and inheriting the throne, Velnorah considered herself capable of governing and expanding a civilization whose power extended across enormous regions of space. However, the catastrophe that followed her Sector's advancement forced her to recognize the consequences of underestimating unfamiliar enemies. She eventually acknowledged that her own arrogance had contributed to the failure of her reign, and the lesson continued influencing her decisions long after becoming a Blood Dragon.[3][14]
Her technological genius is accompanied by a highly experimental view of society. When Victor entrusted her with Elvenorah, she rejected the assumption that an ideal civilization should simply provide unlimited comfort while removing every reason for its citizens to work or develop. Instead, she established a technocratic society in which basic necessities were guaranteed but scientific research, specialization, education and personal achievement remained central values. Velnorah believes that people require meaningful objectives, and that a civilization where machines perform absolutely everything can become intellectually stagnant even if material conditions appear perfect.[8][9]
She is similarly concerned with the danger of relying upon a single individual or automated system. Although her own knowledge is sufficient to resolve problems that would occupy enormous research institutions, Velnorah recognizes the importance of different perspectives. Scientists who lack her extraordinary Draconic capabilities may still identify practical problems she overlooks because their experiences differ from those of the Imperial Family. Consequently, she prefers developing qualified personnel and specialized subordinate teams rather than forcing herself, Ruby and Aline to personally resolve every minor problem.[8][14]
Velnorah's original relationship with Victor was strongly influenced by distrust. Before meeting him, she had experienced betrayal from individuals she considered loyal and learned to treat personal affection as a potential weakness. She initially expected marriage to serve a political purpose: obtaining a powerful ally, producing an heir if necessary and securing sufficient military support to recover Eldoria. She was prepared to accept the humiliation of becoming a political trophy or even betraying a future husband if circumstances left her no other means of regaining independence. These expectations reflected her experiences with powerful rulers rather than a genuine desire to deceive Victor from the beginning.[9][1]
Victor gradually changed her expectations by refusing to treat her as a disposable source of scientific knowledge or military power. He acknowledged that Velnorah understood technology and civilization-building far better than he did, gave her genuine authority over Elvenorah and listened to her decisions without attempting to control every detail. His willingness to delegate responsibility was especially important because Velnorah had previously regarded powerful rulers as individuals who would inevitably subordinate the interests of others to their own. Victor's behavior convinced her that she could remain an independent thinker while belonging to his family.[8][9][1]
Her transformation into a True Dragon made the emotional consequences of this trust even more difficult to ignore. Dragons possess intense emotional characteristics, and Velnorah discovered that the feelings she had previously concealed beneath discipline and calculation were becoming increasingly difficult to suppress. Her affection for Victor developed into possessiveness and genuine vulnerability, emotions she had once regarded as dangerous for an Empress. Rather than interpreting them as evidence that her judgment had failed, she gradually accepted that her relationship with him had become more important than the political arrangement she originally intended to establish.[7][1]
Velnorah's relationship with her daughter Velina reveals another important aspect of her personality. She is deeply affectionate toward Velina and recognizes the extraordinary intelligence the girl inherited from both parents, but she also understands the dangers created by talent without humility. During their work with the Yuurakyo, Velnorah became concerned that Velina had grown accustomed to the overwhelming advantages of True Dragon physiology and the security of Victor's Empire. She considered arranging difficult experiences within Victor's Dream Worlds to remind Velina and her sisters what weakness and genuine danger felt like. The concern originated from Velnorah's memory of losing her own Empire through arrogance and her determination that her daughter should not repeat the same mistakes.[2][14]
Despite her intense dedication to research, Velnorah does not completely neglect family life. She and Velina work together frequently, but she recognizes the importance of ordinary household routines and the emotional responsibilities accompanying motherhood. The Imperial Family's communal dinners remain important even when its members no longer require food for survival, and Velnorah expects Velina to participate rather than spending every moment in a laboratory. This distinguishes her from researchers whose obsession causes them to become isolated from the people around them.[14][19]
Velnorah's current character therefore combines the strengths and weaknesses of two different lives. She remains the proud [[Anna Elderblood|Second Empress]] Thaloria, shaped by conquest, technological achievement and the destruction of her homeland, but she has also become a wife, mother and Imperial researcher whose ambitions no longer revolve exclusively around revenge. She wants to build a civilization capable of surviving threats far greater than those that destroyed Eldoria, while ensuring that the family and society she now values do not repeat the errors of her past.[3][1][14]

### Background
Velnorah was born upon [[Eldoria]], a technologically advanced planet inhabited by the [[Xyrra]] and numerous other supernatural and extraterrestrial species. The Xyrra possessed an extraordinary natural affinity for technology, allowing even ordinary members of the race to manipulate machinery and construct sophisticated devices through abilities that would appear miraculous to less developed civilizations. This characteristic made technological progress a fundamental part of Xyrra society rather than a discipline limited to a small number of specialists.[3]
Her mother, [[Thaloria Xyphora]], was one of the most accomplished individuals ever produced by the race. Although Thaloria was originally considered physically inferior to many members of her species because of her smaller body, she compensated through scientific innovation. She developed advanced equipment capable of strengthening her physical characteristics and maintaining her biological condition, eventually achieving sufficient power to dominate the rival species and political forces of Eldoria.[3]
Thaloria's achievements transformed the planet into the center of an expanding technological Empire. Within fewer than two thousand years, the Xyrra had established control over Eldoria and begun exploring space. Their civilization combined extraordinary technological development with an effort to preserve the natural environment rather than destroying it through uncontrolled industrial expansion. Over time, Thaloria acquired increasingly powerful subordinates, extended her influence beyond the original planet and became the First Empress of Eldoria.[3]
The rise of the [[Thaloria Empire]] eventually brought it into conflict with the Yuurakyo, an extraordinarily powerful warrior race associated with [[Galactic Emperor Kolo]]. The Yuurakyo possessed immense natural strength, advanced combat instincts and an exceptional capacity to adapt to hostile environments. Their infiltration of Thaloria's territory threatened a civilization whose military superiority had previously relied heavily upon its technological systems.[3]
Thaloria initially attempted to capture a Yuurakyo warrior alive. Although she succeeded, the operation cost numerous military personnel and damaged her own equipment. Fascinated by the race's physical characteristics, she began a long series of experiments intended to reproduce or acquire the powers that made the Yuurakyo such formidable warriors. Conventional cloning proved ineffective because of the instability of their genetic structure, while attempts to produce offspring using members of other races failed because the resulting embryos could not develop normally.[3]
Thaloria subsequently attempted to transform other races into Yuurakyo through the alteration of their Souls, recognizing that genuine racial transformation required more than merely changing the physical body. These experiments also failed to reproduce the power associated with a true racial Progenitor. Her eventual capture of a female Yuurakyo allowed further reproductive experiments, which revealed the importance of the race's combat-based social and biological development.[3]
The successful birth of a Yuurakyo child ultimately exposed the source of their unusual genetic instability. Their [[Elders]] employed a special enhancement serum during a coming-of-age ceremony, producing the extraordinary physical development associated with the mature race. Because the resulting changes were not naturally transmitted through ordinary reproduction, Thaloria could study a genetically more stable individual and identify a biological vulnerability.[3]
Using that information, Thaloria developed a virus designed specifically to attack Yuurakyo biology, exterminating the race and eliminating one of the greatest military forces supporting her Galactic enemies. The resulting strategic victory enabled her Empire to conquer its Galaxy, after which Thaloria became the dominant Overlord of her Sector.[3]
Velnorah was born during the era of Thaloria's dominance. She inherited an exceptionally advanced civilization, extensive scientific knowledge and the expectations associated with the dynasty founded by her mother. As she matured, Velnorah developed her own technological and Divine abilities until she became powerful enough to challenge Thaloria directly. She eventually defeated her mother, succeeding her as [[Anna Elderblood|Second Empress]] Thaloria and inheriting the position of Overlord together with the Empire's scientific and political foundations.[3]
The catastrophe that ended Velnorah's rule began with a Convergence. Her original Sector advanced from a Mid-Level Sector into a Higher Sector, exposing Eldoria and its associated territories to civilizations whose personal and military power exceeded what the Thaloria Empire had previously needed to confront. Galactic Emperors appeared possessing enormous fleets, hundreds of subordinate planets, advanced technologies and individual supernatural capabilities powerful enough to destroy entire planets.[3]
Velnorah and Thaloria were capable of directly resisting these enemies, but their civilization lacked the military depth required for a prolonged war. The former extermination of the Yuurakyo proved especially disastrous: the warrior race had possessed precisely the adaptability and combat power that might have allowed Eldoria to resist the superior individual strength of the Galactic Emperors. Velnorah eventually recognized that a victory celebrated during her mother's reign had eliminated one of the Empire's greatest potential defenses against the future.[3]
The resulting conflict destroyed Velnorah's political supremacy and forced her from her homeland. Her title of Overlord no longer accurately described the territory she controlled after the Sector's elevation and the collapse of the Thaloria Empire. She nevertheless retained the scientific knowledge, supernatural power and determination necessary to begin seeking allies capable of supporting a future campaign to recover Eldoria.[3]
Her exile eventually brought her into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Sector, where she encountered a civilization far less technologically developed than her former Empire but populated by an extraordinary concentration of powerful Gods, [[True Dragons]], [[Primordial Entities]] and other Beings whose existence contradicted her expectations. Rather than immediately abandoning the region as strategically useless, Velnorah recognized that its powerful inhabitants might provide the foundation of the alliance she needed.[17][6][3]

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Velnorah entered the narrative as an unfamiliar extraterrestrial woman whose advanced equipment and extraordinary supernatural presence distinguished her from the Gods normally encountered around Earth. After reaching [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Sector, she came into contact with the [[Norse Pantheon]] and spent time in [[Asgard]] under [[Odin]]'s hospitality. Although Odin recognized the potential advantages of obtaining her cooperation, Velnorah remained cautious and did not fully commit herself to his faction. She viewed the arrangement as an opportunity to observe the region while evaluating whether its inhabitants possessed sufficient power to help her recover [[Eldoria]].[17]
Her encounter with the Norse Pantheon also exposed the considerable differences between her own technological civilization and the more traditional supernatural systems of Earth. While Asgard possessed powerful Gods, Divine Artifacts and ancient [[Magic]], Velnorah's equipment originated from a society in which extraordinary technological achievements were ordinary components of civilization. She therefore approached the Pantheon's authorities as a potential equal rather than a subordinate seeking the protection of superior Gods.[17]
After leaving Asgard, Velnorah continued investigating the unusual supernatural powers of the Sector. Her attention was drawn toward the [[Supernatural Gathering]], where Gods and other powerful beings from several Pantheons discussed the consequences of Victor's growing influence. The Owner of Limbo permitted her presence, allowing Velnorah to observe individuals whose strength and authority exceeded the expectations she normally associated with a comparatively underdeveloped Sector.[20][21]
The gathering changed her assessment of the region. Beings such as Victor, Kali, Jeanne and the Primordials possessed characteristics that could threaten or exceed the power of Galactic authorities from much more advanced civilizations. Velnorah was particularly interested in Victor because his exceptional growth, powerful companions and willingness to confront entire Pantheons indicated that he might eventually become an ally capable of challenging the Galactic Emperors responsible for the destruction of her homeland.[20][15]
When Victor organized a campaign against the Gods he intended to eliminate, Velnorah approached him directly. She emerged through a distinctive blue portal resembling a technological quantum tunnel, surprising the Gods accompanying Victor. Rather than offering immediate explanations concerning her past, she introduced herself as Velnorah Xyphora Thaloria II, the [[Anna Elderblood|Second Empress]] Thaloria and former Overlord of Eldoria, a planet then associated with Upper Sector 8975.[6]
Victor noticed that she understood the language of [[True Dragons]]. Velnorah explained that she knew numerous languages and deliberately addressed foreign rulers in their own language as a gesture of respect. She also acknowledged that knowing the language did not grant her the same natural connection to it possessed by genuine Dragons. The distinction demonstrated both her education and her habit of approaching unfamiliar powerful beings through carefully observed diplomatic conventions.[6]
Velnorah offered Victor a direct exchange: she would assist in his current campaign, and he would subsequently listen to her circumstances. Victor accepted the offer without immediately committing himself to an alliance, recognizing that her abilities could prove useful but that he possessed insufficient information to trust her completely.[6]
Her first major contribution occurred during the assault against the sealed [[Egyptian Pantheon]]. Ra had attempted to protect the Egyptian Divine territory by sealing its dimension, but Victor's group forced an entrance. After receiving instructions concerning the Gods he wanted captured, Velnorah employed advanced technology and supernatural power to disable and collect the designated targets.[4]
Mechanical structures formed around her body, while mechanical wings generated enormous wings of blue Energy. Velnorah then released a pulse affecting the entire Pantheon, preventing numerous Gods from accessing their Divine Concepts. Before they could understand what had happened, she released another pulse identifying the selected targets and created a network of blue Energy that reached across the dimension.[4]
The operation concluded when Velnorah interfered with the portable spatial device supplied to Victor's group. The device changed coloration as her systems took control, allowing the captured Gods to be transported into confinement. The operation successfully secured seventy-six selected Gods in a single coordinated action. [[Loki]] and the other witnesses were astonished by the efficiency of a method that avoided the prolonged fighting normally required to defeat such a large number of Divine opponents.[4]
Her intervention also revealed how different her methods were from those employed by most of Victor's allies. Rather than relying solely upon individual combat strength or a single overwhelmingly destructive Divine technique, Velnorah combined specialized machinery, large-scale conceptual interference, target identification and spatial technology to accomplish a complicated objective with minimal unnecessary effort.[4]
Following the Egyptian operation, Velnorah participated in the conflict against the Elder Gods. Her presence complicated Victor's original military preparations because he had not anticipated the involvement of a foreign Overlord possessing such unusual abilities. Nevertheless, her strength proved valuable against enemies whose monstrous physiology and supernatural resilience would ordinarily make them difficult to defeat.[15][16]
Velnorah confronted one of the Elder Gods through a specialized blue barrier capable of restricting the creature's movements and supernatural actions. Rather than immediately killing it, she maintained the confinement while examining the developing battle. When she finally decided to finish the encounter, she compressed the barrier until the enemy's body and Soul were crushed into a compact sphere. [[Shiva]], who had observed the process, was unable to determine whether the result originated primarily from her technological systems or her personal supernatural power.[16]
Shiva subsequently attempted to investigate Velnorah's abilities more directly. She recognized the intrusion and appeared beside him, placing a technologically enhanced hand upon his shoulder despite the protection supplied by his Destruction. When Shiva's power failed to destroy her equipment, Velnorah firmly warned him against treating her as an enemy while they remained allies. The confrontation established that her technology could operate in circumstances where the Divine Concept of Destruction would ordinarily eliminate conventional materials.[16]
During the same conflict, Velnorah analyzed the unusual spatial circumstances surrounding Victor's disappearance. Her equipment detected Energy traces corresponding to coordinates approaching the [[Edge of the Cosmos]], an achievement she considered almost impossible for someone originating from such an underdeveloped Sector. The discovery further increased her interest in Victor and reinforced the suspicion that his existence could not be understood through the conventional standards of her former civilization.[16]
The events concluded with Velnorah recognizing that Victor's faction possessed an extraordinary combination of individual power, Divine authority, unusual races and rapidly developing resources. She had originally entered the Sector believing she might find a useful military ally, but her experience against the Egyptian and Elder Gods revealed that Victor's civilization possessed potential far greater than she had initially imagined.[15][16]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Following the conflict against the Elder Gods, Velnorah remained associated with Dragons Nest while awaiting the opportunity to explain her circumstances to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. When the promised discussion finally occurred, she used advanced holographic technology to present the history of her homeland before Victor and [[Amaterasu]].[22][3]
Her presentation began with [[Eldoria]], a technologically extraordinary world where advanced machinery coexisted with a carefully maintained natural environment. Velnorah explained the characteristics of the [[Xyrra]] and demonstrated the development of their civilization through holographic recordings. Her mother, [[Thaloria Xyphora]], had been an exceptionally talented member of the race who overcame her original physical disadvantages through advanced technological equipment and gradually established the foundations of the [[Thaloria Empire]].[3]
Velnorah then described her mother's conquest of Eldoria and eventual expansion into space. The Empire developed rapidly because the Xyrra possessed instinctive technological abilities, allowing enormous numbers of individuals to contribute to the creation and improvement of machinery. However, the expansion eventually brought the civilization into conflict with the Yuurakyo, a warrior race whose physical strength, adaptability and combat instincts made its members exceptionally difficult to defeat through conventional technological advantages.[3]
Thaloria's obsession with acquiring the power of the Yuurakyo led to increasingly extreme experiments. After capturing members of the race and failing to reproduce their abilities through cloning or ordinary biological methods, she eventually discovered the role of a special enhancement serum in their development. This allowed her to create a targeted biological weapon that exterminated the Yuurakyo and removed a powerful military obstacle to her conquest of the Galaxy.[3]
The campaign established Thaloria as the dominant Overlord of the Sector, but it also created a weakness whose consequences would become apparent only much later. Velnorah explained that she had subsequently grown powerful enough to defeat her mother and inherit both the Imperial throne and the position of Overlord, becoming the [[Anna Elderblood|Second Empress]] Thaloria.[3]
The catastrophe began when the Sector advanced into a Higher Sector. Velnorah's civilization was suddenly exposed to Galactic Emperors possessing enormous military resources and individual power far beyond the standards of its previous enemies. Although Velnorah and Thaloria could personally resist these beings, their military forces lacked the necessary strength, particularly because the Yuurakyo had been exterminated before they could become a defensive asset.[3]
Velnorah eventually fled the conflict and entered Victor's Sector. Her objective was to obtain an ally strong enough to support a campaign against the Galactic Emperors and allow her to recover her homeland. She also explained that she had deliberately presented her former status in a technically accurate but misleading manner: she had been an Overlord before her Sector advanced, not the reigning Overlord of the Higher Sector after its transformation.[3]
Victor questioned her concerning the strength of the beings surrounding Earth. Velnorah considered herself capable of defeating most of the individuals she had observed, but identified Victor, the Primordials and Kali as notable exceptions. The assessment emphasized that she remained extraordinarily powerful despite losing the civilization she once governed.[3]
Their cooperation continued as Victor expanded the supernatural foundations of Dragons Nest. Velnorah possessed scientific knowledge that could accelerate the development of the faction by enormous margins, while Victor could provide resources, protection and supernatural opportunities unavailable within her destroyed Empire. The relationship therefore became mutually valuable well before it developed into personal affection.[3]
Velnorah's racial evolution represented another major turning point. She had already reached the limits of her original Xyrra body and developed her Divinities to an extraordinary degree. Victor's ability to transform her into a genuine True Dragon offered a way to overcome restrictions that could not be resolved simply through additional research or training.[5][18][7]
During the transformation, Victor entered Velnorah's spiritual foundation to reconstruct the internal structures of her existence. Velnorah instinctively attempted to resist the intrusion through her own Divine power, but relaxed after Victor explained that the process was necessary. She could perceive the extraordinary precision and invasiveness of his abilities, recognizing that a sufficiently hostile Being possessing comparable powers could completely transform another individual against their wishes.[7]
The transformation succeeded, granting Velnorah genuine Blood Dragon physiology and a spiritual foundation with greater compatibility toward Creation. The change was particularly beneficial to her because technology itself represents one of the ways intelligent beings interact with and modify Creation. Her original technological abilities consequently acquired room for further development beyond the limits established by her former Xyrra existence.[18][7]
Victor later entrusted Velnorah with the territory associated with the defeated [[Egyptian Pantheon]]. She began transforming the region into Elvenorah, an experimental civilization designed to combine the strengths of advanced technology, [[Magic]], Divine authority and multiple supernatural species.[8]
When Victor visited the developing city, Velnorah had already organized its administration through a carefully planned technocratic system. Essential necessities were supplied to the population, while education, scientific research, technological specialization and productive work remained central to the civilization's development. Rather than simply automating everything, Velnorah deliberately preserved meaningful responsibilities for qualified people and created systems through which trusted deputies could assume important positions when their superiors were absent.[8]
The arrangement differed from the absolute monarchy employed in Hell. Elvenorah was intended to become a society whose development centered upon the creation and application of knowledge, making the city itself a vast experiment in civilization-building. Velnorah considered the model appropriate to Victor's instruction to create an enduring Utopia where numerous species could coexist and develop together.[8]
Victor was impressed by her work and recognized that she possessed experience he lacked. Although he could have attempted to control every aspect of the project through overwhelming supernatural authority, he instead allowed Velnorah to apply her own judgment. His confidence had an enormous emotional effect upon her because she was accustomed to relationships in which rulers sought to control the people and resources beneath them.[8][9]
Their subsequent conversations revealed the personal circumstances behind Velnorah's reluctance to trust others. She explained that her original objective had been to marry a sufficiently powerful man, obtain the necessary military support, produce an heir if circumstances required and eventually decide whether remaining in the marriage served her interests. Having experienced betrayal and political manipulation, she had expected the relationship to remain a strategic arrangement rather than an emotional commitment.[9][1]
Victor rejected the idea that he wanted Velnorah merely for her body, knowledge or political usefulness. He made clear that he wanted her complete trust and wished to share her thoughts, Soul and existence rather than maintaining a relationship based upon calculated convenience. The conversation overcame much of the emotional distance Velnorah had maintained since arriving in his Sector, allowing her to accept genuine affection rather than continuing to evaluate their connection exclusively through political necessity.[1]
The relationship culminated in Velnorah entering a [[Soul Marriage]] with Victor. The former Empress who had originally expected a political alliance became one of his wives, obtaining not only military support but a place within the growing family that would eventually rule the [[Elderblood Empire]].[1]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Following the establishment of the [[Elderblood Empire]], Velnorah's scientific responsibilities expanded enormously. The Empire's ambitions required technologies capable of sustaining civilizations across numerous planets, coordinating vast military forces and eventually surviving encounters with hostile powers from the Higher Sectors. Velnorah's knowledge of [[Eldoria]] and her experience as a former Overlord became especially valuable because she understood the military and technological standards of civilizations far more advanced than those originally surrounding Earth.[2][23]
Her personal life also changed through the birth of her daughter with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Velnorah experienced an extraordinarily long pregnancy associated with the powerful Draconic nature of the child, with the relevant chronology describing approximately 1,950 years of gestation. The daughter was named [[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]], preserving the naming tradition of Velnorah's destroyed dynasty.[24][2]
The name was deliberately chosen. Members of the ancient Thaloria Royal Family inherited the name of their mother together with the surname associated with the First Empress. Velnorah continued the tradition as a tribute to Eldoria and as a declaration that the Thaloria dynasty had survived despite the destruction of its political and territorial foundations.[2]
Velina inherited extraordinary scientific intelligence and developed a close working relationship with her mother. By their first major appearance together as researchers, the two were sufficiently similar in appearance that they resembled sisters more than mother and daughter. Their shared abilities and interests allowed them to collaborate upon increasingly ambitious technological projects, while Velnorah continued instructing Velina in the practical lessons she had learned from her own mother.[2]
One of their greatest early achievements was the development of the prototype that would become the [[Dragon Heart]]. Velnorah and Velina constructed a Divine-technological artifact combining Technomancy, Draconic Runes and advanced supernatural engineering. The device was originally intended to become an extraordinarily powerful Energy source capable of supporting the Empire's future technological infrastructure.[2]
Velina remained dissatisfied with the prototype's possible failure rates and wanted to eliminate every uncertainty. Velnorah reminded her that perfect engineering was impossible when researchers did not possess complete knowledge of the Universe, emphasizing that the correct approach was to continually update their designs as new information became available. The conversation reproduced advice Velnorah herself had once received from Thaloria, demonstrating how the scientific traditions of the dynasty continued through successive generations.[2]
Victor subsequently visited their laboratory and examined the prototype. Rather than replacing the researchers' work, he studied its structure and recognized how his own powers could transform it. He applied the properties of [[Junketsu]], his Soul and Draconic Runes to reconstruct the device into a living Divine Artifact resembling a Dragon Heart. The original sphere became a large dark-violet icosahedron covered in Runes and capable of generating extraordinary quantities of Attributeless Energy.[2]
Velnorah recognized the significance of the transformation almost immediately. The artifact was not merely a stronger version of the original prototype; it had become a supernatural foundation capable of sustaining enormous Imperial systems through its connection to Victor. She also realized that his Soul-based influence could provide an additional security mechanism for artificial beings created through the Energy it generated, allowing the Empire to protect itself against the possibility of rebellion or hostile interference.[2]
The Dragon Heart became one of the central technological and supernatural foundations of the Empire. Velnorah's contribution was therefore essential not only through her own research but also through the knowledge she supplied to Victor, whose personal abilities transformed the prototype into something neither mother nor daughter had initially intended to construct.[2]
The same discussion led to the development of another major Imperial project. Velnorah and Velina began preparing Project Pillar, intended to create a living intelligence capable of coordinating the Empire's increasingly complex systems. Unlike ordinary artificial intelligences, the proposed entity would possess genuine Life and a Soul, making it more than a program executing administrative commands.[2]
Velnorah initially considered using her former [[Xyrra]] race as the biological foundation, supplemented by compatible Dragon characteristics and other genetic improvements. Her objective was not to create another powerful warrior but an individual specialized in intelligence and large-scale system management. Velina suggested that the simplest way to guarantee the new Being's loyalty would be to make her another daughter of Victor, since his children were naturally attached to him and possessed a direct interest in the security of his family.[2]
Velnorah recognized the practical value of the idea. She also understood that Victor would almost certainly intervene because he would regard the creation of another daughter as a personal matter rather than merely a technological project. The preparations therefore proceeded with the expectation that Victor, Ruby and Aline would eventually participate in the creation of the new intelligence.[2]
The resulting project culminated in the creation of [[Akasha Elderblood]]. Victor ultimately constructed her as a genuine living Being from his own Blood, Soul and Eldritch Divinity, making her fundamentally different from the artificial intelligence originally envisioned by Velnorah's researchers. Nevertheless, the development originated directly from the Imperial need identified by Velnorah and Velina: the Empire required a central intelligence capable of coordinating its enormous technological infrastructure without depending upon ordinary computational limitations.[10]
Velnorah continued contributing to the creation of elite Imperial military forces. Research involving genetic engineering, cloning and Soul compatibility revealed that Victor could not simply reproduce his own existence in ordinary bodies, since unrelated individuals could not safely withstand every characteristic of his Soul and power. The researchers therefore developed specialized templates for military personnel rather than attempting to manufacture unlimited copies of the Emperor.[23]
These projects contributed to the establishment of the Emperor's Soldiers and other elite Imperial forces, whose abilities reflected the combination of advanced biological engineering, supernatural Energy, military training and technological support. Velnorah's laboratories became central to the development of a professional military structure capable of operating beyond the private forces originally associated with Victor's wives.[23]
Her influence also extended beyond any single scientific department. As the Empire established increasingly specialized research organizations, Velnorah, Ruby and Aline remained among the figures whose work shaped nearly every important technological development. Although individual departments were formally assigned to different leaders, their actual responsibilities crossed the boundaries of ordinary administrative divisions because their expertise was required throughout the larger scientific system.[11]
During the Empire's expansion, Velnorah also investigated unusual supernatural changes affecting developing [[World Trees]]. When Victor released enormous quantities of Energy, several World Trees located outside the principal Imperial territories absorbed portions of it and underwent abnormal growth. Velnorah recognized that the irregularities were associated with Victor's influence and continued studying the consequences for worlds whose developmental processes were being accelerated beyond ordinary expectations.[25]
Her responsibilities became especially significant during the investigation of the [[Skaarith Empire]] in the Andromeda Galaxy. Imperial intelligence indicated that the civilization had been compromised by forces associated with the Higher Sectors and that its ruling authorities possessed characteristics similar to the previously encountered Hive influence. The planet's population had been infected with a dangerous supernatural virus, while competing outside powers appeared to be using the civilization as an experiment in accelerated military development.[26]
Velnorah commanded the Imperial operation against the compromised planet. Her forces were instructed to eliminate hostile entities, secure relevant biological samples and identify the systems responsible for maintaining the infection. At the same time, she recognized the importance of preserving the local World Tree, since the Tree represented a possible means of recovering information about the original inhabitants and restoring the planet's development after the immediate threat had been contained.[26]
The operation demonstrated the changes in Velnorah's strategic perspective since the collapse of Eldoria. Although she remained capable of employing overwhelming military power, she did not approach every compromised civilization as a target to be destroyed without further consideration. The possibility of retaining biological information and reconstructing viable populations was strategically valuable, especially when the Empire possessed advanced cloning and supernatural technologies that made such efforts increasingly feasible.[26]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
During the Empire's accelerated development, Velnorah became one of the principal researchers responsible for transforming its scientific and technological capabilities. The civilization's growth was no longer measured merely by the number of planets it governed, but by its capacity to develop weapons, transportation systems, artificial intelligences and biological technologies capable of operating across Galactic distances.[12]
The Imperial military was eventually reorganized into specialized Legions. The Fourth Legion, [[Stellar Arsenal]], brought together advanced scientific, magical and technological research under major figures including Velnorah, Ruby and Aline. Its responsibilities included the development of weapons capable of threatening Solar Systems and enemies whose supernatural powers exceeded the limitations of ordinary technological warfare.[12]
Velnorah's knowledge became particularly important because she had personally experienced the consequences of confronting Galactic Emperors whose strength rendered conventional military superiority insufficient. Rather than concentrating exclusively upon the construction of more powerful machines, the Fourth Legion combined Draconic Runes, Divine Concepts, supernatural materials, biological engineering, artificial intelligences and advanced technological systems into weapons designed for enemies that could manipulate or ignore ordinary physical laws.[12]
One of the Empire's greatest classified achievements was Polaris, a massive Divine-technological warship developed as a galaxy-destroyer-class weapon. Construction began around Imperial Year 100,000, and the project remained restricted to a small number of the most trusted Imperial authorities and researchers. Polaris was designed to manipulate Reality, Space and Time, employ Destruction-related powers and operate in conjunction with [[Eldritch Energy]].[13]
Its most dangerous function involved channeling the Concept of <END> through [[Fenrir]], [[Typhon]] and Jormungand. The power was so destructive that its firing mechanism could survive only one complete discharge before being erased. After attempting to solve the limitation, the researchers abandoned the assumption that the cannon itself needed to be indestructible and instead developed replaceable firing systems. This allowed the project to remain operational despite the destructive properties of the Energy it was designed to release.[13]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] intended Polaris primarily for [[Hela Elderblood]], whose relationship with END made her particularly suitable for operating its most dangerous systems. He subsequently instructed Velnorah, Ruby and Aline to continue improving the vessel and permitted Pepper to contribute her extraordinary creativity. [[Scathach]] also joined the project because she distrusted the consequences of allowing Pepper to pursue unrestricted weapons development without supervision.[13]
Velnorah also participated in the strategic discussions concerning the Empire's military response to hostile Higher-Sector powers. The discovery of enemies capable of using corruption, possession and biological manipulation made ordinary military superiority insufficient. Velnorah proposed expanding the use of artificial and mechanical soldiers, reasoning that the Empire should not rely exclusively upon living troops when expendable forces could absorb some of the dangers associated with unknown enemy abilities.[13]
[[Amaterasu]] challenged the proposal by pointing out that mindless machines could themselves become vulnerable to supernatural corruption or hostile control. Velnorah recognized the problem and accepted that no technological system could be declared absolutely secure against every unknown supernatural ability. The resulting strategy emphasized multiple defensive methods, combining artificial forces with the stronger spiritual protections available through Victor's control over Souls.[13]
The discussion became an important influence upon Velnorah's later research. She decided to investigate the possibility of recreating members of her former race and producing specialized technological personnel whose abilities would complement the Empire's existing soldiers. Rather than creating another force of ordinary machines, she intended to develop living researchers and engineers capable of independently supporting complex military projects.[13][14]
During the later Imperial era, Velnorah and Velina revisited the Yuurakyo through advanced cloning and genetic research. The warrior race had once represented one of the greatest military challenges faced by the [[Thaloria Empire]], and Velnorah had previously considered its natural characteristics close to the ideal foundation for powerful soldiers. However, after experiencing the extraordinary abilities of [[True Dragons]], Bloodhunters and other Imperial creations, she recognized numerous weaknesses within the race she had once regarded as exceptional.[14]
The contrast forced Velnorah to reconsider the purpose of her research. She did not need another supreme warrior race, since the Empire already possessed extraordinary combatants whose abilities vastly exceeded those of the original Yuurakyo. Instead, she wanted to preserve the Yuurakyo's adaptability while combining it with the technological affinity of the [[Xyrra]], producing a new population specialized in Technomancy.[14]
Her proposed technomages would possess advanced scientific knowledge from birth, a strong natural relationship with technology and enough personal strength to survive dangerous research environments. Velnorah considered planetary-scale combat capability an appropriate standard for researchers working with the extraordinary materials and Energy systems employed by the Empire, but she did not intend these individuals to replace the strongest members of the Imperial military.[14]
The purpose of the project was specialization and delegation. Velnorah recognized that the Empire's greatest researchers were capable of accomplishing extraordinary feats independently, but also understood that no group of individuals should be responsible for every minor technical decision. Subordinate scientists could identify problems from perspectives unavailable to the Imperial Family, while confidential personnel created for specific tasks would allow Velnorah to continue highly sensitive research without exposing the details to unrelated institutions.[14]
Her conversations with Velina also demonstrated an increasing concern with the upbringing of the younger Elderblood generation. Velnorah recognized that her daughter had grown accustomed to the advantages of True Dragon physiology and the enormous resources of Victor's Empire. Having personally experienced the destruction caused by overconfidence, Velnorah did not want Velina to believe that intelligence and overwhelming power made failure impossible. She considered arranging missions or Dream World experiences capable of exposing her daughter and several sisters to genuine limitations before their arrogance became dangerous.[14]
The period also demonstrated Velnorah's continuing relationship with the Imperial Family. Despite their enormous responsibilities, she and the other researchers participated in regular family gatherings and remained involved in the lives of their daughters. Velnorah expected Velina to attend the traditional family dinner rather than remaining indefinitely in the laboratory, reflecting the importance the household placed upon maintaining personal relationships during centuries of accelerated development.[14]
During a later gathering, the Imperial Family examined the corpse of an enormous extraterrestrial creature whose size and biology exceeded the standards of ordinary planetary life. Ruby, Velnorah and Aline immediately recognized the value of its unusual biological materials. Its fur possessed characteristics associated with Space, while the extraordinary Energy density within its digestive system suggested possibilities for research far beyond conventional biological engineering.[27]
Velnorah was particularly interested in the implications for the Empire's Leviathans. The enormous creatures were already bred and maintained in great numbers, and some possessed the capacity to swallow entire planets. Recognizing the value of even larger organisms encountered near the edges of the Sector, Velnorah instructed that future encounters should prioritize capturing suitable specimens alive when possible rather than destroying them before their biology could be studied.[27]
Her expertise also extended to specialized equipment designed for other members of Victor's family. When [[Tyche]] Elderblood developed increasingly advanced perception associated with Luck and Happiness, the enormous quantity of information available through her eyes became difficult to regulate. Velnorah and Aline developed a special blindfold intended to restrain the sensory effects, allowing Tyche to function without continuously exposing herself to perceptions she could not yet comfortably control.[28][29]
Velnorah's later research became particularly important during the events surrounding the merger of multiple Lower Sectors into Victor's existing Mid-Level Sector. The operation required the movement and incorporation of enormous cosmic structures without prematurely exposing the younger civilizations to the dangers of the Higher Sectors. Velnorah compared the cosmological arrangement to aquariums: Lower Sectors were smaller protected enclosures, the Mid-Level Sector was a larger enclosure and the Higher Sectors represented the open ocean beyond them.[30]
The comparison reflected her familiarity with the consequences of Sector advancement. Velnorah had personally experienced the disaster that occurred when a civilization was exposed to stronger powers before possessing the military foundation necessary to survive. She therefore recognized the value of Victor's decision to incorporate neighboring Lower Sectors into an existing protected domain instead of forcing them directly into the open conflict of the Higher Sectors.[3][30]
Her work with Velina eventually contributed to another highly classified Imperial development. During the aftermath of the Sector merger, Velina discovered an unusual thought experiment concerning paradoxes and recognized its possible relevance to research Velnorah had been conducting. Although the information came from material Velina had obtained without permission, Velnorah immediately recognized the theoretical significance of the idea and began considering how it might be developed into a more general method of manipulating Reality.[31]
Velnorah gathered her usual research companions and began developing the concept with them. The group recognized that the theoretical approach possessed practical potential, and Violet approved further investigation after learning what the resulting technology might accomplish. The project involved methods whose implications extended beyond ordinary engineering, requiring the researchers to consider how paradoxical conditions could be established within the existing rules of the Cosmos.[31]
The following discussions demonstrated the difficulty of the undertaking. Velnorah, Aline, Ruby and Velina attempted to identify a method of creating an absolute paradox, but discovered that producing such a condition through the Universe's own mechanisms was considerably more complicated than altering Reality with powers originating outside its conventional framework. Their difficulties illustrated the limitations of even the most accomplished Imperial researchers when attempting to manipulate fundamental cosmic principles through technical methods rather than relying exclusively upon Victor's extraordinary authority.[32]
By the latest Imperial developments, Velnorah had therefore become considerably more than a former Overlord who had found refuge in Victor's family. Her research influenced the biological, technological, military and conceptual development of a civilization preparing to confront the greatest powers of the Higher Sectors. At the same time, the destruction of [[Eldoria]] continued shaping her judgment, especially her insistence that the next generation should not confuse extraordinary talent with invincibility.[14][31]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Velnorah's husband, Blood Dragon Progenitor, principal political ally and the individual who provided the foundation for her second life after the collapse of [[Eldoria]]. Their relationship began through strategic necessity rather than affection. Velnorah needed an ally capable of challenging the Galactic Emperors who had destroyed her civilization, while Victor recognized that her scientific knowledge and military experience could enormously accelerate the development of Dragons Nest.[6][3]
Velnorah initially evaluated Victor as she would any powerful ruler, attempting to understand his capabilities, ambitions and willingness to cooperate. She had experienced betrayal from individuals she once trusted and therefore considered emotional dependence dangerous. Her original expectations of marriage were pragmatic: she would provide knowledge, political cooperation and possibly an heir in exchange for military support, while preserving the freedom to pursue her own objectives.[9][1]
Victor gradually overcame these defenses by refusing to treat her as a subordinate whose value depended exclusively upon her usefulness. He acknowledged her superior expertise in technology and administration, entrusted her with the development of Elvenorah and avoided interfering with every detail of her work. For Velnorah, this demonstrated that Victor genuinely respected her judgment rather than merely tolerating it while he needed her resources.[8][9]
The transformation into a Blood Dragon also became important to their relationship. Victor reconstructed Velnorah's Soul and removed the racial limitations she had reached as a [[Xyrra]], granting her the opportunity to develop abilities beyond the ceiling of her former existence. The process required considerable trust because Velnorah recognized how thoroughly he could alter her fundamental nature if he chose to misuse the power. Her willingness to accept the transformation therefore reflected her growing confidence in his intentions.[7]
Their relationship eventually culminated in a [[Soul Marriage]]. Victor explained that he wanted Velnorah completely, including her thoughts, Soul, emotions and existence, rather than merely her body or knowledge. The declaration directly addressed the fear that had shaped her original approach to political marriage, convincing her that their relationship could become something far more meaningful than an arrangement created for revenge.[1]
Velnorah's affection subsequently became increasingly possessive. Her Dragon nature intensified emotions she had previously suppressed, while Victor's continued trust and confidence gave her reasons to value the family they were establishing together. Although she remained rational and disciplined in scientific matters, she no longer attempted to deny the importance of her personal feelings toward him.[1]
Their cooperation remained important throughout the Imperial era. Velnorah and Velina developed the prototype [[Dragon Heart]], while Victor transformed it into one of the foundations of his Empire. Later, Velnorah became one of the major researchers involved in the development of Polaris and other weapons intended to protect the civilization from threats comparable to those that destroyed Eldoria.[2][13]

#### Thaloria Xyphora
[[Thaloria Xyphora]] is Velnorah's biological mother, predecessor and founder of the dynasty from which she inherited her name. Thaloria was one of the most extraordinary technological geniuses produced by the [[Xyrra]], compensating for her initially weaker physical constitution through advanced engineering and eventually establishing an Empire that conquered [[Eldoria]] and expanded across its Galaxy.[3]
Velnorah inherited her mother's scientific knowledge and the traditions of the Royal Family, but the relationship was also defined by the struggle for succession. Velnorah ultimately defeated Thaloria and became the [[Anna Elderblood|Second Empress]], inheriting the position of Overlord after proving that she possessed sufficient power to surpass her predecessor.[3]
The two subsequently confronted the greatest catastrophe in the history of their civilization. When the Sector advanced, both possessed enough personal power to challenge the invading Galactic Emperors, but the Empire's forces could not maintain the same level of resistance. The extermination of the Yuurakyo, once considered one of Thaloria's greatest strategic victories, became a central reason their civilization lacked the military strength necessary to survive.[3]
Velnorah continues acknowledging her mother's achievements while recognizing the consequences of her mistakes. She repeats Thaloria's advice concerning the impossibility of perfect engineering when teaching Velina and preserves the dynasty's naming tradition as a tribute to the civilization Thaloria originally created.[2]
Their history therefore combines admiration, rivalry, succession and the shared tragedy of Eldoria's downfall. Velnorah's determination to prevent the [[Elderblood Empire]] from repeating the same mistakes reflects the lasting influence of her mother's achievements and failures.[3][14]

#### Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III
[[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]] is Velnorah's biological daughter with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and one of the most important individuals in her personal life. Velina inherited extraordinary scientific intelligence and a strong interest in technology, allowing her to participate directly in her mother's research from a relatively early stage of her development.[2]
Velnorah deliberately named her daughter according to the traditions of the Thaloria Royal Family. The name preserved the memory of the First Empress and the dynasty that once ruled [[Eldoria]], establishing Velina as the continuation of a lineage whose original civilization had been destroyed.[2]
Their relationship is characterized by affection, intellectual rivalry and extensive cooperation. Velina frequently challenges her mother's conclusions, particularly when she believes a technical problem can be solved through further research. During the development of the [[Dragon Heart]], she attempted to eliminate every possible failure condition, while Velnorah explained that even the greatest researchers could not achieve absolute perfection without possessing complete knowledge of the Universe.[2]
The two share the tendency to become absorbed in scientific problems, but Velnorah recognizes that Velina still lacks certain lessons acquired through personal failure. After becoming accustomed to the extraordinary advantages of True Dragon physiology and the resources of the [[Elderblood Empire]], Velina began displaying arrogance that reminded Velnorah of her own younger years. Velnorah considered deliberately exposing her daughter to difficult experiences so she would understand the danger of underestimating enemies before suffering consequences comparable to the destruction of Eldoria.[14]
Their scientific collaboration remains one of the defining aspects of their relationship. Velina helped develop the prototype Dragon Heart, participated in the research surrounding Akasha's original design, contributed to the proposed technomage population and later supplied an idea that helped Velnorah's research group develop a new approach to fundamental paradoxes.[2][14][31]

#### Ruby Elderblood Scarlett
[[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] is one of Velnorah's closest scientific collaborators within the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Although their origins are completely different, both possess extraordinary intelligence and an intense interest in research. Ruby developed her expertise through [[Magic]], biological engineering and supernatural science, while Velnorah brought the technological traditions and interstellar experience of the [[Xyrra]].[2][11]
Their collaboration became essential to the development of Imperial infrastructure. Together with Aline, they contributed to the projects that eventually produced the [[Dragon Heart]], advanced cloning systems, elite Imperial soldiers and the scientific institutions necessary to support a Galactic civilization.[2][23]
Velnorah recognizes that Ruby's intelligence is sufficient to contribute ideas beyond the limitations of her own expertise, while Ruby benefits from the extensive technological knowledge Velnorah inherited from [[Eldoria]]. Their cooperation is particularly important because the Empire's most advanced technologies frequently require Magic, Biology, Divine Concepts and engineering to function together rather than as independent disciplines.[11]
Both women also participate in the Fourth Imperial Legion, [[Stellar Arsenal]], whose research includes weapons capable of threatening entire Solar Systems and galaxies. Their later involvement in the Polaris project represents one of the most extreme expressions of their scientific cooperation.[12][13]

#### Aline Elderblood Valefar
[[Aline Elderblood Valefar]] is another of Velnorah's principal research partners. Her scientific and supernatural expertise complements the technological specialization of Velnorah and the biological knowledge of Ruby, allowing the three women to conduct experiments that would be difficult for an individual researcher to reproduce independently.[2][11]
Aline also provides perspectives influenced by her Demonic origin, creating a contrast with Velnorah's experience as an extraterrestrial Empress. During the Skaarith investigation, Aline questioned whether the infected population could be saved, while Velnorah approached the problem through the need to eliminate hostile influences and preserve useful biological information.[26]
The three became central figures in the Empire's scientific development. Their cooperation produced technologies ranging from biological engineering and planetary infrastructure to advanced Divine warships and equipment capable of restraining unusual supernatural perceptions.[13][29]
Despite their extraordinary abilities, Velnorah acknowledges that they cannot personally perform every necessary task. The realization influences her later desire to create specialized subordinate researchers who can provide new perspectives while allowing the primary scientists to concentrate upon projects requiring their unique expertise.[14]

#### Akasha Elderblood
[[Akasha Elderblood]] is one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s created daughters and the living central intelligence of the Imperial System. Her existence originated from the need Velnorah and Velina identified during the development of the [[Dragon Heart]]: the Empire required an intelligence capable of coordinating enormous technological and supernatural infrastructure without being restricted to the limitations of conventional artificial intelligences.[2]
Velnorah initially proposed a Being constructed from a [[Xyrra]] foundation supplemented by compatible Draconic and other supernatural characteristics. Velina suggested that making the entity another daughter of Victor would provide a strong personal reason for loyalty, while Velnorah recognized that her husband would inevitably become involved in the creation of such a Being.[2]
Victor subsequently developed the concept beyond its original technological objectives, constructing Akasha from his own Blood, Soul and Eldritch Divinity. Akasha became a genuine living daughter rather than the conventional artificial intelligence originally envisioned by Velnorah's project.[10]
Although Akasha's final creation differed from the original design, she fulfills the central purpose of the project by coordinating the Empire's increasingly sophisticated systems. Her existence therefore represents one of the most important consequences of Velnorah's scientific planning.[2][10]

#### The Elderblood Family
Velnorah entered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family after spending centuries as an independent Empress whose political relationships had been shaped by conquest, rivalry and betrayal. The Elderblood household therefore represented an unfamiliar environment in which extraordinary individuals could cooperate without treating every disagreement as a struggle for political supremacy.[9][1]
Although she initially found the idea of sharing a husband difficult to reconcile with her former mentality, she gradually developed genuine relationships with the other wives. Ruby and Aline became her principal scientific collaborators, while Violet entrusted her with major Imperial responsibilities and Victor encouraged her to pursue her ambitions without requiring constant supervision.[8][12]
Velnorah also became increasingly invested in the upbringing of the younger generation. Her concern for Velina and other daughters reflects an awareness that extraordinary intelligence and power do not eliminate the possibility of failure. She wants the children of the [[Elderblood Empire]] to possess opportunities unavailable to the people of [[Eldoria]], but also recognizes that unrestricted comfort and power can produce dangerous arrogance.[14]
The family consequently became an important part of her identity beyond her original responsibilities as Empress and researcher. The Thaloria dynasty survives through Velina, but the larger Elderblood family provides a political and emotional foundation far stronger than the alliances Velnorah had originally expected to form after losing her homeland.[2][14]

### Abilities

Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II is an ancient extraterrestrial Goddess, former Overlord, former [[Xyrra]], and current Blood Dragon whose abilities combine extremely advanced Divine Concepts, natural Technomancy, technological warfare, Soul-related powers, and the superior physiology of a genuine True Dragon. As the [[Anna Elderblood|Second Empress]] of [[Eldoria]], she possessed extraordinary scientific knowledge and military experience long before encountering [[Victor Elderblood]]. Her subsequent transformation into a Blood Dragon expanded the limits of her physical and spiritual existence, allowing her already highly developed powers to advance beyond the restrictions of her original race.[1][2][3]
Velnorah's combat capabilities are distinguished by her integration of scientific technology with supernatural authority. Rather than relying exclusively upon conventional physical attacks, she employs highly sophisticated machinery capable of interacting with Divine Concepts, Souls, dimensional structures, and the supernatural abilities of powerful Gods. Her equipment can appear directly around her body, change configuration according to her requirements, release large-scale Energy pulses, and coordinate multiple operations simultaneously. During her first major battle alongside Victor, Velnorah suppressed the Divine Concepts of an entire enemy Pantheon, identified the designated targets, and captured seventy-six Gods in a single operation without killing them.[4]
Her original technological abilities also extend far beyond combat. The Xyrra possess an instinctive affinity for technology, allowing them to construct and manipulate devices through abilities that ordinary civilizations would require extensive research to reproduce. Velnorah developed this talent to an extraordinary degree and eventually became one of the greatest technological authorities within the [[Elderblood Empire]], contributing to the development of the [[Dragon Heart]], advanced artificial intelligences, Imperial military clones, dimensional technologies, and weapons capable of threatening entire galaxies.[1][5][6]
Although Velnorah possesses immense individual power, the technologies she develops must be distinguished from her personal supernatural abilities. A weapon constructed through the cooperation of Velnorah, Ruby, Aline, Velina, Victor, and other specialists is not necessarily an ability Velnorah can reproduce independently without equipment. Her personal powers, combat techniques, technological creations, and collaborative Imperial projects are therefore indexed separately.

#### Racial Physiology

##### Xyrra Physiology
[[Xyrra]] Physiology (Formerly): Velnorah was originally a member of the Xyrra, an extraterrestrial species of giant humanoids naturally gifted in technological development. Members of the race commonly exceed three meters in height and possess an unusual ability to understand, construct, and manipulate technology almost instinctively. Unlike ordinary scientific education, this aptitude is an innate racial characteristic, allowing the Xyrra to develop sophisticated machinery with comparatively little effort.[1]
The Xyrra's natural advantages enabled the civilization founded by [[Thaloria Xyphora]] to advance rapidly. Within fewer than two thousand years, the species had established dominance over [[Eldoria]] and developed the foundations necessary for interstellar expansion. Velnorah inherited this technological foundation and eventually reached the limits of her original racial existence before joining [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood Dragon lineage.[1][2]
- Giant Physiology: Velnorah's original Xyrra body stood approximately three meters tall, consistent with the exceptional height of her species.[1]
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics: The Xyrra possess physical capabilities beyond ordinary Humans, although their greatest racial advantage lies in their technological aptitude.[1]
- Natural Technological Affinity: Velnorah inherited the Xyrra's innate compatibility with advanced technology.[1]
- Instinctive Technology Manipulation: Members of the Xyrra can manipulate machinery through their natural racial abilities, with ordinary individuals capable of constructing sophisticated devices through gestures.[1]
- Technomancy: Velnorah possesses the supernatural aptitude for combining technological systems with personal power, allowing machinery to function as an extension of her abilities.[1][5]
- Rapid Technological Construction: She can construct or reconfigure advanced technological equipment without relying exclusively upon conventional manufacturing procedures.[4][1]
- Technological Comprehension: Velnorah possesses the natural foundation necessary to understand complex scientific systems and develop them through further research.[1]
- Technology-Based Adaptation: Her original racial affinity allows her to adapt technological equipment according to different environments, enemies, and operational requirements.[4]
- Enhanced Scientific Learning: The Xyrra's aptitude provides a considerable advantage in acquiring scientific and engineering knowledge.[1]
- Advanced Technological Compatibility: Velnorah can operate sophisticated equipment designed for her original race and combine it with supernatural systems acquired after leaving Eldoria.[4][5]
Although Velnorah no longer possesses a Xyrra body as her principal racial foundation, her technological aptitude was not erased through Dragonization. Instead, the abilities and knowledge developed during her former existence were incorporated into her new Draconic foundation.[2][3]

##### Blood Dragon Physiology
Blood Dragon Physiology: Velnorah became a genuine Blood Dragon after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] reconstructed her Soul and transformed her former [[Xyrra]] existence into that of a True Dragon. The transformation was particularly significant because Velnorah had already reached the natural limits of her original species and developed her Divinities to an extraordinary degree. Her new racial foundation provided additional potential without requiring her to abandon the technological specialization that defined her previous existence.[2][3]
During the transformation, Victor directly altered the structure of Velnorah's Soul. She instinctively attempted to defend herself against the intrusion through her own Divine power before recognizing Victor and allowing the process to continue. Velnorah subsequently perceived the internal foundations of her existence being reconstructed, recognizing that the transformation reached considerably deeper than an ordinary physical metamorphosis.[3]

##### Divine Concepts
Velnorah's existing profile associates her with several Divine titles: War, Strategic Warfare, Technology, Research, Soul, and Wisdom. These titles describe the major fields of her supernatural and intellectual expertise. However, a Divine title, a demonstrated skill, and the possession of a separately named Divine Concept are not always interchangeable. The reviewed novel passages directly establish Velnorah's extraordinarily advanced Divinities and several applications of Divine power, while not individually defining the complete authority and limitations of all six listed Concepts.[14][13][11]
The following entries therefore distinguish powers demonstrated through Velnorah's actions from the broader Divine domains traditionally associated with her.

Velnorah and Aline developed a specialized blindfold intended to help [[Tyche]] Elderblood regulate her extraordinary supernatural perception. Tyche's eyes could perceive aspects of Happiness and Luck whose continuous exposure became increasingly difficult to tolerate.[26]
- Sensory Restraint Engineering: Velnorah contributed to equipment designed to suppress an overwhelming supernatural sense.[26]
- Divine Perception Regulation: The artifact helps control unwanted exposure to information perceived through Tyche's eyes.[26]
- Custom Divine Equipment: Velnorah can collaborate with other researchers to design equipment for the unusual abilities of specific individuals.[26]

##### Absolute Paradox Research
During the later Imperial era, Velina introduced Velnorah to a thought experiment involving paradoxes. Velnorah quickly recognized that the underlying principle could have applications far beyond the original example and gathered Ruby, Aline, and Velina to investigate its possible use as a weapon.[17]
The research aimed to create an absolute paradox through the rules of the Cosmos itself. However, the project proved exceptionally difficult because altering the Universe through its own internal mechanisms was far more complicated than producing distortions through external supernatural power. The researchers recognized that [[Eldritch Energy]] could influence Reality from outside its ordinary framework, but that an internal contradiction capable of genuinely violating the Cosmos' rules presented a different problem.[12]
- Paradox Research: Velnorah investigates methods of creating contradictions within fundamental cosmic rules.[17][12]
- Advanced Theoretical Physics: She can recognize large-scale applications of scientific principles originally presented through simple thought experiments.[17]
- Reality Engineering Research: The project concerns the manipulation of underlying cosmic principles rather than ordinary material engineering.[12]
- Collaborative Conceptual Research: Velnorah works alongside Ruby, Aline, and Velina to investigate the problem.[12]
- External Versus Internal Reality Manipulation: Velnorah recognizes the difference between influencing the Cosmos through foreign Energy and attempting to break its rules from within.[12]
- Advanced Weapon Development: The project was intended to produce a weapon capable of threatening enemies against whom ordinary Imperial technologies might be insufficient.[17][12]

#### Military Command and Strategic Abilities
Velnorah possesses extensive experience ruling and commanding interstellar civilizations. She inherited the technological and military foundation of the [[Thaloria Empire]], defeated her mother to become [[Anna Elderblood|Second Empress]], and subsequently confronted the Galactic Emperors responsible for her civilization's collapse.[1]
Her later Imperial operations demonstrate that she continues applying this experience through the use of reconnaissance, coordinated military forces, technological analysis, and preservation of strategically valuable resources.[18]

##### Military Leadership
- Military Leadership: Velnorah possesses extensive experience commanding military forces across enormous territories.[1]
- Interstellar Warfare: Her experience includes conflicts between technologically advanced civilizations and powerful supernatural individuals.[1]
- Imperial Command: Velnorah has held the position of Empress and exercised authority over a large technological civilization.[1]
- Large-Scale Strategic Planning: She can organize operations involving multiple technological and military components.[4][18]
- Advanced Threat Assessment: Velnorah understands the danger of confronting enemies whose supernatural abilities invalidate conventional technological advantages.[1]
- Military Countermeasure Planning: She develops strategies and equipment intended to address such threats.[9][6]
- Mission Objective Prioritization: Velnorah can distinguish between situations requiring elimination, containment, capture, or preservation.[4][18]

##### Skaarith Operation
During the investigation of the compromised [[Skaarith Empire]], Velnorah commanded Imperial forces assigned to confront a civilization affected by hostile biological and supernatural influences. The operation involved the elimination of dangerous targets, collection of biological samples, and identification of the mechanisms responsible for maintaining the infection.[18]
- Orbital Military Coordination: Velnorah coordinated the use of Imperial military forces against a compromised extraterrestrial civilization.[18]
- Biological Threat Containment: The operation treated the infection as a dangerous supernatural and strategic threat.[18]
- Specimen Preservation: Velnorah prioritized obtaining useful biological samples for later study.[18]
- World Tree Preservation: She recognized the value of protecting the planet's World Tree because it might retain information necessary to restore aspects of the original civilization.[18]
- Strategic Intelligence Collection: The operation involved identifying possible connections between the infected civilization and hostile Higher-Sector powers.[18]

#### Resistances
Velnorah possesses several distinct defensive foundations: her advanced Divine existence, Blood Dragon physiology, personal supernatural control, and highly sophisticated technological equipment. These sources should be distinguished because resistance demonstrated by a specialized artifact does not necessarily establish that her unprotected body possesses the same property.

##### Physical Resistance
- Enhanced Physical Resistance: Velnorah possesses the natural durability of a genuine True Dragon in addition to her preexisting Divine constitution.[3]
- Enhanced Biological Resilience: Her transformation into a Blood Dragon provided a superior physical foundation to her former [[Xyrra]] body.[2][3]
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Velnorah possesses extraordinary regenerative potential through her current Draconic and Divine existence.
- Supernatural Longevity: Her existence is not limited by ordinary Human biological aging.
- Technological Protection: Her combat suit provides additional protection beyond the capabilities of her natural body.[1]
- Resistance to Destruction Through Equipment: Velnorah's technologically enhanced hand withstood contact involving [[Shiva]]'s destructive power.[13]

##### Spiritual Resistance
- Divinity-Based Soul Protection: Velnorah instinctively employed her own Divine power against an intrusion into her Soul.[3]
- Soul Intrusion Awareness: She can recognize activity affecting the internal structure of her spiritual existence.[3]
- Draconic Soul Resilience: Her Soul possesses the superior foundation acquired through transformation into a True Dragon.[3]
- Advanced Divine Spiritual Constitution: Velnorah possesses a highly developed spiritual existence capable of supporting multiple Divinities.[14][3]

##### Detection Resistance
- Power Concealment: Velnorah can suppress the outward manifestation of her supernatural strength.[15]
- Presence Concealment: Her controlled state makes the evaluation of her actual power unusually difficult.[15]
- Divinity Concealment Through Equipment: Her specialized bracelets can conceal the identity of the Goddess responsible for Divine activity.[9]
- Technological Stealth: Velnorah develops advanced armor designed to conceal supernatural power and Divine Concepts.[9]
- Anti-Detection Research: The Empire's Mark I concealment armor was effective against several forms of supernatural perception, although it remained vulnerable to Life-spectrum detection.[16]

##### Known Limitations
- Equipment Dependence: Several of Velnorah's most impressive demonstrated abilities require specialized machinery or technological systems.[4]
- Divinity Concealment Limits: Her concealment bracelets can fail or be destroyed when exposed to Divine power exceeding their tolerance.[9]
- Life-Spectrum Detection: The Empire's early concealment armor was vulnerable to supernatural perception associated with Life.[16]
- Incomplete Scientific Knowledge: Velnorah recognizes that researchers cannot account for every possible variable without complete knowledge of the Universe.[5]
- Technological Vulnerabilities: Even advanced technologies may be threatened by unexpected Divine, conceptual, or Eldritch effects.[1][6]
- No Confirmed Universal Immunity: The reviewed material does not establish unconditional resistance to every form of Soul Manipulation, Conceptual Manipulation, Fate Manipulation, or Existence Erasure.
- No Confirmed Regulatory Status: Velnorah's exceptional Divine comprehension does not establish that she has become the fundamental Regulator of any of her associated Concepts.[14]
- Collaborative Weapons Are Not Personal Techniques: The destructive output of Polaris and other Imperial weapons cannot automatically be assigned to Velnorah's unarmed attacks.[6]
- Racial Compatibility Is Not Complete Mastery: Her Draconic foundation provides exceptional potential, but she has not independently demonstrated every technique available to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] or the strongest members of the Blood Dragon lineage.
