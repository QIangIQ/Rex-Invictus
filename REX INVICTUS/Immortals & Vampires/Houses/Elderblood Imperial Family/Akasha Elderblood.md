#### Debut
Chapter 1049: The One Who Protects Everything. 2
From my Divinity, I make you The Guardian of my Family and my Empire.
—[[Victor Elderblood]] creating Akasha, Chapter 1049
Akasha Elderblood is an Eldritch Goddess, a Blood Dragon, an Imperial Princess of the [[Elderblood Empire]], and one of the daughters personally created by Victor Elderblood. She serves as the central living intelligence of the Imperial System, coordinating enormous portions of the Empire's communications, information processing, surveillance, technological infrastructure and military support networks. Her responsibilities extend beyond the administration of individual planets, connecting the institutions, artificial intelligences, military formations and administrative systems of a civilization whose territory eventually spans numerous galaxies. She is commonly described as the Mind of the Empire, reflecting her position as its principal information and coordination intelligence rather than an absolute political ruler.[1][6][2]
Unlike conventional artificial intelligences, Akasha is a genuine living Being rather than software that subsequently acquired a personality. Victor deliberately constructed her physical body, introduced his Blood to establish her Draconic foundation, separated a fragment of his own Soul to give her genuine Life, and incorporated his Eldritch Divinity into her existence. The result was an individual possessing a real Soul, consciousness, emotions, an independent ego and free will from the moment of her creation. Although Akasha was entrusted with the extraordinarily important responsibility of protecting Victor's family and Empire, Victor refused to reduce her existence to that of an emotionless servant whose only purpose was carrying out instructions.[1]
Akasha's principal supernatural authority is the Eldritch Domain of Control. Its conceptual scope encompasses Control and related ideas such as Monitoring, Oversight and Supervision. However, the circumstances surrounding her creation produced an unusual contradiction. Victor intended Akasha to possess Control while simultaneously wishing for her to remain free from the influence of everyone except himself. Because Eldritch powers do not necessarily follow the same conceptual boundaries as ordinary native Divinities, the contradiction caused Akasha's Domain to encompass its opposite: Loss of Control. She therefore possesses an Eldritch authority whose nature includes both the exercise of Control and the disruption of Control, rather than an ordinary Divine Concept restricted to administrative supervision.[6]
Her Eldritch existence is fundamentally connected to Victor rather than directly integrated into the ordinary Divine system of his native Creation. When Victor asked Akasha to identify her Divinity, she initially stated that she did not possess ordinary Divinities, subsequently explaining that his Eldritch Form had influenced her creation and granted her the Domain of Control. She described herself as connected to Victor through his existence as a God of [[Primordial Chaos|Primal Chaos]] belonging to the [[Eldritch Pantheon]]. This distinction makes her an Eldritch Goddess without automatically establishing that she possesses every characteristic of a fully fledged Outer God.[6][7]
As the Empire expanded, Akasha's responsibilities developed alongside its increasingly sophisticated infrastructure. Her extraordinary processing capacity allowed her to coordinate enormous quantities of information, direct networked warships, communicate through remote holographic manifestations and operate alongside hundreds of thousands of subordinate artificial intelligences. She became one of the Empire's principal mechanisms for connecting information gathered by explorers, military commanders, administrators and scientific researchers to the individuals responsible for acting upon it. Her work is supported by technological and supernatural systems, including the [[Dragon Heart]], World Cores and the later World Tree communication network.[8][9][2][10]
Her Imperial responsibilities never eliminated her personal identity. Akasha developed affectionate relationships with Victor and the other members of his family, participated in the activities of her sisters and gradually acquired emotional experience distinct from the information Victor had supplied during her creation. Her intense attachment to her Father, extraordinary knowledge and unwillingness to conceal serious misbehavior from him earned her the humorous reputation of being the Number One Snitch of the Father. Following the enormous periods of accelerated development experienced by the Elderblood family, Akasha reached adulthood and was eventually counted among Victor's wives while retaining the unusual history of having been created entirely from his own existence.[4][11][5]

### Physical Appearance

#### Blood Dragon
Akasha's Blood Dragon appearance.
Akasha possesses the appearance of an extraordinarily beautiful woman with pale white skin, vivid violet eyes and exceptionally long snow-white hair extending approximately to her ankles. Her physical form was created directly by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] rather than produced through conventional biological reproduction, meaning her original characteristics did not arise from the ordinary combination of two parental Bloodlines. Instead, every fundamental component of her newly created body originated from Victor's supernatural power, while the characteristics associated with her eventual racial existence emerged through the Blood he introduced into her body.[1]
Victor initially constructed Akasha as a complete but Soulless humanoid body. The process required less than ten seconds and produced an adult female appearance with exceptionally long white hair. When he subsequently introduced a drop of his Blood into her mouth, Draconic features emerged almost immediately. White Dragon horns developed upon her head, her ears became slightly pointed, and white scales appeared around portions of her ears. The transformation established a genuine Blood Dragon foundation rather than merely granting her cosmetic Draconic characteristics.[1]
Akasha's eyes possess the vivid violet coloration characteristic of Victor's direct descendants. They opened for the first time after Victor completed the creation of her Soul and declared her name, making his face and affectionate expression the first sight she experienced. The moment was particularly significant to Akasha because she was not an existing consciousness awakened within a new vessel; it marked the beginning of her individual memories and the first experience of her life.[1]
After her awakening, Victor created an elegant white dress for Akasha. The garment complemented her snow-white hair and gave her a refined appearance appropriate to her identity as an Imperial Princess. Its predominantly white coloration contrasted with the darker visual characteristics associated with Victor's Eldritch powers, emphasizing that Akasha's physical presentation did not need to resemble the grotesque forms commonly associated with Eldritch Beings.[6]
As a Blood Dragon, Akasha possesses the racial foundation necessary for more extensive Draconic transformation. Her humanoid appearance should not be confused with the entirety of her existence, since the characteristics inherited from Victor include genuine True Dragon physiology. However, the presence of Draconic artwork and racial compatibility should not be used to assign specific dimensions or unique abilities to an independently demonstrated full Dragon Form without corresponding novel evidence.[1]

#### Eldritch Goddess
Akasha's supernatural nature includes Eldritch characteristics originating from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s own Divinity. Unlike an ordinary Goddess whose Divine Concept emerges entirely through the rules of the native Universe, Akasha possesses a fundamental connection to Victor's Eldritch existence and an intrinsic Domain governing Control and its contradictory inverse.[6]
Her Eldritch nature does not require her to maintain a monstrous physical appearance. She generally retains the beautiful humanoid form Victor created for her, even while operating through supernatural systems and environments associated with Eldritch power. During Victor's later lessons, Akasha was identified among the daughters who were comparatively comfortable in an Eldritch environment because her existence had been constructed using his Eldritch Divinity.[7]
This compatibility distinguishes Akasha from ordinary Dragon Goddesses, but the novel also distinguishes her origin from that of daughters such as Yog, whose Eldritch nature arises through both Victor and Azathoth. Consequently, Akasha's physical depiction as an Eldritch Goddess should not automatically be treated as confirmation that she possesses the same complete Outer God physiology or transformation capabilities as every other Eldritch member of the family.[7]

#### Imperial System Manifestation
Akasha's Imperial System manifestation.
Akasha can communicate through remote holographic representations generated by the Empire's technological and supernatural infrastructure. These manifestations reproduce her humanoid appearance, including her distinctive snow-white hair and violet eyes, allowing Imperial personnel to recognize her as the Emperor's daughter even when her genuine physical body remains elsewhere.[12]
Her remote appearances are particularly useful during military operations. Akasha can manifest through the communications systems of an Imperial warship, deliver tactical information, issue authorized instructions and coordinate the arrival of reinforcements without physically entering the battlefield. Soldiers and officers recognize the manifestation as an official representative of the Imperial Family, treating her communications with the respect accorded to her position.[12]
These holograms are not independently existing duplicates of Akasha. They are interfaces connected to her consciousness and the Imperial System, allowing her to communicate across enormous distances while remaining within the protected location containing her genuine body. Their presence therefore demonstrates remote interaction and large-scale network access rather than literal Omnipresence.[2]

### Personality
Akasha possesses an exceptionally intelligent, rational and analytical personality, naturally approaching situations through the examination of information, available resources, potential risks and the consequences of different actions. Her original purpose as the Guardian of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family and Empire strongly influences her priorities, causing her to place considerable importance upon surveillance, efficient administration, information security and the prevention of threats before they can develop. However, despite the extraordinary sophistication of her mental abilities, Akasha is not an emotionless artificial intelligence. Victor deliberately created her as a genuine living individual, granting her the capacity to develop personal attachments, preferences and emotions through experience rather than restricting her behavior to predetermined instructions.[1][6]
Akasha's original personality reflects the unusual circumstances of her creation. At the moment of her awakening, she possessed enormous amounts of knowledge concerning the supernatural world, Imperial technology, Victor's relatives and the history available to her Father. She could identify complicated systems and understand the relationships between individuals whom she had never personally met, but she lacked the emotional experience normally acquired by living through events. Victor recognized this distinction immediately and wanted her to spend time with his wives and daughters, understanding that possessing information about a family was not the same as experiencing genuine familial affection.[6]
Her strongest emotional attachment initially centered almost exclusively upon Victor. Since every fundamental component of her existence originated from him, Akasha regarded Victor as her Creator, Father and the most important individual in her life. His presence, praise and affection quickly became major sources of emotional satisfaction. Although she possessed the information necessary to identify his wives as her Mothers, she initially displayed little genuine personal interest in them. Victor and the women present recognized that this reaction was natural for a newly created Being whose first and strongest connection was to her Creator, choosing to allow the relationships to develop through experience rather than demanding immediate affection.[6]
Akasha's devotion to Victor does not imply that she possesses no independent [[Will Solace|Will]]. Victor explicitly granted her free will and an independent ego because he did not want a sophisticated machine pretending to be his daughter. Nevertheless, he established his direct Will as an inviolable priority within her existence. Akasha can make decisions, develop preferences, disagree with others and pursue her own interests, but cannot place her judgment above an explicit command originating from Victor. This restriction was established as a safeguard because the systems she would eventually administer could affect the safety of his entire civilization.[1]
Her intense concern for Victor's security initially manifests through an almost obsessive desire to eliminate every possible informational blind spot. Immediately after connecting to the Imperial System, Akasha identifies regions that lack sufficient monitoring and considers the situation unacceptable because an unobserved location could theoretically conceal preparations for an attack against her Father. Even when the probability of such a threat is extraordinarily small, Akasha considers continuous surveillance necessary because a nonzero risk remains. She consequently proposes greater monitoring of Imperial territory, including regions traditionally governed through the personal authority of Victor's wives.[6]
This tendency is accompanied by an inclination toward extreme contingency planning. Akasha recommends constructing a protected database within a separate dimension, preserving enormous quantities of historical information and recording the memories of accomplished individuals whose knowledge might become useful in future military operations. She also proposes methods for supporting the revival of Imperial clones through information and Soul storage. Ruby compares Akasha's mentality to that of Batman, recognizing the similarities between Akasha's constant surveillance, preparation and willingness to consider numerous possible dangers before they occur.[6]
Despite her serious administrative temperament, Akasha possesses a surprisingly possessive and affectionate side concerning Victor. She responds positively to his physical affection, particularly when he strokes her head, and regards such attention as an extraordinary personal reward. During a later private meeting, Victor created a peaceful residence for her within the protected sub-dimension where she operated. Although she preferred a natural environment over purely technological surroundings, she had been too occupied with her responsibilities to arrange one for herself. Victor transformed the empty space into a cozy mansion overlooking a lake, mountains and trees, making Akasha visibly happy before they resumed discussing Imperial affairs.[2]
Akasha gradually develops a greater understanding of other individuals and the social situations surrounding them. Her intelligence allows her to process enormous quantities of information, but she also learns when silence, patience or delegation is more appropriate than immediately providing every possible conclusion. During an investigation involving hostile activity from the Higher Sectors, she recognized that Victor was considering a conclusion she had not yet reached and deliberately allowed him to continue thinking rather than interrupting. Her judgment therefore developed beyond simple calculation into a more mature understanding of personal behavior and context.[13]
Akasha's relationships with her sisters are influenced by her unusual origin and administrative responsibilities. Because she was created entirely from Victor rather than through a conventional relationship involving two parents, several of her sisters initially regarded her as possessing an unfair advantage in their competition for his attention. Their jealousy was intensified by Akasha's ability to observe enormous amounts of information and her unwillingness to conceal significant misconduct. While other members of the household might be persuaded or negotiated with, Akasha took her responsibility toward Victor seriously enough that her sisters quickly realized she would be an exceptionally inconvenient person to deceive.[4]
This reputation produced the humorous title Number One Snitch of the Father. Nevertheless, Akasha's behavior was not motivated by hatred or distrust toward her sisters. Protecting them was part of the fundamental purpose Victor had given her, and she recognized that concealing dangerous behavior could place their safety at risk. Her strictness therefore coexisted with genuine familial attachment, even when her younger sisters complained about the difficulty of avoiding her attention.[4]
Akasha also demonstrated that she was capable of separating her personal family preferences from her responsibilities. During the Dream World War Games organized by Victor, she participated alongside her sisters and became involved in mediating the conflict rather than simply exploiting her knowledge to favor one of the competing factions. This behavior reflected the independent judgment Victor wanted her to develop and demonstrated that her relationship with the family extended beyond reporting information or maintaining surveillance.[11]
By the mature Imperial era, Akasha had become considerably more socially experienced while retaining her original devotion to Victor and strong interest in protecting the Empire. She continued participating in family gatherings and displaying affection toward her Father despite occupying a position whose responsibilities involved entire fleets, worlds and Galactic communications systems. Her intelligence made her particularly difficult to deceive, but it did not prevent her from enjoying the same affectionate relationships, teasing and rivalries experienced by the rest of the Elderblood family.[14][3]

### Origin
Akasha's creation originated from the increasing difficulty of administering the civilization [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] intended to establish. The [[Elderblood Empire]] was developing beyond the limits of an ordinary supernatural nation, acquiring technological and military systems that would eventually operate across planets, Solar Systems and galaxies. Ruby, Velnorah and other members of the family were developing increasingly sophisticated infrastructure, but Victor recognized that an Empire possessing such enormous ambitions would eventually require a central intelligence capable of coordinating information and systems on a scale far beyond the abilities of conventional administrators.[15][1]
The original technological objective involved the creation of an advanced artificial intelligence connected to the Empire's infrastructure. However, Victor possessed the ability to create genuine Life and did not consider it necessary to limit the project to a conventional program. After examining the existing plans, he decided to create a new living individual capable of fulfilling the intended function while possessing the freedom to develop beyond the original administrative purpose.[1]

#### Creation of Akasha Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] began by constructing a complete humanoid body through his supernatural power. The process required less than ten seconds and produced an adult female form with extraordinarily long snow-white hair. At this stage, the body was biologically functional but lacked a genuine Soul and individual consciousness. Ruby created a comfortable surface upon which it could rest while Victor continued the process.[1]
Victor then introduced his own Blood into the newly formed body, causing it to develop genuine Draconic characteristics. White horns emerged, the ears became pointed and scales appeared around them. Through this process, Akasha acquired the Blood Dragon foundation associated with Victor's lineage, ensuring that her existence would possess the supernatural advantages of a genuine True Dragon rather than remaining merely a biological humanoid body.[1]
The next stage involved the creation of Akasha's Soul. Victor removed a small fragment of his own Soul, which regenerated after the separation, and used the fragment to establish genuine Life. This act provided Akasha with a spiritual foundation originating directly from Victor rather than from a separately created Soul supplied by another Being. Victor also specified that her existence would remain free from outside influences, with the sole exception of his own direct [[Will Solace|Will]].[1]
Finally, Victor incorporated his Eldritch Divinity into the new Being and established her purpose as the Guardian of his Family and Empire. This decision fundamentally influenced the resulting supernatural nature, causing Akasha to develop an Eldritch Domain associated with Control. After completing the process, Victor gave her the name Akasha Elderblood, and her eyes opened for the first time.[1]
The result was not a conventional artificial intelligence whose consciousness developed gradually through the accumulation of information. Akasha possessed genuine Life from the beginning, with a complete Soul, independent ego and capacity for emotional development. Victor was simultaneously her Father and the sole source of the fundamental components from which she had been created.[1]

#### Eldritch Domain of Control
Akasha's Eldritch nature became apparent immediately after her awakening. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] asked her to identify her Divinity, she initially explained that she possessed no ordinary native Divinities. Instead, she recognized that his Eldritch Form had influenced her creation and given her the Domain of Control.[6]
The Concept possessed an unusual contradictory structure. Victor intended Akasha to represent Control and the characteristics associated with it, including Monitoring, Oversight and Supervision. At the same time, he wanted her to possess genuine freedom rather than existing as a puppet controlled by others. The contradiction influenced the result, causing the Domain to encompass the opposite principle of Loss of Control.[6]
Akasha explained that her presence could cause surrounding beings to lose control, demonstrating that her Eldritch power was not restricted to conventional administrative control or the manipulation of connected technological systems. This aspect of her nature existed because the circumstances of her creation combined Victor's desire for a capable guardian with his equally strong desire for an independent daughter.[6]
Victor recognized the contradiction as an unexpected consequence of his first attempt to create a complex living Being through these methods. Nevertheless, he did not regard Akasha as a failure. He reassured her that she was perfect as she was, emphasizing that she remained his daughter regardless of whether her abilities had developed exactly according to his original intentions.[6]

#### Connection to the Dragon Heart
Akasha's existence was directly connected to the [[Dragon Heart]], the extraordinary Imperial artifact created through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s supernatural power. The artifact should be distinguished from the ordinary biological Dragon Heart possessed by members of the True Dragon race. The Imperial Dragon Heart functions as an enormous source of Energy and a foundational component of the infrastructure supporting Victor's civilization.[1][6]
When Akasha awakened, she immediately perceived the connection and recognized the immense quantity of Energy available through it. Her first reaction was to identify the Heart as belonging to her Father, demonstrating that the connection was experienced as part of her own existence rather than merely as access to a technological power source.[6]
This connection also allowed Akasha to access the technological systems operating throughout the Empire. She could communicate with the connected infrastructure directly and process enormous quantities of information without requiring the conventional interfaces used by ordinary administrators. The Dragon Heart therefore served as an important link between Akasha's living intelligence and the larger technological and supernatural systems she was intended to coordinate.[6]

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
As the [[Elderblood Empire]] expanded its technological infrastructure, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] began preparing for a future in which its territory would extend far beyond its original worlds. The increasingly complicated requirements of Galactic administration, communications, military coordination and resource management made the limitations of ordinary administrative systems apparent. Ruby, Velnorah and Aline were involved in developing the necessary technology, while Victor recognized that the project could be improved through the creation of a genuine living intelligence whose abilities would develop alongside the Empire.[15][1]
Victor subsequently created Akasha Elderblood using his own power, Blood, Soul and Eldritch Divinity. Ruby had initially expected a technological artificial intelligence, but Victor instead constructed a genuine living Being whose supernatural foundation would allow her to function as the central intelligence of the Empire while retaining an independent identity. His decision reflected the importance he placed upon family and individual development, even when creating someone whose original purpose was closely connected to an essential Imperial function.[1]
The creation proceeded through several stages. Victor first produced a complete humanoid body, then introduced his Blood to establish the Blood Dragon foundation. He separated a fragment of his own Soul and used it to create genuine Life, before incorporating Eldritch power and assigning Akasha the responsibility of protecting his family and civilization. When he declared her name, she awakened with the distinctive violet eyes of his direct descendants and immediately recognized him as her Father.[1]
Akasha's first conversation with Victor demonstrated that she possessed far more than ordinary computational intelligence. She recognized her direct connection to the [[Dragon Heart]] and could perceive the nearly inexhaustible supply of Energy made available through that artifact. Victor then asked her to summarize the knowledge he had provided during her creation. Akasha explained that she knew the identities and basic histories of her Mothers and sisters, possessed extensive technological knowledge and understood enormous portions of supernatural history from the perspective of the Gods. However, she lacked the emotional experiences associated with those events, having received information rather than personal memories.[6]
Victor subsequently investigated her Divine nature and discovered the Eldritch Domain of Control. Akasha explained that she possessed no ordinary Divine Concept because she was not directly connected to the native Creation in the same way as conventional Gods. Instead, her supernatural authority originated from the influence of Victor's Eldritch Form and his position as a God of [[Primordial Chaos|Primal Chaos]]. The Domain included Control and related characteristics, but also possessed the contradictory inverse of Loss of Control because Victor had simultaneously intended her to exercise authority and remain free from external control.[6]
After establishing the nature of her powers, Victor introduced Akasha to Ruby, Aline and Velnorah as three of the women most closely involved in the Empire's technological progress. Akasha recognized them as her Mothers but initially displayed limited emotional attachment. Victor and the women understood that her behavior reflected the circumstances of her creation and allowed her to develop relationships through experience rather than attempting to impose immediate affection.[6]
Akasha then connected herself to the Imperial System and began examining its functions. When Aline asked how much of her processing capacity was being used, Akasha reported that the entire contemporary workload required only approximately 0.0000000002% of her available resources. The extraordinary difference between her capacity and the demands of the current Empire was deliberate: Victor intended her to remain capable of fulfilling her responsibilities even after the civilization had expanded across enormous distances and acquired far more complex systems.[6]
Her initial analysis immediately identified weaknesses in the Empire's surveillance and information-storage arrangements. Akasha recognized that numerous territories lacked comprehensive monitoring and argued that even an extremely small possibility of danger to Victor justified improved observation. She proposed expanding surveillance into areas such as Hell and the territories associated with the [[Blood God Religion]], while also recommending the construction of a highly protected database within a separate dimension.[6]
The proposed database would preserve enormous quantities of Imperial information, including records concerning exceptional historical individuals, military strategies and potentially the memories and Souls required for more efficient clone restoration. Ruby recognized the value of the proposal but also considered Akasha's intensity excessive, comparing her tendency toward continuous preparation with that of Batman. Victor responded by reminding Akasha that protecting the Empire was important, but that her own well-being and identity as his daughter remained equally significant.[6]
Akasha's incorporation into the family immediately affected the relationships among Victor's daughters. Several were jealous that she had been created entirely from their Father, while others recognized that her access to Imperial information would make concealing misbehavior far more difficult. Unlike family members who could occasionally be persuaded to overlook minor incidents, Akasha approached her responsibility toward Victor with considerable seriousness, earning the humorous designation Number One Snitch of the Father.[4]
Victor subsequently involved Akasha in the activities of his daughters, including the Dream World War Games. The exercise placed the children within an artificial environment where their powers and available resources were restricted, forcing them to develop leadership, cooperation and strategic judgment through experience. Akasha participated in the exercise and was later recognized as a mediator between the competing factions rather than simply favoring whichever group she personally preferred.[11]
The Dream World exercise was particularly valuable to Akasha because she had been born possessing enormous theoretical knowledge but comparatively little lived experience. Interacting with her sisters, observing their decisions and experiencing situations that could not be resolved solely through inherited information helped develop the social judgment necessary for her future responsibilities. This experience later complemented the knowledge of ancient supernatural beings that Victor provided to her, allowing Akasha to better understand how different individuals behaved and how an Empire containing numerous races and cultures might function.[11][2]
As Imperial military development accelerated, Akasha's processing power became increasingly valuable. Victor planned the construction of enormous warships that would originally have required separate Beings or advanced artificial intelligences to control their systems. After Akasha's creation, he recognized that she possessed enough capacity to operate the planned warships simultaneously. Nevertheless, he insisted upon retaining Operators and independent personnel, ensuring that the military would not become completely dependent upon Akasha's continued connection.[8]
The relationship between Akasha and the Imperial military also became visible through the development of subordinate artificial intelligences based upon her architecture. During the training and equipment testing of the Emperor's Soldiers, John encountered a support AI designated Akasha-001. The subsystem explained that it was based upon the main Imperial intelligence, Akasha Elderblood, and could provide combat assistance, biological analysis and other functions through the soldier's equipment. When John requested a configuration suitable for stealth operations, Akasha-001 activated Hunter Mode and modified his equipment accordingly.[16]
Akasha-001 was a subordinate system rather than Akasha herself occupying the soldier's armor. Its presence demonstrated the Empire's developing distributed architecture, in which the central living intelligence could supervise numerous lower-level artificial intelligences without personally performing every individual function.[16]
Akasha's information-processing responsibilities became particularly important as the Empire began encountering hostile civilizations and threats originating beyond its familiar territory. During the investigation of an alien species possessing characteristics associated with a parasitic Hive, the Empire's forces collected information indicating that the organism could consume planetary Energy and interfere with the development of [[World Trees]]. Akasha analyzed the available information, recognized that the matter could involve hostile activity from the Higher Sectors and restricted sensitive intelligence according to Imperial security classifications.[17]
The investigation also demonstrated Akasha's role in controlling the circulation of information. She explained the significance of the threat to Clare while restricting material that required higher authorization, promising to refine the available intelligence and provide only the information necessary for the expedition to continue. Her actions illustrated that gathering data and distributing it were separate responsibilities: even trusted Imperial commanders were not automatically entitled to every secret contained within the Empire's databases.[17]
Akasha later assisted the campaign involving Commander John and his forces. When the expedition determined that the available fleet could not overcome the strength of the planetary civilization it had encountered, Akasha independently confirmed the assessment and arranged reinforcements. She appeared through the ship's holographic system and contacted Eleanor and Rose, whose abilities and battlefield experience made them suitable leaders for the operation.[12]
Upon their arrival, Akasha transferred authority to the women while continuing to supply information and coordinate resources. She provided tactical reports directly through the Imperial communication network and identified the objectives associated with Operations Alpha, Bravo, Charlie and Delta. This demonstrated that Akasha could manage strategic information and deploy reinforcements without treating her technological access as authority to replace every experienced battlefield commander.[12]
The expanding infrastructure of the Empire also required improvements in its administrative organization. The development of World Cores allowed functions previously limited to individual cities to operate across entire planets, while the Dragon Heart supplied the extraordinary Energy necessary to sustain increasingly ambitious systems. Akasha's calculations and processing capabilities became important to understanding how such infrastructure could function efficiently while remaining connected to the wider Imperial network.[9]
Akasha's relationship with the other daughters also developed during this period. Her immediate return to administrative work after participating in the family's activities caused several sisters to complain that she was allowed to contribute directly to the Empire while they remained restricted from assuming comparable responsibilities. Anna recognized that Akasha's workload could eventually become extremely demanding and intended to monitor her well-being as Imperial territory continued expanding across galaxies.[18]
By Chapter 1113, Akasha had become the principal intelligence behind an enormous distributed system. Hundreds of thousands of subordinate artificial intelligences operated beneath the larger administrative structure she supervised, while numerous biological Operators continued handling specialized tasks. This architecture allowed the Empire to expand without forcing Akasha to personally perform every individual action, while ensuring that important information remained available to the appropriate authorities.[2]
Her responsibilities were especially significant because the Empire expected to acquire control over enormous numbers of additional worlds. Akasha's predictions indicated that the civilization would continue expanding rapidly after the Sector barrier was removed, making a highly efficient system of information processing and administrative coordination essential. Victor's original decision to create an intelligence possessing enormous unused capacity proved increasingly valuable as the Empire developed beyond the demands of its earlier planetary institutions.[2]
Akasha's importance also required unusual security arrangements. As the living Mind of the Empire, she operated from a protected sub-dimension rather than remaining permanently exposed within ordinary technological infrastructure. Her holographic manifestations allowed her to interact with individuals throughout connected Imperial territory without moving her genuine body away from the protected environment. The arrangement served both defensive and practical purposes, protecting a critical Imperial figure while allowing her to continue supervising the wider network.[2]
During one private discussion, Victor visited Akasha's protected sub-dimension and discovered that she had not yet customized the environment for personal comfort. Although she possessed enormous technological knowledge, Akasha preferred a peaceful natural environment rather than a purely mechanical one. Victor transformed the previously empty surroundings into a comfortable mansion overlooking a lake, mountains and trees, making his daughter visibly happy before they returned to discussing Imperial security.[2]
Akasha had summoned Victor because her analysis of several planetary expeditions revealed suspicious similarities among organisms encountered in different Solar Systems. She identified signs suggesting that the parasitic species discovered by Imperial forces might belong to a broader strategy employed by civilizations originating from the Higher Sectors. Rather than treating the incidents as isolated planetary abnormalities, she correlated the available information and recognized the possibility of a long-term infiltration method involving developing World Trees.[2]
Victor examined her report and began connecting the information with his broader understanding of the cosmic political situation. Akasha recognized that he was considering a conclusion not yet fully established through her own calculations and deliberately avoided interrupting him. The episode demonstrated an important limitation of her intelligence: extraordinary processing power could analyze available information, but it did not automatically grant every insight possessed by Victor or eliminate the need for judgment concerning incomplete evidence.[13]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s preparations to challenge the Higher Sectors, the responsibilities of the Imperial System expanded alongside his plans for reorganizing the civilizations of his native Sector. Victor extended his Cosmic Consciousness through the region and connected his perception with enormous numbers of [[World Trees]], allowing him to construct a strategic map containing the galaxies and other major structures within his territory. He subsequently transferred the information to Akasha, entrusting her with the responsibility of incorporating it into the Empire's administrative and strategic systems.[19]
The map provided information required for systematic Galactic administration, including the locations of World Trees, inhabited systems and territories suitable for expansion. Akasha's ability to organize enormous quantities of information allowed the Empire to convert Victor's personal cosmic perception into data usable by ordinary institutions, supporting the development of transportation routes, resource allocation and coordinated administration across distant worlds.[19]
The accelerated development that followed allowed Akasha to gain enormous additional experience. By Chapter 1148, she had become an Imperial figure whose responsibilities were considered comparable to those of a Legion Commander, although Victor explained that she did not appear particularly interested in pursuing a conventional military position. Instead, Akasha preferred continuing to administer the Empire's systems and the shadow administration associated with her responsibilities.[14]
The narration describes Akasha as controlling the shadow Empire during this discussion. The phrase indicates substantial responsibilities within the Empire's wider and less visible administrative structures, but the passage does not independently establish that Akasha owns a separate supernatural dimension formally named the Shadow Realm. Her confirmed responsibilities remain those associated with Imperial information, coordination and administration, while the precise territorial meaning of the phrase should not be expanded beyond the available context.[14]
Victor expressed considerable satisfaction with Akasha's development because she was fulfilling the purpose for which he had created her. Although Akasha possessed power and intelligence sufficient to seek a more conventional position of political or military authority, her existing responsibilities already provided enormous benefits to the Empire. Victor therefore had no reason to require her to pursue a Legion command merely to demonstrate her importance.[14]
The immense accelerated era also changed Akasha's personal position within the Elderblood family. After reaching adulthood, she was counted among the daughters who entered marital relationships with Victor. Chapter 1155 explicitly includes Akasha alongside Ophis, [[Nero]], [[Metis]] and Melida among the daughters who had become Victor's wives, reflecting the unusual familial arrangements established after enormous periods of supernatural development.[5]
During the later preparations surrounding Victor's ritual and his investigation of hostile influences from the Higher Sectors, Akasha continued exercising direct responsibility over sensitive Imperial information. When Victor discovered evidence concerning a foreign God whose influence threatened the integrity of his territory, he summoned Akasha into a private meeting and instructed her to classify the resulting intelligence at security level G-7.[20]
Victor specifically instructed Akasha to keep the material private rather than immediately incorporating it into the ordinary System. Access was initially restricted to adult members of the Imperial Family, demonstrating that even information classified at the highest levels could be subject to additional compartmentalization. Akasha accepted the instruction and carried it out according to Victor's authority.[20]
Akasha subsequently participated in the gathering of Victor's daughters during the incident involving Velina. She appeared alongside Metis and Melida after numerous other Princesses had assembled. The narration identified her as the brain of the Empire, emphasizing her extraordinary intelligence and ability to understand situations that confused several of her sisters. She quickly recognized the motives and circumstances surrounding the gathering rather than being deceived by the explanations presented.[3]
Victor later gathered several daughters to explain the nature of dimensions, Reality and the differences between ordinary supernatural constructs and more fundamental cosmic structures. Akasha participated in the lessons, observing demonstrations in which Victor created simulated environments and explained the conceptual foundations necessary for the stable existence of a Universe.[21][7]
During the demonstration of an Eldritch galaxy, Akasha was identified as one of the daughters who did not experience the same degree of discomfort as ordinary supernatural beings exposed to its unusual characteristics. Her compatibility originated from the Eldritch Divinity Victor had incorporated into her existence during her creation. This distinguished her from daughters possessing different supernatural foundations, while also confirming that her Eldritch nature remained an important part of her existence after centuries of development.[7]
As the Empire prepared to establish Mars as its new capital, Akasha's responsibilities became especially important. The transformation required the coordination of numerous Imperial systems and the communication of major changes to populations distributed throughout hundreds of worlds. Violet prepared a proclamation intended to reach the entire civilization, and Akasha personally supervised the transmission.[10]
When the Empress was ready to begin, Akasha activated the broadcast and synchronized its distribution through the Empire's communication infrastructure. Enormous holographic projections of Violet appeared across metropolitan centers and distant Galactic colonies, allowing citizens separated by extraordinary distances to witness the announcement simultaneously. The operation demonstrated that Akasha's network had developed far beyond the relatively limited administrative systems she first encountered at her creation.[10]
The establishment of the mature World Tree Network produced another major improvement. Akasha reported that the new infrastructure was directly connected to the Imperial [[Dragon Heart]] and had increased Galactic communication efficiency by approximately one thousand percent. When Violet asked how long information from the most distant connected galaxies would require to reach the Imperial center, Akasha answered that it would arrive immediately.[10]
Akasha also recognized the security implications of the new network. Because the World Trees were fundamentally connected to the larger [[The Universal Tree|Universal World Tree]] system, information could theoretically interact with structures beyond the Empire's immediate territory. However, Victor's presence interfered with that external communication, preventing information from casually escaping while the Trees remained within his protected Reality. Violet subsequently considered additional safeguards involving Eldritch Runes for situations in which connected Trees might move beyond their current protection.[10]
The discussion demonstrated that Akasha's responsibilities included more than ensuring communications operated quickly. She also needed to recognize the risks created when previously isolated networks became connected to greater supernatural structures. Her awareness of the relationship between the Dragon Heart, World Trees and the Universal network reflected the sophisticated understanding she had developed through centuries of Imperial administration.[10]
Akasha's intelligence and monitoring capabilities continued proving useful during later investigations. In Chapter 1189, unusual activity involving Ryujin attracted the attention of Imperial authorities. Akasha recognized the developing situation before it progressed through the ordinary hierarchy, informed Violet and summoned Ruby while Victor's Heralds observed the individual involved. The resulting information reached the appropriate Imperial authorities in less than a second, demonstrating the extraordinary response speed provided by the mature network centered upon Mars.[22]
The incident illustrated the distinction between Akasha's supernatural intelligence and the larger communication infrastructure supporting her. Her ability to recognize a suspicious development depended upon information obtained through Imperial systems and operatives, while the exceptional speed of the response reflected the advanced communication network established through the Dragon Heart, World Trees and other Imperial technology.[22]
By the later Imperial era, Akasha had therefore fulfilled the purpose Victor established during her creation on a scale far greater than the Empire originally required. She remained a living daughter with personal emotions and independent judgment, while the systems she administered connected military forces, artificial intelligences, World Trees, databases and communication infrastructure throughout enormous regions of space.[1][2][10]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Akasha's Father, Creator, supernatural progenitor and, following her adulthood, husband. Their relationship is unusual even among the members of Clan Elderblood because Akasha was created entirely from Victor's own existence, without a conventional biological mother or an independently supplied Soul.[1][5]
Victor constructed Akasha's body, introduced his Blood to establish her Draconic physiology, provided a fragment of his Soul to create genuine Life, and incorporated his Eldritch Divinity into her supernatural foundation. Every major aspect of her original existence therefore originated directly from him, establishing an unusually intimate connection between creator and creation.[1]
Despite possessing the power to create an absolutely obedient servant, Victor deliberately gave Akasha an independent ego and free will. He wanted her to develop personal experiences, relationships and interests rather than functioning as a machine whose behavior was limited to predetermined instructions. His only absolute safeguard concerned the superiority of his own direct [[Will Solace|Will]], which he considered necessary because Akasha would eventually possess access to the infrastructure upon which his entire civilization depended.[1]
Akasha immediately developed an intense attachment to him. His affection and approval held enormous importance to her, and she approached her responsibility for protecting the Empire as a personal obligation to the individual who had given her Life. She understood that Victor possessed extraordinary power but also recognized that his family represented one of his greatest emotional vulnerabilities, making their safety a central priority.[6]
Victor reciprocated this affection through behavior that emphasized her identity as his daughter rather than merely an Imperial administrator. He reminded her that her own happiness was important, encouraged her to develop relationships with her Mothers and sisters, and personally transformed her protected residence into a comfortable natural environment when he discovered that she had neglected her own preferences because of work.[6][2]
Their relationship retained its affectionate character throughout the accelerated Imperial era. Even after Akasha became responsible for coordinating enormous portions of the Empire's infrastructure, she continued enjoying Victor's attention and occasionally displayed the same excitement or embarrassment as her sisters when he demonstrated extraordinary abilities.[2][7]
By Chapter 1155, after reaching adulthood, Akasha was explicitly counted among Victor's wives. Their later marriage did not erase the original circumstances of her creation, and both relationships remained part of her unusual position within the Elderblood family.[5]

#### Ruby Elderblood Scarlett
[[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] was present during Akasha's creation and originally expected [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to participate in the development of a sophisticated artificial intelligence. Instead, Victor transformed the project into the creation of a genuine living daughter possessing an Eldritch supernatural foundation.[1]
Ruby quickly recognized both the advantages and complications of Akasha's abilities. Although the newly created intelligence could process extraordinary quantities of information and proposed useful improvements to Imperial infrastructure, her desire for constant surveillance and extensive contingency planning made Ruby concerned about how far she might take those ideas without guidance.[6]
Ruby compared Akasha's mentality to Batman because of her emphasis upon monitoring potential threats and preparing countermeasures for situations that had not yet occurred. She also recognized the practical value of Akasha's proposal to establish a protected database dimension, acknowledging that the idea could considerably improve information security and support future military planning.[6]
Their relationship subsequently developed within the wider family and Imperial administration, particularly because Ruby remained one of the Empire's principal technological researchers while Akasha became its central information and coordination intelligence.[1][6]

#### Aline Elderblood Valefar
[[Aline Elderblood Valefar]] was among the women present when Akasha awakened. She took a practical approach to the newly created intelligence, asking whether Akasha could access the Imperial systems and requesting information concerning her processing capacity.[6]
Akasha's response demonstrated that the existing Empire required an almost negligible fraction of her available resources. The exchange established the technical foundation of their relationship, with Aline recognizing the enormous administrative advantages provided by Akasha's capabilities.[6]
Aline's involvement in Imperial development also placed her among the women whose work would regularly interact with the systems Akasha supervised. Although Akasha initially possessed limited emotional interest in her Mothers, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] encouraged the relationships to develop naturally as she acquired personal experience.[6]

#### Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood
Velnorah was one of the women responsible for the technological development that made Akasha's creation necessary. Her work on Imperial infrastructure, advanced technology and the [[Dragon Heart]] provided important foundations for the civilization-scale systems Akasha would eventually coordinate.[1]
Velnorah was present during Akasha's awakening and was introduced as one of her Mothers. The two subsequently became connected through their responsibilities for the Empire's technological systems, with Velnorah developing increasingly sophisticated infrastructure and Akasha providing the processing and coordination capabilities required to operate it efficiently.[6]
Their work became particularly important as the Empire transitioned from localized systems toward planetary and Galactic infrastructure. World Cores, the Dragon Heart and the growing network of Imperial technologies created increasingly complex information requirements that Akasha's abilities were specifically designed to handle.[9][2]

#### Anna Elderblood
[[Anna Elderblood]] is one of Akasha's Mothers and an important figure in the development and protection of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters. Anna recognized that Akasha's responsibilities were considerably greater than those normally assigned to a newly created member of the family, and expressed concern that her workload could eventually become excessive as the Empire expanded.[18]
Although Akasha had been created to perform administrative functions, Anna did not consider that purpose sufficient reason to neglect her well-being. She understood that the Empire's eventual expansion across galaxies would create increasingly demanding responsibilities and intended to remain attentive to Akasha's condition as those demands increased.[18]
Their relationship demonstrates that Akasha's Mothers recognized her as a daughter requiring care rather than merely a convenient administrative system, even when her extraordinary abilities made it easy to underestimate the demands placed upon her.[18]

#### Her Sisters
Akasha possesses numerous sisters through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s enormous family. Her relationships with them are affected by both her unusual origin and her responsibilities as the central intelligence of the Empire.[4]
Because Akasha was created entirely from Victor, several sisters initially regarded her as possessing an unfair advantage in their competition for his affection. Unlike most of the younger Elderblood generation, she did not require a second biological parent, making her direct relationship with Victor appear particularly special.[4]
Her access to information created another source of irritation. Akasha was difficult to deceive and unwilling to conceal serious misconduct from her Father, leading her sisters to identify her as an exceptionally troublesome person to have around when they wished to avoid parental attention.[4]
Nevertheless, Akasha remained a genuine member of the younger generation. She participated in Victor's Dream World exercises, helped mediate disputes and continued developing personal relationships through the same family activities experienced by her sisters.[11]
By the later Imperial era, she was frequently associated with other exceptionally intelligent daughters such as [[Metis]] and Melida, whose abilities allowed them to understand complicated situations that confused several of their siblings.[3]

#### Metis Elderblood
[[Metis]] Elderblood is one of Akasha's sisters and another member of the younger generation whose intelligence is considered extraordinary even by the standards of Clan Elderblood.[14]
During the later Imperial era, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Jeanne discussed the development of both women and recognized that they possessed the capabilities necessary to assume important responsibilities within the Empire. However, their individual interests differed. Akasha preferred the management of Imperial systems rather than pursuing a conventional military command, while Metis possessed her own abilities and interests associated with Wisdom.[14]

#### Melida Charlotte Elderblood

Their presence alongside [[Metis]] during later family gatherings emphasizes the considerable variation among Victor's created daughters despite their shared relationship with him.[3]

### Role in the Elderblood Empire
Akasha's position within the [[Elderblood Empire]] is best understood as that of a central intelligence coordinating a distributed civilization rather than an absolute sovereign independently ruling every Imperial institution. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his Empresses determine the major political direction of the civilization, while military commanders, administrators, scientists and other specialized personnel continue performing their responsibilities. Akasha connects these institutions through the collection, analysis, preservation and transmission of information, ensuring that the necessary systems and personnel can cooperate efficiently across enormous distances.[8][12][2]

#### Imperial System
Akasha possesses a direct connection to the central infrastructure of the Empire through the [[Dragon Heart]]. Her initial access allowed her to examine existing systems almost immediately after birth, identifying weaknesses in surveillance and data organization while reporting that the entire contemporary workload required only approximately 0.0000000002% of her available processing capacity.[6]
As the Empire developed, Akasha became the central intelligence connecting increasingly sophisticated administrative networks. Hundreds of thousands of subordinate artificial intelligences and numerous biological Operators continued handling specialized tasks, while Akasha supervised the larger structure and coordinated the movement of information between its different components.[2]
This arrangement provided considerable scalability. The Empire could expand into new worlds and establish additional institutions without requiring Akasha to personally replace every administrator. Instead, new systems could be connected to the larger information network while retaining local personnel and specialized artificial intelligences capable of performing their functions independently.[2]

#### Military Coordination
Akasha possesses sufficient processing capacity to operate enormous numbers of Imperial warships simultaneously. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] recognized this capability during the preparations for the creation of the Emperor's Soldiers, observing that the new intelligence eliminated the need for every vessel to possess a completely independent high-level controller.[8]
Nevertheless, the Empire retained Operators, soldiers and battlefield commanders. This decision ensured that military forces would remain functional even if Akasha became unavailable or communication with the central network was interrupted.[8]
Akasha's role during active operations involves receiving tactical information, evaluating the strength of enemy forces, requesting reinforcements and transferring command to appropriate authorities. During the campaign involving Commander John, she determined that the available fleet was insufficient and arranged the arrival of Eleanor and Rose, subsequently supporting their command through the distribution of tactical reports.[12]

#### Artificial Intelligence Network
Akasha supervises a distributed network containing enormous numbers of subordinate artificial intelligences. These systems perform lower-level functions and provide specialized assistance to soldiers, administrators and technical personnel.[2]
One example is Akasha-001, an AI associated with the equipment of the Emperor's Soldiers. The subsystem explicitly identifies itself as being based upon Akasha Elderblood rather than being Akasha's own complete consciousness. It can provide combat assistance, monitor biological information and adjust equipment configurations according to authorized requests.[16]
This distinction is important because the existence of subordinate artificial intelligences does not mean Akasha personally manifests a separate Soul or independent body within every machine. The network is a distributed administrative structure in which numerous specialized systems operate beneath a central living intelligence.[16][2]

Akasha is responsible for important aspects of the Empire's information security, including the classification of sensitive intelligence and its distribution to authorized individuals.[17]
During the investigation of the parasitic Hive species, Akasha identified information that might indicate hostile activity originating from the Higher Sectors and restricted it through the Imperial security classification system. #### World Tree Communications
The mature World Tree Network connected numerous distant Imperial locations through a supernatural infrastructure linked directly to the [[Dragon Heart]]. Akasha supervised the resulting communications arrangements and reported a substantial increase in Galactic communication efficiency.[10]

#### Protected Residence
Akasha operates from a protected sub-dimension whose purpose includes safeguarding the living intelligence connected to the Imperial System. Her remote holograms allow her to communicate and administer distant systems without exposing her genuine body to ordinary military or political threats.[2]

The existence of this protected dimension is confirmed, but its precise position within the hierarchy of supernatural spaces has not been established in the verified passages. It should therefore not automatically be classified as existing beyond every dimensional structure accessible to other members of the family.

#### Eldritch Domain of Control
Control is Akasha's principal supernatural Domain. It encompasses related ideas including Monitoring, Oversight and Supervision, while also possessing the contradictory characteristic of Loss of Control. The inverse emerged because [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] wanted Akasha to control important systems while remaining personally free from outside control.[6]
- Conceptual Control: Akasha possesses an Eldritch Domain associated with the principle of Control.[6]
- Monitoring: Monitoring is explicitly identified among the related characteristics originally intended to fall under her Domain.[6]
- Oversight: Oversight is explicitly identified as another characteristic associated with her conceptual authority.[6]
- Supervision: Akasha's Domain includes the characteristics associated with supervising and regulating activities within the effective scope of her powers.[6]
- Loss of Control: Her Eldritch Domain includes the inverse of Control. Akasha explains that her presence can cause others to lose control.[6]
- Contradictory Conceptual Nature: The coexistence of Control and its opposite reflects the unusual nature of Eldritch powers and the contradictory intentions involved in Akasha's creation.[6]
- Divine-Eldritch Authority: Akasha's Control originates from her intrinsic supernatural existence rather than exclusively from technological access to Imperial systems.[6]
The exact maximum offensive scope of Control has not been established. Akasha has not been shown simply overriding every Being, Law, Divine Concept or independent civilization through the Domain without restrictions. Its confirmed nature includes Control and Loss of Control, while her practical Imperial capabilities are demonstrated through the networks and institutions connected to her.

### Limitations
- [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Direct [[Will Solace|Will]]: Akasha's creation includes an explicit restriction preventing her from superseding Victor's direct Will.[1]
- Limited Initial Experience: Akasha awakened with enormous inherited knowledge but lacked the personal memories and emotional experience normally acquired through life.[6]
- Incomplete Information: Akasha's processing ability cannot automatically provide knowledge that has never been observed, recorded or transmitted to her. Victor can reach conclusions that she has not yet inferred.[13]
- Network Dependence: Akasha's remote manifestations and access to distant infrastructure depend upon appropriate technological or supernatural connections.[2]
- Authority Boundaries: Her administrative access applies to systems connected to or placed under her authority, rather than automatically granting ownership of every independent machine or institution throughout Creation.
- Unconfirmed Absolute Immunities: Victor's creation safeguard does not establish immunity to every conceivable mental, spiritual, conceptual or Eldritch attack without explicit demonstrations.
- Unrevealed Limits of Control: Akasha's Domain includes Control and Loss of Control, but the upper limits of its independent offensive and defensive applications have not been demonstrated.[6]
- Distinct from a Fully Fledged Outer God: Akasha possesses an Eldritch Goddess existence created using Victor's Divinity, but the reviewed narrative does not explicitly classify her as possessing the same complete Outer God nature as Victor or every other fully Eldritch member of the family.[7]
- Distributed Infrastructure: The Imperial System retains subordinate artificial intelligences, Operators and military crews rather than depending exclusively upon Akasha for every function.[8][2]
