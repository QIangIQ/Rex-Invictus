Hela Elderblood, originally known as Hela or Hel, is an ancient Goddess formerly belonging to the [[Norse Pantheon]], the former ruler of Helheim, the daughter of [[Loki]] and [[Angrboða]], and the sister of [[Fenrir]] and [[Jormungandr]]. She was one of the principal architects of Ragnarök, having spent millennia preparing the destruction of Odin's rule after the All-Father imprisoned her and persecuted Loki's children because of the prophecy surrounding the end of [[Asgard]].[6][7]
Although originally classified as a Third Generation Goddess, Hela became considerably more dangerous than her generation alone suggested. Helheim gave her enormous periods in which to gather the dead, negotiate with powerful Souls, form alliances outside Asgard and prepare for the exact conflict Odin believed her imprisonment would prevent. Her eventual overthrow of the Norse God-King was consequently the culmination of millennia of preparation rather than an impulsive rebellion.[8][9]
Following Ragnarök, Hela increasingly associated with [[Victor Elderblood]] and the civilization forming around him. Their initially pragmatic relationship gradually developed into genuine affection, and Hela eventually chose to become one of Victor's wives after he specifically refused to accept a marriage agreement she had signed while being pressured by the enthusiasm of the Elderblood women.[2] She subsequently became a Blood Dragon Goddess, joined the Elderblood family permanently and later gave birth to [[Helvarya Elderblood]], a daughter whose birth revealed an extraordinary connection to END.[3][4]

### Physical Appearance

#### God
Hela is an exceptionally beautiful woman with fair skin, vivid emerald-green eyes and naturally long black hair. Her appearance during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s first direct encounters with her was considerably different from her healthy state because she had sacrificed approximately 99% of her vitality as part of her preparations involving Nidhoggr, Ragnarök and END. The loss of vitality caused her complexion to become unnaturally pale and turned her hair completely white, giving her an almost corpse-like appearance despite remaining alive and conscious.[10]
Hela after Victor restored her vitality.
Victor later restored the vitality Hela had sacrificed, reversing the physical deterioration almost immediately. Her hair returned to its natural black coloration, her skin regained a healthier appearance and her emerald eyes became noticeably more vibrant. The restoration did not simply make her appear younger; it returned Hela to the physical condition necessary for her to personally participate in the conflict she had spent millennia preparing.[10]
Hela commonly wore a pale Divine robe during her time in Helheim. When she entered serious combat during Ragnarök, the robe transformed into full battle attire while the [[Staff of Death and End]] assumed the form of an enormous black great axe covered in luminous green markings, giving Hela an appearance considerably more martial than the isolated [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] ruler Victor had originally encountered.[11]

#### Dragon Goddess
After becoming one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wives, Hela underwent the same fundamental Draconic evolution that incorporated several members of his family into the Blood Dragon lineage. Her humanoid appearance remained recognizably Hela, retaining her black hair, emerald eyes and mature features, but the supernatural foundation of her body changed into that of a genuine True Dragon while preserving her previous Divine nature.[3]
Her transformation also intensified several Draconic instincts and emotional tendencies. Hela became noticeably more openly affectionate and possessive toward Victor, frequently remaining physically close to him and displaying considerably less restraint than during their early political interactions. Even after nearly two thousand subjective years of development, she was still regarded as comparatively immature as a Dragon Goddess, indicating that her final Draconic appearance and state had not yet been fully established.[3]
The novel has not yet provided a sufficiently complete physical description of Hela's full True Dragon form to establish its exact anatomy, size or coloration.

### Personality
Hela is exceptionally patient, intelligent, calculating and vindictive. Her defining characteristic for much of her life was not simply hatred toward [[Odin]], but her ability to preserve that hatred across immense periods without wasting the opportunity for revenge through premature action. Rather than repeatedly attempting to escape Helheim through direct force, she used the very prison intended to suppress her as a place to develop, gather the dead, accumulate resources and quietly construct the conditions required for Odin's eventual downfall.[6][8]
Her resentment toward Odin is inseparable from her view of prophecy and personal responsibility. Hela despises the logic by which Odin persecuted [[Loki]]'s children for crimes that had not yet occurred merely because Ragnarök predicted that they would become his enemies. From her perspective, Odin created the very future he feared: imprisonment, persecution and the suffering inflicted upon [[Fenrir]], [[Jormungandr]] and herself gave them every reason to deliberately destroy the regime that had treated them as future criminals. She took particular satisfaction in confronting Odin with this contradiction after his defeat.[7]
Hela possesses a pronounced sadistic streak toward enemies she considers personally responsible for harming her family. Her objective during Ragnarök was never limited to killing Odin efficiently. She wanted him to understand that his control was disappearing piece by piece, forcing him to witness betrayal, the collapse of his armies and the destruction of people he cared about before finally confronting him directly. Her treatment of [[Vali]] and the deliberate use of Nidhoggr against [[Frigg]] demonstrated that Hela can be extraordinarily cruel when she believes the target deserves retaliation.[11][7]
This cruelty is not indiscriminate. Hela has little interest in destroying unrelated people merely because they happen to belong to the same civilization as her enemy. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] requested that Elves, Dwarves, Ice Giants and Fire Giants be excluded from her revenge because they were valuable populations who had not personally participated in the persecution of Loki's children, Hela accepted the condition without significant resistance. After Victor restored her vitality, she regarded honoring their agreement as a natural obligation rather than searching for a technicality that would allow her to betray him.[10]
Hela is similarly pragmatic toward political authority. For all the time she spent destroying Odin's control of [[Asgard]], she possessed surprisingly little interest in replacing him as another conventional ruler of the [[Norse Pantheon]]. Odin's defeat gave her what she had actually wanted—revenge, freedom and control over the resources she considered personally important. Once those objectives were complete, she removed the treasures, prisoners and assets she wished to retain and treated the remaining Pantheon as something that could simply be sold rather than as a sacred throne she needed to inherit.[12][13]
Beneath her confidence and calculated cruelty, Hela possesses substantial emotional sensitivity concerning family, abandonment and trust. Loki's failure to meaningfully protect his children left a permanent wound in her opinion of him, while Fenrir and Jormungandr became the members of her original family toward whom she retained the greatest loyalty. Victor gained Hela's trust largely because he repeatedly demonstrated that he would not exploit moments when she was vulnerable: he restored her health without demanding servitude, respected the boundaries of their agreement, helped strengthen her against dangers she did not understand and later refused to accept a marriage that might not have represented her completely independent decision.[10][14][2]
Life within the Elderblood family gradually brought out a considerably warmer side of Hela. After becoming a wife and mother, she grew comfortable participating in the family's chaotic domestic life rather than remaining defined entirely by Helheim and Ragnarök. Her affection for Victor became much more direct, and she developed genuine authority over her daughter instead of leaving Helvarya's upbringing to the other women of the family. When Hela, [[Ariel Elderblood]], [[Kali Elderblood]] and [[Dun Scaith Elderblood]] were casually compared to [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]]'s detached parenting, Hela immediately objected and the narration explicitly confirmed that the four actively participated in raising their children.[15]
Hela nevertheless retains the caution of someone who survived for eons by preparing before acting. Her knowledge of Death and abnormal cosmic environments does not make her assume that every unfamiliar phenomenon falls within her expertise. Encounters with Beings originating from dead cosmic regions taught her that understanding END did not automatically mean understanding every Eldritch entity or every form of supernatural corruption, and she was willing to ask Victor for additional protection rather than allowing pride to create an avoidable weakness.[16][14]

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Hela's direct role remained hidden during most of the Demonic War, but her influence had already begun affecting the larger supernatural balance. Through her cooperation with Nidhoggr, the Norse dimensional structure became entangled with events surrounding the destruction of the [[Bifrost]] and the destabilization of Helheim. The damage contributed to [[Diablo]] losing the control he had previously attempted to exert over the Norse [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]], demonstrating that Hela's confinement never made her politically irrelevant; she had continued building connections and pursuing plans beyond the boundaries [[Odin]] intended to impose upon her.[17]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
By the time [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction began investigating the coming Ragnarök, Hela had already spent millennia converting her imprisonment into preparation. [[Odin]] had confined her within Helheim because of the Ragnarök prophecy and had similarly treated [[Fenrir]] and [[Jormungandr]] as future threats, while [[Loki]]'s unwillingness or inability to meaningfully oppose the persecution destroyed much of Hela's respect for her father. Helheim ironically became one of the worst places Odin could have chosen to contain her: Hela was a Goddess with an extraordinary affinity toward Death, and the [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] continuously provided the environment, Souls and spiritual resources most suited to her development.[18][6]
Over the enormous period of her confinement, Hela accumulated far more than the ordinary dead belonging to the Norse system. She made arrangements with Souls and historical figures originating from other cultures, including [[Leonidas I]], [[Vlad III Tepes]] and [[Julius Caesar]], and preserved military forces associated with them for the conflict she knew would eventually arrive. She also developed external relationships that Odin failed to account for, most importantly her arrangement with Nidhoggr and her business-like dealings with unusual creatures originating from regions connected to genuine cosmic Death.[6][8][9]
Her preparations involving Nidhoggr and END demanded an enormous personal price. Hela sacrificed approximately 99% of her vitality, leaving her pale, white-haired and physically depleted shortly before the conflict she had spent so long preparing. The sacrifice was calculated rather than accidental; Hela was willing to temporarily reduce her personal condition because her plan depended upon resources far larger than her individual combat strength.[10]
Victor eventually entered Helheim while investigating the disturbances surrounding Ragnarök and immediately irritated Hela by refusing to treat her like a terrifying ruler. He casually occupied her preferred seat, spoke to her without reverence and evaluated her plans as another political and cosmic problem rather than something before which he needed to kneel. Hela initially responded with hostility, but their objectives did not actually conflict. Victor had no desire to save Odin, and Hela did not object when he requested that unrelated races such as the Elves, Dwarves, Ice Giants and Fire Giants be excluded from her revenge.[6][10]
The relationship changed significantly when Victor restored the vitality Hela had sacrificed. He did not demand obedience or ownership in exchange, and the restoration returned Hela to a condition where she could personally participate in Ragnarök rather than remaining a weakened strategist commanding events from behind the battlefield. The gesture established the foundation of a relationship that gradually became much more personal after the war.[10]
When Ragnarök finally began, Hela revealed how extensively Odin had underestimated her. The forces hidden within Helheim included approximately 30,000 Spartans associated with Leonidas, large Wallachian forces connected to Vlad III Tepes, Roman soldiers under Julius Caesar and numerous other dead or contracted entities. Fenrir, Jormungandr and Nidhoggr acted alongside the larger conspiracy, while Hela's preparations inside [[Asgard]] ensured that Odin could not treat the conflict as a simple external invasion.[8]
The attack was deliberately structured to destroy Odin psychologically as well as militarily. Nidhoggr was used against [[Frigg]], [[Vali]]'s imprisonment and prolonged torture were revealed, and Hela waited until Odin's situation had deteriorated before exposing [[Idun]] as one of the hidden collaborators undermining him from within. Idun's betrayal had been cultivated from her resentment surrounding the death of her brother and Odin's response to the incident, allowing Hela to transform an existing grievance into one of Ragnarök's most damaging internal fractures.[11][7]
By the time Hela directly confronted Odin, the All-Father's advantages as an older God-King had already been systematically dismantled. His family had been attacked, allies had betrayed him, his armies had been overwhelmed, his body was mutilated and Gungnir was broken beside him. Hela took particular satisfaction in explaining that his attempt to avoid prophecy had produced the exact conditions required for the prophecy to become reality. Her victory therefore represented far more than a direct comparison of personal power: Hela had spent millennia creating a war in which Odin would be forced to fight after nearly every political, emotional and strategic advantage had already been removed.[7]
Once Ragnarök was complete, Hela discovered that she had no particular desire to become another Odin. She retained the Gods, prisoners, artifacts and resources she personally wanted, then announced that the remainder of the defeated [[Norse Pantheon]] would effectively be auctioned to other major powers. A defeated [[Thor]] was presented as undeniable evidence that the old regime had fallen, while Hela carefully controlled how much information about the true conflict was revealed so potential rivals could not reconstruct every resource or alliance she had used.[12]
The negotiations surrounding the former Norse territories eventually concluded without immediately beginning another Divine war. Hela's growing connection to Victor's household became increasingly visible during the aftermath; she socialized with [[Violet Elderblood Snow]], [[Amaterasu]] Elderblood, Haruna and the other women surrounding Victor, gradually moving from an external ally defined by Ragnarök into someone increasingly comfortable inside the family and civilization Victor was building.[13][19]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
After Ragnarök, Hela's knowledge of Death and the regions beyond ordinary inhabited space became increasingly relevant to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s expanding interests. She explained that entire galaxies could reach a genuine End, leaving behind regions where the ordinary conditions associated with living Creation no longer functioned normally. These dead regions were largely ignored by conventional civilizations, yet they contained unusual beings and phenomena that Hela had encountered through her long association with Death and END.[16]
Hela guided Victor toward these regions and demonstrated that even her immense experience had limits. Beings living beyond conventional cosmic environments could interfere with passages she opened and did not necessarily obey the assumptions familiar to ordinary Gods. The encounter convinced Hela that END compatibility alone was insufficient protection against every form of Eldritch influence, and she accepted Victor's offer of deeper safeguards rather than pretending that age or pride made her invulnerable.[16][14]
As Victor's Empire developed, Hela's relationship with Death and END continued evolving alongside her place in his family. By Chapter 1093 she was explicitly identified as the Goddess of Death and The End, reflecting how thoroughly a force she had once accessed through unusual compatibility had become incorporated into her Divine identity.[1]
Hela's romantic relationship with Victor was formalized only after an unusual intervention from Violet. Violet gathered Hela alongside women such as Kali, [[Dún Scáith|Dun Scaith]] and Albedo and aggressively encouraged them to stop circling around Victor without resolving their feelings. Hela signed the resulting marriage agreement, but Victor immediately destroyed it because he did not want an eternal relationship founded on social pressure, even pressure originating from his own wives. He required Hela to reconsider independently and decide whether she genuinely wanted him rather than simply following the momentum created by Violet.[2]
Hela ultimately chose Victor voluntarily. During the same period, it was discovered that her prolonged interaction with entities from dead cosmic regions had altered her future potential enough that she might eventually develop into an Eldritch Goddess, adding another possible path beyond her original Norse Divine identity.[2]
After fully entering Victor's family, Hela evolved into a Blood Dragon Goddess. She and Victor subsequently spent nearly two thousand subjective years together within accelerated Space-Time, giving Hela an enormous period in which to adapt to her new Draconic existence and develop their relationship away from the political circumstances that had originally brought them together.[3]
During this period Hela became pregnant. Initial observation suggested that she carried twins because two separate Soul-like presences could be detected, and Victor himself accepted the interpretation at the time. The unusual pregnancy remained unresolved when the family moved into the next phase of the Empire's development.[3]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Hela entered the age of the [[Elderblood Empire]] not merely as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wife but as one of the ancient supernatural figures incorporated into its ruling family. Her former identity as the isolated ruler of Helheim became increasingly distant as she participated in a civilization spanning worlds and eventually vast regions of a galaxy. Creatures related to those she had once encountered in dead cosmic regions were studied, modified and incorporated into the Empire's defensive infrastructure, with some hidden inside astronomical structures as part of the immense preparations for enemies beyond the Sector.[20]
Her connection to END made Hela particularly important to one of the Empire's most dangerous strategic projects. Polaris was developed as a Galaxy-[[Destroyer]]-class Divine spacecraft whose systems incorporated Reality, Space, Time, Destruction, Eldritch-compatible technology and, most dangerously, a weapon capable of channeling END through suitable sources such as [[Fenrir]], [[Typhon]] and [[Jormungandr]]. The final attack was so destructive that the cannon itself could not survive its own discharge, forcing Imperial engineers to rely on replaceable firing systems rather than attempting to construct a permanently indestructible barrel.[5]
Victor assigned Polaris to Hela and instructed her to master the vessel. By this stage Hela's relationship with END had progressed far beyond the resistance she possessed during Ragnarök; during the discussion surrounding the weapon, Hela and Victor were identified as the only people present who were immune to END. The assignment therefore reflected both Hela's ancient association with the phenomenon and her modern position as a trusted Imperial figure capable of operating technology too dangerous for most of the family to approach casually.[5]
Hela also became firmly established as a mother within the Elderblood household. During the incident surrounding Albedo's neglect of family responsibilities, Naty accidentally grouped Hela, Ariel, Kali and [[Dún Scáith|Dun Scaith]] together with Albedo. Hela immediately rejected the comparison, and the narration clarified that the four women possessed genuine authority over their daughters and actively participated in their upbringing rather than isolating themselves from domestic responsibilities.[15]
The mystery surrounding Hela's supposed twins was finally resolved in Chapter 1177. Victor recalled that two Soul-like presences had genuinely been detectable during Hela's pregnancy and that the presences later merged into a single developing child. Further examination revealed that the second presence had never been another living daughter: it was END itself, preparing an ideal host within the developing child.[4]
The resulting daughter was [[Helvarya Elderblood]], born with an intrinsic connection to both Death and END. Hela therefore has one confirmed daughter from that pregnancy rather than twins, and the earlier assumption of an unnamed second daughter is obsolete. Helvarya's birth also represented the culmination of a process stretching back through Hela's entire life: a woman who had once merely survived contact with END eventually became the mother of a child whom the phenomenon itself had participated in preparing.[4]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
Hela first encountered [[Victor Elderblood]] as an outsider investigating the approaching Ragnarök. Their personalities immediately clashed because Victor refused to display the fear or reverence Hela expected from visitors to Helheim; he treated her as another powerful ruler whose objectives needed to be understood rather than an untouchable Goddess before whom he should submit. Hela found his behavior irritating, but the lack of fear also distinguished him sharply from most people she had dealt with throughout her imprisonment.[6]
Their first relationship was political. Victor had no interest in saving [[Odin]], while Hela had no reason to reject Victor's request that civilizations uninvolved in her family's persecution be spared. The relationship became more personal when Victor restored the enormous quantity of vitality Hela had sacrificed without using the favor to demand obedience. For a Goddess whose life had been shaped by betrayal, imprisonment and family members failing to protect one another, Victor's willingness to help without turning the act into ownership carried substantial weight.[10]
Their trust continued growing after Ragnarök. Hela exposed Victor to knowledge concerning dead cosmic regions, while Victor exposed weaknesses in Hela's defenses and then helped her correct them rather than exploiting what he discovered. The clearest demonstration of his attitude came after Violet pressured Hela into signing a marriage agreement: Victor destroyed it and required Hela to decide again without outside momentum because he refused to treat an uncertain agreement as sufficient for an eternal relationship.[14][2]
Hela ultimately chose Victor voluntarily and became one of his wives. Their relationship developed considerably during the nearly two thousand subjective years they later spent together, and Hela became increasingly affectionate, physically demonstrative and comfortable with the wider Elderblood family. Their daughter, [[Helvarya Elderblood]], was eventually born from the relationship.[3][4]

#### Helvarya Elderblood
[[Helvarya Elderblood]] is Hela and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s biological daughter and Hela's only currently confirmed child.[4] During pregnancy, two Soul-like presences were detected, leading both the family and Victor to believe that Hela was carrying twins. The presences later merged, and investigation after Helvarya's birth revealed that the supposed second child had actually been END itself preparing a compatible host.
Helvarya was consequently born with a direct relationship to Death and END, making the circumstances of her birth unusual even by the standards of Victor's children. Hela remains actively involved in her upbringing and is explicitly established as possessing real parental authority within the family rather than leaving responsibility entirely to Victor, the other mothers or household attendants.[15][4]

#### Fenrir
[[Fenrir]] is one of the members of Hela's original family toward whom she possesses the strongest attachment. Both were persecuted because [[Odin]] feared the events described by the Ragnarök prophecy, and Hela's hatred toward [[Asgard]] cannot be separated from what was done to her brothers as well as what was done to herself.
Fenrir eventually answered the call to Ragnarök and participated in the destruction of Odin's regime. His natural relationship with END continued to remain relevant long after Asgard's collapse, eventually becoming one of the foundations used in the END-related systems developed by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Empire.[21][5]

#### Jormungandr
[[Jormungandr]] is Hela's other full brother and another of [[Loki]] and [[Angrboða]]'s children whose existence became entangled with Ragnarök. Although generally less openly hostile and intense than either Hela or [[Fenrir]], Jormungandr remained connected to his siblings and ultimately supported the war against [[Odin]].
The enormous periods during which Loki's children were separated did not erase the bond created by their shared persecution. Like Fenrir, Jormungandr's natural connection with END later made him relevant to the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s attempts to weaponize the phenomenon on a cosmic scale.[5]

#### Loki
Hela's relationship with [[Loki]] is overwhelmingly negative despite him being her biological father. Her anger does not stem simply from Loki being unable to defeat [[Odin]]; she condemns him for failing to meaningfully stand beside his children when they were persecuted for a future they had not yet created.
From Hela's perspective, Loki continued existing within the same political order that imprisoned her and tormented her brothers instead of demonstrating the loyalty she believed a father owed his children. This failure left [[Fenrir]] and [[Jormungandr]] as the members of her original family she trusted most and ensured that Hela possessed little sympathy for Loki when Ragnarök finally arrived.[18][6]

#### Angrboða
[[Angrboða]] is Hela's mother and the mother of [[Fenrir]] and [[Jormungandr]]. Unlike her intense feelings toward [[Odin]] or [[Loki]], Hela's direct emotional relationship with Angrboða has received comparatively little attention.
Angrboða is nevertheless important to Hela's unusual nature. The circumstances surrounding a mother capable of carrying multiple children with extraordinary compatibility toward END contributed to Hela developing a relationship with the phenomenon different from that of ordinary Gods, even though Hela herself was not originally the same type of natural END Being as her brothers.[6]

#### Odin
[[Odin]] was the central enemy around whom Hela structured millennia of her existence. He imprisoned her and persecuted [[Loki]]'s children because he feared their place in Ragnarök, effectively punishing them for a future he believed prophecy had already decided.
Hela consequently wanted more than Odin's death. Her plan was designed to make him experience the collapse of everything he had attempted to preserve: family, authority, loyal warriors, political stability and finally his own physical superiority. By the end of Ragnarök Odin had been mutilated, Gungnir lay broken and hidden betrayals had destroyed the certainty upon which his rule depended.[11][7]
Hela regarded the result as the ultimate proof of Odin's failure. His attempts to prevent Ragnarök generated the resentment and circumstances necessary to cause it, making his fear of prophecy one of the primary reasons the prophecy was fulfilled.[7]

#### Nidhoggr
Nidhoggr was one of Hela's most important external allies during her preparation for Ragnarök. Their relationship was transactional rather than one of conventional loyalty: Hela possessed resources and opportunities Nidhoggr valued, while the Ancient Dragon gave Hela access to a Being and forces capable of threatening aspects of [[Asgard]] that [[Odin]] believed secure.
Nidhoggr's involvement became especially important during the attack on [[Frigg]] and the larger destabilization of the Norse system. Hela's willingness to negotiate with such a dangerous Being demonstrated how far beyond conventional Asgardian politics her preparations had extended.[18][11]

#### Idun
[[Idun]] became one of Hela's most consequential hidden collaborators within [[Asgard]]. Her loyalty to [[Odin]]'s system had already been damaged by resentment surrounding the death of her brother and the manner in which the incident had been handled.
Hela recognized the grievance and cultivated it rather than forcing a betrayal from nothing. She kept Idun's true allegiance concealed until Odin had already been placed under enormous military and emotional pressure, maximizing the psychological effect when the betrayal was finally revealed.[7]

#### Violet Elderblood Snow
[[Violet Elderblood Snow]] played a major role in transforming Hela from one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s external allies into a permanent member of the Elderblood family. Violet quickly recognized the growing attraction and compatibility between Hela and Victor and made little attempt to conceal her desire to bring Hela into the household.
Her enthusiasm eventually became excessive enough that she pushed Hela toward signing a marriage agreement before Victor believed Hela had made the decision independently. Victor destroyed the agreement and required Hela to choose again without pressure, but the incident did not damage Hela's eventual relationship with Violet. Hela still chose Victor and consequently became one of Violet's sisters within the household.[2]

### Trivia
- Hela was first mentioned long before her direct debut; her first major direct appearance occurs in Chapter 942, "Goddess of the [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]], Hela."[6]
- Hela was originally a Third Generation Goddess, while [[Odin]] belonged to an older Divine generation. Her victory over him was achieved through millennia of preparation, accumulated armies, betrayal, psychological warfare and personal combat rather than being presented as a simple generational power comparison.[7]
- Odin's decision to imprison a Death-aligned Goddess inside an Underworld ultimately gave Hela enormous time and access to the exact environment she needed to prepare his downfall.[6]
- Hela accumulated Souls and military forces originating outside the [[Norse Pantheon]], including [[Leonidas I]], [[Vlad III Tepes]] and [[Julius Caesar]].[8][9]
- Her affinity with Death is described as abnormal even when compared with other powerful Death-associated Beings.[9]
- Hela sacrificed approximately 99% of her vitality during the preparations preceding Ragnarök, causing her hair to turn white until [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] restored her.[10]
- Hela deliberately spared races unrelated to her family's persecution after agreeing to Victor's request.[10]
- She had little desire to become the permanent ruler of [[Asgard]] after defeating Odin and instead removed what she wanted before auctioning the remainder of the Norse Pantheon.[12][13]
- Hela deliberately limited how much information outsiders received regarding the true events and resources involved in Ragnarök.

### Abilities

#### Powers & Abilities
Hela Elderblood is an ancient Divine Being whose combat ability and supernatural versatility substantially exceed what her original status as a Third Generation Goddess would suggest. Her oldest powers are centered around Death, Time, Souls and her former authority over Helheim, but the defining abnormality of her existence is her compatibility with END. Unlike [[Fenrir]], [[Jormungandr]], [[Typhon]] and similar Beings whose bodies are naturally connected to END, Hela was not originally born as a direct embodiment or natural conductor of the Concept. Instead, unusual circumstances surrounding her birth allowed her to survive limited exposure to END, and she spent an enormous portion of her existence developing that compatibility into a practical weapon.[1]
By the time of Ragnarök, Hela could channel END through the [[Staff of Death and End]], combine it with her Death-related abilities, damage both body and Soul simultaneously and threaten considerably older Gods. Her eventual victory over [[Odin]] was not the result of simple raw-power superiority: Hela had spent millennia collecting Souls, preparing armies, establishing contracts, cultivating traitors, securing Nidhoggr and her brothers, studying Odin and creating conditions in which his advantages as an older God-King could be systematically dismantled.[2][3][4]
Hela continued developing after Ragnarök. Her manipulation of END became sufficiently refined for her to locate and open passages toward dead cosmic regions, while her understanding of Death and Souls remained valuable even to [[Victor Elderblood]]. She was later explicitly described as the Goddess of Death and The End. After voluntarily joining Victor's family, Hela evolved into a Blood Dragon and Dragon Goddess, preserving her pre-existing Divine powers while receiving the physical, spiritual and supernatural foundation of a True Dragon.[5][6][7]
Her relationship with END eventually progressed beyond the resistance she possessed during Ragnarök. By Chapter 1159, while the Elderblood family discussed the Galaxy-[[Destroyer]]-class Divine spacecraft Polaris, the narration explicitly identified Hela and Victor as the only individuals present who were immune to END. Hela was consequently selected as the principal operator of Polaris, whose ultimate weapon channels END on a scale vastly beyond Hela's ordinary personal attacks.[8]

##### Divine Physiology
Before her Draconic evolution, Hela was already an ancient Goddess who had survived and developed for enormous periods within Helheim. Her Divine body granted the basic advantages common to powerful Gods, while her extreme age, combat experience and specialized Divinities allowed her to contend with opponents who possessed a superior Divine generation.
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Hela possesses supernatural strength, speed, durability, stamina, reflexes and agility. During Ragnarök she personally entered close combat with [[Odin]] and could threaten him with direct weapon strikes despite the enormous difference in their Divine age.[3]
- Divine Longevity: Hela has existed for immense periods without conventional biological aging and can survive spans of Time that would encompass countless mortal generations.[1]
- [[Divine Energy]] Manipulation: Hela can generate, control and distribute Divine Energy. Her Energy can reinforce herself, empower supernatural equipment and strengthen forces operating beneath her authority.[2]
- Energy Projection: Divine and END-related power can be released through Hela's body or concentrated through her weapon.[3]
- Power Bestowal: Hela can distribute portions of her Divine power among subordinate forces. During Ragnarök, the historical armies serving beneath her were equipped and empowered sufficiently to participate in a battlefield populated by Gods and supernatural armies.[2]
- Divine Equipment Transformation: Hela can alter the active configuration of her Divine equipment. When she entered serious combat against Odin, her white Divine robe transformed into battle armor while the [[Staff of Death and End]] transformed into an enormous battle axe.[3]
- [[Divine Presence]]: As an ancient Goddess and former ruler of a Divine [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]], Hela possesses the supernatural presence associated with a high-ranking Divine Being.

##### Divinities
Hela's principal demonstrated Divine authorities are Death and Time. Her prolonged interaction with END eventually became sufficiently developed that the story later identified her as the Goddess of Death and The End.[9][6]

###### Death
Hela possesses an exceptional affinity for Death. Chapter 962 specifically describes her sensitivity toward Death as abnormal even when compared with major Death-related Beings such as [[Thanatos]] and the [[Heavenly Father]]'s Angel of Death. This affinity does not merely allow her to manipulate corpses; Hela can perceive the approaching cessation of an existence, preserve Souls for enormous periods and build functioning supernatural forces from the dead.[9]
- Death Manipulation: Hela possesses Divine authority over Death and phenomena associated with deceased existence.[1][9]
- Death Perception: Hela can perceive the relationship between a living Being and its eventual death or cessation.[9]
- Death Precognition: She can perceive when someone is approaching the point at which they will die or cease to exist. Hela used this ability to predict the eventual disappearance of the original [[Vlad III Tepes]] and approached him long before that event occurred.[9]
- Existential Cessation Perception: Hela's perception is not restricted to ordinary biological death. Chapter 962 describes her ability in terms of recognizing when a Being will "die" or cease to exist, allowing her to detect endings that involve more than the destruction of a physical body.[9]
- Soul Manipulation: Hela possesses extensive control and knowledge concerning Souls. Her entire Ragnarök military system depended upon the acquisition, preservation, organization and later manifestation of deceased Beings.[2][9]
- Soul Perception: Hela can directly perceive Souls and abnormalities affecting spiritual existence.[9]
- Soul Preservation: Souls acquired by Hela can remain preserved beneath her authority for centuries or millennia rather than passing beyond her reach.[2][9]
- Soul Storage: Hela maintained large numbers of deceased Beings until the moment she required them for Ragnarök.[2]
- Soul Manifestation: Preserved deceased Beings can be returned to an active state capable of communicating and fighting.[2]
- Soul Damage: Her END-infused attacks can injure the spiritual component of an opponent together with the physical body.[3]
- Soul Severing: Hela's axe severed the corresponding spiritual portion of [[Odin]]'s arm together with the physical limb, preventing the attack from being treated as ordinary bodily damage.[3]
- Soul Knowledge: Hela possesses extensive theoretical knowledge of the interaction between memory, identity and the Soul. Her expertise remained relevant during later encounters involving unconventional spiritual phenomena.[10]
- [[Necromancy]]: Hela is one of the most accomplished users of the dead shown among Earth's Divine factions. Her abilities were developed over immense periods inside a realm filled with Souls and were eventually applied on a military scale.[1][2]
- Undead Manipulation: Hela can control and direct supernatural forces formed from deceased Beings.[2]
- Necromantic Manifestation: Dead Souls under her control can be manifested in forms capable of interacting with the material world.[2]
- Mass Necromancy: Her authority operates on the scale of entire armies rather than isolated undead servants.[2]
- Historical Soul Summoning: Hela retained the Souls or agreements of figures from distant periods and later called them forth during Ragnarök.[2][9]
- Army Command: Hela can simultaneously maintain large supernatural armies consisting of individuals originating from different cultures, periods and supernatural jurisdictions.[2]
Among the most notable deceased commanders accumulated by Hela were [[Leonidas I]], Vlad III Tepes and [[Julius Caesar]]. Their appearance during Ragnarök demonstrated that Hela's control over the dead extended far beyond ordinary residents of Helheim and that she had spent centuries secretly obtaining useful Souls from outside the traditional Norse system.[2][9]

###### Time
Hela is also a genuine Goddess of Time. Her authority is less extensively demonstrated than her Death abilities, but Chapter 942 establishes that she can interact with temporal phenomena and observe possible futures. Hela also understands that future events are not necessarily immutable, which directly shaped her contempt for [[Odin]]'s treatment of the Ragnarök prophecy as though a possible future justified punishing [[Loki]]'s children before they had committed the predicted acts.[1]
- Time Manipulation: Hela possesses a Divine authority related to Time and can interact with temporal phenomena through her Divinity.[1]
- Future Sight: Hela can observe possibilities located ahead of the present.[1]
- Possible-Future Perception: Her precognition concerns futures that may occur rather than an absolutely predetermined destiny. This distinction is important because Hela herself recognizes that actions taken in the present can alter what ultimately happens.[1]
- Long-Range Precognition (Limited): Hela can attempt to examine events far into the future, but doing so requires increasingly enormous amounts of Energy and is therefore not unrestricted.[1]
- Temporal Knowledge: Hela possesses enough understanding of Time to recognize the limitations and costs involved in manipulating or observing it rather than treating temporal authority as unlimited omniscience.[1]
- Extreme Temporal Adaptation: Following her entry into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family, Hela could live and develop normally within highly accelerated Space-Time environments. She and Victor accumulated nearly two thousand subjective years together despite vastly less external Time passing.[7]
Hela's Time Divinity should not be confused with unrestricted control over the entire temporal structure of Creation. Higher cosmic authorities and the fundamental nature of Time impose limitations upon ordinary Gods, and Hela herself acknowledges the enormous Energy cost of attempting to perceive excessively distant futures.[1]

###### END
Hela's relationship with END is the most abnormal aspect of her supernatural existence. END represents final cessation rather than ordinary destructive force: entities and attacks connected to it can affect physical matter, Souls and deeper aspects of existence in ways conventional damage cannot easily reproduce.
Hela was not originally born as the same type of END Being as [[Fenrir]] or [[Jormungandr]]. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] theorized that her unusual resistance originated during gestation. [[Angrboða]] carried multiple children naturally connected to END, causing both the mother and Hela to be exposed to small quantities of its Energy during development. Hela survived that exposure and eventually transformed the resulting compatibility into one of her greatest advantages.[1]
For most of her earlier life, Hela did not simply circulate END directly through her body. She instead used the [[Staff of Death and End]], which incorporated material associated with Fenrir, as an external conduit. This distinction gave her unusual flexibility: naturally born END Beings frequently express the Concept through their own bodies, whereas Hela could treat END more like an Energy she could channel, shape and direct through a weapon.[5]
- END Manipulation: Hela can manipulate power originating from END and apply it offensively, defensively and spatially.[1][5][6]
- External END Channeling: Hela historically channeled END through the Staff of Death and End rather than depending upon her body as the primary conduit.[5]
- Creative END Manipulation: Using END externally allows Hela to shape its Energy with considerably greater flexibility than simply coating claws or another biological weapon. Her handling of the force was compared with the more sophisticated manner in which [[True Death]] itself could manipulate END.[5]
- END Evolution: Hela's relationship with the Concept continuously advanced. She began with exceptional resistance and artifact-mediated manipulation, was later called the Goddess of Death and The End, and eventually reached explicit immunity to END.[1][6][8]
- Existence Erasure: END-infused attacks can cause affected targets to disappear rather than merely sustaining conventional physical damage. During Hela's battle against [[Odin]], black Energy released through her weapon erased whatever it successfully struck.[3]
- Matter Erasure: Physical matter caught within sufficiently concentrated END can be erased.[3]
- Area Erasure: Hela's attacks can remove sections of the surrounding environment together with intended targets.[3]
- Spiritual Erasure: Because END can affect the Soul together with the body, conventional physical regeneration does not necessarily address the full injury.[3]
- Existential Damage: END attacks operate on a deeper level than ordinary kinetic or elemental destruction, threatening the continued existence of what they affect.[3][6]
- Durability Negation: Hela's END attacks cannot be evaluated purely through the physical toughness of the target. Odin deliberately avoided allowing her weapon to strike him cleanly because the consequences extended beyond ordinary bodily injury.[3]
- Soul Destruction: END can damage or eliminate spiritual structures together with physical ones.[3]
- Soul Severing: When Hela removed Odin's arm with an END-infused strike, the corresponding portion of his Soul was also severed.[3]
- END Energy Projection: Hela can discharge concentrated black END Energy through her weapon instead of restricting its effects to direct melee contact.[3]
- END-Infused Area Attacks: The Staff's axe configuration can generate large destructive releases of black Energy capable of affecting an extensive area.[3]
- END Detection: Hela can recognize and follow traces associated with END, allowing her to locate phenomena hidden from conventional supernatural senses.[11]
- END-Based Navigation: END can function as a metaphysical guide toward locations that have genuinely reached their End.[11][5]
- Dead-Galaxy Detection: Hela can locate galaxies and cosmic regions where normal Life, Time, Space and the familiar structures of living Creation have ceased functioning.[5]
- Dead-Sector Access: Because such regions exist close to the Domain represented by END, Hela can access them more reliably than ordinary Gods.[5]
- Reality Tearing: Hela can direct END against the local structure of Reality to create an opening toward dead cosmic regions.[5]
- Dimensional Portal Creation: These openings can become traversable passages through which Hela and other Beings may interact with otherwise isolated locations.[5]
- Portal Maintenance: Hela can sustain END-based openings long enough for others to observe, communicate or pass through them.[5]
- Abyssal Navigation: Hela can use END to locate regions inhabited by [[Creatures of the Abyss]], entities that developed within environments largely abandoned by ordinary living civilizations.[11][5]
The limits of this ability were demonstrated when Beings on the opposite side of one of Hela's portals interfered with the passage themselves. END compatibility did not grant Hela automatic superiority over everything inhabiting dead regions, and sufficiently abnormal Eldritch Beings could manipulate an opening she had created.[5]

##### Underworld Authority
For most of her Divine existence, Hela ruled Helheim. [[Odin]]'s decision to confine a Goddess with an exceptional affinity for Death inside an [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] filled with Souls unintentionally gave Hela an ideal environment in which to cultivate the powers that would eventually be used against him.[1]
- Underworld Lordship: Hela possessed authority over Helheim and its supernatural inhabitants while acting as the realm's Goddess and ruler.[1]
- Soul Dominion: Souls existing beneath Hela's authority could be organized, preserved and deployed according to her plans.[2]
- Dead Army Creation: Hela transformed enormous numbers of deceased Beings into an organized supernatural military force rather than merely collecting isolated Souls.[2]
- Cross-Pantheon Soul Acquisition: Her collection was not limited to the normal Norse dead. Hela obtained individuals whose lives and deaths belonged to completely different cultures and supernatural jurisdictions.[2][9]
- Underworld Empowerment: Thousands of years surrounded by the dead provided Hela with extraordinary practical experience in Death, Souls and necromantic systems.[1]
Her later departure from Helheim did not erase the knowledge or personal abilities she accumulated there, although political ownership of the old Norse Underworld should not be confused with Hela possessing absolute authority over every afterlife in Creation.

##### Contracts
A major portion of Hela's strength during Ragnarök did not originate from raw Energy but from agreements prepared centuries or millennia in advance. She repeatedly obtained cooperation from Beings who had something she needed and preserved those agreements until the exact moment they would become useful.
- Supernatural Contract Creation: Hela can establish supernatural agreements with living, deceased and non-human Beings.[2][9]
- Long-Term Contracts: Her agreements can remain relevant across enormous spans of Time before being invoked.[2][9]
- Post-Mortem Agreements: Hela's dealings with historical figures demonstrate that death does not necessarily terminate the supernatural obligations or arrangements she establishes.[2][9]
- Soul-Related Agreements: Hela can incorporate Souls and post-death service into arrangements connected to her authority over Death.[9]
- Cross-Species Contracts: Her negotiating partners are not restricted to Humans or Gods. Hela successfully reached mutually beneficial arrangements with entities originating from dead regions and other abnormal environments.[2][5]
- Abyssal Contracts: Hela maintained business-like relations with [[Creatures of the Abyss]], acquiring access to Beings most Earth Pantheons possessed little knowledge of.[5]
- Contractor Summoning: Beings connected to her through prepared agreements can be called into another battlefield or region when the conditions of their arrangement permit it.[2]
Hela's alliance with Nidhoggr is another example of this approach. She did not control the Ancient Dragon as an obedient servant; instead, she acquired cooperation by offering resources and conditions valuable enough for Nidhoggr to participate in her millennia-long strategy against [[Odin]].[1]

##### Summoning
Hela's Death authority, contracts and access to other regions combine into an unusually broad summoning arsenal.
- Summoning: Hela can bring deceased warriors, contracted entities and supernatural creatures onto the battlefield.[2]
- Mass Summoning: Her abilities are capable of deploying entire military formations rather than individual servants.[2]
- Historical Army Summoning: Hela summoned forces associated with Leonidas, [[Vlad III Tepes]] and [[Julius Caesar]] during Ragnarök.[2]
- Cross-Dimensional Summoning: Beings originating outside the immediate realm can be brought into another battlefield through supernatural openings and prepared agreements.[2][5]
- Abyssal Summoning: Hela can summon or facilitate the arrival of [[Creatures of the Abyss]] with whom she has established agreements.[2][5]
- Army Deployment: Hela can coordinate multiple independently commanded armies simultaneously as components of a larger war plan.[2]

##### Spatial and Dimensional Abilities
Hela does not possess a demonstrated Space Divinity comparable to specialists such as Natalia, but her END manipulation gives her several methods of interfering with dimensional boundaries.
- Dimensional Rift Creation: Hela can create openings between otherwise separated environments.[5]
- Portal Creation: END can be used to construct traversable passages linking conventional Reality with dead cosmic regions.[5]
- Dimensional Traversal: Hela can use those passages to enter regions that conventional travel methods would have difficulty locating or reaching.[5]
- Reality Breaching: Rather than manipulating Space in the conventional sense, Hela can use END to puncture the boundary separating ordinary Creation from regions belonging more closely to final cessation.[5]
- Dimensional Navigation: Hela can use the metaphysical relationship between END and dead regions as a directional reference when normal astronomical navigation is insufficient.[11][5]

##### Weapon Manipulation
Hela is extremely proficient with the [[Staff of Death and End]], both as a supernatural conduit and as a physical weapon. During Ragnarök she demonstrated the ability to change its configuration repeatedly during combat, allowing her to move between long-reach attacks, dual-wielding and ranged weapon trajectories without abandoning her END channeling.[3]
- Weapon Transformation: The Staff can transform into a gigantic black great axe marked by glowing green Runes.[3]
- Twin Axe Transformation: The great axe can divide into two individual axes, allowing Hela to dual-wield.[3]
- Weapon Recombination: The paired axes can be returned to the larger combined configuration.[3]
- Remote Weapon Control: Hela can influence the movement of a thrown axe through the supernatural functions built into its Runes.[3]
- Returning Weapon: Thrown axes can alter trajectory and return to Hela rather than behaving as ordinary projectiles.[3]
- Rune-Enhanced Weaponry: Runes engraved upon the weapon regulate transformations and supernatural movement.[3]
- END Weapon Coating: Hela can saturate the weapon with END so that physical strikes simultaneously carry existential and spiritual effects.[3]
- Energy Discharge: END can be released outward from the weapon as destructive black Energy instead of remaining restricted to its cutting edge.[3]
- Divine Artifact Clashing: Hela's weapon can directly contend against exceptionally powerful Divine equipment.[3]
- Divine Artifact Destruction: During Ragnarök, Hela's weapon contributed to the destruction of Gungnir, eventually breaking the shaft of [[Odin]]'s legendary weapon.[3]

##### Combat Skills
Hela is not merely a ritualist or commander who depends upon minions. Her battle against [[Odin]] demonstrated that she can personally fight at extremely high speed while simultaneously using Divine power, END and transforming weapons.
- Master Combatant: Hela can directly engage experienced [[God-Kings]] in close combat and maintain awareness of a battlefield involving multiple supernatural factions.[3]
- Axe Mastery: She is exceptionally skilled with the great-axe form of the [[Staff of Death and End]].[3]
- Dual-Wielding: Hela can transition to paired axes and fight with both weapons simultaneously.[3]
- Thrown Weapon Mastery: She incorporates supernatural thrown-weapon attacks into close combat, creating attack paths independent from her immediate position.[3]
- Multi-Vector Attacks: Remote weapon control allows Hela to pressure an opponent from one direction while personally attacking from another.[3]
- Combat Adaptability: Hela changes weapon forms and attack patterns according to the opponent's reactions rather than relying upon a single fixed style.[3]
- Opportunistic Combat: She immediately exploits emotional distractions and openings. Odin's attention being divided by threats to his family became another weakness Hela deliberately used against him.[3]
- Psychological Warfare: Hela fights the opponent's emotional stability as deliberately as their body. Her Ragnarök strategy was designed to make Odin experience helplessness, betrayal and loss before the final confrontation.[3][4]

##### Intelligence and Strategy
Hela's greatest demonstrated accomplishment before joining [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was not an isolated attack but the construction of Ragnarök itself. She spent millennia creating a war in which virtually every layer of [[Odin]]'s advantages could be attacked simultaneously.
- Genius Intelligence: Hela possesses extensive knowledge of Divine politics, Souls, Death, Time, END and supernatural contracts accumulated over an enormous lifespan.[1][9]
- Master Strategist: She prepared Ragnarök across millennia rather than attempting an impulsive rebellion against a stronger God-King.[1][2]
- Long-Term Planning: Hela can maintain plans, relationships and hidden assets across centuries or millennia until the required conditions emerge.[1][9]
- Military Strategy: Ragnarök required the coordination of armies from different historical periods, supernatural creatures, Gods, internal collaborators and END-based assets.[2][4]
- Political Manipulation: Hela identified existing resentment inside Odin's own faction and converted those grievances into useful alliances rather than relying exclusively upon external invasion.[4]
- Information Concealment: Odin remained unaware of the full extent of Hela's armies, allies and preparations until the conflict was already underway.[2]
- Power Concealment: The degree to which Hela herself had developed was likewise underestimated before she began fighting directly.[3]
- Counter-Planning: Hela built her strategy around Odin's known strengths rather than assuming she could defeat a considerably older God through a direct contest of [[Divine Energy]] alone.[2][4]
- Cosmic Knowledge: Hela possesses rare information concerning END, dead galaxies, dead Sectors and entities that develop in regions conventional civilizations largely abandon.[11][5]
- Soul Expertise: Her understanding of Souls extends beyond combat and includes the preservation of identity, memory and spiritual information.[9][10]
- Torture Expertise: Hela possesses extensive practical experience with physical and psychological torture, most notably demonstrated through her treatment of [[Vali]].[3]

##### Blood Dragon / Dragon Goddess Physiology
After freely choosing to enter [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family, Hela evolved into a Blood Dragon and Dragon Goddess. Victor's Draconic transformation does not simply replace the powers possessed by the recipient; existing supernatural characteristics are retained and incorporated into the new Draconic foundation. Hela consequently remained a Goddess of Death, Time and END while gaining a True Dragon body and Soul.[12][7]
Her new age as a Dragon should not be confused with her chronological age. Despite having lived for eons as a Goddess and spending roughly two thousand subjective years developing after joining Victor, Hela was still described as a comparatively immature Dragon Goddess. Violet interpreted the length of Hela's maturation as evidence that her eventual Draconic potential was substantially greater than they had first estimated.[7]

###### True Dragon Foundation
- True Dragon Physiology: Hela possesses the physical, spiritual and supernatural foundation of a genuine True Dragon while simultaneously remaining a Divine Being.[7]
- Enhanced Physical Characteristics: Her already formidable Divine strength, speed, durability and endurance were elevated further after Draconic evolution.[7]
- Enhanced Durability: Hela's True Dragon body possesses vastly greater resilience than her previous Divine form.
- Enhanced Stamina: Her Draconic foundation provides an enormous reserve of physical and supernatural endurance.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Hela possesses the regenerative advantages associated with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s True Dragon lineage.
- Enhanced Senses: Her current physiology possesses the supernatural sensory capabilities of a True Dragon.
- True Dragon Soul: The transformation affected the foundation of Hela's existence rather than merely adding external Dragon traits.[7]
- [[Draconic Energy]] Manipulation: Hela can generate and control the Energy associated with her current Draconic physiology.[7]
- Divine-Draconic Integration: Hela's Death, Time, Soul and END-related abilities coexist with and are reinforced by her True Dragon foundation rather than operating as an unrelated second power system.[7]
- Power Preservation: Hela retained the supernatural powers she developed before her transformation instead of losing her original Divinities.[7]
- Draconic Growth: Hela can continue evolving through the natural developmental process of a True Dragon.[7]
- Exceptional Developmental Potential: Even nearly two thousand subjective years were insufficient for her to reach complete Draconic maturity, indicating substantial remaining room for development.[7]

###### Racial Draconic Abilities
The following abilities belong to Hela's established True Dragon foundation. They should be distinguished from individually demonstrated named techniques: Hela possesses the racial capability, but the story has not necessarily shown a personal specialized application of every one.
- Dragon Transformation: Hela possesses an authentic Draconic form rather than being a Goddess who merely carries Dragon Energy.[7]
- Humanoid Transformation: She can maintain a humanoid body despite her underlying True Dragon physiology.[7]
- Shapeshifting: True Dragon physiology permits substantial alteration between Draconic and humanoid states.
- Flight: Hela's Dragon physiology provides the aerial capabilities characteristic of the race.
- Dragon Breath: Her physiology possesses the foundation necessary for Draconic breath attacks, although Hela's individual breath expression has not yet received a sufficiently detailed canonical description to assign it a unique element.
- Draconic Aura: Hela possesses the supernatural pressure associated with a powerful Dragon Goddess.
- Draconic Language: As a True Dragon, Hela possesses access to the supernatural language through which Dragons communicate [[Will Solace|Will]] rather than relying exclusively upon conventional speech.
- Reality Interaction through Draconic Language: At sufficiently high levels, Draconic speech can impose a Dragon's Will upon the surrounding world. No uniquely named Hela-specific command has yet been demonstrated.
- Draconic Runes: Hela's True Dragon lineage gives her access to the supernatural runic system used by Dragons, although no personal named Rune exclusive to Hela has yet been revealed.

###### Reality Shaping
By the Imperial Era, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Dragon Goddesses were explicitly described as exceptionally talented Reality shapers. Beginning around the 70th millennium of Imperial development, the Dragon Goddesses participated in restructuring astronomical bodies throughout the Empire while Victor maintained the overall stability of the galaxy.[13]
Hela belongs to this group. These feats therefore establish capabilities available to her current class of existence, but should be treated as collective Dragon-Goddess feats rather than proof that Hela individually performed every astronomical alteration herself.
- Reality Warping: Dragon Goddesses can alter existing structures through developed Draconic power.[13]
- Celestial Object Manipulation: Their large-scale Imperial engineering involved reshaping astronomical structures throughout the galaxy.[13]
- Asteroid Manipulation: Asteroids were incorporated into the Empire's transformed Galactic defense network.[13]
- Lunar Manipulation: Moons could be manipulated and repurposed as components of large-scale Imperial infrastructure and defenses.[13]
- Stellar Manipulation: Stars were among the structures involved in the Empire's Galactic remodeling.[13]
- Nebula Manipulation: Nebulae were likewise incorporated into large-scale environmental restructuring.[13]
- [[Black Hole]] Manipulation: Even black holes were utilized as components of the Empire's hidden Galactic defenses.[13]
- Cosmic Environmental Engineering: The Dragon Goddesses collectively helped transform an ordinary galaxy into a deliberately engineered defensive environment containing hidden supernatural threats throughout astronomical structures.[13]

##### External Enhancements

###### Victor's Protection
Following Hela's encounters with Beings capable of bypassing conventional spiritual defenses, she requested the greatest protection [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] could provide. Victor explained that the women closest to him possessed multiple safeguards against unconventional attacks and could additionally carry a fragment of his own Soul as an internal defense. Hela requested equivalent protection and Victor agreed.[10]
This protection is external support supplied through Victor rather than an ability Hela developed independently. It is intended to resist hostile interference affecting several layers of existence, including:
- Mental Corruption Protection
- Soul Corruption Protection
- Physical Corruption Protection
- Supernatural Manipulation Protection
- Reality Manipulation Protection
- Law Interference Protection
- Destructive Interference Protection
- Bad Luck Protection
- Soul-Based Intrusion Protection
These safeguards should not be interpreted as unconditional immunity against every Being capable of using those powers. Their demonstrated purpose is to give Hela additional protection against unconventional attacks that could otherwise bypass normal physical and Divine defenses.[10]

###### Eldritch Evolution Potential
Hela's repeated dealings with Creatures originating from dead cosmic regions exposed her existence to supernatural systems far outside conventional Divine development. By Chapter 1110, this history was explicitly connected to the possibility that Hela could eventually undergo an Eldritch evolution.[14]
- Latent Eldritch Evolution: Hela possesses the potential to develop into an Eldritch Goddess.[14]
- Eldritch Compatibility: Long-term interaction with Beings from abnormal regions has made her a viable candidate for supernatural development beyond a conventional Earth-born Goddess.[14]
- Death/END/Eldritch Synergy (Potential): Hela's combination of Death affinity, Soul expertise, END compatibility and exposure to foreign Beings gives her an unusually compatible foundation for such an evolution.[14]
This remains potential. Hela has not yet been confirmed to have completed an Eldritch-Goddess transformation and should not currently be classified as an Outer God solely because the possibility was identified.

##### Resistances and Immunities
Hela's resistances developed substantially across the story. In particular, her relationship with END changed from resistance sufficient to safely use an END-infused artifact into explicit immunity by the Imperial Era.
- END Resistance (Former Level): Hela's unusual development allowed her to survive exposure to END and use the [[Staff of Death and End]] without being erased, despite not originally being a natural END Being.[1][5]
- END Immunity (Current): Chapter 1159 explicitly identifies Hela and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] as the only individuals present who were immune to END during the discussion surrounding Polaris.[8]
- Resistance to END-Based Existence Erasure: Hela can safely operate around END without suffering the normal erasing consequences associated with direct exposure.[8]
- Resistance to END-Based Soul Destruction: Her immunity necessarily includes surviving the same END force capable of attacking physical and spiritual existence simultaneously.[8]
- Soul Intrusion Resistance: Hela possesses natural spiritual defenses and the awareness required to resist conventional intrusion into her Soul.[10]
- Mental Intrusion Resistance: Hela possesses defenses against ordinary attempts to interfere with her mind, although sufficiently advanced methods can bypass those protections.[10]
- Mental Corruption Protection: Victor's safeguards provide additional defense against hostile corruption targeting her thoughts or personality.[10]
- Soul Corruption Protection: Victor's protection reinforces Hela against corruption that attempts to alter her Soul.[10]
- Physical Corruption Protection: The same safeguards extend to hostile transformation or corruption of the body.[10]
- Reality Manipulation Protection: Victor's protection is designed to interfere with hostile attempts to alter Hela through Reality-changing powers.[10]
- Law Manipulation Protection: She possesses additional safeguards against external interference with the Laws governing her existence.[10]
- Bad Luck Protection: Supernatural misfortune was included among the unconventional hostile effects Victor's defenses were intended to counter.[10]
- Draconic Durability: Hela's current True Dragon body provides an additional level of resistance to conventional physical and supernatural damage.[7]
Hela's immunity to END is specific to END and should not be generalized into universal immunity to every form of existence erasure, Conceptual power or Eldritch phenomenon. Chapter 1059 demonstrates that END resistance did not automatically protect Hela against unrelated psychological effects produced by incomprehensible Eldritch existence.[5]

##### Limitations
Despite her enormous power, several explicit restrictions remain relevant to Hela's abilities.
- Future Sight Energy Cost: Looking excessively far into the future consumes enormous quantities of Energy, preventing Hela from constantly observing arbitrarily distant events.[1]
- Mutable Future: Hela's precognition observes possibilities rather than guaranteeing that the future she sees must occur.[1]
- Historically Artifact-Mediated END Manipulation: Hela's original practical manipulation of END depended heavily upon the [[Staff of Death and End]], unlike her brothers whose bodies naturally express the Concept.[1][5] This should not be interpreted as proof that modern Hela remains completely incapable of using END without the Staff, since her relationship with the Concept later advanced into an explicit Divine title and eventual immunity.[6][8]
- Portal Interference: Beings existing within dead regions can interfere with END-based passages. An Eldritch creature was able to seize and widen an opening created from Hela's side.[5]
- No Universal Eldritch Immunity: END compatibility does not make Hela immune to unrelated Eldritch effects, madness or foreign metaphysical systems.[5]
- Incomplete Draconic Maturation: Even after nearly two thousand subjective years, Hela remained comparatively immature as a Dragon Goddess and had not reached the complete potential of her current Race.[7]
- Preparation Dependency (Against Superior Opponents): Hela's victory over [[Odin]] should not be interpreted as simple evidence that her raw power automatically exceeded his. Ragnarök was the result of millennia of preparation, armies, END, betrayals, psychological warfare and carefully engineered opportunities.[2][3][4]

The weapon incorporates material associated with [[Fenrir]], allowing Hela to manipulate END externally instead of needing the natural biological connection possessed by her brothers.[1][5]

##### Polaris
Polaris is a classified Galaxy-[[Destroyer]]-class Divine spacecraft constructed by the [[Elderblood Empire]]. It is equipment assigned to Hela, not an innate personal ability, and its feats should therefore remain separate from Hela's natural destructive output.[8]
Construction began as part of the Empire's major superweapon programs. Polaris combines advanced technology, Divinity and conceptual systems capable of bending Reality, Space and Time through Destruction-related mechanisms. Its most dangerous weapon channels END using [[Fenrir]], [[Typhon]] and [[Jormungandr]] as principal sources.[8]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] personally assigned the vessel to Hela because her END immunity made her one of the few Beings capable of safely operating in close proximity to its ultimate systems.[8]
- Galaxy-Destroyer-Class Divine Artifact: Polaris is classified among the Elderblood Empire's strategic Galactic weapons.[8]
- Reality Manipulation (Through Polaris): The vessel possesses systems capable of bending Reality.[8]
- Spatial Manipulation (Through Polaris): Its integrated conceptual machinery can distort Space.[8]
- Time Manipulation (Through Polaris): The same systems can interfere with Time.[8]
- Destruction Manipulation (Through Polaris): Destruction-based concepts form part of the systems used to manipulate Reality, Space and Time.[8]
- Eldritch Compatibility: The vessel was constructed to remain compatible with Eldritch forces and future Eldritch modifications.[8]
- END Channeling: Polaris can route END into its ultimate offensive system.[8]
- END Cannon: Its primary conceptual weapon releases END as the destructive principle of the attack instead of depending solely upon conventional Energy.[8]
- Reality-Erasing Attack: The final discharge is described as using END to obliterate the Reality struck by the weapon.[8]
- Disposable Firing Mechanism: [[True Death|The END]] discharge is so destructive that the cannon itself cannot survive firing. Rather than continuing to pursue an impossible permanently resistant barrel, the Empire constructed large numbers of replaceable cannons.[8]
- Dedicated Operator: Victor ordered Hela to familiarize herself with Polaris because the vessel was specifically intended for her use.[8]
The destructive capabilities of Polaris should not be used as evidence that Hela can independently reproduce its full Galactic-scale final attack without the spacecraft, its END sources and its integrated Imperial systems.

#### Power Development
Hela's abilities underwent several distinct stages rather than remaining static throughout the story. As the ancient Goddess of Helheim, her principal advantages were Death, Time, Soul authority, necromancy, accumulated armies and extraordinary preparation. Her unusual END resistance allowed her to wield the [[Staff of Death and End]] and became a decisive factor during Ragnarök.[1][2][3]
After Ragnarök, Hela developed more sophisticated END applications and demonstrated the ability to locate and open routes into dead cosmic regions. Her knowledge of Death, Souls and END increasingly extended beyond the traditional boundaries of the [[Norse Pantheon]].[11][5] By Chapter 1093, she was explicitly recognized as the Goddess of Death and The End, demonstrating that END had become integrated deeply enough into her Divine identity to be reflected in her title.[6]
Hela later chose to join [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family and evolved into a Blood Dragon Goddess. Her previous abilities were preserved within her new True Dragon foundation rather than discarded, giving her the combined developmental paths of an ancient Death Goddess and a comparatively young True Dragon.[7] During the vast accelerated period that followed, she accumulated nearly two thousand subjective years of additional development yet remained Draconically immature, leaving significant room for further evolution.[7]
By the Imperial Era, Hela's END compatibility had matured into explicit END immunity, and Victor entrusted her with Polaris, one of the most dangerous weapons ever produced by the [[Elderblood Empire]].[8] Her later lineage also demonstrated how deeply END had become associated with her existence: [[Helvarya Elderblood]], Hela and Victor's daughter, was born with powers over Death and END, while the apparent second "Soul" detected during Hela's pregnancy was eventually revealed to have been END itself preparing Helvarya as a compatible host.[15]
Hela's current abilities therefore combine five major foundations: her ancient Divine nature, exceptional Death authority, Time Divinity, progressively evolved relationship with END and the continuing growth of a True Dragon Goddess. Her greatest danger has never come solely from any single one of those powers; it comes from her ability to combine specialized supernatural abilities with patience, intelligence and preparation on timescales measured in centuries or millennia.
