Sylvia Elderblood is a recurring character in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]], an Imperial Princess of the [[Elderblood Empire]], a Blood Dragon Goddess, and the biological daughter of Victor Elderblood and [[Siena Scarlett Elderblood]]. Born into Victor's second generation of naturally occurring [[True Dragons]], she possesses the extraordinary supernatural foundation of his Blood Dragon lineage while retaining the maternal ancestry of Clan Scarlett. Through Siena, she is also the granddaughter of [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], connecting her to one of [[Nightingale]]'s oldest and most influential martial families. Her distinctive red hair reflects this heritage, making her recognizable among the numerous daughters descended from the different branches of the Scarlett family.[1]
Sylvia belongs to a generation raised with extraordinary privileges but also considerable responsibilities. Victor and his wives provided their daughters with extensive education concerning supernatural society, Imperial administration and the importance of understanding weaker individuals rather than relying exclusively upon overwhelming power. Sylvia shares her sisters' desire to become useful within their father's civilization, although she generally approaches unfamiliar situations with greater caution than several of her more impulsive relatives. Her practical judgment, observational abilities and loyalty toward the Elderblood family are among her most prominent characteristics.[2]
She is particularly close to [[Rosemary Lavina Elderblood]] and [[Priscillia Penny Elderblood]], with whom she frequently spends time reading manga, discussing Imperial affairs and participating in family activities. Although Sylvia recognizes the consequences of her sisters' more reckless decisions, she is unwilling to abandon them when they become involved in trouble. This loyalty leads her to accompany Rosemary and Priscillia during an unauthorized investigation of Pepper's private residence, where the three discover extraordinary scientific experiments, advanced supernatural technology and historical records documenting Victor's earlier life.[3]
Sylvia later participates in the mischievous activities of the younger Imperial Princesses, including the prank involving Velina, and continues attending the family gatherings and educational lessons organized by Victor. Her individual combat specialization remains largely unidentified, although her natural Blood Dragon physiology and advanced Draconic intelligence provide exceptional potential. She should not be confused with [[Silvia Elderblood]], the daughter of Victor and [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]], whose similar name occasionally causes confusion despite their different maternal lineages.[4]

### Physical Appearance
Sylvia is an exceptionally beautiful young True Dragon Goddess with fair skin, red hair and the violet Dragon Eyes characteristic of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s biological descendants. Her red hair provides a particularly obvious connection to [[Siena Scarlett Elderblood]] and the wider Scarlett lineage, whose members frequently possess similar coloration. She appears among a group of red-haired Imperial Princesses descended from several members of the Scarlett family, and her maternal heritage remains visually apparent when she spends time alongside Rosemary and Priscillia. Her youthful Humanoid appearance conceals the extraordinary biological and supernatural characteristics of a naturally born Blood Dragon.[1][2]
Like her sisters, Sylvia belongs to a Race whose physical maturation differs fundamentally from Human aging. Naturally born [[True Dragons]] can accumulate centuries or millennia of experience while retaining comparatively youthful forms, and their supernatural physiology provides extensive control over their appearance through metamorphosis. Victor nevertheless discourages his daughters from constantly transforming themselves into older versions or imitating their mothers, believing that their natural appearances are already beautiful and should be appreciated rather than replaced. Sylvia consequently belongs to a generation whose members are encouraged to preserve their individual identities despite possessing extraordinary transformative abilities.[1]
Her violet eyes reflect the inherited ocular characteristics associated with Victor's Blood Dragon descendants, whose supernatural perception can develop considerably through training. However, Sylvia has not received an individual demonstration establishing the complete extent of her Dragon Eyes or the specialized applications achieved by sisters possessing particularly advanced ocular abilities. Her exact height, weight and complete Dragon Form have likewise not been described in sufficient detail to establish reliable measurements or distinctive Draconic anatomical features beyond her natural racial inheritance.

### Personality
Sylvia is practical, intelligent, observant, curious and deeply loyal to her family. She generally demonstrates greater caution than several of her more impulsive sisters, recognizing potential problems and questioning decisions that appear unreasonable. However, her sensible disposition does not prevent her from becoming involved in troublesome activities when her siblings are concerned. She is particularly unwilling to abandon Priscillia and Rosemary, even when she understands that their plans may lead to punishment, revealing that her commitment to her family frequently takes precedence over her desire to avoid unnecessary trouble.[2][3]
Her practical attitude is evident in the way she approaches the Empire's constantly expanding information systems. Unlike Rosemary and Priscillia, Sylvia does not regularly examine every new administrative or scientific update, considering the continuous flow of information unnecessarily exhausting. Rosemary criticizes this habit because their Draconic intellects allow them to process enormous quantities of information in very little time, while Sylvia dismisses her sisters as nerds rather than accepting their criticism. Her attitude reflects a difference in personal interests rather than inadequate intelligence, since [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s naturally born daughters possess extraordinary cognitive abilities and have received extensive education covering numerous supernatural and technological disciplines.[2]
Sylvia is also willing to criticize her sisters when their enthusiasm causes them to overlook practical considerations. When Priscillia announces that she intends to participate in Pepper's classified research, Sylvia immediately questions how she expects to gain access without understanding the project's purpose or possessing the necessary qualifications. She recognizes that researchers such as Velnorah, Aline and Ruby maintain demanding standards, and that even members of the Imperial Family cannot reasonably expect admission merely because they are related to an existing participant. Sylvia also acknowledges Velina's exceptional competence, attributing it to the enormous amount of experience she accumulated while working alongside her mother rather than simply to her status as an Imperial Princess.[2]
Her familiarity with Priscillia's personality allows her to recognize trouble before it occurs. When Priscillia becomes unusually quiet while considering how to investigate Pepper's research, Sylvia immediately warns Rosemary that something is wrong. Both understand that prolonged concentration is particularly dangerous when undertaken by a naturally born Dragon Goddess possessing an exceptionally powerful mind, since Priscillia is capable of developing elaborate plans once she commits herself to an objective. Sylvia becomes increasingly anxious as her sister continues thinking, eventually abandoning her manga because she can no longer concentrate upon reading. Nevertheless, she follows Priscillia when the latter begins acting upon her plan, unwilling to leave her sister alone despite expecting the situation to end badly.[2]
Once the infiltration succeeds, Sylvia's caution gradually gives way to curiosity. She becomes fascinated by Pepper's unusual laboratories, questions the enormous scale of her aunt's private residence and examines the research documentation associated with an artificial star. The discoveries substantially change her perception of Pepper, whom she had previously associated more strongly with entertainment and culture than advanced scientific development. Sylvia also notices subtle differences between Pepper's youthful personality and the more visibly maternal demeanor of Siena, Ruby and Lacus, demonstrating that she pays attention to the behavior of others rather than judging them exclusively by their appearance or supernatural abilities.[3]
Like many of Victor's daughters, Sylvia possesses considerable affection for her father and is fascinated by the portions of his history that occurred before her birth. She becomes interested in rare photographs and recordings depicting Victor's earlier supernatural life, particularly material showing him before his ascension as God Emperor. Her enthusiasm is already apparent when Leone jokes about exchanging an unusual recording of Victor for favors from one of their mothers; Sylvia blushes when challenged over whether she would do the same and does not deny the suggestion. Her relatively restrained manner therefore conceals many of the same desires and interests shared by the other Imperial Princesses.[1][3]
Sylvia also enjoys ordinary family mischief, particularly when it involves her sisters. She participates in the prank surrounding Velina and subsequently assures Victor that they would never deliberately harm another member of the family. Her reaction distinguishes playful competition from genuine hostility, reflecting the principle that the Elderblood daughters may tease and argue with one another but must remain united. During later educational gatherings, Sylvia also demonstrates familiarity with fictional entertainment and storytelling conventions, comparing Moira's extraordinary Luck with the advantages enjoyed by excessively fortunate protagonists in cultivation novels. Despite recognizing Moira's exceptional circumstances, she acknowledges that Victor's other daughters possess extraordinary privileges of their own.[4][5]

### Heritage
Sylvia is the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Siena Scarlett Elderblood]], combining the evolved Blood Dragon lineage of her father with the ancestry of Clan Scarlett. Her maternal grandmother, [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], is one of [[Nightingale]]'s most accomplished martial figures, while Siena belongs to the ancient family whose supernatural traditions influenced Victor's early development. Sylvia retains this genealogical and cultural connection to the Scarlett family, although her natural physiology differs fundamentally from that of the original Noble Vampires.[1]
Siena was originally a member of Clan Scarlett before becoming one of Victor's Blood Dragon wives. Sylvia was born after the establishment of their advanced Draconic lineage and consequently possesses the foundation of a genuine True Dragon Goddess rather than beginning life as a conventional Noble Vampire. Her existence therefore represents the continuation of the [[Scarlett Bloodline]] through Victor's naturally born descendants, combining the family's inherited characteristics with the extraordinary potential of the [[Blood Dragons]]. Although this ancestry provides a strong foundation for further supernatural development, it does not automatically establish that Sylvia possesses every specialized technique, elemental power or individual Divinity cultivated by Siena and [[Scathach]].
The descendants of the Scarlett family remain particularly close within Victor's enormous household, frequently spending time together and sharing several visible characteristics, especially red hair. Sylvia is closely associated with Rosemary and Priscillia, whose mothers belong to other branches of the same family. Their shared heritage strengthens their companionship, although their individual interests differ considerably, with Rosemary favoring martial pursuits and entertainment, Priscillia displaying an enthusiasm for technological experimentation, and Sylvia generally adopting a more cautious and practical approach to unfamiliar situations.

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Sylvia was born during the extraordinary period of accelerated development experienced by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his family following the establishment of his mature Blood Dragon lineage. As the daughter of Victor and Siena, she inherited the exceptional physical and supernatural characteristics of a naturally born True Dragon Goddess alongside the maternal ancestry of Clan Scarlett. Her upbringing occurred within the protected territories of the Elderblood family, where Victor and his wives provided extensive education intended to prepare their children for independent responsibilities. The daughters were taught to understand ordinary people, recognize the consequences of their actions and avoid relying exclusively upon their overwhelming inherited strength.[1]
As Sylvia and her sisters matured, they became increasingly interested in participating directly in the administration of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Their initial confidence came from the extensive knowledge and practical experience acquired during their education and earlier training exercises, but the scale of Victor's civilization quickly exceeded their expectations. Governing the Empire required planning across enormous populations and entire Galaxies, making even apparently simple decisions capable of influencing billions or trillions of individuals. Although the daughters possessed the intelligence necessary to understand complicated systems, they recognized that theoretical knowledge was not equivalent to experience governing a civilization of such extraordinary size.[1]
The difficulties initially discouraged several Princesses, but Valentina encouraged her sisters to continue learning rather than abandon their ambitions. The daughters agreed to develop at their own pace, understanding that Victor expected them to become capable and responsible individuals rather than simply inherit prestigious offices without preparation. Their education also emphasized the importance of appropriate public behavior, since the actions of an Imperial Princess could affect the reputation of both Victor and the Empire. Sylvia belonged to this group of daughters seeking greater responsibility while continuing to discover the practical limitations of their existing experience.[1]
Following their administrative activities, several daughters returned to their private quarters and resumed their natural appearances after using metamorphosis to imitate older versions of themselves or resemble their mothers. Victor discouraged excessive reliance upon such transformations because he considered each daughter beautiful in her own right and wanted them to preserve their individuality. During the gathering, Leone noticed a suspicious group of red-haired sisters observing something through an unusual supernatural barrier. The group included several daughters descended from the Scarlett family, with [[Yog-Sothoth]] having altered her hair to match the others while providing the spatial and Null-related concealment necessary to observe a protected location.[1]
Leone eventually discovered that the girls were watching Victor and [[Scathach]] training a newly born daughter. Although their observation involved exceptionally advanced supernatural abilities, Yog acknowledged that Victor was allowing them to watch and could prevent their access whenever he wished. Victor briefly looked toward the concealed observation point and smiled, causing the girls to become excited. Leone immediately regretted failing to record the gesture, declaring that such a valuable recording could have been exchanged with one of their mothers for favors. Sylvia blushed when challenged over whether she would do the same but did not deny it, revealing her own fascination with rare material concerning her father.[1]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Sylvia continued developing alongside her sisters as the [[Elderblood Empire]] underwent enormous technological and political expansion. She remained close to Rosemary and Priscillia, frequently spending time with them in the manga section of the Imperial castle's library. During one such gathering, Priscillia excitedly announced that Pepper had become involved in an important Imperial project and declared that she intended to participate as well. Sylvia was surprised that she had not heard about the development, prompting Rosemary to criticize her failure to read the Imperial information updates regularly. Sylvia dismissed the criticism, arguing that following a system constantly receiving new information was an unnecessary use of her time.[2]
Their conversation became more serious when Priscillia admitted that she did not know what Pepper's project involved. Sylvia immediately recognized that the absence of information indicated unusually strict confidentiality, since [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] normally allowed his daughters access to extensive material concerning Imperial administration and research. Projects whose effectiveness depended upon secrecy, including the Empire's most sensitive strategic undertakings, remained restricted even within the Imperial Family. Sylvia consequently questioned how Priscillia expected to participate without knowing the project's purpose or obtaining permission from its researchers. When Priscillia compared herself with Velina, Sylvia explained that Velina possessed specialized qualifications accumulated through years of working alongside Velnorah, making the two situations fundamentally different.[2]
Priscillia refused to abandon her objective and eventually became unusually silent while developing another approach. Sylvia and Rosemary recognized that such prolonged concentration was a warning sign, since their sister's extraordinary intellect allowed her to construct increasingly elaborate schemes whenever she became determined to accomplish something. Their concern grew until Priscillia suddenly announced that she had found a solution and disappeared. Although both considered remaining behind, they ultimately decided to follow her because neither was willing to abandon a sister who might be placing herself in danger. Victor observed their decision through his supernatural senses and privately appreciated their loyalty, even while recognizing that Priscillia was preparing to violate the privacy of one of his wives.[2]
Priscillia approached Pepper's private residence wearing a black infiltration outfit and used hundreds of Draconic Runes to circumvent the protective barriers surrounding the area. The defenses existed because Victor's daughters possessed supernatural perception and abilities powerful enough to make ordinary privacy difficult to maintain, and unauthorized entry into the private residences of their mothers was strictly forbidden. Sylvia and Rosemary arrived shortly afterward, immediately recognizing the danger of being discovered by Anna. Depending upon the circumstances, trespassing could result in an entire year of corrective training beneath [[Scathach]], whose demanding methods were feared by even the exceptionally powerful Imperial Princesses. Despite their apprehension, the two remained with Priscillia, who provided them with matching outfits through her Runic abilities before continuing the investigation.[2][3]
The sisters discovered that Pepper's private residence contained an enormous spatially expanded environment with forests, waterfalls, rivers and other natural features. Sylvia questioned why her aunt required such an extensive private space when the Imperial castle was already extraordinarily large, but Priscillia's curiosity drove them deeper into the residence. They eventually entered a concealed laboratory protected by additional dimensional barriers and discovered an enormous white sphere surrounded by scientific equipment and thousands of Runes. Sylvia located the associated research documentation through a nearby computer, revealing that Pepper was developing an artificial star as part of an experiment involving extraordinary stellar Energy.[3]
Pepper had already stabilized the artificial star and intended to continue supplying it with Energy until the completed experiment possessed an output comparable to approximately a billion exploding suns. The intended result astonished Sylvia, who had previously associated Pepper more strongly with culture and entertainment than advanced scientific research. The laboratory was protected by multiple dimensional containment layers designed to absorb the consequences of a catastrophic failure, while Rosemary examined the technological documentation and used the Imperial network to understand unfamiliar scientific terminology. Sylvia recognized that the experiment represented only one of Pepper's private projects and wondered what even more extraordinary research might be underway within the laboratories maintained by Velnorah, Aline and Ruby.[3]
Their exploration continued into another concealed facility known as the Room for Fun Projects, which contained experimental weapons, advanced armor, unusual crystals and functional recreations of devices inspired by fictional media. Priscillia became particularly excited upon discovering glowing plasma swords resembling lightsabers, while Rosemary examined the technical documentation and explained their actual mechanisms. The girls also found a concealed wrist-mounted blade incorporating Basilisk venom and supernatural petrification-related properties. Although Sylvia had initially objected to the infiltration, she now participated enthusiastically in examining the equipment and discovering the unexpected interests of her aunt.[3]
The most personally significant discovery occurred when Sylvia found an enormous archive of photographs and recordings documenting Victor's early supernatural life. She excitedly summoned her sisters, and the three began examining material depicting Victor during his years as a young Vampire, including his early activities in [[Nightingale]] and several important battles. The archive fascinated them because they had grown up knowing their father primarily as the extraordinarily powerful God Emperor rather than the comparatively inexperienced Vampire shown in the older recordings. Sylvia also noticed that Pepper retained an unusually youthful demeanor despite the immense passage of Time, contrasting her behavior with the more visibly maternal personalities of Siena, Ruby and Lacus.[3]
Their adventure ended when Anna discovered the unauthorized intrusion. Sylvia, Rosemary and Priscillia immediately feared being sent to Scathach for corrective training, while Priscillia attempted to justify their actions by arguing that Pepper should not have concealed so many interesting inventions. Pepper intervened and decided that the girls would receive approximately six months of additional lessons and study because they had not seriously damaged anything. All three accepted the punishment with considerable relief, regarding the additional academic work as exceptionally merciful compared with a year under Scathach. The incident demonstrated that Sylvia's practical judgment did not make her immune to curiosity or family mischief, particularly when her sisters were involved.[3]
Sylvia later became involved in another prank shortly before one of the Elderblood family's traditional communal dinners. After Velina completed her work alongside Velnorah and returned home, a group of daughters intercepted her and carried her away beneath a sack. The participants included several members of the Scarlett branch and Hinata, while Priscillia directed the group toward Sylvia's room. The incident was treated as a playful kidnapping rather than an act of genuine hostility, although Velina was understandably irritated by her sisters' behavior.[6]
The prank became apparent when Victor assembled his daughters for the family gathering and noticed that Velina was missing. She eventually appeared with a disheveled appearance and demanded that the girls responsible be punished. Victor immediately recognized that Velina possessed enough power to escape whenever she wished and understood that she had allowed the game to continue despite her complaints. After comforting her, he reminded the daughters that playful activities were acceptable so long as they did not deliberately harm one another. Sylvia reassured him that they would never intentionally injure their sister, while Silvia responded with a dismissive snort, emphasizing the similarity between their names and manner of speaking.[7][4]
Sylvia continued participating in the household's gatherings and educational activities, including the lessons Victor organized to explain the nature of Divinity and Reality. During a discussion concerning [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]], the youngest daughter of Victor and [[Tyche]], Victor distinguished her innate Eldritch Luck from the potentially separate favor she appeared to receive from the Universe. Rosemary compared Moira with a Mary Sue, while Priscillia likened her to the exceptionally fortunate heroines found in cultivation novels. Sylvia observed that Moira possessed even greater advantages because Reality itself accommodated her wishes, something that exceeded the privileges ordinarily granted to fictional protagonists.[5]
Sylvia wondered whether Moira might effectively be the protagonist of a story, recognizing that she possessed extraordinary parents, an exceptional Bloodline and the affection of the entire Universe. However, she also acknowledged that the other Elderblood daughters shared many similarly extraordinary circumstances through their relationship with Victor. The sisters ultimately agreed that their entire generation enjoyed opportunities and abilities beyond those available to ordinary supernatural beings, revealing Sylvia's awareness of the privileges associated with her upbringing while preserving her familiar interest in fictional entertainment and family conversation.[5]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Sylvia's biological father and one of the most important individuals in her life. Like many of his daughters, Sylvia possesses a strong interest in his personal history and the extraordinary accomplishments that preceded her birth. Her fascination becomes apparent when she blushes after Leone suggests exchanging a rare recording of Victor for favors from one of their mothers, and it becomes considerably more pronounced when she discovers Pepper's collection of photographs and recordings depicting his early years as a Vampire. These materials provide an opportunity to observe a period of Victor's life that Sylvia never personally experienced, making them particularly valuable to her.[1][3]
Sylvia also wishes to contribute to the civilization Victor established, participating in the educational activities intended to prepare his daughters for eventual responsibilities within the Empire. Although Victor possesses the power to protect his children from most threats, he expects them to acquire judgment and practical experience rather than relying exclusively upon their inherited abilities. His affection remains evident during the prank involving Velina, when he responds to the girls' mischief by reminding them of their responsibilities toward one another while reassuring them of his love. Sylvia's insistence that they would never intentionally harm their sister reflects the family solidarity Victor has encouraged throughout their upbringing.[1][4]

#### Siena Scarlett Elderblood
[[Siena Scarlett Elderblood]] is Sylvia's biological mother and the principal source of her maternal Scarlett heritage. Sylvia inherited the red hair characteristic of the family, while her natural Blood Dragon physiology reflects the advanced supernatural lineage established through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his wives. Although the novel provides relatively little detail concerning their private mother-daughter interactions, Sylvia clearly associates Siena with a mature and distinctly maternal personality. This becomes apparent when she compares her mother's demeanor with that of Pepper, whose playful behavior remains unusually youthful despite her immense chronological age.[3]

#### Scathach Elderblood Scarlett
[[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] is Sylvia's maternal grandmother and one of the most accomplished martial instructors within the Elderblood family. Her training methods are exceptionally demanding, and an extended period of corrective instruction beneath her is considered a severe punishment even by naturally born Dragon Goddesses. Sylvia's fear of this possibility becomes apparent when Anna discovers the unauthorized investigation of Pepper's residence, since the girls expect that a year under [[Scathach]] would be considerably worse than ordinary disciplinary lessons. Their relief when Pepper imposes a less severe punishment reflects Scathach's formidable reputation rather than any established hostility toward their grandmother.[3]

#### Priscillia Penny Elderblood
[[Priscillia Penny Elderblood]] is one of Sylvia's closest companions and a fellow descendant of the Scarlett lineage. Priscillia possesses an impulsive, energetic and intensely curious personality, frequently becoming involved in technological experiments and questionable schemes. Sylvia generally serves as a more practical voice, questioning her sister's decisions and warning her about the consequences of acting without sufficient preparation. Nevertheless, she is unwilling to abandon Priscillia when the latter decides to investigate Pepper's residence, accompanying her despite recognizing the risks. Their relationship combines exasperation, shared curiosity and a strong commitment to protecting one another.[2][3]

#### Rosemary Lavina Elderblood
[[Rosemary Lavina Elderblood]] is another of Sylvia's closest companions and the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Ruby. The two frequently spend time together, particularly within the Imperial castle's manga library, where they enjoy reading and discussing the latest developments throughout the Empire. Rosemary is more willing to examine scientific information and criticizes Sylvia for neglecting the constantly updated Imperial database, while Sylvia dismisses her sister's enthusiasm as excessively studious. Despite these differences, both recognize the danger of Priscillia's schemes and cooperate throughout the investigation of Pepper's residence. They later participate together in the discussion concerning Moira's extraordinary Luck, demonstrating their continuing companionship.[2][3][5]

#### Pepper Scarlett Elderblood
[[Pepper Scarlett Elderblood]] is Sylvia's maternal aunt and an important figure in her personal development. Sylvia initially associates Pepper with entertainment, culture and a comparatively playful personality, making the discovery of her private laboratories particularly surprising. The artificial-star experiment and the enormous collection of advanced technology reveal a level of scientific expertise that Sylvia had not previously appreciated, while the historical archive documenting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s early life becomes one of the most fascinating discoveries of the investigation. Pepper also protects Sylvia and her sisters from a more severe punishment after Anna discovers their trespassing, assigning six months of additional study instead of allowing them to be sent to [[Scathach]].[3]

#### Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III
[[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]] is Sylvia's sister and one of the Empire's exceptionally accomplished young researchers. Sylvia recognizes Velina's competence and the experience she accumulated through working alongside Velnorah, using her as an example of someone whose involvement in advanced Imperial research is supported by genuine qualifications. Their relationship also includes ordinary sibling mischief, as Sylvia becomes involved in the prank during which Velina is intercepted and carried away before a family gathering. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] questions the incident, Sylvia insists that the girls would never intentionally harm another member of the family, distinguishing their playful behavior from genuine hostility.[2][6][4]

#### Silvia Elderblood
[[Silvia Elderblood]] is Sylvia's sister through their common father and the biological daughter of [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]]. Although their names differ by only one letter, they possess distinct maternal lineages and inherited characteristics. Sylvia belongs to the red-haired Scarlett branch, while Silvia is associated with the Fulger lineage and its supernatural affinity for Lightning. The similarity becomes particularly apparent when Sylvia comments that the girls would never harm Velina and Silvia responds with a snort, with their voices and manner of speaking appearing remarkably alike despite their different names.[4]

#### Moira Eutychia Elderblood
[[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]] is Sylvia's younger sister and the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and [[Tyche]]. Moira possesses an extraordinary relationship with Luck and Reality, causing the Universe to favor her existence and respond directly to her sincere desires. Sylvia becomes fascinated by this ability during Victor's educational lesson, comparing Moira with fictional protagonists whose extraordinary good fortune allows them to overcome difficulties without ordinary limitations. She recognizes that Moira possesses even greater advantages because Reality itself accommodates her wishes, while also acknowledging that the other Elderblood daughters enjoy extraordinary privileges through their shared parentage.[5]

### Skills & Abilities
Sylvia possesses the extraordinary biological and supernatural characteristics of a naturally born Blood Dragon Goddess, combining the physical foundation of a genuine True Dragon with the advanced intellectual and sensory abilities associated with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s descendants. Her established appearances emphasize practical reasoning, perception, familiarity with Imperial technology and the capabilities broadly shared by the second generation rather than an individually developed combat specialty. The precise name and Authority of her personal Divine Concept remain unidentified, and abilities demonstrated exclusively by other members of the Scarlett family should not automatically be attributed to her.
- Blood Dragon Goddess Physiology: Sylvia was born as the biological daughter of Victor Elderblood and [[Siena Scarlett Elderblood]], inheriting the supernatural foundation of Victor's Blood Dragon lineage rather than undergoing a later transformation from an ordinary Race. Her existence combines genuine True Dragon physiology with Divine characteristics, providing the exceptional physical potential associated with the naturally born second generation.[1]
- True Dragon Physiology: Sylvia possesses the biological and spiritual foundation of a genuine True Dragon, including extraordinary natural strength, speed, reflexes, durability and stamina. Although these characteristics belong to her Race, her individual upper limits have not been established through a major personal battle.
- [[Draconic Energy]]: Her natural physiology supports the supernatural Energy associated with [[True Dragons]], including the Draconic biological foundation inherited from Victor. The exact properties of any individually specialized Energy attacks remain unidentified.
- [[Dragon Heart]]: Sylvia possesses the racial foundation associated with the Energy-generating systems of True Dragons, although the novel has not provided an individual examination of her Dragon Heart or its maximum output.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: As a naturally born Blood Dragon, Sylvia possesses the regenerative foundation associated with the lineage. Her personal regeneration has not been independently tested through a sufficiently severe injury to establish its limits.
- Supernatural Longevity: Sylvia possesses the extraordinary lifespan and developmental characteristics of True Dragons, allowing her to accumulate extensive experience while retaining a youthful Humanoid appearance.
- Draconic Transformation: Her True Dragon nature provides the racial foundation for transformation between Humanoid and Draconic characteristics. Her complete Dragon Form and its specialized features remain undescribed.
- Divine Physiology: Sylvia possesses genuine Divine characteristics as a naturally born Dragon Goddess and belongs to the generation of Imperial Princesses associated with the [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]].[1]
- Metamorphosis: Sylvia belongs to the generation of Blood Dragon daughters capable of altering their Humanoid appearances through supernatural transformation. The girls can imitate more mature versions of themselves or resemble their mothers, although Victor discourages excessive use of the ability because he wants them to retain their natural identities. These transformations concern physical appearance and should not automatically be interpreted as genuine Time-based biological aging.[1]
- Reality Manipulation (Shared Draconic Foundation): Victor's biological daughters possess access to Reality-altering techniques that are considered relatively simple by the standards of their Race. Such abilities can be used to modify the environment or create supernatural barriers, although the concealment employed during the gathering of red-haired sisters was specifically supported by [[Yog-Sothoth]]'s spatial and Null-related powers. Sylvia's independent maximum level of Reality manipulation has not been established through an individual demonstration.[1]
- Dragon Eyes: Sylvia possesses the characteristic violet eyes inherited by Victor's biological daughters, whose supernatural visual abilities can develop through training. Her lineage provides exceptional potential for enhanced perception, but no individual feat establishes that she possesses the complete truth-perceiving abilities of Victor or the particularly advanced eyesight demonstrated by certain sisters.
- Enhanced Supernatural Perception: Sylvia belongs to a family whose supernatural senses and abilities are sufficiently advanced that the personal residences of Victor and his wives require specialized privacy barriers. She understands the purpose of these defenses and the difficulties created by living among beings capable of observing or bypassing ordinary physical security, although her individual maximum sensory range remains unknown.[2]
- Exceptional Intelligence: Sylvia possesses the extraordinary cognitive foundation associated with naturally born True Dragons, allowing her to process information and understand complicated subjects far beyond ordinary Human limitations. She demonstrates practical reasoning when evaluating Priscillia's desire to join a classified research project, recognizes the importance of specialized qualifications and identifies the consequences of entering restricted areas without permission. Her ability to anticipate Priscillia's troublesome plans and recognize behavioral differences among members of the Scarlett family further demonstrates her observational skills.[2][3]
- Scientific and Technological Literacy: Although Sylvia has not demonstrated the same specialized research expertise as Ruby, Velnorah or Aline, she possesses sufficient education to understand the general significance of advanced Imperial technology. She locates Pepper's laboratory records through a computer and recognizes the extraordinary implications of the artificial-star experiment, including its intended Energy output and the importance of the dimensional systems surrounding it. Her participation demonstrates familiarity with scientific documentation without establishing that she personally possesses the expertise necessary to reproduce Pepper's experiments.[3]
- Pocket-Dimensional Storage (Imperial Equipment): Sylvia possesses access to the personal spatial-storage systems provided to Victor's daughters. These inventories function as private pocket dimensions in which clothing, equipment and personal belongings can be stored without conventional containers. The technology represents an advanced development of the dimensional-storage systems created through the achievements of Victor and Natalia, rather than proof that Sylvia personally possesses a specialized Space-related Divinity.[3]
- Imperial Education: Sylvia received the extensive education intended to prepare Victor's daughters for participation in government and the wider supernatural world. Her training includes understanding social relationships, the responsibilities attached to Imperial status and the consequences of decisions affecting enormous populations. She participates in the generation's attempts to become more involved in administration, recognizing that the scale of the Empire requires practical experience beyond ordinary theoretical knowledge.[1]
- Teamwork and Situational Judgment: Sylvia demonstrates an ability to cooperate with her sisters while remaining aware of the risks associated with their decisions. She initially discourages Priscillia's infiltration plan but ultimately accompanies her alongside Rosemary because she refuses to abandon a family member who may be placing herself in danger. Her behavior reflects the family solidarity cultivated by Victor and his wives, although the incident does not establish a specialized combat technique.[2][3]
- Scarlett Heritage: Through Siena and [[Scathach]], Sylvia belongs to the Scarlett lineage and possesses the natural potential associated with its martial traditions. However, her ancestry does not independently establish mastery over Siena's complete combat abilities, Scathach's personal Divinities or every specialized elemental technique historically associated with Clan Scarlett. Such abilities require individual demonstrations or explicit statements before they can be assigned to Sylvia.
- Personal Divinity (Unidentified): Sylvia is a Dragon Goddess, but the exact name, conceptual Authority and individual applications of her personal Divine Concept have not been revealed. Her biological relationship with Victor and Siena provides an extraordinary foundation for conceptual development, but it does not establish unrestricted control over particular Concepts or the possession of abilities demonstrated exclusively by her parents and siblings.

### Status as an Imperial Princess
Sylvia's position as an Imperial Princess carries responsibilities extending far beyond the prestige associated with her ancestry. As the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and a high-ranking Goddess belonging to the [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]], she occupies an important position within the political and religious structure of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Her actions can influence how Imperial citizens perceive the Emperor and his administration, particularly because the Empire governs enormous populations distributed across worlds, Solar Systems and Galactic-scale territories. Victor and his wives therefore expect their daughters to maintain appropriate public behavior, respect the responsibilities attached to their status and avoid treating weaker individuals as unimportant merely because of differences in supernatural power.[1]
The daughters receive extensive education intended to prepare them for these responsibilities, including instruction concerning social relationships, political organization and the practical consequences of government decisions. Sylvia participates in this system and belongs to the generation that attempted to become more directly involved in Imperial administration, only to discover that theoretical education alone was insufficient for managing a civilization of such extraordinary scale. Her practical attitude is particularly evident when she recognizes that specialized scientific positions require genuine competence and that even members of the Imperial Family cannot reasonably expect unrestricted access to every classified project. Although Sylvia has not been assigned a confirmed permanent administrative or military office, her education and position provide the foundation for future responsibilities within the Empire.[1][2]

### Trivia
- Sylvia is the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Siena Scarlett Elderblood]], making her one of the naturally born second-generation [[Blood Dragons]]. She is also a granddaughter of [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] through Siena and retains the distinctive red hair associated with the Scarlett family.[1]
- Sylvia possesses a practical personality and is particularly close to Rosemary and Priscillia. Although she initially discourages their more impulsive decisions, her loyalty frequently causes her to participate in the resulting trouble.[2][3]
- She belongs to the group of red-haired daughters who secretly observe Victor and [[Scathach]] training a newly born sister. When Leone suggests exchanging a rare recording of Victor for favors from one of their mothers, Sylvia blushes without denying that she would be interested in doing the same.[1]
- Sylvia does not regularly follow every Imperial information update because she considers the constantly changing database exhausting. Rosemary and Priscillia criticize her for this habit, while Sylvia dismisses them as nerds.[2]
- Sylvia recognizes that Priscillia's desire to participate in Pepper's classified project is unreasonable without knowing its purpose or possessing appropriate qualifications. Nevertheless, she accompanies her sister when Priscillia decides to investigate the project without permission.[2][3]
- During the investigation, Sylvia discovers Pepper's research records concerning an artificial-star experiment intended to eventually achieve an output comparable to approximately a billion exploding suns. The girls also examine experimental equipment and uncover an extensive archive of photographs and recordings documenting Victor's early supernatural life.[3]
- Sylvia notices that Pepper retains an unusually youthful personality despite her immense chronological age, contrasting her demeanor with the more visibly maternal behavior of Siena, Ruby and Lacus. Her observation reflects the attention she pays to subtle differences among members of her family.[3]
- Sylvia, Rosemary and Priscillia receive approximately six months of additional lessons after Anna discovers their unauthorized intrusion into Pepper's residence. They regard the punishment as merciful compared with the possibility of an entire year of corrective training beneath Scathach.[3]
- Sylvia becomes involved in the prank during which Velina is intercepted and carried away shortly before a family gathering. She later reassures Victor that the girls would never intentionally harm another sister, reflecting the distinction between playful mischief and genuine hostility.[6][4]
- Sylvia is distinct from [[Silvia Elderblood]], the biological daughter of Victor and Sasha. The sisters possess nearly identical names but belong to different maternal lineages, with Sylvia associated with Clan Scarlett and Silvia with Clan Fulger.[4]
- During Victor's lesson concerning Moira's extraordinary Luck, Sylvia compares her youngest sister with excessively fortunate protagonists from cultivation novels. She acknowledges that Moira possesses exceptional privileges because the Universe itself accommodates her wishes, while also recognizing the extraordinary advantages shared by Victor's other daughters.[5]
- Sylvia's individual Divine Concept, complete Dragon Form, exact physical measurements and specialized combat techniques remain unidentified. Her established abilities should be distinguished from powers demonstrated only by other members of the Scarlett family or by groups of sisters acting together.
