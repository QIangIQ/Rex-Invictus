Ariella Victoria Elderblood is an Imperial Princess of the [[Elderblood Empire]], the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Ariel Elderblood]], and the maternal granddaughter of the Heavenly Father. A natural-born True Dragon and Dragon Goddess, Ariella inherited Ariel's Heavenly characteristics together with Victor's Elderblood lineage, producing an unusually compatible existence capable of containing numerous unrelated Divine Concepts without the limitations that would normally accompany them.[1]
Ariella was born possessing all Seven Virtues of Heaven as genuine Divine Concepts: Chastity, Charity, Temperance, Diligence, Patience, Benevolence and Humility. Chapter 1149 initially described her Creator-related inheritance as possessing traces of Victor's Creation Divinity, but Chapter 1177 later clarified the true scale of that inheritance: Ariella was actually born with the Concept of Creation itself and possesses the potential to additionally develop the Concept of Life.[2]
Her birth was also considerably stranger than originally known. During Ariel's pregnancy, two Soul-like presences were detected, initially leading Victor and the others to believe Ariel was carrying twins. The two eventually merged into a single developing child and Ariella's power increased. Later investigation revealed that the apparent "other Soul" had not been another living child at all; it was connected to the power of Creation itself, preparing Ariella as a compatible host.[2] This makes Ariella's conceptual inheritance fundamentally different from merely learning or receiving a Divine ability later in life.
Despite her extraordinary Divine foundation, Ariella is highly curious, playful and comparatively innocent. She possesses enormous natural intelligence and an exceptional aptitude for supernatural systems, famously rewriting significant portions of Heaven's ancient Celestial System during her first visit and improving technology originally created by the Heavenly Father himself.[1] The feat caused her grandfather to consider Ariella a possible successor, although Ariel immediately made clear that Ariella would be allowed to choose her own future.

### Physical Appearance
Ariella strongly resembles Ariel in facial structure while inheriting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s black hair and violet eyes. When she first appears alongside Ariel, the narration describes her as closely resembling her mother except for those distinctly Elderblood characteristics.[1]
Her violet eyes respond visibly to heightened emotion. Normally they possess the characteristic violet coloration found throughout Victor's descendants, but they can glow much more intensely when Ariella becomes excited or agitated. During her argument with the [[Heavenly Father]] concerning Victor's superiority, her eyes became increasingly deep violet and eventually darkened enough for her grandfather to decide that continuing the disagreement was unwise.[1]
Ariella is not an ordinary Angel despite her strongly Heavenly appearance and powers. Her fundamental biological foundation is that of a True Dragon. The Heavenly Father nevertheless regarded her as approaching the idea of a perfect Angel because of her extraordinary purity, complete collection of Heavenly Virtues and inheritance from Ariel.[1]

### Personality
Ariella is extraordinarily curious, intelligent, innocent and emotionally transparent. The [[Heavenly Father]] was unable to detect even the slightest trace of wickedness within her and compared her curiosity to that of a newly born Angel discovering the world. Even that comparison was imperfect because Angels are normally born possessing considerable knowledge, while Ariella approached unfamiliar things with genuine wonder and a desire to understand them herself.[1]
Her innocence should not be confused with passivity. Ariella becomes bored easily, is impatient and possesses the casual attitude toward destruction common among several members of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family. While travelling through Heaven's seemingly endless clouds, she became bored enough to ask Ariel whether she could use Dragon Breath to blast a hole through the Realm toward [[Yggdrasil]] simply to make the surroundings more interesting.[1] Ariel was particularly exasperated by the fact that her daughter literally possessed the Divine Concept of Patience while displaying very little practical patience.
Ariella's behavior demonstrates that possessing the Seven Virtues does not force her personality into an Angelic behavioral pattern. The Virtues have been integrated into her True Dragon existence as actual Concepts. Ariel explicitly reasons that even if Ariella somehow became completely evil, she would still retain them because [[True Dragons]] possess extraordinary compatibility with the powers of existence.[1]
She possesses an intense attachment to Victor and considers her father the standard against which other powerful beings are measured. When she met the Heavenly Father, one of her first genuine questions was why he appeared so weak compared with Victor. The statement was not intended as an insult; Ariella sincerely wanted to understand the difference.[1]
Her loyalty to Victor becomes much more forceful when someone claims superiority over him. Ariella enthusiastically admired the Heavenly Father's Creation abilities until he claimed that even Victor could not surpass him in Creation. She immediately objected, citing Victor's daughters, other Beings he had created and entire worlds as evidence before refusing even the diplomatic compromise that both men could simply be considered equally impressive.[1]
Despite this devotion, Ariella is notably less extreme than some of her sisters. During Kali's visit to the [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]], she mentally classified [[Kassandra Kashvi Elderblood]] among the daughters irrationally protective of Victor and considered Ariella one of the daughters who would have been a considerably less extreme travelling companion.[3]
Ariella also displays genuine concern and affection toward her sisters. During the gathering surrounding [[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]] in Chapter 1165, she was one of the daughters who questioned whether they should be concerned by what was happening and warmly greeted [[Erika Vesta Elderblood]] when she arrived.[4] Her more childlike side remains obvious around Victor; when she, [[Helvarya Elderblood]] and [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]] encountered him in Chapter 1177, Ariella became visibly envious when Moira received his affection and immediately joined her sisters when Victor offered them a family hug.[2]
Ariella's greatest intellectual trait is her instinctive desire to understand and improve things. She does not merely admire supernatural feats based upon their scale. When the Heavenly Father created living animals, what impressed her was his absolute precision and near-total absence of wasted Energy. Likewise, when she encountered Heaven's Celestial System, her first reaction after understanding it was to identify obsolete components and begin replacing them.[1]

### Synopsis

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Ariella first appears when [[Ariel Elderblood]] returns to [[Biblical Heaven]] to meet the [[Heavenly Father]]. The visit formed part of the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s broader effort to prepare its allied Pantheons for the coming conflict with civilizations from the higher regions of Reality. Ariella accompanied her mother not as a diplomatic official but as the Heavenly Father's granddaughter and one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Imperial Princesses.[1]
The narration formally introduces her as Ariella Victoria Elderblood, explaining that Ariel selected the name Ariella while Victor gave her the name Victoria. She strongly resembles Ariel but inherited Victor's black hair and violet eyes. More importantly, she had been born possessing all Seven Heavenly Virtues—Chastity, Charity, Temperance, Diligence, Patience, Benevolence and Humility—as Divine Concepts. She also possessed what was then described as traces of Victor's Creator-related Divinity.[1]
Ariella quickly became dissatisfied with Heaven's endless clouds and Light, openly stating that she preferred her own home and missed Victor. Her boredom escalated until she contemplated using Dragon Breath to create a path through the Realm toward [[Yggdrasil]]. Ariel ordered her to remain patient, only to privately question how a daughter carrying all Seven Virtues could behave so inconsistently with them. She concluded that Ariella's True Dragon nature was responsible: the Race's compatibility allowed Ariella to retain the Virtues independently of whether her personality actually embodied them.[1]
Upon meeting the Heavenly Father, Ariella innocently asked why her grandfather appeared so weak compared with Victor. Ariel immediately reprimanded her, but the Heavenly Father understood that no malice was involved. Examining Ariella directly, he could detect essentially no wickedness within her and concluded from her personality that Victor and Ariel had raised her with enormous affection.[1]
The Heavenly Father explained that fighting was not his true specialty and demonstrated his talent for Creation by creating a living swallow. Ariella immediately recognized that the important part of the demonstration was not simply creating Life but performing the act with effectively perfect Energy control and almost no waste. She openly admitted that she could not reproduce that degree of precision and became increasingly enthusiastic as he continued creating animals until the simple house resembled a miniature Garden of Eden.[1]
The pleasant exchange turned into an argument when the Heavenly Father claimed that even Victor could not surpass his mastery of Creation. Ariella immediately rejected the idea and passionately defended her father, citing Victor's creation of beings such as [[Akasha Elderblood]], [[Metis]] Elderblood and [[Melida Charlotte Elderblood]], as well as other creations and planets. Even after acknowledging her grandfather as impressive, Ariella refused to accept a compromise in which both men were considered equally great. Her eyes darkened increasingly as she insisted Victor was better, eventually causing the Heavenly Father to concede rather than continue arguing with his granddaughter.[1]
Ariella then discovered the Celestial System responsible for managing Heaven. The Heavenly Father isolated the system from the Realm itself so she could interact with it without accidentally destabilizing Heaven. What began as curiosity rapidly transformed into a complete technical overhaul. Ariella criticized portions of its architecture as ancient, altered multiple components and eventually progressed far enough that she was effectively constructing new source code from scratch.[1]
The Heavenly Father was genuinely surprised when the resulting system proved substantially better than the original. The incident demonstrated not that he lacked intelligence, but that Heaven had stagnated while the Elderblood Empire had spent immense periods constantly refining its technology, [[Magic]] and supernatural infrastructure. After observing Ariella's talent, the Heavenly Father concluded that her potential was extraordinary and that she could make a suitable successor. Ariel refused to decide on Ariella's behalf, explaining that her daughter would forge her own path.[1]
Ariel's actual diplomatic mission then continued. She transferred Imperial research regarding Angel biology and further Divine development to the Heavenly Father and explained the scale of the enemies for which Victor was preparing. During this visit, Victor had secretly deployed members of the Imperial Guard and other supernatural subordinates to protect both Ariel and Ariella despite their considerable personal strength, reflecting his continued refusal to leave members of his family unprotected during diplomatic travel.[3]
Ariella later participated in the enormous gathering of Victor's daughters surrounding the incident with Velina. She was initially somewhat concerned by what she witnessed, while [[Valentina Victoria Elderblood]] dismissed the situation as ordinary family trouble. Ariella warmly greeted [[Erika Vesta Elderblood]] when she arrived, after which the number of gathered Imperial Princesses continued increasing until more than thirty-five daughters were present.[4]
By Chapter 1167, Victor explained another inheritance possessed throughout his daughters. The ability originating from his own perception of Truth allows his descendants, to differing degrees, to potentially perceive and eventually manipulate the deeper "source code" of Reality once they understand the Rules involved. [[Norabell Elderblood]] and Melida were specifically identified as daughters in whom this inheritance manifested especially strongly, while Victor stated that the potential exists throughout his daughters even when initially weaker.[5] Ariella has not yet individually demonstrated this form of Reality perception, so it remains inherited potential rather than one of her confirmed mastered techniques.
Ariella later attended Victor's celestial family banquet after the Star Beast incident. Victor transported the family to their personal superplanet and reshaped stars, supernovae, planets, nebulae and other astronomical phenomena into furniture, clothing and decorations. Ariella, remembering Heaven, privately concluded that the spectacle was considerably more interesting than her grandfather's white clouds.[6]
Further discussion of Victor's descendants in Chapter 1171 clarified the broader nature of Ariella's generation. Jeanne described Victor's daughters as carrying both Eldritch and primordial Divine characteristics, while [[True Dragons]] themselves were explained as embodiments of nature whose compatibility with supernatural powers greatly exceeds that of ordinary Dragons. The True Dragon Race remained restricted primarily to the Emperor's immediate family.[7]
Chapter 1177 finally revealed the full nature of Ariella's birth. Victor reflected upon Ariella and [[Helvarya Elderblood]], explaining that both pregnancies had originally appeared to involve twins. In Ariella's case, two Soul-like presences were detected before eventually merging into one, strengthening the resulting child. Later investigation revealed that the apparent second Soul was not another living being but a manifestation associated with the Concept of Creation itself, preparing its compatible host.[2]
The same revelation corrected the earlier understanding of Ariella's Creator inheritance. Rather than merely possessing traces of Victor's Creation Divinity, Ariella had been born with the Concept of Creation and additionally inherited the potential to possess the Concept of Life. Helvarya represented a parallel phenomenon through Death and END, making both daughters unusually conceptually developed even within Victor's already abnormal second generation.[2]
Shortly afterward, Ariella, Helvarya and Moira found Victor sitting near a waterfall after the confrontation involving the Primordial Beings. Moira immediately monopolized their father's attention, leaving Ariella and Helvarya visibly envious. Victor invited all three into his arms, and Ariella immediately abandoned any attempt to behave like an adult Imperial Princess and enthusiastically joined the family hug.[2]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Ariella's biological father and the individual she admires above everyone else. Victor's position in her worldview is so dominant that even the [[Heavenly Father]] is instinctively measured against him. She is capable of sincerely recognizing another Being's superior technique in a particular field, but becomes uncompromising when that recognition is turned into a claim that the other Being is fundamentally superior to Victor.[1]
Her attachment remains highly affectionate rather than purely ideological. Ariella openly misses Victor while travelling with Ariel, becomes jealous when another sister receives his attention and eagerly joins his embraces when offered.[1][2]
Victor reciprocates that attachment through the extreme protectiveness he extends toward his daughters. Even when Ariel and Ariella travelled through allied territory, he covertly assigned Imperial forces and supernatural subordinates to watch over them.[3]

#### Ariel Elderblood
[[Ariel Elderblood]] is Ariella's biological mother and the source of her Heavenly heritage. Ariella inherited Ariel's facial appearance and the Seven Virtues associated with Heaven, although those Concepts were integrated into a True Dragon foundation rather than producing an ordinary Angel.[1]
Ariel acts as the more disciplined parent during their visit to Heaven, repeatedly correcting Ariella's impatience, bluntness and destructive impulses. Nevertheless, she strongly protects her daughter's autonomy. When the [[Heavenly Father]] considered Ariella as a possible successor, Ariel immediately stated that the decision would belong entirely to Ariella because neither she nor [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] intended to force a predetermined path upon their children.[1]

#### Heavenly Father
The [[Heavenly Father]] is Ariella's maternal grandfather. Their first meeting begins awkwardly when Ariella immediately asks why he seems weak compared with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], but he quickly recognizes that the question originates from innocence rather than disrespect.[1]
Ariella becomes genuinely impressed once he demonstrates his extraordinary Creation control. She enthusiastically praises him as an impressive grandfather and openly admits that his Energy precision exceeds her own current ability. Their only real disagreement occurs when he claims superiority to Victor in Creation.[1]
After Ariella rewrites and improves large portions of Heaven's Celestial System, the Heavenly Father concludes that she possesses enough potential to become a suitable successor. No such succession has actually occurred, and Ariella has not accepted an office within Heaven.[1]

#### Helvarya Elderblood
[[Helvarya Elderblood]] is one of Ariella's paternal half-sisters and possesses a particularly notable parallel to her birth. Both Ariella and Helvarya were originally believed to have twin siblings because two Soul-like presences appeared during their mothers' pregnancies, only for those presences to merge before birth. Later investigation revealed the abnormal secondary presence was conceptual rather than another ordinary living child.[2]
Ariella emerged with Creation and potential toward Life, while Helvarya was born with Death and END. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] consequently regards both daughters as having experienced unusually conceptual births even by Elderblood standards.[2]

### Skills & Abilities
- True Dragon Physiology: Ariella is a natural-born True Dragon and one of the Emperor's immediate descendants. She was born with immense strength, and her Draconic existence is explicitly capable of containing powers that would normally impose much stricter limitations upon other species.[1][7]
- Nature Embodiment: [[True Dragons]] are described as embodiments of nature itself rather than merely powerful members of a Dragon species.[7]
- Universal Power Compatibility: The True Dragon Race possesses extraordinary compatibility with supernatural powers. This compatibility is explicitly why Ariella can retain all Seven Virtues regardless of whether her behavior actually corresponds to them.[1][7]
- Enhanced Physical Abilities: Ariella was explicitly born with immense strength like [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s other daughters.[1]
- Dragon Breath: Ariella directly treats Dragon Breath as one of her usable abilities when considering blasting through Heaven toward [[Yggdrasil]].[1]
- [[Draconic Energy]]: As a natural True Dragon, Ariella possesses the supernatural Energy foundation of Victor's Draconic lineage.
- Extreme Longevity: Ariella's True Dragon and Divine existence is not bound by an ordinary mortal lifespan.
- Eldritch and Primordial Divine Heritage: Jeanne later describes Victor's daughters as possessing both Eldritch Goddess and primordial Divine characteristics inherited through Victor's evolved existence.[7] This should not be interpreted as proof that Ariella is currently an independent Primordial Entity; it describes the extraordinary foundation carried by Victor's second generation.
- Seven Virtues of Heaven: Ariella possesses all Seven Heavenly Virtues as genuine Divine Concepts. They are permanently integrated into her existence rather than temporary blessings or behavioral rewards.[1]
- Chastity: One of Ariella's innate Heavenly Divine Concepts.[1]
- Charity: One of Ariella's innate Heavenly Divine Concepts.[1]
- Temperance: One of Ariella's innate Heavenly Divine Concepts.[1]
- Diligence: One of Ariella's innate Heavenly Divine Concepts.[1]
- Patience: One of Ariella's innate Heavenly Divine Concepts despite her frequently impatient behavior.[1]
- Benevolence: One of Ariella's innate Heavenly Divine Concepts.[1]
- Humility: One of Ariella's innate Heavenly Divine Concepts despite her absolute confidence in Victor.[1]
- Conceptual Empowerment: The Virtues function as Divine Concepts that empower Ariella rather than existing merely as moral characteristics.[1]
- Concept Retention: Ariella does not need to behave according to a Virtue in order to continue possessing it. Ariel explicitly concludes that even if Ariella became evil, the Seven Virtues would remain part of her because of her True Dragon nature.[1]
- Multi-Concept Compatibility: Ariella naturally contains all Seven Virtues simultaneously alongside Creation, demonstrating the exceptional capacity of her Draconic existence to integrate multiple Divine Concepts.[1][2]
- Creation: Chapter 1177 clarifies that Ariella was born possessing the Concept of Creation.[2] This supersedes the earlier Chapter 1149 description that she merely carried traces of Victor's Creator Divinity.[1]
- Conceptual Host: During Ariel's pregnancy, an apparent second Soul eventually merged with Ariella. Later investigation revealed that this presence was not another living child but was connected to Creation itself preparing a suitable host.[2]
- Creation Affinity: Ariella possesses extremely high natural compatibility with Creator-related abilities and immediately recognizes advanced Creation techniques when observing them.[1][2]
- Creation Analysis: When the Heavenly Father creates Life, Ariella immediately identifies his near-perfect Energy efficiency as the most exceptional aspect of the technique rather than being distracted solely by the visible result.[1]
- Developing Mastery: Ariella openly admits in Chapter 1149 that she cannot yet reproduce the Heavenly Father's level of Energy control when creating living creatures.[1]
- Life (Latent Potential): Chapter 1177 states that Ariella inherited the potential to also possess the Concept of Life.[2] This is currently potential rather than a demonstrated mastered Divinity, and Life Manipulation should not be treated as a confirmed active ability until Ariella manifests it directly.
- Extreme Spiritual Purity: The Heavenly Father examined Ariella directly and could not detect even the slightest trace of wickedness within her.[1] This is an established characteristic of her Soul and personality, not proof of immunity to every form of corruption or spiritual attack.
- Exceptional Intelligence: Ariella possesses extraordinarily high analytical ability and can understand unfamiliar Divine and technological systems with exceptional speed.[1]
- Accelerated Learning: She advances from initial curiosity about Heaven's Celestial System to meaningful reconstruction almost immediately.[1]
- Technical Analysis: Ariella rapidly identifies obsolete architecture and inefficient components in supernatural systems she has never previously studied.[1]
- Energy Analysis: She can recognize the absence of wasted Energy in the Heavenly Father's Creation technique and understand why that degree of precision is difficult to reproduce.[1]
- Comparative Analysis: Ariella naturally compares unfamiliar supernatural methods against the knowledge and creations she has already encountered within the [[Elderblood Empire]].[1]
- Supernatural Programming / System Manipulation: Ariella possesses exceptional aptitude for manipulating code-based Divine infrastructure.[1]
- Source-Code Analysis: She can inspect the underlying architecture of Heaven's Celestial System and understand how its functions interact.[1]
- Source-Code Rewriting: Ariella's alterations became extensive enough that she was effectively writing a new source code from scratch.[1]
- System Optimization: Her modifications genuinely improved the Celestial System enough to surprise the Heavenly Father, its original creator.[1]
- Legacy-System Modernization: Ariella immediately identified functions she considered ancient or obsolete and began replacing them with more efficient structures.[1]
- Divine Infrastructure Manipulation: The system she altered was responsible for managing Heaven itself, demonstrating that her programming talent applies to supernatural administrative infrastructure rather than only ordinary technology.[1]
- Safe-System Experimentation: The Heavenly Father separated the Celestial System from Heaven before allowing Ariella to experiment, preventing her changes from accidentally damaging the Realm while she learned.[1]
- Latent Truth Perception / Reality Manipulation Potential: Victor explains in Chapter 1167 that his daughters inherit, to different degrees, the potential for the perception he describes as seeing the "source code of Reality". Those in whom the inheritance is highly developed can perceive and interfere with dimensions and structures created according to the Rules they understand.[5] Ariella has not yet individually demonstrated this ability, so it should presently be treated as inherited developmental potential rather than a mastered power.
- Potential Successor to the Heavenly Father: After observing Ariella's Virtues, purity, Creation inheritance and extraordinary ability to improve Heaven's Celestial System, the Heavenly Father concluded that she could become a suitable successor.[1]
- Unclaimed Position: Ariella has not inherited the Heavenly Father's position or accepted responsibility for Heaven.
- Free Choice: Ariel explicitly states that Ariella will forge her own path and that neither her parents nor the Heavenly Father will determine her future for her.[1]

### Limitations
- Developing Creation Mastery: Possessing the Concept of Creation does not mean Ariella has already mastered every possible application of it. As of Chapter 1149, she openly acknowledges that the Heavenly Father's precision exceeds her own.[1]
- Life Remains Potential: Ariella has the potential to possess Life, but Chapter 1177 does not establish that she has already manifested or mastered the Concept.[2]
- Inherited Reality Perception Remains Undemonstrated: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] states that all his daughters possess the potential to develop his Truth-related perception, but Ariella has not yet displayed the advanced source-code manipulation demonstrated by specialists such as [[Norabell Elderblood]] or [[Melida Charlotte Elderblood]].[5]
- Impulsiveness: Ariella can become impatient or bored quickly and sometimes considers excessively destructive solutions to trivial inconveniences.[1]
- Father-Centered Bias: Ariella's extreme admiration for Victor makes comparisons involving her father highly subjective. She can objectively recognize another Being's superior technique but strongly resists conclusions that place them above Victor overall.[1]
