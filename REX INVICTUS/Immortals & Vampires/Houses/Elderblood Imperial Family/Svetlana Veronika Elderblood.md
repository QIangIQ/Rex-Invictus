### Physical Appearance
Svetlana has blonde hair and violet eyes. During the gathering of the Fulger-line sisters in Chapter 1165, she was one of the daughters noted as resembling [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] more strongly than her mother.[2]
The narration occasionally refers to her by her middle name, Veronika, including when comparing her appearance with Victor's.[2]

### Personality
Svetlana has shown a competitive side through her participation in the Fulger family's ongoing rivalry surrounding speed and combat technique. The competition is treated by the family as a friendly method of forcing each generation to continue improving rather than as genuine hostility.[1]
Beyond this competitiveness and her comfortable interactions with her sisters, Svetlana's individual personality has not yet been explored in significant detail.

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Before her name was revealed, Svetlana appeared in Chapter 1056 as Victoria's Daughter during the explanation of the Fulger family's friendly competition over speed and technique.
Among the younger Fulger generation, Sasha's daughter was considered the most capable in the combined categories of speed and technique. Victoria's daughter and Carmila's daughter occupied second place and were described as practically equal in those areas. The competition reflected the Fulger family's tendency to turn their famous obsession with speed into a method of continuous improvement.[1]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Svetlana's complete identity was revealed in Chapter 1165 when she joined a group of blonde Elderblood daughters gathering around [[Valentina Victoria Elderblood]]. The narration formally identified her as Svetlana Veronika Elderblood, daughter of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Victoria.[2]
She appeared beside Ekaterina Natasha Elderblood, [[Julia Elderblood]], [[Alisa Sasha Elderblood]] and other members of the extended Fulger family. All of them had blonde hair and violet eyes. Svetlana—referred to as Veronika during the physical comparison—was specifically stated to resemble Victor more closely, much like Alisa.[2]
She subsequently remained among the more than thirty-five daughters gathered together while Valentina explained the situation to the family.[2]

### Skills and Abilities
- Blood Dragon God Physiology: As [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s biological daughter, Svetlana belongs to the later generation of Blood Dragon Goddesses within Clan Elderblood.
- Exceptional Speed: Even before her name was revealed, Victoria's daughter was already ranked among the most capable Fulger daughters in terms of speed. She and Carmila's daughter occupied second place behind Sasha's daughter during the family comparison.[1]
- Combat Technique: Svetlana's technical ability was ranked alongside her speed, placing her jointly in second place among the Fulger daughters discussed in Chapter 1056.[1]
- Fulger Heritage: Through [[Victoria Fulger Elderblood]], Svetlana belongs directly to the Fulger bloodline and participates in its long-standing culture of developing extreme speed. Her exact inherited supernatural ability has not yet been individually detailed.
- Divinity: Svetlana's personal Divine Concept has not yet been revealed.

### Trivia
- Svetlana was originally referred to simply as Victoria's Daughter before receiving her full canonical name.[1][2]
- She is one of the daughters associated with the Fulger family's friendly competition over the title and standards surrounding extreme speed.[1]
- Chapter 1165 refers to her as Veronika when describing which daughters resemble [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] most closely.[2]
- Her mother, Victoria, belongs to the same extended Fulger family as Sasha, Natashia, Natasha and Carmila.
