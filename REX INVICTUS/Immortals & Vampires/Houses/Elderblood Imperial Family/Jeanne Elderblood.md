#### Debut
Chapter 330: Betrayals

Jeanne Elderblood, formerly known as Jeanne d'Arc and Jeanne Alucard, is an extraordinarily ancient Primordial Spirit, the original Guardian and self-recognized younger sister of [[The Universal Tree]], a former Saint of the [[Heavenly Father]], a former Queen of [[Nightingale]], one of [[Victor Elderblood]]'s wives, and the commander of the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s Sixth Imperial Legion, [[The Silent Judgment]]. Although she presently possesses the physical vessel of a Blood Dragon, her true identity is that of a primordial spiritual Being whose existence predates the established form of the current Universe.[1][2][8][3][4]
Unlike an ordinary Spirit born from the supernatural development of a planet or World Tree, Jeanne was created through the actions of [[Primordial Chaos]] and entrusted with the protection of a seed that would eventually develop into the Universal Tree. The responsibility connected her to one of the most fundamental Beings of Creation before the establishment of the civilizations and supernatural systems that would later govern the Universe. Her relationship with the Tree eventually developed beyond that of Guardian and protected Being, with the two recognizing one another as family after the Tree became conscious and attempted to grant Jeanne's wish for companionship.[1][2][5]
Jeanne's connection to the Universal Tree is extraordinary because the Tree is responsible for maintaining Life and Reincarnation throughout the Universe, while Jeanne was specifically created to protect him. Her Guardian position grants her access to exceptionally pure [[Primordial Energy]] and establishes a direct relationship with the supernatural foundations of Creation. Nevertheless, Jeanne is not herself the Primordial Entity governing Life or Reincarnation. Her authority, spiritual foundation and relationship with the Tree must be distinguished from the complete conceptual responsibilities held by her brother.[2][9]
Jeanne's identity changed dramatically over the course of her existence. After losing much of her original memory, she wandered through the developing world and eventually encountered the Human girl whose destiny was to become Jeanne d'Arc, the Maid of Orléans. Recognizing the suffering awaiting the child, the ancient Spirit voluntarily took the girl's Fate upon herself and assumed her name. She subsequently fought for France, became recognized as a Saint, and was condemned to execution after being betrayed by the people she had fought to protect. Her survival was secured through the intervention of [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]], who saved her and later transformed her physical vessel into a Noble Vampire.[1]
Jeanne married Vlad and became the Fourth [[Vampire Queen]] of Nightingale, giving birth to their son, [[Adam Tepes]]. However, her marriage eventually deteriorated because of Vlad's increasing emotional absence and his dedication to political responsibilities at the expense of his family. After spending a prolonged period in inactivity, Jeanne awakened alongside [[Morgana Elderblood]] and rejected the marriage that had left both women neglected. Their separation from Vlad brought Jeanne into contact with Victor, whose influence eventually allowed her to recover the memories and powers associated with her forgotten existence.[10][11][1]
Victor's involvement became especially important because Jeanne's Soul and her physical body were fundamentally different. As a Higher-Level Spirit, Jeanne could inhabit a body belonging to another Race without her essential nature being completely transformed into that Race. Her original Human and later Vampire vessels therefore served as physical manifestations rather than defining the entirety of her existence. Victor's Progenitor Blood improved her Vampire body but could not overwrite the ancient Spirit, whose spiritual quality far exceeded that of the flesh she inhabited.[2][7][9]
The transformation that eventually allowed Jeanne to recover much of her former power occurred when Victor gave her a genuine True Dragon vessel. Unlike her earlier bodies, the new Draconic foundation possessed exceptional compatibility with Creation, allowing her physical and spiritual existences to become progressively more integrated. Jeanne subsequently recovered intrinsic Holy Light and Wind characteristics alongside the Nature affinity and Primordial Energy originating from her original existence. She also began rediscovering powers associated with the era in which she had served directly as the Universal Tree's Guardian.[8][5]
By the later Imperial era, Jeanne had become one of the most powerful members of Victor's family in terms of raw Energy and existential scale. She served as a principal supporting anchor during the Empire's extraordinary temporal acceleration, eventually sustaining a substantial portion of the ritual's burden. Her progress brought her increasingly close to the level she had possessed as the Universal Tree's original Guardian, while Victor's Blood Dragon lineage provided the potential to surpass her former limitations.[12][13][14]
Despite her ancient age and enormous supernatural responsibilities, Jeanne's personal motivations remain closely connected to the wish she expressed before most of Creation's current civilizations existed. She wanted a family, and the relationships she subsequently established with the Universal Tree, Vlad, Adam, Morgana, Victor, Isabelle and the larger Elderblood household continue reflecting that original desire. Her development is therefore not simply the restoration of an ancient Guardian's power, but the story of a Being who experienced several distinct lives before finally obtaining the family and belonging she had sought from the beginning.[1][15]

### Physical Appearance

#### Original Spirit and Saint
Jeanne's original spiritual existence predates her historical Human identity, and the exact appearance she possessed when first assigned to guard the Universal Tree is not completely established. Her later physical vessels share several recognizable characteristics, particularly her extraordinarily beautiful appearance, fair skin, long golden-blonde hair and generally gentle facial expression. These characteristics became closely associated with the identity she adopted as Jeanne d'Arc.[1]
During her historical Saint period, Jeanne possessed luminous golden eyes and an unusually pure supernatural presence. Her appearance reflected the relationship between her physical vessel, the Blessing of the [[Heavenly Father]] and the spiritual nature she had largely forgotten. Although she appeared Human, the extraordinary purity of her Soul and the supernatural powers available through her Saint status distinguished her from ordinary mortals.[1]
Her golden eyes became particularly striking when she employed her supernatural perception. When observing Souls, Blessings and Curses, they could emit an intense golden radiance, accompanying a form of spiritual sight that allowed her to examine phenomena beyond ordinary physical perception. However, the enormous amount of information available through these powers could place considerable strain upon her Human or Vampire vessel because her physical body lacked the capacity of her original Spirit.[1]
Jeanne frequently wore clothing associated with her historical image as the Maid of Orléans, including white and other light-colored garments that complemented her golden hair and Saintly appearance. Her visual presentation became an important contrast to the dark supernatural environment she later inhabited after becoming a [[Vampire Queen]].[1]

#### Noble Vampire
Following Vlad's intervention at her execution, Jeanne's physical vessel was transformed into that of a Noble Vampire. The transformation allowed her to continue living despite the conclusion of the destiny she had assumed as Jeanne d'Arc, but introduced a fundamental incompatibility between the body she inhabited and the Holy power associated with the [[Heavenly Father]].[16][17]
Despite possessing a Vampire body, Jeanne retained much of the pure spiritual presence associated with her Saint identity. Her appearance consequently remained unusually radiant compared with the characteristics expected from Creatures of the Night. The contradiction reflected the difference between the physical vessel transformed by Vlad and the far older spiritual Being that continued existing within it.[16][2]
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] performed the Servitude Ritual upon Jeanne and Morgana, Jeanne's body underwent another significant improvement. The procedure strengthened and refined her Vampire flesh, increasing its compatibility with the enormous supernatural power residing within her Spirit. Her appearance became more beautiful, while her eyes adopted the blood-red coloration associated with the enhanced Vampire vessel.[7]
The transformation did not alter her true spiritual identity. Victor's Progenitor power could strengthen the body Jeanne inhabited but lacked the necessary authority to fundamentally overwrite the ancient Spirit that formed her existence. Jeanne therefore remained a Primordial Spirit within a stronger Vampire vessel rather than becoming exclusively a Vampire through the ritual.[9]

#### Blood Dragon
Jeanne following her transformation into a True Dragon.
Jeanne's transformation into a genuine Blood Dragon produced an appearance distinct from her previous Vampire form. White Dragon horns with golden coloration emerged from her head, accompanied by large white-and-gold Draconic wings. Her eyes developed the blue coloration associated with her new physical vessel, while her long golden-blonde hair and pale skin remained recognizable.[8]
Unlike the transformation experienced by ordinary Vampires who became [[Blood Dragons]], Jeanne's racial evolution primarily concerned her physical vessel. Her underlying Spirit was already far older and more developed than an ordinary Dragon Soul, meaning [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Bloodline could not simply replace or redefine its fundamental identity. The newly developed True Dragon body instead provided a compatible physical foundation capable of supporting the spiritual power that had previously exceeded her Human and Vampire vessels.[8]
This distinction is essential to understanding Jeanne's present appearance. She is not merely a Blood Dragon who happens to possess unusually powerful spiritual abilities. Her current body is a genuine True Dragon vessel inhabited by an ancient Guardian whose spiritual existence was already extraordinary before the transformation. As her powers continue recovering, the relationship between body and Spirit becomes increasingly integrated, changing the way her original abilities manifest through her physical form.[8][18]
Her Dragon appearance also reflects her principal elemental characteristics. Holy Light and Wind became dominant attributes associated with the Draconic vessel, while Nature remained deeply connected to the Spirit's original existence. The Holy Light she now generates is intrinsic rather than the same borrowed Blessing she previously received through the [[Heavenly Father]].[9][8]
Jeanne can also possess an enormous Draconic appearance. During the preparations surrounding the merger of the Lower Sectors, her Dragon form was explicitly described as the second-largest Dragon in Victor's family, following Victor himself. Zaladrac and [[Scathach]] were described as the next closest in size, demonstrating the extraordinary scale of Jeanne's matured Draconic existence.[19]

#### Imperial Era
By the mature Imperial era, Jeanne retains her beautiful golden-haired appearance while possessing a supernatural presence associated with one of the oldest Spirits within Creation. Her appearance combines the elegance of her former Saint identity with the dignity and imposing characteristics of a True Dragon Goddess.[8][14]
Jeanne is frequently depicted wearing elegant white dresses and other refined clothing, reflecting the aesthetic preferences associated with her former identity. However, she is not restricted to ceremonial garments, and her behavior within the Imperial household is often considerably more informal than her public image as a former Saint and Empress might suggest.
Her most important visual distinction remains the relationship between her apparent humanoid body and the extraordinary existence concealed within it. Although Jeanne can appear as an ordinary beautiful woman, her genuine spiritual foundation is vastly more ancient and complex than her physical presentation suggests. The difference has only increased as her Spirit and Dragon vessel move toward fuller integration.[18][14]

### Personality
Jeanne is a compassionate, patient, gentle and deeply family-oriented woman whose personality contrasts sharply with the extraordinary power and age of her true existence. Her oldest remembered desire was simply to have a family, and this wish remained central to her behavior long after she forgot much of her original identity. Unlike Beings who regard power, authority or knowledge as their ultimate purpose, Jeanne's earliest recorded motivation centered upon genuine companionship. [[The Universal Tree]]'s decision to become her brother gave her the first familial relationship she consciously remembers, establishing an emotional foundation that persisted through the many lives she subsequently experienced.[1]
Her compassion has repeatedly influenced the direction of her life. After losing much of her ancient memory and wandering through the world, Jeanne encountered the Human girl whose destiny involved suffering and eventual execution as the Maid of Orléans. Rather than allowing the child to endure that burden, Jeanne took the girl's Fate upon herself. She believed that children should have the opportunity to live without being forced to shoulder responsibilities that properly belonged to adults. The choice was neither necessary for her personal survival nor undertaken for political advantage; it reflected a willingness to accept suffering in order to protect someone vulnerable.[1]
Jeanne's experiences as the historical Saint also shaped her understanding of Human nature. She fought for France and attempted to fulfill the destiny she had accepted, only to be betrayed by the people she had helped. Her subsequent rescue by Vlad demonstrated that the supernatural categories associated with Good and Evil did not always correspond to the behavior of the individuals occupying them. A Vampire, traditionally regarded as a Creature of the Night, saved the woman recognized as a Saint, while Humans associated with the country she defended condemned her to execution. These events contributed to Jeanne's tendency to judge individuals through their actions rather than relying entirely upon the moral categories assigned to their species or religious affiliations.[1]
Although Jeanne is generally kind, she is not pacifistic. She possesses extensive combat experience originating from her Saint period and subsequent supernatural life, and she is capable of employing lethal force against enemies who endanger her family or innocent people. Her confrontation with [[Paimon]] during the Demonic invasion of [[Nightingale]] demonstrated that she was willing to use the [[Heavenly Father]]'s Judgment despite knowing that its Holy power could destroy her incompatible Vampire vessel. She accepted considerable personal danger because allowing the Demons to continue summoning reinforcements would have endangered the entire city.[17]
Jeanne also possesses a pronounced maternal instinct. Her relationship with Adam remained important even after the collapse of her marriage to Vlad, while her later affection for Ophis, [[Nero]], Isabelle and the numerous younger members of the Elderblood family reflects her longstanding desire to protect and nurture children. She is especially concerned when powerful offspring neglect their training, recognizing that inherited ability cannot replace discipline or the experience necessary to survive dangerous supernatural conflicts.[20][21]
Her relationship with Vlad revealed another side of her personality: Jeanne is capable of enduring hardship for a considerable period, but eventually refuses to remain in circumstances that undermine her personal dignity and emotional well-being. After spending centuries within his household, she recognized that Vlad's political responsibilities and unresolved grief had become excuses for his prolonged neglect. Her decision to divorce him was therefore more than an expression of temporary anger. She wanted a life in which her existence mattered beyond the political role of [[Vampire Queen]], and she refused to continue sacrificing that possibility for a marriage that no longer provided genuine companionship.[11][22]
Jeanne's emotional independence became increasingly apparent through her interactions with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. When she and Morgana initially approached him, both believed he could serve as a political counterweight to Vlad. Victor refused to become an instrument of their resentment, challenging their assumptions and insisting that their children possessed independent lives and choices. Instead of alienating Jeanne, this response gradually increased her respect for him, since he demonstrated that powerful individuals could refuse to exploit the vulnerabilities of people who sought their assistance.[23]
The restoration of Jeanne's original identity also changed her understanding of power. As the Universal Tree's Guardian, she had once possessed extraordinary abilities simply because of the nature of her existence. She later admitted that she had not always considered training necessary because she was already exceptionally powerful and could rely upon her brother's protection. Her weakened Human and Vampire lives, followed by Victor's insistence upon personal growth, forced her to recognize the importance of refining power rather than depending entirely upon natural advantages.[9][14]
Jeanne is notably practical when dealing with large-scale supernatural phenomena. She understands that powers capable of altering the environment or manipulating [[Primordial Energy]] may cause disasters if employed carelessly. After recovering access to her ancient Energy, she explained that manipulating Nature without proper precision could unintentionally disturb tectonic plates and produce destructive earthquakes or tidal waves. Rather than treating her immense power as permission to disregard consequences, she preferred to develop greater control before using it freely upon the surrounding world.[9]
Her concern for collateral damage became visible during the investigation of [[New Dawn]]'s artificial island. Jeanne participated in understanding the manipulation of Faith performed upon its isolated Human inhabitants and supported the evacuation of civilians before Victor destroyed the installation. She subsequently repaired the enormous environmental damage caused by the destruction, demonstrating that her protective instincts extended beyond the immediate members of her family to the wider world affected by their actions.[24][25][26]
Jeanne's calm disposition also makes her an effective instructor. She possesses knowledge acquired through several different forms of existence, including her experiences as a Saint, Vampire, Primordial Spirit and True Dragon. This allows her to explain supernatural phenomena from perspectives unavailable to individuals whose understanding remains limited to one Race or Divine system. Her lessons frequently concern the relationship between Souls, Divinities, Racial development and the fundamental structures supporting Creation.[27][28]
However, her kindness does not prevent her from becoming stern with the younger generation. During the later Imperial era, Jeanne was among the Mothers responsible for the education and discipline of Victor's daughters. When several Princesses demonstrated that they had neglected important aspects of their studies, Jeanne immediately criticized Victor for indulging them excessively. Nevertheless, her resolve weakened almost immediately when Victor responded with an affectionate expression, prompting Morgana to mock her inability to remain strict whenever her husband appeared especially endearing.[29]
Her relationship with Morgana illustrates Jeanne's more informal personality. The two women possess very different temperaments, with Morgana being considerably more provocative, sarcastic and openly Demonic. Yet their long friendship allows them to understand one another exceptionally well, and Morgana frequently enjoys exposing the contrast between Jeanne's public image as a saintly woman and the more affectionate, possessive or easily embarrassed behavior she displays around Victor.[11][29]
Jeanne is also capable of becoming frighteningly protective when the safety of Victor or the children is threatened. Her relationship with the Universal Tree gives her access to extraordinary supernatural power, but her greatest emotional vulnerabilities remain the people she considers family. When her brother casually discussed a hypothetical situation involving Victor's death, Jeanne reacted with immediate hostility and refused to accept the example even as a theoretical possibility.[30]
Her development during the accelerated Imperial era further strengthened her willingness to accept responsibility. Despite possessing more raw Energy than almost every other member of the family, Jeanne continued contributing to the enormous temporal ritual because she understood that Victor could not sustain the entire process indefinitely without assistance. The continuous effort improved her control and brought her closer to her former Guardian peak, demonstrating a commitment to growth that contrasted with her earlier indifference toward training.[14]
Jeanne's present personality therefore combines the compassion of the historical Saint, the maternal devotion developed through centuries of family life, the discipline required of an Imperial Legion Commander and the ancient perspective of a Being who existed before the current Universe. Although her circumstances have changed enormously, her desire for a genuine family remains the emotional foundation connecting these different stages of her existence.[1][15]

### Background
Jeanne's earliest known existence predates the formation of the Universe as it is presently structured. According to the memories she later recovered, [[Primordial Chaos]] created her and assigned her a specific responsibility: protecting a seed that would eventually become the Universal Tree. The seed had not yet developed into the enormous Primordial Entity responsible for maintaining Life and Reincarnation throughout Creation, making Jeanne's original duty fundamentally different from the work performed by most later supernatural Guardians.[1][5][30]
Jeanne remained beside the seed through an immense period of development. Her function was to protect it while it grew, allowing the future Universal Tree to establish the foundation necessary for its eventual role within the Cosmos. Her existence was therefore directly connected to the earliest stages of Creation, although she was not herself responsible for establishing every Law or Concept that would govern the completed Universe.[1]
When the Universal Tree developed consciousness, he recognized that Jeanne had protected him for an extraordinarily long period and attempted to repay her service. He offered to grant a wish, expecting that someone who had fulfilled such a responsibility might desire wealth, power, knowledge or another supernatural reward. Jeanne instead expressed the desire to possess a family.[1]
The Tree could not simply create genuine familial affection through an arbitrary act of supernatural power. A relationship manufactured without independent personal attachment would not satisfy the emotional meaning of Jeanne's request. He therefore offered himself as her family, and the two chose to recognize one another as brother and sister.[1]
The decision established a personal bond that existed independently of their original Guardian relationship. Jeanne had protected the Tree because she was created for that purpose, but the familial relationship emerged because the two chose to acknowledge one another beyond their assigned responsibilities. This distinction remained important throughout their later interactions, since their affection continued influencing their decisions even when those decisions involved the fundamental authorities governing the Universe.[1][15]
Jeanne subsequently wandered through the developing world after losing much of her original memory. The precise circumstances through which every portion of her ancient knowledge disappeared have not been completely explained, but the loss eventually left her unable to remember the identity she had possessed before becoming associated with Human civilization. During this period, she continued existing as a spiritual Being, occasionally intervening to help individuals she encountered without fully understanding the extraordinary scale of her own nature.[1][9]
Her encounter with the Human girl destined to become Jeanne d'Arc represented the most important decision of this wandering period. Jeanne recognized that the child's Fate involved extraordinary hardship, war and eventual betrayal. She believed that an innocent girl should not be forced to carry such suffering merely because destiny had assigned her a historical role. Consequently, she removed the girl's Fate and accepted it as her own, becoming the woman who would later be remembered as the Maid of Orléans.[1]
The change was a genuine alteration of destiny rather than a simple decision to use another person's name. Jeanne assumed the supernatural and historical consequences attached to the girl's future, allowing the original child to escape a destiny that would otherwise have defined her life. This action also gave the nameless Spirit a new identity around which her subsequent memories and relationships would develop.[1]
Jeanne fulfilled the destiny she had taken, fought for France and became recognized as a Saint of the Heavenly Father. Her association with the Biblical Divine system granted access to Holy powers, Oracle-like perception and Divine Judgments. However, the country she had served eventually condemned her to execution, and she was sentenced to be burned at the stake.[1]
[[Vlad Dracula Tepes]] intervened during the execution and saved her. Jeanne's historical Fate as the Maid of Orléans had reached its intended conclusion, but the ancient Spirit herself continued existing through Vlad's intervention. He subsequently transformed her physical vessel into a Vampire, granting her another form of prolonged existence while inadvertently creating the contradiction of a former Saint inhabiting a body naturally vulnerable to the Holy element.[1][16]
The two eventually married, and Jeanne became one of the Vampire Queens of [[Nightingale]]. Their son Adam represented the first biological family Jeanne established during her Vampire life. However, Vlad's increasing emotional distance and the prolonged neglect experienced by several of his wives eventually destroyed their relationship, creating the circumstances that would bring Jeanne into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household.[11]
Jeanne's recovery of her forgotten identity occurred gradually after Victor began examining the abnormalities within her Soul. The revelations exposed the difference between her physical Race and true spiritual existence, allowing her to reconnect with the Universal Tree and recover access to powers associated with her original purpose.[1][2]
One of the most significant discoveries concerned the inheritance of her Primordial Spirit. Jeanne explained that the Energy associated with her Guardian position was bound directly to her Soul rather than an ordinary biological Bloodline. Consequently, children conceived through her physical vessel would not automatically inherit the [[Primordial Energy]] attached to her original position. Her offspring could inherit the racial and supernatural characteristics of the body she currently inhabited, but her Guardian authority did not simply become an inheritable family trait.[2]
This distinction established an important boundary between Jeanne's ancient spiritual existence and the family she would later create with Victor. Although Isabelle inherited extraordinary characteristics and eventually developed a fundamental Divinity associated with Energy, this should not be treated as proof that Jeanne's Guardian authority automatically passed to her daughter through ordinary reproduction.[2][6]

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Jeanne entered the active narrative during the crisis surrounding Vlad's family and the changing political structure of [[Nightingale]]. After awakening from a prolonged period of inactivity alongside the Succubus who would later become [[Morgana Elderblood]], she learned that the circumstances of the Vampire Kingdom had changed significantly during her absence. Their relationship with Vlad had already deteriorated, and both women considered the prolonged neglect they had experienced sufficient reason to abandon their positions as Vampire Queens.[10][11]
Jeanne immediately made clear that she no longer intended to remain Vlad's wife. The decision was not merely a reaction to the conditions surrounding her awakening; it reflected resentment accumulated through centuries of emotional distance and the realization that her position within the Royal Family no longer offered the personal relationships she desired. Morgana shared similar grievances, allowing the two women to coordinate their departure rather than confronting the Vampire King independently.[11]
Their escape was complicated by the condition of their bodies. Jeanne's Vampire vessel had spent an extended period without the nourishment required by her Race, producing intense Blood hunger despite the extraordinary nature of the Spirit residing within it. Both women also lacked current information concerning Nightingale's political situation and the circumstances surrounding their children, making it necessary to seek protection and trustworthy allies before attempting to resolve their conflicts with Vlad.[11][31]
Clan Scarlett became one of the most practical destinations. [[Scathach]] possessed enough personal power to deter most hostile factions, while the growing influence of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] Alucard created another possibility. Jeanne and Morgana eventually approached Victor because the existence of a second Vampire Progenitor fundamentally altered the balance of power within Nightingale and made it conceivable for individuals to resist Vlad without depending entirely upon the established Royal Family.[22]
Their initial proposal involved using Victor's growing strength to oppose Vlad and potentially recover control over the lives of the children they believed had suffered beneath the King's emotional neglect. Victor refused to support such an arrangement merely because the former Queens were dissatisfied with their marriage. He emphasized that their adult children possessed their own wishes and that he had no intention of reorganizing his life around a conflict arising primarily from their resentment toward Vlad.[23]
The refusal forced Jeanne to reconsider the assumptions underlying her approach. Victor possessed the power to exploit their situation, yet he chose to challenge their judgment rather than immediately accepting an opportunity to increase his political influence. The encounter consequently became one of the foundations of Jeanne's growing respect for him, particularly because he treated her as an individual whose future should not remain defined entirely by the marriage she had abandoned.[23]
Victor later investigated Jeanne's memories and discovered that her spiritual history extended far beyond the identity of the historical Saint. Their encounter exposed a vision of Jeanne being burned at the stake, together with memories of the Human child whose destiny she had voluntarily taken. Victor recognized that Jeanne d'Arc was an assumed identity and that the woman before him had existed long before the historical events that made her famous.[1]
Jeanne explained that she had encountered the child originally destined to become the Maid of Orléans and deliberately taken her Fate because she believed that no child should be forced to endure such suffering. The revelation clarified that Jeanne's historical identity originated from an act of compassion rather than ordinary birth or a fabricated name. It also exposed memories associated with a far older Being and the mysterious Tree she had once regarded as family.[1]
As Victor investigated further, he encountered indications that Jeanne's Soul was extraordinarily pure and fundamentally unlike that of an ordinary Vampire. Her Oracle Sight could perceive Blessings, Curses, Souls and other supernatural conditions, but the use of such perception placed considerable pressure upon the physical body she inhabited. The incompatibility suggested that her Human and Vampire identities represented only a portion of her existence rather than its true foundation.[1]
Jeanne's relationship with Adam also became an important part of her new life. She wanted her son to develop independently rather than remain protected indefinitely by Vlad's authority, and she considered Victor a suitable teacher because his emphasis upon strength, responsibility and family differed from the emotional distance she associated with the Vampire King. Her conversations with Adam reflected concern for his future rather than a desire to use him as another instrument in her dispute with Vlad.[20]
When Jeanne confronted Vlad again, her grievances increasingly focused upon his conduct as a father. She recognized that political rule and personal grief could place enormous demands upon an individual, but refused to accept that these responsibilities justified indefinitely neglecting his children. Her criticism reflected the same personal values that had led her to abandon the marriage and seek another environment in which family relationships could be treated as meaningful responsibilities.[32]
By the conclusion of this period, Jeanne had begun separating her identity from the role of Vlad's former Queen. Victor's influence helped expose her forgotten spiritual history, while her relationship with Morgana and the growing Alucard household provided opportunities for companionship and personal development beyond the political restrictions of Nightingale.[1]

#### Demonic War
The invasion of [[Nightingale]] brought Jeanne into direct conflict with the supernatural forces of Hell. The Royal Capital became a battlefield as high-ranking Demons entered through portals and attempted to overwhelm the Vampire authorities. Jeanne and Morgana confronted the invading forces, drawing upon their extensive experience and very different supernatural abilities to resist enemies whose powers threatened the city.[33]
Jeanne's former Saint abilities became particularly important during the battle against [[Paimon]]. Although she possessed a Vampire body and had long since distanced herself from the [[Heavenly Father]], the connection associated with her historical Saint identity had not completely disappeared. When the Demon began producing additional summoning formations throughout the Capital, Jeanne recognized that ordinary combat would not prevent the situation from escalating into a disaster.[17]
After physically confronting Paimon, Jeanne decided to invoke one of the Heavenly Father's Judgments. She contacted the Biblical God and received permission to use the Blessing that remained associated with her existence. The Heavenly Father warned that her Vampire body was incompatible with the Holy element and that excessive use could erase her existence, but Jeanne proceeded because the invading Demons represented an immediate threat to the city.[17]
Golden wings appeared behind her, and she received a golden sword associated with Michael before declaring the Judgment of Israel. Golden Holy fire spread across Nightingale, destroying the [[Magic]] Circles used by Paimon's forces, purifying Demonic Miasma and suppressing the power of the Demons within the affected territory. Vampires were also weakened because the Holy element remained harmful to Creatures of the Night, demonstrating that the Judgment did not discriminate entirely according to Jeanne's personal affiliations.[17]
The attack was extraordinarily effective but placed enormous strain upon Jeanne's body. As the Holy power damaged the Vampire vessel, she began coughing Blood and struggled to maintain the effect. The incident revealed how deeply the physical Race imposed upon her by Vlad conflicted with the powers associated with her former identity, even though her underlying Spirit remained extraordinarily pure.[17]
Jeanne's original nature became more widely known after she reconnected with the Universal Tree. During a discussion concerning Roxanne and the [[World Trees]], Jeanne explained that she was the Guardian of the Universal Tree rather than an ordinary Vampire or Saint. She compared her position with the Guardians associated with ordinary World Trees, but emphasized that her relationship possessed considerably greater autonomy because of the extraordinary nature of her brother.[2]
She explained that the Universal Tree maintained Life and Reincarnation throughout the Universe and served as the progenitor of the World Tree species. Her relationship with him therefore placed her in an unusual position among supernatural Guardians, giving her access to an Energy whose purity exceeded that normally possessed by Gods and other powerful beings.[2]
The revelation also clarified an important aspect of Jeanne's existence. She explained that she was fundamentally a Higher-Level Spirit capable of inhabiting physical bodies belonging to other [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] without completely mixing her original spiritual essence with the body. Consequently, the children she conceived through those bodies would inherit the characteristics of the physical Race rather than automatically receiving the [[Primordial Energy]] bound to her Guardian Soul.[2]
During the same conversation, Jeanne revealed another ancient connection that had not previously been apparent. When Sasha and Natashia questioned whether she had known the ancestral figure associated with the Fulger family, Jeanne explained that she had personally met the woman and had been responsible for naming her. The revelation demonstrated that Jeanne's relationship with the supernatural history of the world extended beyond the Universal Tree and the events associated with the French Saint.[2]
The disclosure also attracted the attention of Roxanne, who recognized that the Universal Tree's relationship with Jeanne made her an aunt within the wider World Tree family. Jeanne accepted the designation according to the familial relationship she shared with her brother, and Roxanne responded enthusiastically to the discovery that she possessed another member of her extended family.[2]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later performed a Servitude Ritual intended to strengthen Jeanne, Ruby and Morgana. The results differed significantly among the women because of the nature of their individual Souls. While Victor's Progenitor power could affect Jeanne's physical Vampire body, it could not overwrite the higher-order Spirit forming her essential existence. The procedure instead improved the strength and resistance of her physical vessel while reducing the influence of the Heavenly Father's Blessing.[7][9]
Jeanne discovered that the borrowed Holy power had been removed and that her own supernatural Energy had become considerably easier to manipulate. Green Primordial Energy appeared within her hand, revealing that the power connected to her original Guardian existence was becoming increasingly accessible. She also recognized the continued presence of other Blessings, including one associated with [[Hestia]], whose effectiveness developed through the strength of the familial relationship involved.[9]
Jeanne explained that her Primordial Energy was connected to the Universal Tree and could be used to influence Nature on a scale far beyond conventional elemental manipulation. Nevertheless, she recognized that the Energy was dangerous when employed without sufficient control. Even a comparatively small quantity could produce enormous environmental changes, and careless use upon a planet might disturb tectonic structures or trigger natural disasters. Jeanne therefore preferred to refine her control rather than immediately employing the Energy's full potential upon the surrounding environment.[9]
Her increasing control became valuable during the later stages of the Demonic War. After Victor conquered Hell, maintaining communications between his location and the rest of the family became increasingly demanding. Jeanne supplied controlled Energy to support Alexios and sustain the connection, demonstrating that her Primordial abilities could be used for large-scale assistance rather than exclusively for combat or environmental manipulation.[34][35]
By the conclusion of the conflict, Jeanne had recovered enough of her original identity to understand that she had never been exclusively Human or Vampire. Her old relationship with the Heavenly Father had ended as an active source of power, while her connection to the Universal Tree and Victor's developing household became the principal foundations of her future.[9]

#### King Among Kings
Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s return from Hell, Jeanne became increasingly integrated into the enormous family forming around him. The experience of losing her original family, assuming another identity, becoming a [[Vampire Queen]] and subsequently abandoning Vlad gave her an unusual perspective on the complicated relationships developing within the household. She understood that enormous supernatural power could not automatically resolve disputes concerning affection, jealousy and personal responsibility, making her one of the women capable of approaching family disagreements with considerable patience.[36]
Jeanne participated in the investigation of [[New Dawn]]'s secret facility located above the Bermuda Triangle. The organization had constructed an isolated Human civilization within a concealed environment, manipulating its inhabitants through religious authority and staged supernatural phenomena. Jeanne recognized the importance of the Faith accumulated by the population and helped explain how concentrated belief could be transformed into a resource supporting the development of Gods.[24][25]
The investigation revealed a system through which ordinary Humans were deliberately manipulated to generate supernatural Faith. Jeanne's knowledge of historical Saints and Divine religions made her particularly well suited to understanding the process, since she had experienced the relationship between religious authority and supernatural power during her life as Jeanne d'Arc.[37]
Victor and his companions subsequently arranged for the manipulated population to be rescued before destroying the facility. After Victor's Dragon Breath eliminated the island and caused enormous environmental devastation, Jeanne used [[Primordial Energy]] to restore the damaged region of the sea. Her intervention demonstrated that the Energy received through her Guardian existence could repair large-scale environmental destruction rather than serving exclusively as a source of raw destructive power.[26][38]
Jeanne's relationship with the Universal Tree became especially important during Victor's ambitious project to create a new world. Although Victor possessed sufficient power to assemble planetary matter, establishing a functioning supernatural world required more than the construction of physical terrain. The planet needed appropriate conditions for Life, environmental development and the supernatural structures that allowed a World Tree to support a living civilization.[39]
Jeanne contributed Primordial Energy to the developing world and cooperated with Roxanne and other members of the family to accelerate its transformation. The Energy supported the formation of life-sustaining systems and the development of environmental conditions necessary for a living planet. Her participation was particularly valuable because the Universal Tree's Energy was connected to the supernatural principles governing Life, Nature and the World Tree species.[39]
The project also reinforced Jeanne's familial relationship with her brother. Although the Universal Tree existed as a fundamental Being responsible for enormous portions of Creation, Jeanne continued interacting with him through the personal bond they had established long before the current civilizations of the Universe existed.[40]
Jeanne's most significant racial transformation occurred when Victor began converting members of his family into genuine [[True Dragons]]. Unlike the women whose previous Vampire, Demon or other supernatural bodies were fundamentally replaced through Draconic evolution, Jeanne possessed an ancient Spirit whose essential foundation was already extraordinarily advanced.[8]
Victor's Blood Dragon lineage consequently transformed her physical vessel while preserving the underlying Spirit. White-and-golden horns and wings appeared, while her body gained a natural relationship with Creation that had been absent from her previous Human and Vampire forms. The new body was capable of supporting her original power without the same incompatibilities that had previously restricted her development.[8]
Jeanne discovered that her new Draconic attributes were primarily associated with Holy Light and Wind. The Holy Light was especially significant because she could generate it directly rather than borrowing it from the Heavenly Father. Despite its sacred nature and its effectiveness against Demons and Creatures of the Night, Jeanne did not acquire the ordinary weakness toward Darkness that would normally accompany such an affinity.[8]
The transformation also improved the relationship between her Soul and physical body. Her ancient Spirit had always been greater than the vessels she inhabited, but the True Dragon form possessed sufficient compatibility with Creation to allow the two components to begin integrating. This established a developmental path through which Jeanne could recover more of her original Guardian power while gaining the additional potential provided by Victor's Bloodline.[8]
Jeanne subsequently began studying Draconic Runes and the supernatural principles available to genuine True Dragons. Her development was no longer restricted to recovering memories and powers from the past; she was acquiring new abilities through a Race whose relationship with Creation differed fundamentally from the bodies she had previously occupied.[41]
During the campaign against the [[Egyptian Pantheon]] and the Elder Gods, Jeanne participated among Victor's most powerful allies. Her Primordial Energy proved important when Victor's enraged Eldritch transformation released overwhelming supernatural pressure that endangered individuals nearby. Rather than simply retreating, Jeanne helped protect the group against the resulting backlash.[42]
Her combat abilities became particularly visible during the battle against the Elder Gods. Wind gathered around her before expanding into a devastating hurricane, and she subsequently generated more than two hundred tornadoes across the battlefield. The attack demonstrated the enormous destructive scale available through her developed True Dragon attributes, while the larger Draconic assault maintained sufficient alignment with Nature to avoid casually destroying every deeper environmental foundation supporting the battlefield.[43]
Victor's perception of Jeanne's existence also developed during this period. He recognized that her Soul possessed a scale and complexity substantially greater than those associated with an ordinary Dragon or Goddess. Jeanne's relationship with the Universal Tree was not merely a Blessing granted by a powerful external Being; she was herself an ancient part of the supernatural history of Creation, with a Guardian position originating before the current civilizations and Divine institutions existed.[44]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Jeanne's development continued after her transformation into a genuine True Dragon. Although the new vessel allowed her to recover powers that had remained restricted during her Human and Vampire lives, she still possessed enormous portions of her original potential that had not yet been fully restored.[8][5]
During a private moment, Jeanne manifested three distinct concentrations of supernatural power. The first possessed a natural green appearance, while the second was pure white and the third pure black. The white and black manifestations were particularly unusual because of the extraordinary purity of their respective appearances. Jeanne recognized them as powers she had once possessed and expressed surprise that [[Primordial Chaos]] had permitted her to retain them after the completion of her original responsibility.[5]
The moment revealed that Jeanne's ancient abilities extended beyond the Nature, Wind and Holy Light characteristics associated with her present Dragon vessel. However, the exact independent Authorities represented by the white and black manifestations were not explicitly identified during the scene. Their appearance should therefore not be treated as sufficient evidence that Jeanne independently possesses the complete authority of Positivity, Negativity or another specific Primordial Concept.[5]
Jeanne also reflected upon the disappearance of Primordial Chaos. She had been created for the purpose of protecting the Universal Tree but had not subsequently received a complete explanation concerning why some of her original abilities remained accessible. The absence of her Creator left her unable to obtain answers, reinforcing the distinction between her ancient responsibilities and the complete knowledge of Creation's origin possessed by the Being who established them.[5]
Her growing integration with the Universal Tree and True Dragon vessel also altered the way [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] understood her vulnerabilities. During a later conversation, Victor recognized that Jeanne did not possess an ordinary exploitable biological weakness comparable to those associated with her previous Human and Vampire bodies. He explained that destroying Jeanne's body would not necessarily destroy the spiritual Being connected to the Universal Tree, and that her true destruction would require circumstances involving the Tree itself.[18]
This conclusion reflected the unusual nature of Jeanne's existence rather than unconditional invulnerability. Her Soul, Guardian position and connection to the Universal Tree had become intertwined with her physical body, making conventional methods of killing a supernatural organism inadequate for describing her survival. The process remained part of her ongoing development, since she had not yet reached the fullest possible integration of her Spirit and Dragon vessel.[18]
Jeanne remained involved in the family's continued expansion after Ragnarök. The appearance of daughters possessing extraordinary powers, including [[Yog-Sothoth]], required the older members of the family to consider how beings with fundamental supernatural abilities should be raised and protected. Jeanne's perspective remained strongly maternal, emphasizing that children should not simply become tools for solving cosmic problems because of the powers inherited from their parents.[45][46]
Her relationship with Victor also continued strengthening as the family increasingly confronted supernatural matters extending far beyond Earth. Jeanne had already lived through enormous changes in the structure of Creation, but Victor's rapidly developing powers introduced circumstances that challenged even her ancient perspective. Nevertheless, she remained one of the people capable of assisting him through the quality of her [[Primordial Energy]] and her knowledge of the Universal Tree.[5][18]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
The development of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Imperial family eventually brought Jeanne into another stage of motherhood. She became pregnant with Victor's daughter, [[Isabelle Elderblood]], whose extraordinary supernatural potential required an exceptionally long period of development.[6]
According to the pregnancy chronology, Jeanne carried Isabelle for approximately 1,990 years, placing her among the women whose children required the longest gestation periods. The unusually long duration was associated with the fundamental level of the Divine Concept developing within the child rather than a simple correlation between gestation time and general combat strength.[6]
Isabelle inherited an extraordinary relationship with Energy, eventually possessing a Divinity associated with Energy itself. Her abilities developed through a combination of the unusual characteristics inherited from Victor and Jeanne, although the precise mechanism should not be simplified into the automatic inheritance of Jeanne's Guardian authority. Jeanne had previously explained that the [[Primordial Energy]] associated with her original position was bound to her Soul and did not ordinarily pass directly to children through physical reproduction.[2][6][47]
Jeanne also became one of the family members responsible for teaching the younger generation about Divinity. During Victor's lessons concerning Gods and their development, she explained subjects involving Divine Concepts, the nature of Souls, spiritual inheritance and the differences between ordinary supernatural beings and those whose existence was supported by more fundamental authorities.[27]
Her experience was particularly valuable because she understood these subjects from several perspectives. As the historical Jeanne d'Arc, she had received powers through the [[Heavenly Father]]'s Blessing; as a Vampire, she experienced the consequences of an incompatible physical vessel; as a Primordial Spirit, she possessed an existence directly connected to the Universal Tree; and as a Blood Dragon, she had acquired a body capable of supporting much of that spiritual power without the same restrictions.[17][2][8]
Jeanne's relationship with the Universal Tree also influenced Victor's personal decisions. When matters concerning [[Otsuki Hana]] raised the possibility of conflict with Primordial authorities, Victor assured Jeanne that her brother's well-being remained important to him. He regarded the Universal Tree as part of the family through Jeanne and was unwilling to abandon that relationship merely because it might eventually create political or supernatural difficulties.[48]
As the Empire developed, Jeanne's restorative capabilities became increasingly important. During a major Imperial intervention, she employed an enormous healing effect across a populated region, restoring citizens suffering from illness, disability and other physical conditions. The feat demonstrated that her role within Victor's civilization extended beyond combat and family protection to include large-scale humanitarian assistance.[49]
Her personal interests also became visible outside major supernatural conflicts. Jeanne developed a preference for gardening, a comparatively peaceful activity fitting her longstanding relationship with Nature and the Universal Tree. The hobby provided a contrast to the enormous responsibilities and cosmic powers associated with her original Guardian identity.[50]
When the Empire began preparing for contact with hostile civilizations from the Higher Sectors, Jeanne was included among the powerful Dragons whose abilities could contribute to the defense of the Imperial Family. Her ancient experience and extraordinary Energy reserves made her valuable against threats whose individual power exceeded that of most Gods belonging to Earth's older Pantheons.[51]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
The [[Elderblood Empire]]'s expansion toward the Higher Sectors required the creation of a more systematic method for classifying supernatural threats. Jeanne was recognized as a Galactic-class existence whose destructive and environmental capabilities exceeded those of ordinary planetary or Stellar Gods.[12]
During a discussion concerning [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ability to manipulate galaxies, Jeanne provided an assessment of her own development. She explained that she could confidently control approximately one or two galaxies at her current level, but could not manipulate such structures as casually as Victor. She also explained that recovering her former Guardian potential would allow her to influence an entire Sector, while cooperation with the Universal Tree could support comparable operations across Universal-scale structures.[12]
Jeanne further clarified the responsibility she had once possessed as the Universal Tree's Guardian. At the height of her former power, she believed herself capable of defending her brother against almost every Primordial threat, with particularly problematic Beings such as Infinity and [[True Death]] remaining exceptions. The statement concerned her former Guardian peak and the relationship between her power and the Universal Tree, not a claim that her current partially restored existence could independently defeat every Primordial Being.[12]
Victor subsequently began the Empire's extraordinary temporal development project, intended to accelerate approximately 200,000 years of internal civilization growth while allowing a much shorter period to pass outside the affected region. The undertaking required controlling the passage of Time throughout enormous Galactic territories, maintaining the stability of planets and stars, and supplying enough Energy to support civilizations undergoing centuries of development within an extraordinarily compressed external period.[52]
Natalia performed the principal Time-related work, while Victor supplied much of the Energy necessary to sustain the operation. Jeanne became one of the principal supporting anchors because of the exceptional purity and quantity of the [[Primordial Energy]] associated with her original existence. Her contribution reduced the burden Victor would otherwise have needed to sustain independently.[52][13]
The initial strain was enormous. The ritual required continuous Energy expenditure over a period far beyond ordinary military or supernatural operations, and the effort affected every participant. Jeanne's original ability to sustain a relatively small portion of the total burden was eventually transformed through the extraordinary training effect produced by the ritual itself.[13][14]
By the later accelerated era, Jeanne had improved sufficiently to support approximately thirty percent of the ritual's burden comfortably and up to forty percent when necessary. She explained that her Energy and control had increased approximately threefold, bringing her closer to the extraordinary level she had possessed as the original Guardian of the Universal Tree.[14]
The development was particularly significant because Jeanne had already existed at an exceptionally high supernatural level before the ritual. Further improvement became increasingly difficult as she approached her former peak, but the continuous expenditure of enormous quantities of Energy forced her to refine her control to an extent that ordinary training could not easily reproduce.[14]
Jeanne recognized that her current form possessed more potential than her former Guardian existence. The True Dragon vessel provided a new developmental foundation, while the recovery of her ancient spiritual abilities allowed her to approach the power she had once possessed before losing her memories. She believed that gaining greater Authority over the Laws of the Universe could allow her to exceed that historical peak rather than merely recover it.[14]
At this point, the narration identified Jeanne as one of the family's greatest Energy reservoirs. In terms of pure Energy, only Victor and Azathoth were stated to exceed her, making her an extraordinary exception even among the powerful Goddesses and [[True Dragons]] associated with the Elderblood Empire.[14]
Nevertheless, Jeanne's raw Energy did not automatically make her the most dangerous combatant after Victor. The family recognized that [[Scathach]] possessed comparatively less Energy but could be more formidable in direct battle because of her extraordinary [[Martial Arts]], tactical experience and specialization in combat. The distinction illustrates that Jeanne's greatest strengths involve existential scale, Energy and Guardian-related powers rather than the exact fighting style of a dedicated martial specialist.[47]
The accelerated era also produced a more organized Imperial military. Victor established specialized Legions responsible for different aspects of the Empire's protection and expansion, with Jeanne becoming commander of the Sixth Legion, [[The Silent Judgment]]. The organization was responsible for confronting large-scale rebellion, corruption and territories requiring direct Imperial judgment or punishment. [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]] could assume command when Jeanne was unavailable.[3]
Jeanne's assignment reflected both her enormous supernatural strength and the trust Victor placed in her judgment. Unlike purely destructive military formations, the Silent Judgment required commanders capable of deciding when a territory needed correction, purification or direct intervention. Jeanne's experience as a Saint, Queen, Guardian and Dragon Goddess made her particularly suited to a position combining overwhelming force with significant responsibility.[3]
Her immense age later became a subject of humor within the family. Maria pointed out that Jeanne had existed long before the present Universe had assumed its current form, prompting others to suggest that she was the oldest woman among them. Jeanne objected because Azathoth had existed before the Universe as well, demonstrating that even someone as ancient as Jeanne recognized the distinction between the age of her native Creation and the existence of Beings originating beyond it.[4]
The younger generation continued developing through the accelerated period. Jeanne's daughter Isabelle acquired an increasingly sophisticated understanding of Primal Energy and possessed a Divinity associated with Energy itself. Jeanne remained involved in her education, recognizing that such extraordinary potential could become dangerous if Isabelle relied entirely upon innate power without learning the principles required for proper control.[47]
Jeanne's son Adam presented a different problem. After passing the Elderblood trials, Adam developed from a Lesser Dragon into a Dragon, gaining access to a more advanced racial foundation. However, his progress remained far below the level Jeanne believed appropriate for someone possessing his extraordinary Soul potential. By the events of Chapter 1171, his combat classification remained around the Planetary level, while many younger members of the family had already developed substantially beyond that stage.[21]
The situation deeply frustrated Jeanne. She recognized that Adam possessed enough natural advantages to become considerably stronger but lacked the motivation and discipline required to realize his potential. She consequently resolved to intensify his training, including the possibility of placing him under Scathach's supervision, because she wanted him capable of protecting himself and his younger sister rather than remaining dependent upon the power of the older generation.[21]
Jeanne's approach toward the younger generation was not limited to Adam. She continued participating in the education of Victor's daughters, helping them understand Divine Concepts and the supernatural principles underlying Creation. During a later conversation, Victor recognized that his daughters possessed considerable practical familiarity with Draconic Runes but lacked a complete understanding of how the underlying Energy and conceptual systems functioned. Jeanne had intended to teach the deeper theory after the girls developed greater command of the practical material, demonstrating that the family's instructors had organized their education around successive stages of complexity.[28]
The birth of [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]] and the attention of the Primordial authorities later threatened to provoke a conflict far beyond the Empire's ordinary military capabilities. Jeanne was among the few members of the family prepared to assist Victor if the situation escalated. While most wives could provide Energy and support, Jeanne's extraordinary reserves and connection to the Universal Tree placed her among the individuals capable of contributing directly to a confrontation involving Primordial-level powers.[53]
[[The Universal Tree]] ultimately intervened during the dispute concerning Moira's future. His position was influenced partly by the familial relationship he shared with Jeanne. Because Jeanne belonged to Victor's family, her children and the wider Elderblood household also formed part of the Tree's extended family. He therefore opposed attempts to remove the child and declared that any threat directed toward her would also involve him.[15]
The confrontation also demonstrated the continued intimacy of Jeanne's relationship with her brother. When the Universal Tree expressed approval of Victor and remarked upon Jeanne's choice of husband, Jeanne became embarrassed and demanded that he stop discussing her personal life while the crisis remained unresolved. The exchange revealed that their familial bond remained recognizably ordinary in emotional terms despite the cosmic status of the Beings involved.[15]
Jeanne's contribution to the temporal project continued through the exhaustion of its participants. After Victor had expended an enormous portion of his available Energy, he instructed Jeanne to rest because he could perceive the strain affecting her even across their supernatural connection. Jeanne initially objected but eventually withdrew, choosing to use the opportunity for training with Adam rather than simply abandoning her responsibilities.[54]
During the preparations for the Sector merger, Jeanne remained one of the most capable members of the family in terms of Energy control. The extended temporal operation had forced both Jeanne and Victor to refine their ability to regulate extraordinary quantities of power, placing them among the most accomplished practitioners within the Empire.[19]
The same period provided a notable comparison between the Dragon forms of the Imperial Family. Jeanne was described as the second-largest Dragon after Victor, with Zaladrac and Scathach occupying the next positions. Her enormous physical scale reflected the continued integration of her ancient Spirit with a True Dragon body capable of supporting more of her original supernatural existence.[19]
Jeanne also participated in the gathering involving the Universal Tree during the Sector merger and the discussion of the supernatural systems surrounding Life, Death and Reincarnation. When Victor questioned the origin of certain conditions affecting a Primordial Being, Jeanne acknowledged that her knowledge was incomplete. She explained that she had originally been created to protect her brother and that [[Primordial Chaos]] had not returned to provide further instruction or explanation after assigning her responsibility.[30]
The admission is important because Jeanne's ancient age and connection to the Universal Tree do not make her all-knowing. Although she possesses knowledge unavailable to almost every modern Goddess, much of Creation's original history remains mysterious even to her. Her responsibility was protection rather than unrestricted access to every decision made by Primordial Chaos, and she continues learning about the deeper structure of the Cosmos through the events surrounding Victor's Empire.[30]
By the latest developments, Jeanne continues serving as one of the most important members of Victor's family, combining the responsibilities of an Imperial Empress, Guardian, mother, instructor and Legion Commander. Her extraordinary spiritual foundation gives her a direct connection to the earliest history of Creation, while the family she has established provides the personal meaning she originally sought long before the development of the present Universe.[1][30]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Jeanne's current husband, the father of her daughter Isabelle, and the individual most responsible for enabling her return toward the power and identity she possessed as the Universal Tree's original Guardian.[6]
Their relationship began through political necessity rather than romance. After Jeanne and Morgana left Vlad, they approached Victor because his position as the Second Vampire Progenitor made him the only practical counterweight to the Vampire King's authority. Jeanne initially hoped he would assist in opposing Vlad and resolving the situation concerning her children, but Victor refused to become a weapon employed solely to satisfy the resentment of the former Queens.[23]
The refusal gradually changed Jeanne's opinion of Victor. He possessed extraordinary power and could have exploited her circumstances for political gain, yet he preferred to challenge her assumptions and encourage personal independence. This behavior appealed to Jeanne because much of her Vampire life had been defined by dependence upon Vlad, whose political authority had eventually become more important to him than the emotional responsibilities associated with marriage and parenthood.[23]
Victor subsequently discovered the ancient Spirit concealed beneath Jeanne's identities and helped her recover memories she had lost long before becoming the historical Saint. His willingness to examine and understand her existence without immediately attempting to exploit her powers was especially important to Jeanne, who recognized that many supernatural authorities would regard the Guardian of the Universal Tree as an extraordinarily valuable resource.[1][2]
The Servitude Ritual and later Blood Dragon transformation produced further changes. Victor improved her Vampire vessel without overwriting her Spirit and eventually granted her a True Dragon body capable of supporting much greater portions of her original existence. These developments provided opportunities for Jeanne to regain the strength and autonomy she had lost throughout her prolonged wandering and marriage to Vlad.[9][8]
Jeanne's affection developed from gratitude and admiration into genuine love. She recognized that Victor valued her as a person rather than merely for the extraordinary powers connected to her brother, while Victor accepted the personal responsibilities accompanying her place within his family. Their relationship eventually produced their daughter Isabelle, who inherited an unusually fundamental relationship with Energy.[2][6]
Their partnership later became important to the survival and expansion of the Empire. Jeanne supplied enormous quantities of [[Primordial Energy]] during Victor's temporal development project, supported him during confrontations involving higher cosmic authorities, and remained among the few family members capable of assisting him directly during operations that exceeded the capabilities of most Gods.[13][14][53]
Despite Victor's overwhelming strength, Jeanne continues expressing concern for his well-being. During the Sector merger and subsequent developments, she remained attentive to the strain imposed upon his body and Soul, while Victor reciprocated by recognizing her exhaustion and ordering her to rest when necessary.[54]
Their relationship therefore combines affection, supernatural partnership, parental responsibility and the immense trust necessary for Beings who regularly participate in the manipulation of cosmic structures.

#### The Universal Tree
[[The Universal Tree]] is Jeanne's brother, the Primordial Being she was originally created to protect, and the oldest known member of her family.[1]
Jeanne's relationship with him began when he existed only as a seed. She remained beside the developing Tree through an immense period of growth, fulfilling the responsibility assigned to her by [[Primordial Chaos]]. Once the Tree became conscious, he recognized the importance of her protection and attempted to grant her a reward.[1]
Jeanne requested a family rather than greater power or knowledge. Because a genuinely affectionate relationship could not simply be manufactured through arbitrary supernatural force, the Tree offered to become her family himself. The two accepted one another as brother and sister, establishing a relationship independent of their original duties.[1]
The familial terminology does not depend upon conventional chronological age. Jeanne existed before the Tree developed consciousness, but the two use sibling designations according to the relationship they chose. Jeanne frequently treats him as an older brother despite having protected him during his earliest development.[1][2]
Their relationship also possesses a profound supernatural foundation. Jeanne remains directly connected to the Universal Tree through her Guardian Soul and can access exceptionally pure [[Primordial Energy]] associated with his existence. However, she does not automatically possess the same complete authority over Life, Reincarnation and the maintenance of the Universe that belongs to her brother.[2][9]
The Universal Tree's relationship with Jeanne also extends to the Elderblood family. He recognizes her children and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wider household as part of his extended family, and this connection influences his decisions when external authorities threaten individuals close to Jeanne.[15]
Their interactions retain a surprisingly ordinary emotional character. Jeanne can become embarrassed when her brother discusses her marriage, while the Universal Tree occasionally behaves like an affectionate relative rather than the distant Primordial authority ordinarily associated with the maintenance of Life.[15]

#### Primal Chaos
[[Primordial Chaos]] is the Being responsible for Jeanne's original creation and the assignment of her Guardian responsibility. Jeanne was created to protect the seed that would become the Universal Tree, connecting her existence directly to the earliest stages of Creation.[5][30]
Although Jeanne possesses extraordinary knowledge of the Universe and its spiritual foundations, she does not completely understand why Primordial Chaos created her in the precise manner he did or why certain abilities remained within her existence after the completion of her initial duty. When she recovered additional powers during her Dragon development, she wondered why her Creator had permitted her to retain them rather than removing them once the original assignment had been fulfilled.[5]
Jeanne also explained that Primordial Chaos did not return to provide additional instruction after assigning her responsibility. As a result, her knowledge concerning his decisions remains incomplete despite her ancient age and exceptional proximity to the origins of Creation.[30]
Her relationship with Primordial Chaos is therefore best understood as one of creation and assigned purpose rather than the ordinary familial relationship she later established with the Universal Tree.

#### Vlad Tepes
[[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] is Jeanne's former husband, the father of her son Adam, and the Vampire Progenitor who saved her from execution after she assumed the destiny of Jeanne d'Arc.[1]
Jeanne originally owed her continued physical life to Vlad's intervention. He rescued her when she was condemned to the stake and subsequently transformed her physical vessel into a Vampire, allowing the Spirit within it to continue living through a body capable of prolonged supernatural existence.[1]
Their marriage eventually produced Adam and gave Jeanne the family she had long desired. However, Vlad's increasing emotional absence and the burdens associated with his rulership gradually undermined their relationship. Jeanne became increasingly dissatisfied with the position of [[Vampire Queen]] because it no longer provided the personal companionship and affection she considered essential.[11]
Following her awakening, Jeanne refused to return to the marriage and sought independence alongside Morgana. Their subsequent disagreements with Vlad centered upon his treatment of his wives and children, particularly the belief that political responsibilities did not excuse neglecting individuals who depended upon his affection and guidance.[11][32]
Jeanne's later relationship with Vlad is defined primarily by their shared connection to Adam rather than renewed romance. She no longer regards him as the center of her personal life, having established a different family and marriage with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].

#### Adam Tepes
[[Adam Tepes]] is Jeanne's biological son with Vlad and one of her earliest personal attachments after becoming a Vampire.[11]
Jeanne's concern for Adam remained constant throughout the collapse of her first marriage. She wanted him to develop into an independent and capable individual rather than remaining sheltered beneath the authority of the Vampire King. She consequently encouraged his training under [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], whose approach to supernatural development emphasized discipline, independence and the willingness to confront personal weaknesses.[20]
After joining the Elderblood family, Adam eventually earned access to Victor's Dragon lineage. He passed the necessary trials and progressed from Lesser Dragon into Dragon, obtaining a more advanced racial foundation through his own efforts.[21]
Nevertheless, Jeanne remained dissatisfied with his development. Despite possessing considerable spiritual potential, Adam's combat classification remained around the Planetary level during the later Imperial era, far below what Jeanne believed he should be capable of achieving. She recognized that his greatest problem involved motivation and training rather than the complete absence of natural talent.[21]
Jeanne resolved to intensify his training because she wanted him capable of protecting himself and his younger sister Isabelle. Her frustration therefore originated from maternal concern rather than disappointment in his personal value. She did not want Adam to become dependent upon the protection of stronger relatives when his own potential should have allowed considerably greater development.[21]

#### Isabelle Elderblood
[[Isabelle Elderblood]] is Jeanne's biological daughter with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and one of the most unusual members of the Elderblood second generation.[6]
Jeanne carried Isabelle for approximately 1,990 years, an extraordinary gestation associated with the fundamental nature of the Divine power developing within the child. Isabelle eventually acquired a Divinity associated with Energy itself, demonstrating the exceptional supernatural potential produced through the union of Victor and Jeanne.[6][47]
Her daughter possesses a profound relationship with Primal Energy and the mechanisms underlying supernatural power. However, Jeanne's ancient Guardian position should not be treated as an ordinary inheritable Bloodline, since she previously explained that the [[Primordial Energy]] connected to her original role was bound directly to her Soul rather than automatically transmitted through biological reproduction.[2]
Jeanne is deeply proud of Isabelle but recognizes the dangers accompanying extraordinary inherited power. She remains involved in her daughter's education and supports the supervision of Divine development, believing that knowledge and discipline are necessary even for children whose natural abilities exceed those of most established Gods.[47][21]

#### Morgana Elderblood
[[Morgana Elderblood]] is Jeanne's closest friend among [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wives and one of the individuals who understands her history most completely.[11]
The two women were formerly married to Vlad and experienced similar emotional neglect within the Royal household. Their eventual awakening and refusal to continue accepting that treatment led them to leave [[Nightingale]] together, creating the circumstances that brought both into contact with Victor.[11]
Although their personalities differ greatly, they possess an unusually strong friendship. Jeanne is generally compassionate, patient and restrained, while Morgana is more openly provocative, sarcastic and willing to employ Demonic methods. Their contrasting temperaments frequently produce amusing interactions, but the differences do not undermine the trust established through their shared experiences.[11][29]
Morgana also enjoys teasing Jeanne concerning her relationship with Victor. Despite Jeanne's public image as a former Saint and composed Empress, she can become embarrassed or affectionate when Victor is involved, and Morgana frequently exposes these reactions for her own amusement.[29]
Both women eventually became important members of the Elderblood family, transforming their shared departure from Vlad into the beginning of entirely different lives.

#### Nero Elderblood
[[Nero Elderblood]] is one of Jeanne's adoptive daughters through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family and a member of the younger generation toward whom Jeanne displays considerable maternal affection.
Jeanne recognizes [[Nero]] as more than an extraordinarily powerful supernatural combatant. Nero's childhood involved persecution, physical suffering and the absence of a stable family, making her eventual incorporation into Victor's household especially important.
Jeanne's experience as an ancient Guardian and mother contributes to her concern for Nero's development. Although Nero possesses enormous combat talent and natural determination, Jeanne supports the broader education necessary for the younger generation to understand the consequences of using their extraordinary abilities.

#### Ophis Elderblood
Ophis Elderblood is another of Jeanne's adoptive daughters and a member of the family whose history closely reflects Jeanne's personal values.
Ophis experienced considerable emotional isolation after the death of her mother, Hana, while Vlad's grief and political responsibilities prevented him from providing the attention she required. Jeanne understands the consequences of this neglect because her own marriage to Vlad was damaged by similar behavior.
Ophis addresses Jeanne as Mother Jeanne, reflecting the Elderblood family's tradition of recognizing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wives as maternal figures beyond the children each woman biologically conceived.
Jeanne's affectionate and protective temperament makes the relationship natural, while Ophis' continued attachment to Victor reflects the importance of the family structure Jeanne had sought throughout her own ancient existence.

#### The World Trees
Jeanne possesses a special relationship with the [[World Trees]] through her position as Guardian and sister of their original progenitor, the Universal Tree.[2]
When Roxanne learned the nature of Jeanne's relationship with the Universal Tree, she immediately recognized Jeanne as an aunt. Jeanne accepted the designation according to the familial bond she shared with her brother, allowing the younger World Trees to regard her as a member of their extended family.[2]
Their relationship is supported by more than family terminology. Jeanne's [[Primordial Energy]] and ancient knowledge make her unusually familiar with the supernatural processes associated with World Trees, while her brother remains responsible for the larger system governing their existence.[2][9]
Jeanne has also participated directly in projects involving the development of living supernatural worlds, including the use of Primordial Energy to support the environmental and spiritual processes necessary for a functioning planet.[39]
Nevertheless, Jeanne's familial position does not grant her unrestricted ownership of every World Tree or every conceptual authority possessed by the Universal Tree. Her responsibilities and abilities remain distinct from those of her brother.

#### The Heavenly Father
The [[Heavenly Father]] is the God who originally granted Jeanne the Saintly Blessing associated with the identity of Jeanne d'Arc.
During her historical life, Jeanne acted as a Saint whose Holy abilities were connected to the Biblical Divine system. Even after Vlad transformed her physical vessel into a Vampire, the supernatural relationship had not completely disappeared, allowing her to call upon the Heavenly Father's authority under exceptional circumstances.[17]
During the Demonic invasion of [[Nightingale]], Jeanne requested permission to invoke a Divine Judgment. The Heavenly Father allowed her to use the power while warning that the Holy element could destroy her incompatible Vampire vessel. The exchange demonstrated that their relationship remained active despite the considerable time that had passed since Jeanne's historical Saint period.[17]
However, Jeanne's subsequent development moved her away from dependence upon borrowed Holy power. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Servitude Ritual removed the remaining influence of the Heavenly Father's Blessing, while her eventual transformation into a Blood Dragon allowed her to generate genuine Holy Light through her own supernatural existence.[9][8]
Her current Holy nature is therefore no longer dependent upon maintaining the former arrangement with the Biblical God.

### Role in the Elderblood Empire
Jeanne occupies an unusual position within the [[Elderblood Empire]] because her responsibilities arise from several different aspects of her identity. She is simultaneously an Imperial Empress, the wife of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], the original Guardian of the Universal Tree, a senior maternal figure of the Elderblood family and the commander of one of its specialized Imperial Legions.[2][3]
Her extraordinary spiritual history provides knowledge unavailable to most supernatural administrators. She understands the relationships between Souls, Racial development, Divine Concepts, the [[World Trees]] and the larger systems supporting Life. This makes her valuable not merely as a combatant but as an instructor and adviser when the Empire encounters problems involving the fundamental structure of supernatural existence.[2][27]

#### The Silent Judgment
Jeanne commands the Sixth Imperial Legion, [[The Silent Judgment]], which functions as a major instrument of Imperial authority against rebellion, corruption and territories requiring direct intervention.[3]
The organization differs from Legions dedicated primarily to intelligence gathering, technological research or ordinary military conquest. Its responsibilities involve identifying and confronting threats whose continued existence undermines the security or established authority of the Empire, requiring personnel capable of exercising substantial supernatural force under strict command.[3]
Jeanne's former experiences as a Saint, [[Vampire Queen]] and Guardian provide a foundation for understanding the responsibilities accompanying such authority. She possesses the power required to confront exceptionally dangerous opponents, but also understands the consequences of using overwhelming force without considering the surrounding population or environment.
[[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]] can assume command of the Legion when Jeanne is unavailable, providing the organization with continuity rather than requiring every operation to depend upon Jeanne's direct presence.[3]

#### Imperial Education
Jeanne is among the senior members of the family responsible for teaching the younger generation. Her instruction includes subjects involving Souls, Divinity, supernatural inheritance and the structure of Creation.[27]
Her experience gives her a particularly broad perspective because she has personally occupied several distinct supernatural states. She understands the limitations of borrowed Divine power through her Saint identity, the incompatibilities of supernatural [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] through her Vampire vessel, and the deeper spiritual structures associated with her Guardian existence.
During later educational discussions, Jeanne and the other Mothers recognized that extraordinary inherited potential could become dangerous if the younger generation neglected fundamental theory. She continued supporting disciplined instruction despite [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s tendency to indulge his daughters.[29][28]

#### Cosmic-Scale Support
Jeanne's connection to [[Primordial Energy]] makes her one of the Empire's most important sources of supernatural support during enormous cosmic operations.[12][14]
She participated in the construction and restoration of living environments, supported the development of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s new world, and contributed extraordinary quantities of Energy to the temporal acceleration that allowed the Empire to experience approximately 200,000 years of internal growth within a much shorter external period.[39][52]
The continuous expenditure improved her own control and brought her increasingly close to her original Guardian peak. Her contribution therefore represented both an essential service to the Empire and a major stage in the restoration of her ancient existence.[14]

### Legacy
Jeanne's significance extends beyond the powers associated with her Guardian position. Her history connects several otherwise separate eras and supernatural institutions: the earliest development of the Universal Tree, the establishment of primordial spiritual relationships, the emergence of Human civilization, the historical Saint of Orléans, the Vampire Kingdom of [[Nightingale]] and the eventual expansion of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Empire.[1][2]
Her original existence demonstrates that Creation contained extraordinarily powerful spiritual Beings long before the appearance of modern Gods, Vampires and supernatural societies. The fact that Jeanne could assume different physical [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] without replacing her essential Spirit also illustrates that a Being's true existence cannot always be determined through the body it presently inhabits.[2]
Her relationship with the Universal Tree provides another example of the distinction between supernatural obligation and genuine affection. Jeanne was created to protect the Tree, but their familial relationship developed through mutual recognition rather than merely through the assignment given by [[Primordial Chaos]]. That relationship continues influencing the decisions of both individuals even after the Tree assumes responsibilities affecting the entire Universe.[1][15]
Jeanne's transformation into a Blood Dragon also demonstrates that possessing an extraordinarily ancient and powerful Soul does not necessarily eliminate the importance of physical compatibility. Her original Spirit possessed abilities that her Human and Vampire bodies could not safely support, while the True Dragon vessel provided a new foundation through which those powers could be expressed and refined.[9][8]
The development of her family represents the personal completion of another important aspect of her history. Jeanne once wandered without a stable identity, accepted another child's Fate, experienced betrayal as a Saint and suffered emotional neglect as a [[Vampire Queen]]. Her marriage to Victor, relationship with Isabelle and Adam, friendship with Morgana and continued bond with the Universal Tree eventually gave her the companionship and belonging that had motivated her from the beginning.[1][6]
By the later Imperial era, Jeanne remains one of the greatest supernatural beings associated with the Elderblood family, but her greatest personal achievement is not measured through the galaxies she can control or the [[Primordial Energy]] she can supply. It is the family she has established across several different forms of existence, fulfilling the wish she originally expressed when the Universal Tree first asked what reward she desired.[1][14]

### Abilities

#### Powers and Abilities
Jeanne Elderblood is an extraordinarily ancient Primordial Spirit, the original Guardian of [[The Universal Tree]], and a Blood Dragon whose abilities originate from several fundamentally different aspects of her existence. Her true nature is not defined by the Human, Vampire or Dragon bodies she has inhabited. She was created by [[Primordial Chaos]] before the formation of the present Universe and entrusted with protecting the seed that would eventually become the Universal Tree. Her original Spirit possesses an exceptional connection to Creation and a unique Guardian inheritance granting access to the abilities of her brother, although the fundamental Authorities responsible for maintaining the Universe remain exclusive to him.[1][2][3]
During her earliest known existence, Jeanne served as the Universal Tree's first Guardian, possessing powers intended to protect a Being responsible for Life, Reincarnation and the maintenance of Creation. Her original strength was extraordinary even compared with the Gods who would appear much later. However, after losing much of her ancient memory and assuming the identity of the historical Jeanne d'Arc, she operated through physical vessels that could not fully support her true spiritual existence. Her Human body, subsequent Noble Vampire transformation and dependence upon the Heavenly Father's Saintly Blessing imposed limitations upon powers originating from an existence far older than those physical forms.[1][4]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s transformation of Jeanne into a genuine True Dragon changed these circumstances. Although his Blood Dragon lineage could not overwrite her ancient Spirit, it provided a physical vessel naturally compatible with Creation. Her Spirit subsequently began assimilating with the body, allowing previously inaccessible powers to return and enabling her to manipulate [[Primordial Energy]] with greater efficiency. She simultaneously developed the intrinsic attributes of Holy Light and Wind, while her original Nature-related powers resurfaced through the restoration of her Guardian existence.[5]
Jeanne's supernatural development continued throughout the Imperial era. She became capable of controlling approximately one or two galaxies through her own power, while her full Guardian potential and cooperation with the Universal Tree were described as extending toward Sector and Universal scales respectively. During the Empire's prolonged temporal acceleration, her Energy reserves and control increased approximately threefold, leaving only Victor and Azathoth above her in pure Energy within the family at that stage. Her present existence consequently combines exceptional Energy quantity, advanced control, enormous spiritual resilience, large-scale restorative powers and the potential to eventually surpass the Guardian state she originally possessed.[6][7]
Jeanne's abilities are organized according to their actual sources. Her original Primordial Spirit and Guardian inheritance are distinguished from the characteristics of her current True Dragon vessel, while the Holy techniques borrowed from the Heavenly Father are retained separately under Former Powers. Abilities confirmed through direct demonstrations are distinguished from those established through statements concerning her Guardian inheritance and those representing future development.

#### Primordial Spirit Physiology
Primordial Spirit Physiology constitutes Jeanne's true nature. Unlike ordinary supernatural beings whose physical Race defines their spiritual foundation, Jeanne existed as an ancient Spirit long before acquiring the Human body associated with Jeanne d'Arc. She subsequently inhabited Vampire and True Dragon bodies without losing the underlying spiritual identity created by [[Primordial Chaos]].[1][2]
Jeanne explains that Higher-Level Spirits can assume physical bodies belonging to different [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] while preserving a spiritual essence distinct from the new body. In her case, this separation was particularly pronounced because her Soul had already attained a quality and scale far beyond ordinary Mortal, Divine and Draconic existence. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s earlier Progenitor transformation therefore strengthened Jeanne's Vampire body without fundamentally altering the Spirit residing within it.[2][4]

Jeanne initially struggled to fully employ it through her incompatible physical vessels, but [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s transformations gradually restored her control.[4][5]
Jeanne explains that although this Energy is fundamentally associated with natural and peaceful processes, its extraordinary quality makes it potentially destructive to Beings existing below the primordial level. The same Energy can therefore support healing, environmental restoration, planetary development and defensive barriers while remaining capable of causing catastrophic damage when used without restraint.[4]

##### Combat Power Versus Energy Reserves
Despite Jeanne's enormous Energy reserves, the novel distinguishes raw supernatural power from practical combat effectiveness. [[Scathach]] possesses less total Energy but is considered more dangerous in direct combat because of her exceptional [[Martial Arts]], technical specialization and experience.[27]
Jeanne's greatest strengths therefore include Energy, spiritual scale, Guardian inheritance, restoration and large-scale environmental manipulation, while Scathach remains more specialized in direct Martial combat.

#### Resistances and Immunities
Jeanne's defensive characteristics originate from several overlapping sources: her ancient spiritual foundation, Guardian connection to the Universal Tree, intrinsic Holy Light, Blood Dragon body and control over [[Primordial Energy]]. These characteristics should be distinguished from absolute invulnerability, since resistance demonstrated against one supernatural effect does not automatically establish immunity to every stronger variation.

##### Physical Resistances
- Supernatural Physical Resistance: Jeanne possesses the enormous natural durability of a True Dragon vessel.[5]
- Enhanced Damage Resistance: Her current body can contain supernatural power that would have damaged her former physical vessels.[5]
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Jeanne possesses the regenerative properties associated with Blood Dragon physiology.[5]
- Environmental Resistance: Her genuine Draconic body possesses extraordinary resilience against environments that would endanger ordinary biological beings.[5]
- No Ordinary Bodily Weakness: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] specifically identifies Jeanne as lacking the conventional bodily weaknesses expected of ordinary [[True Dragons]] because of her connection to the Universal Tree.[9]

##### Spiritual Resistances
- Racial Alteration Resistance: Jeanne's original Spirit cannot be casually replaced through ordinary alteration of the body surrounding it.[4]
- Soul-Based Persistence: Her spiritual existence continues beyond the limitations of a single physical vessel.[2][9]
- Spiritual Resilience: Jeanne possesses an extraordinarily large and high-quality Soul.[4][5]
- Linked [[Immortality]]: Her Guardian existence remains connected to the survival of the Universal Tree.[9]
- Resistance to Conventional Death: Ordinary physical destruction is insufficient to explain the complete eradication of Jeanne's existence.[9]
- Spiritual Transformation Resistance: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s earlier Progenitor influence could improve her physical body but not overwrite the underlying Spirit.[4]

##### Elemental Resistances
- Holy Light Compatibility: Jeanne can generate and control sacred Light without suffering the former incompatibility associated with her Vampire vessel.[5]
- Resistance to Holy Self-Damage: Her current body does not suffer the same damage caused by the [[Heavenly Father]]'s borrowed [[Holy Energy]] during her Vampire era.[20][5]
- Darkness Compatibility: Jeanne's Holy Light attribute does not impose the corresponding weakness toward Darkness normally associated with conventional Holy-aligned beings.[5]
- [[Natural Energy]] Compatibility: Her Dragon vessel possesses extraordinary compatibility with Creation and Nature-related Energy.[5]

##### Temporal Resistance
- Time-Stop Resistance — Demonstrated Circumstance: Jeanne remained unaffected when Time was stopped during the Universal Tree's intervention in Chapter 821.[11]
- Temporal Suspension Awareness: She remained conscious while the surrounding individuals were frozen.[11]

##### Defensive Energy Abilities
- Primordial Barrier Creation: Jeanne can protect others with [[Primordial Energy]] barriers.[17]
- Group Shielding: Her barriers can cover several allies simultaneously.[17]
- Eldritch Backlash Protection: She shielded allies from the dangerous environmental effects of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Eldritch transformation.[17]
- Environmental Defense: Jeanne can use Primordial Energy to restore or stabilize damaged surroundings.[12]
- Extreme Energy Endurance: She can sustain extraordinary Energy expenditure throughout prolonged cosmic operations.[16][7]
