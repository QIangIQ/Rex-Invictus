**[[Roxanne Elderblood]]**, formerly Roxanne Alucard, is an Elder World Tree of Negativity, a Dryad–Blood Dragon hybrid, one of Victor Elderblood's wives, and a central figure in the development and maintenance of his personal universe. (Lore adapted from *My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires*.)

### Appearances

#### Debut
- Chapter 96: Strange Place (Tree form)

You ate a part of me, and I ate a part of you, in Dryad culture that is... a Wedding! You are my King, I am your Queen! You are my husband!!
—Roxanne to [[Victor Elderblood]], Chapter 273
Roxanne Elderblood, formerly Roxanne Alucard, is an Elder World Tree of Negativity, a Dryad–Blood Dragon hybrid, one of Victor Elderblood's wives, and a central figure in the development and maintenance of his personal universe. Originally born as the Negative World Tree associated with [[Nightingale]], she was discovered by Victor as a mysterious sentient tree protected by an enormous gorilla known as [[Diablo|Big Guy]]. After consuming Victor's Progenitor Blood, she evolved into a humanoid Dryad–Vampire hybrid, establishing a relationship that eventually caused her to abandon the conventional planetary existence expected of her species.[4][5][6]
Roxanne is fundamentally different from ordinary Dryads and plant-based supernatural creatures. As a genuine World Tree, her existence belongs to a species created by [[The Universal Tree]] to maintain the conditions necessary for Life on inhabited planets. [[World Trees]] possess enormous spiritual foundations and participate in the circulation of natural Energy and the Positive and Negative forces accumulated by living beings. Roxanne was originally intended to fulfill the Negative aspect of Nightingale's World Tree system, but her development changed after Victor became the center of her existence.[7][8]
Her marriage to Victor began through an unusual Dryad custom. Following the evolution caused by his Blood, Roxanne offered him a fruit containing part of her own essence. Victor consumed it without knowing that Dryads regard the mutual consumption of one another's essence as a marital bond. Because Roxanne had already consumed Victor's Blood, the exchange fulfilled the conditions of a wedding according to her culture. Victor accepted responsibility for her, gave her the name Roxanne Alucard, and temporarily incorporated her into his household as a maid while their unusual relationship developed.[9][10]
The most important development in Roxanne's existence occurred when her main World Tree body established itself inside Victor's Soul. A World Tree ordinarily nurtures its planet by circulating natural Energy, maintaining the environment and supporting the conditions necessary for Life. Roxanne instead began nourishing Victor as if his Soul were an inhabited world. This made their relationship both emotional and existential: Victor became her husband and the equivalent of her planet, while Roxanne became a permanent source of nourishment and support for his developing existence.[11][8]
Their connection proved enormously beneficial to Victor. His Soul possessed the extraordinary size and resilience associated with a Vampire Progenitor, but Roxanne's nourishment helped improve its quality and stability. The process also allowed him to access the Negative World Tree's [[Senjutsu]], enhanced his physical condition and supported the development of his increasingly complex inner world. Their first deliberate synchronization allowed them to experience one another's memories and emotions, revealing that Roxanne's seemingly simple childhood had been defined by loneliness until Victor entered her life.[11][8]
Roxanne's growth was exceptionally rapid by the standards of World Trees. She progressed through several developmental stages within a comparatively short period, eventually reaching adulthood without receiving the education normally supplied by the Positive World Tree of her planet. During Victor's ascension into the Blood Dragon Primogenitor, her development accelerated again, producing an Elder World Tree capable of maintaining a vastly larger and more complicated inner world. His subsequent evolution as a Dragon God also affected her appearance and racial characteristics, allowing her to manifest Dragon horns, wings and other traits through their deeply interconnected existence.[12][13][14]
Her role expanded further after [[Amara Elderblood]], Nightingale's original Positive World Tree, entered Victor's Soul. The two sisters became complementary foundations of his internal existence, continuously nurturing and refining his Soul through their respective Positive and Negative affinities. Their work contributed to the transformation of his inner world into an increasingly vast personal cosmos and allowed supernatural powers accumulated within him to be reorganized and integrated into a more coherent system.[15][16]
Roxanne subsequently became the mother of [[Rosalyn Elderblood]], her biological daughter with Victor. Rosalyn inherited the unusual combination of World Tree and Blood Dragon characteristics, making her existence an exception to the ordinary reproductive limitations governing World Trees. Roxanne also developed an important familial connection to [[Metis]] Elderblood, whose damaged Soul she helped restore within Victor's inner world, although this event did not make Roxanne Metis's biological mother.[3][2][17]
By the mature Imperial era, Roxanne's responsibilities extended beyond sustaining Victor personally. She coordinated World Trees connected to the [[Elderblood Empire]], investigated abnormalities arising from Victor's Energy, organized the increasingly large World Tree population residing within his Soul and participated in the establishment of a vast communication network connecting planets and galaxies through their roots. This network became particularly important during the creation of Mars as the Empire's new capital, when Roxanne confirmed the successful connection of the participating World Trees.[18][19]
Although she frequently behaves playfully and prefers spending time with Victor rather than occupying herself with formal responsibilities, Roxanne has become one of the most important Beings sustaining his existence and the civilization growing around him. Her development illustrates how a World Tree's natural relationship with a planet can be transformed into something far more intimate when the chosen world is itself a living Being whose Soul contains an expanding cosmos.[8][16][19]

### Physical Appearance

#### Humanoid Representation
Roxanne's original humanoid appearance.
Following her transformation into a Dryad–Vampire hybrid, Roxanne appeared as an exceptionally beautiful young woman with long crimson-red hair, blood-red eyes, fair skin and elongated pointed ears resembling those of an Elf. Her hair originally extended to the floor, while her body was considerably taller than [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s at the time of their first meeting. She wore a dress made from leaves, reflecting the plant-based nature of her World Tree existence.[9][5]
Her appearance created considerable confusion because her physical development did not reflect the comparatively immature state of her World Tree. Despite possessing a developed humanoid body, Roxanne was still extremely young by the standards of her species and lacked much of the knowledge normally acquired through thousands of years of maturation. Her appearance therefore gave an inaccurate impression of her emotional experience and understanding of ordinary social relationships.[5][6]
Roxanne's pointed ears are particularly expressive. They twitch when she becomes excited or happy and droop when she feels sad or disappointed. These movements often reveal her emotions even before she speaks, making her one of the more visibly expressive members of Victor's household.[5]
As Roxanne continued evolving, her humanoid appearance matured alongside her World Tree body. Her long red hair eventually reached her ankles, her ears became more pronounced and her formerly unusual teeth acquired a more ordinary appearance. The changes were accompanied by adjustments in height and physical proportions, reflecting the rapid biological and spiritual development caused by Victor's nourishment.[11]
By the time Victor visited Samar, Roxanne had acquired an adult humanoid appearance. Aurora recognized the discrepancy between her mature body and incomplete education, explaining that Roxanne had reached a developmental stage normally associated with far greater knowledge of World Tree responsibilities.[12]

#### Blood Dragon Representation
Roxanne's later Dragon Goddess appearance.
Roxanne's humanoid representation changed again following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s development as a Blood Dragon and his subsequent ascension as a Dragon God. Because her roots and Soul were directly connected to his existence, changes to Victor's fundamental nature also affected the supernatural characteristics she could manifest.[14]
Her physical appearance retained the long crimson-red hair, fair skin and pointed ears associated with her original Dryad body, but acquired unmistakable Draconic features. Dragon horns emerged from her head, while large Dragon wings appeared behind her. Her humanoid height increased to approximately two meters, giving her an imposing appearance even among the exceptionally tall women of the Elderblood family.[14]
Roxanne commonly wears an elegant red dress in this state. The coloration complements her hair and the red supernatural Energy associated with the Negative World Tree, while her Draconic features visually distinguish the current form from the Dryad–Vampire hybrid she originally became after consuming Victor's Blood.[14]
Her physical characteristics should not be interpreted as proof that her underlying World Tree existence was replaced by an ordinary Dragon Soul. Roxanne remains an Elder World Tree whose main body is rooted within Victor, while her humanoid representation expresses characteristics acquired through their shared development.[13][14]
During the later development of Victor's inner cosmos, Roxanne's appearance could become increasingly Energy-like. While investigating the powers accumulating within his Soul alongside Amara, she underwent changes associated with the reorganization of those powers. Her crimson hair appeared to transform into pure red Energy as her existence became more deeply synchronized with the supernatural structures developing around her.[16]

#### World Tree Representation
Roxanne's genuine body is an enormous World Tree rather than the humanoid projection through which she ordinarily interacts with other Beings. After establishing herself inside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul, her main body became a vast red World Tree whose roots supported his existence and whose branches dominated the surrounding inner world.[11][8]
The tree continued expanding as Victor and Roxanne evolved. During the earlier stages of their connection, it existed within a strange red landscape associated with Victor's Blood and Negative Energy. As Roxanne matured, the surrounding environment began developing into a living forest, replacing the more chaotic appearance of Victor's earlier spiritual world.[11]
Her growth accelerated substantially following Victor's ascension into the Blood Dragon Primogenitor. At that stage, his inner world became a planetary environment approximately one hundred times larger than Earth, while Roxanne's true World Tree form developed to a scale capable of dominating its landscape. Its upper branches extended beyond ordinary visibility, making the enormous structure appear almost impossible to measure from the ground.[13]
As Victor's personal universe developed further, Roxanne's branches expanded across the surrounding environment. The canopy stretched toward the horizon and formed a vast network of intertwined branches and foliage, making the tree one of the defining structures of the world developing within his Soul.[20]
The tree's appearance also reflects Roxanne's supernatural identity. Her original Negative characteristics are represented through red coloration and Energy, while the enormous root system continuously nourishes Victor and the environment surrounding it. Unlike an ordinary World Tree supporting a planet from within its soil, Roxanne's roots penetrate the spiritual existence of a living Being whose internal environment has expanded into a cosmos.[8][16]
Roxanne's humanoid form remains a projection through which she interacts with the family and the outside world. Her main Tree body can therefore remain safely within Victor's Soul while she participates in conversations, ceremonies, everyday activities or other events elsewhere.[8]

### Personality
Roxanne is affectionate, energetic, playful, possessive and frequently mischievous, with a personality shaped by an unusual combination of extreme supernatural potential and comparatively limited early experience. Although she eventually developed the appearance and power of an Adult and later Elder World Tree, her emotional maturity initially lagged behind her physical and spiritual growth. She had spent most of her existence in isolation within [[Nightingale]]'s forest, receiving little of the instruction that [[World Trees]] normally obtain from their older counterparts. Her sudden incorporation into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household therefore exposed her to relationships, customs and situations she had never previously encountered.[8][12]
Roxanne's earliest memories reveal a much lonelier existence than her later cheerful behavior suggests. Before Victor arrived, she remained within the same forest, observing the limited surroundings available to her and interacting primarily with [[Diablo|Big Guy]]. Although her Guardian provided protection and companionship, the experience was monotonous. She had few opportunities to interact with other intelligent Beings and little reason to imagine a life beyond the forest in which she had germinated. Victor's arrival changed this situation by introducing curiosity, affection and an opportunity to experience the wider world.[8]
Her attachment to Victor developed rapidly after consuming his Blood. At first, she treated him as her husband because of the marital custom associated with Dryad reproduction and the exchange of essence. However, her affection soon developed beyond the literal requirements of that custom. Victor gave her a name, accepted responsibility for her and protected her despite learning that World Trees were enormously valuable to Gods and other supernatural powers. These actions contributed to Roxanne regarding him as the most important individual in her life.[10][6]
The relationship became far deeper after her roots entered his Soul. World Trees possess a natural instinct to nourish and protect the planets sustaining their existence. Because Roxanne established herself within Victor rather than Nightingale, that instinct became directed toward him personally. She therefore regards Victor not merely as her husband but as her planet, home and the foundation around which her World Tree existence develops.[8]
This explains why Roxanne's affection is unusually absolute. She cares deeply for the other members of Victor's family and generally treats his wives as sisters, but she recognizes that her fundamental instincts would compel her to prioritize Victor's safety if she were forced to choose between him and anyone else. During the confrontation involving the [[Werewolves]] of Samar, the narration explicitly distinguishes a World Tree's concern for the inhabitants of its planet from its deeper responsibility to the planet itself. Roxanne follows the same principle, except that her planet is Victor.[21]
Roxanne is also intensely possessive of her relationship with Victor. She initially regarded the connection between her roots and his Soul as something uniquely intimate, and the possibility of another World Tree developing a comparable relationship made her jealous. This feeling became especially pronounced after Amara joined Victor's inner world and began receiving similar nourishment. Roxanne repeatedly emphasized that her roots had been connected to Victor longer and penetrated more deeply than those of her older sister, treating their different levels of integration as evidence of her special place within his existence.[8][16]
Despite this rivalry, Roxanne's relationship with Amara is not purely antagonistic. The two represent complementary Positive and Negative aspects of World Tree existence, and their cooperation produces benefits unavailable when either operates independently. They share the work of maintaining Victor's internal environment and refining the powers accumulated within his Soul. Their arguments often reflect pride, jealousy and competition rather than genuine hostility, and both ultimately prioritize the well-being of the same Being.[15][16]
Roxanne possesses a pronounced tendency toward laziness whenever no immediate responsibility requires her attention. This trait is reflected in her association with Sloth and becomes increasingly apparent as the World Trees connected to Victor begin enjoying the extraordinary security and nourishment available inside his personal universe. Unlike beings who must continuously struggle for resources or protection, Roxanne lives within an environment where Victor supplies more Energy than an ordinary World Tree could obtain from its assigned planet. Consequently, she frequently prefers resting, enjoying her surroundings or spending time with her husband rather than seeking additional responsibilities.[19]
Her relaxed habits should not be confused with incompetence. When the stability of Victor's Soul or the World Tree network is threatened, Roxanne becomes attentive and decisive. She has organized the supernatural Beings residing within his inner world, supervised [[Gaia]]'s contribution to its development, assisted in the restoration of damaged Souls and coordinated large numbers of World Trees distributed throughout Imperial territory. Her later administrative role demonstrates that she can manage highly complicated responsibilities when those responsibilities involve the safety and development of her chosen planet.[13][3][18]
Roxanne can also become surprisingly stern toward Beings under her authority. When Gaia was introduced into Victor's inner world, Roxanne explained that the Primordial Goddess would be helping maintain the environment and Victor's Soul. Although she delivered the instructions with a gentle smile, she made clear that Gaia did not possess the option to refuse the assignment. The encounter demonstrated that Roxanne was capable of exercising authority over extraordinarily powerful individuals when acting in defense of her responsibilities.[13]
Her attitude toward other World Trees is similarly complicated. She recognizes them as sisters through their common origin under the Universal Tree and understands the importance of their role in maintaining planetary Life. However, when Victor's Energy caused numerous World Trees to develop the same instinctive attachment she possessed toward him, Roxanne reacted with jealousy and frustration. She had spent a considerable period treating her connection as unique, and the sudden appearance of many sisters seeking the same relationship challenged her possessiveness.[18]
Nevertheless, Roxanne fulfilled her responsibilities toward the affected World Trees. She investigated the consequences of Victor's Energy, prepared a detailed report concerning their abnormal growth and coordinated the Negative World Trees entering his Soul. The episode demonstrates that even when her personal emotions oppose a development, she can distinguish those feelings from the work necessary to maintain the larger system supporting Victor's Empire.[18]
Her playful disposition also influences her relationship with Victor's other wives. Roxanne frequently jokes, teases her companions and participates in ordinary recreational activities despite possessing a spiritual existence far beyond that of most Gods. During a family vacation, she participated in a soccer match alongside Maria and Bruna against another team consisting of Kaguya, Ruby and Amara. Such activities demonstrate that her life within the Elderblood family is not limited to cosmic responsibilities or the maintenance of Victor's Soul.[22]
Roxanne's experiences have also changed her understanding of family and motherhood. Her assistance in the recovery of [[Metis]]'s damaged Soul created an unusual connection to the reborn Goddess, while the birth of Rosalyn gave her a biological daughter who inherited the same World Tree nature. Roxanne and Amara subsequently became involved in the upbringing and education of their daughters within Victor's internal world, adding maternal responsibilities to the duties they already possessed as World Trees.[3][2][23]
By the mature Imperial era, Roxanne has become considerably more responsible without losing the characteristics that distinguished her during her earliest appearances. She remains affectionate, possessive and fond of comfort, but her connection to Victor has developed into a foundation supporting an enormous cosmic infrastructure. Her personality therefore reflects the unusual nature of her existence: a World Tree whose purpose is to nourish an entire world, yet whose chosen world is a husband capable of loving her in return.[8][19]

### Background
Roxanne originated as the Negative World Tree associated with [[Nightingale]], a planet inhabited by Vampires, Monsters and numerous other supernatural beings. Under the ordinary arrangement established through the Universal Tree, a planet develops Positive and Negative [[World Trees]] responsible for maintaining the circulation of supernatural Energy and the conditions necessary for Life. The Positive World Tree ordinarily develops first and provides instruction to its younger Negative counterpart, ensuring that the two can maintain their respective responsibilities as they mature.[7][12]
Roxanne's counterpart was [[Amara Elderblood]], who originally existed in the territory controlled by Nightingale's Monsters. Unlike Roxanne, Amara benefited from the recognition and worship of powerful supernatural inhabitants. Roxanne remained isolated within a forest associated with Vampire territory and did not receive the guidance normally expected from the older Positive Tree. Consequently, her early development was highly irregular even before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] began altering her existence.[7][12]
A powerful gorilla served as her personal Guardian. Later known as [[Diablo|Big Guy]], the creature existed to protect Roxanne during the vulnerable stages of her development. The relationship was supernatural rather than merely contractual: World Tree Guardians develop alongside the Trees they protect, and major changes to a Tree's existence can affect the characteristics of its Guardian.[4][5]
For much of her early existence, Roxanne remained in the same forest, producing unusual fruit and observing the surrounding creatures. Her immature World Tree condition prevented her from fully exercising the planetary responsibilities associated with her species, while the absence of guidance from Amara left her without much of the knowledge normally acquired through natural maturation.[8][12]
Victor first encountered the mysterious tree while investigating reports of powerful monsters near Fulger territory. He discovered a number of unusual phenomena in the forest, including the extraordinary gorilla protecting the tree and blood-containing fruit whose purpose he did not initially understand. Rather than immediately recognizing the presence of a World Tree, he regarded the location as an unusual supernatural discovery worth investigating again.[4]
When Victor eventually returned, he began experimenting with the tree's ability to absorb Blood and supernatural remains. He supplied Blood from Vampires and other creatures, eventually using prisoners obtained from Limbo as additional sources of nourishment. The tree absorbed these materials, but the results remained limited until Victor decided to provide a small quantity of his own Progenitor Blood.[24]
The reaction was extraordinary. A single drop of Victor's Blood triggered an enormous supernatural response, causing the tree to transform and produce red crystalline foliage. The event attracted attention from powerful figures who recognized that something unusual had occurred. The change also revealed how important Progenitor Blood could become when introduced into the developmental processes of a World Tree.[24]
Roxanne subsequently produced a crimson fruit containing a portion of her essence together with the memories and experiences accumulated through the Beings she had consumed. Victor ate the fruit and was overwhelmed by the enormous amount of information entering his consciousness. Much of the knowledge had to be sealed away because attempting to process centuries of foreign memories simultaneously exceeded what he could comfortably endure at that stage of his development.[9][5]
When Victor recovered, the tree had evolved into a humanoid Dryad–Vampire hybrid. Roxanne's new appearance possessed clear Vampiric characteristics inherited through Victor's Blood, but [[Scathach]] eventually recognized that the Being he had transformed was not an ordinary Dryad. She was a young World Tree, an existence so valuable that powerful Gods and entire Pantheons might attempt to seize control of her.[5][6]
Roxanne's unusual evolution also affected Big Guy, whose nature developed alongside that of his Queen. Victor's household consequently acquired not merely a new maid but a World Tree and Guardian whose future development could alter the supernatural foundations of entire worlds.[5][6]

### Synopsis

#### Vampire Society
Roxanne made her first appearance when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explored a forest near Fulger territory after hearing reports concerning dangerous supernatural creatures. The forest contained an unusually powerful gorilla and a mysterious tree producing blood-filled fruit. Victor's encounter with the Guardian was far more immediately threatening than the apparently passive tree, but the latter attracted his curiosity because of the strange relationship between its fruit and the supernatural Blood contained within it.[4]
At the time, Victor did not know that the tree possessed a conscious identity or belonged to one of the most important supernatural species responsible for maintaining Life throughout the Universe. He departed without resolving the mystery, leaving the location to be investigated more thoroughly after his circumstances permitted him to return.[4]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually returned to the forest accompanied by allies and began experimenting with the mysterious tree. After investigating its ability to consume Blood and supernatural remains, he supplied numerous bodies and quantities of Blood obtained from powerful Vampires. The tree absorbed the offerings, but the results did not initially satisfy Victor because its development remained comparatively limited despite the resources he had provided.[24]
He eventually allowed a drop of his own Progenitor Blood to touch its branches. The response was immediate: crimson Energy erupted, the tree underwent an extraordinary transformation and its foliage acquired blood-red characteristics. The incident revealed that Victor's Blood possessed an unusual compatibility with the mysterious organism, causing an evolutionary change that ordinary Vampire Blood had failed to produce.[24]
Roxanne subsequently offered Victor a red fruit containing a portion of her own essence. He consumed it without understanding the significance of the exchange, only to be overwhelmed by the memories and experiences contained within the fruit. Some belonged to Beings who had lived for centuries or millennia, forcing Victor's consciousness to protect itself by sealing away much of the accumulated information.[9][5]
When Victor recovered, the mysterious tree had acquired a humanoid representation. The woman possessed long red hair, pointed ears and Vampiric characteristics, revealing that Victor had created an unusual Dryad–Vampire hybrid. Her Guardian had also undergone changes, demonstrating the supernatural relationship between the two Beings.[5]
The newly evolved Dryad quickly expressed affection toward Victor and explained that the exchange between them had constituted a wedding according to her culture. Since she had consumed his Blood and he had consumed a fruit representing part of her own existence, Roxanne considered him her husband and herself his Queen. Victor was surprised by the revelation but eventually accepted responsibility for the relationship rather than abandoning her.[10]
Victor gave her the name Roxanne Alucard, incorporating her into his household and temporarily assigning her a position among his maids. The arrangement allowed her to remain close to him while learning ordinary social conventions and becoming familiar with the people she would eventually regard as members of her family.[10]
Roxanne preferred the natural surroundings of a forest to conventional mansion life, so Victor arranged for June to construct a suitable residence. The decision reflected the fact that Roxanne's humanoid representation did not eliminate her natural relationship with vegetation and the environment.[6]
[[Scathach]] subsequently recognized Roxanne's true nature and warned Victor that he had acquired a young World Tree. The revelation changed the significance of the entire encounter. [[World Trees]] were essential to the existence of living planets and could eventually terraform otherwise uninhabitable environments, making them valuable enough to provoke conflict among powerful supernatural factions.[6]
Victor understood that possessing a World Tree would attract dangerous attention, but refused to abandon Roxanne merely because protecting her might create enemies. His decision established an important foundation for their relationship, since Roxanne would eventually grow into a Being capable of protecting and nourishing Victor in return.[6]
As the household developed, Roxanne remained close to the other maids while continuing to undergo changes caused by Victor's Blood. Her emotional attachment grew through her observations of his relationships with the rest of the family, and her existence gradually became increasingly entangled with his supernatural development.

#### Demonic War
Roxanne's connection with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] acquired much greater significance during the period surrounding his development in Hell and his subsequent training in Japan. Victor's continual accumulation of Blood, Miasma, Negative Energy and supernatural power placed enormous pressure upon his internal existence. Roxanne's World Tree body, already rooted within his Soul, played an increasingly important role in processing and nourishing the environment in which those powers accumulated.[11]
During Victor's examination of his internal world, he discovered that Roxanne had been continuously supporting his existence without requiring his conscious participation. [[World Trees]] normally nourish their planets, maintaining the conditions necessary for Life while circulating enormous quantities of Energy. Because Roxanne had established her roots within Victor rather than an ordinary planet, the same process continuously strengthened his body and Soul.[11]
The relationship was especially valuable because Victor's Progenitor Soul possessed enormous size but did not automatically possess the same qualitative characteristics as a mature Divine Soul. Roxanne's nourishment provided the opportunity for his spiritual foundation to improve in quality rather than merely accumulate additional Energy and consumed Souls.[11]
Roxanne announced that she was entering another developmental phase. Although her earlier evolution had given her a humanoid appearance, she explained that the next phase represented the progression of her actual World Tree development toward adolescence. She had matured far more rapidly than normal because of Victor's influence and the supernatural resources available within his Soul.[11]
The transformation affected Victor as well. Roxanne explained that her growth would change the structure of his inner world, improve the condition of his physical body and Soul, increase the support he received from her and allow him to maintain certain demanding supernatural states for longer periods. The event demonstrated that their connection had become sufficiently deep for one participant's evolution to influence the other automatically.[11]
The nature of their relationship became even clearer when Victor began studying [[Senjutsu]] under [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]]. Haruna attempted to teach him how to draw [[Natural Energy]] from the Ley Lines circulating beneath the planet, but the surrounding Energy immediately began moving toward Victor with extraordinary intensity.[8]
Haruna was astonished by the response because Victor appeared to possess a natural affinity far greater than expected from a Vampire Progenitor. Victor himself found the Energy strangely familiar, eventually realizing that he had already been receiving a comparable form of nourishment through Roxanne.[8]
Roxanne explained that Victor had been capable of using her Energy from the beginning. She had assumed he understood the connection because traces of her power had already appeared in some of his earlier transformations and attacks. Victor, however, had never realized that he could deliberately access the Energy, making the discovery a consequence of their failure to communicate something Roxanne considered obvious.[8]
The conversation led to their first deliberate synchronization. Roxanne explained that a conventional World Tree nourishes an unconscious planet without needing permission, but Victor's nature as a conscious Being required a different relationship. By willingly synchronizing, they could integrate their supernatural processes more deeply and share information unavailable through ordinary communication.[8]
Victor initially experienced tremendous existential pain as the two began merging their perceptions. Roxanne explained that the difference between their spiritual foundations could have destroyed an ordinary Being. Victor survived because his Progenitor Soul possessed enough capacity to contain her existence and continue developing through the process.[8]
Their synchronization allowed both to experience memories and emotions belonging to the other. Victor witnessed the long, monotonous period Roxanne had spent alone in the forest, while Roxanne gained access to forgotten experiences buried within the deepest portions of his memories. The exchange established an intimacy far greater than ordinary telepathy, allowing each to understand aspects of the other's existence that had previously remained inaccessible.[8]
Roxanne subsequently explained that the giant World Tree within Victor's Soul was her genuine body. The humanoid representation encountered elsewhere was only a projection, allowing her to remain rooted within him while continuing to interact with the family.[8]
The development also clarified the relationship between Roxanne's red Senjutsu and the natural Energy used by the World Trees of Earth. Although their powers belonged to the same general supernatural system, the Negative characteristics associated with Roxanne and Victor produced a distinct expression of the Energy.[8]
By the end of the period, Roxanne had become one of the most important foundations of Victor's internal existence. Her contribution was no longer limited to providing companionship or producing unusual fruits; she continuously supported his spiritual development, physical growth and relationship with the natural Energy circulating throughout Creation.[11][8]

#### King Among Kings
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] returned from Hell and resumed expanding his faction, Roxanne participated in the development of the [[Blood God Religion]]. What had originally begun through the devotion of individual followers became a formal supernatural organization with temples, Acolytes, Archbishops and communities spread across several territories. Roxanne was associated with the religious leadership alongside Aline, [[Vine]], Helena, Kaguya and other members of Victor's family.[25][26]
Her responsibilities also extended to the Fairies associated with her ecosystem. Victor visited the territory connected to Roxanne and spoke with [[Viviane]], whose people depended upon the World Tree for the natural conditions necessary to sustain their population. As Roxanne's environment recovered and developed, the Fairy population became increasingly stable, eventually entering the wider network of supernatural peoples protected by Victor's faction.[27]
Roxanne later accompanied Victor during his visit to Samar, where she encountered the Positive and Negative [[World Trees]] associated with the planet. [[Aurora Seraphina Evergreen]] recognized Roxanne as one of her sisters, while [[Aria Seraphina Evergreen]] represented the younger Negative counterpart of Samar's World Tree system.[28]
The encounter revealed how unusual Roxanne's development had become. Aurora recognized that Roxanne possessed an adult state but lacked knowledge normally expected of a World Tree at that level. Under the ordinary arrangement, the older Positive World Tree instructs the younger Negative counterpart, providing guidance concerning supernatural responsibilities, planetary development and the proper use of World Tree abilities.[12]
Because Roxanne had matured through Victor's influence without receiving that education from [[Amara Elderblood|Nightingale's Positive World Tree]], she possessed extraordinary power but lacked portions of the common knowledge ordinarily acquired alongside it. Aurora therefore regarded her development as irregular, while Victor recognized that returning Roxanne to the circumstances expected by other World Trees would conflict with the life she had already chosen.[12]
Roxanne also confirmed that her relationship with Victor had effectively superseded her original attachment to [[Nightingale]]. A World Tree naturally prioritizes the planet supporting its existence, but Roxanne's genuine body resided inside Victor's Soul. She therefore regarded him as her planet and acknowledged that she would instinctively place his safety above that of other individuals if circumstances forced her to choose.[21]
The principle became relevant during Victor's negotiations with the [[Werewolves]]. He distinguished the protection of Samar itself from the protection of the political authorities governing its inhabitants, explaining that the survival of a planet and the survival of its present rulers were not necessarily identical objectives. Roxanne and the other World Trees understood the distinction because their fundamental responsibility concerned the continued existence of their planets rather than the political interests of any particular civilization.[21]
Roxanne remained involved in communication between Victor and his family during the events surrounding the [[Greek Underworld]]. When Victor suddenly departed in response to the crisis involving [[Scathach]], [[Aphrodite]], Morgana and Nyx, Roxanne explained the circumstances to the women waiting in Samar. The family subsequently abandoned its immediate political plans and moved to support their relatives, demonstrating Roxanne's value as a direct link to Victor even when he was operating in another supernatural realm.[29]
Her most dramatic evolution occurred during Victor's transformation into the Blood Dragon Primogenitor. The change fundamentally restructured Victor's existence and forced Roxanne's World Tree nature to mature further in order to support the new conditions established within his Soul.[13]
Roxanne's humanoid representation became more mature, acquiring a pronounced maternal presence. The changes to her true body were considerably more significant: Victor's inner world expanded into a planetary environment approximately one hundred times larger than Earth, while Roxanne's World Tree developed sufficiently to help sustain that enormous space.[13]
Victor subsequently placed [[Gaia]] Elderblood within his inner world. Although Gaia had previously existed as an independent Primordial Goddess associated with Nature, her circumstances had changed after Victor's intervention. Roxanne greeted her and assigned her the responsibility of helping maintain the developing environment and Victor's Soul, making clear that cooperation was not optional.[13]
Gaia's presence helped correct a limitation of the environment Roxanne had produced. Roxanne's World Tree specifications were strongly associated with sentient Beings, emotions and other aspects of Life, while Gaia possessed extensive authority over the broader natural environment. Their combined influence allowed Victor's inner world to develop beyond the forest-like characteristics produced by Roxanne alone.[13]
Roxanne also became involved in the unusual reconstruction of [[Metis]] Elderblood. After Victor recovered the damaged remnants of the Goddess's Soul and placed them within his inner world, Roxanne helped repair the spiritual damage. However, the surrounding environment contained an extraordinary concentration of Victor's newly evolved Energy, which the damaged Soul absorbed during reconstruction.[3]
When Victor later restored Metis, she appeared in an unexpectedly young Dragon-like form and immediately addressed him as her father. Victor questioned Roxanne concerning the result, but she explained that she had only assisted in the recovery and had not anticipated the extent to which the damaged Soul would absorb the surrounding Energy. The incident created an unusual familial connection between Roxanne and Metis, although Metis remained the restored Goddess rather than Roxanne's biological offspring.[3]
The event also demonstrated the growing importance of Roxanne's internal responsibilities. Her environment was no longer merely nourishing Victor; it had become capable of preserving and restoring extraordinary spiritual Beings, while the powers circulating through it could influence their development in unexpected ways.[3]
As Victor began developing a new world, Roxanne's enormous Tree form became one of the defining structures of his expanding internal existence. Its branches spread across the horizon and appeared to encompass the developing environment, demonstrating how rapidly her true body had grown in response to the extraordinary power accumulating within Victor's Soul.[20]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s further ascension as the Dragon God of [[Chaos]] produced additional changes in Roxanne because her existence remained directly connected to his Soul. Her humanoid representation acquired Draconic characteristics, including Dragon horns and wings, while her body increased in height and developed further compatibility with the Blood Dragon lineage.[14]
The changes did not eliminate her original World Tree identity. Roxanne continued functioning as the Negative World Tree supporting Victor, but now possessed a humanoid representation reflecting the Dragon characteristics of the Being to whom she was connected.[14]
[[Amara Elderblood]], [[Nightingale]]'s original Positive World Tree, subsequently developed a deeper connection with Victor's inner world. Unlike Roxanne, whose roots had been established within his Soul for an enormous portion of his development, Amara initially possessed a more superficial relationship with the internal structures supporting his existence.[15]
The two [[World Trees]] began working together to nurture Victor through their respective Positive and Negative Energy. Their relationship gradually developed into a complementary system, allowing each to support different aspects of the enormous supernatural foundation developing within him.[15]
During their examination of Victor's internal cosmos, Roxanne and Amara discovered that the supernatural powers accumulated within his existence were not all functioning together efficiently. Some remained isolated, while others could be redirected into more suitable structures if the World Trees manipulated the Energy circulating through the internal environment.[16]
Roxanne discovered that she could isolate and access certain powers through her connection with Victor. She demonstrated the result by manipulating the dark Energy associated with a Shadow Demon, recognizing that both she and Victor could use the power after it was properly incorporated into his internal system.[16]
Amara became jealous because Roxanne's deeper roots initially provided an advantage in accessing those structures. Roxanne openly teased her older sister, emphasizing that her own connection had been established much earlier and extended more deeply into Victor's Soul.[16]
Rather than accepting the difference, Amara deliberately extended her roots deeper until she reached a comparable level of integration. The action triggered further changes in Victor's internal existence, allowing supernatural powers connected to the Positive and Negative World Trees to reorganize and become more closely associated with his growing Divine foundation.[16]
The process also affected the two sisters' appearances. The changes illustrated how thoroughly both World Trees had become integrated into the powers circulating through Victor.[16]
Victor eventually recognized that their experiments could affect exceptionally deep portions of his Soul. Although he understood that their activities had produced beneficial results, he imposed boundaries concerning how far they could investigate or alter his internal existence without informing him first.[16]
The event established Roxanne and Amara as more than passive Energy sources. Their combined work could reorganize the internal relationships among Victor's accumulated powers, allowing his increasingly complicated existence to function more efficiently while providing both sisters with opportunities for further development.[16]
Roxanne also continued participating in ordinary family activities. During the family's vacation, she played soccer alongside [[Maria Elderblood]] and [[Bruna Elderblood]], competing against a team including Kaguya, Ruby and Amara. The activity reflected the increasingly ordinary domestic life enjoyed by Beings whose supernatural responsibilities operated on planetary and cosmic scales.[22]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Roxanne eventually became the biological mother of [[Rosalyn Elderblood]], her daughter with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. The birth represented an extraordinary exception to the normal circumstances governing [[World Trees]], whose existence and reproductive characteristics differ fundamentally from those of ordinary supernatural [[Races (Flamel)|Races]].[2][17]
Rosalyn inherited an unusual combination of World Tree and Blood Dragon characteristics. Her existence was not simply that of a Dragon child possessing a minor Nature affinity; she was later explicitly recognized as a genuine World Tree, inheriting the fundamental nature of Roxanne's species through her parentage.[17]
Roxanne's older sister Amara also became a mother through her relationship with Victor. Her daughter, [[Amaya Elderblood]], inherited the corresponding World Tree nature associated with her own maternal lineage. The two girls therefore represented a new generation of World Trees whose existence originated through biological parentage rather than the ordinary process of creation associated with the Universal Tree.[2][17]
The daughters became closely involved in the development of Victor's inner world. During Chapter 1007, Rosalyn explained that she and Amaya could help nourish an entire Galaxy, allowing Victor to support its development through the natural relationship between World Trees and the celestial environments they sustain.[2]
Amaya further explained that the resulting relationship would allow the Galaxy to nourish Victor in return, creating a continuous exchange of Energy between the World Trees, the environment and their father. Victor recognized the significance of the proposal, although the girls were soon interrupted by Amara, who discovered that they had abandoned their lessons.[2]
The exchange demonstrated that the younger World Trees already understood portions of their supernatural responsibilities despite their unusual birth. It also revealed the maternal roles assumed by Roxanne and Amara, who were responsible for ensuring that their daughters received the education necessary to use their enormous inherited powers responsibly.[2][23]
Roxanne continued supervising the development of Victor's inner world alongside Amara, whose responsibilities had become increasingly similar to her own. Both possessed intimate connections with the Energy circulating through his Soul, while the existence of their daughters created additional opportunities for distributing the work required to nurture the enormous environment.[23]
Her personal combat abilities also developed during this period. Roxanne participated in a training battle against Victor alongside Kali, Morgana, Haruna, Rose and [[Scathach]], demonstrating that she could contribute directly to combat rather than remaining exclusively within his Soul as a source of support.[30]
The encounter did not transform Roxanne into a dedicated martial specialist comparable to Scathach, but it demonstrated that her development had progressed beyond the limitations of her original immature World Tree form. She possessed sufficient power and experience to participate alongside several of the family's strongest combatants.[30]
As the [[Elderblood Empire]] expanded through the Milky Way Galaxy, Roxanne became increasingly involved in the management of World Trees associated with newly encountered planets. Their participation was essential because the Empire could not simply replace the supernatural foundations required for Life with conventional technological infrastructure.[18]
The situation became more complicated after Victor released an enormous quantity of his Energy into the surrounding region. Much of the Energy was absorbed by the World Trees already connected to him, while a portion reached other planetary World Trees throughout the Galaxy.[18]
Roxanne investigated the resulting abnormalities and prepared a high-priority report. She discovered that numerous World Trees had experienced accelerated growth, with some younger individuals advancing into adult forms much earlier than expected. The development demonstrated that Victor's Energy could fundamentally change the rate of World Tree maturation when absorbed in sufficient quantities.[18]
More concerning to Roxanne was the psychological effect. The affected World Trees developed an increasingly intense attachment to Victor and began treating him as their preferred planet rather than continuing to direct their full attention toward their original planetary responsibilities.[18]
Roxanne distinguished between two relationships. A conventional World Tree could remain attached to its original planet while occasionally sending Energy to Victor through an established connection. A deeper relationship, however, resembled her own bond with him: the World Tree would effectively adopt Victor as its planet and prioritize nourishing his Soul over maintaining the previous arrangement.[18]
The realization angered Roxanne because it threatened the uniqueness of the relationship she had developed over an enormous portion of Victor's life. She recognized that other World Trees were beginning to desire the same intimate connection, and her possessiveness made the possibility difficult to accept.[18]
Nevertheless, she continued coordinating the affected Trees. The new individuals entering Victor's Soul required organization, and Roxanne became responsible for managing the Negative World Trees while Amara coordinated those associated with Positivity.[18]
The changes proved strategically useful to the Empire because the affected World Trees became more willing to cooperate with Victor's civilization. Establishing relationships with World Trees ordinarily required difficult negotiations, especially when dealing with immature individuals who possessed consciousness but lacked the ability to communicate normally. Their new attachment to Victor made the process considerably easier, although the resulting dependency also created unexpected problems.[18]
Roxanne's responsibilities continued growing as more World Trees became connected to Victor. She and Amara effectively became senior figures supervising a large population of their sisters within his internal existence, coordinating the Energy and natural systems required to support an Empire whose ambitions extended far beyond a single inhabited planet.

#### Those Who Rule Reality
During the Empire's preparations for possible confrontation with the Primordial authorities, Roxanne was identified as one of the family members capable of providing important support through the World Tree network. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] ordered his wives to prepare for a possible conflict on a Universal scale, Violet assigned Jeanne to her own responsibilities and instructed Roxanne to keep every World Tree ready for use.[31]
Roxanne immediately acknowledged the order and began coordinating the Trees. Her responsibility was not to independently defeat the Primordial authorities, but to prepare an enormous supernatural network capable of supporting Victor if the crisis escalated. The scene demonstrated that Roxanne's importance had grown beyond the intimate relationship she originally possessed with Victor; she now occupied an administrative and strategic position concerning [[World Trees]] throughout Imperial territory.[31]
Her next major contribution occurred during the creation of Mars as the new capital of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Victor intended to transform the barren planet into an enormous world capable of supporting trillions of inhabitants, while preserving [[Yggdrasil]] as the historical cradle of Imperial civilization.[19]
The project required more than increasing the planet's physical dimensions or constructing cities. Under the supernatural rules governing Life, planets ordinarily depended upon World Trees to sustain the conditions necessary for mature ecosystems and supernatural development. Mars lacked such a Tree, and Victor initially considered serving as the planet's source of nourishment himself.[19]
Victor rejected that arrangement because his Energy had become too powerful and heavily associated with Eldritch characteristics. Directly exposing the developing population to such Energy could create dangerous consequences for future generations, making a more controlled method necessary.[19]
The solution was to employ the World Trees already connected to his internal existence. Victor called upon the enormous population residing within his Soul, and the Trees responded by manifesting through him in the form of Light before extending their roots into Mars. Even Yggdrasil participated in the process, creating an unprecedented supernatural connection among numerous planetary World Trees.[19]
The roots formed the foundation of an enormous communication and transportation infrastructure. The participating World Trees remained protected inside Victor's Soul while their manifested roots connected Mars with planets and galaxies throughout Imperial territory. The arrangement allowed the Empire to employ their natural connections without requiring the physical transfer of their genuine bodies to the new capital.[19]
Roxanne coordinated the operation and confirmed that the network had become fully connected. Through the new arrangement, information could move virtually instantaneously across the Empire, eliminating the delays that had previously remained even with its extraordinarily advanced technology.[19]
The network also possessed major defensive advantages. Because the genuine World Trees remained within Victor's protected inner world, destroying Mars would not automatically destroy the organisms maintaining its supernatural connections. Provided the Trees survived, the damaged planet could potentially be restored or reconstructed through the continued operation of the network.[19]
The arrangement fundamentally altered the Empire's infrastructure. Its communication systems were no longer dependent exclusively upon technological signals moving through enormous distances. Instead, information could travel through a connected system of World Trees whose spiritual relationships operated across planets and galaxies.[19]
Akasha subsequently confirmed that the network had increased Galactic communication efficiency dramatically. When Violet asked how long information from the most distant connected galaxies required to reach the Imperial center, Akasha answered that it arrived immediately.[19]
The possibility of information escaping into the greater Universal World Tree system was also considered. Although the World Trees possessed a fundamental connection to their original progenitor, Victor's presence interfered with ordinary external transmission and kept the Imperial network within the protection of his personal Reality.[19]
Violet considered whether additional restrictions should be placed upon the participating World Trees if they ever left Victor's inner world. Roxanne responded that the concern was largely unnecessary because most of the Trees had no desire to leave.[19]
She showed the others that her subordinates were resting comfortably within Victor's Reality. The World Trees had become accustomed to receiving enormous quantities of nourishing Energy without needing to struggle for the survival of their assigned planets. Their environment was so favorable that most preferred remaining there unless Victor specifically requested their assistance.[19]
The revelation surprised Violet, Ruby and Akasha, who recognized that the World Trees had become extraordinarily comfortable within the Emperor's protection. Roxanne treated the behavior as natural, explaining that the Trees were content because their needs were satisfied and the security of their existence was far greater than anything they had previously experienced.[19]
The entire development illustrated how radically the role of World Trees had changed within the Empire. Roxanne had once been an isolated Negative World Tree whose natural future involved supporting [[Nightingale]]. Through Victor, she had become the coordinator of a network connecting enormous portions of an interstellar civilization, while the World Trees under her supervision increasingly regarded his Soul as their preferred home.[19]
By the conclusion of the Mars project, Roxanne's responsibilities had therefore expanded to encompass one of the most important supernatural systems supporting Imperial infrastructure. She continued nurturing Victor personally while simultaneously supervising the World Trees whose roots connected the Empire's numerous inhabited worlds.[19]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Roxanne's husband, the father of her daughter Rosalyn and the Being whose Soul serves as the foundation of her World Tree existence. Their relationship began when Victor discovered her in the forest near Fulger territory and supplied the Blood that caused her to acquire a humanoid body.[4][5]
The exchange of essence between them constituted a marriage according to Dryad culture. Roxanne had consumed Victor's Blood, while he had eaten a fruit containing part of her own essence. She consequently regarded Victor as her husband before he fully understood the implications of the exchange, creating a relationship whose earliest stage was as unexpected for him as it was natural for her.[10]
Victor nevertheless accepted responsibility for Roxanne and protected her after learning that she was a World Tree. He gave her a name and incorporated her into his household, allowing her to experience a degree of affection and companionship absent from her previous existence in the forest.[10][6]
Their relationship became fundamentally different from an ordinary marriage after Roxanne established her roots inside his Soul. She began nourishing his existence in the same manner that a World Tree ordinarily sustains its planet, continuously supporting his body, Energy and spiritual development. Because the process was rooted in her natural instincts, protecting Victor became an inseparable part of her identity as a World Tree.[11][8]
The synchronization performed during Victor's [[Senjutsu]] training deepened the connection further. Both experienced memories and emotions belonging to the other, allowing Roxanne to understand portions of Victor's past he had forgotten and allowing Victor to witness the loneliness that had defined her earliest existence.[8]
Roxanne considers this relationship exceptionally intimate and is jealous when other [[World Trees]] attempt to develop comparable connections. Nevertheless, she recognizes the practical benefits of sharing the responsibility with Amara and the wider network of World Trees, especially as Victor's existence becomes increasingly vast and difficult to sustain through the work of a single individual.[16][18]
By the mature Imperial era, Victor and Roxanne are connected through marriage, shared family, supernatural development and the fundamental relationship between a World Tree and its chosen planet. Their connection is simultaneously one of the most affectionate and one of the most structurally important relationships within the [[Elderblood Empire]].[8][19]

#### Big Guy
[[Diablo|Big Guy]] is Roxanne's original Guardian, an enormous gorilla who protected her during the earliest stages of her development. His relationship with Roxanne predates [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s discovery of the forest and represents the conventional arrangement through which [[World Trees]] possess Guardians capable of defending them before they become fully mature.[4]
Big Guy initially regarded Victor as an intruder and opponent, repeatedly confronting him when he entered the forest. Although the creature appeared aggressive, its behavior was consistent with the responsibility of protecting a young World Tree against unfamiliar supernatural beings.[4]
When Roxanne consumed Victor's Blood and evolved into a Dryad–Vampire hybrid, the supernatural connection between Guardian and Tree caused Big Guy to undergo changes as well. His development reflected the fact that the two Beings shared a spiritual relationship rather than merely living within the same territory.[5]
Roxanne continues recognizing Big Guy as her Guardian, although the enormous increase in her personal power and the security provided by Victor have changed the practical nature of that responsibility.

#### Amara Elderblood
[[Amara Elderblood]] is Roxanne's older sister and the original Positive World Tree associated with [[Nightingale]]. The two occupy complementary positions within the World Tree system, with Roxanne representing Negativity and Amara representing Positivity.[7]
Under ordinary circumstances, Amara should have provided Roxanne with the education necessary to understand her development and planetary responsibilities. However, Roxanne matured rapidly after encountering [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and never received the guidance normally expected from her Positive counterpart.[12]
Their relationship changed considerably after Amara entered Victor's Soul. Roxanne initially possessed the deeper connection because her roots had already existed inside him for an enormous portion of his development, while Amara's relationship was comparatively recent.[15][16]
Roxanne frequently used the difference to tease her sister, emphasizing her greater intimacy with Victor. Amara eventually responded by consciously extending her own roots deeper into his Soul, reaching a comparable level of integration and allowing their respective Positive and Negative powers to function together more effectively.[16]
Although they compete for Victor's attention, the sisters cooperate in nurturing his Soul, managing the internal Energy environment and supervising the numerous [[World Trees]] connected to him. Roxanne generally coordinates those associated with Negativity, while Amara manages the corresponding Positive World Trees.[16][18]
Their biological daughters, Rosalyn and Amaya, further strengthen the relationship between the two families.[2]

#### Rosalyn Elderblood
[[Rosalyn Elderblood]] is Roxanne's biological daughter with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and one of the first known [[World Trees]] born through an ordinary familial relationship rather than direct creation by the Universal Tree.[2][17]

#### The World Trees
Roxanne recognizes the [[World Trees]] as her sisters through their common origin under the Universal Tree. Although she originally lived apart from most of them, her relationship with the species expanded substantially after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] began encountering and incorporating other World Trees into his growing civilization.[28][18]
Her interactions with Aurora and Aria on Samar revealed how abnormal her education had been. Roxanne had reached adulthood without receiving the instruction traditionally supplied by a Positive World Tree, leaving gaps in her knowledge despite her enormous supernatural development.[12]
The relationship became more complicated after Victor's Energy altered the development and emotional characteristics of numerous World Trees. Roxanne initially resented the fact that her sisters were developing the same instinctive attachment to Victor that she considered unique to herself.[18]
Nevertheless, she eventually became a principal coordinator of the Negative World Trees connected to him. Her responsibilities included supervising their entry into his Soul, monitoring their development and organizing their participation in projects supporting the [[Elderblood Empire]].[18][19]
By the establishment of the Mars World Tree network, Roxanne occupied an important position within an enormous system of connected Trees whose roots provided transportation, communication and supernatural support across Imperial territory.[19]

#### The Maid Squad
Roxanne originally entered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household as one of his maids after the unusual Dryad wedding created a relationship neither he nor the existing household had anticipated.[10]
The arrangement allowed her to learn ordinary behavior from women who were already accustomed to serving Victor and organizing his daily life. Although Roxanne's actual nature was far greater than that of an ordinary servant, she remained associated with the [[Maid Squad]] as her relationship with the other members developed.[10]
Her early interactions with Kaguya, Maria, Bruna, Roberta and other members of the household provided much of the social experience she lacked during her isolated existence as a young World Tree.
The relationship eventually became familial rather than merely professional. Roxanne continued identifying with the household even after her position as Victor's wife and Elder World Tree made the title of maid largely ceremonial.

### Role in the Elderblood Empire
Roxanne's importance within the [[Elderblood Empire]] is rooted primarily in the relationship between [[World Trees]] and the supernatural conditions required for Life. Although she possesses considerable personal power, her most valuable contribution is the continuous support she provides to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul and the World Tree infrastructure incorporated into his growing civilization.[8][19]

#### Nurturing Victor's Soul
Roxanne's earliest long-term responsibility was nourishing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s spiritual existence. A World Tree ordinarily supports the world in which it is rooted, supplying the Energy necessary for the continued development of Life. Because Roxanne established herself inside Victor, the same process supported his body, Soul and increasingly vast inner world.[11][8]
The relationship became particularly important when Victor began accumulating powers and spiritual characteristics whose quantity exceeded what a normal Progenitor could safely contain. Roxanne's nourishment contributed to the stability and quality of his Soul, helping transform his internal existence into a more developed supernatural environment.[11]
Following the arrival of Amara, the two [[World Trees]] began operating through complementary Positive and Negative Energy, creating a more balanced foundation for Victor's continued development.[15][16]

#### Management of the Inner World
Roxanne supervises important aspects of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Personal Universe, particularly those connected to [[World Trees]], Souls and the natural Energy circulating through the environment.[13]
Her responsibilities developed significantly after [[Gaia]] was placed within the inner world. Roxanne assigned the Primordial Goddess tasks associated with maintaining the environment, while other supernatural Beings gradually became involved in the developing system.[13]
The restoration of [[Metis]] further demonstrated Roxanne's role in the preservation of spiritual inhabitants, since she helped recover a severely damaged Soul within the Energy-rich environment maintained through her main World Tree body.[3]
As the internal world expanded into a cosmic environment, Roxanne and Amara also became involved in managing powers and Energy structures accumulating within Victor's existence.[16]

#### World Tree Administration
Roxanne's responsibility eventually extended to the [[World Trees]] connected to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] throughout his Imperial territory. After his Energy caused widespread abnormalities in their development, Roxanne prepared a report concerning accelerated maturation, changes in behavior and the growing dependency of the affected World Trees.[18]
She subsequently helped organize the Negative World Trees entering Victor's Soul, while Amara managed the corresponding Positive Trees. The arrangement transformed the sisters into senior figures within an increasingly large supernatural population.[18]
During the crisis surrounding Moira's birth, Roxanne was instructed to prepare the World Trees for possible use in a confrontation involving Primordial authorities. She immediately began coordinating them, confirming her position as an important administrator of the network.[31]

#### Mars World Tree Network
Roxanne's most important later Imperial achievement was her coordination of the World Tree network established during the reconstruction of Mars.[19]
The project connected [[World Trees]] associated with planets throughout Imperial territory, using Mars as a central point through which their roots could form an enormous communication and transportation infrastructure. The genuine Trees remained protected within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul while their manifested roots provided connections across galaxies.[19]
Roxanne confirmed that the system was fully connected. Its operation allowed information to travel virtually instantaneously between distant regions of the Empire, eliminating the remaining delays associated with conventional interstellar communication.[19]
The network also provided defensive advantages, since the destruction of Mars would not automatically destroy the World Trees maintaining the system. Their true bodies remained within Victor's inner world, preserving the supernatural foundation necessary for later planetary restoration.[19]
The achievement established Roxanne as one of the principal figures supporting the Empire's cosmic infrastructure rather than merely a World Tree assigned to a single planet.

### Legacy
Roxanne represents one of the most unusual examples of World Tree development within the story. Her existence began according to the rules established through the Universal Tree, but her encounter with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] diverted her from the ordinary process through which a Negative World Tree matures alongside the Positive counterpart of its planet.[7][12]
Her relationship with Victor also demonstrates that the fundamental function of a World Tree is not limited to supporting conventional physical planets. By rooting herself within a living Soul, Roxanne redirected the enormous nurturing power of her species toward the development of an individual whose internal existence eventually expanded into a cosmos.[8][16]
The resulting symbiosis played an important role in Victor's development. Roxanne provided natural Energy, supported the quality and stability of his Soul, helped organize the powers circulating through his inner world and eventually became one of the major administrators responsible for its inhabitants and associated [[World Trees]].[11][8][16]
Her development into an Elder World Tree also illustrates how extraordinary circumstances can alter the normal growth of a supernatural species. Roxanne matured far more rapidly than ordinary World Trees, acquiring enormous power before receiving the education usually associated with her developmental stage. Her experiences with Aurora, Amara and the other Trees subsequently forced her to reconcile this unusual growth with the responsibilities her species was created to fulfill.[12][13]
The birth of Rosalyn further expanded the significance of Roxanne's existence. Through Victor, she became the mother of a genuine World Tree possessing Blood Dragon characteristics, creating a new familial lineage distinct from the ordinary method through which World Trees originate.[2][17]
By the mature Imperial era, Roxanne's influence extended throughout the Empire. Her coordination of the World Tree network contributed to the establishment of Mars as a new capital and helped create a communication system capable of operating across enormous Galactic distances with virtually no delay.[19]
Despite these achievements, the emotional foundation of Roxanne's existence remains the relationship she established with Victor. What began as an accidental Dryad marriage became a permanent spiritual symbiosis, transforming an isolated young World Tree into one of the most important individuals sustaining the Emperor and the civilization that developed around him.[10][8][19]

### Abilities

#### Powers and Abilities
Roxanne Elderblood is an Elder World Tree of Negativity, a Dryad–Blood Dragon Balanced Hybrid, and one of the principal supernatural foundations supporting [[Victor Elderblood]]'s existence. Her abilities originate from three interconnected sources: her intrinsic nature as a Negative World Tree, the alterations caused by Victor's Progenitor Blood and subsequent Draconic evolution, and the exceptionally deep spiritual synchronization established between her true World Tree body and Victor's Soul.[1][2][3][4]
[[World Trees]] are not ordinary magical plants. They are fundamental supernatural organisms originating from [[The Universal Tree]], responsible for supporting the development of living planets and maintaining the circulation of natural Energy. Their functions include planetary nourishment, environmental development, the processing of Positive and Negative Energy, and the maintenance of conditions necessary for living civilizations and their associated supernatural systems. Roxanne belongs to the Negative side of this arrangement, giving her a particular affinity for Negativity, corruption, negative emotions, Demonic Energy and other phenomena associated with the Negative spectrum of existence.[1][5]
Her relationship with Victor fundamentally changed how these abilities operate. Instead of maintaining [[Nightingale]], Roxanne established her main body within Victor's Soul, treating him as the planet she exists to nourish. The result is a form of World Tree–Planet symbiosis in which Roxanne continually strengthens his body and Soul, filters excessive Energy, processes accumulated spiritual resources, and contributes to the development of his internal Reality. Their deliberate synchronization subsequently enabled the sharing of memories, emotions, [[Senjutsu]] and several supernatural abilities.[6][2][7]
Her later growth expanded these functions far beyond their original planetary scope. Following Victor's ascension as the Blood Dragon Primogenitor, Roxanne developed into an Elder World Tree. Her subsequent cooperation with [[Amara Elderblood]] enabled the classification and integration of Positive- and Negative-aligned powers within Victor's internal cosmos. During the Imperial era, she also supervised World Trees connected to Victor across enormous Galactic territories and coordinated the network established during the development of Mars as the Empire's capital.[8][9][10]
The following index distinguishes Roxanne's personal abilities from universal World Tree functions, powers available through synchronization with Victor, and effects produced collectively by multiple World Trees. An ability belonging to Victor, the Universal Tree, Amara or the wider Imperial network is not automatically a power Roxanne can reproduce independently.

#### Resistances and Defensive Properties
Roxanne's defensive qualities arise primarily from her World Tree nature, exceptional spiritual foundation, connection to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and ability to supply restorative Energy. Her genuine body and its location inside his Soul must be distinguished from the vulnerability of a projected humanoid form.

##### Existential Resistances
- Higher Existential Quality: Roxanne possesses a spiritual foundation capable of overwhelming unsuitable hosts during synchronization.[2]
- Exceptional Spiritual Capacity: Her existence can participate in sustaining enormous quantities of supernatural Energy and Souls.[13][7]
- Spiritual Persistence: Roxanne's genuine World Tree body remains rooted within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] even when her humanoid projection is located elsewhere.[2]
- Projection Separation: Her true body does not need to accompany her humanoid manifestation.[2]
- Soul Stabilization: Roxanne possesses the ability to maintain the spiritual stability of her synchronized host.[23]
- Existential Reinforcement: Her World Tree nature contributes to improving the quality of Victor's Soul.[6]

##### Biological and Energy-Based Defense
- Healing: Roxanne can supply restorative Energy to a damaged body.[19]
- Poison Removal: She completely removed the poison affecting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] during Chapter 647.[19]
- Biological Stabilization: Her Energy preserved Victor's body during rapid deterioration.[19]
- Energy Overload Prevention: Roxanne regulated the excessive Energy accumulated during Victor's Overdrive state.[7]
- Defensive Empowerment: She strengthened Victor's allies during the confrontation involving [[Zeus]]'s expanding Domain.[25]
- Destructive Barrier Potential: Her Negative [[Senjutsu]] can produce barriers whose properties differ from ordinary protective shields.[22]

##### World Tree Network Protection
- Protected True Body (Network): Participating [[World Trees]] can remain sheltered inside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul while their connections extend to external planets.[10]
- Planetary Destruction Survival (Conditional): Destroying Mars would not automatically destroy the World Trees whose genuine bodies remained protected elsewhere.[10]
- Environmental Restoration Support: Surviving World Trees provide the foundation for the restoration of damaged planetary environments.[10]

##### Known Limitations
- Host Compatibility: Deep synchronization with Roxanne requires an exceptionally resilient spiritual foundation.[13][2]
- Existential Synchronization Strain: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] experienced severe pain during their first complete synchronization.[2]
- Energy Expenditure: Roxanne has an extraordinary renewable Energy supply, but demonstrated operations still involve expenditure and output limitations.[19][28]
- Former Incomplete Education: Roxanne initially lacked much of the knowledge normally taught to an Adult Negative World Tree by its Positive counterpart.[14]
- Planetary Dependency: [[World Trees]] function through a relationship with a planet or compatible host, making the condition of that relationship important to their development.[13][2]
- Negative Power Compatibility: Roxanne's red-sun integration mechanism does not absorb every supernatural power or object without distinction.[8]
- Memory Modification Limitation: She can recover dormant memories through synchronization but cannot freely rewrite them through the demonstrated technique.[2]
- Collaborative Power Attribution: The completed works of Victor, Amara, Jeanne, [[Gaia]] or the collective World Tree network should not automatically be treated as Roxanne's independent powers.[20][18][10]
- No Confirmed Unlimited Soul Destruction: The reviewed chapters establish Soul Control, Soul Storage, nourishment and restoration support, but not a separately demonstrated ability to destroy any arbitrary Soul without restriction.
- No Confirmed Independent Pantheon Creation: Roxanne can support Pantheon development through her World Tree nature, but the creation and administration of a complete Pantheon involve additional supernatural systems.[1][18]
- No Confirmed Universal Authority: Roxanne is a World Tree created by the Universal Tree, not the Primordial Entity possessing the complete administrative authority over Life, Reincarnation or the maintenance of the Universe.[5][30]
