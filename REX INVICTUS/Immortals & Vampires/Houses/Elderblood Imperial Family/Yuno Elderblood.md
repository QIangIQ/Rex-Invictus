Yuno Elderblood is a recurring character in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]], an Imperial Princess of the [[Elderblood Empire]], and the biological daughter of Victor Elderblood and [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]]. She belongs to Victor's second generation of naturally born [[Blood Dragons]], possessing the extraordinary biological and supernatural foundation of a True Dragon and Dragon Goddess from birth. Through Kaguya, she also inherits the ancient [[Blank Clan]]'s affinity for Shadows, which manifests alongside her own powers over Darkness and Black Flames. Her natural abilities, exceptional lineage and close relationship with Victor make her an extraordinarily powerful existence even when compared with the Gods who once dominated Earth's supernatural world.[1][2][3]
Yuno is particularly distinguished by her extraordinary affection for Victor, which frequently develops into intense possessiveness, jealousy and hostility toward individuals she considers unworthy of his attention. Although normally a quiet and comparatively well-behaved girl, her personality changes dramatically whenever her father is involved. She considers his affection exceptionally precious, reacts aggressively toward outsiders who receive his attention and regards his commands as nearly absolute. Her behavior is sufficiently extreme that her sisters consider her one of the most emotionally unstable members of the family, surpassed primarily by [[Yog-Sothoth]] Elderblood. Nevertheless, Yuno is capable of restraint when Victor instructs her to behave and remains deeply sensitive to the possibility of disappointing him.[3][4]
Despite her seemingly delicate appearance, Yuno possesses formidable supernatural abilities. She can manifest wings composed entirely of Pure Darkness, generate concentrated Black Flames, perceive the underlying supernatural characteristics of other beings and manipulate Shadows to store and retrieve physical objects. Her maternal inheritance also provides considerable advantages in stealth and unconventional combat, making her particularly dangerous when employing guerrilla warfare rather than confronting enemies through straightforward contests of strength. Her abilities are sufficiently advanced that, despite being considered comparatively weaker than several of her more exceptional sisters, she is assessed as capable of defeating the [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]] through prolonged Shadow-based warfare, with its Gods of Destruction representing the principal opponents capable of seriously challenging her.[3][4][5]
Yuno's supernatural development extends beyond the Darkness and Shadow abilities inherited through Kaguya. She eventually develops a Yandere-related Divinity, a Divine characteristic directly associated with the possessive disposition that has distinguished her since childhood. The development becomes a source of amusement among Victor's wives, particularly Sasha, who uses the revelation to tease Kaguya about her daughter's resemblance to her. Although the Divinity's existence is established, its precise name, governing Authority and practical applications remain unidentified. It should not be confused with Yuno's earlier joking self-description as a Goddess of the Apocalypse, a title associated more directly with [[Nero Elderblood]].[6][7][8]
Yuno is particularly close to [[Stella Elderblood]], and the two are regarded as practically inseparable. Their shared affection for Victor frequently brings them together, although their desire to monopolize his attention occasionally produces disagreements. She also possesses a complicated but affectionate relationship with Kaguya, whose dedication to Victor resembles her daughter's own possessiveness. While Yuno openly admires her mother's abilities and accomplishments, she is far more willing to challenge Kaguya's authority than Victor's, occasionally requiring her mother to impose discipline despite her extraordinary natural strength. Her devotion toward the family is nevertheless genuine, and she wishes to become strong enough to protect Victor rather than remain permanently dependent upon his protection.[1][4][9][10]

### Physical Appearance
Yuno is an exceptionally beautiful young Dragon Goddess with pale skin, black hair and deep violet eyes. Her early Humanoid appearance is approximately 120 centimeters tall, giving her the physique of a small girl despite her extraordinary chronological age and supernatural characteristics. Her black hair reflects her maternal heritage, while her violet eyes are inherited from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and possess the distinctive appearance associated with the Dragon Eyes of his biological descendants. Her outwardly innocent appearance contrasts sharply with her naturally overwhelming Draconic physiology and the hostile supernatural pressure she can release when angered.[2][3]
During her early appearances, Yuno had already accumulated approximately one thousand years of subjective experience while retaining a youthful body. This discrepancy reflects the developmental characteristics of naturally born [[True Dragons]], whose physical maturation occurs over periods vastly longer than those experienced by ordinary Humans. Her chronological age subsequently increased enormously during the Empire's accelerated temporal development, although the full extent of her later physical maturation has not been individually described in sufficient detail to establish new measurements. Her natural appearance remains an important part of her identity, particularly because her small size allows her to cling to Victor and seek his affection in ways that frequently amuse the other members of the family.[2][11]
Yuno's appearance changes considerably when she becomes hostile. Her ordinarily gentle expression can disappear almost immediately, replaced by a cold and openly murderous demeanor. During her confrontation with [[Leon Walker]], her violet eyes become so dark that they resemble two black holes, while the supernatural pressure of a Dragon God begins radiating from her body. She raises her hand and forms a sphere of Pure Black Flames, revealing the dangerous supernatural power concealed beneath her childlike exterior. The transformation is especially striking because she returns to an innocent and affectionate expression the moment Victor calls her name.[3]
One of her most distinctive supernatural features is the ability to manifest wings composed entirely of Pure Darkness. Unlike ordinary feathered wings or the anatomical structures of conventional Dragons, these are supernatural constructs formed from her own Darkness. Yuno uses them to fly naturally, including when she approaches Leon and circles him while examining his existence through her supernatural senses. The wings provide a visible expression of her affinity for Darkness and distinguish her appearance from sisters whose abilities manifest through more conventional elemental phenomena.[3]
Yuno also possesses an interest in Maid clothing, particularly the elegant uniforms associated with Kaguya's profession. She attends Maid lessons because she finds the clothing attractive and considers the activities entertaining, although she does not share her mother's lifelong ambition to become the perfect servant. Her participation is primarily recreational, and she openly maintains that her status as a True Dragon and Imperial Princess places her beyond ordinary service. Victor is the only individual for whom she would willingly perform genuine Maid duties, reflecting her extraordinary devotion toward him rather than a desire to pursue the same profession as Kaguya.[1]
As a naturally born Blood Dragon, Yuno possesses the biological foundation associated with Draconic transformation and the exceptional supernatural physique shared by Victor's descendants. However, the complete appearance of her natural Dragon Form has not been individually described in sufficient detail to establish its precise dimensions, scale coloration or specialized anatomical characteristics. Her confirmed individual visual characteristics are therefore primarily her black hair, pale skin, violet Dragon Eyes, youthful Humanoid form and supernatural wings of Pure Darkness.

### Personality and traits
Yuno is normally quiet, calm and comparatively reserved, particularly when contrasted with several of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s more openly energetic daughters. Kaguya initially considers her a relatively well-behaved child whose ordinary activities rarely produce serious difficulties. However, Yuno's disposition changes dramatically whenever Victor becomes involved, revealing an exceptionally possessive and emotionally intense personality. She regards her father as the most important individual in her existence, wishes to remain near him whenever possible and becomes jealous when unrelated people receive his affection. Her behavior is extreme even by the standards of the Elderblood family, whose members frequently display obsessive affection and formidable supernatural temperaments.[1][3]
Her possessiveness is accompanied by extraordinary loyalty. Yuno considers Victor's commands nearly absolute and responds to his instructions with enthusiasm, even when her interpretation of obedience becomes alarmingly destructive. When Victor asks his daughters to behave during the gathering involving Leon, Yuno declares that her father's orders are supreme before casually suggesting that the world should be burned. Her enthusiasm for violence also appears when she jokingly describes herself as a Goddess of the Apocalypse, dismissing concerns about her hostile thoughts. These remarks reflect her dramatic and aggressive disposition rather than a demonstrated personal Divinity governing Apocalypse.[2][6]
Kaguya is especially aware of the danger associated with Yuno's personality. When Stella leaves Victor's Personal World without permission, Kaguya is relieved to discover that her daughter has not accompanied her. She believes that Yuno's presence might have transformed an already troublesome expedition into a catastrophe, privately imagining [[Nightingale]]'s capital engulfed in Black Flames merely because Yuno could not locate her beloved father. The concern is intensified by the fact that Yuno and Stella are practically inseparable, frequently becoming involved in the same activities despite their different maternal inheritances. Kaguya therefore regards the absence of her daughter from Stella's unauthorized adventure as an unusually fortunate circumstance.[1]
Yuno's treatment of Leon reveals the most dangerous aspects of her emotional disposition. After examining his existence through her supernatural senses, she recognizes that he resembles Victor but is fundamentally a different individual. Instead of accepting the resemblance as evidence of their familial relationship, she considers Leon an unacceptable imitation of her father and prepares to eliminate him with Black Flames. Her hostility disappears immediately when Victor speaks her name, and she returns to an innocent expression while seeking his affection. Although she remains dissatisfied with allowing Leon to live, she obeys Victor because disappointing her father is more important to her than satisfying her immediate desire to remove someone she dislikes.[3]
Despite her possessiveness, Yuno is capable of restraint and independent judgment. She recognizes that Victor genuinely respects Leon and briefly considers becoming closer to her grandfather because doing so might earn additional affection. However, she dislikes the idea of pretending to appreciate someone whose resemblance to Victor makes her uncomfortable and ultimately decides against pursuing a relationship solely for personal benefit. Her decision demonstrates that she is not completely controlled by her desire for attention; she can recognize when an action would be insincere and may disappoint Victor if undertaken for the wrong reasons.[4]
Yuno's relationship with authority is particularly selective. She demonstrates almost unconditional obedience toward Victor but is substantially more willing to challenge Kaguya, occasionally behaving sarcastically or making expressions intended to mock her mother's decisions. When Kaguya's independent actions are revealed during a gathering with three Imperial assassins, Yuno reacts with an openly smug expression, silently criticizing her mother for acting without first consulting Victor. Kaguya immediately recognizes the disrespect and becomes irritated, while Yuno attempts to avoid the consequences by clinging more tightly to her father. The incident illustrates the different standards she applies to her parents, since she would never casually mock Victor in the same manner.[4]
Although Yuno inherited Kaguya's affinity for Shadows, she does not share her mother's ambition to dedicate her existence to Maid service. She enjoys the appearance of Maid uniforms and willingly participates in related lessons, but considers the activity a hobby rather than a permanent occupation. Her pride as an Imperial Princess and True Dragon makes her unwilling to serve individuals she considers beneath her, and she regards Victor as the only person worthy of receiving such devotion. Kaguya initially hopes that her daughter may have inherited her professional ambitions, but eventually recognizes that Yuno's motivation is fundamentally different from her own.[1]
Her personality also possesses a humorous connection to Victor's Human life. Yuno's name resembles that of a fictional yandere associated with one of the earliest anime Victor watched, and her behavior develops into a strikingly similar form of possessiveness. Victor finds the resemblance amusing and admits that it contributes to his tendency to spoil her. The other wives frequently tease Kaguya about the coincidence, suggesting that the apparently perfect Maid has raised a daughter whose emotional instability reflects her own devotion toward Victor. Kaguya rejects the accusation, insisting that Yuno inherited her extreme characteristics primarily from Victor rather than herself, although the other women point out that Kaguya's own behavior toward her Master can be equally uncompromising.[12]
Yuno's jealousy does not prevent her from recognizing the importance of familial relationships. She accepts that Victor's wives and daughters have legitimate claims upon his affection, even though she frequently wishes to monopolize his attention. Her hostility is generally directed more strongly toward outsiders, particularly individuals who receive affectionate treatment without belonging to the immediate family. When Victor rewards three assassins serving beneath Kaguya, Yuno becomes visibly jealous but remains silent because she understands that openly complaining would make her appear even more spoiled. She similarly reacts when Victor kisses Kaguya, deliberately making noise while eating and drinking in an attempt to interrupt their affectionate interaction, only to obey immediately when he gently reprimands her.[4]
Her willingness to challenge Kaguya eventually results in corrective discipline. After Victor departs from the gathering with the assassins, Yuno immediately attempts to pursue him but is restrained by her mother's Shadows. Despite possessing extraordinary natural strength and her own Shadow-related abilities, she cannot escape Kaguya's superior control. Kaguya reminds her that being a Dragon Princess does not excuse disrespect toward her parents and that Victor's indulgence must be balanced by appropriate discipline. Although Yuno protests, she is unable to avoid the consequences, demonstrating that her mother's authority and experience remain sufficient to restrain her despite the immense potential of her inherited powers.[4]
Yuno is also deeply attached to her sisters, particularly Stella, whose company she frequently enjoys despite their occasional disagreements over Victor. Their relationship combines affection, shared activities and rivalry for their father's attention. During a major Imperial family gathering, Stella becomes jealous when Yuno clings to Victor and demands that she stop monopolizing him. Victor immediately intervenes, reminding the girls that they may compete, argue, spar or duel when appropriate, but must never allow ordinary rivalry to develop into genuine hatred. The correction reflects the central principle of the Elderblood family, whose members are expected to protect one another regardless of personal disagreements.[9]
Beneath her possessiveness, Yuno possesses a genuine desire to protect Victor rather than merely receive his affection. When she senses his anger during the confrontation involving the System and Moira, she recognizes that an exceptionally serious threat has emerged. Rather than becoming frightened or demanding that her father remain with her, she declares that the daughters need to become stronger so they can help him. Her sisters agree, recognizing that their immense inherited abilities do not necessarily make them capable of confronting every enemy their father faces. Yuno's response reveals a more mature aspect of her devotion, since she wishes to share Victor's burdens and contribute meaningfully to the safety of the family.[10]
Yuno also possesses considerable curiosity concerning Victor's abilities and the underlying principles of Reality. During his educational lessons, she participates in discussions concerning Divinity, the fundamental components of the Universe and the relationship between conceptual Authority and direct combat strength. When Victor explains the significance of Positivity and Negativity, Yuno recognizes that his connection with Negativity appears to grant him authority over half the Universe. Victor clarifies that his true strength depends upon much more than that single conceptual relationship, encouraging the daughters to distinguish innate Authority from the abilities developed through experience, training and personal mastery.[13]
Despite the disagreements that occasionally arise between Yuno and Kaguya, she possesses genuine admiration for her mother. During the cosmic family banquet, Yuno becomes visibly pleased when Kaguya refuses Bruna's assistance and insists upon personally serving Victor. Her mother's possessive determination and extraordinary professionalism are familiar characteristics that Yuno regards with pride rather than embarrassment. The resemblance becomes even more significant when Yuno develops a Yandere-related Divinity, prompting renewed teasing among the wives concerning the similarities between mother and daughter. Kaguya responds by observing that the other daughters possess comparable tendencies to varying degrees, refusing to treat Yuno's development as a uniquely abnormal characteristic of their family.[14][8]

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Yuno was born during the extraordinary period of accelerated development experienced by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his family following the establishment of his mature Blood Dragon lineage. As the biological daughter of Victor Elderblood and [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]], she inherited the physical and supernatural foundation of a genuine True Dragon Goddess alongside the Shadow-oriented characteristics of the Blank family. Her maternal lineage originated from an ancient Noble Vampire Clan associated with the Snow family, whose members traditionally served as assassins, covert operatives and specialized servants. Yuno's existence continued that heritage through a fundamentally more advanced Draconic Race, allowing the abilities associated with the Blank lineage to develop alongside the immense supernatural potential inherited from Victor.
During her childhood, Yuno became particularly close to [[Stella Elderblood]], the daughter of Victor and Anna. Their shared affection for Victor and enthusiasm for family activities made them nearly inseparable, although their mothers occasionally worried about the consequences of allowing two exceptionally powerful girls to become involved in mischief together. This concern became especially apparent when Stella left Victor's Personal World without permission and traveled to [[Nightingale]]. Kaguya was relieved to discover that Yuno had not accompanied her, recognizing that her own daughter's possessiveness could have transformed the expedition into a catastrophe if she became distressed over her father's absence.[1]
Yuno was instead occupied with Maid lessons, an activity Kaguya initially interpreted as evidence that her daughter might share her professional ambitions. However, Yuno's primary motivation was the attractiveness of the uniforms, and she regarded the lessons as an enjoyable hobby rather than preparation for permanent service. She maintained that her status as a Dragon and Princess made her unwilling to serve anyone other than Victor. Kaguya recognized that although Yuno had inherited her affinity for Shadows, she had not developed the same dedication to the Maid profession that defined her mother's identity.[1]
Yuno's first major appearance occurred when Victor gathered his biological daughters to introduce them to [[Leon Walker]], his father from his Human life. The meeting brought together numerous members of the second generation, whose extraordinary appearances, powers and personalities reflected the different supernatural inheritances of their mothers. Leon was already struggling to comprehend the size and composition of his son's family when Yuno suddenly rushed toward Victor. Without hesitation, she threw Leon into a wall because he was obstructing her route, embedding him in the structure before continuing toward her father without paying any particular attention to the incident.[2]
Upon reaching Victor, Yuno immediately clung to him like a koala and enthusiastically called him Daddy. Her sisters also began gathering around their father, eventually surrounding him so completely that Leon could barely see him. Yuno refused to relinquish her position despite the complaints of the other girls, demonstrating the possessive attachment that distinguished her from much of the family. When Victor finally asked his daughters to calm down so he could introduce Leon properly, Yuno responded by declaring that her father's orders were absolute before casually suggesting that the world should be burned. Her remark immediately revealed the extraordinary intensity of her personality.[2]
The meeting continued with several daughters displaying unusual reactions toward Leon. Yuno eventually became the center of attention again when she manifested wings composed entirely of Pure Darkness and flew toward him. She circled him while examining his appearance and supernatural characteristics, using her senses to perceive the deeper nature of his existence. Although she recognized his resemblance to Victor, she concluded that Leon was fundamentally a different person who possessed only a superficial similarity to the father she adored.[3]
Yuno reacted with disgust and described Leon as a counterfeit who should be removed. Her expression became increasingly hostile as her violet eyes darkened until they resembled two black holes, while the supernatural pressure of a Dragon God began emanating from her body. She raised one hand and generated a concentrated sphere of Pure Black Flames, preparing to attack Leon despite his lack of hostility toward her. Her sisters watched with curiosity rather than immediately intervening, already familiar with Yuno's emotionally unstable nature and considering her one of the most dangerous members of their generation in that regard.[3]
Victor stopped the attack by speaking Yuno's name. Her demeanor changed almost immediately, and she returned to an innocent, affectionate expression before approaching him again. Victor explained that Leon was someone he genuinely respected and instructed her not to harm him. Although Yuno remained unhappy with his decision, she obeyed without further resistance, demonstrating that Victor's authority over her emotions exceeded the hostility she felt toward her grandfather. Leon was left unsettled not only by Yuno's willingness to attack him but also by the comparatively indifferent reactions of the other daughters.[3]
The confrontation also prompted a discussion among Victor's wives concerning Yuno's personality. Several recognized her strong resemblance to a fictional yandere associated with one of the earliest anime Victor had watched during his Human life. Victor found the resemblance entertaining and acknowledged that it influenced how much he spoiled her, while Kaguya objected to the implication that she had deliberately raised a daughter whose personality imitated an unstable fictional character. Violet, Sasha, Ruby and the other wives used the situation to tease Kaguya, arguing that her own devotion toward Victor was not substantially different from Yuno's extreme possessiveness.[12]
Yuno subsequently accompanied Victor and Kaguya during a meeting involving three assassins operating within the Empire's covert organizations. The women had acted under Kaguya's direction and were awaiting recognition from Victor, whose Imperial Authority governed the institutions to which they belonged. When Victor revealed that he was aware of Kaguya's actions despite not having explicitly authorized every detail, Yuno reacted with a smug expression that silently mocked her mother for taking liberties without consulting him. Kaguya immediately recognized the disrespect and became irritated, while Yuno attempted to avoid the consequences by clinging more tightly to Victor.[4]
Victor nevertheless approved of Kaguya's actions and rewarded the assassins for their service. His affectionate treatment caused their supernatural characteristics to develop dramatically, transforming them into more advanced Dragonoids and reinforcing their loyalty toward the Empire. Yuno watched the women receiving his attention with obvious jealousy, regarding affection from her father as something that should primarily belong to his family. However, she remained silent because she understood that openly objecting would make her appear excessively spoiled and might disappoint Victor.[4]
Her thoughts briefly returned to Leon, whom Victor had described as someone deserving respect. Yuno recognized that developing a friendly relationship with her grandfather might earn additional affection from her father, but she disliked the idea of approaching someone whose resemblance to Victor continued making her uncomfortable. She ultimately decided against forcing herself into an insincere relationship merely to obtain a reward. After reaching this conclusion, she formed Shadows around her hand and retrieved a hamburger, demonstrating that her inherited Shadow abilities could be used for ordinary storage rather than exclusively for combat or concealment.[4]
Victor subsequently approached Kaguya and reassured her of the importance she would always possess in his life. When the two kissed, Yuno reacted with renewed jealousy and began eating her hamburger furiously. She retrieved a drink from her Shadow and deliberately made loud noises with the straw in an attempt to interrupt the affectionate exchange. Victor gently reprimanded her for eating noisily, and Yuno immediately obeyed despite pouting at the correction. The food itself had been prepared through Victor's extraordinary resources, since he insisted that even familiar meals should contain high-quality Divine ingredients rather than provide his daughters with nutritionally poor food.[4]
After Victor completed his discussion with Kaguya and departed, Yuno immediately attempted to pursue him, complaining that her father had abandoned her. Kaguya stopped her with powerful Shadows and prevented her from escaping despite her natural Draconic strength. Having already decided that her daughter's disrespectful behavior required correction, Kaguya disciplined Yuno and reminded her that being a True Dragon and Imperial Princess did not place her beyond parental authority. The confrontation demonstrated the difference between Yuno's immense inherited potential and her mother's superior experience and control over Shadow techniques, while reinforcing Kaguya's determination to prevent Victor's indulgence from producing an arrogant or undisciplined daughter.[4]
Victor later became concerned that the extraordinary natural abilities of his daughters were preventing them from acquiring sufficient practical experience. Most had been born as Blood Dragon Goddesses and possessed physical and supernatural capabilities that ordinary beings could never hope to rival. Although their powers provided overwhelming advantages, Victor recognized that such circumstances could encourage excessive dependence upon natural strength and prevent them from understanding the difficulties faced by weaker individuals. He consequently organized military simulations intended to develop strategy, adaptability, leadership and the ability to respond to unfamiliar supernatural systems.[15][16]
During one of the simulations, Yuno was selected alongside Rosemary, Gina and Stella to experience different supernatural circumstances within a Dream-based world. Their normal racial advantages were restricted, and each daughter received a different role intended to challenge her ability to adapt. Rosemary became a Fairy Queen, Gina appeared as a Dryad and Stella assumed the role of a Demi-Goddess, while Yuno found herself transformed into a Necromancer within a cemetery. Rather than immediately complaining about the unfamiliar circumstances, she considered how [[Persephone Elderblood]] would approach the situation, recognizing that her new role required knowledge and techniques different from those associated with her natural Darkness and Shadows.[16]
The simulation eventually expanded into a complicated civilization-building exercise involving numerous daughters, factions and supernatural conflicts. Victor used the environment to teach his children how to respond to enemies possessing comparable or superior strength, understand the limitations of different [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] and avoid assuming that natural power alone would guarantee victory. Yuno's Necromancer identity belonged exclusively to this controlled training environment and did not represent a permanent transformation or the acquisition of an independent Divinity governing Death or [[Necromancy]].[16]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
As the [[Elderblood Empire]] continued expanding, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his family developed a more systematic understanding of the practical danger posed by powerful individuals and supernatural civilizations. Their threat assessments distinguished between raw destructive power and the ability to defeat opponents through strategy, unconventional tactics or specialized abilities. Yuno became an important example of this distinction because her extraordinary affinity for Shadows allowed her to exploit methods of warfare fundamentally different from those employed by individuals who relied almost entirely upon direct confrontations.[5]
Although Yuno was considered comparatively weaker than several of her more exceptionally talented sisters, her combat potential was already sufficient to threaten entire supernatural factions. Imperial assessments indicated that she could potentially eliminate the [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]] through guerrilla warfare, using Shadows to exploit concealment, mobility and repeated engagements against opponents unable to counter her tactics effectively. Only the Pantheon's Gods of Destruction were specifically identified as capable of presenting serious challenges under those circumstances. The assessment concerned her potential to defeat the Pantheon's forces through sustained unconventional warfare rather than destroying every member simultaneously through a single attack.[5]
The comparison was particularly significant because Yuno remained a young Dragon relative to many ancient supernatural beings, yet possessed enough natural power and tactical advantages to threaten one of Earth's oldest Divine civilizations. Her circumstances reflected the wider development of Victor's daughters, whose abilities had grown far beyond those of the established Pantheons that dominated the world during Victor's early life. The Empire's broad Stellar threat classifications also emphasized practical danger rather than assigning identical destructive output to every individual within the same category.[5]
The Elderblood Empire subsequently underwent an enormous period of accelerated development intended to prepare its civilization for threats originating beyond the boundaries of its native Sector. Victor and Natalia initiated an extraordinary temporal undertaking designed to grant the Empire approximately two hundred thousand years of internal development while only a comparatively short period passed outside. The younger generation used the resulting millennia to mature, train and pursue personal ambitions, while Victor and his wives continued maintaining the Empire's stability and overseeing its development. Yuno remained closely associated with Stella and the other Imperial Princesses throughout these changes, retaining her affectionate and possessive disposition despite her increasing chronological experience.[11][9]
During a major family gathering involving more than thirty-five of Victor's daughters, Yuno once again demonstrated her extraordinary attachment to her father. Victor entered the gathering in a Humanoid body approximately three meters tall, having deliberately increased his height so that his increasingly tall daughters could embrace him more comfortably. Yuno immediately clung to him and enthusiastically complimented his appearance, prompting Stella to become jealous and demand that she stop monopolizing his attention. Their exchange quickly developed into an argument, causing Victor to intervene before the disagreement escalated.[9]
Victor reminded the sisters that members of the Elderblood family were expected to protect one another and that ordinary rivalry must never become genuine hatred. He explained that arguments, competition, sparring and duels were acceptable when both parties agreed and understood their limits, but hostility capable of damaging the family was forbidden. Stella immediately apologized and became cheerful again when Victor affectionately stroked her head, while Yuno remained close to him. The incident reflected both the intensity of her attachment and the importance Victor placed upon maintaining unity among his enormous second generation.[9]
The gathering eventually developed into an extraordinary Imperial banquet involving celestial materials and supernatural cuisine. Victor transformed the surrounding cosmic environment into a suitable location for the celebration, creating stable ground, comfortable conditions and furniture formed from astronomical structures. Although the scale of the banquet reflected the extraordinary power of the ruling family, its purpose remained the preservation of their traditional communal gatherings. Victor wished to spend time with his wives and daughters rather than allow their increasing responsibilities to separate them from ordinary family activities.[14]
When Victor asked Kaguya to serve the assembled family, she accepted with visible pride, continuing to regard her position as the [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank|Perfect Maid]] as one of the most important aspects of her identity. Bruna attempted to assist, but Kaguya immediately refused, her eyes becoming so dark that they resembled black holes as she asserted her exclusive responsibility for serving Victor. Yuno watched her mother's behavior with obvious satisfaction, proudly recognizing the familiar dedication and possessiveness she associated with Kaguya. The resemblance between mother and daughter was so striking that Eve privately remarked upon it, while Kaguya proceeded to serve the entire gathering with extraordinary speed and precision.[14]
Yuno's own supernatural development eventually became another subject of discussion among Victor's wives. Sasha drew Kaguya's attention to a Yandere-related Divinity that had emerged in their daughter, suggesting that the development was particularly appropriate for someone raised by the family's famously devoted Chief Maid. Kaguya immediately recognized the provocation and rejected the insinuation that Yuno's personality represented evidence of some hidden defect in her own disposition. She argued that all of Victor's daughters possessed similar tendencies to varying degrees, prompting Sasha to become genuinely surprised by the suggestion.[8]
The resulting conversation revealed that several mothers regularly examined the Divinities of their daughters to monitor their supernatural development. [[Scathach]], Natasha, Violet, Ruby, Jeanne and Kaguya were among those who acknowledged performing such checks frequently, while Sasha admitted that she did not examine her daughters with the same regularity. Yuno's developing Divinity consequently became part of a broader discussion concerning the unusual powers emerging throughout the second generation, whose supernatural characteristics frequently reflected the personalities, inheritances and experiences of their parents.[8]
The family subsequently faced another extraordinary disturbance when the System directed its attention toward [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]], Victor's newborn daughter with [[Tyche]]. Moira possessed an abnormal relationship with Reality, and her existence attracted the interest of an authority that wished to assign her the vacant Primordial function associated with Destiny. Victor immediately recognized the intrusion and became furious, although he initially concealed the seriousness of the situation from his youngest daughters and instructed them to continue playing while he confronted the source of the disturbance.[10]
Yuno sensed the change in Victor's demeanor and observed that her father was extremely angry. Yog agreed, and the sisters recognized that the situation was serious despite lacking complete knowledge of the System's intentions. Rather than simply expressing concern, Yuno declared that they needed to become stronger so they could help him. After the confrontation with the System and the established Primordial authorities, Victor briefly withdrew to a distant waterfall to regain his composure. His solitude lasted less than two seconds before Moira located him and arrived alongside Ariella and Helvarya. The sisters were astonished because even Yuno, whose extraordinary devotion toward Victor was widely recognized within the family, ordinarily could not locate him when he genuinely wished to remain hidden. Moira accomplished the feat effortlessly through her unusual relationship with Reality, providing another indication of the extraordinary powers inherited by Victor's youngest daughter.[17]
Yuno later attended one of the educational gatherings organized by Victor while he was sustaining the enormous temporal undertaking associated with the Empire's accelerated development. His physical body remained exhausted from the extraordinary Energy expenditure, but his Soul continued functioning independently and conducting lessons for his daughters. Victor adopted the appearance of a scholarly instructor, wearing a black suit and glasses while standing before a blackboard. The unusual presentation immediately fascinated several daughters, who began taking photographs and recording his appearance.[18]

Her behavior demonstrated that her intense attachment had not diminished despite the enormous passage of subjective Time, while also revealing that she could employ supernatural abilities for ordinary recreational purposes rather than exclusively for combat. The precise technique used to obtain the recordings was not identified, so the feat establishes exceptional speed and versatility in documenting the event without independently proving a specific advanced power such as duplication or omnipresent perception.[18]
The educational gathering continued with discussions concerning Divinity, the nature of Divine Concepts and the fundamental structure of Reality. Victor explained that the native Universe consisted of Positivity and Negativity, whose characteristics influenced both physical and nonphysical forms of existence. Yuno recognized that her father's relationship with Negativity appeared to grant him authority over a substantial portion of the Universe, commenting that he effectively possessed half its governing power. Victor clarified that the conclusion was only partially correct because conceptual Authority did not necessarily correspond directly to personal combat strength.[13]
Victor explained that Negativity provided him with extraordinary influence over numerous Concepts, but the foundation of his power remained the abilities he had cultivated throughout his existence, his martial experience, the characteristics of his Blood Dragon lineage and the mastery of his Eldritch nature. Even without the additional Authority supplied by Negativity, he would remain an exceptionally powerful being because his strength did not depend exclusively upon a single conceptual relationship. Yuno and her sisters consequently gained a clearer understanding of the distinction between controlling fundamental aspects of Reality and possessing the practical abilities necessary to defeat powerful opponents.[13]
Throughout these events, Yuno remained an active member of the Imperial Family, continuing to develop her supernatural abilities while maintaining the affection toward Victor and admiration for Kaguya that had distinguished her earliest appearances. Her extraordinary power, Shadow-oriented combat potential and developing Yandere-related Divinity reflected the unique combination of her parents' inheritances, while her emotional growth demonstrated that even naturally born Dragon Goddesses required experience, discipline and understanding to become fully independent individuals.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Yuno's biological father and the central figure in her emotional world. Her affection toward him is exceptionally intense, often manifesting as possessiveness, jealousy and a desire to remain physically close to him whenever possible. She frequently rushes toward Victor without considering the individuals obstructing her route and enthusiastically clings to him once she reaches him. During her introduction, she casually throws Leon into a wall before embracing Victor like a koala, demonstrating that obtaining her father's attention can become more important to her than ordinary social considerations.[2]
Yuno treats Victor's commands as nearly absolute. When she prepares to attack Leon with Black Flames, Victor interrupts her simply by speaking her name, immediately causing her to abandon her hostile demeanor and return to him affectionately. Although she remains dissatisfied with allowing someone she considers an imitation of Victor to remain alive, she obeys because his approval matters more to her than satisfying her hostility. Her willingness to restrain herself also becomes apparent when Victor rewards three Imperial assassins, since she suppresses her jealousy rather than openly complaining and risking his disappointment.[3][4]
Victor openly spoils Yuno and finds her personality amusing because of its resemblance to a fictional yandere associated with his early experiences watching anime. Nevertheless, he recognizes that his daughters must acquire discipline and practical experience rather than relying exclusively upon the privileges of their birth. He consequently includes Yuno in the War Games, assigning her an unfamiliar Necromancer role that prevents her from overcoming every obstacle through her normal Blood Dragon abilities. The exercise reflects his determination to prepare his children for genuine difficulties despite his willingness to protect them from overwhelming external threats.[12][16]
Yuno's devotion also includes a desire to protect Victor rather than merely receive his affection. When she recognizes his anger during the confrontation involving Moira and the System, she declares that the daughters must become stronger so they can help him. Her response reveals that she understands the importance of contributing to the family's security instead of remaining completely dependent upon her father's strength. Even throughout the later Imperial era, her attachment remains evident through her enthusiasm during family gatherings and her willingness to use supernatural abilities to capture enormous numbers of photographs and recordings of Victor whenever he adopts an appearance that appeals to her.[10][18]

#### Kaguya Elderblood Blank
[[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]] is Yuno's biological mother and the source of her hereditary connection to the [[Blank Clan]]. Through Kaguya, Yuno inherits the supernatural characteristics traditionally associated with the family, particularly its affinity for Shadows and covert abilities. Kaguya's own identity is inseparable from her position as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Chief Maid, a role supported by generations of training within the Blank family and her personal ambition to become the perfect servant. Yuno shares her mother's affinity for Shadows but regards Maid service differently, enjoying the uniforms and lessons as a hobby while openly maintaining that Victor is the only person she would willingly serve.[1]
Their relationship is affectionate but frequently complicated by Yuno's selective obedience. She rarely challenges Victor directly but is far more willing to make sarcastic expressions or disregard Kaguya's expectations, particularly when she believes her mother has acted without consulting her father. Kaguya refuses to tolerate such behavior indefinitely and eventually restrains Yuno through superior Shadow control before imposing discipline for disrespectful conduct. The encounter demonstrates that Yuno's extraordinary natural powers do not place her beyond maternal authority and that Kaguya possesses the experience necessary to restrain her despite the immense potential of a naturally born Dragon Goddess.[4]
Yuno nevertheless admires Kaguya and is deeply proud of her accomplishments. During the cosmic family banquet, she reacts enthusiastically when her mother refuses Bruna's assistance and insists upon serving Victor personally, recognizing the dedication and possessiveness associated with the Perfect Maid. Their similarities become a recurring source of amusement among Victor's wives, particularly when Yuno develops a Yandere-related Divinity. Kaguya rejects the suggestion that her daughter's personality originates exclusively from her, arguing that similar characteristics exist throughout Victor's second generation and that Yuno's inheritance cannot be attributed to only one parent.[14][8]

#### Stella Elderblood
[[Stella Elderblood]] is one of Yuno's closest sisters and an important companion throughout her life. Kaguya describes the two as practically inseparable, recognizing that their shared affection for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] frequently brings them together despite their different maternal inheritances. Their closeness occasionally worries the family because both possess extraordinary powers and strong personalities, making their combined involvement in unauthorized activities potentially difficult to control. When Stella leaves Victor's Personal World without permission, Kaguya is relieved that Yuno has not accompanied her, believing that her own daughter's presence would have made the situation considerably more dangerous.[1]
Yuno and Stella retain their companionship throughout the Empire's accelerated development, although their shared desire for Victor's attention occasionally produces jealousy. During a major family gathering, Yuno enthusiastically clings to their father and compliments his appearance, prompting Stella to demand that she stop monopolizing him. Victor immediately intervenes and reminds them that sisters may compete, argue or spar but must never allow such disagreements to become genuine hatred. Their occasional rivalry does not negate their affection, since both remain devoted to Victor and belong to a generation whose upbringing emphasizes the importance of protecting other members of the family.[9]

#### Leon Walker
[[Leon Walker]] is [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s father from his Human life and therefore Yuno's paternal grandfather. Their first meeting is exceptionally hostile because Yuno initially refuses to recognize Leon as someone deserving affection merely because of their familial relationship. She throws him into a wall while rushing toward Victor and subsequently approaches him more deliberately, manifesting wings of Pure Darkness and examining his existence through her supernatural senses. Although she recognizes his resemblance to Victor, she considers him a counterfeit because he possesses similar characteristics without actually being the father she adores.[2][3]
Yuno's disgust develops into an immediate willingness to attack. Her violet eyes darken until they resemble black holes, and she forms a sphere of Pure Black Flames while releasing the pressure of a Dragon God. Victor stops her before the attack can be completed and explains that Leon is someone he respects, forcing her to accept that her grandfather must remain unharmed. Yuno later considers whether becoming closer to Leon could earn additional affection from Victor, but decides against forcing herself into an insincere relationship merely to obtain a reward. Their relationship consequently remains marked by her initial hostility and reluctant acceptance of Victor's wishes.[3][4]
