**[[Lacus Scarlett Elderblood]]**, formerly known as Lacus Scarlett and Lacus Scarlett Alucard, is an adopted daughter of Scathach Elderblood Scarlett, adoptive sister of Ruby Elderblood Scarlett, Siena Scarlett Elderblood and Pepper Scarlett Elderblood, one of the wives of Victor Elderblood, and the mother of Lucrecia Lena Elderblood. (Lore adapted from *My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** This material is adapted into the Rex Invictus world at the lowest priority: Rex Invictus wins any conflict, then the Flamel material, then MTWABV. Events of the MTWABV timeline (Victor's conquests, the fall of the pantheons, the [[Elderblood Empire]]) are distant background or rumor unless a chapter puts them on the page. ### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 35
Lacus Scarlett Elderblood, formerly known as Lacus Scarlett and Lacus Scarlett Alucard, is an adopted daughter of [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], adoptive sister of [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]], [[Siena Scarlett Elderblood]] and [[Pepper Scarlett Elderblood]], one of the wives of [[Victor Elderblood]], and the mother of [[Lucrecia Lena Elderblood]]. Lacus was originally a Noble Vampire princess belonging to an unidentified Vampire Clan before being exiled after her original family judged her to possess little or no talent. [[Scathach]] subsequently adopted and trained her, transforming the supposedly talentless princess into a dangerous combatant whose specialties became extreme speed, [[The Mist|Mist]] transformation, concealment and assassination-oriented combat.[9]
Among Scathach's daughters, Lacus represents the Speed component of her mother's four foundational combat principles. Pepper was trained principally around Strength, Ruby around Resistance and Defense, Siena around Power and supernatural application, while Lacus was trained to maximize mobility, acceleration, repositioning and rapid attacks.[10][11] Her personal Bloodline ability complemented this specialization by allowing her to turn herself into Mist, evade attacks by dispersing her physical form, move through the battlefield from unexpected directions and form Smoke-based weapons. Victor later forced Lacus to broaden these skills instead of relying exclusively upon Scathach's original training, helping turn her from a specialist into a considerably more adaptable fighter.[12]
Lacus eventually became one of Victor's wives through her own developing relationship with him rather than the abnormal Vampire ritual responsible for his original marriages to Violet, Sasha and Ruby. By Chapter 730, Ruby, Siena, Lacus and Pepper had all become publicly associated with Victor, binding essentially the entire central Scarlett household to his growing family.[7] She subsequently entered the Tower of Nightmare and later abandoned the limitations of her original Vampire Race when Victor reconstructed her into a Blood Dragon and genuine True Dragon.[13][4] Her transformation preserved the Mist characteristics anchored to her Soul rather than replacing them with an unrelated generic Dragon ability; her body began physically converting into pure Mist during the transformation itself, and her evolved Smoke later became capable of transporting both Lacus and other people across space.[4][14]
After joining the [[Elderblood Empire]], Lacus experienced enormous periods of accelerated Time alongside the rest of Victor's family and eventually became a mother. The daughter previously left unnamed in older records is Lucrecia Lena Elderblood, explicitly identified in Chapter 1166 as the daughter of Lacus and Victor.[8] By the later Imperial era, Lacus had also changed noticeably from the perpetually sleepy woman of the early story. She retained her relaxed personality, but Chapter 1162 specifically groups her with Siena and Ruby as women who had developed an unmistakably maternal demeanor after thousands upon thousands of years raising their children.[15]

### Physical Appearance
Lacus is a beautiful, comparatively petite woman standing approximately 160 centimeters tall. During her Vampire era she possessed pale skin, long red hair, red eyes and a slim figure, making her noticeably smaller than both [[Scathach]] and Siena.[5] Her physical appearance remained that of a young adult despite already being more than five centuries old before the major time skips, as the outward appearance of ancient Vampires bore little relationship to their chronological age.[1]
After becoming a Blood Dragon, Lacus' racial foundation was completely reconstructed. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s transformation did not simply place Draconic characteristics over her old Vampire body; it rebuilt her into a genuine True Dragon while retaining traits that had become fundamental to her Soul and identity.[4] Lacus' [[The Mist|Mist]] ability was so deeply embedded within her existence that her body began dissolving into pure Mist while the transformation was still underway. Her current humanoid appearance therefore represents the chosen physical form of a Dragon Goddess whose actual body is no longer restricted to conventional mortal biology.

### Personality
Lacus is relaxed, quiet and considerably less confrontational than Siena or energetic than Pepper. Her most famous habit is sleeping whenever circumstances permit it, to the point that other members of Clan Scarlett casually expect to find her asleep while nothing particularly important is happening. During the Adrasteia expedition, Siena was awake dealing with the guards while Lacus and Pepper remained comfortably asleep against their sister, and Lacus later partially awakened while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was carrying them only to recognize his presence and immediately return to sleep because she considered herself completely safe around him.[16]
Her fondness for sleep should not be confused with cowardice or an unwillingness to train. Lacus was personally raised by [[Scathach]], possesses centuries of martial experience and served as one of Victor's early sparring partners during his first period in [[Nightingale]]. She was also knowledgeable enough about Noble Vampire Bloodlines to explain their hereditary mechanics to Victor when he was still learning the basic rules of supernatural society.[17] When actual danger appears, Lacus can immediately abandon her sleepy demeanor and operate as a fast, disciplined assassin, scout or battlefield controller.
Compared with the more explosive personalities surrounding Victor, Lacus usually reacts to bizarre situations with dry humor and relative emotional balance. During the early story she attempted to lighten uncomfortable situations with jokes rather than escalate them, while later chapters group her with women such as Sasha, Natalia, Mizuki, Siena, Pepper and Haruna as some of the comparatively balanced personalities within Victor's household.[18][6] She can participate in the absurd behavior of the Elderblood family without normally becoming one of its most chaotic members.
Lacus possesses immense respect and affection for Scathach. Her adoptive mother did more than simply provide her with a home: Scathach accepted the girl another Noble family had discarded as supposedly talentless and personally forged her into an elite fighter.[9] Lacus consequently becomes hostile when she believes Scathach is being disrespected, although centuries of living together also give her enough familiarity to joke about her mother's battle obsession and recognize almost immediately when Scathach intends to transform an ordinary event into another brutal training session.
She is equally affectionate toward Siena, Ruby and Pepper. Their adoptive and biological distinctions have never prevented the women from behaving as genuine sisters, and Lacus is especially comfortable with Pepper despite their opposite personalities. During Victor's training in Chapter 334, Pepper analyzed Lacus' movements and suggested ways she could adapt to Victor. Rather than becoming defensive because her younger sister was correcting her, Lacus accepted the advice and openly expressed happiness at Pepper's improvement.[12] This attitude reflects an important aspect of Lacus' personality: while she can be proud and competitive, family success matters more to her than proving personal superiority.
Her relationship with Victor developed through the same familiarity. Unlike Ruby, Lacus possessed no magical marriage connection forcing emotional intimacy between them. She met Victor as her sister's newly transformed husband, taught and fought him during his early training, gradually trusted him enough to sleep without concern in his presence, endured his increasingly harsh attempts to improve the Scarlett sisters and eventually chose to join him as a wife.[17][16][12][7]
The later Imperial era changed Lacus without erasing these qualities. She remains relatively relaxed but is no longer accurately described as nothing more than a sleepy NEET. After subjective millennia of marriage, warfare, Imperial life and motherhood, younger members of the family recognize her as possessing the mature demeanor of an experienced mother.[15] Her current personality therefore combines the easygoing Lacus of Nightingale with the immense lived experience of a woman who has spent more than a hundred thousand subjective years inside Victor's civilization.

### Synopsis

#### Vampire Society
Lacus was not born into Clan Scarlett. She originated from another Noble Vampire family in which she apparently held the status of a princess, but her relatives eventually judged her untalented and exiled her. The most direct explanation of this past comes from [[Zagan]], who later described Lacus as an exiled princess abandoned by her Clan because of her supposed lack of talent and claimed that the Clan subsequently disappeared.[9] Zagan's account should not be treated as entirely neutral because he was deliberately attempting to destabilize the Scarlett sisters and immediately tempted Lacus with information concerning possible survivors. What is certain is that Lacus was rejected by her birth family, adopted by [[Scathach]] and ultimately became vastly more powerful than the people who had dismissed her could have expected.
Scathach raised Lacus as her second adopted daughter alongside Siena and later Pepper. Her training divided important combat fundamentals according to each daughter's natural strengths, with Lacus becoming particularly associated with Speed.[10][11] Her [[The Mist|Mist]] Bloodline evolved naturally around this foundation, allowing her to combine extremely rapid movement with concealment and a partially non-solid form rather than imitating Scathach's overwhelmingly direct style.
Lacus first appeared during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s initial stay in [[Nightingale]] after Scathach brought him into the Vampire world.[19] She soon became one of the people responsible for educating him about Noble Vampires. When Victor attempted to understand why Ruby's abilities differed from Scathach's despite belonging to the same Bloodline, Lacus explained how hereditary Vampire powers evolved between generations and how descendants could inherit altered or strengthened versions of parental abilities.[17] The conversation became particularly relevant because Victor's own abnormal nature allowed him to access powers belonging to several wives simultaneously, something that conventional Vampire Bloodlines were never designed to accomplish.
Lacus also trained Victor directly. Despite being only a newly transformed Vampire, Victor began copying and understanding combat movements at a rate that shocked the far older Lacus and other Scarlett women.[20] Her early interactions with him therefore centered less upon hostility than Siena's and more upon curiosity toward the increasingly obvious anomaly growing inside their family.
As Victor became closer to Scathach, Lacus and her sisters noticed that their mother's relationship with him was becoming unusually personal. The Scarlett daughters displayed varying degrees of jealousy and discomfort when Scathach and Victor interacted with an intimacy that went well beyond a normal master-student relationship.[21] These early reactions became increasingly ironic as Scathach, Siena, Lacus and Pepper would all eventually join Victor's family directly.

#### A New Pillar
Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s rise through the Vampire tournament and the creation of his new Pillar, Lacus remained among the Scarlett women increasingly drawn into his affairs. When the conflict surrounding Sasha and the Inquisition moved into the Human World, [[Scathach]] arrived with Siena, Lacus and Pepper and temporarily moved much of the Scarlett household into the Walker residence.[22][5]
Victor used their presence as another opportunity to train. He fought Ruby, Siena, Lacus and Pepper together, forcing himself to deal with a coordinated group whose different Bloodlines and specializations attacked from multiple angles.[23][24] Lacus' speed was especially relevant; Victor concluded that without relying upon the acceleration supplied by Fulger Lightning, Lacus remained faster than his ordinary physical movement at the time.[24] Her performance reinforced the logic behind Scathach assigning Speed as her principal combat foundation.
Lacus' technical knowledge was not limited entirely to personal combat. Sasha's development in basic martial arts also benefited from instruction associated with Lacus, showing that the supposedly lazy Vampire possessed enough understanding to teach foundational combat skills to other Noble Vampires.[10] She later accompanied the Scarlett group during events involving the Count families, including the confrontation between Natashia and [[Niklaus Horseman]] and Anderson's visit to Victor.[25][26]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually concluded that Siena, Lacus and Pepper had become too comfortable within the familiar environment created by centuries of ordinary Clan life. With [[Scathach]]'s permission, he effectively kidnapped the sleeping sisters and brought them into the expedition involving [[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]] and Clan Adrasteia.[16] Lacus' reaction illustrated the trust already existing between them: after partially waking and realizing Victor was carrying her, she simply returned to sleep rather than questioning whether she was in danger.
The expedition became an extended training opportunity. Victor fought Lacus and Pepper directly and deliberately prevented them from remaining dependent upon the same techniques and weapons Scathach had taught them.[12] Lacus demonstrated a combat style considerably more assassin-like than the straightforward brutality normally associated with her adoptive mother. She dispersed into [[The Mist|Mist]] to evade Victor's Lightning, used the vapor to change position, appeared behind him and attacked with daggers at high speed. Victor repeatedly altered the conditions of the fight and gave her different weapons, forcing Lacus to become more flexible rather than assuming one familiar attack pattern would always succeed.[12]
Pepper participated actively in the same training by observing Victor's movements and helping Lacus recognize weaknesses she could exploit. Lacus accepted the advice naturally and praised Pepper's progress, reinforcing how little personal rivalry interfered with the sisters' cooperation.[12] Her Mist transformations also became faster as the fight progressed, showing that Lacus could improve practical execution when placed under sufficient pressure.
Lacus and Pepper later trained beneath [[Mizuki Elderblood]], expanding their experience beyond Scathach and Victor.[27] Their relationship with the modern Human World also grew during this period. The sisters occasionally sought time outside [[Nightingale]] simply because they wanted access to greater "modernity", entertainment and conveniences unavailable in the isolated Vampire society in which they had spent most of their lives.[28]

#### Demonic War
[[Diablo]]'s invasion turned the skills Lacus had been refining into genuine warfare. Demonic portals opened across [[Nightingale]], and the Scarlett territory was attacked by [[Zagan]], the 61st Key of Solomon, and his forces.[29][30] While Siena coordinated the defense, Lacus immediately transformed into [[The Mist|Mist]] and moved through the territory to confirm that civilians had already been evacuated before returning to the battle.[30]
The Scarlett sisters then deployed one of the strongest combined techniques demonstrated by the Clan. Lacus rose above the battlefield and released enormous quantities of dark gray and black Smoke, expanding the cloud until it covered the surrounding territory and obscured the region beneath supernatural darkness. Pepper saturated Lacus' Smoke with Water, Siena froze the atmospheric structure with her Ice, and Ruby used her superior control to coordinate the completed effect into an enormous supernatural snowstorm.[30] [[Scathach]] had trained the sisters specifically for this sort of cooperation, and the resulting phenomenon possessed sufficient destructive potential to devastate a large city while simultaneously controlling the battlefield and differentiating between enemies and allies.
Lacus did not remain exclusively responsible for preparing the environment. She condensed her Smoke into daggers reinforced with supernatural Energy and joined Ruby and Pepper in killing the invading Demons directly.[30] Her performance was an explicit contradiction of the judgment made by the family that had once abandoned her as talentless: the exiled princess had become one of four daughters capable of turning an entire Clan territory into a controlled supernatural killing field.
Zagan subsequently attempted psychological warfare against the sisters. In Lacus' case, he brought up her status as an exiled princess and claimed knowledge concerning her lost Clan and possible surviving relatives.[9] Lacus reacted with anger rather than abandoning the family that had raised her. By this point Scathach, Siena, Ruby and Pepper had been her real family for centuries, and information concerning her biological relatives no longer carried enough weight for Zagan to separate her from them.
The confrontation also foreshadowed Lacus' later marriage. Zagan openly provoked Lacus and Pepper by questioning whether they intended to join [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s growing group of wives, prompting a possessive response from Ruby.[9] What was initially used as mockery eventually became reality when both women formally joined Victor's household.

#### King Among Kings
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s centuries within [[Biblical Hell]] and the enormous expansion of his faction, Lacus had become thoroughly integrated into his family. Unlike Ruby, she had never been connected to Victor by the original accidental marriage ritual; their bond developed through centuries of ordinary interaction, training, warfare and trust. By Chapter 729, Lacus was already described among Victor's wives and grouped with Sasha, Natalia, Mizuki, Siena, Pepper and Haruna as some of the comparatively balanced women within his otherwise increasingly extreme household.[6]
Chapter 730 made the arrangement public. The central women belonging to [[Nightingale]]'s former Count families formally entered Victor's expanding political family, and the Scarlett women associated with him included Ruby, Siena, Lacus and Pepper.[7] The announcement transformed the relationship between the old Noble Vampire Clans and Victor's faction; Clan Scarlett was no longer simply allied to the Second Progenitor but had become connected to him through marriage and Blood.
Lacus subsequently became part of the greater Elderblood lineage and continued participating naturally in Victor's expanding household. Prior to the [[Greek Underworld]] expedition, she joked about whether [[Scathach]] would finally gain a God-Slayer title, only for Ruby to point out that their mother had already killed enough Divine opponents for the distinction to be redundant.[31]
Scathach later became dissatisfied with the developmental difference between Ruby and her adopted sisters. Ruby had already endured the Tower of Nightmare, whereas Siena, Lacus and Pepper lacked the same experience. The imbalance was corrected when the three entered the Tower with numerous members of Victor's faction in Chapter 843.[13] The Tower's repeated cycles of lethal scenarios, pain, failure and resurrection were especially appropriate for Lacus because they eliminated the possibility of complacency and forced her to remain alert, adapt and survive under circumstances where habitual reliance upon one [[The Mist|Mist]]-based strategy could result in immediate death.
During this period Scathach also formally taught Victor, Ruby, Siena, Lacus and Pepper about Runes. She explained that true Runes combined a supernatural language, Energy and intention into instructions imposed upon Reality, distinguishing them from ordinary written magical languages.[32] Lacus therefore possessed formal Runic education before her later transformation into a True Dragon gave her access to the considerably deeper relationship Dragons possessed with languages of power.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Lacus eventually accepted [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s transformation into the new Blood Dragon Race. She entered the second transformation group alongside Siena, Pepper, Victoria, Carmila, Natasha, Maya and Haruna.[4] The transformation rebuilt her body and Soul into those of a True Dragon rather than merely adding Draconic features to her existing Vampire body.
Her [[The Mist|Mist]] ability reacted immediately. Lacus began coughing out pure Mist before her body progressively transformed into the substance itself, briefly panicking when she realized that her physical form was dissolving.[4] Victor's process demonstrated that the Blood Dragon transformation preserved characteristics anchored deeply within an individual's Soul; instead of losing the supernatural ability developed throughout centuries of Vampire life, Lacus' Mist became integrated into her new Draconic biology.
She emerged as a Blood Dragon whose distinctive supernatural identity remained centered upon Mist and Smoke. The transformation nevertheless expanded the scale and versatility of those abilities enormously. By Chapter 925, Lacus could employ Smoke as a genuine transportation method, disappearing through it and transporting additional people with herself rather than using the ability purely for local concealment and assassination.[14] What had once functioned principally as a Vampire combat technique had developed into a broader Draconic mobility system applicable to travel, infiltration, rescue and repositioning allies.

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
As Dragon's Nest expanded into the foundation of the [[Elderblood Empire]], Lacus became thousands of subjective years older through the family's earlier periods of accelerated development. She remained simultaneously a Scarlett daughter, Elderblood wife and Dragon Goddess rather than abandoning one identity for another.
She also became a mother. Older versions of the article left her child unnamed, but the late Imperial chapters identify [[Lucrecia Lena Elderblood]] as the daughter of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Lacus.[8] Lucrecia belongs to the large group of red-haired Elderblood daughters associated with the Scarlett branch and is described as comparatively quiet despite still participating in the chaos created by her sisters.[8]
Lacus' long-term development accelerated even further when Victor initiated the Empire's most ambitious temporal project. The ritual was designed to grant approximately 200,000 internal years of civilization-wide development while roughly one year passed externally.[2] Natalia controlled the temporal element, Victor served as the principal power source and stabilizing anchor, and his wives shared portions of the burden while the Empire's military, science, population and supernatural development advanced through millennia.

#### Those Who Rule Reality
By Imperial Year 100,000, the temporal project had already transformed the Elderblood civilization almost beyond recognition.[33] The process continued until at least approximately 190,000 subjective years had elapsed, giving Lacus a current experienced age measured in well over one hundred thousand years when her original Vampire life and previous accelerated periods are included.[3] The five centuries that once made her an ancient Vampire consequently became a tiny fraction of her total lived experience.
Her domestic development became equally visible. During the incidents surrounding Pepper's daughters, Sylvia observed that Pepper still lacked the obvious maternal atmosphere possessed by Siena, Ruby and Lacus.[15] The comparison established that Lacus had developed into an experienced mother even though her relaxed personality and youthful physical appearance remained recognizable.
The original Scarlett household also remained intact as a social unit despite the enormous size of Clan Elderblood. When Pepper's daughters entered one of her private research areas and the situation escalated into another family incident, Ruby contacted Siena, Lacus and [[Scathach]]. Their gathering was jokingly treated as a Scarlett family meeting with their mother-in-law, illustrating that hundreds of millennia of growth, marriages, children and Imperial responsibilities had not erased the bonds established in [[Nightingale]].[15]
Lacus and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughter became more visible during the following family gathering. In Chapter 1166, Victor jokingly addressed several of the red-haired daughters after another bout of sibling chaos; among them was [[Lucrecia Lena Elderblood]], explicitly identified as Lacus and Victor's daughter and noted for normally possessing a quieter disposition than several of her sisters.[8] Lacus' lineage therefore continues directly into the second Elderblood generation alongside the daughters of Ruby, Siena and Pepper, preserving the old Scarlett branch inside Victor's far larger dynasty.

### Relationships

#### Scathach Elderblood Scarlett
[[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] is Lacus' adoptive mother, principal teacher and the person most responsible for the direction Lacus' life ultimately took. Lacus' biological Clan judged her talentless and discarded her, whereas [[Scathach]] accepted her as a daughter and spent centuries developing precisely the abilities her previous family had failed to recognize.[9] The contrast between the two families explains much of Lacus' loyalty: whatever blood relationship she once possessed elsewhere became secondary to the woman who actually raised, protected and trained her.
Lacus respects Scathach enormously but is not intimidated into complete formality around her. Centuries of family life allow her to recognize Scathach's battle obsession, joke about her behavior and anticipate when an ordinary gathering is about to become another training exercise. Scathach's eventual marriage to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] created the unusual situation in which Lacus and her adoptive mother are simultaneously mother and daughter as well as fellow wives inside the Elderblood household, but the original parent-child relationship remains recognizable.

#### Victor Elderblood
Lacus first met [[Victor Elderblood]] when [[Scathach]] brought him to [[Nightingale]]. Unlike Ruby, she possessed no supernatural marriage bond tying them together and initially interacted with him as a teacher, sparring partner and member of his wife's family. She helped explain Noble Vampire Bloodline inheritance to him and fought him during his early training, witnessing firsthand how impossibly quickly Victor learned techniques that had taken ordinary Vampires years or centuries to refine.[17][20]
Their relationship grew through familiarity rather than a single dramatic event. Victor continued training Lacus, dragged her beyond the comfortable routine she had built in Nightingale, trusted her in dangerous situations and increasingly treated Scathach's adopted daughters as members of his own family rather than simply Ruby's relatives.[16][12] Lacus' trust became so complete that she could partially awaken while Victor was transporting her, identify him and immediately return to sleep without needing an explanation.[16]
By Chapter 729 she was already treated as one of his wives, and Chapter 730 publicly formalized her place beside Ruby, Siena and Pepper.[6][7] Victor later transformed Lacus into a Blood Dragon, giving her an entirely new racial foundation while deliberately preserving the [[The Mist|Mist]] abilities that had become part of her identity.[4] Their relationship eventually produced [[Lucrecia Lena Elderblood]].[8]

#### Siena Scarlett Elderblood
[[Siena Scarlett Elderblood]] is Lacus' older adoptive sister and one of the people with whom she has spent the largest portion of her life. Siena's more responsible and administrative personality naturally contrasts with Lacus' tendency to sleep whenever nothing requires immediate action, yet the difference has never damaged their closeness. During the Adrasteia expedition, Lacus and Pepper comfortably slept against Siena while their older sister handled interaction with the guards, illustrating an intimacy created by centuries of shared family life.[16]
They also complement one another in battle. Lacus' mobility and Smoke can establish a battlefield environment that Siena's overwhelming elemental output then weaponizes. Both women eventually married [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], entered Clan Elderblood and became Dragon Goddesses while retaining their identities as Scarlett sisters.

#### Pepper Scarlett Elderblood
[[Pepper Scarlett Elderblood]] is Lacus' younger adoptive sister and frequent training partner. Pepper's energetic, eccentric personality contrasts almost perfectly with Lacus' relaxed and sleepy behavior, making their interactions particularly distinctive. They often trained together and naturally supported one another rather than competing for status.
Chapter 334 demonstrates this relationship particularly clearly. Pepper watched Lacus fight [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], analyzed his behavior and suggested tactical changes Lacus could use. Lacus accepted the advice without wounded pride and expressed happiness at seeing how much Pepper had improved.[12] Their powers also function effectively together: during the Demonic War Lacus provided the massive Smoke cloud while Pepper saturated it with Water before Siena and Ruby completed the sisters' environmental attack.[30]

#### Ruby Elderblood Scarlett
[[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] is Lacus' adoptive sister and [[Scathach]]'s biological daughter. Ruby's original marriage to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was one of the principal reasons Lacus encountered him, but Lacus ultimately developed an independent relationship with Victor rather than existing merely as Ruby's relative. Ruby, Siena, Lacus and Pepper eventually entered his household together, turning virtually the entire central Scarlett family into a branch of Clan Elderblood.[7]
In battle, Ruby's exceptional supernatural control complements Lacus' atmospheric manipulation. Their Demonic War technique depended upon Lacus first generating the enormous Smoke medium before Ruby helped regulate the completed phenomenon with sufficient precision for it to distinguish allies from enemies.[30]

#### Lucrecia Lena Elderblood
[[Lucrecia Lena Elderblood]] is the daughter of Lacus and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Earlier records listed Lacus' child as unnamed, but Chapter 1166 explicitly identifies Lucrecia as their daughter.[8] She belongs to the enormous second generation of the Elderblood family and is included among Victor's red-haired daughters during the late Imperial family gathering. Unlike several of her more disruptive sisters, Lucrecia is described as normally being relatively quiet, although she still became involved in the group mischief surrounding Velina and the rest of Victor's daughters.[8]
Lucrecia's existence confirms that Lacus became a mother during the long Imperial era. By Chapter 1162, Siena, Ruby and Lacus were already described as possessing visibly maternal demeanors after subjective millennia raising children, making Lacus' development from an exiled and frequently sleeping Vampire princess into an experienced Dragon-Goddess mother one of the quieter long-term transformations within the Scarlett branch.[15]

#### Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia
[[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]] became close to Lacus through the longstanding relationship between Clan Scarlett and Clan Adrasteia. Lacus accompanied [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], Siena and Pepper into Eleanor's territory during the expedition that became one of Victor's attempts to break the Scarlett sisters out of their familiar routine.[16] Both women eventually became Victor's wives and [[Blood Dragons]], further integrating the ancient Count families into the Elderblood dynasty.

- By the mature Imperial era, Lacus, Siena and Ruby are explicitly described as possessing recognizable maternal demeanors, in contrast to Pepper's unusually youthful behavior.[15]
- Despite joining Clan Elderblood and living through enormous subjective periods of Imperial history, Lacus continues to be treated as part of the Scarlett family alongside Scathach, Ruby, Siena and Pepper.[15]

#### Skills and Abilities
Lacus Scarlett Elderblood is a speed-oriented combatant and assassin whose fighting style combines extreme mobility, mist and smoke manipulation, dagger combat and the martial arts taught by [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]]. Even during her early life as a Noble Vampire, Lacus was one of the fastest of [[Scathach]]'s daughters and possessed the ability to transform into mist, allowing her to disappear from an opponent's sight and emerge from unexpected directions. Continued training under Scathach, [[Victor Elderblood]] and later [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]] refined this natural specialization into a dedicated assassination style. Following her transformation into a Blood Dragon, the Vampire abilities, physical characteristics and supernatural powers she had developed were incorporated into a considerably stronger Draconic existence.[1][2][3][4][5]
- Blood Dragon Physiology: Lacus eventually underwent Victor's Dragonization process and became a genuine Blood Dragon. Rather than discarding the abilities she possessed as a Vampire, her existing supernatural characteristics became part of her new Draconic foundation.[5][6][7]
- Immense Physical Strength: Lacus possesses physical power vastly superior to that of ordinary Humans and conventional Vampires as a consequence of her True Dragon body.
- Extreme Superhuman Speed: Speed was already Lacus' principal physical specialization before becoming a Dragon. Her Blood Dragon physiology substantially magnifies this characteristic, making rapid movement one of the foundations of her combat style.[2][3][4]
- Superhuman Reflexes: Lacus possesses reactions sufficient to exploit her extreme movement speed during close-range assassination attacks.
- Immense Durability: Her Draconic body grants extremely high resistance to physical and supernatural damage.
- Enhanced Agility: Lacus possesses exceptional balance, coordination and control over her movement, qualities particularly important to her speed-oriented fighting style.
- Enhanced Senses: Her Vampire sensory abilities were retained and enhanced by her Draconic transformation.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Lacus possesses the powerful regenerative ability associated with Vampires and [[Blood Dragons]].
- [[Immortality]] and Longevity: Her supernatural physiology grants a lifespan vastly exceeding that of mortal species and prevents conventional aging from functioning normally.
- True Dragon Form: Lacus is a full Blood Dragon rather than merely a creature temporarily possessing Draconic characteristics. She can manifest the horns, wings and other physical characteristics associated with her Dragon nature.[5][6][7]
- Flight: Lacus can fly through the use of her Draconic wings.
- Shapeshifting: As a True Dragon, Lacus can alter her outward physical state and transition between a humanoid appearance and more overtly Draconic characteristics.
- Size Alteration: [[True Dragons]] can substantially modify their bodily dimensions, allowing Lacus to assume a larger Draconic scale than her normal humanoid body.
- Dragon Breath: Lacus possesses the racial ability of True Dragons to release supernatural Energy through a Breath attack. The specific elemental or conceptual nature of her personal Breath has not yet been individually explained.
- [[Draconic Energy]]: Her True Dragon body produces and supports far greater quantities of supernatural Energy than her former Vampire physiology.
- True Dragon Soul: Victor's Dragonization reconstructs the recipient as a genuine Dragon on both physical and spiritual levels rather than simply altering the body.[5][6]
- Draconic Runes: As a True Dragon, Lacus possesses natural compatibility with the Draconic Language and its Runes, which allow Dragons to encode their intent into supernatural Energy and phenomena.[8]
- Dragon Goddess: In the later Imperial era, Lacus possesses the status of a Dragon Goddess in addition to being a Blood Dragon. Her exact personal Divinity has not yet been clearly identified in the available chapters, and therefore no specific conceptual Divinity should be assigned to her without further confirmation.
- [[Divine Energy]]: As a Divine-level Blood Dragon, Lacus possesses Energy and existence superior to her earlier Vampire state.
- Unspecified Divinity: Although Lacus is classified as a Dragon Goddess, the specific Concept or Concepts forming her personal Divinity have not yet been explicitly named.
- [[The Mist|Mist]] Manipulation: Lacus' signature supernatural ability is control over mist. Unlike Ruby and Pepper, whose early elemental abilities were Ice and Water respectively, Lacus possessed the ability to control mist and used it principally for concealment, movement, confusion and assassination.[1]
- Mist Generation: Lacus can generate supernatural mist around herself and the battlefield.
- Mist Transformation: Lacus can convert her body into mist, allowing her physical form to disperse and rapidly relocate before reforming elsewhere.[1][2][3]
- Rapid Mist Transition: Continuous combat training greatly improved the speed and efficiency with which Lacus could transform. During Victor's training in Chapter 334, she transformed into mist and reappeared elsewhere significantly faster than she normally could while consuming less Energy than expected.[3]
- Concealment: Lacus can obscure her position through mist, making it considerably more difficult for an enemy to track her movements.[1]
- Visual Obstruction: Her mist can interfere with an opponent's ability to visually identify her exact position, complementing her high-speed assassination style.
- Escape and Evasion: Lacus originally used her mist ability to confuse enemies and escape unfavorable situations. Under Scathach and Victor, she developed the same ability into an offensive movement method.[1]
- Surprise Attacks: Lacus can disappear into mist before rematerializing near or behind a target, allowing her to attack from directions that are difficult to predict.[2][3]
- Mist Flight: Lacus can maintain her dispersed mist state while moving through the air. During the Demonic invasion of [[Nightingale]] she transformed into mist and ascended into the clouds before spreading her bloodline power across the surrounding territory.[9]
- Partial Battlefield Concealment: Her ability can be expanded beyond merely hiding her own body and used to alter the visual conditions of a wider battlefield.[9]
- Smoke Manipulation: Lacus can produce and manipulate a dark gray-to-black supernatural smoke related to her bloodline ability. Unlike ordinary smoke, Lacus can shape it into weapons, spread it across large areas and employ it as a medium for movement.[9][10]
- Smoke Generation: Lacus can directly produce large quantities of dark gray smoke with black coloration from her supernatural power.[9]
- Large-Scale Smoke Generation: She can release enough smoke to spread throughout a substantial territory, transforming the surrounding environment into terrain advantageous to her assassination style.[9]
- Smoke Shaping: Lacus can condense her smoke into stable physical-like forms rather than using it exclusively as an obscuring cloud.
- Smoke Weapon Creation: Lacus demonstrated the ability to produce daggers directly from smoke during the Demonic invasion of Nightingale.[9]
- Smoke Daggers: Lacus forms short blades from condensed smoke. During Chapter 524, the daggers were additionally concentrated with a purple form of supernatural Energy before she entered combat against the invading Demons.[9]
- Smoke Transportation: Lacus can use smoke as a medium to move herself from one location to another.[10]
- Passenger Transportation: Her smoke-based transportation is not restricted to herself; she can also use the ability to carry other beings.[10]
- Battlefield Obscuration: By filling a battlefield with supernatural smoke, Lacus can greatly reduce an opponent's ability to track her while remaining capable of exploiting the environment herself.[9]
- Assassination Field: Her combination of smoke, mist and extreme speed allows Lacus to transform an obscured battlefield into an environment particularly suited to surprise attacks and rapid executions.
- Natural Assassin: Lacus is explicitly described as being naturally suited to assassination. Her speed, temperament, mist-based bloodline ability and preference for surprise attacks complement the role exceptionally well.[2][4]
- Stealth: Lacus is skilled at approaching opponents without immediately exposing her position, particularly when her mist or smoke abilities are available.
- Presence Concealment: Her supernatural powers allow her to obscure the visual and physical signs of her approach before striking.
- Surprise Attacks: Lacus routinely attacks from behind or from an opponent's blind spot after using speed or mist to conceal the transition.[2][3]
- Rapid Execution: After recognizing that she lacked the explosive power possessed by some of her sisters, Lacus deliberately began developing methods of killing enemies more quickly rather than attempting to imitate their fighting styles.[4]
- Assassination Training under Kaguya: Lacus personally sought instruction from Kaguya Elderblood Blank, whose Clan specializes in assassination and intelligence work. She wanted an outside perspective in addition to Scathach's teachings so she could improve her ability to eliminate enemies efficiently.[4]
- Hit-and-Run Combat: Lacus can exploit her superior speed to enter striking distance, attack and immediately disengage before the enemy can properly retaliate.
- Blind-Spot Exploitation: Her fighting style emphasizes striking when an opponent's attention has been redirected elsewhere, particularly during group combat.[2]
- Ambush Combat: Lacus is particularly dangerous when she is not required to confront an enemy directly and can instead attack from concealment.
- Target Capture: Despite her assassination specialization, Lacus is also experienced with capturing rather than killing enemies. During the Demonic War, she confidently noted that her family were specialists in capturing people before participating in the attempt to seize a Demon Duke alive.[9]
- Advanced [[Martial Arts]]: Lacus has been trained since childhood by Scathach Elderblood Scarlett, one of the greatest martial artists in the setting. Her training was deliberately adapted around her natural advantages rather than forcing every Scarlett daughter into the same combat style.[2][11]
- Scathach's Foundational Martial Art: Lacus is one of the original practitioners of Scathach's five-part foundational combat system.[11]
- Speed: Lacus was specifically assigned the Speed branch of Scathach's martial art. This branch focuses on movement, footwork, acceleration, breathing and the correct transmission of force through the legs, and can eventually be combined directly with supernatural abilities.[11]
- Strength: Although Speed was her initial specialization, Lacus later trained the other fundamental branches necessary to become a more complete fighter.
- Power: Lacus trained to improve the supernatural force delivered through her attacks, addressing her historical weakness in explosive offensive power.[4]
- Defense: By Chapter 621, Lacus was specifically noted to have developed good defensive ability despite her earlier characterization as relatively fragile during the fight against Victor.[2][4]
- Control: The complete foundational system culminates in Control—the ability to combine Strength, Power, Speed and Defense efficiently instead of applying them as isolated principles.[11]
- Extreme Movement Technique: Lacus' specialization in the Speed branch allows her to use proper footwork, breathing, acceleration and force transmission instead of relying solely upon supernatural statistics to move quickly.[11]
- Combat Adaptation: Training under both Scathach and Victor forced Lacus to fight opponents using constantly changing weapons, elements and environments. This greatly reduced her dependence upon one predetermined style of battle.[3]
- Harsh Combat Training: Victor deliberately trained Lacus and Pepper with dangerous elemental attacks, including Lightning and Fire, forcing them to evade under pressure rather than practicing exclusively through controlled drills.[3]
- Pain and Injury Tolerance: Scathach's daughters routinely trained by fighting until one of them was seriously injured, using Vampire regeneration to allow much harsher practical training than would be possible for ordinary martial artists.[4]
- Dagger Mastery: Daggers are Lacus' preferred weapons and best complement her extreme speed and assassination style.[2][3][9]
- Dual Dagger Combat: Lacus commonly wields a dagger in each hand, allowing her to perform numerous rapid cutting and stabbing attacks during a single approach.[3]
- High-Speed Barrage: Lacus can unleash many dagger attacks within an extremely short period. During her training against Victor, she emerged from mist behind him and launched a series of thrusts at blinding speed, believing she had cut him repeatedly within a millisecond.[3]
- Limb Severing: Lacus possesses enough precision and cutting force to target and remove limbs. During the Scarlett sisters' training fight against Victor, she appeared behind him and severed his arm with her daggers.[2]
- Ice Daggers: During her earlier Vampire period, Lacus utilized daggers formed from Ice while fighting Victor.[2]
- Smoke Daggers: Following further development of her own bloodline powers, Lacus learned to form daggers directly from supernatural smoke rather than depending only upon Ice weapons created through the Scarlett family's resources.[9]
- Energy Reinforcement: Lacus can concentrate supernatural Energy into her smoke daggers, increasing their effectiveness beyond that of an ordinary constructed weapon.[9]
- Multiple Weapon Proficiency: Although daggers remain her natural preference, Scathach taught Lacus not to become helpless if deprived of her chosen weapon. Victor subsequently expanded this philosophy by forcing Lacus and Pepper to train amid a battlefield containing numerous different weapon types.[3]
- Improvised Weapon Usage: Victor taught Lacus that against weaker opponents, virtually any sufficiently durable object or battlefield weapon could be used instead of wasting time searching specifically for her preferred daggers.[3]
- Unarmed Combat: Scathach's training emphasized that losing a weapon does not mean becoming defenseless; Lacus remains capable of relying upon her own body and martial arts if necessary.[3]
- Scarlett Sisters Combination: Lacus has centuries of experience training alongside Ruby, Siena and Pepper. Their familiarity allows the four sisters to coordinate their radically different specialties with very little verbal communication.[2]
- Assassin Role: Within the sisters' standard formation, Lacus acts as the assassin. Ruby occupies and pressures the opponent at close range, Siena attacks from range, Pepper provides destructive physical pressure and Lacus exploits openings to attack unexpectedly.[2]
- Blind-Side Attacks: Lacus is particularly effective when another sister has already drawn the opponent's attention. During the battle against Victor, Ruby immediately exploited the moment Victor turned toward Lacus after she severed his arm, while Lacus disappeared back into mist once the opening had been used.[2]
- Coordinated Hunting: During the Demonic invasion of Nightingale, Lacus, Ruby and Pepper moved together against the Demon forces and slaughtered enemies through coordinated high-speed attacks.[9]
- Battlefield Preparation: Lacus can improve the effectiveness of the entire team by spreading smoke over the battlefield before the sisters begin their assault.[9]
- High-Level Combat Experience: Lacus has centuries of supernatural combat training under Scathach and later participated in wars against increasingly dangerous enemies.
- Demon War Veteran: Lacus directly fought invading Demon forces during the attack on Nightingale and assisted her sisters in defending Scarlett territory.[12][9]
- Combat against Stronger Opponents: Lacus routinely trained against individuals considerably stronger than herself, particularly Victor and Scathach, developing experience in surviving and creating openings against opponents she could not simply overpower.[1][2][3]
- Battle Sense: Victor's training deliberately forced Lacus to react to unpredictable elemental attacks and changing combat conditions, improving her instinctive ability to identify danger and move before being struck.[3]
- Precision: Her assassination style requires significantly more precise timing and targeting than indiscriminate large-scale attacks, and Lacus repeatedly aims for vulnerable areas or disabling strikes.[2][3]
- Energy Efficiency: Lacus improved the efficiency of her mist transformation to the point that a faster transition required less Energy than she expected during Chapter 334.[3]
- Defensive Improvement: Although Chapter 170 described Lacus as possessing relatively poor defense, continued training corrected the weakness; Chapter 621 explicitly describes her as fast and possessing good defense while identifying explosive killing power as the area she still needed to improve.[2][4]

##### Former Vampire Abilities
- Noble Vampire Physiology (Former as a separate Race): Lacus was originally a Noble Vampire before undergoing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Dragonization. Her Vampire physiology and bloodline power became part of the foundation of her later Blood Dragon existence rather than simply disappearing.[1][5]
- Superhuman Strength: Lacus possessed strength vastly beyond an ordinary Human even before becoming a Dragon.
- Superhuman Speed: Speed was Lacus' outstanding physical characteristic as a Vampire and the attribute around which [[Scathach]] structured her martial training.[2][11]
- Enhanced Reflexes: Her Vampire senses and reactions enabled her to properly use her extreme speed in close combat.
- Enhanced Agility: Lacus possessed exceptional bodily control and mobility.
- Enhanced Durability: Her Noble Vampire body could withstand injuries that would be fatal to Humans.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Lacus possessed the rapid regeneration characteristic of Vampires.
- Enhanced Senses: She possessed the heightened sensory abilities common to her Race.
- Supernatural Longevity: As a Noble Vampire, Lacus already possessed an extraordinarily long lifespan before becoming a Dragon.
- Blood Sustenance: Lacus could use blood as nourishment and supernatural sustenance in accordance with Vampire physiology.
- [[The Mist|Mist]] Bloodline Ability: Lacus' defining Noble Vampire power was her ability to manipulate and transform into mist, distinguishing her from Pepper's Water and the Ice users among the Scarlett family.[1]
- Smoke Manipulation: Her bloodline later demonstrated the ability to generate dark supernatural smoke and construct weapons from it while she was still operating through her Vampire-derived powers.[9]
