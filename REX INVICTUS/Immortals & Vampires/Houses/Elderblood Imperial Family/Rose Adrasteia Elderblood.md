### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 325
I lived by the sword... I suffered from the sword. I fought for the sword... So that in the end... I became the sword itself.
—Rose Adrasteia, Chapter 880
Rose Adrasteia Elderblood, formerly Rose Adrasteia and Rose Adrasteia Alucard, is a Blood Dragon, one of the foremost sword-oriented Grandmasters in the [[Elderblood Empire]], a senior commander of Clan Adrasteia, and one of the wives of [[Victor Elderblood]]. Originally a Noble Vampire belonging to the Main Lineage of Clan Adrasteia, Rose served as the commander responsible for maintaining [[WarFall]] whenever its Countess, Eleonor Adrasteia, was absent. Having devoted over sixteen centuries to defending [[Nightingale]] against Monsters and refining her Martial Art, she became widely recognized as the Sword of Clan Adrasteia, earning her reputation through exceptional technical mastery rather than relying exclusively upon the supernatural characteristics of her bloodline.[3][4]
Rose belongs to the Direct Bloodline of the First Adrasteia, a Dragon Rider whose descendants inherited dormant Draconic characteristics. Despite belonging to the same Main Lineage as Eleonor and being considerably older than her, Rose originally failed to awaken the ancestral Dragon Blood required to become the Clan Leader. Rather than allowing this limitation to destroy her ambitions, she redirected her frustration into swordsmanship, eventually developing a Martial Art so advanced that her blade could impose supernatural cutting effects beyond ordinary physical technique. Her dormant heritage subsequently awakened through her intimate relationship with Victor, before her later transformation into a Blood Dragon provided an entirely new physical foundation for the Martial Art she had cultivated throughout her life.[5][2][6]
Rose's greatest development occurred during the attack upon Nightingale by the Elder Gods. Having become a True Dragon, she discovered that her body, Soul and existence could embody the qualities of a sword without requiring an external weapon. Her attacks could sever the environment through ordinary gestures, produce enormous invisible slashes and prevent the regeneration of Monsters previously capable of recovering from conventional injuries. She also demonstrated the ability to manifest a translucent sword from her own Soul. The narration placed her at the pinnacle of conventional swordsmanship and emphasized that her specialization had advanced beyond the breadth of [[Scathach]]'s training in that particular discipline, although this comparison did not establish superiority in total combat power.[7]
Following Victor's ascension and the expansion of his Empire, Rose continued developing as both a martial instructor and an Imperial military commander. She became an alternate commander of the Fifth Legion, the Imperial Guard, and participated alongside [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood and [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] in establishing the First Legion, the Spartan Legion. Rose and Scathach personally trained its warriors, while Rose could assume command of the Legion in place of John when circumstances required it. Her responsibilities consequently expanded from defending a single fortress against Monsters to helping train and command the military forces of a Galactic civilization.[8]

### Appearance
Rose Adrasteia.
Rose is a tall, physically fit woman with pale skin, long burgundy hair extending to approximately her buttocks, and matching burgundy eyes. Her expression is generally composed and serious, reflecting her extensive military experience and disciplined temperament. At her debut, she wore black full-body armor appropriate for the commander of Clan Adrasteia's forces, whose members traditionally favored practical equipment suitable for constant warfare rather than the elaborate aristocratic clothing associated with other Noble Vampire territories.[3]
Her appearance reflects the martial culture of Warfall, a fortress settlement in which even ordinary inhabitants and merchants frequently carried weapons or wore protective equipment. Rose's physique was cultivated through centuries of training, and her identity as a swordswoman remained apparent in her posture and controlled movements. Following her Draconic evolution, she acquired a much larger and more powerful True Dragon body capable of supporting techniques that her former Vampire physiology could not fully withstand. During later Imperial operations, Rose and Eleonor appeared as towering Dragon Goddesses exceeding four meters in height, dressed in armor that left their heads exposed, while the Valkyries accompanying them had also become physically larger than in earlier periods.[3][7][9]
Rose's Draconic transformation was especially important because of the relationship between her physiology and her Martial Art. Whereas most warriors utilize their bodies to support a weapon, Rose developed toward a condition in which her own body could serve as the weapon itself. The durability, strength and supernatural structure of a True Dragon allowed her to embody the sword without being restricted by the physical limitations that had previously prevented her from fully realizing this ambition.[7]

### Personality
Rose is disciplined, composed, responsible and exceptionally dedicated to her chosen path. Her personality was shaped by more than sixteen centuries of military service in Warfall, where the constant threat of Monster attacks demanded vigilance, decisiveness and the ability to remain calm under overwhelming pressure. She approaches dangerous situations with a practical mentality, prioritizing the survival and effectiveness of her forces rather than becoming distracted by unnecessary displays of pride. Her experience as a commander also makes her capable of recognizing when a situation requires direct intervention, delegation or cooperation with individuals possessing different specializations.[3]
Her defining characteristic is her almost absolute dedication to swordsmanship. Rose originally possessed the potential to inherit the Main Lineage's dormant Dragon Blood, but her inability to awaken it prevented her from becoming Clan Leader despite her seniority and competence. The restriction became a source of frustration during her earlier life, particularly because the title was reserved for those capable of manifesting the ancestral heritage. Instead of remaining consumed by resentment, Rose converted those emotions into determination and devoted herself to mastering the sword. Over the centuries, this dedication ceased being merely an alternative to the power she lacked and became the foundation of her personal identity. By the time her dormant Dragon Blood awakened, she had long since accepted her position and no longer regarded obtaining the appearance or privileges of the Main Lineage as necessary to define her worth.[2]
Unlike warriors who continually seek new supernatural abilities, Rose considers technical perfection to be the most important element of combat. She values efficient movement, precision, control and understanding over excessive displays of destructive power. This philosophy remained unchanged after she became a Dragon. Although her evolution granted new supernatural capabilities, Rose regarded the development of her swordsmanship as the most significant consequence of the transformation. She had spent centuries refining techniques her former body could not completely accommodate, making the acquisition of a superior Draconic physique far more meaningful to her than simply receiving additional abilities.[7]
Rose nevertheless possesses a passionate and intensely competitive side beneath her composed exterior. She enjoys confronting opponents capable of testing her abilities and recognizes exceptional martial talent even when it appears in individuals considerably younger than herself. Her admiration for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] developed partly because of his natural combat instincts, refusal to retreat from dangerous opponents and willingness to respect the abilities of people whose backgrounds differed from his own. She also respects martial honor, believing that genuine warriors should distinguish between armed enemies and individuals incapable of defending themselves. During the later campaigns involving foreign civilizations, this principle remained important even when Rose and Eleonor made harsh decisions concerning populations connected to their historical enemies.[4][10]
Despite her militaristic background, Rose is capable of considerable warmth and affection toward her family. Her relationship with Eleonor combines the responsibilities of an experienced commander with the emotional closeness of surviving relatives from a nearly extinguished Main Lineage. With Victor, she displays a gentler and more openly affectionate personality, becoming embarrassed by his teasing or the increasingly uninhibited conversations among his wives. Her affection is nevertheless accompanied by the same fierce protectiveness that characterizes the Elderblood family. During the Elder God attack, Victor's sudden disappearance provoked genuine fury from Rose, who abandoned her customary restraint and directed her anger toward the enemies she believed responsible for taking him away.[2][7]
Rose is also a demanding instructor who believes that talent without discipline is insufficient. Her centuries of experience taught her that martial progress depends upon repeated refinement of the fundamentals rather than relying solely upon inherited strength. This mentality became especially apparent after Victor attained Grandmaster-level swordsmanship, when Rose joined [[Scathach]] in recognizing the importance of his breakthrough and subsequently participated in training other members of the family. Even when teaching exceptionally powerful supernatural beings, she expected complete dedication and treated their abilities as potential that still required extensive cultivation.[11][12]

### Synopsis

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Rose made her first appearance in Chapter 325, shortly before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] arrived in Warfall alongside Eleonor and his companions. At the time, she was preparing routine administrative reports inside the Clan's enormous fortress, a responsibility she had performed for more than sixteen centuries. The settlement differed significantly from the aristocratic territories of Clans Snow, Fulger and Scarlett. Warfall functioned as a heavily militarized defensive stronghold, with warriors, merchants and craftsmen organized around the constant threat of Monsters emerging from the unexplored regions of [[Nightingale]]. Clan Adrasteia's economy relied heavily upon materials recovered from slain Monsters, which its craftsmen used to produce advanced equipment and weapons while maintaining considerable secrecy concerning the Clan's actual activities.[3]
As the senior commander serving beneath Eleonor, Rose possessed the authority to organize the fortress's defenses and direct the Valkyries whenever the Countess was absent. She was accompanied by experienced warriors including Dorothy, Alexa, Judith, Martha, Juliet and Anrietha, who had developed a close relationship through their common service. Their conversations revealed both the discipline of the organization and the unusually familiar behavior permitted among its senior members. When Rose received information concerning the approach of a Monster horde led by an Alpha, she reacted with composure rather than alarm, considering the appearance of such enemies a problem her forces were capable of handling. The Valkyries were sufficiently accustomed to fighting dangerous creatures that several eagerly competed for the opportunity to engage the approaching enemies.[3]
The unexpected arrival of Victor disrupted the established routine. His excitement at encountering Warfall's Monsters, combined with his overwhelming predatory presence, drew the attention of the fortress's defenders and its senior commanders. Rose immediately recognized that the newly appointed Fifth Count represented an extremely dangerous individual, although his behavior differed considerably from the aristocratic Vampires she normally encountered. Victor's willingness to engage the Monsters personally despite being unfamiliar with the region established the beginning of his relationship with the warriors of Clan Adrasteia.[3]
Victor subsequently returned to Warfall for an expedition into the Monster-infested territories beyond the Clan's defensive lines. Rose accompanied Eleonor, Victor and the Valkyries, contributing her extensive knowledge of the wilderness and the creatures inhabiting it. The expedition also exposed Victor to the nature of Clan Adrasteia's military organization, whose warriors possessed specialized abilities, transformations and equipment developed through generations of warfare. Unlike ordinary Vampire soldiers, the Valkyries were accustomed to confronting enemies whose physiology and regenerative characteristics required highly specialized methods of extermination.[13][14]
Rose's extraordinary swordsmanship was demonstrated during the confrontation with an Alpha and the powerful creatures accompanying him. As additional Monsters entered the battlefield, including Predators and enormous centipede-like [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]], Rose personally confronted the enemy forces and announced that she would require only three moves to eliminate them. Her first technique, Dawn, produced an apparently simple slash whose effects were registered only after a brief delay. The air, terrain, clouds and Monsters throughout the affected area were severed, with the enemy's body initially failing to react to the cuts before the resulting injuries became visible.[4]
Her second technique, Hesperus, extended the destruction through another portion of the battlefield. The attack produced enormous cutting force, severing numerous enemies and sections of the environment while also bisecting a mountain located several kilometers away. Rose subsequently used Nightfall, a strike so difficult for the Alpha to perceive that he failed to register the sword swing before the remaining creatures were eliminated. The confrontation established Rose's reputation as the Sword of Clan Adrasteia and demonstrated that her greatest advantage lay in the precision and refinement of her techniques rather than an overwhelming reliance upon large quantities of supernatural Energy.[4]
The expedition also created opportunities for Rose and the Valkyries to become better acquainted with Victor. Their attraction toward him was influenced by his combat abilities, confidence and willingness to treat their unusual Monster characteristics as desirable rather than repulsive. This response was particularly meaningful because the members of Clan Adrasteia possessed hybrid physiologies and transformations that many ordinary Vampires would regard as disturbing or monstrous. Victor's reaction demonstrated that he did not share those prejudices, strengthening the personal connection developing between him and the warriors.[15]
Following the revelation of Clan Adrasteia's secret ritual for incorporating Monster traits, Rose suggested that Victor could obtain access to the Clan's techniques by marrying Eleonor. The proposal was intended as a practical solution to the restrictions surrounding the ritual, but Victor rejected the implication that he would pursue a relationship merely to acquire another ability. He became angry at the possibility that Eleonor's emotions might be treated as a resource to exploit, insisting that marriage should not be reduced to an instrument for obtaining power. His reaction impressed Rose and the other Valkyries, reinforcing their perception that Victor's possessive nature was accompanied by genuine loyalty toward individuals he acknowledged as family.[15]

#### Demonic War
During the Demonic War, Rose remained one of the most prominent martial specialists associated with Clan Adrasteia, even when the wider conflict shifted toward [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], [[Diablo]], the Pantheons and the rulers of Hell. Her abilities were specifically referenced during discussions concerning the distinction between ordinary martial mastery and the Grandmaster level, revealing why her swordsmanship could threaten opponents possessing far greater quantities of supernatural power than conventional warriors.[5]
When the principles of Grandmaster-level martial arts were explained, Rose served as a direct example. Kaguya acknowledged that she could not properly perceive Rose's sword movements despite possessing enhanced supernatural senses. [[Aphrodite]] explained that Grandmaster-level martial arts begin applying a Concept through the weapon or technique, making such attacks dangerous even to supernatural beings and Gods. Rose's abilities consequently demonstrated that technical mastery could develop properties beyond ordinary physical speed or strength, although the precise effects still depended upon the wielder's understanding, skill and overall capabilities.[5]
This distinction also established an important comparison with [[Scathach]]. Although Scathach possessed extensive knowledge of numerous martial disciplines, her most advanced specialization remained the spear. Rose had instead devoted nearly all of her efforts to the sword, permitting her to pursue a narrower but exceptionally refined path. Their respective approaches demonstrated that accumulating knowledge of many weapons and achieving the highest possible mastery of one discipline were related but fundamentally different undertakings.[5]

#### King Among Kings
Rose's relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Eleonor eventually led to a major revelation concerning the origins of Clan Adrasteia. During a private conversation, Eleonor explained that the First Adrasteia had been a Dragon Rider, establishing the foundation of the dormant Draconic characteristics inherited by the Clan's Main Lineage. The traditional Monster ritual had been developed to preserve and strengthen the bloodline, while prospective spouses were expected to possess compatible Monster traits so that future descendants would have a greater likelihood of awakening the ancestral Dragon Blood.[2]
Rose confirmed that she belonged to the Direct Bloodline and revealed her relationship with Eleonor. Eleonor's mother had been Rose's niece, making Rose the Countess's great-aunt. Victor was surprised to learn that the woman serving as Eleonor's senior commander was also an older relative from the same Main Lineage. However, the rules of Clan Adrasteia required its leader to awaken the inherited Dragon Blood, and Rose had originally failed to satisfy this condition. Despite possessing the experience and competence necessary to command the Clan, she could not become its Countess while the hereditary requirement remained unfulfilled.[2]
The conversation further revealed that Rose had already undergone an important transformation. Through her intimate relationship with Victor and exposure to his extraordinary Blood Dragon-related characteristics, Rose had awakened the dormant Dragon Blood she previously failed to manifest. Eleonor recognized the change immediately and explained that Rose's appearance would gradually develop characteristics more closely associated with the Main Lineage. For Eleonor, the event represented an emotional victory because the Adrasteia Main Lineage had been devastated throughout its history, leaving the Countess with very few surviving blood relatives. Rose's awakening meant that the ancient heritage had become active within another surviving member of their family.[2]
Rose's reaction was comparatively restrained. Having abandoned the pursuit of the dormant heritage long before, she no longer considered awakening the Dragon Blood necessary for personal fulfillment. The frustration she had experienced earlier in life had already been redirected into swordsmanship, which had become far more important to her than acquiring the appearance or hereditary privileges associated with the Clan's leaders. Nevertheless, she was happy to see Eleonor's excitement and recognized the emotional importance of the awakening to the younger Countess.[2]
The awakening also removed the remaining traditional objections to Rose's marriage with Victor. Eleonor explained that Victor's position as a Dragon Rider and Progenitor, combined with Rose's awakened heritage, satisfied the requirements for their union. Victor nevertheless made clear that he intended to marry Rose and Eleonor regardless of those traditions, since his relationships were not conditional upon the political or biological advantages they might provide. Rose accepted his proposal, formally entering the family that would later become the foundation of the Elderblood dynasty.[2]
As Victor's power continued developing, his family underwent increasingly significant supernatural transformations. Rose eventually became a Blood Dragon, abandoning the limitations of her former Noble Vampire physiology and gaining a body capable of supporting the Martial Art that had defined her existence for centuries. By the events immediately preceding the attack upon [[Nightingale]] by the Elder Gods, her Draconic transformation had already been completed, and she openly recognized that her new body allowed her swordsmanship to function at a level previously impossible for her.[6]
The full consequences of this evolution became apparent during the battle in Warfall. When Victor's presence suddenly disappeared amid the conflict with the Elder Gods, Rose and the other members of his family were overcome by anger and concern. Warfall itself was under attack by immense hordes of enhanced Monsters possessing extraordinary regenerative abilities, while the defenders struggled to determine the origin of the enemy forces. Rose, already an accomplished swordswoman and True Dragon, abandoned her restraint and entered the battlefield with the intention of destroying the creatures threatening her family.[7]
Rather than relying upon the additional elemental capabilities obtained through her Draconic transformation, Rose demonstrated the culmination of her lifelong dedication to the sword. She released the physical weapon in her hand, causing the surrounding Monsters and terrain to be severed into thousands of pieces. She subsequently produced enormous slashes through gestures of her hands, explaining that her body had been forged into a blade and that her Soul had become sharp enough to cut what was invisible. Her attacks prevented the affected Monsters from regenerating, removing one of the principal advantages that had made the enemy army difficult to eliminate.[7]
Rose then manifested a translucent white sword from power originating in her Soul, producing a weapon resembling the one she had previously wielded. She declared that she no longer needed to hold a physical sword because her existence itself had become one. The narration described her swordsmanship as standing at the absolute peak of its conventional development and emphasized that the transformation of her body into a Dragon had made this accomplishment possible. Although the chapter also described her as approaching a further martial threshold, her demonstrated abilities already represented the extreme refinement of a single weapon discipline.[7]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Following the defeat of the Elder Gods and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s subsequent ascension, Rose remained an important member of the expanding Elderblood household. Her evolution into a Blood Dragon represented a fundamental change in her existence, but it did not replace the experience or martial philosophy she had cultivated before becoming one. Instead, the transformation strengthened the foundations of her original path, allowing the techniques developed across centuries of combat to benefit from the superior strength, durability and supernatural characteristics of a True Dragon.[7]
Rose and Eleonor also continued overseeing the affairs associated with Clan Adrasteia and its surviving warriors. The transition into Victor's growing faction presented the Clan with opportunities unavailable during its earlier isolation, including access to Divine technology, improved equipment and allies capable of confronting enemies far beyond the Monsters traditionally inhabiting [[Nightingale]]. Despite the changes, Rose remained closely connected to the Valkyries, whose loyalty to Eleonor and her leadership had been established long before the creation of the [[Elderblood Empire]].[10]
During preparations surrounding the next stage of Victor's Dragon civilization, he visited Eleonor and the Valkyries while accompanied by his daughters. The encounter demonstrated the considerable resentment still felt by the Adrasteias toward the creatures and civilizations responsible for the destruction of their ancestors. Rose and Eleonor, among the last survivors of the ancient Main Lineage, understood these grievances personally, yet their positions as leaders required them to consider more than immediate revenge. The defeated populations connected to their historical enemies were subjected to harsh subjugation rather than indiscriminate extermination, reflecting the distinction between the Valkyries' desire for retaliation and the responsibilities borne by those directing their forces.[10]
Victor reinforced the importance of trusting Eleonor and Rose's judgment, reminding the Valkyries that the two women understood the Clan's history and had repeatedly demonstrated their willingness to protect its interests. He also presented the Valkyries with personally crafted Divine weapons designed to complement their individual fighting styles. The artifacts had been created through his own power and the craftsmanship of [[Hephaestus]], allowing each warrior to utilize equipment capable of developing alongside them. Rose's subordinates received specialized swords, firearms, spears and other weapons suited to their established techniques, considerably improving the capabilities of the surviving Adrasteia forces.[10]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
As [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s influence expanded and the Elderblood household entered a new era, Rose became one of the mothers of the next generation. During Jeanne's lessons concerning the differences between mortal, Divine and Draconic Souls, one of the daughters participating in the class was identified through Rose's name. The gathering also included children associated with numerous other members of Victor's family, demonstrating how the once-small household had developed into an extensive dynasty whose younger generation inherited extraordinary supernatural characteristics.[1]
Rose's importance to Victor's martial development also increased. When he began constructing the [[Seven Arts of the Blood Dragon Primogenitor]], she participated in examining the resulting system alongside [[Scathach]] and other experienced martial artists. Rose immediately recognized that the first level required more than ordinary gravity manipulation, explaining that Victor was distorting Reality itself to create a stable surface upon which he could stand. Her understanding demonstrated that she possessed considerable theoretical knowledge of supernatural [[Martial Arts]] in addition to her own specialized swordsmanship.[16]
Rose also participated in discussions surrounding the restrictions that would govern the transmission of Victor's newly created Martial Art. Because the techniques required the inherent ability to distort Reality and could become extraordinarily dangerous when mastered, Victor's family agreed that access should be carefully controlled. Rose's participation reflected her position as one of the household's principal martial authorities, whose judgment was valued in determining whether advanced techniques could be safely taught to the younger generation.[16]
Victor subsequently began training with Rose, Scathach, Haruna and Morgana while attempting to overcome the limitations preventing his own advancement. During their sparring, Rose and Scathach used their respective weapons to pressure Victor through the extraordinary precision of their attacks. Although Victor possessed vastly greater supernatural power, the two women remained capable of providing meaningful martial challenges because their movements had been refined through centuries of experience and Grandmaster-level understanding.[11]
The training ultimately contributed to Victor's realization that his pursuit of increasingly elaborate abilities had caused him to neglect the perfection of his foundations. By returning to the simplest principles of the greatsword, he crossed the threshold into Grandmaster-level Martial Art. Rose recognized the breakthrough and congratulated him, subsequently joining the others in responding to the enormous damage caused by his newly refined attack. When Victor proposed continuing their training, Rose emphasized that those learning beneath the three Grandmasters should understand the extraordinary opportunity they had received and devote themselves completely to improvement.[12]
Rose's military experience remained equally important during the expansion of Victor's Imperial Domain. When [[Akasha Elderblood]] identified an extraterrestrial civilization possessing several powerful Gods and a mature World Tree, she transferred Rose, Eleonor and the Valkyries to reinforce John and the Imperial forces operating in the region. Their arrival immediately changed the balance of command, with John acknowledging their superior authority and allowing Eleonor to assume leadership of the operation.[9]
Rose and Eleonor demonstrated the advantages of their Draconic senses and extensive military experience by rapidly analyzing the foreign civilization. They recognized that the society's leadership contained numerous arrogant Gods whose behavior had created underlying dissatisfaction, allowing the Empire to approach the conflict through targeted attacks against the ruling powers rather than indiscriminate destruction of the general population. Eleonor assigned Rose responsibility for confronting the enemy's strongest Primordial God, while Eleonor herself intended to pursue the World Tree. The Valkyries and John were assigned to deal with the remaining Divine defenders, demonstrating the confidence both commanders possessed in one another's abilities.[9]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
The accelerated development of the [[Elderblood Empire]] transformed the scale of Rose's responsibilities. As Imperial civilization expanded from a collection of supernatural territories into a Galactic power, the military was reorganized into specialized Legions capable of defending Imperial territory, conducting interstellar invasions and responding to threats beyond the boundaries of the original Sector. Rose's combination of martial mastery and military experience made her particularly valuable during this process because she possessed practical leadership experience developed through centuries of constant warfare.[8]
The Fifth Legion, the Imperial Guard, was created to defend [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], the Imperial Family, the Empire's sacred locations and important individuals associated with Imperial institutions. Its forces consisted primarily of exceptionally skilled elite warriors selected for duties requiring greater personal combat effectiveness than ordinary military formations. [[Nero Elderblood]] and Ophis Elderblood became its principal commanders, while Rose was designated to assume command whenever they were unavailable. This appointment placed her among the senior officers responsible for protecting the most important individuals and institutions within the Empire.[8]
Rose also helped establish the First Legion, the Spartan Legion, alongside [[Aphrodite]] and [[Scathach]]. This organization was designed as the Empire's principal offensive military formation, responsible for invading hostile territory, overcoming planetary and stellar defenses, and conducting large-scale campaigns against external enemies. Scathach and Rose personally trained its warriors, ensuring that the Legion benefited from their extensive experience in [[Martial Arts]] and battlefield discipline. Although John became its principal commander, Rose, Eleonor and Kali could replace him depending upon the requirements of a particular operation.[8]
The Legion's creation represented a significant development in Rose's military career. Her original responsibilities had involved maintaining a single fortress and directing specialized warriors against the Monsters of [[Nightingale]]. The Spartan Legion instead required the training and coordination of enormous military formations capable of confronting advanced interstellar civilizations. Rose's experience remained relevant because the underlying principles of discipline, organization, martial efficiency and command responsibility were applicable regardless of the scale of the conflict.[8]
Rose continued participating in the affairs of the Imperial Family during the later era. When [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]], one of Victor's youngest daughters, was introduced to the household, the child affectionately climbed onto Rose and called her "Mama Muscle". Rose gently corrected the description by explaining that she was toned rather than muscular, before responding warmly when Moira complimented her appearance and hair. The exchange demonstrated a softer aspect of Rose's personality, contrasting the formidable military commander and Grandmaster with the affectionate mother recognized by the younger generation.[17]

### Skills and Abilities
Rose is an exceptionally accomplished supernatural warrior whose abilities derive from her former Adrasteia hybrid physiology, awakened ancestral Dragon Blood, later transformation into a Blood Dragon, and extraordinary mastery of swordsmanship. Although her Draconic evolution greatly increased her physical and supernatural capabilities, the defining characteristic of her combat style remains the Martial Art she developed independently across centuries of training.

#### Blood Dragon Physiology
Rose's transformation into a Blood Dragon replaced the physical limitations of her former Noble Vampire body with the extraordinary capabilities of a True Dragon. Her new physiology was particularly important because it provided the durability and supernatural structure required for her body to function as the physical manifestation of her swordsmanship.[6][7]
- Enhanced Physical Strength: Rose possesses immense physical power derived from her Draconic physiology. Her body can sustain and execute sword techniques that would have placed excessive strain upon the limits of her former Vampire existence.[7]
- Enhanced Durability: Her Dragon body possesses extraordinary resistance to physical damage, allowing Rose to treat her own body as a weapon without being restricted by the limitations of ordinary biological tissue. The durability of her transformation was specifically identified as essential to the realization of her Sword Embodiment.[7]
- Supernatural Speed: Rose demonstrated extraordinary speed long before becoming a Dragon, executing attacks whose effects appeared to be registered only after the sword movement had already occurred. Her later evolution further strengthened the physical foundation supporting her advanced Martial Art.[4][7]
- Enhanced Senses: Rose possesses highly developed Draconic perception capable of evaluating foreign environments and civilizations. During the Imperial campaign in Chapter 1084, she and Eleonor examined an extraterrestrial society through their Dragon senses and quickly understood important aspects of its political and social organization.[9]
- [[Draconic Energy]]: As a True Dragon, Rose possesses the supernatural Energy associated with her Draconic existence. Her transformation provides the foundation for abilities and [[Martial Arts]] that require characteristics unavailable to ordinary Vampires.[6][16]
- Supernatural Longevity: Rose had already lived for more than sixteen centuries before her Draconic evolution. Her subsequent True Dragon existence possesses longevity far beyond ordinary mortal limitations.[3][7]
- Draconic Transformation: Rose acquired a superior Dragon body through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s lineage. During later Imperial operations, her humanoid form was shown as considerably larger than her original appearance, reflecting the physical changes associated with her evolution.[6][9]

#### Adrasteia Bloodline
Rose belongs to the Main Lineage descending from the First Adrasteia, a Dragon Rider whose descendants inherited dormant Dragon Blood. Her original physiology also incorporated the Monster-related characteristics developed through Clan Adrasteia's traditional rituals. Unlike Eleonor, Rose initially failed to awaken her ancestral Draconic heritage, although she subsequently achieved this awakening through her relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] before becoming a Blood Dragon.[2]
- Monster-Hybrid Physiology: Rose formerly possessed the characteristics associated with Clan Adrasteia's Monster bloodline, which was developed through rituals integrating Monster traits into the bodies and Souls of Noble Vampires.[18]
- Enhanced Physical Attributes: Members of Clan Adrasteia possess physical capabilities superior to those of many ordinary Vampires because of their hybrid constitution and the combat-oriented development of their bloodline.[18][13]
- Monster Transformation: The Adrasteia bloodline possesses Monster-related transformation abilities. Rose's original failure to awaken her Dragon Blood prevented her from developing the same Main-Line manifestation as Eleonor, although the dormant heritage subsequently became active.[18][2]
- Specialized Monster-Slaying Energy: Clan Adrasteia possesses an unusual form of Energy associated with its ability to fight and kill [[Nightingale]]'s Monsters, including creatures whose regenerative or immortal characteristics make them difficult for conventional supernatural warriors to eliminate.[18]
- Monster Substance Tolerance: Clan Adrasteia's inhabitants can utilize Monster meat and blood as resources and nourishment despite the harmful effects such substances may possess for other races. This capability forms part of the distinctive hybrid characteristics of its bloodline.[14]
- Dormant Dragon Blood [[Awakening]]: Rose awakened the Draconic heritage inherited from the First Adrasteia after becoming involved with Victor. This development occurred before her later transformation into a Blood Dragon and enabled the ancestral characteristics of her Main Lineage to develop further.[2]

#### Earth and Land Manipulation
- Earth Manipulation: Rose acquired the ability to control the land through her Draconic evolution. The narration identifies this as a characteristic associated with Clan Adrasteia, although Rose considered it secondary to her development as a swordswoman.[7]
- Environmental Manipulation: Rose can affect the surrounding terrain through her sword techniques. Her attack Hesperus severed sections of the battlefield and a mountain located several kilometers away, while her evolved Sword Embodiment allowed her to cut surrounding land without wielding a physical blade.[4][7]
The exact maximum range and versatility of Rose's personal land-manipulation abilities have not been established. Her connection to the Adrasteia bloodline should not be used to attribute every Earth-related technique demonstrated by Eleonor directly to Rose without an independent feat.

#### Swordsmanship and Martial Arts
Rose's foremost ability is her exceptional mastery of the sword. She devoted centuries to refining a single weapon discipline and eventually developed swordsmanship advanced enough to apply supernatural cutting principles through her techniques. Following her Draconic evolution, the relationship between her body, Soul and Martial Art deepened until Rose could perform sword attacks without holding a physical weapon.[5][7]
- Grandmaster Swordsmanship: Rose possesses Grandmaster-level mastery of the sword, placing her among the most technically accomplished martial artists associated with the Elderblood family. Her abilities are based upon extraordinary refinement of fundamental movements rather than simply overwhelming opponents through raw physical power.[5][12]
- Conceptual Swordsmanship: Rose's swordsmanship exemplifies the application of conceptual principles through Martial Art. During the explanation of Grandmaster-level combat, her techniques were specifically referenced as an example of attacks capable of threatening even supernatural beings and Gods.[5]
- Extreme Precision: Rose's sword movements possess extraordinary accuracy and refinement. Kaguya acknowledged that she could not properly perceive the movements of Rose's blade despite her own enhanced supernatural senses.[5]
- High-Speed Sword Attacks: Rose can execute slashes whose effects appear delayed relative to the movement of her weapon. This characteristic was demonstrated during her use of Dawn and Hesperus against the Monsters surrounding Warfall.[4]
- Large-Scale Cutting: Rose's sword techniques can affect enormous sections of the environment. Hesperus severed a mountain several kilometers away, while her later Sword Embodiment produced slashes capable of cutting through extensive areas without an external weapon.[4][7]
- Sword Embodiment: Rose's Draconic evolution allowed her body and existence to embody the qualities of a sword. She can release cutting attacks through her body without requiring a conventional blade, treating her physical form as the instrument through which her Martial Art is expressed.[7]
- Soul-Forged Swordsmanship: Rose's Soul became closely integrated with her Martial Art, allowing her to produce attacks that she described as sufficiently sharp to cut what was invisible. This represents an advanced supernatural application of her swordsmanship rather than proof of unrestricted Soul Destruction against every possible target.[7]
- Soul Weapon Manifestation: Rose can condense translucent white power originating from her own Soul into the form of a sword resembling the physical weapon she previously wielded.[7]
- Weapon Independence: Rose no longer requires a separate physical sword to perform her advanced sword techniques. Her body itself can release slashes, while her Soul can manifest a weapon when desired.[7]
- Regeneration Negation: Rose's evolved attacks prevented Monsters from regenerating after being severed during the Elder God invasion. The feat establishes effectiveness against those creatures' demonstrated regenerative abilities without proving that every conceivable form of immortality or regeneration is automatically negated.[7]
- Advanced Martial Perception: Rose possesses the experience and understanding necessary to recognize the fundamental requirements of complex [[Martial Arts]]. She correctly identified the Reality-distorting principle underlying the first level of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Seven Arts and explained why the techniques required a compatible supernatural physiology.[16]
- Martial Instruction: Rose can teach advanced combat techniques and assess the development of other martial artists. She participated in the training surrounding Victor's Grandmaster breakthrough and later helped [[Scathach]] instruct the warriors of the Spartan Legion.[12][8]

#### Combat Experience and Military Skills
- Master Combatant: Rose possesses more than sixteen centuries of practical combat experience accumulated while defending Warfall and combating the Monsters inhabiting [[Nightingale]]'s dangerous territories.[3]
- Battlefield Analysis: Rose possesses extensive experience evaluating enemy formations, reinforcements and combat capabilities. Her leadership during the Warfall expedition and later Imperial campaigns demonstrates her ability to apply that experience against both monstrous and organized opponents.[4][9]
- Military Command: Rose commanded the forces of Clan Adrasteia whenever Eleonor was absent and later became qualified to assume command of the First and Fifth Imperial Legions.[3][8]
- Strategic Planning: Rose can participate in military operations against advanced civilizations possessing powerful Divine defenders. During the Imperial campaign of Chapter 1084, she and Eleonor coordinated their targets and assigned responsibilities to the Valkyries and John according to the enemy's capabilities.[9]
- Tactical Adaptability: Rose can coordinate with warriors possessing different abilities and combat specializations, allowing her to operate effectively alongside Dragon Goddesses, military officers and specialized Valkyries.[9]
- Military Instruction: Rose personally trained the Spartan Legion alongside [[Scathach]], transferring her knowledge of [[Martial Arts]] and battlefield discipline into the Empire's principal large-scale offensive military formation.[8]
- Leadership and Administration: Rose possesses considerable administrative experience, having maintained the daily operations and reports of Warfall throughout Eleonor's absences for more than sixteen centuries.[3]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
Rose initially encountered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] through his association with Eleonor and his arrival in Warfall. His unusual reaction to the Monsters surrounding the territory and his willingness to confront dangerous creatures immediately attracted her attention, while his exceptional growth and martial talent made him an increasingly interesting individual to the veteran swordswoman. Her attraction intensified during the expedition into the Monster-infested wilderness, where Victor demonstrated his ability to fight alongside the Valkyries without dismissing their experience or treating their unusual physiology with disgust.[3][4]
The development of their relationship was influenced by Victor's refusal to exploit Eleonor's emotions merely to obtain access to Clan Adrasteia's ritual. Rose initially suggested marriage as a practical method of joining the Clan, but Victor reacted angrily to the possibility of using a woman's affection as a bargaining instrument. His response demonstrated that he regarded emotional loyalty as more important than obtaining another supernatural ability, strengthening the Valkyries' respect for him.[15]
Rose later became intimately involved with Victor, and their relationship unexpectedly resulted in the awakening of her dormant Dragon Blood. Despite the enormous biological significance of this development, Victor made clear that his willingness to marry her did not depend upon the awakening. Rose subsequently accepted his proposal and joined his family, eventually becoming a Blood Dragon through his lineage.[2][6]
Their relationship developed around a shared appreciation for [[Martial Arts]] and continuous self-improvement. Rose became one of Victor's most valuable training partners because her extraordinary technical precision could expose weaknesses that raw supernatural power alone might conceal. During his pursuit of Grandmaster-level swordsmanship, she fought beside [[Scathach]] against him and later congratulated him upon successfully crossing the threshold. Victor also respected her judgment sufficiently to involve her in the examination and restriction of the [[Seven Arts of the Blood Dragon Primogenitor]].[16][11][12]
Rose's affection for Victor is accompanied by intense protectiveness. During the Elder God attack, his sudden disappearance caused her to abandon her usual restraint and fight with overwhelming anger against the forces threatening [[Nightingale]]. Their relationship later produced at least one daughter, and Rose continued supporting Victor's expanding civilization through her military and instructional responsibilities.[7][1][8]

#### Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia
Eleonor is Rose's great-niece, since Eleonor's mother was Rose's niece. Both women descend from the Main Lineage of the First Adrasteia, making them among the surviving members of the ancient Dragon Rider bloodline. Despite Rose being considerably older and possessing extensive military experience, she did not become the Countess because she had failed to awaken the Dragon Blood required for Clan leadership.[2]
Rose nevertheless became one of Eleonor's most trusted commanders and assumed responsibility for Warfall whenever the Countess was absent. Their relationship combines familial affection with a well-established military hierarchy, although Eleonor's position as Clan Leader did not prevent Rose from offering advice or directing the Valkyries independently when necessary. Rose's awakening of the dormant Dragon Blood was particularly meaningful to Eleonor, who regarded the restoration of their ancestral heritage as an important development for the devastated Main Lineage.[3][2]
Both women later married [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], became part of the Elderblood family and continued fighting alongside one another. During the expansion of the Imperial Domain, Akasha transferred Rose and Eleonor together to command a campaign against a powerful extraterrestrial civilization. Their cooperation remained effective despite the immense changes in their status, reflecting the experience and mutual trust they had developed throughout their earlier lives in Warfall.[9]

#### Scathach Elderblood Scarlett
Rose and [[Scathach]] possess a relationship founded upon mutual respect for [[Martial Arts]] and the pursuit of combat perfection. Although both are exceptional warriors, their approaches differ substantially. Scathach developed expertise across numerous weapons and fighting disciplines, while Rose concentrated almost exclusively upon the sword, eventually attaining a level of specialization that the narration regarded as surpassing Scathach's own swordsmanship at that particular stage.[5][7]
Their differing approaches do not establish that Rose possesses greater overall combat power than Scathach. The comparison concerns specialization and martial development rather than every aspect of their supernatural abilities. Both later became among [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s principal instructors and training partners, and their shared understanding of combat allowed them to identify weaknesses and developments that other members of the family might overlook.[11][12]
During the Imperial era, the two worked together to train the Spartan Legion. Their responsibility was to transform the Empire's offensive forces into disciplined warriors capable of surviving against powerful interstellar enemies, applying the same principles of refinement and effective combat that had shaped their personal development.[8]

#### The Valkyries
Rose commanded the Valkyries, an elite group of warriors serving Clan Adrasteia and protecting Warfall against the Monsters threatening [[Nightingale]]. Her subordinates included Dorothy, Alexa, Judith, Martha, Juliet and Anrietha, whose different personalities and combat specializations allowed the unit to operate effectively against numerous types of enemies.[3]
Although Rose maintains considerable discipline, she possesses a close relationship with her subordinates and tolerates their competitive personalities provided they fulfill their responsibilities. During the early Warfall incidents, the Valkyries repeatedly argued over opportunities to fight dangerous Monsters, while Rose treated these disagreements as familiar aspects of their behavior. Their loyalty to her remained strong even as the Clan's circumstances changed following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s arrival.[3]
Rose continued leading the Valkyries after becoming a member of the Elderblood family. Victor recognized their importance to both Rose and Eleonor, eventually providing them with personalized Divine weapons crafted to improve their abilities. During the Empire's expansion, they accompanied Rose and Eleonor in campaigns against foreign civilizations, remaining a trusted military unit even after the establishment of the much larger Imperial Legions.[10][9]

#### Children
Rose and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] have at least one daughter who belongs to the younger generation of the Elderblood family. During Jeanne's lessons concerning Divinity and the Soul, a daughter associated with Rose was identified among those attending the class. Her complete personal name and individual abilities have not been established in the cited passage, and she should therefore remain listed as an unnamed daughter until directly identified by the novel.[1]
Rose also shares a maternal relationship with the wider Elderblood household. During the introduction of [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]], the young Dragon Goddess affectionately referred to Rose as "Mama Muscle", an interaction that Rose answered with gentle humor. The exchange illustrated Rose's relationship with the younger generation beyond her formal military responsibilities.[17]
