Leone Luna Elena Elderblood is an Imperial Princess of the [[Elderblood Empire]] and the biological daughter of Victor Elderblood and [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]]. Born into Victor's second-generation Blood Dragon lineage, Leone is a natural True Dragon and Dragon Goddess who also carries the ancient heritage of the [[Lykos Clan]].[2]
Despite being born into one of the most privileged and powerful families in the Sector, Leone is characterized primarily by her desire to become genuinely useful rather than merely relying upon inherited status. Her first major appearance follows her attempt to learn Imperial administration, where she discovers that the education received within the Dream World was only preparation for the far greater responsibility of governing an interstellar civilization. Instead of abandoning the effort, she accepts her lack of experience and continues learning.[2][3] This mentality later extends directly into her combat development: after naturally reaching adulthood and recognizing that several of her sisters remain ahead of her, Leone decides that her long-term benchmark will be [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], one of the greatest martial combatants in the Empire. Leona responds by arranging an entire year of direct training under [[Scathach]].[1]
Later information regarding Victor's descendants further establishes the extraordinary foundation Leone inherited at birth. [[True Dragons]] are described as embodiments of nature with exceptional compatibility toward supernatural systems, while Victor's daughters possess mixed Eldritch and Primordial heritage in addition to their individual maternal inheritances.[4] Like Victor's other natural True Dragon daughters, Leone also possesses inherited knowledge and dormant racial abilities that must still be actively developed through training rather than automatically mastered simply because she was born with them.[5]

### Physical Appearance
Leone strongly resembles her mother, [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]], while displaying the deep-violet eyes characteristic of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s biological daughters. Her natural hair is extremely long and white with black tips, a coloration compared to that of a white tiger, and during her earlier appearance it reaches approximately to her knees. She sometimes binds the end of her hair with a decorative bow composed of violet butterflies, creating a hairstyle that reminds her of the video-game character Tifa.[2]
Her physique is highly athletic. Even while using her younger natural appearance in Chapter 1120, Leone possesses clearly developed musculature and a prominent six-pack. Her preferred clothes are similarly practical and sporty: after completing her Imperial duties she changes from the restrictive formal attire expected of an Imperial Princess into white sneakers, jeans, a black top exposing her abdomen and an additional shirt that leaves her shoulders uncovered. Her style is deliberately inspired by the way Leona dressed when she was younger.[2]
Leone possesses sufficiently advanced metamorphosis to modify her Humanoid body's apparent age, proportions and superficial traits. During her introduction she spends part of the day maintaining an adult form resembling Leona before returning to her genuine younger appearance, and she can also alter her hair color when desired.[2] Victor's daughters are discouraged from excessively using metamorphosis to imitate their mothers because their parents want them to develop distinct personal identities. Leone is particularly tempted by the practice because of her admiration for Leona. By Chapter 1160, however, enough subjective Time has passed that Leone has naturally matured into an adult woman and no longer requires metamorphosis merely to appear grown.[1]
Her deep-violet eyes are supernatural rather than purely cosmetic. All of Victor's biological daughters possess some degree of the perception associated with his eyes, although the initial strength varies from daughter to daughter and must still be developed through training.[6]

### Personality
Leone is ambitious, curious, competitive and strongly family-oriented, combining genuine concern for others with a mischievous streak inherited from the household in which she was raised. She dislikes the idea of remaining permanently sheltered inside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Personal World and wants to contribute directly to the civilization created by her father. Consequently, when Victor finally permits several of his daughters to begin learning Imperial administration, Leone eagerly participates and initially believes that her previous education has prepared her for the work.[2]
The experience quickly corrects that assumption. The Dream World taught Victor's children how to communicate with ordinary people, understand perspectives radically different from their own and solve problems without immediately relying upon overwhelming supernatural power, but administering the [[Elderblood Empire]] requires decisions affecting entire worlds and potentially populations numbering in the millions, billions or trillions. Leone becomes frustrated and overwhelmed, yet refuses to give up after finally receiving the opportunity to help Victor. Her response demonstrates one of her defining characteristics: she is willing to acknowledge that she is inadequate at something without accepting that she must remain inadequate forever.[2]
Her sense of responsibility is closely connected to genuine empathy. During Chapter 1121, Leone admits that the sheer number of lives potentially affected by Imperial decisions frightens her because those lives matter to her. Leona considers this concern a positive quality rather than weakness, explaining that caring about the consequences of rulership already distinguishes Leone from many powerful Gods who treat lesser beings as disposable.[3] Leone also possesses an awareness of how privileged her own life is; while spending time with Leona, she spontaneously remarks that she is genuinely blessed by the family, safety and opportunities surrounding her. The comment greatly pleases Leona because Victor's wives deliberately attempted to prevent children born as Imperial Princesses and Dragon Goddesses from growing into entitled rulers incapable of appreciating what they possess.[3]
Leone's serious qualities coexist with a highly mischievous personality. When [[Priscillia Penny Elderblood]] refuses to explain what several sisters are secretly observing, Leone threatens to expose one of Priscillia's secrets to Anna. Priscillia responds by revealing that she possesses embarrassing information concerning Leone as well, causing both sisters to immediately negotiate mutual silence.[2] After Victor notices the girls secretly observing him and silently acknowledges their presence, Leone's embarrassment lasts only until she realizes nobody recorded the moment; she promptly complains that she could have traded such a recording to Leona for favors. Her sisters appropriately describe the idea as extremely capitalistic.[2]
She is deeply affectionate toward both of her parents but sometimes becomes embarrassed about displaying that affection openly. Her admiration for Leona is particularly obvious through her clothing, metamorphosis and desire to imitate aspects of her mother's appearance, while her bias toward Victor affects the way she evaluates other people. When Leone and [[Mirabelle Elizabeth Elderblood]] encounter Victor's Human-era friends, both initially struggle to understand why comparatively weak individuals such as [[Andrew Walter]], [[Edward Jonathan Elizabeth Lykos]] and [[Frederick Winter]] are so important to him. Leone ultimately reasons that whatever Victor values about them does not necessarily need to be combat power, demonstrating that although she shares her sisters' extreme respect for strength, she is capable of recognizing qualities outside it.[6]
By Chapter 1160, her competitive nature has become increasingly focused upon martial improvement. Leone openly admits that several sisters remain stronger than her and chooses [[Scathach]] as the standard she eventually wants to reach. Her motivation is not simply jealousy or a desire for status; she wants sufficient strength to protect the people she loves. Leone understands that Scathach's danger comes from accumulated skill, warfare experience, instinct and relentless refinement rather than raw Energy alone, yet still refuses to lower her objective.[1] Her confidence becomes considerably less absolute once Scathach actually appears in Divine armor to begin the promised training.

### Name
Leone's canonical name is Leone Luna Elena Elderblood. Each of her three personal names originates from a different member of her maternal or immediate family.[3]
Leone was chosen by Leona, with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] amusingly noting how closely it resembles Leona's own name. Luna was chosen by Victor Elderblood because of Leona's Werewolf heritage and the traditional association between [[Werewolves]] and the Moon; Leone privately admits that she particularly likes the name because of the way Victor says it. Elena was suggested by [[Adam William Lykos]], who chose it in remembrance of someone important from his past. Rather than discard one of the suggestions, the family retained all three names, something Leone appreciates because each represents the affection of the person who selected it.[3]
Some chapter text and older indexes reverse the middle names as Leone Elena Luna Elderblood. For the MTW Wiki continuity, the corrected canonical order is Leone Luna Elena Elderblood. The separate name Liliana that appeared in later extracted material in connection with Victor and Leona's daughter is likewise treated as a continuity error rather than an additional daughter or an alias of Leone.

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Leone makes her first major appearance during the period in which [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s rapidly expanding Empire begins preparing members of the second generation for genuine responsibility. Having already spent extensive subjective Time inside the Dream World, Leone and several of her sisters request permission to begin helping govern the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Their previous education had deliberately placed them in environments where they were forced to communicate with people possessing vastly less power than themselves, develop ordinary social understanding and solve problems without simply overpowering everything around them. Leone therefore begins her administrative education confident that she is ready, only to discover that the experience barely prepares her for the scale of Imperial government. Victor's civilization plans on Galactic scales, continuously absorbs new Solar Systems and contains populations large enough that seemingly minor administrative decisions can affect millions, billions or eventually trillions of inhabitants.[2]
The experience exhausts Leone, but she refuses to abandon the opportunity after Victor finally accepted the daughters' request to participate. Once the workday ends she returns to her private quarters, discards the uncomfortable formal clothing of an Imperial Princess and restores her preferred casual appearance. She had also been using metamorphosis to maintain an adult body resembling Leona, but her sisters remind her that the family discourages such excessive imitation. Leone reluctantly returns to her genuine younger appearance, styling her extremely long white-and-black hair with violet butterflies before becoming distracted by several sisters gathered behind a concealed barrier.[2]
Her curiosity immediately overrides any desire to rest. After trading threats of mutual embarrassment with [[Priscillia Penny Elderblood]], Leone is allowed to join the concealed group and examines the barrier surrounding them. She initially identifies the phenomenon as ordinary Reality alteration, a technique she considers simple enough that any of the daughters present could reproduce it, but closer inspection reveals that [[Yog-Sothoth]] Elderblood has combined spatial manipulation with Null-related power to produce much more sophisticated concealment. The group is secretly observing Victor, and when Leone questions how Yog could possibly hide something from their father, Yog explains that Victor is allowing it. Victor confirms this shortly afterward by looking directly toward their supposedly hidden position and silently signaling the daughters to remain quiet. Leone is embarrassed for only a moment before becoming frustrated that none of her sisters recorded the interaction, reasoning that Leona would have paid well for it.[2]
Chapter 1121 continues Leone's development away from raw supernatural power and toward the responsibilities associated with being Victor's daughter. Leona personally waits for Leone after her administrative training and asks about her experience. Their conversation turns toward Leone's full name, allowing Leona to explain that Leone, Luna and Elena were selected by different members of the family. Leone responds that she loves all three because they were chosen with affection, prompting an emotional reaction from her mother. While walking through the extraordinary environment available to the Imperial Family, Leone then remarks that she is genuinely blessed. Leona is especially pleased because the Elderblood parents had always feared that children born surrounded by limitless wealth, Divine status and supernatural power could grow incapable of gratitude.[3]
When the discussion returns to administration, Leone admits how difficult she finds the responsibility. The sheer number of lives affected by the Empire makes every serious decision frightening, and she struggles to understand how Victor appears capable of carrying such burdens so naturally. Leona tells her that this concern is exactly why Leone should continue learning: a ruler who genuinely cares about the lives beneath them possesses something many ancient Gods never developed. Leone's early administrative education therefore becomes as much a lesson in accountability as bureaucracy. She learns that overwhelming power does not reduce responsibility for weaker citizens; within Victor's philosophy, greater power produces an even greater obligation to understand the consequences of one's decisions.[3]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
By the 100,000th Imperial Year, Leone has undergone enormous further development and appears alongside the Lykos side of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family during the celebrations surrounding the Imperial parade. She accompanies [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]], [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] and [[Mirabelle Elizabeth Elderblood]] to a gathering attended by several people who knew Victor before his transformation. Leone and Mirabelle quietly examine [[Andrew Walter]], [[Edward Jonathan Elizabeth Lykos]] and [[Frederick Winter]], struggling to reconcile their comparatively modest supernatural strength with the importance Victor places upon them.[6]
The sisters communicate telepathically while using their inherited deep-violet eyes to examine the men. Mirabelle initially suspects that Victor's friends may be concealing their strength, but their perception reveals no hidden power great enough to explain their father's attachment. Leone eventually proposes the more sensible conclusion: if Victor values them, then whatever makes them special may simply have nothing to do with combat strength. Their supposedly private conversation is overheard by Leona and Maya, who silently find the younger generation's extreme bias toward Victor amusing.[6] The scene illustrates how dramatically the standards of Victor's daughters have been distorted by growing up among Dragon Goddesses and cosmic beings while also showing that Leone is capable of reconsidering assumptions based entirely upon power.
Her next major appearance occurs in Chapter 1160 after she has naturally matured into an adult woman. Leone is training directly with Leona inside an accelerated-Time environment, and although her mother praises her progress, she remains dissatisfied with how far she stands below several stronger sisters. Leone decides that she wants to become powerful enough to protect those close to her and names [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] as the minimum benchmark she eventually wants to reach.[1]
Leona immediately explains why that objective is far more difficult than simply increasing Leone's Energy. [[Scathach]] has accumulated thousands upon thousands of years of martial development, warfare experience and technical refinement while continuously training despite already occupying the highest levels of their civilization. Leone nevertheless refuses to abandon the goal. Instead of discouraging her, Leona contacts Scathach and explains her daughter's ambition. Scathach enthusiastically agrees to personally train Leone for an entire year, arriving in Divine armor with enough enthusiasm that Leone immediately realizes she may have underestimated what she requested. Leona even creates an orchestra to accompany the dramatic arrival, prompting Leone to wonder why she can hear "Boss music." When Leone attempts to argue that she is not ready, Scathach dismisses the objection and begins her training immediately.[1]
Later explanations concerning Victor's children further clarify the foundation behind Leone's development. As one of Victor's natural True Dragon daughters, she belongs to a race described as an embodiment of nature itself and exceptionally compatible with the powers of existence.[4] Victor's daughters are also stated to possess mixed Eldritch and Primordial heritage, although only particular children develop Divinities explicitly connected to those aspects.[4] Their racial inheritance additionally includes basic knowledge and dormant techniques passed down through Victor's Dragon lineage; these abilities are not equivalent to mastery and must be awakened, practiced and refined through individual effort.[5] Leone's decision to train beneath Leona and Scathach therefore reflects the broader Elderblood distinction between possessing extraordinary potential and actually becoming skilled enough to use it.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Leone's biological father and one of the primary motivations behind her administrative and martial ambitions. Leone does not want to remain permanently protected inside his Personal World while Victor alone carries the responsibilities of an expanding Empire, which is why she eagerly participates when the daughters are finally allowed to begin administrative training.[2] Experiencing even a small part of Victor's workload substantially increases her appreciation for the burden he carries, and Leone remains intensely biased toward him when evaluating other people. Her meeting with Victor's old Human friends demonstrates this clearly: rather than accepting that people valued by her father might simply be ordinary, her first assumption is that they must possess some extraordinary quality she has not yet identified.[6]
Victor chose the Luna portion of her name in reference to the Moon and Leona's Werewolf heritage. Leone is especially attached to the name because she enjoys hearing Victor say it.[3]

#### Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos
[[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]] is Leone's biological mother, role model and one of her principal instructors. Leone resembles her closely enough that she frequently imitates Leona's younger clothing style and, during her earlier development, uses metamorphosis to reproduce aspects of Leona's adult appearance.[2] This admiration extends well beyond appearance. Leona plays an important role in Leone's moral education, encouraging her concern for the ordinary people affected by Imperial government and teaching that responsibility toward weaker citizens is a quality to preserve rather than suppress.[3]
Leona is also responsible for much of Leone's known martial development. By Chapter 1160 she directly trains Leone inside an accelerated-Time chamber. When Leone declares her intention to eventually reach [[Scathach]]'s level, Leona takes the ambition seriously enough to immediately contact Scathach and arrange direct instruction rather than merely warning her that the objective is unrealistic.[1]

#### Scathach Elderblood Scarlett
[[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] becomes Leone's highest known martial benchmark and later her direct instructor. Leone specifically wants to reach [[Scathach]]'s overall level as a warrior rather than merely matching the quantity of Energy she possesses. Leona explains that Scathach's true danger lies in the combination of overwhelming supernatural power with extraordinary martial knowledge, combat instincts and an accumulation of battlefield experience that continues increasing despite tens of thousands of years of training.[1]
After hearing of Leone's ambition, Scathach enthusiastically accepts her for an entire year of individual training. Leone's immediate fear upon seeing Scathach arrive does not change the arrangement; Scathach begins the training before Leone can reconsider the decision.[1]

#### Mirabelle Elizabeth Elderblood
[[Mirabelle Elizabeth Elderblood]] is one of Leone's close sisters from the Lykos branch of the Imperial Family. The two appear together in Chapter 1146 while meeting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Human-era friends. They privately discuss the enormous difference between those men and the standards of power normal to the Elderblood children, eventually using their inherited eyes in an attempt to determine whether Victor's friends are concealing hidden strength.[6]
Although both daughters are strongly biased toward Victor, Leone is more willing than Mirabelle to accept that the qualities their father values in someone may have nothing to do with raw power. Their mental conversation is secretly overheard by Leona and Maya.[6]

#### Priscillia Penny Elderblood
[[Priscillia Penny Elderblood]] shares a mischievous sibling relationship with Leone. When Priscillia refuses to explain what several daughters are secretly watching, Leone threatens to reveal one of Priscillia's secrets to Anna. Priscillia immediately counterattacks with embarrassing information concerning something Leone had searched for in Leona's room, resulting in a mutual truce.[2] Their interaction demonstrates that despite their enormous supernatural abilities and Imperial positions, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters retain many ordinary sibling dynamics.

### Lykos Heritage
Leone is one of the principal descendants connecting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s second generation directly to the ancient [[Lykos Clan]]. Her maternal line descends through [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] and her youngest son [[Adam William Lykos]] to [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]], before combining with Victor's Blood Dragon lineage.

Her heritage is reflected most visibly through her strong resemblance to Leona and the distinctive white hair inherited through the Lykos line, although Victor's Blood Dragon lineage modifies those characteristics through the black coloration at the ends of her hair and the deep-violet Draconic eyes shared by his daughters.[2][6]
Leone should not, however, automatically be assigned every classical Werewolf ability solely because of her ancestry. Any specifically Werewolf-exclusive transformations or abilities require separate evidence before being attributed to her.

### Skills & Abilities
- Blood Dragon / True Dragon Goddess Physiology: Leone was naturally born from [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]] as a second-generation Blood Dragon, True Dragon and Dragon Goddess.[2] Later explanations describe [[True Dragons]] as embodiments of nature itself whose existences possess extraordinary compatibility with supernatural systems and the powers of existence.[4]
- Enhanced Physical Abilities: Leone possesses the extreme strength, speed, durability, endurance and physical development characteristic of Victor's True Dragon descendants. Even during her earlier youthful appearance she already possessed a highly developed athletic body and defined musculature.[2]
- Extreme Stamina: True Dragon Goddesses do not require ordinary mortal necessities such as sleep and food for survival, although they may still eat for pleasure.[7]
- Supernatural Compatibility: True Dragons are extraordinarily compatible with the supernatural powers of existence, allowing their natural racial foundation to harmonize with systems that would normally be much more restrictive to lesser races.[4]
- Inherited Knowledge: Victor's natural True Dragon daughters inherit foundational knowledge and dormant techniques through his established Draconic legacy. This provides a starting foundation rather than automatic mastery; each daughter must still train to awaken and refine what she inherited.[5]
- Dragon Language: Dragon Goddesses possess the basic powers of their race, including the Dragon Language, which allows True Dragons to impose their [[Will Solace|Will]] upon phenomena through Draconic authority. No advanced Leone-specific application has yet been shown.[8]
- [[Draconic Energy]]: As a True Dragon Goddess, Leone possesses the supernatural Energy foundation of Victor's Draconic lineage. Her exact current reserves have not been quantified.
- Natural Growth Potential: Leone belongs to a generation whose potential is considered exceptional even by Primordial standards. Later discussion establishes that almost all of Victor's daughters possess the potential to eventually attract the attention of Primordial Beings and, through sufficient development, potentially approach such states themselves.[5]
- Eldritch and Primordial Heritage: Victor's daughters are stated to possess mixed Eldritch and Primordial-Goddess heritage through their father's evolved existence.[4] This does not mean that Leone currently possesses a named Eldritch or Primordial Divinity; only particular daughters have manifested Concepts specifically associated with those aspects.
- Metamorphosis / Shapeshifting: Leone can alter her Humanoid appearance, apparent age, proportions, hair and other superficial characteristics.[2]
- Apparent Age Alteration: During her earlier development she could maintain an adult body resembling Leona despite her natural body still being physically younger.[2]
- Body Modification: She can rapidly return from her enlarged adult appearance to her natural proportions and can alter superficial traits such as hair color.[2]
- Maternal Appearance Imitation: Leone particularly enjoys using metamorphosis to resemble Leona, although the Elderblood parents discourage excessive imitation so that their daughters develop distinct identities.[2]
- Reality Manipulation: Leone belongs to the group of Elderblood daughters for whom basic alteration of Reality is treated as a comparatively simple technique. When examining the concealment around her sisters in Chapter 1120, she initially assumes it is a Reality-altering barrier because every daughter present is capable of producing that general class of effect.[2]
- Reality-Warping Familiarity: Leone possesses enough practical familiarity with Reality alteration to distinguish a conventional Reality-based concealment technique from [[Yog-Sothoth]]'s more complicated use of Space and Null-related power.[2]
- Limitation: This does not establish that Leone possesses a specialized Divinity of Reality comparable to characters whose personal Concepts directly govern Reality.
- Deep-Violet Draconic Eyes / Supernatural Perception: Like Victor's other biological daughters, Leone inherited supernatural perception through her deep-violet eyes.[6]
- Truth-Piercing Perception: The eyes of Victor's daughters are derived from the same general perceptual characteristic that allows Victor to look beyond ordinary appearances and perceive deeper truths.[6]
- Power Perception: Leone can examine another individual's supernatural strength and determine whether their apparent level corresponds to what she perceives.[6]
- Concealment Analysis: Her perception contributes to her ability to analyze supernatural barriers and distinguish between different forms of manipulation.[2]
- Trainable Ability: The natural strength of these eyes varies between Victor's daughters, and later training determines how far each individual develops the gift.[6]
- Limitation: Leone's eyes are not omniscience and should not be treated as automatically capable of overcoming every superior form of concealment.
- Telepathy / Mental Communication: Leone can communicate silently with members of her family. She and Mirabelle maintain an extended mental conversation during Chapter 1146.[6]
- Privacy Limitation: Their conversation is still perceived by Leona and Maya, demonstrating that the family communication system is not inherently private from sufficiently capable or appropriately connected relatives.[6]
- [[Martial Arts]] Training: Leone has undergone extensive combat education as a daughter of Victor and later focuses heavily upon refining her practical martial ability.[1]
- Training Under Leona: Leona directly supervises Leone's later martial development and trains her inside accelerated-Time environments.[1]
- Accelerated-Time Training: Leone can accumulate far more subjective training than the external chronology alone would suggest because portions of her development occur in environments where Time flows at a radically different rate.[1]
- Direct Training Under [[Scathach]]: Leone is accepted for an entire year of direct individual instruction under [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]].[1]
- Combat Analysis: Leone understands that reaching Scathach's level requires more than accumulating Energy; she must develop technique, experience, instinct and practical martial mastery.[1]
- Imperial Education: Leone received extensive education designed specifically for the Imperial children.[2][3]
- Dream World Education: Her earlier training taught her to interact with ordinary people, understand perspectives outside the Imperial Family and solve problems without simply using overwhelming supernatural abilities.[2]
- Royal Etiquette: Leone understands that an Imperial Princess publicly represents Victor and cannot behave without regard for how her actions reflect upon the Throne.[2]
- Governmental Training: She has begun learning the administrative systems necessary to govern the [[Elderblood Empire]] and understands that decisions made by the Imperial Family can affect enormous populations.[2][3]
- Developing Administrator: Leone is still depicted as a student of government rather than an established master administrator.
- High Intelligence and Learning Capacity: Leone combines the educational resources of the Elderblood dynasty with the inherited mental foundation of Victor's second-generation True Dragons. She is capable of rapidly examining supernatural techniques, reassessing incorrect assumptions and evaluating her own weaknesses rather than treating inherited power as proof of mastery.[2][1]
- Divine Status: Leone is explicitly identified as a Dragon Goddess.[2] Her individual Divine Concept has not yet been named in the currently available material and should not be inferred from her name, Lykos heritage or appearance.

### Training and Growth
Leone's development demonstrates the distinction the Elderblood family makes between extraordinary potential and genuine mastery. She was born as a True Dragon Goddess with extraordinary racial compatibility, inherited knowledge, supernatural perception and access to some of the greatest resources available within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Empire, but none of those advantages are treated as substitutes for personal development.[2][5]
Her earliest known formal development occurs through the Dream World, which focuses upon social understanding and functioning among beings far weaker than the Imperial children. She subsequently enters direct Imperial administrative education and discovers how little even extensive preliminary training prepares her for Galactic-scale government.[2] Later, she shifts increasing attention toward martial development, training under Leona within accelerated Time until naturally reaching adulthood. Her dissatisfaction with the remaining gap between herself and stronger sisters ultimately leads her to establish [[Scathach]] as her benchmark and accept an entire year of direct instruction beneath the Empire's Supreme Commander.[1]
