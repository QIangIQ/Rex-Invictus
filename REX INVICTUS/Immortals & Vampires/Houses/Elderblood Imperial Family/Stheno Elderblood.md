### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 1169
Stheno Elderblood is the daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Roberta Elderblood]], and one of the second-generation members of the Elderblood Clan. She is closely associated with [[Euryale Elderblood]], the daughter of [[Medusa]] Elderblood, with the two having previously been referred to collectively as Roberta and Medusa's twins.[1]
Chapter 1169 clarified that [[Stheno]] and [[Euryale]] are not technically twins. They are two separate daughters born to two separate mothers, but Roberta and Medusa's unusual existence—two distinct women who historically shared the same body—caused their daughters to be treated as twins and to develop extremely similar appearances. Stheno is specifically identified as Roberta's daughter, while Euryale is Medusa's daughter.[2]
Her name was given in honor of Stheno, one of Medusa's deceased sisters.[2]

### Physical Appearance
[[Stheno]] strongly inherited her mother's physical characteristics. She possesses a voluptuous figure, long black hair and the characteristic violet dragon eyes shared by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters.[2]
She and [[Euryale]] look extremely similar despite not being biological twins. Their resemblance is attributed to the extraordinarily close supernatural relationship between Roberta and [[Medusa]], who were separate individuals occupying the same body for much of their existence.[2]

### Personality
[[Stheno]] is affectionate and comfortable around her father. When she and [[Euryale]] approached [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] during the Elderblood family's gathering, both immediately became absorbed in receiving his affection after he patted them on the head, momentarily forgetting why they had approached him in the first place.[2]
Of the pair, Stheno was the one who directly asked Victor for one of his scales, suggesting that she is comfortable requesting unusual materials from her father. She and Euryale also appear to share a somewhat mischievous disposition; Roberta immediately warned them not to do anything foolish with the scales, while [[Medusa]] reinforced the warning. The girls protested that they had no such intention but later hid behind Victor in embarrassment when the adults continued criticizing how much he spoiled them.[2]
Despite their playful behavior, both girls immediately obeyed when Medusa seriously instructed them not to misuse the scales.[2]

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
[[Stheno]]'s existence was first established indirectly during [[Leon Walker]]'s visit to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family. After Leon witnessed the enormous number of Victor's daughters gathering around their father, he asked how many children Victor actually had. [[Anna Elderblood]] attempted to calculate the total using Victor's number of wives and specifically noted that some of the women had produced twins, citing Roberta/Medusa as an example. Anna ultimately abandoned the calculation after estimating that Victor already had more than thirty daughters.[1]
At the time, neither daughter was individually named and the exact nature of their parentage was not explained. They consequently remained known only as Roberta and [[Medusa]]'s unnamed twin daughters.[1]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
[[Stheno]] and [[Euryale]] were finally introduced individually during the Elderblood family's enormous gathering in Chapter 1169. The narration corrected the earlier simplification that they were twins, explaining that the girls had different mothers. Stheno was born to Roberta, while Euryale was born to [[Medusa]]. Their unusual situation resulted from Roberta and Medusa being two different people who had shared the same body, unlike the earlier case of Natashia and Naty, whose circumstances involved a personality eventually developing an independent Soul and form.[2]
The girls had been named Stheno and Euryale in honor of Medusa's deceased sisters. Stheno inherited Roberta's appearance, including her voluptuous physique and long black hair, while Euryale physically resembled Stheno but inherited a more obvious expression of Medusa's supernatural characteristics.[2]
During the gathering, the two approached [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] while calling for their father. Victor affectionately referred to them as his "little snakes" before patting both of them on the head. After briefly becoming distracted by his affection, Stheno remembered their original purpose and asked Victor whether he could give them one of his scales.[2]
Victor manifested the black-and-purple scales of his primary body and removed two of them, giving one to each daughter. Despite their small visible size, the scales were so extraordinarily heavy that Stheno and Euryale visibly struggled to lift them. Victor explained that they had come directly from his main body, whose enormous true form surpassed several galaxies in size. He subsequently infused the scales with violet Energy and altered them so the girls could carry them normally.[2]
Roberta immediately warned her daughters not to do anything foolish with such dangerous material. Medusa then emerged from Roberta's body and reinforced the warning, ordering them not to play with the scales. Stheno and Euryale obediently agreed. The concern was justified by the amount of power contained within even a single scale from Victor's true body, which [[Scathach]] stated possessed enough Energy to sustain an ordinary empire for more than one hundred thousand years.[2]

### Skills & Abilities
- Dragon Physiology: [[Stheno]] belongs to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s second generation of Dragon daughters. Her appearance explicitly includes the violet dragon eyes characteristic of the family. Chapter 1169 does not yet provide a detailed individual demonstration of her racial abilities.[2]
- Enhanced Draconic Physical Characteristics: As one of Victor's Dragon daughters, Stheno possesses a supernatural physiology vastly beyond ordinary species. Her individual limits have not yet been demonstrated, and Chapter 1169 does not provide enough information to assign a specific destructive scale to her.[2]
- Inherited Characteristics — Roberta: Stheno strongly inherited [[Roberta Elderblood]]'s physical traits, including her body type and long black hair. Unlike [[Euryale]], the chapter does not yet identify a specific unique supernatural ability inherited from Stheno's mother.[2]

### Trivia
- [[Stheno]] and [[Euryale Elderblood]] were originally referenced only as the unnamed twins of Roberta/Medusa in Chapter 998.[1]
- Chapter 1169 clarified that the designation "twins" is technically inaccurate. They have different mothers and are paternal half-sisters, although their mothers' shared existence caused the girls to be treated much like twins.[2]
- Stheno is specifically Roberta's biological daughter; [[Euryale]] is specifically [[Medusa]]'s biological daughter.[2]
- She was named after one of Medusa Elderblood's deceased sisters.[2]
- Despite their different maternity, Stheno and Euryale look extremely similar because Roberta and Medusa are extraordinarily closely connected.[2]
- Stheno was the sister who asked [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] for one of his scales.[2]
- Victor gave both girls scales taken directly from his primary body and had to make them lighter before they could comfortably carry them.[2]
