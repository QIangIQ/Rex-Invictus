### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 697
Aline Valefar Elderblood is a former Ice Demon, a current Demonic Ice Blood Dragon, one of the Rulers of Hell, a senior scientist of the [[Elderblood Empire]] and one of the wives of [[Victor Elderblood]]. She is the daughter of the former Sixth-ranked Demon Pillar Duke Valefar and an unnamed Ice Demon, inheriting both the status of an upper-class Demonic lineage and the elemental characteristics of her mother's race.[5]
Aline was originally one of four Commanders nominally serving beneath [[Vepar Elderblood]] and simultaneously the Head of Abaddon's Technological Development Department. Her outward military rank concealed considerably greater importance. Victor recognized her intelligence, administrative ability and technological talent early in his reign over Hell and entrusted her with increasingly sensitive responsibilities until she became the Ruler responsible for major portions of the realm's Soul administration. Her position eventually placed her at least alongside Victor's Generals in practical importance, earning her the unofficial title Fifth Phantom General of Hell.[6][1]
Following the unification of the Biblical and Greek infernal systems, Aline and [[Persephone Elderblood]] became the principal Rulers of Victor's new Hell, with their Authorities adjusted to the merged realm.[7] Her relationship with Victor likewise developed beyond that of ruler and subordinate, with Aline eventually joining his family as one of his wives and transforming into a True Dragon while preserving the Demonic and Ice characteristics of her original existence.[8][2]
Aline's importance continued growing long after Hell ceased to be the greatest civilization under Victor's authority. Alongside [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] and [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]], she became one of the central scientific architects of the Elderblood Empire and a principal figure within its Fourth Legion, [[Stellar Arsenal]]. The trio became responsible for enough of the Empire's major technological and supernatural projects that they were recognized among the greatest contributors to its development.[9][10][11] Her later research extends far beyond conventional science into Galactic weapons, Divine phenomena, Eldritch systems, Conceptual mechanics and attempts to produce technologies capable of threatening beings operating near the foundations of Reality itself.[4][12][13]

### Physical Appearance
Aline is a tall woman standing approximately 182 centimeters, with chocolate-colored skin, long snow-white hair and completely white eyes. Her original Demonic appearance includes a pair of white horns and a white tail, making her coloration unusual even among the varied populations of Hell.[5] The traits originate from her mixed heritage as the daughter of Duke Valefar and an Ice Demon and give her a visually distinctive appearance contrasting strongly with the darker or more monstrous forms common among many ancient Demons.
Her humanoid appearance is generally elegant and controlled rather than overtly monstrous. This mirrors Aline's temperament: despite possessing the physiology and instincts of a high-ranking Demon, she normally presents herself with the restraint of an administrator or scientist rather than attempting to intimidate people through appearance alone.
After entering [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family, Aline underwent the large-scale Draconic evolution applied to many of his wives. Her former Demonic characteristics were not discarded; Victor's Blood Dragon nature incorporated and elevated them, transforming her into a Demonic Ice Blood Dragon and complete True Dragon.[2] Her humanoid form continues to retain recognizable traits from her original appearance, including her snow-white hair, unusual eyes, horns and Demonic heritage, while her fundamental physiology is now Draconic.

### Personality
Aline is highly intelligent, analytical, practical and unusually composed. She tends to approach even supernatural or metaphysical problems as systems that can be observed, understood and improved, allowing her to move naturally between technological engineering, government, Soul administration, weapons research and the increasingly abstract sciences of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Her calm demeanor was evident from her introduction, when she encountered [[Lilith Elderblood]], the historical Mother of Demons, without displaying the overwhelming worship or terror that Lilith expected from ordinary Demons.[5]
Her apparent emotional restraint does not mean Aline lacks enthusiasm. Scientific problems can break through her professional exterior almost immediately. She becomes much more expressive when discussing inventions, research results or opportunities for development and takes obvious pride in systems she has helped construct. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] recognized this intellectual curiosity as one of her defining traits, alongside a combination of intelligence and comparatively compassionate judgment that reminded him in different ways of Ruby and Sasha.[14]
Aline's loyalty to Victor is based upon considerably more than instinctive submission to a stronger Demon King. Victor entrusted her with authority, resources and freedom on a scale few rulers would have granted a subordinate, while repeatedly judging her according to results rather than ancestry or political prestige. Aline responded by becoming one of the most productive administrators in his government and one of the scientific minds responsible for advancing Hell by several technological eras.[1] Their relationship consequently developed from professional loyalty into personal attachment and eventually marriage.
Her own philosophy strongly reflects the meritocratic civilization Victor attempted to establish. Aline believes privileges and opportunities should correspond to contribution rather than simply being granted because someone possesses status or asks for special treatment. When explaining this principle to [[Valeria Alekerth]], she emphasized that people seeking greater benefits under Victor's rule should first demonstrate that they had earned them.[15]
Aline nevertheless remains a Demon in temperament and can display a deliberately mischievous side beneath her professional demeanor. She is capable of teasing, manipulating reactions and enjoying another person's discomfort when the situation is harmless enough to allow it, consciously recognizing that centuries spent functioning as a researcher did not erase the instincts associated with her original race.[15]
Her greatest personal weakness is the same obsession that makes her an exceptional scientist. Aline can become so absorbed in research that ordinary Time and routine cease to matter. Within the later Elderblood household, she, Ruby and Velnorah are all recognized as capable of disappearing into their work if left completely unsupervised. Unlike [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]], however, Aline continues making deliberate time for the family rather than allowing scientific obsession to replace her relationships entirely.[16] This balance becomes increasingly important after she becomes Victor's wife and a mother.
Aline is also notably adaptable. She began as a Demonic engineer managing technological development in Abaddon, became a ruler responsible for Souls and an entire infernal realm, adapted to True Dragonhood and ultimately became a scientist working on problems involving Galactic warfare, Concepts, Eldritch forces and the fundamental rules of the Universe. Rather than being overwhelmed as the scale of Victor's civilization expanded, she repeatedly expanded the scale of her own thinking to match it.

### Synopsis

#### King Among Kings
Aline entered the story during the enormous transformation of [[Biblical Hell]] following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s rise as Demon King. When [[Lilith Elderblood]] awakened and explored the realm she once knew, she discovered a civilization that had changed almost beyond recognition. Cities had expanded, infrastructure had been rebuilt and administrative institutions had replaced much of the disorder associated with the former Demon Kings. Aline represented this new order particularly well: she introduced herself as the daughter of Duke Valefar and an Ice Demon, one of [[Vepar Elderblood]]'s four Commanders and, more importantly, the Head of Abaddon's Technological Development Department.[5]
The department existed because Victor did not intend to rule Hell as merely another overwhelmingly powerful Demon sitting above an undeveloped civilization. Aline oversaw research divided between military and domestic applications, with responsibilities extending from ordinary civilian conveniences to weapons of mass destruction. Her work helped transform technological advancement into a permanent institution rather than something dependent upon isolated inventors or captured artifacts.[5]
Aline soon became directly involved with Victor after his return. Hell's abnormal concentration of Miasma threatened the balance of the realm, while reducing it carelessly could damage the environment and interfere with the conditions necessary for Demonic life. Aline worked with Victor and Vepar to develop a more controlled system capable of reducing the dangerous excess without eliminating the Energy upon which Hell depended.[14] Changes to the Miasma had also reduced the natural birth rate of Demons, leading Aline to develop seven specialized orbs that concentrated and redistributed the Energy through the Seven Hells. The project restored suitable conditions for the creation of new Demons and demonstrated her ability to solve biological, environmental and technological problems through the same systematic approach.[14]
Victor's assessment of Aline rapidly moved beyond viewing her as one of Vepar's useful Commanders. Her intelligence, reliability and ability to manage systems without requiring constant supervision made her suitable for far greater responsibilities. She was secretly elevated into the position of Ruler of Biblical Hell, gaining authority over the realm and becoming involved in the judgment and management of Souls entering its system. Her continued public position beneath Vepar consequently became increasingly misleading: among Victor's infernal leadership, Aline's actual authority placed her at least alongside the major Generals.[6]
The scale of Victor's trust was reflected in the resources assigned directly to her. Aline received the protection of ten Demon Legions and possessed a private room directly beside Victor's own chambers in the Demon King's Castle so she could perform her duties without unnecessary delay.[6] Her control over the infernal system was practical rather than ceremonial. While Victor's forces hunted remnants of [[Diablo]]'s order, Aline used her Ruler Authority to search Hell's Souls and territories and locate Sloth, one of the remaining Seven Deadly Sins.[6]
Her administrative work was accompanied by increasing involvement in the [[Blood God Religion]]. Aline joined Roxanne, [[Vine]], Helena, Kaguya and other members of Victor's faction in developing what had begun as scattered worship into a functioning religious institution with temples, education, clergy and communities extending through Hell, [[Nightingale]] and Earth.[17][18] The religion reflected the same broader philosophy found throughout Victor's civilization: followers were expected to learn useful skills and contribute rather than existing solely to offer worship.
Aline also remained directly involved with the technology supporting Victor's followers. She personally explained to [[Valeria Alekerth]] the advanced staff constructed through Victor, [[Thanatos]] and [[Hephaestus]], including its protections, supernatural functions and compatibility with abilities involving Death, [[Necromancy]] and Demonic summons.[15] The interaction also displayed Aline's belief that privileges within Victor's faction should be earned through contribution rather than granted automatically through proximity to the Emperor.
The structure of Hell changed again when Victor fused the Biblical Hell with the [[Greek Underworld]]. Aline's authority had originally been connected specifically to the Biblical system, while [[Persephone Elderblood]] possessed corresponding authority over the Greek realm. Victor strengthened and restructured both Authorities for the newly created Hell, establishing Aline and [[Persephone]] as its principal Rulers beneath him.[7] Aline immediately focused upon studying the altered mechanics of her position rather than treating the new title primarily as a ceremonial promotion.
Her accomplishments as administrator and scientist became so extensive that the distinction between Commander and General effectively lost meaning. Aline's research programs were credited with advancing Hell's technology by several eras, while Victor gave her enormous freedom to use governmental resources because her results repeatedly justified the expense.[1] Although she remained technically associated with the rank of Commander, the workload and authority she exercised led to her being regarded as the unofficial Fifth Phantom General of Hell.
Her relationship with Victor also changed from the loyalty of a highly trusted subordinate into genuine personal attachment. The King who had recognized her potential and given her virtually unrestricted opportunity to develop eventually became the man she chose to join romantically, placing Aline among the women increasingly integrated into Victor's family before the next stage of their evolution.[1]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Aline's integration into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household became permanent as his family transitioned into an increasingly Draconic dynasty. She was recognized among his wives and participated in the transformations through which Victor elevated compatible members of his family into [[Blood Dragons]].[8] Her Ice Demon nature did not disappear during the process; it was incorporated into the new racial foundation, creating a Demonic Ice Blood Dragon whose True Dragon physiology carried forward the characteristics she had developed throughout her previous life.[2]
True Dragonhood drastically increased the scale of existence Aline could physically and mentally accommodate, but it did not cause her to abandon the responsibilities that had defined her before the transformation. She continued serving as one of Hell's principal administrators alongside [[Persephone]], Lilith, Lily, Helena and the other figures responsible for the infernal system while simultaneously pursuing scientific development.
The scale of that administrative burden became clear when Hell experienced an enormous influx of Souls. Aline and the other Rulers were faced with numbers too large for a system in which every Soul received direct personal judgment. Rather than allowing the process to collapse, they expanded the automated infrastructure already governing the realm.[19][20] Ordinary cases could be categorized through predetermined standards, useful warriors and exceptional individuals separated for appropriate treatment, and only unusual or sensitive Souls forwarded for direct examination by higher authorities.
The crisis represented the culmination of Aline's transformation from a technological department head into an administrator of metaphysical infrastructure. The systems under her responsibility no longer handled only buildings, weapons or Demonic industry; they governed the movement, judgment and destination of Souls belonging to enormous populations.

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
The civilization surrounding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] continued expanding until Aline's original work within Hell represented only a small part of her scientific responsibilities. Thousands of subjective years of development exposed her to technology, [[Magic]], Runes, Divinity, Draconic systems, biological engineering and supernatural phenomena gathered from multiple civilizations. She became increasingly associated with [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] and [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]], forming a scientific group capable of approaching problems through completely different backgrounds while operating with the resources of Victor's growing Empire.[9]
Aline also became a mother during the expansion of the Elderblood dynasty, having an unnamed daughter with Victor. Her role within the household consequently developed beyond that of scientist, Ruler and wife as the second generation of the Elderblood family began growing around the older generation.
As Imperial research became too extensive to remain a loose collection of laboratories, scientific and military institutions were reorganized into dedicated structures. Aline became one of the principal figures within the Fourth Legion, [[Stellar Arsenal]], alongside Ruby and Velnorah.[10] The Legion consolidated the Empire's most dangerous scientific, magical and technological research, with its mandate extending specifically toward the development of weapons capable of remaining relevant against enemies operating at Solar-System scales and beyond. Ruby functioned as its primary Commander, while Velnorah and Aline possessed sufficient authority and expertise to assume command when necessary.[10]
Aline's value to the Empire therefore became substantially broader than the specialties of her original technological department. Her knowledge now contributed to a civilization preparing for interstellar and eventually Galactic warfare, requiring its principal scientists to understand not only conventional engineering but the interactions among supernatural Energy, Divine Artifacts, Runes, Concepts and the physical laws governing large astronomical structures.

#### Those Who Rule Reality
The accelerated era transformed Aline from one of several brilliant Imperial scientists into one of the central architects of a civilization that developed for approximately two hundred thousand subjective years while preparing for contact with the Upper Sectors.[3] The mature Empire formally placed Aline beside Ruby and Velnorah among the principal minds directing [[Stellar Arsenal]], whose projects were increasingly designed around enemies capable of destroying Solar Systems, galaxies and entire regions of Reality.[10]
Her expertise was not limited to offensive technology. When [[Tyche]] Elderblood's developing relationship with Luck and Probability caused her perception to become overwhelmed by the enormous number of possible outcomes visible around her, Aline and Velnorah constructed a specialized blindfold capable of suppressing the excessive information.[21] The device demonstrated how far Aline's engineering had evolved: Imperial technology could now be designed around the senses of a Goddess whose perception interacted directly with probability.
One of the greatest products of the Empire's long preparation was Polaris, a Galaxy-[[Destroyer]]-grade Divine Artifact developed as a gigantic warship capable of manipulating Reality, Space and Time through Destruction-related systems while remaining compatible with Eldritch forces.[4] Its ultimate weapon could channel <END> through [[Fenrir]], [[Typhon]] and Jormungand, producing an attack so extreme that the firing mechanism could not survive its own complete activation. Rather than attempting indefinitely to create a cannon immune to END, the scientists designed replaceable firing systems. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later ordered Aline, Ruby and Velnorah to continue improving Polaris without artificial limitations and brought Pepper into the project specifically because her unconventional creativity might reveal possibilities the more systematic researchers overlooked.[4]
Aline's position among the Empire's greatest researchers became increasingly explicit. Ruby, Velnorah and Aline were collectively regarded as the primary giants of Imperial research and development, with [[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]] later joining the group. The Empire's major projects repeatedly carried their influence, and they were counted among the greatest contributors to its technological development.[11] Their effectiveness had reached the point where the three could theoretically perform enormous portions of the Fourth Legion's research themselves, although they deliberately maintained large scientific institutions because different perspectives could produce ideas even a True Dragon genius might overlook.
This concern was particularly relevant because the Imperial Family no longer possessed an ordinary person's perspective. Aline and the others understood that a civilization designed only by nearly immortal beings capable of surviving planetary destruction could easily overlook problems affecting normal citizens. Stellar Arsenal consequently used researchers, assistants and subordinate specialists not only for efficiency but to provide perspectives its monstrous leaders could no longer naturally reproduce.[11]
Aline remained an active experimental scientist rather than becoming merely an executive overseeing other researchers. When Victor's family obtained the corpse of a massive Star Animal, she immediately joined Ruby and Velnorah in treating the specimen as an unprecedented research opportunity. The trio collected samples from its organs and unusual physiology while studying characteristics such as fur partially integrated with Space and a digestive system containing extraordinary Energy density.[22] Aline's understanding of the creature's biology included recognizing that such organisms naturally consumed stars, further demonstrating how far the subjects of her research had moved beyond anything imaginable during her early life in Hell.
Despite this increasing obsession with cosmic research, Aline continued participating in ordinary family life. Within Victor's household, Ruby, Velnorah and Aline were specifically contrasted with Albedo: all four women could become completely absorbed in research, but Aline and the other two still deliberately returned to their family and maintained relationships outside their laboratories.[16] Without structures such as the household's shared routines and Kaguya's organization, even they could potentially lose track of Time, yet scientific productivity was not accepted as an excuse for abandoning the people they loved.
The birth of Moira Elderblood presented Aline with a phenomenon that directly contradicted much of what she had learned about Divinity. Moira could acquire a Solar Divinity almost immediately after consuming a star and then discard that same Divinity simply because she no longer wished to possess it.[23] Aline reacted with open disbelief because conventional Divine development did not permit such casual acquisition and rejection of Concepts. The incident forced even the Empire's greatest researchers to acknowledge that some existences around Victor were no longer operating according to the supernatural frameworks they had spent millennia studying.
The scientific frontier eventually moved beyond weapons intended merely to destroy large targets. After Velina drew inspiration from the old thought experiment of Schrödinger's cat, she brought an idea to Velnorah that developed into research shared with Aline and Ruby. The group began exploring the theoretical [[Absolute Paradox]], a mechanism intended to force two mutually contradictory states to become simultaneously true rather than directly destroying an opponent.[12] Instead of simply killing a target, such a phenomenon could theoretically force Reality to recognize conditions such as existence and nonexistence, life and death or occurrence and non-occurrence at the same time.
The concept represented an entirely different approach to combating entities protected by the rules of the Universe. A sufficiently advanced form of the weapon could theoretically impose contradictory states upon Space, Time, Concepts or even the Universe itself, forcing Reality into a condition it could not consistently resolve.[12] Victor regarded the idea as important enough to continue the enormous temporal ritual despite his extreme exhaustion, while Aline, Ruby, Velnorah and Velina investigated the conditions necessary to create a stable contradiction.
The difficulty proved far greater than simply assembling enough Energy. Reality actively corrected contradictions created within it, while external forces such as [[Eldritch Energy]] distorted the Universe rather than producing the precise internal paradox the researchers wanted. Aline, Ruby, Velnorah and Velina consequently spent years of subjective research examining how a contradiction might be created, maintained and transferred without the Universe immediately eliminating it.[13]
Their work led to the conclusion that any practical Absolute Paradox would require several components: a genuine core containing incompatible states, an Energy source powerful enough to prevent Reality from instantly correcting the contradiction and a method of imposing that contradiction upon a selected target.[12][13] Even those requirements remained theoretical because the mere creation of such a phenomenon could provoke the Universe's own defensive mechanisms.
Aline's latest work therefore operates at a level almost unrecognizable compared with the technological department she once managed in Abaddon. She began her service under Victor by balancing Hell's Miasma and restoring Demon birth conditions; she now participates in research attempting to understand how the laws of an entire Universe might be manipulated, contradicted or bypassed in preparation for enemies approaching the foundations of Reality itself.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Aline's husband, former King and the person most responsible for creating the conditions in which her talents could fully develop. Their relationship began professionally after Victor conquered and reconstructed Hell. Rather than treating Aline simply according to her formal position beneath Vepar, Victor judged her through competence and rapidly recognized that her intelligence was being underused.[14]
Victor responded by granting Aline authority and resources on a scale unusual even among his highest-ranking servants. She became Ruler of [[Biblical Hell]], controlled major governmental systems, received the protection of ten Demon Legions and obtained enormous freedom to use resources for technological advancement.[6][1] The arrangement functioned because Aline repeatedly justified his trust through results rather than requiring Victor to supervise her constantly.
Their mutual trust gradually developed into romantic attachment. Aline entered Victor's family as one of his wives and underwent a [[Soul Marriage]], tying her existence more deeply to his family. Victor later transformed her into a Blood Dragon alongside the other compatible women who chose to share his Draconic lineage.[8][2]
Victor's continued expansion never reduced Aline to an ornamental Imperial wife. He continued assigning her some of the civilization's most sensitive research, including weapons and systems that many members of the family themselves did not fully understand. Aline likewise remains willing to devote enormous periods of subjective Time to research intended to protect Victor and the Empire, including the later [[Absolute Paradox]] project.[12][13]

#### Ruby Elderblood Scarlett
[[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] is one of Aline's closest scientific collaborators. Both possess powerful analytical minds but originated from very different technical traditions: Ruby developed through Vampire science, [[Magic]] and the teachings available through [[Scathach]]'s family, while Aline's earliest expertise emerged from Demonic technology and Hell's supernatural infrastructure.
Their combined perspectives became increasingly valuable as the Empire began integrating every available scientific and supernatural discipline. Together with Velnorah, they became the central trio associated with major Imperial research and eventually served as leading figures within [[Stellar Arsenal]].[9][10]
By the late Imperial era, the three were considered among the Empire's greatest contributors. They collaborated on projects ranging from cosmic biology and Divine technology to Polaris and attempts to manipulate the underlying structure of Reality itself.[4][11][13]

#### Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II
[[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]] is another of Aline's principal research partners. Velnorah brought knowledge originating from an advanced extraterrestrial civilization into a scientific environment already containing Aline's Demonic engineering and Ruby's supernatural research, making their collaboration one of the primary intellectual foundations of the [[Elderblood Empire]].
Aline and Velnorah worked together directly on specialized equipment such as the blindfold created to restrain [[Tyche]]'s overwhelming perception of probability.[21] Alongside Ruby, they later became senior members of [[Stellar Arsenal]] and were collectively regarded as scientific giants whose work could influence nearly every major Imperial project.[10][11]
Their research partnership ultimately extends into the most dangerous fields pursued by the Empire, including Galaxy-class weapons and attempts to create contradictions capable of affecting Reality at a fundamental level.[4][12][13]

#### Persephone Elderblood
[[Persephone Elderblood]] is Aline's fellow Ruler of Hell. Their original Authorities came from different infernal systems: Aline governed [[Biblical Hell]], while [[Persephone]]'s position was connected to the [[Greek Underworld]]. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] merged those realms, the previous Authorities no longer corresponded cleanly to the new metaphysical structure.
Victor consequently restructured and strengthened both Authorities, establishing Aline and Persephone as Rulers of the unified Hell beneath his supreme authority.[7] They subsequently share responsibility for the realm's administration and the enormous systems governing Souls entering Victor's infernal territories.

#### Vepar Elderblood
[[Vepar Elderblood]] was Aline's formal superior during the early period of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Hell. Aline was publicly classified as one of four Commanders operating beneath Vepar, but this hierarchy increasingly became nominal once Victor made Aline Ruler of the realm.[5][6]
Despite the unusual overlap in their authority, Aline remained integrated into the same infernal government rather than attempting to compete with Vepar for military prestige. Her specialization increasingly shifted toward administration, Souls, research and technological development while Vepar remained associated more directly with the Demonic military hierarchy.

#### Unnamed Daughter
Aline and [[Victor Elderblood]] eventually have an unnamed daughter as part of the enormous second generation of the Elderblood dynasty. The available material has not yet established the girl's canonical name, detailed appearance, Divinities or principal abilities.
Aline's behavior during the later Imperial era nevertheless establishes that becoming a mother did not remove her from scientific work. She remains one of the Empire's busiest researchers while still deliberately making time for her household, a balance specifically contrasted with Albedo's excessive isolation in her laboratories.[16]

### Abilities

#### Skills and Abilities
Aline's current abilities originate from several distinct sources that should not be conflated with one another. Her original existence was that of a high-ranking Ice Demon, after which her body, Soul and racial Essence were reconstructed into a complete Blood Dragon and True Dragon.[1][2][3] Her transformation preserved compatible characteristics of her previous Demonic and Ice Demon nature instead of replacing them with a completely standardized Dragon physiology. She additionally possesses the supernatural Authority associated with her position as a Ruler of Hell, a [[Soul Marriage]] connection with [[Victor Elderblood]], and more than two hundred thousand subjective years of experience as one of the principal scientists of the [[Elderblood Empire]].[4][5][6][7]

##### Blood Dragon and True Dragon Physiology
- Blood Dragon Physiology: Aline is a full Blood Dragon produced through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Primogenitor Essence. [[Blood Dragons]] possess a unified supernatural foundation derived from the complete integration of True Dragon and Noble Vampire characteristics rather than functioning as ordinary hybrids whose racial halves operate independently. In Aline's case, that foundation additionally incorporated the useful characteristics of her former Demonic existence and Ice Demon lineage.[2][3][8]
- True Dragon Physiology: Aline possesses a genuine True Dragon body rather than simply having cosmetic Draconic characteristics. Her physical and spiritual existence was reconstructed into the complete racial state that separates [[True Dragons]] from Dragonoids and other incomplete Draconic species.[2][9]
- True Dragon Soul: Full Blood Dragons possess the spiritual foundation required for genuine True Dragon existence. Aline's Dragonhood consequently exists at the level of her Soul as well as her physical body rather than functioning as an external transformation.[3][9]
- [[Dragon Heart]]: As a full True Dragon, Aline possesses a Dragon Heart, the supernatural internal Energy reactor supporting Draconic physiology. It supplies the enormous output required for [[Draconic Energy]], physical enhancement, regeneration, transformation, Breath attacks and other racial abilities.[10][3]
- Draconic Energy Manipulation: Aline can naturally generate, circulate, contain and utilize Draconic Energy through her True Dragon physiology. This Energy exists alongside compatible supernatural systems preserved from her earlier existence rather than requiring her Demonic nature to be completely erased.[2]
- Enhanced Energy Capacity: True Dragon bodies are specifically adapted to contain enormous concentrations of supernatural Energy. Aline's Dragon Heart, body and Soul therefore support reserves far beyond those possible for ordinary mortal species or her original Demonic physiology.[10][2]
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics: The transformation dramatically elevated Aline's already superhuman Demonic body. Her current physical condition possesses the superior strength, speed, durability, stamina, endurance, agility and reflexes inherent to complete True Dragon physiology.[2]
- Enhanced Strength: Aline possesses the immense physical strength of a genuine True Dragon even though direct combat is not her principal specialization.[2]
- Enhanced Speed: Her Dragon physiology grants supernatural movement and reaction speed vastly exceeding the limitations of ordinary biological races.[2]
- Enhanced Durability: Aline's reconstructed body possesses the exceptional resilience of Draconic flesh, bones, organs and scales.[2]
- Enhanced Stamina and Endurance: A True Dragon body can sustain extraordinary physical and supernatural activity for immense periods without suffering ordinary mortal exhaustion.[2][7]
- Enhanced Agility and Reflexes: Her nervous system, instincts and bodily control operate at a supernatural Draconic level even if she rarely relies upon martial combat.[2]
- Natural Armor: Draconic scales provide Blood Dragons with extremely durable biological protection. Aline can consequently rely upon a body naturally designed to withstand forces that would annihilate conventional organisms.[2]
- Natural Weaponry: Aline's complete Draconic physiology possesses the racial foundation for claws, fangs, horns, scales, wings, a tail and other natural weapons. Her pre-existing Demonic horns and tail were incorporated into the individualized form produced by her conversion rather than simply discarded.[2][8]
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Blood Dragons combine extraordinary Draconic durability with the powerful regenerative characteristics incorporated into Victor's Race. Aline can consequently recover from physical damage far beyond the capabilities of ordinary Demons or mortal species.[2]
- Extreme Longevity / Agelessness: Aline possesses the immense biological lifespan of a True Dragon and no longer develops according to ordinary mortal aging. The Elderblood family's accelerated development demonstrates that mature Blood Dragons can experience enormous subjective periods while remaining physically youthful.[11][6]
- Enhanced Senses: Dragon physiology grants highly developed supernatural senses capable of detecting phenomena beyond ordinary sight, hearing, smell and other mortal sensory limitations.[12]
- Dragon Eyes / Draconic Perception: True Dragons naturally possess an abnormal way of perceiving the world, seeing supernatural phenomena and Energy closer to their underlying nature than conventional beings can. Aline possesses the racial foundation for this perception through her complete Dragon physiology; the exact upper limit of her personal Dragon Eyes has not been separately demonstrated.[12]
- Draconic Instincts: Dragons possess powerful instinctive awareness associated with danger, predation, territory and supernatural perception. Aline inherited the racial foundation for these instincts when her Soul and body became Draconic, although her exceptional self-control and scientific temperament prevent them from defining her normal behavior.[12]
- Fear Inducement: The presence and predatory nature of a sufficiently powerful Dragon can trigger instinctive fear in weaker beings. The effectiveness varies according to the power and existential difference between the Dragon and the observer rather than functioning as an irresistible mental attack.[12]
- Shapeshifting / Metamorphosis: Aline can maintain a Human-like form despite possessing a complete True Dragon body. Draconic physiology is sufficiently adaptable to restructure its external manifestation while retaining the individual's actual racial nature.[2]
- Partial Draconization: Blood Dragons can selectively express portions of their Draconic physiology while remaining predominantly Humanoid, including scales, claws, altered eyes, wings, horns, fangs and other racial structures. Aline has not yet received a detailed catalogue of which traits she prefers to manifest individually.[2]
- True Dragon Form: Aline possesses a genuine full Dragon Form rather than merely growing isolated Draconic characteristics. Its exact size, coloration and complete appearance have not yet been individually described.[2][9]
- Flight: A complete Draconic form possesses natural aerial mobility through its wings and supernatural physiology. Aline therefore possesses the racial foundation for physical Draconic flight.[2]
- Dragon Breath: Dragon Breath is a natural weapon of complete Dragon physiology, allowing enormous quantities of Energy to be concentrated and expelled as an attack. Blood Dragon Breath is individualized according to the user's previous Race, Element, Bloodline, Soul and supernatural affinities; Aline's personal Breath has not yet been described in sufficient detail to establish whether it manifests specifically as Ice, Miasma or another property.[13][8]
- Self-Sustenance: True Dragons do not require conventional food to remain alive. Their bodies can sustain themselves through supernatural Energy drawn from Nature, although Aline can still eat normally for pleasure, culture or family life.[14]
- [[Natural Energy]] Absorption: Aline's Draconic physiology can absorb ambient Energy from Nature and process it as a source of sustenance.[14]
- Reduced Biological Needs: Ordinary biological necessities such as conventional nutrition and the normal exhaustion associated with mortal bodies have become dramatically less relevant to Aline's continued existence.[14][7]
- Creation Affinity: True Dragons possess an unusually deep natural compatibility with the phenomena and Energies belonging to Creation. This compatibility contributes to the flexibility of Aline's current body and allowed her Demonic and elemental characteristics to survive conversion into a Blood Dragon.[2]
- Power Compatibility: Different supernatural characteristics can coexist within Aline's Draconic foundation without requiring one system to completely erase the others.[2]
- Limitations: This affinity is not unrestricted Creation Manipulation and should not be confused with Victor's Progenitor-level authority over Creation.
- Environmental Adaptation: Dragon physiology can gradually develop according to its environment, surrounding Energy and supernatural conditions. This is a long-term racial developmental property rather than Victor's much more extreme reactive Adaptation ability.[10]
- Environmental Enhancement / Life Enrichment: Dragons possess a positive interaction with Creation through which their prolonged presence can gradually enrich the vitality of their surroundings.[15]
- Draconic Language: Aline possesses natural racial compatibility with the supernatural Language of Dragons. Draconic speech carries far more than ordinary verbal meaning and can encode Energy and intention into supernatural commands.[16]
- Speech of Power: Draconic Language can combine Energy + Voice + Intention to impose an effect upon the surrounding world. The scale and complexity of any result depend upon the user's Energy, knowledge, understanding and Runic proficiency rather than the language granting unrestricted Reality Manipulation.[16][17]
- Draconic Runes: Runic ability is a natural racial aptitude of True Dragons. Even comparatively untalented members of a True Dragon-derived Race retain enough compatibility to construct basic Draconic Runes, making Runic interaction part of Aline's racial foundation independently of her considerable scientific intelligence.[18]
- Runic Technology Compatibility: Aline's technological specialization allows her to understand and work with technologies incorporating Runes, supernatural languages and magical instructions. This should be distinguished from claiming that she personally possesses Victor- or [[Scathach]]-level Runemaster proficiency.[19][6]
- Power Assimilation / Racial Trait Preservation: Blood Dragon conversion preserves compatible Bloodlines, racial abilities, mutations and supernatural affinities from the recipient's previous existence. Aline's Demonic and Ice Demon characteristics therefore became part of her Draconic existence instead of vanishing during transformation.[2]
- Adaptive Racial Evolution: Preserved powers can be reconstructed and strengthened by the Blood Dragon foundation rather than remaining mechanically identical to their former versions. This evolutionary property applies to racial development and should not be confused with unrestricted instantaneous adaptation to every attack or phenomenon.[8]
- Racial Individualization: Blood Dragons do not all possess an identical Dragon Form, Element, Breath or set of secondary characteristics. Aline's current nature is consequently individualized around her own Soul, Demonic ancestry, Ice Demon heritage and personal development.[2][8]

##### Demonic Ice Dragon Heritage
Demonic Ice Dragon Heritage: Aline's original nature was not erased when she became a Blood Dragon. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Race specifically preserves compatible characteristics belonging to a recipient's former existence, and Aline is consequently a Demonic Ice Blood Dragon rather than a completely standardized member of the Race.[1][2]
- Demonic Essence: Aline retains characteristics originating from her former high-ranking Demonic existence within her current Draconic racial foundation. Her Demonic Energy affinity, Ice Demon lineage and recognizable physical traits remain part of her identity despite the Blood Dragon Race becoming her dominant physiology.[2]
- Miasma / Demonic Energy Manipulation: Aline possesses a natural relationship with Miasma, the supernatural Energy associated with Demons and Hell. Her understanding of this Energy was sufficiently advanced for her to work directly with Victor and [[Vepar Elderblood]] in regulating Hell's environmental Miasma after Victor's conquest.[20]
- Miasma Generation and Control: High-Level Demons naturally produce and manipulate Miasma. Aline's retained Demonic Essence provides the racial basis for continuing to interact with Demonic Energy after becoming a Blood Dragon.[21][2]
- Corrosive Miasma: Demonic Miasma is naturally harmful to incompatible living beings, capable of causing pain, weakness and environmental corrosion. More advanced variants can become considerably more dangerous. Aline has not yet demonstrated the maximum offensive potency of her personal Miasma after Dragonization.[21]
- Regeneration Suppression: Demonic Miasma can interfere with the regenerative capabilities of other supernatural races. Aline possesses the racial and scientific knowledge required to understand this property, although no dedicated Aline combat technique centered upon regeneration suppression has been named.[21]
- Miasma Regulation: Aline understands the relationship between Miasma concentration, Hell's environment, Demon development and the realm's abnormal conditions well enough to participate in large-scale regulation of the Energy throughout Hell.[20]
- Miasma Concentration Technology: Aline designed seven specialized Orbs capable of concentrating and redistributing Miasma through the Seven Hells of Sinners after changes to the realm caused Demon birth rates to decline. The system restored the environmental conditions necessary for Demon production.[20]
- Ice Demon Heritage / Cryokinetic Affinity: Aline is the daughter of Duke Valefar and an unnamed Ice Demon, and her current Race specifically preserves this heritage as part of her Demonic Ice Dragon existence.[1][2] Her ancestry establishes an innate supernatural affinity with Ice, but the novel has not yet provided enough direct combat demonstrations to catalogue the exact scale, techniques or special properties of Aline's Cryokinesis.
- Elemental Compatibility: Aline's True Dragon body is exceptionally compatible with elemental Energy and can support the Ice affinity inherited from her original Race without rejecting it.[2]
- Unrevealed Ice Dragon Expression: Her individual Dragon Breath, complete Dragon Form and higher elemental applications have not been described in sufficient detail to determine how strongly her Ice Demon heritage altered them.

##### Ruler of Hell Authority
Ruler's Authority: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] secretly appointed Aline as the Ruler of [[Biblical Hell]], granting her considerably more than an administrative title. The office provided a branch of supernatural Skills and Authorities governing Souls, judgment and portions of the infernal system. Aline initially described these abilities as fundamentally different from the powers she had used as a Demon and admitted that she had only begun learning their deeper applications.[22][23]
- Soul Manipulation: Aline's Ruler Authority grants supernatural interaction with Souls as part of the metaphysical infrastructure of Hell. Her demonstrated applications center upon perception, searching, judgment and administration rather than unrestricted offensive Soul destruction.[22][23]
- Soul Judgment: Aline can directly judge Souls that require individual examination instead of relying entirely upon Hell's automated processing system.[22][23]
- Soul Management: She can administer Souls entering Hell and determine how exceptional or problematic cases should be handled within the infernal system.[22]
- Soul Search: Aline can direct her Authority toward a particular Soul and search for it through the infernal system. She successfully used this function while investigating Sloth.[22][23]
- Remote Soul Perception: Soul Search allowed Aline to perceive the desired Soul from a distance even before she had determined the target's exact physical location.[23]
- Soul Identification: Her Authority can distinguish and identify particular Souls among Hell's enormous population rather than requiring physical observation of their bodies.[23]
- Developing Soul Location: Aline believed that sufficiently improving her mastery would allow the Authority to identify the actual location associated with a Soul instead of merely showing the Soul itself. This was a projected higher application rather than an ability she had fully mastered during her earliest demonstrations.[23]
- [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]] Authority: Aline possesses supernatural administrative authority connected to Hell itself and its Soul-processing infrastructure. Her status as Ruler consequently placed her above what her nominal Commander rank suggested and made her one of the most important individuals in Victor's infernal government.[22]
- Infernal System Access: Her Authority allows her to interact with supernatural mechanisms governing Souls and judgment instead of functioning only through conventional bureaucracy. The exact limits of this access have never been presented as unrestricted control over every phenomenon inside Hell.[22][23]
- Enhanced Ruler Authority: The merger of Biblical Hell and the Greek Underworld caused Aline's original domain and [[Persephone Elderblood]]'s corresponding realm to cease existing as independent systems. Victor therefore renewed and strengthened both women's Authorities, formally establishing them as Rulers of the newly created unified Hell.[4]
- Soul-System Administration: Aline later worked with [[Persephone]], Lilith, Lily, Helena and the other infernal administrators when Hell received an enormous influx of Souls, helping redesign the procedures through which Souls were classified, evaluated and distributed.[24][25]
- Automated Soul Processing: Rather than requiring every ordinary Soul to be judged personally, Aline helped expand systems capable of automatically handling conventional cases while reserving unusual, valuable or complicated Souls for direct examination.[24][25]
- Metaphysical Logistics: Her responsibilities involve administration on a scale extending beyond ordinary government, coordinating systems that process the dead of enormous populations.[24][25]
- Ruler-System Restructuring: Victor's later Hell became accepted by The System and increasingly sustained through his own existence as King of Hell and ruler of the Pantheon's afterlife. The older externally necessary Ruler arrangement therefore ceased being indispensable to the realm's survival.[26] This should not be interpreted as proof that every supernatural ability previously granted to Aline vanished; she continued serving as one of Hell's principal administrators, while the precise status of every original System-linked Ruler Skill after the restructuring has not been individually restated.

##### Soul Marriage
[[Soul Marriage]]: Aline is spiritually married to [[Victor Elderblood]], creating a connection between their Souls that extends beyond an ordinary political or biological marriage.[5] The exact manifestations of Victor's different Soul Marriages can vary according to the individuals involved, but several properties of his family-wide spiritual network have been directly established.
- Soul Connection: Aline's Soul is supernaturally connected to Victor rather than existing as a completely isolated spiritual structure.[5][27]
- Soul Anchor / Spiritual Preservation: Victor's Soul Marriages leave a spiritual trace of the wife within his own Soul. This provides an additional anchor protecting the wife's continued spiritual existence beyond reliance upon a single physical body.[27]
- Limitations: The preserved Soul trace should not be described as unconditional instantaneous resurrection. The demonstrated mechanism establishes spiritual preservation and an additional safeguard, but the novel has not stated that every possible form of death automatically causes immediate restoration.[27]
- Emotional Awareness: Victor's mature Soul network allows his wives to perceive significant changes in his emotional state through their spiritual connection.[28]
- Telepathic Communication: Victor can communicate mentally with his wives through the family Soul network, allowing rapid coordination without conventional communication systems.[28]
- Energy Support / Sharing: Wives who cannot personally participate in a sufficiently dangerous confrontation can make their Energy available to Victor through the family's spiritual network, allowing Aline to contribute to a crisis even when direct battle against the enemy would be impractical.[28]

##### Genius Intelligence and Scientific Expertise
Genius Intelligence: Aline is one of the greatest scientific minds within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s civilization. She began as the Head of Abaddon's Technological Development Department while Hell was still being reconstructed, later advanced Hell's technology by several entire eras and ultimately became one of the principal researchers responsible for the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s most dangerous and ambitious projects.[1][29][30][7]
- Scientific Expertise: Aline possesses an extraordinarily broad scientific education developed across enormous periods of subjective Time. Her original department already covered both Domestic and Military technology, while her later research incorporates conventional science, [[Magic]], Runes, supernatural Energy, Souls, biology and technologies originating from extraterrestrial civilizations.[1][30][6]
- Multidisciplinary Research: Aline does not treat supernatural phenomena and conventional science as incompatible disciplines. She operates within an Imperial research culture that combines engineering, Magic, Runecraft, Divine materials, supernatural biology and exotic Energy systems into unified technological projects.[30][6]
- Engineering: Aline can design and implement complex technological systems rather than merely studying theory. The seven Miasma-concentrating Orbs created for Hell are one of her earliest demonstrated large-scale engineering accomplishments.[20]
- Realm-Scale Energy Engineering: Her work on Hell's Miasma required understanding the relationship between Energy concentration, environmental stability, Demon production and the broader operation of an entire supernatural realm.[20]
- Energy-System Analysis: Aline possesses enough knowledge of supernatural Energy generation, storage, transfer and conversion to participate in a civilization whose infrastructure uses Dragon Hearts, Divine Artifacts, magical systems and multiple exotic Energy sources.[30][6]
- Magical and Supernatural Engineering: Aline can understand technologies incorporating Magic, Souls, Miasma, Runes and other supernatural principles. She was capable of explaining the operation and safeguards of the complex staff produced for [[Valeria Alekerth]], whose functions incorporated Death-related power, [[Necromancy]], Demonic summoning, spiritual perception and protective Runes.[19]
- Runic Technology Literacy: Aline is capable of working within scientific systems that integrate Runes into advanced devices and military technologies. This technological familiarity operates alongside her racial access to basic Draconic Runecraft, although no formal advanced Runemaster rank has been assigned to her.[19][18][6]
- Research Administration: Aline can supervise enormous research institutions rather than functioning solely as an individual inventor. She managed Abaddon's technological department before becoming one of the central scientific authorities of a multi-system Empire.[1][30]
- Administrative Genius: Victor simultaneously entrusted Aline with scientific development, governmental resources and metaphysical Soul administration, demonstrating exceptional ability to manage unrelated high-complexity systems without requiring constant supervision.[22][29]
- Military Research and Development: Aline became one of the principal researchers of the Fourth Legion, [[Stellar Arsenal]], which centralizes the Elderblood Empire's most dangerous scientific, magical and technological military research.[6]
- Substitute Legion Command: [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] normally commands Stellar Arsenal, while [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]] or Aline can assume leadership when Ruby is unavailable.[6]
- Advanced Weapons Development: Stellar Arsenal develops weapons intended to remain relevant against enemies capable of operating at Solar-System scales and beyond. As one of its senior leaders, Aline contributes to this level of military engineering.[6]
- Strategic Superweapon Research: Aline's later work involves technologies whose scale vastly exceeds the weapons of mass destruction developed during her early career in Hell.[6][31]
- Forbidden and Restricted Technology: The Fourth Legion handles technologies too destructive, dangerous or strategically sensitive for unrestricted civilian access.[6]
- Biological and Genetic Research: The mature Imperial research system contains specialized biological and genetic programs, and Aline is repeatedly grouped with Ruby and Velnorah among the researchers responsible for the Empire's major developments.[30][6][7]
- Artificial-Life and Clone Research Knowledge: Aline works within Imperial research programs that use clones, artificial personnel and engineered beings as research staff and military infrastructure. She should not be credited with individually creating every Imperial artificial life-form, but she possesses substantial knowledge of the technologies involved.[30][7]
- Temporal Technology Research: Time-related technology falls within Stellar Arsenal's advanced research mandate. Aline's senior position grants her access to and participation in the scientific framework used to study such systems, although no unique personal Time Manipulation ability has been attributed to her.[6]
- Alien Technology Analysis: Stellar Arsenal acquires and studies technologies and scientific principles obtained from extraterrestrial civilizations. Aline's position among the Empire's principal researchers makes analysis and adaptation of such foreign systems part of her professional expertise.[6]
- Technological Integration: Aline specializes in incorporating discoveries from otherwise incompatible fields into functional systems rather than treating each form of Magic, Energy or science as an isolated discipline.[30][6]
- Probability-Perception Suppression Technology: Aline and Velnorah created a specialized blindfold for [[Tyche]] Elderblood after Tyche's relationship with Luck and Probability caused her perception to become overwhelmed by the possible outcomes surrounding everything she observed. The device suppresses or limits that excessive perception enough for Tyche to function normally.[32]
- Galactic-Scale Weapons Engineering: Aline participated with Ruby and Velnorah in the continued development of Polaris, one of the Empire's greatest strategic weapons and a platform designed for conflicts taking place on Galactic scales.[31]
- Polaris Development: Victor specifically directed the principal scientists to continue improving Polaris without unnecessarily limiting what could be achieved, later adding [[Pepper Scarlett Elderblood]] to the project because her unconventional creativity could produce approaches the more systematic researchers might overlook.[31]
- Important Distinction: Polaris' destructive functions, including powers or Energies supplied by other beings, are properties of the weapon and its integrated systems rather than personal supernatural abilities possessed by Aline.
- Xenobiology / Cosmic Organism Research: Aline joined Ruby and Velnorah in dissecting and collecting samples from a gigantic Star Animal, taking material from organs such as its heart, brain, eyes, bone marrow and stomach while studying a creature naturally adapted to consume stars.[33]
- Exotic Anatomy Analysis: The Star Animal's unusual biology included structures capable of holding extreme quantities of Energy and tissues displaying properties unlike conventional planetary organisms. Aline participated directly in the study of these abnormalities.[33]
- Divinity and Conceptual Mechanics Knowledge: Aline possesses enough theoretical understanding of Divinity to immediately recognize when a phenomenon violates the normal process through which Divine Concepts are developed. She reacted with disbelief when [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]] acquired a Solar Divinity and then discarded it almost immediately, because such behavior contradicted conventional Divine development.[34]
- Important Distinction: Expertise concerning Divinity does not itself establish that Aline possesses a personal Divinity or Conceptual Authority.
- Reality-System Analysis: Aline's scientific work eventually expanded from manipulating physical and supernatural Energy into investigating the rules through which Reality resolves contradictory states.[35][36]
- Paradox Engineering: Together with Velnorah, Ruby and [[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]], Aline researches the theoretical [[Absolute Paradox]], an attempt to force mutually incompatible states to become simultaneously valid instead of destroying an enemy through conventional force.[35][36]
- Contradiction Theory: The researchers investigate how states such as existence and nonexistence or other mutually exclusive conditions could theoretically be maintained at the same time without Reality automatically correcting the contradiction.[35][36]
- Universal Law Analysis: Their work requires examining why the Universe automatically repairs internal contradictions and why external forces such as [[Eldritch Energy]] distort native Reality more easily than native forces can directly break its own rules.[36]
- Paradox Stabilization Research: The theoretical project requires a genuine contradiction, sufficient Energy to prevent immediate correction and a mechanism capable of imposing that contradiction upon a selected target.[35][36]
- Primordial Countermeasure Research: The Absolute Paradox project is explicitly motivated by the need to develop technologies capable of threatening enemies near the level of the Primordial Beings rather than leaving Victor as the only member of the family capable of meaningfully confronting such entities.[36]
- Important Distinction: Absolute Paradox remains experimental. Aline does not currently possess unrestricted Paradox Manipulation, Reality Manipulation or the ability to personally force contradictory states upon opponents.
- Analytical Intelligence: Aline can rapidly assess unfamiliar supernatural systems, determine which rules govern them and identify whether an apparent anomaly is technological, biological, energetic, Divine or metaphysical in origin. Her ability to move between radically different disciplines is one of the primary reasons she remains grouped with Ruby and Velnorah as a central architect of Imperial research.[30][7]
- Extreme Research Endurance: Her True Dragon body allows Aline to perform research for extraordinary periods without ordinary biological exhaustion. During work on the Absolute Paradox, she and the other principal scientists continued their research for years of subjective Time without needing conventional sleep.[36]
- One of the Empire's Principal R&D Architects: Aline, Ruby and Velnorah are explicitly treated as the central giants of Elderblood research and development, with Velina later joining them. The Empire's highlighted technological development is repeatedly associated with the work of these scientists, making Aline one of the greatest contributors to the civilization's scientific growth.[7]

#### Former Racial State
Aline's original Demon physiology was superseded when her body and Soul were reconstructed into those of a Blood Dragon. The transformation did not erase every Demonic characteristic; compatible properties were incorporated into her new Race.[2] However, abilities belonging specifically to the old mechanics of Demon existence should not automatically be assumed to function identically after her conversion unless the novel reconfirms them.

##### Demon Physiology
- Demon Physiology: Aline was formerly a high-ranking Demon born from Duke Valefar and an Ice Demon. Her original body possessed the supernatural characteristics common to powerful inhabitants of Hell.[1]
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: Aline possessed strength, speed, durability, stamina, agility and reflexes far exceeding ordinary Humans even before becoming a Blood Dragon.
- Enhanced Senses: Demonic physiology provided supernatural sensory abilities beyond those of ordinary Humans.
- Regeneration: Demons can regenerate injuries to their actual Demonic bodies through their supernatural physiology.
- Dependent [[Immortality]] / Agelessness: Demons do not naturally die from old age and can exist across enormous periods so long as the conditions sustaining their existence remain intact.[37][38]
- Demonic Resurrection: Destroying a Demon's physical manifestation does not necessarily kill it permanently. Under the original infernal system, a destroyed Demon could eventually reform within the Hell from which it originated unless its Soul was destroyed, consumed or its spiritual connection with Hell was severed.[39][40]
- Limitations: This resurrection mechanism belonged to Aline's former Demon state. Her current survival mechanics are based upon Blood Dragon physiology and her [[Soul Marriage]] in addition to whatever Demonic characteristics remained compatible, and the story has not explicitly confirmed that the original infernal resurrection process continues to function identically for her.
- Demonic Possession: Demons can possess compatible Human vessels associated with damned Souls, with stronger Demons requiring correspondingly more suitable vessels. Aline has not received a major personal possession feat, and the continued relevance of this mechanism after becoming a Blood Dragon is unconfirmed.
- Soul Consumption: Demons can consume the Souls of the damned as supernatural nourishment.[41] Aline has not been shown relying upon this ability after her transformation.
- Demonic Flight: Demons possessing functional wings can fly through their natural Demonic physiology. Aline's current Dragon physiology independently provides a superior racial basis for flight.
- Demonic Natural Weaponry: Demonic bodies can possess claws, fangs, wings, horns and prehensile tails capable of functioning as weapons. Several of Aline's recognizable Demonic physical traits remained visible after joining [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family.
- Negative Energy Absorption: Demons possess a natural relationship with the Negative and corruptive Energies of Hell and can draw nourishment or power from suitable negative supernatural sources. Aline's current Blood Dragon body instead supports a far broader energetic foundation.
- Miasma Dependency (Former): Demon birth, growth and continued existence were deeply connected to Hell's Miasma. Aline's work on the realm's Miasma demonstrated how reducing its concentration could directly affect Demon production.[20] Her complete True Dragon physiology is no longer biologically dependent upon the same conditions for survival.

#### Limitations and Unrevealed Abilities
- Aline's exact Dragon Breath has not been individually revealed. Her Ice Demon heritage makes an Ice-related manifestation plausible, but it should not be indexed as confirmed Ice Breath without direct evidence.
- Aline possesses an inherited Ice affinity through her mother and is currently identified as a Demonic Ice Dragon, but the exact techniques, scale and limitations of her personal Cryokinesis remain unrevealed.[1][2]
- Aline possesses the racial aptitude required for Draconic Language and Draconic Runes, but she has not been assigned an advanced Runemaster rank or demonstrated the same level of Runic mastery as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], [[Scathach]] or other dedicated specialists.[18]
- No personal Divinity or Divine Concept has been securely established for Aline Valefar Elderblood. Her extensive knowledge of Divine mechanics should not be converted into an unsupported Divine Authority.[34]
- Aline should not be assigned unrestricted Blood Manipulation, Soul Manipulation, Creation Manipulation, Reality Manipulation, Conceptual Manipulation or Victor's Progenitor abilities merely because she belongs to the Blood Dragon Race.
- Her Soul Manipulation is specifically supported through her Ruler of Hell Authority and should not be expanded into unrelated offensive Soul powers without further evidence.[22][23]
- [[Absolute Paradox]] is a collaborative experimental research project rather than a completed personal ability.[35][36]
- The offensive capabilities of Polaris and other [[Stellar Arsenal]] weapons belong to those technologies and should not be indexed as Aline's innate supernatural powers merely because she participated in their development.[31]
- Aline's Blood Dragon transformation preserved compatible Demonic characteristics, but this does not prove that every former Demon racial mechanic—particularly possession, Soul consumption and infernal resurrection—continues to function unchanged in her current True Dragon state.[2]
