Norabell Elderblood is a recurring character in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]], an Imperial Princess of the [[Elderblood Empire]], and the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Natalia Elderblood née Alioth]]. She is a naturally born Blood Dragon Goddess whose existence combines the Draconic and Eldritch characteristics of her father with Natalia's extraordinary authority over Space and Time. Norabell is explicitly described as having been born a Primordial Goddess of Time and Space, possessing an innate relationship with these fundamental Concepts that distinguishes her even among Victor's exceptionally powerful second-generation daughters.[1][2]
Norabell inherited Natalia's exceptional aptitude for manipulating Space, with her earliest abilities already possessing a potential comparable to her mother's highly developed spatial powers. Initially, this inheritance manifested exclusively through Space, without the temporal Aspect Natalia had cultivated over many years of training. Her powers subsequently evolved to encompass both Time and Space, allowing her to manipulate these principles directly. The Eldritch characteristics inherited from Victor have repeatedly altered the nature of her abilities throughout her development, leaving their ultimate manifestations unknown even to Norabell herself.[1][2]
In addition to her Space-Time authority, Norabell possesses one of the strongest manifestations of Victor's inherited supernatural eyesight among his daughters. Her Dragon Eyes allow her to perceive the underlying truth of Reality, examine the conceptual foundations of supernatural structures, distinguish different levels of dimensional existence and recognize the Divine Concepts supporting them. Victor compares this ability to observing the "source code of Reality", explaining that sufficient understanding of what her Eyes perceive allows Norabell to interfere with dimensional structures that would ordinarily remain inaccessible to conventional supernatural manipulation.[2]
Her exceptional perception complements her maternal inheritance, giving her the means to examine the very structures she attempts to manipulate through Space-Time authority. Norabell can distinguish between ordinary dimensions that depend upon the surrounding Universe's Rules and more complicated existences supported by higher Divine Concepts. She has demonstrated the ability to transfer ownership of an artificial dimension, recognize differences in existential weight and create a stable subordinate dimension. However, her attempts to manipulate structures possessing substantially greater conceptual foundations reveal that her powers remain subject to limitations involving Authority, understanding and the metaphysical nature of the target.[2][3]
Although Norabell's Eyes are exceptionally powerful, they have not reached the level possessed by [[Melida Charlotte Elderblood]], whose inherited ocular abilities are particularly complete and whose supernatural foundation is further distinguished by her existence as [[Merlin]]'s reincarnation. Nevertheless, Norabell remains among the most gifted members of Victor's family in matters involving dimensional perception, conceptual observation and the manipulation of Space-Time.[2]
Despite her extraordinary Divine status, Norabell retains a warm and affectionate personality, especially around Victor. She frequently seeks his attention and guidance, while he affectionately calls her "my little cosmos". Although she becomes embarrassed when he continues treating her like a young girl despite her considerable chronological age, she secretly enjoys his affection. Her willingness to seek assistance when her training becomes difficult also reflects a diligent and intellectually curious personality rather than someone satisfied with relying exclusively upon inherited power.[2]
Norabell's classification as a Primordial Goddess should be distinguished from the completed state of existence associated with the [[Regulators of Existence]]. Although she possesses an extraordinary natural affinity with the Concepts of Time and Space, she has not been established as the universal regulator responsible for maintaining those forces throughout Creation. Her powers remain developing, and her present accomplishments do not establish unrestricted control over all temporal and spatial phenomena or the ability to independently create a fully functioning Universe.[3]

### Physical Appearance
Norabell is an exceptionally beautiful young woman with a slender, graceful figure, long black-and-gold hair and deep violet eyes. Her appearance reflects the extraordinary supernatural beauty characteristic of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s biological daughters, while her distinctive hair coloration and inherited ocular abilities make her immediately recognizable. Unlike several of her more voluptuous sisters, Norabell possesses a comparatively slim physique, although she does not appear particularly concerned about the difference. Her beauty is described alongside her affectionate behavior toward Victor, whom she approaches with the appearance of a mature young woman despite continuing to enjoy the attention normally associated with a much younger child.[2]
Her violet Dragon Eyes are her most significant physical and supernatural characteristic. Although they outwardly resemble the eyes possessed by Victor's other biological descendants, Norabell's inheritance is exceptionally advanced. She can concentrate her vision upon an object and perceive aspects of its existence that ordinary senses cannot detect, including dimensional layers, the Divine Concepts incorporated into its structure and the underlying principles governing its interaction with Reality. This perception allows her to distinguish between supernatural constructs that appear visually similar but possess fundamentally different metaphysical foundations.[2][3]
Norabell's Eyes are particularly effective when examining spatial and dimensional phenomena. During her training with Victor, she recognizes that a miniature galaxy contains more levels of existence than a comparatively simple artificial dimension and later distinguishes between two superficially identical cubes through the different Divine Concepts incorporated into them. Her eyes therefore provide considerably more than enhanced sight, allowing her to observe the conceptual organization and existential properties of supernatural structures. This ability originates from Victor's truth-perceiving inheritance, while her authority over Time and Space primarily reflects Natalia's maternal lineage.[2][3]
As a naturally born True Dragon Goddess, Norabell possesses a supernatural constitution fundamentally different from that of ordinary Humans or conventional Divine beings. Her Humanoid appearance conceals the advanced Draconic physiology inherited from Victor, including the biological and spiritual characteristics associated with genuine [[True Dragons]]. Her actual age also differs considerably from what might be inferred from her appearance, since she has already experienced more than a thousand years of life when she approaches Victor during her named introduction. Nevertheless, her father continues treating her as his little girl, a habit that occasionally embarrasses her despite her genuine enjoyment of his affection.[2]
Norabell's complete Dragon Form has not been individually described in sufficient detail to establish its precise dimensions, scale coloration, anatomical features or other unique physical characteristics. Her confirmed individual appearance consists primarily of her long black-and-gold hair, slender figure and violet eyes, with her broader biological nature established through her membership in Victor's naturally born Blood Dragon lineage.

### Personality
Norabell is affectionate, intelligent, curious, diligent and determined to improve her supernatural abilities. Although she possesses an extraordinarily powerful Divine inheritance and has already accumulated considerable chronological experience, she retains a warm and occasionally childish personality around [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. She openly adores her father and values his approval, becoming visibly pleased when he praises her accomplishments. Her affection is accompanied by a degree of embarrassment because Victor continues treating her like a young girl regardless of her maturity, although she rarely remains annoyed with him for long.[2]
Her relationship with Victor is particularly affectionate. When she approaches him seeking help with dimensional creation, he immediately lifts her into his arms and greets her as "my little cosmos". Norabell blushes and protests that she is already more than a thousand years old, but Victor responds that she will remain his little girl even if she eventually becomes billions of years old. Although she pouts at his response, her reaction is not a genuine rejection of his affection. She enjoys the attention and does not seriously attempt to prevent him from pampering her, revealing that her desire to be treated as a mature individual coexists with the emotional attachment she has maintained since childhood.[2]
Norabell possesses considerable curiosity concerning the fundamental structure of Reality. Her interest in dimensional manipulation extends beyond ordinary spatial movement or the creation of convenient pocket spaces, eventually leading her to experiment with the construction of entire supernatural environments. Her ambition is supported by her extraordinary inherited abilities, but she does not yet possess the knowledge necessary to understand every implication of the techniques she attempts. Rather than abandoning an unfamiliar task when she encounters difficulties, Norabell repeatedly experiments with her powers until she recognizes that the problem requires greater understanding.[2]
Her determination becomes particularly apparent when she attempts to create alternative dimensions. Although she possesses the innate authority of a Primordial Goddess of Time and Space, the cube-shaped structures she produces repeatedly collapse before stabilizing. Norabell finds the failure frustrating because she believes she is attempting a relatively simple application of her existing abilities. However, she does not dismiss the problem or assume that the technique is impossible. Instead, she approaches Victor and openly asks him for assistance, demonstrating an awareness that extraordinary natural talent does not eliminate the need for instruction and deliberate practice.[2]
Norabell is also highly observant and analytical. When Victor places an ordinary dimension beside a miniature galaxy, she concentrates her Eyes upon both objects and immediately recognizes that the galaxy possesses additional levels of existence and a greater number of Divine Concepts. She subsequently distinguishes between the two structures through their different existential weights, recognizing that the ordinary dimension feels comparatively light while the conceptual galaxy feels extraordinarily heavy. Her observations reveal an ability to evaluate supernatural phenomena through their underlying principles rather than relying solely upon their apparent size or physical structure.[2]
Although Norabell is exceptionally talented, she does not react well to every failure. After effortlessly changing the ownership of an ordinary dimension, she becomes irritated when the same technique proves ineffective against Victor's miniature galaxy. She attempts to overcome the difficulty by combining her Space-Time powers with the Dragon Language, but the structure remains beyond her control. Her frustration increases when Victor explains that only a few exceptionally powerful members of the family might possess the necessary authority to accomplish the feat. Nevertheless, she listens carefully when he explains the difference between the structures and gradually recognizes that the limitation originates from the galaxy's conceptual foundation rather than a simple lack of physical strength.[2]
Her willingness to reconsider her assumptions is one of her most important personal qualities. Norabell does not insist that her initial understanding must be correct merely because she was born with extraordinary powers. She recognizes that her failed dimensional constructs were not actually ordinary dimensions, but incomplete attempts at establishing independent realities. Once she understands the distinction, she immediately modifies her approach and successfully creates a stable dimension, demonstrating an ability to apply new knowledge directly to her supernatural development.[3]
Norabell also possesses a strong desire to make Victor proud. His praise visibly improves her mood, and she reacts happily whenever he recognizes her progress. Her gratitude becomes especially evident after she overcomes her difficulties with dimensional creation, immediately embracing him despite having previously complained about being treated like a child. The interaction reflects the close relationship between Victor and his daughters, whose extraordinary age and supernatural status do not diminish their affection for their father.[3]
Despite being born a Primordial Goddess, Norabell remains conscious that her abilities are incomplete. Her half-Eldritch heritage has caused her powers to change repeatedly, and she does not fully understand every characteristic they may eventually develop. This uncertainty encourages continued experimentation and training rather than complacency. Her personality consequently combines the affectionate behavior characteristic of Victor's daughters with an intellectual curiosity particularly suited to the study of Space, Time and the fundamental principles governing Reality.

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Norabell was born during the extensive period of accelerated development experienced by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his family following the establishment of his mature Blood Dragon lineage. As the biological daughter of Victor Elderblood and [[Natalia Elderblood née Alioth]], she inherited an extraordinary combination of Draconic physiology, Divine characteristics and specialized authority over Space. Her existence represented the continuation of Natalia's unusual supernatural inheritance through Victor's naturally born second generation, providing her with a foundation considerably more advanced than that of ordinary Divine beings.
Natalia's own abilities had undergone enormous development following her transformation into a True Dragon. Her preexisting affinity for Space and Time was strengthened by her new Draconic physiology, causing her spatial powers to increase so dramatically that she required extensive training to control them properly. During the approximately two thousand years of accelerated Time experienced by the family, Natalia devoted much of her attention to developing these abilities and spending time with Victor. Her specialization allowed her to cultivate an exceptional relationship with Space-Time, eventually reaching levels of control that exceeded those of many beings possessing broader but less specialized Divine powers.[1]
Natalia's ocular inheritance also contributed to her extraordinary development. The unusual eyes associated with the Alioth lineage had originally been inherited through her father, [[Alexios Alioth]], and possessed characteristics connected to the fundamental principles of Space and Time. Natalia's transformation into a True Dragon allowed this inheritance to develop far beyond its original limitations, giving her exceptional control over spatial phenomena and the temporal Aspect she subsequently cultivated. Victor recognized that Natalia's specialization was extraordinary even among his wives, whose powers extended across numerous Divine Concepts and supernatural systems.[1]
Norabell inherited a similarly remarkable affinity for Space. Even during her earliest development, Victor recognized that his daughter with Natalia possessed spatial powers comparable in potential to her mother's. However, Norabell initially lacked the temporal Aspect that Natalia had acquired through years of training, meaning that her manifested specialization remained restricted to Space. This distinction reflected the different developmental stages of mother and daughter rather than an inability to eventually acquire temporal authority. Norabell was still growing, and her supernatural inheritance possessed considerable potential for further evolution.[1]
Her paternal inheritance also provided an extraordinary advantage through Victor's Dragon Eyes. Whereas Natalia's visual abilities were associated primarily with her specialized Space-Time inheritance, Norabell possessed a particularly powerful form of Victor's truth-perceiving eyesight. This combination eventually allowed her to perceive the deeper structures of Reality while manipulating the fundamental forces governing their spatial and temporal organization. Her Draconic and Eldritch characteristics further complicated her development, causing her abilities to evolve in directions that were not entirely predictable from either parent's powers alone.
Norabell continued developing within the protected territories of the Elderblood family, where Victor and his wives provided extensive education and training for their children. Although the daughters were born with exceptional abilities, their parents encouraged them to acquire practical experience and understanding rather than relying exclusively upon natural strength. This environment allowed Norabell to cultivate her Space-Time powers while gradually developing the perception and conceptual knowledge necessary for more complicated applications.

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Norabell's supernatural inheritance continued evolving as the [[Elderblood Empire]] underwent enormous periods of accelerated development. The temporal Aspect absent from her earlier powers eventually manifested, allowing her to manipulate both Time and Space directly. Her nature as a naturally born Primordial Goddess of Time and Space became increasingly apparent, while the Eldritch characteristics inherited from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] repeatedly altered the expression of her abilities. These changes made her powers unusually difficult to classify because she possessed innate authority over fundamental Concepts without yet understanding every manifestation of that authority.[2]
Her Dragon Eyes also developed into one of the strongest manifestations of Victor's truth-perceiving inheritance among the second-generation daughters. Although Melida remained superior in this particular field, Norabell possessed the ability to examine the underlying structure of Reality and perceive distinctions between different forms of dimensional existence. Her exceptional perception complemented her Space-Time powers, allowing her to investigate increasingly advanced applications rather than remaining restricted to conventional spatial manipulation.[2]
Norabell eventually approached Victor while he was spending time with several daughters, including Ophis, [[Nero]], Melida, [[Metis]] and Akasha. The gathering began with Ophis assuming a smaller appearance and climbing onto Victor's shoulder, allowing her to enjoy the affectionate treatment she remembered from childhood. Her behavior immediately attracted the attention of the other daughters, many of whom considered using their own transformative abilities to obtain similar affection. Norabell subsequently arrived with a problem concerning her supernatural training, addressing Victor affectionately as Papa before asking whether he could assist her.[2]
Victor immediately greeted her as "my little cosmos" and lifted her into his arms. Norabell became embarrassed and protested that she was already more than a thousand years old, but Victor insisted that she would remain his little girl regardless of how much time passed. Although she pouted at the response, she accepted his affection and began explaining her difficulty. She had been attempting to create alternative dimensions but could not stabilize the structures formed through her powers, despite possessing an extraordinary natural authority over both Time and Space.[2]
Norabell demonstrated the problem by attempting to form a cube-shaped dimension in her hand. The construct appeared briefly before collapsing, leaving her frustrated because she believed the technique should be relatively simple. Victor examined her attempt and immediately recognized that she was trying to accomplish something much more complicated than ordinary dimensional creation. Rather than forming an alternative space supported by the existing Universe, Norabell was unconsciously attempting to establish an independent Reality possessing its own fundamental Time-Space framework. Her powers were sufficient to produce portions of the structure, but she lacked the additional conceptual foundations necessary to stabilize it.[2]
Victor was impressed that Norabell had already developed enough to attempt such an advanced application. Instead of simply correcting her mistake, he decided to teach the gathered daughters how dimensional structures functioned and why superficially similar existences could possess fundamentally different levels of metaphysical complexity. He explained that an ordinary subordinate dimension and a cosmic structure incorporating higher Divine Concepts were not equivalent creations, since the latter required additional principles to establish its existence independently.[2]
To demonstrate the difference, Victor combined several of his powers and used Negativity and Beginning as conceptual anchors while constructing a miniature galaxy in one hand. In his other hand, he formed a comparatively simple cube-shaped dimension. The daughters were astonished by the ease with which he produced both structures, particularly because the miniature galaxy appeared to contain its own stars and cosmic phenomena despite fitting within his hand. Victor asked them to examine the objects and identify their differences, but prevented Melida and Isabelle from answering because their exceptional perception would make the exercise too easy.[2]
Norabell concentrated her Eyes upon the constructs and immediately recognized that the miniature galaxy possessed more levels of existence than the cube. She also identified a greater number of Divine Concepts incorporated into the galaxy's foundation, earning Victor's approval. Her observation demonstrated that the structures differed in more than their visible size or complexity, since their underlying conceptual composition directly affected their existence and the difficulty of manipulating them.[2]
Victor explained that ordinary alternative dimensions could be constructed by taking advantage of the Rules already established within the surrounding Universe. By supplying the necessary Energy and organizing an appropriate spatial structure, the creator could establish an additional dimension without independently producing every fundamental law required for its existence. However, this convenience meant that the resulting dimension remained subordinate to the greater Reality supporting it. A sufficiently powerful individual capable of perceiving and manipulating those underlying Rules could therefore interfere with the dimension more easily than with a structure possessing a stronger independent conceptual foundation.[2]
Norabell's inherited Eyes made her particularly suited to this kind of interference because she could perceive the deeper truth of dimensional structures. Victor extended the ordinary cube-shaped dimension toward her and instructed her to change its ownership. Norabell obeyed and effortlessly transferred its governing authority from Victor to herself, demonstrating that her powers could alter more than the physical shape or position of a dimensional structure. The transfer succeeded because Victor deliberately allowed it, but the exercise nevertheless established her ability to interfere with the authority governing an ordinary subordinate dimension.[2]
Victor then presented the miniature galaxy and challenged her to accomplish the same feat, promising not to interfere with the attempt. Norabell concentrated her Space-Time powers upon the structure and reinforced her efforts through the Dragon Language. Her exertion was significant enough that Reality briefly trembled, requiring Victor to stabilize the surrounding environment with a thought. Nevertheless, the galaxy remained beyond her control, and she could not transfer its authority or remove it from Victor's possession.[2]
Norabell became frustrated because she had successfully manipulated the ordinary dimension moments earlier. Victor reassured her that the second task was far beyond what he expected her to accomplish at her present level of development, noting that only a very small number of exceptionally powerful individuals within the family might possess the necessary authority to overcome such a structure. Rather than allowing her to focus exclusively upon the failure, he asked her to describe the difference she had experienced while attempting to manipulate the two objects.[2]
Norabell recognized that the distinction felt like one of weight. The ordinary cube-shaped dimension had seemed extremely light to her supernatural perception, while the miniature galaxy felt extraordinarily heavy, as though she were attempting to move something vastly beyond her physical capabilities. Victor identified this sensation as existential weight, explaining that higher Divine Concepts possess a fundamentally different metaphysical significance when incorporated into the foundation of an existence. Their presence can make a structure more deeply established within Reality and consequently more resistant to interference by beings lacking comparable conceptual authority.[2]
The principle extended beyond ordinary differences in physical mass or accumulated Energy. Two structures could appear similar while possessing vastly different levels of resistance because their metaphysical foundations were composed of different Divine Concepts. A sufficiently powerful individual might overwhelm some restrictions through sheer strength, but direct authority over the relevant Concepts provided a more natural and effective means of altering their structure. Norabell's difficulty therefore arose from the conceptual organization of Victor's miniature galaxy rather than a simple deficiency in her spatial power.[2][3]
Victor also explained how her inherited perception related to dimensional techniques employed by other supernatural beings. He used [[Agares]] as an example, describing how the Demon could enter an alternate layer of Reality and attack from a position ordinarily inaccessible to his opponents. Such techniques were extremely dangerous against individuals unable to perceive the concealed layer, but their effectiveness declined when confronting beings capable of observing and interacting with it directly. Norabell's Eyes operated upon an even more fundamental principle, allowing her to perceive the underlying Rules determining how those dimensional layers existed.[2]
Victor compared this ability to seeing the "source code of Reality". Once Norabell properly understood the information revealed through her Eyes, dimensional structures created according to the Rules of the surrounding Universe became visible and potentially manipulable to her. He further explained that all of his biological daughters possessed varying potential to develop similar perception, although some, particularly Norabell and Melida, were born with exceptionally powerful manifestations of the inheritance. Their eventual development depended upon continued training, understanding and the circumstances influencing their individual powers.[2]
The lesson then turned toward the nature of Victor's miniature galaxy. He revealed that its apparent stars were enormous Eldritch horrors, causing most of the assembled daughters to recoil in alarm. Yog and Nigredo reacted differently, regarding the creatures as beautiful rather than frightening because of their unusually strong connection to Eldritch existence. Victor explained that the galaxy's structure incorporated his Eldritch powers alongside Negativity and Beginning, producing an existence whose characteristics differed substantially from those of an ordinary subordinate dimension.[2][3]
However, Victor clarified that the miniature galaxy was not a fully independent cosmic system. Although its appearance was extraordinarily convincing, it lacked the complete conceptual framework required for a genuine independent Reality. To demonstrate the distinction, he summoned two small Eldritch beings and asked his daughters to compare them with the apparent inhabitants of the galaxy. The girls recognized that the latter were essentially successful replicas rather than genuine living entities possessing a complete independent existence.[3]
Victor privately understood that combining his powers with Azathoth's could potentially allow the creation of a genuine Eldritch galaxy, but doing so would attract unwanted attention from the cosmic authorities responsible for monitoring the stability of Creation. He therefore refrained from pursuing the possibility and continued concentrating upon the lesson. The demonstration reinforced the distinction between reproducing the appearance of a cosmic structure and establishing the independent conceptual laws necessary for a fully functioning Reality.[3]
After dismissing the miniature galaxy, Victor produced two apparently identical cubes and asked Norabell to examine them. She immediately recognized that one contained a greater number of Divine Concepts than the other, despite their similar appearances. When Victor asked what this implied, Norabell explained that structures incorporating more Divine Concepts possessed greater existential weight and were more difficult to alter without the corresponding authority. She compared the difficulty to attempting to modify the structure of Reality without possessing the appropriate keys, recognizing that enormous power alone was not always sufficient to bypass conceptual restrictions.[3]
Victor then returned to her original problem and asked what she had been attempting when her dimensional constructs collapsed. Norabell finally recognized that she had unconsciously incorporated her own Concepts of Time and Space into the structures. Rather than producing ordinary dimensions supported by the existing Universe, she had been attempting to construct independent systems without providing the additional Divine Concepts necessary for their stability. She compared the mistake to assembling a computer while omitting essential components, understanding that her powers had established portions of the intended structure without supplying the complete framework required for it to function.[3]
With this understanding, Norabell attempted the exercise again. She deliberately refrained from imposing her personal higher Divine Concepts upon the construct and instead allowed the surrounding Universe to provide the fundamental Rules supporting its existence. The correction immediately succeeded, and she effortlessly created a stable dimension in her hand. The accomplishment demonstrated that Norabell had possessed sufficient power for ordinary dimensional construction all along, with her earlier failures resulting from attempting a substantially more advanced application without understanding the additional requirements involved.[3]
Victor praised her accomplishment, and Norabell immediately embraced him in gratitude. The success resolved the difficulty that had originally brought her to him while providing a much deeper understanding of how Divine Concepts influenced the stability and manipulability of supernatural structures. It also established a distinction between her confirmed ability to create ordinary subordinate dimensions and her still-developing potential to construct independent realities possessing their own conceptual foundations.[3]
The gathering continued with further discussions concerning dimensional theory, Divine Concepts and the nature of Primordial existence. Victor explained that the numerical classification of higher dimensions sometimes employed in theoretical models was primarily a simplified means of describing increasingly advanced supernatural states. Such explanations could help ordinary individuals understand complicated concepts, but they did not automatically establish that every powerful being occupied a literal numbered spatial dimension. Victor used the Universal Tree and other Primordial authorities as examples of existences whose characteristics involved universal consciousness and fundamental conceptual functions rather than conventional spatial superiority alone.[3]
The distinction was particularly relevant to Norabell because her status as a Primordial Goddess did not automatically grant the complete cosmic responsibilities or authority of an established Primordial Being. Victor explained that a genuine Primordial authority embodied a fundamental component of Creation and that attaining such a position required more than merely possessing exceptional power or a strong affinity for the corresponding Concept. Norabell's innate relationship with Time and Space therefore represented an extraordinary foundation for further development rather than proof that she already governed every manifestation of those forces throughout the Universe.[3]
Her continued development remained unpredictable because of her half-Eldritch heritage. Although Norabell could already manipulate Space-Time, perceive the underlying structure of Reality and create subordinate dimensions, the full extent of her evolving powers was still unknown. Her progress demonstrated the exceptional potential inherited from both Natalia and Victor while also emphasizing the importance of conceptual understanding, since even a naturally born Primordial Goddess could encounter limitations when attempting to manipulate structures supported by higher principles.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Norabell's biological father and the individual toward whom she displays her strongest emotional attachment. She openly adores him and enjoys receiving his affection, although she occasionally becomes embarrassed because he continues treating her like a young girl despite her considerable chronological age. Victor affectionately calls her "my little cosmos", a nickname reflecting her relationship with Time, Space and the fundamental structure of Reality. When she protests that she is already more than a thousand years old, he reassures her that she will remain his little girl even if she eventually becomes billions of years old. Norabell pouts at his response but does not seriously reject the affection, revealing that she secretly enjoys being pampered by him.[2]
Their relationship also possesses an important educational dimension. Norabell respects Victor's extraordinary understanding of Reality and regards him as the person most capable of explaining the limitations she encounters during training. When her attempts to create stable dimensions repeatedly fail, she approaches him directly rather than abandoning the task. Victor recognizes that her mistake originates from attempting to construct an independent Reality and develops a practical exercise that allows her to understand the difference between ordinary subordinate dimensions and structures supported by higher Divine Concepts.[2]
Victor's instruction emphasizes understanding rather than the indiscriminate application of supernatural power. He encourages Norabell to use her inherited Eyes to observe the conceptual foundations of his creations, allows her to manipulate an ordinary dimension and then challenges her to attempt the same feat against a more complicated structure. By comparing their different existential weights, Norabell gradually recognizes why her earlier attempts were unsuccessful. Once she corrects her approach, she successfully creates a stable dimension and affectionately embraces Victor in gratitude, demonstrating both the effectiveness of his instruction and the close emotional bond between them.[2][3]
Victor is openly proud of Norabell's potential and praises the progress she has already achieved. Although her powers remain incomplete, he recognizes that she possesses the foundation necessary for extraordinary future development. His affection and confidence encourage her to continue training, while his explanations provide the conceptual knowledge necessary to understand increasingly complicated applications of her Space-Time authority.

#### Natalia Elderblood née Alioth
[[Natalia Elderblood née Alioth]] is Norabell's biological mother and the principal source of her inherited affinity for Time and Space. Natalia possesses an exceptional supernatural relationship with these forces, originally connected to the unusual eyes inherited through the Alioth lineage. Her powers underwent enormous development after she became a True Dragon, eventually reaching levels of spatial and temporal mastery that distinguished her even among [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s exceptionally powerful wives. The transformation greatly increased her abilities but also required considerable training because their expanded scope made them difficult to regulate without sufficient understanding.[1]
Norabell inherited this extraordinary potential, initially manifesting spatial powers comparable in nature to Natalia's while lacking the temporal Aspect her mother had developed through extensive practice. As Norabell matured, her inheritance expanded to include Time as well, allowing her to manipulate both fundamental principles directly. Their abilities therefore share a common foundation while differing in their individual development and the additional characteristics inherited by Norabell through Victor.[1][2]
Natalia's inheritance primarily provides Norabell's conceptual affinity for Space-Time, while Victor contributes the Draconic, Eldritch and truth-perceiving characteristics that make her existence particularly unusual. Her extraordinary Eyes complement the powers inherited from Natalia by allowing her to examine the underlying structure of the spatial and temporal phenomena she manipulates. Norabell consequently possesses a distinctive combination of her parents' abilities rather than simply reproducing Natalia's complete supernatural development.

#### Melida Charlotte Elderblood
[[Melida Charlotte Elderblood]] is one of Norabell's sisters and an important point of comparison for her inherited visual abilities. Both possess exceptionally powerful manifestations of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s truth-perceiving Dragon Eyes, allowing them to examine aspects of Reality inaccessible to ordinary supernatural perception. However, Melida's inheritance is described as more complete, and her existence as [[Merlin]]'s reincarnation further distinguishes her from the other daughters. Norabell therefore remains below Melida in this particular field despite already possessing one of the strongest ocular abilities within the family.[2]
Their differing levels of perception become relevant during Victor's dimensional lesson. When he produces an ordinary artificial dimension and a miniature galaxy, he specifically prevents Melida from answering his questions because her exceptional eyesight would make the exercise too easy. Norabell instead examines the structures herself and successfully recognizes their different conceptual foundations, demonstrating that her own abilities are highly developed even though they have not reached her sister's level. The comparison also reflects the broader nature of Victor's inherited Eyes, whose strength varies among his daughters while retaining the potential for further development through training.[2]

### Skills and Abilities
Norabell possesses an extraordinary combination of naturally born True Dragon physiology, Primordial Goddess characteristics, Space-Time authority, exceptional truth-perceiving Dragon Eyes and half-Eldritch heritage. Her specialized abilities primarily involve the manipulation of Time and Space, the perception of Reality's underlying structure, dimensional creation and interference with the authority governing subordinate dimensions. Unlike individuals who merely travel between spaces or distort physical distance, Norabell can examine the conceptual foundations upon which dimensional existence depends, allowing her to recognize and manipulate certain aspects of Reality that remain inaccessible to ordinary supernatural beings.[2][3]
Her strongest personally demonstrated feats include effortlessly changing ownership of an ordinary dimension, perceiving the additional Divine Concepts incorporated into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s miniature galaxy, identifying differences in existential weight, using the Dragon Language to reinforce her powers and successfully creating a stable subordinate dimension. Her inability to manipulate Victor's conceptual galaxy establishes an important limitation: perceiving the underlying truth of a structure does not automatically grant the authority necessary to override it, particularly when the target is supported by higher Divine Concepts.[2][3]

- Norabell possesses one of the strongest manifestations of Victor's inherited Dragon Eyes among his daughters, although [[Melida Charlotte Elderblood]] remains superior in this particular field because of her unusually complete inheritance and exceptional supernatural foundation.[2]

Norabell recognizes that the galaxy possesses more levels of existence and a greater number of Divine Concepts.[2]

- Her attempt to manipulate the galaxy briefly causes Reality to tremble before Victor stabilizes the surrounding environment. Norabell describes the difference between the structures through their respective existential weights, recognizing that the galaxy feels extraordinarily heavy compared with the ordinary dimension.[2]
- Victor's miniature galaxy incorporates Negativity, Beginning and Eldritch characteristics, but it is not a fully independent cosmic structure possessing every foundation required for genuine existence. Its apparent inhabitants are convincing replicas rather than complete independent lifeforms.[2][3]
- Victor privately recognizes that combining his powers with Azathoth could potentially allow him to create a genuine Eldritch galaxy, but refrains from attempting it because of the attention such an act would attract from the greater cosmic authorities.[3]
- Norabell correctly distinguishes between two visually identical cubes through the different Divine Concepts incorporated into their foundations. She recognizes that structures possessing greater existential weight are more difficult to alter without the corresponding authority.[3]
- After correcting her misunderstanding, Norabell successfully creates a stable subordinate dimension using the existing Rules of the surrounding Universe. Her earlier failures therefore do not indicate an inability to perform ordinary dimensional creation.[3]
- Norabell's status as a Primordial Goddess does not automatically establish that she occupies the completed cosmic office of a Primordial Regulator. Her inherited authority and exceptional potential remain distinct from the full governing position of an established Primordial Being.[3]
- Her complete Dragon Form, individual maximum combat capabilities and ultimate Space-Time authority remain unidentified. Although she possesses extraordinary inherited powers, she has not demonstrated unrestricted independent Universe creation or mastery over every manifestation of Time and Space.
