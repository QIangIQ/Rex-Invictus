**[[Lily Elderblood]]**, formerly known as Lily Bael née Lucifer, is the biological daughter of Lucifer Morningstar and Lilith Elderblood, the former political wife of the First Duke of Hell Bael, the mother of Luka Bael, one of Victor Elderblood's Demon wives and the fourth General appointed under Victor's rule over Hell. (Lore adapted from *My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires*.)

> **Rex Invictus canon.** This material is adapted into the Rex Invictus world at the lowest priority: Rex Invictus wins any conflict, then the Flamel material, then MTWABV. Events of the MTWABV timeline (Victor's conquests, the fall of the pantheons, the [[Elderblood Empire]]) are distant background or rumor unless a chapter puts them on the page. Lily Elderblood, formerly known as Lily [[Bael]] née [[Lucifer]], is the biological daughter of Lucifer Morningstar and [[Lilith Elderblood]], the former political wife of the First Duke of Hell Bael, the mother of [[Luka Bael]], one of [[Victor Elderblood]]'s Demon wives and the fourth General appointed under Victor's rule over Hell. Born as an ancient Succubus from the union of the Mother of Demons and the first Fallen Angel, Lily inherited an exceptionally prestigious Demonic Bloodline and the unusual ability known as Dark Light, a fusion of Demonic power with remnants of the Holy Light Lucifer retained after his Fall. This mixed nature makes her Light especially destructive against other Demons.[5]
Lily is distinguished less by overwhelming personal might than by her political intelligence, adaptability and willingness to exploit changing power structures. While many of Hell's ancient nobles resisted Victor after he abolished the old Pillar hierarchy, Lily immediately recognized that Bael and [[Diablo]]'s political order was already collapsing. She made herself useful to the new King, spread his reputation throughout the Demonic population, exposed reactionaries and accumulated enough merit to establish a position independent of Bael. Her decision culminated during the final stage of the Demonic War, when Lily and Helena Gremory Elderblood betrayed Bael and helped Victor eliminate him. Victor fulfilled his earlier promise and elevated Lily as his fourth General.[6][7][1]
Under Victor, Lily became one of the principal administrators of the reformed Hell, specializing in the regions responsible for punishing condemned Souls. Her creativity in torture became infamous even among other Demons, and she designed specialized punishments and executors such as Lily's Toy for the infernal sectors under her authority.[8] Her position only expanded as Victor's civilization grew; centuries later she was still identified as the General responsible for maintaining the Hells that receive sinners after death.[2] Lily was eventually recognized as one of Victor's wives and later entered his Blood Dragon lineage, becoming one of the Demonic Dragon Goddesses serving the [[Elderblood Empire]].[4][3]

### Physical Description

#### Succubus
Lily originally appeared as an extraordinarily beautiful woman with long black hair whose ends possess a distinct red coloration, blood-red eyes and the supernatural attractiveness characteristic of a powerful Succubus. Her Demonic body possesses horns, large leathery wings and a long, thin tail.[6] Her appearance primarily reflects her Demonic inheritance from Lilith, while the unusual Angelic component inherited through [[Lucifer]] manifests more prominently through her Dark Light than through conventional Angelic physical traits.
Although some artwork depicts Lily with Fallen Angel characteristics because of her connection to Lucifer, she should not be classified as a Fallen Angel. Lily was born as a Demon from Lucifer and Lilith rather than beginning as an Angel and undergoing a Fall comparable to her father.

#### Demonic Dragon Goddess
After entering [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood Dragon lineage, Lily acquired the physiology of a genuine True Dragon without losing the Demonic attributes that had defined her previous existence. In the later Elderblood era, Victor's Demon wives are identified as Demonic Dragon Goddesses, combining their former Demonic characteristics with the vastly superior body, Energy and potential of [[Blood Dragons]].[3] Lily can consequently possess Draconic characteristics alongside the horns, wings, tail and Dark-Light affinity inherited from her original Succubus form. The novel has not yet provided sufficient individual detail to establish the exact dimensions or complete physical appearance of Lily's maximum Dragon form.

### Personality and Traits
Lily is ambitious, ruthless, calculating, politically perceptive and extraordinarily pragmatic. She understands authority as something that must be supported by genuine power and usefulness rather than inherited titles alone. During her confrontation with Luka, Lily summarized a principle she had taught him throughout his life: power is everything. Her marriage to [[Bael]] gave her enormous political influence, remaining beneath [[Diablo]] protected her from unnecessary conflict, and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s conquest of Hell forced her to recognize that the previous balance of power no longer mattered. Rather than allowing pride in her ancient lineage to make her irrational, she adapted faster than most of Hell's nobility.[5]
This worldview does not make Lily mindlessly obedient to whichever individual appears strongest. Her defining talent is understanding how a political system functions and positioning herself where her abilities can produce the greatest benefit. After Victor abolished the Pillars, Lily did not simply surrender and expect him to protect her. She promoted his reputation, identified opposition within the new government, personally suppressed conspirators and accepted Victor's meritocratic principle that even an ancient Bloodline did not automatically entitle someone to authority. When Victor offered her a reward for her usefulness, Lily chose political office rather than simply requesting personal strength or wealth because she wanted an independent position earned through her own achievements.[6][7]
Lily nevertheless remains deeply proud of her ancestry. Being the daughter of [[Lucifer]] and Lilith is an important part of her identity, and she repeatedly reminds Luka that he possesses the Blood of two of the most important figures in Demonic history. Her pride differs from blind aristocratic entitlement because she expects a prestigious Bloodline to be matched by conduct worthy of that heritage. Luka's repeated failures anger her precisely because she considers his behavior beneath what someone of their lineage should display.[5]
She is also an accomplished manipulator and propagandist. Lily recognized how quickly Victor's image was spreading through Hell and deliberately reinforced increasingly extravagant descriptions of him, encouraging ideas such as the new King being Hell's "Messiah" or "Antichrist". Victor understood what she was doing almost immediately, but rather than reject the manipulation, he recognized its political usefulness.[6] Lily's ability to understand public perception became another reason she adapted so naturally to Victor's new government.
Her most disturbing characteristic is her creativity concerning punishment. Lily does not merely accept the violence traditionally associated with Demonic civilization; she genuinely enjoys inventing punishments and torture methods. After seeing what she had done to the conspirators involved with Luka's rebellion, Victor considered her creativity comparable to or potentially worse than [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] when the subject was torture.[7] Lily later designed Lily's Toy as a specialized executor for one of Hell's punishment sectors, and even thousands of years later other Demonic women remained uncomfortable when recalling the methods she had used upon Victor's enemies. Members of the [[Blood God Religion]] eventually learned several of their own infamous punishment techniques directly from her.[8][9]
Despite her cruelty, Lily is not incapable of affection. The earlier interpretation that she felt nothing for Luka is contradicted by her behavior and by how other Demons perceived their relationship. Lily had repeatedly protected her son and covered for his mistakes before his final rebellion.[7] When Luka continued conspiring against Victor despite her warnings, Lily brutally punished him because his behavior threatened both his life and the political position she had worked to create. Even during his public judgment she searched for an alternative to execution, ultimately proposing that Luka be transformed into a controlled puppet for ten million years. The punishment was horrific, but Victor recognized the contradiction behind it: Lily was still attempting to save her son's life.[7] Her affection follows Demonic rather than Human standards; she can genuinely love someone while simultaneously believing extreme pain is an acceptable consequence for stupidity or betrayal.
Her relationship with Bael contained none of that emotional attachment. Their marriage had always been transactional. Bael gained prestige by marrying the daughter of Lucifer and Lilith, while Lily obtained the protection and authority associated with the First Duke. Once Victor destroyed the hierarchy that made Bael politically useful, Lily saw no reason to preserve a marriage that had never been founded upon love.[1] Her loyalty to Victor initially contained obvious self-interest as well, but it developed into something more permanent. Victor gave Lily what she had desired for most of her existence—authority earned through her own achievements—and she remained one of his highest-ranking Demonic subordinates before eventually becoming one of his wives.[1][4]

### Background
Lily was born from [[Lucifer]] Morningstar, the first Fallen Angel and first King of [[Biblical Hell]], and [[Lilith Elderblood]], the Mother and Progenitor of Demons. Her name was apparently chosen in reference to Lilith, and her ancestry gave her one of the most prestigious Bloodlines in Hell. Lilith supplied the overwhelmingly Demonic foundation of Lily's existence, while Lucifer's remaining Angelic characteristics produced the unusual Holy component that later manifested through Dark Light.[6][5]
Lily eventually entered a political marriage with [[Bael]], the First Duke of the Ars Goetia. Neither side treated the relationship as a romance. Bael strengthened his prestige by marrying the biological daughter of Hell's first King and the Mother of Demons, while Lily gained influence, protection and freedom from other Demonic nobles who might otherwise have attempted to claim her. Their union produced [[Luka Bael]]. When Bael later questioned whether their marriage had ever meant anything during his final confrontation with Lily, she reminded him that both of them had always understood its transactional nature.[1]
Lily was indirectly introduced long before her formal appearance through the history of [[Sitri]]. After Sitri's successful schemes against Gremory made him excessively confident, he attempted to challenge Bael's position and eventually tried to sexually assault Bael's wife, the daughter of Lucifer. Lily retaliated personally and castrated him, leaving Sitri with a humiliation that remained notorious throughout Hell.[10]

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Lily formally entered the story after [[Victor Elderblood]] conquered [[Biblical Hell]] and dismantled the political order established around [[Diablo]] and the seventy-two Pillars. While much of Hell's old nobility reacted with confusion, resentment or denial, Lily quickly understood that Victor had already changed the foundations upon which their authority depended. He had damaged structures previously regarded as virtually indestructible, broken through Hell's ancient defenses, gained recognition from its oldest inhabitants and inspired enormous numbers of lesser Demons to treat him as their legitimate King. [[Bael]]'s title as First Duke could no longer guarantee Lily's future in a society where Victor had abolished the very hierarchy that made the title valuable.[6][5]
Rather than join the reactionaries attempting to restore the old regime, Lily deliberately positioned herself within Victor's emerging government. Survival was one motive, since she understood that Bael's continued loyalty to Diablo would eventually place him against the new King, but Lily was equally attracted to Victor's meritocratic system. Under the previous hierarchy, much of her political identity came from being [[Lucifer]]'s daughter and Bael's wife; Victor's system allowed her to obtain authority through her own accomplishments. She began spreading Victor's reputation through Hell, reinforcing increasingly grand titles attached to him and presenting herself as a useful political operator instead of another aristocrat waiting for the former order to return.[6]
The first major test of Lily's relationship with Victor came through [[Luka Bael]]. Luka participated in a reactionary conspiracy against the new King despite Lily having already protected him and warned him against provoking Victor. Once he repeated the mistake, Lily stopped attempting to shield him from consequences and requested permission to deal with him personally. She brutally confronted Luka, explained why resisting Victor had become political suicide and reminded him of the philosophy she had taught him throughout his life: power determined the reality in which they operated. During the confrontation she demonstrated her hereditary Dark Light, combining Demonic Energy with the remnants of Holy Light inherited through Lucifer and producing an ability particularly dangerous to Demons.[5]
Lily subsequently hunted the other conspirators and dragged them before Victor during a public judgment. The spectacle was politically valuable because the Demonic population already knew Lily had repeatedly protected Luka; seeing Lucifer's daughter apparently deliver her own child for punishment presented her as someone willing to place the new King's authority above family privilege. Victor deliberately used the situation to strengthen both Lily's reputation and the legitimacy of his new laws. Lily nevertheless did not want Luka executed and proposed a punishment derived from an old method associated with Lilith: Luka would be transformed into a controlled puppet and remain under Lily's authority for ten million years. Victor accepted, recognizing that the horrific sentence simultaneously punished treason and allowed Lily to keep her son alive.[7]
Victor then allowed Lily to request a reward for the service she had provided. She did not ask for personal strength, wealth or a special artifact, instead requesting the highest political office her accomplishments could justify. Her ambition was fulfilled during the final confrontation with Diablo's faction. As the old alliances surrounding the former Demon King collapsed, Lily and Helena Gremory Elderblood betrayed Bael directly on the battlefield. The two Succubi attacked him at close range, with Lily intensifying her Dark Light to exploit the Holy component's effectiveness against a Demon. When Bael questioned the meaning of their marriage, Lily openly stated that their union had always been mutually beneficial rather than romantic: Bael had gained the prestige of Lucifer and Lilith's daughter, while Lily had gained the authority of Rank One. With Victor having destroyed the system that made Bael's rank valuable, that arrangement no longer served her.[1]
Helena introduced Victor's Blood into the incapacitated Bael, allowing Victor to reduce the First Duke into Blood and consume him. Afterward, Lily knelt before Victor alongside the Demons who had chosen his rule. Victor declared that Bael's death completed the final mission he had assigned to her and fulfilled his promise by appointing Lily his fourth General. The promotion represented the culmination of Lily's entire strategy: she had entered the conflict as the politically powerful wife of another Demon and emerged as a high-ranking authority whose position existed independently of Bael.[1]

#### King Among Kings
Under [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s established reign, Lily became one of the central administrators of the reorganized Hell. Her area of responsibility was particularly suited to her personality: she was placed in charge of the sectors where condemned Souls were punished. Rather than functioning merely as a battlefield General, Lily developed punishment systems capable of operating as part of Hell's afterlife infrastructure. Victor trusted her to administer sinners in his name, giving her precisely the kind of institutional authority she had sought when she first joined him.[8]
One of the clearest examples of her work was Lily's Toy, a grotesque Demonic executor personally created for the Sector of Lust. The entity administered punishments to Souls whose crimes involved the sexual exploitation of vulnerable victims, demonstrating that Lily's role had expanded beyond personally torturing individual prisoners. She could design specialized punishments and executors that continued carrying out her judgment even while she was occupied elsewhere.[8] Her position therefore developed into something far greater than the old status she once possessed as [[Bael]]'s wife: she now held direct authority over portions of the infernal system itself.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
As [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction expanded beyond Hell and developed into the multiracial [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]], Lily remained one of its principal Demonic leaders. When Victor began consolidating populations and factions within the territories associated with the former [[Egyptian Pantheon]], Lily arrived with Helena Gremory Elderblood, [[Vine]] Elderblood, [[Vepar Elderblood]] and [[Lilith Elderblood]], accompanied by significant Demonic forces.[4]
During the gathering, [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood referred to Helena, Lily, Vine and Vepar collectively as her husband's wives. All four accepted the description, while Lilith—whose circumstances with Victor were still different at that point—reacted separately. The scene establishes that Lily's relationship with Victor had progressed beyond General and sovereign by Chapter 888 and that she was recognized within the household as one of his Demon wives.[4] Unlike her former marriage to [[Bael]], Lily entered Victor's family after she had already established her own authority as a General rather than depending upon marriage to provide her political relevance.
Lily later became a Blood Dragon as Victor extended his evolving lineage to members of his family. Her transformation did not erase her Succubus ancestry or Dark-Light Bloodline; the later Elderblood forces explicitly identify Victor's Demon wives as Demonic Dragon Goddesses, beings whose Demonic characteristics coexist with genuine True Dragon physiology.[3] This evolution elevated Lily from an ancient Succubus into a True Dragon Goddess while preserving the qualities that had made her unique among Hell's Demons.

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Lily's responsibilities continued increasing as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s territories developed into the [[Elderblood Empire]]. During Victor's vacation, changes to the supernatural afterlife suddenly caused [[Biblical Hell]] to receive an enormous influx of Souls. Lily was among the officials hit hardest by the crisis because she was explicitly responsible for maintaining all of the Hells where sinners went after death. The number of incoming Souls exceeded what the existing structure could efficiently process, forcing Lily to call upon [[Persephone Elderblood]], [[Aline Elderblood Valefar]] and [[Lilith Elderblood]] while the other Demonic authorities handled their own sectors.[2]
The incident demonstrated how completely Lily's political position had evolved. She was no longer simply a torturer, battlefield General or administrator of a few infernal prisons; she was responsible for portions of an afterlife system receiving enormous populations of dead Souls. The administrators of Hell consequently began revising classification, judgment and distribution procedures so that ordinary cases could be processed efficiently while exceptional Souls remained available for individual attention.[2]
Lily's influence also extended outside Hell itself. By Chapter 1017, members of the [[Blood God Religion]] had developed a reputation for exceptionally elaborate punishments inflicted upon serious criminals. When the origin of those methods was questioned, Lily proudly admitted that she had taught them. The other Demonic women immediately remembered the techniques Lily had historically used against Victor's enemies and reacted with discomfort despite their own familiarity with Hell, emphasizing how extreme her creativity remained even by Demonic standards.[9]
In the later Imperial era Lily continued serving as one of Victor's Demonic Dragon Goddesses and an established authority within the civilization he had created. Her progression consequently represents one of Hell's most dramatic political transformations: the daughter of [[Lucifer]] who once relied partly upon [[Bael]]'s position became an independent General, administrator of the sinner Hells, wife of the Emperor and True Dragon Goddess whose authority was secured through her own accomplishments rather than the prestige of her father or former husband.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
Lily's relationship with [[Victor Elderblood]] began through political calculation. She recognized earlier than most ancient Demons that [[Bael]] and [[Diablo]]'s system was collapsing and deliberately made herself useful to the new ruler. Lily promoted Victor's reputation, informed his government about conspiracies and personally suppressed the rebellion involving her son rather than demanding immunity because of her heritage.[6][7] Victor understood that Lily was ambitious and self-interested, but he regarded those characteristics as useful so long as she remained competent and her ambitions aligned with his government.
Their relationship changed decisively after Lily helped eliminate Bael. Victor fulfilled his promise and appointed her his fourth General, giving Lily the independent political authority she had sought for centuries.[1] Over time her place beside Victor developed beyond ruler and subordinate, and by Chapter 888 she was explicitly recognized by [[Aphrodite]] as one of Victor's Demon wives.[4] The available chapter material establishes her wife status but does not independently identify the specific mechanism of their union as a [[Soul Marriage]], so that label should not be assigned without a direct source.

#### Bael
[[Bael]] was Lily's former husband and the father of [[Luka Bael]]. Their marriage was political rather than romantic: Bael benefited from marrying the daughter of [[Lucifer]] and Lilith, reinforcing the prestige associated with his position as First Duke, while Lily gained the protection, influence and political reach connected to Rank One.[1] Their relationship functioned while both sides continued receiving those benefits, but [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s abolition of the Pillar hierarchy made Bael's status increasingly worthless to Lily.
During Chapter 686, Lily and Helena betrayed Bael directly. When he attempted to invoke their marriage during the confrontation, Lily rejected the idea that either of them could suddenly pretend the relationship had been based upon love. She subsequently helped immobilize him and enabled Victor to consume him.[1]

#### Luka Bael
[[Luka Bael]] is Lily's son with [[Bael]] and provides the clearest evidence that Lily's cruelty does not mean she lacks genuine attachment. Other Demons knew Lily for repeatedly protecting Luka and covering for his failures, but his refusal to adapt after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s conquest gradually exhausted her tolerance.[7] Lily viewed his rebellions not merely as political disobedience but as stupidity that would inevitably cause Victor to kill him if she continued shielding him.
When Luka participated in another conspiracy, Lily personally punished him and forced him to confront how completely the balance of power in Hell had changed.[5] During his later public judgment she did not request another simple pardon, understanding that Victor could not permit repeated treason without consequences. Instead she proposed turning Luka into a controlled puppet for ten million years. The sentence was monstrous by Human standards, but it preserved his life, and Victor recognized that Lily was still protecting her son in the only manner politically available to her.[7]

#### Lilith Elderblood
[[Lilith Elderblood]] is Lily's mother and the Progenitor of the Demonic Race. Lily's name was apparently chosen in reference to Lilith, and the overwhelming majority of her original supernatural nature derives from Lilith's Demonic lineage.[6] Lily also possesses knowledge of techniques associated with her mother; when searching for an alternative to Luka's execution, she remembered an old method Lilith had used to transform hostile beings into controlled war puppets.[7]
Their family relationship becomes considerably more unusual in the Elderblood era because both eventually become members of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household. Despite that development, Lily remains one of Lilith's most direct descendants and combines her mother's Demonic nature with the remaining Angelic characteristics inherited through [[Lucifer]].

#### Lucifer Morningstar
[[Lucifer]] Morningstar is Lily's biological father and the source of the unusual Holy component within her Bloodline. Lily takes considerable pride in descending from the first Fallen Angel and first King of Hell, repeatedly emphasizing that heritage while speaking to Luka.[5] Lucifer's Holy Light did not disappear completely after his Fall, and traces of that power were inherited by Lily through his union with Lilith, eventually manifesting as Dark Light.[5]
Unlike Lucifer, Lily was never originally an Angel. She was born as a Demon and therefore should not be categorized as a Fallen Angel simply because her father possessed that history.

#### Helena Gremory Elderblood
Helena Gremory Elderblood became one of Lily's closest political counterparts during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s conquest of Hell. Both women were ancient Succubi capable of recognizing the opportunities created by the collapse of [[Diablo]]'s system, and both eventually earned positions as Victor's Generals.
Their most important joint action occurred during Chapter 686, when Lily and Helena personally betrayed [[Bael]]. Lily restrained and attacked him with Dark Light while Helena introduced Victor's Blood into his body, allowing Victor to eliminate the First Duke.[1] Both women later remained major Demonic officials and were recognized among Victor's wives.[4]

### Abilities

#### Skills and Abilities
Lily Elderblood is an ancient Demonic noble, combatant, political strategist, infernal administrator and current Blood Dragon Goddess. Her ability set is unusually broad because her present existence combines the racial foundation of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s [[Blood Dragons]] with supernatural traits preserved from her original Succubus body and the exceptional Bloodline produced by the union of [[Lucifer]] Morningstar and [[Lilith Elderblood]]. Before her Draconic transformation Lily was already a powerful ancient Demon whose signature ability, Dark Light, combined Demonic power with surviving traces of Lucifer's Angelic Holy Light and was consequently extraordinarily effective against other Demons.[1]
After entering Victor Elderblood's Blood Dragon lineage, Lily was reconstructed into a genuine True Dragon while retaining compatible aspects of her former Demonic existence. Blood Dragon transformation does not simply place Dragon characteristics over an older body; compatible racial traits and supernatural powers can be integrated into the new Draconic foundation and strengthened by its vastly superior body, Soul and Energy system.[2][3] By Chapter 1085 Lily is identified among Victor's Demonic Dragon Goddesses.[4]
The exact personal Divine Concept belonging to Lily has not been explicitly named in the checked chapters. Her Dark Light is a hereditary Bloodline ability, her Demonic powers originate from her former Succubus existence, and her current Draconic abilities originate from Blood Dragon physiology. Concepts such as Torture, Punishment, Sadism, Dark Light or Hell should therefore not automatically be listed as Lily's Divinities without a direct statement from the novel.

##### Demonic Blood Dragon Goddess Physiology
Blood Dragon Physiology: Lily possesses the complete physiology of a Blood Dragon rather than merely having acquired superficial Dragon characteristics. [[Blood Dragons]] combine the compatible foundations of [[True Dragons]] and Noble Vampires into a unified Race while being capable of preserving important supernatural characteristics belonging to the individual before conversion.[2][5] In Lily's case, the resulting existence retained substantial aspects of her Succubus and Demonic nature, producing the Demonic Dragon Goddess classification used for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Demon wives.[4]
- True Dragon Body: Lily possesses a genuine Draconic body capable of sustaining supernatural power far beyond the limits of her original Succubus physiology.[2]
- True Dragon Soul: Blood Dragon transformation reconstructs the individual's spiritual foundation as well as the physical body. Lily consequently possesses a genuine Draconic Soul rather than simply a Demon body enhanced with scales, wings or horns.[2]
- [[Dragon Heart]]: Lily possesses the specialized [[Draconic Energy]] system centered around the Dragon Heart, which functions as one of the principal supernatural engines sustaining a True Dragon's enormous body and power.[6]
- Draconic Energy Generation: Her Dragon Heart naturally generates and circulates the supernatural Energy associated with Dragonkind.
- Draconic Energy Manipulation: Lily can manipulate the Energy generated by her True Dragon physiology. True Dragons possess such extensive compatibility with Energy that ordinary Energy manipulation does not require a Rune or external magical framework.[7]
- [[Natural Energy]] Command: A True Dragon can directly command and shape Energy as an instinctive racial capability. Runes become important when imposing more complicated instructions, Concepts or changes upon Reality rather than being required for every basic supernatural action.[7]
- Enhanced Energy Capacity: True Dragon bodies are constructed to contain supernatural Energy reserves vastly beyond those available to ordinary biological species.
- Energy Storage: Draconic anatomy functions as an enormous supernatural reservoir, with the Dragon Heart, body and additional structures supporting quantities of Energy that could destroy weaker bodies.
- Energy Sustenance: True Dragons can sustain themselves through supernatural Energy rather than depending entirely upon conventional nutrition. Food and other ordinary biological requirements consequently become far less important to Lily's continued survival than they were for conventional mortal species.[8]
- Immense Strength: Lily possesses the enormous physical strength characteristic of True Dragons. Even before her transformation, she demonstrated enough physical power to pierce [[Bael]]'s body with her own hands while attacking alongside Helena.[9]
- Immense Speed and Reflexes: Her current Blood Dragon body possesses vastly enhanced movement speed, reflexes and reaction capability compared with her original Succubus state.
- Immense Durability: True Dragon physiology possesses exceptional biological resilience and can contain supernatural forces that would destroy ordinary Demonic bodies.[2]
- Immense Stamina and Endurance: Lily's Dragon physiology allows her to maintain supernatural activity and combat for periods far beyond normal living beings.
- Enhanced Agility: Lily combines her former aerial experience as a Succubus with the superior physical control of a True Dragon.
- Enhanced Senses: True Dragons possess highly developed supernatural senses capable of detecting Energy and phenomena far beyond conventional mortal perception.
- Dragon Eyes: Genuine True Dragons possess supernatural Draconic Eyes capable of perceiving Energy and other supernatural phenomena. Lily has not demonstrated an individually named ocular ability comparable to specialized Elderblood Eyes, so only the ordinary True Dragon sensory baseline should be assigned to her.[10]
- Draconic Instincts: Lily possesses the powerful biological instincts belonging to Dragonkind, including instinctive control over her body, Energy and supernatural presence.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Blood Dragons combine the regeneration inherited from their Vampiric foundation with the exceptional durability of True Dragon physiology, creating an unusually resilient form of recovery.[2][5]
- Vampiric Regeneration–Dragon Durability Synergy: Damage inflicted upon Lily must contend with a durable Draconic body while also being opposed by the regenerative characteristics incorporated into Blood Dragon physiology.
- Shapeshifting: Blood Dragons possess highly mutable bodies and can maintain humanoid forms while expressing selected Draconic or inherited racial characteristics.[2]
- Humanoid Transformation: Lily can retain an appearance closely resembling her former Succubus body despite possessing genuine True Dragon physiology.
- True Dragon Transformation: As a full Blood Dragon rather than a Dragonoid, Lily possesses an actual Dragon Form.[11]
- Flight: Lily could already fly through her Succubus wings and currently possesses the aerial mobility of a True Dragon.
- Natural Weaponry: Her Draconic body can possess claws, fangs, horns, wings, scales and a tail capable of functioning as natural weapons.
- Dragon Breath: Full True Dragons possess Dragon Breath as an innate racial weapon. The exact manifestation of Lily's personal Breath has not been individually described, so it should not yet be assigned a specific Element or property.[3]
- Attribute-Compatible Dragon Breath: Dragon Breath can reflect an individual's inherited powers, Element, Bloodline or supernatural nature.[3] Lily's Demonic and Dark-Light heritage could therefore influence her eventual individual Breath, but a specific Dark-Light Breath or Miasma Breath should not be treated as confirmed until demonstrated.
- Extreme Longevity: Lily had already lived for millennia as a Demon and now possesses the vastly greater developmental lifespan of a True Dragon.
- Creation Compatibility: True Dragons possess an exceptional natural compatibility with the phenomena and Energies belonging to Creation. This compatibility is one of the reasons radically different abilities can coexist inside a Blood Dragon without simply destroying one another.[2]
- Power Retention: Becoming a Blood Dragon does not automatically erase compatible abilities belonging to the individual's previous Race. Lily consequently retained her Demonic nature and Dark Light instead of becoming an interchangeable generic Dragon.[2]
- Power Integration: Compatible abilities and racial traits are incorporated into the dominant Blood Dragon foundation rather than functioning as completely disconnected transformations.[2]
- Power Enhancement: Existing supernatural abilities can become stronger or develop new characteristics after being supported by the superior body, Soul and Energy system of a True Dragon. The transformations of Victor's wives demonstrate that old racial powers were preserved and elevated rather than simply discarded.[3]
- Adaptive Racial Evolution: Blood Dragon physiology can integrate and develop retained supernatural characteristics over time instead of permanently fixing every member to one identical racial template.[2][3]
- Hybridization: Lily's existence demonstrates the Blood Dragon Race's ability to preserve a powerful secondary racial lineage. Her Draconic foundation did not erase her Demonic Essence, resulting in a Demonic Blood Dragon rather than a standardized Dragon identical to Victor's Vampire-born wives.[2]
- Environmental Adaptation: Dragon physiology can gradually develop according to surrounding Energy, environment and supernatural conditions. This is a long-term racial characteristic rather than instantaneous combat adaptation.[12]
- Environmental Enrichment: Dragons possess a positive interaction with Creation in which their prolonged presence can gradually enrich the surrounding environment with Life and supernatural vitality.[13]
- Vampiric Weakness Negation: The Draconic foundation of the Blood Dragon Race removes most of the conventional weaknesses associated with ordinary Vampire physiology. Sunlight and weapons created solely around ordinary Vampire weaknesses are consequently not meaningful racial vulnerabilities to a complete Blood Dragon.[14]
- Reduced Biological Dependency: Blood Dragons do not possess the same survival dependence upon Blood or conventional nourishment as ordinary Vampires.[8]

##### Dragon Goddess Status
By Chapter 1085, Lily is explicitly counted among [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Demonic Dragon Goddesses, establishing that her current existence is Divine in addition to Draconic.[4] The exact Concept responsible for Lily's personal Godhood has not been named, however, meaning her Divine status should not be expanded into an invented Divinity.
- Divine Existence: Lily currently exists as a Dragon Goddess rather than solely as a supernatural mortal or ancient Demon.[4]
- Divine-Level Energy Capacity: Her Godhood operates together with the enormous Energy system of a Blood Dragon, allowing her current existence to function on a fundamentally higher level than her former Succubus state.
- Divinity (Unknown): Lily possesses Goddess status, but the specific personal Concept or Domain incorporated into her existence has not yet been identified in the checked material.
- Conceptual Power Potential: As a Dragon Goddess, Lily belongs to the class of Divine [[True Dragons]] capable of expressing powers through Divinity and Draconic existence. No specific Lily-exclusive conceptual application should be indexed until the novel actually identifies her Concept.

##### Draconic Language and Runecraft
Full [[Blood Dragons]] possess natural compatibility with the supernatural language and Runic system of Dragonkind. This does not mean every Blood Dragon is automatically equal to specialists such as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], [[Scathach]] or Zaladrac, but even comparatively untalented members retain access to the racial foundation necessary for basic Draconic Rune usage.[5]
- Draconic Language: Lily possesses racial compatibility with the language of [[True Dragons]]. Draconic speech carries Energy, intent and supernatural meaning rather than functioning solely as ordinary communication.[15]
- Speak of Power / Power Words: A Dragon can combine language, Energy, intention and [[Will Solace|Will]] to produce supernatural effects through spoken commands.[15]
- Reality Imposition: The Draconic Language can impose the speaker's Will upon Reality when the user possesses sufficient understanding and power. The exact scale depends heavily upon the individual Dragon.[16]
- Basic Draconic Runes: Victor later explains that even comparatively untalented full Blood Dragons possess enough racial affinity to use basic Draconic Runes.[5]
- Runic Reality Manipulation: Draconic Runes can encode instructions, supernatural qualities and changes into Reality. Lily possesses the racial foundation necessary to use this system, although no advanced individual Runemaster feat has been shown for her.
- Energy-and-Intent Encoding: Draconic Rune use combines Energy with meaning and Will rather than depending solely upon physical symbols.
- Innate Runic Affinity: Lily's True Dragon status gives her a natural advantage in understanding Draconic Script compared with non-Dragons.[5]
Lily should not be credited with universal mastery of every Draconic Rune merely because she became a Dragon. Basic access is racial; advanced Runic knowledge depends upon training, intelligence, experience and individual talent.[5]

##### Dark Light
Dark Light is Lily's signature hereditary power and one of the most unusual Bloodline abilities possessed by a Demon. It originated through the union of [[Lilith Elderblood]], Mother and Progenitor of Demons, and [[Lucifer]] Morningstar, the first Fallen Angel.[1]
Although Lucifer's Angelic nature became corrupted after his Fall, his original Holy Light was not completely erased. Traces of that Holy characteristic survived in his Bloodline and manifested within Lily, whose fundamentally Demonic existence could simultaneously produce Energy carrying properties naturally hostile to Demons.[1]

###### Dark-Light Generation and Manipulation
- Dark-Light Generation: Lily naturally produces Dark Light through her Bloodline.[1]
- Energy Manipulation: She can control the unique Energy after generating it rather than merely releasing it involuntarily.
- Energy Projection: Dark Light can be projected outside Lily's body and applied offensively.
- Output Amplification: Lily can deliberately increase the quantity and intensity of Dark Light she produces. During [[Bael]]'s defeat she increased its output while physically restraining him.[9]
- Aura Manifestation: Dark Light can manifest around Lily's body as a supernatural aura.
- Black-and-White Energy Manifestation: The ability has been visually represented through contrasting black-and-white supernatural Energy when Lily releases it.[1]
- Energy Reinforcement: Lily can combine Dark Light with direct physical attacks rather than treating it solely as a ranged ability.[9]

###### Demonic and Holy Dual Nature
Dark Light is neither ordinary Demonic Energy nor conventional Holy Light. Its structure combines properties belonging to traditionally opposed supernatural forces.
- Demonic Component: Dark Light remains naturally compatible with Lily's Demonic body rather than damaging its own user.
- Holy-Light Component: Remnants of [[Lucifer]]'s former Angelic Holy Light remain incorporated into the ability.[1]
- Hybrid Energy Nature: The coexistence of Demonic and Holy characteristics distinguishes Dark Light from ordinary Miasma, conventional Demonic Energy and pure Angelic Light.
- Opposing-Energy Compatibility: Lily's Bloodline allows her to contain a Holy characteristic inside an otherwise Demonic supernatural existence without immediately suffering the destructive incompatibility normally expected between those powers.
The Holy component consists of surviving traces inherited through Lucifer. It does not prove that Lily possesses every Holy ability available to Lucifer, an original Seraph or the [[Heavenly Father]].

###### Anti-Demon Properties
Dark Light is explicitly described as extraordinarily dangerous to Demons because it allows a Demon to attack others of her Race with Energy containing the Holy property to which they are naturally vulnerable.[1]
- Anti-Demonic Damage: Dark Light possesses increased effectiveness against Demonic targets.[1]
- Holy-Attribute Damage: The Holy component allows Dark Light to exploit the supernatural weakness Demons possess toward [[Holy Energy]].
- Pain Inducement: Increasing Dark Light around [[Bael]] caused the First Duke to writhe from the resulting pain.[9]
- High-Ranking Demon Effectiveness: The ability remained dangerous even to Bael, one of the strongest ancient Demons beneath [[Diablo]].[9]
- Demonic Defense Bypass (Attribute Advantage): Dark Light's Holy component gives it an advantage against durability or resistance based primarily upon ordinary Demonic physiology. This should not be interpreted as unrestricted durability negation against every target.
Dark Light's special effectiveness is specifically demonstrated against Demons. Equivalent super-effectiveness against Angels, Gods, Vampires, Dragons or unrelated [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] should not be assumed.

###### Bloodline Exclusivity
Despite being Lily's biological son, [[Luka Bael]] did not inherit Dark Light. His inherited characteristics leaned more heavily toward [[Bael]]'s lineage rather than reproducing [[Lucifer]]'s unusual contribution to Lily.[1] Dark Light should consequently be treated as a rare hereditary manifestation rather than an ability automatically transmitted intact to every descendant Lily produces.

##### Former Succubus and Demonic Physiology
Before becoming a Blood Dragon Goddess, Lily was an ancient Succubus descended directly from the Mother of Demons and the first Fallen Angel. Many compatible characteristics of this previous existence survived her Draconic transformation, but abilities listed in this subsection as features of ordinary Demonic physiology should not automatically be assumed to function identically after her Race was fundamentally reconstructed.
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics: Lily possessed strength, speed, durability, stamina and reflexes far beyond mortal limits even before becoming a Dragon.
- Demonic Energy Manipulation: Her Succubus body naturally generated and manipulated the supernatural Energy associated with Demons.
- Miasma Compatibility: As a Hell-born Demon, Lily possessed natural compatibility with the Miasma saturating [[Biblical Hell]].
- Miasma Resistance: Ordinary levels of infernal Miasma that are harmful to Holy or non-Demonic beings were naturally compatible with Lily's physiology.
- Demonic Regeneration: Lily possessed the regenerative capabilities associated with powerful Demons before obtaining the vastly superior regeneration of her current Blood Dragon body.
- Demonic Longevity: Lily survived for millennia while retaining a youthful physical appearance.
- Demonic Wings: Her Succubus body possessed large leathery wings capable of natural flight.
- Demonic Horns: Lily manifested horns as part of her Demonic physiology.
- Demonic Tail: She possessed a long, thin Succubus tail.
- Demonic Claws / Natural Weapons: Lily could use her body directly as a supernatural weapon.
- Supernatural Beauty: Like other powerful Succubi, Lily possessed an unnaturally attractive appearance.
- Hell Adaptation: Her former body was naturally adapted to the hostile supernatural environment of Biblical Hell.
- Conditional [[Immortality]] / Demonic Reconstitution (Former Racial Ability): Full Demons killed through ordinary means can eventually reform within Hell, with more powerful Demons requiring greater periods to reconstruct themselves. Holy Light, specialized anti-Demon weapons and complete absorption can bypass this process.[17] Lily's current Blood Dragon body fundamentally altered her Race, so the exact status of this old reconstitution mechanism after her transformation has not been individually confirmed.
Generic Succubus abilities such as unrestricted Charm, possession, universal Life Absorption or automatic sexual mind control should not be attributed to Lily unless she individually demonstrates them.

##### Demonic Restoration
Lily possesses at least a limited ability to restore injuries in another Demon. After severely injuring Luka during their confrontation, Lily caused her eyes to glow and deliberately restored part of his physical damage so that he would remain alive while she continued dealing with him.[1]
- Limited Healing: Lily can restore at least a portion of another Demon's injuries.
- Life Preservation: She can selectively repair enough physical damage to prevent immediate death.
- Controlled Restoration: Lily did not need to completely heal Luka and could restore only the amount necessary for her purpose.
The mechanism has not been explained sufficiently to classify this as a universal Healing Divinity, complete Resurrection or unrestricted biological restoration.

##### Demonic Ritual and Forbidden-Technique Knowledge
Lily possesses extensive knowledge regarding old Demonic techniques, political practices and supernatural methods developed during Hell's ancient conflicts. Her position as Lilith's daughter, [[Lucifer]]'s daughter and [[Bael]]'s former wife exposed her to knowledge unavailable to ordinary Demons.

###### Puppetization / Mind-Control Technique (Technical Knowledge)
During Luka's public judgment, Lily remembered an old method previously used by [[Lilith Elderblood]] to reduce captured hostile Demons and beasts into obedient war puppets.[18] She understood the method sufficiently to propose applying it to Luka as an alternative to execution, resulting in his sentence of ten million years as her slave and puppet.
- Puppetization Knowledge: Lily understands a Demonic technique capable of converting a target into a controlled subordinate.
- Mind Suppression / Free-[[Will Solace|Will]] Suppression (Ritual Method): The underlying technique suppresses or removes the target's capacity to act freely.
- Long-Term Control: The method was considered suitable for maintaining Luka as a controlled servant for an enormous period.
- War-Puppet Knowledge: Lily knows historical Demonic methods used to repurpose defeated enemies into combat assets.
Because the relevant chapter primarily shows Lily proposing the technique rather than exhaustively demonstrating its mechanics herself, it should be indexed as confirmed technical knowledge rather than unrestricted Mind Manipulation usable instantly against any opponent.

##### Demonic Entity Creation (Limited/Unexplained)
Lily personally created the supernatural entity known as Lily's Toy, which operates as an executor within the Sector of Lust under [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s infernal system.[19]
- Demonic Entity Creation: Lily possesses sufficient supernatural and technical knowledge to create or construct specialized Demonic beings.
- Artificial Servant Creation: Lily's Toy functions as an independent punishment executor capable of carrying out the purpose for which Lily designed it.
- Specialized Biological/Supernatural Design: The creation was constructed for a particular infernal function rather than existing as an ordinary naturally born Demon.
- Autonomous Punishment Execution: Lily can delegate punishment to entities she has designed instead of personally supervising every condemned Soul.
The exact creation process remains unexplained. Lily's Toy is therefore not evidence that Lily possesses unrestricted Life Creation, Soul Creation, spontaneous species creation or a Creation Divinity.

##### Torture and Punishment Mastery
Lily is one of the greatest specialists in torture and punitive design within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s entire faction. Her expertise extends considerably beyond simply enjoying violence; Victor entrusted her with the infernal sectors responsible for punishing condemned Souls, turning her knowledge into an institutional component of Hell.[19][20]
- Torture Expertise: Lily possesses exceptional practical knowledge of physical pain, psychological breaking, prolonged punishment and methods of maintaining targets through extended suffering.
- Demonic Anatomy Knowledge: Her experience allows her to exploit the physiology, resilience and regeneration of Demonic targets rather than accidentally killing them too quickly.
- Pain Management and Life Preservation: Lily understands how much punishment supernatural beings can survive and how to prolong suffering without immediately ending the target's life.
- Psychological Torture: Her punishments can be designed around fear, humiliation, guilt and the psychological weaknesses of the target rather than relying solely upon physical injury.
- Punishment Design: Lily creates punishments according to the nature of an individual's crimes, particularly after becoming responsible for the sinner sectors of Hell.[19]
- Specialized Executor Design: Lily can create servants such as Lily's Toy to continuously administer a punishment system without her direct presence.[19]
- Extreme Creativity: After seeing Lily's treatment of the conspirators associated with Luka's rebellion, Victor considered her creativity in torture comparable to or potentially greater than even [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]].[18]
- Torture Instruction: Lily can teach her methods to other people. By Chapter 1017, members of the [[Blood God Religion]] were using punishment techniques learned directly from her.[21]
- High-Level Prisoner Punishment: Lily and [[Medusa]] Elderblood were later involved in the prolonged punishment inflicted upon [[Athena]] rather than simply executing the Goddess immediately.[22]

##### Infernal Administration and Soul-System Knowledge
Lily's responsibility over the Hells for sinners requires extensive practical knowledge concerning the administration of deceased Souls. This should be distinguished from unrestricted supernatural Soul Manipulation; Lily is confirmed to administer the system, classify Souls and organize punishment, but the checked chapters do not establish that she personally possesses every metaphysical authority [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] or dedicated Death Gods use over Souls.
- Soul Classification: Lily participates in determining how condemned Souls should be processed and distributed through the infernal system.[20]
- Punishment Assignment: She oversees systems assigning appropriate punishment to Souls sent into the sinner Hells.
- Infernal Law Knowledge: Her position requires familiarity with the laws and standards determining which Souls belong in the punishment sectors.
- Soul Containment Administration: Lily manages sectors designed to hold enormous populations of deceased Souls over extended periods.
- Afterlife-System Administration: She operates as one of the high-ranking administrators responsible for keeping Victor's infernal afterlife functioning.[20]
- Mass-Soul Processing: During the enormous Soul influx of Chapter 953, Lily and the other Hell authorities were required to process populations of dead Souls on a scale vastly beyond the original capacity of the old system.[20]
- Crisis Management: Lily recognized when the volume of Souls exceeded the resources under her immediate control and summoned [[Persephone Elderblood]], [[Aline Elderblood Valefar]] and [[Lilith Elderblood]] rather than allowing the system to fail.[20]
- Automation and System Design Knowledge: Lily participated in an infernal administration that increasingly separated routine Souls through predetermined standards while preserving exceptional cases for individual judgment.[20]

##### Close-Quarters Combat
Lily is a capable direct combatant even though her greatest strengths are political intelligence, Dark Light and administration. During [[Bael]]'s defeat she and Helena attacked the First Duke at close range, physically piercing his body and restraining him while Lily increased Dark Light around the target.[9]
- Hand-to-Hand Combat: Lily is capable of fighting directly at close range.
- Claw / Hand Combat: She can use supernatural physical strength and natural weaponry to pierce powerful Demonic bodies.
- Energy-Reinforced Combat: Dark Light can be combined directly with physical restraint or close-range attacks.[9]
- Aerial Combat: Her former Succubus wings and current True Dragon physiology allow Lily to fight with unrestricted three-dimensional movement.
- Ambush Tactics: Lily willingly exploits deception, betrayal, positioning and surprise instead of limiting herself to formal one-on-one combat.
- Coordinated Combat: She worked effectively with Helena while attacking Bael and understood how to exploit [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood once it entered their target.[9]
- Combat Pragmatism: Lily possesses no ideological objection to attacking an opponent while they are disadvantaged if doing so produces the most efficient victory.
Bael's death should not be treated as proof that pre-Dragon Lily individually surpassed him. Lily attacked with Helena during an internal betrayal, Dark Light possessed a natural advantage against Demons, and Victor ultimately performed the decisive consumption process.[9]

##### Political Intelligence and Manipulation
Lily is one of the most politically capable Demons serving [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Her rapid rise from [[Bael]]'s politically useful wife to one of Victor's Generals resulted primarily from her ability to understand changing systems of authority before the rest of Hell's ancient nobility accepted that the old order was already dead.
- Political Adaptability: Lily recognized almost immediately that Victor's conquest had rendered the old Pillar hierarchy obsolete and repositioned herself before loyalty to Bael or [[Diablo]] could destroy her.[23]
- Political Strategy: She deliberately accumulated accomplishments under Victor's meritocratic government until her promotion could be justified through results rather than ancestry.
- Propaganda: Lily actively contributed to the growth of Victor's reputation among the population of Hell and encouraged descriptions that reinforced his image as its overwhelming new ruler.[23]
- Public-Opinion Manipulation: Lily understood the political effect produced when the population saw [[Lucifer]]'s daughter apparently place Victor's law above the protection of her own son.[18]
- Social Manipulation: She possesses an excellent understanding of greed, fear, ambition, pride, Bloodline obsession and political self-interest within Demonic society.
- Psychological Insight: Lily can identify the motivations driving individual Demons and exploit those motivations during negotiations or intimidation.
- Maternal Manipulation: Lily understands Luka's pride in Lucifer and Lilith's Bloodline and deliberately uses that heritage when attempting to force him to recognize his failures.[1]
- Political Ambition: When Victor offered her a substantial reward, Lily requested political authority rather than raw power, eventually obtaining the position of fourth General.[18][9]
- Long-Term Planning: Lily abandoned the political security attached to Bael only after creating an independent route into Victor's government, preventing the destruction of the old hierarchy from leaving her powerless.
- Deception and Betrayal: She successfully concealed the full extent of her alignment with Victor until the moment when betraying Bael became strategically decisive.[9]

##### Leadership and Generalship
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] formally appointed Lily his fourth General after she helped eliminate [[Bael]], granting her military and administrative authority independent of the status she had once borrowed from her marriage.[9]
- Generalship: Lily commands Demonic personnel and military assets under Victor's government.
- Demonic Command: She possesses sufficient rank to issue orders to subordinate Demons throughout the sectors under her authority.
- Territorial Administration: Lily manages significant infernal territory rather than functioning exclusively as a battlefield officer.
- Sinner-Hell Authority: She possesses administrative authority over the sectors where condemned Souls are punished.[19][20]
- Large-Scale Organization: Lily manages personnel, punishment infrastructure, condemned Souls and specialized executors across multiple infernal sectors.

- Imperial Military Role: In the later era, Victor's Demonic Dragon Goddess wives function among the powerful forces available to the Empire and Hell.[4]
- Infernal Conquest: During Victor's consolidation of Hell, Lily conquered dangerous regions including the Fields of Hatred, Famine, Destruction and Fear, territories associated with the production of unusually dangerous Infernal Demons.[17]

##### Demonic Nobility and Historical Knowledge
As the daughter of [[Lucifer]] and Lilith and former wife of the First Duke, Lily possesses knowledge accumulated through millennia at the highest levels of Hell's society.
- Ars Goetia Knowledge: Lily understands the old Pillar hierarchy, its ranks, political relationships and important Bloodlines.
- Ancient Demonic Bloodline Knowledge: She possesses extensive familiarity with the lineages descending from Hell's oldest rulers and nobles.
- Lucifer-Era Knowledge: Lily possesses historical knowledge concerning the order established during and after her father's rule.

- [[Diablo]]'s Government Knowledge: Lily understood how Diablo's hierarchy operated and how its collapse would affect individual nobles.
- Demonic Social Customs: She understands the significance of rank, marriage, Bloodline, personal power and political protection in ancient Hell.

- Infernal Geography: Her later conquests and administrative responsibilities require extensive practical familiarity with regions throughout [[Biblical Hell]].[17]

##### Current Power and Scaling
Before becoming a Blood Dragon, Lily was already an ancient high-ranking Succubus with one of Hell's most prestigious Bloodlines, a uniquely anti-Demonic supernatural ability and sufficient competence to operate within the highest political level of Demonic society. Her pre-Dragon combat capabilities should nevertheless not be exaggerated solely from [[Bael]]'s defeat. That confrontation involved betrayal, Helena's assistance, Dark Light's advantageous Holy component and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood entering Bael's body before Victor completed the kill.[9]
Lily's current state is enormously superior to her former Succubus incarnation. She now possesses the body, Soul, Energy system, racial compatibility and supernatural potential of a genuine Blood Dragon while retaining her Demonic Essence and Dark Light.[2][4] The Blood Dragon transformations specifically established that the women's previous abilities were not merely retained unchanged; existing Bloodlines and supernatural characteristics could be reconstructed into more powerful Draconic forms.[3] Lily should therefore be considered dramatically stronger than the General first appointed during the Demonic War, although the novel has not isolated enough individual late-era combat feats to assign her a precise destructive tier independently from the other Demonic Dragon Goddesses.

#### Resistances
Lily's current resistances are substantially greater than those of her former Succubus body because they now operate through complete Blood Dragon physiology.
- Physical Damage Resistance: True Dragon physiology possesses extreme resistance to conventional physical damage.[2]
- Energy Resistance: Her Blood Dragon body can survive and contain supernatural Energy far beyond the limits of ordinary Demons.
- Miasma Resistance: Lily's original Demonic nature is naturally compatible with infernal Miasma.
- Demonic-Energy Resistance: As a former ancient Demon whose characteristics remain integrated into her Dragon body, Lily possesses strong compatibility with Demonic Energy.
- Environmental Resistance: True Dragon bodies can function under supernatural and environmental conditions far beyond those survivable by ordinary species.
- Soul Resilience: Lily possesses a True Dragon Soul, giving her spiritual existence a vastly stronger foundation than an ordinary mortal Soul.[2]
- Age Resistance / Agelessness: Lily survived for millennia as a Demon and currently possesses the enormous lifespan of a True Dragon.
- Pain Tolerance: Her ancient Demonic background, combat experience and extreme familiarity with torture give Lily exceptional practical tolerance and understanding of pain.
- Vampiric Weakness Resistance: Blood Dragon physiology negates most conventional Vampire vulnerabilities despite incorporating the Noble Vampire racial foundation.[14]
- Sunlight Immunity (Blood Dragon Baseline): Full [[Blood Dragons]] do not retain sunlight as a meaningful ordinary Vampire weakness.[14]
- Anti-Vampire Weapon Resistance: Weapons designed solely to exploit conventional Vampire biology are far less effective against a Race whose body is fundamentally Draconic.[14]
- [[Natural Energy]] Compatibility: [[True Dragons]] possess such broad compatibility with Energy that simple Energy manipulation is an innate biological function rather than something inherently harmful to their bodies.[7]

#### Weaknesses and Limitations
- Anti-Dragon Effects: Lily is fundamentally a True Dragon, meaning weapons, abilities and properties specifically designed to exploit Dragonkind can potentially affect her more efficiently than ordinary attacks.[14]
- Dark Light Is Not Universal Holy Power: Dark Light contains traces of Holy Light but does not grant Lily the complete power set of an original Angel, Seraph or Creator God.[1]
- Dark Light's Special Advantage Is Demon-Specific: Its exceptional effectiveness is directly demonstrated against Demons and should not automatically be applied to unrelated species.
- Unknown Personal Divinity: Lily is a Dragon Goddess, but her individual Divine Concept has not yet been identified. Torture, Punishment, Dark Light and Hell should not be listed as her Divinities without explicit confirmation.
- Advanced Draconic Rune Mastery Unconfirmed: Lily possesses the racial foundation and basic Rune capability of a Blood Dragon, but she has not demonstrated mastery comparable to dedicated specialists such as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] or [[Scathach]].[5]
- Individual Dragon Breath Unknown: Lily possesses the racial ability to use Dragon Breath, but its exact Element and special properties have not been described.
- Current Demonic Reconstitution Unconfirmed: Lily possessed the ordinary reconstitution mechanics of a full Demon before her transformation, but whether that exact method remains active after her Soul and Race became fundamentally Draconic has not been individually established.
- [[Bael]] Scaling Requires Context: Lily helped defeat Bael through a coordinated betrayal alongside Helena while possessing a natural anti-Demon advantage; the event does not prove pre-Dragon Lily was independently superior to the First Duke.[9]
