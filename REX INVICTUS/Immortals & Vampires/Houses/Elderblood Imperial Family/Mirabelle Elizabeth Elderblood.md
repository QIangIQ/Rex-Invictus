### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 1146
That's true, but as friends of our beloved father, shouldn't they be strong?
—Mirabelle to Leone, Chapter 1146
Mirabelle Elizabeth Elderblood is a recurring character, an Imperial Princess of the [[Elderblood Empire]], and the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]]. She represents the direct union of Victor's Blood Dragon lineage with the ancient [[Lykos Clan]], possessing the complete foundation of a True Dragon while physically inheriting many of the characteristic traits associated with Maya's family.[1]
Mirabelle first appears during the Elderblood Empire's 100,000th Imperial Year, when Victor and Violet descend through their avatars and participate in a massive Imperial parade broadcast throughout the civilization. Rather than joining the formal procession, Maya, Leona and several members of the Lykos family spend part of the event inside a bar in [[Nightingale]] with [[Andrew Walter]], [[Edward Jonathan Elizabeth Lykos]], [[Frederick Winter]], [[Adam William Lykos]] and other people who had known Victor long before he became Emperor.[1]
Her debut focuses less upon combat and more upon the enormous difference between Victor's new generation and the people who knew him during his Human life. Mirabelle is already a fully developed Dragon Goddess with extraordinary perception, yet she is genuinely confused that several of the men Victor considers close friends appear so weak. Rather than immediately conclude that Victor's judgment is wrong, she assumes there must be something about them she has failed to understand.[1]
The scene also establishes one of the most important traits shared by Victor's biological daughters: their deep-violet eyes possess a supernatural perceptual ability derived from Victor. Mirabelle can evaluate the apparent strength of other Beings and is confident enough in her perception to reject the possibility that Andrew, Edward and Frederick are secretly hiding enormous power directly in front of her. The narration explains that all of Victor's daughters inherit some version of these eyes, although the starting strength differs from daughter to daughter and can continue developing through training.[1]
Unlike several of Victor's more openly chaotic daughters, Mirabelle appears relatively composed and well-mannered. Her immense bias toward Victor remains obvious, but she does not openly insult his friends even when she privately considers them unimpressive. The chapter specifically notes that Victor's daughters were properly raised by their mothers and did not behave like spoiled children despite possessing extraordinary power and a nearly fanatical admiration for their father.[1]

### Physical Appearance
Mirabelle is a tall, physically imposing and highly athletic woman standing approximately 182 centimeters tall. She possesses the tanned complexion characteristic of Maya, short snow-white hair reaching approximately to her neck and the deeply violet eyes inherited by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters.[1]
Her physique strongly reflects the women of the [[Lykos Clan]]. Mirabelle has an athletic, heavily developed body with clearly defined abdominal muscles, including the pronounced six-pack described as common among Lykos women. Compared with her sister Leone, Mirabelle possesses a noticeably more voluptuous build while remaining slightly shorter.[1]
Although her overall appearance strongly favors Maya's side of the family, Victor's influence is immediately recognizable through her violet eyes and extraordinary beauty. Andrew explicitly remarks upon how striking Victor's daughters are, although his poorly worded compliment immediately earns threatening looks from several members of the Lykos family.[1]
The violet coloration of Mirabelle's eyes is more than a cosmetic inheritance. Victor's daughters possess supernatural visual perception through these eyes, allowing them to observe aspects of Reality and other Beings that would remain concealed from ordinary eyesight.[1]

### Personality
Mirabelle is calm, respectful, observant and strongly family-oriented.
Her first interaction with Maya demonstrates immediate respect toward her mother. When Maya greets her after she enters the bar, Mirabelle simply answers "Yes, Mother" and later follows Maya and Leona without argument when told to sit with the rest of the family.[1]
This obedience should not be confused with passivity. Mirabelle possesses considerable confidence in herself and in the abilities inherited through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. When Leone proposes that Victor's apparently weak friends may secretly be concealing their strength, Mirabelle seriously considers the possibility before rejecting it because she believes their level is too low to successfully hide something from her perception.[1]
Importantly, she does recognize that beings much stronger than herself could potentially defeat or deceive her senses. Mirabelle uses Maya as the example of someone powerful enough that concealment would be believable. Her confidence is therefore not presented as the assumption that her perception is absolutely infallible; she simply considers Victor's old friends far below the level required to fool her in that manner.[1]
Mirabelle also possesses the extreme bias toward Victor common among his daughters.
Her confusion about Andrew, Edward and Frederick does not begin from the idea that the three men are objectively unimpressive. The contradiction that bothers her is more specific: how could apparently weak people have earned the friendship of Victor Elderblood?[1]
To Mirabelle, Victor's status makes association with him an implicit sign that somebody should possess exceptional qualities.
When the men fail to display extraordinary strength, she begins searching for an explanation rather than simply lowering her opinion of her father.
Leone eventually suggests the most obvious answer: if Victor values them, then they must possess something special even if that quality is not raw supernatural power.
Mirabelle accepts that possibility.[1]
This willingness to reconsider her initial judgment distinguishes Mirabelle's pride from simple arrogance. She begins with a strong assumption, analyzes contradictory evidence and changes her conclusion when another explanation becomes reasonable.
Her behavior toward Victor's friends is similarly restrained. Mirabelle and Leone conduct their entire discussion mentally rather than saying anything insulting aloud. The narration explicitly notes that Victor's daughters were properly educated by their mothers and do not act like spoiled children merely because they were born overwhelmingly powerful.[1]
The scene contains an additional irony: Mirabelle and Leone believe the conversation to be private, but Maya and Leona can hear everything they are saying. Their mothers simply exchange amused looks at the daughters' extreme bias toward Victor rather than interrupting them.[1]

### Synopsis

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Mirabelle makes her debut during the celebrations surrounding the 100,000th year of the [[Imperial Calendar]].
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Violet descend through avatars to participate in an enormous parade alongside the Empire's military forces. The event is broadcast throughout every Imperial world, serving simultaneously as a celebration of the civilization's first hundred millennia and a demonstration of the power Victor has accumulated during the accelerated-development project.[1]
While the Imperial capital celebrates, several figures from Victor's Human-era social circle watch the parade from a bar in [[Nightingale]].
[[Andrew Walter]], Edward, [[Frederick Winter]], [[Adam William Lykos]] and [[Johnny Lykos]] behave much as they had tens of thousands of years earlier, insulting one another, telling old jokes and arguing despite the civilization around them having transformed almost beyond recognition.[1]
[[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]], [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]], [[Bellatrix Lykos]] and [[Leone Elena Luna Elderblood]] eventually enter the establishment.
Mirabelle arrives shortly afterward.
Maya immediately greets her daughter warmly.
"Mira, you're here."
"Yes, Mother."[1]
The narration formally identifies Mirabelle as the daughter of Maya and Victor and describes the Lykos characteristics she inherited. Like Leone, she has an athletic body, tanned skin, white hair and the characteristic physique of the Lykos women, though Mirabelle is more voluptuous and stands slightly shorter at approximately 182 centimeters.[1]
Andrew openly comments upon the beauty of Victor's daughters.
The compliment almost becomes dangerous because Edward, Adam and Johnny interpret his wording as potentially implying that Maya is unattractive. Andrew immediately backtracks and argues that Victor's genetics simply enhanced what was already present.[1]
Frederick makes the situation worse by commenting upon Victor's appearance as a man.
Mirabelle, Leone, Maya and Leona immediately scrutinize the group with the same threatening expression commonly associated with the Lykos men when their family is discussed improperly.
Leona ends the confrontation and tells the daughters to sit.
Mirabelle obediently joins the family while everyone watches the parade.[1]
The older generation begins reminiscing about Victor.
Their conversation exposes a side of her father that Mirabelle's generation never personally witnessed: the sickly Human boy, the troublesome child, the young man who had friends before Vampire society, Divinity and the [[Elderblood Empire]] existed.
Mirabelle and Leone remain largely silent because it is their first time personally meeting these people.[1]
Eventually Mirabelle mentally contacts her sister.
"They seem weak."[1]
Leone replies that nearly everyone appears weak compared with Victor's daughters.
Mirabelle accepts that point but remains dissatisfied.
If the men are genuinely among their beloved father's closest friends, she believes they should themselves possess considerable strength.
Leone suggests they could be concealing it.
Mirabelle immediately questions the possibility.
Her perception does not reveal anything suggesting hidden power. She admits that Maya could probably conceal her strength from them because their mother is genuinely powerful, but considers Andrew, Edward and Frederick far too weak to perform the same feat.[1]
Leone offers a different explanation: perhaps what makes them special has nothing to do with strength.
They are Victor's friends.
For Leone, that fact alone means there must be something about them worthy of recognition.
Mirabelle eventually concedes that this is possible.[1]
The narration then explains the supernatural eyes inherited throughout Victor's second generation.
Every one of Victor's daughters possesses some degree of perceptual ability connected to her violet eyes. The strength at birth varies considerably, with some daughters beginning exceptionally gifted and others possessing weaker initial versions, but every daughter can strengthen the ability through later development and training.[1]
These eyes resemble Victor's own and are described as capable of piercing the truth of the world, making concealment exceptionally difficult.
Mirabelle's confidence when evaluating Victor's friends is therefore based upon an inherited supernatural sense rather than simple visual estimation.[1]
Unknown to Mirabelle and Leone, their mental conversation is not actually hidden from Maya and Leona.
Both mothers hear the exchange and become amused by how intensely their daughters idealize Victor.
They also recognize that Mirabelle and Leone are not unusual among their sisters; many of Victor's daughters would have reacted almost identically.[1]
The gathering later turns toward [[Scathach]] when Johnny asks Leona whether the Supreme Commander could train his children.
Mirabelle remains present as the older generation discusses Scathach's famously brutal standards, continuing the chapter's central contrast between the comparatively ordinary relationships of Victor's Human-era friends and the supernatural civilization inherited by his descendants.[1]
The currently reviewed late-chapter chronology does not provide another Mirabelle-focused scene of comparable length after Chapter 1146. Her individual Divinity, specialized combat style and eventual Imperial position therefore remain unrevealed rather than being filled through inference.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Mirabelle's biological father and the individual she admires most.
Mirabelle's debut occurs while Victor is participating in the hundred-thousand-year Imperial parade, meaning her first on-screen characterization develops almost entirely through how she evaluates people connected to him.[1]
She has difficulty understanding how Andrew, Edward and Frederick could be close friends of Victor because none appears remotely comparable to the supernatural standard Victor represents.
The issue is not contempt for people weaker than herself in general.
Mirabelle's reasoning is that anyone personally valued by her father should logically be exceptional in some manner.
When raw strength fails to explain the friendship, she begins searching for another quality that could.[1]
This reflects the extreme admiration Victor's children possess for him. Mirabelle instinctively assumes that the contradiction lies in her incomplete understanding of his friends rather than in Victor having poor judgment.

#### Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood
[[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] is Mirabelle's biological mother.
Mirabelle inherits a large portion of her visible appearance from Maya, including her tanned complexion, white hair and athletic Lykos physique.[1]
Their first interaction establishes an affectionate but respectful relationship. Maya greets her daughter gently, and Mirabelle immediately answers respectfully before following her mother's instructions.
Mirabelle also considers Maya one of the standards by which genuinely powerful Beings should be judged.
When discussing whether [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s friends might be suppressing their strength, she admits that Maya could potentially hide information from her perception because Maya is powerful enough to make such deception believable.[1]
Maya is also directly involved in Mirabelle's upbringing. Chapter 1146 states that Maya's priorities during the mature Imperial era increasingly revolve around family projects and the education of her daughters, reflecting the calmer life she developed after joining Victor.[1]

#### Leone Elena Luna Elderblood
[[Leone Elena Luna Elderblood]] is one of Mirabelle's closest known sisters within the Lykos branch of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family.
The two strongly resemble the women of the [[Lykos Clan]], although Leone is slightly taller while Mirabelle possesses the more voluptuous physique.[1]
Their first demonstrated interaction is a silent mental discussion concerning Victor's Human-era friends.
Mirabelle initially focuses upon the apparent contradiction between the men's weakness and their friendship with Victor, while Leone offers alternative explanations.
Neither sister simply accepts the other's conclusion automatically. They exchange observations, possibilities and counterarguments until Mirabelle accepts that Victor's friends may possess qualities unrelated to combat strength.[1]
Their mothers can hear the supposedly private conversation, something the sisters apparently fail to notice.

#### Victor's Human-Era Friends
Chapter 1146 is Mirabelle's first meeting with several people connected to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s life before his transformation into the figure she knows as the Dragon Emperor.
[[Andrew Walter]], [[Edward Jonathan Elizabeth Lykos]] and [[Frederick Winter]] initially confuse her because they fail to match the image Mirabelle associates with people worthy of standing close to Victor.
Her supernatural perception tells her that they are weak compared with the standards of her own generation.[1]
Mirabelle briefly considers the possibility that they are hiding their power but ultimately rejects it.
Leone instead proposes that the qualities Victor values in them may have nothing to do with combat.
Mirabelle accepts that possibility rather than openly disrespecting the men.[1]
The encounter exposes Mirabelle to an important part of Victor's history: not every person he values was chosen because of strength, Divine authority, political usefulness or supernatural potential.
Some relationships survived simply because they existed before any of those things mattered.

### Skills & Abilities
- Blood Dragon Goddess Physiology: Mirabelle is the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]], possessing a complete Blood Dragon and True Dragon foundation from birth.[1]
- True Dragon Body: Mirabelle possesses the biological foundation of a genuine True Dragon rather than a lesser Draconic hybrid.
- True Dragon Soul: Her spiritual existence possesses the foundation characteristic of Victor's Blood Dragon descendants.
- [[Dragon Heart]]: As a complete Blood Dragon, Mirabelle possesses the supernatural Energy-generating system characteristic of [[True Dragons]].
- [[Draconic Energy]]: Her body naturally generates and circulates the Energy associated with Dragonkind.
- Enhanced Physical Abilities: Mirabelle possesses the immense strength, speed, durability, stamina and reflexes expected from Victor's naturally born Blood Dragon daughters.
- Enhanced Physique: Her body also expresses the highly athletic physical characteristics associated with the women of the [[Lykos Clan]], including exceptionally developed musculature and abdominal definition.[1]
- Enhanced Senses: Mirabelle possesses supernatural Draconic perception in addition to the specialized visual ability inherited through Victor.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Her Blood Dragon foundation provides powerful supernatural regeneration.
- Extreme Longevity: Mirabelle's existence follows the lifespan and developmental standards of True Dragons rather than ordinary Humans.
- Draconic Shapeshifting: As a complete True Dragon, Mirabelle possesses the racial foundation for Draconic bodily transformation.
- Connection to Creation: Like other True Dragons, Mirabelle possesses the Race's natural compatibility with the powers and Energies of Creation.
- Draconic Language: Mirabelle possesses the racial foundation for the supernatural [[Will Solace|Will]]-bearing language of Dragons.
- Draconic Runes: Her True Dragon nature gives her compatibility with basic Draconic Runic principles. No Mirabelle-specific advanced Runemaster feat is currently demonstrated.
- Dragon Breath: Mirabelle possesses the racial foundation for Dragon Breath, although the exact properties of her personal Breath have not been revealed.
- Deep-Violet Draconic Eyes / Supernatural Perception: Mirabelle possesses the unusual perceptual ability inherited by all of Victor's daughters through their deep-violet eyes.[1]
- Truth-Piercing Perception: The eyes are described as resembling Victor's own and possessing the ability to pierce through the truth of the world.[1]
- Extrasensory Perception: Mirabelle can perceive information concerning another Being that cannot be determined through ordinary eyesight alone.[1]
- Power Detection: She can evaluate the apparent supernatural strength of people around her and immediately recognizes that Victor's old friends are weak compared with herself and her family.[1]
- Comparative Power Assessment: Mirabelle can compare the apparent strength of different individuals rather than merely sensing whether supernatural Energy is present.
- Concealment Piercing: She believes a person would require considerable power to successfully hide their true strength from the perception of Victor's daughters.[1]
- Detection Confidence: Mirabelle considers Andrew, Edward and Frederick too weak to conceal a hidden superior level directly in front of her.
- Recognition of Superior Concealment: She nevertheless admits that someone on Maya's level could plausibly hide information from her, establishing that Mirabelle does not consider the ability absolutely infallible.[1]
- Trainable Perception: The inherited eye ability is not static. The narration explicitly states that every daughter can improve it through training, regardless of how strong or weak the ability is at birth.[1]
- Variable Inherited Potency: Different daughters begin with different levels of perceptual ability; birth determines the starting point rather than the maximum level.[1]
- Elderblood Family Telepathy: Mirabelle can communicate mentally with members of her family.[1]
- Silent Mental Communication: She and Leone hold a complete conversation concerning Victor's friends without verbally speaking.[1]
- Thought Exchange: Mirabelle can transmit questions, judgments and responses directly through the family mental connection.
- Family-Network Limitation: Maya and Leona can hear the daughters' supposedly private exchange, demonstrating that the conversation is not automatically hidden from stronger or appropriately connected family members.[1]
- Analytical Ability: Mirabelle demonstrates the ability to compare evidence against her expectations rather than rigidly accepting her first impression.[1]
- Hypothesis Testing: When Victor's friends appear unexpectedly weak, Mirabelle considers Leone's suggestion that they may be suppressing their real strength.
- Evidence-Based Rejection: She rejects that explanation only after comparing it against what her perception actually detects.
- Alternative-Explanation Acceptance: Mirabelle ultimately accepts that the men may possess qualities valuable to Victor that have nothing to do with supernatural combat power.[1]
- Emotional Discipline / Etiquette: Despite privately possessing strong prejudices regarding Victor's friends, Mirabelle does not openly insult them.[1]
- Imperial Upbringing: Chapter 1146 explicitly notes that Victor's daughters were properly raised by their mothers and did not behave like spoiled children despite their extraordinary status and power.[1]
- Respect for Maternal Authority: Mirabelle immediately follows Maya and Leona's directions during the gathering.
- Divinity (Unidentified): Mirabelle is a Dragon Goddess, but the currently reviewed material does not reveal the canonical name, nature or applications of her individual Divinity. No specific Divine Concept should be assigned until the novel directly identifies it.

### Limitations
- Perception Is Not Omniscience: The statement that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters possess eyes capable of piercing the truth of the world describes an extraordinarily powerful supernatural perception, but it should not automatically be interpreted as literal knowledge of everything in existence.[1]
- Concealment Can Remain Possible: Mirabelle herself acknowledges that someone sufficiently powerful, such as Maya, could plausibly conceal information from her perception.[1]
- Power Detection ≠ Exact Numerical Scaling: Mirabelle can recognize comparative differences in strength, but Chapter 1146 does not establish that she can assign exact numerical values to another Being's complete combat potential.
- Truth-Piercing Sight ≠ Anna's <Truth> Authority: Mirabelle's eye ability is perceptual. It should not be confused with [[Anna Elderblood]]'s conceptual Truth powers or Stella's Falsehood-related authority.
- Lykos Heritage ≠ Confirmed Werewolf Powers: Mirabelle visibly inherited numerous traits from Maya and the Lykos family, but the reviewed chapter does not individually show her using Werewolf transformation or another specifically Werewolf racial technique.
- Unknown Divinity: Mirabelle's status as a Dragon Goddess does not justify inventing a Divine Concept without a direct statement.
- No Confirmed Limitless Growth: The generic claim sometimes applied to Victor's daughters that their Divinities possess automatically limitless growth has not been added because Chapter 1146 does not establish such an ability for Mirabelle.
