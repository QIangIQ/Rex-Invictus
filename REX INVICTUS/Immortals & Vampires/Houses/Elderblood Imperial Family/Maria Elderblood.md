### Appearances

#### Debut
[[Ch16 - Rex Invictus IX|Chapter 16]]
Maria Elderblood is a former Inquisition hunter, the Queen of Ghouls, one of [[Victor Elderblood]]'s personal Battle Maids, his wife and a member of Clan Elderblood. Originally a Human hunter partnered with her childhood companion and lover [[Carlos Reiss]], Maria participated in the attack that massacred Sasha Fulger's household and killed Julia. Sasha retaliated by turning Maria into a defective Vampire known as a Ghoul, intending to use her as both a servant and part of her revenge against Carlos.[1]
Maria's forced existence among Vampires gradually destroyed the worldview taught to her by the Church. She came to recognize that the supernatural beings she had hunted were capable of ordinary relationships and emotions, while the organization she had trusted was capable of atrocities no different from those it attributed to monsters.[2] Her final break with her former life came when Carlos rejected her as a monster and attempted to kill her. Victor Elderblood saved Maria, repaired the defective nature of her Ghoul body through his blood and transformed her into a complete Noble Vampire without erasing the powers she had developed as a Ghoul. Instead, the two aspects merged, allowing Maria to retain and later develop the authority of a Ghoul Queen.[3][4][5]
From that point onward Maria became fiercely loyal to Victor and established herself as one of his most reliable Battle Maids. She was eventually accepted permanently into Victor's Clan and family, became his wife and later evolved again into a True Blood Dragon. Her Draconic transformation further enhanced her unique Ghoul characteristics until she became capable of directly creating Ghouls from corpses, an ability explicitly described as approaching domains normally associated with Death and Souls.[6] In the later Elderblood era, Maria and Victor also have a biological daughter, [[Melina Elderblood]].[7]

### Physical Description

#### Human
Maria was a tall young woman standing approximately 178 cm, possessing a lean and athletic physique developed through years of training as a Hunter. She had blonde hair and sapphire-blue eyes. Her combat style emphasized precision, agility and the manipulation of supernatural threads rather than overwhelming physical strength, complementing Carlos, who acted as the physically stronger member of their partnership.[2]

#### Ghoul
After Sasha transformed her into a Ghoul, Maria's appearance deteriorated alongside her defective physiology. Her complexion became unnaturally pale with a grayish shade and dark veins became increasingly visible beneath her skin, physical signs of the gradual decay associated with Ghouls. Although she possessed Vampiric traits and regeneration, the transformation was incomplete and her body lacked the stability of a true Noble Vampire.[1][2]

#### Noble Vampire
Maria underwent her second major physical transformation after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] used his own blood to reconstruct her defective existence. Her decaying Ghoul body was stabilized and transformed into that of a complete Noble Vampire while retaining the supernatural characteristics she had developed as a Ghoul. Her hair became longer and took on a more vivid golden coloration, her unhealthy pallor disappeared, her figure developed into its physical peak and she became somewhat taller. Most visibly, her original sapphire-blue eyes permanently changed to the red eyes characteristic of Victor's Vampiric lineage.[5]
Unlike an ordinary Vampire conversion, Victor's intervention did not simply erase Maria's previous Race. The Ghoul characteristics already embedded in her existence merged with her new Noble Vampire state, causing Maria to become an unusual hybrid-like existence capable of continuing to use and develop Ghoul powers while possessing the stability and potential of a proper Vampire.[5]

#### Blood Dragon
Maria later underwent another complete racial evolution when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] transformed members of his family into [[Blood Dragons]]. Her Vampire foundation was reconstructed around a genuine True Dragon existence while her individual Ghoul characteristics remained part of her new nature rather than being discarded. Like the other women transformed alongside her, Maria developed Draconic horns and wings corresponding to her hair coloration and gained the superior body, Soul and supernatural potential of a True Dragon.[6]
Her transformation also caused her Ghoul Queen abilities to evolve dramatically. Instead of merely infecting living targets through the methods available to her previous Ghoul existence, Maria became capable of creating Ghouls directly from corpses, demonstrating an interaction with death and the Soul that had not been available to her before becoming a Blood Dragon.[6]

### Personality and Traits
Maria was originally a disciplined, serious and pragmatic woman shaped by poverty and her upbringing within the Inquisition. She rarely displayed unnecessary emotion and approached hunting supernatural beings primarily as a profession rather than a personal crusade. Unlike fanatical members of the Church, Maria later admitted that she and Carlos held no particular hatred toward Vampires; hunting was simply the work that allowed two impoverished orphans to survive and eventually escape the streets.[2]
Her pragmatic nature also made Maria more capable of questioning the Church than Carlos. After spending months living around Sasha, Ruby, Natalia and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household, Maria observed that Vampires possessed friendships, families, insecurities and ordinary emotions little different from Humans. This realization led her to question whether the creatures she and Carlos had killed were truly the indiscriminate "monsters" the Church had described. Maria never attempted to use her childhood as an excuse when explaining herself to Sasha; when Sasha asked whether the story was supposed to make her sympathize with Maria, Maria directly answered that it was not. She understood why Sasha hated her and accepted responsibility for participating in the attack that killed Julia.[2]
Her relationship with Sasha became increasingly contradictory. Maria initially existed as a prisoner and living object of revenge, yet months of serving Sasha created genuine familiarity between them. Sasha continued hating Maria for Julia's death but simultaneously became accustomed to her quiet companionship and eventually admitted that she did not actually want Maria to disappear. When Carlos attempted to kill Maria, Victor forced Sasha to confront those contradictory feelings rather than hiding behind hatred; Sasha ultimately chose to save the woman she had once intended to destroy.[3]
Carlos' betrayal broke the remaining connection Maria possessed to her former life. Having spent her childhood relying upon him, Maria was devastated when Carlos declared that the woman he loved had already died and attempted to "purify" what remained. After surviving because of Sasha and Victor, Maria ceased resisting what she had become and redirected the intensity once attached to Carlos and the Church toward her new family.[3][8]
Victor's transformation amplified this change. Maria developed an extreme devotion toward him, repeatedly referring to Victor as the person who gave her a new beginning and regarding him almost as her personal god. Unlike Bruna's openly religious worship, Maria's devotion is generally quieter and expressed through complete confidence, obedience and willingness to carry out Victor's orders. When Victor warned his Battle Maids that they needed armor to protect themselves from him during a dangerous situation, Maria immediately rejected the possibility that her Master would intentionally harm them.[9]
Maria also became substantially more ruthless after abandoning her former morality. Her treatment of the captive Carlos demonstrated a sadistic side that had previously been suppressed by her Human upbringing, and her later role as Ghoul Queen made her entirely comfortable with raising defeated enemies as members of an undead army. Despite this brutality toward enemies, she remains controlled, dependable and strongly protective toward Victor's household.

### History

#### Early Life
Maria and [[Carlos Reiss]] were both orphans raised in the impoverished areas of Camden, New Jersey. The two survived together on the streets from childhood, with Carlos frequently protecting Maria from people who attempted to exploit her. Even at approximately ten years old Carlos possessed physical abilities superior to ordinary Humans, an early manifestation of the potential that would later allow him to manipulate Faith Energy.[2]
Their circumstances forced the pair to survive however they could, and the opportunity offered by the Church eventually provided them with a way out of poverty. Both possessed enough spiritual potential to be trained as [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]], although Carlos possessed considerably greater raw affinity with Faith. Maria compensated through intelligence and technical skill. She specialized in supernatural threads capable of restraining Vampires and described their partnership simply: Maria was the brain while Carlos was the muscle.[2]
Maria later explained that neither of them originally possessed a personal hatred of supernatural beings. They treated service within the Inquisition as employment: the Church identified something as a monster, they hunted it, and in exchange they received the resources and stability they had lacked as children.[2]

### Synopsis

#### The Awakening
Maria first appeared as Carlos' partner during the Inquisition operation against [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]]. With Clan Fulger politically weakened after losing its Count status, the [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]] considered Sasha a vulnerable target and attacked her residence. The operation resulted in the slaughter of the household servants, including Julia, the woman Sasha regarded as her true mother. Maria used her supernatural threads to restrict Sasha while Carlos attempted to capture her.[1]
Sasha responded with overwhelming rage. Forcing her Lightning beyond what her body could comfortably withstand, she escaped the ambush and seized Maria. Rather than simply killing the Hunter, Sasha deliberately transformed her into a defective Vampire—a Ghoul—forcing Maria to become the very kind of supernatural creature she had spent her career hunting. Maria was subsequently taken back with Sasha and placed under her authority as a maid and prisoner.[1][10]

#### Vampire Society
While [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] trained in [[Nightingale]], Maria remained close to Sasha and gradually adapted to her unwanted supernatural existence. During a conversation with Sasha, Ruby and Natalia, Maria finally explained her childhood with Carlos and the circumstances that led them into the Inquisition. She emphasized that hunting had originally been employment rather than ideology and openly admitted that living among Vampires had begun changing her perception of them.[2]
Maria had expected Vampires to resemble the monsters described by the Church. Instead, she found individuals who lived, argued and cared for one another in ways essentially identical to Humans. The realization caused her to question whether she and Carlos could have chosen differently, marking the beginning of Maria's ideological separation from the Inquisition even though she remained deeply attached to Carlos.[2]

#### A New Pillar
Maria continued serving Sasha after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] returned to the Human World. Their relationship gradually became considerably more complicated than captor and prisoner. Sasha remained unable to forgive Maria for participating in Julia's death, yet Maria became a constant presence during months in which Victor was repeatedly occupied elsewhere, and Sasha unconsciously began relying upon that presence.[3]
The issue finally reached its breaking point when Maria obtained an opportunity to meet Carlos again. Despite knowing the danger, she still possessed enough attachment to her former lover to seek him out. Carlos' response destroyed whatever hope of reconciliation she retained. He rejected Maria's continued existence, insisting that the Human woman he loved was already dead and that the Ghoul before him was an abomination that needed to be purified.[3]
When Carlos attempted to kill Maria, Sasha initially claimed she did not care. Victor refused to accept the contradiction between her words and emotions and forced Sasha to state what she actually desired. Sasha finally admitted that while she still hated Maria for Julia's murder, she had grown accustomed to her and did not want to be left alone again. Victor consequently intervened and saved Maria, after which Sasha fed her blood to the critically wounded Ghoul to keep her alive.[3][4]
The confrontation permanently destroyed Maria's remaining loyalty to Carlos. Once he was captured, Maria participated personally in his punishment and abandoned the hesitation that had defined her divided loyalties. Victor's blood had already stabilized her enough to survive, but he went further and reconstructed her existence completely. Maria ceased being a defective Ghoul and became a complete Noble Vampire carrying Victor's blood.[8][5]
The transformation did not remove her previous Ghoul characteristics. Instead, Victor's blood merged the two states. Maria retained the potential associated with a Ghoul Queen while gaining a stable Vampire body, permanently red eyes and dramatically greater supernatural potential.[5]
From that point Maria regarded Victor as the person who had given her a genuine second life. Her loyalty shifted completely toward him and his household, and she became one of Victor's personal maids alongside Kaguya, Eve, Bruna and Roberta. By Chapter 315, Victor explicitly considered the women of his [[Maid Squad]] to be his own people and reacted violently to the idea of anyone harming them.[11]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Maria continued developing as one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Battle Maids throughout the political conflicts preceding the Demonic War. Her place within the household ceased being based solely upon Sasha's punishment; she fought for Victor, trained with the other maids and increasingly formed genuine relationships with the people surrounding him. Victor's own perception of Kaguya, Eve, Bruna, Roberta and Maria likewise changed until he no longer regarded them as replaceable servants but as precious members of his personal circle.[12]
This distinction became increasingly important as Victor's Clan expanded. The Battle Maids occupied the position closest to him after his wives and family, serving simultaneously as attendants, bodyguards, assassins and trusted operatives capable of acting upon Victor's personal authority.

#### Demonic War
During the Demonic War period, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] finally confronted the increasingly artificial distinction between his maids and his family. Maria, Kaguya, Bruna, Roberta and Eve had repeatedly risked their lives beside him and were already among the people he trusted most. Victor therefore offered the women a permanent place within his Clan and family rather than leaving their relationship defined merely by employment or servitude.[9][13]
Maria's response was characteristic of the devotion she had developed: she accepted the opportunity rather than hesitating over the enormous consequences of becoming permanently tied to Victor's lineage. The process deepened the relationship between the Battle Maids and their Master and prepared the foundation for their formal incorporation into what would eventually become Clan Elderblood.[14]
Maria's status as the Ghoul Queen also became militarily important. While discussing how to strengthen his forces, Victor personally assigned Maria responsibility for increasing their army. When asked what kind of force he intended to create, Victor identified Ghouls, immediately exciting Maria. At that point she already possessed supernatural beings captured during earlier operations and could use them as the beginning of a dedicated Ghoul force.[15]
Victor later summoned Maria together with Bruna and Roberta to formalize their position. The women were offered permanent inclusion within his Clan with full knowledge that Victor considered his Clan synonymous with his family rather than an ordinary political organization. Maria accepted and became a permanent member of Victor's bloodline and household.[16][17]
This marked the definitive end of Maria's life as Sasha's captured Hunter. The woman who had entered the family as an enemy was now one of the people permanently bound to its founder.

#### King Among Kings
Maria continued serving as a Battle Maid as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction evolved from a Vampire Clan into a multinational supernatural power. During Victor's campaign against the [[Greek Pantheon]], Maria joined Kaguya and the other Battle Maids when Victor released them against [[Olympus]]. He coated their weapons in his Blood to ensure that they possessed the capacity to permanently kill Divine opponents and ordered Kaguya to lead them against the Greek Gods.[18]
Maria's contribution was especially suited to large-scale warfare. As the Battle Maids tore through Olympus, fallen Gods and other supernatural opponents could be converted into Ghouls rather than merely left dead. During the assault, Gods were explicitly shown returning as Ghouls under the attacks of Victor's forces, demonstrating that Maria's Ghoul Queen authority was powerful enough to affect Divine beings once the difference in strength permitted it.[19]
By this stage Maria was no longer merely an individual combatant. Her personal abilities made defeated enemies potential additions to Victor's army, giving her strategic value fundamentally different from the straightforward destructive abilities of many of the other Battle Maids.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ascension and the continuing development of his Draconic Race, Maria was among the members of his family selected for complete Blood Dragon conversion. Together with Kaguya, Eve, Natalia, Roberta and numerous others, Maria was reconstructed as a genuine True Blood Dragon.[20]
The transformation preserved the individual characteristics she had accumulated throughout her previous evolutions. Maria did not become a generic Dragon who happened to remember being a Ghoul; her Ghoul Queen nature was incorporated into the foundation of her new Draconic existence. She developed Dragon horns and wings while her original supernatural abilities increased enormously in strength.[6]
Her Ghoul creation underwent one of the most important evolutions. Maria became capable of directly converting a corpse into a Ghoul without relying upon the previous infection process. The narration specifically describes this development as reaching areas normally accessible to beings possessing authority over Death and Souls, demonstrating that her old defective Ghoul physiology had ultimately become the foundation of one of her strongest Draconic characteristics.[6]
Despite the enormous change in Race and status, Maria remained one of Victor's maids and continued participating naturally in the family's less serious activities. During one of Victor's vacations, Maria and Bruna joined Roxanne in a soccer match against Kaguya, Ruby and Amara.[21]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
The enormous difference between external time and the accelerated environments available to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family eventually allowed centuries and millennia of subjective development to occur while only a comparatively short period passed outside. Maria matured alongside the rest of Clan Elderblood during this period, with her position evolving beyond that of maid and military subordinate into that of a wife and mother within Victor's enormous household.[22]
Maria and Victor eventually had a biological daughter, [[Melina Elderblood]]. Melina belongs to the later generation of Elderblood children born from Victor's Blood Dragon lineage and his wives after the family had already reached a vastly greater level of supernatural development.[7]
Maria therefore completed an extreme transformation over the course of the story: a Human orphan became an Inquisition Hunter; the Hunter became Sasha's defective Ghoul prisoner; the Ghoul became a complete Noble Vampire under Victor; the former enemy became a Battle Maid, Clan member and wife; the Ghoul Queen became a True Blood Dragon; and she ultimately became one of the mothers of the Elderblood dynasty.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
Maria's relationship with [[Victor Elderblood]] is the defining relationship of her second life. Victor originally represented the leader of the Vampires surrounding her captor, but gradually became the individual responsible for saving her from Carlos, correcting the defects of her Ghoul existence and giving her a place where she was no longer defined by the Church or her former lover.[3][5]
Maria's gratitude developed into extreme loyalty and eventually love. She regards Victor as the source of her new beginning and repeatedly demonstrates absolute trust in him, even when other people are concerned by his power or behavior. Victor reciprocates by treating Maria as one of his personal people rather than merely a servant. He later offers her permanent inclusion within his family, incorporates her into his lineage and eventually shares a daughter with her.[9][16][7]
Although Maria continues addressing Victor as her Master because of her identity within the [[Maid Squad]], their relationship eventually exists simultaneously as master and maid, Clan founder and Clan member, husband and wife, and parents of Melina.

#### Sasha Elderblood Fulger
[[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] began as Maria's victim before becoming her captor and first supernatural master. Maria participated in the attack that killed Julia and the remainder of Sasha's Human-world household, creating a hatred that Sasha could never simply erase.[1]
Nevertheless, their forced proximity created a strange bond. Maria served Sasha faithfully for months, and Sasha eventually became accustomed to the quiet presence of the woman she had originally intended to use only for revenge. When Carlos attempted to kill Maria, Sasha was forced to recognize that hatred and attachment could coexist; she still despised what Maria had done but no longer wanted her gone.[3]
Sasha's decision to save Maria became one of the decisive moments in Maria's life. Without Sasha admitting her true desire, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] would have had little reason to preserve the dying Ghoul. Their later relationship is consequently far more complicated than conventional friendship: Maria remains the woman partially responsible for Sasha's greatest childhood loss, but also became one of the servants and family members Sasha chose to keep beside her.

#### Carlos Reiss
[[Carlos Reiss]] was Maria's childhood companion, protector, partner and first lover. Having survived as orphans together in Camden, the two relied upon one another for years before entering the Inquisition. Carlos' greater physical power complemented Maria's tactical intelligence and thread-based combat style, making them an effective Hunter team.[2]
Maria continued loving Carlos even after Sasha transformed her into a Ghoul and initially hoped that he would still recognize the person she had always been. Carlos instead rejected her, declaring that the real Maria was dead and attempting to kill the supernatural being standing before him.[3]
The betrayal destroyed Maria's remaining emotional attachment to her former identity. After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] saved her, Maria eventually subjected the captured Carlos to her revenge and embraced the supernatural existence he had condemned. Carlos' rejection therefore ironically became one of the final events that drove Maria completely away from the Church and toward Victor.[8][5]

#### Maid Squad
Maria possesses a particularly close relationship with [[Kaguya Elderblood Blank]], [[Bruna Elderblood]], [[Eve Elderblood]] and [[Roberta Elderblood]], who fought beside her as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s primary Battle Maids. Despite their radically different backgrounds, the women share an intense loyalty toward Victor and repeatedly function as a coordinated combat and support unit.[11][9]
Their bond eventually transcended ordinary employment. Victor himself concluded that women who repeatedly placed their lives at risk for him could no longer reasonably be considered replaceable servants and incorporated them into his family.[23]

#### Melina Elderblood
[[Melina Elderblood]] is Maria and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s biological daughter. Her birth permanently establishes Maria as one of the mothers of the later Elderblood generation and represents the final reversal of Maria's original circumstances: the orphan who grew up without a family ultimately became part of one of the largest supernatural families in existence and created a family of her own.[7]

### Trivia
- Maria and Carlos were not originally fanatical Vampire hunters. Maria explicitly stated that they treated hunting as employment and possessed no personal grudge against Vampires before joining the Inquisition.[2]
- Maria was the tactical half of her partnership with Carlos. Because her Faith Energy was weaker than his, she specialized in supernatural threads and trapping techniques while Carlos supplied greater raw power.[2]
- Maria is one of the clearest examples of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s blood improving rather than erasing an abnormal pre-existing Race. His transformation corrected her defective Ghoul condition while allowing her Ghoul Queen characteristics to survive inside her Noble Vampire physiology.[5]
- Her later Blood Dragon transformation repeated the same principle on a higher level: rather than losing the Ghoul Queen abilities, those abilities became substantially stronger and developed an affinity approaching the domains of Death and Souls.[6]
- Maria's ability to create and command Ghouls makes her unusually valuable in prolonged warfare because fallen enemies can potentially become members of her own forces.[19][6]
- Maria is associated with the Sin of Gluttony among Victor's group of Deadly Sin-themed maids.
- During Victor's vacation, Roxanne, Maria and Bruna played a soccer match against Kaguya, Ruby and Amara.[21]
- Maria and Victor's daughter [[Melina Elderblood]] is one of the children born during the later Elderblood era.[7]

#### Skills and Abilities
Maria Elderblood has undergone several successive racial evolutions throughout the story. She began as a highly trained human Inquisition hunter specializing in wires and traps, was forcibly converted into a defective Vampire/Ghoul by [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]], was later restored and evolved by [[Victor Elderblood]] into a full-fledged Noble Vampire while retaining and merging her Ghoul Queen characteristics, and eventually became a Blood Dragon. Rather than losing her former abilities with each transformation, Maria's existing powers were repeatedly incorporated into her newer racial states, causing her original thread-based fighting style and Ghoul abilities to become substantially more powerful.[1][2][3]
- Blood Dragon Physiology: Maria's current Race is that of a Blood Dragon, the hybrid Race created by Victor from the complete integration of his Vampire Progenitor and Dragon characteristics. Her transformation further strengthened the powers she already possessed rather than replacing them, with Maria being specifically noted among the women whose primary abilities became considerably stronger following Dragonization.[3]
- Immense Physical Strength: Maria possesses physical power vastly superior to her former Human, Ghoul and Vampire states as a consequence of her Blood Dragon body.
- Superhuman Speed and Reflexes: Her Draconic physiology grants greatly enhanced movement, reaction speed and combat reflexes.
- Immense Durability: Maria's body possesses the natural toughness of a True Blood Dragon and can withstand levels of physical and supernatural force far beyond what her previous bodies could endure.
- Enhanced Agility: Her supernatural coordination and bodily control are dramatically superior to those of an ordinary Human.
- Enhanced Senses: Maria possesses the heightened sensory faculties inherited from both Vampires and Dragons.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: The regenerative characteristics she possessed as a Vampire were retained and strengthened by her later evolution into a Blood Dragon.
- [[Immortality]]/Longevity: Maria no longer ages according to ordinary Human limitations and possesses the supernatural longevity associated with her current Race.
- True Dragon Form: As a full Blood Dragon rather than a Blood Dragonoid, Maria possesses a genuine Draconic state. Following the mass Dragonization, she manifested the characteristic horns and wings associated with the transformed members of Victor's family.[3]
- Shapeshifting and Size Alteration: Like other [[True Dragons]], Maria can transition between a humanoid appearance and more overtly Draconic characteristics, with Dragon physiology permitting substantial alteration of bodily size and form.
- Flight: Maria possesses functional Draconic wings following her transformation and can move aerially alongside the other transformed members of Victor's family.[3]
- Dragon Breath: As a True Blood Dragon, Maria possesses the standard capacity of Dragons to expel supernatural power through a Breath attack. The specific elemental or conceptual nature of Maria's personal Breath has not yet been individually explained.
- True Dragon Soul: Maria's transformation reconstructed more than her physical body, granting her the spiritual foundation belonging to a genuine Dragon rather than merely a creature possessing Draconic traits.[3]
- [[Draconic Energy]]: Maria can utilize the tremendous supernatural Energy generated and supported by her Draconic existence, greatly amplifying the abilities inherited from her previous racial states.
- Draconic Runes: Full Dragons possess an instinctive relationship with the Draconic Language and its Runes. Consequently, Maria can understand and employ Draconic Runes considerably more naturally than beings who merely learn the system academically.[4]
- Divinity: Maria has reached a Divine/Dragon Goddess-level existence in the current era; however, her individual Divinity has not yet been explicitly named or explained. Her title as the Sin of Gluttony should therefore not automatically be treated as confirmation of a Gluttony Divinity.
- Noble Vampire Physiology: After Maria was mortally wounded and Sasha decided to save her, Victor used his blood to transform her into a genuine living Noble Vampire. Because a Ghoul is fundamentally a defective offshoot of Vampirism, Maria did not simply lose her Ghoul Queen characteristics when this happened. Her restored Vampire nature and her pre-existing Ghoul powers instead merged together into a new and stronger state.[2]
- Enhanced Physical Capabilities: Victor's blood dramatically strengthened Maria's body compared to her deteriorating Ghoul state. This enhancement later became part of the foundation of her Blood Dragon body.[2]
- Vampiric Regeneration: Becoming a full-fledged Vampire restored Maria from the degenerative condition of a Ghoul and granted the superior regenerative ability of Victor's lineage.[2]
- Blood Sustenance: Like other Vampires, Maria can recover and sustain herself through blood. Victor's blood in particular had an extraordinary strengthening and restorative influence upon her because he was the Vampire Progenitor responsible for recreating her.[2]
- Progenitor Blood Enhancement: Victor's influence as a Night King and Vampire Progenitor strongly affected Maria's second transformation, allowing her to become considerably more powerful than she had been as Sasha's defective Ghoul.[2]
- Bloodline Connection: Vampires personally created through Victor's Progenitor lineage possess a supernatural blood connection to their progenitor, allowing psychic communication across distance. Maria became one of Victor's direct lineage members when he reconstructed her as a Noble Vampire.[5]
- Death Conversion Recipient: Maria is the principal demonstrated example of Victor's ability to transform an already undead creature into a genuine living Noble Vampire. Although this is technically Victor's ability rather than Maria's, the result permanently altered Maria's racial composition and allowed her Ghoul and Vampire powers to coexist instead of one replacing the other.[5]
- Ghoul Queen Physiology: Sasha's imperfect transformation originally turned Maria into a Ghoul, an undead and defective form of Vampire. Maria eventually developed the characteristics of a superior Ghoul, and when Victor reconstructed her as a full Noble Vampire, those powers were not erased; because Ghouls originate as a Vampiric subspecies, the two racial powers merged successfully. Her later Blood Dragon transformation again preserved and enhanced this Ghoul-derived foundation.[1][2][3]
- Ghoul Queen Influence: Maria possesses a superior form of the authority ordinary Ghouls exercise through their racial instincts. This became one of the defining powers she retained through all of her later evolutions.
- Ghoulification: Maria can transform beings weaker than herself into Ghouls, creating undead subordinates connected to her Ghoul Queen authority.
- Ghoulification through Biting: The more conventional form of the ability functions through a bite, reproducing the basic mechanism through which a target can be reduced to a Ghoul.
- Corpse Ghoul Creation: Following her Blood Dragon transformation, Maria's Ghoulification underwent a major mutation. She became capable of directly converting a corpse into a Ghoul rather than requiring a conventional living transformation. This application was specifically described as reaching into the domains associated with Death and the Soul, demonstrating that her Ghoul power can interfere with the boundary separating a corpse from an animated supernatural existence.[3]
- Limited Death and Soul Interaction: Through Corpse Ghoul Creation, Maria can affect death and the spiritual state of a deceased body sufficiently to reconstruct it as a Ghoul. This is a specialized extension of Ghoulification and should not be confused with unrestricted Death Manipulation or unrestricted Soul Manipulation.[3]
- Divine Ghoulification: Maria's Ghoul authority has advanced far enough to affect Divine Beings. During the assault on [[Olympus]], Gods attacked by Victor's Battle Maids were converted and returned to battle as Ghouls, demonstrating that Maria's ability is not inherently restricted to ordinary mortal or supernatural species.[6]
- Ghoul Manipulation: Maria can command the Ghouls brought under her influence, allowing transformed enemies to be redirected against their former allies.[6]
- Ghoul Domination: As the Queen of Ghouls, Maria occupies the dominant position within the Ghoul forces associated with her and can issue commands to them rather than merely creating uncontrolled undead.
- Ghoul Army Command: Maria serves as the commander/queen of the Ghoul forces within Victor's faction. Her ability to create and control Ghouls allows defeated enemies to be converted into additional combatants instead of merely being removed from battle.[6]
- Retention of Former Racial Powers: Maria's Ghoul abilities survived both her transformation into Victor's Noble Vampire lineage and her eventual Blood Dragon evolution. Chapter 920 explicitly demonstrates this principle when her Ghoul ability mutates and becomes stronger after Dragonization rather than disappearing.[2][3]
- Blood String Manipulation: Maria's signature combat ability is the use of extremely fine strings. She was already a highly proficient wire user while serving as a Human hunter for the Inquisition, employing wires to prepare traps against supernatural targets. After becoming a Ghoul and later receiving Victor's blood, this fighting style evolved into a supernatural ability that produces numerous blood-red strands around her body.[1][7]
- String Generation: Maria can create large quantities of blood-red strands directly through her supernatural power. By Chapter 749, numerous strands could float around her without the need for the mundane equipment she depended upon as a Human.[7]
- String Manipulation: Maria possesses fine control over the direction, movement and arrangement of her strings, allowing them to surround targets or cover an area from multiple directions.
- Cutting: The extreme thinness and supernatural reinforcement of Maria's strings allow them to function as cutting weapons capable of shredding objects and attacks that enter their effective area.[8]
- Piercing: Maria can use concentrated strands offensively rather than merely as restraints, allowing groups of threads to penetrate opponents.
- String Reinforcement: Multiple strands can be brought together to form thicker and physically stronger structures, trading the subtlety of an individual thread for increased penetrating or restraining power.
- Restraint and Binding: Maria can arrange strings around a target to restrict movement. This style originated in her Human hunter days, when her wire traps were capable of temporarily restraining Sasha Elderblood Fulger.[1]
- Trap Creation: Maria is extremely proficient at preparing wire fields and concealed traps. During the ambush against Sasha's household, she used her wires as part of the trap prepared for the Vampire heiress.[1]
- Area Control: Because Maria can produce and manipulate numerous threads simultaneously, she can fill the space around an enemy with attack vectors, severely restricting safe movement.
- Defensive [[Radar Warning Receiver (RWR)|Interception]]: Her strings are not limited to attacking living targets. Maria can construct a thread formation in front of herself and shred incoming attacks before they reach her, as demonstrated during the family's game in Chapter 952.[8]

##### Techniques
- Execution Cage: Maria forms numerous blood strings into an intersecting cage around or in front of a target. Anything passing into the thread formation is simultaneously struck from numerous angles and cut apart. During Chapter 952, Maria formed the technique with her blood threads and used it to mince incoming attacks before they could reach her.[8]
- The novel names the technique "Execution Cage"; the older ability page's wording of "Executioner Cage" is therefore better treated as an inaccurate rendering rather than a separate technique.

##### Skills
- Expert Inquisition Hunter: Long before becoming a supernatural being, Maria was trained by the Inquisition alongside [[Carlos Reiss]]. The pair spent years hunting supernatural creatures selected by the Church, giving Maria practical knowledge of how to track, ambush, restrain and kill targets physically superior to ordinary Humans.[1]
- Anti-Supernatural Combat Training: Maria was taught specifically to fight supernatural species rather than ordinary Human opponents and was already experienced enough to participate in an operation targeting a Noble Vampire heiress.[1]
- Wire Weapon Mastery: Her supernatural Blood String Manipulation evolved from genuine technical experience with wires. Maria was capable of preparing and operating complex wire traps before possessing Ghoul, Vampire or Dragon powers.[1]
- Ambush Tactics: Maria and Carlos successfully prepared an ambush inside Sasha's residence, killing members of the household and arranging their battlefield before Sasha arrived.[1]
- Trap Setting: Maria can hide and position wires in advance so that an enemy enters a prepared killing or restraining area rather than confronting the target only through direct attacks.[1]
- Supernatural Creature Knowledge: Her career as an Inquisition hunter gave Maria practical familiarity with supernatural creatures, their behavior and the methods Human hunters use against them.[1]
- Elite Battle Maid: Maria is one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s personally selected Battle Maids and later one of the maids associated with the Deadly Sins, bearing the title Sin of Gluttony. Victor deliberately developed his Battle Maids as combat assets capable of surviving increasingly dangerous supernatural conflicts, and Maria participates alongside Kaguya, Eve, Bruna, Roberta and the others in high-level operations.[9]
- High-Level Combat Experience: Maria progressed from hunting Vampires as a Human to directly participating in wars involving Gods and other high-ranking supernatural beings. During the fall of [[Olympus]], she operated as part of [[Scathach]]'s assault group against Olympian Gods.[6]
- Team Combat: Maria is experienced at fighting within Kaguya's Battle Maid formations, using the different abilities of the maids in combination rather than relying only upon individual duels.[6]
- Tactical Discipline: During the assault on Olympus, Maria and the other Battle Maids followed Scathach's instructions to operate in groups, conserve their strength, retain trump cards and prioritize survival while subduing enemies.[6]
- Combat Reflexes: Maria can rapidly deploy her blood strings as both an offensive and defensive response, including forming Execution Cage quickly enough to intercept attacks already moving toward her.[8]
- Army Leadership: As the Queen and commander of the Ghoul forces within Dragons Nest, Maria possesses experience directing supernatural subordinates in addition to personally fighting on the battlefield.[6]

##### Former Racial States
- Human Physiology (Former): Maria was originally an ordinary Human whose effectiveness came from Inquisition training, weapons, planning and experience rather than innate supernatural physiology.[1]
- Human Potential and Adaptability: Like other Humans in the setting, Maria originally possessed Humanity's capacity for growth and adaptation. Her survival through multiple radical racial transformations demonstrates that this potential was not entirely meaningless even after leaving Humanity behind.
- Ghoul Physiology (Former): Sasha's bite transformed Maria into a defective Ghoul rather than a proper Noble Vampire. Her body became undead and gradually displayed the deterioration characteristic of an incomplete Vampiric existence.[1]
- Undead Nature: Maria continued functioning after her Human death because Sasha's transformation reanimated her as a Ghoul.
- Master Compulsion: In this state Maria was bound to Sasha as the Vampire responsible for creating her and was compelled to obey direct orders. This was primarily a racial limitation rather than an advantage.[1]
- Ghoul Instincts: Her Ghoul state intensified predatory instincts and hunger, traits that remained relevant even after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] restored her as a full Vampire.[2]
- Noble Vampire Physiology (Former as a separate Race): Victor eventually transformed Maria from an undead Ghoul into a living Noble Vampire belonging directly to his lineage, something noteworthy because ordinary Vampire transformation normally could not restore an undead Ghoul in this manner. Her Ghoul powers merged with her Vampire nature instead of disappearing.[2][5]
- Maria no longer exists as an ordinary Noble Vampire because this racial foundation was subsequently incorporated into her Blood Dragon existence.[3]
