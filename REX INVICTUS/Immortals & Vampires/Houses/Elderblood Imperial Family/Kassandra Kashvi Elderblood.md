Kassandra Kashvi Elderblood is a recurring character in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]], an Imperial Princess of the [[Elderblood Empire]], and the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Kali Elderblood]]. She is a naturally born Blood Dragon Goddess whose existence combines the predatory physiology of a genuine True Dragon with an extraordinary conceptual inheritance. Unlike conventional Gods who possess a Divinity associated with Destruction, Kassandra was born as the incarnation of Destruction itself, possessing a fundamental relationship with the Concept rather than merely exercising Divine Authority over it.[1]
Kassandra's existence represents one of the most unusual manifestations of Victor's second generation. Both of her parents possess exceptionally powerful relationships with Destruction, but their combined inheritance produced something that neither had deliberately attempted to create. Kassandra was born as a conceptual incarnation whose natural foundation already resembled the state Kali had spent an enormous portion of her existence attempting to attain. Her birth also granted her the potential to become a Primordial Being of Destruction, a fundamental component of the Universe responsible for governing the Concept on a universal scale. However, possessing the nature and potential of a conceptual incarnation does not automatically confer the governing position of a Primordial Regulator.[1][2]
Her conceptual nature was further altered by the predatory characteristics inherited from Victor. Although Kassandra was born as the reincarnation of a Concept, her Blood Dragon physiology did not treat that state as something beyond assimilation. Instead, the innate predatory properties of her lineage incorporated Destruction directly into her Draconic existence, causing her supernatural characteristics to express destructive authority naturally. Even her Dragon Language became saturated with Destruction, allowing her to impose destructive effects upon Reality through spoken commands without necessarily expending the enormous quantities of Energy ordinarily associated with attacks of comparable severity.[1]
This inheritance made Kassandra extraordinarily dangerous from birth. She possessed so much power and so little practical control that ordinary involuntary actions, including sneezing or coughing, could potentially cause an entire planet to disappear. Victor and Kali therefore raised her upon a private planet located inside Victor's Soul, isolating her from populations incapable of surviving accidental exposure to her Destruction. She was allowed to leave only after her parents succeeded in sealing most of her natural strength behind a limiter. The restriction was designed to regulate the power she could release while preserving her potential, allowing her to develop the control necessary to interact safely with the surrounding world.[1]
One of the most important parts of Kassandra's limiter is a pair of gloves previously worn by Victor during his younger years. Although the gloves possess a practical function in regulating her abilities, Kassandra treasures them primarily because they once belonged to her father. Their sentimental importance transforms what might otherwise have been an unwelcome restriction into one of her most prized possessions. Her sisters are similarly fascinated by the gloves because they are personal artifacts connected to Victor's earlier life, making Kassandra especially proud of having received them.[1]
Kassandra's conceptual development also differs from that of her mother in an important way. Although she could potentially ascend as the native Universe's Primordial Being of Destruction, Victor later explains that her half-Eldritch existence provides another possibility. Because Kassandra possesses Eldritch characteristics in both her Soul and body, she may eventually become an Eldritch Primordial embodiment of Destruction, deriving her higher existence from a different cosmological framework rather than occupying the native Universe's unique governing position. This would allow Kali to continue pursuing the native Primordial office without requiring mother and daughter to compete for the same position. Neither ascension has been confirmed as completed.[3]
Despite her extraordinary supernatural nature, Kassandra remains an affectionate daughter whose greatest emotional priority is Victor. She belongs to the more possessive members of his second generation and displays intense hostility toward individuals she believes might disrespect him or threaten his relationships. Her personality is aggressive, direct, stubborn and easily bored, frequently requiring Kali to remind her of the responsibilities associated with being an Imperial Princess. Nevertheless, Kassandra trusts Victor completely, respects his decisions regarding her powers and genuinely loves her mother despite the arguments produced by her extreme devotion toward her father.[1][4]

### Physical Appearance
Kassandra is an exceptionally tall and beautiful Dragon Goddess with pale skin, a powerful and curvaceous figure, long black-and-red hair and deep violet eyes. She possesses an enormous Humanoid stature comparable to [[Kali Elderblood]], whose height is approximately 476 centimeters. Their similar proportions and facial characteristics make mother and daughter appear almost like twins at first glance, although their distinctive supernatural features and different hair colors allow an attentive observer to distinguish them. Kassandra's appearance combines the imposing presence inherited from Kali with the violet Dragon Eyes and other Draconic characteristics associated with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s biological descendants.[1]
Her hair is particularly unusual because it is composed of pure Destruction rather than behaving entirely like ordinary biological hair. Its black-and-red coloration reflects the Concept incorporated into her existence, creating a visual resemblance to Kali's own hair, which is formed from the same destructive supernatural power. Kassandra's mother possesses predominantly red hair and black-red horns, whereas Kassandra's hair contains a stronger mixture of black and red, accompanied by a pair of black-violet Dragon horns. These features distinguish the two while emphasizing the extraordinary similarity between their physical forms.[1]
Kassandra's eyes are deep violet, inherited through Victor's Dragon lineage. Their appearance changes considerably when she becomes angry or possessive, with the pupils and surrounding coloration darkening until they resemble empty violet-black holes. During her confrontation with Kali concerning [[Shiva]], her eyes become so unnervingly vacant that Kali immediately recognizes the same expression Victor sometimes displays when his own possessive nature becomes dominant. Her supernatural gaze can also become saturated with destructive Energy, particularly when she identifies something that she considers offensive or threatening.[4]
Her clothing consists of an elegant black Divine robe with violet accents, resembling a combination of ceremonial dress and Divine armor rather than ordinary fabric. The outfit complements her enormous stature and powerful supernatural presence while retaining the appearance expected of an Imperial Princess. Her mother's clothing possesses a comparable design but uses predominantly red coloration, reinforcing their visual similarities without making them identical. Kassandra's formal attire also reflects Kali's expectation that her daughter maintain the dignity and composure appropriate to a member of the ruling Imperial Family.[1]
One of Kassandra's most distinctive possessions is the pair of limiter gloves originally worn by Victor. They serve as part of the mechanism restraining her destructive power, but their significance extends considerably beyond their supernatural function. Kassandra frequently touches the gloves affectionately and becomes visibly delighted when remembering that they once belonged to her father. The gloves are therefore both protective equipment and treasured family heirlooms, representing Victor's concern for her safety and the exceptional importance she assigns to objects connected to his past.[1]
Kassandra's natural presence is dangerous even without a deliberate attack. Her Destruction is an intrinsic component of her existence, and individuals without sufficient resistance can be annihilated merely by approaching too closely when her powers are not adequately restrained. The surrounding environment can also begin disappearing when she releases greater amounts of destructive authority, making her limiter essential for interacting with ordinary populations and maintaining the stability of relatively fragile dimensions. Her immense physical stature, unusual hair, prominent horns and overwhelming conceptual presence consequently give her an appearance that is both majestic and intimidating.[1]
As a genuine Blood Dragon Goddess, Kassandra possesses a fundamentally Draconic biological and spiritual nature despite her Humanoid appearance. However, the complete appearance of her individual True Dragon Form has not been described in sufficient detail to establish its dimensions, scale coloration or specialized anatomical characteristics. Her confirmed personal appearance is primarily defined by her extraordinary height, resemblance to Kali, black-and-red hair of Destruction, black-violet horns, violet Dragon Eyes and distinctive Divine attire.

### Personality
Kassandra is aggressive, straightforward, stubborn, possessive and emotionally intense, possessing a temperament that makes her particularly difficult to control despite her extraordinary natural power. Although Kali also embodies destructive forces and possesses an intimidating personality, she has accumulated immense experience and developed considerably greater restraint. Kassandra lacks much of that patience, frequently regarding troublesome circumstances as problems that could be resolved immediately through the application of Destruction. Her parents' decision to restrain her power is therefore especially important because her personality often causes her to react before fully considering the consequences of her actions.[1][4]
Her strongest emotional characteristic is her extraordinary devotion toward [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Kassandra considers her father the most important person in her existence, trusts his judgment and believes that his decisions concerning her wellbeing are made with her best interests in mind. She accepts the limiter placed upon her without resentment because she understands that Victor wishes to protect both her and the individuals who might otherwise suffer from accidental exposure to her power. Rather than focusing upon the restriction itself, she becomes delighted by the fact that the gloves used in the system once belonged to him, treating the gift as something far more important than an ordinary piece of supernatural equipment.[1]
Kassandra belongs to the faction of Victor's daughters whose attachment to their father is exceptionally intense. Her affection frequently develops into suspicion toward individuals she believes might interfere with his relationships, and she is particularly intolerant of behavior she interprets as disloyalty. When Kali speaks casually with [[Shiva]] during their diplomatic visit, Kassandra immediately suspects that her mother may be behaving improperly toward another man. She privately accuses Kali of potentially betraying Victor and declares that she would support her father without hesitation if her mother ever became unfaithful. The accusation is especially unreasonable because Kali and Shiva have known each other for an extremely long time, but Kassandra considers their friendship suspicious rather than reassuring.[1]
Her distrust is partly influenced by [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood and [[Pepper Scarlett Elderblood]], who previously taught her that men describing themselves as friends of attractive women might conceal romantic or sexual intentions. Kassandra accepts this advice almost unconditionally and reasons that Kali's beauty makes Shiva's friendship especially suspicious. Kali initially rejects the accusation and insists that genuine friendships between men and women are possible, although she privately remembers that Shiva had attempted to court her in the past. The recollection makes the advice less entirely baseless than Kali would prefer, but it does not justify Kassandra's insistence that her mother should never interact affectionately with another man.[1]
Kassandra's possessiveness occasionally becomes genuinely threatening. After Kali disciplines her for disrespectful behavior, Kassandra returns to the question of whether her mother intends to betray Victor. When Kali firmly declares that she loves her husband and would never exchange him for another man, Kassandra's eyes become unnervingly empty and resemble two violet-black holes. She calmly remarks that she would hate to have to hurt her mother, and Kali immediately understands that her daughter is entirely serious. The incident demonstrates the extent to which Kassandra's loyalty can distort her judgment, although her hostility originates from a desire to protect the relationship between her parents rather than an actual hatred of Kali.[4]
Despite these extreme reactions, Kassandra genuinely loves her mother and recognizes her authority. She follows Kali's commands when explicitly instructed to suppress her powers or maintain appropriate behavior, even if she initially complains or attempts to argue. Kali remains one of the few individuals capable of disciplining her directly because Kassandra's unrestricted conceptual power is sealed and her mother possesses overwhelming authority over Destruction. Their relationship is therefore characterized by affection, frustration and repeated disagreements arising from Kassandra's impulsive personality and tendency to prioritize Victor above nearly everything else.[1][4]
Kassandra is also easily bored, particularly when required to participate in political discussions. During Kali's meeting with Shiva, she quickly loses interest in the negotiations and begins privately complaining about the fragility of the surrounding dimension, the appearance of the assembled Gods and her inability to spend time with Victor. She considers the possibility of destroying the environment merely to relieve her boredom, recognizing that increasing her power slightly could potentially collapse the entire dimension. Her thoughts demonstrate both the abnormal scale of her natural strength and the difficulty she experiences when expected to behave with diplomatic restraint.[4]
To entertain herself, Kassandra uses Destruction to recreate an image of Victor sitting upon his throne. She becomes increasingly pleased with her ability to reproduce his appearance accurately and privately celebrates her progress, demonstrating that her destructive abilities can be employed for detailed supernatural construction rather than exclusively for attacks. Her amusement also reflects her obsessive devotion, since even when attempting to distract herself from a tedious political meeting, she chooses an activity centered upon her father.[4]
Although Kassandra is capable of adopting the formal posture expected of an Imperial Princess, maintaining that behavior for extended periods is difficult for her. She can remain motionless during important negotiations and appear composed, but her thoughts frequently reveal impatience, irritation and a desire to escape. Her natural aggression makes the combination of political responsibility and overwhelming destructive power particularly troublesome, requiring Kali to monitor her behavior closely whenever they interact with individuals who lack the strength necessary to survive Kassandra's uncontrolled reactions.[1][4]
Kassandra's extraordinary attachment to Victor is also accompanied by a strong sense of pride in belonging to his family. She reacts aggressively when Hindu Gods consider her as a potential political marriage partner and becomes immediately hostile toward [[Veylan Valentin Walker]] because he resembles her father without actually being Victor. Her emotional priorities are therefore closely connected to the identity and dignity of the Elderblood family, although her responses frequently exceed what would be considered reasonable or diplomatically appropriate.[1][4]
Her personality possesses a more thoughtful aspect when discussing supernatural development. During Victor's lessons concerning Divinity and the hierarchy of Primordial Beings, Kassandra asks whether Divine Concepts become more influential as the Sector containing them develops. She also recognizes that a God's strength and understanding affect the influence they can exercise over a Concept, demonstrating that she is capable of serious intellectual engagement when a subject concerns her own powers and future. Her questions eventually lead Victor to explain that she could potentially become an Eldritch Primordial Being of Destruction, a possibility that reassures her because she would prefer to attain such a position within the cosmological framework associated with her father rather than compete with Kali for the native Universe's corresponding role.[3]
Kassandra therefore combines the overwhelming aggression of a naturally born incarnation of Destruction with intense familial affection, possessiveness and developing intellectual curiosity. Her greatest difficulty is not a lack of power or potential, but the need to cultivate the judgment and emotional restraint necessary to exercise those abilities responsibly.

### History

#### Birth and containment
Kassandra was born from the union of [[Victor Elderblood]] and [[Kali Elderblood]], both of whom possessed extraordinarily powerful relationships with Destruction. Her inheritance produced an existence fundamentally different from that of an ordinary daughter who merely acquired compatible supernatural abilities from her parents. Rather than being born as a Goddess who governed destructive phenomena through a conventional Divinity, she emerged as the reincarnation and embodiment of Destruction itself. Her conceptual foundation was therefore established from birth, granting her a state of existence that Kali had spent an enormous portion of her life attempting to achieve through personal development.[1]
Her birth granted her the aptitude to become a new Primordial Being of Destruction, potentially attaining the cosmic position responsible for regulating the Concept throughout the native Universe. However, neither Victor nor Kali wished for her to pursue that position immediately. Kassandra possessed an extraordinary amount of power without the experience or control necessary to regulate it, making unrestricted development exceptionally dangerous both to herself and to the civilization surrounding her. Her parents therefore prioritized her safety and education rather than allowing her natural power to increase without restraint.[1]
The danger became apparent through ordinary involuntary actions. Kassandra's Destruction was so deeply incorporated into her existence that an uncontrolled sneeze or cough could potentially cause an entire planet to disappear. Her powers did not require the deliberate concentration normally associated with large-scale destructive techniques, because the Concept itself was an intrinsic component of her being. This made her especially hazardous as an infant, when she lacked sufficient understanding to prevent emotional or instinctive reactions from affecting her surroundings.[1]
Victor and Kali consequently raised her upon a private planet situated inside Victor's Soul, isolating her from ordinary populations and supernatural environments unable to withstand her uncontrolled Destruction. The location provided a secure environment in which Kassandra could develop without placing Imperial citizens in constant danger. Her parents eventually succeeded in sealing most of her natural strength, creating a limiter that restricted how much power she could express while allowing her underlying potential to remain intact.[1]
The mechanism was explicitly compared with the limiters Victor had used during his own development. Rather than damaging Kassandra's existence or permanently removing her abilities, it functioned as a means of regulating output until she could safely control the power she had inherited. The restriction allowed her to leave the isolated environment of her early childhood and participate in activities alongside her mother without immediately destroying everything around her.[1]
Kassandra received the gloves Victor had once worn to limit his own strength, incorporating them into her restricted-power equipment. She considered the gift extraordinarily precious because it connected her directly to her father's earlier life, while several of her sisters became jealous of the personal artifact she had received. Kassandra's response demonstrated that the sentimental significance of Victor's possessions could be more important to her than the supernatural function they performed.
Her birth also revealed the influence of Victor's predatory Blood Dragon lineage upon conceptual existence. Although Kassandra had been born as the incarnation of Destruction, her Draconic blood assimilated the Concept into her supernatural biology rather than allowing it to remain an entirely separate authority. This integration caused her ordinary powers to express destructive properties naturally, including her Dragon Language and the Reality alterations performed through her Draconic [[Will Solace|Will]]. The fusion also demonstrated that the predatory characteristics inherited from Victor could influence even beings possessing the nature of fundamental Concepts.[1]

### Synopsis

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Kassandra's first major appearance occurs when she accompanies Kali on a diplomatic visit to the [[Indian Pantheon|Hindu Pantheon]]. The journey takes place as the [[Elderblood Empire]] prepares its allied civilizations for the larger conflict expected to emerge from beyond their native Sector. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] wishes to strengthen the Pantheons associated with his family and provide their leaders with the information necessary to prepare for enemies possessing extraordinary technological and supernatural capabilities. Kali's visit to [[Shiva]] serves a purpose comparable to Ariel's diplomatic journey to Heaven, with the Imperial wives accompanied by their daughters as a demonstration of respect toward their historical allies.[1]
Kali and Kassandra travel toward Shiva's temple while maintaining their imposing Divine appearances. Both possess extraordinary physical stature and overwhelming destructive characteristics, making their approach intimidating even to powerful Gods. The guards stationed around the temple do not attempt to interfere, recognizing Kali's elevated position within the Elderblood family and understanding that approaching either Dragon Goddess without sufficient resistance could result in annihilation. Their presence demonstrates the enormous difference between the conceptual power of Victor's family and that possessed by the conventional Divine beings residing within the Pantheon.[1]
Upon entering the temple, Kali formally introduces herself as the Dragon Goddess of Destruction and wife of Victor Elderblood before presenting Kassandra as their daughter and an Imperial Princess. The assembled Hindu Gods immediately recognize the political importance of Kassandra's existence, and several begin considering the advantages that might result from establishing a marital connection with the Elderblood family. Kassandra perceives their intentions and becomes openly hostile, her expression changing so dramatically that Kali intervenes before she can say something capable of destroying the diplomatic atmosphere.[1]
Kali releases enough of her own Destruction to make the surrounding environment begin disintegrating and warns the assembled Gods against treating Victor's daughters as political resources. She explains that the Imperial Princesses possess the right to determine their own futures and that arranging their marriages without their consent would violate the Emperor's wishes. Kassandra reluctantly suppresses her power when ordered to calm down but complains that she intends to report the incident to Victor, further irritating her mother. Shiva subsequently dismisses the other Gods, recognizing that allowing individuals with inappropriate intentions to remain near two exceptionally powerful Dragon Goddesses would only worsen the situation.[1]
Once the formal audience ends, Kali and Shiva begin speaking more casually because of their long-standing friendship. Kassandra immediately becomes suspicious of their familiarity and privately accuses Kali of behaving improperly toward another man. She declares that she would report any betrayal of Victor and support her father without hesitation, refusing to accept Kali's explanation that Shiva is merely an old friend. Her suspicion is reinforced by advice received from [[Aphrodite]] and Pepper, who had taught her that male friends might conceal romantic intentions toward attractive women. Kali becomes increasingly irritated, particularly because she remembers that Shiva had attempted to court her in the past, making the accusations more difficult to dismiss completely.[1]
Kassandra continues objecting until Kali temporarily excuses herself from the meeting and transports her daughter into a private dimension through the Dragon Language. There, she creates a belt composed of pure Destruction and disciplines Kassandra for her disrespectful behavior. Kassandra protests that she will not remain silent even if punished, demonstrating the stubbornness that makes her difficult to control despite her extraordinary affection for her mother. The incident also reveals the practical effectiveness of the limiter placed upon Kassandra, since her unrestricted conceptual nature does not automatically protect her from Kali's discipline while most of her power remains sealed.[1][4]
After the punishment, Kassandra threatens to inform Victor of what happened, but Kali dismisses the complaint and orders her to behave like an Imperial Princess. Kassandra then returns to her accusation concerning Shiva, asking whether her mother truly intends to betray Victor. Kali responds sincerely that she loves her husband and would never exchange him for another man. Kassandra's eyes become unnervingly empty as she studies the answer, eventually smiling and remarking that she would hate to have to hurt her mother. Kali recognizes that the statement is genuine and becomes disturbed by the intensity of her daughter's possessiveness, particularly because the expression in Kassandra's eyes resembles Victor's own obsessive demeanor.[4]
The two return to Shiva's throne room and resume their diplomatic mission. Kali presents Shiva with a sphere containing extensive knowledge concerning Destruction alongside an intelligence report documenting the Empire's understanding of the enemies expected to emerge from the Upper Sectors. The gift is intended to strengthen the Pantheon by improving Shiva's comprehension of his own Divinity rather than merely increasing his reserves of [[Divine Energy]]. Kali explains that the Empire's allies must prepare for enemies whose ordinary military forces may possess power comparable to the strongest combatants of earlier supernatural civilizations, making continued development essential for their survival.[4]
While Kali and Shiva discuss the Empire's preparations, Kassandra quickly becomes bored. She remains outwardly composed, adopting the appearance expected of an Imperial Princess, but privately complains about the tedious conversation and the fragility of the surrounding dimension. She recognizes that releasing only a small amount of additional Destruction could potentially collapse the environment, demonstrating that her power remains enormously dangerous even while restrained. Her thoughts repeatedly return to Victor, whom she wishes to see again, and she begins entertaining herself by shaping Destruction into a representation of him sitting upon his throne. She becomes pleased with the increasing accuracy of the recreation, using the exercise as a distraction from the political proceedings.[4]
Kassandra's attention abruptly shifts when she detects an unfamiliar presence hidden nearby. Without warning Kali or Shiva, she bends Reality, relocates to the concealed individual and seizes him by the neck. She returns to the throne room and throws the boy before the two ancient Gods, demanding an explanation for his existence. The boy possesses a remarkable resemblance to Victor, sharing approximately half of his visible characteristics while the remainder resembles Shiva. Kassandra's Dragon Eyes become saturated with destructive Energy as she demands to know why an unfamiliar child looks so similar to her father.[4]
The discovery forces Shiva to reveal a secret project involving Victor's Human ancestry. He explains that the boy, [[Veylan Valentin Walker]], originated from an ancient branch of Victor's original family and had been incorporated into Shiva's own Bloodline through a Divine ritual. Shiva had sought an individual possessing even a fraction of Victor's extraordinary inherited potential, hoping to create a powerful [[Demigod]] warrior capable of strengthening the Hindu Pantheon. The experiment involved a transaction with a Primordial authority and the retrieval of information preserved within the [[Akashic Records]], allowing Shiva to construct a being resembling one of Victor's deceased ancestors.[5]
Kali immediately recognizes the seriousness of the situation. Shiva's experiment involves the ancestry of her husband without his permission and attempts to reproduce qualities associated with the Imperial Bloodline. Kassandra reacts even more violently because the boy's resemblance to Victor appears offensive to her, especially when combined with Shiva's visible characteristics. Kali restrains her daughter, understanding that an uncontrolled response could destroy the boy and potentially provoke a much greater diplomatic crisis. She consequently contacts Victor and requests his immediate presence, recognizing that the matter has progressed beyond something she can resolve through an ordinary discussion with Shiva.[5]
Victor arrives personally within Shiva's temple, his presence becoming apparent throughout the Pantheon. His first action is to approach Kassandra and affectionately stroke her head, calming her anger almost immediately. She responds by embracing him and complaining about the boy, while Kali observes that her daughter has instantly returned to the affectionate behavior she displays whenever Victor is present. Victor instructs Kassandra to remain beside Kali, and she obeys without hesitation, demonstrating the extraordinary influence he possesses over her emotions despite her recent hostility.[5]
Victor examines Veylan through his supernatural perception and identifies the original individual whose information was used to construct the boy. The historical Veylan Valentin Walker had lived during the eighteenth century and completed an ordinary Mortal life long before Victor's birth. He belonged to the Walker lineage, whose inherited characteristics had gradually accumulated potential across generations until producing Victor's extraordinarily unusual Human blood. However, the boy standing before them is not the continuous individual who once lived. Shiva's ritual instead used information preserved in the Akashic Records to create a convincing reconstruction, which was subsequently altered through Divine means to incorporate characteristics associated with Shiva's Bloodline.[5]
Kassandra remains hostile throughout the examination. When Veylan briefly interprets Victor's gentle treatment as something resembling paternal affection, Kassandra becomes furious at the possibility of an unfamiliar boy considering her father his own. Kali immediately orders her to remain quiet, preventing her possessiveness from interfering with Victor's investigation. Her reaction demonstrates that the intensity of her attachment extends beyond concern for Victor's physical safety to the preservation of what she considers the proper relationships surrounding him.[5]
Victor concludes that Shiva's attempt to reproduce his inherited potential has failed because the reconstructed Veylan does not possess the continuous biological and supernatural history that eventually culminated in Victor. Although the original Walker lineage possessed extraordinary latent characteristics, the boy is fundamentally a copy assembled from historical information rather than a genuine restoration of the individual who once lived. Victor also recognizes that Shiva's willingness to manipulate his ancestry without permission represents a serious breach of trust, particularly because the experiment was concealed through assistance associated with Primordial powers.[5]
Victor consequently removes Shiva from leadership of the Hindu Pantheon and appoints Kali in his place. Shiva is ordered to participate directly in the most dangerous front lines of the future Upper-Sector war, making his continued position within the Empire dependent upon service rather than political authority. Victor allows the reconstructed Veylan to disappear, treating the original ancestor's completed life with respect rather than preserving an artificial imitation indefinitely. Kassandra remains close to her father throughout the confrontation, continuing to display satisfaction whenever he intervenes on her behalf.[5]
After the matter is resolved, Kassandra immediately approaches Victor again and clings to him affectionately. She also mocks Kali over the diplomatic failure, prompting her mother to become visibly irritated. Victor reprimands Kassandra and instructs her to respect her mother, causing her to apologize immediately. However, she subsequently decides to report Kali's earlier familiarity with Shiva, provoking Victor's possessive reaction and leaving Kali to deal with the consequences. Victor departs with his daughter, while Kali remains behind and directs her accumulated irritation toward Shiva, whose reckless experiment has endangered the political relationship between the Pantheon and the Empire.[5]
Kali's disciplinary response continues after Victor's departure. Shiva is subjected to hours of punishment while the other Hindu Gods recognize that the Dragon Goddess of Destruction is deeply dissatisfied with his actions. The confrontation eventually concludes with Kali assuming responsibility for the Pantheon's administration and Shiva accepting his future military obligations. Kassandra's discovery of Veylan consequently becomes the event that exposes an important weakness in the Empire's intelligence systems and forces a significant change in the leadership of one of its allied Pantheons.[6]
The incident also reinforces the exceptional importance of Victor's daughters within the political organization of the Empire. Their natural abilities, extraordinary ancestry and intense familial loyalty make them difficult targets for conventional manipulation, while their reactions can transform seemingly minor diplomatic mistakes into conflicts involving the Emperor himself. Kassandra's introductory experiences therefore demonstrate the extraordinary consequences of combining overwhelming conceptual power with a personality that remains intensely protective of Victor and the relationships surrounding him.
Kassandra's supernatural nature later becomes relevant during discussions concerning the hierarchy of Divine beings and the distinction between ordinary conceptual authority and genuine Primordial existence. Victor explains that possessing enormous power or representing a Concept does not automatically grant a being the universal governing position associated with the corresponding Primordial authority. Such positions form part of the fundamental mechanisms maintaining Reality and require acceptance into the established structure of Creation. Kassandra's birth as the incarnation of Destruction consequently places her in an exceptional category without automatically making her the native Universe's active Primordial Regulator.[2]
Her birth is also discussed when Victor and his wives prepare for the arrival of [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]]. The possibility of another unusually powerful daughter attracts considerable interest because Kassandra, alongside individuals such as Isabelle and Nigredo, represents one of the most extraordinary examples of Victor's second generation. Natasha expresses enthusiasm for [[Tyche]]'s daughter to be born with similarly exceptional characteristics, while the family recognizes that naturally born conceptual Goddesses often possess abilities far beyond the levels associated with conventional Divine inheritance.[7]
Moira's subsequent birth produces a different but equally dangerous manifestation of supernatural power. Her abnormal relationship with Luck and the favor of Reality itself makes it difficult for Victor to allow her to interact freely with ordinary Imperial environments. When discussing the precautions necessary for raising Moira, the family recalls Kassandra's early circumstances as a comparable example. Even as a newborn, Kassandra could have devastated enormous portions of the Empire merely through her uncontrolled presence, demonstrating why Victor had confined her to a private planet and imposed restrictions before allowing her to interact with the wider civilization.[8]
Kassandra later attends Victor's educational gatherings alongside the other Imperial Princesses, participating in discussions concerning the origins of Energy, the nature of Divine Concepts and the relationship between ordinary Gods and Primordial Beings. Her interest becomes particularly pronounced when the conversation turns toward the hierarchy of Concepts across different Sectors. She raises her hand and asks whether Divine Concepts expand or change as the Sector containing them develops, prompting Victor to explain the relationship between a God's authority, the number of competing Divine beings and the cosmological conditions of the surrounding Reality.[3]
Victor explains that the fundamental nature of a Concept remains recognizable across different Sectors, but the amount of influence exercised by individual Gods depends upon their personal understanding and the existence of others governing the same principle. A God who dominates a Concept within a Lower Sector may encounter numerous competitors upon entering a more advanced Sector, reducing the influence that individual can exercise unless their strength and comprehension are sufficient to maintain their position. Kassandra recognizes that the hierarchy therefore depends considerably upon the user's own power and understanding rather than merely the title associated with a particular Divinity.[3]
The lesson develops into a discussion of genuine Primordial existence. Victor explains that a being who becomes the Primordial authority of a Concept does not simply possess exceptionally advanced Divine powers; they become a fundamental incarnation of the principle itself and assume a function necessary for maintaining the Universe. Once the governing position has been established, other Gods associated with the same Concept derive their authority through that fundamental existence. The removal of such a Primordial could consequently cause the corresponding principle to cease operating throughout the Universe, creating enormous disruptions to Reality.[3]
Victor identifies Kassandra as an extraordinary exception to the ordinary progression. She was born as the incarnation of Destruction without first undergoing the conventional development required to reach that state, while Kali remains close to attaining a comparable conceptual condition through her own training and growth. However, Victor explains that the native Universe ordinarily supports only one Primordial authority for a given Concept, creating an apparent problem if both mother and daughter eventually wish to occupy the same cosmic role.[3]
When Kassandra asks how Kali could advance if she herself becomes the Primordial Being of Destruction, Victor reveals that her Eldritch inheritance provides another possibility. He explains that she does not necessarily need to become the incarnation of Destruction belonging to the native Universe. Because Kassandra possesses a half-Eldritch Soul and body, she may eventually become an Eldritch Primordial Being of Destruction, attaining a fundamental conceptual position associated with another cosmological framework rather than taking the unique native office Kali seeks.[3]
Victor further clarifies that such a development would be easier for Kassandra than for Kali because the necessary Eldritch characteristics already exist within her spiritual and physical nature. The Soul is particularly important to this process, and Kassandra's half-Eldritch Soul provides a natural foundation that her mother does not presently possess to the same degree. Kali could potentially pursue a similar path in the future, but her current development remains more closely associated with the native Universe's conceptual framework.[3]
Kassandra is visibly relieved by the explanation. She would prefer to attain a Primordial position associated with her father's Pantheon rather than compete with her mother for authority within another cosmological structure. The possibility also reassures her that Kali's advancement does not necessarily have to be sacrificed to accommodate her own future. Her response reflects the importance she places upon Victor's lineage and the personal relationships surrounding her supernatural development, demonstrating that even questions concerning cosmic authority are closely connected to her affection for her family.[3]
The discussion establishes an important distinction between Kassandra's existing nature and her possible future. She is already an incarnation of Destruction whose Concept has been assimilated into her Draconic existence, but she has not yet received the complete governing authority of a Primordial Regulator. Her half-Eldritch inheritance provides an alternative developmental route that could eventually allow her to achieve a comparable state without preventing Kali from pursuing her own ascension. Neither possibility should be indexed as an accomplished transformation until the corresponding development actually occurs.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Kassandra's biological father and the most important individual in her emotional life. She possesses extraordinary devotion toward him and regards his affection, approval and decisions as matters of overwhelming significance. Her trust is sufficiently complete that she accepts the restriction of most of her natural power without resentment, believing that Victor would never impose a limitation unless it were necessary for her wellbeing. Kassandra's attachment frequently manifests through extreme possessiveness. She becomes hostile toward individuals she believes might disrespect Victor or interfere with his relationships, and she is willing to challenge even Kali if she suspects that her mother intends to betray him. Her reaction to Veylan is particularly intense because the boy resembles Victor while possessing characteristics associated with [[Shiva]], making his existence appear offensive to her. Nevertheless, Victor can calm her almost immediately through affection or direct instruction, demonstrating the extraordinary influence he possesses over her emotional state.[4][5]
Victor's concern for Kassandra extends beyond protecting her from external enemies. He recognizes that her natural incarnation of Destruction makes ordinary childhood dangerous, and he therefore establishes the isolated environment and limiter necessary for her to develop safely. His intention is not to suppress her potential permanently but to provide sufficient time for her to acquire self-control and learn to interact with others without causing accidental devastation. Their relationship consequently combines extraordinary affection with the practical responsibility of raising a child whose existence possesses fundamental conceptual power.[1]
Kassandra also regards Victor as the ultimate authority concerning her supernatural development. During his lessons about Primordial Beings, she asks questions concerning the nature of Divine Concepts and the possibility of her becoming a fundamental incarnation of Destruction. [[Kali Elderblood]] is Kassandra's biological mother and the principal source of her inherited affinity with Destruction. Kali initially experiences mixed emotions concerning Kassandra's birth. Her daughter naturally achieves a state of conceptual existence that Kali herself had spent an enormous portion of her life attempting to reach, producing a small amount of understandable envy. Nevertheless, Kali is overwhelmingly relieved and happy that her daughter was born healthy and powerful, regarding Kassandra's wellbeing as more important than any personal frustration concerning their different paths of development. She therefore supports [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s decision to restrain Kassandra's abilities until her natural power can be controlled safely.[1]
Their relationship is complicated by Kassandra's possessiveness toward Victor. She accuses Kali of behaving improperly with [[Shiva]], refuses to accept the explanation that they are old friends and threatens to support Victor if her mother ever betrays him. The possibility of Kassandra eventually becoming an Eldritch Primordial Being also has important consequences for Kali's development. Because the native Universe ordinarily permits only one fundamental Primordial authority for a Concept, Kassandra's ascension within that framework could prevent Kali from occupying the same position. ### Destruction and END
Kassandra's conceptual power must be distinguished from the higher principle referred to as END. Although both Concepts are associated with annihilation, their effects differ fundamentally. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is explicitly relieved that Kassandra was born as the incarnation of Destruction rather than END because the latter would have introduced a substantially more dangerous form of conceptual erasure into the existence of an infant who initially possessed almost no practical control over her powers.[1]
Destruction eliminates a target from its present state of existence, potentially removing matter, living beings or other supernatural structures through the direct application of destructive authority. However, the Concept does not necessarily erase every historical trace that the target existed. Records, consequences and information associated with the destroyed existence may remain preserved through Time, Space or the [[Akashic Records]], while the underlying substance involved in the process can return to the broader cycle of Creation. Destruction therefore functions as a fundamental component of the Universe's ongoing cycle rather than automatically removing an existence from every aspect of Reality.[1]
END represents a more absolute form of termination. An existence affected by END would not simply be destroyed in its present form; the corresponding record could cease to exist as though the target had never existed at all. This distinction involves the removal of deeper historical and conceptual traces rather than merely the destruction of physical matter or an existing supernatural structure. Victor's concern reflects the extraordinary consequences such a power could produce if it were expressed involuntarily by a newborn conceptual incarnation.[1]
Kassandra's Destruction consequently supports extraordinary applications of conceptual existence erasure, but it should not automatically be classified as unrestricted historical erasure, complete Akashic Records deletion or control over every possible form of nonexistence. Her natural abilities remain tied to Destruction, whose effects are explicitly distinguished from those associated with END.
Her developing conceptual nature must also be distinguished from the completed state of a Primordial Regulator. Kassandra already embodies Destruction as an intrinsic aspect of her existence, but the universal office responsible for governing a fundamental Concept requires more than extraordinary natural potential. Victor explains that a being occupying such a position becomes a necessary component of the Universe, with other Gods drawing authority from the corresponding Primordial existence. Kassandra may eventually attain that position through the native cosmological framework or pursue an Eldritch alternative, but neither outcome has yet been confirmed as accomplished.[2][3]
The Eldritch possibility is particularly important because the native Universe ordinarily permits only one Primordial authority for a given Concept. If Kassandra occupied the native position of Destruction, Kali would be unable to attain the same office through ordinary means. However, Kassandra's half-Eldritch Soul and body provide the foundation for a different developmental path, potentially allowing her to become a Primordial embodiment of Destruction associated with another cosmological system. Victor further explains that the limitation concerning the number of Primordial Beings ultimately depends upon the Rules established by the corresponding Creator, meaning that the existing hierarchy is not necessarily immutable under every conceivable circumstance.[3]
Kassandra's current classification is therefore best understood as a naturally born conceptual incarnation of Destruction with Primordial and Eldritch Primordial potential. This preserves the extraordinary significance of her birth without incorrectly assigning her the completed cosmic responsibilities, universal governing Authority or formally established status of a Regulator of Existence.
