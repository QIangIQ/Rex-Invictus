### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 989
Where is MY Daddy?
—Stella Elderblood demanding to know [[Victor Elderblood]]'s whereabouts, Chapter 989
Stella Elderblood is a recurring character in [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]], an Imperial Princess of the [[Elderblood Empire]], and the biological daughter of Victor Elderblood and [[Anna Elderblood]]. She belongs to Victor's second generation of naturally born [[Blood Dragons]], possessing the biological and supernatural foundation of a genuine True Dragon alongside an exceptionally advanced Divine inheritance. Her principal supernatural abilities concern Reality, Truth and Falsehood, and a developing Blood Domain, combining Anna's unusual conceptual powers with Victor's extraordinary Draconic physiology and martial potential. Her ability to directly bend Reality makes her one of the more unusual members of the younger Imperial Family, particularly because she possessed considerable control over such an extraordinary power while still physically immature.[1][2][3]
Stella's existence also reflects the extraordinary transformation of the Walker family. Anna was originally Victor's Human mother before entering the supernatural world, becoming a Noble Vampire and eventually joining his Blood Dragon family as one of his wives. Consequently, Stella occupies an unusual genealogical position: Victor is her biological father while also being her maternal half-brother through Anna's original relationship with him. The circumstances of their ancestry are a consequence of the supernatural development of the family, whose members have undergone repeated transformations and reached states of existence fundamentally different from ordinary Humans.[2]
Stella inherited Anna's ability to bend Reality and perceive falsehood, two extraordinarily powerful characteristics that distinguish her from conventional Dragon Goddesses. During her childhood, she demonstrates enough authority to reconstruct her own physical appearance into an adult Humanoid body, alter the surrounding Reality and forcibly tear open dimensional boundaries to travel between Victor's Personal World and [[Nightingale]]. These feats are particularly remarkable because access to the Personal World is normally controlled through supernatural permissions associated with Victor and his wives, yet Stella can bypass conventional movement restrictions by directly manipulating the Reality and Space separating the locations.[2][4]
Her inherited perception of Falsehood is equally unusual. Stella can recognize lies even when the speaker possesses far greater supernatural power and experience than herself, demonstrating this ability when [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] attempts to avoid revealing Victor's location. Her perception subsequently develops to the point that concealing information through ordinary false statements becomes exceptionally difficult in her presence. During the later Imperial era, she employs this ability to identify the deception of [[Hinata Elderblood]] and [[Irene Elderblood]] following the disappearance of photographs from Anna's private collection, demonstrating a level of Truth-related awareness that remains effective even when Stella deliberately restrains her powers.[2][5]
Stella also possesses a Blood Domain and considerable martial talent inherited from Victor. Anna explicitly recognizes that her daughter's powers over Reality and Falsehood, her inherited combat aptitude and her Blood Domain have all become stronger through training. However, the existence of a Blood Domain should not automatically be interpreted as confirmation that Stella possesses a separate Divine Concept of Blood. Her demonstrated personal Divinities are associated with Reality and Truth-related abilities, while the exact mechanics and conceptual classification of her Blood Domain remain incompletely described.[3]
Although her powers possess extraordinary potential, Stella initially displays a mischievous, possessive and impulsive personality closely resembling Victor's younger temperament. She frequently searches for her father, attempts to escape the areas where her parents expect her to remain and becomes distressed whenever she fears disappointing him. Her childhood demonstrates the dangers of raising naturally born Dragon Goddesses whose supernatural abilities are already sufficient to distort Reality, making parental supervision and practical education essential despite the immense intelligence and natural strength of the younger generation.[2][4]
Stella undergoes considerable development as Victor begins preparing his daughters for responsibilities beyond the protection of the Imperial household. She participates in the War Games, temporarily assuming the role of a Demi-Goddess whose abilities are associated with Truth rather than relying upon the overwhelming advantages of her Blood Dragon existence. The exercise improves her practical judgment and contributes to the development Anna later recognizes, allowing Stella to begin supervised training within the Empire alongside [[Gina Elderblood]]. Their growing desire to contribute eventually earns them temporary access to Imperial operations classified at G-5 and below, marking an important stage in Stella's progression toward greater independence.[6][3]
Her later appearances reveal further applications of her supernatural abilities. Stella contributes the power of Truth to an experiment involving the creation of Eldritch Life, participating alongside Yog, Nigredo and several other sisters in the development of the organism that becomes [[Cthugha]]. She also demonstrates an increasingly advanced understanding of the principles governing Runes, the [[Akashic Records]] and Divine Concepts during Victor's educational gatherings. These developments establish that Stella's inherited abilities are not limited to personal transformation, dimensional movement and detecting lies, but can also contribute to the modification of supernatural organisms and the practical study of Reality's underlying Rules.[7][8][9]

### Physical Appearance
Stella is an exceptionally beautiful young True Dragon Goddess with fair skin, black hair and deep-violet eyes. Her facial features strongly resemble those of [[Anna Elderblood]] and [[Victor Elderblood]], making her recognizable as the biological daughter of both parents. Her black hair reflects characteristics shared by Anna and Victor, while her violet Dragon Eyes are inherited through Victor's evolved Blood Dragon lineage. Her natural physical appearance during childhood is considerably younger than her extraordinary supernatural intelligence and abilities might suggest, since naturally born Dragons mature according to developmental timescales fundamentally different from those experienced by ordinary Humans.[2]
During her first major appearance, Stella has already used Reality manipulation to assume an adult Humanoid appearance standing approximately 190 centimeters tall. In this form, her long black hair extends toward her buttocks, and her body possesses an athletic, warrior-like physique with clearly developed musculature and defined abdominal muscles. [[Scathach]] compares her appearance with the formidable women associated with the Norse Valkyries and the warriors serving beneath [[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]], recognizing that Stella has deliberately created the appearance of a fully developed and physically powerful Dragon woman.[2]
The appearance is not Stella's natural physical form during her early childhood. When Scathach discovers that she has used her powers without appropriate supervision, Stella immediately abandons the transformation, causing Reality to distort around her as she returns to her actual childlike body. Her natural appearance possesses the same black hair, violet eyes and recognizable facial characteristics, but also displays the Dragon horns associated with her biological nature. The transformation therefore demonstrates genuine Reality-based alteration of her physical presentation rather than evidence that she had naturally matured into an adult by the time of her introduction.[2]
Stella's childlike appearance is particularly important because of the extraordinary difference between chronological experience and physical maturation among [[True Dragons]]. Scathach considers the younger Imperial Princesses children until their Draconic bodies reach appropriate developmental stages, regardless of whether some have already accumulated centuries or millennia of experience. Stella's ability to imitate a mature body does not change the expectations placed upon her by her parents, who continue regarding her as someone requiring education and supervision despite her exceptional supernatural potential.[2]
Her appearance during the later Imperial era once again includes a mature Humanoid body approximately 190 centimeters tall. She possesses long black hair, violet Dragon Eyes and a muscular physique with clearly defined abdominal muscles, combining the imposing presence of a Dragon Goddess with the aesthetic characteristics inherited from Anna and Victor. Her clothing includes a modern black outfit consisting of fitted leggings, high-heeled shoes and a long-sleeved shirt exposing part of her abdomen. The resulting appearance emphasizes her confidence and warrior-like physical development, although her earlier ability to alter her body through Reality means that the appearance alone does not establish the exact progression of her natural Draconic maturation.[5]
Stella's violet eyes can become particularly expressive when she is angry or attempting to intimidate someone. During her childhood confrontation with Scathach, she directs an intense, possessive gaze toward her mother while demanding to know Victor's location. Although the pressure would have been intimidating to an ordinary Mortal, Scathach remains unaffected because of her vastly superior strength and experience. Stella's visible expression consequently reflects both the extraordinary power she possesses and the emotional immaturity still present during her early development.[2]
As a genuine Blood Dragon Goddess, Stella also possesses a fundamentally Draconic biological and spiritual existence beneath her Humanoid appearance. Her body provides the racial foundation associated with Dragon transformations, advanced supernatural senses and extraordinary physical development. However, her complete natural True Dragon Form has not been individually described in sufficient detail to establish its precise size, scale coloration or unique anatomical characteristics. Her confirmed appearances therefore primarily involve her natural child form, the adult Humanoid appearance created through Reality alteration and the mature presentation observed during the later Imperial era.

### Personality and traits
Stella is possessive, confident, stubborn, mischievous, competitive and deeply attached to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Her temperament strongly resembles several aspects of her father's younger personality, particularly his unwillingness to submit to intimidation and his tendency to become impatient when prevented from obtaining something he considers important. Although Stella possesses extraordinary intelligence and a supernatural understanding considerably beyond that of ordinary Human children, she initially lacks the emotional restraint and practical judgment necessary to employ her powers responsibly. Her childhood consequently involves repeated conflicts between her desire for independence and the restrictions imposed by Victor and his wives.[2][3]
Her affection for Victor is among her most prominent characteristics. Like many of her sisters, Stella's first spoken word is associated with her father, and she becomes visibly excited whenever someone mentions his presence. During her confrontation with [[Scathach]], her interest in defending her unauthorized transformation quickly disappears when she becomes more concerned with discovering Victor's whereabouts. Her possessiveness becomes apparent when she demands to know where her Daddy has gone, emphasizing ownership with an intensity that reminds Scathach of Victor himself.[2]
Although Stella can become confrontational toward weaker individuals, she is genuinely afraid of disappointing Victor. When Scathach discovers her unauthorized adult transformation, the possibility of being reported to him immediately makes Stella panic. She abandons her altered form and attempts to explain that she was merely training, despite knowing that the adults had forbidden unsupervised use of dangerous abilities. Her fear does not originate from an expectation that Victor will physically harm her, but from the importance she assigns to his approval and the possibility that he will become dissatisfied with her behavior.[2]
Stella is also sensitive to embarrassment, particularly when her intentions are exposed. Scathach correctly recognizes that Stella's adult form has been deliberately constructed according to the appearance she believes Victor would find attractive, prompting the girl to blush and turn away. Stella's ability to manipulate Reality does not prevent her from experiencing ordinary childhood emotions, and she struggles when her parents identify the personal motivations concealed beneath her apparently reasonable explanations. Her supernatural intelligence therefore coexists with emotional immaturity rather than replacing it.[2]
Her stubbornness becomes apparent when Scathach releases a small amount of killing intent after Stella attempts to pressure her into revealing Victor's location. Although Stella recognizes that Scathach is overwhelmingly stronger, she initially refuses to retreat. The behavior reminds Scathach of Victor's attitude when he was younger, particularly his determination to confront seemingly insurmountable opponents rather than immediately surrender. Stella lacks the complete battle-obsessed temperament inherited by [[Siren Elderblood]], but possesses enough of Victor's competitive spirit for Scathach to recognize substantial martial potential.[2]
Despite her pride, Stella eventually acknowledges the difference between her natural talent and the actual strength possessed by the family's most experienced warriors. When Scathach increases her supernatural pressure, Stella realizes that she cannot continue resisting indefinitely. Her reaction demonstrates that she is not completely incapable of recognizing danger or accepting authority, although she often requires a sufficiently compelling reason to abandon her preferred course of action. Her willingness to challenge stronger individuals consequently represents a developing competitive temperament rather than proof of an established ability to defeat opponents possessing vastly superior power.[2]
Stella's mischievous personality frequently causes her to violate the household's restrictions. She repeatedly attempts to leave Victor's Personal World without permission, even though the area is closely monitored and protected by powerful supernatural beings. Rather than rely upon conventional teleportation or request assistance from her parents, she bends Reality and tears through the dimensional boundary separating the Personal World from [[Nightingale]]. Her behavior reflects curiosity and a desire for freedom, but her true motivation is frequently the search for Victor rather than genuine interest in independent exploration.[4]
Her ability to lie convincingly is complicated by her own connection to Falsehood. Stella can recognize deception almost immediately, yet is still willing to invent explanations when adults question her behavior. She claims that she merely wishes to explore Nightingale and observe its inhabitants, while Kaguya recognizes that the explanation is an excuse concealing her desire to find Victor. The contradiction reflects Stella's developing social intelligence rather than a lack of understanding concerning the nature of lies, since she recognizes deception but has not yet acquired the maturity necessary to avoid using it herself.[4]
Stella shares an especially close relationship with [[Yuno Elderblood]], whose extraordinary possessiveness toward Victor resembles her own. The two are described as practically inseparable, frequently participating in the same activities and displaying considerable enthusiasm whenever their father becomes involved. Their companionship concerns Kaguya because both possess dangerous supernatural abilities and strong emotional attachments, making their combined involvement in unauthorized adventures particularly troublesome. Kaguya is consequently relieved when Stella leaves the Personal World without bringing Yuno, recognizing that the situation could have become substantially more chaotic if the girls had acted together.[4]
Her childhood also reveals an impatient side that becomes increasingly important as she begins seeking greater responsibility. Anna recognizes that Stella possesses substantial intelligence and remarkable supernatural talent, but worries that prolonged administrative pressure could cause her to lose patience and abandon her duties. The temperament is partly associated with Victor's younger personality and the naturally volatile disposition of young Dragons, whose extraordinary power can make ordinary inconvenience feel unnecessarily restrictive. Stella's development therefore requires more than acquiring additional strength; she must also learn to endure frustration and consider the consequences of her actions.[3]
Stella becomes more mature following Victor's War Games, which deliberately force the daughters to function without relying exclusively upon their overwhelming natural abilities. Her temporary role as a Demi-Goddess associated with Truth requires her to respond to a civilization experiencing political conflict rather than immediately overcoming every obstacle through Reality manipulation. She recognizes that her simulated identity has inherited a power resembling Anna's and begins examining the circumstances of the civil war affecting her territory. Her willingness to adapt demonstrates growing practical judgment, particularly because the simulated conflict is associated with another sister's faction rather than an ordinary enemy she can simply destroy.[6]
The desire to become useful to Victor eventually becomes one of Stella's principal motivations. Alongside Gina, she approaches Anna and requests permission to begin participating in Imperial administration. Her ambition is no longer merely an excuse to escape the family's protected territory or satisfy temporary curiosity. Instead, she genuinely wishes to help govern the civilization established by her parents, particularly after witnessing Akasha assume major responsibilities shortly after her creation. Stella becomes visibly upset when Anna initially refuses the request, revealing that her desire for recognition extends beyond receiving Victor's affection and includes proving that she is capable of contributing meaningfully to the Empire.[3]
Although she initially reacts emotionally to the refusal, Stella accepts the restrictions imposed when Victor eventually authorizes supervised administrative training. She understands that an Imperial Princess represents the Emperor in public and that inappropriate behavior can damage the reputation of the entire family. Anna warns her that the responsibilities of government require patience, appropriate presentation, careful observation and the ability to endure considerable pressure. Stella's acceptance demonstrates that her personality has begun developing beyond the impulsiveness associated with her earlier childhood, even if she continues retaining the competitive and possessive characteristics inherited from Victor.[3]
Her later behavior reveals additional progress. When Hinata and Irene take photographs belonging to Anna, Stella recognizes their false explanations and confronts them directly. However, she does not simply employ overwhelming Reality manipulation to force them into submission. Victor's household rules prohibit his daughters from casually using dangerous powers against one another, requiring them to resolve ordinary disputes without allowing minor disagreements to escalate into supernatural conflicts. Stella consequently relies upon her perception of Falsehood and direct confrontation rather than treating her extraordinary powers as an unrestricted means of controlling her sisters.[5]
Stella nevertheless remains emotionally intense during family gatherings. When Yuno clings to Victor and compliments his appearance, Stella becomes jealous and insults her sister for refusing to share their father's attention. Victor immediately intervenes, reminding them that rivalry and ordinary arguments are acceptable but genuine hostility between members of the family is forbidden. Stella becomes frightened when she realizes that she has disappointed him, but her expression immediately softens when Victor affectionately strokes her head. The incident demonstrates that her increased maturity has not eliminated her possessiveness or the extraordinary influence Victor possesses over her emotions.[10]
Her practical judgment continues developing through later educational and experimental activities. When Velina questions why Stella is participating in a dangerous attempt to create Eldritch Life, Stella calmly reasons that Victor must be observing the experiment and would intervene if the girls were about to cause a genuine catastrophe. The argument convinces Velina to join the activity rather than prevent it, demonstrating Stella's understanding of her father's protective behavior while also revealing that she remains willing to participate in questionable experiments when she believes sufficient safeguards exist.[9]
Stella also displays intellectual curiosity concerning the underlying principles of Reality. During Victor's lessons, she demonstrates an understanding of the relationship between Runes, conceptual comprehension and the [[Akashic Records]], correctly recognizing that the effectiveness of a Rune depends partly upon the user's understanding of the phenomenon being expressed. She later asks difficult questions concerning the age of the Akashic Records, the Creator and the source of the principles nourishing Divine Concepts. Her questions reflect a desire to understand the actual structure of Creation rather than merely obtain additional destructive power.[7][8]
Stella consequently combines the extraordinary natural confidence of a Dragon Goddess with strong familial affection, possessiveness and a developing sense of responsibility. Her growth is characterized less by the acquisition of additional raw power than by learning when that power should be used, how to recognize the limitations of her own understanding and how to participate meaningfully in a civilization whose scale demands more than overwhelming supernatural strength.

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Stella was born during the extraordinary period of accelerated development experienced by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his family following the establishment of his mature Blood Dragon lineage. Her existence resulted from the union of Victor and Anna, whose own supernatural development had transformed her from an ordinary Human into a Noble Vampire and eventually a genuine Dragon Goddess. Stella consequently inherited an unusually powerful combination of Draconic characteristics and Anna's authority over Reality and Falsehood, giving her a supernatural foundation exceptional even among Victor's naturally born daughters.[1][2]
Her birth also represented a profound change in the structure of the Walker family. Anna had originally been Victor's Human mother and the wife of [[Leon Walker]], but the extraordinary supernatural events surrounding their transformation eventually altered their relationships and the lives they had previously expected to lead. By the time Stella was born, Anna had become one of Victor's wives and an important member of the ruling Elderblood family, making the child both Victor's daughter and his maternal half-sister through Anna's original biological relationship with him.
Stella was raised within Victor's Personal World, where the children of the Imperial Family lived under the protection of their parents and numerous supernatural guardians. The daughters possessed extraordinary natural intelligence and powerful Divine characteristics, but Victor and his wives remained concerned that their overwhelming abilities could encourage dangerous behavior before they developed sufficient judgment. Stella was particularly troublesome because her ability to alter Reality allowed her to bypass restrictions that would have been effective against ordinary young Dragons, making direct supervision and clearly established boundaries essential.[2][4]
Her first major appearance occurs when [[Scathach]] encounters an unusually tall black-haired woman walking through the family's castle. The woman's mature appearance initially resembles that of an exceptionally accomplished warrior, with a height of approximately 190 centimeters, long black hair, violet eyes and a heavily trained physique comparable to the women associated with the Norse Valkyries. Scathach immediately recognizes that the woman is Stella and that her adult appearance has been produced through unauthorized Reality manipulation rather than genuine physical maturation.[2]
Scathach confronts Stella and demands an explanation for her behavior. Stella initially claims that she was merely training, but Scathach reminds her that Victor and the mothers have explicitly forbidden the unsupervised use of dangerous abilities. When Stella attempts to argue, Scathach announces that she will report the incident to Victor. The threat immediately causes Stella to panic, and Reality distorts around her as she abandons the transformation and returns to her natural childlike body, complete with the Dragon horns concealed by her mature appearance.[2]
Scathach recognizes that Stella's transformation was not simply an exercise intended to develop greater control over her powers. The adult body closely resembles the appearance Stella believes Victor would find attractive, making her actual motivation considerably more personal than her initial explanation suggests. Stella becomes embarrassed when Scathach exposes the truth and attempts to redirect the conversation toward Victor's whereabouts. Her behavior reveals both her extraordinary attachment to her father and the emotional immaturity concealed beneath her advanced supernatural intelligence.[2]
When Scathach attempts to avoid revealing Victor's location, Stella immediately recognizes the false statement through her inherited perception of Falsehood. The ability functions effectively despite Scathach possessing far greater combat experience and supernatural power than her young daughter. Stella consequently demands a direct answer, expressing her possessiveness so openly that Scathach recognizes the same temperament she once observed in Victor. The older Dragon Goddess releases a small amount of killing intent, causing Stella to tremble but initially refuse to retreat.[2]
Scathach becomes interested in the girl's determination because it resembles Victor's willingness to confront seemingly unbeatable opponents during his early training. Although Stella's fighting spirit is considerably less extreme than that of her father, the resemblance is sufficient for Scathach to recognize an exceptional martial foundation. However, Stella eventually realizes that she cannot withstand her mother's escalating pressure, and the confrontation ends when Anna appears through a distortion of Reality.[2]
Anna immediately questions Stella, whose own ability to recognize deception makes it especially difficult for her to conceal the truth from her biological mother. Stella reluctantly admits that she altered her appearance without supervision and was discovered by Scathach. Rather than continuing the argument, Anna orders her daughter to return to her room and complete her responsibilities, confirming that Victor will still be informed of the incident. Stella obeys despite preferring an ordinary punishment to the prospect of disappointing her father.[2]
The confrontation causes Scathach to reconsider the amount of training provided to Victor's daughters. She realizes that her approach has become considerably more lenient than the methods she employed while raising her older children, and she concludes that the younger generation requires stricter discipline to compensate for its extraordinary natural advantages. Stella's misbehavior consequently contributes to Scathach's decision to increase the difficulty of their future training, unintentionally affecting the lives of numerous sisters who were not directly involved in the incident.[2]
Stella subsequently attempts another unauthorized excursion, this time leaving Victor's Personal World and appearing in [[Nightingale]]. Her movement is especially remarkable because the boundaries of the Personal World are protected by exceptionally advanced supernatural systems and conventional access normally requires the appropriate permission or connection. Rather than using an ordinary portal, Stella bends Reality around herself and forcibly tears through the Space separating the two worlds, creating a passage that allows her to travel directly to her destination.[4]
Kaguya intercepts Stella and recognizes that the girl has once again violated the restrictions established by her parents. Stella claims that she merely wishes to explore Nightingale and observe its inhabitants, but Kaguya quickly understands that she is actually searching for Victor. Stella complains that remaining at home is boring and that she should have brought Yuno with her, unaware that Kaguya considers the latter possibility especially dangerous. The two girls are practically inseparable, and Yuno's extraordinary possessiveness could have transformed the unauthorized excursion into a catastrophe if she became distressed by Victor's absence.[4]
Stella eventually recognizes a change in Kaguya's demeanor when Anna communicates the news that Victor has returned home. She immediately demands an explanation, correctly identifying that Kaguya's expression is associated with information concerning her father. Once Kaguya confirms his return, Stella abandons her excuses and uses Reality manipulation to force another passage between Nightingale and the Personal World. The movement is sufficiently unusual that Kaguya recognizes it as dimensional tearing rather than conventional teleportation, demonstrating the extraordinary freedom provided by Stella's conceptual authority.[4]
Kaguya also understands that Victor has almost certainly permitted the excursion to occur. His Personal World remains directly connected to his Soul, allowing him to perceive the individuals entering and leaving its boundaries. The presence of his Heralds and other protective forces further ensures that Stella is not genuinely unguarded even when she believes she has escaped ordinary supervision. Victor's willingness to allow limited freedom therefore reflects confidence in his ability to protect his daughter rather than a lack of awareness concerning her movements.[4]
The incident also reveals the origin of Stella's name. Anna named her daughter after her own grandmother, who had provided important maternal care during Anna's earlier Human life. Leon remembers that Anna had long intended to use the name Stella if she ever gave birth to a daughter, preserving the memory of the woman who had been especially important to her upbringing. Stella's name consequently maintains a connection to the older Walker family's Human history despite the extraordinary supernatural transformation experienced by its descendants.[4]
Victor later begins preparing his daughters for circumstances in which their overwhelming natural abilities cannot guarantee success. Although the girls possess genuine True Dragon physiology, powerful Divine Concepts and extraordinary intellectual potential, he recognizes that their protected upbringing has limited their experience confronting opponents capable of matching or surpassing their power. He consequently organizes military exercises designed to teach them strategy, adaptation, leadership and the consequences of decisions made under unfamiliar supernatural limitations.[11][6]
The War Games initially involve several daughters commanding opposing military forces constructed through Victor's powers. The simulated soldiers behave according to realistic social and psychological principles, allowing the commanders to experience problems involving fear, morale, resources and uncertainty without endangering actual Imperial personnel. As the exercises develop, Victor expands the simulation into a much more elaborate Dream World through the upgraded Tower of Nightmare, altering the participants' supernatural characteristics and limiting their normal advantages.[6]
Stella is selected alongside Rosemary, Gina and Yuno to participate directly in the expanded simulation. Each receives an unfamiliar identity and is placed within a different social or political environment. Rosemary becomes a Fairy Queen, Gina assumes the characteristics of a Dryad, Yuno becomes a Necromancer and Stella appears as a Demi-Goddess within a Human civilization. Her temporary power is associated with Truth, resembling the supernatural ability possessed by Anna to influence honesty and distinguish genuine statements from deception.[6]
Stella immediately recognizes the resemblance between her simulated powers and those of her mother, initially wondering whether her assigned identity has reduced her to little more than a reflection of Anna. However, she dismisses the thought after considering that her own existence possesses considerably more potential than the temporary identity provided by the Dream. Upon receiving the memories associated with her new body, she discovers that the city in which she resides is experiencing a civil war instigated by Priscillia's faction. Recognizing that the situation will require careful judgment, Stella leaves her room and begins working rather than attempting to resolve the conflict through the unrestricted Reality manipulation available to her natural Dragon Goddess form.[6]
The exercise provides practical experience involving limited resources, political instability and the necessity of responding to opponents without relying exclusively upon overwhelming natural strength. Stella's role is particularly appropriate because Truth-related authority can influence social relationships and decision-making, forcing her to consider the behavior of ordinary individuals rather than treating every problem as something that can be eliminated through raw power. Her participation contributes to the broader training program that Victor intends to prepare the second generation for conflicts against enemies possessing unfamiliar abilities and comparable supernatural strength.[6]
The training eventually produces visible results. As the [[Elderblood Empire]] continues expanding, Stella and Gina begin seeking greater responsibility within its political and administrative institutions. Both recognize that their parents already possess extraordinary military forces, powerful supernatural officials and advanced technology, but nevertheless wish to contribute rather than remain permanently sheltered within the Personal World. They consequently approach Anna directly and request permission to begin helping Victor govern the Empire.[3]
Anna initially treats the request seriously but questions how the girls expect to contribute. Although Gina possesses considerable combat ability and Stella has inherited exceptional powers over Reality and Falsehood, the Empire already commands elite soldiers, Dragons, Demons, Leviathans and powerful individuals such as Scathach. Simply adding another powerful combatant does not necessarily improve the administration of an enormous civilization, especially when that individual lacks practical experience managing the pressures associated with government.[3]
Anna explicitly recognizes Stella's progress, explaining that the Reality-bending and Falsehood-perceiving abilities inherited from her, the martial talent inherited from Victor and Stella's Blood Domain have all become considerably stronger through the girls' recent training. The assessment establishes that Stella has continued developing her abilities rather than remaining dependent exclusively upon the supernatural potential present at birth. Nevertheless, Anna insists that these improvements do not automatically qualify her to govern large populations or exercise unrestricted Imperial authority.[3]
The responsibilities of an Imperial Princess extend beyond possessing sufficient power to defeat ordinary enemies. Every public appearance, statement and decision reflects upon Victor and the civilization he established, making presentation, etiquette and emotional restraint essential. Anna explains that even the most powerful members of the family must maintain appropriate conduct while representing the Empire, using Violet and Victor as examples of individuals whose public responsibilities require substantially different behavior from their ordinary private personalities.[3]
Stella becomes increasingly disappointed as Anna continues explaining the difficulties involved. She had hoped that her exceptional abilities and recent development would be enough to justify greater responsibility, particularly because Akasha has already assumed an important administrative role despite being a comparatively recent addition to the family. When Anna initially refuses the request, Stella becomes tearful and complains that the decision is unfair, revealing that her desire to participate has developed into a genuine ambition rather than a temporary attempt to escape boredom.[3]
Victor overhears the conversation through his connection with Anna and recognizes that the girls' motivations have changed. Sparring, training and mutually agreed competitions remain acceptable, but ordinary arguments are expected to be resolved without attempting to overpower siblings through Reality manipulation, Divine authority or other extraordinary techniques. Stella's progress becomes apparent during a dispute involving photographs from Anna's private collection. The incident demonstrates an important stage of her emotional development because her strongest ability is not necessarily the most appropriate tool for resolving every problem. Her reliance upon Falsehood perception and direct confrontation reflects the principles of restraint and judgment emphasized throughout her education, even though her anger and possessiveness remain apparent.[5]

Stella responds calmly that their father must already be observing the activity and would intervene if the girls were about to cause a genuine disaster. Her response demonstrates both confidence in Victor's protective behavior and a willingness to explore dangerous supernatural possibilities when she believes that appropriate safeguards are already present.[9]

The experiment eventually produces [[Cthugha]], a new Divine Eldritch being recognized by Victor as a Great Old One. Its existence combines properties originating from the native Universe and the foreign Eldritch framework, making it an unusually advanced example of supernatural hybridization. Stella's participation establishes that her Truth-related authority can be contributed to the development of another supernatural organism, although the exact individual effect of that contribution is not described separately from the completed creature's combined characteristics. The experiment should therefore be understood as a cooperative feat rather than proof that Stella independently created Cthugha or possesses every ability displayed by the resulting Great Old One. Victor's observation and permission also distinguish the experiment from an uncontrolled accident, since he explains that he would have intervened if the daughters were approaching a genuine catastrophe.[9]
Stella's continued development reflects the central challenge faced by Victor's second generation. She possesses extraordinary powers capable of altering Reality, recognizing Falsehood and influencing the supernatural properties of living beings, but her growth also requires practical judgment, theoretical knowledge and the ability to cooperate with individuals whose powers differ from her own. Her progression from an impulsive child who forcibly escaped the Personal World toward an Imperial trainee and participant in advanced supernatural experiments demonstrates an increasingly mature approach to the abilities she inherited from Victor and Anna.

The relationship reflects the supernatural transformation of the Walker family and its subsequent integration into Victor's Draconic dynasty.
