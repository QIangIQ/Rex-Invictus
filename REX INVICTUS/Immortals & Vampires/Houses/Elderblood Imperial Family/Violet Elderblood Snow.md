### Appearances

#### Debut
[[Ch02 - Rex Imperator II|Chapter 2]]: [[Awakening]]
Let there be order.

Violet Elderblood Snow, formerly Violet Snow and Violet Alucard Snow, is one of the principal female protagonists of [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]]. She is the biological daughter of [[Agnes Elderblood Snow]] and [[Adonis Snow]], heiress of the ancient Clan Snow, the first wife of [[Victor Elderblood]], and the biological mother of [[Valentina Victoria Elderblood]]. Originally a Noble Vampire who inherited her family's extraordinary Fire, Violet eventually became a Blood Dragon, a genuine True Dragon, and a high-ranking Dragon Goddess of Order possessing the Divinities of Order and Obsession. She is also one of the principal Empresses responsible for administering the [[Elderblood Empire]] and maintaining the organization of Victor's enormous household.[12][9]
Violet is the woman most directly responsible for Victor's introduction to supernatural society. Having encountered him during childhood, she became fascinated with the sickly Human whose admiration for her contradicted the fear ordinary people might have shown upon discovering her Vampiric nature. She subsequently spent approximately sixteen years secretly protecting him from supernatural predators, arranging magical concealment around his life and removing Vampires who attempted to approach him. When Victor eventually suffered injuries that threatened his life, Violet refused to allow him to die and used an ancient Vampire marriage ritual to transform him. The unexpected involvement of her childhood friends [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] and [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] caused the ritual to bind all three women to Victor, establishing the original marital relationship around which the series develops.[13]
At the beginning of the story, Violet's personality is defined by her overwhelming obsession with Victor, extreme jealousy, violent temper and unwillingness to share what she considers hers. She initially struggles to accept even Ruby and Sasha as fellow wives despite their longstanding friendships, and her reactions to threats against Victor frequently involve anger so intense that she must be restrained before endangering herself or others. However, the gradual development of Victor's family transforms the practical expression of those traits. Violet's possessiveness expands beyond the desire to keep Victor exclusively for herself, becoming a determination to protect and organize the entire household surrounding him. Her authority as First Wife consequently develops into a genuine position of responsibility through which she coordinates the women, children, allies and institutions connected to Victor.[14]
Her emotional attachment also produces one of her most remarkable personal qualities. Violet has observed Victor for so much of her life that she possesses an extraordinary understanding of his expressions, habits and hidden feelings. Even after Victor experienced approximately seven hundred years of warfare within Hell and learned to conceal his emotions beneath the mentality of a ruler, Violet remained capable of recognizing what he was suppressing. This intimate understanding, together with her organizational talent and determination to protect everyone Victor values, makes her influence within the family substantially greater than a simple comparison of combat strength would suggest.[15][16]
Violet's supernatural development follows a similarly extraordinary progression. Victor eventually incorporated her into his own Vampire Progenitor Bloodline while preserving the ancestral characteristics of Clan Snow, allowing her hereditary powers to become stronger without being erased. She later endured the Tower of Nightmares, developed increasingly advanced Future Vision and accepted Victor's transformation into a genuine True Dragon. Her Divine evolution subsequently produced two distinct but intertwined Concepts: Order, reflecting her need to organize and stabilize the people surrounding Victor, and Obsession, reflecting the intensity of her devotion to him. Unlike the ordinary interpretation of Order as universal balance, Violet's authority developed around the unusual structure of Victor's household, where enormous personal attachments coexist within a system she continuously attempts to maintain.[17][9]
Following the creation of the Elderblood Empire, Violet became one of its most important senior administrators. Her responsibilities expanded through the development of Imperial institutions, the management of Victor's household, the integration of powerful new family members and the defense of a civilization distributed across enormous Galactic territories. After approximately two hundred thousand years of accelerated Imperial development, she possessed sufficient authority and experience to coordinate administrative affairs across multiple Sectors, supervise important research projects and speak officially on behalf of the Empire. Her position was publicly demonstrated when Victor entrusted her with announcing Mars as the new Imperial capital, marking the transformation of the young Snow heiress into one of the principal rulers of an interstellar civilization.[4]

### Physical Appearance
Violet is an exceptionally beautiful woman possessing pale skin, an elegant and curvaceous physique, extraordinarily long white hair and naturally vivid violet eyes. Her ordinary humanoid height is approximately 180 centimeters. The distinctive white hair and pale complexion reflect her ancestry within Clan Snow, while her eyes are one of her most recognizable individual characteristics. Her Vampiric nature can cause them to become red during heightened emotional or supernatural exertion, whereas activation of her more advanced Future Vision produces a brilliant violet coloration associated directly with her precognitive abilities.[6][5]
During her original Noble Vampire existence, Violet generally favored elegant clothing with dark colors, frequently creating a striking contrast against her white hair and pale complexion. Her fashion choices often combined aristocratic refinement with a Gothic appearance appropriate to the traditions of [[Nightingale]]'s ancient Count families. However, she was not restricted to formal dresses and could wear comparatively practical clothing when circumstances required it. Violet also attached sentimental importance to particular appearances, sometimes deliberately reproducing outfits or hairstyles connected to important moments in her relationship with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. During their private date in [[Paris]], she selected clothing that reminded him of an earlier stage of their relationship, demonstrating that even apparently ordinary personal details possessed considerable significance to her.
Violet's appearance is particularly expressive when her obsession becomes pronounced. Her violet eyes can acquire an unnervingly intense quality, while her expressions shift rapidly between affection, jealousy, delight and open hostility. Her transformation from an affectionate young wife into an intimidating Vampire noblewoman can therefore occur almost immediately whenever someone threatens Victor or behaves in a manner she considers unacceptable. Despite the intimidating quality of those reactions, Violet's familiar appearance remains recognizable throughout much of the story, even as her supernatural existence develops far beyond its original limitations.
After becoming a genuine Blood Dragon, Violet's body and Soul were fundamentally reconstructed through Victor's Draconic lineage. Her transformation did not simply add temporary Dragon characteristics to an existing Vampire body; it created a superior supernatural foundation while preserving the recognizable characteristics of her Snow ancestry. Violet retained her white hair, Fire and distinctive eyes, but acquired the biological and spiritual qualities of a True Dragon. Her humanoid body consequently became one of several possible manifestations of an existence capable of assuming far larger proportions.[17]
By the later story, Violet could manifest substantially enlarged humanoid forms when acting openly as a Dragon Goddess. During an encounter with mortals, she complained about the inconvenience of moving around at approximately four and a half meters tall, demonstrating that her Divine appearance could differ greatly from her ordinary 180-centimeter body. The enlarged form was an expression of her supernatural state rather than a permanent change to her natural humanoid dimensions.[18]
Her Divine manifestation is frequently accompanied by the visual characteristics of Order, including luminous violet eyes, elegant Divine garments, a supernatural crown and the release of brilliant golden-white Energy. During her intervention on an alien planet, Violet appeared as a translucent projection carrying a white staff decorated with gold and a violet gemstone. Her crown revolved as she enlarged the manifestation to approximately 1,346 meters, presenting herself as an imposing Divine authority before pacifying the hostile environment. The enormous appearance was deliberately theatrical and should not be mistaken for her ordinary physical height or the dimensions of her unrestricted True Dragon body.[1]
A possible future perceived through Violet's own supernatural abilities also revealed an older white-winged Dragon Empress version of herself. However, because the vision represented one possible development rather than an established inevitable future, its precise physical characteristics cannot automatically be attributed to Violet's current appearance.

### Personality
Violet is intensely possessive, obsessive, passionate, territorial and emotionally expressive, possessing a temperament that can change almost instantly from affectionate playfulness to murderous hostility when someone threatens the people she loves. Her devotion to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is the central feature of her personality, having begun during childhood and continued through more than two hundred thousand subjective years of development. Unlike an ordinary romantic attachment that emerged gradually after marriage, Violet's feelings were already deeply established long before Victor became aware of supernatural society. She had secretly observed him, learned his habits, arranged his protection and imagined a future in which they would remain together permanently. The intensity of this fixation made the unexpected inclusion of Ruby and Sasha in the marriage ritual especially difficult for her to accept, since the event immediately threatened the exclusive relationship she had spent years preparing to establish.[13]
Violet's jealousy originally produced extreme reactions toward virtually any woman who appeared interested in Victor. She regarded his attention as something belonging to her and frequently reacted to perceived romantic threats with hostility disproportionate to the circumstances. Her Vampire psychology further intensified those feelings because the transformation and development of supernatural Bloodlines amplify existing emotions rather than replacing them. Nevertheless, Victor's own possessive nature made their relationship unusually compatible. Instead of being frightened by Violet's obsessive behavior, he often recognized similar tendencies within himself, and their mutual understanding allowed them to develop a relationship that would have been extraordinarily difficult for less compatible partners. Their affection consequently combines genuine tenderness, playful competitiveness and a shared unwillingness to abandon anyone they consider part of their family.
The abnormal marriage ritual forced Violet to confront the limitations of her original expectations. Ruby and Sasha had been her childhood friends, but she initially regarded their marriages to Victor as betrayals of the future she had imagined. The resulting rivalry could become extremely hostile, particularly because all three women possessed powerful supernatural abilities and equally possessive personalities. Victor nevertheless refused to solve the problem by choosing one woman and abandoning the others, forcing Violet to recognize that her feelings were not the only ones deserving consideration. Although she never completely lost her jealousy, she gradually learned to distinguish between the emotional discomfort of sharing Victor and genuine threats to their relationship. This distinction became the beginning of a larger transformation in which Violet's obsession developed into a willingness to protect a family much larger than the one she had originally intended to create.
Her possessiveness also gives Violet an extraordinary ability to observe Victor. She has studied his expressions, speech, habits and emotional reactions for so long that even subtle changes can immediately attract her attention. This understanding becomes particularly important after Victor's extended imprisonment in Hell, where centuries of warfare and rulership forced him to develop the detached mentality of an invincible King. Although Victor became exceptionally skilled at hiding his emotions from others, Violet continued recognizing the feelings beneath his composure and could identify when he was troubled even when he attempted to behave normally. She does not always respond through direct comfort, understanding that Victor sometimes needs space or an appropriate challenge rather than immediate reassurance. Her emotional awareness therefore represents a more sophisticated form of attachment than the simple jealousy that characterized her earliest appearances.[16]
Violet's growing experience also reveals a pronounced aptitude for organization. She strongly prefers situations in which responsibilities, relationships and institutions possess clear structures and where people understand the roles they are expected to fulfill. Originally, this tendency appeared through her desire to control the circumstances surrounding Victor's life, including his interactions with other Vampires and the dangers associated with supernatural society. As his household expanded, however, the same impulse became increasingly useful. Violet began coordinating women with radically different personalities, managing conflicts, establishing expectations and ensuring that newly accepted members could integrate into an enormous family without destabilizing its existing relationships. Her position as First Wife eventually became a genuine center of social and administrative authority rather than a title based exclusively upon being the first woman Victor married.[14]
This talent became reflected directly through Violet's Divine development. Her two confirmed Divinities, Order and Obsession, originate from qualities she possessed long before obtaining Godhood. Their interaction is highly unusual because her Order does not function merely as the detached maintenance of universal balance. Instead, it has developed around the chaotic but deeply connected structure of Victor's household, where numerous intensely possessive individuals coexist because of shared loyalty, affection and the restrictions imposed by the family itself. Violet's Obsession strengthens her desire to maintain that structure, while Order gives supernatural significance to her ability to organize and stabilize it. The combination creates an authority that reflects her personal identity rather than an ordinary abstract interpretation of the two Concepts.[9]
Nevertheless, Violet's desire for organization can become a serious flaw when she begins treating the personal decisions of other people as matters that should be arranged according to her judgment. She occasionally assumes that because she recognizes a possible benefit to Victor or the Empire, she possesses sufficient justification to manipulate the circumstances necessary to obtain it. This tendency is particularly visible in her attitude toward powerful women who might become additional members of Victor's family. As the Empire grew and its enemies became increasingly dangerous, Violet began considering whether allowing certain extraordinary individuals to remain entirely outside the Elderblood household created strategic vulnerabilities. Her original reluctance to accept any additional wives consequently developed into a willingness to recruit them, sometimes for reasons combining genuine compatibility with political and supernatural advantage.
The situation involving [[Tyche]] became one of the clearest demonstrations of this flaw. Violet considered the Goddess of Fortune extraordinarily dangerous because her increasingly advanced control over Luck and probability could influence outcomes on a scale that conventional military defenses could not reliably prevent. Rather than simply propose a diplomatic arrangement, Violet began attempting to encourage a romantic relationship between Tyche and Victor, partly because she believed the Goddess would fit their family and partly because she wanted her powers safely incorporated into the Empire. Victor and several senior wives objected to treating romance as an administrative acquisition. Victor emphasized that genuine relationships could not be created merely because a woman's power or political position made her strategically valuable, forcing Violet to recognize that her authority as First Wife did not grant her unlimited control over the personal choices of other people.[19]
Violet eventually accepted the criticism rather than using her status to overrule Victor and the household. The lesson remained relevant during a later disagreement involving Albedo and Evie Moriarty. When Albedo attempted to secure Violet's approval for a relationship between Evie and Victor, Violet refused after learning that Evie did not actually desire such an attachment. Evie acknowledged Victor's attractiveness and extraordinary status but distinguished admiration from genuine romantic intention. Violet accepted that distinction, demonstrating that her tendency to organize relationships had developed enough for her to recognize when intervention would violate an individual's wishes. Although she remained enthusiastic about the possibility of additional women joining the family, she no longer considered usefulness or attraction sufficient justification for manufacturing a relationship.[20]
Her personality also retains a playful and frequently childish side despite the enormous authority she eventually acquires. Violet enjoys teasing Victor, competing with his other wives, trading private information and photographs within the family, and celebrating circumstances that allow her to spend additional time with him. She can behave with extraordinary dignity while confronting Gods or issuing Imperial orders and then immediately return to quarrelling with Sasha or becoming jealous over an apparently insignificant romantic detail. The contrast is particularly pronounced because Violet has experienced enough subjective Time to qualify as an ancient Being by the standards of nearly every civilization surrounding her, yet she retains the enthusiasm and emotional spontaneity that characterized her youth.
Violet's protectiveness gradually expands to include Victor's daughters, relatives and subjects. She becomes one of the senior mothers responsible for ensuring that the younger generation receives affection without being allowed to ignore discipline or the responsibilities attached to their extraordinary power. Her insistence that access to Victor's Draconic Bloodline must be earned reflects this mentality. Although she recognizes the natural desire of relatives to benefit from their connection to the Emperor, she believes that granting an immensely powerful lineage without evaluating character, loyalty and suitability would undermine the standards established by the dynasty. Her views are supported by several senior wives, including Ruby, Sasha, Kaguya, Anna and [[Scathach]], demonstrating that Violet's authority is increasingly exercised through collective judgment rather than unilateral demands.
Her understanding of motherhood also extends beyond biological relationships. Although Valentina is her own daughter, Violet regards Ophis, [[Nero]] and the other children accepted into Victor's household as members of the same family. She recognizes that even young Dragon Goddesses capable of devastating planets or Solar Systems remain children who should not automatically be burdened with the responsibilities of the adults raising them. During Victor's later efforts to educate his daughters while sustaining enormous Imperial rituals, Violet explained this principle to Albedo, emphasizing that Victor deliberately avoided demanding their assistance when capable adults could accept those burdens instead.[21]
By the late Imperial Era, Violet's response to threats has become considerably more strategic. Although she remains capable of immediate violent anger when Victor or the family is endangered, she increasingly seeks solutions that will prevent future crises rather than relying exclusively upon retaliation. Following the confrontation surrounding Moira and the Primordial Beings, Violet recognized that Victor's ability to threaten universal catastrophe depended upon exposing his own existence to extraordinary danger. She considered such a deterrent unacceptable because it required the person she most wanted to protect to become the family's ultimate weapon. Her subsequent proposal to develop an independent universal-level deterrent demonstrates the scale at which her protective instincts now operate: she is no longer merely attempting to keep one Human boy alive but preparing defenses against beings capable of threatening the existence of an entire cosmic civilization.
Despite these developments, Violet remains fundamentally obsessive. Her affection for Victor has not diminished through the passage of Time, and she continues noticing subtle changes in him almost immediately. Her maturity lies not in becoming detached or abandoning possessiveness, but in learning when her feelings should be expressed through affection, organization, restraint, diplomacy or decisive action. The same woman who once struggled to tolerate another wife standing beside Victor eventually becomes one of the principal authorities responsible for maintaining order within a household containing extraordinary numbers of wives, daughters, Gods and other supernatural Beings.

### History

#### Early Life
Violet was born into the ancient Clan Snow as the biological daughter of [[Agnes Elderblood Snow]] and [[Adonis Snow]]. Her mother possessed the rank of Vampire Countess and governed one of [[Nightingale]]'s principal aristocratic families, giving Violet a childhood surrounded by supernatural privilege, powerful servants and the expectations associated with inheriting an ancient Noble Vampire Bloodline. She inherited the Snow family's extraordinary Fire and other hereditary characteristics, including resistance to weaknesses that affected ordinary Vampires. Her upbringing also brought her into contact with Ruby Scarlett and Sasha Fulger, the young heiresses of two other great Vampire families who became her childhood friends.
Violet's life changed after she encountered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] Walker during childhood. Unlike people who might have reacted fearfully upon seeing the supernatural characteristics of a Vampire, Victor admired her appearance and called her beautiful. His reaction had an enormous effect upon Violet, whose attachment quickly developed into an obsession with the Human boy. Victor's extremely rare Blood and chronic illness made him particularly vulnerable within supernatural society, but Violet refused to allow those circumstances to separate them. She began secretly monitoring his life and arranging protection against other supernatural predators, including the use of magical concealment to prevent Vampires from identifying his extraordinary Blood.[13]
Over approximately sixteen years, Violet became increasingly familiar with Victor's habits, surroundings and personal history. She claimed the area surrounding his life as territory under her protection, eliminated Vampires who attempted to approach him and arranged for trusted supernatural individuals to assist with his security. Victor remained largely unaware of these activities, believing himself to be an ordinary sick Human whose future was limited by a condition associated with his rare RH-null Blood. Violet, however, regarded his continued survival as a personal responsibility and became determined to transform him into a Vampire before his Human mortality could take him away from her.
Her decision eventually culminated in the ancient Vampire marriage ritual intended to transform a Human spouse. Violet enlisted the assistance of Ruby and Sasha, expecting the ceremony to bind Victor exclusively to herself. Instead, Victor's abnormal Blood caused him to consume the Blood of all three women, transforming them into his original wives through a connection that ordinary Vampire marriage rituals were not designed to create. The unforeseen result became the foundation of Victor's later Bloodline and family, establishing relationships that would ultimately connect the major Vampire Clans and contribute to the creation of an independent Imperial civilization.[12]

### Synopsis

#### The Awakening
Violet entered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s conscious life immediately after his transformation. The newly awakened Vampire had escaped his parents' house because his unfamiliar bloodlust made remaining near ordinary Humans dangerous, and his attempts to satisfy his hunger through the Blood of criminals had only demonstrated that ordinary Human Blood was repulsive to his new instincts. Violet approached him without fear, allowing him to drink from her and establishing a supernatural exchange that immediately felt far more natural than the feeding he had attempted previously. Through their Blood, Victor began receiving memories connected to the transformation, gradually discovering that the beautiful white-haired woman already regarded him as her husband. Violet subsequently brought him to the Snow residence, where she introduced the basic rules of Vampire society and explained the abnormal characteristics he had inherited.[12]
Victor's existence contradicted established assumptions from the beginning. Humans transformed into Vampires were ordinarily considered Plebeians, while Noble Vampires inherited special Bloodline abilities through birth. Yet Victor possessed the Snow family's Fire and associated characteristics despite having been transformed from a Human, suggesting that the ritual had altered more than his species. Violet explained the supernatural marriage ritual and the consequences of exchanging Blood, while Victor began recognizing that the emotional intensity he experienced around her was connected both to their new marital bond and the amplification of feelings caused by Vampirism. Their mutual possessiveness rapidly became apparent, establishing a relationship whose intensity was far greater than ordinary romantic attraction.[22]
Violet also introduced Victor to the dangers associated with supernatural society. When Lucy and Karen appeared near Snow territory, she demonstrated the territorial authority possessed by an aristocratic Vampire and refused to allow outsiders to interfere freely with matters concerning her husband. The subsequent encounters with Inquisition hunters became particularly important because blessed equipment could injure Vampires even when the Humans wielding it possessed comparatively little physical power. Violet's immediate reaction to Victor being harmed was overwhelming anger, and Victor had to restrain her long enough to obtain useful information. The incident revealed that the emotional amplification associated with Vampirism could endanger even powerful Noble Vampires when they allowed instinctive hatred to overwhelm tactical judgment.
The situation became more complicated when Kaguya investigated the abnormal circumstances surrounding Victor's transformation. Ruby and Sasha had both become connected to him during the ceremony, and prolonged separation was producing increasingly severe bloodlust because the ritual had established a relationship requiring mutual Blood exchange. Violet initially refused to accept the possibility that her childhood friends had become Victor's wives, regarding it as a threat to the exclusive future she had imagined for years. When Sasha eventually appeared before Victor, their confrontation threatened to become a serious conflict until he refused to choose one woman and abandon the other. Sasha asserted her own feelings, Violet reluctantly accepted that the bond could not be ignored, and the two began the difficult process of sharing a husband without destroying their friendship.[23]
Violet's emotional attachment was tested again when Victor confronted the stronger hunters operating under Mizuki. The battle revealed how dangerous blessed weapons could become against inexperienced Vampires, and Victor suffered injuries severe enough that the original wives were forced to coordinate a retreat. Violet initially reacted with anger and a desire to retaliate, but Ruby emphasized that protecting Victor was more important than remaining on the battlefield. Violet and Ruby combined their Fire and Ice to create a thick cloud of steam, while Sasha carried Victor and Violet away from the immediate danger. The cooperation demonstrated that the three women could overcome their jealousy when a genuine threat endangered their husband.[24]
Victor's subsequent exchanges of Blood with all three women revealed more of the transformation ritual and the circumstances surrounding his earlier Human life. Violet's role became increasingly clear: she had known about his extraordinary Blood for years and had secretly eliminated supernatural threats long before he became aware of their existence. Instead of being frightened by the revelation that his life had been monitored, Victor recognized the depth of her attachment, while Violet found that the husband she had spent years protecting was far more accepting of her obsessive personality than she had expected. By the conclusion of the opening volume, the abnormal marriage had become a genuine family relationship connecting three powerful Noble Vampire heiresses to the newly transformed Victor.

#### Vampire Society
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s introduction to [[Nightingale]] began when [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett|Scathach Scarlett]] recognized his unusual potential and decided to undertake his personal training. The ancient Countess transported him to the Vampire world without allowing his wives to prevent the departure, temporarily separating Violet from the husband she had only recently acquired. Violet eventually followed him to Nightingale, where their reunion immediately revealed that her possessiveness had not diminished during his absence. However, the experience also exposed the difference between the young Noble Vampires and an ancient warrior whose training, control and practical knowledge far exceeded their own.
Violet's obsessive concentration upon Victor had caused her to neglect aspects of her personal development. Although she possessed extraordinary Fire through Clan Snow, her natural talent did not compensate completely for weaknesses in technique and control. [[Hilda Snow Elderblood]], the experienced woman responsible for much of the Snow family's practical training, consequently subjected Violet to demanding instruction intended to correct those deficiencies. Violet initially preferred spending every possible moment near Victor, but the increasing dangers surrounding him demonstrated that improving her own abilities was necessary if she wanted to remain capable of protecting him.
Another important development involved Kaguya. The maid had originally served Violet through the historical relationship between Clan Blank and Clan Snow, but her interest in Victor was becoming increasingly personal. Rather than insist upon retaining Kaguya solely because of inherited obligation, Violet released her from service so that she could choose Victor as her master. The decision was unusual for someone so possessive, but it demonstrated that Violet could recognize genuine loyalty and personal wishes when the circumstances affected someone she considered important. Kaguya subsequently became increasingly central to Victor's household and eventually developed into one of its most trusted members.
Violet also received an opportunity to spend private time with Victor after months of training and political conflict. Their trip to [[Paris]] allowed them to behave more like an ordinary married couple, shopping, eating and enjoying one another's company without the constant intervention of other Vampires or supernatural enemies. Violet deliberately chose an appearance connected with their earlier history, placing sentimental value upon details that Victor remembered from before he fully understood the supernatural world. The trip became particularly meaningful because the circumstances of their abnormal marriage had rarely allowed her to enjoy Victor's exclusive attention without immediately competing with Ruby or Sasha.
During their continued exchanges of Blood, Victor experienced memories revealing more of Violet's childhood and the extraordinary period she had spent secretly protecting him. The revelation established that their relationship had not originated suddenly with his transformation but had developed through years of devotion that Victor had been unable to perceive. Violet's attachment remained extreme, but Victor's acceptance of it allowed their relationship to become more open rather than forcing her to conceal the circumstances of her earlier actions.[13]

#### A New Pillar
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s recognition as the Fifth Vampire Count transformed Violet's position within [[Nightingale]]. She was no longer merely the daughter of Agnes Snow or the heiress of an ancient Clan; her marriage connected Clan Snow to a new Pillar whose power, political influence and relationships with other Noble families were expanding at extraordinary speed. Clan Alucard became an increasingly important institution, and Violet's position as First Wife gave her influence over matters affecting the relationship between Victor's household and the established aristocracy of the Vampire Kingdom.
Violet continued displaying her extreme protectiveness during the assassination attempts and political confrontations surrounding Victor. When Inquisition forces targeted Sasha and Kaguya, the resulting violence threatened to expose supernatural society to ordinary Human authorities, forcing members of the household to coordinate both retaliation and the concealment of evidence. Violet participated in the family's response, while Victor's anger demonstrated that he increasingly considered attacks against his servants and dependents just as unacceptable as attacks against his wives. The incident also reinforced Violet's growing understanding that Victor's family could not function if its members treated one another as expendable political or military resources.
Her administrative instincts began developing as Clan Alucard expanded. Victor attracted new servants, allies, dependents and potential romantic partners, creating an environment in which unrestrained jealousy could easily destabilize the household. Violet gradually learned to distinguish genuine threats from individuals whose relationships with Victor did not automatically endanger her marriage. Nevertheless, her obsession remained extraordinarily methodical. She maintained an extensive diary recording Victor's behavior, preferences and activities, demonstrating that her attachment involved careful observation rather than simple emotional intensity. The diary also became a source of humor within the family because its detail exceeded what most people would consider ordinary devotion.[25]
Violet's position also changed as outsiders began evaluating Victor's growing faction. Being his First Wife became a source of authority independent of her mother's rank, and her familiarity with the principal members of his household allowed her to coordinate relationships that conventional political officials could not easily manage. Although she remained inexperienced compared with ancient administrators, Violet was beginning to develop the skills that would later make her indispensable to the Elderblood dynasty.

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Violet's responsibilities expanded as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] began confronting threats beyond the political disputes of [[Nightingale]]. She became increasingly involved in the affairs of Clan Snow and the broader household while Ruby, Sasha and the other women developed their own specializations. The original three wives gradually operated as a coordinated group rather than spending all their efforts competing for Victor's attention, recognizing that their husband's expanding influence required reliable cooperation among the people closest to him.
The discovery of extraordinary supernatural powers and the development of Victor's independent faction also changed Violet's attitude toward authority. Although she continued considering herself the First Wife, she increasingly recognized that personal seniority needed to be supported through judgment and the ability to manage complicated relationships. Her organizational talent proved valuable when women with different backgrounds, loyalties and expectations became connected to Victor. Rather than treat every new relationship as an immediate threat, Violet began establishing informal procedures through which newcomers could understand the family and its existing members.
Her authority became more visible during preparations for larger supernatural conflicts. Violet increasingly represented the household during Victor's absence, coordinating servants and family members while responding to outsiders who attempted to exploit personal connections with her husband. The shift was significant because her original impulse had been to define Victor's household according to her exclusive desires. By this period, she had begun protecting an existing collective identity even when its members included people she would once have considered rivals.

#### Demonic War
The Demonic War accelerated [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s separation from the political structures of the Vampire Kingdom. As the conflict spread through [[Nightingale]] and the supernatural world, the women surrounding him increasingly recognized that their decisions affected more than the survival of one Noble Vampire Clan. Violet remained one of the principal figures coordinating the expanding family, while her position as Agnes's daughter connected Victor to the Snow family's resources and political influence.
A major transformation occurred when Victor began incorporating his wives directly into his Vampire Progenitor lineage. Agnes and Violet were among the first to undergo the process, receiving his Blood in a manner that altered their supernatural foundations without erasing the hereditary characteristics of Clan Snow. Their Fire and associated qualities remained intact while becoming stronger, demonstrating that Victor's Bloodline could improve existing racial characteristics rather than replacing every recipient with an identical set of abilities. Violet consequently acquired a deeper biological connection to Victor while preserving the ancestry and identity she had possessed since birth.[11]
The development also established an important precedent for Victor's later Dragon lineage. Violet's transformation revealed that his Blood could preserve individuality even while producing substantial supernatural evolution, allowing members of different families and [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] to develop in ways reflecting their own histories. For Violet, the change strengthened her ability to remain beside Victor as he approached levels of power increasingly distant from those of ordinary Vampires.
Victor's subsequent imprisonment within Hell created another important period of separation. Violet and several other wives reacted with overwhelming anger upon discovering that he had been removed from their reach, while his extended experience within Hell further widened the difference between his accumulated knowledge and that of the people waiting outside. His eventual return marked the beginning of another stage in Violet's development as both a wife and a member of the increasingly independent civilization forming around him.

#### King Among Kings
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] returned from Hell after experiencing approximately seven hundred subjective years of warfare, rulership and personal transformation. Although far less Time had passed for Violet, she immediately recognized that his experience had changed the way he expressed himself. He had learned to conceal vulnerability beneath the mentality of a powerful ruler because openly displaying weakness in Hell could invite betrayal or exploitation. Yet Violet continued perceiving emotional changes that others failed to notice, demonstrating the extraordinary familiarity she had acquired through years of observing him. Their discussions revealed that even Victor's increasingly advanced supernatural perception could not replace the intimate understanding possessed by someone who had studied his behavior since childhood.[15]
The same period clarified Violet's position as First Wife. Rather than deriving authority primarily from physical superiority, she possessed influence through her knowledge of Victor, her relationships with the other wives and her ability to organize the enormous household. Women who might have rejected orders from another powerful combatant could accept Violet's intervention because they recognized her history, responsibilities and commitment to protecting the family. Victor also supported her position while refusing to allow it to become a justification for arbitrary domination, establishing an important distinction between genuine seniority and the unrestricted imposition of personal desires.[14][16]
Violet also confronted the unresolved emotions surrounding her father, [[Adonis Snow]]. His death had left both Violet and Agnes without the opportunity for an ordinary farewell, particularly because his unusual existence had become connected to Victor's development. After obtaining sufficiently advanced access to information preserved within the [[Akashic Records]], Victor reconstructed an informational echo of Adonis containing memories and personality characteristics capable of interacting with his family. The manifestation was not equivalent to restoring the original Adonis permanently, but it allowed Violet and Agnes to speak with a version of him and address grief that had remained unresolved. For Violet, the encounter demonstrated that even extraordinary supernatural power could not simply erase every emotional consequence of death, although it could sometimes provide opportunities for understanding and closure.
Victor subsequently spent private time with Violet, Ruby and Sasha together, returning attention to the three women whose ritual had begun his supernatural life. Their relationship had become almost unrecognizable compared with the opening chapters: Victor was now an ancient and exceptionally powerful Being by ordinary standards, while the women who had originally transformed him were confronting the possibility that their natural potential alone would no longer be enough to remain capable of supporting him.
Violet consequently participated in the Tower of Nightmares, a training environment designed to confront participants with situations that deliberately exploited their weaknesses. Her earliest attempts demonstrated excessive reliance upon overwhelming Fire, and the Tower punished the assumption that an opponent could always be defeated through greater destructive output. Violet encountered enemies resistant to her preferred Element, hidden dangers and unfamiliar environments where ordinary instincts proved insufficient. Repeated defeats forced her to improve her judgment and employ the fundamental Vampire techniques she had neglected while concentrating upon Victor and her inherited Fire.
During these trials, Violet demonstrated the development of short-term Future Vision, allowing her to perceive approximately five seconds ahead and use the information to avoid attacks. She also employed ordinary Vampire transformation and movement techniques, including turning into a swarm of bats, demonstrating that her natural abilities could become considerably more effective when combined with the judgment necessary to use them appropriately. The experience transformed her combat development by emphasizing prediction, versatility and tactical awareness rather than simple elemental superiority.
Her Future Vision subsequently advanced beyond the scale of individual combat. During a major gathering, Violet attempted to observe possibilities much farther into the future and encountered an older version of herself possessing the appearance of an imposing white-winged Dragon Empress. The experience was extraordinarily demanding because perceiving distant futures placed considerably greater strain upon her than examining the immediate consequences of an attack. The encounter also revealed that her supernatural perception could expose possibilities that had not yet become established events, requiring her to distinguish potential futures from the current Reality.[26]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ascension as the Dragon God of [[Chaos]] created the foundation for another transformation of his family. The women surrounding him had already developed beyond the limitations of ordinary Noble Vampires, but their existing racial foundations remained increasingly inadequate when compared with the beings threatening the emerging Dragon civilization. Victor therefore offered selected members of his household the opportunity to become genuine [[True Dragons]], emphasizing that the transformation would remain voluntary and that each recipient's identity should be preserved.
Violet accepted alongside Ruby, Sasha and numerous other members of the family. Victor's Essence and Draconic power reconstructed their bodies and Souls through a process involving enormous Dragon eggs, allowing their existing characteristics to become integrated into a superior biological foundation. When Violet emerged, she possessed the physiology of a genuine Blood Dragon rather than an ordinary Vampire carrying temporary Draconic enhancements. Her Snow Fire, accumulated powers and personal characteristics remained part of the resulting existence, demonstrating that Victor's lineage could preserve the distinct identities of its members while transforming them into a fundamentally different Race.[17]
Her combat development continued after Dragonization. During a confrontation with [[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]] in Victor's coliseum, Violet displayed considerably greater proficiency in direct combat than the impulsive Fire specialist she had once been. Her movement, tactical decisions and ability to combine supernatural powers with physical techniques demonstrated the cumulative value of her earlier training, particularly the lessons forced upon her within the Tower of Nightmares.
The collapse of [[Asgard]] and the events surrounding Ragnarök subsequently expanded Violet's responsibilities beyond family affairs. Victor's faction became involved in disputes concerning the former Norse territories, their Divine resources and the extraordinary problems created by the integration of different supernatural afterlife systems. Violet participated in the negotiations and demonstrated an increasingly refined understanding of how political and technological resources could be used to influence Pantheons whose leaders were accustomed to measuring authority primarily through Divine power.
One of the most important developments involved the appearance of [[Shiro-Hime]], Violet's extraordinarily advanced vessel. The technology became a major bargaining instrument during negotiations over former Norse assets, demonstrating that Victor's civilization could offer capabilities that established Pantheons had not achieved through ordinary Divine Artifacts. Violet used the negotiations to exploit the expectations and rivalries of the participating Gods rather than attempting to win every advantage through intimidation alone.
It was also during this period that Violet's Divine development became increasingly explicit. Her two Divinities, Order and Obsession, reflected qualities that had defined her since childhood. The combination was highly unusual because her Obsession influenced the way Order manifested, turning what might ordinarily have become a detached universal authority into a supernatural expression of the relationships surrounding Victor. Her Divine presence affected the Gods participating in the negotiations, who recognized that the emerging power was unlike ordinary Divinities with which they were familiar.[9]
Violet's growing confidence also changed her treatment of women entering Victor's household. Instead of automatically resenting every powerful Goddess who approached him, she began supporting the integration of individuals whose loyalty and personal relationships made them suitable for the family. Her assistance with Hela's developing position represented an important example of this transition, demonstrating that the woman who had once been unwilling to share Victor with two childhood friends could now recognize the practical and emotional value of a larger household.

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
The family's accelerated developmental era brought extraordinary changes to Violet's life. Through controlled temporal environments, she and many of the people closest to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] experienced approximately two thousand subjective years while comparatively little Time passed outside. The period allowed enormous development in their supernatural abilities, personal relationships and responsibilities, transforming members of the original Vampire household into mature Dragon Gods and Goddesses with experience far beyond their apparent physical ages.
Violet eventually became pregnant with Victor's daughter, [[Valentina Victoria Elderblood]]. The pregnancy lasted approximately one thousand subjective years because the child inherited the extraordinary supernatural foundation of two highly developed Draconic and Divine parents. Valentina's birth established Violet as a biological mother and introduced another important responsibility into her life, extending her protective instincts beyond Victor and the women who had originally formed their household.[3]
Her position as a Dragon Goddess also became increasingly formal. By Chapter 1023, Violet was explicitly identified as the Dragon Goddess of Order, and her authority could be applied directly to supernatural problems that ordinary administrative influence could not resolve. When members of the [[Blood God Religion]] recovered an artifact carrying dangerous supernatural contamination, Violet personally examined it rather than allowing inexperienced subordinates to expose themselves to an unknown curse. She also assisted the organization in obtaining controlled access to Imperial technology, demonstrating how her Divine authority and administrative responsibilities increasingly complemented one another.[7]
Within the Dream World created for Victor's family, Violet occupied the position of Queen of Mercenaries. Unlike Sasha, who became an independent adventurer, or Ruby, who developed economic and organizational influence through another path, Violet naturally established herself as the leader of a powerful group. The experience reinforced her aptitude for commanding individuals and creating systems of cooperation even when the artificial world imposed conditions substantially different from her ordinary existence.[8]
The formal establishment of the [[Elderblood Empire]] expanded Violet's responsibilities further. Victor's territory had become too large and diverse to be governed as a conventional Vampire Clan, requiring an increasingly sophisticated political hierarchy, specialized departments and methods of coordinating supernatural institutions across immense distances. Violet participated in the creation and refinement of these structures alongside the other senior Empresses, using the experience she had accumulated while organizing Victor's household to address problems affecting the wider civilization.
The development of the Empire also forced her to reconsider the traditional methods through which monarchies secured alliances. Victor refused to treat his daughters as political resources who could be exchanged through arranged marriages, removing an approach that many ruling dynasties had historically regarded as normal. Violet and the other administrators consequently had to rely upon economic cooperation, diplomacy, technological advantages, military deterrence and genuine personal loyalty rather than attempting to bind every important family through marriage. The decision reflected Victor's conviction that his daughters were children to be protected and raised rather than instruments of Imperial expansion.
Violet's Divine authority eventually produced one of its most important direct demonstrations during an operation involving a Demon in Yama's Hell. Her opponent found that Space had been sealed and his access to Energy and supernatural techniques had become restricted before he could properly understand who was pursuing him. Violet appeared in an imposing Divine manifestation, explaining through her actions that her authority could impose a particular interpretation of Order upon the surrounding Reality. The affected Demon recognized that ordinary countermeasures were insufficient and that resisting the Goddess would require a superior Divine authority or an appropriate opposing supernatural power. The confrontation established the practical significance of Violet's Divinity as something far more versatile than ordinary Fire manipulation.[27]
As the Imperial family continued expanding, Violet also became increasingly involved in determining which extraordinary women might be suitable to join Victor's household. She evaluated the relationships surrounding figures such as Kali, Albedo and [[Dún Scáith|Dun Scaith]], recognizing that individuals possessing overwhelming power could become important allies or sources of conflict depending upon their emotional and political connections. Her efforts were not always appropriate, particularly when she approached personal relationships as problems that could be resolved through organization, but she gradually learned to distinguish genuine affection from strategic convenience.
By the end of this period, Violet had become one of the central administrators of a civilization preparing to confront the higher regions of the Cosmos. Her authority now extended beyond Clan Snow and Victor's immediate household, encompassing the coordination of institutions, supernatural diplomacy and the development of the Empire's political identity.

#### Those Who Rule Reality
The discovery of civilizations beyond [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Sector revealed that the [[Elderblood Empire]] remained underdeveloped compared with some of the powers operating in the higher regions of the Cosmos. Victor consequently initiated an extraordinary temporal project intended to provide approximately 200,000 years of internal Imperial development while roughly one external year passed. The ritual required immense supernatural Energy and sustained cooperation among Victor, Jeanne, Natalia and numerous other powerful members of the family. Violet participated as one of the senior Empresses responsible for ensuring that the civilization could continue developing while its strongest individuals maintained the temporal structure.[28]
The accelerated period transformed the scale of Violet's administrative responsibilities. The Empire developed across enormous Galactic territories, established new institutions, expanded its military and integrated populations originating from different supernatural [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] and civilizations. Violet became involved in the organization of these systems, coordinating personnel and ensuring that the enormous passage of subjective Time did not cause the ruling family to lose control of the civilization it was attempting to strengthen. Her experience as First Wife proved useful because many of the Empire's most important officials were also members of Victor's household, creating a political structure in which personal relationships and institutional responsibilities were closely connected.
Despite the immense changes in her experience and authority, Violet retained her familiar possessiveness. The situation involving [[Tyche]] demonstrated that she had become willing to accept additional women into Victor's family, but could still approach romantic relationships with the same excessive determination that once made her unwilling to share him. Tyche's advanced control over Luck and probability created a strategic concern because her abilities could influence outcomes in ways that conventional defenses could not reliably counter. Violet consequently attempted to encourage a relationship between the Goddess and Victor, treating the possibility partly as a means of incorporating an exceptional supernatural authority into the Empire. Victor and several senior wives objected to the manipulation, forcing Violet to acknowledge that even a potentially beneficial relationship needed to develop through genuine personal choice rather than an administrative decision.[19]
Violet's strategic instincts nevertheless remained valuable when applied to genuine military and political problems. When Victor developed protections intended to reduce the danger of foreign supernatural corruption affecting Imperial soldiers, she immediately considered whether similar defenses could eventually be extended to the broader population. The idea demonstrated how her thinking had expanded beyond protecting Victor personally, since the same principles that prevented enemies from manipulating elite combatants might eventually safeguard ordinary citizens living across enormous Imperial territories.
Her emotional understanding of Victor also survived the accelerated era. After he obtained dangerous intelligence concerning external powers, Violet recognized that something was disturbing him even though he attempted to maintain his usual composure. She used her advanced perception to examine possible consequences and involved Anna when additional assistance became necessary. The incident demonstrated that Violet's intimate familiarity with Victor remained relevant even after both had experienced enough subjective Time to exceed the lifespans of most ancient civilizations.
One of her most extraordinary demonstrations of Divine authority occurred when [[Frederick Winter]] and [[Andrew Walter]] requested assistance during an Imperial operation on a hostile alien planet. Instead of merely supplying a quantity of Energy remotely, Violet personally directed her attention toward the invocation and manifested a Divine projection. She examined the circumstances surrounding the soldiers, assessed the hostile creatures and scanned the wider Solar System to ensure that unrelated Life would not be harmed by her intervention. Her decision reflected a degree of restraint and situational awareness that contrasted with the indiscriminate destruction often associated with extraordinarily powerful supernatural combatants.[1]
Violet then enlarged her projection to approximately 1,346 meters, manifesting a crown and an elaborate staff before invoking her authority over Order. Golden-white Energy spread across the planet, immediately pacifying hostile creatures, stabilizing its dangerous climate and purifying the atmosphere. The soldiers witnessing the event regarded it as an overwhelming Divine miracle, while Frederick explained that the manifestation represented only a projection of Violet's true power. Violet privately considered the spectacle excessive because she could have achieved the practical objective through a far simpler application of her authority, but she deliberately followed Victor's philosophy that the presentation of power could possess political and psychological value of its own.[1]
The incident demonstrated that Violet's authority was not limited to destroying enemies. Order could directly alter the conditions governing an environment, pacify hostile beings and stabilize phenomena across an entire planet without requiring its extermination. The distinction gave her a strategic role different from that of Goddesses whose most prominent abilities concerned overwhelming offensive power.
Violet also participated in the restrictions governing access to Victor's Dragon Bloodline. Although Victor was comparatively willing to consider granting lesser Draconic transformations to qualified members of his extended family, Violet joined Sasha, Ruby, Kaguya, Anna and [[Scathach]] in insisting that entry into the lineage must be earned. Blood relations alone were insufficient, since even weaker branches of the Emperor's Bloodline possessed extraordinary potential and remained connected to Victor as their Progenitor. Violet considered unrestricted distribution dangerous both for the integrity of the dynasty and for the wider civilization that would eventually need to coexist with its descendants.[29]
Her ability to use Order defensively became evident during [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]]'s training confrontation with [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]]. Scathach invoked Heptagrammakos, an exceptionally complicated Draconic–Eldritch expression that enveloped the reinforced dimension in abnormal Darkness and manifested enormous numbers of supernatural eyes and mouths. The phenomenon began affecting the minds of spectators, producing headaches and deteriorating their mental stability. Violet intervened through her Order authority, reducing the influence upon the observers while the others examined the nature of the technique. Her actions demonstrated that Order could serve as a means of stabilizing individuals exposed to hostile supernatural conditions rather than functioning exclusively through direct attacks.[30]
The birth of [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]], the daughter of Victor and Tyche, subsequently created a crisis involving the System and the Primordial Beings. Moira's extraordinary relationship with Luck and Destiny attracted the attention of cosmic authorities who wished to incorporate her into the established structure of Reality, but Victor refused to surrender his daughter. The resulting confrontation exposed an important strategic weakness: although Victor possessed means of threatening the Primordial order, employing those means could require him to damage his own existence or unleash consequences catastrophic enough to endanger the Universe itself.
Violet considered that dependence unacceptable. Following the crisis, she remained with Sasha and examined what could be done to protect the family without requiring Victor to become their only effective deterrent. Her proposed solution was an independent Imperial weapon capable of threatening the entire Universe if necessary, forcing even overwhelmingly powerful enemies to reconsider attacking the Elderblood family. Rather than pursue an immediate conflict with the Primordial Beings, she wanted to establish a threat severe enough that they would have no reason to risk provoking the Empire.[31]
The project would require cooperation among the Empire's greatest researchers and potentially involve extraordinary powers such as [[Eldritch Energy]] and principles associated with the END. Violet recognized that such a weapon would be dangerous even to the civilization creating it, but regarded the alternative of forcing Victor to repeatedly endanger himself as worse. Her reasoning demonstrated the full development of her protective instincts, which had originated in the desire to preserve the life of a single Human boy and now extended to the creation of strategic deterrence against universal-level threats.
The Empire's approaching two-hundred-thousandth anniversary also brought the selection of a new Imperial capital. Victor decided that [[Yggdrasil]] should eventually remain protected as the historical cradle of the civilization rather than being endlessly expanded to accommodate its population. Mars, by contrast, could be reconstructed into an enormous megaplanet capable of supporting trillions of inhabitants and functioning as the political and logistical center of Imperial civilization. Victor, Anna, Natalia, Jeanne and the [[World Trees]] participated in the transformation, producing extraordinary infrastructure that allowed the Empire to communicate and transport resources across its vast territories.
After the reconstruction, Victor entrusted Violet with the official announcement. Working with [[Akasha Elderblood]], she prepared a broadcast transmitted throughout the Empire, allowing enormous projections of the Empress to appear across distant worlds and Galactic settlements. Violet explained that the civilization had expanded beyond the world where it originally began and that preserving Yggdrasil would allow future generations to remember their origins. She then formally proclaimed Mars the new capital of the Elderblood Empire, establishing the planet as the center of its next historical era.[4]
The announcement demonstrated the extraordinary transformation of Violet's political position. The young Vampire who had once secretly watched a sickly Human boy was now speaking officially for an Empire distributed across galaxies. Her authority reflected not only her position as Victor's First Wife but the accumulated experience and institutional responsibilities she had developed during the preceding millennia.
Following the proclamation, the Empire initiated the incorporation of the neighboring Arcane, [[Nightingale]] and Samar Sectors into its greater structure. Their integration created new logistical, administrative and cosmological problems, but also consolidated territories that had remained historically separated despite their growing connections to Victor. Violet participated in the continuing organization of the Empire as these formerly independent regions became part of the civilization governed by the Elderblood dynasty.
The incorporation also provided another opportunity for Violet to demonstrate the boundaries she had developed concerning romantic relationships. When Albedo brought Evie Moriarty before her and attempted to obtain approval for a relationship with Victor, Violet refused after determining that Evie did not genuinely want such an attachment. Evie admitted that she admired Victor and considered him attractive, but explained that admiration did not amount to a desire to become his wife. Violet accepted the distinction, demonstrating that the earlier conflict involving Tyche had produced a lasting change in the way she approached family integration.[20]
The research into an independent universal deterrent continued. During discussions concerning the impossible requirements of constructing a weapon capable of threatening even Primordial-level Beings, Velina Elderblood proposed an unusual approach that attracted the attention of Velnorah and the Empire's researchers. After examining the idea, they concluded that it possessed genuine potential and brought the proposal to Violet. The Empress immediately approved further investigation, regarding the possibility as another means of ensuring that no enemy could threaten Victor merely because he remained the Empire's principal source of overwhelming power.[32]
Meanwhile, Victor's condition became increasingly concerning after sustaining the enormous temporal ritual for approximately two hundred thousand subjective years. The strain placed upon him had increased substantially, and his ability to participate in ordinary Imperial administration was limited by the need to preserve the structure supporting the Empire's accelerated development. Violet recognized the deterioration almost immediately because of her continuous attention to his condition, even though Victor did not openly complain about the burden. She consequently assumed additional responsibilities for organizing the newly integrated territories, working with Albedo and Akasha to maintain the administrative systems Victor could no longer supervise personally.[21]
During this period, Albedo questioned why Victor refused to demand help from daughters who possessed enough supernatural power to contribute to his ritual. Violet explained that Victor considered such burdens the responsibility of adults and did not want his children forced to sacrifice their own experiences merely because they were extraordinarily powerful. Even young Dragon Goddesses capable of destroying planets or Solar Systems remained children within the family's understanding, and Victor preferred searching for alternatives rather than treating their natural abilities as an obligation to support the Empire. Violet understood the apparent contradictions within his reasoning, but regarded his determination to preserve their daughters' childhoods as an expression of the protective personality she had known since before his transformation.[21]
Her responsibilities continued increasing as Imperial research advanced and the Empire consolidated its newly acquired territories. The same tendency toward organization that had originally manifested as possessive interference in Victor's personal relationships now allowed Violet to coordinate enormous administrative systems while the Emperor remained occupied with sustaining the civilization's development. She worked with Akasha to distribute instructions and manage personnel, demonstrating that her authority as Empress had become a practical institutional necessity rather than merely a ceremonial extension of Victor's position.
Another important incident occurred when an Imperial Dragon named [[Ryujin Ikaros]] discovered an unusual object associated with fragments of Divine existence. The object attached itself to his Soul, creating a situation that could not be resolved safely through ordinary physical removal. Akasha detected the abnormal event and informed Violet, who summoned Ruby because of her extensive scientific and supernatural knowledge. The Empire's communication systems transmitted the information almost immediately, while Victor also became aware of the danger through his own monitoring network. Violet treated Ryujin as a subject whose survival mattered rather than a disposable individual whose death would conveniently eliminate a dangerous problem.[33]
The incident also demonstrated how much the Empire's infrastructure had developed since the transformation of Mars. Information concerning a potentially dangerous supernatural object could travel from a distant Imperial location to the highest authorities in effectively instantaneous Time, allowing Violet, Ruby and Victor to respond before the situation escalated. Violet's reaction reflected the institutional philosophy she had helped establish: extraordinary supernatural resources should be used to resolve a threat while preserving Imperial citizens whenever possible, rather than assuming that an individual lacking sufficient power could be sacrificed for administrative convenience.
The developing crisis beyond the Sector barrier eventually forced Violet to prepare for a more direct confrontation. As dangerous gaps began appearing in the boundary separating the Empire from the Upper Sectors, Victor determined that the situation could no longer be postponed indefinitely. Violet discussed the progress of the universal deterrent and other research efforts, acknowledging that the scientists had not yet discovered a satisfactory solution capable of fulfilling the required conditions without creating contradictions in the underlying supernatural systems. The problem was not a failure of effort, but the extraordinary difficulty of producing a genuine threat to Beings whose existence operated on a cosmological scale.[34]
Victor eventually declared that the Empire would go to war within approximately one year. Violet initially reacted with surprise at how soon the confrontation would occur, but immediately recovered and accepted responsibility for preparing the civilization. She departed to activate the Imperial institutions necessary for the coming conflict, demonstrating that even when confronted with an unexpectedly accelerated strategic deadline, she possessed the experience and authority required to organize a response.[34]
When Victor subsequently dispatched Krieg through the Sector barrier, Violet questioned whether sending one Herald into potentially overwhelming opposition was appropriate. Victor explained that Krieg possessed extraordinary capabilities and could operate as a member of his most powerful independent forces. Violet initially considered the numerical disadvantages he might confront, but quickly understood the broader strategy when Victor alluded to the nature of the warfare he had experienced within Hell. Her ability to recognize his intentions without requiring an extensive explanation again demonstrated the intimate understanding that had distinguished their relationship since its earliest days.[34]
By the latest documented events, Violet remains Victor's First Wife, a mature Blood Dragon and Dragon Goddess of Order and Obsession, the mother of Valentina, a senior ruler of the Elderblood Empire and one of the principal administrators preparing the civilization for confrontation with powers beyond its original Sector. Although she has developed into an ancient Imperial authority capable of coordinating institutions across enormous territories, her devotion to Victor and instinctive desire to protect their family remain the defining motivations behind her actions.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Violet's husband, the father of their daughter Valentina and the individual around whom much of her personality and development has been structured. Violet became attached to Victor during childhood after he responded to her Vampiric appearance with admiration rather than fear. She subsequently watched over him for approximately sixteen years, arranging supernatural protection and eliminating threats while he remained unaware of the world surrounding him. Her determination to prevent his death eventually led her to perform the Vampire marriage ritual that transformed him, an act that unintentionally connected Ruby and Sasha to him as well.[13]
Their relationship is characterized by an unusually intense compatibility between two individuals possessing extreme forms of possessiveness. Violet's obsessive attachment does not frighten Victor because he recognizes similar tendencies within himself, and his willingness to accept her personality allows them to express affection in ways that ordinary couples might regard as excessive. Nevertheless, Victor does not interpret his love for Violet as permission for her to control every decision affecting the household. He challenges her when her attempts to organize relationships violate the wishes of others, as demonstrated during the conflict surrounding [[Tyche]], while Violet in turn remains one of the few people capable of recognizing when his outward composure conceals genuine emotional distress.
Violet's understanding of Victor becomes increasingly important as his existence develops beyond the standards of ordinary supernatural beings. After he spends centuries in Hell learning to conceal vulnerability, she can still perceive emotional changes that escape the notice of ancient Gods and exceptionally powerful observers. Her relationship with him therefore extends beyond romantic obsession into a form of intimate recognition that remains useful even when Victor's overwhelming strength makes conventional physical protection increasingly unnecessary.[16]
Their marriage eventually produces Valentina and survives enormous periods of accelerated Time. Despite Victor's development into a God Emperor and Violet's assumption of Imperial responsibilities, their private interactions retain much of the playfulness, jealousy and affectionate possessiveness that characterized their earliest relationship.

#### Ruby Elderblood Scarlett
[[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] is Violet's childhood friend and one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s three original wives. Their friendship predates Victor's transformation, but the abnormal marriage ritual initially created substantial tension because Violet had intended to become his exclusive wife. Ruby's comparatively analytical temperament provides a contrast to Violet's emotional intensity, and their differences occasionally produce arguments even after they become comfortable within the same household.
Over Time, their relationship becomes one of the principal foundations of Victor's family. Ruby develops extensive scientific and technological responsibilities while Violet concentrates more heavily upon organization, diplomacy and Imperial administration. Their specializations frequently complement one another, particularly when supernatural discoveries require both technical analysis and political decisions concerning how the Empire should respond.
The two continue cooperating throughout the Imperial Era, including the investigation of dangerous supernatural discoveries such as the Divine fragments attached to [[Ryujin Ikaros]]. Their friendship therefore develops far beyond the rivalry created by the original marriage, becoming a relationship between senior Empresses whose combined experience helps govern an enormous civilization.[33]

#### Sasha Elderblood Fulger
[[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] is Violet's childhood friend and the third woman incorporated into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s original marriage ritual. Sasha's unexpected status as Victor's wife created one of Violet's earliest major emotional conflicts, since Violet initially refused to share the husband she had spent years planning to claim. Sasha eventually asserted her own feelings, forcing both women to acknowledge that their relationship had become permanently more complicated than their childhood friendship.[23]
Their rivalry continues throughout the series, but gradually becomes less destructive as the women confront increasingly serious threats. Sasha develops extraordinary combat experience and later assumes responsibilities involving the Empire's police, while Violet becomes one of the principal administrators coordinating the political structure surrounding Victor. They retain a habit of teasing and competing with one another, particularly when discussing their respective families, daughters or influence within the household.
Their cooperation becomes particularly important following the Primordial crisis involving Moira. Rather than focus upon personal rivalry, Violet and Sasha jointly examine the Empire's strategic weakness and support the development of a deterrent capable of protecting Victor without requiring him to endanger his own existence. Their relationship consequently represents both the enduring competitiveness of the original wives and the mature cooperation necessary for governing the Empire.[31]

#### Agnes Elderblood Snow
[[Agnes Elderblood Snow]] is Violet's biological mother and the former Vampire Countess responsible for much of her original upbringing. Their relationship reflects the strong emotions associated with Clan Snow, including intense familial affection and the possessive tendencies that both women frequently display toward [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
Agnes's eventual romantic relationship with Victor created an unusual complication because of her history with [[Adonis Snow]]. Violet initially needed to distinguish between Agnes developing genuine feelings for Victor himself and attempting to treat him as a replacement for her deceased husband. As the relationship developed, the family gradually accepted that Agnes's attachment was directed toward Victor rather than merely his connection with Adonis.
Victor later incorporated Violet and Agnes into his Progenitor Bloodline together, strengthening their Snow ancestry without erasing the characteristics inherited through their original family. Their continued development eventually placed both women among the mature supernatural authorities governing the Elderblood dynasty.[11]

#### Adonis Snow
[[Adonis Snow]] was Violet's biological father and an important figure within the history of Clan Snow. His unusual existence, knowledge and relationship with Agnes shaped the circumstances surrounding Violet's upbringing, while his eventual death left unresolved grief within the family.
Adonis's connection to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] became particularly complicated because of the extraordinary circumstances surrounding the transfer of his remaining existence and memories. [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]] is one of the exceptionally intelligent supernatural women incorporated into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s expanding family. [[Tyche]] is the Goddess of Fortune whose extraordinary control over probability attracted Violet's attention during the later Imperial Era. #### The Elderblood Children
Violet is one of the senior maternal authorities responsible for the younger generation of the Elderblood dynasty. Violet understands that the family's daughters may possess abilities capable of devastating planets or entire Solar Systems, but continues regarding them as children who deserve affection and the opportunity to develop without being forced prematurely into adult responsibilities.
Her explanation to Albedo during Victor's later homeschooling period illustrates this philosophy. Violet emphasized that Victor deliberately avoids making his daughters sustain dangerous Imperial rituals simply because they possess the strength to contribute. For the family, the existence of extraordinary power does not erase the difference between a child's potential and the responsibilities of the adults raising her.[21]

### Role in the Elderblood Empire
Violet is one of the principal senior Empresses of the [[Elderblood Empire]] and occupies a position of extraordinary influence through her status as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s First Wife, her Divine authority and her extensive experience organizing the ruling family. Although her position originally developed from the unusual circumstances of Victor's marriage, it became a practical institutional responsibility as the household expanded into a civilization encompassing enormous numbers of people, supernatural [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] and independent organizations.
Her authority is particularly important because the Empire's ruling family possesses an unusual political structure. Rather than operating as a conventional monarchy in which the Emperor maintains a small number of political marriages primarily for alliances, Victor's household contains numerous powerful women with independent knowledge, abilities and responsibilities. Many are ancient Gods, former rulers, exceptional scientists or military commanders whose influence extends beyond ordinary family relationships. Violet's position requires her to manage the interactions among these individuals and ensure that their personal attachments do not undermine the functioning of the larger civilization.
Her responsibilities therefore encompass several overlapping areas. She participates in Imperial administration, coordinates senior family members, evaluates the integration of newcomers and helps ensure that the standards governing Victor's Bloodline remain consistent. During major political negotiations, her ability to recognize the motivations and rivalries of other supernatural authorities allows her to support agreements that would be difficult to secure through raw military intimidation alone.
The Great Acceleration expanded these responsibilities enormously. With Victor and several other powerful individuals sustaining the temporal structure, Violet and the senior Empresses were required to supervise institutions developing across Galactic territories. The resulting civilization demanded systems for communication, security, resource distribution, transportation, education and the administration of increasingly diverse populations. Violet's experience organizing a large household became the foundation for broader Imperial responsibilities, although the enormous scale of the Empire required cooperation with specialists such as Anna, Ruby, Kaguya and Akasha.
Her Divine authority also provides a practical advantage beyond ordinary political influence. As the Dragon Goddess of Order, Violet can impose stabilizing conditions upon environments and supernatural phenomena, allowing her to resolve certain threats without resorting to the overwhelming destruction available to many other members of Victor's family. Her intervention on an alien planet demonstrated that she could pacify hostile creatures, stabilize the climate and purify the atmosphere through the direct application of Order, while her intervention during [[Scathach]]'s Eldritch technique showed that the same Concept could protect observers from destabilizing supernatural influences.[1][30]
Violet also possesses a strong interest in strategic deterrence and the protection of Victor's existence. Following the Primordial crisis involving Moira, she recognized that the Empire could not remain permanently dependent upon its ruler threatening catastrophic harm to himself and the Universe whenever an enemy possessed sufficient power to challenge the family. Her proposed universal-level weapon consequently represented an attempt to establish independent deterrence and prevent hostile cosmic authorities from assuming that threatening the Elderblood dynasty could force Victor into sacrificing his own existence.[31]
Her formal political authority was demonstrated when Victor entrusted her with announcing Mars as the new Imperial capital. The proclamation was transmitted throughout the Empire and established the reconstructed planet as the center of its next era, while [[Yggdrasil]] remained preserved as the historical cradle of the civilization. Violet's ability to speak officially for the Empire demonstrated the trust placed in her by Victor and the other senior members of the ruling family.[4]
By the latest documented period, Violet has assumed further responsibility for organizing newly incorporated territories while Victor remains occupied sustaining the extraordinary rituals supporting Imperial development. She works with Akasha and other senior authorities, evaluates potentially dangerous discoveries and coordinates preparations for the conflict approaching from the Upper Sectors. When Victor announces that war will begin within approximately one year, Violet immediately begins mobilizing the administrative systems necessary to prepare the Empire, demonstrating that her position as Empress has developed into a functional center of Imperial government.[34]

Violet's current existence is the result of several successive evolutions rather than a single power system. She began as a Pure-Blooded Noble Vampire of the [[Snow Clan]], was later incorporated directly into [[Victor Elderblood]]'s Progenitor Bloodline, awakened the inherited [[Eyes of Causality]], developed the Divinities of <Order> and <Obsession>, and was ultimately reconstructed as a genuine Blood Dragon, True Dragon and Dragon Goddess. Victor's Dragonization did not erase Violet's previous abilities; compatible powers, Bloodline characteristics and supernatural systems were incorporated into her new Draconic foundation.[1]

- Single-Command Pacification: Violet privately noted that the theatrical manifestation was largely unnecessary and that, as a high-ranking Dragon Goddess of Order, she could have pacified the planet with a much simpler command.[24]
- Mental Stabilization: During Scathach Elderblood Scarlett's use of the Draconic-Eldritch expression Heptagrammakos, observers experienced mental destabilization, headaches and declining psychological stability. Violet applied Order to lessen the effect upon them.[25]
- Negative Mental Effect Suppression: Order can be used to stabilize minds affected by hostile supernatural phenomena instead of solely suppressing offensive powers.[25]

###### <Obsession>
- Divinity of Obsession: Violet possesses a second Divinity representing her overwhelming fixation, possessiveness and obsessive emotional nature, particularly where [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is concerned.[3]
- Conceptual Empowerment: Violet's existence and power are strengthened by the conceptual significance of the obsessive nature that has defined her throughout her life.[3]
- Yandere–Obsession Resonance: Violet's <Obsession> possesses exceptional compatibility with Victor's <Yandere> Divinity because both arise from closely related emotional and conceptual principles.[3]
- Mutual Divinity Synergy: Victor's Yandere-related power feeds naturally into Violet's Obsession, allowing the two Divinities to reinforce one another rather than conflict.[3]
- Order–Obsession Synergy: Violet's two Divinities are naturally complementary: <Order> reflects her desire to organize and stabilize everything surrounding Victor, while <Obsession> reflects the emotional fixation driving much of that behavior.[3]
Note: The novel establishes Violet's Divinity of Obsession and its resonance with Victor's Yandere Divinity, but has not yet demonstrated enough independent feats to treat abilities such as unrestricted "Obsession Mind Control" or absolute control over every form of obsession as confirmed powers.

##### Blessings
- Blessing of Beauty: Violet is among [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wives who possess the supernatural Blessing associated with Beauty, contributing to the extraordinary supernatural attractiveness characteristic of the women surrounding him.[3]

##### Violet's Secret Techniques
- Violet's Secret Techniques: Violet developed a collection of 99 specialized techniques. The techniques are not simply physical attacks; many are designed to interfere with biological functions by targeting the information associated with the victim's body and Soul.[2]
- Soul Information Manipulation: Violet can manipulate Soul-level information corresponding to bodily functions.[2]
- Biological Manipulation: Altering the information underlying a biological structure allows Violet to impose physical changes that are not necessarily reversed merely by repairing ordinary tissue.[2]
- Pain Inducement: Several Secret Techniques are deliberately designed to produce overwhelming pain rather than immediate death.[2]
- Physiological Status Manipulation: The techniques can forcibly alter involuntary bodily responses and biological functions.[2]
- Mental Status Manipulation: Secret Techniques numbered 90–99 are focused primarily upon psychological and emotional conditions rather than conventional physical anatomy.[2]
- Secret Technique 01 — Testicular Torsion: Manipulates corresponding biological/Soul information to inflict extreme reproductive-organ pain upon an applicable target.[2]
- Secret Technique 02 — Phallic Reduction: Alters the information associated with the target's anatomy, making simple physical reconstruction insufficient to casually undo the effect.[2]
- Secret Technique 03 — Impotence: Interferes with the target's reproductive function through biological and Soul-information manipulation.[2]
- Secret Technique 04 — Quick Shot: Alters physiological sensitivity and bodily response as an incapacitating and humiliating status effect.[2]
- Secret Technique 05 — Explosive Diarrhea: Produces severe gastrointestinal dysfunction capable of crippling concentration and combat effectiveness.[2]
- Secret Technique 90 — Deep Depression: Imposes an extreme depressive state intended to destroy the victim's motivation and desire to continue resisting.[2]
Only Secret Techniques directly revealed or described in the novel are individually indexed here; the remaining numbered techniques should not be invented merely to fill the 99-technique list.

#### Skills
- Master-Level [[Martial Arts]]: Violet's combat training progressed far beyond her early reliance upon Fire. By the period following Dragonization, observers placed her near the upper limit of [[Poseidon|the Master]] level of Martial Arts rather than treating her as a simple elemental fighter.[19]
- Combat Adaptation: Repeated deaths within the Tower of Nightmares forced Violet to abandon her previous habit of attempting to solve every encounter through overwhelming Fire.[9]
- Environmental Analysis: Violet learned to examine unfamiliar environments and use minor details such as airflow, terrain and movement patterns to determine dangers and possible routes.[9]
- Tactical Analysis: She can rapidly evaluate enemy behavior, terrain, supernatural abilities and information acquired through her Eyes before deciding how to engage.[9][19]
- Weapon Proficiency: Violet can competently use ordinary and supernatural melee weapons rather than depending exclusively upon racial powers.[9]
- Swordsmanship: She demonstrated effective sword use in the Tower while combining sword strikes with Fire and Future Vision.[9]
- Dagger Proficiency: Violet is trained in the use of daggers and possesses established dagger techniques, although she did not use them during every later training match.[19]
- Power Control: Violet learned to regulate output and shape her abilities precisely rather than simply releasing greater quantities of Energy.[9]
- Future-Assisted Combat: She combines Precognition, movement, shapeshifting and melee technique so that future information immediately informs physical action.[9]
- Historical Combat Analysis: Violet combines Retrocognition with tactical reasoning, studying an opponent's previous behavior to anticipate present choices.[19]
- Multidisciplinary Power Integration: Her current combat style can combine Draconic physiology, Fire, Martial Arts, Causality Eyes, [[Divine Energy]], Soul-level techniques and <Order> rather than treating each system as an isolated ability.[19][11]
- Exceptional Emotional Perception: Violet's obsessive familiarity with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] makes her extraordinarily capable of identifying his true emotional state even after centuries of rulership taught him to conceal it.[26]
- Political Leadership: As Victor's First Wife and one of the primary Empresses, Violet possesses extensive authority within the [[Elderblood Empire]] and routinely participates in decisions affecting Gods, Dragons, Clans and entire populations.[27]
- Imperial Administration: Violet is one of the principal organizers of Victor's household and civilization and can govern independently without requiring Victor to personally resolve every matter.[27]
- Family Leadership: Her position as First Wife grants her enormous informal influence over the structure and internal relationships of the Elderblood family.[27]
- Strategic Planning: Violet routinely evaluates security vulnerabilities, possible future threats and methods of ensuring the Elderblood family is not dependent upon Victor alone to answer every crisis.[18]
- Strategic Deterrence Planning: Following the confrontation between Victor and the Primordial Beings, Violet concluded that the family required an independent weapon theoretically capable of threatening the entire universe so that Victor himself would not need to become the family's sole final deterrent.[28]
- Public Oratory / Imperial Communication: Violet is capable of acting as the public voice of the Imperial throne. Victor specifically entrusted her with announcing Mars as the new capital of the Elderblood Empire to citizens distributed across its worlds.[29]

#### Resistances & Immunities
- Sunlight Immunity: Violet inherited Clan Snow's ability to remain beneath sunlight despite her original Vampiric physiology.[7]
- Vampiric Weakness Supersession: Her current body is a genuine True Dragon rather than an ordinary Vampire, meaning weaknesses that function solely because of standard Vampire biology no longer define her present physiology.[1]
- High Energy Tolerance: Violet's True Dragon body and [[Dragon Heart]] allow her to safely contain and circulate supernatural Energy that would overwhelm her former Vampire body.[1]
- High Physical Trauma Resistance: Dragonization vastly increased Violet's durability and capacity to survive damage beyond her previous Noble Vampire limits.[1]
- Mental Effect Countermeasures through Order: Violet can actively suppress destabilizing supernatural mental effects by imposing Order, as shown when she mitigated the psychological effects produced around [[Scathach]]'s Heptagrammakos.[25]
No blanket immunity to all mental, Soul, conceptual, [[Chaos]], temporal or reality-altering abilities is currently indexed without a direct feat. Violet's extraordinary status as a True Dragon Goddess should not be used to invent unshown resistances.

#### Limitations
- Distant Future Vision Cost: The farther Violet pushes her Causality Eyes into possible futures, the greater the Energy burden. Extremely deep observation can leave her almost completely exhausted.[17]
- Order Is Not Absolutely Uncontestable: Violet's restricted spaces and imposed laws can be challenged by appropriate opposing Divinities or sufficiently superior conceptual Authorities.[21]
- Attributeless Energy Cannot Simply Be Dominated: Violet herself recognized that Creator-level Attributeless Energy was not something she could casually command as though it were ordinary Energy; her successful application involved directing it through Order rather than establishing unrestricted ownership over it.[23]
- Personal Dragon Breath Properties Unknown: Although Dragon Breath is part of the complete Blood Dragon racial foundation, Violet's individual Breath and its unique attributes have not yet been directly demonstrated.[1]
- Obsession Applications Remain Partially Undemonstrated: Violet possesses the Divinity of <Obsession>, but most of its individually named offensive or reality-altering applications have not yet been shown directly. Only demonstrated conceptual resonance and synergy are indexed as confirmed abilities.[3]

- Noble Vampire Physiology: Violet was originally a Pure-Blooded Noble Vampire of Clan Snow. Her original race possessed enhanced physical ability, supernatural senses, regeneration, longevity and Vampire-specific racial techniques. These characteristics were later incorporated into and superseded by her Blood Dragon physiology rather than simply being lost.[7]

- - Vampiric Regeneration: Violet possessed powerful Noble Vampire regeneration before her body was rebuilt as a True Dragon.[7]
- Enhanced Vampire Senses: Her original senses greatly exceeded those of ordinary Humans even before Dragonization.[7]

Violet understood the theory but deliberately chose to train before attempting it, so complete mastery should not be listed as a confirmed feat without later evidence.[27]
