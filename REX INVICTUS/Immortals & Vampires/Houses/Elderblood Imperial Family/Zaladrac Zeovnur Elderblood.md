### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 522: The Legion of Demons
The path to divinity has already been opened to you from the moment my Companion obtained a World Tree for himself in his Soul. Self-discovery is still necessary. You need to understand who you are in existence and what role you will play, but the difficult part of the path has already been completed.
—Zaladrac, Chapter 792
Zaladrac Zeovnur Elderblood is a natural-born True Dragon, a Dragon Goddess, one of [[Victor Elderblood]]'s wives and one of the most important individuals behind the development of the [[Blood Dragons]]. Originally a Demonic Dragon who had developed in Hell and served [[Diablo]], Zaladrac encountered Victor during the final stages of the Demonic War. After Victor defeated her, recognized that she was only an adolescent by Dragon standards and healed the injuries covering her body instead of attempting to kill or harvest her, Zaladrac instinctively chose him as her Companion and formed a direct Soul bond with him.[4] The bond began transferring characteristics in both directions: Zaladrac gradually acquired physical traits associated with Victor, while Victor obtained genuine Draconic senses, organs, physiological characteristics and increasingly powerful expressions of Dragon Energy.[9][3]
Zaladrac is particularly important because she was a genuine Dragon before joining Victor. Most of the Dragon Goddesses who later populated the Elderblood family were transformed through Victor's Blood Dragon lineage, whereas Zaladrac carried the original inheritance system of ancient Dragonkind within her Soul. Generations of accumulated knowledge had been passed directly from her ancestors to their descendants, leaving Zaladrac with an enormous internal archive concerning Dragon biology, culture, supernatural languages, hierarchy and ancient metaphysical principles.[10] Processing that inheritance required extraordinary periods of sleep and millions of years of accumulated experience, helping explain both her vast knowledge and her characteristic laziness.[1]
Her bond with Victor became one of the decisive foundations of his racial evolution. Before possessing a genuine Dragon Soul, Victor had already acquired a [[Dragon Heart]], Dragon veins, Dragon senses, Dragon skin and other Draconic characteristics through Zaladrac.[3] During his battle against the Greek [[Primordial Gods]], those characteristics finally fused with his Vampire Progenitor nature and contributed to the birth of an entirely new lineage, eventually recognized as the Blood Dragons.[11][12] Victor later reversed the direction of their inheritance by incorporating Zaladrac herself into his newly established Progenitor lineage, replacing her older racial authority with his own and transforming her into a Blood Dragon while preserving the Demonic characteristics produced by her upbringing in Hell.[2] Chapter 1177 later directly reiterates that Zaladrac was the Dragon whose union with Victor's Vampiric nature allowed the Blood Dragon lineage to develop.[13]

### Physical Appearance

#### Dragon Form
At her debut, Zaladrac appeared as an enormous western-style Dragon covered in completely black scales, with sharp fangs, massive leathery wings, powerful limbs and blue reptilian eyes. Her body measured over fifty metres across even though her Soul was still immature enough to classify her as an adolescent Dragon.[7][4] The disparity between her size and developmental state was so extreme that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] initially assumed she must be an Ancient Dragon. Roxanne corrected him after examining Zaladrac's Soul, explaining that the Soul lacked the maturity and solidity expected from an Elder Dragon.[4]
The Soul bond with Victor gradually altered her appearance. Their connection allowed each participant to acquire characteristics from the other, and Zaladrac's blue eyes eventually changed into the same deep violet associated with Victor while her black scales developed a dark-violet coloration.[9][14] These changes visually reflected a connection that was already restructuring both of their bodies at a deeper level.
Zaladrac continued becoming vastly larger as she matured and later joined the Blood Dragon lineage. During the hunt against the surviving Elder Gods she manifested a Dragon form exceeding one hundred metres, surrounded her body with Miasma and attacked with destructive [[Draconic Energy]].[8] That measurement is only a historical snapshot and should not be treated as her modern maximum. By Chapter 1178 the scale of Victor's Dragon family had increased beyond ordinary planetary measurements. Anna and Natalia could already exceed Mars while heavily restraining themselves and could reach Solar-System scale when unrestricted; the narration then identifies Jeanne as the second-largest Dragon in the family, with Zaladrac immediately behind her and approximately equal to [[Scathach]].[6] No precise maximum measurement for Zaladrac's current body has been given.

#### Humanoid Form
Zaladrac did not originally have much interest in adopting a Humanoid appearance. After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] asked whether she could transform, she searched the knowledge inherited from her ancestors and successfully compressed her Dragon body into a form approximately 184 centimetres tall.[9] She appears as a tall woman with fair and healthy skin, a toned and well-proportioned physique, long black hair containing dark-violet shades, pointed elf-like ears and deep-violet eyes with vertical slit pupils. Two dark Draconic wings can remain visible from her back.
Her Humanoid form possesses an ethereal and distant beauty noticeably different from the more Human or Vampire appearance of many of Victor's other wives. Zaladrac initially regarded the smaller body as comparatively fragile, although Victor quickly pointed out that even a compressed Dragon body remained extraordinarily powerful and durable. The transformation also demonstrated an important feature of her ancestral inheritance: Zaladrac did not need to have personally practiced every technique contained in her memories beforehand. She could search the accumulated knowledge passed through her bloodline, understand the principles involved and then adapt them to her own body.[9]

### Personality
Zaladrac possesses an intensely Draconic mentality. She is proud, territorial, possessive, direct and instinctively sensitive to strength and hierarchy. Dragons naturally recognize territory and dominance in ways that do not always resemble Human social relationships, and Zaladrac can react aggressively when another Dragon intrudes upon what she considers hers without first acknowledging the existing hierarchy. Her reaction to the newly restored [[Metis]] Elderblood is one of the clearest examples. Upon finding an unfamiliar young female Dragon covered in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s scent, Zaladrac immediately spread her wings and released overwhelming Draconic pressure. Metis instinctively lowered her head and moved away from Victor in submission; once the hierarchy had been acknowledged, Zaladrac immediately calmed down.[15]
Her pride does not prevent intense loyalty. Once Zaladrac recognizes someone as worthy of her trust, that acceptance is extremely difficult to break. She chose Victor after he defeated and healed her, rapidly accepted him as her permanent Companion and later entrusted him with one of the greatest secrets of Dragonkind: the Dragon Language. Zaladrac explained that passing the language to a bonded Rider traditionally represents a Dragon's final test. Some Riders can spend their entire lives with a Dragon without ever receiving it, because the transfer means the Dragon has placed complete trust in the Rider.[16] Zaladrac had actually trusted Victor enough to perform the transfer years earlier, but admitted that she had forgotten because she had been sleeping.
The transfer also marked an important emotional change for Zaladrac. Dragons are naturally secretive and traditionally reveal very little of their culture even to allied species. While resting beside Victor after sharing her language, Zaladrac experienced an unfamiliar feeling of belonging and wondered whether that sensation was what other beings called home.[16] Her relationship with Victor therefore developed from an instinctive Dragon-Companion bond into genuine romantic and familial attachment.
Zaladrac is notoriously lazy. Part of this behavior has a biological explanation: natural [[True Dragons]] receive enormous quantities of ancestral information through their Souls and often spend long periods sleeping while assimilating it.[10] Zaladrac later revealed that completing her own inherited archive required millions of years, and she did not finish processing all of it until after Victor incorporated her into the Blood Dragon lineage.[1] Nevertheless, not all of her laziness can be blamed on ancestral knowledge. Even after completing the process, Zaladrac remained one of Victor's least administratively active wives. Chapter 1177 explicitly describes her as a Dragon through and through: when there is nothing important to do, she normally sleeps or searches for trouble. When Anna questioned why she rarely participated in construction or government, Zaladrac simply replied that she was better at destroying things than building them.[13]
This inactivity should not be confused with unreliability. Natalia defended Zaladrac by stating that whenever something genuinely matters, she is always present.[13] Zaladrac demonstrated that immediately afterward. During the reconstruction of Mars she initially watched Victor, Anna and Natalia work, became uncomfortable with remaining idle and voluntarily joined the operation without needing to be ordered.[6]
Her attitude toward battle is similarly straightforward. Zaladrac tends to favor the overwhelming physical and destructive advantages of being a Dragon rather than developing unnecessary complexity. When Victor discovered the combat potential of Speak of Power and asked why Dragons did not use it constantly, Zaladrac effectively answered that if she could simply incinerate the opponent with Dragon Breath, there was little reason to complicate the process.[16] Her simplicity is practical rather than unintelligent. Once Victor suggested using the Dragon Language for self-enhancement, battlefield manipulation, gravity alteration and reducing the enormous weight of a Dragon body, Zaladrac immediately understood the possibilities and began experimenting with a command for Lightness.[16]
Zaladrac is also highly protective of Victor's family. When [[Hassan-i-Sabbah]] arrived to train Victor's women, Zaladrac personally appeared above him, spread her wings and warned that any harmful action, wrong move or hostile intention would result in the erasure of his existence.[17] She remained nearby even after confirming that Hassan was being monitored by Clan Blank and Victor's Demons. Her protectiveness is especially intense where Victor himself is concerned. During his battle against [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]], [[Erebus]] and [[Nocturnus]] she became furious because her Companion was facing three [[Primordial Gods]] without summoning her, leaving the women around her uncertain how to approach the enraged Dragon.[18]
Like the traditional depiction of Dragons, Zaladrac can hold grudges for extraordinary lengths of time. [[Agares]] once repeatedly escaped her by moving between dimensional layers and took pleasure in frustrating her pursuit. Zaladrac never forgot the insult. When Victor later ordered Agares captured alive, she immediately accepted the assignment, reached him before Nyx expected and spoke through the Dragon Language to seal the surrounding Space itself. Agares was consequently unable to enter the dimensional state that normally made him inaccessible.[19] Zaladrac openly admitted that she still remembered how he had mocked her; the chapter itself emphasizes the Draconic principle that a Dragon does not forget an offense.

### Background
Zaladrac descends from an ancient natural True Dragon lineage associated with Zeovnur. [[True Dragons]] historically preserved their accumulated knowledge by passing it directly into the Souls of later generations. The practice ensured that a young Dragon could inherit information even if its parents or elders were killed before personally teaching it.[10] Such precautions were particularly important because Dragons were hunted extensively throughout supernatural history. Virtually every part of a Dragon—Blood, Heart, Soul, scales, bones, organs and other materials—could possess extraordinary supernatural value.
This system made Zaladrac fundamentally different from the Dragons later transformed through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Victor's transformed family members were genuine Dragons, but they had not been born into an ancient continuous Dragon lineage and therefore did not automatically possess the enormous ancestral archive carried by Zaladrac.[10] Her inherited knowledge covered subjects that had become obscure or almost completely unknown to other races, including the Dragon Language, Draconic supernatural principles and fragments of ancient metaphysical knowledge significant enough that Jeanne concluded Zaladrac's ancestors must once have been exceptionally important Dragons.[20]
Having information inside the Soul does not mean a Dragon understands all of it immediately. The inherited archive must be assimilated, which can require immense periods of sleep. Zaladrac herself required millions of years to completely absorb her inheritance.[1] This distinction is important: her ancestral archive represents inherited knowledge, not instantaneous omniscience.
Zaladrac developed within Hell, and that environment permanently changed her physiology. Dragon development is strongly influenced by childhood conditions; surrounding Energy, environment and nourishment can alter the characteristics a Dragon develops. Zaladrac grew while consuming enormous quantities of Miasma and Negative Energy. Those energies entered her metabolism and altered her body, Energy and Draconic flames, producing what the story calls a Demonic Dragon.[3]
The classification does not mean Zaladrac is a biological Dragon-Demon Hybrid. She remained a genuine True Dragon whose development had adapted to Hell. Her infernal upbringing instead gave ordinary Draconic characteristics a Demonic expression. Her flames became even more destructive, and those adaptations were later inherited by Victor through their Soul bond.[3] This eventually contributed to Victor developing his Demonic Dragon Breath and to the mutation of powers that had originally belonged to his Vampire bloodlines.
At some point before the Demonic War, Zaladrac entered [[Diablo]]'s service. The exact circumstances have never been completely explained. Although older descriptions sometimes characterize Diablo as having simply "tamed" her, the available chapters do not establish the precise method through which he acquired her loyalty or control. Roxanne instead observed that Zaladrac's relative youth probably explained why she could serve him at all; an Elder Dragon's pride would normally make voluntary subordination extraordinarily unlikely.[4] By the modern era, Demonic Dragons were rare enough to be regarded by much of supernatural society as effectively extinct.

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Zaladrac debuted during [[Diablo]]'s global invasion, appearing above Russia while Demonic forces transformed large portions of the Human World into environments resembling Hell. Natalia encountered an enormous black Dragon measuring more than fifty metres across, with blue reptilian eyes, long wings and sharp fangs, and narrowly escaped when Zaladrac attempted to attack her.[7] Her appearance was significant because genuine Dragons had become extraordinarily rare.
Zaladrac later became directly involved in the larger Demonic War and fought alongside Lilith against forces of Heaven, including Michael, [[Gabriel]] and [[Azrael]].[21][22] She remained one of the most dangerous assets available to Diablo until [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] entered the conflict after taking control of Hell.
Victor eventually confronted Zaladrac directly. Despite the tremendous size of her Dragon body, Roxanne examined the condition of her Soul and realized she was not an ancient or Elder Dragon at all but an adolescent.[23][4] Victor confirmed the observation through his own perception and was surprised that a Dragon whose body was already so enormous could still be considered comparatively young.
Rather than killing Zaladrac for the supernatural resources contained within her body, Victor noticed the wounds covering her and healed them with his own Energy. He then told her that she finally looked like a proud Dragon again.[4] The treatment strongly affected Zaladrac. Following her instincts, she established a direct Soul connection between them. Those observing the event immediately recognized the bond as the type of relationship associated with a Dragon and its chosen Rider or mate.
Zaladrac initially described Victor through the traditional terminology of a Dragon's knight or Rider. Victor rejected the implication that he would subordinate himself to another being and declared that if she intended to belong beside him, he would be her King. Zaladrac accepted without hesitation.[4] Their Soul bond immediately began connecting their senses, emotions and growth. Victor obtained direct access to genuine Draconic physiology while Zaladrac began receiving characteristics originating from him.
After choosing Victor, Zaladrac ceased functioning as one of Diablo's weapons and remained with the new King of Hell. Their bond would eventually become one of the most consequential events in Victor's entire development, although neither yet understood how far the exchange of racial characteristics would progress.

#### King Among Kings
Following the war, Zaladrac remained connected to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] during the centuries he experienced within Hell. When Victor eventually returned to the wider supernatural world, their reunion demonstrated how much the Soul bond had already changed both of them. Zaladrac's originally blue eyes had become deep violet, her black scales had developed dark-violet characteristics and Victor had acquired increasingly obvious Draconic features of his own.[9] Their connection also allowed them to sense one another's emotions directly.
Victor questioned whether Zaladrac could adopt a smaller form so that she would not need to remain isolated whenever he visited places unable to accommodate an enormous Dragon. Zaladrac searched her inherited memories and successfully transformed into her 184-centimetre Humanoid form despite never previously having much reason to attempt such a change.[9] Victor provided clothing suited to her transformed body and helped the inexperienced Dragon adjust to a form designed for ordinary social environments.
Her introduction to Victor's wives created predictable tension. Dragons naturally respond to questions of territory and hierarchy, while Victor's existing wives were themselves extremely possessive and had no intention of behaving submissively merely because another powerful female had joined the family.[14] Zaladrac gradually adapted to the household rather than attempting to replace its existing relationships.
The biological consequences of their bond became considerably clearer while Victor studied his own developing body. Zaladrac's infernal upbringing had produced a Demonic Dragon rather than a Dragon-Demon Hybrid: consuming Miasma and Negative Energy while developing had altered her metabolism and flames.[3] Because Victor's Soul was connected to hers, those adaptations transferred to him as well. [[Scathach]] identified Dragon skin, Dragon senses, a [[Dragon Heart]] functioning like a massive Energy reactor and Dragon veins capable of supporting enormous quantities of power among the characteristics he had acquired. Victor remained incomplete as a True Dragon because his Soul had not yet become Draconic, but physically he was already approaching one.[3]
The destructive significance of Zaladrac's inheritance became obvious through Dragon Fire. True Dragon flames possessed properties dangerous enough to completely destroy Gods rather than merely damaging their temporary Divine bodies; the resulting destruction could eliminate the Soul and prevent ordinary resurrection or reincarnation.[3] Zaladrac's Demonic adaptation made her flames still more destructive, while Victor's existing elemental powers mutated through contact with those characteristics.
Zaladrac later introduced Victor to the Dragon Language and Speak of Power.[16] Dragons possessed a supernatural language comparable in age to the languages of the Gods. Through Energy, voice and intention, a Dragon could issue an instruction that Reality attempted to obey. Zaladrac demonstrated the principle by speaking the command "Rot", causing a tree to decay almost immediately.
The system resembled Runes but could be activated through spoken Draconic authority rather than requiring the same lengthy inscription process. Zaladrac explained that Dragons traditionally used such techniques for their lairs, treasures and other practical purposes. Victor immediately recognized much broader combat applications and suggested using the language to enhance the body, create defenses, change gravity or manipulate battlefield conditions. Zaladrac tested his idea with Lightness, reducing the limitations created by a Dragon's immense physical weight.[16]
Zaladrac then transferred the Dragon Language itself directly to Victor. She revealed that providing this knowledge to a Rider was traditionally considered a Dragon's final test and a declaration of complete trust. Some Riders lived beside Dragons for their entire lives without ever receiving the language.[16] The transfer also gave Victor access to ancient Dragon writing and became the foundation from which he later developed increasingly sophisticated Draconic Runes.
When Victor sent [[Hassan-i-Sabbah]] to teach the women of his family, Zaladrac acted as one of their principal guardians. She immediately threatened to erase Hassan if he made any genuinely hostile move and continued observing the training even after confirming that Victor had already surrounded the area with other forms of surveillance and protection.[17]
Zaladrac's ancestral knowledge proved valuable beyond combat. During the discussion concerning the Path of Divinity, she corrected the women around Victor when they began overcomplicating the problem. According to Zaladrac, Victor's possession of a World Tree within his Soul had already opened much of the difficult path ahead for those connected to him; they still needed self-discovery and had to understand what they represented within existence, but a place for their development had effectively already been established.[20] Jeanne understood enough of the statement to conclude that Zaladrac's ancestors must once have been extremely important Dragons for such knowledge to have survived in their inheritance.
During Victor's battle against [[Tartarus (Realm)|Tartarus]], [[Erebus]] and [[Nocturnus]], Zaladrac remained outside the isolated battlefield and became increasingly furious because Victor refused to summon her despite fighting three [[Primordial Gods]].[18] Their Soul connection nevertheless allowed her to remain unusually sensitive to his condition while his Draconic characteristics approached their final transformation.
The pressure of the Greek conflict eventually caused Victor's Vampire and Dragon characteristics to stop behaving like separate systems. They fused into a new racial existence, and Victor became the Primogenitor of a legitimate Draconic Vampire lineage—the race that would later be known as the [[Blood Dragons]].[11][12] Zaladrac's original contribution to that evolution was fundamental: without the genuine Dragon physiology supplied through their bond, Victor would not have possessed the same Draconic foundation from which the new race developed.
By Chapter 810, Zaladrac herself was explicitly used as the comparison for an Adult Dragon, confirming that she had developed beyond the adolescent state identified when Victor first encountered her.[5]
Victor's return to [[Nightingale]] after the Greek conflict triggered an especially strong Draconic reaction. Zaladrac rushed toward him, only to stop after noticing the newly restored [[Metis]] Elderblood sitting near him in the body of a young female Dragon. Metis was saturated with Victor's scent, immediately activating Zaladrac's territorial instincts.[15] She released enough Draconic pressure to make the younger Dragon instinctively lower her head in submission. Once Metis acknowledged the hierarchy, Zaladrac accepted her presence.
Victor's transformation also intensified Zaladrac's attraction toward him. The male she had originally bonded with was no longer simply a Vampire carrying borrowed Dragon characteristics: he had become a genuine, exceptionally powerful male Dragon, the Primogenitor of a new Draconic lineage and her own Companion.
When Victor's family created its private world, Zaladrac worked directly alongside Victor and Scathach to establish its defenses. The three covered the newly created planet with Runes, Draconic protections and other systems intended to make infiltration extraordinarily difficult even if an enemy somehow discovered the realm.[24][25]
Victor subsequently brought Zaladrac directly into his newly established Blood Dragon lineage. Because she was already a True Dragon, the process was different from transforming a non-Dragon. Victor overwrote the older lineage authority governing her existence with his own Progenitor Bloodline, making Zaladrac one of his direct racial descendants as a Blood Dragon.[2] The story can consequently describe Victor with parent/progenitor terminology in relation to Zaladrac's Blood Dragon lineage; this is a racial relationship created by Progenitor authority and should not be confused with their biological or personal relationship. Zaladrac remained Victor's wife and Companion.
Her original infernal adaptations were not erased. Zaladrac remained a Dragon whose physiology had been shaped by Hell, but her racial foundation now descended from Victor's Blood Dragon Progenitor lineage rather than only the older natural Dragon ancestry.
When Victor later assembled the [[True Dragons]] participating in the hunt against the surviving Elder Gods, Zaladrac was specifically distinguished from the others because she had been a genuine Dragon from birth.[10] The newly transformed Dragons were real Dragons, but Zaladrac alone carried millions of pieces of inherited ancestral knowledge from the older race.
During the hunt itself, Zaladrac fought openly in her Dragon form. She manifested at more than one hundred metres, released Miasma around her body and attacked with pure destructive Draconic Breath while already enraged by the repeated occasions on which Victor had entered dangerous battles without calling her.[8]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s rise as the Dragon God of [[Chaos]], Zaladrac remained one of the principal natural-Dragon authorities within his household even as the number of [[True Dragons]] surrounding him increased enormously. Her ancestral knowledge and practical command of the Dragon Language made her valuable as a teacher in areas that Victor's newly transformed Dragons had never needed to understand before their evolution.
One of her most notable independent actions during this period was the capture of [[Agares]]. The Demon possessed the ability to enter other dimensional layers, becoming effectively inaccessible to conventional opponents. He had previously used the technique to escape Zaladrac repeatedly and deliberately frustrate her.[19] When Victor finally ordered Agares captured alive because his ability could be useful, Zaladrac accepted the mission with obvious enthusiasm.
She arrived at the battlefield before Nyx expected. When Agares attempted to retreat into another dimensional layer, Zaladrac spoke through the Dragon Language and imposed a command upon the surrounding Space. The entire region became sealed, cutting Agares off from his primary escape method.[19] Zaladrac openly reminded him that she remembered the previous humiliation. With Nyx, Clan Blank and Victor's forces surrounding him, Agares was left without a viable route of escape.
Zaladrac's experience with Draconic speech later made her one of the people considered capable of helping [[Anna Elderblood]] control her extraordinarily dangerous Reality-altering ability. When Anna's spoken intention began manifesting phenomena based upon the meaning she subconsciously assigned to words, Victor concluded that additional raw power would solve nothing. She instead needed control and training from people familiar with martial discipline and reality-affecting speech, including [[Scathach]], [[Dún Scáith|Dun Scaith]] and Zaladrac.[26]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
The enormous period of development following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ascension radically changed Zaladrac's personal timeline. By Chapter 1005 she had completed the ancestral assimilation process that had occupied her for millions of years.[1] The subject arose because Zaladrac and Victor had produced an unnamed daughter who inherited the same traditional True Dragon knowledge-transfer system.
Their daughter proved exceptionally talented. Zaladrac watched the girl process her inherited memories at a rate vastly exceeding her own. Zaladrac admitted that she herself had required millions of years to completely assimilate the inheritance and only finished after Victor transformed her into a Blood Dragon; her daughter was already approaching completion after less than several thousand years.[1] [[Nero]] immediately recognized why Zaladrac appeared irritated: her child was accomplishing in millennia what had taken her mother millions of years. Zaladrac was simultaneously frustrated and proud.
The comparison provided one of the clearest explanations of what made a naturally born True Dragon culturally different from a transformed Dragon. Each generation traditionally transmitted knowledge to the next so that young Dragons could inherit their ancestors' teachings even if those ancestors were killed. The practice had developed partly because Dragonkind was so aggressively hunted for the value of its bodies.[1][10]
Zaladrac's own Soul was also used to demonstrate the natural superiority of mature Dragon Souls. During Jeanne and [[Rhea]]'s lesson concerning different categories of Souls, an ancient Dragon such as Zaladrac was given as an example of a Dragon Soul naturally much stronger and more robust than the Soul of a Second Generation Primordial God.[27] Acquiring Divine Power could strengthen a Dragon Soul even further.
This comparison concerns the natural quality and durability of the Soul. It should not be misinterpreted as a statement that Zaladrac was automatically a Second Generation Primordial Being merely because her Soul exceeded that category in robustness.
As Victor's civilization continued expanding, Zaladrac remained relatively detached from administration. Unlike wives whose talents naturally placed them within science, government, military command or logistics, Zaladrac generally preferred sleeping, training or responding when an actual problem required overwhelming power. Her lack of administrative ambition did not reduce her position in the family or her importance to its Draconic foundations.

#### Those Who Rule Reality
By the late Imperial era, the [[Elderblood Empire]] had undergone almost two hundred thousand subjective years of accelerated development. Zaladrac was still one of the quieter wives within the family, enough that Anna complained she could attend a family gathering without being noticed.[13] The narration directly describes her as a Dragon through and through: if nothing requires her attention, she sleeps or looks for trouble. Her daughter inherited many of the same tendencies.
Anna jokingly asked whether Zaladrac wanted the same corrective training [[Scathach]] had imposed upon Albedo. Instead of reacting with fear, Zaladrac admitted that she would not necessarily object. Anna momentarily forgot the obvious: a Dragon normally does not refuse a genuine challenge.[13]
The conversation then revisited Zaladrac's historical importance. Unlike the wives transformed into Dragons later, she had possessed Dragonhood from the beginning, and her connection with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had been the original event through which genuine Draconic characteristics entered his Vampiric Bloodline. Chapter 1177 explicitly credits her with the evolution that ultimately produced the Blood Dragon, a Dragon possessing characteristics of both [[True Dragons]] and Noble Vampires.[13]
When Zaladrac attempted to justify her limited participation in government by claiming that she was better at destroying than building, Anna immediately accused her of simply being lazy. Victor stopped the teasing from becoming excessive while Natalia defended Zaladrac, noting that despite her habits, Zaladrac always appears when something actually matters.[13]
The statement was demonstrated almost immediately. The Empire's population had grown so large that Samar, [[Nightingale]] and [[Yggdrasil]] were becoming increasingly crowded economic centers. Victor decided not to solve the problem by endlessly expanding Yggdrasil. Instead, he chose Mars as the foundation of a new Imperial capital and brought Anna and Natalia to help reconstruct the planet.[6]
Zaladrac initially accompanied them without a defined construction role. After watching the others work, she became uncomfortable remaining idle and volunteered to participate.[6] The scene also provides the best modern indication of her physical Draconic scale. Anna and Natalia transformed into Dragons larger than Mars while still heavily restraining themselves and were stated to be capable of reaching Solar-System scale at full size. The narration identifies Jeanne as the second-largest Dragon in Victor's family, followed closely by Zaladrac, whose size is approximately comparable to Scathach.[6] The earlier hundred-metre measurement from the Elder-God war is therefore only an obsolete historical manifestation.
Victor, Anna, Natalia and Zaladrac collectively began reconstructing Mars. The original planet was enlarged into a megaplanet capable of supporting trillions of inhabitants.[6] Rather than creating a completely safe paradise, they filled it with regions of extreme gravity, weightlessness, magma, eternal ice, concentrated Miasma, floating islands and dangerous ecosystems. Leviathans, mutated primeval creatures and Monsters originating from Nightingale were introduced throughout the world so that the Imperial capital itself would continue challenging its inhabitants.
Mars subsequently became the central junction of Victor's new World Tree communication network. The [[World Trees]] connected to Victor established roots through the planet while their true existences remained protected within his inner world, allowing communication and transportation across the Empire's enormous territory with vastly reduced delay.[6]
When Violet publicly announced the completed project, she specifically credited the Emperor, the [[Anna Elderblood|Goddess of Truth]], the Goddess of Time and the Dragon Goddess—Victor, Anna, Natalia and Zaladrac—as the Beings responsible for recreating Mars.[6] Mars was formally declared the new capital of the Elderblood Empire, making Zaladrac one of the four principal Dragons directly responsible for physically establishing the heart of the Empire's next era.

### Dragon Heritage and Importance
Zaladrac occupies a unique position within the Elderblood family because she forms a living bridge between ancient natural Dragonkind and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s newer Blood Dragon race. Most of Victor's wives obtained Dragonhood through transformation. Zaladrac was born with it.[10] Her Soul therefore contains a continuous ancestral inheritance unavailable to the first generation of transformed Elderblood Dragons.
This inheritance preserved information extending beyond ordinary combat abilities. Zaladrac understood aspects of Dragon hierarchy, Dragon developmental biology, supernatural language, the Speak of Power, the relationship between a Dragon and its chosen Rider and metaphysical principles concerning Divinity that even ancient Gods did not casually explain.[16][20] Jeanne's reaction to Zaladrac's explanation of Divinity suggests that the Dragons responsible for creating her ancestral archive were significant figures within ancient Dragon history.
The inheritance system also demonstrates one of the weaknesses of natural Dragon development. Enormous quantities of information require equally enormous periods of assimilation. Zaladrac spent millions of years processing the memories passed to her, and Chapter 873 directly links the famous tendency of Dragons to sleep for very long periods with the need to absorb this knowledge.[10][1] Victor's descendants can inherit similar systems while benefiting from a civilization in which Dragons are no longer expected to survive alone while being hunted by nearly every powerful supernatural faction.
Zaladrac is equally important as an example of environmental Draconic adaptation. A Dragon's childhood surroundings can permanently influence its eventual nature. Hell's Miasma and Negative Energy entered Zaladrac's metabolism while she developed, turning her into a Demonic Dragon without changing her fundamental status as a True Dragon.[3] Her existence therefore demonstrates that Dragon subtypes can emerge through adaptation rather than conventional crossbreeding.
The most important effect of her existence, however, was her influence upon Victor. Their Soul bond allowed Victor to acquire genuine Dragon physiology before he possessed a Dragon Soul of his own. Dragon senses, Dragon skin, Dragon veins, a [[Dragon Heart]] and characteristics affecting his elemental powers progressively accumulated inside his Vampire body.[3] The bond did not immediately make Victor a complete Dragon—his Soul remained the defining missing component—but it created the physical foundation necessary for what followed.
During the Greek conflict, Victor's Vampire Progenitor nature and the Dragon characteristics originating from Zaladrac finally fused into one racial system.[11] The resulting Blood Dragon lineage did not merely place Vampire powers inside the body of a Dragon. It became a new Progenitor race whose foundation incorporated the properties of both lineages.
Chapter 1177 explicitly recognizes Zaladrac as the original source of that Draconic influence.[13] Her importance to the Elderblood dynasty is consequently much greater than that of a powerful wife or ancient Dragon adviser: her decision to bond with Victor altered the biological and metaphysical direction of Victor himself and ultimately contributed to the race inherited by much of his family.
After Victor became the Blood Dragon Primogenitor, their racial relationship reversed. Victor used his Progenitor authority to incorporate Zaladrac into his Blood Dragon lineage, replacing the older authority within her racial ancestry with his own.[2] In lineage terms Victor therefore became her Progenitor and can be described through parent-descendant terminology by the Blood Dragon system, even though their personal relationship remains that of spouses and Companions. The distinction is necessary to avoid interpreting Progenitor lineage terminology as an ordinary biological family relationship.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Zaladrac's husband, Companion, Dragon-Rider Soul partner and current Blood Dragon Progenitor. Their relationship began during the Demonic War when Victor defeated Zaladrac but chose not to treat her as a monster, weapon or collection of valuable Dragon materials. After discovering that she was an adolescent and seeing the injuries covering her body, Victor healed her and told her that she finally looked like a proud Dragon again.[4] Zaladrac responded to his strength and treatment of her by initiating their Soul bond herself.
Traditional Dragon terminology initially framed Victor as her Rider or knight. Victor immediately rejected any implication of subordination and informed Zaladrac that he would be her King instead. Zaladrac accepted his declaration.[4] This early exchange established a relationship suited to both personalities: Zaladrac wanted a Companion strong enough to stand above ordinary beings, while Victor had no intention of entering a bond in which another creature regarded him as a subordinate.
Their Soul connection is bidirectional. Victor acquired Draconic physical characteristics from Zaladrac, while Zaladrac gradually acquired traits associated with him, most visibly her deep-violet eyes and dark-violet scales.[9][14] They can perceive one another's emotions through the bond and remain connected even across considerable separation.
Zaladrac's transfer of the Dragon Language became one of the clearest demonstrations of how much her feelings had developed. She revealed that a Dragon giving the language to its Rider represented the final traditional test of trust.[16] The act symbolically moved Victor beyond merely being a powerful individual with whom she had instinctively bonded and placed him inside the cultural boundaries Dragons normally reserved for those they completely accepted.
Her affection remains strongly Draconic and possessive. She reacts aggressively to unfamiliar female Dragons carrying Victor's scent, becomes furious when Victor enters dangerous battles without calling her and continues responding strongly to his affection despite the enormous amount of Time they have spent together.[18][15] During the reconstruction of Mars, Victor simply thanking her and calling her "honey" was enough to make Zaladrac blush.[6]
Their relationship eventually became one of the foundations of an entire species. Zaladrac gave Victor his first genuine Draconic physiological foundation, Victor later became the Primogenitor of the [[Blood Dragons]], and he subsequently incorporated Zaladrac into that lineage.[2][13] They later had a daughter together.[1]

#### Unnamed Daughter
Zaladrac and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] possess at least one canonically unnamed daughter.[1] The girl strongly resembles the Draconic side of her parents and inherited the ancient True Dragon system through which generations transmit their knowledge directly to their descendants.
Her rate of assimilation dramatically exceeded Zaladrac's. Zaladrac needed millions of years to fully absorb her ancestral inheritance, while her daughter approached completion in less than several thousand years.[1] Zaladrac was visibly frustrated by the comparison but also proud of the child's talent.
The daughter also inherited several aspects of Zaladrac's personality. Chapter 1177 specifically notes that she shares many of her mother's natural Dragon tendencies, including sleeping when nothing important is happening and seeking excitement or trouble when awake.[13]

#### Metis Elderblood
Zaladrac's first encounter with [[Metis]] Elderblood immediately activated her territorial instincts. Metis had recently awakened in the form of a young female Dragon and was covered in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s scent after remaining close to him.[15] Zaladrac spread her wings and imposed enough Draconic pressure that Metis instinctively lowered her head in submission.
The display was an assertion of hierarchy rather than permanent hostility. Once Metis acknowledged the older Dragon's position, Zaladrac's aggression disappeared and Metis continued living normally as part of the Elderblood family.

#### Scathach Elderblood Scarlett
[[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] and Zaladrac represent very different expressions of Draconic combat. Zaladrac was born a Dragon and naturally approaches many problems through overwhelming racial power, instinct and the inherited knowledge of Dragonkind. [[Scathach]] instead developed through thousands of years of deliberate martial refinement before later acquiring Dragonhood through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
Both worked alongside Victor to establish the Draconic and Runic defenses protecting the family's private world.[25] Zaladrac's knowledge also became useful when the newly transformed Dragons needed instruction in Draconic systems they had never possessed as Vampires, Gods or other races.
By Chapter 1178 their Dragon bodies were described as approximately comparable in size, with Jeanne ranked second in the family and Zaladrac following closely behind while being roughly on par with Scathach.[6]

#### Jeanne Elderblood
[[Jeanne Elderblood]] repeatedly recognized the value of Zaladrac's ancestral knowledge. When Zaladrac explained that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had already opened much of the Path of Divinity for his family, Jeanne understood the deeper implication and concluded that Zaladrac's ancestors must have been exceptionally important Dragons to possess such knowledge.[20]
During Victor's battle against the Greek [[Primordial Gods]], Jeanne and the other women were also aware that Zaladrac's direct Soul connection to Victor gave her a unique perspective on his condition despite being unable to enter the battlefield.[18]

#### Agares
Zaladrac held a long-running grudge against [[Agares]] because the Demon repeatedly used his dimensional movement to escape her and mock her inability to reach him. Dragons do not easily forget such insults. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later ordered Agares captured alive, Zaladrac personally joined the operation and sealed the surrounding Space through the Dragon Language, removing Agares' greatest defensive advantage.[19]

#### Skills and Abilities
Zaladrac Zeovnur Elderblood is an ancient natural-born True Dragon whose physiology was permanently altered by spending her childhood within Hell. Unlike the overwhelming majority of [[Victor Elderblood]]'s current Dragon family, Zaladrac did not originate as another Race before being transformed. She was born a genuine Dragon and therefore possesses the complete biological, spiritual and hereditary foundation of traditional True Dragonkind, including the ancestral-memory system that allows Dragon knowledge to be transmitted through successive generations.[1]
Her upbringing turned her into a Demonic Dragon. The term does not describe a Dragon/Demon Hybrid. Chapter 706 explicitly explains that Zaladrac remained completely Draconic; her body was changed because she grew up consuming Miasma and Negative Energy as part of her natural development.[2] These Energies altered her metabolism and made her Draconic Flames even more destructive than ordinary Dragon Fire.
Following Victor's ascension as the Blood Dragon Primogenitor, Zaladrac's original lineage was incorporated into his new Race. She consequently became a Demonic Blood Dragon while retaining the racial qualities, inherited memories and Hell-adapted characteristics she had possessed since birth.[3]

##### Demonic Blood Dragon Physiology
Blood Dragon Physiology: Zaladrac currently possesses the biological and spiritual foundation of a True Dragon belonging directly to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood Dragon lineage.
Unlike transformed members of the Race, Zaladrac's Blood Dragon nature was built upon an already complete True Dragon existence.
- Immense Strength: [[True Dragons]] possess extreme physical strength even without supernatural enhancement. Zaladrac's enormous body allows her to overpower most mortal supernatural beings through sheer physical force.
- Immense Speed: Despite her size, Zaladrac can move and fly at extraordinary speeds. Her physical mass does not prevent her from keeping pace with God-class combatants.
- Immense Durability: Draconic scales and internal organs possess extreme resistance to ordinary physical and supernatural attacks.
- Enhanced Reflexes: Zaladrac possesses predatory reactions appropriate to a natural Dragon.
- Enhanced Agility: She retains exceptional aerial and bodily control despite her enormous size.
- Immense Stamina: A [[Dragon Heart]] can sustain extreme output for extended periods.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: True Dragons possess highly developed natural recovery.
- Longevity: Zaladrac has already lived for millions of years.[4]
- True Dragon Form: Zaladrac's genuine biological form is that of an enormous Dragon.
- Humanoid Transformation: She can shapeshift into a humanoid woman approximately 184 cm tall.[5]
- Size Manipulation: Dragons can alter the scale of their manifestations. Zaladrac's later Dragon form exceeds one hundred meters.[6]
- Flight: Zaladrac possesses enormous natural wings and exceptional aerial mobility.
- Natural Weaponry: Her claws, fangs, horns, wings and tail can all function as weapons.
- Draconic Scales: Her scales provide natural supernatural armor.
- Enhanced Lung Capacity: Dragon lungs are specifically adapted to store and release the Energy required for Draconic Breath.
- Draconic Veins: A Dragon's internal structure can support quantities of Energy vastly beyond most mortal species.[2]
- Dragon Heart: Zaladrac possesses a genuine Dragon Heart functioning as an extraordinarily powerful biological Energy reactor.[2]
- Energy Storage: True Dragons can store supernatural Energy throughout their bodies rather than relying upon a single conventional reserve.
- Energy Adaptation: Dragons develop characteristics according to the Energies and environments surrounding them during growth.[2]

##### Demonic Dragon Adaptation
Zaladrac became a Demonic Dragon because she was raised within Hell rather than because of Demonic ancestry.[2]
During her childhood she continuously consumed:
- Miasma
- Negative Energy
These Energies became part of her metabolism and permanently altered her Dragon physiology.

###### Miasma Adaptation
Zaladrac's body naturally tolerates and uses Miasma.
- Miasma Resistance: Miasma that is toxic or corruptive to many ordinary beings is natural to Zaladrac's body.
- Miasma Manipulation: She can release and manipulate the Miasma accumulated within her Draconic physiology.
- Miasma Aura: During combat Zaladrac can exude large quantities of Miasma from her body.[6]
- Miasma Empowerment: Hell's environmental Energy helped shape and strengthen her racial attributes.
- Demonic-Environment Adaptation: Zaladrac can operate naturally in the hostile conditions of Hell.
This should not be interpreted as evidence that Zaladrac possesses complete Demon Physiology. She is not a biological Demon.

###### Negative-Energy Adaptation
Zaladrac also consumed Negative Energy while developing.
The Energy changed the characteristics of her flames and metabolism, helping produce the extraordinarily destructive Breath later inherited by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] through their Soul bond.[2]

##### Draconic Energy
Like other [[True Dragons]], Zaladrac naturally generates a distinct form of [[Draconic Energy]].
- Energy Generation: Her [[Dragon Heart]] continuously produces enormous quantities of Energy.
- Energy Projection: Zaladrac can release Draconic Energy directly as supernatural force.
- Physical Reinforcement: Energy can reinforce her body, scales and natural weapons.
- Breath Fuel: Draconic Energy powers Zaladrac's primary Breath attack.
- Rune Fuel: Her Energy can be combined with intent and language to activate Speak of Power.[7]

##### Demonic Dragon Flames
Zaladrac possesses extremely destructive Dragon Fire altered by her Hell upbringing.
Her flames are not merely ordinary [[Pyrokinesis]]. Their properties derive from the combination of True Dragon physiology, Miasma and Negative Energy absorbed during her childhood.[2]
- Pyrokinesis: Zaladrac can generate and manipulate supernatural Draconic Flame.
- Extreme Heat: Her flames possess vastly greater destructive ability than ordinary elemental Fire.
- Miasma-Infused Flame: The influence of Hell is incorporated into the Dragon Fire.
- Negative-Energy Properties: Negative Energy further increases its destructive nature.
- Divine Effectiveness: Dragon Flames are dangerous even to Gods.
- Regeneration Negation: The damage produced by sufficiently powerful Dragon Fire can exceed ordinary supernatural regeneration.
- Soul Damage: Dragon Breath is capable of directly damaging or destroying Souls.[2]
- Reincarnation Negation: Chapter 706 explains that sufficiently destructive Dragon Fire can eliminate a target's Soul so completely that ordinary reincarnation is no longer possible.[2]
The novel compares the destructive property of Dragon Flames to effects associated with a God of Destruction.[2]
This comparison concerns the destructive result and should not automatically be interpreted as Zaladrac possessing a completed Destruction Divinity.

##### Demonic Draconic Breath
Demonic Dragon Breath is Zaladrac's signature direct attack.
She gathers supernatural Energy through her enormous lungs and [[Dragon Heart]] before releasing it as an overwhelming blast of destructive Draconic Flame.
- Massive Energy Beam: Her Breath can manifest as an enormous stream or beam of concentrated destructive Energy.
- Miasma Integration: Zaladrac's Hell-adapted biology can mix Miasma into the Breath.
- Negative-Energy Integration: Her original adaptation increases the destructive characteristics of the flames.
- Soul Destruction: Dragon Breath can destroy the spiritual body in addition to the target's physical form.[2]
- Anti-Regeneration: Merely possessing strong physical regeneration does not guarantee survival against Draconic Flame.
- Anti-Divine Properties: Gods are not naturally immune to Dragon Breath.
- Area Destruction: Zaladrac can use the Breath against entire groups or enormous battlefield regions.
- Battlefield Clearing: During the Elder-God invasion she attacked large numbers of enemies while in her 100+ meter Dragon body.[6]
The destructive potential of Dragon Breath increases with the age, power and quality of the Dragon using it.

##### Draconic Soul
The Soul is considered the most important aspect distinguishing a genuine Dragon from another being merely possessing Dragon-like physical traits.[2]
Zaladrac possesses a natural True Dragon Soul that has existed for millions of years.

###### Extreme Soul Strength
Ancient Dragon Souls possess extraordinary durability.
During Jeanne and [[Rhea]]'s lesson on Souls, Zaladrac was specifically used as an example of an Ancient Dragon whose Soul is naturally much stronger than that of a Second Generation Primordial God.[8]
This grants:
- Soul Durability: Zaladrac is exceptionally difficult to damage through ordinary Soul-based attacks.
- Soul Capacity: Her Soul can contain enormous quantities of inherited information.
- Spiritual Stability: The Soul can sustain [[Draconic Energy]] and extremely powerful supernatural abilities.
- Divine Compatibility: Dragons gaining Divinity develop even stronger and more defined Souls.[8]
This does not mean Zaladrac automatically possesses every ability belonging to a Second Generation Primordial God. The comparison concerns the quality/strength of her Soul.

##### Ancestral Dragon Inheritance
One of Zaladrac's most important abilities is the traditional True Dragon system of Inherited Knowledge.[1]
[[True Dragons]] transmit enormous amounts of accumulated information directly into the Souls of descendants.
The practice historically ensured that important Draconic knowledge survived even when individual Dragons were hunted and killed.

###### Inherited Memories
Zaladrac contains enormous quantities of ancestral memories and knowledge.
Her inheritance includes information concerning:
- Dragon history;
- Dragon culture;
- Dragon language;
- Dragon hierarchy;
- supernatural Energies;
- Speak of Power;
- ancient supernatural systems;
- paths toward Godhood;
- and information accumulated by preceding generations.
The exact total quantity is not numerically defined, but Chapter 873 describes Zaladrac as carrying millions of pieces of ancestral knowledge.[1]

###### Knowledge Assimilation
Receiving the information does not mean a newborn Dragon can instantly use all of it.
The Dragon must gradually:
1. process the inherited memories;
2. understand them;
3. integrate useful information;
4. and separate its own identity from accumulated ancestral experience.
For Zaladrac this process required millions of years.[4]
She only completely finished assimilating the inheritance after becoming a Blood Dragon.

###### Sleep-Based Processing
The enormous inheritance is one reason [[True Dragons]] sleep for very long periods.
Sleep allows the mind and Soul to organize inherited information without overwhelming the Dragon.[1]
Zaladrac's tendency to sleep therefore has a biological and spiritual purpose.

###### Hereditary Transmission
Zaladrac passed this system to her daughter with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
Their daughter inherited the ancestral information and began assimilating it at an extraordinarily accelerated rate, approaching completion within several thousand years.[4]

##### Draconic Vision
True Dragon Eyes possess supernatural perception allowing Dragons to observe the world beyond conventional physical sight.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] inherited a version of Zaladrac's Dragon Vision through their Soul bond.[9]
- Enhanced Vision: Zaladrac possesses extraordinary visual acuity.
- Long-Range Vision: Dragon Eyes can perceive details at enormous distances.
- Energy Perception: She can perceive supernatural Energy and structures invisible to ordinary sight.
- Illusion Resistance: Dragon Eyes are highly resistant to conventional visual deception.
- Concealment Detection: They can detect many hidden supernatural phenomena.
- True-Sight Characteristics: Dragon perception is repeatedly described as seeing the world closer to its actual state rather than relying only upon ordinary senses.
- Rune Perception: Draconic lines of power invisible to ordinary beings can be perceived through Dragon Eyes.
Draconic Vision should not be treated as unconditional immunity to every possible Illusion or Concealment. Extremely high-level Conceptual effects and phenomena above the Dragon's perception can still potentially interfere with their senses.

##### Predatory Instinct
Dragons possess extraordinarily developed instinct.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] inherited a portion of this characteristic through his connection with Zaladrac.[9]
- Danger Intuition: Zaladrac can instinctively recognize approaching danger.
- Predatory Awareness: She naturally evaluates the strength and threat of nearby beings.
- Combat Instinct: Her body can react before conscious analysis is complete.
- Territorial Instinct: Zaladrac rapidly identifies unfamiliar Dragons or powerful beings entering territory she regards as belonging to her.
- Mate Recognition: Draconic instinct strongly influences her response toward Victor as an overwhelmingly powerful male Dragon.

##### Draconic Presence and Dominance
Zaladrac's mere presence can trigger instinctive fear in weaker beings.
When [[Anna Elderblood]] first encountered Zaladrac's humanoid form, she unconsciously froze and experienced the biological fear of prey confronted by a superior predator.[9]
When Zaladrac later confronted the newly reborn [[Metis]] Elderblood, she released Draconic pressure powerful enough to make the younger Dragon instinctively lower her head in submission.[10]
- Fear Inducement: Weaker beings can experience involuntary fear in her presence.
- Draconic Dominance: Other Dragons can instinctively recognize Zaladrac's position in the hierarchy.
- Intimidation: Zaladrac can deliberately intensify her presence when threatening an opponent.
- Submission Pressure: Inferior Dragons may respond through instinctive submissive behavior.
This ability is primarily an expression of racial pressure and hierarchy rather than a demonstrated independent Fear Divinity.

##### Dragon Language
Zaladrac possesses complete fluency in the ancient Dragon Language.[7]
The language is described as being approximately as old as the languages associated with the Gods.
Dragons are extremely protective of it.

###### Knowledge Transfer
Through the Soul bond, Zaladrac can transmit linguistic knowledge directly to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
She transferred:
- spoken Dragon Language;
- written Dragon Language;
- and the fundamental knowledge required to understand its construction.[7]
Victor received the information as naturally as though Dragon Language had been one of his native languages.

###### Final Test of the Dragon Rider
Giving Dragon Language to one's bonded Rider is considered one of the greatest acts of trust in traditional Dragon culture.[7]
A Dragon Rider can spend an entire lifetime beside a Dragon without necessarily receiving this privilege.
Zaladrac explained that the act represents the Dragon's final test and confirms complete trust between the two partners.

##### Speak of Power
Speak of Power is the traditional Draconic equivalent of Runes.[7]
Rather than requiring the user to physically carve every command, a Dragon combines:
- Draconic Language;
- Energy;
- Voice;
- Intention;
- and [[Will Solace|Will]].
The resulting command generates a supernatural instruction imposed upon Reality.

###### Rot
Zaladrac demonstrated Speak of Power to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] by commanding:
Ikih — Rot.
The spoken command caused a nearby tree to rapidly decay until effectively nothing remained.[7]
This demonstrates that Speak of Power can produce direct reality-affecting phenomena rather than simply strengthening ordinary elemental attacks.

###### Lightness
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] suggested applying Dragon Words to self-enhancement rather than merely attacking directly, Zaladrac tested a command corresponding to Lightness.[7]
The effect dramatically reduced the burden of her body's mass and increased her movement speed.
Zaladrac immediately recognized that this could become extremely useful in her enormous Dragon form.

###### Barrier Creation
Zaladrac can use Dragon Language to create supernatural barriers.
During Hassan's training, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] asked her to create a dome through which neither sound nor images could pass. Zaladrac spoke in Dragon Language and immediately generated the requested barrier.[11]
This demonstrates:
- Sound Isolation: Speech inside the barrier cannot be heard externally.
- Visual Isolation: Outside observers cannot view what occurs within the dome.
- Privacy Barrier: Speak of Power can be used for support rather than destruction.

###### Space Sealing
Zaladrac later demonstrated an advanced Speak of Power application against [[Agares]].
Agares specialized in escaping through dimensions, making conventional capture extremely difficult.
Zaladrac used Draconic Language to seal the surrounding Space, preventing him from opening his usual routes of escape.[12]
- Spatial Sealing: The local Space can be locked.
- Teleportation Prevention: Spatial escape abilities can be prevented from activating.
- Dimensional Travel Negation: Agares could no longer jump between dimensions after the seal was established.
This should not automatically be generalized into unrestricted Space Manipulation. Zaladrac achieved the demonstrated effect through Speak of Power.

##### Draconic Runes
Zaladrac possesses knowledge of Draconic Runic techniques derived from Speak of Power.
Unlike ordinary Rune users, a Dragon's natural language allows Energy and [[Will Solace|Will]] to directly reinforce the meaning of the command.
- Rune Creation: Zaladrac can construct Draconic inscriptions.
- Enchanting: She historically used Draconic lines of power to enchant possessions and treasures.[7]
- Lair Modification: Zaladrac used the system to improve the comfort and supernatural properties of her lairs.[7]
- Self-Enhancement: After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s suggestion, she began developing more combat-oriented self-buffing applications.
- Environmental Manipulation: Runes can modify battlefield or environmental conditions.
- Defensive Rune Networks: Zaladrac worked with Victor and [[Scathach]] to install Draconic Runes throughout an entire planet.[13]
- [[Planetary Security]]: These Rune systems became part of the defensive infrastructure protecting Victor's private world.
Zaladrac's direct knowledge of ancient Dragon Language makes her one of the most valuable Runic specialists within the early Elderblood family.

##### Soul Bond with Victor Elderblood
Zaladrac and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] formed an exceptionally deep Draconic Soul bond in Chapter 685.[14]
The bond functions similarly to a racial form of [[Soul Marriage]].

###### Mutual Trait Exchange
Both individuals receive characteristics from the other.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] inherited:
- Dragon Eyes;
- enhanced organs;
- Draconic instincts;
- improved lungs;
- Dragon-scale manifestation;
- a stronger Heart;
- and the biological foundation necessary to use Dragon Breath.
Zaladrac simultaneously inherited characteristics and increases derived from Victor's existence.

###### Emotional Connection
The two can directly sense portions of one another's emotions.
This allowed [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to recognize when Zaladrac was withholding information and gives both individuals unusual awareness of the other's emotional state.[7]

###### Telepathic Communication
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Zaladrac can communicate mentally across distance.
Their conversations frequently occur without physically speaking.

###### Vision Sharing
The bond allows [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to perceive through Zaladrac's visual senses and vice versa.

###### Power Growth Synergy
The bond allows improvements in one partner to affect the other.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s increasingly powerful physiology altered Zaladrac, while the Draconic foundation gained from Zaladrac was one of the factors eventually allowing Victor's own Dragon evolution.

###### Long-Distance Connection
The bond remains active across very large distances.
During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s fight against the Greek [[Primordial Gods]], Zaladrac could follow what was occurring and provide spiritual support despite being physically separated from him.[15]

##### Blood Dragon Lineage Integration
After becoming the Blood Dragon Primogenitor, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] incorporated Zaladrac into his lineage.[3]
This was different from transforming a Human, Vampire or God into a Dragon because Zaladrac was already a True Dragon.
The process:
- replaced/overwrote her former racial lineage;
- connected her directly to Victor's Progenitor Blood Dragon Race;
- strengthened her body;
- preserved her Demonic Dragon adaptations;
- preserved her ancestral knowledge;
- and made her a Demonic Blood Dragon.
It also helped Zaladrac finally complete the assimilation of the inherited ancestral knowledge she had been processing for millions of years.[4]

##### Unnamed Divinity
Zaladrac is currently classified as a Dragon Goddess, but her individual personal Divinity/Concept has not been explicitly named in the checked chapters.
This distinction is important.
Her demonstrated abilities involving:
- Dragon Fire;
- Miasma;
- Negative Energy;
- Speak of Power;
- Space Sealing;
- and ancestral knowledge
are primarily derived from her True Dragon physiology, upbringing and learned Draconic arts.
They should not automatically be converted into separate Godly Divinities without an explicit statement.

##### Guardian Instinct
Zaladrac frequently acts as a guardian for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wives and daughters.
When [[Hassan-i-Sabbah]] arrived to train the family, Zaladrac immediately appeared in front of him, spread her wings and warned him that any hostile action or even sufficiently suspicious thought would cause her to destroy him.[16]
This role is particularly appropriate because [[True Dragons]] possess:
- exceptional instincts;
- powerful senses;
- overwhelming durability;
- and enough direct combat power to deter most supernatural threats.

##### Knowledge of Divinity
Zaladrac's ancestral inheritance gives her considerable knowledge concerning the process through which mortals and other supernatural beings approach Divinity.
In Chapter 792 she explained that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s possession of a World Tree had already opened a path toward Divinity for those connected to him.[11]
According to Zaladrac, the remaining requirement was still genuine self-discovery:
- understanding who one is;
- understanding one's role within Existence;
- and discovering the Concept appropriate to that existence.
Jeanne was surprised by the depth of Zaladrac's knowledge and inferred that Zaladrac's ancestors must have possessed considerable importance.[11]

##### Draconic Knowledge
Beyond Divinity, Zaladrac's ancestral memories give her knowledge concerning numerous old supernatural subjects.
This includes:
- Dragon society;
- Dragon customs;
- Dragon-Rider relationships;
- Ancient Runes;
- Speak of Power;
- Dragon physiology;
- racial hierarchy;
- and supernatural phenomena encountered by her ancestors.
The knowledge remains limited by what Zaladrac has actually processed. She cannot instantly access every inherited memory simultaneously.[1][4]

##### Draconic Hierarchy
Zaladrac naturally understands and enforces the hierarchical instincts of Dragons.
When she encountered [[Metis]] after the latter's rebirth as a Dragon, Zaladrac immediately asserted dominance until Metis acknowledged her higher standing.[10]
This is not a formal supernatural Mind-Control ability.
It is a combination of:
- overwhelming Draconic presence;
- racial instinct;
- power disparity;
- and social hierarchy encoded deeply into Dragon behavior.

##### Combat Skill
Zaladrac is an enormously experienced combatant simply through the scale of her lifespan.
Her fighting style historically emphasized the natural advantages of Dragons:
- raw physical power;
- aerial mobility;
- natural weapons;
- Draconic Breath;
- Miasma;
- instinct;
- and overwhelming durability.
She possessed less interest in complicated artificial techniques before meeting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] because natural Dragon abilities were already sufficient to defeat most opponents.
Victor's influence gradually caused her to explore more technical applications of Speak of Power and battlefield manipulation.[7]

##### Battlefield Destruction
During the Elder-God invasion Zaladrac manifested as a Dragon exceeding one hundred meters and entered a destructive rampage against the invading monsters.[6]
Her body released Miasma while her Breath consisted of concentrated destructive Energy.
This demonstrates her ability to function as a large-scale battlefield combatant rather than only fight individual enemies.

##### Resistances
- Physical Resistance: True Dragon scales and flesh provide extreme resistance against conventional physical attacks.
- Energy Resistance: Dragon physiology can sustain enormously high concentrations of supernatural Energy.
- Miasma Resistance: Zaladrac grew up consuming Miasma and can naturally tolerate the Energy.[2]
- Negative-Energy Resistance: Negative Energy was incorporated into her development from childhood.[2]
- Heat Resistance: A Dragon capable of generating extreme Draconic Flame is naturally resistant to high temperatures.
- Soul Resistance: Zaladrac's ancient Dragon Soul is much stronger than the Soul of a Second Generation Primordial God.[8]
- Illusion Resistance: Draconic Vision allows her to detect and resist many ordinary illusions and visual deceptions.
- Concealment Resistance: Dragon Eyes are particularly effective at perceiving hidden supernatural phenomena.
- Fear Resistance: Zaladrac is an apex supernatural predator whose instincts make ordinary intimidation ineffective.
- Environmental Resistance: She naturally survived and developed in Hell's hostile environment.
