**[[Siena Scarlett Elderblood]]**, formerly known as Siena Scarlett and later Siena Scarlett Alucard, is the eldest adopted daughter of Scathach Elderblood Scarlett, the elder adoptive sister of Ruby Elderblood Scarlett, Lacus Scarlett Elderblood and Pepper Scarlett Elderblood, one of the wives of Victor Elderblood, and the mother of Sylvia Elderblood. (Lore adapted from *My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires*.)

### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 35
Siena Scarlett Elderblood, formerly known as Siena Scarlett and later Siena Scarlett Alucard, is the eldest adopted daughter of [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], the elder adoptive sister of [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]], [[Lacus Scarlett Elderblood]] and [[Pepper Scarlett Elderblood]], one of the wives of [[Victor Elderblood]], and the mother of [[Sylvia Elderblood]]. Originally an ancient Noble Vampire belonging to Clan Scarlett, Siena spent centuries functioning as [[Scathach]]'s practical right hand and administrator, taking responsibility for political, territorial and bureaucratic duties that her battle-obsessed mother rarely wished to handle. She later joined Victor's family, became a Blood Dragon and genuine True Dragon, and eventually lived through almost two hundred thousand subjective years of development during the temporal expansion of the [[Elderblood Empire]].[6][8][5][2][3]
Among Scathach's daughters, Siena was trained around the martial principle of Power: the proper integration, application and amplification of supernatural abilities during combat. Scathach divided four important foundations among the sisters according to their natural strengths—Pepper emphasized Strength, Lacus Speed, Ruby Resistance/Defense, and Siena Power—while Scathach herself possessed all four.[11][12] Siena's specialization complemented her enormous Ice output, making her particularly effective at large-scale battlefield manipulation. During the Demonic invasion of [[Nightingale]], her Ice formed the destructive core of the Scarlett sisters' combined weather technique and allowed her to freeze the sky, generate supernatural snowfall and rain nearly invisible Ice spikes across an entire territory.[13]
After becoming a True Dragon, Siena's elemental nature expanded rather than simply replacing her original Scarlett abilities. Her Ice remained part of her existence while her Draconic transformation awakened a second environmental affinity associated with volcanic phenomena, including Heat, Lava and Magma.[5] In the Elderblood Empire, however, Siena became equally notable for achievements entirely unrelated to warfare. She restored enormous quantities of Humanity's lost historical, artistic and intellectual heritage after Judgment Day, including famous paintings, original classical music, scriptures and other historical records. Imperial citizens consequently began calling her the Goddess of Art, Goddess of Knowledge and Goddess of Culture.[4] These were initially public titles rather than automatically completed Divinities, although [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood warned Siena that sufficiently concentrated Faith could eventually transform such recognition into genuine Divine Concepts.[4]

### Physical Appearance
Siena is an exceptionally attractive and mature-looking woman with pale skin, long red hair and red eyes characteristic of her Scarlett lineage. She stands approximately 180 centimeters tall and possesses a highly curvaceous physique; Chapter 166 specifically describes her as having an F-cup bust.[1] Despite already being roughly seven centuries old during the early story, Siena physically appeared as a young adult because the biological appearance of ancient Vampires was largely disconnected from chronological age.
Her appearance commonly reinforces the more mature role she occupies among the Scarlett sisters. During the defense of Clan Scarlett, for example, she was shown wearing a dark-purple dress with a high leg opening and black stockings while working through administrative documents shortly before the Demonic invasion began.[13] Unlike Pepper's consistently youthful mannerisms or Lacus' sleepy disposition, Siena generally presents herself with the composure expected from the eldest sister and Clan administrator.
Siena's transformation into a Blood Dragon replaced her old Vampire racial foundation with the body and Soul structure of a genuine True Dragon while preserving characteristics deeply connected to her identity.[5] As with other Dragon Goddesses, her present physical body is therefore not limited by the biological constraints that governed her earlier Vampire existence, although her customary humanoid appearance retains the red-haired Scarlett characteristics by which she has always been recognized.

### Personality
Siena is responsible, pragmatic, proud and considerably more administratively minded than most of Clan Scarlett. She spent centuries compensating for [[Scathach]]'s indifference toward politics and paperwork, which made her accustomed to reading reports, governing territory, organizing personnel, understanding [[Nightingale]]'s aristocratic structure and preparing responses to threats before they became immediate emergencies. This practical mentality was especially visible when [[Zagan]]'s army invaded Clan Scarlett: Siena had already evacuated the local population into fortified shelters on Scathach's warning, continued working until she detected the invasion and immediately transitioned from administrator to battlefield commander once the enemy arrived.[13]
Her early worldview nevertheless contained much of the arrogance and racial prejudice common among Nightingale's ancient Noble Vampires. Siena considered Humans cattle and regarded former Humans as inherently inferior to Vampires born into old Noble Bloodlines. These beliefs heavily shaped her initial treatment of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]; rather than simply disliking his personality, she was offended that someone she regarded as newly transformed Human livestock behaved casually around Scathach and addressed the legendary Countess without the reverence Siena believed she deserved.[6][14] Victor's growth rapidly dismantled the assumptions supporting that prejudice. Within approximately six months, someone Siena had dismissed as cattle developed enough Killing Intent and combat potential to register as a genuine danger to her, forcing her to acknowledge that age, birth and inherited status were not reliable measures of worth.[15]
Siena is deeply loyal to Scathach and possesses an unusually strong sense of her mother's dignity. She frequently became irritated when Victor behaved too familiarly with Scathach, but her annoyance gradually acquired a more complicated quality as the attraction between the two became impossible to ignore. Siena could be simultaneously protective of her mother's status, exasperated by Scathach's behavior and jealous of the effortless intimacy developing between Scathach and Victor. Her reactions illustrate that Siena's mature administrative personality does not eliminate her more emotional, competitive or childish tendencies around family.
Her first marriage explains why Siena's search for a suitable partner became so important to her. Before the story began, she had already married an unnamed Vampire, only to discover that the man was primarily interested in gaining proximity to Scathach and benefiting from the prestige of marrying the Countess' eldest daughter. Siena killed him after discovering his intentions.[6] Scathach later suggested that Siena simply transform, raise or shape a suitable husband herself, but Siena rejected the idea because a relationship deliberately manufactured to satisfy her requirements would not feel genuine. What Siena wanted was someone who chose and valued her, not her mother's reputation, political power or the privileges attached to Clan Scarlett.[6] Her eventual marriage to Victor is therefore particularly ironic: the woman who once recoiled at the idea of taking a former Human as a husband ultimately found the genuine relationship she had wanted with someone who had originally been Human.
Despite her sternness, Siena is intensely attached to Ruby, Lacus and Pepper and never treats adoption as making their relationship less real. The sisters have lived, trained, fought and shared a household for centuries, and their casual physical closeness reflects a family whose members spent a significant portion of their lives together.[16] This familial loyalty remained unchanged after all four sisters became part of Victor's household. Even after Clan Scarlett was effectively absorbed into the much larger Elderblood dynasty, Siena continued functioning socially as the eldest Scarlett sister rather than abandoning the identity around which her early life had been built.
Siena also possesses a strong appreciation for history, art and accumulated knowledge. Where Pepper became the family member most strongly associated with recovering popular entertainment, fiction and modern cultural media, Siena focused upon older cultural material whose disappearance represented an actual loss of Human historical memory. Her efforts to restore paintings, music, documents, scriptures and preserved artifacts were therefore consistent with the same personality that had once made her the keeper of Clan Scarlett's political and administrative continuity.[4]
The immense subjective time experienced by Victor's family eventually changed Siena further. By the late Imperial era, younger members of the family immediately recognized a maternal quality in Siena, Ruby and Lacus that distinguished them from Pepper's comparatively youthful demeanor despite all four sisters having changed little physically.[17] Motherhood and hundreds of millennia of family life did not erase Siena's personality, but they made the once-arrogant Vampire administrator considerably more mature than the woman who first met Victor in Nightingale.

### Synopsis

#### Vampire Society
Siena's biological parents and original family have never been identified. She was adopted by [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] at a young age, became the eldest of [[Scathach]]'s adopted daughters and was subjected to the same brutal philosophy of training that later defined the entire Scarlett household. Because Scathach cared far more about combat than territorial bureaucracy, Siena gradually became the practical administrator of Clan Scarlett and gained extensive familiarity with [[Nightingale]] politics, the responsibilities of the Count Clans and the hidden dangers confronting Vampire society.
Before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] entered her life, Siena married an unnamed Vampire. The marriage ended when she learned that her husband had approached her primarily because becoming Scathach's son-in-law would grant him proximity to one of the most powerful Vampires in the world. Siena killed him and became far more resistant to relationships driven by political advantage.[6] When Scathach suggested deliberately raising an ideal husband, Siena refused because she believed that engineering a partner would defeat the purpose of finding genuine affection. Her refusal to consider Humans for the role also exposed the extent of her early racial arrogance, as Siena still regarded them as livestock rather than viable partners.[6]
Siena first appeared when Victor entered Scathach's presence during his first stay in Nightingale and casually addressed the Countess by name. His lack of formal deference infuriated Siena, and learning that he had been Human only worsened her opinion of him.[6][14] Her hostility continued through the discussions surrounding Clan Fulger and the gathering inside Scathach's mansion, where Siena repeatedly attempted to correct Victor's behavior. Tension rose enough that Victor's wives were becoming increasingly irritated with her treatment of their husband.[18]
The confrontation ended in an unexpectedly one-sided fashion when Victor's eyes glowed and he commanded Siena to remain quiet. Her body obeyed despite Siena never willingly submitting to him, leaving her unnaturally passive until the effect ended. The incident became one of the earliest demonstrations of the supernatural authority later associated with Victor's developing Bloodline and showed that even an Elder Vampire could be affected by powers he himself barely understood at the time.[19]
Scathach's martial philosophy later revealed why her daughters fought so differently from one another. She had emphasized four foundational qualities according to each daughter's natural inclination: Pepper was Strength, Lacus Speed, Ruby Resistance, while Siena represented Power—the effective integration and application of supernatural abilities during combat.[11] Siena's confidence in her centuries of experience was soon challenged by Victor's development. After approximately six months of Scathach's training, the former Human she had treated as cattle could release enough Killing Intent to make Siena instinctively regard him as dangerous, permanently undermining her assumption that age and Noble birth determined superiority.[15]

#### A New Pillar
Siena immediately appreciated the political significance of [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] announcing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s newly created Clan as a Fifth Pillar of Vampire Society. Establishing an entirely new Count Clan was, from her perspective, even more extraordinary than defeating an existing Count and inheriting the position because Victor possessed almost none of the centuries of infrastructure, bloodline history or political legitimacy normally expected from a Pillar.[20] Her response reflected the political education gained from administering Clan Scarlett and distinguished her from sisters who were less interested in [[Nightingale]]'s institutional structure.
When the Inquisition conflict involving Sasha drew [[Scathach]] toward Earth, Siena, Lacus and Pepper accompanied their mother to the Walker residence.[21] Chapter 166 formally described Siena as the eldest adopted Scarlett daughter, approximately seven hundred years old and 180 centimeters tall.[1] The Human World stay also displayed her tendency to think in terms of wider systems; during a discussion about Vampires' sunlight weakness, Siena initially reasoned that Witches might refuse to create a solution because doing so could disrupt supernatural balance, while Ruby cynically argued that the Witches probably simply lacked the capability.
Scathach soon determined that Siena had become too comfortable and resumed training her with extreme severity. Victor, having experienced Scathach's methods himself, understood that the cruelty was intended to eliminate habits that might otherwise kill Siena in genuine combat.[22] Victor later fought Ruby, Siena, Lacus and Pepper simultaneously and used the coordinated battle to study his own weaknesses. He specifically recognized that Siena's generation and control of Ice could be more efficient than his own in situations where his raw talent had not yet compensated for her centuries of practice.[23]
Siena continued participating in the family's wider political and martial affairs, accompanying Scathach and the others during major Nightingale events such as Natashia's challenge against [[Niklaus Horseman]]. She was also among the Scarlett household present during the period surrounding Anderson's visit and Victor's increasingly visible emergence as a threat capable of competing with far older supernatural beings.[24][25]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later decided that the Scarlett sisters needed more experience beyond the environment in which they had spent centuries growing comfortable. With [[Scathach]]'s approval, he effectively "kidnapped" Siena, Lacus and Pepper and brought them into the expedition involving [[Eleanor Elderblood Adrasteia]] and Clan Adrasteia.[16] Siena naturally became the sister most prepared to handle practical communication with guards and local authorities, while her reputation as Scathach's eldest daughter ensured that members of another ancient Vampire Clan immediately understood who they were dealing with.
The expedition also demonstrated Siena's exceptional eyesight. She could clearly perceive and identify relevant details across distances measured in several kilometers, giving her natural long-range observational ability useful for both scouting and battlefield awareness.[16] Victor's continued involvement with the sisters increasingly forced them outside the narrow specializations around which Scathach had originally trained them.
When Pepper later struggled with the feeling that she was falling behind the rest of her family, Victor revisited Scathach's four martial foundations. Siena's specialization was again identified as Power, not simply meaning raw destructive force but the ability to properly incorporate supernatural abilities into combat instead of allowing physical technique and supernatural power to function as disconnected systems.[26][12] Siena's later development during the Demonic War demonstrated how valuable this principle could become when paired with improved control.

#### Demonic War
When [[Diablo]]'s invasion began, Demonic portals opened across [[Nightingale]]'s major territories. Siena was working in Clan Scarlett's administrative office when she sensed the invasion and received confirmation from Luna that their attackers were [[Zagan]] and the 61st Demonic Legion.[13] [[Scathach]] had already warned her that an attack was likely, and Siena had taken the warning seriously enough to evacuate the local population into a protected bunker beneath the Scarlett castle. By the time Ruby, Lacus, Pepper and their allies arrived, the civilian territory had already been emptied.
The four Scarlett sisters then deployed one of their most powerful coordinated techniques. Lacus spread the cloud-like substance produced by her Bloodline across the territory, Pepper saturated the enormous formation with Water, and Siena projected Ice power into it before Ruby used her superior supernatural control to organize the completed phenomenon.[13] Siena's contribution was powerful enough to freeze the cloud structure across the sky, producing an enormous artificial frozen weather system. Scathach had previously judged the combined technique capable of destroying large cities while possessing enormous potential for battlefield control.[13]
Once the supernatural snow began falling, every flake carried enough concentrated Ice Energy to freeze ordinary Demons almost immediately while the sisters maintained enough control for the phenomenon to avoid their allies. Ruby then ordered Siena to control the battlefield directly. Siena increased the intensity of the snowfall and generated thousands of Ice spikes so small that they were nearly invisible, sending them across the territory as lethal projectiles.[13] The first controlled wave alone killed approximately thirty percent of the invading Demons and incapacitated many others, while stronger enemies were forced to defend themselves with Demonic Miasma.
Siena openly admitted that fine supernatural control had historically been more strongly associated with Ruby than with herself. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s training had nevertheless greatly improved her ability to regulate the enormous quantity of Ice she could generate, allowing Siena to perform battlefield-wide precision attacks that would have been much more difficult for her earlier self.[13] The battle became one of the clearest demonstrations of why Scathach's daughters were so dangerous together: each sister possessed a specialized strength, but centuries of teamwork and Victor's later corrections allowed those specialties to overlap rather than remain isolated.

#### King Among Kings
As [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction expanded, [[Scathach]] began teaching Victor and her daughters more formally about Runes. Siena, Ruby, Lacus and Pepper participated in the lessons explaining the difference between a supernatural language and a true Runic system: Runes combined a language of power, Energy and intention into instructions capable of imposing a desired result upon Reality.[27] The training added another formal magical discipline to Siena's already extensive combat education and later became particularly relevant once she acquired a True Dragon's natural relationship with the Dragon Language.
By the period surrounding Chapter 729, Siena was already being openly treated as one of Victor's wives.[7] The relationship represented an enormous reversal from their first meeting. Siena had originally considered him inferior because of his Human origin; after years of shared experience, repeated training and Victor's integration with the Scarlett household, she chose him as the genuine partner she had once believed she might never find.
The political reality became public in Chapter 730 when the major women of the original Count families formally joined Victor's family structure. In Clan Scarlett's case, Ruby, Siena, Lacus and Pepper were publicly associated with Victor's marriages, completing the near-total integration of Scathach's immediate family with Victor's own Clan.[8] Siena subsequently adopted the Alucard and eventually Elderblood family identities while retaining Scarlett as part of her name.
She later entered the Tower of Nightmare alongside Lacus, Pepper and numerous other members of Victor's faction.[28] The Tower's lethal scenarios were designed to force experienced supernatural beings beyond the stagnation created by centuries of comparative safety, exposing them to repeated situations in which knowledge, instinct, cooperation and psychological resilience mattered as much as simple accumulated Energy.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Siena eventually participated in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s large-scale transformation of his family into [[Blood Dragons]]. She belonged to the same transformation group as Lacus, Pepper, Victoria, Carmila, Natasha, Maya and Haruna.[5] The process did not merely add Draconic features to an existing Vampire body. Victor reconstructed their racial foundation into genuine [[True Dragons]] while preserving the powers and qualities rooted deeply enough in their Souls to remain part of the resulting existence.
Siena's transformation produced one of the more unusual elemental combinations among Victor's wives. Her original supernatural identity had been dominated by the Ice inherited and developed through Clan Scarlett, but becoming a Dragon expanded rather than replaced that affinity. Her new Draconic nature manifested powers connected to volcanic phenomena, giving Siena control over extreme Heat, Magma and Lava while retaining her pre-existing Ice abilities.[5] The result left her capable of manipulating environmental extremes that would normally appear directly opposed.
The transformation also massively increased the physical and metaphysical foundation supporting her abilities. Siena gained the body, Energy capacity, regeneration, longevity and adaptive potential of a Blood Dragon while retaining centuries of Vampire combat experience, [[Scathach]]'s martial training, her knowledge of Runes and the control developed through Victor's instruction.[5]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
The destruction of old Human civilization during Judgment Day erased far more than cities. Paintings, written records, historical artifacts, musical compositions, religious and cultural texts and enormous portions of accumulated Human heritage disappeared alongside the societies preserving them. Rebuilding infrastructure could restore civilization materially, but it could not automatically return the cultural information that had been lost.
Siena became one of the Elderblood family members most strongly associated with solving this problem. While Pepper concentrated on the restoration of modern popular culture and entertainment, Siena reconstructed older forms of cultural and historical heritage. Her work returned objects and information such as the Mona Lisa, original classical music, historical scriptures and numerous other preserved works that had vanished during the destruction of Earth.[4]
Imperial citizens responded by granting Siena the public titles Goddess of Art, Goddess of Knowledge and Goddess of Culture. Siena initially dismissed them because she understood that being called the Goddess of a Concept did not automatically mean she actually possessed that Concept as a Divinity.[4] [[Aphrodite]] immediately warned her not to dismiss the titles too casually. Faith possessed genuine metaphysical power, and if enough Mortals consistently worshipped or understood a God through a specific Concept, that concentration of Faith could eventually awaken the associated Divinity. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was deliberately controlling many of his wives' public appearances for exactly this reason, using important Imperial achievements to cultivate compatible future Concepts.[4]
Siena and Victor also became parents during the expanding Imperial era. Their daughter, [[Sylvia Elderblood]], was introduced among Victor's enormous second generation and inherited the Scarlett family's red-haired appearance alongside the broader traits of Victor's Dragon-Goddess descendants.[9] Sylvia later became closely associated with the daughters of Ruby and Pepper, continuing the close bond between the different branches of the Scarlett family into the next generation.[10]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
The [[Elderblood Empire]] eventually began its most extreme developmental project: a temporal ritual designed to provide roughly 200,000 subjective years of internal development while only around a year passed outside the affected system.[2] Siena and the rest of the ruling family therefore accumulated a quantity of lived time dwarfing the centuries that had once made [[Nightingale]]'s oldest Vampires seem ancient. By the end of the project [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had sustained the system for almost two hundred thousand subjective years, making Siena's present experienced age well above two hundred millennia when her original lifespan and previous accelerated periods are included.[3]
The enormous time span did not dissolve the old Scarlett family structure. Sylvia remained closely connected to the daughters of Ruby and Pepper, and younger family members could immediately recognize the difference between the sisters as mothers. Chapter 1162 specifically contrasts Pepper's unusually youthful aura with Siena, Ruby and Lacus, whose demeanors had developed the unmistakable presence of women who had spent millennia raising children despite their physical appearances changing very little.[17]
The same period demonstrated that Siena, Ruby, Lacus, Pepper and [[Scathach]] continued treating themselves as a distinct family unit inside the vastly larger Dragon's Nest. When events surrounding Pepper's daughters escalated into another household incident, the sisters were called together with Scathach in what was jokingly described as a Scarlett family meeting with their mother-in-law. Even after becoming Dragon Goddesses, Imperial figures and members of an interstellar dynasty, the relationships established during their centuries in Nightingale remained intact.[17]
Siena likewise remained involved in wider family affairs rather than retreating entirely into cultural or administrative work. When Albedo's excessive isolation from the family finally led Scathach to intervene, Siena was among Victor's wives observing the resulting disciplinary training through a projection.[29] The contrast was appropriate for Siena: she had spent most of her life accepting responsibility for both Clan administration and family matters, making prolonged withdrawal from the household fundamentally unlike her own behavior.

### Relationships

#### Scathach Elderblood Scarlett
[[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] is Siena's adoptive mother, original teacher and the central figure around whom most of Siena's early identity developed. Siena's devotion to her was strong enough that she initially treated [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s casual use of [[Scathach]]'s name as an insult. Scathach also trusted Siena with responsibilities she had little interest in performing herself, making her eldest daughter the practical administrator and political right hand of Clan Scarlett while Scathach concentrated upon combat and training.[6][13]
Their relationship combines immense respect with centuries of ordinary familial familiarity. Siena understands Scathach's personality well enough to recognize when her mother has become obsessed with a new opponent, complain internally about her recklessness and participate in family jokes at Scathach's expense without diminishing her admiration. Scathach's influence also defines Siena's martial identity: Siena's Power specialization, extreme training standards and refusal to become complacent all originate in the system her mother created.[11][22][12]
Scathach also influenced Siena's romantic expectations in a more unconventional manner. After Siena killed her first husband, Scathach suggested simply raising or creating a partner who met her standards. Siena rejected the proposal because she wanted genuine affection rather than a relationship designed in advance.[6] The irony that both mother and daughter eventually became wives of Victor became another part of the increasingly complicated but close-knit Elderblood household.

#### Victor Elderblood
Siena's relationship with [[Victor Elderblood]] began in open contempt. She saw him as a recently transformed Human who behaved far too casually around her mother and repeatedly challenged him according to the social assumptions of ancient Vampire society.[6][14] Victor's rapid growth, refusal to submit to status and willingness to challenge both Siena and [[Scathach]] gradually made those assumptions impossible to maintain. Within six months his Killing Intent alone could make Siena treat him as a legitimate danger.[15]
Repeated training transformed their relationship further. Victor fought Siena and her sisters as serious opponents, identified flaws created by their narrow specializations and helped improve Siena's control over the enormous supernatural power she naturally produced.[23][12][13] He treated Scathach's adopted daughters as individuals rather than merely Ruby's relatives, giving Siena a relationship with him independent of Ruby's original marriage.
By Chapters 729–730 Siena had become one of Victor's wives and publicly joined the family that would eventually become Clan Elderblood.[7][8] Victor later reconstructed her into a Blood Dragon, transforming the ancient Vampire into a True Dragon Goddess without erasing the identity she had spent centuries creating.[5] Their marriage eventually produced [[Sylvia Elderblood]].[9]
The relationship represents one of Siena's largest personal reversals. She began the story unwilling even to entertain the possibility of a Human-derived husband, yet eventually chose a former Human as the genuine partner she had wanted since the failure of her first marriage.

#### Ruby, Lacus and Pepper
Siena is the eldest sister of the Scarlett daughters and has spent centuries living beside [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]], [[Lacus Scarlett Elderblood]] and [[Pepper Scarlett Elderblood]]. Their adoption and differing biological origins have never prevented them from treating one another as genuine sisters. The four trained under [[Scathach]], shared a household, fought as a single combat unit and eventually joined [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family together.
Their fighting styles were deliberately complementary. Siena possessed Power and enormous Ice output, Pepper emphasized Strength and Water, Lacus specialized in Speed and her Smoke-like ability, while Ruby possessed exceptional Resistance and supernatural control.[11][13] Their defense of Clan Scarlett demonstrated what happened when those individual specializations were combined rather than treated separately.
The sisters' differences continued into Imperial life. Pepper became strongly associated with modern popular culture and technological enthusiasm, whereas Siena concentrated upon historical preservation; Ruby became one of the Empire's principal scientific minds, while Lacus retained her relaxed disposition. Despite developing radically different responsibilities, the Scarlett sisters remained socially close enough that late-Imperial family incidents could still summon all four together with Scathach almost instantly.[4][17]

#### Sylvia Elderblood
[[Sylvia Elderblood]] is the biological daughter of Siena and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[9] She belongs to the enormous second generation of the Elderblood dynasty and maintains especially close contact with the daughters of Ruby and Pepper. Chapter 1161 directly identifies her as Victor and Siena's daughter while she participates with Rosemary and Priscillia in one of the younger generation's many unauthorized explorations.[10]
The later Imperial chapters reveal more about Siena through Sylvia than through direct mother-daughter scenes. Sylvia immediately recognizes that Siena possesses a maternal demeanor very different from Pepper's youthful behavior, placing Siena alongside Ruby and Lacus as Scarlett women whose immense experience raising children has become obvious despite their bodies remaining physically youthful.[17]

#### Skills and Abilities
Siena Scarlett Elderblood is the eldest adopted daughter of [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] and one of the most powerful members of the [[Scarlett Clan]]. Although she was not born into the Scarlett main bloodline, Siena underwent the Clan Initiation Ritual and gained the potential to wield its characteristic Ice ability. Her development was exceptionally successful, with Siena being specifically regarded as an anomaly among initiated Clan members because of the extraordinary strength of the Ice power she awakened. Unlike [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]], who developed into the sisters' close-range defensive fighter, Siena specialized in overwhelming raw elemental output, long-range attacks, support and battlefield control. After centuries of training under [[Scathach]] and later [[Victor Elderblood]], she eventually underwent Dragonization and became a genuine Blood Dragon, with her pre-existing powers becoming incorporated into and strengthened by her new Draconic existence.[1][2][3][4][5][6]
- Blood Dragon Physiology: Siena eventually underwent Victor's Dragonization and became a genuine Blood Dragon. The transformation reconstructed the recipients at a fundamental level while preserving and evolving the supernatural abilities that already defined them.[5][6]
- Immense Physical Strength: Siena possesses the tremendous physical strength inherent to [[True Dragons]], vastly surpassing the body she possessed as a Noble Vampire.
- Superhuman Speed and Reflexes: Her Draconic physiology grants greatly enhanced movement speed and reactions.
- Immense Durability: Siena possesses the extremely resilient body of a genuine Dragon and can withstand levels of damage far beyond ordinary Vampires.
- Enhanced Agility: Her coordination, bodily control and mobility have been dramatically increased by her transformation.
- Enhanced Senses: Siena's already superhuman Vampire senses were retained and enhanced by her Draconic physiology.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: The powerful regenerative properties she possessed as a Vampire remain present in her Blood Dragon existence at a considerably greater level.
- [[Immortality]] and Longevity: Siena possesses the extraordinary lifespan and resistance to ordinary aging associated with both Vampires and Dragons.
- True Dragon Form: Siena possesses a genuine Draconic state rather than merely a transformation imitating Dragon characteristics. Her Dragonization produced the horns, wings and supernatural physiology characteristic of Victor's [[Blood Dragons]].[5][6]
- Flight: Siena can fly through the use of her Draconic wings.
- Shapeshifting: As a True Dragon, Siena can alter her outward form between a humanoid appearance and more overtly Draconic characteristics.
- Size Alteration: Siena can assume bodily dimensions far greater than those of her ordinary humanoid appearance through her Dragon physiology.
- Dragon Breath: Siena possesses the standard Draconic ability to expel supernatural power as a Breath attack. The exact form of her personal Breath has not yet been individually described in sufficient detail.
- [[Draconic Energy]]: Her Blood Dragon body possesses vastly greater supernatural Energy capacity and output than her former Vampire physiology, providing an enormously stronger foundation for her elemental powers.
- True Dragon Soul: Victor's Dragonization fundamentally reconstructed the recipients as genuine Dragons rather than simply altering their bodies.[5][6]
- Draconic Runes: As a True Dragon, Siena possesses an instinctive compatibility with the Draconic Language and its Runes, which allow Dragons to encode their [[Will Solace|Will]], meaning and supernatural instructions into Energy.[7]
- Dragon Goddess: Siena later attained the status of a Dragon Goddess in addition to her Blood Dragon Race. However, her exact completed personal Divinity has not yet been explicitly identified in the available material.
- Divine-Level Existence: Siena's current existence operates on a level greatly beyond her earlier Elder Vampire state, possessing the supernatural foundation of both a True Dragon and Divine Being.
- Unspecified Personal Divinity: No specific Concept should presently be assigned as Siena's definitive personal Divinity without an explicit later confirmation.
- Faith-Based Divinity Development: During the Imperial era, Siena became publicly associated with several Concepts through her activities preserving Humanity's lost history and culture. [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood explained that sustained Faith directed toward a Being with particular Concepts in mind can eventually cause that Being to awaken corresponding Divinities, and Victor deliberately controlled the public appearances of his wives partly for this purpose.[8]
- Goddess of Art: Siena became popularly known as the Goddess of Art after recovering and preserving major artistic works that had been lost during the destruction caused by the Demonic War and later catastrophes.[8]
- Goddess of Knowledge: The Imperial population associated Siena with Knowledge because of her work recovering historical information, texts and records from Humanity's former civilization.[8]
- Goddess of Culture: Siena similarly received the title Goddess of Culture for restoring and preserving cultural heritage within the [[Elderblood Empire]].[8]
- These titles were explicitly stated not to automatically constitute completed Divine Concepts at the time they were introduced. Rather, the Faith associated with them could contribute toward Siena eventually awakening corresponding Divinities.[8]
- Scarlett Clan Initiation: Siena was not born as a biological member of Scathach's main bloodline. She became a member of Clan Scarlett through the Clan Initiation Ritual, in which a Clan leader gives a Noble Vampire part of their blood and Essence. The process granted Siena the physical characteristics of Clan Scarlett and the potential to awaken its Ice ability.[3]
- Exceptional Bloodline Assimilation: Siena's development was extraordinarily successful even by the standards of initiated Count Clan members. She is explicitly described as an anomaly because of how powerful her acquired Ice ability became.[3]
- Scarlett Ice Potential: Clan Initiation cannot ordinarily give an adopted member greater bloodline potential than the direct main family, but Siena nevertheless developed an exceptional degree of power within those limitations.[3]
- Dormant Original Bloodline: Chapter 577 establishes that Siena possessed a biological bloodline of her own before joining Clan Scarlett. Unlike her Scarlett-derived Ice ability, the exact nature of this original Vampire bloodline was not explained during her earlier life.[3]
- Cryokinesis: Siena's primary supernatural ability throughout most of her Vampire life is the creation and manipulation of Ice. She possesses extraordinarily high raw Ice output and can generate large constructs extremely quickly, making her one of the Scarlett family's strongest ranged elemental combatants. Her speed and output when constructing Ice were already superior to Victor's during his early Vampire period, although remaining below Scathach's mastery.[9][1]
- Ice Generation: Siena can directly create enormous quantities of Ice through her supernatural power without requiring a pre-existing source.[9]
- Ice Manipulation: She can freely move, reshape and direct Ice according to her Will.
- Rapid Ice Construction: Siena can create large and complicated Ice structures extremely quickly, allowing her to continuously pressure an opponent from range.[9][1]
- Large-Scale Ice Creation: Her raw Ice output allows her to create enormous constructs considerably larger than her own body.[9]
- Giant Ice Hands: Siena can construct enormous hands made from Ice and control them as extensions of her own movements. During her training battle against Victor, she generated two gigantic Ice hands and attempted to crush him between them by bringing her own hands together.[9]
- Ice Spikes: Siena can produce large spikes or thorns of Ice and launch them toward opponents as projectiles.[9][1]
- Ice Spike Barrage: She can generate hundreds of Ice spikes simultaneously and bombard a target continuously from range.[9]
- Fine Ice Needles: Siena's later control became precise enough to create thousands of extremely thin Ice spikes that were almost invisible to the naked eye before raining them across the battlefield.[10]
- Cold Generation: Siena can release supernatural cold directly from her body and spread it through the surrounding environment.[9]
- Freezing: She can rapidly freeze objects and areas by releasing her Ice power.
- Immobilization: Siena can trap an opponent inside Ice to restrict their movement. She demonstrated this during her training against Victor by spreading cold around him and freezing him in place before launching an Ice barrage.[9]
- Ice Barriers: Siena can create Ice defensively to block incoming attacks.
- Ice Armor and Reinforcement: She can cover parts of her body with Ice, including creating Ice gauntlets around her hands during combat.[9]
- Ranged Bombardment: Siena's preferred early role within the Scarlett sisters' formation was attacking continuously from a distance while Ruby occupied the opponent at close range.[1]
- Area Denial: By filling an area with Ice spikes, freezing zones and projectiles, Siena can restrict an opponent's movement and force them into positions advantageous to her allies.
- Battlefield Control: Siena possesses sufficient scale and precision to manipulate the conditions of a large battlefield rather than merely attacking individual opponents.[10]
- Atmospheric Freezing: During the Demonic invasion of Clan Scarlett, Siena fired her Ice power into the clouds after Lacus and Pepper had prepared the atmosphere with smoke and Water. Her power froze the accumulated material across the sky, creating an enormous frozen formation over the territory.[10]
- Cloud Freezing: Siena can freeze Water and moisture distributed through large cloud formations.[10]
- Artificial Snow Generation: By freezing the Water and cloud material prepared by her sisters, Siena contributed to the creation of supernatural snowfall across Clan Scarlett's territory.[10]
- Lethal Snowfield: The Scarlett sisters' combined technique created snow carrying an enormous concentration of Ice power. Contact could freeze Demons into statues while the sisters retained enough control to avoid their allies.[10]
- Mass Ice Spike Rain: After being assigned control of the battlefield, Siena generated thousands of microscopic or nearly invisible sharp Ice spikes and caused them to fall throughout the territory. The attack alone eliminated a significant portion of the invading Demon army.[10]
- Wide-Area Targeting: Siena can direct large numbers of projectiles throughout an occupied battlefield rather than concentrating entirely on one opponent.[10]
- Volcanic Manipulation: Following her transformation into a Blood Dragon, Siena awakened a new supernatural affinity associated with volcanic phenomena. This ability is distinct from the Scarlett Ice power she acquired through Clan Initiation and emerged during the fundamental reconstruction of her existence through Victor's Dragonization.[5]
- Lava Manipulation: Siena can manipulate supernatural lava as part of her volcanic authority.[5]
- Magma Manipulation: Her post-Dragonization power encompasses molten subterranean material associated with volcanic phenomena.[5]
- Heat Manipulation: Siena can generate and manipulate extreme heat associated with her volcanic power.[5]
- Volcanic Energy: During the beginning of her Dragonization, Siena's entire body was described as boiling as though she were inside a volcano, marking the emergence of this new aspect of her existence.[5]
- Dual Thermal Extremes: Siena's current powers therefore encompass both her extremely developed Cryokinesis and the opposite thermal extreme represented by volcanic heat and molten material.[5]
- Supernatural Vision: Siena possesses extraordinarily powerful eyesight even by supernatural standards. While traveling toward the [[Gate of Tartar]], she used her enhanced vision to clearly observe structures located several kilometers ahead of the carriage.[11]
- Long-Range Vision: Siena can clearly perceive distant terrain and structures across distances of several kilometers.[11]
- Enhanced Visual Acuity: Her supernatural eyes can resolve distant details that would be impossible for an ordinary Human to distinguish unaided.[11]
- Battlefield Observation: Siena's exceptional eyesight complements her role as a ranged fighter and battlefield controller by allowing her to observe events occurring far from her immediate position.
- Advanced [[Martial Arts]]: Siena was personally trained by Scathach Elderblood Scarlett, whose martial system was distributed among her daughters according to each girl's strongest natural characteristic.[9][2]
- Scathach's Foundational Martial Art: Siena learned one of the principal divisions of Scathach's foundational combat system before later training to balance the remaining aspects.[2]
- Power: Siena was specifically given the Power branch of Scathach's martial art. Her specialization reflects her greatest natural strength: enormous supernatural Ice output and the ability to apply overwhelming force through ranged attacks.[2]
- Strength: Siena later worked to balance the physical strength component of Scathach's system instead of relying only upon elemental power.
- Speed: Continued training under Victor and Scathach improved her speed and mobility so that she would not remain a stationary ranged combatant.[4]
- Defense: Defense was historically Siena's most obvious weakness. After substantially improving her other basic attributes through Victor's training, she deliberately concentrated on strengthening her defensive abilities.[4]
- Control: The fifth and unifying principle of Scathach's martial system combines Power, Strength, Speed and Defense into a complete fighting foundation. Siena's later training was directed toward balancing these attributes instead of remaining hyper-specialized only in raw Power.[2][4]
- Raw Power Specialization: Siena historically possessed the highest emphasis on pure supernatural power among the adopted Scarlett sisters. Where Pepper specialized in Strength and Lacus in Speed, Siena was trained to maximize the force and output of her abilities.[2]
- Defensive Training: By Chapter 621, Siena had already substantially balanced her other attributes and deliberately identified Defense as the primary weakness she still needed to correct. She consequently trained through repeated serious battles with her sisters until severe injury, using Vampire regeneration to permit an intensity impossible for normal fighters.[4]
- Combat against Superior Opponents: Siena repeatedly trained against Scathach and later Victor, both of whom could punish flaws in her technique immediately. This gradually forced her to improve beyond simply overwhelming weaker enemies with Ice.[9][1][4]
- Pain Tolerance: Scathach's training routinely inflicted extreme physical trauma upon Siena, including broken limbs, spinal injuries and wounds through the chest. Although Siena openly dislikes pain and training far more than her mother or Victor, she has nevertheless endured centuries of Scathach's methods.[9]
- Expert Ranged Combatant: Siena is the principal ranged attacker among the four Scarlett sisters. Her combat style emphasizes maintaining distance and overwhelming opponents with repeated Ice constructs and projectile barrages.[1]
- Long-Range Support: While Ruby occupies an enemy at close range, Siena attacks from outside the opponent's immediate reach and forces them to divide their attention.[1]
- Projectile Barrages: Siena can continuously create Ice thorns and spikes in sufficient numbers to pressure an opponent without approaching melee range.[9][1]
- Crossfire Creation: Siena's ranged attacks become particularly dangerous when used with the sisters' close-range pressure because defending against her projectiles creates openings for Ruby, Pepper or Lacus.[1]
- Precision Area Attacks: Her later training allowed Siena to create extremely small Ice projectiles over a large area, increasing both the difficulty of detecting the attacks and the number of targets she could threaten simultaneously.[10]
- Scarlett Sisters Combination: Siena possesses centuries of familiarity fighting alongside Ruby, Lacus and Pepper. Their combat styles developed around complementary roles rather than four fighters independently using similar abilities.[1]
- Ranged Support Role: Siena functions as the team's principal long-range attacker and elemental artillery.[1]
- Ruby — Frontline: Ruby keeps an enemy occupied through superior stamina, defense and spear combat.
- Pepper — Bruiser: Pepper exploits her exceptional strength to pressure and break enemy defenses.
- Lacus — Assassin: Lacus uses speed and mist to attack from blind spots.
- Siena — Artillery/Controller: Siena pressures the battlefield from range and uses her high Ice output to restrict movement, bombard targets and create openings for the others.[1]
- Combination Timing: The sisters have trained together long enough to immediately exploit one another's attacks. If an opponent redirects attention toward Siena or Lacus, Ruby and Pepper can exploit the opening, while Siena can bombard an enemy already occupied by her sisters.[1]
- Environmental Combination: During the Demonic invasion, Lacus produced a massive dark cloud, Pepper supplied Water, Siena froze the accumulated moisture and Ruby applied her exceptional control to the resulting Ice environment. The combination produced widespread supernatural snow capable of freezing Demons throughout Clan Scarlett's territory.[10]
- Battlefield Control Role: Once the combined technique was established, Ruby specifically instructed Siena to control the battlefield. Siena answered by increasing the snowfall and generating thousands of extremely fine Ice spikes throughout the territory.[10]
- Exceptional Leadership: Siena is widely recognized for her ability to lead. As Scathach's eldest adopted daughter and practical right hand, she has spent centuries carrying responsibilities that Scathach herself frequently ignored.[10][12]
- Clan Administration: Siena routinely manages the political and administrative affairs of Clan Scarlett in Scathach's place.
- Territorial Management: She possesses extensive experience governing and organizing the territory belonging to one of [[Nightingale]]'s Vampire Count Clans.
- Crisis Preparation: Before the Demonic invasion of Clan Scarlett, Siena took Scathach's warning seriously and prepared the territory in advance rather than waiting for the enemy to arrive.[10]
- Civilian Evacuation: Siena evacuated Clan Scarlett's population into the protected bunker beneath Scathach's castle before the invading Demon army could reach them, leaving the city effectively empty and preventing unnecessary civilian casualties.[10]
- Military Command: Siena can issue orders to Clan Scarlett's servants and forces during emergencies and organize defenses against large supernatural armies.[10]
- Threat Assessment: Upon learning that the invader was [[Zagan]], the 61st Key of Solomon, Siena maintained the planned defense rather than recklessly abandoning preparation to personally confront the Demon Duke.[10]
- Political Knowledge: Siena has historically been one of Scathach's daughters most familiar with Nightingale's internal politics and the responsibilities of the Vampire Count Clans.
- Strategic Support: Her ability to simultaneously command an area and provide long-range elemental pressure makes Siena especially suited to coordinating large battles rather than exclusively engaging in individual duels.
- High-Level Combat Experience: Siena possesses centuries of martial and supernatural combat experience accumulated under Scathach's training and through participation in increasingly dangerous conflicts.
- Demon War Veteran: Siena personally commanded and fought during the defense of Clan Scarlett when Nightingale was invaded by the armies of [[Diablo]].[10]
- Large-Scale Warfare: Unlike combatants whose experience is primarily based on duels, Siena has directly fought while defending an entire territory against an invading army.[10]
- Multi-Target Combat: Siena's Cryokinesis allows her to attack many enemies simultaneously, making her particularly effective against armies and groups.
- Battlefield Awareness: Her enhanced eyesight, leadership experience and ranged specialization allow Siena to maintain awareness of threats beyond her immediate surroundings.[11][10]
- Energy Control: Although raw output was historically Siena's greatest advantage, Victor's training forced her to refine control and improve the efficiency with which that output was applied.[4]
- Adaptive Training: Siena eventually moved away from focusing exclusively on the abilities that came naturally to her and began deliberately developing weaker attributes such as Defense.[4]
- Cultural Preservation: During the development of the Elderblood Empire, Siena participated in recovering cultural and historical works that had been lost during the collapse of Human civilization.[8]
- Historical Restoration: Siena helped recover ancient artworks, original classical music, historical texts, scriptures and other surviving records of Humanity's previous civilizations.[8]
- Knowledge Preservation: Her activities preserved information and cultural artifacts that otherwise would have disappeared following planetary destruction and social collapse.[8]
- Art Preservation: Siena contributed to restoring famous artistic works, including historical pieces such as the Mona Lisa, which contributed to the public associating her with Art.[8]
- Administrative Experience: Centuries spent acting as Scathach's practical representative gave Siena considerable experience in governance, resource management and organization long before the creation of the Elderblood Empire.

##### Former Vampire Abilities
- Noble Vampire Physiology (Former as a separate Race): Siena was originally a Noble Vampire before becoming a Blood Dragon. Her Vampire physiology and powers were incorporated into her Draconic existence rather than being discarded.[3][5]
- Superhuman Strength: Siena possessed strength far beyond ordinary Humans even during her Vampire life.
- Superhuman Speed and Reflexes: Her Noble Vampire body provided the speed and reactions characteristic of experienced Vampires.
- Enhanced Durability: Siena could withstand severe physical trauma that would immediately kill an ordinary Human.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Her Vampire regeneration could repair extreme injuries within a short period. During [[Scathach]]'s training, wounds including mangled limbs, spinal damage and a destroyed chest were treated as recoverable training injuries.[9]
- Enhanced Agility: Vampire physiology provided greatly enhanced bodily coordination and movement.
- Enhanced Senses: Siena's already exceptional supernatural eyesight existed during her Vampire period, allowing her to clearly observe structures several kilometers away.[11]
- Supernatural Longevity: Siena lived for centuries as a Noble Vampire before her later Dragon transformation.
- Blood Sustenance: Like other Vampires, Siena could consume blood as supernatural nourishment.
- [[Scarlett Clan]] Ice Ability: Siena gained the potential for Ice Manipulation through Scathach's Clan Initiation Ritual and developed it to an exceptionally high degree.[3]
- Vampire Regeneration Exploitation: Siena and her sisters routinely trained until one participant was severely injured, deliberately taking advantage of Vampire regeneration to permit otherwise lethal levels of combat training.[4]
