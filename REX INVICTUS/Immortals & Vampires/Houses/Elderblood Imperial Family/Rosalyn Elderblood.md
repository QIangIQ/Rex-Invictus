### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 1007
Daddy, if you wish, you can easily nurture this Galaxy with our help!
—Rosalyn to [[Victor Elderblood]], Chapter 1007
Rosalyn Elderblood is the biological daughter of Victor Elderblood and [[Roxanne Elderblood]], making her a unique descendant of both the Blood Dragon lineage created by Victor and the Negative World Tree lineage represented by Roxanne.[1]
Rosalyn first appears by communicating directly with Victor from within his inner existence. Alongside her sister [[Amaya Elderblood]], she explains that the two of them can help their father nurture an entire Galaxy, which would in turn allow that Galaxy to continuously nourish Victor and create a self-reinforcing cycle of Energy generation.[1] This statement establishes Rosalyn as far more than an ordinary second-generation Dragon: even while still young, her inherited World Tree nature already allows her to think and operate on an astronomical ecological scale.
She later appears again alongside Amaya while observing Victor assume his colossal Dragon Form. The two enthusiastically watch their father, with Rosalyn ordering Amaya to record everything so that they can show the rest of their sisters.[2]
Rosalyn is consequently one of the most unusual members of Victor's second generation. Through her father she belongs to the Blood Dragon dynasty, while through Roxanne she inherits the characteristics of a Negative World Tree—a class of planetary entity normally born to nourish and regulate entire worlds rather than exist as the biological child of another species.[1][2]

### Physical Appearance
Rosalyn's complete physical appearance has not yet been described in detail in the novel.
Existing information confirms that she possesses a humanoid identity and is capable of directly communicating with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and her family, but no chapter presently provides a sufficiently detailed description of her height, hair, clothing, horns, wings, or Dragon Form to construct a reliable physical profile.
As Victor and Roxanne's daughter, Rosalyn is a hybrid existence carrying both Draconic and World Tree characteristics, but the exact manner in which these traits manifest visually has not yet been explicitly shown.

### Personality
Rosalyn is cheerful, energetic, inquisitive, and extremely affectionate toward her father.
Her first spoken line immediately demonstrates how naturally she thinks in terms of helping [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. When Victor wondered about the limits of his enormous Energy reserves, Rosalyn enthusiastically told him that she and Amaya could help him nurture an entire Galaxy.[1] Her response did not focus on her own growth or benefit, but instead on how her abilities could strengthen her father.
Rosalyn also displays the intelligence and intuitive understanding expected from a World Tree. Rather than discussing Energy only as something stored inside Victor, she immediately proposed a symbiotic Galactic system: Victor could nourish the Galaxy with the help of his World Tree daughters, while the resulting living Galaxy would nourish him in return.[1] This reflects the same fundamental principle that governs [[World Trees]] and their planets, but applied on a vastly larger scale.
She is also mischievous and willing to avoid her lessons when something more interesting catches her attention. During her debut, Rosalyn and Amaya were apparently supposed to be studying, but instead contacted Victor. Their conversation ended when [[Amara Elderblood]] discovered that both girls were skipping lessons and ordered them to return immediately.[1]
Rosalyn additionally possesses the characteristic admiration many of Victor's daughters display toward him. When Victor later assumed a Dragon Form large enough to dwarf astronomical structures, Rosalyn and Amaya reacted with excitement rather than fear. Rosalyn immediately instructed Amaya to record the sight so they could show their sisters.[2]
Her behavior around Amaya suggests that the two are particularly close. They repeatedly appear together, share similar interests, communicate naturally, and cooperate in matters involving their father.[1][2]

### Nature

#### World Tree–Blood Dragon Hybrid
Rosalyn was born from the union of [[Victor Elderblood]], the Primogenitor of the [[Blood Dragons]], and [[Roxanne Elderblood]], a Negative World Tree. Her existence therefore represents a combination of two fundamentally different classes of supernatural beings.
Her paternal side gives her membership within Victor's Draconic bloodline, while her maternal side gives her a direct World Tree heritage. This makes Rosalyn fundamentally different from ordinary Dragons and ordinary [[World Trees]].
Unlike a conventional World Tree, which normally develops as part of a planet's ecosystem, Rosalyn was born directly as Victor and Roxanne's daughter and exists within Victor's family from birth.[1]

#### Negative World Tree Lineage
Roxanne belongs to the Negative side of the World Tree duality. Rosalyn consequently belongs to that same Negative branch rather than the Positive World Tree lineage inherited by [[Amaya Elderblood]] from [[Amara Elderblood]].
The two daughters therefore parallel their mothers:
- [[Roxanne Elderblood]] → Rosalyn Elderblood — Negative World Tree lineage.
- Amara Elderblood → Amaya Elderblood — Positive World Tree lineage.
This distinction is especially significant because Positive and Negative [[World Trees]] are normally complementary planetary entities. Within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family, both branches of that cosmic duality exist directly among his wives and daughters.

#### Connection to Victor
Rosalyn can communicate with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] mentally from within his inner existence.[1]
Her first appearance occurs without her physically entering the location where Victor was training Kali. Victor simply hears Rosalyn speaking directly to him, followed immediately by Amaya and later Amara.[1]
This strongly connects Rosalyn to the same inner World Tree environment inhabited by Roxanne and Amara, whose existence is integrated into Victor's Soul rather than attached to ordinary external planets.

### Synopsis

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Rosalyn was introduced during the period in which [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was training [[Kali Elderblood]] and evaluating the absurd scale of his own Energy reserves.
After absorbing the excess Energy generated by Kali, Victor noted that the amount had restored only approximately three percent of his reserves. Kali, unable to comprehend how much Energy Victor actually contained, asked him to quantify it. Victor admitted that even he did not know how to properly measure the total.[1]
While Victor considered the problem, Rosalyn suddenly contacted him mentally.
She enthusiastically told Victor that, with the assistance of herself and Amaya, he could easily nurture the entire Galaxy. Amaya immediately expanded upon her sister's idea, explaining that once the Galaxy was properly nurtured, it could nourish Victor in return, creating a perpetual cycle capable of providing effectively endless Energy.[1]
Their suggestion demonstrated that Rosalyn and Amaya had already inherited the most fundamental instinct and function of [[World Trees]]: nurturing a larger living system while simultaneously receiving Energy back from that system.
The scale, however, was radically different from an ordinary World Tree. Instead of discussing the nourishment of a single planet, Rosalyn casually suggested applying the same principle to an entire Galaxy.[1]
Victor was pleased by his daughters' suggestion, but before he could respond properly, Amara interrupted the conversation after discovering that Rosalyn and Amaya had once again skipped their lessons. She angrily ordered both girls to return, revealing that the two young World Trees were still undergoing education despite already understanding planetary and Galactic energy systems.[1]
Rosalyn later appeared again during Victor's exploration of the scale of his Dragon Form.
After Victor transformed into an enormous Dragon and continued expanding until planets and stellar structures appeared insignificant from his perspective, Rosalyn and Amaya excitedly observed their father.[2]
Both girls exclaimed over the sight. Rosalyn then instructed Amaya not to waste time and to record everything so that they could show the event to their other sisters later.[2]
The scene also confirms that Rosalyn and Amaya were capable of observing Victor even while he operated on astronomical scales, reinforcing their unusual relationship with both him and his internal World Tree network.

### Skills & Abilities
World Tree–Blood Dragon Hybrid Physiology: Rosalyn is the biological daughter of the Blood Dragon Primogenitor [[Victor Elderblood]] and the Negative World Tree [[Roxanne Elderblood]]. Her physiology therefore combines Victor's Draconic lineage with the planetary World Tree nature inherited from Roxanne.[1]
Because Rosalyn has not yet received an extended combat scene, only abilities directly demonstrated or strongly established through her World Tree role are listed below.

#### World Tree Abilities
- Galactic Nurturing: Rosalyn explicitly states that she and Amaya can help [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] nurture an entire Galaxy.[1] This is currently her clearest demonstrated large-scale ability and shows that her World Tree functions are not restricted to supporting a single conventional planet.
- Energy Ecosystem Creation: Rosalyn understands and can participate in the creation of a reciprocal energetic system in which Victor nourishes a Galaxy and the Galaxy subsequently nourishes him in return.[1] Amaya describes the result as a perpetual cycle capable of granting Victor effectively endless Energy.
- World Tree Nourishment: Rosalyn inherits the central function of [[World Trees]]: providing Energy and nurturing a larger living system. In her case, this ability can operate in cooperation with Victor and Amaya on a Galactic scale.[1]
- Negative World Tree Nature: Through Roxanne, Rosalyn belongs to the Negative branch of the World Tree family. She therefore represents the negative side of the same duality in which Amaya represents the Positive side through Amara.
- Energy Perception: Rosalyn possesses enough understanding of Victor's Energy system to immediately propose a viable method of expanding and recycling his reserves through a Galaxy.[1]
- Mental Communication: Rosalyn can speak directly to Victor without being physically present beside him. Her first appearance consists entirely of a mental conversation between Rosalyn, Victor, Amaya, and Amara.[1]
- Remote Observation: Rosalyn and Amaya were able to observe Victor while he assumed his colossal Dragon Form in space.[2]

#### Blood Dragon Heritage
Rosalyn is a direct biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] after his establishment as the Blood Dragon Primogenitor and therefore belongs to his second-generation Draconic lineage.
Her exact personal applications of Draconic strength, breath, transformation, Dragon Language, or Runes have not yet been individually demonstrated in a dedicated scene. These abilities should therefore not be assigned specific feats to Rosalyn until they are shown directly.

#### World Tree Intelligence
Rosalyn displays a natural understanding of the symbiotic relationship between a World Tree and the environment it nourishes.
Her suggestion concerning Galactic nourishment shows that she understands:
- How large living systems can be nourished through World Tree Energy.
- How those systems can return Energy to their source.
- How such a relationship can become self-reinforcing.
- How multiple [[World Trees]] can cooperate in the same energetic structure.[1]
This understanding is particularly notable considering that Rosalyn was still young enough to be attending lessons under the supervision of Roxanne and Amara.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Rosalyn's father.
Rosalyn clearly adores him and consistently refers to him as Daddy.[1] Her first instinct when considering her own abilities is to determine how they can be used to strengthen and support him.
When Victor wondered about his Energy reserves, Rosalyn immediately proposed using herself and Amaya to nurture an entire Galaxy for him.[1]
Later, when Victor revealed the immense scale of his Dragon Form, Rosalyn reacted with excitement and pride, wanting the moment recorded so the rest of her sisters could see it.[2]

#### Roxanne Elderblood
[[Roxanne Elderblood]] is Rosalyn's biological mother and the source of her Negative World Tree heritage.[1]
Roxanne herself is a World Tree whose natural planetary function became redirected toward [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] after she established a permanent connection with his Soul. Rosalyn was consequently born into a very different environment from ordinary [[World Trees]]: rather than awakening on an independent planet, she was born directly into Victor's family and inner World Tree system.

#### Amaya Elderblood
[[Amaya Elderblood]] is one of Rosalyn's closest sisters and the daughter of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and [[Amara Elderblood]].
The two frequently appear together. During Rosalyn's debut, the sisters jointly proposed nurturing a Galaxy for Victor, with Rosalyn presenting the initial idea and Amaya explaining how the resulting feedback loop would provide their father with enormous Energy.[1]
They also skipped their lessons together and were reprimanded simultaneously by Amara.[1]
In Chapter 1020, Rosalyn again appears beside Amaya while they observe Victor's Dragon Form. Rosalyn tells her sister to record the event so they can show it to the rest of their siblings.[2]
The pair also embody complementary World Tree lineages: Rosalyn descends from Roxanne's Negative side, while Amaya descends from Amara's Positive side.

#### Amara Elderblood
[[Amara Elderblood]] is one of Rosalyn's maternal World Tree relatives and appears to participate in educating both Rosalyn and Amaya.
During Rosalyn's debut, Amara catches the two girls skipping their lessons and immediately orders them to return.[1]

#### The Universal Tree
[[The Universal Tree]] is the Progenitor and creator of the World Tree race and is therefore Rosalyn's maternal ancestor through Roxanne.
Unlike normal [[World Trees]], Rosalyn is not one of The Universal Tree's directly created planetary daughters; she belongs to a later biological generation produced through Roxanne and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
