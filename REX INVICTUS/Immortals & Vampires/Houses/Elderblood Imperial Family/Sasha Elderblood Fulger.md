#### Debut

Sasha Elderblood Fulger, formerly Sasha Fulger and Sasha Alucard Fulger, is one of the principal female protagonists of [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]] and one of the three original wives of [[Victor Elderblood]], alongside [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] and [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]]. She is the biological daughter of [[Annasthashia Elderblood Fulger]] and [[William Salvatore Florence]], the maternal granddaughter of [[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]], heiress of the ancient Clan Fulger, and mother of [[Silvia Elderblood]]. Originally a Noble Vampire distinguished by her extraordinary Lightning and speed, Sasha eventually became a genuine Blood Dragon, True Dragon, Dragon Goddess and one of the senior Empresses of the [[Elderblood Empire]].[7]
Sasha's early life was defined by the contradiction between her extraordinary natural potential and the neglect she experienced within her own family. Born into one of [[Nightingale]]'s ancient Noble Vampire Bloodlines, she inherited the Lightning of Clan Fulger and characteristics associated with her father's Florence lineage, including unusually developed Time perception and physical resilience. Rather than nurturing her abilities through a supportive upbringing, her mother subjected her to increasingly dangerous experiments intended to determine the limits of her Lightning, while William failed to provide the protection or emotional support she needed. The one consistent source of affection in Sasha's childhood was her maid Julia, whom she regarded as her real mother. Julia's murder during an Inquisition ambush consequently devastated Sasha and almost drove her into a suicidal campaign of revenge.[8]
Her accidental marriage to Victor became the beginning of a radically different existence. Although Sasha initially knew him primarily through Violet's stories, the abnormal Vampire transformation ritual bound her to him alongside her two childhood friends. Their relationship developed during the immediate aftermath of Julia's death, when Victor became one of the first people to listen to Sasha's experiences without treating her suffering as an acceptable price for the development of her Bloodline. His willingness to confront her parents and his refusal to abandon her gradually gave Sasha the emotional security necessary to recover, reconcile with members of her family and recognize that she did not need to become cruel merely to survive supernatural society.[9]
Sasha subsequently developed from an inexperienced Lightning specialist into a formidable supernatural warrior. Her initial reliance upon the extraordinary speed of Clan Fulger concealed serious deficiencies in conventional Martial Arts, but repeated instruction, battles and accelerated training forced her to develop the technical foundation needed to confront opponents possessing comparable power. She eventually learned the ancestral Martial Arts created by Carmila, refined her inherited abilities through Victor's Progenitor Blood and accumulated experience through the Tower of Nightmares and the [[Dream Realm]]. Her achievements within the latter included becoming its only SSS-ranked Adventurer and acquiring the title The Fastest Woman Alive, a distinction carrying particular significance within Clan Fulger's competitive martial tradition.[3]
Following Victor's ascension as the Dragon God of [[Chaos]], Sasha accepted his offer to reconstruct her existence through the Blood Dragon lineage. The transformation preserved her Lightning, inherited characteristics and personal identity while replacing her original Vampire limitations with the superior physiology of a genuine True Dragon. Her subsequent development continued through extraordinary periods of accelerated Time, during which Victor's family evolved from a supernatural faction into the ruling dynasty of an immense Imperial civilization.[4]
Within the Elderblood Empire, Sasha eventually assumed responsibility for its police and became one of the senior authorities associated with Imperial security and military affairs. Her present position must be distinguished from the Supreme General depicted in a possible future witnessed through Violet's supernatural perception of causality. That future describes Sasha possessing extraordinary wartime command authority, but it does not establish that she currently holds the office.[2]
Despite the enormous changes in her circumstances, Sasha remains closely associated with Violet and Ruby. Their friendship predates Victor's transformation, and their shared history continues distinguishing them from the many women who later joined his family. Sasha's journey from a traumatized young Vampire to a powerful Dragon Goddess, mother, Empress and Imperial security authority is one of the principal examples of the personal development made possible through Victor's expanding family.

### Physical Appearance

#### Noble Vampire
Sasha is an exceptionally beautiful woman with fair skin, blonde hair and naturally blue eyes. During her original Vampire existence, her eyes could become red whenever she invoked her Vampiric nature, particularly while channeling Lightning or experiencing intense emotions. At the beginning of the story, her blonde hair was relatively short, and her youthful appearance contrasted with the extraordinary physical strength and supernatural potential inherited from her family. She eventually reached approximately 180 centimeters in height, developing a mature, athletic physique whose proportions were especially suited to the intense acceleration associated with Fulger Lightning. Her lower-body musculature was particularly well developed, reflecting the physical demands placed upon her legs and joints whenever she employed extreme speed.
Sasha generally preferred clothing that allowed unrestricted movement. Jeans, leggings and similarly practical garments were among her ordinary choices, contrasting with the more elaborate dresses favored by some members of the Vampire aristocracy. Her appearance changed gradually through prolonged exposure to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood, which improved the physical characteristics of his wives and caused Sasha's hair to become substantially longer. During the later refinement of her Progenitor Bloodline, her eyes acquired a permanent blood-red coloration, while her body became more resilient and her natural beauty was further refined. The transformation did not fundamentally change her recognizable features, but it represented a significant improvement to the supernatural characteristics inherited from both sides of her family.[10]
During the Demonic War, Sasha wore specialized silver armor constructed from materials Victor had obtained while visiting Clan Adrasteia's territory. The armor covered most of her body but was deliberately designed to avoid interfering with her speed, allowing her to perform extreme acceleration without the weight or restrictions of conventional heavy equipment. Victoria assisted with its preparation and arranged Sasha's blonde hair into a braided ponytail so that it would not obstruct her vision. The armor incorporated a special cloth manufactured from the hides of monsters sensitive to Lightning; this material helped regulate the circulation of Sasha's Electricity, stored excess Energy and could be used to release accumulated power. When activated, the originally white cloth became golden, and Lightning spread across the armor, giving Sasha the appearance of an armored supernatural knight surrounded by electrical light.[11]

#### Blood Dragon
Sasha's appearance underwent a more fundamental transformation when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] reconstructed her as a genuine Blood Dragon. Unlike the partial Draconic enhancements previously available through his Bloodline, the process rebuilt her physical and spiritual foundations, replacing the limitations of her Noble Vampire physiology with those of a True Dragon. The transformation preserved the characteristics that made Sasha recognizable, including her blonde hair, Fulger Lightning and extreme speed, while granting her the superior biological and supernatural potential associated with Victor's new Race.[4]
In her humanoid Draconic appearance, Sasha continues resembling the woman she had been before Dragonization, although her body possesses qualities far beyond those of an ordinary Vampire. Her mature existence is no longer restricted to the dimensions or biological limitations of her earlier form, and she can manifest the characteristics associated with her complete True Dragon physiology. During her experiences within the Dream World, her Draconic identity was represented through a magnificent golden Dragon, reflecting the Lightning and speed that had remained central to her development.[3]
Like the other mature Dragon Goddesses of Victor's immediate family, Sasha retains a youthful appearance despite experiencing enormous periods of accelerated Time. Her physical transformation and continued development reflect the progression of her Bloodline without erasing her personal identity.

### Personality
Sasha is a proud, competitive, affectionate and emotionally intense woman whose outward restraint conceals a possessive temperament similar to those of Violet and Ruby. Among [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s three original wives, she traditionally occupies a position between Violet's openly explosive personality and Ruby's comparatively composed and analytical nature. She is capable of embarrassment and hesitation, particularly when discussing her feelings directly, but her apparent shyness does not indicate a lack of determination. When she decides that something matters to her, Sasha can become extraordinarily stubborn and unwilling to retreat, especially when the safety of her husband or family is involved.
Much of Sasha's original personality developed in response to her childhood. Born into a wealthy and powerful Vampire family, she nevertheless grew up under conditions in which her supernatural potential was treated as more important than her happiness. The cold personality controlling her mother's body repeatedly subjected her to dangerous Lightning training, while William remained emotionally distant and failed to intervene. Sasha consequently learned to suppress fear and endure physical suffering rather than expect comfort from her parents. The emotional isolation produced by this upbringing made Julia's affection especially important, since Julia was the one person who consistently treated her as a child deserving care rather than an exceptional Bloodline requiring further development.[8]
Julia's murder exposed the intensity of Sasha's suppressed emotions. When Sasha discovered the bodies of the Fulger servants and the woman who had raised her, her grief immediately developed into hatred and a desire for revenge. Her treatment of Maria demonstrated the cruelty she could display under such circumstances. Instead of killing the hunter directly, Sasha forced her into a defective Vampire transformation because she considered the humiliation of becoming the creature Maria hated more appropriate than ordinary death. Sasha's subsequent willingness to attack the Inquisition without regard for her own survival revealed that Julia's death had temporarily destroyed the emotional stability she had maintained throughout her childhood.
Nevertheless, Sasha's capacity for hatred was never the entirety of her personality. Her relationship with Victor revealed an underlying kindness that she sometimes regarded as a weakness. Having grown up within a supernatural society where powerful individuals frequently imposed their desires upon those beneath them, Sasha initially believed that becoming stronger required suppressing compassion and accepting cruelty as normal. Victor challenged this belief by refusing to dismiss her suffering and by distinguishing the necessity of confronting enemies from the desire to inflict pain unnecessarily. His support allowed Sasha to recognize that protecting herself and her family did not require abandoning the gentler aspects of her personality.[9]
This distinction became particularly important during her gradual reconciliation with Maria. The former hunter had participated in one of the worst events of Sasha's life, yet months of proximity forced Sasha to acknowledge that Maria had become a person with whom she shared experiences rather than merely a symbol of Julia's murder. Sasha struggled with the possibility of forgiving someone responsible for such suffering because she feared that doing so would diminish Julia's importance. Eventually, however, she recognized that continuing to define Maria exclusively through the past would only perpetuate the pain surrounding the event. When the opportunity for revenge against Carlos finally arrived, Sasha attempted to force herself to witness the punishment despite taking no pleasure in it. Victor recognized her discomfort and reminded her that kindness was not something she needed to destroy simply because their world was brutal.[12]
Sasha's relationship with her mother underwent an equally significant transformation. During childhood, she had known only the cold Annasthashia who treated her abilities as subjects for experimentation. When the affectionate Natashia personality emerged during Victor's confrontation with her parents, Sasha suddenly found herself being embraced and praised by a woman wearing the face of the person responsible for much of her suffering. Her initial confusion and suspicion were natural consequences of that history, but she gradually accepted that the situation gave her an opportunity to experience maternal affection she had previously believed impossible. The later separation of the Souls and identities associated with the Fulger lineage complicated matters further, allowing Sasha to develop individual relationships with Natashia, Natasha and Carmila rather than confronting the entire history of her family through one divided existence.[13]
Her competitive nature also distinguishes her from many of Victor's other wives. Although Sasha possessed extraordinary speed from birth, she eventually recognized that moving faster than an opponent did not automatically make her a superior fighter. Her early battles had often ended before enemies could react, preventing her from developing the technical foundation possessed by individuals who had been forced to confront stronger opponents regularly. Training with Ruby, Lacus and Natashia gradually corrected these weaknesses, while later experiences within the Tower of Nightmares demonstrated that superior judgment and the ability to adapt were just as important as raw acceleration. Sasha consequently became increasingly willing to endure severe training because she understood that her natural advantages could become liabilities if she depended upon them exclusively.[14]
Her personal ambition is particularly apparent in the importance she attaches to the title The Fastest Woman Alive. Within Clan Fulger, the distinction does not belong automatically to the individual capable of achieving the greatest movement speed; it reflects overall proficiency in the use of Lightning, including technique, control and practical effectiveness. Sasha could exceed other members of her family in pure speed without necessarily possessing greater overall mastery of the Element. During her Dream World experiences, she obtained the title through independent accomplishments, finding satisfaction in an achievement that reflected her own development rather than her position as Victor's wife.[3]
The Dream World also demonstrated Sasha's capacity for responsibility and restraint. Unlike Violet and Ruby, who developed influential organizations through the political and economic structures of the artificial world, Sasha pursued an independent reputation through adventuring. Her eventual position as the only SSS-ranked Adventurer granted her enough influence to interfere directly with the government of the surrounding kingdom, but she chose not to seize authority merely because she possessed the power to do so. Sasha believed that a country existed for the people living within it and that overthrowing a functioning government for personal prestige would not automatically benefit its population. Her decision reflected the practical judgment developed through years of training and exposure to the consequences of supernatural conflict.
Sasha also possesses a strong protective instinct toward the people she considers family. Although she can be possessive of Victor and jealous of his attention, her attachment extends beyond their romantic relationship. She is willing to fight for Violet and Ruby despite their rivalry, remains protective of her daughter Silvia and increasingly supports restrictions intended to ensure that the younger Elderblood generation develops proper discipline. Her own childhood provides an important reason for this approach. Sasha knows what happens when parents prioritize supernatural potential over a child's emotional well-being, and she does not regard extreme training as sufficient justification for neglecting affection or personal responsibility.
As the Elderblood family expanded, Sasha gradually became more comfortable expressing the exaggerated and uninhibited temperament associated with the Fulger women. During the Imperial Era, she openly teased Violet about the numerical advantage possessed by the Fulgers within Victor's household and participated in conversations that would have embarrassed her younger self. Natasha and Natashia took particular satisfaction in this change, regarding Sasha's increasing shamelessness as evidence that she had finally become more comfortable with her maternal lineage. Nevertheless, her newfound confidence did not eliminate her ability to recognize poor decisions, and she remained willing to oppose Violet or other senior family members when their actions created unnecessary problems.[15]
Sasha's development eventually extended into government and military affairs. Her instinctive response to danger increasingly involved examining the threat, identifying weaknesses and preparing a practical solution rather than reacting solely through personal anger. This was particularly evident following the crisis involving [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]] and the Primordial order. While Victor's family struggled with the implications of confronting beings beyond the Empire's present military capacity, Sasha remained with Violet to discuss what could be done. She supported the development of an independent deterrent, recognizing that even a family protected by Victor should not depend upon one individual to resolve every future crisis.
Despite becoming a Dragon Goddess and accumulating enormous subjective experience, Sasha retains the emotional characteristics that originally defined her. She remains affectionate toward Victor, competitive with her fellow Fulgers, playfully antagonistic toward Violet and Ruby, and deeply protective of the family that replaced the isolation of her childhood. Her development has given her greater confidence and judgment without eliminating the passionate personality beneath her outward composure.

### Background
Sasha was born as the biological daughter of [[Annasthashia Elderblood Fulger]] and [[William Salvatore Florence]], making her the direct heiress of one of [[Nightingale]]'s ancient Noble Vampire Bloodlines. Her maternal ancestry originated from Carmila Fulger, a powerful Lightning Spirit whose transformation into a Vampire established the supernatural characteristics inherited by subsequent generations. The conversion also introduced biological limitations, since the full potential of Carmila's original Lightning could place enormous strain upon a body developed through ordinary Vampire physiology. Members of Clan Fulger consequently possessed extraordinary speed and destructive power but required equally exceptional physical conditioning to use those advantages safely.[16]
William belonged to a different Vampire lineage whose abilities complemented those of Clan Fulger. His family possessed enhanced perception of Time, allowing members to observe events in greater detail and experience surrounding movement as though it were occurring more slowly. Such perception was especially valuable to individuals whose combat style involved extreme acceleration, since physical speed becomes less useful when the user's senses cannot properly follow the resulting movement. Sasha also inherited exceptional resilience through her father's lineage, further improving her compatibility with the destructive Lightning of Clan Fulger. Natashia had selected William because these characteristics could improve the potential of their descendants, but the marriage provided Sasha with little emotional security.[10]
Sasha demonstrated unusual potential from childhood and became the subject of dangerous training intended to force the [[Fulger Bloodline]] beyond its ordinary limitations. The personality controlling her mother's body repeatedly subjected her to experiments involving extreme Lightning output, placing enormous stress upon her physical and nervous systems. The training was sufficiently severe that Sasha feared it might eventually kill her, while William's failure to intervene left her without the protection she expected from a father. Julia, one of the servants responsible for her care, consequently became the most important emotional figure in her upbringing. She comforted Sasha after training, helped educate her and treated her with the affection her biological parents rarely provided, eventually becoming the person Sasha regarded as her true mother.
Sasha also grew up alongside Violet Snow and Ruby Scarlett, the heiresses of two other ancient Noble Vampire families. Their friendship developed long before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] entered supernatural society, and Sasha became familiar with the Human boy Violet intended to marry through years of listening to her friend's stories. Although the three girls possessed different personalities and supernatural abilities, they shared the experience of growing up within Nightingale's aristocratic political structure and inherited expectations concerning the continuation of their respective Clans.
Clan Fulger's political position eventually deteriorated after losing its Count title to Clan Horseman. The loss weakened the family's former protection and made its heiress a more attractive target for organizations that would previously have hesitated to attack one of Nightingale's principal Count families. By the beginning of the novel, Sasha remained an exceptionally talented young Vampire but possessed little independence from her family's political circumstances. Her life changed almost immediately after Victor's transformation, when the accidental marriage ritual was followed by the destruction of her household and Julia's death.

### Synopsis

#### The Awakening
Sasha's involvement with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] began when she and Ruby agreed to assist Violet with the ritual intended to save the dying Human Victor Walker by transforming him into a Vampire. The ceremony was supposed to establish Violet as the sole Noble Vampire responsible for his transformation, but Victor's abnormal Blood caused the process to develop unpredictably. After drinking Violet's Blood, he also fed upon Ruby and Sasha, incorporating all three women into the same supernatural marital connection despite none of them having intended that outcome. Sasha initially found the situation difficult to believe, since she knew Victor mainly through Violet's stories and had barely interacted with him personally. The ritual nevertheless created a powerful connection between them, producing an intense craving for one another's Blood and establishing a relationship that could not simply be dismissed as an ordinary mistake.[7]
Before Sasha could properly understand what had happened, she returned to the Fulger residence and discovered that the household had been attacked. The servants had been murdered, and Julia's body lay among the dead. The attackers were [[Carlos Reiss]] and Maria, members of the Inquisition who had taken advantage of Clan Fulger's weakened political position to target its heiress. Maria restrained Sasha through blessed threads while Carlos prepared to capture her, but they underestimated the extent of her emotional response. Sasha forced her Lightning beyond its usual safe output, breaking free through extreme acceleration and capturing Maria before the hunters could complete their plan. Rather than kill her immediately, Sasha performed an incomplete Vampire transformation, deliberately leaving Maria in a defective state because she regarded forcing a Vampire hunter to become the creature she despised as a more fitting punishment.[17]
The attack left Sasha consumed by grief, Blood thirst and the desire to destroy everyone connected with Julia's murder. Kaguya eventually found her preparing to pursue revenge without regard for her own survival and recognized that allowing her to continue would likely result in her death. She incapacitated Sasha and brought her to Victor, whose unusual Blood could alleviate the dangerous imbalance created by the marriage ritual. Their first proper meeting immediately revealed the intensity of the supernatural attraction between them. After exchanging Blood, Victor received memories concerning Sasha's participation in his transformation and learned that the ritual had bound him to all three women unintentionally.
The encounter also allowed Sasha to explain what had happened to her. She described Julia's death, the experiments performed during her childhood and the absence of meaningful affection from her parents, eventually losing the emotional control she had struggled to maintain since returning to the destroyed mansion. Victor listened and comforted her instead of demanding that she suppress her grief, promising that she would not need to confront the Inquisition alone. He subsequently insisted that Sasha remain near him rather than isolate herself while consumed by revenge, establishing the first genuine emotional foundation of their relationship.[8]
Their developing marriage immediately caused conflict with Violet, who had expected Victor to belong exclusively to her. Sasha initially hesitated to confront her childhood friend, but Victor refused to resolve the problem by abandoning either woman. Eventually, Sasha acknowledged her feelings and asserted that Victor was her husband as well. The confrontation marked an important moment of personal independence for someone accustomed to suppressing her own desires to avoid conflict. Although Violet and Sasha remained possessive and competitive, they gradually began learning to coexist within the abnormal family created by the ritual.[18]
The three original wives were forced to cooperate during the subsequent confrontation with the Inquisition. When Victor was severely injured, Sasha wanted to retaliate immediately, but Ruby recognized that remaining on the battlefield would endanger his survival. Sasha used her extraordinary speed to assist the retreat, carrying Victor and Violet away while Ruby and Maria withdrew separately. The incident demonstrated that the women could place their husband's safety above their jealousy when circumstances required it and established the practical cooperation that would continue developing throughout their marriage.[19]

#### Vampire Society
Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s departure for [[Nightingale]] under [[Scathach]]'s instruction, Sasha began adapting to the household and confronting the limitations of her original fighting style. Her inherited Lightning provided such enormous speed that most opponents of comparable age could be defeated before they reacted, but this advantage had also reduced her need to develop refined [[Martial Arts]]. Ruby, who possessed more formal training through Scathach, recognized the problem and began helping Sasha correct it with assistance from Lacus. Their exercises emphasized movement, timing, control and the ability to fight effectively even when speed alone was insufficient. The training became the beginning of Sasha's progression from an unusually talented Noble Vampire into a more complete combatant.[20]
Clan Fulger's weakened political position remained a serious concern. [[Victoria Fulger Elderblood|Victoria Rider]] had challenged Clan Horseman in an attempt to recover the Count title previously lost by the family, placing substantial economic resources and political influence at risk. Scathach eventually questioned Victor about the consequences if old political arrangements allowed another Clan to claim authority over Sasha. Victor separated the issue of titles and wagers from the question of his wife's freedom, declaring that he would become strong enough to prevent anyone from taking her from him. For Sasha, who had spent much of her childhood treated primarily as an asset of the [[Fulger Bloodline]], his refusal to accept that her future could be decided through Clan politics was profoundly meaningful.
The most important confrontation of the period occurred when Sasha's parents arrived in Nightingale. Victor had already learned how William and Annasthashia treated her, and their behavior during the meeting only strengthened his hostility. The situation escalated when Annasthashia reacted indifferently to Julia's death, treating the woman who had effectively raised Sasha as though she were merely a replaceable servant. Victor considered the neglect and experimentation Sasha endured intolerable, particularly because both parents possessed enough power and influence to provide her with a far better life. His anger became so intense that the meeting developed into direct combat, culminating in William's death and the emergence of the affectionate Natashia personality from within Annasthashia's divided existence.[21]
Natashia's behavior contrasted sharply with the mother Sasha remembered. She openly embraced her daughter, expressed affection and attempted to repair their relationship, leaving Sasha confused by the sudden change. Victor subsequently informed Sasha that he had killed William rather than allowing her to discover the truth through someone else. Sasha acknowledged that the death affected her because William had been a persistent presence throughout her life, but she refused to blame Victor for acting out of anger over the suffering she had endured. More importantly, she recognized that someone had finally become genuinely furious because of what had happened to her, a response she had rarely experienced from the people who were supposed to protect her.[9]
The events gradually transformed Sasha's relationship with her family. Although she could not simply forget the experiments and neglect of her childhood, Natashia's attempts to care for her created the possibility of a different future. Sasha also began becoming more comfortable within Victor's household, accepting the increasingly complicated relationships connecting the original wives and recognizing that she no longer needed to face every personal crisis alone.

#### A New Pillar
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s elevation to the position of Fifth Vampire Count greatly improved Sasha's security within [[Nightingale]]. The young heiress whose weakened Clan had recently been vulnerable enough for the Inquisition to attack was now married to a newly recognized Pillar whose influence and supernatural strength were expanding rapidly. Nevertheless, the political change did not erase Sasha's past, and she eventually returned to the abandoned Fulger residence with Maria to recover a family heirloom associated with her ancestral lineage. The visit forced both women to confront the place where Julia had died and the violent circumstances that had originally connected them. Sasha understood that Maria's defective transformation had left her physically and psychologically unstable, while Maria remained aware that her continued existence was tied to the resentment of the woman who had created her.
The visit became the site of another organized Inquisition ambush. [[Father Julian]] and the other attackers intended to capture Sasha because her Bloodline remained valuable to their superiors, and they continued assuming that Clan Fulger's former loss of political status made its heiress an acceptable target. Maria fought beside Sasha while Kaguya intervened as Victor's representative, but the confrontation became critical when Kaguya was gravely wounded protecting her mistress. Victor arrived shortly afterward and retaliated with extraordinary violence, demonstrating the danger of attacking members of his family. The incident also created a serious threat to supernatural secrecy because Human authorities and surveillance systems became involved, forcing Sasha, Ruby, Violet and Natalia to help manage the aftermath.[22]
Sasha blamed herself for Kaguya's injuries because the attack had occurred during her attempt to revisit the Fulger mansion. Victor refused to condemn Kaguya for protecting her but remained angry that the maid had treated her own death as an acceptable price for carrying out her responsibilities. The experience reinforced the principle that the members of Victor's household were not expendable servants whose lives could be sacrificed without consequence, an attitude that contrasted sharply with the traditions of many supernatural organizations.
Sasha's relationship with Maria became increasingly complicated during the following months. What had begun as hatred gradually developed into familiarity as Maria continued serving the household and participating in its dangers. Victor eventually acknowledged that his own desire to retaliate against Sasha's enemies had sometimes caused him to make decisions that properly belonged to her, particularly concerning Maria's punishment. He recognized that Sasha, rather than her husband, needed to decide whether she wanted Maria killed, forgiven or retained within the family. The acknowledgment was important because Sasha's childhood had been dominated by people making decisions on her behalf, and Victor did not want his attempts to protect her to reproduce the same loss of agency.
The confrontation with Carlos eventually forced Sasha to confront the difference between revenge and genuine emotional recovery. Maria began inflicting punishment upon the surviving hunter while Sasha attempted to remain present, believing that she needed to witness his suffering because she had demanded justice for Julia. Victor recognized that Sasha was not enjoying what occurred and was instead forcing herself to become crueler than she naturally wanted to be. He stopped her and explained that kindness did not make her weak. Sasha gradually accepted that she could seek justice without allowing Julia's murder to determine every aspect of her personality, and Maria eventually became a more permanent member of the family after Victor reconstructed her defective Vampire existence.[12]
At the same time, Natashia continued attempting to restore Clan Fulger. Sasha gradually became willing to support her mother's efforts, particularly as Natashia demonstrated a genuine desire to repair their relationship and reclaim the family's political position. When Natashia challenged [[Niklaus Horseman]] for the Count title, Sasha openly wanted her to succeed. The moment demonstrated how far she had progressed since the beginning of the story: rather than associate Clan Fulger exclusively with fear and pain, she had begun regarding its future as something she could choose to support.

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Sasha continued developing independently as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction expanded beyond the structure of an ordinary Noble Vampire Clan. Natashia resumed teaching her the more advanced applications of Fulger Lightning, encouraging Sasha to refine her control and develop the practical techniques necessary to confront opponents who could survive her natural acceleration. The instruction also brought Sasha into closer contact with the historical abilities of her family, gradually revealing the martial traditions originating from Carmila. Although Sasha initially possessed considerably less combat experience than her mother, she inherited enough potential to develop techniques that many older Fulger Vampires would struggle to reproduce.
The original three wives also became increasingly coordinated. Victor's repeated absences and growing responsibilities demonstrated that constant jealousy would undermine their ability to protect him and manage the household. Violet, Ruby and Sasha remained competitive, but they gradually learned to share information, coordinate training and support one another during conflicts. Their relationship developed beyond the rivalry created by the accidental marriage ritual, becoming one of the central foundations of Victor's expanding family.
Sasha's romantic relationship with Victor continued developing during this period. Although she had been married to him since the beginning of the story, the emotional consequences of Julia's death and her troubled childhood had delayed certain aspects of their intimacy compared with Violet and Ruby. As Sasha became more secure within the household, she gradually developed the confidence necessary to express her feelings openly. Their relationship eventually became fully intimate during private time together in Japan, representing another stage in Sasha's recovery from a life in which other people had repeatedly controlled her future.
The period also strengthened Sasha's relationship with Maria and her increasingly complicated maternal family. She continued learning that forgiveness did not require pretending the past had never happened, while Natashia's growing attachment to Victor created situations that Sasha initially found deeply embarrassing. Nevertheless, her confidence increased as she began regarding her family as a collection of relationships she had chosen to maintain rather than merely the political structure into which she had been born.

#### Demonic War
The Demonic invasion of [[Nightingale]] provided Sasha with one of her first major opportunities to act independently as the defender of an entire territory. Demonic portals opened across the Vampire Kingdom while many of its strongest leaders were occupied elsewhere, leaving the great Count Clans responsible for protecting their own lands against forces that included several of Hell's most dangerous commanders. Clan Fulger was particularly vulnerable because its military organization had not completely recovered from the political and economic consequences of losing the Count title. The situation became even more dangerous when the invading forces were revealed to include Phoenix, the 37th Key of Solomon, whose Fire represented a particularly unfavorable elemental matchup for the Lightning-specialized Fulgers.[11]
Sasha prepared for the attack by equipping specially constructed armor designed to support her extreme speed and supernatural Electricity. Victoria helped her adjust the equipment, while Tatsuya joined her as one of the Clan's principal defenders. Sasha chose to employ a pair of Lightning constructs instead of ordinary daggers, generating enough concentrated Energy to give the weapons a stable physical appearance. The effort astonished Victoria and Tatsuya because maintaining such constructs would ordinarily require enormous expenditure for a Fulger Vampire, but Sasha's natural inheritance and repeated consumption of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood had already given her reserves exceeding their expectations.
As the Demons advanced, Sasha took responsibility for directing the defenders. She ordered those without sufficient combat experience to retreat into protected areas and instructed capable Adult and Elder Vampires to take up their weapons. Her authority was not based upon extensive conventional command experience, but the confidence and determination she displayed were enough to inspire the frightened citizens. Sasha then employed the ancestral [[Martial Arts]] created by Carmila, transforming her Lightning constructs into longer blades and activating the first form, Lynet. Golden Lightning spread across the territory as she traveled several kilometers in seconds, destroying enormous numbers of invading Demons before returning to the position from which she had begun. Victoria recognized the resemblance between Sasha's techniques and those of Carmila, understanding that her niece had begun reproducing abilities associated with the original Fulger ancestor.[16]
The arrival of Phoenix escalated the battle. His presence caused surrounding structures to heat and ignite, and the Demon Duke regarded the weakened [[Fulger Clan]] as an easy target until witnessing Sasha's extraordinary performance. When he questioned her identity, Sasha introduced herself as the heiress of Clan Fulger, openly accepting the name that had once represented so much suffering. Phoenix subsequently used his powers to resurrect the Demons Sasha had already destroyed, forcing the defenders to confront enemies who could return from apparent death. Sasha and Tatsuya coordinated their movements, dividing responsibility for the surrounding forces while continuing to challenge the Demon Duke. The confrontation demonstrated Sasha's growing ability to lead, adapt and fight for people beyond her immediate household.
The wider Demonic War further emphasized the importance of her connection with Victor. As the conflict expanded, his Bloodline and influence continued changing the supernatural characteristics of the women connected to him, eventually allowing Sasha to undergo another important transformation. Victor refined the Progenitor Blood present within Sasha and Natashia, strengthening their existing Bloodlines instead of simply granting unrelated new abilities. Their Lightning output increased, and Sasha's inherited resilience became considerably more pronounced. Victor tested the change through a controlled physical strike, discovering that an attack expected to send a Vampire of Sasha's age flying barely affected her body. The experiment revealed that William's Florence lineage had also been enhanced, while Sasha's perception of Time improved alongside her Lightning.[10]
The changes confirmed that Sasha's inherited abilities complemented one another more effectively than previously understood. Fulger Lightning allowed her to move at extraordinary speed, the Florence perception ability helped her follow the surrounding world while accelerating, and her enhanced physical resilience allowed her body to survive the resulting strain. The transformation consequently improved Sasha as a complete combatant rather than simply increasing the destructive output of her Electricity.
Victor's later imprisonment within Hell created another period of uncertainty for Sasha and the other wives. While he experienced an enormous subjective period of warfare and transformation, they remained outside, unable to accompany him or directly affect his circumstances. The experience reinforced Sasha's determination to become stronger and more independent, particularly as the enemies confronting Victor increasingly possessed abilities far beyond the standards of conventional Noble Vampire society.

#### King Among Kings
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s return after centuries of subjective experience within Hell coincided with the continuing expansion of his family and political influence. The household now contained powerful individuals originating from numerous supernatural [[Races (Flamel)|Races]], making it increasingly necessary to establish systems for cooperation, responsibility and the management of internal disagreements. Sasha remained part of the original leadership core alongside Violet and Ruby, possessing a personal history with Victor that predated his elevation to Progenitor and the increasing number of Divine beings associated with his faction.
Her principal frustration during this period concerned the difference between natural strength and practical experience. By approximately thirty years of age, Sasha had already reached a level that most Vampires of comparable age could never approach, but she understood that the highest supernatural opponents possessed centuries or millennia of combat experience. Her Lightning could create enormous advantages against ordinary enemies, yet exceptional opponents could survive her attacks, predict her movements or exploit weaknesses in her judgment. Sasha consequently recognized that further development required confronting difficulties beyond those available through conventional sparring.[1]
Victor created the Tower of Nightmares to provide precisely that experience. The Tower placed participants into dangerous artificial scenarios that adapted to their weaknesses, forcing them to confront opponents and situations that could not be overcome through familiar techniques alone. Participants could be killed within the training environment and subsequently restored, allowing repeated encounters with dangers that would otherwise carry unacceptable permanent consequences. Sasha, Violet and Ruby became among the first major participants and willingly accepted the experience despite understanding how painful and psychologically demanding it could become.[14]
For Sasha, the Tower was particularly valuable because it punished the habits created by her extraordinary speed. Moving faster than an opponent provided little advantage if she failed to recognize a trap, misunderstood an enemy's ability or reacted before properly evaluating the situation. Repeated lethal encounters gradually improved her judgment, combat awareness and adaptability, allowing her to develop beyond the limitations of a warrior who depended primarily upon being faster than everyone else.
The same period brought an important glimpse of Sasha's possible future. Through Violet's causal perception, a distant version of the Elderblood family was observed in which Sasha occupied the position of Supreme General, wielding extraordinary wartime authority over Imperial military forces. The vision depicted a political arrangement in which command during total war could supersede portions of the ordinary administrative hierarchy, placing Sasha among the highest authorities beneath Victor. However, the account belongs to a possible future rather than the confirmed present chronology. Its importance lies in what it reveals about the direction of Sasha's development, not in establishing a title she already possesses.[2]
The Fulger family's complicated Soul history also reached an important turning point. Victor discovered that Carmila and Natasha remained connected to the spiritual structure inherited through Natashia's lineage, resulting in several identities and generations becoming entangled within one existence. Through his supernatural authority, he separated these individuals into independent bodies, allowing Sasha to interact directly with her grandmother and the women associated with different periods of her mother's life. The change transformed Sasha's understanding of her ancestry, replacing incomplete explanations and childhood trauma with the opportunity to confront the people and circumstances responsible for the development of her family.
Although the resulting Fulger household became increasingly unconventional, Sasha gradually developed a stronger sense of belonging within it. Carmila's experience and martial achievements provided another standard for Sasha to pursue, while her interactions with Natashia and Natasha demonstrated that the history of her Bloodline could no longer be reduced to the suffering she had experienced as a child.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s development into the Dragon God of [[Chaos]] created the possibility of a transformation far beyond the limits of conventional Vampire evolution. His family was preparing for conflicts involving Gods, Pantheons, [[True Dragons]] and civilizations whose supernatural foundations exceeded those of [[Nightingale]]'s original ruling Clans. Victor consequently began offering selected members of his household the opportunity to become genuine Dragons through his Blood Dragon lineage, emphasizing that the transformation was voluntary and that those who preferred to retain their original Race would not be forced to change.
Sasha accepted alongside Violet, Ruby and numerous other members of the family. Victor initiated the reconstruction of their physical and spiritual foundations, using the supernatural characteristics of his Blood Dragon existence to create bodies suited to a much higher level of development. The process differed from an ordinary enhancement because the women's original biological limitations were replaced rather than merely reinforced. As the transformation progressed, Dragon eggs formed around their bodies, allowing the complex reconstruction to continue until their new existences were complete.[4]
When Sasha emerged, she was no longer a Noble Vampire carrying a few Draconic characteristics. She had become a genuine True Dragon and member of Victor's Blood Dragon lineage, possessing a fundamentally superior body and Soul while retaining the Lightning and other inherited characteristics of Clan Fulger. The transformation was especially significant because her original Bloodline had always been restricted by the difficulty of channeling extreme Electricity through Vampire physiology. Her new Dragon body provided a stronger foundation for continuing that development without remaining bound to the same racial limitations.
However, Sasha and the other newly transformed family members recognized that receiving a superior body did not automatically provide complete mastery of its abilities. Their previous training had been developed around Vampire physiology, while True Dragons possessed different relationships with Energy, language, transformation and supernatural authority. They consequently continued training to adapt to their new bodies and understand how Draconic power differed from the abilities they had used throughout their earlier lives.
The establishment of Dragons Nest further transformed Sasha's political position. The young heiress who had once been vulnerable because Clan Fulger lost its protection now belonged to the ruling family of an independent Divine civilization whose members included Gods, Vampires, Dragons, Demons and numerous other supernatural beings. Sasha became one of Victor's increasingly formal Empresses, participating in the creation of a political structure intended to govern a population far larger and more diverse than anything associated with the old Vampire Kingdom.
Her relationship with Victor and the original wives remained central despite these changes. Sasha's personal history with him extended back to the beginning of his supernatural existence, giving the three original wives a unique place within the expanding family. Although their powers, titles and responsibilities continued changing, they retained the emotional connection established when Victor was still a newly transformed Vampire.

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
The period following the fall of [[Asgard]] brought enormous changes through accelerated Time. Although a comparatively short interval passed externally, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and many members of his family experienced approximately two thousand subjective years, allowing their abilities, relationships and personal responsibilities to develop far beyond their earlier limits. Sasha eventually became pregnant with Victor's daughter, carrying the child for approximately one thousand subjective years before giving birth to [[Silvia Elderblood]]. The unusually prolonged pregnancy reflected the extraordinary supernatural foundation inherited through Victor's evolved Draconic lineage, while Silvia acquired characteristics associated with Sasha's Fulger ancestry.[23][6]
Motherhood became an important stage in Sasha's development. Her own childhood had demonstrated the harm caused when parents treated supernatural potential as more important than the well-being of their children, and Silvia's birth gave her the opportunity to create a different environment. Although the younger Elderblood generation possessed extraordinary inherited abilities and would eventually require demanding instruction, Sasha's experiences reinforced the importance of providing affection, stability and protection alongside training.
Her personal development also continued through the artificial world created for Victor's family. Within the Dream World, Sasha chose a path centered upon combat and adventuring rather than political organization. She eventually became the only SSS-ranked Adventurer, acquiring a reputation so extraordinary that she could travel freely without ordinary restrictions or fear of interference. Her titles included Thunder Queen, Lioness Queen and Furious Queen, but the designation she valued most was The Fastest Woman Alive, which had particular significance because of her family's tradition of competing over Lightning proficiency.[3]
Sasha used the Dream World to refine her swordsmanship and develop skills she had not yet mastered despite thousands of years of prior training. Carmila remained an important standard for her development, particularly because the original Fulger ancestor had accumulated extraordinary experience with the Sword. Although Sasha had already spent approximately two thousand subjective years studying fencing, she acknowledged that her proficiency had not reached the same level. The artificial world's restrictions consequently became an opportunity to return to fundamental techniques and gain experience without depending exclusively upon the overwhelming power of her outside existence.
The Dream World also demonstrated Sasha's restraint concerning political power. Her fame and achievements provided enough influence that she could have attempted to reclaim or overthrow the kingdom associated with her artificial identity, but she refused because its inhabitants were satisfied with their current government. Sasha recognized that a country existed for its people and that taking control merely because she possessed the necessary strength would not automatically benefit anyone. Her decision distinguished her from individuals who treated power and political authority as goals that should always be pursued regardless of the consequences.
Outside the artificial world, the Elderblood civilization continued expanding, gradually requiring more formal political and administrative institutions. Sasha increasingly participated in security and military matters, while Violet concentrated upon Imperial organization and Ruby became one of the principal scientific authorities. Their different specializations reflected the maturation of Victor's original three wives into individuals capable of contributing to the Empire through their own experience rather than relying exclusively upon their shared position within his family.

#### Those Who Rule Reality
The discovery of civilizations beyond [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Sector forced the [[Elderblood Empire]] to undertake its most ambitious developmental project. Having encountered powers capable of threatening entire Solar Systems and possessing knowledge far beyond the old Pantheons, Victor concluded that his civilization required a much longer period of preparation before attempting to confront the higher regions of the Cosmos. He consequently initiated a temporal ritual intended to provide approximately 200,000 years of internal development while roughly one external year passed, supported by Natalia's control over Time and the enormous supernatural Energy supplied by himself, Jeanne and the other Empresses.[24]
Sasha participated directly in the administration and supervision of the accelerated Empire. The ritual required its strongest participants to maintain the extraordinary expenditure necessary to accelerate entire Galactic systems, while other members of the family rotated between supporting the structure and descending through avatars to supervise the civilization's development. Sasha remained particularly attentive to Victor's condition, helping monitor the strain placed upon him as the enormous project continued. When the energy requirements proved far greater than initially expected, the family had to reconsider how responsibilities should be distributed to avoid placing unnecessary pressure upon the individuals sustaining the ritual.
The accelerated era also required the creation of institutions capable of managing a rapidly expanding population. Sasha participated in discussions concerning the Empire's economy, resource distribution, security and internal administration, recognizing that even a civilization whose rulers possessed extraordinary power could suffer instability if its systems were poorly organized. Kaguya and Nyx began developing the Empire's intelligence and counterintelligence forces, while Sasha expressed frustration that some citizens and lesser Gods continued behaving selfishly despite receiving extensive benefits. Nevertheless, she understood that the problem could not be resolved simply by assuming everyone would remain satisfied forever.[25]
The same discussions extended to the Imperial afterlife. As the population expanded, the existing systems for judging, preserving and reincarnating Souls became increasingly inadequate. Sasha helped consider how the new structure should be presented to ordinary citizens, suggesting that its name and associated mythology should emphasize rest, renewal and the possibility of a new life rather than relying exclusively upon the intimidating terminology of Hell or the [[Greek Underworld|Underworld]]. Her contribution reflected a growing appreciation for the importance of symbolism, public perception and cultural identity within a civilization governed by supernatural beings.
The Fulger family became increasingly involved in the Empire's administration during the first tens of thousands of accelerated years. Carmila, Natashia, Natasha, Victoria and Sasha were assigned different responsibilities, forcing them to cooperate despite their competitive temperaments. Sasha initially complained about being separated from Victor during important periods of the project, but she gradually accepted that the Empire could no longer depend upon its ruler personally supervising every aspect of government. The enormous scale of Imperial development required delegation, independent authority and the willingness of trusted subordinates to make decisions without waiting for Victor's immediate intervention.[26]
Sasha's own position became clearer as the accelerated civilization developed. During [[Tyche]]'s evaluation of the principal women surrounding Victor, she identified Anna with Internal Affairs, Ruby with scientific development and Sasha as the person responsible for the police of the entire Empire. The statement established Sasha's confirmed present institutional role and distinguished it from the possible-future Supreme General position previously observed through Violet's causal perception. Her responsibility concerned Imperial law enforcement and security, making her one of the senior figures entrusted with maintaining order throughout Victor's enormous civilization.[5]
The passage of accelerated Time also changed Sasha personally. The reserved young woman who had previously become embarrassed by Natashia's shameless behavior gradually adopted many of the exaggerated characteristics associated with the older Fulger women. During the controversy surrounding Violet's attempt to recruit additional wives without consulting the rest of the household, Sasha joined the other women in refusing to rescue the Empress from the objections of their daughters. She also teased Violet about her attempt to increase the representation of Clan Snow, boasting that the Fulgers possessed a decisive advantage because multiple generations of their lineage were connected to Victor. Natasha and Natashia expressed enormous pride in Sasha's increasingly uninhibited personality, regarding the change as evidence that she had finally become comfortable with her family's temperament.[15]
The incident also demonstrated Sasha's relationship with Silvia. Her daughter joined Valentina and Rosemary in protesting Violet's actions, and the three girls were recognized as a particularly troublesome group of Imperial Princesses. Their friendship reflected the enduring connection between Victor's three original wives, whose children continued forming relationships with one another across the enormous Elderblood household.
The appearance of threats connected to the higher regions of the Cosmos soon returned Sasha to serious Imperial responsibilities. Victor gathered the senior members of his family after obtaining intelligence concerning the [[Black Eclipse Pantheon]] and other external powers. Sasha was among the trusted women included in these discussions, reflecting her responsibilities within the Empire's security hierarchy and her position among the individuals expected to understand the dangers facing their civilization.
During the enormous family gathering that followed, Sasha also demonstrated practical knowledge of Dragon Language. The preparation of a Star Animal larger than [[Jupiter]] required tools capable of affecting material far beyond the durability of ordinary equipment. Sasha contributed by employing Draconic speech to assist with the creation of suitable cutting instruments, while other members of the family used Divine, Demonic and supernatural methods to prepare the enormous creature. The incident demonstrated how the extraordinary abilities of Victor's family had become integrated into activities far removed from ordinary warfare.[27]
Her linguistic knowledge became more apparent during [[Scathach]]'s confrontation with [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]]. Scathach invoked Heptagrammakos, an exceptionally complicated expression combining Draconic and Eldritch Runic principles. As the surrounding Reality became enveloped in abnormal Darkness and supernatural manifestations, Sasha examined the expression and interpreted its meaning as approximately Seven signs that begin Creation. Her ability to recognize the linguistic structure demonstrated that she had developed beyond merely using elementary Dragon Language and could analyze complicated supernatural expressions even when they were employed through unfamiliar combinations of Runic systems.[28]
Sasha also supported restrictions surrounding the expansion of Victor's Dragon Bloodline. When the possibility of granting Draconic transformations to more distant relatives was discussed, she joined Violet, Ruby, Kaguya, Anna and Scathach in insisting that access to the lineage should be earned rather than granted automatically through family connections. The position reflected their understanding that even weaker branches of the Blood Dragon lineage possessed extraordinary potential and that admitting individuals without appropriate evaluation could undermine the standards established for the Imperial family.
The birth of [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]] subsequently produced a crisis involving the System and the existing Primordial order. Moira's extraordinary relationship with Luck and Destiny attracted the attention of beings who wished to incorporate her into the established cosmic structure, but Victor refused to surrender his daughter. The resulting confrontation exposed the limits of the Empire's current ability to oppose certain [[Primordial Entities]] and forced the family to consider the possibility that even their enormous civilization remained vulnerable to powers operating at a higher level of Reality.
After the immediate crisis subsided, Sasha remained with Violet to examine its strategic implications. She considered the situation infuriating but manageable, recognizing that the Empire possessed substantial resources and could continue developing. However, she also agreed that relying upon rules supposedly followed by potential enemies would be foolish. Violet proposed creating an independent deterrent capable of threatening the entire Universe if necessary, providing an alternative to forcing Victor to remain the only individual whose powers could frighten the Primordial order. Sasha supported investigating such an option, demonstrating that her response to discovering an overwhelming enemy was to assess the situation and prepare rather than surrender to despair.[29]
As the Great Acceleration approached its intended conclusion, the Empire neared the 200,000th anniversary of its [[Imperial Calendar]]. The enormous passage of subjective Time demonstrated how thoroughly Victor's civilization had changed, although the exact age of an individual member of the ruling family cannot be determined solely from the Imperial Calendar without accounting for earlier periods of accelerated existence and personal circumstances. Victor subsequently selected Mars as the new Imperial capital, reconstructing the planet to support the requirements of an immense interstellar civilization.
During the public announcement of the new capital, Violet addressed the Imperial population while Ruby and Sasha stood beside her. The three original wives remained together at the ceremonial center of an Empire whose origins could be traced back to the abnormal Vampire transformation ritual that had first connected them to Victor. For Sasha, the moment represented the culmination of an extraordinary personal journey: the young heiress once targeted because of her family's political weakness now stood as an ancient Dragon Goddess, mother, Empress and senior security authority within one of the most powerful civilizations in their Sector.[30]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Sasha's husband, the father of their daughter Silvia and the person most closely associated with her emotional recovery. Their marriage began accidentally during the ritual intended to transform Victor into Violet's Vampire husband, but his abnormal Blood incorporated Sasha and Ruby into the same supernatural connection. Sasha initially knew Victor mainly through years of listening to Violet speak about him, making the sudden marriage difficult to accept. However, their first proper interaction occurred immediately after Julia's murder, when Sasha was overwhelmed by grief and considering revenge without regard for her own survival. Victor listened to her experiences, comforted her and refused to treat her suffering as something she should simply endure, establishing a relationship based upon emotional support rather than the political expectations governing Sasha's earlier life.[8]
Victor subsequently became furious upon learning how Sasha's parents had treated her, eventually killing William during their confrontation. Sasha did not regard the death as meaningless, but she also recognized that William had never provided the affection or protection she needed. Rather than blame Victor for defending her, she thanked him for becoming angry over her suffering, a reaction that demonstrated how little genuine protection she had received throughout her childhood. Their relationship continued developing through shared battles, training, intimacy and the expansion of Victor's family, eventually producing Silvia and surviving enormous periods of accelerated Time. Despite Victor's transformation into a Progenitor, Dragon God and Emperor, Sasha remains attached to the man who first treated her pain as something worth caring about.[9]

#### Annasthashia Elderblood Fulger
[[Annasthashia Elderblood Fulger]] is Sasha's biological mother and one of the most complicated figures in her personal history. The personality Sasha knew throughout much of her childhood was cold, demanding and responsible for dangerous experiments involving the [[Fulger Bloodline]]. Sasha therefore associated her mother's appearance with pain and fear, particularly because Annasthashia rarely demonstrated the affection Julia provided. When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s confrontation with the Fulgers caused the affectionate Natashia personality to emerge, Sasha suddenly found herself being embraced by a woman whose appearance was inseparable from her traumatic memories. The contradiction initially overwhelmed her, but she gradually accepted the possibility of developing a genuine relationship with the mother who now wished to care for her.
Their relationship continued changing as Natashia resumed training Sasha in the ancestral [[Martial Arts]] of Clan Fulger and participated in restoring the family's political standing. The later separation of the Souls and identities connected with Carmila and Natasha allowed Sasha to confront the history underlying her mother's divided existence more directly. By the Imperial Era, her relationship with Natashia had become considerably more affectionate and playful, while Sasha's increasingly uninhibited personality gave the older Fulger women reason to celebrate how thoroughly their daughter had embraced the family's temperament.

#### William Salvatore Florence
[[William Salvatore Florence]] was Sasha's biological father and the source of important characteristics within her inherited Bloodline. His family's abilities included enhanced perception of Time and physical resilience, both of which complemented the extreme acceleration provided by Clan Fulger's Lightning. Natashia had selected William partly because these traits could improve the potential of their children, but his relationship with Sasha remained emotionally distant. Although he was present throughout her childhood, he failed to protect her from the experiments that caused so much suffering, leaving Julia to provide the affection and reassurance expected of a parent.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually killed William during their confrontation in [[Nightingale]]. Sasha acknowledged that his death produced complicated emotions because he had remained part of her life for many years, but she did not consider his treatment of her worthy of the unconditional loyalty he might have expected from a daughter. She refused to blame Victor for reacting violently to the abuse she had endured, while the supernatural characteristics inherited from William continued forming an important part of her development. Victor's later refinement of Sasha's Bloodline strengthened her paternal inheritance, improving her resilience and Time perception alongside her Fulger Lightning.[10]

#### Julia
Julia was Sasha's maid and the woman she regarded as her real mother. Throughout Sasha's childhood, Julia provided affection, education and emotional support following the dangerous training imposed by her biological parents. Their relationship became the principal source of stability in Sasha's early life, making Julia's murder one of the most devastating events she experienced. The loss drove Sasha into a dangerous emotional state and contributed directly to her decision to transform Maria into a defective Vampire as punishment for participating in the attack.
Julia's importance did not disappear when Sasha gradually reconciled with Natashia. Instead, the affection Julia had provided became one of the standards through which Sasha understood what genuine maternal care should involve. Her later development as Silvia's mother consequently carries particular significance, since Sasha had personally experienced the difference between adults who valued a child's supernatural potential and someone who genuinely cared for the person possessing it.

#### Maria Elderblood
[[Maria Elderblood]] originally entered Sasha's life as an Inquisition hunter involved in the ambush that killed Julia. Sasha retaliated by forcibly transforming her into a defective Vampire, intending the punishment to force Maria to endure the condition she had spent her life fighting against. Their relationship initially involved hatred, domination and resentment, but prolonged contact gradually changed its nature. Maria became involved in the dangers facing [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household and remained present during a period when Sasha was learning to recover from the trauma surrounding Julia's death.
Sasha eventually recognized that she no longer wanted Maria to disappear. Although the events surrounding their first meeting remained part of their history, she began regarding the former hunter as an individual capable of becoming part of her life rather than merely a living punishment. Victor subsequently stabilized Maria's supernatural existence through his Blood, allowing her to develop into a more complete member of the family. Their relationship demonstrates Sasha's capacity for forgiveness without requiring the erasure of the suffering that originally brought them into conflict.

#### Violet Elderblood Snow
[[Violet Elderblood Snow]] is Sasha's childhood friend, fellow original wife and one of the individuals most consistently present throughout her life. Their relationship became strained when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s transformation ritual unexpectedly married him to Sasha and Ruby as well, since Violet had intended to become his only wife. Sasha initially hesitated to confront her friend, but Victor insisted that neither woman should be required to abandon her feelings simply because the situation had become complicated. Sasha eventually asserted that Victor was her husband too, establishing a rivalry that would continue developing throughout their marriage.[18]
Their jealousy gradually became less destructive as they confronted increasingly dangerous opponents and recognized the importance of cooperation. Sasha and Violet eventually developed into senior Empresses with different areas of responsibility, frequently coordinating during matters involving the security of Victor's family and Empire. Their discussion following the Primordial crisis surrounding Moira demonstrated the maturity of their relationship, as both women focused upon preparing an independent strategic deterrent rather than allowing personal rivalry to distract them from the danger.

#### Ruby Elderblood Scarlett
[[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] is Sasha's childhood friend and the third member of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s original marriage ritual. Ruby's comparatively analytical personality often provides a contrast to Sasha's emotional intensity and Violet's impulsiveness, but the three women share a relationship extending back long before Victor became a Vampire. Ruby also played an important role in Sasha's early martial development, helping her recognize that extreme Lightning speed could not replace proper technique and combat judgment. Their training gradually strengthened Sasha's foundations and contributed to her ability to confront enemies capable of surviving her natural acceleration.[20]
The two subsequently underwent enormous periods of development alongside Violet, including participation in the Tower of Nightmares, transformation into [[True Dragons]] and experiences within the Dream World. Their chosen paths eventually reflected their different personalities: Ruby became one of the Empire's principal scientific authorities, while Sasha developed toward security, law enforcement and military responsibilities. Despite the enormous expansion of Victor's family, Sasha and Ruby remain closely connected through their shared history and position among his original wives.

#### Carmila Fulger Elderblood
[[Carmila Fulger Elderblood]] is Sasha's maternal grandmother, the original source of the Fulger Lightning Bloodline and one of the most important figures in her martial development. Carmila had originally been a Lightning Spirit before becoming a Vampire, and the resulting transformation established the supernatural characteristics inherited by subsequent generations. She also developed the ancestral [[Martial Arts]] of Clan Fulger, which Sasha eventually learned through Natashia and demonstrated during the Demonic invasion of [[Nightingale]].[16]
For much of Sasha's life, Carmila's existence remained entangled with the complicated Soul structure inherited through her maternal line. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually separated her into an independent body, allowing Sasha to interact directly with her grandmother rather than knowing her only through the history of Clan Fulger. Carmila's exceptional swordsmanship subsequently became another standard for Sasha to pursue, particularly during the Dream World period when Sasha devoted additional years to improving her fencing. Their relationship reflects the gradual transformation of Sasha's feelings toward her ancestry from fear and resentment into admiration, rivalry and a desire to surpass the accomplishments of earlier generations.

#### Natasha Elderblood Fulger
[[Natasha Elderblood Fulger]] is the independent existence associated with the colder maternal personality that dominated much of Sasha's childhood. Her earlier behavior had subjected Sasha to dangerous training and emotional neglect, leaving an enduring mark upon the heiress's development. After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] separated the intertwined Souls and identities within the Fulger lineage, Natasha became capable of interacting with Sasha independently rather than remaining part of the divided existence previously associated with Natashia.
Their later relationship developed in a considerably different direction from its origins. Natasha became one of the older Fulger women who encouraged Sasha's increasingly shameless and competitive behavior, expressing particular pride when the formerly reserved young woman began teasing Violet and openly embracing the family's eccentric temperament. Although their shared history remained complicated, the circumstances of the Elderblood household allowed them to develop a relationship beyond the abuse that had originally defined Sasha's memories.[15]

#### Victoria Fulger Elderblood
[[Victoria Fulger Elderblood]] is Sasha's maternal aunt and an important member of Clan Fulger's political and military history. She participated in attempts to restore the family's former position within [[Nightingale]] and remained familiar with the ancestral abilities developed through Carmila's Bloodline. During the Demonic War, Victoria helped Sasha prepare specialized armor suited to her Lightning and extreme speed, ensuring that the equipment could support her movements without exposing her to unnecessary physical strain. She subsequently observed Sasha employ Carmila's ancestral [[Martial Arts]] and recognized the remarkable degree to which her niece's development resembled that of the original Fulger ancestor.[11]

#### Tatsuya Rider
[[Tatsuya Rider]] is Sasha's cousin and another talented combatant associated with the Fulger lineage. #### Silvia Elderblood

### Role in the Elderblood Empire
Sasha occupies an important position within the [[Elderblood Empire]] through her status as one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s three original wives, her accumulated combat experience and her responsibilities involving Imperial security. Her authority predates the formal creation of the Empire, originating from the earliest family structure surrounding Victor and developing through the conflicts that transformed his faction from a small Vampire household into an independent Divine civilization. As the Empire expanded, the responsibilities of its senior women became increasingly specialized, with Violet concentrating upon political organization, Ruby upon scientific development and Sasha upon matters more closely associated with security and military affairs.
By Chapter 1155, Sasha was explicitly identified as the person in charge of the police of the entire Empire. The statement establishes an institutional responsibility distinct from ordinary household seniority, placing her among the principal authorities associated with maintaining order throughout Victor's territories. Although the novel does not provide a complete administrative chart of every police organization operating beneath her, her position is consistent with the practical experience she developed through military conflicts, the Tower of Nightmares and independent adventuring.[5]
Her position should not be confused with the Supreme General depicted in Violet's vision of a possible future. That future describes Sasha possessing extraordinary wartime command authority within a centralized military hierarchy, but the vision is not guaranteed to occur exactly as witnessed. The confirmed present Sasha is an Empress responsible for Imperial policing and security, while the Supreme General remains a separate possible-future office rather than her established current title.[2]
The Great Acceleration further expanded Sasha's responsibilities by forcing the Imperial family to develop administrative systems capable of supporting enormous populations and increasingly complicated supernatural institutions. Sasha participated in discussions concerning resource distribution, the reincarnation system, intelligence operations and the dangers posed by discontented elements within the Empire. Her involvement demonstrated that she had developed beyond the mentality of a warrior whose only responsibility was defeating the next enemy and had begun considering the stability of the civilization she helped govern.
Sasha also became involved in strategic preparation against external threats. She participated in discussions concerning the [[Black Eclipse Pantheon]] and higher-order supernatural powers, supported restrictions governing access to Victor's Draconic Bloodline and joined Violet in considering independent deterrence after the confrontation involving Moira and the Primordial order. These activities reflect the direction of her mature development: Sasha remains an exceptionally powerful combatant, but her responsibilities increasingly involve protecting the Empire through planning, institutions and the coordinated application of resources rather than relying exclusively upon her personal strength.

### Abilities

#### Powers & Abilities
Sasha's current abilities are the result of several supernatural systems developing into one unified existence. She was born as a Pure-Blooded Noble Vampire carrying the [[Fulger Clan]]'s Lightning Spirit-derived Bloodline and unusual physical characteristics inherited through her father, was later incorporated more deeply into [[Victor Elderblood]]'s Progenitor lineage, and finally underwent complete racial reconstruction into a Blood Dragon, True Dragon and Dragon Goddess. Victor's Dragonization did not erase the powers Sasha had already developed; her Lightning, hereditary perception, Fulger [[Martial Arts]] and other compatible traits were incorporated into a vastly superior Draconic foundation.[1][2][3][4]

##### Blood Dragon / True Dragon Physiology
- Blood Dragon Physiology: Sasha was reconstructed through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood Dragon lineage into a complete Blood Dragon possessing genuine True Dragon biology rather than an incomplete Dragonoid body.[2][3][4]
- True Dragon Body: Sasha possesses the complete biological foundation of a True Dragon. Her former Vampire body was fundamentally reconstructed rather than simply receiving superficial Draconic characteristics.[2][3]
- True Dragon Soul: Dragonization affected Sasha spiritually as well as physically, giving her the Soul foundation necessary for complete True Dragon existence.[2][5]
- True Dragon Form: Sasha possesses a genuine full Dragon Form in addition to the Humanoid form she normally uses.[4][5]
- Draconic Shapeshifting: Sasha can maintain a Humanoid body despite possessing a complete Draconic existence, while compatible characteristics inherited from her previous Race remain integrated into the new body.[2][4]
- Flight: Sasha's complete Draconic form possesses the natural aerial mobility of a True Dragon.[4]
- Natural Draconic Weaponry: A manifested True Dragon body possesses natural weapons such as reinforced claws, fangs, horns, wings, scales and other Draconic structures. Sasha has not yet received a detailed individual breakdown of every natural weapon belonging to her Dragon Form.[4]
- Gigantic Draconic Physiology: Sasha's full Dragon existence is not restricted to the dimensions of her Humanoid body. True Dragon forms possess the enormous scale and physical architecture required to contain vastly greater quantities of Energy than an ordinary humanoid body.[2][4]

###### Dragon Heart and Draconic Energy
- [[Dragon Heart]]: Sasha possesses a genuine Dragon Heart, the specialized supernatural organ supporting the enormous Energy circulation and generation required by True Dragon physiology.[6][2]
- Immense Energy Capacity: Sasha can safely contain quantities of supernatural Energy vastly exceeding the capacity of her former Noble Vampire body.[6][2]
- Continuous Energy Generation: The Dragon Heart functions as an extraordinarily efficient internal Energy source rather than leaving Sasha dependent entirely upon previously accumulated reserves.[6][2]
- Enhanced Energy Circulation: Her Draconic veins, organs and body are naturally capable of circulating supernatural output that would severely damage ordinary biological vessels.[2]
- Energy Storage: Sasha's True Dragon body functions as an enormous supernatural Energy reservoir, allowing Energy to be stored throughout a physiology specifically constructed to withstand Draconic output.[2]
- [[Draconic Energy]]: Sasha possesses the supernatural Energy naturally generated and manipulated by [[True Dragons]] in addition to the elemental characteristics preserved from her previous existence.[2]
- Innate Energy Manipulation: True Dragons do not require Runes or conventional spells for every elementary act of Energy control. Sasha can command supernatural Energy directly because such interaction is a biological characteristic of her Race.[7]
- Energy Reinforcement: Sasha can reinforce her body, movement and attacks with supernatural Energy, now through a foundation vastly stronger than the Vampire body in which she originally learned to fight.[2][4]
- High Energy Compatibility: True Dragons possess extraordinary compatibility with supernatural Energies and phenomena belonging to Creation. This allows Sasha to accommodate and develop multiple compatible systems within the same body.[2][7]
Sasha's compatibility does not grant the near-universal Energy domination possessed by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] as the Blood Dragon Primogenitor.

###### Possessive Intuition
- Possessive Intuition / "Yandere-Sense": [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wives jokingly describe a recurring instinct in which they feel a supernatural "tingling" when Victor becomes involved with another woman. Sasha belongs to the family group demonstrating this shared possessive reaction.[10]
"Yandere-Sense" is a humorous family name rather than a formally identified Divinity or standardized supernatural discipline.

##### Vampiric Charm
- Vampiric Charm: Sasha possessed the mental influence characteristic of Noble Vampires and demonstrated its practical use before becoming a Dragon.[29]
- Mental Compulsion: Sasha can exert supernatural influence over a susceptible target's mind.[29]
- Interrogation: She used Charm on a Human commander during the aftermath of the Fulger mansion attack and extracted information regarding General James' interference in the Human operation.[29]
- Command Influence: The power can facilitate obedience from significantly weaker or vulnerable minds.[29]
Blood Dragon evolution preserves compatible Vampiric characteristics, but Sasha has not demonstrated the current upper limit of her Charm against modern Divine, Draconic or cosmic opponents.

##### Self-Destructive Lightning Acceleration (Former Technique)
- Self-Destructive Lightning Acceleration: During the Inquisition attack that killed the Fulger household, Sasha forced her Lightning and physical body beyond safe limits to escape captivity.[19]
- Extreme Burst Movement: Sasha reached sufficient speed to escape while carrying Maria with her.[19]
- Overclocking: She forced electrical output through her nervous and muscular systems far beyond what her young Vampire body could safely tolerate.[19]
- Self-Damage: The technique damaged Sasha herself and was an emergency measure rather than a sustainable combat state.[19]
The biological limitation responsible for this early technique's danger has been superseded by Sasha's later Bloodline evolution and True Dragon physiology.

- Combat Adaptation: Sasha adjusts tactics after recognizing when speed or theoretical advantage alone is insufficient against a more experienced opponent.[20]
- Combat Coordination: Sasha coordinated attacks with [[Tatsuya Rider]] and [[Hecate Moriarty]], timing her movement around allied suppression and charged attacks during the Demonic invasion.[20]
- Large-Scale Battlefield Combat: Sasha is exceptionally effective against dispersed armies because her speed and area Lightning allow her to engage numerous separated targets during one continuous sequence.[18]

- Body Language Reading: Carmila taught Sasha to analyze physical behavior as part of the education expected of the future Fulger leader.[30]
- Behavioral Analysis: Sasha can interpret posture, movement and involuntary physical reactions to estimate another person's emotional state or intentions.[30]
- Political Application: Carmila specifically considered this useful for negotiating with supernatural individuals capable of lying or concealing their intentions.[30]
- Draconic Linguistics: Sasha can analyze the linguistic roots and implied meaning of advanced Draconic expressions.[14]
- Eldritch-Draconic Expression Analysis: Her interpretation of Heptagrammakos shows that she can reason through language partially incorporating Eldritch principles without assuming every unfamiliar expression is incomprehensible.[14]
- Divinity Monitoring: Sasha can periodically inspect the Divine development of her children.[16]
- Intelligence Analysis: Sasha can rapidly compare new intelligence against existing Imperial information and draw strategic connections, as demonstrated during the Black Eclipse briefing.[11]

##### Leadership and Military Skills
- Battlefield Command: During the Demonic invasion Sasha assumed direct responsibility for Clan Fulger's defenders, ordered weaker Vampires into shelter and mobilized combat-capable Clan members against the invasion.[18]
- Leadership: Sasha's decisiveness, personal strength and position as Fulger heir allowed her to rally frightened Clan members during a crisis.[18]
- Rapid Threat Assessment: Sasha naturally evaluates immediate danger and available responses rather than freezing when confronted with an unknown enemy.[18][23]
- Strategic Threat Assessment: Following the confrontation involving Moira and the Primordial order, Sasha evaluates the Empire's position alongside Violet and agrees that depending upon rules established by potential enemies is strategically unacceptable.[23]
- Deterrence Planning: Sasha supports Violet's conclusion that the Empire requires an independent weapon capable of threatening enemies on a Universal scale so [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] is not forced to remain the civilization's only final deterrent.[23]

- Law-Enforcement Command: Sasha therefore possesses senior command responsibility over Imperial policing and internal law-enforcement structures.[31]
- Civilizational Security Experience: Her responsibilities operate across a Galactic civilization rather than one Vampire territory.[31]
- Military Development: Sasha's long-term path increasingly centers upon warfare and command. A possible future witnessed through Violet's perception identifies Sasha as The Supreme General, possessing extraordinary wartime authority over Victor's military.[32]
The Supreme General is a possible future position rather than Sasha's confirmed current office.

##### Clan and Imperial Knowledge
- [[Fulger Clan]] Knowledge: As Clan heiress, Sasha possesses extensive knowledge concerning Fulger history, territory, ancestral techniques, traditions and Bloodline characteristics.[17][18]
- Bloodline Knowledge: Her direct training under Natashia and Carmila gives Sasha substantial practical understanding of the family's Lightning Spirit ancestry.[17][18]
- Dragon Lineage Knowledge: By the current era Sasha understands the hierarchy and biological differences among [[True Dragons]], Dragons and Lesser Dragons and participates in determining who may enter [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s restricted lineage.[16]
- Imperial Security Knowledge: Sasha's police responsibilities require familiarity with the law-enforcement and internal-security structures of the [[Elderblood Empire]].[31]

#### Former Powers

##### Noble Vampire Physiology
- Noble Vampire Physiology: Before Dragonization Sasha was a Pure-Blooded Noble Vampire possessing the physical, sensory and supernatural characteristics of [[Nightingale]]'s highest hereditary Vampire class.[19]
- Enhanced Vampire Strength: Sasha possessed physical strength far beyond Human limits.
- Enhanced Vampire Speed: Even without advanced Lightning techniques, her body was naturally superhuman.
- Enhanced Vampire Durability: Sasha could survive damage impossible for ordinary Humans.
- Enhanced Vampire Senses: Her senses were substantially superior to Human perception.
- Vampiric Regeneration: Sasha could recover from otherwise lethal physical injury. After Kaguya cut her throat to incapacitate her, her Vampire body regenerated the damage.[19][26]
- Vampire Longevity: Sasha possessed the extended lifespan of a Noble Vampire before gaining the much greater longevity of a True Dragon Goddess.
- Blood Dependency: Sasha formerly required Blood as part of her Vampire existence, with the abnormal marriage ritual creating a particularly intense craving for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood.[19][25]
- Blood Empowerment: Repeated consumption of Victor's exceptional Blood strengthened Sasha and accelerated development of her existing powers.[20]
- Vampire Transformation: Sasha possessed the racial ability to turn Humans through her bite and Vampiric Blood.[19]
- Defective Transformation / Ghoul Creation: Sasha deliberately transformed [[Maria Elderblood]] incorrectly after Julia's murder, creating an unstable Vampire/Ghoul rather than a normal healthy Vampire.[19]
- Servant Connection: Maria's defective state left her connected to Sasha until Victor later stabilized and elevated her existence with his own Blood.[33]

##### Paternal Super-Vampire Traits
Sasha's father possessed an unusual Vampire Bloodline selected specifically because its physical characteristics could help a child survive the destructive power inherited from Clan Fulger.[17][1]
- Enhanced Durability: Sasha's body was unusually durable even by Noble Vampire standards.[17][1]
- Enhanced Endurance: Her paternal heritage allowed her to endure considerably more physical strain than the traditional Fulger body.[17]
- Enhanced Stamina: Sasha possessed an unusually high capacity for sustained physical exertion.[1]
- Lightning Backlash Resistance: Her body could withstand significantly more of the Fulger element before suffering the damage historically produced by forcing Lightning Spirit power through Vampire flesh.[17]
These characteristics are no longer a separate current racial physiology because [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later transformations incorporated them into Sasha's superior Blood Dragon body.

##### Vampire Count Form
- [[Vampire Count Form]]: Sasha awakened the transformation associated with Count-class Vampires at an extraordinarily young age and used it to amplify her Lightning and physical abilities.[24][20]
- Power Amplification: Count Form substantially increased Sasha's supernatural output.[24]
- Speed Amplification: Her already exceptional Lightning-enhanced movement became considerably faster.[20]
- Lightning Wings: Sasha manifested enormous bat-like wings surrounded by or constructed through Lightning.[24]
- Energy Discharge: Her transformed wings assisted in expressing the enormous electrical output generated during advanced Fulger techniques.[24]
- Enhanced Time Difference Application: Greater acceleration increased the importance of Sasha's inherited temporal perception.[20]
- One Million Hits Compatibility: At her former level Sasha required Count amplification to reproduce the million-hit technique.[24][20]
Count Form belongs to Sasha's former Vampire evolutionary path and has been superseded by her complete True Dragon physiology.

#### Resistances & Adaptations
- Conventional Vampire Weakness Negation: Sasha's current existence is fundamentally Draconic rather than an ordinary Noble Vampire body, meaning weaknesses that depend specifically upon normal Vampire physiology can no longer be assumed to function against her in the same manner.[34][2]
- Sunlight Resistance / Immunity: Sunlight is no longer a standard racial weakness of Sasha's Blood Dragon body.[34]
- Resistance to Anti-Vampire Weapons: Weapons designed solely to exploit conventional Vampire physiology lose much of their specialized effectiveness against a True Dragon body.[34]
This does not grant immunity to all Holy, Divine or Faith-based attacks regardless of power.
- Extreme Lightning Tolerance: Sasha's physiology can safely contain and circulate enormous quantities of her supernatural Lightning.[17][4]
- Electrical Backlash Resistance: Her paternal Bloodline already gave exceptional resistance to self-inflicted Fulger strain before her Dragon evolution.[17]
- Draconic Lightning Compatibility: Dragonization replaced the remaining incompatibility between a Lightning Spirit-derived power and Vampire flesh with a body capable of expressing the element natively.[4]
- Plasma Self-Tolerance: Sasha can generate and manipulate her own Plasma-state Lightning without immediately destroying her body.[22]
- Extreme Energy Tolerance: Her [[Dragon Heart]] and True Dragon body can safely circulate quantities of Energy that would overwhelm conventional supernatural beings.[2]
- Extreme Physical Trauma Resistance: True Dragon durability and regeneration provide extraordinary resistance to bodily destruction.[2][4]
- Environmental Resilience: Sasha's current physiology is capable of operating under environmental conditions far beyond those survivable by conventional biological species.[2]
- Limited Reactive Biological Adaptation: True Dragon biology attempts to adapt automatically to unfamiliar supernatural conditions, although extremely powerful effects can exceed the speed or effectiveness of this adaptation.[14]
- Mental Resilience (High but Not Absolute): Sasha's ancient Draconic mind and enormous experience provide exceptional mental stability, but she is not immune to sufficiently powerful mental or Eldritch phenomena. Merely witnessing Heptagrammakos affected the mental stability of observers until Violet used Order to ease the effect.[14]
- Residual Anti-Dragon Vulnerability: Sasha remains fundamentally Draconic. Effects specifically designed to exploit Dragons can therefore remain relevant even though her old Vampire weaknesses have been superseded.[34]

#### Limitations and Unconfirmed Abilities
- Personal Divinity Unrevealed: Sasha is a confirmed True Dragon Goddess, but the reviewed canon has not securely named an individual personal Divinity or Concept belonging specifically to her. Lightning, Speed, War, Police, Battle and similar ideas should not be indexed as Sasha's Divinity without an explicit statement.
- Dragon Breath Properties Unrevealed: Sasha possesses Dragon Breath as part of complete Blood Dragon physiology, but its individual element and secondary effects remain unconfirmed.[4]
- Not a Confirmed Runemaster: Sasha understands Draconic Language and Draconic Runic principles and can analyze highly advanced expressions, but she has not been established as a specialist Runemaster comparable to Anna or [[Scathach]].[14][7]
- Grandmaster Status Unconfirmed: Chapter 932 places Sasha at the limit of [[Poseidon|the Master]] level and states that she still needs the necessary enlightenment to enter Grandmaster.[22]
- Static Field Current Range Unknown: The 100-meter and 500-meter figures belong to Sasha's former Vampire-era state and should not be presented as the maximum range of her current True Dragon Goddess version.[8]
- Golden-Eyed Perception Unnamed: Sasha's golden-eyed Lightning manifestation in Chapter 1177 is real, but its exact classification has not been explained.[23]
- Living Lightning Has Undefined Limits: Becoming a Lightning-like elemental existence does not automatically prove unrestricted intangibility, unrestricted transformation into electromagnetic radiation, or a specific numerical movement speed.[4]
- Racial Adaptation Is Not [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Adaptation: Sasha possesses the natural adaptability of a True Dragon body, but there is no evidence that she inherited Victor's later acquired ability to continuously evolve complete counters to attacks or resurrect with new resistances.[14]
- No Blanket Conceptual Immunity: Being a Dragon Goddess does not automatically make Sasha immune to Soul, Mind, Space, Time, Reality, Conceptual, Eldritch or other high-order supernatural manipulation. Such resistances should be listed only when supported by direct feats.
- No Universal Energy Domination: Sasha's innate True Dragon Energy manipulation should not be confused with the vastly broader Energy Authority belonging to Victor as the Blood Dragon Primogenitor.[7]
- Possible Supreme General Is Not a Power: The Supreme General represents Sasha's potential future military office and command authority, not a supernatural ability automatically possessed by her current self.[32]
