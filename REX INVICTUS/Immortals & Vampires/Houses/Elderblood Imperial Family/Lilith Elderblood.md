(Lore adapted from *My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires*.)

Lilith Elderblood, formerly known as Lilith Alucard and originally simply as Lilith, is the ancient First Woman, former Human Progenitor, Demon Progenitor, Mother of Demons, and an Ascendant Goddess who once ruled a major faction of [[Biblical Hell]]. She is one of the oldest figures connected to the [[Biblical Pantheon]], a former wife of Adam and [[Lucifer]] Morningstar, and later became one of [[Victor Elderblood]]'s wives. She is the mother of [[Lily Elderblood]] and [[Liriel Elderblood]], the latter inheriting the extraordinary characteristics of both a Demon Progenitor and a Blood Dragon.[1][8]
Originally created by the [[Heavenly Father]] during the age of Genesis, Lilith belonged to the earliest generation of Humanity and was among its original Progenitors. After the events surrounding Genesis and her banishment, she eventually became a Demon, establishing an entirely different existence from the one intended by her creator. Her transformation culminated in her position as the Mother of Demons and a Goddess associated with the Divine Concepts of Beginning, Rottenness, Corruption and Despair. Unlike [[Diablo]], whose existence originated from the sins of Humanity, Lilith retained a remnant of her original Human Soul even after becoming a Demon Goddess.[9][2]
As the origin of Demonkind, Lilith occupies a position fundamentally different from an ordinary Demon or even a conventional Demon King. Her authority over the race arises from her existence as its Progenitor rather than from a throne, military organization or political appointment. This relationship enables her to create Demons directly and impose her authority upon Demons whose wills are insufficient to resist her. Her status as the Mother of Demons therefore remains independent of the political changes that have repeatedly transformed Hell and its government.[2]
Throughout the ancient history of Hell, Lilith was one of the realm's greatest powers, commanding her own faction and possessing sufficient strength to confront Diablo personally. Her military forces were recognized for their loyalty, while her influence over Succubi and other Demons extended beyond ordinary political authority. Following the destruction of her army, she was captured by Diablo and enslaved through a magical ritual that suppressed her free will. Her existence as one of the most powerful Demonic Goddesses consequently became a weapon used by her greatest enemy during the events leading to the Demonic War.[10][11]
Lilith eventually regained her freedom through Victor's intervention and the cooperation of her daughter and several Demonic allies. Her liberation restored one of Hell's oldest independent powers and allowed her to confront Diablo using her full strength, including her God-Slaying sword Genesis, True Demon Form and Divine authorities. Although Victor ultimately killed Diablo himself, Lilith accepted his subsequent invitation to witness the civilization he had created in Hell.[12][13]
Under Victor's rule, Lilith gradually abandoned her former position as a factional monarch and became one of the most recognizable public figures in New Hell. She developed an unexpectedly successful career as a singer and idol, later accepted the responsibility of its Third Ruler, and eventually relinquished that administrative title when Victor reorganized the Soul-management system. Her integration into the Elderblood family also brought a major change to her personal life: after millennia dominated by conflict, betrayal, enslavement and political struggle, she gained a household in which she could pursue her own interests while remaining the Mother Goddess of Demonkind.[7][5][6]

### Physical Appearance
Lilith is an exceptionally tall, voluptuous and strikingly beautiful woman standing approximately 200 centimeters in height. Her distinctive Demonic characteristics consist of two prominent horns, a long pointed tail and two exceptionally large wings extending from the region of her hips rather than the upper back. Her natural appearance includes long, night-black hair, fair skin and golden eyes, as seen in the ancient recordings and memories associated with [[Morgana Elderblood]]. Her features bear a strong resemblance to those of other powerful Succubi, although her overwhelming supernatural presence distinguishes her as their original Progenitor.[14][10]
During her enslavement under [[Diablo]], Lilith's appearance changed substantially. Her hair became white, her complexion turned unnaturally pale and her eyes appeared blood-red. She generally wore a black dress with a divided skirt that allowed unrestricted movement, while her expression remained empty and indifferent. Although her body retained its extraordinary beauty and Demonic characteristics, the absence of her former vitality reflected the suppression of her personality and free will. Once [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] destroyed the magic binding her to Diablo, her black hair and healthier appearance returned, accompanied by the expression and overwhelming hostility characteristic of her true self.[1][12]

#### True Demon Form
Lilith possesses a powerful True Demon Form that expresses the full strength of her existence as the Demon Progenitor and an Ascendant Goddess. Her Demonic wings become longer and more imposing, her horns grow sharper, and her tail becomes covered by exceptionally dense Miasma. The corrupt substance spreads across her body, surrounding her in a concentrated manifestation of infernal power. This transformation substantially increases her combat capabilities and complements her ability to use multiple Divine Concepts simultaneously, allowing her to fight powerful Demonic existences such as [[Diablo]] without relying exclusively upon her racial authority.[2]

### Personality
Lilith is intensely proud, fiercely independent, hedonistic, provocative and unwilling to accept restrictions upon her freedom. Having existed since the earliest age of Humanity, she has accumulated an extraordinary range of experiences, but neither age nor Divinity has diminished her impulsiveness or tendency to pursue whatever attracts her interest. Her rejection of imposed authority is one of the defining characteristics of her identity, making the centuries she spent deprived of her free will particularly devastating. She strongly resents being treated as another person's possession, and even after accepting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s position as the King of Hell, she initially resisted every attempt to involve her in the responsibilities of government.[5]
Her independence is accompanied by considerable pride in her status as the Mother of Demons. Lilith is fully aware of the significance of being the origin of an entire race and possesses little patience for those who underestimate her. Her attitude toward [[Diablo]] is especially aggressive, combining deeply personal hatred with an absolute refusal to acknowledge his supposed superiority. Even after enduring enslavement, she confronts him without hesitation once her will is restored, repeatedly insulting him and attacking with the full intention of inflicting the suffering he had caused her.[12][15]
Despite her reputation, Lilith is not defined exclusively by the destructive instincts commonly associated with Demons. Her Soul originated as Human, and the remnants of that existence continued to influence her personality even after she became a Demon Goddess. Whereas Diablo emerged from the sins of Humanity and possessed an existence fundamentally rooted in Demonic Negativity, Lilith retained the capacity for emotions and attachments not solely governed by cruelty or corruption. This difference explains why she is capable of genuine affection and loyalty despite her often ruthless behavior. Her complex personality existed long before her connection with Victor, although centuries of conflict caused many of her more positive characteristics to become increasingly obscured.[9]
Lilith nevertheless possesses a genuinely cruel and pragmatic side. The system through which she acquired powerful followers in ancient Hell included deadly trials that forced aspiring servants to survive overwhelming danger before receiving her attention. Her treatment of the young Succubus who would later become Morgana demonstrates that Lilith valued survival, perseverance and personal strength far more than conventional compassion. She could transform hardship into a method of identifying exceptional soldiers, and her expectations of those who served her were extraordinarily demanding.[14]
Outside warfare, Lilith frequently behaves irresponsibly, particularly when money, alcohol or entertainment are involved. Her enjoyment of luxurious goods and gambling eventually produced enormous financial liabilities, while her tendency to become destructive when intoxicated created additional expenses for Hell's administration. Her accumulated debts exceeded three billion Knull, including more than 1.3 billion in direct spending. Although she disliked the responsibilities attached to political office, she eventually accepted the position of Third Ruler after Victor confronted her with the consequences of her lifestyle. This incident also demonstrates that Lilith is capable of recognizing when resistance would be pointless, even if she remains deeply irritated by the situation.[5]
The modern infernal civilization created by Victor brought out another side of Lilith's personality: curiosity and adaptability. The discovery of organized cities, advanced technology, regulated commerce and ordinary Demons enjoying peaceful lives initially left her astonished. She was particularly surprised that her divine status did not exempt her from laws applicable to other inhabitants, yet she continued exploring rather than rejecting the new order. Her fascination with food, commercial products, weapons and entertainment gradually replaced the expectation that everything in Hell revolved around domination and conflict.[16][17]
Lilith's hatred toward Heaven and its Angels remains deeply rooted in her past. Her banishment, the conflicts of Genesis and her experiences with the Biblical order produced grievances that were not erased merely because Victor established diplomatic relations with the [[Heavenly Father]]. Nevertheless, she eventually ceased allowing that resentment to determine the entirety of her life. Her decision to express much of her hostility through performances and music, rather than directly reigniting the ancient conflict, illustrates the distinction between retaining a grievance and remaining consumed by it.[7][18]
Her relationship with Victor gradually allowed her original personality to re-emerge. Instead of remaining confined to the roles of Demon Queen, Progenitor, former prisoner or political enemy, Lilith gained the freedom to pursue entertainment, personal pleasure and family life. [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] eventually regarded her as an example of someone who had recovered a more authentic version of herself after becoming part of the Elderblood household. The change did not remove Lilith's selfishness, pride or dangerous temperament, but it provided an environment in which those qualities no longer had to define her entire existence.[19]
Lilith also demonstrates considerable affection as a mother. Although she is neither excessively gentle nor inclined to shelter her daughters from hardship, she takes their development seriously and expects them to become capable individuals rather than relying exclusively upon their ancestry. Her instruction of Liriel combines brutal practical combat with warnings against arrogance, overconfidence and unnecessary isolation. Lilith is particularly insistent that even an extraordinarily powerful descendant of two Progenitors should never hesitate to seek assistance from family. Her pride in Liriel's progress is unmistakable, although she frequently finds herself exasperated by the girl's inherited obsession with Victor and overwhelming determination to become stronger.[8]

### Synopsis

#### A New Pillar
Lilith's existence belongs to the ancient supernatural history surrounding the [[Biblical Pantheon]], long before the emergence of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] Alucard as the Second Vampire Progenitor. Her name was already associated with Hell's supreme powers and the conflicts that had shaped its relationship with Heaven. During Victor's growing involvement with Demons and Divine Beings, the differences between Hell's ancient rulers and its lesser inhabitants became increasingly relevant, particularly as powerful Demon Dukes such as [[Belial]] began interfering in the Human World.[20]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Lilith's past is closely connected with [[Morgana Elderblood]], one of the most powerful Succubi to emerge from [[Biblical Hell]]. Before becoming the feared General known as the Reaper, Morgana was a nameless, comparatively weak Succubus who survived in a civilization where weaker Demons were routinely exploited or killed. As a Succubus, she possessed an instinctive connection to Lilith and could locate the Mother of Demons even when her residence was concealed from most other Demons.[14]
The apparent entrance to Lilith's castle was not a sanctuary for those who sought her protection. Those who approached without her permission were directed into a deadly environment containing powerful Demons, where survival depended upon the ability to fight, recover and continually grow stronger. Morgana was repeatedly injured and mutilated while attempting to survive, eventually discovering a river capable of healing her wounds. She used that advantage to continue hunting rather than surrendering, gradually developing her Dream abilities and learning how to kill increasingly powerful opponents through deception, patience and precise attacks.[14]
After numerous unsuccessful attempts, Morgana finally defeated a stronger Demon by trapping it inside a dream before killing and consuming it. The experience established a pattern of hunting and consuming enemies that continued for an enormous period of time. Each successive victory improved her physical abilities, Demonic nature and mastery of illusions, transforming the weak Succubus into an exceptionally dangerous existence. After thousands of years of survival and growth, Lilith personally appeared before her and bestowed a Demonic name upon her, recognizing the strength she had developed through adversity. The transformation accompanying that recognition gave Morgana a more Human-like appearance while further increasing her power. Within a comparatively short period, she became one of Lilith's high-ranking soldiers and acquired the title of the Reaper during the conflict against [[Diablo]]'s faction.[14]
Morgana's original name bestowed by Lilith is distinct from the name Morgana that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later gave her. Her earlier memories established Lilith's direct role in elevating her from a nameless Succubus into one of Hell's most feared Generals. They also revealed the severity of Lilith's standards for those who wished to approach her, as well as the extraordinary importance Demonic names could possess.[21][14]
Lilith's opposition to the Gods was reinforced by her possession of a genuine God-Slaying weapon. Morgana regarded her former mistress as the only being she personally knew who routinely used such a weapon as her principal armament. The sword, Genesis, was capable of threatening Divine existences in a manner ordinary weapons could not reproduce, reflecting both Lilith's personal strength and her ancient hostility toward the Biblical order.[22]
Hell's internal balance eventually collapsed when Diablo launched a decisive offensive against Lilith's faction. Although she possessed fewer soldiers, Lilith had previously been capable of fighting Diablo personally, making her subsequent overwhelming defeat difficult to explain through ordinary differences in military strength. Her army was destroyed, and the Mother of Demons was captured, chained and placed under Diablo's authority. A recording obtained by [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] revealed her condition, demonstrating that Diablo's preparations extended far beyond the ordinary struggles between Hell's ancient factions.[10]
Diablo's victory was especially suspicious because both combatants belonged to the highest ranks of ancient Demonic existence. Beings who had already reached such extraordinary levels of power ordinarily required considerable periods to achieve meaningful improvements. The sudden destruction of Lilith's forces therefore suggested an external advantage, an extraordinary artifact or intervention beyond Diablo's conventional capabilities. Her confinement subsequently became central to his preparations for opening the Gates of Hell and initiating a new supernatural war on Earth.[10]
Lilith's original Human nature further distinguished her from her conqueror. Although she had become a Demon Goddess, the fundamental origin of her Soul had not been completely overwritten. A small remnant of her original existence remained, preserving aspects of the emotional complexity associated with Humanity. Diablo, in contrast, was a Demon born directly from the sins of Humanity and identified with the original corruption produced by the Biblical system. This distinction contributed to the differences in their personalities, motives and relationships with the inhabitants of Hell.[9]

#### Demonic War
Lilith entered the [[Supernatural Gathering]] as the unexpected representative of Hell, accompanied by [[Diablo]]. Her arrival immediately provoked hostility from the Angelic representatives, particularly Michael and [[Gabriel]], who recognized her as the woman banished from Genesis by the [[Heavenly Father]]. Her identity as a former Human Progenitor, the Mother of Demons and a Demon Goddess was openly acknowledged before the representatives of the other supernatural factions.[1]
Her appearance differed considerably from the ancient image preserved in Morgana's memories. Instead of the black-haired, golden-eyed woman associated with her former freedom, Lilith possessed white hair, an unnaturally pale complexion and blood-red eyes. Her overwhelming allure affected many of the male representatives, although her influence was not identical to the Divine authority over Love, Sex and Beauty possessed by [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood. Lilith's existence was fundamentally connected to a race and its origin rather than to Aphrodite's particular Divine Concepts.[1]
Despite her immense power, Lilith behaved with an unnatural lack of independence. Her compliance with Diablo's instructions and generally lifeless expression reflected the magical control placed upon her. Diablo subsequently revealed that the invasion of Earth had already begun, turning the Gathering into the public announcement of a conflict that had been prepared long before the other supernatural rulers learned the full extent of his plans.[1]
The Demonic War further exposed the mechanism through which Diablo exploited Lilith. Rather than merely defeating her in battle and demanding obedience, he had obtained assistance in constructing a magical ritual that suppressed her freedom while leaving her body and extraordinary powers available for his use. The arrangement allowed him to direct one of Hell's greatest combatants against her own interests, maintaining the appearance of submission while her independent consciousness remained trapped beneath the ritual.[11]
Diablo also used Lilith's status to maintain the administrative operation of [[Biblical Hell]]. Although he had seized the keys associated with the realm, he had not become its genuine Ruler through the required process. Lilith was consequently used as a temporary substitute within the Soul-management structure, allowing the system to continue functioning while Diablo pursued objectives outside Hell. This imposed position was distinct from the genuine authority she would later receive as a Ruler of New Hell.[4]
Her enslavement became particularly devastating during Diablo's confrontation with [[Lucifer]] Morningstar. Knowing that Lucifer retained genuine affection for his former wife, Diablo used Lilith as bait to draw him into an ambush. Lucifer understood that the situation was a trap but nevertheless confronted Diablo rather than abandoning her. The scheme culminated in Lucifer being grievously wounded by the [[Spears|Spear]] of Longinus, while Lilith's controlled body participated in the confrontation despite her internal resistance.[11]
Although Lilith's expression remained largely empty, the ritual did not eliminate her consciousness or emotional attachments. She recognized Lucifer's suffering, desperately attempted to resist her own movements and shed tears while trapped inside her body. Her condition demonstrated that Diablo had suppressed her ability to act without completely destroying the individual beneath the enchantment. The humiliation of being forced to injure someone she once loved became another source of the hatred she later directed toward him.[11]
Information obtained through Morgana and other Demonic sources increasingly convinced [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] that Lilith's apparent allegiance to Diablo was artificial. Morgana's knowledge of her former mistress made the behavior especially suspicious, since the proud and independently minded Demon Goddess she remembered was fundamentally incompatible with the obedient figure following Diablo. Victor consequently began regarding Lilith as a potential ally to be liberated rather than simply another enemy who had to be killed.[23][24]
After conquering Hell and spending centuries developing his strength and authority, Victor returned to the greater battlefield with the resources necessary to confront Diablo directly. His plans included freeing Lilith while avoiding unnecessary injury to her, an objective made possible through cooperation with [[Lily Elderblood]], Lilith's own daughter, and the Demonic forces that had entered Victor's service.[12]
During the confrontation, Lily and the other participants created an opportunity for Victor to approach Lilith despite her overwhelming strength. Victor blocked her attack and directly confronted the magical mechanism controlling her. He made clear that defeating a mindless puppet held little interest for him; he wanted the genuine Lilith restored rather than a powerful enemy whose will had been suppressed. His intervention attacked the ritual binding her to Diablo and ultimately destroyed the connection, restoring the Mother of Demons' free will.[12]
The change was immediately visible. Lilith's lifeless expression disappeared, her white hair returned to black and her enormous Demonic presence erupted as the centuries of suppressed hatred resurfaced. Diablo realized that his control had been severed and reacted with disbelief that Victor had successfully destroyed the magic responsible for her enslavement. Lilith immediately summoned Genesis and turned against the man who had stripped her of her autonomy.[12]
Victor allowed the confrontation to proceed rather than immediately eliminating Diablo himself. Lilith launched an exceptionally violent assault, combining her physical strength, Demonic claws, God-Slaying sword and enormous quantities of Miasma. Her attacks and insults reflected a hatred accumulated throughout her imprisonment, while Diablo retaliated with his own corrosive Demonic powers. The exchange quickly developed into a devastating battle between ancient existences whose abilities threatened the surrounding region.[15]
To prevent their conflict from causing unnecessary destruction to Earth, Victor coordinated with the Heavenly Father to establish a protective barrier around the battlefield. The remaining Demonic armies were ordered to withdraw into Hell, while Victor removed much of the lingering Miasma from the surrounding environment. Lilith and Diablo continued fighting within the isolated region, with neither initially willing to withdraw from the confrontation.[15]
Lilith eventually entered her True Demon Form and began using the full strength of her Divinities. Her Concept of Beginning expressed her fundamental relationship with Demonkind, while Rottenness, Corruption and Despair provided destructive effects capable of affecting Diablo even through his formidable defenses. The Miasma surrounding her body was extraordinarily toxic, and the combination of her powers continuously damaged the former Demon King's flesh.[2]
Despite Diablo's exceptional regeneration as an Ancestral Demon, Lilith inflicted injuries serious enough that he was forced to sever affected portions of his own body to prevent further deterioration. Her conceptual attacks eventually interfered with his normal regeneration, demonstrating that his Demonic constitution did not make him immune to the powers of another Progenitor. Diablo's accumulated strength from consuming Souls over hundreds of thousands of years allowed him to rival Lilith in raw power, but he struggled against the cumulative effects of her authorities.[2]
[[The Spear of Longinus]] did not provide Diablo with the decisive advantage he expected. Although its special property was particularly dangerous to beings originating from the Heavenly Father's creation, Lilith's ascension and the fundamental transformation of her Soul had altered her existence beyond its former classification. Her development into a Demon Goddess prevented the weapon's specific killing property from functioning against her as it would have against a conventional creation of the Heavenly Father.[2]
As the battle continued, Diablo became increasingly desperate and resorted to the exceptional resources he had accumulated, including the hearts associated with Michael and Gabriel. His attempts to transform his existence and overcome the deteriorating balance of power further destabilized the confrontation. Lilith remained determined to kill him, but Victor eventually intervened when the conflict reached its final stage.[2][25]
Victor's release of immense Negativity and the manifestation of his increasingly monstrous nature overwhelmed the instincts of the Demons present. Lilith herself was affected by the suffocating presence of his power, demonstrating the extraordinary difference between even a Demon Goddess and the abnormal existence Victor was becoming. Diablo ultimately perished after Victor personally defeated and consumed him, ending the ancient Demon's attempt to seize control of the wider supernatural world.[26][27]
Following Diablo's death, Victor acknowledged that he had deprived Lilith of the satisfaction of delivering the final blow against the enemy who had enslaved her. Rather than dismissing her grievance, he apologized and recognized the personal significance of the revenge he had interrupted. Lilith accepted his explanation and agreed to visit the Hell he now ruled, beginning a relationship that would eventually extend beyond their temporary alliance against Diablo.[13]

#### King Among Kings
Lilith's restoration of freedom did not immediately remove the consequences of her imprisonment. After the Demonic War, the enormous psychological burden of centuries spent beneath [[Diablo]]'s control left her mentally exhausted. She subsequently fell into a prolonged sleep, while the exceptional quantities of Miasma leaking from her body made ordinary confinement dangerous. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s forces therefore maintained an isolated room within the Demon King's Castle capable of containing her power without placing the surrounding inhabitants at risk.[28]
Upon awakening, Lilith encountered [[Vine]] Elderblood and [[Vepar Elderblood]], who explained the precautions surrounding her residence and informed her of the remarkable changes that had occurred in Hell. She learned that the old infernal territory had been transformed through Victor's rule, that a new political and military hierarchy had emerged, and that even the Demon King's Castle had undergone considerable development. The presence of [[Zaladrac Zeovnur Elderblood]], a Dragon residing within the realm, further illustrated how radically the balance of power had changed.[28]
Lilith was also introduced to the achievements of [[Aline Elderblood Valefar]] and Hell's developing technological institutions. Rather than remaining a collection of hostile territories dominated by hereditary powers, Victor's Hell increasingly operated through organized administration, industrial production, scientific development, commerce and regulated military activity. Its principal cities had become centers of economic and cultural activity instead of existing solely as strongholds for ancient Demon Kings and Dukes.[16]
Victor eventually visited Lilith personally and informed her that she was free to explore. He had no intention of imprisoning her merely because she possessed extraordinary power, but he warned that her freedom did not exempt her from the laws protecting the civilization he had established. To assist her adaptation, he gave her a Learning Sphere containing extensive information about modern Hell and 100,000 Knull to cover her initial expenses.[16]
The outside world proved far more unfamiliar than Lilith expected. Looking across Abaddon, she discovered immense skyscrapers, orderly districts, commercial establishments and ordinary Demons living in relative peace. The sight of lesser Demons laughing, eating and moving freely through the city contradicted her memories of a Hell defined primarily by predation, fear and the arbitrary violence of those possessing greater strength.[17]
Among Abaddon's most prominent structures were the enormous buildings known as the Seven Pillars. Lilith learned that they formed part of an advanced urban system built through the combined efforts of Victor's subordinates, including Helena's specialized Demonic abilities and Aline's technological development. Their residential and administrative functions reflected the merit-based society created under Victor, where service, ability and contribution could provide advancement even to Demons lacking prestigious ancestry.[17]
Lilith's exploration immediately tested the limits of her new freedom. After flying into a restricted area, she encountered guards responsible for enforcing the city's aerial-traffic regulations. Although they eventually recognized her identity as the Mother Goddess, they did not simply ignore the violation. She was informed that the laws established beneath Victor's authority applied to everyone, including exceptionally powerful Demons. The experience initially irritated her, but it also demonstrated that the new civilization was not governed exclusively through personal favoritism or the privilege of ancient bloodlines.[17]
As she moved through the city, Lilith explored ordinary shops and encountered a variety of Demonic products, including prepared food, advanced equipment and weapons manufactured through Hell's expanding technological industry. Many inhabitants showed affection and respect toward her as their Mother Goddess, occasionally offering discounts or special treatment without granting her political immunity. Helena and Lily observed portions of her journey and recognized that Lilith's interaction with ordinary Demons was gradually strengthening the population's affection toward both her and the new society that had allowed them to live this way.[17]
Lilith's freedom also reopened unresolved matters from her former life. Her relationship with Morgana remained affected by an ancient promise made when Morgana departed her army. In exchange for permission to leave, Morgana had agreed to eventually provide another General capable of replacing her. She originally intended to raise her first daughter for that purpose, but her maternal attachment prevented her from treating the child merely as a military asset. Later, she used her exceptional Dream powers together with Vlad's Energy to create [[Elizabeth Tepes]], yet once again could not bring herself to surrender her daughter to that role.[29]
Victor learned of the promise after Lilith became intoxicated and spoke about her past. He subsequently encouraged Morgana to resolve the matter directly, recognizing that the old agreement belonged to a period when both women lived under very different circumstances. By this time, Lilith no longer commanded her former army, and her position in Victor's civilization provided opportunities for a resolution that did not require sacrificing Morgana's family. The incident also exposed Lilith's poor behavior when drunk and the extent to which her former authority had become disconnected from her current lifestyle.[29]
Lilith's political position changed again following Victor's reconstruction of the infernal realm. With the merger of the relevant Hells, the existing Ruler authorities required reorganization. Victor formally established Aline Elderblood Valefar and [[Persephone Elderblood]] as Rulers of New Hell, adapting their previous positions to the newly created structure. He then approached Lilith about accepting the third office.[5]
Lilith refused immediately, having no interest in assuming another exhausting position after finally recovering her independence. Victor responded by presenting the accumulated financial consequences of her lifestyle. Aline's records revealed that Lilith had spent 1,305,340,466 Knull since arriving in Hell, while gambling, drinking, property damage and other incidents had increased her total liabilities beyond three billion Knull. Numerous complaints from subordinates, particularly Lily, further demonstrated how frequently her behavior created problems for the administration.[5]
Unable to dismiss the debt or realistically oppose Victor's decision, Lilith accepted the appointment and became the Third Ruler of New Hell. Her authority placed her alongside Aline and [[Persephone]] within the formal administration of the merged infernal system, although her Progenitor status continued to exist independently of that political office. Victor's method of obtaining her agreement also reflected the difference between their relationship and her previous enslavement: Lilith remained capable of making decisions and expressing her objections, but she was not permitted to ignore the consequences of her conduct.[5][3]
Her relationship with Victor's household continued developing as the Demonic and other supernatural members of his faction became increasingly integrated. Lilith regularly appeared alongside Lily, Helena, Vine and Vepar, participating in matters concerning the expanding territories under Victor's control. Despite being socially associated with his Demon wives, she initially continued distinguishing herself from women who had already formally married him. The affection and familiarity surrounding their relationship eventually developed into a genuine place within the Elderblood family.[30]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ascension as the Dragon God of [[Chaos]], Hell became part of an increasingly vast network of territories and civilizations connected to [[Dragons Nest Pantheon]]. Lilith remained one of its most influential Goddesses, although the development of modern infernal society introduced a position she had never previously occupied: an immensely popular public entertainer.[7]
During the [[Heavenly Father]]'s diplomatic visit to New Hell, Lilith's cultural influence became impossible to overlook. A gigantic statue of her stood within Abaddon, and her image appeared across the city's entertainment infrastructure. Victor explained that she had become one of Hell's greatest idols, possessing popularity that potentially rivaled his own among the Demonic population. The revelation astonished both the Heavenly Father and [[Ariel Elderblood]], neither of whom expected the ancient Mother of Demons to have adopted a profession associated with modern popular culture.[7]
Lilith's concerts combined performances involving Succubi with a variety of musical styles, including pop, rock and heavier forms of music. Her repertoire openly expressed her continuing dislike of Heaven and Angels, transforming ancient hatred into cultural performance. Ariel reacted strongly to the provocations and became increasingly disturbed by the freedom and happiness Lilith appeared to enjoy. The encounter illustrated how the old Angel-Demon conflict remained emotionally powerful even after their rulers began establishing diplomatic relations.[7]
The Heavenly Father recognized that the Lilith performing before him resembled the spontaneous and independent woman who had existed during Genesis more closely than the bitter political figure she later became. Victor had originally encouraged her entertainment career partly because of its practical benefits and her difficult financial situation, but Lilith ultimately developed a genuine enjoyment of performing. Her public identity therefore became one of the clearest indications that she had acquired an existence beyond the ancient conflicts that once dominated her life.[7]
As Victor's territories expanded, the responsibilities of Hell's administrators became increasingly complex. Lilith worked alongside Lily, [[Persephone]], Aline and Helena during the enormous influx of Souls associated with the collapse of the [[Norse Pantheon]]. The scale of the incoming population overwhelmed ordinary processing arrangements, forcing the women responsible for the realm to evaluate additional methods of handling the dead. The crisis demonstrated that the infernal administration was no longer concerned merely with Demonkind, but with the Soul systems of multiple civilizations and Pantheons.[31][32]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Lilith's integration into the Elderblood household continued during the period in which [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s position as God Emperor became firmly established. The women surrounding him increasingly regarded themselves as an interconnected family rather than separate factions temporarily sharing the same ruler, although their histories and personal grievances did not disappear simply because they belonged to one household.[18]
The arrival of Ariel brought the ancient conflict between Angels and Demons directly into Victor's family. Lilith's resentment toward Heaven remained powerful, and she was among the women who initially responded with hostility to the Seraphim's presence. Despite her lingering hatred, Lilith no longer intended to devote her entire existence to vengeance against the [[Heavenly Father]]. Her family and personal happiness had become more important than indefinitely repeating the conflicts of Genesis.[18]
The Heavenly Father's own reflections upon Genesis revealed another dimension of Lilith's earliest life. Adam, Eve and Lilith had been created with adult bodies but lacked the corresponding emotional and psychological maturity. Their inexperience made them vulnerable to manipulation by beings who already possessed extensive knowledge of other supernatural factions and Divine politics. The ancient events surrounding their creation were therefore not simply the actions of fully experienced adults confronting one another; the original Humans entered an already complicated supernatural world without the experience necessary to recognize every danger surrounding them.[33]
Lilith's confrontation with Ariel became an extended physical contest between two exceptionally powerful beings representing opposing sides of the Biblical order. The combat continued for weeks, with Ariel becoming increasingly exhausted while Lilith demonstrated the extraordinary endurance associated with her Demonic physiology and immense experience. Their rivalry gradually became part of Ariel's difficult introduction to the Elderblood family rather than the beginning of another unrestricted war between Heaven and Hell.[34]
Lilith's position as one of Victor's wives also brought her into discussions concerning the larger supernatural system governing Souls. As Victor developed a new dimension and acquired greater influence over the flow of the dead, he recognized that the existing Ruler titles connected his family unnecessarily to the Primordial authorities responsible for the old structure.[6]
Victor consequently requested that Lilith, Aline and [[Persephone]] relinquish their Ruler titles. Lilith understood that the Water Dimension and the connection created through Victor's authority as King of Hell would allow the Soul-management system to continue operating without their personal attachment to the previous offices. The redesigned arrangement also allowed Victor to retain greater control over exceptional Souls while reducing the influence external Primordial authorities could exercise through the former titles.[6]
Lilith therefore relinquished the office of Third Ruler of New Hell without losing her position as the Mother of Demons, Demon Progenitor or Goddess. Her removal from the Ruler structure was not a demotion in personal power, but a consequence of the Empire's increasingly independent infrastructure and Victor's desire to prevent external authorities from obtaining unnecessary leverage over his family.[6]
The Elderblood family's extraordinary growth continued changing Lilith's life. Her relationship with Victor had moved beyond the alliance originally created through the Demonic War, and she was increasingly recognized as one of his wives. Violet regarded Lilith's development as evidence that entering the household could allow someone to recover a more authentic identity rather than merely forcing every woman to conform to a single personality. In Lilith's case, the stability of family life brought back qualities that had been obscured by ancient resentments, ambition and suffering.[19]
Lilith also participated in the family's preparations surrounding Victor's investigations of civilizations beyond their original Sector. While Victor observed the activities of a younger clone exploring the higher regions of the Cosmos, his wives gathered around him to watch the projected events. Lilith sat with Victoria, Carmila, Persephone, Tasha and Maya, sharing the Emperor's attention with the other women. The scene reflected how thoroughly the former Queen of Hell had become integrated into the ordinary private life of the Imperial household.[35]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
During the enormous period of accelerated development undertaken by the [[Elderblood Empire]], Lilith became directly involved in preparing the next generation of the Imperial Family. Her principal focus was [[Liriel Elderblood]], her daughter with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], whose inherited nature combined the extraordinary characteristics of two Progenitors belonging to different races.[8]
Liriel was born possessing the foundations of both a True Demon and a True Dragon. Her body inherited exceptional Draconic and Demonic potential, including a form of rapid physical adaptation resembling a lesser expression of Victor's own evolutionary capabilities. Unlike her father's deeper Conceptual adaptation, Liriel's ability was concerned primarily with corporeal attacks, allowing her body to become resistant to physical methods it had already experienced. Her extraordinary potential nevertheless came with the pronounced pride associated with both parental lineages.[8]
Lilith personally trained Liriel within the Freezing Hell, an environment saturated with exceptionally dense Miasma. Rather than concentrating on formal martial instruction, which she left primarily to [[Scathach]], Lilith focused upon genuine battle experience. She repeatedly attacked her daughter with the intention of forcing her to respond under pressure, exploiting the girl's natural adaptability and ensuring that her inherited power did not become a substitute for practical judgment.[8]
The training also revealed the unusual combination of powers Liriel inherited. Her arsenal included extremely toxic Hell Miasma, Hellfire combined with Dragonfire, the ability to open portals connected to different Hells and advanced proficiency with the Draconic Language. Her fighting style placed considerable emphasis upon inflicting negative conditions, becoming more dangerous as those effects accumulated upon her opponents. Lilith understood that the sheer variety of these abilities made discipline and emotional control especially important.[8]
Lilith demonstrated the ruthless practicality she expected from her daughter by exploiting openings that Liriel considered unfair. Her training included using supernatural distortions and sudden attacks to seize an advantage, emphasizing that genuine opponents would not respect the conventions of an organized sparring match. Her objective was to teach Liriel to use every available resource rather than allowing pride to restrict her actions. The girl was expected to preserve her confidence without becoming arrogant enough to ignore danger.[8]
Liriel's ambitions extended beyond personal strength. She wanted to become a commander within the Imperial Legions and contribute directly to the Empire, refusing to remain behind sisters such as [[Nero Elderblood]], [[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]], [[Metis]] Elderblood and [[Velina Velnorah Elderblood Thaloria III]], who had already begun assuming significant responsibilities. Lilith supported that ambition through increasingly demanding training, understanding that the command of Imperial forces would require considerably more than possessing an exceptional bloodline.[8]
The emotional intensity of Victor's daughters became especially apparent when Liriel misunderstood references to the possibility of her father's death. Her concern rapidly escalated into an obsessive determination to protect or conceal him, despite Victor possessing strength far beyond anything she could ordinarily defend against. Lilith immediately corrected her, insisting that she recognize Victor's own abilities rather than allowing fear to override reason. Even after countless years of experience, Lilith found raising Liriel considerably more exhausting than raising Lily.[8]
Above all, Lilith emphasized the importance of the Elderblood family. She warned Liriel that no amount of personal potential justified isolating herself from those willing to support her. Whenever she encountered a problem beyond her own capabilities, she was expected to rely upon her parents and sisters rather than treating assistance as a humiliation. The lesson reflected a significant change from Lilith's ancient experiences in Hell, where weakness and dependence had so frequently been punished through exploitation or death.[8]
As the accelerated Imperial era continued, Lilith remained active within the enormous Elderblood household. She attended the Imperial Family's gathering alongside [[Gaia]], [[Persephone]], Morgana and Lily, participating in the increasingly ordinary domestic affairs of a dynasty whose members included ancient Goddesses, Progenitors, [[True Dragons]] and numerous exceptionally powerful descendants. The former Queen of Hell had become part of a family whose scale and influence extended far beyond the infernal civilization she once ruled.[36]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Lilith's husband, the King who liberated her from [[Diablo]]'s enslavement and the individual most responsible for the transformation of her later life. Their relationship initially developed through the conflict surrounding the Demonic War, when Victor recognized that Lilith was being controlled and decided to restore her independence rather than treat her as an ordinary enemy. His willingness to confront the mechanism of her enslavement ultimately allowed her to recover the freedom she had lost for centuries.[24][12]
Lilith initially viewed Victor with considerable curiosity and caution. Although he had freed her, he also possessed the authority of Hell's new King and displayed a willingness to enforce his laws regardless of her former status. Her exploration of the civilization he created, along with his treatment of ordinary Demons, gradually altered her understanding of his rule. Victor encouraged her independence while refusing to exempt her from the responsibilities or consequences that applied to others.[16][17]
Their relationship developed further through her integration into his household and the restoration of the personality buried beneath her previous experiences. Her position eventually changed from an ancient Goddess associated with his faction into that of a genuine wife and member of the Elderblood dynasty. Their daughter, [[Liriel Elderblood]], represents the union of their two Progenitor lineages, while Lilith's participation in family affairs demonstrates how thoroughly her life has moved beyond the political struggles of ancient Hell.[6][19][8]

#### Heavenly Father
The [[Heavenly Father]] is Lilith's creator and the central figure associated with her earliest existence as a Human Progenitor. Her banishment from Genesis and the events surrounding the first Humans became the foundation of a resentment that persisted for thousands of years. Even after acquiring immense power as the Mother of Demons, Lilith continued regarding Heaven and its creator with considerable hostility.[1]
The Heavenly Father's encounter with Lilith's modern life in New Hell revealed how profoundly her circumstances had changed. Rather than remaining solely a resentful enemy of Heaven, she had developed a successful entertainment career and recovered much of her original spontaneity. Although her hatred had not disappeared, she no longer organized her entire existence around revenge. The ancient events of Genesis also remained complicated by the original Humans' psychological immaturity and their vulnerability to manipulation during the earliest stages of their lives.[7][33]

#### Adam
Adam was Lilith's first husband and one of the original Human Progenitors created during Genesis. Their relationship belongs to the earliest period of her existence, before she became the Mother of Demons and entered Hell's supernatural conflicts. The circumstances surrounding the first Humans involved individuals whose physical maturity did not correspond to their emotional experience, leaving them vulnerable to influences they were not equipped to understand. Lilith's later banishment separated her from the position originally intended for her within Humanity.[1][33]

#### Lucifer Morningstar
[[Lucifer]] Morningstar was Lilith's former husband and the father of [[Lily Elderblood]]. Their relationship remained significant long after their separation, as Lucifer continued to possess genuine affection for her despite the changes both had undergone. [[Diablo]] deliberately exploited this attachment by using Lilith to lure Lucifer into an ambush, correctly anticipating that he would attempt to intervene even while recognizing the danger.[11]
Lilith's inability to resist Diablo's commands during the confrontation caused profound distress. Her body participated in the violence against Lucifer while her consciousness remained trapped beneath the controlling ritual. Her tears and desperate internal resistance demonstrated that her former attachment had not been erased, even though she possessed no ability to prevent the actions being forced upon her.[11]

#### Diablo
[[Diablo]] was Lilith's greatest ancient rival and later her enslaver. Their competition for control of [[Biblical Hell]] divided the realm into opposing factions, with Lilith possessing sufficient individual strength to challenge him despite commanding fewer forces. Diablo eventually destroyed her army and captured her through circumstances involving advantages beyond their previously comparable personal strength.[10]
The magical ritual used to control Lilith stripped her of her ability to act independently while preserving her consciousness. Diablo exploited her as a military asset, a political tool and a means of maintaining the infernal Soul system. His decision to use her against [[Lucifer]] further intensified the cruelty of her imprisonment. Once [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] restored her freedom, Lilith immediately turned against Diablo and attacked him with Genesis, her True Demon Form and the full extent of her confirmed Divine authorities.[11][4][12][2]
Although Diablo ultimately died at Victor's hands rather than hers, the destruction of the former Demon King permanently ended the power he had held over her. Lilith accepted Victor's apology for intervening in her revenge, allowing the conclusion of the Demonic War to become the beginning of a new period in her life rather than merely the end of an ancient conflict.[13]

#### Lily Elderblood
[[Lily Elderblood]] is Lilith's daughter with [[Lucifer]] and one of the most important people connected to her liberation. Lily inherited the characteristics of two exceptionally powerful figures in Biblical history and developed into a cunning and influential Succubus whose political ambitions frequently brought her into contact with Hell's highest authorities.[37]
Lily participated in the efforts surrounding Lilith's release from [[Diablo]], providing assistance that allowed [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to confront the magical control mechanism without unnecessarily injuring her mother. Their relationship later became far less concerned with warfare and considerably more affected by ordinary family disputes, particularly Lilith's excessive spending, drinking and property damage. Lily was responsible for many of the complaints presented during the investigation of her mother's enormous debts, demonstrating that even the Mother of Demons could become a source of frustration for her own daughter.[12][5]

#### Liriel Elderblood
[[Liriel Elderblood]] is Lilith's daughter with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and the inheritor of two extraordinary Progenitor lineages. Her combined True Demon and True Dragon nature grants her exceptional potential, including powerful Demonic Energy, Draconic abilities and rapid physical adaptation. Lilith is particularly aware of the dangers created by the extreme pride associated with both sides of her daughter's inheritance.[8]
Lilith personally trains Liriel in genuine combat while entrusting much of her formal martial education to [[Scathach]]. Her instruction emphasizes practical ruthlessness, adaptability, emotional control and the distinction between confidence and arrogance. Despite the severity of their training, Lilith openly takes pride in Liriel's development and repeatedly assures her that the Elderblood family will support her whenever individual strength proves insufficient.[8]

#### Morgana Elderblood
[[Morgana Elderblood]] was one of Lilith's most accomplished Generals during the ancient conflicts of Hell. Originally a nameless Succubus, Morgana survived the deadly environment surrounding Lilith's castle for thousands of years, developing extraordinary strength through repeated combat and the consumption of other Demons. Lilith eventually recognized her accomplishments, bestowed a Demonic name upon her and elevated her into the military structure of her faction. Morgana subsequently became known as the Reaper, one of the most feared warriors serving the Mother of Demons.[14]
Their relationship was shaped by Lilith's harsh expectations and the responsibilities attached to serving her faction. Morgana eventually obtained permission to leave by promising to provide another sufficiently powerful General, an agreement that later became complicated by her attachment to her daughters. Following Lilith's liberation and the collapse of her former military organization, the ancient promise became an unresolved personal matter rather than an immediate obligation to provide a new soldier.[29]

#### Ariel Elderblood
[[Ariel Elderblood]] and Lilith share an ancient antagonism rooted in the historical conflict between Angels and Demons. Ariel was particularly disturbed by Lilith's prominent position in New Hell, where the former Queen had become an exceptionally popular idol and openly expressed her hostility toward Heaven through music. Their contrasting lives and circumstances contributed to Ariel's growing emotional instability during the [[Heavenly Father]]'s visit.[7]
Their rivalry later developed into a prolonged confrontation when Ariel entered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household. The two fought for weeks, displaying immense endurance and the hostility accumulated through their opposing backgrounds. Their eventual coexistence as members of the same family demonstrated that the political and racial divisions of the ancient Biblical order no longer completely governed their personal lives.[34]

#### Aline Elderblood Valefar and Persephone Elderblood
[[Aline Elderblood Valefar]] and [[Persephone Elderblood]] were Lilith's fellow Rulers within New Hell. Their relationship became especially important following the merger of the relevant infernal realms, when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] assigned the three women formal authority over the newly organized system. Aline's administrative responsibilities also placed her in direct contact with Lilith's financial misconduct, and her detailed accounting became the means through which Victor convinced the reluctant Demon Goddess to accept her position.[5]
All three later returned their Ruler titles when Victor developed an alternative Soul-management structure. The change removed their unnecessary dependence upon the previous Primordial system while allowing them to remain powerful Goddesses and members of the Elderblood family.[6]

#### Helena Gremory Elderblood, Vine Elderblood and Vepar Elderblood
Helena Gremory Elderblood, [[Vine]] Elderblood and [[Vepar Elderblood]] were among the principal Demonic officers responsible for the administration and military development of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Hell. They played important roles in the organization of the new civilization and the circumstances surrounding Lilith's recovery and integration into the realm.[12][28]
Although Lilith had once been one of Hell's supreme rulers, her relationship with the three women developed within a political structure where Victor's authority stood above the former divisions between Demonic factions. Helena and Lily also observed Lilith's exploration of modern Hell and recognized how her growing affection for its inhabitants contributed to the stability of the civilization Victor had established.[17]

### Abilities

#### Skills and Abilities
Lilith Elderblood is an ancient Progenitor, the Mother of Demons, an exceptionally powerful Succubus and an Ascendant Goddess whose existence combines several distinct supernatural systems. Originally one of the Progenitors of Humanity, Lilith subsequently became the origin of Demonkind, acquiring racial authority over Demons and attaining Godhood. Her Divine nature places her at a level comparable to a God-King, while her status as a Progenitor provides capabilities separate from the conventional abilities granted by Divinity.[1][2]
Lilith's greatest demonstrated combat capabilities arise from combining her Demonic physiology, immensely concentrated Miasma, Divine Concepts and the God-Slaying Sword Genesis. During her confrontation with [[Diablo]], she maintained a prolonged offensive against an Ancestral Demon whose power rivaled her own despite having spent hundreds of thousands of years accumulating Souls and Energy. Her conceptual attacks inflicted persistent deterioration, damaged Diablo's exceptionally durable body and eventually interfered with his regeneration. Although Diablo possessed sufficient raw power to confront her, Lilith's combination of Progenitor characteristics and multiple destructive Divinities provided advantages that he could not easily overcome.[3]
Her four explicitly established Divinities are Beginning, Rottenness, Corruption and Despair. Beginning is intrinsically connected to her position as the origin of the Demonic Race, while the other three Concepts contribute to her exceptionally destructive combat abilities. Her Succubus ancestry also grants a separate foundation associated with supernatural allure and the dream-based powers characteristic of that Demonic lineage. These racial characteristics should be distinguished from Lilith's personal Divine authorities, as supernatural attraction does not establish possession of [[Aphrodite]]'s Divinities of Love, Sex or Beauty, and the Dream abilities demonstrated by other Succubi do not independently establish a Dream Divinity for Lilith.[1][3]

#### Divine Abilities

##### Ascendant Goddess Physiology
Lilith is a genuine Goddess whose ascension fundamentally transformed her Soul and existence rather than merely increasing her Demonic Energy. Her Divine characteristics coexist with her original Progenitor nature, granting access to conceptual powers that ordinary Demons cannot reproduce simply through Miasma or physical development. This transformation also altered her relationship with the [[Heavenly Father]]'s creation, making her existence fundamentally different from the original Human Progenitor he created during Genesis.[3]
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics: Lilith possesses physical strength, speed, durability, reflexes, agility and endurance vastly surpassing those of ordinary supernatural beings. Her combat performance allows her to confront enemies possessing God-King-class power through both physical attacks and supernatural techniques.[2][3]
- [[Divine Energy]] Manipulation: As an Ascendant Goddess, Lilith possesses the ability to wield Divine power and exercise the authorities associated with her Divinities. Her combat style allows Divine abilities to operate alongside Demonic Miasma and conventional physical attacks.[3]
- Conceptual Manipulation: Lilith can invoke the supernatural properties of the Concepts she governs, imposing effects that originate from Divine authority rather than merely conventional elemental or Demonic Energy.[3]
- Divine Soul: Her ascension caused a complete metamorphosis of her Soul, merging and transforming the characteristics of her previous existence into a fundamentally different Divine state. This alteration became particularly important when the [[Spears|Spear]] of Longinus failed to exercise its special killing property against her.[3]
- [[Immortality]] / Supernatural Longevity: Lilith has existed since the earliest period of Humanity and remained alive throughout the ancient conflicts of Genesis, the formation of [[Biblical Hell]] and the subsequent ages of supernatural history. Her existence is not governed by an ordinary Human lifespan.[1]
- Enhanced Endurance: Lilith can maintain combat against exceptionally powerful enemies for prolonged periods. Her endurance is especially apparent during her confrontation with [[Diablo]] and her later continuous fighting against [[Ariel Elderblood]], whose own extraordinary stamina eventually proved insufficient to maintain the same level of activity indefinitely.[3][4]
- Spiritual Physiology: Lilith's Divine existence is sustained by a fundamentally transformed Soul rather than merely her original mortal biology. Her transition into Godhood represents a qualitative change in existence and provides the basis for her resistance to abilities specifically targeting her former classification as a creation of the Heavenly Father.[3]

##### Divinities
Lilith possesses four confirmed Divine Concepts: Beginning, Rottenness, Corruption and Despair. These authorities allow her to affect targets through several different conceptual mechanisms, which can be employed individually or combined into exceptionally destructive attacks. Her battle with [[Diablo]] demonstrated that the simultaneous application of several hostile Concepts can threaten even an Ancestral Demon possessing immense quantities of Energy, exceptional durability and powerful regenerative abilities.[3]

###### Beginning
Beginning is Lilith's principal Progenitor-related Divinity and the conceptual foundation of her title as the Goddess of Demons. The authority arose from her position as the beginning and Mother of the Demonic Race. Unlike ordinary Demonic rulers, whose authority depends upon military hierarchy, territory or political recognition, Lilith possesses a direct relationship with Demonkind through the very nature of her existence as its Progenitor.[3]
- Beginning Manipulation: Lilith possesses an expression of Beginning associated with her existence as the origin of Demonkind. This Concept provides the foundation for her racial authority and ability to create Demons independently of the conventional infernal reincarnation process.[3]
- Demonic Racial Authority: Lilith possesses direct conceptual authority over Demons because she is their Progenitor. This authority exists independently of her former position as Queen of Hell or any later administrative appointment.[3]
- Racial Domination: Lilith can impose her authority upon Demons whose wills are insufficiently powerful to resist. Rather than requiring ordinary persuasion, political loyalty or an external contract, her Progenitor authority can compel qualifying members of the Demonic Race to obey her.[3]
- Mind Control / Command Inducement: Through her racial authority, Lilith can force weaker-willed Demons to perform actions according to her commands. The effectiveness of this ability depends upon the target's ability to resist her authority and should not be considered unconditional domination of every Demon regardless of power or will.[3]
- Creation / Demon Creation: Lilith can directly create Demons through her Progenitor power. This differs from the ordinary Biblical process in which Demons originate from the Negative remnants remaining after condemned mortal Souls undergo processing through the reincarnation system.[5][3]
- Racial Origin: Lilith's identity as the Mother of Demons is a fundamental characteristic of her existence. Changes in the political government of Hell do not remove the connection between Lilith and the race she originated.[3]

###### Rottenness
Rottenness is one of Lilith's most destructive Divinities, allowing her to impose supernatural decay upon targets through conceptual attacks. Unlike ordinary injuries caused by physical force, the deterioration produced by Rottenness directly expresses the Divine Concept and can remain dangerous even to beings possessing powerful supernatural bodies.[3]
- Rot Manipulation: Lilith can induce supernatural rotting and deterioration through her Divine authority. This allows her attacks to damage a target through conceptual decay instead of relying solely upon ordinary physical destruction.[3]
- Decay Manipulation: Her conceptual attacks accelerate deterioration in affected matter, producing damage capable of threatening exceptionally durable supernatural bodies.[3]
- Biological Manipulation / Biological Decay: [[Diablo]]'s hand rapidly deteriorated after being affected by Lilith's attacks. The corruption was sufficiently dangerous that he immediately severed the hand rather than allowing the effect to continue spreading.[3]
- Regeneration Negation / Regeneration Interference: Rottenness contributes to Lilith's arsenal of persistent destructive effects. Her combined conceptual attacks eventually prevented Diablo's ordinary regeneration from functioning, although the precise contribution of Rottenness relative to her other Divinities is not individually isolated.[3]
- Durability Negation (Limited): Rottenness can cause deterioration through a supernatural conceptual effect rather than depending exclusively upon an attack's physical force. Its effectiveness nevertheless depends upon the properties and resistance of the target.[3]

###### Corruption
Corruption is a directly confirmed Divine Concept belonging to Lilith and forms part of the destructive authority she employs against powerful supernatural opponents. Her attacks can combine Corruption with Rottenness, Despair and concentrated Miasma to produce overlapping forms of deterioration and damage.[3]
- Corruption Manipulation: Lilith can exercise the Divine Concept of Corruption and incorporate it into her attacks, allowing them to inflict a supernatural corruptive effect rather than merely conventional physical damage.[3]
- Conceptual Manipulation: Corruption operates as a Divine authority and can affect an opponent through a conceptual mechanism distinct from ordinary Demonic Energy.[3]
- Corrosive Attacks: Lilith can combine Corruption with Rottenness, Despair and toxic Miasma, creating attacks dangerous to highly resistant supernatural beings.[3]
- Power Combination: Corruption can be employed simultaneously with Lilith's other destructive Divinities, allowing the effects of several Concepts to operate within the same offensive sequence.[3]
The specific independent applications of Corruption beyond her demonstrated combat techniques remain undefined. Her possession of the Concept does not alone establish unrestricted control over morality, personality, Souls or every possible manifestation of corruption.

###### Despair
Despair is another confirmed Divinity belonging to Lilith, representing a supernatural authority over the Concept of Despair. In direct combat, she uses it as part of her combined conceptual attacks, contributing to their destructive effects against powerful opponents.[3]
- Despair Manipulation: Lilith can invoke the Divine Concept of Despair and incorporate it into supernatural attacks.[3]
- Conceptual Manipulation: Despair contributes an additional conceptual component to her offensive powers, operating alongside Rottenness and Corruption instead of functioning merely as ordinary Demonic Energy.[3]
- Destructive Energy Manipulation: Lilith's combined attacks are powerful enough to threaten an enormous surrounding area. The concentrated Miasma and harmful Concepts released during her battle with [[Diablo]] would have devastated the battlefield without the barrier established by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and the [[Heavenly Father]].[3]
- Power Combination: Despair can be applied simultaneously with her other Divinities, allowing Lilith to attack through several supernatural effects rather than depending upon a single method of destruction.[3]

##### Combined Divine Techniques
Lilith's ability to use multiple Divinities together is one of the principal reasons she can confront beings whose raw Energy and physical capabilities rival her own. Her attacks do not need to rely exclusively upon the destructive force of Genesis or Miasma. Instead, she can impose several harmful Concepts simultaneously, forcing an opponent to resist different supernatural effects while also enduring direct physical attacks.[3]
- Multiple Power Manipulation: Lilith can invoke multiple Divinities within the same battle, transitioning between their effects without abandoning her physical or Demonic abilities.[3]
- Power Combination: Rottenness, Corruption and Despair can be incorporated into the same attack, creating overlapping destructive effects.[3]
- Conceptual Damage: Lilith's attacks can produce supernatural deterioration rather than merely ordinary bodily injuries. This makes conventional durability and regenerative capabilities insufficient when the target cannot resist the conceptual effects.[3]
- Damage Amplification: Combining multiple harmful Concepts with Miasma and weapon attacks increases the danger of her offensive techniques and allows her to exploit weaknesses that a single attack method might fail to overcome.[3]
- Regeneration Negation: [[Diablo]]'s regeneration ceased functioning during his extended confrontation with Lilith. The deterioration was associated with the cumulative effects of her abilities rather than ordinary physical injury, although the exact technique responsible was not separately identified.[3]

##### Reality Warping
Lilith possesses the ability to distort Reality during combat. While training her daughter [[Liriel Elderblood]] within the Freezing Hell, she initially relied upon physical speed to approach and test Liriel's reactions. After Liriel successfully anticipated her movement, Lilith disappeared from her previous position and reappeared behind her daughter, immediately securing her with her claws. Liriel recognized this second maneuver as an application of Reality bending rather than ordinary speed.[6]
- Reality Warping: Lilith can distort Reality to perform combat maneuvers that cannot be attributed solely to conventional physical movement.[6]
- Spatial Movement (Reality Distortion): Her demonstrated technique allows her to bypass an opponent's immediate positioning and reappear behind the target. The precise mechanism has not been established as conventional teleportation or Space manipulation independent of her Reality-bending ability.[6]
- Combat Maneuvering: Lilith integrates Reality distortion into close-range combat, allowing her to change an engagement after an opponent has adapted to her ordinary movement patterns.[6].

##### Divine Resistances
- Resistance to Existence Erasure (Specific): Lilith's ascension into Godhood changed the fundamental identity of her Soul sufficiently that the [[Spears|Spear]] of Longinus' special property against the [[Heavenly Father]]'s creations no longer applied to her. This is a specific resistance to the weapon's conditional killing ability rather than proof of universal immunity to all forms of existence erasure.[3]
- Resistance to Soul Manipulation (Specific): The Divine metamorphosis of Lilith's Soul placed her outside the original creation-based condition exploited by Longinus. This does not establish universal protection against all Soul-targeting techniques.[3]
- Resistance to Anti-Creation Abilities (Specific): Although Lilith originated as one of the Heavenly Father's creations, her later ascension invalidated the specific condition Longinus was designed to exploit. She blocked the weapon with Genesis, physically caught it and returned it toward [[Diablo]] without suffering the anticipated killing effect.[3]

#### Demon Progenitor Physiology
Lilith, the Progenitor and Mother of Demonkind.
Lilith is the Progenitor of Demons, occupying a racial position distinct from ordinary Biblical Demons, Demon Dukes and even conventional Demon Kings. While most Biblical Demons originate from Negative remnants of mortal Souls, Lilith began her existence as one of the original Human Progenitors and subsequently transformed into the origin of Demonkind. Her Demonic nature therefore developed from an already existing Soul rather than through the conventional system of condemned Souls becoming Demons.[5][1]
Her position as the Mother of Demons provides extraordinary racial authority, immense Demonic power and a profound relationship with the Miasma associated with Hell. The full nature of her powers cannot be attributed exclusively to Demon physiology because her Ascendant Goddess state and Progenitor Divinity operate alongside her racial characteristics.[3]

##### Progenitor Authority
- Demon Creation: Lilith can create Demons directly through her Progenitor authority instead of depending upon the conventional process of generating Demonic existences from condemned Souls.[3]
- Racial Domination: Lilith possesses authority over members of Demonkind and can force weaker-willed Demons to obey her commands.[3]
- Supernatural Leadership: As the Mother and original Progenitor of Demons, Lilith possesses an authority rooted in racial origin rather than political rank. Her Progenitor status remains intact even when she no longer occupies a Ruler's throne.[3]
- Progenitor Physiology: Her existence incorporates the exceptional characteristics associated with the origin of a supernatural race, granting her capabilities and privileges beyond those of ordinary descendants.[1][3]

##### Miasma Manipulation
Miasma is the principal Demonic Energy used by Lilith and one of the foundations of her combat abilities. Her Miasma possesses extraordinary density, toxicity and destructive potential, allowing her to inflict extensive damage through direct projection, physical contact and combinations with her Divinities. Even outside combat, the Miasma naturally emanating from her body can become dangerous to other inhabitants of Hell.[3][7]
- Miasma Manipulation: Lilith can generate, control and weaponize immense quantities of Demonic Miasma.[3]
- Energy Generation: Her Demonic body produces enormous amounts of Miasma, with the quantity and concentration increasing dramatically during her True Demon Form.[3]
- Energy Projection: Lilith can unleash Miasma offensively against opponents, including enemies with sufficient power to rival [[God-Kings]].[3]
- Energy Manipulation: She can direct Miasma through her own body and combine it with attacks made through her claws, tail or Genesis.[3]
- Corrosion Inducement: Lilith's Miasma is exceptionally corrosive and toxic, capable of destroying matter upon contact.[3]
- Poison Manipulation / Toxic Miasma: Her Miasma possesses extremely harmful properties, creating a dangerous environment for opponents who cannot resist its effects.[3]
- Environmental Destruction: The amount of Miasma released during her battle against [[Diablo]] was sufficient to threaten the surrounding region. Without the barrier isolating the confrontation, the battlefield would have suffered catastrophic devastation.[3]
- Energy Coating: Lilith can envelop portions of her body in Miasma, including her tail and other Demonic appendages, reinforcing their offensive properties.[3]
- Energy Combination: She can combine Demonic Miasma with her Divine Concepts and Genesis, producing attacks that possess multiple supernatural damage mechanisms.[3]
- Passive Energy Emission: Lilith naturally emits exceptionally concentrated Miasma. While recovering from the Demonic War, the substance leaking from her body was dangerous enough to require an isolated room rather than ordinary accommodation within the Demon King's Castle.[7]

##### True Demon Form
Lilith possesses a True Demon Form that expresses the full power of her Demonic physiology. The transformation causes her wings to lengthen, her horns to become sharper and her tail to become enveloped in dense Miasma. The substance progressively spreads across her entire body, producing an overwhelmingly corrupt and destructive aura. Her power increases substantially in this state, allowing her to use her physical capabilities, Miasma and Divine Concepts with greater effectiveness.[3]
- Transformation: Lilith can enter a more powerful Demonic state that changes the characteristics of her body and significantly increases her strength.[3]
- Statistics Amplification: The True Demon Form directly increases her overall combat power beyond her ordinary state.[3]
- Natural Weaponry: Her horns, wings and tail become more pronounced and dangerous during the transformation, complementing her claws and physical combat techniques.[3]
- Miasma Amplification: Dense corrupt Miasma envelops her body and appendages, substantially increasing the danger of close-range engagement.[3]
- Energy Aura: Her transformed body becomes surrounded by concentrated Miasma that can damage anything unable to withstand its corrosive properties.[3]

##### Demonic Anatomy
- Flight: Lilith possesses enormous Demonic wings extending from the region of her hips and can fly naturally through her Demonic physiology.[1][3]
- Natural Weaponry: She possesses sharp claws, horns and a long pointed tail that can be incorporated into close-quarters combat.[1][3]
- Enhanced Strength: Her Demonic body possesses sufficient physical power to clash directly with [[Diablo]], an Ancestral Demon whose physical durability and accumulated Energy were among his greatest advantages.[3]
- Enhanced Speed: Lilith can execute high-speed attacks and movements against extraordinarily powerful opponents. Her speed alone allows her to repeatedly challenge Liriel's exceptional Draconic reflexes during combat training.[3][6]
- Enhanced Durability: Lilith can survive prolonged exchanges against opponents possessing immense destructive power. She endured Diablo's attacks while continuing to fight effectively and was in better physical condition than him during much of their confrontation.[3]
- Enhanced Reflexes: Her combat reactions allow her to engage in high-speed exchanges, defend against powerful attacks and exploit extremely brief openings.[3]
- Enhanced Stamina: Lilith possesses extraordinary physical and spiritual endurance, allowing her to fight at high intensity for extended periods and maintain continuous combat far beyond ordinary supernatural limits.[3][4]
- Demonic Energy Affinity: As a Demon Goddess and Progenitor, Lilith possesses immense compatibility with Demonic Miasma and can maintain its use throughout prolonged combat without losing control of her own powers.[3]

##### Negativity Affinity
Lilith's existence as a Demon Goddess gives her an intrinsic relationship with the Negative aspects of the Biblical supernatural system. Although she was originally a Human Progenitor, her transformation into a Demon established an affinity with the Demonic energies and influences governing her race. This nature became particularly apparent when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] manifested an overwhelming quantity of pure Negativity during his confrontation with [[Diablo]], provoking a powerful instinctive reaction among the Demons present.[8]
- Negative Energy Affinity: Lilith possesses an intrinsic affinity with the negative supernatural characteristics associated with Demonkind.[3]
- Energy Sensitivity: Her Demonic nature responds to exceptionally powerful manifestations of Negativity, including the overwhelming authority released by Victor.[8]
- Miasma Manipulation: Lilith can directly generate and manipulate the negative Demonic Energy associated with her own race.[3]
Lilith's Demonic connection to Negativity is distinct from Victor's higher conceptual authority over Negativity itself. Her racial affinity does not grant her unrestricted control over the fundamental Concept.

#### Succubus Physiology
Lilith belongs to the Succubus lineage and possesses its supernatural foundation as the original Mother of Demonkind. Succubi differ from ordinary Demons through their unnatural attractiveness, affinity with desire and dreams, and racial abilities involving vitality and supernatural essence. Powerful Succubi such as [[Morgana Elderblood]] have demonstrated advanced applications of these characteristics, including Dream manipulation capable of producing effects in physical Reality.[9][10]
Lilith's unnatural allure is directly demonstrated, while several other Succubus abilities are established primarily through other members of the race. Their inclusion as racial characteristics should not be interpreted as evidence that Lilith has personally demonstrated every known high-level Succubus technique.

##### Supernatural Beauty and Allure
Lilith possesses extraordinarily potent supernatural attractiveness. Her appearance and voice can provoke intense lust among susceptible individuals, including supernatural beings of considerable status and power. Her presence at the [[Supernatural Gathering]] caused male Gods to respond to her allure, although [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s developed resistance to Charm prevented her from affecting him as strongly as she affected others.[1]
- Supernatural Beauty: Lilith possesses unnatural physical attractiveness far beyond ordinary Human standards, characteristic of exceptionally powerful Succubi.[1]
- Attraction Inducement: Her presence can provoke supernatural attraction in susceptible beings, including Gods.[1]
- Lust Inducement: Lilith's Succubus nature can stimulate lust and sexual desire through supernatural influence rather than depending solely upon ordinary physical appearance.[1]
- Supernatural Voice: Her melodious voice carries a seductive quality capable of appealing directly to the desires of the beings listening to her.[1]
- Passive Charm: Lilith possesses a natural supernatural allure associated with her Succubus physiology. Its effectiveness can be reduced or resisted by individuals possessing sufficient resistance to Charm or comparable supernatural influence.[1]

##### Dream Manipulation
Succubi possess a racial affinity with Dreams and can use supernatural abilities to create, manipulate and weaponize dream experiences. Among the strongest members of the race, these powers can extend beyond ordinary mental illusions and influence physical Reality. Morgana's combat against [[Dantalion]] demonstrates the exceptionally dangerous nature of advanced Succubus Dream techniques, including the manifestation of dream constructs capable of physically and mentally harming an opponent.[9][10]
The following abilities are established for high-level Succubi as a racial system. Lilith's position as a Progenitor makes this system relevant to her physiology, but its individual applications have not all been independently demonstrated by Lilith.
- Dream Manipulation: Powerful Succubi can manipulate Dreams and construct supernatural experiences affecting another individual's consciousness.[9][10]
- Dream Construction: High-level Succubi can create detailed dream environments and scenarios that interact with a target's mental state.[9]
- Illusion Manipulation: Succubi can create deceptive experiences that interfere with an opponent's perception of Reality, allowing them to exploit confusion, fear and psychological weaknesses.[10]
- Mental Manipulation: Dream-based attacks can affect an opponent's mind and psychological condition, producing effects that remain dangerous even when the target understands the experience is supernatural.[10]
- Dream-to-Reality Manifestation: Exceptionally powerful Succubi can project Dreams into physical Reality, allowing dream constructs to interact with and harm physical beings.[10]
- Physical and Mental Damage: Advanced Dream projections can cause damage through both psychological and physical mechanisms instead of functioning merely as harmless illusions.[10]
- Fear Inducement: High-level Succubus illusions can exploit the fears of their opponents, as demonstrated through Morgana's battle against Dantalion.[10]

##### Life-Force and Essence Absorption
Succubi possess a racial form of nourishment involving the vitality and supernatural essence of their partners. This ability can be exceptionally dangerous to weaker beings, as powerful Succubi are capable of draining victims to death through sexual activity. Other Demons recognize this danger, and even physically powerful members of the race may avoid Succubi because of their ability to consume vitality.[11]
- Life Absorption: Succubi possess the racial ability to drain vitality from sexual partners, potentially killing beings unable to withstand the process.[11]
- Essence Absorption: High-level Succubi can acquire supernatural essence as part of their racial powers. Morgana's history demonstrates that Succubus abilities can interact with the essence of other powerful supernatural beings.[9]
- Lust Empowerment: Sexual activity functions as a source of nourishment and empowerment for Succubi, comparable in broad biological function to the role Blood plays for Vampires.[11]
- Vitality Drain: The life-draining characteristics of Succubi can produce potentially fatal consequences for susceptible partners.[11]

##### Bodily Fluid Manipulation
Members of the Succubus lineage can possess supernatural control over their own bodily fluids. Morgana demonstrates this ability even after undergoing a substantial racial transformation, explaining that the remaining characteristics of her original Succubus physiology allow her to control bodily fluids through supernatural means.[12]
- Bodily Fluid Manipulation: Succubi can manipulate their own bodily fluids through supernatural control.[12]
- Biological Manipulation (Self-Targeted): The racial characteristic permits control over specific bodily functions involving fluid production and release.[12]

#### Combat Skills
Lilith possesses extensive combat experience accumulated throughout the ancient conflicts of Genesis, the struggles between Heaven and Hell, and the wars between the great Demonic factions. Her fighting style combines the physical capabilities of a Demon Progenitor with Divine authority, Miasma and skilled use of Genesis. Unlike warriors who depend exclusively upon overwhelming quantities of Energy, Lilith can exploit several different supernatural mechanisms to damage opponents possessing extreme durability and regeneration.[13][3]

##### Swordsmanship
Lilith's principal weapon is the God-Slaying Sword Genesis, which she has wielded throughout an exceptionally long period of supernatural history. [[Morgana Elderblood]] regarded Lilith as the only person she personally knew who routinely possessed a genuine God-Slaying weapon as her main armament, establishing the exceptional nature of her equipment among even the elite forces of [[Biblical Hell]].[14]
- Swordsmanship: Lilith is an accomplished swordswoman capable of using Genesis against exceptionally powerful supernatural opponents.[14][3]
- Weapon Mastery: She employs Genesis effectively in prolonged combat while simultaneously manipulating Miasma and her Divinities.[3]
- Defensive Swordsmanship: Lilith successfully used Genesis to intercept and block the [[Spears|Spear]] of Longinus when [[Diablo]] attempted to kill her with its special properties.[3]
- Offensive Swordsmanship: Her sword techniques allow her to pressure powerful opponents through rapid attacks, direct thrusts and combinations with supernatural powers. Genesis pierced Diablo's body during their confrontation.[3]
- Weapon and Energy Integration: Lilith can incorporate Genesis into offensive sequences involving Demonic Miasma and Divine Concepts, making her sword attacks dangerous beyond their ordinary physical impact.[3]

##### Hand-to-Hand Combat
- Hand-to-Hand Combat: Lilith is proficient in close-range physical combat and does not depend exclusively upon Genesis. Her confrontation with [[Diablo]] includes direct physical exchanges, attacks using her claws and the exploitation of openings created through supernatural techniques.[15][3]
- Natural Weapon Combat: She can employ Demonic claws and other anatomical weapons as part of her fighting style, combining them with Miasma and Divine authority.[15][3]
- Enhanced Combat Reflexes: Lilith can react to high-speed attacks, intercept dangerous supernatural weapons and counterattack against enemies capable of fighting at God-King-class levels.[3]
- Combat Speed: Lilith possesses extraordinary movement speed and can close distances rapidly enough to challenge even the enhanced perception and reflexes of her Dragon daughter, Liriel.[6]
- Unarmed Weapon Defense: Lilith physically caught the [[Spears|Spear]] of Longinus after intercepting it with Genesis and then threw the weapon back toward Diablo, demonstrating exceptional physical control and confidence against an artifact feared by numerous supernatural beings.[3]

##### Combat Intelligence and Experience
- Combat Intelligence: Lilith possesses extensive battlefield experience and can apply several different supernatural systems against a single opponent, allowing her to exploit weaknesses that ordinary physical attacks cannot overcome.[3]
- Tactical Combat: She can switch between direct physical combat, swordsmanship, Miasma and conceptual attacks rather than restricting herself to one approach.[3]
- Power Combination: Lilith can combine Divine Concepts and Demonic Energy within sustained offensive sequences, overwhelming an opponent's defenses through multiple simultaneous threats.[3]
- Adaptive Combat Tactics: Her training with Liriel demonstrates a willingness to change techniques when an opponent begins successfully anticipating her attacks, including replacing ordinary speed with Reality distortion.[6]
- Weakness Exploitation: Lilith recognizes that strong opponents must be defeated through methods appropriate to their abilities rather than simply through greater quantities of Energy. Her attacks against [[Diablo]] demonstrate the danger of persistent conceptual deterioration against a regenerating enemy.[3]
- Ancient Warfare Experience: Lilith has fought in the wars and internal conflicts of [[Biblical Hell]] since antiquity, including prolonged struggles against Diablo's faction.[13]

##### Combat Endurance
Lilith's endurance is extraordinary even among powerful supernatural beings. During her battle with [[Diablo]], she maintained high-intensity combat for more than an hour, continuing to apply overwhelming pressure despite suffering injuries herself. While Diablo's body accumulated increasingly serious damage and his regeneration eventually ceased functioning, Lilith remained in better physical condition.[3]
Her endurance became even more apparent during her confrontation with Ariel. The two engaged in continuous combat for several weeks, pushing Ariel to exhaustion despite the Seraphim's exceptional natural stamina. Lilith, in contrast, remained comparatively unaffected and appeared to have begun enjoying the confrontation rather than treating it as a serious burden. The endurance displayed by her Divine and Demonic physiology therefore greatly exceeds what could be expected from ordinary combatants, including powerful Angels.[4]
- Supernatural Stamina: Lilith can maintain intense physical and supernatural combat for exceptionally long periods.[3][4]
- Enhanced Endurance: She is capable of continuing combat despite injuries and sustained expenditure of supernatural power.[3]
- Fatigue Resistance: Lilith can remain active during extended confrontations even after exceptionally powerful opponents begin suffering serious exhaustion.[4]
- Pain Tolerance: She can continue fighting effectively despite cuts, bruises and other combat injuries sustained against powerful supernatural opponents.[3]

##### Combat Instruction
Lilith serves as one of the principal instructors responsible for developing [[Liriel Elderblood]]'s combat capabilities. Unlike [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], who provides much of Liriel's formal martial training, Lilith concentrates upon actual fighting experience, emotional discipline and the practical use of supernatural abilities against an opponent who does not restrict herself to the conventions of a training arena.[6]
- Combat Teaching: Lilith personally trains Liriel through genuine combat, forcing her daughter to confront difficult opponents and refine her responses under pressure.[6]
- Tactical Instruction: She emphasizes the importance of winning through available means rather than allowing pride or personal expectations of fairness to interfere with combat.[6]
- Emotional Control: Lilith teaches Liriel to maintain a calm and analytical mind even while expressing powerful Demonic and Draconic emotions.[6]
- Combat Adaptation Training: Her methods repeatedly expose Liriel to powerful attacks and changing techniques, taking advantage of the girl's inherited ability to adapt to physical damage.[6]
- Battlefield Discipline: Lilith teaches that confidence and pride are valuable only while they do not interfere with the primary objective of survival or victory.[6]

#### Leadership and Non-Combat Skills

##### Military Leadership
Lilith formerly commanded one of the principal Demonic factions of [[Biblical Hell]]. Her organization possessed powerful Generals and elite soldiers, including Morgana, who rose from an initially weak Succubus into one of the most feared combatants serving the Mother of Demons. Lilith's faction possessed sufficient military strength to maintain a prolonged rivalry against [[Diablo]] despite eventually being defeated.[16][13]
- Leadership: Lilith has experience commanding large Demonic factions and directing powerful supernatural subordinates.[16][13]
- Military Strategy: Her leadership experience includes prolonged warfare against rival infernal factions and the management of Generals possessing exceptional individual powers.[13]
- Personnel Evaluation: Lilith recognized Morgana's extraordinary potential after the Succubus survived for thousands of years through repeated combat and predation. She granted Morgana a Demonic name and incorporated her into the hierarchy of her faction.[16]
- Supernatural Army Command: Lilith has experience commanding powerful Demonic soldiers and maintaining a military organization capable of confronting other major powers of Biblical Hell.[13]

##### Political and Administrative Ability
- Political Leadership: Lilith has held several major positions within [[Biblical Hell]], including Queen of Demons, leader of an independent faction and Third Ruler of New Hell.[17]
- Infernal Administration: Despite initially resisting formal governmental responsibilities, Lilith participated in the administration of New Hell and its increasingly complex supernatural institutions.[17]
- Soul-System Administration: Lilith participated alongside Aline, [[Persephone]], Lily and other administrators when the enormous influx of Souls from the Norse conflict placed pressure upon Hell's existing infrastructure.[18][19]
- Adaptability: Lilith successfully adjusted to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s radically transformed Hell despite its technological, economic and administrative structures being fundamentally different from those of the infernal civilization she previously governed.[20][21]

##### Performance and Entertainment
Lilith became one of New Hell's most popular public figures after adopting a career as a singer and idol. Her natural supernatural allure, enormous cultural status as the Mother of Demons and personal enjoyment of performance contributed to extraordinary popularity among the population. Her fame became so widespread that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] considered her potentially more popular than himself among ordinary Demons.[22]
- Singing: Lilith is an accomplished singer who became an extremely successful idol within New Hell.[22]
- Stage Performance: She is capable of performing before enormous audiences and successfully employing different musical styles, including pop, rock and heavy metal.[22]
- Charisma: Her public presence and status as the Mother Goddess inspire enormous enthusiasm and loyalty among Demonkind.[22]
- Entertainment: Lilith adapted to the modern entertainment industry created under Victor's rule and transformed it into a major part of her personal life.[22]

#### Equipment

##### Genesis
Genesis is Lilith's principal weapon and a genuine God-Slaying Sword. The weapon is exceptionally rare even among powerful supernatural beings, with Morgana identifying Lilith as the only individual she personally knew who routinely carried a genuine God-Slayer as her main armament. Genesis possesses extraordinary durability and can withstand clashes against immensely powerful Divine artifacts, including the [[Spears|Spear]] of Longinus.[14][3]
- God Slaying: Genesis is a genuine God-Slaying weapon capable of injuring and killing Divine beings.[14]
- Anti-Divine Attacks: Its properties allow Lilith to threaten Gods through a weapon specifically suited to combat against Divine opponents.[14]
- Weapon Summoning: Lilith can call Genesis into her possession. Immediately after her liberation from [[Diablo]]'s control, she summoned the weapon before attacking him.[23]
- Enhanced Durability: Genesis successfully intercepted the Spear of Longinus without being destroyed, demonstrating its exceptional resilience against powerful Divine equipment.[3]
- Energy Conduction: Lilith can wield Genesis while applying Demonic Miasma and her Divine Concepts, incorporating the sword into attacks possessing several different supernatural properties.[3]
- Swordsmanship: Lilith possesses extensive practical experience with Genesis and can use it effectively against God-King-class opponents.[3]

#### Resistances
Lilith's resistance profile is derived primarily from her Demon Progenitor physiology, extraordinary Divine Soul and demonstrated combat endurance. Although her nature provides exceptional protection against several forms of supernatural harm, the novel does not establish universal immunity to every ability that could threaten a Demon, Progenitor or Goddess.

##### Divine and Existential Resistances
- Resistance to Existence Erasure (Conditional): Lilith is unaffected by the [[Spears|Spear]] of Longinus' special killing property against creations of the [[Heavenly Father]] because ascension to Godhood fundamentally transformed the nature of her Soul. The resistance applies to the demonstrated creation-dependent condition rather than to every possible form of existence erasure.[3]
- Resistance to Soul-Based Abilities (Conditional): Lilith's Divine Soul metamorphosis removed the original mortal characteristics that would have allowed Longinus' special condition to recognize her as one of the Heavenly Father's creations. This demonstrates a particular form of protection arising from her transformed existence, not universal immunity to Soul manipulation.[3]
- Resistance to Anti-Divine Weapons (Conditional): Lilith successfully defended against Longinus through Genesis and physically intercepted the weapon without suffering its special killing effect. This does not establish immunity to the ordinary physical or supernatural damage of every anti-Divine weapon.[3]

##### Demonic Resistances
- Resistance to Miasma: Lilith naturally functions within enormous concentrations of Demonic Miasma, including the extremely toxic substance generated by her own True Demon Form.[3]
- Resistance to Corrosion (Self-Generated Miasma): Her body remains unaffected by the corrosive Miasma surrounding it, even though the same substance can destroy other matter upon contact.[3]
- Resistance to Poison (Self-Generated Miasma): Lilith can withstand the exceptionally toxic properties of her own Demonic Energy. This does not establish immunity to unrelated poisons or every supernatural toxin.[3]
- Resistance to Hostile Environments: Lilith can remain active and conduct prolonged combat training within the Freezing Hell, an environment saturated with concentrated Miasma.[6]
- Resistance to Conventional Aging: Lilith has survived since the ancient era of Genesis without undergoing the ordinary biological deterioration associated with Human aging.[1]

##### Physical and Combat Resistances
- Damage Resistance: Lilith can withstand attacks from exceptionally powerful opponents, including [[Diablo]], without being immediately incapacitated.[3]
- Pain Tolerance: Lilith continues fighting despite injuries sustained during her confrontation with Diablo.[3]
- Fatigue Resistance: Her Divine and Demonic constitution allows her to sustain combat for weeks, maintaining activity even when an exceptionally powerful Seraphim such as Ariel becomes exhausted.[4]
- Supernatural Endurance: Lilith can endure prolonged exchanges involving physical attacks, Demonic Energy and Divine power while retaining the ability to continue fighting effectively.[3][4]
