#### Debut
Chapter 829
Here I proclaim. You Must Protect Me, Because You Are My Knight. Heroic Class [[Magic]]: Rho Aias.
—Albedo Moriarty, Chapter 833
Albedo Moriarty, later known as Albedo Moriarty Elderblood, is an ancient Witch, alchemist, magical researcher, former Ascendant Goddess and current True Dragon/Dragon Goddess. She is the creator of Arcane, mother and former teacher of Evie Moriarty, grandmother of [[Emily Moriarty]], one of the wives of [[Victor Elderblood]], and the mother of [[Nigredo Elderblood]].[3][8] Her directly established Divine fields are [[Alchemy]] and Research.[5][6]
Long before personally entering Victor's life, Albedo had already become one of the most exceptional researchers produced by Earth's supernatural civilization. She accumulated several thousand years of knowledge across Witchcraft, Alchemy, Energy theory, biology, Souls, dimensions and Divine phenomena, regularly ignoring religious, political and even dimensional boundaries whenever they interfered with something she wished to study. While still mortal, she was audacious enough to enter Helheim as a living Soul and steal supernatural Artifacts, an incident [[Hela Elderblood]] remembered thousands of years later.[1]
Albedo's connection with Victor began indirectly when she granted him the supernatural Blessing responsible for allowing a Vampire to use Witch-style Magic despite lacking the normal qualification for it.[4][12] Their relationship eventually progressed from distant curiosity to research collaboration, teacher and student, romantic partners and finally husband and wife. Victor in turn became the person who forced Albedo to confront the greatest flaw in her enormous accumulated knowledge: she had studied Reality for millennia while observing only a comparatively tiny portion of it.[2][9]
By the late Imperial Era, Albedo had evolved again into a True Dragon and Dragon Goddess. Her magical output, Energy compatibility and ability to impose supernatural structures upon Reality grew enormously, but the same obsessive isolation that had once made her an exceptional researcher left her with poor combat fundamentals, comparatively weak Divine recognition and badly neglected family relationships. Victor, [[Anna Elderblood]] and [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] therefore forced her into an extensive program of social, conceptual and martial correction rather than allowing her to remain permanently inside a laboratory.[8][13][7][10]

### Physical Appearance
Albedo possesses extremely pale skin, long white hair, sharp teeth and distinctive eyes composed of black sclera surrounding red irises. Her skin has been described as pale enough to resemble a corpse, contributing to the otherworldly appearance expected of an ancient Witch.[1][8]
Her normal clothing remains deliberately traditional despite living within the technologically advanced [[Elderblood Empire]]. Albedo favors long dresses and a large Witch's hat and continues presenting herself visually as a Witch even after becoming a Dragon Goddess.[8]
During [[Scathach]]'s mandatory corrective training, Albedo replaces her normal Witch clothing with a complete leather training outfit. Four specialized [[Gravity Bracelets]] are attached to her wrists and ankles, forcing her True Dragon body to develop physically instead of allowing her to solve every problem through [[Magic]].[10]
Despite her immense chronological age, Albedo retains a youthful adult appearance. She had already surpassed ordinary Human aging through Divine ascension, while her later evolution into a True Dragon placed her biological existence even further outside the limitations of her original Human body.

### Personality
Albedo is defined above everything else by an obsessive hunger for knowledge, experimentation and discovery. Unknown phenomena rarely frighten her in the manner they frighten ordinary supernatural beings; the more unusual or dangerous something is, the more likely Albedo is to interpret it as an unexplored system worth studying. [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood originally describes her as an extraordinarily reclusive existence who almost never interferes with mortal affairs, making Albedo's decision to personally Bless [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] noteworthy even before her motives are fully understood.[12]
Her curiosity extends far beyond conventional Witchcraft. Albedo studies [[Magic]], [[Alchemy]], supernatural biology, Souls, [[Divine Energy]], other racial Energy systems, dimensional travel, artificial bodies, cosmology, the [[Akashic Records]], Primal Energy and eventually Eldritch phenomena existing outside the assumptions upon which much of her earlier research had been built.[11][1][9][8]
Danger itself does little to suppress this curiosity. During Arcane's inter-world experiment, she remains fascinated even after the portal develops into a potentially catastrophic dimensional and temporal anomaly. Her instinct is repeatedly to observe, theorize and test rather than panic, a disposition Victor immediately understands because he possesses a similar attraction toward things he has never encountered before.[14][15][3][16]
Thousands of years spent almost completely isolated in research severely distorted Albedo's social and ethical perspective. She became accustomed to examining people, Artifacts, Souls and dangerous supernatural phenomena according to how much information they could provide. Victor recognizes that the two of them share extreme curiosity but also identifies an important difference: Victor maintains powerful boundaries regarding his family and subjects, whereas Albedo had gradually sacrificed many comparable restraints in pursuit of research.[2]
Her experimentation was not limited to other subjects. Albedo repeatedly modified her own body while pursuing what she considered a superior version of herself. Over millennia, these experiments and her fixation upon research altered even ordinary biological and psychological priorities, leaving the pursuit of knowledge overwhelmingly more important to her than reproduction, intimacy or ordinary companionship.[2]
Victor does not attempt to remove the obsession that made Albedo extraordinary. He instead wants to preserve her intelligence, curiosity and relentless willingness to discover the unknown while repairing the emotional, social and ethical damage produced by extreme isolation.[2]
Joining the Elderblood Family did not immediately correct those problems. Albedo genuinely fell in love with Victor and came to value the family, but she continued disappearing into laboratories for exceptionally long periods. By Chapter 1165, her relationship with Nigredo had deteriorated to the point that her own daughter could disregard Albedo's command while immediately obeying Anna, publicly exposing how little maternal authority Albedo had developed.[8]
The problem becomes impossible for the family to ignore in Chapter 1171. Anna notes that researchers such as [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]], [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]] and [[Aline Elderblood Valefar]] can be equally obsessive while still participating in family life every day. Albedo instead becomes so absorbed in experimentation that even a warning from her newly developed Dragon senses is almost immediately forgotten when one of her experiments succeeds.[13]
Albedo also tends to understand a criticism intellectually before actually changing the behavior responsible for it. When [[Scathach]] begins punishing her, Albedo initially believes acknowledging the problem and apologizing should be sufficient. Scathach deliberately refuses to accept an intellectual concession as a replacement for practical correction.[7]
Her isolation had even begun damaging the very quality she valued most: her ability to innovate. Anna explains that Dragon Gods rely heavily upon imagination when manifesting their abilities, meaning that Albedo's habit of remaining permanently inside the same laboratory was slowly limiting her conceptual creativity and therefore both her research and supernatural development.[10]
Despite complaining extensively when subjected to Scathach's training, Albedo possesses exceptional determination. Once she learns that younger members of the Elderblood Family have already endured conditions she had considered unreasonable, she stops attempting to escape the curriculum and asks Scathach to continue training her.[10]
Albedo is also far more emotionally expressive than her reclusive reputation initially suggests. She enjoys portions of Human entertainment, understands modern fictional references, jokes, becomes embarrassed, displays envy, grows irritated and openly teases members of her family.[11] Her reaction to [[Melida Charlotte Elderblood]] is particularly characteristic: Albedo is openly frustrated that Melida possesses an instinctive understanding of Primal Energy that she herself cannot reproduce despite enormous research, but converts that jealousy into motivation to study harder rather than hostility toward the younger woman.[8]
Her later interactions with Evie also show gradual improvement. Albedo remains intrusive and initially attempts to push her daughter toward Victor, believing she can sense Evie's interest. When Evie distinguishes simple attraction and admiration from actually loving or wanting a relationship with Victor, however, Albedo stops, considers the argument and admits that Evie is correct rather than continuing to force the issue.[17]

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Albedo influences [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s supernatural development long before meeting him directly. During the [[Supernatural Gathering]], the Owner of Limbo identifies Victor as someone "Blessed by the Ground Witch" and names Albedo Moriarty as the Being responsible.[4]
The purpose of that intervention becomes clearer during the Demonic War. Ordinary Vampires are not naturally qualified to use Witch [[Magic]], yet Victor can interact with that system because Albedo's Blessing bypasses the incompatibility.[12]
Victor later includes Albedo among the extraordinary Beings whose Blessings have accumulated around him. Around the same discussion he describes [[Qliphoth]] as a World Tree and, from his research, the opposite of "Sephiroth", representing a Negative counterpart to its Positive structure.[18] This becomes particularly relevant much later when Albedo develops Draconic Magic structured around the Kabbalistic Tree of Life.

#### King Among Kings
Albedo's reputation as a researcher considerably predates her direct appearance. When [[Fenrir]] develops an abnormal supernatural condition severe enough to incapacitate him, [[Ariel Elderblood]] identifies Albedo alongside beings such as [[Tiamat]] and [[Gaia]] Elderblood as one of the extremely few specialists worth consulting, referring to her as the Goddess of [[Alchemy]] and Research.[5]
During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s conquest of [[Olympus]], Seth recognizes the structure of an enormous Strategic-Class magical formation as an Original Spell created by Albedo. Victor acquired its framework through memories taken from ancient Demons with whom Albedo had formed contracts while still mortal and whom she later killed after they ceased being useful to her.[19]
Victor does not reproduce Albedo's spell unchanged. He incorporates his own Soul and Creation-related abilities until the formation can interfere with the [[Greek Underworld]]'s System. The System-interference effect is therefore part of Victor's altered application and should not be attributed automatically to Albedo's original spell.[19]
Albedo finally appears personally during the enormous dimensional experiment conducted by Evie, Emily and the Witches of Arcane. Victor bypasses Arcane's conventional security while observing the ritual, yet Albedo is still capable of noticing him and directly addressing the hidden observer.[14]
Victor projects himself into Arcane and sits beside her while the experiment continues. Their intellectual compatibility becomes obvious almost immediately: instead of responding to the increasingly dangerous ritual with conventional alarm, Victor and Albedo eat popcorn while examining its development like spectators observing an especially interesting experiment.[15]
Albedo reveals that Victor is not the only unusual person she had chosen to Bless. She also intervened in [[Merlin]]'s development because his research into the relationship between supposedly distinct supernatural Energies interested her, describing him as one of her more successful investments.[11]
She demonstrates her own understanding by producing Demonic Energy/Miasma, [[Holy Energy]], Nature Energy, [[Magic]] Energy and colorless [[Divine Energy]] simultaneously. Albedo explains that supernatural civilizations frequently classify such Energies as fundamentally separate because of how races use them, whereas she views them as different expressions of deeper energetic principles. Divine Energy itself is naturally without a specific attribute until the God's nature modifies it.[11]
The experiment eventually connects Arcane with an inhabited world. Albedo detects Energy entering the portal from the distant side and recognizes that another intelligence is interfering with the connection.[3]
Arcane's Witches initially believe their dimension and civilization to be effectively inaccessible to foreign intrusion. The confrontation overturns that confidence while also revealing Albedo's historical importance: the assembled Witches recognize her as the legendary founder, and Albedo personally confirms that she created Arcane.[3]
After Victor crosses into the foreign world and returns, the portal becomes catastrophically unstable. Albedo immediately assumes control of the magical response. Using Victor's enormous Energy reserves as her power source, she produces approximately one million interconnected Magic Circles within seconds.[20]
She follows this with Heroic Class Magic: Rho Aias. Numerous heroic figures manifest from Albedo, position themselves around the portal and petrify into statues. Victor recognizes that Albedo is not merely creating conventional magical constructs; the technique is drawing upon the information of myths contained within the [[Akashic Records]] and converting those myths into functional supernatural manifestations.[20]
The heroic manifestations, Albedo's million-circle structure and Victor's supplied Energy are combined to isolate the unstable portal region inside a separated space.[20] The feat is collaborative and should not be described as Albedo independently creating the entire isolated dimensional structure.
The crisis worsens when the different rates of Time belonging to the two connected worlds begin contaminating one another. Albedo recognizes the phenomenon as part of a Convergence and explains the wider structure of cosmic Sectors and protections limiting unrestricted interference with less-developed regions. The instability eventually becomes serious enough to draw intervention from [[The Universal Tree]] and [[The Judges of The Abyss]], establishing that the experiment had begun affecting systems far beyond Arcane's laboratory.[16]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Albedo remains overwhelmingly occupied with research during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s transformation into the Dragon God of [[Chaos]] and the restructuring of his faction into a civilization capable of operating beyond Earth. Although she is not one of the principal combatants of this period, the ideas she demonstrated during Arcane—particularly the possibility of analyzing supposedly incompatible Energy systems through common underlying principles—become increasingly relevant as Victor's family encounters Divine, Draconic, Demonic and cosmic systems that cannot be understood through traditional Witchcraft alone.[11]
Her own later development follows the same direction. Rather than abandoning Witchcraft after entering a civilization filled with Dragons and Gods, Albedo progressively integrates it with Divine power, Dragon Language, conceptual structures and knowledge obtained from realms completely outside the environment in which her original theories were created.

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
During the Imperial Era, Albedo becomes directly involved in classified Elderblood research surrounding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul and the construction of artificial biological vessels.[1]
Victor brings Hela into the project because of her extraordinary understanding of Death and Souls. Hela immediately recognizes Albedo, revealing that they had already encountered one another thousands of years earlier when the still-mortal Witch entered Helheim as a living Soul and stole supernatural Artifacts for research.[1]
Their later cooperation demonstrates that their specialties are complementary rather than interchangeable. Hela possesses an enormously deeper innate relationship with Souls through Death, whereas Albedo understands how Souls interact with living biological vessels. Hela consequently requires Albedo's biological knowledge when participating in a project involving artificial bodies.[1]
The research attempts to produce Mortal-compatible vessels capable of carrying extremely small fragments of Victor's Soul. Even fragments that would normally seem insignificant remain extraordinarily dangerous because they retain traces of Victor's increasingly Eldritch/Nightmare existence. Albedo's task includes developing bodies capable of surviving contact with those fragments without immediately dying, collapsing or becoming corrupted.[1]
Albedo's value eventually becomes great enough that Violet regards allowing such an exceptional researcher to remain permanently outside the family as wasteful.[21] In Chapter 1123, Violet formally permits Albedo, Kali and [[Dún Scáith|Dun Scaith]] to openly pursue Victor. This is permission for genuine courtship rather than an instantaneous declaration that the women have already become his wives.[22]
Victor subsequently examines Albedo herself as critically as she normally examines her experiments. He confronts the psychological damage created by millennia of isolation and repeated self-modification, explaining that entering his family does not mean those flaws will simply be ignored. His intention is to preserve everything extraordinary about her while forcing the damaged portions of her personality to develop.[2]
The next lesson attacks the limitations of her accumulated knowledge. Victor compares Albedo to a frog at the bottom of a well: the frog may be exceptionally intelligent within the environment it understands, but intelligence cannot compensate for never seeing what exists beyond the well.[9]
Victor takes Albedo into intergalactic space and exposes her to phenomena completely beyond the scale upon which she had historically researched, including a colossal existence consuming galaxies. Her initial shock rapidly transforms into intense fascination as she realizes how narrow her previous understanding of Reality actually was.[9]
He then gives her access to information associated with his deeper Eldritch existence and cosmic structures that do not operate according to the assumptions of ordinary supernatural civilization. The knowledge is dangerous enough that Victor must stabilize Albedo's Soul while she processes it.[9]
The lesson is deliberately more profound than simply giving Albedo additional facts. Victor trains her to stop interpreting everything through the senses and systems with which she is already familiar and instead to expand the framework through which she perceives Reality itself. By the end of the process, Albedo can understand Victor while he uses the Language of the Universe, although comprehension of that language should not be confused with possessing the cosmic administrative Authority of a Regulator.[9]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
By the late Imperial Era, Albedo's position has transformed completely. She has married [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], produced a daughter with him and evolved into a True Dragon/Dragon Goddess.[8][7]
Her research obsession remains intact. During the gathering of Victor's daughters, Albedo appears visibly exhausted from attempting to understand and manipulate Primal Energy. Even with the biological advantages of a True Dragon, she considers the Energy extremely difficult to handle correctly.[8]
Her frustration becomes stronger when she compares herself with [[Melida Charlotte Elderblood]], whose unusual nature allows her to interact with Primal Energy far more instinctively and even affect portions of Victor's Soul. Albedo is jealous of the difference in natural talent but immediately converts the emotion into greater determination to decipher the Energy through research.[8]
Albedo nevertheless considers her new life almost ideal. Victor has given her access to a virtually inexhaustible supply of new phenomena, knowledge extending beyond her old cosmology and the resources of an Empire whose scientific and supernatural development dwarfs the environment within which she spent most of her earlier existence. More importantly, what began partially as intellectual and opportunistic interest has become genuine love for Victor.[8][23]
Her daughter [[Nigredo Elderblood]] demonstrates the absurd potential of this new family. Nigredo possesses Victor's Darkness and develops toward becoming a possible Primordial Goddess of Darkness, with her Eldritch expression producing a black-hole-like region in which conventional distinctions involving Being and Non-Being collapse.[8]
Albedo continues the alchemical symbolism through her daughter's name and considers using Rubedo if she has another daughter, referencing another stage of the alchemical process.[8]
The gathering also exposes Albedo's greatest personal failure. Nigredo can ignore her biological mother's commands but immediately responds to Anna. Anna bluntly attributes the problem to Albedo spending almost all of her existence inside laboratories instead of establishing an actual maternal presence in her daughter's life.[8]
The problem escalates in Chapter 1171. Anna notes that Ruby, Velnorah and Aline are also capable of becoming consumed by research, yet all three still participate in the family consistently. Albedo is worse: when her Dragon instincts warn her that something is approaching, she begins investigating the feeling only to abandon the concern almost immediately because one of her experiments succeeds.[13]
The approaching danger is [[Scathach]]. She physically drags Albedo into a reinforced training dimension and makes clear that the Witch's complete absence from ordinary family life will no longer be tolerated. Albedo apologizes and eventually attempts to obtain Victor's assistance, but Victor explicitly calls her his wife, refuses to rescue her from the consequences and authorizes Scathach to intensify the punishment.[7]
Victor modifies the dimension until one external day corresponds to one hundred internal years and reinforces the environment sufficiently to withstand a confrontation between two [[True Dragons]]. Scathach identifies Albedo as a Dragon Goddess and demands that the ancient Witch finally demonstrate what she has accomplished with that existence.[7]
Albedo materializes a sword and speaks through the Dragon Language. Her first Kabbalistic sequence—Keter, Hokhmah, Netsah—creates thousands of Draconic [[Magic]] Circles. Their combined output is sufficient to destroy the planet approximately one hundred times over, and the narration uses Albedo's performance to establish that she has reached the general level of power expected from a Being capable of destroying an entire Solar System.[7]
The display impresses observers but does almost nothing to Scathach. She destroys the formation through superior control and closes the distance before Albedo can capitalize upon the attack, smashing the Dragon Goddess hundreds of meters into the ground. The exchange immediately exposes the distinction between Albedo's extraordinary magical output and her comparatively primitive close-combat foundation.[7]
Albedo responds with Hokhmah, Binah, Yesod. Thousands of additional circles spread through the battlefield and begin inverting Reality: the environment becomes hostile, the air poisonous and numerous negative effects are layered throughout the area in an attempt to weaken Scathach continuously.[7]
Anna identifies the structure as being derived from the Kabbalistic Tree of Life. Albedo is taking the meanings carried by the Sephirotic framework and combining them with Dragon Language, allowing the conceptual model to possess far greater supernatural weight when imposed upon Reality. The theory is valid, but Albedo attempts to impose it upon a Being whose own existence, personal Reality and Divine development exceed hers; Scathach simply refuses the altered conditions.[7]
Scathach eventually activates Heptagrammakos, a technique integrating Draconic and Eldritch Runes strongly enough to freeze the surrounding Reality, immobilize Albedo and shut down her spellcasting. Albedo's True Dragon body immediately begins adapting to the effect, but the adaptation cannot progress quickly enough to overcome a technique of that quality before Victor intervenes.[7][10]
Victor's assessment is deliberately severe. He calls Albedo weak despite her enormous destructive capacity because the foundations supporting that power are far below what should be expected from a Dragon Goddess. He had expected the sword she manifested to become a genuine close-combat weapon, only to discover that Albedo was effectively using it as a magical staff and conceptual aid.[10]
Victor therefore removes her unrestricted laboratory schedule and orders daily training with Scathach until her basic combat ability catches up with her existence. He warns that if another thousand years pass without adequate improvement, he will personally join Scathach in training her.[10]
Anna simultaneously attacks the conceptual problem in Albedo's Magic. She explains that the sword and Tree of Life are functioning as crutches: external structures helping Albedo imagine an effect that a properly developed Dragon Goddess should increasingly be able to express through herself. Anna demonstrates this by speaking simply of Life and manifesting the relevant qualities directly into Reality.[10]
Anna goes further. Using the Tree of Life is not inherently incorrect, but if Albedo insists upon an external Tree as the conceptual medium, far greater existences are available. Victor himself possesses enormous metaphysical weight, and if Albedo specifically requires a Tree, Anna directly suggests The Universal Tree. The superior long-term solution remains using Albedo herself as the medium because her own existence as a Dragon Goddess should be capable of carrying conceptual authority.[10]
The lesson also exposes the consequences of her social isolation. A God's Concept gains practical weight through recognition and development, yet Albedo has spent enormous periods almost completely absent from the civilization around her. Her Divinities therefore possess far less public recognition and corresponding influence upon Reality than the Concepts of Goddesses who actively participate in the Empire.[10]
Scathach returns her to the fundamentals of Dragonhood. True Dragons possess exceptional natural compatibility with Energy and do not require elaborate Runes merely to accomplish basic manipulation. Dragon Runes are instead a code for imposing Concepts and instructions upon Reality and should be used where that level of supernatural command is actually appropriate.[10]
Physical conditioning follows. Scathach attaches four [[Gravity Bracelets]] to Albedo's wrists and ankles. Their levels reproduce astronomical gravitational conditions rather than simple numerical multipliers. Albedo operates around stellar-class conditions while progressing toward neutron-star gravity, whereas the highest black-hole/singularity setting remains capable of crushing her at that stage.[10]
Albedo initially considers the training excessive until Scathach points toward [[Siren Elderblood]], who has already endured the singularity setting. The comparison destroys Albedo's excuse; she stops protesting and asks Scathach to continue because she refuses to remain behind members of the family's younger generation.[10]
The correction remains active long afterward. In Chapter 1179, Albedo is sent into the Lower Sectors during preparations for the great Sector merger. She walks through the Witch planet in a disguised form, teleports above its cities and casually perceives magical blood spreading through the population as well as the presence of Demigods among its inhabitants.[23]
The visit demonstrates how distorted Albedo's perception of power has become. After spending so much time surrounded by Victor, Nigredo and the Elderblood Dragons, she briefly struggles to remember that an accomplished Witch Queen such as Evie is already an extraordinary existence by ordinary standards. Albedo internally admits that her isolation has dulled her perspective and that she genuinely needs to leave her laboratory more often.[23]
Although she privately regards herself as the weakest Dragon participating in this stage of the operation, her comparison is entirely relative. Her unrestricted presence is still too great for the Lower Sector, forcing Albedo to seal part of her power merely to remain there safely.[23]
Albedo reunites with Evie and Emily and reveals another major emotional development. When Evie accuses her of originally pursuing Victor opportunistically, Albedo does not completely deny it, but states that whatever her initial ambitions were, she genuinely came to love him. Evie is visibly struck by a gentleness in her mother's expression she had rarely witnessed before.[23]
Albedo delivers a dark-purple sphere required for the coming Sector merger. Evie attempts to understand the object directly and immediately suffers pain, forcing Albedo to warn her not to probe something capable of overwhelming her mind. Albedo herself admits that she does not completely understand the sphere because her time has been divided between research and Scathach's increasingly brutal training.[23]
Albedo, [[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]] and [[Tasha Fenrir Elderblood]] are each assigned corresponding objects during the operation connecting the Lower Sectors with Victor's greater cosmic territory.[23]
Her reunion with Evie and Emily continues through the final preparations. Albedo's old tendency to interfere in the lives of people around her resurfaces when she attempts to push Evie toward Victor and comments upon Emily's own romantic interests.[24] The matter eventually reaches Violet. Evie plainly explains that admiration and physical interest in Victor are not equivalent to wanting a romantic relationship with him. Albedo initially resists the distinction, then genuinely thinks about it and concedes that her daughter is correct. Shortly afterward, Scathach summons her back to training and Albedo immediately addresses her as Master and departs, showing how firmly the once-reclusive Witch has been placed under Scathach's continuing discipline.[17]

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was connected to Albedo hundreds of chapters before the two met directly. Albedo noticed something sufficiently unusual about the newly transformed Vampire to grant him her Blessing, allowing Victor to use Witch-style [[Magic]] despite a racial incompatibility that should ordinarily have prevented it.[4][12]
Her initial interest is primarily intellectual. Victor is an anomaly who continuously violates the assumptions upon which supernatural systems are supposed to function, making him exactly the kind of existence capable of attracting Albedo's attention.
Their first personal interaction demonstrates unusually strong compatibility. Both can watch a potentially catastrophic experiment with genuine amusement while calmly discussing its mechanics, and Victor is capable of following arguments concerning Energy and cosmology that would be meaningless to most people around Albedo.[15][11]
Their professional connection deepens when Albedo joins classified Imperial research involving Victor's Soul and artificial vessels.[1] Violet eventually concludes that Albedo's talent is too valuable to leave permanently outside the family and allows her to openly pursue Victor in Chapter 1123.[21][22]
Victor subsequently becomes one of Albedo's most important teachers. Rather than teaching conventional Witchcraft, he gives her knowledge of higher cosmology, Eldritch existence, the limits of familiar perception and the necessity of changing the conceptual framework through which she observes Reality.[2][9]
By Chapter 1165, Albedo regards Victor as her husband, while Victor directly identifies her as his wife during Chapter 1172.[8][7] Their daughter is [[Nigredo Elderblood]].[8]
For Albedo, the marriage provides nearly everything her old life lacked: an inexhaustible intellectual frontier, access to cosmic phenomena she could never have reached alone, enormous research resources and genuine personal affection. By Chapter 1179, she explicitly distinguishes her original ambitions from her current feelings and states that she truly came to love Victor.[23]
Victor nevertheless refuses to allow love to become permission for self-destruction or stagnation. When Albedo's isolation begins harming both her family relationships and her development as a Dragon Goddess, he sides with [[Scathach]] instead of rescuing Albedo from her punishment and personally threatens to join the training if her progress remains inadequate.[7][10]

#### Evie Moriarty
Evie Moriarty is Albedo's daughter, former student and eventual successor as the principal political authority of the Witches.
Albedo personally taught Evie, but their historical relationship contains far less ordinary maternal warmth than would be expected from most families. Research consistently received more of Albedo's attention than maintaining a conventional relationship with her daughter, one of the social defects [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later identifies in Albedo's character.[2]
The difference between teacher and student remains visible during Arcane's dimensional catastrophe. Evie is an extraordinarily accomplished Witch Queen, yet once the experiment exceeds the younger Witches' ability to control it, Albedo assumes a central role and creates approximately one million interconnected [[Magic]] Circles within seconds.[20]
Their relationship appears considerably more relaxed during the late Imperial Era. Albedo deliberately uses her assignment to the Witch planet as an opportunity to see Evie and openly says that part of the reason she came personally was because Evie is her daughter.[23]
Albedo nevertheless remains intrusive. She becomes convinced Evie should pursue Victor and even attempts to bring the matter before Violet. When Evie clearly explains that being interested in or attracted to Victor does not mean she loves him or wants to become his wife, Albedo eventually accepts the distinction and drops the argument.[17]

#### Nigredo Elderblood
[[Nigredo Elderblood]] is Albedo's daughter with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and one of the clearest examples of the extraordinary second generation created by the Elderblood Family.[8]
Nigredo possesses an exceptional relationship with Darkness and the potential to develop toward a Primordial Goddess of Darkness. Her Eldritch expression can produce a black-hole-like region associated with the collapse of conventional distinctions involving Being and Non-Being.[8]
Albedo's naming reflects her alchemical background. Nigredo is associated with a stage of alchemical transformation, and Albedo considers continuing the pattern by naming another future daughter Rubedo.[8]
Their relationship also exposes Albedo's worst failure as a mother. Nigredo can disregard Albedo's authority while immediately responding to Anna, and the family identifies Albedo's prolonged laboratory isolation as the direct cause.[8] The incident becomes one of the principal motivations behind the intervention culminating in [[Scathach]]'s mandatory training.[13][7]

#### Emily Moriarty
[[Emily Moriarty]] is Albedo's granddaughter through Evie.
Emily was present during the Arcane dimensional experiment and therefore witnessed the absurd scale of her grandmother's [[Magic]], including the million-circle formation created during the portal crisis.[20]
When they reunite during the Lower-Sector operation, Emily respectfully calls Albedo "Grandmother". Albedo immediately tells her to use her name instead, joking that she is much too young to be called a grandmother despite being older than numerous generations of ordinary Humans.[23]
Albedo later becomes interested in Emily's romantic life as well, illustrating that her attempts to become more involved with her descendants can still manifest in an extremely intrusive manner.[24]

#### Scathach Elderblood Scarlett
[[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] is Albedo's fellow wife, disciplinary superior and current martial instructor.
Their philosophies of power are almost opposites. Albedo spent millennia making [[Magic]] increasingly elaborate, abstract and large-scale, whereas [[Scathach]] considers foundations, physical ability, direct Energy control, combat instinct and the capacity to continue fighting when specialized techniques are unavailable to be indispensable.
This difference becomes decisive in Chapter 1172. Scathach destroys formations capable of devastating the planet, crosses Albedo's preferred range before she can properly react and defeats her despite the Witch possessing enormous magical power.[7]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] subsequently places Albedo under Scathach's direct instruction. Her curriculum includes martial fundamentals, physical conditioning, direct Energy manipulation, proper Draconic Rune usage, extreme-gravity training and the combat mentality expected from an Elderblood True Dragon.[10]
The relationship persists rather than ending as a temporary punishment. Chapter 1179 confirms that Albedo is still enduring Scathach's daily regimen, while Chapter 1182 shows her immediately answering Scathach as Master when summoned back to training.[23][17]

#### Anna Elderblood
[[Anna Elderblood]] is one of the primary members of the Elderblood household responsible for correcting Albedo's familial and conceptual stagnation.
Nigredo initially recognizes Anna's authority more readily than that of her own mother because Anna consistently participates in the lives of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters while Albedo historically remained absent.[8]
Anna later provides one of Albedo's most important lessons as a Dragon Goddess. She explains that Albedo's isolation has damaged her imagination and Divine recognition and demonstrates that a Dragon Goddess should increasingly be capable of using her own existence as the medium for supernatural Concepts instead of depending upon an elaborate external symbolic system every time she wants to affect Reality.[10]
Anna specifically critiques Albedo's use of the Tree of Life, explaining that she could use a metaphysically heavier external reference such as Victor or the Universal Tree if necessary, but should ultimately learn to make herself the conceptual foundation of her powers.[10]

#### Hela Elderblood
Albedo and [[Hela Elderblood]] first encountered one another thousands of years before either woman became part of the Elderblood Family. Mortal Albedo entered Helheim as a living Soul and stole supernatural Artifacts for research, an event memorable enough that Hela recognizes her immediately when they meet again.[1]
They later cooperate on [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul-vessel project. Their expertise is explicitly complementary: Hela understands Souls through her relationship with Death, while Albedo possesses superior specialized knowledge concerning the interaction between Souls and living biological structures.[1]

#### Merlin
[[Merlin]] is one of the rare researchers outside Albedo's direct family whose ideas impressed her enough to provoke personal intervention.
Albedo Blessed Merlin after becoming interested in his theory that apparently different supernatural Energies were ultimately expressions of a deeper common foundation. She later regards him as one of her successful research investments.[11]

#### Melida Charlotte Elderblood
[[Melida Charlotte Elderblood]] becomes an unusual source of professional jealousy for Albedo. Despite Albedo's enormous age and accumulated knowledge, Melida demonstrates a vastly more instinctive relationship with Primal Energy and can interact with phenomena that remain difficult for Albedo even after becoming a True Dragon.[8]
Albedo does not respond by resenting Melida personally. The disparity irritates her precisely because she cannot explain it yet, turning Melida's natural talent into another problem Albedo becomes determined to understand.[8]

### Skills & Abilities
Albedo is one of the most accomplished magical researchers originating from Earth. Her current existence combines several thousand years of Witchcraft, [[Alchemy]], Divine ascension, multiple Energy systems, higher cosmological knowledge and the physiology of a True Dragon.
Her late-era raw output is enormously greater than that of her original Ascendant-Goddess existence. Chapter 1172 demonstrates a magical formation capable of destroying the surrounding planet approximately one hundred times over and uses her performance as proof that she has reached the general level expected from a Being capable of destroying an entire Solar System.[7] This should not be confused with equal combat skill: [[Scathach]] defeats her precisely because Albedo's foundations and practical experience lag far behind her raw power.[7][10]

#### True Dragon Physiology
- Supernatural Physical Characteristics: Albedo possesses the vastly enhanced body of a True Dragon. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] specifically reinforces the training dimension because the confrontation involves Albedo and [[Scathach]] as two [[True Dragons]].[7]
- Enhanced Durability: She remains conscious and capable of continuing after Scathach violently drives her hundreds of meters into the ground.[7]
- Enhanced Senses: Her Draconic senses can detect abnormalities and danger around her, including the instinctive warning she receives before Scathach comes to collect her.[13]
- Reactive Adaptation: Albedo's True Dragon biology automatically begins adapting when exposed to Heptagrammakos, although the process is too slow to counter an effect of that quality before Victor intervenes.[7]
- [[Natural Energy]] Compatibility: Scathach explains that True Dragons are naturally compatible with Energy itself and should not require elaborate Runes merely for basic Energy manipulation.[10]
- Imagination-Based Power Expression: Dragon Gods depend heavily upon imagination and conceptual understanding when expressing their power, making creativity a practical component of Albedo's supernatural development.[10]
- Power Suppression: By Chapter 1179, Albedo must deliberately seal part of her own power before entering a Lower Sector because her unrestricted presence is excessive for the environment.[23]
- Extreme Existential Superiority: Even while suppressed in the Lower Sector, Albedo is sufficiently beyond the local standard that Evie acknowledges attacking her would be meaningless.[23]

#### Dragon Language
- Dragon Language: Albedo can speak the supernatural language of Dragons, whose words possess enough metaphysical weight to impose the speaker's intent upon Reality.[7][10]
- Conceptual Invocation: She uses Draconic words corresponding to concepts from the Kabbalistic Tree of Life as the activation structure for her late-era [[Magic]].[7]
- Spell Amplification: Combining Dragon Language with Witchcraft allows the symbolic meaning behind a magical framework to carry greater supernatural weight than a conventional spell formula alone.[7]
- Reality Imposition: The effectiveness of such commands depends upon the user's own Divinity, understanding and existential weight; Albedo's effects therefore fail when she attempts to impose them upon [[Scathach]]'s superior personal Reality.[7]

#### Draconic Runes
- Draconic Rune Manipulation: Albedo can incorporate Draconic structures into her [[Magic]] and large-scale spell formations.[7]
- Conceptual Imposition: [[Scathach]] describes Dragon Runes as a form of supernatural code capable of imposing Concepts or instructions upon Reality.[10]
- Reality Alteration: Albedo integrates Draconic principles with magical formations capable of modifying the environmental conditions surrounding her target.[7]
- Cross-System Spell Construction: Her formations combine Witch Magic Circles, symbolic conceptual structures, Dragon Language and her Divine/Draconic existence within the same supernatural process.[7]
Albedo initially relies on this system excessively. Scathach specifically teaches her that a True Dragon should manipulate ordinary Energy directly and reserve Dragon Runes for circumstances where genuine Conceptual imposition upon Reality is necessary.[10]

#### Divine Physiology
Albedo had already transcended her original Human/Witch existence and become an Ascendant Goddess before later evolving into a True Dragon.
- [[Divine Energy]] Manipulation: Albedo can create and manipulate naturally colorless Divine Energy.[11]
- Divinity: Her directly confirmed Divine identities are [[Alchemy]] and Research.[5][6]
- Blessing Bestowal: Albedo can grant supernatural Blessings capable of altering another being's available supernatural systems. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blessing lets him access Witch-style [[Magic]] despite normal Vampiric incompatibility, while [[Merlin]] was Blessed because his research impressed her.[4][12][11]
- Conceptual Influence: Albedo possesses genuine Divine Concepts capable of interacting with Reality, although their practical influence is weakened by the relatively poor public recognition caused by her prolonged social isolation.[10]
- Divine Recognition Dependency: Chapter 1173 directly establishes that her absence from wider civilization reduced how strongly her Divine Concepts were recognized and therefore how effectively they could express themselves upon Reality.[10]
[[Hecate|Goddess of Magic]] should not presently be indexed as one of Albedo's confirmed principal Divinities merely because she is an extraordinary magician. The novel directly identifies Alchemy and Research, while Magic remains one of her greatest skills rather than a separately confirmed named Divinity.[5][11][6]

##### Alchemy
- [[Alchemy]] Mastery: Albedo is explicitly recognized as the Goddess of Alchemy and possesses several thousand years of research involving alchemical systems.[5][6]
- Biological Modification: She repeatedly modified and experimented upon her own body while attempting to create a superior version of herself.[2]
- Supernatural Biology: Her expertise extends to the structure, alteration and compatibility of supernatural living bodies.[1][2]
- Soul–Body Interaction: Albedo possesses specialized knowledge concerning how Souls interact with living biological vessels.[1]
- Artificial Vessel Design: She participates in developing artificial bodies intended to contain fragments of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Soul without immediately collapsing.[1]
- Clone and Artificial-Life Research: Albedo contributes to Imperial projects involving artificial biological vessels and replicated bodies.[1]
- Eldritch Corruption Mitigation: She investigates methods of preventing the Eldritch/Nightmare qualities carried by fragments of Victor's Soul from destroying or corrupting their host vessels.[1]
- Specialist Supernatural Medical Knowledge: Ariel considers Albedo one of the extremely small number of experts who might be capable of diagnosing [[Fenrir]]'s abnormal condition.[5]
Ariel's recommendation demonstrates the scale of Albedo's expertise but does not establish that Albedo personally healed Fenrir or possesses unrestricted Healing through Alchemy.

##### Research
- Research Divinity: Albedo is explicitly recognized as the Goddess of Research.[6]
- Supernatural Research Mastery: She possesses several thousand years of accumulated research spanning numerous supernatural disciplines.[1][2]
- Scientific Genius: Her demonstrated fields include [[Magic]], [[Alchemy]], Energy theory, supernatural biology, Souls, dimensional phenomena, Divine systems, artificial vessels and cosmology.[11][3][1]
- Information Analysis: Albedo rapidly analyzes unknown supernatural phenomena and constructs theories explaining their mechanics.[3][16]
- Experimental Development: She habitually combines systems other researchers consider unrelated, such as multiple racial Energies, Witchcraft and Draconic conceptual structures.[11][7]
- Cross-System Integration: Albedo combines Witch Magic, Divine power, Dragon Language and symbolic conceptual frameworks inside a single spell architecture.[7]
- Self-Experimentation: Albedo has repeatedly used her own body as experimental material.[2]
- Forbidden Research: Religious, dimensional and political taboos rarely prevent her from investigating something she believes is scientifically valuable.[1][2]
- Research Adaptability: When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] proves that the cosmological assumptions beneath much of her knowledge are incomplete, Albedo becomes fascinated and begins rebuilding her understanding rather than refusing the contradictory evidence.[9]
- Primal Energy Research: By Chapter 1165, she is actively experimenting with Primal Energy despite acknowledging that her practical understanding remains inferior to Melida's instinctive affinity.[8]

###### Tree of Life and the World Tree Archetype
The novel explicitly identifies the system Albedo invokes as the Kabbalistic Tree of Life, but it does not directly state that Albedo is literally summoning one specific planetary World Tree. There is, however, strong internal evidence connecting her chosen Kabbalistic model with the same broader Tree/World Tree archetype that exists physically and metaphysically throughout the setting.
Long before Albedo demonstrates these spells, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] identifies [[Qliphoth]] as a World Tree and describes it from his research as the opposite of "Sephiroth", representing what is Negative rather than Positive.[18] The setting therefore already uses the terminology surrounding the Sephirotic/Qliphothic framework in direct relation to actual [[World Trees]] rather than restricting it entirely to abstract occult symbolism.
[[Roxanne Elderblood]] provides the planetary side of the connection. After [[Scathach]] identifies Roxanne as a young World Tree, she explains that a sufficiently developed World Tree can transform a hostile or lifeless world and establish the conditions required for the planet to support life, comparing Roxanne's fundamental nature with [[Yggdrasil]].[25]
The relationship becomes even more explicit during the later reconstruction of Mars. Because the planet possesses no established World Tree, Victor directly confronts the problem of how to provide the world with the supernatural foundation normally supplied by one. He eventually uses the interconnected World Trees—including Yggdrasil—to establish that planetary foundation and connect Mars with the greater Imperial World Tree network.[26]
Most importantly for Albedo herself, Anna directly links the abstract and cosmological forms while criticizing her spell construction. Anna does not say that using the Tree of Life is inherently invalid; instead, she asks why Albedo is using such a limited external reference when she could select a metaphysically greater one. After suggesting Victor himself, Anna specifically tells her that if she wants to use a Tree, she should use the Universal Tree.[10]
Taken together, Chapters 549, 274, 1172, 1173 and 1178 strongly support interpreting Albedo's Kabbalistic Tree of Life as overlapping with the setting's broader metaphysical World Tree/planetary-life archetype. This interpretation is particularly notable because the first directly demonstrated Kabbalistic formation possesses enough destructive output to destroy its planet around one hundred times over.[7] The connection should nevertheless remain identified as a strongly supported interpretation rather than a confirmed literal identity: the narration has not yet directly stated that "Albedo's Tree of Life is the World Tree of the planet" or that a specific World Tree is being summoned whenever she invokes the Sephirotic names.

#### Energy Manipulation
Chapter 831 directly demonstrates Albedo generating five separate supernatural Energy types at the same time, illustrating both her practical versatility and the energy theory underlying much of her research.[11]
- [[Magic]] Energy Manipulation: Magic is Albedo's oldest and most developed demonstrated Energy system.[11]
- Demonic Energy Manipulation: She can generate Demonic Energy/Miasma despite not being a Demon.[11]
- [[Holy Energy]] Manipulation: Albedo produces Holy Energy normally associated with Positive or Heavenly existences.[11]
- Nature Energy Manipulation: She generates the green Nature Energy used by supernatural races such as [[Werewolves]] and Youkai.[11]
- [[Divine Energy]] Manipulation: As a Goddess, she generates naturally colorless Divine Energy and explains how a God's nature subsequently gives that Energy its characteristics.[11]
- Simultaneous Multi-Energy Generation: Albedo demonstrates all five known Energy types simultaneously rather than switching between them one at a time.[11]
- Energy Analysis: She understands apparently incompatible supernatural Energies as different expressions of deeper underlying principles.[11]
- Direct Energy Manipulation (Developing): [[Scathach]] retrains Albedo to manipulate Energy naturally through her True Dragon existence instead of automatically converting every action into a Magic Circle or Rune system.[10]
Albedo's demonstration proves control over several major Energy systems but should not be generalized into unrestricted mastery over every form of Energy in existence.

##### Primal Energy (Developing)
- Primal Energy Interaction: By Chapter 1165, Albedo can experimentally interact with Primal Energy, although doing so remains extremely difficult even after becoming a Dragon.[8]
- Primal Energy Analysis: She actively studies the Energy's structure and attempts to understand why it behaves differently from conventional systems.[8]
- Developing Manipulation: Her experiments demonstrate limited practical interaction rather than complete mastery.[8]
- Inferior Instinctive Affinity: Albedo openly recognizes that Melida possesses a vastly superior natural understanding of Primal Energy at this stage.[8]
Primal Energy should therefore be indexed as a major developing field of research rather than one of Albedo's fully mastered Energy systems.

- Energy Perception: She can distinguish and analyze different supernatural Energies by their properties.[11]

- Emotional/Mood Perception: In the late Imperial Era, Albedo claims sufficient sensory acuity to recognize the emotional interest of members of her family, although her interpretation of those emotions can still be socially incorrect.[24][17]

- Cosmological Knowledge: Albedo understands the broad existence of cosmic Sectors and the protections restricting interference with less-developed regions before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later Imperial expansion makes such knowledge commonplace within his faction.[16]
- Akashic Record Application: Her Heroic Class [[Magic]] can convert mythological information preserved within the [[Akashic Records]] into functional supernatural manifestations.[20]

- Higher-Reality Perception: Victor trains Albedo to examine unfamiliar phenomena without automatically reducing them to interpretations supplied by familiar Witch senses or magical theories.[9]
- Language of the Universe Comprehension: Following Victor's instruction, Albedo becomes capable of understanding him while he speaks the Language of the Universe.[9]

Understanding the Language of the Universe does not automatically give Albedo the Authority or cosmic administrative position belonging to the [[Regulators of Existence]]. The feat demonstrates comprehension and expanded perception, not ownership of their functions.

- Magical Civilization Creation: Arcane itself demonstrates that Albedo's abilities extend beyond isolated spellcraft into establishing supernatural environments capable of sustaining an entire Witch civilization.[3]
Creating Arcane should not be used as evidence that Albedo independently created an entire Universe from nothing. The series treats supernatural territories, dimensions, worlds and full Universes as distinct scales of creation.

- Soul–Body Compatibility Analysis: Her specialty is understanding how Souls interact with living biological structures.[1]

- Weapon Materialization: Albedo directly materializes a sword during her battle with [[Scathach]].[7]

#### Extreme Gravity Conditioning
Albedo's physical training is specifically designed to force the body of a True Dragon to develop alongside the magical abilities upon which she historically relied.
- Gravity Resistance: Albedo can remain functional while wearing four [[Gravity Bracelets]], one attached to each wrist and ankle.[10]
- Astronomical Gravity Conditioning: The bracelets reproduce gravitational conditions associated with astronomical bodies rather than simple ordinary training multipliers.[10]
- Stellar-Class Training: Albedo is capable of enduring approximately stellar-level conditions during this stage of her training.[10]
- Progressive Adaptation: [[Scathach]] intends to increase the burden toward neutron-star gravity as Albedo's True Dragon body develops.[10]
- [[Black Hole|Singularity]]-Level Limit (At Chapter 1173): The maximum black-hole/singularity setting remains capable of crushing Albedo during this stage, establishing a clear current limit rather than implying unrestricted gravitational resistance.[10]
- Continuous Conditioning: Chapter 1179 confirms that the training continues well beyond the original disciplinary incident.[23]

#### Resistances and Adaptation
- Reality-Alteration Adaptation (Developing): Albedo's True Dragon body automatically begins adapting after [[Scathach]] freezes the surrounding Reality through Heptagrammakos, although the process cannot overcome the technique quickly enough to free her before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] intervenes.[7]
- Extreme Impact Resistance: Albedo survives Scathach driving her hundreds of meters into the ground and remains capable of continuing the confrontation.[7]
- Extreme Gravity Resistance: Her body is progressively conditioned under gravity corresponding to stellar and eventually greater astronomical environments.[10]
- Mental Caution Against Higher Information: Albedo understands that certain late-era cosmic objects contain information dangerous enough to injure or overwhelm lesser minds, warning Evie not to attempt to comprehend the merger sphere directly.[23]

### Current Power
Albedo's present raw power enormously surpasses the Ascendant Goddess introduced during the Arcane period.
- Planetary Destructive Output: Her Keter, Hokhmah, Netsah formation contains enough combined destructive power to destroy the surrounding planet approximately one hundred times over.[7]
- Solar-System-Class General Level: The narration uses the same battle to establish that Albedo has reached the broader class of power associated with destroying an entire Solar System.[7]
- True Dragon Existence: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] explicitly reinforces the training dimension for a confrontation involving two [[True Dragons]], identifying Albedo's current racial level rather than treating her merely as a powerful Witch Goddess.[7]
- Reality-Altering [[Magic]]: Her second Kabbalistic formation can invert local Reality, transform the environment, poison its atmosphere and continuously impose weakening effects.[7]
- Lower-Sector Presence Suppression: By Chapter 1179, Albedo must suppress herself simply to enter a Lower Sector without allowing her unrestricted presence to overwhelm the environment.[23]
- Continuous Development: Her mandatory training remains active through Chapters 1179–1182, meaning the performance demonstrated immediately before that training should not automatically be treated as the permanent upper limit of her later combat ability.[23][17]
Albedo privately calls herself the weakest Dragon among the Elderblood Dragons participating in the late Imperial operation, but this comparison is relative to an abnormal family containing beings such as Victor, [[Scathach]], Anna, Natalia, Jeanne, Zaladrac and their descendants. Her need to suppress herself merely to operate inside a Lower Sector demonstrates that "weak" within the Elderblood hierarchy is radically different from weakness by ordinary supernatural standards.[23]
Raw power should likewise not be confused with combat effectiveness. Scathach overwhelmingly defeats Albedo's sophisticated formations because Albedo spent millennia developing Magic while neglecting movement, close combat, direct Energy control, physical conditioning and the foundations required to apply that power against another top-level Being.[7][10]

### Weaknesses and Limitations
- Excessive Reliance on [[Magic]]: Albedo historically attempts to answer threats through increasingly elaborate spell structures even when a simpler direct response would be faster and more efficient.[10]
- Poor Close-Quarters Fundamentals: Her melee ability is severely underdeveloped relative to the power of her Dragon-Goddess body, allowing [[Scathach]] to exploit close range almost immediately.[7][10]
- Casting Dependency: Large and sophisticated formations require Albedo enough opportunity to construct or activate them; extremely fast high-level opponents may not permit that opportunity.[7][10]
- Conceptual Crutches: The sword, Tree of Life and other external frameworks initially function as aids Albedo relies upon to visualize truths that Anna believes a properly developed Dragon Goddess should increasingly express through herself.[10]
- Weak Divine Recognition: Millennia of isolation leave Albedo's Divine Concepts with comparatively poor recognition among the wider population, limiting their practical influence upon Reality compared with more publicly established Goddesses.[10]
- Research Obsession: Albedo can neglect danger, family, social development and even her own training when an experiment captures her attention.[8][13]
- Family Neglect: Her historical absence becomes severe enough that Nigredo initially possesses little respect for Albedo's parental authority.[8]
- Limited Primal Energy Mastery: Despite intense experimentation, Albedo remains far below Melida's instinctive understanding of Primal Energy during Chapter 1165.[8]
- Hierarchical Reality Manipulation: Her ability to modify Reality is not absolute; a sufficiently superior existence can reject conditions she attempts to impose upon them.[7]
- Extreme Gravity Limit (Developing): [[Black Hole|Singularity]]-level gravity remains beyond what Albedo can safely endure during Chapter 1173.[10]
- Distorted Scale of Comparison: Prolonged exposure to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and the Elderblood Family causes Albedo's perception of what constitutes an ordinary powerful person to become unreliable, something she personally realizes after comparing Evie with Nigredo and Victor's other daughters.[23]

- Albedo entered contracts with ancient Demons and later killed some of them after they ceased being useful to her. Victor eventually obtained one of her Original Strategic-Class spells from Demonic memories.[19]
- Her Blessing affected Victor hundreds of chapters before Albedo personally debuted in Chapter 829.[4][14]
- She also Blessed [[Merlin]] because his ideas concerning the underlying unity of supernatural Energy interested her.[11]
- Albedo historically spent almost all of her available time researching and only a comparatively small portion consuming Human entertainment and other information, explaining why she can still understand modern fictional references despite her age and isolation.[11]
- Her Heroic Class Magic uses myths preserved within the [[Akashic Records]] as supernatural information.[20]
- One of Albedo's Original Strategic-Class spells is recognized by Seth during Victor's conquest of [[Olympus]].[19]
- Victor's later ability to use the modified formation to interfere with the [[Greek Underworld]]'s System originates from Victor's additions and should not be automatically assigned to Albedo's original spell.[19]
- Albedo repeatedly experimented upon her own body while attempting to create a superior version of herself.[2]
- Victor's "frog at the bottom of a well" lesson is specifically aimed at the difference between possessing enormous accumulated knowledge and actually having a sufficiently broad perspective from which to understand the greater Cosmos.[9]
- Albedo's exact transformation scene into a True Dragon has not yet been tied to one precise chapter in the available material; she is already explicitly Draconic by Chapter 1165 and directly identified as a True Dragon/Dragon Goddess during Chapter 1172.[8][7]
- Albedo and Victor's daughter is [[Nigredo Elderblood]].[8]
- Nigredo's name follows an alchemical naming theme, and Albedo considers naming another possible daughter Rubedo.[8]
- Despite becoming a Dragon Goddess, Albedo continues dressing in the recognizable style of an ancient Witch.[8]
- Primal Energy is one of Albedo's major late-era research subjects.[8]
- Albedo is jealous of Melida's natural Primal Energy affinity but converts that jealousy into research motivation rather than hostility.[8]
- Her Kabbalistic combat Magic directly invokes names belonging to the Tree of Life while combining them with Dragon Language and enormous networks of Magic Circles.[7]
- The setting separately connects Kabbalistic terminology with actual [[World Trees]]: [[Qliphoth]] is explicitly identified as a World Tree and described as the Negative opposite of "Sephiroth", while World Trees such as Roxanne and [[Yggdrasil]] function as supernatural foundations for planetary life.[18][25][26]
- Anna later tells Albedo that if she insists upon using a Tree as an external conceptual medium, she could use the Universal Tree, reinforcing the thematic connection between Albedo's abstract Tree-of-Life framework and the setting's greater Tree archetype.[10]
- The novel has not yet explicitly stated that Albedo's Kabbalistic Tree of Life is literally identical to one particular planetary World Tree; that equivalence remains a strongly supported interpretation rather than a directly confirmed statement.
- Chapter 1172 contains the first major direct combat demonstration of Albedo after she becomes a True Dragon.
- Her first displayed Kabbalistic formation possesses enough power to destroy its planet approximately one hundred times over.[7]
- Chapter 1173 reveals that the sword Albedo created during the battle functioned primarily as a magical/conceptual aid rather than a weapon she knew how to use properly in close combat.[10]
- Victor temporarily removes Albedo's unrestricted laboratory privileges and orders her to train with [[Scathach]] every day.[10]
- Albedo trains with four [[Gravity Bracelets]] simultaneously, one attached to each wrist and ankle.[10]
- During Chapter 1173, she is capable of enduring approximately stellar-class gravitational training while progressing toward neutron-star conditions; the maximum singularity setting remains beyond her current limit.[10]
- [[Siren Elderblood]] having already endured the singularity setting is what finally convinces Albedo to stop treating Scathach's expectations as impossible.[10]
- Chapter 1179 confirms that Scathach's training continued well beyond the original punishment.[23]
- Albedo can disguise herself and teleport across large distances, both directly demonstrated during her visit to the Lower-Sector Witch civilization.[23]
- While surveying that world, she can perceive magical blood spreading through its population and detect Demigods among its inhabitants.[23]
- Although Albedo regards herself as comparatively weak among the Elderblood Dragons, she must still seal part of her existence to enter the Lower Sector safely.[23]
- Albedo is entrusted with one of the special objects used during the Sector-merging operation alongside Ophis and Tasha.[23]
- Emily calling her "Grandmother" irritates Albedo only because she considers herself much too young to be addressed that way despite being chronologically ancient.[23]
- By Chapter 1179, Albedo openly admits that although her original interest in Victor included ambition and opportunity, she genuinely came to love him.[23]
- In Chapter 1182, Albedo ultimately accepts Evie's distinction between simply being attracted to Victor and actually wanting a romantic relationship with him, showing that her social judgment remains imperfect but is becoming more flexible.[17]
- By the same period, Albedo addresses Scathach as Master and immediately returns when called for continued training.[17]
