Scathach Elderblood Scarlett, formerly Scathach Scarlett and Scathach Alucard Scarlett, is one of the principal characters of [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]]. She is the ancient matriarch of Clan Scarlett, the original martial master and later wife of Victor Elderblood, the biological mother of [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] and [[Siren Elderblood]], and the adoptive mother of [[Siena Scarlett Elderblood]], [[Lacus Scarlett Elderblood]] and [[Pepper Scarlett Elderblood]]. Originally one of the most feared Vampire Countesses in [[Nightingale]], she subsequently became the Ancestor of the Red Dragons and eventually assumed command of the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s Armed Forces.[6][3][2]
Long before Victor entered supernatural society, Scathach had already spent more than two thousand years pursuing martial perfection. Her extraordinary experience, discipline and personal strength earned her recognition as the Strongest Female Vampire, a reputation that distinguished her even among the ancient Countesses serving beneath [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]]. Despite her political status, Scathach possessed little interest in ordinary administration and preferred pursuing powerful opponents, training exceptional individuals and developing her own understanding of combat.[4]
Historically, Clan Scarlett served as the military spear of Nightingale, responsible for confronting enemies and conducting counterattacks during major conflicts. However, Scathach's personal philosophy made her reluctant to maintain a conventional military organization. She generally considered the instruction of large numbers of unexceptional soldiers less worthwhile than personally developing a smaller number of promising warriors, leaving her territory comparatively isolated and her household much smaller than those of other Count families.[7]
Her philosophy originated partly from the training she received from [[Dun Scaith Elderblood]], an ancient Runemaster and warrior who instructed her during an earlier period of her life when she was known as Siren. Dun emphasized the importance of surviving without relying exclusively upon supernatural advantages, forcing Scathach to develop practical technique, judgment and self-control under conditions where her natural Vampire strength could not compensate for mistakes. Scathach eventually adopted these principles as the foundation of her own teaching.[1]
Scathach's life changed dramatically after meeting Victor, the newly transformed husband of Ruby, Violet and Sasha. Although Victor initially possessed only a fraction of her experience and strength, his response to her overwhelming presence immediately attracted her attention. Rather than surrender to his fear, he forced himself to regain control and became excited by the possibility of eventually challenging her. Scathach recognized the same desire for improvement that had defined her own existence and personally undertook his training.[8]
Their relationship subsequently developed from master and disciple into mutual attraction, rivalry and marriage. Victor eventually surpassed the limitations of Scathach's original Vampire existence, but the martial foundations she established remained important throughout his development. Their relationship also became reciprocal when Victor evolved into the Blood Dragon Primogenitor and reconstructed Scathach as the Ancestor of the Red Dragons, allowing her to acquire a superior supernatural foundation while preserving her accumulated experience, personal identity and martial achievements.[3][9]
Scathach's transformation into a genuine True Dragon eventually led to her development as a Dragon Goddess. By the later stages of the story, her established Divine authorities reflected the Concepts of War, Teaching and Ice, corresponding to the qualities she had cultivated throughout her original existence.[10]
The establishment of the Elderblood Empire expanded Scathach's responsibilities beyond the leadership of Clan Scarlett. She became the Supreme General and subsequently the Supreme Commander of the Empire's Armed Forces, overseeing military doctrine, the preparation of Imperial forces and the training of enormous numbers of soldiers. During the Empire's accelerated development, an entire planet was dedicated to military instruction beneath her authority, with one documented intake containing approximately 100 million recruits.[2][11]
Despite acquiring extraordinary status and responsibilities, Scathach remains fundamentally devoted to the pursuit of martial perfection. She continues training personally and supervising exceptional students, including members of the younger Elderblood generation. Her severe methods have made her one of the most feared instructors within Victor's family, but she regards discipline as essential when raising children whose natural supernatural potential can surpass that of established Gods and ancient civilizations.

### Background
Little is known about [[Scathach]]'s biological parents or earliest childhood. She was born more than two thousand years before the beginning of the story, during an era characterized by extensive warfare and supernatural conflict. These circumstances strongly influenced her personality, eventually producing a woman who regarded strength, determination and practical experience as essential qualities.[6]
During an earlier period of her life, Scathach became the disciple of [[Dun Scaith Elderblood]], a legendary warrior and Runemaster. She was then known as Siren, a name she later abandoned after completing her apprenticeship. The novel establishes that Siren belonged to an earlier stage of her life, but does not conclusively establish whether it was her literal birth name or an identity associated with her training.[1]
Dun's instruction was deliberately severe. Rather than allow Scathach's Vampire physiology to compensate for every mistake, she employed Limiter Runes to restrict her physical strength and forced her to confront dangerous opponents without relying upon supernatural superiority. One remembered exercise involved reducing Scathach's strength to approximately Human levels before sending her to hunt wild bears. Scathach suffered a humiliating defeat, but the experience taught her that natural advantages could become weaknesses when a warrior never learned to survive without them.[1]
Scathach subsequently spent centuries refining her fighting abilities through genuine warfare. Her reputation eventually spread throughout supernatural society, earning her recognition as one of the most dangerous Vampires in existence. During her long life, she also encountered important historical figures, including Jesus, whose unusual potential attracted her interest. She attempted to recruit him as a disciple, but he declined because his responsibilities prevented him from accepting her instruction.[6]
Scathach eventually became one of [[Nightingale]]'s ancient Countesses and assumed leadership of Clan Scarlett. Her territory differed considerably from those governed by the other Count Clans because she possessed little interest in maintaining conventional cities or large administrative organizations. Rather than build an extensive army of ordinary subordinates, she preferred operating independently and training individuals who demonstrated exceptional potential.
Approximately one thousand years before the main story, Scathach's relationship with [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]] deteriorated when the Vampire King decided that she was too uncontrollable for one of his plans. Using supernatural powers associated with [[Clan Alioth]], he imprisoned her within a hostile isolated world for approximately twenty years. Scathach survived the experience and became stronger, but the decision damaged the trust she had previously placed in Vlad.[23]
Scathach eventually established a family through adoption. She accepted Siena, Lacus and Pepper into Clan Scarlett and raised them as her daughters, treating the relationships as genuine despite the absence of ordinary biological descent. Their individual personalities and interests differed considerably, but Scathach remained responsible for their upbringing and training.
Approximately two decades before the beginning of the story, Scathach decided that she wanted a biological daughter. She selected a Vampire whose Bloodline was compatible with her own and entered a coercive relationship intended to produce a child. She also performed a Vampire marriage ritual with him as part of the arrangement. After confirming that she was pregnant, Scathach killed the man because she considered his purpose fulfilled.
The surviving ritual could not be safely dissolved during pregnancy without risking harm to the developing child. Scathach therefore allowed the connection to remain active until Ruby's birth. However, seeing her daughter caused Scathach to recognize the emotional significance of the man she had treated as expendable. Her guilt prevented her from immediately severing the remaining ritual, and she eventually allowed the resulting bloodlust to continue as both punishment for her earlier actions and another form of personal training.
Ruby grew up within Clan Scarlett and inherited its extraordinary Ice affinity. Her existence became one of Scathach's most important emotional attachments, although her mother's severe personality and difficulty expressing ordinary affection sometimes made their relationship complicated.
By the beginning of the story, Scathach had accumulated thousands of years of martial experience, raised four daughters, developed a complicated relationship with Vlad and established a reputation feared throughout supernatural society. Her life changed dramatically after encountering [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], whose unusual mentality and potential provided the challenge and companionship she had spent centuries seeking.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is [[Scathach]]'s husband, former disciple, principal training partner and the father of their daughter Siren.
Their relationship began through Ruby, but Scathach quickly became interested in Victor independently of his marriage to her daughter. His reaction to overwhelming danger demonstrated the qualities she admired most: determination, ambition and the willingness to confront challenges regardless of the difference in strength.[8]
Scathach became his first true martial master and established the foundations of his supernatural fighting style. Her instruction emphasized discipline, movement, control, pain tolerance, battlefield awareness and the necessity of continuing to improve regardless of how much natural power an individual possessed.
Victor eventually incorporated those lessons into increasingly advanced systems of his own, but Scathach's philosophy remained central to his development.
Their relationship gradually became romantic. Scathach initially struggled to recognize the depth of her feelings because she possessed little experience with genuine emotional vulnerability, but she eventually acknowledged that she wanted to become more than Victor's master or mother-in-law.[19]
Their extended martial confrontation following Victor's development as a Progenitor represented an important milestone. The two could finally exchange meaningful attacks without Scathach needing to restrain herself to the same degree as during his early training.[58][59]
Victor later transformed Scathach into the Ancestor of the Red Dragons, allowing her to acquire a superior supernatural foundation without losing the experience she had accumulated throughout her earlier life.[3]
The transformation reversed their original teaching relationship in certain areas. Victor possessed knowledge of True Dragon physiology that Scathach lacked and personally instructed her in the martial principles necessary to control her new body.[9]
Their relationship remains reciprocal. Both continue seeking opportunities to challenge one another, and whichever possesses knowledge the other lacks can temporarily assume the position of teacher.
Victor is also one of the few individuals capable of safely redirecting Scathach when her battle enthusiasm becomes excessive. He demonstrated this during her confrontation with Albedo, intervening before the training escalated into catastrophic destruction and provoking her into fighting him instead.
Their marriage eventually produced [[Siren Elderblood]], a daughter whose personality reflects the enthusiasm for combat and self-improvement shared by both parents.

#### Dun Scaith Elderblood
[[Dun Scaith Elderblood]] is [[Scathach]]'s original master and one of the most important influences upon her philosophy.
Dun deliberately deprived Scathach of the advantages associated with Vampire physiology during training, forcing her to develop technique, judgment and survival instincts rather than rely entirely upon superior physical strength or regeneration.
The older woman also taught Scathach advanced Runic principles and encouraged an approach to combat emphasizing control, adaptation and the elimination of unnecessary dependencies.[1]
Scathach continues respecting Dun despite becoming a Dragon Goddess. She recognizes that her master possesses extraordinary knowledge and remains capable of teaching her unfamiliar supernatural principles.
Dun is also among the few individuals capable of embarrassing Scathach through stories concerning the period when she was known as Siren.

#### Ruby Elderblood Scarlett
[[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] is [[Scathach]]'s biological daughter and one of the most important emotional attachments within her life.
Scathach deliberately sought a biological child and selected a Vampire possessing compatible supernatural characteristics as Ruby's father. Her treatment of the man was ruthless, but Ruby's birth awakened feelings of guilt that caused Scathach to preserve the surviving marriage ritual rather than sever it immediately after the pregnancy.
Ruby grew up within Clan Scarlett and inherited the extraordinary Ice affinity associated with her mother. Their relationship was complicated by Scathach's severe temperament and difficulty expressing affection in ordinary ways, but Scathach remained intensely protective of her.
The Inquisition's attack against Ruby demonstrated the depth of that attachment. Scathach retaliated against the Vatican because the organization had already been warned not to endanger her daughter.[6]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s arrival helped improve their relationship. Ruby gradually discovered that her mother could behave more openly when she was no longer burdened by the same bloodlust and emotional isolation. The change allowed them to interact more comfortably as family rather than exclusively through the strict relationship between Countess and heiress.[17]
Ruby eventually developed interests extending beyond her mother's military specialization, particularly in science and supernatural research. Scathach accepted that her daughter would pursue an independent path while continuing to regard her as one of the most important people in her life.

#### Siena Scarlett Elderblood
[[Siena Scarlett Elderblood]] is one of [[Scathach]]'s adopted daughters and a member of the original Scarlett household.
Scathach accepted Siena into her family through the Vampire adoption ritual and raised her according to the demanding standards applied to the other Scarlett daughters. Their relationship is characterized by genuine maternal attachment alongside repeated disagreements concerning discipline and personal behavior.
Scathach considers Siena capable of substantial development but becomes frustrated when she grows complacent. During an early visit to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s underground residence, she determined that Siena had become too comfortable and immediately resumed corrective training.[33]
Although their personalities can clash, Scathach does not regard Siena as less important than her biological children. Their familial relationship remained intact throughout the later development of the Elderblood dynasty.

#### Rose Adrasteia Elderblood
[[Rose Adrasteia Elderblood]] is another accomplished martial artist whose development can be compared with [[Scathach]]'s.

#### Albedo Moriarty Elderblood
[[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]] became one of [[Scathach]]'s most important late-era disciples after her prolonged isolation within laboratories caused her to neglect family responsibilities and broader combat development.
The senior mothers concluded that Albedo required intervention because she had allowed her research to dominate nearly every aspect of her life. Her relationship with Nigredo had deteriorated, and her habits had become increasingly incompatible with the expectations placed upon a member of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family.
Scathach accepted responsibility for correcting the situation and brought Albedo into a fortified training dimension. Their initial confrontation demonstrated that Albedo possessed extraordinary supernatural power but remained overly dependent upon complex [[Magic]] and had neglected fundamental physical discipline.
The session escalated when Scathach became increasingly excited by the challenge and attempted an attack that Victor considered inappropriate for training a family member. He intervened, prevented the confrontation from causing catastrophic damage and redirected Scathach's attention toward a challenge with himself.[85]
Albedo eventually accepted Scathach as her master and began a prolonged period of demanding instruction. The training continued through the later Imperial chapters, with Albedo eventually addressing Scathach as Master instinctively whenever summoned.
Their relationship illustrates Scathach's belief that even an extremely powerful Dragon Goddess may need to rebuild her fundamentals when years of specialization have created avoidable weaknesses.

#### Leone Elena Luna Elderblood
[[Leone Elena Luna Elderblood]] is the biological daughter of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]]. Her full name is explicitly established during her family interactions.[76]
Leone developed an ambition to become one of the most dangerous combatants within Dragons Nest and specifically identified [[Scathach]] as the standard she wanted to reach. Leona recognized the difficulty of the goal because Scathach possessed enormous accumulated experience and had continued training throughout the Empire's accelerated development even when progress became increasingly slow.
Rather than discourage Leone, Leona arranged for her daughter to receive personal instruction. Scathach enthusiastically accepted the opportunity and began an intensive year of training.
Leone's determination made her particularly appealing to Scathach because the young Dragon sought difficult instruction voluntarily rather than viewing it solely as punishment.

#### The Elderblood Daughters
[[Scathach]] is one of the principal maternal figures responsible for the martial education and discipline of the younger Elderblood generation.
Her influence extends far beyond Ruby, Siena, Lacus, Pepper and Siren. Numerous Imperial Princesses have undergone training beneath her, and many regard the possibility of additional instruction as one of the most severe punishments available within the household.[79]
Scathach nevertheless regards the training as essential. Many of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s daughters possess extraordinary supernatural abilities from birth, creating the possibility that an immature decision could harm other people on an enormous scale. She therefore considers emotional maturity, self-control and responsibility more important than simply increasing the amount of power a child can release.
Her recommendation concerning Moira demonstrates this approach. Despite the girl's extraordinary relationship with Luck, Scathach concluded that her earliest education needed to concentrate upon the control of thoughts, emotions and supernatural abilities rather than conventional strength.[87]
Scathach also belongs to the group of senior mothers who maintain restrictions upon Victor's tendency to spoil his daughters. They recognize that his affection can undermine the consistent discipline necessary for children whose natural potential already exceeds the standards of most supernatural civilizations.

### Role in the Elderblood Empire
[[Scathach]] occupies the highest dedicated military command position beneath [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] within the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Her title developed from Supreme General to Supreme Commander of the Empire's Armed Forces, reflecting the expansion of the military institutions under her authority.[2]
Her responsibilities extend across military training, the preparation of Imperial forces, the supervision of elite personnel and the maintenance of military standards within a civilization containing numerous different [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] and supernatural power systems.
The scale of the position differs enormously from her former role as a Vampire Countess. Within [[Nightingale]], Scathach governed a powerful Clan and possessed the authority to participate in conflicts involving the principal Vampire families. Within the Elderblood Empire, she became responsible for military institutions operating on a vastly greater scale.
During the accelerated Imperial Era, an entire planet was dedicated to training new soldiers. Its facilities included extensive fortifications, arenas, training fields and logistical infrastructure capable of supporting enormous numbers of recruits. One documented intake contained approximately 100 million individuals, demonstrating the extraordinary administrative responsibilities associated with her office.[11]
Scathach's authority also extends to the training of Divine combatants. Her military position reflects the fact that the Imperial Armed Forces include Gods and other exceptionally powerful supernatural beings rather than ordinary military personnel alone.
Despite those responsibilities, Scathach does not grant formal discipleship indiscriminately. Individuals may receive ordinary military instruction or be assigned to her training programs without automatically becoming recognized personal disciples. She reserves that status for those who demonstrate the talent, ambition and determination she considers worthy of exceptional attention.
Her military role is closely connected to her responsibility within the Elderblood household. The younger generation contains numerous children whose natural abilities are dangerous enough to require specialized instruction, while several adult members of the family possess extraordinary power without equivalent experience in every aspect of combat.
Scathach therefore functions as both a military commander and a disciplinary authority. Her position requires her to identify weaknesses, develop appropriate instruction and ensure that individuals possessing overwhelming supernatural power can use it responsibly.
Her authority is not merely ceremonial. She continues personally participating in training, supervising difficult students and evaluating the effectiveness of the principles she expects the Imperial military to follow.
The position also reflects the enormous transformation of her life. Scathach once served as a Countess beneath [[Vlad III Tepes|Vlad Tepes]], but Victor's rise established a new civilization in which her accumulated experience could influence the development of entire armies and generations of supernatural combatants.

### Abilities

#### Powers & Abilities
Scathach Elderblood Scarlett is the Ancestor of the Red Dragons, a Blood Dragon, a genuine True Dragon, a Dragon Goddess possessing the confirmed Divinities of War, Teaching and Ice, and one of the most accomplished martial artists and combat-specialized Runemasters within the [[Elderblood Empire]]. Originally recognized as the Strongest Female Vampire, [[Scathach]] had already spent more than two thousand years refining her [[Martial Arts]], elemental abilities, Runecraft and combat experience before [[Victor Elderblood]] reconstructed her existence through his Blood Dragon lineage. The transformation replaced the biological limitations of her former Noble Vampire body with a fundamentally superior Draconic foundation while preserving the powers, knowledge and experience she had accumulated throughout her life.[1][2]
Red Dragon Ancestor
Scathach's greatest advantage is the integration of overwhelming supernatural power with extraordinary technical proficiency. Rather than depending exclusively upon raw Energy, she combines Grandmaster-level Martial Arts, elemental manipulation, conventional Runes, Draconic Runes, Sinister or Eldritch Runes, Divine authority and an exceptionally refined understanding of combat. She specializes in identifying the weaknesses of an opponent's fighting style and neutralizing them through precise application of force, supernatural Energy or Runic effects. Her mature combat system is sufficiently advanced to interfere with Reality itself, allowing her to confront opponents whose abilities would overwhelm conventional supernatural warriors.[3][4][5]
During her confrontation with [[Albedo Moriarty Elderblood]], Scathach demonstrated the effectiveness of this approach by destroying thousands of powerful Draconic [[Magic]] Circles with a controlled sweep of her spear, forcing Albedo into close combat and resisting attempts to impose hostile supernatural conditions upon her personal Reality. Her subsequent invocation of Heptagrammakos demonstrated the enormous development of her Runecraft, freezing the Reality of an entire training dimension and suppressing another Dragon Goddess before Victor intervened.[5][6]
By the later Imperial Era, Scathach had accumulated more than two hundred thousand subjective years of experience through the Empire's accelerated development. She was included among the exceptional combatants associated with the Empire's V-06 Galactic Threat Classification, a category encompassing beings with the overall capability to threaten or annihilate multiple galaxies. The classification represents the potential of her mature existence rather than establishing that every ordinary attack she performs produces destruction on that scale.[7]
Scathach's practical lethality was also emphasized by [[Leona Elizabeth Elderblood Lykos]], who considered her among the most dangerous combatants within Dragons Nest. Leona distinguished Scathach's extraordinary experience and technical proficiency from raw Energy reserves, regarding her as a particularly dangerous opponent even when compared with certain individuals possessing greater supernatural power. This evaluation reflects Leona's judgment rather than an absolute ranking applicable to every possible confrontation.[8]

##### Heptagrammakos
Look! Creation is beautiful, and everything begins with an explosion.
—Scathach Elderblood Scarlett, Chapter 1209
Heptagrammakos is one runic sentence in the Draconic language. The words are not a name stuck onto a spell. They become reality. [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] reads them as seven signs that begin creation. Hepta is seven. Gramma is a letter, an inscription, and an indelible trace. Kos is the cosmos, the beginning of creation. Spoken through, the sentence runs that beginning as a weapon: a local Big Bang, held inside a boundary Scathach Elderblood Scarlett sets. Her creation keeps the expansion going, so the wave does not thin out with distance.[5][38]
It is the later form of Three Steps to Creation. As a vampire count she joined absolute control of ice to ancient runes, and that older form could destroy a continent without an army. She was not satisfied with it. What she speaks now is Draconic and Eldritch together. Violet Elderblood names the sinister runes the first time the sentence is spoken. On the battlefield the Dragon runes and the Eldritch runes are already burning along her spear when the sentence leaves her mouth. The spear was made by [[Victor Elderblood]]. When the bang begins, the spear is gone.[5][39][38]
The sentence freezes the reality it takes and replaces it. The place becomes a total, primeval dark. Nothing in it can be seen. Thousands of eyes and mouths open in that dark, and the eyes glow. Anyone caught in it takes a throbbing head, and mental stability drops. Movement stops. [[Magic]] stops. A True Dragon body starts adapting at once and still cannot keep up. The runes by themselves would take an absurd amount of energy. Her control is why that cost does not appear on her. She is still standing at the center when a finished cast ends.[5][38]
The space is then crushed, as if a piece of the universe were being forced into one point. Loose matter is dragged into the core. A point of light appears there, small enough to pass for a distant star, and brightens until lines of light cross the void. The point goes out. For a fraction of a moment there is no light, no movement, and no expansion.
The bang follows from that dark. It is not a sphere she throws, and it is not an ordinary explosion that weakens as it travels. A sphere of light appears where the emptiness was. Space expands with it in every direction, faster than ordinary perception. The wave is primordial energy, and it carries matter, heat, light, and radiation. Distances stretch. Gravitational fields break. The orbits of neighboring bodies change. Regions are swallowed. Inside the boundary she chose, matter is destroyed and made again, and the skin of the growing sphere looks like the birth of a universe. That birth is the weapon. She does not keep a universe afterward.[38][40]
The first wave takes armor apart before the wearer can move, then the body, down to atoms and then to elementary particles. Weapons go with them. Nothing is left from which those bodies can be put back together. [[Aetheryon]], watching the dark unfold, treats the attack as one that destroys souls and makes a later resurrection impossible. What is left on that ground, once the wave subsides, is residual energy of creation.[38]
A finished speaking inside the training dimension destroys the dimension. The planet under it is badly damaged and killed. Between the galaxies, uncut, the chain reaches distant galaxies. Infinity names it as Creation used as a weapon. If the Primordial Beings had not cut it short, the chain would have erased hundreds of galaxies in that region. A galaxy of the Upper Sector is hundreds of times larger than a galaxy of the middle or lower sectors. Victor Elderblood is who stops the sentence the first time, on the word before "explosion," and who holds the training dimension together while it is spoken. Limbo and Infinity are who limit the spread of the finished cast. The [[World Trees]] are not part of the technique. They buy the lives after the bodies are already gone.[5][6][38][40]
- Existence Erasure: The first wave erases the armor, the weapons, and the bodies of [[Laelzorith]], [[Dravakar]], and [[Seraphel]]. The breakdown goes to atoms, then to elementary particles. No matter is left from which any of it can be rebuilt. Aetheryon reads the same cast as the erasure of their souls, with resurrection afterward impossible.[38]
- Creation Manipulation: Inside the boundary, matter is made again. Clouds of new matter, radiation, and particles fill the vacuum. The cast reenacts the birth of a universe on a local scale, and that birth is the attack. She does not rule a universe when it is over. What remains of the three is residual energy of creation.[38]
- Destruction Manipulation: A finished cast destroys the reinforced training dimension and kills the planet under it. Left alone between the galaxies, the chain erases hundreds of galaxies in the region.[6][40]
- Big Bang Inducement: The finished sentence is a Big Bang. A piece of space is crushed to a point, the point of light is put out, and the bang is released from that dark. It is held local, inside the universe that is already there, and her creation keeps it going.[39][38]
- Matter Manipulation: The same boundary destroys matter and creates it. Armor and flesh are unmade. New matter is left behind in the vacuum.[38]
- Quantum Manipulation: The unmaking does not stop at dust or at a whole atom. The bodies are taken to the atomic level and then to elementary particles.[38]
- Spatial Manipulation: A chunk of space is compressed into a single point. After the point dies, that same space expands with the blast. Distances grow. Nearby structures take the pressure of the expansion.[38]
- Spatial Expansion: The expansion goes out in every direction, faster than ordinary perception. Space is not merely crossed by the wave. It grows with the wave, and the growth does not weaken with distance.[38][40]
- Gravity Manipulation: Loose matter and fragments are dragged into the core. The wave then disrupts gravitational fields and changes the orbits of neighboring bodies.[38]
- Energy Manipulation: What spreads is a wave of primordial energy, omnidirectional, carrying immense matter and radiation. The runes alone would demand an absurd expenditure. Her control spends hardly any of it, and she is not weakened at the end.[5][38]

- Radiation Manipulation: The wave carries immense radiation, and that radiation sweeps the void.[38]

##### Gae Bolg
Main article: [[Gae Bolg]]
Gae Bolg is [[Scathach]]'s signature Runic spear and one of the most important weapons associated with Clan Scarlett. Its effectiveness originates from the combination of her Grandmaster spearmanship and the extensive Runic systems developed around the weapon.
The documented earlier configuration possessed exactly 2,669 interconnected Runes, arranged among principal, supporting and tertiary instructions. These inscriptions formed a complicated system providing offensive, defensive and utility properties rather than functioning as thousands of unrelated enchantments.[4]
