Tasha Fenrir Elderblood is an ancient Goddess, a Dragon with Werewolf traits, the Queen of Samar, the Progenitor of the Werewolf Race, the former wife of Volk Fenrir and one of the wives of [[Victor Elderblood]]. Originally a Goddess of the [[Egyptian Pantheon]], Tasha survived the dangerous politics of the ancient Divine world, eventually became a Fallen Goddess and later established herself on Samar, where she rose alongside Volk as one of the central rulers of Werewolf civilization.
Although Volk possessed greater direct military authority during much of their marriage, Tasha was never simply the King's consort. She managed diplomacy, intelligence, covert operations, political relationships and many of the mechanisms that allowed Samar to function beyond raw Alpha dominance.[2][10] Her ancient reputation as the Monarch of Beasts reflected the far more dangerous woman beneath her controlled royal demeanor: a politically cunning and brutally pragmatic Goddess who had survived betrayal long before her life among the [[Werewolves]].[3]
Following the collapse of her marriage to Volk, Tasha reclaimed the portions of herself she had suppressed throughout thousands of years of compromise. Her awakening transformed her into the Goddess of the Wolf Race and subsequently the new Werewolf Progenitor, connecting her Soul to the Race and placing her above the ordinary Alpha hierarchy.[6] After defeating Volk, she stripped away most of his accumulated power, expelled him from that hierarchy and reduced the former True Alpha to an Omega, with the resulting increase placing Tasha within the Low-Level God-King range.[7]
Tasha later willingly entered a [[Soul Marriage]] with Victor while retaining her own sovereignty over Samar.[11] Her Divinity and understanding as a Progenitor continued developing through the Elderblood family's accelerated eras, eventually reaching maximum Mastery, and she and Victor produced a daughter who became the first Nature Blood Dragon God.[12] Tasha herself later underwent another racial evolution and is currently described as a Dragon with Werewolf traits, while continuing to govern the greatly expanded Werewolf civilization as part of the [[Elderblood Empire]].[5]

### Physical Appearance
Tasha is a tall, powerfully built woman with chocolate-colored skin, long straight black hair and normally green eyes. During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s earliest personal interactions with her, she stood approximately 175 cm tall and possessed a mature, athletic physique befitting an ancient Goddess and Werewolf ruler.[8] Her usual appearance projects the controlled composure expected of a Queen, but the more bestial elements of her nature become increasingly visible whenever her instincts, anger or Divine power are stimulated.
Her Werewolf characteristics include black wolf ears and a long black tail, while sharpened teeth and claws can become more pronounced when she becomes aggressive. After Victor deliberately brought her suppressed Monarch of Beasts mentality back to the surface, her green eyes became so dark and empty that they were likened to emerald-colored black holes.[3] During the emotional aftermath of Volk's betrayal, her eyes instead shone with a mixture of celestial blue and gold, while her ears and tail betrayed emotions she otherwise attempted to control.[9]

#### Wolf Goddess / Progenitor Form
Tasha's awakening as the Goddess of the Wolf Race produced a much more dramatic transformation. Golden Divine light enveloped her body before revealing a form approximately two meters tall, with a larger and more developed physique, wild black hair extending toward her knees, more pronounced wolf ears and a long black tail marked by golden stripe-like patterns. Additional golden markings appeared across her body and face, visually representing the fusion of her Divine and lupine nature.[6]
The transformation was far more significant than a physical change. Her Soul became connected to the Werewolf Race itself, converting Tasha from a Goddess possessing Werewolf characteristics into the supernatural Source and Progenitor standing above its conventional Alpha structure.[6]

#### Current Dragon-Werewolf Nature
Tasha's later evolution altered her racial nature again. Although she remained one of the two major Progenitor Goddesses in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family who had not become a True Dragon during an earlier stage of the Elderblood dynasty, her current existence is explicitly described as a Dragon hybrid, more precisely a Dragon with Werewolf traits.[12][5] Victor's Draconic Blood is sufficiently dominant and predatory that the Dragon aspect became the principal racial foundation rather than being subordinated to her Werewolf nature.
True Dragon form
Her Werewolf characteristics and position as the Race's Progenitor nevertheless remain intact. The available material does not identify the precise event in which this evolution occurred or provide a complete visual description of Tasha's current Dragon-Werewolf form.

### Personality
Tasha is calculating, pragmatic, independent and exceptionally difficult to politically corner. Thousands of years spent surviving first among the Egyptian Gods and later within Samar's Alpha-centered society taught her to approach problems through information, preparation and leverage rather than assuming overwhelming strength alone would solve everything. She cultivates intelligence networks, prepares fallback positions, maintains independent resources and evaluates the long-term consequences of political decisions before committing herself. This made her especially suited to the less visible side of ruling Samar, while Volk historically functioned more effectively as its overwhelming military deterrent.[2][10]
Her caution is not simple paranoia. Tasha has experienced repeated betrayals and consequently regards trust as something far more valuable than obedience. Even while married to Volk, she never surrendered control of every resource, follower or shelter she possessed, yet she trusted him enough for the discovery of the precautions he had secretly prepared against her to hurt far more deeply than the political maneuver itself. Her response demonstrated the difference between caution and panic: rather than immediately throwing herself into revenge, she secured Thomas, reorganized the people still loyal to her, evaluated the situation and only then prepared to retaliate.[10][9]
Tasha possesses a strong sense of responsibility toward the civilization under her authority. When her roles as wife, Goddess and Queen came into conflict, she ultimately placed Samar and her people above preserving a failing marriage. The same mentality continued after becoming [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wife. Joining the Elderblood family did not cause Tasha to abandon the [[Werewolves]] or surrender her political identity; she remained their Queen, Progenitor and principal ruler while gaining access to a vastly larger family and Imperial structure.
Beneath her cultivated self-control exists the personality that once earned the name Monarch of Beasts. Victor recognized the ancient Tasha as a woman capable of extraordinary savagery while still possessing the intelligence necessary to manipulate political environments filled with Gods.[3] Her brutality is dangerous precisely because it is rarely mindless. Tasha is willing to use deception, psychological warfare, assassination tools, overwhelming force or indirect methods when circumstances demand them, and she rejects the idea that an opponent who already abandoned fair play is entitled to demand honorable limitations from her.
The philosophy of [[Hassan-i-Sabbah]], her former master, remains deeply rooted in her mentality. Tasha interprets his teachings concerning truth, permission and choice as an acknowledgment that individuals are free to make decisions but cannot escape the consequences created by those decisions.[9] This worldview strongly shapes how she responds to Volk: his eventual downfall is not treated merely as emotional revenge, but as the consequence of years of fear, distrust and choices that destroyed the very relationship and political structure he had attempted to preserve.
Tasha is also intensely maternal. Her first direct appearance contrasts the feared Queen with an affectionate mother who overwhelms [[Thomas Fenrir]] after his return and insists upon being addressed as "Mommy".[13] Her children remain among the people she protects most aggressively, but maternal affection does not make her incapable of opposing them when their actions threaten Samar. This is demonstrated most clearly through her response to Fenir's cooperation with [[New Dawn]].
Victor attracts Tasha largely because his attitude toward powerful women is the inverse of what she experienced with Volk. Tasha directly questions why Victor does not fear his wives eventually becoming strong enough to betray him, explaining that Volk restricted her precisely because of that fear.[14] Victor instead regards the growth of his wives as an increase in the strength of the family as a whole. For Tasha, being valued specifically for the intelligence, ambition, independence and danger another husband had considered threatening becomes one of the foundations of her eventual trust in him.

### History

#### Ancient Egypt
Tasha originated in the ancient [[Egyptian Pantheon]] as a Goddess associated with wolves. Much of the exact chronology of her earliest life remains unrevealed, but her history establishes that she existed within the dangerous political environment of the Egyptian Gods, experienced severe betrayal and eventually became a Fallen Goddess before leaving that civilization behind.[3][15]
Her reputation during this period was formidable enough for the Egyptian Gods to know her as the Monarch of Beasts. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later regarded the title as especially significant because it had been earned among Divine beings rather than ordinary supernatural creatures. Even after remaining away from Egypt for thousands of years, Tasha retained enough old influence and relationships for Lesser Egyptian Gods to remain connected to her.[3]
One of the most important people in Tasha's ancient life was Yunet Semet, whom she regarded as her best friend. Yunet recorded Tasha's history in a manuscript titled The Fall of the Wolf Goddess and Her Rise. The surviving manuscript eventually reached the [[Snow Clan]]'s library, where Victor discovered it before returning the work to Tasha.[16]
Tasha was also trained by [[Hassan-i-Sabbah]]. His teachings concerning responsibility, freedom, truth and the consequences of individual choice continued influencing her thinking thousands of years after their time together.[9]

#### Samar and Volk Fenrir
After leaving her old Divine life behind, Tasha eventually reached Samar and became integrated into Werewolf civilization. She married [[Volk Fenrir]], one of the planet's dominant True Alphas, and together they became the principal monarchs of the Werewolf Race. Both were personally Blessed by [[Fenrir]], the Beast of Ragnarok, and were permitted to carry his name.[4]
Their union created an extraordinarily powerful ruling pair but never transformed Tasha into a conventional subordinate Alpha. Her Divine origin, political intelligence, personal followers and independent influence placed her in a position Volk could never completely dominate. Volk interpreted that independence as a potential future threat and secretly prepared measures against her from the beginning of their marriage, including obtaining her signature on documents capable of dissolving their union and undermining the political relationships connected to it.[10]
Tasha nevertheless spent thousands of years helping build Samar. She established diplomatic relationships, cultivated contacts beyond the planet, developed intelligence networks, oversaw covert operations and handled many of the administrative responsibilities Volk was poorly suited to perform.[2][10] Their marriage produced several sons, including Fenir, Anderson and [[Thomas Fenrir]], while Tasha's responsibilities expanded until she effectively represented the political and informational counterpart to Volk's military authority.

### Synopsis

#### A New Pillar
Tasha is first introduced indirectly through Anderson and his connection to the Werewolf Royal Family. His background establishes that his mother is an ancient former Egyptian Goddess and one of the principal monarchs of Samar long before [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] becomes personally involved with the Werewolf world.[17]

#### Demonic War
Tasha makes her first direct appearance when [[Thomas Fenrir]] returns to Samar. Her reunion with him immediately reveals the contrast defining much of her character: the feared Queen overwhelms her son with maternal affection and insists that he call her "Mommy", yet quickly returns to the mentality of the ruler responsible for Samar's intelligence and concealed political machinery.[13]
The mysterious deterioration of [[Fenrir]] becomes one of Tasha's principal concerns. A Being of his nature should not simply suffer from an inexplicable decline, causing her to consider the possibility that the conspiracies and supernatural instability spreading through other regions have also reached Samar. Her involvement establishes that the Werewolf Kingdom is not as isolated from the wider conflict as its rulers had hoped.

#### King Among Kings
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s arrival on Samar radically changes the political environment surrounding Tasha. During negotiations concerning [[Fenrir]]'s condition, Victor invokes the principle of equivalent exchange and names Tasha Fenrir as the price he wants in return for helping the ancient Beast.[2] The demand is politically explosive because Tasha is not simply Volk's wife; she is the Queen, one of Samar's principal administrators and the ruler responsible for much of the kingdom's intelligence and covert infrastructure. Secretly observing the negotiations, Tasha understands Victor's ruthless reasoning more easily than many of those present because she has spent thousands of years making similarly pragmatic choices on behalf of her family and nation.
Victor subsequently begins interacting with Tasha as an individual rather than treating her merely as part of his dealings with Volk. He recognizes that much of her exceptional potential has been consumed by repairing problems created by other people and questions why someone with her experience, intelligence and Divine history has allowed herself to remain so restricted.[8][14] Their conversations increasingly center upon rulership and trust. Tasha asks why Victor is unafraid of his wives becoming powerful enough to betray him, while Victor explains that deliberately weakening the people he loves because they might theoretically become rivals would contradict the very foundation of his family. The contrast forces Tasha to confront how completely Volk's fear of her potential has shaped their marriage.
Victor also returns Yunet Semet's surviving manuscript, The Fall of the Wolf Goddess and Her Rise.[16] The gift reconnects Tasha with an existence preceding Samar and with the memory of one of the people who had understood her best. At the same time, evidence emerges that Fenir has become involved with [[New Dawn]]. Werewolf tradition does not inherently condemn a powerful heir challenging an established ruler, but allowing an outside organization to manipulate Samar for personal ambition crosses a line Tasha will not tolerate. She participates in dismantling her son's faction despite being his mother, placing the security of the Werewolf civilization above maternal favoritism.
Victor increasingly describes Tasha as an Alpha among Alphas, recognizing the mentality of a ruler whose authority has been constrained more by circumstance than capability.[1] His presence destabilizes Samar's established hierarchy further when his confrontation with Volk reveals an Alpha capable of physically and instinctively dominating the [[Lycaon|Werewolf King]]. Tasha and Maya both feel the significance of the display because Werewolf instincts respond to strength and dominance regardless of political titles.[18]
The final collapse of Tasha's marriage begins when Volk chooses to reject Victor's growing alliance and activates the contingencies he had prepared against his wife. He strips politically vulnerable Alpha families from Tasha's faction, declares himself Samar's sole ruler and produces divorce documents carrying a signature Tasha had unknowingly provided during their first night as husband and wife.[10] The maneuver damages her formal political position but fails to destroy the organization beneath it. Tasha had never placed every shelter, follower, intelligence contact or resource under Volk's control, allowing her to secure Thomas and reorganize the people personally loyal to her almost immediately.
Tasha also rejects the implication that her attraction toward Victor justified Volk's actions. Although she had developed feelings for Victor, their secret meetings had remained conversations while she was still married.[10] Volk's attempt to prevent an imagined betrayal therefore becomes one of the principal causes of the very separation he feared.
Victor refuses to let Tasha define herself entirely through what Volk has done to her. Instead, he reminds her of the woman who existed before thousands of years of compromise and invokes the ancient title Monarch of Beasts. Her suppressed predatory personality resurfaces immediately, accompanied by the dark emerald-black eyes associated with that earlier identity.[3] Further discoveries concerning Volk's conduct provoke enormous anger, yet Tasha follows the discipline embedded in her by [[Hassan-i-Sabbah]]: protect the people who matter, secure her position, understand the battlefield and only then punish the person responsible.[9]
Her retaliation against Volk is consequently not a conventional duel between two [[Werewolves]]. Tasha uses prepared allies, psychological pressure, specialized weapons, deception and every practical advantage available to dismantle him. When Volk condemns these methods, she refuses to accept rules from someone who had already used hidden contingencies and political betrayal against her.[19] The conflict also forces Tasha to stop suppressing her Divine nature, allowing the power she had restrained throughout her life on Samar to return and raising her once again to the level of a High-Level Goddess.
That restoration develops into something far greater. Tasha awakens authority over the Wolf Race and its subspecies, and her body transforms beneath golden Divine power as both she and Volk become candidates for the vacant position of Werewolf Progenitor.[6] Their struggle ceases to concern only the throne of Samar; it determines which of them will become the supernatural Source and supreme Alpha of the Race itself.
Tasha emerges victorious. Her Soul establishes connections throughout the Werewolf Race, elevating her above the normal Alpha hierarchy and granting her authority fundamentally different from that of an exceptionally powerful pack leader.[6] She can impose that hierarchy even upon Volk, despite his former status as a True Alpha.
After securing the Progenitor position, Tasha extracts most of Volk's accumulated supernatural Power and absorbs it into herself.[7] She receives the raw power rather than his memories, experience or personal techniques, and the resulting increase elevates her to approximately the Low-Level God-King range. Tasha then expels Volk from the hierarchy she now governs and reduces him to an Omega. For a man whose identity had been built around being the supreme Alpha, his continued existence at the bottom of the racial hierarchy becomes a punishment more meaningful than simple execution.
With her former marriage ended and her position secured, Tasha no longer suppresses her relationship with Victor. Victor supports her rule without replacing it, including supplying hundreds of Demons capable of functioning as a vast intelligence network across Samar.[20] Their relationship culminates in a [[Soul Marriage]], an especially significant decision for a woman whose life has repeatedly taught her not to surrender trust lightly.[11]
Marriage into the Elderblood family does not diminish Tasha's sovereignty. She continues ruling Samar and exercising direct Progenitor authority over the Werewolf Race, including the ability to alter positions within its hierarchy and elevate suitable Werewolves beyond their previous rank.[4] She later appears among the great supernatural powers accompanied by Fenrir, no longer as the politically constrained wife of the Werewolf King but as an independent God-King, Progenitor and ruler in her own right.[21]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
The immense periods of accelerated development experienced by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family allow Tasha to deepen both her Divinity and her understanding of what being a Progenitor actually means. She and [[Lilith Elderblood]] remain notable for following a different racial path from most of Victor's other wives during this period: both are powerful Progenitor Goddesses who have not become [[True Dragons]], yet neither is left behind by the Draconic evolution surrounding the rest of the family.[12]
Tasha's Divinity reaches its maximum state of Mastery, while Victor assists her in developing a substantially deeper comprehension of the relationship between her own existence and the Race for which she serves as Progenitor.[12] Her position is no longer simply the result of winning a contest for racial dominance; she matures into the supernatural origin point and governing authority responsible for the future development of the [[Werewolves]].
Tasha and Victor also produce an unnamed daughter whose existence combines two Progenitor lineages. Victor's Blood Dragon nature supplies a complete True Dragon foundation, but Tasha's mature Werewolf and Divine characteristics are strong enough to remain integrated rather than disappearing beneath the overwhelmingly dominant Draconic inheritance.[12] Their daughter consequently becomes the first Nature Blood Dragon God.
The girl's affinity with Nature is instinctive and extraordinarily broad. She understands natural systems and evolutionary development at a level allowing processes that would normally require thousands of years to be compressed into comparatively short periods, potentially creating new racial developments through accelerated evolution.[12] These capabilities belong to Tasha's daughter rather than Tasha herself, but their manifestation demonstrates how completely Tasha's Progenitor characteristics survived within a child born from Victor's dominant Dragon Blood.

#### Those Who Rule Reality
The enormous developmental eras of the [[Elderblood Empire]] transform Samar alongside the rest of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s civilization. Tasha remains directly responsible for the Werewolf world rather than abandoning it for the central Imperial court. Under her rule, Samar grows to approximately three times the size of old Earth, while its Royal Capital expands across an area greater than South America.[5] The scale of the civilization bears almost no resemblance to the kingdom Tasha once governed beside Volk.
The Werewolf population develops just as dramatically. Its bloodline becomes substantially purer and stronger, approaching the ancestral condition of the Race by the standards of the old era. Tasha nevertheless identifies a major weakness in Samar's military structure: only around fifty individuals possess planetary-class strength, an inadequate number compared with the standards of the mature Elderblood Empire.[5]
Rather than attempting to solve the shortage exclusively through traditional Werewolf breeding, Tasha takes advantage of the Empire's success in stabilizing supernatural hybridization. All fifty of Samar's principal planetary-level elites are hybrids, combining the Werewolf foundation with compatible characteristics from spiritually powerful races such as Gods, Angels, Demons, Spirits and other supernatural lineages.[5] The decision reflects the same mentality Tasha has always brought to rulership: preserve what is valuable in the Race while using whatever methods produce the strongest practical result.
Tasha herself has also undergone another evolution. Her previous status as one of Victor's major non-Dragon Progenitor wives is no longer current; she is explicitly identified as a Dragon hybrid, more precisely a Dragon with Werewolf traits.[5] Victor's Blood is described as too dominant and predatory to become subordinate to her original Werewolf nature, making the Draconic component the primary racial foundation while her lupine characteristics and Progenitor authority remain preserved. The exact circumstances of this transformation have not yet been shown.
When the Empire prepares to integrate the Lower Sectors associated with Arcane, [[Nightingale]] and Samar into Victor's Mid-Level Sector, Tasha is entrusted with the sphere assigned to Samar. The sphere contains a fragment of Victor himself and acts as [[Phlegethon Wound|the anchor]] through which his influence can permeate and draw the entire Lower Sector toward his own cosmological territory.[5][22] Corresponding anchors are placed within the other two Sectors, allowing the three regions to be moved without forcing them prematurely into the dangerous Higher Sectors.
The merger successfully draws Samar, Nightingale and Arcane into the greater Sector while Victor reorganizes their galaxies and preserves the astronomical structures necessary for their civilizations.[23] Samar and Nightingale receive particular protection because of their unusual planetary environments and are ultimately positioned only a few hundred light-years apart within one of the Empire's heavily secured central regions.[24] Tasha's kingdom therefore ceases to exist as an isolated Lower-Sector civilization and becomes a literal region of the same cosmic territory ruled by the Elderblood Empire, while Tasha continues governing the [[Werewolves]] from within that vastly larger political structure.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Tasha's second husband and one of the few individuals to whom she eventually gives the level of trust she had learned not to offer lightly. Their relationship begins under deliberately provocative circumstances when Victor identifies Tasha as the equivalent price he wants for helping [[Fenrir]], but his interest in her rapidly develops beyond the political bargain.[2]
Victor admires precisely the characteristics Volk had come to regard as dangerous. He values Tasha's intelligence, ruthlessness, ambition, independence, political experience and capacity for further growth, and repeatedly argues that a ruler of her ability should not spend eternity repairing the mistakes of people determined to restrict her.[8][14] Tasha becomes equally interested in Victor after observing how differently he treats the powerful women around him. His wives are encouraged to become more dangerous rather than deliberately held beneath him, because Victor views their strength as part of the strength of the family.
Their growing attraction does not cause Tasha to betray Volk while the two remain married. Her private meetings with Victor consist of conversations rather than an affair, something she makes explicit after Volk uses the possibility of betrayal to justify his actions.[10]
Victor later becomes instrumental in restoring Tasha's suppressed sense of self. Rather than personally solving the conflict with Volk for her, he reminds Tasha of the Monarch of Beasts who existed long before their relationship and allows her to determine what she wants to do with that identity.[3] After she becomes Werewolf Progenitor, Victor continues strengthening her independence instead of replacing it, even providing hundreds of Demonic agents for her intelligence network.[20]
Their eventual [[Soul Marriage]] permanently connects them at the spiritual level.[11] Tasha nevertheless remains the Queen of Samar and ruler of the [[Werewolves]] rather than surrendering her responsibilities to Victor. Their later daughter combines Victor's Blood Dragon Progenitor nature with Tasha's mature Werewolf Progenitor characteristics and becomes the first Nature Blood Dragon God.[12]

#### Volk Fenrir
[[Volk Fenrir]] was Tasha's first husband, her former co-ruler on Samar and the father of several of her sons. Their marriage united two extremely powerful figures but was undermined by a fundamental absence of complete trust. Tasha was too independent to fit comfortably beneath Volk's Alpha mentality, while Volk increasingly feared that her influence and potential might eventually threaten his position.
That distrust existed from the beginning. Volk secretly obtained Tasha's signature on documents capable of dissolving their marriage and weakening the political alliance surrounding her during their first night together.[10] Tasha likewise maintained followers, shelters, political relationships and other resources outside his direct control because her earlier life among the Egyptian Gods had already taught her never to place every means of survival in another ruler's hands.
Despite these precautions, Tasha spends thousands of years helping Volk build Samar and does not plan to betray him before he acts against her. Much of the kingdom's diplomacy, intelligence structure and foreign influence exists because of her work.[10] Volk's attempt to politically isolate her consequently destroys the marriage he claimed to be protecting.
Their conflict ultimately ends with Tasha becoming the Werewolf Progenitor, defeating Volk within the hierarchy he once dominated, draining most of his accumulated supernatural Power and reducing him to an Omega.[6][7] She chooses not to kill him, leaving the former supreme Alpha alive beneath the racial hierarchy now governed by the wife he had feared would surpass him.

#### Fenrir
[[Fenrir]] is the Beast of Ragnarok and one of the most important supernatural Beings connected to Tasha's life on Samar. Both Tasha and Volk were personally Blessed by him and received permission to use his name.[4] Tasha understands that Fenrir is not a conventional servant of the Werewolf monarchy; the [[Werewolves]] occupy a world connected to him, but the ancient Beast decides for himself whom he recognizes as a companion.
Tasha is one of the people with whom Fenrir shares genuine familiarity. Their long relationship allows her to interact with him in a manner ordinary Werewolves could not realistically imitate, and Fenrir later accompanies Tasha during her appearance among the supernatural world's rulers.[25][21]

#### Thomas Fenrir
[[Thomas Fenrir]] is one of Tasha's sons and the child toward whom her maternal affection is displayed most openly. Her direct debut centers upon embracing Thomas following his return and refusing to accept the comparatively formal "Mother", instead insisting that he call her "Mommy".[13]
Her protectiveness becomes especially important during the collapse of her marriage. Even while dealing with the emotional and political consequences of Volk's betrayal, securing Thomas is one of Tasha's first priorities before she begins planning retaliation.[10][9]

#### Anderson
Anderson is one of Tasha's sons and the character through whom her existence is first introduced.[17] He is already an experienced Alpha with responsibilities of his own when he becomes involved in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s life, causing Tasha to grant him considerably more independence than she displays toward Thomas.
Despite that freedom, Tasha remains both his mother and the ultimate racial authority once she becomes Werewolf Progenitor. Anderson's status as an Alpha does not place him outside the hierarchy governed by her.

#### Fenir
Fenir is Tasha and Volk's eldest known son. His ambition to challenge Volk for rulership is not itself treated as unacceptable because Werewolf culture recognizes the right of a sufficiently capable Alpha to challenge existing authority. Tasha's objection arises from his cooperation with [[New Dawn]], which risks turning Samar and its people into assets for an external organization.[16][1]
Tasha consequently participates in dismantling Fenir's faction despite being his mother, demonstrating that protecting the Werewolf civilization takes precedence over shielding a child from the consequences of his political decisions.

#### Unnamed Daughter
Tasha and [[Victor Elderblood]] have an unnamed daughter who becomes the first Nature Blood Dragon God.[12] Unlike Tasha's sons with Volk, the girl is born with a complete True Dragon foundation derived from Victor, but the strength of Tasha's mature Divine and Progenitor nature allows her Werewolf characteristics to survive within that dominant lineage.
Their daughter possesses instinctive understanding of Nature and evolutionary processes, including the ability to accelerate developments that would ordinarily require thousands of years.[12] Her existence demonstrates the exceptional compatibility produced when two different Progenitor lineages contribute to the same descendant.
No canonical personal name for Tasha's daughter has yet been established in the available material.

#### Hassan-i-Sabbah
[[Hassan-i-Sabbah]] is Tasha's master and one of the most important philosophical influences in her life. His teachings concerning truth, freedom, responsibility and the consequences of individual decisions remain embedded deeply enough in her worldview to guide how she responds to Volk's betrayal thousands of years later.[9]
Rather than using those teachings to justify meaningless violence, Tasha interprets them as an acknowledgment that people are responsible for the world created through their own choices. This philosophy is one of the reasons she refuses to let uncontrolled anger dictate her response after her marriage collapses.

#### Yunet Semet
Yunet Semet was Tasha's best friend during an earlier period of her life and one of the people who preserved the story of the woman Tasha had once been. Yunet authored The Fall of the Wolf Goddess and Her Rise, an account of Tasha's history that survived long after the two were separated.[16]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually discovers the manuscript and returns it to Tasha. The gesture carries considerable emotional significance because the book reconnects her not only with her own forgotten history but with the memory of a person she had genuinely loved and trusted.

#### Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood
[[Maya Elizabeth Lykos Elderblood]] is another of Samar's great matriarchs and eventually one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wives alongside Tasha. Their political foundations differ: Tasha's authority originates through the monarchy, her Divine history and eventual Progenitor status, while Maya represents the enormous influence and ancient lineage of the [[Lykos Clan]].
The two become important partners within the later administration of Samar and members of the same Elderblood family. During the preparations for Samar's Sector merger, Maya remains beside Tasha as the pair observe the enormous civilization the Werewolf world has become under Imperial development.[5]

#### Aurora Seraphina Evergreen
[[Aurora Seraphina Evergreen]] is the Positive World Tree associated with Samar. Her existence becomes especially significant following Tasha's awakening as Progenitor because both women occupy foundational positions within the supernatural structure supporting the Werewolf world.[6][4]
Aurora supports the planetary and natural foundation of Samar, while Tasha serves as the supernatural Source and ruler of its dominant Race. Their positions therefore connect two of the most important structures maintaining the later Werewolf civilization.

### Abilities

#### Powers & Abilities
Tasha Fenrir Elderblood possesses a composite supernatural existence built from several independent systems: her original existence as an Egyptian Goddess, her later Werewolf physiology, the Blessing of [[Fenrir]], her Divine Authority over the Wolf Race, the racial Authority she obtained after becoming the Werewolf Progenitor, support from [[Aurora Seraphina Evergreen]], and, in the current era, a later Draconic evolution that transformed her into a Dragon with Werewolf traits.[1][2][3][4]
Her largest early increase occurred during her final conflict with [[Volk Fenrir]]. Tasha first ceased suppressing her Divine nature and restored herself as a High-Level Goddess; she then awakened a new Divinity encompassing the Wolf Race and its subspecies, became the new Werewolf Progenitor, and finally absorbed most of Volk's accumulated Power, bringing her overall Energy into the Low-Level God-King range at that point in the story.[5][2][6]
This was only an early benchmark. After approximately two thousand years of additional development, Tasha and [[Lilith Elderblood]] were explicitly identified as the two non-True-Dragon Progenitor Goddesses who had evolved the most within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s group. Their respective Divinities had reached the maximum state of Mastery, and they possessed a much deeper understanding of both themselves and their functions as racial Progenitors.[7]
By Chapter 1179, Tasha's previous non-Dragon state had changed. She is directly described as a Dragon hybrid—more precisely, a Dragon with Werewolf traits. Victor's Draconic blood is stated to be so dominant and predatory that it would not submit to her Werewolf component.[4]

#### Current Composite Physiology

##### Draconic-Werewolf Physiology
Tasha's current body combines her Werewolf-Progenitor existence with a later Draconic evolution. The complete mechanics of this transformation have not yet been shown, so only characteristics actually established by the current material should be treated as confirmed.
- Draconic Hybrid Physiology: By Chapter 1179, Tasha is explicitly described as a Dragon hybrid rather than remaining solely a Werewolf-Goddess Progenitor.[4]
- Werewolf Trait Preservation: Her original Werewolf characteristics were not erased by the Draconic transformation; the narration specifically calls her a Dragon with Werewolf traits.[4]
- Dominant Draconic Blood: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Draconic blood is stated to be exceptionally dominant and predatory within Tasha's current hybrid existence.[4]
- Racial Hybridization: Tasha demonstrates that Victor's later Dragon lineage can coexist with the pre-existing nature of an exceptional Progenitor rather than completely removing the older racial component.[4]
- Continued Progenitor Existence: Nothing in the later material states that becoming Draconic removed Tasha's established position as the Werewolf Race's Progenitor; she continues ruling Samar and the Werewolf civilization after the transformation.[4]
The novel has not yet directly demonstrated Tasha using [[Draconic Energy]], Dragon Breath, Dragon Eyes, the Dragon Language, Draconic Runes or a complete Dragon Form after this change. Those abilities should therefore not yet be indexed as personally demonstrated powers merely because she is currently Draconic.

##### Werewolf Progenitor Physiology
Tasha remains the supernatural Source and absolute Alpha of the modern Werewolf Race. Her Progenitor existence vastly exceeds the physiology and hierarchy of ordinary [[Werewolves]].[2][6]

###### Supernatural Physical Characteristics
- Immense Strength: Tasha possesses exceptional physical strength even before completing her Progenitor ascension, allowing her to physically contend with and overpower [[Volk Fenrir]], one of Samar's strongest True Alphas.[8][5]
- Superhuman Speed and Reflexes: Tasha is capable of fighting Volk at close range and reacting within combat between beings operating far beyond ordinary supernatural warriors.[8]
- Immense Durability: Her combination of Werewolf physiology, High-Level Goddess existence and later Progenitor evolution grants her a body far beyond conventional supernatural durability.[8][2]
- Superhuman Endurance: Tasha remained capable of continuing her confrontation while undergoing enormous physical, emotional and supernatural strain.[8][5]
- Enhanced Stamina: Her Werewolf and Divine physiologies allow prolonged supernatural combat without mortal levels of exhaustion.[8][5]
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Werewolf physiology grants supernatural regeneration, while Tasha's current status places her far above ordinary members of the Race.[9]
- Enhanced Senses: Tasha inherits the powerful smell, hearing and predatory sensory capabilities characteristic of [[Werewolves]].[9]
- Natural Weaponry: Tasha can manifest sharpened claws, fangs and other lupine features suitable for supernatural close combat.[8]
- Predatory Instincts: Her Werewolf nature supplies extremely developed predatory instincts, further reinforced by the mentality associated with the Monarch of Beasts.[10][8]
- Supernatural Longevity: Tasha had already lived for more than five thousand years before the later large-scale temporal accelerations of the Elderblood civilization.[11]

##### Nature Energy
[[Werewolves]] possess an intrinsic relationship with Nature Energy, and Tasha inherits this racial affinity at a level amplified by her extraordinary status.
- Nature Energy Manipulation: Tasha can utilize the Nature Energy associated with Werewolf physiology.[12]
- Physical Reinforcement: Nature Energy can be circulated through a Werewolf's body to improve physical performance and supernatural combat capability.[12]
- Nature Affinity: Tasha possesses the natural affinity toward the environment characteristic of the Werewolf Race.[12]
- World Tree Compatibility: Her nature is additionally compatible with [[Aurora Seraphina Evergreen]], Samar's Positive World Tree, which later directly supports Tasha's position.[2][3]
Tasha's daughter later manifests an extraordinarily powerful Nature-based inheritance originating from Tasha's side of the lineage, but this does not by itself establish `<Nature>` as the formal name of one of Tasha's personal Divinities.[7]

Tasha originated as a Goddess of the [[Egyptian Pantheon]], later existed as a Fallen Goddess and ultimately reclaimed and surpassed her original Divine condition.
- God Physiology: Tasha possesses the supernatural physical, energetic and spiritual characteristics of a genuine Goddess.[5][2]

Tasha removes the limitations suppressing her Divine nature during her final battle against Volk.

#### Divinities

##### Wolf Race / Wolves
Tasha's clearest confirmed current Divinity is an Authority associated with the entire Wolf Race and its subspecies.[2]
- Wolf Divinity: Tasha became a genuine Wolf Goddess following the restoration and evolution of her Divine existence.[2]
- Racial Conceptual Authority: Her Divinity encompasses an entire Race rather than merely a single pack or individual type of Werewolf.[2]
- Subspecies Authority: The Concept explicitly extends to the Wolf Race's subspecies.[2]
- Divine-Racial Synchronization: Tasha simultaneously possesses Divine Authority over the Wolf Race and biological/spiritual Authority as its Progenitor, causing the two systems to reinforce one another.[2]
- Race-Wide Influence: Her conceptual relationship is not restricted to [[Werewolves]] who consciously worship her or voluntarily join her personal faction.[2]
- Maximum Mastery: By Chapter 989, Tasha's developed Divinities had reached maximum mastery.[7]
The source establishes an Authority over the Wolf Race, but it does not yet demonstrate unrestricted puppeteering of every wolf or every conceivable application of generic "Wolf Manipulation." Specific effects should therefore be indexed only as they are demonstrated.

##### Full Moon Summoning / Lunar Manifestation
Tasha possesses an ability associated with the supernatural relationship between Wolves and the Full Moon.
- Full Moon Summoning: Tasha can cause the natural Full Moon and its influence to manifest during conditions in which it would not ordinarily be available.[8]
- Daytime Lunar Manifestation: The Full Moon manifested while Samar was still experiencing daytime, distinguishing the ability from merely waiting for the planet's normal lunar cycle.[8]
- Lunar Empowerment: Werewolf physiology receives substantial enhancement beneath a Full Moon, meaning Tasha can deliberately create favorable racial conditions for herself and other [[Werewolves]].[8][15]
- Transformation Support: By producing Full-Moon conditions, Tasha can facilitate Werewolf transformations and empowerments that normally depend upon the Moon.[8][14]
- Mass Support Potential: Because the manifested Full Moon affects Werewolf physiology generally rather than only Tasha personally, nearby members of the Race can benefit from the same lunar conditions.[8]
This ability is more accurately described as Full Moon Summoning or Lunar Manifestation rather than literal "Moon Creation." The chapter specifically distinguishes the phenomenon from simply producing an artificial celestial object.[8]

#### Werewolf Progenitor Authority
After defeating Volk and proving herself the superior candidate, Tasha became the current Progenitor and supernatural Source of the Werewolf Race.[2][6]
Her transformation was highly abnormal. Tasha lacked the conventional blood condition previously discussed in relation to Night-Creature Progenitors, yet after the contest between the two candidates ended, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] perceived an unidentified external intervention that completed the change.[2]

##### Progenitor Soul
- Progenitor Soul: Tasha's Soul underwent a fundamental transformation corresponding to her new position as racial Progenitor.[2]
- Superior Soul Quality: Her Soul exists on a fundamentally higher supernatural level than that of ordinary [[Werewolves]] and Alphas.[2]
- Racial Source Function: Her Soul became the supernatural center of the modern Werewolf hierarchy.[2][6]
- Instinctive Authority Knowledge: Tasha instinctively understood several functions of the Progenitor position immediately after acquiring it despite never having possessed those powers before.[6]
- Advanced Progenitor Understanding: Her later millennia of training with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] transformed that initial instinctive understanding into much more complete comprehension.[7]

##### Distributed Power Architecture
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] recognizes that Tasha's Progenitor Soul operates according to a principle fundamentally different from his own.
- Power Distribution: A significant portion of Tasha's racial Power is externalized through members of the Werewolf Race rather than being stored exclusively inside herself.[2]
- Externalized Power Network: Connected [[Werewolves]] function as individual components within Tasha's greater racial structure.[2]
- Centralized Source: Although Power is distributed through the Race, Tasha remains the central Queen and Source from which the system is organized.[2]
- Collective Power Return: The connected members can contribute additional Power back toward Tasha through the same racial network.[2]
- Population-Based Scaling: Expanding the Werewolf population increases the potential number of contributors connected to the Progenitor system.[2]
- Quality-Based Scaling: Developing stronger Werewolves can increase the value of the Race supporting Tasha's greater Power structure.[2]
The system is compared to a supernatural hive because Tasha functions as the central Queen/Progenitor, but there is no evidence that she continuously reads every Werewolf's thoughts or memories.

##### Universal Werewolf Bond
When Tasha became Progenitor, spiritual connections automatically formed between her and the Werewolf Race.
- Automatic Species Bond: [[Werewolves]] became connected to Tasha without individual Soul Contracts or conscious consent being required.[2]
- Race-Wide Spiritual Network: The bond extends beyond members of Tasha's former personal faction to the greater Werewolf population.[2]
- Soul Threads: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] directly perceived supernatural threads extending from Tasha toward Werewolves connected to her new existence.[2]
- Universal Pack Structure: The modern Werewolf Race effectively became one greater supernatural pack beneath Tasha's Progenitor position.[2]
- Racial Awareness: Tasha possesses an intrinsic supernatural relationship with the Race as a collective due to these connections.[2]
The bond is automatic but not literally unbreakable, because Tasha personally demonstrated that she can sever an individual connection.[6]

##### Alpha Among Alphas
Tasha occupies the absolute summit of the Werewolf hierarchy.
- Prime Alpha: Tasha stands hierarchically above conventional Werewolf Alphas.[2]
- Universal Subordination: Ordinary [[Werewolves]] occupy subordinate positions beneath Tasha regardless of their previous individual pack rank.[2]
- Instinctive Submission: Werewolves can instinctively recognize Tasha's superior position even when their conscious pride resists submission.[2][6]
- Progenitor Presence: Her existence produces supernatural racial pressure that other Werewolves instinctively recognize.[2]
- Authority-Based Commands: Tasha can impose commands backed by her position as the Race's absolute Alpha.[2]
- Silencing: She demonstrated the ability to stop Volk from speaking when he attempted to challenge her new position.[2]
- Hierarchy Override: Her Authority supersedes the ordinary Alpha, Beta and Omega structures that previously governed Werewolf society.[2][3]

##### Pack-Based Power Amplification
Tasha receives additional Power from the [[Werewolves]] connected to her greater racial pack.
- Follower Empowerment: Connected Werewolves contribute additional Power on top of Tasha's independent personal strength.[2]
- Population-Based Amplification: A larger Werewolf population can correspondingly increase the collective Power available through the system.[2]
- Follower-Quality Amplification: More powerful Werewolves provide more valuable components within the racial structure.[2]
- Race Growth Scaling: Strengthening the Race as a civilization indirectly improves Tasha's own long-term potential.[2]
- Independent Base Power: Tasha retains her own personal Goddess/Progenitor strength independently from contributions provided by followers.[2]
- Reduced Follower-Loss Penalty: Losing an individual Werewolf removes that member's additional contribution but does not erase Tasha's personal base Power.[2]
- Potentially Massive Scaling: Because the system can expand alongside the entire Race, the ceiling of Tasha's racial amplification can rise as the Werewolf civilization itself grows.[2]

##### Racial Power Regulation
As the Progenitor and Source of the current Werewolf Race, Tasha can directly interfere with supernatural Power possessed by [[Werewolves]] connected to her.
- Power Withdrawal: Tasha can forcibly pull supernatural Power out of a Werewolf belonging to her Race.[6]
- Complete Depowering: She removed essentially all of Volk's accumulated supernatural strength, leaving the former True Alpha in an extremely weakened state.[6]
- Power Condensation: The removed Power can be externally condensed into a visible sphere of Energy.[6]
- Power Absorption: Tasha can consume Power withdrawn from another Werewolf and add the raw supernatural strength to herself.[6]
- Restriction-Free Racial Integration: Volk's extracted Power was incorporated without the complications [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] normally experiences when absorbing entire Souls.[6]
- Raw-Power Absorption: Tasha receives Power rather than the target's memories, personality, combat experience or personal abilities.[6]
- Race-Restricted Absorption: This demonstrated form of unrestricted withdrawal applies specifically to members of Tasha's own Race rather than every supernatural species.[6]

##### Racial Hierarchy Manipulation
Tasha can directly restructure the supernatural hierarchy of the Werewolf Race.
- Rank Manipulation: She can alter the racial position of a Werewolf beneath her Authority.[3]
- Alpha Status Bestowal: Tasha can personally create a new Alpha by selecting another Werewolf for the position.[3]
- Beta-to-Alpha Promotion: She explicitly demonstrates elevating a Werewolf from Beta into Alpha through her Progenitor Authority.[3]
- Hierarchy Reorganization: Tasha can reorganize leadership beneath herself instead of being completely limited by hereditary Alpha structures.[3]
- Succession Control: Her Progenitor status allows her to determine who occupies important positions within the racial hierarchy when restructuring the Werewolf leadership.[3]

##### Racial Expulsion
Tasha can remove an individual Werewolf from the supernatural structure centered upon her.
- Pack Expulsion: Tasha formally expelled Volk from the greater Werewolf pack.[6]
- Omega Demotion: Volk was forcibly reduced from True Alpha and King to the position of an Omega.[6]
- Soul-Thread Severing: Expulsion cuts the supernatural connection linking the target to Tasha's Progenitor structure.[6]
- Soul Damage: Severing Volk from the current Source of the Race directly damaged his Soul.[6]
- Power-Source Severance: Once cut away from Tasha, Volk could no longer rely upon the normal supernatural structure of the Werewolf Race to recover the strength he previously possessed.[6]
- Long-Term Growth Suppression: The damage was severe enough that simply healing Volk's physical body would not restore his previous racial progression or Power while he remained disconnected from the Source.[6]

##### Racial Expansion (Potential / Progenitor Function)
Tasha's Progenitor structure is capable of expanding as additional [[Werewolves]] and Alphas become part of the Race.
- Werewolf Race Expansion: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s analysis of Tasha's new system explicitly considers further expansion of the Werewolf population as a method of increasing the network beneath her.[2]
- Additional Alpha Integration: The system can contain multiple subordinate Alphas beneath Tasha's absolute Progenitor position.[2][3]
- Racial Expansion Empowerment: Every additional valid member potentially becomes another contributor to the distributed Power system.[2]
Tasha has not yet been shown personally creating a Werewolf through a unique Progenitor-exclusive conversion method, so ordinary Werewolf infection/conversion and hypothetical Progenitor conversion should not be presented as demonstrated personal techniques without a direct feat.

Following Tasha's Progenitor awakening, [[Aurora Seraphina Evergreen]], Samar's Positive World Tree, openly supports her.
- Positive Energy Access: Tasha can receive and utilize supernatural Energy supplied through Aurora's support.[2][3]
- World Tree Connection: Tasha possesses a direct relationship with Samar's World Tree after becoming the central Progenitor of the Werewolf Race.[2][3]

- Body Restoration: After stripping Volk of his supernatural Power, she reconstructed his heavily damaged physical body.[6]
- Limb Restoration: Lost or catastrophically damaged limbs can be restored through the healing Energy she used.[6]

Tasha is one of the exceptional [[Werewolves]] personally Blessed by [[Fenrir]], the Beast of Ragnarok.

- Supernatural Empowerment: Fenrir's Blessing forms part of the supernatural foundation Tasha possessed even before becoming Progenitor.[3]
The exact complete ability set personally granted to Tasha by Fenrir's Blessing has not been fully explained. Fenrir's own connection to The End therefore should not be automatically converted into unrestricted `<End>` manipulation for Tasha without a personal demonstration.

Tasha possesses biologically generated supernatural pheromones associated with her advanced Werewolf physiology.

#### Progenitor Reproduction and Inheritance
Tasha's status as a Goddess and racial Progenitor strongly affects what can be inherited by her descendants.

- Divine Trait Inheritance: Tasha's status as both Goddess and Progenitor allowed maternal supernatural characteristics to survive the overwhelmingly dominant Blood Dragon inheritance.[7]
- Nature-Aligned Inheritance: Victor and Tasha's daughter was born as the first Nature Blood Dragon God, with her Nature characteristics explicitly originating from the maternal side of the hybridization.[7]
The following are abilities of Tasha's daughter, not automatically powers of Tasha herself:
- Instinctive Understanding of Nature: The daughter possesses an innate comprehension of Nature and its processes.[7]
- Life Creation: The daughter can create Life through her Nature-based power.[7]
- Evolution Manipulation: She can accelerate evolutionary developments that would ordinarily require thousands of years into only a few years.[7]
- Race Creation through Evolution: This accelerated natural development can eventually result in new [[Races (Flamel)|Races]].[7]
- Limited Death Affinity: The daughter's Nature authority possesses influence over Death insofar as Death is part of the natural cycle of Life.[7]
These inherited abilities provide evidence concerning the depth of Tasha's mature Nature-aligned characteristics, but they are not sufficient evidence to list all of them as Tasha's own demonstrated abilities.

#### God-King-Level Power
Tasha entered the God-King class immediately after becoming Progenitor and absorbing Volk's Power.
- Low-Level God-King Power: Her overall Energy entered the Low-Level God-King range after absorbing Volk's accumulated Power.[6]
- God-King Energy Reserves: Her supernatural reserves became comparable to Beings occupying a tier far above normal Gods.[6]
- Independent Divine Growth: Tasha can continue increasing her strength through greater understanding and mastery of her own Divinities.[7]
- Race-Based Growth: Her Progenitor system can increase with the size and quality of the Werewolf Race beneath her.[2]
- Maximum Divinity Mastery: Her Divinities later reached maximum mastery after approximately two thousand additional years.[7]
- Further Unquantified Growth: Chapter 850's Low-Level God-King rating represents Tasha immediately after awakening and cannot reasonably be treated as her later ceiling after millennia of additional development.[6][7]

#### External Enhancements

##### Soul Marriage
Tasha entered a [[Soul Marriage]] with [[Victor Elderblood]] after willingly accepting him as her husband.
- Soul Bond: Tasha and Victor are connected through a supernatural marriage established directly at the Soul level.[16]
- Spiritual Connection: Their relationship possesses a metaphysical connection significantly deeper than an ordinary legal or political marriage.[16]
- Elderblood Family Integration: Tasha became part of the supernatural family network Victor establishes around those closest to him.[16]

##### Victor's Soul Fragment Protection
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later reveals that his wives possess the highest class of personal protection he can provide: each carries a fragment of his Soul.[17]
As one of Victor's wives, Tasha benefits from this general protection system.
- Soul Manipulation Protection: Victor's wives are protected against attempts at spiritual corruption and interference.[17]
- Mental Corruption Protection: They possess protection against hostile corruption or manipulation of the mind.[17]
- Physical Corruption Protection: Victor specifically establishes protections against corruptive interference with the body.[17]
- Manipulation Protection: His defensive system is intended to oppose unconventional supernatural manipulation rather than only physical attacks.[17]
- Destruction Protection: Victor includes supernatural destruction among the classes of effects against which his wives are protected.[17]
- Bad Luck Protection: Victor explicitly lists defenses against supernatural bad luck among his protection systems.[17]
- Reality Manipulation Protection: His defensive framework includes protection against hostile interference with Reality.[17]
- Law Manipulation Protection: Victor references protection even against Beings capable of distorting the Laws of Creation.[17]
- Soul-Fragment Anchor: The underlying mechanism of the highest protection granted to his wives is a fragment of Victor's own Soul existing within them.[17]
These protections should not be treated as unconditional immunity against arbitrarily stronger Beings. Chapter 1060 establishes the protections themselves but does not provide an absolute upper limit for every effect they can resist.

#### Demonic Surveillance Network (External)
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] supplied Tasha with hundreds of Demons distributed across Samar as a covert intelligence network.
- Shared Senses: Tasha can connect to the sensory perspectives of Demons serving as her remote observers.[14]
- Remote Vision: Red supernatural circles appear within her eyes while she observes locations through connected Demons.[14]
- Remote Hearing: Victor describes the Demons as both Tasha's eyes and ears throughout Samar.[14]
- Perspective Switching: She can move her observation between different Demonic agents stationed around the planet.[14]
- Long-Range Surveillance: Tasha can monitor distant locations without physically travelling there.[14]
- Intelligence Gathering: The network massively expands the amount of information available to Tasha as Samar's ruler.[14]
This network is external support supplied by Victor and should not be confused with Tasha's innate Werewolf-Progenitor Soul network.

#### Resistances

##### Progenitor Mental Resistance
Night-Creature Progenitors possess extremely high resistance to ordinary mind-control methods.
- Mind Manipulation Resistance: Progenitor physiology offers powerful natural resistance against attempts to directly control the mind.[18]
- Hypnosis Resistance: Ordinary hypnotic or domination abilities should have extreme difficulty overcoming a Progenitor-level existence.[18]
- Non-Absolute Resistance: Exceptionally powerful Divine Concepts directly governing Love, Mind, Emotion or similar areas can potentially overcome racial mental defenses if sufficiently superior.[18]
This is a class-based Progenitor resistance rather than a Tasha-specific immunity feat.

##### Other Resistances
- Corruption Resistance / Protection: Tasha benefits from [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later defenses against physical, mental and Soul corruption.[17]
- Soul Manipulation Resistance / Protection: Her Divine-Progenitor Soul is further protected through Victor's Soul-fragment system.[2][17]
- Reality Manipulation Protection: Victor's wife-protection system includes resistance against hostile Reality interference.[17]
- Bad Luck Manipulation Protection: Victor explicitly includes supernatural bad luck among the effects against which his protective framework is designed.[17]
- Destruction Protection: Tasha benefits from the defensive measures Victor gives his wives against supernatural destruction effects.[17]
- High Pain Tolerance: Tasha remains functional through severe combat and emotional stress during her conflict with Volk.[8][5]
- Mental Discipline: [[Hassan-i-Sabbah]]'s teachings allow Tasha to regain control over herself even after psychologically devastating discoveries.[13]

#### Skills

##### Master Assassin
Tasha possesses extensive assassination training associated with [[Hassan-i-Sabbah]] and thousands of years surviving hostile supernatural politics.
- Assassination Arts: Tasha understands methods designed to kill powerful supernatural targets through preparation and exploitation of weaknesses rather than honorable frontal confrontation.[1][5]
- Target Analysis: She studies an opponent's physiology, supernatural advantages and exploitable weaknesses before committing to an elimination plan.[5]
- Assassination Preparation: Tasha arranged concealed support specifically equipped to interfere with Volk's greatest racial advantages.[5]
- Anti-Werewolf Weapon Planning: Her hidden forces used Demon Metal and Wolfsbane ammunition specifically to disrupt Volk's Werewolf regeneration.[5]
- Ambush Coordination: Tasha incorporated concealed attackers into her confrontation at the moment most useful to her overall strategy.[5]
The long-range shots against Volk were fired by Tasha's prepared agents, not Tasha personally. They demonstrate assassination planning and command rather than personal sniper-level Marksmanship.[5]

##### Master Espionage
- Espionage: Tasha is one of Samar's foremost experts in covert intelligence work.[1]
- Covert Surveillance: She secretly monitored important negotiations through surveillance placed around Volk and his political circle.[1]
- Counter-Espionage: Tasha habitually investigates the possibility that enemy agents have infiltrated her territory.[19]
- Intelligence Analysis: She reconstructs threats and political intentions from incomplete information rather than requiring complete certainty before beginning countermeasures.[19][1]
- Covert Government Administration: Tasha managed much of the "dark" side of Samar's government, including spies, assassins and hidden political operations.[1]
- Large-Scale Intelligence Management: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later expands Tasha's intelligence infrastructure by granting her hundreds of Demonic observers across Samar.[14]

##### Master Strategist
- Strategic Intelligence: Tasha consistently prioritizes preparation, information and favorable conditions rather than unnecessary direct confrontation.[20]
- Long-Term Planning: She maintained contingency systems capable of remaining useful even after thousands of years of marriage and political coexistence with Volk.[20]
- Emergency Infrastructure: Tasha prepared independent shelters, fallback positions and secure assets in case the Werewolf monarchy turned against her.[20][10]
- Resource Preservation: During Volk's coup, she immediately prioritized securing critical people, documents and resources rather than wasting time on emotional retaliation.[20][13]
- Independent Power Structure: Tasha deliberately maintained followers and resources outside Volk's total political control.[20]
- Political Manipulation: Her experience among Egyptian Gods and Werewolf rulers made her exceptionally dangerous within supernatural politics.[10]
- Pantheon-Level Cunning: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] notes that Tasha retained influence over Lesser Egyptian Gods despite being absent from Egypt for thousands of years.[10]
- Threat Assessment: Tasha habitually plans around betrayal even when dealing with individuals who are technically allies.[20][13]

##### Psychological Warfare
- Provocation: Tasha deliberately exploits emotional weaknesses to push opponents into predictable reactions.[8]
- Jealousy Exploitation: She weaponized Volk's insecurity and obsession regarding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] during their conflict.[8]
- Personal Weakness Exploitation: Thousands of years of marriage gave Tasha extensive knowledge of Volk's pride, habits and psychological vulnerabilities.[20][8]
- Psychological Breaking: After defeating Volk, Tasha deliberately explains the humiliating future she intends for him instead of providing the death he would consider more merciful.[6]
- Intimidation: Her predatory presence as the Monarch of Beasts can be intentionally projected to unsettle or terrify weaker individuals.[10]

##### Emotional Discipline
- Emotional Suppression: Tasha can conceal extreme anger, grief and fear when exposing those emotions would weaken her political position.[13]
- Rapid Emotional Stabilization: After discovering additional evidence of Volk's betrayal, she deliberately regains control instead of immediately attacking him.[13]
- Ruler's Mask: Tasha can maintain the calm external demeanor expected of a Queen while internally experiencing intense emotional turmoil.[13]
- [[Hassan-i-Sabbah]]'s Mental Discipline: Her master's philosophy allows Tasha to acknowledge emotional impulses without automatically surrendering her judgment to them.[13]

##### Leadership and Administration
- Rulership: Tasha governed Samar alongside Volk for thousands of years and handled substantial portions of its actual administration.[1][20]
- Crisis Management: When [[Fenrir]] mysteriously deteriorated, Tasha immediately considered sabotage and began investigating threats to Samar.[19]
- Civilian Protection Planning: Her contingency network included preparations intended to preserve loyal personnel and civilians if Samar's central government collapsed around her.[20][10]
- Military Command: Tasha can command Werewolf forces, assassins and covert operatives as part of her larger political structure.[5]
- Independent Faction Leadership: Even before becoming Progenitor, Tasha possessed a political faction loyal directly to her rather than Volk.[20]
- Racial Leadership: Becoming Progenitor elevated Tasha from Queen of one civilization into the supernatural ruler standing above the entire Werewolf hierarchy.[2][3]
- Hierarchy Reconstruction: She can restructure Alpha leadership according to her own judgment.[3]
- Civilizational Development: By Chapter 1179, Samar had expanded to approximately three times the size of old Earth, its Werewolf bloodline had greatly improved, and Tasha had developed approximately fifty planetary-level elites through strategic hybridization.[4]
- Hybrid-Elite Development: Tasha deliberately exploited the Empire's advances in stable hybridization to compensate for Samar's difficulty producing sufficiently powerful elites through pure Werewolf bloodlines alone.[4]

##### Close-Quarters Combat
- Experienced Combatant: Tasha possesses thousands of years of supernatural experience and can personally fight powerful Werewolf opponents.[8][5]
- Werewolf Combat Mastery: She understands the strengths, transformations, regeneration and weaknesses of Werewolf physiology intimately.[8][5]
- Grappling: Tasha physically seized Volk by the neck and controlled his body during their confrontation.[5]
- Powerful Strikes: Her direct attacks inflicted severe bodily damage upon Volk.[8]
- Combat Feinting: She mixes direct attacks, psychological manipulation and concealed support to force opponents into unfavorable decisions.[8][5]
- Hybrid-Form Combat: Tasha is capable of fighting effectively while using her advanced Werewolf Hybrid Form.[8]
- Divine Combat: Her combat effectiveness rises substantially once her suppressed Divine Power begins returning.[8][5]

#### Former Powers and States

##### Original Egyptian Goddess
Before Samar, Tasha existed as a comparatively minor Goddess within the [[Egyptian Pantheon]].
- Minor Goddess Physiology: Her original Divine state was substantially less developed than the High-Level Goddess she later became.[21][5]
- Wolf-Related Divinity: Tasha's ancient Divine nature was associated with Wolves.[21]
- Limited Conceptual Development: Her earlier Divine authority was comparatively underdeveloped before her later awakening and Progenitor evolution.[21][2]
- Monarch of Beasts: Her savage temperament and political cunning nevertheless earned her an infamous title among the Egyptian Gods.[10]
Descriptions of ancient Tasha as "evil" or extraordinarily cruel should not automatically be converted into separately confirmed `<Evil>` or `<Cruelty>` Divinities unless the novel directly names those Concepts.

##### Fallen Goddess
After leaving or losing her former Egyptian position, Tasha existed for an extended period as a Fallen Goddess.
- Retained Divine Nature: Her fall did not reduce Tasha into a normal mortal Werewolf.[1][5]
- Retained Divine Power: Portions of her original Divine nature remained available even before her complete restoration.[1][8]
- Reduced Divine Status: Her effective Godhood remained below the level she eventually recovered during the Volk conflict.[5]
- Suppressed Growth: Tasha's own choices and political circumstances prevented her from developing her Divine existence to its true potential for an extended period.[21][5]
This state ended when she reclaimed and evolved her Godhood.[5]

##### Alpha Werewolf (Former Rank)
Before becoming Progenitor, Tasha was already an extremely powerful Alpha.
- Alpha Presence: Tasha possessed the instinctive authority and pressure associated with an Alpha Werewolf.[8]
- Pack Command: She commanded [[Werewolves]] formally belonging to her own faction.[20]
- Pack Empowerment: Her previous Alpha existence operated through the ordinary Werewolf relationship between Alpha and pack before being superseded by the Progenitor network.[2]
- Hybrid Transformation: She already possessed a powerful Hybrid Form before becoming Progenitor.[8]
- Lunar Empowerment: Like other Werewolves, she benefits from Full-Moon conditions.[8][15]
These ordinary Alpha mechanics were later superseded by her position as the Alpha Among Alphas.

#### Unconfirmed or Frequently Misattributed Abilities
The following powers should not presently be presented as individually confirmed Tasha abilities without additional novel evidence.
- Beginning Divinity: Tasha's position as a racial Progenitor conceptually places her at the "beginning/source" of the Werewolf Race, and supplementary material has associated Progenitors with Beginning. However, Chapters 849 and 989 do not separately name `<Beginning>` as one of Tasha's demonstrated personal Divinities.[2][7]
- Nature Divinity: Tasha has Nature Energy, World Tree compatibility and produces a daughter whose Nature authority originates from her maternal inheritance, but Chapter 989 does not directly state that Tasha possesses a formal `<Nature>` Divinity.[7]
- Animals Divinity: Chapter 849 specifically establishes a Divinity encompassing the Wolf Race and its subspecies; it does not independently establish `<Animals>` as another Divine Concept.[2]
- Cruelty / Evil Divinities: Tasha's ancient reputation was extremely violent, but descriptive references to an "evil Goddess" are insufficient to establish formal `<Evil>` or `<Cruelty>` Divinities.[10]
- The End Manipulation: [[Fenrir]] possesses an exceptional relationship with The End, but receiving his Blessing does not automatically grant Tasha Fenrir's complete conceptual powers.[3]
- [[Sanguis Noctis Regis]] / Blood of the Night King: Tasha's Progenitor transformation explicitly involved unusual external intervention despite her lacking the conventional prerequisite discussed for other Night-Creature Progenitors. It should not be indexed as a normal inherited Tasha bloodline without direct confirmation.[2]
- Unrestricted Hive Mind: The Werewolf Race is spiritually networked through Tasha, but no chapter establishes continuous unrestricted access to every member's thoughts, memories or senses.[2]
- Personal Sniper Marksmanship: The long-range anti-Werewolf shots used during the Volk battle were fired by Tasha's hidden operatives, not Tasha herself.[5]
- Moon Creation: Tasha summons/manifests the Full Moon's presence and influence; the feat should not be described as creating a new celestial Moon from nothing.[8]
- Complete Dragon Ability Set: Chapter 1179 establishes that Tasha is currently Draconic, but it does not personally demonstrate Dragon Breath, Dragon Eyes, Dragon Language, Draconic Runes, a giant Dragon Form or every other standard True Dragon ability.[4]

#### Limitations
- Pack Amplification Depends on the Race: The additional Power supplied through Tasha's Progenitor network depends upon connected [[Werewolves]]. Losing a member removes that member's contribution even though Tasha retains her own independent strength.[2]
- Racial Power Withdrawal Is Species-Restricted: Tasha's demonstrated consequence-free withdrawal and absorption of Power applies to members of her own Werewolf Race.[6]
- Raw Power Only: Taking another Werewolf's Power does not automatically grant Tasha their memories, experience or unique personal abilities.[6]
- No Demonstrated Universal Thought Reading: Her Progenitor bond does not currently provide evidence of unrestricted access to every Werewolf's mind.[2]
- Demonic Shared Senses Require External Agents: Tasha's planet-wide remote surveillance relies upon Demons supplied by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] rather than her own Progenitor network.[14]
- [[Fenrir]]'s Concept Is Not Automatically Hers: Fenrir's Blessing should not be used as evidence for unrestricted End Manipulation.[3]
- Later Draconic Powers Remain Undemonstrated: Tasha is currently a Dragon-Werewolf hybrid, but the complete powers created by that evolution have not yet been displayed in the available material.[4]

#### Notes
- Chapter 850's Low-Level God-King rating is an early benchmark. It represents Tasha immediately after becoming Progenitor and absorbing Volk's Power rather than her later maximum strength.[6][7]
- Chapter 989's "non-True-Dragon" statement is historical rather than current. At that point Tasha and Lilith were specifically the two non-True-Dragon Progenitor Goddesses of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s main group, but Chapter 1179 later describes Tasha as a Dragon with Werewolf traits.[7][4]
- The exact chapter of Tasha's Draconic transformation is currently unidentified. The available late-story transcript confirms the completed state but does not explain when or how the change occurred.[4]
- Full Moon Summoning is preferred over Moon Creation. The Chapter 847 feat concerns the Full Moon's supernatural manifestation/influence during daytime rather than manufacturing an unrelated artificial Moon.[8]
- The Progenitor network is hive-like, not a confirmed unrestricted Hive Mind. Its demonstrated functions concern spiritual connection, hierarchy and Power distribution.[2]
- Tasha's Progenitor Soul specializes in distribution. Victor explicitly contrasts her system with his own accumulation-oriented Progenitor nature.[2]
- The Werewolf bond can be severed. Tasha demonstrates this personally by expelling Volk from the racial network.[6]
- The external force that completed Tasha's Progenitor ascension remains unidentified. Victor recognized that something intervened after the candidate contest but did not establish whether it was the System, Balance or another higher mechanism.[2]
- Nature-based abilities shown by Tasha's daughter should remain separated from Tasha's direct arsenal. They demonstrate inheritance from Tasha, not automatic proof that the mother can personally reproduce every one of the daughter's techniques.[7]
- Tasha's current Draconic state should be updated as new chapters explain it. Chapter 1179 confirms the hybrid state but does not yet provide enough evidence to assign the standard complete Blood Dragon arsenal.[4]
