**[[Roberta Elderblood]]**, formerly known as Roberta Alucard, is a former Witch, a True Blood Dragon, one of Victor Elderblood's personal Battle Maids and one of his wives. (Lore adapted from *My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires*.)

Events of the MTWABV timeline (Victor's conquests, the fall of the pantheons, the [[Elderblood Empire]]) are distant background or rumor unless a chapter puts them on the page. ### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 60
Roberta Elderblood, formerly known as Roberta Alucard, is a former Witch, a True Blood Dragon, one of [[Victor Elderblood]]'s personal Battle Maids and one of his wives. She is a descendant and current host of [[Medusa]] Elderblood, the ancient Divine Spirit, original Queen and Progenitor of the [[Gorgons]]. Roberta possesses an unusually complex existence produced by the interaction between her own traumatic psychology, an innate ability capable of manipulating memories and minds, and the vengeful Divine Spirit of Medusa that resided within her long before she entered Victor's family.[5][3]
Roberta initially appeared within [[The Lost Club]] as a mysterious woman believed by others to be one of [[Johnny Lykos]]' lovers. That history was largely false. Roberta had manipulated Johnny's memories until he genuinely believed she occupied an established place within his life, using the Alpha Werewolf and his supernatural presence as camouflage while she kept herself hidden from other supernatural factions.[6][7] Victor immediately detected inconsistencies surrounding her existence and discovered that an unknown entity protected her when he attempted to test her. Rather than eliminate the potentially dangerous woman, Victor became fascinated by both Roberta and whatever was living inside her, imposed a magical contract restricting whom she could manipulate and brought her into his household.[7][8]
Over time Roberta voluntarily became one of Victor's maids, a Noble Vampire and a permanent member of his family. Victor eventually asked Roberta, Kaguya, Bruna and Maria whether they wanted to enter his Clan permanently rather than remain people connected to him only through service. Roberta immediately accepted and received the name Roberta Alucard, describing the new connection created between herself and Victor as something essentially unbreakable.[2]
Her already abnormal existence became far more significant when the Spirit inside her finally revealed herself as Medusa. Medusa was not simply another personality of Roberta but her ancient ancestor: the original Gorgon Progenitor who survived death as both a Heroic and Divine Spirit. Medusa remained capable of expressing her own personality, memories, abilities and will through Roberta's body while simultaneously influencing the psychological development of her descendant.[3]
Roberta later evolved with Victor's family into a True Blood Dragon. Rather than losing the traits inherited from Medusa, the transformation integrated them more deeply into Roberta herself. Her eyes acquired Medusa's ability to petrify beings with a controlled glance, while her pre-existing Memory Manipulation became vastly more powerful.[1] She eventually became one of the Dragon Goddesses belonging to Victor's family and the [[Elderblood Empire]].

### Physical Description

#### Human/Witch
At the beginning of the story Roberta possessed the appearance of a mature woman approximately forty years old. She stood at roughly 188 cm and possessed a highly voluptuous figure, very long silky black hair that extended almost to the floor, healthy white skin and striking violet eyes with a naturally playful quality. Her overall demeanor produced an unusual contradiction: she possessed an extremely seductive presence while simultaneously seeming to carry a deep underlying melancholy.[9]
Roberta commonly wore long, flowing dresses that emphasized her mature appearance. Before becoming one of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s maids she was frequently seen in black dresses reaching approximately her ankles.[9]
Her extraordinarily long hair was eventually revealed to possess more than aesthetic importance. Roberta can consciously manipulate it as though it were alive, using individual locks to surround, bind and restrain targets.[10] This serpentine quality foreshadowed the truth of the being hidden within her long before [[Medusa]] openly revealed her identity.

#### Noble Vampire
Roberta was originally a Human Witch, but she was eventually transformed into a Noble Vampire after entering [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household. By Chapter 394 she explicitly identifies herself as someone who had been a Witch before becoming a Vampire.[10]
Like Victor's other descendants, her transformation provided the physical characteristics and advantages of a Noble Vampire while preserving rather than erasing her unusual pre-existing abilities. Her eyes became Vampiric red, although they could assume a distinctly serpentine appearance when she invoked powers connected to [[Medusa]].[10]
Roberta adopted the black-and-white maid uniform associated with Victor's household after formally entering the [[Maid Squad]], although her extremely long hair and elegant demeanor continued distinguishing her visually from the other Battle Maids.

#### Blood Dragon
Roberta was eventually reconstructed as a True Blood Dragon alongside Kaguya, Eve, Maria, Natalia and numerous other members of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family. She acquired the horns, wings, Draconic Eyes, enhanced physiology, enormous lifespan and racial potential associated with Victor's Blood Dragon lineage.[1]
Her previous powers were not replaced by generic Dragon abilities. The transformation strengthened the individual supernatural foundation Roberta already possessed. Her eyes gained the same innate Petrification carried by [[Medusa]], allowing Roberta herself to turn beings to stone through a glance while consciously controlling whether the effect activates.[1]
Her Memory Manipulation likewise underwent another substantial evolution. An ability that once required her to carefully establish convincing false experiences inside Johnny's mind became powerful enough after the Dragon transformation to alter another being's memories through direct visual interaction.[1]
The transformation therefore blurred the distinction between abilities that had originally belonged specifically to Medusa and abilities available directly to Roberta. Roberta remained her own person, but her Dragon body had incorporated significant characteristics of the Progenitor residing inside her.

### Personality and Traits
Roberta possesses one of the most psychologically complicated personalities within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household. Her apparently gentle behavior should not be interpreted as simple deception: the affectionate and harmless Roberta is a genuine part of her personality. At the same time she possesses an extraordinarily possessive, manipulative and sadistic side capable of treating another person's life and memories as toys.
[[Scathach]] studied Roberta directly and concluded that her condition was substantially more complicated than an ordinary split personality. At the time, three distinguishable presences could be identified: Roberta's gentle and affectionate self, a sadistic and possessive protective personality and the vengeful Heroic Spirit residing within her.[5]
Scathach theorized that the second Roberta developed through a combination of Roberta's own trauma—particularly emotional wounds connected to abandonment and family—and the influence of the violent memories and hatred carried by the Spirit inhabiting her. The cruel personality consequently functioned in part as a protector for the gentler Roberta, expressing the aggression and willingness to hurt other people that the original personality attempted to suppress.[5]
The third presence was later proven to be [[Medusa]] Elderblood. This distinction is important: Medusa is not simply Roberta's third personality. She is an independent ancestral Divine Spirit possessing her own history, consciousness, Divine authority and Race. However, centuries of coexistence left Roberta's psyche, Medusa's emotions and the protective personality so heavily intertwined that they significantly influenced one another.[5][3]
Unlike Natashia, whose alternate personality could be identified more cleanly as another aspect of the same individual, Scathach considered Roberta's situation substantially harder to separate because an actual external Spirit had become entangled with Roberta's trauma.[5]
Roberta is aware that her behavior can be abnormal. Rather than being completely oblivious to her own cruelty, she frequently accepts it with a disturbing degree of casualness. She can move from a pleasant smile and polite conversation to threatening murder without substantially changing her tone.
This became increasingly visible after joining the [[Maid Squad]]. Roberta is intensely possessive of Victor and the other women she regards as belonging to him. When several men stared inappropriately at the women serving Victor, Roberta used her hair to restrain one of them and calmly warned that Victor would not even need to punish such behavior himself because his maids would do it for him.[10]
Despite this possessiveness, Roberta is not unintelligent or ruled entirely by instinct. She is highly observant, understands supernatural politics and can explain complicated subjects clearly enough that the other maids occasionally defer to her when introducing inexperienced individuals to supernatural society.[10]

#### Manipulative Nature
Roberta's instinct for manipulation predates [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Her unusual memory ability allowed her to create an entire false history with [[Johnny Lykos]] and convince him that their relationship was genuine. Rather than merely using conventional seduction, she inserted false memories deeply enough that Johnny's mind naturally accepted her presence within his life.[7]
Roberta later explained that Johnny had been deliberately selected because his mind was comparatively easy to manipulate while his scent and status as an Alpha Werewolf made him an excellent supernatural shield. Remaining close to Johnny allowed Roberta to hide behind the presence of a much more obvious supernatural individual while concealing both herself and [[Medusa]] from beings that might have discovered them.[10]
This demonstrates that much of Roberta's apparently passive existence before Victor was intentional. She was not simply a vulnerable woman Johnny happened to rescue. She had actively constructed a protected position around herself.

#### Devotion to Victor
Roberta's attachment to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] developed partially from his reaction to discovering what she had done. Almost anyone else who realized she could rewrite memories would have treated Roberta exclusively as a threat. Victor instead became fascinated.
He recognized that whatever existed inside her possessed extraordinary potential and deliberately chose to preserve that potential rather than destroy it. When Roberta questioned whether he could actually guarantee her safety, Victor's confidence that there was effectively nowhere safer than beside him appealed strongly to her.[7][8]
Her original relationship with Victor was therefore founded upon mutual curiosity rather than immediate love. Roberta believed Victor might eventually become powerful enough to oppose the Gods connected to the hatred residing within her and [[Medusa]], while Victor wanted to see what Roberta and the unknown entity would eventually become.
That curiosity gradually became genuine devotion. By Chapters 491–492, Roberta and the other Battle Maids had become precious to Victor beyond their usefulness as servants, while Roberta's own feelings toward him had similarly passed beyond ordinary loyalty.[11][12]
When Victor finally offered her permanent membership in his family, Roberta accepted almost immediately. She leaned against him and, in an unusually vulnerable moment, asked him to take care of them. Victor responded by naming her Roberta Alucard, transforming what had previously been an intense master-servant attachment into a permanent familial bond.[2]

### Early Life
Very little has been revealed about Roberta's original family or childhood. What is known is that she was born Human and possessed enough connection with [[Magic]] to be classified as a Witch. Unlike highly trained Witches such as members of the Moriarty family, however, Roberta described herself as only possessing basic knowledge of spells and admitted that she was never particularly proficient in conventional Witchcraft.[10]
Her most dangerous abilities were therefore never ordinary Witch Magic. Roberta specifically distinguishes her Memory Manipulation from the spells used by Witches, comparing the effect more closely to an innate supernatural ability such as Vampiric Charm.[10]
At some point Roberta discovered that another being existed inside her. The realization caused her to deliberately keep a low profile. This instinct for concealment prevented the highly opportunistic Witch community from discovering her unusual abilities and the ancient Spirit hidden inside her.[10]
The Spirit was eventually revealed to be [[Medusa]] Elderblood, meaning Roberta is a descendant of the original Gorgon Progenitor. Exactly how Medusa's surviving Spirit passed through the generations before becoming attached to Roberta remains incompletely explained, but by Roberta's adult life the two had already become deeply connected.[3]
Roberta also experienced severe personal trauma involving family and abandonment before entering the story. [[Scathach]] believed those experiences played an essential role in producing Roberta's protective sadistic personality, although the precise events responsible have not yet been fully revealed.[5]

### Synopsis

#### Vampire Society
Roberta first appeared publicly during events involving [[Johnny Lykos]] and [[The Lost Club]]. At the time she was presented as one of the women associated with Johnny and frequently maintained a gentle, almost angelic smile. She also appeared around Jinsei and other members of the Club's supernatural community.[9]
Johnny believed that he possessed an established intimate relationship with Roberta and behaved accordingly. Nothing initially suggested to him that there was anything false about their history.
This impression eventually proved to be artificial. Roberta had used her supernatural Memory Manipulation to insert herself into Johnny's life and construct memories of a relationship that had never actually occurred. Johnny's supposed history with Roberta was consequently one of the earliest demonstrations of just how deeply she could alter another person's perception of reality.[7]

#### A New Pillar
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s first meaningful encounter with Roberta occurred after he accidentally returned to [[The Lost Club]]. Unlike the people already accustomed to her presence, Victor immediately noticed that something about the woman's history was wrong. Memories and emotional connections surrounding her did not fit together naturally, and his instincts detected something unusual existing inside her.[6][7]
When Victor questioned Johnny about Roberta, the Werewolf could not provide a coherent explanation capable of satisfying him. Victor consequently began testing the woman directly.
The situation changed immediately when Victor attacked. A supernatural presence associated with Roberta responded defensively, creating enough protection to stop Victor's strike. Victor compared what he sensed to the type of Heroic Spirit associated with beings such as [[Abe-no-Seimei]], but the entity remained indistinct apart from its strange presence and eyes.[7]
Rather than frightening Victor away, this made Roberta exponentially more interesting to him.
Victor realized that she had modified Johnny's memories and that removing the false experiences produced severe consequences for the Werewolf, who subsequently fell into a catatonic or comatose condition while his mind recovered from the interference.[7][8]
Victor then made a characteristically unusual decision. Instead of killing the unknown woman or turning her over to another supernatural faction, he decided to take Roberta with him and personally observe what the existence inside her would eventually become.
To prevent Roberta's abilities from becoming a danger to his family, Victor established a magical contract. Roberta remained largely free to act as she wished, but she was forbidden from using her powers against anyone Victor designated as protected. Victor also intended to keep her under observation while eventually discovering a method of awakening the Spirit hidden inside her.[7][8]
Roberta asked whether he could actually protect her if she accepted. Victor effectively answered that if she was not safe by his side, she would not be safe anywhere. His confidence satisfied her, and she joined him.[7]
Her arrival at Victor's home immediately complicated an already chaotic household. Anna encountered Roberta alongside figures such as Natashia and Bruna and struggled to determine exactly which of the ever-growing number of women surrounding her son were wives, relatives, servants or potential romantic interests.[13][14]
During the prolonged period Victor experienced on Earth because of [[Nightingale]]'s altered temporal flow, Roberta's temporary association developed into permanent service. She eventually became a Noble Vampire and one of Victor's Battle Maids. By the time Victor returned to Nightingale, Roberta was firmly part of the group centered around Kaguya.[15][10]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Roberta's early career as an actual maid demonstrated that supernatural talent did not automatically produce domestic competence. Unlike Kaguya, whose entire childhood had been devoted to perfect service, Roberta had essentially no comparable training and could be catastrophically bad at ordinary household duties. One infamous cooking attempt resulted in enough destruction that the kitchen required reconstruction, contributing to the recurring question of whether Roberta could genuinely be called a successful maid.[16]
Despite her shortcomings in domestic work, Roberta proved considerably more useful in combat, intelligence and supernatural analysis. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had never chosen the [[Maid Squad]] purely because he wanted servants capable of cooking and cleaning; he wanted a handful of exceptional women capable of surviving the increasingly dangerous enemies surrounding his family.
[[Scathach]] eventually examined Roberta directly while evaluating several of Victor's people. Her analysis exposed just how complicated Roberta's psychological condition had become. Scathach identified the gentle Roberta, the sadistic protective Roberta and the unknown vengeful Spirit as interconnected but distinguishable presences. She theorized that Roberta's more violent personality had been produced through the collision between her personal trauma and the hatred carried by the Spirit.[5]
Unlike a conventional second personality, however, the Spirit was an independent existence. Its emotions had simply become intertwined deeply enough with Roberta's own psyche that determining precisely where one influence ended and another began was extremely difficult.[5]
Roberta's own explanation in Chapter 394 revealed additional information about her past. She confirmed that she had originally been a Witch before becoming a Vampire, although she knew only basic [[Magic]]. Her Memory Manipulation had never been ordinary Witchcraft, but an innate supernatural ability that she compared more closely to Vampiric Charm.[10]
She further explained why the Witches had failed to discover someone with such an unusual power. Once she realized another being existed inside her, Roberta instinctively remained hidden. Johnny had simply been the easiest available camouflage: his mind was vulnerable to her influence, while the presence and scent of an Alpha Werewolf discouraged other supernatural beings from investigating too closely.[10]
Chapter 394 also demonstrated how profoundly possessive Roberta had become toward Victor. When men who had recently entered Victor's service looked at the women surrounding him in a manner she disliked, Roberta used her hair to restrain one of them and calmly explained that inappropriate behavior toward women belonging to her Master could be enough to make them disappear.[10]
By the following chapter the emotional relationship between Victor and his maids had also become increasingly explicit. Victor no longer regarded them merely as employees, while Roberta and the other women made little effort to conceal that their devotion contained romantic attachment.[17]
The same development continued throughout the remainder of the volume. Victor openly acknowledged Kaguya, Eve, Bruna, Roberta and Maria as precious people in his life while the distinction between his "maids" and his broader family became increasingly artificial.[18][19]

#### Demonic War
Roberta fought beside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction throughout the Demonic War as a fully established Battle Maid. Her Memory Manipulation, hair, abilities inherited through the Spirit within her and increasingly refined Vampiric power made her particularly dangerous against opponents vulnerable to unconventional forms of attack.
During Victor's intervention in the battle surrounding the Vatican, Roberta was among the women accompanying him through the Shadow-based support network connected to Kaguya. Together with Kaguya, Bruna, Eve, Maria, Roxanne and the others, Roberta remained close to Victor even as he entered battle against the Demonic armies threatening the region.[20][21]
Her most important personal development came before Victor's confrontation with [[Otsuki Elderblood Haruna]]. Victor gathered the Battle Maids and confronted a problem he had allowed to persist for too long: Kaguya, Bruna, Maria and Roberta were being called servants despite already functioning as members of his family.[2]
Victor therefore asked whether they wanted to become permanent members of his Clan and lineage.
Roberta accepted. Unlike the playful and sadistic manner she frequently displayed, her reaction became unusually vulnerable. She approached Victor, rested against him and asked him to take care of them. Victor formally accepted her and spoke the new name Roberta Alucard.[2]
Roberta immediately felt the relationship between them become substantially deeper. The insecurities and uncertainties surrounding whether she truly possessed a permanent place beside Victor largely disappeared once he personally recognized her as family rather than merely another useful subordinate.[2]
Victor subsequently used his Progenitor Blood to strengthen the Battle Maids and bring them more directly into his lineage. Roberta's existing supernatural characteristics were preserved rather than erased, continuing the pattern through which Victor's blood amplified unusual traits already present within the people entering his family.[22]
The greater mystery within Roberta was finally resolved shortly afterward.
During events surrounding [[Persephone Elderblood]] and the [[Greek Underworld]], the entity residing within Roberta openly identified herself as [[Medusa]]: the ancient Queen of the [[Gorgons]], a Divine Spirit and the original Progenitor of the Gorgon Race.[3]
The revelation shocked even the people who already knew Roberta possessed Petrification and that a Spirit lived inside her. The Battle Maids had been familiar with some of Medusa's abilities, but they had not known that the being hidden within their companion was an actual Progenitor.[3]
Medusa then summoned surviving Gorgons and declared her intentions toward Victor. She offered the Fate of herself and her Race to him and proposed a permanent spiritual bond equivalent to the union previously formed between Victor and [[Aphrodite]]. Roberta was not excluded from this decision: the two women had already discussed the matter internally and Roberta herself supported Medusa's choice.[3]
The women around Victor recognized the bond as a [[Soul Marriage]]. Importantly, however, the Soul Marriage itself was between Victor and Medusa. Roberta's own position as Victor's wife and Clan member came through her separate relationship with him and her incorporation into his Bloodline.[3]
Victor accepted Medusa but refused to absorb the entire Gorgon Race into Clan Alucard. He clarified that his Clan was intended for genuine family rather than becoming an organization swallowing every species aligned with him. The Gorgons could follow Medusa and ally themselves with Victor while retaining their own racial identity and leadership.[3]
Roberta and Medusa therefore occupied two overlapping but distinct positions within Victor's growing faction: Roberta as his Battle Maid, Clan member and wife, and Medusa as the Gorgon Progenitor and spiritually married Queen whose Race had aligned its Fate with Victor.
While Victor remained trapped in Hell later in the war, Roberta joined Kaguya, Eve and Maria in unleashing their frustration upon the Demonic forces operating in Japan. Roberta fought alongside the Basilisk and used Petrification against Demonic enemies while the maids vented the rage produced by their inability to reach Victor.[23][24]

#### King Among Kings
The centuries [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and his followers experienced through Hell's altered temporal conditions significantly deepened the relationships inside his family. Roberta's marriage with Victor ceased being a merely political or technical Clan connection and became fully intimate alongside the other women who had entered his family.[25]
Roberta also became one of the early women influenced by Bruna's religious devotion toward Victor. What began with Bruna eventually spread through Maria, Eve and Roberta before moving into the Snow and Fulger Clans and developing into the organized [[Blood God Religion]].[26]
Roberta's connection with [[Medusa]] became particularly important when Victor turned his attention toward the [[Greek Pantheon]]. Medusa's hatred of the Olympian Gods was not abstract. Her history and death had been intimately connected with the Greek Divine order, leaving her existence saturated with resentment toward several of its members.
When Victor invaded [[Olympus]], he placed Maria, Roberta, Bruna, Kaguya and Eve under Kaguya's command and supplied them with weapons infused with his own Blood so that the Battle Maids could permanently kill Gods.[27]
For Roberta/Medusa, the campaign represented the opportunity to confront the Pantheon associated with Medusa's suffering. Medusa called upon the [[Gorgons]] and participated alongside Roberta in Victor's destruction of Olympus, turning what had once been an ancient source of helpless hatred into a war fought from a position of overwhelming strength.[27]
By this stage Roberta's identity within Victor's faction was no longer mysterious. She was simultaneously one of his elite maids, a wife, the host and descendant of Medusa, an early adherent of his growing religion and one of the combatants trusted to participate directly in conflicts against Pantheons.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] ascended as the Dragon God of [[Chaos]] and founded the [[Blood Dragons]], Roberta became one of the closest family members selected for full racial conversion into a True Blood Dragon.[1]
The transformation did not erase the unusual structure created by [[Medusa]]'s presence. Roberta remained herself and Medusa remained an independent Divine Spirit sharing her existence, but the characteristics Roberta had inherited or borrowed through their connection became far more deeply integrated into her new Draconic body.
Roberta acquired Draconic horns and wings matching her dark hair, Draconic Eyes and all of the immense physical and spiritual advantages inherent to a True Dragon. Her primary abilities simultaneously underwent enormous improvement.[1]
The greatest development was Petrification. Before the transformation Roberta could access Medusa-related abilities, but the power fundamentally belonged to the Gorgon Progenitor. After becoming a Blood Dragon, Roberta's own eyes possessed the same basic property as Medusa's eyes: she could petrify another being simply by looking at them and could consciously determine when the effect activated.[1]
Her Memory Manipulation advanced alongside it. The power that once allowed her to construct false histories inside Johnny's mind could now be applied through her gaze at a substantially more invasive level.[1]
Roberta thus became one of the clearest examples of Victor's Blood Dragon transformation preserving and magnifying unique supernatural characteristics. Rather than reducing her to a standardized Dragon, the Race elevated the strange combination of Witch, Vampire, Gorgon-descendant and memory manipulator that already constituted Roberta's identity.

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
The accelerated temporal environments available to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family eventually allowed Roberta to experience approximately two thousand years of additional development while comparatively little time passed outside Dragon's Nest. By the beginning of the Imperial era, she had completely matured into one of Victor's Dragon Goddess wives.
Her relationship with [[Medusa]] remained unusually close because the two continued sharing the same existence rather than simply becoming independent women with separate bodies. Later family discussions consequently frequently refer to them together as Roberta/Medusa.
The desire to have children became especially intense after Victor's first biological daughters began appearing. Roberta/Medusa were among the wives who reacted most strongly to seeing Victor interact with his children and wanted a daughter with him themselves.[28]
They eventually succeeded.
Chapter 998 originally describes Roberta/Medusa as one of the cases in which a wife had produced twins.[29] Later material, however, refers specifically to Victor's daughter with Roberta/Medusa in the singular when discussing children born from Progenitor mothers.[4] Because the later chapter is the more recent statement, this article currently lists a single unnamed daughter rather than preserving the earlier "twin daughters" wording as definitive.
Their daughter inherited Victor's dominant Blood Dragon nature despite Medusa herself being a Progenitor. Victor's Blood Dragon lineage was described as sufficiently hierarchically dominant that even children born from mothers possessing their own Progenitor [[Races (Flamel)|Races]]—including Tasha, Lilith and Roberta/Medusa—were born fundamentally as True [[Blood Dragons]], with the mother's Progenitor characteristics existing beneath the dominant Draconic foundation.[4]
Roberta therefore completed one of the stranger developmental paths among Victor's wives: a hidden Human Witch manipulating memories to survive became a Vampire maid, host of a resurrected Divine Progenitor, permanent member of Clan Elderblood, True Blood Dragon, Goddess, wife and eventually mother within the Elderblood dynasty.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
Roberta's relationship with [[Victor Elderblood]] began through danger and curiosity rather than immediate affection. Victor was the first person shown to recognize that Roberta's supposed history with Johnny did not make sense and one of the first beings powerful enough to directly challenge both her memory abilities and the unknown Spirit protecting her.[7]
Roberta had built her previous survival strategy around remaining hidden. Victor immediately destroyed that strategy by seeing through it—and then surprised her by offering something better.
Rather than demanding complete submission immediately, Victor created a contract preventing Roberta from harming people he designated as forbidden while otherwise allowing her an unusual degree of personal freedom. His real objective was to watch Roberta develop and eventually awaken the being inside her.[7][8]
Roberta accepted because Victor could offer something Johnny never actually could: genuine protection. The Alpha Werewolf had only been camouflage. Victor possessed enough strength and connections that Roberta could begin abandoning the life of constant concealment she had constructed around herself.
Their relationship gradually shifted from mutual usefulness into loyalty and then love. Victor's increasingly explicit tendency to regard his maids as irreplaceable people rather than employees affected Roberta deeply, while her own possessiveness toward him became increasingly obvious.[10][17]
Chapter 590 marks the decisive transition. Victor offered Roberta permanent membership in his family, and Roberta responded with rare vulnerability instead of her normal teasing confidence. Victor accepted her under the name Roberta Alucard, and Roberta described the resulting connection as stronger than anything she had possessed before.[2]
Her later relationship with Victor exists independently from [[Medusa]]'s. Both women love and marry Victor, but the supernatural mechanisms are different: Roberta enters his direct family and Bloodline, while Medusa separately establishes a [[Soul Marriage]] with him in Chapter 633.[3]
After millennia together Roberta becomes one of Victor's established wives and eventually bears their daughter through the shared Roberta/Medusa existence.[4]

#### Medusa Elderblood
[[Medusa]] Elderblood is Roberta's ancestor and the most important supernatural influence upon her existence.
For much of Roberta's life she knew only that another being existed inside her. The presence possessed its own emotions, memories and powers, particularly a deep hatred connected to the Gods. This knowledge was enough for Roberta to instinctively hide herself from the wider supernatural world.[10]
The Spirit's emotional state also influenced Roberta psychologically. [[Scathach]] theorized that Medusa's accumulated revenge and Roberta's own trauma combined to help create the sadistic protective personality that existed beside Roberta's gentler self.[5]
The relationship is therefore significantly more complicated than ordinary possession. Medusa and Roberta can communicate, disagree and make decisions together, while simultaneously influencing one another strongly enough that outsiders can have difficulty identifying which qualities originated from which woman.
Medusa finally revealed her identity in Chapter 633, identifying herself as the Queen and Progenitor of the [[Gorgons]] as well as a Divine Spirit.[3]
Roberta supported Medusa's decision to bind her Fate to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], but importantly requested that Medusa make the choice because Medusa herself wanted it rather than simply following Roberta's preference. This demonstrates that Roberta recognizes the Progenitor as a separate person whose autonomy matters despite their shared body.[3]
Their connection became even more intertwined after Roberta's transformation into a Blood Dragon. Roberta's Draconic body acquired Medusa's Petrification directly through its eyes, while Medusa continued existing within the shared form.[1]
Later narration consequently refers to them collectively as Roberta/Medusa, especially in discussions concerning their daughter and the family they share with Victor.[4]

#### Johnny Lykos
[[Johnny Lykos]] was never Roberta's genuine husband or lover despite believing otherwise.
Roberta selected Johnny because he was convenient. His Alpha Werewolf status created a powerful supernatural presence behind which she could conceal herself, while his mind proved sufficiently vulnerable to her Memory Manipulation.[7][10]
She implanted false memories into Johnny and created the impression that she had long occupied an established romantic position in his life. Johnny accepted these memories naturally enough that people around him initially treated Roberta as one of his women.
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] exposed the manipulation, the constructed memories collapsed. Johnny suffered severe mental consequences and required a substantial period to recover.[7][8]
Roberta later spoke about him with remarkably little emotional attachment, describing him primarily as the easiest available target and useful camouflage.[10]

#### Maid Squad
The [[Maid Squad]] became Roberta's first stable peer group after joining [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
Kaguya occupies the leadership position, while Bruna, Eve, Maria and Roberta possess dramatically different personalities and specialties. Roberta initially lacked Kaguya's domestic competence and could be disastrous at ordinary maid work, but she rapidly proved useful in the areas Victor actually valued most: combat, psychological warfare and unusual supernatural powers.[16]
The women gradually became much closer than coworkers. They fought together, lived together, protected one another and developed the same extreme possessiveness toward Victor and his family.
Victor eventually acknowledged this directly when he offered the maids permanent places within his Clan and told them that their relationship had already grown beyond ordinary service.[2]
Roberta entered Clan Alucard alongside the other Battle Maids and later became their fellow wife within Clan Elderblood.

#### Gorgons
Roberta possesses an intimate connection to the [[Gorgons]] because she is a descendant and host of their Progenitor, but she should not be confused with their Queen.
That title belongs to [[Medusa]] Elderblood. When Medusa revealed herself and summoned surviving members of her Race, the Gorgons recognized Medusa as their Progenitor and sovereign.[3]
Roberta nevertheless shares Medusa's body and consequently interacts with the Gorgon Race more directly than virtually anyone outside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family. Her later Blood Dragon evolution also causes specifically Gorgon-like powers—most notably Petrification—to become innate characteristics of Roberta's own body.[1]

#### Unnamed Daughter
Roberta/Medusa and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] eventually have a biological daughter together.[4]
Because Roberta and [[Medusa]] continue sharing one body and existence, later narration identifies the child specifically as Victor's daughter with Roberta/Medusa rather than assigning maternity exclusively to only one of them.[4]
The daughter was born fundamentally as a True Blood Dragon. Even Medusa's status as a Progenitor could not supersede Victor's Blood Dragon inheritance, although her Gorgon characteristics remain part of the daughter's hereditary foundation.[4]

#### Skills and Abilities
Roberta Elderblood is a former Human/Witch and Noble Vampire who later became a True Blood Dragon under [[Victor Elderblood]]. Her abilities are unusual even among Victor's wives because her existence is shared with [[Medusa]] Elderblood, the First Known Gorgon Progenitor, Heroic Spirit and Divine Spirit. Consequently, Roberta possesses her own highly developed Memory Manipulation alongside several abilities originating from Medusa, most prominently Petrification. After becoming a True Blood Dragon, these abilities were drastically strengthened and partially incorporated into Roberta's own Draconic physiology.[1][2][3]
- True Blood Dragon Physiology: Roberta was eventually transformed into one of Victor's True [[Blood Dragons]]. The conversion greatly strengthened her existing abilities rather than replacing them, combining the superior characteristics of a True Dragon with the supernatural traits she already possessed. Following the transformation, Roberta manifested Draconic horns and wings and her primary powers underwent significant enhancement and mutation.[3]
- Superhuman Physical Characteristics: As a True Blood Dragon, Roberta possesses the greatly enhanced physical characteristics inherent to Victor's Race, vastly surpassing her former Human and Vampire states.[3][4]
- Enhanced Senses: Roberta possesses the heightened sensory abilities inherent to Blood Dragons and [[True Dragons]].[4]
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Roberta possesses the supernatural regeneration characteristic of Victor's Blood Dragon Race, allowing her to recover from physical injuries far beyond the capabilities of ordinary beings.[4]
- [[Immortality]] / Longevity: Roberta possesses the supernatural longevity associated with both her former Vampiric nature and current Blood Dragon physiology.[4]
- Wing Manifestation: After completing her True Dragon transformation, Roberta developed Draconic wings matching the coloration associated with her body and hair.[3]
- Enhanced Metamorphosis / Shapeshifting: Becoming a True Dragon substantially improved the metamorphic abilities available to Roberta. Victor explicitly observed that the newly transformed Dragons possessed superior metamorphosis compared with their previous states.[3]
- Power Augmentation: Roberta's pre-existing supernatural abilities became substantially stronger following her conversion into a True Dragon. Her Memory Manipulation and Medusa-derived Petrification experienced particularly drastic improvements.[3]
- Limited Creation Manipulation: Like the other True Dragons created through Victor, Roberta possesses the natural ability to manipulate Creation itself on a smaller scale than Victor. This stems from True Dragons' unusually intimate relationship with Nature and Creation.[3]
- Nature Affinity: True Dragons are described as beings especially beloved by Nature, granting Roberta an exceptionally strong natural compatibility with Creation and natural forces.[3]
- Elemental Affinity: Roberta acquired additional elemental affinities as part of her True Dragon evolution.[3] (Note: Chapter 920 confirms that the newly transformed True Dragons gained new elemental affinities, but Roberta's individual additional Elements are not named in that chapter.)
- Draconic Rune Usage: As a Blood Dragon, Roberta possesses the inherent racial aptitude for Draconic Runes. Victor later explains that even the least talented member of his Race retains all fundamental Blood Dragon characteristics and can instinctively produce basic Draconic Runes.[4] (Note: Roberta has not currently demonstrated the same advanced Runic mastery as Victor, [[Scathach]] or Natalia; therefore, "basic Draconic Rune usage" is more accurate than unrestricted Runic mastery.)
- Memory Manipulation: Roberta's principal original supernatural ability is the power to manipulate the memories of other beings. Before joining Victor, she used this ability to establish an entire fabricated history within [[Johnny Lykos]]' mind, allowing her to live beside him without her true background being questioned.[1]
- False Memory Implantation / Memory Fabrication: Roberta can implant completely artificial memories into another person's mind. Johnny was made to sincerely believe that Roberta was his wife and that the two possessed an established romantic history despite those events never having occurred.[1]
- Memory Alteration: Rather than merely erasing individual pieces of information, Roberta can rewrite a target's understanding of past events and relationships, creating an internally coherent false history that the victim accepts as genuine.[1]
- Mental Manipulation: Roberta's manipulation of memory can fundamentally alter a person's perception of their relationship with her. Johnny's actions, assumptions and emotional responses were influenced by the artificial history she had placed within him.[1]
- Memory Concealment / Identity Concealment: Roberta used false memories to conceal the uncertainty surrounding her actual identity and origins, effectively hiding in plain sight by ensuring that people around her believed they already knew who she was.[1]
- Memory Dissipation / Removal: The false memories Roberta implanted can disappear once her interference is removed, causing the target's fabricated history and associated assumptions to collapse.[1]
- Catatonia Inducement (Indirect): The collapse of Roberta's implanted memories can have severe neurological and spiritual consequences. After the false memories affecting Johnny disappeared, he entered a catatonic/comatose condition. Roberta explained that this was a side effect of the artificial memories and that his Soul would require approximately two weeks to recover.[1] (Note: This is demonstrated as a consequence of her Memory Manipulation rather than a separately activated "coma attack".)
- Soul-Affecting Mental Interference: The effectiveness of Roberta's Memory Manipulation is directly connected to the target's Soul. Even before her Dragon transformation, Roberta explained that Victor's exceptionally powerful Soul and unyielding [[Will Solace|Will]] prevented her ability from functioning properly against him.[1] After Roberta became a True Dragon, the narration again states that a sufficiently strong Soul is required to resist her memory attack.[3] (Note: This supports Memory Manipulation capable of interacting with the Soul, but does not by itself establish unrestricted Soul Manipulation.)
- Gaze-Based Memory Manipulation: After becoming a True Blood Dragon, Roberta's Memory Manipulation underwent an enormous qualitative improvement. Merely looking at another being is sufficient for her to intrusively alter their memories.[3]
- Enhanced Memory Manipulation: Her transformed ability no longer requires the prolonged subtle setup demonstrated during her manipulation of Johnny. Chapter 920 describes her as being capable of drastically altering another being's memories through a glance.[3]
- Resistance Negation through Soul Superiority: Ordinary mental resistance is insufficiently described as a countermeasure to Roberta's evolved ability; resistance is explicitly associated with possessing a Soul strong enough to endure the attack.[3] (Note: This should not be treated as absolute resistance negation. Beings with sufficiently powerful Souls can resist.)
- Petrification Manipulation: Roberta can transform living beings into stone. Originally, Petrification belonged primarily to Medusa rather than Roberta herself, but Roberta was repeatedly capable of using Medusa's abilities because the Divine Spirit resided within her body.[2]
- Medusa Power Access: Long before Roberta became a True Dragon, the other members of Victor's household were already familiar with her using abilities belonging to Medusa, with Petrification specifically identified as one of the powers Roberta regularly displayed.[2]
- Petrifying Gaze: Roberta's True Dragon evolution caused her own eyes to develop the same supernatural property possessed by Medusa: the ability to petrify beings through sight alone.[3]
- Gaze-Based Petrification: Roberta no longer needs Medusa to separately manifest in order to access the effect; her transformed Draconic eyes themselves possess the petrifying property.[3]
- Selective Petrification / Petrification Control: The petrifying gaze is explicitly described as an ability Roberta can control, preventing it from functioning indiscriminately whenever she looks at someone.[3]
- High-Level Petrification: Chapter 920 describes Roberta's evolved eyes as capable of petrifying "any being" with a glance.[3] (Note: This describes the extraordinary potency of the ability, but should not be interpreted as a no-limits claim that absolutely every higher-order existence in the cosmology must be affected regardless of resistance.)

##### Medusa Connection
Roberta's power-set cannot be completely separated from [[Medusa]] Elderblood. Medusa is not simply one of Roberta's personalities: she is an independent Heroic Spirit, Divine Spirit and First Known Progenitor of the Gorgon Race residing within Roberta's body.[5][2] The two coexist willingly and are sufficiently compatible that, when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later considered whether Medusa would want a separate body, he concluded that neither woman would accept the separation because they functioned extremely well together.[6]
- Spirit Possession / Symbiotic Possession: Medusa resides inside Roberta as an independent Divine Spirit. Unlike hostile possession, their relationship is cooperative and both women retain their own identities.[2][6]
- Shared Body: Roberta and Medusa physically coexist within the same body, with either personality capable of influencing the body's supernatural characteristics.[5][6]
- Dual Consciousness: Roberta and Medusa simultaneously retain independent awareness rather than one consciousness completely replacing the other.[2]
- Internal Communication / Telepathy: Roberta and Medusa can communicate directly with one another internally without audible speech. Their conversation remains private while sharing the same physical body.[2]
- Power Sharing / Ability Access: Roberta can employ supernatural abilities belonging to Medusa. Petrification is the clearest confirmed example and was being used by Roberta before her True Dragon evolution permanently incorporated a similar power into her eyes.[2]
- Partial Gorgon Transformation: When Medusa's influence becomes dominant, Roberta's body partially changes. Her eyes become reptilian, her complexion becomes paler, dark crack-like markings form around her eyes and her hair becomes increasingly animated in response to hostile intent.[5]
- Dual Voice / Spirit Manifestation: During stronger manifestations of Medusa, Roberta speaks with two voices simultaneously, representing both Roberta and the Divine Spirit occupying her body.[5]
- Barrier Generation / Force-Field Generation (via Medusa): When Victor suddenly attacked Roberta during their first meeting, the Spirit residing inside her reacted and produced an invisible barrier directly in front of Roberta. The barrier stopped Victor's punch before it could reach her face.[1]
- Reflexive Defense: Medusa's defensive response can manifest rapidly when Roberta is directly threatened, allowing the Spirit inside her to defend their shared body even before Roberta consciously performs a conventional attack.[1]
Note: The original version of this page described the barrier as stopping Victor "at full force". Chapter 208 confirms that it stopped his punch, but does not establish that Victor attacked Roberta with his absolute maximum power. The feat should therefore not be worded as blocking full-power Victor.[1]
- Hair Manipulation: Roberta possesses supernatural control over her extremely long hair, which can move independently of normal gravity and respond directly to her intentions.[7]
- Prehensile Hair: Roberta's hair can move like numerous independent appendages rather than behaving as ordinary hair.[7]
- Binding / Restraint: She can wrap her hair around another person's body and restrain their movement. Shinji initially failed to realize what was happening until Roberta's hair had already surrounded and immobilized him.[7]
- Autonomous Hair Movement: Roberta's hair can float and move around her body without physical assistance, appearing to defy ordinary gravity.[7]
- Emotion-Responsive Hair: During Medusa's manifestation, Roberta's hair becomes increasingly animated in response to their murderous intent, visibly reacting to the mental state of the Roberta/Medusa pair.[5]

##### Gorgon & Progenitor Powers via Medusa
The following abilities originate specifically from [[Medusa]]. They are relevant to Roberta because Medusa permanently resides within her body and can manifest through it, but they should not be mislabeled as Roberta's personal Divinities.
- [[Magic]] Circle Creation (via Medusa): While manifesting through Roberta, Medusa created an enormous green Magic Circle merely by stamping her foot.[5]
- Gorgon Summoning / Kin Summoning (via Medusa): Through the green Magic Circle, Medusa caused ten female [[Gorgons]] with scaled bodies and serpentine hair to emerge into the surrounding area.[5]
- Progenitor Physiology (Medusa): Medusa is the First Known Progenitor of the Gorgon species. Her Progenitor status belongs to the Divine Spirit residing within Roberta rather than automatically making Roberta herself a Gorgon Progenitor.[5][2]
- Heroic Spirit Physiology (Medusa): Medusa continued existing after the death of her original body as a Heroic Spirit and subsequently inhabited her descendant Roberta.[5][2]
- Divine Spirit Physiology (Medusa): Medusa achieved the status of a Divine Spirit after death, allowing her continued spiritual existence and supernatural authority despite lacking an independent physical body.[5][2]
- Conceptual Empowerment — Beginning of a Race (Medusa): Medusa explicitly states that the Concept imposed by her Soul is <Beginning of a Race>. This Concept is responsible for her extraordinary status as a Divine Spirit and Gorgon Progenitor.[5]
Important: <Beginning of a Race> belongs to Medusa, not Roberta. The current wording "Divinity: Petrification, Memory & Beginning (?)" incorrectly merges Roberta's personal abilities with Medusa's conceptual nature. No chapter currently examined explicitly confirms that Roberta herself possesses a personal Divinity called <Beginning>.

##### Soul Marriage / Spiritual Connection
- [[Soul Marriage]] Connection (through [[Medusa]]): Medusa eventually pledged her Fate and the Fate of the Gorgon Race to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]], creating the equivalent of a Soul Marriage. Because Medusa permanently inhabits Roberta, the resulting spiritual relationship directly affects the shared Roberta/Medusa existence.[2]
- Emotional Perception: After the spiritual connection was formed, Medusa could directly perceive Victor's emotions through their link.[2]
Note: The Soul Marriage is specifically established between Victor and Medusa in Chapter 633. Roberta later independently became part of Victor's central family as well, but the mechanics demonstrated in this scene belong primarily to Medusa's spiritual connection.[2]

##### Skills
- Psychological Manipulation: Roberta is highly effective at using her Memory Manipulation in subtle social situations rather than only direct combat. She successfully created an entire false relationship with Johnny and maintained the deception until [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] deliberately investigated the inconsistencies surrounding her.[1]
- Deception / Infiltration: By combining false memories with an innocuous demeanor, Roberta was able to remain inside [[The Lost Club]] while most people around her accepted an invented history concerning her identity.[1]
- Combat Training: After becoming one of Victor's maids, Roberta received training under [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]]. [[Scathach]] initially considered Roberta one of the more difficult prospects because her considerable supernatural potential was accompanied by poor control and severe psychological complications.[8]
- Close-Range Restraint: Roberta can combine rapid positioning with her prehensile hair to immobilize opponents before they properly react. Shinji did not notice Roberta moving behind him until her hair had already restrained his body.[7]

#### Limitations
- Roberta's Memory Manipulation is not absolute. A sufficiently powerful Soul and [[Will Solace|Will]] can resist it; [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was already effectively beyond her ability during their first meeting, and Chapter 920 reiterates that Soul strength is the primary defense against her evolved memory attacks.[1][3]
- The catatonic condition suffered by Johnny is demonstrated as a side effect of having extensive artificial memories destabilized or removed rather than a separate freely activated coma ability.[1]
- Roberta's early Petrification originated from [[Medusa]]. Only after the True Dragon transformation does Chapter 920 explicitly establish Roberta's own eyes as independently possessing Medusa-like Petrification.[2][3]
- Roberta's early invisible barrier was generated by Medusa's Spirit when Victor threatened their shared body; independent conscious mastery of that exact barrier by Roberta has not been clearly demonstrated in the examined chapters.[1]
- <Beginning of a Race> is Medusa's Soul Concept. It should not be listed as one of Roberta's personal Divinities unless a later chapter explicitly establishes that Roberta independently acquired the same Concept.[5]
- Chapter 920 confirms that Roberta gained additional elemental affinities after becoming a True Dragon but does not identify those Elements individually.[3]
- [[Blood Dragons]] possess inherent access to basic Draconic Runes, but Roberta has not demonstrated the advanced Runic proficiency necessary to classify her alongside dedicated Rune Masters.[4]
