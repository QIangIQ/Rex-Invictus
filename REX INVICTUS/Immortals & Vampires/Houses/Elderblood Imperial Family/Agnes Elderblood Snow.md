(Lore adapted from *My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires*.)

### Appearances

#### Debut
Chapter 37
You yourself are an ancient hedonist, Agnes. You slaughtered innocents, drank the blood of countless helpless victims, burned entire villages; you did whatever you wanted because you're a Vampire who was born 'strong'.
—[[Persephone Elderblood]] confronting Agnes with her own past, Chapter 810
Agnes Elderblood Snow, formerly known as Agnes Snow and briefly as Agnes Alucard Snow, is the former Vampire Countess of the [[Snow Clan]], the mother of [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] and [[Agatha Agnessa Elderblood]], the widow of [[Adonis Snow]], and one of the wives of [[Victor Elderblood]]. Once an ancient Noble Vampire and one of the principal rulers of [[Nightingale]], Agnes is currently a Blood Dragon and genuine True Dragon belonging to Victor's direct lineage.
For most of her early life, Agnes's identity was inseparable from three things: the Snow Clan, its extraordinary Fire Bloodline, and Adonis. She possessed enough raw power to stand among Nightingale's Vampire Countesses, inherited the [[Blade of Fafnir]] as the greatest treasure of the Snow lineage and was capable of devastating enormous areas when she fully released her former Vampire Count power. Despite that potential, Agnes spent centuries allowing her own combat development to stagnate because virtually all of her emotional attention revolved around protecting and remaining beside her chronically weakened husband.[3]
Adonis's death became the central turning point of Agnes's character. Before dying, he allowed Victor to absorb his Soul, memories and accumulated experiences, leaving Victor with intimate knowledge of a man Agnes had loved for more than a millennium.[4] Agnes initially struggled to separate Victor from the traces of Adonis contained within him, but Victor consistently refused to become a replacement for the dead man. Their later marriage therefore developed only after Agnes accepted that Adonis was gone and consciously chose Victor as an entirely separate person.[5][6]
Agnes subsequently underwent two fundamentally different transformations under Victor. Chapter 634 reconstructed Agnes and Violet more deeply beneath Victor's Vampire Progenitor lineage, strengthening their Blood and changing Agnes's previously golden eyes to a much stronger blood-red; this was still a Vampire evolution rather than Dragonization.[7] Much later, Victor completely rebuilt Agnes as a True Dragon during the mass evolution of his family, preserving compatible characteristics of the Snow lineage and her Fire affinity within her new Draconic foundation.[8][9]
Agnes should therefore currently be classified as a Blood Dragon / True Dragon. Becoming a Blood Dragon did not by itself make her a Goddess, and no separate Divine ascension has been established for her simply because several of Victor's other wives combine Dragonhood with Godhood.

### Physical Appearance
Agnes is an exceptionally tall and curvaceous woman standing approximately 186 centimeters tall. She possesses extremely pale skin, long snow-white hair reaching around her waist and the striking appearance characteristic of the main Snow lineage. During her original Noble Vampire life she possessed unusual golden eyes and wore simple glasses despite having perfect supernatural eyesight; the glasses served no medical purpose and were worn purely because Agnes believed they improved her appearance.[10] Her early clothing usually consisted of tight black leather pants and a long dark upper garment rather than the heavily ceremonial clothing associated with some of [[Nightingale]]'s other rulers.
Agnes's appearance deteriorated temporarily after Adonis's death because she ceased caring about the aesthetic habits that had previously mattered to her. One symbolic moment occurred when she crushed the glasses she had once worn purely for vanity, reflecting how thoroughly grief had disrupted her sense of self.[11] This neglect was psychological rather than the result of lasting physical damage.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Progenitor-lineage ritual in Chapter 634 visibly changed Agnes again. Her Vampire complexion became healthier and less corpse-like while her golden eyes became a vivid blood-red associated with the superior Blood running through Victor's lineage.[7] This evolution strengthened Agnes as a Vampire but should not be confused with her later Draconic transformation.
Agnes eventually accepted full Blood Dragon conversion alongside other members of Victor's family. Her body was reconstructed around the physiology of a True Dragon while preserving traits compatible with her identity and [[Snow Bloodline]].[8][9] Like the other complete Dragons of Victor's immediate family, she can retain a humanoid appearance despite possessing a genuine Draconic existence.

### Personality
Agnes is proud, possessive, emotional, temperamental and intensely devoted to the people she considers hers. Many of Violet's most recognizable characteristics are inherited reflections of her mother: both women are extraordinarily jealous, deeply territorial toward their partners and liable to respond violently when they perceive someone as threatening their family. Agnes's personality is less controlled than Violet's in several respects, particularly when anger or romantic jealousy is involved.
For more than a millennium, almost the entirety of Agnes's emotional stability revolved around Adonis. Her love for him was genuine and she spent centuries caring for the damage left by his history with [[Aphrodite]] and [[Persephone]], but her devotion also became suffocating. Agnes disliked allowing Adonis away from her, reacted intensely to women showing interest in him and frequently prioritized her husband above the [[Snow Clan]] and even Violet. Chapter 227 makes the imbalance particularly clear: Violet understood that Agnes loved her but also knew that her mother had always devoted the overwhelming majority of herself to Adonis.[12]
Agnes historically relied too heavily upon her natural gifts. The [[Snow Bloodline]] gave her exceptional Firepower, and [[Scathach]] repeatedly criticized her for allowing that inherited advantage to substitute for disciplined technical improvement.[3] Agnes could therefore be terrifying by ordinary Noble Vampire standards while remaining less complete as a warrior than Scathach, who spent thousands of years continuously refining technique, experience and martial understanding. Adonis's death was partly responsible for changing this attitude: after Violet finally broke through her mother's paralysis, Agnes retrieved [[Fafnir]] and deliberately returned to serious training instead of remaining bedridden.[11]
She also possesses a pronounced hedonistic streak and a historically casual attitude toward violence against weaker beings. Natashia mocked Agnes as an ancient hedonist, and Persephone later confronted her with the same accusation far more seriously. Agnes had spent centuries condemning the Greek Goddesses for exploiting Adonis through superior power while having herself slaughtered weaker people, consumed helpless victims and burned settlements because few possessed the strength to stop her.[13][14] Persephone's criticism was effective precisely because Agnes could not honestly deny the hypocrisy.
Adonis's death exposed how dependent Agnes had become upon her husband. She temporarily lost the ability to properly govern the Snow Clan and struggled to distinguish the memories and traces of Adonis within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] from the living man standing in front of her. Victor refused to exploit this vulnerability and repeatedly forced Agnes to confront the distinction between them. By the time she chose to marry Victor, Agnes could openly admit that she still loved Adonis while also recognizing that her feelings for Victor had become independent of him.[5][6]
Her later confrontation with Persephone and the temporary reconstruction of Adonis's informational echo forced another stage of emotional maturity. Agnes finally heard Adonis discuss their marriage from his own perspective instead of reconstructing his wishes through grief or Victor's inherited memories. She was forced to acknowledge that loving someone did not automatically mean every possessive choice she made on their behalf had been healthy. The experience did not erase Agnes's jealousy, aggression or obsessive tendencies, but it gave her enough closure that Adonis ceased controlling the direction of her future.
By the Imperial era, Agnes is substantially more relaxed within Victor's family. She still competes aggressively for her husband's attention, still reacts impulsively and remains perfectly capable of joining the household's more ridiculous schemes, but her identity is no longer built entirely around one man. She is more involved with Violet, openly enjoys being a mother again and takes pride in Agatha's existence. Chapter 1070 shows this later Agnes particularly well: she seeks Victor's affection, complains that their daughters monopolize too much of his attention, proudly displays Fafnir's evolution and casually suggests that she and Victor should have another daughter.[15]
Motherhood and the enormous duration of the Elderblood family's accelerated Imperial life mellowed Agnes further without making her docile. In Chapter 1171 she participates naturally in the wives' discussion about [[Tyche]]'s future child, speculating about inherited traits and discussing the increasingly abnormal powers appearing among Victor's daughters. By this point Agnes functions less like the isolated Countess introduced in the early volumes and more like one of the senior mothers within an enormous multigenerational Dragon dynasty.[16]

### Synopsis

#### Vampire Society
Agnes first appeared after Violet returned to the [[Snow Clan]] estate. She was already the established Countess of Clan Snow, an ancient Noble Vampire and one of [[Nightingale]]'s principal rulers.[10] Her household revolved heavily around Adonis, whose fragile physical condition remained a constant source of concern. Agnes's overwhelming attachment to him was immediately evident, as was the similarity between her own possessiveness and the traits Violet had already begun displaying toward [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].
When Victor later interacted more directly with Nightingale's ruling families, Agnes observed the newly created Vampire who had become her daughter's husband. Victor's behavior rapidly made clear that he was not a conventional subordinate who would quietly accept the expectations of older Vampires. His lack of fear toward Countesses, willingness to challenge established authority and extreme protectiveness toward his wives distinguished him from the kind of son-in-law Agnes might otherwise have expected.
Agnes also displayed one of the principal weaknesses that defined her early character: she possessed enormous raw Firepower but had allowed her combat development to stagnate. [[Scathach]] considered Agnes excessively dependent upon the natural strength of the [[Snow Bloodline]], contrasting her approach with Scathach's own obsession with continual martial refinement.[3]

#### A New Pillar
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ascension as another Vampire Count changed [[Nightingale]]'s political balance, but Agnes remained far more concerned with Adonis than with the emergence of another Pillar. His health continued worsening as the Divine contamination and supernatural consequences accumulated throughout his life gradually overwhelmed his Vampire body.
Agnes's devotion to Adonis also continued affecting Violet. Chapter 227 highlights that although Agnes cared for her daughter, most of her time, concern and emotional investment remained centered upon her husband.[12] Violet had grown accustomed to receiving considerably less of Agnes's attention than Adonis did, one of several reasons mother and daughter lacked the intimacy they later developed within the Elderblood family.
Adonis, meanwhile, was making decisions Agnes did not completely understand. His unusual ability to perceive potential futures convinced him that catastrophic events were approaching, and his deteriorating condition made it increasingly obvious that he could not remain beside his family indefinitely. Those decisions eventually led him to entrust his remaining existence to Victor.

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] returned to the [[Snow Clan]] and learned the full truth surrounding Adonis. The former Human had once attracted the attention of [[Aphrodite]] and [[Persephone]], and the resulting Divine influences left his body permanently damaged even after Agnes arranged for Vlad to transform him into a Vampire.[4] Agnes had spent centuries caring for him, but Vampirism could not erase everything that had already been done to his existence.
Before death, Adonis deliberately allowed Victor to absorb his Soul. Victor inherited an enormous quantity of Adonis's memories, experiences and personal information, creating an emotionally difficult situation for Agnes. Her husband was dead, yet another man now remembered portions of their marriage from Adonis's perspective and carried supernatural traces that Agnes could recognize.
The grief broke Agnes. She temporarily stopped functioning as the effective ruler of the Snow Clan at precisely the moment hostile elements were exploiting instability inside its territory. Victor assumed practical control of the emergency, eliminated traitors and reorganized the Clan's immediate administration while Hilda handled responsibilities Agnes was not psychologically capable of managing.
Victor simultaneously refused to let Agnes collapse into the idea that he was Adonis reborn. He had inherited memories, but those memories did not make Adonis's identity his own. Victor would honor Adonis's final request to protect Agnes and Violet, but any relationship beyond that had to develop because Agnes wanted Victor himself.
Violet eventually forced Agnes to confront how completely she had surrendered to grief. Agnes responded by retrieving the [[Blade of Fafnir]], the ancestral treasure of the Snow Clan, and returning to training.[11] The decision represented her first meaningful attempt to rebuild an identity independent of Adonis. She could continue grieving without allowing grief to render everything else in her life meaningless.
By Chapter 494 Agnes accepted that the supernatural marriage connection inherited through Adonis could no longer define her relationship with Victor. The distinction became particularly obvious when [[Scathach]] temporarily disappeared with Victor and Agnes reacted with jealousy belonging to her own present emotions rather than the remnants of her previous marriage.

#### Demonic War
At the beginning of the Demonic War, Agnes finally addressed her feelings directly and announced that she wanted to marry [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. Violet and Leona immediately challenged her, forcing Agnes to distinguish attraction produced by Adonis's memories from genuine feelings toward Victor.[5] Agnes admitted that the situation had originally been complicated by Adonis and that she still loved her deceased husband, but she no longer wanted Victor merely because traces of Adonis existed within him.
Victor did not ask Agnes to erase her first marriage or pretend that Adonis had never mattered. He demanded only that Agnes choose him as himself. Agnes accepted the distinction, and the two deliberately performed a new Vampire Marriage Ritual, establishing a bond Agnes consciously entered rather than continuing one inherited from a dead husband.[6]
The marriage placed Agnes directly within Victor's rapidly expanding family. Victor also made clear that accepting Agnes meant accepting responsibility for her circumstances, relatives and problems rather than treating the relationship as purely romantic. Agnes soon joined [[Scathach]] and Natashia as the three ancient Countesses representing [[Nightingale]] at the [[Supernatural Gathering]], each carrying the greatest treasure of her respective Clan. Agnes brought [[Fafnir]].
Later in the war, Victor performed another major transformation upon Agnes and Violet. Rather than Dragonizing them, he reconstructed their Vampiric lineages more deeply beneath his own Progenitor Blood.[7] Agnes's complexion became healthier, her eyes turned blood-red and the quality of her Vampire existence rose substantially. This transformation became an important intermediate stage between the ancient Countess she had been and the True Dragon she would eventually become.
When Victor finally returned after personally experiencing centuries inside Hell, Agnes was among the wives with whom he reunited. Her marriage was no longer treated as an uncertain extension of Adonis's existence; she had become Victor's wife in her own right.

#### King Among Kings
Agnes's unresolved hatred toward [[Persephone]] remained one of the most serious emotional remnants of her marriage to Adonis. Both Agnes and Violet were considered dangerous enough in this respect that allowing them unrestricted access to the [[Greek Underworld]] risked provoking unnecessary conflict.
After [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s victory over [[Olympus]], Agnes finally confronted Persephone directly. [[Fafnir]] reacted to Agnes's anger while Persephone accepted that she had committed serious wrongs against Adonis. She nevertheless refused to allow Agnes to judge her from a position of moral purity, pointing out that Agnes herself had abused weaker beings for centuries simply because she possessed the strength to do so.[14] The argument did not absolve Persephone, but it forced Agnes to acknowledge that power had enabled cruelty on both sides.
Victor also demonstrated the enormous difference between hatred and actual capability. Even with her evolved Bloodline and Fafnir, Agnes was not strong enough to simply kill Persephone while the latter remained fully connected to the Greek Underworld and operated at a level associated with the highest Divine powers of the Pantheon.
The more important resolution came when Victor used preserved information to temporarily reconstruct an informational echo of Adonis. Agnes, Violet, Persephone and the others whose lives had been shaped by him finally received an opportunity to speak to Adonis without interpreting his wishes through someone else's memories. Agnes was forced to hear his own assessment of their marriage, including the fact that her possessiveness and overwhelming personality had sometimes damaged his pride and independence.
The echo eventually disappeared, but the conversation provided closure Agnes had never received at the moment of Adonis's death. She no longer needed Victor's memories to serve as a bridge toward her first husband.
A later vision of the Elderblood family's future revealed another major change awaiting Agnes: she and Victor would eventually have a daughter, [[Agatha Agnessa Elderblood]].[1]

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Agnes underwent the greatest physical transformation of her life when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] began converting members of his family into genuine [[True Dragons]]. Unlike her Chapter 634 evolution, this process did not merely alter her Vampire Bloodline. Victor's Essence and developing powers reconstructed Agnes from the foundation upward as a member of the Blood Dragon Race.[8][9]
Her former [[Snow Bloodline]] was not simply discarded. Victor's method of Dragonization was designed to preserve compatible characteristics belonging to the transformed individual and incorporate them into a superior Draconic foundation. Agnes therefore retained the identity, Fire affinity and inherited characteristics that had defined the Snow line while gaining the physiology and potential of a genuine True Dragon.
Agnes ceased being an ordinary Noble Vampire at this point. The distinction is important because her Draconic transformation did not automatically grant Godhood. Her current confirmed classification is a Blood Dragon / True Dragon, and claims that she is a Dragon Goddess require an independent Divine ascension rather than merely assuming every one of Victor's Dragon wives is automatically a God.
The newly transformed Dragons subsequently trained rather than treating their evolution as a substitute for skill. This was particularly relevant to Agnes, whose ancient weakness had always been an excessive dependence upon inherited power. The Dragon transformation gave her a much greater foundation, but exploiting that foundation still required training and control.

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
By the Imperial era, Agnes had experienced thousands of subjective years alongside [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s rapidly expanding family. The future daughter previously glimpsed through temporal information had become a real member of the second Elderblood generation: [[Agatha Agnessa Elderblood]], biological daughter of Victor and Agnes.[1]
Agnes's temperament remained immediately recognizable. She continued seeking Victor's attention, competing with the other women around him and expressing affection in an impulsive, possessive manner. Chapter 1070 particularly emphasizes this aspect of her mature personality when she appears while Victor is working with Velnorah, complains about the difficulty of obtaining private time with her husband and settles against him without ceremony.[15]
The same interaction revealed how dramatically [[Fafnir]] had changed. The ancient Snow treasure was no longer merely a powerful sword forged from the remains of the Fire Dragon King. Following Victor's modifications and the enormous amount of development that occurred around Agnes, Fafnir had evolved toward a genuine Fire Elemental existence capable of interacting with her far more intimately than an ordinary weapon.[15] Agnes proudly demonstrated this evolution to Victor before characteristically changing the subject and suggesting they should have another daughter.
The moment illustrates how radically Agnes's priorities had changed. Her early life had been defined by protecting what remained of Adonis and preserving the [[Snow Clan]]. Her Imperial-era life instead revolved around a future she actively wanted to expand: Victor, Violet, Agatha, Fafnir and the enormous Elderblood household surrounding them.

#### Those Who Rule Reality
Agnes remained an active senior member of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household as the [[Elderblood Empire]] passed through enormously accelerated eras. By the late Imperial period, the family had experienced subjective periods measured in hundreds of thousands of years, making Agnes's old status as a seventeen-century Vampire only a small fraction of the lifetime she had now personally experienced.
Motherhood noticeably affected Agnes alongside the other senior wives. During the family's discussions surrounding [[Tyche]] and the child she might eventually have with Victor, Agnes participated naturally with Violet, Ruby, Sasha, Anna, Natasha, [[Scathach]], Jeanne and Kaguya, speculating about what characteristics the future daughter might inherit.[16] She also participated in the discussion concerning the increasingly extraordinary nature of Victor's descendants, recognizing that the second generation contained daughters whose Divine, Draconic, Primordial and Eldritch inheritances produced results far beyond the standards of ordinary supernatural families.
The same era further clarified how seriously the family treated Victor's True Dragon lineage. Being related to one of Victor's wives did not automatically entitle someone to Dragonhood; even members of the extended family needed to demonstrate sufficient worth before receiving one of the lesser Draconic bloodlines, while complete True Dragonhood remained principally associated with Victor's immediate family. Agnes, Violet and the other wives supported this standard rather than demanding that every Snow relative receive Victor's lineage simply because of blood relation.[16]
Agnes therefore remains one of the clearest examples of the transformation experienced by [[Nightingale]]'s old ruling class. She began as an ancient Countess whose world revolved around one Clan and one husband, survived the destruction of that life, rebuilt herself through grief and confrontation and eventually became part of a family and Empire operating on a scale far beyond anything the original Snow Countess could have imagined.

### Skills & Abilities
Main article: Agnes Elderblood Snow/Abilities
Agnes's abilities originate primarily from the ancient [[Snow Bloodline]], her former Noble Vampire physiology, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s later Progenitor lineage and her current True Dragon body. Her greatest individual specialization remains Fire, while her combat effectiveness is strongly connected to the [[Blade of Fafnir]].
- Blood Dragon Physiology: Agnes is currently a genuine Blood Dragon and True Dragon created through Victor's direct lineage. Her current body possesses vastly greater physical potential, durability, Energy capacity and supernatural adaptability than her former Noble Vampire physiology.[8][9]
- Snow Bloodline — Fire Manipulation: Agnes inherited the primary Bloodline power of Clan Snow and historically ranked among [[Nightingale]]'s greatest Vampire fire users. Her memories demonstrated a former Vampire Count state capable of devastating an entire mountain range.[17]
- Vampire Progenitor Lineage (Integrated): Chapter 634 incorporated Agnes much more deeply into Victor's Vampire lineage before her later Dragonization, increasing the quality of her Blood and supernatural foundation.[7]
- Draconic Language: As a genuine True Dragon, Agnes possesses the racial compatibility with Draconic speech demonstrated by Victor's transformed family.
- Draconic Runes: Agnes possesses the natural Runic compatibility of a True Dragon, although the novel has not established her as one of the family's dedicated high-level Runemasters.
- Enhanced Regeneration and Longevity: Agnes already possessed the extreme longevity and regeneration of an ancient Noble Vampire before acquiring a superior Draconic body.
- True Dragon Form: Her current existence includes a genuine Draconic body rather than a superficial transformation.
- Combat Experience: Agnes possesses centuries of experience as a Vampire Countess and later resumed serious training after Adonis's death, though [[Scathach]] historically considered her technically underdeveloped relative to her raw potential.[3][11]
- Blade of Fafnir: Agnes wields the ancestral weapon of Clan Snow, a Divine-class sword created from the scales of [[Fafnir]] and bathed in the Fire Dragon King's heart blood. The weapon channels and amplifies Snow Fire and later evolved into a genuine Fire Elemental existence bonded much more deeply to Agnes.[11][15]

### Relationships

#### Adonis Snow
[[Adonis Snow]] was Agnes's first husband and the central emotional relationship of most of her early life. Agnes encountered him after the experiences that connected him to [[Aphrodite]] and [[Persephone]] and ultimately brought the dying Human before Vlad so that Vampirism could extend his life.[4] The transformation prevented ordinary mortality from taking him immediately but could not completely eliminate the incompatible Divine influences damaging his body.
Agnes spent centuries caring for him. Her devotion was genuine, but the relationship became profoundly unbalanced because Agnes made Adonis the center of nearly everything she did. Her protectiveness limited his independence, her jealousy prevented him from living freely and her fixation upon him reduced the attention she gave Violet and the [[Snow Clan]].
His death devastated Agnes because she did not merely lose a husband; she lost the person around whom she had structured her identity. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s absorption of Adonis's Soul complicated the grieving process by placing Adonis's memories inside another man, but Victor consistently refused to allow Agnes to confuse memory with identity.
The informational echo reconstructed after the fall of [[Olympus]] finally allowed Agnes to hear Adonis speak for himself again. Their last conversation provided enough closure for Agnes to preserve her love for her first husband without allowing his memory to determine every choice she made afterward.

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] entered Agnes's life as Violet's husband and therefore as her son-in-law. Agnes initially evaluated him through the standards expected of [[Nightingale]]'s old ruling families, but Victor rapidly demonstrated a degree of power, possessiveness and absolute devotion to family that Agnes understood extremely well.
Their relationship became emotionally complicated only after Adonis's death. Victor carried Adonis's memories and supernatural traces, making it difficult for Agnes to determine whether some of her reactions came from genuine attraction toward Victor or her inability to release her first husband. Victor refused to exploit the ambiguity and repeatedly insisted that Agnes recognize him as himself.
By Chapter 501 Agnes had reached that conclusion. She openly admitted both that she continued loving Adonis and that her earliest attraction toward Victor had been influenced by what he inherited from her husband, but she also recognized that her current feelings belonged to Victor.[5] Their Vampire Marriage Ritual formalized that independent relationship.[6]
Victor later strengthened Agnes through his Progenitor lineage and finally transformed her into a Blood Dragon.[7][8] They eventually had [[Agatha Agnessa Elderblood]], and by the Imperial era their relationship had become openly domestic. Agnes still competes aggressively for Victor's attention and remains one of the wives most likely to act on an impulsive romantic desire without lengthy preparation.[15]

#### Violet Elderblood Snow
[[Violet Elderblood Snow]] is Agnes and Adonis's biological daughter. Their early relationship was much less intimate than the devotion Agnes showed toward Adonis because Agnes spent the majority of her emotional energy on her husband.[12] Violet nevertheless inherited several defining aspects of Agnes's temperament, particularly possessiveness, jealousy, violent protectiveness and the tendency to treat romantic attachment as something absolute.
Their relationship improved substantially after Adonis's death. Grief forced the two women to interact more honestly, while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s growing family gradually placed Agnes and Violet within the same household rather than leaving them separated by the formal roles of Countess and heiress. They underwent Victor's Chapter 634 lineage evolution together, confronted [[Persephone]] together and later received closure from Adonis's reconstructed echo together.[7][14]
By the Elderblood era, Agnes is considerably more involved in Violet's life than she had been during the old Snow household. The two still possess personalities volatile enough to create chaos when they agree on something, but their relationship is no longer defined by Agnes neglecting her daughter in favor of Adonis.

#### Agatha Agnessa Elderblood
[[Agatha Agnessa Elderblood]] is Agnes and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s biological daughter. Her existence was initially revealed through information concerning the family's future in Chapter 865, before she later appeared as part of the enormous second Elderblood generation.[1]
Agatha represents a direct continuation of Agnes's lineage within Victor's Dragon family and is distinct from Violet, whose father was Adonis. Her existence also completes one of the largest changes in Agnes's life: the woman whose first marriage became defined by illness, grief and fear of loss eventually chose to build a new generation rather than remain permanently fixed upon what she had lost.

#### Scathach Elderblood Scarlett
[[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] is one of Agnes's oldest contemporaries among [[Nightingale]]'s ancient Vampire Countesses. Their relationship combines rivalry, familiarity and an enormous difference in philosophy toward personal improvement. [[Scathach]] spent thousands of years refining herself and repeatedly criticized Agnes for depending too heavily upon the natural destructive power of Clan Snow instead of developing equivalent technical mastery.[3]
Agnes nevertheless trusted Scathach during major crises and eventually became one of her fellow wives within the Elderblood family. Their ancient rivalry consequently survived inside a household where both women now share the same husband, daughters and larger family interests.

#### Annasthashia Elderblood Fulger
Natashia is another of Agnes's ancient Countess contemporaries. Both women possess loud, competitive, possessive personalities and routinely irritate one another. Natashia's willingness to call Agnes an ancient hedonist demonstrates that their relationship has always contained enough familiarity for direct insults to become normal conversation.[13]
Their later incorporation into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household turned the rivalry into another part of the Elderblood family's internal chaos rather than an actual political conflict between separate Count Clans.

#### Persephone Elderblood
Agnes hated [[Persephone Elderblood]] for centuries because of [[Persephone]]'s history with Adonis. To Agnes, Persephone represented one of the Goddesses who had used superior Divine status to interfere with a man incapable of truly resisting her.
Their confrontation after [[Olympus]] prevented Agnes from maintaining a completely simple moral interpretation of the past. Persephone accepted responsibility for what she had done but reminded Agnes that Agnes herself had also abused people who were weaker than her.[14] The accusation did not erase Persephone's actions, but it forced Agnes to acknowledge that possessing overwhelming power had historically allowed both women to become cruel.
The reconstructed Adonis later provided further closure by explaining his own perspective directly. Agnes and Persephone eventually became members of the same Elderblood household, an outcome that would have been almost unimaginable during Agnes's earlier life.

#### Skills and Abilities
Agnes Elderblood Snow is one of the oldest and most powerful members of the [[Snow Clan]], having formerly served as one of [[Nightingale]]'s [[Vampire Counts]] and the head of the southern territory. Her supernatural abilities were originally centered almost entirely around the Snow Clan's exceptionally destructive Fire, an elemental power particularly dangerous to other Vampires because Fire is one of their natural weaknesses. Despite historically neglecting conventional martial training in favor of overwhelming enemies with raw elemental power, Agnes accumulated more than a millennium of experience using the Snow bloodline and reached the level expected of a genuine Vampire Count.[1][2]
After marrying [[Victor Elderblood]] and entering his personal lineage, Agnes' Snow bloodline was dramatically strengthened by Victor's Progenitor Blood.[3][4][5] She later became a genuine Blood Dragon, retaining and further evolving the Fire abilities that had defined her as a Vampire. In the later Imperial era, Agnes also developed a much deeper synchronization with the [[Blade of Fafnir]], allowing the weapon and its Fire to fuse directly with her body and produce a specialized Fire Armor and elemental state.[6]
- Blood Dragon Physiology: Agnes eventually underwent Victor's Dragonization and became a genuine Blood Dragon. Her former Vampire physiology and Snow Clan Fire were incorporated into her new Draconic foundation rather than being discarded.[7][8][9]
- Immense Physical Strength: Agnes possesses the tremendous physical strength characteristic of [[True Dragons]], greatly surpassing her former Noble Vampire body.
- Superhuman Speed and Reflexes: Her Draconic body possesses vastly enhanced movement speed, reactions and combat responsiveness.
- Immense Durability: Agnes possesses the naturally resilient body of a True Dragon and can withstand levels of force far beyond those tolerated by ordinary Vampires.
- Enhanced Agility: Her balance, coordination and control over her body are greatly enhanced.
- Enhanced Senses: Agnes retains the heightened supernatural senses of a Vampire at a considerably more advanced level through her Blood Dragon existence.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Agnes possesses the powerful regeneration characteristic of [[Blood Dragons]].
- [[Immortality]] and Longevity: Her current existence is unaffected by conventional biological aging and possesses an extraordinary supernatural lifespan.
- True Dragon Form: Agnes is a full True Dragon and can manifest the horns, wings, scales and other Draconic characteristics associated with Victor's Blood Dragon lineage.[7][9]
- Flight: Agnes can fly through the use of her Draconic wings.
- Shapeshifting and Metamorphosis: True Dragons possess highly developed control over their physical forms, allowing Agnes to shift between humanoid and increasingly Draconic appearances.
- Size Alteration: Agnes can alter her body's dimensions in accordance with the metamorphic characteristics of True Dragons.
- Dragon Breath: As a True Dragon, Agnes possesses the racial capacity to release supernatural Energy through a Draconic Breath attack. Considering her dominant affinity, her Breath can naturally be combined with her Fire, although specific named applications have not yet been extensively demonstrated.
- [[Draconic Energy]]: Agnes possesses the vast supernatural reserves of a True Dragon, considerably increasing the amount of Fire she can generate and maintain.
- True Dragon Soul: Her transformation reconstructed her spiritual foundation as well as her physical body.
