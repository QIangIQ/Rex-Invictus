### Appearances

#### Debut
[[Ch08 - Rex Invictus I|Chapter 8]]: Grudges From the Past

Ruby Elderblood Scarlett, formerly Ruby Scarlett and Ruby Alucard Scarlett, is one of the principal female protagonists of [[MTWABV Series|My Three Wives Are Beautiful Vampires]]. She is the biological daughter of [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], heiress of the ancient Clan Scarlett, one of [[Victor Elderblood]]'s three original wives alongside [[Violet Elderblood Snow]] and [[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]], and the biological mother of [[Rosemary Lavina Elderblood]]. Originally a Noble Vampire who inherited Ice from her mother and Water from her father, Ruby eventually became a genuine Blood Dragon, True Dragon, Dragon Goddess and one of the senior Empresses of the [[Elderblood Empire]]. She is particularly distinguished by her extraordinary scientific intelligence and her position as Commander of [[Stellar Arsenal]], the Empire's Fourth Legion, which is responsible for research, development, advanced weapons and the controlled study of dangerous supernatural technologies.[6][7]
Ruby was born into one of [[Nightingale]]'s oldest and most powerful Vampire families, but her childhood differed considerably from that of an ordinary Noble Vampire heiress. [[Scathach]] was already recognized as the strongest female Vampire and had spent more than two thousand years refining her supernatural powers and [[Martial Arts]]. Ruby consequently grew up beneath the reputation of a mother whose accomplishments were almost impossible to reproduce. Although she inherited extraordinary potential, she became frustrated by the expectation that she would eventually follow Scathach's path toward martial supremacy. Ruby admired her mother and understood the importance of strength, but her own interests increasingly centered upon medicine, scientific knowledge and understanding the principles governing supernatural existence. Her desire to become someone whose accomplishments could be recognized independently of Scathach became one of the defining motivations of her early life.[8]
Her marriage to Victor originated from the abnormal Vampire ritual that transformed him. Violet had intended to bind Victor exclusively to herself, but his unusual Blood caused him to feed upon Violet, Ruby and Sasha during the same ceremony, creating an unprecedented marital connection among all four individuals. Ruby initially regarded the situation analytically because she recognized that the ritual had produced a supernatural dependency she could not safely ignore. Nevertheless, their subsequent interactions transformed the involuntary connection into a genuine relationship. Victor repeatedly treated Ruby as an individual rather than merely Scathach's daughter or one of his three original wives, while Ruby gradually developed an emotional attachment that became inseparable from her determination to protect him and the family they were building.[9]
Unlike several other members of Victor's household, Ruby has historically pursued security through knowledge, preparation and the ability to understand threats before confronting them. Her scientific interests began with medicine and Vampire biology but eventually expanded into the study of Bloodlines, supernatural [[Races (Flamel)|Races]], Souls, [[Magic]], advanced technology, artificial intelligence, extraterrestrial civilizations and cosmological engineering. She demonstrated this philosophy early when she established an intelligence network through Esther and [[The Lost Club]], then later insisted upon recovering the Church's research into artificial hybrids before allowing its laboratory to be destroyed. Rather than reject an enemy's knowledge simply because it had been obtained through reprehensible methods, Ruby sought to preserve whatever information could help prevent similar threats from endangering her family in the future.[10]
Ruby's personal development also extended beyond scientific research. Victor's Progenitor Blood gradually improved the compatibility of her inherited characteristics, eventually allowing her separate Vampire Bloodlines to function as parts of a more unified supernatural existence. She later undertook the brutal training of the Tower of Nightmares, recognizing that theoretical understanding alone could not compensate for insufficient combat experience. Following Victor's ascension as the Dragon God of [[Chaos]], Ruby accepted his offer to reconstruct her body and Soul through the Blood Dragon lineage, emerging as a genuine True Dragon whose former abilities had been preserved within a fundamentally superior Draconic foundation.[4][1]
Her greatest institutional achievement emerged during the Elderblood Empire's Great Acceleration, when approximately two hundred thousand years of internal development transformed Victor's civilization into a Galactic power possessing extraordinary scientific and supernatural capabilities. Together with [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]], [[Natalia Elderblood née Alioth]] and Alice, Ruby established Stellar Arsenal in Imperial Year 50 to unify the Empire's rapidly expanding scientific knowledge. The organization became the Fourth Imperial Legion and acquired responsibility for developing technologies and weapons intended to remain effective against enemies capable of threatening entire Solar Systems and greater structures. Ruby's leadership eventually placed her among the principal figures responsible for advancing the Empire's biological, technological and military understanding.[7]
By the later Imperial Era, Ruby's scientific expertise had expanded far beyond the disciplines she originally studied as a young Vampire. She participated in research concerning organisms whose anatomy incorporated Space, explained aspects of the process used to merge neighboring Sectors with Victor's domain, investigated fragments of Divine existence and worked alongside the Empire's greatest scientists on an experimental deterrent intended to threaten even universal-level enemies. Despite these responsibilities, she remained involved in the upbringing of Rosemary and the ordinary affairs of Victor's enormous household, refusing to allow scientific ambition to replace the family she originally wanted her knowledge to protect.[11][12]

### Physical Appearance
Ruby is an exceptionally beautiful woman with pale skin, long crimson-red hair, striking eyes and a mature, curvaceous physique. Her ordinary humanoid height is approximately 175 centimeters. During her original Noble Vampire existence, her eyes were naturally green, resembling those of her mother, [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett|Scathach Scarlett]], while the activation of her Vampiric nature could cause them to become red. Her appearance made her easily recognizable as a member of Clan Scarlett, although her generally composed expression and reserved demeanor distinguished her from the much more aggressive and openly confrontational Countess who had raised her.
During her youth, Ruby frequently wore loose or baggy clothing because she disliked attracting unnecessary attention to her body. This preference was especially noticeable while she attended university in the Human World, where her beauty and aloof personality attracted interest she generally had little desire to reciprocate. Ruby preferred to concentrate upon her studies, hobbies and personal affairs rather than entertain ordinary romantic approaches, and her tendency to appear uninterested in surrounding conversations reinforced the impression that she was difficult to approach.
Ruby's clothing preferences also reflected her personality. Unlike Violet, who frequently favored elegant and deliberately provocative outfits, Ruby ordinarily prioritized comfort and practicality unless an occasion demanded a particular appearance. She could dress formally when attending family or political events, but her natural inclination was toward clothing that allowed her to move freely and concentrate upon whatever activity currently interested her. Her scientific work later reinforced these habits, since elaborate clothing was rarely convenient when handling experimental equipment or investigating unfamiliar supernatural phenomena.
Her physical appearance changed gradually through repeated consumption of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood. His exceptional lineage improved the physical characteristics of the women connected to him, strengthening their supernatural foundations and refining their bodies over Time. Ruby's most noticeable transformation occurred when Victor deliberately incorporated her into his Vampire Progenitor Bloodline. The process strengthened and reorganized the characteristics inherited from her parents, causing her previously separate supernatural affinities to become more compatible. Her eyes subsequently acquired a permanent blood-red coloration, marking the deeper connection between her existence and Victor's Bloodline.[4]
The transformation into a genuine Blood Dragon produced another fundamental change. Rather than simply manifesting a few Draconic features upon her former Vampire body, Ruby underwent a reconstruction of her physical and spiritual existence within a Dragon egg. Her crimson hair, recognizable appearance and inherited elemental affinities remained, but her body acquired the superior biological characteristics of a True Dragon. As a result, her ordinary humanoid appearance became one possible expression of an existence capable of assuming far larger Draconic forms and supporting amounts of supernatural Energy impossible for her original Vampire physiology.[1]
Ruby's appearance within the Dream World reflected another aspect of her personality. Having accumulated enormous wealth and economic influence within the artificial civilization, she adopted the image of an exceptionally refined noblewoman, wearing an elegant red dress and gloves while carrying a fan decorated with the image of a spear piercing gold coins. The outfit emphasized her position as someone whose control over economic institutions had become more important than ordinary aristocratic titles, representing the way Ruby instinctively recreated influence through information and material resources even when separated from the extraordinary advantages of her normal existence.[5]
Despite experiencing more than two hundred thousand subjective years of accelerated development, Ruby retains her youthful appearance through her immortal Draconic and Divine physiology. Her mature Imperial presentation may be more imposing than the comparatively reserved medical student introduced at the beginning of the story, but the red hair, distinctive eyes and composed expression that characterize Ruby remain recognizable.

### Personality
Ruby is exceptionally intelligent, analytical, ambitious, introverted and emotionally restrained, possessing a personality that frequently contrasts with the explosive behavior of Violet and the more openly passionate temperament of Sasha. Among [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s three original wives, she is often the person most inclined to evaluate a situation before acting, particularly when an immediate emotional response would sacrifice useful information or create unnecessary complications. Her calm demeanor does not indicate a lack of strong feelings. Ruby possesses the same intense possessiveness, jealousy and attachment to Victor that characterize his other founding wives, but she usually expresses those emotions through planning, investigation and the desire to eliminate threats before they can reach the people she loves.
Her defining motivation is the pursuit of security through knowledge and control. Having grown up in a supernatural world where ordinary morality offered little protection against stronger individuals, Ruby concluded that survival required the ability to understand and influence the circumstances surrounding her life. The lesson was reinforced by [[Scathach]]'s upbringing, since her mother represented a level of personal power that made ordinary political restrictions largely irrelevant. Nevertheless, Ruby did not believe that direct combat was the only means of achieving security. Information, wealth, scientific discoveries, useful allies and the ability to anticipate an enemy's actions could be just as valuable as physical strength, particularly when dealing with threats that could not be defeated through conventional methods.
This mentality became explicit during Ruby's early negotiations with Esther. Rather than simply obtain information about enemies threatening Clan Alucard, Ruby envisioned restructuring the existing supernatural order so thoroughly that Victor and their family would no longer need to live beneath the constant threat of hostile organizations. She regarded the conflicts among Vampires, [[Hunters of Artemis|Hunters]], Witches and other factions as evidence that the established political structure was fundamentally unstable, and she considered exploiting that instability an acceptable means of creating something more secure. Her ambition to place the world under her control and give it to Victor was therefore not merely a declaration of romantic devotion. It represented a political philosophy in which overwhelming influence could be used to prevent outsiders from repeatedly threatening the people she considered important.[10]
Ruby's scientific curiosity is equally important. Where many supernatural beings respond to unknown powers with fear, suspicion or immediate hostility, Ruby instinctively wants to understand the principles behind them. An unfamiliar Bloodline, unusual magical phenomenon, artificial organism or dangerous weapon represents a potential source of knowledge rather than simply an obstacle to destroy. This tendency can make her appear disturbingly calm when confronting research that ordinary investigators would consider horrifying. She does not necessarily approve of the methods through which information was obtained, but she recognizes that destroying the evidence without first understanding it can allow the same danger to reappear elsewhere.
The investigation of the Church's artificial hybrid experiments demonstrated this difference particularly clearly. Violet considered destroying the laboratory an appropriate response to the atrocities committed there, while Ruby immediately recognized that its records, equipment and specimens represented years of research into supernatural biology. She insisted that the useful material be recovered before the facility was eliminated, reasoning that the knowledge could be employed to understand the Church's methods and develop better countermeasures. The incident established a principle that would remain central to her work: knowledge should not be discarded merely because it originated from an enemy, although the people responsible for obtaining it through cruelty could still be punished.[13]
Ruby's ambition also creates a complicated relationship with her mother. Scathach pursued strength through centuries of warfare, [[Martial Arts]] and direct confrontation, while Ruby initially preferred medicine, analysis and independent research. Both women nevertheless possess extraordinary stubbornness and an unwillingness to accept permanent inferiority. Ruby admires Scathach but has long resented being compared with her, particularly because many individuals regard the daughter of the strongest female Vampire as someone who should eventually reproduce her mother's achievements. Ruby wants recognition for what she personally accomplishes rather than merely for the potential inherited through the [[Scarlett Bloodline]].
Her relationship with Scathach is consequently marked by affection, frustration and a persistent desire to understand someone whose behavior often appears impossible to predict. Ruby recognizes her mother's extraordinary intelligence and combat experience but frequently becomes irritated when Scathach avoids answering questions directly or deliberately manipulates a conversation to escape an uncomfortable subject. Their discussion concerning Scathach's unusual interest in Victor is particularly revealing. Ruby attempts to understand why her mother is so fascinated by the newly transformed Vampire, only for Scathach to redirect the conversation toward maternal affection and teasing. Ruby eventually realizes that she has once again been diverted from her original question, expressing frustration that even after growing up beneath Scathach she still cannot reliably anticipate her mother's intentions.[8]
Ruby's composure is therefore not absolute. When sufficiently irritated, she can become just as emotional and destructive as other members of her family. Following one particularly frustrating exchange with Scathach, she vented her anger upon the surrounding environment, attacking trees, rocks and other objects while complaining about her mother's evasiveness, Corneliu's arrogance and Violet's tendency to create problems that other people were forced to resolve. The incident demonstrated that the apparently restrained young Vampire possessed a temperament far closer to Scathach's than she ordinarily wanted to admit.
Her attachment to Victor also contains an intensely obsessive element. Ruby occasionally imagines keeping him entirely to herself and becomes irritated when Violet or Sasha monopolize his attention. However, she recognizes the irrationality of allowing such feelings to determine every action and usually attempts to maintain enough composure to avoid creating unnecessary conflict. This self-awareness distinguishes her from Violet's early behavior, although it does not make Ruby less possessive. Her jealousy is simply more likely to manifest through calculation, sarcasm or quiet competition than through immediate violence.
Victor's acceptance of Ruby as an individual becomes one of the most important influences upon her personality. Unlike people who view her primarily as Scathach's daughter, Victor recognizes the importance of her intelligence, research interests, hobbies and desire for independence. His willingness to acknowledge Ruby's accomplishments without demanding that she reproduce her mother's martial achievements gives her a sense of personal validation that she had rarely experienced. Their relationship consequently becomes connected not only through the supernatural Blood ritual but through mutual respect for the different ways in which they pursue strength and security.
Ruby is also exceptionally ruthless when she concludes that an enemy presents an unacceptable threat. Her preference for analysis does not make her excessively merciful, and she can regard the complete destruction of an organization as appropriate once its useful knowledge has been recovered. This quality frequently makes her approach more frightening than Violet's immediate anger. Violet may attempt to incinerate an enemy in the moment, while Ruby is capable of studying its structure, identifying everything of value and dismantling it systematically after ensuring that nothing useful will be lost.
Nevertheless, Ruby's caution is not equivalent to cowardice. She recognizes that scientific understanding becomes insufficient when a powerful opponent can kill her before she has the opportunity to analyze the situation. This realization contributed to her decision to participate in the Tower of Nightmares alongside Violet and Sasha. The Tower repeatedly exposed them to lethal encounters, forcing them to confront enemies and environments that punished habitual responses and weaknesses in practical judgment. Ruby willingly endured the experience because she understood that knowing how an ability works is different from possessing the instincts necessary to respond correctly while that ability is being used against her.[2]
Her training gradually transformed her into a more complete combatant without replacing her scientific identity. Ruby continued valuing knowledge and experimentation, but she became increasingly conscious of the need to maintain physical proficiency and situational awareness appropriate to the extraordinary power she possessed. The distinction became even more important after her transformation into a True Dragon, since Draconic physiology provided a foundation capable of supporting direct combat at levels far beyond those of ordinary Vampires.
Ruby's scientific interests frequently cause her to become absorbed in projects to the point of losing track of ordinary Time. During the Imperial Era, she, Velnorah and Aline are repeatedly associated with the family's most demanding scientific work, while their laboratories investigate subjects that conventional researchers would consider impossible. Ruby can become intensely excited when encountering unfamiliar organisms, exotic technologies or supernatural systems whose principles have not yet been established, sometimes shifting her attention away from the social circumstances surrounding the discovery.
However, the responsibilities of motherhood and Imperial administration eventually teach Ruby the importance of balance. She recognizes that acquiring knowledge is supposed to protect and improve the family rather than replace her participation in it. Although she continues conducting exceptionally demanding research, she remains involved in the upbringing of her daughter Rosemary and regularly monitors her development. The contrast becomes particularly clear when the senior wives criticize Albedo for becoming so isolated within her laboratory that she has neglected ordinary familial relationships and the practical training necessary for a Dragon Goddess. Ruby understands the attraction of research well enough to recognize the danger, but she does not regard intellectual accomplishment as justification for ignoring the people dependent upon her.[14]
Her relationship with Rosemary also reveals a more patient and protective side. Ruby knows that her daughter's extraordinary Draconic intelligence gives her the capacity to understand advanced scientific material, but she does not expect Rosemary to share all her interests. Rosemary openly prefers enjoying her life rather than dedicating herself to complicated research, demonstrating that Ruby's daughter has developed a personality distinct from her mother's. Ruby's maturity allows her to recognize that possessing the potential to become an exceptional scientist does not make scientific work the only acceptable path for a member of the Elderblood family.[15]
Ruby also retains a strong attachment to Human popular culture. Anime, manga, games and other forms of entertainment remain among her favorite hobbies despite her development into an ancient Dragon Goddess. She regularly uses references that confuse Gods and supernatural beings who have little familiarity with Human media, and she can become irritated when other members of the family employ terminology she considers embarrassingly derivative. During the controversy surrounding Violet's attempts to recruit additional wives, Ruby objected to Kaguya's designation Yandere Sense because it reminded her of Spider-Sense, demonstrating that even after enormous periods of accelerated Time she continued recognizing references originating from the culture of her youth.[16]
Her greatest psychological development is ultimately the transformation of her ambition from personal control into institutional responsibility. The young Ruby wanted to place the world beneath her influence so that Victor and their family would never need to worry about outside dangers. The mature Ruby commands [[Stellar Arsenal]], one of the Empire's most important scientific and military institutions, and possesses the means to influence the development of entire civilizations. Yet the fundamental motivation remains recognizable. She pursues knowledge because she believes understanding the world is one of the most reliable means of protecting the people she loves, and she refuses to accept that an unfamiliar problem is necessarily beyond the reach of further investigation.

### History

#### Early Life
Ruby was born as the biological daughter of [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], one of [[Nightingale]]'s oldest Vampire Countesses and the woman widely recognized as the strongest female Vampire. Unlike Siena, Lacus and Pepper, whom [[Scathach]] adopted after recognizing their potential and circumstances, Ruby was deliberately conceived as her biological successor. Scathach wanted a daughter who could inherit her own extraordinary Ice while possessing additional characteristics that might eventually allow her to surpass the limitations of the existing [[Scarlett Bloodline]].
To accomplish this, Scathach selected a male Noble Vampire whose supernatural characteristics were compatible with her own. The man possessed Water Manipulation and the ability to breathe underwater, providing a complementary elemental foundation for a child inheriting Scathach's Ice. Scathach forced the man into a relationship and performed the Vampire marriage ritual, intending to maximize the potential of the child they would produce. Ruby consequently inherited both parental affinities, gaining Ice from Scathach and Water from her father, alongside the ability to breathe underwater and combine her elemental characteristics in ways unavailable to most ordinary Vampires.[6]
The circumstances surrounding her father's death were later explained in greater detail. Scathach killed him after discovering that she was pregnant, believing he had already fulfilled the purpose for which she had selected him. However, she did not immediately dissolve the supernatural marriage ritual because disrupting the connection while carrying Ruby might endanger the unborn child. After Ruby's birth, Scathach became aware that the man had contributed to the creation of someone she genuinely treasured and ultimately allowed the ritual's consequences to remain as a form of punishment for her own decision. The resulting Bloodline connection and associated bloodlust became another burden she incorporated into her own harsh approach to training.[17]
Ruby therefore grew up without knowing her biological father, surrounded instead by Scathach and the three adopted Scarlett sisters. Being raised by one of the strongest and most feared Vampires in existence provided extraordinary security against external enemies, but it also created a demanding emotional environment. Scathach's reputation meant that Ruby was constantly compared with a woman whose accomplishments had accumulated across millennia of warfare, and her mother's tendency to prioritize training and personal strength made it difficult for Ruby to communicate interests that did not revolve around combat.
Although Ruby respected Scathach, she gradually became frustrated by the assumption that the daughter of the strongest female Vampire should necessarily follow the same path. Her intelligence and curiosity were directed toward understanding supernatural phenomena rather than simply increasing her ability to defeat opponents through [[Martial Arts]]. She wanted to know why supernatural bodies possessed particular characteristics, how Bloodlines could combine and what distinguished the various [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] inhabiting their world. These interests eventually led her toward medicine and scientific research, disciplines that offered a means of establishing her own identity beyond the accomplishments of her mother.
Ruby subsequently spent considerable Time in the Human World, attending university while concealing her supernatural nature. Her beauty and reserved demeanor attracted attention from ordinary students, but she generally ignored romantic advances and preferred concentrating upon her studies and private interests. Her relationship with Violet and Sasha remained important during this period, since the three had known one another from childhood and shared an understanding of the political expectations surrounding Nightingale's ancient Noble families.
Violet's attachment to the Human [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] Walker eventually brought Ruby directly into the circumstances that would transform her life. When Victor suffered mortal injuries, Violet prepared an ancient marriage ritual intended to save him by turning him into a Vampire. Ruby and Sasha agreed to assist their childhood friend, but none anticipated that Victor's abnormal Blood would cause the transformation to connect all three women to him. Ruby consequently entered a supernatural marriage before developing an ordinary romantic relationship with her husband, beginning a period in which she would need to understand both the consequences of the ritual and the person to whom she had unexpectedly become bound.

### Synopsis

#### The Awakening
Ruby's first direct appearance occurred while [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was attempting to resume his ordinary university life following his transformation into a Vampire. She observed him from a distance during a confrontation involving [[Luan Davis]], but Victor's newly awakened supernatural instincts almost detected her despite his complete inexperience with Vampiric perception. The incident unsettled Ruby because she understood that an ordinary newborn Vampire should not be capable of recognizing a concealed Noble Vampire so easily. It also drew the attention of [[Corneliu Funar]], a Noble Vampire who had become obsessed with Ruby and regarded Victor as a threat to his own romantic ambitions.[18]
Ruby was already suffering from the effects of the abnormal marriage ritual. Because Victor had instinctively consumed her Blood during his transformation, her Vampiric bloodlust had begun orienting itself toward him, producing a craving that ordinary feeding could not adequately satisfy. When Kaguya investigated the condition of Violet's childhood friends, she found Ruby attempting to understand the situation while her maid Luna remained concerned about the consequences of prolonged separation. Ruby recognized that she had become connected to a man she had barely met and that the supernatural relationship could not safely be ignored merely because Violet would dislike discovering another wife.[19]
The situation became critical when Ruby and Luna encountered Inquisition hunters operating under Mizuki. Ruby attempted to defend herself while arguing that the hunters were attacking her simply because she was a Vampire rather than because she had committed any offense against Humans. The confrontation nevertheless escalated into direct combat, and both Ruby and Luna were injured while attempting to escape the surrounding hunters.
Victor was simultaneously engaged in a confrontation with Corneliu when he sensed Ruby's suffering through their abnormal bond. Rather than continue pursuing his personal fight, he immediately abandoned Corneliu and rushed toward his injured wife. Ruby recognized the flaming Vampire who arrived on the battlefield as Victor despite having barely spoken to him personally, while his immediate willingness to endanger himself for her established that the supernatural connection was already influencing their priorities.
Victor's extraordinary power was insufficient to compensate for his lack of combat experience. Mizuki exploited his unfamiliarity with blessed weapons and specialized anti-Vampire techniques, eventually inflicting a severe injury that left him vulnerable. Ruby recognized the danger and attempted to protect him, but the confrontation continued escalating until Violet, Sasha, Maria and Kaguya became involved. When Violet nearly lost control upon seeing Victor wounded, Ruby helped redirect the group toward retreat rather than allowing anger to prolong a battle they were poorly equipped to finish.
The original wives eventually coordinated their abilities to escape. Ruby combined her Ice with Violet's Fire, producing an enormous cloud of steam that obscured the battlefield, while Sasha used her extraordinary speed to carry the injured Victor away. The incident represented one of the earliest demonstrations of effective cooperation among the three women, whose jealousy and unfamiliar marital arrangement had previously made ordinary communication difficult.
[[Scathach]] subsequently learned that the Inquisition had harmed Ruby and responded with overwhelming retaliation against the Church. Her intervention demonstrated the political and military consequences of attacking a daughter of Clan Scarlett, but it also reinforced Ruby's complicated feelings concerning her mother. Scathach possessed enough power to make even a major supernatural organization fear her anger, yet Ruby herself wanted to become someone capable of protecting her own family without relying permanently upon her mother's reputation.
Several days after the battle, Victor finally awakened from his injuries and found Ruby sleeping beside him. Their direct exchange of Blood satisfied the unnatural thirst created by the marriage ritual and transmitted memories that allowed them to understand one another far more deeply than an ordinary first conversation would permit. Ruby discovered that Victor possessed an intense attachment to all three women, while Victor recognized that Ruby's apparent composure concealed a possessiveness comparable to those of Violet and Sasha. The exchange also completed his understanding of the abnormal transformation, revealing that his newborn instincts had caused him to feed upon every woman involved in the ceremony.[9]
Their meeting transformed the connection from a dangerous supernatural dependency into the beginning of a genuine marriage. Ruby remained cautious and analytical, but Victor's immediate concern for her survival and willingness to accept the circumstances of their relationship gave her reason to believe that the accidental bond might eventually become something she genuinely wanted.

#### Vampire Society
Following [[Scathach]]'s decision to take [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to [[Nightingale]] for training, Ruby initially remained in the Human World rather than immediately accompanying her husband. She recognized that Victor's departure had left several unresolved threats surrounding his parents and the people connected to him, and she began investigating the consequences of the Inquisition's activities. Her responsibilities included assessing the prisoners and supernatural dangers remaining near the Walker residence, ensuring that Anna and Leon were properly protected and examining the abnormal condition of Maria, whose defective transformation had already become a concern for the entire household.
Ruby's approach demonstrated the practical difference between her personality and Violet's. Rather than abandon an unfamiliar problem after identifying its immediate danger, Ruby wanted to understand the mechanisms involved and prevent similar circumstances from recurring. Maria's incomplete transformation was particularly interesting because it violated assumptions concerning ordinary Vampire reproduction and demonstrated that supernatural rituals could produce unstable results when performed without proper knowledge or control.
Before returning to Victor, Ruby confronted Scathach about her unusual interest in her husband. The ancient Countess had displayed far more enthusiasm for the newborn Vampire than Ruby considered normal, even arranging extraordinary training facilities to develop his potential. Ruby understood that Scathach was fascinated by exceptional warriors, but she was disturbed by the intensity of her mother's attention toward someone who had recently become her son-in-law. Their discussion eventually revealed another aspect of Ruby's frustration: despite growing up beneath Scathach, she still found her mother's intentions extraordinarily difficult to understand. Scathach repeatedly avoided direct answers and turned the conversation toward affectionate teasing, leaving Ruby uncertain whether she had learned anything useful.[8]
The encounter also revealed Ruby's possessiveness more openly. After leaving Scathach, she became increasingly frustrated by the possibility that her mother might develop an inappropriate interest in Victor, as well as by the continuous problems created through Corneliu's obsession and Violet's impulsiveness. Ruby vented her anger upon the surrounding environment until Victor unexpectedly appeared and witnessed the less restrained side of her personality. Their subsequent interaction helped establish that Victor did not consider Ruby valuable merely because she was Scathach's daughter or one of the women connected to him by the ritual. He recognized her individuality and treated her preferences, curiosity and emotions as important in their own right.
Their reunion became a significant turning point because Ruby had spent much of her life being compared with her mother. Victor admired Scathach's strength, but he did not expect Ruby to duplicate it. Instead, he acknowledged the different abilities and interests that made Ruby herself extraordinary, giving her an opportunity to develop an identity separate from the reputation of the strongest female Vampire.[20]
Ruby subsequently became involved in the training of Sasha and the development of the original household. Sasha had relied heavily upon the overwhelming acceleration produced by Fulger Lightning, allowing her to defeat many opponents before they could respond but leaving deficiencies in conventional [[Martial Arts]]. Ruby possessed a more structured martial foundation through her upbringing beneath Scathach, and she helped Sasha recognize the importance of timing, technique and control. The instruction demonstrated that Ruby's preference for intellectual analysis could contribute directly to combat training, even though she did not personally pursue Scathach's level of martial specialization.
The period also exposed Ruby to further aspects of Vampire society and the political circumstances surrounding Victor. As his relationship with the old Count Clans developed, Ruby became increasingly aware that information, family connections and supernatural research could provide advantages comparable to conventional military influence. Her developing marriage consequently began influencing not only her personal life but her understanding of the political structures governing Nightingale.

#### A New Pillar
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s recognition as the Fifth Vampire Count created the foundation for a new political faction connecting Clan Alucard to the great families of [[Nightingale]]. Ruby became increasingly involved in the informational side of its development, recognizing that ordinary aristocratic intelligence networks could not adequately account for the activities of Witches, Inquisition agents, hidden supernatural organizations and other groups operating outside the conventional Vampire hierarchy.
Her early negotiations with Esther and [[The Lost Club]] became one of the first major examples of her independent strategic ability. Esther occupied an influential position within an organization whose members dealt in information and supernatural services, giving Ruby access to channels unavailable to ordinary Noble Vampires. Rather than simply purchase a few isolated secrets, Ruby sought to establish a continuing relationship that could eventually become part of the security infrastructure surrounding Victor's faction.
During these discussions, Ruby revealed the extraordinary scale of her ambition. She considered the existing supernatural order fundamentally unsatisfactory because hostile organizations could attack innocent Vampires, political factions could exploit ancient grudges and powerful individuals could impose their preferences upon those unable to resist. Ruby wanted to transform those conditions so completely that Victor and their family would no longer need to live beneath constant external threats. Her willingness to exploit the instability among competing factions shocked Esther and Natalia, who recognized that the proposal could eventually affect the entire supernatural world rather than merely the political future of Clan Alucard.[10]
The negotiations also established a recurring pattern in Ruby's development. She did not consider information valuable only when it concerned the immediate situation; she sought relationships and institutions capable of producing useful knowledge continuously. Esther and The Lost Club consequently became more than occasional allies, gradually providing the foundation for research, intelligence gathering and covert activities that would support Victor's faction throughout subsequent conflicts.
Another important development occurred during the investigation of the Church's artificial hybrid experiments. Victor's confrontation with modified supernatural enemies revealed that the Inquisition and associated researchers had been conducting experiments intended to produce combinations of different [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] and abilities. Ruby recognized that the laboratory contained potentially valuable knowledge concerning Bloodline compatibility, supernatural transformation and the biological foundations of artificial hybrids. When Violet proposed destroying the facility, Ruby insisted that the equipment, records and specimens should be recovered first, preserving information that could be used to understand the threat and prevent future experiments from succeeding against Victor's family.[13]
The resulting research broadened Ruby's interests beyond conventional Vampire medicine. She began investigating the biological differences between natural and artificial supernatural beings, the circumstances under which Racial characteristics could coexist and the consequences of attempting to force incompatible systems into one body. The Church's experiments demonstrated that biological modification was possible but also revealed how unstable and destructive poorly understood hybridization could become. The information would later provide important context for Ruby's observations of Victor's Bloodline, whose ability to reorganize supernatural characteristics operated through mechanisms far more sophisticated than ordinary forced experimentation.
Ruby's relationship with Victor also continued developing independently of her scientific and political responsibilities. Victor deliberately arranged private time with her, allowing them to travel away from the constant conflicts surrounding Clan Alucard. Their journey eventually brought them to a remote northern environment, where Ruby and Victor could enjoy one another's company without the immediate demands of political negotiations, combat training or the other wives. The experience strengthened their relationship because it demonstrated that Victor wanted to spend time with Ruby herself rather than simply fulfill the obligations created by their marriage ritual.[21]
The date also emphasized Ruby's unusual mixture of personalities. She could behave as an exceptionally composed Noble Vampire, become intensely embarrassed by Victor's affection, criticize his impulsive curiosity and then demonstrate the same possessive enthusiasm that characterized her childhood friends. Victor found these contradictions appealing rather than attempting to force Ruby into a more conventional romantic role.
As the family's political situation became increasingly complicated, Ruby continued using her scientific interests and intelligence contacts to support the development of Clan Alucard. By this stage, she was no longer merely reacting to the circumstances created by Victor's unusual transformation. She had begun independently constructing the systems she believed necessary to protect the family in the future.

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
Ruby's growing intelligence network became increasingly integrated into the infrastructure surrounding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s faction. [[The Lost Club]] provided hidden facilities, access to information and relationships with Witches capable of operating outside the political structures of [[Nightingale]]. Ruby recognized that these resources could serve purposes extending beyond ordinary espionage, particularly as the faction began encountering supernatural phenomena whose origins were not understood by conventional Vampire society.
Her research also became more closely connected with the practical development of Clan Alucard. Victor's expanding responsibilities required improved territorial defenses, more secure information systems, methods of protecting his Human-world family and means of transporting or storing important resources without exposing them to enemy interference. Ruby participated in the discussions surrounding these problems, providing the scientific and analytical perspective that complemented Victor's more direct approach to supernatural threats.
One of their conversations revealed the extent to which Victor had begun overworking himself. He was simultaneously training, governing, conducting research, preparing military operations and attempting to maintain relationships with his expanding household. Ruby initially admired his ability to manage so many responsibilities but became concerned when she discovered that part of his solution involved neglecting ordinary sleep. Although Victor's supernatural body could endure conditions that would incapacitate an ordinary Human, Ruby warned that physical immortality did not automatically remove the mind's need for appropriate rest. Her response reflected both her medical education and her willingness to challenge Victor when his determination endangered his own well-being.[22]
The discussion also revealed the breadth of Victor's preparations. His plans involved establishing new territory, developing secure residences, improving dimensional storage and constructing systems capable of supporting a faction whose influence was expanding beyond Nightingale. Ruby's involvement demonstrated that her ambition to control the circumstances surrounding Victor was beginning to produce practical results rather than remaining a distant personal objective.
Her relationship with the original wives also became more cooperative. Violet, Sasha and Ruby remained possessive and competitive, but the increasing scale of their enemies forced them to recognize that unnecessary rivalry could undermine the security they all wanted. Ruby frequently provided the analytical perspective required to prevent emotional reactions from determining important decisions, while Violet contributed organizational authority and Sasha developed the direct combat proficiency necessary to confront immediate threats.
By the end of the period, Ruby had become one of the principal figures responsible for ensuring that Victor's household possessed the information and infrastructure necessary to survive increasingly complicated supernatural conflicts.

#### Demonic War
The Demonic invasion of [[Nightingale]] forced Ruby to confront the military consequences of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s expanding faction directly. Demonic portals opened across the territories controlled by the great Vampire Count Clans, threatening populations whose principal defenders were occupied with political meetings and military preparations elsewhere. Ruby joined Siena, Lacus and Pepper in the defense of Scarlett territory, demonstrating that her preference for research had never eliminated the combat abilities inherited through [[Scathach]]'s Bloodline.
As the biological daughter of the strongest female Vampire, Ruby possessed extraordinary Ice and Water abilities that could be employed directly against invading Demons. Her inherited characteristics allowed her to manipulate the battlefield through elemental attacks and contribute to the defense of the territory alongside her adopted sisters. The conflict also demonstrated the importance of cooperation within Clan Scarlett, whose members possessed different supernatural talents despite sharing Scathach as their mother.
The Demonic War provided another major turning point when Victor began deliberately improving the Bloodlines of individuals connected to him. Following earlier transformations involving other members of the family, he turned his attention toward Ruby, Jeanne and Morgana, whose supernatural foundations presented particularly interesting possibilities.
Ruby's original Bloodline combined Ice inherited from Scathach with Water inherited from her father. Although the two characteristics were compatible, they remained distinct components of her supernatural existence. Victor's Progenitor Blood reorganized that relationship, allowing the previously separated characteristics to become integrated into a more unified foundation. Rather than merely increasing the quantity of Energy available to Ruby, the transformation altered the way her inherited abilities interacted with her Soul and body.[4]
The changes were particularly important because Ruby had spent years studying the difficulties associated with combining different supernatural [[Races (Flamel)|Races]]. The Church's artificial hybrids frequently relied upon unstable methods of forcing incompatible characteristics together, while Victor's lineage appeared capable of reorganizing existing supernatural information so that the resulting being remained coherent. Ruby consequently gained direct personal experience of a process whose implications extended far beyond her own development.
The transformations involving Jeanne and Morgana further broadened the discussion. Their supernatural foundations differed considerably from Ruby's, and the resulting changes raised questions concerning the distinction between an entirely new Race and a subspecies derived from an existing Progenitor. Ruby recognized that Morgana's transformation did not automatically make her the Progenitor of a separate Race, since the resulting existence still belonged fundamentally to the Vampire lineage established through Victor. She consequently proposed that the more accurate classification was a Vampire subspecies, comparing the situation with established examples such as Ghouls and the [[Adrasteia Bloodline]].[23]
The discussion demonstrated Ruby's ability to approach supernatural evolution through systematic classification rather than relying exclusively upon the explanations offered by Gods or other ancient beings. She understood that the existence of unusual abilities did not automatically establish a separate Race and that the deeper structure of the Soul remained important when determining how supernatural characteristics should be categorized.
Victor's Bloodline development also strengthened Ruby's connection to the Progenitor himself, further improving her existing potential and creating a foundation for subsequent evolution. The changes reinforced her scientific conviction that the principles governing supernatural life could be studied, classified and eventually manipulated deliberately rather than accepted as immutable mysteries.

#### King Among Kings
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s return following centuries of subjective experience within Hell marked another transformation of his faction. The household had expanded to include numerous powerful women, Gods, Demons and supernatural allies whose interests could no longer be managed through informal personal arrangements alone. Ruby participated with Violet, Sasha and the other senior wives in the development of a more organized internal structure, recognizing that their personal attachments needed to coexist with the responsibilities of an increasingly powerful political civilization.
The alliance between Clans Scarlett, Snow, Fulger and Alucard became increasingly important during this period. Victor's marriages connected several of [[Nightingale]]'s greatest Bloodlines, while the resulting faction had developed sufficient military and supernatural influence to operate independently of the traditional political structures established by Vlad. Ruby's position as [[Scathach]]'s biological daughter and Victor's original wife consequently acquired importance beyond her personal relationship with her husband.
Nevertheless, Ruby's principal contribution continued concerning information and scientific understanding. She had become accustomed to examining unfamiliar supernatural phenomena and interpreting their implications, but she increasingly recognized that theoretical knowledge alone was inadequate when opponents possessed enough power to kill a researcher before any meaningful analysis could occur. Victor's extraordinary rate of development made this limitation particularly apparent, since the enemies surrounding him were reaching levels where ordinary Noble Vampire experience offered little practical preparation.
At approximately thirty years of age, Ruby, Violet and Sasha were already exceptionally powerful compared with ordinary Vampires of the same generation, but they remained inexperienced beside beings who had spent centuries or millennia fighting enemies of comparable strength. Victor consequently created the Tower of Nightmares, an artificial training environment designed to confront participants with scenarios that deliberately exposed their weaknesses. The Tower could repeatedly kill and restore participants, allowing them to accumulate practical experience through encounters that would otherwise have resulted in permanent death.
Ruby entered the Tower alongside Violet and Sasha, accepting the dangerous training because she understood that knowledge acquired through books or laboratory experimentation could not fully reproduce the instincts developed through actual combat. The Tower punished hesitation, excessive confidence and dependence upon familiar techniques, forcing Ruby to recognize dangerous situations and respond correctly under pressure. Her experience consequently transformed her into a more complete supernatural warrior without requiring her to abandon the scientific path she had chosen.[2]
The training also reinforced the shared development of the original three wives. Ruby, Violet and Sasha possessed different personalities and supernatural specializations, but all three recognized the necessity of improving independently so they could remain capable of supporting Victor as his power continued increasing.
Their relationship with Victor was further emphasized when he spent private time with the original wives upon a world their family had helped create. The moment demonstrated how dramatically their circumstances had changed since the abnormal Vampire marriage ritual. The young medical student who had once observed an unfamiliar Human from a university rooftop was now standing beside a Progenitor whose influence extended across entire supernatural civilizations.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Following [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ascension as the Dragon God of [[Chaos]], the limitations of ordinary Vampire existence became increasingly apparent. His family was preparing for conflicts involving Gods, Pantheons, Dragons and civilizations far beyond the standards of [[Nightingale]], making the continued development of their supernatural foundations essential.
Victor consequently offered selected members of his household the opportunity to become genuine Dragons through his Blood Dragon lineage. The transformation was voluntary, and those accepting it would undergo a reconstruction intended to preserve their previous identities and characteristics within a fundamentally superior Draconic existence.
Ruby accepted alongside Violet, Sasha and numerous other members of the family. Enormous Dragon eggs formed around their bodies while Victor's Essence and [[Draconic Energy]] began rebuilding their physical and spiritual foundations. Rather than merely strengthening their existing Vampire physiology, the process created genuine True Dragon bodies capable of supporting the supernatural characteristics associated with Victor's lineage.[1]
For Ruby, the transformation possessed extraordinary scientific significance. She had already studied artificial hybridization, Progenitor Bloodline evolution and the reorganization of supernatural [[Races (Flamel)|Races]], but becoming a True Dragon allowed her to experience another form of biological reconstruction directly. Her Ice, Water and accumulated supernatural characteristics were preserved within the new body, demonstrating that Victor's lineage could integrate abilities from different origins without erasing the identity of the recipient.
The transformation also required a change in how Ruby approached supernatural development. [[True Dragons]] possess a natural compatibility with Energy and Reality that differs fundamentally from the limitations governing ordinary Vampires, and Ruby recognized that understanding the new body's theoretical capabilities would not automatically provide complete mastery. She consequently continued training and investigating the principles underlying Draconic physiology while remaining one of the family's most important researchers.
Her responsibilities also expanded as Victor began consolidating the territories surrounding Dragons Nest. The civilization now included populations originating from different supernatural Races, Pantheons and worlds, requiring systems for transportation, communication, defense and administration. Ruby's scientific interests became increasingly valuable because the family needed to understand technologies and supernatural methods developed by civilizations whose histories were entirely different from those of Earth or Nightingale.
Despite these responsibilities, Ruby continued participating in ordinary family life. During Victor's enforced vacation, she joined Kaguya and Amara in a soccer game against Roxanne, Maria and Bruna, demonstrating that the family's rapidly increasing supernatural power had not eliminated their interest in ordinary recreation. She later accompanied Victor, Sasha, [[Hestia]], [[Nero]] and Ophis while traveling through their developing civilization, observing how the household that had begun with a handful of Noble Vampires was becoming the center of an independent supernatural society.[24]
The development of Dragons Nest established the foundation for Ruby's eventual transition from independent researcher to the commander of a major Imperial scientific institution.

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
The accelerated developmental period following the fall of [[Asgard]] transformed the circumstances surrounding [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family. Although a comparatively short interval passed outside the controlled temporal environments, Ruby and many of the people closest to Victor experienced approximately two thousand subjective years, allowing their supernatural abilities, relationships and accumulated knowledge to develop far beyond the limits of their earlier lives.
During this period, Ruby became pregnant with Victor's daughter. The pregnancy lasted approximately one thousand subjective years because the child inherited the extraordinary Draconic and Divine foundations of both parents. Ruby eventually gave birth to [[Rosemary Lavina Elderblood]], establishing her as one of the biological mothers of the increasingly large second generation of the Elderblood dynasty.[25][26]
Motherhood introduced responsibilities that could not be fulfilled merely through the accumulation of knowledge or the delegation of tasks. Although Ruby remained deeply interested in research, she increasingly recognized the importance of remaining personally involved in Rosemary's development. Her daughter's extraordinary Draconic nature required careful observation, particularly because the abilities and Divinities possessed by Victor's children could develop in unpredictable ways. Ruby therefore continued monitoring Rosemary rather than allowing laboratory work to replace her relationship with the child.
The Dream World provided another important demonstration of Ruby's ability to develop influence independently. Rather than recreate her usual identity as a scientist, she used the artificial world's economic and political systems to establish enormous financial power. Over the simulated years, Ruby accumulated enough wealth and control over important institutions that Violet jokingly described her as Mrs. "I Have the Whole Economy of the Country in My Pocket". The title reflected the scale of her influence rather than an official aristocratic rank, demonstrating that Ruby instinctively approached unfamiliar environments by gathering information and constructing systems capable of providing long-term control.[5]
The experience reinforced the importance of her intelligence beyond conventional laboratory work. Although supernatural power was restricted within the artificial environment, Ruby could still acquire influence through economic strategy, investment and the careful organization of resources. Her accomplishments demonstrated that the ambition expressed to Esther during her early Vampire life had not depended exclusively upon the physical advantages provided by her Bloodline.
The formal creation of the [[Elderblood Empire]] further expanded the importance of Ruby's scientific interests. The civilization now needed to integrate the knowledge of Vampires, [[Werewolves]], Witches, Demons, Gods, Dragons, Humans and numerous other supernatural populations while preparing for threats originating beyond its original Sector. Traditional distinctions between science, [[Magic]] and Divine Artifacts became increasingly inadequate because advanced research often required several systems to operate together.
Ruby's willingness to investigate knowledge regardless of its origin made her particularly suited to this environment. She participated in the developing Imperial research structure alongside figures such as Aline and Velnorah, whose expertise originated from different supernatural and technological traditions. The collaboration demonstrated that the Empire's most important advances would not necessarily emerge from one Race or discipline but from the ability to combine principles previously regarded as unrelated.
By the end of the period, Ruby had become one of the senior Empresses associated with the scientific advancement of Victor's civilization. Her early ambition to acquire enough information to protect a single household was developing into an institutional philosophy capable of influencing the survival and military development of an entire Empire.

#### Those Who Rule Reality
The discovery of civilizations operating beyond [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Sector revealed that the [[Elderblood Empire]] remained underdeveloped compared with some of the powers occupying the higher regions of the Cosmos. Victor consequently initiated the Great Acceleration, an extraordinary temporal project intended to provide approximately 200,000 years of internal Imperial development while comparatively little Time passed outside the affected region. The project required immense supernatural Energy and sustained cooperation among Victor, Natalia, Jeanne and numerous other powerful members of his family, while the Empresses used avatars to supervise important stages of development within the accelerated civilization.[27]
Ruby's greatest institutional achievement began during the early decades of this period. By Imperial Year 50, the Empire's scientific knowledge had already expanded enough that maintaining independent research departments without central coordination threatened to create inefficiency and unnecessary duplication. Ruby, Velnorah, Natalia and Alice therefore proposed unifying the Empire's most important scientific and technological work beneath a dedicated military organization. The resulting institution became the Fourth Legion, [[Stellar Arsenal]], whose purpose was to gather, preserve, develop and control the extraordinary knowledge acquired throughout the Empire.[7]
Stellar Arsenal differed from ordinary military Legions because its principal function was not direct battlefield deployment. It was responsible for creating the technology, weapons and supporting systems that would allow other Imperial institutions to survive conflicts against enemies possessing overwhelming supernatural power. Its research encompassed biological engineering, genetic experimentation, supernatural Bloodlines, [[Magic]], extraterrestrial technology, artificial intelligence, temporal manipulation, dangerous materials and weapons capable of producing destruction on the scale of entire Solar Systems or greater structures.
Ruby became the Legion's principal commander, while Velnorah and Aline were among the exceptionally capable researchers able to assume important responsibilities when she was occupied. Their collaboration established a multidisciplinary approach in which the origin of a particular technology mattered less than whether its principles could be understood and applied safely enough to benefit the Empire. The organization consequently became the institutional expression of Ruby's earlier research philosophy: even hostile or unfamiliar knowledge should be investigated rather than rejected merely because it originated outside conventional Imperial science.
The Legion's development also required Ruby to become more than an exceptional individual researcher. Although her Draconic intelligence and accumulated knowledge allowed her to investigate extraordinary problems directly, the Empire's scientific infrastructure could not depend exclusively upon one individual performing every experiment. Ruby and her collaborators therefore relied upon specialized researchers, advanced artificial personnel, automatons and subordinate laboratories capable of processing enormous quantities of information. This division of labor allowed the Legion's senior authorities to concentrate upon discoveries and projects whose complexity exceeded the abilities of ordinary researchers.
The distinction became increasingly important as the Empire's technological development accelerated. New discoveries emerged continuously, while the population, military infrastructure and available resources expanded beyond anything associated with the original Vampire Kingdom. Stellar Arsenal consequently became responsible for ensuring that the resulting knowledge was organized and that dangerous technologies could be studied without being distributed indiscriminately throughout the civilization.
The Empire's development eventually involved projects extending far beyond conventional weapons engineering. By Imperial Year 100,000, advanced strategic technologies had reached a level where specialized Divine Artifacts and other superweapons could threaten enormous Galactic structures. One of the classified weapons discussed by Victor's senior family members was Polaris, a Galaxy-[[Destroyer]]-grade Divine Artifact incorporating extraordinarily advanced systems associated with Destruction, Space, Time and Eldritch compatibility. Although the project's existence reflected the overall technological capabilities of the Empire, individual responsibility for its construction should not be attributed exclusively to Ruby without a direct statement establishing her role.[28]
Ruby's personal participation in family affairs continued throughout the accelerated era. She remained one of the senior mothers responsible for monitoring the condition and developing abilities of the younger Elderblood generation, particularly Rosemary. The extraordinary intelligence and natural power of Victor's children made ordinary assumptions about childhood inadequate, but Ruby understood that exceptional biological potential did not eliminate the need for education, personal attention and emotional development.
The importance of this distinction became apparent when several younger Imperial Princesses entered Pepper's private territory without permission. Rosemary participated alongside Silvia and Priscilla, eventually discovering that Pepper possessed an enormous collection of classified research material and advanced scientific projects hidden behind her public reputation as a Goddess associated with Culture and entertainment. Rosemary recognized the extraordinary complexity of her aunt's equipment but displayed little desire to imitate the research-intensive lifestyles of Ruby, Velnorah and Aline. The incident demonstrated that even children possessing exceptional inherited intelligence could develop interests substantially different from those of their parents.[29]
Ruby's involvement in the larger family also remained recognizably Scarlett. When Pepper summoned her under the pretense of an urgent matter, Ruby initially believed her sister required assistance with an important problem. After discovering the actual circumstances, she quickly accepted the invitation and joined the other Scarlett women, demonstrating that even after enormous periods of development she remained closely connected to the personal relationships and playful behavior of her original family.
Her scientific reputation became increasingly prominent during the later Imperial Era. Velnorah regarded Ruby and Aline as among the greatest contributors to the Empire's technological progress, while the introduction of Velina created another exceptionally capable researcher whose ideas could support their ongoing work. Their abilities were extraordinary even by the standards of [[True Dragons]], but the scale of Imperial research remained too great for every task to be performed personally. The resulting reliance upon specialized personnel became part of the Legion's administrative philosophy, allowing its most capable researchers to concentrate upon major breakthroughs rather than ordinary routine analysis.[30]
One of Ruby's most memorable research opportunities occurred during the enormous Elderblood family gathering organized after [[Scathach]] returned with the corpse of a colossal Star Animal larger than [[Jupiter]]. Although the creature was intended to be prepared as food, Ruby, Velnorah and Aline immediately recognized that its biology presented scientific questions far more interesting than ordinary culinary preparation. The organism possessed fur that appeared partially integrated with Space itself, while its digestive system contained such extraordinary concentrations of Energy that Ruby compared its stomach to a black hole.[31]
The researchers also examined evidence concerning even larger organisms capable of interacting with stellar structures. Ruby approached the material through calculation and observation rather than simply reacting to its astronomical size, while Velnorah began considering the possibility of capturing suitable specimens alive for future study. The incident demonstrated how dramatically Ruby's research interests had expanded since her original investigations into Vampire medicine and artificial hybrids. She was now capable of examining lifeforms whose physical structures incorporated Space and whose biological processes operated on scales ordinarily associated with stars or planetary systems.
The same family gathering revealed the importance of maintaining discipline within Victor's extraordinary Bloodline. Violet, Sasha, Ruby, Kaguya, Anna and Scathach supported the principle that individuals wishing to become part of Victor's Dragon lineage needed to earn that privilege rather than receive it automatically through distant family relationships. Even lesser branches of the Blood Dragon lineage possessed extraordinary potential, and Ruby understood that unrestricted distribution could create serious long-term problems for a civilization whose ruling family already possessed overwhelming supernatural advantages.
The discussion also exposed concerns surrounding Albedo's isolation. Although Albedo possessed extraordinary intelligence and the body of a True Dragon Goddess, she had spent so much Time within laboratories that her practical combat instincts and ordinary family relationships had developed inadequately. Ruby recognized the problem because she understood the attraction of research herself, but she also understood that knowledge did not excuse neglecting one's daughter or failing to develop the skills necessary to survive outside the laboratory. Scathach subsequently became involved in correcting Albedo's deficiencies through direct combat training, while the other senior women emphasized the importance of integrating research with ordinary familial responsibilities.
The birth of [[Moira Eutychia Elderblood]], Victor and [[Tyche]]'s daughter, subsequently produced another challenge to Ruby's understanding of supernatural biology. Shortly after Moira's birth, Victor created an artificial star and allowed the newborn Dragoness to consume it. Ruby immediately objected because even True Dragons ordinarily required sufficient maturity or an appropriate supernatural affinity before attempting to consume stars or black holes. From Ruby's perspective, the act presented an unnecessary danger to a child whose existing Divinity did not appear connected to stellar phenomena.[32]
Moira's response overturned those expectations. Rather than suffering the consequences Ruby anticipated, she adapted to the star and rapidly developed a Solar Divinity before discarding it with almost casual indifference. The event demonstrated that the youngest Elderblood daughter's unusual relationship with Luck and Destiny allowed phenomena that ordinary models of Draconic development could not adequately explain. For Ruby, whose scientific approach depended upon identifying the principles governing supernatural existence, the incident represented another example of a Being whose capabilities exceeded the assumptions established through conventional research.
Moira's birth also attracted the attention of the System and the existing Primordial order, producing a confrontation that threatened to escalate beyond the Empire's capacity for ordinary military retaliation. Victor ordered his family to prepare for the possibility of a universal-scale extinction event while he confronted the forces attempting to claim his daughter. Ruby joined the other senior wives in the resulting state of readiness, recognizing that the problem could not be resolved simply through the overwhelming military capabilities developed during the Great Acceleration.
The confrontation revealed a fundamental weakness in the Empire's strategic position. Although Victor possessed enough power to threaten catastrophic consequences against extraordinarily powerful enemies, the most extreme deterrent available to him depended upon abilities intimately connected with his own Soul and higher-order existence. Violet and Sasha regarded this dependence as unacceptable because it meant the family's strongest protection required Victor himself to become the weapon.
Ruby's role became increasingly important when the family began considering an independent alternative. The proposed deterrent would need to threaten Beings operating at the level of universal principles without requiring Victor to damage his own existence or initiate a catastrophe through his personal powers. Such a project extended far beyond ordinary superweapon construction and would require the cooperation of the Empire's greatest researchers alongside specialists possessing access to Eldritch and other exceptional supernatural systems.[33]
The project was initially theoretical, since the team needed to determine whether a weapon could produce the necessary effect without immediately contradicting or destroying the principles supporting its own operation. Nevertheless, Ruby's participation was consistent with the purpose of Stellar Arsenal. The Legion had been established to investigate and control dangerous technologies beyond the scope of ordinary Imperial science, making it one of the few institutions capable of attempting such a problem.
Ruby also participated in the Empire's next major cosmological project. After Victor reconstructed Mars and established it as the new Imperial capital, the ruling family began preparing to integrate the neighboring Arcane, [[Nightingale]] and Samar Sectors into Victor's Mid-Level Sector. The merger required extraordinarily advanced manipulation of the structures governing separate cosmological regions, and Ruby participated in the explanation of the mechanisms involved.
She clarified that the targeted Lower Sectors had already advanced significantly through their connections to Victor's domain, allowing the Empire to accelerate the remaining stages of their development and initiate their incorporation into the larger Sector. Rather than simply increasing an existing region's classification without changing its structure, the merger produced new Galactic formations as the incoming regions became integrated into the Mid-Level Sector. Ruby's explanation demonstrated the breadth of her scientific expertise, which now extended into controlled cosmological engineering alongside the work of Aline, Velnorah and Victor.[11]
The independent universal deterrent also began receiving more serious investigation. During a discussion involving Velnorah and her daughter Velina, the younger Dragoness proposed an unusual approach inspired by a thought experiment concerning the structure and limitations of Reality. Velnorah recognized that the fundamental principle might possess applications far beyond the original example and gathered Ruby, Aline and the other principal researchers to evaluate it.
Their combined analysis suggested that the idea possessed enough potential to justify continued development. The researchers subsequently informed Violet, who approved further investigation because the project represented one of the few possible methods of protecting the family from higher-order enemies without requiring Victor to remain the Empire's only effective deterrent.[34]
Ruby's involvement in the project became more explicit as the research continued. The proposed weapon required the team to investigate whether an absolute paradox could be created through the manipulation of the Universe's own Rules, an objective whose difficulty was far greater than the construction of ordinary weapons or even reality-altering Divine Artifacts.
The researchers discovered that using a Universe's own laws to create a contradiction capable of threatening its foundations was considerably more difficult than applying external supernatural powers against it. Velnorah recognized that influencing the Cosmos from outside its established framework could be simpler than forcing its internal Rules to destroy themselves, while Aline questioned whether the project might need to be abandoned.
Ruby refused to accept that conclusion. She acknowledged that the researchers had been struggling to solve the problem, but emphasized that only a comparatively small portion of the remaining accelerated Time had passed. Rather than surrender because the first approaches had failed, she insisted that they continue investigating alternative methods. Her determination restored the group's enthusiasm and demonstrated that the stubborn refusal to accept permanent limitations she had inherited from Scathach remained central to her scientific personality.[35]
Her involvement in Imperial research also continued through the investigation of dangerous Divine fragments discovered within the Empire. When a Dragon named [[Ryujin Ikaros]] found an unusual object that attached itself to his body, the matter was reported through the Imperial hierarchy until Akasha identified the anomaly and summoned Violet and Ruby. The Empire's communications network transmitted the information almost immediately, allowing the highest authorities to respond before the situation could develop into a wider crisis.
Ruby examined the object and confirmed that it was another fragment of Divine existence. More importantly, she determined that it had wrapped itself around Ryujin's Soul, making ordinary removal dangerous because attempting to detach the object incorrectly could destroy or permanently damage the spiritual foundation supporting the affected Dragon.[12]
Victor subsequently appeared and identified the fragment as belonging to a Light-related Divine existence connected with the forces threatening the Empire. He removed the object through his superior Soul Manipulation, separating it from Ryujin while preserving the Dragon's life. The intervention demonstrated the danger posed by fragments capable of gradually transforming their hosts into vessels for hostile supernatural powers.
Rather than destroy the fragment after removing it, Victor sealed the object and gave it to Ruby as a new research project. Ruby examined the sphere alongside Violet, but even their advanced senses did not immediately reveal its deeper significance beyond the presence of a fragment of Divine existence. Victor deliberately withheld part of what he understood, amused by the prospect of allowing Ruby to investigate the mystery herself.
Ruby recognized his behavior and became irritated by the teasing, but accepted the artifact and decided to bring it to the other researchers. The incident demonstrated that even after more than two hundred thousand years of scientific development, Ruby continued approaching unfamiliar phenomena through investigation rather than assuming that every important discovery had already been made.[12]
By the latest documented Imperial period, Ruby remains one of the principal scientific authorities of the Elderblood Empire. She commands Stellar Arsenal, collaborates with Velnorah, Aline and Velina on research involving the fundamental structures of Reality, participates in projects concerning the merger of Sectors and investigates dangerous supernatural artifacts that could threaten Imperial security.
Her position represents the culmination of an extraordinary personal development. The young Vampire who originally studied medicine and sought recognition beyond Scathach's reputation has become one of the principal architects of an interstellar civilization whose scientific capabilities extend into the manipulation of worlds, galaxies and cosmological structures. Nevertheless, her fundamental motivation remains the protection of Victor and the family they established together.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is Ruby's husband, the father of their daughter Rosemary and the individual most closely associated with her personal and scientific development. Their marriage began accidentally when Ruby participated in Violet's ritual to transform Victor into a Vampire, but the abnormal nature of his Blood caused the ceremony to bind all three original wives to him. Ruby initially regarded the situation as a supernatural problem requiring careful attention because the connection produced bloodlust that could not be satisfied through ordinary feeding.
Their relationship changed when Victor sensed Ruby's suffering during the Inquisition attack and immediately abandoned his confrontation with Corneliu to reach her. He had barely interacted with Ruby personally, yet he was willing to endanger himself rather than allow one of his wives to die. Their subsequent exchange of Blood allowed them to experience memories and emotions that would ordinarily have required a much longer period of acquaintance, establishing the foundation for a relationship based upon more than the supernatural consequences of the ritual.
Victor's acceptance of Ruby's individuality became particularly important because she had spent much of her childhood being compared with [[Scathach]]. Unlike people who regarded her principally as the daughter of the strongest female Vampire, Victor valued Ruby's intelligence, curiosity and scientific interests without expecting her to reproduce her mother's path toward martial supremacy. The recognition allowed Ruby to become increasingly comfortable pursuing her own ambitions while remaining deeply attached to him.
Their relationship eventually developed through shared experiences, private dates, Bloodline transformations and the creation of an enormous supernatural family. Victor frequently trusted Ruby with important scientific discoveries, dangerous artifacts and the development of systems intended to protect their civilization. His willingness to delegate command of [[Stellar Arsenal]] demonstrated that he regarded her as someone capable of making important decisions independently rather than merely carrying out his instructions.
Ruby remains extremely possessive of Victor despite her comparatively restrained demeanor. However, her affection is expressed primarily through the desire to protect him, understand the dangers surrounding his existence and create institutions capable of allowing him to live without constantly confronting every enemy personally.
Their marriage eventually produced Rosemary Lavina Elderblood, further strengthening the relationship through their shared responsibilities as parents.

#### Scathach Elderblood Scarlett
[[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] is Ruby's biological mother, the original source of her Ice Bloodline and one of the most important influences upon her development. Ruby was deliberately conceived through a relationship [[Scathach]] established with a Water-affinity Noble Vampire, allowing her to inherit supernatural characteristics from both parents.
Growing up beneath Scathach's reputation was difficult because Ruby was constantly reminded that her mother had already reached the pinnacle of conventional Vampire combat. Although Ruby admired Scathach and recognized the importance of the training she provided, she disliked having her identity reduced to that of the Countess's daughter. Her preference for medicine and scientific investigation represented both a genuine personal interest and a means of becoming exceptional through accomplishments distinct from her mother's.
Their relationship is characterized by affection, frustration and mutual stubbornness. Ruby frequently becomes irritated because Scathach avoids answering questions directly or deliberately changes the subject through teasing, while Scathach finds her daughter's attempts to analyze everything amusing. Their conversation concerning Scathach's unusual interest in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] demonstrates these differences, since Ruby repeatedly attempts to understand her mother's motivations without obtaining a direct explanation.
Despite their contrasting approaches, Ruby and Scathach possess several fundamental similarities. Both are extraordinarily ambitious, deeply protective of their family and unwilling to accept permanent inferiority. Scathach expresses these qualities primarily through [[Martial Arts]] and direct combat, while Ruby pursues knowledge, technology and institutional influence.
Their relationship changes further after both become [[Blood Dragons]] and members of Victor's expanding family. Scathach remains one of its greatest martial authorities, while Ruby becomes one of the Empire's principal scientific leaders. Their different specializations allow them to contribute to the same civilization without requiring either woman to abandon her chosen path.

#### Violet Elderblood Snow
[[Violet Elderblood Snow]] is Ruby's childhood friend, fellow original wife and one of the individuals who has remained closest to her throughout the story. The abnormal marriage ritual initially created tension because Violet had intended to bind [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] exclusively to herself, but the three founding wives gradually learned to coexist despite their extraordinary possessiveness.
Ruby's analytical personality frequently contrasts with Violet's more impulsive temperament. When Violet responds to a threat through immediate anger, Ruby is more likely to examine the situation and identify whether useful information or resources would be lost through a direct attack. The investigation of the Church's artificial hybrid laboratory demonstrates this difference, since Ruby insisted upon recovering the facility's research before Violet could destroy it.
Their disagreements nevertheless rarely threaten the family permanently. As Victor's household expands, Violet becomes increasingly associated with administration and the management of personal relationships, while Ruby develops scientific and technological institutions that support the Empire. Their specializations complement one another, allowing the two to cooperate on problems that neither could resolve as efficiently alone.
By the late Imperial Era, Ruby and Violet can move from ordinary teasing or arguments concerning Victor to decisions affecting the security and development of entire Galactic territories. Their shared history remains one of the principal foundations of the Elderblood dynasty.

#### Sasha Elderblood Fulger
[[Sasha Elderblood Fulger]] is Ruby's childhood friend and the third member of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s original marriage ritual. Ruby knew Sasha before the traumatic events surrounding Julia's murder and the deterioration of Clan Fulger's political position, giving her an understanding of Sasha's insecurities that later members of Victor's family did not initially possess.
Their relationship is generally less confrontational than Ruby's relationship with Violet, although both women remain competitive and possessive concerning Victor. Ruby frequently provides a more analytical perspective when Sasha becomes emotionally overwhelmed, while Sasha's directness can prevent Ruby from becoming excessively absorbed in theoretical questions.
Ruby also participated in Sasha's early martial development, helping her recognize the limitations created by relying almost exclusively upon Fulger Lightning. The experience demonstrated that Ruby's knowledge of combat could contribute to the improvement of another warrior even though she preferred research to [[Scathach]]'s demanding martial lifestyle.
The three original wives subsequently participated in the Tower of Nightmares together, underwent Dragonization within the same general era and became senior Empresses of the Elderblood civilization. Their daughters also developed close relationships, further connecting the families that had originally become united through Victor's abnormal transformation.

#### Rosemary Lavina Elderblood
[[Rosemary Lavina Elderblood]] is Ruby and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s biological daughter, born following an exceptionally prolonged pregnancy during the family's accelerated Draconic development.
Rosemary inherited the extraordinary potential associated with Victor's Blood Dragon lineage and grew up surrounded by powerful Gods, Dragons and supernatural researchers. Her natural intelligence allows her to understand complicated information rapidly, but she does not share Ruby's desire to devote enormous amounts of Time to scientific investigation.
Rather than become another researcher simply because she possesses the necessary intellectual potential, Rosemary prefers enjoying the opportunities available through her extraordinary upbringing. She remains capable of recognizing advanced technology and understanding its principles when necessary, but generally considers it unnecessary to perform work that other members of the family genuinely enjoy.
Ruby's relationship with Rosemary demonstrates an important distinction between her scientific ambitions and her responsibilities as a mother. Although she continues leading one of the Empire's largest research institutions, she remains involved in her daughter's development and regularly monitors her condition and emerging Divinities.
The relationship also reflects Ruby's personal history with [[Scathach]]. Having grown up beneath the expectations surrounding an extraordinarily powerful mother, Ruby possesses reasons to understand why a daughter might wish to pursue interests different from those of her parent.

#### Siena, Lacus and Pepper Scarlett
[[Siena Scarlett Elderblood]], [[Lacus Scarlett Elderblood]] and [[Pepper Scarlett Elderblood]] are Ruby's adopted sisters through [[Scathach]]. Although Ruby is Scathach's original biological daughter, she regards the three women as genuine members of her family rather than treating their adopted status as a meaningful distinction in their relationships.
The four grew up within Clan Scarlett and developed different supernatural characteristics and personal interests. They later defended Scarlett territory together during the Demonic War, demonstrating the importance of their shared upbringing despite their contrasting personalities.
Pepper is particularly compatible with Ruby's interest in Human popular culture, technology and unusual discoveries. However, Pepper's later scientific projects also demonstrate that Ruby is not the only Scarlett daughter possessing extraordinary intellectual capabilities. During the Imperial Era, Pepper maintained extensive private research facilities while publicly concentrating upon Culture and entertainment, surprising her nieces when they discovered the true scale of her work.
Ruby's relationship with her sisters remains playful and affectionate despite their extraordinary development. They continue participating in ordinary family activities and maintain a strong connection with Scathach even after becoming mature members of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Draconic dynasty.

#### Siren Elderblood
[[Siren Elderblood]] is Ruby's younger biological maternal half-sister, born from the relationship between [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and [[Scathach]].
Her birth means Ruby is no longer Scathach's only biological daughter, although the circumstances are entirely different from those surrounding Ruby's original upbringing. By the time Siren becomes part of the family, Ruby has already accumulated extraordinary subjective experience and established herself as one of the Empire's senior Empresses and scientific authorities.
The relationship consequently differs from Ruby's upbringing alongside Siena, Lacus and Pepper. Siren belongs to a younger generation of [[Blood Dragons]] whose natural potential and family circumstances are far beyond those Ruby experienced as a Noble Vampire.

#### Esther
Esther is one of Ruby's earliest important independent allies and subordinates. Ruby recognized that Esther's position within [[The Lost Club]] provided access to information and supernatural resources that conventional Vampire aristocrats could not obtain easily.
Rather than regard Esther merely as someone capable of supplying a few secrets, Ruby attempted to establish a relationship that could provide continuing access to intelligence concerning the wider supernatural world. Their negotiations became particularly important when Ruby revealed her ambition to restructure the existing political order so that her family would no longer remain vulnerable to external threats.
Esther's reaction demonstrated the enormous scale of Ruby's intentions, since the proposed changes could destabilize entire supernatural factions rather than simply improve Clan Alucard's position.
Their relationship represents one of the earliest examples of Ruby converting an individual contact into an institutional advantage capable of supporting a much larger organization.

#### Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II
[[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]] is one of Ruby's principal scientific collaborators during the Imperial Era.
Velnorah possesses knowledge originating from an extraterrestrial civilization whose technological development followed a path different from the supernatural systems traditionally studied on Earth and [[Nightingale]]. Ruby contributes extensive experience with Vampire Bloodlines, [[Magic]], biological modification and the increasingly advanced research conducted through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family.
Their different specialties complement one another, allowing them to investigate questions whose complexity would be difficult for an individual researcher to resolve independently. Their collaboration became particularly important during the creation of [[Stellar Arsenal]], where both women helped establish a multidisciplinary institution intended to unify the scientific knowledge of the Empire.
They continued working together during the investigation of the colossal Star Animal and the development of an independent universal deterrent, eventually extending their research into cosmological structures and the fundamental Rules of Reality.

#### Aline Elderblood Valefar
[[Aline Elderblood Valefar]] is another of Ruby's most important scientific collaborators within the [[Elderblood Empire]].
Aline possesses exceptional knowledge of [[Magic]], Souls, supernatural systems and advanced research, allowing her to contribute expertise that complements Ruby's biological and technological interests. Together with Velnorah, she forms part of the principal scientific group responsible for investigating many of the Empire's most complicated projects.
Their collaboration becomes especially important during the Great Acceleration, when ordinary distinctions between science, Magic and Divine technology are increasingly inadequate for understanding the phenomena encountered by the Empire.
Ruby, Aline and Velnorah later work alongside Velina on the proposed universal deterrent, attempting to determine whether an absolute paradox could be created through the internal Rules of the Cosmos. Their discussions demonstrate both the extraordinary sophistication of Imperial research and the limitations that even ancient Dragon Goddesses encounter when attempting to manipulate Reality at a fundamental level.

#### Velina Elderblood
Velina Elderblood is Velnorah's daughter and an increasingly important member of the Empire's scientific community.
Although younger than the principal researchers of [[Stellar Arsenal]], Velina possesses extraordinary intellectual potential through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s Blood Dragon lineage and Velnorah's scientific inheritance. Her unusual perspective becomes particularly valuable during the investigation of problems that older researchers have begun approaching through established assumptions.
Velina's proposal concerning an experimental paradox eventually attracts Velnorah's attention and leads to further investigation by Ruby, Aline and the other researchers. Their collaboration demonstrates that the Empire's scientific development depends not merely upon accumulated knowledge but also upon allowing new ideas to challenge methods previously regarded as sufficient.
By the later Imperial Era, Velina is recognized as one of the important contributors to projects involving the most advanced systems developed within Stellar Arsenal.

#### Ophis Tepes Elderblood and Nero Elderblood
[[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]] and [[Nero Elderblood]] are [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s adopted daughters and members of the family Ruby helped establish after the original marriage ritual.
Although Ruby is not their biological mother, she belongs to the original group of wives who accepted responsibility for Victor's expanding household. Their inclusion within the family predates Rosemary's birth and contributes to Ruby's understanding of the responsibilities associated with raising children possessing extraordinary supernatural potential.
Ruby regards them as members of the same dynasty rather than distinguishing their importance according to biological ancestry, reflecting the collective familial structure developed throughout Victor's household.

### Role in the Elderblood Empire
Ruby occupies one of the most strategically important positions within the [[Elderblood Empire]] through her status as an Imperial Empress and commander of the Fourth Legion, [[Stellar Arsenal]]. Her authority is simultaneously familial, scientific, technological and military, reflecting the unusual political structure developed through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s civilization.
As one of Victor's three founding wives, Ruby belongs to the oldest core of the Elderblood family and possesses a personal relationship with the Emperor that predates the creation of the Empire itself. However, her institutional importance does not depend exclusively upon marital status. Ruby earned her position as one of the Empire's principal scientific authorities through sustained research, practical experience and the development of systems capable of supporting an increasingly sophisticated civilization.
Stellar Arsenal was established during Imperial Year 50 when Ruby, Velnorah, Natalia and Alice recognized that the Empire's growing scientific knowledge had become too extensive to remain divided among independent institutions. The resulting Legion was designed to centralize advanced research, develop technologies capable of confronting extraordinary enemies and preserve dangerous information under controlled Imperial authority.[7]
The Legion's principal responsibilities include:
- Research and Development: Investigation and development of advanced technologies, supernatural systems, materials and infrastructure capable of improving Imperial civilization.[7]
- Superweapon Development: Creation of weapons intended to remain effective against threats capable of devastating Solar Systems and greater structures.[7]
- Extraterrestrial Technology: Acquisition, reverse engineering and integration of useful technologies originating from foreign civilizations.[7]
- Biological Research: Investigation of supernatural [[Races (Flamel)|Races]], Bloodlines, genetic development and methods of improving biological characteristics.[7]
- Temporal Research: Study of technologies and supernatural systems involving Time, including applications supporting the Empire's accelerated development.[7]
- Magitech Integration: Combination of conventional science with [[Magic]], Runes, Divine Artifacts, Draconic systems and other supernatural principles.[7]
- Dangerous Technology Management: Controlled investigation and storage of technologies considered too hazardous for unrestricted distribution throughout the Empire.[7]
- Advanced Automation: Employment of specialized researchers, artificial personnel and automatons capable of supporting experiments whose complexity or danger exceeds ordinary laboratory requirements.[7]
- Extraterrestrial Biology: Research involving alien organisms and lifeforms whose biology incorporates unusual supernatural or spatial characteristics.[31]
- Cosmological Engineering: Participation in projects involving the development and merger of Sectors and other large-scale supernatural structures.[11]
- Reality Research: Investigation of theoretical approaches to manipulating the fundamental Rules of Reality, including research associated with the proposed universal deterrent.[35]
- Divine Fragment Analysis: Examination of dangerous remnants of Divine existence recovered through Imperial operations.[12]
Ruby's command also demonstrates the importance of delegating genuine authority within Victor's civilization. Although the Emperor possesses extraordinary personal intelligence and supernatural abilities, the Empire cannot depend upon him individually conducting every experiment, supervising every laboratory or responding to every unfamiliar discovery. Stellar Arsenal instead places considerable resources beneath specialists capable of continuing research independently while remaining accountable to the broader Imperial structure.
The Legion's scientific philosophy is deliberately multidisciplinary. Knowledge is not rejected because it originates from Humans, Witches, Demons, Gods, Dragons or extraterrestrial civilizations. Its value depends upon whether its principles can be understood and applied in ways that improve the Empire's capabilities. This approach directly reflects Ruby's early insistence upon recovering the Church's experimental research rather than destroying everything merely because its creators were enemies.
By the later Imperial Era, the organization's responsibilities extend far beyond the requirements of ordinary warfare. Ruby's researchers investigate the structure of cosmological Sectors, develop technologies intended to threaten higher-order enemies and examine anomalies capable of affecting Souls or the fundamental laws governing supernatural existence.
Her position therefore makes her one of the principal architects of the Empire's long-term development. A powerful warrior can defeat an enemy through personal strength, but the technologies and systems developed by Ruby and Stellar Arsenal can allow entire Imperial armies and civilizations to confront similar threats without requiring the direct intervention of the Emperor.

### Abilities

#### Powers & Abilities
Ruby's current power-set is the result of several independent systems being accumulated and integrated across her life. She began as a Pure-Blooded Noble Vampire carrying separate Ice and Water Bloodlines, developed specialized Vampiric techniques and [[Scathach]]'s [[Martial Arts]], was later integrated into [[Victor Elderblood]]'s Progenitor Bloodline, underwent extensive combat development inside the Tower of Nightmares, and was ultimately reconstructed as a complete Blood Dragon and True Dragon. Victor's Dragonization did not erase Ruby's previous powers; compatible racial, elemental and supernatural characteristics were preserved inside a superior Draconic foundation.[1][2]

##### Blood Dragon / True Dragon Physiology
- Blood Dragon Physiology: Ruby was among the first members of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family to undergo his perfected Blood Dragon Race Change. The transformation rebuilt her physical and spiritual existence instead of merely adding Draconic traits onto a Vampire body.[1][2]
- True Dragon Body: Ruby possesses the genuine biological foundation of a complete True Dragon rather than the Noble Vampire body she possessed previously.[1][2]
- True Dragon Soul: The Race Change reconstructed Ruby's spiritual foundation together with her physical form, making her a complete Draconic existence instead of a Vampire possessing Dragon characteristics.[1][2]
- True Dragon Form: Ruby possesses a genuine Draconic form in addition to the Humanoid appearance she normally uses.[2][3]
- Humanoid Transformation: Ruby can maintain a Human-like body despite her complete Draconic nature, allowing her to function normally without permanently remaining in her full Dragon form.[2][3]
- [[Dragon Heart]]: Ruby possesses the supernatural heart characteristic of complete [[True Dragons]], providing the enormous Energy generation and circulation required to sustain a Draconic existence.[1][2]
- Immense Energy Capacity: Her Dragon Heart and reconstructed physiology can safely contain supernatural Energy vastly beyond the limits of her former Noble Vampire body.[1][2]
- Continuous Energy Generation: Ruby's Dragon Heart functions as an enormous internal supernatural Energy source rather than leaving her dependent entirely upon previously accumulated reserves.[1][2]
- Enhanced Energy Circulation: Ruby's Draconic body is naturally designed to channel quantities of Energy that would overwhelm less compatible supernatural races.[1][2]
- [[Draconic Energy]]: Ruby possesses the supernatural Energy inherent to Dragons in addition to the elemental systems preserved from her previous existence.[1][2]
- Innate Energy Manipulation: True Dragons do not require a Rune or conventional spell for every basic act of Energy manipulation. Their connection with Energy is part of their biology, allowing them to instinctively command supernatural Energy without treating every action as an external magical technique.[4]
- Multi-System Energy Compatibility: The natural compatibility of True Dragons allows Ruby to support multiple supernatural systems inside one body without the severe incompatibility problems common to artificially constructed hybrids.[1][4]
- Energy Reinforcement: Ruby can use supernatural Energy to reinforce her body and physical actions, an ability inherited from her earlier Vampire existence and massively improved by her Draconic foundation.[1][2]
- Enhanced Strength: Ruby's True Dragon body possesses physical strength vastly beyond the former Vampire benchmark she demonstrated earlier in the series.[2]
- Enhanced Speed: Dragonization dramatically increased Ruby's physical speed and acceleration.[2]
- Enhanced Durability: Ruby's reconstructed body can withstand levels of physical and supernatural punishment far beyond those her previous Vampire physiology was designed to endure.[2]
- Enhanced Endurance: True Dragon physiology allows Ruby to continue operating through extreme physical and supernatural strain.[2]
- Enhanced Stamina: Ruby possesses enormous stamina supported by her Dragon Heart and Draconic Energy.[2]
- Enhanced Reflexes: Her reaction speed was drastically improved through her superior physiology and centuries of subsequent training.[2]
- Enhanced Senses: Ruby's already-superhuman Vampire senses were enhanced further by her reconstruction as a complete Dragon.[1][2]
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Ruby possesses powerful Draconic regeneration, superseding the already considerable healing factor of her former Noble Vampire body.[1][2]
- Extreme Longevity: Ruby's True Dragon existence can survive enormous subjective periods without ordinary biological aging. She ultimately lives through more than two hundred thousand years of accelerated Imperial history while remaining physically youthful.[1][5]
- Flight: Ruby can naturally fly through her Draconic physiology.[2]
- Draconic Shapeshifting: Ruby possesses the bodily adaptability required to transition between her enormous Draconic existence and smaller forms suitable for ordinary interaction.[2][3]
- Connection to Creation: Like other genuine True Dragons, Ruby possesses the unusually deep compatibility with Creation and supernatural phenomena characteristic of the Race. This gives her an exceptionally versatile foundation but does not grant the universal Energy control possessed by Victor as the Blood Dragon Primogenitor.[1][4]
- Racial Essence Preservation: Victor's Dragonization preserved compatible characteristics Ruby had already developed rather than replacing them with a standardized Dragon power-set.[1][2]
- Bloodline Preservation: Ruby retained the Ice and Water powers inherited from her parents.[1][2]
- Technique Preservation: Compatible techniques and supernatural knowledge accumulated during her Vampire life remain usable through her superior Draconic foundation.[1][2]
- Adaptive Racial Evolution: Preserved abilities can continue developing through the vastly greater potential of a True Dragon body.[1][2]
- Vampiric Weakness Supersession: Ruby is no longer biologically an ordinary Vampire. Weaknesses that function solely because of standard Vampire physiology therefore cannot automatically be assumed to operate against her current Blood Dragon body in the same manner.[2][3]

###### Draconic Language and Reality Interaction
- Draconic Language: Ruby possesses and actively trained the supernatural language of Dragons. Draconic speech combines language, Energy, intention and the authority of the speaker rather than functioning as ordinary communication.[6][7]
- Speak of Power: Properly spoken Draconic words carry supernatural authority and can directly influence the world according to their meaning.[6]
- Simple Reality Imposition: Following Dragonization, members of the Scarlett and Fulger families discovered that words spoken with sufficient Draconic intent could impose relatively simple effects directly upon Reality.[6]
- Meaning Manifestation: The effect of Draconic speech is determined through the combination of the user's intended meaning, Energy and authority rather than through sound alone.[6]
- Reality-Based Object Manifestation (Racial Application): The newly transformed Dragons demonstrated that sufficiently simple objects or effects could be produced through Draconic commands, establishing direct manifestation as a basic application of the language.[6]
- Draconic Language Training: Ruby and the other transformed women deliberately trained Draconic speech after receiving their new bodies instead of assuming racial knowledge alone was enough for mastery.[7]
Ruby possesses the racial Draconic speech demonstrated by the transformed Scarlett family, but no unique Ruby-specific Reality command comparable to Anna's specialized expressions has yet been individually named.
- Draconic Runes: Ruby possesses the natural compatibility with Draconic Runes granted by True Dragon physiology.[6][7]
- Reality Instruction: Draconic Runes can combine language, Energy and [[Will Solace|Will]] to communicate instructions directly to Reality.[7]
- Conceptual Instruction: Advanced applications can impose meanings, Concepts, conditions or supernatural processes that go beyond ordinary elemental manipulation.[4]
- Innate Runic Compatibility: Ruby's True Dragon nature makes Draconic Runecraft naturally compatible with her existence, although advanced Runemaster-level techniques still require knowledge and training.[4]

###### Celestial Consumption
- Celestial Consumption (Mature True Dragon Racial Capability): Mature [[True Dragons]] can consume and digest astronomical objects that would destroy conventional supernatural bodies. Ruby herself demonstrates detailed knowledge of this racial capability when warning that a newborn Moira should not ordinarily be allowed to consume such objects.[8]
- Stellar Consumption: Mature Dragons can eventually consume stars, particularly when their bodies or powers possess sufficient compatibility with the phenomenon being eaten.[8]
- [[Black Hole]] Consumption: Ruby explicitly identifies black holes among the celestial phenomena mature Dragons can eventually consume.[8]
- Extreme Draconic Digestion: True Dragon physiology is capable of processing forms of matter and Energy that ordinary living beings could not safely ingest.[8]
The chapter establishes this as a mature True Dragon racial capability and Ruby possesses an ancient mature True Dragon body, but Ruby has not yet personally been shown eating a star or black hole on-page.

###### Dragon Breath
- Dragon Breath (Racial Capability): Complete [[Blood Dragons]] possess the biological foundation necessary to release a Dragon Breath shaped by their individual Draconic characteristics.[3]
Ruby's personal Breath has not yet received a directly demonstrated unique element, name or secondary effect, so no specific Ice Breath, Water Breath or conceptual Breath is indexed without further evidence.

##### Dual Elemental Bloodline — Ice and Water
- Dual Elemental Bloodline: Ruby inherited two independent elemental abilities through her parents. [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] passed down the Scarlett affinity with Ice, while Ruby's unnamed biological father possessed supernatural Water control and the ability to survive underwater. Ruby inherited both instead of receiving only one parental power.[9]

##### Ice Transition / Partial Elementalization

- Draconic Enhancement: Ruby's current True Dragon body provides vastly greater physical durability, Energy capacity and elemental compatibility than the Vampire body in which Ice Transition was originally developed.[1][2]

- Combat Adaptation: [[The Tower of Nightmares]] deliberately attacked Ruby's tendency to overanalyze and assume theoretical knowledge was sufficient to understand real enemies.[11][30]
