Nero Elderblood is an Imperial Princess of the [[Elderblood Empire]], a Blood Dragon, Dragon Goddess, Eldritch God of Apocalypse and one of the commanders of [[The Imperial Guard]]. She was naturally born from the otherwise incompatible union of a Noble Vampire woman and a Royal Werewolf man, making her existence an extraordinary exception among the two Night [[Races (Flamel)|Races]]. Her uniqueness eventually caused her to be captured by the organization surrounding [[Niklaus Horseman]], where she was designated Experiment No. 0 and used as the original natural specimen around which later Hybrid research was developed.[7][3]
[[Nero]] eventually escaped and survived as a fugitive until a chance encounter with Ophis Tepes Elderblood permanently changed her life. Despite possessing an unstable body, inferior regeneration and every practical reason to abandon the lost Vampire Princess, Nero protected Ophis throughout their flight across Japan and continued fighting even after suffering catastrophic injuries. [[Victor Elderblood]] responded not only by saving her but by reconstructing her damaged physiology with his Blood, stabilizing her as a Noble Vampire while preserving useful remnants of her Royal Werewolf inheritance. He subsequently brought her into his family as Nero Alucard, ending the identity imposed upon her as Number Zero.[8][9][10][11][12]
Victor's training transformed Nero from a child who survived primarily through firearms, preparation and desperation into one of the Elderblood family's most accomplished warriors. Her remaining Werewolf characteristics merged with her Vampiric foundation, and she later entered Victor's Blood Dragon lineage, gaining the adaptive physiology of a True Dragon. Long before acquiring a personal Divinity, Nero had already developed combat capability comparable to a Low-Level Combat God.[13][14][15]
Her greatest evolution came through the awakening of <Apocalypse>, an Eldritch Divinity associated with Calamity, Cataclysm, Disaster and Large-Scale Tragedy. Rather than merely producing a powerful explosion, Nero can turn separate failures and disasters into an escalating chain of catastrophes capable of driving an entire civilization toward extinction. The awakening completed her evolution into the White Apocalypse Dragon and revealed a deeper Nightmare Form connected to her Eldritch Soul.[16][2][17]
Across the immense accelerated history of the Elderblood Empire, Nero matured into one of the senior members of Victor's second generation and assumed a direct military role protecting the Imperial Family and its most valuable assets. After living for enormous periods of subjective time as an adult Dragon Goddess, her relationship with Victor also changed from the paternal relationship of her childhood into a marital one. Nero herself distinguishes the two periods, regarding Victor as the man who rescued and raised her when she was young while identifying herself in the present as his adult wife.[4][5]

### Physical Appearance

#### Hybrid
[[Nero]] originally appeared as a small girl approximately ten to thirteen years old with pale skin, short white hair and striking golden eyes. Her youthful appearance concealed the body of a naturally born Vampire-Werewolf Hybrid, but the combination was far from biologically stable. The powers of the two [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] did not integrate cleanly, her regenerative ability was considerably poorer than that expected from either a normal Vampire or Werewolf, and severe injuries remained genuinely life-threatening rather than temporary inconveniences.[11]
Years of experimentation worsened those defects. Nero's physiology carried accumulated damage and foreign substances produced by the procedures performed upon her, leaving her body in such poor condition that [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had to address far more than the injuries sustained during the Crimson Nightmare. Her small frame and apparent youth therefore contrasted with a body already carrying years of supernatural violence, experimentation and survival.[11]

#### Noble Vampire
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s reconstruction replaced [[Nero]]'s unstable Hybrid foundation with a predominantly Noble Vampire body created through his own Blood. Her golden eyes became blood red, her regeneration improved enormously and several Vampiric characteristics that her original body had been unable to sustain correctly finally became usable.[11]
The reconstruction did not completely erase her paternal lineage. A small portion of Nero's Royal Werewolf inheritance remained integrated into her new physiology, influencing her instincts, sensory perception, [[Natural Energy]] and Shapeshifting. She could produce unusually long claws, alter parts of her arms and legs into bestial forms and move with distinctly lupine posture and predatory behavior despite being biologically dominated by her Vampiric foundation.[3][13]
Rather than recreating the same incompatibility that once damaged her body, those remaining characteristics adapted to the Noble Vampire foundation. Nero consequently developed into an unusual Vampire whose fighting style retained meaningful Werewolf characteristics without requiring her to return to the defective condition of her birth.[13]

#### Blood Dragon
[[Nero]]'s transformation into a Blood Dragon altered both her supernatural structure and physical maturation. She developed prominent white Draconic horns, blood-red Draconic eyes and an increasingly mature body, eventually appearing as a young adult rather than the child [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] had originally adopted.[18][19]
True Dragon physiology was particularly significant for Nero because adaptability solved the central biological contradiction that had defined her earliest life. Vampire, Werewolf, Draconic and later Divine characteristics could coexist within a vastly superior foundation instead of damaging one another through incompatibility. What had once made Nero an unstable anomaly consequently became part of a much broader and more adaptable existence.
Nero retained her practical, tomboyish appearance after becoming a Dragon. She commonly favors trousers or leggings, high boots and training tops rather than elaborate dresses, frequently leaving her shoulders, arms or trained abdomen visible. Even after immense periods of Imperial development, the same general clothing preference remains immediately recognizable.[20][4]

#### White Apocalypse Dragon
[[Nero]]'s awakening of <Apocalypse> produced her most dramatic physical evolution. Her white hair became much longer and wilder, extending toward her waist while portions spread outward in a manner compared to a lion's mane. Her body matured to approximately 190 centimetres and developed a visibly athletic, muscular physique reflecting her immense period of martial training.[2]
Her Draconic characteristics became more pronounced, including large white wings, prominent horns and completely predatory Draconic eyes. White supernatural power mixes with black [[Eldritch Energy]] around her body, visually expressing the coexistence of her Dragon Goddess and Eldritch aspects.[2]
Nero can assume the immense complete Draconic form known as the White Apocalypse Dragon. Unlike a conventional elemental Dragon transformation, the form represents the combination of her True Dragon physiology, Eldritch nature and personal Concept of Apocalypse.[2]
Her Soul possesses an additional Nightmare Form associated with the deeper configuration of her Eldritch existence. Compared with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s increasingly incomprehensible Eldritch nature, Nero's Nightmare Form retains a comparatively humanoid structure while representing something fundamentally more alien than an ordinary Divine transformation.[17]

### Personality
[[Nero]]'s childhood produced a personality centered upon survival, distrust and self-reliance. Losing her parents, being treated as an experimental subject and surviving as a fugitive taught her to evaluate unfamiliar people as potential threats before considering friendship. Young Nero was suspicious, sarcastic, observant and uncomfortable displaying weakness, particularly because vulnerability had repeatedly been exploited during the period in which other people controlled virtually every aspect of her life.
Her defective body forced her to become resourceful long before she acquired overwhelming power. Nero could not depend upon the extreme regeneration normally associated with Vampires or [[Werewolves]], so she relied heavily upon firearms, knowledge of supernatural weaknesses, concealment, tactical positioning, deception and the willingness to retreat when necessary. The habits developed during this period remained valuable even after [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] gave her a body powerful enough to survive mistakes that would once have killed her.
Beneath the defensive personality, Nero possessed an extremely strong protective instinct. Her relationship with Ophis began without obligation, yet Nero continued defending the lost Princess even after doing so became increasingly dangerous. She repeatedly placed herself between Ophis and stronger pursuers until accumulated injuries left her near death, establishing that her instinct to protect someone she had accepted could override even the survival mentality developed through years as a fugitive.[9][10][11]
Nero initially found the affection of Victor's family almost as difficult to process as hostility. Victor openly declared her his daughter, Ruby rapidly adopted a maternal attitude toward her, Anna treated her as a granddaughter and Violet welcomed her without demanding that she prove her usefulness first. Nero's embarrassment whenever those relationships caused her to cry reflected how thoroughly she had learned to associate emotional exposure with danger.[21][22][23]
Violet directly challenged that mentality by teaching Nero that escaping a physical prison while continuing to imprison her own emotions would still allow the people who hurt her to control how she lived. The lesson became an important part of Nero's gradual transition from someone who merely survived to someone capable of enjoying the security of belonging to a family.[23]
As Nero became more comfortable, her naturally energetic and competitive personality emerged much more strongly. She is tomboyish, combative, stubborn and enthusiastic about physical training, weapons and difficult opponents. Politics and administration interest her considerably less than a meaningful fight, and she commonly becomes far more animated when presented with a new combat challenge.
Her rivalry with Ophis reflects this combination of affection and competitiveness. The sisters spar aggressively, tease one another and deliberately attempt to surprise each other with new techniques, yet there is little genuine insecurity beneath the competition. Nero's role as Ophis' protector eventually evolves into that of an elder sister and equal who expects Ophis to continue developing rather than remaining dependent upon others.[24][20]
Nero retains noticeably predatory characteristics from her Werewolf ancestry. Her posture can become animalistic during close combat, her instincts readily classify dangerous opponents through concepts of predator and prey, and she can display territorial or lupine behavior despite the overwhelmingly Draconic nature of her current existence.[13]
One of her most persistent flaws is the tendency to associate strength with security. Victor repeatedly works to prevent Nero's extraordinary talent from becoming reckless confidence, emphasizing that possessing superior statistics does not erase differences in knowledge, technique, perception or experience. Nero gradually develops the discipline to combine her natural aggression with observation and tactical judgment rather than simply charging whatever appears in front of her.[14]
Her hatred of weakness is rooted far more deeply than ordinary pride. Nero spent much of her life attempting to distance herself from the powerless child who could not save her parents or prevent her own captivity. That refusal to accept her past eventually became an obstacle to the development of her Divinity. Victor's trial forced Nero to recognize that the helpless child and the overwhelmingly powerful Dragon Goddess were the same person separated by growth rather than two different identities.[16][2]
Accepting that truth did not cause Nero to embrace weakness. Instead, she stopped treating former helplessness as personal guilt. She could acknowledge that she had once lacked the power to protect herself without accepting the idea that her captors had therefore been justified in deciding her fate. The emotional breakthrough became the foundation through which <Apocalypse> finally awakened.[2]
Adult Nero remains emotionally less direct than Ophis. She frequently attempts to present herself as the more mature and independent sister, but still enjoys Victor's affection and can become visibly jealous when Ophis openly receives attention that Nero secretly wants as well. Ophis recognizes this habit immediately and regularly mocks Nero for refusing to admit what she wants.[5]
Her preference for battle has likewise survived almost two hundred thousand years of Imperial development. Even after mastering most of her Draconic abilities, Nero remains dissatisfied while anything is left incomplete and intends to participate directly in future wars. In contrast to Ophis, who possesses enormous talent but little personal interest in warfare, Nero actively wants to test herself on the battlefield and considers complete mastery of her powers an ongoing personal objective.[5]

### Background
[[Nero]] was born naturally from an unnamed Noble Vampire woman and an unnamed Royal Werewolf man. Such a birth should have been extraordinarily unlikely because the two Night [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] were not merely political enemies; their biological and supernatural characteristics were fundamentally difficult to reconcile within a single body. Nero nevertheless survived, making her existence an invaluable natural example of a phenomenon other factions were attempting to reproduce artificially.[3]
The same uniqueness that allowed Nero to exist also left her vulnerable. Her body possessed traits from both parent Races without the biological stability required to use either inheritance normally. Her regeneration was poor, her abilities placed abnormal stress upon her and the physiology supporting them was fragile compared with the powerful supernatural creatures from whom she descended.[11]
Nero lost both biological parents while still young and eventually fell into the hands of the organization connected to [[Niklaus Horseman]]. She was designated Experiment No. 0 or Number Zero, becoming the reference specimen for research surrounding Vampire-Werewolf Hybrids. Her importance extended beyond merely reproducing another Hybrid; the unusual properties associated with her blood and biology were studied for potential applications capable of threatening members of both parent Races.[25][7]
The experiments left substantial physical and psychological damage, but Nero eventually escaped before the project could completely consume her life. She subsequently survived as a fugitive, moving through the supernatural underworld without a stable family or organization protecting her. That period ended only when Ophis accidentally appeared near her Japanese hideout and drew Nero into the chain of events that would connect her with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].

### Synopsis

#### A New Pillar
Long before [[Nero]] physically entered [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s life, information surrounding Number Zero began emerging through Clan Horseman's hidden activities. June recovered a fragmented recording containing references to a supposedly exceptional subject identified only by that designation, together with indications that whatever research surrounded the subject had not produced the result its creators expected. Ruby immediately recognized that the information pointed toward a deeper experimental program hidden beneath the political disputes involving Clan Horseman and began investigating the mystery through more extensive information networks.[25]
The project remained active even after Victor's position in Vampire society had changed dramatically. Niklaus and Theo continued discussing Experiment Number Zero while evaluating how the Hybrid research might be extended beyond simply manufacturing abnormal soldiers. The surviving information suggested applications capable of affecting much larger supernatural populations, making the unidentified subject important to a conspiracy that extended far beyond a single laboratory experiment.[7]

#### Discoveries, Changes, And War
[[Nero]] finally entered the story after Ophis accidentally teleported herself out of [[Nightingale]] while attempting to reach [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]. The young Princess appeared in Japan without understanding where she was or how to return, eventually encountering a white-haired, golden-eyed girl carrying firearms and living outside ordinary supernatural society. Nero initially treated Ophis as an inconvenient stranger whose presence could attract danger, but learning that the girl considered Victor her father gave her enough reason to keep Ophis nearby rather than abandon her immediately.[8]
What began as temporary assistance became genuine protection as supernatural pursuers closed in around them. Nero supplied Ophis with clothing capable of protecting her from sunlight and repeatedly moved between hiding places while attempting to keep the inexperienced Princess alive. Ophis' uncontrolled use of Space-related abilities made escape increasingly difficult, forcing Nero to bear most of the direct fighting despite possessing a body that regenerated far less effectively than those of ordinary Night creatures.[9][10]
The pursuit culminated in the Crimson Nightmare. Nero continued fighting after accumulating catastrophic injuries and placed herself between Ophis and their attackers even when her own survival had become unlikely. Victor eventually arrived, saw the condition of both girls and responded with overwhelming violence against the forces responsible.[11]
Saving Nero required more than closing her wounds. Victor found experimental contamination and longstanding defects throughout her body and chose to rebuild her instead of restoring the same unstable Hybrid physiology. His Blood became the foundation of the reconstruction, purging foreign material, repairing accumulated damage and restructuring Nero as a Noble Vampire while retaining compatible remnants of her Royal Werewolf inheritance.[11]
Victor's reaction to Nero was shaped as much by her actions as by her unusual biology. She had risked her life repeatedly for Ophis without expecting a reward, and Victor regarded that debt as something ordinary compensation could not adequately repay. He began treating Nero as part of his family and publicly introduced her as Nero Alucard, his adopted daughter, abruptly replacing the laboratory designation that had defined so much of her childhood with a personal name and family identity.[12]
Nero initially objected to Victor effectively deciding that adoption had already happened without consulting her, but resistance became increasingly difficult once the rest of the household accepted her just as naturally. Victor openly encouraged her to think of him as her father, Ruby developed into her principal maternal figure and Ophis wanted Nero beside her as an older sister rather than merely remembering her as the fugitive who once protected her.[26][27]
Victor quickly discovered that Nero's reconstruction had not erased everything inherited from her biological father. Her Noble Vampire body retained a small but meaningful Royal Werewolf component, allowing lupine instincts, [[Natural Energy]] and altered Shapeshifting to manifest through an otherwise Vampiric foundation. Nero's arms and legs could adopt bestial characteristics, her claws became formidable natural weapons and her movement reflected the instincts of a predator accustomed to fighting without the luxury of limitless regeneration.[3][13]
Rather than allowing her to depend entirely upon the stronger body he had given her, Victor began teaching Nero formal [[Martial Arts]]. Her survival experience had already produced quick reactions, improvisation and a strong sense for danger, but those instincts lacked the disciplined foundations necessary for fighting powerful trained opponents. Victor began correcting her posture, movement, control and decision-making while encouraging her to preserve the unconventional habits that genuinely worked.[28]
Life inside the Alucard household challenged Nero in ways combat never had. Anna immediately treated her as a granddaughter, Ruby understood many of the defenses created by Nero's trauma and Violet explicitly welcomed her as another daughter. Nero struggled with the resulting emotions because affection without exploitation was unfamiliar, but repeated interactions with the family gradually convinced her that she no longer needed to behave as though every moment of vulnerability would be used against her.[21][22][23]
Her social circle also expanded beyond Victor and Ophis. Eve understood several aspects of Nero's defensive behavior because of her own traumatic history, while Pepper became increasingly friendly with her. The relationships gave Nero something she had lacked during most of her childhood: peers she could interact with without every conversation being connected to survival, captivity or experimentation.[23][29]
By the later stages of the Volume, Nero had ceased functioning as a rescued guest living inside Victor's household. She had become one of his daughters, trained with the rest of the family and possessed a stable identity within Clan Alucard that no longer depended upon Number Zero or the Hybrid Project.

#### Demonic War
[[Nero]]'s place within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family continued solidifying as the supernatural world entered large-scale war. Victor consistently treated both Nero and Ophis as daughters while refusing to let that protection turn them into helpless dependents. Nero's natural talent was encouraged through training, but the expectation remained that she would eventually become capable of making decisions and surviving dangerous situations without requiring Victor to solve every problem personally.[24]
Ophis formally embraced Nero as her big sister, giving a name to the relationship that had effectively existed since Japan. Their personalities were already visibly different—Ophis remained more openly affectionate and dependent upon people she trusted, while Nero preferred presenting herself as the tougher and more independent elder sister—but the original protective bond between them remained the foundation of their relationship.[24]
Nero also began participating in conflicts far beyond the scale of the pursuers she once fled from. During the wider Demonic War she fought alongside members of Victor's faction during the confrontation involving the Amazons, placing her among warriors whose battles influenced entire supernatural powers rather than small groups of hunters and fugitives.[30]
The contrast with her childhood was already enormous. Nero had entered Victor's life possessing an unstable body and only enough power to survive through caution, weapons and stubbornness; she was now being raised inside a family of Progenitors, Count-level Vampires, Gods, Demons and master combatants while receiving direct training from one of the fastest-growing supernatural beings in existence.

#### King Among Kings
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s return after centuries spent ruling Hell brought a much more experienced version of him back into [[Nero]]'s training. His lessons increasingly focused upon judgment rather than merely physical improvement. Nero already possessed exceptional instincts and talent, but those advantages could become liabilities if they convinced her that direct confrontation was always the correct response.[14]
During one training exercise, Victor deliberately criticized Nero for rushing a superior opponent simply because external conditions had made their raw parameters appear comparable. He emphasized that experience, technique, perception, hidden abilities and battlefield knowledge remained meaningful even when obvious differences in strength were removed. Nero was expected to learn how to identify what an enemy could do before committing herself to an attack rather than treating talent as permission to ignore risk.[14]
The expanding investigation into [[New Dawn]] and the surviving conspiracies surrounding Niklaus also placed Nero's childhood within a much larger context. What had happened to her was not an isolated atrocity committed by a few researchers but part of a network of experiments, political schemes and attempts to develop supernatural weapons. Victor's growing access to information connected those fragments together while ensuring that the people responsible could no longer simply disappear behind the organizations that had protected them.[31]
Nero nevertheless continued moving farther away from being defined exclusively through that history. Accelerated training, the security of Victor's family and repeated exposure to powerful opponents allowed her to mature physically and psychologically, transforming the frightened child who once had to calculate whether every injury would kill her into a warrior capable of deliberately seeking opponents who could challenge her.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s ascension and the discovery of civilizations beyond Earth's familiar supernatural sphere changed what he expected from the next generation. [[Nero]] received a pair of exceptionally powerful linked Divine firearms designed to use her own Energy as ammunition. Their output could be adjusted according to the situation, allowing Nero to preserve the weapon-based fighting habits of her childhood while scaling them to a level appropriate for the opponents Victor expected his daughters eventually to face.[6]
Victor also granted Nero the Blessings associated with Wisdom of War and Peace of Nature. The first complemented her lifelong orientation toward combat, while the latter provided a balancing influence against the more aggressive and predatory characteristics accumulated through her unusual lineage.[6]
The family's next great transformation offered Nero something even more important than stronger equipment. Victor began giving members of his family the opportunity to enter his True Dragon lineage without simply erasing the identities and powers they already possessed. Nero accepted, evolving from the Noble Vampire body Victor had once built for her into a Blood Dragon whose adaptive physiology could integrate her Vampiric, Werewolf and other supernatural characteristics within one stable existence.[18][32]
The change finally eliminated the central weakness of Nero's birth. The coexistence of incompatible [[Races (Flamel)|Races]] had originally made her a medical impossibility whose own body threatened her survival; True Dragonhood instead turned adaptability into one of the defining principles of her physiology. The characteristics that once conflicted could now exist as components of a much greater whole.
Nero accompanied Victor, Ophis, [[Metis]] and Ariel during the diplomatic journey to meet the [[Heavenly Father]]. Exposure to Heaven and Victor's discussions regarding identity, [[Chaos]] and the limitations imposed by cosmological systems further broadened the environment in which Nero and Ophis were developing. Their education was no longer restricted to Vampire society or Earth's Pantheons but increasingly involved the structure of Divinity, Concepts and the larger Reality surrounding their Sector.[33][34]
Her combat growth continued advancing independently of Divine awakening. Nero became capable of fighting at a level comparable to a Low-Level Combat God while still lacking a genuine personal Divinity of her own, demonstrating how far True Dragon physiology, Victor's training and accumulated experience had already carried her.[15]
Nero and Ophis also began functioning more visibly as experienced members of Victor's family rather than protected children. They explored the civilization developing around him, trained together and accumulated practical experience that later distinguished them from many of their extraordinarily talented younger sisters.[35][36]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
[[Nero]] and Ophis eventually reached a problem raw power could not solve. Both possessed extraordinary racial abilities, vast Energy and extensive training, yet neither had awakened the personal Divinity expected from beings developing toward genuine Godhood. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and Jeanne therefore focused their education upon what a Divinity actually represented: not an arbitrary ability granted because someone desired more power, but an expression of identity, understanding and the Concept with which the individual genuinely resonated.[37]
Victor transformed the lesson into an experiential trial rather than leaving it as theory. Nero was placed inside an artificial Reality where the protections, family connections and overwhelming advantages defining her current life could no longer be taken for granted. The constructed circumstances forced her to confront the child she had spent immense periods attempting to leave behind and the memories she still associated with helplessness.[19][38]
The Energy responding to Nero was fundamentally unusual. She had inherited more than Victor's Draconic physiology; because Victor's own deeper existence had developed beyond the conventional native systems of the Cosmos, traces of that Eldritch nature could also appear within his descendants. Nero's developing Divinity consequently possessed an externalized quality different from that of a conventional native Goddess.[39][40]
The trial repeatedly returned Nero to the defining moments of her childhood: the deaths of her parents, captivity, experimentation and the inability of her younger self to prevent stronger people from determining what happened to her. Nero initially resisted those memories by mentally separating the helpless child from the woman she had become. The breakthrough came when she stopped doing so. She accepted that she really had been weak and powerless while rejecting the conclusion that those facts made what happened to her deserved or inevitable.[16][2]
That acceptance caused the concept developing around her to crystallize into <Apocalypse>. Victor identified its major expressions through ideas such as Calamity, Cataclysm, Disaster and Large-Scale Tragedy. Nero did not merely become a Goddess capable of producing enormous destructive force; her authority concerned the process through which independent disasters combine until an existing order can no longer survive.[16]
The artificial world around Nero immediately displayed the distinction. Technological failures, atmospheric disturbances, violent weather, collapsing infrastructure, supernatural conflict, geological catastrophes and other disasters began occurring together. Individual events amplified one another until civilization was approaching terminal collapse in an extraordinarily short period. Apocalypse therefore functioned less like one conventional attack and more like Reality itself entering a cascading state of failure.[2]
Nero physically evolved with the awakening, becoming the White Apocalypse Dragon. Her body reached approximately 190 centimetres, her hair lengthened, her Draconic characteristics intensified and the power surrounding her combined the white appearance of her Dragon form with the darker influence of her Eldritch nature.[2]
The deeper transformation also affected her Soul. Victor's examination revealed a Nightmare Form connected to Nero's Eldritch existence, confirming that <Apocalypse> had changed more than the superficial expression of her Divine power. Nero remained far younger and less developed than ancient Eldritch existences, but she had unquestionably crossed into a category that ordinary Gods could not completely describe.[17]
Her first reaction after completing the ordeal was not triumph. Nero immediately sought Victor, while Victor and Ruby reassured her that the artificial world was gone and that she was home. The moment reflected how much her development had changed: she no longer needed to deny vulnerability in order to consider herself strong.[17]
The enormous passage of subjective time that followed transformed Nero and Ophis into senior figures among Victor's daughters. Their younger sisters possessed extraordinary inherited talent, but Nero and Ophis held an advantage that could not simply be inherited at birth—vast accumulated experience. Their sparring demonstrated how naturally they combined Dragon abilities, advanced movement and the Martial foundations passed down through Victor and [[Scathach]].[20]
Nero remained the more openly combat-oriented of the pair. Ophis paid greater attention to Imperial information and administrative matters, while Nero preferred training and practical fighting. The difference did not reduce their closeness; their complementary personalities instead became one of the defining features of their adult relationship.[20]
Her connection with the expanding Elderblood family also became more developed. Nero particularly spoiled [[Rosemary Lavina Elderblood]], treating Ruby's biological daughter with special affection because Nero genuinely regarded Ruby as her own mother. The former child who once lacked any secure family had consequently become one of the older sisters responsible for guiding and protecting an enormous younger generation.[20]
As Nero matured into a fully adult Dragon Goddess with immense subjective experience, her feelings toward Victor eventually developed beyond the paternal bond that had defined her childhood. The transition occurred only after her independent growth into an adult warrior and would later become an acknowledged marital relationship during the Imperial era.[20]

#### Those Who Rule Reality
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s preparations for the civilizations and rulers of the Upper Sectors transformed the [[Elderblood Empire]] on a scale unlike anything [[Nero]]'s generation had previously experienced. A colossal temporal ritual accelerated Imperial development toward approximately two hundred thousand internal years while only a fraction of that time passed outside. Victor deliberately expected his daughters to use the era for growth rather than carrying the primary burden of maintaining the ritual, allowing Nero and the rest of the second generation to accumulate histories longer than many entire Mortal civilizations.[41][1]
The same period formalized the Empire's increasingly specialized military structure. [[The Imperial Guard]] became the Fifth Legion, retaining responsibility for the direct protection of the Emperor, the Imperial Family and other strategically irreplaceable Imperial assets. Nero's military position became one of the established roles distinguishing her from younger Imperial Princesses who were still deciding how they wanted to contribute to the civilization created by their parents.[42][43]
The contrast between Nero's beginnings and her present responsibilities was extreme. She had once survived by hiding from forces capable of exploiting a child with poor regeneration; she now belonged to the senior generation of Imperial Princesses serving within a civilization whose elite military forces, Gods, Dragons, technology and cosmological infrastructure had been refined across tens of thousands of years.
Nero and Ophis continued occupying a unique position among their sisters. During a massive gathering involving more than thirty-five of Victor's daughters, the pair arrived together and were openly recognized as the family's "sister wives". Their arrival drew immediate reactions from the younger Princesses, while Nero's appearance remained unmistakably hers despite the extraordinary amount of time that had passed: black trousers, high boots and a top displaying the trained physique produced by generations of combat practice.[4]
The Imperial reunion also showed how naturally Nero now existed inside the enormous dynasty. [[Metis]], Akasha, Melida and numerous younger sisters gathered beside her, while [[Scathach]] and [[Dún Scáith|Dun Scaith]]'s appearance immediately restored the discipline learned through generations of family training. Nero was no longer an adopted outsider attached to Victor's household; she was one of the senior women around whom younger members of the Elderblood generation had grown up.[4]
The [[Imperial Calendar]] eventually approached its two-hundred-thousand-year anniversary. Nero had lived through the civilization's transformation from Victor's comparatively young faction into an Empire spread across countless worlds and galaxies, gaining a subjective lifespan that dwarfed the historical periods separating every earlier stage of her life.[44][1]
When Victor became severely depleted from maintaining the colossal ritual, Nero and Ophis personally sought him out and inspected his condition rather than simply accepting his assurance that he was fine. Nero's first concern was the state of his Soul, reflecting both her knowledge of Victor's tendency to endure excessive burdens and the depth of the attachment that had survived every transformation in their relationship.[5]
The conversation directly clarified how Nero interprets that relationship in the present. Ophis openly stated that Victor would always remain her father even after becoming her husband, while Nero rejected applying the same description to herself. She acknowledged that Victor saved her, brought her into his family and gave her a future when she was young, but considered the daughter stage of their relationship part of her past and identified herself in the present as his adult wife.[5]
Victor remained considerably more sentimental about the distinction. Watching Nero and Ophis mature reminded him of the children he had once protected, and he continued regarding both wives and daughters as irreplaceable members of his family even while accepting Nero's adult identity. Nero attempted to behave accordingly, sitting beside him with deliberate composure while Ophis immediately claimed his lap, only to quietly sulk until Victor pulled Nero onto his other leg. Her instant acceptance exposed the same emotional stubbornness Ophis had teased for years.[5]
The sisters briefly considered motherhood after Ophis announced that she wanted a daughter of her own. Nero initially joined the idea, imagining what her own child might be like, but both quickly reconsidered once Victor reminded them of the responsibility involved in raising daughters born with the potential of [[True Dragons]]. Having witnessed the difficulties their mothers and sisters experienced raising children capable of affecting entire worlds, Nero and Ophis decided that motherhood could wait.[5]
Nero had an additional reason for postponing it. She had mastered most of her Draconic abilities but did not consider "most" sufficient, and the possibility of another major war made her unwilling to remove herself from combat. She correctly understood that Victor would never allow her to enter a dangerous battlefield while pregnant and preferred completing her development before accepting that responsibility.[5]
Ophis made the opposite choice concerning the coming war. Although highly talented and still maintaining her combat training, she had little personal desire to fight. Nero accepted her sister's decision despite wishing Ophis would join her and instead promised to return with trophies from the battlefield. Their differing choices illustrate the freedom Victor's family ultimately gave them: both possessed the ability to become terrifying combatants, but only Nero genuinely wanted warfare to remain part of her life.[5]
Nero consequently enters the current era as the complete reversal of Experiment No. 0. The child whose incompatible body prevented her from properly expressing either parental Race now possesses the adaptable foundation of a True Dragon, a civilization-ending Eldritch Divinity, immense martial experience, an established military position within the Elderblood Empire and the freedom to pursue battle because she chooses it rather than because survival leaves her no alternative.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
[[Victor Elderblood]] is the central figure in [[Nero]]'s life. Their relationship began after Nero nearly died protecting Ophis during the Crimson Nightmare. Victor could have simply healed the immediate injuries and compensated her for helping his daughter, but he considered Nero's willingness to risk her life for Ophis worth substantially more. He rebuilt her body, removed the contamination left by experimentation and gave her a stable supernatural foundation capable of surviving the future she had been denied.[11]
Victor subsequently brought Nero into his family. He began referring to her as his daughter and eventually presented her publicly as Nero Alucard, effectively replacing Experiment No. 0 with an identity belonging to a household rather than a laboratory.[12][26]
Nero initially found Victor's paternal attitude irritating because he frequently decided what he believed was best for her before waiting for permission. The same behavior nevertheless came with unconditional protection, training and personal investment. Victor did not treat Nero as valuable because of the scientific possibilities contained within her blood; he wanted her to become strong enough that no one could ever reduce her to a specimen again.
His role as teacher became nearly as important as his role as protector. Victor built upon Nero's survival instincts while correcting recklessness, taught her [[Martial Arts]], supplied equipment designed specifically around her fighting style and repeatedly pushed her to understand why she was using an ability instead of simply accumulating more power.[28][14][6]
Victor also became the person Nero mentally associated with safety. During the artificial Reality created for her Divine awakening, the belief that Victor still existed outside the nightmare became one of the thoughts anchoring her sense of self. Once the trial ended, her immediate instinct was to call for him, after which Victor and Ruby reassured her that she was home.[2][17]
Their relationship eventually changed after Nero had matured into an adult Dragon Goddess and lived through immense periods of independent experience. Nero developed romantic feelings for Victor and ultimately became his wife.[20][4]
Nero personally draws a clear distinction between those periods. She remembers Victor as the father who rescued and raised her when she was young but no longer considers the paternal relationship the primary definition of their present bond. Victor is more sentimental and continues remembering the child he raised even while accepting Nero as an adult wife.[5]

#### Ruby Elderblood Scarlett
[[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]] is [[Nero]]'s principal adoptive mother. Ruby becomes attached to Nero soon after the Japanese crisis, rapidly treating the newly adopted girl as part of the household rather than a temporary responsibility created by [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s gratitude.[12]
Ruby's analytical personality also makes her well suited to understanding Nero's emotional defenses. When other family members become concerned by Nero's reactions, Ruby can explain the background producing them without reducing Nero to a collection of symptoms or treating every defensive response as irrational.[22]
Ruby remains beside Victor during Nero's awakening of <Apocalypse> and is one of the first people to reassure her after the artificial Reality ends. Nero's identification of Ruby as her mother remains strong enough that it later influences her relationship with Ruby's biological daughter Rosemary, whom Nero treats with especially intense affection.[17][20]

#### Ophis Tepes Elderblood
[[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]] is [[Nero]]'s closest sister and the person whose accidental appearance ultimately brought Nero into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family. Their relationship began when Ophis' failed teleportation left her stranded in Japan and Nero reluctantly assumed responsibility for keeping the inexperienced Princess alive.[8]
That responsibility quickly became personal. Nero continued protecting Ophis across days of pursuit even as her defective Hybrid body accumulated damage it could barely repair. She ultimately reached a condition in which continuing to fight could realistically kill her but still refused to abandon Ophis.[9][10][11]
After Nero's adoption, Ophis explicitly wanted her as an elder sister and Nero accepted the role. Their relationship subsequently grew across centuries and then immense periods of accelerated Time, changing from one frightened child protecting another into a partnership between two extraordinarily powerful adult Dragon Goddesses.[24]
The sisters train and spar together frequently. Ophis possesses exceptional abilities connected to Infinity and Reality while Nero is the more naturally combat-oriented fighter, creating a rivalry in which both continuously attempt to surprise the other. Their fights can appear extremely violent to outsiders, but the aggression exists upon a foundation of absolute trust.[20]
Their personalities remain markedly different. Ophis is more willing to display affection openly and has greater interest in information outside direct combat, while Nero prefers presenting herself as the independent older sister and gravitates toward martial development. The difference remains visible even in the current era: Ophis has no particular desire to participate in the coming war, whereas Nero considers the battlefield part of her own future.[5]
Both eventually entered adult marital relationships with Victor and are collectively referred to as sister wives within the Elderblood family. Ophis nevertheless continues defining Victor as both father and husband, while Nero personally separates her former daughter relationship from her present marriage.[4][5]

#### Violet Elderblood Snow
[[Violet Elderblood Snow]] became one of the first women in [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family to confront the psychological prison [[Nero]] had carried out of the laboratory. Nero expected Victor's existing household to resent another child suddenly being inserted into the family, but Violet instead welcomed her directly as a daughter.[23]
When Nero became ashamed of crying and displaying vulnerability, Violet challenged the assumption that emotion was equivalent to weakness. She warned Nero that allowing old trauma to dictate every future emotional response would merely replace the physical cage she had escaped with another prison built inside her own mind.[23]
The interaction became important to Nero's gradual acceptance that strength did not require permanent emotional isolation.

#### Anna Elderblood
[[Anna Elderblood]] initially knew [[Nero]] as [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s adopted daughter and consequently treated her as a granddaughter. Rather than approaching Nero through the extraordinary circumstances of her biology, Anna's instinctive response was the ordinary familial concern she showed toward Victor's children.[21]
Anna quickly noticed the intensity of Nero's defensive reactions and asked Ruby about what the girl had experienced instead of interpreting those reactions as simple hostility. The concern contributed to Nero's growing realization that members of Victor's family could care about her wellbeing without demanding usefulness in exchange.[22]
Anna's later position within the Elderblood dynasty became considerably more complicated as Victor's family evolved, but her role as one of its principal matriarchs and disciplinarians continues placing her among the senior figures Nero respects.

#### Biological Parents
[[Nero]]'s biological parents were an unnamed Noble Vampire woman and an unnamed Royal Werewolf man. Their relationship produced Nero naturally despite the incompatibility traditionally associated with the two [[Races (Flamel)|Races]].[3]
Both died while Nero was still a child. The inability to protect them became one of the memories most strongly connected to Nero's hatred of weakness, and she spent much of her later life psychologically distancing herself from the powerless girl who had witnessed those losses.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s trial eventually forced Nero to confront those memories directly. Her acceptance that she genuinely had been helpless—without accepting responsibility for what stronger people chose to do—became one of the psychological foundations necessary for awakening <Apocalypse>.[16][2]

#### Radu Tepes / Niklaus Horseman
[[Niklaus Horseman]] is one of the principal figures associated with [[Nero]]'s childhood suffering. The organization surrounding him recognized the strategic importance of a naturally born Vampire-Werewolf Hybrid and used Nero as Experiment No. 0, turning her body into the reference point for research intended to reproduce, weaponize or otherwise exploit the phenomenon.[7]
Niklaus' influence extended beyond the physical experimentation. The experiences created beneath his organization reinforced Nero's belief that weakness allowed stronger people to determine what happened to her. Even after escaping, that idea remained deeply embedded in how she understood herself and became one of the central psychological barriers obstructing her later Divine development.[2]
Nero's evolution into the White Apocalypse Dragon represents the complete inversion of the condition in which Niklaus first knew her. The child once valuable because others could restrain and experiment upon her became an Eldritch Dragon Goddess capable of turning the mechanisms sustaining entire civilizations into a cascading apocalypse.

#### Eve Elderblood
[[Eve Elderblood]] forms an easy friendship with [[Nero]] because both understand what it means to enter [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family after severe abuse. Their histories are different, but Eve recognizes several of the defensive habits Nero developed and can interact with her without treating distrust or aggression as incomprehensible behavior.[23]
Their connection becomes part of Nero's expansion beyond the extremely narrow social world she possessed as a fugitive.

#### Pepper Scarlett Elderblood
[[Pepper Scarlett Elderblood]] becomes increasingly close to [[Nero]] after her adoption into [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s household.[29]
Pepper's energetic personality and her own insecurities surrounding personal strength give Nero another relationship based upon ordinary family interaction rather than trauma, experimentation or protection. Nero's friendship with Pepper also helps connect her more closely with the wider Scarlett branch of Victor's family.

#### Rosemary Lavina Elderblood
[[Rosemary Lavina Elderblood]] is one of [[Nero]]'s younger sisters and receives especially intense affection from her. Because Nero sincerely regards Ruby as her mother, Ruby's biological daughter occupies a particularly close place within Nero's understanding of the family.[20]
Nero consequently spoils Rosemary considerably and treats her as one of the younger sisters for whom her seniority carries a direct protective responsibility.

#### Powers & Abilities
Nero Elderblood is an exceptionally powerful hybridized existence whose current power combines multiple successive stages of supernatural evolution. She was originally born as a natural Vampire-Werewolf Hybrid, was later stabilized by [[Victor Elderblood]] as a Noble Vampire while retaining useful characteristics inherited from her Werewolf father, and eventually evolved into a genuine Blood Dragon. After awakening her personal Divinity, [[Nero]] further became a Dragon Goddess and an Eldritch God whose defining Concept is <Apocalypse>.[1][2]
Her present existence should therefore not be treated as a simple replacement of her previous [[Races (Flamel)|Races]]. Blood Dragon evolution is highly adaptive and incorporates compatible characteristics from the previous existence into the resulting True Dragon foundation. Nero's former Vampiric powers, remnants of her Royal Werewolf bloodline, combat instincts and unusual Hybrid characteristics consequently became part of a vastly superior Draconic body rather than simply disappearing.
Nero's greatest personal power is her Divinity of Apocalypse, representing events capable of producing catastrophic collapse on the scale of civilizations and potentially greater structures. Her demonstrated usage does not merely destroy targets directly: she can manipulate the circumstances, causes and effects surrounding an area so that multiple disasters converge toward an apocalyptic result.[3][2]

##### Eldritch God Physiology
After awakening her Divinity, [[Nero]] became an Eldritch God of Apocalypse. Her Divine existence is consequently connected to the Eldritch side of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] rather than functioning exclusively according to the conventional native Divine system.[3][4]

###### Eldritch Energy
- [[Eldritch Energy]] Manipulation: [[Nero]] possesses and can release the foreign supernatural Energy associated with Eldritch Beings.[2]
- Foreign Cosmological Energy: Eldritch Energy does not originate from the conventional supernatural framework established by this Universe's native Primordials.
- Rule-Defying Nature: [[Outer Gods]] originate outside the native cosmology and are therefore not naturally restricted by all of the same Rules established by [[Primordial Chaos]] for native Creation.[5]
- Eldritch-Divine Fusion: Nero's Eldritch Energy coexists with her Draconic and Divine power rather than replacing them. During her awakening, white Draconic Power became intertwined with dark Eldritch Energy.[2]

###### Cosmological Rule Independence
As an Eldritch Being, [[Nero]] possesses a fundamentally foreign relationship with the native cosmology.[5]
- Native Rule Resistance: Rules belonging specifically to the native cosmological system do not automatically possess the same absolute authority over Nero that they would possess over native Beings.
- Foreign Existential Nature: Nero's current existence partly originates from a supernatural framework external to ordinary Creation.
- Conceptual Rule Interference Resistance: Native conceptual systems can interact with Nero but cannot always suppress her abilities through the same mechanisms that would work against an ordinary native God.[3]
This property should not be interpreted as unconditional immunity to every Law, Rule, Concept, Primordial or superior Being. Sufficiently powerful existences can still oppose, reduce or overwhelm Nero.

###### Eldritch Growth
[[Nero]] is still comparatively young by the standards of Eldritch existence.
- Open-Ended Eldritch Development: Nero's current classification as an ordinary Eldritch God represents a stage of development rather than necessarily her permanent ceiling.[4]
- Potential Higher Eldritch Evolution: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] states that Nero and [[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]] possess the possibility of eventually reaching stages comparable to higher Eldritch Beings if they continue developing.[4]
- Potential Conceptual Evolution: Advanced Eldritch Beings can eventually become increasingly inseparable from the Concepts they represent.[4]
- Potential Madness-Inducing True Presence: At sufficiently advanced stages, perceiving the true form of powerful Eldritch entities can threaten the minds of ordinary Beings.[4]
These are developmental possibilities and should not be confused with abilities current Nero has already demonstrated at their maximum level.

###### Nightmare Form
[[Nero]] possesses a personal Nightmare Form representing the deeper Eldritch shape of her Soul.[4]
Nightmare Form
- True Eldritch Soul Form: Nero's Nightmare Form is associated with the genuine form of her current Eldritch Soul rather than being merely cosmetic armor or an illusion.[4]
- Humanoid Eldritch Form: Unlike [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s overwhelmingly vast and fundamentally formless Eldritch existence, Nero's own Nightmare Form remains comparatively humanoid.[4]
- Eldritch Manifestation: Entering this state allows Nero's foreign supernatural nature to manifest more openly.

##### Divinity — <Apocalypse>
[[Nero]]'s personal Divine Concept is <Apocalypse>.[3]
The Concept represents events that produce large-scale destruction, societal collapse or the ending of a civilization. [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] identifies several principal expressions within Apocalypse: Calamity, Cataclysm, Disaster and Large-Scale Tragedy.[3]

###### Apocalypse Manipulation
- Apocalypse Inducement: [[Nero]] can cause conditions within a region to develop toward an apocalyptic outcome.[3][2]
- Civilization Collapse: Her Concept is particularly effective at creating chains of events capable of destroying organized civilizations rather than merely damaging individual opponents.[3]
- Large-Scale Catastrophe Generation: Nero can simultaneously trigger numerous independent forms of catastrophe throughout a sufficiently large affected region.[2]
- Apocalyptic Convergence: Separate environmental, technological and social failures can occur together and reinforce one another until the situation becomes civilization-ending.[2]

###### Causality Manipulation
One of the most dangerous characteristics of Apocalypse is its relationship with Cause and Effect.
- Cause-and-Effect Manipulation: [[Nero]] can influence causal relationships within the affected area so that circumstances capable of producing disaster begin occurring.[3]
- Causal Disaster Induction: Instead of manually creating every destructive event, Nero can alter the conditions that cause disasters to emerge.
- Apocalyptic Causal Chains: One catastrophe can produce the conditions for another, creating a self-reinforcing sequence of destructive events.
- Latent-Cause Exploitation: Nero's Concept can exploit vulnerabilities or circumstances that already possess the potential to become catastrophic.[3]
- Future-Cause Exploitation: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s explanation indicates that Apocalypse can draw upon events or vulnerabilities that could normally develop later and bring their consequences into the active chain of disaster.[3]
This should not be interpreted as unrestricted manipulation of every possible causal relationship in existence. Nero's demonstrated Causality application is specifically tied to the manifestation of Apocalypse.

###### Disaster Manipulation
[[Nero]] can create or provoke numerous kinds of disasters through Apocalypse.
- Natural Disaster Inducement
- Environmental Disaster Inducement
- Meteorological Disaster Inducement
- Geological Disaster Inducement
- Technological Disaster Inducement
- Infrastructure Collapse
- Social Disaster Inducement
During her awakening, several of these effects manifested simultaneously across [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s artificial Earth.[2]

###### Meteorological Disasters
- Extreme Weather Manipulation: The planetary environment became violently unstable following the activation of Apocalypse.[2]
- Tornado Generation: Large tornadoes manifested as part of the cascading disasters.[2]
- Storm Inducement: Atmospheric conditions deteriorated into destructive supernatural weather.

###### Geological Disasters
- Earthquake Inducement: [[Nero]]'s Apocalypse caused enormous seismic activity.[2]
- Tectonic Rupture: The ground began splitting apart as the planet entered widespread geological collapse.[2]
- Volcanic Eruption Inducement: Previously dormant or stable volcanoes began erupting as part of the Apocalypse.[2]

###### Technological and Infrastructure Disasters
- Infrastructure Destruction: Essential infrastructure began failing throughout the artificial world.[2]
- Energy-System Failure: Power and energy systems destabilized as the cascading disasters expanded.
- Power-Plant Destruction: Industrial and energy installations began exploding or failing catastrophically.[2]
- Systemic Collapse: [[Nero]]'s Concept can turn localized technological vulnerabilities into civilization-wide breakdown when enough systems depend upon one another.

###### Societal Disaster Inducement
[[Nero]]'s Apocalypse does not affect only the physical environment.
- Social Collapse: Civilization itself can begin destabilizing under the Concept's influence.[2]
- Mass Conflict Inducement: Individuals within the artificial world began attacking one another as social order collapsed.[2]
- Civil Disorder Inducement: Existing tensions and instability can become part of the Apocalypse's cascading destruction.
This should not automatically be categorized as unrestricted Madness Manipulation. The demonstrated scene is better understood as part of the broader social catastrophe produced by Apocalypse unless a chapter explicitly states that Nero was directly inflicting madness upon their minds.

###### Calamity Manipulation
Calamity is one of the four primary aspects [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] associates with [[Nero]]'s Apocalypse.[3]
- Calamity Inducement: Nero can produce events characterized by widespread misfortune and catastrophic damage.
- Large-Scale Harm Generation: Her calamities are not limited to single-target attacks and can affect cities, civilizations or larger environments depending upon her output.
The novel has not explicitly established Nero as a literal personification or abstract embodiment of the independent Concept `<Calamity>`. Therefore Calamity Embodiment should not presently be listed as a separate confirmed ability.

###### Cataclysm Manipulation
Cataclysm is another major Aspect of Apocalypse.[3]
- Cataclysm Inducement: [[Nero]] can produce catastrophic environmental events of extreme scale.
- Multi-Disaster Synchronization: Geological, climatic and social catastrophes can manifest simultaneously rather than sequentially.[2]
- Civilization-Ending Cataclysm: The combined effects can reach the point where an organized civilization can no longer continue functioning.

###### Large-Scale Tragedy Manipulation
[[Nero]]'s Apocalypse also encompasses Large-Scale Tragedy.[3]
- Mass-Casualty Event Inducement: Conditions can be manipulated toward events involving enormous loss of life.
- Civilizational Tragedy: Nero's Concept is capable of turning disasters into events permanently altering or ending an entire society.
The precise metaphysical limits of this Aspect have not yet been fully demonstrated.

###### Passive Apocalypse Manifestation
[[Nero]] does not necessarily need to consciously issue an individual command for every disaster generated by her Concept.
- Passive Disaster Generation: If Nero releases Apocalypse without properly restraining it, disasters can begin manifesting automatically around her.[3]
- Environmental Instability: The surrounding region can become increasingly unstable simply from the Concept being allowed to influence Reality.
- Self-Propagating Catastrophe: Once sufficient conditions exist, one disaster can naturally generate the causes for additional disasters.
This passive effect is associated specifically with Nero releasing her Apocalypse Divinity. It should not be written as though every supernatural ability Nero possesses is permanently active at maximum power.

###### Resistance to Order Suppression
A sufficiently powerful Divine authority associated with Order can interfere with Apocalypse.[3]
However, [[Nero]]'s Eldritch nature makes complete suppression substantially more difficult than it would be against an ordinary native Concept.
- Order Resistance: Order-based powers can reduce or redirect the chaos produced by Nero's Apocalypse but do not necessarily erase every manifestation.[3]
- Foreign-Rule Persistence: Because Nero's Divinity possesses Eldritch characteristics, some effects can continue operating according to a supernatural framework different from the native one.[3]
- Conceptual Suppression Resistance: Counter-Concepts can oppose Nero without automatically nullifying her power completely.
This does not mean Nero is absolutely immune to Order. A sufficiently superior Being can still overpower or suppress her.

###### Planetary-Scale Apocalypse
During her Divinity awakening, [[Nero]] demonstrated the ability to induce an Apocalypse across an artificial recreation of Earth.[2]
- Planetary Disaster Propagation: Catastrophic events spread across the artificial planet rather than remaining localized.
- Planetary Civilization Collapse: The artificial civilization rapidly entered a state from which survival as an organized global society became impossible.
- Rapid Apocalypse Induction: The constructed Earth began approaching complete collapse in approximately thirty seconds.[2]
This feat should be described as planetary-scale Apocalypse generation rather than generic "Cosmic Destruction". Nero did not simply fire an attack that directly exploded a real planet; her Concept caused the artificial world to destroy itself through cascading catastrophic events.

###### Apocalyptic Energy Sphere
In her White Apocalypse Dragon state, [[Nero]] gathered dark Divine/Eldritch Energy inside her jaws before releasing her Apocalypse.[2]
- Apocalyptic Energy Concentration: Nero can gather her Divine Power into a concentrated sphere.
- Dragon-Mouth Casting: The sphere can be formed within her Dragon Form's mouth in a manner superficially resembling a Breath attack.
- Apocalypse Release: Releasing the gathered Power can act as a trigger or medium through which her Concept spreads over a large region.[2]

##### White Apocalypse Dragon Physiology
After fully accepting herself and awakening her Divinity, [[Nero]] manifested as the White Apocalypse Dragon.[2]
This form represents the fusion of her True Dragon existence, Divine Concept and Eldritch nature.

###### Blood Dragon Physiology
- True Dragon Physiology: [[Nero]] possesses a complete True Dragon body and Soul rather than merely possessing Draconic traits.
- Immense Strength: Her physical strength greatly exceeds what she possessed as a Vampire or Hybrid.
- Immense Speed and Reflexes: Nero possesses the extremely high physical speed and reaction ability expected of a developed Blood Dragon.
- Immense Durability: Her scales, muscles, bones and supernatural body possess extraordinary resistance to physical damage.
- Enhanced Agility: Despite her size and strength, Nero can perform extremely complex movements during close combat.
- Enhanced Stamina: Her True Dragon body can sustain prolonged high-level combat.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Nero possesses the exceptional regeneration available to [[Blood Dragons]].
- [[Immortality]] and Longevity: Nero possesses the supernatural longevity inherited from Vampire, Dragon and Divine existence.
- Enhanced Senses: Her senses are vastly beyond Human levels and further enhanced through her previous Vampire/Werewolf heritage.

###### True Dragon Soul
[[Nero]] possesses a complete True Dragon Soul.
- Draconic Spiritual Foundation: Her current Soul is compatible with the immense power required by a True Dragon existence.
- High Soul Durability: The spiritual durability of a genuine Dragon is vastly greater than that of her former unstable Hybrid body.
- Racial Integration: Nero's old racial characteristics can coexist inside the superior Draconic foundation rather than damaging one another.

###### White Apocalypse Dragon Form
When [[Nero]] manifested her mature Divine identity, she transformed into an enormous white Dragon.[2]
- Huge Size: Nero's full Draconic body is vastly larger than her humanoid form.
- White Draconic Scales
- Gigantic Wings
- Draconic Horns
- Draconic Claws
- Draconic Fangs
- Flight: Nero can fly naturally through her Dragon wings.
- Draconic Aura: Her complete Dragon Form radiates immense supernatural pressure.
- Eldritch-Draconic Aura: During the awakening of Apocalypse, white Dragon Energy and dark [[Eldritch Energy]] simultaneously surrounded her.[2]

###### Dragon Breath
As a True Dragon, [[Nero]] possesses the natural foundation necessary for Dragon Breath.
Her demonstrated Apocalypse release in Chapter 1018 uses an Energy sphere formed within her Dragon mouth before its release.[2]
- Energy Breath / Discharge
- Large-Scale Energy Projection
- Apocalypse-Infused Draconic Attack
The full elemental nature of Nero's ordinary non-Apocalypse Dragon Breath has not yet been described in enough detail to assign a specific conventional Element.

###### Shapeshifting
[[Nero]] retains and vastly expands the Shapeshifting abilities she demonstrated as a Vampire.
- Humanoid Form: Nero can maintain a Human-like appearance despite being a gigantic True Dragon.
- Partial Transformation: Draconic characteristics can be manifested without entering her complete Dragon Form.
- Full Dragon Transformation: Nero can fully manifest her White Apocalypse Dragon body.

###### Draconic Language and Runes
Full [[True Dragons]] possess an instinctive relationship with Draconic Language.
- Draconic Language: [[Nero]]'s True Dragon Soul naturally understands the supernatural language of Dragons.
- Draconic Rune Affinity: She possesses the racial foundation necessary to use Draconic Runes.
- Rune Comprehension: Draconic Rune structures come considerably more naturally to Nero than to Beings without a complete True Dragon Soul.
This does not mean that Nero automatically possesses Rune mastery equal to [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] or ancient Rune specialists. Natural language affinity and technical Rune mastery are separate matters.

###### Creation Affinity
[[True Dragons]] possess an exceptionally close relationship with Creation.
- Creation Compatibility: [[Nero]]'s Draconic body can safely interact with multiple supernatural systems and Energies.
- Racial Adaptation: The Blood Dragon foundation incorporated characteristics that had previously been incompatible within her Hybrid existence.
- Power Integration: Vampiric, Werewolf, Divine and Eldritch characteristics can coexist within Nero without producing the severe biological instability of her childhood.
- Ability Evolution: Powers originating from older racial stages can become stronger or develop new expressions after integration into the True Dragon foundation.

##### God Physiology
[[Nero]] became a genuine Goddess through the awakening of Apocalypse.
- Divine Existence: Nero possesses a genuine conceptual Divine nature.
- [[Divine Energy]] Manipulation: She can generate and manipulate Divine Power.
- Divine Aura: Nero possesses the supernatural pressure of a high-ranking God.
- Conceptual Authority: Her relationship with Apocalypse is conceptual rather than merely elemental.
- Divine Soul: Nero's Soul possesses the supernatural stability and conceptual structure associated with Godhood.
Before awakening her own true Divinity, Nero had already reached the general combat level of a Low-Level God through her accumulated racial power and training.[6]

##### Integrated Vampire Heritage
[[Nero]]'s Noble Vampire abilities should not be treated as completely lost Former Powers.
Her later Blood Dragon existence incorporated her previous supernatural characteristics.

- Partial Bestial Transformation: She can alter specific parts of her anatomy without completely abandoning humanoid form.
Her unusually early proficiency demonstrates exceptional adaptability, although it should not automatically be described as mastery equal to every thousand-year-old Vampire specialist.

###### Claws

- Dispersed Movement

##### Original Hybrid Characteristics

Whether this exact biological property continues unchanged after Nero's repeated racial evolutions has not yet been explicitly demonstrated, so it is best kept under her original Hybrid characteristics rather than automatically treated as an active present-day Blood Dragon ability.

Most of these problems were eliminated when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] transformed Nero into a complete Vampire and were later rendered even less relevant by her evolution into a Blood Dragon.

##### Blessings
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] personally granted [[Nero]] complementary Divine Blessings after becoming a God.[8]

###### Wisdom of War
[[Nero]] received a Blessing corresponding to the Wisdom aspect of <War>.[8]

[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] also granted [[Nero]] the Peace aspect of <Nature>.[8]
- Emotional Stabilization: The Blessing helps balance Nero's aggressive impulses.

- Nature Affinity Reinforcement: The Blessing complements the natural relationship with Nature inherited from her Werewolf side.
Victor intentionally paired Peace of Nature with Wisdom of War so that increased affinity toward conflict would not cause Nero's naturally aggressive personality to become uncontrollable.[8]

##### Martial Arts
[[Nero]] became a highly experienced martial combatant after thousands of years of training.
- Advanced [[Martial Arts]]: Nero regularly trains directly with Ophis using the martial system developed through [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] and the combat foundations inherited from [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]].[9]
- Hand-to-Hand Combat: Nero can engage another Dragon Goddess in extremely high-speed close combat.
- Aerial Combat: Her Draconic physiology allows martial techniques to be used while airborne.
- Draconic Martial Integration: Nero incorporates True Dragon strength and transformations into physical technique.
- Combat Adaptability: She can react to Ophis changing tactics rather than relying entirely on memorized patterns.
- High-Speed Combat Perception: Nero can follow attacks and movements occurring at speeds difficult for weaker observers to visually perceive.[9]

##### Marksmanship
[[Nero]] demonstrated exceptional firearms proficiency while still a young Hybrid.
- Expert Marksmanship: Nero could accurately use large-caliber handguns against supernatural opponents.
- Dual-Wielding: She is proficient in simultaneously using two Desert Eagle-style firearms.
- Moving Fire: Nero can shoot accurately while repositioning, retreating or fighting under pressure.
- Supernatural Targeting: She learned to target vulnerabilities belonging to Vampires, [[Werewolves]] and Yokai rather than wasting ammunition against highly durable areas.
- Energy Firearm Mastery: After receiving the [[Black Deagles]], Nero learned to regulate the amount of supernatural Energy supplied to each weapon.[8]

[[Nero]] survived as a fugitive while still a child and while possessing a biologically defective body.
- Exceptional Survival Instincts: Nero remained alive despite being hunted by multiple supernatural organizations.
- Situational Awareness: She constantly evaluates escape paths, threats and changes in an opponent's behavior.
- Resourcefulness: Nero uses whatever equipment or environmental advantage is available rather than depending exclusively upon supernatural power.
- Improvised Combat: During the Japanese incident she fought several supernatural species while badly injured and with limited resources.[10]
- Tenacity: Nero continued fighting even after sustaining injuries severe enough that abandoning Ophis would have been the rational survival choice.[11][12][10]

[[Nero]] developed a highly practical form of intelligence through survival and later military education.
- Combat Analysis
- Weakness Exploitation
- Threat Prioritization
- Battlefield Adaptation
- Ambush Awareness

[[Nero]] received direct education concerning the nature of Godhood from [[Jeanne Elderblood]].[13][14]
- Conceptual Understanding: Nero understands that a genuine Divinity requires comprehension rather than merely obtaining Power.
- Self-Recognition: Her Apocalypse only fully awakened after Nero confronted and accepted the weakness that had defined her childhood.[2]
- Identity-Based Divinity: Apocalypse represents Nero's personal relationship with destruction, helplessness and the catastrophic consequences produced when the strong determine the fate of the weak.

- Imperial Guard Command: Nero possesses command authority over one of the [[Elderblood Empire]]'s principal elite Legions.

- Joint Command: Nero shares primary leadership with Ophis, while [[Rose Adrasteia Elderblood]] can assume command when necessary.[15]
- Military Education: Nero possesses the training necessary to direct elite supernatural soldiers rather than functioning solely as an individual fighter.

#### Equipment

##### Original Desert Eagles
[[Nero]] originally fought using a pair of Desert Eagle handguns.
The weapons were used extensively during the events in Japan and were severely damaged during her battles against the supernatural factions pursuing Ophis and herself.[10]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later arranged for the weapons to be repaired and improved through supernatural craftsmanship.
- Energy Ammunition: The repaired guns no longer depend entirely upon conventional physical ammunition.
- Energy Projection: They can fire supernatural Energy-based shots.
- Improved Durability: Their rebuilt structure is more suitable for combat involving supernatural Beings.
These weapons were later surpassed by the [[Black Deagles]] Victor personally commissioned for Nero.

##### Black Deagles
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later gifted [[Nero]] a new pair of black Desert Eagle-style Divine weapons created through cooperation between Victor and [[Hephaestus]].[8]
The weapons are extremely high-grade Divine firearms specifically designed around Nero's fighting style.
- Divine Artifact Physiology: The [[Black Deagles]] are genuine Divine weapons rather than conventional firearms.
- Energy-Based Ammunition: They generate projectiles using Nero's supernatural Energy rather than conventional ammunition.[8]
- Effectively Unlimited Ammunition: As long as Nero possesses sufficient Energy, she does not need to reload them with ordinary cartridges.
- Variable Energy Output: Nero can control how much Energy is supplied to an individual shot.[8]

###### Rapid-Fire Mode
By reducing the amount of Energy condensed into each projectile:
- the [[Black Deagles]] can fire numerous Energy bullets in rapid succession;
- the firing pattern can resemble a supernatural machine gun;
- recoil and Energy consumption per individual shot are reduced.

###### Concentrated Beam Mode
By increasing the amount of Energy supplied to a shot:
- the weapon can release a vastly more powerful concentrated Energy blast;
- the projectile can resemble a beam rather than an ordinary bullet;
- recoil increases dramatically with output.[8]
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] demonstrated the high-output setting, [[Nero]] needed assistance simply to avoid being thrown backward by the recoil.

###### Energy Regulation
- Output Adjustment: [[Nero]] can choose between quantity and destructive power.
- User-Energy Scaling: The weapons become more powerful as their wielder gains greater Energy reserves and control.
- Initial Safety Limiter: [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] initially instructed Nero to use only approximately 10% of their maximum intended output because she lacked the strength and control necessary to use the full setting safely.[8]
Nero has since become dramatically stronger than she was when the restriction was originally imposed, although the novel has not provided a later exact percentage representing her current maximum output.
