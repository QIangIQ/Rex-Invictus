Ariel Elderblood, formerly known simply as Ariel, is one of the oldest and highest-ranking former Angels of the [[Biblical Pantheon]], the former Heavenly Virtue representing Generosity, former Commander of Heaven's Angelic armies and a current Blood Dragon Goddess married to [[Victor Elderblood]]. Created directly by the Heavenly Father, Ariel spent eons serving as one of Heaven's principal military, political and administrative authorities. Even before becoming a True Dragon she stood above ordinary Seraphim because the Seven Heavenly Virtues possessed genuine conceptual authority corresponding to the Virtues they represented.[2][7]
Ariel's original defining Concept was Generosity. After the deaths of Michael and [[Gabriel]] during the Demonic War, her age, competence and military experience caused her to assume operational control over Heaven's surviving forces, and by Chapter 873 she formally occupied Michael's former position as the Commander of the Angels.[8][1]
Her later transformation fundamentally changed her existence without erasing her Heavenly heritage. Ariel became a genuine True Dragon of Victor's Blood Dragon lineage while preserving her Holy nature and established Divine Concepts. Her principal current Divinities include Generosity and Light, while a dormant seed of Love placed within her Soul by the Heavenly Father awakened after Ariel personally learned the meaning of romantic love during approximately 1,500 subjective years of development with Victor.[9][10]
Ariel is the biological mother of [[Ariella Victoria Elderblood]]. Although Chapter 1102 originally presented Ariel's pregnancy as a twin pregnancy, Chapter 1177 later clarified that there was never a second living daughter. Two Soul-like presences were detected during gestation, but they eventually merged. The apparent "second Soul" was actually the power of Creation preparing Ariella as its compatible host. Ariella was consequently born with the Concept of Creation and inherited the potential to also develop the Concept of Life.[6]

### Physical Description

#### Angel Form
Ariel originally appeared as an exceptionally beautiful Angel approximately 190 centimeters tall, with fair white skin, green eyes, long white hair containing golden tones and six enormous white wings.[2]
She generally wore white-and-gold Divine armor appropriate to her position as one of Heaven's highest-ranking military authorities. Her appearance embodied the idealized supernatural beauty of the [[Heavenly Father]]'s high-order creations, while the six wings visibly identified her as a Seraph.
Ariel's wings could also reflect instability in her Angelic state. When accumulated frustration, envy and suppressed emotions pushed her dangerously close to becoming a Fallen Angel, portions of her wings began shifting between white and black.[11][12]

#### Blood Dragon Goddess
Following her transformation into a Blood Dragon Goddess and her long subjective development within [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family, Ariel became noticeably more mature and powerful.
Her hair grew dramatically longer, eventually reaching approximately her ankles, and its earlier white-golden coloration became increasingly golden. Her green eyes remained recognizable, and Chapter 1149 describes her in the mature Imperial era as a long-blonde-haired, green-eyed woman wearing Divine armor.[9][5]
Ariel now possesses the complete racial foundation of a True Dragon. Her Heavenly characteristics were not discarded by the transformation; they were incorporated into the far more adaptable True Dragon existence, allowing her Holy powers and Divine Concepts to continue developing instead of being replaced.
Her complete Dragon Form has not yet received a sufficiently detailed physical description to establish its exact size, coloration or anatomical features.

### Personality and Traits
Ariel is disciplined, compassionate, dutiful, rational and naturally suited for leadership. She spent eons commanding beings whose lives depended upon her decisions and consequently developed considerably more restraint than many of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s more impulsive wives. Even [[Aphrodite]] Elderblood regarded Ariel's comparatively balanced disposition as valuable within a family already filled with extremely aggressive personalities.[12]
Her disciplined exterior originally made her appear almost impossibly proper. Ariel rarely allowed personal anger to interfere with her duties and was willing to prioritize innocent lives even when those same populations had failed her. During her debut, after an infiltrator among the Human forces nearly killed her with Miasma-contaminated ammunition, Ariel still ordered her subordinates not to indiscriminately massacre the surrounding Humans.[13]
Her loyalty is equally genuine. When Victor's wives temporarily freed Ariel from the [[Heavenly Father]]'s influence and considered demanding Heaven's Creation Tree in exchange for abandoning the marriage arrangement, Ariel immediately refused. She believed her Creator had effectively discarded her into a political marriage and was furious with him, yet still refused to sacrifice an irreplaceable resource belonging to Heaven merely for her own freedom.[14]
This convinced Victor's wives that Ariel's devotion to Heaven was not simply artificial obedience.
Ariel's outward perfection nevertheless concealed enormous suppressed frustration. Angels created by the Heavenly Father possessed genuine emotions and personalities, but their fundamental nature strongly encouraged obedience toward their Creator, while sufficiently severe rejection could lead to a Fall. Ariel therefore spent thousands of years possessing grievances she could not safely express.[7][14]
When that restriction was temporarily removed, Ariel changed almost immediately. She insulted the Heavenly Father, cursed him in Ancient Hebrew and Latin, complained about the immense responsibilities created by [[Lucifer]] and Lilith's ancient conflict and openly challenged Lilith. Ruby, Violet and the others considered this much more expressive Ariel more authentic than the flawless Heavenly commander they had initially encountered.[14]
Her hatred toward [[Lilith Elderblood]] was particularly complicated. Ariel blamed the ancient rebellion and its consequences for enormous amounts of work, death and conflict later Angels had been forced to inherit. Seeing Lilith genuinely happy, free and surrounded by family therefore generated not only resentment but intense envy. Ariel realized that one of Heaven's ancient enemies possessed the personal life Ariel herself had never been permitted to develop.[11]
Their later multi-week confrontation became cathartic rather than an attempt at mutual murder. After much of their resentment had been exhausted, the old hatred became considerably less important than the family they eventually shared.[15]
Ariel is also highly perceptive and politically experienced. She quickly reads hierarchies, interpersonal tensions and military danger, and unlike many Gods she understands when pride should be separated from practical survival. This becomes particularly visible in the mature Imperial era when she personally returns to Heaven carrying Elderblood research intended to ensure the [[Biblical Pantheon]] remains capable of surviving the coming Upper-Sector war.[5]
Her relationship with Victor forced Ariel to reconsider her understanding of romantic love. Heaven taught Love as an abstract ideal, but Ariel had virtually no experience with the intimate, possessive and deeply personal form of love common inside Victor's family. Victor refused to equate attraction, Divine compatibility or political marriage with genuine affection. He instead defined love through care, worry, trust, endurance and acceptance, then directly told Ariel that neither of them truly loved the other yet.[16]
Rather than being offended, Ariel used that honesty as the foundation for a real relationship. She ultimately chose Victor herself instead of simply carrying out the alliance arranged by the Heavenly Father.
Motherhood further changed Ariel. She is affectionate toward Ariella but significantly more disciplined than Victor, frequently correcting their daughter's bluntness and destructive impulses. At the same time, Ariel refuses to repeat the restrictions that defined her own upbringing: when the Heavenly Father considered Ariella talented enough to become his successor, Ariel immediately stated that her daughter would choose her own future.[5]
By the later Imperial era, Ariel had become a fully active member of the Elderblood household. Although considerably kinder than many of Victor's wives, her kindness does not extend to allowing outsiders to threaten the family. Even the more compassionate members of the household are prepared to retaliate when one of Victor's children is targeted.[6]

### History

#### Creation and Heaven
Ariel was created directly by the [[Heavenly Father]] as one of Heaven's highest-order Angels.
Across the immense history of the [[Biblical Pantheon]], she became one of the Seven Heavenly Virtues and represented the Concept of Generosity.[2]
The Seven Virtues existed above ordinary Seraphim because each carried genuine conceptual authority. They therefore approached the nature of Gods despite remaining within Heaven's Angelic hierarchy.
Ariel eventually became one of the Heavenly Father's principal military leaders. Her responsibilities included commanding Angelic armies, protecting Heaven's interests in the Human World and coordinating with allied Human organizations such as the Inquisition.

### Synopsis

#### Demonic War
Ariel entered the main story during the worldwide conflict involving the Inquisition, Heaven and [[Diablo]]'s Demonic forces.
She was stationed at the Vatican battlefield and was already powerful enough to dramatically influence its strategic situation. [[Sitri]], despite commanding Demonic forces in the region, was forced into a defensive approach because directly overcoming one of the Seven Heavenly Virtues within heavily protected Holy territory was impractical.[2]
Ariel was accompanied by the Seraphim [[Zaniel]] and Daniel, who served as her direct subordinate guards.
[[Victor Elderblood]] eventually arrived and immediately disrupted both armies. His overwhelming presence caused Angels, Humans and Demons alike to focus upon him. Ariel reacted cautiously rather than recklessly attacking, recognizing that Victor had not actually shown hostility toward her forces and ordering the Angels to withdraw instead of provoking an unnecessary confrontation.[2]
The situation changed when an infiltrator among the Human defenders shot Ariel with specialized Hell Metal ammunition contaminated by Miasma. The substance was extremely dangerous to her Holy physiology and began corrupting her faster than the surrounding Angels could heal the injury.[13]
Ariel collapsed and began coughing golden blood.
Despite having been betrayed by someone among the Human forces, she explicitly prevented her Angels from retaliating indiscriminately against the surrounding Humans.
Victor eventually approached and offered assistance. He removed the Miasma by consuming the corruptive Energy directly from Ariel's body. The method required intimate physical contact and unintentionally resulted in Ariel losing her first kiss to Victor.[13]
Ariel recovered completely.
Her immediate response was embarrassment and anger rather than romantic attraction, and she declared that she did not want to see Victor again. Nevertheless, their first significant interaction had ended with Victor saving her life despite the fact that they belonged to separate factions.
The Demonic War later escalated considerably. Ariel fought major Demons including [[Baal]], while Heaven suffered catastrophic losses.[17]
The deaths of [[Gabriel]] and Michael created an enormous vacuum in Heaven's military hierarchy. Ariel assumed operational command over the surviving Angelic armies and helped reorganize Heaven's forces during the later stages of the war.[8]

#### King Among Kings
By Chapter 873, Ariel had formally inherited Michael's previous position as the Commander of the Angels.[1]
When [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] later initiated an international operation involving multiple major supernatural factions, the [[Heavenly Father]] sent two of Heaven's strongest representatives:
- [[Azrael]], Angel of Death;
- Ariel, Virtue of Generosity and Commander of the Angels.[1]
Ariel's participation reflected the dramatic improvement in relations between Heaven and Victor's growing faction. She increasingly encountered Victor as an allied ruler rather than merely the Vampire who had humiliated her during their first meeting.

#### Dragon God of Chaos
Ariel later acted as Heaven's guide when [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] arrived in the Seventh Heaven accompanied by [[Ophis Tepes Elderblood]], [[Nero Elderblood]] and [[Metis]] Elderblood.[18]
She escorted Victor through Heaven and remained one of the major intermediaries between the [[Biblical Pantheon]] and Dragons Nest.
The [[Heavenly Father]] eventually accepted Victor's invitation to visit the transformed Hell, with Ariel accompanying him.[19]
Hell was almost unrecognizable compared with the infernal civilization Ariel remembered. Victor's cities possessed enormous infrastructure, order, commerce and technology, forcing Ariel to confront the fact that the civilization of her historical enemies had advanced far beyond Heaven's traditional assumptions.
The person who affected Ariel most strongly was [[Lilith Elderblood]].
Ariel had spent eons viewing Lilith as one of Heaven's ancient enemies, yet Lilith now possessed enormous personal freedom, a loving family and a life detached from the endless Biblical conflict. Ariel's resentment turned inward as she realized how little freedom she herself possessed.
Her wings began fluctuating toward black and she moved dangerously close to becoming a Fallen Angel.[11]
The Heavenly Father recognized the instability and remained attentive to Ariel's condition.
By Chapter 1041, the future relationship between Heaven and Victor's Empire had become impossible to ignore. The Heavenly Father concluded that strengthening the alliance through family ties was strategically useful and eventually selected Ariel as a potential marriage partner for Victor.[7]
Ariel was abruptly transported into Dragons Nest and found herself surrounded by more than thirty of Victor's wives.[12]
The situation terrified her more than most conventional battlefields, but she retained sufficient composure to study the household and understand that its apparent chaos concealed a functional hierarchy.
The wives evaluated Ariel both as an individual and as a potential family member. Her combat experience, temperament, loyalty and compatibility with Victor were examined carefully. [[Aphrodite]] in particular considered Ariel's comparatively reasonable personality a valuable addition to a family already overflowing with extreme individuals.[12]
The most important test involved temporarily separating Ariel from the supernatural influence of her Creator and placing her under [[Anna Elderblood]]'s power so that she could not hide her genuine thoughts.[14]
For the first time in her existence, Ariel could directly insult the Heavenly Father without immediately risking a Fall.
She tested this freedom.
Nothing happened.
Thousands of years of frustration immediately erupted.
Ariel cursed the Heavenly Father for effectively "selling" her into a marriage alliance, complained about [[Lucifer]] and Lilith's ancient actions and released emotions that Heaven's social and supernatural structure had forced her to suppress for eons.[14]
The Elderblood women actually trusted her more afterward because they finally saw Ariel acting as an individual instead of as the apparently perfect representative of Heaven.
Ariel's loyalty to Heaven nevertheless remained genuine. When the possibility of demanding Heaven's Creation Tree instead of accepting Ariel was discussed, she refused even though doing so could have freed her from the marriage arrangement.[14]
Her attention then turned toward Lilith.
The two women fought beneath controlled conditions for several weeks of accelerated Time.[15]
The confrontation gradually became therapeutic. Once centuries of anger had been exhausted, much of their hatred lost its force, and Ariel began integrating more naturally into Victor's family.
By Chapter 1048, the arrangement had progressed enough for Ariel to be recognized as Victor's fiancée.[3]

#### God Emperor Victor Elderblood
Political acceptance did not automatically create romantic love.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] confronted this directly. Ariel was attracted to him and increasingly comfortable with his family, but Victor rejected the idea that attraction, political usefulness or supernatural compatibility automatically constituted love.
He told Ariel that real love required care, worry, trust, acceptance and endurance, then stated plainly that he did not yet truly love her and that she did not truly love him either.[16]
Instead of ending their relationship, the conversation removed the artificial expectations created by the [[Heavenly Father]]'s arrangement.
Ariel and Victor began developing a genuine personal relationship.
Ariel eventually chose Victor for herself and became his wife.[4]
She subsequently transformed into a Blood Dragon Goddess. Her previous Holy and Conceptual characteristics were incorporated into the superior compatibility of a True Dragon rather than erased.
Her Divinities continued evolving.
Generosity and Light became her principal established Divine Concepts, while the dormant Conceptual seed of Love that the Heavenly Father had placed within her Soul finally began awakening. The Concept could not simply be activated through theoretical knowledge; Ariel had to personally understand genuine Love. She eventually achieved that understanding after approximately 1,500 subjective years of development with Victor.[9][10]
Ariel and Victor then conceived what everyone initially believed were twins.[9]
The pregnancy was itself extraordinary. Beings at their level normally have extreme reproductive difficulty, and conception involved an enormous interaction between the parents' Souls. Ariel had already sustained exceptionally prolonged Soul contact with Victor, demonstrating unusual compatibility and resilience.
At the time, two distinct Soul-like presences appeared within Ariel, seemingly confirming that she was carrying two daughters.[9]
Later events would reveal that this interpretation was incomplete.

#### Those Who Rule Reality
By the mature Imperial era, Ariel had fully integrated into the Elderblood family while retaining affection and responsibility toward Heaven.
She travelled to the Seventh Heaven with her daughter [[Ariella Victoria Elderblood]] to visit the [[Heavenly Father]].[5]
Ariella strongly resembled Ariel facially, while inheriting [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s black hair and violet eyes. She had also inherited the entire collection of Heaven's Seven Virtues—Chastity, Charity, Temperance, Diligence, Patience, Benevolence and Humility—as genuine Divine Concepts.[5]
Despite this inheritance, Ariella's personality was far less restrained than Ariel's. The girl quickly grew bored of Heaven's endless clouds and even considered using Dragon Breath simply to make the scenery more interesting. Ariel repeatedly attempted to teach her patience and proper manners while privately blaming Victor for excessively spoiling his daughters.[5]
Their meeting with the Heavenly Father also showed how Ariel's relationship with her Creator had changed. Instead of behaving as an absolutely obedient subordinate, Ariel interacted with him naturally as a daughter. The Heavenly Father himself recognized that Ariel had become substantially stronger since their previous meeting.[5]
When Ariella bluntly asked why her grandfather seemed so weak compared with Victor, Ariel immediately reprimanded her.
The Heavenly Father instead found the girl's innocence amusing and demonstrated his specialization in Creation. Ariella became fascinated by his near-perfect Energy efficiency, but an argument quickly began when he claimed that even Victor could not surpass him as a Creator. Ariel immediately recognized the approaching disaster and attempted to signal her father to stop arguing with the extremely father-loyal child.[5]
Ariella later discovered Heaven's Celestial System and began modifying it. Ariel initially feared that her daughter might damage the entire Realm, forcing the Heavenly Father to reassure her that he had isolated the System before allowing Ariella to experiment.
Ariella ultimately rewrote enormous portions of its underlying source structure and produced a substantially superior version.
The Heavenly Father became impressed enough to consider Ariella a possible successor.
Ariel immediately established that the decision would belong to Ariella herself:
“It all depends on her. We don't forbid her anything. She's forging her own path.”[5]
The statement demonstrated how fundamentally Ariel's approach to parenthood differed from her own upbringing. Having spent eons constrained by the expectations and supernatural influence of her Creator, Ariel refused to impose an equivalent predetermined destiny upon her daughter.
Ariella's visit was only one purpose of Ariel's journey.
Ariel had also returned as an Imperial representative.
She raised her hand and manifested a golden sphere containing knowledge accumulated through [[Velnorah Xyphora Elderblood Thaloria II]], [[Ruby Elderblood Scarlett]], [[Aline Elderblood Valefar]] and Victor concerning Angel biology, methods of making Angels more Divine and additional Creator-related resources Victor had made available to Heaven.[5]
The information was intended to accelerate the [[Biblical Pantheon]]'s development before the coming Upper-Sector war.
Ariel explained the standards the Empire expected from the conflict. Combatants classified around CODE: V-03 / Continental Level could function merely as ordinary enemy foot soldiers, while advanced hostile vessels could destroy complete Solar Systems by attacking stars directly.[5]
The scale disturbed both Ariel and the Heavenly Father. Ariel regarded the casual destruction of inhabited Solar Systems and potentially entire galaxies with clear disgust because Life remained extremely precious to her despite her transformation into a Dragon Goddess.
She warned the Heavenly Father to remember the scale of these enemies when creating future generations of Angels.
Victor had also secretly assigned Imperial Guards and other supernatural subordinates to protect Ariel and Ariella during their journey despite both women already possessing immense personal power.[20]
Ariel continued participating actively in the Elderblood household during the later Imperial era. She was counted among the mothers who genuinely participated in raising their daughters and maintaining family life, contrasting with more neglectful members of the household.[21]
Later events finally clarified the mystery surrounding Ariella's supposed twin.
Victor reflected upon the pregnancies of both Ariel and [[Hela Elderblood]]. In both cases, the family had detected two Soul-like presences and logically assumed twins. As each pregnancy progressed, however, the two presences merged into one and the remaining child's power became significantly stronger.[6]
Further investigation established that the secondary presence within Ariel had never been another living child.
It was Creation itself preparing a compatible host.[6]
Ariella was therefore born with the Concept of Creation and additionally inherited the potential to develop the Concept of Life.[6]
The old assumption that Ariel gave birth to one daughter while an unnamed twin remained unaccounted for is therefore obsolete.
Ariel and Victor have one confirmed biological daughter from that pregnancy:
Ariella Victoria Elderblood.

### Relationships

#### Victor Elderblood
Ariel and [[Victor Elderblood]] first met as members of different supernatural factions during the Demonic War.
Their first significant personal interaction occurred when Ariel was critically wounded by Miasma. Victor saved her by removing the corruptive Energy directly from her body, accidentally taking Ariel's first kiss during the process.[13]
Ariel was furious and humiliated but could not deny that Victor had saved her life.
Their relationship later became increasingly political as cooperation between Heaven and Dragons Nest expanded.
The [[Heavenly Father]]'s attempt to formalize that alliance through marriage initially angered Ariel because she had not chosen the arrangement herself.[12][14]
Victor ultimately removed the principal problem by refusing to treat political marriage as proof of love.
He openly told Ariel that neither of them truly loved the other yet and explained that genuine love required knowing and accepting another person beyond convenient traits or physical attraction.[16]
Their relationship subsequently developed voluntarily.
Ariel eventually became Victor's wife, entered his Blood Dragon lineage and developed a genuine Love Divinity through their relationship.[4][9]
They later became the parents of [[Ariella Victoria Elderblood]].[5][6]

#### Heavenly Father
The [[Heavenly Father]] is Ariel's Creator and father.
For most of her existence, Ariel served him as both daughter and soldier. Angelic nature made complete rejection of his authority extremely difficult, but her obedience should not be mistaken for the absence of personal thought.
When the Elderblood women temporarily removed his influence from Ariel, she immediately exposed thousands of years of suppressed frustration.[14]
She condemned him for using her in a political alliance, insulted him repeatedly and vented grievances she had never previously been able to express without threatening her Angelic nature.
The experience ultimately improved their relationship.
Ariel did not stop loving him, and even while furious she refused to sacrifice Heaven's Creation Tree for her own freedom.[14]
By Chapter 1149, they were capable of interacting naturally as father and daughter. Ariel visited him voluntarily, brought Ariella to meet him, transferred Imperial research intended to strengthen Heaven and openly discussed the future of both civilizations.[5]
Their relationship had therefore evolved from absolute Creator-subordinate obedience into a much healthier familial and political bond.

#### Lilith Elderblood
[[Lilith Elderblood]] was one of Ariel's oldest enemies.
Ariel blamed Lilith and [[Lucifer]]'s ancient conflict with Heaven for creating thousands of years of problems later Angels were forced to manage. Her resentment intensified after seeing Lilith enjoying the freedom and family Ariel herself lacked.[11]
Once Ariel gained unrestricted freedom to express her feelings, she challenged Lilith directly.
Their fight lasted several weeks of accelerated Time.[15]
The confrontation gradually exhausted their old resentment and ultimately allowed both women to move beyond much of the institutional hatred between Angels and Demons.
They later became fellow members of [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s family.

#### Michael and Gabriel
Michael and [[Gabriel]] were among Ariel's most important Angelic siblings and senior members of Heaven.
Their deaths during the Demonic War caused a major collapse in the Angelic command structure.
Ariel assumed responsibility for the remaining forces and eventually inherited Michael's former office as Commander of the Angels.[8][1]

#### Azrael
[[Azrael]] is another high-ranking Angelic sibling of Ariel and one of Heaven's strongest surviving warriors.
Ariel's strengths traditionally centered more heavily upon leadership, conceptual authority and military command, while Azrael was particularly associated with direct combat and execution.
The two later represented Heaven together during [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s multinational operation in Chapter 873.[1]

#### Ariella Victoria Elderblood
[[Ariella Victoria Elderblood]] is Ariel and [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s biological daughter.[5][6]
Ariella resembles Ariel strongly in facial appearance but inherited Victor's black hair and violet eyes.[5]
Through Ariel she inherited all Seven Heavenly Virtues as Divine Concepts.
Chapter 1177 further clarifies that Ariella was born with the Concept of Creation and possesses the potential to develop Life.[6]
Ariel is affectionate but comparatively strict with her daughter. She corrects Ariella's impatience, destructive impulses and blunt speech while still allowing her enormous personal freedom.
When the Heavenly Father considered Ariella capable of becoming his eventual successor, Ariel refused to make that choice for her. She and Victor intend to provide their daughter with opportunities rather than assign her a predetermined destiny.[5]

The family later discovered that the second presence was not another living Soul.
It was Creation itself preparing Ariella as its host.[6]

#### Skills and Abilities
Ariel Elderblood is one of the most powerful beings ever produced by the [[Biblical Pantheon]]. Even before becoming a genuine Goddess and Blood Dragon, she occupied the highest levels of the Angelic hierarchy as one of the Seven Heavenly Virtues. The Virtues are concept-bearing Seraphim whose authority makes them considerably closer to conventional Gods than ordinary Angels.[1]
Ariel's original Concept was Generosity. She also possessed extremely advanced Angelic Light and [[Holy Energy]], and her long position as one of Heaven's principal military leaders gave her enormous combat and command experience.
Her later evolution inside Clan Elderblood fundamentally increased this foundation. Ariel became a genuine True Dragon Goddess, with her former Angelic powers integrated into the vastly more powerful physiology of a Blood Dragon. Her current major Divine authorities are Generosity and Light, while Love exists as a lesser but developing Divinity that awakened as Ariel personally came to understand the Concept.[2][3]

##### Blood Dragon Goddess Physiology
Blood Dragon Goddess Physiology: Ariel's current Race is that of a genuine Blood Dragon. Her Angelic foundation, Holy attributes and Conceptual authorities were preserved and integrated into her new Draconic existence rather than erased.
- Immense Strength: Ariel possesses the enormous physical power naturally associated with [[True Dragons]].
- Immense Speed and Reflexes: Her current Draconic physiology vastly surpasses the physical capabilities of her old Seraphim body.
- Immense Durability: Ariel can withstand quantities of supernatural force that would destroy ordinary Angels.
- Enhanced Agility: Her Dragon physiology retains the exceptional aerial and martial coordination developed during eons of Angelic warfare.
- Enhanced Senses: Ariel combines high-level Divine perception with the supernatural sensory abilities of a True Dragon.
- Regenerative Healing Factor: Blood Dragon physiology grants Ariel advanced regeneration superior to that of her former Angelic body.
- [[Immortality]] and Longevity: Ariel was already effectively ageless as an Angel and remains free from ordinary aging as a Dragon Goddess.
- True Dragon Form: Ariel possesses a genuine complete Draconic body rather than merely producing Dragon-like characteristics.
- Shapeshifting and Metamorphosis: She can maintain a humanoid appearance despite her current True Dragon Race.
- Size Alteration: Ariel's complete Dragon manifestation is not restricted to the proportions of her humanoid body.
- Flight: Ariel possessed supernatural flight through six Angelic wings before becoming a Dragon and currently possesses Draconic aerial capability as well.
- Dragon Breath: Like other True Dragons, Ariel possesses the biological capacity to release supernatural Energy through a Draconic Breath. A specialized individual Breath unique to Ariel has not yet been described in sufficient detail.
- [[Dragon Heart]]: Her True Dragon physiology generates and circulates vastly greater quantities of Energy than her former Angelic body.
- True Dragon Soul: Ariel's current Soul possesses Draconic qualities while preserving the Conceptual foundation she originally developed as a Heavenly Virtue.

###### Draconic Language and Runes
As a True Dragon, Ariel possesses natural compatibility with the Draconic Language and Runes used by Dragonkind.
This should not automatically be treated as technical mastery equivalent to specialists such as [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]], [[Dun Scaith Elderblood]], [[Zaladrac Zeovnur Elderblood]] or Natalia Elderblood Alioth.
Natural Draconic comprehension and dedicated Runic expertise remain separate skills.

##### Former Heavenly Virtue Physiology
Before becoming a Blood Dragon, Ariel was one of the Seven Heavenly Virtues and therefore one of the highest-ranking Seraphim created by the [[Heavenly Father]].
The Seven Virtues possess conceptual characteristics that distinguish them from ordinary Angels.[1]
- High-Level Angel Physiology: Ariel possessed physical power, speed, durability and regeneration vastly beyond lesser Angels.
- Six Wings: Ariel manifested six white Angelic wings.
- [[Holy Energy]]: As a Seraphim, she naturally generated and manipulated extremely high-quality Holy Energy.
- Holy Light: Ariel possessed a powerful affinity with supernatural Light capable of harming beings naturally opposed to Heaven.
- Angelic [[Immortality]]: Ariel did not age conventionally.
- Angelic Regeneration: Ordinary wounds could be restored through Angelic Energy, although sufficiently corruptive effects such as concentrated Miasma could interfere with this process.[4]
- Angelic Armor Manifestation: Ariel can manifest her characteristic white-and-gold Divine armor directly around her body.[5]
- Conceptual Authority: Even before becoming a conventional Goddess, Ariel's position as a Virtue granted her genuine authority connected to Generosity.[6]
Ariel's Angelic Race is no longer her primary current physiology, but the Holy and Conceptual characteristics developed during that existence remain incorporated into her Blood Dragon state.

##### Divinities
Ariel currently possesses three principal Divine Concepts:
Greater/Major Divinities:
- Generosity
- Light
Lesser/Developing Divinity:
- Love[3]

##### Generosity Divinity
Generosity is the Concept Ariel represented throughout her existence as one of the Seven Heavenly Virtues.
Her relationship with the Concept predates her conventional Godhood. As a Heavenly Virtue, Ariel already possessed sufficient Conceptual authority to be considered considerably closer to a God than an ordinary Angel.[1][6]
Following her evolution into a Blood Dragon Goddess, Generosity became one of her genuine major Divinities.[3]

###### Generosity Authority
Ariel possesses supernatural authority over the conceptual principle of Generosity.
The exact full extent of this Divinity has not yet been comprehensively demonstrated, and it should therefore not be expanded into unrestricted transfer of every property, ability or abstract quality without direct evidence.

###### Intent and Disposition Perception
Ariel possesses an unusual capacity to read another individual's intentions extremely quickly.
During her debut she could assess [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s behavior, intent and the danger represented by his actions while commanding the Vatican battlefield.[1]
This ability is associated with her status as a Heavenly Virtue and enormous experience rather than demonstrated literal omniscience.
- Intent Detection: Ariel can rapidly distinguish overt hostility from neutrality.
- Emotional/Behavioral Reading: She can interpret subtle changes in another being's attitude.
- Battlefield Assessment: The ability helps Ariel determine whether engaging another supernatural power would be strategically justified.

##### Light Divinity
Light is one of Ariel's current major Divine Concepts.[3]
Ariel possessed extraordinary Light and [[Holy Energy]] manipulation even before becoming a Goddess because of her Seraphim nature. Dragon evolution greatly strengthened these qualities and elevated them into a true Divine authority.

###### Light Manipulation
Ariel can generate, shape and weaponize supernatural Light.
- Light Generation: Ariel can create intense Divine/Holy Light from her own Energy.
- Light Projection: The Light can be launched at distant opponents.
- Light Constructs: Ariel can shape Light into functional weapons.
- Holy-Light Damage: Her attacks are naturally extremely effective against many Demonic beings.
- Divine-Light Enhancement: Her current Godhood allows Light to be reinforced through genuine Divinity rather than functioning solely as Angelic [[Holy Energy]].

###### Spear of Light
Ariel can form concentrated Light into a supernatural spear and launch it at enemies.[7]
The technique possessed sufficient power during the Demonic War to kill high-ranking Demonic combatants.
- Light Weapon Creation: Ariel constructs the spear directly from Light.
- Ranged Piercing Attack: The weapon can be thrown across the battlefield.
- Anti-Demonic Effectiveness: Holy Light gives the attack particular effectiveness against Demons.
The [[Spears|Spear]] of Light predates Ariel's Blood Dragon transformation and should be considerably stronger in her current state, although its exact modern output has not been quantified.

##### Holy Energy Manipulation
As one of Heaven's highest Angels, Ariel possessed enormous quantities of [[Holy Energy]].
- Holy Energy Generation: Ariel naturally generated Holy Energy as part of her Angelic existence.
- Holy Energy Projection: She can release Holy Energy offensively.
- Weapon Reinforcement: Holy Energy can strengthen manifested weapons and Angelic equipment.
- Anti-Demonic Properties: Holy Energy directly opposes many Demonic Energies and is particularly effective against conventional Demons.
- Holy Aura: Ariel's presence naturally possessed the sanctified characteristics expected from one of Heaven's highest Seraphim.
Her current Draconic state retains this Holy foundation while no longer being limited by ordinary Angel physiology.

##### Love Divinity
Ariel later developed a lesser Divine Concept associated with Love.[2][3]
Unlike Generosity, which Ariel had represented for eons, Ariel did not originally understand Love through personal experience.
The [[Heavenly Father]] had placed a seed related to the Concept deep within her existence, but the Divinity could not properly develop merely because Ariel intellectually understood the definition of Love.[2]
It began blossoming only after Ariel personally experienced the emotional conditions required by the Concept.
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s explanation in Chapter 1098 was especially important. He defined genuine love through:
- care;
- concern;
- endurance;
- acceptance;
- and trust.[8]
Ariel's developing relationship with Victor subsequently allowed the dormant Concept to become a genuine lesser Divinity.

###### Love-Based Soul Reinforcement
Divine Concepts influence the nature of a God's Soul.
Ariel's Love Divinity greatly increased the compatibility and endurance of her Soul during prolonged Divine union with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]].[2]
God-level reproduction places enormous strain upon the Souls involved because conception requires the parents' spiritual Essences to interact at a very deep level.
Ariel demonstrated enough endurance to sustain this process over approximately 1,500 subjective years, a feat considered exceptional even within Victor's family.[2]
This feat should be classified primarily as exceptional Soul endurance and Love-Divinity compatibility, not simply ordinary physical stamina.

##### Former Angelic Obedience
Angels created by the [[Heavenly Father]] historically possessed an extremely deep connection to their Creator.
The relationship went beyond ordinary military discipline.
If the Heavenly Father issued an order, Ariel's Angelic nature placed enormous pressure upon her to obey. Excessive rejection of her Creator's authority could push an Angel toward Falling.[6]
This condition should not be described as Ariel having possessed literally no thoughts or personality of her own. Ariel clearly had opinions, emotions, criticism and personal judgment.
The limitation concerned her ability to fully reject her Creator's influence.

##### True Free Will
During Ariel's evaluation in Dragons Nest, [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s wives temporarily removed the [[Heavenly Father]]'s influence from her.[5]
For the first time in Ariel's existence she could express opposition toward her Creator without experiencing the normal Angelic consequences.
- Creator-Influence Removal: The direct supernatural pressure associated with the Heavenly Father's authority was removed.
- Fall-Mechanism Suppression: Ariel could criticize or reject her father without her wings automatically turning black or experiencing the normal pain associated with Falling.
- Independent Decision-Making: Ariel was able to assess the marriage arrangement entirely according to her own desires.
- Emotional Release: Thousands of years of suppressed frustration immediately surfaced once the restriction disappeared.
The event proved that Ariel's genuine personality existed separately from the obedience encoded within Angelic nature.
Following her later transformation into a Blood Dragon, Ariel ceased to be biologically dependent upon ordinary Angelic racial mechanics, making the old Fall weakness largely obsolete.

##### Mental Resilience
Ariel possesses exceptional mental discipline developed across eons of military command.
When abruptly placed before more than thirty True Dragon Goddesses in Chapter 1042, Ariel experienced extreme fear but remained functional, observant and capable of conversation.[9]
Ruby and [[Scathach]] specifically recognized that Ariel was handling the overwhelming pressure considerably better than ordinary Angels could.
- Fear Resistance: Ariel can continue functioning under enormous supernatural pressure.
- Mental Discipline: Eons of military experience allow her to control panic sufficiently to make decisions.
- Command Composure: Ariel can continue directing others even during catastrophic battlefield situations.
- Emotional Suppression: Her old Angelic training made her extraordinarily capable of hiding frustration and other feelings, although this trait was not always healthy.
This resilience is not absolute. Ariel experienced genuine existential terror upon hearing [[Azathoth Elderblood]]'s voice, demonstrating that sufficiently superior existences can overwhelm even her mental defenses.[10]

##### Extraordinary Soul and Physical Endurance
Ariel possessed exceptional endurance even as a pure Angel.
She fought [[Lilith Elderblood]] continuously for several weeks at full strength before finally reaching physical and spiritual exhaustion.[10]
- Extended Combat: Ariel can remain in high-intensity combat for weeks under accelerated conditions.
- High-Level Energy Reserves: Her Seraphim/Virtue body contained enormous supernatural reserves.
- Soul Endurance: High-level combat between Divine beings taxes the Soul in addition to the body.
- Post-Dragon Enhancement: Her transformation into a Blood Dragon greatly increased these qualities.
- Love-Divinity Synergy: Her developing Love Concept further strengthens her Soul under conditions involving genuine emotional union.[2]

##### Angelic Martial Arts
Ariel was trained in the martial system historically developed for Heaven's Angels.
She possesses genuine experience rather than relying exclusively upon raw Angelic power.
When [[Rose Adrasteia Elderblood]] examined Ariel in Chapter 1042, however, she immediately recognized an important weakness: Ariel had limited herself almost entirely to the old Angelic [[Martial Arts]], which had become extremely archaic compared with more advanced systems developed elsewhere.[9]
- Armed Combat: Ariel is skilled with swords, spears and other conventional Angelic weapons.
- Unarmed Combat: Her military training includes close-range physical combat.
- Aerial Combat: Ariel can integrate flight into martial engagements.
- Energy-Weapon Integration: Holy/Light Energy can be incorporated into her weapon techniques.
- Extensive Battle Experience: Ariel has fought Demons and other supernatural opponents across eons.

###### Martial Limitation
Ariel's historical [[Martial Arts]] should not be placed at the same technical level as dedicated modern Grandmasters such as [[Rose Adrasteia Elderblood]], [[Scathach Elderblood Scarlett]] or [[Victor Elderblood]].
Rose specifically considered the traditional Angelic style outdated and something that would need to be changed.[9]
Ariel's later membership in Clan Elderblood gives her access to far more advanced martial knowledge, but a chapter explicitly demonstrating her mastering the family's highest Martial Arts should be cited before assigning such techniques to her.

##### Weapon Proficiency
Ariel possesses extensive experience using conventional and supernatural weapons.
During her confrontation with Lilith she immediately selected a sword from the battlefield and fought with it while simultaneously manifesting her Angelic armor.[5]
Her principal historical weapons include:
- swords;
- spears;
- Holy/Light constructs;
- and standard Angelic military equipment.

##### Angelic Armor Manifestation
Ariel can directly manifest the white-and-gold Divine armor associated with her military position.
The armor appeared around her body immediately when her fight against Lilith began.[5]
Its exact individual enchantments have not been explained in sufficient detail to assign specialized abilities beyond functioning as high-level Angelic combat equipment.

##### Battlefield Leadership
Ariel is one of the most experienced military commanders produced by Heaven.
Her leadership abilities became especially important during the Demonic War after the deaths of [[Gabriel]] and Michael.
Ariel voluntarily assumed overall command of Heaven's remaining army and successfully reorganized forces that had been losing momentum.[7]
- Army Command: Ariel can direct massive Angelic forces.
- Rapid Strategic Assessment: She evaluates threats according to both political and military consequences.
- Defensive Warfare: Ariel's presence at the Vatican forced [[Sitri]]'s Demonic forces into a defensive strategy.[1]
- Retreat Coordination: She can recognize when a battlefield has become strategically untenable and organize withdrawal.
- Faction Coordination: Ariel has experience cooperating with Humans, Inquisition forces and later foreign Pantheons.
- Command Succession: She successfully assumed responsibility after Heaven lost several of its most important original commanders.
- Current/Former Angel Commander: By Chapter 873 Ariel explicitly held Michael's former position as Commander of the Angels.[11]

##### Tactical Intelligence
Ariel is rational and strategically cautious.
During [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]]'s first appearance at the Vatican, she did not attack simply because he was a Vampire Progenitor standing near Heaven's enemies. She observed that Victor had not targeted her forces and ordered her Angels to retreat toward safety instead of creating another enemy unnecessarily.[1]
This discipline distinguishes Ariel from combatants whose first response to unknown power is immediate confrontation.

##### Empathy and Intent Reading
Ariel can gain significant information concerning another person's intentions through observation and her conceptual nature.[1]
This manifests as an unusually strong form of supernatural intuition.
- Hostility Recognition: Ariel can recognize whether another being genuinely intends to attack.
- Emotional Perception: She can infer mental and emotional states through supernatural perception.
- Deception Awareness: Obvious mismatches between intention and behavior are difficult to hide from her.
- Leadership Application: The ability assists with diplomacy and battlefield command.
It should not be described as unrestricted telepathy or perfect Mind Reading without further evidence.

##### Polyglot
Ariel has existed across enormous portions of Human history and understands numerous mortal languages.
Confirmed examples include:
- Ancient Hebrew
- Latin[5]
Her knowledge almost certainly extends beyond these languages because of her long role coordinating Heaven's interactions with Human civilizations, but only directly demonstrated examples should be treated as confirmed.

##### Divine/Angelic Perception
Ariel possesses highly developed supernatural senses.
- Energy Perception: She can perceive supernatural Energy and distinguish different supernatural beings.
- Divinity Perception: Ariel recognized Divine authority manifested around other beings.
- Threat Assessment: She can estimate the supernatural danger posed by another existence.
- Soul/Sin Awareness: During her first encounter with [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] she could perceive the enormous quantity of bloodshed and sin associated with his existence.[1]
Her current Blood Dragon senses further amplify these abilities.

##### Anti-Demonic Advantage
Holy Angelic power possesses a natural advantage against many conventional Demons.
During the Demonic War, this attribute allowed Heaven's high-ranking Angels to inflict particularly severe injuries upon [[Diablo]]'s forces.
Ariel's Light and [[Holy Energy]] therefore operate not only as ordinary destructive power but as Energies naturally harmful to many Hell-born beings.
This advantage is not absolute. Exceptional Demons, Gods, Hell rulers or beings with sufficient resistance can contend with Angelic Holy power.

##### Former Vulnerability to Miasma
Ariel's Angelic nature made concentrated Miasma exceptionally dangerous.
When struck by a specialized Hell Metal bullet infused with Miasma, Ariel's normal Angelic healing was unable to properly restore the injury because the corruptive Energy continued spreading through her body.[4]
[[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] was required to physically extract and consume the contaminating Energy before Ariel could recover.
This weakness applies most directly to her former pure-Angel physiology.
Her current Blood Dragon body is vastly superior and no longer consists solely of the Holy Angelic foundation that made the original interaction so dangerous. The exact degree of her current Miasma resistance has not been separately quantified.

##### Former Fall Mechanism
Ariel historically risked becoming a Fallen Angel if her thoughts and actions moved too far from the [[Heavenly Father]]'s authority.
Her instability became visible through her wings darkening when resentment and envy toward Lilith grew severe.[12]
The Elderblood women demonstrated that removing the Heavenly Father's direct influence prevented this automatic consequence.[5]
Ariel's subsequent Race change further makes the old biological mechanism largely irrelevant to her current existence.

#### Power and Scaling
Ariel belonged to the highest level of Heaven's military even before becoming a Blood Dragon.
Important indicators of her pre-Dragon power include:
- being one of the Seven Heavenly Virtues;[1]
- forcing [[Sitri]]'s forces into defensive warfare at the Vatican;[1]
- being trusted to protect one of Heaven's principal Human organizations;
- fighting [[Baal]] during the Demonic War;[13]
- taking command after Michael and [[Gabriel]]'s deaths;[7]
- and eventually occupying Michael's position as Commander of the Angels.[11]
The previous claim that Ariel casually defeated or completely stalemated War, Death and Sitri simultaneously should be worded more carefully.
Ariel's presence and the Holy position she defended made direct action extremely difficult for the Demons, and War and Death resorted to using an infiltrator to neutralize Ariel before advancing their own objective. This demonstrates Ariel's strategic and supernatural importance without requiring an unsupported claim that she was effortlessly overpowering all three elite Demons at once.
Her current Blood Dragon-Goddess state is vastly stronger than the form responsible for these early feats.

#### Resistances
- Mental Pressure Resistance: Ariel maintained functional composure while surrounded by more than thirty True Dragon Goddesses.[9]
- Fear Resistance: Eons of military command allow her to continue functioning under extreme supernatural intimidation.
- Holy/Divine Energy Resistance: Ariel naturally tolerates enormous quantities of Holy and [[Divine Energy]].
- Light Resistance: Her own major Divinity and historical Angelic affinity grant extremely high resistance to Light-based effects.
- Physical Resistance: Blood Dragon physiology grants extraordinary resistance to conventional physical attacks.
- Soul Resilience: Her Dragon Goddess Soul can withstand extremely prolonged high-level combat and Divine interaction.
- Creator-Influence Resistance (Current): Ariel has experienced existence without the [[Heavenly Father]]'s supernatural influence and no longer possesses an ordinary Angel body dependent upon the traditional Fall mechanism.[5]
- Longevity/Age Resistance: Ariel has existed for eons without ordinary biological deterioration.

#### Notable Feats
- As the Virtue of Generosity, possessed Conceptual authority placing her above ordinary Seraphim.[1]
- Forced [[Sitri]]'s Demonic forces into a defensive strategy merely through the difficulty of overcoming Ariel and the Holy territory she protected.[1]
- Survived a Hell Metal/Miasma attack that prevented ordinary Angelic healing long enough for [[Victor Elderblood|Victor]] to remove the contamination.[4]
- Fought [[Baal]], one of the highest Demon Dukes participating in the Demonic War.[13]
- Assumed leadership over Heaven's army after the deaths of Michael and [[Gabriel]].[7]
- Became the current Commander of the Angels occupying Michael's former position by Chapter 873.[11]
- Maintained enough mental composure to undergo interrogation/evaluation while surrounded by more than thirty True Dragon Goddesses.[9]
- Demonstrated genuine loyalty to Heaven even after the [[Heavenly Father]]'s influence was removed, refusing to trade the Creation Tree for her own freedom.[5]
- Exercised unrestricted free will without immediately Falling once the supernatural influence responsible for the old mechanism was suppressed.[5]
- Fought [[Lilith Elderblood]] continuously for several weeks at full strength.[10]
- Developed genuine Godly Divinities over Generosity and Light and a lesser Divinity over Love.[3]
- Sustained exceptionally prolonged Soul interaction with Victor, demonstrating Divine-Soul endurance comparable only to some of the most spiritually resilient women in his family.[2]
- Became the mother of twin Blood Dragon daughters, including [[Ariella Victoria Elderblood]].[2][14]
